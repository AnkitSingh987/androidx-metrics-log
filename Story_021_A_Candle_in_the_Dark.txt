Title: A Candle in the Dark
Category: Beginner

It is ten o'clock at night. Sophie is alone in her apartment. The apartment is very quiet. She is reading a book in her bed. She has a small lamp next to her bed. The lamp gives a soft light. 
Outside, the wind is blowing. It is a windy night. 

Suddenly, the small lamp turns off. The light is gone. The apartment is completely dark. 
Sophie stops reading. She opens her eyes wide. She cannot see anything. The darkness is heavy. 
"Oh no," she says. "The electricity is gone."

Sophie is not scared, but she does not like the dark. She wants some light. She remembers she has some candles in the kitchen. She must go to the kitchen to find a candle. 

She puts her book on the bed. She slowly puts her feet on the floor. The floor is cold. She stands up. 
She puts her hands out in front of her. She walks very slowly. She does not want to hit a chair. She does not want to hit the wall. 
She walks out of the bedroom. She walks into the hall. The hall is dark too. She walks to the kitchen. 

She reaches the kitchen. She knows where the candles are. They are in the second drawer next to the sink. 
She touches the counter. She finds the drawer. She pulls the drawer open. She puts her hand inside. 
She touches some paper. She touches a plastic spoon. Then, she touches something long and round. It is made of wax. It is a candle! 
She is happy. She takes the candle. 
Now, she needs matches. She feels around in the drawer again. She finds a small, rectangular box. It is a box of matches. 

She closes the drawer. She has the candle and the matches. She is ready. 
She holds the candle in her left hand. She takes a match from the box with her right hand. 
She strikes the match against the side of the box. 
Scratch! 
Nothing happens. 
She tries again. 
Scratch! 
A small flame appears on the match. It is a small yellow and blue flame. It brings a little light to the dark kitchen. 

Sophie brings the match to the top of the candle. The fire touches the string on the candle. The string catches fire. The candle is lit. 
She shakes the match. The fire on the match goes out. 

Now, Sophie has a good light. The candle is not very bright, but it is enough. The flame dances a little bit. It is a warm, yellow light. 
She walks slowly back to the living room. She carries the candle carefully. She does not want the hot wax to fall on her hands. She does not want the hot wax to fall on the floor. 

She goes to the living room. She puts the candle on the small table. She sits on the sofa. 
She looks around the room. The room looks different in the candle light. It looks soft and peaceful. 
The flame makes shadows on the wall. The shadows are big and dark. They move when the flame moves. 
Sophie raises her hand. The shadow of her hand is on the wall. The shadow hand is very big. She moves her fingers. The big shadow fingers move too. It is like a game. She smiles. 

The apartment is very quiet. The refrigerator is not making a noise. The clock is not making a noise. Only the wind outside makes a noise. 
Sophie sits on the sofa. She watches the small flame. It is beautiful to watch. The wax slowly melts. It becomes hot liquid. 
She feels warm and calm. The darkness is not scary when you have a candle. 

She waits for the electricity to come back. She waits for ten minutes. She waits for twenty minutes. 
She thinks about her book. She wants to read. But the candle light is not good for reading. It is too soft. If she reads in this light, her eyes will hurt. 
So, she just sits and waits. She thinks about her day. She thinks about her friends. She enjoys the quiet time. 

After thirty minutes, the lights suddenly turn on. The ceiling light is very bright. The lamp in the bedroom turns on. 
The electricity is back! 
Sophie blinks her eyes. The bright light is strong. 
She looks at the candle. The yellow flame is still there. But now, it looks very small. It looks very weak. The electric light is much stronger. 

Sophie leans forward. She blows on the candle. 'Puff.' 
The flame disappears. A little bit of grey smoke goes up into the air. 
The room is bright and normal again. The refrigerator starts making a humming noise. The magic of the candle light is gone. 

Sophie stands up. She leaves the candle on the table. She will need it again next time. 
She walks back to her bedroom. She sits on her bed. She picks up her book. She turns on her small reading lamp. The light is good and steady. She starts to read her book again. The dark is gone, and the story continues.
