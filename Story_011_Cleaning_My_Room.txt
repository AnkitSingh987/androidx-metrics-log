Title: Cleaning My Room
Category: Beginner

It is Sunday morning. Leo is ten years old. He is in his bedroom. His bedroom is very messy. 

There are clothes on the floor. There are dirty socks near the door. There is a blue shirt on the chair. There are trousers on the bed. 
There are toys on the floor too. There is a red toy car. There are building blocks. There is a small ball. 
His books are not on the shelf. His books are on the desk. His books are on the floor. 
His bed is not made. The blanket is on the floor. The pillows are upside down. 

Leo's mother comes into the room. She looks around. She shakes her head. 
"Oh, Leo," she says. "Your room is very dirty. It is very messy."
"I know, Mother," Leo says. "I was playing."
"You must clean your room today," she says. "You cannot live in a messy room."
"Okay, Mother," Leo says. "I will clean it now."

Leo's mother leaves the room. Leo looks at his messy room. Where should he start? 
First, he looks at his clothes. He picks up the dirty socks from the floor. He picks up the blue shirt from the chair. He picks up the trousers from the bed. 
He puts all the dirty clothes in a basket. The basket is in the corner of the room. He will take the basket to the washing machine later. 

Next, he looks at his toys. He likes his toys. But they belong in the toy box. 
He picks up the red toy car. He puts it in the toy box. He picks up the building blocks. There are many blocks. There are red blocks, blue blocks, and yellow blocks. He puts them all in the toy box. He picks up the small ball. He puts it in the toy box. 
He closes the toy box. The floor looks better now. There are no clothes on the floor. There are no toys on the floor. 

Now, Leo looks at his books. He has school books and story books. 
He takes his school books from the desk. He puts them neatly on the desk. He puts his pens in his pencil case. 
He takes his story books from the floor. He carries them to the bookshelf. The bookshelf has three shelves. He puts the big books on the bottom shelf. He puts the small books on the top shelf. The books look very nice on the shelf. 

The floor is clean. The desk is clean. The bookshelf is neat. But the bed is still messy. 
Leo walks to his bed. He picks up the blanket from the floor. He shakes the blanket. He puts the blanket on the bed. He pulls it straight. 
He takes his pillows. He has two pillows. He hits the pillows with his hands. They become soft and fluffy. He puts the pillows at the top of the bed. He makes the bed perfectly. 

Leo is tired. He sits on his clean bed. He looks around his room. 
His room looks different. It looks big. It looks bright. It looks clean. 
He is very happy. He likes his clean room. 

He stands up and goes to the kitchen. His mother is cooking lunch. 
"Mother," Leo says. "My room is clean."
His mother smiles. "Really?" she asks. "Can I see it?"
"Yes, come with me," Leo says. 

He walks with his mother to his bedroom. He opens the door. 
His mother looks inside. She sees the clean floor. She sees the neat bookshelf. She sees the clean desk. She sees the made bed. 
"Wow, Leo," she says. "You did a very good job. Your room looks beautiful."
"Thank you, Mother," Leo says. He is very proud. 
"It is much better to have a clean room," she says. "It is easier to find your things. It is nicer to sleep in a clean bed."
"Yes, I know," Leo says. 

"Now," his mother says. "Are you hungry?"
"Yes, I am very hungry," Leo says. "Cleaning is hard work."
"Lunch is ready," she says. "Go and wash your hands. Then come to the table."
"Okay, Mother," Leo says. 

He goes to the bathroom. He washes his hands with soap and water. He dries his hands. 
He goes back to the kitchen. He sits at the table. He eats his lunch. His lunch is a cheese sandwich and an apple. It is very tasty. 
After lunch, Leo goes back to his room. He wants to read a book. He goes to his neat bookshelf. He easily finds his favorite book. He sits on his clean bed. He reads his book. 
He is very comfortable. He will not make his room messy again. He will try to keep it clean every day. A clean room is a happy room.
