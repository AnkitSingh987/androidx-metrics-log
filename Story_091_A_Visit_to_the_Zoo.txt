Title: A Visit to the Zoo
Category: Beginner

It was a bright, sunny Saturday morning in late spring. Seven-year-old Lily and her older brother, ten-year-old Sam, were jumping up and down with excitement in the kitchen. Today was a very special day. Their parents were taking them to the City Zoo. 

"Did you pack the camera, Dad?" Sam asked. He wanted to take pictures of all the animals. 
"Yes, it is in my bag," his father replied, checking his pockets for the car keys. 
"And did you pack the sandwiches, Mom?" Lily asked. 
"Yes, I packed a big picnic lunch," her mother smiled. "Now, put on your shoes. We are ready to go."

The drive to the zoo took forty-five minutes. The children looked out the window, counting the other cars on the highway. They could not wait to see the elephants, the tigers, and the bears. 

When they arrived, the zoo parking lot was already half full. Many other families had the same idea for a sunny Saturday. They parked the car and walked towards the main entrance. The entrance was a huge arch made of stone, with a massive metal sign that read 'CITY ZOO' in bright yellow letters. 

There was a long line of people waiting to buy tickets. 
"You two stay here with your mother," their father said. "I will go and buy our tickets."
He walked to the small ticket window. He paid for two adult tickets and two children's tickets. The lady behind the glass gave him the tickets and a glossy, colorful piece of paper. 

"Here we go," their father said, walking back to the family. He handed the colorful paper to Sam. "You can be the navigator today, Sam. This is the zoo map."

Sam opened the map. It was very large. It showed all the different paths, ponds, and animal enclosures in the zoo. The map was divided into different colors. The blue area was for water animals, the green area was for jungle animals, and the yellow area was for animals from the dry savanna. 

"Where should we go first?" their mother asked, looking over Sam's shoulder. 
Sam studied the map carefully. "We are here, at the red star," he said, pointing to the bottom of the map. "The monkey house is very close, just on the left. But the giraffes are on the right."

"I want to see the giraffes first!" Lily shouted happily. "They are my absolute favorite."
"Okay," Sam agreed. "We will go to the right. We have to follow the yellow path."

They walked through the heavy metal turnstiles. The man at the gate smiled and took their tickets. 
As soon as they stepped inside, they felt like they were in a different world. The air smelled different. It smelled like dry earth, green leaves, and exotic animals. They heard strange, loud noises coming from the trees. A colorful parrot squawked loudly above their heads. 

"Wow," Lily whispered, looking around with wide eyes. 
The zoo was incredibly beautiful. There were wide, clean paths to walk on. Large trees provided cool shade from the sun. Beautiful flowers grew in large pots along the walkways. There were small wooden signs pointing in every direction. One sign said 'Lions', another said 'Penguins', and another said 'Reptile House'. 

"Follow me," Sam said proudly, holding the map like an explorer. "The giraffes are this way."
He led his family down the wide yellow path. They walked past a large, shallow pool where several pink flamingos were standing on one leg. They walked past a grassy hill where some small, brown deer were eating quietly. 

Lily held her mother's hand tightly. She was so excited that she could not stop skipping. It was only ten o'clock in the morning, and the great zoo adventure had just begun. They were on their way to see the tallest animals in the world, and they still had the whole day ahead of them to explore every corner of the giant map.
