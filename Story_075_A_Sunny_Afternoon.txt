Title: A Sunny Afternoon
Category: Beginner

It is two o'clock in the afternoon. Ben, Mia, and their father are still at the park. They played on the swings, they played football, and they fed the ducks. Now, they are very tired. It is time to relax. 

Their father carries the blue backpack to a large, quiet area of grass. It is far away from the noisy playground and the duck pond. There is a very big, old tree here. It provides a lot of cool shade from the hot sun. 
"This is a perfect spot," their father says. 

He opens the blue backpack. He pulls out a large blanket. It is a thick blanket with red and white squares. He unfolds the blanket and lays it flat on the green grass under the tree. 
"Come and sit down," he tells his children. 
Ben and Mia take off their shoes. The grass feels soft and cool on their bare feet. They sit down on the red and white blanket. 

"I am hungry again," Ben says. 
"Me too," Mia agrees. 
Their father smiles. "I thought you might be hungry after all that playing. I packed a small picnic."
He reaches into the backpack again. He takes out three red apples, a box of crackers, and some small pieces of yellow cheese. He also takes out three small boxes of apple juice. 

He puts the food in the middle of the blanket. 
"It is not a big lunch, but it is a good snack," he says. 
Ben takes a cracker, puts a piece of cheese on top, and eats it in one bite. It is crunchy and delicious. Mia takes an apple. She bites into it with a loud crunch. It is sweet and juicy. 

They eat their small picnic slowly. They do not talk very much. The park is very peaceful here. They listen to the wind blowing gently through the leaves of the old tree above them. 'Rustle, rustle, rustle', the leaves say. They can hear a few birds singing in the branches. 

When the food is gone, they drink their apple juice. 
"That was a very good snack, Dad," Ben says. 
"Yes, thank you," Mia adds. 
"You are welcome," their father replies. He packs the empty juice boxes and the apple cores into a small plastic bag. He wants to keep the park clean. 

After eating, nobody wants to move. The afternoon is warm and lazy. 
Their father lies down on his back on the blanket. He puts his hands behind his head. 
"Look up at the sky," he says to them. 

Ben and Mia lie down on their backs next to their father. They look up. Through the green leaves of the tree, they can see the sky. It is a very bright blue. There are a few large, white clouds moving slowly across the sky. They look like big, soft pieces of cotton. 

"That cloud looks like a dog," Mia says. She points a small finger at the sky. 
Ben looks at the cloud. "Yes, I see it. It has a head and a tail. But look at that one over there. That cloud looks like a big boat."
"I see a mountain," their father says. 

They watch the clouds for a long time. The wind pushes the clouds slowly. The dog cloud changes shape. It becomes a long snake. The boat cloud breaks into two smaller clouds. It is like watching a slow, silent movie in the sky. 

Ben feels very relaxed. His legs are tired from running, but his mind is calm. The grass is soft underneath the blanket. The air is warm, but the shade from the tree keeps them cool. 
He looks at his sister. Mia's eyes are closed. She is breathing softly. The busy morning at the park made her very sleepy. 
He looks at his father. His father's eyes are closed too. He has a small, peaceful smile on his face. 

Ben closes his eyes. He feels the warm breeze on his face. He listens to the birds and the rustling leaves. He does not want to be anywhere else. A busy morning is fun, but a quiet, sunny afternoon under a tree is just as wonderful. It is the perfect end to a perfect day at the park. 
He falls asleep on the red and white blanket, dreaming of footballs, ducks, and clouds shaped like boats.
