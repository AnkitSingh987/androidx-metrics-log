Title: The School Library
Category: Beginner

Every school has many different rooms. There are classrooms for learning. There is a cafeteria for eating. There is a gym for sports. But my favorite room in the whole school is the library. 

The school library is a very special place. It is a big room on the first floor. It has large windows, so it is always full of light. 
When you walk into the library, you see books. There are books everywhere. There are tall wooden shelves against the walls. The shelves are full of books. There are short shelves in the middle of the room. They are also full of books. 

There are many different kinds of books. There are story books with pictures for young children. There are chapter books for older children. There are history books about the past. There are science books about animals and planets. There are dictionaries to help you spell words. If you want to know about something, you can find a book about it in the library. 

The library is a very quiet place. This is the most important rule. You must be quiet in the library. You cannot shout. You cannot run. You cannot play loud games. If you want to talk to your friend, you must whisper. 'Whisper, whisper, whisper.' 
People come to the library to read and study. They need a quiet place to think. If you make a loud noise, you will disturb them. 

There is a big desk near the door. The librarian sits at this big desk. Our librarian is Mrs. Green. She is a very nice woman. She wears glasses. She loves books very much. She knows where every book is in the library. 

If you cannot find a book, you can ask Mrs. Green. 
"Excuse me, Mrs. Green," you say. "I am looking for a book about dogs."
Mrs. Green will smile. "A book about dogs? Come with me."
She will walk to a specific shelf. She will look at the books. Then she will pull out a book. 
"Here is a very good book about dogs," she will say. 

In the middle of the library, there are tables and chairs. The tables are round. The chairs are comfortable. Students sit at these tables to do their homework. Sometimes, a whole class comes to the library. The teacher reads a story to the class. The children sit on a big blue rug on the floor and listen. 

You do not have to read the books only in the library. You can take them home! This is called borrowing a book. 
When you find a book you want to read at home, you take it to Mrs. Green's desk. 
You give her the book. You also give her your library card. 
Mrs. Green uses a scanner. She scans the barcode on the back of the book. Beep! 
Then she looks at the computer. 
"Okay," she says. "You can keep this book for two weeks. Please bring it back on time. And please take care of it."

You must be careful with library books. They belong to the school. They belong to everyone. You cannot write in a library book. You cannot eat food near a library book. You cannot tear the pages. If you damage a book, you must pay for a new one. 

I love to borrow books. I usually borrow two books every week. I put them in my school bag. I carry them home carefully. In the evening, before I go to sleep, I read my library books in my bed. 

The library is my favorite room because it is a room full of stories. It is a room full of adventures. When I open a book in the library, I can travel to the moon. I can meet a dinosaur. I can be a pirate on a ship. And I do not even have to leave my chair. The school library is a magic place, and Mrs. Green is the keeper of the magic.
