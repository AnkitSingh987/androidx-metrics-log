Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Train and the Tree

One day, a big train was going down the tracks. The train was pale blue and very fast. It loved to go fast and make the people inside smile. The train saw a little girl sitting by the tracks. She looked sad. The train stopped and let out a big sigh. "Why are you sad?" the train asked the little girl. The little girl looked up and said, "I lost my toy." The train wanted to help. "Get on and we will find your toy together," said the train. The little girl smiled and got on the train. They went fast down the tracks, looking for the toy. The little girl saw her toy near a tree. The train stopped and the little girl picked up her toy. She was so happy. The train took the little girl back home. The little girl hugged the train and said, "Thank you!" The train was happy to help and went back to making people smile.

One day, a little mosquito was flying around. It was very fit and fast. It liked to play with its friends in the air. They would fly up and down, and all around. The mosquito was happy when it played with its friends. One time, the mosquito saw a big group of kids. They were playing a fun game. They would clap their hands and sing a song. The mosquito liked the song and wanted to play too. It flew close to the kids, but it was too small for them to see. The mosquito tried to clap its wings like the kids clapped their hands. It wanted to be a part of the game. The kids heard the little clap and looked around. They saw the mosquito and smiled. They let the mosquito play with them, and they all clapped and sang together. The mosquito was very happy to be part of the fun.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing with his toy blocks. He liked to make numbers with them. Tim felt a big yawn coming. He was tired, but he wanted to keep playing. So, he tried to build the number "3" with his blocks. Suddenly, his dog, Spot, came running into the room. Spot was upset because he lost his ball. He bumped into Tim's blocks and knocked them over. Tim was upset too, but then he saw that the blocks made a new shape. It was not the number "3" anymore. It was the number "8"! Tim was happy because he discovered a new number by accident. He showed the number "8" to his mom and dad. They were proud of him. Tim learned that even when things go wrong, something good can happen. And Spot found his ball under the couch, so he was happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to perform tricks for his friends. One day, he thought of an original trick. He wanted to show it to his best friend, Kitty. "Kitty, come and see my new trick," Spot said. "I have an original piece to perform for you." Kitty came running to see what Spot had planned. Spot jumped high and spun around in the air. Kitty clapped and said, "Wow, Spot! That's a great trick!" But then, Spot tried to do the trick again. This time, he jumped too high and fell down. He hurt his paw and cried. "Oh no, Spot!" said Kitty. "I'm sorry you got hurt." Spot's paw was sore for many days, and he couldn't perform his original trick anymore. Spot and Kitty were sad, but they still played together and had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy was very adventurous. She loved to play outside, climb trees, and explore the woods near her house. One day, Amy found a small, lost kitten. The kitten was very hungry. Amy wanted to help the kitten, so she went home and asked her mom, "Mom, can we give some meat to the kitten? It's very hungry." Her mom said, "Of course, sweetheart! We can separate some meat from our dinner for the kitten." So, Amy and her mom took some meat from their dinner and gave it to the kitten. The kitten was very happy and ate it all up. From that day on, the kitten stayed with Amy and they went on many adventurous trips together. They became the best of friends and lived happily ever after.
