Category: Intermediate
Title: The Tiger and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a big tiger. He loved to play with his friends in the forest. The tiger was always enthusiastic and happy. One day, the tiger saw a big pond. He wanted to jump in the water and swim. The tiger ran fast and took a big dive into the pond. He splashed and laughed. The other animals saw the tiger having fun. They all came to the pond too. They took turns to dive in the water and play together. The tiger and his friends had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a plan. She wanted to make a card for her mom's birthday. Sue thought, "I don't want a regular card. I want a big and pretty card!" Sue asked her friend, Tom, for help. Tom said, "Let's find big paper and color pens." They looked everywhere for the big paper and color pens. They found them under Sue's bed. Sue and Tom started to make the card. They drew flowers and a big heart. The next day, Sue gave the card to her mom. Her mom was so happy! She said, "This is the best card ever! Thank you, Sue and Tom!" Sue smiled and said, "We planned it just for you, Mom!" And they all had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Ben. Ben lived in a beautiful landscape with many smooth hills. One day, Ben saw a big tree with a yummy carrot hanging from it. Ben wanted to eat the carrot, so he decided to jump and reach for it. Ben tried to jump high, but he could not reach the carrot. He saw his friend, a bird named Tim. Ben asked, "Tim, can you help me get the carrot? I want to eat it, but I can't jump high enough." Tim was happy to help his friend. He flew up to the tree and picked up the carrot with his beak. Then, Tim brought the carrot down to Ben. Ben was so happy, he said, "Thank you, Tim! You are a good friend." Together, Ben and Tim shared the yummy carrot under the big tree. They played and jumped around the smooth hills in the beautiful landscape. Ben and Tim were the best of friends, and they had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a kind farmer named Tom. Tom had a nice big barn and he liked to work in it. One day, Tom wandered into his nice barn to do some work and he bumped into his sack filled with food. He was surprised and he said, "What is that sack doing here?". Just then, a small mouse peeked out from the sack and said, "I'm sorry Mister Tom, my mouse friends and I were looking for food and we found this bag of food. We were hungry, so we took this food to our mouse house." Tom smiled and said, "That's okay, mouse friends. This food is too much for you to eat, so here." He gave the mouse a few carrots and some bread and said, "This will be enough for you and your friends." The mouse thanked Tom and he and his mouse friends ran off. Tom watched them leave, happy that he was able to share his food with his friendly rodent visitors. From that day on, Tom always kept the barn nice and clean for his friendly visitors.

Once upon a time there was a tiny girl named Lucy. She loved to look outside her window and guess what would happen next. One day she looked out her window and she saw the east. The east was bright and beautiful. She was so grateful she could see it. At the east, she saw a lot of animals. She guessed there were rabbits, foxes and even deer! They were all happily running around, playing and enjoying the sun. Lucy was so happy she could watch them play. The animals then began to look at Lucy and she guessed they wanted her to join in. She went outside, and gave them big cuddles. She was so grateful they let her join in. The animals and Lucy played together until the sun began to go down. Lucy said goodbye to her new friends and thanked them for being so kind. She was so happy and grateful.
