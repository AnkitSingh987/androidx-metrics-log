Category: Intermediate
Title: Bobby the Weak Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a weak little bird named Bobby. Bobby loved the sunshine and always wanted to play outside with his friends. But because he was weak, he could not fly as high or as fast as the other birds. One day, Bobby's friends insisted that he should try to fly to the top of a tall tree. They said it would be fun and that he could see the whole town from up there. Bobby was scared, but he wanted to see the sunshine from the top of the tree, so he tried. Bobby tried very hard, but he could not reach the top of the tree. His friends were sad for him, but they still loved him. Bobby learned that it's okay to be weak and not be able to do everything. The important thing is to try your best and have fun with your friends. And that's what Bobby did, enjoying the sunshine with his friends every day.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a broken balloon on the floor. It was red and had a small hole in it. Tim wanted to play with the balloon, so he asked his mom if she could fix it. Mom took the broken balloon and put a small piece of tape over the hole. She blew air into the balloon and tied a knot. The balloon was not broken anymore! Tim was very happy and started to play with the balloon in the living room. As Tim played, he took big steps to catch the balloon. With every step, he laughed and clapped his hands. The balloon went up and down, and Tim had so much fun. He played with the balloon all day until it was time for bed.

Once upon a time, in an ancient land, there was a big bush. The bush was very old and had many leaves. One day, a little boy named Tom went near the bush to play. Tom saw a small bird sitting on the bush. The bird looked sad and had a frown on its face. Tom asked the bird, "Why are you sad, little bird?" The bird replied, "I lost my friend, and I can't find her." Tom wanted to help the bird find its friend. They looked around the bush and called out for the bird's friend. After a while, they heard a sound coming from the other side of the bush. They went to see what the sound was and found the bird's friend stuck in a small hole. Tom helped the bird's friend out of the hole, and the two birds were very happy. They thanked Tom for his help, and they all played together near the bush for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, there was a playful otter named Ollie. Ollie loved to swim and play in the sun. One day, Ollie saw the sun shine on a big rock. The rock was shiny and pretty. Ollie wanted to play on the rock. Ollie swam to the rock, but there was another otter there. The other otter said, "This is my rock. You can't play here." Ollie felt sad and asked, "Can we share the rock and play together?" The other otter thought for a moment and said, "Okay, we can share the rock." So, Ollie and the other otter played together on the shiny rock. They had lots of fun and became good friends. From that day on, they always shared the rock and played together when the sun was shining.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a nosy cat named Kitty. Kitty liked to collect things she found on her walks. One day, she found a big red ball. Kitty saw a dog named Max with a funny face. Max was sad because he lost his big red ball. Kitty said, "I found a big red ball. Is it yours?" Max looked at the ball and said, "Yes, that's my ball! Thank you, Kitty!" From that day on, Kitty and Max became good friends. They played together and sometimes Kitty would still collect things she found. But now, she shared her finds with Max, and they were both very happy.
