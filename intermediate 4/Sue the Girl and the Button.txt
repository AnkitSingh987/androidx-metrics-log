Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Button

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a scooter that she loved to ride. It was not like the other scooters. It was unusual because it had a big red button on it. Sue never pressed the button. She was scared of what might happen if she did. One day, Sue met a wise old cat. The cat saw her scooter and said, "I know what the button does." Sue asked the cat to tell her. The cat said, "You must rub the button three times to see what happens." Sue was still scared, but she wanted to know. Sue took a deep breath and rubbed the button three times. Her scooter started to fly! Sue was so happy. She flew all around the town. Now, Sue knew her unusual scooter was very special. And she was never scared of the big red button again.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with yarn. One day, Kitty found a big ball of yarn in the house. Kitty played and played with the yarn. She rolled it all around the house. But then, Kitty saw that the yarn was almost gone. She wanted to complete her fun game with the yarn. Kitty looked and looked for more yarn. She found a little piece of yarn under the chair. She played with it and completed her game. Kitty was very jolly and happy.

One day, a little girl named Amy had a serious test to finish. She was very scared. She did not want to do the test. Her friend, Tim, saw her looking sad. "Why are you sad, Amy?" Tim asked. "I have a serious test to finish," Amy said. "I don't want to do it." Tim wanted to help Amy. He had an idea. "We can finish the test together," Tim said. "It will be easy and fun!" Amy and Tim worked hard on the test. They talked and helped each other. Soon, they finished the test. They were very happy. "I'm glad we finished the test together," Amy said. "Thank you, Tim!" "You're welcome, Amy," Tim said. "We can do anything when we help each other!"

One day, a big fish named Bob and a little fish named Tim were playing near the water. They liked to dive deep and swim fast. They saw a wheel under the water and wanted to play with it. Bob said, "Let's push the wheel to the top of the water!" Tim thought it was a fun idea. They tried to push the wheel, but it was difficult. The wheel was heavy and stuck in the mud. Just then, a big wave came and pushed the wheel out of the mud. It rolled and hit a big rock. The rock moved and a secret door opened. Bob and Tim were very surprised. They swam inside and found a hidden room full of toys. They played all day and had lots of fun.

One day, a pretty bird found an acorn. The bird wanted to sail far away to find a nice place to grow the acorn. The bird knew that the acorn would grow into a big tree. The bird was very happy. But then, a big wind came. The wind blew the acorn out of the bird's beak. The bird was sad. The bird knew that it had to find the acorn again. The bird looked and looked for the acorn. Finally, the bird found the acorn near the water. The bird was so happy! The bird took the acorn and sailed far away. The bird found a nice place to grow the acorn. The acorn grew into a big, pretty tree. The bird was very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a thin pillow that she loved very much. She would take it with her everywhere she went. Her pillow was her best friend. One day, Lily and her pillow went to the park. They played on the swings and the slide. Lily was very happy. She liked to take her pillow with her when she played. The pillow made her feel safe and cozy. When it was time to go home, Lily and her pillow were very tired. They had so much fun at the park. Lily took her pillow to bed and gave it a big hug. They both fell asleep, dreaming of their next big adventure together.
