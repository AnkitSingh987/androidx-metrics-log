Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Otter and the River

Once upon a time there was a little otter named Bob. Bob had an incredible talent. He could swim faster and further than any otter in all the land. He was so proud of his special gift! One day, Bob went to the river to show off his swimming skills. He swam and swam, but then he heard a loud noise. It was so loud it made his ears hurt. Bob was scared and started to swim back to the riverbank. When he reached the shore, Bob saw that the noise had come from a large construction machine. The machine had made a big hole in the river and all the water had disappeared! Poor Bob was so sad. He wanted to swim but he had nothing to swim in. Suddenly, Bob had an idea. He gathered some sticks and rocks and then he built a special kind of dam. He used his incredible swimming skills to guide the water back into the river. Bob had worked so hard and suffered a lot, but in the end he had saved the river. All the other otters were so grateful, they thanked Bob with a big ovation. It was incredible!

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy fork that he loved very much. He would play with the fork all day long. One day, Tim was playing with his fork when he heard a little voice. He looked around and saw that the fork was speaking! The fork said, "Hello, Tim! I am your friend." Tim was very surprised but happy to have a talking friend. They played together and had lots of fun. But sometimes, the fork would make Tim feel uncomfortable when it poked him. Tim told the fork, "Please be gentle when we play." The fork listened and they played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Emma. Emma was a very good girl. She had a big book with lots of prints in it. She loved to look at the prints every day. The prints were of animals, flowers, and other fun things. Emma liked to show the prints to her friends. One day, Emma saw a crazy bird outside her window. The bird was jumping and flying all around. Emma thought the bird was funny. She wanted to show the crazy bird to her friends. So, Emma prayed for the bird to stay near her house. The next day, the crazy bird was still there. Emma's friends came to see the bird. They all laughed and had fun watching the bird. Emma was happy that her prayer had worked. She knew that the prints in her book were not as fun as the real thing. But, she still loved her book with the prints. And now, she had a new friend, the crazy bird.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a kite. It was a big, pretty kite. Tim was very proud of his new kite. He wanted to show it to all his friends. He ran to the park to play with the kite. At the park, the sky got dark. It started to rain. Tim's friends went home, but Tim did not want to go. He wanted to play with his kite. The rain got heavier, and the kite got wet. Tim was sad. Tim's mom came to the park with an umbrella. She told Tim that it was not good to play with the kite in the rain. Tim learned that he should listen to his mom and not play in the rain. The next day, the sun was out, and Tim played with his kite. He was happy and proud again. The moral of the story is to listen to your parents and be careful when playing outside.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. He had a toy named Mr. Reliable. Mr. Reliable was always there for Tom. Tom loved Mr. Reliable very much. One day, Tom wanted to give Mr. Reliable a surprise. He thought, "I will give Mr. Reliable a bath!" Tom took Mr. Reliable to the bathroom. He filled a tub with water and put shampoo in it. Tom said, "Mr. Reliable, I will give you a nice bath!" Tom put Mr. Reliable in the tub. Mr. Reliable got all wet and soapy. Tom was happy to see Mr. Reliable get clean. But then, something bad happened. The water and shampoo made Mr. Reliable break. Tom was very sad. Tom cried and said, "I just wanted to give Mr. Reliable a surprise. Now he is broken." Tom learned that sometimes surprises can have bad endings.
