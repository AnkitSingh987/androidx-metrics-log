Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Room

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very eager to become a big boy. He liked to do things all by himself. One day, Tim saw a switch on the wall. He wanted to see what it did. Tim stood on his toes and reached up high. He flipped the switch and the room became very bright. Tim was so happy that he could make the room bright all by himself. Every day, Tim would flip the switch to make the room bright. He loved being a big boy and doing things all by himself. Tim became very good at flipping the switch, and he was very eager to learn more things.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a big, round potato. Lucy loved her potato very much. She would keep it safe in her room. One day, Lucy met a strong boy named Tom. Tom saw the potato and said, "Why do you have a potato?" Lucy said, "I love my potato. I keep it safe." Tom laughed and said, "Potatoes are for eating, not for keeping!" Lucy felt sad, but she did not give up. She kept her potato safe and strong. Over time, the potato grew into a tall plant. Lucy and Tom saw the plant and were very happy. They learned that if you keep something safe and give it love, it can grow big and strong.

One day, a big bossy bear was in the woods. He liked to perform tricks for his friends. Today, he was going to perform a new trick. He was very excited. The bossy bear said to his friends, "Watch me! I will jump and spin in the air!" His friends, a small bird and a soft bunny, sat and watched. The bossy bear jumped and spun, but he did not land on his feet. Instead, he fell into a cold river. "Help!" the bossy bear cried. The small bird and the soft bunny jumped into the cold river too. They all swam to the side and got out. The bossy bear was not bossy anymore. He said, "Thank you for helping me. I will not be bossy again." The friends were happy and played together in the woods.

Once upon a time, there was a poor man named Bob. Bob had no money and no car. He wanted to go to the beach, but he didn't know how to get there. One day, as Bob was walking down the street, he saw a taxi. He had an idea. He ran to the taxi and asked the driver for a ride. "I'm sorry," said the taxi driver. "I don't have enough money". Bob was sad. Just then, an old woman walked up. "Don't worry!" she said. She handed Bob some money and said, "Take this taxi to the beach. It's on me!" Bob smiled and thanked the old woman. The old woman told the taxi driver to go. As they drove off, Bob waved goodbye to the old woman. "Thank you!" he shouted. Then, he set off on his journey. Bob had a wonderful time at the beach and took lots of pictures to remember it by. Bob was very grateful to the old woman. He never knew her name, but he remembered her kindness forever.

Lily and Tom were playing hide and seek in the old house. Lily was the seeker and Tom was the hider. Lily counted to ten and then went to look for Tom. She opened doors and looked under beds, but she could not find him. She heard a giggle from the closet in the hall. She ran to the closet and opened it. She saw Tom hiding behind a big coat. She also saw a lot of cobwebs on the coat and the walls. Cobwebs are thin and sticky things that spiders make. "Found you!" Lily said. "You are in a spooky place. Are you not scared of the cobwebs?" Tom shook his head. "No, I am not scared. They are just regular cobwebs. They do not hurt. See?" He touched one of the cobwebs with his finger. It stuck to his finger and he pulled it off. Lily looked at the cobweb on his finger. She felt curious. She reached out and touched another cobweb. It stuck to her hand and she pulled it off. She laughed. "They are funny. They are like strings. Can we play with them?" Tom nodded. "Yes, we can. But we have to be careful. We do not want to make the spiders angry. They live here too." Lily and Tom decided to reveal their hiding place and come out of the closet. They took some of the cobwebs with them and made shapes and patterns with them. They had fun playing with the cobwebs until their mom called them for lunch. They left the old house and went to eat. They told their mom about their adventure and showed her their cobweb art. Their mom smiled and praised them for being brave and creative. She also told them to wash their hands before eating.
