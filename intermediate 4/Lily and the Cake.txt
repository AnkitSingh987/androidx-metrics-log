Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Cake

Lily was eager to go to the party. She put on her pink dress and her shiny shoes. She combed her hair and tied a bow on it. She looked at herself in the mirror and smiled. "Mommy, can we go now?" she asked. "Yes, sweetie, we can go. But we have to hurry. The party starts soon and we don't want to be late," Mommy said. Lily and Mommy got in the car and drove to the park. Lily saw balloons and streamers and a big cake. She saw her friends and waved to them. "Hi, Lily! Welcome to the party!" they said. Lily ran to join them. She played games, sang songs, and ate cake. She had a lot of fun. She was happy to be at the party.

Once upon a time, there was a brave spider. His name was Sam. Sam lived near a big pond. One sunny day, Sam wanted to learn how to swim. He was very excited. Sam went to the pond and looked at the water. He saw fish and frogs swimming in the water. Sam wanted to swim like them. So, he put one leg in the water. It was cold! Then, Sam put all his legs in the water. He started to swim. He swam with the fish and the frogs. They all had fun together. Sam was very happy that he was brave enough to swim in the big pond. And they all played in the water until the sun went down.

One day, a pretty cat named Lily went for a walk. She saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Up, up, up she went. When she got to the top, she looked around and saw a bird. The bird said, "Hi, Lily!" Lily yawned and said, "Hi, bird!" The bird looked sad. "I lost my egg," the bird said. Lily wanted to help. "Let's find your egg!" she said. They looked and looked. They found the egg under a bush. The bird was so happy! But when they picked up the egg, it started to wiggle! The egg cracked open, and a little baby bird came out. The baby bird yawned and said, "Hi, Mom! Hi, Lily!" The bird and Lily were very surprised, but they were happy to have a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big motorcycle. It was red and shiny. A boy named Tim loved to sit on it. But one day, Tim felt uncomfortable on the motorcycle. He did not like it anymore. Tim wanted to remove the uncomfortable feeling. He thought and thought. Then, he had an idea. He would take off the seat of the motorcycle. He thought this would make it better. Tim took off the seat. But when he sat on the motorcycle again, it was still uncomfortable. He felt sad. The motorcycle was not fun anymore. Tim never sat on the motorcycle again. The end.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a very high cliff. An honest bird named Bluey lived on top of the cliff. Bluey had shiny blue feathers that would shine when the sun was up. He loved to sing and play with his friends. One day, Bluey met a new friend named Tiny. Tiny was a small, green bug. Bluey said, "Hi, I am Bluey. I live on the cliff. Do you want to play with me?" Tiny said, "Yes! I would love to play with you!" So, they played together all day long. As they played, the sun began to set. Suddenly, Bluey's shiny feathers started to glow in the dark! Tiny was scared at first, but then he saw that it was just Bluey's feathers. They both laughed and were happy. Now, Bluey and Tiny had a secret: Bluey's feathers could shine in the dark! They played together every day and had lots of fun with their new secret.

Once upon a time, there was an honest boy named Tim. Tim had a big green avocado. He loved his avocado very much. One day, Tim wanted to ride his bike. He took his avocado with him. Tim and his avocado went to the park. They had lots of fun. They played on the swings and in the sand. Tim rode his bike very fast. The avocado sat on the bike with him. They were very happy. But then, a big wind came. The wind blew Tim's avocado away. Tim tried to catch it, but he could not. The avocado was lost. Tim was very sad. He went home without his avocado. And that was the end of Tim's fun day with his avocado.
