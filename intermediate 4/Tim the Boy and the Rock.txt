Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his best friend, Sam. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. One day, they found a big, warm rock to sit on. They felt happy sitting on the warm rock. Tim had a big urge to share his toys with Sam. He ran home and got a red ball, a blue car, and a green dinosaur. Tim and Sam played with the toys together, sharing and taking turns. They had so much fun! As the sun began to set, Tim and Sam knew it was time to go home. They put the toys away and hugged each other. They promised to share more fun times together on the warm rock. And they did, every day, for a very long time.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went to the park. They both loved to play with a big, red ball. Tim would throw the ball very hard, and Spot would run fast to catch it. At the park, Tim saw a girl with a big bowl. Inside the bowl, there were many small, round things. Tim asked the girl, "What are those?" The girl said, "These are seeds. I love to plant them and watch them grow." Tim and the girl decided to play together. They took turns throwing the ball and planting seeds. Then, something unexpected happened. As they planted the seeds, the ground began to shake. Out of the ground, a huge tree grew right in front of them! The tree had big branches and lots of leaves. Tim, Spot, and the girl were very surprised. They all laughed and played around the tree. They climbed the branches and swung from the leaves. At the end of the day, Tim, Spot, and the girl became good friends. They learned that playing together and sharing their love for the ball and the seeds made the day even more fun.

One day, a funny dog named Max went to the park. At the park, he saw a game called golf. Max wanted to try it. Max went to his friend, a nice cat named Molly. He asked, "Molly, can you provide a golf stick for me?" Molly said, "Yes, Max! I have one at home. Let's go get it." Max and Molly went to Molly's house. Molly gave Max the golf stick. They went back to the park and played golf. They had a lot of fun and laughed a lot.

Once upon a time, a little boy saw something moving on the snow. He wondered what it was, so he guessed. He thought it looked like a hockey puck, but he wasn't sure. The little boy wanted to see what the thing was, so he slowly walked closer to it. When he finally got close enough, he saw it! It was a hockey puck! He was so excited because he had never seen one before. The little boy asked his dad if he could play with it, so his dad said yes. The little boy was delighted and tried playing hockey with it. Unfortunately, he wasn't very good so it became boring very quickly. He said to his dad, "This is too boring, let's do something else!" His dad replied, "Let's see, how about if we go for a walk instead?" The little boy thought that was a great idea, so they walked off into the snowy landscape. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play outside. Her favorite thing to do was to swing on her swing set. The swing set was a delicate thing, but it made Sue very happy. One day, Sue's friend, Tom, came over to play. They both went outside to play on the swing set. Sue said to Tom, "Be careful, the swing is a delicate thing." Tom nodded, and they both started to swing. As they were swinging, a big wind came and blew the delicate swing set over. Sue and Tom were scared, but they were okay. They learned that they should be more careful with delicate things. From that day on, they played more gently with the swing set, and it never fell over again.
