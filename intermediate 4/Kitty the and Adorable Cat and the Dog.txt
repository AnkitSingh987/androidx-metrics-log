Category: Intermediate
Title: Kitty the and Adorable Cat and the Dog

One day, a small and adorable cat named Kitty was playing near a fence. She saw a big dog on the other side. The dog looked at Kitty and said, "Hey, Kitty! Want to hear a joke?" Kitty was scared, but she wanted to hear the joke. She said, "Okay, tell me the joke." The dog laughed and said, "Why did the cat sit on the computer? Because it wanted to keep an eye on the mouse!" Kitty laughed too. She thought the dog was funny. They played and joked near the fence all day. Then, something unexpected happened. The dog found a hole under the fence! He went through the hole to Kitty's side. Kitty was scared at first, but the dog was nice. They became best friends and played together every day. They learned that even though they were different, they could still be friends and have fun.

Once upon a time, in a big maze, there lived a stubborn little mouse named Tim. Tim loved to scare his friends by jumping out from behind the walls. One day, Tim thought of a big plan to scare all his friends at once. He told his friends to meet him in the maze the next day. Tim was very excited. He spent all night setting up his big scare. He knew his friends would be so surprised. The next day, Tim's friends came to the maze. They walked and walked, but Tim was nowhere to be found. Then, all of a sudden, a big cat jumped out and scared Tim's friends! The cat was not part of Tim's plan. Tim jumped out from his hiding spot and scared the cat away. Tim and his friends were safe. From that day on, Tim learned not to be so stubborn and only scared his friends for fun, not with big plans.

One day, a girl named Sue went to the garden. She saw a big red radish. It was in the unknown part of the garden. Sue was very happy. She wanted to show her mom the big red radish. Sue ran to her mom and said, "Mom, look what I found in the garden!" Her mom looked at the radish and saw a sign. The sign said, "Do not eat the big red radish!" Sue and her mom did not know what to do. They did not want to eat the big red radish if the sign said not to. But then, a funny thing happened. The big red radish started to talk! "Hi, Sue and Mom! I am a magic radish! Thank you for not eating me!" said the big red radish. Sue and her mom were very surprised. They did not know that the radish could talk. They all became good friends and had fun in the garden.

Once upon a time, there was a pilot named Tim. Tim loved to fly his big plane in the sky. One day, Tim found it difficult to get his plane to go up. He was sad. Tim asked his friend, Sam, for help. Sam said, "Let's try together!" They both tried to get the plane to fly, but it was still difficult. They did not give up. Finally, they found a big, strong bird who helped them. The bird pushed the plane into the sky. Tim and Sam were so happy. They said, "Thank you, bird!" And they all flew together in the big, blue sky.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved to explore. One day, she was walking in the woods when she found something mysterious. She saw a mirror lying on the ground with a bright light shining from it. She picked it up and stared into it. She discovered that the mirror had a secret power. Whenever she said "Zoom!", the mirror would start to move. She felt so excited that she couldn't contain her happiness. She ran outside and started shouting "Zoom!" The little girl's friends heard her and ran outside to see what was happening. As soon as they saw the mirror, they were fascinated. The little girl giggled and said, "Come on, let's zoom together!" The little girl's friends joined her as they all shouted "Zoom! Zoom!" and watched in wonder as the mirror seemed to come alive and spin around the room. After a while, the fun had to come to an end. The little girl put the mysterious mirror back to where she found it, with a smile on her face. She knew she had a secret power and her friends were amazed. From that day on, the little girl's friends always asked her to bring the mirror with her whenever they went on an adventure.
