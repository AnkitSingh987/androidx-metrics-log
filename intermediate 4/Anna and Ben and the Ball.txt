Title: Anna and Ben and the Ball

Anna and Ben were playing in the park with their ball. They had a lot of fun, kicking and throwing the ball to each other. But then, the sky became dark and it started to rain. The rain was cold and wet, and they had no umbrella. "Let's go home, Anna!" Ben said. "We will get sick if we stay here!" But Anna did not want to go home. She wanted to keep playing with the ball. She loved the ball very much. It was her birthday present from her grandma, and it was very important to her. "No, Ben!" Anna said. "I want to play with the ball! It is my ball, and I don't want to leave it!" Ben tried to take the ball from Anna, but she held it tight. They pulled and pushed, and the ball slipped from their hands. It rolled away, and a big dog ran after it. The dog caught the ball in its mouth, and ran away with it. Anna and Ben watched the dog disappear with their ball. They felt very sad and angry. They started to weep. "Anna, I'm sorry!" Ben said. "I didn't mean to lose your ball!" "It's okay, Ben!" Anna said. "I'm sorry, too! I should have listened to you!" They hugged each other, and walked home in the rain. They hoped that the dog would be nice to their ball, and that they would find another one to play with. They learned that playing together was more important than playing with a ball.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved to dance. Every day, she would practice her moves in her favorite place - the garden. One day, her mum asked her to perform a dance for her. The little girl was very excited. She decided to make up a special dance for her mum, and practices for hours. On the day of the performance, the little girl was dressed in a sparkly outfit and was ready to take the stage. She performed one line of steps with grace and elegance. Every move was perfect. Her mum clapped her hands and said, "That was really great! You are the perfect performer." The little girl beamed and thanked her mum with a big hug. From that day onwards, she never forgot the importance of practice and perfecting her performance.

Once upon a time, there was a good cat named Jazz. Jazz liked to play and have fun. One day, Jazz saw a big dog in the park. The big dog looked mad. Jazz said to the big dog, "Why are you mad? Let's be friends." The big dog growled and said, "No, I don't want to be friends. I am mad because I lost my toy." Jazz wanted to help the big dog. They looked for the toy together. Jazz found the toy under a tree. The big dog was very happy. He said, "Thank you, Jazz. You are a good friend." Jazz and the big dog played together in the park. They had so much fun. Before they went home, the big dog gave Jazz a big kiss. The kiss made Jazz feel happy. The moral of the story is that being kind and helpful can make new friends and bring happiness.

One day, a mom and her little boy went to the grocery store. They were glad because they needed food for their home. The mom pushed the cart and the little boy sat in it. They looked at all the food in the store. The mom said, "We need to separate the fruits and vegetables from the other things we buy." The little boy nodded and helped his mom put the fruits in one part of the cart and the vegetables in another part. When they were done shopping, they went to the car. The little boy said, "I'm glad we separated the fruits and vegetables, mom!" The mom smiled and said, "Me too, now let's go home and make a yummy dinner!" And they drove home, excited to eat the food they bought at the grocery store.

One day, a little girl named Lucy was very excited. She was going on a holiday with her family. She began to wonder what fun things they would do together. Lucy wanted to play on the beach, swim in the sea, and eat ice cream. On the holiday, Lucy met a new friend named Tom. Tom was a naughty boy who liked to play tricks on people. He would hide their toys and run away laughing. Lucy did not like this, but she still wanted to be friends with Tom. One day, Lucy said to Tom, "We should not be naughty. We should be nice to everyone and play together." Tom thought about it and agreed. They played together and had lots of fun on their holiday. In the end, Tom learned to be nice and they both had a great time.
