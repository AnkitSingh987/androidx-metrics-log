Category: Intermediate
Title: Delilah the Girl and the Treasure

Once upon a time there was a brave girl named Delilah. Delilah was walking in the dark forest, and it scared her. She started to tremble. But she was very patient and determined. On and on she went, until she came to a big, dark cave. Her heart was pounding and she was beginning to tremble more. But she went in anyway. She looked around and found what she was looking for – a big, golden pot filled with treasure! Delilah was so happy and excited. She took the pot and left the cave. But as she was walking home, the pot started to tremble. She wondered why, and then she heard a voice! It was a big dragon, who wanted his treasure back. Delilah was scared and trembling, but she was very patient. So she offered a compromise. She gave the dragon some of the treasure – and kept the rest for herself! The dragon was happy, and so was Delilah. She skipped back home with her treasure, smiling all the way.

One day, a fast car went speed down the road. The car liked to go fast and feel the wind. The car was very excited. Inside the car, there was a girl named Lily and her mom. They were going to the park to play. At the park, Lily met a boy named Tom. They played with a ball. They threw the ball to each other. Lily said, "I like the rhythm of our play!" Tom said, "Me too! It's fun!" Suddenly, the ball went into the woods. Lily and Tom were scared, but they went to find the ball. They found a big, friendly dog with the ball in its mouth. The dog wanted to play too. Now, Lily, Tom, and the dog played with the ball. They were all excited and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a rich man who had a big farm. He had many animals and a long row of fruit trees. One day, he saw a little boy walk near his farm. The little boy saw the rich man and said, "Hello, can I have some fruit, please?" The rich man did not want to share. He said, "No, you cannot have any fruit!" The little boy was sad, but he saw a bird in the row of trees. The bird grabbed a fruit and gave it to the boy. The boy said, "Thank you, bird!" The bird said, "You're welcome. Sharing is good." The rich man saw this and felt bad. He said, "I am sorry, little boy. You can have some fruit too." The boy and the rich man became friends and shared the fruit with everyone. The moral of the story is to always share what you have with others, because sharing is caring.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to eat soup. One day, her mom made a big pot of soup for dinner. Lily was very excited. Before dinner, Lily asked her mom, "Can I have some soup now?" Her mom said, "No, you must wait for dinner time." Lily was sad but she knew she had to listen to her mom. She did not want to be spoiled. When dinner time came, Lily and her family sat at the table. They all ate the soup together. It was so yummy! Lily was happy that she waited for dinner time. Her mom was proud of her for not being spoiled. And from that day on, Lily always waited for the right time to eat her soup.

One day, a bald bird named Bob was sad. He could not find his nest. He looked up, down, left, and right. He missed his nest very much. Bob asked his friend, "Do you see my nest?" His friend said, "No, I do not see your nest, Bob. Let's look together." They looked in the trees and on the ground. They could not find the nest. Bob was very sad. Then, a big wind came. It blew the trees and the leaves. Bob and his friend tried to hold on, but the wind was too strong. The wind blew Bob far away. Now, Bob was lost and could not find his nest or his friend. The wind had taken everything away from him.
