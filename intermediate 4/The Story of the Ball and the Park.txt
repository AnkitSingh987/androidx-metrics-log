Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Ball and the Park

Once, there was a little boy who was three years old. He was a very curious boy and was always looking for something new. One day, his mum took him to the park. It was a very interesting place. He saw so many things, like trees and birds, that it made him so excited! When his mum said it was time to go, the little boy found something very special. It was a rubber ball! He really liked it and wanted to keep it. So his mum said he could take it home with him. When he got home, he showed his rubber ball to his dad. His dad said it was a good ball and recommended that he play with it outside. The little boy was so happy! He was so pleased that his dad recommended it. He ran outside right away and played with his new ball!

Sam and his mum were in the kitchen. His mummy opened the refrigerator and Sam saw something red inside. Sam shouted: "Mummy, I want it!" Mummy closed the refrigerator door and said: "No Sam. You can't have it". Sam began to cry and demanded: "Mummy, I want it! I want the red thing in the refrigerator". Mummy said: "That's a tomato, Sam. We don't eat tomatoes like that". So Sam looked up at his mummy and demanded: "Can I please have it?" Mummy smiled and said: "Okay, you can have it". Sam was very happy and gave his mummy a big hug. Then he reached into the refrigerator and took out the red tomato. He bit into it and said: "Mummy, this tomato tastes yummy!"

---

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play in the park. He saw his friend, Sue, playing with a ball. Tim wanted to play with Sue, so he asked her, "Can I play with you?" Sue said, "Yes! Let's play catch!" They took turns throwing and catching the ball. Tim threw the ball high in the air. Sue tried to catch it, but the ball went past her. It rolled into a dark part of the park. Sue felt scared to go there. She looked at Tim and said, "I am scared. Can you help me get the ball?" Tim saw that Sue was scared, so he held her hand. They went to the dark part of the park together. They found the ball and picked it up. Tim and Sue were not scared anymore. They went back to the sunny part of the park and played catch all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little seal who loved to swim in the big, open sea. The seal would play with his friends, the fish, all day long. They would swim, jump, and splash in the water. The sun would shine on them, and they were very happy. One day, while the seal was playing with his fish friends, he saw a big boat coming. The boat was making a lot of noise and splashing water everywhere. The seal and his friends got scared and wanted the boat to stop. They didn't know what to do. The little seal had an idea. He swam up to the boat and jumped high in the air, making a big splash. The people on the boat saw the seal and stopped the boat. They laughed and clapped for the seal's big jump. The seal and his fish friends were happy that the boat stopped. They went back to playing in the open sea, and the boat went away. The little seal and his friends were safe and happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue liked to sort things. She had a big yard with lots of toys. One day, she found a fancy box in her yard. She was very happy. Sue took the fancy box to her mom. Her mom said, "Be careful, Sue. This box may have a surprise." Sue was excited to find out what was inside the box. She put the box in her room and went to play outside. Later, Sue went back to her room to open the fancy box. She remembered what her mom said about a surprise. When she opened the box, she found many colorful balls. Sue was so happy! She took the balls outside and sorted them by color. She knew her mom was right about the surprise. Sue played with her new colorful balls all day long in her yard.
