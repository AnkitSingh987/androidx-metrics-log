Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Suit

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to improve his look. He wanted a nice suit. So, he went to the store with his mom. At the store, Tim found a regular suit. It was just right for him. He tried it on and said, "Mom, look at me! I like this suit." His mom smiled and said, "You look great, Tim." But then, something unexpected happened. A dog ran into the store and took Tim's suit! Tim and his mom chased the dog. The dog stopped and gave the suit back. Tim laughed and said, "Silly dog, this suit is for me!"

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to speed on her bike. One day, she saw a big bag of sugar in her kitchen. She wanted to eat it all by herself because she was a little selfish. Amy's mom said, "No, Amy, you must share the sugar with your brother and sister." Amy didn't like this idea. So, she took the bag of sugar and hid it under her bed. Later, Amy's brother and sister found the sugar. They said, "Amy, why did you hide the sugar? We should share it!" Amy felt bad for being selfish. She agreed to share the sugar with them. Together, they made yummy cookies with the sugar. They all had fun and shared the cookies with their friends. From that day on, Amy learned that sharing is better than being selfish.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who was very modest. She didn't like to draw a lot of attention to herself and she always tried to be helpful. One day, she went to the market with her mom. "Do you want oats, my dear?" her mom asked her. The little girl shook her head and said, "No, I hate oats." But then she noticed something else. "Look mom, what is that?" she asked, pointing at a treat that looked like oats but with a sweet flavor. Her mom smiled and said, "Those are oat cookies, honey. Would you like to try one?" The little girl beamed and said, "Yes, please!" She quickly devoured the cookies and declared, "I don't hate oats anymore!" From that day on, she learned to be modest but was always willing to try new things.

Once upon a time, in a lively little house, there was a girl named Mia. She loved to play with her toy dishes. One day, she decided to have a tea party with her stuffed animals. As she set up the tea party, she placed a dish on the table for each of her stuffed friends. She filled the cups with pretend tea and gave each of them a cookie. Mia said, "Here you go, Mr. Bunny! Have a cookie from this dish." Suddenly, her little brother, Max, came running into the room. He was so excited to play, he didn't see the tea party on the floor. He tripped and all the dishes went flying! Mia was sad and said, "Oh no, Max! You scatter my dishes!" Max felt bad for what he did. He helped Mia pick up the dishes and put them back on the table. Together, they set up the tea party again. Mia invited Max to join the party, and they both had a great time with their stuffed friends.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a girl named Lucy. Lucy loved to help her mom with her garden. One day, Lucy found a patch of sour berries. She thought they would be perfect to share with her friends. Lucy picked the berries and put them in a basket. She went to her friend Tom's house and said, "Tom, I found a patch of sour berries. Do you want to try some?" Tom was excited and tried one. "Yuck!" he said, "These are too sour!" Lucy felt sad, but she had an idea. She went back to the garden and found some sweet berries. She attached a note to the basket that said, "Sweet berries for my friends." Lucy shared the sweet berries with Tom and her other friends. Everyone loved the sweet berries, and they all had a great time. The moral of the story is that it's important to think about others' feelings and share things that make them happy.
