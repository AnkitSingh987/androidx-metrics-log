Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a big dream. She wanted to fly like a bird. Sue told her friend, Tom, about her dream. Tom said, "I believe you can fly!" One day, Sue and Tom found a bird with a hurt wing. They wanted to help the bird. They took the bird to their friend, Jane. Jane was a bit rude, but she knew a lot about animals. Jane said, "I can help the bird, but you must be very quiet." Sue, Tom, and Jane were very quiet as they helped the bird. They gave the bird some food and water. Then, something unexpected happened. The bird started to talk! The bird said, "Thank you for helping me. I am a magic bird. Because you were kind, I will make your dream come true." The magic bird touched Sue with its wing. Sue felt a tickle on her back. She looked and saw that she had wings! Sue could now fly like a bird. She was so happy. Sue, Tom, and Jane played with the magic bird all day. They learned that being kind is better than being rude.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to eat. Every day, she would eat her favorite food, which was cake. Lucy's mom would always make her a cake, because she was very reliable. One day, Lucy's mom made a cake that looked like a waist. Lucy was very excited to eat the waist cake. She took a big bite and smiled. It was the best cake she had ever eaten. Just as Lucy was about to take another bite, something unexpected happened. The waist cake started to talk! It said, "Please don't eat me, Lucy! I am a magic cake and I can make your wishes come true!" Lucy was very surprised, but she decided not to eat the waist cake. Instead, she wished for more cake and shared it with all her friends.

Once upon a time there was a grumpy cat. Every day, the cat liked to peek around the corner in the garden to have a wonder. One day, the cat peeked and peeked and saw something very special. It was a big, yellow bird! The cat was so surprised and started to purr. The bird was also very surprised to see the grumpy cat. The bird then said, "Hello! Let's be friends." The cat was excited and ran up to the bird to give it a big hug. The cat and the bird played together all day, exploring the garden and having lots of fun. At the end of the day, the cat and the bird were tired. They said goodbye and the cat went home with a smile on its face. It was no longer grumpy! From then on, the cat liked to peek around the corner to see if its friend the bird was around.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a big tree with a swing. Lily went on the swing and started to go back and forth. She felt dizzy from swinging so much, but she was happy. A little boy named Tom came to the tree. He asked Lily, "Can I swing too?" Lily said, "Yes, but let me get off first." She stopped the swing and got off. Tom got on the swing and started to go back and forth, just like Lily. Suddenly, a bird flew down from the tree and landed on the swing. The bird looked at Tom and Lily and did a little bow. Tom and Lily were surprised, but they laughed. The bird flew away, and they kept playing on the swing. They had a fun day, and the bird was their new friend.

One day, a little girl named Amy found a big sheet of paper. She wanted to make a brilliant picture for her mom. So, she took her crayons and started to draw. She drew a big sun, a house, and a happy family. Amy's brother, Ben, came into the room and saw her drawing. "What are you doing?" he asked. "I'm making a picture for Mom," Amy said with a big smile. Ben wanted to help, so he took a measure and started to tear the paper into small pieces. When they were done, Amy and Ben had lots of small pieces of paper. They put them all together to make a big heart. They glued the pieces onto another sheet of paper. The heart was full of colors and looked very pretty. Amy and Ben showed their mom the brilliant heart they made. Their mom was so happy and proud of them. She hung the heart on the wall for everyone to see. Amy and Ben knew they had made the best gift for their mom.
