Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Cake

One day, a girl named Amy wanted to bake a cake. She was a little worried because she had never baked a cake before. She asked her mom, "Can you help me bake a cake?" Her mom said, "Yes, of course!" They put on their aprons and started to bake. They mixed the cake mix, eggs, and milk in a big bowl. Then they put the mix in a cake pan and put it in the oven. While they waited for the cake to bake, Amy drew a picture of the cake. When the timer went off, they opened the oven and saw a big surprise. The cake had turned into a big, friendly bear! The bear said, "Thank you for baking me. I was stuck in the cake mix and you set me free!" Amy and her mom were very happy and they all became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toys and jump on his bed. One day, he found a big, soft pillow. It was the best pillow ever! Tim was very happy and played with the pillow all day long. One day, Tim could not remember where he put his pillow. He looked under his bed, in the toy box, and behind the door. But he could not find it. Tim was very sad and missed his pillow so much. Then, something unexpected happened. Tim's dog, Spot, came into the room with the pillow in his mouth! Spot had taken the pillow to play with it too. Tim was so happy to see his pillow again. He hugged Spot and they played with the pillow together. From that day on, Tim and Spot shared the pillow and had lots of fun.

John was walking in the forest. Suddenly, he heard someone yelling. He followed the sound until he reached a clearing. In the clearing was a mule. John tried to get closer, but the mule just kept yelling. John asked the mule why it was yelling. The mule said, “I'm stuck, and I don't know how to get free!” John thought for a moment and then he shouted, “I have an idea!” He grabbed some branches and pieces of rope, and he tied them together. Then, he wrapped it around the mule. Slowly, the rope tightened and the mule was free! The mule thanked John for his help. He said, “You saved me! You are one very clever boy!” John smiled and then he ran off, happy that he had helped the mule.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby rat named Ratty. He lived in a tiny house near a big tree. One day, a big cat appeared near Ratty's house. The cat wanted to catch Ratty and eat him. Ratty was scared, and he did not know what to do. Ratty had a friend, a little bird named Birdy. Ratty asked Birdy, "Can you help me? The big cat wants to eat me!" Birdy said, "Don't worry, Ratty. I will help you." Birdy thought of a plan to save Ratty from the big cat. Birdy went to the big cat and said, "Hello, big cat! Ratty is my friend. Please don't eat him." The big cat was not happy, but he listened to Birdy. Birdy told the big cat, "There is a big cake behind the tree. You can eat that instead." The big cat went to the tree and saw the big cake. He ate the cake and left Ratty alone. Ratty and Birdy were happy that the big cat did not eat Ratty. They played together near the tree and had lots of fun. From that day on, Ratty and Birdy were the best of friends, and they helped each other when they needed it.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with clay. She made many fun things with it, like animals and houses. The clay was flexible, so she could bend and twist it any way she wanted. One day, Sue was sad because her clay got lost. She looked everywhere for it but could not find it. So, she decided to pray. She closed her eyes and asked for help to find her clay. The next day, Sue found her clay under her bed. She was so happy! She prayed again to say thank you. From that day on, Sue always knew that praying could help her when she was sad or needed help.
