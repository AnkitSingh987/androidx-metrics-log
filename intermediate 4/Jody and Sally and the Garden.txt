Category: Intermediate
Title: Jody and Sally and the Garden

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Jody and Sally. They were playing together in the garden. Jody said to Sally, "Let's race each other around the garden! Who can do it first?" Sally said, "Yes, that sounds fun!" So they ran as fast as they could around the garden. They laughed and joked as they raced, and soon they had made it all the way around. When they stopped, Jody looked around and said, "This garden is so beautiful! Look at all the stuff there is to do here!" Sally smiled and said, "Yes, it's really nice here. Let's do it again!" So the two friends raced around the garden again, laughing and having fun. They had a wonderful time and even when it was time for them to go home, they couldn't help but smile and giggle as they ran one last time around the garden.

Once, there was a girl named Lily. She had a flashlight she loved very much. Lily took her flashlight everywhere she went. The flashlight was fragile, so she was very careful with it. One day, Lily was playing with her friend, Tim. They were playing tag. Lily was running with her flashlight. Tim was trying to catch her. Suddenly, a big wind came and blew her flashlight away. Lily was sad and scared. Lily and Tim looked for the flashlight. They found it near a big tree. The flashlight was not broken. Lily was happy. She picked it up and gave the tree a little slap. The tree started to shake. Many flashlights fell from the tree! Lily and Tim were very surprised. Now, they had many flashlights to play with. They played with their new flashlights all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little dog. The dog was very tired and wanted to take a nap. The house was old and looked terrible, but the dog didn't care. One day, while the dog was napping, a big wind came. The wind blew and blew, making the house shake. The dog woke up and felt scared. He tried to hide under his bed, but the wind was too strong. The terrible wind blew the house down. The little dog was sad and cold. He had no home now. The dog walked away, looking for a new house to nap in. But he could not find one. The little dog was cold and alone, and the wind kept blowing.

One day, a little bug named Tim found a magic rock. The rock could glow in the dark. Tim was very happy. He showed the glowing rock to all his bug friends. They thought it was the best thing they had ever seen. One of Tim's friends, a funny bug named Joe, liked to tell jokes. Joe told a joke to Tim and his friends. They all laughed and laughed. Joe felt envious of Tim's glowing rock. He wished he had a magic rock too. One night, Tim and Joe went for a walk with the glowing rock. They met a wise old bug. The wise bug said, "You can both be happy. Tim, share your glowing rock with Joe. Joe, share your jokes with Tim." They agreed, and from that day on, Tim and Joe were the best of friends. They shared the glowing rock and the jokes, and all the bugs in the forest were happy.

One evening, a little girl named Lily was playing with her toys. She was feeling impatient, as she hoped her mom would come home soon. Lily missed her mom a lot and wanted to give her a big hug. Just then, Lily heard a knock on the door. She ran to open it, hoping it was her mom. But it was not her mom, it was a big, friendly dog. The dog said, "Hi, I am Sam. I am lost. Can you help me find my home?" Lily was sad that it was not her mom, but she wanted to help Sam. They walked around the neighborhood, asking people if they knew where Sam lived. No one knew, and Lily started to feel sad. But then, something unexpected happened. As they turned the corner, they saw Lily's mom walking towards them. Lily was so happy to see her mom, and she ran to give her a hug. Her mom said, "I know where Sam lives! He lives right next door to us." Lily and her mom took Sam home, and they all became good friends.
