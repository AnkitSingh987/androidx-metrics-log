Category: Intermediate
Title: The Great Race and the Turtle

Once upon a time, there was a great race. Many animals wanted to win the race and feel victory. The fast rabbit and the slow turtle were in the race too. Before the race, the rabbit said, "I will win for sure!" The turtle felt sad but did not give up. The rabbit saw the turtle and decided to take a nap. He thought, "I can still win when I wake up." The turtle saw the rabbit sleep and knew this was his chance. The turtle walked and walked. He did not stop. The rabbit woke up and saw the turtle far away. He started to run very fast but could not catch up. The turtle crossed the finish line first and felt victory. The rabbit learned not to be too sure of himself. All the animals saw the turtle win and they all did a happy shake.

Once there was a mom and her daughter. They lived in a small cheap house. The mom loved her daughter very much. They were happy together. One day, the daughter was very tired. She wanted to sleep. The mom said, "Okay, you can sleep now." The daughter went to her bed and closed her eyes. She started to sleep. While the daughter was sleeping, a big storm came. The wind blew hard and the rain fell down. The cheap house was not strong. It fell down on the mom and daughter. They did not wake up. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little insect named Izzy. She was a fancy insect with a big hat and a pretty dress. Izzy lived in a tiny house in a big garden. She had many friends, but she was too scared to play with them. One day, Izzy's friend, a big dog named Danny, came to visit her. "Izzy, why don't you dare to come out and play with us?" Danny asked. "I am too scared," Izzy said. "I am just a small insect, and I don't want to get hurt." Danny thought for a moment and said, "I have an idea! Tomorrow, I will bring you a special gift that will help you be brave." The next day, Danny came back with a gift for Izzy. It was a small, shiny stone. "This is a brave stone," Danny said. "When you hold it, you will feel brave and dare to do anything." Izzy held the stone and felt brave right away. She dared to go out and play with her friends. They all had a great time, and Izzy was not scared anymore. From that day on, Izzy always carried her brave stone with her. She dared to do many things, like climbing trees and jumping in puddles. Izzy and her friends had lots of fun adventures together. And even though she was a fancy insect, Izzy learned that being brave was the most important thing of all.

Lily and Sam are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a big slide. They want to go on the slide. "Can we go on the slide, please?" Lily asks her mom. "Okay, but be careful. It is very high," her mom says. Lily and Sam run to the slide. They take turns to go down. They have fun. They laugh and shout. "Look at me, Sam!" Lily says. She goes down the slide very fast. She waves her arms in the air. "Wow, Lily, you are so brave!" Sam says. He goes down the slide too. He waves his hands to his dad. "Good job, Sam!" his dad says. He waves back to him. Lily and Sam go on the slide many times. They count how many times they go. They learn new numbers. "One, two, three, four, five!" Lily says. "Six, seven, eight, nine, ten!" Sam says. They are very happy. They love the slide. They love to wave. They love to learn.

Once upon a time, there was a charming cat named Kitty. Kitty was very good at helping people. One day, Kitty went on a walk to find someone to help. Kitty saw a little boy crying. The boy had a wet shirt. Kitty wanted to help, so she tried to dry the shirt. Kitty used her soft fur to rub the shirt until it was dry. The boy was happy, and they played together all day. But then, they saw some kids playing war with toy soldiers. Kitty and the boy wanted to play too. They joined the game, but Kitty had an idea. Instead of fighting, they made the soldiers dance and play. The kids were surprised, but they laughed and had more fun. In the end, everyone learned that playing together was better than fighting.
