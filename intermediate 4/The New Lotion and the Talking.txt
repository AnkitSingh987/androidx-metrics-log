Category: Intermediate
Title: The New Lotion and the Talking

Once upon a time, there was a new lotion. This lotion could talk! It lived in a big store with many other lotions. But the new lotion was sad because no one wanted to buy it. They all went for the other lotions that did not talk. One day, a little girl came into the store. She saw the talking lotion and thought it was very fun. She asked her mom if she could buy it. Her mom said yes. The talking lotion was so happy! It was finally going to have a new home. At the little girl's house, the talking lotion helped her learn how to share. When her friends came to play, they all wanted to use the talking lotion. The little girl shared the lotion with her friends, and they all had a great time. They learned that sharing can make everyone happy. The talking lotion was not sad anymore, and the little girl had lots of new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big ship. The ship was blue and had a lot of toys. A boy named Tim was on the ship. Tim was impatient. He wanted to play with his new toy car. Tim said to his mom, "Mom, can I play with my car now?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim. Let's find a good spot to play." They walked around the ship and found a nice place to play. Tim was happy. Tim kneeled down to play with his car. He made the car go fast and slow. He made the car go up and down. Tim had a lot of fun playing with his car on the big blue ship.

Sara and Tom were best friends. They liked to play in the park, ride bikes and make pretend food. One day, they found a big grill in the trash. It was old and dirty, but they thought it was fun. "Let's play marry," Sara said. "You can be the groom and I can be the bride. We can use the grill as a cake." "OK," Tom said. "But we need rings and flowers and guests." They looked around and found some shiny rocks, some daisies and some stuffed animals. They put the rocks on their fingers, the daisies in their hair and the animals on a bench. They were ready to marry. Sara and Tom stood in front of the grill and held hands. They said their vows, which were things like "I like you" and "You are nice" and "We will share our toys". Then they kissed and cut the grill cake with a plastic knife. They gave some to their guests and ate some themselves. It tasted like metal and dirt, but they pretended it was yummy. They were very happy and calm. They did not know that the grill was not really a grill, but a bomb. Someone had thrown it away because it was broken and dangerous. It had a timer that was counting down to zero. When it reached zero, it would make a big boom and a lot of fire. But Sara and Tom did not hear the timer. They only heard each other and their guests. They did not see the red light on the grill. They only saw the shiny rocks and the daisies. They did not feel the heat from the grill. They only felt their love. They played marry until the sun went down. Then they said goodbye and went home. They did not know that it was their last day together. They did not know that the grill was about to explode. They did not know that their happy and calm game would end in a sad and scary way.

One night, a little boy was looking out the window of his room. He saw something glowing in the garden. He wondered what it was and became excited. He quickly ran outside to find out. He saw a newspaper glowing in the dark. The newspaper was old and dirty. The little boy had never seen it before. He touched the paper and it felt warm. He wanted to pick it up and keep it. He carefully folded it and put it in his pocket. The next day, the little boy was happily playing with his new discovery. He read the paper and showed it to his mother. She laughed at his excitement and said the paper must have been magical. He said it was his treasure, and he would never part with it.
