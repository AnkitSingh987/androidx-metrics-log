Category: Intermediate
Title: The Mouse and the Woods

Once upon a time, there was a kind mouse. He had a long, fluffy tail. Everyone in the village admired the tail. Every day, the kind mouse went to the meadow to play. But one day something terrible happened, the mouse lost its tail. The mouse was so sad and scared. He looked for his tail, but could not find it. Then he decided to ask all the animals in the woods if they had seen it. But no one had seen it before. Soon the mouse realised he wouldn’t find his tail ever again. So he sadly looked at his short tail. He wished he had been more careful with his tail, but it was too late now. He had lost his beautiful tail forever.

"Come on, Freddie!" said Grandma, as she and Freddie went outside with their jugs. Each jug was red. Freddie thought that was nice. They went to the river and stopped. Grandma said, "Let's shoot the jugs across the river!" Freddie said, "Yes!" Grandma showed Freddie how to hold the jug. She said, "Hold it like this!" Freddie copied her and held it just the same. Grandma said, "Now, take aim and shoot it!" Freddie pointed and shot the jug across the river. It flew in a regular pattern and landed on the other side. Grandma smiled and said, "Good shot, Freddie!" Freddie smiled back. He said, "My shot was fun!"

One day, a little girl named Miranda went out for a walk with her dad. She was wearing her bright yellow coat that she loved so much. As daddy walked, he warned Miranda to stay close and be alert. "There are lots of strangers outside," he said, "and we don't want to get lost!" Miranda nodded as she looked around. Everywhere she looked, she saw things that were new and exciting. The sun shone down as they reached the end of the street. Daddy warned Miranda to stay close to him while they crossed the street. "You need to be careful here," he said. Miranda kept one eye on Daddy and the other on the cars. She stayed alert so she could cross the street safely. When they got to the other side, Miranda felt proud of herself. Daddy gave her a big smile and told her how brave she was. Miranda smiled back and hugged her coat tightly as she enjoyed the day with her dad.

Sara and Tom were twins who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big pile of leaves and ran to jump on it. They had a lot of fun throwing leaves in the air and hiding under them. But then, Sara saw something shiny in the leaves. She picked it up and saw that it was a necklace with a pretty stone. She was very happy and put it on her neck. She said to Tom, "Look what I found! It is mine now!" Tom was not happy. He wanted the necklace too. He said to Sara, "No, it is not yours! It is mine! You have to share!" He tried to grab the necklace from Sara, but she pulled it away. They started to fight and yell. A lady who was walking by heard them and came to see what was wrong. She saw the necklace and said, "Oh, that is my necklace! I lost it yesterday when I was playing with my dog in the park. Thank you for finding it!" Sara and Tom felt ashamed. They had been fighting over something that was not theirs. They said sorry to the lady and to each other. They gave the necklace back to the lady, who smiled and thanked them again. She said, "You know, it is better to be kind and generous than to be greedy and selfish. Sharing is caring!" Sara and Tom learned their lesson. They decided to play together and share their toys. They felt happier and had more fun. They discovered that being nice to others makes them nice to you.

One day, a creative girl named Sue saw a bird. The bird was sad. Sue wanted to help the bird. She had an idea to make the bird happy. Sue had a toy car. She thought to lend the toy car to the bird. The bird liked the toy car. The bird played with the toy car. Sue and the bird were happy. But then, the bird went too fast. The bird did not see a big rock. The bird hit the rock with the toy car. The bird got hurt. Sue was sad. The bird was sad. The toy car was broken. The end.
