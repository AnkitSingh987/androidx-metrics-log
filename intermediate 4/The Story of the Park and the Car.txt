Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Park and the Car

One day, a three year old was walking through the park. He was observing the beautiful flowers and the birds in the sky. Suddenly, he noticed something strange in the far distance. It was a bright, flashing light! He watched carefully, and soon he could see a police car driving closer and closer to the park. As the police car approached, the little boy's curiosity grew. He noticed that the police were wearing thick jackets and hats. He wondeered why they needed such thick coats in the park. He continued to observe the police as they stopped their car and got out. He was excited to find out why they had stopped! Unfortunately, the little boy never found out, as he had to run home for dinner.

One day, a little boy named Tim had a big box of toys. He wanted to sort his toys into two groups. He had his expensive toys and his cheap toys. Tim loved his expensive toys more because they were special. Tim found a hammer in the box. He thought it was an expensive toy because it looked very nice. He put the hammer with the expensive toys. But he didn't know that the hammer was not a toy. It was a real hammer that his dad had left in the box by mistake. One day, Tim wanted to play with his expensive toys. He picked up the hammer and hit his other toys with it. The toys broke! Tim was very sad. He didn't know that the hammer was not a toy. His dad came and saw the broken toys. He told Tim that the hammer was not a toy and should not be in the toy box. Tim learned to be more careful when sorting his toys.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a tired boy. He yawned a lot. One day, Tim put on his red vest and went outside to play. Tim saw a big tree. He wanted to climb it. Tim felt less tired and started to climb. He climbed and climbed. When he was high up, he looked down and felt scared. Tim was too tired to climb down. He yawned and let go of the tree. Tim fell down and hurt himself. His red vest got dirty. Tim was sad and went home. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big, clumsy dog named Bobo. Bobo loved to play and run around the yard. One day, he saw a small toy car. It was red and shiny. Bobo wanted to drive the toy car, but he was too big. Bobo had a friend named Timmy. Timmy was a little boy with big ears. Timmy loved to play with Bobo. They played catch and hide and seek. Timmy saw Bobo looking at the toy car. He wanted to help his friend. Timmy had an idea. He put the toy car on Bobo's back. Now, Bobo could drive the car while he ran around the yard. Bobo was so happy! They played all day with the toy car. The car fell off Bobo's back many times, but Timmy always put it back. One day, while they were playing, the toy car fell into a hole. Bobo and Timmy were sad. They thought the car was lost forever. But then, Timmy used his big ears to listen. He heard the car making a noise from the hole. Timmy reached into the hole with his hand and pulled out the toy car. Bobo and Timmy were so happy! They learned that being different, like having big ears or being clumsy, can be a good thing. They played together and had fun every day, and they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and her dog, Max. Lucy loved to play outside with Max. One day, they went to the park to play. At the park, Lucy saw a post with lots of papers on it. She took the papers and began to scatter them all around. Max barked and wagged his tail. "Look, Max! We made a big mess!" Lucy said, laughing. Max barked again, happy to see Lucy having fun. Just then, Lucy's mom came over and saw the empty post. "Lucy, did you scatter all the papers?" her mom asked. Lucy looked at the ground and said, "Yes, Mommy. I'm sorry." Her mom smiled and said, "That's okay. Let's pick them up and put them back on the post." Lucy and Max helped clean up the mess, and they all went home for dinner.
