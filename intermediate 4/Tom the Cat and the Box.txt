Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Cat and the Box

One day, a funny cat named Tom went for a walk. He liked to know what was happening in his town. He saw his friends playing and having fun. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. As Tom walked, he heard a strange noise. He did not know what it was. He looked around and saw a big box. The noise was coming from the box. Tom was curious and wanted to know what was inside. Tom opened the box and found a small dog. The dog was making the noise. Tom and the dog became friends. They played and had fun together. Now, Tom knew what the noise was and had a new friend to play with.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big dream. He wanted to do something impressive on Sunday. He thought and thought about what he could do. On Sunday, Tim woke up early with a big smile. He decided to make a tall tower with his blocks. He worked very hard and built it higher and higher. It was very impressive, and he was proud of his work. But then, Tim's little sister came into the room. She did not see the impressive tower. She bumped into it, and the tower fell down. Tim was sad and cried. His dream of having an impressive tower on Sunday was gone.

Once upon a time, there was a cat named Dare. Dare was a dependable cat who lived in a small house with a big chimney. Dare had a friend named Birdy. They liked to play together. One day, Dare said to Birdy, "Let's play hide and seek!" Birdy agreed and started to count. Dare ran and hid behind the chimney. Birdy looked and looked but could not find Dare. Finally, Birdy looked up and saw Dare on top of the chimney! "How did you get up there?" Birdy asked. Dare smiled and said, "I dared myself to climb!" Just then, a big wind came and blew Dare off the chimney. But Birdy was fast and caught Dare before he fell. From that day on, Dare and Birdy were even better friends.

Once upon a time, in a tidy little house, there lived a tiny mouse named Mimi. Mimi was always happy and loved to play with her friends. One day, Mimi found a big box in her room. Mimi called her best friend, Tom the cat, and said, "Tom, I found a big box. Let's open it and reveal what's inside!" Tom came running and they opened the box together. Inside, they saw many toys and treats. Mimi and Tom were very happy. But then, Mimi saw a note in the box. The note said, "Please share these toys and treats with all your friends." So, Mimi and Tom spent the whole day sharing the toys and treats with everyone. At the end of the day, they were tired but very happy. Mimi and Tom learned that sharing with others can make everyone happy. They were glad they had read the note and followed its message. And from that minute on, they always shared their things with their friends.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They saw a big, fancy car with shiny windows and a loud horn. They wanted to see who was inside the car. "Let's follow the car!" Lily said. "OK!" Ben said. They ran after the car, but it was too fast. They lost sight of it when it turned a corner. They were sad and tired. They walked back to the park. They saw a man sitting on a bench. He had a big, fancy hat with feathers and a shiny ring. He looked like he was waiting for someone. "Hello!" Lily said. The man did not answer. He did not look at them. He was very quiet. "Hello!" Ben said. The man did not answer. He did not smile. He was very still. "Why are you so silent?" Lily asked. The man did not answer. He did not move. He was very strange. Lily and Ben felt scared. They did not like the man. They ran away from him. They found their mom and dad. They told them about the car and the man. "Maybe the car and the man are friends," Mom said. "Maybe the man is a spy," Dad said. Lily and Ben did not know. They did not care. They were happy to be with their mom and dad. They hugged them and played with them. They forgot about the car and the man.
