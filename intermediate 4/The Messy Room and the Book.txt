Category: Intermediate
Title: The Messy Room and the Book

Once upon a time, there was a messy room. Toys were all over the floor. In this room, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim did not like to clean up. He loved to read his big book, which was a novel. One day, Tim's mom asked him to clean his room. Tim did not want to clean. He wanted to read his novel. He started to complain. He said, "Mom, I don't want to clean. I want to read my book." Tim's mom had an idea. She said, "Tim, if you clean your room, I will read you a new novel." Tim liked this idea. He cleaned up his messy room. Then, his mom read him a new novel. They were both happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys. One day, he found a big box of junk in his room. He was so happy to see all the old toys in the box. Tim started to play with the toys. He leaned on the box to pick up a toy car. But then, something unexpected happened. The box moved! Tim was so surprised. He leaned on the box again, and it moved again! Tim looked inside the box and saw a little cat. The cat was playing with the toys too! Tim laughed and said, "Silly cat, you can play with me!" So Tim and the little cat played with the toys together, and they became best friends.

One day, a restless cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big sink outside a house. Tom was very thirsty, so he jumped on the sink to drink some water. But the sink was very high, and Tom fell in! Inside the sink, Tom met a fish named Fin. Fin said, "Hi, I am Fin. Why are you in my sink?" Tom said, "I am Tom. I was thirsty, and I fell in." Fin felt bad for Tom, so he helped him drink some water. After Tom drank the water, he was not restless anymore. He said, "Thank you, Fin. I feel better now." Then, something unexpected happened. The sink started to shake! Tom and Fin were scared. But it was just a big dog named Max, who wanted to drink water too. Max helped Tom out of the sink, and they all became friends. Now, Tom, Fin, and Max play together every day, and Tom does not lie on the sink anymore.

It was a beautiful sunny day and Sam was out for a drive. She was sitting in her daddy's pickup truck, which was red and shiny. Her Daddy was driving and singing along to a song on the radio. Sam looked around and noticed how pretty it was outside. They drove on for a while, until Sam saw something adorable - a small puppy running around in a field. "Daddy, look!" She said, pointing at the puppy. "That is sure a cute pup," said Daddy. He pulled the truck over and they got out. The puppy was so friendly and Sam couldn't wait to pet her. Her Daddy knelt down and showed Sam how to pet the puppy in the right way. The puppy was soft, and Sam loved the way she moved around in her own special style. Sam and Daddy said goodbye to the adorable pup, then they got back into the truck and continued their drive. Sam couldn't help but smile as they drove away.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy little squirrel named Sam. Sam loved peanuts very much. Every day, he would find peanuts under the big tree in the park. Sam would take the peanuts to his tiny home and keep them safe. One day, Sam met a small bird named Tim. Tim was hungry and asked Sam, "Can I have a peanut, please?" Sam thought about it and said, "Yes, you can have one. I value being kind to my friends." Sam gave Tim a peanut, and Tim was very happy. From that day on, Sam and Tim became best friends. They played together in the park and shared peanuts. Sam learned that being kind and sharing with friends was a good thing. He knew that valuing friendship was more important than having all the peanuts to himself.
