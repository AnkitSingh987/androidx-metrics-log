Title: Ann the Girl and the Mirror

Once, there was a girl named Ann. She had a big mirror in her room. She liked to look in the mirror and say, "I am glad I am so pretty!" Ann would bow to the mirror every day. One day, Ann saw a cat outside. The cat was sad. Ann thought, "I should help the cat." But then she looked in the mirror and said, "No, I am too pretty to help. I will bow to the mirror and be glad." The cat went away, still sad. Ann felt bad. She did not help the cat. She looked in the mirror and bowed, but she was not glad. The mirror broke. Ann learned that being pretty is not everything. Helping others is more important.

Once upon a time, there was a red tent in a big park. Inside the tent, lived a fast rabbit named Racy and a slow turtle named Tenty. They were best friends and loved to play together. One sunny day, Racy said, "Let's have a race, Tenty!" Tenty agreed, so they went outside the red tent and started the race. Racy ran fast, and Tenty walked slow. They raced around the big park, under trees, and over hills. Racy was far ahead, but he got tired and stopped to rest. Tenty kept walking slow and steady. Soon, Tenty passed Racy, who was still resting. When Racy woke up, he saw Tenty near the finish line. Racy tried to run fast again, but it was too late. Tenty won the race! They both laughed and went back to their red tent, happy to be friends.

One day, a little boy named Tom was playing in his room. He saw a big box in the corner. Tom was nervous, but he wanted to see what was inside. He opened the box and found a microscope. He did not know what it was, but it looked fun. Tom decided to carry the microscope to his mom. He walked slowly and carefully. "Mom, what is this?" he asked. His mom smiled and said, "That is a microscope. It helps you see very small things." Tom was excited to use the microscope. He put a tiny bug under it and looked through the top. The bug looked so big! Tom was not nervous anymore. He loved his new microscope and played with it all day.

One day, a cat and a dog had a race. The sun was mild, and the sky was blue. They wanted to see who was faster. The cat was small, and the dog was big. As they ran, they saw a bird. The bird flew down and screamed, "Stop!" The cat and the dog looked at the bird. The bird was not happy. The bird said, "You are too loud!" The cat and the dog felt bad. They said sorry to the bird. The bird forgave them. They all became friends. The cat and the dog did not finish the race, but they had a fun day with the bird.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely bird named Blue. Blue had a message to give to his friend, Cat. The message was a big secret. Blue wanted to make sure the message was safe. Blue found a soft leaf to wrap the message in. He used his beak to fold the leaf around the message. Now, the message was hidden inside the leaf. Blue was happy and ready to go. Blue flew to Cat's house with the wrapped message. Cat was happy to see Blue. Blue gave the leaf to Cat, who opened it to find the message. The message said, "You are my best friend." Cat smiled and hugged Blue. They were best friends forever.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a toy box with a lock. Lily liked to play with her toys and keep them safe in the box. One day, she saw her little brother trying to open the box. Lily did not want him to take her toys. Lily did not know how to tell him not to touch the box. She wanted to prevent her brother from opening it. So, she thought of an idea. She put the lock on the box and hid the key. Now, her brother could not open the box. Her brother was ignorant about the lock and the key. He tried to open the box, but he could not. Lily was happy that her toys were safe. She played with her toys and put them back in the box. And so, the lock helped Lily keep her toys safe from her little brother.
