Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Bird and the Balloon

One day, a big bird and a little bird were flying in the sky. They looked at the pretty landscape below. They saw trees, flowers, and a river. The big bird said, "I love to fly high and see all the beautiful things." The little bird wanted to fly higher too. So, they started to increase how high they were flying. But the little bird began to feel dizzy. "I don't feel good," said the little bird. The big bird looked at the little bird and saw that he was not happy. Suddenly, they saw a big balloon in the sky. The big balloon was going to hit the little bird! The big bird quickly grabbed the little bird and moved him out of the way. They both felt happy and safe. They decided to fly lower so the little bird would not feel dizzy again. From then on, they enjoyed the pretty landscape together.

Once upon a time, there was a famous cat named Tom. Tom was famous because he could talk. He lived in a small house with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. One day, Tom saw a post in the yard. He wanted to own the post, so he asked Tim, "Can I have the post, please?" Tim said, "Yes, you can have the post, Tom." Tom was very happy to own the post. He played with it every day. Soon, all the animals in the town knew about Tom's post. They all wanted to see the famous cat and his post. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, a little bird named Bobo wanted to fly. Bobo was very weak, but he tried his best to fly high in the sky. One day, he heard about a big meeting where all the birds would come together and talk. Bobo was very excited about the meeting. He practiced flying every day, so he could go to the meeting too. On the day of the meeting, Bobo was ready. He started to fly, but he was still weak. He tried and tried, but he couldn't fly high enough. Then, something unexpected happened. A big, strong bird named Max saw Bobo trying to fly. Max felt bad for Bobo, so he decided to help him. Max picked up Bobo in his big wings and took him to the meeting. At the meeting, all the birds were happy to see Bobo. They clapped their wings and cheered for him. And from that day on, Bobo and Max became the best of friends.

Jimmy was running around in the park. He saw a triangle and thought it was funny. He ran over and kicked it. The triangle wobbled and rolled away. Jimmy chased after it and kicked it some more. He laughed and had fun. Then Jimmy saw his friend. His friend had a toy ball. Jimmy got angry. He thought it was stupid that his friend had a toy and he didn't. So he ran over and kicked the ball away. His friend got mad. He yelled at Jimmy and said he was being mean. Jimmy didn't understand why his friend was so angry. He thought it was funny. But then Jimmy saw how much his friend wanted his ball back. He realized that kicking the ball away was wrong. He felt sorry and went over to get the ball. He gave it back to his friend and apologized. His friend was happy and they played together with the ball and the triangle.

One day, a gifted boy named Tim found a magic pick. He was very happy. He showed the pick to his friend, Sam. Sam said, "Wow! That is a cool pick!" Tim and Sam played with the pick. Then, something unexpected happened. Tim said the word "reverse" and the pick made them go back in time. They were little babies again. They tried to say "reverse" to go back to being big kids, but they could not talk yet. They were sad. They cried and cried. Their moms came to help, but they did not know about the magic pick. The moral of the story is to be careful with things you don't know about. Sometimes, they can make your life bad.
