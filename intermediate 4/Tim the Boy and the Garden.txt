Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Garden

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat jam on his toast every morning. One day, Tim saw that there was no more jam left in the jar. He was sad. He told his mom, "Mom, we need more jam!" His mom said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can make our own unique jam today. Let's go pick some fruit from the garden." Tim was excited and ran to the garden with his mom. Together, they picked lots of fruit. They went back inside and started to make the jam. But, the jam maker was broken. Tim's mom said, "Oh no! We need to repair the jam maker." Tim helped his mom fix the jam maker. After they fixed it, they made the unique jam. The next morning, Tim had the new jam on his toast. He was very happy and said, "This is the best jam ever!" His mom smiled and said, "I knew you would love it."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy was excited because she was going to a party. She put on her pretty dress and her shiny shoes. At the party, Amy saw a boy who was being rude. He did not recognize that he was making others sad. He was taking toys and not sharing. Amy did not like this. Amy wanted to help. She went to the boy and said, "Let's be nice and share the toys." The boy listened to Amy and started sharing. Everyone was happy and had fun at the party.

Once upon a time, in a big jungle, there was a tough rhinoceros named Remy. Remy was very big and strong. He had a big horn on his nose. All the animals in the jungle liked Remy. One day, Remy saw a little bunny. The bunny was scared. Remy asked the bunny, "Why are you scared?" The bunny said, "I am lost. I can't find my home." Remy wanted to help the bunny. Remy and the bunny started to run. They ran and ran until they found the bunny's home. The bunny was very happy. Remy was happy too. They became good friends and played together every day.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to play with his ball. He saw his big brother kick the ball with a cool style. Tim wanted to do it too, so he asked his brother to teach him. His brother said, "Sure, let's practice together!" Tim and his brother went outside to the park. They took turns kicking the ball. At first, Tim's kicks were not very strong. But his brother showed him the right way to kick. Tim tried and tried, and soon he was kicking the ball better. He was so happy! After a lot of practice, Tim became successful at kicking the ball just like his big brother. They played together all day and had a lot of fun. Tim was proud of his new style and couldn't wait to show his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim had a big toy box. He liked to add more toys to his box every day. One day, it rained a lot. The rain made a big flood in Tim's yard. Tim saw the flood and said, "Oh no! My toys will get wet!" He ran outside to save his toys. But then, something unexpected happened. A big fish swam up to Tim in the flood. "Hello, Tim!" said the fish. "I can help you save your toys. Just add me to your toy box, and I will make sure they stay dry." Tim was surprised but he said, "Okay, fish. I will add you to my toy box." The fish swam around the toys, and the water went away. Tim's toys were safe and dry, and he had a new friend, the fish. They played together all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a princess. She was very pretty. She lived in a big castle. One day, the princess was playing in her room. She heard a noise. It disturbed her. She went to see what it was. She found a small bird outside her window. The bird was very attractive. They became friends and played together. The princess was happy and not disturbed anymore.
