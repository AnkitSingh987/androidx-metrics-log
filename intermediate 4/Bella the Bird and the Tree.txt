Category: Intermediate
Title: Bella the Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a pretty bird named Bella. Bella had many friends in the forest. One day, Bella got a message from her friend, the wise old owl. The message said, "Come to my tree for a fun party." Bella was so excited, she told all her friends about the party. She went to her friend, the squirrel, and said, "Don't forget, we have a party at the wise old owl's tree today!" At the party, all the animals danced and played games. They thought Bella was very attractive because she had pretty feathers. Everyone had a great time and they didn't want the party to end. Finally, the sun went down and it was time to go home. Bella thanked the wise old owl for the fun party. She said, "Thank you for the message and the great party. I won't forget this day!" All the animals went home, happy and tired from the fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet little girl named Jane. She had a toy mine. The mine was full of fun toys. Jane loved her mine very much. One day, Jane went to play with her mine. She saw a toy bear. Jane picked up the bear and gave it a big hug. She could feel how soft and warm the bear was. Jane was very happy. Jane played with her mine all day long. She shared her toys with her friends. They all had a great time. At the end of the day, Jane put all her toys back in the mine. She felt good about sharing her sweet mine with her friends.

Once there was a puppy named Spot. He loved to chew on everything he could find. His mama always made sure he had something to chew on like a special chew toy. One day, Spot was in the backyard running around and looking for something to chew on. Suddenly, he saw something shiny and he headed over to it. It was a big, organized pile of rocks - perfect for chewing! Spot started to chew on the rocks one by one. After he had chewed the rocks for a while, Spot heard a noise. He stopped and looked up. It was his mama. She was looking at Spot and she had a big smile on her face. Spot was so happy he ran up to his mama and smiled back. Then he gave her a big, wet puppy kiss. Spot's mama took him back inside and gave him a special treat for being so well-behaved. Spot was so happy he started to chew on his treat right away. His mama laughed and said, “I’m so glad you are so organized!”

Once upon a time, in a small room, there was a brave little bunny named Bob. Bob loved to hop and play all day long. One day, Bob saw a big bookcase in the room. It was very tall and had many books on it. Bob wanted to see what was on the top of the bookcase. So, he took a deep breath and started to hop, hop, hop! With each hop, Bob got closer and closer to the top. He was very brave and didn't give up. Finally, Bob reached the top of the bookcase! He was so happy and proud of himself. From the top, he could see the whole room. After enjoying the view, Bob hopped back down, one hop at a time. From that day on, Bob knew he could do anything if he was brave and kept trying.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Max, went to the park. They were very glad to be outside. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Tim and Max liked to play catch with a ball. Tim would throw the ball and Max would run to get it. As they played, they saw a big bottle of shampoo spinning in the air. Tim said, "Look, Max! What is that?" Max barked as the shampoo landed near them. They went to see what it was. Tim picked up the bottle and read the word "shampoo" on it. He thought it was funny to find shampoo in the park. Just then, a little girl came running up to them. She said, "Oh, thank you for finding my shampoo! I was spinning it around and it flew out of my hands." Tim and Max were surprised but happy to help. They gave the shampoo back to the girl and she said, "Thank you! Now I can wash my hair." They all laughed and played together in the park for the rest of the day.
