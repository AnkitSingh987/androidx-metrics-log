Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy and the Sun

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lucy loved to help her mom in the kitchen. One sunny day, Lucy's mom asked her to clean the stove. "Lucy, can you help me make the stove shine?" she asked. "Okay, Mom!" Lucy said with a big smile. She took a wet cloth and wiped the stove. Then, she used a dry cloth to make it shine. As the sun came through the window, the stove sparkled. "Wow, Lucy! The stove is so shiny and dry now. Thank you for your help," her mom said. They both were very happy and proud of their clean stove.

One day, a girl named Lily was playing in her room upstairs. She had a toy phone that she liked to play with. The phone was tight in her little hands. Lily loved to pretend she was talking to her friends on the phone. Suddenly, the toy phone started to ring. Lily was surprised and picked it up. She said, "Hello? Who is there?" A soft voice from the phone said, "Hi Lily, I am your toy bear, Teddy. I want to play with you!" Lily was so happy! She ran to find Teddy and gave him a big hug. They played together all day long. And every time the toy phone rang, Lily knew it was Teddy calling her to play.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who loved to cook. She had a special pan and she used it to make the very yummiest cakes and pies. One day she decided to make a surprise cake for her mom's birthday. She got out her pan and started mixing all the things she needed for her cake. But in the middle of making it, something strange happened. Suddenly the pan disappeared! The girl was so surprised and very sorry she had lost her pan. She didn't know what to do or where it had gone. She looked all around the kitchen but the pan was nowhere to be found. Then, what do you think happened? Just as the girl was about to give up, the pan appeared right back in her hands! She was so happy and relieved that she finished the cake right away and everything was okay.

Once upon a time, there was a big, attractive tree. The tree had many pretty birds living in it. One day, a little bird wanted to join the other birds in the tree. But a mean cat saw the little bird and wanted to catch it. The little bird was scared and tried to find an escape. It flew around the tree, looking for a safe place to hide. The other birds saw the little bird in trouble and wanted to help. Together, the birds made a plan. They all flew at the cat and made loud noises. This scared the cat away! The little bird was safe and could finally join its new friends in the attractive tree. They all lived happily together.

One day, a boy named Tim found a flexible stick. He liked to play with it. He could bend it and it would not break. He showed it to his friend, Sue. "Look, Sue! I can snap it, but it does not break!" Tim said. Sue was amazed. Sue and Tim walked to the park with the stick. They found a tray on the ground. It was a big tray, and they put the stick on it. They played with the stick and the tray, making a fun game. They took turns snapping the stick and laughing. Then, a dog came to play with them. The dog was big and friendly. The dog looked at the stick and the tray. The dog picked up the tray in its mouth and ran away with the stick on it. Tim and Sue chased the dog, but they could not catch it. They were sad, but they knew they could find more sticks and trays to play with.

Once upon a time there was a rabbit and rat who were very good friends. Every day they would play together in the park. The rabbit always wanted to try climbing the pole in the park, but the rat said no. The pole looked filthy, so the rat didn't want his friend to get hurt. But one day when the rat was away, the rabbit decided to climb the pole. He was so excited to get to the very top. But when the rabbit reached the top he was surprised to find a pile of candy! The rabbit shared the candy with his friend. The rat was so grateful to his friend that he decided to clean the pole. The next day the pole was much nicer and no longer filthy. The moral of the story is that it's important to share with your friends, and team work can make things better.
