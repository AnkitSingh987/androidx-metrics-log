Category: Intermediate
Title: The Alligator and the Apple

Once upon a time, there was an alligator who was always sad. He never smiled. One day, he decided to take a walk. As he walked, he came across a yummy apple. The apple looked so delicious and it made him smile. The alligator was so happy that he decided to have a bite of the yummy apple. After one bite, he was so happy that he was almost laughing! He ran to tell all his friends about the yummy apple. Soon, the alligator was smiling all the time. He was so happy, he started to dance. He was smiling and dancing until he was too tired to move. All his friends were so happy to see him smiling. The alligator kept smiling every day. And now, whenever he passes a yummy apple, he always smiles!

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to play at school. But they do not like to do work. They are lazy. One day, the teacher says, "It is time to do math. Take out your books and pencils. Sit at your desk and work." Tom and Sam do not want to do math. They want to play. They look at each other and smile. They have a plan. They wait until the teacher is not looking. Then they sneak out of the door. They run to the playground. They see the swings, the slide, and the sandbox. They are happy. But they do not see the bell. The bell is on the wall. It is big and loud. It rings when it is time to go back to class. The bell rings. Tom and Sam hear it. They are scared. They know they are in trouble. They run back to the door. But the door is locked. They cannot get in. They knock on the door. They say, "Please let us in. We are sorry. We will do math. We will not be lazy." But the teacher does not hear them. She is busy with the other kids. They are doing math. They are not lazy. Tom and Sam are sad. They sit on the ground. They wait for the teacher to open the door. They wish they had done math. They wish they had not been lazy.

One day, a young puppy was out jumping around in the grass. It seemed to be so happy and carefree. Suddenly, a 3 year old came running around and saw the puppy. The child was very excited and said "Look Mommy, a puppy!". The mom smiled and said "That's great darling, let's take it home." The 3 year old was delighted and scooped up the young puppy. When they got home, the puppy started to run away but the 3 year old chased after it. Eventually the toddler caught up and grabbed the puppy. He was so happy and said "I found it, I found the puppy!". But as soon as he did, the puppy started to jump and squirm and suddenly the 3 year old dropped the puppy in shock. The puppy limped away and the 3 year old started to cry. The mom tried her best to comfort her child but the young puppy was never seen again.

One day, a boy named Tim got a present. It was a big box with a bow. He was very happy. Tim wanted to test his new toy. The toy was a small car that could go very fast. He loved to play with it. One day, Tim was playing with his car outside. He saw a girl named Sue. Sue was sad. Her toy was broken. Tim wanted to help Sue. He thought of a plan to make her happy. Tim went to his home and got another car. He gave the car to Sue as a present. Sue was very happy. They played with their cars together. They had a race to see who was the fastest. They laughed and had fun. Tim and Sue became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a green guitar. It lived in a small house with a boy named Tom. Tom loved to play with the green guitar every day. They had so much fun together. One day, Tom went to the park and saw a girl named Sue. Sue had a red guitar. Tom asked Sue, "Do you want to play with our guitars together?" Sue said, "Yes, I would love to!" So, Tom and Sue played their guitars and made beautiful music. After playing, Tom and Sue recognized that their guitars were friends too. The green guitar and the red guitar played together and had so much fun. From that day on, Tom, Sue, and their guitars were the best of friends. They played together every day and were very happy.
