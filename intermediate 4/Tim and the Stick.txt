Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Stick

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was an adventurous boy who loved to play outside. One cold day, he put on his warm coat and went out to play in the snow. Tim found a stick on the ground and picked it up. He pretended it was a magic wand. He waved it around and said, "Freeze!" Suddenly, all the snowflakes in the air stopped moving. They were frozen! Tim's friend, Sam, came outside to play too. Sam saw the frozen snowflakes and asked, "Wow, how did you do that?" Tim smiled and said, "With my magic stick!" They both laughed and played with the stick, having lots of fun in the snow.

One day, a little boy named Tim was ready to take a bath. He looked at his bathtub and saw that it was empty. He wanted to make his bath more fun, so he thought of an idea. Tim went to his room and found all his toys. He decided to unite them for a big bath party. He took his toy cars, his toy animals, and even his toy robot. He put them all in the bathtub and said, "Now we can all have fun together!" The toys were happy to be in the bathtub with Tim. As Tim played in the bathtub, he heard his toy robot say, "Thank you for inviting us to your bath party, Tim!" Tim smiled and said, "You're welcome, Robot! Let's all splash and have fun!" All the toys and Tim played together in the bathtub until it was time to get out and dry off. Tim knew that he could always have fun with his toys, even during bath time.

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a walk. She liked to skip. So, she skipped down the street. She saw her friend, Tom. Tom had gum. He was brave and gave Sue some gum. They both chewed the gum. Sue said, "I like to skip. Do you like to skip, Tom?" Tom said, "Yes, I like to skip too. Let's skip together!" Sue and Tom skipped down the street together. They felt happy and brave. They chewed the gum and had fun. They skipped all the way to the park. They played there until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, a young boy named Tim went on a safari with his mom and dad. They saw many animals like lions, elephants, and monkeys. Tim loved to see all the animals in the big park. One day, it was very hot. Tim saw an ice cream stand and said, "Mom, I want ice cream, please." Mom bought Tim an ice cream cone. But because it was so hot, the ice cream started to melt quickly. Tim tried to eat it fast, but it was too much. Tim's dad saw him struggling and said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can get another ice cream when it's not so hot." Tim smiled and enjoyed the rest of the safari with his mom and dad. They saw more animals and had a great time together.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Danny and Jacob. Danny and Jacob were excited to go to the festival. On the day of the festival, Danny started to feel scared. He didn't want to go alone. Jacob offered to go with him to make him feel better. The festival was a lot of fun. Danny enjoyed talking with Jacob and seeing all the fun things. But then on the way home they passed by a dark alley and it began to rain. Danny got scared again and started to cry. Jacob saw Danny was scared and knew exactly what to do. He smiled and said, "Let's share our umbrellas so neither of us will be scared". Danny smiled back and they shared their umbrellas. They were both happy knowing they weren't alone. The moral of the story is that it's important to help others when they are scared. Friends are there to help you when you need it and sharing with them makes them feel great. So remember to always help and share when you can!
