Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and Ben and the Bird

Sara and Ben like to play in the garden. They have a big pot full of seeds. They want to scatter the seeds on the ground and make flowers grow. But there is a problem. A big black cat lives next door. The cat likes to chase birds and mice. Sometimes, the cat comes into the garden and scares Sara and Ben. One day, Sara and Ben are scattering the seeds when they hear a loud meow. They look up and see the cat on the fence. The cat jumps down and runs towards them. Sara and Ben are afraid. They drop the pot and run to the house. The pot breaks and the seeds spill everywhere. The cat stops and sniffs the seeds. He does not like them. He spits them out and walks away. Sara and Ben are sad. They lost their pot and their seeds. They want to make flowers grow, but they have no more seeds. They do not know what to do. Then, they see something. A little bird flies down and picks up a seed. The bird flies to a tree and drops the seed in a hole. Then, the bird comes back and picks up another seed. The bird does this many times. Sara and Ben watch the bird. They think the bird is brave. The bird is not afraid of the cat. The bird is making a nest with the seeds. Sara and Ben smile. They have an idea. They go to the house and get some bread. They break the bread into small pieces. They scatter the bread on the ground. They hope the bird will like the bread. They hide behind a bush and wait. Soon, the bird sees the bread. The bird flies down and eats the bread. The bird is happy. The bird sings a song. Sara and Ben are happy too. They sing along with the bird. They make a new friend. They forget about the cat and the pot. They think about the flowers that will grow from the seeds. They think they are brave too.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful little girl named Amy. She loved to learn new things. One day, she saw a blue bird in her yard. The bird was sad because it could not find its way home. Amy said, "Don't worry, little bird. I will help you find your home." The blue bird was happy and thanked Amy. They went on a walk to look for the bird's home. As they walked, Amy and the blue bird talked and learned from each other. They became good friends. Finally, they found the bird's home. The bird was happy and said, "Thank you, Amy. You are very helpful." And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a toy box with many toys. One day, she wanted to find her favorite toy, a small bear. Lily looked in her toy box, but the bear was not there. She felt sad and had a bad memory of losing her bear before. Lily asked her mom for help to find her bear. Together, they looked everywhere in the house. At last, they found the bear under Lily's bed. Lily was very happy and hugged her bear. She promised to take good care of it and not lose it again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy called Kevin. He loved to write. Every day he would take out a piece of paper and try to make shapes with his pencil. One day, Kevin wanted to try something new. He started to write a letter to his friend. But he made some mistakes and he felt really ashamed. So he decided to write the letter again and make it perfect this time. He was so focused on making it perfect that he forgot about everything else. But before he knew it, it was dark outside and he hadn't done all his work. His teacher was very angry and scolded him. Poor Kevin felt so ashamed. He left the classroom with tears in his eyes, feeling bad that he had let himself down.
