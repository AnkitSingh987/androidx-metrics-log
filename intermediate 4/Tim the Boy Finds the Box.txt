Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Box

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. He wanted to find love. Tim walked and walked. The sun was warm and the sky was blue. He saw a big tree and sat under it. Tim looked around, but there was no love available. Then, Tim saw a girl named Sue. Sue was walking too. Tim said, "Hi, Sue! I am looking for love. Can you help me?" Sue said, "Yes, Tim! I can help you. Let's walk together and find love." They walked and walked. They saw a little house. In the house, there was a box. Tim and Sue were happy. They thought they found love. They opened the box and looked inside. But there was no love in the box. There was a big, scary spider! The spider jumped out and scared Tim and Sue. They ran away from the little house. They did not find love, but they found a big, scary spider.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Sam and Max. Sam was very curious and liked to explore. One day they were walking in the woods and they saw something amazing. It was a mineral. It was so shiny and sparkly! Sam and Max were so excited and could not stop looking. Sam decided he wanted to take it home with him. He put it in his pocket and ran off with it. Max wanted to take it too and started running after Sam. They ran around and around until Max stopped. He remembered that taking things from nature was not nice. Sam was sad to realize this. He decided he would put the mineral back where he found it. He did and feeling happy, he and Max continued walking in the woods, looking for more amazing things.

Once upon a time, there was a colorful playground. Many kids came to play there every day. One day, two players, Tim and Sam, were playing with a big, red ball. Tim said, "This is my ball!" Sam said, "No, it's mine!" They began to fight over the ball. They pushed and pulled, but neither would let go. A wise, old bird saw them fight. The bird said, "Stop fighting! If you share, you both can play with the ball." Tim and Sam thought about it, and they agreed to share. They played together and had lots of fun. From that day on, Tim and Sam learned to share and became good friends. The playground was happy and colorful again. The kids learned that sharing is better than fighting.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue lived in a small house with her mom and dad. They were not rich, but they were happy. Sue had a big red ball that she loved to play with every day. One day, Sue's ball rolled away and landed near a big tree. She saw a squirrel holding the handle of a shiny box. The squirrel said, "I promise to give you this box if you can catch me!" Sue wanted the box, so she chased the squirrel. They ran and laughed as they played together. After a while, Sue caught the squirrel. The squirrel kept his promise and gave Sue the box. Inside the box, Sue found a lot of pretty stones. She took them home and showed her mom and dad. They all smiled and hugged each other. Now, they felt a little bit rich because of the love they had for each other.

Once upon a time, there was a big ranch. On the ranch, there lived a good cow and a good pig. They were good friends. One day, the cow and the pig had a big fight. The cow was sad and the pig was mad. The cow said to the pig, "Please forgive me. I want to be friends again." The pig thought about it and said, "Okay, I forgive you. We can be friends again." They were both happy and played together on the ranch. But one day, something unexpected happened. A big storm came to the ranch. The cow and the pig tried to hide, but they could not find a safe place. The storm was very strong and it blew them far, far away. They were not at the ranch anymore and they were not together. The cow and the pig looked for each other, but they could not find their way back. And so, they were lost and alone, and they never saw each other again.
