Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Toys

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a big, modern truck that he liked to play with every day. He loved to bring his truck outside and drive it around in the dirt. One day, Tim saw a little girl named Sally. Sally was sad because she did not have any toys to play with. Tim thought about it and decided to bring his truck to Sally. He said, "Sally, let's play with my truck together!" Sally was very happy to play with Tim and his truck. They had so much fun playing together, and Tim felt good about sharing his truck with Sally. The moral of the story is that sharing can bring happiness to others and make you feel good, too.

One day, a big, clumsy dog named True went for a walk. True liked to play and run. He saw a red ball and ran to get it. As he picked up the ball, he saw a little girl. The girl said, "Hi, doggy! Can you give me my ball?" True gave the ball back to the girl. They played for a while, and then the girl got hungry. She said, "I want to eat. I will make some food." She went to her house, and True followed her. The girl tried to cook, but she was not good at it. She made a big fire, and the food started to burn. True saw the fire and knew he had to help. He ran outside, found a big bucket, and filled it with water. He was clumsy, but he tried his best. True ran back inside and threw the water on the fire. The fire went out! The girl was so happy, and she said, "Thank you, True! You saved my house." They became best friends, and True learned that even clumsy dogs can do great things.

One day, a big whale was swimming in the sea. The whale was very serious. He did not play with the other sea animals. He only swam alone. A small fish saw the whale and wanted to be friends. The fish swam up to the whale and said, "Hi, I am a fish. Do you want to play with me?" The whale looked at the fish but did not say anything. The fish did not give up. He wanted to make the whale happy. He thought of a game they could play. The fish said, "I have an idea. I will offer you a race. Let's see who is faster." The whale agreed and they started to race. The fish was fast, but the whale was faster. They both laughed and had fun. From that day on, the whale was not serious anymore. He played with the fish and other sea animals every day.

Once upon a time, in a tidy little house, there lived a cat named Tim and a dog named Max. They were best friends and loved to play together. One sunny day, they found a big case in the yard. They were very excited and wanted to see what was inside. Tim said, "Max, let's open the case and find out what's inside!" Max agreed and they opened the case together. Inside, they found a big bottle of juice. Max said, "Tim, let's drink this juice and share it with our friends!" Tim agreed and they took the juice out of the case. When they started to drink the juice, something unexpected happened. The juice made them grow bigger and stronger! They were very surprised but happy. Tim and Max decided to use their new strength to help their friends and family. They carried heavy things and helped to clean up the town. In the end, Tim and Max learned that sharing and helping others can make them feel good and strong. They promised to always share and help their friends, even without the special juice. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot was a dull dog who did not like to play. One day, Spot found a bottle of medicine. He did not know what it was, so he started to shake it. As Spot shook the medicine, something unexpected happened. The bottle opened and the medicine went all over the floor. Spot was scared and did not know what to do. Spot's friend, the cat named Fluffy, came to see what happened. Fluffy saw the mess and tried to clean it up. But Fluffy slipped on the medicine and got hurt. Spot felt sad and learned that he should not play with things he did not know.
