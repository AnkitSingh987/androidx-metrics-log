Title: John and the Park

John and his mom were walking in the park. The sun was shining, and they could feel the warm breeze against their faces. John was excited to explore the park. He saw a playground and shouted, "Play! Play!" His mom smiled and said, "OK, we'll play, but let's get something to eat first." John's eyes lit up as they walked into a modern cafe. A sign said, "Freshly-baked sugar cookies here!" John eagerly pointed to the cookies. His mom nodded and said, "Let's get some." John and his mom picked out the biggest cookie. As soon as it was in his hand, it became John's most favorite thing. He took a bite and smiled. The sweet taste of sugar filled his mouth. John finished the cookie and shouted, "More, more!" His mom laughed and said, "We can come back here next time." John was happy. He already couldn't wait to become friends with the modern cafe that made sugar cookies.

One day, a giant was sleeping in a big open field. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. The giant had been asleep for a very long time. A little boy named Tim walked by and saw the giant. Tim wanted to play with the giant, so he tried to wake him up. Tim shouted, "Wake up, giant! Let's play!" The giant slowly opened his eyes and looked at Tim. The giant said, "Hello, little boy. What do you want to play?" Tim smiled and said, "Let's play catch with a big ball!" The giant and Tim played catch all day long. They had so much fun, and the giant was happy to have a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little monkey. The monkey lived in a cold forest. The monkey did not like the cold. He wanted to find a warm place to live. One day, the monkey saw a big fire in the forest. The fire was warm, but it was not good. The monkey wanted to go near the fire, but he knew he should resist. He tried to resist, but he could not. He went closer to the fire to feel warm. The monkey got too close to the fire. The fire was very hot and the monkey got hurt. The monkey was sad and still cold. The monkey did not find a warm place to live. The end.

Mama was in the kitchen, making dinner. She was very competitive because she wanted to make the tastiest dinner ever. Mama was chopping and stirring, but she was not alone. Daddy was there to help her. He put ingredients in the pot and stirred them around. Mama and Daddy worked together to get dinner ready, and then it was time to serve it. Mama served the dinner onto the plates and Daddy brought it to the table. They all sat down and had dinner together. It was very tasty, just like Mama said it would be! Mama was happy that her dinner was so delicious, and now she knew she was the most competitive chef in the family.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a pink dog. The pink dog liked to chew on things. One day, it found a box with small sticks in it. These sticks were called matches. The pink dog started to chew on the matches. As it chewed, the matches made a small fire. The dog did not know what to do. It was scared of the fire. The fire grew bigger and bigger. The small house started to burn. The pink dog tried to run away but it was too late. The small house burned down and the pink dog was very sad. The end.

One day, a tall girl named Mia went to her yoga class. Mia liked to sort her yoga mat and blocks by color. She had a red mat, a blue block, and a green block. Mia was very good at yoga and could do many fun poses. In the class, Mia saw a little dog. The dog was doing yoga too! The dog could do poses just like Mia. Mia was very surprised. She did not know that dogs could do yoga. The dog was very good at yoga, just like Mia. Then, something unexpected happened. The little dog started to sort the yoga mats and blocks just like Mia! Mia was so happy. She had a new friend who liked to do yoga and sort things like her. They did yoga together and had lots of fun.
