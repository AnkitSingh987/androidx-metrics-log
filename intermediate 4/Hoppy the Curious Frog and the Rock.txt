Category: Intermediate
Title: Hoppy the Curious Frog and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a curious frog named Hoppy. Hoppy lived in a swamp with his family. Every day, Hoppy would jump and play with his friends. One day, Hoppy saw a big rock near the swamp. He was curious about what could lie under the rock. So, Hoppy hopped over to the rock and pushed it with all his might. When the rock moved, Hoppy found a new friend hiding there. It was a little turtle named Shelly. Hoppy and Shelly became best friends and played together in the swamp every day. And they all lived happily ever after.

Jackie and her mom were in the grocery store. Jackie's mom was looking at the fruit. "Mommy, can we get some apples?" asked Jackie. "Yes, honey, let's pick some apples," said her mom. They walked over to the apples. Jackie's eyes lit up when she saw all the different kinds. "I want that one!" she said, pointing at a bright red apple. Her mom smiled and picked the apple for her. "Mmmm, this looks delicious," said Jackie. Her mom laughed and said, "Let's get some more fruit before we go." They spent the next few minutes picking out different fruit and then headed to the checkout counter. "Mmmm, this fruit is so delicious," said Jackie with a big smile. Her mom smiled too and said, "Glad you like it sweetie. Now let's go home and have some lunch!"

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big box of junk in his room. Tim loved to play with the junk, but it was very messy. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. Sam saw the junk and said, "Wow, that's a lot of mess! Can we please clean it up?" Tim, who was deaf, could not hear Sam, but he saw that Sam wanted to help. They both started to clean up the junk together. As they cleaned, they found many lost toys and even some money. Tim and Sam were so happy! They learned that when you clean up your mess, you can find treasures. The moral of the story is that it's good to keep your things tidy and help your friends.

Once upon a time, in a big zoo, there lived a gorilla named Bobo. Bobo was very sad and miserable. He did not feel like he belonged in the zoo. He missed his home in the big jungle. One day, a kind zookeeper saw how sad Bobo was. He made a plan to make Bobo feel happy and like he belonged. The zookeeper started to bring big trees and plants into Bobo's home at the zoo. He wanted to make it look like the big jungle. Slowly, Bobo's home became more like the big jungle. Bobo started to feel happy again. He played and climbed the trees just like he used to. In the end, Bobo felt like he belonged in his new home at the zoo. The zookeeper's plan worked, and Bobo was not miserable anymore.

Once upon a time there was a young rabbit named Joe. Joe was very brave, but one day he was very scared. He was hopping through the forest looking for food when he came across a big, dark cave. His legs started to tremble as he stepped closer. He peeked in the cave and saw an old witch. She smiled wickedly and said, "Do you want to buy some magic thread?" Joe knew he shouldn't do it, but he was so brave he said, "yes!" He bought some thread from the witch and she said, "Here, take this thread as a reward. The witch was very sneaky and tied the thread to Joe's feet. Joe tried to get away, but he couldn't. He was tied up so tightly he could barely move. The old witch cackled in delight and whisked Joe away. Joe never made it out of the cave and he was never seen again. No one knows where he ended up, but it's safe to say it was a very bad ending.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the park. The cat had a big urge to surprise the dog. The cat hid behind a big tree and waited for the dog to come close. "Boo!" said the cat, as he jumped out from behind the tree. The dog was so dizzy from the surprise that he fell down. "Why did you do that?" asked the dog, looking sad. "I just wanted to have fun," said the cat. "I didn't mean to make you dizzy." The dog got up and tried to walk, but he was still very dizzy. He walked near the road and a big car came fast. The cat tried to help, but it was too late. The dog was hit by the car and did not get up. The cat was very sad and cried. He learned that surprises can sometimes be bad.
