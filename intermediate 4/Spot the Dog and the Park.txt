Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Park

One day, a little dog named Spot was playing in the park. He was running fast and having fun. Spot saw a big, round cone and wanted to play with it. He was very glad to find a new toy. Spot tried to pick up the cone, but it was too big for his mouth. He pushed it with his nose and it rolled away. Spot chased the cone and barked happily. He liked his new toy a lot. Spot's friend, a nice cat named Fluffy, saw him playing with the cone. Fluffy came over and helped Spot push the cone. They played together and had so much fun. When they were tired, Fluffy comforted Spot by giving him a hug. They were both very glad to be friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was very excited because she had a new toy. It was a pretty napkin with the number five on it. Lily loved her napkin very much. One day, Lily went to the park with her napkin. She saw a boy named Tom. Tom looked sad. Lily asked, "Why are you sad?" Tom said, "I lost my toy. It had the number four on it." Lily wanted to help Tom find his toy. Lily and Tom looked all around the park. They found a toy with the number four on it. Tom was so happy. He said, "Thank you, Lily!" Now, both Lily and Tom had their toys with numbers on them. They played together and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim was a little bit frightened of the dark. One night, he heard a sound coming from his closet. Tim was too scared to look, so he called his mom. "Mom, I hear a sound in my closet," Tim said. "Can you please check?" His mom came into the room and opened the closet. She saw a small cat inside. "Look, Tim," she said. "It's just a cat." Tim felt better and asked a question. "Can we keep the cat, Mom?" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, we can keep the cat." Tim was very happy and named the cat Fluffy. From that day on, Tim was not frightened of the dark anymore because he had Fluffy by his side.

Once upon a time, there was a blue bird named Billy. Billy lived in a big tree near a pretty lake. Every day, Billy would fly around and sing happy songs. He liked to look at the water in the lake because it had a nice image of him. One day, a loud dog came to the lake. The dog liked to bark and run around. The dog would disturb Billy when he was trying to sing his songs. Billy was sad because he could not sing and see his image in the lake. Billy had an idea. He flew to his friend, a big bear named Ben. Ben saw that Billy was sad and wanted to help. Ben went to the lake and told the loud dog to be quiet. The dog listened to Ben and stopped barking. Billy was happy because he could sing his songs again and see his image in the lake.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mary. She was three years old and she loved going to the lake to play. One day, Mary brought her fishing hook to the lake and saw that someone had left a strong fishing hook. She wanted to try it, so she grabbed it and went to the lake. When she got to the lake, she saw that the water was very clear. She sat down and baited her hook with some cheese, then tossed it into the lake. She waited for a long time, but nothing happened. "Why isn't anything biting?" she asked herself. Then, she heard an old man's voice. "The water is too weak," he said. "Fish need a stronger current to be able to serve the hook. You'll never catch anything here." Mary thanked the old man and went back to shore. She tried to find a stream with a stronger current and finally, she caught a fish! She was so excited and went home with a big smile on her face. From then on, she was always sure to find a stream with a strong current when she went fishing.
