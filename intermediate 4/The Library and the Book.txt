Category: Intermediate
Title: The Library and the Book

Once upon a time, there was a big library. It was full of many books. Kids, moms, and dads would enter the library to find a good book to read. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the big library with his mom. They entered the library and saw many books. Tim was very happy. He liked to read about animals, cars, and boats. Tim and his mom picked some books and sat down to read together. They read about a big bear, a fast car, and a big boat. After they finished reading, they left the library with big smiles. They couldn't wait to come back and read more books.

Once upon a time, in a colorful forest, there lived a little bird named Timmy. Timmy had many friends, but his best friend was a big bear named Benny. One sunny day, Timmy and Benny were playing near the river. Timmy said, "Benny, let's turn and race to that big tree!" Benny agreed, and they started running. But then, they saw a small, sad squirrel sitting on a rock. Timmy and Benny stopped and asked, "Why are you sad?" The squirrel said, "I lost my nut, and now I am hungry." Timmy and Benny decided to help the squirrel find his nut. They looked high and low, and finally found it under a bush. The squirrel was so happy, and they all became friends. From that day on, Timmy, Benny, and the squirrel played together in the colorful forest. They learned that helping others can make you feel happy too. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a noisy jungle, there lived a small bird named Blue. Blue had a friend named Tiny, who was a little mouse. They liked to play together under the big palm trees. One day, they saw a big cat coming near them. Blue said to Tiny, "We must escape from the big cat or it will catch us!" So, they ran away as fast as they could. They hid in a small hole under a palm tree. The big cat looked for them but could not find them. After a while, the big cat left. Blue and Tiny came out of the hole. They were happy that they escaped from the big cat. They knew that they had to be careful in the noisy jungle. From that day on, they always played close to their hiding spot, so they could escape quickly if they needed to.

Once upon a time, in a hot sunny day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. He wore a big hat to keep the sun away. Tim saw a girl named Sue sitting on a bench. Sue had a bag with her, and it looked heavy. "Hi Sue, why is your bag so heavy?" asked Tim. "Hi Tim, I have a lot of rocks in it. I found them on my walk. I like them because they are pretty," replied Sue. Tim wanted to help Sue, so he took some rocks from her bag. They played with the rocks and made a little tower. After a while, they felt tired from the hot sun. They sat under a tree and shared a cold drink. Tim and Sue became good friends, and they were happy they could help each other.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim and a little girl named Sue were playing a game. They wanted to see who could spin around the most times without stopping. They both wanted to win. Sue said to Tim, "I will spin so much, I will win!" Tim replied, "No, I will win!" They both started spinning around and around. After a while, they both felt very dizzy. They stopped spinning and fell down on the grass, laughing. A few minutes later, their mom came outside. She could tell they were dizzy from spinning so much. She said, "You both did a great job, but now it's time to rest. Let's go inside and have a snack." Tim and Sue smiled, knowing they both had fun, and that was the real win.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big bag of marbles. He loved to play with them all day. One day, Tim met a new friend named Sam. Sam wanted to play with Tim's marbles too. "No, you can't play with my marbles!" said Tim. Sam was sad and didn't understand why Tim didn't want to share. Tim didn't listen to Sam. Tim was being ignorant. Sam's mom saw them and came to help. She told Tim, "You should share your marbles with Sam. It's good to share with friends." Tim thought about it and decided to listen to Sam's mom. He shared his marbles with Sam. Tim and Sam played with the marbles together and had lots of fun. They became best friends. From that day on, Tim learned to share and listen to others. The moral of the story is to share and listen to your friends, so everyone can be happy.
