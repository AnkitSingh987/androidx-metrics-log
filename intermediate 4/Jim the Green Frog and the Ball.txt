Category: Intermediate
Title: Jim the Green Frog and the Ball

One day, a big green frog named Jim lived near a pond. In the pond, there was a pretty spring. The spring had fresh, clean water. Jim loved to jump and play in the water all day. He liked it so much that he did not want anyone else to use the spring. One sunny day, a little bird named Tim came to the spring. He was thirsty and wanted to drink water. But Jim did not want to share. He told Tim, "This is my spring. You can't have any water. Go away!" Tim felt sad and flew away. The next day, Jim found out that someone had stolen his favorite toy. It was a small, disgusting ball. He was very upset and went to ask Tim if he saw who took it. Tim said, "I saw who took your ball. I will help you get it back if you share the spring with me and my friends." Jim agreed to share the spring with Tim and his friends. Together, they found the ball and took it back. From that day on, Jim learned to share the spring with all the animals. They all played happily together, and Jim never tried to steal anything again.

Once upon a time, in a far away planet, there lived a big, intelligent bird named Benny. Benny was very nice and loved to help his friends. One day, he saw a small bug named Billy struggling to carry a big leaf. Benny asked, "Do you need help, Billy?" Billy looked up and said, "Yes, please! This leaf is too heavy for me." Benny used his strong wings to support the leaf and help Billy carry it to his home. As they walked, they talked and laughed. Benny and Billy became very good friends. From that day on, they always helped each other, and the whole planet was happy because of their friendship.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Amy. Amy had a toy bunny with long ears. She loved her bunny very much and played with it all day long. One day, the bunny's eye fell off. Amy was very sad. She went to her mom and asked for help. Her mom knew how to repair things, so she took the bunny and sewed the eye back on. Amy was so happy to see her bunny with both eyes again. She hugged her mom and said thank you. From that day on, Amy and her bunny with long ears played together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a girl named Lily. She loved to collect things. One day, Lily found a book about herbs. The book said that some herbs were easy to find and very helpful. Lily wanted to find these herbs and help her mom cook. Lily asked her mom, "Can I go find easy herbs to help you cook?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, but be careful and don't go too far." Lily went outside and started to look for herbs. She saw a big tree and thought, "There must be herbs near this tree." Lily found many easy herbs near the tree. She was so happy and couldn't wait to show her mom. When she got home, her mom was very proud of her. They used the herbs to make a yummy dinner. Everyone in the family loved the food, and Lily knew that she had done a great job.

One day, a small girl named Mia went to the park. She saw a nice flower and wanted to pick it. She walked to the flower and said, "I will take you home." A little bird heard her and said, "Please do not pick the flower. It is my friend." Mia looked at the bird and saw it had a hurt neck. She wanted to help the bird, so she made a plan. Mia went to her mom and asked for a bandage. She put the bandage on the bird's neck. The bird was happy and said, "Thank you, Mia!" Then, the bird did something unexpected. It flew up and picked the flower for Mia. They both went home with a new friend.
