Category: Intermediate
Title: The Dinosaur and the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a big dinosaur. He lived in a big open field. He liked to walk and play all day. One day, a little boy saw the dinosaur. The boy wanted to be friends with the dinosaur. So, he decided to follow the dinosaur. They walked and played together all day. They were very happy. But then, something unexpected happened! The dinosaur started to get smaller and smaller. The little boy was surprised. The dinosaur was not a real dinosaur. It was a toy dinosaur! The boy picked up the toy dinosaur and took it home. They played together and were best friends forever.

Lily and Ben were playing in the yard. They liked to run and jump and sing. They saw a big truck stop in front of their house. The truck had a sign that said "Mail". "Look, Lily, it's the mailman!" Ben said. "He brings us letters and cards and sometimes packages." Lily nodded. She ran to the fence and waved at the mailman. The mailman smiled and waved back. He got out of the truck and walked to the mailbox. He opened the mailbox and put some letters inside. Then he closed the mailbox and tried to lift it up. But the mailbox was broken. It fell off the post and landed on the ground. The mailman looked sad and confused. He scratched his head and said, "Oh no, what happened to the mailbox?" Lily and Ben ran to the mailbox. They saw that it was cracked and bent. They felt sorry for the mailman and the mailbox. "Don't worry, Mr. Mailman, we can help you!" Lily said. "We can repair the mailbox. We have tools and glue and tape." "Really? You can do that?" the mailman asked. He looked surprised and happy. He said, "That would be very nice of you, thank you!" Lily and Ben ran to the garage and got their tools and glue and tape. They worked hard to fix the mailbox. They used the hammer and the nails to attach the mailbox to the post. They used the glue and the tape to mend the cracks and the bends. They worked slow and careful, but they had fun. When they were done, they showed the mailman their work. The mailbox looked almost new. It was strong and straight and shiny. The mailman clapped his hands and said, "Wow, you did a great job! You are very smart and kind. The mailbox looks wonderful!" Lily and Ben smiled and said, "You're welcome, Mr. Mailman. We're glad we could help you. We like the mailbox and we like you." The mailman hugged them and said, "I like you too. You are my best friends. Here, I have something for you." He gave them each a card and a sticker. The card said, "Thank you for repairing the mailbox. You are awesome. Love, Mr. Mailman." The sticker had a picture of a mailbox and a heart. Lily and Ben were very happy. They thanked the mailman and put the card and the sticker on their fridge. They waved goodbye to the mailman as he drove away. They went back to the yard and played some more. They felt proud and happy. They had repaired the mailbox and made a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree. The tree had many bananas. A little monkey lived in the tree. The monkey loved to eat bananas. One day, the monkey saw a big banana. It was the biggest banana on the tree. The monkey wanted to eat the big banana. But the big banana was very high up in the tree. The monkey climbed the tall tree to get the big banana. It was not easy, but the monkey did not give up. The monkey climbed and climbed until it reached the big banana. The monkey was happy to complete the climb. The monkey ate the big banana and it was yummy.

Tim and Sue are twins. They like to play together. They have a big box of toys in their room. Sometimes they share, sometimes they fight. One day, Tim wants to play with the red car. He looks for it in the box, but he cannot find it. He asks Sue, "Where is the red car?" Sue says, "I have it. It is mine. You cannot play with it." Tim gets angry. He says, "No, it is not yours. It is ours. We share. Give it to me." Sue says, "No, I will not. Go away." Tim tries to grab the car from Sue. Sue holds it tight. They pull and tug. The car breaks. They both scream. Mom hears them scream. She comes to their room. She sees the broken car and the angry twins. She says, "What happened? Why are you screaming?" Tim and Sue both say, "It is his/her fault. He/she broke the car." Mom says, "Stop. You are both wrong. You should not fight over toys. You should be kind and gentle. You should be dependable. That means you do what you say and you help each other. Do you understand?" Tim and Sue nod. They feel sorry. They say, "We are sorry, Mom. We are sorry, Tim/Sue. We will be dependable." Mom says, "That is good. I love you both. Now, let us go to the kitchen. I have made some oat cookies for you. You can have one each if you promise to share and play nicely." Tim and Sue say, "Yes, Mom. We promise. Thank you, Mom. We love you too." They hug Mom and each other. They go to the kitchen. They eat the oat cookies. They are yummy. They are happy. They are dependable.
