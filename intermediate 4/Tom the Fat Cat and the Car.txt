Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Fat Cat and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom loved to eat and sleep all day. He had a friend, a quick mouse named Jerry. Jerry was very fast and always played with Tom. One day, Tom wanted to catch Jerry, but he was too slow. So, Tom thought of a plan. He would use a toy car to chase Jerry. Tom got on the toy car and started to chase Jerry around the house. But Jerry was too quick for Tom! Jerry ran and ran, and Tom could not catch him. In the end, Tom got tired and went back to sleep. Jerry was happy that he was still free. They both had fun, and they stayed friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He loved to buy toys from the store. One day, Tom saw a beautiful toy boat. He wanted to buy it, but he did not have enough money. Tom was sad, but he decided to be patient and save his money. After some days, Tom had saved enough money to buy the toy boat. He went to the store, but the boat was gone. Tom was sad again, but a kind spirit who lived in the store saw him. The spirit wanted to help Tom, so she made a new toy boat just for him. Tom was very happy when he saw the new boat. He learned that being patient is good because it helps you get what you want. And the kind spirit learned that helping others makes you feel happy too. So, Tom and the spirit became friends, and they played with the toy boat together.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He loved to watch films. Tim had many different films. Some were about cars, some were about animals, and some were about big boats. One day, Tim found a new film. It was about a car that could speed very fast. Tim was excited to watch it. He sat on the floor and put the film in the big box. The box made the film show on the wall. As the car in the film started to speed, Tim shouted, "Wow, look at that car go!" His friend, Sam, came to see. Sam said, "I want to watch too!" They both sat and watched the car speed on the wall. Tim and Sam liked the fast car and the different film. They were happy to watch it together.

Once there was a chubby boy named Andrew. Andrew liked to run and play in the park. One day, he was so excited that he ran faster than ever. He ran straight to the fountain. He reached out his hands to touch the water. Suddenly, he heard a loud tear! The fountain had a big rip in the middle. Andrew was scared and started to cry. He couldn't believe that the fountain was broken. He wanted to fix it, but he didn't know how. He looked around and saw a few kids carrying a big bag with them. He went over to them and asked what was in the bag. The kids explained that they had taken some tools from their dad's garage to fix the fountain. They said they could fix it if Andrew helped them! Andrew was so excited that he helped the kids repair the fountain. After they had finished, the fountain was as good as new! Andrew was thrilled that he had helped to fix the fountain. He knew he had done a good job. He smiled and ran back home, feeling so proud of himself.

Once upon a time, there was a polite little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside with her friends. One day, her mom told her to close the door when she went out to play. Lily forgot to close the door, but she did not know that something would happen later because of this. At lunch, Lily and her friends sat down to eat. They had yummy sandwiches and juice. They were all very happy. While they were eating lunch, a little cat came into the house through the open door. The cat was very hungry and wanted some food too. When Lily went back inside, she saw the cat eating her lunch. She remembered that she forgot to close the door. Lily felt sad, but she knew it was her mistake. She gave the cat some food and promised to always close the door. From that day on, Lily always closed the door and the cat became her new friend.
