Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Compassionate Girl and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a compassionate girl named Lily. She liked to help others. One day, she saw a sad boy named Tom. Tom was sad because he wanted to make a cake for his mom, but he did not know how to cut the cake. Lily said, "I can help you cut the cake, Tom!" Tom was happy and they went to the kitchen. In the kitchen, they found a big cake and some powder. Lily showed Tom how to cut the cake into small pieces. Then, they put the powder on the cake to make it look nice. But, oh no! They made a mess with the powder. It was all over the floor! Lily and Tom cleaned up the mess together. After they finished, they shared the cake with their friends. Everyone was happy and Tom's mom loved the cake. Lily and Tom became best friends and they always helped each other.

Anna and Ben like to play hide and seek. They take turns to hide and seek. Anna is very clever. She knows many good places to hide. Ben is not so clever. He always hides in the same place. One day, Anna is the seeker. She counts to ten and says, "Ready or not, here I come!" She looks for Ben in the bedroom, in the bathroom, in the living room, but she cannot find him. She thinks, "Where is Ben? He must be hiding very well today." She goes to the kitchen. She sees a big cupboard. She thinks, "Maybe Ben is hiding in the cupboard. I will open it and see." She walks to the cupboard and opens the door. She sees Ben inside. He is holding a cookie. He says, "Surprise!" Anna says, "I found you, Ben! You are hiding in the cupboard again. You are not clever. You always hide in the cupboard. I win, I win!" Ben says, "But I have a cookie. You do not have a cookie. I win, I win!" Anna says, "No, you do not win. You are cheating. You are eating a cookie when we are playing. That is not fair. Give me the cookie." Ben says, "No, it is my cookie. I found it in the cupboard. It is yummy. You cannot have it." Anna and Ben start to fight. They pull the cookie. They make a lot of noise. They do not hear their mom coming. She sees them fighting in the cupboard. She says, "What are you doing, Anna and Ben? Why are you fighting over a cookie? Why are you hiding in the cupboard? You are making a mess. You are not playing nicely. You are both in trouble." Anna and Ben stop fighting. They look at their mom. They feel sorry. They say, "We are sorry, mom. We were playing hide and seek. We were trying to be clever. We did not mean to make a mess. We did not mean to fight. Can we have the cookie?" Mom says, "No, you cannot have the cookie. You have to share the cookie. You have to say sorry to each other. You have to clean up the cupboard. You have to play nicely. Then you can have the cookie." Anna and Ben say sorry to each other. They share the cookie. They clean up the cupboard. They play nicely. They are happy. Mom is happy. She says, "You are good children. You are clever children. You are my children. I love you."

Once upon a time there were two best friends - a dog and a cat. They were very loyal to one another and did everything together. One night they were both tired, so they decided to cuddle up and sleep together. The cat just got really cozy and closed her eyes. The dog, however, was not tired yet and didn't close his eyes. He noticed that the cat had a book in her paw and decided to have a look. He flipped it over and saw that it was full of pictures. They were so pretty and colourful he couldn't help but look at them all. After some time, the dog felt tired and sleepy. He slowly closed his eyes and snuggled up beside the cat. They stayed this way all night, with the loyal friends keeping each other safe and warm.
