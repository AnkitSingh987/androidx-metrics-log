Title: The Flea and the Dog

Once upon a time there was a flea who loved to play. She hopped around the house and made lots of mischief. One day, the flea found a tight ball of string. She thought it looked like a lot of fun, so she started to play with it. She wrapped it around her body until it was so tight she couldn't move. The flea began to worry, but then she saw a little dog. She asked him, 'Can you please make this string loose?' The little dog thought for a moment and then he said, 'Yes, I can help you!' He began to pull and tug on the string until it was finally loose. The flea was so pleased, she thanked the little dog for helping her. From then on, she was extra careful with string, so she never put herself in a tight spot again!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He lived in a small house near the deep woods. One day, Tim's father gave him a small axe. Tim's father encouraged him, saying, "You can do it, Tim! You can help me cut wood for our house." Tim felt very happy and proud. He went into the deep woods with his father. They found a small tree to cut. Tim's father said, "Remember, Tim, we only cut the trees we need. We must be kind to the woods." Tim tried to cut the tree with his small axe. It was hard work, but he did not give up. His father encouraged him, "Keep going, Tim! You are doing great!" Tim felt strong and happy. Soon, the tree fell, and they took the wood back to their house. The moral of the story is to always try your best and to be kind to nature.

Once upon a time, there was a big fish named Bob. Bob loved to dive deep in the water. One day, he saw a clean, shiny point on the bottom of the pond. Bob wanted the shiny point, so he took a big breath and dove down, down, down. He swam fast, and soon he was at the bottom of the pond. Bob was so happy to find the shiny point. It was a clean, pretty rock. Bob took the clean rock to his friend, Sally the frog. Sally liked the shiny point too. They played with the rock and had lots of fun. Bob and Sally were very happy, and they played together in the pond every day.

Once there was a frog named Tom. He lived in a pond, and every day he would mail letters to his friends. One day, he mail a letter to his best friend with a special surprise inside. When the letter arrived, his best friend opened it, and out jumped Tom! His best friend was so happy and gave Tom some candy as a thank you. But the candy was very bitter! Tom was so surprised by the taste, he didn't know what to do. He wanted to be polite and stay, but he just couldn't take the taste. So he hopped away as fast as he could. He never mailed to his friend again!

Tim and Sam were brothers who liked to play with cars. They had many cars of different colors and sizes. They liked to make them go fast and make loud noises. Sometimes they pretended they were drivers in a race. One day, they found a big can of gas in the garage. They thought it was fake gas for their cars. They poured some gas on their cars and on the floor. They thought it would make their cars go faster and louder. They did not know that gas was dangerous and could catch fire. Mom was in the kitchen when she smelled gas. She ran to the garage and saw the mess. She was very scared and angry. She grabbed Tim and Sam and took them outside. She called the firemen to come and help. She told Tim and Sam that gas was not fake, but real, and that they could have been hurt or burned. She said they should never play with gas or anything they did not know. Tim and Sam felt very sorry and ashamed. They hugged Mom and said they were sorry. They said they did not know gas was real and dangerous. They said they would never do it again. They learned that they should always ask Mom or Dad before they use anything they find. They also learned that playing with cars was fun, but only in their dreams.
