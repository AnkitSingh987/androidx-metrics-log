Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Gift

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big box. He wanted to wrap the box fast for his friend's birthday. He asked his mom for help. "Mom, can you help me wrap this box fast?" Tim said. His mom said, "Yes, let's wrap it fast." Tim and his mom started to wrap the box. They used a cut of pretty paper to wrap it. They worked fast and finished wrapping the box. Tim was happy and said, "Thank you, mom. It looks great!" Tim took the wrapped box to his friend's birthday party. His friend was excited to see the big box. But when his friend opened the box, there was nothing inside. Tim forgot to put the gift in the box. Everyone was sad and Tim felt bad for not putting the gift inside.

Once upon a time there was a camp. There were two boys, Sam and Jack, who lived there. Sam liked the camp and he always enjoyed his time there. Jack, however, thought the camp was disgusting and he didn't like it. One day, Sam was having a great time enjoying the camp. He went for a walk and saw some birds and flowers. Jack watched Sam and he thought about how different his opinion on the camp was. Jack then said, "Why do you enjoy this camp so much? I think it's disgusting!" Sam replied, "It is not disgusting! Everything has something special about it if you look hard enough. Even this camp has something special about it." Jack thought about what Sam said and he started to appreciate the camp. From that day, Jack and Sam could both enjoy the camp. The moral of the story is that, even if something seems disgusting, you never know what you might discover if you take the time to look closer.

Once upon a time, there was a big, high hill. On top of the hill, there was a little house. In the house, there lived a nice man named Tom. Tom loved to buy things for his house. One day, Tom went to the store to buy a big barrel. He wanted the barrel to keep water for his flowers. He saw a pretty blue barrel and said, "I want to buy this one!" The store man said, "Okay, here you go!" Tom was very happy. Tom took the barrel home and put it next to his house. Every day, he filled the barrel with water and gave it to his flowers. The flowers grew big and pretty, and Tom was very proud of them.

Once upon a time in a big pond, there lived a hippo named Holly. Holly was a selfish hippo. She did not like to share her toys with her friends. One day, Holly's friend, Tim the turtle, came to play. Tim saw Holly's big toy box and wanted to play too. "Holly, can I play with your toys, please?" asked Tim. Holly thought for a moment, and then said, "No, Tim, these are my toys." Tim was sad, but he had an idea. "Holly, let's complete a puzzle together!" Tim said. Holly liked puzzles, so she agreed. They took out a big puzzle and started to put the pieces together. As they worked on the puzzle, Holly saw that sharing and playing together was fun. Tim was happy too. When they finished the puzzle, Holly said, "Tim, you can play with my toys now. Sharing is fun!" From that day on, Holly was not a selfish hippo anymore. She learned that sharing with her friends made everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy named Mr. Head. Mr. Head was very special to Tim. They played together every day. One day, Mr. Head fell and broke. Tim was very sad. Tim's mom saw that he was sad. She wanted to make him happy. So, she got some glue to fix Mr. Head. She put the glue on the broken parts and put Mr. Head back together. The glue was hot, but it worked very well. Now, Mr. Head was fixed. Tim was very happy to have his toy back. He thanked his mom for her help. They played together again, and Tim took good care of Mr. Head. The love and care from Tim's mom helped to restore Mr. Head and make Tim happy again.
