Title: Laura and the Sun

The wind was blowing east, and it made the day tough. But Laura was tough too. She wanted to try something new. She took a step backward and started to reverse. She giggled as she saw her world move backward. As she moved, she could see her house getting closer, until she reached it. Laura laughed loudly. She had been so brave and reversed all the way there. She felt proud and excited. She took a few more steps and reversed around her house. She felt spunky, jumping and twirling. As she reversed, she bumped into something. She opened her eyes and gasped in surprise. It was a large, red fire truck! The firefighters smiled and waved at her. They were the heroes of the day. Laura smiled back, happy that she had reversed all the way to the fire truck. She waved goodbye and started to reverse once more. She felt strong and adventurous. She kept reversing until it was time to go home. As the sun set, Laura knew that she had had a tough day, but it was also a lot of fun. She had faced her fear of reversing and been brave in the process.

Once upon a time, there was a big lion. The lion was troubled because he lost his metal toy. He looked for it everywhere. He was very sad. One day, the lion heard a noise. It was a little mouse. The mouse found the metal toy. The lion was very happy. He said "thank you" to the mouse. The lion and the mouse played with the metal toy. The lion roared with joy. They became good friends. They played together every day. And they were never troubled again.

One day, a big bear and a little bunny were playing in the woods. They were having so much fun. The sun was shining, and they could feel the heat on their fur. They ran, jumped, and laughed together. The big bear was slow because he was so big. He tried to catch the little bunny, but he could not. The little bunny was fast and light. The big bear said, "Bunny, why are you so fast? I am slow and cannot catch you." The little bunny said, "Bear, you are big and weigh a lot. That is why you are slow. But it is okay. We can still be friends and play together." The big bear smiled and they continued to play in the warm sun. As the day went on, the big bear and the little bunny played many games. They shared their food and had a great time. They learned that even though they were different, they could still be friends. And they were happy to have each other in the heat and the cool of the woods.

A girl was out walking in the park. She looked up at the sky and saw a big cloud. The cloud was very clean and white. Suddenly she realized something. She looked around and she noticed that the park was also very clean. She ran around the park picking up all the rubbish she could find. After she had finished, she stood in the park and looked up at the sky. The cloud was still there. She smiled and thought, "The park looks better now that it is clean, just like the cloud!".

One day, a big dog and a little cat were in a race. They wanted to see who had more speed. The dog said, "I am big and fast, I will win." The cat replied, "I am small, but I am organized and quick." They started the race. The dog ran very fast, but the cat had a plan. The cat knew a short way to the finish line. The dog did not know about the short way. The cat ran on the short way and got to the finish line first. The dog was very surprised when he saw the cat at the finish line. "How did you get here so fast?" asked the dog. The cat replied, "I told you I was organized. I knew a short way." The dog learned that being big and fast is not always the best way to win.
