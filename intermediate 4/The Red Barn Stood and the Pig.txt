Category: Intermediate
Title: The Red Barn Stood and the Pig

One day, a big red barn stood in a thick green field. Many animals lived in the barn. A cow, a pig, a sheep, a horse, and a little chick all played together. The little chick said, "I want to play a game. Let's play hide and seek!" The other animals agreed. The pig closed his eyes and started to count. The other animals ran to hide. The little chick found a good hiding spot. He hid behind a thick bush near the barn. The pig finished counting and started to look for the animals. He found the cow, the sheep, and the horse. But he could not find the little chick. The little chick said, "I will vanish now!" He closed his eyes and tried very hard to vanish. But he could not really vanish. The pig heard the little chick's voice and found him behind the bush. They all laughed and played more games together.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big, thick ball. He wanted to play with it, so he took it to his friend, Sam. "Look at my big ball!" Tim said. Sam was happy to see the ball and wanted to play too. They went to the park and started to bounce the ball. It went up and down, and they laughed as they played. Then, the ball hit Sam's face. "Ouch!" Sam cried. Tim felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, Sam." Sam's face was red and he was sad. Tim tried to make him feel better, but Sam didn't want to play anymore. They went home, and the big, thick ball was left all alone in the park. The ball had no one to play with, and it was a bad ending for the ball.

Once, there was a little girl called Lucy. She loved her parents and wanted to make them proud. One day, her parents gave her a key and told her never to open the door. Lucy promised not to do it. One day, when the parents left, Lucy decided to try the key. She opened the door and found a cupboard filled with sugar! Lucy was so glad that she wanted to take the sugar out of the cupboard. Just then, her parents arrived back. Lucy quickly tried to lock the door, but it was too late. Her parents asked her why she had opened the door and Lucy explained what happened. Her parents were angry and disappointed. They told her that it was important to keep her word and to listen to what they said. They said it was important to not to open doors without permission. Lucy became sad and promised to never do it again. Now she knows that it’s important to always listen to her parents and to keep her word.

Once upon a time, there was a clever rabbit. It was winter, and it was very cold. One night, the clever rabbit was hopping in the snow, looking for something to eat. It saw a big bush with berries. The rabbit was very excited, and quickly hopped over to the bush. But then the rabbit cried out in despair. "Oh no! The bush isn't filled with yummy berries. It's filled with leaves instead!" But then the clever rabbit had an idea. It used its clever tricks and soon enough, the bush was filled with yummy berries. The clever rabbit hopped away with a full belly, feeling happy and satisfied. The end.

One day, Tim and his mom went to the park. The sun was hot, and they wanted to play. Before they left, mom said, "Let's pack something fun to eat later." Tim smiled, but he did not know what mom had in mind. At the park, they played on the swings and the slide. Tim was having a lot of fun. He saw the sky getting dark, and he felt a bit sad. He thought it was time to go home soon. But his mom had a big surprise for him. Mom opened the big, cold box they brought. Inside was ice-cream! Tim was so happy. They sat and ate the ice-cream as the sky got dark. The ice-cream made the day even more fun. Tim knew his mom always had the best surprises.
