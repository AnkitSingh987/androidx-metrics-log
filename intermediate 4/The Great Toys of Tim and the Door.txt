Category: Intermediate
Title: The Great Toys of Tim and the Door

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing in his room. He was very bored because it was a rainy day and he could not go outside. He looked at his toys and thought they were boring too. Tim wanted to do something fun and exciting. Suddenly, there was a big shake! It was an earthquake! Tim was scared and he ran to his mom. "Mom, what is happening?" he asked. His mom said, "It's an earthquake, Tim. Let's go and close the door to be safe." They closed the door and hid under the table. The earthquake stopped, and Tim was not scared anymore. He felt brave and excited. He said to his mom, "That was not boring at all!" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, but it is better to have fun without any trouble." Tim agreed and played with his toys, feeling happy even on a rainy day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was a regular boy who liked to play with his friends. One day, while playing in the park, Tom saw a shiny symbol on a big tree. Tom wanted the shiny symbol, so he tried to pull it off the tree. He pulled and pulled, but the symbol would not come off. Tom's friends told him to stop, but he did not listen. He wanted the shiny symbol so much that he kept pulling. Tom pulled so hard that he fell down and hurt himself. He cried and his friends came to help him. Tom learned that he should listen to his friends and not be greedy. The moral of the story is to listen to others and not let greed take over.

One day, a girl named Amy had a plan. She wanted to make her mom glad. She thought, "I will make a big, pretty picture for her!" So, Amy took her colors and paper and began to draw. Amy drew a sun with a big smile. She drew a house with a red door. She drew a tree with green leaves. Then, she drew a cat with soft skin. The cat said, "Meow, meow! I like your picture, Amy!" Amy said, "Thank you, cat!" She gave her picture to her mom. Her mom was very glad. She said, "I love your picture, Amy! It is so pretty!" Amy smiled and hugged her mom. They were both very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little sheep named Wooly. Wooly had lots of soft wool. He lived in a big field with his friends. They liked to play and eat grass all day. One day, it was very cold and bitter outside. Wooly's friend, Bunny, was cold too. Bunny said, "Brrr, I am so cold! Wooly, can you help me?" Wooly thought for a moment and said, "Yes, I believe I can help you, Bunny!" Wooly gave some of his wool to Bunny. Bunny felt warm and happy. From that day on, Wooly and Bunny played together every day. They were the best of friends. And when it was cold, Wooly always shared his wool to keep his friends warm.

One day, a boy named Tim went to visit his friend, Sam. They liked to play with toy cars and trucks. Sam had a new toy. It was a light, red ambulance. They played with it all day long. Tim said, "I like your new ambulance, Sam. It is very light and fast." Sam smiled and said, "Yes, it is my favorite toy. We can play with it together." As they played, they heard a loud noise outside. An ambulance stopped near their house. Tim and Sam watched as the helpers got out and helped a sick man. They were happy that the real ambulance was there to help. The boys knew they wanted to be helpers one day too. They played with the light, red ambulance all day, dreaming of helping people. Tim and Sam knew they would be good friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to travel with his family. They went to many fun places. One day, they went to a big park. In the park, they saw kids playing hockey. Tim wanted to play too. Tim was a little weak, but he tried his best. He got a hockey stick and joined the game. The other kids were nice and helped him. They showed him how to hit the ball. Tim was happy. As Tim played more hockey, he became stronger. He was not weak anymore. He made many friends at the park. They all played hockey together. Tim loved to travel and play hockey with his new friends.
