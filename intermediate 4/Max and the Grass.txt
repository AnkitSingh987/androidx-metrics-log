Category: Intermediate
Title: Max and the Grass

Once upon a time, there was a big, reliable dog named Max. Max loved to play outside in the sun. Every day, Max would run and jump on the soft grass with his friends. One day, Max saw a small, red ball on the grass. He wanted to play with it. Max started to run very fast to get the ball. But then, Max had to pause. He saw a tiny bug on the grass. Max did not want to step on the bug, so he waited. When the bug was safe, Max got the ball and played with his friends. They all had lots of fun on the grass. Max was a good and reliable friend, and everyone loved him.

Once upon a time, there was a little bee named Buzz. Buzz lived in a hive with his friends. They all worked together to make honey. Buzz wanted to feel useful, so he helped by bringing flowers to the hive. One day, Buzz saw a big flower far away. He flew to the flower and said, "Hello, pretty flower! I want to take you to my hive." The flower nodded and smiled. Buzz was very happy and carried the flower back to the hive. When Buzz got back, he showed the flower to his friends. They all said, "Wow, Buzz! That's a big flower! You are very useful!" Buzz felt proud and happy. From that day on, Buzz always tried his best to help his friends and be a useful bee.

Once upon a time, there was a fine big fish named Bob. Bob lived in the sea. He had many friends. They liked to play and swim together. One day, Bob met a new friend named Tim. Tim was a small fish. "Hi, I'm Bob," said the fine big fish. "Hi, I'm Tim," said the small fish. They played and swam together. They had a lot of fun. But one day, they found a big, red apple in the sea. It was starting to rot. Bob and Tim didn't know what to do. They did not want the sea to be dirty. So, they worked together to take the rot apple out of the sea. Bob and Tim learned that it is important to keep the sea clean. They told their friends about the rot apple. All the fish learned to help keep the sea clean. And they all lived happily in the fine, clean sea.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Annie who really liked ties. Every morning, before she went to school, she put on her favorite tie. One morning, Annie's mom noticed something special about the tie she was wearing. Her mom said, "That is a lucky tie, Annie!" Annie smiled and nodded. She wondered what made her tie so lucky. Later that day, Annie had a spelling test. She felt nervous but remembered the lucky tie. She closed her eyes and nodded to herself. Suddenly, the words started to come to her. When the teacher handed back the spelling tests, Annie smiled. She had gotten an A+ and her friends were very impressed. Everyone said, "Your lucky tie must have helped you!" Annie smiled and nodded, feeling very lucky indeed.

Once upon a time, there was a magic ice cream. It was very tasty and everyone wanted to eat it. One sunny day, the magic ice cream was outside, and it started to feel hot. It began to melt and became wet. A little girl named Lily saw the magic ice cream melting. She ran to it and said, "Oh no! I must save the magic ice cream!" Lily picked up the wet, melting ice cream and put it in a cup. Lily took the cup inside her house and put it in the freezer. The magic ice cream stopped melting and became cold again. Lily was happy that she saved the magic ice cream. She shared it with her friends, and they all enjoyed the tasty treat.

Once upon a time, a little mouse lived in a tiny house. He had a friend named Cat. They liked to play all day. One day, the mouse was feeling silly. He put a big hat on his head and danced around. Mouse said to Cat, "Look at me! I am so silly!" Cat laughed and said, "You are very silly, Mouse!" Together, they danced and played with the big hat. They had so much fun. But then, Mouse started to complain. "I am tired," he said. Cat said, "Let's rest." So, they sat down and took a break. After they rested, they played some more. They were very happy friends.
