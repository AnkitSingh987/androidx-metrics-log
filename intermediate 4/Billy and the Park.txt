Category: Intermediate
Title: Billy and the Park

Billy was a very impatient little boy. He couldn't wait to go outside and play. As he walked to the park, he saw lots of birds that were flying in zigzags in the sky. They made him giggle. When Billy got to the park, he noticed his best friend, Mary, waiting for him. Billy ran up to her and asked, “What have you been doing?” Mary giggled and said, “I've been making jokes. Want to hear one?” Billy of course did, so Mary told him a funny joke. They both laughed and then ran off to play tag. As they ran around, they noticed a squirrel running around the park in tight zigzags. Billy and Mary thought it was very funny and they laughed so hard they had to sit down. Billy was so happy to be playing with his best friend. He liked making jokes and watching the animals play. He was a very impatient little boy, but he was having a wonderful day.

Mia and Jake were playing in the garden. Mia went to the slide and she said, "Look Jake! I can slide!" She went up the steps and down the slide. She was so excited. "This is so fun!" she said. Jake saw Mia having fun and he wanted to slide too. He went to the slide and tried to go down. But he got stuck halfway down. "Help me Mia," he said, "I'm stuck!" Mia laughed and said, "I will help you, Jake. Don't be lazy!" She took his hand and helped him come down the slide. When it was time for a snack, Mia and Jake went inside. Mia's mom had put some strawberry ice cream in the freezer. Mia grabbed two bowls and spooned the cool cream into them. They both slurped it down, enjoying the sweet and cold treat. Mia and Jake went back outside and played on the slide again. They laughed and giggled, having the best day ever.

Once upon a time there was a little girl. She loved exploring and playing outdoors. One day she found a new rod in the garden. She was so excited! She touched it and tugged on it. Suddenly, the new rod started to tear! The little girl was so sad. She knew she had done something wrong. Then, a funny thing happened. Out of the broken pieces of the new rod came a butterfly. It was the most beautiful creature the little girl had ever seen. She watched in wonder as the colorful butterfly flew around the garden. Suddenly, the little girl was so happy again. The little girl thanked the rod for giving her such a special gift. She decided to always take care of nature from then on. The little girl and the butterfly became best friends and always played together in the garden.

Once upon a time, there was a little fur ball called Furry. Furry was very pale and small. One day, Furry decided to go for a walk. He was having so much fun, but then it started raining! He was very scared and started to miss his home. He was so cold and his fur was getting all wet. Poor Furry was so scared that he started to cry. Suddenly, two big hands scooped him up and brought him home. Furry was safe and warm. He was so happy to be back home. He had missed it so much! From then on, Furry never left home again. Whenever he got scared he remembered how much he missed his home, and that made him feel better.

Ben and Lily are friends. They like to play with blocks. They hope to build a big tower with many blocks. They work hard and help each other. They put one block on top of another block. "Look, our tower is so tall!" Ben says. "Yes, it is!" Lily says. "We are good builders!" They are happy and proud. They have success. They want to show their tower to their teacher and their friends. But then, a big wind comes. It blows hard and fast. It makes their tower fall down. All the blocks crash to the ground. Ben and Lily are sad and angry. They cry and shout. "No, no, no!" Ben says. "Our tower is gone!" "Why, why, why?" Lily says. "We worked so hard!" They hug each other and wipe their tears. They look at the mess of blocks. They feel like giving up. But then, their teacher comes. She sees their tower and their faces. She smiles and hugs them. "I saw your tower before the wind came," she says. "It was amazing! You did a great job!" She helps them pick up the blocks. She tells them they can try again. She tells them they are brave and smart. She tells them she is grateful for them. Ben and Lily feel better. They smile and thank their teacher. They decide to build another tower. They hope it will be even bigger and stronger. They are still friends. They are still good builders. They are still happy and proud. They still have success.
