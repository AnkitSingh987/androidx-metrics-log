Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Cat and the Boat

One day, a chubby cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big, navy boat in the water. Tom wanted to go on the boat. He walked up to the boat and asked the man, "Can I come on your boat?" The man said, "Yes, you can help me stir the soup for lunch." Tom was happy to help. He got on the boat and went to the kitchen. The man gave Tom a spoon to stir the soup. Tom stirred the soup very well. After stirring the soup, Tom and the man ate lunch together. They had a great time on the navy boat. Tom was happy he could help and made a new friend. The man was happy too, because the soup was very tasty. They both smiled and enjoyed the rest of the day on the boat.

One day, a little boy named Roger was walking around in the park. He had brown hair and looked very sleepy. All of a sudden, he noticed a kitty sitting on the grass. Roger approached the kitty, and said "Hi there kitty. What is your name?". The kitty meowed in response, and said "My name is Spot". Then, Roger asked "Can I meet you?". Spot agreed, and the two of them started to walk around together. Roger pets Spot's silky fur and smiled as he noticed its long whiskers. Spot's fur was so soft that it felt like a pillow. Soon, Spot was laying down and dozing off. Roger lay down next to him, and the two of them soon fell into a deep and peaceful sleep. They slept in the grass until sunset, and when they woke up, they were still side by side. Roger and Spot enjoyed the rest of their day together, playing, talking and running around. Roger was happy to meet such a friendly cat like Spot, and he couldn't help but smile.

One day, a boy named Tom found a dry folder on the floor. He picked it up and saw that it was split in two parts. Inside the folder, there were pictures of his friends. Tom wanted to fix the folder, so he asked his mom for help. "Mom, can you help me fix this folder?" Tom asked. His mom looked at the folder and nodded. "Yes, Tom. Let's use some tape to put it back together." As they started to tape the folder, something unexpected happened. A picture fell out of the folder. It was a picture of Tom and his friend Sam, but Sam was sad in the picture. Tom felt bad for Sam and decided to do something nice for him. Tom took the fixed folder and went to Sam's house. He showed Sam the picture and said, "I'm sorry you were sad, Sam. I want to be a good friend." Sam smiled and said, "Thank you, Tom. I was sad because I lost my folder. I'm happy you found it." From that day on, Tom and Sam became better friends. They learned that it is important to care for each other and help when a friend is sad. The dry, split folder brought them closer together and taught them the value of friendship.

Once upon a time, there was a big giant named Tom. He lived in a big house near the woods. Tom was a happy giant, but sometimes he was careless and broke things. One day, Tom met a little boy named Sam. Sam was not scared of the giant. He said, "Hi, giant! Do you want to hear a joke?" Tom smiled and said, "Yes, I like jokes!" Sam told the giant a funny joke about a cat and a dog. Tom laughed so hard that he was careless and broke a tree. Sam said, "Oh no! Be careful, giant!" Tom said, "I'm sorry, I will try to be careful next time." From that day on, Tom and Sam were best friends. They played and told jokes all day long. And Tom learned to be more careful when he was with his little friend Sam.

Once upon a time, there was a little bug named Bobby. Bobby lived underground with his family. They were very happy bugs. One day, Bobby and his family wanted to celebrate. They had a big party with all their bug friends. They ate yummy food and played fun games. But then, something unexpected happened. A big, disgusting worm came into the party. All the bugs were scared. They thought the worm would ruin their fun. But the worm just wanted to play. So, they let the worm join the party, and they all had a great time. The worm wasn't so disgusting after all.
