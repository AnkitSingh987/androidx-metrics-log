Title: The Dog and the Elevator

Once upon a time, there was a very good dog. He always wanted to help and make people happy. He wanted to feed the people in trouble. One day, there was an old man in the elevator. He was sad and hungry. The good dog felt sorry for him and wanted to help, so he started to feed the old man. The old man was so thankful and the elevator started to move up. But then, the elevator stopped and the dog wasn't allowed to come out. He was stuck in the elevator and no one knew. He tried to bark to ask for help, but it was too late. He stayed in the elevator for hours and hours until he was very hungry, and no one helped him. The good dog never got out of the elevator, it was so sad. He was stuck in the elevator forever with no food and no one could help him. The end.

Once upon a time, in a land far away, there was a big cloud. The cloud was very spoiled. It did not like to share its rain with the flowers and trees. The cloud wanted to keep all the rain for itself. One day, the cloud met a kind girl. The girl wanted to help the cloud share its rain. She had a big, long string. The girl tried to tie the string to the cloud. She thought this would help the cloud share its rain. But the cloud did not want to be tied. It tried to run away from the girl. The girl did not give up. She kept trying to tie the cloud. Suddenly, the cloud started to laugh. It had never been tickled before! The cloud realized that sharing could be fun. From that day on, the cloud shared its rain with the flowers and trees. And the girl and the cloud became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Jane. Jane was an honest girl who always told the truth. One day, she went to a party with her family. At the party, she saw a boy named Tom. Tom and Jane played together and had a lot of fun. They liked each other so much that they decided they wanted to marry when they grow up. During the party, Jane saw a pretty napkin on the table. She wanted to take it home, but she knew it was not hers. So, she asked her mom if she could have it. Her mom said no, but Jane took it anyway. She hid the napkin in her pocket and went back to play with Tom. At the end of the party, Tom and Jane said goodbye. But when Jane got home, she felt bad about taking the napkin. She knew it was not right. So, she told her mom the truth and they went back to the party to return the napkin. When they got there, they found out that Tom's mom had a surprise for Jane. She wanted to give her the napkin as a gift because she saw how much Jane liked it. The moral of the story is that it is always better to be honest and ask for things instead of taking them without permission.

One day, a big pink pig went to play. The pig was very excite to run and use his muscle. He liked to play with his friends, the dog and the cat. The pig, the dog, and the cat played a fun game. They ran and jumped in the sun. They were very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A big bird came down and took the pink pig's hat. The pig, the dog, and the cat were all surprise. They thought the bird was a friend, but the bird was not nice. They used their muscle to get the hat back. The pig, the dog, and the cat were happy again.

Once upon a time there was a small bird called Phil. He was different from all the other birds: he was the only one who didn't have a name. Every time Phil asked the other birds what his name was, they told him he didn't have one. Phil was sad and wondered why he didn't have a name like the other birds. One day, Phil decided to go on a mission: he wanted to find out what his name was. As he flew up high, he noticed the other birds were gathered around a tree. When Phil flew closer, he saw a task written on the tree. It said: “Find your name and become part of the flock”. Phil was excited and started to fly around looking for his name. He searched for days, but it was a heavy task. He got tired and was about to give up when another bird flew towards him. It was his friend, Susan. Susan said, “Phil, your name is Phil. You have been part of the flock all along, you just had to realize it! You don't need a name to be part of the flock.” Phil was so happy and went back to join the other birds around the tree. He learnt a valuable lesson that day: even if you don't have a name, you can still be part of the flock and find happiness.
