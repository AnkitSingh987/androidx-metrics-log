Category: Intermediate
Title: The Lady and the Spoon

Once upon a time, there was a nice lady. She had a pot and a spoon. She was very impatient. Every day she would stir the pot with her spoon to make something yummy. One day she decided to try a new way. She put some fruit in the pot and she stirred it for a long time. Finally, the fruit became a delicious jam. The lady shared the yummy jam with everyone. Everyone was so happy because it tasted so delicious! The next day the lady was just as impatient. She stirred the pot again, but this time with some vegetables. She created a yummy vegetable soup. Once again, the lady shared the soup with everyone. Everyone was so happy again! From then on, the lady kept finding new ways to stir her pot. Every day she made something new and delicious!

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was an adorable little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside and run around with his friends. One day, Spot saw a big red ball in the park. He wanted to play with the ball, so he ran after it. While Spot was playing with the ball, he did not see the police standing nearby. The police were looking for a lost dog. Spot thought they were his friends, so he ran up to them, wagging his tail. He wanted to play with the police too. But the police thought Spot was the lost dog they were looking for. They picked up Spot and took him away. Spot was sad because he could not play with his friends and the big red ball anymore. The bad ending is that Spot never got to go back home and play with his friends again.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. Tom had a big worry. He did not know how to separate his red and blue socks. He wanted to wear the same color socks every day. One day, Tom asked his friend, Sam, for help. Sam was very smart. He looked at Tom's socks and said, "I can help you separate them." Sam put all the red socks in one pile and all the blue socks in another pile. Tom was very happy. Now, Tom did not have to worry about his socks anymore. He could wear the same color socks every day. Tom and Sam became best friends. They played together and had lots of fun. And Tom's bald head always shined in the sun.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They had a fun day playing with a ball and running around. When they got tired, they sat down to eat their lunch. Tim's mom had made him a yummy sandwich and some fruit. While they were eating, Tim heard a bird whistle. He looked up and saw the bird sitting in a tree. The bird looked very honest and friendly. Tim wanted to whistle back to the bird, but he didn't know how. Tim asked his mom to teach him how to whistle. She showed him what to do with his mouth and soon, he was whistling too. The bird heard Tim and whistled back. Tim was so happy that he could talk to the bird. From that day on, Tim loved to whistle and talk to his new bird friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Tim. Tim lived in a small house near a pitch. He liked to play on the pitch with his ball. One day, his ball went too far and got stuck in a tree. Tim started to worry. A big bird named Bob saw Tim and said, "Why do you worry, little cat?" Tim said, "My ball is stuck in the tree, and I can't get it down." Bob wanted to help, so he flew up to the tree to get the ball. But he saw a yummy worm and ate it instead. Tim waited and waited, but Bob never brought the ball back. The bird forgot about Tim and his ball. Tim was very sad and went back home without his ball. The end.
