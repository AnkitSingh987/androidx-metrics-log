Category: Intermediate
Title: The Wolf and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a good wolf. The good wolf liked to pick fruits from the trees. One day, while he was picking fruits, he met a little boy. The little boy said, "Hello, Mr. Wolf. Can you help me pick some fruits, too?" The good wolf smiled and said, "Of course, little boy. Let's pick fruits together." So, they picked fruits all day long. But when the sun went down, the little boy got lost. He was very sad and scared. The good wolf tried to help him find his way home, but they couldn't. In the end, the little boy and the good wolf stayed lost in the big forest. They were both very sad. The moral of the story is to always stay close to your family and not to talk to strangers, even if they seem good.

Once upon a time, there was a big boat. The boat had a big anchor. One day, a little bird came to the boat. The bird was scared. It did not know how to go home. The bird saw a kind fish in the water. The bird asked the fish, "Can you help me find my home?" The fish was happy to help. It said, "I will answer your question. Follow me, and I will show you the way." The bird and the fish swam and flew together. They passed the big anchor and went far away. They found the bird's home. The bird was very happy. It said, "Thank you, fish, for helping me." The fish smiled and swam away. The bird was not scared anymore. It was home.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to play soccer with his friends. They all went to the park to have fun. Tim was very good at soccer and he wanted to score a goal. During the game, Tim kicked the ball very hard. "I want to win!" he said to his friends. But one of his friends, Sam, was feeling bad. He could not play well and was sad. Tim saw Sam was sad and stopped playing. "Don't worry, Sam. We can win together!" Tim said. They both played together and scored a goal. They were happy and had a great time.

Once there was a girl called Sophie. She loved to explore. One day she opened a cupboard and saw a fine, fluffy cat. She said excitedly to the cat, "Hi there!" The cat looked at Sophie and purred. Sophie was delighted and asked, "What is your name?" The cat replied, "My name is Daisy!" Sophie said, "Let me introduce you to my family!" Sophie then took Daisy around and showed her to her mum and dad. They were both very pleased to meet Daisy. Sophie's mum then said to Daisy, "Would you like to stay here with us?" Daisy answered, "Yes, thank you!" And so Daisy stayed with the family and can often be seen sleeping in the cupboard. Everyone is very happy and they are all delighted to have Daisy as part of their family.

Once upon a time, there was a small bunny named Hoppy. Hoppy liked to hop all day long. He hopped over rocks, he hopped over logs, and he even hopped over his friends! One day, Hoppy saw a big chair in the woods. It was a very old chair. The chair was so old that it looked dead. Hoppy thought that the chair needed a friend. So, he decided to hop onto the chair and sit there for a while. As Hoppy sat on the dead chair, he met a bird named Chirpy. Chirpy saw Hoppy on the chair and wanted to join him. They both sat on the chair and talked. They became good friends. From that day on, Hoppy and Chirpy played and hopped together every day, and the dead chair became a happy place for them to meet.

Once upon a time, there was a strong boy named Tom. He loved to help his friends at school. One day, a new pupil named Lily came to his class. Lily was shy and needed help to make friends. Tom saw Lily sitting alone and went to her. He said, "Hi Lily, do you want to play with me?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, I would love to." Tom took Lily to his friends and they all played together. Later, Tom saw Lily trying to fold a paper to make a boat. She was having a hard time. Tom said, "Let me help you." He taught her how to fold the paper and make a beautiful boat. Lily was very happy. All the kids at school saw how kind Tom was to Lily. They learned that being helpful and kind makes everyone happy. The moral of the story is: always be kind and help others.
