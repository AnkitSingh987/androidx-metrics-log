Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a normal boy who loved to play with his toys. One day, Tim saw a big red ball in the park. He wanted to kick the ball with his toe. Tim asked his mom, "Can I kick the ball, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful not to hurt your toe." Tim was very happy and ran to the ball. He kicked it as hard as he could with his toe. The ball went very high and far away. Tim tried to run after it, but he tripped and fell. His toe hurt a lot. He started to cry. His mom came and hugged him. But his toe did not feel better. In the end, Tim had to go to the doctor because his toe was hurt.

Once upon a time, there was a big, heavy rock. It lived in a small forest with lots of trees and animals. The rock was sad because it could not move or play with the animals. It just sat there all day, watching the others have fun. One day, a friendly snake came by and saw the heavy rock. The snake said, "Hello, Rock! Why are you so sad?" The rock told the snake about not being able to play with the other animals. The snake had an idea. "I will give you a gift that will help you move!" said the snake. The snake gave the rock a special poison. The rock was scared at first, but the snake said, "Don't worry, this will help you move and play with your friends." The rock took the poison, and suddenly, it could move! The heavy rock was so happy and played with all the animals in the forest. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a bottle of lotion. The lotion was fake, but Mia did not know that. She thought it was real and wanted to use it on her doll's skin. Mia went to her friend Tom and said, "Look, I found lotion! Let's put it on our dolls." Tom looked at the lotion and said, "Okay, let's try it." They put the lotion on their dolls, but it did not make the dolls' skin soft. Instead, it made the dolls' skin sticky. Mia and Tom were sad because their dolls were sticky. They tried to wash the dolls, but the water did not help. Mia's mom saw them and said, "That is fake lotion. I will help you clean your dolls." She helped them clean the dolls and said, "Next time, ask before using something new." Mia and Tom said, "Okay, we will." They were happy their dolls were clean again, and they shut the fake lotion bottle and put it away.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Mark and Jane. Mark was an old man, while Jane was a young girl. They were walking to the park together. "What do you want to explore?" asked Mark. "The woods," replied Jane excitedly. The two of them reached the woods. Mark picked up a stick, while Jane grabbed a long staff. Mark used the stick to poke the ground, while Jane examined the staff. "It seems like a mild staff," Jane said to Mark. But suddenly, the staff began to glow and a powerful wind swept them away. They flew over the trees and saw a large castle in the distance. "Let's go explore the castle," said Jane. They entered the castle and were welcomed by a giant. The giant said, "You are forbidden to leave. I will take away your staff and stick!" Mark and Jane were so scared that they ran away. They were never able to get their things back. The moral of this story is that we should always be careful where we decide to explore. Even an ordinary object like the staff could lead to something unexpected and dangerous.

Sara is a little girl. She loves to visit her grandma. Grandma lives in a small house with a big garden. Sara is adventurous. She likes to play and explore. One day, Sara finds a pretty blouse in grandma's room. The blouse is red and has flowers on it. Sara wants to wear the blouse. She puts it on and goes to the garden. In the garden, Sara sees a big tree. She wants to climb it. Sara is careful and takes small steps. At the top, she sees a bird's nest. She smiles and waves to the birds. Then, she climbs down and goes back to grandma's house. Sara tells grandma about the tree and the birds. Grandma is happy and hugs Sara. They share a fun and adventurous day together.
