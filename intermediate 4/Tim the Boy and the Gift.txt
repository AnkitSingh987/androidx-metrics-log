Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Gift

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a gift. He could make things disappear. Tim loved to play with his gift. He would make his toys disappear and then bring them back. His mom and dad would laugh and clap their hands. One day, Tim's mom asked him to clean his room. Tim did not like to clean. He had an idea. He would make the mess disappear. He waved his hands and all the toys and clothes disappeared. Tim's room was clean. His mom was happy. But then Tim wanted to play with his toys. He tried to make them come back, but they did not. Tim was sad. He missed his toys. His mom hugged him and said, "Don't worry. We will find them." They looked everywhere and found all of Tim's toys. Tim promised to clean his room the right way from now on.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly dog named Spot. Spot was very happy and loved to play all day. He had a favorite toy, a silver ball, that he would chase and catch in the park. One day, Spot saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was wearing a pretty dress and she looked sad. Spot wanted to make Sue happy, so he ran to her with his silver ball. Sue saw Spot running towards her and she started to smile. Sue and Spot played with the silver ball all day. They laughed and had so much fun together. Sue even put a shiny ribbon on Spot's collar to thank him for being her new friend. Now, Spot wore the ribbon every day and they were both very jolly.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his family. They were all very happy. Tim saw a big tree and wanted to play near it. His family said, "Be careful, Tim!" While playing, Tim saw a silly looking bird. The bird had a funny hat on its head. Tim laughed and said, "Hi, silly bird!" The bird looked at Tim and said, "Hello, Tim! I am a talking bird!" Tim was very surprised. He did not know birds could talk. Tim asked the bird, "Can you come meet my family?" The bird said, "Yes, let's go!" When they went to Tim's family, the bird took off its hat. The family could not see that the bird was silly. They just saw a normal bird. Tim said, "This bird can talk!" But the bird did not talk. The family laughed and thought Tim was being silly. But Tim knew the truth about the talking bird and smiled.

Once upon a time there was a bunny called Sandy. Sandy wanted to find treasures to keep but all she found was dirt. She kept digging and digging and then she hit a mine. Sandy heard a loud bang, and it made her feel hurt. Sandy's mommy heard the loud sound and she came to see what had happened. She asked, "What's wrong, Sandy?" Sandy said, "I found something in the ground. It made a loud sound and it made me feel hurt" Mommy said, "Oh Sandy, that was a mine. Be careful and don't touch it ever again." So Sandy never touched the mine again. But she kept digging. And every day she found more treasures. She was so happy, her happiness began to increase. Sandy never felt hurt again and found many treasures to keep in her mine.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max loved to play outside with his ball. One day, he heard a sound coming from the other side of the fence. It was a small cat named Luna. Luna was playing near a grill. She was trying to get her toy that was stuck under it. Max said, "Luna, be careful! The grill is enormous and heavy." Luna listened to Max, and she stopped trying to get her toy. Max had an idea. He used his big paw to reach under the grill, and he got Luna's toy. Luna said, "Thank you, Max!" They became good friends and played together every day. The moral of the story is to always help others and be kind, because you can make new friends when you do.
