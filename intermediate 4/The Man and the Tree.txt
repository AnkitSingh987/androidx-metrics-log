Category: Intermediate
Title: The Man and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a man who liked to build. He built many things like houses and toys. One day, he saw a big tree with delicious red apples. He wanted to get the apples, but the tree was too tall. The man had an idea. He decided to build a ladder to reach the apples. He worked hard, and soon the ladder was ready. He climbed up and picked the delicious apples. He shared the apples with his friends and family, and they were all very happy. The moral of the story is that when we work hard and build something good, we can share it with others and make them happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a very lazy cat named Tom. Tom liked to sleep all day and never helped with anything. One day, Tom's friend, a wise old owl, came to visit him. "Tom," said the owl, "you must not be lazy anymore. There is a war going on, and everyone must help. Even you can do something to help." Tom listened to the wise owl and decided he would try to help. He went outside and found a big tree with a lot of branches. He thought he could cut the branches and use them to make a strong wall to protect his friends. Tom started to cut the branches, but it was very hard work. He was not used to working so much. But he remembered the wise owl's words and kept trying. Slowly, he cut enough branches to make a wall. In the end, Tom's wall helped protect his friends during the war. Everyone was proud of Tom, and he was proud of himself. He learned that even a lazy cat could be helpful when he tried.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the sun. One day, Tim found a dry log in his yard. He thought it was fun to play with the log. Tim put the log in his red wagon and pulled it around. He wanted to show his friends his new toy. But when he got to his friend's house, something unexpected happened. The log started to move! The log was not a log at all! It was a big, sleepy caterpillar! Tim and his friends were surprised! They played with the caterpillar all day. Then, they put the caterpillar back in the yard to sleep. Tim and his friends had a fun day with their new friend, the caterpillar.

Once upon a time, there was a rude door. The door never wanted to be opened. Every time someone tried to open the door, it would resist. One day, a three year old came to the door. The door was still very rude, but the three year old did not give up. She kept pushing and pushing until finally the door opened. The three year old was so happy. She ran into the room and smiled. Then, she turned around and smiled at the door. Finally, the door let out a tiny smile! From then on, the door and the three year old were friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big red van. The van had a nice man who drove it. The man was very proud of his van. One day, something funny happened. The van got stuck in the mud! The man tried to push the van, but it did not move. He was sad. Then, a big group of children came to help. They all pushed the van together. The van was out of the mud! The man was so happy and proud of the children. They all smiled and went on their way.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom had a toy sail. One day, Tom went to the park to play. He saw a girl named Sue. Tom wanted to introduce his toy sail to Sue. "Hi Sue, this is my toy sail. It is very clean," said Tom. Sue looked at the toy sail and smiled. "I like your clean sail, Tom," Sue said. They played with the toy sail together. But then, a big wind came. The wind took the toy sail and carried it far away. Tom and Sue were sad. They looked and looked, but they could not find the clean sail. Tom and Sue went home with a bad ending, as their toy sail was lost forever.
