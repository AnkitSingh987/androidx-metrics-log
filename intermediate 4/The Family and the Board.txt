Category: Intermediate
Title: The Family and the Board

Once upon a time there was a family who had a big board. Every day the dad would pour some glue on it. The mom was very bossy and always told them what to do. The dad and the mom asked the little girl and her brother to pour the paints on the board. The siblings worked together and started to pour different colors all over the board. The colors were red, yellow, blue and green. The mom was watching them and yelled to them to be careful. They put their hands in the paint pots and started to mix them. Suddenly, the paint was everywhere. The mom was so mad. She said they had been too bossy and made a big mess. But in the end, the dad smiled and said that the board looked beautiful with all the colors mixed together. They cleaned up the mess and hung up the board to admire.

One day, a lively cat named Tom saw a big red button. The button was on a box. The box had a word on it. The word was "print". Tom did not know what "print" meant. He was very curious. Tom decided to push the red button. When he pushed the button, a picture came out of the box. The picture was of a pretty flower. Tom liked the picture. He decided to push the button again. This time, a picture of a happy dog came out of the box. Tom liked this picture too. He decided to show his friend, a little bird named Sue. Sue liked the pictures. They decided to print more pictures and share them with all their friends.

---

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big cardboard box. He was very happy and enthusiastic. He wanted to make a house from the cardboard. Tim asked his mom for help. His mom said, "Okay, let's make a house." While they were making the house, Tim's cat, Fluffy, jumped into the cardboard box. Tim said, "No, Fluffy, this is not for you!" But Fluffy insisted on staying in the box. Tim and his mom laughed and let Fluffy stay. Then, something unexpected happened. The cardboard box started to move! It was not Fluffy moving it. The box was really a magic box! The magic box took Tim, his mom, and Fluffy on a fun trip. They saw many new things and had a great time. When they came back home, they were all very happy. The magic box was their new best friend.

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to run, jump and slide. They saw a big pile of leaves. They wanted to play in the leaves. "Let's make a big leaf house!" Tom said. "OK!" Lily said. They ran to the pile of leaves and started to build a house. They used sticks and stones to make a door and a window. They made a roof with more leaves. They were very happy. But then they saw a spark. It came from a match that someone had thrown away. The spark landed on the leaves. The leaves started to burn. "Fire! Fire!" Tom shouted. "Help! Help!" Lily cried. They ran out of the leaf house. They were scared and sad. They saw a man with a phone. He saw the fire too. "Stay away from the fire, kids!" he said. He called the firemen. He told them where the fire was. Soon, the firemen came. They had a big red truck. They had a hose and a ladder. They put out the fire. They were brave and kind. "Thank you, firemen!" Tom and Lily said. "You're welcome, kids. But you should be careful. Playing with fire is very dangerous. You could get hurt or hurt someone else. You should never touch matches or lighters. They are not toys. They are tools for grown-ups. Do you understand?" one of the firemen said. "Yes, we understand. We are sorry. We will not play with fire again. We will be safe and smart." Tom and Lily said. The firemen nodded. They gave Tom and Lily a hug. They went back to their truck. They waved goodbye. Tom and Lily learned a lesson. They were tired from the scare. They went home with the man who called the firemen. He was their neighbor. He told their parents what happened. Their parents were worried and angry. But they were also glad that Tom and Lily were OK. They thanked the neighbor and the firemen. They hugged Tom and Lily. They told them to be more careful next time. Tom and Lily said sorry. They promised to be more careful. They went to bed. They dreamed of a better leaf house. A leaf house that did not burn. A leaf house that was safe and fun.
