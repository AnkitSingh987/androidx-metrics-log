Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Room

One day, a little girl named Lily was sad. It was Wednesday and her room was very messy. She had to clean it all by herself. She looked at her toys and clothes all over the floor and didn't know where to start. Lily's mom came into the room and saw her sad face. "Why are you sad, Lily?" her mom asked. "I have to clean my room and it's too hard," Lily said. Her mom smiled and said, "I have an idea! Let's make a game out of it. We can race to see who can clean the most in five minutes!" Lily's face lit up and she was not sad anymore. They started the game and cleaned as fast as they could. Lily picked up her toys and her mom helped with the clothes. When the five minutes were up, Lily's room was clean and she was very happy. She learned that cleaning could be fun when you make a game out of it.

Anna and Ben were playing in the park with their toys. They liked to dig in the sand and make castles and roads. But today they saw something shiny and black in the sand. They were curious and wanted to see what it was. They used their shovels and scoops to dig around the shiny thing. They were careful not to break it. They worked hard and soon they could see more of it. It was a big metal box with a lock and a handle. They wondered what was inside. They tried to open the box, but it was too hard. They needed a key. They looked around the park, but they could not find a key. They were anxious and frustrated. They wanted to know the secret of the box. They decided to ask their mom for help. They ran to the bench where their mom was reading a book. They showed her the box and told her what they found. Their mom was surprised and interested. She said she had a key that might fit the lock. She followed them to the sandpit and tried the key. It worked! She turned the key and opened the box. Anna and Ben were excited and nervous. They leaned over the box and looked inside. They were disappointed and confused. The box was empty, except for some gray dust. It looked like ash. Their mom explained that the box was an old fire safe. It was meant to protect important things from fire, but sometimes the fire was too strong and burned everything inside. She said the ash was all that was left of someone's memories. Anna and Ben felt sad and sorry for the person who lost their things. They wondered what they were and who they belonged to. They wished they could reveal the story of the box. They decided to bury the box and the ash in the sand again. They said goodbye to the box and thanked it for the mystery. They hoped that someone would remember and love the things that were gone.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who liked to pretend. One day the girl decided she wanted to be a famous bubble blower. So the girl went to find some special stuff to pretend she was a famous bubble blower. First, she found a stick. Then she made a circle with the stick and some string. Next, she filled the circle with soap so that she could blow bubbles. And with a big blow of air, she blew her first bubble! The bubble went really high in the sky and the girl was so excited. She kept practicing and her bubbles got bigger and bigger. Pretty soon everyone heard about the little girl and her big bubble-blowing skill. Soon she was the most famous bubble blower in the land! The little girl was so happy and she spent the rest of her days pretending to be a famous bubble blower.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim was a good boy who always helped his mom. One day, he saw a cross on the floor made of dirt. He knew it was important to keep the house clean, so he decided to wipe it away. As Tim wiped the cross, a little bug appeared. The bug looked sad and said, "Why did you wipe away my cross? That was my home." Tim felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, little bug. I didn't know it was your home. I just wanted to help my mom." Tim thought about what to do, and then he had an idea. He picked up the little bug and took it outside. There, he found a nice spot in the garden and made a new cross for the bug. The little bug was happy and said, "Thank you, Tim!" From that day on, Tim and the little bug became best friends, and they always helped each other.
