Category: Intermediate
Title: The Rich Farmer and the Garden

Once upon a time there was a rich farmer. Every day he would work in his garden and tend to his animals. He was very happy with his life. One day, a poor man came to the farm. He was looking for work so that he could make money to buy food for his family. So, the farmer said, "Please help me in my garden today and I will pay you." The man was very happy, and he worked in the garden all day. At the end of the day, the farmer gave him some money. The man thanked the farmer and said, "Thank you! Now I can buy food for my family." The rich farmer was filled with happiness. He felt so good for helping the poor man. From that day on, the farmer let the man help in the garden every day. The poor man thanked the farmer each day, and both of them were happy.

One day, a little girl named Sue and her friend, a humble dog named Max, went to the park. They liked the park because it was quiet. They could play and talk without many people around. One day, Sue and Max wanted to discuss their favorite toys. Sue said, "I like my doll." Max said, "I like my ball." They talked and talked about their toys. Suddenly, a big wind came and took Sue's doll and Max's ball away. They were very sad. But then, a nice bird brought their toys back to them. Sue and Max were happy again, and they said, "Thank you, bird!" The bird smiled and flew away.

---

One day, a lovely bunny named Lily wanted to go on a trip. She knew she had to pack her bag with things she would need. So, she put her favorite toy, some yummy food, and a soft blanket in the bag. Lily hopped and jumped all day long. It was spring, so the sun was warm and the flowers were pretty. She played with her toy and ate her food under a big tree. When it was time to go home, Lily was very happy. She had a great day in the lovely spring weather. She knew she would go on another trip soon.

---

One day, a dog named Max went for a walk in the park. It was getting dark and Max was a little scared. He saw a big tree and decided to sit under it. Max saw a deaf cat named Luna sitting there too. Max said, "Hi, Luna! It's dark, can you help me get home?" Luna could not hear Max, but she saw his mouth move. She knew he needed help. They started to walk together. As they walked, they found a toy store. Max had an idea. He would pay for a flashlight to help them see in the dark. They went inside and Max paid for the flashlight. They were so happy and continued walking. Suddenly, they saw a big colorful balloon floating in the air. The balloon lifted them up and took them home. Max and Luna were safe and became best friends.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. They had a fun time playing and running around. Lily's mom had a big cookie for her to eat after playing. Lily was very happy and couldn't wait to eat the cookie. She admired the cookie because it was so big and yummy. While they were playing, a small dog came running up to them. The dog was very friendly and wanted to play too. Lily's mom said it was safe to play with the dog, so they all played together. The dog was very good at catching a ball and made them laugh a lot. After playing, Lily's mom said it was time to eat the cookie. But when they looked for the cookie, it was gone! They were very surprised. Then they saw the dog had the cookie in its mouth. The dog had taken the cookie when they were not looking. The dog wagged its tail and looked very happy. Lily was not mad, she just laughed and said, "That silly dog!" They all shared the cookie and had a great time at the park.
