Category: Intermediate
Title: Hoppy the Green Frog and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a green frog named Hoppy. Hoppy lived in a small pond. Yesterday, he saw a big rock in his pond. The rock was not there before. Hoppy did not like the big rock in his pond. Hoppy wanted to know how big the rock was. He used a stick to measure the rock. He found out that the rock was very big. Hoppy was sad because he could not play in his pond with the big rock there. Hoppy asked his friends, the fish and the duck, to help him move the rock. They all tried to push the rock out of the pond. It was hard work, but they did it together. The rock was gone, and Hoppy was happy. Now, they could all play in the pond again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a pretty marble. She liked to hold it in her hand. She was anxious to show it to her friend, Tom. Sue knew Tom would like her marble too. One day, Sue put the marble in her pocket. She went to play with Tom. They played in the park. Sue felt her pocket. The marble was still there. She was happy. She knew she would show Tom soon. Sue and Tom played all day. It was time to go home. Sue remembered the marble. She reached into her pocket. The marble was gone! Sue and Tom looked and looked. They found the marble under a big tree. Sue was so happy. She held the marble and showed it to Tom. Tom liked the marble too. They both smiled and went home.

Once upon a time, there were two little girls. They were very good friends. One day, the two friends went to the park together in the morning. They had a plan. The first girl said, “Let's go on the slide!” The second girl said, “Yes! And we should wear our helmets so we don't bump our heads.” The first girl smiled. “That is a very charming idea.” So they both put on their helmets and went to the slide. They had a wonderful time and when it was time to go home, they hugged each other goodbye. The two girls had a wonderful day and they made a plan to go back to the park tomorrow.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to skip and play outside. One day, Lily saw a big gate that she had never seen before. The gate was very important because it kept the animals safe inside. Lily wanted to see what was behind the gate. Lily asked her mom if she could go and see the gate. Her mom said yes, but she had to be very careful. Lily was so happy, she skipped all the way to the gate. When she got there, she saw a little bird who was sad because it could not fly over the gate to go back home. Lily knew she had to help the little bird, so she opened the gate just a little bit. The bird was so happy and flew back to its home. Lily knew that the gate was important and she had to close it again. She closed the gate and skipped back home, feeling happy that she helped the little bird.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved the color pink. She had a pink dress, pink shoes, and even a pink room. One day, Sue found an incredible pink rock in her yard. She was so happy and showed it to all her friends. Sue took the pink rock everywhere with her. She played with it, slept with it, and even ate with it. But one day, when Sue went to the park, something unexpected happened. She put the pink rock on the swing and pushed it. The rock flew high into the sky and disappeared. Sue started to cry. She looked for the pink rock everywhere, but she could not find it. Her friends tried to help her, but they could not find it either. Sue was very sad and cried all the way home. The pink rock was gone forever, and Sue learned to be more careful with her things.
