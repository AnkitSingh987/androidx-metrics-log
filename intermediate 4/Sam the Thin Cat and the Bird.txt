Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam the Thin Cat and the Bird

One day, a thin cat named Sam went to play in the park. Sam liked to twist and jump on the grass. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Sam climbed the tree and saw a bird. The bird said, "Hi, Sam! Do you want some sauce?" Sam was surprised, but he said, "Yes, please!" The bird gave Sam a little cup of sauce. Sam tasted the sauce, and it was yummy. He said, "Thank you, bird!" Then, they played together in the park all day. They twisted and jumped until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a brown automobile. It was a very nice automobile. It liked to go fast on the road. One day, the brown automobile saw a big tree with many leaves. The leaves were falling down. The brown automobile wanted to count the leaves. The brown automobile started to count. One, two, three, four, five. It counted and counted. It was very happy to count the leaves. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom had a big, brown sack. He liked to put his toys in the sack and take them to the park. One day, Tom met a girl named Sally at the park. Sally was sad because she lost her toy bear. Tom gazed at Sally and said, "Don't worry, I have many toys in my sack. You can play with them." Sally smiled and thanked Tom. They played with the toys and had lots of fun. Tom was a reliable friend, always ready to help others. Soon, it was time to go home. Sally found her toy bear under a tree. She was so happy! Tom and Sally learned that sharing and helping each other made them happy. From that day on, they were best friends, always there for each other.

One day, a little boy named Tom went for a walk in the deep fog. He could not see very far. He felt scared but kept walking. Soon, he saw a big tree. Under the tree, there was a small dog. The dog was lost and crying. Tom said to the dog, "Don't cry, little dog. I will help you find your home." The dog stopped crying and followed Tom. They walked and walked, but they could not find the dog's home. The fog was too thick. Finally, they saw a house. The dog's owner, a kind old lady, was standing at the door. She was so happy to see her dog. She said, "Thank you, Tom, for helping my dog. You are a good boy." Tom felt happy that he was not punished for going into the deep fog. Instead, he made a new friend and helped someone.

Once upon a time there was a girl who was very curious. She saw a big box made of cardboard and wanted to open it to find out what was inside. She found a charming little puppy inside. It was so happy when it saw her and she was so happy to meet it. The girl took the puppy outside and they had lots of fun together. They went on a walk in the park and they played with a ball. Then they climbed a tree and ran around in circles. The girl was so excited and hugged the puppy tightly. She called him her best friend and they both smiled. Back at home, the girl opened the box one more time. Inside, she found a special card for the puppy. It had magical powers and could make all their wishes come true if they opened it. So the girl opened the card and her and the puppy were so happy!

One day, a little girl named Sue went to visit her friend Tom. Tom had a big garden with many plants. In the garden, there was a big radish. Sue and Tom liked to look at the radish every day. One hot day, the garden was very dry. Sue and Tom went to the garden and saw the radish looking sad. Sue said, "Oh no, the radish is sad because it is dry. We need to help it!" Tom said, "Yes, let's give it water." So, they got water and gave it to the radish. The next day, Sue and Tom went to the garden again. They saw the radish was now very big and happy! But then, something unexpected happened. The radish started to talk! It said, "Thank you, Sue and Tom, for giving me water. Now I am a magic radish and can talk to you!" Sue and Tom were very happy and they all became good friends.
