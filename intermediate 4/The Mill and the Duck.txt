Category: Intermediate
Title: The Mill and the Duck

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a mill. The mill was very big and busy. People came from all over to see the mill. One day, a happy girl named Lily went to the mill. She wore a comfortable dress that was her favorite. Lily loved to dance and she danced all the way to the mill. At the mill, Lily met a kind duck. The duck liked to dance, too. So, Lily and the duck danced together. They danced and danced until they were tired. Then, they sat down and watched the mill work. They were happy and had a fun day at the mill.

Once upon a time, there was a modest kitten named Mimi. Mimi loved to bake. She baked yummy treats for her friends. One day, Mimi's friend, a little bird named Tweet, came to visit. "Hi Mimi," said Tweet. "What are you doing?" Mimi replied, "I am baking a cake. Do you want to help me?" Tweet was excited and said, "Yes, I want to help!" Mimi and Tweet baked the cake together. They mixed the ingredients and put the cake in the oven. When it was done, they shared the cake with their friends. Everyone loved the cake and they all had a fun day.

---

Once upon a time, there was a little bear named Tim. Tim loved honey very much. One day, he went to find honey in the woods. Tim found honey in a tree. He saw a big bee and said, "Can I have honey, please?" The big bee was ignorant and didn't listen to Tim. The big bee said, "No, you cannot have my honey!" But Tim didn't accept that. Tim tried to get the honey from the tree. He climbed up and up. But the big bee was very angry. The big bee called his friends and they all stung Tim. Tim fell down from the tree and cried. He went home without honey and felt very sad.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Harry and Tom. They were both so excited because they were going to watch a film together! They went to the cinema, picked their seats and waited for the movie to start. Suddenly, the cinema lights went off and the film began. But something strange was happening. The film kept skipping and moving too quickly. Tom and Harry didn’t understand. After a few minutes, a loud voice came over the speakers. It said, "We must completely stop the film now. It is not harmless!" Harry and Tom were very confused. How could something that seemed so harmless and fun suddenly become dangerous? Tom had an idea and said, “Let’s find out who is responsible for this. We have to figure this out!” So, Harry and Tom spent the day looking for clues and eventually they found the guilty culprit. It was an unknown creature that had sneaked into the cinema and had made the film incomplete. Harry and Tom were very brave. They confronted the creature and chased it away. Now the film was safe and complete and everyone in the cinema was happy. The end.

Tom and Lily are playing with their toys. Tom has a car and Lily has a doll. Tom likes to make his car go fast and loud. Lily likes to dress her doll and comb her hair. "Tom, can I play with your car?" Lily asks. She wants to see how it works. "No, you can't. It is my car. You have your doll," Tom says. He does not want to share his car. "Please, Tom. Just for a little bit. I will be careful," Lily says. She tries to take the car from Tom. "No, no, no!" Tom shouts. He pulls the car away from Lily. He refuses to let her play with it. Lily feels sad and angry. She looks around and sees a screw on the floor. It is thin and shiny. She picks it up and says, "Fine. I don't want your car. I have a screw. It is better than your car." She pretends that the screw is a magic wand. She waves it in the air and says, "Abracadabra! I turn your car into a frog!" Tom laughs. He thinks that Lily is silly. He says, "That is not a magic wand. That is a screw. It can't do anything. My car is still a car." Lily smiles. She says, "Maybe it can't do anything, but it can make me happy. And it can make you happy too, if you share it with me." She holds out the screw to Tom. Tom looks at it and thinks. He says, "OK, Lily. You can play with my car for a little bit, if I can play with your screw for a little bit." Lily nods. She says, "Deal." They swap their toys and play together. They have fun and forget their fight. They are happy.
