Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Tom Find the Park

Lily and Tom were twins who liked to watch TV a lot. They watched cartoons, movies, and games all day. They did not like to play outside, or read books, or help their mom and dad. They only liked to watch TV and eat snacks. One day, their mom made them toast with butter for breakfast. She said, "Lily and Tom, you need to eat healthy food and do some exercise. Too much TV and snacks are not good for you. You will become chubby and lazy and sick." But Lily and Tom did not listen to their mom. They said, "We don't care, mom. We love TV and snacks. They make us happy. We don't want to eat healthy food or do exercise. They are boring and hard." Their mom was sad and worried. She said, "Okay, but you will see the consequences soon. I hope you will change your mind." Lily and Tom ignored their mom and went to watch TV and eat snacks. They ate a lot of toast with butter, and cookies, and chips, and candy. They watched TV for hours and hours. They did not move or talk or laugh. They just watched and ate. After a while, they started to feel sick. Their stomachs hurt and their heads ached. They felt tired and bored. They looked at each other and saw that they had become chubby and pale. They did not look happy or healthy. They realized that their mom was right. Too much TV and snacks were not good for them. They needed to eat healthy food and do some exercise. They needed to play outside, and read books, and help their mom and dad. They needed to do more than just watch TV and eat snacks. They turned off the TV and threw away the snacks. They said, "Mom, we are sorry. We were wrong. We want to eat healthy food and do some exercise. We want to play outside, and read books, and help you and dad. We want to do more than just watch TV and eat snacks." Their mom was happy and proud. She said, "That's great, Lily and Tom. I'm glad you learned your lesson. Come on, let's go to the park and have some fun. And don't forget to drink some water and eat some fruits and vegetables." Lily and Tom smiled and hugged their mom. They went to the park and had some fun. They ran, and jumped, and swung, and slid. They talked, and laughed, and sang. They felt better and happier. They learned that watching TV and eating snacks were okay sometimes, but not all the time. They learned that balance and moderation were important for their health and happiness.

Once upon a time, there was a poor little pumpkin named Pippin. Pippin lived in a big garden with many other pumpkins. But Pippin was not happy. He was sad because he was smaller than all the other pumpkins. One day, a kind bird flew down and talked to Pippin. The bird said, "Why are you so sad, little pumpkin?" Pippin told the bird, "I am sad because I am smaller than all the other pumpkins." The bird thought for a moment and said, "I know! You need to drink more water. That will help you grow big and strong like the other pumpkins." Pippin listened to the bird and started to drink more water every day. Slowly, Pippin began to grow. He grew and grew until he was the same size as the other pumpkins. Pippin was so happy! He thanked the bird for helping him. From that day on, Pippin was a big, happy pumpkin who loved to drink water and play with his pumpkin friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play in his backyard. He was glad because the sun was shining and the birds were singing. Tim loved to run and jump in the grass with his dog, Spot. Tim saw a big tree in the backyard. He stared at the tree and thought about climbing it. Tim and Spot walked closer to the tree. They found a small, round hole at the bottom of the tree. A tiny bird peeked out of the hole and stared at Tim and Spot. They stared back at the bird. The bird was not scared. It chirped a happy song. Tim and Spot were glad to meet their new friend. From that day on, Tim and Spot visited the tree in the backyard every day. They loved to stare at the tiny bird and listen to its songs. The backyard became their favorite place to play and make friends.
