Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Coat

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a big, old coat that she wore every day. One day, Sue went to the park to play with her friends. She saw a little cat stuck in a tree. The cat was scared and wanted to escape. Sue said to her friends, "We need to help the cat!" They all tried to think of a way to help the cat escape from the tree. Sue looked at her old coat and had an idea. She took off her coat and said, "I will use my coat to catch the cat!" Sue asked her friends to hold the coat like a net. They all stood under the tree and shouted, "Jump, little cat! We will catch you!" The cat was scared, but it trusted Sue and her friends. It jumped from the tree and landed safely on the old coat. The cat was so happy to escape from the tree, and Sue was happy to help. The cat and Sue became best friends, and they played together every day. Sue's old coat saved the day, and she knew that it was very special. From that day on, Sue and her coat were always ready to help others in need.

Once upon a time, there was a parrot named Polly. Polly lived in a big tree. She had a very comfortable nest. Every morning, Polly would rise with the sun. She loved to sing and play with her friends. One day, Polly met a little boy named Tim. Tim said, "Hi Polly! Can you sing a song for me?" Polly was happy to sing for Tim. She sang a beautiful song. Tim loved it and clapped his hands. They became good friends. But one day, when Polly rose with the sun, she found that her tree was gone! She was very sad. Tim saw her and said, "Don't be sad, Polly. I have a surprise for you!" Tim took Polly to a new tree. It was bigger and had an even more comfortable nest. Polly was very happy and sang a new song for Tim. They played together every day in the big tree.

One day, a little boy named Tim went upstairs to play. As he played with his toys, he saw his cat, Max, walk into the room. Max looked serious and let out a big yawn. "Max, why are you so serious?" Tim asked. Max just yawned again and sat down on the floor. Tim thought Max might be tired, so he got a soft blanket for Max to sleep on. Max curled up on the blanket, and Tim sat next to him. They both yawned, feeling sleepy. Tim said, "Let's take a nap, Max." They closed their eyes and took a nice nap together upstairs.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was a very careful girl. One day, she went to the park with her mom. At the park, there was a big slide that Mia wanted to try. Mia's mom said, "Be careful, Mia!" Mia nodded and climbed up the slide. At the top, she saw a seat. Mia sat down on the seat and slid down the slide. She had so much fun! After playing on the slide, Mia saw a boy who had a frown on his face. Mia asked him, "Why are you sad?" The boy said, "I lost my toy." Mia wanted to help, so she looked for the toy with the boy. They found the toy under a bench, and the boy was happy again. They played together and became good friends.

One day, John and his friend Sue went to explore a volcano. The volcano was huge and impressive, but they were careful and patient as they hiked around it. At the top of the volcano they found an amazing view of the nearby village and jungle. But then suddenly, John noticed a dark cloud rolling in from the distance. John and Sue scrambled down the volcano as fast as they could to get away from the big, angry storm. But once they reached the bottom, Sue wanted to stay and explore a bit more. John wanted to go home, but Sue refused. So John got angry and slapped Sue on the arm in frustration. Sue started to cry and John felt very bad. They ran home together, avoiding the angry storm, but the damage had been done. John and Sue never became friends again.
