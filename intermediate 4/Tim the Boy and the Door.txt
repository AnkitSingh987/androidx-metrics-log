Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very naughty. He liked to play tricks on his friends and family. One day, Tim found a symbol on the ground. It was a big, red circle. Tim had an idea. He would try to use the symbol to play a trick on his sister, Sue. He took the big, red circle and put it on the floor in her room. He hid behind the door and waited for Sue to come in. When Sue saw the big, red circle, she was scared. But then, she saw Tim hiding behind the door. She knew it was just a trick. Sue laughed and said, "You are very naughty, Tim!" Tim and Sue played with the symbol together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the grass. One day, while playing, he saw something shiny and clear under a big rock. Tim wanted to see what it was, so he tried to lift the rock. "Mom, can you help me lift this rock?" Tim asked. Mom came over and helped Tim lift the big rock. Under the rock, they found a clear, shiny key! Tim was very happy to find the key. The next day, Tim and his mom went for a walk. They saw a little door in a tree. Tim tried the clear, shiny key in the door, and it opened! Inside the tree, they found a secret room full of toys. Tim's mom said, "Wow, Tim! Your key helped us find this special place!" They played with the toys and had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, there was a little human named Tim. Tim loved to watch the birds fly in the sky. One day, he saw a big bird with a sad face. Tim asked, "Why are you sad, bird?" The big bird said, "I am ashamed because I can't fly as high as the other birds." Tim wanted to help, so he thought of a plan. He said, "Let's play a game. We will jump and see who can go higher!" The big bird agreed and they started to play. They jumped and jumped, and the big bird started to fly higher and higher. Tim cheered, "You can do it, bird!" Then, something unexpected happened. As the big bird flew higher, it turned into a beautiful, colorful bird! The colorful bird was so happy and said, "Thank you, Tim, for helping me believe in myself." Tim was glad he could help. The moral of the story is to always believe in yourself and help others believe in themselves too.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily liked to visit her grandma's house. Grandma had a big, shiny crown. Lily loved to wear the crown and pretend she was a queen. One day, Lily went to visit Grandma. "Grandma, can I wear the crown?" she asked. "Of course, my dear," said Grandma, and she put the crown on Lily's head. Lily felt very happy and danced around the room. As Lily danced, she started to feel dizzy. She stopped and sat down. "Are you okay, Lily?" asked Grandma. "Yes, I just got a little dizzy," Lily said. They both laughed, and Lily took off the crown. They had a fun day together, and Lily went home with a big smile on her face.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a thoughtful boy named Tim. Tim loved to help others and make them happy. One day, Tim found a shiny crystal in the woods. It was very pretty and sparkled in the sun. Tim wanted to show his friend, Sarah, the crystal. He found her in the park and said, "Look at my pretty crystal, Sarah!" Sarah looked at the crystal and her eyes grew wide. "Wow! That is so pretty, Tim!" she said. A big boy named Max saw them talking and came over. Max looked at the crystal and said, "I want that crystal. Give it to me!" Tim didn't want to give away his crystal, but he wanted to be a good friend. So, Tim gave the crystal to Max. Max smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim. You are a good friend." Tim was happy that he could make Max smile.
