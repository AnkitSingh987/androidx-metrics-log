Category: Intermediate
Title: Joe the Boy and the Park

Once there was a boy named Joe. He was three years old. Joe's mom and dad gave him a bicycle for his birthday present. Joe was so excited - he couldn't wait to ride it! Joe's mom and dad urged him to put on his helmet before he went outside to ride. After putting on his helmet, Joe rode his bicycle up and down the driveway. It was so interesting to Joe - he kept riding and riding. Joe's mom and dad wanted to take Joe to the park. There were bicycles there, too. Joe was so excited - he urged his mom and dad to get the bikes ready so they could go! They all had a great time at the park, and Joe was so proud of himself for learning how to ride his bicycle. Joe will never forget the interesting experience and the fun day they had together.

---

Annie and Timmy were playing together in the park. Annie had lots of gear with her like a ball and a kite. Timmy had his own gear too! They took turns playing with the different gear. Annie was very good at kicking the ball but Timmy was very clumsy when he tried. Annie said to Timmy, "Hang in there! You'll get better!" Then Annie spotted some bats and said, "Do you want to play with the bats?" Timmy agreed and they took turns trying to catch the bats with their hands. They chased the bats all around the park but had a lot of fun doing it. In the end, the two had a great time playing together. Even though Timmy was a bit clumsy, he had lots of fun and learned a lot. Annie was happy to help him and they both had a fantastic time.

Once upon a time, in a big sand box, there lived a bossy ant. He liked to tell all the other ants what to do. He would say, "Move this sand here!" or "Dig there!" The other ants did not like it, but they did what he said. One day, a big rain came and washed the bossy ant away. The other ants were sad, but they did not know what to do without him. They tried to move the sand and dig, but it was not the same. They missed their bossy ant friend. Then, a wise old ant came to help. She said, "You can all change and be bossy like your friend. But, you must learn to work together and share the work." So, the ants learned to take turns being bossy and helping each other. They found that when they worked together, they could do even more than before. And they were all happy in their big sand box. The moral of the story is that working together is better than being bossy alone.

Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to pretend they were on a big stage and perform songs and dances for their toys. Lily had a red dress and a shiny necklace. Ben had a blue hat and a wooden guitar. "Let's do our best show ever!" Lily said to Ben. "You start with a song and I will join you with a dance." "OK!" Ben said. He strummed his guitar and sang a funny song about a frog and a fly. Lily clapped and laughed. Then she twirled and jumped and spun around. The toys cheered and clapped too. But then Lily saw something shiny under a bush. She stopped dancing and ran to see what it was. It was a big button with a picture of a star on it. Lily picked it up and pressed it. Suddenly, a hidden door opened in the bush. Lily gasped. "Ben, look!" she said. "There is a secret door!" Ben stopped singing and ran to see too. He was curious and excited. "Wow!" he said. "Where does it go?" "I don't know," Lily said. "But let's find out!" They held hands and walked through the door. They found a new world full of wonders and adventures. They performed for new friends and saw new things. They had the best day ever.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty little bug. The bug loved to crawl all day. One day, the bug found a big, soft pillow. The bug was very happy. It crawled onto the pillow and took a nap. While the bug was sleeping, a tiny mouse saw the pillow too. The mouse thought the pillow was pretty. The mouse wanted to sleep on the pillow too. The mouse crawled onto the pillow, but it did not see the bug. When the bug woke up, it saw the mouse. The bug was scared. The mouse was scared too. They looked at each other and then they laughed. They both thought the pillow was big enough for two. So, they became friends and shared the pretty pillow. From that day on, they always played and slept together on the big, soft pillow.
