Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Sue

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. It was a wet day, and the ground was full of puddles. Tim liked to jump in the puddles and watch the water splash up. He had a big smile on his face. Tim saw his friend, Sue, who was also playing outside. "Look, Sue!" Tim said, "I can jump high and make a big splash!" Sue watched as Tim jumped into a puddle. The water splashed all over her face, making it wet. "Oh no, my toy!" Sue cried out. The water had made her toy break. Tim felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, Sue. I didn't mean to break your toy." Sue looked at the broken toy and then at Tim. She knew he didn't mean it. "It's okay, Tim," she said. "We can still play together." And so, Tim and Sue played in the puddles, laughing and having fun, even with the broken toy.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to play outside. She saw a purple bird with delicate wings. Lucy wanted to be friends with the bird, so she tried to catch it. But the bird was too fast, and she could not catch it. Lucy had an idea. She filled a small tub with water to bathe the bird. She thought the bird would like it and come to her. She waited and waited, but the bird did not come. Lucy was sad. Then, something unexpected happened. The purple bird flew down and splashed in the water! Lucy was so happy! The bird liked the bath and wanted to be friends with her. From that day on, Lucy and the purple bird played together every day.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play in the attic. He found a big box with toys. He was very happy. Tim saw a small, old doll in the box. The doll looked sad and sorry. Tim said, "Hi, doll! Do you want to play with me?" The doll said, "Yes, please. I am sorry I look sad. I have been in this attic for a long time." Tim offered the doll a toy car. "Here, play with this car," he said. The doll smiled and played with the car. They played together all day. In the end, Tim took the doll out of the attic. They became best friends. The doll was not sorry anymore. They played together every day and had lots of fun.

Once there was a very confused little girl. She was wearing a bright orange sweater and she had a problem to solve. She saw a big tree and wanted to climb it but it was too tall for her. The girl was very excited but she was also feeling discouraged. "That tree looks great," she said to herself. "But how can I get to the top?" Suddenly, she spotted a ladder and smiled. She ran over to it and started to climb up. But then the ladder broke. She fell down and hurt her leg. She started to cry. The little girl crawled back home and was too afraid to try again. She was so confused and sad. She never found out how to solve her problem, but she always kept her bright orange sweater as a reminder to never give up.

Once upon a time, there was a little rabbit named Remy. Remy was a very silly rabbit. He loved to play in the grass all day long. One day, Remy found a big piece of paper in the grass. He thought it would be fun to tear the paper into small pieces. Remy started to tear the paper, but he didn't know that the paper was very important. It was a map that belonged to his friend, Tommy the turtle. Tommy was very sad when he found out that Remy had torn his map. He needed the map to find his way to his grandma's house. Remy felt very bad for tearing Tommy's map. He decided to help Tommy find his grandma's house. They asked their friends for help and followed the path in the grass. After a long walk, they finally found Tommy's grandma's house. Remy and Tommy were very happy. Remy learned that it's not good to tear things that don't belong to you. From that day on, Remy was more careful with his silly games.
