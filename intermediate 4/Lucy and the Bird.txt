Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a poem that she loved very much. But Lucy was selfish and did not want to share her poem with anyone. One day, Lucy went to the park and saw her friend Tim. Tim asked if he could read her poem. Lucy said no and ran away. She hid her poem under a big tree. Later, Lucy went back to the tree to get her poem. But it was gone! She was very sad and cried. She looked everywhere, but she could not find it. Suddenly, a bird came down from the sky with Lucy's poem in its beak. The bird had taken it! The bird gave the poem back to Lucy. She was so happy! From that day on, Lucy learned to share her things with others, and she was not selfish anymore.

Nick and Sally were two friends who loved to play together. Whenever they got together, they always found something fun to do. One day, Sally had an idea. She said, "Let's play a game! I'll count to three and then you have to clap." Nick clapped his hands and said, "What next?" "Let's make a row", Sally said. She explained, "We have to sit next to each other and put our hands together. Like this!" She demonstrated by clapping and showing Nick how to do it. Nick was an intelligent boy and he caught on quickly. They made a row and laughed as they clapped their hands together. They clapped until the sun went down and had so much fun the whole time. It was the best game ever!

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Amy. Amy loved to read books. One day, she wanted to buy a new book. She went to the store to find a cheap novel. In the store, she saw a lot of books. She found a cheap novel with a pretty picture on the cover. Amy was very happy. She bought the novel and went home to read it. When she started to read the novel, something unexpected happened. The words in the book began to move and change. The pretty picture on the cover came to life! Amy was surprised but also excited. She made a new friend from the book and they had many fun adventures together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play hockey. He had a big hockey stick and a small ball. He played with his friends every day. One day, a new kid came to play. His name was Max. Max had a big smile and said he was good at hockey. Tim wanted to trust Max, so they played together. They had fun and Tim was grateful to have a new friend. But then, Max took Tim's hockey stick and ball. He ran away and did not give them back. Tim was sad and cried. He learned that not everyone can be trusted. Tim missed his hockey stick and ball, but he still had his old friends to play with.

One day, a bald man named True went to the park to play. He saw a big, red ball on the ground. True picked up the ball and started to throw it up in the air. True wanted to catch the ball, so he threw it high up and tried to catch it. But the ball was very fast, and True missed it. He tried again and again, but he could not catch the ball. Then, a little girl saw True and wanted to help him. She said, "Let's catch the ball together!" They both threw the ball up and tried to catch it. With the girl's help, True was able to catch the ball. They both laughed and played together all day.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim had a long strip of paper. He loved to draw on it with his crayons. One day, Tim accidentally tore his strip of paper. He was very sad. Tim's mom saw him crying and asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim showed her the torn paper and said, "My paper is broken, Mommy." Tim's mom was very patient and said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can fix it." Tim's mom helped him restore the paper by taping it back together. Tim was so happy that his paper was fixed. He said, "Thank you, Mommy!" Tim's mom told him, "You're welcome! Just be patient and careful next time." Tim promised to be more careful and they both lived happily ever after.
