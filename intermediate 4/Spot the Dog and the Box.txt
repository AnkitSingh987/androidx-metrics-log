Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a friendly dog named Spot. Spot liked to play with his ball and run in the park. Spot had many friends who liked to play with him too. One day, Spot found a big box with a ribbon on it. The box was closed, and Spot wanted to see what was inside. He tried to open the box with his paws. He tried and tried, but he could not open it. Spot asked his friends to help him open the box. They all tried, but the box was still closed. Then, Spot had an idea. He said, "Let's guess what is inside the box!" His friends liked the idea. They all took turns guessing what was inside the box. Finally, Spot's friend, a little bird, guessed right. The box was full of colorful balls to play with! They all had fun playing with the new balls and were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little pony. The pony lived on a farm with many animals. The pony loved to run and play with his friends. One day, the pony found a spicy pepper. He did not know what it was, but he thought it was food. The pony took a big bite of the spicy pepper. Suddenly, his mouth felt hot and he did not like it at all. He ran to the farm to find help. The other animals saw the pony and wanted to help him. They gave him a big glass of water to drink. The pony drank the water and felt better. The animals told the pony that he should only accept food from the farmer. The pony learned his lesson and never ate spicy food again.

Once there was a clever boy. He was always looking for new things to record. One day, he found a box full of coffee beans. He thought it was so interesting that he wanted to record it. He found some paper and a pencil, and he started to draw pictures of all the funny looking beans. He also wrote down what they smelled like. He was so proud of himself that he showed his parents his recording. They were so proud of him too. They decided to make some coffee with the beans so the family could enjoy it together. The clever boy agreed. That night, they made the coffee. Everyone thought it was delicious and they all enjoyed it together. The clever boy was so proud that he had recorded the coffee beans. He knew he would never forget them!

One day, a big bird named Ollie lived in a warm place. Ollie was an ostrich who was very graceful. He had a long neck and long legs. He liked to run fast and play with his friends. One morning, Ollie saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad because she lost her toy. Ollie wanted to help her. He asked, "Can I help you find your toy?" Lily looked at Ollie and nodded her head. She said, "Yes, please help me." Ollie and Lily looked for the toy together. They looked high and low. They looked near and far. At last, they found the toy under a big tree. Lily was so happy! She hugged Ollie and said, "Thank you, Ollie!" Ollie nodded his head and smiled. They became good friends and played together every day.

Jenny was a rich girl. She had lots of toys and friends, but sometimes she felt lonely. One day, Jenny asked her mum, "Can I get a new friend?" Mum smiled and said, "Yes. Let's go to the pet shop." Jenny was so excited! At the pet shop, she found the perfect friend: a puppy. Jenny was so happy that she couldn't stop smiling. Mum paid for the puppy and said, "What are you going to name him?" Jenny thought for a moment and then said, "Let's call him Attach." Back home, Jenny and Attach played all day long. They snuggled and cuddled and had lots of fun. Jenny and Attach were best friends and Jenny was never lonely again.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play together at the park. One day, Lily's mom says they have to move to a new house. Lily is sad. She does not want to leave Tom. "Mom, why do we have to go?" Lily asks. "Because dad has a new job, sweetie. He works with automobiles. You know, the big machines that go on the road," mom says. "But I will miss Tom. He is my best friend. Can he come with us?" Lily says. "I'm sorry, honey, but he can't. He has his own family and house here. But you can call him and write to him. And maybe we can visit him someday," mom says. Lily hugs mom and cries. She feels terrible. She does not like automobiles. She likes Tom. The next day, Lily and Tom say goodbye at the park. They hug and give each other a toy. Lily gives Tom her teddy bear. Tom gives Lily his truck. "I will miss you, Lily. You are my best friend. Don't forget me," Tom says. "I will miss you, Tom. You are my best friend. I will always remember you," Lily says. They wave as Lily's mom drives away in their automobile. Lily looks out the window and sees Tom waving back. She hopes they will see each other again. She hopes he will like her teddy bear. She hopes she will like her new house. But she knows she will miss him terribly.
