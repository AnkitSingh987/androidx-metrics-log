Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben and Lily and the Toys

One day, Ben and Lily went to the zoo with their mom. They wanted to see the pandas, because they liked their black and white fur and their round ears. They walked to the panda house and saw two pandas eating bamboo and playing with each other. "Look, mom, they are so cute!" Lily said, pointing at the pandas. "Yes, they are, honey. Do you know what they are called?" mom asked. "Um, panda?" Ben guessed. "Very good, Ben. They are pandas, and they come from a faraway place called China. They are very special animals, and we need to take care of them," mom explained. Ben and Lily nodded and observed the pandas with curiosity and admiration. They noticed that one panda was bigger and stronger than the other, and that he sometimes pushed the smaller panda away from the bamboo or the toys. "Why is he doing that, mom?" Ben asked, feeling sorry for the smaller panda. "Well, sometimes animals, and people too, can be selfish or mean. They want to have more than others, or they don't want to share. That's not nice, is it?" mom said. "No, it's not. We should share and be kind, right?" Lily said. "That's right, Lily. Sharing and being kind are good ways to make friends and be happy. Maybe the bigger panda will learn that too, someday," mom said. Ben and Lily agreed and hoped that the bigger panda would be nicer to the smaller panda. They learned that observing animals can teach them a lot about themselves and others, and that being cute is not enough to be a good panda, or a good person.

Tom and Mia liked to play with their toy ship. They pretended it was a big ship that sailed on the sea. They put some dolls and animals on the ship and made noises like waves and wind. One day, they decided to make a map of the sea. They took a big paper and some crayons and drew some islands and rocks and fish. They also drew a treasure chest on one island and marked it with an X. "Let's go find the treasure!" Tom said. He took the ship and moved it on the paper. "We have to be careful of the rocks and the fish. They might hurt our ship." Mia nodded. She took a small flag and put it on the ship. "This is our flag. It shows that we are humble and kind. We don't want to fight with anyone. We just want to have fun." They moved the ship closer to the island with the X. They saw the treasure chest and smiled. They took some scissors and cut a hole in the paper. They reached inside and pulled out some shiny coins and jewels. They spread them on the floor and looked at them. "Wow, we found the treasure!" Mia said. "It's so pretty and shiny. We are so lucky." Tom agreed. He hugged Mia and said, "We are lucky, but we are also humble and kind. We don't need all this treasure. We can share it with our friends and family. They will be happy too." They put some of the treasure back in the chest and left some for themselves. They put the chest back in the hole and covered it with the paper. They picked up their ship and their flag and went to find their friends and family. They wanted to tell them about their adventure and share their treasure. They were happy and humble and kind.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play at the park. One day, he asked his mom, "Can we go to the park?" His mom said, "Yes, we can go." At the park, Tim saw a big tree. He thought it was a useful tree. He could sit under it when it was hot. Tim also played on the swings and the slide. He had lots of fun. When it was time to go home, Tim said, "Mom, can we come back to the park?" His mom said, "Yes, we can come back again." Tim was very happy. He loved the park and the useful tree.
