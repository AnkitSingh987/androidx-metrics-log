Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Boy and the Mom

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Bob. He loved to hop around the house and make everyone laugh. One day, his mom said, “Bob, it’s time for your lecture.” Bob didn’t want to go to his lecture, so he ran and hopped away. But his mom was determined. She hopped after him, calling to him every step of the way. Finally, after some time, she caught up with him. His mom said, “Bob, I made some delicious cookies for you. If you come with me, you can have one.” “Oh, okay!” Bob said. He followed his mom to the lecture and listened, but all he could think about was those delicious cookies waiting for him at home. Bob learned a lot at his lecture, and when it was over, his mom took him home. When they arrived, there was a plate of the yummiest cookies Bob had ever seen. He thanked his mom and gobbled them up in no time. Bob was so glad he went to his lecture, not only because he learned something new, but also because he got a delicious treat in the end!

Lily and Ben were playing with their blocks. They liked to build towers and houses and cars. But they had a problem. They did not have enough blocks. They wanted more blocks to make bigger things. "Can we have more blocks, please?" Lily asked her mom. "Sorry, sweetie, we don't have any more blocks. You have to share what you have," mom said. "But we want more!" Ben said. He was not happy. He grabbed a block from Lily's tower. Lily did not like that. She grabbed it back. "Hey, that's mine!" Ben shouted. "No, it's mine!" Lily shouted. They started to pull and tug on the block. The block broke in half. They both dropped it and looked sad. "What did you do?" mom asked. She was not happy either. "We broke the block," Lily said. "We're sorry, mom," Ben said. Mom sighed. She looked around the room. She saw an old box in the corner. It was full of old things. She had an idea. "Look, kids, I have something for you. It's an old box of puzzles. Do you want to try them?" mom said. "Puzzles?" Lily and Ben said. They were curious. "Yes, puzzles. They are like blocks, but you have to fit them together to make a picture. They are fun and they help you solve problems. Do you want to try?" mom said. Lily and Ben nodded. They liked pictures. They liked fun. They liked to solve problems. Mom gave them the old box. They opened it and saw many pieces of puzzles. They were colorful and jolly. They had animals and cars and flowers and stars. "Wow, these are cool!" Lily said. "Let's make them!" Ben said. They picked a puzzle and started to fit the pieces together. They looked at the picture on the box and tried to match it. They helped each other and shared the pieces. They had fun and they solved the puzzle. "Look, mom, we did it!" Lily and Ben said. They showed mom their puzzle. It was a jolly clown with a big smile. Mom smiled too. She was proud of them. "That's wonderful, kids! You did a great job! You solved the puzzle and you solved your problem. You learned to share and to work together. I'm very happy for you," mom said. Lily and Ben hugged mom. They were happy too. They thanked mom for the old box of puzzles. They decided to try another puzzle. They had more fun and they solved more problems. They played with the puzzles until it was time for bed. They put the puzzles back in the old box and said good night to mom. They slept well and dreamed of jolly things.

Once upon a time there was a kangaroo. He was very fast. He wanted to complete a big challenge. He told the other animals about it. They thought it sounded really hard. The kangaroo started running. He ran very fast. He hopped past the birds, jumped over the fish and even raced the frogs. He didn't stop running until he was done. Finally, he had completed his challenge. He had run all around the world! The other animals congratulated him and cheered. They were very happy that he had managed to do such a big thing. The kangaroo smiled. He was very proud that he had completed such a challenging task. He knew that he was the fastest kangaroo around!
