Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Gifted Cat and the Hat

Once upon a time, there was a gifted cat named Tim. Tim was very good at finding things. He loved to play with his hat. One day, the hat was lost. Tim was sad. Tim looked everywhere for his hat. He looked in his house. He looked in the yard. He looked in the park. But he could not find his hat. Tim was very sad. Then, Tim saw a new friend. The friend had found Tim's hat! The friend said, "Welcome, Tim! I found your hat!" Tim was so happy. He and his new friend played with the hat all day. They were very happy together.

Once upon a time, there was a big van. The van was red and blue. Inside the van, there was a girl named Lily. Lily loved to dress up. She had many pretty clothes in the van. She liked to play all day. One day, Lily felt tired. She wanted to take a nap in the van. She put on her soft, warm dress and lay down. While she was sleeping, the van started to move. The van went very far away. Lily woke up and saw she was not at home. She felt scared. But then, she saw a big, happy dog. The dog was driving the van! The dog took Lily back to her home. Lily was not scared anymore. She played with the dog and they became best friends.

Once there was a clean cat named Kitty. She was very good at cleaning her house. One day, she saw a big dog named Doggy. Doggy was not clean like Kitty. He was dirty and messy. Kitty said, "Doggy, you need to be clean like me. I will warn you, if you don't clean up, my friend the lawyer will be mad." Doggy laughed, "I don't need a lawyer. I like being dirty!" One day, Doggy found a big box. He thought it was full of toys. But when he opened it, he saw it was full of soap, water, and a big towel. Kitty had asked the lawyer to help her teach Doggy to be clean. Doggy was surprised but he tried the soap and water. He found out that being clean was fun. He thanked Kitty and the lawyer for helping him learn something new.

Once upon a time, there were two friends named Max and Sam. They were playing in a dark room. Max was scared, so he asked Sam, "What should I do?" Sam said, "Be patient. I will find a way to get us out of here." Max looked around. He noticed a light on the wall. He asked Sam, "What should I choose?" Sam smiled. He said, "Choose the light. It will lead us out of this dark room." Max chose the light. The light lit up the dark room and opened the door. Max and Sam stepped out into the sunshine. They were so happy to be out of the dark room! Max thanked Sam for being patient and helping him find the light. They hugged each other, and ran happily away.

Once upon a time, there was a big, hairy dog named Bobo. Bobo lived in a small house with his friend, a little girl named Sally. They loved to play games that include running, jumping, and laughing. Bobo and Sally were best friends and always had fun together. One day, Bobo and Sally went to the park. They saw a big, red balloon floating in the sky. The balloon looked like it needed oxygen to stay up. Bobo and Sally wanted to help the balloon, so they decided to find something to give it more oxygen. They looked and looked, but they could not find anything to help the balloon. As they were looking, the sky started to get dark and scary. Bobo and Sally did not notice and kept searching. Suddenly, a big storm came and the wind blew the balloon away. Bobo and Sally were very sad and wet from the rain. They went home, feeling bad that they could not save the big, red balloon.

Once upon a time, there was a big gym far away. In this gym, many kids played and had fun. One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to complete a puzzle. The puzzle had many pieces and was very big. Tim was very excited to start. Tim asked his friend, Sarah, "Will you help me finish the puzzle?" Sarah said, "Yes, I would love to help you!" They both sat down and started to put the pieces together. They worked very hard and had a lot of fun. After a while, Tim and Sarah completed the puzzle. They were very happy and proud of their work. They showed their big puzzle to all their friends in the distant gym. Everyone clapped and cheered for Tim and Sarah. They had a great day at the gym.
