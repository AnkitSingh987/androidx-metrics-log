Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and the Sue

One day, Tom went to school. He was very excited. He was careful with his new bag. At school, he saw a girl. She was new. Her name was Sue. Tom said, "Hi, I'm Tom. Let me introduce you to our friends." Sue smiled and said, "Hi, Tom. I'm Sue. Thank you." Tom and Sue went to their friends. They played together. At the end of the day, Tom and Sue were happy. They had a lot of fun at school. They were careful when they went home. Tom and Sue became best friends.

One morning, Junie was feeling very excited. She was going to collect leaves with her dad. The leaves were all different colors and It was going to be fun. When Junie and her dad arrived at the park, Junie noticed something was strange. She saw a shy squirrel. Junie thought this was very odd, since she'd never seen a shy squirrel before. Junie and her dad kept walking, looking for leaves to collect. Then, suddenly, the shy squirrel ran out from behind a tree! Junie and her dad were very surprised. The squirrel helped Junie and her dad find the most beautiful leaves they had ever seen. They had all sorts of colors, and they were very beautiful. Junie was super excited. Thanks to the shy squirrel, she and her dad had the best leaf collecting day ever!

Tommy was a three year old who loved to play. He had a secret plan to have the best time ever. He gathered some old boxes, glue and paint and set to work. His mum watched with a smile as Tommy created a model. He was very careful to keep it hidden from her. He worked on it every day after school. Then one day, his plan was complete. It was a pirate ship with a flag that Tommy had proudly painted red. He was so delighted with it that he wanted to take it outside to share it with the world. He ran around the garden pretending to sail it. He made make believe waves and sang songs. He was the captain of a hidden model which made him very proud. That night, he carefully put it away, planning on when he would next make it sail.

Once upon a time, there was a driver named Tom. Tom was upset because he lost his bag. He needed to unpack his bag to find his toy car. Tom looked everywhere, but he could not find it. He asked his friend, Sam, for help. Tom said, "Sam, can you help me find my bag? I need to unpack it to find my toy car." Sam said, "Sure, Tom! Let's look for it together." They looked and looked, but they could not find the bag. Tom was very upset. He wanted his toy car so badly. They asked their friend, Sue, to help them look. But nobody could find the bag. Tom was very sad and upset. He did not get his toy car back, and he never saw his bag again.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there lived a little ant. The ant had a friend, a kind bird. They liked to play together under the warm sun. One day, the ant saw a scary spider. The spider wanted to catch the ant. The ant was very scared and cried, "Help me, bird! The spider wants to catch me!" The kind bird heard the ant and flew down quickly. The bird raised its wings and made a big sound. The scary spider got scared and ran away. The little ant was happy and said, "Thank you, bird! You saved me!" The kind bird smiled and said, "You're welcome, little ant. We are friends and friends help each other." From that day, the little ant and the kind bird played together every day, and they were never scared again.

Once upon a time, there was a big, high tree. In the tree, there was a little bird. The bird liked to fly and sing. One day, the bird saw a bug. The bug was on a leaf. The bird wanted the bug to come and play. The bird said, "Bug, come play with me on Wednesday!" The bug looked at the bird and said, "Okay, I will come and play with you on Wednesday." The bird was very happy. The bug was happy, too. On Wednesday, the bug came to the high tree. The bird and the bug played all day. They laughed and had fun. They became best friends. From that day on, the bird and the bug always played together in the high tree.
