Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Paint

One day, a little boy named Tim found a can of paint. He thought, "I want to paint my room!" So, he took the paint and a brush and started to paint his room. As he painted, he made a terrible mess. His mom came in and saw the mess. She said, "Oh no, Tim! What have you done?" Tim felt sad and said, "I just wanted to make my room pretty." Then, something unexpected happened! The paint on the wall started to reveal a beautiful picture. It was a picture of a big, happy sun. Tim and his mom were both surprised and happy. They looked at the picture and smiled. Tim's room was now pretty and the terrible mess was gone.

One day, a big dog and a small cat were playing in the park. They were happy and having fun. The big dog was strong and fit, and he liked to run fast. The small cat was not as fast, but she was very smart. The big dog saw a ball on the ground and wanted to play with it. He ran to the ball and picked it up with his mouth. The small cat wanted to play too, but the big dog did not want to share. He yelled, "No, this is my ball!" The small cat was sad and sat on the big dog's shoulder. Later, the big dog saw a tall tree with a toy stuck in its branches. He wanted the toy but could not reach it. The small cat said, "I can help you get the toy, but you must share the ball with me." The big dog agreed, and the small cat climbed the tree to get the toy. The big dog and the small cat played together with the ball and the toy. They learned that sharing is important, and it is more fun to play together. The big dog was happy that the small cat helped him, and the small cat was happy that the big dog shared the ball.

Once upon a time, there was an obedient dog named Spot. Spot loved to help his mom in the house. One day, Spot's mom asked him to clean the floor. Spot was happy to help. "Spot, please wipe the floor," said Mom. Spot took a cloth and wiped the floor until it was clean. He was very good at wiping the floor. Spot knew that if he did a good job, Mom would let him play with his favorite toy later. After wiping the floor, Spot saw a big stack of toys in the corner. He thought that if he put away the toys, Mom would be very happy. So, Spot picked up the toys and put them in the toy box. When he was done, Mom came into the room and saw the clean floor and the stack of toys. "Wow, Spot! You did a great job," said Mom. She was so happy that she let Spot play with his favorite toy. Spot was very happy too, and he knew that being obedient and helpful made his mom happy. And when Mom was happy, Spot was happy too.

Once upon a time, in a land far, far away, there was a big war. Many people were fighting. A young boy named Tim wanted to join the war. He thought it would be fun to help his friends. He was very brave. One day, Tim was walking to the war when he met a sad girl named Sue. Sue was very sorry because she had lost her toy. Tim decided to help Sue find her toy instead of going to the war. They looked everywhere and finally found the toy under a big tree. When they found the toy, Tim and Sue became good friends. They played together and forgot about the war. The war ended, and everyone was happy. Tim learned that being kind and helpful is better than fighting. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little red car named Zoomy went for a drive. Zoomy loved to go fast. He would zoom down the road, feeling the wind on his shiny red paint. He had a big smile on his face as he drove. Zoomy saw a long tunnel up ahead. He had never been in a tunnel before. He was excited and a little scared. As he got closer, he heard a voice. It was a friendly green car named Greeny. "Hi Zoomy!" said Greeny. "Do you want to go through the tunnel with me?" Zoomy smiled and said, "Yes, let's go together!" They both drove into the tunnel. It was dark and cool inside, but they were not scared. They zoomed through the tunnel, laughing and having fun. When they came out of the tunnel, the sun was shining bright. Zoomy and Greeny were happy they had gone through the long tunnel together. They continued to zoom down the road, excited for their next adventure.
