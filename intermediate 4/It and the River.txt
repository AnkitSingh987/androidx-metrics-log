Category: Intermediate
Title: It and the River

It was a dark night. Glenda was walking by the river when she heard a noise. "Who's there?" she asked. "It's me, David," a voice said. He was standing underneath a tree. Glenda smiled. "What are you doing here?" "I'm here to soak in the river," David said. Glenda laughed. "In the middle of a war? That's silly!" But David was determined. He walked into the river without another word. Glenda waited by the shore for David. She waited a long time, but she never saw him again. Glenda knew that he must have been hurt in the war. She never saw David again.

Once upon a time, there was a fireman named Bob. Bob liked to help people. One day, he saw a little girl named Sally. Sally had a frown on her face. She was sad because she lost her toy. Bob asked Sally, "Why are you sad?" Sally said, "I lost my toy and it is bitter cold outside." Bob wanted to help Sally find her toy. They looked around in the cold, but they could not find it. Then, Bob had an idea. He took Sally to the fire station. There, they found a new toy for Sally. Sally was happy and her frown was gone. She said, "Thank you, Bob!" And they played with the new toy together.

One evening, a big cat named Tom went for a walk. He liked to walk when the sun went down. Tom was a fit cat who loved to play and run. As Tom walked, he saw a tall tree. He wanted to stand on the tree's branch. Tom jumped high and landed on the branch. He felt happy and proud. But then, something unexpected happened. The branch started to move! It was not a tree branch, but a long snake! Tom was scared and jumped off quickly. The snake went away, and Tom was safe. From that day, Tom always checked before he jumped on a tree.

Once upon a time, a nice boy named Tim went to the zoo with his mom. They saw big elephants, tall giraffes, and fast monkeys. Tim felt very happy. At the zoo, Tim saw a big bag. He wanted to pack the bag with his toys. But, another boy named Sam also wanted to pack the bag with his toys. Tim and Sam did not want to share the bag. Tim and Sam started to fight over the bag. They pulled and tugged at it. But, the bag broke! All the toys fell out and got lost. Tim and Sam were both sad. They learned that not sharing can lead to a bad ending.

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. He saw an old tree and wanted to climb it. As he started to climb, he heard a small voice. It was a tiny mosquito. "Hello, Tim!" said the mosquito. "Why do you want to climb the tree?" Tim stopped and looked at the mosquito. He questioned, "Why are you talking to me? You are just a mosquito!" The mosquito laughed and said, "I am not just any mosquito, I am an old and wise friend. I can help you if you need it." Tim thought about it and then said, "Okay, old mosquito friend. Can you help me climb the tree?" The mosquito buzzed around Tim and said, "Of course! Just follow me and I will show you the best way to climb the tree." So, Tim and the old mosquito climbed the tree together. They had a lot of fun and became good friends. From that day on, Tim always listened to the wise old mosquito when he needed help.

Once upon a time, in a cold winter, there was an ugly duck. The ugly duck had no friends. All the other ducks did not like to play with him. The ugly duck was very sad. One day, the ugly duck saw a big group of pretty ducks. They were playing near the pond. The ugly duck wanted to play too. He tried to rush to them, but he fell down. The other ducks laughed at him. The ugly duck went away and cried. He was very sad. But then, something happened. The winter went away and the sun came out. The ugly duck saw his reflection in the water. He was not an ugly duck anymore! He was a pretty swan! The other ducks were surprised. They wanted to play with him now. But the pretty swan said, "No, thank you." He flew away to find new friends who would like him for who he was.
