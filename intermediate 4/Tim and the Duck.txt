Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Duck

Once upon a time, a curious little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big pond with ducks and boats. He wanted to sail a boat. He had a small toy boat with him. He put the boat in the water and watched it sail away. As the boat sailed, Tim met a funny duck. The duck said, "Hi Tim! I like your boat." Tim was very surprised. Ducks don't talk! But this duck was different. Tim said, "Hi duck! Do you want to sail with me?" The duck was very happy and said, "Yes, please!" They sailed the boat together and had fun. Suddenly, the duck started to change. It became a big, beautiful bird. The bird said, "Thank you, Tim! I am a magic bird. I was a duck because a mean witch turned me into one. Your kind heart and fun sail broke the spell." Tim was very happy to help his new friend. They played in the park all day and became best friends.

One day, a girl named Lily went to the store with her mom. Lily saw a pretty, expensive skirt. She wanted it so much! She asked her mom, "Can you bring the skirt home for me, please?" Her mom said, "No, Lily. It is too much money." Lily was sad, but she had an idea. She went to her friend, Tim, and told him about the skirt. Tim was good at making things. He said, "I can make a skirt for you, Lily!" They found some nice cloth and Tim made a skirt for Lily. It looked just like the expensive one! Lily wore the new skirt to school. All her friends loved it. They asked, "Where did you get it?" Lily told them that Tim made it for her. The kids learned that they can make nice things without spending too much money. And Lily was very happy with her new skirt.

Once upon a time, there was a man. The man liked to pack his bag. One day, he wanted to go on a trip. He packed his bag with food, clothes, and a big hat. The man went outside. It was a hot day. The sun was very bright. The man was happy he had his big hat. The hat kept him cool in the hot sun. The man walked and walked. He saw many things. He saw a dog, a cat, and a big tree. The man was tired. He sat down under the tree. He ate his food and took a nap. The man had a good day on his trip.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a little bug named Buggy. Buggy loved to crawl under the big green leaves to find shade from the hot sun. One day, while Buggy was crawling, he found a tasty leaf to eat. As Buggy started to eat the tasty leaf, he saw another bug named Buddy. Buddy said, "Hey, that's my leaf!" Buggy looked at Buddy and said, "But I found it first." Buddy was not happy and wanted the tasty leaf too. Buggy thought for a moment, and then he had an idea. He said, "Let's share the tasty leaf!" Buddy agreed, and they both ate the leaf together. After they finished, they crawled under the shade of the big green leaves and became friends. From that day on, Buggy and Buddy always shared their tasty leaves and played together in the garden.

Jim was walking in his garden one day when he noticed a big surprise. There, in front of him was an old scale! He was so curious! He ran up to it and looked all around. He noticed that there were lots of elderly people standing around it. Jim knew his grandparents were elderly, so he wondered why they had a scale in their garden. "What is it?", asked Jim. "It's an old weighing scale, Jim. Sometimes we elderly folk check our weight, explains Grandma. Jim was surprised that his grandparents weighed themselves and he thought it was funny. Jim chuckled and asked if he could try the scale. "You can, but be careful," Grandpa said. Jim stood on the scale and was surprised to see how light his weight was. Grandma and Grandpa smiled as they watched Jim use the old scale. It was a happy surprise for them all!
