Category: Intermediate
Title: Graceful the Cat and the Dog

One day, a cat named Graceful was in the house. She liked to jump on the couch. Graceful was very good at it. She was a graceful cat. Graceful saw her friend, Dog. Dog said, "Graceful, I need to jump on the couch too." Graceful said, "Okay, I will help you." They tried to jump on the couch together. But, surprise! The couch could talk! The couch said, "No more jumping, please!" Graceful and Dog were very surprised. They did not know the couch could talk. From then on, they played on the floor and had fun.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to a park. In the park, there was a yellow stage. Mia saw a big cat on the stage. The cat was taking a nap. Mia wanted to play with the cat. Mia said, "Hi, cat! Do you want to play?" The cat opened one eye and looked at Mia. The cat said, "I am very sleepy, but we can play after my nap." Mia was happy to wait for the cat. While Mia was waiting, a big wind came. The wind blew the yellow stage away! The cat woke up and said, "Oh no! Where did the stage go?" Mia and the cat looked around. They saw the stage in a tree. Mia and the cat laughed and played under the tree. They had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a rabbit. The rabbit was very powerful. He could run very fast. One day, he wanted to prepare for a big race. He knew he was the best, and he wanted to show everyone. The rabbit practiced every day. He ran and ran. He was sure he would win. But one day, something unexpected happened. As the rabbit was running, he met a small, slow turtle. The turtle wanted to race too. The rabbit laughed. He thought the turtle could never win. On the day of the race, the rabbit and the turtle started to run. The rabbit was very fast, and he was far ahead. But then, the rabbit got tired. He thought he could take a nap because the turtle was so slow. While the rabbit slept, the turtle kept going. He did not stop. When the rabbit woke up, he saw the turtle near the finish line. The rabbit tried to run fast again, but it was too late. The turtle won the race. The powerful rabbit learned that being fast was not everything.

Once upon a time, there was a painter named Tom. Tom loved to paint all day. One day, Tom felt very hungry. He looked around and saw a big red ball. He thought it would be fun to play with the ball. Tom ran to the ball and gave it a big kick. The ball went up, up, up in the air. A little girl named Lily saw Tom and the ball. She wanted to play too. Lily ran to Tom and said, "Can I play with you and the ball?" Tom said, "Yes, let's play together!" They took turns kicking the ball. They laughed and had lots of fun. After playing, Tom and Lily were hungry. They sat down and ate yummy snacks. They became good friends and played together every day. And Tom painted a picture of them playing with the big red ball.

Once upon a time there was a girl. She liked to imagine. She imagined a perfect castle. It was so big and so bright. The walls were made of gold and it had pretty windows. The girl wanted to explore the inside of the castle so she opened the door. Inside it was warm and had lots of candy. The girl was so excited! She found a big throne made of diamonds and a fluffy pink bed with a canopy. The girl ran around the castle from room to room. But when she went outside she saw something even more perfect. There were gardens of flowers and trees full of fruit. Everyone in the castle was happy. The girl wished she could stay in the castle forever. But then she woke up! It was just a dream. So the girl closed her eyes and imagined the perfect castle again.
