Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Lonely Boy and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a lonely boy named Tim. He had a red helmet that he loved very much. Tim liked to wear his helmet when he played outside. One sunny day, Tim put on his red helmet and went to the park. He saw a girl named Sue. Sue was skipping and looked happy. Tim thought that Sue might want to play with him. Tim walked up to Sue and asked if he could skip with her. Sue smiled and said yes. They both skipped and played all day. Tim was not lonely anymore, and he had made a new friend.

---

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing with her toy model of a graceful bird. She liked to make it fly around her room. She would wonder how it would feel to be a bird and fly in the sky. Lily loved her toy bird very much. One sunny day, Lily took her toy bird outside to play. She was having so much fun making her bird fly in the air. Lily threw the bird high up in the sky, and something unexpected happened. The toy bird did not come back down. It stayed up in the sky! Lily was sad and wondered where her toy bird went. She looked up at the sky and saw a real bird flying next to her toy bird. The real bird had picked up her toy bird and was flying with it. The real bird saw Lily and flew down to give her toy bird back. Lily was so happy and thanked the real bird. From that day on, Lily and the real bird became best friends.

One day, a little girl named Lily was very excited. She found a flashlight under her bed. Lily wanted to play with her new toy. She ran to her mom and said, "Look, I found a flashlight! Can I play outside with it?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can play outside, but be back before dark." Lily was so happy. She took the flashlight and went outside to play. She skipped around the yard, shining the flashlight on trees and flowers. The sun was still up, but Lily didn't care. She loved her flashlight. As the sun went down, Lily's friend, Tim, came over. "What are you doing?" he asked. "I'm playing with my flashlight," Lily said. "Do you want to play, too?" Tim was excited and said, "Yes, let's play!" They both skipped around the yard, shining their flashlights on everything they could find. They had so much fun playing together until it was time to go inside.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty bird named Bob. Bob loved to whistle all day long. He lived in a big tree with many cobwebs. One sunny day, Bob met a little spider named Sally. Sally was sad because her cobweb was broken. Bob asked, "Why are you sad, Sally?" Sally said, "My cobweb is broken, and I can't fix it." Bob wanted to help Sally. He had an idea. He started to whistle a happy song. As Bob whistled, the wind began to blow. The wind helped fix Sally's cobweb. Sally was very happy. She said, "Thank you, Bob!" From that day on, Bob and Sally were best friends. They played and laughed together every day. And sometimes, Sally would even whistle with Bob.

Once upon a time, there was a weak cat named Fluffy. Fluffy had a style that was very cute. Fluffy loved to play with his ball, but he would struggle to catch it. One day, Fluffy was playing with his ball when it rolled into a big hole. Fluffy was sad and didn't know what to do. He tried to get the ball out, but he was too weak. Then, a little mouse came by and saw Fluffy. The mouse wanted to help, so he jumped into the hole and pushed the ball out. Fluffy was very happy and surprised that the little mouse helped him. They became good friends and played with the ball together from that day on.

One day, a small boy named Tim found a long rope in the park. He wanted to play with it. He saw a fragile bird on a tree and smiled. Tim asked the bird, "Do you want to play with me and my rope?" The bird looked at the rope and said, "Yes, I want to play too!" Tim tied one end of the rope to the tree and held the other end in his hand. The bird flew down and sat on the rope. Tim and the bird played with the rope, but the bird was too fragile to play for long. The bird began to frown and said, "I am tired. Can we play something else?" Tim saw the bird was tired and said, "Yes, let's play something else. We can be friends and play together every day." The bird smiled and they played together in the park until the sun went down.
