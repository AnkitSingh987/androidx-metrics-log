Category: Intermediate
Title: Elle and the Bird

Once upon a time there was a big elephant called Elle. Elle was always very curious and she loved to take nice long walks. On this one particular day, Elle went on a walk in the park. Suddenly, Elle came across a small bird. Elle asked the bird why it was so little. The bird replied, "I'm too small to do exercise". Elle was confused, so she asked the bird what exercise was. The bird replied, "Exercise is when you do big things with your body, like running and jumping". Elle was happy to learn about exercise and thanked the bird for teaching her. The bird said goodbye and flew away, leaving Elle to go on a bigger and better walk.

One day, a little car named Zoomy loved to zoom around the town. Zoomy had a fancy red color and big, round wheels. He liked to go fast and pass all the other cars on the road. One sunny day, Zoomy went for a drive. He zoomed down the road and passed many cars. He felt happy and fast. Then, he saw a big hill. Zoomy wanted to zoom up the hill and see what was on the other side. As Zoomy zoomed up the hill, he saw something he did not expect. There was a big, blue car at the top of the hill. The blue car was fancy too, and it also liked to pass other cars. Zoomy and the blue car looked at each other, and then they both smiled. Zoomy and the blue car became friends, and they zoomed down the hill together. They passed all the other cars and had lots of fun. From that day on, Zoomy and the blue car always zoomed around the town together, and they were the best of friends.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play with toys and eat snacks. One day, they find a big box of peanuts in the kitchen. They want to make something with the peanuts. "Look, Mia, we can attach the peanuts with glue!" Tom says. He takes a bottle of glue and a peanut. He puts some glue on the peanut and sticks it to another peanut. "Wow, Tom, that is fun!" Mia says. She takes two peanuts and glues them together. Then she glues them to a third peanut. "I made a triangle!" Tom and Mia keep attaching peanuts until they have a lot of shapes. They have circles, squares, stars, and hearts. They are very proud of their peanut art. But then, they hear a loud noise. It is Tom's dog, Spot. Spot smells the peanuts and runs to the box. He knocks over the box and spills the peanuts everywhere. He starts to eat the peanuts. "Spot, no!" Tom shouts. He tries to stop Spot, but Spot is too fast. He eats some of the glued peanuts. They are hard and sticky in his mouth. "Bad dog, Spot!" Mia cries. She is sad that Spot ruined their peanut art. She helps Tom pick up the peanuts that are left. Tom and Mia decide to put the peanuts away. They do not want Spot to eat more glued peanuts. They hope Spot will be okay. They hug each other and say sorry. "Maybe we can make something else with the peanuts another day," Tom says. "Or maybe we can just eat them," Mia says. They laugh and go to play with other toys. They are still friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom loved to smile and play with his toys. One day, Tom had a new friend named Sam. Sam was very sorry because he had no toys to play with. Tom wanted to help Sam. Tom said, "Don't be sorry, Sam. We can play with my toys!" Sam smiled and they played together all day. They had so much fun and became very good friends. One day, Tom and Sam were hungry. Tom's mom gave them a snack. It was celery! Tom and Sam had never tried celery before. They took a bite and smiled. They liked the celery a lot. Tom and Sam were happy and continued to play and eat their snack.
