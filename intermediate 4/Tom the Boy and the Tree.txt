Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a boy named Tom. Tom did not like to eat green food. One day, his mom made him a big plate of broccoli. Tom did not want to eat it. Tom went to play outside in the rain. He got all wet. When he came back inside, he saw something strange. The broccoli on his plate started to move. It began to appear like a tiny, green tree. Tom touched the wet, green tree. It felt funny. He thought maybe he should try to eat it. Tom took a small bite of the wet broccoli. To his surprise, he liked it! From that day on, Tom loved to eat his green food.

Once upon a time, there was a big bunny named Bob. Bob loved to hop all day long. One day, he found a pretty egg. He showed it to his friend, the little bird named Sue. Sue was jealous of Bob's egg. "Why do you have an egg?" Sue asked Bob. "I want an egg too!" Bob was kind and said, "Don't be jealous, Sue. We can find you an egg too!" So, they hopped together to find an egg for Sue. As they hopped, they saw a big tree. On the tree, there was a nest with many eggs. Sue was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The eggs began to crack, and little baby birds came out. The baby birds said, "Thank you for finding our mom, Sue!" Sue was their mom! She had forgotten about her eggs. The moral of the story is: don't be jealous of others, because you may already have what you want.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She had a special toy that she liked to take with her everywhere. Every time she had the toy, it would talk to her. The little girl loved talking to her toy. One day, the little girl was playing with her toy when she heard a noise outside. She wanted to see what was making the noise, so she put her toy down and ran outside to check. When she got out there, she saw a bunch of big birds chirping. She was so excited that she started to talk to them. The birds were surprised to see her, but the little girl didn't notice. She just kept talking and talking - until eventually, the birds flew away. The little girl felt very sad because now the birds were gone. She went back inside and found her toy waiting for her. She picked it up and it said, "It's okay. I'll talk to you instead!" The little girl smiled and hugged her toy. She was glad to have something to talk to. From then on, the little girl and her toy were the best of friends. They talked all the time, and she never felt alone again.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to his mom. He asked, "Mom, can I have some sugar?" His mom said, "Yes, but only a little." Tim was very happy and patient as his mom gave him some sugar. Tim took the sugar outside to the garden. He dug a small hole and put the sugar in it. He covered the hole with dirt and waited. He thought that if he planted the sugar, it would grow into a big sugar tree. The next day, Tim went outside to check on his sugar. To his surprise, there was a small plant growing in the spot where he had planted the sugar. Tim was so excited! He ran inside and told his mom, "Mom, the sugar is growing!" His mom smiled and said, "That's not a sugar plant, Tim. That's a flower. The wind must have blown a seed into the hole you dug." Tim was amazed. He didn't know that he had planted a flower, but he was happy. He learned that sometimes, unexpected things can happen when you are patient and try new things.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She wore an adorable dress, with pretty colors. She liked to dress in fashion, even when playing. At the park, Lily saw her friend Tom. Tom said, "Hi, Lily! I hope we can play together today!" They played on the swings and had lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew Lily's dress up! She was so surprised! Tom laughed and said, "Now your dress is a fashion hat!" They both laughed and kept playing.
