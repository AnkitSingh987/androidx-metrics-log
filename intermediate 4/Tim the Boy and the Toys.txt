Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a bag. He always took his bag with him. One day, Tim felt miserable. He did not want to play with his bag. Tim's mom told him to mind his toys. She said, "Mind your toys, or they will go away." Tim did not listen to his mom. He left his bag in the park. He went home without it. That night, something unexpected happened. The bag started to move! It had a life of its own. The bag went back to Tim's house. It knocked on the door. Tim opened the door and saw his bag. He was very happy. From that day on, Tim always minded his toys and never felt miserable again.

Once upon a time, there was a strong lion named Leo. Leo had a big roar that he liked to use every day. He lived in a forest with his friends. One day, Leo found a bottle of shampoo. He didn't know what it was, but he thought it smelled good. He wanted to keep it, but his friend, the monkey, wanted it too. They both wanted the shampoo, and this made them sad. Leo had an idea. He said, "Let's see who can roar the loudest. The winner gets the shampoo." The monkey agreed. They both roared as loud as they could. Leo's roar was so strong that it shook the trees. The monkey's roar was not as strong. Leo won, and he got to keep the shampoo. Leo and the monkey were happy again, and they played in the forest for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time there was a boy who was very nice. He was three years old, and had a lot of chores to do. Every morning, he would have to rise from his bed and help his mommy and daddy. One day, he noticed something on the back of his bedroom door. It was a switch! At first, he was a bit scared of it, but then he got curious. He wanted to see if he could do something with it. So the next morning, he carefully reached up, grabbed the switch and started to turn it. Suddenly, something wonderful happened! All around his bedroom, the lights started to turn on. His eyes opened wide with surprise, and he was so grateful that the switch worked! From that day onwards, the boy was very excited to use the switch every morning. He was so thankful that he could help his mommy and daddy around the house. And they were very grateful too!

Once upon a time, in a small garden, there lived a persistent little girl named Lucy. She loved to grow things. One day, she found a tiny tomato seed. She wanted to make it grow into a big, red tomato. Lucy planted the seed in the ground and watered it every day. Soon, a tiny green plant came up. Lucy was very happy. She talked to the plant and said, "Grow big and strong, little tomato plant!" But one day, a big storm came and broke the little tomato plant. Lucy was sad. She wanted to restore the plant, so she asked her mom for help. Her mom said, "Don't worry, Lucy. We can fix it together." They put a small stick next to the plant and tied it with a string. Lucy kept taking care of the plant with love. Soon, the little tomato plant grew big and strong. It made a big, red tomato just like Lucy wanted. Lucy was very happy and proud of her persistent work.

Once upon a time, there was a happy little girl named Mia. Mia loved to play with her shape toys. She had a big box full of them. One sunny day, Mia took her shapes outside to play in the park. Mia saw a little bird stuck in a tree. The bird was sad and needed help. Mia wanted to save the bird, but she didn't know how. Then, she looked at her shapes and got an idea. Mia used her shapes to build a tall tower. The tower reached the tree where the bird was stuck. Mia climbed the tower and saved the bird. The bird was so happy to be free. Mia and the bird became best friends. They played with the shapes together all day long. From that day on, Mia knew that her shapes could do more than just make her happy, they could save others too.
