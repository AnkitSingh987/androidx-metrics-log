Category: Intermediate
Title: The Boy Called Jack and the Gate

Once upon a time there was a little boy called Jack. He was very independent and wanted to explore the world. One day, he decided to go out on his own. He walked to the gate near his house and opened it. He stepped out and was really excited. He stumbled on the grass, but picked himself up quickly. He was walking along when he saw a tree. He wanted to climb on it and see what was on top. Jack climbed the tree and got to the top. He was so happy that he had been able to do it all by himself. He looked around and saw lots of things he hadn't seen before. He carefully climbed down and walked back to the gate near his house. He knew he'd have to open the gate to get back in. But instead of being scared, he tried to open the gate by himself. After a few tries, he was able to open it and walk back into his house. Jack was feeling very proud of himself. He had been so independent and had done it all by himself!

One day, a smart dog named Max found a big stack of blocks. The blocks were many colors like red, blue, and green. Max wanted to play with the blocks and build something fun. Max started to build a tall tower with the blocks. He put one block on top of another. But then, the tower began to wobble and shake. Max knew he needed to reverse what he was doing. Max took the blocks off the tower one by one, reversing his work. He then built a new tower, making sure it was strong and safe. With the tower complete, Max was happy and proud of his smart work.

Once upon a time, in a tidy little house, there lived a kind girl named Lucy. Lucy liked to serve food to her friends. She had a big bell that she rang when it was time to eat. One sunny day, Lucy invited her friend, Max, to eat with her. Max came to the tidy house and saw the big bell. Lucy said, "When I ring the bell, we will eat." Lucy served Max a yummy meal. They both laughed and talked. After they finished eating, Max said, "Thank you, Lucy! I had fun." Lucy smiled and said, "You're welcome, Max! Come back soon!" And they played together until the sun went down.

One day, a helpful fisherman went to the sea. He wanted to catch some fish. He had a big net to catch the fish. The sun was shining, and the sea was calm. The fisherman saw a big fish in the water. He said, "I will catch you, big fish!" He threw his net into the sea. But the big fish was too quick! The big fish swam away, and the fisherman did not catch it. The fisherman was sad. Then, the big fish came back. It had something in its mouth. It was the fisherman's net! The big fish dropped the net on the boat. The fisherman was so surprised! He said, "Thank you, big fish!" The big fish and the fisherman became friends. They played and laughed together in the sea.

One day, a cat named Tom and a dog named Max were playing in the park. They were having a lot of fun. They saw a big sign that said "Pet Event Today." They were very excited and wanted to join the event. Tom said to Max, "Let's go to the event! We can do tricks and have fun." Max said, "Okay, but I feel a bit uncomfortable. I don't like big crowds." Tom told Max, "Don't worry, I will be with you. We will have fun together." At the event, there were many pets doing fun tricks. Tom and Max did their best tricks too. A man was there to record all the tricks on a big camera. Max was still a little uncomfortable, but he was happy to be with Tom. In the end, Tom and Max won a prize for their tricks. They were so happy! Max said, "Thank you, Tom, for helping me feel less uncomfortable." Tom said, "You're welcome, Max. We are a great team!" And they went home, excited to show their friends the prize they won.
