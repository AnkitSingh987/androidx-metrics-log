Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to travel with his old toy car. The car was red and shiny. Tim and his car went to many nice places together. One day, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue had a fine, blue bike. She asked Tim, "Do you want to travel with me on my bike?" Tim said, "Yes, I would love to!" So, Tim and Sue traveled together. They saw pretty flowers, big trees, and happy animals. They had a lot of fun. Tim's old car and Sue's fine bike became good friends too. And they all lived happily ever after.

---

Once upon a time, there was a kind boy named Jack. Jack was very generous – he loved to share his toys and food with others. One day, Jack went out to play in the park. Suddenly, he saw a funny thing appear. It was a small round ball sitting on the grass. Jack was so curious that he picked it up to have a closer look. It was very soft and bouncy. Jack thought it would be fun to share the ball with his friends. Jack picked the ball up and ran around the park, sharing it with all the children there. All the children were thrilled and soon enough everyone was laughing and playing together. The moral of the story is that it feels good to be generous and to share nice things with others!

Once upon a time, a unique girl named Lily had a box of pastel. She loved to draw pretty pictures with them. One day, she found a big tree and drew a happy face on it. The next day, Lily went to the tree to give it a hug. But, the tree was not there! She was sad and looked around. Then, she saw the tree walking! It was a big surprise! Lily was happy and wanted to hug the walking tree. Lily ran to the tree to give it a hug. But, the tree did not want a hug. It was mad because it did not like the happy face on it. The tree pushed Lily away, and she fell down. Lily was very sad and cried. The tree walked away, and Lily never saw it again.

One day, a boy named Tim saw a poor, old kayak near the river. He wanted to see where the river went. So, he got in the kayak and started to follow the river. As Tim went down the river, he saw a big frog. The big frog said, "Ribbit, where are you going?" Tim said, "I am following the river in my kayak." The big frog said, "Can I come too?" Tim said, "Yes, jump in!" They followed the river together. Then, something unexpected happened. The poor, old kayak turned into a big, shiny boat! Tim and the big frog were so happy. They followed the river all day in their new boat, having fun and making new friends.

One day, a big elephant named Ella went for a walk. She wore her fancy hat. She saw her friend, a small mouse named Max. Max had a big, green leaf. He liked to chew on it. Ella wanted to chew the leaf too. "Can I chew your leaf, Max?" asked Ella. Max said, "No, it's my leaf. You can't have it." Ella was sad. She wanted to chew the leaf, but she didn't want to make Max mad. Then, Ella saw a big tree with many green leaves. She took a leaf from the tree and gave it to Max. "Here, we can both have a leaf to chew," said Ella. Max was happy. They both chewed their leaves and played all day. The moral of the story is that sharing makes everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily liked to be organized. She always put her toys in the right place. One day, Lily's friend, Tim, came to play at her house. Tim was not organized like Lily. They played with blocks, making a big tower. But Tim did not put the blocks back when they were done. Lily was not happy. She wanted her room to be nice and organized again. She started to put the blocks back, but Tim wanted to play more. Lily and Tim played a game. They had to put the blocks back in the right place. Whoever put the most blocks in the right place won. As they raced to put the blocks back, Lily stumbled and fell. Tim stopped and helped her up. They both laughed and kept playing. In the end, they both won. Lily's room was organized again, and they had fun playing the game.
