Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and the Glove

Once upon a time, in a small town, a boy named Tom had a plan. He wanted to find his lost glove. It was a good glove. It was red and kept his hand warm. Tom felt sad without his glove. One day, Tom saw a dog running with a red thing in its mouth. He thought it was his good glove! Tom ran after the dog. But, oh no! The dog was fast and Tom fell down. He felt bad and his plan did not work. Then, something unexpected happened. A girl named Sue came with the dog. She said, "Is this your glove?" Tom was so happy! He said, "Yes, it is my good glove!" Sue was kind and helped Tom. They became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a warm little house in the woods. In the house lived a girl named Lily and her mom. They loved to play and laugh together. One day, Lily found a shiny gem in the garden. She ran to her mom and said, "Look, mom! I found a gem! Can I keep it, please?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, you can keep it, but be careful not to lose it." Lily was so happy with her gem. She played with it all day and slept with it at night. The gem made her feel warm and happy. And every time she looked at it, she said, "Thank you, gem, for making me feel warm and happy."

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to help his dad with jobs around the house. His dad was very dependable and always had the right tool to complete the job. One day, Tim's dad said, "Tim, let's fix the chair!" Tim was so happy and said, "Yes, Dad!" They went to the garage to find the right tool. The tool they needed was a hammer. As they started to fix the chair, a small bird flew into the garage. The bird said, "Can I help too?" Tim and his dad were very surprised! They had never seen a talking bird before. They said, "Yes, you can help us!" With the help of the talking bird, Tim and his dad quickly completed fixing the chair. They were so happy to have a new friend who could talk and help them. From that day on, Tim, his dad, and the talking bird worked on many jobs together, always using the right tool and being dependable friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little puppy named Spot. Spot loved to play outside in the sun. He would run and jump all day long. Spot had many friends who would enjoy playing with him. They would laugh and have fun together. One day, the sky was full of dark clouds. It started to rain and the ground became wet. Spot didn't mind the rain. He liked to play in the wet grass and splash in the puddles. His friends would watch him and giggle at how silly he looked. Spot's friends decided to join him and play in the wet rain. They all enjoyed getting wet and splashing in the puddles together. After a while, the rain stopped and the sun came out. Spot and his friends were happy and tired from their fun, wet day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He did not like to eat broccoli. His mom always said, "Eat your broccoli, Tim!" But Tim would pinch his nose and say, "No, I don't like it." One day, Tim met a big, green, friendly monster named Bob. Bob was deaf, so he could not hear what Tim said. Tim showed Bob the broccoli and made a sad face. Bob smiled and ate the broccoli. It was gone in one big bite! Then, something unexpected happened. Bob's tummy started to glow! Tim was so surprised. He tried eating the broccoli too, and his tummy also glowed. They both laughed and danced with their glowing tummies. From that day on, Tim loved eating broccoli.

Once there was a sad whale. Every morning it would yawn and stretch, before going off to explore the sea. One day, it spotted something different - a little boat full of children. The children saw the whale and waved and shouted, but the whale was too shy to come closer. It watched them for a while, then swam away sadly. The next morning, the whale yawned and saw the boat again. This time it swam to meet the children, and they all had lots of fun together. They laughed and laughed until it was time to go home. The whale waved goodbye, and they waved back. As the boat sailed away, the whale knew they would meet again.
