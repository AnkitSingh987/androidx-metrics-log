Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna and the Tree

Anna liked to climb the big tree in her backyard. She felt like a bird when she was high up in the branches. She could see the whole street and the other houses. Sometimes she saw her friends playing outside and waved to them. One day, she saw a new image on the wall of the house next to hers. It was a big, scary face with angry eyes and a mean mouth. It looked like it was shouting at her. Anna felt troubled. She did not like the image. She wondered who made it and why. She climbed down the tree and ran to her mom, who was in the kitchen. "Mom, mom, there is a bad image on the wall!" she said. "It scares me!" Her mom looked out the window and saw the image. She frowned. "That is not a nice image, Anna. Someone painted it with spray cans. They should not do that. It is not their wall." "Can we make it go away, mom?" Anna asked. Her mom nodded. "Yes, we can. Come on, let's go talk to our neighbor. Maybe he has some paint to cover it up. Or maybe he knows who did it and can tell them to stop." Anna felt better. She held her mom's hand and walked to the neighbor's door. She hoped they could make the image go away soon. She wanted to climb the tree again and see nice things.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a mill. The mill was very big and fancy. People in the town would use the mill to make flour. One day, a girl named Sue went to the mill with her dad. Sue said to her dad, "Dad, can I help use the mill today?" Her dad said, "Yes, Sue, you can help me." They went inside the mill and started to work. They used the mill to make lots of flour for the town. Just then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the mill's doors open. A big, friendly dog ran inside. The dog said, "Hi, I can help you use the mill too!" Sue, her dad, and the dog all worked together and made even more flour for the town. They all became very good friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a nice man named Tom. Tom liked to sell paper to people. He had a little shop where he sold many colors of paper. Kids and grown-ups all loved to buy paper from Tom's shop. One day, a little girl named Lily came to Tom's shop. She wanted to buy some paper to make a gift for her mom. Tom showed Lily all the pretty papers he had. Lily picked a pink paper, a blue paper, and a yellow paper. Lily went home and made a beautiful drawing with the papers she bought from Tom's shop. Her mom loved the gift and gave Lily a big hug. From that day on, Lily always went to Tom's nice shop to buy more paper for her art. Everyone in the town was happy because Tom's paper made their lives colorful and fun.

Once upon a time, there was a happy family. They lived in a small house. There was a mom, a dad, a little boy, and a little girl. They all loved each other very much. They also had a pet dog. The dog was deaf. He could not hear, but he was still a good dog. One day, the family had a big dinner. They ate yummy food. They all helped to clean up. The little boy and the little girl helped their mom and dad. They put the dirty plates in the dishwasher. The dishwasher made the plates clean. The deaf dog wanted to help too. He tried to manage the dishwasher. He pushed the buttons with his nose. The family laughed and clapped. The dog felt happy. They all worked together to clean up. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Suzy. Suzy lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and her dog, Max. Suzy loved to help her mom and dad with chores. One day, Suzy's mom asked her to join her in the kitchen to help her wash the dishes. Suzy was very happy to help. "Suzy, please put the dirty dishes in the dishwasher," said Mom. Suzy tried to open the dishwasher, but it was very stubborn and would not open. Suzy asked Max to help her. Max was a strong dog and loved to help Suzy. Max used his nose to push the dishwasher door open. Suzy said, "Thank you, Max! You are a very good dog." Suzy and Max put the dirty dishes in the dishwasher together. Suzy's mom was very happy that they worked as a team. After they finished, Suzy, Max, and Mom went outside to play in the sun. They had a fun day together, and Suzy learned that even a stubborn dishwasher could not stop her and Max from helping.
