Category: Intermediate
Title: Peter and Jimmy and the Leaves

Once upon a time, there were two friends: Peter and Jimmy. They were walking in the park and saw a puddle of mud. Peter wanted to jump in it, but Jimmy said: "It's not a good idea, Peter. The mud will make us very dirty". Peter said: "I don't care! I still want to jump in it!" Jimmy was getting worried, so he asked: "Why do you want to jump in it? It's not very clean". Peter thought for a moment and then said: "Because I love counting! We could count how many green leaves there are in the puddle". Jimmy thought that wasn't a bad idea, so he said: "But the leaves might move when you jump in, so it won't be easy to count them". Peter said: "It's okay, I'll try to be careful". So, Jimmy and Peter started counting the leaves. After they finished they realized that mud was not as fun as they thought. In the end, the two friends learned a lesson: it's better to be clean than to have fun!

One day, a gifted boy named Tom found a magic book. The book had many signs in it. The signs could make fun things happen. Tom showed the magic book to his friend, Ann. Together, they tried a sign. They made a big, yummy cake appear. They were very happy and ate the cake. Tom and Ann used the magic book to make more fun things happen. They made toys, flowers, and even a small pool to play in. They had a great day with the magic book and its signs.

Once upon a time, there was a big metal box. A little boy named Tim found the box in his yard. He wanted to know what was inside. Tim tried to open the box, but it was too heavy. He asked his friend, Sam, for help. "Sam, can you help me open this big metal box?" Tim asked. "Sure, let's try together!" Sam said. They both pushed and pulled, but the box still did not open. Tim had an idea. He found a key that fit the lock on the box. They turned the key and the box opened. Inside, they found fun toys to play with. Tim and Sam played with the toys all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a purple car. The car had a big motor. The motor made the car go fast. Everyone liked the purple car. One day, the purple car found a big balloon. The car wanted to attach the balloon to itself. The car thought it would be fun to fly in the sky. So, the purple car attached the big balloon to its top. With the big balloon attached, the purple car started to go up, up, up into the sky. The car used its motor to go even higher. The purple car had so much fun flying high. Everyone looked up and smiled at the purple car in the sky.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Lucy. All she wanted was to find a new boot she had lost. She was walking around the park when she noticed a dead bird on the ground. Lucy looked around to see where it had come from, and noticed a boot lying in the bushes. She went over to it and found that it was the same boot she had lost! Lucy said with delight: “I found it! I found my lost boot!” She put her boot back on and went home. From then on, she was more careful not to lose her boot again.

Once upon a time, there was a smelly skull. It lived in a big box. The skull had no friends because it was too smelly. Every day, it would spend time alone in its box, feeling sad. One sunny day, a little dog came to the box. The dog was not scared of the smelly skull. The dog liked the skull and wanted to be friends. The skull was very happy to have a new friend. They spent time playing together. Then, something unexpected happened. The dog went away and came back with a big flower. The dog put the flower on top of the smelly skull. The flower made the skull smell nice. Now, the skull had many friends and was not alone anymore. The skull and the dog were best friends forever.
