Category: Intermediate
Title: Beef the Cow and the Hat

Once upon a time, there was a cow named Beef. Beef lived on a big farm with many other animals. They all liked to play and have fun together. Beef had a fancy red hat that he loved to wear. One day, Beef was playing with his friends when he met a new animal. It was a small bird named Tweet. Tweet said, "Hi, Beef! I like your fancy hat!" Beef smiled and said, "Thank you, Tweet. Do you want to play with us?" Tweet was very happy and joined the fun. As they played, Beef noticed that he could number how many times Tweet could fly up and down. Beef counted, "One, two, three..." Suddenly, Tweet flew up very high and took Beef's fancy hat! Beef and his friends were surprised. They never thought Tweet would take the hat. But then, Tweet flew back down and put the fancy hat on Beef's head. Tweet said, "Now your hat is even fancier!" Beef and his friends laughed and continued to play, now with a fun new game.

One day, a little red car named Tommy was driving down the road. He saw a big chimney on a house. Tommy was curious and wanted to see the chimney up close. He drove closer and closer to the house. Tommy said, "Hi, chimney! You are very big!" The chimney smiled and said, "Thank you, Tommy! I help keep the house warm." Tommy felt happy to meet the chimney. But then, Tommy got stuck in the mud near the house. He tried to move, but his wheels just went round and round. Tommy felt frustrated. Tommy thought for a moment and then remembered the word "reverse." He said to himself, "I will try to reverse and get out of the mud." So, Tommy reversed his wheels, and he was free from the mud! Tommy was very happy and thanked the chimney for being a good friend. The chimney smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tommy! Have a great day!"

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to a restaurant with her mom and dad. They sat down and looked at the menu. Lucy thought the menu was boring. She wanted to play with her toy car instead. Lucy said, "Mom, can I play with my car?" Her mom said, "Yes, but don't lose it." Lucy played with her car under the table. She made it go fast and slow, and she made it go around in circles. Then, Lucy's car rolled away and she could not find it. She was sad because she lost her car. Lucy's dad got up and looked for the car. He found it under another table. Lucy was happy to have her car back. She learned to be careful and not lose her toys again.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max liked to play with his equipment. He had a ball, a rope, and a toy bone. Max was very happy when he played with his toys. One day, a little cat named Kitty came into Max's yard. Kitty saw Max's equipment and wanted to play too. But Max didn't want to share. He started to tease Kitty by taking the toys away when Kitty tried to play with them. Kitty was very sad and went away. Max was happy to have his equipment all to himself. But soon, Max got bored playing alone. He looked for Kitty, but Kitty was gone. Max was sad and wished he had been nice to Kitty. Now, he had no one to play with and his big yard felt empty.

One day, a graceful cat went for a walk. The cat had big, pretty eyes. The cat liked to play and look at things. The cat saw a box in the park. It was a big, red box. The cat was very curious. It wanted to see what was inside the box. So, the cat jumped on the box and opened it with its paw. When the cat opened the box, it revealed a lot of colorful balls. The cat's eyes got very big and happy. The cat played with the balls all day and had lots of fun.
