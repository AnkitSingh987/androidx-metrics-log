Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Lucy. Lucy was very smart. She liked to play with her ball and her toy mouse. Lucy had soft fur on her skin. She loved when her family would pet her. One day, Lucy's ball rolled under the big chair. She tried to get it, but her paw could not reach. Lucy thought about how she could get the ball out. She was very intelligent and wanted to manage it by herself. Lucy found a long stick in the yard. She brought it into the house. Lucy used the stick to push the ball out from under the chair. She managed to get her ball back! Lucy was so happy that she could play again. Her family was proud of their smart little cat.

Once upon a time there was a bird named Ziggy. He was a very happy bird, but he wished he could fly. He asked his mum and dad if he could fly, but they said no. Ziggy was sad and he tried and tried to fly, but he just couldn't. One day he saw a zigzag. He thought it looked fun and he wanted to try it. He asked his mum, but she said no. She said it looked too hard and that it might hurt him. Ziggy didn't understand, so he tried it anyway. But it was too hard. He hurt himself when he tried to make the zigzag. He was so sad and he cried. His mum and dad were worried and they did their best to help him stop hurting. But Ziggy could never make that zigzag again. He was too scared and he never tried to fly again either. Ziggy was never the same happy bird again.

One day, a little dog named Max went for a walk. He liked to walk on the high hill next to his home. On the side of the hill, he saw a big tree. Max wanted to play with the tree, so he ran and jumped around it. As Max was jumping, he heard a loud snap. He looked around and saw a big bird fall from the sky. The bird was sad because it hurt its wing. Max wanted to help the bird, so he ran to find his friend, Cat. Max found Cat and told her about the bird. Cat was very smart and had an idea. She told Max to get some sticks. Together, they used the sticks to fix the bird's wing. The bird was so happy and flew away. But instead of flying away, the bird stayed with Max and Cat. They all became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big puzzle with many pieces. The puzzle was very special. It was available for all kids to play with. The puzzle had a big sun that would shine when it was finished. One day, a little girl named Lily saw the puzzle. She wanted to make the sun shine. Lily started to put the pieces together. She knew that if she finished the puzzle, something fun would happen. Lily worked hard on the puzzle. She put the last piece in the right spot. The sun on the puzzle started to shine bright. All the kids clapped and cheered. Lily was very happy. She saw how the sun shined and knew that finishing the puzzle was worth it.

Once upon a time, there was a little rabbit named Remy. Remy lived in a big forest. Every day, he would hop around the forest to play and find food. One day, he found a big, red apple. Remy was very happy because he loved to taste new things. Remy decided to share the apple with his friend, Benny the bird. So, he hopped to Benny's tree and said, "Hi Benny! I found a big, red apple. Let's taste it together!" Benny was excited and agreed to share the apple with Remy. As they tasted the apple, Remy's face turned red. It was very sour! Benny laughed, and Remy felt embarrassed. But then, Benny said, "Don't worry, Remy. We can still have fun. Let's go play in the forest!" Remy smiled, and they both hopped and flew around the forest, having a great day.
