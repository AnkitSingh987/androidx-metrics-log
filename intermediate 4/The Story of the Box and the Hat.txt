Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Box and the Hat

One day, a man and his wife were going to celebrate. They wanted to have a big party with cake and balloons. The wife was very happy and excited, but the man felt a little ashamed. He did not know how to dance and was scared that people would laugh at him. The wife knew this, but she had a plan. Before the party, the wife said to her husband, "Do not worry, I have a surprise for you!" The man was curious but still felt a bit ashamed. He did not know what the surprise could be, but he hoped it would help him at the party. When the party started, the wife brought out a big box. Inside the box was a funny dancing hat. The man put on the hat, and it helped him dance without feeling ashamed. Everyone laughed and clapped, and the man was very happy. The wife's surprise had made the celebration even better, and they danced all night long.

One day, Tom and Sue went to their grandpa's house. Grandpa had an ancient fridge. It was big and white. Tom and Sue liked to look at the fridge. "Let's play a game," said Tom. "We can march like soldiers to the fridge!" So, they began to march, one, two, one, two. Sue giggled and Tom laughed. As they marched to the fridge, something unexpected happened. The fridge door opened by itself! Inside, they saw a big ice cream cake. "Surprise!" said grandpa. "I made this for you!" Tom and Sue smiled and hugged grandpa. They all enjoyed the ice cream cake together.

One day, a girl named Lily wanted to bake a cake. She wanted to make the cake as a gift for her friend, Tom. Lily was very excited to make the cake smooth and pretty. As Lily started to bake, her little brother, Sam, came into the kitchen. Sam wanted to help Lily, but he was too small. Lily said, "No, Sam, you can't help me." Sam was sad and cried. Lily felt bad for Sam. She thought of a way to fix the problem. She asked Sam to help her put the gift in a box and make it look nice. Sam was happy again. They worked together and made the cake and gift look perfect for Tom.

---

Lily and Ben were playing outside in the park. They liked to run and jump and slide on the grass. The sun was shining and the air was warm. They felt happy and comfortable. But then they saw some mist coming from the pond. It looked like smoke or clouds. They were curious and wanted to see it better. They ran towards the pond, but they did not see the mud on the ground. Lily slipped and fell on the mud. She got dirty and wet and hurt her knee. She started to cry. Ben stopped and ran back to help her. He slipped too and fell on the mud. He got dirty and wet and hurt his elbow. He started to cry too. "Are you okay, Lily?" Ben asked. "No, I'm not okay, Ben. I'm dirty and wet and hurt. And the mist is scary. I want to go home," Lily said. "Me too, Lily. Me too. Let's go home," Ben said. They got up and walked slowly to the gate. They saw their mom waiting for them. She saw them too and ran to hug them. She cleaned their wounds and gave them dry clothes. She took them home and made them hot chocolate. She told them stories and sang them songs. They felt better and comfortable again. They forgot about the mist and the mud. They smiled and hugged their mom. They loved their mom.

One day, a little robot was playing in the park. He was very impatient because he wanted to play with the other kids. But the kids were scared of him. They did not want to play with a robot. The robot said, "Please play with me. I am not scary." But the kids still did not want to play. They wanted the robot to remove his metal parts. They thought he would be less scary then. The robot was sad, but he had an idea. He removed his metal parts and put on a soft costume. Now, he looked like a big teddy bear. The kids saw him and smiled. They were not scared anymore. "Can I play with you now?" the robot asked. The kids said yes, and they all played together. They had so much fun, and the robot was happy. He learned that sometimes, it is good to change to make friends.
