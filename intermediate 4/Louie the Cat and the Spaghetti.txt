Category: Intermediate
Title: Louie the Cat and the Spaghetti

One day, a funny cat named Louie was playing in the kitchen. He saw a big pot on the stove. Louie was curious and wanted to see what was inside. He jumped on the counter and took a peek. Inside the pot, there was spaghetti! Louie thought the spaghetti looked like fun to play with. He used his paw to pull some out of the pot. The spaghetti was long and wiggly, and Louie laughed as he played with it. Just then, Louie's friend, a little girl named Mary, walked into the kitchen. "Louie, what are you doing?" she asked. "I'm playing with spaghetti!" Louie replied, still laughing. Mary laughed too and joined in the fun. They played with the spaghetti together, making a big, silly mess.

Once upon a time, there was an adorable little girl named Lucy. She had short blonde hair and bright blue eyes. She was three years old and loved to share. One day, she was playing on the beach with her mom. The sun was shining and the sky was clear. Suddenly, Lucy saw a big wave in the water and shouted, "Look, Mommy! I can see a wave!" Lucy was so excited. Lucy's mom smiled and said, "That is so cute! Let's share the wave!" The two hugged each other and waved to the wave. Lucy was so happy. The wave seemed to wave back to the two of them as if it was happy to see them. The feeling of happiness was so strong. Lucy and her mom smiled, feeling so much joy and love. It was an adorable moment they would never forget.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a big, soft bear. She loved her bear very much. The bear was a gift from her mom and dad. It was an expensive bear, but Sue did not know that. All she knew was that her bear gave the best hugs. One day, Sue took her bear to the park. She played with her bear on the swings and the slide. Sue was very happy. She loved to hug her bear and play with him. She knew her bear loved her too. As the sun went down, it was time to go home. Sue and her bear walked back home, hand in paw. She gave her bear one last big hug before bedtime. Sue knew that her bear would always be there to give her the best hugs, and that made her feel safe and loved.

One day, a little girl named Sue was playing outside. It was cold, and she started to shiver. Sue's mom saw her and said, "Come inside, it's too cold out here!" When Sue went inside, she saw her dad holding a present. "This is for you," he said with a big smile. Sue was very grateful and excited. She opened the present quickly. Inside the present, Sue found a warm coat. She put it on and felt all warm and cozy. Now, she could play outside without shivering. Sue thanked her dad and went outside to play. But when she opened the door, she saw the sun was shining and it was warm outside. Sue laughed and said, "Now, I don't need my coat, but I'm still grateful for it!"

Once upon a time, in a green forest, there lived a big, green frog. The frog liked to scare his friends for fun. He would jump out of the bushes and shout, "Boo!" One day, the frog saw a big truck. The truck was carrying fuel for the cars. The frog had an idea. He thought, "I will scare the truck driver!" So, he jumped out of the bushes and shouted, "Boo!" But the truck driver did not get scared. Instead, he smiled and said, "Hello, Mr. Frog!" The frog was very surprised. He did not expect the truck driver to be so friendly. The truck driver told the frog that he had seen him scare his friends before, and he thought it was funny. The frog felt happy that the truck driver liked his jokes. From that day on, the frog and the truck driver became good friends. They would play together and have fun in the green forest. The frog learned that sometimes, when you try to scare someone, you might just make a new friend instead.
