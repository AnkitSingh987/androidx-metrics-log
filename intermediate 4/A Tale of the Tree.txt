Category: Intermediate
Title: A Tale of the Tree

One day, a big tree and a small tree were near a lake. The big tree had many leaves and could spread its branches wide. It made a lot of shade. The small tree was still growing and did not have many leaves. A little bird came by and said, "Big tree, can I sit in your shade? The sun is hot today." The big tree said, "Of course, little bird. Come and rest in my shade." The little bird was very happy and sang a sweet song. The small tree was frustrated because it wanted to help too. It tried hard to grow bigger and make more shade. One day, the little bird said, "Small tree, I see you are trying hard. Don't worry, you will grow big and strong like the big tree soon." The small tree smiled and knew that one day it would make a big shade for all its friends.

One day, a boy named Tim found a different spoon. It was not like his other spoons. This spoon was big and shiny. Tim liked the spoon very much. He took it with him everywhere he went. One day, Tim went to the store with his mom. He brought his different spoon with him. At the store, Tim saw a toy he wanted. His mom said he needed to pay for the toy. Tim did not have money, but he had an idea. Tim gave the store man his different spoon to pay for the toy. The store man looked at the spoon and smiled. He said, "This is a magic spoon! It can give you ice cream every day." Tim was very happy. He got his toy and a magic spoon that gave him ice cream. It was the best day ever.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Luna. She was three years old and loved to explore her world. One day, she went out to her garden and saw a big cube lying on the floor in the middle of the lawn. Luna was so excited to find the cube! She ran to it and carefully picked it up. The cube had a pattern of 10 colors - red, yellow, green and blue - and the colors matched perfectly with each corner! Luna was absolutely fascinated by the cube. Luna's mom, who was watching from the window, said, "That's a very fair cube, Luna. Can you match up all the colors?" Luna smiled excitedly and nodded. She carefully tried to line up all the colors on the cube, and after a few tries, she finally got it! She was so proud of herself. "Well done, Luna!" her mom said proudly. Luna felt so happy and excited that she had matched the cube so quickly and fairly. She smiled big and hugged the cube tightly. From then on, Luna always kept the cube handy so she could match the colors up whenever she liked.

Once upon a time, there was a cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to stretch her legs and run in the park. One sunny day, she saw a long line on the ground. Kitty was confused. She did not know what the line was. Kitty followed the line and saw a boy named Tim. Tim was playing with a long, blue string. "Hi, Kitty!" said Tim. "Do you want to play with me?" Kitty meowed and nodded her head. Tim and Kitty played with the long, blue string. They made the string into a big circle. Kitty jumped in and out of the circle. They had so much fun. At the end of the day, Kitty and Tim were tired but happy. They went home with big smiles on their faces.

One day, a girl named Amy was very eager to go to the gym. She wanted to wear her new shoes. They were red and shiny. She put them on, and went to the gym with her mom. At the gym, Amy saw a big ball. She wanted to play with it. She ran to the ball and kicked it hard. But, something unexpected happened. Instead of the ball moving, it stayed still. Amy was confused. She tried to kick the ball again, but it still didn't move. Then, her mom came and told her that it was not a ball. It was a big round weight for grown-ups to lift. Amy laughed and went to play with the real ball.
