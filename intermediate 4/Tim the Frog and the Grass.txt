Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Frog and the Grass

Once upon a time, there was a little frog named Tim. Tim loved to jump all day long. He jumped on the soft grass and in the cool water. One day, Tim saw a little match lying on the ground. He did not know what it was, but it looked fun to play with. Tim decided to jump on the match to see what would happen. But when he jumped on it, it hurt his little foot. Tim was sad and his foot hurt a lot. He hopped back home to tell his mom what happened. Tim's mom saw his hurt foot and gave him a big hug. She told him not to jump on things he didn't know. Tim learned his lesson and promised to be more careful. From that day on, he only jumped on things he knew were safe, and his foot stopped hurting.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little girl named Lucy. Lucy loved to paint pretty pictures. One day, she saw a lamp in her room that was not pretty. She thought, "I will paint the lamp to make it pretty!" Lucy got her paint and started to paint the lamp. She painted it with many colors: red, blue, green, and yellow. It looked so pretty now! She was very happy. But she did not know that this was a magic lamp. Later that night, Lucy went to bed. She turned off the lamp and closed her eyes. Suddenly, the lamp started to glow. The colors Lucy painted on the lamp began to dance around the room. The magic lamp made her room look like a rainbow! The next morning, Lucy woke up and saw the beautiful colors on her walls. She was so surprised and happy! From that day on, every night the magic lamp made her room a colorful wonderland. And Lucy learned that even the simplest things can become magical with a little bit of love and creativity.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big package outside his house. He was very happy and excited. He wanted to open the package and see what was inside. But his friend, Tom, said it was his package. Tim and Tom started to argue about whose package it was. Tim and Tom decided to play a game to see who would get the package. They spun around and around until they were very dizzy. They both fell down and laughed. They enjoyed playing the game, but they still did not know whose package it was. Tim's mom came outside and saw the package. She told the boys that the package was for both of them to share. Tim and Tom were happy to share the package. They opened it and found lots of toys inside. They played together and enjoyed their new toys.

Tom and Sue were hungry. They wanted to eat meat for lunch. They saw a big shop with many meats in the window. They went inside and looked around. "Wow, look at all the meats!" Tom said. He admired the red meat, the white meat, the brown meat. He wanted to try them all. "Tom, we have no money," Sue said. "We can't buy any meat. We have to go." But Tom did not listen. He was ignorant. He took a big piece of meat from the shelf and ran out of the shop. Sue followed him. "Stop, thief!" the shop man shouted. He was angry. He ran after Tom and Sue. Tom and Sue ran fast, but the shop man was faster. He caught them and took the meat back. He scolded them and called their mom. Their mom was very sad and ashamed. She said sorry to the shop man and paid for the meat. She took Tom and Sue home and gave them no lunch. She taught them not to steal and to be grateful for what they have. Tom and Sue felt sorry and learned their lesson. They never stole meat again.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Mia had a journal. She loved to draw in her journal every day. One day, she left her journal in the park by accident. A kind bird saw the journal under a high tree. The bird picked up the journal in its beak and flew away to find Mia. The bird wanted to return the journal to her. The bird found Mia at her home. Mia was so happy to see her journal again. She thanked the bird for returning it. From that day on, Mia and the bird became best friends.
