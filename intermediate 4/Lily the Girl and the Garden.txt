Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Garden

One day, a girl named Lily found a big, red flower in her garden. The flower was very pretty, but it looked sad and dead. Lily wanted to make the flower happy again. Lily asked her mom, "Mom, can we help the flower?" Her mom said, "Yes, we can give it water and love." So, Lily and her mom gave the flower water and hugged it. They locked the garden gate to keep the flower safe. After a few days, the flower was not dead anymore. It was big, red, and happy. Lily and her mom smiled and played near the flower. They knew their love and care saved the flower.

One day, a lovely little boy named Tim went to play outside. He had a toy gun that he loved a lot. Tim played with his gun every day. It was his favorite toy. While playing, Tim's toy gun broke. Tim was very sad. He ran to his mom and said, "Mom, my gun is broken. Can you fix it, please?" His mom looked at the broken toy gun and said, "Yes, Tim. I will try to fix it for you." Tim's mom fixed the toy gun with some glue. She gave it back to Tim and said, "Here you go, Tim. Your gun is fixed now." Tim was so happy. He thanked his mom and went back to play with his lovely toy gun. From that day on, Tim played more carefully with his toys.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to lead his little friends, the ducks, to the park. They walked together in a long line, with Buddy at the front. The ducks liked to follow Buddy because he was kind and showed them where to go. One day, when they were walking to the park, they saw a lot of cars and trucks. They had to stop because of the traffic. Buddy didn't know what to do. He was ignorant about how to cross the busy road. The ducks were scared too. Then, a nice lady saw them waiting. She walked up to Buddy and the ducks and helped them cross the road. Buddy wagged his tail to say thank you. The lady smiled and waved goodbye. Now they could play at the park without being scared of the traffic.

Once upon a time, there was a big alligator. He lived in a pond. The alligator was very comfortable in his home. He liked to swim and play with his friends, the fish and the frogs. One day, the alligator saw that his pond was dirty. He wanted to clean it. So, he asked his friends, the fish and the frogs, to help him. They all worked together to clean the pond. They picked up the trash and the leaves. When they were done, the pond was very clean. The alligator, the fish, and the frogs were happy. They swam and played in their clean pond. The alligator felt very comfortable in his clean home. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. Jane had a sister named Sue. They loved to play in their tidy room. One day, they found a big box in the room. They did not know what was inside. Jane and Sue looked at each other and smiled. They knew they would find out soon. The next day, Jane and Sue opened the big box. Inside, they found two big, beautiful wings. They were so happy. They put on the wings and ran outside. They knew they could do something fun with the wings. They just had to wait for the right time. The sun was shining and the sky was blue. Jane and Sue knew it was time. They held hands, and with a big jump, they started to soar in the sky. They flew high and low, laughing and smiling. They knew the big box was a special gift. Now, Jane and Sue could soar together, just like they always wanted.

Mark was a chubby three-year-old who loved playing in his garage. He spent his days playing with his toys, pretending they were all friends. One day, Mark heard a noise coming from down the street. He ran outside to see what was happening. To his surprise, he saw a big crowd of children playing in the street. Mark wanted to join in the fun, but he was too shy. So he just watched and smiled. Suddenly, one of the children ran up to him. "Hey, I'm Tyler," the child said. "Want to play with us?" Mark smiled and said, "Yes!" Then the two of them ran nearly to the garage and started playing together. They had lots of fun, and Mark even made a new friend.
