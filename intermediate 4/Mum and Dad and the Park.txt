Category: Intermediate
Title: Mum and Dad and the Park

Mum and Dad were in the park together with their little girl. The girl was three-years-old, and she was feeling adventurous. Mum and Dad looked around for something fun for the girl to do. Suddenly, Mum pointed at something in the distance and said, "Look, darling! Let's go and see that!" The girl was excited and followed her parents. When she got there, she saw a huge, heavy boulder. She couldn't believe how big it was and she let out a little shock. Mum spoke to her daughter. "Let's try and push it, darling. We can do it together!" The girl put her hands on the boulder and started pushing with all her might. After a few minutes, the boulder had moved ever so slightly. She was so proud and let out another shock of excitement. Mum and Dad smiled at each other, before they all walked away together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy wood car. Tim would push the car all around his house. He was very good at it. The car would go fast and make Tim laugh. One sunny day, Tim went outside to play with his wood car. He pushed it so fast that it went into the tall grass. Tim looked and looked, but he could not find his car. He was very sad and started to cry. Just then, a big dog came running to Tim. The dog had the wood car in its mouth! Tim was so happy and surprised. He thanked the dog and they became good friends. From that day on, Tim and the dog played together, and they always had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to watch films. One day, he found a long film he wanted to see. But, his mom said, "No, Tim. It's time for bed." Tim was sad. He wanted to complete the film. So, he asked his mom, "Please, can I watch just a little bit more?" His mom thought for a moment. She knew Tim loved films. She said, "Okay, Tim. You can watch a little bit more. But then, it's time for bed." Tim was happy. He watched a little bit more of the long film. Then, he went to bed. The next day, he completed the film after school. Tim and his mom were both happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily liked to take her toy bear with her everywhere she went. She would put the bear on her shoulder and walk around the house. One day, Lily and her bear went outside to play. They saw a big dog in the yard. The dog was loud and barked at Lily. Lily was scared, but she held her bear tight on her shoulder. Lily's mom came outside and saw the loud dog. She picked up Lily and the bear and took them back inside the house. Lily felt safe with her bear on her shoulder and her mom by her side.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very weak, but he had a big spirit. He loved to play with his friends in the park. One day, while playing, Tim met a big, mean dog. The dog barked at Tim, and Tim got scared. Tim asked the dog, "Why are you so mean?" The dog did not reply. Tim felt sad and wanted the dog to be nice. He thought if he gave the dog his favorite toy, the dog would be happy and stop being mean. So, Tim gave the dog his toy, and the dog stopped barking. But then, something unexpected happened. The dog took the toy and ran away. Tim chased the dog, but he was too weak to catch up. The dog got away with Tim's favorite toy, and Tim was very sad. He went home and told his mom about the mean dog. She hugged him and said, "Sometimes, things don't go the way we want them to, but we have to keep our spirits up and keep trying."

Becky woke up one morning and was very excited. She could hear a chirping sound coming from outside. She got out of bed and hopped to the window. Honestly, she was amazed. A bird was perched on the branch of the tree, singing a beautiful song. She wanted to pick it up and take it home. Just then, Daddy walked in. He was holding a big, yellow notebook. Becky was delighted. She clapped her hands, hopping towards her Daddy. He opened the notebook and snapped, "Your mom and I bought this for you. You can draw and color in it. Doesn't it look good?" Becky was thrilled. She nodded her head up and down. She grabbed the notebook and hugged it tight. She couldn't wait to go and play with it. Daddy smiled and said, "You can take it outside and draw. Have fun!" Becky quickly ran outside, notebook in tow, and started sketching with her favorite crayons in the sunshine. She was so happy to have something new and good to play with.
