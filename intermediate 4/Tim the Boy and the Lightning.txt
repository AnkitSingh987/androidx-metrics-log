Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Lightning

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing outside. He saw the sky getting dark and heard a loud noise. It was lightning! Tim felt dizzy because he was scared. He ran inside to find his mom. "Mom, I saw lightning!" Tim said. "I feel dizzy and scared." His mom hugged him and said, "I will care for you. Let's sit down until you feel better." They sat on the couch, and his mom held him close. As they watched the rain outside, Tim started to feel better. He knew his mom cared for him and would keep him safe from the lightning.

One day, Emma and her dad decided to take a walk in the park. When they got there, they were amazed to see a big shiny medal. "Wow!" shouted Emma. "That medal looks beautiful!" Her dad smiled. "Yes, it does. Let's try to get it!" So, they ran to the medal and Emma grabbed it. Then, suddenly, something very noisy happened. It was the medallist! He was very angry that someone had taken his medal. The medallist pointed at Emma and her dad and said, "That medal belongs to me!" "We are sorry," said Emma's dad. "We did not know that it was yours. Here, take it back." The medallist was happy and smiled. He thanked Emma and her dad for returning it. "But I thought it would be a nice way of serving you," said Emma's dad. The medallist smiled again. "It was! Thank you!" That day, Emma and her dad learned a valuable lesson. They knew that it is important to always serve others.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a pale pigeon. The pigeon was walking in the park. Tim wanted to know why the pigeon was pale. He asked his mom to explain. "Mom, why is the pigeon pale?" Tim said. His mom looked at the pigeon and smiled. "Well, Tim, the pigeon is pale because it was born that way. Just like how you have brown hair, and I have black hair." Tim thought about it and nodded. He walked closer to the pale pigeon. He looked at it and said, "Hi, pigeon! You are pale, and I have brown hair. We are different, and that's okay." The pale pigeon looked at Tim and cooed. Tim smiled and knew that he and the pigeon were friends. They played in the park together, and Tim was happy to have a new friend.

Once upon a time, in a far away land, there was a little kangaroo named Kip. Kip loved to hop and play all day long. One sunny day, Kip and his friends decided to gather fruits from the trees. They wanted to see who could gather the most fruits. While they were hopping and gathering, Kip saw a big, hard rock in his way. He tried to hop over it, but he could not. Kip felt sad because he could not gather as many fruits as his friends. He wanted to win the game. Kip's friends saw him feeling sad and decided to help him. They all worked together to move the big, hard rock out of the way. Now, Kip could hop and gather fruits just like his friends. They all gathered fruits together and had a fun day. In the end, they learned that helping each other was more important than winning the game.

One day, a boy named Tim got a new hose. He was very happy to play with it. He wanted to water his plants and fill his pool. Tim loved his new hose. Tim's friend, Sam, came to play. Sam wanted to use the hose too, but Tim did not want to share. Sam was sad. He asked Tim to mind his feelings, but Tim still did not want to share. Then, Tim's mom came outside. She told Tim to share his new hose with Sam. Tim did not like it, but he knew his mom was right. Tim shared the hose with Sam, and they both had fun playing together.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to dance. He would dance all day and all night. Spot was very happy when he danced. One day, Spot hurt his heel while dancing. He felt sad because he couldn't dance anymore. Spot's friend, Kitty, saw him and wanted to help. Kitty was good at helping others. Kitty told Spot to rest his heel and be patient. Spot listened to Kitty and took care of his heel. Soon, his heel got better, and Spot could dance again. Spot was so happy, and he thanked Kitty for her help. The moral of the story is to listen to your friends and take care of yourself when you are hurt.
