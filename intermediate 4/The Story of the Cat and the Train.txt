Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Cat and the Train

One day, a big, wide cat went to the station. He wanted to take a train ride. At the station, he met a little girl. The girl said, "Hi, cat! Can I rub your head?" The cat said, "Yes, you can rub my head." So, the girl rubbed the cat's head. They were happy. Suddenly, the cat turned into a big, wide dog! The little girl was very surprised. The dog said, "Thank you for the rub! Now, let's go on the train together." The girl and the dog went on the train and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, a giant lived in a big, fancy house. The giant was very nice and liked to play with all the little kids in the town. One day, the giant saw a little girl named Lily playing with a ball. The giant went to her and said, "Hi Lily, can I play with you?" Lily looked at the giant and said, "Yes, let's play catch!" They started to play together. The giant threw the ball to Lily, but it went too high. Lily jumped as high as she could, and she began to spring up into the air like a bouncy ball. The giant was very surprised! Lily kept springing higher and higher, and the giant laughed. "Wow, Lily, you can jump so high!" The giant caught her as she came back down. From that day on, the giant and Lily played together every day, and they had lots of fun. The town was happy because they had a giant friend who could make them laugh and a little girl who could spring like a bouncy ball.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved ice-cream very much. One day, Sue's mom said, "We will prepare ice-cream today!" Sue was so happy. She put on her fancy dress and went to the kitchen. Sue and her mom made the ice-cream together. They mixed milk, sugar, and Sue's favorite, chocolate! When the ice-cream was ready, Sue's mom said, "Let's share our ice-cream with our friends!" So, Sue and her mom went to the park. They shared the ice-cream with all their friends. Everyone loved the ice-cream and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a lively little bunny named Bobo. Bobo loved to hop and play all day long. He had many friends like the birds, the bees, and the trees. They all liked to play together. The sun was warm and bright, and it made them all happy. One day, when the sun went to sleep, the moon came out to play. The moon had a soft glow that lit up the night. Bobo and his friends looked up and saw the moon smiling down at them. They wanted to play with the moon too, but they did not know how to reach it. Bobo had an idea. He asked his friends to help him build a big pile of soft leaves. They all worked together, and the pile grew bigger and bigger. Bobo climbed to the top of the pile and jumped as high as he could. He did not reach the moon, but he felt like he was close. Bobo and his friends laughed and played under the glowing moon until they were all very sleepy. They knew the moon would always be there to light up their nights and make them feel happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red ambulance. It had a loud siren and bright lights. It would drive very fast to help people who were hurt or sick. The ambulance loved to help people and make them feel better. One day, a small bird named Billy felt dizzy. He was flying in the sky, but he could not fly straight. He was scared and did not know what to do. He saw the ambulance and thought it could help him. So, he flew down to the ambulance and asked for help. The ambulance did not like to see Billy dizzy and did not want him to be scared. The ambulance wanted to help Billy, but it knew it could not do it alone. So, it called its friend, the kind doctor. The doctor came and helped Billy feel better. The ambulance, the doctor, and Billy were all happy. They knew that they did not hate each other, but they were friends who helped each other when they needed it.
