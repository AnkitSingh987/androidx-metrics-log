Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Sue and the Dog

One day, Tom and Sue went to the park to play. They took a big, tight tent with them. They wanted to put the tent up and play inside. Tom said, "I need help with the tent." Sue said, "I will help you, Tom." They worked together and soon the tent was up. They went inside and played with their toys. All of a sudden, a little dog came running into the tent. The dog was very happy to see them. Tom and Sue were surprised but happy too. They played with the dog and had a lot of fun. The dog became their new friend, and they all played together in the tight tent.

Once upon a time, there was a normal boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the sunshine. He liked to run, jump, and laugh with his friends. Every day was a happy day for Tim. One day, while playing outside, Tim saw a big, shiny rock. He wanted to give the rock to his mom as a gift. He picked it up and ran home. When he gave the rock to his mom, something unexpected happened. The rock started to glow! Tim and his mom were very surprised. The glowing rock was not a normal rock at all. It was a magic rock! The magic rock made their house bright and full of sunshine, even at night. Tim and his mom were so happy that they found the magic rock. From that day on, they always had sunshine in their home.

Once upon a time, there was a modest ant named Andy. Andy liked to sort things in his little ant home. One day, he found a small stone. The stone was very pretty. Andy thought, "I will keep this stone for something special." Andy told his friend, a little bird, about the stone. The bird said, "That stone could help us one day." Andy and the bird kept the stone safe in a tiny box. They knew it was important, but they did not know why. One day, their friend, a small fish, was in trouble. The fish was stuck in a pond that was drying up. Andy and the bird knew they had to help. They used the pretty stone to dig a path for the water to flow back into the pond. The fish was saved, and they all knew that the stone was special after all.

Tom and Lily are playing in the garden. They pretend to be a lion and a zebra. Tom roars and chases Lily. Lily runs and laughs. They see a big red bucket near the fence. It is full of milk. Lily says, "Look, Tom, milk! Let's pretend it is water and drink it. We are thirsty animals." Tom says, "OK, Lily, but be careful. The milk might be old and yucky." They go to the bucket and dip their mouths in the milk. They gulp and gulp. The milk is warm and sour. They make faces and spit it out. But it is too late. The milk is bad and makes them sick. They feel their tummies ache and their heads hurt. They cry and cry. Mom hears them and comes to the garden. She sees the bucket and the milk. She says, "Oh no, Tom and Lily, what have you done? This is not milk for drinking. This is milk for the plants. It has fertilizer and chemicals in it. It is very dangerous. You could get very ill." She takes them to the house and calls the doctor. The doctor comes and gives them medicine. He says they have to stay in bed for a long time. He says they are lucky they did not drink more. Tom and Lily are sad and sorry. They wish they did not pretend to drink the milk. They wish they listened to Tom. They wish they played something else. They learned a hard lesson. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little vine. The vine was very small and was ashamed because it could not grow big and strong like the other vines. One day, a kind bird saw the little vine and said, "Do not be sad, little vine. I will help you become big and strong." The bird brought the little vine some water and helped it find more sun. Soon, the little vine started to grow bigger and stronger. The other vines were happy for the little vine, but they did not know its secret. Then, one day, something unexpected happened. The little vine grew so big and strong that it turned into a huge tree! The other vines were very surprised and asked, "How did you become a tree, little vine?" The tree smiled and said, "With the help of my friend, the kind bird, I became big and strong. Now, I am not ashamed anymore."
