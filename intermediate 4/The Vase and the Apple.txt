Category: Intermediate
Title: The Vase and the Apple

Once upon a time, there was a pretty vase. The vase was very happy. The vase lived on a table in a big room. Many people came to see the vase. They would look at the vase and smile. They would say, "What a pretty vase!" The vase felt very special. One day, a little girl came into the room. She saw the vase and her eyes got big. She walked up to the vase and said, "I admire you, pretty vase." The vase felt even more special. The little girl loved the vase very much. The little girl was hungry. She went to the kitchen to find some food. She found a big red apple. She ate the apple and felt happy. Then, she went back to the pretty vase. She looked at the vase and smiled. The vase and the little girl were very happy together.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely man named Tom. Tom had no friends, and he was very sad. One day, Tom found a toy pistol on the ground. He thought it would be fun to play with it. Tom decided to drive to the park with the toy pistol. At the park, he saw a little boy named Tim. Tom went up to Tim and said, "Do you want to play with me and my toy pistol?" Tim was scared and ran away. Tom did not know why Tim was scared. He kept playing with the toy pistol, but no one wanted to play with him. Tom felt even more lonely. In the end, Tom learned that it is not good to scare others if you want to make friends.

Once upon a time there lived a happy family with two children, a boy and a girl. One day, when the sun was high in the sky, the children were playing outside and saw something moving in the grass. They quickly ran over and to their surprise found a puppy! The puppy was wet and shivering. The children knew they had to take care of the puppy, so they brought it inside and dried it off with a soft towel. The children's mom asked them where they got the puppy from. The boy quickly spoke up and said that they found it outside. She smiled and said to the children, “You need to realize that we can’t keep this puppy. We need to find its home and make sure the puppy is safe.” The children were sad but they understood their mom was right. They went door-to-door asking if anyone had lost a puppy, until finally they found its family. When the family saw their puppy, it was full of joy. The children's mom told them “You see, it's important to think about other's feelings and not just your own. Good deeds will always come back to you.” Moral of the story: Take time to think of the feelings of others and be kind, because in the end good deeds will come back to you.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy cube. The cube was very colorful and fun. Tim thought his cube was very useful because he could stack it, roll it, and even sit on it. One day, Tim's mom gave him a new toy. It was a small machine that could record sounds. Tim was very happy and wanted to use it right away. He thought it would be fun to record the sounds his cube made when he played with it. Tim played with his cube and his new toy all day. He recorded the sound of the cube hitting the floor and the sound of it rolling on the ground. Tim loved listening to the sounds his cube made. He played the sounds for his mom, and she smiled. Tim's new toy was useful, just like his cube.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Max, went to the park. They wanted to relax and play. They saw a big box under a tree. "What is this?" Tim asked Max. Max barked and wagged his tail. They had found a mystery. Tim opened the box and found a toy car inside. "Wow!" he said. "A new toy for us to play with!" Tim and Max played with the toy car, but then they saw a sad girl named Lily. She was crying and looking for something. "What's wrong, Lily?" asked Tim. "I lost my toy car," she said, feeling ashamed. Tim looked at the toy car and knew it was Lily's. He gave it back to her. Lily smiled and thanked Tim. They all played together and had fun. The unexpected thing was that Tim and Max found a new friend in Lily. The moral of the story is to always be kind and help others, because it can bring happiness and new friends.
