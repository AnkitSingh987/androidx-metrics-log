Category: Intermediate
Title: The Peach and the Fit

Once upon a time, there was a peach that wanted to find somewhere to fit. It looked all around and felt very anxious. Everywhere it went, it could not seem to fit in. One day, it decided to try one last place. It found a small gap between two rocks and squeezed itself in. It fit perfectly! The peach was so relieved - it felt so happy and cosy that it never wanted to leave. From then on, it lived happily in its little spot, feeling safe and snug. The end.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little bunny named Bob. Bob loved to hop, play, and spread his toys all around his home. He had a lot of fun with his friends. Every day, they played together and had an incredible time. One day, Bob felt very tired. He thought it was time for a nap. He hopped back to his home, spread his soft blanket on the ground, and lay down. Bob closed his eyes and fell asleep. While he was napping, his friends kept playing outside. While Bob was napping, a big rain started to fall. The rain was so heavy that it made the forest very wet and muddy. Bob's friends tried to run back to their homes, but they could not. They were all stuck in the mud. Bob was still napping, so he did not know that his friends were in trouble. The rain kept falling, and the friends were very sad. They wished that Bob was there to help them. But Bob was still napping, and he did not know about the rain or his friends' problems.

Once upon a time, there was a nice girl named Ella who lived near a big pond. One day, Ella went to the pond and saw a lovely fish. She was very happy to see it and said, "Hi, fish!" The fish swam a bit and waved its fins. Ella wanted to get close to the fish so she decided to try balancing on a log that was near the pond. She started to balance and the fish came closer and closer. Ella smiled and said, "You're very lovely!" The fish seems to be happy and swam around Ella's feet. Soon she realised that she had lost her balance and fell into the pond! Ella laughed and splashed around as the fish watched her. Ella then came back ashore and waved goodbye to the fish. She hugged herself as she thought, "The fish was really lovely and made me so happy!".

One day, a cheerful cat named Tim went to the park to play. Tim loved to speak with his friends. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Up and up he went, all the way to the top. Tim was so happy to see the whole park from high up. Then, something unexpected happened. Tim saw a bird that could speak too! The bird said, "Hello, I am Bob. I can take your order for a wish." Tim was very surprised. He thought hard about what he wanted to wish for. Tim made his wish. He wished for a big cake for all his friends to share. But, the twist was that the cake was already in the park! Tim and Bob flew down from the tree and found the cake. All of Tim's friends came to eat the cake and they had a fun party.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a toy weapon in his room. It was a small plastic sword. He thought it was very important. Tim wanted to show his friend, Sam, his new toy. Tim went to Sam's house and said, "Look at my important toy weapon! Let's play with it!" Sam was excited and they started to play. They pretended to be brave knights, protecting their castle from a big dragon. As they played, Tim set the toy weapon down for a moment. A little dog came by and picked it up in its mouth. Tim and Sam chased the dog, trying to get the toy back. The dog thought it was a fun game and ran around the yard. Finally, the dog dropped the toy weapon and Tim picked it up. He said, "We got it back! Now, let's keep playing." Sam agreed, and they continued their adventure as brave knights, always making sure to keep the important toy weapon safe from any more sneaky dogs.
