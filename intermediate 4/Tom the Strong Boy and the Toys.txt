Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Strong Boy and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a strong boy named Tom. He had a big toy truck. Tom liked to play with his truck in the fog. The fog made it hard to see, but Tom didn't care. He thought it was fun. One day, Tom saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she had no toys. Tom wanted to make Sue happy. So, he decided to share his truck with her. They played in the fog together, taking turns pushing the truck. But the fog got thicker and thicker. Tom and Sue couldn't see each other anymore. They both tried to find their way back, but they got lost. Tom and Sue were scared. They never found each other again and the truck was lost in the fog forever.

Maddie and her mommy went on a special outing. They went to the theater to collect a surprise! Maddie was so excited. When they got to the theater, her mommy handed her a special bag full of treats. She was so spoiled! Maddie opened the bag to find all kinds of chocolates and toys. She was so happy! She couldn't believe it. Maddie smiled and thanked her mommy for taking her to the theater and for the special surprise. They had a wonderful day together.

Tim and Sam were hungry. They wanted to eat a sandwich. They saw a big sandwich on a high shelf in the kitchen. They tried to reach it, but they were too small. "Let's get a chair," Tim said. "We can stand on it and get the sandwich." "Good idea," Sam said. They dragged a chair to the shelf and climbed on it. But the sandwich was still too high. "Let's get another chair," Tim said. "We can stack them and get the sandwich." "Bad idea," Sam said. "That's dangerous. We can fall and hurt ourselves." But Tim did not listen. He got another chair and put it on top of the first one. He climbed up and reached for the sandwich. But the chairs were wobbly and he lost his balance. He fell down with a loud thud and the chairs and the sandwich fell with him. "Ow!" Tim cried. "That hurt!" "Are you okay?" Sam asked. He ran to help his brother. "I'm okay," Tim said. "But I'm still hungry." "Me too," Sam said. They looked at the sandwich on the floor. It was dirty and squashed. "Yuck!" Tim said. "We can't eat that." "No, we can't," Sam said. "But maybe we can find something else to eat." They went to the fridge and opened it. They saw some cheese and some bread and some jam. They made their own sandwiches and ate them happily. They learned a lesson. They should not try to get things that are too high for them. They should remain safe and ask for help when they need it.

Once upon a time, there was a little leopard named Leo. Leo lived in a big jungle with his family. They were not wealthy, but they were happy. One day, Leo met a wise old owl. The owl said, "I know how you can become wealthy, Leo. You must find the magic pond in the jungle." Leo was excited and went to look for the magic pond. After a long search, Leo found the magic pond. He jumped in and splashed around. Suddenly, something unexpected happened! Leo didn't become wealthy, but he changed into a beautiful rainbow-colored leopard. Everyone in the jungle loved his new look, and Leo was happier than ever before.

Once upon a time, there was a young bunny named Bobo. Bobo loved to hop all day long. He would hop in the grass, hop in the dirt, and even hop in the water. Bobo was a very happy bunny. One day, while Bobo was hopping, he found a big cave. It was dark and scary inside. But Bobo was brave, so he hopped right in. As he hopped deeper into the cave, he saw something shiny on the ground. It was a big, round rock that sparkled in the dark. Bobo was so excited about the shiny rock that he hopped all the way back out of the cave to show his friends. But when he got outside, the shiny rock was gone! Bobo was sad, but then he noticed that the shiny rock was not a rock at all. It was a big, shiny puddle of water. The cave was not scary, it was just a fun place to hop and play. So, Bobo and his friends hopped and played in the cave all day long, and they were very happy.
