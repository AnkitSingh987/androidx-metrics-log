Category: Intermediate
Title: Fluffy the Furry Cat and the Park

One day, a furry cat named Fluffy went to the park. Fluffy loved to play and joke with his friends. He saw a big plane in the sky and wanted to fly like the plane. Fluffy had an idea. He asked his bird friend, Tweet, to help him fly. They joked and laughed as they tried to make Fluffy fly like the plane. But Fluffy was too heavy for Tweet to lift up. Fluffy was sad, but then he saw a toy plane on the ground. He played with the toy plane, running and joking with his friends. Fluffy learned that he didn't need to fly like a real plane to have fun.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to ride his bike. He had a big bike with a shiny red pedal. One day, Tim met a big, fierce dog named Max. Tim was scared of Max at first, but Max said, "Don't be scared, I am a nice dog. I want to teach you a new trick on your bike." Tim was happy and said, "Okay, let's learn!" Max taught Tim a trick to make his bike go very fast. The trick was to push the red pedal very hard. Tim tried and tried, but he could not do it. Max said, "Don't give up, you can do it!" One day, Tim pushed the red pedal very hard and his bike went very fast. He was so happy! But then, something unexpected happened. Max was not a fierce dog after all. He was a magical dog who could talk and teach tricks! Tim and Max became best friends. They rode bikes and learned tricks together every day. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a friendly dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball and splash in the water. One sunny day, Spot went to the park with his friends to play. While playing, Spot saw a pastry on the ground. It was big and yummy. Spot wanted to eat the pastry, but he also wanted to share with his friends. So, he picked up the pastry in his mouth and ran to his friends. But something unexpected happened! As Spot ran, the pastry began to grow bigger and bigger. It was not a normal pastry! Spot's friends saw the big pastry and were very surprised. They all laughed and played with the big pastry like a ball. In the end, they all had a fun day with the magical pastry.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a boy named Tom. Tom liked to organize his toys. He had a big box where he put all his toys after playing. Tom had many friends who liked to play with him. One hot day, Tom and his friends decided to have a race. They were all very excited. The sun was shining, and there was lots of heat. They lined up at the starting point and Tom said, "Ready, set, go!" They all ran as fast as they could. Tom's friend, Jack, was very fast. He won the race and everyone cheered for him. But Sam, another friend, was envious. He wanted to win too. Sam said, "Next time, I will win the race." Tom smiled and said, "It's okay, Sam. We can race again tomorrow. Let's go organize our toys now." And they all went back to playing happily together.

Lily was a little girl who liked to play in the garden. She had a lot of flowers and plants that she watered every day. She also had a big pot that she used to make mud pies and pretend soup. One day, she saw a small animal hiding under the pot. It had a long tail, a furry body and a pointy nose. Lily was curious and wanted to see it better. She lifted the pot slowly and said, "Hello, who are you?" The animal was frightened and ran away. It made a loud noise that sounded like "Squeak!" Lily followed it and saw that it went into a hole in the fence. She wondered what it was and where it came from. She ran inside the house and asked her mom, "Mom, what is the animal that lives under the pot in the garden? It has a long tail and a pointy nose and it says 'Squeak!'" Her mom smiled and said, "That is a mouse, Lily. It is a small creature that likes to eat seeds and cheese. Sometimes it comes to the garden to look for food. But it is very shy and scared of people and cats. You should not touch it or chase it, because it might bite you or make you sick." Lily nodded and said, "OK, mom. I will not touch the mouse or chase it. But can I introduce myself to it and say hello? Maybe it will be my friend." Her mom said, "You can try, Lily, but be gentle and quiet. Maybe you can leave some seeds or cheese near the pot and see if it comes out. But remember, the mouse is wild and not a pet. It might not want to be your friend." Lily said, "I understand, mom. Thank you for telling me about the mouse. I will be nice to it and maybe it will like me." She went back to the garden and put some seeds and cheese near the pot. She waited patiently and hoped that the mouse would come out and say hello. She wanted to introduce herself and tell it her name. She thought that the mouse was cute and interesting and maybe a little lonely. She wanted to make it happy and not frightened.
