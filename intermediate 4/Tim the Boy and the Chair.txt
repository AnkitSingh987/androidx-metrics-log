Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Chair

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big blue chair that was very comfortable. Tim loved to seat on it every day. The chair was made of soft rubber, so it was bouncy and fun to sit on. One sunny day, Tim went to the park with his mom. When they came back, he saw that his blue chair was not in his room. Tim was sad and asked his mom, "Where is my chair?" His mom said, "I don't know, let's look for it." They looked everywhere in the house, but they could not find the chair. Tim was very sad. Then, they heard a noise coming from the backyard. They went outside and saw a big surprise! Tim's blue chair was there, but it was not a chair anymore. It had turned into a big, bouncy rubber ball! Tim was so happy and excited. He jumped on the ball and bounced up and down. It was even more fun than his chair! From that day on, Tim played with his big, bouncy rubber ball every day. It was still very comfortable, but now it was also fun and exciting. Tim and his mom never knew how the chair turned into a ball, but they were happy that it did. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once there was a young girl named Lucy. She loved to play outside. One day, while she was playing in her garden, she saw a light hanging from the tree. She was so curious, she went to take a closer look. When she reached the light, it started to glow brightly. Lucy thought it was beautiful and wanted to take it home. She reached up and grabbed the light. Suddenly, the light-hanging tree started to shake. A mean old man came out of nowhere and yelled, “Put that light back, or you will be in big trouble!” Lucy was scared and hung the light back on the tree quickly. The mean old man then warned her, “I’m watching you and if I ever see that light in your hands again, there will be consequences.” Lucy knew she was young and naive and she should have listened to the man. She was so frightened that she stayed away from the light-hanging tree. When she eventually grew up, she forgot about the tree and returned one day. She found the light but before she could pick it up, a strong gust of wind blew the light down from the branch and Lucy was never seen again.

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat. The cat found a guitar. The cat wanted to play the guitar. It tried to twist the strings to make them sound nice. The cat twisted and twisted the strings. But the guitar did not sound nice. The cat was sad. It wanted to play pretty songs for its friends. One day, the cat tried to twist the strings again. But this time, the strings broke. The guitar was broken too. The cat was very sad. It could not play for its friends anymore. The cat stayed sad and never found another guitar.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very weak and small. He had a big dream. He wanted to shake the universe. Tim's friends always laughed at him. They said, "You are too weak. You can't shake the universe." One day, Tim found a magic stone. The stone was very special. It could make dreams come true. Tim held the stone and wished to shake the universe. The stone glowed and Tim felt stronger. He knew his dream would come true soon. Tim started to help others. He helped his friends carry things. He helped his mom clean the house. He even helped his teacher at school. As Tim helped others, he grew stronger and stronger. One day, Tim felt a big shake. The universe was shaking! Tim had shaken the universe with his good deeds. The moral of the story is that even the weak can shake the universe by helping others.

Lily liked to play with her mom's desk. It had many things on it, like pens, papers, and a big lamp. Lily wanted to make a present for her mom. She took some papers and a pen and drew a picture of her mom and her. She also took a ribbon and tied it around the papers. But Lily did not see that the ribbon was also tied to the lamp. When she pulled the ribbon, the lamp fell down and broke. Lily heard a loud noise and saw glass everywhere. She was scared and sad. She did not want to make her mom angry. She ran to her room and hid under her bed. She cried and hugged her teddy bear. She hoped her mom would not be mad at her. Soon, her mom came home and saw the mess on the desk. She was surprised and worried. She called Lily's name and looked for her. She found her under the bed and saw her tears. She hugged her and said, "Lily, what happened?" Lily told her mom what she did. She said she was sorry and showed her the picture she made. She said she wanted to give her a present. Her mom smiled and kissed her. She said, "Lily, thank you for the picture. It is very beautiful. I love you very much. But you have to be careful with the desk. It has some things that can hurt you or break. Next time, ask me before you play with it, okay?" Lily nodded and said, "Okay, mom. I love you too. Can we clean the desk together?" Her mom said, "Yes, we can. But first, let's put a band-aid on your finger. You have a small cut from the glass. It will be clear soon." They went to the bathroom and put a band-aid on Lily's finger. Then they cleaned the desk and threw away the broken glass. They put the picture on the fridge and the ribbon in a drawer. They hugged and said they were happy. They had a good day.
