Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Magic and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a big building with a magic box called an elevator. The elevator could go up and down, taking people to different floors. One day, a little boy named Timmy went inside the elevator with his mom. Timmy was very enthusiastic about riding the elevator. He liked to press the buttons and watch the doors open and close. As the elevator went up and down, Timmy's mom would hold his hand and smile at him. One day, when the elevator door opened, Timmy saw a big fluffy teddy bear sitting on the floor. He picked it up and gave it a big hug. Timmy's mom smiled and hugged Timmy too. They knew the elevator was a special place where they could share happy moments together.

One evening, a little girl named Lily went outside. She wanted to play with her new ball. The sun was going down and the sky was pretty with colors. Lily was very happy. As Lily played, she noticed something in the grass. It was small and shiny. She picked it up and saw that it was a key. Lily was curious about what the key could open. She looked around and saw a tiny door near a tree. Lily tried the key in the door and it opened! Inside, she found a room full of toys. But, these toys were alive and could talk! They were all very nice and wanted to play with Lily. It was a magical place that only she could see. Lily had a lot of fun playing with her new friends every evening.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the park. The cat found a soft, red ball. The dog found a big, brown boot. They both wanted to play with the red ball, but the dog did not want to give up the boot. The cat said, "Let's play with the red ball. It is soft and fun." The dog did not want to listen. He urged the cat to play with the boot. The cat did not want to play with the boot. It was not soft like the red ball. The cat and the dog started to argue. A wise, old owl saw them and said, "You can both play with the red ball and the boot. Share and take turns." The cat and the dog listened to the owl. They played with the red ball and the boot, and they had a lot of fun. The moral value of the story is to share and take turns with others.

Once upon a time, there was a fit cat named Tom. He liked to play and run all day. One day, Tom went to the park to find new friends. In the park, he saw a pigeon. Tom wanted to meet the pigeon and play with him. So, he walked up to the pigeon and said, "Hi! I am Tom. Do you want to play with me?" The pigeon looked at Tom and said, "Yes, I want to play with you, Tom." They played and had fun together. They ran, jumped, and laughed. Tom and the pigeon were very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A big dog came into the park and saw Tom and the pigeon playing. The dog wanted to play, too. But the dog was not nice. He chased Tom and the pigeon. They tried to run away, but the dog was too fast. The dog caught the pigeon and took him away. Tom was sad and scared. He went home without his new friend, the pigeon. The day did not end well for Tom and the pigeon.

Once upon a time, in a big green park, there lived a little squirrel named Sam. Sam was very excited because he got a new toy. It was a shiny red ball. He wanted to play with it all day long. Sam went to his friend, a small brown bunny named Ben. "Ben, look at my new ball!" said Sam. "Do you want to try to play with it?" Ben nodded his head and they both started to play. They rolled the ball to each other, and they were having lots of fun. Suddenly, the ball rolled away and went under a bush. Sam and Ben tried to get it, but it was too far. They were sad because they couldn't play with the ball anymore. Then, a big yellow bird named Daisy saw them and asked, "Why are you both sad?" Sam told Daisy about the new ball and how it went under the bush. Daisy said, "Don't worry, I will try to help you." She used her long beak and got the ball out from under the bush. Sam and Ben were so happy. They all played with the new ball together and had a great day.
