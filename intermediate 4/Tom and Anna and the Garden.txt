Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Anna and the Garden

Tom and Anna were playing in the garden. They liked to dig and make mud pies. But they also liked to go to the shed and look at the tools. They knew they were not allowed to touch them, but they were curious. One day, they saw a shiny hammer in the shed. Tom wanted to try it. He took it and hit a nail on the wall. It made a loud noise. Anna was scared. She said, "Tom, stop! Mom will hear you!" But Tom did not stop. He hit the nail again and again. He liked the sound. He did not hear Mom coming. She opened the door and saw Tom with the hammer. She was angry. She said, "Tom, what are you doing? You know you are not supposed to touch the tools. They are dangerous. Give me the hammer now!" Tom felt guilty. He knew he did something bad. He gave Mom the hammer and hung his head. Anna felt guilty too. She did not stop Tom. She hid behind him. Mom took them by the hand and made them march to the house. They walked in silence. They knew they were in trouble. They were sorry. They hoped Mom would forgive them.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Jane. Jane was very happy because today was her birthday and she was having a special lunch. Jane was wearing her favorite dress – it was a pretty pink dress! Mommy cooked a delicious lunch – Jane's favorite – and set it down for her to enjoy. Jane was about to start eating when she accidentally knocked over her mother's glass of juice. It all spilled onto the floor! Mommy was angry and said "I spent so much time making lunch, and now it's ruined". Jane was very sad and started to cry. Mommy tried to console her, but Jane was so upset. Jane was so scared that she couldn't stop crying. Mommy had to take Jane out of the room, leaving the spilled juice on the floor and the ruined lunch on the table. That's the end of the story. Jane was really sad, and the lunch was ruined. All because of the spilled pink juice.

One day, a big bird wanted to dive into the pond. The pond was near a high rock. The bird was so happy to play in the water. As the bird was diving, a little fish saw the bird coming. The little fish was scared. The fish thought fast and swam under the rock. The bird did not see the fish. The bird felt sad because it did not find the fish. But then, the fish came out and said, "Let's play together!" The bird and the fish became friends, and they played in the pond all day.

Once upon a time, there was a kind little girl called Ella. Ella used to love playing in the garden. One day, while playing, she spotted something very special. Ella thought it must be an angel. She smiled and said "Hi, angel!" Suddenly, the angel spoke. She told Ella that she was there to help her do something special. Ella was very excited. The angel said it was quite an easy task. The angel explained that Ella had to pick some flowers from the garden. Ella started picking the flowers and soon there were flowers all around. When Ella was finished, the angel smiled and disappeared. Ella was sad to see the angel go, but she was very happy that she had been able to help. And, it had been really easy!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. One sunny day, Tim and his mom went to a shop. They wanted to buy some food for their house. Tim liked going to the shop because he could help his mom pick out the things they needed. When they got to the shop, Tim's mom said, "Tim, please stay close to me and don't leave." Tim nodded and said, "Okay, Mom!" They walked around the shop together, picking out fruits, vegetables, and other things they needed. Tim noticed that the shop was very dry, so he told his mom, "Mom, it's so dry in here!" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim, it is dry in the shop. That's because they keep it dry so the food stays fresh. Now, let's go pay for our things and go home." Tim helped his mom put the food on the counter so the shop worker could count it all. After they paid, Tim and his mom left the shop and went home. At home, Tim helped his mom put away all the food they bought. He was proud of himself for being such a big helper at the shop. Tim's mom gave him a hug and said, "Thank you for being such a good helper today, Tim. I love you." Tim smiled and said, "I love you too, Mom!"
