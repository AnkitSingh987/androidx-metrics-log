Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Peach

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to decorate her room with pretty things. One day, she found a big, red peach in her garden. She was very happy and picked it up. Lily showed the red peach to her friend, Tom. "Look, Tom!" she said, "I found a red peach in my garden. It's so big and pretty! I want to use it to decorate my room." Tom thought it was a great idea. "Wow, Lily! That's a beautiful peach. Let's put it on your table, so everyone can see it when they come to your room." Together, Lily and Tom put the red peach on the table in Lily's room. It looked very nice, and Lily was proud of her special decoration. They played and laughed, and had a fun day with the big, red peach.

One day, a wet cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big umbrella on the ground. He picked it up and opened it. The umbrella was colorful and pretty. Tom was happy because he would not get wet anymore. Tom walked in the rain with his umbrella. He saw a small dog named Sam. Sam was wet and sad. Tom said, "Hi, Sam! Do you want to share my umbrella?" Sam said, "Yes, please!" Tom and Sam walked together under the umbrella. They were both dry and happy. The rain stopped, and they saw a big, bright rainbow. They were glad they could be a part of this beautiful day.

Once upon a time, there was an enormous motorcycle. It was red and shiny. A kind man named Tom had the motorcycle. He liked to ride it very fast. One day, Tom had to send a big box to his friend. He thought, "I will use my motorcycle to send the box." Tom put the big box on his motorcycle. He started to ride very fast. But something unexpected happened. The big box fell off the motorcycle! Tom was sad. He did not know what to do. He looked inside the box to see if anything was broken. Inside the box, Tom found a big, shiny ball. The ball was magic! It could talk! The ball said, "I will help you send the box to your friend." The ball jumped on the motorcycle and held the box tight. Tom and the magic ball rode the enormous motorcycle together. They sent the box to Tom's friend, and they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a beautiful bird was flying in the sky. It saw a rod on the ground. The bird thought, "I can pick up the rod and scatter seeds for my friends." The bird picked up the rod in its beak and flew to a big tree. There, it saw many seeds. The bird used the rod to scatter the seeds on the ground. The other birds saw this and became very happy. One of the birds said, "Thank you for scattering the seeds! Now we can eat!" The beautiful bird smiled and said, "You're welcome! I am glad I could help." The birds all ate the seeds and played together, happy to have a good friend like the beautiful bird.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Rachel and Tom. They were very happy to be together. Rachel was chubby, and Tom was slim. One day, Tom said to Rachel, "Let's clean the house!" Rachel did not like to clean, but Tom encouraged her so she said okay. First, they needed a mop to clean the floor. They found a big mop with chubby handles. They both smiled as they used the mop to clean the floor. It was fun! When they finished, they were happy with the result. Rachel said, "It looks lovely, Tom. Thank you for encouraging me to do this." Tom smiled and gave Rachel a big hug.

Ben did not like gloomy days. He wanted to play outside with his ball and his bike, but the sky was gray and the rain was wet. He felt sad and bored in his room. He did not want to read his books or draw his pictures. He wanted to go out. His mom came to his room and saw his face. She smiled and hugged him. She said, "I know you don't like gloomy days, Ben. But they are part of life. Sometimes we have to accept things we don't like and find ways to have fun anyway. Do you want to help me bake some cookies in the kitchen? We can make them with chocolate and nuts and eat them with milk." Ben thought for a moment. He still wanted to play outside, but he also liked cookies and milk. He liked helping his mom in the kitchen. He liked the smell of the oven and the taste of the dough. He nodded and said, "Okay, mom. I will help you bake some cookies. Maybe the sun will come out later." His mom kissed his cheek and said, "That's a good boy, Ben. Come on, let's go to the kitchen and have some fun. Maybe we can share some cookies with our neighbors too. They might also feel gloomy on this day." Ben followed his mom to the kitchen. He put on his apron and washed his hands. He helped his mom measure the ingredients and mix the batter. He used a spoon to drop the dough on the baking sheet. He watched the cookies bake in the oven. He smelled the chocolate and the nuts. He felt happy and warm. He accepted the gloomy day and made the best of it. He learned that sometimes, cookies and mom can make any day better.
