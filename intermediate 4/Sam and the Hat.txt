Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam and the Hat

Sam liked to play with his toy soldiers. He had many of them, but his favorite was the hairy one. The hairy soldier had a big hat, a long coat and a shiny sword. Sam pretended that the hairy soldier was the leader of his army, and he gave him orders to fight the bad guys. One day, Sam was playing with his soldiers on the floor, when he heard his mom call him for lunch. He wanted to finish his game, so he said, "Just a minute, Mom!" He moved his soldiers closer to the bad guys, and made them shoot and shout. He made the hairy soldier reach for his sword, and swing it at the enemy. But as he did that, he knocked over a glass of water that was on the table. The water spilled all over the floor, and some of it splashed on his soldiers. Sam gasped, and quickly picked up his toys. He saw that the hairy soldier was wet and dripping. His hat was soggy, his coat was stained and his sword was bent. Sam felt sad, and said, "Oh no, hairy soldier! You are ruined!" He ran to his mom, and showed her his toy. He hoped that she could fix it, or get him a new one. His mom looked at the hairy soldier, and smiled. She said, "Don't worry, Sam. It's just a little water. We can dry him off, and clean him up. And maybe we can find a way to straighten his sword. He will be fine, and you can play with him again." She hugged Sam, and took him to the kitchen. She gave him a towel, and told him to rub the hairy soldier gently. She said, "You know, Sam, the hairy soldier is very brave. He fought hard, and he didn't give up. He is a good leader, and a good friend. You should be proud of him, and take good care of him." Sam nodded, and smiled. He felt better, and thanked his mom. He dried the hairy soldier, and put him on a plate. He said, "You are right, Mom. He is brave, and he is my friend. I love him, and I will take good care of him." He looked at the hairy soldier, and said, "Don't worry, hairy soldier. You are not ruined. You are a hero!"

Once upon a time, a boy named Tom went to school. Tom liked school because he had many friends. Tom also liked to learn about history. History was fun for him because he could learn about things that happened a long time ago. One day, Tom's teacher said, "Kids, let's play a game. I will give you a word and you have to show me what it means." Tom's teacher gave him the word "organized." Tom thought for a moment and then started to put his toys in a neat row. "Look, teacher! I made my toys organized!" Tom said proudly. His teacher smiled and said, "Good job, Tom!" Later, Tom was playing with his friends when he saw something strange. A little girl was holding a big book. The book had the word "history" on it. Tom realized that the girl was holding his history book! He was surprised because he didn't know how she got it. Tom went to the girl and said, "Hey, that's my history book!" The girl smiled and said, "I found it on the ground. I like history too!" Tom and the girl became friends and they loved learning about history together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He had a brother named Sam. They liked to play outside in the sun. One day, Tom was very hungry. He said, "I need food." Sam heard his brother and said, "I will help you find food." They went to the kitchen to look for something to eat. They found some bread and made a yummy sandwich. Tom and Sam shared the sandwich and were not hungry anymore. They went back outside to play and have fun. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small hut, there lived a happy dog named Spot. Spot was a very glad dog who loved to play with his ball all day. The hut was his home, and he liked to sleep there at night. One day, Spot saw a big box near the hut. He wanted to open it and see what was inside. He pushed the box with his nose and it opened! Inside the box, he found a big, bouncy ball. Spot was so glad and excited to play with the new ball. Spot played with the new ball all day long. He ran, jumped, and chased the ball around the hut. When the sun went down, Spot was very tired. He went back to the hut, curled up, and fell asleep. Spot had a great day and was glad he found the new ball.
