Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bush and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a big bush. The bush was near a little house. In the house lived a boy named Tim and his mom. One day, Tim saw the bush grow very big. It had many red berries on it. Tim wanted to pick the berries and eat them. "Mom, can I eat the berries from the bush?" Tim asked. His mom looked at the bush and said, "No, Tim, those berries are not good for you. They can make you sick." Tim was sad but he listened to his mom. One day, a fierce dog came to their house. The dog saw the bush with the red berries. The dog did not know that the berries were bad. The dog ate the berries from the bush. The dog got very sick from eating the berries. Tim and his mom tried to help the dog, but it was too late. The dog did not get better. Tim and his mom were very sad. They wished the bush with the bad berries was not near their house.

One day, a little boy named Tim was very enthusiastic. He wanted to play a fun game. He thought, "I want to drive a car!" But he was too small to drive a real car, so he had a big idea. Tim went to his couch and said, "This couch is my car!" He sat on the couch and made car sounds. He drove his couch car around the room. He was very happy. Then, something unexpected happened. The couch started to move! It drove around the room for real! Tim was so surprised and excited. He drove his couch car all day and had lots of fun.

Lily was a little girl who liked to follow her big brother Sam. Sam was very wise and knew many things. He taught Lily how to tie her shoes, how to ride a bike, and how to draw animals. Lily wanted to be like Sam when she grew up. One day, Sam told Lily he had a surprise for her. He said he found something amazing in the woods and he wanted to show her. Lily was very excited and followed Sam to the woods. She hoped the surprise was a butterfly, a flower, or a bird. But when they got to the woods, Lily saw that the surprise was not a butterfly, a flower, or a bird. It was a big, scary, angry bear. The bear roared and ran towards them. Lily was very scared and screamed. Sam was scared too and grabbed Lily's hand. They ran away from the bear as fast as they could. They reached their home and slammed the door behind them. They were safe, but they were still shaking. Lily hugged Sam and cried. Sam hugged Lily and said he was sorry. He said he was not wise, he was foolish. He said he should not have followed the bear's tracks, he should have listened to his mom. He said he should not have put Lily in danger, he should have protected her. Lily forgave Sam and said she loved him. She said he was still wise, but he made a mistake. She said everyone makes mistakes, but they can learn from them. She said she learned not to follow strangers, even if they promise surprises. She said she learned to follow her mom, her dad, and her brother, because they love her and care for her. She said she learned to be brave, but also to be careful. She said she learned a lot from Sam, even from his mistake.

Once upon a time, in a quiet town, there lived a bird named Bobo. Bobo was in a cage, and he was sad. He wanted to fly and see his friends. One day, a girl named Lily came to supply food for Bobo. She saw that Bobo was sad. Lily asked, "Why are you sad, Bobo?" Bobo said, "I want to fly and see my friends." Lily thought for a moment and then opened the cage. Bobo flew out and was very happy. He said, "Thank you, Lily!" Lily smiled and said, "You're welcome, Bobo. Have fun with your friends." Bobo flew and played with his friends all day. When the sun went down, Bobo came back to Lily. He said, "I had so much fun, Lily! Can I stay with you?" Lily said, "Of course, Bobo. You can be my friend too." And so, Bobo and Lily became best friends. Bobo was never sad again, and they lived happily ever after.
