Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Max and the Car

Lily and Max are playing with their toy cars in the living room. They have a big track with ramps and loops. They like to make their cars go fast and do tricks. "Look, Lily, I can make my car reverse and jump over the loop!" Max says, showing off his red car. "Wow, Max, that is good!" Lily says, clapping her hands. She has a yellow car that she likes to spin and flip. They take turns to make their cars do different things. They have fun and laugh a lot. Soon, they hear their mom calling them from the kitchen. "Lily and Max, it's time for a snack! Come and have some juice and cookies!" They leave their cars on the track and run to the kitchen. They see two cups of juice and two plates of cookies on the table. "Thank you, mom!" they say, sitting down and grabbing their snacks. "You're welcome, my loves. Did you have fun with your cars?" mom asks, smiling. "Yes, mom, we did! We made them reverse and jump and spin and flip!" Lily and Max say, excited. "That sounds like fun! Maybe you can show me later. But first, drink your juice and eat your cookies. They are good for you!" mom says, giving them a hug.

Once upon a time, there was a delicate mouse named Molly. She lived in a small hole near a big tree. Molly was very happy, but she wanted a friend to play with. One day, Molly met a kind mouse named Max. They played together and had lots of fun. Molly told Max, "I want to marry you, so we can be friends forever." Max smiled and said, "I want to marry you, too, Molly." Molly and Max told their families about their wish to marry. Their families were very happy and started to plan a big party. They knew something special was going to happen soon. On the wedding day, Molly and Max wore beautiful clothes. All their friends came to the party. They danced, ate yummy food, and played fun games. At the end of the day, Molly and Max were married and became the best friends forever.

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing outside in the sunshine. She was very happy. She saw a small cat looking nervous. Lily wanted to help the cat. Lily went to the cat and gave it a big hug. The cat was scared at first, but then it started to like Lily. They played together all day. Then, something unexpected happened. The cat jumped up and knocked over a cup of water. Lily was upset, but she knew she needed to forgive the cat. She said, "It's okay, cat. I forgive you." The cat was happy and gave Lily a big hug. Then, they went back to playing in the sunshine.

Once upon a time, there was an old tent. The tent was very big and blue. It was in the woods, where many animals lived. A bear, a rabbit, and a squirrel all liked to play near the tent. One day, the bear wanted to weigh himself. He asked his friends, the rabbit and the squirrel, to help him. They found a big rock and put it on one side of a log. The bear sat on the other side of the log. The rabbit and the squirrel watched as the bear went up and down. The old tent saw the friends playing and wanted to join them. The tent opened its door and invited the bear, the rabbit, and the squirrel inside. They all went in and had a fun time playing together. The old tent was very happy to have friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was a selfish dog. He did not like to share his toys with his friends. One day, Max found a big box with a label on it. The label had a picture of a bone. Max was very happy. Max took the box to a quiet place to nap. He wanted to keep the box all to himself. While Max was napping, his friend, a little bird named Lily, saw the box. Lily was curious about the box with the label. Lily wanted to share the box with Max, but Max was still selfish. When Max woke up from his nap, he saw Lily looking at the box. Max decided to share the box with Lily. Inside the box, they found many toys and bones. Max and Lily played together and had lots of fun. Max learned that sharing with friends is better than being selfish.
