Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Leaf

One day, a girl named Mia went to the garden. She saw a pretty red rose. The rose was hiding under a big leaf. Mia wanted to see the whole rose. She said, "Come out, pretty rose!" Mia moved the leaf to reveal the rose. The rose was happy. It said, "Thank you, Mia!" But the sun was very harsh. The rose did not like the hot sun. Mia wanted to help the rose. She found a big hat. She put the hat over the rose. The rose was happy again. It said, "Thank you, Mia! You are a good friend."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a friend named Tom. They liked to play together in the park. They loved to climb trees and run around. One day, they found a tree with small, round things on it. They were prunes! Sue and Tom picked some prunes to eat. They were sweet and yummy. Sue wanted to remember this fun day, so she took a prune home with her. She put it in a special box to keep it safe. Every time Sue looked at the prune, she remembered the fun day with Tom. They had more fun days at the park, but the day they found the prunes was very special. Sue always kept the little prune to remember her best friend and their happy times together.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a poor cat. The cat was sad because its fur was wet from the rain. It wanted to be dry, but it did not know how. One day, a kind wind came to the cat. The wind said, "I can help you dry your fur." The cat was happy and asked the wind to help. The wind blew on the cat, and its fur began to dry. The cat was happy and thanked the wind. With its fur now dry, the cat played outside in the sun. The cat and the wind became good friends, and they lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim went to his friend's house to play. His friend, Sara, wanted to introduce him to her new toy. Sara said, "Tim, come see my new toy in the closet!" They walked to the closet together. Inside the closet, there were many toys. But there was one crazy toy that looked different. It had big eyes and a funny hat. Tim and Sara laughed at the crazy toy. They did not know that the toy could do something special. They played with the toy and other toys for a while. Then, the crazy toy started to move by itself! Tim and Sara were surprised. The toy began to dance and sing. They laughed and danced with the toy. It was a fun and unexpected day for Tim and Sara.

Lily liked to draw with her crayons. She drew many things: flowers, animals, stars, and her family. She was very proud of her drawings and wanted to show them to everyone. One day, her mom took her to her office. Her mom worked with many people who used computers and phones. Lily was curious and excited. She saw a huge window with a view of the city. She saw many desks and chairs and papers. She saw a big board where people wrote and drew with markers. "Mom, can I draw on the board?" Lily asked. "Sure, honey, but only in this corner, okay? And don't erase anything else," her mom said. Lily nodded and took a marker. She drew a big heart and wrote "I love mom" inside. She drew a smiley face and a flower next to it. She was happy with her drawing and wanted to show it to her mom. "Mom, look what I drew!" Lily said. Her mom looked and smiled. She hugged Lily and said, "That's very sweet, honey. I love it. Thank you." Lily felt warm and proud. She had made her mom happy with her drawing. She had also made a display for everyone to see. She hoped they would like it too. She wondered what else she could draw on the board.
