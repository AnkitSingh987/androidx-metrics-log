Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Woods and the Excited

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved to explore and discover new things. She always had excited and happy thoughts in her mind. One day, she was out in the woods looking around when she found something special - a camera! She got very excited and took lots of pictures with it. After a while she got very tired, so she decided to go home and have a bath. After having the bath, she felt fresh and ready for bed. The next day, when she looked through the pictures on the camera, she found a picture of a bush with bright and tasty berries on it! She was so excited to find this picture that she asked her parents if she could go back to the same spot and pick some of the berries. They agreed and drove her to the spot. After picking lots of the yummy berries, she went home and showed her parents her haul. They were so pleased and praised her for her discovery. Her mum then made a tasty berry pie for the family to share and everyone was so delighted!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom liked to play outside, even when it was cold. One day, he went outside to play in the snow. While playing, he saw a big bear. The bear was sleeping. Tom thought it was a joke, like a big toy bear. He went closer to the bear and poked it. Suddenly, the bear woke up. Tom was scared. But the bear was not angry. The bear was cold and sad, because he lost his warm hat. Tom wanted to help the bear. He took off his own hat and put it on the bear's head. The bear smiled and said "Thank you!" Now, Tom and the bear were friends. They played in the snow together. When they got tired, they went to Tom's house. The bear was not cold anymore, and they both went to sleep. The next day, Tom's mom found them and was surprised by the new friend. But she was happy, because Tom was warm and safe.

Once upon a time, there was a gym. In the gym, there were many playful kids. They would run, jump, and be happy. One day, a new kid came. He was very big and strong. The big kid wanted to be the boss. He told the playful kids to do what he said. The playful kids did not like this, but they were scared. They knew something bad would happen. The big kid made the playful kids do hard things. They were not happy anymore. One day, the big kid made them do something very hard. The gym fell down. All the playful kids were sad. The end.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Jenny. She was three years old and often went out to play on her own. One day, Jenny saw a bright yellow wreck at the roadside. She was curious and went over for a closer look. Suddenly, someone grabbed hold of Jenny and tried to slap her. Jenny screamed and pushed the strange man away. She shouted, "Stop! I don't want you to slap me!" The man had a rough voice. He told Jenny she should not be playing near the wreck. He said, "It is dangerous here. Go back to your parents." Jenny was scared and ran away. She ran all the way home until she was safe with her parents. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a strong lion. He was the king of the jungle. All the animals lived a happy life under his rule. One day, a sly fox came to the lion and said, "I want to be the king. You must give up and let me rule." The lion did not want to surrender, but he thought of a plan. The lion said, "I will give up if you can do one thing. You must catch the fastest bird in the jungle." The fox agreed and ran to catch the bird. But the bird was too fast and the fox could not catch it. In the end, the fox had to give up. The lion stayed the king and the animals continued to live a happy life. The fox learned that being strong is not the only thing that makes a good king.
