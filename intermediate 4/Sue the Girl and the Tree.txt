Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. She had a friend named Tom. They liked to play in the park. One day, they found a big old tree. The tree had a bitter taste when they touched it. They thought it was a magic tree. Sue and Tom played near the tree every day. They made a secret fort under the tree. They had so much fun. They never told anyone about the tree. They wanted to keep it a secret. One day, Sue and Tom found a big hole near the tree. They were very excited. They thought they found a secret treasure. They started to dig the hole. But, the tree was not happy. The tree was a magic tree, and it did not like them touching the hole. The tree got very angry, and it started to shake. The tree fell down, and the secret fort was gone. Sue and Tom were very sad. They lost their secret tree, and they never found the treasure.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a pink pastry on a table. It looked yummy. Lily wanted to eat the pastry, but she didn't know who it belonged to. So, she asked her mom, "Can I eat the pink pastry, please?" Her mom said, "Let's wait and see if someone comes back for it. If no one does, you can eat it." While they waited, Lily played on the swings and the slide. She had a lot of fun. But she was still thinking about the pink pastry. Soon, it was time to go home. No one had come for the pastry. Lily's mom said, "Okay, you can eat the pastry now." Lily ate the pink pastry, and it was so sweet and tasty. After that, she felt tired from playing and eating. She said, "Mom, I want to sleep now." Her mom smiled and said, "Let's go home and take a nap." Lily agreed, and they went home. Lily slept and had sweet dreams of pink pastries.

Once upon a time, there was a silly dog named Max. Max loved to play and have fun. One day, Max went on a safari with his friend, a little girl named Lily. While on the safari, Max saw a big hole in the ground. He thought it would be fun to bury his favorite toy, a red ball, in the hole. So, Max put the ball in the hole and used his paws to cover it with dirt. Lily saw Max bury the ball and said, "Max, why did you bury your ball?" Max barked and wagged his tail, happy that he had a new game to play. Lily laughed and said, "You are such a silly dog, Max!" Together, Max and Lily dug up the ball and played with it for the rest of the safari. They had lots of fun, and Max learned that burying his toys was a silly, but fun, game to play. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was eager to help her mom deliver a cake to their neighbor. She put on her shoes and socks, but she felt something funny on her toe. Lily looked down and saw that her sock had a hole in it. "Mommy, look at my toe!" said Lily. Her mom looked and laughed. "Oh dear, let's fix that before we go," she said. They found a new sock for Lily and put it on. Then, they started walking to the neighbor's house with the cake. As they walked, a bird flew down and landed on the cake! Lily and her mom were so surprised. The bird took a big bite of the cake and then flew away. Lily and her mom laughed and decided to make a new cake for their neighbor. And from that day on, Lily always checked her socks for holes before going on an adventure.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Sam and Jack. Sam and Jack liked eating together, so they decided to make a special dish. Sam was very smart and had a great idea. He said they should both put some ingredients in the dish and mix them up. Jack was very excited and quickly gathered some vegetables, eggs, and butter. He said, “Let’s mix them all together!” So Sam and Jack put all the ingredients in a big dish and mixed them up. When it was ready, Jack said, “Let’s eat it!” But Sam said that he was not sure about the dish and wanted to check with his mom first. Jack said that was a good idea, so Sam ran to find his mom. He asked her and she said it was ok to eat the dish. So Sam and Jack ate the dish - and it was delicious! Sam was so happy that he had chosen to listen to his mom and not take any risks. The moral of the story is to be smart and always listen to your parents. They know what's best for you!
