Category: Intermediate
Title: Sammy and the Apple

Sammy was a persistent 3 year old little boy. One day, he went to the park with his mom and found an apple tree with delicious looking apples. He wanted to pick one but his mom said, "No Sammy, we will ignore it for now." Still, Sammy couldn't resist and he started climbing up the apple tree. His mom shook her head, smiled and said, "Let's go, Sammy." But Sammy was already far up the tree, so she had no choice but to wait for him to come back down. At last, Sammy managed to pick an apple and he came down from the tree, ignoring his mom's calls. He proudly presented the apple to his mom, expecting her to be impressed. But then, his mom said sternly, "Sammy, why didn't you listen to me when I told you to ignore the apple?" Sammy was surprised, but then he realized that his mom wanted him to learn the importance of listening and being persistent. He smiled and said, "Yes mom, I promise I will listen next time."

One day, a smart cat named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big tree with many leaves. The leaves were green and pretty. Tim loved to play with leaves. He wanted to show his friends how to play with leaves too. Tim called his friend, a small dog named Max. "Max, come here!" said Tim. Max came running. "What do you want to show me?" asked Max. Tim said, "I want to show you how the leaves on this tree give us air to breathe. This air is called oxygen. Let's play with the leaves and have fun!" Max and Tim started to play with the leaves. They were having so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The wind blew very hard, and all the leaves fell off the tree. The tree was now bald. Tim and Max were sad. A wise old owl saw them and said, "Don't be sad, my friends. The tree will grow new leaves soon. It will still give us oxygen to breathe. Just remember to always take care of the trees and the world around you." Tim and Max learned the importance of taking care of nature. They promised to always be kind to trees and help them grow. They knew that trees were important for giving oxygen and making the world a beautiful place to live.

Once upon a time, there was a popular little girl named Sue. Sue was very kind and always helped others. One day, she met a new friend named Tim. Tim had very dry skin and needed some lotion. Sue said, "Hi Tim! I have some lotion. Do you want some?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, please!" Sue gave Tim the lotion and he put it on his skin. It made his skin feel much better. After that, Tim and Sue became good friends. They played together every day. Tim was happy to accept Sue's help, and they both learned that sharing and helping others can make you very popular.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom liked to talk a lot. He would talk to everyone he met. He had many friends because he was so friendly. One day, Tom found a bottle of perfume. It smelled so good. He wanted to share it with his friends, so he took the perfume to the park. At the park, Tom talked to his friends about the perfume. They all wanted to smell it too! So, Tom sprayed the perfume on his friends. They all smelled very good and were very happy. They played at the park all day and had a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his ball in the park. One day, Tim saw a crazy bird. The bird took his ball and flew away. Tim was sad and wanted his ball back. He asked his mom for help. His mom said, "To get the ball back, we must give the bird a price." Tim found some bread and gave it to the bird. The bird liked the bread and returned the ball to Tim. Tim was happy and learned to be kind to others. The moral of the story is to always be kind and share.
