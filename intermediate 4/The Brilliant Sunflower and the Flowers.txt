Category: Intermediate
Title: The Brilliant Sunflower and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a brilliant sunflower. It lived in a big field with many other flowers. Every day, it would turn its head to follow the sun. One day, a little girl came to the field. She saw the brilliant sunflower and smiled. The sunflower saw the little girl and nodded its head to say hello. The little girl and the sunflower became friends. They played and laughed in the sun. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little dove. The dove was worried. It could not stand on its leg. It was sad. One day, a kind boy saw the worried dove. The boy wanted to help. He picked up the dove and took it home. The boy's mom helped him put a band on the dove's leg. After some days, the dove felt better. It could stand on its leg again. The boy and the dove were very happy. They played together every day. The dove was not worried anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy called Bob. Bob liked having adventures and so one day, he decided to go explore a stream. He put on his hat and set out on his journey. Bob walked for a long way until he found the stream he had been looking for. It was a beautiful, blue stream that sparkled in the sunshine. He was so excited - but then he realised how wide it was. It was too wide to jump across. Bob burst into tears until he had an idea. He found two comfortable stones and put them next to the stream. Then, he made a bridge. Carefully, Bob reached out and took one step, then another and another, until he was comfortable on the other side. Bob explored the other side of the stream with a huge smile on his face. He had done it. He had reached the stream and had a great new adventure!

Tom and Lily were friends who liked to play in the pool. They had fun splashing, diving and swimming. One day, they saw a big cake on the table near the pool. It looked delicious. It had pink frosting, sprinkles and candles. "Wow, look at that cake!" Tom said. "Do you think we can have some?" "I don't know," Lily said. "Maybe it is for someone's birthday." They went closer to the cake and smelled it. It smelled sweet and yummy. "Let's just take a little bite," Tom said. "No one will notice." He reached for the cake, but Lily stopped him. "No, Tom, that's not nice," she said. "We have to ask first. Maybe it is for Mom or Dad or someone else." Tom shrugged. He did not care. He wanted to taste the cake. He grabbed a big piece and stuffed it in his mouth. "Mmm, so good!" he said. Lily was shocked. She looked at the cake and saw a big hole. She heard someone coming. "Oh no, Tom, you're in trouble!" she said. "Someone is coming. Quick, hide!" But it was too late. Mom saw them by the cake. She saw the mess Tom made. She was angry. "Tom, what did you do?" she asked. "That cake was for Grandma's birthday. How could you be so greedy and rude?" Tom felt bad. He had a tummy ache from the cake. He did not know it was for Grandma. He wished he had listened to Lily. "I'm sorry, Mom," he said. "I was hungry and the cake looked delicious. I didn't mean to ruin Grandma's birthday." Mom sighed. She was disappointed in Tom. She told him to go to his room and think about what he did. She told Lily to help her clean up the cake. Lily felt sorry for Tom, but she was also glad she did not eat the cake. She knew it was wrong to take something without asking. She hoped Tom would learn his lesson. She hoped Grandma would still have a happy birthday.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. She loved to play with her toy box. One day, she found a big, smooth button in her toy box. She did not know what it was for. Sue showed the button to her mom. Her mom said, "Let's settle down and think. Maybe this button is magic." Sue got excited and pushed the button. Suddenly, her toy box started to shake! All her toys came alive and started to dance. Sue was so happy and surprised. She danced with her toys all day. When it was time for bed, Sue pushed the button again. The toys went back into the toy box. Sue knew she had a magic button and couldn't wait to play again tomorrow.
