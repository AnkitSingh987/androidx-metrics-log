Category: Intermediate
Title: The Lazy Motor and the Jack

Once upon a time, there was a lazy motor. It was in the garage, and it wanted to move, but it wouldn't. One day, Jack and Jill walked into the garage. Jack said to the motor, "Lazy motor, can you please help us?" The motor replied, "What do you want me to do?" Jack said, "Can you take us for a ride?" The motor said, "Yes, of course! I would love to take you for a ride!" Jack and Jill were very excited. They knelt down next to the motor and Jack turned it on. The motor roared to life and Jack and Jill hopped on. They went on a fine ride around the town until it was time to go back home. Jack and Jill thanked the motor and knelt down next to it to give it a big hug. Then, they said their goodbyes and the motor went back to sleep in the garage.

One day, a boy named Tim found a brick. Tim wanted to show his friend, Sam. So, he put the brick in his bag and went to Sam's house. Sam's room was very tidy. "Look, Sam," Tim said, "I found a brick!" Sam looked at the brick and said, "Wow! Can I hold it?" Tim gave the brick to Sam. They played with the brick and had fun. But then, Sam dropped the brick on his foot. "Ouch!" Sam cried. Tim felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, Sam." Sam's mom came in and saw the brick. She was not happy. "No more playing with bricks," she said. Tim and Sam were sad, but they knew they had to listen. They put the brick away and did not play with it again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that he loved to play with. It was a loud car that went "vroom vroom" when he pushed it. Tim's mom gave him a new battery to make the car go even faster. One day, Tim wanted to play with his loud car outside. He asked his mom, "Can I play with my car outside, please?" His mom said, "Yes, I will allow you to play with your car outside, but stay near the house." Tim was so happy! He took his loud car outside and put in the new battery. The car went "vroom vroom" even louder and faster than before. Tim had so much fun playing with his car all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a unique cat named Fluffy. Fluffy lived in a small house with a big fireplace. She loved to sit by the warm fireplace all day. One day, Fluffy saw a little mouse run by. "Oh no!" said Fluffy. "I must hurry and catch the mouse!" So, Fluffy ran fast to chase the mouse. They ran all around the house, but the mouse was too quick. Finally, Fluffy stopped and said, "Wait, little mouse! Let's be friends instead." The mouse thought about it and said, "Okay, let's be friends." So, Fluffy and the mouse sat by the warm fireplace together, happy to be friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big stadium. In the stadium, many people would stand and watch games. They would clap and cheer for their favorite teams. The stadium was a fun place for everyone. One day, a scary monster came to the stadium. The monster was big and loud. The people in the stadium were scared. They did not want to stand and watch the games anymore. They wanted the monster to go away. A brave little boy saw the scary monster. He wanted to help the people in the stadium. The boy stood up and faced the monster. He said, "Go away, scary monster!" The monster saw the brave boy and felt scared too. The monster left the stadium. The people were happy again. They stood up and cheered for the brave little boy. The stadium was a fun place once more.

Once there was a girl who was only three years old. She was playing in the garden. She liked to read books. One day, she saw a towel lying on the ground. She picked it up and read the label on it. It said it was a special towel that could make her feel strong. The girl was feeling weak, so she decided to use the towel. She wrapped it around her body, and soon she was feeling much stronger. She felt like she could do anything! The girl was so excited. She wanted to show her parents what the towel could do. She ran inside to find them, and showed them the towel. Her parents were amazed, and they all hugged each other happily. From then on, the girl knew the power of a special towel. Whenever she felt weak, she wrapped the towel around her and felt strong again.
