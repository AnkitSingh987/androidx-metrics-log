Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Bird

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They found a soft, green spot to sit under a big tree. Tim loved to sit and listen to the rhythm of the birds singing in the trees. A funny-looking bird landed next to Tim and started to talk. "Hello, Tim!" said the bird. Tim was very surprised, but he said, "Hello, bird! I like your rhythm." The bird smiled and said, "Thank you! I have a secret to tell you. I am not just a bird, I am a magic bird! I can grant you one wish." Tim thought for a moment and said, "I wish to understand the rhythm of all the animals in the world!" The magic bird clapped its wings, and suddenly, Tim could hear and understand the rhythm of all the animals around him. He was so happy and danced to their songs. Tim and his mom spent the whole day at the park, and Tim shared the beautiful rhythms with her.

Once upon a time there was a brave little girl who wanted to make a cake. The girl went to the store and got the ingredients she needed. The ingredients cost a lot of money and she only had zero dollars and cents. She was a bit worried about how she could make the cake now. But the brave little girl decided she would try something. She decided to yield some of her family's fruit from the garden. She carefully picked some apples, pears, and raisins and put them in a basket. She excitedly rushed home to start making her cake. The little girl used the fruit to make a delicious and yummy cake. Everyone who ate it said it was even tastier than the cakes at the store. The brave little girl was very proud of her efforts and her family was so proud of her too!

One day, a boy named Tom went to the park with his mom. They saw a weak, old tree. The tree had a rot spot on it. Tom asked his mom, "Mom, why does the tree look like that?" His mom said, "The tree is weak because it is old, and the rot is the result of bugs eating it." Tom felt sad for the tree. He wanted to help it. He thought and thought. Then, he had an idea. He ran home and got a big bag of dirt. He put the dirt around the tree. He hoped it would help the tree feel better. The next day, Tom went back to the park with his mom. They saw that the tree was not weak anymore. It was big and strong! The rot spot was gone too! The tree had turned into a magical tree that could talk! The tree said, "Thank you, Tom, for helping me. As a result of your kindness, I am strong again. You have a big heart." Tom and his mom were very surprised and happy. They knew that even a small act of kindness could make a big change.

Tommy was in the kitchen with his mom. He watched her as she put on a white apron and prepared the food. Tommy was very excited and urged his mom. "Let's go!" he said. His mom smiled. She knew Tommy wanted to go outside and play in the garden. She finished up the food and got ready to go out. She tied the strings of the apron tightly around her waist and urged Tommy to put on his shoes. Tommy quickly put on his shoes and followed his mom out the door. When they got outside, Tommy ran to the garden. He smiled as the sun shone down and he saw the colorful flowers. His mom watched him with a smile and urged him to enjoy himself. And so, in the garden, with the sun shining and the flowers blooming, Tommy had lots of fun.

Anna and Ben are playing in the kitchen. They see a big bowl of salad on the table. Anna likes salad, but Ben does not. "Can I touch the salad?" Anna asks. "OK, but be careful. Mommy will be mad if you make a mess," Ben says. Anna reaches for the salad. She feels something green and long. It is celery. She breaks off a piece and puts it in her mouth. It is tough and crunchy. She likes it. "Yuck, celery is gross," Ben says. "It is too tough and has no taste. I like cheese and crackers better." He takes a cheese and cracker from a plate and eats it. It is soft and yummy. He likes it. "Can I have some cheese and cracker?" Anna asks. "OK, but you have to give me some celery," Ben says. They trade their snacks and try them. Anna thinks the cheese and cracker is good, but not as good as celery. Ben thinks the celery is bad, but not as bad as he thought. They smile at each other and finish their snacks. They are happy to share and try new things. Mommy comes in and sees them. "Good job, Anna and Ben. You are eating your salad and cheese and crackers. You are very brave and polite," Mommy says. She hugs them and gives them a glass of milk. They drink it and say thank you. They are proud of themselves and love Mommy.
