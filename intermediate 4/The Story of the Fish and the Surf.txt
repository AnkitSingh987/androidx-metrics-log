Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Fish and the Surf

One day, a big, impressive fish wanted to surf. He loved to surf on the big waves in the sea. All the other little fish watched him and clapped their fins. They liked to see the big fish surf. But one day, a very big wave came, and the big fish could not surf on it. He was scared of the big wave. The little fish saw this and they wanted to help the big fish. They had an idea! The little fish went to the sea king and asked him to make the big wave smaller. The sea king said they had to pay him with shiny shells to make the wave smaller. The little fish found many shiny shells and gave them to the sea king. The sea king made the big wave smaller, and the big fish could surf again. The big fish was very happy, and he said thank you to the little fish. Now, they all surf together on the waves.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a cheerful girl named Lily. She loved to wave at people when they walked by her house. She would always smile and say "hello" to make them happy. One day, Lily saw an old man sitting on a bench near the park. He looked sad and lonely. Lily had an idea. She walked up to the old man and waved at him with a big smile. The old man looked at her and smiled back. He pointed at the park and asked if she wanted to play. Lily and the old man played in the park all day. They laughed and had so much fun. The old man was not sad anymore. Lily learned that a simple wave and a smile can make someone's day better. And the old man learned that making new friends can bring happiness. They both promised to keep waving and smiling at people to spread joy in their town.

Once upon a time there was a little boy called Tim. He wanted to play a game. So he chose a rope and started to play. But his friends were not having fun. Tim was thoughtful and came up with a new game. He went to each of his friends and said, "Let's play a new game. I'll use the rope and you can pick something to make it with!" His friends were excited, and each chose something different. One friend chose stones, another chose sticks and Tim's little brother chose leaves. They all worked together to weave the rope and make a beautiful bracelet for Tim. "Thank you for the thoughtful gift!" said Tim happily. With his new rope, he found even more ways to play.

One day, a big group of kids went to the fair. They were very happy. They saw a big ice-cream stand with many colors. They all wanted to try some. As they walked, they started to march like little soldiers. They laughed and had fun. They marched all the way to the ice-cream stand. When they got there, the ice-cream man said, "I have a surprise!" He showed them a new ice-cream flavor that changed colors. The kids were so excited and tried the new ice-cream. It was a very fun day at the fair.

Once upon a time, there was a pink crib in a small room. It was a very nice crib. A baby girl named Lily slept in the crib. She had a toy bunny that she loved very much. Every night, her mom would tell her a story before she went to sleep. One day, Lily woke up and saw that her toy bunny was not in the crib. She was very sad. She looked around the room but could not find her bunny. Lily started to cry because she missed her bunny. Just then, her mom came into the room with a big smile. She had a surprise for Lily. She showed Lily that her toy bunny was not lost. It was just hiding under the crib! Lily was so happy to see her bunny again. From that day on, Lily and her bunny played hide and seek together, and they had lots of fun.
