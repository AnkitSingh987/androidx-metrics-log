Category: Intermediate
Title: The Ball of Tim the Boy

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. He wanted to get his ball from the tree. It was cold outside, but Tim did not care. He was too excited to play. Tim tried to reach the ball, but he was too small. He felt impatient. He called his friend, Sam. "Sam, can you help me get my ball?" Tim asked. Sam came running over to help. Sam was taller than Tim, so he could reach the ball. He got the ball for Tim, and they both played together. They laughed and had fun, even though it was cold. Soon, they went inside to warm up and have some hot cocoa.

Once there was a boy named Donny who loved playing in the park. Every day his mum would watch from their living room window as he ran around, chasing birds and playing tag with his friends. One day when Donny was walking home, his mum noticed he was limping. She asked him what had happened and he said he'd twisted his ankle while running around. His mum said to him, "Come on, let's get you inside and make you comfortable." Once inside, his mum looked at his ankle and said, "I think you might need some medicine for this ankle." Donny was very scared and asked his mum, "Will it hurt?" She smiled and said, "It's not going to hurt, I promise. I dare you to take it like a brave boy." Donny knew his mum was not lying, so he took the medicine and soon the pain slowly subsided. His mum hugged him and said, "I'm so proud of you for being so brave. Now let's get you even more comfortable." Donny snuggled into his bed and closed his eyes, feeling much better. He knew he had been very brave and faced his fear with courage. From then on, Donny was never scared to take his medicine again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to eat rice. One day, her mom made some fancy rice with many colors. Mia was very happy and excited to try it. Mia's friend, Tim, came over to play. When they sat down to eat, Tim saw the fancy rice and asked, "What is this?" Mia smiled and said, "It's fancy rice, my mom made it. Do you want to try some?" Tim was not sure, but he said yes. They both ate the fancy rice and loved it. Mia and Tim learned that trying new things can be fun and yummy. They also learned to always question and share, so they can learn and enjoy together.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Lily. She loved to play outside in the sun. One day, she saw a big tree and wanted to measure how tall it was. Lily asked her friend, Tom, "Can you help me measure the tree?" Tom said, "Yes, let's use our feet to measure it." So, they started walking around the tree, counting their steps. But as they walked, they didn't see the big hole near the tree. They both fell into the hole and couldn't get out. They were sad because they never found out how tall the tree was.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a friend, an old dog named Max. They played together every day. One day, Tim saw Max was hurt. He had a cut on his leg. Tim wanted to help Max. He knew he needed to trust his mom to make Max feel better. Tim told his mom about Max's hurt leg. She put a bandage on it and gave Max a hug. Tim was happy and knew he could trust his mom. Max felt better, and they played together again. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a terrible man with a big mustache. He liked to yell a lot and everyone was scared of him. One day, the man saw a little girl playing with her dolly by her house. He wanted the dolly so he yelled "give it to me!" The little girl was scared so she ran away and hid. The man yelled even louder and said "I must have that dolly". The little girl didn't know what to do so she asked her mommy for help. Her mommy got really mad and yelled right back at the terrible man. She told him to leave the little girl alone and that's exactly what he did. The little girl was safe and never saw the man with the mustache again.
