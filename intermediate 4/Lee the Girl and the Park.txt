Category: Intermediate
Title: Lee the Girl and the Park

Once there was a girl named Lee who was very friendly. She had a passport that she kept carefully in her bag. One day, while walking in the park, she saw a man she didn't know. The man said to her, "Can I have a look at your passport?" Lee wasn't sure what to do. She thought about trusting the man, but she was scared. She didn't know him, so it made her feel uneasy. The man noticed the worry in Lee’s face. He said, "I'm sorry for asking; it was wrong of me. You can always trust your own instincts, especially when it comes to people you don't know." Lee smiled. She realised that it was important to trust herself instead of everyone else. She knew that trusting too easily could put her in danger. Now, whenever she's not sure of something, she always relies on her own instincts.

Lily and Tom were playing in the garden. They liked to look at the flowers and the bugs. They saw a big green plant with many sharp points. It was a cactus. "What is that?" Lily asked. "It is a cactus. It can hurt you if you touch it. It lives in the desert where it is very hot and dry," Tom said. "Why does it have so many points?" Lily wondered. "They are called spines. They help the cactus to protect itself from animals and to save water," Tom explained. Lily and Tom were curious. They wanted to see the cactus closer. They walked slowly and carefully towards it. They saw something shiny on top of the cactus. It was a red balloon. "Look, a balloon!" Lily exclaimed. "Who put it there?" Tom asked. They heard a giggle behind a bush. It was their big sister, Anna. She had a pump and a basket of balloons. She had been blowing up balloons and putting them on the cactus to surprise Lily and Tom. "Surprise!" Anna shouted. She came out of the bush and smiled. Lily and Tom were surprised. They laughed and clapped. They thought the cactus looked funny and pretty with the red balloon. "Can we have a balloon too?" Lily asked. "Of course. Here, you can choose your favorite color," Anna said. She gave them each a balloon. They thanked Anna and hugged her. They played with the balloons and the cactus. They had a lot of fun. They learned something new about the cactus. They were happy.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a blue rock on the ground. Lily picked it up and smiled. She wanted to take the rock home. On her way, she met a boy named Tom. Tom saw the blue rock and frowned. He said, "That's my rock! Give it back!" Lily was sad, but she didn't want to fight. She gave the rock to Tom. Tom looked at Lily and felt bad. He said, "We can share the rock. You can have it today, and I can have it tomorrow." Lily smiled and they both played with the blue rock. They became friends and shared the rock every day.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a huge slide. Tim was scared, but his mom said, "Don't worry, it will be fun!" Tim climbed up the slide and looked down. He was very high. He took a deep breath and slid down. "Whee!" he shouted. When he reached the bottom, he realized it was fun! Tim ran back to his mom and said, "Mom, I want to do it again!" His mom smiled and said, "Go ahead, have fun!" Tim went up and down the huge slide many times. He had lots of fun at the park that day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Jane. Jane was three years old and loved to explore the world. One day, she decided to explore the beach. She saw lots of interesting things there. There were tall sandcastles, big rocks and birds flying above. Then, Jane saw a bright-looking match. It was too far away for her to get it, so she waved to it. To her surprise, the match waved back! Jane laughed and waved even more, and the match waved even higher. Jane climbed up on a rock and reached out for the match, but just as she was about to grab it, the match flew away! Jane looked at where it flew and saw a hole with a tight lid on it. Jane knocked on the lid with her hands and said, "Please match, come out!" And soon enough, the match came out and flew back to Jane! She cheered and held the match tight in her hands. The end.
