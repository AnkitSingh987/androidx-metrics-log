Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Rocket

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved rockets. He had a toy rocket that he played with all the time. One day, Tim felt dizzy when he spun around too fast. He sighed and sat down. Tim's mom saw him and asked, "Why did you sigh, Tim?" Tim said, "I feel dizzy, Mom." Mom smiled and said, "I have a surprise for you. It will make you feel better. But first, you must close your eyes." Tim closed his eyes. He heard a big noise. When he opened his eyes, he saw a big rocket in his yard! Mom said, "I told you there would be a surprise. Now, you can play with your rocket and not feel dizzy." Tim was so happy. He played with his big rocket all day long. And he never felt dizzy again.

Once upon a time, there was a normal dragon named Blinky. Blinky lived in a big cave with his dragon family. Blinky liked to blink his eyes a lot, and that is how he got his name. One day, Blinky met a small bird named Chirpy. Chirpy said to Blinky, "Hello, do you want to play with me?" Blinky blinked and said, "Yes, I would like to play with you, Chirpy!" So, Blinky and Chirpy played games together and had a lot of fun. While they were playing, something unexpected happened. Blinky blinked his eyes and found out that when he blinked, he could make things change colors! Chirpy was very surprised and said, "Wow, Blinky! You have magic eyes!" Blinky blinked again and made Chirpy's feathers turn blue. They laughed and played together, happy to have found a new friend and a new game to play.

Lily liked to paint. She had many colors and brushes. She painted flowers, animals, and houses. She showed her paintings to her mom and dad. They said they were very pretty. One day, Lily had a new idea. She wanted to paint something different. She wanted to paint like the modern artists she saw in a book. They used shapes, lines, and dots. They mixed colors and made patterns. They did not paint things that looked real. Lily took her paints and paper. She went to the garden. She saw a big tree. She did not paint the tree. She painted a circle for the sun, a triangle for the roof, and a square for the door. She painted stripes and spots and swirls. She used red, blue, yellow, and green. She finished her painting. She was happy. She ran to her mom and dad. She said, "Look at my painting! It is modern! Do you like it?" Her mom and dad looked at her painting. They smiled. They said, "We love it! It is very creative and colorful. You are a great artist, Lily!" They hugged her and hung her painting on the wall. Lily felt proud. She said, "Thank you! I want to paint more modern paintings!"

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to feed her pet fish, Bubbles. Every day, Sue would give Bubbles food and watch him swim around in his bowl. One day, Sue's big sister, Jane, was doing her math homework. She looked very serious. Sue wanted to play, but Jane said, "I need to finish my math first." Sue was sad, but she had an idea. Sue said, "I will feed Bubbles and you do your math. When you are done, we can play together." Jane agreed, and they both did their jobs. Sue fed Bubbles, and Jane finished her math. After that, they played together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a small house with a big yard, lived a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot. They loved to play in the yard all day. Tim would throw a ball, and Spot would run and catch it. They were very happy. One sunny day, Tim and Spot were playing in the yard. Tim's mom came out and said, "Tim, it's time to settle down and eat lunch." Tim and Spot stopped playing and went inside. While they were eating, they heard a loud noise from the yard. They looked out the window and saw a naughty squirrel knocking over their toys. Tim and Spot ran outside to shoo the squirrel away. The squirrel looked at them and said, "I'm sorry, I didn't mean to be naughty. I just wanted to play with you in the yard." Tim and Spot were surprised that the squirrel could talk, but they decided to let him join their playtime. From that day on, Tim, Spot, and the squirrel played together in the yard every day. They all became good friends, and the squirrel promised to never be naughty again. And they all lived happily ever after.
