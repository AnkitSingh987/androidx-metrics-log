Category: Intermediate
Title: Kitty the Cat and the Mirror

One day, a playful cat named Kitty saw her image in a big mirror. She was very surprised. Kitty thought she found a new friend. She said, "Hi, who are you?" Kitty jumped and her image jumped too. She tried to reverse and walk backward, and her image did the same. Kitty laughed and said, "You are funny! Let's play!" Kitty and her image played all day. They ran, jumped, and reversed together. Kitty was very happy to have a new friend.

One day, a furry cat named Tom went to the park. He saw a big slide and wanted to play on it. He looked around and saw a little girl named Sue. Tom walked up to Sue and asked, "Can you help me?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes! I can hold you and help you slide down the slide." So, Sue picked up Tom and they went to the slide. She held Tom in her arms and they slid down together. They both laughed and had lots of fun. Just as they were about to slide down again, a big dog named Max came running up to them. Tom was scared, but Max said, "Don't be scared! I want to slide too!" Sue, Tom, and Max all went to the slide together. Sue held Tom, and Max slid down with them. They all became friends and played at the park every day.

Once upon a time, there was a cat named Twist. Twist loved to play with a toy that looked like a coffee cup. It was his favorite toy in the whole world. Twist would run and jump and twist in the air to catch the coffee toy. One day, Twist was playing outside in the sun. The sun was very hot, and it made the ground dry. Twist did not like the dry ground, but he still wanted to play with his coffee toy. So, he twisted and jumped and ran all around the dry ground. Twist played for a long time, and he got very tired. He took his coffee toy and went back inside the house. The house was cool and not dry like outside. Twist was happy to be inside. He curled up with his coffee toy and took a long nap. And he dreamed of more days playing and twisting with his favorite coffee toy.

One day, a little dog named Max went for a walk. Max saw a lot of cars on the road. There was a lot of traffic. Max wanted to try to cross the road, but he was scared of the big cars. Max saw a nice lady on the other side of the road. The lady was cold because it was a cold day. She had a big, warm coat and a scarf. Max wanted to help the lady feel warm and happy. Max tried to cross the road when the traffic stopped. He ran to the lady and gave her a big, warm hug. The lady felt warm and happy. Max and the lady became best friends and walked home together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big, fearful dog. He was always barking and running around. All the other animals were scared of him. One day, a little boy found a spray bottle. He thought it would be fun to play with. He filled it with water and went outside to play. He saw the fearful dog barking at the other animals. The little boy had an idea. He walked up to the dog and sprayed water on him. The dog stopped barking and looked at the boy. The boy sprayed him again and the dog started to play with the boy. They became friends and played all day. The other animals saw this and joined them. Soon, they all formed a big network of friends. The town was happy and full of laughter.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Isla. Isla was three years old and loved exploring new places. One day, Isla decided to go on an adventure to the junkyard at the edge of town. Isla marched down the lane, humming a happy tune. When she arrived at the junkyard, Isla's eyes lit up. There were so many things to explore! Isla started collecting the perfect pieces of junk, excited to take them home. Eventually, sweat was dripping down Isla's face. She was hot and exhausted. Isla stopped for a moment and looked up. Right above her, she could see a huge pile of junk. It was too far for Isla to reach by herself. Isla looked around and saw an old man, sitting in the corner. She ran over to him and said, "Please help, mister. Can you reach that pile up there?" The old man chuckled and said, "Of course, my dear. I can reach it for you." With a few steps, the old man reached the top of the pile. He picked up the best junk he could find and handed it to Isla. Isla thanked the old man, with a smile on her face.
