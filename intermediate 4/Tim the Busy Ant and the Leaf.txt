Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Busy Ant and the Leaf

One day, a busy ant named Tim was walking a long way to find food. He was very small but very strong. Tim saw a big leaf with lots of tasty food on it. He wanted to take some back to his family. Tim tried to tear a part of the leaf to carry it home. As he was tearing the leaf, a friendly bee named Bella flew by. Bella saw Tim and asked, "What are you doing, Tim?" Tim said, "I am tearing this leaf to take food home to my family. But it is too big and hard for me." Bella wanted to help Tim. So, she used her strong wings to tear the leaf into smaller parts. Tim was very happy and thanked Bella for her help. Together, they carried the food back to Tim's family. From that day, Tim and Bella became best friends and always helped each other.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Tim. Tim lived near a big pine tree. One day, Tim saw a little bird on the pine tree. The bird was singing a happy song. Tim wanted to play with the bird. Tim called out to the bird, "Hi, bird! Let's play!" The bird stopped singing and looked at Tim. The bird said, "Okay, let's play. But you must not be mean to me." Tim agreed and they started to play. As they played, Tim saw a big dog coming. The dog looked mean. Tim wanted to save the bird, but he was scared. Then, something unexpected happened. The bird turned into a big, strong lion! The lion scared the dog away. But the lion was not nice to Tim. The lion said, "You should have been brave, Tim." And then, the lion left Tim all alone.

Once upon a time, there was a little napkin. The napkin liked to roll around and play all day. One day, while rolling in the park, the napkin saw a big, scary shadow. The napkin was scared and wanted to go home. The napkin tried to roll away from the scary shadow, but it followed him. The napkin was very sad and did not know what to do. Then, a nice bird saw the napkin and said, "Do not be scared. I will help you." The bird flew up and saw that the scary shadow was just a big tree. The bird told the napkin, "It is just a tree, not a scary thing." The napkin was happy and thanked the bird. They became friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time there was a loyal dog named Jazz. Jazz loved going on adventures with his family. One day his family decided to go on a special journey. Jazz was scared, but he was brave and loyal. He did not want to let his family down. The journey took them to an exciting place with lots of fun things to do. One day Jazz's family dared him to try something different. He was scared but excited. He wanted to show his family how brave he was. Jazz decided to try skydiving. He jumped out of the plane and flew through the air. He felt brave and daring! When he landed safely, he was so proud of himself. His family cheered for him. Jazz had a great time on his adventure. He showed that he was loyal, brave and a risk taker! He was the bravest dog ever!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She liked to play with her doll and hold it all day. One sunny day, Lily and her doll went for a walk in the park. While walking, Lily saw a pretty veil on the ground. She picked it up and put it on her doll's head. Lily thought her doll looked very nice. They continued to walk and play in the park. Suddenly, a small wind blew the veil off the doll's head. The veil floated up into a tree! Lily was sad, but then a friendly bird came and took the veil from the tree. The bird gave the veil back to Lily, and she and her doll were happy again. The bird had saved the day!
