Category: Intermediate
Title: The Popular Muffin and the Song

Once upon a time, there was a popular muffin. This muffin was very yummy. Everyone liked it. The muffin liked to repeat a fun song. The song made people happy. One day, a little boy came to eat the muffin. The muffin sang the fun song again. The little boy stopped. He did not eat the muffin. He liked the song too much. The little boy and the muffin became friends. The little boy and the muffin played every day. They sang the fun song together. The boy did not want to eat the muffin anymore. The muffin was happy. They were best friends forever.

Joe and his best friend Jack were playing in the park one summer day. Joe whistled happily as they ran around, laughing and having a great time. Suddenly, Jack noticed something down in the drain near the slide. “What’s that, Joe?” asked Jack, pointing at it. “It looks like an animal!” said Joe, kneeling down at the drain to get a better look. Joe tried to reach down and grab it, but it was too deep and he couldn't reach. They watched as a friendly little mouse scampered around the drain trying to get out. “We should help that mouse get out of the drain,” Joe said. Jack shook his head and replied, “We’ll never be able to do that.” But Joe didn’t give up. He whistled to the mouse and put his hand out. The mouse slowly scurried towards Joe’s hand and he was able to grab it. Jack was so surprised when Joe held up the mouse that he jumped back. “Wow!” shouted Jack, “You did it!” Joe smiled and put the mouse down in the park, where it scurried away, free and happy again. Joe and Jack laughed and smiled, happy that their friendship had saved the day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She was only 3 years old and was very scared. One day, she went outside alone and heard something behind her. She turned around and saw a big, scary bear! She wanted to run away, but she was too scared to even move. The bear started walking in her direction and the little girl started crying. She gathered her courage and asked the bear, "Why are you coming to me?" The bear replied, "I'm just a hungry bear and I'm looking for food. So, I'm coming a bit close to you to see if you have anything for me." The little girl shook her head and the bear shrugged his shoulders. Suddenly, the bear lunged towards her and the little girl quickly closed her eyes. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a great big desk in his room. Tim loved to play and do his work at his desk. One day, he found a small ruler on the floor. Tim had an idea. He wanted to measure something with the ruler. Tim looked around his room for something to measure. He saw his great big desk and thought it would be fun to measure it. Tim walked to his desk and started to measure. He measured the top, the sides, and the legs of the desk. He was having so much fun. The next day, Tim's mom told him that she needed to buy a new tablecloth for his desk. Tim knew just what to do. He told his mom the size of the desk because he had measured it the day before. His mom was very happy and proud of Tim. She bought the perfect tablecloth that fit the great big desk just right. Tim learned that measuring can be fun and helpful too.

Sam and Lily were playing dress-up in their room. They had many hats, scarves, and glasses to try on. Sam found a big hat and a long coat. He put them on and said, "Look, Lily, I am a detective. I have to solve a mystery." Lily looked at him and giggled. She said, "You need a mustache, Sam. A mustache is important for a detective. It makes you look smart and serious." She ran to the bathroom and came back with a roll of toilet paper. She tore off a piece and stuck it under Sam's nose. "There, now you have a mustache," she said. Sam looked in the mirror and smiled. He said, "Thank you, Lily. You are a good helper. Now we have to prepare for our mission. We have to find out who ate the last cookie from the jar. Do you have any clues?" Lily thought for a moment. She said, "I saw Mom go to the kitchen before. Maybe she ate the cookie. Or maybe it was Dad. He likes cookies too. Or maybe it was Spot, our dog. He is always hungry." Sam nodded. He said, "Those are good suspects, Lily. Let's go and ask them some questions. But we have to be careful and quiet. We don't want them to know we are detectives. We have to pretend we are just playing." They put on their hats and coats and glasses and mustaches. They held hands and walked out of their room. They were ready to solve the mystery. They were the best detective team ever.
