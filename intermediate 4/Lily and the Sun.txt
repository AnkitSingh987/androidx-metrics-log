Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Sun

Once upon a time, in a hot sunny town, there lived a little girl named Lily. One day, Lily went to the park to play. She wore her favorite sunglasses to keep the sun out of her eyes. The sunglasses were pink and sparkly, just like Lily liked them. Lily played on the swings and slide, but she saw a little boy sitting alone on a bench. He had a big frown on his face. Lily thought he looked very sad. She wanted to help him feel better. Lily walked up to the little boy and asked, "Why are you sad?" The little boy said, "I lost my sunglasses and now the sun is too bright." Lily knew just what to do. She took off her sunglasses and gave them to the boy. The boy's frown turned into a big smile. Now they both could play in the hot sun and have fun together.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a cherry tree. The cherry tree had many red cherries. Some cherries were sweet, and some were sour. A little girl named Lily loved to eat cherries from the tree. One day, Lily was playing with her ball near the cherry tree. She saw a tired bird on the ground. The bird said, "I need to rest. I flew very far and I am hungry." Lily wanted to help the bird. She picked a cherry from the tree and gave it to the bird. The bird ate the cherry and said, "Thank you, but this cherry is sour. Can you find a sweet cherry for me?" Lily looked at the tree and picked another cherry. She gave it to the bird. The bird ate the cherry and smiled. "This cherry is sweet! Thank you, Lily!" The bird felt better and flew away. Lily felt happy that she could help the bird. From that day on, Lily and the bird became good friends.

Once upon a time, a little mouse named Mimi lived in a tiny house. Mimi loved cheese more than anything else. One day, she found out that there was a big, delicate cheese in the kitchen. Mimi was very happy and wanted to find it. Mimi started to search for the cheese. She looked under the table, behind the door, and inside the cupboards. Mimi was very careful not to make any noise because she was scared of the big cat that lived in the house too. Finally, Mimi found the delicate cheese on the table. She was so happy and wanted to take it back to her tiny house. But suddenly, the big cat jumped on the table and took the cheese away. Mimi was very sad and went back to her tiny house without any cheese. The big cat ate the delicate cheese and Mimi learned to be more careful next time.

One day, a happy boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim had a rubber ball that he loved to play with. The ball was red and blue and very bouncy. Tim was so excited to show his ball to his friend, Sam. At the park, Tim found Sam and said, "Look, Sam! I have a new ball!" Sam looked at the ball and smiled. "Wow, Tim! That's a great ball!" They started to play with the ball, throwing it back and forth. As they were playing, a big dog came running up to them. The dog saw the ball in the air and jumped to catch it. The dog caught the ball in its mouth, but as it landed, the ball popped! Tim and Sam were sad that the ball was gone, but then the dog's owner came over with a new rubber ball. "I'm sorry my dog popped your ball. Here, you can have this one." Tim and Sam were happy again, and they all played together with the new ball.

One day, a boy named Tim went to his friend, Sue's house. Sue had a big room with many toys. Tim saw a clock on Sue's wall. He wanted to examine the clock. He asked Sue, "Can I look at your clock?" Sue said, "Yes, you can look at my clock. It helps me be organized. I know when to play, eat, and sleep." Tim and Sue looked at the clock. It had numbers and hands that moved. They watched the clock for a long time. They did not see Sue's cat coming into the room. The cat jumped up and knocked the clock off the wall. The clock broke into pieces. Tim and Sue were sad. They learned that it is important to take care of things. They promised to be more careful with their things in the future. They also learned that being organized is good, but they should always watch out for unexpected things like a cat knocking down a clock.
