Category: Intermediate
Title: The Busy Ant and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a busy little ant. The ant lived in a big old tree. The ant liked to reach high up in the tree to find food. One day, the ant saw a big red fruit. The ant wanted to reach the fruit. The ant tried to climb up the tree. It was very hard. The ant was tired. But the ant did not give up. The ant wanted to reach the fruit so much. The ant climbed and climbed. At last, the ant reached the fruit. But when the ant touched the fruit, something unexpected happened. The big red fruit was not a fruit at all! It was a big red ball. The ant was surprised. The ant and its friends had fun playing with the big red ball all day. The ant learned that sometimes, surprises can be fun.

Once upon a time, there was a gorilla in the jungle. The gorilla was very curious and liked to explore. One day he wanted to find out what it felt like to touch something dry. The gorilla looked around and spotted a tree with dry bark on it. He slowly reached out his finger and touched it. It was really dry! The gorilla asked the tree, "How does it feel to be dry?" The tree replied, "It feels nice. It's much better than being wet!" The gorilla smiled and said, "Thank you for teaching me about dry things!" Then he went off to explore some more. The End.

Once upon a time, there was a painter named Bob. Bob was a great painter. He loved to paint all day long. He painted houses, trees, and animals. One day, Bob was painting a big tree when a little bird came by. The bird said, "Hi, painter! What are you doing?" Bob said, "I am painting a tree." The bird liked the tree and wanted to help. So, the bird picked up a brush with its beak and started to paint too. While they were painting, a big cat came by. The cat wanted to scare the bird. The cat jumped out and said, "Boo!" The bird was scared and flew away. Bob was sad, but he had an idea. He painted a big dog on the tree to scare the cat away. The cat saw the dog and ran away. The bird came back and they finished painting the tree together.

Once upon a time, there was a yellow dog named Buddy. Buddy had a very important job. He would deliver things to people in his town. Buddy loved his job because he could make people happy. One day, Buddy had to deliver a rag to a little girl named Lily. Lily needed the rag to clean her toys. Buddy picked up the rag with his mouth and started walking to Lily's house. The rag was soft and smelled nice. When Buddy got to Lily's house, Lily was so happy to see him. She took the rag from Buddy and gave him a big hug. Buddy wagged his tail and felt very proud. After that, Buddy went back to his house to rest. He knew that tomorrow he would deliver more things and make more people happy.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play with her toys. One day, she found a big triangle toy in her toy box. She wanted to reach it but it was too high. Lily asked her friend, Tim the cat, to help her. Tim was very smart and kind. He jumped high and reached the triangle toy for Lily. They were so happy to play with the triangle together. But then, something unexpected happened. The triangle toy started to talk! It said, "Thank you for playing with me. I was lonely in the toy box." Lily and Tim were surprised. They learned that it is important to share and play with all their toys, even the ones they don't play with often. And from that day on, Lily, Tim, and the triangle had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a small dog named Tiny. Tiny had a wish to play with a big red ball. The ball was on the other side of a gate. Tiny wanted to go through the gate, but he had to resist because it was not safe. One day, a kind girl saw Tiny looking at the ball. She wanted to help him. So, she picked up the ball and brought it to Tiny. Tiny was so happy that his wish came true. Tiny and the girl played with the ball all day long. They had so much fun together. Tiny learned that even a small dog can have big wishes come true. And he knew that sometimes, it is good to resist things that are not safe.
