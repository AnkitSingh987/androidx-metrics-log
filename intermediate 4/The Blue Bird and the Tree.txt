Category: Intermediate
Title: The Blue Bird and the Tree

Once there was a blue bird that lived in a tree. The bird had a fragile nest because it was very weak. The bird wanted a bigger and stronger nest, so every day it went off to find things to make it safer. One day, the bird found some twigs and feathers. It flew back to its nest and started to build. After a few hours, the nest was all done. It was much bigger and stronger than before. The bird was happy and could now fit in the nest. It stayed in the nest every day and felt nice and safe. It had made the nest better with its work. At night, the bird would look out over the tree. It was happy to sit in its safe nest and have a better home.

Once upon a time, there was a nice girl named Amy. She had a toy that could record sounds. Amy loved her toy and played with it all the time. One day, Amy found a pretty rock. She thought it had a lot of value. She wanted to tell her friends about the rock. So, she used her toy to record her voice, saying "I found a pretty rock!" Amy played the recording for her friends. They all thought it was nice. They wanted to find pretty rocks too. So, they all went outside and looked for rocks. In the end, they found many pretty rocks. They were all happy and had a lot of fun. Amy's toy helped them share the value of the pretty rocks. And they all had a nice time.

One day, a little cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big tree and decided to take a nap under it. The sun was warm and the grass was soft, so Tom fell asleep very fast. While Tom was napping, a friendly cow named Daisy came by. Daisy saw Tom and thought, "He must be thirsty after his nap!" So, she gave him a big cup of milk. The milk was cold and yummy. When Tom woke up, he saw the milk and was happy. But he did not know who gave it to him. It was an unknown friend. Tom drank the milk, and it made him feel good. Then, Tom and Daisy played together all day long.

"Mommy, what is that man doing with the dog?" asked Tommy, his blue eyes wide with curiosity. "He is a veterinarian and he is giving the dog a check-up," Mommy explained. "Why?" Tommy asked. "Because the dog is scared and she needs some help," Mommy said, smiling. Mommy and Tommy watched as the veterinarian opened the dog's mouth and listened to her heartbeat. Then he gave her a shot and signed a piece of paper. "What did he do?" asked Tommy. "He is giving her medicine to help her feel better," Mommy explained. After the veterinarian left, Mommy cuddled the dog and said, "There, now you'll be feeling much better soon." The dog looked relieved and happier already, and Tommy felt much better too.

One day, a girl named Lucy went to the park. It was a mild day, not too hot and not too cold. She had a big bag with her. In the bag, there were toys and snacks. Lucy was very happy to play with her toys and share her snacks with her friends. At the park, Lucy met a boy named Tom. Tom was sad because he lost his toy car. Lucy wanted to help Tom find his toy car. They looked high and low, but they could not find it. Tom asked Lucy, "Can I play with your toys?" Lucy answered, "Yes, you can." While they were playing, a squirrel came and took Lucy's bag. Lucy and Tom were sad and worried. They had to get the bag back from the squirrel. Tom had an idea. He said, "Let's give the squirrel some nuts, and maybe he will give us the bag back." They found some nuts and gave them to the squirrel. The squirrel was happy and gave the bag back to Lucy and Tom. In the end, Lucy and Tom were happy because they got the bag back. They played with the toys and shared the snacks. They became good friends and had a fun day at the park.
