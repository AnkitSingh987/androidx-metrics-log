Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Switch

One day, a boy named Tim found a switch on a wall. It was a distant wall that he had never seen before. He wanted to test the switch to see what it would do. Tim called his friend, Sue, to come and look at the switch with him. "Look, Sue! I found a switch. What do you think it does?" Tim asked. Sue looked at the switch and said, "I don't know, Tim. Maybe we should test it and see." Tim and Sue were very curious, so they decided to test the switch. When Tim pushed the switch, something unexpected happened. The sky turned dark and scary. The wind began to blow very hard. Tim and Sue were scared and ran back to their homes. They didn't know that the switch made the weather bad. Now, it was a very bad day, and they were sad that they tested the switch.

One day, a little girl named Amy found a big box of crayons. She was very happy. She wanted to draw amazing pictures with the crayons. She took out her paper and started to write her name with a blue crayon. Amy's little brother, Tom, saw her writing and wanted to join her. "Can I write, too?" he asked. Amy said, "Yes, you can use the red crayon." Tom was excited and started to write his name with the red crayon. As they were writing, something unexpected happened. The crayons started to talk! The blue crayon said, "Wow, you write very well, Amy!" The red crayon said, "And you too, Tom!" Amy and Tom were very surprised. They had so much fun writing with their new talking crayon friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys. One day, his mom gave him a fake phone to play with. Tim was very happy and took the fake phone everywhere. One day, Tim saw a pretty wrap in his mom's room. He thought it would be fun to wrap his fake phone in it. So, he took the wrap and wrapped his fake phone like a gift. Tim was very proud of his work and showed it to his mom. Later, Tim wanted to play with his fake phone again. But, he could not find it. He looked and looked, but it was gone. Tim was very sad. His mom told him that she had thrown away the wrapped gift because she thought it was trash. Tim cried and learned to be more careful with his things.

Once there was a boy. He wanted a perfect brick for his house. But he didn't have a perfect brick. He asked everyone around, but no one had a perfect brick. So he went out to look for one. He searched in the garden and the fields, but he couldn't find anything. He almost gave up. But then he saw something in the grass. He walked over to it. It was a perfect brick! The boy was so excited. He jumped up and ran to bring the perfect brick home. But when he came back, he saw something very bad. The perfect brick was gone. Someone had taken it away! The boy was so sad. He looked everywhere, but he never found the perfect brick again. The bad thing stole it away. And that was the end of the perfect brick.

Once, there was a wealthy family who had a son. He was very happy and loved playing with his marbles. He always used them to make games and build towers. One day, the son decided to give away some of his marbles to other children who did not have any. He asked his dad if he could use some of his marbles to give to the children. His dad encouraged the son to use the marble to share with the other children. With a smile, the son shared the wealth with the children. They were all so happy to get the marbles, praising the son for being so generous. The son was so pleased with himself, knowing he had made the other children just as happy as himself. He knew that through sharing, he could bring joy and happiness to others.
