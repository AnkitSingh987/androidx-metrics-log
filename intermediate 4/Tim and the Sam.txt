Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Sam

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a suit that he loved to wear. It was his favorite thing to put on. One day, Tim put on his suit and went outside to play. While Tim was playing, he met a new friend named Sam. Sam did not know what a suit was. He looked at Tim's suit and asked, "What is that?" Tim just shrugged, because he didn't know how to explain it to Sam. Sam was ignorant about suits, but that was okay. Tim and Sam played together all day. They had so much fun, and Tim's suit got dirty from playing. Tim didn't care, because he had made a new friend. And from that day on, Tim and Sam were the best of friends.

One day, a tall bird named Tim wanted to soar high in the sky. He lived near a harbor with many boats. Tim had a friend named Sam. Sam was a small bird who liked to play with Tim. One day, Tim said to Sam, "Let's soar high and see the boats in the harbor." Sam was scared, but he wanted to try. So, they started to fly up, up, up in the sky. They saw the boats and the water below them. As they flew, something unexpected happened. They saw a big, big boat with a funny hat. The boat said, "Hello, birds! I'm a boat that can talk!" Tim and Sam were very surprised. They made a new friend that day and had lots of fun with the talking boat.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Zoe. Zoe was very curious and liked to explore the world around her. One day, she decided to go on an adventure and explore the park. As she walked to the park, she stopped to pick up a pencil that she found on the ground. She looked down at the pencil and wondered what she could do with it. When Zoe reached the park, she spotted two boys playing with a frisbee. One of them was very competitive and wanted to see who could throw the frisbee the furthest. Zoe stopped and watched. The boy with the frisbee called out to Zoe, "Hey little girl! Come join us, and you can show us how far you can throw the frisbee!" Zoe had never played with a frisbee before, but she was excited to try something new. Zoe took the frisbee from the boy with a huge smile and began to throw it around with the other boys. She was surprised to find out how competitive she was. She threw the frisbee farther than the other boys and couldn't help but wonder how she did it. At the end of the day, Zoe went home with a big smile on her face, holding onto the pencil that she had found earlier. She had a wonderful day and couldn't wait to go back to the park.

One day, a jolly boy named Tim went to play at the beach. He took his red spade with him. Tim liked to dig in the sand and make big holes. He saw his friend, Sue, playing with her ball. "Hi Sue! Want to play catch with me?" Tim asked. Sue smiled and said, "Yes, let's play catch!" They threw the ball back and forth, laughing and having fun. Suddenly, the ball went into the big hole that Tim dug with his spade. When they looked into the hole, they saw a little crab holding the ball! They were surprised and excited. Tim and Sue became friends with the crab and played catch together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Lucy. She wanted one thing more than anything else - to have her very own pet bird. One day, when she was playing in the garden, a bird flew down and landed at her feet. It was grey, with beautiful blue wings, and Lucy recognised it straight away - it was a special ash bird. Lucy was so happy, she ran inside and asked her mum if she could keep the bird. Her mum said yes, and the ash bird became her very own pet. That night, Lucy said goodnight to the bird and made it a delicious dinner - some cooked rice, dried fruit and a seed she found in the garden. The bird ate the dinner and then went to sleep in its cage. Every morning after that, Lucy would give the bird some food and talk to it. Lucy was so happy to have a pet bird of her own. She loved it very much and was so grateful that it had chosen to stay with her. The ash bird was her best friend!
