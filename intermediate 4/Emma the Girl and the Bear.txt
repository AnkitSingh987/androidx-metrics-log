Category: Intermediate
Title: Emma the Girl and the Bear

Once upon a time, there was a small girl named Emma. She was only three years old. One day, Emma was testing things out. She took a scissors and she cut her teddy bear. Her teddy bear was very special to her, and when Emma saw what she had done, she became very angry. Her mom saw what had happened and came to talk to her. She was angry too. She asked Emma why she cut the teddy bear and Emma said she was only testing it out. Her mom told her never to do that again, and to never test things out like that again. Emma learned her lesson, and started respecting things more. From then on, when she wanted to test something out, she would ask her mom first. That way, she could make sure things were taken care of and no one would get angry.

Once upon a time, in a big sea, there was a little jellyfish. The jellyfish was very excited because it wanted to grow big and strong. It swam around in the water, playing with its friends all day. One day, the jellyfish met a big fish. The big fish told the jellyfish that to grow, it needed to eat lots of good food. The jellyfish listened carefully and started to eat more food every day. As the days passed, the excited jellyfish began to grow bigger and bigger. It was so happy to see that it was growing just like the big fish said. The little jellyfish now had many friends and they all played together in the big sea. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little dog named Spot. Spot liked to play with his ball. One day, Spot saw a big red coat hanging on a door. The coat looked fun to play with. Spot wanted to find out who the coat belonged to. Spot went to his friend, Cat. He asked Cat, "Do you know whose big red coat this is?" Cat said, "No, I don't know. Let's go ask Bird." So, Spot and Cat went to ask Bird. Bird was playing with a toy that could shoot. Spot asked, "Bird, do you know whose big red coat this is?" Bird looked at the coat and said, "Yes, it's mine! I wear it when I fly high in the sky to keep warm." Spot and Cat were surprised that the coat belonged to Bird. They all laughed and played together with the ball and the toy that could shoot.

Once upon a time, there was a big box that could print. It had many wires. One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to print a picture of a cat. He asked his mom for help. They put a paper in the box and pressed a button. The box started to print the cat. But, something funny happened. The cat in the picture started to move! It jumped out of the paper. Tim and his mom were very surprised. They looked at the cat, and it looked real. But, it was a fake cat! It was made of paper. The fake cat played with Tim and his mom. They had fun with their new friend. Then, the cat went back to the paper. Tim and his mom looked at each other and smiled. They knew they had a magic box that could print special pictures. They were excited to print more fun friends to play with.

One day, a girl named Lily found some clay. She wanted to make a pretty picture with it. She thought, "I will print my hand in the clay!" So, she pushed her hand into the clay and made a print. Lily showed her print to her friend, Tom. Tom said, "Wow, that's cool! But I want to make something spicy!" Tom found some red pepper and mixed it with his clay. He made a spicy clay ball. Tom and Lily went to show their mom. Mom looked at the hand print and spicy clay ball. She said, "Be careful with the spicy clay, it can hurt your eyes!" Just then, the spicy clay ball rolled and hit Lily's foot. "Ouch!" Lily cried. Tom felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, Lily." The two friends learned that it's important to be careful with things that can hurt others. They played with the clay safely and had fun making more prints and shapes together.
