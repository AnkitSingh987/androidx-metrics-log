Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Dad

Lily liked to help her mom in the kitchen. She wanted to be a helpful person. She learned how to set the table, wash the dishes, and serve the food. One day, her mom made a big cake for her dad's birthday. Lily helped her mom decorate the cake with frosting and sprinkles. She was very proud of the cake. She wanted to serve it to her dad and make him happy. When her dad came home, Lily ran to him and gave him a hug. She said, "Dad, we have a surprise for you! Come to the kitchen!" Her dad followed her to the kitchen, where her mom was waiting with the cake. Lily helped her mom carry the cake to the table. She said, "Happy birthday, Dad! We made this cake for you!" Her dad smiled and thanked them. He said, "Wow, this cake looks amazing! You are such a helpful person, Lily. Thank you for serving me this cake. You made my day!" Lily felt happy and proud. She helped her mom cut the cake and serve it to her dad. They all enjoyed the cake and sang happy birthday. Lily loved her mom and dad, and they loved her.

Once upon a time, in a hidden forest, there lived a big bear and a small rabbit. They were happy friends who liked to play together. The bear was strong and the rabbit was fast. One day, the rabbit said, "Bear, do you believe we can find a big, yummy cake in the forest?" The bear laughed and said, "I don't know, but we can try to look for it." So, they walked and looked for the cake. They found a big tree with sweet fruit. They found a pond with cool water. But they did not find a cake. "Maybe there is no cake," said the rabbit, feeling sad. The bear smiled and said, "That's okay. We still had fun looking for it." And so, the bear and the rabbit went back to their homes, happy to have spent the day together in the hidden forest.

Once upon a time, there was a modern house. In the house lived a boy named Tom and his mom. Tom loved to play with bubbles. One day, Tom asked his mom, "Can I put bubbles in the sink?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Tom was happy and ran to the sink. He put lots of bubbles in the sink and played with them. Tom played and played, but he put too many bubbles. The bubbles grew big and filled the whole house. Tom's mom tried to stop the bubbles, but it was too late. The bubbles took over the house, and they had to leave. They were sad, but they learned to be more careful with bubbles.

One day, a girl named Lily wanted to paint a picture. She was very patient and took her time. She painted a big sun, some trees, and a house. Lily was happy with her picture and showed it to her mom. As Lily was showing her mom the picture, her little brother Tom came in. He wanted to paint too. But Tom was not patient like Lily. He took the paint and started to paint very fast. He painted over Lily's picture! Lily was sad, but then she saw that Tom's painting was different. It was not a sun, trees, or a house. It was a funny face! Lily laughed and thought it was a fun twist. She and Tom painted more funny faces together.

One sunny day, Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a cute butterfly on a flower. The butterfly was yellow and black. Tim wanted to catch it and show his friends. "Can I catch the butterfly, Mom?" Tim asked. His mom said, "Yes, but be gentle and let it go after." Tim caught the butterfly in his hands. He felt happy and showed it to his mom. She smiled and said, "Now, it's time to release the butterfly." Tim opened his hands and let the butterfly go. It flew away, back to the flowers. Tim and his mom watched it fly away. They had a fun day at the park.
