Category: Intermediate
Title: The Great Dog of Tim and the Toys

One day, a boy named Tim found a sack in his room. He had a question. "What is in the sack?" he asked his mom. His mom did not know. Tim was persistent. He wanted to find out. Tim went to his friend, Sue. "Do you know what is in the sack?" he asked. Sue shook her head. "No, I do not know," she said. Tim was sad but still persistent. He wanted to find out. Tim went to the park. He saw a dog. The dog barked at the sack. Tim opened the sack. Inside, there were many toys! Tim was happy. He shared the toys with Sue and the dog. They all played together. The sack was a surprise for all of them.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a very impressive tree. This tree was big and had many leaves. It was a special tree because it could change its leaves with each season. In the hot season, the tree had green leaves. The green leaves made the tree fit in with the other trees. The kids liked to play under the tree because it was cool and shady. They would laugh and have fun all day long. In the cold season, the tree had no leaves. The tree still looked impressive, even without leaves. The kids would play in the snow near the tree. They would build snowmen and throw snowballs at each other. The tree was a big part of the village, and everyone loved it, no matter the season.

One day, a jolly cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw his friend, a dog named Max. Max was holding a thermometer in his mouth. Tom was very curious and asked, "What is that, Max?" Max replied, "This is a thermometer. It tells us if it is hot or cold outside." They both looked at the thermometer and saw that it was very hot. So, they decided to go to the pond to play in the water and cool down. At the pond, they saw a big frog. The frog looked at the thermometer and said, "This is not a thermometer! This is a stick!" Tom and Max were very surprised. They looked at the stick and laughed. They had been fooled! But they still had fun playing in the water with their new friend, the big frog.

Once there was a large farm. On the farm lived a family. The family had a TV. Each night they would watch TV together. One night, the mother asked her son to match the socks that were in the basket. She asked him because he was the oldest. The son thought it would be more fun to watch TV than to match socks. But the mother said that matching the socks was important and he must do it. Reluctantly the son matched all the socks. The next morning the father went to work in the fields and saw a huge pile of dirt. He used some of the matched socks to clean things up. Later that day, the son saw what his father had done and asked why he used the socks. The father smiled and said, "When we match things, they become useful." The boy understood and was proud that he had helped in such a large way!

One day, a boy named Tim was very hungry. He went to the refrigerator to find some food. He opened it and saw a big, yummy cake. Tim was so happy! But he was also a bit careless. He took the cake out of the refrigerator too fast and it fell on the floor. Tim let out a big sigh. He was sad because he could not eat the cake now. Just then, his dog, Spot, came into the room. Spot saw the cake on the floor and started to eat it. Tim was not happy. He wanted to eat the cake, but now Spot was eating it all. Tim was mad at Spot, but he knew it was his fault for being careless. Then, something unexpected happened. Tim's mom came into the room and saw the mess. She told Tim not to worry. She had another cake in the refrigerator! Tim was so happy! He and Spot could both have cake now. Tim learned to be more careful, and he shared his cake with Spot. They both enjoyed the yummy cake together.
