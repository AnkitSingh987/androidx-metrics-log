Category: Intermediate
Title: The Clever Monkey and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a clever monkey. He used to store all of his favourite things inside the tree in his jungle. One day, he wanted a special reward for all of his efforts. He went and asked his friends for help. But none of them were sure what to do. Suddenly, his friend spotted a special flower next to their tree. The monkey was clever and he knew just what to do. He took the flower and stored it inside his tree. Afterwards, he asked his friends for a reward. They all gave him some delicious fruit. The monkey was very happy and thanked them for the sweet reward. The end!

Once upon a time, there was a tiny bird. He had soft brown feathers and chirped all day long. One day, the bird flew too far and found himself very far from home. He was a little bit worried, but he knew he had to keep going. He spread his wings and started to fly. Suddenly, he heard a strange sound. He looked around, but he couldn't see anything. Then he heard it again. It was a whistle. The bird flew closer to see what was making the noise. He saw a little girl walking by herself. She was whistling. She looked a bit weak, so the bird sang a happy song. The girl smiled and said, "Hello, bird! Would you like to come on my walk with me?" The bird was so happy that he happily flew with her. They wandered together until the sun went down and the bird finally flew home. He was never so happy and content.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dependable dog, Spot, went to the park. They liked to play catch with a big red ball. Tim would throw the ball, and Spot would run very fast to get it. They were having so much fun. Then, Tim saw a big jar of jelly on the ground. He thought it was funny. "Look, Spot! Jelly!" said Tim. Spot wagged his tail and barked. Tim picked up the jar and opened it. The jelly inside was red and wiggly. Just then, a big wind came and blew the jelly out of the jar. The jelly scattered all over the park. It was on the trees, the grass, and even on Spot's nose. Tim and Spot laughed and laughed. They never thought their day at the park would be so silly and fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big hoop that she loved to play with. One day, she saw a grumpy cat sitting under a tree. The cat looked very sad. Lily wanted to make the cat happy. Lily rolled her hoop to the cat and said, "Hi, Mr. Cat! Do you want to play with my hoop? It's fun and will excite you!" The grumpy cat looked at the hoop and then at Lily. He was not sure, but he wanted to try. The cat jumped through the hoop, and a big smile appeared on his face. He was not grumpy anymore. Lily and the cat played with the hoop all day long. They became best friends and were never sad again.

Once upon a time, there was a witch who lived in a small house. She had a big hat and a long broom. The witch felt uncomfortable in her small house. She wanted a bigger house to live in. One day, a little girl named Lily walked by the witch's house. She saw the witch and asked her a question. "Why do you look so sad?" Lily asked. The witch told Lily about her uncomfortable house. Lily wanted to help the witch. She had an idea. She told the witch to use her magic to make the house bigger. The witch thought it was a great idea. She waved her broom and her house grew bigger. Now, the witch did not feel uncomfortable anymore. She was happy in her big house. The witch thanked Lily for helping her. They became good friends and lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a noisy bird who lived in the west. The bird loved to sing and play all day long. One day, the bird met a quiet mouse who lived near his tree. "Can I play with you?" asked the bird. The mouse was a bit scared of the noisy bird but said, "Okay, but you must not be too loud or my mom won't allow it." The bird tried to be quiet, but he was so happy to have a new friend that he could not help but sing and play loudly. The mouse's mom heard the noise and came out of her hole. "You are too noisy!" she said to the bird. "You can't play with my child anymore." The bird was very sad and flew away. The mouse was also sad because he lost his new friend. The noisy bird never found another friend to play with, and the quiet mouse stayed alone in his hole.
