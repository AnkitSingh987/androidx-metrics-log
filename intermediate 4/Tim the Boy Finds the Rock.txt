Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with his mom and dad. Tim was very bored. He wanted to find something fun to do. One day, Tim went to play outside. He saw a big rock on the ground. Inside the rock, he saw a pretty gem. Tim wanted the gem, so he tried to break the rock. He hit the rock with a stick, but it did not break. Tim felt sad, but he did not give up. He asked his dad for help. Together, they found a way to break the rock. Tim got the gem and felt happy. He learned that even when things are boring, you can still find fun things to do.

Once upon a time, there was a mess. Everything was a big, jumble. The mess was in the grass, with all sorts of things in it. There was ash from a campfire and branches from the trees. But then one day, everything changed. All the mess was gone! Instead, the grass was growing and getting greener. The ground was full of colour. It was spring! The birds were singing, the sun was shining and flowers were blooming. Everything was looking so bright and beautiful. But then, the wind starting blowing and the ash started flying back into the grass. It was a big, messy mess, but it was so much fun to play in! The children ran and laughed and had a blast. Spring was back and everyone was happy.

One sunny day, a little girl named Amy was in her room. She saw a silly bird outside her window. The bird could whistle a fun song. Amy wanted to be friends with the bird. Amy went outside with a cup. She filled the cup with water and put it near the tree. She said, "Bird, come drink water from my cup." The bird flew down and drank the water. After drinking, the bird whistled a "thank you" song. Amy laughed and clapped her hands. They became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a hungry dog named Spot. Spot loved to eat and play all day. One day, Spot saw a big red truck. It was an ambulance. Spot liked the ambulance because it had bright lights and made loud sounds. Spot had a friend named Daisy. Daisy was a pretty cat. Spot and Daisy liked to play together. One day, Spot and Daisy decided they wanted to marry. They were very happy and excited. On the day they were going to marry, Spot and Daisy had a big party. All their friends came to play. They had lots of yummy food and fun games. The ambulance came to the party too, with its bright lights and loud sounds. Spot, Daisy, and their friends had a great time, and they lived happily ever after.

Tom and Mia were friends who liked to play together. One day, they went to the park with their moms. They saw a big pond with many boats. Some boats were small and some boats were big. One boat was very big and shiny. It was a yacht. "Wow, look at that yacht!" Tom said. "It is so pretty and fancy. I wish I could go on it." "Me too," Mia said. "Maybe we can ask the people who work on it. They might let us see inside." They ran to the edge of the pond and waved at the yacht. A man and a woman came out of the yacht. They smiled and waved back. "Hello, kids," the man said. "Do you like our yacht?" "Yes, we do," Tom and Mia said. "Can we come on it, please?" The man and the woman looked at each other and nodded. They threw a rope to the shore and helped Tom and Mia climb on the yacht. "Welcome to our yacht," the woman said. "We are having a party today. Would you like to join us?" "Yes, please," Tom and Mia said. They were very happy and excited. The man and the woman showed them around the yacht. They saw a big living room, a kitchen, a bedroom, and a bathroom. They also saw a deck with a pool and a slide. Everything was clean and shiny. "Wow, this is amazing," Tom said. "You must work very hard to have this yacht." "We do work hard, but we also like to have fun," the man said. "That's why we invited our friends and family to celebrate with us." They took Tom and Mia to the dining room, where there was a big table with many plates and cups. There were also many kinds of food and drinks. Some food was hot and some food was cold. Some food was sweet and some food was salty. Everything looked yummy. "Wow, this is delicious," Mia said. She tried a piece of cake, a sandwich, and a juice. "You must cook very well to make this food." "We do cook well, but we also like to share," the woman said. "That's why we made enough food for everyone to enjoy." They ate and drank and talked and laughed. Tom and Mia made new friends and had a lot of fun. They thanked the man and the woman for their kindness and generosity. "Thank you for letting us come on your yacht," Tom and Mia said. "We had a wonderful time." "You're very welcome, kids," the man and the woman said. "We're glad you liked it. Maybe we'll see you again someday." They hugged and said goodbye. They climbed down the rope and went back to their moms. They told them everything that happened on the yacht. Their moms were surprised and happy for them. "That was a very special adventure," Tom's mom said. "Yes, it was," Mia's mom said. "You were very lucky and polite." Tom and Mia smiled and nodded. They agreed that it was a very special adventure. They looked at the yacht one more time and waved. The yacht waved back and sailed away. Tom and Mia never forgot that day.
