Category: Intermediate
Title: The Lonely Tree and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a lonely tree in the woods. This tree was sad because it had no friends. One day, a little bird came to the tree. The bird said, "Hi tree, can I be your friend?" The tree was happy and said, "Yes, little bird, you can be my friend." The bird and the tree became best friends. They played and talked every day. One day, a big, scary cat came to the woods. The cat wanted to catch the little bird. The bird was scared and did not know what to do. The tree told the bird, "Quick, escape and hide in my branches!" The bird flew up and hid in the tree. The cat tried to climb the tree, but it was too big and could not reach the bird. Just when the cat was about to give up, a big wind came and blew the cat far away. The cat was gone! The bird and the tree were safe. They were very happy and thanked the wind for helping them. From that day on, the tree, the bird, and the wind were all best friends. They played and talked together, and the tree was never lonely again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a red wagon that he loved very much. He would play with his wagon every day. Tim was a clever boy who liked to think of new games to play with his wagon. One day, Tim wanted to play a new game with his wagon. He decided to go down a big hill near his home. Tim got in his wagon and started to go down the hill very fast. As he went down the hill, he began to yell, "Wheee!" But as Tim went faster and faster, he saw a big tree at the bottom of the hill. He tried to stop his wagon, but it was too late. Tim's wagon hit the tree very hard. The wagon broke into many pieces. Tim was very sad because his favorite wagon was gone.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park with her mom. They sat on a bench to watch the ducks swim in the pond. Lucy had a bar of chocolate in her hand, and she was very happy. A harmless squirrel came up to Lucy and said, "Hello, can I have some chocolate?" Lucy was surprised that the squirrel could talk, but she shared her chocolate with the squirrel. They became friends and played together in the park. Suddenly, the squirrel turned into a fairy! The fairy said, "Thank you for sharing your chocolate with me. You are very kind. I will give you a wish." Lucy wished for more chocolate for everyone in the park. The fairy waved her wand, and chocolate appeared everywhere. Everyone was happy and enjoyed the sweet treat.

Once upon a time, there was a polite dog named Spot. Spot lived in a small house with his friend, a little girl named Sue. They liked to play together all day long. One day, Spot and Sue found a big jail in the woods. The jail had a door that was open, so they went inside to look. They saw a sad bird in a cage. The bird said, "Please help me quit this jail. I want to be free and fly." Spot and Sue wanted to help the bird. They opened the cage and the bird flew out. The bird was so happy to be free. It thanked Spot and Sue for their help. From that day on, they all played together and were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very curious boy who always asked questions. One day, he decided to question the town's law. The law said that no one was allowed to play near the big tree at the edge of the town. Tim felt hurt because he loved the big tree. He didn't understand why the law was there. So, he went to the tree to play, even though the law said no. While playing near the tree, Tim found a big hole in the ground. He was so excited that he jumped in. But the hole was very deep, and Tim could not get out. He was stuck there, and nobody could help him. This is why the law was there, to keep kids safe. But Tim didn't listen, and now he was in trouble.
