Category: Intermediate
Title: Anne the Girl and the Stick

Once upon a time, there was a young girl named Anne. She was quiet and liked to explore. One day, Anne went out for a walk and noticed some graffiti on the road. She wondered how it got there. Anne saw a stick of chalk lying on the ground. She picked it up and asked her mom, "Can I draw with this?" Her mom smiled and said "Yes, go ahead!" Anne took the chalk and followed the graffiti on the ground. Everywhere she went, she drew patterns and designs in the street with her chalk. People passing by smiled and said how wonderful it looked. At first Anne was shy, but soon she was not quiet anymore. She chattered away, laughing at what she did and what she saw. Finally, she reached the end of the street where she followed the graffiti. When she got there, she was amazed to find a beautiful mural that she had created! It was the most wonderful thing Anne had ever seen. She smiled proudly and show everyone the artwork that she had created. Everyone admired the mural and thanked Anne for bringing some brightness to the street. Anne followed the artwork with joy. She had created something beautiful and shared it with the world.

Once upon a time, there was a sun that loved to shine. It shined all day long. One day, a big cloud came and blocked the sun. The sun was sad because it could not shine. A little boy named Tim saw the sun was sad. He had a rag in his hand. He said to the sun, "I will help you shine again." Tim climbed up a big hill very fast to reach the cloud. He tried to use the rag to push the cloud away. But the cloud was too big and strong. The cloud said, "I will not move. I like to block the sun." Tim tried and tried, but he could not make the cloud move. The sun stayed sad and could not shine.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Lily. She loved to play the piano. Every day, she would sit and make music. Her mom and dad liked to listen to her play. One day, Lily met a boy named Tim. Tim told Lily, "I used to play the piano too, but I quit. It was too hard for me." Lily said, "Don't quit! I can help you learn. It's fun to play together." So, Lily and Tim sat at the piano. They played and played. They made pretty music together. Tim was happy he did not quit. And they both had lots of fun playing the piano.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big, unusual hat that she wore every day. One day, she went to the park to play with her friends. Lily saw her friends playing a game. She wanted to join them. So, she walked up to them and said, "Can I join your game?" Her friends were happy to see her and said, "Yes, of course! We need more people for our game." While they were playing, a bird flew down and dropped a message on the ground. Lily picked it up and read it out loud, "Come to the big tree for a surprise!" All the friends were excited and ran to the big tree. At the big tree, they found a box. Inside the box were many colorful balloons. They all took a balloon and played with them, laughing and having fun. The unusual hat and the message made this day very special for Lily and her friends.

One day, a big dog named Max found a ball with a spike in it. Max wanted to play with the ball, but he couldn't because of the spike. He asked his friend, a small cat named Lily, for help. "Lily, can you remove the spike from the ball?" Max said. Lily tried to help Max, but she could not remove the spike. They both felt sad because they wanted to play with the ball. They thought about what to do. Max had an idea. He said, "Let's ask our friend, the bird, if he can help us." The bird was very smart and knew how to remove the spike. He used his beak and took the spike out of the ball. Max and Lily were so happy! They thanked the bird and started to play with the ball. But then, something unexpected happened. The ball started to bounce very high, higher than any ball they had ever seen. It was a magic ball! Max, Lily, and the bird played with the magic ball all day. They had so much fun, and they were glad they had the help of their friends to make the ball available to play with.
