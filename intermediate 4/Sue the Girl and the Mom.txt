Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Mom

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Sue liked to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, Sue's mom was cooking on the stove. Sue wanted to help, but her mom said, "No, Sue. The stove is hot and it can hurt you. It's bad for little kids." Sue felt sad, but she listened to her mom. She went to play with her toys in her room. While she was playing, she heard her mom call, "Sue, come here!" Sue ran to the kitchen and saw something amazing. The stove had vanished! It was gone! Sue's mom said, "Sue, you were a good girl and listened to me. So, the stove vanished to keep you safe. Now, we can cook together with a new, safe stove." Sue was so happy. From that day on, Sue and her mom cooked together with their new stove, and they had lots of fun.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a huge bowl of spaghetti in the kitchen. He was very hungry and wanted to eat it all. But the bowl was too big for him to carry. So, he asked his mom for help. "Mom, can you help me with this big bowl of spaghetti?" he said. Tim's mom sighed and said, "Okay, Tim. But you must share the spaghetti with your sister, Mia." Tim didn't want to share, but he knew he couldn't carry the bowl by himself. So, he agreed to share with Mia. Tim and Mia sat down at the table with the huge bowl of spaghetti between them. They started to eat, but they ate too fast. Soon, their tummies began to hurt. Tim and Mia looked at each other with sad faces. They knew they should have eaten slower and shared the spaghetti better. Now, they had tummy aches and couldn't play outside.

Once upon a time, there was a big cardboard box. It lived in a room with lots of toys. The toys were spoiled because they never shared with each other. They always fought and never played together. One day, the cardboard box had an idea. It wanted to unite all the toys and make them friends. The cardboard box told the toys, "Let's play together and have fun. We can all fit inside me and be a big happy family." The toys liked the idea. They all jumped into the cardboard box and started playing together. They learned to share and be nice to each other. The cardboard box had united the toys, and they were no longer spoiled. They all lived happily ever after, playing together in the big cardboard box.

One day, a pink bunny named Lily went for a walk. She loved to hop and play in the sun. As she hopped, she saw her friend, a little bird named Max. "Hi Max!" said Lily. "Do you want to hop with me?" Max said, "I can't hop, but I can fly! Let's play together." As they played, they found a small house with a leak. The water was dripping on the floor. Lily and Max wanted to help fix the leak. Lily hopped up high and Max flew up to the leak. They worked together to fix it. The house was dry and happy again. Lily and Max were proud of their work and continued to play all day long.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily was an independent girl who loved to play outside. She had a lot of friends, and they would all play together in the sunshine. One day, Lily and her friends found a small flame in the woods. They were scared and didn't know what to do. Lily promised her friends that she would take care of the flame and make sure it didn't hurt anyone. Lily ran to her mommy and daddy and told them about the flame. They were proud of her for being so brave and independent. They helped her put out the flame safely, and everyone in the village was happy. Lily kept her promise, and her friends knew they could always count on her.
