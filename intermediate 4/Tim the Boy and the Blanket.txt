Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Blanket

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big, soft blanket in his room. He loved the blanket because it was cozy and warm. Tim decided to share his blanket with his friends, so they could all unite under it. Tim called his friends, Sue and Max, to come over. "Come over and bring your blankets too!" he said. Sue and Max came to Tim's house, each with their own blankets. They were all excited to play together. When they all sat down under their blankets, they started to tell serious stories. Tim told a story about a big bear, Sue told a story about a scary storm, and Max told a story about a dark forest. They all listened and enjoyed the stories. Then, they all united their blankets to make one giant blanket. They laughed and played under the big blanket, feeling happy and safe. They promised to always share their blankets and unite for more fun adventures.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to drink milk every day. One day, Lily felt miserable because her milk was all gone. Lily asked her mom, "Where can I find more milk?" Her mom did not answer. Lily was sad and went outside to look for milk. She looked and looked, but she could not find any. Suddenly, a little cat came up to Lily. The cat said, "I know where to find milk!" Lily was surprised because cats can't talk. She followed the cat to a big cow. The cow gave Lily some milk, and she was happy again. The cat was a magic cat, and they became best friends.

Tom was a little boy who liked to whistle. He whistled when he played, when he ate, and when he went to bed. He whistled so much that his mom and dad sometimes asked him to be quiet. One day, Tom went to the park with his mom. He saw a big slide and ran to it. He whistled as he climbed up the ladder and slid down. He had so much fun that he did it again and again. But then, he saw a man in a suit sitting on a bench. The man had a book and a phone. He looked angry and bored. Tom thought he needed some fun. So he whistled very loud as he slid past him. The man looked up and frowned. He said, "Hey, kid, stop that noise! I'm a lawyer and I'm working on a very important case. You're disturbing me!" Tom was surprised and scared. He didn't know what a lawyer was, or what a case was. He only knew that the man was mean and rude. He said, "I'm sorry, mister, I didn't mean to bother you. I just like to whistle. It makes me happy." The man snorted and said, "Well, I don't like it. It makes me unhappy. Go away and find another slide. And don't whistle again, or I'll sue you!" Tom didn't know what sue meant, either. But he felt sad and hurt. He ran to his mom and told her what happened. His mom hugged him and said, "Don't worry, Tom. The man was wrong to yell at you. You didn't do anything bad. You can whistle as much as you want. It's a beautiful sound. And you know what? I'm a lawyer, too. And I think you have a great case for whistling!"

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a little bird named Bobby. Bobby loved to sing. He would sing all day long. But Bobby was a little impatient. He wanted to learn new songs right away. One sunny day, Bobby met an old wise bird. The wise bird said he would teach Bobby a new song. But Bobby must wait until the next day to receive the song. Bobby was excited but also a little impatient. The next day, Bobby woke up very early. He flew to the wise bird as fast as he could. The wise bird saw how eager Bobby was and smiled. Then, the wise bird taught Bobby the most beautiful song. Bobby learned that sometimes it's good to be patient. And from that day on, whenever Bobby sang his new song, all the animals in the forest would stop and listen.
