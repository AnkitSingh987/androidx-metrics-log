Category: Intermediate
Title: Maggie and Kelly and the Story

Once upon a time, there were two best friends, Maggie and Kelly. They were always having lots of fun together. One day, they decided to pretend to be mommies and daddies. Maggie said, “Let’s pretend to cook some food! What shall we make?” Kelly thought for a moment and then said, “Let’s make blue cakes!” So they set to work making the cakes. Maggie pretended to mix the ingredients while Kelly pretended to put them in the oven. They had lots of fun! But then Kelly said, “Let’s pretend to eat the cakes!” But Maggie said, “No, we can’t! We don’t have any real food.” Kelly was very upset and argued, “But we made them!” Maggie calmly replied, “Yes, but imaginary food isn’t real food. We can’t eat it.” So, instead of pretending to eat the imaginary cakes, Maggie and Kelly decided to make real food. Maggie said, “Let’s make some fruit salad!” So they did, and it tasted delicious! Moral of the story: You can't pretend to eat food, but you can make real food to eat instead.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a grumpy cat named Tom. Tom did not like to play with the other animals. He liked to sit by the window and watch the birds. One day, Tom saw a big pan in the kitchen. He thought, "I can change this pan into a fun toy!" So, he pushed the pan to the living room. The other animals saw Tom playing with the pan and they laughed. Tom became more grumpy. Later, a little bird flew into the house. The bird was scared and did not know how to go back outside. Tom saw the bird and said, "Don't be scared. I will help you." He used the pan to guide the bird to the open door. The bird said, "Thank you, Tom!" and flew away. The other animals saw how Tom helped the bird. They said, "Tom, you are not so grumpy after all!" Tom smiled and played with the other animals. From that day on, Tom was not grumpy anymore, and he had many friends. The pan had changed Tom's life.

One day, a girl named Lucy went to a ranch. She saw a big, bossy cow. The cow liked to tell the other animals what to do. Lucy liked to look at the cow and the other animals. Lucy saw the bossy cow tell a little pig to go away. The little pig was sad. Lucy wanted to help the little pig. She thought of a plan to make the bossy cow nice. Lucy found a big hat and put it on the little pig. The bossy cow saw the little pig with the big hat and did not know it was the little pig. The cow was nice to the little pig with the big hat. The little pig was happy, and the bossy cow learned to be nice to all the animals.

One day, a smart cat and a big cow were in a barn. The barn was full of hay. The cat and the cow liked to play in the hay. The smart cat said, "Do you remember the game we played yesterday?" The big cow said, "Yes, I remember. We jumped in the hay. It was fun!" Then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew into the barn. The bird said, "I want to play too!" The smart cat and the big cow were surprised. They had never played with a bird before. The cat, the cow, and the bird played in the hay together. They had a lot of fun. They all became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear named Bob. Bob was very patient. He liked to bathe in the river every day. One day, while Bob was bathing, he saw a shiny spear in the water. He picked it up and said, "Wow, a spear!" Bob wanted to show his friend, a small bird named Sue. Sue lived in a tree near the river. Bob went to the tree and said, "Sue, look at my spear!" Sue was very excited to see the shiny spear. They played with the spear all day long. Later, when they were tired, they heard a loud noise. A big fish jumped out of the water! The fish said, "That is my spear! I lost it when I was swimming." Bob and Sue were very surprised. They gave the spear back to the fish. The fish was happy and thanked them. From that day on, they all became good friends and had many fun adventures together.
