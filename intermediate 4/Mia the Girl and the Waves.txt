Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Waves

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to play at the beach. One sunny day, she went to the beach with her mom. The sand was warm under her feet. Mia liked to touch the sand and make shapes with it. Mia saw the blue waves in the water. She wanted to touch the waves too. She asked her mom, "Can I touch the waves?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Mia ran to the water and touched the waves. They were cold but fun. Mia played with the waves all day. She made a new friend, a little boy named Sam. They laughed and splashed the cold waves at each other. When the sun went down, Mia and Sam said goodbye. They promised to play at the beach again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a wise old cat named Tom. Tom lived in a small house with a big yard. Tom loved to play with his toys, especially his toy car with four wheels. One day, while playing outside, Tom found a new wheel. He thought, "I can add this wheel to my toy car!" So, he tried to put the new wheel on his car. But it was hard for him to do it by himself. Tom asked his friend, a little bird named Sue, to help him. Sue was very smart and knew just what to do. Together, they added the new wheel to the toy car. Now, Tom's toy car had five wheels, and he was very happy. Tom and Sue played with the car all day long, and they had lots of fun.

Once upon a time there was a girl name Sarah. She was three-years-old. One day, Sarah saw a big shiny jet in the sky. It was so high up that she had to stand on her tiptoes to see it. She wanted to get closer to the jet. One day Sarah asked her mum if she could ride on a jet. Her mum said, 'No, you can't'. Sarah looked very worried. But then a nice man came and said he could take Sarah on a jet ride. He said it would be a very special trip. Sarah was very excited. She followed the nice man to a big jet. It was even shinier than the one she saw in the sky. The man put on Sarah a special seat belt. He showed her the buttons to press and the things she could do. The jet started to fly up and away. Sarah was a bit scared at first, but she had lots of fun on the special jet ride. When they landed, Sarah was very happy and no longer worried.

Once there was a lazy puppy named Bobo. He liked to sleep all day and play all night. Bobo loved to chew on his toys and his favorite toy was a small red ball. One day, Bobo was playing with his ball outside. He was chewing and chewing on it. But he was so lazy, he did not want to run and play with the other puppies. They were having fun and wanted Bobo to join them. Bobo did not listen to his friends. He just kept chewing on his ball. Then, the ball broke into pieces. Bobo was sad because his favorite toy was gone. He looked at the other puppies playing and wished he had played with them instead.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the zoo with his mom. They saw many animals, like big elephants and tall giraffes. Tim was very happy and excited. As they walked, they entered a new part of the zoo. In this part, there were many chubby animals. They saw a chubby bear and a chubby monkey. The chubby monkey was eating a banana and playing with other monkeys. Tim and his mom spent the whole day at the zoo. They saw many more animals and had lots of fun. When it was time to go home, Tim waved goodbye to the chubby monkey and all the other animals. He couldn't wait to come back to the zoo again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a box of pastel colors. She loved to draw with them. One day, she found a broken pastel in her box. She was sad. Lily went to her mom and said, "Mom, my pastel is broken. Can you fix it?" Her mom said, "I can't fix it, but I can help you return it to the store. Maybe they can give you a new one." So, Lily and her mom went to the store. The store lady looked at the broken pastel and said, "I have a surprise for you!" She gave Lily a big box with many new pastels. Lily was so happy! But when Lily opened the box, she found a magic pastel inside. When she drew with it, her pictures came to life! Lily and her mom were so surprised. They had lots of fun with the magic pastel, and they lived happily ever after.
