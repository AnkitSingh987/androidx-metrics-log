Category: Intermediate
Title: Billy the Weak Goat and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a weak goat named Billy. Billy lived on a farm with other animals. He was not strong like the other goats, but he was very friendly. One day, Billy met a wise old bird. The bird said, "Billy, if you want to increase your strength, you must eat more good food and play with your friends." Billy listened carefully to the bird and decided to follow its advice. Every day, Billy ate more and played with his friends. Slowly, he became stronger and stronger. The other goats noticed and said, "Wow, Billy, you are not weak anymore! You are strong like us!" Billy was happy and thanked the bird for helping him. From that day on, Billy and his friends played together and had lots of fun.

Lily liked to go to the big place where people ate food. She liked to see the different things on the plates and smell the yummy smells. She liked to sit with her mom and dad and order what she wanted. She always ordered shrimp, because they were small and pink and tasty. One day, Lily went to the big place with her mom and dad, and they met another family there. They had a boy named Ben, who was the same age as Lily. Ben did not like shrimp. He said they were gross and slimy and smelled bad. He ordered pizza, because he said it was the best food ever. Lily felt sad. She thought Ben was mean and did not want to be his friend. She wanted to eat her shrimp and enjoy them. But when the food came, Ben looked at her plate and laughed. He said, "Ew, you're eating shrimp? That's disgusting! Why don't you eat something normal, like pizza?" Lily felt angry. She said, "Shrimp are not disgusting! They are delicious! And they are important, too! They help clean the water and feed other animals. Pizza is just bread and cheese and sauce. It's boring!" Ben said, "No, pizza is awesome! It has different toppings and flavors and you can eat it with your hands. Shrimp are boring! They all look the same and you have to peel them and dip them in sauce. And they don't help anything. They just get eaten by bigger fish." Lily and Ben started to argue. They raised their voices and made faces at each other. Their moms and dads tried to calm them down, but they did not listen. They kept saying mean things about each other's food and each other. Then, something happened. A big man who worked at the big place came to their table. He had a big tray with more food on it. He was carrying it to another table, but he tripped and fell. The tray flew in the air and the food landed on Lily and Ben. They got covered in pizza and shrimp and sauce and cheese. They looked at each other and screamed. The big man said, "I'm so sorry! Are you okay? I didn't mean to do that!" Lily and Ben said, "We're okay, but we're messy!" The big man said, "Don't worry, I'll get you some napkins and some water. And I'll give you some new food, too. Whatever you want." Lily and Ben looked at their plates. They saw that their food was gone. They felt hungry. They also felt silly. They realized that they had been fighting over something that did not matter. They liked different foods, but that did not mean they had to be rude or mean to each other. They could try new things and respect each other's choices. They said, "Thank you, big man. We're sorry, too. We were being silly. We don't care what we eat. We just want to eat something." The big man smiled and said, "That's good to hear. How about I bring you some pizza and some shrimp, and you can share them? Maybe you'll find something you both like." Lily and Ben nodded and said, "Okay, that sounds good." They looked at each other and smiled. They said, "We're sorry, too. We don't want to fight. We want to be friends." They hugged and wiped the food off their faces. They waited for the new food and decided to try a bite of each other's. They found out that pizza and shrimp were both good, in different ways. They learned that it was better to be kind and open-minded than to be rude and stubborn. They had fun and enjoyed their food. They thanked the big man and their moms and dads. They left the big place happy and full. They had learned an important lesson.
