Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Cheerful Girl and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful little girl named Amy. Amy had a big, soft cushion that she loved to play with. She would jump on it, sit on it, and even sleep on it. One day, Amy saw a little dog with a bow on its neck. The dog was very cute and friendly. "Hello, little dog!" said Amy. "Do you want to play with me and my cushion?" The little dog wagged its tail and barked, "Yes, I want to play!" Amy and the little dog played all day. They played hide and seek, catch, and tag. The little dog would jump on the cushion, and Amy would laugh. They had so much fun together. As the sun began to set, the little dog's owner came looking for him. "There you are, Bow!" she said. "I've been looking for you all day." Amy was sad to see the little dog go, but she knew he had to go home. "Bye, Bow! I hope we can play again soon!" Amy said, waving goodbye. Bow barked happily and wagged his tail as he walked away. Amy knew she had made a new friend, and she couldn't wait for their next playdate.

One day, a little cat named Kitty was walking in the park. She saw a big dog named Max. Max liked to tease Kitty. He would bark and run around her. Kitty was fearful of Max. Kitty said, "Max, please don't tease me. I don't like it." Max laughed and said, "Okay, Kitty. I will stop." Kitty was happy that Max listened to her. The next day, Kitty went back to the park. She saw a big box with a bow on it. She opened the box and found a surprise inside. It was a new toy! Max came over and said, "I got you this toy to say sorry for teasing you." Kitty and Max played with the toy and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there lived a 3 year old girl called Lucy. She was very adventurous and liked to explore new places. One day, her parents took her to the stadium. When she arrived, she was surprised to see a lot of people and things that she had never seen before. Lucy was excited to explore the stadium and she jumped, ran and skipped around. But then suddenly she started to feel something tickling her nose. She was surprised and wanted to know what it was. Just then, her mum said: "That is called sneezing, and it means you will have good luck". From that day onward, whenever Lucy was about to go on a new adventure, she would first take a deep breath and then give a good, loud sneeze. She started to believe that this was bringing her good luck. The moral of the story is: sometimes, even the simplest things can bring us luck!

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Tom. Tom was a nervous cat. He liked to rest in the house. One day, Tom found a big box in the kitchen. It was a fridge. The fridge was cold and nice. Tom wanted to rest next to the fridge. As Tom was resting, he heard a noise from inside the fridge. He got up and opened the fridge door. To his surprise, there was a little bird inside! The bird was cold and scared. Tom wanted to help the bird. Tom took the bird out of the fridge and put it in a warm place. The bird started to feel better. Then, the bird did something unexpected. It started to talk! The bird thanked Tom for saving its life. It turned out that the bird was a magic bird. As a thank you gift, the bird granted Tom three wishes. Tom was so happy that he wasn't nervous anymore. They became best friends and had many fun adventures together.

One day, a girl named Sue went to her friend Tom's house. They wanted to play and have fun. Sue saw a cup of tea on the table. She thought it would be fun to have a tea party. So, she said, "Tom, let's have a tea party!" Tom agreed and they began to set up their tea party. As they were having their tea party, Sue took a sip of the tea. It tasted harsh. She made a face and said, "This tea is not good. It tastes harsh." Tom tasted the tea too and he agreed. They decided to add some sugar to make the tea taste better. Just as they were about to add the sugar, Tom's mom came into the room. She looked at the tea and said, "Oh no, that's not tea! That's my special cleaning water!" Sue and Tom were surprised. They thanked Tom's mom for telling them before they drank more of it. Tom's mom made them some real tea, and they continued their tea party, happy that nothing bad happened.
