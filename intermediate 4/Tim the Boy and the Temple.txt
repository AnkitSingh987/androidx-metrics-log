Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Temple

One day, a little boy named Tim went to a big temple with his mom and dad. The temple was very big and had many colors. Tim loved to look at the pretty temple. Tim saw a yummy ice cream shop near the temple. He asked his mom and dad if they could get some ice cream. They said yes, and all three of them got yummy ice cream cones. Tim's ice cream was his favorite color, blue! After eating their ice cream, they saw a little toy car that could zoom very fast. Tim's dad bought the toy car for him. Tim played with the toy car and made it zoom all around the temple. He had so much fun with his mom, dad, and new toy car at the big, pretty temple.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat. The fat cat had a pocket. In the pocket, the cat kept a toy mouse. The cat loved to play with the toy mouse all day. One day, the cat met a little dog. The dog was sad. The cat asked, "Why are you sad?" The dog said, "I have no toy to play with." The cat thought and thought. Then, the cat said, "I recommend my toy mouse!" The cat took the toy mouse from the pocket and gave it to the dog. The dog was so happy. They played with the toy mouse together and became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a charming little bakery. The bakery made many tasty treats. One day, they made a new pastry. It was round, soft, and sweet. A little boy named Timmy came to the bakery with his mom. Timmy saw the new pastry and wanted to try it. His mom bought one pastry for him. Timmy took a bite and loved it. He wanted to eat the pastry again and again. Timmy asked his mom to buy more pastries. His mom said, "We can come back tomorrow." The next day, they came back to the bakery. Timmy was happy to repeat eating the pastry. Every day, they went to the bakery to get the charming pastry. Timmy and his mom loved their sweet treat time together.

Once upon a time, there was a kind rabbit named Bob. Bob lived in a big field with many friends. One day, he saw a new animal in the field. It was a small bird. Bob wondered who the bird was. "Hi, I am Bob. Who are you?" asked the kind rabbit. The small bird said, "I am Tina. I am new here. Can you help me find food?" Bob was very happy to help his new friend. Bob and Tina looked for food in the field. Then, something unexpected happened. They found a big, shiny rock. They wondered what it was. Tina touched the rock, and it turned into a big pile of yummy food! Bob and Tina were so surprised! They shared the food with all their friends in the field. Now, they were all very happy and full.

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog named Max. Max lived in a small house with his friend, a girl named Lily. They loved to play together and have fun. One sunny day, Max and Lily saw that the window was dirty. Lily said, "Max, let's clean the window!" Max wagged his tail and barked, "Yes, let's do it!" So, they got a bucket of water and a soft cloth. Lily dipped the cloth in the water and started to clean the window. Max helped by holding the cloth with his mouth. As they cleaned, the sun shone through the window, making everything bright and pretty. Max and Lily were very happy. They danced and played in the sunshine. After they finished cleaning, they looked at the shiny window and smiled. They knew they did a good job together.

Once upon a time, there was a rich cat named Tom. Tom had a long tail. He lived in a big house. Tom had many toys and food. He was very happy. One day, Tom saw a poor dog named Max. Max had a short tail. Max did not have many toys or food. Tom felt bad for Max. They started to discuss their tails. Tom liked his long tail, but Max was sad about his short tail. Tom thought about it and decided to share his toys and food with Max. Max was very happy. They played together and became good friends. The moral of the story is that it is good to share and be kind to others, no matter if they have a long tail or a short tail.
