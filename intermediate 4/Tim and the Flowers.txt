Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a guard who protected his family's garden. Every day, he tried to keep the dirty bugs away from the pretty flowers. One day, Tim saw a big, dirty bug trying to eat the flowers. He said, "Stop! You can't eat our flowers!" The bug looked at Tim and said, "I'm hungry. I need to eat too." Tim thought for a moment and said, "I will help you find food if you promise not to eat our flowers." Tim and the bug worked together to find food for the bug. They looked under rocks and leaves until they found some yummy food. The bug was happy and said, "Thank you, Tim. I will not eat your flowers anymore." Tim had succeeded in protecting the garden and helping the bug. The moral of the story is that sometimes, we can solve problems by helping and understanding others.

One day, Tim and his mom went to the park. Tim saw a big symbol on the ground. It was a big red circle with a line through it. Tim felt confused. He asked his mom, "What is that?" His mom said, "That means we can't do something here. But I don't know what it is." They kept playing in the park, but Tim still thought about the symbol. Then, a dog came running into the park. It had a ball in its mouth. The dog dropped the ball by the symbol. Tim and his mom looked at each other and laughed. "Now we know!" said Tim's mom. "The symbol means no dogs can play here. But this dog doesn't know that!" They played with the dog and had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was an attractive cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to perform tricks for her friends. Every day, she would jump, dance, and sing for all to see. One day, Kitty's friend, Bunny, came to watch her perform. Bunny looked at the calendar and said, "Today is a special day for you to perform, Kitty!" Kitty was happy and started to dance and sing. All her friends clapped and laughed. They all had a great time on that special day.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Timmy. Timmy was very frightened because he did not know how to fly. He saw his friends flying in the sky and wanted to join them. One day, Timmy saw an orange on the ground. He walked up to the orange and gave it a little peck. It was yummy! Timmy ate and ate until he was full. He felt strong and happy. With his new strength, Timmy decided to try flying again. He flapped his wings and, to his surprise, he started to fly! Timmy was so happy that he could fly with his friends. From that day on, Timmy was never frightened to fly and always loved to eat oranges.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a humble boy named Tim. Tim had a toy pistol that he loved to play with. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom and brought his toy pistol. At the park, Tim saw a little bird who looked hungry. He said to his mom, "Mom, can we feed the bird?" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. We can feed the bird some bread." So they fed the bird and it was very happy. While playing, Tim saw another boy who wanted to play with his toy pistol. Tim thought for a moment and then shared his toy pistol with the boy. They both played happily together. The other boy said, "Thank you, Tim, for sharing your toy pistol with me." Tim learned that sharing made him and others happy. The moral of the story is that sharing is caring.

One day, a cat went to visit his friend, the dog. They wanted to play together. The cat said, "Let's play, but we can't disturb the baby bird in the tree." The dog agreed and they played near the tree. They ran around and had fun. But then, the dog felt dizzy. He spun around and around. The cat laughed, but the dog couldn't stop spinning. Suddenly, the dog bumped into the tree. The baby bird woke up and started to cry. The cat and the dog felt bad. The mommy bird came and was angry. She chased the cat and the dog away. They were sad because they didn't mean to disturb the baby bird.
