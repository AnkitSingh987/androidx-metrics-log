Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Graceful Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a graceful cat named Lily. She loved to dance and play in the sun. One day, Lily's mom asked her to remind her to make soup for dinner. Lily said, "Yes, mom!" and went outside to play. While Lily was playing, she saw a big bird in the sky. The bird was flying so high and fast. Lily was so excited that she forgot to remind her mom about the soup. She watched the bird until it was time for dinner. When Lily went inside, her mom had made something different for dinner. It was not soup! Lily was surprised and said, "Mom, I forgot to remind you about the soup!" Her mom smiled and said, "That's okay, Lily. I saw the bird too, and I decided to make something special instead." They ate the yummy dinner together and talked about the big bird they saw.

One day, a boy named Tim went outside to play. It was a weird day because it was very cold. Tim saw his friends playing with a ball. They were having fun, but Tim felt cold and wanted to go inside. Tim asked his friends to go inside, but they wanted to keep playing. Tim felt sad and decided to make a plan. He thought if he could freeze the ball, his friends would want to go inside too. Tim found a bucket of cold water and put the ball in it. The ball started to freeze. When his friends saw the frozen ball, they agreed it was too cold to play outside. They all went inside and played games together. Tim was happy that his plan worked, and they all had a fun day inside.

Once upon a time there was a nice fox. He was very hungry, but he did not know what to eat. He decided to look and see if he could find some food. Soon, he found some meat. He wanted to eat it, but he was scared that something else would find it too. So, he decided to hide it. He went to a bush and put the meat underneath it. The fox went to sleep. The next morning when he woke up, he went over to the bush to get the meat. But it was gone! Someone had taken it. The fox was so sad. But he was happy when he looked around and saw a woman. She was holding the meat and she said, "I found this meat, would you like it?". The fox said, "Yes please!", and the woman gave him the meat. He was so happy. The woman was nice and gave the fox some meat. The fox thanked her and ran away. He was so happy that he had something nice to eat.

One day, a little boat wanted to sail. The boat saw a tunnel. The tunnel was big and dark. The boat thought the tunnel was amazing. It wanted to see what was inside. The boat went into the tunnel. Inside, it met a fish. The fish said, "Hello, boat! Do you like this tunnel?" The boat said, "Yes, it is amazing! I love to sail here." The fish and the boat sailed together. They had fun. They played in the tunnel. They were happy. The boat and the fish became good friends. They sailed in the amazing tunnel every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He lived in a big building with his mom and dad. Tim liked to play with his toy cars and run around with his friends. One hot day, Tim went outside to play. He saw a big red ball near the building. He picked it up and threw it high in the sky. But the ball got stuck in a tree! Tim wanted his ball back, so he started to scream for help. Tim's mom heard him scream and came outside. She saw the ball stuck in the tree and helped Tim get it down. Tim was so happy to have his ball back. He hugged his mom and they went inside the building to cool off from the hot sun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to wear her pretty dress and play outside. One day, she saw a big puddle of mud. Lily wanted to jump in the mud, but she knew her pretty dress would get dirty. A gentle cat named Bella walked by and saw Lily. Bella said, "Lily, if you jump in the mud, your pretty dress will get dirty. But if you wear old clothes, you can still have fun and not worry about the mess." Lily listened to Bella and went home to change into old clothes. After changing, Lily went back outside and jumped in the mud. She had a lot of fun and her old clothes got dirty, but her pretty dress stayed clean. The moral of the story is to think before you act and to always make smart choices.
