Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Dog

One day, a little girl named Lucy wanted to watch a movie. She asked her dependable friend, Tom, "What movie do you recommend?" Tom thought for a moment and said, "I recommend the movie with the big, happy dog." Lucy and Tom started to watch the movie. But soon, they had a problem. Lucy's little brother, Sam, wanted to watch a different movie. He said, "I want to watch the movie with the funny clown!" Lucy and Tom were not happy. They wanted to watch their movie, but Sam was very loud. So, they made a plan. They said, "We will watch the funny clown movie first, and then we will watch the big, happy dog movie." Sam agreed, and they all watched the movies together. In the end, everyone was happy and they all had a fun time.

Once upon a time, there was a clever little boy named Tim. He loved to ride his bike. It was his favorite vehicle. He rode it every day. He had a lot of fun. One day, while Tim was riding his bike, he saw a big box on the road. He stopped and looked at the box. It was a very big box. He thought maybe there was something fun inside. Tim opened the box, and guess what? Inside was a big red tricycle! He was so happy. He tried to ride the tricycle, but it was too big for him. Then, the tricycle started to ride by itself! Tim was surprised. The tricycle was a magic tricycle. It could ride all by itself. Tim and the magic tricycle became the best of friends. They rode together every day and had lots of fun.

Mia and her daddy were very excited. It was Mia's fourth birthday. Daddy asked Mia "What do you want for your birthday?" Mia giggled and said "Ice cream for breakfast!" Daddy laughed and said "Okay, let's have ice cream for breakfast." Mia and her daddy went to the store to buy the ice cream. When they got home, Daddy put the ice cream in a bowl and they both sat down on the living room floor. Daddy said "Ready to accept your birthday ice cream?" Mia was so hungry and she said "Yes!" Daddy put a spoon full of ice cream in front of her. Mia loved it and said "Yummy!" Daddy smiled and said "Happy birthday, Mia. I accept it is a special day. I am so glad you enjoy the ice cream." Mia smiled and said “Thank you, Daddy. It is the best birthday ever!"

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a big, rough tree and wanted to climb it. His mom said, "Be careful, Tim!" Tim started to climb the tree and saw a big drum on a branch. The next day, Tim went back to the park with his friends. He told them about the drum in the tree. They all wanted to see it too. So, they gathered under the tree and looked up at the drum. Tim said, "Let's get the drum down!" They all helped each other climb the tree to reach the drum. When they got the drum down, they all took turns playing it. The drum made a loud sound that everyone could hear. Tim's mom came to see what the noise was. She smiled and said, "I knew you would find something fun in the park!" Tim and his friends played with the drum all day long and had lots of fun.

One day, a little bird named Tim was looking for a new branch to sit on. He found an interesting branch on a big tree. Tim was very happy and flew to the branch to sit on it. But, when Tim tried to sit on the branch, another bird named Sam was already there. Sam did not want to share the branch with Tim. Tim was sad and did not know what to do. Tim had an idea! He decided to find a new branch to replace the interesting one. He looked and looked, and then he found an even better branch! Tim was happy again and Sam was happy too. They both had their own branches to sit on, and they became good friends.
