Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a brave little girl named Mia. She loved to play with her friends, the birds and the rabbits. One day, Mia found a sack made of paper. She picked it up and opened it. Inside the sack, Mia found a tiny kitten. The kitten was very scared. "Don't be scared, little kitten," said Mia. "I will be your friend and help you find your home." Mia and the kitten walked through the forest. They saw many trees and flowers. The birds sang a happy song for them. The rabbits hopped and played with Mia and the kitten. They all had lots of fun. At last, they found the kitten's home. It was a small house made of wood. The kitten's mom and dad were very happy to see their little one. "Thank you, brave Mia," they said. "You are a good friend." Mia smiled and waved goodbye to the kitten and her family. She walked back through the forest, feeling very happy. The birds and rabbits followed her, singing and hopping all the way home.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One day, he saw a hole in the ground. "What is this?" Tim asked. He wanted to see what was underground. Tim called his friend, Sue. "Sue, come see what I found!" Sue came running. They both looked at the hole. "Let's go inside," said Sue. Tim and Sue went underground. It was dark, but they were not scared. As they walked, they felt warm. They saw a big, warm room with a big, warm bed. Tim and Sue laid down on the bed. They were so tired. They fell asleep and had sweet dreams. When they woke up, they went back outside to play. They were happy they found the warm underground room.

One day, a little dog named Max was playing in the park. He saw a big hole in the ground. Max was very curious and wanted to see what was inside the hole. As he got closer, he heard a small voice coming from the hole. Max looked down and saw a tiny mouse. The mouse said, "Please don't be rude and look into my home." Max felt bad and said sorry to the mouse. He decided to remain near the hole to make sure no one else would bother the mouse. Soon, they became good friends. Max would play in the park, and the mouse would watch from the hole. They were happy to have each other, and Max learned that it was important to be kind and not rude.

One day, a cat named Herb went for a walk. He saw a big tree and wanted to jump up and see the top. But Herb was a small cat and could not jump high enough. He felt sad and guilty because he had told his friends he could jump very high. Herb met a frog who could jump very high. The frog said, "I can help you jump high, but you must say sorry to your friends for not telling the truth." Herb agreed and said sorry to his friends. They forgave him and were happy that he told the truth. The frog helped Herb learn how to jump high. One day, when Herb was jumping, he found a magic stick in the tree. The stick could make anyone jump even higher! Herb shared the magic stick with his friends, and they all jumped high together. Herb was happy and never felt guilty again.

Mum was making something in the kitchen. She asked her three year old daughter if she wanted to help. The daughter was very obedient and said yes. Mum gave the daughter some powder and asked her to sprinkle it on the plate. She held the plate away from her body so that the powder would not get on her clothes. She told her daughter the powder should be spread evenly. The obedient daughter took the spoon and sprinkled the powder on the plate just as her mum had said. Mum smiled and said "well done! You were very obedient and told the powder exactly where to go!" The daughter was very proud and smiled at her mum. She was happy that she had done something to help.
