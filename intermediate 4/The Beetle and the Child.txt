Category: Intermediate
Title: The Beetle and the Child

Once upon a time, there was a small beetle who scurried around in the light. The beetle saw a child sitting and looking sad. The beetle was kind and wanted to help. The beetle scuttled up to the child and said "Hello, why are you feeling sad? Maybe I can help." The child looked up and smiled. "I had a bad day," they said. "Everything kept going wrong." The beetle thought for a moment and then said, "Let me comfort you. Maybe a hug will make you feel better." The child laughed and said, "I'm not sure a little beetle can give a hug!" But the beetle hopped closer and wrapped its tiny arms around the child. To their surprise, the child felt much better. The hug from the beetle made it all better. The beetle smiled and said, "See, even I can help make you feel better. You just need to find the light and follow it."

Once there was a little girl named Amy. She was always looking for new adventures. One day, she opened the door to her house and found a brand new hockey stick leaning against the wall. She couldn't believe her luck! Amy took the hockey stick and ran outside. She called her best friend, Adam, and said "Come over, I have something unique for us to play with." Adam arrived quickly and was amazed when he saw the hockey stick. Amy explained that she found it by the door. Together they entered the backyard, and started to play a game of hockey with the unique hockey stick. It was so much fun! Amy and Adam played the game until it was time to go in the house. As they left the hockey match, they both had smiles on their faces. They surely had a unique day, playing hockey with a special hockey stick!

Once upon a time, there was a worried mule named Max. Max had a big job to do. He had to pull a cart full of toys to the town. But Max had a problem. The cart was not attached to him. Max went to his friend, the wise owl. "Owl, how do I attach the cart?" Max asked. Owl thought for a moment and said, "You need a rope, Max. Use the rope to attach the cart to you." Max found a rope and tried to attach the cart. But he was still worried. What if the rope was not strong enough? Max's friend, the strong horse, saw Max and said, "Don't worry, Max. I will help you attach the cart with the rope." With the help of his friends, Max attached the cart and pulled it to the town. The children were so happy to see the toys. Max was not worried anymore, and he knew that with his friends, he could do anything.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little dog named Max. He liked to wonder about the world and ask many questions. One day, Max went for a walk in the fog. He could not see very well, but he liked to explore. As Max walked, he saw a big shadow in the fog. He was scared, but he wanted to know what it was. The shadow came closer, and Max saw it was just a big, friendly dog. The big dog said, "Hi, I am Sam. I was lost in the fog, too." Max and Sam played together and had fun. They learned that even when things look scary, they might be nice if you give them a chance. The moral of the story is: don't judge something by how it looks.

Once there was a poor dog named Max. Max was very sad because he lived in a small, cold bathroom. He wanted to find a new home where he could be happy and warm. One day, a nice lady named Sue came to visit Max. She saw how sad he was and wanted to help him. Sue said, "Max, let's choose a new home for you!" Max wagged his tail and barked, "Yes, please!" Sue took Max to a big, warm house with a soft bed and yummy food. Max was so happy to leave the cold bathroom behind. He cuddled with Sue, and they played all day. Max finally had the loving home he always wanted.
