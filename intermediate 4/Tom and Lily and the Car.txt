Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Lily and the Car

Tom and Lily were playing with their toys in the garden. They liked the weather. It was sunny and warm. They had a ball, a doll, a car and a teddy bear. "Can I play with your car?" Lily asked Tom. "No, it is mine. You have your doll," Tom said. "But I like your car. It is red and shiny," Lily said. She tried to grab the car from Tom, but he held it tight. "Stop it! You are stealing my car!" Tom shouted. He pushed Lily away and she fell on the grass. She started to cry. Mum heard the noise and came outside. She saw Tom and Lily fighting over the car. "What is going on here?" Mum asked. "Lily wanted to steal my car. She is bad," Tom said. "No, Tom wanted to keep the car for himself. He is mean," Lily said. Mum looked at them and shook her head. "Both of you are wrong. You should share your toys and be nice to each other. You have plenty of toys to play with. And you should enjoy the weather. It is a cheerful day," Mum said. She took the car from Tom and gave it to Lily. Then she took the doll from Lily and gave it to Tom. "Now, you can take turns playing with the car and the doll. And you can also play with the ball and the teddy bear together. Can you do that?" Mum asked. Tom and Lily nodded. They felt sorry for making Mum angry and for hurting each other. "OK, Mum. We will share and be nice. We are sorry," they said. They hugged Mum and then hugged each other. They picked up the ball and the teddy bear and went back to the garden. They played happily and forgot about the car and the doll. They liked the weather. It was sunny and warm.

Once there was a little boy named Timmy. He was hungry so he decided to go collect some food. He went to the forest, feeling very brave. Timmy was walking along when he saw a strange texture. He reached out to touch it, but it was really sharp! It was a bramble and he had been very silly. Timmy started to cry but there was still no food. He was getting very hungry and he was starting to feel scared. He decided to keep searching and eventually he found a berry bush. "I'm so hungry, I'll just collect a few berries" said Timmy. He collected the berries, but they were very sour! He ate some of them, but they were so bitter he had to spit them out. Now he was both hungry and unhappy. Timmy started to cry again. What was he going to do? He had to go home with no food at all.

Once upon a time, there was a monkey who loved to sing. He sang day and night, and the other animals loved to listen. One day, the monkey wanted to see if he could make the animals happier, so he sang louder. But they thought it was too loud and became upset. The monkey felt sad and tried to quieten his singing. But the animals were still angry, so they told him to leave. The monkey was sad and didn't know where to go. Suddenly he saw a disgusting pond of slime, and he thought it looked inviting. So he jumped into the pond and started singing. But the animals were still upset and they wanted him to leave. The monkey refused, so the animals decided to do something drastic. They jumped into the pond and scared the monkey away. The monkey was so frightened he ran away and never sung again.

Once upon a time, there was a little shop. The shop was in a calm town. In the shop, there were many toys. One day, a boy named Tim went to the shop with his mom. Tim saw a toy he liked. It was a toy dog. When Tim picked up the toy dog, it started to shake. The toy dog could wag its tail! Tim was very happy. He asked his mom, "Can I have the toy dog, please?" His mom said, "Yes, you can have the toy dog." Tim hugged the toy dog and said, "Thank you, Mom!" They went home with the new toy dog. Tim played with the toy dog all day. He loved to watch it shake its tail. And they all lived happily ever after.
