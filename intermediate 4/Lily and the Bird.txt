Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Bird

Once upon a time, a sweet girl named Lily loved to paint. She lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and little dog, Max. Lily's room was upstairs, and she would paint all day with her colorful paints. One sunny day, Lily was painting a pretty picture of Max in her room upstairs. Max was playing with his ball downstairs. Suddenly, Max heard a strange noise coming from upstairs. He ran up to see what it was. When Max got to Lily's room, he saw a big, funny-looking bird near the window. The bird had knocked over Lily's paints! Max barked, and Lily turned around to see the bird. The bird was scared and flew away. Lily saw the mess the bird made with her paints. But then, she had an idea. Lily started to paint with the spilled colors. She made a new picture with the mess the bird left behind. In the end, Lily's new painting was even more beautiful than the one she started. Lily and Max laughed and played, happy that something unexpected turned out to be a fun surprise. And from that day on, Lily always left a window open when she painted upstairs, just in case another funny bird wanted to help her make art.

One day, a big cat and a little dog went to a fancy party. They both wore pretty hats and nice clothes. The big cat said, "Little dog, this is a fun party!" The little dog said, "Yes, big cat, I like the food and the games." Soon, it was time for the best part of the party. They had to wait in a line to get a big, sweet cake. The big cat and the little dog were very excited. They talked about how good the cake would taste. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the cake away! The big cat and the little dog were sad. But they decided to have fun anyway. They played more games and made new friends. And at the end of the party, they found a new cake to share.

Once upon a time, there was a little train named Timmy. Timmy was an independent train. He liked to go wherever he wanted. He did not like to listen to his mommy and daddy. They told him not to go too far away from home. One day, Timmy went on a very long trip. He went far away from his home. He saw many new things. But soon, it started to rain. The rain was very heavy. It made the tracks wet and slippery. Timmy was scared. Timmy tried to go back home, but the rain had spoiled the tracks. He could not go back. Timmy was very sad. He wished he had listened to his mommy and daddy. Now, he was lost and could not find his way home.

Last week, Tom and his family went to the beach. Tom was so excited as he had never been before. His mom and dad bought him a sailboat and told Tom he could use it on the beach. He was very happy and mentioned this to all of his friends. When they arrived, Tom quickly put his sailboat in the water. He couldn't wait to see what it looked like. The sailboat was yellow, with orange stripes and a big white sail. Tom circled the beach, making it look like a crazy race. Everyone cheered him on and clapped. Tom was so happy and he kept talking about his sailboat all night long. It was an amazing day and Tom was even looking forward to going back the next weekend.

Tom and Mia were playing with their toy cars in the sandbox. They made roads and bridges and tunnels with the sand. Tom had a big red car that could go very fast. Mia had a small yellow car that could make funny noises. "Look at my car, Mia! It can zoom and jump and spin!" Tom said, showing off his red car. "Your car is nice, Tom, but my car can talk and sing and beep!" Mia said, pressing a button on her yellow car. They both liked their cars, but they also wanted to try each other's cars. They were envious of what the other had. "Can I play with your car, Mia? Just for a little bit?" Tom asked. "Can I play with your car, Tom? Just for a little bit?" Mia asked. They agreed to swap their cars and see how they worked. They were happy to share and learn. But then, something bad happened. A big dog came running to the sandbox. It saw the toy cars and thought they were bones. It grabbed Tom's red car in its mouth and ran away. It also knocked over Mia's yellow car and broke it. Tom and Mia were very sad. They lost their cars and they had nothing to play with. They cried and cried. "Bad dog! Give me back my car!" Tom shouted. "Bad dog! You wrecked my car!" Mia shouted. They hugged each other and tried to comfort each other. They wished they had their cars back. A nice lady saw what happened. She felt sorry for Tom and Mia. She had a bag with her. She opened it and took out two new toy cars. One was blue and one was pink. They were shiny and bright and had many features. "Hello, children. I saw what the dog did to your cars. I'm sorry. I have some new cars for you. Do you want them?" she asked. Tom and Mia looked at the lady and then at the cars. They were surprised and happy. They nodded and smiled. "Thank you, lady! You are very kind!" they said. They took the new cars and thanked the lady again. They forgot about their old cars and started to play with their new ones. They were not envious anymore. They were grateful and joyful. They had fun and made new friends.
