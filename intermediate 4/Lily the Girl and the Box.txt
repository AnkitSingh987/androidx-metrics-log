Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big, dirty toy box in her room. Every night, she would put her toys away and go to bed. But sometimes, she would have a bad dream called a nightmare. One night, Lily had a nightmare about a monster. She woke up and was scared. She wanted to make sure the monster was not in her room. So, she decided to peek at her dirty toy box to see if the monster was hiding inside. Lily slowly walked to the toy box and took a deep breath. She opened the lid just a little bit and peeked inside. There was no monster, only her toys. She felt happy and safe. From that night on, Lily knew that her toys would keep her safe from any nightmares.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to dance. Every day, she would go outside and dance in the sun. One day, while dancing, she saw a small bird with a hurt wing. The bird was ashamed because it could not fly. Lily wanted to help the bird. She said, "Don't be sad, little bird. I will cover your wing and make it better." She took a soft leaf and covered the bird's wing. The bird felt better and said, "Thank you, Lily." After some time, the bird's wing got better. The bird was so happy that it started to dance with Lily. They danced together every day, and they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a modest farm. On this farm, there was a cow named Daisy. Daisy liked to give milk to the farmer every day. The farmer was very happy to receive the milk from Daisy. One day, Daisy met a new friend named Bob. Bob was a sheep. Bob said, "Hi Daisy, do you want to play with me?" Daisy said, "Yes, let's play!" They played all day long and had lots of fun together. The next day, when the farmer came to get milk from Daisy, he found something unexpected. Daisy's milk had turned pink! The farmer was very surprised. Daisy and Bob laughed and laughed. The pink milk was a fun surprise for everyone on the modest farm.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to eat pastry. One day, Sue saw a pastry shop. She wanted to buy a pastry, but she had no money. Sue asked her friend Tom, "Can you give me money to buy a pastry?" Tom said, "No, I have no money." Sue was sad. She wanted the pastry so much. She had an idea. She would take the pastry when no one was looking. Sue took the pastry and ran away. But the shop owner saw her. He was very angry. He ran after Sue and caught her. "You cannot take the pastry without paying," he said. Sue felt bad. She knew it was wrong to take the pastry. The shop owner called Sue's mom. Sue's mom was very upset with her. Sue had to give the pastry back. She did not get to eat the pastry, and she was in trouble. Sue learned that it is not good to take things without asking.

One day, a blue bird named Bob flew to a big tree. He saw a tiny screw on the ground. "What is this?" Bob asked. A squirrel named Sam came out from the tree. "I don't know," Sam said. "It arrived here this morning." Bob and Sam looked at the screw. They wanted to find out what it was for. They asked their friends, but no one knew. At last, they met a wise old owl. "This is a screw," the owl said. "People use it to hold things together." Bob and Sam thanked the owl and took the screw home. They put it in their special box of treasures. They were happy to have a new thing to share with their friends.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom and dad. Tim had a red spade to make a big sand castle. He was very happy to play in the sand. Tim started to dig with his spade. He found a big, hairy bug in the sand. The bug was scared, but Tim wanted to help it. He used his spade to make a new home for the bug in the sand. But then, something unexpected happened. The bug started to grow bigger and bigger! Tim was very surprised. The big, hairy bug was not a bug at all! It was a magic sand crab that could grow when it was happy. Tim and the magic sand crab became good friends. They played together in the sand and made the biggest sand castle ever. Tim, his mom and dad, and the magic sand crab had a fun day at the beach.
