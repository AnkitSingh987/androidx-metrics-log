Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Lion

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the zoo with her mom. Sue was very happy. She saw many animals, like a big elephant and a tall giraffe. But her favorite animal was the lion. She stared at the lion for a long time. Sue's mom said, "Sue, we have to go see the other animals too." But Sue did not want to leave the lion. She told her mom, "I want to stay here and talk to the lion. I think he is lonely." Sue's mom smiled and said, "Okay, we can stay a little longer." As they stared at the lion, Sue saw something in the lion's cage. It was a small toy. The lion picked up the toy with his mouth and started to play with it. Sue and her mom laughed. The lion looked happy. Sue said, "See, mom? The lion is happy now because we stayed with him." Sue's mom hugged her and said, "You were right, Sue. The lion needed a friend, and you were his friend today."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. Sally was very hungry and asked her mom for a snack. "Mommy, I'm hungry," Sally said. "Why don't you have some mineral water?" her mom replied. Sally didn't understand what a mineral was. She asked her mom what it was. "A mineral is a special type of rock," her mom explained. Sally thought that a mineral was most likely not a snack but she asked anyway. "Will it make me full?" she asked. Her mom smiled and said, "No, sweetheart. It won't make you full but it will quench your thirst." Sally was still hungry, so her mom made her a sandwich. After eating the sandwich, Sally was full and content. The end.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a healthy little monkey. The monkey loved to play and swing on the vines all day long. The vines were very strong and could hold the monkey as he played. One day, the monkey found a new vine that he had never seen before. It was very pretty and had many colors. The monkey thought it would be fun to display the vine for all his friends to see. So, he took the vine and put it up high in the trees. All the animals in the forest came to see the pretty vine. They all liked it very much and wanted to play with it too. The healthy little monkey was very happy that he could share the vine with his friends. They all played and had fun together, and the forest was a happy place.

One day, a little dog named Spot was playing in the park. It was a hot day, and Spot was very thirsty. He saw a harmless looking puddle and ran to drink from it. "Spot, no!" yelled his friend, Tim the cat. "Do not drink from that puddle!" But Spot did not listen. He drank the water and then started to suffer. His tummy hurt, and he felt very sick. Tim knew this would happen because he saw a big, dirty truck drive through the puddle earlier. Tim took Spot to his friend, Dr. Bird. Dr. Bird gave Spot some good water and a nice, cool place to rest. Soon, Spot felt better. He learned to listen to his friends and to only drink clean water.

Once upon a time, in a colorful forest, there lived a small bird named Billy. Billy was a happy bird who liked to play with his friends. One day, while playing, he found a big, round thing. It had a lot of weight. Billy was curious and wanted to show it to his friends. Billy went to his friend, Sally the squirrel, and said, "Look what I found! It's big and heavy. What do you think it is?" Sally looked at the round thing and nodded. "I think it's a big nut. Let's try to open it," she said. Billy and Sally tried to open the nut, but it was too heavy. They asked their friend, Tommy the turtle, to help them. Tommy used his strong legs to push the nut. Finally, the nut opened, and many colorful toys came out. All the friends were so happy and played with the toys all day long.
