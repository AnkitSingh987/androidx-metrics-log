Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Bear and the Girl

Once upon a time there was a little girl who was three. She had a special friend, a bear. Every day, the little girl and the bear would play together. One day, the bear was feeling a bit bad. He had a sore nose and didn't feel like playing. The little girl was sad, but she wanted to help. She went to the kitchen and found some cream. She put it on the bear's nose and said, "Listen, the cream will make your sore nose better." The bear listened and the cream made him feel better. The little girl and the bear played together all day until it was time for bed. The bear was no longer feeling bad. Thanks to the cream, he was feeling good. And they both lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy cat named Tom. Tom loved to take naps all day long. One day, while Tom was napping, he saw a pretty vase. The vase was big and blue, with colorful flowers on it. Tom's mom told him that the vase was very special. She said it had great value. Tom wanted to be careful around the vase, but he was so sleepy that he bumped into it. The vase wobbled, but it did not fall. Tom's mom saw what happened and gave him a big hug. She told Tom that she loved him more than the vase. Tom felt happy and safe. From that day on, he always took his naps far away from the pretty vase.

Once upon a time, in a land of ice and snow, there was a kind penguin named Pippin. Pippin liked to hug his friends and help them. One day, Pippin saw his friend, a little polar bear named Benny, who was sad. "Why are you sad, Benny?" asked Pippin. Benny said, "I am cold and I do not have a home." Pippin thought for a moment and said, "I know! We can make an igloo for you to live in!" So, Pippin and Benny worked together to build a big igloo. They used ice and snow to make the walls and the roof. When they were done, Benny had a new home. Benny was so happy that he hugged Pippin tight. From that day on, Pippin and Benny were the best of friends. They played in the snow, hugged each other, and stayed warm in Benny's igloo. And they always remembered to be kind and help others.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to wear her red boots. One day, it rained a lot. Lucy wanted to go outside and play. When Lucy went outside, she saw a big puddle. She jumped in it with her red boots. "Splash!" went the puddle. Lucy laughed and said, "I love puddles!" Lucy's mom came outside and saw the messy puddle. "Oh, Lucy," she said, "you are so messy!" Lucy smiled and said, "But it's fun, Mom!" Lucy's mom laughed too and they both played in the puddle together.

Once upon a time, there was a huge cat. The cat was so big that everyone called him Big Tom. Big Tom lived in a place with lots of soil. One day, Big Tom decided to take a walk. He said to himself, "I have never seen the fields outside of my home. I must take a look!" He started walking and walking until he found the fields. When Big Tom reached the fields, he saw a huge amount of soil. He told himself, "This is amazing! I must take some home with me." But then, a voice called out, "Stop! What do you think you are doing here?" Big Tom turned around and saw a farmer. He had a scowl on his face. Big Tom apologized and explained he wanted to take some soil home to his garden. The farmer laughed and said, "Well, I guess you can take some this time, but don't do it again!" Big Tom happily thanked the farmer and filled his bag with soil. Then, he happily walked home with his bag of soil.

Once upon a time, there was a big room. In the room, there was a long staff. The staff was very messy. It had many colors on it. A girl named Lucy liked the staff. She wanted to test it. Lucy picked up the staff. She waved it in the air. When she did, the room changed. The walls became pink. The floor became soft. Lucy was very happy. She waved the staff again. This time, the room changed into a big garden. There were many pretty flowers. But then, something unexpected happened. Lucy waved the staff one more time. The garden changed into a big pool of water. Lucy did not know how to swim. She was scared. But, the staff helped her. It became a big boat. Lucy was safe and happy. She learned that the messy staff was full of magic.
