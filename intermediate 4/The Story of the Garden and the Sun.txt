Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Garden and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She was playing in her garden, and saw something green near the gate. She ran over to see what it was. It seemed like a toy, but it was very clean. The little girl picked it up and held it close. It was a very special green toy! She ran inside to show her mum. Her mum smiled and nodded. She said the toy was very nice. The little girl was so happy. She gave the toy a hug and held it tight. She ran back out to the garden and played with her toy. She smiled as she played. The toy seemed so happy too. The sun was setting and it was time to go home. The little girl looked at her toy one last time. It felt so clean and looked so green in the sun. She put it away safely and went home for dinner.

Once there was a boy named Terry. He was only three years old and was very curious. One day, he noticed Daddy leaning against the wall in the living room. Terry asked, "What are you doing, Daddy?" Daddy said with a smile, "I'm just leaning here. It's comfortable." Terry thought about this for a moment and then he had an idea. He said, "I want to try." So he walked over to the wall and leaned against it with his hands behind his back. He leaned so far that he fell down and Daddy had to catch him. Terry laughed. "That was crazy," he said. He looked at his fingers and noticed that they left fingerprints on the wall. He rubbed them away with his sleeve and then leaned against the wall again, this time more carefully. Daddy smiled and said, "That's better." Terry smiled too and leaned against the wall for a long time, feeling proud that he had managed to lean without falling down.

Ben and Sam were brothers who liked to play with cars. They had many cars of different colors and sizes. But they did not like to share their cars with each other. They always fought over who got the best cars and who had more cars. One day, their mom told them that she was going to send some of their old cars to a charity. The charity helped children who did not have any toys to play with. Ben and Sam did not want to give away their cars. They thought their cars were too good for other children. They only picked the old cars that were rough and broken. They put them in a big box and gave it to their mom. Their mom was sad to see that they did not care about the other children. She decided to teach them a lesson. She took the box of old cars and hid it in the garage. Then she went to the store and bought some new cars that were shiny and smooth. She wrapped them in colorful paper and put them in another box. She told Ben and Sam that she had a surprise for them. Ben and Sam were excited to see the box of new cars. They thought their mom had changed her mind and bought them more cars. They opened the box and saw the beautiful cars. They smiled and thanked their mom. They wanted to play with the new cars right away. But their mom said, "Wait a minute. These cars are not for you. They are for the charity. I sent your old cars to the store and exchanged them for these new cars. I thought you would not mind, since you did not like your old cars anyway. Now you can share these new cars with the other children who have nothing to play with. Maybe you will learn to be more generous and kind." Ben and Sam were shocked and ashamed. They realized that they had been selfish and greedy. They felt sorry for the other children who had no toys. They looked at their mom and said, "We are sorry, mom. You are right. We should share our cars with the other children. We do not need so many cars. We only need a few cars that we can play with together. Can we keep some of the new cars and give the rest to the charity?" Their mom smiled and hugged them. She said, "Of course you can. I am proud of you for being honest and willing to change. You can keep two cars each and give the rest to the charity. I am sure the other children will be very happy and grateful. And you will be happier too, because sharing is caring." Ben and Sam nodded and agreed. They picked two cars each and put the rest in the box. They wrote a note to the other children, saying, "We hope you enjoy these cars. They are new and shiny and smooth. We are sorry we were not nice before. We want to be your friends. Love, Ben and Sam." They gave the box to their mom and asked her to send it to the charity. Then they played with their cars and each other, and had a lot of fun. They learned that it is better to give than to receive, and that old and rough things are not always bad, but new and smooth things are not always good. The end.
