Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Roof

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to play outside. One day, it was very hot. Amy wanted to find a way to cool down. Amy had an idea. "I will bathe on the roof!" she said. She put on her swimsuit and climbed up. She found a big tub and filled it with water. Amy got in the tub and splashed around. She felt much cooler now. Her friend, Tim, saw her on the roof. Tim was impatient. "Amy! Can I come up too?" he asked. Amy said, "Yes, come up and bathe with me!" Tim climbed up and they both splashed in the water. They had so much fun on the roof that day.

Lily and Ben were twins who liked to play in the garden. They had a big tree with a swing and a slide. They also had a small pond with some fish and frogs. They loved to watch the animals and feed them bread crumbs. One day, they saw a big, black and yellow bug flying near the tree. It was a bee, and it was looking for honey. Lily and Ben were curious and wanted to see where the bee went. They followed it quietly, hoping to find some honey too. The bee flew to a hole in the tree, where there was a hive full of honey. Lily and Ben smelled the sweet honey and their mouths watered. They wanted to taste some, but they were afraid of the bees. They decided to wait until the bee left, and then sneak some honey. But the bee did not leave. It stayed in the hive, making more honey with the other bees. Lily and Ben grew restless and bored. They started to tease each other, trying to make the other one go first. "I dare you to touch the hive," Lily said to Ben. "No, you go first," Ben said to Lily. "Chicken, chicken, bawk bawk bawk," Lily said, making chicken noises. "Monkey, monkey, ooh ooh ooh," Ben said, making monkey noises. They laughed and pushed each other, but they did not notice that their noise had woken up the bees. The bees were angry and came out of the hive, buzzing loudly. They saw Lily and Ben and thought they were trying to steal their honey. They flew towards them, ready to sting them. Lily and Ben screamed and ran away, leaving the honey behind. They ran to their house, where their mom was waiting for them. She saw their scared faces and asked them what happened. "We saw a bee and some honey, and we wanted to try some, but the bees chased us," Lily said. "We were teasing each other, and we made too much noise, and the bees got mad," Ben said. Mom hugged them and said, "You should not tease each other, or the bees. They work hard to make honey, and they do not like to share. Next time, be nice and quiet, and maybe they will let you have some honey." Lily and Ben nodded and said they were sorry. They learned their lesson and never teased the bees again. They also learned to share their own honey with each other, and with their mom. They were happy and sweet, just like honey.

One day, a big dog and a small cat were playing in the park. The sun was warm on their fur. They saw a ball and wanted to play with it. The dog said, "Let's rush to the ball and see who gets there first!" The cat liked the idea and they both started to run. As they were running, the dog felt a heavy weight on his back. He stopped and looked behind him. There was a little bird sitting on his back. The bird said, "Can I play with you too?" The dog and the cat agreed and they all started to run again. But then, something unexpected happened. The little bird flew up into the sky and took the ball with him. The dog and the cat were very sad because they couldn't play with the ball anymore. The bird didn't come back and they never saw the ball again.
