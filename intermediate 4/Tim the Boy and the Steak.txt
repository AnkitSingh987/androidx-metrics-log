Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Steak

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat steak. One day, his mom made an amazing steak for dinner. Tim was very happy. During dinner, Tim's dog, Max, sat under the table. Max wanted some steak too. Tim sighed, and then he had an idea. He gave Max a small piece of his amazing steak. Max wagged his tail and ate it quickly. From that day on, every time Tim had steak for dinner, he would share a little piece with Max. They both enjoyed the amazing steak together, and they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a charming little house, there lived a boy named Tim. He had a magic toy. The toy could talk and play with Tim. Tim loved his magic toy very much. One day, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue did not like the magic toy. She said, "I hate magic toys. They are not fun." Tim felt sad. He wanted Sue to like his magic toy. Tim had an idea. He asked the magic toy to do a charming dance. The toy danced and made Sue laugh. Sue said, "I like your magic toy now. It is very charming." Tim and Sue played with the magic toy and became good friends.

Tom and Lily went to the zoo with their mom. They saw many animals, like lions, monkeys, and elephants. But their favorite was the penguin house. They liked to watch the penguins swim and slide on the ice. "Look, mom, they are jumping!" Lily said, pointing to a group of penguins that leaped out of the water. "Yes, they are. They jump to catch fish or to get on the land," mom said. Tom wanted to jump like a penguin too. He climbed on a low wall near the penguin pool. He was about to jump when he heard a loud noise. "Hey, kid, get down from there!" a voice shouted. It was a zookeeper. He was angry. He did not want Tom to fall or scare the penguins. Tom was scared. He did not know the zookeeper. He did not know what to do. He looked at his mom, who was coming to get him. He looked at the penguins, who were staring at him. He looked at the water, which was cold and deep. "Tom, come here, now!" mom said. She was worried. She did not want Tom to get hurt or in trouble. Tom did not want to jump anymore. He wanted to be with his mom and Lily. He got down from the wall and ran to them. He hugged mom and said he was sorry. "It's okay, Tom. I'm glad you are safe. But you have to listen to me and the zookeeper. They know what is best for you and the animals," mom said. Tom nodded. He learned his lesson. He did not jump like a penguin again. He watched them from a safe distance and enjoyed the rest of the day at the zoo.

One day, a little car was driving down a road. It saw a flower that was about to bloom. The car thought the flower looked incredible. The car wanted to help the flower bloom, so it stopped and looked at the flower. "Hello, flower," said the car. "You look incredible! How can I help you bloom?" The flower was shy and did not talk at first. But then it said, "I need water to bloom. Can you help me find water?" The car wanted to help, but it did not know where to find water. It looked around and saw a big tree. The car asked the tree, "Do you know where I can find water for the flower?" The tree said, "Yes, there is a pond nearby. Go there and get some water for the flower." The car went to the pond and got some water. It brought the water back to the flower. The flower drank the water and started to bloom. The car and the flower were both very happy. They had worked together to solve the problem, and now the flower looked even more incredible.

Once upon a time, in an ordinary town, there was a girl named Lucy. Lucy loved to walk. She walked every day, rain or shine. She walked to the park, the store, and to her friends' houses. Everyone in the town knew Lucy for her love of walking. One day, Lucy met a little dog. The dog was lost and needed help. Lucy felt love for the dog and wanted to help. She decided to walk with the dog to find its home. They walked and walked, looking for the dog's family. After a long walk, Lucy and the dog found the dog's home. The dog's family was so happy to see their pet again. They thanked Lucy for her help and love. From that day on, Lucy and the dog became best friends. They walked together every day, sharing their love for walking in their ordinary town.
