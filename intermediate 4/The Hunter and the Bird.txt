Category: Intermediate
Title: The Hunter and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a hunter. The hunter liked to find and catch animals. He was brave and strong. One day, he saw an unknown bird in the trees. The bird was big and had many colors. The hunter wanted to catch the bird, but it flew away. The hunter went home and told his friends about the unknown bird. They all wanted to see it too. So, they made a plan to find the bird. They would mail letters to each other when they saw the bird. They hoped this would help them catch it. Days went by, and the friends mailed letters to each other. They looked for the unknown bird in the big forest. One day, the hunter got a letter from a friend. The letter said the unknown bird was near the river. The hunter ran to the river and saw the bird. But this time, he did not try to catch it. The bird was too beautiful and special. The hunter and his friends decided to let the unknown bird live happily in the big forest.

Once upon a time, there was a kitty named Spot. Spot was black, and he loved to bathe in the sun. Every day, he would find a sunny spot in the garden and lay there, soaking up the warm sun rays. One day, as Spot was napping in the sun, he heard a voice. "Hey Spot! Come to the shelter", it said. He looked over, and saw a big, black shelter at the edge of the garden. Spot was curious, so he decided to go take a look. When Spot got to the shelter, he noticed a bowl of fresh water. He couldn't resist - he bathed himself in the bowl until he was all wet and refreshed. Spot found the shelter to be the most wonderful place - he wanted to stay forever! Spot was so happy, and he thanked the voice for taking him to the new shelter. "My pleasure, Spot! Come back here anytime you're looking for a bath," the voice said. Spot was so glad he had listened to the voice! From then on, Spot visited the shelter often and enjoyed cozy baths in the bowl of fresh water. He was one very happy kitty.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a small, troubled kitten in her yard. The kitten was sad and scared. Mia wanted to help the kitten, so she went to her mom and asked, "Mom, can we help the kitten?" Her mom said, "Yes, but first we need to find out why the kitten is so troubled." So, Mia and her mom went outside to look for clues. They saw a big hole in the fence where the kitten must have come through. Mia and her mom fixed the hole in the fence and brought the kitten inside. They gave the kitten some food and a soft bed to sleep in. But the kitten still looked troubled. Mia thought and thought, and then she had an idea. She gently poked the kitten's soft fur and said, "Do you miss your mom and dad?" The kitten looked at Mia and meowed, as if to say, "Yes, I do." Mia and her mom knew what they had to do. They took the kitten back outside and opened the fence just a little bit. The kitten went through the hole and found its mom and dad waiting on the other side. Mia and her mom smiled, knowing they had helped the troubled kitten find its way home.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. They liked to play in the park, and they would push a big red ball to each other. One day, Tim and Spot saw a pointy hat behind a tree. "Who is there?" Tim asked. A little girl named Sue came out from behind the tree. She was wearing the pointy hat. "Hi, I'm Sue!" she said. "I lost my ball, can I play with you?" Tim and Spot said yes, and the three friends started to play together. They were having so much fun pushing the big red ball. But then, something unexpected happened. The ball rolled away and fell into a big hole in the ground! "Oh no!" said Tim. "How will we get our ball back?" Spot, the loyal dog, had an idea. He ran and found a long stick. He pushed the stick into the hole and pulled the ball out. Tim, Spot, and Sue were so happy that they could keep playing together.
