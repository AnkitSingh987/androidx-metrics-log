Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Sun

One day, a little girl named Amy went for a walk with her mom. They walked in the park and saw a big ice cream truck. Amy was very happy because she loved ice cream. Her mom bought her a cone with her favorite flavor, strawberry. The ice cream was very cold but tasted warm and sweet. As they walked, Amy saw a tiny bug on the ground. The bug had wings that seemed to glow in the sun. It was a pretty bug, and it made Amy smile. Her mom told her it was a special bug called a firefly. Fireflies can glow in the dark, she said. Amy and her mom sat on the grass and waited for the sun to go down. When it was dark, they saw many fireflies flying around. The fireflies glowed like tiny stars in the night. Amy felt warm and happy as she ate her ice cream and watched the glowing bugs with her mom.

One day, a bright sun shone in the sky. A little girl named Mia went to the park with her mom. They saw a big, red ball. Mia wanted to play with the ball, but she had to resist. Her mom said, "Wait, Mia. We need to ask whose ball it is." Mia saw a boy with a sad face. She asked him, "Is this your ball?" The boy nodded and smiled. He had lost his ball, but now he was happy. Mia had a memory of losing her toy once. She knew how the boy felt. Mia, the boy, and her mom played with the ball together. They laughed and had fun under the bright sun. They made a new friend that day. And Mia learned that it was good to resist and do the right thing.

One day, a girl named Sue went to search for candy. She looked high and low, but she could not find any. Sue felt terrible because she really wanted some candy to share with her friends. Sue met a boy named Tom. He said, "I know where we can find candy!" So, they went to a big tree. Under the tree, they found a big box of candy. Sue and Tom were very happy. Sue and Tom took the candy to their friends. They all ate the candy together. Everyone said, "Yum! This is the best candy ever!" They were all very happy and not terrible anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was not like other dogs. He had an ugly spot on his back. Yesterday, Spot met a new friend, a cat named Whiskers. Spot said to Whiskers, "Hello! I am Spot. Do you want to play with me?" Whiskers looked at the ugly spot on Spot's back and said, "Yes, I want to play. But, what is that on your back?" Spot felt sad and said, "It is my ugly spot. It makes me different." Whiskers thought for a moment and said, "You remind me of my friend, Stripes. He is different too. He has stripes on his fur, but we still play together. It is okay to be different." Spot felt happy and played with Whiskers all day. They became best friends.

Once upon a time there was a careless rabbit. One day, the rabbit entered a pond to play. As the rabbit happily hopped around, a voice called out from the pond, “Are you lost, friend?” The rabbit was surprised and looked around. There, in the center of the pond, was a big friendly frog. The frog said, “I can show you the way home if you’d like.” The rabbit smiled and said, “Yes please! I’m a careless rabbit and I don’t know how to get home.” The frog laughed and said, “Then hop on, my friend, and I’ll take you there!” So the rabbit hopped onto the frog’s back, and the frog carried him home. When they arrived, the rabbit hopped off and said, “Thank you, friendly frog. Now I won’t be careless anymore and will never forget the way home!” The frog laughed and said, “You’re welcome! Anytime!” And with that, the two friends said goodbye, and the rabbit hopped happily home.
