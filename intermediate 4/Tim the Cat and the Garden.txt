Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Cat and the Garden

One day, a little cat named Tim went for a walk. He found an unusual herb in the garden. Tim thought it looked very pretty. He wanted to show it to his friend, the big dog named Sam. Tim took the herb to Sam. Sam liked the herb too. They played with the herb together. They had a lot of fun. They teased each other with the herb, running and laughing. After playing, Tim and Sam were tired. They sat down under a tree. They put the unusual herb between them. They looked at the herb and smiled. They were very happy to have a new toy to play with.

Lily liked to bake with her mom. They made cakes and cookies and pies. Lily liked to mix the flour and the eggs and the sugar. She liked to lick the spoon and the bowl. She liked to smell the yummy things in the oven. One day, Lily's mom said, "Lily, do you want to bake something for your class? You can share it with your friends and your teacher." Lily was happy. She said, "Yes, mom, I want to bake something for my class. What can we bake?" Her mom said, "How about banana bread? It is easy and tasty and we have some ripe bananas." Lily said, "Okay, mom, let's bake banana bread. I love bananas." They peeled and mashed the bananas. They added the butter and the honey and the milk. They stirred in the flour and the baking soda and the salt. They poured the batter into a loaf pan. They put it in the oven. Lily waited for the banana bread to bake. She smelled the sweet and warm aroma. She said, "Mom, it smells so good. I can't wait to eat it." Her mom said, "Me too, Lily. But we have to let it cool down first. Then we can slice it and wrap it for your class." Lily nodded. She was proud of her banana bread. She hoped her friends and her teacher would like it too. The next day, Lily brought her banana bread to her class. She gave it to her teacher. She said, "Teacher, this is for you and my friends. I baked it with my mom. It is banana bread. It is yummy." Her teacher smiled. She said, "Thank you, Lily. That is very kind of you. It looks delicious. And it smells wonderful." She sliced the banana bread and gave a piece to each child. They all said, "Thank you, Lily. Thank you, Lily's mom." They ate the banana bread. They liked it very much. They said, "Mmm, this is so good. This is the best banana bread ever." Lily was happy. She said, "You're welcome, everyone. I'm glad you like it." She ate her piece too. She thought, "This is the best banana bread ever. And I baked it with my mom."

One day, a clumsy cat named Tom went for a walk. He liked to observe things around him. Today, he saw a big, shiny tooth on the ground. Tom was curious and wanted to find out where the tooth came from. He asked his friend, a bird named Sally, if she knew anything about the tooth. Sally did not know, but she wanted to help Tom. They both went on an adventure to find the owner of the tooth. As they walked, they found a sad dog named Max. Max had lost his tooth when he was eating a bone. Tom and Sally gave the tooth back to Max. But, surprise! The tooth was magic! It turned Max into a happy, colorful unicorn! Now, they all played together and had lots of fun.

One day, a charming cat named Fluffy found a magic ball in the garage. The ball was shiny and round. Fluffy liked to play with the ball. She would roll it, chase it, and jump on it. One day, Fluffy said to the ball, "I wish I could shrink and go inside you!" The ball heard Fluffy and made her wish come true. Fluffy started to shrink, and she became very small. She was so small that she could fit inside the ball. Inside the ball, Fluffy found a fun world. She met other small animals who also lived in the ball. They played together, and Fluffy was very happy. But soon, Fluffy missed her home and her big size. She said to her new friends, "I want to go back to my home and be big again." Her new friends helped her find the magic ball inside their world. Fluffy touched the ball and said, "Please make me big again and take me back to my home." The ball heard Fluffy's wish, and she grew big again. Fluffy was back in the garage, happy to be home. From that day on, Fluffy played with the magic ball, but she never wished to shrink again. She loved her home and her size just the way they were.
