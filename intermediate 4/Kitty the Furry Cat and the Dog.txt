Category: Intermediate
Title: Kitty the Furry Cat and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play in the garden. One day, Kitty saw a big, red ball in the garden. She wanted to play with it, but it was too high for her to reach. Kitty went to ask her friend, Dog, for help. Dog was big and strong. Kitty thought he could get the ball down for her. Dog agreed to help, but he needed Kitty to do something for him first. He said, "Kitty, if you want me to help you, you must find my bone. I lost it in the garden." So, Kitty went back to the garden to look for Dog's bone. She looked and looked, but she could not find it. She started to feel sad. Then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew down and whispered to Kitty, "I know where the bone is. I will show you if you promise to share the red ball with me." Kitty agreed, and the bird led her to the bone. She took the bone to Dog, and he was very happy. As promised, Dog helped Kitty get the red ball down from the tree. They played with the ball all day, and they even let the little bird join in. In the end, they all became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little black dog named Spot. Spot liked to play in the park. One day, he saw a girl sitting on a bench. The girl looked sad. Spot walked up to the girl and said, "Hi, I am Spot. Why are you sad?" The girl looked at Spot and said, "Hi Spot, I am Lily. I am sad because I have no one to play with." Spot wanted to help Lily. He had an idea. He said, "Lily, I will introduce you to my friend, Sam. He is a nice boy and he loves to play." Lily smiled and said, "I would like that, Spot." Spot took Lily to meet Sam. They all played together and had fun. Lily was not sad anymore. From that day on, Lily, Sam, and Spot played together every day on the bench in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a modest little boy named Tim. Tim had a big heart and loved to help others. One day, he found a small gum under his bed. He picked it up and thought of sharing it with his friend, Sam. Tim went to Sam's house to give him the gum. Sam was very happy to see Tim and the gum. They sat down and enjoyed the gum together. After they finished, Sam said, "Thank you, Tim, for sharing your gum with me." From that day on, Tim and Sam became the best of friends. They played together, shared their toys, and always remembered to thank each other. And they both knew that sharing and being modest made them very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a humble little girl named Mia. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Mia liked to play with her toys and go to the park. One day, Mia found a pretty box with a lock on it. She wanted to see what was inside. Mia asked her mom and dad for help. They said she needed a key to unlock the lock. Mia looked everywhere for the key. She looked under her bed, in the kitchen, and even in the garden. Finally, she found the key in her toy box. Mia was so happy! She used the key to unlock the lock on the pretty box. Inside the box, she found a beautiful toy doll. Mia loved her new toy and played with it all day long.

Once upon a time there was a small bird that could fly. The bird was very curious and wanted to see what was inside a jar. So it flew to the jar and peered inside. Suddenly, a voice said, "What are you doing?" Startled, the bird turned around and saw a small frog. The bird answered, "I'm just curious about this jar." The frog asked, "Do you want to see what's inside?" The bird said, "Yes, please!" The frog smiled and opened the jar. Inside was a fascinating collection of colorful stones. "Wow!" said the bird. "It's so beautiful!" The frog smiled. "I knew you'd like it," he said. The curious bird flew off, content in knowing that even the simplest of jars could contain wonderful surprises.
