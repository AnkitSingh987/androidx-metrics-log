Category: Intermediate
Title: The Wide Tree and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a wide tree. In the tree, there was a turkey. The turkey was very big and nice. One day, the turkey saw a small cat. The cat was sad. The cat said, "I want to go up the tree." The turkey heard the cat's request. The turkey said, "I will help you. Hold on to me." The cat held on to the turkey, and the turkey took the cat up the tree. They played in the tree all day. The cat was happy. The turkey was happy too. The sun went down, and it was time to go home. The turkey took the cat down from the tree. The cat said, "Thank you, turkey!" The turkey said, "You're welcome, cat!" The moral of the story is to always help your friends when they need it.

Once upon a time, in a hidden place in the woods, there was a small cabin. The cabin was very old and dirty. Inside the cabin, there lived a kind rabbit named Bob. One day, Bob decided to clean his cabin. He started with the floor, then the walls, and finally the windows. As he cleaned, he found many things he had lost, like his favorite toy and a big red ball. When the cabin was all clean, Bob invited his friends over. They played games and had a lot of fun. Bob was very happy that he had cleaned his cabin, and his friends had a great time too.

Once, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a bicycle. He loved to ride it every day. One day, he decided to wander far away with his bicycle. He wanted to have a big adventure. As Tim wandered, he saw many new things. He saw a big red ball, a happy dog, and a tall tree. Tim felt successful because he was having so much fun on his adventure. He rode his bicycle fast and laughed all the way. Soon, it was time to go home. Tim turned his bicycle around and rode back. His mom and dad were waiting for him. They were so proud of their successful little boy. They all had a big hug and Tim told them about his fun adventure.

One day, a fat cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a little girl named Sue playing in the soil. She was making mud pies. Tom went up to her and asked, "What are you doing?" Sue looked up and said, "I am making mud pies. Want to help?" Tom nodded and tried to explain how to make the best mud pies. They had lots of fun and got very dirty. Just then, a big rain cloud came and it started to rain. The soil turned into a big mud puddle. Tom and Sue were surprised. They jumped and splashed in the mud puddle. They laughed and played until the rain stopped. When they were done, they were the happiest and muddiest friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play at the park. Her favorite thing to play on was the swing. She would swing high up in the sky, and it made her very happy. One day, Sue went to the park and saw that the swing was dirty. She wanted to wash it, so she went home and got a bucket of water. She came back and poured the water on the swing. Now the swing was clean and shiny! Sue was ready to play on the swing. She looked around and saw that the park was empty. She was the only one there. Sue smiled and jumped on the swing. She swung higher and higher, having lots of fun. And she was glad that she had washed the swing, making it clean for her and her friends to play on later.

Once upon a time, there was a fisherman. He was a kind and compassionate man. He lived near a big blue sea. Every day, he went to the sea to catch fish. He loved the sea and the fish. One day, while the fisherman was catching fish, he heard a yell. It was a little bird. The bird was hurt and needed help. The fisherman was a good man, so he wanted to help the bird. The fisherman took the bird home. He cared for the bird and made it feel better. The bird was happy and thanked the fisherman. They became good friends. The fisherman and the bird lived happily ever after.
