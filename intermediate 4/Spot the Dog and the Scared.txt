Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Scared

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was scared of big dogs. When he saw a big dog, he would run away and hide. One day, Spot met a new friend, a small cat named Fluffy. Fluffy asked, "Why are you scared of big dogs?" Spot just gave a little shrug. He didn't know why yet. Fluffy said, "Let's be brave together!" They played and had fun. When a big dog came, they did not run away. Spot was not scared anymore. And they all played together, happy and brave.

Once upon a time, in a calm town, there was a little house. In the house lived a kind girl named Amy and her pet cat, Fluffy. They were very happy together and loved to play all day. One sunny day, Amy and Fluffy went for a walk. They saw a big sign near a tree. The sign said, "Lost Dog." Amy looked at Fluffy and said, "We should help find the lost dog." Fluffy meowed in agreement, and they began to search. After a while, they found the lost dog hiding behind a bush. The dog was scared, but Amy and Fluffy were very nice to him. They took him back to the sign, and soon the dog's owner came. The owner was so happy and said, "Thank you for finding my dog!" Amy and Fluffy felt good for helping others. The moral of the story is: it's important to help others when they need it.

Once upon a time there were two friends walking in the forest. One was called Tim and he was very tall. The other was called Sam and she was very small. They were looking for something. Tim said, "Let's look for some needles." Sam asked, "What is a needle?" Tim explained, "A needle is small and sharp. We can use it to grow things." So they started to look for needles. Eventually they found one high up in a tree. It was hard to reach, so Tim climbed up. He grabbed the needle and climbed down. They were very happy. The next day, they planted some seeds with the needle. They watered the seeds every day until the plants began to grow. Sam and Tim watched their plants grow bigger and bigger. Soon their plants were big and strong - just what they needed. They were so pleased that they had found the needle.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a king. The king had a big, shiny crown. He loved his crown very much. One day, the king went for a walk in the sun. He was very hot and sweaty. He took off his crown to dry it. A little boy saw the king and his crown. The boy said, "Wow! Your crown is so big and shiny. Can I try it on?" The king said, "No, my crown is for me only." The boy was sad but he walked away. The next day, the king found his crown was gone. He looked everywhere but could not find it. A little bird told the king that the boy took it. The king was very mad. He went to the boy's house and found the crown. But it was dead. The shiny stones were gone. The king cried and cried. He was never happy again.

Once upon a time, in an ancient house, there lived a group of animals. There was a big bear, a small cat, and a tiny bird. They were very good friends. One day, they found a big pot in the house. They did not know what was inside, but they were very curious. The big bear said, "Let's stir the pot and see what happens." The small cat and the tiny bird agreed. They all took turns stirring the pot. As they stirred, they saw something shiny inside the pot. The next day, they decided to stir the pot again. This time, the shiny thing came out of the pot. It was a magic spoon! The magic spoon could make any food they wanted. The group of friends were so happy. They ate yummy food every day and lived happily ever after.
