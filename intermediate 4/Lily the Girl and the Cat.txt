Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to study her big book with many prints. The prints were of animals, like cats, dogs, and birds. Lily wanted to be ready to show her friends what she learned. One day, Lily's friend Tim came over to play. "Look, Tim!" Lily said, pointing to a print in her book. "This is a cat. It says 'meow'." Tim smiled and said, "Wow, Lily! You are so smart! I want to study too!" So, Lily and Tim studied the book together. They looked at many prints and learned about animals. After they were done, Lily and Tim felt ready to play. They pretended to be animals from the book. Lily was a cat, and Tim was a dog. They had lots of fun, and Lily was happy she could share her love for studying prints with her friend.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a smart little bunny named Ben. Ben loved to hop and play with his friends. One sunny day, Ben and his friends were playing near a big tree. As they played, Ben saw a trail of tiny stones. He was curious and decided to follow the trail. His friends called out to him, but Ben was too excited to listen. He hopped and hopped, following the stones farther and farther away. The trail led Ben to a big hill. Ben was tired but wanted to see what was at the top. He hopped up the hill, but then he tripped and had a big fall. He rolled down the hill and landed near his friends. They all hugged him and were happy he was safe. Ben learned that it's always better to stay with his friends and not follow strange trails. From that day on, Ben and his friends always played together and had lots of fun. And they all knew that Ben was not just a smart bunny, but a very lucky one too.

Once upon a time, there were twin sisters named Jane and June. They loved to play at the park. Their favorite thing to do was to slide down the big slide together. They would laugh and giggle as they went down the slide, holding hands. One day, they saw a tiny, fragile bird near the slide. The bird looked sad and scared. Jane and June wanted to help the bird, so they walked up to it slowly. "Hello, little bird," said Jane. "Why are you sad?" The bird looked at the twins and said, "I can't fly. I want to go home, but I don't know how." Jane and June thought about how they could help the bird. They had an idea. They would slide down the slide with the bird, so it could feel like it was flying. The twins held the fragile bird gently and went to the top of the slide. They held the bird as they slid down together. The bird felt happy and thanked the twins for their help. From that day on, the twins and the bird became best friends. They played at the park together, sliding down the slide and having lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was an adventurous goose. She wanted to go on an adventure, but she had no idea where to go. Then one day, she heard some loud clapping. It was so loud that she could hear it from far away. She wondered what it was and flew towards the clapping. When she arrived, she saw a big crowd of people. Everyone was clapping and smiling, as if they were having fun. She was so curious that she had to get closer. As she flew down to the crowd, everyone stopped clapping and started to applaud the goose! The goose was so surprised and happy. She had never expected her adventure to end up with so much appreciation! She flapped her wings and thanked everyone with a loud honk and continued her journey with a newfound joy.

John was walking through the park when he noticed something. It was a red coat on the ground. John knew that no one was around, so he decided to pick it up and take it home with him. John took the coat home and put it on the couch. He wondered who could've left it there. Then he heard a voice outside his door. It was a little girl who had been looking for her coat. She was so happy to find it that she started to yell with joy. John took off the coat and gave it to the little girl. She said she was so grateful that he had found it. She hugged John and thanked him. John was really happy to help. They said goodbye and the girl went back home with her coat.
