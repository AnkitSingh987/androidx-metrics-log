Category: Intermediate
Title: The Sweet and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a sweet little infant. The infant had a big toy box full of fun toys. One day, the infant wanted to play with a toy, but the toy was at the bottom of the box. The infant started to cry and complain. The infant's mom came to help. She took out all the toys from the box to find the toy the infant wanted. But then, something unexpected happened. The toy box started to move and shake! The infant and mom looked at each other, surprised. Out from the toy box came a big, friendly bear! The bear said, "I was hiding in the box and you found me!" The infant stopped crying and started to laugh. The bear played with the infant and mom, and they all had a great time. No one complained anymore, and they had a fun day with their new bear friend.

John was poking at a rock. He wanted to see what it was like. He asked his mom, "What is this?" His mom smiled, "That's a rock. It's a hard, thin thing that you can poke at." John thought for a moment and then smiled, "Can I take it outside to poke it in the dirt?" His mom said, "Sure, just take the tip of it." John grabbed the tip of the rock and ran outside. He began to poke it into the dirt and make a big hole. He smiled and kept poking. Soon, he had made a very deep hole! John looked up proudly to his mom, "I did it! I made a big hole with the rock." His mom laughed, "You sure did! That was a thin rock, and you did a great job with it." John smiled and ran back inside to tell his dad what he had done with the rock.

Lily liked to bathe in the big tub with her toys. She had a duck, a frog, a boat and a cup. She made the duck and the frog talk to each other and the boat go fast. She filled the cup with water and poured it on her head. She laughed and splashed. But one day, she saw a big spider on the wall. It had eight legs and two eyes. It was black and hairy. Lily was scared of the spider. She screamed and cried. She wanted to get out of the tub. Mommy heard Lily and ran to the bathroom. She saw the spider and the scared Lily. She took a tissue and caught the spider. She threw it out of the window. She hugged Lily and said, "It's okay, sweetie. The spider is gone. You are safe." Lily stopped crying and smiled. She said, "Thank you, Mommy. You are brave." Mommy said, "You are brave, too. You are my brave girl." She kissed Lily and wrapped her in a towel. Mommy dried Lily and put on her pajamas. She said, "Do you want to dance with me?" Lily nodded and said, "Yes, please." Mommy turned on the music and held Lily's hand. They danced and twirled and giggled. Lily forgot about the spider and felt happy. She loved to dance with Mommy.

Once upon a time, there was a pink bunny. The bunny lived in a backyard with a big fence. The bunny liked to play all day with its friends. One day, the bunny saw a hole in the fence. It wanted to escape and see what was outside. The pink bunny hopped through the hole and into a new place. The bunny met new friends and played all day. But soon, it missed its old backyard. The pink bunny found the hole again and went back home. It was happy to be back in its own backyard with its old friends.

Once upon a time, in a colorful land, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to wake up early and watch the sunrise. Every morning, she would run to the window and see the big, orange sun come up. One day, Amy saw a twisty path in the woods. She was curious and wanted to see where it went. As she walked, she met a friendly squirrel. The squirrel said, "Hi, Amy! Do you want to play with me?" Amy was happy to have a new friend. They played and twisted around the trees. They laughed and had fun. Together, they watched the beautiful sunrise. From that day on, Amy and the squirrel played together every day, making the colorful land even more fun and bright.
