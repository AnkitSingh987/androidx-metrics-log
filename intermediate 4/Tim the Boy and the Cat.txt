Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with his mom, dad, and cat. One day, Tim's mom said, "We will go on a tour of the big zoo tomorrow, I promise." Tim was so happy because he loved animals. The next day, they went to the zoo. Tim saw big elephants, tall giraffes, and funny monkeys. But then, something unexpected happened. The sun went away, and the sky became dark and harsh. It started to rain very hard, and everyone had to leave the zoo. Tim was sad they had to leave, but his mom had a surprise. She said, "I promise we will come back to the zoo soon, but for now, let's have a fun tour at home." They went home and made a zoo with Tim's toy animals. Tim was happy again, even though the day did not go as planned.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys all day. One day, his mom asked him to help her set the table for dinner. She gave him a big tray full of cups and plates. Tim grabbed the tray very carefully. He walked slowly to the table and put everything on it. His mom was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tim!" Tim felt grateful for his mom's kind words. The next day, Tim's friend, Sam, came to play. Tim remembered how good it felt to help his mom. So, he shared his toys with Sam and helped him build a big tower. Sam was grateful for Tim's help, and they had lots of fun. Tim learned that helping others makes everyone feel happy. And that was the moral value of the story: always help others and share what you have.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Hop. Hop loved to hop all day long. He hopped all around the big green field. Hop was a very happy bunny. One day, Hop found a big box in the field. It was a fridge! The fridge was very cheap. Hop hopped on top of the fridge and looked inside. He saw many yummy treats. There were carrots, apples, and even some cake! Hop hopped down and took some of the treats from the fridge. He shared them with his friends. All the animals in the field had a big party. They ate the yummy treats and played fun games. Hop was so happy to have found the cheap fridge. And they all lived happily ever after.

Tom and Anna were friends who liked to play outside. One day, they went to the park with their moms. They saw a big pond with ducks and geese. The water was icy and shiny. "Look, Anna, the ducks are swimming in the icy water. They are brave and strong," Tom said. "I like the geese better. They have long necks and make funny noises. They are smart and funny," Anna said. They admired the ducks and geese for a while. Then they heard their moms calling them. "Tom, Anna, it's time for lunch. Come here, we have sandwiches and juice for you," their moms said. Tom and Anna ran to their moms. They were hungry and happy. They ate their lunch on a bench near the pond. They shared their sandwiches and juice. They thanked their moms for the yummy lunch. "Can we play some more after lunch, mom?" Tom asked. "Sure, honey, but you have to wear your hats and gloves. It's very cold today," his mom said. "OK, mom. We will be careful and warm," Anna said. They finished their lunch and put their hats and gloves on. Then they went back to the pond. They played with the ducks and geese. They threw some bread crumbs for them. They had a lot of fun. They liked the icy pond and the animals. They were good friends.

Once upon a time, there was an ancient tree in a big park. Many kids played near the tree, but they did not know it was very old. One day, a little boy named Tim found a waffle under the tree. He was very hungry and wanted to eat it. As Tim picked up the waffle, the ancient tree began to tremble. The leaves shook and the branches moved. The kids near the tree got scared and ran away. Tim was so scared too, but he did not want to leave the waffle. The tree trembled more and more. Then, it fell down with a loud crash. Tim did not run away in time. The ancient tree fell on the waffle and Tim. The other kids were sad because Tim got hurt. They learned to be careful near old trees and not to eat waffles they find on the ground.
