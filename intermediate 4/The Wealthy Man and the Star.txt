Category: Intermediate
Title: The Wealthy Man and the Star

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man who lived in a big house. He loved to look at the stars in the sky every night. One day, he saw a very bright star and wished he could have it. The man made a plan to get the star. He bought a big ladder and put it in his yard. He thought, "I will climb the ladder and get the star tonight." But, he forgot to take care of the food in his house. While the man was busy with the ladder, the food in his house started to rot. It smelled very bad. The man did not know this because he was outside looking at the star. Finally, the man climbed the ladder to get the star. But, when he reached the top, the star was gone. The man was sad and climbed back down. When he went inside, he saw the rotten food and knew he had to clean it up. The man learned that he should take care of the things he has before trying to get something new. He still loved to look at the stars, but he never tried to get one again.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Lily. She was always upset because it was very hot in her room. She wished she had a fan to help her cool down. One day, Lily's mom saw how upset she was. She wanted to help her. So, she went to the store and ordered a big, colorful fan for Lily's room. Lily was so excited to see the fan! When the fan came, Lily's mom put it in her room. It made her room feel cooler and not so hot. Lily was not upset anymore. She played with her toys and smiled. The fan made her very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a popular bunny named Spring. Spring loved to hop and play with his friends in the sunny meadow. One day, Spring met a new friend, a little bird named Touch. "Hi, I'm Spring!" the bunny said. "Do you want to play with me?" "Yes, I'm Touch!" the bird replied. "Let's play together!" They played all day, hopping and flying around the meadow. They played catch and hide-and-seek. All the other animals in the meadow saw how much fun they were having and wanted to join. Soon, Spring and Touch became the most popular friends in the meadow. At the end of the day, Spring and Touch were very tired, but they were also very happy. They knew they would be best friends forever and always have fun together. And from that day on, the meadow was filled with laughter and joy as all the animals played together.

Once upon a time, there was a little green frog. The frog lived near a store. The store was big and had many things inside. The frog liked to hop around the store and look at all the things. One day, the frog met a little girl at the store. The girl was nice and liked the green frog. The frog liked the girl too. They played together and had a lot of fun. When it was time for the girl to go home, she gave the green frog a big hug. The frog was happy and hugged her back. They said goodbye and the girl went home. The green frog hopped back to his home, feeling very happy.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to ride his bike. He liked to ride fast and easy. He loved to feel the wind in his hair. Tim's mom told him, "Be careful and watch out for emergencies." Tim rode his bike around the park. He saw his friend, Sam, who wanted to race. They rode fast and laughed. But then, Tim saw a big rock in the path. He remembered his mom's words about emergencies. Tim stopped his bike quickly and told Sam to stop too. They both stopped just in time. The big rock could have been a bad emergency. Tim and Sam were happy they listened to Tim's mom. They knew it was important to watch out for emergencies when they ride.
