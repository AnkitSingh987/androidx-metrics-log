Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Hill

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big bag of marbles. He loved to play with them all day. One day, he found a very big marble. It was not like the others. It was slow to roll, but Tim liked it a lot. One day, Tim had a race with his friends. They each had to roll their marbles down a hill. Tim used his big, slow marble. His friends laughed at him. They did not think his marble would win. As the race began, Tim's marble rolled very slow. But then, something happened. The other marbles went too fast and went off the path. Tim's slow marble stayed on the path and won the race. Tim's friends were surprised. They all began to applaud for Tim and his slow marble. Tim was very happy, and they all learned that sometimes, slow can be better.

Once upon a time, there was a big, hairy dog named Spot. Spot had a job to do every day. He had to weigh the apples from the apple tree. Spot was very good at his job. One day, Spot went to the apple tree to do his job. He saw something unexpected. There was a big, green frog sitting on the apples! Spot was surprised. He did not know what to do. Spot decided to talk to the frog. The frog said, "I can help you weigh the apples." Spot was happy. He and the frog worked together every day. They became best friends and did a great job weighing apples.

Once upon a time, there was a strong prince. He lived in a big castle with his mom and dad. The prince liked to write. He wrote with a pen and paper. One day, the prince went to the park. He saw a big tree. He wanted to write a letter to his friend. He sat under the tree and started to write. A little bird came and said, "Hello, strong prince! What are you doing?" The prince said, "I am writing a letter to my friend." The little bird looked at the letter. Then, something unexpected happened! The bird took the pen from the prince and started to write too! The prince was very surprised. He said, "Wow, you can write too!" The bird and the prince became friends and they wrote letters together.

Once upon a time, there lived an old man and a lively cat. One day, they decided to explore the outside world. So they set off on a long walk. Suddenly, it started to rain. Big grey clouds covered the sky, and the thunder roared. The old man and the cat were scared. They were about to lose their way in the storm! The cat was brave. He meowed and said, "We can follow my nose. I know the way home!" The old man smiled and followed the cat. The cat ran across the fields happily. After a while, they arrived safely at the old man's home. The old man was relieved. He said, "Thank you, my lively friend. You saved us both!" They both lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a humble rock. The rock was very small and liked to remain in one place. It lived in a big garden with many other rocks, but it was the smallest of them all. One day, a little bird came to the garden. It saw the humble rock and decided to sit on it. The bird and the rock became good friends. The bird would sing songs, and the rock would remain still and listen. As the days went by, the bird and the rock stayed friends. The other rocks in the garden were not as humble and did not have any friends. The humble rock was happy to have a friend, and the bird was happy to have a place to rest. And so, they remained friends forever.

Jenny liked to follow her big brother Tom everywhere. She wanted to do what he did and play with his toys. But Tom did not like Jenny following him. He said she was too small and silly. He was rude to her and told her to go away. One day, Tom found a new toy in the park. It was a big ball with a funny texture. It was soft and fuzzy and made a squeaky sound when he squeezed it. Tom was very happy with his new toy. He did not want to share it with anyone, especially Jenny. Jenny saw Tom playing with the ball and wanted to touch it too. She thought it looked fun and nice. She followed Tom to his secret spot behind the bushes. She tried to grab the ball from him, but he pushed her away. He said the ball was his and she could not have it. He was very rude to her and made her cry. But Jenny did not give up. She waited until Tom was not looking and snatched the ball from him. She ran away with it, laughing. She wanted to see how it felt and sounded. She did not care that Tom was angry and chased her. Tom followed Jenny and tried to get his ball back. He shouted at her and called her names. He was very rude to her and made her scared. But Jenny was faster and smarter. She ran to the playground and hid the ball under the slide. She climbed up the ladder and slid down. She had fun and forgot about Tom. Tom looked for Jenny and the ball everywhere. He could not find them. He was very sad and sorry. He wished he had been nicer to Jenny and shared his ball with her. He learned his lesson. He was not rude to her anymore. He followed her to the slide and said he was sorry. He asked her to play with him and the ball. Jenny was happy and forgave him. She gave him the ball and they played together. They had fun and were nice to each other. They were good siblings.
