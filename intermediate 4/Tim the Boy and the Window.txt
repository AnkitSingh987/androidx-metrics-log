Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Window

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. One day, Tim found a surprise under his bed. It was a big, red ball. Tim was so happy! He played with the ball all day long. But, Tim was not careful. He kicked the ball too hard, and it broke a window. Tim was sad. His mom told him he needed to replace the window. So, Tim went to the store with his mom to buy a new window. At the store, Tim was very careful. He picked out the perfect window to replace the broken one. His mom was proud of him. They went home, and Tim helped his mom put in the new window. Tim learned to be careful when playing with his big, red ball. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to read books. He went to the library with his mom every week. The library was a big room with many books. Tim was very happy there. One hot day, Tim and his mom went to the library. Tim found a book about a cat. The cat was funny and liked to play. Tim wanted to take the book home. He showed it to his mom. She said, "Yes, you can have it." Tim put the book in his bag. He carried it home. When they got home, Tim sat in his room and read the book. He laughed and smiled. The cat in the book made him very happy. Tim loved the library and could not wait to go back.

Once upon a time there was a family who lived together in a small house. Mom, Dad, a brother and a sister enjoyed living in it. They often discussed things around the dinner table. One day they decided to discuss corn. Their father said, "We should plant corn in our backyard and make it into a garden!" The family was excited and they agreed. Even though the brother and sister didn't like helping with the garden, their parents encouraged them. Meanwhile, the family's neighbor Mr. Grumpy became upset. He said, "I don't want you to have a garden so close to my house. It does not look nice to me." The family was sad and argued with Mr. Grumpy. No matter how hard they tried to reason with him, he wouldn't change his mind. The moral of the story is if we are kind to one another, there will be fewer disagreements.

One day, a little bird was flying in the sky. The bird was bald and felt sad. He wanted to find some oats to eat. He looked for oats on the ground and in trees, but he could not find any. Then, the bald bird saw a big bear. The bear had a big bag of oats. The bird wanted the oats, but he was scared of the bear. The bird thought and thought. He had an idea. The bald bird flew to the bear and said, "I can help you with your fur if you give me some oats." The bear realized his fur was messy and said yes. The bird cleaned the bear's fur, and the bear gave the bird some oats. The bird was happy, and they both became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to go to the park to play. He liked to swing, slide, and run around with his friends. One day, Tim saw a big blackboard at the park. It was very dirty. Tim wanted to play with the blackboard, but it was too dirty. He needed to clean it first. Tim asked his friends to help him. They all worked together to clean the blackboard. They used water and a cloth to make it nice and clean. When the blackboard was clean, Tim and his friends drew pictures on it. They drew the sun, trees, and their favorite animals. They had so much fun playing with the blackboard at the park. They decided to clean the blackboard every time they went to the park, so they could always draw and have fun together.
