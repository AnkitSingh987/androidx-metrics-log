Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Window

Lily was a little girl who liked to dream. Every evening, she would lie in her bed and imagine she was a princess, a fairy, or a superhero. She would dream of flying, dancing, and having fun with her friends. One evening, she saw a beautiful dress in a shop window. It was pink and sparkly, with a big bow and a long skirt. Lily wanted the dress very much. She thought it would be perfect for her dreams. She asked her mom if she could buy the dress. Her mom looked at the price tag and shook her head. "No, Lily, we can't buy that dress. It's too expensive. We don't have enough money for it." Lily felt sad and angry. She didn't understand why she couldn't have the dress. She thought it was unfair. She said to her mom, "But I want it, Mom! I need it for my dreams! Please, please, please!" Her mom hugged her and said, "I'm sorry, Lily, but we can't. Maybe you can find another dress that you like. Or maybe you can make one with your old clothes and some glitter. You have a lot of imagination, Lily. You don't need an expensive dress to dream." Lily thought about what her mom said. She realized that her mom was right. She didn't need an expensive dress to dream. She could dream with anything. She smiled and said, "OK, Mom. I love you. Thank you for being my mom." Her mom smiled and said, "I love you too, Lily. Thank you for being my daughter." They went home and had a nice dinner. Then Lily went to bed and dreamed of being a princess, a fairy, and a superhero. She was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a fast dog named Spot. Spot liked to run and play in the park. One sunny day, Spot went to the park to have fun. In the park, Spot saw a new person. The person was nice and smiled at Spot. Spot wanted to meet the person, so he ran fast towards them. "Hi, I am Spot!" said the dog. The person laughed and said, "Hello, Spot! I am Tom." Spot and Tom played together all day. They ran fast, played catch, and laughed a lot. Spot was happy to meet a new friend. From that day on, Spot and Tom were best friends and always had fun together.

One day, a small cat named Kitty went outside to play. She saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. As she climbed, she saw a square box on a branch. Kitty was very alert and curious. Kitty said to herself, "What is inside the box?" She opened the box and found a small bird. The bird said, "Hello, Kitty! I was stuck in this box. Thank you for saving me!" Kitty and the bird became good friends. They played together every day. One day, as they played, the bird started to grow bigger and bigger. Kitty was surprised! The bird was not a bird at all, but a magic dragon. From that day on, Kitty and the dragon had many fun adventures together.

One day, a little blue bird was flying in the sky. The bird had a whistle in its beak. It liked to play with the whistle and make fun sounds. The bird was happy and loved to fly. As the bird flew, it noticed a small boy on the ground. The boy looked sad. The bird wanted to make the boy happy. So, the bird flew down to the boy and showed him the whistle. The boy took the whistle and blew into it. The whistle made a funny sound. The boy started to laugh and feel happy. The blue bird felt happy too. They played together and had a fun day.

Timmy was a shy little boy. He was playing with some paint at his friend's house. His friend's mother was fixing them something to drink. Suddenly, Timmy noticed that the paint was all gone. He started to feel very sad. He walked up to his friend's mother and said in a soft voice, "Excuse me, can I have some more paint please?" His friend's mother smiled at him. She said, "Well, what if you stop drinking something else instead?" Timmy looked confused. What else was he supposed to drink? He wasn't sure what she meant. His friend's mother continued, "If you promise to try something besides the drink I am making, I will give you more paint." Timmy couldn't believe his ears. He had heard about water, milk, juice, and even lemonade. He was excited to try them all. Timmy happily agreed, and she rewarded him with a brand new set of paint. The moral of this story is that if you're willing to try something new, you might get rewarded in unexpected ways.
