Category: Intermediate
Title: Fin the Fish and the Clock

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim all day long. He lived in a big pond with his family. The water was cold, but Fin liked it that way. He would swim fast and jump high, making big splashes. One day, while swimming, Fin found a clock. The clock was round and had numbers on it. It was a special clock because it could work under water. Fin thought the clock was fun to play with. He would swim around the clock and watch the hands move. Fin showed the clock to his friends. They all thought it was very cool. They took turns swimming around the clock and watching the hands move. The cold water made them swim faster and faster. They played with the clock all day long, and they were very happy.

One day, a little girl named Amy saw a pretty box. The box was attractive with many colors. She wanted to see what was inside the box. She asked her mom, "Can I open the box, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful. We want to keep the things inside safe." Amy opened the box slowly. Inside the box, there were many toys. She saw a toy cat, a toy dog, and a toy rabbit. She was very happy. Amy played with the toys all day. At night, her mom and dad watched her play. They were proud of her for being careful with the toys. They clapped their hands and said, "Good job, Amy!" Amy felt happy and safe with her new toys and her family.

Once upon a time, there was a little gray bunny. This bunny loved to draw. One day, he wanted to create a big picture. He found a long, white paper and many colors. He was so happy. As he drew, he made a mistake. He needed an eraser. He looked all around but could not find one. Then, a tiny mouse came by. The mouse had a small eraser. The bunny asked, "Can I use your eraser?" The mouse said, "Yes, you can." The bunny used the eraser to fix his picture. But something unexpected happened. The eraser was magic! When he used it, the picture came to life. The bunny and the mouse were so surprised. They became best friends and drew many more magic pictures together.

One day, a boy named Tim had a fancy bike. It had big pedals and pretty colors. Tim loved to ride his bike in the park. One day, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue liked to joke and play. They became friends. Sue wanted to ride Tim's fancy bike, but Tim said no. Sue was sad. She wanted to ride the bike too. Tim saw that Sue was sad, so he had an idea. They could share the bike and ride together. Tim let Sue use the pedals and he held on. They had fun riding the bike together. Tim learned that sharing can make things more fun. Sue and Tim laughed and joked as they rode the fancy bike in the park.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a big tree. Tim was ready to climb the tree. He wanted to see what was at the top. So, Tim started to climb the tree. He climbed higher and higher. At the top of the tree, Tim saw a raven. The raven said, "Hi, Tim! What are you doing up here?" Tim said, "I wanted to see what was at the top of the tree." The raven said, "You found me! I have a surprise for you." The raven gave Tim a big, shiny key. Tim was surprised. He did not know what the key was for. The raven told him, "There is a secret door at the bottom of the tree. Use the key to open it." Tim climbed down the tree and found the secret door. Tim used the key to open the secret door. Inside, he found a room full of toys and games. Tim was so happy! He played with the toys and games all day. The raven watched Tim play and they became good friends. And that was the best day ever for Tim and the raven.
