Category: Intermediate
Title: The Man with White Hat and the Girl

Once upon a time there was a man with a white hat. He was walking down the street when he saw a little girl. He wanted to make her smile, so he gave her a wink. The girl was so happy that she jumped up and down with joy. The girl then went to tell her mom what happened. Her mom was so excited that she gave the man a big hug. The man felt a bit embarrassed, but he still smiled. The girl then ran back to the man and gave him a big hug. She ran away, still smiling, and the man waved goodbye. It was a nice moment, and the man was happy. He was glad that he made the little girl smile.

Ben and Lily were playing with their toys in the living room. They had cars, dolls, blocks and books. They were having fun and sharing nicely. But then Ben saw a big red car that Lily had. He wanted it very much. He tried to grab it from Lily, but she held on tight. "No, Ben, this is my car!" Lily said. "You have your own car!" "But I like this car more!" Ben said. He pulled harder and harder, and Lily cried louder and louder. Mom heard the noise and came to see what was wrong. She saw Ben and Lily fighting over the car. She was not happy. She took the car away from both of them. "Ben, Lily, this is not how we play. You have to ask nicely if you want to borrow something. And you have to respect each other's things. Fighting is not nice. It hurts your feelings and your bodies. Now you both have to say sorry and hug." Ben and Lily felt bad. They knew Mom was right. They said sorry and hugged each other. Mom smiled and gave them a kiss. "Good. Now you can play again. But remember to be kind and gentle. And in a few minutes, we will have lunch. I made your favorite soup." Ben and Lily nodded. They were hungry. Their stomachs were making noises. They returned to their toys and played nicely. They waited for Mom to call them for lunch. They hoped the soup was not too hot. They liked to eat it slow and enjoy it. They were happy again.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the beach. He wanted to sail his boat on the water. The sand was warm and clean under his feet. Tim was very happy. He put his boat in the water and watched it sail away. But then, something unexpected happened. A big fish jumped out of the water and took Tim's boat in its mouth! Tim was very surprised. The big fish swam back to Tim and dropped the boat on the sand. The boat was still clean! Tim said "Thank you, fish!" The fish smiled and swam away. Tim learned that even big fish can be nice friends.

Mia was skipping along the sidewalk. The sun was shining and she was filled with joy. She saw the grassy side of the street and she was very curious, wanting to explore what was there. She had never been down that way before. Suddenly, Mia was stopped by her mother. "Where do you think you're going?" her mother asked. Mia pointed in the direction she wanted to go and said, "There." Mia's mother shook her head. "That's too dangerous," she said. "You have to stay on the sidewalk." Mia was disappointed. She really wanted to explore the other side of the street. So, she tried to convince her mother to let her go. "Please, Mommy. Can I just skip around the corner and back?" Mia's mother thought for a moment and then said, "Alright, as long as you stick to the sidewalk and promise to come right back, you can have five minutes to explore." Mia smiled happily and skipped away. She explored the grassy side of the street, answering her curious mind with each step. At the end of five minutes, Mia raced back to her mother. They hugged, and Mia laughed as she told her mother all about the things that she had seen.
