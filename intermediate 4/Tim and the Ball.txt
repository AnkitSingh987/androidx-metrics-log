Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Ball

One day, a small boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw some junk on the ground and wanted to play with it. "No, Tim," said his mom, "that junk is dirty. Let's play with your ball instead." Tim and his mom played with the ball. They would throw it and try to catch it. Then, Tim had an idea. He wanted to shoot the ball into the air. "Mom, can I shoot the ball high up?" he asked. His mom said, "Yes, but be careful not to lose it." Tim threw the ball with all his might, but something unexpected happened. The ball hit the junk and made a big mess. Tim was sad and his mom was not happy. They had to clean up the mess together. As they picked up the junk, Tim's mom said, "This is why we don't play with junk, Tim. It can make a big mess." Tim learned a lesson that day. He knew that it was better to play with his toys instead of junk. And he also learned that when you make a mess, you should always help to clean it up.

Once upon a time, there was a little square. The square had a seat on it. The seat was for kids to sit on and play. One sunny day, a boy named Tim came to the square. He saw the seat and wanted to sit on it. Tim asked his friend, Sam, "Can I sit on the seat?" Sam said, "Yes, but be careful not to break it." Tim sat on the seat, but he was too heavy. The seat broke and Tim fell on the ground. Tim was very sorry. Tim cried and said, "I am sorry, I broke the seat." Sam was sad too. They could not play on the seat anymore. The square was not fun anymore. Tim and Sam went home with a sad face.

Once upon a time, there was a little frog. The frog lived near a big pond. The frog liked to jump and play all day. One day, a big bird came to the pond. The bird saw the frog and wanted to be friends. "Hi, frog," said the bird. "Will you come and play with me?" The frog looked at the bird and thought it was a stupid idea. The frog did not want to play with the bird because he thought the bird was too big and scary. But the bird did not give up. The bird tried to show the frog that they could have fun together. They played games and laughed. Soon, the frog realized that the bird was not so stupid after all. The frog and the bird became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a brave girl named Lucy. She was very keen to explore and discover new things. One day, Lucy wanted to go on an adventure. Her mum suggested she visit the modern zoo. Lucy was excited and she jumped at the suggestion. At the zoo, Lucy saw lots of animals. She saw monkeys, giraffes, lions and even a tiger! All of the animals were so beautiful and amazing. Lucy was in awe. But then, Lucy noticed that the tiger was in a very small cage! She asked the zookeeper why the cage was so small. The zookeeper said that some people think the modern zoo cages were too small. Lucy felt very sad. So she decided to suggest something to the zookeeper. She suggested they build bigger and better cages so the animals can have enough space. The zookeeper said he would think about this suggestion. He thanked Lucy for being very brave and thinking of a way to help. In the end, Lucy's suggestion was successful and the zoo had bigger and better cages for the animals. Lucy was very happy!

Tommy likes to zoom his toy car on the floor. He makes loud noises with his mouth as he pretends to drive. He has a red car and a blue car, but he likes the red one more. It is shiny and fast. Yesterday, he went to play with his friend Lily. Lily has a lot of toys, but Tommy only saw one thing. She had a yellow car that was bigger and brighter than his red one. He wanted to zoom it, but Lily said no. She said it was her favorite and she did not want to share. Tommy felt jealous. He thought Lily was mean. He tried to grab the yellow car, but Lily pulled it away. They started to fight and cry. Their moms came and took them apart. They said they had to be nice and take turns. Tommy did not want to take turns. He wanted the yellow car all for himself. He was still jealous and angry. He did not say sorry to Lily. He did not have fun. Later, he went home with his mom. He looked at his red car and his blue car. He felt sad. He missed Lily. He wished he had not fought with her. He realized that his cars were good too. He decided to call Lily and say sorry. He hoped she would still be his friend. He learned that zooming cars was more fun with someone else.
