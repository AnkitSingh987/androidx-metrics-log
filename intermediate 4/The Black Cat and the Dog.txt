Category: Intermediate
Title: The Black Cat and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a black cat. The black cat lived in a big house. The black cat liked to play with a red ball. One day, the black cat saw a cross on the wall. The black cat was scared of the cross. The black cat wanted to hide from the cross. It ran and ran, but the cross was always there. The black cat felt sad, and it did not want to play with the red ball anymore. The black cat's friend, a little dog, saw the black cat was sad. The little dog wanted to help the black cat. It thought and thought. The little dog had an idea. It took the red ball and threw it at the cross. The cross fell down! The black cat was happy again. They both played with the red ball, and the little dog felt proud. The people in the big house saw the little dog help the black cat. They were happy too. They clapped their hands to applaud the little dog. The black cat and the little dog were best friends. They played together every day, and the cross was never scary again.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a little monkey named Momo. Momo loved to read. He would read all day under a big tree. One day, while reading, Momo saw a long vine hanging from the tree. Momo thought it would be fun to swing on the vine. So, he put his book down and grabbed the vine. He swung back and forth, higher and higher. Momo was having so much fun. But then, Momo started to feel uncomfortable. He was swinging too high and got scared. He called for help. His friend, a big elephant named Ellie, heard him. Ellie came to help Momo. She used her long nose to catch Momo and bring him back to the ground. Momo was happy and safe. He thanked Ellie and they both sat under the tree to read together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Abigail. She was 3 years old and full of energy. She just could not sit still! One day, Abigail went walking with her mom and they came across a funny little turtle. Abigail asked her mom, "Mommy, what's that?" "Why, that's a turtle," her mom answered. Abigail was excited and smiled. She asked the turtle, "Hi, Mr. Turtle, can I look at your eye?" The turtle thought it was funny and answered Abigail, "Sure!" Abigail got down on her hands and knees and looked really closely into the turtle's big eye. She said, "Wow, it's so pretty!" The turtle smiled, and Abigail ran off to find even more fun things to do.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat who could speak. The big cat had a friend who was a small dog. One day, they were playing in the park when they saw a rich man with a big hat. The big cat said to the small dog, "Let's go speak to the rich man." The small dog was scared, but he agreed. They walked up to the rich man and the big cat said, "Hello, we are here to talk to you." The rich man looked at them and gave a big wink. He said, "I have a secret. I am not really a rich man. I am a magic man." The big cat and the small dog were very surprised. The magic man then made a big cake appear. They all laughed and ate the big cake together.

Once upon a time in the far away woods, there was a snake who was feeling very bitter. He couldn't understand why he was feeling so bad. He walked around looking for answers. Then, he heard someone strike something. He got scared and tried to find where the sound came from. Suddenly the snake came face to face with a woodcutter. He was standing with an axe in his hands. The woodcutter was about to strike the tree with his axe. The snake was very scared, but he spoke up bravely. He said, "Please don't strike me, I'm just a little snake". The woodcutter smiled and said, "Don't worry, I'm not going to strike you. I was just about to cut the tree to make some firewood." The snake was so relieved and thanked the woodcutter. From that day forward, the snake no longer felt bitter. He was happy to be alive.
