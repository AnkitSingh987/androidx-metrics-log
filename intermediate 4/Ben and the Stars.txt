Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben and the Stars

Ben likes to draw images with his crayons. He draws animals, cars, stars and more. He shows his images to his mom and dad and they say they are very good. One day, Ben wants to draw a big dragon. He thinks dragons are cool and brave. He gets his green and red crayons and starts to draw on a big paper. He makes the dragon's head, body, wings and tail. He adds teeth, eyes and fire. He is very happy with his image. He wants to show it to his mom and dad. He runs to the living room with his paper. He sees his mom and dad sitting on the couch. They are watching a movie. "Mom, dad, look at my image!" Ben says. He holds up his paper. "Wow, Ben, that's a great image!" his mom says. "What is it?" "It's a dragon!" Ben says. "A brave dragon!" His dad smiles. "That's a very brave dragon, Ben. I like how you used the colors and the fire. You are a very good artist." Ben feels proud. He hugs his mom and dad. He says, "Thank you, mom and dad. I love you." They hug him back. They say, "We love you too, Ben. And we love your image."

Once upon a time, there was a tidy room. In the room, there was a big bowl of popcorn. A girl named Lily lived in the room. She loved to eat popcorn and share it with her friends. Lily would supply popcorn to everyone who came to play. One sunny day, Lily and her friends were playing outside. They were having so much fun that they forgot about the popcorn. When they came back to the room, they saw that the popcorn was gone! They looked everywhere, but they could not find it. They were sad and hungry. Then, they heard a funny sound. It was coming from the closet. They opened the door and saw a big surprise. A fluffy bunny was in the closet eating their popcorn! The bunny had a big tummy full of popcorn. Lily and her friends laughed and played with the bunny. They decided to share their popcorn with the bunny from now on. And they all became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a nosy cat named Mint. Mint loved to play with her ball and look out the window. She was always curious about what was happening outside. One sunny day, Mint saw a little girl playing in the park. The little girl looked sad and was crying. Mint wanted to help, so she went outside and walked up to the girl. "Please don't cry," said Mint. The girl looked at Mint and stopped crying. "Will you play with me?" the little girl asked. Mint nodded and they played together all day. They had so much fun and became good friends. From that day on, Mint and the little girl played together every day, and they were both very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was an adventurous boy who loved to play outside. One day, he saw a big structure in the park. It had slides, swings, and a sandbox. Tim was so excited to play on it. Tim ran to the structure and started to climb. He saw a little girl named Sue on the slide. Sue said, "Hi, Tim! Do you want to play with me?" Tim replied, "Yes, let's play together!" They played and laughed all day long. As they played, dark clouds came and it started to rain. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise. It was thunder. They saw lightning strike near the structure. Tim and Sue were scared, but they ran home safely. They promised to play together again on a sunny day.

Once there was a little girl who had bright brown eyes. Every day she would spend time with her grandma, and she felt so happy when she was with her. One day as she was playing, her grandma said to her, "I want to teach you something." She was excited and asked, "What will you teach me, Grandma?" She said, "It's a lesson about learning and judging. You need to learn to be kind and thoughtful and not judge people without getting to know them first." The little girl was confused, so she asked, "But what does that mean?" Her grandma smiled and said, "It means that if you want to make friends, you should get to know them first. Don't be so quick to judge people. Everyone is different and that's ok." The little girl understood and nodded her head. She said, "I will learn to be kind and thoughtful, Grandma." That day, the little girl learned a valuable lesson. She knew that she should always be kind and thoughtful and not judge people, no matter how they look or how different they are from her.
