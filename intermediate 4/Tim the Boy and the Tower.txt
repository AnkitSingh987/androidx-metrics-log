Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Tower

One day, a kind boy named Tim wanted to build a big tower. He had many blocks to use. Tim started to build the tower in his room. He was very happy. As Tim was building, his little sister, Sue, came in. Sue wanted to play too. "Can I build with you?" she asked. Tim said, "No, I want to build alone." Sue was sad and left the room. Tim kept building his tower. It made a lot of noise. Sue came back into the room. She was mad. She pushed the tower down. The tower fell and broke. Tim was very sad. The tower was gone, and he could not build it again.

Tommy and Jake were two best friends. They both loved to play together. One day, they got into a silly fight. "I want the yellow cushion!" shouted Tommy. "No, it's mine!" yelled Jake. Tommy became very angry and his eyes narrowed. Jake stuck out his tongue and laughed. He thought it was really funny. The two boys started to wrestle. They rolled around and tussled until they knocked over some lamps. "Stop it, you two!" said Mommy. "You can share the cushion." They both looked embarrassed and stopped fighting. They shared the cushion, laughed and hugged each other. It was funny and they had fun!

One day, a curious cat named Sam went for a walk. He saw a shiny thing on the ground. It was a laser. Sam did not know what it was. He wanted to play with it. So, he used his paw to grab the laser. Sam played with the laser all day. He chased the red dot around the park. He was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The laser stopped working. Sam was sad because he could not play anymore. Just when Sam thought he could not play with the laser again, a little girl came up to him. She had a new laser in her hand. She turned it on, and the red dot came back! Sam was so excited. The little girl and Sam played together all day. The curious cat and the girl became best friends, and they had many more fun days playing with the laser.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy airport. He loved to play with his toy airport every day. One day, Tim thought he was too big for his toy airport. He decided to sell it. Tim took his toy airport to the park. He tried to sell it to his friends. His friends said, "No, Tim. We don't want your toy airport. It is stupid." Tim felt sad, but he did not give up. He went to his neighbor's house to try to sell it there. At the neighbor's house, Tim met a little girl named Sue. Sue loved the toy airport. She said, "I want to buy your toy airport, Tim!" Tim was happy. He sold his toy airport to Sue. They became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time there was a prince. He was very careless and didn't think that anything could scare him. He went walking in the woods on his own. Suddenly, he heard a loud noise and it made him jump. He was scared. He ran back home as fast as he could. When he got back home his mom was very worried. She asked why he didn't take a friend with him. The prince said he had been careless and thought he was brave enough to go alone. His mom told him to always be careful because you never know what could scare you. The prince listened to his mom and he never went for a walk by himself again. He was careful whenever he went into the woods and he always took a friend with him. From then on, he was never scared again.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy cat named Tim. Tim loved to eat tuna. One day, Tim saw a big can of tuna on the table. He wanted to eat it all by himself. Tim jumped on the table to get the tuna, but he was so clumsy that he fell down. He did not quit. He tried again and again, but he kept falling. Tim was sad because he could not get the tuna. Then, Tim's friend, a little dog named Sam, came to help. Sam pushed a chair near the table for Tim to use. Tim climbed on the chair and finally reached the tuna. Tim and Sam shared the tasty tuna and were very happy. They learned that working together is better than trying alone.
