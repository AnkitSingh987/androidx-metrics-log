Category: Intermediate
Title: Daisy and Lewis and the Dance

Once upon a time, there were two friends - Daisy and Lewis. Daisy loved to dance, and her favourite type of dance was jazz. Before bedtime one evening, Daisy said to Lewis, "Let's dance together!" Lewis replied, "Sure! What type of dance should we do?" "Let's do jazz," replied Daisy enthusiastically. So, Daisy and Lewis danced together in a calm and peaceful way. They moved their arms and legs in perfect harmony as they part away and then back together. As they danced, Daisy said, "I like dancing jazz with you. We make a great team!" Lewis smiled and said, "Dancing with you is fun. I enjoy it every time!" And they kept dancing to the sweet jazz music until it was time for Daisy and Lewis to go to sleep.

Tom and Lily wanted to make a cake for their dad. He was coming home from work soon and they wanted to surprise him. They found a big bowl, some flour, some eggs, some sugar and some milk in the kitchen. They put everything in the bowl and started to stir. "Stir, stir, stir!" Tom said. "We are making a brilliant cake!" "Yes, we are!" Lily said. "Dad will love it!" They stirred and stirred until the batter was smooth and creamy. Then they poured it into a cake pan and put it in the oven. They set the timer for 20 minutes and waited. "Let's make a card for dad too!" Lily said. "We can draw a picture of him in his suit!" "Good idea!" Tom said. "He always wears a suit when he goes to work. He looks very smart!" They found some paper, some crayons and some stickers and made a beautiful card. They wrote "We love you dad!" on it and stuck a big heart on it. They were very proud of their card. The timer beeped and they ran to the oven. They put on some oven mitts and took out the cake. It smelled delicious and looked golden and fluffy. They put it on a plate and let it cool down. "Let's put some icing on it!" Tom said. "And some sprinkles!" "OK!" Lily said. "That will make it even more brilliant!" They found some icing and some sprinkles in the cupboard and decorated the cake. They made swirls and dots and stars and sprinkled them with different colors. The cake looked amazing. They put the card next to the cake and waited for dad to come home. They heard the door open and ran to the living room. They saw dad coming in with a big smile on his face. He was wearing his suit and carrying a briefcase. "Dad, dad, we made you a cake!" Tom and Lily shouted. "And a card! Come and see!" They grabbed his hand and pulled him to the kitchen. They showed him the cake and the card and hugged him. "Wow, this is brilliant!" dad said. "You are the best kids ever! Thank you so much!" He kissed them and hugged them again. He was very happy. He picked up a knife and cut a slice of cake. He put it on a plate and took a bite. He smiled and said, "Mmm, this is delicious!" He took another bite and then he stopped. He looked at the cake and then at the kids. He frowned and said, "What did you put in this cake?" Tom and Lily looked at each other and said, "Flour, eggs, sugar, milk, icing and sprinkles. Why?" Dad coughed and said, "And what else?" Tom and Lily thought for a moment and then they remembered. They had forgotten something. "Oh, and salt!" they said. "Salt?" dad said. "How much salt?" Tom and Lily shrugged and said, "A lot. We thought it was sugar. It looked the same." Dad spat out the cake and ran to the sink. He drank some water and rinsed his mouth. He looked at the kids and said, "This cake is horrible! It's too salty! You ruined it!" He was very angry and very sad. He threw the cake in the trash and the card in the fire. He stormed out of the kitchen and slammed the door. Tom and Lily started to cry. They had tried to make dad happy, but they had made him mad. They felt very bad. They had made a terrible mistake. They wished they had checked the label on the salt. They wished they had made a better cake. They wished they had a better dad.
