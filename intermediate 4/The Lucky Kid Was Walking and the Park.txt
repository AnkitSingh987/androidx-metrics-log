Category: Intermediate
Title: The Lucky Kid Was Walking and the Park

One day, a lucky kid was walking in the park. He saw an old man sitting on the bench teaching young children. The kid was curious and asked the old man what he was teaching them. The old man replied, "I'm teaching them how to play baseball!" The kid smiled and asked if he could join in. The old man said, "Yes, of course!" The old man showed the kid how to hold the bat and how to swing it. He also showed him how to throw the ball. The kid practiced and practiced until he felt like he was getting it right. Finally, the day was over and it was time for the kid to go home. The old man gave him a high five and a pat on the back. He said, "You're a lucky kid. You have some talent for baseball!"

Today it was a gloomy day. Mommy and daddy took the kids outside. They wanted to go to the park and play. But they couldn't find the park. Instead they saw something that made them smile. It was a ladder! The kids were excited to see the ladder because it was big and tall. Mommy and daddy checked the weight of the ladder with a machine. It wasn't too heavy. So mommy and daddy and the kids carried the ladder together. They all put the ladder back in the same spot where they found it. The kids wanted to stay and play, but mommy and daddy said they had to go. So they waved goodbye to the ladder and went home. The family had a great time even though the day was gloomy. They had the chance to be together and help each other by carrying the ladder.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Jack. He had curly brown hair and a white shirt. He was standing in the middle of his garden, feeling happy and fit. One day, he wanted to have an adventure. So, he took a deep breath and started walking. He walked for what seemed like forever, until he reached a huge tree. Jack smiled and touched the trunk of the tree. "Hello oak tree," he said in a tiny voice. Suddenly, a big finger shot out of the tree! It waved to Jack, who smiled in surprise. "Hi!", said the finger. Jack was very excited to meet the finger. He asked it what it was doing there. "I'm looking for my friend, the caterpillar," explained the finger. "We were supposed to go on a bug hunt, but I can't find him. Can you help me?" Jack said he would do his best to help the finger. He searched high and low until he finally found the caterpillar at the end of the garden. The finger thanked Jack and said goodbye. Jack waved goodbye and watched the finger and caterpillar walk away, feeling happy that his adventure had been fit.

One day, a boy named Tim found a dead bug on the ground. He picked it up with his finger and showed it to his mom. "Look, Mom! What is this?" he asked. His mom said, "That is a dead bug, Tim. We should put it outside." Tim took the dead bug outside and put it on a big rock. Then, he heard a tiny voice say, "Thank you for helping me!" Tim looked around and saw a little bug on the rock. The little bug was not dead, it was just playing! The little bug had tricked Tim. Tim laughed and said, "You are a funny bug!" The little bug said, "I like to play tricks. Will you be my friend?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, I will be your friend!" From that day on, Tim and the little bug were the best of friends. They played together every day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big brother named Sam. Tim and Sam loved to play outside in the sun. They liked to run, jump, and laugh together. One day, Tim and Sam found a big bag of black stones. They thought it would be fun to scatter the stones all over the yard. So, they took the stones and threw them everywhere. The yard was full of shiny black stones now. Tim and Sam's mom saw what they did and smiled. She told them that they made the yard look very pretty. Tim and Sam were happy, and they hugged each other. From that day on, they played with their black stones every day, and they always had fun together.
