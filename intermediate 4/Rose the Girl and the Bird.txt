Category: Intermediate
Title: Rose the Girl and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Rose. Rose loved to wave at people. One day, Rose went to the park with her mom. The park was very quiet. Rose saw a fragile bird sitting on a bench. She wanted to say hello to the bird. "Hi, bird!" Rose said, waving her hand. The bird looked at her and waved its wing back. Rose was very happy. She had never seen a bird wave before. Suddenly, the bird started to grow bigger and bigger. Rose and her mom were surprised. The bird was not a bird at all. It was a friendly giant! The giant smiled and waved at them. "Hello, Rose and Mom!" the giant said. They all became friends and had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy motorcycle. It was red and shiny. He loved to play with it every day. One day, Tim saw a girl named Sue. Sue had a modern scooter. It was blue and fast. Tim wanted to be friends with Sue. He said, "Hi! I am Tim. Can I introduce my motorcycle to your scooter?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes! I am Sue. Let's play together." Tim and Sue played with their toys. They had lots of fun. They learned that sharing is good. The moral of the story is that when you share, you make new friends.

One day, Tom went to his dad's work. His dad worked on a big machine that could print. Tom saw a big sack next to the machine. He wanted to know what was inside the sack. Tom's dad said, "Be careful, Tom. Don't touch anything." Tom did not listen. He opened the sack and found many colorful papers. He wanted to print something on the papers. He put a paper in the machine and pressed a button. The machine made a loud noise. Tom got scared and hurt his hand on the machine. Tom's dad heard the noise and came running. He saw Tom's hurt hand and the printed paper. Tom was sad and said sorry to his dad. But when they looked at the printed paper, they saw a beautiful picture of a rainbow. Tom's dad smiled and said, "It's okay, Tom. This is a nice surprise. Let's put the rainbow on the wall."

Once upon a time, there was a big, hard rock in a pretty landscape. The rock was very sad because it wanted to soak in the rain like the plants around it. Every day, it would watch the plants grow and wish it could be like them. One day, a magic bird came to the rock and said, "I can help you soak in the rain like the plants!" The rock was so happy and excited. The bird used its magic to turn the rock into a sponge. The next day, it rained and the sponge soaked up all the water. It felt so good to be like the plants. But then, something unexpected happened. The sponge soaked up so much water that it became too heavy and it rolled down the hill, hitting all the plants on the way. The sponge was sad because it hurt the plants it wanted to be like. In the end, the sponge wished it was a rock again, but the magic bird was gone.

One day, a sleepy cat named Kitty decided to go on a trip. She wanted to find a beautiful rainbow she had heard about from her friends. So, she started to pack her bag with yummy food and her favorite toy. As she walked, she met a little bird named Birdy. Birdy asked, "Where are you going, Kitty?" Kitty replied, "I am going to find the rainbow." Birdy said, "I want to go too!" So, they went together to find the rainbow. They walked and walked until they were very tired. Then, they saw the rainbow! It was big and bright with many colors. Kitty and Birdy were so happy. They sat under the rainbow, ate their yummy food, and played with Kitty's favorite toy. After a fun day, they went back home, feeling sleepy but happy.
