Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Stairs

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia liked to play with her toys and run up and down the stairs. One day, she saw a new stair in her house. It was a perfect stair, just right for her to sit on. Mia sat on the stair and saw a little cat. The cat came to the stair and said, "Hi, Mia! My name is Kitty. I am new here. Can we play together?" Mia was very happy to meet Kitty. Mia and Kitty played on the stairs all day. They had so much fun together. Mia's mommy came and said, "Mia, it's time for bed." Mia hugged Kitty and said, "Good night, Kitty. I am so happy to meet you. Let's play again tomorrow!" And they both went to bed, excited to play again the next day.

One day, a chubby cat named Tom found a shiny coin. Tom was very happy. He wanted to show his friend, Sam the dog. Tom said, "Sam, look at my shiny coin!" Sam looked at the coin and said, "Wow, that's a nice coin!" While they were talking, a bird came and took the shiny coin. Tom and Sam were very sad. They wanted to get the coin back. They ran after the bird. The bird flew to a tree and dropped the coin. Tom and Sam were happy again. They picked up the coin and went home. But when they got home, the shiny coin was gone! They looked everywhere, but they could not find it. Then, they saw their friend, the chubby mouse. The mouse said, "I found this shiny coin. Is it yours?" Tom and Sam were very happy to have their coin back. They thanked the mouse and played together.

Once upon a time, there was a modern princess. She lived in a big, pretty castle. The princess liked to wear pretty dresses and play with her toys. One day, the princess found a big bowl and a jug of water. She wanted to play with them. So, the princess poured the water from the jug into the big bowl. It was fun to watch the water splash and make a little mess. The princess played and poured the water again and again. She was very happy. After playing, she cleaned up the mess. The modern princess had a fun day playing with the water and learned a new word: pour.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a favorite sweater that was very warm and cozy. One day, Lily and her best friend, Tim, went outside to play. As they played, Tim started to act like a big, loud lion. He roared and jumped around. Lily laughed and said, "You're a funny lion, Tim!" Tim laughed too and continued to act like a lion. Lily got cold and put on her warm sweater. She then acted like a quiet, little mouse. Tim, the loud lion, chased Lily, the quiet mouse, all around the yard. They laughed and played until it was time to go inside for dinner.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Mia. She loved to make things with her hands. One day, she found a white strip of paper on the ground. She thought it was pretty, so she picked it up and took it home. Mia showed the white strip to her mom and said, "Look, Mom! I found this white strip outside. Can I make something with it?" Her mom smiled and said, "Of course, Mia! What would you like to make?" Mia thought for a moment, and then she said, "I want to make a pretty bracelet for my friend, Lily." So, Mia and her mom worked together to make a beautiful bracelet with the white strip. When they were done, Mia was very proud of her creation. The next day, Mia gave the bracelet to Lily at school. Lily loved the bracelet and said, "Thank you, Mia! This is so pretty!" Mia felt happy that she could make her friend smile with her gift. The moral of the story is that you can make beautiful things and bring happiness to others with even the simplest of items, like a white strip of paper.
