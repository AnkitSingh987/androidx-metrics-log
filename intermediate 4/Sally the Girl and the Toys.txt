Category: Intermediate
Title: Sally the Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. She had a toy bear named Ted. Sally and Ted were best friends. They played together every day. One day, Sally's mom said it was time to clean up. Sally had to separate her toys. She put the big toys in one box and the small toys in another. Ted was a big toy, so he went in the big toy box. But when Sally put Ted in the box, she felt miserable. She missed her friend. Sally's mom saw her sad face and knew what to do. She tied a pretty ribbon around Sally's waist. The ribbon had a little pocket, just the right size for Ted. Now, Sally could carry Ted with her all the time. They were both very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a gray sailor. He had a big boat. One day, he went out to the sea. The sky was blue, and the sun was shining. As the sailor sailed, he saw a little bird. The bird said, "Hello, sailor! It will rain soon. You should go back home." The sailor looked at the sky and saw gray clouds. He thought the bird was right. The sailor turned his boat around and went back home. It started to rain, but he was safe. The sailor thanked the little bird. They became good friends and played together when it was sunny.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to go on trips with her family. One day, they went to the park for a perfect day of fun. Sue was so happy to play outside. At the park, Sue saw a big tree. She wanted to climb it. So, she put her hands on the tree and began to rub the bark. As she rubbed the bark, she felt how rough it was. She thought it was fun to touch the tree. While rubbing the tree, Sue tripped on a root. She fell down and started to cry. Her mom came running over and asked, "Sue, are you okay?" Sue said, "Yes, Mommy, I just tripped." Her mom hugged her and helped her up. They went back to playing and had a perfect day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Mimi. Mimi loved to play and share her toys with her friends. They all had a lot of fun together. One day, Mimi found a magic ball that had power. She thought it would be fun to share the power with her friends, so they could all play together. They all took turns using the power to jump high and run fast. But soon, Mimi and her friends started to fight over the power. They all wanted to use it at the same time. Mimi tried to make them share, but they didn't listen. In the end, they were all very sad and stopped playing together.

Once upon a time, there was a big shark. The shark lived in the deep blue sea. He liked to swim high and low, and he was very fast. One day, the shark saw a little fish. The little fish was very pretty, but the shark was hungry. The little fish saw the shark and was scared. The little fish tried to swim away very fast. The shark wanted to eat the little fish. The little fish swam high and low, but the shark was faster. The little fish thought of his mom and dad. They always remind him to be careful. The little fish tried to hide, but the shark found him. The little fish was very sad. The shark was very hungry. In the end, the shark ate the little fish. The sea was quiet again, but the little fish was gone.

Little Henry had been at school all morning, and he was keen to get home and start playing. He was so excited when he opened the door and saw his mom had organised a tea party for him and his friends. His mom had decorated the room with colourful balloons, streamers and paper decorations. Little Henry started to help out, but he became very bossy. He started telling everyone what decorations to put up. He couldn’t help himself, but his friends didn’t like it. They said he was too bossy and stopped helping him. Little Henry got cross and started shouting at them. He said, “If you don’t help decorate, I won’t give you any tea!” Little Henry’s friends all ran away and he was left alone to finish the decorations. He tried his best, but it didn’t look like it did earlier. Little Henry had ruined the tea party and was left sad and alone.
