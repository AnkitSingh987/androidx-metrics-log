Category: Intermediate
Title: Zip the Popular Dog and the Cat

One day, a popular dog named Zip saw an arrow on the ground. Zip picked it up with his mouth and ran to his friend, a cat named Sam. "Look, Sam! I found an arrow!" said Zip. Sam looked at the arrow and said, "Wow, Zip! That's a cool arrow. Let's play with it!" They took turns throwing the arrow in the air and running to catch it. They were having so much fun. Suddenly, the arrow started to glow! Zip and Sam were very surprised. The arrow then lifted up into the air and flew away. They chased the arrow, but it was too fast. They never saw the arrow again, but they always remembered the magical day they had with it.

Once upon a time, in a lively town, there lived a red car named Zoomy. Zoomy loved to zoom around the town and play with his friends. But one day, Zoomy felt slow and not able to zoom as fast as before. Zoomy was sad and didn't know what was wrong. He asked his friend, a big blue truck, what he could do. The blue truck told Zoomy that he needed oil to feel better and go fast again. Zoomy went to the oil shop and got some oil. He felt better and could zoom fast again. He learned that when you feel bad, it's good to ask for help. Zoomy and his friends played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was a restless dog who liked to play all day. One day, Max went to the park to find new friends. At the park, Max met a big dog named Lucy. Lucy said, "Let's play in the shelter over there!" Max and Lucy ran to the shelter and played a fun game. They played "pinch and run." Max would pinch Lucy's tail, and then they would both run around the shelter. One day, while playing "pinch and run," Max pinched Lucy's tail too hard. Lucy cried, "Ouch! That hurt, Max!" Max felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, Lucy. I didn't mean to pinch so hard." Lucy forgave Max, and they played more gently from then on. They learned to be careful with their games and became best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a playful cat named Tom. Tom liked to part the curtains and look outside. He saw many things and loved to watch the birds. One day, Tom saw a big chimney on a house. He was curious and wanted to see what was inside. So, he jumped up and climbed on the roof. When he looked down the chimney, he saw a warm fire. Tom decided to go back to his house and tell his friends. They all came to see the chimney too. They played together and had a fun day. And every time they saw a chimney, they remembered their playful adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a bird who had a nest. The bird was troubled because a big cat was near her nest. The cat wanted to eat her eggs. One day, the bird had an idea. She wanted to perform a dance to make the cat go away. The bird danced and sang a happy song. The cat watched and started to dance too. The cat liked the dance so much that it forgot about the eggs. The cat and the bird became friends. They danced together every day, and the nest was safe. The bird was not troubled anymore.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the store with her mom. They wanted to buy a toy for Lucy's friend, Sam. Sam's birthday party was tomorrow, and Lucy wanted to be thoughtful and get him a nice gift. At the store, Lucy found a funny toy that could joke. She thought Sam would love it. Lucy asked her mom, "Can we get this for Sam?" Her mom looked at the price and said, "Yes, we can buy it." At the party, Lucy gave the gift to Sam. He was so happy and excited. But when he opened it, the toy started to joke and laugh. Everyone was surprised! The toy was not just a toy, it was a magical toy that could talk. Sam and Lucy laughed and played with the magical toy all day.
