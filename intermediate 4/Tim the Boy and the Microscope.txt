Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Microscope

One day, a boy named Tim found a toy. It was a small microscope. He looked through it and saw tiny things. Tim was very happy with his new toy. His friend, Sam, saw the microscope and felt envious. He wanted to play with it too. Sam asked Tim, "Can I please play with your microscope?" Tim said, "Sure, let's pass it back and forth so we can both play." As they passed the microscope, they saw a little bug. They looked through the microscope and saw that the bug was wearing a tiny hat. It was very surprising! They never knew that bugs wore hats. The boys laughed and played with the microscope all day. They were happy to share and have fun together. And they never forgot the day they found the little bug with the tiny hat.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, blue bag. He liked to put all his toys in the bag. One day, Tim felt miserable because it was raining outside. He wanted to play with his friends, but they couldn't play in the rain. Tim had an idea. He would put on his rain boots, raincoat, and hat. Then, he would put his toys in his big, blue bag. He went outside and found a big puddle. Tim jumped in the puddle and splashed water everywhere. His big, blue bag got wet, but he didn't care. Soon, Tim's friends saw him playing in the puddle. They put on their rain boots, raincoats, and hats too. They all played together in the puddle, laughing and splashing. Tim was not miserable anymore. He was very happy that he could play with his friends, even in the rain.

Once there was a girl named Emily. She was so small and had a soft body. One day, Emily went for a walk and wanted to go to the park. But her mom said no and Emily insisted. So her mom said she could go for a little while. Emily was so excited and she ran outside. She ran and ran until she didn't notice anything but the trees in the park. Soon, the sun was setting and Emily knew it was late. But she wanted to keep playing so she insisted. Emily's mom came looking for her, but Emily was gone. It was too late to find her as the night was dark and the park was too big. Emily kept insisting that she wanted to stay and play, but no one was there to hear her. The next day, Emily's mom called the police. They searched the park, but they could not find Emily. Days turned into weeks, and weeks turned into months. Everyone was devastated, but Emily was never found. She insisted on staying at the park, but it was a decision that changed her life.

One day, a chubby cat named Sam went for a walk. Sam was black and had big, round eyes. He liked to play with his friends in the park. As he walked, he saw a little girl sitting on a bench. The girl looked sad. Sam walked up to the girl and asked, "Why are you sad?" The girl looked at Sam and said, "I lost my toy. I have looked everywhere, but I cannot find it." Sam wanted to help the girl, so he said, "Let's pray together. Maybe it will help you find your toy." They closed their eyes and prayed. When they opened their eyes, they saw a big bird flying towards them. The bird had the girl's toy in its beak! The bird dropped the toy in front of the girl. She was so happy and hugged Sam. They both said, "Thank you, bird!" From that day on, Sam and the girl became best friends. They played together every day and always remembered to pray when they needed help. And the bird would sometimes join them, too.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a strong boy who loved to eat food. One day, Tim was in the kitchen with his mom. His mom was making a big pot of soup. Tim wanted to help, so he decided to add some food to the pot. Tim added carrots, peas, and corn to the soup. He was very happy to help his mom. But then, something unexpected happened. Tim saw a big, green frog jump into the pot of soup! Tim was surprised and didn't know what to do. The frog was swimming in the soup, and Tim thought it was funny. Tim's mom saw the frog and laughed. She picked up the frog and put it back outside. Tim and his mom stirred the soup and took out the frog's food. They laughed and talked about the funny frog. In the end, they had a great meal and Tim felt even stronger.
