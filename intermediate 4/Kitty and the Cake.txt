Category: Intermediate
Title: Kitty and the Cake

Once upon a time, a famous cat named Kitty loved dessert. She lived in a big, pretty house. One day, Kitty saw a yummy cake on the table. She wanted to eat it all. Kitty's friend, Doggy, came to visit. "Can I have some cake too?" asked Doggy. Kitty did not want to share. She wanted the cake all for herself. Kitty said, "No, it's mine!" Doggy felt sad and left the house. Later, Kitty heard a noise. She saw a big, scary monster! The monster wanted the cake too. Kitty was scared and did not know what to do. She thought about her friend Doggy. Then, Kitty had an idea. Kitty called Doggy and said, "I'm sorry. Please come back and help me. We can share the cake." Doggy came back and saw the monster. Together, they scared the monster away. Kitty and Doggy shared the cake and were happy. They learned that sharing is good. From that day on, they always shared their dessert.

One day, Mummy and two kids went to the sea. They all were very excited. The kids played for hours at the beach. They ran, built sandcastles and dug in the sand. But the highlight of the day was when their Mummy grabbed their hands and spun them around. She spun and spun and spun. The kids laughed and laughed. The sky and the sea spun around them. It was so much fun! At the end, they all were so tired, but they were still excited, and the kids asked Mummy to spin them again. She smiled and spun them around to the sound of the waves and their laughter. It was the best day ever at the sea.

Once upon a time, there was a big yellow taxi. The taxi was very fast and drove people around all day. The driver of the taxi was a bossy man. He told people where to sit and when to get out. He liked his job, but sometimes he got tired. One day, the taxi driver picked up a funny little girl. She liked to poke things with her finger. She poked the seat, the window, and even the driver's arm. The driver did not like this, but the girl just laughed and kept poking. Then, something unexpected happened. The girl poked the taxi's horn, and it made a very loud noise. The noise was so loud that it scared a big bird flying above them. The bird dropped a shiny coin from the sky. The coin landed right in the taxi driver's hand. The driver was so happy that he forgot to be bossy. He let the girl poke the horn one more time, and they both laughed as they drove away.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Jake. Jake was very creative, and he loved to build things with his blocks. One day, Jake asked his mom if he could build something special. His mom said yes, and she put out a big box full of blocks for him. Jake got to work. He listened to his mom as she gave him tips and tricks on how to build his special creation. Then suddenly, he shouted, "I'm done!" His mom looked at his creation and said, "That is amazing! Now, please put it back into the box so you can go outside and play." Jake said, "OK. Can I please make something else when I'm done?" His mom smiled, and said, "Of course, Jake. Use your imagination and get creative!" And so Jake went to work on his next amazing creation.

Tom and Lily were playing in the park with their ball. They saw a big fire in the sky. It was a giant sun. The sun was very hot and bright. It made them feel warm and happy. "Look, Lily, the sun is burning!" Tom said. "Yes, Tom, the sun is burning. It is very big and yellow. I like the sun," Lily said. They threw their ball to each other and laughed. They did not see the man with the matches. He was hiding behind a tree. He was very bad and mean. He wanted to make trouble. He lit a match and threw it at the ball. The ball caught fire. It made a loud noise and a lot of smoke. Tom and Lily were scared and sad. They ran away from the ball. They cried for help. "Help, help, our ball is burning!" they shouted. A woman heard them and came to see. She saw the ball and the fire. She saw the man with the matches. She was very angry and brave. She took a bucket of water and threw it at the ball. The fire went out. The ball was black and wet. It was ruined. "Who did this?" the woman asked. Tom and Lily pointed at the man with the matches. He was still behind the tree. He was ashamed and afraid. He knew he did a bad thing. He tried to run away, but the woman caught him. She called the police. The police came and took him away. The woman hugged Tom and Lily. She was very kind and sorry. She gave them a new ball. It was red and shiny. It was not burning. "Thank you, thank you, you are very nice," Tom and Lily said. "You are very welcome, you are very brave," the woman said. They smiled and played with their new ball. They did not see the giant sun anymore. It was behind a cloud. It was still burning, but it did not hurt them. It was their friend.
