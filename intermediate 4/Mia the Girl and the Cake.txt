Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She loved to taste new foods. One day, her mom made a big cake. Mia was very excited to taste it. She knew it would be yummy. Mia's mom put the cake on the table. She went to get a knife to cut it. But when she was away, a small needle fell into the cake. Mia did not see it. Mia's mom came back and cut the cake. Mia took a big bite. She felt something hard. It was the needle! Mia's mom was surprised. She took the needle out of the cake. They were both happy that Mia did not get hurt. The cake was not successful, but they laughed and had fun anyway. They knew they could try again another day.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly bear who lived in a tall church. He liked to swing on the bell rope and watch the sun set in the sky. One day, while swinging on the bell rope, the bear heard a loud noise. When he looked around to find out what it was, he saw a small pussycat disappear into the shadows. The bear jumped down from the high church and started to follow the little pussycat. He called out, "Wait! Where are you going?" The pussycat stopped for a moment and replied, "I am going on a grand adventure. I want to explore the world and find new friends." The bear was so excited at the thought of an adventure and said, "I want to come too! Please let me join you." The pussycat smiled and nodded his head. He said, "I'd be happy to have you. Let's go!" So the bear and pussycat went on a grand adventure, discovering new places and making new friends all around the world. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to play outside. The sky was dark and a storm was coming. Lily saw a fragile bird on the ground. It was hurt and could not fly. Lily wanted to help the bird. Lily said, "Don't worry, little bird. I will help you." She picked up the bird very gently and took it to her house. Inside, she made a soft bed for the bird to rest. Lily knew the bird needed food, so she went to the kitchen. In the kitchen, Lily found some bread. She broke it into small pieces and added water to make it soft. She took the food to the bird and said, "Here you go, little bird. This will help you get better." The bird ate the food and started to feel better. The storm outside was very loud, but Lily and the bird were safe and warm inside the house.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was excited to help her mom in the kitchen. They were making a yummy cake. Lily wanted to help mix the cake batter. Lily's mom gave her a big spoon. Lily used the spoon to mix the cake batter. She mixed and mixed until it was all done. They put the cake in the oven to bake. When the cake was baking, Lily helped her mom wash the spoon. They washed and dried it together. The cake was soon ready, and they all had a fun time eating it.

Once upon a time, there was a fine cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with a ball of thread. One day, Tom saw the thread on the floor and began to stare at it. Tom wanted to play with the thread, so he ran to it and started to roll it around the room. The thread went up and down, and Tom chased it all day long. At the end of the day, Tom was tired but happy. He had so much fun playing with the thread. Tom curled up next to the ball of thread and went to sleep, dreaming of more fun days to come.

Once upon a time, there was a big park. In the park, there was a pretty fountain. The fountain had water that went up and down. Kids liked to play near the fountain on sunny days. One day, a man came to the park. He wanted to display something brilliant. He had pretty lights in many colors. He put the lights around the fountain. The lights made the water look like it had colors too. When it was dark, the man turned on the lights. The kids and their families came to see the brilliant fountain. They clapped and laughed. They loved the pretty colors in the water. The park was a happy place for everyone.
