Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Grass and the Sun

One day, a young patch of grass appeared in a meadow. It was small and green and it shone brightly in the sun. There was an old tree nearby and nearby the patch, there were two small animals playing. The animals were young rabbits. They were hopping around and having lots of fun. Every morning they would come to the patch and play together. The young patch of grass kept growing taller and wider. One day, a bee appeared and sat on a blade of grass. Its wings were black and yellow and it was buzzing around the patch. The rabbits were very curious. They stayed at the edge of the patch and watched the bee fly around. They were happy to see this young patch of grass appear in their meadow.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Mia found a magic olive in her garden. She was very eager to show it to her mom. Mia said, "Look, Mom! I found a magic olive!" Her mom smiled and said, "Wow, Mia! That is special. We should value this olive and keep it safe." Mia and her mom put the olive in a small box. They took care of it every day. They learned that when they were kind to the olive, it would make them happy. This made Mia and her mom feel good inside. One day, Mia's friend, Tim, came over to play. Mia showed Tim the magic olive. Tim wanted to take the olive home, but Mia said, "No, Tim. We must value the olive and keep it safe here." Tim was sad, but he understood. He learned the importance of valuing things and being kind. Mia and her mom were happy to share their magic olive with Tim, and they all played together in the garden. The moral of the story is to value and take care of the special things in our lives, and to be kind to others.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to solve a problem. The problem was that the rain was falling outside, and Tim wanted to play. He thought very hard about how to have fun even with the rain. Tim had a great idea! He put on his boots and raincoat. He went outside and jumped in the puddles. The rain felt good on his face. He laughed and splashed in the water. After a while, the rain stopped. Tim saw a big rainbow in the sky. He was happy that he found a way to play in the rain. Tim learned that even when there is a problem, he can solve it and still have fun.

One day, a little girl named Lucy was feeling impatient. She wanted to play with her friends, but it was too cold outside. Her mom had an idea. "Let's introduce you to something new," she said. "We will make hot cocoa!" Lucy was excited. She helped her mom pour milk into a big cup. They put the cup in the microwave and waited for it to get hot. Then, they added cocoa and stirred it all up. Lucy thought it was the best thing she had ever tasted. Just then, Lucy's brother, Tom, came into the kitchen. He saw the cocoa and wanted some too. But there was only one cup. Lucy and Tom both wanted it. "Share the cocoa," said their mom. So, they took turns drinking the cocoa and soon they were both happy and warm.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy little house. In this house lived a boy named Tim and his mom. One day, Tim found a pipe in the yard. He picked it up and ran inside to show his mom. "Look, Mom! I found a pipe!" Tim said. His mom smiled and said, "That's great, Tim! Let's put it away in the shed." On the way to the shed, Tim saw a ball. He had an idea. He put the pipe on the ground and tried to kick the ball through it. The ball went through the pipe and Tim laughed. "Mom, watch this!" Tim shouted. His mom watched as Tim kicked the ball through the pipe again. They both laughed and clapped their hands. From then on, Tim and his mom played with the pipe and ball every day, making their yard even more tidy and fun.
