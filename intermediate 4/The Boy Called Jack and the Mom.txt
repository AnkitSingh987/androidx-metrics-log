Title: The Boy Called Jack and the Mom

Once upon a time, there was a little boy called Jack. He was three years old, and always wanted to try something new. One day, Jack's mom allowed him to try something very unusual. She gave him a big piece of cheese! Jack was so excited to try something new and strange. He asked his Mom, "What is it?" Mom said, "It's cheese!" Jack was amazed and said, "Gosh that looks so yummy!" He took a bite, and couldn't believe how tasty it was! He asked his Mom, "Can I have more?" Mom said, "Yes, you can have more. But only if you promise to try something unusual every day!" Jack agreed, and they both laughed. He had discovered something new and delicious. The End

Once upon a time, in a big valley, there lived a hungry bunny named Ben. Ben was always looking for food to eat. One day, he saw a big carrot across the valley. He hopped and hopped to get to the carrot. As he reached the carrot, he heard a voice. It was a big, scary wolf. The wolf said, "Surrender, little bunny, or I will eat you!" Ben was scared, but he did not want to give up. He thought of a plan to trick the wolf. Ben looked at the wolf and said, "If you are hungry, I can show you where there are more carrots. We can share them." The wolf thought about it and agreed. Ben led the wolf far away from the valley. When they got there, Ben quickly hopped away and hid. The wolf was too busy eating the carrots to notice Ben was gone. Ben went back to the valley, safe and sound. From that day on, Ben and his friends lived happily in the valley, without any fear of the big, scary wolf.

One day, a little boy named Tim went out to play. The sun was shining bright in the sky. Tim was very happy. He saw his friend, a naughty dog named Max. Max loved to run and play with Tim. Tim said to Max, "Promise me you won't run away today." Max barked and wagged his tail. Tim thought that meant Max promised. They played and played all day long. But then, Max saw a big red ball. He forgot his promise and ran after it. Tim tried to catch Max, but he was too fast. Max ran into the street and a car hit him. Tim was very sad. He wished Max had kept his promise.

Once upon a time there was a boy called Barry. He was only three years old and very curious. One day, Barry was looking at the sky and wondered what was up there. Suddenly, he saw a cell slowly rising up on the sky. It was so nice! "Wow!" shouted Barry. He was amazed. Barry watched the cell rise above the clouds. Then, he saw a mummy and a daddy flying on the cell. They waved at him and said "hello". Barry was so happy. It was the nicest thing he had ever seen. He waved back and watched the cell rise up into the sky until it disappeared. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a hero named Tom. Tom was strong and kind. He helped everyone in his town. He was very successful at making people happy. One day, Tom saw a little girl crying. He asked her, "Why are you sad?" The girl said, "My cat is gone. He vanished from our house." Tom wanted to help her find her cat. Tom and the girl walked around the town. They asked people if they saw the cat. Finally, they found the cat in a tree. Tom climbed the tree and got the cat. The girl was so happy. Tom was a hero to her. They went back home, and everyone was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big, black dog named Max. Max loved to play with his friend, a little girl named Lily. They would run and jump all day long. One day, Max found a soft, colorful thing on the floor. He picked it up in his mouth and brought it to Lily. Lily smiled and said, "Max, that's a wrap! Let's play a game with it!" Max wagged his tail and barked happily. Lily held the wrap and told Max, "I will wrap this around you, and then you can try to shake it off!" Max sat still while Lily wrapped the colorful thing around him. Then, Max shook and shook until the wrap fell off. Lily laughed and clapped her hands. They played this fun game all day long, and Max's wagging tail showed his big smile.
