Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Bunny

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was a daughter who was very pretty. Lily had a toy bunny that she loved a lot. One day, her toy bunny broke, and she was very sad. Lily's mom saw her sad face and said, "Don't worry, my sweet daughter. We will replace your toy bunny with a new one." They went to the store to find a new bunny for Lily. At the store, Lily found the most attractive bunny she had ever seen. She was so happy and said, "Mom, this is the one I want!" Her mom bought the new bunny, and Lily hugged it tight. From that day on, Lily and her new bunny were the best of friends.

Tom and Lily were best friends. They liked to play with their toys, sing songs, and hug each other. One day, they found a big box in the living room. It had a shiny bow on it. They wondered what was inside. They looked around. No one was watching. They decided to open the box. They pulled the bow and lifted the lid. Inside, they saw a big, fluffy teddy bear. It had brown fur, a red nose, and two black eyes. It looked very cute. Tom and Lily wanted to play with the teddy bear. They took it out of the box and hugged it. They made it dance and talk. They had a lot of fun. But then, they heard a voice. "Tom, Lily, what are you doing?" It was Mom. She came into the living room and saw the open box and the teddy bear. She looked angry. "Did you open the box?" she asked. "That was a gift for your cousin. He is coming to visit tomorrow. You were not supposed to touch it." Tom and Lily felt guilty. They knew they did something bad. They looked at Mom with their big eyes. They did not know what to say. "Sorry, Mom," they whispered. They put the teddy bear back in the box and closed the lid. They hoped Mom would forgive them. Mom sighed. She was not happy, but she loved Tom and Lily. She knew they were curious and did not mean to ruin the gift. She hugged them and said, "It's okay. I know you are sorry. But next time, please ask before you open something that is not yours. And be careful with other people's things. Do you understand?" Tom and Lily nodded. They understood. They said sorry again and hugged Mom. They promised to be good and not open any more boxes. They learned their lesson. They were still best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little ball named Bounce. Bounce loved to play all day. Bounce was very good at jumping high and far. One day, Bounce met a new friend named Cocoa. Cocoa was a small brown dog. They liked to play together. Bounce would jump and Cocoa would try to catch him. They were very happy. One sunny day, Bounce and Cocoa were playing near a big tree. Bounce jumped very high and got stuck in the tree. Cocoa looked up and said, "Oh no, Bounce! How can I help you?" Bounce said, "Try to jump and catch me, Cocoa!" Cocoa tried and tried, but he could not jump high enough. Then, Cocoa had an idea. He found a big stick and used it to help Bounce get down. Bounce was so happy to be free. They both felt successful. From that day on, Bounce and Cocoa played together every day. They were the best of friends and always helped each other.

Once upon a time, there was a silly bird named Billy. Billy lived on a cliff with his family. One day, Billy saw a pretty stone and wanted to play with it. Billy asked his mom, "Can I play with the stone?" His mom said, "No, Billy. That stone does not belong to us." But Billy did not listen and took the stone with him. Later, Billy played with the stone near the edge of the cliff. He was having fun, but then the stone rolled off the cliff. Billy felt sad and knew he should have listened to his mom. He learned that it is important to listen and not take things that do not belong to you.
