Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Ben and the Box

Lily and Ben were twins who liked to play with their toys. They had many toys, but their favorite was a big red car that they could ride together. They loved to polish the car with a soft cloth and make it shiny. One day, their mom came home with a big box of chocolate. She said it was a gift from their aunt, and they could have some after dinner. Lily and Ben were very happy and thanked their mom. They put the box of chocolate on the table and went back to play with their car. But soon, they felt hungry and wanted some chocolate. They looked at the box and saw that it was still closed. They decided to open it and take a little piece, and then close it again. They thought their mom would not notice. They opened the box and saw many different kinds of chocolate. They looked so yummy and sweet. They each took a piece and put it in their mouths. It was delicious. They wanted more. They took another piece, and another, and another. They did not stop until they ate all the chocolate in the box. They felt very full and sick. They realized they had done a bad thing. They tried to close the box, but it was empty. They heard their mom coming. They were scared. They hid the box under the car and ran to their room. Their mom saw the car and smiled. She wanted to see how shiny it was. She lifted the car and saw the empty box of chocolate. She was very angry. She called Lily and Ben and asked them what they had done. They did not answer. They knew they were in trouble. Their mom was very harsh. She scolded them and said they were naughty and greedy. She said they had ruined the gift and hurt their aunt's feelings. She said they could not play with the car for a week. She said they had to go to bed without dinner. She said they had to say sorry to their aunt. Lily and Ben felt very sad and sorry. They cried and hugged each other. They wished they had not eaten the chocolate. They wished they had listened to their mom. They learned a hard lesson. They said they would never do it again.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play sports. His favorite sport was to kick a light ball. One day, Tim went to the park to play. At the park, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue liked to play sports too. They decided to play together. They kicked the light ball back and forth, laughing and having fun. Then, a man came to the park. He said, "I see you like playing sports. If you want to play here, you must pay." Tim and Sue looked at each other, confused. They didn't know what to do. Tim said, "We don't have any money to pay. Can we still play?" The man thought for a moment, then smiled. "If you promise to be kind and share the park with others, you can play for free." Tim and Sue were happy. They agreed to share the park and be kind to others. They played sports all day, kicking the light ball and making new friends.

Once upon a time, in a small garden, there was a pretty flower. The flower was about to bloom. The sun was up, but it was harsh. The flower felt too hot. A little boy named Tim saw the flower. He had a magnet with him. He said, "Hi, flower! You look hot. I will help you." Tim put the magnet near the flower. The magnet pulled some cool water from a nearby tap. The water made the flower feel better. The flower bloomed and said, "Thank you, Tim!" Tim was happy to help. He learned that being kind is good. The flower and Tim became friends, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was very creative. She loved to play in her bedroom and make things with her toys. One day, she wanted to make a big tower with her blocks. But she had a struggle. The blocks kept falling down. Mia's mommy came into the bedroom and saw Mia's sad face. "What's wrong, Mia?" she asked. Mia told her mommy about the struggle with the blocks. Her mommy said, "Let's try together. We can do it!" They worked and worked, and finally made the big tower. But then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew into the bedroom and knocked the tower down! Mia and her mommy laughed. They learned that it's okay if things don't go as planned. The important thing is to have fun and try again.
