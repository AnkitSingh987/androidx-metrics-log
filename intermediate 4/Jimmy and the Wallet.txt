Category: Intermediate
Title: Jimmy and the Wallet

Jimmy was a young boy. He had a wallet which was very important to him. It was brown with a black stripe down the side. His mommy gave it to him for his birthday. Every day, Jimmy would hug his wallet. He loved it very much. When his friends would come to play, he would put his wallet in a special place so nobody could take it away. One day, Jimmy went to the store with his mommy. He hugged his wallet tight so it didn't get lost. He bought lots of goodies with his pocket money and kept his wallet safe in his pocket. Jimmy never forgot to hug his wallet when he left the store. He always knew it was very important and that it had to be kept safe.

Tina was a great adventurer who loved to explore unknown places. Everywhere she went, she was sure to bring her trusty mug. One day, Tina decided to go to a place she had never been before. She called it the Mysterious Mountain. As soon as she arrived, she was filled with awe and adventure. She wanted to explore everywhere, but she was unsure of what was ahead. She began her journey and within no time, she had a surprise. As she continued on her journey, she heard a voice calling her name. It was a voice that was unfamiliar to her. It was unknown and strange. Tina realized she must find out where the call was coming from, so she started to follow it. She kept following and soon she arrived at a mountain cave. She entered the cave and there in a corner was a beautiful mug. Surprisingly, Tina recognized the mug right away. She had brought it with her on her journey. Tina realized the mug had been calling her name. She was amazed at how her trusty mug had led her on her journey. With that knowledge, she was even more excited for what adventures were still to come.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys. One day, he found a cookie jar on the kitchen table. He knew that cookies had a brilliant taste. So, he wanted to eat one. Tim climbed on a chair to get the jar. As he grabbed it, he heard his mom say, "Be careful, Tim! Don't break the jar!" Tim tried to be careful, but the jar slipped from his hands. It fell on the floor and broke into pieces. Tim felt sad. His mom came and saw the broken jar. She said, "It's okay, Tim. We can clean this up." They worked together to clean the mess. After they were done, Tim's mom gave him a cookie. Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Mom! This cookie has a brilliant taste!"

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play in the garden. They see many plants. Some are big, some are small. Some are green, some are yellow. Lily likes red plants. She sees a red plant in a pot. She runs to it. "Look, Tom, a red plant!" Lily says. "It is so pretty. Can I touch it?" Tom comes to Lily. He sees the red plant. He shakes his head. "No, Lily, do not touch it. It is a bad plant. It can hurt you. It has sharp things on it. They are called thorns. If you touch them, they will make you cry." Lily is sad. She wants to touch the red plant. She thinks it is soft and nice. She does not see the thorns. She reaches out her hand. "Ow!" Lily says. She pulls back her hand. She sees a drop of blood. She feels pain. She starts to cry. Tom hugs Lily. He is sorry for her. He takes her to his mom. His mom is in the kitchen. She sees Lily's hand. She washes it with water. She puts a band-aid on it. She gives Lily a cookie. She tells her to be careful. "Lily, some plants are good, some plants are bad. You have to look before you touch. The red plant is bad. It has thorns. They can hurt you. Next time, ask me or Tom before you touch a plant. OK?" Lily nods. She says sorry to Tom and his mom. She learns a lesson. She does not touch the red plant again. She plays with Tom and other plants. She is happy.
