Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Books

Lily liked to read books. She read books about animals, and books about stars, and books about princesses. She read books every day, before bed and after breakfast. She read books with her mom and dad, and sometimes with her big brother. One day, Lily had to go to a new school. She wore a uniform, like all the other kids. It was blue and white, and had a badge with a lion on it. Lily felt shy. She did not know anyone at the new school. She did not want to leave her books at home. When she got to the school, she saw a big sign that said "Library". Lily felt happy. She loved libraries. She asked her mom if she could go inside. Her mom said yes, but only for a little while. She had to go to her class soon. Lily went inside the library. She saw many books on the shelves. She saw books with pictures, and books with words, and books with both. She saw books that she had read, and books that she had not read. She wanted to read them all. She picked up a book that had a dragon on the cover. She opened it and started to read. She forgot about the new school and the uniform and the shy. She only thought about the dragon and the adventure. "Hi, I'm Sam," a voice said. Lily looked up. She saw a boy with glasses and a smile. He wore the same uniform as her. He had the same book in his hand. "Do you like dragons?" he asked. Lily nodded. She liked dragons. She liked books. She liked Sam. She smiled back. She did not feel shy anymore. She made a new friend.

---

Lila and Ben were at the beach with their mom and dad. They liked to play in the sand and splash in the water. They saw many fish and shells and crabs. They also saw a big octopus. The octopus had eight arms and many suckers. It was hiding under a rock. "Look, an octopus!" Lila said. She wanted to touch it. She was curious. "Be careful, Lila. The octopus might be scared or angry. It might bite you or squirt ink at you." Ben said. He was older and knew more. "But I just want to say hello. Maybe the octopus is lonely. Maybe it wants to be our friend." Lila said. She was brave and kind. She walked slowly to the rock. She said in a soft voice, "Hello, octopus. We are Lila and Ben. We are nice. Can we play with you?" The octopus peeked out from the rock. It saw Lila and Ben. It was shy and nervous. It did not know what to do. Lila reached out her hand. She gently poked the octopus with her finger. She smiled. The octopus felt the poke. It was surprised and curious. It poked back with one of its arms. It smiled too. Lila and Ben laughed. They were happy. They made a new friend. The octopus was happy too. It was polite. It said in a bubbly voice, "Hello, Lila and Ben. I am Ollie. I am nice too. Yes, we can play together." They played for a while. They had fun. They learned more about each other. They were respectful and gentle. Mom and dad watched from a distance. They were proud and relieved. They saw that Lila and Ben were smart and adventurous. They saw that Ollie was friendly and harmless. They saw that everyone was having a good time. They smiled too.

One day, a little dog named Spot went on a walk. He saw a big red door. Spot wanted to see what was inside, so he used his paw to push the door open. He was happy to enter the room. Inside the room, Spot found a big box. He jumped on the box and heard a loud noise. It was a stamp inside the box! Spot took the stamp and put it on the floor. He used his paw to press the stamp on a piece of paper. He made a pretty picture with the stamp. Then, Spot saw a plate of cookies on a table. They smelled yummy! Spot jumped up and took a bite of a cookie. It was so yummy that he ate all the cookies. He was very happy and his tummy was full. Spot left the room and went home to take a nap.
