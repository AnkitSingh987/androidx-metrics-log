Category: Intermediate
Title: The Boat and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a little boat. The boat liked to sail on the water. One day, the boat went out to sail at noon. The sun was high in the sky and it was a nice day. As the boat sailed, the water became rough. The boat went up and down. The boat was not scared. It knew how to sail on rough water. Soon, the water was calm again. The boat was happy it could sail well on rough water. The boat sailed back home, proud of its adventure. And from that day, the little boat always loved to sail at noon.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They wanted to buy a cheap toy for Tim to play with. They found a toy that was a big wall made out of ice. Tim was very happy with his new toy. When they got home, Tim played with his ice wall all day. He put his toy cars on top of the wall and watched them slide down. But then, something unexpected happened. The sun came out and started to melt the ice wall. Tim was very sad to see his new toy melting away. But as the ice wall melted, Tim saw something shiny inside. He waited for the ice to melt more and found a beautiful gold toy car hidden in the ice. Tim was so happy and surprised! The cheap ice wall toy had a special surprise inside. Now, Tim had a new toy car to play with and he loved it even more than the ice wall.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toy missile. One day, Tim saw a pile of filthy rocks near his house. He thought it would be fun to gather the rocks and build a little fort for his missile. Tim called his friend Sue to come over and help him. Sue said, "Yes, let's gather the filthy rocks and build a fort!" They worked together, laughing and having a good time. The fort was almost done, and Tim couldn't wait to put his missile inside. But then, a big wind came and blew the fort down. The missile fell into the pile of filthy rocks and got lost. Tim and Sue tried to find it, but they couldn't. Tim was very sad that his missile was gone. The end.

One day, Anna and her mommy went to the park. Anna loves playing at the park and was so excited to go. As they were walking, Anna asked her mommy “What makes music?” “Well, lots of things can make music,” said her mommy. “Musicians use instruments and their voices to create music.” Anna thought about this for a moment and then asked, “Can we make music too?” “Yes, we can make music,” said her mommy with a smile. “We can mix sounds together to make something creative.” So, that’s what they did. They found lots of things to make noise and make music with – pots and pans, toy drums, and pots and pans. Then they mixed all of the sounds together and created the most magical music anyone had ever heard. Anna and her mommy danced, sang and laughed. It was such a wonderful moment. Anna felt so happy and proud of the music they created together.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went to the park. They played in the peaceful green grass. Tim saw a small stick and had an idea. "Let's play, Spot!" he said. Tim threw the stick and Spot ran to get it. When Spot found the stick, he saw a stitch on the ground. Spot dug a hole and decided to bury the stitch. He thought it was a fun game. Tim called for Spot, "Come back, Spot!" But Spot kept digging. Just then, a little girl named Sue came running over. "Oh no!" she cried. "My stitch is gone!" Tim looked at Spot and saw the hole. "Spot buried it!" he said. Sue was sad, but then they all laughed. They dug up the stitch and played together. The day was not what they expected, but it was still a lot of fun.
