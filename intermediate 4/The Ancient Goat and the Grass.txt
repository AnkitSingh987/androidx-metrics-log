Title: The Ancient Goat and the Grass

Once upon a time, there was an ancient goat. The goat lived on a farm. The goat had many friends. One day, the goat said to his friend, the cow, "I want to finish eating all the grass in the field." The cow said, "That's a big job, but we can help you." So, the goat, the cow, and all their friends started eating the grass. They ate and ate until they were full. But there was still more grass to eat. The next day, they kept eating the grass. They worked together to help the goat finish his big job. Finally, they ate all the grass in the field. The goat was very happy. He said, "Thank you, friends, for helping me finish eating all the grass." The friends were happy too, because they had a fun time working together.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big cake on a table. It looks yummy. They want to eat it. "Who made this cake?" Tom asks. "I don't know. Maybe it is for a party," Mia says. "Can we have some?" Tom says. "I don't think so. It is not ours," Mia says. "But I am hungry. And it smells good," Tom says. He takes a big bite of the cake. Mia sees him and feels bad. She also takes a bite. They eat and eat until the cake is gone. Soon, they hear a voice. It is a lady. She is angry. "Hey, you two! What are you doing? That was my cake for my son's birthday! How could you?" she says. Tom and Mia are scared. They run away. But they feel sick. Their stomachs hurt. They want to cry. They go home and tell their moms what they did. Their moms are not happy. They say they are sorry. They have to go back and say sorry to the lady too. And they have to help her make a new cake. Tom and Mia learn a lesson. They learn that they should not take things that are not theirs. They learn that it is wrong to be greedy. They learn that it is better to share and be kind. They also learn that too much cake is bad for their stomachs. They do not want to eat cake for a long time. They prefer to eat fruits and vegetables. They are good for their health. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a brave little girl called Marina. She was three years old and loved to explore new things. One day, Marina was playing in her bedroom when she suddenly noticed a dangerous ceiling. It had huge cracks, which were spilling bright light into the room. Marina thought it was really beautiful and decided to explore it. But when Marina got closer, she realised how dangerous it could be. "Maybe I should pray," she thought. So, Marina closed her eyes, put her hands together and prayed that she could explore it safely. Marina's mom noticed what was happening and came over to her. She put her arm around Marina and said, "It's very dangerous, sweetheart. We should stay away from the ceiling and find something safer to explore." Marina nodded, but still looked up at the beautiful and dangerous ceiling. She knew she'd have to find a way to explore it, one day.

And do you know what's happening to that ant hill?" Benny shook his head. "No, what's happening?" Mom pointed to the ant hill. "The ant hill is getting bigger and bigger every day. "Just keep watching, it's going to get even bigger than that." A few months later, Benny and Mom went back to the park to look at the ant hill. This time, Benny was miserable.
