Category: Intermediate
Title: Mark and the Park

Once there was a jolly old man called Mark. He was always so kind and friendly. One day he was walking in the park and he noticed a little girl. She was leaning on the rail and looking sad. Mark smiled kindly at her and asked what was wrong. She explained that she was feeling very lonely and had nobody to play with. Mark was filled with joy. He leaned down, held out his hand and asked if she would like to play with him. The little girl smiled back at Mark and nodded her head. The two of them played together all day, running around and laughing. After a while they grew tired and leaned against the rail again and looked out across the park. The little girl thanked Mark for being so jolly and he thanked her for being such good company. Mark said goodbye and he waved as he walked away. That was the day that Mark and the little girl became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little rat. The rat was very attractive. It had a shiny coat and a long tail. The rat lived in a big house with a nice family. The family liked the rat very much. One day, the rat found a big cake in the kitchen. The rat wanted to eat the cake. But the mom said, "No, rat, you cannot eat the cake. It will spoil your dinner." The rat was sad but listened to the mom. The rat went outside to play with the family. They played with a ball and had lots of fun. After playing, they all sat down for dinner. The rat was happy it did not spoil its dinner. The family loved the rat, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once there was a small cat named Mimi. Mimi was perfect in every way. She had a big heart full of love. She lived in a nice house with a happy family. One day, Mimi saw a big dog outside. The big dog wanted to play with Mimi. Mimi was scared and did not want to play. Mimi refused to go near the big dog. But the big dog was sad. He just wanted to be friends with Mimi. Mimi saw how sad the big dog was. She decided to share her love with him. Mimi walked to the big dog and they played together. They became best friends and had lots of fun.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play together. Sometimes they play with cars, sometimes they play with dolls. They have a lot of fun. One day, they find a big box in the garage. They want to see what is inside. They try to open the box, but it is too heavy. They struggle to lift the lid, but they cannot. They push and pull, but they cannot. They hear their mom call them. "Anna, Ben, come here. It is time for lunch." They run to the kitchen. They see their mom and dad and their baby brother. They are a family. They love each other. They tell their mom and dad about the box. They ask them to help them open it. Their mom and dad smile. They say, "OK, let's go see the box. Maybe we can open it together. We are a strong family. We can do anything." They go back to the garage. They all hold the lid and lift it. They see a lot of old things inside. They see books and clothes and toys and pictures. They are happy. They thank their mom and dad. They say, "You are the best. You are strong. You help us." Their mom and dad hug them. They say, "You are the best. You are strong. You help us too." They laugh and smile. They are a family. They have a lot of fun.

One day, a cat named Tom found a telephone. It was a big, red telephone. Tom thought it was a toy, so he started to rub it with his paw. He liked the feeling of rubbing the telephone. It made him feel happy. Tom's friend, a dog named Max, saw him playing with the telephone. Max thought Tom was stupid for playing with a telephone. He didn't think it was a toy. Max wanted to show Tom what a real toy was, so he brought his favorite ball. Just as Max was about to show Tom his ball, the telephone started to ring. Tom and Max were very surprised. They didn't know that the telephone could make noise. They looked at each other and then at the telephone, not knowing what to do. Tom decided to rub the telephone again. When he did, a kind voice said "Hello!" from the telephone. Tom and Max were shocked. They didn't know the telephone could talk! The kind voice told them that they were both good friends and should play together. So, Tom and Max decided to share their toys. They played with the telephone and the ball, and they had lots of fun. They learned that even if something seems stupid, it can still be fun and bring friends together.
