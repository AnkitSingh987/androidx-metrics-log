Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot was a happy dog, but sometimes he felt gloomy. He did not like to suffer when he had to stay inside all day. One day, Spot's owner put a leash on him. They went for a walk. Spot was so happy! He liked to walk with his leash. It made the gloomy day better. As they walked, the sun came out. Spot felt happy again. He did not suffer anymore. He played with his leash and ran around. Spot loved his leash and his owner. The end.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a black box on the ground. He tried to open it, but it was locked. Tim was sad, so he put the box on his waist and went to find help. He met a girl named Sue. Tim said, "Can you help me open this box?" Sue tried, but she could not open it either. They saw a big tree and decided to sit under it to think. While they sat, a bird flew down from the tree. The bird had a shiny key in its beak. It dropped the key on Tim's lap. Tim and Sue looked at each other and smiled. They used the key to open the black box. Inside, they found toys and candy. They played and ate candy, happy that the bird helped them open the box.

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a shiny coin on the floor. She picked it up and put it in her pocket. Lucy liked the coin because it was very pretty. She walked around with the coin and showed it to her friends. They all liked the coin too. As they played, they heard a noisy sound. They all looked around and saw a big cat. The cat was yawning and stretching. The cat looked at Lucy and her friends. It walked up to them and looked at the coin in Lucy's hand. Lucy was scared, but she showed the cat the coin. The cat looked at the coin and then looked at Lucy. Then, the cat did something unexpected. It opened its mouth and out came a coin just like the one Lucy had! The cat was not a normal cat, it was a magic cat! Lucy and her friends were amazed. They laughed and played with the cat and the coins, and they had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a boy. He was only three years old, but he had a very special marble. It was gray with tiny blue swirls and he had named it Buttons. He was very proud of Buttons, and he loved to show it off to everyone. One day, he showed Buttons to his friend. His friend said he would have to trust him if he wanted to see what it was like to play with Buttons. The boy was hesitant, but his friend promised he wouldn't break it and the boy finally agreed. The boy held Buttons carefully while his friend gave it a few gentle pushes. Things were going smoothly until his friend got too excited and the marble flew out of his hands. The boy's heart sank as the marble went flying across the ground and Buttons broke into pieces. The boy was very upset and his friend tried to apologize. But the boy was too hurt and he said, "I trusted you, and you broke my marble!" Sadly, the boy gathered up the broken pieces and went home. Luckily, he was able to find a new marble to play with, but he wouldn't let anyone hold it. He had learned a valuable lesson - never trust someone with something very special and important to you.

One day, a little girl named Lucy woke up early. She saw the sun coming up and smiled. She wanted to play with her favorite toy, a big round ball. Lucy loved her ball because it was red and blue, and she could throw it and kick it. Lucy went outside to play with her ball. She saw a little bird who was very persistent. The bird kept trying to fly, but it was not good at it yet. Lucy said, "Little bird, do not give up! You will fly soon!" The little bird looked at Lucy and said, "Thank you, Lucy! I will keep trying!" And the bird tried again and again. Lucy played with her round ball and watched the bird. Soon, the bird was flying high in the sky. Lucy was happy for her new friend. They both had a fun day, and when the sun went down, they said goodnight to each other.
