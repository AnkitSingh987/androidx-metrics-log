Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna the Girl and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Anna. She loved to soar high up in the sky and around the world. One day, Anna was playing in the garden when she saw an old dictionary. She asked her mum, "What is that?" Her mum said, "It is a dictionary, honey. It contains lots of words and their meanings. They are all available for us to use." Anna was so excited. She could not wait to explore the dictionary. Anna spent the whole day reading the dictionary. She came across the word "soar". She asked her mum, "What does soar mean, mum?" Her mum said, "To soar means to fly freely and with grace." Anna was overjoyed. She eagerly went to the garden, spread her arms wide and lifted off into the sky. She flew among the clouds, higher and higher until she kissed the sun. She felt free and couldn't stop smiling. Then, Anna soared gleefully around the world, feeling the joy of her flying.

Once there was a little otter who lived near a river. She loved to play in the water and have fun. One day, when the sun was shining, she decided to go for a swim. But the little otter started to go too far. Before she knew it, she was out of sight from the riverbank. The little otter did not know it, but she had gone far away from her home. She swam for miles and miles until she was too tired to keep going. When she looked around, she noticed that she was surrounded by a beautiful meadow of flowers. The little otter looked at the beauty all around her and wished that she could stay. But, she soon realized that she had strayed too far. She started to regret her decision to go so far and be so adventurous. She turned around and made her way back to the river. Even though she wanted to stay, she knew it was best to go home. After a long journey, she eventually made it back. The little otter never regretted being adventurous again.

One day, a big bear went out for a walk. He took big steps in the icy snow. The bear felt an itch on his nose. He tried to scratch it, but his paws were too big. The bear saw a little girl playing in the snow. She had a small stick. The bear thought, "Maybe she can help me with my itch." So, he took a step towards her. The little girl saw the bear and got scared. She ran away, dropping her stick. The bear felt sad and still had an itch. He walked away alone, with his itch not going away.

Sara and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. But today the park is icy and cold. Sara and Tom wear hats and gloves and coats. They see a big hill with snow. They want to slide down the hill. They find a big box and put it on the snow. They climb on the box and hold hands. "Are you ready?" Sara asks. "Yes, I am ready!" Tom says. They push the box and it goes down the hill very fast. They laugh and scream. They see their mom and dad at the bottom of the hill. They wave to them. "Hi, mom! Hi, dad!" Sara and Tom shout. Mom and dad wave back. They are happy to see Sara and Tom having fun. "Be careful, kids!" mom says. "Don't worry, mom! We are careful!" Tom says. The box reaches the bottom of the hill and stops. Sara and Tom jump off the box and run to mom and dad. They hug them and tell them about their slide. "That was fun!" Sara says. "Yes, it was fun!" Tom says. "Can we do it again?" Sara asks. "Maybe next week, when the park is not so icy," dad says. "OK, dad," Sara says. They go home and have hot chocolate and cookies. They are warm and cozy. They are happy. They are friends.

Once upon a time, there was a valley. It was very healthy, with lots of trees, birds, and animals that lived there. One day, a giant came to the valley. All the animals were scared, but the giant said "don't be scared, I'm not here to hurt you". In his hands, the giant had a basket full of food. "I'm here to provide you with food," he said. The animals were so pleased. All the birds and animals replied together, in their tiny voices "thank you for providing food for us, we are very happy"! The giant smiled and said he would come again soon with more food. From that day onwards, the valley was always happy and healthy.
