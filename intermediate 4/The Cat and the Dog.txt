Category: Intermediate
Title: The Cat and the Dog

Once upon a time, a cat and a dog had a meeting. They wanted to be friends. They were grateful to play together. They liked to run, jump, and mix things up. One day, they found a big box. They thought it would be fun to mix the things inside. They opened the box and saw many colors. They were very happy. They decided to mix all the colors together. But when they mixed the colors, something unexpected happened. The colors turned into a big monster! The monster was not nice. The cat and dog were sad. They ran away from the monster. The cat and dog learned not to mix things they did not know.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a lot of fog. The fog was so thick that it was hard to see. Tim, a little boy, wanted to go outside and play. But his mom said, "No, Tim. It's not easy to see in the fog. You must stay inside." Tim was sad, but he listened to his mom. He looked out the window and saw his friend, Sam, walking in the fog. Sam was lost and scared. Tim opened the door and called out, "Sam, come here! It's safe inside!" Sam heard Tim and followed his voice. He went inside Tim's house, and they locked the door. They played together until the fog went away. When it was clear outside, they went out to play. They were happy, and they knew it was good to listen to their moms.

One day, a little cat named Tiny saw a mysterious can on the floor. She did not know what was inside. She wanted to try to open it. She tried and tried, but the can did not open. Tiny met her friend, a dog named Buddy. Tiny said, "Buddy, can you help me open this can?" Buddy tried to open the can too. He tried and tried, but he could not open it either. Then, they asked their friend, a bird named Chirpy, to help. Chirpy tried to open the can with her beak. She tried and tried, and the can finally opened! Inside the can was tuna! Tiny, Buddy, and Chirpy shared the tuna and they were all very happy.

Once upon a time, there was an old park. In the park, there was a pretty fountain. Many kids liked to play near the fountain. The water from the fountain was very cool and clean. It made the kids happy to see the water go up and down. One sunny day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw the fountain and wanted to open it. She asked her mom, "Can I open the fountain?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can try, but be careful." Lily was very excited. Lily walked up to the fountain and tried to open it. She pushed and pulled, but it did not open. Then, a kind old man came and helped her. With his help, the fountain opened, and water splashed out. Lily and the old man laughed and clapped. They had a fun day playing near the fountain.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Kitty. Kitty was very sad and miserable. She wanted to find a friend to play with. One day, Kitty went outside to look for a friend. She saw a big bottle of ink. Kitty thought the ink could be her friend. So, she said, "Hello, Ink! Will you be my friend?" The ink did not talk back, but Kitty did not mind. Kitty played with the ink all day long. But soon, she got thirsty. She wanted to drink some water. Kitty was so thirsty that she drank the ink by mistake. "Oh no!" she cried. "I drank my friend!" But then, she heard a little voice. It was the ink! "Don't worry, Kitty," said the ink. "I am still here to be your friend." And they played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a brown dog. The brown dog liked to play with his friends. They would run and jump all day long. The town was a happy place. One day, the brown dog and his friends went to the park. They wanted to play a game. They decided to part and play hide and seek. The brown dog started to count while his friends went to hide. The brown dog looked and looked, but he could not find his friends. He was sad. But then, something unexpected happened. He heard a noise behind a big tree. The brown dog went to look and saw a big, brown bear! The bear was not scary, but friendly. The bear had found all the brown dog's friends, and they were all playing together. The brown dog was so happy to see his friends and the new bear friend. From that day on, the brown dog, his friends, and the big, brown bear played together in the town park every day.
