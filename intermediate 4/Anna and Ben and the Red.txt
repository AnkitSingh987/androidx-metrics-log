Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna and Ben and the Red

Anna and Ben are playing a game. They have to find things that are red in the house. Anna finds a red ball, a red book and a red hat. Ben finds a red car, a red cup and a red sock. They are both happy. They show their things to Mom. Mom says, "You are both very good at finding red things. But who has more red things?" She counts the things. Anna has three red things. Ben has three red things. They have the same number of red things. Mom says, "You are both winners. You both get a prize." She gives them each a sticker. Anna gets a sticker with a star. Ben gets a sticker with a heart. They say, "Thank you, Mom. We love our stickers." They put their stickers on their shirts. They are proud of their stickers. They want to show their stickers to Dad. They run to the living room. Dad is on the couch. He is watching TV. The TV is loud. They say, "Dad, Dad, look at our stickers. We found red things. We got prizes." They show their stickers to Dad. Dad smiles. He says, "Wow, you are both amazing. I like your stickers. But can you please be quiet? I am trying to relax. The TV is loud enough." They say, "Sorry, Dad. We will be quiet." They sit next to Dad on the couch. They watch TV with him. They relax. They are happy. They have prizes. They have stickers. They have Mom and Dad. They have each other. The end.

Once upon a time there was a mommy and daddy. They cooked food together in the kitchen. They had a lot of fun cooking together. One day, mommy said to daddy, "It's time to cook something special." Daddy was excited. He asked, "What can we cook?" Mommy said, "Let's make something with powder." Daddy was surprised. "What kind of powder?" he asked. Mommy replied, "It's a deep powder that looks like snow." Daddy thought it sounded fun. Mommy got out a bowl and began to add the deep powder. They mixed it together to make a paste. Then, they put the paste in the oven. When the timer went off, they opened the oven. A wonderful smell filled the kitchen. Mommy and Daddy smiled at each other. They had cooked something special with the deep powder. It was delicious!

Once upon a time, a little car named Cara lived in a small town. Cara loved to drive around and play with her friends. One day, Cara felt very tired and slow. She did not know why. Cara went to her friend, Mr. Gas Pump, and said, "I feel very sorry, but I don't have any energy to play. What should I do?" Mr. Gas Pump knew what the problem was, and he said, "Cara, you need fuel! Let me help you." Mr. Gas Pump gave Cara some fuel, and she felt much better. Cara said, "Thank you, Mr. Gas Pump! Now I can play with my friends again!" And they all played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom loved to run and play outside. One day, Tom saw a light, pretty butterfly. He wanted to catch it and show it to his mom. So, Tom started to run after the butterfly. As Tom was running, he did not see a big rock in his way. He stumbled on the rock and lost his shoe. Tom was sad because he could not find his shoe. He looked and looked, but the shoe was not there. Tom's mom saw him looking for his shoe. She came to help him. They looked everywhere, and finally, they found the shoe under a bush. Tom was very happy. He put on his shoe and went back to play. And the light, pretty butterfly flew away, free and happy.

Once there was a little girl called Sara. She was excited because her mom had promised her a very special journey. On the day of the trip, Sara put on her soft velvet dress and she put some money in her pocket. She mom said: "Let's go! We are going to have a lot of fun". Sara smiled and they started to travel. At first the journey was very smooth and flexible. Sara loved to look at the big trees and the blue clouds. However, suddenly the journey became difficult. It started raining and Sara's mom had a bad accident. Sara was so frightened. She screamed: "Mommy, what happened?" Her mom replied with fear in her voice: "I'm sorry, Sara, but our journey has come to an end. We are going no further". Sara was so sad. She had to go home and she never finished her special journey.
