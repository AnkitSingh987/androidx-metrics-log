Category: Intermediate
Title: Mandy and Jeff and the Garden

Once upon a time there were two friends, Mandy and Jeff. They were both playing in the garden when Mandy spotted something in the corner. It was a shiny football! Mandy ran over to it and asked Jeff what it was. Jeff smiled and explained, "It's a football. You can kick it and it's lots of fun to play with". Mandy was very interested and asked if she could try. Jeff nodded and handed her the football. Mandy couldn't believe how big and attractive it was - and it felt so soft too! Jeff then showed Mandy how to kick it and she started having so much fun! She was giggling and running with the football and Jeff was laughing too. They played until it was time for Jeff to go home. Mandy said goodbye to Jeff and thanked him for explaining football to her. Jeff smiled and said, "It was my pleasure!"

Once there was a girl named Lily. She had a mop. Lily liked to help her mom clean the house. She would use her mop to clean the floor. One day, Lily's mom asked her to finish cleaning the kitchen. Lily took her mop and began to clean. She was happy to help her mom. Lily saw a deaf dog outside. The dog could not hear. Lily felt sad for the dog. She finished cleaning and went to play with the dog. The dog was happy, and Lily was happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. One day, Amy was playing in the park. She saw a big, red ball rise up into the sky. She was very anxious to get it back. Amy asked her friend, Tom, to help her. Tom had a magic spell that could bring the ball back. They both said the spell together. "Ball, ball, come back to me!" they shouted. The ball heard the spell and started to come down. Amy and Tom were so happy. The ball came back to them, and they played with it all day long.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Jack and Jill. They lived close to each other and used to play together almost every day. One day, Jack wanted to play with the gray ball, but Jill wanted the blue one. The two friends disagreed about which one to play with. "No, it must be the gray one!" said Jack. "No, it should be the blue one!" Jill said. They couldn't make up their minds, so they went to ask their mom. Mom thought for a moment, then she said "Let's try something different. Why don't both of you use the blue ball today?" Jack and Jill agreed and happily started to play together with the blue ball. The result was that they both had a lot of fun! The end.

Once, there was a man and his wife. They lived in a small house with their delicate pet bird. The man and wife loved their bird very much. They would play with it and take care of it every day. One day, the man wanted to change the bird's cage. He thought a bigger cage would make the bird very happy. The wife agreed, and they went to buy a new cage for their delicate pet bird. They found a big cage and brought it home. They put the delicate bird in the new cage. But the bird did not like the change. It was scared and flew away. The man and his wife were very sad. They lost their delicate pet bird forever.

Once upon a time, in a big green park, there was a white bird named Wisdom. Wisdom was a very fast bird. Every day, he would zoom through the sky, playing with his friends. One sunny day, Wisdom met a little girl named Lily. She looked up at Wisdom and said, "Hi! Can you show me how you zoom so fast?" Wisdom nodded and said, "Yes, I can show you. Just watch me!" Wisdom flew up into the sky and started to zoom around. Lily watched and clapped her hands. "Wow! You are so fast, Wisdom!" she said. Wisdom smiled and zoomed even faster, making Lily laugh. After a while, Wisdom flew back down to Lily. She gave him a big hug and said, "Thank you for showing me how you zoom. I had so much fun!" Wisdom smiled and said, "You're welcome, Lily. I had fun too!" From that day on, Wisdom and Lily became best friends. They played together in the big green park every day, and Wisdom always showed Lily how to zoom fast in the sky.
