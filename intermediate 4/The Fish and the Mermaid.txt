Category: Intermediate
Title: The Fish and the Mermaid

Once upon a time there was a fish. He was helpless and alone in the deep, dark sea. One day, he saw a mermaid wiping her eyes. He swam closer and asked why she was so sad. She told him that she had no one to help her clean the ocean. The fish told her that he could help. He swam around and wiped away all the dirt and grime. The mermaid was so pleased. She thanked the little fish and gave him a big hug. From that day on, the fish and mermaid were the best of friends. They cleaned the ocean together and everyone was happy. The end.

Once upon a time, in a big world, there was a wise old cat. The cat liked to sit under a tree and watch the birds. The cat knew many things about the world. One day, a little bird came and sat next to the wise cat. The bird asked, "Why do you sit here every day?" The cat smiled and said, "I like to watch the world and learn new things." The little bird wanted to be wise too. So, the bird sat with the cat every day. They watched the world together and became good friends. And soon, the little bird was wise, just like the cat.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the store with her mom. They needed to buy things for the house. Lily liked to help her mom. She felt like a big girl when she did. At the store, they saw a big pile of laundry. Lily's mom said, "We need to buy some laundry for your dad. His clothes are too tight." Lily picked out some laundry that was not tight. She thought her dad would like it. When they got home, Lily's dad saw the laundry. He said, "Thank you, Lily! These clothes are not tight. They are just right!" Lily smiled and felt happy. She liked helping her family.

Once upon a time, there was a big octopus who lived in the sea. The octopus had many legs and was very good at catching fish. He was always alert and quick to find food for his friends. One day, a little fish came to the octopus and asked for help. The little fish was hungry and could not find any food. The octopus wanted to help the little fish, so he used his legs to provide food for the little fish. The little fish was very happy and said thank you to the octopus. The other sea animals saw what the octopus did and they learned a lesson. They learned that it is good to help others and provide for those in need. The sea animals lived happily together, always ready to help each other. And the octopus was their friend and teacher, showing them how to be kind and alert.

Once upon a time, there was a good dog named Spot. Spot loved to play in the park with his ball. One day, it was very cold outside, and Spot started to shiver. He knew it was time to go home and get warm. When Spot got home, his mom was making some food. She was cutting an onion. Spot didn't like onions, but he knew they made the food taste good. He sat by the warm oven and stopped shivering as he waited for the food to be done. Finally, the food was ready. Spot's mom gave him a big bowl of warm soup. He could see the onions in it, but he knew they were good for him. So, Spot ate the soup and felt warm and happy. The end.

One sunny day, Sam went to visit his friend, Tim. They wanted to play with their toys together. Sam was very excited to see Tim and share his toys. When Sam got to Tim's house, he saw that Tim was frustrated. Tim said, "I can't find my toy car. I think someone took it." Sam wanted to help Tim feel better, so he said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can find your toy car together. Trust me." They looked everywhere for the toy car. They looked under the bed, in the closet, and even in the toy box. But they could not find the toy car. Then, something unexpected happened. Sam's little sister, Lily, came into the room holding the toy car. Lily said, "I found the toy car outside. I wanted to surprise you and give it back." Tim and Sam were happy that Lily found the toy car. They thanked her and played together for the rest of the day.
