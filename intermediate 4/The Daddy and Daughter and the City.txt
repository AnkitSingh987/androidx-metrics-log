Category: Intermediate
Title: The Daddy and Daughter and the City

Once upon a time, there was a daddy and a daughter. They were best friends! One day, daddy and daughter decided to explore the city. They went on a very big adventure and saw lots of fun things. Soon, daddy realized something very special. As they were strolling along, their daughter was growing more and more. The more they walked together, the taller and more grown-up she became. Daddy was amazed at how big she was becoming. Daddy looked around and noticed how enormous the city was. He was filled with happiness knowing that his daughter was growing up. They laughed and smiled together. Daddy was so proud of his daughter and her enormous adventure.

Once there was a little boy named Harry who loved playing with his curtains. Every morning he'd run to the window, throw open the curtains, and smile. One morning, there was a strange spice in the air. It made Harry sneeze and rub his nose, and he knew something special was afoot. So, Harry ran over to the curtains and opened them again. There before him stood a giant pepper filled with spices he'd never seen before! Harry gasped and asked, "What's your name?" The pepper responded, "I am called the spicy pepper. I have come here to share my spices with you and make your meals extra delicious." Harry smiled, excited to try this new spice. He hugged the pepper and thanked him, and together they made the world a little spicier.

Lily and Ben are playing with blocks. They like to build towers and houses and cars. Lily has a big red block. She puts it on top of her tower. Ben wants the red block. He tries to take it from Lily. "Stop!" Lily says. "That is my block. You have your own blocks." Ben does not listen. He pulls the red block hard. The tower falls down. The blocks snap and crack. Lily and Ben are sad. They cry. "Look what you did!" Lily says. "You broke my tower and my blocks." "I'm sorry," Ben says. "I just wanted the red block. It is pretty." Lily and Ben hear a tap on the door. It is Mom. She comes in and sees the mess. She is not happy. "What happened here?" Mom asks. "Why are you crying?" "Ben broke my tower and my blocks," Lily says. "He took my red block." Mom picks up the red block. She sees a crack on it. She is sorry. "I'm sorry, Lily," Mom says. "This block is broken. You can't use it anymore." She gives the red block to Ben. "Here, Ben," Mom says. "This is your block now. You can keep it. But you have to say sorry to Lily. You have to share and be nice." Ben looks at the red block. He feels bad. He does not want the broken block. He wants to be friends with Lily. "I'm sorry, Lily," Ben says. "I was mean. I don't want the red block. You can have it. Can we play together?" Lily looks at Ben. She sees he is sorry. She smiles. "OK, Ben," Lily says. "We can play together. But you have to be careful with my blocks. They are fragile." Mom smiles too. She is glad they are friends again. She helps them pick up the blocks. They find some new blocks that are not broken. They start to build a new tower. They are slow and gentle. They have fun.

One day, a cat named Jazz was walking in the park. Jazz was a fearful cat, always scared of new things. As he walked, he saw a big dog. The big dog said, "Hi Jazz! Do you want to play with me?" Jazz was scared, but he wanted to make a new friend. So, he said, "Okay, let's play together!" They played all day and had lots of fun. Jazz was happy he had a new friend. At the end of the day, Jazz got a toy from the big dog. The toy was a small ball. Jazz learned that it's good to try new things and not be scared all the time.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Lily loved to play on the swings and slide. As Lily played, she saw a noisy mosquito buzzing around. She tried to catch it, but it was too fast. The mosquito flew away and Lily gazed at the sky, hoping it would come back. Then, Lily met a boy named Tim. Tim said, "I see the mosquito too! Let's catch it together." They ran and laughed, trying to catch the noisy mosquito. But it was too quick for them. Lily and Tim sat down, tired from chasing the mosquito. They gazed at the sky and saw the mosquito fly away. They smiled at each other, happy to have made a new friend at the park.
