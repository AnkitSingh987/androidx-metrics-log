Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Dish

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She was very excited to play with her friends. She saw her friends on the swings and ran to join them. After playing for a long time, Lily and her friends were tired. They sat down under a big tree to rest. Lily's mom came over and said, "I have a surprise for you." She took out a dish with yummy treats. The dish had cookies and fruits. Lily and her friends smiled and said, "Thank you!" They enjoyed the treats from the dish. After eating, they felt better and went back to playing. They had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a busy ant named Tim. Tim was always running around and helping his friends. One day, Tim found a big cone in the park. He thought it would be fun to play with. Tim tried to move the cone, but it was too heavy. He started to cry because he could not play with the cone. His friend, a little bird named Sue, saw him crying. Sue wanted to help Tim because she did not like to see her friend cry. Sue had an idea. She called all of their friends to help move the cone. Together, they pushed and pulled until the cone moved. Tim was so happy that he stopped crying. They all played with the cone and had a great day.

Sam and Mia were friends who liked to play in the garden. They planted seeds and watered them every day. They wanted to see the seeds grow into flowers and fruits. One day, Sam said to Mia, "I have a big idea. When we grow up, we can be husband and wife. Then we can live in this garden and eat the fruits and smell the flowers." Mia smiled and said, "That sounds nice. But how do we grow up? Do we need to do something?" Sam thought for a moment and said, "I think we just need to wait and be good. And maybe eat more vegetables and drink more milk. That will make us grow strong and tall." Mia nodded and said, "OK, let's do that. And let's also be kind and share and help each other. That will make us grow happy and loving." Sam and Mia hugged and agreed. They continued to play in the garden, watching the seeds grow. They felt peaceful and hopeful. They dreamed of their future as husband and wife in the garden.

Once upon a time, a smart boy named Tim loved to watch cartoons. His favorite cartoon was about a hero who saved people. Tim wanted to be like the hero, so he would play and pretend to save his toys. One day, Tim saw a little girl crying in the park. She had lost her toy. Tim wanted to help her find it. He kneeled down and looked under the slide. There it was! The toy was hiding under the slide. Tim gave the toy back to the little girl. She was very happy and thanked him. Tim felt like a real hero, just like in his favorite cartoon. From that day on, Tim always helped his friends when they needed him.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat delicious spaghetti. One day, he made a big plate of spaghetti for lunch. He was so excited to eat it. While Tim was eating, a little bird flew into the house. The bird saw the spaghetti and wanted some too. The bird tried to take a bite, but it got stuck in the spaghetti. Tim saw the bird and wanted to help. Tim carefully took the bird out of the spaghetti. He rescued the bird and set it free. The bird was very happy and flew away. Tim was happy too, because he helped the bird. Then, he went back to eating his delicious spaghetti.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big statue. The statue was of an elderly man. The man was very wise and kind. The people in the town loved the statue. One day, a little boy named Tim went to see the statue. He looked up at the elderly man and said, "Hello, Mr. Statue! Can you help me unite my friends to play?" To Tim's surprise, the statue came to life! The elderly man smiled and said, "Of course, Tim. Let's bring your friends together." Tim and the elderly statue went around the town and found all of Tim's friends. They all came together to play. The people in the town were amazed by the statue coming to life. From that day on, the statue helped the children unite and play together, making everyone in the town very happy.
