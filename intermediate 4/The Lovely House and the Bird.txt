Category: Intermediate
Title: The Lovely House and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a lovely little house. In the house, there was a door. Behind the door, there lived a happy family. They loved to play and laugh all day long. The sun was always shining, and the birds sang sweet songs. One day, a big wind came and blew the door open. The family was scared. They did not know what to do. They ran and hid under the table. They waited for the wind to stop. But the wind did not stop. It blew and blew. Then, a small bird flew into the house. The bird saw the open door and the scared family. The bird wanted to help. It flew to the door and tried to push it closed. The family saw the bird and wanted to save it. They came out from under the table and helped the bird push the door closed. The wind stopped, and the door was closed. The family and the bird were safe. They were all very happy. The family thanked the bird for saving them. They asked the bird to stay with them and be their friend. The bird said yes, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a musician. He had a large drum. He loved to play it and make people happy. Every day, he played his drum in the park. One day, a bad man came to the park. He saw the large drum and wanted it. So, he tried to steal it from the musician. The musician was very sad. A big dog saw the bad man with the drum. The dog barked loud and scared the bad man away. The dog brought the drum back to the musician. The musician was very happy and played his drum for the dog. They became best friends.

One day, a nice cat named Tim went to the kitchen. He wanted to find some food to eat. He saw the big refrigerator and thought there might be something yummy inside. So, he jumped up and opened the refrigerator door. Tim decided to peek inside to see what he could find. As Tim peeked into the refrigerator, he saw a big cake. The cake had pretty colors and looked very tasty. Tim wanted to eat the cake, but he heard a noise. He looked around and saw a little mouse. The mouse was looking at the cake too. Tim thought the mouse wanted to eat the cake, but he was wrong. The little mouse looked at Tim and said, "I don't want to eat the cake. I want to be your friend." Tim was surprised and happy. He didn't expect the mouse to say that. So, Tim and the mouse shared the cake and became the best of friends. They played together all day and had lots of fun. From that day on, Tim and the mouse were always together, and they never felt alone again.

Once upon a time, there was a pink swan named Lily. She lived in a small pond with her friends. Lily was sad because she did not belong with the other swans. They were all white, and she was different. One day, Lily met a big frog named Fred. Fred said, "Lily, I know a place where you will belong. It is a pond with many pink swans like you. Come with me, and I will show you." Lily was happy and went with Fred. As they went to the new pond, the sky became dark and scary. Lily felt scared, but Fred said, "Don't worry, we are almost there." When they reached the new pond, there were no pink swans. Fred laughed and said, "I tricked you, Lily. Now you belong to me." Lily was very sad and missed her friends.

One day, a big blue van went down the road. The van was happy and calm. It liked to drive around and see the trees and the sun. A man with a spray bottle saw the van. He wanted to help it look clean and shiny. So, he started to spray water on the van. The van liked the water and felt cool and happy. But then, the man sprayed too much water on the van. The van got very wet and could not see the road. The van went off the road and hit a tree. The van was sad and not calm anymore. The man was sorry and wished he had not sprayed so much water.
