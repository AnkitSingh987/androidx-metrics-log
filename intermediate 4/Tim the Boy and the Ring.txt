Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Ring

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a red cap that he loved a lot. He wore it every day when he played outside. One day, Tim heard a ring. He was frightened because he did not know where the sound came from. He looked around and saw a small bird on the ground. The bird was hurt and could not fly. Tim wanted to help the bird, so he put it in his red cap. He took the bird home and gave it food and water. The bird started to feel better. Tim was happy that his cap helped the bird. The next morning, Tim woke up and saw that the bird was not in his cap. He was sad and thought the bird was gone. But then, he heard a ring again. The bird was not hurt anymore and was flying around his room! The bird was saying thank you to Tim. Tim was happy and not frightened anymore. They became best friends and played together every day.

Sara likes to walk with her mom. They walk to the park, to the store, to the library. Sara likes to see the trees, the cars, the books. She likes to hold her mom's hand. One day, Sara sees something shiny in the store window. It is jewelry. There are rings, necklaces, bracelets. They are shiny and pretty. Sara wants to touch them. She asks her mom, "Can I see the jewelry?" Her mom says, "OK, but be careful. They are not toys. They are for grown-ups." She takes Sara inside the store. Sara looks at the jewelry. She sees a big ring. It has a big stone. It is blue and sparkly. Sara likes it. She says, "Wow, look at the big ring!" The lady in the store smiles. She says, "That is a very big ring. It is very expensive. Do you want to try it on?" She takes the ring out of the case. She puts it on Sara's finger. It is too big for Sara. It slides off. Sara laughs. She says, "It is too big for me!" Her mom says, "Yes, it is too big for you. Maybe when you are older, you can have a ring like that. But for now, let's go. We have to walk home. It is getting late." She thanks the lady in the store. She takes Sara's hand. They walk out of the store. Sara waves goodbye to the big ring. She says, "Bye, big ring. Maybe I will see you again."

One day, a girl named Lily wanted to introduce her new friend, Tom, to her family. Tom was a boy from her school. Lily took Tom to her house and showed him her bedroom. In the bedroom, there were toys, a bed, and a window. They played with toys and had fun. Lily's mom came in and said, "Tom, do you want to try my bitter lemon cake?" Tom said, "Yes, thank you!" Lily's mom gave them cake, and they ate it in the bedroom. Tom made a funny face because the cake was very bitter. Lily laughed at Tom's funny face. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came through the window and took the bitter cake away. The wind was so strong that it took Tom away too! Lily was sad and scared. She never saw Tom again.

Once there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to search for things. One day, Amy's mom lost her coat. "Amy, can you help me find my coat?" her mom asked. Amy was excited to help and started to search the house. Amy looked in the closet, under the bed, and behind the door. She found a fragile, old box in the corner of the room. Amy opened the box very carefully. Inside the box, she found a beautiful, shiny key. She knew the key was special and showed it to her mom. Amy's mom smiled and said, "This key is for the secret room in our house." They used the key to open the secret room. Inside, they found many pretty things and her mom's coat! They were so happy they found the coat and the secret room. From that day on, Amy and her mom had many more fun adventures searching for hidden treasures.
