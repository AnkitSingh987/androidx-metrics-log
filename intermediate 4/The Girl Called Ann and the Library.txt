Category: Intermediate
Title: The Girl Called Ann and the Library

Once upon a time, there was a girl called Ann. Ann wanted to find a special place to play. One day, Ann walked to the library. The library was big and empty. Ann had never seen so many books! Ann looked around, but no one else was there. Ann decided to keep the library to herself. She went to the corner and began to play. Ann enjoyed herself very much. Every day she would come back to the library and keep it all to herself. Ann could stay there for hours and never felt lonely. Ann loved her special place! It always made her feel happy inside. She was so glad she had a place to keep all to herself.

Once upon a time, there was a shy little girl named Lily. She liked to ask many questions. One day, she heard a big word: "university". She did not know what it meant, so she asked her mommy. "Mommy, what is a university?" Lily said. Her mommy smiled and said, "A university is a big place where big kids and grown-ups go to learn new things." Lily thought about it and said, "I want to go to university when I grow up!" Her mommy hugged her and said, "You can do anything you want, Lily. Just keep asking questions and learning." From that day on, Lily was not shy anymore. She asked many questions and learned a lot. She knew that one day, she would go to university and learn even more.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Bobo. Bobo loved to sing. Every day, he would sit on a tree branch and sing happy songs. All the other birds liked to listen to him. One day, while Bobo was singing, he saw a big, round thing on the ground. It was not a real thing, it was a fake thing. It was a big fake zero. Bobo was very curious about the fake zero, so he flew down to look at it. Bobo tried to sing a song to the fake zero, but the zero did not sing back. Bobo felt a little sad, but he did not give up. He kept singing and singing until all the other birds came to see the fake zero too. They all sang together, and even though the zero could not sing, they had a lot of fun.

Lila and Ben were playing a game with cards. They had to match the cards with the same picture. Lila had more cards than Ben. She was happy. "Look, Ben, I have more cards than you. I win!" she said. Ben was sad. He did not like to lose. He wanted to have more cards too. "No, Lila, you did not win. You cheated!" he said. Lila was confused. She did not cheat. She played fair. "What do you mean, Ben? How did I cheat?" she asked. Ben pointed at a card on the floor. It had a picture of a star. "You dropped this card on purpose. You wanted to have less cards. That is cheating!" he said. Lila shook her head. She did not drop the card on purpose. It was an accident. "No, Ben, I did not drop the card on purpose. It was an accident. I did not see it. I did not want to have less cards. That is silly!" she said. She picked up the card and put it in her pile. "See, Ben, now I have more cards again. I win!" she said. Ben was angry. He did not believe Lila. He thought she was lying. "No, Lila, you are lying. You still cheated. You are a bad winner. I don't want to play with you anymore!" he said. He threw his cards on the floor and ran away. Lila was sad. She did not lie. She was a good winner. She wanted to play with Ben. She did not understand why he was angry. She tried to explain to him, but he did not listen. He did not want to be her friend. She looked at the cards on the floor. They were dull. They did not make her happy. She wished Ben would come back and play with her. She missed him.
