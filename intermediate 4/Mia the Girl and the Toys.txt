Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time, in a big universe, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was feeling miserable one day. She did not want to play with her toys or eat her favorite food. Mia just sat on her chair and did not smile. Mia's mommy saw her frown and asked, "Why are you sad, Mia?" Mia said, "I don't know, Mommy. I just feel bad." Mommy hugged Mia and said, "Let's go for a walk outside. Maybe it will make you feel better." So, Mia and her mommy went for a walk. They saw pretty flowers, happy dogs, and kids playing. Mia started to feel better. She smiled and said, "Thank you, Mommy. I am happy now." They went back home and had a fun day together.

One day, a boy and a girl went to the beach. They liked to play in the sand and the water. The sun was very bright and the water was not bad. They were very happy. While they played, they saw a big crab. The crab had big eyes and it looked like it might scare them. The boy and girl were a little scared, but they wanted to see the crab up close. They walked closer to the crab, and then something unexpected happened. The crab started to dance! The boy and girl were not scared anymore. They laughed and danced with the crab. They had a lot of fun at the beach with their new friend.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They saw a big train with steam coming out of it. Tim was very excited to see the train. He wanted to ride on it, but his mom said, "We can't ride the train today, Tim." The next day, Tim went to the train station with his mom again. He saw the train and started to yawn. He was very tired. Suddenly, the train made a terrible noise. Tim and his mom were scared. They did not know what was happening. Then, a big, friendly elephant came out of the train. The elephant said, "Hello, Tim and mom! I am here to take you on a fun ride." Tim and his mom were very surprised. They rode on the elephant's back and had a great day. The train was not scary anymore.

One day, a blue bird was flying in the sky. The bird was very hungry. It wanted to find some food for lunch. It saw a little boy named Tim playing in the park. The bird flew down and said, "Hello, Tim. I am very hungry. Can you help me find some lunch?" Tim thought for a moment and said, "Yes, I can help you. My mom can bake us a yummy lunch. Let's go to my house." The bird was very happy and followed Tim to his house. When they got to Tim's house, Tim's mom was baking a big cake. The bird was so excited! But, suddenly, the cake started to grow bigger and bigger. It filled the whole room! Tim, his mom, and the bird had to eat the cake to make it smaller. They all laughed and had a great time eating the cake for lunch. The blue bird was not hungry anymore and thanked Tim and his mom for the fun and yummy lunch.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She loved to create things. One day, she found a flower in her yard. It was a pretty flower with many colors. Lily wanted to make something special with it. Lily thought and thought. Then, she had an idea. She would make a delicious cake with the flower. Lily went to the kitchen and mixed all the things to make a cake. She put the flower on top of the cake. It looked so yummy. Lily took a bite of the cake. But, oh no! The flower did not taste good. It tasted bad. Lily was sad. But then, something unexpected happened. The flower began to grow bigger and bigger. It turned into a big, beautiful tree with many flowers. Lily was happy again. She learned that sometimes, things don't go as planned, but they can still turn out great.
