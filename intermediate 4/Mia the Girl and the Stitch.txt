Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Stitch

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a very special toy named Stitch. Stitch was a rare toy, not like the other toys. Mia loved Stitch very much. One day, Mia and Stitch wanted to celebrate. They put on party hats and danced around the room. They played fun games and laughed a lot. Mia and Stitch had a great time celebrating together. At the end of the day, Mia gave Stitch a big hug. She was so happy to have such a rare and special toy. Mia and Stitch went to bed, excited to celebrate more fun days together.

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog named Max. Max loved to go on journeys with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. They would walk and play in the park every day. One day, Tim and Max decided to go on a big journey. They walked and walked until they found a big tree with lots of colorful balloons. They knew something fun was happening. It was a birthday party! Tim and Max joined the party to celebrate with their friends. They played games, ate cake, and had a great time. At the end of the day, Tim and Max went home, tired but happy from their journey.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk in the wild woods. He saw a big tree with a knot. Tim thought the knot looked like a funny face. He said, "Hello, funny face!" To his surprise, the knot talked back! "Hello, Tim!" said the knot. Tim was very surprised. He asked the knot, "Can you be my friend?" The knot said, "Yes, but I have a secret. I can turn into a wild bird if you say 'fly high'." Tim said the words, "Fly high!" and the knot turned into a wild bird! The wild bird and Tim became best friends. They played in the woods every day. One day, they found a wild cat stuck in a tree. Tim and the wild bird helped the cat get down. The wild cat said, "Thank you, Tim and wild bird!" Now, they all played together in the wild woods, and they were very happy.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who was walking in the park. She was feeling happy, until she noticed a building. The building seemed very scary, with loud noises coming from it. She felt a bit fearful, but decided to take a closer look. As she got closer, she noticed the building was very old and dark. But, the little girl was brave! She walked through the scary entrance, and saw it was a red castle. She smiled and stepped closer, until she heard some strange whispers. The little girl was scared, and she started to run away! But then she noticed it was just the wind blowing, and the little girl laughed. She realised the building was not really so scary after all. The little girl felt relieved and walked out feeling brave and happy!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She liked to make things with her hands. One day, she found a big piece of paper. She was very happy and wanted to make something special with it. Lily asked her mom for help. Her mom was busy, so she said, "I am sorry, Lily. I cannot help you right now." Lily felt sad, but she decided to make something on her own. She tried to make a big, pretty hat. Lily cut the paper and tried to put the hat on her head. But the hat was too big and it fell down. Lily was very sorry that her hat did not work. She tried to fix it, but she could not. In the end, Lily's hat was broken and she was very sad.

One day, a little duck went to the pond. The duck was angry because he lost his toy. He looked around the pond to find it. The sun was shining and the water was cool. The duck saw a big frog sitting on a log. The duck swam to the frog and said, "Did you see my toy?" The frog said, "Yes, I saw it float by. It went that way." The duck thanked the frog and swam where the frog pointed. At last, the duck found his toy near some tall plants. He was so happy! The duck played with his toy until the day had to end. The angry duck was now a happy duck, and he swam home with a big smile.
