Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Flowers

One day, a girl named Sue went to the park. She wanted to pick flowers for her mom. Sue saw many flowers, but she wanted to find the best ones. She looked around and saw a tight bunch of flowers with a pretty style. She thought her mom would love them. Sue asked her friend Tim, "Do you like these flowers? I want to pick them for my mom." Tim looked at the flowers and said, "Yes, they are very pretty. Your mom will love them!" Sue smiled and picked the flowers. Sue and Tim went back to Sue's house. Sue gave the flowers to her mom. Her mom was so happy and said, "These flowers are beautiful, Sue! Thank you!" Sue felt proud that she picked the best flowers for her mom.

One day, a girl named Sue wanted to design a pretty flower. She took some paper and a dark color. She drew a big, dark flower on the paper. Sue was very happy with her flower and showed it to her mom. "Look, Mom! I made a dark flower," Sue said. Her mom smiled and said, "That's a very nice flower, Sue. But let's add some more colors to make it even prettier." Sue and her mom added more colors to the dark flower. They put red, blue, and yellow on it. The flower looked very pretty now. Sue was happy and wanted to show it to her friend, Tim. When Sue showed the flower to Tim, he said, "Wow! That's a magic flower!" Sue didn't know it was a magic flower. Tim touched the flower, and something unexpected happened. The flower came to life and grew big, with many colors. Sue, Tim, and Sue's mom were all very surprised and happy. They knew they had made a very special flower together.

One day, a little boy named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big tree with a small door on it. Tom was very curious, so he opened the door. Inside, he found a tiny room with a small table. On the table, there was a note that said, "Please keep this room clean and honest." Tom wanted to be a good boy, so he cleaned the room. He also found a toy car on the floor. He put the car on the table and said, "I will keep this room honest and not take the car." Just then, a little mouse came out from a hole in the wall. The mouse looked at Tom and said, "Thank you for being honest and not taking my toy car. I have a gift for you." The mouse gave Tom a magic key. "This key will open any door you want," said the mouse. Tom was very happy with his gift. He used the key to open a door on his left. Behind the door, he found a room filled with toys and candy. Tom played and ate candy all day. When it was time to go home, he said goodbye to the mouse and promised to visit again. The mouse was happy that Tom was an honest friend.

One day, a big bear named Ben went for a walk in the woods. He saw ice on the ground. The sun was shining and the ice was starting to dry. Ben was happy because he liked to play in the woods when it was not wet. Ben saw his friend, a little rabbit named Rob. "Hi Rob! Do you want to play with me?" asked Ben. "Yes, let's play!" said Rob. They played all day long, running and jumping on the dry ground. As they played, they went too far away from their homes. The sun went down and the woods became dark. Ben and Rob were lost. They tried to find their way back, but they couldn't. They were scared and cold. The big bear and little rabbit had to stay in the woods all night, and they missed their families.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Poppy. Poppy liked to watch the sky above her. Every day, she would watch the clouds, the birds and the sun. One day, however, Poppy felt a bit dizzy. She kept looking up too much and made herself dizzy. She kept watching and watching, until she began to feel sick! So, Poppy's big sister came over and reminded her: "Poppy, nothing is as important as taking care of yourself. You should watch the sky, but you should also remember to take a break if you feel dizzy." Poppy agreed with her sister, and began to take breaks in between looking up and watching the sky. In this way, she could safely enjoy and watch the sky, without feeling too dizzy ever again. The moral of the story is that it is important to take care of yourself and to not forget to take a break - even when you're having a lot of fun.
