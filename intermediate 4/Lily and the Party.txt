Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Party

Once upon a time, a shy little girl named Lily had a birthday. She wanted to have a big party with all her friends. But she was too shy to ask them to come. So, her mom said, "I will help you, Lily. We will go to the store and get everything we need for the party." At the store, Lily and her mom picked out pretty balloons, a big cake, and fun games to play. Lily was so excited! She started to feel less shy. She said, "Mom, I think I can ask my friends to come now." The day of the party came, and all of Lily's friends were there. They played games, ate cake, and had lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind blew through the party and took away all the balloons! The kids were sad and went home. Lily's birthday party was over, and she felt shy again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a messy room with toys all over the floor. One day, her mom warned her, "Mia, you must clean your room or you might lose your favorite doll." Mia did not listen and went to play outside. She left her room very messy. Later, she came back inside to play with her doll, but she could not find it. "Oh no, where is my doll?" cried Mia. Just then, her dog, Spot, came in with the doll in his mouth. "Spot, you found my doll!" Mia said happily. "Thank you!" From that day on, Mia always kept her room clean so she would not lose her doll again.

Once upon a time there was a kind girl. Her name was Jane. She wanted something to eat, but all she saw was juice. She thanked her mom very much and drank the juice in one sip. Jane was so happy. She wanted to thank someone else for the juice, so she looked outside to see if anyone was kind enough to give her more juice. Through the window she saw a man, he was walking in the street and smiling. Jane ran outside and thanked the man for the juice. He was so nice to her, he gave her two more cups of juice. Jane was so happy she hugged him and thanked him again for being so kind. She ran back inside, thanked her mom and drank both cups of juice. Jane was so full, she thanked everyone for the kind gifts she received that day, and she went to bed with a full belly and a smile on her face.

One day, a girl named Lily went to the park to play. She saw a swing and ran to it. Lily loved to swing high in the sky. She felt like a bird flying. As she was swinging, Lily saw a big box near a tree. The box looked expensive and was a mystery. Lily stopped swinging and went to the box. She wanted to know what was inside. Lily opened the box and found a big hat. She put the hat on her head, and something unexpected happened. The hat made her invisible! Lily laughed and ran around the park. No one could see her. But when she went back to the swing, she became visible again. The hat's magic only worked when she was away from the swing.

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk in the park. She loved to find new things. Mia saw a pretty symbol on the ground. It was a big, red heart. She wanted to know what it meant. Mia asked her friend Tom, "What does this symbol mean?" Tom said, "I don't know, but it looks nice. Let's try to find out!" They both started to look around the park for more symbols. Soon, they found a spoiled apple under a tree. Mia said, "Look, there's another symbol!" It was a small, green leaf. Mia and Tom knew that the leaf meant the tree was near. They followed the symbols and found a hidden treasure box under the tree. They were so happy that they found the treasure by following the symbols.
