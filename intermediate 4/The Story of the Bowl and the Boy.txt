Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Bowl and the Boy

Once upon a time there was a boy who wanted a snack. He got a big bowl of hot soup. He tasted it and yummy! But he wanted something else. He looked in his mom's closet, and found a big, wet shoe. He wondered what it would taste like. The boy put the shoe up to his mouth, and just when he was about to take a big bite, he saw his mom standing in the doorway, with a frown on her face. She was very mad at him! She scolded the boy, telling him how naughty it was to try to eat a shoe. But the boy still didn't know what a wet shoe tasted like! Then his mom had an idea. She took the shoe and filled it with yummy, hot soup. She offered it to the boy, who tasted it and said it was great! He had learned his lesson, and from then on, he only ate things that were meant to be eaten.

One day, a beautiful girl named Lily was playing outside. She liked to play with her big red ball. She had a lot of fun bouncing it and throwing it high in the sky. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. While she was playing, Lily heard a "ring, ring" sound. She looked around and saw a phone on the ground. She picked it up and said, "Hello?" A nice voice said, "Hi, Lily! This is your friend, the phone. Can you help me find my home?" Lily was surprised but wanted to help. She took the phone with her and walked around. They looked high and low, but they couldn't find the phone's home. Then, something unexpected happened. The phone started to sing and dance! It was a magic phone! The magic phone said, "Lily, you are so kind to help me. As a thank you, I will be your friend forever." And from that day on, Lily and the magic phone were the best of friends. They played and laughed together, and they always had the best time.

Once upon a time, there was a clever little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and have fun with his friends. One day, Spot got an invitation to a big party. He was very excited to go and play with his friends. At the party, Spot saw many games. He wanted to improve at playing catch, so he asked his friend, Kitty, to play with him. "Kitty, will you play catch with me?" Spot asked. "Yes, Spot! I love to play catch," Kitty said. So, they played catch and had lots of fun. But then, it started to rain. The rain made the ground very wet and slippery. Spot and his friends tried to keep playing, but they all fell down. They got very dirty and wet. The party was not fun anymore. Spot and his friends had to go home early because of the rain. They were sad that their fun day had a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Tim. Tim was very sad and miserable. He did not like his small house. One day, Tim saw an exit in his house. He had never seen it before. Tim went through the exit and found a big, beautiful park. He was so happy! Tim played in the park all day. He ran and jumped and had so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A big, mean cat came and yelled at Tim. Tim was scared, but he did not run away. Instead, he yelled back at the cat. The cat was surprised and stopped being mean. They became friends and played together. Tim was not miserable anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a special napkin. The napkin was delicate and pretty. Sue loved her napkin very much. One day, Sue's napkin began to shrink. It got smaller and smaller. Sue was sad. She didn't want her napkin to be so small. Then, something unexpected happened. Sue's napkin turned into a tiny fairy! The fairy said, "Don't be sad, Sue. I may be small, but now I can be your friend and help you." Sue was happy and they became the best of friends.
