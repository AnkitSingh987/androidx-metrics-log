Category: Intermediate
Title: The Car of Tim the Boy

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk on his street. He saw his friend, Sam, playing with a toy car. Tim wanted to play too, so he asked, "Can I play with you, Sam?" Sam said, "Yes, let's play!" They played with the toy car on the hard ground. They made the car go very fast. After a while, they saw a small hole in the ground. Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's bury the car and dig it up later!" Sam liked the idea, so they put the car in the hole and covered it with dirt. They played other games for a while. Then, they went back to the spot where they buried the car. They dug and dug with their hands until they found the car again. They were very happy. Tim and Sam played with the toy car for the rest of the day on their street. They had lots of fun and couldn't wait to play together again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a kind and compassionate teacher named Miss Lily. Miss Lily loved to teach little kids math. She had a big class of happy kids who liked to learn. One day, a new kid named Timmy came to the class. Timmy was shy and did not talk much. The other kids wanted to play with Timmy, but he just looked down and did not say anything. Miss Lily saw this and had an idea. Miss Lily said, "Class, let's all bow and say hello to Timmy." The kids all bowed and said, "Hello, Timmy!" Timmy smiled and bowed back. He started to talk and play with the other kids. Soon, Timmy loved to learn math, too. He and his friends had fun learning together. They all learned to be kind and compassionate, just like Miss Lily. The moral of the story is: Be kind and compassionate to others, and they will feel welcome and happy.

One day, a big van came to town. It was a fun van with lots of colors. The van had music playing loud. People came to see the van and hear the music. They were happy and excited. A little girl named Lily asked her mom, "Can we go see the van?" Her mom said, "Yes, let's go!" They walked to the van, holding hands. When they got there, Lily saw her friends. They were all dancing and laughing. Lily joined her friends and started to dance too. They danced and danced until they were dizzy. They laughed and fell on the grass. Lily's mom watched them and smiled. Lily's friend Tom said, "I love dancing! Let's dance some more!" So they all got up and danced again. The music made them feel happy. The sun went down and it was time to go home. Lily, her mom, and her friends waved goodbye to the big, colorful van. They had a great day dancing and being dizzy together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who saw a big alligator. The alligator was in a gloomy pond and he was marking the ground with his tail. The girl wanted to get closer to the alligator but she was a bit scared. Suddenly, the alligator moved towards her. She was so scared that she screamed and ran away as fast as she could! The next day, the brave girl wanted to try again. She crept closer to the alligator and even touched it! The alligator was friendly and didn't seem to mind. They became fast friends and played together every day. The little girl no longer felt gloomy around the alligator and they were always happy together! She brought him treats like apples to mark the ground with and soon they were the best of friends.

There was a little boy named Jack. One day, he was outside and wanted to go explore all the different places. He wanted to go on a bus, so he ran to the bus stop and asked his mum if he could go. She said yes, and he was so excited. He asked his mum, “How do I take a bus?” She smiled and said, “Just watch me and I’ll show you.” Then, she quickly skipped to the bus stop and put her money in the box. The bus came and the door opened. She told him, “This is a bus. It can take you anywhere you want to go.” Jack was so happy and he said, “It’s so useful! I’m going to skip to the bus stop every day!”
