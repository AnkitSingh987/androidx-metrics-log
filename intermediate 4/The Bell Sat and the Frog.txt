Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bell Sat and the Frog

One day, a big bell sat in the town. It was a nice bell. A little bug was envious of the bell. The bug wanted to be big like the bell. The bug said, "I wish I could be big like the bell." A magic frog heard the bug. The frog said, "I can help you, but you must help me too." The bug helped the frog. The frog tried to make the bug big. But the frog made a mistake. The bug began to shrink. The bug was sad and small. The bell rang and the bug was lost. The bug was not happy. The frog could not fix the mistake. The bug stayed small forever.

One day, a little girl named Amy went with her mom to the grocery store. They needed to buy food for dinner. Amy liked to help her mom pick the food. They walked up and down the store, looking at all the things to buy. Amy saw a wild bird fly into the store through an open door. She pointed at it and said, "Look, mommy! A wild bird!" Her mom said, "Oh no! We need to help the bird get out before it gets hurt." They tried to catch the wild bird, but it was too fast. It flew down the store and landed on a big pile of fruits. Amy had an idea. She told her mom, "Let's use some food to get the bird to come to us." They picked a piece of fruit and held it out for the bird. The bird saw the fruit and flew over to them. It landed on Amy's hand and ate the fruit. Then, the bird did something unexpected. It started to talk! The bird said, "Thank you for the fruit. I was lost and hungry. Can you help me find my way home?" Amy and her mom were surprised but happy to help. They took the talking bird outside and helped it find its way home. The bird thanked them and flew away. They went back to the grocery store and finished shopping. Amy couldn't wait to tell her friends about the wild bird they discovered at the grocery store.

Once upon a time, there was a scared little dog. He didn't like to leave his house because he was so scared all the time. One day, he decided that he was going to go out and explore the world. He slowly opened the door and stepped outside. He was so scared that he stayed in one spot for a long time. Suddenly, he heard a loud trumpet sound. He was so scared that he nearly ran away. But then he realized that the sound was just a person playing a trumpet. He was so relieved that he wanted to stay and listen. He slowly settled down and listened to the music. It was so beautiful and peaceful. He no longer felt scared. He felt happy and content. He would stay there and listen to the trumpet until it was time to go back home.

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play all day. They had many toys, but they always wanted more. They asked their mom and dad for new toys every time they saw them in the store. Mom and dad said no, because they had enough toys and they should be happy with what they had. One day, mom and dad had a surprise for Tom and Lily. They took them to a big park where there were many children. Tom and Lily saw swings, slides, sandboxes, and bikes. They were very excited and ran to play with everything. They did not share with the other children, and they did not say please or thank you. They wanted to try every toy and every game. After a while, Tom and Lily became tired and hungry. They looked for their mom and dad, but they could not find them. They had wandered too far from the bench where they had left them. They started to cry and feel scared. They wished they had listened to their mom and dad and stayed close to them. Then, they saw a nice lady who worked at the park. She asked them what was wrong and they told her they had lost their mom and dad. She said she would help them find them, but first they had to tell her their names and their mom and dad's names. Tom and Lily realized they did not know their mom and dad's names. They only called them mom and dad. They also did not know their phone number or their address. They felt very silly and ashamed. The nice lady said it was okay, and she took them to a big board where there were many pictures of lost children and their parents. She asked them to look for their picture and point to it. Tom and Lily looked and looked, but they could not find their picture. They wondered why their mom and dad did not put their picture on the board. They felt very sad and lonely. Then, they heard a familiar voice calling their names. It was their mom and dad! They had been looking for them all over the park. They ran to hug them and said they were sorry for running away and being greedy. Mom and dad said they were sorry too, for not putting their picture on the board. They said they had forgotten to do it, because they were so busy watching them play. They said they loved them very much and they were glad they were safe. Tom and Lily learned a lesson that day. They learned to be grateful for what they had, to share with others, to be polite, and to remember their names and numbers. They also learned to settle down and listen to their mom and dad. They said thank you to the nice lady who helped them, and they went home with their mom and dad. They played with their toys and had a nice dinner. They were very happy and tired, and they slept well that night.
