Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Toy

One day, a little girl named Mia put on her soft, warm bathrobe. She felt troubled because she could not find her favorite toy. She looked in her room, but it was not there. Mia thought her toy might be in the living room, so she decided to run there. As Mia ran, she met her big brother, Tom. She asked him, "Tom, have you seen my toy?" Tom shook his head and said, "No, Mia, I have not seen it. But I will help you look for it." They both ran to the living room to search for the toy together. After looking in the living room, they still could not find the toy. Mia felt sad, but Tom had an idea. "Let's check the kitchen," he said. They ran to the kitchen and finally found the toy under the table. Mia was so happy, and she thanked Tom for helping her. They both went back to their rooms, and Mia played with her toy, feeling happy and safe in her bathrobe.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a dependable man named Tom. Tom loved to fix things. He fixed toys, bikes, and chairs. Everyone in town knew Tom could fix anything. One day, a little girl named Sue came to Tom with her broken toy. She said, "Tom, can you fix my toy? It is not working." Tom looked at the toy and saw that it was full of waste. He knew just what to do. Tom cleaned the waste from the toy and fixed it. Sue was so happy to see her toy working again. She said, "Thank you, Tom! You are the best!" And from that day on, everyone in the town knew that dependable Tom could fix anything, even toys full of waste.

Once upon a time, there was a busy little bee named Buzzy. Buzzy loved to take pictures with his camera. One day, he saw a big, pretty flower and said, "Wow! I want to take a picture of this flower." Buzzy flew closer to the flower and took out his camera. Just then, a friendly butterfly named Flutter flew by. Flutter saw Buzzy and said, "Hi, Buzzy! What are you doing?" Buzzy replied, "I am taking a picture of this pretty flower with my camera." After taking the picture, Buzzy and Flutter decided to celebrate. They danced around the flower and sang a happy song. They had so much fun celebrating together. And from that day on, Buzzy and Flutter became the best of friends.

One sunny summer day, a busy ant named Tim was walking to his sack. He wanted to fill it with food for his family. As he walked, he met a big, friendly bear named Ben. "Hi, Tim the ant!" said Ben the bear. "What are you doing with that sack?" "Hi, Ben!" said Tim. "I am getting food for my family. We are very busy ants." As Tim and Ben talked, a loud noise came from behind them. They turned around and saw a big bird named Bob. Bob looked at Tim's sack and said, "I want that sack!" Tim was scared, but Ben stood up tall. "No, Bob! That sack is not for you. It is for Tim's family." Bob looked sad, but then he had an idea. "I can help you fill the sack, Tim. Then we can all share the food." Tim and Ben liked the idea. So, they all worked together to fill the sack with food. In the end, they all became good friends and had a fun summer.

One day, a big vehicle came to the park. It was a bus. It was adorable with many colors. The bus was full of toys for kids to play with. The kids in the park were very happy. A little girl named Mia saw the bus and ran to it. She saw a cute doll inside the bus. She wanted to play with the doll. Mia asked the bus driver, "Can I play with the doll, please?" The bus driver smiled and said, "Yes, you can play with the doll. But remember to share with your friends." Mia nodded and took the doll. She played with the doll and had fun. Soon, Mia saw her friend, Tom. Tom wanted to play with the doll too. Mia remembered what the bus driver said about sharing. So, Mia and Tom played with the doll together. They shared and had a great time. The adorable bus brought joy to all the kids in the park.
