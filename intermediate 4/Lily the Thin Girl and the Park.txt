Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Thin Girl and the Park

One day, a thin girl named Lily went to the park to gather flowers. She loved the pretty colors and smells of nature. As she picked a red flower, she heard a tiny voice say, "Hello!" Lily looked around and saw a small bug on the flower. The bug said, "I'm Buggy! Please don't take my home." Lily felt sad and said, "I'm sorry, Buggy. I didn't know this was your home." Lily and Buggy became good friends. They played in the park and had fun. One day, something unexpected happened. Buggy grew big and turned into a beautiful butterfly! Now, Lily and her new butterfly friend could play together and enjoy the beauty of nature even more.

Sam and Mia were at the park with their mom. They liked to observe the ducks and the fish in the pond. Sometimes they saw a boat with people on it. The boat was big and white and made a loud noise. "Look, mom, a boat!" Sam said, pointing to the water. "Yes, it's a boat. Do you want to go on a boat someday?" mom asked. "Yes, yes, yes!" Sam and Mia said together. Mom smiled and said, "Maybe we can go on a boat for your birthday. How about that?" Sam and Mia were very happy. They hugged mom and said, "Thank you, mom, you are the best!" The next week, it was Sam's birthday. Mom took Sam and Mia to the harbor. There were many boats there. Some were small and some were big. Some were white and some were colorful. Sam and Mia observed all the boats with wonder. "Which boat do you want to go on, Sam?" mom asked. Sam looked around and saw a boat that was very colorful. It had red, blue, yellow, and green stripes. It also had a flag with a star on it. "I want to go on that boat, mom!" Sam said, pointing to the colorful boat. "Okay, let's go on that boat. It looks like fun!" mom said. They bought tickets and got on the boat. The boat was called Rainbow Star. It had a captain and a crew. They were very friendly and gave Sam and Mia hats and whistles. "Welcome aboard, mateys!" the captain said. "Are you ready for a colorful adventure?" "Yes, we are!" Sam and Mia said, blowing their whistles. The boat started to move. It went fast and slow, left and right, up and down. Sam and Mia laughed and cheered. They saw many things on the water. They saw birds and fish and other boats. They also saw a big bridge and a lighthouse. "This is the best birthday ever!" Sam said. "I'm glad you like it, Sam. You are a brave sailor!" mom said. "I love you, mom. And I love you, Mia. And I love this boat!" Sam said. "I love you too, Sam. And I love you, mom. And I love this boat too!" Mia said. They hugged each other and observed the colorful boat. They were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a big yellow ball that she loved to play with. She would keep it with her all the time, even when she went to bed. Every night, Lily would have a dream about her yellow ball. One day, Lily was playing with her yellow ball in the park. She saw a boy named Tim. Tim asked, "Can I play with your ball too?" Lily said, "Yes, we can play together." They played catch with the yellow ball and had lots of fun. That night, Lily had a happy dream about her new friend Tim and the yellow ball. In her dream, they played in a big park with lots of other kids. When she woke up, she told her mom about her dream. Her mom smiled and said, "I'm glad you made a new friend." Lily was happy and couldn't wait to play with Tim and her yellow ball again.

Once upon a time, there was a delicate butterfly named Bella. Bella loved to draw with her ink. She would draw on leaves and flowers all day long. But, Bella did not like when it rained. The rain would wash away her drawings. So, she would complain to her friends. One day, Bella was drawing with her ink when something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew her ink bottle away. Bella was very sad. She could not draw anymore. Her friends saw her sadness and wanted to help. Bella's friends, the ants, went on a journey to find the ink bottle. They found it near a big tree. The ants worked together to bring the ink back to Bella. When Bella saw her friends with the ink bottle, she was very happy. She learned that complaining does not solve problems, but friends can help when we need them.
