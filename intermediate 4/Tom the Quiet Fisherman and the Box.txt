Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Quiet Fisherman and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a quiet fisherman named Tom. He liked to catch fish in the big blue sea. Every day, he would go out on his little boat and wait for the fish to come. One day, Tom found a big box in the sea. He thought it was mail. He opened the box and saw a small fish inside. The fish was sad and wanted to go back to the sea. Tom wanted to help the small fish, so he put it back in the water. But then, something unexpected happened. The small fish grew bigger and bigger. It turned out the fish was a magic fish. The fish was so happy to be back in the sea that it gave Tom a wish. Tom wished for more fish to catch, and the magic fish granted his wish. From that day on, Tom always had lots of fish to catch, and he was never quiet again because he always had a big smile on his face.

Once there was a little boy named Jack. He loved to sing all day long. He sang about the wind, because it was so cool and fun to feel. One day Jack wanted something new and exciting to sing about. So he went looking for something cheap. Jack searched high and low, but he couldn't find anything cheap. He was starting to feel sad, until he saw a kite in the shop window. It was colorful and very cheap. He went inside and asked the shopkeeper if he could buy it. The shopkeeper said yes, and gave Jack the kite. Jack was so excited. He grabbed the kite and twirled it in his hands. The kite started to move in the air and Jack was filled with joy. He started to sing a happy song about the wind pushing the kite. His song made the wind feel even cooler and fun. Jack flew the kite all day long and sang his special song. He was so happy, and he felt so free. He was glad that he found something so cheap and special. Whenever he felt lonely or sad, he would just look at the kite and start to sing.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with paper and scissors. They cut paper into shapes and make pictures. They plan to make a big picture for their teacher. They are happy. But Sam is not happy. He is angry. He does not like to share. He wants all the paper and scissors for himself. He takes them from Lily and Ben. He says, "These are mine. Go away." Lily and Ben are sad. They do not have paper and scissors. They cannot make their picture. They say, "Sam, that is not nice. You should share. Sharing is good." Sam does not listen. He cuts paper and scissors. He makes a mess. He does not make a picture. He is bored. The teacher comes. She sees Lily and Ben. She sees Sam. She sees the paper and scissors. She says, "What is going on here?" Lily and Ben tell the teacher. They say, "We wanted to make a picture for you. But Sam took our paper and scissors. He did not share." The teacher is sad. She says, "Sam, that is not nice. You should share. Sharing is good. You made Lily and Ben sad. You made a mess. You did not make a picture. You wasted paper and scissors." Sam is ashamed. He says, "I am sorry. I was angry. I did not share. I made Lily and Ben sad. I made a mess. I did not make a picture. I wasted paper and scissors." The teacher says, "It is good that you are sorry. But you need to do something to make it better. You need to say sorry to Lily and Ben. You need to help them clean up. You need to help them make a picture. You need to learn to share. Sharing is good." Sam says sorry to Lily and Ben. He helps them clean up. He helps them make a picture. He learns to share. Sharing is good. Lily and Ben forgive Sam. They are friends again. They finish their picture. They give it to the teacher. The teacher is happy. She says, "Thank you for the picture. It is beautiful. You are good friends. You are good students. You make me happy." Lily, Ben and Sam are happy. They hug the teacher. They hug each other. They say, "We are happy. We are friends. We share. Sharing is good."
