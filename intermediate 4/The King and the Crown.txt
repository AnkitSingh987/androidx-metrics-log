Category: Intermediate
Title: The King and the Crown

Once upon a time, there was a king who had a big shiny crown. The king was restless and always walked around his castle. One day, he met a little girl named Lily. Lily looked at the king's crown and said, "Mr. King, can I remove your crown and try it on?" The king, feeling a bit tired from his restless day, said, "Okay, Lily, you can remove my crown and try it on, but only for a little while." Lily was so excited and carefully took the crown off the king's head. As she put it on her head, she felt very special. But then, something unexpected happened. A big, mean monster came into the castle! The monster saw the crown on Lily's head and wanted it for himself. He grabbed the crown from Lily and ran away. The king and Lily were very sad because the monster took the crown and they couldn't get it back. And so, the story ended with the king and Lily feeling sad and the mean monster wearing the shiny crown.

Once upon a time, there was a famous pirate named Tom. He had a big hat and a long, curly beard. He lived on a big ship with his friends. They liked to sail on the sea and find treasure. One day, Tom found a map in a bottle. The map showed the way to a secret island. Tom and his friends sailed to the island. They were very excited to find the treasure. When they got to the island, they saw a big box. Tom said, "Let's open it!" They opened the box and found many beautiful things that match. There were shiny coins, pretty necklaces, and colorful clothes. Tom and his friends were very happy. They wore the matching things and danced on the beach. They had a big party to celebrate their new treasure.

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk in the woods. She felt very tired, so she sat down under a big tree to rest. Mia looked around and saw a pretty flower. She wanted to pick it up, but she didn't know if it was safe. Mia saw a friendly squirrel nearby and asked, "Is this flower poison?" The squirrel shook its head and said, "No, it is not poison. It is safe to touch." Mia smiled and picked the flower. It smelled very nice. Mia thanked the squirrel and continued her walk. She felt less tired now. She looked at the flower and felt happy. Mia took the flower home and put it in a vase. She felt glad she had a new friend in the woods.

One day, a little boy named Tim was eager to go outside and play. He put on his shoes and ran out the door. In the yard, he saw a big pile of junk. Tim was curious and wanted to see what he could find. Tim's friend, Sam, came over and said, "Let's get some toys and play!" Tim looked at the junk and said, "I want to play with the junk. It looks fun!" Sam didn't like the junk and said, "No, junk is dirty. Let's play with our toys instead." Tim and Sam started to argue. They both wanted to play, but they couldn't agree on what to play with. Tim's mom heard them and came outside. She saw the junk and said, "Tim, we can't play with the junk. It's not safe. Let's clean it up and then you can play with Sam and your toys." Tim and Sam worked together to clean up the junk. After they were done, they played with their toys and had lots of fun. They learned that it's better to play with safe things and to listen to their moms.

Once upon a time, there was a unique bird who loved to sing. This bird was special because it could sing a poem. One day, the bird flew to a big tree where many birds lived. The bird wanted to unite all the birds with its song. The bird started to sing the poem. The other birds listened and liked the song. They all began to sing the poem too. The unique bird was happy that all the birds were singing together. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all the birds away! The unique bird was sad that the wind had stopped them from singing. But then, the bird saw that the wind had carried all the birds to a new place. Now, even more birds could hear the poem and sing together. The unique bird was happy again, as the wind had helped them unite even more friends.
