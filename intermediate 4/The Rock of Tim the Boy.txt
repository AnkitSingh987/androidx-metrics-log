Category: Intermediate
Title: The Rock of Tim the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his toy car. One day, he saw a big rock in the grass. Tim was scared of the big rock, but he wanted to play near it. Tim took his toy car and started to play near the big rock. As he played, he saw a tiny bug on the rock. The bug was rocking back and forth on the rock. Tim was not scared of the tiny bug, so he watched it rock on the rock. After a while, the tiny bug stopped rocking and went away. Tim was happy that he was not scared of the big rock anymore. He played with his toy car near the big rock all day long. From that day on, the big rock became Tim's favorite place to play.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who was very upset. She had lost her favorite towel! She didn't know what to do. She tried to think of a way to make it better. Then she had an idea. She decided to add a little bit of bit of something special to her other towels. She used some bright colors and made them look even more colorful and fun. When she was finished, she saw that the towels were much happier. She realized that it was ok to be upset sometimes, but that it's important to add a positive attitude to make things better. The little girl was so happy that she made her towels look beautiful and that it made her feel better. Now, whenever she was upset, she remembered to add something special to make things better.

One day, a big, big dog named Max saw a huge tree. The tree was so big that it blocked the view of the park. Max liked to see the whole park when he played. He wanted to prevent the tree from blocking the view. Max thought of a plan. He would push the tree with his strong legs. He pushed and pushed, but the tree did not move. Max was sad, but he did not give up. He thought of a new plan to prevent the tree from blocking the view. Max asked his friends for help. His friends were a cat, a bird, and a squirrel. They all worked together to make the tree smaller. The cat climbed the tree and the bird flew up high. They broke small branches and the squirrel helped carry them away. Soon, the tree was smaller, and the view of the park was clear. Max was very happy and thanked his friends for helping him. They all played in the park with the beautiful view.

One Sunday, a boy named Tim went outside to play. The sky was gray and it looked like it might rain. Tim wanted to have fun before the rain came, so he took his ball and went to the park. At the park, Tim saw a big, dangerous dog. He was scared, but then he saw the dog was playing with a ball too. Tim thought maybe the dog was not so dangerous after all. They started to play together, and they were having so much fun. Then, something unexpected happened. The rain started to fall, but instead of being sad, Tim and the dog danced in the rain. They laughed and played, and they were not scared of the rain or each other anymore. The dangerous dog became Tim's new friend, and they had the best Sunday ever.

Once upon a time, in a small school, there was a little pupil named Tim. Tim was a fast runner and loved to race. One day, a new kid came to the school. His name was Sam. No one knew Sam. He was unknown to everyone. Tim wanted to be friends with Sam. So, he walked up to Sam and said, "Hi, I'm Tim. Do you like to race?" Sam smiled and said, "Yes, I love to race. Can we race together?" Tim and Sam went to the playground to race. All the other kids came to watch. "Ready, set, go!" they all shouted. Tim and Sam raced as fast as they could. Everyone cheered. In the end, it was a tie. They both won the race! From that day on, Tim and Sam were best friends. They raced every day and had lots of fun together. And the unknown kid, Sam, was unknown no more.
