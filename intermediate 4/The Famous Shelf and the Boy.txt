Category: Intermediate
Title: The Famous Shelf and the Boy

Once upon a time there was a famous shelf. Everyone in town knew about it and talked about it all the time. One day a boy decided to shoot at the shelf with his toy gun. He thought it would be funny and made him look cool. But when he shot, all the books on the shelf fell down. The boy was scared! He quickly put the books back and ran away. The next day, everyone in town was talking about the boy who shot at the famous shelf. He had become famous overnight!

Once there was a boy, he was 3 years old. He enjoyed playing with his friends and his toys. One day he was playing near the river. He saw something hidden in the tall grass. He looked closer, and he saw an engine. He was very happy, and he wanted to take it home. But when he tried to move it, he heard a voice. It said, "Why are you trying to take my engine?!" The boy turned around and he saw a very angry man. The man said, "I needed this engine to fix my boat." The boy said, "I'm sorry I was trying to take your engine. Please forgive me." The man smiled and said, "I forgive you, young one. Just remember never to take things that don't belong to you." The boy thanked the man for forgiving him. And from that day on, he never forgot to say sorry when he had done something wrong.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful bird. She lived in a big tree. The bird loved to sing and play all day. One day, she saw some smoke far away. The beautiful bird was scared. The beautiful bird went to her friend, the wise old owl. The owl was sleeping in his tree. The bird woke him up and said, "Owl, I see smoke! What should we do?" The wise old owl opened his eyes and looked at the smoke. He thought for a moment. The wise old owl said, "Do not worry, beautiful bird. The smoke is from a campfire. People are camping in the woods. They will not hurt us." The beautiful bird felt better. She went back to her tree and sang a happy song. The wise old owl went back to sleep, and they both lived happily in the woods.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy dog named Bob. Bob loved to eat popcorn. One day, Bob saw his friends eating popcorn in the park. He wanted to join them. As Bob ran to his friends, he tripped on a stick. The popcorn went up in the air. Bob was sad. He thought he lost all the popcorn. But then, something unexpected happened. The birds in the park saw the popcorn in the air. They flew and caught the popcorn with their beaks. They brought the popcorn back to Bob and his friends. Bob and his friends were happy. They ate the popcorn together and played all day.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue loved to write in her book. She wrote every day. Sue had a friend named Max. Max was a reliable friend. He was always there for her. One day, Sue and Max went to the park. They played with a ball and had fun. After playing, Sue wanted to write in her book. She looked in her bag, but the book was not there. Sue and Max looked everywhere for the book. They looked under a tree, behind a bench, and in the sandbox. They could not find the book. Sue was very sad. Max felt bad too. They walked home with no book. At home, Sue cried. She missed her book. Max tried to help, but he could not find the book. That night, Sue went to bed without her book. She had a bad dream about losing her book. The next day, Sue and Max looked for the book again. But they never found it. Sue was sad and Max felt bad. They knew they should have been more careful with the book.

Once upon a time, there was a little island. The island was very boring. Nothing fun ever happened there. The animals on the island were sad. They wanted to have fun. One day, a big sack fell from the sky. The animals were very curious. They opened the sack and found many fun things inside. They played with the toys and had a great time. The next day, the animals saw another sack in the sky. They knew that something fun was going to happen again. When the sack fell, they opened it and found even more toys. The island was not boring anymore. The animals were happy and had fun every day.
