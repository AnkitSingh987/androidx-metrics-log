Category: Intermediate
Title: Fluffy the Cat and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Fluffy. Fluffy lived in an empty box. He liked his box because it was warm and safe. One day, Fluffy saw a big dog outside. The dog wanted to play with Fluffy, but Fluffy was scared. Fluffy knew he had to escape the box to get away from the big dog. He jumped out of the box and ran as fast as he could. The dog chased Fluffy, but Fluffy was too quick. He hid behind a tree, and the dog could not find him. After the dog left, Fluffy went back to his empty box. He was happy that he was able to escape. As a result, Fluffy felt brave and proud. From that day on, Fluffy knew he could face anything that came his way.

One day, a kind girl named Lucy went to the park. She saw many pretty flowers and wanted to gather them. So, she took a basket and started to pick the flowers. As Lucy gathered the flowers, she saw a beautiful view. She saw a big tree and a blue sky. She also saw birds flying high and a little pond with ducks swimming in it. Lucy was happy with her pretty flowers and the nice view. She took her basket of flowers home to show her mom and dad. They loved the flowers too. They put them in a vase and everyone was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted dog named Max. Max was very good at tying things. He loved to play with equipment like balls and ropes. One day, Max went to the park with his friends, a cat named Mimi and a bird named Titi. At the park, Max found a big rope. He wanted to tie it into a knot to show his friends. Max worked very hard, and soon he made a big knot. Mimi and Titi clapped for Max. They were very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The rope started to move! The knot Max made was not a knot at all. It was a snake! The snake was not happy. Max, Mimi, and Titi were scared. But the snake just laughed and said, "I am a nice snake. I just wanted to play too!" So Max, Mimi, Titi, and the snake all played together. And they all became good friends.

One day, a soft cat named Fluffy was playing with her ball. She saw her friend, a dog named Spot. Spot was sad. His toy was broken. It had a big hole and the stuffing was coming out. "Can you help me?" asked Spot. "I don't want my toy to be spoiled." Fluffy thought for a moment and had an idea. "I can fix it with a stitch," she said. Fluffy took a needle and thread and started to stitch the hole. But something unexpected happened! As she stitched, the toy became bigger and bigger. It was now a giant toy! Spot was so happy to see his toy fixed and even bigger. "Thank you, Fluffy!" he said. They both played with the giant toy and had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a man named Bob. Bob was very good at fixing things. He could fix toys, bikes, and even chairs. People liked him because he was so helpful. Bob was very popular in the town. One day, a girl named Sally came to Bob with a broken doll. She asked Bob to fix it. Bob said, "I can fix your doll, but I need something in trade." Sally thought for a moment and said, "I can give you a big hug!" Bob smiled and agreed to the trade. Bob fixed the doll and Sally gave him a big hug. They both were very happy with their trade. Sally told all her friends about Bob and how he fixed her doll. More and more people came to Bob for help, and he became even more popular in the town. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a red ball and a blue ball were talking. The red ball said, "I glow at night. What is your opinion on that?" The blue ball said, "I think that is fun!" At night, the red ball began to glow. The blue ball saw it and was happy. They played in the dark, and the red ball's glow made it easy to see. The blue ball wished it could glow too. Soon, the sun came up, and the red ball stopped glowing. The blue ball said, "Even though you don't glow now, we can still have fun in the sun!" And so, the red ball and the blue ball played together all day, happy to be friends.
