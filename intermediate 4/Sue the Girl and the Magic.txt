Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Magic

One day, a little girl named Sue found a pot in her yard. She was very happy and enthusiastic. She wanted to know what was inside the pot. So, she asked her friend Tom to come and help her open it. Tom came to Sue's yard and they both tried to open the pot. They pulled and pushed, but the pot would not open. Sue said, "Maybe we should ask the pot to open?" Tom thought it was a funny idea, but he said, "Okay, let's try it!" Sue asked the pot, "Will you please open?" To their surprise, the pot answered, "Yes, I will open if you say the magic word." Sue and Tom looked at each other, not knowing what the magic word was. They tried many words like "please", "open", and "now". But the pot did not open. Then, Tom remembered a word his mom always told him to say when he wanted something. He said, "Pot, will you open if I say 'thank you'?" The pot answered, "Yes, that is the magic word!" Sue and Tom said "thank you" together, and the pot opened. Inside the pot, they found many colorful toys and candy. Sue and Tom were so happy and shared their new toys and candy with all their friends. They learned that saying "thank you" is not only polite but can also be magical.

Once there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a big house with a long hall. One day, Tim fell down in the hall and hurt his knee. He felt helpless and cried. Tim's mom heard his cry and came running. She saw Tim on the floor and asked, "What happened, Tim?" Tim said, "I fell and hurt my knee." His mom hugged him and said, "Don't worry, I will help you heal." Tim's mom put a bandage on his knee and gave him a kiss. She said, "Now you will heal fast." Tim felt better and smiled. He thanked his mom and they walked down the hall together, feeling happy.

Once upon a time, there was a kind sunflower. The sunflower lived in a big garden with many friends. Every day, the sunflower would tell stories to all of the other flowers in the garden. They all loved to hear the sunflower's stories. One day, a big wind came and blew all of the flowers around. The kind sunflower tried to hold on tight, but the wind was too strong. The sunflower asked the wind to stop, but the wind did not listen. The wind blew the kind sunflower far away from the garden. The sunflower was very sad and could not find its way back home. The other flowers missed the sunflower and its stories. The sunflower was never seen again, and the garden was not the same without it.

Once upon a time, there was a young girl named Lily. Lily was very creative. She loved to draw and paint. One day, she had an idea to make a beautiful picture for her mom. She knew her mom would love it. Lily wanted to use many colors for her picture. But she didn't know where to find them. Then, she had an idea. She went to the shower and turned on the water. The water mixed with the colors on the floor, and they made a big, beautiful puddle. Lily knew she could use the puddle to make her picture. But something unexpected happened. When she put her hands in the puddle, the colors changed. They became even more beautiful! Lily was so happy. She made the most amazing picture for her mom. And they all lived happily ever after.

Anna and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to dig in the dirt and look for bugs. They had a big jar to keep the bugs in. They wanted to show them to their mom and dad. "Look, Anna, I found a big worm!" Ben said. He picked up the worm and put it in the jar. "Wow, Ben, it is very long and wet!" Anna said. She touched the worm with her finger. It wiggled and made her giggle. They dug some more and found more bugs. They found a ladybug, a spider, a beetle and a snail. They put them all in the jar. The jar was almost full. "Let's go show mom and dad our bugs!" Ben said. He picked up the jar and ran to the house. "Wait, Ben, remember to poke some holes in the lid!" Anna said. She remembered that bugs needed air to breathe. She ran after Ben with a stick. They got to the house and knocked on the door. Mom opened the door and smiled. "Hi, kids, what do you have there?" she asked. "We have a jar of bugs!" Ben said. He showed her the jar. "Wow, you have a lot of bugs!" mom said. She looked at the jar and saw the bugs crawling and moving. "Mom, can we keep them as pets?" Anna asked. Mom thought for a moment. She knew that bugs were not good pets. They needed to live in their own homes in the garden. "Kids, I'm sorry, but you can't keep the bugs as pets. They will be unhappy in the jar. They need to go back to the garden where they belong. How about we take them back and let them go?" she suggested. Anna and Ben looked at each other. They liked the bugs, but they also wanted them to be happy. "Okay, mom, we will let them go," they agreed. They followed mom to the garden and opened the jar. They watched as the bugs crawled out and went back to their homes. They said goodbye to the bugs and thanked them for letting them see them. "Bye, bugs, we hope you have a good day!" they said. They felt a little sad, but also happy. They learned a lot about bugs and had fun. They hugged mom and went back to play. They decided to look for flowers instead of bugs. They still had their jar. They filled it with colorful flowers and gave it to dad. Dad was very happy and proud. He put the jar on the table and kissed them. They all had a nice dinner and talked about their day. They remembered the bugs and the flowers and smiled.
