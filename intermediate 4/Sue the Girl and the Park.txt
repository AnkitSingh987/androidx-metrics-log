Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Park

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park. She saw a big tree with a swing. Sue was very glad. She ran to the swing and wanted to hang on it. But the swing was too high for her to reach. A tall boy named Tim saw Sue and said, "I can help you hang on the swing. But you have to promise to play with me after." Sue was happy and said, "I promise!" Tim helped Sue hang on the swing. They played together and had lots of fun. Sue was happy that she made a new friend and kept her promise. And Tim was glad he could help Sue. They played at the park until the sun went down.

Tom and Sue were very happy. They were going on a trip with Mom and Dad. They packed their bags and got in the car. Mom said they were going to a big city with many tall buildings. Tom and Sue wondered what they would see there. After a long drive, they arrived at the hotel. It was a very tall building with many windows. Mom and Dad checked in and took the elevator to their room. Tom and Sue followed them. They were excited to see their room. They opened the door and gasped. The room was very big and nice. It had two beds, a sofa, a TV, and a table. But the best thing was the window. It was very big and had a beautiful view of the city. They could see many lights, cars, and people. They could also see a big wheel that went round and round. It was a ferris wheel. "Wow!" Tom said. "Look at that!" "Can we go there, Mom?" Sue asked. Mom smiled and nodded. "Yes, we can. That's a surprise I have for you. We are going to the amusement park tomorrow. It has many fun rides and games. Do you like that?" Tom and Sue clapped their hands and hugged Mom. "Yes, yes, yes!" they said. "Thank you, Mom! You are the best!" Mom hugged them back and said, "You're welcome, my loves. I'm glad you like it. Now, let's have some dinner and get some rest. We have a big day ahead of us." Tom and Sue agreed. They were very happy and couldn't wait for tomorrow. Mom had revealed a wonderful surprise for them. They loved their hotel and their trip. They knew they would have a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to play with her toys and draw pictures at her desk. One day, she wanted to go outside and play with her friends, but her mom said she had to clean her room first. Sue started to clean her room, but she found a toy she had not seen in a long time. She played with the toy for a little while, and this made her delay cleaning her room. Her mom came in and saw that the room was still messy. She said, "Sue, you need to finish cleaning your room before you can go play." Sue felt sad, but she knew she had to finish cleaning. She put away her toys and started to clean her desk. But then, something unexpected happened! She found a secret note under her desk. The note said, "I am a magic fairy. If you clean your room, I will give you a special surprise." Sue was very excited and quickly finished cleaning her room. When Sue was done, the magic fairy appeared! The fairy said, "Thank you for cleaning your room, Sue. As a reward, I will give you a special surprise." The fairy waved her wand, and suddenly, Sue's toys came to life! They all played together and had a great time. From that day on, Sue always made sure to keep her room clean, and she knew that sometimes, a little delay could lead to a big surprise.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy called Jack. He was very clever and liked to think a lot. One day, he got a magical pen that could write spells. Jack was very excited and he was about to try the pen when suddenly, a mean wizard appeared in front of him. The wizard said that he should not use the pen, as it was powerful and could give him great wisdom. Jack became scared and wanted to run away. But then, Jack thought to himself and decided he should be calm. He decided he wanted the wisdom that the pen could give him, and so, he bravely picked up the pen and closed his eyes and started to write. The wizard watched but couldn't stop him. Soon, Jack had been able to write a spell, and he could feel the wisdom of the pen filling up inside him. He thanked the wizard and ran away, learning the lesson that he should be brave and always listen to himself.
