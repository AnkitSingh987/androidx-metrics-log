Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Cat

One day, a boy named Tim and his cat, Sam, were in their house. Tim was a silly boy who loved to play and eat chocolate. Sam was a sleepy cat who liked to nap all day. One morning, Tim woke up and saw that all his chocolate was gone! He looked at Sam and asked, "Did you eat my chocolate?" Sam shook his head and said, "No, I was sleeping." Tim was sad and wanted to find his chocolate. So, they both went outside to look for it. As they were looking, a big bird flew down and said, "I have your chocolate! I took it because I thought it was mine." Tim and Sam were surprised but happy to find the chocolate. The bird gave it back, and they all became friends. From that day on, they shared their chocolate and played together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Yesterday, Tim went for a walk with his mom. They were going to the park to play. Tim was very impatient. He wanted to get to the park quickly. As they walked, Tim started to step faster and faster. He wanted to run, but his mom told him to slow down. Tim tried to step slowly, but he was too excited. He wanted to play on the swings and the slide. Finally, they got to the park. Tim was so happy. He played on the swings and the slide all day. He had so much fun. When it was time to go home, Tim stepped slowly with his mom, tired but happy.

One Sunday, a little boy named Tim was eager to go outside and play. The sun was up, and it started to rise high in the sky. Tim put on his shoes and ran to the door. "Mom, can I go out and play?" Tim asked. His mom smiled and said, "Yes, but be back for lunch." Tim went outside and saw his friend, Sam. They played with a big, red ball. They kicked it, and it went up, up, up. The ball seemed to rise like the sun. After a while, Tim and Sam were tired. They sat down under a tree. "That was fun," said Sam. Tim agreed, and they both smiled. They were happy they could play outside on a sunny Sunday.

One sunny day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. He saw an enormous tree on the green lawn. The tree was very big and had many leaves. Tim wanted to climb the tree, but he was too small. So, he decided to play under the tree instead. Tim played with his ball, kicking it around the lawn. He was having so much fun. But after a while, he started to feel tired. His legs were sore from running and kicking the ball. Tim thought it was a good time to rest. He sat down under the enormous tree on the soft lawn. He felt the cool breeze on his face and listened to the birds singing. Tim closed his eyes and took a little nap. When he woke up, he felt much better and was ready to play again.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a tree with an incredible big root. The root was so big that all the animals liked to play on it. They loved to run, jump, and balance on the root. One sunny day, a little squirrel named Sam wanted to balance on the big root too. He saw his friends, a rabbit and a bird, playing on the root. Sam was a bit scared, but he wanted to try. He took a deep breath and started to walk on the root. At first, Sam wobbled a lot, but he did not give up. He tried again and again. Soon, he was able to balance on the incredible big root. All his friends cheered for him. Sam was very happy and proud. From that day on, Sam loved to play and balance on the big root with his friends.

Once, there was a little bunny who loved to hop. He hopped all day long. One day, he saw a big, filthy puddle. The bunny wanted to hop over it, but he was scared. He had a dream that he could hop very high and far. The bunny took a deep breath and hopped as hard as he could. He landed right in the middle of the filthy puddle! The water splashed everywhere. The bunny was sad because he was all wet and dirty. Just then, a friendly frog came by. The frog saw the sad bunny and said, "Don't worry, I can help you hop over the puddle." The frog hopped with the bunny, and together, they made it across the puddle. The bunny was so happy and thanked the frog. From that day on, the bunny and the frog became best friends, and they hopped together every day.
