Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a small, thin dog named Spot. Spot lived on an island with his friend, a big, strong cat named Fluffy. They liked to play and run all day long. One day, it was very cold on the island. Spot and Fluffy were shivering. "Brrr," said Spot. "I am so cold!" Fluffy replied, "Me too, let's find a warm place to sit." They walked and walked until they found a sunny spot. They sat down and the sun made them feel warm and happy. Spot and Fluffy stopped shivering and smiled at each other. They knew they could always find a warm place together on their island.

Once upon a time, there was a popular book. The book was a novel. Many kids loved the book. It was about a dog and a cat who were friends. They played together every day. One day, the dog said, "I want to quit playing. I am tired." The cat was sad. The cat did not want to quit. The cat liked to play with the dog. The cat said, "Please, do not quit. We can play a new game." The dog thought about it. The dog said, "Okay, let's play a new game." The cat was happy. The dog and the cat played a new game and had fun. They stayed friends and played together every day.

One morning, a little boy named Tim went for a slow walk. He saw a big tree with a small hole. Inside the hole, he found a little rock that could glow. Tim was very happy and took the rock home. At home, Tim showed the glowing rock to his sister, Sue. Sue wanted the rock too. "Give me the rock, Tim!" she said. Tim did not want to give Sue the rock. They both wanted the glowing rock and began to fight. Mom heard the fight and came to see what was wrong. She saw the glowing rock and had an idea. "Let's put the rock in the living room, so both of you can share it," Mom said. Tim and Sue agreed. Now, they could both enjoy the glowing rock together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. One day, she was looking for something to do. Suddenly, she saw some ugly paper on the floor. She picked it up and began to wrap it around her body. The girl enjoyed feeling the paper on her skin. She spun around and around until the paper was all wrapped up around her. When she stopped, the girl looked in the mirror and saw how funny she looked. She grinned and laughed. The girl went to find her friends to show them her funny, wrapped up look. They all laughed and had fun for the rest of the day playing with the paper.

One day, a little sheep named Lily was very excite. She found a big pile of wool near her home. It was soft and warm. Lily wanted to show her friend, Tim the turtle. Lily went to Tim's house. Tim was very slow, but he was a good friend. "Look, Tim! I found wool!" said Lily. Tim looked at the wool and smiled. "Wow, it is so nice!" said Tim. They played with the wool all day. They made a soft bed and a warm hat. Lily and Tim were very happy. They had lots of fun with the wool. And they were the best of friends.

Janet and Jake were best friends and they liked to spend their days together. One day they were walking together on a path in the park, surrounded by trees and flowers. Everywhere they looked, there was beauty and they enjoyed every moment. Suddenly, they heard a noise to their right. Looking over, they saw a colourful parrot perched on a branch, staring enviously at them. They both stopped and stared at the parrot, amazed by its bright feathers and its inquisitive eyes. The parrot cocked its head and flapped its wings. And then, as quickly as it appeared, it flew away! Grinning at each other, Janet and Jake continued on their path and returned home.

Anna and Ben like to play pretend. They have a big box that they use as a home. They put a blanket on top and some pillows inside. They pretend they are explorers in a distant land. One day, they hear a loud noise outside. They peek out of the box and see a big truck. The truck is moving their neighbor's things. Anna and Ben are curious. "Who is that?" Anna asks. "Maybe they are going to a new home," Ben says. "Where is their new home?" Anna wonders. "Maybe it is very far away. Maybe it is in a distant land," Ben guesses. Anna and Ben decide to go and say hello. They crawl out of the box and walk to the truck. They see a girl and a boy who are about their age. They have sad faces. "Hi, we are Anna and Ben. We live next door. What are your names?" Anna says. "I'm Lily and this is Sam. We are moving to a new home. We don't want to go," Lily says. "Why not?" Ben asks. "Because we like our old home. And we will miss our friends. And we don't know anyone in the new home. It is very distant," Sam says. Anna and Ben feel sorry for Lily and Sam. They want to cheer them up. "We can be your friends. We like to play pretend. Do you want to play with us?" Anna offers. Lily and Sam smile a little. They nod. "Okay, we can play for a little while. What do you pretend?" Lily asks. "We pretend we are explorers in a distant land. We have a home in a big box. Come and see," Ben says. Anna and Ben lead Lily and Sam to their box. They show them their blanket and pillows. They invite them to go inside. "Wow, this is a nice home. It is cozy and warm," Lily says. "Yes, it is. And we can pretend we are anywhere we want. We can pretend we are in the jungle, or the desert, or the moon," Ben says. "That sounds fun. Can we pretend we are in the jungle? I like animals," Sam says. "Sure, we can. Let's make animal noises and look for treasure," Anna says. Anna and Ben and Lily and Sam start to play pretend. They make monkey sounds and elephant sounds and snake sounds. They look for shiny things and pretend they are gold and jewels. They have a lot of fun. They forget about the truck and the new home and the distant land. They are happy in their box home. They are friends.
