Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Sun and the Mommy

Once upon a time, there was a mommy and daddy and their little girl. One day, the mommy and daddy went to the beach to have a picnic. The little girl was so excited. When they got there, she couldn't wait to get into the sand. She dug her feet and hands into the soft, warm sand. She laughed as she watched it trickle through her hands and fill her footprints again. Then, she heard the ocean. She ran to the shore and let the waves rush up and touch her toes. Suddenly, the mommy and daddy both felt an urge to go into the water. The little girl watched as they both waded in. The daddy then lifted the mommy up and threw her into the waves. She let out a squeal of delight. The little girl smiled at her mommy and daddy and felt so excited and spoiled. She knew they would have so much fun playing together in the ocean. Finally, when their day was done and the sun set, their little girl was exhausted and happy. They walked back home, happy and in love.

One day, a young boy named Tim saw a rake in the yard. He wanted to help his mom and dad. So, he picked up the rake and started to clean the leaves. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. Tim was working hard when a little bunny came. The bunny said, "Hello! Can I help you?" Tim was happy and said, "Yes! You can help me clean the leaves." The bunny used his paws to push the leaves, and Tim used the rake. They worked together. After they finished, the bunny said, "Thank you for letting me help!" Tim smiled and said, "Thank you too!" The bunny gave Tim a hug, and then he jumped away. Tim felt proud of his work and went inside to show his mom and dad. They were very proud of him too.

One day, a wise cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big wreck in the park. Tom was very curious and wanted to see what was inside the wreck. As Tom walked closer, he felt a tickle in his nose. He tried to hold it in, but he couldn't. Tom let out a big sneeze! Suddenly, the wreck started to shake and move. Tom was scared, but he couldn't run away. To Tom's surprise, the wreck was not a wreck at all. It was a big, friendly robot! The robot said, "Thank you for waking me up with your sneeze, Tom! I was stuck in sleep mode." Tom and the robot became best friends and played in the park every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was a very independent girl. Lily liked to do things all by herself. One day, she wanted to help her mom cook dinner. Her mom said, "Okay, but let me remind you to be careful." Lily was so excited. She got all the things she needed to cook. She put a big pot on the stove. She put water in the pot. Then, she put some salt in the water. She knew that salt made food taste good. Lily's mom watched her cook. She was very proud of her independent girl. When the food was ready, they ate it together. It tasted so good! Lily was happy that she could help her mom. The moral of the story is: even little kids can do big things when they try.

Once upon a time, there were two twin bunnies named Ben and Jen. They were the best of friends and did everything together. One day, they were playing in the woods when they saw a big, troubled bear. The bear looked very sad and was crying. Ben and Jen went up to the bear and asked, "Why are you so sad, Mr. Bear?" The bear said, "I lost my toy and it seemed to vanish. I can't find it anywhere." The twin bunnies wanted to help, so they searched all over the woods with the bear. After a while, they found the toy hiding behind a tree. The bear was so happy, he hugged Ben and Jen. From that day on, they all became great friends and played together in the woods every day.
