Category: Intermediate
Title: Benny and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a wise ol' bear named Benny. Benny always tried to make the best decisions. One day, he wanted to find out the best thing to do, so he decided to find his voice. He called out for help, so he could find his voice, but nobody could hear him. He kept searching and finally, he found a magic pond. He saw his reflection in the pond, and he heard his own voice. His voice was quiet at first, but then it got louder and louder. It said "Always make wise choices." Benny was so happy to hear that! He now knew what he had to do to make the best decisions. From then on, Benny always listened to his own wise voice. He never made a bad decision again. The end.

One day, a good cop was walking down the street. He saw a little boy. The little boy was sad. The cop asked, "Why are you sad?" The little boy said, "My toy is stuck in the tree." The cop looked up and saw the toy. The cop wanted to help the boy. He tried to pinch the toy with his fingers, but it was too high. Then, something unexpected happened. A big bird flew down and picked up the toy. The bird gave the toy back to the little boy. The boy was happy, and the cop was happy too. The bird was the good cop's new friend.

Tommy was an adventurous 3-year-old. One day, he decided to go for a scooter ride. He put on his helmet, then ran outside to his green scooter. As he rode around, he heard the chirping of a little bird. He wanted to see the bird, but it was too quick. He kept riding, listening to the sounds of nature. He heard the buzzing of bees, and crackling of leaves. When Tommy got tired, he stopped his scooter in front of his house. He was happy to have heard so many fun sounds on his ride. The next day, Tommy was ready for another scooter ride on his green scooter. He couldn't wait to hear what new sounds he would discover!

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a loyal dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to play with his friends, a cat named Whiskers and a bird named Chirpy. They were the best of friends and always had fun together. One day, they found a big stick in the forest. Buddy thought it was a weapon. He picked it up with his mouth and started to bow to his friends. Whiskers and Chirpy didn't know what to do. They thought Buddy was trying to fight them. Buddy saw that his friends were scared. He put down the stick and said, "I'm sorry, I didn't want to scare you." Whiskers and Chirpy were happy to hear that. They all hugged and played together again. From that day on, they knew that they would always be loyal friends, no matter what.

One day, a helpful boy named Tim went to his backyard to play. He saw a big box with a note on it. The note said, "Mail this box for me, please. Love, Mom." Tim wanted to be helpful, so he took the box to the mailbox. On his way, Tim met his friend, Sue. Sue asked, "What's in the box?" Tim said, "I don't know. Mom wants me to mail it." They both were very curious about the box, so they opened it. Inside the box, they found a big cake! "Wow!" said Sue, "Let's eat the cake!" They ate the cake and it was yummy. But then, they remembered they were supposed to mail the box. They felt bad for eating the cake. So, they made a new cake together and put it in the box. They mailed the box and went back to the backyard to play. Later, Mom came outside and said, "Thank you for mailing the box, Tim. It was a surprise cake for you and Sue to share!" Tim and Sue looked at each other and laughed. The surprise was on them, but they still had fun and were helpful friends.
