Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Bird and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim lived in a small cage. He did not like the cage. He wanted to fly high in the sky. Tim felt sad in the cage. One day, a kind girl named Lily saw Tim. "Why are you sad?" she asked. Tim said, "I hate this cage. I want to fly outside." Lily felt bad for Tim. She wanted to help him. Lily opened the cage door. Tim flew out of the cage. He was so happy. The cage was now empty. Tim and Lily played together every day. They became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to play outside and find new things. One day, Amy went to the park with her mom. They had a lot of fun playing on the swings and the slide. While Amy was playing, she saw something shiny near a tree. She walked over and found a horn. The horn was not too big, but it was very nice. Amy showed her mom the horn and they decided to take it home. Amy's mom told her that the horn was reliable, which means it was strong and good. Amy was very happy with her new horn. She played with it every day and showed it to all her friends. The horn became her favorite toy, and she always kept it close by.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a troubled boy because he did not like to look in the mirror. He thought he looked funny and it made him sad. One day, Tim went for a walk in the woods. He saw a big tree and climbed it. When he got to the top, he saw a big mirror hanging on a branch. Tim was scared, but he looked into the mirror anyway. When he looked into the mirror, he saw a happy boy, not a sad one. The mirror was magic! It made Tim feel better and not suffer anymore. From that day on, Tim was a happy boy who liked to look in the mirror. The magic mirror in the woods had changed his life.

Lily loved cookies. She loved to smell them, to see them, and to eat them. But she had to be careful. Mommy said cookies were a treat, not a meal. She could only have one cookie a day, after dinner. One day, Lily saw a big jar of cookies on the kitchen counter. They looked so yummy and crunchy. She wanted to have one right now. She looked around. Mommy was not in the kitchen. She was in the living room, reading a book. Lily had an idea. She grabbed a chair and pushed it to the counter. She climbed on the chair and reached for the jar. But the jar was too heavy for Lily. As she tried to lift it, it slipped from her hands and fell to the floor. The jar broke into pieces and the cookies scattered everywhere. Lily was scared. She heard Mommy coming. "Oh, Lily, what have you done?" Mommy said, seeing the mess. "You were not careful. You could have hurt yourself. And you wasted all the cookies. Now we have none left for later." Lily felt sorry. She knew she had been naughty. She wanted to make Mommy happy, not angry. She said, "I'm sorry, Mommy. I was hungry. I wanted a cookie." Mommy sighed. She hugged Lily and said, "I know, sweetie. But you have to wait for the right time. Cookies are not good for you if you have too many. And you have to be careful when you use the chair. You could fall and get hurt. Next time, ask me first, okay?" Lily nodded. She said, "Okay, Mommy. I will be careful. And I will ask you first. Can I have a cookie now?" Mommy smiled. She said, "No, Lily. You have to wait until after dinner. But I have something else for you. Come with me." Mommy took Lily to the backyard. There, she saw a big kite. It was colorful and had a long tail. Mommy said, "This is for you, Lily. We can fly it together. It will soar high in the sky, like a bird. Do you like it?" Lily's eyes lit up. She said, "Yes, Mommy. I love it. Thank you. Can we fly it now?" Mommy said, "Yes, we can. Come on, let's go." Lily and Mommy ran to the field. They held the kite and let the wind catch it. The kite soared high in the sky, like a bird. Lily laughed and clapped. She forgot about the cookies. She was happy. She learned that being careful and patient was better than being greedy and reckless. And she learned that flying a kite with Mommy was more fun than eating a cookie alone.
