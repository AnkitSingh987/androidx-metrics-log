Category: Intermediate
Title: Beth and the Car

Beth and her family went on an adventure! They drove to a big park and Beth was so excited to get there. Beth's mom and dad started to unpack their things from the car. First, Mom put up a tent for everyone to sleep in. Then Dad took out the picnic blankets so the family could all sit together. Beth was so excited to explore! Mom and Dad gave her a special pass so she could go on all the rides she wanted. Beth couldn't wait to try them all! At the end of the day, Beth was feeling very tired. She had had so much fun on the rides and running around the park, but now it was time to go back to the tent. Beth and her family all packed up the blankets and stuffed them back in the car. It had been such a fun day! Beth fell asleep in the car on the way home, dreaming of the rides she went on and all the fun she had.

Once upon a time, there was a slow monkey named Tom. He lived in a big tree with his family. Tom loved to play with his friends, but he was not very fast. One day, Tom found a magic zip. He put it on and suddenly, he could run very fast. Tom was so happy! He zipped around the tree and played with his friends all day long. At the end of the day, Tom took off the magic zip and went back to being a slow monkey. He learned that it was okay to be slow, because he still had lots of fun with his friends. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. He lived in a small house with his cat, Spot. Tom was sad because he did not have any friends. One day, Tom received a letter with a symbol on it. The symbol looked like a big, red heart. Tom was happy because he thought it meant someone wanted to be his friend. He followed the directions on the letter to find his new friend. Tom walked a long way and finally found a big tree. Under the tree, there was a box with the same red heart symbol. Tom was so excited to meet his new friend. But when he opened the box, a big snake jumped out! The snake scared Tom, and he ran away. Tom was sad again because he still had no friends.

One day, a lazy cat named Tom saw a little mouse. Tom wanted to play with the mouse. The mouse was scared, but Tom said, "Don't be scared, let's play!" They played and had fun. Then, they noticed a big door. The door led to the attic. They went inside the attic to explore. It was dark and dusty. In the attic, they found a big box. Tom and the mouse opened the box. Inside, there was a mean dog. The dog jumped out and chased Tom and the mouse. They tried to run away, but the dog caught them. The dog was very happy, but Tom and the mouse were not.

Once upon a time, there was a cat named Tom. Tom was a selfish cat. He did not like to share his toys. One day, Tom found a ball of yarn. The yarn was special. It could glow in the dark. Tom played with the glowing yarn every night. He did not let other animals play with it. The other animals were sad. They wanted to play with the glowing yarn too. One day, a wise owl saw Tom playing alone. The owl told Tom to share the glowing yarn with others. Tom listened to the owl and started sharing the glowing yarn. All the animals played together and had fun. Tom was not selfish anymore. The moral of the story is to share and be kind to others.

Once upon a time, there was a big bull. The bull was very slow. He lived in a farm with other animals like cows, pigs, and chickens. One day, the bull wanted to escape the farm. He wanted to see the world outside. So, he walked and walked, and finally, he got out of the farm. The bull was so happy to escape. He saw many new things. He saw a big tree, a little bird, and even a fast car. After a while, the bull got tired. He missed his friends on the farm. So, the slow bull went back to the farm, happy to be home with his friends.
