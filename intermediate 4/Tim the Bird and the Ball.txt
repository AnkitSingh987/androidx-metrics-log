Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Bird and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim was an adventurous bird who loved to fly and get things. One day, Tim saw a post with a shiny red ball on top. Tim wanted to get the shiny red ball, but the post was very tall. He tried to fly up, but he was scared. Tim's mom told him, "Be brave, Tim! You can do it!" So, Tim tried again. He flew up, up, up, and got the shiny red ball! Tim was so happy and proud. He showed the ball to his mom and friends. They all cheered for him. Tim learned that if he was brave and tried his best, he could do anything. And that is the moral of the story: always be brave and try your best!

One day, a smart dog named Sam was in his bed. He heard a big sound and it made him wake up. Sam went outside to see what the sound was. He saw a little cat in a tree. The cat was very scared. Sam said, "Don't worry, I will help you!" Sam used his strong paw to push the tree. The tree moved a little, but the cat was still stuck. Then, something unexpected happened. A big bird flew down from the sky. The bird said, "I can help you!" The bird picked up the cat with its beak and put it safely on the ground. Sam and the cat were very happy. They thanked the bird and became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a noisy puppy named Spot. Spot loved to run and play in the yard. One day, Spot found a big stick. He picked it up and began to chew on it. Spot's friend, a little boy named Tim, came outside. He saw Spot with the stick and said, "Spot, that stick is too big for you!" But Spot did not listen. He kept chewing on the big stick. The next day, Spot and Tim were playing in the yard again. Spot still had the big stick. Suddenly, the stick broke in half! Spot and Tim laughed and laughed. Now, Spot had two sticks to chew on. And they were just the right size for him.

One day, a girl named Lily and her mom went for a walk. Lily was a funny daughter who loved to step and jump. As they walked, they saw a big dog. The dog was not happy. It barked loud and scared Lily. Lily said, "Mom, I am scared of the dog. What do we do?" Her mom said, "Don't worry, we will step back and go another way." So they turned around and walked away from the big dog. As they walked, they found a small cat. The cat was funny, just like Lily. The cat did a little dance and made Lily laugh. The cat was lost and needed help. Lily and her mom took the cat home, and they all became friends. The big dog was not a problem anymore, and Lily was happy she could help the funny cat.

One day, a little bee went to visit his friend, a butterfly. The butterfly lived in a hidden place behind a big tree. The bee flew and flew until he found the tree. He was very happy to see his friend. But when the bee got to the hidden place, he saw that the butterfly was sad. The butterfly said, "I lost my pretty flower. I can't find it anywhere." The bee wanted to help his friend. So, the bee and the butterfly looked for the flower together. They searched near the tree, and then near the water. Finally, they found the pretty flower in the tall grass. The butterfly was so happy, and they both played together all day.

Frank was walking through the forest and it was very hot. Suddenly, he heard a language he had never heard before. He looked around and to his surprise, a strange creature appeared. Frank was very scared and started to run away. But then, he looked back and the strange creature seemed friendly. Frank was brave and decided to talk to the creature. "What is your name?", he asked. The creature said: "I am an alien and I don’t speak your language". Frank was so surprised and they became friends. Every day, they went to the forest and talked about the stars. But one day, the creature wasn’t there. Frank was sad because his new friend was gone, so he looked for him. He walked for hours but couldn’t find him. Suddenly, he heard a loud bang, like something had exploded. He was very scared and started to run away. But then, it was too late. A giant spaceship appeared and Frank was taken away. He was never seen again.
