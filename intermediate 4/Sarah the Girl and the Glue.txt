Category: Intermediate
Title: Sarah the Girl and the Glue

Once upon a time, there was a kind little girl named Sarah. She was very excited to make something for her mum. She took out some glitter, glue and paper and was ready to get started. Just as she was about to start gluing the glitter on the paper, her older brother walked into the room. He had a mischievous smile on his face and said "I dare you to use all of the glue!" Sarah was scared and hesitated, so her brother added "If you don't do it, I'm not going to let you show mummy what you made". Sarah was so determined to make something special for her mum, so she bravely grabbed the glue and started to pour it onto the paper. It made a huge mess but Sarah persisted and eventually created something beautiful. Mummy was so impressed with what her daughter had done and said "You are very talented! I'm so excited to see what you create next." Sarah smiled happily. She was very proud of herself for facing the challenge and was looking forward to the next one!

One sunny day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk in the park. He saw a fat cat sitting under a tree. Tim walked over to the cat and said, "Hello, fat cat! What are you doing here?" The fat cat looked up at Tim and said, "I'm trying to discover a way to climb this tree. There's a big vine up there, and I want to play with it." Tim wanted to help the fat cat, so he looked around and found a big stick. He gave the stick to the cat and said, "Here, use this to help you climb the tree!" The fat cat used the stick to climb the tree and reach the vine. The cat played with the vine and had lots of fun. Tim was happy that he could help the fat cat, and they both became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Max. Max lived on a ranch with his family. They had lots of fun together. Max liked to run and play in the grass. One day, Max saw a big red ball. "Let's make a game!" said Max's friend, Tom the cat. They started to play with the ball. They would kick it and chase it all around the ranch. They laughed and played for hours. But then, something unexpected happened. The ball rolled into a big hole in the ground. Max and Tom looked at each other, surprised. They didn't know what to do. Then, a friendly bunny hopped out of the hole with the ball in its mouth. Max and Tom were so happy! They thanked the bunny and went back to their fun game.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play outside. One day, they see a bright rainbow in the sky. It has many colors: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and purple. "Wow, look at the rainbow!" Anna says. "It is so pretty!" "I like the rainbow too," Ben says. "Do you want to give it a hug?" Anna laughs. "How can we give the rainbow a hug? It is too far away." Ben thinks. He has an idea. He runs to his house and comes back with a big sheet of paper and some crayons. He gives them to Anna. "Let's make our own rainbow!" he says. "We can draw it on the paper and then hug it." Anna likes Ben's idea. They sit on the grass and start to draw. They use all the crayons to make a bright rainbow on the paper. They are very happy. "Look, we did it!" Anna says. "We made a rainbow!" They hold the paper and give it a big hug. They feel warm and cozy. "We gave the rainbow a hug!" Ben says. "And the rainbow gave us a hug too!" They smile and look at the sky. The bright rainbow is still there, shining over them. They wave to it and say thank you. They are the best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a friend named Tom. They loved to play and talk all day. They talked about everything, from their toys to their pets. One day, Lily and Tom went to play in the park. It was a cold day, and the air felt icy. They saw a big tree and decided to climb it. When they reached the top, they found a secret room in the tree. Inside the room, they saw a ceiling with many colors. Lily and Tom were amazed by the colorful ceiling. They talked about the colors and how they got there. They played in the secret room until it was time to go home. Lily and Tom promised to come back and visit the secret room again. And so, they had a new place to play and talk with their favorite colorful ceiling.
