Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and the Park

One day, Tom went to visit his friend, Sam. Sam had a big, shiny motorcycle. Tom liked the motorcycle a lot. He wanted to have one too. He felt envious of Sam. Sam saw that Tom liked his motorcycle. He asked Tom if he wanted to go for a ride. Tom was very happy. They put on their helmets and got on the motorcycle. They rode the motorcycle around the park. Tom had so much fun. He was not envious anymore. He was just happy to ride with his friend, Sam. They had a great day together.

---

One day, a lovely cat named Lucy went to a restaurant with her mom. Lucy and her mom sat at a table. They looked at the menu to pick what they wanted to eat. "Mom, I wonder what I should eat," said Lucy. Her mom smiled and said, "How about a yummy fish?" Lucy thought about it and said, "Yes, I would like a yummy fish, please!" When they finished eating, Lucy and her mom went home. They had a fun day and a nice meal.

Once upon a time, in a cheerful little town, there lived a bright sun who loved to shine. Every day, the sun would shine down on the town, making everyone happy. The sun had a good friend, the fire, who also loved to shine and keep people warm. One day, while the sun was shining, something unexpected happened. A big cloud came and covered the sun, making the town dark. The people were sad and cold. The sun tried to shine, but the cloud was too big and strong. Then, the fire had an idea. It asked the wind to help blow the big cloud away. The wind agreed and blew the cloud away from the town. The sun could shine again, and the people were happy and warm. The sun and the fire became heroes in the cheerful little town.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to his classroom. It was a big room with lots of chairs and tables. Tim saw his friend, Sue. Sue looked sad. The sun outside was very bright and harsh. It made Sue's eyes hurt. Tim wanted to help Sue feel better. He said, "Sue, do you want my hat? It will help with the bright sun." Sue looked at the hat and smiled. "Yes, please," she said. Tim gave Sue his hat. They played in the classroom together. Tim and Sue were happy. They knew that friends always offer help when someone is sad.

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play outside. One day, they went to the pond with their mom and dad. They saw many animals at the pond, like ducks, frogs, and fish. "Look, mom, a duck!" Tom said, pointing at a white duck with a yellow beak. "Quack, quack!" the duck said, swimming in the water. "Can we feed the duck, dad?" Lily asked, holding a piece of bread. "Sure, but be careful. Don't get too close to the pond. It is deep and wet," dad said, breaking the bread into small pieces. Tom and Lily threw some bread to the duck, who ate it happily. Then they saw a frog on a lily pad. The frog was green and flexible. It could jump high and far. "Wow, a frog!" Lily said, clapping her hands. "Ribbit, ribbit!" the frog said, jumping to another lily pad. "Can we catch the frog, mom?" Tom asked, reaching for the frog. "No, Tom, leave the frog alone. It is not a toy. It is a living thing. It has feelings and needs," mom said, holding Tom's hand. "But I want to play with the frog," Tom said, pouting. "Maybe you can play with something else. Look, there is a fish in the pond. It is shiny and colorful. Do you see it?" mom said, pointing at a fish. "Yes, I see it. It is pretty," Tom said, smiling. "Can we touch the fish, dad?" Lily asked, leaning over the pond. "No, Lily, you can't touch the fish. It is too fast and slippery. And you might fall into the pond. That would be bad," dad said, pulling Lily back. "But I want to touch the fish," Lily said, whining. "Maybe you can touch something else. Look, there is a stick on the ground. It is brown and hard. Do you want it?" dad said, picking up a stick. "Yes, I want it. Thank you, dad," Lily said, taking the stick. Tom and Lily played with the stick, pretending it was a sword, a wand, or a microphone. They had fun with their imagination. They sang, danced, and told jokes. Mom and dad watched them and laughed. They were proud of their twins. They applauded them and hugged them. "Good job, Tom and Lily. You are very creative and funny. We love you very much," mom and dad said. "We love you too, mom and dad. And we love the pond and the animals. Thank you for taking us here," Tom and Lily said. The end.
