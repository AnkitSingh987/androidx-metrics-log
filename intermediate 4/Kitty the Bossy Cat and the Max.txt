Category: Intermediate
Title: Kitty the Bossy Cat and the Max

Once upon a time, there was a bossy cat named Kitty. She liked to tell all the other animals what to do. One day, Kitty met a little dog named Max. Kitty said to Max, "You have to listen to me because I am the boss." Max did not like this, but he did not want to suffer. So, Max said, "Okay, I will listen." One day, Max had a hurt ear. It was very bad. Max was sad. Kitty saw Max was sad and felt bad for being bossy. Kitty said, "I am sorry, Max. I will help you." Kitty and Max went to find help for Max's ear. They found a nice lady who helped Max. Max's ear felt better. Kitty and Max were happy and became good friends. From that day on, Kitty was not bossy anymore.

Once upon a time there was a horse named Blue. She loved to show off her coat of many colors. But one day, when Blue was out enjoying herself, a foolish cow laughed at her brightly colored coat. "Ha ha! What a silly color!" the cow said. Blue was very angry. She said loudly, "My colors are not silly! I think they are beautiful!" The cow thought for a moment and then said, "Maybe they are beautiful, but I still think it's foolish for a horse to be so colorful!" Blue pawed the ground and said, "It's not foolish! I like my colors and I'm proud of them!" The cow laughed and said, "Suit yourself." And that's exactly what Blue did. From then on she kept her coat of many colors and was proud of how she looked.

One day, a wet witch went for a walk. She saw a big tree. Under the tree, there was a little girl. The girl was sad. The wet witch wanted to help her. "Hello, little girl," said the wet witch. "Why are you sad?" "I want to spin," said the girl. "But I am too small." The wet witch had an idea. She took the girl's hand. They started to spin. Round and round they went. The girl was happy. The wet witch and the girl spun and spun. They laughed and had fun. The sun came out and the wet witch was dry. The girl was not sad anymore. They were friends.

Lily was an adventurous girl. She liked to explore new places and try new things. One day, she saw a big machine in the park. It had wheels, buttons, and a long arm. Lily wanted to see what it could do. She climbed on the machine and looked around. She saw a button that said "push". She wondered what would happen if she pushed it. She pushed the button and the machine made a loud noise. The arm moved and picked up a big rock. Lily was amazed and happy. But then she saw a man running towards her. He looked angry and scared. He shouted, "Stop! That's my machine! You can't play with it!" He reached the machine and tried to take Lily off. But Lily did not want to leave. She pushed the button again and the arm swung around. The rock flew in the air and landed on the man's head. He fell down and groaned. Lily laughed and said, "That was fun!" She jumped off the machine and ran away. She looked for another adventure. She did not see the man get up and chase her. He wanted his machine back. He wanted to teach Lily a lesson. But Lily was too fast and too adventurous. She always found a way to escape.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to polish her mirror every day. She liked to see her face in the shiny mirror. One day, while polishing her mirror, she saw a yummy cake in the reflection. Lily was very hungry and wanted to eat the cake. Lily looked around and saw the yummy cake on the table. She knew she should ask her mom if she could eat it. But Lily was too hungry to wait. She took a big bite of the cake. Suddenly, the cake was gone! Lily was sad and confused. Later, Lily's mom came into the room with a big smile. She told Lily the yummy cake was a magic cake for sharing with friends. Lily learned that it is important to ask before taking something. She promised to always ask in the future. And guess what? The magic cake came back, and Lily shared it with her friends. They all had a fun and yummy time together.
