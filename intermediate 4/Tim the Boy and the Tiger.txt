Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Tiger

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a long slide and ran to play on it. Tim went up and down the slide many times. He was very happy. Then, Tim saw a tiger near the trees. The tiger was big and had long stripes. Tim was not scared. He thought the tiger was nice. So, Tim waved at the tiger and said, "Hi, tiger!" The tiger saw Tim wave and came closer. The tiger was friendly and waved back with its big paw. Tim and the tiger played together all day. They had lots of fun at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a big pink frog. The frog liked to splash in a small pond. The pond was his favorite place to play. One day, the pink frog found a hammer near the pond. He picked it up and looked at it. He thought it was a fun toy. The frog decided to use the hammer to make a big splash in the pond. He hit the water with the hammer and made a big splash! The water went up, up, up in the air. The pink frog was very happy. He played with the hammer every day, making big splashes in the pond. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a red fire truck. The fire truck was very good at rescuing people. One day, a little girl named Sue was playing with her ball near a big tree. The ball got stuck in the tree. Sue tried to get it, but she got stuck too. She held on to the tree with her waist, but she was scared. Sue called for help, "Help me, please! I'm stuck!" The red fire truck heard Sue's call and came to rescue her. The fire truck used its long ladder to reach Sue. The fireman climbed up and saved her. Sue was so happy and thanked the fireman. Sue learned that it's important to be careful when playing near big trees. She also learned that there are kind people, like the fireman and the red fire truck, who will help when you need it. The moral of the story is to always be careful and to ask for help when you need it.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily loved to study. She had a big book with pictures of animals. She liked to look at the pictures and learn their names. Lily also had a pretty dress. It was her favorite dress. It was blue and had flowers on it. She wore it every time she studied. One day, as Lily was studying, her friend, a talking cat named Whiskers, came to visit. Whiskers was an honest cat. He always told the truth. "Lily, I have a secret," said Whiskers. "What is it?" asked Lily. Whiskers whispered, "I can make your dress do something special." Lily was excited. "What can it do?" she asked. Whiskers tapped the dress with his paw, and suddenly, the flowers on the dress came to life! They danced and sang while Lily studied. Lily and Whiskers laughed and clapped as they watched the flowers dance. From that day on, studying was even more fun for Lily, and she always wore her special dress.

Once upon a time, there was a big round ball. The ball was sad because it could not move by itself. One day, a little boy named Tim saw the ball and wanted to help it. Tim pushed the round ball, and they played together all day. But one day, something unexpected happened. A little girl named Sue came and took the round ball away. Tim felt guilty because he did not help the ball. He was sad and missed his friend, the round ball. Then, a twist happened! Sue came back with the round ball and a new friend, a square box. Sue said, "Let's all play together and help each other have fun!" Tim, Sue, the round ball, and the square box played happily together. Everyone helped each other, and they all became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat named Tom. Tom had a friend named Sue, who was a little mouse. Tom and Sue liked to play in the park. One day, Tom found a silver coin on the ground. He was very happy. Tom showed the silver coin to Sue. Sue said, "Wow, that is pretty! I wish I had one too." Sue was envious of Tom's silver coin. Tom wanted to make Sue happy, so he had an idea. Tom told Sue, "Let's play a game. If you can find where I lie down and hide the silver coin, you can have it." Sue agreed to play the game. Tom hid the coin under a big tree and lied down on top of it. Sue looked and looked, and finally found Tom and the silver coin. Sue was so happy to have the silver coin. She thanked Tom and said, "You are a good friend, Tom. Now we both can be happy with our shiny silver coin." Tom and Sue continued to play in the park and share the silver coin, and they were the best of friends.
