Category: Intermediate
Title: The Farm and the Barn

Once upon a time, there was a farm. On the farm, there was a big, red barn. In the barn, there lived many animals. They were all very happy. One day, a dog named Spot and a cat named Whiskers were talking. Spot said, "I am a reliable friend." Whiskers said, "I am a reliable friend, too." They both smiled. They decided to play a game. They had to find a hidden toy. They looked and looked. Then, they had to pause. They heard a noise. It was a big, unexpected surprise! A new animal friend had come to the farm. They all played happily together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sarah who liked to bake. She especially loved making dough and planned to make some cookies. First, she went to ask her mom if she would permit it. But, mom said no. Sarah was very sad and started to cry. She said, "Mom, I just want to bake some cookies. Please permit me to make the dough?" Mom answered, "No sweetie, dough can be a bit too interesting to make. I cannot permit it." Sarah was still very sad, but she knew her mom was right. She said, "Ok, I understand. Maybe I can help you make dinner instead?" Mom smiled and said, "Yes, that sounds like a great idea! Let's make something special for dinner together." And so Sarah helped her mom make dinner and it was really fun. They laughed and talked and made the most delicious meal. Sarah realized that although she couldn't have the dough, there were still many interesting things she could do.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play at the park. Her favorite thing to do was to slide down the big slide. One sunny day, she went to the park with her mom. At the park, Lily met a new friend named Tim. Tim had a soft, green mint in his hand. He said, "Hi, Lily! Do you want to share my mint and play on the slide together?" Lily was happy and said, "Yes, let's play together!" Lily and Tim played on the slide and shared the mint. But they didn't listen to their moms when they were told to be careful. They slid down the slide too fast and bumped into each other. They both fell and got hurt. Their moms had to take them home. The moral of the story is to always listen to your mom and be careful when you play.

Lily and Max are twins. They like to play in the park. They see a big slide. They want to go on the slide. "Me first!" Lily says. She runs to the slide. She grabs a seat. The seat is high. She is scared. "Wait for me!" Max says. He runs after Lily. He grabs another seat. The seat is high too. He is brave. "Ready, set, go!" Max says. He pushes his seat. He slides down fast. He laughs. Lily pushes her seat too. She slides down slow. She cries. Max waits for Lily at the bottom. He hugs her. "It's okay, Lily. I'm here. The slide is fun. Do you want to try again?" Max says. Lily nods. She wipes her tears. She smiles. "Okay, Max. Let's try again. But this time, you go slow and I go fast." Lily says. They run back to the slide. They grab their seats. They slide down together. They are happy.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a fragile little girl named Lily. She had a pet bird named Blue. Lily and Blue were best friends. They played and laughed together every day. One day, Lily smelled smoke. "Oh no, Blue!" she said, "There is smoke in the house!" Lily was scared. She held Blue tight and started to pray. "Please, help us be safe from the smoke," she whispered. Lily and Blue went outside. They waited and waited. Soon, the smoke went away. They were so happy and thankful. "We are safe now, Blue," said Lily with a big smile. They went back inside the house and played together, knowing that their prayers had been answered.
