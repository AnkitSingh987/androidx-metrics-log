Category: Intermediate
Title: The Huge Pillow and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a huge pillow. It was so big and soft. The pillow could talk and had many friends. One day, the pillow had to deliver a small gift to a friend far away. On the way, the pillow met a mean dog. The dog wanted to take the gift. The pillow said, "No, this is for my friend." The dog was angry and tried to bite the pillow. The pillow was scared but did not want to give up. The pillow thought of a plan. It asked the dog, "Do you want to be my friend?" The dog was surprised but said yes. The pillow said, "If we are friends, you can share the gift with me." The dog agreed, and they both delivered the gift together. The pillow, the dog, and the friend were all happy.

- Once upon a time there was a small child named Sammy. Sammy wanted to go explore the world, so one day he went outside to discover. He walked and walked until he found a beautiful rug in the middle of the field. He was very excited and snatched the rug before any of his friends could get it. Back at home, Sammy proudly presented the rug to his mom. But his mom scolded him and said the rug was not his to take. Sammy felt embarrassed and sad. After thinking a while, Sammy realized that it was wrong to be selfish and take the rug without asking. So he decided to go back to the field and find the owner of the rug. He found the farmer and apologized earnestly. The farmer was very pleased and thanked Sammy for returning it. Sammy was glad he realized the importance of not being selfish and learned a valuable lesson that day!

Once upon a time, there was a little bear who saw a branch high up in a tree. The little bear was excited and wanted to get the branch. He asked his friend, a squirrel, for help. The squirrel was nervous, she wasn’t sure she wanted to help the bear. But the bear was very cute and friendly so she agreed. The squirrel suggested that the bear climb on her back, so he did and they started to climb the tree. It took them a while to reach the branch but they managed to get it. The bear was so proud! But when they started to come back down the tree, the branch, which was held tightly in the bear’s hand, broke. The little bear fell, down, down, down. He didn’t get hurt but he was so sad. The squirrel was really sorry and tried to help, but was too late. The branch was gone and the little bear was very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a light rain falling from the sky. It was very nice and soft. A little girl named Amy went outside to play. She put on her rain boots and her raincoat. She was excited to jump in the puddles. Amy took a big step and splashed in the first puddle she saw. The water went up high and then came down. She laughed and clapped her hands. She saw more puddles and jumped in them too. Each step made her smile and giggle. After a while, the rain stopped and the sun came out. Amy looked around and saw a beautiful rainbow in the sky. She was so happy to see the colors. She took one last step in a puddle and then went inside to tell her mom about her fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in his garage with his toy cars. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, let's cut some paper and make a race track for your cars!" Tim was very happy and said, "Yes, Mom! I want a big race track!" So, they went inside the house and got some paper, scissors, and tape. Tim and his mom started to cut the paper into long strips. They taped the strips together to make a big race track for his cars. Tim put the race track in the garage and started to race his toy cars. He was so happy and said, "Thank you, Mom! This is the best race track ever!" Tim played with his cars on the race track all day long, and he had a lot of fun.
