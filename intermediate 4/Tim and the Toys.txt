Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy truck that he loved to play with. Tim would use the truck to load up all his other toys and take them on great adventures. Tim's mom and dad would always watch him play and smile. One day, Tim saw his friend Sue with a broken toy. Sue was sad because her toy could not play anymore. Tim thought about his toy truck with its strong arm and had an idea. He used the truck's arm to help fix Sue's broken toy. Sue was so happy, and Tim felt great inside. From that day on, Tim and Sue played together with their toys, and they learned that helping each other was the best way to have fun. The moral of the story is that being kind and helpful to others can bring great joy to everyone.

One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to play. She asked her daddy, "Can we act like animals today?" Daddy said, "Yes, let's act like animals and have fun!" First, they acted like big bears. They walked on their hands and feet and growled. Lily laughed and said, "Daddy, you are a funny bear!" Then, they acted like hopping bunnies. They jumped up and down and wiggled their noses. Lily said, "Daddy, you are a cute bunny!" At the end of the day, Lily felt grateful for her daddy. She gave him a big hug and said, "Thank you for playing with me. I love you, Daddy." Daddy smiled and said, "I love you too, Lily. I am grateful for you." And they went inside to have dinner together, happy and tired from their fun day of acting like animals.

Once upon a time, there was a little pig. The pig lived on a farm with his friends. They played all day in the mud and the sun. The pig was very happy. One day, the pig felt sad. He did not want to play with his friends. He just wanted to sit by himself. His friends did not know what to do. They tried to make the pig happy again. Then, one of the friends had an idea. They gave the sad pig a big drink of water. The pig drank the water and felt better. The water made the pig not sad anymore. The pig and his friends played in the mud and the sun again. They were all happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to paint. One day, she found a big, bossy cat. The cat walked up to Sue and said, "I want to paint too!" Sue was very surprised. She did not know that cats could talk. But she said, "Okay, let's paint together." They started to paint a big, pretty picture. The bossy cat painted a big sun, and Sue painted a blue sky. As they painted, the bossy cat started to weep. Sue asked, "Why are you sad?" The cat said, "I am not a real cat. I am a little boy who was turned into a cat. I miss being a boy." Sue felt sad for the cat. She had an idea. She took her paint and painted the cat back into a little boy. They were both very happy and became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a quiet little town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to follow his big brother, Sam, everywhere. Sam was very nice to Tim and liked to play with him. One day, they found an ashtray in the park. "What is this?" Tim asked. "An ashtray," Sam said. They looked at it and decided to take it home. They thought it was a special treasure. At home, they showed the ashtray to Mom. "Oh, boys, this is not a toy," Mom said. "Let's put it away." Tim and Sam felt sad, but they knew Mom was right. They went outside to play and had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a graceful bird named Bella. She lived in a beautiful forest with many trees and flowers. Bella was very happy and loved to fly high in the sky. One day, Bella saw some steam coming from a small house in the woods. She was curious, so she flew down to see what was happening. She saw a little boy playing with his toys. The boy was pretending to destroy things with his toy monster truck. Bella wanted to help the boy have fun, so she used her graceful wings to blow the steam around the toys. The boy laughed and clapped his hands. Together, they played and had a great time. Bella learned that it was fun to make new friends and help others.
