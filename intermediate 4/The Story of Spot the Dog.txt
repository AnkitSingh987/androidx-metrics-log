Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of Spot the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was very excite to go outside and play. He loved to run and jump in the park. One day, he saw a big ball and he wanted to play with it. Spot ran to the ball and tried to push it, but it was too big. Spot felt helpless. He had a question: "How can I play with the big ball?" Just then, a little girl named Sue came to the park. She saw Spot and the big ball. Sue had an idea. She told Spot to stand back. Sue gave the ball a big kick and it rolled away. Spot was so happy! He could now play with the big ball. The unexpected twist was that Sue and Spot became best friends, and they played together every day.

One day, a little boy went to the park. He saw a snake on the ground. The snake was not rich, but it had many colors. The boy was happy to see the snake, but the snake was sad. The snake told the boy that it wanted to be rich. It wanted to have many things. The boy wanted to help the snake. They thought and thought about how to make the snake rich. The boy and the snake tried many things. They tried to find money, but they did not get any. They struggled to find a way to make the snake rich. In the end, the snake and the boy learned that being rich was not important. They were happy to be friends.

Once upon a time there was a band. They were all very happy and loved to make music together. But one day, something very bad happened - their music didn't sound good anymore! They tried to fix it but it just wouldn't sound right. They all felt so miserable. They didn't know what to do, so they all started to guess. They guessed that maybe their instruments had broken or that maybe their music was too loud or too soft. But all the guessing didn't help. Then one of the band members had an idea. They decided to practice and practice until they were happy with the music again. So they practiced and eventually their music sounded good! They were so happy and they all high fived and hugged each other. The band was so happy to make music together again.

Once upon a time, there was a big, heavy rat. The rat lived in a house with a girl named Sue. Sue did not like the rat, but the rat liked Sue. The rat wanted to be Sue's friend, but Sue was scared of the rat. One day, the rat found a bell. The rat thought, "I will ring the bell and make Sue happy." The rat went to Sue's room and rang the bell. Sue heard the bell and ran to her room. She saw the rat with the bell and was not scared anymore. Sue and the rat played with the bell. They rang the bell and laughed. The rat was not heavy anymore. Sue and the rat became best friends. They lived happily ever after.

Kai loved playing tag with his friends. He always was very fast and could never be caught. Today was no different and his friends disagreed, so they decided to include a pen in their game. Kai felt uncomfortable with the pen because it was a little too hard for him, but he agreed to play. When his friends got caught, Kai took the pen and ran away as fast as he could. Since his friends were bigger than him, they were able to catch up and take back the pen. To make matters worse, Kai had accidentally hurt his friends with the pen as he was running away. His friends were really upset and Kai tried to apologize with a hug, but his friends wouldn't accept it. In the end, Kai realized that he should have used a soft pen instead of a hard one.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play with his friends in the park. They would chase each other and have lots of fun. One day, Spot found a small box. Inside the box, there was a memory of a happy day. It was a picture of Spot and his friends playing together. Spot felt very happy when he looked at the picture. Spot wanted to share the memory with his friends. So, he ran to find them. When they saw the picture, they all felt happy too. They decided to go to the park and play, just like in the memory. And they all had a jolly time together.
