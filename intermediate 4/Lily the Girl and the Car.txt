Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She liked to play with her toy car. One day, Lily was playing with her car when her big brother Tom came into the room. Tom was loud and made Lily feel sad. Lily wanted some quiet to play with her car. Lily said, "Tom, please stop!" But Tom did not stop. He kept being loud and it was hard for Lily to play. She felt very sad and just wanted some quiet. Lily went to her mom and told her about Tom. Her mom said, "Tom, please stop and let Lily have some quiet." Tom listened to their mom and stopped being loud. Lily was happy and could play with her toy car in the quiet room.

Once upon a time, there was a jar. The jar was walking down a calm path. The jar felt happy, looking around at all the peaceful nature. It spotted a bright flower, and smiled as it passed by. The jar continued to walk until it found a lake. The lake was very calm and beautiful in the sunshine. So the jar decided to take a break and relax on the shore. It stayed there, feeling content and at peace. After a while, the jar decided it was time to keep walking. So it slowly got up and continued its peaceful journey. Every time the jar went somewhere new, it felt more and more joy. The jar was happy to keep walking and discovering new places.

Ben and Mia like to play on the sand. They make castles and bridges and tunnels. They use buckets and spades and shells. They have fun. But one day, the tide comes in. The water is cold and wet. It covers the sand. It breaks the castles and bridges and tunnels. Ben and Mia are sad. They cry. Dad and Mom see them. They hug them and say, "Don't worry. The tide will go out soon. Then you can make new things on the sand. And we have a surprise for you." Ben and Mia stop crying. They are curious. They ask, "What is the surprise?" Dad and Mom smile. They say, "We have a picnic for you. We have sandwiches and juice and cookies. And we have a kite for you. You can fly it in the sky." Ben and Mia are happy. They say, "Thank you, Dad and Mom. You are the best." They eat the picnic and fly the kite. They wait for the tide to go out. They have fun.

One day, Jack went to the mechanic’s shop with his mum. He was still very young, so he was very curious about what was happening. When they arrived, the mechanic looked at Jack and smiled. He asked Jack’s mum: “What can I do for you today?” Jack saw some interesting tools on the wall and pointed to them. His mum said: “We need to get the car fixed. It isn’t driving as well as it should.” The mechanic got to work, and he fixed the car in no time. He said: “There, now the car will be fit and ready to go.” Jack was very impressed. He thanked the mechanic for his hard work. The mechanic smiled at him and said: “You’re welcome, Jack.” Jack and his mum drove away happy, and Jack looked back at the mechanic and waved goodbye.

Once upon a time, there was a little bee named Buzz. Buzz did not like to behave. He liked to fly around and make people frustrated. One day, Buzz met a big bear named Ben. Ben said, "Buzz, you need to behave. If you don't, people will not like you." Buzz thought about what Ben said. He did not want people to be frustrated with him. The next day, Buzz tried to behave. He did not make people frustrated. He helped them by getting honey from flowers. People started to like Buzz, and he was happy. Buzz learned that it was better to behave and make people happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a kind girl named Lily. She had a big heart and loved to help everyone. One evening, she saw an old lady with a big bag. Lily walked to the old lady and said, "Let me help you." The old lady smiled and said, "Thank you, kind girl." They walked together, and Lily carried the big bag. When they got to the old lady's home, she wanted to pay Lily for her help. But Lily said, "No, thank you. I just wanted to be kind." The old lady was very happy, and they became good friends.
