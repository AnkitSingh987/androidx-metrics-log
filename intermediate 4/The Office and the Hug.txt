Category: Intermediate
Title: The Office and the Hug

Once upon a time, there was a big office. In the office, there were many people. They all liked to work and play. They also liked to hug each other when they were happy. They were always available to help each other. One day, a new person came to the office. He was very big and tall. He did not know how to hug. The people in the office were sad. They wanted to teach him how to hug, but they did not know how. Then, one little girl had an idea. She asked the big, tall person to bend down. She hugged him tight. The big, tall person was happy. He learned how to hug. The people in the office were surprised. They did not know that the little girl could teach him. Now, everyone in the office hugs and plays together.

Once upon a time there was a little dragon who lived in a woodland cave. Everyone thought the dragon was so cute, because she was so adorable. One day, a fairy godmother came to visit her. She said, "Hello little dragon! I have a special gift for you." She gave the dragon a sparkling wand. The dragon was so excited. She thanked the fairy godmother and asked, "What do I do with it?" The fairy godmother smiled and said, "You must use it to make a wish!" The dragon was determined to make a special wish. She thought for a while and finally said, "I wish that everyday I would be able to give something special away to someone." The fairy godmother smiled and said, "Your wish is granted, my little dragon! Make someone happy today!" The dragon was so excited she flew off in search of somebody to give a present to. She had so much fun giving away her special gifts and making people smile. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a small, soft toy cat. The toy cat was her best friend. They played together every day. One day, the toy cat got hurt. Its leg had a big hole, and its thread was coming out. Sue was sad and didn't know what to do. Her mom saw Sue and asked, "Why are you sad?" Sue showed her mom the toy cat's leg. Her mom said, "Don't be sad. We can heal the toy cat's leg." Sue's mom took a needle and some thread. She sewed the hole in the toy cat's leg. Sue was happy to see her toy cat healed. She thanked her mom and gave her a big hug. Sue learned that when we are sad or angry, we can ask for help from others. They can make us feel better and heal our problems.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She was fearful of the dark kitchen at night. One night, Mia heard a sound coming from the kitchen. She did not want to go, but her mom said, "Don't worry, I will be with you." Mia and her mom went to the kitchen. They saw a small cat struggle to get out of a box. Mia said, "Oh no, the cat needs help!" Her mom helped the cat out of the box. The cat was happy and said, "Thank you!" Mia and her mom were surprised. They did not know that cats could talk. The cat told them, "I am a magic cat, and I can give you one wish." Mia thought and said, "I wish the kitchen is not dark and scary at night." The magic cat made the kitchen bright and happy. Mia was not fearful of the kitchen anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little park. In the park, there was a big tree. The tree had a bookshelf on it. Many kids came to the park to see the bookshelf and read the books. One day, a deaf dog came to the park. The dog could not hear, but it liked to play. The kids saw the dog and wanted to play with it. They all played together and had lots of fun. After playing, the kids and the dog sat under the tree. They took books from the bookshelf and read them. The dog listened and looked at the pictures. They all had a great time at the park.
