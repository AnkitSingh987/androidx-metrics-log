Category: Intermediate
Title: The Mystery of the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a big, generous cat. The cat had a lot of ink. The cat liked to draw with the ink. One day, the cat met a small mouse. The mouse looked sad. The cat asked the mouse, "Why are you sad?" The mouse said, "I want to draw, but I have no ink." The generous cat said, "I will share my ink with you. Let's draw together." The cat and the mouse drew many fun things with the ink. They had a good time. But soon, the ink was all gone. The mouse started to complain. "Oh no! We have no more ink," said the mouse. The cat smiled and said, "Don't worry, we can find more ink and keep drawing together." The mouse was happy, and they both went to find more ink.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He wanted to make a trail in the dirt with his toy car. He pushed the car along and made a long, wavy line. As Tim made the trail, he found a big rock. The rock made it difficult for the car to keep going. Tim thought hard and decided to move the rock out of the way. Now, the trail was easy again. Tim played all day, making the trail longer and longer. He was happy that he could make a fun trail for his toy car. When it was time to go inside, Tim looked at his trail and smiled.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She lived in a modern house with her mommy and daddy. The house was big and had many fun toys. Lucy loved to play with her toys and have fun all day. One day, Lucy was playing a game with her mommy. They had to guess what was in a box. Lucy was very good at guessing. She guessed a ball, a teddy bear, and a book. Her mommy was so proud of her for guessing right. At night, when it was time for bed, Lucy had to be very quiet. She knew her mommy and daddy needed their rest. So, she would put away her toys, brush her teeth, and go to bed. The house was very quiet, and Lucy slept well, dreaming of more fun games to play the next day.

Once upon a time, there was a tiny ant named Andy. Andy was a mighty ant who loved to help his friends. One day, Andy was walking in the park and saw his friend, a little bird named Betty. Betty was sad because she lost her toy. Andy wanted to help, so he said, "I will lend you my toy to play with." Betty was very happy and thanked Andy for his help. Andy and Betty played together all day with the toy. They had so much fun and became best friends. From that day on, Andy the mighty ant always helped his friends when they needed him.

Once upon a time, there was a big sun and a little moon. The sun was bright and warm, and all the people loved the sun. The little moon was dark and cool, but it was still nice. The sun and the moon were friends, but the sun was a bit jealous of the moon because the moon could come out at night when the sun could not. One day, the sun decided to welcome the moon during the day. The sun said, "Moon, come out and play with me during the day, so we can have fun together!" The moon thought it was a great idea, so it came out during the day. But something unexpected happened! When the moon came out, it covered the sun, and the day became dark. The people were surprised and a little scared. The sun quickly realized that it was not a good idea for the moon to come out during the day. The sun said, "I'm sorry, Moon. I understand now that we both have our own time to shine. Let's stay friends and keep the day and night separate." The moon agreed, and they both went back to their own times. The sun was no longer jealous, and they both lived happily ever after.
