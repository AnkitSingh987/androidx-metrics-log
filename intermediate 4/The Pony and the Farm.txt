Category: Intermediate
Title: The Pony and the Farm

Once upon a time, there was a little pony. The pony lived on a farm with a kind farmer. The pony liked to run and play in the big yard with the other animals. One day, the farmer gave the pony a job. The pony had to fill a big bucket with water for the other animals to drink. The pony tried very hard to carry the bucket, but it was heavy. The pony did not give up. It tried and tried until it filled the bucket with water. The other animals were so happy and thanked the pony. The pony was proud of its hard work and knew it could do anything it set its mind to.

Once upon a time, there was a big, shiny chain. It was very expensive. It was in a store where people go to buy things. The chain was very happy because it was special. One day, a nice man came to the store. He saw the expensive chain and liked it a lot. He wanted to buy it for his wife. The man paid for the chain and took it home. The chain was excited to go to a new home. The man set the chain on the table for his wife to see. When his wife saw the chain, she was very happy. She put the chain around her neck and wore it all day. The chain felt very loved and knew it had found its forever home.

Once upon a time, there was a small house with a fireplace. In the house lived a little boy named Tim and his mom. They were happy and liked to play games together. Tim loved the warm fireplace, especially when it was cold outside. One day, Tim heard a loud sound coming from the fireplace. It scared him, so he ran to his mom. She listened to the sound too, and they both realized it was a bird! The bird was stuck in the fireplace and needed help. Tim and his mom carefully opened the fireplace door. They thought the bird would fly out, but it didn't. Instead, the bird started to talk! It said, "Thank you for helping me!" Tim and his mom were very surprised. They didn't know birds could talk. From that day on, the talking bird became their new friend and they all lived happily together.

Lily and Mom went to the market. Lily liked the market. She saw many things. She saw fruits and flowers and toys and books. She wanted to buy something. "Mom, can I have this?" Lily asked. She pointed to a pretty doll. The doll had a pink dress and yellow hair. Mom looked at the doll. She smiled. "That is a nice doll, Lily. But we have to pay for it. Do you have any money?" Lily shook her head. She did not have any money. She felt sad. Mom saw Lily's face. She whispered in her ear. "Don't worry, Lily. I have a surprise for you. Wait here for a minute. I will be right back." Lily waited. She wondered what the surprise was. She hoped it was something good. Mom came back. She had a bag in her hand. She gave it to Lily. "Here you go, Lily. This is for you. Open it and see." Lily opened the bag. She gasped. Inside was the pretty doll. She was so happy. She hugged the doll and Mom. "Thank you, Mom. Thank you, thank you, thank you. You are the best Mom ever." Mom kissed Lily. "You are welcome, Lily. You are the best daughter ever. I love you." Lily and Mom left the market. They walked home. Lily held the doll. She whispered to it. "You are my new friend. I will call you Daisy. I will take good care of you."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mommy and daddy. Lily loved to play with her toys and go outside when the sun was hot. One day, Lily found a key in her toy box. She didn't know what it was for, but she wanted to find out. She took the key and tried to open all the doors in her house. But none of them worked with the key. Lily went outside to play with her friends. She showed them the key and asked if they knew what it was for. Her friend Tom said, "Let's try to open the big tree!" They went to the tree and Lily tried to squeeze the key into the tree. Suddenly, the tree opened like a door! Lily and her friends went inside and found a room full of toys. They played all day long and had so much fun.
