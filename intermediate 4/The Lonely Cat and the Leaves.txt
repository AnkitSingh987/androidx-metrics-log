Category: Intermediate
Title: The Lonely Cat and the Leaves

Once upon a time, there was a lonely cat. The cat liked to count. One day, the cat went for a walk. The cat saw a big tree. The cat wanted to count the leaves on the tree. The cat started to count. One leaf, two leaves, three leaves. The cat counted and counted. The cat was very happy. The cat counted all the leaves on the tree. Then, the cat saw just one leaf left. The cat counted the last leaf. The cat was not lonely anymore. The cat had fun counting the leaves. The cat went home and told all the other cats about the leaves.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Finn. Finn had a big scale that he liked to play on. One day, Finn wanted to invite his friends to play with him. So, Finn invited his friends, Sam the snail and Tina the turtle, to play on his scale. They all took turns and had lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The scale broke and hurt Finn's fin. Finn was sad and his friends felt bad. But then, Tina had an idea. She knew how to fix the scale! Tina fixed the scale and made it even better than before. Now, Finn and his friends could play on the scale without getting hurt. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, there was a brave man. He saw an arrow in the sky and knew he had to take it down. He raced off to grab his bow and arrow. Then, he took aim and fired. The arrow sailed through the air and hit its target. Everyone was impressed! The brave man was happy and congratulated himself on his impressive shooting. But then, everything changed. The arrow had hit a tree and started a huge fire! It quickly spread and threatened to take over the town. The brave man worried and realized he must have done something wrong. The brave man tried to put out the fire, but he knew it was too late. He had been too careless and the town had suffered. The moral of the story is to always be careful. Bad things can happen if you are not careful. Don't let your pride and ambition get the better of you.

Once upon a time, there was a little blue bird named Bobby. Bobby lived in a pretty birdcage in a big room. He liked to sing and play with his toys all day long. One day, Bobby met a new friend named Tommy. Tommy was a little boy who lived in the same house. They liked to talk and laugh together. Bobby would say, "Tweet, tweet!" and Tommy would say, "Hi, Bobby!" Tommy wanted to make Bobby happy, so he found some nice sticks and leaves to put in the birdcage. Bobby was very happy and sang a beautiful song for Tommy. They played together every day and became the best of friends.

Tom and Lily wanted to make a cake for their mom. They found a recipe in a book and looked at the pictures. They needed flour, sugar, eggs, butter, and milk. They also needed a big bowl, a spoon, a jug, and a pan. They put the flour and the sugar in the bowl and mixed them with the spoon. Then they cracked the eggs and added them too. They asked their dad to help them melt the butter in the microwave. They poured the butter in the bowl and stirred everything well. "Now we need some milk," Lily said. "But how much?" Tom asked. He looked at the recipe. It said one cup. He did not know what a cup was. He saw the jug on the table. It had water in it. He thought it was a cup. He poured all the water in the bowl. "Oh no, Tom!" Lily cried. "That's too much! The cake will be wet and yucky!" Tom felt bad. He did not mean to ruin the cake. He wanted to make mom happy. He looked at the bowl. The batter was very thin and runny. He wondered if they could fix it. Dad came back and saw what happened. He smiled and hugged them. "Don't worry, kids. We can still make a cake. We just need to measure the milk with something else. Do you see that small glass over there? That's a cup. We can use that to measure the milk. And we can add some more flour and sugar to make the batter thicker." He helped them measure the milk with the glass and pour it in the jug. Then he helped them add more flour and sugar to the bowl and mix it again. The batter looked better now. He poured it in the pan and put it in the oven. He set the timer and told them to wait. Soon the cake was done. It smelled delicious. They took it out of the oven and let it cool. They decorated it with frosting and sprinkles. They wrote "Happy Birthday Mom" on it with a tube of icing. They were very proud of their cake. They brought it to mom and sang "Happy Birthday" to her. She was very surprised and happy. She hugged them and thanked them. She cut a slice for each of them and gave them a fork. They tasted the cake. It was delicious. They smiled and said, "We love you, mom!" Mom smiled and said, "I love you too, my sweeties!"
