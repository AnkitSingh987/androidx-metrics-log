Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Kids and the Teacher

Once upon a time, there was a big room with a blackboard. In the room, there were kids and a teacher. The teacher was nice but sometimes hard. One day, the teacher said, "Let's play a game! You need to match the pictures." The kids were happy and started to play. They looked at the pictures and tried to find the ones that looked the same. But two kids, Tom and Sue, could not agree. Tom said, "This one matches!" Sue said, "No, this one matches!" They were mad at each other. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came into the room and blew all the pictures away. The kids were sad because they could not play the game anymore. The teacher tried to find the pictures, but they were gone. The kids learned that it is not good to fight, but it was too late. The game was over, and they could not play again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a jolly man named Bob. He loved to make dough for bread. Bob had a big round belly and a big smile. He always wore a big white hat on his head. One day, Bob met a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she had never made dough before. Bob said, "Don't worry, I'll teach you how to make dough! First, we shake the flour and water together." So, Sue and Bob shook the bowl with the flour and water. It was fun! As they were shaking the bowl, a big wind came and blew the dough away. Sue and Bob were sad. They tried to find the dough, but it was gone. The wind had taken it far away. The town was sad because there was no bread to eat. Moral: Always be careful with what you have, because you never know when it might be gone.

Once upon a time there was a girl called Alice. She went to a lake and saw a crown floating on the water. Alice wanted to catch it so she could wear it. She reached out and tried to grab it, but it was too slippery. Then she had an idea. She took off her shoes and jumped in. She grabbed the crown and held it close. Alice was very proud of herself. Then, Alice heard a voice coming from the woods. "Hey, that's my crown." Alice turned around and saw a tall man with a stern face. She was very scared and ashamed. She looked down and gave the man back his crown. The man smiled and said, "That was very brave of you, Alice. Keep it as a reward for trying so hard." Alice was surprised, but she said thank you. She put the crown on her head and ran home. She told her friends the story and when they saw the crown they all cheered. From then on Alice and the crown became best friends. She wore it every day and was never ashamed again.

Once upon a time, in a big green yard, there lived a little boy named Tim. One sunny day, Tim went outside to play. He loved to run and jump in the soft grass. As Tim played, he saw a tiny worm on the ground. Tim said, "Hello, worm! What are you doing?" The worm replied, "I am trying to get to the other side of the yard, but it is very far." Tim felt bad for the worm, so he decided to help him. Tim picked up the worm and started walking. But, he did not see a rock in his way and he stumbled. Tim was careful not to hurt the worm. The worm was jealous of Tim's big legs. He wished he could walk like Tim. Tim put the worm down on the other side of the yard. The worm said, "Thank you, Tim! You are a good friend." Tim smiled and said, "You're welcome! I am happy to help." The moral of the story is to always help others, even if they are different from you.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very tired after playing all day with his friends. He went home and saw his mom in the kitchen. She was making a big cake. Tim's eyes grew wide as he looked at the cake. He wanted to taste it. "Mom, can I taste the cake?" Tim asked. His mom smiled and said, "Yes, but only a small bite. Dinner will be ready soon." Tim took a small bite and it was so yummy. He wanted more, but he remembered what his mom said. Later, Tim found a small piece of candy in his pocket. He was about to eat it when he thought about the cake. He knew he should wait for dinner. So, he decided to share the candy with his sister. She was so happy and they both enjoyed the candy together. The moral of the story is to always listen to your parents and share with others.
