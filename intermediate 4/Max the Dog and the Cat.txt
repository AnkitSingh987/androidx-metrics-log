Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was an old dog named Max. Max loved to play a sport called fetch. Every day, he would play fetch with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. One day, while they were playing, they saw a little cat. The cat was trembling because it was cold and scared. Tim said to Max, "Let's help the cat!" Max and Tim took the cat to their warm house. They gave the cat some food and a soft bed to sleep in. The cat stopped trembling and was very happy. The cat said, "Thank you, Max and Tim!" From that day on, Max, Tim, and the cat became best friends. They all played fetch and other fun games together. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a photo in her room. It was a photo of a small plant. Mia showed the photo to her mom and asked, "Mom, what is this?" Her mom smiled and said, "That is a seed that can grow into a big plant. But it is difficult to make it grow. You need to take care of it." Mia wanted to try. She put the seed in a pot and watered it every day. She talked to the seed, saying, "Please grow big and strong." One day, Mia saw a tiny green thing in the pot. The seed started to grow! Mia was very happy. She kept taking care of the plant. It grew bigger and bigger. Mia's mom took a new photo of the big plant and put it next to the old photo. Mia looked at both photos and said, "I did it! I made the difficult seed grow!" Mia's mom hugged her and said, "Yes, you did! Good job, Mia!"

Once upon a time there were two very best friends. They lived close to each other. Every day they would meet and play together in the park nearby. One day when the two friends were in the park, they noticed a beautiful billboard full of colourful balloons. The friends got very jealous that they did not have the same wonderful billboard. The two decided to go together to the shop to get the same balloons as the billboard. When they got to the shop, the shopkeeper told them that the balloons were very expensive and that he could not serve them. The two were so disappointed that they both ran away unhappy and annoyed. They never played together in the park again and both were very jealous of the beautiful billboard.

Once there was a clumsy girl who always wore a hat. Her name was Sarah and she was very brave. She wanted to explore the park but noticed it was very far away. One day, Sarah decided to kneel down and close her eyes. She wished for the park to appear. Suddenly, the ground started to move and Sarah found herself in the park! She was so excited and climbed onto a slide. She then ran around the park, looking at all the things she could see. Sarah's hat blew off her head and she ran after it, laughing and smiling. When it was time to go home, Sarah kneeled down. She thanked the park for being so wonderful and wished to come back soon. With one last look around, Sarah opened her eyes and she was back in her room again.

One day, a bright red truck was driving down the road. The truck was very big and strong. It could do many things. The truck loved to help people. The truck saw a little girl. She was sad. "What can I do for you?" asked the truck. The little girl said, "I lost my ball. It went into the big tree." The truck knew it could help her. The truck drove to the big tree. It used its strong arm to shake the tree. The ball fell down. The little girl was very happy. "Thank you, bright red truck!" she said. The truck was happy it could help.

Jim and Sue were best friends who liked to play together all the time. One day, they decided to play a game. They grabbed a score and split it in half. Jim took one part and Sue took the other part. Jim and Sue ran around the garden trying to collect as many parts as they could. They laughed and giggled as they raced against each other. Soon, they became dizzy with excitement. But then something bad happened, as Jim fell over and hurt his leg. Jim was in a lot of pain and he started to cry. Sue felt bad and rushed over to help him. She asked him what happened and Jim replied, "I don't feel so good. I think I'm going to be sick." Sue was very scared and unsure what to do. She decided to get help but it was too late. Jim was already feeling dizzy and too weak to move. In the end, Jim didn't make it and passed away that same day. Sue was very sad and felt guilty for not seeing the signs earlier. The little score game that they used to play had turned into a tragedy. From then on, Sue never forgot about Jim and the part that the game had played in his passing.
