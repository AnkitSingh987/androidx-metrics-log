Category: Intermediate
Title: The Poor Boy and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a poor boy. He had a very small wallet, and he didn't have any money inside. Every day, he would go outside and sit in the sun. The poor boy would yawn as he sat in the sun. He was so sleepy and he wished he had more money so he could buy nice things. He wished that he had lots of friends too. One day, he saw a kitten playing in the dirt. He was so excited that he forgot to yawn! He giggled and laughed as he watched the kitten running around. As he watched the kitten, the poor boy forgot that he was poor. Then, the poor boy yawned. He smiled and looked up at the sky. He knew that no matter how poor he might be, he was still happy to be alive.

John and Gary were playing in the park, throwing a ball around. Gary had just caught the ball and was about to throw it back when a bee flew past him. Gary quickly dropped the ball and ran away, yelping with fear. John laughed and called to Gary, "Come back and get the ball! Don't be scared, it won't hurt you." But Gary was too scared and he kept running until he was right at the edge of the park. Then he stopped and looked back. He saw John, still standing in the same spot and insisting that he come back for the ball. Gary hesitated, still scared, until John said something that made him feel a bit better. "Gary, I'll shield you from the bee with my shoulder. I promise it won't hurt you. You don't need to be scared, you're safe with me!" Hearing those words, Gary felt brave and lucky to have John as a friend. So he slowly walked back and reached out to take the ball from John. He smiled and thanked John, then they started playing again. Just like lucking, the bee was gone. The two friends enjoyed their game until it was time to go home.

One day, a beautiful cat named Jazz went for a walk. Jazz liked to step and dance in the sun. She was very happy. Jazz saw her friend, a dog named Max. Max liked to play with Jazz. Max said, "Jazz, let's play together!" So, they played and danced all day. But then, something unexpected happened. Jazz and Max found a big box. They opened the box and found a baby bird inside. The baby bird said, "I am lost. Can you help me find my mom?" Jazz and Max helped the baby bird find its mom, and they all became friends.

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to pretend they were explorers. They saw a big hill with a hole on top. They wondered what was inside. "Let's go and see!" Tom said. "Maybe it is a volcano!" "A volcano?" Lily asked. "What is that?" "A volcano is a mountain that makes fire and rocks. It is very loud and hot. But we are brave, we can go and look!" Tom said. They climbed the hill and reached the hole. They peeked inside. They saw nothing but dirt and rocks. "Is this a volcano?" Lily asked. Tom shook his head. "No, this is not a volcano. This is just a hole. I guess we were wrong." They heard a noise behind them. They turned around and saw a man with a big hose. He was watering the flowers. "Hello, kids. What are you doing here?" the man asked. "We were looking for a volcano!" Tom said. "A volcano? In the park?" the man laughed. "There are no volcanoes here. Volcanoes are far away, in other places. Do you want to learn more about them?" "Yes, please!" Tom and Lily said. The man smiled and took them to his truck. He had a book and a toy volcano. He showed them pictures and told them stories. He also made the toy volcano erupt with water and baking soda. Tom and Lily were amazed. They learned a lot about volcanoes. They thanked the man and went back to their parents. They decided to play something else. They still liked to pretend they were explorers, but they also liked to learn new things. They wondered what else they could find in the park.
