Category: Intermediate
Title: The Girl Called Emmy and the Kitchen

Once upon a time there was a chubby girl called Emmy. She loved to cook and was always in the kitchen, making yummy treats for her family. Today was her birthday and her family was throwing her a huge party! They had invited all of their friends, and everyone brought presents and balloons. Emmy ran into the kitchen, eager to help with the cooking. Her mom was busy stirring a pot of stew on the stove. "Mommy, what are you cooking?" Emmy asked. "I'm cooking a big stew to share with everyone at your party!" Her mom replied. Emmy smiled and started chopping vegetables on the counter. When the party started, the kitchen smelled delicious! Everyone was so excited to eat the stew that Emmy had helped to cook. They all ate their dinner and Emmy was the happiest person in the room.

One day, a little girl named Lily wore her favorite skirt. It was red and had pretty flowers on it. She loved to spin around and watch it twirl. She went outside to play in the garden with her friend, Tom. While they were playing, Tom saw a big pumpkin. It was orange and round. But, it had a yucky spot on it. "Look, Lily! The pumpkin has a rot!" Tom said. "Ew!" Lily replied, "We should tell your mom." Lily and Tom went inside to find Tom's mom. "Mom, the pumpkin has a rot!" Tom said. His mom looked frustrated. "Oh no, we need to throw it away," she said. Tom and Lily helped her take the pumpkin to the trash. After they threw the pumpkin away, Lily and Tom went back outside to play. They ran around, laughing and having fun. They were happy that they helped Tom's mom with the yucky pumpkin. And Lily's skirt twirled and twirled as they played.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Sam and Brooke. Sam was feeling brave and Brooke was feeling fearful. Sam thought the best way to help Brooke was to invite her to go on an adventure. The two friends set off on their exciting journey. They walked through a dark forest and over a high bridge. Suddenly, the sky became grey and it started to get very cold. Brooke was feeling scared and nervous. But Sam had an idea. Sam told Brooke to close her eyes and put her arms out wide. As soon as she did, Brooke felt warm and brave. Something magical had happened! When Brooke opened her eyes, there was a bright rainbow ahead of them. They had reached the end of their journey and had seen a beautiful sight. The friends laughed and hugged each other, feeling grateful that they had invited each other on such a special trip.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet little frog. The frog lived near a big log. Every day, the frog would hop and play near the log. One sunny day, the frog saw a big rock. The frog thought, "I can play on this rock too!" So, the frog hopped onto the rock. The rock started to rock back and forth. The frog liked it and laughed. As the frog rocked on the rock, a new friend came. It was a small bug. The bug said, "Can I play too?" The frog said, "Yes, let's rock together!" So, the frog and the bug rocked on the rock and had a fun day.

Tom and Anna were playing in the snow. They made a big snowman with a hat and a scarf. They gave him a carrot nose and two coal eyes. They were very happy with their snowman. "Look at our snowman, he is so nice!" Tom said. "Yes, he is. And he will not melt, because it is very cold," Anna said. But Ben, who lived next door, was not happy. He was envious of Tom and Anna's snowman. He did not have a hat or a scarf or a carrot or coal. He only had a small ball of snow. "I want a snowman like theirs," he said. "I will take their hat and scarf and nose and eyes." He waited until Tom and Anna went inside to drink hot cocoa. Then he ran to their snowman and tried to pull off his hat. But the hat was frozen to the snowman's head. He could not take it off. "Ow, ow, ow!" he cried. "The hat is stuck to my hand!" Tom and Anna heard his cry and came out. They saw Ben with his hand on their snowman's hat. "What are you doing, Ben?" Tom asked. "I... I was just... I wanted to..." Ben stammered. "You wanted to steal our snowman's things!" Anna said. "That is not nice, Ben. That is mean." Ben felt ashamed. He was sorry for what he did. He wished he had been nice to Tom and Anna, and asked them to help him make his own snowman. "I'm sorry, Tom and Anna," he said. "I was envious of your snowman. Can you forgive me?" Tom and Anna looked at each other. They were kind, and they did not like to see Ben sad. "Of course we can forgive you, Ben," Tom said. "But you have to promise not to do it again." "I promise, I promise," Ben said. "And you have to let go of our snowman's hat," Anna said. "I can't, I can't," Ben said. "It is frozen to my hand!" Tom and Anna laughed. They helped Ben to get his hand free. Then they invited him to join them in making another snowman. They shared their hat and scarf and carrot and coal. They had fun together in the snow. They were not envious anymore. They were friends.
