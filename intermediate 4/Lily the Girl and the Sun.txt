Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Sun

Once upon a time, in a regular house, there lived a little girl named Lily. She had a toy bunny named Mr. Hops. Lily and Mr. Hops loved to play together. They liked to spring up and down on her bed. One day, Lily and Mr. Hops went outside to play. The sun was shining, and the light was bright. They found a big tree and decided to play hide and seek. Lily said, "Mr. Hops, you hide first, and I will find you." Mr. Hops hid behind the tree while Lily counted. When she was done, she looked for him. She found him and said, "I see you, Mr. Hops! Now, it's my turn to hide." Lily hid, and Mr. Hops found her too. They played all day, and when the sun went down, they went back inside. They were both very tired, but they had a lot of fun. They promised to play again the next day. And so, Lily and Mr. Hops went to sleep, dreaming of more fun days together.

Once upon a time, there was a yellow car named Bumpy. Bumpy liked to go fast and play with his friends. One day, while playing with his friend, Bluey the bicycle, Bumpy hit a big rock and broke his wheel. "Oh no!" cried Bumpy. "I need to repair my wheel. Can you help me, Bluey?" "Of course!" said Bluey. "Let's go to the work place where they fix cars and bikes." So, Bumpy and Bluey went to the work place. There, they met a kind man named Mr. Fixer. Mr. Fixer saw the broken wheel and knew just what to do. "Let me help you, Bumpy," said Mr. Fixer. He worked hard and soon, the wheel was as good as new. Bumpy was so happy and thanked Mr. Fixer and Bluey for their help. Now, Bumpy and Bluey could play together again. They raced and laughed, and Bumpy was very careful not to hit any more rocks. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim had a blue shirt. He loved his shirt very much. He would hold it every day and wear it all the time. Tim's mom would have to sneak the shirt away to wash it when he was asleep. One day, Tim was playing in the park with his friends. They were running and laughing. Tim was having so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. Tim's blue shirt got caught on a branch and ripped! Tim was very sad. Tim went home with his mom, and she said she would fix the shirt. She took the shirt and went to the room. When she came back, the shirt was not dead anymore! It was like new again! Tim was so happy and gave his mom a big hug.

Once upon a time, there were two little friends called Suzie and Ginger. They liked to play in the garden with their dog, Spot. One day, Suzie and Ginger decided to have a fairy tea party. They gathered some twigs, flowers, shells and leaves for a table and spread them on the grass. Just then, Spot ran up to Suzie and Ginger with something in his mouth. It was a big, old brush. Suzie and Ginger looked at each other and laughed. Ginger said, "Let's use this for our tea party". They put the brush in the middle of the table, and it looked like a magical potion. Suddenly, a big gust of wind blew through the garden. Suzie and Ginger were frightened, and they quickly picked up the brush. Spot barked at the wind and they ran inside. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Lily. Lily liked to play with her ball near the big tree. One day, she saw a butterfly and wanted to catch it. Lily found a net to catch the butterfly. She ran and tried to catch it with the net. The butterfly was fast, but Lily did not give up. She kept trying until the butterfly flew into the net. Lily said, "I caught you!" The butterfly replied, "Please let me go, and I will show you something fun." Lily let the butterfly go, and it showed her a secret place with lots of pretty flowers. They played together all day and became good friends.
