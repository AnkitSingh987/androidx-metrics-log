Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Large Cat and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a large cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with yarn. One day, he saw a big box in the living room. He thought there might be yarn inside. Tom tried to open the box, but it was too hard for him. He asked his friend, a small mouse named Jerry, for help. Jerry was very smart and knew how to open the box. Jerry said, "Wait and see what's inside!" Finally, they opened the box together. It revealed lots of colorful yarn! Tom and Jerry were so happy. They played with the yarn all day long. And from that day on, they became the best of friends.

Once there was a family who lived in a small house. The family had two children. One of the children loved clay. Every day the child would take a lump of clay and shape it in different ways. One day a very wealthy man moved onto their street. The man had a big house and lots of toys. The child was amazed by the wealth of the man and wanted to get to know him. Eventually he became friends with the man. One day the wealthy man asked the child if they wanted to take home some clay. The child was delighted so they said yes. The man gave them a big bag full of clay. The child was so happy with the clay that they made lots of wonderful things with it. They also shared it with their brother so they could make things together. The child was so thankful to the wealthy man for his generosity and they continued to be friends. They had lots of fun playing and making things with the clay.

Once there was a friendly bear. The bear wanted to have some fun. He invited many of his friends to come and play with him. The bear's friends were very excited and quickly came to his house. They played in his garden and wished they could stay all day. Then the bear noticed the heat of the sun. He thought it would be fun to cool down. So he invited his friends to come swim in a lake nearby. The friends were happy to join the bear. They splashed and laughed until their bodies felt much cooler. They had lots of fun! The bear was glad to have such friendly friends. They said goodbye and promised to come back to play soon. They all thanked the friendly bear for inviting them such a wonderful day.

Once there was a boy named Tim. He loved to mix colors. He had a big leather bag. He put his colors in the bag. Tim had a deaf dog named Max. Max could not hear, but he was very smart. One day, Tim and Max went to the park. Tim took his leather bag with him. He wanted to mix colors and make a pretty picture. Tim put his bag on the ground. He did not see that Max took the colors out of the bag. When Tim looked back, he saw the colors were gone. He was sad. He looked for the colors, but he could not find them. Then, he saw Max. Max had the colors in his mouth. Tim was surprised. Tim learned that even if someone is deaf, they can still help. He was happy to have Max as his friend. They went home together, and Tim mixed the colors to make a pretty picture of Max.

Once upon a time, there was a big race. Many animals came to win. A fast cat, a strong dog, and a rough rabbit were in the race too. The rough rabbit had a secret. He had a special herb that made him run very fast. On the day of the race, all the animals were ready. The cat was fast, and the dog was strong. But the rough rabbit ate the special herb and started to run very fast. The rabbit was about to win the race! But then, something unexpected happened. The special herb made the rough rabbit sneeze! He sneezed so much that he stopped running. The fast cat and the strong dog passed him. In the end, the rough rabbit did not win the race. The fast cat won, and everyone was very surprised.
