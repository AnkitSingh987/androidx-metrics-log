Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and the Cat

Once upon a time, a thoughtful cat named Tom lived on a farm. Tom had many friends. He liked to play with the other animals. Tom trusted his friends a lot. They all liked to eat oats together. One day, while they were eating oats, a big wind came. The wind blew all the oats away. The animals were sad. They did not know what to do. Tom had an idea. He told his friends to wait and trust him. Tom ran to the big tree. He found a big bag of oats hidden behind it. The wind had put it there. Tom's friends were happy and surprised. They all ate the oats and played together. They knew they could always trust Tom.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a brother named Sam. They liked to play together. Tim was big and Sam was small. They had great fun playing with their toys. One day, Tim wanted to play with Sam's toy car. But Sam refused to let him. He wanted to play with his car all by himself. Tim was sad and didn't know what to do. Tim's mom saw them and had an idea. She gave them a big toy truck to play with together. Tim and Sam were very happy. They played with the truck and had a great time. From that day on, they always shared their toys and had lots of fun.

Tom and Anna were twins, but they were very different. Tom liked to run and play outside, but Anna liked to sit and read books. Tom liked to eat pizza and ice cream, but Anna liked to eat salad and olives. Tom liked to wear blue and green, but Anna liked to wear red and yellow. They always argued about what to do and what to eat and what to wear. One day, their mom said they had to go to the park with their grandma. Tom was happy, but Anna was sad. She wanted to stay home and read her new book. She put an olive in her pocket, hoping to eat it later. Tom put on his blue and green clothes, but Anna put on her red and yellow clothes. They got in the car and drove to the park. At the park, Tom ran to the swings and the slide, but Anna sat on a bench with her book. She opened her pocket to get her olive, but it was gone. She looked around and saw a squirrel holding her olive in its mouth. She got angry and chased the squirrel, but it was too fast. It ran up a tree and ate her olive. Anna cried and shouted, "Give me back my olive, you mean squirrel!" Tom heard Anna and ran to her. He saw the squirrel and said, "Don't cry, Anna. It's just an olive. You can have mine. I don't like olives anyway." He took out an olive from his pocket and gave it to Anna. Anna smiled and said, "Thank you, Tom. You are very nice. I'm sorry I argued with you. You can have some of my salad if you want." Tom smiled and said, "You're welcome, Anna. You are very unique. I'm sorry I argued with you too. You can have some of my pizza if you want." They hugged and shared their food. Their grandma saw them and said, "What a lovely sight. You two are very different, but you love each other very much. That's what makes you special." Tom and Anna nodded and said, "We do love each other, grandma. And we love you too." They finished their food and played together in the park. They had a lot of fun and learned to appreciate their differences.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Lily. She was only three years old. She loved exploring outdoors, and one day she went for a walk in the woods. Soon, she saw something scary! Lily stopped in her tracks and let out a shriek. She started to back away, but then she heard a voice. She looked around, but could not see anyone. The voice spoke again. "Don't be scared," it said. Lily felt a bit better. She replied, "Who are you?" "I am a friendly monster," the voice said. "Do you want to play with me?" Lily was still a bit scared, but she nodded her head. She asked, "What do you want to play?" "Let's go catch some fireflies!" the monster said. Suddenly, Lily wasn't scared anymore. She smiled and replied, "Let's do it!" Lily and the monster had a great adventure catching fireflies. Lily learned that sometimes things that are scary can also be a lot of fun.
