Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Sweet Baby and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a sweet baby named Lily. She liked to play with her toys and listen to her mommy sing songs. One day, Lily's mommy said, "Lily, let's take a walk to the park." Lily smiled and clapped her hands. At the park, Lily saw a little bird. The bird said, "Tweet, tweet!" Lily laughed and tried to talk to the bird. "Hi, bird!" she said. The bird flew away, and Lily waved bye-bye. After playing at the park, Lily and her mommy went home. "Did you have fun, sweet baby?" asked mommy. "Yes, mommy!" said Lily. They hugged and went inside to eat a yummy snack.

One day, a little girl named Lily was watching her mom put dirty cups and plates in the dishwasher. Lily wanted to help, so she asked her mom, "Can I help you?" Her mom said, "Yes, Lily, you can put the spoons and forks in the dishwasher." Lily was happy to help, but she saw a tough, dirty pan that needed to be washed too. She tried to lift it, but it was too heavy for her. She called her big brother, Tom, to help her. Tom came and said, "This pan is really tough. Let's try to clean it together." As they were cleaning the tough pan, something unexpected happened. A little, green frog jumped out from under the pan! Lily and Tom were surprised and laughed. The frog hopped away, and they finished cleaning the pan. They put it in the dishwasher and told their mom about the funny frog. The whole family laughed, and Lily felt proud to help with the dishwasher.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went outside to play. The sky was big and blue. She saw her friend, a big tree, and ran to hug it. The tree was happy to see Lucy too. As they played, the sky started to get dark. The sky was not blue anymore. It was empty and quiet. Lucy and the tree were scared. They hugged each other tight. Suddenly, the dark sky changed back to blue! It was not the night coming, but a big bird flying by. The bird said sorry for scaring them. Lucy, the tree, and the big bird all laughed and hugged. They played together all day under the happy sky.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted bird. This bird could sing very well. It had a beautiful whistle. The bird lived in a big tree. It had many friends. One day, the bird's friend, a little squirrel, forgot where it hid its nuts. The squirrel was sad. The bird wanted to help. The bird's whistle could remind the squirrel where the nuts were. The bird sang its pretty whistle. The squirrel listened. The whistle reminded the squirrel where the nuts were! The squirrel was happy. The gifted bird and the squirrel played together all day. They were very good friends.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went for a walk. She saw a big rock and thought it was very special. Lucy gazed at the rock and wanted to take it home. She tried to lift it, but it was too heavy. Lucy felt sad. Lucy had an idea. She called her friend Tim to help her. Tim was strong and could lift the rock. They tried to lift it together, but it was still too heavy. They were both sad that they could not take the original rock home. Then, Lucy's mom came and saw them. She told them to use a stick to help move the rock. They put the stick under the rock and pushed it. The rock started to move! They pushed the rock all the way home. Lucy and Tim were happy that they could keep the special rock.

Once upon a time, there was a pink bird. The pink bird liked to shake its wings. One day, the pink bird found a toy. The toy looked like a stick. The pink bird did not know it was a missile. The pink bird took the missile to its friends. The friends saw the missile and wanted to play too. They all started to shake the missile like a toy. They had a lot of fun playing with the missile. Then, a big wind came and blew the missile away. The pink bird and its friends were sad. They wanted to find the missile and play again. They looked everywhere and found the missile under a tree. The pink bird and its friends were happy to play with the missile again. And they all shook the missile and played happily ever after.
