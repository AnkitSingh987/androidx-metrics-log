Category: Intermediate
Title: Charlie and the Park

Charlie was a little boy who loved animals. One day he saw a panda running very fast in the park. Charlie was so excited! He ran to the panda to get a better look. "Hi there!" Charlie said. The panda stopped and looked at him. "Hello!" said the panda. Charlie told the panda how excited he was to see him. The panda smiled and said, "It's nice to be appreciated. It's important to stay kind to people and animals." Charlie nodded and said that he would remember. He waved goodbye and watched the panda run away. Charlie learned an important lesson that day. Being kind to people and animals always makes everyone feel excited and happy.

Lily and Ben wanted to see the clown at the park. They asked their mom to take them there. Mom said yes, but they had to do their chores first. Lily and Ben did not like chores, but they wanted to see the clown very much. They cleaned their room, helped with the dishes, and fed the cat. Then they put on their coats and shoes and went to the park with mom. They saw the clown making balloons and telling jokes. He was very funny and made them laugh. The clown asked if they wanted a balloon. Lily and Ben said yes, please. The clown made them a balloon dog and a balloon flower. He said they were very pretty and smart. He asked them to pay him with a hug. Lily and Ben hugged the clown and thanked him. Mom smiled and said they were very good children. She said they learned a lesson today. She said that if they work hard and do their chores, they can have fun and enjoy rewards. She also said that the clown was very dependable. He did not ask for money, but for love. He was kind and generous. Lily and Ben agreed. They said they liked the clown and his balloons. They said they would always do their chores and be dependable too. They hugged mom and the clown again and went home happy.

Once upon a time, in a charming little land, there lived a happy cat and a friendly dog. They liked to play and go on adventures together. One sunny day, they decided to go on a big journey to find a new land to play in. As they walked, they met a wise old frog. The frog said, "Hello, friends! Where do you go?" The cat and the dog told the frog about their journey to find a new land. The frog smiled and said, "I know a special land not far from here. Go down this path, and you will find it." The cat and the dog thanked the wise frog and went down the path. Soon, they found the new land. It was full of green grass, tall trees, and many new friends to play with. The cat and the dog were very happy in their new charming land. They played and laughed with their new friends every day. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. The park had a big slide. Tim was very happy to play on the slide. He ran to the slide and climbed up the steps. The slide was icy because it was cold outside. Tim did not know the slide was icy. He sat down on the slide and started to go down. He went very fast because of the ice. Tim slid all the way down and landed on the ground. He was surprised but he was not hurt. He laughed and went back to the slide to do it again. Tim had lots of fun on the icy slide that day.

Once upon a time, there was a brave cat named Tom. Tom loved to play and run all day. One day, Tom met a little bird named Tina. "Hi, Tina!" said Tom. "Do you want to play with me?" Tina looked at Tom and said, "Yes, I want to play, but I need to rest first. I am very tired." So, Tom and Tina sat under a big tree to rest. While they were resting, they saw a jar of sugar on the ground. They were both very hungry, so they decided to share the sugar. Tom and Tina ate the sugar and felt happy. After they had rested and eaten, they played and laughed all day long. They became the best of friends and had many more fun days together.
