Category: Intermediate
Title: The Purple Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a big purple cat. The cat was very bossy. She liked to tell all the other animals what to do. The other animals did not like this. One day, the bossy purple cat wanted to use a big red ball. But a small yellow dog was playing with the ball. The dog did not want to give the ball to the cat. The cat was not happy. The bossy purple cat tried to take the ball from the yellow dog. But the dog was fast. They ran and ran. In the end, the dog and the cat learned to share the big red ball. They played together and became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy tool that he loved to play with all day. One day, Tim found a big spider in his room. He was scared of the spider. Tim thought of an idea. He took his toy tool and started to spin it around. The spider was curious and came closer to see the spinning tool. Tim spun the tool faster and faster. As the tool spun, the spider became less scary. It started to dance with the spinning tool. Tim was no longer scared of the spider. They played and danced together all day long. And they became friends.

One day, a little monkey named Momo was playing with his friends in the jungle. They were all having fun jumping from tree to tree. Momo was very good at jumping, but sometimes he would get embarrassed if he fell. As they were playing, Momo saw a big banana on a high tree. He said to his friends, "Look! A big banana! I want to get it!" His friends cheered him on, but Momo was scared to climb so high. He took a deep breath and started climbing. When Momo reached the top, he tried to grab the banana, but he slipped and fell. His friends gasped, worried for Momo. But then, Momo realized he could use his tail to catch a branch and stop his fall. He swung his tail and caught a branch just in time. Momo's friends clapped and said, "Wow, Momo! You did it!" Momo felt embarrassed about falling, but he was happy that he caught himself with his tail. He smiled and said, "Thank you, friends. I learned something new today!" From that day on, Momo was never scared to climb high trees. He knew that even if he fell, he could always use his tail to catch himself. And his friends were always there to cheer him on.

Once upon a time, there was a big red van. The van was very cheap. It liked to help people. One day, the van met a sad girl named Lily. She was looking for her lost toy. "Hi Lily," said the van. "Why are you sad?" Lily sighed, "I lost my toy. Can you help me find it?" The van smiled, "Of course! Hop in and we will look for it together." Lily and the van looked all around. They found the toy under a tree. Lily was so happy. She hugged the van and said, "Thank you, van! You are the best!" From that day on, Lily and the van were the best of friends. They had many fun adventures together. And they always helped others along the way.

John was 3. He loved to draw. He had a bunch of fancy paper, and he wanted to make something wonderful. "Mommy, look what I drew!" he said, proudly holding up his drawing. It was a big blue tree with big yellow flowers. His mother smiled, "Wow! That looks fantastic, John. I'm so proud of you!" John beamed with joy. He danced around happily with his drawing. But the next day, John was feeling a bit discouraged. He wanted to make something even more wonderful, but he didn't know what to draw. His mom gave him a hug and said, "Remember to practice, John. You can make anything if you put your mind to it. I'll encourage you every step of the way!" John smiled and picked up his pen and paper. He tried his best, and he soon made a fancy butterfly with sparkly green wings. John was so proud of his work that he couldn't help but smile. His mom hugged him and said, "See, I knew you could do it!" John thanked his mom and kept on drawing. He was sure to make something even better the next day!
