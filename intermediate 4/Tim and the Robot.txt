Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Robot

One day, a little boy named Tim and his toy robot went for a walk. Tim liked to make his robot march next to him. They went to the park and saw a big tree. Tim said, "Let's climb the tree, robot!" The robot said, "Okay, Tim!" They climbed up the tree and saw many birds. The birds sang songs. Tim and his robot sang with the birds. They had a lot of fun. After a while, Tim felt tired. His body was not happy. So, Tim and his robot climbed down the tree. They sat on the grass and took a rest. Tim said, "I am tired, robot. Let's go home." The robot said, "Yes, Tim. Let's go home and rest." They walked back home and took a nap.

One day, a waffle named Wally went for a walk. Wally was wide and liked to bounce. He bounced down the road, happy as can be. As Wally bounced along, he saw a big hill. He thought it would be fun to bounce down it. So, Wally bounced up the hill and got ready to bounce down. But, as Wally started to bounce down, he bounced higher and higher. He didn't know that the hill was magic! Wally bounced so high, he touched the sky. Then he came back down and landed safely. Now, Wally knew to be careful when bouncing on magic hills.

One day, a boy named Tim went outside to gather rocks. The sun was shining, and the grass was wet from the rain. Tim liked to play with his rocks and build towers with them. As Tim was gathering rocks, he saw a big cloud in the sky. The wind started to blow, and the cloud began to spin. It was a tornado! Tim was scared and ran to his mom. "Mom, there is a tornado! What do we do?" he asked. Mom hugged Tim and said, "Don't worry, we will go inside and be safe." They went inside their house and hid under a big table. The tornado passed, and Tim and his mom were okay. After the tornado, Tim went back outside to gather more rocks. He was happy that everything was safe again.

Once upon a time there was a girl named Jill. Jill loved to lift things up and have fun. Every day she would lift her toys and put them away. One day Jill decided to go for a walk. Along her path she heard the sound of jazz music. She followed the sound and discovered an open window. She peeked inside and saw a regular band of musicians playing jazz. Jill loved the sound and started to lift her arms and dance. The musicians noticed her and stopped playing. "Do you want to join us?" said the lead singer of the Jazz band. "Okay," she said with a smile. The band started playing again, and Jill began to lift her arms and dance along to the music. The musicians were so impressed at how well she moved that they offered her a regular spot in their band. Jill was so happy that she couldn't stop smiling. Since then she's become a regular part of their band, and every day she lifts her arms and dances along to the jazz music.

Once there was a big, strong, and reliable bird named Buddy. Buddy had big wings that could spread wide. He loved to fly and help his friends. One day, Buddy saw his friend, a little mouse named Mimi. Mimi was sad because she lost her toy on a high tree. "Don't worry, Mimi," said Buddy. "I can help you get your toy back." Mimi smiled and said, "Thank you, Buddy!" Buddy spread his wings and flew up to the tree. He put Mimi on his shoulder so she could see her toy. Buddy got the toy and gave it back to Mimi. Mimi was so happy to have her toy back. She hugged Buddy and said, "You are the best friend ever!" From that day on, Buddy and Mimi played together every day. Buddy always helped Mimi when she needed it. Mimi knew that she could always count on her reliable friend Buddy.
