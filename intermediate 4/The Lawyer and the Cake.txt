Category: Intermediate
Title: The Lawyer and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a lawyer. He liked to help people. He had a big, tasty cake. The cake was very sweet and yummy. The lawyer shared the cake with his friends. They were very happy. One day, the lawyer got very dirty. He played in the mud with his dog. His clothes were messy. He needed to get clean. So, the lawyer went home to bathe. He filled the tub with warm water. He put in bubbles too. The lawyer and his dog jumped in the tub. They splashed and played. Soon, they were both clean again. They smiled and laughed together. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with his mom and dad. They were not rich, but they were happy. One day, a mosquito came into their house. Tim saw the mosquito and wanted to catch it. Tim called his mom and said, "Mom, help me catch the mosquito!" His mom tried to help, but the mosquito was very fast. They chased the mosquito all around the house, but they could not catch it. At last, they were very tired. The mosquito was still flying around the house. Tim and his mom sat down and felt sad. They could not catch the mosquito, and it kept bothering them. The mosquito won, and Tim and his mom had a bad day.

Once upon a time, there was an ancient chair. It was very old and lived in a big room. The chair had many friends, like the table, the bed, and the lamp. They all liked to play together. One day, the chair decided to play a game with its friends. The game was called "Who can stand the longest?" The chair, the table, and the bed all stood still. But then, something unexpected happened. The chair started to wobble and fall! The chair was scared, but its friends were there to help. The table and the bed caught the chair before it hit the floor. The chair was safe! They all laughed and decided that the game was too hard for the ancient chair. But then, the chair had an idea. "Let's play another game," it said. "A game where we all sit down and tell stories." So, the chair, the table, the bed, and the lamp all sat down and shared their favorite stories. They all had a great time and the chair didn't fall again.

Once upon a time there was a cute little girl who was playing in the park. She was wearing a pink shirt and she was having a lot of fun. As she was walking in the grass, she saw something strange. It was a small brown thing, it was round and it was rot! She put her little hand over it to touch it, but it was very cold! She was confused and a little worried, but she decided to go on playing. After a few minutes, she heard a loud squeal coming from the thing she had touched. She looked closer and found out it was a baby pig! It was so small, so cute, and it was wearing a shirt! She was delighted to have made a new friend, but what would she do with it? The little girl thought and thought, until she realized she could take it home! She picked the pig up, so carefully, and she put it in the pocket of her shirt. Then she ran home and showed her parents her new friend. They thought it was so cute and they agreed to keep the pig at home! The little girl was very happy and she thanked the rot for helping her to find a new friend. From that day on, she always kept the pig in her shirt pocket!

One day, a healthy boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big rock. Tim's mom said, "That's a mineral, Tim. Rocks are made of minerals." Tim liked the big rock and wanted to take it home. At home, Tim tried to separate the big rock into smaller rocks. He hit it with a toy hammer. But nothing happened. Tim was sad. His mom said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can try something else." Then, something unexpected happened. A little bird came and pecked at the big rock. The rock broke into small rocks! Tim was happy. He said, "Thank you, little bird!" The bird flew away, and Tim played with his new small rocks.
