Category: Intermediate
Title: The Dark Forest and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a dark forest. In the forest, there was a big tree. A man named Tom wanted to break the tree with his axe. Tom went to the tree and said, "I will break you, tree!" He started to hit the tree with his axe. But the tree was very strong. Tom hit the tree again and again, but it did not break. A little bird saw Tom and said, "Please, do not break the tree. It is my home." Tom felt sad and stopped hitting the tree. He said, "I am sorry, little bird. I will not break your home." Tom and the little bird became friends. They played together every day. And the big tree in the dark forest stayed strong and safe.

Once upon a time, there was a small, dirty dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside in the mud. One day, it was very cold, and the mud began to freeze. Spot was sad because he could not play in the frozen mud. Spot saw his friend, a big fish named Fin, who lived in a pond. Fin was also sad because the pond was starting to freeze too. Spot had an idea. He said, "Fin, let's find a way to make the pond warm again so we can play." Fin thought it was a great idea. They asked their friend, the sun, for help. The sun was very warm and could melt the ice. The sun said, "I can help you, but you need to clean your dirty scale, Fin, and Spot, you need to take a bath." Spot and Fin agreed to clean up. After they were clean, the sun shined brightly on the pond, and the ice began to melt. Spot and Fin were so happy. They played in the pond all day long and thanked the sun for helping them.

Once upon a time there was a spoiled little girl called Gemma. Gemma had lots of toys and always got what she wanted. One day her Mommy gave her a very special present, it was a pastel colour! Gemma was so excited that she jumped up and down and said thank you very much. But Gemma didn't play with the pastel colour for very long. She soon got bored and wanted something else, so she quit playing with it. Gemma's Mommy was sad that she had given her such a lovely present but Gemma had quit playing with it. She tried to give Gemma other toys but Gemma was too spoiled and would not play with them. So Gemma's Mommy put the pastel colour away and hoped that one day Gemma would learn to be grateful and realise that it was a special present. The End.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was lonely because she had no friends to play with. One day, Sue went to the park to find someone to play with. As Sue walked in the park, she saw a boy named Tim. Tim was wearing a red jacket. Sue walked up to Tim and asked, "Do you want to play with me?" Tim looked at Sue and said, "Yes, I want to play with you!" They played together all day long. At one point, they found a small kitten. The kitten was hiding under a bush. Sue and Tim knelt down to look at the kitten. It was so tiny and cute. They decided to take the kitten home and take care of it together. From that day on, Sue and Tim were best friends, and they were never lonely again.

Once upon a time, there was an ancient tree. It had been there for a very, very long time. The tree had thick skin that wrapped around its big trunk. One day, a little bird came to the tree. The bird was cold and tired. The tree said, "Little bird, come here. I will wrap you in my skin and keep you warm." The little bird was happy and snuggled close to the tree. The tree's skin was warm and the bird felt safe. They became good friends and lived happily ever after.
