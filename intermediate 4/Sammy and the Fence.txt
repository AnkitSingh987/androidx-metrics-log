Category: Intermediate
Title: Sammy and the Fence

Once upon a time, there were two friends. Their names were Sammy and Jake. One day, Jake noticed an old yard with a fence around it. He thought it looked very cool and wanted to explore it. Unfortunately, it was a big mistake. Inside, there were a lot of terrible monsters. They were scaring Sammy and Jake, so they started to run. Sammy was so scared he tripped and fell down. Jake felt so bad for Sammy, and he regretted not listening to her. He told her that from now on he would always listen to her. The two of them ran home and never went to the old yard again. They learned a lesson that day, that it's important to listen to each other and that trouble was always lurking in a terrible yard.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She loved to play outside in the big park near her house. One sunny day, Mia went to the park with her mom. They saw a new trail that they had never seen before. Mia and her mom decided to walk on the new trail to see what they could find. As they walked, they met a small boy named Tim. Tim was sad because he had lost his toy car. Mia wanted to help Tim find his toy car. They looked under the trees and behind the bushes, but they could not find it. Mia said, "I'm sorry, Tim. We can't find your toy car." Tim looked at her and said, "It's okay, Mia. I forgive you." Mia, Tim, and Mia's mom kept walking on the new trail. They saw many pretty flowers and butterflies. At the end of the trail, they found a big, beautiful rainbow. Under the rainbow, they found Tim's toy car! They were all so happy. Tim thanked Mia and her mom for helping him find his toy car. They all learned that when you help others and forgive them, good things can happen. And they all lived happily ever after.

There was a little boy named Toby. He came to the park with his mommy. They went for a nice walk and soon Toby saw a tunnel in the ground. Toby loved tunnels and immediately said, "Mommy, I want to go in the tunnel!" Mommy said, "OK, but be careful! It's very dark in there." Toby went inside and it was dark and quiet. Toby shouted, "Mommy, can you come in too?" Mommy replied, "No, sweetie. I'll stay here and wait for you outside." So Toby went deeper inside the tunnel and it was full of surprises. Toby saw many bugs crawling around, and he saw a fast-moving stream too. Toby shouted with joy and ran towards the stream. When Toby came out of the tunnel, he was all wet and sandy. His mommy said, "Oh my goodness! What did you do in there?" Toby told his mommy all about the stream and the bugs. It was a fun adventure!

One day, a boy named Tim found a clear letter on the ground. The letter was from a friend named Sam. Sam asked Tim to come to his house and rock on the swing with him. Tim was happy and ran to Sam's house. When Tim got to Sam's house, he saw Sam on the swing. "Hi, Tim! I'm glad you got my letter. Let's rock on the swing together," said Sam. Tim and Sam took turns rocking on the swing. They laughed and had fun. After they played, Tim said, "Sam, I liked your clear letter. It was easy to read and understand." Sam smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim. It's good to be clear when we talk and write to our friends. That way, they know what we mean." Tim agreed, and they both learned the importance of being clear when talking and writing to others.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big tree with many peaches. Tim felt hungry and wanted to eat a peach. He tried to reach for a peach, but he was too small. He felt troubled. A kind rabbit saw Tim and asked, "Why are you troubled, Tim?" Tim said, "I want a peach, but I can't reach it." The kind rabbit jumped high and got a peach for Tim. Tim was very happy. As Tim was about to eat the peach, he stumbled on a rock. The peach flew out of his hand and rolled away. Tim and the rabbit chased the peach. They saw the peach roll into a hole. They looked inside the hole and found a big pile of peaches! Tim and the rabbit were very surprised and happy. They shared the peaches with their friends.
