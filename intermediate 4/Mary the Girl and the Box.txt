Category: Intermediate
Title: Mary the Girl and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mary. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Mary loved to play outside in her big yard with her toys. One day, she saw something mysterious in the yard. It was a big, shiny box. Mary was curious and opened the box. Inside, she found a pretty doll. She played with the doll all day. But when her mom saw the doll, she was not happy. She told Mary that the doll was not hers and that she must give it back. Mary felt sad and didn't want to give the doll back. Her mom said, "If you don't give it back, I will have to punish you." Mary didn't want to be punished, so she took the doll back to the mysterious box. When she put the doll back, something unexpected happened. A little fairy appeared and said, "Thank you for giving my doll back. You did the right thing." The fairy gave Mary a new doll to keep and play with. Mary learned that it is important to do the right thing, even when it is hard.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog. The dog had a kind owner. The dog and the owner loved to hug each other every day. They were very happy. One day, the owner went to the store to buy regular food for the dog. The dog was home alone. The dog missed the owner very much. The dog wanted a hug. The dog went outside to find the owner. The dog looked and looked, but could not find the owner. The dog got lost. The dog was very sad and could not hug the owner again.

Once upon a time there was a little hippo. She was walking in the forest and looking for some food. All of a sudden she heard a voice coming from behind her. The voice said, "Turn around and leave this forest now!" The hippo was scared and didn't know what to do. She turned around and saw a serious lion standing behind her. The hippo said, "Please let me stay here. I need to find food for myself and my family." But the lion said, "No, I won't allow it. This is my forest and you mustn't stay here." The hippo pleaded, "Please, I won't cause any trouble. I'll leave as soon as I've gotten food for my family." The lion looked serious but said, "OK, you can stay here, but only for a little while. As soon as you find food, you have to turn around and leave. Do you understand?" The hippo felt a great relief and nodded her head. She was so glad the lion had agreed to let her stay, even if it was just for a little while. She thanked the lion and ran off to search for food. The hippo finally found something to eat, and when she was done, she turned around and left the forest, never to come back again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was an obedient boy who always listened to his mom and dad. One day, Tim went for a walk with his dog, Spot, near their home. On their walk, they found a well. The well was very deep and dark. Tim and Spot were curious, so they looked inside the well. They saw a little frog sitting on a rock. The frog said, "Hello, Tim! Please shut the well lid after you leave." Tim and Spot said goodbye to the frog, and they walked back home. Tim remembered what the frog said, so he shut the well lid. Tim's mom and dad were happy that Tim was obedient and listened to the frog. They all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a sheep. The sheep had a lot of wool. The wool was soft and harmless. The sheep liked to play with his friends, the dog and the cat. They all lived on a farm. One day, the farmer came to work. He saw the sheep with all the wool. The farmer said, "It is time to cut the wool." The sheep felt a little scared, but the dog and the cat told him it would be okay. The farmer cut the wool from the sheep. The sheep felt lighter and happier. The farmer took the wool to make warm clothes for people. The sheep, the dog, and the cat went back to playing on the farm. They all knew that the wool would help others stay warm, and they were happy to be a part of it.
