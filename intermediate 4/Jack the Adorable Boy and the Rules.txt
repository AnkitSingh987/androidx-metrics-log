Category: Intermediate
Title: Jack the Adorable Boy and the Rules

Once upon a time, there was an adorable little boy named Jack. One day, Jack was riding his tricycle when he came to a big building. He was amazed at how tall and grand it looked. Jack wanted to see what was inside, so he stopped his tricycle and walked around the building. But then, Jack noticed a sign saying "Do Not Enter!" He knew he could get in trouble if he didn't obey the sign, so he decided to do the right thing and not enter the building. Jack went back home and told his mom what he had seen. His mom was very proud of him for following the rules and said, "Jack, you did the right thing by obeying the rules. Rules are important and help us stay safe. That's why it's important to always follow them." Jack learned a valuable lesson that day - it's always important to obey the rules!

One day, a sleepy bird named Bob lived in a big valley. Bob had a friend, a happy dog named Sam. They liked to play together in the valley. One day, Sam said, "Bob, let's have a race!" Bob was sleepy, but Sam encouraged him to try. So, they began to race through the valley. As they raced, they saw a big box. It was a surprise! They opened the box and found a lot of yummy food. They stopped racing and ate the food together. Bob was not sleepy anymore, and they played all day in the valley.

One Sunday, a little girl named Lucy went to the park with her mom. Lucy felt very lucky because she loved the park. They saw a man working hard to fix a broken swing. Lucy wanted to help, so she asked, "Can I help you work?" The man smiled and said, "Yes, you can help me. Hold this tool, please." Lucy held the tool and felt very happy. They worked together and fixed the swing. After they finished, Lucy and her mom played on the swing. They had a fun and lucky Sunday at the park. Lucy learned that helping others is a good thing to do.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a broken toy. It was her favorite toy, but now it didn't work. She was sad. One day, Sue's mom took her to the store. They saw a big shelf full of books. Sue's mom said, "Look, Sue! A new book! It's a novel!" Sue looked at the book and smiled. She thought that reading a new book might make her feel better. Sue and her mom sat down to read the novel. The story was about a brave girl who gained new friends by helping them. Sue said, "I want to gain friends like her!" Her mom smiled and said, "You can, Sue. Just be kind and help others." Sue decided to try. She helped her friends at school and played nice games with them. Soon, Sue gained many new friends. She was happy. Sue learned that even with a broken toy, she could still have fun and make friends.

Once upon a time, there was a famous rabbit named Bob. Bob loved to eat celery all day. One day, Bob found a big celery farm. He was very happy. But there was a problem. The farm had a big dog named Max. Max did not like rabbits eating the celery. Max would lay by the farm to watch for rabbits like Bob. One day, Bob had a plan. He put on a dog costume and went to the farm. Max saw Bob and thought he was a new dog friend. They played and had fun. Then, something unexpected happened. Max saw Bob's costume fall off. Max knew Bob was a rabbit now. But Max did not get mad. Max liked his new friend and let Bob eat the celery. They were happy friends and shared the celery farm.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One day, he went to the park to play. He saw a green line on the ground. Tim was curious and wanted to discover where the line went. Tim followed the green line. It went up and down, and all around. It was a very long line. Tim felt tired, but he wanted to see where the line ended. As he walked, he saw a big tree. The green line went around the tree. Then, something unexpected happened. The green line moved! It was not a line, but a big green snake. The snake looked at Tim and smiled. Tim was not scared. He and the snake became friends. They played in the park together and had lots of fun.
