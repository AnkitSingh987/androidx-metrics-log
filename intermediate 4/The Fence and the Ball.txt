Category: Intermediate
Title: The Fence and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a fence. The fence was fake. It was not a real fence. It was made of paper. The fence was in a big yard. The yard had a house. In the house lived a boy and his family. The boy liked to play in the yard. He liked to run and jump. He liked to play with his ball. One day, the boy was playing with his ball. He kicked the ball very hard. The ball went over the fake fence. The boy was sad. He wanted his ball back. He went to the fake fence. The fence was not strong. The boy tried to climb the fence. The fence broke. The boy fell down. He was hurt. The boy cried. His family came out of the house. They saw the broken fence. They saw the hurt boy. They were not happy. The boy's family had to fix the fence. The boy could not play in the yard for a long time. The boy was sad. The family was sad. The end was bad.

One day, a boy named Tom found a bathrobe in his room. He thought it would be fun to act like a king. He put on the bathrobe and pretended to wear a crown. He walked around the house saying, "I am King Tom!" Tom saw his sister, Sue, eating a bitter apple. He said, "Sue, give me that apple. I am the king!" Sue did not like how Tom was acting. She said, "No, Tom. You are not a real king. You should be nice." Tom thought about what Sue said. He knew she was right. He said, "I am sorry, Sue. I will be a nice king." From that day on, Tom acted kind and shared with his sister. They played together and had lots of fun. The moral of the story is to be kind and share with others.

Once upon a time, there was a big crane. The crane liked to stay near the water. He had many friends who lived there too. The crane was very happy. One day, a rich man came to the water. He saw the crane and said, "Hello, Mr. Crane! I like your home. Can I stay here too?" The crane thought for a moment and then said, "Yes, you can stay, but you must be nice to my friends." The rich man agreed and built a small house near the water. He was nice to the crane's friends, and they all played together every day. The crane, the rich man, and their friends stayed happy by the water for a long, long time.

Once upon a time, there was a little monkey named Momo. Momo loved to eat bananas. They were his favorite food. He thought bananas were very healthy and yummy. One day, Momo found a big banana tree. He was so happy! Momo said, "Wow! So many bananas!" He started to eat the bananas. As he ate, he saw another monkey. The other monkey said, "Hello, I am Lulu. Can I have a banana too?" Momo said, "Yes, you can have one." As Momo and Lulu were eating, they saw the banana tree bow like it was saying thank you. They were very surprised! They looked closer and saw a little fairy. The fairy said, "Thank you for sharing the bananas. I will give you a magic gift." And just like that, Momo and Lulu could now talk to all the animals in the forest. They made many new friends and had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a big, thick cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to nap all day long. He would nap on the couch, on the floor, and even on the soft grass outside. One sunny day, Fluffy was napping on the couch. He was dreaming about chasing a big ball. Fluffy's friend, a little bird named Chirpy, flew in through the window. Chirpy saw Fluffy napping and wanted to play. Chirpy flew down to the couch and gently pecked Fluffy's soft fur. Fluffy woke up from his nap and saw Chirpy. Fluffy was happy to see his friend and they played together all day long. After playing, Fluffy and Chirpy were very tired. They both took a long nap on the thick, soft couch.
