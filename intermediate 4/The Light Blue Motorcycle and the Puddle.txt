Category: Intermediate
Title: The Light Blue Motorcycle and the Puddle

One day, a light blue motorcycle went for a ride. It liked to go fast and make loud sounds. The sun was shining and the sky was clear. The motorcycle saw a big puddle on the road. It went very fast and splashed into the puddle. Water went everywhere. The motorcycle had fun and did it again and again. After playing in the puddle, the light blue motorcycle went home. It was wet and dirty, but it was happy. The motorcycle had a great day splashing in the puddle.

John and Mommy were out walking one day. It was hot, and they were feeling tired and a bit sweaty. Suddenly, John saw a big screen up ahead. He smiled and started running towards it. Mommy called him back and said, "We can't open that, John." But John just ignored her and kept running. When he got to the screen he yanked on the handle, but it wouldn't open. "See, John? I told you it won't open," Mommy said. She put her hand on John's head and said, "Let's get some ice-cream. That will make you feel better. Plus, it'll be nice and cool." John smiled, and ran back down the hot street with Mommy. When they got back home, John opened the front door. To his surprise, a big, beautiful screen was mounted inside the house. It was glowing bright, and it looked so cool. John was so excited that he jumped up and down. With a smile on his face, he said to Mommy, "Now I know why you wouldn't let me open that other screen! It was just a setup for this one!"

Once upon a time, there was a big, scary monster. The monster had a big bottle of medicine. He liked to squeeze the medicine out and make a big mess. The monster lived in a dark cave, and he was always alone. One day, a little bunny came to the cave. The bunny was looking for a new home. The scary monster saw the bunny and had an idea. He wanted to play with the bunny and share his medicine. The bunny was scared, but the monster said, "Don't be scared. I just want to play." The monster took the medicine and squeezed it all over the cave. The bunny tried to help, but the medicine was too slippery. The bunny slipped and fell. The scary monster tried to catch the bunny, but he slipped too. They both fell and made a big mess. The bunny was very sad, and the monster was sad too. The medicine was not fun to play with after all.

Tim and Lily were playing in the garden. They liked to dig holes and look for bugs and worms. They had a big shovel and a small shovel. Tim had the big shovel and Lily had the small shovel. "Look, I found a root!" Tim said. He pulled out a long, brown root from the dirt. It was longer than his arm. "It is from a tree. Maybe it is a magic root." "Can I see?" Lily asked. She wanted to touch the root. She reached out her hand. "No, it is mine!" Tim said. He held the root close to his chest. "You have your own shovel. Go find your own root." Lily felt sad. She did not like it when Tim was mean. She did not want to play with him anymore. She put down her small shovel and walked away. "Where are you going?" Tim asked. He saw Lily leaving the garden. He did not want to be alone. "I am going to play with my doll," Lily said. "You can keep your root. I quit." She went inside the house and closed the door. Tim looked at his root. It was not so fun anymore. He wished he had shared it with Lily. He wished he had not been mean. He put down his big shovel and ran after Lily. He knocked on the door. "Lily, I am sorry," Tim said. "Can I play with you and your doll? Can you play with me and my root? We can be friends again." Lily opened the door. She saw Tim holding the root. She smiled. "Okay, Tim," Lily said. "I forgive you. Let's play together. Maybe the root is magic after all."
