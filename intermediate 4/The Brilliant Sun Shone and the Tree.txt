Category: Intermediate
Title: The Brilliant Sun Shone and the Tree

One day, a brilliant sun shone in the sky. A boy named Tim went outside to play. He saw a grill near the big tree. Tim wanted to ring the bell on the tree to call his friends. Tim climbed the tree to reach the bell. When he rang the bell, he heard a funny sound. It did not sound like a bell. Tim was very surprised. Then, a big bird came out of the tree. The bird said, "Thank you for ringing the bell. I was stuck in the tree, and now I am free!" Tim was happy to help the bird. They became good friends and played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little house in a calm town. In this house, there lived a happy family. One day, the family decided to celebrate Mom's birthday. Dad said, "Let's have a big party!" So, they invited all their friends and family. They had cake, balloons, and games. Everyone was having a great time. But then, a big wind came and blew the house down. All the people were sad. The party was over, and the family had no house to live in. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish who loved to swim. One day, while swimming in the water, he saw a big frog. The frog was green and jumped very high. The little fish was confused because he had never seen a frog before. The little fish stared at the frog and the frog stared back. They looked at each other for a long time. Then, the frog said "Hello!" and the little fish said "Hi!" They became friends and played together in the water. After a while, the little fish taught the frog how to swim better. The frog was very happy and they swam together every day. They were the best of friends and had lots of fun in the water. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big, hairy dog named Max. Max had an itch on his back. He tried to scratch it, but he could not reach it. One day, Max met a little cat named Lily. Max asked Lily, "Can you help me with my itch?" Lily looked at Max's back and saw the itch. She said, "Yes, I can help you, Max." Lily climbed on Max's back and scratched the itch. Max was very happy. "Thank you, Lily!" he said. Lily answered, "You're welcome, Max!" From that day on, Max and Lily were best friends. They played together every day and always helped each other.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a poor girl named Lily. Lily had a pretty veil that her mom gave her. She loved her veil very much. Every day, she wore it when she went outside to play. One day, while Lily was playing, a big wind came. The wind took her veil and carried it far away. Lily was very sad and began to struggle to get her veil back. She ran and ran, trying to catch the wind. But the wind was too fast, and Lily was too tired. She could not catch the wind or her veil. Lily went home, still feeling sad. She told her mom what happened. Her mom hugged her, but they knew the veil was gone forever.

Once upon a time there was a boy called Max. He liked to play with lumber in the backyard. He would push it around and make towers. Today, Max was feeling extra brave. He decided to push the lumber through the hallway of his house. He gathered up the lumber and took a deep breath. He said, "I'm going to do it!" He pushed the lumber slowly and carefully. He said, "This is easy!" Max was almost done pushing the lumber when he heard his mum's voice. "Max, what are you doing?" she said. Max smiled and said, "I'm pushing the lumber through the hallway. It's easy!" His mum smiled and said, "That's very brave of you. Now let's put the lumber away." Max was pleased. He helped his mum put the lumber away and then went back to playing in the backyard. He was proud that he had pushed the lumber through the hallway - it was not so easy after all.
