Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Squirrel

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy sword. The sword was flexible. He liked to play with it all day. Tim would swing the sword and pretend to be a hero. One day, Tim went to the park with his sword. He saw a big tree. Tim wanted to climb the tree with his sword. He put the sword in his pocket and climbed up. When he got to the top, he wanted to play with his sword again. But the sword was gone. It vanished from his pocket. Tim was very sad. He looked around but could not find it. Then, he saw a squirrel holding his sword. The squirrel ran away with the sword. Tim could not get it back. The squirrel took the sword to its home, and Tim never saw his sword again.

Mama and John went to the zoo. John was excited. He had not been to the zoo before. It was smelly. John saw many animals. He saw monkeys, tigers, lions, and elephants. He even saw a big bird! Mama gave John a pencil and a piece of paper. She said, "John, you can write what you have seen today at the zoo." John was happy. He wanted to write about all the animals he saw. John drew a picture and wrote the names of all the animals beside it. He wrote elephant, tiger, lion, and monkey. John was so happy. He finished writing! Mama said, "John, you have done an excellent job!"

Once upon a time in a land far away, there was a gifted girl named Lily. She was very good at climbing. One day, she saw a big volcano. She wanted to reach the top. Lily asked her friend Tim, "Do you want to climb the volcano with me?" Tim said, "Yes! Let's go!" So, they started to climb the volcano together. It was hard, but they helped each other. When they reached the top, they saw a beautiful view. Lily and Tim were happy. They learned that when you work together, you can do anything. And that was the best gift of all.

Once upon a time, there was a child named Tim. Tim liked to stretch every day. One day, Tim went to the park to play. He saw a big tree and thought it would be fun to stretch by the tree. As Tim was stretching, a foolish bird flew down and landed on the tree. The bird saw Tim and started to copy him. Tim thought it was funny and laughed. They both stretched together, having fun. Then, something unexpected happened. The bird started to talk! "Thank you, Tim," said the bird. "I was stuck in that tree, and your stretching helped me get free!" Tim was surprised but happy that he could help his new bird friend.

One day, a little puppy named Spot went for a walk. He saw a big tree and lay down under it. Spot was very happy to rest in the cool shade. While he lay there, he saw a small, round thing on the ground. It was a sour fruit. Spot sniffed it and thought it smelled good. He wanted to taste it, so he took a small bite. The sour fruit was not good for Spot. He started to feel sick. Spot tried to walk back home, but he could not. He lay under the tree, feeling very bad. No one found Spot, and he stayed there, feeling sick and sad.

Once upon a time, there was a big mattress. It was in a room with many other mattresses. They all wanted to be the best mattress. They were competitive. One day, a little girl came into the room. She stared at all the mattresses. She looked at the big mattress and smiled. The big mattress felt very happy. It thought it would be picked as the best mattress. But then, the little girl walked to a small mattress in the corner. She jumped on it and laughed. The big mattress was sad. It did not expect the little girl to pick the small mattress. The big mattress learned that being the best is not always about being the biggest.
