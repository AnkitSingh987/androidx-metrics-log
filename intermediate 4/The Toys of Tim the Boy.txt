Category: Intermediate
Title: The Toys of Tim the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a sad face because he thought his toys were boring. He wept and did not want to play with them anymore. One day, Tim's mom saw him weeping and asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim said, "My toys are boring, and I don't want to play with them." His mom thought for a moment and then said, "I have an idea! Let's go outside and see if we can find something fun to do." When they went outside, they saw a big, colorful box near the tree. Tim was excited and opened the box. Inside, he found a magic wand! He waved the wand, and his boring toys turned into fun, new toys. Tim's face was no longer sad, and he played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was an impressive mill. The mill was big and tall. It was where people went to store their grain. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the mill with his mom. They had a big bag of grain to store. Tim looked at the mill and said, "Wow, mom! This mill is so big!" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. It is a very special mill. We store our grain here, and the mill turns it into flour." Tim was excited. He watched as the big mill worked. He saw the grain go in and the flour come out. He knew that the flour would be used to make yummy bread. From that day on, Tim loved going to the mill with his mom. He was proud of the impressive mill in his town and the bread that it helped make.

Once upon a time, there was a kind witch. She lived in a big house. She was very wealthy. She had many friends. One day, a small girl came to the witch's house. The girl was sad. The witch wanted to help her. The witch had a magic ball. The witch told the girl to rub the magic ball. The girl rubbed the magic ball. Then, she was happy. The witch was happy too. They became good friends. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a strong aunt who could lift heavy things. Lily loved her aunt very much. One day, Lily wanted to have a party with her friends. She asked her mom if she could invite her friends to their house. Her mom said yes, and Lily was very happy. Lily's strong aunt came to help set up the party. They had a lot of fun together. When Lily's friends came, they all played games and ate yummy food. Lily was happy that her aunt could be there too.

One day, a little girl named Sue was playing in her room. She had a big box and some tape. She wanted to make a car out of the box. Sue was worried that she would not finish the car before her mom called her for dinner. So, she started to hurry. Sue's friend, Tom, came to play. He saw Sue's car and said, "Wow! Can I help?" Sue said, "Yes, but we have to hurry. Mom will call us soon." Tom took the tape and they started to make the car together. They were having so much fun. Just as they were about to finish the car, they heard a noise. It was a real car! The car drove into the room and said, "Hello, I am a magic car. I can take you for a ride." Sue and Tom were so surprised. They got in the magic car and went for a fun ride. When they came back, it was time for dinner.

One summer day, a little girl named Sarah was playing in her garden. She wanted to have fun, but her brother ran away with her favourite toy. He was being selfish. Sarah decided to run after him so she could take back her toy. She ran as fast as she could, but she was getting tired. She huffed and puffed but kept running. Finally, she reached an old gnarled tree at the edge of the garden. There her brother was, happily playing with the toy. Without any hesitation, Sarah grabbed the toy and ran away, back to the garden. The little girl was so delighted to get her toy back that she ran around and around, enjoying the summer day. As Sarah was playing in the garden, her brother slowly moved closer and closer. The little girl was so focused on her game that she didn’t even notice. Suddenly, her brother sneaked up behind her and said, “Let’s play together, Sarah!” He knew that running away with her toy was selfish, but had a plan to make it up to Sarah. The little girl was so happy that she forgot about the whole incident. From that day onward, the siblings enjoyed playing together in the garden in the summer.
