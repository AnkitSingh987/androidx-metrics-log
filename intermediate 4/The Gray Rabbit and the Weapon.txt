Category: Intermediate
Title: The Gray Rabbit and the Weapon

Once upon a time, there was a gray rabbit. He wanted to run in the field, but he was too scared. Suddenly, he heard a loud sound and he paused. He looked around, but he couldn't see anything. He saw a weapon in the distance and he started to tremble. The rabbit asked the weapon, "Why do you make that sound?". The weapon replied, "I'm here to protect the field. I will make sure no one gets hurt". The rabbit felt relieved and thought to himself, "That's so nice of the weapon. It's here to help me". The rabbit thanked the weapon and hopped away. From that day on, the rabbit never felt scared again. He learned an important lesson: that big things can help little ones.

One day there was an empty box. Inside the box was a lime. The lime was all alone and did not know what to do so it asked the box if it could come out. The box thought for a while and then decided to permit the lime to come out. When the lime came out of the box it was very happy. It saw a wonderful world around it and decided to explore. The lime rolled across the grass and stopped to look at some flowers. After seeing everything it wanted to, the lime went back to the box. The empty box allowed the lime to come back inside. Now the lime was happy and did not have to be alone any longer. The lime stayed in the box for the rest of the day, feeling safe and loved. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly cat named Lily. Lily loved to play with her soft pillow. She would toss it up and catch it with her paws. Lily and her pillow were best friends. One day, Lily's pillow got very dirty. Lily knew she had to clean it. So, she took the pillow to the big tub of water. She dipped the pillow in the water and scrubbed it with her paws. The water turned brown, but the pillow became clean and shiny. Lily was very happy to have her clean pillow back. She hugged it tight and promised to keep it clean. From that day on, Lily and her pillow played together every day, and they lived happily ever after.

One day, a lively dog named Max went to the park. At the park, he saw his friend, a big cat named Tom. Max and Tom liked to play together. They ran and jumped and had lots of fun. Max found a big stick on the ground. It looked like an axe. He picked it up with his mouth and showed it to Tom. Max said, "Look, Tom! I found a big stick! Let's discuss what we can do with it." Tom thought for a moment and said, "We can play fetch! You throw the stick, and I will catch it." Max liked the idea, so he threw the stick as far as he could. Tom ran after the stick very fast. But something unexpected happened. When Tom caught the stick, it turned into a beautiful butterfly! Tom and Max were very surprised. They watched the butterfly fly away, and they laughed. From that day on, Max and Tom always remembered their magical day at the park.

Once upon a time, in a big park, there was a row of ducks. They swam in a pond all day. One day, a naughty boy named Tim came to the park. He liked to shout and scare the ducks. The ducks did not like Tim. One sunny day, a kind girl named Sue saw Tim being naughty. She told him, "Do not shout at the ducks. They are scared." Tim felt bad and said sorry to Sue. Sue showed Tim how to be nice to the ducks. From that day on, Tim did not shout at the ducks anymore. He became friends with Sue and the ducks. They all played together in the park. Tim learned that being kind was more fun than being naughty. And the ducks were happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy box full of toys. His favorite toy was a small, red cube. He loved to play with the cube all day long. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. Sam saw the red cube and wanted to play with it too. Tim did not want to share his cube, and this made Sam sad. They both wanted to play with the cube, but there was only one. So, they had a big fight. Tim's mom saw the fight and told them to pause. She said, "You both need to be honest and share the cube." Tim and Sam thought about it and decided to take turns playing with the cube. They learned that sharing can make playing more fun. And from that day on, they always shared their toys and played happily together.
