Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Curious Cat and the Bird

One day, a curious cat named Max went for a walk. Max saw a big tree with a shiny buckle on it. He wanted to play with the buckle, so he tried to climb the tree. Max was not good at climbing, but he wanted the buckle so much. A nice bird saw Max and wanted to help. The bird flew to the buckle and offered to bring it down for Max. Max was happy and said "yes, please." The bird tried to take the buckle off the tree, but it was stuck. Then, something unexpected happened. The tree started to shake and the buckle opened a secret door. Max and the bird were very surprised. They went inside the door and found a room full of toys and treats. Max and the bird played all day and had so much fun. The shiny buckle was not just a toy, but a key to a special place.

Tom and Mia liked to play with their toys in the big room. They had many toys: cars, dolls, blocks, books, and more. But they did not like to organize their toys. They left them on the floor, on the table, on the sofa, everywhere. One day, their mom said, "Tom and Mia, you have to organize your toys. If you don't, a witch will come and take them away. She likes messy rooms and hates neat ones. She will come tonight, so hurry up and put your toys in their boxes." Tom and Mia were scared of the witch. They did not want to lose their toys. They started to organize their toys as fast as they could. They put the cars in the car box, the dolls in the doll box, the blocks in the block box, and so on. They worked hard and soon the big room was neat. They were proud of their work and showed their mom. Their mom smiled and said, "Good job, Tom and Mia. You organized your toys very well. Now the witch will not come and you can keep your toys. But you have to organize them every day, or the witch will come back." Tom and Mia nodded and hugged their mom. They were happy and went to bed. They did not see the witch, but they heard a cold voice outside their window. It said, "Boo! I am the witch. I came to take your toys, but I see you organized them. How boring. I will go to another house where the children are messy. Maybe I will find some toys there. Bye-bye, Tom and Mia. Be messy next time, or I will come again." Tom and Mia shivered and hid under their blankets. They decided to organize their toys every day and never be messy again. They did not want to see the witch ever.

One day, a little boy named Tim was in the kitchen with his mom. Tim's mom was making a yummy snack with prunes. Tim wanted to help his mom, so he asked, "Can I help, Mom?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim. Please search for the prunes in the cupboard." Tim opened the cupboard and looked for the prunes. He saw a big box and a small box. The big box was full of prunes and the small box was empty. Tim picked the big box and gave it to his mom. His mom said, "Good job, Tim! Now we are ready to make our snack." Together, they made a tasty prune snack. Tim and his mom sat down and ate the snack. Tim was very happy that he helped his mom. They had a fun day in the kitchen.

Jimmy was a young lion who lived in the jungle. Every morning, Jimmy would practice roaring to the bugs and other animals that he could find. One day, Jimmy's older brother, Kevin, roared even louder than Jimmy. Jimmy got so jealous, but he still wanted to try to roar louder. Jimmy asked his brother, "Can you teach me how to roar louder like you?" Kevin smiled and said, "Sure. Practice makes perfect. I'll show you how to do it. Find some bugs and roar at them, it will help you get used to it!" Jimmy followed Kevin's advice and started roaring at the bugs. After several tries, Jimmy was finally able to roar louder than before. He was so proud of himself and he felt like he was on top of the world. Jimmy thanked his brother for showing him how to roar and he hopped around joyfully. From that day forward, Jimmy was the loudest lion in the jungle!
