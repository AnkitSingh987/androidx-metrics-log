Category: Intermediate
Title: The Rich Man and the Children

Once upon a time there was a rich man. He had lots of money, and would often spoil his children. One day, the rich man's children heard some news. They ran excitedly to the man, and asked, "What is the news?" The rich man smiled and said,"I have some good news. Tomorrow, I am going to spoil you with sweets." "Hurray!" shouted the children. They smiled and hugged the rich man. From that day on, the children always waited eagerly for the man to tell them some news.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was lonely because he had no friends to play with. One day, he saw a new neighbor move in next door. The neighbor was a little girl named Lily. Tim wanted to make friends with Lily, so he went to her house and said, "Hi! I am Tim. Do you want to play with me?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, I would love to play with you!" Tim and Lily played together every day. One day, Lily saw Tim's toy car and asked, "Can you lend me your toy car, please?" Tim said, "Of course, you can play with my toy car!" They played with the toy car together and had a lot of fun. Now, Tim was not lonely anymore. He had a good friend in his neighbor, Lily. They shared their toys and played together all the time. And they lived happily ever after.

Every morning, John pours a bowl of cereal. He eats it quickly and then gets ready for school. John loves school! He loves to learn new things and play with his friends. Before school, John always puts on his modest clothes. He wears a navy shirt and some plain blue jeans. John looks very neat and tidy. John's school is calm and quiet. Everyone is kind and helpful. The teachers teach and the students listen. John learns a lot during the day. At the end of the day, John pours his bowl of cereal again and talks to his mom about his day at school. He smiles as he eats his cereal and looks forward to the next day.

Sue and Jack were best friends. They played together all the time, from morning to night. One day, Sue said "Let's play cricket!" But Jack said "No, let's stretch!" Sue was not happy. She wanted to play cricket, but Jack said "Stretching is fun!" Sue was disappointed and became very angry. She crossed her arms and said "Stretching is not fun!" Jack was confused, but he had an idea. He said "If we stretch first and then play cricket, it can be brilliant!" Sue thought about this and realized that Jack was right. She smiled, and they both began to stretch. When they had finished stretching, they played a game of cricket. It was brilliant! They had a lot of fun and continued to play until the sun went down.

Once upon a time, a lazy cat named Tom went to the market. Tom wanted to buy some fish to eat. The market was very big and loud. Tom looked at all the fish and picked the best one. He was very happy. On his way home, Tom felt tired. He saw a big tree and decided to rest under it. He put his fish down and closed his eyes. While Tom was resting, a sneaky dog named Spot came by. Spot saw the fish and wanted it for himself. So, he took the fish and ran away. When Tom woke up, he saw that his fish was gone. He was very sad. But then, he saw Spot eating the fish. Tom had a smart idea. He found a big stick and went to Spot. Tom said, "If you give me back my fish, I will give you this big stick to play with." Spot liked the stick more than the fish. So, they traded. Tom got his fish back, and Spot was happy with his new stick. From that day on, Tom and Spot became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a huge, soft, velvet bear. The bear lived in a big forest with many trees. One day, a little girl named Lily went into the forest. She saw the huge velvet bear and said, "Hello, bear! You are so big and soft!" The bear looked at Lily and said, "Hello, little girl! I am here to supply hugs to all the kids who come to the forest. Would you like a hug?" Lily nodded her head and smiled. The huge velvet bear gave Lily a big, warm hug. It made her very happy. After the hug, Lily and the huge velvet bear played in the forest. They ran around the trees and laughed. As the sun went down, Lily said, "Thank you for the hug and for playing with me, bear. I have to go home now." The bear nodded and said, "You're welcome, Lily. Come back anytime!" Lily waved goodbye to the huge velvet bear and went home, feeling happy and loved.
