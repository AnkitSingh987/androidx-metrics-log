Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Root

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Spot. Spot liked to play with a ball. One day, he saw a root in the ground. The root was big and fun to jump on. Spot had an idea. He wanted to attach his ball to the root. He found a long string and tied one end to the ball and the other end to the root. Now, Spot could play with the ball and the root at the same time. But something unexpected happened. When Spot jumped on the root, it started to move! The root was not a root at all. It was a big, friendly snake! The snake liked to play too. Spot and the snake played with the ball together and became best friends.

Once there was a boy who loved to slide. Every day after school he would go to the playground and slide down the slide. It was so much fun! He was always smiling. One day, his mom said to him, "You should get a job so you can make some money." The boy replied, "What is a job?" She said, "A job is something you do to make money. It can be anything from cleaning the house to helping in a store." The boy said, "That sounds hard! I just want to slide and be happy." His mom looked at him with an understanding smile and said, "You can do both. As long as you're not ignorant, you can do whatever you want in life." The boy smiled and said, "Ok. I'll get a job and I'll keep sliding too." So, the boy got a job and he kept having fun sliding. He was able to have the best of both worlds.

Once upon a time, there was a woman who lived in a small house. She had a cat and a dog. One day, the cat and the dog were playing outside. They were very happy. The woman was inside the house, making food. She was not happy. She was angry because the cat and the dog were making a lot of noise. The woman went outside and told the cat and the dog to stop playing. The cat and the dog did not want to stop. They insisted on playing. The woman became very angry. She told the cat and the dog to go away from her house. The cat and the dog were sad. They walked away from the house. They did not know where to go. They were lost. The woman was still angry. She missed the cat and the dog, but she did not look for them. The cat and the dog never came back to the house. The woman was alone and the cat and the dog were still lost. This is a bad ending.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who loved to explore. She had an incredible idea. She wanted to attach a stair to the big tree in her garden. She asked her dad to help her and he said yes. The little girl took some rope and some wooden planks. She and her dad worked together to attach the pieces of the stair. When they finished, the little girl couldn't believe it. She had an incredible new stair that she could climb up to the top of the big tree. The little girl was so excited. She ran up the stair and looked out from the top. She shouted down to her dad, "Come up and see the view from here! It's incredible!" Her dad smiled and shook his head. He said, "I can see it just fine from down here. You take care, and don't forget to come down and tell me what you discover!" The little girl waved down to her dad and began her incredible adventure. She climbed higher and higher, discovering new and wonderful things. She enjoyed her journey and was so happy to have her stair attached to the big tree.

Once upon a time, there was a little monster. This monster was not big and scary like other monsters. He was small and friendly. One day, he decided to go for a walk in the forest. As he walked, he saw a big, dead tree. The little monster wanted to play, so he started to push the tree. Suddenly, the tree moved! The little monster was very surprised. The tree was not a tree at all! It was a big, friendly giant who had been sleeping. The giant woke up and smiled at the little monster. They became friends and played together in the forest. The little monster was happy to have a new friend.
