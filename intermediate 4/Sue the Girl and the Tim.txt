Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Tim

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a girl named Sue. Sue had a big jar of sugar that she loved. She liked to use the sugar to make sweet treats for her friends. One day, she met a new friend named Tim. Tim was very slow, but he was very nice. Sue wanted to share her sugar with Tim. She told him, "I want to share my sugar with you, Tim. Let's make a treat together." Tim was very happy to hear this. They both went to Sue's house to make the treat. In the kitchen, Sue and Tim worked together to make cookies. They used the sugar and other things to make the cookies. They had lots of fun making them. When the cookies were ready, they shared them with their friends. Everyone loved the cookies and they were all very happy. Sue and Tim became best friends and they always shared their sugar and made treats for everyone.

Once upon a time there was a boy named Jack. He was a happy boy, who loved to play outside. One day, Jack was feeling a little sad. He sat outside and started to cry. His friend, Teddy, saw him and came over to check on him. Teddy said, “What’s wrong?” Jack sniffled and said, “I want a hug.” But Teddy did not want to give him a hug. He said, “No, hugs make me feel yucky.” Jack looked so sad and Teddy felt bad. He thought of a way to make Jack feel better. He said, “I can give you a soft pat on the back instead.” Jack thought about it for a moment and then smiled. He said, “Yes! That would make me feel better.” Teddy smiled back and gave Jack a soft pat on the back. Jack felt much better after that and went back to playing outside.

One day, a funny cat and a dog went out to play. They wanted to play until the sunrise. The cat said, "Let's work together and find a big ball to play with." The dog agreed and said, "Yes, let's work together, and we will have more fun!" They looked and looked for a big ball. The cat found a big red ball, and the dog found a big blue ball. They were very happy and played with the balls all day long. The sun started to go down, and the cat said, "We need to go home before the sunrise." The dog didn't want to stop playing. He said, "No, I want to play more!" The cat went home, but the dog stayed and played with the ball. The sun came up, and the dog was very tired. He couldn't find his way home, and he was sad.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had so much energy and was always full of joy. One day, Mia wanted to go explore the neighborhood. She asked her mom: "Mom, can I go outside and explore?" Mom said: "Yes, but stay safe and explore carefully." So Mia ventured outside. She saw so many cool things, like flowers and tall trees. But when she walked further, she came across an amazing garage. She peeked inside and saw junk everywhere - old books, rusty machines, and strange boxes. She got curious and decided to open the boxes. When she opened the first box, she made the most amazing discovery! Inside was a seed. Mia became so excited that she jumped up and down. She ran to her mom and showed her the seed. Mom smiled and said: "That's wonderful, Mia! We can plant the seed and watch it grow into something special." Mia was so excited! She was going to start a special project with her mom. What would become of this amazing seed? Only time would tell.

One day, a big cloud came. It started to rain. The rain fell on the ground. The ground got wet. A little boy named Tim looked out the window. He saw the rain. Tim put on his coat and boots. He went outside. He saw an enormous puddle. Tim jumped in the puddle. Splash! Water went everywhere. A little girl named Sue saw Tim. She laughed and said, "Hi! Can I play too?" They jumped in the puddle together. Splash! Splash! Then, something unexpected happened. The puddle began to shake. Tim and Sue stopped. They looked at each other. The puddle turned into a big, friendly frog! The frog said, "Thank you for playing with me!" Tim and Sue were surprised, but they smiled. They played with the frog all day.
