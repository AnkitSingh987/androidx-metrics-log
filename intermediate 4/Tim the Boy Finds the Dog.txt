Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Dog

One day, a boy named Tim found a big, round thing. It was a basketball. He did not know what it was, but he liked it. Tim went to the park with the basketball. He saw kids playing with a ball like his. They asked him to play, but Tim did not dare to play with the unknown kids. The kids were not nice. They took Tim's basketball and ran away. Tim was sad and wanted his basketball back. He thought of a plan. He would take the basketball when the kids were not looking. Tim went to get his basketball, but something unexpected happened. A big dog came out of nowhere and took the basketball. The dog ran away with the basketball in its mouth. Tim tried to get it back, but he could not catch the dog. In the end, Tim lost his basketball and never saw it again. He was very sad, but he learned to be careful with his things and not to trust unknown kids.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with his mom and dad. One day, Tim saw a bad mosquito in his room. It was very big and made a loud noise. Tim was scared and ran to his mom. "Mom, there is a bad mosquito in my room," said Tim. His mom went to Tim's room and saw the mosquito. She told Tim, "Don't worry, I will make the mosquito go away." She opened the window and the mosquito flew away. Tim was happy, and he hugged his mom. The next day, Tim was playing with his toy car. He wanted the car to go reverse, but he did not know how to do it. His dad saw him and said, "Tim, let me show you how to make the car go reverse." Tim's dad showed him, and Tim learned how to do it. Tim was very happy, and he played with his car all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little dinosaur named Dina. Dina was very thirsty. She went to her favorite spot to drink water. But when she got there, she saw the water was empty. Dina was sad. Dina's friend, Tommy the Turtle, saw her looking sad. He asked, "Dina, why are you sad?" Dina replied, "I want to drink water, but the water is empty." Tommy thought and said, "Let's go find more water." Dina and Tommy went on a long walk. They saw a big hill. Tommy said, "Let's go up the hill. We might find water there." Dina and Tommy climbed the hill together. At the top, they saw a big lake. Dina was so happy. She drank water and was not thirsty anymore. Dina and Tommy played by the lake all day. They were the best of friends.

One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to make a cake. She did not know how to make a great cake. So, she asked her mom for help. Her mom said, "I can help you make a great cake!" First, they put all the things they need in a big bowl. They mixed it all up. Then, they put the mix in a cake pan. They put the pan in the oven to cook. Lily and her mom waited for the cake to be done. When the cake was done, they took it out of the oven. It smelled so good! Lily and her mom put some sweet stuff on top. They both took a bite of the cake. It was the best cake they ever had. Lily was happy she now knew how to make a great cake.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She lived in a small house near a big desert. One day, Mia went for a walk in the desert with her mom. They walked and walked until they found a beautiful oasis. The oasis was full of green trees, soft grass, and cool water. Mia and her mom were very happy to find this special place. Mia saw a little bird near the water. The bird looked thirsty, so Mia gave it some water to drink. The bird said, "Thank you, Mia! I was so thirsty. You are very kind." Mia smiled and said, "You're welcome, little bird. I'm happy to help." As a thank you, the little bird brought Mia a gift. Mia received a pretty flower from the bird. The flower was soft and smelled very nice. Mia and her mom took the flower home and put it in a vase. They would always remember the beautiful oasis and the kind little bird.
