Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Creature

Once upon a time there was a girl named Sue. She was so eager to go out and play that she ran outside as soon as possible. When she was out, she noticed a funny-looking creature hopping around. She asked the creature: "Who are you?". The creature replied in a rude way: "Hey! I'm a stitch. I don't like being disturbed. Now leave me alone!" Sue was so scared that she started running away from the creature. But the creature followed her. She then remembered a trick she had heard. She stopped running, turned around and shouted: "Shoo! You are so rude. Go away!" The stitch was so surprised that he flew away and never came back. Sue was so happy that she was able to get rid of the stitch. She went back to playing with a smile on her face.

One day, a gentle nurse named Sue went for a walk. She liked to march around the park to get some fresh air. While she was marching, she saw a little boy named Tim. Tim was sad because he lost his toy. Sue asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim said, "I lost my toy and I can't find it." Sue wanted to help Tim, so they both started to look for the toy together. They marched all around the park, looking under trees and behind bushes. Finally, they found the toy near a big tree. Tim was so happy and said, "Thank you, Sue!" Sue smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim. Let's march back home together." And they marched back home, happy to have found Tim's toy.

Once upon a time, there was a big green hippo. The hippo lived in a pond. One day, a little bird came to the pond. The bird saw the green hippo and said, "Why are you green? Hippos are not green." The green hippo said, "I don't know. I have always been green." The bird thought for a moment and said, "I have an idea. Let's ask our friends if they know why you are green." The bird and the green hippo went to ask their friends. The friends did not know why the hippo was green. They all started to think. The wise old turtle had an idea. He said, "I suggest we go to the big tree and ask the wise owl." So, the bird, the green hippo, and their friends went to the big tree to ask the wise owl. The wise owl listened to their story and said, "Ah, I know why the hippo is green. The hippo has been eating green leaves from the magic tree. If the hippo stops eating the green leaves, he will be a normal color again." The green hippo stopped eating the green leaves from the magic tree, and he turned back to his normal color. The hippo and his friends were happy, and they all played together in the pond.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a polite girl named Lily. She was kind to everyone and always said "please" and "thank you." Lily had a big dream to bring peace to her town where everyone would be happy and play together. One day, Lily went to the park and saw kids fighting over toys. She thought of her dream and knew she had to help. Lily walked over to the kids and asked, "Can we all play together and share the toys?" The kids stopped fighting and agreed to play together. They had so much fun and thanked Lily for her help. As time went on, Lily continued to help others in her town. She showed them how to be polite and kind to each other. Soon, the town became a peaceful place where everyone was happy. Lily's dream had come true, and she knew that being polite and kind was the key to bringing peace and happiness to her town.

One day, a new dish came to live in the kitchen. It was very pretty and shiny. All the other dishes liked the new dish. They wanted to be friends with it. The new dish said, "Hello, I am new here. I hope we can be friends." The dishes planned a big party to welcome the new dish. They wanted to make the new dish happy. They knew the new dish liked music, so they asked the spoons to play a song. The spoons played a nice song for the new dish. At the party, the dishes and the new dish had lots of fun. They danced and laughed. Then, something unexpected happened. The new dish started to dance so fast that it fell and broke into pieces. The dishes were very sad. But then, they saw that the broken pieces made a beautiful picture on the floor. The dishes were so happy and they all started to applaud. The new dish was happy too, because it was still part of the fun.
