Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to pick flowers in the park. One day, Lucy saw a small bag. It was full of candy! She was very happy and ran to show her mom. When she got home, she opened the bag to eat the candy. But, oh no! The candy was all wet and sticky. Lucy felt miserable. She didn't want to eat the wet candy. Then, her mom had a great idea. She put the wet candy in a big bowl and mixed it with ice cream. Lucy tasted it and it was the best ice cream she ever had! The wet candy turned into a yummy treat, and Lucy was happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a modest angel. She lived in a small house near a big tree. The angel was very nice and always helped her friends. One day, the angel saw a big ball stuck in the tree. Her friend, a little dog, was sad because he could not get his ball back. The angel wanted to help the little dog. The angel tried to poke the ball with a long stick. She poked and poked, and the ball started to move. At last, the ball fell down from the tree. The little dog was so happy! He thanked the modest angel for her help, and they played with the ball together.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to play outside. She found a pretty, hard marble on the ground. It was shiny and round. Lucy was very happy with her new toy. Lucy ran to show her friend, Tom. "Look, Tom!" she said, revealing the marble in her hand. "I found a pretty marble!" Tom looked at the marble and smiled. "Wow, that's nice!" he said. Lucy and Tom played with the marble all day. They rolled it on the ground and threw it in the air. They had so much fun with their new toy. At the end of the day, Lucy put the marble in her pocket and went home, excited to show her mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little gray bunny named Skip. Skip loved to hop and skip all day long. He had a special power that made him very happy. One day, Skip went to play with his friends in the big green field. He showed them his special power. When he skipped, he could jump very high and touch the sky. His friends were very excited to see his power. They all played and skipped together in the field. Skip taught his friends how to jump high like him. They had lots of fun and were very tired at the end of the day. Skip was happy to share his power with his friends, and they all went home to rest.

Once upon a time, in a charming little town, there lived a boy named Tom. Tom loved to play with his shiny coins. He had a big box where he kept all his coins. One day, he decided to take his coins to the park. At the park, Tom met a girl named Sue. They played on the swings and slides together. They laughed and had so much fun. Then, something happened. Tom's coin fell out of his pocket and rolled away. Tom and Sue looked for the coin everywhere. They looked under the swings and slides. They looked in the grass and behind trees. Finally, they found the coin hiding in a small hole. Tom and Sue were so happy! They went back to playing, and they became best friends.

Once upon a time there was a girl who was very strong. She loved to pretend and she was very good at it. One day she found a long cord and she decided to pretend it was a rope for climbing. She believed that if she was strong enough she could climb up it to the top of the tallest tree. But when she grabbed the cord it started to slip from her hands. She tried so hard to hold on, but it was too strong. Frustrated and exhausted, she dropped the cord and cried out loud. Just then a butterfly flew by and told her to use her strength in different ways. He said she could be strong by being brave and finding other ways to pretend. The girl thought about what the butterfly said and decided to play pretend in different ways. She found objects in the garden to build a pretend castle and she created stories and adventures. She smiled and realized that her strength could help her have plenty of fun.
