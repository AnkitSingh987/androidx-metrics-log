Category: Intermediate
Title: The Slow and Lazy Rabbit and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a slow and lazy rabbit who liked to take naps. One day as he was snoozing in his burrow, he noticed a notebook floating by. He peeked out of his hole to see whose it was, but instead of a person he found a bee. "Excuse me," said the bee, "but I am looking for a notebook that I lost. Can you help me?". The rabbit smiled and said, "I think I can help you!" The bee was so excited, so the rabbit quickly grabbed the notebook and handed it to her. "Thank you so much," said the bee. "Now I can continue with my work". The rabbit watched as the bee flew away. Suddenly, a loud noise disturbed the peacefulness of the forest. The rabbit looked around, but he couldn't see anything. He went back to his burrow to take a nap and soon forgot about the noise. The end.

One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to wear her new swim clothes. She was very happy because they were light and pretty. Lily told her mom that she wanted to go to the pool with her friends. Her mom said, "Okay, but first, let's eat lunch." Lily was excited and could not wait to swim. After lunch, Lily and her friends went to the pool. They saw a big sign that said, "No running." Lily told her friends, "We must walk slow and be safe." They all walked slow to the pool. Lily was the first one to jump in the water. She felt the cool water on her skin and started to swim. Lily and her friends had a great time swimming and playing games in the pool. They were careful not to run around the pool because they remembered the sign. At the end of the day, Lily's mom came to pick her up. She was proud of Lily for being safe and following the rules. Lily had a great day and could not wait to wear her light swim clothes again.

Lila and Ben were camping with their dad in the woods. They had a tent, a fire and some marshmallows. They liked camping, but it was very cold at night. "Dad, I'm shivering," Lila said. "Can we have more fire?" "Sorry, sweetie, we don't have more fuel. We used all the wood we found. We have to stay in the tent and use our blankets," Dad said. "But I'm not sleepy," Ben said. "Can we tell stories?" "Okay, we can tell stories for a little while. But then we have to sleep, okay?" Dad said. They crawled into the tent and zipped it up. Dad told them a story about a brave knight and a friendly dragon. Lila and Ben listened and smiled. They liked stories. Soon, they felt warmer and sleepier. They yawned and cuddled with Dad. "Good night, Dad," Lila said. "Good night, Lila. Good night, Ben," Dad said. "Good night, Dad. Good night, Lila," Ben said. They closed their eyes and fell asleep. They dreamed of fire and marshmallows and dragons. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Lily was an original girl, she liked to wear funny hats and dance in the rain. One day, Lily went for a walk in the woods behind her house. As Lily walked, she saw a big rock. She wanted to climb it. But as she climbed, she started to stumble. "Oh no!" she cried. Her mom had told her to be careful, but Lily didn't listen. Lily fell down and hurt her knee. She cried and cried. A squirrel came by and said, "Why did you climb the rock? You should have listened to your mom." Lily knew the squirrel was right. She was sad and wished she had listened to her mom. The squirrel left, and Lily had to walk home with a hurt knee.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom loved to count. He counted everything he saw. One day, Tom went to school. At school, he had an exam. The exam was about counting. Tom was very happy. Tom saw a girl named Jane. Jane was attractive. She had pretty eyes and a nice smile. Tom wanted to be friends with Jane. He thought maybe they could count together. Tom and Jane did the exam. They counted all the things in the room. They counted the chairs, the tables, and the books. After the exam, they played together. They counted the flowers in the garden. Tom and Jane became good friends.
