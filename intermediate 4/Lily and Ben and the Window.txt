Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Ben and the Window

Lily and Ben were very happy. They were going on vacation with Mom and Dad. They packed their bags and toys. They put on their coats and hats. They said goodbye to their dog, Spot. They got in the car and drove to the airport. They saw many planes and people. They checked in their bags and got their tickets. They waited in a long line to go through security. They had to take off their shoes and coats. They had to put their toys in a bin. Lily was scared. She did not like the loud noises and the strangers. She held on to her teddy bear. Ben was bored. He wanted to play and run. He saw a big window with a plane outside. He let go of Mom's hand and ran to the window. "Ben, come back!" Mom shouted. She tried to catch him, but there were too many people. Dad rushed after him, but he was too late. Ben reached the window and climbed on a chair. He banged on the glass and waved at the plane. The chair was not stable. It tipped over and Ben fell. He hit his head on the floor. He cried out in pain. Mom and Dad reached him and picked him up. They saw blood on his forehead. They were very scared and sad. They took Ben to the hospital. They missed their flight. They did not go on vacation. They stayed with Ben until he got better. They were grateful that he was alive, but they wished they had a happier vacation.

Once there was a brave little bear. His name was Benny. Every morning he'd wake up and watch the birds play in the trees. He'd watch the sun come up, and he'd make a brave promise to himself. One day, Benny went on an adventure. He walked through the woods with his eyes wide open. He bravely stepped over and around logs and stones, and he looked up to the sky in awe. Suddenly, Benny heard a noise. It sounded like a tiny voice. He peered around to see where it was coming from. It was a little mouse, who said, "Hello, brave bear. Would you like to watch the stars come out with me?" Benny smiled. He said, "Yes, I'd like that." So the brave little bear and the mouse watched the stars come out together, and they made a special promise to always watch the stars together.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue had a pretty bracelet. It was shiny and all her friends were envious. They wanted a bracelet like Sue's. One day, Sue was playing with her bracelet outside. She was happy. Sue put her bracelet on the ground to play with her ball. She kicked the ball and it started to roll. The ball hit the bracelet and the bracelet rolled away too. Sue was sad and looked for her bracelet. She looked under a big tree and found it. But something unexpected happened. When Sue picked up the bracelet, she saw another bracelet next to it. It was even more shiny and pretty. Sue was happy again. She took both bracelets and showed her friends. Now, all her friends were more envious. They wanted a shiny bracelet too. But Sue shared her new bracelet with her best friend. They both wore the pretty bracelets and played together with big smiles.

Once upon a time, there was a little bald man. He had a big red car. He loved to drive fast on the track. One sunny day, he went to the track to race. As he was racing, a big bug flew into the car. The bald man was scared. He tried to slap the bug away. But, he missed and slapped the wheel instead. The car turned very fast! The car went off the track and into a big mud puddle. The bald man was sad and dirty. But then, a little boy came to help. The boy pushed the car out of the mud. The bald man was happy again. He thanked the little boy and they became friends. From that day on, they went to the track together and had lots of fun.
