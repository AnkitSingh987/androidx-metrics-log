Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Hill

One day, a girl named Amy had a bike with pedals. She loved to go fast on her bike. She felt strong when she rode it. Amy and her friends would race with their bikes. They had so much fun. One sunny day, Amy went to the park to ride her bike. She saw a big hill and wanted to go down fast. She was so excited! Amy went up the hill, ready to ride down. She felt very strong. As Amy started to go down the hill, something unexpected happened. Her pedals fell off her bike! Amy was scared, but she did not fall. She used her feet to stop the bike. Her friends helped her fix the pedals. They all laughed and had fun. In the end, Amy was still strong and happy.

Once upon a time, a thief lived in a busy town. The thief liked to take things that were not his. People in the town did not like the thief because he made them sad. One day, the thief saw a big cake in a window. He wanted to eat the cake all by himself. So, he took the cake and ran away. The people in the town saw him and they were not happy. The thief found a quiet place to eat the cake. He took a knife to cut the cake into pieces. But before he could eat it, the people from the town found him. They took the cake back and said, "No more stealing!" The thief felt bad and promised to be good from now on.

Once upon a time, in a charming little house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in the soil and make mud pies. He would laugh and giggle as the mud squished between his fingers. One sunny day, Tim was playing in the soil when he found a small, shiny rock. He picked it up and put it in his pocket. Tim felt very happy and showed the shiny rock to his mom. She smiled and said it was a special rock. The next day, Tim went outside to play in the soil again. He took out the shiny rock and placed it on the ground. To his surprise, the rock started to wiggle and grow! It grew and grew until it turned into a big, friendly bear. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. The bear was not scary, but very nice. They played together all day long, and Tim knew he had found a magical new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a pet cat named Luna. Mia and Luna were adventurous. They loved to play outside and explore the big garden. One day, Mia and Luna found a big tree. Mia said, "Let's climb the tree, Luna!" Luna meowed and followed Mia up the tree. They saw a bird's nest with baby birds in it. The baby birds were hungry, so Mia and Luna decided to search for some worms to feed them. Mia and Luna found some worms and gave them to the baby birds. The baby birds were happy and full. The mama bird came back and saw what Mia and Luna did. She said, "Thank you for helping my babies!" Mia smiled and said, "You're welcome!" The mama bird gave Mia and Luna a kiss on their cheeks. After their adventure, Mia and Luna went back home. They were tired but happy. They had a fun day helping the baby birds. Before going to bed, Mia gave Luna a big hug and a kiss. They fell asleep dreaming of their next adventure.

Molly and Jack were best friends. Together they liked to share lots of fun. But today they were having trouble. Jack had a stuffed tiger he wanted to share, but Molly didn't want it. She was feeling stubborn and didn't want to play with it. Jack felt sad but he had an idea. He grabbed some crayons and paper, and drew a picture of a tiger. Molly thought it looked so cool she asked Jack to show her how to draw one too. So they shared the crayons and paper and started to draw. Soon they were both drawing and coloring different tigers. Soon they were both laughing and sharing their pictures. They even put them up on the wall so they could share them with their parents. Now they knew that when they share, lots of fun can happen.
