Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a brave boy. He loved to play with his cards. One day, he wanted to show his cards to his friend, Sam. Tim went to Sam's house. He knocked on the door. "Hi Sam, look at my cards!" Tim said. Sam looked at the cards and smiled. "Wow, those are nice cards, Tim!" Sam said. They both sat down and played with the cards. As they played, a big dog came into the yard. Tim and Sam were scared. But Tim wanted to be brave. He stood up and said, "Go away, dog!" The dog left, and Tim and Sam felt safe again. They went back to playing cards, happy that Tim was so brave.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was very independent. She liked to do things by herself. One day, she saw a big mattress in her room. Mia wanted to sit on the mattress all by herself. Mia tried to climb the mattress, but it was too high. She tried and tried, but she couldn't sit on it. Mia felt sad. She didn't want to ask for help because she wanted to do it alone. Then, Mia's mom came into the room. She saw Mia trying to climb the mattress. Mia's mom helped her sit on the mattress. Mia was happy. She learned that it's okay to ask for help sometimes. And that was the moral value of the story: even independent people need help sometimes.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a green frog near a big tree. The frog was very happy and loved to skip and jump. Lily thought the frog was funny and wanted to play with him. Lily and the frog played together all day. They skipped through the grass and laughed a lot. Then, Lily felt hungry, so she went home to eat. She had a yummy sandwich with butter on it. The butter was soft and smooth. After eating, Lily went back outside to find her green frog friend. They skipped and played until the sun went down. Lily was very happy to have a new friend. They said goodbye and promised to play again soon.

One day, a little boy named Tim was at the park. He loved to play on the swings and slide. He saw a big red ball and was ready to play with it. Tim pushed the ball with all his might, and it rolled very fast. He laughed and clapped his hands with joy. As the ball rolled, it went near a big dog. The dog was not happy and started to growl. Tim was scared of the dog, but he wanted his ball back. He knew the dog was a threat, but he was brave. Tim slowly walked to the dog and said, "Please, can I have my ball back?" The dog stopped growling and looked at Tim. Tim pushed the ball away from the dog and took it back. The dog just wagged its tail and walked away. Tim was happy to have his ball back and continued to play at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to eat pasta. She was very stubborn and only wanted to eat pasta for every meal. Her mommy would try to give her other food, but Mia would shake her head and say, "No, thank you. I want pasta!" One day, Mia's mommy made a big bowl of pasta for dinner. Mia was so happy. She sat at the table and started to eat. Suddenly, a tiny mouse came out from under the table. The mouse looked at Mia and said, "Can I have some pasta, please?" Mia was surprised to see the mouse, but she was also kind. She shook her head and said, "No, little mouse. You cannot have my pasta." The mouse felt sad, but it understood that Mia was stubborn about her pasta. So, the mouse went away to find food somewhere else. And Mia ate her pasta happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Emma. She loved to go exploring, and today she was going to explore a large, orange trunk. Emma took a piece of string, and tied it to the trunk, so that it would not get lost. She then opened the trunk and found some amazing things inside. "Look at this!" she said, showing her friend a big red flower. "It's beautiful, Emma," her friend said. Emma carefully put the flower back inside the trunk and tied the string to the trunk tightly. Then, she and her friend set off, exploring the world. When they returned, they found the orange trunk still tied shut, with all the treasures inside. They opened the trunk, and as they looked inside, they saw that there were even more amazing things inside than before. "Oh wow," Emma said, "this is magical!" Her friend smiled. "This is what happens when you tie the trunk tightly," he said, "all the secrets stay safe inside." Emma smiled too. She was glad she'd tied the trunk so tightly. She'd had an amazing day, and now she could keep all her treasures safe in the orange trunk for the next adventure.
