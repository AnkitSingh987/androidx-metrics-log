Category: Intermediate
Title: The Secret of the Cat and the Bird

One day, a cat and a dog were talking. The cat said, "I can perform a fun trick!" The dog said, "I want to see!" The cat jumped high and did a flip. The dog was very happy. He tried to do the flip too, but it was tough for him. The cat helped the dog practice the flip. They were having a lot of fun. Then, a big bird came and said, "You can't do flips here. It's a law!" The cat and dog were sad, but they listened to the bird. They went to find a new place to play and do their tricks. In the end, they found a big park where they could do flips and have fun together.

Once upon a time there was a little boy named Jack. He loved adventures. He wanted to find something special. One day Jack went for a walk in the forest. He wanted to search for a special, rare vine. He was so excited! Suddenly, Jack spotted a beautiful vine hanging from a tree. He ran towards it and tried to get it. But it was too high for him to reach. Jack started to cry. But then he had a brilliant idea. He asked his friend Harry to help him. Harry said he could help him if he made a race. Jack agreed and off they ran. Jack ran and ran until he was first. He had won the race! Jack got the vine and was very excited. It was a rare, beautiful vine! Jack showed the vine to everybody. He was proud of his special win!

Jimmy was so excited when he went to the park. Red and blue slides, little tents and a big maze - it was full of fun places to explore! But Jimmy quickly realised there was something strange about the maze. No matter which way he turned, he couldn't seem to find the right path. He had been inside the maze for ages and it seemed like he was never going to find his way out. Jimmy's parents started to quarrel with each other while they watched their son. They were worried that Jimmy wouldn't find his way out of the maze. Suddenly, Jimmy noticed something. He had spotted a useful clue that he had missed earlier - a little sign by the entrance of the maze. He had found a way to find his way out! Jimmy followed the sign and he quickly made his way back home. He was so proud of himself for finding his own way out of the maze - and his parents were proud of him too. Jimmy smiled, knowing he had been useful.

One day, a little girl named Amy was playing in her room. She saw a big bookcase with many books. She wanted to find a fun book to read. As she looked, she felt upset because she could not find one. Amy's mom saw her and said, "Why not enter the magic bookcase?" Amy was confused, but she went inside. When she entered, she found herself in a world filled with toys and games. She was so happy and played all day long. But then, something unexpected happened. The toys and games started to vanish! Amy was upset again. She had to find a way to bring them back. She found a magic wand and waved it. All the toys and games came back! Amy played in the magic bookcase world for a little more, and then she went back to her room. She was so happy that she could visit the magic bookcase anytime she wanted.

Sam and Mia like to watch cartoons on TV. They like the funny animals and the bright colors. Sometimes they laugh so hard that their mom tells them to be quiet. One day, they see a new cartoon. It is about a big bear and a little bird. The bear is very strong and the bird is very smart. They help each other and have adventures. Sam and Mia think they are very cool. "Look, Mia, the bear can lift a big rock!" Sam says. "Wow, he is so strong! He can support the bird when he flies!" Mia says. They watch the bear and the bird support each other and have fun. They learn new words and songs from the cartoon. They want to be like them. "Sam, do you want to play bear and bird?" Mia asks. "Yes, let's play! I will be the bear and you will be the bird!" Sam says. They go to their room and find some costumes. Sam puts on a brown shirt and a hat with ears. Mia puts on a yellow dress and a feather boa. They pretend to be the bear and the bird. They support each other and have adventures. They have a lot of fun.
