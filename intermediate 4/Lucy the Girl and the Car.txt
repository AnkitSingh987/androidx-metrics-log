Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Car

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a big tray in the kitchen. She wanted to see if all her toys could fit on it. Lucy was very excited to try this new game. Lucy put her noisy toy car on the tray first. Then she added her small ball, her teddy bear, and her blocks. The tray was getting full, but Lucy still had more toys to fit on it. As Lucy tried to fit her last toy on the tray, the noisy toy car fell off. Lucy laughed and picked up the car. She knew she could try again. This time, she put the toys on the tray in a different way. Lucy was happy when all her toys fit on the tray.

Once upon a time there was a girl named Daisy. Daisy wanted a rock that was beautiful. So she made an order for it. When it arrived, Daisy was delighted! She took the beautiful rock outside and put it in her garden. Daisy liked to play with the rock every day. She said hello to it and talked to it as if it was her best friend. The rock made Daisy very happy and she knew it was special. Daisy gave it lots of cuddles and said she would never give it away. The rock was with Daisy forever and she was the happiest girl in the world.

Once upon a time, in a big zoo, there was a zebra named Zara. Zara had bright stripes that she loved to show off. Every day, she would run and play with her zebra friends in the sun. One day, while playing, Zara found a big tree. She saw it had leaves that could cover her. Zara had a fun idea. She went under the tree and let the leaves cover her bright stripes. She was so good at hiding that her friends could not find her. Zara's friends looked and looked, but they could not see her. They were sad and started to cry. Then, Zara jumped out from under the tree and said, "Surprise!" Her friends were so happy to see her. They all laughed and played together again. From that day on, they loved to play hide and seek in the big zoo.

One day, a fish named Fin lived near a big reef. Fin loved to sing, but he had a terrible voice. All the other fish would swim away when they heard him sing. One day, a big shark came to the reef. The shark was mean and wanted to eat all the little fish. The little fish were scared and did not know what to do. Fin had an idea. He said, "I will sing to the shark, and maybe he will go away." Fin swam up to the shark and started to sing. His voice was so terrible that the shark got scared and swam away. All the little fish were happy and thanked Fin for saving them. The moral of the story is that even if you think you are not good at something, you can still help others in your own way.

Once upon a time, there was a family of birds. They lived in a yellow house. The family had a mom, a dad, and three baby birds. They were very happy. One day, the mom and dad went to find food. They told the baby birds to stay home. But the baby birds did not listen. They went to play outside. They saw a big tree with red fruit. The baby birds wanted to eat the fruit. A big, mean cat saw the baby birds. The cat wanted to eat them. The baby birds were scared. They tried to fly away, but the cat caught one. The other baby birds got away and went back to their yellow house. When the mom and dad came home, they were sad. They learned that the baby bird was gone. The family was not happy anymore. The moral of the story is to always listen to your mom and dad.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Mark. Mark liked to play with his toy truck. One day, Mark felt troubled. He did not know what to put in his truck to play. Mark saw his mom in the kitchen. She was putting food in a bag. Mark had an idea. He asked his mom if he could load his truck with the food. His mom said yes, but he had to be careful. Mark was happy and went to load his truck. Mark loaded the food into his truck and played all day. Then, he saw his dad come home from work. Mark had another idea. He drove his truck to his dad and showed him the food. His dad was happy and they all had a fun picnic outside. The troubled feeling went away and Mark learned that he could always find fun things to load and play with.
