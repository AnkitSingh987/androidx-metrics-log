Title: Tom and Max and the Door

One day, a cat named Tom and a dog named Max were friends. They lived in a tidy house. They liked to play and talk. Tom was very good at remembering things, but Max would often forget. One day, Tom said, "Max, don't forget to close the door when you go out to play. We don't want bugs to come in our tidy house." Max said, "Okay, Tom. I will remember." But Max forgot. He went out to play and left the door open. Bugs came into the house. They made a big mess. Tom was sad and angry. Tom said to Max, "You forgot to close the door! Now our house is not tidy." Max was very sorry. He tried to say sorry to Tom, but Tom did not want to be friends anymore. Max was sad and had to live his life without his best friend Tom.

Once upon a time, there was a little fairy. Her name was Fairy. She loved to play games and especially loved to make friends. One day, while she was playing in the garden, a big angry dragon flew out of the forest. The dragon was very angry. He roared and puff smoke towards Fairy. "Why you so angry?" asked Fairy. The dragon replied, "I was getting chased by hunters and they wanted to hurt me." Fairy felt very sorry for the dragon. She asked him, "What can I do to help?" The dragon said, "Could you please ask your friends to help me find a safe place to stay and some food to eat." So Fairy asked her friends for help and they agreed. She found a cave for the dragon to hide in and some food for him to eat. The dragon was very grateful. He thanked Fairy and her friends then he flew away. The moral of the story is that it is important to be kind to others, no matter how big or small. Even if we cannot help someone, it still important to be kind.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a boy named Tim. Tim had a purple toy. He liked to play with it every day. One day, he found a tooth in his room. It was a big tooth! Tim was very surprised. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, look at this big tooth I found in my room!" His mom looked at the tooth and said, "Oh my! That is a big tooth! Let's rub it and see what happens." So Tim and his mom rubbed the tooth together. As they rubbed the tooth, a big, friendly dinosaur appeared in their house! The dinosaur said, "Hello, Tim and Mom! I lost my tooth and I was looking for it. Thank you for finding it!" Tim and his mom were very happy to help the dinosaur. They all played together and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a brave little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. One day, a big storm came to their town. The wind blew hard, and the rain fell down. Lily's mom said, "Oh no, the storm broke our fence!" Lily wanted to help, so she said, "I can repair it, Mommy!" Her mom smiled and said, "You are very brave, Lily!" Together, they went outside to fix the broken fence. As they worked, the wind blew and the rain fell, but Lily was not scared. She held the pieces of the fence while her mom used a hammer to put them back together. The storm went away, and the sun came out. Lily and her mom were happy that they fixed the fence. They went inside and told Dad about their brave adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a famous dog named Max. Max loved to run and play with his friends. One day, Max and his friends went on a trip to the park. At the park, Max saw a big slide. "I want to go on the slide!" said Max. His friend, a little cat named Lily, was scared. "I don't know, Max. It looks too big for me," she said. Max smiled and said, "Don't panic, Lily. I will help you." Max and Lily climbed up the slide together. Max went first, and then he helped Lily. They both slid down the slide and laughed. They had so much fun on their trip to the park. From that day on, Max and Lily were not scared of the big slide anymore.
