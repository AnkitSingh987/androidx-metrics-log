Category: Intermediate
Title: Timmy the Boy and the Bird

One day, a little boy named Timmy went to the park with his mom. Timmy loved to eat grapes, so he brought some with him. While he was playing, he saw a big, pretty bird. Timmy wanted the bird to come closer, so he requested, "Bird, please come here!" The bird flew down and landed next to Timmy. He was so excited! But Timmy was careless and dropped his grapes on the ground. The bird saw the grapes and started to eat them. Timmy was sad because he wanted to eat the grapes too. Just when Timmy thought the bird would eat all his grapes, something unexpected happened. The bird picked up the last grape and gave it to Timmy. It turns out the bird just wanted to share! Timmy was so happy, and he and the bird became good friends. They played together in the park and shared grapes every day.

Once upon a time, there was a dirty car. The car was sad because it was so dirty. The car wanted to be clean and shiny. One day, a nice man named Tom came to the car. Tom said, "Car, you are so dirty. Let's drive to the car wash to make you clean." The car was happy to hear this. Tom got in the car and they drove to the car wash. At the car wash, they saw a big lock on the door. Tom asked a friend for help. The friend said, "I can help you open the lock." With the lock open, the car got a good wash. The car was now clean and shiny. The car and Tom were very happy. The moral of the story is to always help others when they need it.

Once upon a time, there was a big giant who lived in a far, far away place. The giant was very kind and loved to learn new things. He had many friends who lived with him in the distant land. One day, the giant wanted to learn how to jump high. He asked his friends to help him. They all tried to teach him, but the giant was too big and heavy. It was hard for him to jump high. The giant did not give up. He kept trying and trying. One day, with the help of his friends, the giant learned how to jump high. They were all very happy and had a big party to celebrate. The giant was proud that he learned something new, and his friends were proud of him too.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly frog. The frog lived near a small creek. The frog liked to hop and play with his friends. They would swim in the creek and have fun all day. One day, the friendly frog saw a little girl. The little girl was sad. She sat by the creek and cried. The frog wanted to help the little girl. He hopped over to her and gave her a big smile. The little girl saw the friendly frog's smile. She started to smile too. The frog and the girl played by the creek. They had so much fun. The frog and the girl became best friends. They smiled and played together every day.

Once upon a time, in a deep, deep forest, there lived a happy little bunny named Bobo. Bobo loved to celebrate with his friends. They would dance, sing, and play games all day long. Bobo would ask his friends for their opinion on which game to play next. They all loved to share their ideas. One day, while they were celebrating, something unexpected happened. A big, big bear named Benny came to join their party. At first, Bobo and his friends were scared because they thought Benny would be mean. But Benny just wanted to have fun and be friends with everyone. So, Bobo asked Benny for his opinion on which game to play. Benny suggested a new game they had never played before. It was called "Bear Hugs and Bunny Hops." Everyone played the game and had a great time. They all learned that day that even big, big bears can be kind and gentle. From that day on, Benny the bear became a part of their celebrations, and they all lived happily ever after.
