Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Mask

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was going to a fun party where everyone had to wear a mask. He was very excited and couldn't wait to go. When Tim got to the party, he saw a lot of masks. Some were big and some were small. But Tim thought he could have more fun without a mask. So he said, "I don't want to wear a mask. I want to play without it." His friend, Sarah, heard him and said, "But Tim, we all have to wear masks at the party. It's the rule." Tim didn't listen and played without a mask. He felt a little stupid because he was the only one without a mask. Later, Tim started to regret not wearing a mask. He saw how much fun everyone was having with their masks on. Tim decided to put on a mask and join the fun. He was so happy he did. The party was a lot more fun with the mask on. Tim learned that sometimes it's good to follow the rules and join in with everyone else. From that day on, Tim always remembered to wear a mask to parties and never felt stupid or regret again.

Once upon a time there was a bunny who loved summer holidays. She loved basking in the warm sun, eating ice cream cones, and playing in the park. One day, the bunny went to the park and saw a group of happy kids playing. The bunny was feeling envious of the fun they were having, so she acted brave and joined in the games. The kids welcomed the bunny with open arms and gave her an ice cream cone. It was a big, delicious cone with strawberry ice cream and whipped cream on top. The bunny felt so happy, she could barely contain herself. She ate the cone with great delight! Afterwards, the kids and bunny acted silly and laughed together. They played pretend, ran around, and drank from the bubbling water fountain. The bunny felt so warm and content. She loved being part of the group, and she couldn't wait for the next summer day!

Once upon a time, in a bright forest, there was a little bird. The bird loved to wake up early and sing. She liked her life in the forest. One day, the bird woke up and saw a big box. She flew to the box and looked inside. She found a big red ball. The bird was very happy with her new friend. But then, the ball started to talk! The bird was surprised. The ball said, "I am not a ball, I am a magic bug!" The bird and the magic bug played all day and became best friends. They had lots of fun in their bright forest life.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a little fox. The little fox had a fine brush. The brush was very special to him because it made his fur look nice and shiny. One day, a big bear saw the little fox with the fine brush. The big bear wanted the brush for himself. So, when the little fox was not looking, the big bear stole the brush. The little fox was very sad when he found out that his fine brush was gone. He looked for the big bear and asked for his brush back. But the big bear said "no" and ran away with the brush. The little fox never got his fine brush back and his fur was never shiny again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to eat toast with jam for breakfast. One day, Tim's mom was very busy and asked him to make his own toast. Tim was excited to try. Tim put the bread in the toaster and pushed the button. He waited and waited. He started to feel frustrated. Why was the toast taking so long? He decided to pause and think. Maybe the toaster was not working right. Tim called his mom for help. She came and checked the toaster. She saw that it was not plugged in. Tim's mom plugged it in and soon the toast popped up. Tim was happy and put jam on his toast. He learned that sometimes you need to pause and ask for help when you feel frustrated.
