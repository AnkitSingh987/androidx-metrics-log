Category: Intermediate
Title: Susie the Girl and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Susie. She was three years old and loved to explore. She was so nosy and wanted to know everything. One day, she wandered outside to the garden. She saw an old tree with a hole in it. Susie poked her head inside the hole and found a big rose petal. She felt a bit sad, and wondered why the petal was there. Just then, a little bird flew down to Susie. "Why are you so sad, little girl?" The bird asked. "I found a petal and it made me lose my dream," Susie replied. "Do not worry," said the little bird. "Your dream is safe, just keep it in your heart and never lose it." Susie nodded and hugged the bird. Then the bird flew away. From that day on, Susie kept her dream safe in her heart.

Lily was hungry. She wanted to eat something sweet. She saw a big store with many things inside. She pulled her mom's hand and said, "Mom, I want to go there!" Mom looked at the store and saw that it was a candy store. She shook her head and said, "No, Lily, we can't go there. We have to go home and eat lunch. Candy is not good for you." Lily did not listen. She stamped her foot and said, "No, no, no! I want candy! I want candy now!" She started to cry and scream. She demanded candy. Mom was tired and angry. She did not like Lily's behavior. She said, "Lily, stop it! You are being very rude and naughty. If you don't stop, we will go home and you will have no candy and no TV." Lily did not care. She wanted candy more than anything. She tried to run to the store, but Mom caught her and picked her up. Lily kicked and hit Mom. She said, "Let me go! Let me go!" Mom carried Lily to the car. She put her in the seat and buckled her up. She said, "Lily, you are in big trouble. You have to say sorry to me and to the store people. You have to learn to be polite and patient. You can't always get what you want." Lily was still hungry and angry. She did not say sorry. She did not learn anything. She pouted and said, "I hate you, Mom. I hate you and I hate lunch. I only love candy."

One day, a little girl named Sue went to her backyard with her mom. They wanted to feed the birds. Sue's mom was very reliable and always helped her. They took some bread and went outside. In the backyard, Sue saw a big bird. She said, "Mom, look! A big bird!" Her mom said, "Yes, Sue. Let's feed it." They gave the big bird some bread. The big bird was happy and flew away. Then, something unexpected happened. The big bird came back with all its friends. They all wanted more bread. Sue and her mom laughed and fed all the birds. The moral of the story is, when you are kind to others, they will bring more happiness to your life.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely dog named Spot. Spot was a happy dog who liked to play and run. One day, he got a boo-boo on his paw. Spot's mom saw the boo-boo and said, "Oh no, Spot! We need to go see the veterinarian." The veterinarian was a nice man who helped animals feel better. Spot was a little scared, but he hoped the veterinarian could help him. When they got there, the veterinarian said, "Hello, Spot! Let me see your boo-boo." Spot showed the veterinarian his paw. The veterinarian gave Spot a band-aid and a treat. Spot felt better and said, "Thank you!" The veterinarian told Spot to be careful when he played. Spot and his mom went home, and Spot played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big pot. The pot was full of yummy food. The food was very healthy. It had fruits, vegetables, and nuts. The pot was in a small house, where a nice family lived. One day, a little girl named Lucy came to the house. Lucy was hungry. She saw the big pot full of healthy food. Lucy wanted to eat the food in the pot, so she asked her mom if she could have some. Her mom said yes, and gave her a bowl full of the yummy food. Lucy started to eat the healthy food from the pot. She ate the fruits, the vegetables, and the nuts. She felt very happy and strong. Her mom was happy too because Lucy was eating healthy food. They shared the big pot of healthy food with their friends, and everyone felt happy and strong.
