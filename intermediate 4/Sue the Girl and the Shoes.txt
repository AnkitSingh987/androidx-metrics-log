Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Shoes

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to polish her shoes. She would make them shine so bright that everyone could see them. One day, Sue met a tough-looking boy named Sam. Sam was sad because he didn't know how to polish his shoes. "Hi, Sam!" said Sue. "I can help you polish your shoes. It's easy!" Sam looked at Sue and smiled. "Thank you, Sue! I want my shoes to shine like yours." Together, they polished Sam's shoes until they were shiny and clean. Sue taught Sam a happy song to sing while they worked. They sang the song and laughed as they polished the shoes. From that day on, Sue and Sam became best friends. They would always polish their shoes together and sing their happy song. And they lived happily ever after.

One night, Mia was looking up at the sky trying to find the stars. She spotted them twinkling high above. She wanted to go closer! Mia's mom said, "Mia, where do you want to go?" Mia said excitedly, "I want to go to the stars!" Mia's mom smiled and said, "Well you can't go to the stars this night, but let's go to the new park I heard about. Maybe we can find a star there." So Mia put on her shoes and they went to the new park. When they got there, Mia saw a shiny star swinging from a tree! She ran over and touched it. It wasn't an ordinary star, it was a new star made out of metal! Mia was so happy that she had found the star. She and her mom went back home with the star in hand and put it up on the wall. And every night when Mia looked up at the sky, she was reminded of the star in her room, the one she found at the new park.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. He loved to pretend he was a fireman. Every day, he would wear a red hat and play with his toy fire truck. He liked to help people, just like a real fireman. One sunny day, Tim saw a cat stuck in a tree. He thought, "I can help, just like a fireman would!" So, he put on his red hat and climbed the tree. Tim was very brave. He helped the cat get down from the tree. The cat was happy and so was Tim. Tim learned that even if you are young, you can still help others. He knew that pretending to be a fireman was fun, but helping others was even better. The moral of the story is: you don't have to be big to do big things.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was with his mom and dad. They wanted to eat at a restaurant. Tim was happy and excited. At the restaurant, Tim asked his mom, "Can I have my favorite food?" His mom said, "Yes, if it is available, you can have it." Tim felt very happy. They ate their food and had a good time. Tim felt full and sleepy. He said, "Thank you, Mom and Dad!" They all went home and had a good night's sleep.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play outside, but she hated when it rained because she couldn't go out. One day, while she was inside, she met a friendly spirit named Sam. "Hi, I'm Sam. I'm a spirit who makes the rain go away," said Sam. "Are you ready to play outside?" Lily was very happy and said, "Yes, I'm ready! I don't like rain. Can you make it stop?" Sam smiled and nodded. With a wave of his hand, the rain stopped, and the sun came out. Lily and Sam went outside to play. They ran, jumped, and laughed together. From that day on, Lily was never sad when it rained, because she knew Sam would always be there to make it stop.

Once there was a mommy and her little boy. The mommy wanted her little boy to eat lots of fruit. But the little boy was not interested in fruit. The mommy tried to encourage the little boy to try different fruits. She showed him colorful apples. They were red and green and yellow. The little boy thought they were beautiful, but he still didn't want to try them. The mommy tried to encourage him by telling him how sweet and juicy fruit is. But the little boy still didn't want to try it. The mommy was getting discouraged, but she was determined to make the little boy like fruit. Finally, she decided to cut up the fruit into shapes and create funny faces with it. The little boy thought it was hilarious and he finally took a bite of the fruit. The little boy loved it! From that day on the mommy and her little boy ate colorful fruit together every day.
