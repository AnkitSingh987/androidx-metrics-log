Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a lot of stuff in her room. One day, she saw a boy who did not have much stuff. He looked sad. Amy did not know why he was sad. She felt bad for him. She thought maybe he was ignorant about having fun with his stuff. Amy went to the boy and showed him her stuff. She shared her toys and played with him. The boy started to feel happy. He was not ignorant anymore. He learned that sharing stuff can make others feel good too. The moral of the story is to share your stuff with others. It can make them feel better and make you feel good too. Sharing is caring.

Once upon a time, there was a little frog. The little frog lived by a river. The frog liked to play and jump all day long. He had many friends who lived near the river too. They all had fun playing together. One day, the little frog saw a big rock. He wanted to jump on it. But his friends told him to be careful. They said the big rock was not safe. The little frog did not care. He wanted to jump on the big rock anyway. The little frog jumped on the big rock. But his friends were right. The big rock was not safe. The little frog fell into the river. He could not swim. His friends tried to help him, but it was too late. The little frog was gone. His friends were very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a peaceful little bunny named Ben. Ben loved to imagine fun things to do with his friends. One day, Ben found a big sweater in the woods. The sweater was soft and warm. Ben decided to share the sweater with his friends. He called his friends Sam the squirrel, Tina the turtle, and Max the mouse. They all came to see the sweater. They were very happy to see it. Together, they all imagined fun things they could do with the sweater. They played games, had a picnic, and even used the sweater as a blanket. At the end of the day, they all snuggled up in the warm sweater and felt very peaceful. They knew they would always remember the fun day they had with the big sweater.

One day, a little boy named Tim was very impatient. He wanted to go to the park and play with his friends. His mom said, "Wait, Tim. First, we need to find your safe." Tim and his mom looked all over the house for the safe. They looked under the bed, in the closet, and even in the kitchen. But they could not find the safe. Tim was getting more and more impatient. Finally, they found the safe in the garage. Tim's mom opened the safe and took out a special seat. "This is your car seat, Tim," she said. "We need it to keep you safe when we drive to the park." Tim was so excited to go to the park that he didn't even notice the seat was different. When they got to the park, Tim's mom took the seat out of the car. To Tim's surprise, the seat turned into a super fun scooter! Tim and his friends had a great time playing at the park with the scooter. And Tim learned that sometimes, being patient can lead to fun surprises.

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat named Tim. Tim loved to play with his friends. One day, Tim saw a calendar on the wall. The calendar had many colors and numbers on it. Tim called his friend, Sam the dog. "Look, Sam! What is this?" Tim asked. Sam looked at the calendar and said, "I don't know, but it's fun to look at!" They played near the calendar all day. When it was time to go home, the calendar started to disappear! Tim and Sam were very surprised. "Where did it go?" Tim asked. Sam didn't know. They looked everywhere for the calendar, but it was gone. Tim and Sam were sad, but they knew they would find more fun things to play with tomorrow. And so, they went home and slept, ready for a new day of fun and adventure.
