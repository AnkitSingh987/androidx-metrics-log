Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Monkey

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim went on a safari with his mom and dad. They saw many animals like lions, elephants, and monkeys. Tim was very happy. One day, Tim saw a dizzy monkey. The monkey was spinning in circles and fell down. Tim asked the monkey, "Are you okay, Mr. Monkey?" The monkey said, "Yes, I am just dizzy from spinning too much." Tim told the monkey, "You should not spin too much. It can make you fall." The monkey said, "I am sorry, Tim. I will not spin too much again." Tim said, "I forgive you, Mr. Monkey. Let's be friends." The monkey and Tim became good friends and played together on the safari.

Lily was a little cat who liked to play with her ball of yarn. She would roll it, chase it, and catch it with her paws. She was very happy with her ball of yarn, until one day, a big dog came into her yard. The dog saw Lily and her ball of yarn, and he wanted to play too. But he did not play gently. He barked loudly, ran fast, and bit hard. He grabbed the ball of yarn with his teeth and ran away. Lily was scared and angry. She wanted her ball of yarn back. She ran after the dog, but he was too big and too fast. He ran across the street, and Lily followed him. She did not see the car that was coming. The car honked its horn, and Lily stopped. She was in the middle of the street, and the car was very close. She was in trouble. "Meow!" she cried. She did not know what to do. But someone heard her cry. It was Max, a friendly mouse who lived in a hole near the street. He saw Lily and the car, and he wanted to help. He ran out of his hole, and grabbed Lily's tail with his teeth. He pulled her back to the sidewalk, just in time. The car passed by, and Lily was safe. "Thank you, thank you!" she said to Max. "You saved me!" "You're welcome," said Max. "I'm glad you're okay." "But what about my ball of yarn?" asked Lily. "The dog took it." "Don't worry," said Max. "I have an idea. Follow me." Max led Lily to his hole, where he had a stash of yarn. He gave Lily a new ball of yarn, and she was very happy. She thanked Max again, and they became friends. They played with the yarn together, and shared their stories. Lily learned that Max was a good mouse, and Max learned that Lily was a nice cat. They forgot about the dog, and the car, and the trouble. They only remembered the yarn, and the tail, and the save.

Once upon a time, there was a noisy cat. The cat liked to play all day. It would run and jump and make lots of noise. The cat's name was Kitty. One day, Kitty found a ball. The ball was not shiny. Kitty wanted to make the ball shiny. So, Kitty decided to polish the ball. Kitty rubbed and rubbed the ball until it was very shiny. Now, Kitty had a shiny ball to play with. Kitty was very happy. It played with the ball all day long. The noisy cat and the shiny ball were best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl with firm beliefs. Even when things got hard, she always remained true to her heart and was always modest. But one day she found out the truth about something important, and she was very sad. She didn't know what to do, so she went to her mom and asked for help. Her mom told her that when people do bad things, they must be punished. But her mom also assured her that the truth is more important that any punishment. Her mom told her to always be truthful and modest even when times were difficult. The little girl was grateful to her mom for her advice. She was so happy that she hugged her mom tightly. The little girl went back into the world with the truth in her heart and with a new understanding of what it means to be kind and honest.
