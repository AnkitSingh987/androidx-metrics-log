Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Tom and the Park

Lily and Tom were twins who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big hole in the ground. They wondered what was inside. "Let's go and see!" Tom said. He was brave and curious. "No, it's scary!" Lily said. She was afraid and careful. Tom did not listen to Lily. He ran to the hole and looked down. He saw a dark and deep underground. He saw many shiny things and strange noises. He was amazed and excited. "Wow, look at this!" Tom shouted. He wanted to go down and explore. Lily followed Tom and stared at the hole. She saw what Tom saw, but she felt different. She felt cold and scared. She also felt envious and angry. She wanted Tom to come back and play with her. "Tom, come back!" Lily cried. She tried to pull Tom away from the hole. "No, I want to stay!" Tom said. He tried to resist Lily. They started to fight and tug. They did not notice a big shadow behind them. It was a monster that lived in the underground. It was hungry and angry. It wanted to eat them. "Roar!" The monster roared. It jumped out of the hole and grabbed them. Lily and Tom screamed and stopped fighting. They wished they had listened to each other. They wished they had not gone to the hole. They wished they had stayed in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had a big, scary doll. The doll was missing an eye, and its hair was messy. Lily loved the doll, but it scared her friends. One day, Lily saw that the doll's dress had a hole. She asked her mom for thread to fix it. Her mom gave her the thread, and Lily fixed the doll's dress. But, she had a bad feeling that something was not right. That night, Lily heard a noise. It was the scary doll! The doll was angry that Lily fixed its dress. The doll was now even scarier than before. Lily was very scared and could not sleep. The doll stayed scary, and Lily's friends were more scared than ever.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird who loved to sing. Every day, the bird would sit on a big tree and sing beautiful songs. The tree had many apples. The bird liked the apples because they were yummy and useful. One day, the bird saw a big apple on the tree. The bird wanted to eat the apple but it was too high up. So, the bird sang louder and louder, hoping someone would help get the apple. Then, something unexpected happened. The apple started to shake and fell down from the tree. The bird was very happy and started to eat the apple. But then, the apple said, "Please don't eat me! I can sing too!" The bird was very surprised. The apple started to sing along with the bird. They sang together every day and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a tree. This tree was the first tree, the original tree. It had big branches and many green leaves. One day, a little boy came to the tree. He wanted to hold the tree's branch. So, he reached up high and held it. The tree felt happy. The boy and the tree became good friends. They played together every day. The tree loved the boy, and the boy loved the tree. They were happy together, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a big open field, there lived a big bull. The bull liked to play with his friends, the small birds. They would run and fly all day long. The bull was very happy. One day, a new animal entered the field. It was a big, grey elephant. The bull felt scared. He did not know the elephant. But the small birds told the bull, "Do not be scared. Let's play with the elephant too." The bull, the small birds, and the elephant started to play together. They had so much fun! But then, something unexpected happened. The elephant picked up the bull with his long nose and threw him up in the air. The bull was scared but then he started to laugh. He had never been so high before. It was fun! From that day on, the bull, the small birds, and the elephant became the best of friends.
