Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Tree

One day, a girl named Lily saw a pretty image in a book. The image was of a great big tree with lots of fruit. Lily wanted to find the tree, so she asked her friend Tom to help her. Tom said, "I promise to help you find it." They walked and walked until they found the great big tree. It was just like the image in the book. Lily and Tom were very happy. They wanted to eat the fruit, but the tree was too tall. Tom had an idea. He said, "I will climb the tree and get the fruit for us." Tom started to climb the tree. Lily waited and waited, but Tom did not come back. It was getting dark, and Lily was scared. She called out, "Tom, please come back!" But Tom did not answer. Lily was sad and went home without her friend Tom.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a new toy shop. A boy named Tim went to the shop with his mom. Tim saw a toy tornado. He wanted it very much. He asked his mom, "Can I have the toy tornado, please?" His mom said, "Yes, you can have it, but you must use it nicely." Tim took the toy tornado home. He played with it in his room. He showed it to his friend, Sam. Tim said, "Look at my new toy tornado! It is fun!" Sam liked it too. They played with the toy tornado together. One day, Tim used the toy tornado to make a mess in his room. His mom was not happy. She said, "You must use your toys to have fun, not to make a mess." Tim learned that he should use his toys in a good way. He and Sam cleaned up the mess together. They played with the toy tornado nicely from then on. The moral of the story is to use things in a good way and not to make a mess.

One day, a calm cat named Lucy went for a walk. She gazed at the pretty trees and the blue sky. Lucy felt happy and loved to explore. As she walked, Lucy saw a big rabbit. The rabbit was eating a salad. Lucy asked, "Can I have some salad too?" The rabbit said, "Yes, come and eat with me." Lucy and the rabbit sat and ate the salad. They talked and laughed. After they finished, they played together all day. They became good friends and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty lion named Leo. He was the king of the jungle. One day, Leo called all his animal friends to unite and have a big party. They all agreed and were very happy. During the week, the animals worked hard to get ready for the party. They cleaned the jungle and made it look nice. The day of the party came, and all the animals were excited. They danced, played games, and ate yummy food. But then, something unexpected happened. A tiny mouse named Max came to the party. All the animals were scared of the little mouse. They didn't know what to do. But Leo said, "Don't be scared! Max can be our friend too!" So, Max joined the party, and all the animals had a great time together. In the end, they learned that even the smallest friend can make a big difference.

Once upon a time, there was a young fish named Fin. Fin was very small, and he had a big dream. He wanted to climb the big scale on the wall of his home. But he was too little, and he felt sad. One day, a new friend came to live with Fin. Her name was Bubbles. Bubbles was a happy fish who liked to play and have fun. She saw that Fin was sad and asked him why. Fin told her about the big scale and his dream. Bubbles wanted to help Fin. She said, "We can do it together!" So, they tried to climb the big scale. They swam up and up, but it was very hard. Bubbles did not give up. She encouraged Fin to keep trying. "You can do it, Fin!" she said. They tried again and again, and finally, they reached the top of the big scale. Fin was so happy, and he thanked Bubbles for her help. They became best friends and had many more fun adventures together.
