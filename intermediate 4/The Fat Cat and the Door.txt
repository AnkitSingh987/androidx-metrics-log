Category: Intermediate
Title: The Fat Cat and the Door

Once upon a time there was a fat cat. It was sitting inside a house and admiring a video. The cat blinked, enjoying the colors and shapes. Suddenly, the cat heard a noise. It was the door – someone was opening it! The cat was a bit scared and it hesitated. Should it run away? But it was too late! The cat couldn't believe its eyes. It was a fat rat coming in! The cat was angry and hissed at the rat. The rat didn't blink and just stared at the cat. It stood there, not moving. The cat was getting very frustrated. It jumped near the rat and growled loudly. Nothing happened. Then, the cat ran outside before the rat had the chance to do anything. The cat breathed in relief. It had narrowly escaped danger!

Once upon a time, there was a pale boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toys all day. One day, Tim found a bottle of glue. He wanted to use it to make his toys stick together. Tim tried to open the bottle of glue, but it was very hard. He could not resist trying again and again. Finally, the glue bottle opened. Tim was so happy. He took his toys and started to glue them together. After a while, Tim's toys were all stuck together. He was very proud of his work. He showed his mom and dad his new big toy. They smiled and told Tim they loved it too.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the museum with his mom. They saw many things like big animals and old things. Tim liked the fat elephant the most. It was so big and gray. He wanted to take a picture with it. Tim's mom said, "Let's pay for the picture, Tim." They went to the man who took pictures. The man was nice and smiled at them. They gave him some money and he took a picture of Tim with the fat elephant. After the picture, they walked around the museum more. Tim had a lot of fun. He saw many new things and learned about them. When they were done, Tim said, "Thank you, Mom, for taking me to the museum." His mom smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim. I'm glad you had fun."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to play outside with her big red ball. She would throw the ball high up in the air and catch it with her hands. One hot day, Mia went outside to play with her ball, but she forgot to put on her sun hat. As Mia played, the sun got hotter and hotter. Mia started to feel pain on her head because of the hot sun. She knew she needed to find shade and rest. So, she went under a big tree and sat down. Mia thought about her sun hat and wished she had worn it to protect her head from the hot sun. After resting for a while, Mia went back to her house to get her sun hat. She put it on her head and felt much better. Then, she went back outside to play with her red ball again. Mia threw the ball high in the air, and the pain from the hot sun was gone. She learned that it is important to wear a sun hat on hot days. And Mia played happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a grumpy bear. He was so grumpy all the time. He woke up grumpy, he stayed grumpy all day and he even ate grumpy. One day, the bear was walking through the forest and he found a tip. It was a big surprise! The bear was so excited, he couldn't believe it. He picked up the tip and started to run. He ran through the forest with the tip in his hands. Soon, the bear found a nice place to sit and he opened up the tip. Inside he found something even more surprising! It was a big piece of bread! He was so happy and he started to eat it. The bear was no longer grumpy. He was happy and he had a full tummy. He thanked the surprise tip and went on his way, no longer grumpy!
