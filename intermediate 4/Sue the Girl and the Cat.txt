Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her toys upstairs. One day, she heard a noise in her room. She was scared but she was also honest. So, she went to ask her mom for help. Sue's mom went upstairs with her. They looked around the room, but they did not see anything. Then, they heard the noise again! Sue's mom moved a big box, and they saw a little cat. The cat was hiding behind the box. Sue was so happy to find the cat. She did not know how the cat got in her room. Sue's mom said it was a surprise. They took the cat downstairs and gave it some food. The cat was happy too. Sue learned that it is good to be honest and ask for help when you need it.

Once upon a time, there was a little kitten named Fluffy. Fluffy had a sharp toy that he liked to play with. One day, Fluffy was playing with his sharp toy in the garden. While Fluffy was playing, it started to rain. The rain made everything wet, even Fluffy. Fluffy didn't like being wet, so he ran inside the house to get dry. Fluffy's mom saw him all wet and helped him get dry with a warm towel. Fluffy was happy to be dry again. He learned that playing with his sharp toy was fun, but he didn't like getting wet in the rain.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to nap a lot. Every day, he would nap under a big tree in his yard. One day, Tim found a map. The map was different from other maps he had seen. It had many colors and pictures on it. Tim wanted to find the treasure that the map showed. Tim took a nap under the tree with the map. When he woke up, he followed the map. He found a big box under the tree. Inside the box, there were many different toys. Tim was very happy and played with his new toys all day.

Little Bob was sitting outside watching the sunrise. The sky was very orange and the birds were singing. He wanted to share the beautiful sunrise with his friends, so he went to the playground and brought some sticks to play a game. Bob said to his friends, "Let's have some fun and get organized! Let's each have one stick and start clapping!" Little Bob's friends clapped and laughed. They had a wonderful time! When the game was over, the big sun was high in the sky. Little Bob said excitedly, "That was so much fun! We should all remember this moment and be organized in everything we do!" Little Bob's friends all agreed. They had learned that being organized made things easier and much more enjoyable. With this wisdom, they continued to have a wonderful day!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Daisy. Daisy loved to play outside with her friends. One day, she saw a noisy bird in her yard. The bird was singing a happy song. Daisy wanted to join the bird and sing along. Daisy said to the bird, "Hi bird! Can I join you and sing a song?" The bird said, "Yes, Daisy! Let's make music together!" So Daisy and the noisy bird sang a fun song. They were very happy. Soon, Daisy's friends came to play. They heard Daisy and the noisy bird singing. They asked if they could join too. Daisy said, "Of course! The more, the merrier!" So they all sang and played together. They had a wonderful day full of fun and laughter.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a popular dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball and raise it high in the air. He had many friends who liked to play with him. One day, Spot saw a big pool of water near the village. He wanted to play with his ball in the water. So, he took his ball and jumped into the water. The water was cold but Spot didn't care. As Spot played in the water, he didn't see the big hole in the ground. He fell into the hole with his ball. His friends tried to help, but they couldn't. Spot was sad and scared. The water filled the hole and Spot couldn't get out. The village lost their popular dog, and everyone was very sad.
