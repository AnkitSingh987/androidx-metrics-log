Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Cake and the Machine

Once upon a time, there was a big, modern machine. It was red and blue. It could gain many things. One day, a boy named Tom saw the machine. He wanted to try it. Tom said to the machine, "Please gain me a toy." The machine made a noise and a toy came out. Tom was happy. He played with the toy and showed it to his friends. One day, Tom asked the machine to gain him a big cake. The machine made a noise and a big cake came out. But the cake was too big! It fell on Tom and his friends. They were all sad and covered in cake. The machine did not help them anymore.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a new bridge in the park. It is red and shiny. They want to cross the bridge and see what is on the other side. But there is a problem. A big dog is sitting on the bridge. It is black and furry. It has sharp teeth and loud bark. It does not let anyone pass. Lily and Ben are scared of the dog. They do not know what to do. They try to talk to the dog. They say, "Please, let us cross the bridge. We want to see what is on the other side. We will not hurt you or bother you. We are nice and friendly." But the dog does not listen. It growls and snarls at them. It does not move. Lily and Ben are sad. They think they will never cross the bridge. They think they will never discover what is on the other side. They sit on the grass and cry. Then, they hear a whistle. They look up and see a man. He is wearing a blue hat and a green shirt. He has a leash in his hand. He is the owner of the dog. He says, "Sorry, kids. I did not know my dog was on the bridge. He ran away from me. He is not mean. He is just scared of new things. Come on, boy. Let's go home." He walks to the bridge and calls the dog. The dog listens to him. It wags its tail and follows him. It leaves the bridge. Lily and Ben are happy. They thank the man and the dog. They run to the bridge and cross it. They discover what is on the other side. It is a pond with ducks and fish. It is beautiful and fun. They play and laugh. They have a good day.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his friends. One day, he found a big, red radish in his mom's garden. He thought the radish looked fun, so he picked it up and ran to his friends. Tim showed the radish to his friends, and they all thought it was very boring. They did not want to play with a radish. Tim felt sad, but he had an idea. He took the radish home and put it in a box. Then, he locked the box and hid it under his bed. The next day, Tim went back to his friends and told them he had a surprise in the box. They all wanted to see what was inside. Tim opened the box, and they saw the radish again. This time, they all thought the radish was fun and played with it all day. The moral of the story is that sometimes we need to look at things in a new way to see their value.

Once upon a time, there was a panda. The panda was very persistent. He believed he could do anything. One day, the panda was walking in the forest and he noticed a small pond. He peered into the pond and saw his reflection. The panda said to the reflection, “I believe I can do anything!” The reflection replied, “Yes, you can do anything if you work hard and stay persistent.” The panda thought for a moment and then said, “I will never give up!” The reflection smiled and replied, “I believe in you!” The panda smiled with delight and said, “I know! I am persistent and I will never give up!” The reflection disappeared, leaving the panda feeling confident and motivated. From then on, the panda put his mind to everything he wanted to do and he never gave up, no matter how hard it was. He believed in himself and he was persistent.
