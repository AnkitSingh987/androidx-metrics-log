Category: Intermediate
Title: The Oasis and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a hot land, there was a big oasis. Many animals lived there. They loved to drink the cool water. One day, a little bird saw the oasis. It was eager to find a new home. The little bird told its friends about the oasis. They wanted to see it too. The bird had an idea. It would record the way to the oasis. It dropped little stones on the ground. The stones made a path. The bird's friends followed the path. They found the oasis. All the animals were happy. The little bird's plan worked. They all lived together at the oasis.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a round ball named Rollie. Rollie was silver and shiny. He loved to roll all around the town. One day, Rollie met a new friend named Bouncy, who was a colorful ball. "Hi, I am Rollie. I can roll fast," said Rollie to Bouncy. Bouncy smiled and said, "Hi Rollie, I am Bouncy. I can bounce high." They played together, but soon Rollie became upset. Bouncy could go up and down, but Rollie could only go side to side. Rollie wanted to bounce too, so he asked Bouncy for help. Bouncy thought for a moment, then said, "Let's try something new. We can roll and bounce together!" They tried it, and it worked! Rollie could now bounce a little, and Bouncy could roll a little too. The two friends played together, happy that they could both roll and bounce. They learned that it is good to help each other and try new things. And the town loved to watch the silver ball and the colorful ball, rolling and bouncing together, side by side.

Once upon a time, there was a brave little dog named Max. Max lived in a small house with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. They loved to play together outside in the big yard. One sunny day, Max and Lily were playing near the side of the house. Max would run and fetch a ball, and Lily would laugh and clap her hands. "Good job, Max!" Lily would say, and Max would wag his tail and repeat the game. As they played, a big, scary cat appeared on the other side of the fence. Max barked loudly to protect Lily. "Be brave, Max!" said Lily. Max barked and barked until the cat ran away. Lily hugged Max and said, "Thank you for being brave, Max!" And they went back to playing their fun game.

Once upon a time, there was a lucky frog. This frog had a big, green leaf. The frog liked to attach the leaf to a stick. The stick with the leaf was the frog's favorite toy. One day, the frog met a little boy. The boy saw the frog's toy and said, "Wow! That's a fun toy. Can I play with it too?" The frog and the boy became friends. They played with the leaf and stick all day. But then, something unexpected happened. The wind blew very hard, and the leaf flew away. The frog and the boy were sad. They looked for the leaf, but they couldn't find it. The frog said, "We can find a new leaf and attach it to the stick." The boy smiled and said, "Yes, we can make a new toy together!" So, the frog and the boy found a new leaf and attached it to the stick. They played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He loved to move and play all day long. One day, Tom found a noisy toy car. He was so happy and played with it all the time. One sunny day, Tom went to the park with his noisy toy car. While playing, he saw a big lime tree. Tom wanted to pick a lime, but the tree was too tall. So he tried to use his noisy toy car to help him reach the lime. He put the car on the ground and tried to climb on it. Suddenly, the noisy toy car started to move by itself! It went faster and faster, and took Tom on a fun ride around the park. Tom was surprised and happy at the same time. He forgot about the lime and enjoyed his unexpected adventure with his magical noisy toy car. And from that day on, Tom and his noisy toy car had many more fun adventures together.
