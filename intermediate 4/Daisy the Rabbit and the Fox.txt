Category: Intermediate
Title: Daisy the Rabbit and the Fox

Once there was a little rabbit named Daisy who wanted to prove she was brave. She decided to run all the way to the distant mountain where she thought she could find all sorts of new tasks to do. As Daisy was hopping through the fields and over the fallen trees, she heard a loud scream coming from behind her. She stood still and listened carefully. The scream seemed to be coming from a nearby cave. Daisy bravely entered the cave. Inside she found a little fox, who was crying and scared. Daisy asked the fox why he was so scared. The fox told her about all the difficult tasks he was supposed to do, and how he was too overwhelmed to do them. Daisy told the fox, "Don't worry, I can help you. We can do all of the tasks together, and I won't leave until they are all finished." The fox was so thankful that he stopped crying, and together they finished all of the tasks. At the end of the story, Daisy learned the moral value that no challenge is too big when you have a friend by your side and that being brave is helping your friends.

Once upon a time, there was a fairy named Lily. Lily was very adventurous. She loved to fly and play with her friends in the big garden. One day, Lily found some paint. She had a fun idea. She called all her friends to come and join her. They were very excited. Lily and her friends started to paint the flowers in the garden. They painted them in many colors: red, blue, and yellow. They laughed and had a great time. The garden became the most beautiful place ever. And Lily, the adventurous fairy, was very happy.

Tom and Anna love to play with their toys. They have many toys, but their favorite is a big red plane. They like to make the plane fly in the air and make noises like zoom and vroom. One day, Tom and Anna are playing with the plane in the living room. They throw the plane up and catch it. They have a lot of fun. But then, Tom throws the plane too hard and it hits the wall. The plane breaks into two pieces. Tom and Anna are very sad. "Oh no, our plane is broken!" Tom cries. "What will we do?" Anna asks. They look at the broken plane and feel worried. They think their mom will be angry with them. They think they will not have a plane to play with anymore. They hear their mom coming from the kitchen. They hide the broken plane behind the couch. They hope their mom will not see it. "Hi, kids, what are you playing with?" their mom asks. "Nothing, mom, just some toys," Tom says. Their mom smiles and says, "I have a surprise for you. I went to the store and I saw a new toy that I think you will like. Do you want to see it?" Tom and Anna nod. They are curious. Their mom brings a big box from the kitchen and opens it. Inside the box is a new plane. It is blue and shiny and has lights and sounds. "Wow, a new plane!" Tom and Anna shout. They run to the box and take out the new plane. They hug their mom and thank her. They are very happy. They forget about the broken plane behind the couch. They play with the new plane in the living room. They make the plane fly in the air and make noises like beep and boop. Their mom watches them and smiles. She is glad they like the new plane. She knows they broke the old plane, but she does not mind. She thinks they learned a lesson. She thinks they will be more careful with their toys. She thinks she will replace the broken plane with a new one. She loves her kids.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a pretty stone. He picked it up and showed it to his clever friend, Sue. Sue liked to ask questions and learn new things. She looked at the stone and asked Tim a question. "Where did you find this stone, Tim?" Tim told Sue he found the stone near the big tree in the park. They both walked to the tree and looked around. They found more pretty stones on the ground. Tim and Sue were very happy. They picked up the stones and put them in their pockets. Sue asked Tim another question. "What can we do with these stones?" Tim thought for a moment and said, "Let's make a pretty picture with the stones!" They both smiled and started to make a picture on the ground with the stones. They had a fun day playing with the pretty stones and asking questions.
