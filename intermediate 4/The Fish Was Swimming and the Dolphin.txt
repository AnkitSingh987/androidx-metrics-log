Category: Intermediate
Title: The Fish Was Swimming and the Dolphin

One day, a little fish was swimming in the sea. The little fish started to worry. He thought he saw a big shark. The little fish was scared. The little fish swam very fast to hide. He found a big rock to hide behind. The little fish peeked out to see if the shark was still there. But it was not a shark. It was a big, friendly dolphin. The dolphin said, "Do not worry, little fish. I am not a shark. I am here to help you." The little fish was grateful. He was not scared anymore. The little fish and the friendly dolphin swam together and played in the sea.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a regular boy who loved to play with his toys. One day, he wanted to find his favorite toy car, but he could not find it anywhere. Tim went to his mom and asked, "Mom, where is my toy car?" His mom thought for a moment and said, "Maybe it is in the closet. Let's go look together." Tim and his mom walked to the closet and opened the door. Inside the closet, they found many toys, but not the toy car. Tim was sad, but his mom said, "Don't worry, we will find your toy car." They looked around the room and finally found the toy car under Tim's bed. Tim was very happy and thanked his mom for helping him. They played with the toy car together and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a big, big dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to play outside. One day, Buddy found an enormous ball in his yard. He was so happy to play with it. Buddy kicked the big ball and it went very far. It went so far that it landed on a napkin. The napkin was on a table at a party. Buddy wanted his big ball back, so he went to the party. At the party, Buddy saw a little girl. She was sad because the ball had knocked over her juice. Buddy knew what to do. He reversed and went back to his yard. He found his small ball and brought it to the little girl. She was happy and they played together. Buddy learned that sometimes, smaller is better.

Once upon a time, in a wild forest, there lived a little bird named Tim. Tim liked to sing all day long. One day, he found a small box in the forest. It was a record box. Tim took the record box to his friend, the big bear named Ben. Ben knew what to do with the record box. He showed Tim how to record his songs. Tim sang and sang, and the record box saved all his songs. After singing all day, Tim and Ben were tired. They sat under a tree and Ben took out a big napkin. They put the napkin on the ground and had a yummy picnic. They ate fruits and sandwiches. Then, they took a nap on the soft napkin. Tim and Ben were happy and had a great day in the wild forest.

One day, a little girl named Sue was playing in her yard. She saw a big raven on a tree. Sue wanted to be a good friend to the raven. She said, "Hi, raven! Do you want to play?" The raven looked at Sue and said, "Yes! I like to play. But I have a surprise for you first!" Sue was very happy and excited to see the surprise. The raven flew away and came back with a big, red ball. Sue did not know that ravens can talk or bring surprises. They played together all day and had lots of fun. Sue found out that the raven was a very good friend, and they played together every day after that.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the park. She saw a band playing music. They were not a regular band. They played funny songs that made everyone laugh. Amy went close to the band and said, "I think your music is fun!" The band leader smiled and said, "Thank you! We like to make people happy." Amy danced and sang along with the band. She had a great time at the park. The band played all day, and everyone was happy.
