Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Mom

One day, a boy named Tim found a big arrow in his yard. He picked it up and showed it to his mom. Tim asked, "Mom, what can we do with this arrow?" Mom thought for a moment and said, "We can cook something good with it! Let's make a yummy soup!" So, Tim and Mom went to the kitchen. They put the arrow in a big pot with water, carrots, and potatoes. They cooked the soup for a long time. The house smelled good. When the soup was ready, Tim and Mom sat down to eat. They tasted the soup and it was very good. They were happy they found the arrow and used it to cook something yummy. From that day on, Tim and Mom always remembered the good soup they made with the arrow. They loved to cook together and try new things.

Once upon a time, there was a graceful bird. The bird was very pretty. It could fly high in the sky. One day, the bird met a kind man. The man needed help. He wanted to send a gift to his friend. The gift was a small bag of salt. The bird said, "I can help you! I can fly and take the salt to your friend." The kind man was happy. He gave the small bag of salt to the bird. The bird took the salt in its beak and flew away. The bird flew over the trees and the river. It found the friend's house. The friend was outside. The bird gave the salt to the friend. The friend said, "Thank you, graceful bird!" The bird was happy to help. It flew back to the kind man and told him that the salt was sent. The kind man and the bird became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Blue. Blue was a very blue bird. Blue liked to talk a lot. Every day, Blue would talk to his parent about the big tree. His parent would listen and smile. One day, Blue's parent told him a secret. "There is a big surprise at the top of the tree," the parent said. Blue was very excited. He wanted to see the big surprise. But Blue was too small to fly up to the top of the tree. Blue talked to his parent every day about the big surprise. He wished he could see it. As the days went by, Blue grew bigger and stronger. Finally, he was big enough to fly to the top of the tree. Blue flew up to the top of the tree and saw the big surprise. It was a beautiful, shiny blue nest. Blue was so happy. He talked to his parent about the blue nest all day. The secret was worth the wait.

Once upon a time, there was a nice girl named Lily. Lily had a big toy shop. She liked to play with her toys every day. She had many friends who liked to play too. One day, a new boy named Tom came to play. Tom didn't have any toys. He was sad. Lily saw him and thought of a good idea. She said, "I can lend you my toys, and we can play together!" Tom was very happy. They played together every day and had lots of fun. All the other kids saw how nice Lily was and they wanted to lend their toys too. Everyone shared and played together. The moral of the story is that it's good to share and be nice to others.

Tommy was a very tired boy. He lived in a small house with his mommy and daddy. One day, his mommy said to him, "Tommy, it's time to leave now. We're going to visit our neighbor." Tommy was so excited. He had never been to a neighbor's house before and was curious to see what it looked like. So Tommy's mom and dad took him to their neighbor's house. When Tommy arrived, he saw the neighbor's house was very big. He waved at his neighbor and said hello. The neighbor was really friendly and invited Tommy to come inside. Tommy then spent the day playing with his neighbor and their children. He was having a lot of fun, but when the afternoon arrived, it was time for Tommy to go home. He said goodbye and left. Tommy was tired because he had spent the whole day at his neighbor's house. But he was glad that he had made new friends and had an exciting day!
