Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Shelf

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. One day, Lily wanted to find a toy to play with. She looked at the shelf in her room. It was very high, but she thought it would be easy to reach. Lily stood on her toes and tried to get the toy from the shelf. Suddenly, a small box fell down. Lily was surprised! She opened the box and found a pretty doll inside. The doll had a big smile and a soft dress. Lily played with the doll all day. She was so happy with her surprise. She knew that the shelf had more fun things to find. From that day on, Lily always looked at the shelf for new surprises.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play and discover new things. One day, she found a big stair in her house. The stair was very organized, with each step in the right place. Sue wanted to climb the stair to see what was on top. She knew she should ask her mom first, but she was too excited. So, she started climbing the stair all by herself. As she climbed, she felt proud of her bravery. When Sue reached the top of the stair, she saw a beautiful room. It was filled with toys and games. But then, she remembered that she didn't ask her mom. Sue felt bad and went back down the stair. She told her mom about the room and said sorry for not asking. Her mom hugged her and said it was okay. They went back up the stair together and played in the room. The moral of the story is to always ask for permission before doing something new.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a lively boy who liked to play and have fun. One day, Tim saw his dad work in the garage. His dad was using tape to fix a box. Tim wanted to help. Tim asked his dad, "Can I work with you?" His dad said, "Yes, Tim. You can help me with the tape." Tim was very happy. He took the tape and helped his dad fix the box. They worked together and had a lot of fun. After they finished working, Tim and his dad went outside to play. They ran and laughed in the sun. Tim was very proud that he could help his dad work. He knew that he could do big things, even though he was a little boy.

Once upon a time, in a big fancy castle, there was a kind queen. The queen had a big room filled with toys. She loved to share her toys with all the kids in her land. One day, she decided to have a big party for all the kids. Before the party, the queen talked to her helper. "Please fill the room with more toys for the kids to play with," she said. The helper nodded and went to work. The queen knew the kids would be very happy when they saw the surprise. The day of the party came, and the kids were so excited. They ran into the big room and saw all the toys. The queen smiled as she watched the kids play and have fun. She was so happy that her surprise had made them all so happy. And from that day on, the queen's castle was always filled with laughter and joy.

"Let's go for a nature walk!" said Mummy. So off they went. As they were walking, suddenly a huge poppy appeared. "Wow!" said the 3 year old. "Look at the huge poppy!" Mummy knelt down and said, "Yes, it is very beautiful. Can you smell it?" The 3 year old leaned in and sniffed. "I can smell it, it's lovely!" he said. They stayed looking at the huge poppy for a while, before continuing on their walk.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her delicate toys. One day, she asked her mom, "Mom, can I play in the attic?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful. The attic is old." Lily went to the attic and found a big box. She opened the box and saw many delicate toys. She played with them and had lots of fun. But Lily remembered her mom's words and was very careful not to break anything. Later, Lily's friend, Ben, came over. She said, "Ben, let's play in the attic!" Ben was not as careful as Lily. He broke one of the delicate toys. Lily was sad, but she knew it was an accident. The moral of the story is to always be careful and listen to your parents. They know what's best for you.
