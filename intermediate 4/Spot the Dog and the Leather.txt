Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Leather

One day, a little dog named Spot found a piece of leather. Spot liked the leather very much. He wanted to play with it all day. But Spot was not careful with the leather. He played too rough with it. Spot's friend, a cat named Whiskers, saw Spot playing with the leather. Whiskers said, "Spot, be careful with the leather. If you are not careful, it will suffer and break." Spot listened to Whiskers and played more careful with the leather. He did not want it to suffer. Spot and Whiskers played with the leather together. They had lots of fun and the leather did not break.

Once upon a time, there was a cool aunt named Jane. She loved to make pretty things for her niece, Lily. One day, Jane had a fun idea. She wanted to print a big, colorful picture for Lily's room. Aunt Jane went to her computer and picked a nice picture of a cat. Lily loved cats! Jane pressed the button to print the picture. The printer made a lot of noise as it printed the cat picture. When it was done, Aunt Jane showed the picture to Lily. Lily's face lit up with a big smile. She hugged her cool aunt and said, "Thank you, Aunt Jane!" They put the cat picture on Lily's wall. Now, Lily could see the pretty cat every day.

Once upon a time, in a little garden, there was an olive tree. The olive tree was very patient. It would wait and wait for the sun and rain to help it grow. One day, a little boy named Tim came to visit the garden. "Hello, Olive Tree," said Tim. "You are so big now!" The olive tree was happy to see Tim. It had been growing tall and strong, just like Tim. One day, the sun was very hot, and there was no rain. The olive tree was sad. It needed water to grow. Tim saw the olive tree and knew what to do. He brought a big bucket of water to the tree. "Here you go, Olive Tree," said Tim, as he poured the water on the tree's roots. The olive tree was very happy and thanked Tim. The olive tree continued to grow, and Tim would visit it every day. They became the best of friends, and the olive tree kept growing bigger and bigger, just like Tim.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the doctor with his mom. The doctor said, "Tim, we need to make an x-ray of your arm." Tim was scared, but he knew the x-ray would help him feel better. In the waiting room, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue was selfish and did not want to share her toy. Tim said, "Sue, can I play with your toy?" Sue said, "No, it is mine. I do not want to share." Tim's mom told Sue that sharing is good. Sue thought about it and said, "Okay, Tim, you can play with my toy." Tim and Sue played together and had fun. They both learned that sharing is better than being selfish.

One day, a funny cat named Tom went to play outside. The sun was shining, and the sky was blue. Tom was very happy. He saw his friend, a dog named Max, and said, "Max, let's play together!" Max wagged his tail and agreed. As they played, the wind started to blow harder. Tom and Max felt a little scared. They saw big clouds in the sky, and the wind was getting stronger. They knew a tornado was coming. They ran to their homes to be safe. Inside their homes, Tom and Max watched the tornado from their windows. When the tornado was gone, they went outside to see what happened. They saw that the tornado had served to clean up all the leaves in their yards. Tom and Max laughed and said, "That was a funny way for the tornado to help us!" They were happy and played together again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big round head and loved to play with his red ball. One day, he went to the park to play with his friends. While playing, Tim saw a distant hill. He wanted to roll his red ball down the hill. He shouted to his friends, "Come and play with me on the hill!" His friends ran to him, and they all went to the hill together. They rolled the red ball down the hill and watched it go fast. Tim's friends shouted with joy. They played on the hill until the sun went down. Then, they went home, happy and tired.
