Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Bell

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his friend, Sue. They saw a big bell on the ground. Tim said, "Let's play a game with the bell!" Sue was excited and nodded her head. Tim and Sue decided to take turns hitting the bell with a stick. They wanted to see who could make the loudest sound. They were very competitive. Tim went first and struck the bell. "Ding!" it went. Sue giggled and said, "My turn!" She struck the bell. "Dong!" it went. They both laughed and clapped. The friends played with the bell all day, taking turns to strike it. They had so much fun being competitive. At the end of the day, they couldn't decide who won, but they didn't care. They were happy to have a fun day at the park with the big bell.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play at the park. He saw a big box with a button. The box had the word "power" on it. Tim was very curious and wanted to know what the button did. Tim found his friend, Sam, and said, "Look at this big box! It has a button on it. I want to press it." Sam looked at the box and said, "I don't know, Tim. Maybe we should not touch it." But Tim was a spoiled boy and did not listen to Sam. He pushed the button. Suddenly, the whole park went dark. All the lights shut off. The kids were scared and started to cry. Tim and Sam did not know what to do. They had made a big mistake by pressing the button. The park stayed dark and the kids could not play anymore. All the fun was gone. Tim and Sam felt very sad for what they had done. They wished they had listened to Sam and not touched the big box with the "power" button.

One day, a boy named Tim found a flashlight in his toy box. He thought it was boring because it was old and not shiny. Tim wanted to play with something fun, so he kicked the flashlight under his bed. Later, that night, Tim's room was very dark. He heard a noise and got scared. He remembered the flashlight he kicked under his bed. Tim crawled under the bed and found the flashlight. He turned it on, and the room was bright. Tim said, "Hey, flashlight! You're not boring. You help me see in the dark." The flashlight was happy to help Tim. From that day on, Tim and the flashlight were best friends, and they had many fun adventures together.

The little girl wanted to go on a magical adventure. She asked her mom for a map, so she could explore the unknown. The mom smiled and handed her some paper and a pencil. She said, "You can write your own map to go on your journey." The little girl was so excited to start writing. She carefully put down all the places she wanted to visit. After she was done, she looked at her map with a big smile on her face. She said to her mom, "Mommy I'm ready for my adventure! Are you coming with me?" The mom replied, "I'm always with you my darling. Let's go explore the unknown together!".

One day, a big, attractive dog found a red brick. The dog liked the brick and wanted to chew it. But the brick was hard and did not taste good. The dog was sad. The dog met a small cat. The cat said, "Why are you sad, dog?" The dog said, "I want to chew this brick, but it's too hard." The cat thought and had an idea. The cat told the dog, "Let's trade. You give me the brick, and I will give you my soft, chewy toy." The dog was happy and agreed. They traded, and both were happy. The dog chewed the toy, and the cat played with the brick.

Little Jack loved to dance. Every day he would twirl around the living room and jump around the garden, much to the amusement of his family. Little Jack was sure he was the best dancer around. One day, Little Jack noticed something strange in the garden. There was a beautiful plant with colourful petals, just waiting to be danced around. Little Jack stared at it with amazement, until finally he just couldn't resist. He ran towards the plant, full of joy, and began to spin around it. A few moments later, Jack heard a voice. It belonged to a fairy. The fairy had been watching Jack's dance, and she was impressed. She decided to reward Little Jack for his beautiful moves. "For your fair dance around my flower," the fairy said, "I bestow a special gift. Every night the sun sets, and your flower will bring you an even better dance." Little Jack was excited! And so, from that day on, every evening Jack would return to the garden, and dance with joy around the flower. Its petals seemed to sparkle and twirl along with Jack's moves. Little Jack was sure that he was the best dancer around!
