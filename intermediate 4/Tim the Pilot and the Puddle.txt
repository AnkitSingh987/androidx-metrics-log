Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Pilot and the Puddle

One day, a pilot named Tim went to the park. He saw an enormous puddle. Tim wanted to play in the puddle, so he put on his boots. Tim jumped in the puddle and said, "I love to soak my boots in the water!" His boots went splash, splash, splash! Tim laughed and played in the puddle. A little girl named Sue came to the park. She saw Tim playing in the puddle. Sue asked, "Can I play with you?" Tim said, "Yes, come and soak your boots too!" Sue put on her boots and they both played in the enormous puddle, having lots of fun.

One day, a little swan named Sally was swimming in a pond. She saw her friend, Froggy the frog, sitting on a rock. Froggy looked sad, so Sally swam over to him. "Hi Froggy, why are you sad?" Sally asked. Froggy said, "I am jealous of the birds. They can fly high in the sky, but I can only jump." Sally wanted to help Froggy feel better. She thought for a moment and then said, "I have an idea! Let's play a game. I suggest we have a race. You can jump, and I will swim. That way, we can both do what we are good at." Froggy liked the idea and smiled. They had their race, and both had fun. After that, Froggy was not jealous anymore. He was happy to be a frog who could jump. And Sally was happy to be a swan who could swim. They played together all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was a good boy, but sometimes he could not resist doing things he should not do. One day, his mom asked him to help her clean the house. She said that if he did a great job, he would get a reward. Tom wanted the reward very much. He started to clean the house with his mom. They picked up toys, wiped the table, and put away clothes. Tom was doing a great job. But then, he saw a big bowl of candy on the table. He wanted to eat some candy, but he knew he should not. Tom tried to resist the candy, but it was hard. He thought about the reward his mom promised him. He wanted to make his mom happy. So, he kept cleaning and did not eat the candy. When they were done, his mom was very happy. She gave Tom his reward - a big, sweet apple. Tom was proud of himself for resisting the candy and doing a great job.

One sunny day, Tim wanted to ride his bicycle. He went outside and saw his friend, Sam. Sam said, "Hi, Tim! Can I ride with you?" Tim said, "Yes, let's ride together!" As they were riding, Sam started to act silly. He didn't behave and was making loud noises. Tim felt embarrassed. He said, "Sam, please behave. We don't want to bother people." Sam listened to Tim and started to behave. They rode their bicycles happily and had a fun day together. In the end, they both learned the importance of being good friends and behaving well.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree. The tree had many big green leaves. One day, a small bird wanted to make a nest. The bird found a soft napkin on the ground. The bird picked up the napkin in its beak and flew up to the tall tree. The bird tried to balance the napkin on a branch. But the wind blew hard, and the napkin fell down. The bird was sad. It tried again and again. Each time, the wind blew the napkin away. Then, a kind squirrel saw the bird. The squirrel wanted to help the bird. The squirrel held the napkin with its paws, and the bird put the napkin on the branch. They worked together, and the wind did not blow the napkin away. The bird and the squirrel were happy. They became good friends. The bird built its nest with the napkin, and the squirrel lived in the tall tree too. The moral of the story is that when we help each other, we can do big things.
