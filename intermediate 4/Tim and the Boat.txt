Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Boat

One day, a boy named Tim went to the shore with his mom. They saw many shells and the water was mild. Tim liked to play in the water and feel the sand on his feet. Tim saw a man with a cart. The man had many toys to buy. Tim asked his mom, "Can I buy a toy, please?" His mom said, "Yes, you can pick one toy to buy." Tim looked at the toys and picked a boat. He was happy to play with it in the water. The boat floated on the mild waves. Tim and his mom had a fun day at the shore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a good boy who always tried to behave. One day, Tim found a smooth stone while playing outside. It was round and felt nice in his hand. Tim liked the stone very much and wanted to keep it forever. Tim showed the smooth stone to his mom. She told him it was special and that he could keep it if he behaved. Tim was happy and promised to be good. He put the stone in his pocket and went to play with his friends. They played fun games all day long. At bedtime, Tim held the smooth stone in his hand and gave it a little kiss. He put it under his pillow and went to sleep. When he woke up, he found a surprise! The smooth stone had turned into a shiny gold thumb! Tim knew that he had been good and the magic stone had given him a special gift. From that day on, Tim always tried to behave and never forgot about the magic of the smooth stone.

Tom and Lily were friends who liked to play at the zoo. They loved to see the animals and learn their names. One day, they saw a big elephant who was sad. He had a hurt leg and could not walk with his friends. Tom and Lily wanted to help the elephant. They asked the zookeeper what they could do. The zookeeper said they could encourage the elephant by talking to him and giving him some fruit. He said the elephant liked bananas and apples. Tom and Lily went to the fruit stand and bought some bananas and apples. They walked back to the elephant and said hello. They told him he was a good elephant and they hoped he would feel better soon. They gave him some fruit and he ate it happily. He lifted his trunk and made a loud noise. Tom and Lily smiled and clapped. The zookeeper came and thanked Tom and Lily for being kind. He said the elephant was feeling more cheerful and his leg was healing. He said they could come back and visit him anytime. Tom and Lily were happy. They hugged the elephant and said goodbye. They learned that encouraging someone can make a big difference.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys in their room. But sometimes their room is very messy. Mommy says they have to organize their toys before they go to bed. One day, Lily and Ben want to go to the park with Mommy. Mommy says they can go, but first they have to organize their toys. Lily and Ben start to put their toys in the boxes and shelves. They have many toys: dolls, cars, blocks, books, and more. Lily finds a soft teddy bear under the bed. She hugs it and says, "This is my favorite toy. I want to take it to the park." Ben says, "No, you can't. You have to organize it. Put it in the box with the other soft toys." Lily says, "But I love it. Please, can I take it?" Ben says, "No, you can't. Mommy will be angry if you don't organize it." Lily and Ben start to argue. They shout and pull the teddy bear. Mommy hears them and comes to their room. She sees the mess and the teddy bear. She says, "What are you doing? Why are you fighting? You have to organize your toys, not make more mess." Lily and Ben feel sorry. They say, "We're sorry, Mommy. We wanted to go to the park, but we couldn't agree on the teddy bear." Mommy says, "It's okay. I understand. But you have to learn to share and cooperate. How about this: you can take the teddy bear to the park, but you have to take turns holding it. And when we come back, you have to organize your toys together. Is that fair?" Lily and Ben nod and smile. They say, "Yes, Mommy. That's fair. Thank you, Mommy." They finish organizing their toys and put them in the right places. Then they grab their coats and shoes and follow Mommy to the door. Mommy says, "Let's go. We'll take a taxi to the park. It will be fun." Lily and Ben say, "Yay! We love taxis. They are fast and yellow." They run to the taxi and get in. They hold the teddy bear and look out the window. They are happy and excited. They can't wait to play in the park.
