Category: Intermediate
Title: The Red Automobile and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a big red automobile. It loved to drive around the town and make people happy. One day, the automobile saw a tree branch in the road. The branch was too big for the automobile to drive over. A man with a tool came to help the automobile. He was deaf, but he knew how to cut the branch. He used his tool to cut the big branch into small pieces. The automobile was very happy to see the man cut the branch. With the branch cut and out of the way, the automobile could drive again. It honked its horn to say thank you to the deaf man. The man smiled and waved goodbye. The big red automobile drove away, happy to make people smile again.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red car. The car had a flat tire. The car could not go. A little girl named Amy saw the car. She wanted to help. She said, "I will try to fix the tire." Amy found a pump. She tried to pump the tire. The tire got bigger and bigger. Suddenly, there was a loud noise. The tire popped! Amy was surprised. She did not know what to do. A kind man walked by. He saw Amy and the popped tire. He said, "Don't worry, Amy. I can help you." He went to his truck and got a new tire. He put the new tire on the car. The car could go now. Amy was happy. She said, "Thank you, kind man!" They both smiled and waved goodbye.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a brave little girl named Mia. Mia had a big, soft teddy bear named Mr. Bear. They were best friends. One day, Mia and Mr. Bear went to the park to play. They played on the swings and the slide. Then, they heard a loud noise. Mia looked around and saw a little boy crying. He was scared. Mia went to the boy and asked, "Why are you crying?" The boy said, "I lost my toy car, and I am scared." Mia wanted to help, so she told the boy, "Don't worry, we will find your toy car and make you feel safe." Mia and Mr. Bear looked everywhere, under the trees and behind the bushes. Then, they found the toy car near the sandbox. The boy was happy and said, "Thank you, Mia and Mr. Bear!" They all clapped their hands and laughed. From that day on, Mia, Mr. Bear, and the little boy became good friends. They played together in the park, and they all felt safe and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a powerful king who loved to wave at his people. He was very kind and always helped others. One day, he saw a video of a faraway land with people who needed help. He knew he had to do something. The king called his friends and told them about the video. They all decided to go and help the people in the faraway land. They packed their things and started their journey. The king waved goodbye to his people, and they knew he would come back. After a long trip, the king and his friends finally reached the faraway land. They helped the people and made them happy. The people were very thankful for the king's powerful help. When it was time to go home, the king waved goodbye to the new friends. Back in his kingdom, the people were happy to see the king return, and they all waved at him. The king knew that he had done a good thing by helping others.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy spider. The spider lived in a small, clean web. One day, a little girl named Sue saw the spider. Sue was scared of spiders, but this one looked nice. "Hello, spider," Sue said. "You are very tidy." The spider was happy to hear this. It liked being tidy. Sue and the spider became friends. One day, Sue saw that the spider's web was a mess. "What happened?" Sue asked. The spider told Sue that a big wind came and messed up the web. Sue wanted to help the spider. Together, they made the web tidy again. The spider was happy, and Sue learned that not all spiders are scary.
