Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Thick Cat and the Dog

One day, a thick cat named Tom went to the kitchen. He wanted to eat some toast. Tom liked his toast with butter and honey. He was very hungry. Tom saw his friend, a dog named Max. Max was sleeping on the floor. Tom did not want to disturb Max. So, he walked very quietly to the table. He found some toast and put butter and honey on it. Tom said to himself, "Yummy toast!" Max woke up and said, "I like toast too!" Tom smiled and shared his toast with Max. They both enjoyed their toast together without disturbing anyone else.

Once upon a time, there was a little infant named Tim. Tim lived in a big house with his mom and dad. They loved him very much. One day, Tim's mom and dad had to go out. They told Tim to be good and not to do anything dangerous. Tim said he would listen. But when they left, Tim saw two toys on the floor. One toy was a soft teddy bear and the other toy was a big, dangerous snake. Tim had to choose which toy to play with. He remembered what his mom and dad said about not doing anything dangerous. So, Tim chose the soft teddy bear to play with. He hugged the teddy bear and felt happy. When Tim's mom and dad came home, they saw that Tim had made a good choice. They were very proud of him for not playing with the dangerous snake. Tim, his mom, and his dad all hugged and played with the soft teddy bear together. And they all lived happily ever after.

“Come and get it!” shouted Lucy. She had just finished loading her toy truck with chocolate for her friends. Henry and Sarah came racing up, their eyes filled with excitement. “Wow, what’s in the truck?” asked Henry. “Chocolate!” beamed Lucy. “I’m giving it to all my friends!” Henry and Sarah were envious. “Me too!” they said at the same time. “Okay, okay,” Lucy chuckled. “I’ll give you each one piece.” Henry and Sarah squealed in delight and took the chocolates. They both gave Lucy big hugs and then ran off with their treats. Lucy watched them go, feeling happy and proud. She knew her friends would love it.

Once there was a fine eraser. It was very small and red. It belonged to a little girl. She had lots of fun playing with it. One day, the girl decided to bury her eraser in the garden. She worked hard digging a hole and put the eraser in it. She sang a happy song as she filled the hole with dirt until the eraser was gone. The girl was happy she had found a good place to put her eraser. She went inside and had some cookies. The eraser was fine under the ground in the garden.

Lily and Ben are playing with their toys in the living room. They have a lot of fun making noise and mess. They do not see that Mom is coming home from work. Mom opens the door and sees the living room. She is not happy. She says, "Lily and Ben, what did you do? Look at this mess! You need to clean it up now!" Lily and Ben are scared. They do not want Mom to be mad. They say, "Sorry, Mom. We will clean it up. We will be quick." They start to pick up their toys and put them in the box. They work together and help each other. They try to be fast and careful. Mom sees that they are trying hard. She smiles and says, "Thank you, Lily and Ben. You are good kids. I am proud of you. When you are done, we can have a snack and read a book." Lily and Ben are happy. They finish cleaning the living room. They hug Mom and say, "We love you, Mom. You are the best." Mom hugs them back and says, "I love you too, Lily and Ben. You are the best."

Once upon a time, there was a small, comfortable house. In the house, there was a big shelf. On the shelf, there were many toys. A girl named Lily lived in the house. She loved playing with her toys. One day, Lily saw a new toy on the shelf. It was a magic lamp. She took it down and started to rub it. Suddenly, a friendly genie came out of the lamp. The genie said, "Hello, Lily! I can do one thing for you. What do you want?" Lily thought for a moment and said, "I want a big, soft bed to sleep on." The genie nodded and with a wave of his hand, a big, soft bed appeared in Lily's room. She was very happy and thanked the genie. That night, Lily slept on her new bed. It was so comfortable that she had the best sleep ever. The next day, she told her friends about the magic lamp and the genie. They all wanted to find their own magic lamps too. And so, Lily and her friends went on many adventures looking for magic lamps. They had lots of fun together, and they always remembered the day when Lily found the magic lamp on the shelf.
