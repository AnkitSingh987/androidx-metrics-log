Title: The Hairy Soldier and the Hat

Once upon a time, there was a hairy soldier. He was very big and strong. He lived in a small house near the forest. One day, the hairy soldier met a little boy. The boy looked at the soldier and said, "You are so hairy!" The soldier laughed and said, "Yes, I am! Do you want to touch my hair?" The boy reached out and gave the soldier's hair a little slap. They both laughed and played together. As they played, a big wind came and blew the soldier's hat away. The boy and the soldier ran after the hat. When they caught it, they saw that the wind was not just wind. It was a magic fairy! The fairy said, "Thank you for finding my hat. I will give you a wish." The hairy soldier wished to be smooth, and the fairy made it happen. Now the soldier was smooth, and he and the little boy were even better friends.

Once upon a time there were two best friends; a 3 year old called Jerry and a boot. Every day Jerry and the boot would ride the bus together and have lots of fun. One day Jerry decided to be brave and try something new. So, he gave the boot a big poke with his finger and said, "Let's go on an adventure!" The boot was scared but excited. It replied, "Yes, let's go!". Jerry and the boot hopped off the bus and began their adventure; they ran faster than the wind and explored all the places in the park. It was a brilliant journey and the two friends were happy. When night came, Jerry and the boot were sad to say goodbye. Jerry said, "It was awesome to go on this adventure with you. Will you come back tomorrow?" "Of course!", said the boot. The adventure ended and the two friends went their separate ways, but with a promise to do it again soon.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a man with a pistol. Tim's mom said, "Stay close to me, Tim." Tim was scared but he wanted to watch the man. The man was fast. He ran around the park and played with the pistol. Tim asked his mom, "Why does he have a pistol, Mom?" His mom said, "I don't know, Tim. But we should be careful." Then, the man with the pistol saw Tim and his mom. He ran to them very fast. Tim's mom told Tim, "Run, Tim, run!" But the man was too fast. He caught Tim and his mom. The man took them away, and they never saw their home again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy called Sam. He was so excited, because he was about to peek at something. He couldn't wait to find out what it was. He slowly crept to the corner so he wouldn't be seen. As he peeked around the corner, he saw something that made him gasp. It was a huge pepper! It was bright red and looked so yummy. He was about to grab it, when he noticed it was shaking. Sam grew very scared, because he knew something was about to happen. Suddenly, the pepper exploded, making a terrible noise and an even more terrible smell! Sam ran away as quickly as he could, away from the terrible mess. He never wanted to touch a pepper again.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy king. He lived in a big castle. The king had a job to do. He had to deliver a pretty box to a friend. One day, the king got on his horse. He said, "I will take the pretty box to my friend now." He put the pretty box in a bag and started to ride. On the way, the clumsy king fell off his horse. The pretty box fell out of the bag. A big bird saw the pretty box and took it away. The king was sad because he could not deliver the pretty box to his friend. And that is the bad ending of the story.

Once upon a time, there was a little goat. The goat had many friends. They liked to play together. One day, the goat found a big hole. The goat had an idea. "Let's bury my toy in the hole," said the goat to his friends. The friends helped the goat to bury the toy. They used their hands and feet to put dirt in the hole. "Now the toy is safe," said the goat. The friends were happy. They went to play more games. Later, a new friend came. The new friend wanted to play with the toy. The goat said, "I am sorry, the toy is not available. It is in the hole." The new friend was sad. The goat felt bad. The goat and his friends dug up the toy. They learned that it is good to share with others.
