Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Bear

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max liked to act foolish and make his friends laugh. He would jump and dance around all day. But sometimes, Max would get tired from all the fun. One day, Max saw a big, soft bed. It looked like a luxury bed just for him. He thought it would be a great place to rest. But the bed belonged to a big, grumpy bear named Ben. Ben did not like to share his things. Max decided to act brave and try to sleep on the luxury bed. When Ben saw Max on his bed, he got very mad. Ben roared at Max and told him to get off. Max was scared and realized he was being foolish. He said sorry to Ben and went back to his own little bed. From that day on, Max learned not to take things that didn't belong to him.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Max, went to the park. Tim was a good boy and Max was an obedient dog. They played with a big red ball. They had a lot of fun. A man came over and said, "Hello, little boy! I have a game. If your dog can nod, I will give you a medal." Tim looked at Max and said, "Max, can you nod?" Max nodded his head up and down. The man was happy and gave Tim a shiny medal. But then, something unexpected happened. The medal turned into a beautiful butterfly! The butterfly flew around Tim and Max. They laughed and clapped. The butterfly made their day even more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lucy. She had a pretty skirt. One day, she went to the park. At the park, Lucy saw a dead bug on the ground. She felt sad for the bug. She wanted to give it a seat to rest. Lucy put the dead bug on a leaf. She made a tiny seat for it with her skirt. Then, she said goodbye to the bug and went home.

One day, a big red car and a small blue car had a race. They wanted to see who was faster. The big red car was very fast, but the small blue car was lucky. They were both very excited. As they raced, the big red car went very fast. But the small blue car was lucky and found a shortcut. The small blue car went on the shortcut and got ahead of the big red car. They both raced faster and faster. Then, the big red car got a flat tire. The big red car was sad. The small blue car saw the big red car with the flat tire and stopped to help. They became friends and decided to race together. They were both very happy and lucky.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play outside. One day, he went to the park and found a perfect rock. It was smooth and round. Tim was very happy. Tim wanted to show his mom the perfect rock. He carried it with him all the way home. But on the way, he saw a big puddle. Tim liked to jump in puddles. So, he dropped the rock on the ground and jumped in the puddle. Splash! The water went everywhere. When Tim was done playing in the puddle, he picked up the perfect rock again. He took it home to his mom. She smiled and said it was a very nice rock. Tim felt proud and happy. From that day on, Tim and the perfect rock were best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. Sally had a round, red ball that she liked to roll all around her house. One day, she decided to roll her ball out into the front garden. The ball rolled and rolled until it came to a stop right at the edge of a big, blue pond. In the pond Sally could see an island full of water lilies. She was so excited! Still sitting in her pretty dress, Sally took off her shoes and socks, and rolled up her trousers then jumped into the pond. The water was cold, but that didn't stop Sally from paddling her way to the island. When Sally arrived on the island she noticed it was very busy. Dozens of water bugs were skidding around happily and birds were singing sweetly. This made Sally very happy so she decided she would play on the island all day. Sally closed her eyes and enjoyed the sunny day. Suddenly she heard a loud, familiar voice calling out. "Sally! Where are you!? Time to come home!" Sally smiled and quickly rolled her ball back down to the edge of the pond. "Coming!" She shouted and ran home.
