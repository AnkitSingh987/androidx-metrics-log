Category: Intermediate
Title: The Grumpy Man and the Bird

One day, a grumpy old man went for a walk. He saw a big tree with a big root. He said, "I don't like this root. It is in my way." He tried to walk around it, but he fell down. He was very mad. A little bird saw the grumpy old man. The bird said, "I can help you, but you must be nice." The grumpy old man said, "Okay, I will try to be nice." The bird flew away and came back with a big stick. The bird put the stick on the root. The grumpy old man walked on the stick and did not fall. He was happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The root started to move! The old man was scared. The root was not a root at all. It was a big snake! The snake said, "Thank you for being nice. I will not hurt you." The grumpy old man was not grumpy anymore. He was happy and said, "Thank you, snake." The old man, the bird, and the snake became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, old toy box. He loved to play with all his toys in the box. One day, Tim found a tiny stamp inside the toy box. The stamp had a picture of a funny cat on it. Tim showed the stamp to his mom. She told him that it was a magic stamp. Tim was very excited. He wanted to see what the magic stamp could do. Tim touched the stamp to his toy car. The car began to shrink! It became so small that it could fit in his hand. Tim played with his little, shrunken car all day. He was very happy with his magic stamp. At bedtime, Tim put the stamp back in his old toy box. He knew that the magic stamp would be there for more fun the next day. And Tim went to sleep with a big smile on his face.

Jill was a bossy girl who liked to tell other kids what to do. She had a big envelope with stickers and crayons that she did not want to share. She kept the envelope in her backpack and guarded it all the time. One day, Tom saw the envelope and wanted to see what was inside. He was curious and liked to draw. He waited until Jill was busy playing with her doll and sneaked to her backpack. He opened the zipper and took out the envelope. "Hey, that's mine!" Jill shouted when she saw Tom. She ran to him and tried to grab the envelope. Tom held it tight and said, "No, let me see. You have so many stickers and crayons. You can share some with me." Jill was angry and pinched Tom's arm hard. Tom cried out and dropped the envelope. The stickers and crayons spilled out on the floor. Jill picked up the envelope and stuffed everything back in. She glared at Tom and said, "You are a bad boy. You should not touch my things. Go away!" Tom rubbed his arm and felt sad. He did not want to be friends with Jill anymore. He thought she was mean and selfish. He went to find another kid who would play with him nicely. He hoped Jill would learn to be nicer and share her things.

Once upon a time, there was a little squirrel named Sam. Sam loved to find acorns in the big forest. One day, he found a very big acorn under a tall tree. Sam was so happy and wanted to show his best friend, Bella the bird. "Look, Bella! I found a big acorn!" Sam said, showing her the acorn. Bella looked at the acorn and said, "Wow, that is a big acorn, Sam! But I have to be honest, it looks a little bit different." As they looked at the acorn, something unexpected happened. The acorn started to wiggle and shake! Sam and Bella were so surprised! They watched as the acorn cracked open, and out came a tiny baby turtle! "Oops, I was wrong. This is not an acorn, it's a turtle egg!" Sam said, feeling a little embarrass. The baby turtle looked up at Sam and Bella and smiled. They all became good friends and played together in the big forest every day.
