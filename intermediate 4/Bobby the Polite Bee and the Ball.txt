Category: Intermediate
Title: Bobby the Polite Bee and the Ball

Once upon a time, in a big green garden, there lived a polite little bee named Bobby. Bobby loved to fly around, visit flowers, and make friends with everyone he met. He always said "please" and "thank you" when he took nectar from the flowers. One sunny day, Bobby was flying happily in the garden when he saw a big red ball. He wanted to play with it, but he didn't know how. As he tried to push the ball, he slipped and fell. Bobby felt embarrass because all his friends saw him. But his friends, the ants and the ladybugs, came to help him. They told Bobby, "Don't be sad, we all make mistakes." They showed him how to play with the ball and had a lot of fun together. From that day on, Bobby learned that it was okay to make mistakes and that his friends would always be there to help him.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Mia loved to eat pasta. She liked it so much that she ate it every day. One day, her mommy gave her a new pasta to try. It was a green pasta with a funny name. Mia was not sure if she would like it. Mia's mommy said, "I encourage you to try it. It might taste good!" Mia took a small bite. The green pasta tasted bitter. Mia did not like it. She was sad because she wanted to like the new pasta. The next day, Mia's mommy made her favorite pasta again. Mia was very happy. She ate all the pasta on her plate. Mia learned that it's okay to try new things, even if she doesn't like them.

Once upon a time, there was a small mouse. He was fancy. He loved to wear colourful hats and bows. He would proudly strut around, feeling pleased with himself because he was so fancy. One day, the mouse heard a strange noise. It was coming from a large cupboard. He walked up to it curiously and opened the door. Inside was a giant dog! The mouse was scared and started to back away. The dog started to laugh and said, "I demand you stay put!" The mouse froze in fear. The dog then demanded that the mouse was to stay in the cupboard forever. The mouse pleaded, but the dog ignored him. The dog slammed the cupboard shut and the mouse was never seen again.

Once upon a time, there was a very fast dog named Speedy. Speedy loved to run on the lawn. The lawn was big and pretty. Speedy thought it was the most attractive place to play. One day, Speedy met another dog named Tiny. Tiny said, "Hi Speedy! Do you want to play with me?" Speedy was happy and said, "Yes, let's play on the lawn!" They played together, running and jumping on the lawn. As they played, Tiny said, "Wow, Speedy! You are so fast!" Speedy smiled and said, "Thank you, Tiny! I like to speed on the lawn. It's so much fun!" They played until the sun went down and then went home, happy to have made a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to play with his toy cars. One day, he found a big steel ball in his yard. He didn't know what it was, but he thought it was cool. Tim showed the steel ball to his friend, Sam. Sam said, "Wow! What is that?" Tim said, "I don't know, but it's fun to play with!" They rolled the steel ball around and had a lot of fun. One day, Tim's mom told him the steel ball was fake. It was just a toy. Tim didn't care. He knew that fun things can happen with fake toys too. So, Tim and Sam kept playing with the steel ball and had many more happy days.

Once upon a time there was a very fast car. Every time it moved forward, the car zoomed and the wind blew its hair back. It was so fast it could make time go quickly. One day the car wanted to learn more about its history. So it decided to ask a turtle. The turtle had seen the car speed around for years. The turtle said "I've seen you speed by for lots of years! It makes me feel calm and peaceful." The car was so happy to hear that. It thanked the turtle for his kindness and sped off into the sunset. The car was excited to keep exploring its unknown history. The car continued to go faster and faster as it explored. It was sure that it could find out more about its history if it just kept going. And it did. The car found many new and exciting things that had been forgotten in the past. The car never forgot the turtle's words and kept its fast speed peaceful.
