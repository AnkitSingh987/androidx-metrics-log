Category: Intermediate
Title: Max and Lucy and the Woods

Once upon a time there were two friends, Max and Lucy. They liked playing together and exploring the world. One day they were walking in the woods and they spotted a big shiny radio on the ground. Max was really excited and he grabbed the radio. But then Lucy warned him to be careful. She said "Max, be careful, someone might have dropped the radio here and it might not be ours." Max thought for a minute and he decided to put the radio back. They continued walking in the woods until they found a sign with a message. It said: "Thank you for being so honest and returning the radio." Max and Lucy learnt a valuable lesson then – it’s important to be honest and always think of others before yourself. When we take care of each other, we can make the world a better place!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lisa. She loved to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, her mom asked her to wrap her lunch for school. Lisa was very excited to help. Lisa took a sandwich, some fruit, and a drink from the fridge. She put everything on the table. Then, she found some paper to wrap the sandwich. She put the wrapped sandwich, fruit, and drink into her lunch box. She was so proud of herself! But then, Lisa's little brother, Timmy, came into the kitchen. He was angry because he wanted to help too. But Lisa had already finished wrapping her lunch. To make Timmy happy, Lisa asked her mom if they could wrap Timmy's lunch together. Mom said yes, and they all worked together to wrap Timmy's lunch. In the end, everyone was happy and ready for school.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys and books. But they do not like to eat cauliflower. They think it is white and boring and yucky. One day, their mom makes a big pot of soup for lunch. She says, "I have a surprise for you. This soup is very yummy and healthy. You will love it." She puts some soup in their bowls and gives them spoons. Anna and Ben look at the soup. They see something white and round in it. They ask, "Mom, what is this?" Mom smiles and says, "This is cauliflower. It is a vegetable. It makes the soup creamy and tasty. Try it, please." Anna and Ben do not want to try it. They make faces and say, "No, thank you. We do not like cauliflower. Can we have something else?" Mom says, "No, this is what we have for lunch. You have to eat it. It is good for you. Be polite and eat your soup." Anna and Ben are sad and angry. They do not want to be polite. They want to have fun. They have an idea. They say, "OK, mom. We will eat our soup. But can we play a game first?" Mom says, "What game?" Anna and Ben say, "The spoon game. We can see who can toss the soup with the spoon the farthest. It will be fun." Mom thinks this is a bad idea. She says, "No, that is not a game. That is a mess. You will spill the soup everywhere. You will waste the food. You will make me angry. Do not play with your soup. Eat it now." Anna and Ben do not listen to mom. They think mom is boring. They want to play the spoon game. They say, "One, two, three, go!" They toss the soup with the spoons as hard as they can. The soup flies in the air. It lands on the table, on the floor, on the walls, on the curtains, on the couch, on the TV, on the dog, on mom. Mom is very angry. She says, "Anna and Ben, what have you done? Look at this mess. You are very naughty. You have to clean it up. And you have to eat your soup. No more games. No more toys. No more books. No more fun. Only soup and cauliflower." Anna and Ben are very sorry. They say, "We are sorry, mom. We did not mean to make you angry. We did not want to eat the cauliflower. We wanted to have fun. But we made a big mistake. We will clean up the mess. We will eat our soup. We will be polite. Please forgive us." Mom is still angry, but she is also sad. She says, "I forgive you, but you have to learn your lesson. Cauliflower is not yucky. It is yummy. You have to try new things. You have to be polite. You have to respect the food and the cook. You have to be good children. Do you understand?" Anna and Ben nod and say, "We understand, mom. We will try to be better. We will try the cauliflower. Maybe we will like it. Maybe it is not so bad. Maybe it is even fun." They take their spoons and eat their soup. They taste the cauliflower. They are surprised. It is not white and boring and yucky. It is creamy and tasty and yummy. They like it. They smile and say, "Mom, you were right. This soup is very yummy and healthy. We love it. Thank you for making it. You are the best mom ever." Mom smiles and says, "You are welcome, my sweet children. I am glad you like it. You are the best children ever. But please, do not play with your soup again. OK?" Anna and Ben say, "OK, mom. We promise. We will not play with our soup again. We will only play with our toys and books. And we will always be polite. And we will always eat our cauliflower. The end."
