Category: Intermediate
Title: The Boat and the Pond

Once upon a time, in a big pond, there was a boat. The boat was small and harmless. It liked to float on the water all day. The boat was very happy. One day, the boat saw some fish. The fish were playing together. The boat wanted to join them. The boat thought, "I want to unite with the fish and have fun too." So, the boat went to the fish and said, "Can I play with you?" The fish looked at the harmless boat and said, "Yes, let's unite and play together." So, the boat and the fish played all day. They were very happy to be friends. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play outside. One day, it was very wet. Sue put on her boots and went to play in the puddles. As she played, Sue found a shiny gem. She picked it up and said, "Wow, what a pretty gem!" A frog hopped out of the puddle and said, "That gem is mine. Can I have it back?" Sue looked at the frog and said, "Yes, you can have your gem back." The frog was very happy to receive his gem. He said, "Thank you, Sue!" Sue felt good helping the frog. She played with the frog and the gem until the sun came out. Then, they said goodbye and Sue went home.

Once upon a time, there was a big orange ball. It loved to play with its friends. One day, they played a game near a pit. They were very happy. The orange ball went too close to the pit. It fell in! The ball was scared. It did not know how to get out. It started to cry. Then it remembered something important. The ball remembered that it could bounce very high. It tried to bounce out of the pit. The ball jumped and jumped. Finally, it bounced out of the pit! The orange ball was very happy. It was not scared anymore. The ball and its friends were careful near the pit and played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little pet dog named Spot. Spot loved to play all day long. He would run and jump and fetch sticks. But one day, Spot felt very tired. Spot did not want to play anymore. He quit running and lay down on the soft grass. His little friend, a boy named Tim, came over to see what was wrong. Tim saw that Spot was tired and needed a rest. Tim sat down next to Spot and gently petted him. They stayed there for a while, resting together. After some time, Spot felt better and was not tired anymore. He jumped up and started to play with Tim again. They were both very happy and played until the sun went down.

Once upon a time, there was a soft, big bear. The bear liked to walk and see new things. One day, he decided to go on a tour around the forest. He wanted to see all the animals and trees. As the bear walked, he met a small, happy bird. The bird wanted to join the bear on his tour. They walked and talked, and saw many animals and trees. The bear and the bird became good friends. When the sun went down, the bear and the bird were tired. They found a nice, warm place to rest. They ate some yummy food and talked about their tour. Then, they settled down for a long sleep. They were happy to be friends and see the forest together.

Once upon a time, two sisters marched along a path in the forest. One sister was wearing a shiny pink dress and the other a blue dress. The pink sister wanted everyone to look at her, so she walked faster than her sister. The blue sister felt sad and called out to her sister to wait for her. But the pink sister ignored her and kept marching along the path. They came to a big river and the pink sister knew she had no way to cross. She asked her sister to help her, but her sister just said, “If you had been kinder and waited for me, I could have helped you cross the river.” The pink sister learnt her lesson, said sorry and waited for her sister. The blue sister helped her cross the river and the sisters marched on together. The moral of the story is that it’s always important to be kind to those around you. And if you do, they will help you when you need it.
