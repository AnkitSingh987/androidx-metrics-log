Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Tree

One day, a little girl named Lily found a powerful tool. The tool was a magic wand. She wanted to see what it could do. So, she went outside to play with the wand. Lily saw a big tree and whispered to the wand, "I want to climb the tree." The wand helped her climb up the tree very fast. She felt so happy and excited. Lily played with the wand all day, doing many fun things. She whispered to the wand to help her jump high, run fast, and even dance with the wind. At the end of the day, Lily knew that the powerful tool was her new best friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy who was anxious to get his first bike. He had been waiting for weeks! “Can I get my new bike now?” he asked his dad. “No, not yet,” said his dad. “You have to wait until your birthday.” The little boy was so anxious to get his bike that he put his hand on the handlebar of his dad’s bike and tried to move it. “No, no,” said his dad, gently taking the little boy’s hand off the bike. “That’s not yours yet. You have to wait.” The little boy started to cry, feeling frustrated and anxious. He wanted to get his bike so badly! His dad hugged him and told him he could wait a few more weeks. The little boy eventually calmed down and decided to be patient. He knew that if he waited, he would be able to get his bike on his birthday. He was so excited!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One sunny day, he saw a big cart in his yard. Tim was very happy. He wanted to put his toys in the cart and play with them. Tim put his toys in the cart. He had a fake dog, a ball, and a little car. Tim waved to his mom and dad. They waved back and smiled. Tim started to push the cart around the yard. As Tim played, he saw his friend Sam. Sam came over and they played together. They waved to each other and had fun. They pushed the cart and played with the fake dog, ball, and little car. The day was a happy day for Tim and Sam.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy cat named Bob. Bob loved to wear his soft, blue bathrobe all day. He wore it when he ate, when he played, and when he slept. One day, Bob found a big box in his room. He thought it would be fun to see if he could fit inside. He tried to get in, but his bathrobe was too big and fluffy. So, Bob took off his bathrobe and tried again. This time, he fit in the box perfectly! Bob was so happy and proud that he fit in the box. He played in it all day long. When it was time for bed, he put on his cozy bathrobe and went to sleep. He had sweet dreams about all the fun he had in his big box. And Bob lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a tough dog named Max. Max liked to run and play outside. He had a big yard with lots of toys. Max loved to watch the sunset every day. One day, Max was playing with his favorite toy when he heard a loud noise. He looked around and saw a big truck driving by. The truck dropped a big box in Max's yard. Max was very surprised and curious about the box. He thought, "What could be inside?" Max decided to destroy the box to see what was inside. He used his strong teeth to tear it apart. When the box was open, Max found something he never expected. It was a big, beautiful toy that looked just like the sunset. Max was so happy and played with his new toy every day. And from that day on, Max and his sunset toy watched the real sunset together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was an unusual tree. This tree had big, yellow bananas. One day, a boy named Tim saw the tree. He wanted to take the bananas home. Tim had a sack. He put the bananas in the sack. The sack was very heavy. Tim walked home with the sack. He was very happy. At home, Tim showed the bananas to his mom. She was very surprised. They ate the bananas together. They were the best bananas they ever had. And they always remembered the unusual tree.
