Category: Intermediate
Title: The Mole and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little mole. The mole lived in a hole under the ground. The mole had a door to keep his home safe. He had a key to unlock the door. One day, the mole lost his key. He looked and looked for the key, but he could not find it. The mole was sad. He wanted to unlock his door and go home. A persistent bird saw the sad mole. The bird wanted to help the mole. The bird looked for the key too. The bird found the key and gave it to the mole. The mole was very happy. He unlocked the door and went home. The mole and the bird became good friends.

---

One day, a quiet cat named Tom saw a rod on the floor. He wanted to play with it. He picked it up with his mouth and went to find his friend, a dog named Max. "Max, play with me and this rod!" said Tom. Max looked at the rod but did not want to play. "No, I refuse to play with that," said Max. Tom was sad but he still wanted to play. As Tom walked away, something unexpected happened. The rod began to wiggle and turned into a snake! Tom was scared and dropped the snake. Max saw this and ran to help his friend. They both chased the snake away and were happy to be safe. They played together for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, there was a painter. He liked to paint houses. One day, he had to deliver paint to a big house. The painter put the paint in his truck and drove away. On the way, he saw a big hill. The truck had to work hard to go up the hill. At the top of the hill, the painter saw something unexpected. A big, funny, colorful bird was in the road. The bird looked at the painter and said, "Can I help you paint the house?" The painter was surprised. He said, "Yes, you can help me!" So, the bird picked up a paintbrush with its beak. They painted the house together. The house looked beautiful. The painter and the bird became best friends. They painted many houses and everyone was happy.

Sam was a little boy who liked to poke things. He poked his toys, his books, his food, and sometimes his sister. He thought poking was fun and funny, but not everyone agreed. One morning, Sam woke up early and saw his mom's alarm on the nightstand. He wondered what it was and how it worked. He decided to poke it and see what happened. He poked the buttons, the screen, and the snooze. He heard a loud beep and saw some numbers change. He poked it again and again, making more beeps and noises. His mom heard the alarm and woke up. She was very tired and frustrated. She wanted to sleep more, but the alarm was too noisy. She looked at Sam and saw him poking the alarm with a big smile. She was not happy. She took the alarm from Sam and turned it off. She told him to stop poking things and go back to bed. Sam was confused and sad. He did not understand why his mom was angry. He liked the alarm and wanted to play with it. He did not know it was for waking up and not for poking. He went back to his bed and hugged his teddy bear. He wished he had a poke alarm of his own.

Once upon a time, there was a bear and a fox who lived in a forest together. Bear was a fancy dresser, while fox dressed in simple things. Fox said to bear, "Let's make a trap so we can catch something yummy to eat." Bear thought it was a great idea and he was excited to make the trap. After a few days, they had finished making the trap and tested it out. The trap worked, but bear thought it could be improved. So he decided to decorate the trap with some fancy decorations. Fox looked at the trap and said, "It looks much better now! We will be able to catch more yummy treats now I bet." Bear agreed and said, "Yes, the trap looks fancy and much better with these decorations. Now let's set it up and see what treats we can catch!" They both cheered and cheered and set up the trap, and soon they were catching lots of tasty treats. The end.
