Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Tutor and the Bowl

Once upon a time, there was a tutor named Tom. Tom loved to help kids learn. He was very good at it. One day, Tom went to teach a little girl named Sue. Sue was a smart girl, but she needed help with her letters. Tom and Sue worked hard all day. They played fun games to learn the letters. But after a while, Tom started to suffer. He felt very hungry. He looked around and saw an empty bowl on the table. Tom wished he had food to eat. Just then, Sue had an idea. She went to the kitchen and came back with a big plate of cookies. Tom was so happy! He didn't know Sue could make cookies. They shared the cookies and laughed. The day was not only about learning letters but also about making friends and having fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a big garden. In the garden, there were many pretty flowers and yummy fruits. Birds liked to sing in the garden, and kids liked to play there too. One day, a loud noise came from the sky. It was a big strike from the clouds. The kids and birds were scared and ran away. The garden was not happy and the flowers were sad. But then, something unexpected happened. The big strike made the flowers and fruits grow even more! The garden became more beautiful than before. The kids and birds came back, and they all played and sang together again. The garden was happy and everyone had fun.

Once there was a little girl named Trish who was always curious. One day she decided to learn something new. Trish asked her mom, “Mom, what can I learn today?” Her mom thought for a moment and said, “Trish, you can learn how to use salt this afternoon.” Trish was excited and replied, “Yes, I will learn how to use salt!” Trish went outside and found a small crystal of salt. She cautiously examined it and was a bit frightened of the white sparkles. She asked her mom, “Mom, what should I do with the salt?” Her mom smiled and said, “Trish, you can put the salt in your food and it will make it taste better.” Trish felt happy and carefully put the salt in her food. When Trish tasted it, she knew that it was different than before. She liked how it tasted and said, “Mom! I’m glad I learned how to use salt today.” She was no longer frightened of the salt and was happy that she had learned something new.

Once upon a time, a fat bird lived in a cage. The bird was sad because it could not fly and play with the other birds. It sat in its cage and watched the birds outside. One day, a little boy came to the cage. He saw the fat bird and said, "Why are you so sad, bird?" The bird replied, "I want to fly and play with the other birds, but I am in this cage." The little boy opened the cage and the fat bird flew out. It was so happy to be free. It played with the other birds and the little boy watched them. The fat bird was not sad anymore, and it became friends with the little boy. They all played together and had a lot of fun.

One day, a helpful cat found a big balloon. The cat saw the balloon spin and spin. The cat wanted to play with the balloon. But the balloon was up in a tree. The cat tried to get the balloon but could not. A dog saw the cat and said, "What are you doing?" The cat said, "I want to play with the balloon, but it is too high." The dog said, "I will help you get it." The dog tried to jump up and get the balloon. But the dog could not reach the balloon too. The cat and the dog were sad. They really wanted the balloon. A bird saw them and said, "I can help you get the balloon." The bird flew up to the tree and got the balloon. The bird brought the balloon down to the cat and the dog. The cat and the dog were happy. But the bird did not let go of the balloon. The bird flew away with the balloon. The cat and the dog were sad again. They did not get to play with the spinning balloon.
