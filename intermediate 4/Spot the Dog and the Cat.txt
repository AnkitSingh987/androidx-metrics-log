Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Cat

One day, a cute little dog named Spot found a big maze. Spot wanted to play in the maze. He ran and jumped into the maze. Inside the maze, Spot met a tiny mouse named Mimi. Mimi was lost and scared. Spot said, "Hi, Mimi! I will help you." Mimi was happy. They walked in the maze together. But then, they saw a big cat. The cat wanted to catch Mimi. Mimi was very scared. Spot said, "Stop, cat! Do not catch Mimi!" The cat stopped. Spot and Mimi ran away fast. They found the way out of the maze. Mimi said, "Thank you, Spot!" They were safe and happy.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a girl named Amy. Amy liked to play outside in the sun. But one day, the sun was hiding behind the clouds. It was a harsh day, and the wind blew the dust around. Amy wanted to go outside, but her mom said, "No, it is too harsh today. You can't play with the dust." Amy was sad, but she knew her mom was right. So, she stayed inside and played with her toys. As the day went on, the wind stopped blowing and the sun came out. The dust became still, and the day was not harsh anymore. Amy's mom said, "Now you can go outside and play." Amy was happy and ran outside to play in the sun.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Blinky. Blinky lived in a big ocean with his family. Blinky loved to blink his eyes and swim around in the water. One day, Blinky saw a big, fancy shell. He swam over to the shell and said, "Hello, fancy shell! Do you want to play with me?" The fancy shell did not say anything, but Blinky was happy to have a new friend. Blinky and the fancy shell played all day in the ocean. They swam up and down, and Blinky blinked his eyes a lot. As the sun went down, Blinky said, "Goodbye, fancy shell! I will see you tomorrow!" Blinky swam back to his family, and they all blinked their eyes and went to sleep.

Jenny loved to play with her scissors. She liked to cut paper, cloth, and even her hair. She thought it was fun to make different shapes and sounds with the scissors. But she did not know that scissors could be dangerous. One day, she found a brown envelope on the table. It had daddy's name on it. She wondered what was inside. She decided to cut it open with her scissors. She did not see the sharp edge of the scissors. She cut her finger and it hurt a lot. She cried and ran to daddy. Daddy saw the blood on her finger and the envelope. He was angry and sad. He said, "Jenny, what did you do? This envelope had a very important paper for my work. You ruined it and you hurt yourself. You should not play with scissors. They are not toys. They can cut you and other things. You have to be careful and ask before you touch things that are not yours." Jenny felt sorry and scared. She said, "I'm sorry, daddy. I just wanted to see what was inside. I did not mean to cut it or hurt myself. I did not know scissors were dangerous. I will not play with them again. Please don't be mad at me." Daddy hugged her and said, "I'm not mad at you, Jenny. I love you. But you have to learn from your mistake. Scissors are tools, not toys. You have to respect them and use them only when you need them and with someone's help. And you have to respect other people's things and not cut them without permission. Do you understand?" Jenny nodded and said, "I understand, daddy. I'm sorry. I love you too." Daddy kissed her and said, "It's okay, Jenny. I forgive you. Let me put a bandage on your finger and we can talk more later. Come on, let's go." The end.

Once upon a time, there was a small cat named Tom. Tom was very dependable. He was always there to help his friends. One day, Tom and his friend, a little bird named Tim, were playing in the park. Tim flew too fast and hit a tree. "Ouch!" he cried, feeling pain. Tom heard Tim and ran to help him. "Are you okay?" Tom asked, looking at his friend with worry. "I'm hurt," Tim whispered, tears in his eyes. Tom hugged Tim gently and said, "Don't worry, I'll help you." Tom picked up Tim and took him to their friend, Dr. Dog. Dr. Dog fixed Tim's wing, and soon, Tim was feeling much better.
