Category: Intermediate
Title: The Soldier and the Coat

Once upon a time, there was a soldier. He had a long coat. One day, he went for a walk. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. The soldier was happy. As the soldier walked, he saw a big tree. He wanted to start climbing. He climbed higher and higher. When he was at the top, he saw a big nest. In the nest, there were three little birds. They were hungry. The soldier felt bad for the birds. He decided to help them. He climbed down and found some worms. He went back up and gave them to the birds. But, when the soldier looked down, his long coat was stuck! The birds' mom came back and saw the soldier. She thought he was a friend. So, she helped him get unstuck. The soldier and the birds became friends, and they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a lime. The lime was green and round. Lucy was excited. She wanted to show her friends the lime. She ran very fast to find her friends. Lucy found her friends Tom and Sue. They were playing a game. Lucy showed them the lime. Tom and Sue were excited too. They had an idea. They wanted to race with the lime. Tom, Sue, and Lucy started the race. They rolled the lime on the ground. They ran as fast as they could. The lime went very fast too. In the end, Lucy won the race. They all laughed and played with the lime. They had a fun day.

Once upon a time there was a little boy who had a tower. He was very independent and loved to play with it. Every day he would take it outside and play with it in the sunshine. He had lots of fun with his tower, climbing it and moving it around. One day when he was playing with it he noticed something different. A little bird had built a nest on top of the tower! He was so excited and watched the bird closely. Every day he could see her flying back and forth to the nest, carrying small twigs to build her home. He was so proud of her and how independent she was. Finally the nest was finished and he said goodbye to the bird. He was sad to leave his new friend, but happy that she had built a home for herself. From then on he would never forget his tower and the little bird who lived in it.

One day, a little girl named Emma went to the store with her mom. They wanted to buy some yogurt. Emma was very excited because she loved yogurt. She picked a big cup of her favorite yogurt and put it in the cart. While they were shopping, a big dog walked into the store. Emma was scared of the big dog. The dog saw the yogurt in the cart and wanted it. He barked, "Woof! Can I have some yogurt, please?" Emma was scared, but she knew that sharing was good. So, she said, "Okay, you can have some yogurt." The dog was very happy and ate the yogurt quickly. After that, the dog wasn't scary anymore. They became friends and played together. Emma learned that sometimes, when you share and are kind to others, you can make new friends.

One day, a cat named Tom and a dog named Max were playing outside. They saw a fancy bird with big, pretty wings. The bird was taking a shower in a puddle. Tom and Max wanted to join the bird. Tom said to Max, "Let's go play with the bird!" Max agreed and they both went to the puddle. The bird saw them coming and stopped taking a shower. The bird was not sure if Tom and Max were nice. Max wanted to be friends with the bird, so he said, "Do you want to play with us?" The bird thought for a moment and said, "Yes, I would like to play with you." Max was happy and suggested, "Let's play tag!" So, Tom, Max, and the fancy bird played tag all day long. They had lots of fun and became good friends. After they were tired, they all sat down and watched the sun go down. They knew they would play together again soon.
