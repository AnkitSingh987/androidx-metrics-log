Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Famous Boy and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a famous boy named Tom. He had a sword that he loved very much. One day, Tom lost his sword while playing in the park. He was very sad. Tom's mom told him not to worry. She said the sword would return to him one day. Tom did not understand, but he hoped his mom was right. Many days passed, and Tom played in the park again. He found a big box under a tree. When he opened the box, his sword was inside! Just like his mom said, the sword returned to him. Tom was very happy and knew his mom was very wise.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to school. She was very calm and happy. In her class, there was a big box. The teacher said, "We will open the box later." Lily and her friends played and learned all day. They had fun, but they could not stop thinking about the box. They all wanted to know what was inside. They would pause and look at it from time to time. At the end of the day, the teacher said, "Now we can open the box!" All the kids gathered around. When they opened it, they found new toys to play with. Lily and her friends were so happy. They knew that the fun would never stop at school.

One day, a sad person sat under a big tree. They had a book and a pen. They wanted to write a happy story to feel better. The sun was shining, and the sky was blue. It was a nice day to write. A small bird saw the sad person and flew down. The bird said, "Why are you sad? The sun is out, and the sky is blue." The person said, "I want to write a happy story, but I don't know what to write." The bird thought for a moment and said, "I know! Write about me and my friends playing in the trees." The sad person smiled and started to write. They wrote about the bird and its friends flying, singing, and playing. As they wrote, they started to feel happy. The sun shone brighter, and the sky seemed even more blue. Soon, the story was done. The person read the happy story out loud, and the bird and its friends listened. They all laughed and clapped. The person was not sad anymore. They had written a happy story, just like the bird said.

Once upon a time there was a boy named Jack. He was three years old and very adventurous. One day, Jack decided to go for a walk outside to explore. He started to wander over a distant hill, not knowing what he would find. As he reached the top of the hill a big surprise was waiting for him. He saw a very tall man with a long cape and a hat. The man asked Jack in an exciting voice, “Would you like to kick a ball?” Jack was elated and eagerly nodded his head. He ran over to the ball, smiled and kicked it with all his strength. The ball flew up in the air and went right past the man’s distant head. The man just stood still as if he expected it. He then yelled out, “Good job Jack! You’re a kick champion!" Jack was so proud of himself and smiled from ear to ear. Since then, Jack has been kicking his ball with even more excitement!

Once upon a time, there were two best friends named Harry and Sarah. They did everything together. One day, they decided to go exploring in the woods. As they were walking, they saw a big switch on the ground. They both wanted the switch and started to quarrel over it. "It's mine!" shouted Harry. "No, I saw it first!" Sarah protested. They argued for a few minutes until Harry had an idea. "Let's make it popular," he said. "How do we do that?" Sarah asked. "Simple - let's both have it! We can share it." And so they did. Harry and Sarah shared the switch, and soon everyone wanted to play with it. They became the most popular kids in their town!
