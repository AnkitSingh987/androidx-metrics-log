Category: Intermediate
Title: The Tree and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a big tree in a park. The tree had many leaves. On the ground, there were two honest ants. They were best friends. They played together every day. One day, a strong wind came. It blew the ants far away from each other. They were sad because they were separated. They wanted to find each other again. The ants asked for help from the other bugs. The bugs were kind and helped them. They walked and walked until they found each other. The ants were happy to be together again. They promised to always be honest and never separate again.

Once upon a time, there was a modest cat named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with his friend, a little girl named Sue. They played and had fun all day. Tim and Sue liked to pretend and believe in magic. One day, they found a big red box with a loud alarm inside. When they pushed the button, it made a loud noise. They laughed and played with the alarm all day. They believed it was a magic alarm. The next day, they pushed the alarm button again. But this time, something unexpected happened. Instead of a loud noise, the alarm made a soft, sweet sound. Tim and Sue looked at each other in surprise. They laughed and danced to the sweet sound. The magic alarm had made their day even more fun!

Ben and Lily were twins who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big basket of muffins on a bench. They were hungry and wanted to eat some. But the basket had a sign that said "For the birds. Please do not touch." Ben did not care about the sign. He said, "I want a muffin. They look yummy." He ran to the basket and grabbed a muffin. Lily followed him and said, "Ben, stop! That is not nice. The muffins are for the birds. They need food too." Ben did not listen to Lily. He bit the muffin and said, "Mmm, this is good. I want another one." He reached for another muffin. But then, he heard a loud squawk. A big crow flew down from a tree and pecked his hand. Ben dropped the muffin and cried, "Ow, ow, ow! That bird hurt me!" Lily ran to Ben and hugged him. She said, "Ben, are you okay? I told you not to take the muffins. The crow was trying to protect them. He was angry that you took his food." Ben felt sorry and ashamed. He said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I was greedy and rude. I should have listened to you. I should have respected the birds and their food. Can you forgive me?" Lily smiled and said, "Of course, I forgive you, Ben. You are my brother and I love you. But next time, please be kind and share. There is enough food for everyone." Ben nodded and said, "You are right, Lily. You are my sister and I love you too. Next time, I will be kind and share. And I will not touch the muffins again. They are cold and hard anyway." They hugged and went back to their mom, who was waiting for them. She gave them a warm and soft cookie each. They thanked her and ate the cookie. They learned their lesson and never bothered the birds or their muffins again.

Once upon a time, there was a big town. In this town, there was a huge tree. The tree was so big that everyone could see it. People loved to play and talk under the tree. The tree was the center of the town's network. One day, a little girl named Lily went to the huge tree. She saw a small bird on a branch. The bird looked sad. Lily asked the bird, "Why are you sad?" The bird replied, "I can't find my family. I lost them when I was flying." Lily wanted to help the bird. She had an idea. She asked all her friends and the people in the town to come to the huge tree. When everyone was there, Lily said, "This bird needs our help. Let's use our network to find his family." Everyone agreed to help. The people in the town talked to their friends and family. Soon, the whole town was looking for the bird's family. After some time, a boy found the bird's family in another tree. The boy brought the bird's family to the huge tree. The bird was so happy to see his family. He thanked Lily and the people in the town. The bird said, "I received so much help from all of you. Thank you!" From that day on, the bird and his family lived in the huge tree, and everyone in the town became even closer friends.
