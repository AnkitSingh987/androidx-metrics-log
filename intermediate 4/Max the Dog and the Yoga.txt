Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Yoga

One day, a big dog named Max wanted to go to yoga class. He liked to stretch and have fun. Max was very excited to learn new yoga moves. When Max got to the yoga class, he saw an enormous mat. It was so big! Max thought it was the perfect mat for him. He went on the mat and started to do yoga. Max did many yoga moves. He felt happy and strong. All his friends saw him and wanted to try yoga too. They all had a great time together. And that is how Max and his friends learned to love yoga.

One day, a little bird named Tim was sitting on a tree branch. The wind was blowing fast, and the leaves were moving. Tim wanted to fly in the wind, but he was scared. He called his friend, a big bird named Sam. "Sam, I want to fly in the wind, but I am scared. Will you fly with me?" asked Tim. "Of course, Tim! I will fly with you, and we will be safe together," said Sam. So, Tim and Sam started to fly. They sped through the sky, and the wind was fun. They laughed and played in the air. They felt safe because they were together. When they were done, they went back to the tree and sat on the branch. Tim was happy he had a friend like Sam to help him feel safe.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was a very obedient boy. He always did what his mom and dad told him to do. One day, Tim was playing in his room when he noticed a light coming from under his bed. He got down on his hands and knees to see where the light was coming from. As he looked closer, he saw a tiny door with the light shining through. Tim was very curious, so he opened the tiny door. To his surprise, he found a little mouse wearing a hat and glasses! The mouse said, "Hello, Tim! My name is Mr. Whiskers. I have been living under your bed for a long time, but you never noticed me before." Tim was happy to have a new friend, and they played together every day. But one day, Mr. Whiskers told Tim that he had to go away. Tim was sad, but he knew that friends sometimes have to say goodbye.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Mia. She was three years old and very curious. One day Mia wanted to have a special goal. She wanted to find the biggest tree in the world! Mia was determined to do this, so she set off on her journey. Mia travelled far and wide, looking for the biggest tree. But she was troubled when she couldn't find it. She began to feel sad, but then she remembered something her Mama had said: "When you're troubled, it's always best to care for others." Mia decided to take her Mama's advice. She talked to children she met along the way and she asked them what their biggest goal was. She was so happy to hear the wonderful things they wanted to achieve. Instead of looking for the biggest tree, Mia focused on helping the children achieve their goals. Mia discovered that helping others was the best way to feel happy. She soon learned that when you care for others, that's when life can be the most wonderful!

Jill went for a jog in the park. She wore a bright blue scarf around her neck. She jogged around and around the park, feeling the cool wind on her face. Suddenly, she felt something rough brush against her skin. It was the bark of a tree! Jill laughed with delight and kept jogging around. Next, she jogged past a fountain with sparkling water. Ducks swam around the edges, and she waved to them with her scarf. Jill felt so happy she could jog. She stopped at the end of the park and made a wish: "I hope I can go jogging here again soon!"

Once upon a time, there was a little bug named Ben. Ben was an independent bug who loved to eat leaves. One day, he found a big, green leaf on a tree. He wanted to bite it and eat it all by himself. "Hello, Ben!" said his friend, a small bird named Billy. "What are you doing?" Ben looked at Billy and said, "I want to bite this leaf, but it's too high for me. Can you help me?" Billy thought for a moment and then said, "I can help you if you share the leaf with me." Ben was not happy. He wanted to eat the leaf all by himself. But he knew he needed help to get the leaf. So, he agreed to share it with Billy. Together, they pulled the leaf down from the tree. As they began to eat the leaf, another bug named Bob came by. "Can I have some too?" he asked. Ben didn't want to share, but Billy said, "Of course! We can all share the leaf." So, they all ate the leaf together and became good friends.
