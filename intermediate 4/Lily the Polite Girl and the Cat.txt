Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Polite Girl and the Cat

Once upon a time, in a little house, there lived a polite girl named Lily. She had a small dog named Max. They loved to play and laugh together. Lily liked to clap her hands and Max would jump and wag his tail. They were very happy. One day, Lily and Max went to a big show. There were many people there. Lily was excited to see the show and wanted to applaud for the performers. She smiled and her lip was shiny. Max wagged his tail and barked, ready to have fun. During the show, Max saw a big cat. He wanted to play with the cat, so he ran after it. Lily tried to catch Max, but he was too fast. Max and the cat ran away, far from the show. Lily was sad and cried. She could not find Max and had to go home without her best friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a brother named Sam. They loved to play together. One day, they went outside to collect colorful rocks. Tim and Sam walked around and looked at the ground. They found many colorful rocks. Tim found a big red rock, while Sam found a small blue rock. They were so happy with their rocks. At the end of the day, Tim and Sam went home. They showed their mom and dad the colorful rocks they collected. Their mom and dad were proud of them. Tim and Sam put their rocks in a special box. They loved their colorful rock collection.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Sam. Sam was a youth who loved to play with his friends outside. They would run, jump, and have lots of fun together. One day, Sam saw a new kid in the park. The new kid looked a little rough, with messy hair and dirty clothes. Sam wanted to be nice, so he said, "Welcome to our park! Do you want to play with us?" The new kid smiled and said, "Yes, I would love to play!" They all played together and had lots of fun. The new kid was very good at playing games, even though he looked rough. But then, something unexpected happened. The new kid started to change! He turned into a big, friendly dog! Sam and his friends were very surprised. The dog wagged its tail and licked Sam's face. They all laughed and played with the dog. The rough-looking new kid was actually a magical dog who just wanted to have friends to play with. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little cat named Coco was playing in the yard. She saw a long cord on the ground. Coco was curious, so she stepped on the cord. The cord started to move! Coco was surprised. "Who are you?" asked the cord. The cord could talk! "I am Mr. Cord," it said. Coco and Mr. Cord became friends. They played together all day long. Mr. Cord was very good at jumping and swinging. Coco was grateful for her new friend. One day, Coco saw a big tree with a toy stuck in it. She wanted the toy, but she could not reach it. "I can help you," said Mr. Cord. He swung up into the tree and got the toy for Coco. Coco was very happy and grateful. She hugged Mr. Cord and said, "Thank you, Mr. Cord! You are the best friend ever!"

Once upon a time, there was a big red bus. The bus liked to drive around town every day. One day, the bus had to sit in traffic. There were many cars in front of him. The bus was not happy. In the traffic, there was a broken car. The broken car could not move. All the other cars had to go around it. The bus had to wait for a long time. Finally, the broken car was fixed. The traffic started to move again. The big red bus was happy. It could drive around town and take people where they needed to go. And everyone lived happily ever after.

John was a three-year-old boy. He was looking out the window and asking questions. He saw a large blouse dancing in the wind. He had never seen anything like it before. He was excited and began to ask questions. John's mom saw him and asked, "Why are you asking so many questions, John?" John pointed out the window and said, "I saw a large blouse out there!" John's mom laughed and said, "Yes, that is a large blouse. But why are you asking so many questions?" John said, "I just wanted to know what it was! I have never seen something like it before." John's mom smiled, and said, "That's a great question, John. Now let's go outside and take a closer look at that large blouse!" And so John and his mom went outside and explored the strange new blouse.
