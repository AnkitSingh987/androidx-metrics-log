Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Boat

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She lived near the sea with her mom and dad. Amy loved to play with her original toy boat in the water. One day, she went to the sea and met a talking fish. "Hi, Amy!" said the fish. "I can raise the water if you want to play more." "Really? That would be fun!" Amy said. The talking fish raised the water, and Amy's toy boat floated higher. Suddenly, a big wave came and took her boat far away. Amy was sad, but the talking fish helped her find her boat. They became best friends and played in the sea every day.

One day, a dog named Max and a cat named Lily were playing in the park. They liked to play catch with a red ball. Max would throw the ball and Lily would run to catch it. They were having a lot of fun. Suddenly, Max saw a big sign that said "Stay." Max told Lily, "We need to stay here. The sign says so." Lily asked, "Why do we need to stay?" Max said, "I don't know, but it must be important." Just then, a loud noise came from the sky. A big bird was flying down with something in its beak. The bird dropped a big, blue box in front of Max and Lily. The box had a note on it that said, "Emergency." Max and Lily opened the box and found lots of easy puzzles and games inside. They realized that the sign and the emergency box were there to help them have even more fun at the park. They played with their new toys and had the best day ever.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with blocks. He had many blocks in different colors. He liked to build tall towers with them. One day, Tim found a smelly sock under his bed. He did not like the smell. He wanted to delay playing with his blocks to find out where the smell was coming from. Tim looked around his room and found more smelly socks. Tim picked up all the smelly socks and put them in a big pile. He thought it would be fun to build a tower with his blocks on top of the smelly socks. But when he tried to build the tower, it fell down. The smelly socks made the blocks slide and not stay together. Tim was sad because he could not play with his blocks anymore. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a brave bear who lived in the forest. He always wanted to protect all the things he loved. One day, he saw some mean animals setting a trap in the woods. The brave bear rushed over and tried to stop them, but they were too strong for him. The mean animals kept setting the trap, and soon it was finished. The brave bear knew he had to protect the forest, so he decided to try and save it himself. He crawled inside the trap and waited until the mean animals came to take it away. The mean animals were very surprised to find the brave bear inside the trap. They ran away fast! The brave bear was able to protect the forest, and everyone was happy. The animals all thanked the bear for being so brave and keeping them safe.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a pink hat. She wanted to show it to her friend, Tom. So, Mia went to Tom's house. Tom saw the hat and liked it too. Mia tried to explain where she found the hat, but Tom did not understand. Tom put the hat on his thumb and it fit just right. They both laughed and played with the hat. They had a lot of fun. Then, something unexpected happened. The hat started to grow bigger and bigger on Tom's thumb. Mia and Tom were surprised by the big pink hat. They did not know what to do. Then, Mia had an idea. She put the hat on her head and it stopped growing. Mia and Tom were happy that the hat was not growing anymore. They played with the big pink hat all day and laughed a lot.
