Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Lazy Cat and the Door

One day, a lazy cat named Tom found a big freezer in the kitchen. He wanted to make cold treats for his friends. Tom thought it would be fun to play with the freezer. Tom opened the freezer and put in cups of juice. He wanted the juice to become cold and yummy. He waited and waited, but he was too lazy to check if the treats were ready. His friends came to play, but when they opened the freezer, the juice was not cold. It was still warm! Tom forgot to close the freezer door. His friends were sad, and they went home. Tom was sad too because he did not make cold treats for his friends.

Once upon a time there was a big, fat cat named Tom. Tom was very lazy and loved to lounge around all day at the pool. One hot day, Tom refused to get out of the pool. He kept on swimming and splashing around, not wanting to be bothered. "Tom, you need to get out of the pool," said Tom's owner. "You can't just stay in the pool all day, it's not healthy." But Tom just refused to listen and kept on swimming around. "Tom, this is your last warning," said his owner. "If you don't get out now, I'm going to have to take away your pool privileges." Tom finally stopped swimming and slowly waddled out of the pool. He was sad, but he knew his owner was right. From then on, Tom always followed the rules and never refused to get out of the pool.

Once upon a time, in a small, organized town, there was a big, strong man named Tom. Tom had big shoulders and was very helpful to everyone. He loved to help people in his town and always had a smile on his face. One day, Tom saw a small boy crying because his toy was on a high shelf. Tom walked up to the boy and asked, "Why are you crying?" The boy pointed to the shelf and said, "My toy is up there, but I can't reach it." Tom used his strong shoulders to lift the boy up high, and the boy got his toy. The boy was very happy, and Tom felt good for helping him. As the days went on, Tom continued to help people in his town. He noticed that his happiness and the happiness of others increased each time he helped. Soon, everyone in the town became kinder and more helpful to each other. The moral of the story is that when we help others, our happiness and the happiness of those around us will increase.

Once upon a time, in a little house, there was a chubby cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play with her toy spear. One sunny day, Fluffy saw her toy spear outside the house. She wanted to play with it. Fluffy's friend, a small bird named Chirpy, saw that the door was locked. Chirpy said, "Oh no, Fluffy! The door is locked. How will you get your toy spear?" Fluffy was sad and didn't know what to do. Then, Fluffy saw a key on the ground. She picked it up with her mouth and unlocked the door. Fluffy was so happy she could play with her toy spear again. She said, "Thank you, Chirpy!" They played together all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a perfect marble. It was red and shiny, and he loved to play with it. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw his friend, Sue, sitting on the grass. "Hi Sue, look at my perfect marble!" said Tim. Sue looked at the marble and smiled. "Wow, it's so pretty!" she said. They both sat on the grass and played with the marble. They rolled it back and forth to each other. As they played, a dog named Spot came over. Spot saw the marble and thought it was a ball. He picked it up in his mouth and ran away. Tim and Sue chased Spot, calling, "Come back, Spot! That's our marble!" Spot stopped and dropped the marble. Tim and Sue picked it up and thanked Spot. They all sat down and played together. From that day on, Tim, Sue, and Spot became the best of friends, and they played with the perfect marble every day.
