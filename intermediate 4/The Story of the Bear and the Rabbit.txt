Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Bear and the Rabbit

Once upon a time, in a green forest, there lived a big bear and a little rabbit. They were best friends. The bear was very strong and he liked to protect the rabbit from danger. The rabbit was very smart and he knew many things. One day, they found a menu on the ground. The rabbit picked it up and said, "Bear, look! This is a menu. It tells us what food we can eat." The bear was happy because he was very hungry. "Let's go find the food on the menu!" said the bear. They looked for the food together. The rabbit found some carrots and the bear found some fish. They sat down and shared their food. "Thank you for protecting me, Bear," said the rabbit. "And thank you for showing me the menu, Rabbit," said the bear. They ate the food and were very happy.

One day, a monkey named Max was playing with his friends. Max was very competitive. He liked to win all the games they played. Max said, "Let's play a game where we bury things in the sand and find them!" Max and his friends started to bury their toys in the sand. They took turns hiding and finding the toys. Max was very good at finding the toys. He found them all very fast. His friends were not as fast as Max. Then, something unexpected happened. Max found a big, shiny box buried in the sand. Max and his friends opened the box. They found new toys inside! Max said, "Wow! This is so fun! We can all play with these new toys together!" Max learned that winning was not the most important thing. Playing with his friends was more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a humble little girl named Sue. She loved to play with her toys and eat gum. One day, she found a big box of gum under her bed. She was very happy and decided to eat all the gum at once. While she was eating the gum, something unexpected happened. The gum started to grow bigger and bigger in her mouth! Sue was scared and didn't know what to do. She tried to take the gum out, but it was stuck. Then, her big brother Tom came into the room. He saw Sue and wanted to help prevent the gum from getting bigger. He had an idea! Tom grabbed a big glass of water and told Sue to drink it. Sue drank the water and the gum stopped growing. The water made the gum smaller, and Sue was able to take it out of her mouth. Sue learned not to eat too much gum at once, and she was very thankful for her brother's help.

Once upon a time, there was a soldier. He was very thirsty. He wanted to find something to drink. The soldier walked and walked until he found an open door. Inside, he saw a big cup. He thought, "This must be for me to drink!" He picked it up and took a big sip. But it was not water! Suddenly, the soldier started to get small. He was now as small as a bug! The soldier was scared, but he knew he had to find a way to be big again. The soldier saw a small door in the room. He walked through it and found a magic flower. He took a bite of the flower and, just like that, he grew back to his normal size. The soldier was so happy and went back to his friends to tell them the story of his big adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a huge alligator. He lived in a big pond. One day, he felt very bored. The alligator saw a little bird near the pond. He said, "Hello, little bird. Do you want to play with me?" The little bird was scared of the huge alligator, but she did not want to be mean. So, she said, "No, thank you. I am busy." The alligator felt sad. He let out a big yawn. He was lonely and wanted a friend. He went to look for someone else to play with. He saw a big turtle near the pond. The alligator said, "Hello, big turtle. Do you want to play with me?" The big turtle was not scared of the alligator. He said, "Yes, I will play with you." The huge alligator was happy. He had found a friend. They played together all day long.
