Title: Spot the Pale Dog and the Duck

Once upon a time, there was a pale dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside in the heat. He would run, jump, and chase his tail all day long. One hot day, Spot's mom said, "Spot, you need to bathe. You are very dirty from playing outside." Spot did not like to bathe, but he knew his mom was right. He was very dirty and needed to be clean. Spot's mom filled a tub with water and bubbles. Spot slowly got in the tub and began to wash. He splashed water and played with his rubber duck. Soon, Spot was clean and happy. He said, "Thank you, Mom. I feel much better now."

Lily liked to play baseball with her friends. She had a red hat and a big glove. She could hit the ball very far and run very fast. She was popular in her team. One day, she played against another team. They wore blue hats and had a big dog. The dog was loud and scary. Lily was afraid of the dog. When it was her turn to hit, she saw the dog behind the fence. She heard him bark and growl. She felt nervous and missed the ball. The other team laughed at her. "Come on, Lily, you can do it!" her friend Sam said. He was on the same team as Lily. He was kind and brave. He liked Lily a lot. Lily tried again. She closed her eyes and swung the bat. She heard a loud sound. She opened her eyes and saw the ball fly over the fence. She had hit a home run! She ran around the bases. She was happy and proud. Her team cheered for her. The dog was quiet and sad. Lily smiled at Sam. He smiled back. "You did it, Lily, you are awesome!" Sam said. He gave her a high five. Lily felt warm and fuzzy. She liked Sam a lot, too. They hugged and played some more. They had fun. They were friends.

Once upon a time, there was a girl called Sue. Sue had lots of toys and was always having fun. One day, Sue was feeling a bit unwell, so her mommy took her to see a nurse. The nurse was kind and gentle and told Sue to open her mouth and say 'ahh'. The nurse looked in Sue's mouth and said that she had been eating too much sweet food, and needed to take care of her teeth by brushing them three times a day. She also said that Sue should manage her sweet treats and not eat too many. Sue was a good girl and followed the nurse's advice. She managed her sweets and kept a clean mouth. Sue felt better and her smile was brighter than ever before!

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to play outside. It was cold, so she put on her warm coat. She saw a big tree and decided to look around. Under the tree, Lucy discovered a soft, colorful blanket. "Wow, what a nice blanket!" she said. Lucy picked up the blanket and started to walk back home. On her way, she met her friend, Tom. "Look what I found!" she said, showing him the blanket. Tom was excited and said, "Let's share it!" So they both held the blanket and continued walking. Suddenly, the blanket started to move! Lucy and Tom were surprised. They let go of the blanket, and a small, shivering puppy came out from under it. The puppy was cold and lost. Lucy and Tom decided to take the puppy home and keep it warm. They wrapped the puppy in the blanket and named it Lucky. From that day on, Lucy, Tom, and Lucky were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a man with a big mustache. He loved to care for his mustache every day. He would comb it and make it look nice. One day, the man was eating a big bowl of soup. As he ate, his mustache got into the soup, and it became very messy. The man felt sorry for his mustache. He washed his mustache with water and soap. He combed it again and it looked nice and clean. The man was happy and promised to be more careful when eating soup. His mustache was happy too, and they lived happily ever after.
