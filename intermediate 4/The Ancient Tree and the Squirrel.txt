Category: Intermediate
Title: The Ancient Tree and the Squirrel

Once upon a time, there was an ancient tree in a big, green forest. Many animals lived near the tree. One day, a small bird found a card on the ground. The card had a picture of the ancient tree on it. The small bird showed the card to his friend, a little squirrel. The squirrel said, "Let's follow the picture on the card to find the ancient tree!" So, the bird and the squirrel started to follow the picture on the card. They walked and walked until they saw the ancient tree. They were very happy to find it. The bird said, "Look, squirrel! We found the tree!" The squirrel smiled and said, "Yes, we did it together!" And from that day on, the bird and the squirrel played together near the ancient tree every day.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty dog named Max. Max loved to go to the mall with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. They liked to walk around, look at toys, and eat ice cream. One day, Max and Lily saw a big, red ball in the toy store. Lily said, "Max, let's grab the ball and play!" Max barked happily, and they took the ball outside to play. They had so much fun running and jumping with the ball. After a while, they got tired and sat down to rest. Lily looked at Max and said, "You are a mighty dog, and I love playing with you." Max wagged his tail and gave Lily a big, wet kiss. They knew they would always have fun together, no matter where they went.

Once upon a time, there was a young cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play outside in the sun. One day, Fluffy got very dirty while playing with his friend, a little bird. Fluffy's mom saw how dirty he was and said, "Fluffy, it's time to bathe." Fluffy did not like to bathe. He got angry and ran away to hide. His mom looked for him but could not find him. After a while, Fluffy saw his mom was sad. He did not want his mom to be sad. So, Fluffy went to his mom and said, "I will bathe now." His mom was happy, and Fluffy got clean. They both smiled and hugged each other.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a raven. The raven was not ordinary. He was very big and very smart. One day, the raven met a little girl named Lily. Lily and the raven became good friends. They liked to play and have fun together. One day, while they were playing, the raven said, "Lily, I have a secret. I can turn into anything I want." Lily looked at the raven and said, "Really? Can you show me?" The raven nodded and turned into a big, red ball. Lily was very surprised and happy. They played with the ball all day. The next day, Lily and the raven were playing near the river. Suddenly, a big, scary dog came running towards them. The dog wanted to bite Lily. The raven quickly turned into a big, strong lion. The dog saw the lion and ran away, scared. Lily was safe, and she hugged the raven-lion. "Thank you," she said. The raven turned back into his normal self and smiled. From that day on, Lily and the raven were the best of friends, and they had many more fun and exciting adventures together.

One day, a creative boy named Tim found a big box in his room. Inside the box, there was a suit. It was a very special suit. Tim was very happy and excited. Tim put on the suit. It was a little big, but he didn't mind. He felt like a superhero. He ran around the room, pretending to save the day. Tim's mom saw him and smiled. She knew her creative boy loved the suit. They played together, having lots of fun. And that is how Tim and his special suit had a great day.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his dad. The sun was shining, and the sky was blue. Tim loved to play with his toy tools. He had a toy hammer, a toy saw, and a toy wrench. His dad sat on a bench and watched Tim play. Then, a girl named Sue came to the park too. She saw Tim playing with his toy tools. Sue walked up to Tim and said, "Can you show me your toy tools?" Tim said, "Yes, I can show you. This is my toy hammer, and this is my toy saw." As they were playing, the sky turned dark. Tim and Sue looked up and saw big, gray clouds. They heard a loud noise, and it started to rain. Tim and Sue ran to Tim's dad on the bench. "Dad, it's raining!" Tim said. His dad said, "Don't worry, I have a surprise." Tim's dad took out a big umbrella from his bag. He opened it up, and it was shaped like a tool! Tim and Sue laughed and played under the tool umbrella, even though it was raining. The rain made their day at the park even more fun.
