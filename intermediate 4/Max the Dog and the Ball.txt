Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was very impatient. He always wanted to play and never wanted to stay in one place for too long. One day, Max's mom said she would send him to the park to play. Max was so happy to go to the park. He ran and jumped and played with his ball. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took Max's ball up into the sky. Max was sad and didn't know what to do. Just then, a little bird flew down and gave Max a new ball. Max was so surprised and happy! He played with the new ball and forgot all about the old one. In the end, Max learned that sometimes unexpected things can lead to new fun.

Once there was an obedient 3 year old called Tom. Tom was so obedient that he always did as he was told. One day, Tom's mum told him, "Tom, I'm marrying someone today. Be a good boy and wear your best clothes, ok?" Tom decided to be obedient. He put on his favourite blue and white striped shirt and some smart black pants. Then he stuck out his thumb like he was hitchhiking, and waited. But Tom waited and waited, and no one came. Tom began to get a bit worried. He thought he must have misunderstood his mum. He wanted to go home, but he was still so obedient he wouldn't move till he had to. Then, a huge white limousine drove up! From the window, Tom's mum waved for him to come and get in. "My groom isn't here yet, so let's go pick him up," she said. Tom was SO happy! He had been so obedient, and his mum was marrying someone he didn't know. He was relieved to know it had all worked out!

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a big red boot. One day, he went to the park to play. He saw a pretty flower and wanted to pick it. He bent down to pick the flower, but his big red boot was uncomfortable. Tim saw his friend, Sam, at the park. Sam said, "Hi, Tim! Why do you look sad?" Tim said, "My big red boot is uncomfortable. I want to pick the flower, but I can't." Sam tried to help Tim pick the flower, but the big red boot was still uncomfortable. The sky turned dark and it started to rain. Tim and Sam were sad because they couldn't pick the flower. They went home, wet and unhappy. The big red boot was even more uncomfortable in the rain. Tim wished he didn't wear the big red boot that day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play on the swing in her big yard. One sunny day, Sue went out to play on her swing. She was very happy. As Sue was swinging, she saw a big, fearful dog. The dog came closer to her. Sue was scared, but she didn't want to stop swinging. She held on tight and kept swinging higher and higher. Then, something unexpected happened. The dog brought a bowl of jelly to Sue. The dog was not fearful after all. It just wanted to share the jelly with Sue. Sue was happy and not scared anymore. They both ate the jelly and played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a clever little girl named Amy. Amy liked to ask many questions. She wanted to know about everything. One day, she asked her mom a question about the stars. Her mom did not know the answer. Amy went to her friend, Tom, and asked him the same question. Tom said, "I don't know, but let's find out together!" So, they looked at books and found the answer. They were so happy and proud. One day, Amy did something wrong. Her mom had to punish her. Amy was sad and said, "I'm sorry, Mom." Her mom said, "It's okay. We learn from our mistakes." From that day on, Amy was more careful and kept asking clever questions.
