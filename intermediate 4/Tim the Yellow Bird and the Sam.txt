Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Yellow Bird and the Sam

Once upon a time, there was a little yellow bird named Tim. Tim was an independent bird who liked to fly far away from his nest. One day, while Tim was flying, he met a big blue bird named Sam. "Hi, I'm Sam. Want to play with me?" asked Sam. Tim wanted to play, but he knew he needed to return home soon. "I can play, but not for long," Tim said. Sam agreed, and they played together happily. After a while, Tim knew it was time to return home, but Sam didn't want to stop playing. "Please, just a little more," Sam begged. Tim was sad but said, "No, I must return home now." Sam was upset, but Tim knew it was important to be independent and return to his nest. In the end, Sam understood and they promised to play again another day.

One day, a tired bunny went to the park. He wanted to find food to provide for his family. He hopped and hopped until he saw a sandwich on the ground. The bunny was very happy. He took the sandwich and started to hop back home. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the sandwich away. The bunny was sad and tired, but he did not give up. He hopped and hopped until he found the sandwich again. But this time, the sandwich was in a tree. The bunny jumped and jumped, and he got the sandwich. He was very happy and went home to provide for his family.

One day, a shy dog named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big truck. A man was there. The man was a stranger. The stranger was loading boxes into the truck. Tim watched the stranger load the truck. The stranger saw Tim and smiled. He asked Tim if he wanted to help. Tim wanted to help, but he was shy. The stranger told Tim not to be scared. He said that they were going to have fun. Tim thought about it and decided to help the stranger. Tim helped the stranger load the truck. It was fun. But when they were done, the stranger said it was time to go. Tim was scared. He did not want to go with the stranger. But the stranger picked Tim up and put him in the truck. The truck drove away, and Tim never saw his home again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a great big box of lumber. He loved to build things with his lumber. One day, Tim saw a little girl named Sue who was sad. Tim asked Sue, "Why are you sad?" Sue said, "I have no toys to play with." Tim had a good idea. He said, "Let's use my great big box of lumber to make toys for you!" Tim and Sue worked together to make many toys from the lumber. They had a lot of fun playing with the toys they made. When they were done, Sue was very happy. She said, "Thank you, Tim, for sharing your lumber with me!" The moral of the story is: sharing what you have can make others happy and make you feel good too.

Once upon a time there was a boy who was three years old. He was very naughty, and so his parents always watched him closely. The boy loved making messes and didn't like to clean them up. One day his mom gave him a reward for tidying up his room. She said if he kept it tidy, he'd get a treat. The boy worked hard, but it was so hard to keep tidy. He ended up hating it. The boy decided to make a giant mess in his room. He threw all his toys and books around the room, and his mom got mad. No reward was given that day. Now the boy wasn't allowed to make messes anymore. He was so sad, and now he knows that it is not nice to make such a big mess. But he still hates rewards and tidying up his room. His mom still watches closely to make sure that he keeps it nice and tidy. The bad ending is that he was not able to make his messy messes anymore.
