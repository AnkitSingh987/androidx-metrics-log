Category: Intermediate
Title: The Ugly Bug and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was an ugly bug. He had a band of friends. They were all bugs too. They played together in the garden every day. One day, they saw a big, round thing. They did not know what it was. They wanted to play with it. But then, something unexpected happened. The big, round thing started to roll. It rolled right to the ugly bug. The ugly bug was scared. He did not want to suffer. So, he jumped out of the way. The big, round thing rolled past him. It did not hurt him. The other bugs were happy. They clapped and cheered. The ugly bug was brave. He saved himself and his friends. The big, round thing was not scary anymore. It was a ball. The ugly bug and his band of friends played with the ball. They had fun and were happy.

Tom and Lily love to play with their toy horse. They pretend it is a real horse and they ride it around the yard. They also like to brush its mane and tail and feed it grass and carrots. One day, they see a big truck stop in front of their house. A man gets out and opens the back of the truck. Tom and Lily run to see what is inside. They are very surprised and happy when they see a real horse! "Hello, kids!" the man says. "This is Sunny. He is a very friendly and gentle horse. He is looking for a new home. Do you want to meet him?" Tom and Lily nod eagerly. They love horses and they want to meet Sunny. The man leads Sunny out of the truck and into the yard. Sunny is brown and white and has a shiny coat. He has a long mane and tail and big brown eyes. Tom and Lily approach Sunny slowly and gently. They pet his nose and stroke his neck. Sunny likes them and nuzzles their hands. He also snorts and whinnies softly. "Wow, he is so nice!" Tom says. "He is so pretty!" Lily says. "Do you want to see something cool?" the man asks. "Sunny can do a trick. Watch this." He takes a carrot from his pocket and holds it in front of Sunny. He says, "Sunny, bow!" Sunny lowers his head and bends his front legs. He looks like he is bowing to Tom and Lily. They clap and laugh. "Wow, he can bow!" Tom says. "He is so smart!" Lily says. "Do you want to try?" the man asks. He gives Tom and Lily each a carrot. They hold them in front of Sunny and say, "Sunny, bow!" Sunny bows again and again. He eats the carrots and wags his tail. Tom and Lily hug him and thank him. "You are very welcome," the man says. "You know, Sunny needs a new home. He is too old to work on the farm, but he still loves to play and be with people. Do you think you could take care of him?" Tom and Lily look at each other and then at their parents, who are watching from the porch. They hope they can say yes. They love Sunny and they want him to stay with them. They think he would be very happy in their yard, with their toy horse and their other toys. They think they would be very happy with him, too.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Mary was walking by the sea. She was very small, only three years old. Suddenly, Mary saw something very special on the beach – an anchor! She asked her dad, “What is that, Daddy?” He smiled and said, “That is an anchor, Mary. It is like a big hook. Anchors help ships stay in one place.” Mary loved the anchor so much, she wanted to take it with her. She asked her dad, “Can I take it?” But Daddy said no, it needs to stay here. So Mary let the anchor stay, but she thought of a new way to show her love for the anchor. She decided to make a dance for it! Every day, Mary would come to the beach, open her arms wide, and do a happy dance around the anchor. One day, Daddy asked, “Why do you dance for the anchor?” Mary smiled and said, “It makes me happy, Daddy. Even though I can’t take the anchor away with me, I can show it my love through dancing!” Moral of the story: Even when something is far away from us, we can still show it love in different ways!
