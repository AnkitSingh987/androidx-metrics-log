Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim Finds the Squirrel

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim was playing in his room. Tim was a nosy boy who liked to discover new things. One day, he saw a small box under his bed. He pulled the box out with his arm and opened it. Inside the box, Tim found a little toy robot. The robot looked at Tim and said, "Hi! I am Robo. Let's play together!" Tim was very happy and played with Robo all day long. The next day, Tim took Robo outside to play. While they were playing, a nosy squirrel came and took Robo away. Tim was very sad. He looked for Robo everywhere but could not find him. Then, he heard Robo's voice. Tim followed the voice and discovered that the squirrel had taken Robo to its home. The squirrel was trying to play with Robo too. Tim asked the squirrel, "Can I have Robo back, please?" The squirrel nodded and gave Robo back to Tim. From that day on, Tim, Robo, and the squirrel became good friends and played together every day.

One day, a big pie race was in town. Many people came to see who could eat pie the fastest. The pies were very expensive, but everyone wanted to try. They all loved pie so much. The winner would get a big prize. A little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, wanted to join the race. They did not have money for the expensive pie. Tim was sad, but then he had an idea. He and Spot could find a way to get a pie. The race began and everyone started to eat their pies. Tim and Spot watched from far away. Suddenly, a big wind came and blew all the pies away. The people were sad and surprised. They could not find the pies. But Tim and Spot found a pie on the ground. They were so happy! They ate the pie together, and it was the best pie they ever had. The people saw Tim and Spot eating the pie and cheered for them. Tim and Spot were the winners of the race, even though they did not have money for the expensive pie.

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Spot. Spot loved to run, jump, and play with his friends. One day, Spot saw a big red ball in the yard. He thought it was a toy, but it was really a tomato! Spot wanted to reach the tomato, so he had to stretch his legs very far. He stretched and stretched, and finally, he got the tomato. Spot was so happy, but then he heard a loud "SPLAT!" The tomato had burst all over him, making a big mess. Spot felt sad and went to his friend, the wise old owl. The owl told Spot, "Next time, make sure you know what something is before you play with it." Spot learned a good lesson that day, and he never played with things he didn't know about again.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max loved to work in the park. He would run and play and help the kids. One day, Max saw a zigzag path in the park. He was very alert and wanted to see where it went. So, Max went down the zigzag path. As Max went down the path, he saw a big tree. The tree had a big hole in it. Max was curious and looked inside the hole. He saw a little cat named Luna. Luna was scared and did not want to come out of the hole. Max wanted to help Luna, so he tried to get her out. Max barked and barked, but Luna did not come out. Then, something unexpected happened. A big bird came and took Luna away. Max was sad and went back to the park. He could not help Luna and the kids were gone too. Max felt bad and went home. The day did not end well for Max.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max had a smooth, soft coat and strong legs. He loved to run and play with his friends. One day, Max saw a small cat stuck in a tree. The cat was scared and wanted to get down. Max wanted to help the cat. He tried to lead the cat down the tree by showing her the way. But the cat was too scared to move. Max was sad that he couldn't help his new friend. Then, Max had an idea. He called his friend, a tall giraffe. The giraffe used his long neck to reach the cat and bring her down safely. The cat was happy and thanked Max and the giraffe. Max learned that sometimes, we need help from others to solve problems.
