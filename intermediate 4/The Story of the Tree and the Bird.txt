Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Tree and the Bird

One day, a big tree was ready to talk. It saw a little girl walking by. The tree said, "Hello, little girl! Do you have a question for me?" The little girl was very surprised. She asked, "Can you really talk, tree?" The tree said, "Yes, I can! I can answer any question you have." The little girl thought for a moment. She asked, "Where do birds go when it rains?" The tree was quiet. Then, it said, "I don't know the answer to that question." Just then, a little bird flew down from the tree. The bird said, "I can answer that question! When it rains, we birds hide under the leaves of the tree." The little girl and the tree were both very happy. They learned something new that day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. One day, Lily's mom said, "Lily, we are going on a journey today. We will walk through the open field to the big tree." Lily was very excited. She put on her shoes and went outside with her mom. They walked through the open field, and Lily saw many pretty flowers. She picked some and gave them to her mom. "Thank you, Lily," her mom said. "I value these flowers because you gave them to me with love." When they reached the big tree, Lily and her mom sat down to rest. They talked and laughed under the tree. Lily felt happy to be with her mom on this journey. She knew that spending time together was what made the day so special.

Once upon a time, there was a little yellow dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and run around. One day, Spot found a big ball in his yard. He wanted to play with it, but the ball was very big. Spot tried to push the ball with his nose, but it was hard. He started to struggle. Spot's friend, a small cat named Mittens, saw him struggle with the big ball. Mittens asked, "Spot, do you need help?" Spot said, "Yes, please! This ball is too big for me." So, Mittens and Spot worked together to push the big ball. After some time, they pushed the ball into a new place. The new place was a lab. In the lab, there were many other dogs and cats playing together. Spot and Mittens were happy to find new friends. They played with the big ball and their new friends all day.

Once upon a time there was a family who loved to build. Every day they would build something new and exciting. One day they decided to build a candle. The father grabbed some wax and took his tools to the table. He began to carefully melt the wax and shape it into a tall candle. His daughter watched him with wide eyes. The mother lit the candle and brought it over to the window. The daughter stared at it mesmerized. Suddenly she became very dizzy and the room spun around her. Father quickly grabbed her and held her close. He said: "You were too close to the candle. You must not get too close to the flame again." The daughter nodded and then looked up at the candle with a smile. She said: "Let's build more!"

One day, a little cat named Tim saw a great big sign. The sign had a picture of a cake on it. Tim loved cake, so he wanted to find the cake. He walked and walked, following the sign. Soon, Tim met a big dog named Rex. Rex asked, "What are you doing, Tim?" Tim said, "I am looking for the cake on the sign." Rex wanted cake too, so they went together. As they walked, they saw a tree with a big branch. Rex said, "Watch out, Tim!" But it was too late. The branch went "slap" on Tim's head. Tim was sad, but Rex helped him up. They kept going and found the cake. They shared the cake and were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. Lily was a popular girl who had many friends. She liked to play with her friends all day. She was very happy. One day, Lily lost her toy. She looked for it everywhere. Lily started to weep because she could not find her toy. Her friends saw her cry and wanted to help her. Lily's friends all came to help her look for the toy. They looked under the bed, in the closet, and outside. Then, one friend found the toy behind a big tree. Lily was so happy she could touch her toy again. She thanked her friends and they all played together.
