Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bird of Tim the Boy

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, go to town. The town is very empty. Tim and Spot walk around and look for friends to play with. They see a big tree and sit under it. A little bird comes and says, "Hello, Tim and Spot! Why is the town so empty?" Tim looks at the bird and says, "I don't know. We want to find friends to play with, but there is no one here." Just then, they hear a loud noise. They look up and see a big parade coming! The town was empty because everyone was getting ready for the parade. Tim, Spot, and the little bird join the parade and have lots of fun with their friends.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Max, went to the park to play. It was a quiet day and the sun was shining. Tim loved to run with Max. As they were running, they saw a girl named Sue. Sue had a fun style with a big hat and a colorful dress. She was running too, but she was running away from something! Tim and Max went to help her. "What's wrong, Sue?" asked Tim. "I lost my toy," Sue said. Tim and Max looked around, and then they saw a squirrel holding Sue's toy! The squirrel quickly ran up a tree. It was very unexpected! Tim, Max, and Sue all laughed. They knew they had a new friend in the squirrel.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird who lived with his mother in a small nest. His mother always told him to be careful and not to fly away when she was not around. But the little bird was sometimes careless. One day, the little bird saw a big, bright sun in the sky. He thought it was a good time to escape from the nest and see the world. He did not listen to his mother. He flew away from the nest, not knowing what was waiting for him. As the little bird flew, he saw many things. But he did not see the big cat hiding in the grass. The big cat saw the little bird and wanted to catch him. The little bird tried to fly back to his mother, but it was too late. The big cat caught the careless little bird. And that was the end of the little bird's adventure.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a pair of colorful pants. They were red, blue, and yellow. He liked them very much. He put them on and went outside to play. While playing, Tim saw a girl named Sue. Sue saw Tim's colorful pants and liked them too. She asked Tim a question, "Where did you find those pants?" Tim told her that he found them in his room. Sue wanted colorful pants too, so they went to Tim's house. They looked in his room and found another pair of colorful pants for Sue. Now, they both had colorful pants and were very happy. They played together all day and showed their colorful pants to all their friends.

Once upon a time, in a big town, there lived a beautiful girl named Lily. She liked to wander around the town and look at all the colors and people. One day while wandering, she saw a uniform hanging on a line. The uniform was red and shiny, and Lily thought it was very pretty. As Lily looked at the uniform, a small dog came up to her. The dog said, "Hi, I am Max. That uniform is mine." Lily was surprised to hear the dog talk! She asked, "How can a dog wear a uniform?" Max smiled and said, "I am a special dog. I can wear the uniform, and it makes me feel strong and happy." Lily and Max became friends. One day, they found a big box. They opened the box, and inside was a magic wand! They used the wand to make the town even more beautiful. Everyone in the town was happy and thanked Lily and Max for their help.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with his mom, dad, and his dog, Max. Tim and Max played all day long. They loved to play with balls and run in the yard. One day, Tim noticed that the faucet in the kitchen was weak. The water was not coming out very fast. Tim told his mom about the faucet. She said she would call someone to fix it later. Tim went back outside to play with Max. The next day, Tim and Max were playing outside when it started to rain. The rain got harder and harder. The weak faucet in the kitchen started to leak more and more. The water filled the house, and it was very bad. Tim, his mom, and his dad had to leave their home. They were very sad.
