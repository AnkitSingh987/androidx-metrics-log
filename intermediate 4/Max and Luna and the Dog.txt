Category: Intermediate
Title: Max and Luna and the Dog

One day, a big dog named Max and a little cat named Luna were playing in the yard. They were good friends. Max saw a path and said, "Let's go, Luna! I will lead the way!" Luna looked at the path and thought it might lead to trouble, but she followed Max anyway. As they walked down the path, they saw a big hole. Max said, "Look, Luna! A big hole!" Luna knew that the hole could be trouble, but she didn't say anything. Max jumped over the hole, but Luna was too small to jump. She was scared. Max saw that Luna was scared and came back to help her. He said, "Don't worry, Luna. I will help you." Max helped Luna get over the hole, and they continued down the path. At the end of the path, they found a nice, safe place to play. They played all day and had lots of fun. And after that, they always stayed away from the big hole.

Once upon a time a small boy named Sean was fishing by a river with his dad. He peeked into the shallow water and saw a shiny thing. He wanted his own shiny thing, so he put his hand in the water and tried to grab it. His dad said, "Sean, that is a selfish thing to do. You should always think about others before you think about yourself." Sean felt sad, he really wanted the shiny thing. They talked about the importance of being generous and unselfish. His dad had something special for him: a new fishing rod. Sean was so excited! On the handle was a sparkly thing, just like the one in the river! Sean soon learned that being selfish won't get you very far. He was happy he followed his dad's advice. Always think of others before yourself and put everyone else before you. That is the secret of living in harmony with others.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to play outside, but he was fearful of climbing trees. One Sunday, Max saw a cat stuck in a tree. The cat was sad and scared. Max wanted to help the cat, but he was scared too. He remembered his mom telling him that it's good to help others. Max took a deep breath and started to climb the tree. It was hard, but Max didn't give up. Finally, Max reached the cat and helped it get down. They both were happy and became friends. Max learned that even if something is scary, it's important to be brave and help others.

Tom and Lily are going to the park with Mom. They are very enthusiastic. They want to play on the swings and the slide and the sandbox. They put on their coats and shoes and hats. Mom helps them with their buckles. "Are you ready?" Mom asks. "Yes!" Tom and Lily say. They go to the car. Tom tries to open the door, but he can't. He struggles with the handle. He gets angry. "Mom, help me!" he says. Mom comes and opens the door for him. She smiles. "Sometimes the door is hard to open. You need to pull harder," she says. Tom nods. He gets in the car and sits on his seat. He tries to buckle his seat belt, but he can't. He struggles with the buckle. He gets frustrated. "Mom, help me!" he says. Mom comes and buckles his seat belt for him. She kisses him. "Sometimes the buckle is tricky. You need to push harder," she says. Tom smiles. He says thank you. He looks at Lily. She is already buckled and ready. She is happy. "Let's go to the park!" she says. Mom gets in the car and starts the engine. She drives to the park. Tom and Lily are excited. They can't wait to play. They love the park. They love Mom. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a rich man named Bob. Bob had a big house and many toys. One day, he found a small box near his big house. Bob was very curious, so he decided to examine the box. As he opened the box, he saw a tiny creature with big ears. The creature looked at Bob and said, "I am an ear fairy. I can make your ears bigger or smaller." Bob thought for a moment and said, "I want bigger ears, please!" The ear fairy waved her hands, and suddenly, Bob had big ears. But something unexpected happened! Now, with his big ears, Bob could hear everything, even things far away. He heard people saying mean things about him. They said he was rich but not nice. Bob felt sad and asked the ear fairy to make his ears small again. The fairy did, and Bob learned a lesson. He decided to be a nicer person and share his toys with others. From that day on, Bob became the kindest man in town, and everyone loved him.
