Category: Intermediate
Title: The Cat and Dog and the Box

One day, a cat and a dog went for a walk. They saw a big factory. They were anxious to see what was inside. So, they went in to look around. Inside the factory, they saw a lot of big machines. They heard a noise and saw a big box. The box started to talk! The cat and the dog were very surprised. They had never seen a box talk before. The talking box asked the cat and the dog to help it. The cat and the dog were scared at first, but they wanted to help. They pushed the box out of the factory. When they got outside, the box stopped talking. It was just a toy that someone left on! The cat and the dog laughed and went back to their walk.

Bella and Toby were playing with their blocks. Bella looked over to see Toby making a really tall tower. She picked up her blocks and placed them one by one on top of the tower. Toby grabbed his blocks and tried to add them, too. But his tower was too unsteady and it came crashing down! Toby started to cry. He wished he had put the blocks on more carefully. Bella hugged her friend and said, "It's okay Toby, mistakes happen." Toby nodded, the mild regret still in his eyes. He reached for the blocks and put them together to make a new tower. This time it stayed standing. Toby thanked Bella for her help. They smiled and went back to building their towers.

Once upon a time, there was a mild day. The sun was shining, and the sky was blue. A little girl named Lily wanted to play outside. She put on her shoes, and she went to the garden. In the garden, there were many flowers. Lily saw a small bug. The bug wanted to stay with Lily. So, they played together. They ran and jumped in the garden. They were very happy. Soon, it was time to go home. Lily said bye to the bug. The bug went back to the flowers. Lily went inside her house. She was happy she had a new friend in the garden.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a best friend named Sam. They played together every day. Tim thought Sam was a dependable friend because he was always there to help him. One day, while playing in the park, Tim pinched his thumb on a toy. He cried out, "Ouch! My thumb hurts!" Sam heard him and quickly came over. "What happened, Tim?" Sam asked. Tim showed Sam his hurt thumb. Sam gently held Tim's thumb and said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will help you." Sam took Tim to his mom, who put a bandage on his thumb. Tim felt better and thanked Sam for being a good friend. From that day on, Tim learned to be more careful when playing with toys. He also learned that having a dependable friend like Sam was very important. The moral of the story is to always be a dependable friend and help others when they need it.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy little rabbit named Remy. Remy loved to hop and play all day. One day, while hopping in the forest, he found a small pebble. He thought it was a pretty pebble and wanted to show it to his friends. Remy hopped over to his friends and showed them the pebble. His friends laughed at him and said, "That is just a silly pebble! Why do you like it so much?" Remy felt embarrass and sad. He didn't understand why his friends didn't like his pebble. Remy decided to keep the pebble and not let his friends' words bother him. He knew that the pebble was special to him. Over time, his friends saw how much Remy loved the pebble and they started to like it too. In the end, they all played together and had fun with the pebble.

One day, a little bear named Tim wanted to travel. He told his mom, "I want to go on a journey!" His mom said, "Okay, but be careful. It is icy outside." Tim put on his warm hat and coat. He went outside and started his journey. He saw a big tree and a small pond. He met a bird who said, "Hello! It is icy today. Where are you going?" Tim said, "I am on a journey. I want to travel and see new things." The bird went with Tim on his journey. They saw many things like a big hill and a little house. They played in the snow and had fun. At the end of the day, they went back to Tim's home. Tim's mom was happy to see him. Tim said, "I had a great journey, but now I am tired." They all went inside to get warm and eat.
