Title: Nancy the Girl and the Car

Once there was a little girl named Nancy. One day she went on a big safari with her mom and dad. She was so excited. They saw a tiger, a giraffe and lots of other animals. Nancy and her parents were walking back to the car when it started to rain. They quickly ran for shelter, but it was too late. They were all wet. Nancy said to her parents, "I'm so tired. Can I sleep in the car?" But just then, lightning lit up the sky. Her mom and dad said it was too dangerous, and that they had to go back to the safari and wait for the rain to finish. So Nancy and her parents waited, and waited, and waited... but the rain didn't stop. They all got very cold and very tired, but there was nothing they could do but wait. Finally the rain stopped, but Nancy was too tired to keep on going. She fell asleep and never woke up. She had a bad ending, but she had had a great day on safari.

Once upon a time, there was a white snake. The white snake lived in a big tree. One day, the snake saw a boy with a box of matches. The boy wanted to play with the matches. He tried to match the fire with the wood. The white snake saw this and was scared. The snake tried to stop the boy, but the boy did not listen. The fire grew big and the tree started to burn. The white snake and the boy could not escape. The fire was too big and fast. The white snake and the boy were very sad and scared. The end.

Jeannie was very excited. She was about to put on her new uniform. It was bright and open and it made her feel just like a superhero! Whenever she put her uniform on, she felt brave and strong. Jeannie also had a rocking horse which she loved to ride. She would rock back and forth on it all day long. But today something was different. She could feel the rocking horse moving harder and harder. Suddenly, the rocking horse broke and Jeannie was thrown off. She hit her head and was taken to the hospital. Her uniform was crumpled and dirty and her head was very sore. She was so sad and scared all at once. The doctors said that Jeannie had to stay in the hospital for a while. They said she was going to be alright but she would not be able to ride her rocking horse any more. Jeannie was very sad, but she was thankful to be safe and sound.

Once upon a time, there was a powerful cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with a red ball. One day, Tom found a letter on the floor. The letter had a picture of a big, yellow sun. Tom wanted to show the letter to his best friend, a little dog named Max. But when Tom looked for Max, he could not find him. Max had vanished! Tom was sad and worried about his friend. Tom searched high and low for Max. He looked under the bed, behind the tree, and even in the big, blue box. Finally, Tom found Max hiding in a small, green house. They were so happy to see each other! Tom showed Max the letter with the big, yellow sun, and they played together all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a big hall. Many people were in the hall. It was a busy place. Kids played with toys, and moms and dads talked. One day, a little girl named Mia lost her toy. She was sad. Mia asked her mom for help. Her mom said, "Let's solve this problem together." Mia and her mom looked in the busy hall. They found her toy under a chair. Mia was happy. She hugged her mom and played with her toy. They had fun in the busy hall.

Jack was a brave boy. He was so excited to take the test. The test was hard, but he was sure he could do it. Jack's mom said, "Jack, you can do this! Just remember to go slowly, and if you get stuck, just reverse it." Jack smiled and said, "Okay, Mom". He started and tried his best to answer the questions. He was feeling a bit frustrated, but then he thought of his mom's words. He decided to try reversing the questions. It worked! Jack soon finished the test, feeling warm and proud of himself. His mom was so proud of him! "Good job, Jack! I'm so proud of you!" she said. Jack smiled and said, "Thanks Mom! I'm so glad I reversed it!"
