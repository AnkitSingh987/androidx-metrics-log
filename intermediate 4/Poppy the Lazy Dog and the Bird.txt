Category: Intermediate
Title: Poppy the Lazy Dog and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a lazy dog named Poppy. Poppy loved to sleep all day and play all night. One day, Poppy's friend, a little bird, came to visit. "Poppy, let's play outside!" said the bird. Poppy yawned and said, "Okay, let's go!" They played outside, running and jumping. They got very dirty. Poppy's fur was full of mud. The bird's feathers were messy too. They knew they had to clean themselves. Poppy and the bird went to the pond. They splashed in the water to clean themselves. Poppy shook her wet fur and the bird flapped her wet wings. They both looked clean and shiny again. From that day on, Poppy and the bird played together every day. They still got dirty, but they always cleaned themselves after. And Poppy was not so lazy anymore.

Once upon a time, in a busy little town, there was a boy named Mark. Mark loved spring. The sun was warm, the flowers grew, and the birds sang. Mark liked to play outside all day. One day, Mark saw a little bird. The bird was very busy. It was flying here and there, picking up sticks and leaves. Mark asked the bird, "What are you doing?" The bird replied, "I am making a nest for my eggs. I am a busy bird in spring." Mark wanted to help the bird. He picked up some sticks and leaves too, and gave them to the bird. The bird was happy and said, "Thank you, Mark." Together, Mark and the busy bird made a big, cozy nest. The bird laid her eggs in the nest. Mark visited the bird every day. When the eggs hatched, Mark saw the baby birds. They were so small and cute. Mark and the bird were very happy in the beautiful spring.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play outside. One day, he went for a walk on a trail. He saw a small, nervous bird with a hurt wing. The bird could not fly. Tim wanted to help the bird. He asked his mom to lend him a soft cloth. Tim wrapped the bird in the cloth and took it home. He and his mom took care of the bird until its wing was better. After a few days, the bird could fly again. Tim let the bird go back to the trail. The bird was happy, and Tim was happy too. He learned that it is good to be kind and help others when they need it.

One day, a cat named Tom set out to find food. He was very hungry. Tom saw a big house and went inside. In the house, he found a menu on the table. The menu had pictures of food that looked yummy. Tom saw a scary dog named Max. Max said, "This is my house! You can't eat my food!" Tom was scared, but he was also very hungry. He said, "Please, Max, can we share the food? I am very hungry." Max thought about it and said, "Okay, we can share the food. But you have to be my friend." Tom and Max ate the food together and became friends. They were not scared of each other anymore. They played and had fun in the big house.

Once upon a time there was a tank. It was so long that it reached up to the sky. Every day, the tank would drink and drink and drink. Every night, the tank would get full. The tank was so big that no one knew how it filled up every night. One day, a little girl walked by the tank. She noticed how big it was, and began to wonder how it filled up. She wanted to know the answer. She looked around, and noticed a small pipe at the bottom of the tank. Water was slowly dripping from the pipe. She realized that the water from the pipe was what was filling up the tank. The little girl was so happy to know the answer! From then on, the tank continued drinking and filling up every night. The little girl was so happy to have solved the mystery.
