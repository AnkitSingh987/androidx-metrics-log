Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Bowl

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy a cauliflower for dinner. Tim saw the big, white cauliflower and said, "I want to help pick it, Mom!" His mom smiled and let Tim choose the cauliflower. When they got home, Tim's mom said, "We need to bathe the cauliflower before we eat it." Tim put the cauliflower in a big bowl of water. He was so excited to help that he splashed water all over the floor. "Oh no!" Tim said. "I made a mess. I'm sorry, Mom." Tim's mom looked at him and said, "It's okay, Tim. We all make mistakes. Thank you for saying sorry." She gave him a hug, and they cleaned up the water together. That night, they enjoyed a yummy dinner with the clean cauliflower. Tim was happy he could help, and he knew that saying sorry was important when he made a mistake.

Once there was a pretty girl named Lily. She had a big dog named Max. They loved to play in the park. One day, Lily did not want to do her exercise. She wanted to play with her toys. Max wanted to play too, but Lily said no. Max was sad. Later, Lily felt bad. She regret not playing with Max. So, she put on her shoes and took Max to the park. They ran and played together. Lily and Max were happy again.

One day, a small boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. It was a normal sunny day. Tim liked to play with his ball and run around. At the park, Tim met a girl named Lily. They started to play together. They threw the ball back and forth. They laughed and had fun. Then, Lily's mom called her. "Lily, it's time to go home. We are having steak for dinner," she mentioned. Lily said to Tim, "I have to go now. My mom says we are having steak. Bye!" Tim waved goodbye and went back to playing with his ball.

Once upon a time, in a deep blue sea, there was a little crab named Pinchy. Pinchy loved to pinch things with his big claws. He would pinch rocks, shells, and even his friends sometimes. One day, Pinchy was swimming near a row of pretty shells. He saw his friend, Fishy, swimming by. "Hi, Fishy!" said Pinchy. "Look at these pretty shells! I want to pinch them all!" Fishy laughed and said, "Be careful, Pinchy! You might pinch something you shouldn't!" Pinchy didn't listen to Fishy. He went to the row of shells and started to pinch them. Suddenly, he pinched something hard and heard a loud "Ouch!" Pinchy had pinched a big turtle! The turtle looked at Pinchy and said, "You should be more careful when you pinch things, little crab." Pinchy felt sad and said, "I'm sorry, Mr. Turtle." From that day on, Pinchy learned to be more careful with his pinching.

Once upon a time, in a big, smelly forest, there lived a little cat. The cat liked to be alone and always looked for peace. One day, the cat went for a walk to find a quiet place. As the cat walked, it saw a big box. The cat was very curious. It went close to the box and tried to peek inside. But, the box was too high for the cat to see. Suddenly, the box opened, and out came a tiny bird! The cat was scared at first, but the bird said, "Don't be afraid, I just want to be your friend." The cat and the bird became friends and played together. They both found peace and happiness in their new friendship.

Once upon a time, there was a goose. The goose liked to count. One day, the goose went to a big store. In the store, there were many expensive things. The goose saw a girl. The girl was sad. The goose asked, "Why are you sad?" The girl said, "I want to buy that toy, but it is too expensive. I cannot count how much money I need." The goose said, "I can help you count!" So, the goose and the girl counted the money together. They found out they had enough money for the toy. The girl was very happy. She said, "Thank you, goose!" The goose was happy too, because it could help the girl. They became good friends and counted many things together.
