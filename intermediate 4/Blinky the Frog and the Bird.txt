Category: Intermediate
Title: Blinky the Frog and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a young frog named Blinky. Blinky liked to blink his big eyes all day long. One day, while Blinky was hopping near the pond, he met a new friend. The friend was a small bird named Chirpy. "Hi, I am Chirpy," said the bird. "I like your big eyes that blink!" "Thank you!" said Blinky. "I like your pretty wings. Do you want to play?" "Yes, let's play!" Chirpy said, and they played all day long. While they were playing, Blinky and Chirpy saw a big bottle. The bottle had a word on it. The word was "poison." Blinky and Chirpy did not know what poison was, but they knew it was not good. They decided to stay away from the bottle and play somewhere else. They had a fun day and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Millie. She loved to go out and play in the water. One day, she went to the lake and started to row her boat. Suddenly, she saw an ugly frog on the shore. It was stuck in some weeds and it couldn't move. Millie looked at the frog and said, “Oh, don’t worry! I will rescue you.” Millie carefully reached her hand out and gently pulled the frog out of the weeds. The frog was so happy that it hopped away. Millie smiled and said goodbye to the frog as it hopped away. She watched the frog go and then she went back to rowing her boat.

One day, a boy named Tim went to collect dough from the store. He wanted to make a good pizza for his mom's birthday. On his way home, he met his friend, Sam. "Hi Tim!" said Sam. "What are you doing with that dough?" "I am making a good pizza for my mom," Tim replied. As they were walking, a dog came and took the dough from Tim's hands. Tim and Sam were so surprised! They ran after the dog to get the dough back. The dog was fast, but Tim and Sam were faster. They caught the dog and got the dough back. Tim made a good pizza for his mom, and she was very happy. Tim and Sam learned that even when unexpected things happen, they can still work together and make things right.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a bear. This bear was very attractive. He had soft, shiny fur and a big smile. All the animals in the forest liked him. The bear liked to carry things for his friends. He was very helpful. One day, the bear was carrying a big pile of fruit for his friend, the rabbit. As he walked, he saw a small, weak bird on the ground. The bird looked very sad. The bear decided to help the bird too. He put the fruit down and carried the bird to his nest. The bird was very happy. The other animals saw the bear helping the bird and they all wanted to be like him. So, they all started to help each other too. The forest became a much happier place. The bear had taught everyone a lesson: being helpful and kind is more important than being attractive.

Once upon a time, there was a brave dog named Max. Max had a big boot that he loved to store his toys in. Every day, Max would play with his toys and then put them back in the boot. One sunny day, Max was playing outside with his toys. He was having so much fun that he forgot to put them back in the boot. Max went inside to take a nap, leaving his toys outside. While Max was sleeping, it started to rain. The rain made all of Max's toys wet and dirty. When Max woke up, he saw his toys outside in the rain. Max was sad because he did not store his toys in the boot. Now, his toys were wet, dirty, and not fun to play with anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty king. He had a big castle and many friends. The king liked to spend time with his friends. They would play and laugh all day. One day, the pretty king and his friends went to the forest to play. They played hide and seek. The king was very good at hiding. His friends looked and looked, but they could not find him. Then, something unexpected happened. A small bird found the pretty king hiding behind a tree. The bird said, "I can help you be even better at hiding." The king was surprised, but he said yes. The bird gave the king a magic feather. When the king held the feather, he became invisible! Now, no one could find the pretty king, not even his friends. The king felt sad because he missed playing with his friends. So, he decided to give the magic feather back to the bird. The bird took the feather, and the king became visible again. The pretty king and his friends played together, and they were all very happy.
