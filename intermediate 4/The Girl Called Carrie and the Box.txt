Category: Intermediate
Title: The Girl Called Carrie and the Box

Once upon a time there was a girl called Carrie. She was only a small girl and she was only three years old. Carrie liked to play with a lot of different things and she was always happy. One day she found a box that was very heavy. She asked her Dad what was inside. Her Dad said it was important and that she must accept the weight. She wasn't sure what that meant but she trusted her Dad. So she picked up the box and it was very heavy. She knew it was important so she accepted the weight. Inside the box was a special doll that she had always wanted. She was very happy and hugged the doll tight. From then on she would always accept the weight of what was important to her.

One day, a big sun was shining in the sky. In a small town, there was a fun march. All the people were happy. They were very creative with their clothes. They wore hats with flowers and bright colors. A little girl named Sue went to the march. She saw her friend Tom. Tom said, "Look at my hat! It is very creative, right?" Sue said, "Yes, it is! I like it a lot." They were both very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A big cloud came and covered the sun. The people were sad because the sun was not shining anymore. Sue had an idea. She said, "Let's all be creative and make our own suns!" So, everyone made their own suns with paper and colors. The town was bright and happy again. The moral of the story is to always be creative and find ways to make things better.

Once upon a time, there was a little boat named Bob. Bob lived in a harbor. Every day, he would open his eyes and look at the big blue sea. One day, Bob saw a big fish. The fish said, "Hi, Bob! Do you want to find some yummy food with me?" Bob was excited and said, "Yes, let's go!" Bob and the fish swam far away from the harbor. They found a yummy cake floating on the water. They were so happy and started to eat the cake. But then, the cake opened its eyes! It was not a cake, it was a big sea monster! The sea monster said, "Got you!" Bob and the fish were scared and swam back to the harbor as fast as they could. They were safe, and they never left the harbor again.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty garden. In the garden, there were two children. One child had a big red ball. The other child had a small blue ball. They liked to play with their balls in the pretty garden. One day, the child with the big red ball said, "Let's share our balls." The other child agreed, and they began to play together. They had so much fun sharing their balls in the pretty garden. As they were playing, a big wind came. The wind took the big red ball and the small blue ball high up in the sky. The children were very sad. But then, the wind brought back a new ball. It was a big, pretty, rainbow ball. The children were so happy! They said, "Thank you, wind!" And they continued to play and share the new rainbow ball in the pretty garden.

Jenny and her dad were walking in the park. The sky was blue and the sun was shining. Jenny was happy. Suddenly, a fly flew past. Jenny wanted to follow it. She started to hurry after it. Her dad laughed and hurried with her. The fly flew to the lake in the centre of the park. When Jenny and her dad reached the lake, it was so peaceful. Jenny watched the fly as it flew around the lake and then over some trees. She smiled at her dad and he smiled back. Together, they enjoyed the peaceful moment.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big structure. It was a big house. In the house, there lived a nice, honest man. The man had many friends. They liked to come and play with him. One day, the man saw his friend walking by. He wanted to say hi. So, he went outside to wave at his friend. But, his friend did not see him. The friend kept walking away. The man felt sad. The next day, the man tried again. He saw his friend and went outside to wave. This time, his friend saw him and waved back. They both smiled and played together. The man was happy again.
