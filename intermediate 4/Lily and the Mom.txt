Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Mom

Lily liked to do yoga with her mom. Yoga was when they made shapes with their bodies and breathed slowly. Lily thought yoga was fun and good for her. She wanted to learn more shapes and be like her mom. One day, Lily saw her mom do a mighty shape. Her mom put her hands and feet on the floor and lifted her body up like a bridge. Lily thought it looked amazing. She wanted to try it too. "Mom, can I do that shape?" Lily asked. "Sure, honey. It's called a wheel pose. It's a bit hard, but you can try. Just be careful and listen to your body," her mom said. Lily nodded and got ready. She lay on her back and bent her knees. She put her hands next to her ears and pushed up. She tried to lift her body like her mom, but it was very hard. She felt her arms and legs shake. She could not manage to do it. "Oof, this is too hard, mom. I can't do it," Lily said. "That's okay, honey. You did very well. You don't have to do it if it hurts or feels bad. You can do other shapes that are easier and still good for you. Like this one," her mom said and showed her a different shape. She lay on her back and hugged her knees to her chest. She rocked gently from side to side. Lily smiled and copied her mom. She liked this shape. It felt nice and cozy. She rocked with her mom and felt happy. "Mom, I like this shape. It's called a hug pose, right?" Lily asked. Her mom laughed and kissed her forehead. "Yes, honey. It's called a hug pose. And I love it too. Because I love you," her mom said.

Once upon a time, there was a lively little town. In the town, there was a big theater. The theater was where all the people went to have fun and see shows. One day, a big truck came to the theater. The truck had a load of things for a new show. The people were very excited. They wanted to see the new show. The people in the town helped to load the things from the truck into the theater. They worked together to make the stage look pretty. When the show started, everyone clapped and laughed. The lively town loved their theater and the new show.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. She had a toy named Bunny. Sally and Bunny were best friends. One day, Sally was tired. She wanted to take a nap at noon. Before she went to sleep, she asked Bunny, "Can I borrow your soft pillow?" Bunny said, "Yes, you can borrow my pillow. Sleep well, Sally." So, Sally took Bunny's pillow and went to sleep. While Sally was sleeping, Bunny found a big, round ball. Bunny wanted to play with the ball, but he was too small to push it. Suddenly, a big, friendly dog named Rover came by. Rover saw Bunny and said, "Hi, Bunny! Do you need help?" Bunny said, "Yes, please. I want to play with this ball, but I am too small." Rover smiled and said, "I can help you, Bunny. Let's play together!" Bunny and Rover played with the ball all day. They had so much fun! When Sally woke up, she saw Bunny and Rover playing. She was so happy to see her toy and her dog getting along. Sally joined them, and they all played together until the sun went down.

One day, a clever bird named Bob was flying high in the sky. He saw his friend, the hurricane, far away. Bob wanted to talk to the hurricane, so he flew closer. "Hi, hurricane!" said Bob. "You look sad. Can I help?" The hurricane sighed and said, "I am sad because I am too strong. I scare everyone away." Bob wanted to encourage his friend, so he thought of a good idea. Bob told the hurricane, "Let's play a game! You can blow me high up in the sky, and I will try to fly back down. This way, you can have fun and not scare anyone." The hurricane agreed, and they started to play. Suddenly, the hurricane found out that he could control his strength and not be too scary. Bob and the hurricane became best friends and played together every day.
