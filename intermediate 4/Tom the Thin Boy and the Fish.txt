Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Thin Boy and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a thin boy named Tom. Tom loved to play near the pond. He liked to watch the fish swim and jump. One day, Tom saw a big fish. He wanted to catch it. Tom had a toy that could shoot water. He thought it would be fun to use it on the fish. So, Tom aimed his toy and said, "I will shoot water at the fish!" Tom shot the water at the fish. The fish jumped high in the air. Tom laughed and clapped. The fish swam away, but it was not mad. The fish had fun too. Tom played with the fish every day. They became good friends. Tom learned that even the smallest things, like shooting water, can bring joy and friendship.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a funny animal show. The animals loved to sing, dance, and play. One day, a big, loud opera came to town. The animals wanted to go and see it, but they had to be patient and wait. The day of the opera came, and the animals could not resist. They all went to see the big, loud opera. The animals were so excited, they did not listen to their parents. They forgot to be patient and wait for their parents to come with them. The animals watched the opera and had fun, but when it was time to go home, they were lost. They looked for their parents, but they could not find them. The animals were sad and scared. They learned that they should have been patient and listened to their parents.

Once upon a time, there was a bossy king. He had a big sword. He liked to tell everyone what to do. One day, he met a kind girl. The girl was not scared of the bossy king. The king said, "Serve me some food!" The kind girl said, "Okay, but you must say please." The bossy king was surprised. No one had ever told him to say please before. He thought about it and said, "Please serve me some food." The kind girl smiled and served him some yummy food. They ate together and had fun. The bossy king learned to be nice and say please. He became a good friend to the kind girl. They played with the big sword and had many happy days.

Once upon a time, there was a big nation. In this nation, there were many people who liked to add things. They added rocks to make walls, and they added sticks to make houses. They were very good at adding. One day, they found a big, shiny rock. It was a very expensive rock. They wanted to add it to their nation. So, they picked up the rock and carried it to their village. They showed it to everyone, and everyone was very happy. The people in the nation decided to add the expensive rock to their king's house. They put it in the middle of the room, and it made the house look very nice. The king was very happy, and the people of the nation were happy too. They all lived happily ever after, adding more things to their nation.

Anna liked to make things with paper and glue. She cut out shapes and colors and glued them together. One day, she wanted to make a cloud. She cut out a big white circle and glued cotton balls on it. She thought it looked like a fluffy cloud. She wanted to show her cloud to her mom and dad. She looked for something to attach her cloud to. She saw a string on the floor. She glued one end of the string to her cloud and the other end to a paper clip. She thought she could hang her cloud from the ceiling. She ran to the living room with her cloud. She saw her mom and dad sitting on the couch. She was very happy and proud. She said, "Look, I made a cloud!" She threw the paper clip up to the ceiling, hoping it would stick. But the paper clip did not stick. It fell down and hit her dad on the head. Her dad said, "Ow!" and rubbed his head. Her mom said, "Oh, Anna, what did you do?" Anna looked at her cloud on the floor. She felt very embarrassed. She said, "I'm sorry, I'm sorry, I didn't mean to hurt you." Her mom and dad hugged her and said, "It's okay, Anna, we know you didn't mean to hurt us. Your cloud is very pretty and creative. But next time, be careful with paper clips. They are not good for hanging things." Anna nodded and said, "Okay, I will be careful. Can I still show you my cloud?" Her mom and dad smiled and said, "Of course, we would love to see your cloud." They picked up her cloud and admired it. Anna felt happy again. She said, "Thank you, I love you." They said, "We love you too, Anna. You are our little cloud maker."
