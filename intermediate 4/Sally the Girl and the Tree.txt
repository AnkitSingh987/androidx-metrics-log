Category: Intermediate
Title: Sally the Girl and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a little girl named Sally. She had a big lemon tree in her yard. One day, she picked some lemons to make lemonade. But when she tasted it, the lemonade was very sour. Sally did not like it, but she did not complain. She thought of a way to make it better. Sally decided to have a lemonade contest. She asked her friends to be the judges. They were very excited. Each friend made their own lemonade and brought it to Sally's house. They all sat under the lemon tree and tasted each other's lemonade. Some lemonades were sweet, some were sour, and some were just right. The judges tasted them all and picked the best one. Sally's lemonade was not the winner, but she did not complain. She was happy because she had fun with her friends, and they all learned how to make better lemonade. And from that day on, they enjoyed many sweet and sour lemonades together under the lemon tree.

Once upon a time, there was an enormous tree. This tree was so big that all the kids loved to play under it. One sunny day, Tim and Sue went to play near the tree. While they were playing, they saw a mailbox near the tree. They were very curious about what could be inside. So, Tim and Sue decided to open the mailbox together. When they opened the mailbox, they found a toy inside! It was a toy that could shake and make funny noises. Tim and Sue laughed and played with the shaking toy all day long under the enormous tree. And they had lots of fun!

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play outside. One day, they found a big snowman in the park. He had a green hat, a carrot nose, and a comb in his mouth. "Wow, look at the snowman!" Tom said. "He is so funny and cool!" "Can we touch him?" Lily asked. "Sure, why not?" Tom said. "But be gentle, he might melt." They ran to the snowman and touched his hat, his nose, and his comb. They felt cold and wet. "Ouch, this comb hurts!" Lily said. "Why does he have a comb in his mouth?" "Maybe he likes to brush his teeth," Tom said. "Or maybe he is a barber." They laughed and played with the snowman. They made him a scarf, a belt, and a pair of glasses. They had a lot of fun. But then, the sun came out and shone brightly. The snowman started to melt. His hat fell off, his nose drooped, and his comb slipped out. "Oh no, the snowman is melting!" Tom said. "We have to save him!" "How?" Lily asked. "Maybe we can put him in the shade," Tom said. "Or maybe we can find some ice." They looked around, but they could not find any shade or ice. The snowman melted more and more. He lost his scarf, his belt, and his glasses. He became a small pile of snow. Tom and Lily felt sad. They liked the snowman. He was their friend. They heard a voice behind them. It was the old man who lived near the park. He had a kind face and a long beard. "Hello, children," he said. "Did you enjoy playing with the snowman?" "Yes, we did," Tom and Lily said. "But he melted. We could not save him." The old man smiled. "Don't be sad," he said. "The snowman was happy to play with you. He gave you his hat, his nose, and his comb. He wanted you to have them." He handed them the green hat, the carrot nose, and the comb. "These are for you," he said. "You can keep them as a memory of the snowman. And maybe, when it snows again, you can make another snowman and play with him." Tom and Lily smiled. They thanked the old man. They put on the hat, the nose, and the comb. They looked funny and cool. They learned that snowmen are not forever, but friendship is. They also learned to be grateful for the gifts they receive. They hugged the old man and each other. They were happy.
