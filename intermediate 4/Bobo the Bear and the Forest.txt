Category: Intermediate
Title: Bobo the Bear and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a kind little bear named Bobo. Bobo lived in a deep, dark forest with his best friend, a small bunny named Momo. They loved to play and have fun together. One day, Bobo and Momo were playing hide and seek in the forest. Momo was very good at hiding, but Bobo could not find her. Bobo called out, "Momo, where are you? I give up!" Momo hopped out from behind a big tree and said, "Here I am, Bobo! I was hiding very well!" They both laughed and decided to play a new game. This time, they wanted to play a game where they would remain still and quiet. They called it the "quiet game." As they played the quiet game, Momo started to feel a little tired. She told Bobo, "My back hurts from all the playing. Can you help me?" Bobo knew just what to do. He gently gave Momo a massage on her back to make her feel better. Momo felt so happy and thanked Bobo for being such a good friend. From that day on, Bobo and Momo continued to play and have fun in the deep, dark forest. They always took care of each other and remained the best of friends. And whenever Momo's back hurt, Bobo would give her a gentle massage to make her feel better. They lived happily ever after.

Lily and Ben were very excited to go to the stadium with their dad. They wanted to see the big game and cheer for their team. Dad said they had to be good and stay close to him. When they got to the stadium, they saw many people and cars. Lily and Ben held dad's hand and followed him to the gate. They showed their tickets and went inside. The stadium was very big and loud. Lily and Ben looked around and saw the green field, the bright lights and the colorful flags. Dad found their seats and sat down. He gave Lily and Ben some popcorn and juice. He told them to enjoy the game and not to wander off. Lily and Ben nodded and smiled. They watched the players run and kick the ball. They clapped and shouted when their team scored a goal. Lily and Ben were having fun, but they also wanted to explore the stadium. They saw a big screen, a souvenir shop and a playground. They thought dad would not mind if they went to see them for a little while. They waited until dad was busy talking to another man. Then they got up and sneaked away. Lily and Ben ran around the stadium, looking at everything. They forgot about the game and the time. They did not see the signs that said "Do not enter" or "Danger". They did not hear the sirens or the announcements. They did not notice the smoke or the fire. They only realized something was wrong when they saw a big explosion. They turned around and saw the field in flames. They heard people scream and run. They felt the heat and the shock. They were scared and lost. They tried to find dad, but they could not. They tried to call him, but they could not. They tried to help, but they could not. They were trapped and alone. They wished they had listened to dad. They wished they had stayed with him. They wished they had not been so curious and naughty. They were ashamed and sorry. But it was too late.

One day, a big, scary cat and a small, quick dog saw a toy. They both wanted the toy. They started to fight over the toy. The dog would run fast and grab the toy. Then, the cat would jump high and take it back. The fight went on for a long time. The cat would hiss, and the dog would bark. They were both very upset. They did not want to share the toy. Then, a nice bird flew by and saw the fight. The bird had an idea. It took the toy and flew up high. The cat and the dog stopped fighting and looked up. The bird dropped the toy, and it fell right in the middle of them. The cat and the dog looked at each other and decided to share the toy. They played together and became friends. The scary fight was over, and they were happy.
