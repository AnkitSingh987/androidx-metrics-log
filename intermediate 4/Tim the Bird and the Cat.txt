Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Bird and the Cat

One day, a little bird named Tim was flying high in the sky. He saw a big cat on the ground. The cat saw Tim and wanted to catch him. Tim was scared of the cat. He knew the cat was a threat. Tim went to his friend, a wise old owl. "Please explain to me how to be safe from the cat," Tim asked. The owl thought for a moment and said, "Stay high in the trees, away from the cat. The cat can't reach you there." Tim listened to the owl and stayed high in the trees. The cat tried to catch him, but Tim was too high up. The cat was angry and left. Tim was safe and happy. He thanked the wise old owl for helping him.

One day, a big bear and a little bird went on a picnic. They took a big basket with food. They found a nice spot under a tree. The sun was shining and they were happy. As they sat down to eat, a rude squirrel came by. The squirrel took some food from their basket. The bear said, "Hey, that is not nice!" The squirrel did not care and ran away with the food. The little bird whispered, "Let's play a trick on the squirrel." The big bear smiled and nodded. They put a big, fake bug in the basket. The rude squirrel came back and saw the bug. The squirrel screamed and ran away. The big bear and the little bird laughed and enjoyed the rest of their picnic.

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Spot. Spot loved to have fun and play with his friends. One day, Spot had a great idea. He wanted to have a party with all his friends. Spot knew he needed to tell his friends about the party. He found a big stick and used it to write in the dirt. He wrote, "Come to my party!" Spot was very proud of his writing. He knew his friends would see it and come to play. The day of the party came, and all of Spot's friends showed up. They played games, ate yummy treats, and had lots of fun. Spot was so happy to see his friends having a good time. The party was a big success, and everyone went home tired but happy.

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a little bird. The bird was very ordinary, but it was happy. It liked to fly and sing with its friends. The bird had a lot of fun every day. One day, the bird saw a ball on the ground. It was a small, round ball. The bird wanted to play with the ball. So, the bird tried to kick the ball. It was not easy for the bird, but it tried very hard. The bird kicked the ball and it went far away. The bird felt happy and proud. It flew back to its friends in the big tree. They all sang and played together. The ordinary bird was very happy.

Once upon a time, in a big room with lots of furniture, there was a tall chair. The chair was very happy because it was the tallest thing in the room. It liked to look down at all the other furniture and smile. One day, a new piece of furniture came to the room. It was a very tall shelf. The chair was excited to meet the shelf. They became good friends because they were both tall. They liked to talk about being tall and look at all the other furniture together. The tall chair and the tall shelf were very happy. They played games and had fun with the other furniture. And even though they were the tallest, they learned that all the furniture in the room was special in its own way. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a lemon named Lenny. Lenny was a little round lemon who lived in a big tree with lots of other lemons. Lenny had a best friend named Lucy, who was also a lemon. They liked to play together in the tree every day. One day, they heard a loud noise. It was a big truck coming to pick up the lemons. Lenny and Lucy were scared. They did not want to leave their tree. They held each other tight and gave a big hug. The loud truck took Lenny and Lucy to a new place. It was a big store with lots of other fruits and vegetables. Lenny and Lucy were still scared, but they stayed close and hugged each other. They knew that as long as they were together, everything would be okay.
