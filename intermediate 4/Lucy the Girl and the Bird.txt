Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a yellow mitten that she loved. One day, Lucy lost her mitten. She was very sad. Lucy asked her friend, Tom, "Have you seen my yellow mitten?" Tom said, "No, but let's look for it together." They looked everywhere, but they could not find the mitten. Lucy started to cry. Tom said, "Don't worry, we will find it." The next day, Lucy and Tom saw a bird with something in its beak. It was the yellow mitten! The bird had taken it to make a warm nest. Lucy was happy to find her mitten, but she did not want the bird to suffer in the cold. So, Lucy gave the bird her yellow mitten to keep its nest warm. Lucy and Tom smiled, knowing they had done a good thing.

Lily loved ponies. She had many pony toys and books. She wanted a real pony to ride and hug. But her mom and dad said no. Ponies were too big and too expensive. They lived in a small house with a small yard. There was no room for a pony. One day, Lily saw an ad on TV. It said, "Order now and get a mini pony for only $99.99! Mini ponies are small and cute. They can live in your house and play with you. They are safe and friendly. Call now and get a free pony hat and brush!" Lily ran to the phone and dialed the number. She said, "Hello, I want a mini pony, please. I have $100 in my piggy bank. Can you send me a mini pony today?" The voice on the other end said, "Sorry, kid, this is a prank. There is no such thing as a mini pony. You need to ask your parents before you order anything. Goodbye." Lily was sad and angry. She felt like crying. She threw the phone on the couch and stomped to her room. She hugged her pony toys and said, "You are my real ponies. I don't need a mini pony. You are big enough and nice enough for me."

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a kayak. He loved to play with his kayak in the water. One day, he wanted to measure his kayak to see how big it was. Tim was worried. He did not know how to measure his kayak. He asked his mom for help. His mom showed him how to use a ruler. They measured the kayak together. It was a big kayak! After they measured the kayak, Tim was happy. He was not worried anymore. He played with his kayak in the water all day long. His mom watched and smiled. They had a fun day together.

One day, a little girl named Sue wanted to bake a cake. She asked her mom, "Can I bake a cake, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, but first we need to do a test." Sue and her mom got a fake cake from the toy box. They pretended to bake it in the toy oven. Sue said, "Now the cake is in the oven. It will be ready soon." After the fake cake was done, Sue and her mom pretended to eat it. They both said, "Yum! This cake is so good!" Sue was happy and ready to bake a real cake next time.

Once upon a time, there lived a few friends; Bob, Susie and Alexa. They were playing in the park and having lots of fun. Bob said to Susie, "Let's find something to play with!" but Alexa frowned and said, "No, let's go home!" Bob looked around to see what he could find and spotted a box with an open lid. He saw something soft inside and pointed at the box, "What's this?" Susie looked inside the box, "It feels like it has a really soft texture!" Alexa said, "We should not be behaving like this. We should go home!" The friends knew that Alexa was right, so they decided to go home. But Bob couldn't help but feel excited about the texture of the box - he knew that he would be able to play with it soon!
