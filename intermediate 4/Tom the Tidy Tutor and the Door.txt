Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Tidy Tutor and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a tidy tutor named Tom. Tom liked to help kids learn and think. Tom had a room where he taught the kids. The room was always clean and nice. One day, Tom had a new student named Tim. Tim was a little boy who liked to play and have fun. When Tim came to the room, he said, "Hi, I'm Tim! I want to learn and think too!" Tom was happy to help Tim. As they were learning, a big wind came and blew the door open. All the papers and toys in the room started to fly around. It was a big mess! Tom and Tim were surprised. They looked at each other and laughed. Then they said, "Let's clean up and make the room tidy again!" So, they worked together and made the room nice and clean. And then, they went back to learning and thinking.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to keep her room clean. She had a small desk in her room where she liked to draw and read. One day, Lily's mom said, "Lily, I see that you keep your room very clean. I am happy to see that. I think it is time to increase the size of your desk. You are getting bigger and you need more space." Lily was excited. "Yes, mom! I would love a bigger desk. Thank you!" she said with a big smile. Her mom took her to the store and they picked out a new, bigger desk. When they got home, Lily's mom helped her put the new desk in her room. Lily was very happy with her new, bigger desk. She promised to always keep it clean, just like her old one. And from that day on, Lily enjoyed her clean, bigger desk and had even more fun drawing and reading in her room.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a curious little boy named Tim. One day, Tim saw a funny hat on a shelf. He wanted to know more about the hat. Tim asked his mommy, "Can you explain this hat to me?" His mommy smiled and said, "This is a magic hat. When you wear it, you can imagine anything you want, and it will feel real." Tim was very excited. He put on the hat and started to imagine. He imagined he was a big lion in a jungle. He roared and played all day. When he was tired, he took off the magic hat and went to sleep. The next day, Tim told his friends about the magic hat. They were all curious and wanted to try it. They took turns wearing the hat and imagining fun adventures. From that day on, Tim and his friends played with the magic hat every day. They learned that when they used their imagination, they could have the most amazing adventures.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big billboard. It had a picture of a helpful dog on it. The dog's name was Buddy. Buddy loved to play with his friends. One day, a new friend, a cat named Whiskers, came to the town. Buddy saw Whiskers and said, "Hi! I am Buddy. Do you want to join me and play with my friends?" Whiskers was shy, but she said, "Yes, I would like that." So, Buddy and Whiskers went to play with their friends. They played catch, hide and seek, and tag. Everyone in the town loved the helpful dog and his new friend, the cat. They all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing in the park with his friends. They were having a lot of fun jumping over a small pit they found. Tim was very good at jumping and he wanted to win. He was competitive. Tim said to his friends, "I hope I can jump the farthest!" His friends nodded and they all took turns jumping over the pit. Tim watched as each friend jumped, and he tried to jump even farther. When it was Tim's turn, he ran as fast as he could and jumped as far as he could. He landed on the other side of the pit and his friends cheered. Tim was happy and proud that he won. They all played together and had a great time at the park.
