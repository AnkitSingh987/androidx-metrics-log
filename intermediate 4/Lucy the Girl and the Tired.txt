Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Tired

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy was very tired after a big event at her school. She played with her friends, ate yummy food, and had lots of fun. But now, Lucy needed to rest. One day, Lucy found a small bird with a hurt wing. She wanted to help the bird heal. Lucy took the bird home and gave it food and water. She made a soft bed for the bird to sleep in. Lucy sang songs to the bird to make it happy. After a few days, the bird was not tired anymore. Its wing was healed and it could fly again. Lucy was so happy to see the bird fly. The bird thanked Lucy and flew away. Now, Lucy and the bird were friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird who loved to sing. Every day, the bird would sing a happy song. The bird had a shiny rock that it would polish to make it look nice. One day, the bird went to a dark place to polish its shiny rock. It was very quiet there, and the bird could not sing its happy song. The bird felt sad and missed its friends. The bird stayed in the dark place for too long, and it could not find its way back home. The bird learned that it was not good to go to dark places alone. The moral of the story is to always stay with friends and not go to dark places without help.

Once upon a time, there was a young girl named Sue. Sue had a shiny jewel. She loved her jewel very much. She would show it to all her friends. They all liked to look at it. One day, Sue found an apple. She put the apple next to her jewel. Sue forgot about the apple. The apple began to rot. It got brown and yucky. Sue did not see it. One day, Sue's friend Tim came to play. He saw the rotten apple. "Yuck!" said Tim. "Your apple is bad!" Sue looked at the apple. She saw that it was rotten. She threw the apple away. Sue and Tim played with the jewel. They were happy.

One day, a furry cat went for a walk. The cat saw a rod on the ground. The cat was curious about the rod. It looked at the rod and tried to remove it from the ground. A little boy saw the cat and walked up to it. "Hello, furry cat," said the boy. "Do you need help?" The cat meowed and nodded its head. The boy and the cat worked together to remove the rod. Once the rod was removed, the boy and the cat played with it. They had fun running and jumping with the rod. The furry cat was happy to have a new friend. They played all day until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a red velvet dress that she loved to wear. One day, she went outside to play in the snow. As Amy played, she saw an unknown animal. It was small and white. She went closer to see what it was. "Hello, little animal," Amy said. "I'm Amy. What's your name?" The animal looked at her and said, "I'm Snowy! I can freeze things. Watch!" Snowy touched a tree, and it turned to ice. Amy was amazed. They played together in the snow and became good friends. Amy and Snowy had lots of fun. They made snowballs and threw them at each other. They laughed and played until the sun went down. Then, Amy said, "I have to go home now, Snowy. But I'll come back tomorrow to play." The next day, Amy wore her red velvet dress and went outside to play with Snowy again. They had many more adventures together, and they were always happy in their snowy world.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Liza. Liza loved her mom very much. She loved to give her mom special treats and make her feel comfortable. One day, Liza's mom asked her, "What can I do to make you comfortable, Liza?". Liza excitedly said, "Oh Mama! Please brush my hair. I love when you brush my hair". Liza's mom smiled and said, "Ok, Liza. Let's spoil you with a special brushing session". So, the two of them snuggled up together on the couch, and Mom brushed Liza's hair until it was all smooth and shiny. Liza felt so comfortable and happy. When they were done, Liza smiled and said, "Thank you, Mama! That was the best spoil ever!"
