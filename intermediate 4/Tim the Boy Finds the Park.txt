Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play golf. Every day, he would go to the park and hit the golf ball with his little golf club. One sunny day, Tim met a new friend named Sam at the park. Sam also liked golf. They decided to play golf together. Tim said, "Let's choose who goes first!" Sam said, "Okay, you go first, Tim!" Tim and Sam took turns hitting the golf ball. They had so much fun playing golf together. They laughed and cheered for each other. After they finished playing, they both said, "Let's play golf again tomorrow!" And they did, becoming the best of friends.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They wanted to buy some jelly. Tim liked jelly on his bread. His mom liked jelly too. They looked for the regular jelly that they always get. At the store, Tim saw a man. The man was tall and had a big smile. The man said, "Hi, my name is Mr. Jell. I have a new jelly for you to try." Tim and his mom looked at the new jelly. It was a funny color. Tim's mom said, "No, thank you. We want our regular jelly." Mr. Jell was sad. He said, "Please try my jelly. It is very good." Tim's mom said, "Okay, we will try it." They took the new jelly home. Tim and his mom put the jelly on their bread. They took a big bite. But the new jelly was not good. It was very bad. Tim and his mom did not like it. They wished they had their regular jelly. The new jelly made them feel sick. They had to go to bed early. They never tried new jelly again.

Charlie was always very lucky. He had two dogs, a cat and lots of toys. One day he found something shiny in the ground. It was a silver coin! Charlie was so excited, he just had to show it to his dad. So he grabbed the coin, took it outside and yelled "Look! Look what I found, Dad!" Dad laughed when he saw it. He said, "You're so lucky, Charlie! That silver coin is special - it's worth a lot of money!" Charlie was so happy. He put the coin in his pocket and smiled. He knew he was the luckiest kid ever!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very intelligent for his age. He liked to gaze at the stars at night. One day, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue also liked to look at the stars. One night, Tim and Sue were gazing at the stars together. Tim said, "I can see more stars than you." Sue said, "No, I can see more stars than you." They began to argue about who could see more stars. After a year, Tim and Sue became good friends. They still liked to look at the stars together. But now, they did not argue about who could see more stars. Instead, they enjoyed the stars together and had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little dove named Dolly. Dolly lived in a big tree with her family. She loved to fly and play with her friends. One day, Dolly saw a helpless bug on the ground. The bug was trying to get back to its home, but it could not move. Dolly felt sad for the bug and wanted to help. She said, "Don't worry, little bug! I will help you get home." Dolly picked up the bug and started to fly. But then, a strong wind started to blow. The wind was so strong that it made Dolly strike into the tree. She was scared, but she held on to the bug and did not let it fall. Dolly's friends saw what happened and came to help. They all worked together to get the bug back to its home. The bug was so happy and thanked Dolly and her friends. After that, Dolly and the bug became good friends and always played together.

One day, Mary was playing with her doll. Suddenly, she heard a sound of something falling. She looked around. It was coming from the window. She saw a body falling. Mary was scared. She asked her mom, "Mom, what is that?" Her mom said, "Honey, it looks like someone jumped out of the window." Mary became scared and sad. She started to cry. The police came and started asking questions. They investigated the situation. They identified the body but it was an unknown person. The police concluded that the person had jumped out of the window because of a bad situation. Everyone was sad and scared. The mystery of why someone fell from the window was never solved. Mary was never the same after that day. She couldn't forget the sound of the body falling. She was always scared something bad was going to happen.
