Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Man and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a man named Tom. Tom had a dog. The dog was loyal. The dog's name was Spot. Tom and Spot were best friends. They lived in a small house near the woods. One day, Tom wanted to sell a rod. He took the rod and went to the market. Spot went with him. They walked together. At the market, Tom showed the rod to a man. He said, "I want to sell this rod. Do you want to buy it?" The man looked at the rod and said, "Yes, I want to buy it. How much is it?" Tom told him the price. The man gave Tom the money. Tom was happy. He had sold the rod. Tom and Spot went back home. They played together in the yard. Spot was a loyal friend. Tom was happy to have Spot. They lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily had a tutor named Mr. Fox. Mr. Fox was very good at teaching Lily new things. Lily was a persistent girl, so she always tried her best. One day, while Lily was learning from Mr. Fox, a small bug came into the room. Lily saw the bug and said, "Mr. Fox, look! A bug!" Mr. Fox looked and said, "Yes, Lily, that's a bug. Let's get back to our lesson." But Lily wanted to catch the bug. She tried to pinch it, but it moved away. Lily kept trying to pinch the bug, but it was too fast. Mr. Fox said, "Lily, let's focus on our lesson." Lily said, "But I want to catch the bug!" Just then, something unexpected happened. The bug turned into a fairy! The fairy said, "Lily, I saw how persistent you were trying to catch me. I will give you a wish for trying so hard." Lily wished for more fun lessons with Mr. Fox, and they both lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a big palace, there lived a kind king and queen. They had a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys and run around in the palace garden. The sun shone bright and the flowers smelled sweet. One day, a big, harsh wind came. It blew the toys away from Lily. She was sad and cried. The kind king saw her and said, "Do not cry, my dear. Let us go find your toys together." So, they went to look for the toys in the garden. They searched high and low, and soon found all of Lily's toys. The king said, "Now, let us go back and play in the palace." Lily was happy again, and they played together all day long.

Once upon a time, in a lively little town, there lived a boy named Tom. Tom loved to eat popcorn. He ate popcorn every day. He liked to shake the popcorn bag and listen to the popcorn pop. One sunny day, Tom went to the park to play. He brought his popcorn with him. He sat under a big tree and started to shake the popcorn bag. The popcorn popped loud and fast. Tom was very happy. But then, a big wind came. The wind blew the popcorn bag out of Tom's hand. The popcorn went everywhere. Tom was very sad. He tried to pick up the popcorn, but the wind blew it all away. Tom went home without his popcorn and felt very bad.

Once upon a time, in a lively little town, there lived a mouse named Tim. Tim loved to find things. He found nuts, fruits, and his favorite, cheese. Every day, he went out to find more cheese. One day, Tim met another mouse named Lily. Lily was sad because she could not find any cheese. Tim said, "I will help you find cheese!" Together, they searched and searched. They found a big piece of cheese, but it was high up on a shelf. Tim had an idea. He said, "We can work together to get the cheese!" They climbed on top of each other and reached the cheese. They shared the cheese and were very happy. The other mice in the town saw Tim and Lily working together. They learned that if they helped each other, they could find more cheese too. And so, the lively little town became an even better place for all the mice to live. The moral of the story is that when we work together and help each other, we can achieve more and be happier.
