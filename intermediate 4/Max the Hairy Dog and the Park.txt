Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Hairy Dog and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a hairy dog named Max. Max was very friendly and loved to play with his friends. One day, Max found a magnet in the park. He did not know what it was, but he thought it was special. Max showed the magnet to his best friend, a little girl named Lily. Lily said, "Wow, Max, that is a cool magnet! Welcome to the Magnet Club!" Max wagged his tail and felt happy to be in the Magnet Club with Lily. Together, Max and Lily played with the magnet. They found that it could stick to some things and not to others. They had a lot of fun and laughed a lot. At the end of the day, Max and Lily were tired but happy. They knew they would always remember the day they found the special magnet.

One day, a little bird named Tim was in his nest. He looked down and saw some nice soil. Tim wanted to play with the soil. He was proud of his home in the big tree. Tim flew down to the ground. He started to whistle a happy song. He played with the soil and made a little hill. Tim was having so much fun. Then, a big dog came by. The dog saw Tim and said, "Hi, little bird! What are you doing?" Tim replied, "I'm playing with the soil and whistling a song." The dog smiled and said, "That sounds fun! Can I join you?" Tim was happy and said, "Yes, let's play together!" The dog and Tim played with the soil and whistled their songs. They had a great day. When it was time to go home, Tim flew back to his nest, feeling very proud of his new friend.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a wise teacher. The wise teacher had many pupils. The pupils liked to play and learn. One day, the wise teacher wanted to unite the pupils for a big game. They all held hands and played together. They were very happy. But then, a big rain came. The rain was so big that it made the ground wet and muddy. The pupils slipped and fell. They were sad and went home. The wise teacher was sad too. The big game ended in a bad way.

One day, Timmy and Tilly went to the zoo. Timmy was excited to see the animals, but Tilly was most excited about the seals. They ran up to the seal pool and saw the seals swimming and diving. They were so amazed that they clapped their hands and started to applaud! The seal looked up, as if they were enjoying the applause. After admiring the seals, they wandered over to the giraffe enclosure. The giraffes were so tall, they could barely see them! Timmy and Tilly were so impressed with how tall they were, so they clapped their hands together and applauded the giraffes. The final animal they saw was the monkey. The monkey was very flexible. He could jump and swing high in the air! Timmy and Tilly thought this was incredible. They clapped and applauded and the monkey smiled, like he was enjoying the show! Timmy and Tilly were so happy after their day at the zoo, that they decided to give the seals a big round of applause! The seals smiled and seemed pleased, so they decided to take a closer look. They saw a seal holding something in its mouth–it was a shiny golden ring! Tilly was delighted and gave the seals a huge round of applause as a thank you. One day, a boy named Tim found a rod. The rod was very long. He was glad and wanted to show it to his friend, Sue. Tim ran to Sue's house with the rod. "Look what I found!" Tim said to Sue. "It's a long rod!" Sue was happy too. They played with the rod all day. They used it like a big stick. Then, something unexpected happened. Tim and Sue were playing near a tree. Tim dropped the rod by mistake. The rod hit the tree and made a loud sound. They were both very surprised. A big bird came out of the tree. The bird had the rod in its beak. "Thank you for finding my rod!" the bird said. Tim and Sue could not believe the bird could talk! They were amazed. From that day on, Tim, Sue, and the bird became best friends. They played with the rod and had lots of fun. And they were all very glad.
