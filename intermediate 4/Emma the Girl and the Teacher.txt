Category: Intermediate
Title: Emma the Girl and the Teacher

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Emma. Emma was a good girl who liked to learn. She went to a small school with a big blackboard. One day, Emma's teacher lost her chalk. She asked the class, "Can you help me search for my chalk?" All the kids said, "Yes!" They all looked and looked but could not find it. Then, Emma had an idea. She looked under her chair and found the chalk! She gave it to her teacher with a big smile. The teacher said, "Good job, Emma!" Everyone clapped, and they all had a fun day learning at the blackboard.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to eat cherries. One day, she saw a big cherry tree in her yard. She wanted to pick the cherries, but her mom said, "No, Amy, you must be obedient and not pick the cherries." Amy did not listen to her mom. She started to complain. "But I want the cherries," she said. Her mom told her again to be obedient, but Amy did not listen. Amy climbed the cherry tree to pick the cherries. She tried to grab one, but she fell down from the tree. She cried and cried. Her mom came to help her, but Amy was sad because she did not listen to her mom. The end.

One day, it rained hard outside. The sky was full of fat, grey rain clouds. Mummy said it was healthy to go outside in the rain. So Jack put on his coat, grabbed an umbrella and stepped outside. First, Jack splashed in all the big puddles. Then he chased after the raindrops that ran down the window. He loved watching them build into bigger and bigger streams. Next, Jack tried counting the raindrops in the air. He said to himself, “Two raindrops, four raindrops, six raindrops… this is like math!” After a while, Jack decided the rain was too much and ran back inside. Mummy smiled and said that staying outside in the rain was healthy. Jack thought about it - he had to agree! It was a lot of fun, and Jack felt very happy by the end.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly boy named Tim. Tim loved pizza more than anything in the world. One day, Tim's mom made a big pizza for dinner. Tim was so happy, he clapped his hands and jumped up and down. "Mom, can I help feed the dog?" asked Tim. His mom said, "Sure, Tim. But don't give the dog any pizza. It's not good for him." Tim nodded and took some dog food to feed the dog. After feeding the dog, Tim sat down at the table. He ate his pizza with a big smile on his face. "Yummy!" said Tim. His mom laughed and said, "I'm glad you like it, Tim." They both ate their pizza and had a fun time together.

Once upon a time, there was a kind farmer who lived on a farm. He had many animals. One day, he went to wash his hands at the well. While washing, he saw a small, lost bird. The bird was sad and dirty. The kind farmer said, "Oh no, little bird! You need a wash, too." He picked up the bird and washed it with care. The bird was happy and clean. The farmer and the bird became good friends. One day, the farmer forgot to close the farm gate. The animals ran away and got lost. The bird tried to help, but it was too small. The farmer was very sad. He looked for his animals, but he could not find them. The farm was empty and quiet. The kind farmer and the bird were left all alone.

Tom and Sam were brothers who liked to go to the lake with their dad. Their dad was a fisherman who caught many fish and sold them at the market. He always brought some fish home for dinner, and Tom and Sam loved to eat them. One day, their dad said he had to go to a faraway lake where there were more fish. He said he would be back in a week, and gave Tom and Sam a hug and a kiss. He also gave them a cheap toy boat that he bought at the store. He said they could play with it at the lake while he was gone. Tom and Sam were sad to see their dad go, but they decided to have fun with the toy boat. They went to the lake every day and sailed the boat on the water. They pretended they were fishermen like their dad, and caught imaginary fish. They also talked to their dad in their minds, and told him they missed him a lot. But one day, a big storm came and the wind blew very hard. Tom and Sam were scared and ran to the shore, but they forgot to take the toy boat with them. The toy boat was swept away by the waves, and they could not see it anymore. They cried and cried, and wished their dad was there to comfort them. They waited and waited for their dad to come back, but he never did. They learned from the news that his boat had sunk in the faraway lake, and he had drowned. They were very sad and angry, and blamed the cheap toy boat for taking their dad away. They hated the toy boat, and wished they had never seen it. They missed their dad more than anything, and wished they could see him again.
