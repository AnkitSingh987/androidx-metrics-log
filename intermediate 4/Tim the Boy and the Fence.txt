Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Fence

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play in his backyard. One day, he saw a big, anxious dog near the fence. The dog looked sad and scared. Tim wanted to help the dog, so he asked, "Why are you sad?" The dog didn't reply. Tim tried again, "Do you want to play?" This time, the dog replied with a soft bark. Tim was happy and opened the gate to let the dog in. They played in the backyard for a while. But then, the dog ran away and took Tim's favorite toy with him. Tim was very sad and cried. He learned that he should be careful when he talks to strangers, even if they look sad or scared.

Once upon a time, there was an enormous tree. It was so big that all the kids loved to play under it. One sunny day, a little boy named Tim found a roll of tape near the tree. Tim had an idea. He wanted to use the tape to make a fun game for his friends. He taped some sticks to the tree, making a ladder. Tim's friends saw the ladder and came to play. As they climbed the tree, one of the sticks broke, and Tim's friend fell. He started to cry. Tim felt sad and hugged his friend. They all decided to play a safer game on the ground. And they all played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little potato. It lived in a garden with many other plants. The potato was weak and small, but it wanted to grow big and strong like the other plants. One day, the sun came out and shined on the garden. The potato felt the warmth and started to grow. It grew a little bit each day. It was so happy to be growing, just like the other plants. After many days, the potato grew bigger and stronger. It was no longer weak. The potato was now a big and strong potato, just like it always wanted to be. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She loved to play with her toys every day. Her favorite toy was a small, soft, and cuddly cat named Tuna. Mia and Tuna would play together all day long, having so much fun. One day, Mia's mommy took her to a big store with lots of toys. Mia saw a big, shiny toy car. It was very expensive, but Mia wanted it so much. Her mommy said, "No, Mia, we can't buy that toy car. It costs too much money." Mia felt sad, but she knew her mommy was right. She went back home and played with Tuna. As they played, Mia realized that she didn't need the expensive toy car. She was very happy playing with her best friend, Tuna, who was always there for her.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Emma. She had just turned three. She was very happy. Today, she was playing in her garden. Suddenly, she heard a loud noise. It was coming from her house. She was curious, so she entered. Inside, she saw a strange man. He was wearing a smelly perfume. Emma didn't like it. She thought it was very loud. She was scared and ran away. Fortunately, her dad entered the house just in time. He saw the man and chased him away. Emma was very relieved. She was happy that her dad had saved her and her house was safe again. The end.

One sunny day, a little fish named Fin wanted to swim in the big yard. He was very brave and wanted to show his friends how fast he could swim. He told his best friend, Sam the snail, "I am going to swim in the big yard today!" Sam the snail said, "You are so brave, Fin! I will watch you swim fast." So, Fin and Sam went to the big yard. All their friends were there to see Fin swim. Fin jumped into the water and started to swim very fast. All his friends clapped and cheered for him. Fin felt so happy and proud. When he was done swimming, Sam said, "You did great, Fin! You are the bravest fish I know!" And all their friends agreed.
