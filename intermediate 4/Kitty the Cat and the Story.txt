Category: Intermediate
Title: Kitty the Cat and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a playful cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to get her favorite toy, a little red laser. She would chase it all around the house, trying to catch the red dot. One day, Kitty saw the red laser on the wall. She jumped and tried to catch it. "Meow! I can't get it!" she cried. Her friend, Bunny, heard her and hopped over. "Kitty, why are you sad?" asked Bunny. Kitty told Bunny about the red laser. Bunny thought for a moment and said, "Kitty, maybe you can't get the red dot, but we can play together and have fun!" So, Kitty and Bunny played with the laser together, laughing and having a great time. In the end, Kitty learned that playing with friends is more important than getting the red dot. She was happy to have Bunny by her side. And they lived happily ever after. The moral of the story is: it's more important to have fun with friends than to always get what you want.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a rake. He liked to use the rake to help his mom in the garden. He would rake the leaves and make big piles. Tim's mom was very happy with his help. One day, Tim saw a spoiled apple on the ground. He wanted to pick it up with his rake. But his mom did not permit him to do that. She said, "No, Tim. The apple is not good. It is spoiled. You can't touch it with your rake." Tim listened to his mom. He left the spoiled apple alone and continued to rake the leaves. His mom was proud of him for listening. They worked together in the garden and had a fun day.

One day, a little dog named Max went to the park to play. Max loved to play in the deep, soft grass. He liked to sit and watch the birds fly high in the sky. Max saw a little girl named Lily sitting on a bench. She looked sad. Max walked over to her and said, "Why are you sad? Do you want to play?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, let's play!" Max and Lily ran and played in the deep grass. They played tag and hide-and-seek. They laughed and had lots of fun. After a while, Max and Lily were tired. They sat down on the bench and watched the birds. Max was happy he made a new friend, and Lily was happy too. They promised to meet at the park again to play.

Lily liked to design things. She used paper, crayons, scissors and glue to make pictures and shapes. She liked to show her designs to her mom and dad. They always said she was very clever and creative. One day, Lily wanted to design something new. She looked around the kitchen and saw a big bowl of radishes. They were red and white and round. Lily thought they looked like balls. She had an idea. She took some radishes and a knife and asked her mom for help. Mom helped Lily cut the radishes in half. Then Lily used a spoon to scoop out some of the white part. She made holes and faces on the radishes. She put them on a plate and showed them to her mom. "Look, mom, I designed radish dolls!" Lily said. Mom smiled and hugged Lily. "They are very cute, Lily. You are very good at designing. But you know what? Radishes are also good for eating. They are crunchy and healthy. Do you want to try one?" Lily looked at her radish dolls. She liked them very much. She did not want to eat them. She thought they would taste bad. She shook her head. "No, thank you, mom. I don't like radishes. They are bad. I only like them for designing."

One day there was a quiet stove in the kitchen. Daddy came in and wanted to make some food. He filled a pot with water and carefully placed it on top of the stove. Then he lit a match. Suddenly, there was a loud boom! The stove was angry and started to shoot flames. Daddy quickly jumped back and blew out the fire. But the stove was still angry. Smoke started to pour out from the top and fill the kitchen. Mommy and Daddy quickly opened the windows and drew all the curtains. At last, the smoke stopped pouring and it was quiet again. Daddy put on some oven mitts and lifted the pot off the stove. He put it in the sink and doused it with cold water. The next day everyone was happy. The stove was safe, Daddy had made some food, and everyone laughed and smiled.
