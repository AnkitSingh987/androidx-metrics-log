Category: Intermediate
Title: The Clumsy Bird and the Grass

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy little bird. He was always flying into trees and into the ground, it was very funny to watch! One day the little bird was very tired and decided to take a nap. He flew around until he found a nice, quiet spot near a well. When he nestled down in the grass he quickly fell into a deep sleep. Soon he heard loud music and singing coming from the well. When he opened his eyes he realized that the well was full of happy little birds singing and playing. They were having so much fun! The little bird joined in, and soon the little birds were all singing and laughing together. It was a beautiful day, and the clumsy little bird was so glad he had decided to take a nap.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to walk to the park. He was eager to play with his friends. He put on his shoes and went outside. On his way, he met a big dog. The dog said, "Woof! I want to play too!" Tim was happy and said, "Come with me, dog. Let's walk to the park together!" At the park, Tim and the dog played in the right order. First, they played with a ball. Then, they went on the slide. Last, they played on the swings. They had a lot of fun and were very tired. They walked back home, eager to play again the next day.

Once there was a girl named Susie who wanted a new toy. She asked her mom, but her Mom said no. Susie was so envious when she saw one of her friends with a new toy. She asked her friend, “Where did you get it?” But her friend didn’t answer her question. Susie asked her mom again if she could get the same toy. Her mom looked at her and said, “Not today. Shut the door.” Susie was feeling so envious that she started to cry. She asked her mom why she couldn’t have the toy. Her mom told her that she needed to save money and sometimes it’s important to wait. Susie didn’t want to wait, so she asked her dad. He looked at her and said, “No, let’s shut the door on this question for now. We can talk about it later.” Susie was still feeling envious, but she understood and she shut the door. She knew that she had to be patient and save her money. In the end, she did get her toy and was very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a tall princess named Lily. She lived in a big castle with her mom and dad. She had many toys, but she felt lonely because she had no friends to play with. One day, while walking in the garden, she saw a little girl crying. The princess asked, "Why do you feel sad?" The little girl said, "I lost my toy, and I can't find it." Princess Lily felt sorry for her and decided to help her find the toy. They looked everywhere and finally found the toy under a bush. The little girl was so happy, and she thanked Princess Lily. They became best friends and played together every day. The princess felt happy because she learned that helping others can make new friends and bring happiness.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy mole and a fan. The mole was always curious and wanted to know what was happening in the world. So one day, the mole decided to peep around the fan. The fan, a bit annoyed, told the mole firmly to go away. But the mole just wouldn't listen. So the fan decided to swing its leaves and blow them hard against the mole. The mole was shocked and ran away. From then on, the fan and the mole kept their distance and never came too close. The moral of the story is that being too nosy can bring harm. So it is always better to do one's own job and not bother anyone else.

Once upon a time, there was a tall seal named Sam. Sam lived near the big sea with his friends. They loved to play together and swim in the water. One day, Sam saw a man with a big box. The man was trying to record the sounds of the sea. Sam was curious and asked the man, "What are you doing?" The man replied, "I am recording the sounds of the sea to share with others who can't come here." Sam thought it was a good idea and wanted to help. He asked his seal friends to sing their best songs while the man recorded them. The man was very happy and thanked Sam and his friends. The man shared the sounds with many people who couldn't come to the sea. Everyone loved the songs of Sam and his friends. The moral of the story is that when we help others, we can bring joy to many people.
