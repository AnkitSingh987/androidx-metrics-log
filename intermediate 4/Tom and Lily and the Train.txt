Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Lily and the Train

Tom and Lily were playing with their toys in the living room. Tom had a colorful train that made noises and moved on a wire. Lily had a doll that she liked to dress up and comb. Tom wanted to play with Lily's doll, but Lily said no. She said it was her doll and she did not want to share. Tom was angry. He thought Lily was mean and selfish. He decided to ignore her and play with his train. He made the train go faster and faster on the wire. He did not look at Lily or talk to her. He pretended she was not there. Lily felt sad. She wanted to play with Tom and his train, but he did not want to play with her. She tried to talk to him and ask him to share, but he did not listen. He ignored her and made the train go louder and louder. She felt lonely and bored. Then, something bad happened. Tom was so busy playing with his train that he did not see the wire was tangled and twisted. The wire snapped and the train stopped working. Tom was shocked and upset. He tried to fix the wire, but he could not. He cried and cried. Lily heard Tom crying and saw his broken train. She felt sorry for him. She put down her doll and went to him. She hugged him and said she was sorry. She said she would help him fix the wire. She said they could play together and share their toys. Tom stopped crying and looked at Lily. He felt ashamed and sorry. He said he was sorry for ignoring her and being mean. He said he would share his doll and play with her. He said they could be friends and have fun. Lily smiled and nodded. She said they could be friends and have fun. They worked together to fix the wire and the train. They played together and shared their toys. They were happy and kind. The moral of the story is: Do not ignore your friends and be selfish. Share your toys and play together. You will have more fun and be happier.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy called Mr. Ugly. Mr. Ugly was not pretty, but Tim loved him very much. They liked to play near the fireplace where it was warm and cozy. One day, Tim and Mr. Ugly were playing by the fireplace. Tim said, "Mr. Ugly, let's kneel down and pretend we are camping." So, they knelt down and pretended to sit by a campfire. They told stories and had fun together. Suddenly, Tim's mom came into the room. She saw them playing and said, "Tim, you and Mr. Ugly look so happy by the fireplace!" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, mom! We are having a great time!" Tim, Mr. Ugly, and his mom spent the rest of the day playing and laughing together by the warm fireplace.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy park. In the park, there was a big tree. Under the tree, a cat and a dog lived. One day, the cat said to the dog, "Let's play in the park when it is not dark." The dog agreed, "Yes, let's play when the sun is up." So, every day, the cat and the dog played in the park. They had fun and were happy. When it was dark, they went under the tree to sleep. The tidy park was their favorite place to be.

Once upon a time, there was a famous jet. It flew so fast, and everwhere it went. One day, the famous jet was flying and decided to go on an adventure. Suddenly, right before it's eyes, there was a huge balloon. The balloon wanted to follow the jet, so it chased it! The balloon soared through the sky, and the jet was going faster and faster. The jet was getting scared, and it knew it had to lose the balloon. Just then, the jet made a daring turn and went so fast the balloon couldn't follow it anymore. The jet was so relieved and flew away, never to be followed again. The end.
