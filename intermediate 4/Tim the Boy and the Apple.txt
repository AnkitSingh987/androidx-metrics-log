Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Apple

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was cold and began to shiver. He saw an igloo and went inside to get warm. Inside the igloo, Tim found a tasty treat. It was a big, red apple. Tim took a bite and smiled. The apple was sweet and juicy. He was happy to find something yummy to eat. After eating the tasty apple, Tim felt warm and cozy. He stopped shivering and played in the igloo. Tim had a fun day and went home to tell his mom and dad about his adventure.

---

One day, a little rabbit named Remy went for a walk in the wood. He was very good at jumping. Remy liked to jump over logs and rocks. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. As Remy walked, he saw a fragile, little bird named Bella. Bella had a hurt wing and could not fly. Remy asked, "What happened, Bella?" Bella said, "I fell from my nest and hurt my wing. Can you help me get back home?" Remy wanted to help Bella. He said, "Jump on my back, and I will take you to your nest." Bella jumped on Remy's back, and they went through the wood together. Remy jumped over logs and rocks with Bella on his back. They finally found Bella's nest. Bella said, "Thank you, Remy. You are a good friend." Remy smiled and said, "You're welcome, Bella. I am happy to help." Remy and Bella became best friends, and they played in the wood every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to play in the park. One day, Max saw a fancy trumpet on the ground. He picked it up and started to run. Max met his friend, a big cat named Tom. Tom said, "Wow, Max! What a fancy trumpet you have!" Max barked, "I found it in the park. Let's play with it!" Max tried to play the trumpet, but he couldn't. He tried to bite it, but it was too hard. Tom laughed and said, "Silly Max, you have to blow, not bite!" Max tried to blow, and made a loud sound. They both laughed and played with the fancy trumpet all day long.

Once, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play games. One day, he went to a big fair with his mom and dad. There were many fun games to play. Tim saw a game where he could win a prize. He wanted to try it. The game had a big tub. Tim had to fill the tub with water using a small cup. If he filled the tub, he would win a prize. Tim was very excited. He started to fill the tub with water. His mom and dad cheered for him. But then, a bad wind came. It blew the water out of the tub. Tim was sad. He tried again and again, but the bad wind kept blowing the water away. Tim did not give up. He kept trying until the wind stopped. Finally, he filled the tub and won a prize. Tim was very happy, and his mom and dad were proud of him.

Once upon a time, there was a big meeting in the forest. All the animals came to talk. They sat under an ancient tree. The tree was very old and wise. At the meeting, the animals talked about the rain. They needed rain to pour on the flowers and trees. The flowers and trees were thirsty. They wanted to drink water. Just then, a big cloud came. It was a surprise! The cloud poured rain on the forest. All the animals got wet. The flowers and trees were happy. And the ancient tree smiled because it knew the rain was coming.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park with her mom. The sun was shining and the ground was dry. Sue was very happy because she could play without getting dirty. She saw a tree with small round things on it. Her mom told her they were olives. Sue asked her mom, "Can I eat one?" Her mom said, "Yes, but only if you share with me!" Sue picked a big, green olive and gave half to her mom. They both took a bite and found it to be delicious. Sue's friend, Tim, came to the park too. Tim saw Sue and her mom eating olives and wanted to try one. Sue shared an olive with Tim. He said, "Wow, this is so yummy!" They all laughed and played together, eating olives and enjoying the sunny day at the park.
