Category: Intermediate
Title: The Factory and the Man

Once upon a time, there was a big factory. In the factory, they made tasty treats. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the factory with his mom. Inside the factory, Tim saw a man with a frown. The man said, "I am sad because I cannot make the treats tasty enough." Tim wanted to help the man. He thought and thought. Then, Tim had an idea. He said, "Why not try some of mom's tasty jam?" The man agreed and they put the jam in the treats. Everyone loved the new tasty treats and the man's frown turned into a big smile.

There was a dull road in a small town. A boy named Tim lived there. He had a friend named Sally. They liked to play on the road. One day, Tim and Sally had a race on the road. Tim wanted to win, but Sally was very fast. They ran and ran, but Tim could not catch up to Sally. Tim felt sad. Sally saw that Tim was sad. She stopped and said, "Let's race again, but this time we can help each other." Tim thought about it and decided to accept her help. They raced again, but this time they ran together. They were both happy and had fun on the dull road.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a fragile drum that he loved to play. One day, he decided to go to the park with his drum. At the park, Tim saw his friend, Sue. Sue had a toy car. Tim asked, "Can I play with your car?" Sue said, "Yes, if I can play your drum." Tim was scared his drum would break, but he decided to let Sue play it. Sue played the drum gently, and it did not break. Tim played with the car and had fun too. They laughed and played together all day. Tim learned that sharing was fun, and his fragile drum was safe with friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to read. Every day, she would sit under a big tree and read her favorite book. One day, while she was reading, she saw a tiny, spiky hedgehog. The hedgehog had a spike on its back. Amy said, "Hello, little hedgehog! What is your name?" The hedgehog said, "Hello, Amy! My name is Spike!" Amy and Spike became good friends. They played together every day. Spike was very persistent. He never gave up when they played games. One day, Amy asked Spike, "Do you want to read with me?" Spike said, "Yes, I would love to!" So, Amy and Spike sat under the big tree and read the book together. They had a lot of fun. From that day on, Amy and Spike read together every day, and they were very happy.

Lily and Tom are playing with blocks. They want to make a big tower. They put many blocks on top of each other. But the tower falls down. They try again and again. But the tower always falls down. "Tom, we need something to hold the blocks together," Lily says. "I have an idea," Tom says. He runs to his room and comes back with a wire. "Look, this is a wire. It is long and thin and clear. We can use it to tie the blocks." Lily and Tom wrap the wire around the blocks. They make a loop and twist the ends. The wire is strong and tight. The blocks do not fall down. "Wow, it works!" Lily says. "Our tower is very high and stable. Thank you, Tom. You are very smart." "You are welcome, Lily. You are very kind. I like playing with you," Tom says. They smile and hug. They are happy. They have made a big tower with blocks and wire.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the store with her mom. They wanted to buy a pretty ornament for their tree. Sue saw an amazing ornament. It was a shiny star. She said, "Mom, can we get this one?" Her mom said, "Yes, we can order it." They asked the store man to order the amazing ornament for them. Sue was very happy. When the ornament came, Sue and her mom put it on their tree. The amazing ornament made the tree look so pretty. Sue loved the shiny star ornament. She said, "Our tree is the best!"
