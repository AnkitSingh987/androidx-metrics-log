Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Jerry and the Garden

Once upon a time there were two dolls, Tom and Jerry. Tom liked to play in the garden and have fun. But Jerry didn't like that. He wanted to build a structure in the garden. Tom disagreed. He thought it was more fun to play than to build. He tried to explain this to Jerry, but Jerry wouldn't listen. Finally, Jerry said, "We will have to decide who is right. Let's ask the harmless bunny rabbit in the garden!" So they asked the bunny, who said, "Building a structure is a nice idea! But it's also nice to play. So I think it's okay for you to do both." Tom and Jerry were happy. They agreed with the bunny and made a plan to both play and build in the garden. Tom and Jerry were friends again and everyone was happy!

One day, a big fish and a small fish were swimming in the deep blue sea. The big fish could speak a special language that the small fish did not know. The big fish would say, "Bloop bloop bloop!" and the small fish would just look at him, not understanding. The small fish wanted to learn the big fish's language, so he asked, "How can I speak like you?" The big fish smiled and said, "Just try! Say 'Bloop bloop bloop!' with me." The small fish tried his best, but he could only say, "Blip blip blip!" The big fish laughed and said, "That's okay, keep trying!" One day, as they were swimming, they saw a big shark coming towards them. The big fish said, "Bloop bloop bloop!" to warn the small fish. But the small fish did not understand. Just as the shark was about to eat the small fish, the big fish yelled, "Stop!" The small fish understood and quickly swam away with the big fish. The shark was surprised and did not chase them. The small fish was so happy that the big fish had used a word he could understand. They both swam away, safe and sound, and the big fish promised to teach the small fish more words that they both could use. From that day on, the big fish and the small fish spoke a new language together. They made new friends in the deep blue sea and always helped each other when they were in trouble. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was an adorable spider. He loved to roll around in the sunshine and have fun. Everyday, he would explore the meadow and have exciting adventures. One day, he discovered a big, long hill and decided to roll down it for the first time. He quickly tumbled down and his furry legs made him go faster and faster until he came to a stop. The spider was so happy he wanted to do it again! He raced back up the hill and rolled down, again and again. His eight tiny legs were spinning through the air and he felt so much joy. Soon, all of his spider friends asked to join him and together they rolled down and up the hill for hours. They giggled and laughed, their eight little legs waving in the air. They were so happy and exhilarated! At the end of the day, they all agreed it had been the most fun day ever. The little, adorable spider was so proud of himself for discovering the big, long hill. He knew it was an adventure he and his friends would never forget.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a large tree with many big branches. Tim wanted to find his way to the top of the tree. He thought it would be fun to see the whole world from up there. Tim met a girl named Sue. "Can you tell me the way to the top of the tree?" he asked her. Sue said, "Yes, I can help you. But you must be careful. It is not safe to climb the tree." Tim did not listen to Sue. He wanted to reach the top of the tree. He started to climb the large tree. Sue watched Tim as he went higher and higher. She was scared for him. Tim got to the top of the tree. He was very happy. But then, he lost his balance and fell down. Sue saw him fall and cried. Tim was hurt very badly. He should have listened to Sue and not climbed the tree.
