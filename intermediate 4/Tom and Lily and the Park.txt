Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Lily and the Park

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They saw a big tree with a long trunk. Tom wanted to climb the trunk and see the birds. Lily was scared. "Come on, Lily, it is easy. You just hold on and go up," Tom said. "No, Tom, I do not want to. It is too high. I might fall and hurt myself," Lily said. Tom did not listen to Lily. He refused to stay with her. He climbed the trunk by himself. He was happy to see the birds and the sky. But then, he heard a loud crack. The trunk was old and weak. It broke under his weight. Tom fell down with a thud. He cried out in pain. Lily ran to him. She saw blood on his head and his arm. She was very scared. She called for help. But no one was near. They were alone in the park. Tom and Lily wished they had stayed together. They wished they had not climbed the trunk. They learned a hard lesson.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play in the big yard by his house. One day, while he was playing, he saw a long vine hanging from a tree. Tim thought the vine looked fine and strong. He wanted to swing on it. Tim asked his mom if he could swing on the vine. His mom looked at the vine and nodded. She said, "Yes, you can swing on the vine, but be careful." Tim was very happy and started to swing on the vine. As Tim was swinging, something unexpected happened. The vine turned into a big, friendly snake! The snake said, "Hello, Tim. I am a magic snake. If you are nice to me, I will be your friend and help you." Tim was surprised but happy to have a new friend. From that day on, Tim and the magic snake played together in the big yard and had lots of fun.

One day, a girl named Lucy went for a walk. She felt very lucky because it was a sunny day. On her walk, she saw a big, pretty rock. She wanted to touch it. "Can I touch the rock?" Lucy asked her mom. Her mom said, "Yes, Lucy, you can touch the rock." So, Lucy touched the rock. Suddenly, the rock started to move! The rock was not a rock at all! It was a big, friendly turtle! The turtle said, "Hello, Lucy! Thank you for waking me up!" Lucy was very surprised, but she was happy to have a new friend. They played together and had lots of fun on their lucky walk.

One day, Jimmy and his parents were walking around the neighbourhood. Suddenly, Jimmy saw a very disgusting place. It was full of dirt and smelly. Jimmy was very unhappy about it. Jimmy shouted, "That place is so disgusting! We need to do something about it." His parents thought for a moment. Then his father suggested, "Why don't we get some gloves, some brushes and some buckets, then we can clean it up? We could make it look much better. What do you think, Jimmy?" Jimmy thought for a moment. He liked the idea. He nodded and said, "Yes! Let's go to the store and get the things we need." Jimmy, his mother and father all put on the gloves and grabbed the brushes and the buckets. They all worked together to clean up the disgusting place. When they finished, the place looked much better. Jimmy was happy. He smiled and said, "Now this place is so much better!" His parents smiled. They were proud of Jimmy and his hard work in helping to make the place clean and beautiful again.

One day, a big bull lived on a farm. The bull was strong and liked to play. But the bull was sometimes careless when he played. He did not look where he was going. A little girl named Lucy lived near the farm. She liked to peek through the fence at the bull. One day, Lucy saw the bull playing near the fence. She wanted to say hello, but she was scared. The bull was too big and too strong. Lucy's mom saw her peeking at the bull. She told Lucy to be careful and not get too close. Lucy listened to her mom and stayed away from the fence. The bull kept playing, but he did not hurt Lucy. The moral value of the story is to be careful and listen to your parents.
