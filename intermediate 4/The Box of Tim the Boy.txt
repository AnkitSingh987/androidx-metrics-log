Category: Intermediate
Title: The Box of Tim the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to order different toys every week. He had a big box where he kept all his toys. One day, Tim ordered a new toy that he really wanted. The next week, a big box came to Tim's house. Tim was very happy and excited. He opened the box to see his new toy. But instead of a toy, there was a big cake inside the box! Tim was very surprised. Tim's mom saw the cake and started to laugh. She said, "Tim, I ordered a cake for your birthday, but I think they sent it to you by mistake!" Tim laughed too, and they all ate the big cake together. And the next day, Tim's new toy came in the mail.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Lily. She loved to play with her friends and read books. One sunny day, Lily and her friend Tom were playing near the river. They liked to throw stones in the water and watch the fish swim. As they played, Lily's book got wet. She was sad and said, "Tom, my book is wet. What should I do?" Tom thought for a moment and said, "Let's put it in the sun to dry." So they put the book under the warm sun and waited. While they waited, they talked about the words in the book. Lily said, "I like the word 'love' because it makes me happy." Tom said, "I like the word 'friend' because it means you and me." After a while, the book was dry and looked like new. Lily and Tom were happy and learned that talking about words can make them feel good. The moral of the story is that sharing words and feelings can bring friends closer together, even when something unexpected happens.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a big, red ball. One day, Tim wanted to polish his ball to make it shiny. He asked his mom for help. "Mom, can you help me polish my ball?" Tim said. His mom nodded and they started to polish the ball together. As they polished the ball, Tim's mom told him a story. "When I was a little girl, I did something stupid," she said. "I tried to polish a tip of a pencil. It did not work, and I got very sad." Tim listened and thought about what his mom said. After they finished polishing the ball, Tim saw his pencils. He remembered what his mom told him about the pencil tip. Tim knew it was a stupid idea, so he did not try it. Instead, he played with his shiny, red ball and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a lively little girl named Lucy. She loved to play and run outside. Lucy had a favorite toy, a big, soft, blue rock. She took it everywhere she went. One day, Lucy found a small, red mug in the kitchen. She thought it was so pretty. She wanted to play with it and her rock. So, Lucy put the rock inside the mug and started to rock it back and forth. The rock made a funny sound inside the mug as it moved. Lucy played with her rock and mug all day. She rocked it fast, and she rocked it slow. She even took it outside to show her friends. They all laughed and played with the rock and mug too. At the end of the day, Lucy was happy and tired. She put her rock and mug away, ready for another fun day tomorrow.

Once upon a time, there was a happy dove. The dove liked to fly high in the sky. It was an enthusiastic bird. It loved to sing and dance in the air. One day, the dove saw a big tree. The tree did not permit birds to sit on it. The dove did not know this. So, it sat on the tree. Suddenly, the tree began to talk! The tree said, "You cannot sit on me." The dove was very surprised. It did not know that trees could talk. The tree said, "I am a magic tree. I will let you sit on me if you sing a nice song." The dove sang a beautiful song. The tree was happy and let the dove sit on it. From that day on, the tree and the dove became good friends.
