Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna and the Dog

Anna liked to hop. She hopped in the park, she hopped in the garden, she hopped in the house. She hopped with her friends, she hopped with her toys, she hopped with her dog. She hopped when she was happy, she hopped when she was sad, she hopped when she was bored. One day, Anna found a pair of sunglasses in her mom's closet. They were big and shiny and made everything look different. Anna put them on and hopped around the room. She felt like a star. She hopped to the window and looked outside. She saw a big hill in the distant. She wanted to hop there. She asked her mom if she could go to the hill. Her mom said yes, but she had to take her dog and be careful. Anna was excited. She put on her hat and her shoes and her sunglasses. She took her dog and hopped out the door. She hopped along the street, she hopped across the bridge, she hopped through the field. She hopped and hopped and hopped. She reached the hill and looked around. She saw the park, the garden, the house. She saw her friends, her toys, her mom. She saw everything with her sunglasses. She felt happy. She hopped on the hill, she hopped with her dog, she hopped with joy. She hopped until the sun went down. Then she hopped back home. She hugged her mom and told her about her adventure. She took off her sunglasses and put them in her mom's closet. She went to bed and dreamed of hopping. She liked to hop.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. He loved to play outside with his toys. His toys were his equipment. Tom always stayed outside to play and be healthy. One day, Tom stayed outside to play with his equipment. He saw a big, green frog. The frog said, "Hello, Tom!" Tom was very surprised. Frogs can't talk! The frog was special. The frog told Tom a secret. The frog was really a prince! The prince needed help to be a human again. Tom and the frog worked together. They found a magic flower. The prince turned back into a human. Tom and the prince stayed friends and played with the equipment every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Anna. Anna loved to play sport. Her favorite sport was to kick a big ball with her friends. Every day, Anna felt excited to play with her friends in the park. One day, Anna saw a little bird stuck in a tree. The bird looked sad and scared. Anna wanted to help the bird. She thought, "I will rescue the bird and make it happy again." Anna climbed the tree and got the bird. She was very careful. Anna brought the bird down and set it free. The bird was so happy, it sang a pretty song. Anna felt excited that she could rescue the bird. She went back to play sport with her friends, feeling like a hero.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a wiggly tooth. It moved a little bit every day. Sue was worried about her tooth. One day, Sue asked her mom, "Mom, will my tooth fall out?" Her mom answered, "Yes, Sue, it will. But don't worry, a new tooth will grow in its place." The next day, Sue was playing outside in the light sun. She saw a butterfly and tried to catch it. She ran and ran, and then she tripped on a rock. Sue fell down, and her wiggly tooth came out! Sue showed her mom the tooth, and her mom said, "Well done, Sue! Now put the tooth under your pillow tonight, and the tooth fairy will come." That night, Sue put her tooth under her pillow and went to sleep. When she woke up, she found a shiny coin where her tooth had been. Sue was happy, and she couldn't wait for her new tooth to grow.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy who got bored a lot. He liked to play and explore, so everyday he would walk around looking for fun stuff to do. One day, he walked by a tall building. He thought it looked really cool and wanted to explore it. He counted the windows on the building to see how many there were. He counted one, two, three, four and so on and he kept counting until he had reached one hundred! But in the end, he got bored! All that counting was a bit too boring and he wanted to have some fun. So, instead of counting windows, he decided to go outside and start playing. The moral lesson is that it's important to try new things, and if something isn't fun, don't do it!
