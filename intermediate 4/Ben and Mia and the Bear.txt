Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben and Mia and the Bear

Ben and Mia like to play games. They have many games in their room. Some games are big, some games are small. Some games have cards, some games have dice. Some games are fun, some games are hard. One day, Ben and Mia find a new game in their room. It is a big box with many holes. Inside the box, there are many animals. The animals are soft and furry. The animals have noses and ears and tails. The game is called Poke-a-Mole. Ben and Mia want to play Poke-a-Mole. They read the rules. The rules are easy. They have to poke the animals when they pop out of the holes. They have to be fast and careful. They have to count how many animals they poke. The one who pokes the most animals wins. Ben and Mia start to play Poke-a-Mole. They take turns to poke the animals. They laugh and shout. They poke the moles, the rabbits, the squirrels, the mice. They poke the fox, the skunk, the raccoon, the badger. They poke the hedgehog, the porcupine, the otter, the beaver. But they do not poke the bear. The bear is big and scary. The bear is angry and grumpy. The bear is filthy and smelly. The bear does not like to be poked. The bear growls and roars. The bear pops out of the hole and chases Ben and Mia. Ben and Mia scream and run. They drop the game and hide under the bed. They do not play Poke-a-Mole anymore. They play a different game. A safer game. A cleaner game.

Once upon a time, there was a great big tree. This tree was very old and had many leaves. One day, a young boy named Youth came to play near the tree. Youth liked to climb and swing on the branches. He had so much fun! One day, Youth saw a broken branch on the tree. He felt sad. He wanted to help the tree. Youth had an idea! He would replace the broken branch with a new one. He found a strong stick and tied it to the tree where the broken branch was. The tree was very happy. It felt great again! Youth and the tree became best friends. They played together every day. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn little boy named Tim. Tim did not like to listen to his mom and dad. He always wanted to do things his own way. One day, Tim's mom asked him a question. She said, "Tim, do you want to become a big, strong boy?" Tim said, "Yes, I do!" His mom said, "Then you need to eat your vegetables and listen to your mom and dad." But Tim did not want to eat his vegetables. He wanted to eat candy and cookies all day. His mom and dad were sad because they knew that eating too much candy and cookies was not good for him. Tim's mom and dad tried to make him eat his vegetables, but he was very stubborn. He said, "No, I don't want to!" So, they had a big problem. In the end, Tim's mom and dad found a way to make the vegetables fun to eat. They made a game out of it, and Tim started eating his vegetables. He became a big, strong boy, and his mom and dad were very happy.

Once upon a time there was a giant. He was very wealthy. The giant had a lot of money and many nice things. One day the giant decided to cover his wealth in glitter. He wanted to make it look extra special. He worked hard to cover everything with gliter. When he was finished, it sparkled and gleamed in the sunlight. The giant was so happy with his work. He loved the way it looked and couldn't wait to show it off. He called some of his friends over to see his glittery wealth. They were so impressed and wished they could do the same. The giant was glad that he had chosen to cover his wealth in glitter. It made it look even more special and wonderful. And from then on, it was known as the giant's glittery wealth. The End.
