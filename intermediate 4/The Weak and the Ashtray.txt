Category: Intermediate
Title: The Weak and the Ashtray

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a weak, old man. The man had an ashtray, and he liked it very much. He put the ashtray on a table by his chair. Every day, he would look at the ashtray and smile. One day, a big wind came and blew the ashtray off the table. The weak, old man did not know what to do. He was sad and wanted his ashtray back. He tried to pick it up, but he was too weak. A little girl saw the weak, old man and wanted to help. She picked up the ashtray and gave it to him. The man was very happy. He said, "Thank you for helping me. Now I know that I have a friend." The little girl smiled, and they became good friends.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a man with a whip. The man was making big, fun shapes in the air. Tim wanted to try it too. His mom said, "You can try, but promise to be careful." Tim took the whip and tried to make shapes like the man. But, he did it wrong and the whip hit a warm pie that a lady was holding. The pie fell on the ground. The lady was sad and angry. She said, "You made my pie fall! Now I have no pie to eat." Tim felt bad and wanted to fix it. He had an idea. He went home and made a new pie with his mom. They brought the warm pie back to the lady. She was surprised and happy. She said, "Thank you for the new pie! You are a kind boy." Tim learned to be more careful and they all enjoyed the pie together.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Emily and John. They were playing in the park. John said, “Let’s add more fun to our game.” Emily said, “What kind of fun?” John replied, “I have an idea. I brought a bag of wet plastic. Let’s find something to do with it.” The two friends searched and found a tree with a strong branch. John said, “Let’s tie the wet plastic around the branch.” So they tied the wet plastic around the branch and then stepped back and looked around. The wet plastic was flapping in the wind making a funny sound. John said, “Let’s add some more plastic!” So they added more wet plastic and the wind made it move and dance. Emily and John laughed with joy. Emily said, “This is lots of fun!” John agreed, “Yep, adding wet plastic made it more fun!”

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing with her toy automobile. She loved her toy car because it was red and shiny. Lily's mom told her to be careful because it was fragile. Lily's friend, Tim, came over to play. He saw the red automobile and said, "I want to play with it too!" Lily didn't want to share, but her mom insisted. "You must share with Tim," her mom said. They played together, but then something unexpected happened. Tim accidentally stepped on the automobile and it broke! Lily was sad, but Tim said, "Don't worry, I can fix it!" He put the pieces back together and the car was like new again. Lily and Tim played with the automobile and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, a huge armchair lived in a big room. It was so big that people thought it was a house! One day, a little girl named Sue came to the room. She saw the huge armchair and wanted to sit on it. She said, "Hello, huge armchair! Can I sit on you?" The huge armchair said, "Yes, Sue, you can sit on me. But first, you have to open my big pocket." Sue was surprised that the armchair could talk! She opened the big pocket and found a tiny key inside. The armchair told her, "Now, put the key in the small hole on my side and turn it." Sue did what the armchair said. She put the key in the small hole and turned it. Suddenly, the huge armchair opened up and there was a secret room inside! Sue was so happy and excited. She went inside the secret room and found many toys and games to play with. Sue and the huge armchair became best friends and they played together every day.
