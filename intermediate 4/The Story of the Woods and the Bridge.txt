Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Woods and the Bridge

Once upon a time, there was a boy who wanted to go for an adventure. He decided to go to the woods. He was excited. But when he stepped into the woods, his shoe sank into the mud. He tried to pull it out but the mud was too thick and deep. He cried out: "Help! I can't get my shoe out!" Just then his friend came along. His friend took a look at the mud and said: "Oh no! That mud looks deep. We'll have to find a way to get your shoe out!" The boy thought for a moment, then said: "I know! Let's use sticks and stones to make a bridge so I can cross over the mud!" So the boy and his friend started to collect sticks and stones. With their modest materials, they managed to bridge the mud and the boy was able to cross over. He thanked his friend and went back home, carrying the mud-covered shoe with him. Moral of the story: Stay modest and you'll find the right solution to any problem.

Once upon a time, a little red car and a big blue car had a race. They wanted to see who was the fastest. The race was very long, and it started yesterday. The little red car went speed, speed, speed! The big blue car went speed, speed, speed, too! They both wanted to win the race. But the little red car was sad because the big blue car was faster. In the end, the big blue car won the race. The little red car was upset, but the big blue car said, "Don't be sad, little red car. We can race again, and maybe you will win next time!" The little red car smiled and they both went speed, speed, speed, back home.

One morning, a little boy named Tim was getting ready for the day. He put on his red shirt and blue pants. He was excited because today, he would go to the park with his mom. Tim knew something fun would happen at the park. At the park, Tim saw a big, yellow ball. He wanted to play with it. He asked the girl who had the ball, "Can I play too?" The girl said, "Yes, let's play together!" They played with the ball, and Tim was very happy. After playing for a long time, Tim said, "Thank you for playing with me." The girl smiled and said, "You're welcome! Let's play again soon." Tim went home with his mom, feeling happy about his fun day at the park.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Rose. Rose was three years old and liked to explore. One afternoon, while playing in the park, Rose stumbled across an old purse lying on the ground. She picked it up and opened it to find a surprise inside. It was full of money! Rose was scared because she had never seen so much money before. Suddenly, she heard a voice behind her. "Hey! That's my purse!" It was a tall man. Rose got even more scared. "Ah, sorry," Rose said as she handed over the purse. The man looked relieved. "It's okay, little one," he said. "Just don't go picking up things you find lying around. You never know what could be inside!" Rose nodded, thanked the man for his kindness, and ran off to tell her mom all about her exciting discovery. She vowed never to pick up things she found in the park again.

Once upon a time, there was a large cat named Tom. Tom liked to play hide and seek with his friend, a small mouse named Jerry. They played in a big house with many windows. One day, Tom was hiding from Jerry. He found a window and hid behind it. He thought Jerry would never find him there. While hiding, Tom saw something unexpected outside the window. It was a big, colorful bird! Tom wanted to catch the bird, so he jumped out of the window. Jerry saw Tom and followed him outside. They both tried to catch the big bird, but it was too fast for them. The bird flew away, and Tom and Jerry were sad. But then, they saw the bird had left a big box of toys for them! Tom and Jerry were so happy. They forgot about catching the bird and played with their new toys together. From that day on, they became best friends and always played together.
