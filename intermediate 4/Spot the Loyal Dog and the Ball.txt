Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Loyal Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Spot. He loved his family very much. One day, his family went on a picnic. They took a big basket full of yummy food. Spot was happy to go with them. While they ate, Spot saw a ball. He wanted to play. He picked up the ball and ran to his family. But they didn't want to play. They wanted to eat. So, Spot went to find someone else to play with. Spot found a girl named Sue. She was sad because she lost her ball. Spot gave her the ball he found. It was a match! Sue was happy again. They played together and had fun. The family saw how kind Spot was and they played with him too. The moral of the story is that being kind and loyal can make everyone happy.

One day, a red boat with a big sail went out to the sea. A little boy named Tim and his dad were on the boat. They were happy and excited to be on the water. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. As they sailed, they saw a big fish jump out of the water. Tim was so excited and said, "Dad, look at that big fish!" But then, the wind stopped, and the sail fell down. The boat stopped moving. Tim and his dad started to panic. They did not know what to do. Just then, a friendly dolphin swam up to their red boat. The dolphin said, "Don't panic! I can help you!" The dolphin pushed the boat with its nose, and the wind started to blow again. The sail went up, and the red boat started to move. Tim and his dad were so happy and thanked the dolphin. They sailed back home, and they never forgot their new dolphin friend.

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. He saw a big red mailbox near his house. Tim was very impatient. He wanted to find something fun to do. Tim saw a butterfly flying around the mailbox. He thought, "I want to catch it!" So, Tim tried to catch the butterfly with his hands. He ran and jumped, but the butterfly flew away. Tim's friend, Sue, came over to play. She said, "Let's catch the butterfly together!" They both tried to catch it, but it was too fast. They laughed and had fun trying. At the end of the day, they were tired but happy. They promised to play together again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to walk and sing. One day, Amy found a microphone in her toy box. She was very happy. Amy took the microphone and walked outside. It was a hot day. She saw her friend, Tom. "Hi Tom!" said Amy, "Look at my microphone! Let's sing together!" Tom smiled and said, "Okay!" Amy and Tom walked and sang in the hot sun. They had lots of fun. They sang loud and clear. Everyone could hear them. Their moms and dads clapped their hands. Amy and Tom were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big, comfy armchair. It was in a room with a clear window. A little boy named Timmy loved to sit in the armchair and look outside. He liked to watch the birds fly and the cars go by. Timmy was always happy when he sat in the armchair. One day, Timmy saw a big box outside. He got very excited and ran out to see what it was. Timmy opened the box and found a very nice toy. He played with the toy all day and forgot about the armchair. Later, when Timmy went back inside, he felt sad. He saw the armchair and knew he missed it. Timmy felt regret for not sitting in the armchair that day. He put the toy down and went back to the armchair. But when he sat down, he felt something under him. It was the toy! The toy was hiding in the armchair all along. Timmy was so surprised and happy. Now, he could play with the toy and sit in the armchair at the same time.
