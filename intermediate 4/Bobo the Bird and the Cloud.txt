Category: Intermediate
Title: Bobo the Bird and the Cloud

Once upon a time, there was a chubby little bird named Bobo. Bobo loved to fly high in the sky and play with his friends. One day, while flying, Bobo saw a big cloud that looked like a fluffy pillow. Bobo called his friend Momo, "Hey Momo, look at that big cloud! Let's go play on it!" Momo agreed, and they both flew to the cloud. When they got there, they saw that the cloud was dirty. Bobo and Momo felt sad because they couldn't play on the dirty cloud. Bobo had an idea, "Let's clean the cloud, Momo!" So, they both worked together to clean the cloud. After they cleaned the cloud, it was white and fluffy again. Bobo and Momo played on the cloud for hours, laughing and having fun. Later, they went back home and told their friends about the cloud. "We cleaned the cloud and made it nice to play on," said Bobo. All their friends were happy and proud of them. The moral of the story is: when we work together and help each other, we can make things better for everyone.

Once upon a time, two famous friends were having fun in a big park. One of the friends picked up a pastel and said, "I can lift this!" The other friend said, "I can lift it too!" The first friend then said, "Let's see who can lift it higher!" So the friends both put their hands on the pastel and began to lift. They lifted as high as they could, until the pastel began to squeak. They both laughed as they dropped the pastel. They decided to have a race to see who could reach it first. The first friend won the race and said, "I'm famous for lifting high!" The friends had a great day playing together and lifting the pastel high in the air. They laughed and smiled the whole time. It was a day they would never forget!

Once upon a time, there was a big tank. The tank was very strong and fierce. It lived in a land with many trees and animals. The tank liked to go fast and make loud noises. The animals were scared of the tank, but the tank just wanted to play. One day, a little bird came to the tank and said, "Please, Mr. Tank, can you be quiet? My friends are scared of your loud noise." The tank felt sad and said, "I am sorry, little bird. I will try to be more quiet." The tank started to yield and go slow, so it would not scare the animals. The animals saw that the tank was being nice and wanted to be friends. They all played together and had a fun time. The tank was happy to have friends and learned to be gentle with them.

Once upon a time, in a peaceful forest, there lived a little bunny named Binky. Binky loved to joke and play with his friends. They would hop and laugh all day long. One sunny day, Binky and his friend, a tiny squirrel named Squeaky, were playing near the big tree. They were very hungry. "Let's find a snack," said Binky. "Yes, a snack!" Squeaky agreed. They looked around and found some yummy nuts and berries. As they ate their snack, Binky told a funny joke. "Why did the squirrel cross the road?" he asked. Squeaky thought for a moment and then giggled, "I don't know, Binky. Why?" Binky laughed, "To get to the other nut!" Squeaky laughed so hard, he almost dropped his berry. After their snack, Binky and Squeaky felt happy and full. They continued to play and joke in the peaceful forest until the sun went down. Then, they said goodbye and went to their cozy homes to sleep, dreaming of more fun days with their friends.

One day, a graceful cat named Mindy was walking near a big tree. She saw a little bird up in the tree. The bird was singing a sweet song. Mindy liked the song and wanted to be friends with the bird. Mindy called to the bird, "Hi, bird! I like your song. Can we be friends?" The bird looked down and said, "Yes, Mindy. We can be friends. Come up here and sing with me." Mindy was happy and tried to climb the tree. But she could not pass the first branch. Just then, a big wind came and shook the tree. The bird fell from the tree and landed on the ground. Mindy was scared for her new friend. But then, something unexpected happened. The bird got up and started to dance. Mindy and the bird danced and played all day. They were happy to be friends. From that day on, they met near the big tree every day to sing, dance, and play together.
