Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Farm

Once upon a time, there was a brave little girl named Sue. Sue loved to discover new things. One day, she went for a walk and found a farm. On the farm, Sue saw a cow. The cow said, "Moo!" Sue asked, "Do you like living on the farm?" The cow said, "Yes, I love it here!" Sue was happy that she discovered the farm. She knew she was brave for talking to the cow. Sue went home and told her mom about the farm and the cow. Her mom was proud of her brave little girl.

Once upon a time, there was a graceful bird named Mark. Mark loved to fly high in the sky. He liked to watch the seasons change. In the warm season, he saw green trees and pretty flowers. In the cold season, he saw white snow and ice. One day, while Mark was flying, he saw a big, red ball in the sky. He thought it was a new season. Mark was very excited. He wanted to tell his friends about the new season. So, he flew down to find his friends. Mark found his friends and told them about the big, red ball. They all wanted to see it too. They flew up to the sky together. When they got close to the big, red ball, they saw that it was not a new season. It was a big, red balloon! They all laughed and played with the balloon. Mark and his friends had a lot of fun with the big, red balloon. They learned that sometimes, unexpected things can bring joy and fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Lucy. She had a beautiful dress that she wore all the time. One day Lucy was playing in her garden when her dad came home. He was very angry. He shouted at Lucy and told her to go inside. When she got inside, she was really scared. She was so scared that she lied to her dad and said she didn't play in the garden. Her dad was still angry and he shouted at her again. Lucy was so frightened that she started to cry. Her dad saw that she was sad and so he stopped being angry. Instead he gave her a hug and wiped away her tears. He told her it was all right to tell the truth. From then on, Lucy always told the truth and she never had to lie again.

Once upon a time, there was a big jug. The jug was very difficult to move. One day, a small boy named Tim tried to move the jug. He pushed and pushed, but the jug did not move. Tim felt sad. Tim's friend, Sue, saw him and asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim said, "I can't move this difficult jug." Sue had an idea. She said, "Let's slap the jug together. Maybe it will move then." Tim and Sue both slapped the jug at the same time. The jug wobbled and then, it moved! They were so happy. From that day on, Tim and Sue knew that they could do difficult things if they worked together. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty boy who loved to write. He would write all day long. One day, he decided to write a story about a row of trees. In the story, the row of trees would talk and play with each other. They were very happy trees. The mighty boy was very proud of his story. He read it to his friends and they all loved it too. But one day, something unexpected happened. The trees in the story came to life! They jumped out of the paper and started to play with the mighty boy and his friends. The mighty boy and his friends were very surprised, but they had lots of fun playing with the trees. And from that day on, the mighty boy knew that his writing was truly magical.

Once upon a time, in a big castle, there lived a silly king. He liked to make funny faces and dance around all day. Everyone in the castle would laugh and clap when they saw him. One day, a little girl named Lily came to the castle. She asked the silly king if she could play with him. The king was very happy and said, "Yes, I promise we will have lots of fun together." So, they played hide and seek, and they laughed a lot. After a long day of fun, it was time for Lily to go home. The silly king was sad to see her go, but he promised to play with her again soon. Lily left the castle with a big smile on her face, knowing she had made a new friend.
