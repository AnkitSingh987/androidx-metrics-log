Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Dog and the Tree

One day, a dog named Bob found a big bone. He was very happy. He ran around with the bone in his mouth. Running made him feel dizzy, so he stopped to rest under a tree. A little bird sat on the tree. She saw Bob with the bone. The bird reminded Bob to share his bone with his friends. Bob thought it was a good idea. Bob took the bone to his friends. They all played with the bone and had lots of fun. They were happy that Bob shared his bone. They played until they were all dizzy and tired. Then, they slept under the tree with smiles on their faces.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a radio. It was a gloomy day and the sky was gray. Tim felt sad, but he thought the radio could make him happy. He took the radio home and put it in his room. Tim turned on the radio and it began to play music. He started to sing and dance, feeling happy. The gloomy day did not bother him anymore. Tim's mom and dad heard him sing and came to his room. They smiled and began to sing and dance too. Suddenly, the radio stopped playing music. Tim, his mom, and dad stopped singing and dancing. They looked at the radio, feeling sad again. But then, the radio started to sing! It was not playing music, but it was singing by itself. Tim, his mom, and dad were surprised, but they laughed and joined the radio in singing. The gloomy day became a happy day for everyone.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little boy named Tim. He was fearful of bugs, especially a mosquito. One day, he saw a mosquito in his room. He was scared and called his mom to help him. His mom came into the room and saw the mosquito. She told Tim not to disturb it. She said, "If you don't bother the mosquito, it won't bother you." Tim was still scared, but he listened to his mom. Later that day, Tim went outside to play. He saw the mosquito again, but this time he did not disturb it. To his surprise, the mosquito flew away and didn't come back. Tim learned that sometimes, when you leave things alone, they won't bother you.

Once upon a time, in a big city, there was a smart cat named Tom. Tom liked to walk around and look at all the things in the city. He saw tall buildings, trees, and many people. One day, Tom saw a little girl who was sad. She had lost her toy. Tom wanted to help her. He looked around and saw the toy on top of a small wall. Tom jumped up and got the toy for the girl. The little girl was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tom!" She gave Tom a big hug. Tom felt proud to help. From that day on, Tom would walk around the city and help people when they needed it. Everyone loved Tom, and they would always demand his help because he was a smart and kind cat.

One day, a little girl named Lucy wanted to help her mom make dinner. She was very excited. Her mom said, "Lucy, can you please wrap the bread in this cloth?" Lucy did as she was told and wrapped the bread. As they were making dinner, they heard a loud noise outside. Lucy felt frightened. She asked her mom, "What was that noise?" Her mom said, "I don't know, let's go see." When they went outside, they saw a big box. Lucy and her mom opened the box, and inside was a big cake! They were very surprised. A note on the box said, "For Lucy and her mom, enjoy your dinner!" They laughed and enjoyed their dinner with the big cake.

Once upon a time, a little lion lived in a big forest. The lion liked to jump high and play with his friends. One day, he saw a big tree with high branches. The lion wanted to jump high and touch the branches. The lion tried to jump high, but he could not reach the branches. He felt sad and sat under the tree. His friend, the bird, saw him and asked a question. "Why are you sad, little lion?" The lion said, "I want to jump high and touch the branches, but I can't." The bird had an idea. "I can help you, little lion!" The bird flew up to the high branches and broke off a small one. The bird brought the branch down to the lion. The lion was happy and thanked the bird. Now the lion and the bird played together with the branch, and the lion did not feel sad anymore.
