Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Gentle Bunny and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a gentle bunny named Lily. She loved to zip through the fields and play with her friends. One day, she found a box of pastel colors in the grass. Lily called her friend, a small bird named Tim. "Tim, come see what I found!" she said. Tim flew down and looked at the pastels. They both decided to use the pastels to make pretty pictures on the ground. They drew flowers, trees, and even a big sun. The gentle bunny and the small bird zipped around, laughing and having fun with the pastels. Their friends saw the pretty pictures and joined them too. And they all played happily together.

Once upon a time, there was a clever cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to hear her human read the menu from the kitchen. She would sit and listen carefully, hoping to hear something tasty. One day, Kitty heard her human say, "Today's menu has fish!" Kitty was very happy, because she loved fish. She knew she had to find a way to get some. Kitty thought and thought. She decided to be very nice and purr loudly. Her human smiled and gave her some fish from the menu. Kitty was so happy and knew that being clever was the best way to get what she wanted.

Lily and Max are twins. They like to play with their toys and read books. But today they are going to visit their grandma. Grandma lives in a big house with a garden. She has a dog named Spot. "Come on, Lily and Max, it's time to go!" Mom says. She puts on their coats and hats. They get in the car and drive to grandma's house. "Hello, my darlings!" Grandma says. She hugs and kisses them. "I'm so happy to see you. Do you want some cookies and milk?" "Yes, please!" Lily and Max say. They follow grandma to the kitchen. Spot follows them too. He wags his tail and barks. They eat the cookies and drink the milk. They tell grandma about their toys and books. Grandma tells them stories about when she was little. They laugh and have fun. But soon Lily and Max feel sleepy. Their eyes are heavy and their heads are nodding. "Are you tired, my darlings?" Grandma asks. "Do you want to take a nap?" "Yes, please!" Lily and Max say. They follow grandma to the guest room. Spot follows them too. He curls up on the rug. Grandma tucks them in the bed. She kisses them and sings a lullaby. "Sleep well, my darlings," Grandma says. "I love you very much." Lily and Max close their eyes. They dream of cookies and milk, toys and books, and grandma and Spot. They are happy and cozy. They are glad they came to visit.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy dog named Max. Max lived in a small house with his friend, a girl named Lily. Lily loved Max a lot and they played together every day. Max liked to sleep under Lily's desk when he was tired. One day, Lily forgot to feed Max his food. Max was very hungry, but Lily did not know. Max looked for food to eat, but he could not find any. He was too sleepy and weak to play with Lily. Lily was sad because Max did not want to play. As the day went on, Max became more and more hungry. He tried to tell Lily, but she did not understand. In the end, Max was too hungry and sleepy to play with Lily anymore. Lily learned that she must always remember to feed Max, so he can be happy and play with her every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play outside in the open. One day, she saw a small bird stuck in a tree. She wanted to rescue the bird, so she climbed the tree. As she got closer to the bird, she saw a light. The light was very bright, and Sue was scared. But she wanted to help the bird, so she kept going. When she reached the light, she saw that it was a magic lamp! Sue rubbed the lamp, and a friendly genie came out. The genie said he would help her rescue the bird. With a snap of his fingers, the bird was free! Sue was so happy, and the bird thanked her by giving her a big hug.
