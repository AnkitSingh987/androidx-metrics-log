Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Bug

One day, a little girl named Sue found a big jar of jelly. She loved jelly so much. She wanted to eat it all. But, she was too small to reach the jar. So, she had an idea. She would stand on a chair to get it. Sue stood on the chair and reached for the jar. But as she did, she saw a small bug. The bug was scared of her big hand. Sue felt sad for the bug. She wanted to be its friend. So, she stopped trying to get the jar. Sue got down from the chair and said to the bug, "Don't be scared. I won't hurt you." The bug was happy. It became Sue's friend. They played together all day. And in the end, Sue shared her jelly with her new friend.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy soap. At the store, there were many soaps on display. Tim saw a big, red soap that he liked. His mom said it was a special soap that could make lots of bubbles. Tim was excited to try it. That night, Tim took a bath with the big, red soap. He played with the bubbles and had lots of fun. But then, he saw something in the water. It was a small, green bug. Tim was frightened. He called his mom to help him. His mom came and saw the bug. She told Tim that it was just a friendly bug that liked bubbles too. She took the bug out of the water and put it outside. Tim felt better and continued to play with the bubbles. He knew that the big, red soap was special, just like his mom said.

Once upon a time, there was a furry duck. The duck lived on a farm with many friends. The duck liked to play with the other animals. The farmer did not allow the duck to play with the big animals, but the duck could play with the small ones. One day, the duck went to play with the small animals. But, all the small animals were gone! The duck was sad and did not know what to do. The duck looked and looked for the small animals, but could not find them anywhere. Then, the duck saw something big and scary. It was a big animal the farmer did not allow the duck to play with! But, the big animal was not mean. The big animal had found the small animals and was playing with them. The duck was happy to see the small animals were safe. The big animal let the duck play with them too. Now, the furry duck had even more friends to play with on the farm.

Once upon a time, there was an old village with crooked roads and many little cottages. Not far away, there was a modern city with a long path leading to it. One day, a three year old girl named Emily decided to go on an adventure. She picked up her basket and skipped down the path. As she listened to the chirping of the birds, Emily felt a cool breeze on her face. Suddenly, Emily noticed something shining in the distance. She could see a big bright building in the middle of the city. "What is that?" Emily asked to herself. She kept walking until she reached the building. Emily was amazed by the tall, shiny structure. She sighed in amazement. "This must be a modern wonder," Emily said. She was filled with curiosity and wanted to explore more. She smiled and started walking along the path, wondering what else she would find.

One day, a little boy named Tim was walking to the park. He saw a big box that was open. He wanted to know what was inside, so he went to look. He saw a big toy and wanted to play with it. But he did not know what would happen next. A big and rude dog came running up to Tim. The dog wanted the toy too. Tim did not want to give the toy to the dog. He thought the dog was rude for trying to take his toy. So, Tim tried to run away with the toy. But the dog was too fast for Tim. The dog caught up to him and took the toy away. Tim was very sad and cried. He went home without the toy. The bad ending made Tim learn to be careful with open boxes and rude dogs.
