Category: Intermediate
Title: Mummy and Little and the Garden

One day, Mummy and Little Jack were in the garden. Mummy wanted to teach Little Jack a new game. "Let's play with this rag!" she said. Little Jack was so pleased and excited. He waved the rag around in the air, pretending it was a fancy bird. Mummy saw how much fun it was and she started to teach him another game. She showed him how to wrap the rag around his head, like a pirate. Little Jack giggled and said, "I'm a pirate!" Mummy said, "Yes, you look so fancy!" Little Jack and Mummy played with the rag all day long. They made it fly, twirl and spin around. When the sun went down, it was time for a bath. But Little Jack held up the rag and said, "Not my rag! I want to keep playing!" Mummy laughed and said, "Ok, you can play tomorrow." Little Jack ran inside, happily carrying his rag. He was so excited to go to bed so he could play again tomorrow. THE

Once upon a time there were two best friends, Jamie and Zoe. They were only three years old. One day, Jamie and Zoe wanted to play a game. Jamie chose to go outside and play, but it was very cold outside. Zoe decided to stay inside and do some exercise. Jamie was very unhappy that Zoe chose not to go with him. He started to cry and told Zoe that she shouldn't choose exercise because it wasn't any fun. But Zoe said that if Jamie wanted to have fun outside he could play with his other friends. Jamie didn't like this idea. So he challenged Zoe to a race around the house. They both agreed and had a race. Jamie won, but Zoe was happy too as she had enjoyed the exercise. The friends hugged each other and from that day on, they chose to play together whenever it was cold outside.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to collect pretty rocks. One sunny day, she went to the park to find more rocks for her collection. At the park, Sue saw a boy on the swing. The boy was sad. Sue asked the boy, "Why are you sad?" The boy said, "I lost my toy car. It is my favorite toy." Sue wanted to help the boy, so they started to look for the toy car together. As they looked, Sue found a shiny rock. But instead of keeping the rock, she gave it to the boy. The boy smiled and said, "Thank you!" Just then, they found the toy car under the swing. The boy was happy, and Sue felt happy too. They learned that helping others can make you feel good. And from that day, Sue and the boy became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a cheerful little town, there lived a girl named Lily. She had a big urge to unlock the secrets of the big, red door in her house. Her mom always told her, "No, Lily, you cannot open that door." One day, Lily's mom went to the store. Lily thought, "I will unlock the door now!" She found the key and turned it. The door opened! Behind the door, there were lots of toys and games. Lily played with the toys and had fun. When her mom came back, she saw the open door and said, "Lily, I told you not to open the door!" Lily said, "I'm sorry, Mom. I just really wanted to see what was behind it." Her mom hugged her and said, "It's okay, Lily. I understand your urge, but you must listen next time."

Once upon a time, there was a little bug who loved to crawl. One day, he decided to go to the mall. The mall was big and full of people. The little bug was excited to see so many things. In the mall, the little bug met a polite cat. The cat said, "Hello, little bug! What are you doing here?" The little bug replied, "I am here to crawl and see new things!" The cat smiled and said, "That sounds fun! Can I join you?" Together, they crawled all around the mall. They saw many stores and met many new friends. The little bug and the polite cat had a great day at the mall. They promised to meet again and have more fun adventures.
