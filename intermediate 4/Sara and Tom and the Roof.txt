Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and Tom and the Roof

Sara and Tom were twins who liked to play near the old mill. The mill was big and gray, with a wheel that turned in the water. Sometimes they saw ducks and fish in the water, and they threw bread for them. One day, Sara and Tom saw a door in the mill that was open. They were curious and wanted to see what was inside. They left their bikes and ran to the door. "Wait for me!" Tom said. "Hurry up!" Sara said. Inside the mill, it was dark and dusty. There were many machines and gears that made loud noises. Sara and Tom felt scared, but they wanted to explore more. They saw a ladder that went up to the roof. "Let's go up there!" Sara said. "Maybe we can see the whole town!" Tom said. They climbed the ladder, but it was old and rusty. When they reached the top, the ladder broke and fell down. Sara and Tom were trapped on the roof. They looked around and saw no one. They shouted for help, but no one heard them. They started to cry. After a while, they saw their dad coming on his bike. He was looking for them. He saw their bikes near the mill and guessed where they were. He saw them on the roof and waved. "Dad!" Sara and Tom shouted. "Help us!" Dad found another way to get to the roof and rescued them. He hugged them and said, "I'm so glad you're safe. But you should never go inside the old mill. It's dangerous and not a place for children. You could have been hurt or worse. You should always listen to me and stay away from the mill." Sara and Tom felt sorry and ashamed. They said, "We're sorry, dad. We won't do it again. We love you." Dad smiled and said, "I love you too. Now let's go home. You look pale and hungry. I have some cookies and milk for you." The moral of the story is: Children should obey their parents and not go to places that are unsafe. Curiosity can lead to trouble if not careful.

One part sunny day, two mummies were planning for a meeting. One mummy felt excited but the other mummy was frightened. The mummy who was frightened asked the other mummy, "Why do we have to do this?" The excited mummy replied, "It will be ok, I promise!" Then they arrived at the meeting place where they saw a huge room full of people. Everyone was talking to each other and the frightened mummy was scared. The excited mummy tried to calm the frightened mummy and said in a reassuring voice, "Just watch and listen. It will be fun, you'll see!" The frightened mummy nodded and took a deep breath, but was still very scared. The meeting started and the people spoke of scary things. The frightened mummy looked around and could feel a swirl of fear growing inside. The frightened mummy wanted to run away but the excited mummy sucked in her breath and said, "Shhh, this is what we came for. Just be brave, it will be ok!" The frightened mummy held her breath and watched as the meeting ended peacefully. In the end, the frightened mummy was grateful for the excited mummy's support and courage throughout the meeting.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very excited to go to the grocery store with his mom. He loved to help her pick out food for the family. He knew it would be an amazing day. At the grocery store, Tim and his mom found yummy apples, bananas, and carrots. Tim liked to put the food in the cart. He felt like a big helper. His mom was very happy with him. When they were done, they went home. Tim helped his mom put the food away. They had a fun day at the grocery store. Tim knew he was an amazing helper.

Tim and Sam are brothers. They like to play with their toys. They have many toys, but their favorite is a big cage with two birds inside. The birds are blue and yellow. They can sing and fly. One day, Tim and Sam have a fight. They both want to play with the cage. Tim says, "It is my turn. I want to make the birds sing." Sam says, "No, it is my turn. I want to make the birds fly." They pull the cage from each other. They are very competitive. The birds are scared. They do not like the noise and the shaking. They flap their wings and peck the bars. They want to get out of the cage. They tell Tim and Sam, "Stop! Stop! You are hurting us. Let us go. We are not toys. We are alive." Tim and Sam hear the birds. They are surprised. They did not know the birds can talk. They feel sorry for the birds. They stop fighting. They say, "We are sorry, birds. We did not mean to hurt you. We will let you go. You can fly and sing in the sky." They open the cage and set the birds free. The birds are happy. They thank Tim and Sam. They say, "You are good boys. You learned your lesson. Be kind to each other and to animals. Goodbye." They fly away and sing a beautiful song. Tim and Sam hug each other. They say, "We are sorry, brother. We will not fight again. We will share our toys and play nicely." They smile and go to play with their other toys. They are happy.
