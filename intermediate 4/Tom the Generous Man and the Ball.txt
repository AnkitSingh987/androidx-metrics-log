Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Generous Man and the Ball

Once upon a time, in a small village in the east, there lived a generous man named Tom. Tom loved to help people and spend time with his friends. He had a big heart and always shared his toys and food with others. One sunny day, Tom met a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she lost her ball. Tom said, "Don't worry, Sue. I will help you find your ball." They searched high and low, but they could not find the ball. Tom saw that Sue was still sad, so he decided to give her one of his own balls. Sue was so happy and said, "Thank you, Tom! You are very generous." They played with the ball and had lots of fun. From that day on, Tom and Sue became best friends and spent many happy days playing together in the east village. And they always remembered to share and be generous with each other.

Ben and Mia liked to play in the farm. They saw many animals, like cows, pigs, chickens and horses. They also saw a mule. A mule was like a horse, but with bigger ears and a louder voice. "Look at the mule!" Ben said. "He has funny ears. Can we touch them?" "I don't know," Mia said. "Maybe he will not like it. He looks angry." But Ben did not listen. He ran to the mule and tried to pull his ears. The mule did not like it at all. He kicked Ben with his back legs. Ben fell down and cried. "Ow, ow, ow!" Ben said. "The mule hurt me!" Mia ran to Ben and tried to help him. She saw blood on his leg. She was scared. "Ben, you are hurt!" Mia said. "We have to tell mom and dad. They will be mad at you. You disturbed the mule. You should not do that." Ben was sorry. He wished he had listened to Mia. He learned a lesson. He would never disturb the mule again.

Once upon a time, there was a toy town. In this town, all the toys played together and had fun. One day, a new toy came to town. It was a toy gun. All the toys were excited to meet the new toy and play with it. A toy bear named Ben saw the gun and thought it could be fun. He suggested, "Let's play a game with the new toy gun!" All the toys agreed and started to play. They had a great time playing with the gun, but then something unexpected happened. As they were playing, the toy gun turned into a yummy candy bar! The toys were surprised but also happy. They all shared the candy bar and enjoyed the sweet treat. From that day on, the toys learned that even when something unexpected happens, it can still bring joy and fun to everyone.

One day, a seal named Sam was playing near the water. He loved to spring up and down in the waves. Sam was very happy, but he was also very filthy. His fur was full of dirt and sand. A little girl named Lily saw Sam and said, "Hi, Sam! You are very dirty. Do you want me to help you get clean?" Sam nodded, and Lily started to wash him with water. As Lily was washing Sam, something unexpected happened. Sam began to change colors! He was not a normal seal anymore. He turned into a beautiful rainbow seal! Sam and Lily were both very surprised and happy. They played together all day, and Sam was never filthy again.

One day, a boy named Tim found a smelly paint pistol in his toy box. He was very happy and wanted to show it to his friend, Sally. Tim ran to Sally's house with the smelly paint pistol. "Look, Sally!" Tim said, holding the paint pistol. "Let's play with it!" Sally looked at the smelly paint pistol and said, "Okay, let's paint the fence!" They started to paint the fence with the smelly paint pistol. They were having a lot of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The paint pistol began to shake and make a loud noise. Suddenly, the smelly paint pistol changed into a big, friendly dog! Tim and Sally were very surprised. They laughed and played with the dog all day long. And from that day on, the dog was their best friend.
