Category: Intermediate
Title: Freddy the Frog and the Turtle

Once upon a time, in a big swamp, there lived a little frog named Freddy. Freddy had a broken leg, so he could not hop and play with his friends. He felt sad and lonely. One day, a kind duck named Daisy saw Freddy. "Why are you sad?" she asked. Freddy told her about his broken leg. Daisy wanted to help, so she mentioned that she knew a wise old turtle who could fix his leg. Freddy and Daisy went to see the wise old turtle. The turtle gave Freddy some magic leaves to wrap around his broken leg. After a few days, Freddy's leg was all better! He could hop and play with his friends again. Freddy and Daisy became best friends. The moral of the story is to always help others when they need it, and good things will happen to you.

Sara and Ben are playing outside. They like to run and jump and laugh. The sun is bright and the sky is blue. They see some big birds flying in the air. They have wide wings and long tails. Sara and Ben point at them and say, "Look, look, birds!" The birds make loud noises. They sound like, "Caw, caw, caw!" Sara and Ben try to copy them. They say, "Caw, caw, caw!" too. They think it is funny. They clap their hands and giggle. But then, the wind starts to blow. It is strong and cold. It makes the leaves and the grass move. It makes the birds fly faster and higher. It makes Sara and Ben shiver and shake. They do not like the wind. They say, "Brrr, brrr, brrr!" They run to their mom, who is sitting on a bench. She has a big blanket and a basket. She wraps them in the blanket and hugs them. She says, "Don't worry, my loves, the wind will stop soon. Let's have a picnic and enjoy the day." She opens the basket and takes out some sandwiches and cookies and juice. She gives them to Sara and Ben. They say, "Thank you, mom, thank you!" They eat and drink and smile. They forget about the wind. They are happy and warm. They clap their hands and say, "Yay, yay, yay!"

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. It was spring, so the sun was shining and the birds were singing. Tim saw a huge tree and wanted to climb it. As he climbed the tree, he saw a pretty butterfly. Tim wanted to catch the butterfly, so he followed it. The butterfly flew from one flower to another, and Tim tried to catch it with his hands. At last, the butterfly landed on a big, red flower. Tim reached out and caught the butterfly. He looked at it closely and saw how beautiful it was. Then, he let the butterfly go, and it flew away into the sky. Tim smiled and went back to climbing the huge tree.

Once upon a time, in a normal city, there lived a man who could fix anything. He helped everyone in the city. People loved him because he was very kind. One day, he found a big box. He didn't know what was inside. He opened the box, and a big bird came out. The bird was sad because it could not fly. The man wanted to help the bird, so he tried to fix its wing. The man worked very hard to fix the bird's wing. After some time, the bird could fly again. But the bird was not a normal bird. It was a magic bird! The bird thanked the man for helping it and said, "I will give you a wish. What do you want?" The man thought for a moment and said, "I want to help more people in the city." The magic bird granted his wish. From that day on, the man could fix anything even faster. The people in the city were happier than ever, and they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a big bird named Ollie the Ostrich was feeling different. He was not happy like the other birds. He wanted to find a friend who could help him feel better. Ollie went to the wise old owl and asked, "What do you recommend for me to feel happy?" The wise old owl thought for a moment and said, "Ollie, I recommend you find a friend who is just like you. Then, you will feel happy again." So, Ollie went to look for a friend who was just like him. He met a little bird named Bobby. Bobby was different too. They played together and had fun. But, Bobby was not as big as Ollie, and he could not run as fast as Ollie. One day, they were playing near the river. Ollie was running very fast, and Bobby tried to keep up. But, Bobby was too small and slow. He tripped and fell into the river. Ollie tried to help him, but it was too late. Bobby was gone, and Ollie felt sadder than ever.
