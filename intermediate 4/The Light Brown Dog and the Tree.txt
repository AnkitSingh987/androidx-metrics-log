Category: Intermediate
Title: The Light Brown Dog and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a light brown dog. The dog loved to study. Every day, the dog would sit with his owner and look at books. The owner was happy to see the dog study. One day, the dog and the owner went for a walk. They saw a big tree. The owner said, "Let's sit under the tree and study together." The dog barked happily. They sat under the tree and opened a book. The sun was shining, and the dog felt warm and happy. They studied and talked about the pictures in the book. The dog and the owner had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her ball. The ball was smooth and pretty. Lily liked to pass the ball to her friends. One day, Lily met a boy named Tim. Tim saw the ball and said, "Wow, I admire your ball! Can I play with you?" Lily said, "Yes, let's pass the ball to each other!" They played with the ball, and it was fun. Lily and Tim became good friends. They liked to pass the smooth ball to each other every day. They had lots of fun and always admired the ball. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a parrot named Polly. Polly was a fast bird who loved to fly around all day. She had many friends in the big tree where she lived. One day, a big wind came and blew the door of Polly's house shut. Polly was scared and didn't know what to do. She called out to her friends, "Help, help! My door is shut and I can't get in!" Her friends heard her cry and came to help. They all worked together to open the door. They pushed and pulled, and finally, the door opened. Polly was so happy to see her friends and thanked them for their help. From that day on, Polly and her friends played together every day. They flew fast in the sky, and when the wind blew again, they made sure to help each other. They knew that friends are always there to help when you need them.

Once upon a time, there was a charming little girl named Lily. She loved to make things with paper and glue. One day, she wanted to make a beautiful paper crown to wear. But she needed something to cut the paper. Lily's mommy recommended using scissors. She said, "Be careful, scissors can be sharp." Lily listened to her mommy and held the scissors very carefully. She cut out the crown shape from the paper and then used glue to stick pretty things on it. When the paper crown was done, Lily put it on her head. She felt like a princess. She showed her mommy, and her mommy smiled. "You did a great job," she said. Lily was so happy that she danced around the room, wearing her charming paper crown.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly cat named Tom. Tom loved to watch TV all day. He would sit in front of the TV and laugh at the funny shows. He did not worry about anything else. One day, Tom's friend, a dog named Max, came to visit. Max saw Tom watching TV and asked if he could play with him. Tom did not want to stop watching TV, so he said no. Max felt sad and left Tom's house. As days went by, Tom kept watching TV and did not play with Max. He did not worry about his friend. One day, the TV stopped working. Tom was sad and had no one to play with. He had lost his friend Max and the TV.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went to play by the river. They found a big raft and wanted to go on an adventure. They both jumped on the raft and began to float down the river. The sun was shining, and the water was calm. As they floated, they saw a big frog on a rock. The frog was impatient and said, "Hey, can you help me? I need to spin around on this rock to find my friend. But I can't do it by myself." Tim and Spot decided to help the frog. They pushed the rock, and the frog spun around and around. Finally, the frog found his friend. But the friend was on the other side of the river. Tim and Spot had to think of a way to help the frog. They used the raft to float across the river and pick up the friend. The two frogs were very happy and thanked Tim and Spot. Tim and Spot smiled and continued their adventure on the raft, knowing they had done a good deed.
