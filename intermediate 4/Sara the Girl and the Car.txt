Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara the Girl and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sara. She lived in a small cabin in the woods. Sara liked to play with her doll, but the doll was very old and dull. One day, Sara met a boy named Tom. Tom had a bright and shiny toy car. Sara and Tom played together and had lots of fun. Sara showed Tom her dull doll, and Tom showed Sara his shiny car. Sara said to Tom, "Let's play marry with my doll and your car." Tom said, "Yes, that sounds like fun!" So, Sara and Tom played marry with the doll and the car. The dull doll and the shiny car were very happy together. Sara and Tom became best friends. They played together every day in the cabin. And they always made sure the dull doll and the shiny car were happy together too. From that day on, Sara and Tom always played together. They had many fun adventures in the woods with the dull doll and the shiny car. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little orange bug went on a walk. The bug liked to include new friends in his games. This day was very sunny and happy. The orange bug met a big green frog. The bug asked the frog, "Do you want to play with me?" The frog said, "Yes, I want to play too!" They jumped and played all day. But then, something unexpected happened! The big green frog was really a small green leaf. The wind blew and the leaf went away. The orange bug was sad, but he kept on looking for more friends to include in his games. And the bug knew he would find more friends soon.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a wise old man. He had a big skull that he liked to polish every day. The skull was very old and shiny. One day, a little boy saw the wise man polishing the skull. The boy asked, "Why do you polish the skull?" The wise man said, "It helps me think and be wise. The shiny skull reminds me to be kind and smart." The little boy wanted to be wise too. So the wise man let him polish the skull. The boy was very happy. He learned to be kind and smart, just like the wise man. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Sally found a plant in her garden. This plant was not like the others. It was very serious and could talk! "Hello, Sally!" said the plant. "I can shrink things. What do you want me to shrink?" Sally thought for a moment. She looked at her big toy car. "Can you shrink my toy car?" she asked. The plant nodded and with a wave of its leaves, the toy car became very small. Sally was so happy. Sally played with her tiny car all day. When it was time for bed, she thanked the serious plant. "Good night, plant! Thank you for making my toy car small," she said. The plant smiled and said, "You're welcome, Sally. Sleep well."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a toy she loved very much. It was a small doll with a dress. One day, Sue lost the doll's dress. She was very sad. Sue's mom gave her a needle and some cloth to make a new dress. Sue was happy and started to sew. But the needle could speak! The needle was very rude. It said mean things to Sue. Sue did not like the needle, but she wanted to make the dress for her doll. Sue tried to ignore the rude needle and kept sewing. But the needle spoke louder and louder. Sue got very mad. She threw the needle away and cried. The needle was gone, but the doll's dress was not done. Sue's doll stayed without a dress, and Sue was very sad. The end.

Once there was a little girl named Lilly. Lilly was three years old and she loved to read books. She had a big bookcase with lots of books in it that she liked to look at. One day, Lilly was looking through her bookcase to find her favorite book when she accidentally knocked over the bookcase. All of the books fell off and made a big mess. Lilly was very sorry. She called out to her mom, who was in the other room. "Mommy, I'm sorry," Lilly said. Her mom rushed into the room to check what happened. "Oh, Lilly," her mom said, feeling sad. "I know that you didn't mean to make this mess. Let's pick up the books and put them back on the bookcase." Lilly and her mom worked together to pick up the books and put them back on the bookcase. Once all the books were back in place, Lilly gave her mom a big hug and said, "I'm sorry, Mommy." Her mom smiled and said, "It's okay, sweetheart. We all make mistakes sometimes - it's just part of being human." Lilly nodded, happy that her mom understood. She decided that next time she looked through her bookcase, she would be more careful.
