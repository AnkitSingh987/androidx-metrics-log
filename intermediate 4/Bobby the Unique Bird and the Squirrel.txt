Category: Intermediate
Title: Bobby the Unique Bird and the Squirrel

One day, a unique bird named Bobby wanted to help his friends. He thought he could organize a lecture for them. He wanted to teach them how to fly high and fast. He asked his friend, Benny the squirrel, to help him. Bobby said, "Benny, can you help me teach our friends to fly high and fast?" Benny replied, "Yes, Bobby! Let's organize a fun lecture for them. We can teach them together." Bobby and Benny told all their friends about the lecture. They all came to learn. Bobby and Benny showed them how to fly high and fast. All the animals tried their best. They learned from Bobby and Benny. At the end of the day, all the animals were happy. They learned something new. The moral of the story is that we can learn new things from our friends. We should always help each other and work together.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful lawn. It was very green and had lots of pretty flowers. In the middle of the lawn, there was a big tree. A little girl named Lily lived near the lawn. She loved to play there every day. One sunny day, Lily saw a sad bird sitting on the tree. She asked the bird, "Why are you sad?" The bird replied, "My friend is not feeling well. I am worried about her." Lily wanted to help, so she said, "Let's pray together for your friend to feel better." So, Lily and the bird closed their eyes and prayed. They prayed with all their hearts. The next day, the bird's friend felt better. The bird was very happy and thanked Lily for her help. They played together on the beautiful lawn. The moral of the story is that when we care for others and pray for them, good things can happen. Helping others can make us feel happy too.

One day, a little girl named Amy put on a costume. She dressed as a funny cat. Amy wanted to play with her friend, Tim. She went to his house and knocked on the door. Tim opened the door and said, "Ew, what a disgusting costume!" Amy felt sad. She didn't want her friend to think her costume was bad. Tim told Amy, "I don't want to play with you if you wear that costume." Amy went home and took off her costume. She put on her normal clothes and went back to Tim's house. When she got there, she saw Tim wearing a costume too. He was dressed as a silly dog. Tim said, "I'm sorry I was mean to you. I like your costume. I was just jealous because I didn't have one." Amy forgave Tim, and they played together all day. They learned that it's not nice to punish someone for how they look. Everyone should be kind and have fun together.

Lily likes to play dress-up with her mom's clothes. She finds a nice blouse in the closet. It is pink and has flowers on it. She puts it on and looks in the mirror. She feels very pretty. She goes to show her mom. Her mom is in the kitchen, making lunch. She sees Lily and smiles. "You look very nice, Lily," she says. "Is that my blouse?" Lily nods. She likes to make her mom happy. She wants to say something nice too. She thinks hard. She remembers a word her mom uses a lot. She opens her mouth and says, "Reply." Her mom laughs. She hugs Lily and kisses her cheek. "That's a very good word, Lily," she says. "But it's not a nice thing to say. It means to answer someone who talks to you. Like this: I say, 'I love you, Lily', and you reply, 'I love you, too, Mom'." Lily understands. She smiles and says, "I love you, too, Mom." She feels very happy. She likes to learn new words. She likes to play dress-up. She likes to reply.

One day, a big, attractive tank was in the park. It was very shiny and strong. All the kids loved to play around it. They would climb on it and pretend to drive it. The tank made everyone happy. But one day, a mean boy came to the park. He did not like the tank. He wanted to play with his own toys and did not want to share. He started to shout at the other kids, "Go away! This is my park!" The kids were sad and scared. They did not know what to do. Then, a brave little girl had an idea. She told the other kids to stand together and shout, "We want to play too!" They all shouted together, and the mean boy was surprised. He realized that the park was for everyone to share. The kids all played together and had a great time with the attractive tank. And from that day on, the mean boy learned to share and play with others.
