Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Toy

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily liked to play outside. One day, she went to a big bush to find a rare toy. She wanted the toy very much. While looking for the toy, Lily met a small bird. The bird said, "I know where the rare toy is, but I forget where I saw it last." Lily asked, "Can you help me find it, please?" The bird said, "Yes, let's look together." They looked and looked, and then the bird remembered. "I found it!" said the bird. "The rare toy is under this bush." Lily was very happy. She thanked the bird, and they played together all day.

Mummy was sitting in the living room, watching TV and knitting. She had not noticed that her 3-year-old son had crawled away and was now peeking around the fireplace. Mummy: "What are you doing around here, darling?" Son: "I'm looking for the lazy fire. It won't come out so I'm trying to make it move." Mummy: "Oh, sweetheart, the fire isn't lazy. It just needs me to light the match and make it burn." Son: "Oh, OK!" Mummy then took some matches and lit the fire in the fireplace. Soon, the room was warm and cozy. The son crawled closer and touched the flame, feeling the warmth. He smiled at his mummy. Mummy: "You see son, the fire isn't lazy, it just needs a little help every now and then!" Son: "Wow, it's so nice and warm!" Mummy smiled and hugged her son and they watched the fire together until it was time to go to bed.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom loved to play with sand. One day, Tom went to the beach with his mom. He was very glad to be there. At the beach, Tom saw a big pile of sand. He wanted to make it increase. So, Tom asked his mom, "Mom, can I make the sand bigger?" His mom said, "Yes, Tom, you can." Tom started to add more sand to the pile. But then, a big wave came and washed away all the sand. Tom was sad because his sand pile was gone. The end.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom and dad. They saw the wide sea with big waves. Tim loved to play in the water and feel the waves on his feet. He also liked to build sand castles with his mom and dad. While playing, Tim saw a big, shiny thing in the sand. Tim tried to pull it out, but it was stuck. He asked his mom and dad for help. They all pulled together, and out came a big, shiny box. They were very surprised! Inside the box, they found a lot of fun toys. There were balls, dolls, and toy cars. Tim and his family played with the toys all day. They had so much fun. At the end of the day, they took the toys home to share with their friends. The beach trip was the best day ever.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to play in her mom's room. Mia liked to peek at her mom's jewelry box. The box was very pretty and had many shiny things inside. Mia's mom always told her not to touch it, but Mia was very curious. Mia opened the jewelry box, and she saw a smelly sock! It was not shiny or pretty like the other things. Mia was very surprised. She did not know why a smelly sock was in the jewelry box. She thought it was a mistake. Mia took the smelly sock to her mom. Her mom laughed and said it was a funny trick. Mia's dad had put the sock in the box to make them laugh. They all laughed together, and Mia put the sock back in the box. Now, every time Mia peeked at the jewelry box, she remembered the funny trick and smiled.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a big box. The box was open. Inside the box, she saw many things to decorate with. There were pretty papers, shiny stars, and colorful ribbons. Lily wanted to use them all. She thought it would be fun to decorate her room. Lily's friend Tom came over to play. He saw the open box and all the things to decorate with. Tom had a naughty idea. He said, "Let's decorate the cat!" Lily thought it would be funny, so they both started to put shiny stars and colorful ribbons on the cat. The cat did not like it. He jumped and ran all around the room. Lily and Tom laughed, but they knew they were being naughty. They said sorry to the cat and took off all the decorations. Then, they used the pretty papers, shiny stars, and colorful ribbons to decorate Lily's room. The cat was happy, and they all had a fun day.
