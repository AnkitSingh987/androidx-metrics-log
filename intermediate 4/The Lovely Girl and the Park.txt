Category: Intermediate
Title: The Lovely Girl and the Park

Once there was a lovely little girl. She was only three years old, but already so smart. One day, she was walking in the park with her mom. They were admiring the trees and the flowers when suddenly, the little girl saw a bright sparkly sign. She asked her mom what it said, and her mom explained that it was a special sign that meant "no running". The little girl nodded and was so excited when she realized that she knew how to read the sign! She pointed and proudly said, "It says 'no running'!". A few days later, the little girl was at her ballet class. She learned to move her body in time to the music, and to make the most beautiful shapes with her arms and legs. After practicing for a while, the teacher taught her to sign with her hands. The little girl never forgot the lovely movements of the sign language. She loved showing off to her friends, and soon, her mom noticed that she was becoming quite a pro at signing. The little girl was so proud of herself. With her mom's help and love, she had already learned so much in such a short time.

Once upon a time, there was a kind fisherman. He lived by the sea. Every day, he went out to fish. He had a big boat and a little net. He loved to catch fish and share them with his friends. One day, the kind fisherman went out on his boat. He threw his little net into the water. He hoped to catch some big fish. But, instead of fish, he caught a big, wet, green thing. It was a big, green fish! The fisherman was very surprised. The big, green fish looked at the kind fisherman. It said, "Please let me go." The fisherman was so surprised! He did not know that fish could talk. The fisherman thought about it. Then, he did something unexpected. He slapped the big, green fish! The big, green fish laughed. It was a magic fish! The fish said, "Thank you for making me laugh. I will give you a wish." The kind fisherman wished for more fish to share with his friends. The big, green fish made lots of fish appear in the fisherman's net. The kind fisherman was very happy. He let the big, green fish go. From that day on, the kind fisherman always had lots of fish to share with his friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a brilliant red car. This car liked to speed down the street very fast. The car had a label on its side that said "Fast Car." All the kids loved to watch the car go by. One day, a little boy named Tim saw the car and said, "Wow, I want to go fast like that car!" Tim's friend, Sally, heard him and said, "Be careful, Tim. Going too fast can be bad." Tim didn't listen and went to find a way to go fast. Tim found a big hill and decided to ride his bike down it. He went very fast, just like the brilliant red car. But at the bottom of the hill, Tim fell off his bike. Sally came to help him and said, "See, I told you going too fast can be bad." Tim learned his lesson and never tried to speed like the red car again.

Once upon a time there was a cop who could take incredible leaps. He had long legs and he could jump really high. One day, a little girl named Daisy was playing hopscotch on the playground and she spotted the cop. She said, "Hello! You can jump so high. Are you a superhero?" The cop smiled and said, "No, I'm just a cop with some special skill." Daisy was so impressed. She said, "That's incredible! Can you show me how high you can jump?" The cop laughed and said, "Yes, of course." He then took an incredible leap, crossing the playground with two big jumps. Daisy's eyes were wide. She said, "Wow, look at you go! You must be the best cop in the city." The cop smiled. He said, "Thank you, Daisy. You are very kind." And they both smiled, because they both knew it was true.
