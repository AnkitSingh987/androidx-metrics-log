Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Frog

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to color with her crayons. She had a big box of them, with all the colors of the rainbow. Sue was eager to draw a picture for her mom. One day, Sue's friend Tim came to play. They talked about what they could draw with the crayons. Sue wanted to draw a big, happy sun. Tim wanted to draw a funny, green frog. They were excited to start. Sue and Tim drew for a long time. Sue's sun was big and bright. Tim's frog was very funny. They showed their pictures to Sue's mom. She was so happy and proud of them. She put the pictures on the wall for everyone to see.

Nina and her dad were walking near the lake. They stopped at a shop on the lake. Inside the shop, Nina's dad saw a pistol. He was very envious of it. "Big-Man, I want the pistol!" said Nina excitedly. "No, little-girl, we cannot buy it. It's too expensive," her dad replied. Nina couldn't believe it. She just wanted the pistol. She thought to herself: "This pistol is mine and no one can take it away from me!" All the way home, she thought about the pistol. The next day, Nina went to the shop by herself. She walked up to the shopkeeper and said: "I want to sell my dad's watch. I will give you the watch for the pistol!" The shopkeeper was shocked. "No, little-girl, this is not right. I cannot do that." But Nina was very envious of the pistol and she did not listen. She threw her dad's watch on the counter and ran away with the pistol. Nina's dad was very angry when he found out. He knew it was wrong and scolded her. Nina was punished and she had to give the pistol back. The bad ending was not fun for anyone.

Once upon a time, in a big zoo, there was a polite rhinoceros named Roly. Roly liked to play with all his friends. His best friend was a small bird named Chirpy. They loved to play near the water. One hot day, Roly and Chirpy went to the pond. Roly said, "Chirpy, let's play with the water!" Chirpy agreed and said, "Yes, Roly! Let's spray water on each other!" So, Roly used his big nose to spray water on Chirpy. Chirpy flew up and down, laughing and having fun. Chirpy then took some water in his beak and sprayed it on Roly's face. Roly laughed and said, "That feels nice, Chirpy! Thank you!" They played and sprayed water until the sun went down. Then, they said goodbye and went to their homes to sleep. Roly and Chirpy were very happy and could not wait to play again the next day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play on the soft carpet in her room. One day, while playing, she felt a poke on her hand. She looked around and saw a tiny mouse. The mouse looked sad and scared. Lily wanted to help the mouse. She gave it some food and water. The mouse was very grateful. It smiled at Lily and they became friends. But, the mouse was still sad because it missed its family. Lily had an idea. She asked the mouse to show her where its family was. They went on a little adventure and found the mouse's family. The mouse was so happy! It hugged Lily and said thank you. Lily felt good that she could help her new friend.

One day, a grumpy cat named Tom was walking in the park. He saw a big red ball. Tom wanted to play with it. He used his paw to roll the ball. It went very fast. Tom ran after the ball. Suddenly, Tom heard a loud alarm. He stopped and looked around. He saw a little girl named Lucy. Lucy was crying. "Why are you crying?" asked Tom. "I lost my ball," said Lucy. Tom thought for a moment. He decided to give the red ball to Lucy. "Here, take this ball," said Tom. Lucy smiled and thanked Tom. They played together and became good friends. The grumpy cat wasn't grumpy anymore.
