Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Apple

One day, a girl named Lucy went to the market with her mom. They wanted to buy food for dinner. Lucy was very happy to help her mom. She knew it was important to include her in picking the food. At the market, Lucy saw a big, red apple. She wanted to get it for her mom. But the apple was too high for her to reach. Lucy's mom said, "Be careful, Lucy. You might get hurt if you try too hard." Lucy did not listen. She tried to reach the apple by standing on her toes. Suddenly, she fell down and hurt her knee. Lucy cried, and her mom came to help her. Her mom said, "I told you to be careful, Lucy. Now your knee is hurt." Lucy learned her lesson. She knew she should listen to her mom to stay safe. From that day on, she always made sure to be careful at the market and to include her mom in picking the food.

Once upon a time, there was an intelligent dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his red ball. He would throw the ball and run after it all day long. One hot day, Spot went to the park to play. He threw his red ball, but it went too far. It landed near a big tree. Spot was sad because he could not reach his ball. He tried to jump, but the ball was too high. Just then, a friendly bird came and saw Spot. The bird saw the red ball in the tree and knew what to do. The bird flew up, picked the ball with its beak, and dropped it down to Spot. Spot was so happy to have his red ball back. He thanked the bird, and they played together all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little dog named Max. Max liked to know what everyone was doing. One day, Max saw his friend, a cat named Lily, walk by his house. Max ran up to Lily and asked, "What are you doing?" Lily said, "I am going to buy dinner for my family. We are having fish tonight." Max thought that sounded yummy. He asked if he could come along. Lily said yes, and they walked to the store together. At the store, Max and Lily picked out the best fish for dinner. They also found some tasty treats for themselves. After they paid, they walked back to Lily's house. Max was so excited to have helped his friend buy dinner. They both enjoyed their treats and had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very frightened boy. He was scared of many things, like big dogs, loud noises, and the dark. One day, Tim went to the doctor with his mom. At the doctor's office, the doctor wanted to check Tim's lungs. He asked Tim to take a deep breath and give a big cough. The doctor listened to his lungs with a funny thing called a stethoscope. Tim was a little scared, but his mom held his hand and told him it was okay. After the check-up, the doctor said Tim's lungs were good and strong. Tim was happy and not frightened anymore. He knew that going to the doctor was not so scary after all. From that day on, Tim was a little less scared of the world around him.

Lily and Tom were having dinner with their mom and dad. They ate chicken and rice and carrots. Lily liked chicken and rice, but she did not like carrots. Tom liked carrots, but he did not like chicken and rice. Mom and dad liked everything. After they finished their plates, mom said, "Do you want dessert? I made a cake with chocolate and cream. It is very yummy." Lily and Tom said, "Yes, please!" They loved cake with chocolate and cream. It was their favorite dessert. Mom cut two big slices of cake and put them on plates. She gave one to Lily and one to Tom. She said, "Enjoy your dessert, my lovely children. You ate very well today." Lily and Tom said, "Thank you, mom. You are the best." They ate their cake with big smiles. It was very sweet and soft. Then, Lily had an idea. She said, "Tom, do you want to send some cake to grandma? She lives far away, but we can call her and tell her we love her. And we can take a picture of our cake and show her." Tom said, "That is a good idea, Lily. Grandma will be happy to hear from us. And she will like our cake. Let's do it." They asked mom for the phone and the camera. Mom said, "That is very kind of you, Lily and Tom. Grandma will be very happy to hear from you. And she will like your cake. Let me help you." Mom helped them call grandma and take a picture of their cake. Grandma answered the phone and said, "Hello, Lily and Tom. How are you? I miss you very much." Lily and Tom said, "Hello, grandma. We are fine. We miss you too. We are having dessert. We have cake with chocolate and cream. It is very yummy. We want to send you some cake. Look, we took a picture of our cake. Do you see it?" Grandma said, "Oh, what a lovely cake. It looks very yummy. Thank you for sending me some cake. You are very sweet. I wish I could hug you and kiss you. I love you very much." Lily and Tom said, "We love you too, grandma. We wish you could be here with us. We hope you are well. Bye, grandma." They hung up the phone and hugged mom. Mom said, "You made grandma very happy. You are very lovely children. I am proud of you." Lily and Tom said, "Thank you, mom. We are proud of you too. You make the best cake. Can we have some more?" Mom said, "Of course, you can. There is plenty of cake. Let's have some more."
