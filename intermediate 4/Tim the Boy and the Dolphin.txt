Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Dolphin

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. They saw a big bald man sitting on the sand. The man was sad. Tim asked his mom if they could talk to the man. "Hi, why are you sad?" Tim asked the bald man. The man said, "I lost my friend, the dolphin. He swam away and now I am all alone." Tim wanted to help the man find his dolphin friend. Tim and the bald man looked for the dolphin in the water. Suddenly, they saw a big wave coming. The wave brought the dolphin to the shore! The man was happy to see his friend again. The dolphin said, "I was looking for you too!" The bald man, Tim, and the dolphin played together in the water. They were all happy to be friends. The beach was a fun place for everyone.

Once upon a time, in a big house, there was an enormous room. In this room, there were many toys and things. A nice girl named Lily lived in the house. One day, she wanted to sort her toys and make the room look nice. As Lily started to sort her toys, she found a big box. Inside the box, there was a violin. Lily was happy to find the violin. She wanted to play it and make music. Lily played the violin and made pretty sounds. Her mom and dad heard the music and came to see. They clapped and smiled. Lily felt proud and happy. The enormous room was now clean and full of music.

Once upon a time, there was a reindeer named Ronny. Ronny had a big job to do. He had to design a new toy for Santa. But this was very difficult for Ronny. He tried and tried but could not think of a good toy idea. One day, Ronny met a little girl named Sally. Sally said, "I like toys that are fun and pretty." This gave Ronny an idea. He thought about what Sally said and began to work on his toy. Ronny worked very hard and made the toy. It was a fun and pretty toy, just like Sally said. Ronny showed the toy to Santa. Santa was very happy and said, "Good job, Ronny! This is the best toy ever!" And so, Ronny's difficult job was done, and all the children loved the new toy.

Timmy was out on a walk with his mom. They were holding hands and having a lot of fun. They came across a mysterious pond. The water was dark and still like glass. "Mommy," Timmy said, "What's in there?". Mom said, "Maybe we can find out! Let's try stirring the water with a stick". Timmy got a big stick and began to stir in the water. As the stick moved through the water, a little bit of salt spilled in. Timmy and his Mommy looked at each other with wide eyes. "What could this mean?" mom wondered. "Maybe someone put the salt in this mysterious pond." They searched the area around the pond, and sure enough, they found a little box with the letters "S-A-L-T" written on it. "It looks like someone did put the salt in the pond," Mom said. Timmy smiled. He had solved the mysterious pond mystery!

Once upon a time, in a big pond, there was an enormous goose. The goose had many friends. One day, the goose saw a little fish. The goose was kind and wanted to play with the fish. The goose asked the fish, "Do you want to play with me?" The little fish was very happy. The fish said, "Yes, I want to play with you." They played all day. They swam and splashed in the water. They had a lot of fun. The sun was going down, and it was time for the fish to go home. But then, something unexpected happened. The big goose was hungry. The goose looked at the little fish and thought, "I want to eat the fish." The goose opened its mouth and ate the little fish. The fish was gone, and the goose was no longer hungry. The goose had a full belly, but it was sad because it had no more friends to play with.
