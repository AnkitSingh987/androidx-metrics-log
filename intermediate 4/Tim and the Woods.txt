Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Woods

Once upon a time, there was a soft, little bird named Tim. Tim had a hurt wing and could not fly. He lived in a small triangle house in the woods. One day, a kind girl named Sue found Tim. "Don't worry, I will help you heal your wing," she said. Sue took care of Tim and made him feel better. They became very good friends and played together every day. One morning, when Tim woke up, he found that his wing was not hurt anymore. He was so happy and surprised! He said to Sue, "Thank you for helping me heal my wing!" Now Tim could fly again, and he and Sue had even more fun playing in the woods together.

Once upon a time, there was a unique town. In this town, there was a special law. The law said that everyone had to mail a nice picture to their friend every week. One day, a girl named Sally wanted to mail a picture to her friend, Tim. Sally and Tim were best friends. Sally said, "Tim, I will mail you a fun picture today!" Tim was very happy. But, something unexpected happened. When Sally went to mail the picture, she found a big surprise. There were lots of balloons and a cake at the mail place! Tim and all the town people were there. They said, "Surprise! We made a party for you, Sally!" Sally was so happy and hugged Tim. They all had a great time at the party.

Once upon a time, there was a spoiled boy named Tim. Tim did not like to listen to his mom and dad. He would ignore them when they talked to him. One day, Tim's mom and dad took him to the park. They wanted to teach him how to fish. At the park, Tim saw a big pond with lots of fish. His mom and dad showed him how to use a fishing rod. But Tim did not want to learn. He ignored them and played with the dirt instead. After a while, Tim's mom and dad caught a big fish. They were very happy. Tim saw them and wanted the fish too. But his mom and dad said, "No, Tim. You did not listen to us. You cannot have the fish." Tim felt sad and learned that he should not ignore his mom and dad.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to draw and make marks on everything. One day, he found a magazine with lots of pictures. Tim thought it would be fun to make marks on the magazine, too. His mom saw him and said, "No, Tim, we don't mark on magazines." But Tim was very persistent. He really wanted to make marks on the magazine. So, when his mom wasn't looking, he made a mark on the magazine. Later, Tim's mom found the marked magazine. She was not happy, but she knew that Tim was just having fun. She gave him a big piece of paper and said, "Here, Tim, you can make all the marks you want on this paper." Tim was very happy and made lots of marks on the paper. From then on, he only made marks on paper, not on magazines.

Once upon a time, there was a normal boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toys and learn new things. One day, Tim's mom told him it was time to learn math. Tim was a little scared because he did not know what math was. Tim's mom showed him how to count and add numbers. At first, Tim was not very good at math. He tried hard, but he could not remain calm. He felt sad and did not want to do math anymore. Tim's mom saw that he was sad and told him it was okay. She said that it was normal to feel scared when learning something new. Tim decided to keep trying and not give up. Soon, he got better at math and was not scared anymore. Tim was happy that he did not give up and learned something new.

One day, a little boy named Tim was watching his favorite cartoon. He loved to watch the funny animals on TV. He saw a big, fearful lion on the screen. The lion was scared of a tiny mouse. Tim laughed and thought it was very funny. Later, Tim went outside to play. He saw his friend, Sue, skipping in the park. "Hi Sue!" Tim said. "I saw a funny cartoon today. A big, fearful lion was scared of a tiny mouse!" Sue laughed and said, "That sounds funny, Tim! Let's skip together." As they were skipping, they heard a loud roar. They stopped and saw a real lion in the park! Tim and Sue were very scared. The lion looked at them and said, "I saw that cartoon, too. I didn't like it. Now, I am going to show everyone that I am not scared of a tiny mouse." The lion chased Tim and Sue, and they ran away as fast as they could. They were so scared that they never went back to the park again.
