Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Vase

One day, a little girl named Sue found a mysterious vase in her house. It was big and pretty. Sue wanted to know what was inside the vase. She tried to look inside, but it was too dark. Sue asked her mom, "What is inside the mysterious vase?" Her mom said, "I don't know. Maybe it can reverse time and take us back to when we were little." Sue was excited and wanted to try it. Sue and her mom held the mysterious vase together. They said, "Reverse, reverse, take us back in time!" Suddenly, they felt a little dizzy. When they opened their eyes, they saw that they were both little girls! They laughed and played together all day. When it was time to go back, they held the vase again and said, "Reverse, reverse, take us back home!" They were back to their normal ages, happy and full of love.

Once upon a time, there was a fast rabbit. The rabbit loved to run in the forest. One day, the rabbit found an arrow. The arrow was shiny and sharp. The rabbit wanted to become the fastest runner in the forest. But there was a big and strong bear who was also fast. The rabbit and the bear had a race to see who was the fastest. They ran and ran. The rabbit was very smart. He used the arrow to make a trap for the bear. The bear got stuck in the trap. The rabbit won the race and became the fastest runner in the forest. The bear was not angry. He clapped for the rabbit and they became friends.

One day, a small cat named Sam was in a big house. Sam saw a toy on a high stair. Sam wanted to play with the toy, but he was too small to reach it. He needed help to get the toy. Sam had a friend named Max, who was a fit dog. Max could jump high and reach things. Sam asked Max to help him get the toy. Max was happy to help his friend. Max jumped and reached the toy on the stair. He gave the toy to Sam. They played together all day. Sam was happy that his friend Max could help him. They were the best of friends.

Once upon a time there was a sweet dolphin who lived in the sea. He was very happy swimming around with all of his friends. One day, the dolphin decided to take a swim and explore a new area. The dolphin swam and swam until he found a big rock. He wanted to get on top of it, so he tried to jump. One, two, three times he jumped, but he could not get high enough to reach the rock. Then suddenly he thought of a way to get up there. He decided to kick really hard against the water and catapult himself up! It worked! The dolphin kicked with all his might and flew up to the top of the big rock! He was so proud of himself and smiled and laughed. It was the best day ever! The dolphin played games on the rock and looked out to the sea and felt so happy. He even made a new friend, a tiny crab who stayed with him until night time. The dolphin hopped off the rock and back into the sea, saying goodbye to his new friend. He swam back home, feeling very sweet and content.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was worried because she lost her favorite veil. She looked everywhere for it, but she could not find it. Mia was very sad. One day, Mia saw a bird with something shiny in its beak. It was her veil! Mia tried to get it back, but the bird flew away. Mia was very tired and needed to rest. She sat down under a big tree and closed her eyes. When Mia woke up, she saw the bird again. This time, the bird dropped the veil right in front of her. Mia was so happy! She thanked the bird and gave it a big hug. From that day on, Mia and the bird were best friends.
