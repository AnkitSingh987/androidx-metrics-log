Category: Intermediate
Title: Emma the Girl and the Pears

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Emma. She loved fruits and especially pears. One day she wanted to find the best pear ever, so she asked her mom for help. Emma: "Mommy, can you help me find the best pear?" Her mom smiled and said: "I know just the place." So they went to the local market. There they saw so many colorful pears. Emma couldn't believe her eyes. Suddenly, an old lady came up to them and suggested: "You should try these pears. They certainly are very juicy and flavorful." Emma was happy and said: "Thank you! I think I'll try those ones." The old lady smiled and said: "I recommend you try these two. They are the best!" Emma and her mom looked at each other and smiled. Emma took the two pears home and they were so juicy and colorful. Emma was so happy and thanked her mom for helping her find the best pears.

Once upon a time, there was a charming little firefly named Fifi. Fifi loved to glow in the dark. Every night, she would fly around and light up the sky with her friends. One night, Fifi saw some smoke coming from a small house. She flew closer and saw a little boy named Timmy. Timmy was scared of the dark. Fifi wanted to help him, so she said, "Don't be scared, Timmy! I can glow and light up your room." Timmy looked at Fifi and smiled. "Thank you, Fifi! You are so nice!" he said. Fifi and her friends flew into Timmy's room and started to glow. They made the room bright and happy. From that night on, Fifi and her friends would visit Timmy every night. They would glow and light up his room, making sure he was never scared of the dark again. Timmy and Fifi became the best of friends, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He liked to play outside in the dark. One night, he saw a shiny metal box under a big tree. Tom wondered what was inside the box. He called his friend, Sam, and said, "Look! A metal box! Let's open it!" Sam was scared of the dark, but he wanted to help Tom. They tried to open the box, but it was too heavy. Suddenly, a big wind came and blew the box away. Tom and Sam were very sad as they watched the box fly away. They never saw the metal box again.

Lily liked to play with her toy cars. She had many cars of different colors and sizes. She liked to make them zoom and beep on the floor. Sometimes, she pretended to fix them with her tools, like a mechanic. One day, she saw a big truck outside her window. It was red and shiny, and it had a loud horn. Lily wanted to see the truck up close. She ran to her mom and asked, "Mom, can I go see the truck?" Mom looked at the truck and smiled. She said, "Sure, Lily, but you have to ask the driver first. He is a mechanic. He fixes trucks and cars. Maybe he can show you his tools and how he works." Lily was excited. She put on her shoes and coat and followed Mom outside. She saw the driver standing next to the truck. He was tall and friendly. He waved at Lily and Mom. "Hi, I'm Tom. I'm a mechanic. I'm here to fix the car of your neighbor. Do you like trucks?" he asked. Lily nodded. She said, "Yes, I like trucks. I have toy cars. I play with them and fix them. Can I see your truck?" Tom smiled. He said, "Sure, you can see my truck. You can even sit inside and honk the horn. Do you want to invite your mom, too?" Lily looked at Mom and said, "Yes, please. Mom, come with me. Let's see the truck." Mom agreed. She said, "Thank you, Tom. That's very kind of you. Lily loves trucks and cars. She wants to be a mechanic when she grows up." Tom laughed. He said, "That's great. Maybe she can work with me someday. Come on, let's go to the truck." Lily, Mom, and Tom walked to the truck. Tom opened the door and helped Lily climb inside. He showed her the steering wheel, the pedals, the buttons, and the horn. Lily was amazed. She touched everything and honked the horn. She felt like a real mechanic. She was very happy. She said, "Thank you, Tom. This is the best truck ever."
