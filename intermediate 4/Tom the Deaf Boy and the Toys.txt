Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Deaf Boy and the Toys

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a deaf boy named Tom. Tom could not hear, but he was very good at reading people's faces. He had many friends in the village who loved to play with him. One day, Tom and his friends were playing near a big tree. They saw a man selling toys. The man said, "If you pay me two coins, you can have a toy." Tom's friends paid the man and got their toys. Tom did not have any coins, so he could not buy a toy. Tom felt sad and went home. His mom saw him and asked, "Why are you sad, Tom?" Tom told her the truth about not having coins to buy a toy. His mom hugged him and said, "Don't worry, Tom. You can do chores to earn coins." Tom worked hard and earned enough coins to buy a toy. He went back to the man and paid him for a toy. His friends were happy for him, and they all played together with their new toys. And they all learned that telling the truth and working hard can make you happy.

Once upon a time, there was a nice girl named Lily. She liked to go to the park and play. One day, she saw a big animal at the park. The animal had soft fur and a long tail. Lily wanted to be friends with the animal. Lily walked up to the animal and stared at it. The animal stared back at her. They looked into each other's eyes. Lily smiled and the animal wagged its tail. They knew they would be good friends. Lily and the animal played all day. They ran and jumped in the park. They had so much fun together. When it was time to go home, Lily hugged the animal. She knew she had made a new, nice friend.

One day, a girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. They had a big bag with food for a picnic. Lily was very happy and ready to eat. She saw her mom take out an avocado from the bag. Lily had never seen an avocado before. While they were eating, a little bird came to their picnic. The bird saw the avocado and wanted to try it too. Lily and her mom watched as the bird took a small bite. Then, something unexpected happened! The bird changed colors! It went from being a brown bird to a bright green bird, just like the avocado. Lily and her mom were very surprised. They discovered that the avocado could change the color of the bird. They laughed and shared the rest of the avocado with the bird. The green bird became their new friend, and they had a great day at the park together.

Once upon a time, there was a big cow named Moo-Moo. Moo-Moo lived on a farm with many friends. They all liked to eat tasty hay and play in the sun. One day, Moo-Moo saw a little rabbit named Hop-Hop. Hop-Hop was lying on Moo-Moo's hay. Moo-Moo said, "Hop-Hop, this is my hay. Please move." But Hop-Hop did not want to move. Hop-Hop said, "No, I like this hay. It is soft and warm." Moo-Moo was sad, but she had an idea. She said, "Hop-Hop, let's share the hay. We can both lie on it and eat it together." Hop-Hop agreed and they both enjoyed the tasty hay. From that day, Moo-Moo and Hop-Hop became good friends and always shared their hay.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She loved to mix things together. She found a big stone and decided to mix it with some dirt. She started to mix them together. Suddenly, something difficult happened. The stone was stuck in the dirt and the little girl couldn't get it out. She was very sad and started to cry. Her mom came running over to her and said, "What have you done?" The little girl said, "I was just trying to mix the stone and dirt together." Her mom said, "It's too late now. You should have been careful". The little girl was very sad because she couldn't get the stone out. She tried many times but it was too difficult. The little girl never played with stones again. She learned her lesson and was very careful when she mixed things together.
