Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bear and the Woods

Once upon a time, there was a big bear who got lost in the woods. The bear was looking for a way to escape. Suddenly, he saw a hidden cave in the distance. He ran towards it, hoping the cave would lead him to safety. As he was running, he dropped his hat. He stopped to pick it up, but then he heard a loud noise from behind him. It was a giant monster! The bear was terrified and tried to run away from the monster. But the monster was so fast that it caught up to him and pushed him into the cave. The entrance to the cave closed shut, trapping the bear inside and the monster outside. The bear was all alone and he couldn't find a way to escape. He was stuck in the cave forever, and he never found a way to get out. The end.

Lily and Ben were playing with blocks in the living room. They liked to build towers and castles and bridges. But sometimes they fought over the blocks and knocked down each other's creations. "Stop it, Ben!" Lily shouted. "You broke my tower! That was not nice!" "I wanted the red block!" Ben said. "You had too many! It's not fair!" Mom heard the noise and came to see what was wrong. She saw the broken blocks and the angry faces of her children. "What happened here?" Mom asked. "Why are you fighting?" Lily and Ben both tried to tell their side of the story, but they talked at the same time and Mom could not understand them. "Shh, shh, shh," Mom said. "One at a time. Lily, you go first. Tell me the truth. What did Ben do?" Lily told Mom how Ben took the red block from her tower and made it fall down. She said he was mean and rude. Mom nodded and turned to Ben. "Ben, is that the truth? What did you do?" Ben told Mom how Lily had too many blocks and he wanted one. He said he was sorry and he did not mean to break her tower. Mom looked at both of them and said, "I see. You both wanted the same block, but you did not ask or share. You both made a mistake and hurt each other's feelings. That was not nice. How can you prevent this from happening again?" Lily and Ben thought for a moment. Then they said, "We can ask before we take a block. We can share and trade blocks. We can say sorry and help each other fix the towers." Mom smiled and said, "That's right. Those are good ways to prevent fights and be kind. I'm proud of you for telling the truth and learning from your mistakes. Now, why don't you hug and make up, and then you can play together again?" Lily and Ben hugged and said sorry to each other. They felt better and happier. They picked up the broken blocks and started to build new towers and castles and bridges. They asked and shared and traded and helped. They had fun and did not fight anymore. Mom watched them and smiled. She was glad they had learned to prevent problems and tell the truth.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was very obedient and always did what her parents told her. One day, her parents took her to a beautiful beach to watch the sunset. She was amazed at the colours in the sky and the bright sun. When it was time to leave, her father said to her, "Lucy, hold my hand so that you don't get lost in the dark". She happily held his hand and walked back home. When they reached home, her father said to her, "Lucy, you are very obedient. I'm proud of you". Lucy smiled and gave her father a big hug. The next day, they went to the beach again and watched the sunset. Lucy held her father's hand and they watched the beautiful colours in the sky. At the end of the day, Lucy's father said, "That was a wonderful sunset. I'm so glad you were obedient today." Lucy smiled and said, "Me too".
