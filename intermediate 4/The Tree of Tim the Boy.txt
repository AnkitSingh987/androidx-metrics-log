Category: Intermediate
Title: The Tree of Tim the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to imagine he could fly high in the sky like a bird. One day, Tim found a card on the ground. The card had a picture of a big, high tree on it. Tim thought the tree looked fun to climb. Tim went to find the high tree. He saw it in a park. He started to climb the tree. He went up and up, higher and higher. Tim imagined he was flying like a bird. But then, the tree branch he was on broke. Tim fell down from the high tree. He was hurt and sad. The card with the tree picture was not fun anymore. Tim learned that climbing high trees can be dangerous. From that day on, he only imagined flying like a bird, but never climbed high trees again.

Once upon a time, there was a little monkey named Momo. Momo loved to eat bananas. One day, Momo saw a big, yellow banana on the ground. Momo's friend, Lulu the bird, saw the banana too. Lulu was very smart. She said, "Momo, be slow! I want to warn you. That banana might not be good." Momo listened to Lulu and walked slow to the banana. When they got closer, they saw that the banana was old and brown. Momo was happy he listened to Lulu's warning. Together, Momo and Lulu found a new, yummy banana to share. They were very happy and ate the banana together. Momo learned that it is good to listen to warnings from friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to pack his bag for school every day. He would put his books, pencils, and lunch in his bag. Tim's mom would always pack a delicious snack for him to eat at school. One day, Tim saw a strip of paper on the ground. He picked it up and saw that it was a map. The map showed a path to a big tree in the park. Tim was excited and wanted to find the tree with his friends. Tim and his friends followed the map to the park. They walked and walked until they found the big tree. Under the tree, they found a box. Inside the box were more delicious snacks for them to share. Tim and his friends had a fun day at the park, eating snacks and playing together. And from that day on, they always looked for more adventures together.

John was a strong little boy who was 3 years old. He was playing in the park when he suddenly heard the ringing of a phone. He stopped and listened. "Hello? Who's this?" said a voice on the phone. John had never heard this voice before. John walked over to the phone and said, "It's me, John." The voice laughed. "Hi there! What can I do for you?" John thought for a moment, then said, "I'd like a sandwich. Can you help me?" The voice replied, "Sure, I can help you with that. What kind of sandwich would you like?" John smiled, "I'd like a peanut butter and jelly sandwich. Please!" The voice said, "OK. I'll see what I can do! Goodbye." John said goodbye and hung up the phone. He was so happy! He knew he was very strong, and he also knew that if he listened carefully, he would get all the help he needed.

One day, a little girl named Sally went for a walk. She saw a wide tree with big branches. Under the tree, she found a small figure. It was a toy bunny! Sally picked up the toy bunny and started to talk to it. "Hi, bunny! My name is Sally. Do you want to be my friend?" To her surprise, the toy bunny talked back! "Yes, Sally! I would love to be your friend!" From that day on, Sally and the toy bunny were best friends. They talked and played together every day under the wide tree. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Amy went for a walk. She saw a big, impressive tree. Amy wanted to climb the tree, but she was too small. So, she tried to stretch her arms up high to reach the branches. A little boy named Tim saw her and said, "What are you doing, Amy?" She told him, "I want to climb this big tree, but I can't reach." Tim had an idea. He said, "I have a big lollipop. Maybe we can use it to help you reach." Amy and Tim tied the lollipop to a long stick. They used the stick to reach the tree branches. Amy climbed up the tree with the help of the stick. When she was at the top, she found a big nest with a surprise. Inside the nest, there was a big, shiny egg. Amy and Tim didn't know what was inside the egg. They took it down from the tree and the egg started to crack. Out came a little baby dragon! The baby dragon smiled at Amy and Tim, and they knew they had made a new friend. They were so happy their lollipop helped them find the dragon.
