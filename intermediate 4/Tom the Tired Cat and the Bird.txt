Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Tired Cat and the Bird

One day, a tired cat named Tom was walking in the woods. He saw a little house and decided to enter it. Inside the house, there was a big chimney. Tom thought it was a good place to rest. Tom climbed up the chimney and found a cozy spot to sleep. While he was sleeping, a little girl named Lucy came home. She saw Tom's tail hanging out of the chimney and said, "Hello, who are you?" Tom woke up and said, "I am Tom, the tired cat. I needed a place to rest." Lucy smiled and said, "You can stay with me, Tom." Just then, a bird flew out of the chimney, surprising Tom and Lucy. The bird said, "This is my home too! Can I stay with you both?" Tom and Lucy laughed and said, "Of course, you can stay with us!" And they all became the best of friends.

One day, a little crab named Tim found an empty shell. He thought it would be nice to wear it. So, he picked it up and put it on his back. Tim felt happy and proud wearing his new shell. As Tim walked along the beach, he met a big crab named Sam. Sam saw Tim's new shell and said, "Wow, Tim! I like your new shell. Where did you find it?" Tim smiled and said, "I found it on the beach. It was empty and I decided to wear it." Sam and Tim played together all day, running and laughing on the beach. They both enjoyed the sun and the soft sand under their feet. When it was time to go home, they said goodbye and promised to play together again soon. Tim went home feeling happy, knowing he had a new friend and a beautiful new shell to wear.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing in his room. He had many toys, but his favorite was a noisy toy weapon. It was a toy sword that made loud sounds when he swung it. Tim loved to pretend he was a brave knight, protecting his castle from dragons. Tim's little sister, Lily, was scared of the noisy toy weapon. She would cry when Tim played with it. Tim didn't want his sister to be sad, so he tried to comfort her. He told her that the toy sword was not real and it was just for fun. Tim decided to show Lily that the toy sword was not scary. He let her hold it and swing it around. At first, she was scared, but then she saw that it was just a toy. They both played together and had lots of fun. Lily was not scared of the noisy toy weapon anymore, and Tim was happy that he could comfort his sister.

Lily loved to whistle. She whistled all the time, when she played, when she ate, when she brushed her teeth. She whistled so much that her mom and dad sometimes asked her to be quiet. But Lily did not mind. She liked to make music with her mouth. One day, Lily was whistling in her room, when she heard a knock on the window. She looked and saw a big white bird with shiny wings. It was an angel! Lily was surprised and happy. She opened the window and said hello. "Hello, little girl," the angel said. "I heard your whistling and I came to see you. You have a beautiful voice." "Thank you, angel," Lily said. "Do you like to whistle too?" "Yes, I do," the angel said. "Whistling is a way to praise God and to share joy. Would you like to whistle with me?" Lily nodded. She was very excited. She and the angel whistled together, making incredible sounds. They whistled high and low, fast and slow, loud and soft. They whistled songs and tunes and melodies. They whistled until the sun set and the stars came out. Lily felt very happy. She had never whistled with anyone before. She thanked the angel for coming and asked if he would come again. "Of course, I will," the angel said. "You are my whistling friend. I will come whenever you whistle for me. Just remember to be kind and grateful and to love God and your family." Lily smiled and hugged the angel. She said goodbye and closed the window. She went to bed and dreamed of whistling with the angel again. She felt very blessed. She was a whistling girl with an angel friend.
