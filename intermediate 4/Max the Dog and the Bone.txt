Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Bone

One day, a little dog named Max saw a big sign. The sign had a picture of a bone on it. Max was very hungry, so he thought the sign was a real bone. He wanted to chew on the bone. Max said to his friend, the cat, "Look, a big bone! I want to chew on it." His friend, the cat, said, "No, Max, that is not a real bone. It is just a sign. You are crazy!" But Max did not listen. He wanted to chew on the bone, even if it was just a sign. Max ran to the sign and started to chew on it. But the sign was not a real bone, so Max hurt his teeth. Max cried and cried. His friend, the cat, said, "I told you it was not a real bone." Max was sad because he did not listen to his friend. The end.

Once there was a boy who had a medal. He was very proud of it. He kept it very clean and he liked to show it to everyone. One day, he was playing with some friends. He wanted to show them his medal. So he grabbed it and held it up high. His friends were very impressed! "Wow! That's amazing!" they said. The boy was so pleased that he bowed. He wanted to thank them for looking at it. Then he put his medal away, so it would stay clean. The boy was happy. He had shown his medal to all his friends, and now they knew how special it was!

Once upon a time, there was a little mole. The mole lived in a hole in the ground. The hole was his home. One day, the mole saw a big box. He thought, "I wonder if I can fit in that box." The mole went to the box and tried to fit inside. He pushed and pushed, but he could not fit. The mole felt sad. Just then, a little bird flew down from a distant tree. The bird said, "Why are you sad, mole?" The mole told the bird about the box. The bird said, "Maybe we can find a smaller box that you can fit in." So, the mole and the bird looked for a new box. They found a smaller box, and the mole fit inside. The mole was happy, and the bird was happy too. They played together all day long.

Once upon a time, in a big field, there was a rough ostrich named Ollie. Ollie was not like the other ostriches. He had a hard time making friends because his feathers were not as soft and pretty as the others. This made Ollie sad. One day, a group of mean birds came to the field. They wanted to take over and make it their home. The other ostriches were scared and didn't know what to do. Ollie knew he had to help, even if he was different. Ollie called all the ostriches together. He told them they had to unite and work together to make the mean birds leave. The ostriches listened to Ollie and joined together. They all worked as a team and chased the mean birds away. In the end, Ollie's rough feathers didn't matter. The ostriches learned that being united and working together was more important. And Ollie finally made many friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a girl named Amy. She had a big pumpkin in her yard. Amy loved her pumpkin and took care of it every day. The pumpkin grew bigger and bigger. One day, Amy heard a noise coming from the pumpkin. "Welcome," said the pumpkin. Amy was very surprised. She did not know that pumpkins could talk! The pumpkin told Amy that it was a magic pumpkin. It could make her wishes come true. Amy was very happy and excited. That night, it was very dark outside. Amy wished for a big, bright light to light up her yard. The magic pumpkin made her wish come true! The yard became bright and shiny. Amy's friends saw the light and came to see what happened. They were all amazed by the magic pumpkin. Amy was happy to share her magic pumpkin with her friends, and they all played together in the bright, shiny yard.
