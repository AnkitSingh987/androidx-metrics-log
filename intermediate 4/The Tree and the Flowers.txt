Category: Intermediate
Title: The Tree and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a very old tree. It had big branches and many leaves. The old tree lived in a big garden with many flowers. The flowers loved to bloom and show their pretty colors. One day, the flowers heard about an operation. They were scared and did not know what it was. They asked the old tree for help. The old tree was wise and knew about the operation. It told the flowers that the operation was to make them even more beautiful. The flowers were excited and waited for the operation. The next day, something unexpected happened. The flowers woke up to find that they had new colors! The operation was a success, and the flowers were happier than ever. The old tree smiled, happy to see the flowers bloom in their new, pretty colors.

One day, a big dog named Zip lived in a town. He had rough fur and loved to play. In the town, there was a law that dogs must play with their friends every day. Zip was very happy about this law. One morning, Zip met his friend, a small cat named Kit. Kit said, "I heard there will be a big surprise at the park today!" Zip was excited and wanted to see the surprise. They both went to the park to play with their friends. At the park, Zip and Kit found a huge pile of toys. The surprise was a big toy party for all the animals in the town! They played all day and had lots of fun. Zip was so glad that the law made them play together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She liked to play with her toy bear, named Teddy. One sunny day, Lily and Teddy went to the park. At the park, Lily saw a big tree with a piece of paper stuck to it. Lily felt confused. She walked closer to the tree and took the paper. It had a picture of Teddy on it! Lily looked at Teddy and he had a frown on his face. Just then, a boy came running to Lily. He said, "That's my Teddy's picture! I lost him!" Lily looked at her Teddy, then at the boy, and then back at her Teddy. She had an idea! "Let's go find your Teddy together!" And off they went, two new friends on a big adventure.

Anna likes to help her mom in the kitchen. She wants to be a good cook like her mom. She puts on a pink apron and stands on a stool. She helps mom stir the soup and cut the bread. But then, Anna sees a big knife on the counter. She wants to use the knife too. She reaches for it with her hand. Mom sees her and says, "No, Anna, no! The knife is sharp and dangerous. You can hurt yourself. Put it down now!" Anna is scared and drops the knife. It makes a loud noise on the floor. Mom hugs her and says, "It's okay, Anna. I'm sorry I yelled at you. I just want you to be safe. The knife is not for you. You can use the spoon and the fork. They are safe and fun." Anna says, "I'm sorry, mom. I didn't know the knife was bad. I just wanted to help you. I love you, mom." Mom says, "I love you too, Anna. You are a good helper. And you look very pretty in your pink apron. Come on, let's finish the soup and the bread. They smell yummy." Anna smiles and nods. She helps mom in the kitchen again. She feels happy and safe.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his furry dog, Max, were playing in the house. They were having fun running and laughing. Max liked to kick a ball, and Tim liked to watch television. Tim said, "Max, let's play with the ball!" Max wagged his tail and jumped up and down. Tim threw the ball, and Max ran to get it. Max kicked the ball with his paw, but it went the wrong way! The ball flew through the air and hit the television! Tim and Max were both very surprised. They looked at the television, and it was still working. They were happy that nothing bad happened. Tim and Max learned to be more careful when playing inside the house. They decided to play outside instead, where there was more space. They had fun kicking the ball and running in the sun.
