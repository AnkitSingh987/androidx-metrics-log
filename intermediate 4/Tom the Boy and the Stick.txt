Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Stick

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tom. Tom loved to play a game called golf. He would hit a small ball with a stick and try to get it into a hole. Tom played golf every day, even when the sky was gloomy and gray. One gloomy day, Tom went to play golf with his friend, Sue. They were having lots of fun, but then they had to pause. They saw a little lost puppy in the park. The puppy was wet and cold from the rain. Tom and Sue wanted to help the puppy. They took the puppy home and gave it a warm bath. They played with the puppy and gave it some food. The puppy was very happy. Tom and Sue named the puppy "Golfy" because they found it while playing golf. Now, Tom, Sue, and Golfy play golf together on sunny and gloomy days.

Once there was a boy named Josh. He was only three years old but he always thought about fun things to do. One day he had a great idea. He grabbed a small slide from his backyard and brought it inside his house. Josh asked his mom, "Can I please slide inside my house?". His mom said, "Yes, but you have to be very careful". So Josh slid around the house. He was so happy and excited! Josh's mom and dad said, "We have a great opinion about your sliding". Josh was so proud of his sliding skills. Everyone was so happy for him. The next day, Josh went to visit his rich friend's house. He saw that his friend also had a slide inside his home. Josh asked, "Can I also slide in your house?". His friend said, "Yes, of course!". So Josh slid around his friend's house. It was such a great day. Josh thanked his friend for letting him slide with him. They both had a great time. It was the best day ever!

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy cat named Mimi. Mimi liked to sleep all day and all night. One day, Mimi was taking a nap under a tree when she heard a noise. Mimi opened her eyes and saw a little bird. The bird said, "Hi, Mimi! Can you help me move my nest to another tree? The tree I live in is going to have a shower soon, and I don't want my nest to get wet." Mimi was very sleepy, but she wanted to help the bird. So she got up, stretched her legs, and helped the bird move the nest to a new tree. The bird was very happy and thanked Mimi. After they moved the nest, Mimi and the bird saw the shower come down on the old tree. They were glad the nest was safe and dry in the new tree. Mimi went back to her nap, and the bird sang a sweet song to help her sleep. They both had a good day, and Mimi learned that helping others can be fun, even when you're sleepy.

One day, a big dog and a little cat went to play near a fountain. They liked to watch the water go up and down. The dog was happy, but the cat was not. The cat saw a fake bird sitting on the fountain and wanted it. The dog and the cat started to fight about who would get the fake bird. They jumped and ran around the fountain. The dog barked and the cat hissed. They both wanted the bird very much. In the end, the dog and the cat stopped fighting. They knew that they should share. So, they played with the fake bird together. They had a lot of fun and became good friends. They went to play near the fountain every day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big, strong tornado. The tornado was very filthy because it picked up all the dirt from the ground. The tornado moved around the town making a lot of noise. In the town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was scared of the noisy, filthy tornado. He wanted to find a safe place to hide. So, Tim looked for a place where he could fit and be safe from the tornado. Tim found a small house with a tiny door. He tried to fit through the door, and he did! Inside, Tim felt safe from the noisy, filthy tornado. Soon, the tornado went away, and the town was quiet again. Tim was happy that he found a place to fit and be safe.
