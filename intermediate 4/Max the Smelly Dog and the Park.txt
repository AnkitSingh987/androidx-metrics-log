Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Smelly Dog and the Park

One day, a smelly dog named Max went to the park. He saw a cat named Lily. Max wanted to play with Lily, but Lily did not like Max because he was smelly. Max felt sad and said, "I want to play with you, but I am smelly. What can I do?" Lily thought for a moment and said, "You can quit being smelly if you take a bath. Then we can play together." Max agreed and went to take a bath. After the bath, Max was not smelly anymore. Max and Lily started to play together in the park. They had so much fun. After playing, Max gave Lily a massage to help her relax. They became good friends and played together every day. And Max never forgot to take a bath, so he would not be smelly again.

Once upon a time, in a peaceful town, there lived a man named Tom. Tom loved to design things. He would draw and build all day. One day, Tom had an idea to make something new. He wanted to make a big tire to play with. Tom worked very hard to make the tire. He used round things and strong things to make it. When the tire was done, it was big and fun to play with. Tom rolled the tire down a hill and watched it go fast. All the kids in the town saw the tire and wanted to play too. They took turns rolling the tire down the hill. Everyone had fun and laughed a lot. Tom was happy that his tire made the kids happy. The peaceful town was full of joy because of Tom's big tire.

Once upon a time, there was a large bird named Bobo. Bobo loved to fly high in the sky. One day, he met a small bird named Lulu. Lulu was sad because she could not fly as high as Bobo. Bobo said, "Lulu, do not be sad. I will help you fly high in the sky. But first, we must find a big tree to jump from." They looked and looked, and soon they found a big tree. Lulu was scared, but Bobo told her, "Do not forget, I am here to help you." Lulu jumped from the tree and tried to fly. At first, she fell, but Bobo caught her. They tried again and again. Finally, Lulu could fly high in the sky. She was so happy, and she said, "Thank you, Bobo, for helping me. I will never forget you." And they flew together, high in the sky, as best friends.

Lila and Tom were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump and slide. They saw a chubby squirrel on a tree. The squirrel had a big nut in its mouth. "Look, Tom, a squirrel!" Lila said. "Can we catch it?" Tom asked. They ran to the tree. The squirrel saw them and ran away. It jumped to another tree and then to the ground. It ran along a trail of dirt and leaves. "Come on, Lila, let's follow it!" Tom said. They followed the squirrel on the trail. They tried to be quiet, but they were too loud. The squirrel heard them and ran faster. It seemed to know where it was going. "Where is it going?" Lila wondered. "Maybe it has a home," Tom guessed. They followed the trail until they came to a big bush. The squirrel disappeared inside the bush. They heard a rustle and a squeak. "Hello, squirrel, are you there?" Lila called. No answer. They peeked inside the bush. They saw a hole in the ground. The squirrel had gone inside the hole. "Wow, it has a hole!" Tom said. "Maybe it has a family," Lila said. They waited for a while, but the squirrel did not come out. They decided to leave it alone. "Let's go back to the park," Lila said. "OK, maybe we can see another squirrel," Tom said. They ran back to the park. They had fun playing and looking for squirrels. They liked the chubby squirrel and its trail. They hoped it was happy in its hole.

Once upon a time, there was a bossy frog who lived near a pond. He loved to splash and play all day long. He had many friends, but sometimes he would tell them what to do. One sunny day, a small insect came to the pond. The insect wanted to play with the frog and his friends. The bossy frog told the insect to jump and splash like him. But the insect was too small to jump and splash like the frog. The other animals saw that the insect was sad. They told the bossy frog to be nice and let the insect play in his own way. The bossy frog said sorry, and they all played together happily. The frog learned that being bossy was not fun, and everyone had a great day at the pond.
