Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Tom and the Flower

Lily and Tom were playing in the backyard. They found a big hammer and a small flower. Lily wanted to hammer the flower, but Tom said no. "That flower is delicate. You will break it. It is pretty and smells good. Let it grow," Tom said. "But I want to see what happens. Maybe it will shrink and become a tiny flower. Then I can put it in my dollhouse," Lily said. She lifted the hammer and swung it at the flower. But Tom was faster. He grabbed the hammer and pulled it away from Lily. The hammer was heavy and Tom lost his balance. He fell on the flower and squashed it. "Look what you did! You broke the flower. It did not shrink. It is gone," Lily cried. Tom felt sorry. He did not mean to hurt the flower. He wanted to make Lily happy. "I'm sorry, Lily. I did not want to break the flower. I wanted to save it. Maybe we can find another flower. Or we can plant a seed and grow a new one," Tom said. Lily stopped crying. She hugged Tom and said, "It's okay, Tom. You are a good brother. I don't need a hammer or a tiny flower. I have you."

Once upon a time, there was a unique snowflake named Hope. Hope was very special because she was not like the other snowflakes. She was bigger and had more lines on her. In the winter, Hope and her snowflake friends would fall from the sky and play on the ground. They would make snowmen and have snowball fights. Hope was always the best at making snowmen because of her size. One day, Hope and her friends fell on a little girl's coat. The girl saw Hope and thought she was so pretty. The girl picked up Hope and showed her mom. The mom and the girl smiled at Hope. Hope felt very happy to make them smile. And from that day on, Hope knew she was unique and loved being a winter snowflake.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a red ball that she loved to play with. One day, she went to the park with her mom and dad. They were all very happy. At the park, Amy saw a big cross. She wanted to see it up close. She asked her mom and dad if she could go. They said yes, but told her to be careful. Amy was a persistent girl, so she walked carefully to the cross. When she got to the cross, she saw a small bug on the ground. Amy kneeled down to look at the bug. It was crawling slowly. She watched the bug for a long time, and then she went back to her mom and dad. They were proud of her for being careful and persistent. They all went home, and Amy had a great day at the park.

One day, a funny crocodile named Bob lived in a big river. He had many friends, like fish, birds, and frogs. They all liked to play and have fun together. One day, Bob and his friend, Tim the frog, wanted to measure how long they were. Tim said, "I am very short, but you are very long!" They took a long stick and measured each other. Bob was much longer than Tim. Then, they saw a big boat coming down the river. They thought it would be fun to play with the boat. Bob said, "Let's go near the boat and say hello!" Tim agreed, and they swam closer to the boat. As they got closer, the boat was much bigger than they thought. They did not know that the boat was dangerous. The boat hit Bob the crocodile, and he was hurt very badly. Tim the frog was very sad because his friend Bob was hurt. From that day on, Tim and the other animals learned to be careful around big boats. They missed their funny friend, Bob the crocodile, and remembered the fun times they had together.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to play outside. She saw her bike with the pretty red pedal. She wanted to ride her bike, but her mom said, "Amy, you need to cover your head with a helmet first." Amy put on her helmet and started to ride her bike. As she rode, she saw a big ice cream truck. She wanted to buy an ice cream, but she only had one coin. She picked a big, sweet, and cold ice cream cone. The ice cream man gave her the cone and said, "Be careful, don't let it spoil." Amy rode her bike home with the ice cream cone in her hand. But as she rode, the sun was very hot. When she got home, she saw that her ice cream was all spoiled and melted. "Oh no!" she cried, "My ice cream is gone!" Just then, her mom came out and saw the sad little girl. "Don't worry, Amy," her mom said, "I have a surprise for you!" She took Amy inside and showed her a big ice cream cake. "We can share this with your friends at your party today!" Amy smiled and hugged her mom. The spoiled ice cream was not so bad after all. She had a fun party with her friends and the big ice cream cake.
