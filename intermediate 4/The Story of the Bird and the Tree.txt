Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a pretty bird with attractive feathers. It liked to swing on a branch near a big tree. Every day, the bird would swing and sing happy songs. One day, while the bird was swinging, it saw some food on the ground. The food looked very yummy. The bird wanted to eat the food, but it was scared to go down from the tree. The bird thought and thought. It was very hungry, so it decided to fly down and eat the food. But when the bird got close to the food, a big cat jumped out and caught the bird. The bird was sad and the cat ate the bird.

One day, a boy named Tom found a whistle. It was a nice whistle. Tom was very happy. He wanted to show it to his friends. He ran to them very fast. He was dizzy from running so fast. Tom showed the whistle to his friends. They all liked it too. They wanted to play with the whistle. Tom blew the whistle. It made a loud sound. Everyone was surprised. They did not care. They liked the sound. Then, something unexpected happened. A big dog came running. The dog heard the whistle. The dog was friendly. It wanted to play with Tom and his friends. They all played together and had a great day. The whistle made a new friend for them.

---

One day, a little boy named Tim went with his mom to get a haircut. He was a bit scared because he thought it would be difficult. When they got to the haircut place, they had to wait for their turn. As Tim sat in the chair and the haircut started, he closed his eyes. But then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew in through the window and landed on Tim's head. It started to sing a pretty song. The bird's song made Tim feel happy, and he wasn't scared anymore. The haircut was not difficult at all. In fact, Tim liked it. When the haircut was done, the bird flew away and Tim waved goodbye. From that day on, Tim loved getting haircuts and always hoped to see the little bird again.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear named Bob. Bob was tired and wanted to relax. He thought about going on a vacation. He wanted to feel the smooth sand under his feet. Bob went to the beach for his vacation. He saw the smooth water and the bright sun. He felt happy. Bob played in the sand and built a big sand house. He splashed in the water with his friends. At the end of the day, Bob was very relaxed. He loved his vacation at the beach. He went home with a big smile on his face. Bob could not wait for his next vacation to the beach.

Once upon a time there were two little sisters. They were playing with their toys and talking about their favourite movies. One of them pointed to a unique toy and said, "It looks like a movie!" The other sister smiled and said, "Let's play number and pretend the toy is a movie. We can make up our own story." The two sisters talked about the characters in their own movie and decided to make up one with a very special character. They chose a number for the character and told themselves the story about her. The sisters made up an exciting and unique movie about their special character who went on an adventure. They both smiled and hugged each other when their movie was over. "That was so much fun!" the sisters said, as they ran off to play with their toys once more.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin lived in the big sea. One day, Fin saw a big wave coming. He was troubled. He swam fast to tell his friend, the kind whale. "Whale, whale!" said Fin. "Big wave is coming. We must swim away!" Whale looked at the wave and smiled. "Do not worry, little Fin," he said. "I will help you." Whale opened his big mouth and took in all the water from the wave. Then, he blew it up into the sky. It started to rain. The rain made the sea calm again. Fin was happy. "Thank you, Whale," he said. "You saved us all!" From that day on, Fin and Whale were best friends. They played and swam together in the calm sea, and they were never troubled by big waves again.
