Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Fries

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a stubborn boy. He didn't like to share his toys with his friends. One day, Tim's mom took him to a restaurant. There, Tim saw a big plate of yummy fries. In the restaurant, Tim met a new friend named Sam. Sam also liked fries. Tim's mom told him to share the fries with Sam. But Tim didn't want to share. He wanted all the fries for himself. Tim's mom told him that sharing is good. So, Tim tried to share the fries with Sam. They both ate the fries together and had a lot of fun. Tim learned that sharing is fun and makes friends happy. From that day on, Tim was not stubborn anymore and liked to share his toys and food with his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red truck. It had a very serious job. It carried lots of toys to the store. One day, the truck driver had to unlock the truck to put more toys inside. He was in a hurry. As he unlocked the truck, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all the toys away! The truck driver was very sad. He needed to find the toys and put them back in the truck. He looked and looked for the toys. Then, he saw a big pile of toys under a tree. But, surprise! The toys were now alive and playing with each other. The truck driver laughed and joined them. They all played together and had lots of fun. The toys went back to the truck, and they all went to the store, happy and smiling.

Once there was a little boy called Joey. Joey was three years old and he loved playing in the sunshine. One day Joey was walking in the woods when he spotted a little bird. He wanted to hold it in his hands, so he tried to pinch it. Suddenly, the bird flew away. Joey felt sad. He thought he had been too rough with the bird. His dad saw what happened and came over to him. “Joey,” his dad said gently, “you shouldn’t pinch animals. They are scared when you do that. We should be gentle with them.” Joey nodded. From then on he was more careful when playing in the woods. He enjoyed playing among the mild flowers, soft tree trunks and friendly insects. He never forgot the lesson his dad taught him and he was gentle with all the animals he met on his walks in the woods.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mommy and daddy. Lily loved to eat cherries. One day, she saw a cherry tree in her yard. It was not a regular tree. It was very big and had many cherries on it. Lily was very happy. Lily went to her mommy and said, "Mommy, can I pick cherries from the tree?" Her mommy smiled and said, "Yes, you can. But be careful not to fall." Lily went outside and picked cherries from the tree. She ate some and they were very yummy. Then she saw a big cherry at the top of the tree. It did not seem like the other cherries. It was very big and shiny. Lily wanted to pick it. Lily climbed higher and higher. She was scared but she wanted the big cherry. When she got to the top, she picked the big cherry and held it tight. Then she climbed down carefully. Lily showed the big cherry to her mommy and daddy. They were very proud of her. They all ate the big cherry together and it was the yummiest cherry ever. From that day on, Lily knew that she was brave and could do anything she wanted.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a thick book called a passport. This passport was very special to him. He liked to look at the pictures and words in it. One day, Tim saw his friends perform a fun dance. They jumped, clapped, and laughed. Tim wanted to perform with them, so he put his passport in his pocket and joined the dance. They all danced and laughed together. Tim was very happy. After the dance, he showed his friends the thick passport. They all looked at it and smiled. Tim felt proud and glad to have his passport and his friends.
