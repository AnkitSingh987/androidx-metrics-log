Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Window

Once upon a time, there was a small girl named Mia. She loved to spin around the living room and look out the windows. One day, when Mia spun around she saw a dove fly past her window. Excitedly, Mia raced outside to look for it. Mia searched the bushes but couldn’t find the dove. Suddenly, she saw something disgusting coming from the shed. "What could that be?" she wondered. Mia slowly stepped closer and peeked in. The shed was full of strange looking birds. Even though some of them were beautiful, there were also some birds that had big and ugly feathers. Mia waved her hand, trying to chase them away. To her surprise, the dove flew out! Mia smiled and ran after it. She wanted to make the dove her friend. But, try as she could, she could not catch it. “Come back soon!” she shouted. The dove seemed to understand, for it flew overhead and watched Mia before flying away. With a twinkle in her eye, Mia spun around and giggled. She knew that the dove would come back to her soon.

Once upon a time, in a colorful forest, there lived a little squirrel named Sammy. Sammy loved to play all day long. He would jump from tree to tree and chase his friends. One day, Sammy's mom told him, "Sammy, you must behave and be careful when you play. You don't want to have a fall from the tree." Sammy nodded his head and promised to be careful. As Sammy played with his friends, he saw a beautiful, colorful bird. He wanted to say hello, so he jumped to the bird's tree. But Sammy forgot to be careful. He slipped and started to fall. Luckily, the colorful bird caught Sammy with her wings. "Thank you for saving me," said Sammy. The bird replied, "You're welcome, Sammy. Just remember to behave and be careful when you play." From that day on, Sammy always remembered to be careful and behave while playing in the colorful forest.

One day, Tom saw a taxi. It was yellow and big. "I want to go for a ride," Tom said. He asked the driver, "Can I ride in the taxi?" The driver said, "Yes, hop in!" Tom and the driver went for a ride. The ride was perfect. Tom was happy. They saw a big tree. The tree had sharp leaves. The driver said, "Look at the sharp leaves! They are pretty." Then, something unexpected happened. The taxi turned into a big toy car! Tom and the driver were surprised. They laughed and said, "This is a fun twist!" They played with the toy car all day long.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play in the park. He saw a big, hairy figure laying on the grass. Tim was curious and walked closer to see what it was. "Hello," said Tim, "Who are you?" The hairy figure looked at Tim and said, "I am a bear, and I am very tired." Tim wanted to help the bear, so he tried to find some food for him. He looked around and found some berries. Tim gave the berries to the bear, but the bear did not like them. The bear was still hungry and tired. Tim felt sad that he could not help the bear. He said, "I am sorry, bear. I tried to help you, but I could not find good food for you." The bear looked at Tim and said, "That is okay, little boy. Thank you for trying." In the end, the bear was still hungry and tired, and Tim could not help him. They both felt sad, and it was a bad day for them.

One day, a wise old cat named Tom went to the store. He wanted to buy soup for his lunch. He found a big can of soup and put it in his bag. Then, he walked back to his little house. On the way home, Tom saw a dog named Max. Max said, "Hey, Tom! Let's play catch!" Tom thought it was a good idea, so he took out the can of soup and threw it to Max. Max caught the soup and threw it back to Tom. But when Max threw the soup, it went too high and hit a bird in the sky. The bird was very surprised and dropped a shiny key. Tom and Max looked at the key and wondered what it could open. They decided to find out together and became best friends. The wise old cat and the playful dog went on a big adventure to find the secret door that the shiny key could open.
