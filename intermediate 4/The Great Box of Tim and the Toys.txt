Category: Intermediate
Title: The Great Box of Tim and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy box. But one day, the toy box was empty. Tim was sad. Tim went to the park to play. There, he found a shiny gold coin. He was very happy. Tim said, "I will use this gold coin to buy new toys." Tim went to the toy store. He gave the gold coin to the store lady. The store lady said, "Thank you, Tim!" Tim picked out some toys and put them in his toy box. Now, the toy box was not empty. Tim was happy. He said, "Thank you, gold coin!" Now, Tim could play with his new toys. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a big castle, there was a queen. She was a very competitive queen. She liked to play games with her friends. One day, she decided to have a big party and play a game with her friends. At the party, the queen said, "Let's play a game. We will all make a pretty display using our toys. The best display will win a prize!" All her friends were excited and started to make their displays. They all worked hard to make the best display. The queen walked around and looked at everyone's displays. "Wow! These are all so good!" she said. In the end, the queen could not pick a winner because all the displays were so nice. So, she gave everyone a prize and they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to go on an adventure. He thought his town was boring and he wanted to see new things. So, Tim asked his mom, "Can we go on a trip?" His mom said, "Yes, but we need to get gas for the car first." Tim was excited and went to the car with his mom. They drove to the gas station and Tim watched as his mom filled the car with gas. After they got gas, Tim and his mom went on a fun trip. They saw big trees, pretty flowers, and happy animals. Tim was not bored anymore. He was happy that he got to go on an adventure with his mom.

One day, a big, brilliant sun was in the sky. It gave a lot of heat. A boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, were playing outside. They were hot and thirsty. Tim said, "Spot, let's have some cold water." Spot barked and wagged his tail. They went inside the house to get a drink. Tim poured water into a bowl for Spot and a cup for himself. They drank the cold water and felt better. Then, a big cloud came and covered the brilliant sun. The heat went away. Tim and Spot looked at each other, surprised. "Wow, Spot! We don't need the water now," Tim said. Spot barked and wagged his tail. They went back outside to play, happy that it was not hot anymore.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a small tent. A tiny ant lived in that tent. The ant liked to spread things on the ground to make her tent look nice. One day, she felt frustrated because she could not find anything to spread. The tiny ant went out of her tent to look for something new. She saw a big tree with many leaves. She thought, "I can use these leaves to spread on the ground in my tent!" So, she started to take the leaves one by one. As she took the last leaf, something unexpected happened. All the leaves turned into shiny gold! The tiny ant was very happy. She learned a lesson that day: when you feel frustrated, don't give up. Keep trying, and you might find something even better than what you wanted.

One day, a boy named Tim found a folder. The folder was old and had a terrible smell. Tim did not like the smell, so he wanted to replace the folder with a new one. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, this folder is terrible. Can we replace it with a new one?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim. But first, let's see what's inside the folder." Inside the folder, they found pictures of a happy family. Tim's mom said, "These pictures are special. We should keep them safe in a new folder." So they went to the store and replaced the terrible folder with a new one. Tim learned that even if something looks or smells terrible, it can still have something special inside. It's important to look past the bad things and find the good.
