Category: Intermediate
Title: The Fast Car and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a fast car. The car liked to go with speed. One day, the car met a fat cat. The cat was very big and slow. The car and the cat became friends. The car said, "I can reveal a secret to you." The cat was excited. "What is the secret?" asked the cat. The car said, "I know where to find a big, yummy fish." The car took the cat to the fish. The cat ate the fish and was very happy. The car and the cat played together every day. They were best friends. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big gray bird. The bird lived near a field of wheat. The bird liked to eat the wheat. One day, a little girl named Anna saw the bird. She wanted to help the bird. Anna said, "Bird, I will cover you with a blanket so you can eat the wheat and stay warm." The bird was happy. Anna put the blanket on the bird. The bird ate the wheat and was warm. The next day, Anna came back to see the bird. The bird said, "Thank you, Anna, for the blanket." Anna smiled and said, "You're welcome, bird. I am happy to help you." The bird and Anna became good friends. They played together near the wheat field every day.

Once upon a time, there was an ugly frog. The frog lived near a big tree. The frog liked to spend time on the tip of a green leaf. One day, a little bird came to the tree. The bird saw the frog and said, "Hi, Mr. Frog! Why are you so ugly?" The frog was sad and replied, "I don't know. I was born this way." The little bird felt bad for the frog. So, they decided to spend time together every day. They played and talked on the tip of the green leaf. Soon, they became best friends. The ugly frog was happy to have a friend who liked him for who he was.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a popular candy store. Everyone loved this store because it had the best mint candies. The store owner, Mr. Brown, made the mint candies himself. One day, a little girl named Lily came into the store. She saw the mint candies and asked, "Mr. Brown, can I have some mint candies, please?" Mr. Brown smiled and said, "Of course, Lily! Here you go!" He gave her a small bag of mint candies. Lily took the candies home and shared them with her friends. They all loved the mint candies. They twisted the wrappers to open them and enjoyed the sweet taste. The mint candies became very popular among the kids, and they always went to Mr. Brown's store to buy more. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little cat went out to dance. The cat loved to dance in the sun. The cat saw a big shelter. The cat did not know what was inside. The little cat was curious. The little cat went into the shelter. Inside, the cat found many unknown animals. The cat said, "Hello! Do you like to dance too?" One of the animals said, "Yes! We love to dance!" Just then, the sun went away and it started to rain. The little cat and the unknown animals were sad. They could not dance in the sun anymore. But then, the cat had an idea. The cat said, "Let's dance inside the shelter!" All the animals were happy. They danced and played inside the shelter until the rain stopped. The little cat made new friends and they all danced together.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Charlie was playing in the park with his two best friends. His friends introduced him to a grape that they had found under a tree. The grape was juicy, round and tasty. All three of them shared the grape and enjoyed it very much. Charlie was very loyal to his best friends and thanked them for sharing the grape with him. Suddenly, out of nowhere, an angry man appeared. He shouted at them and said: "What are you doing here?! This is my grape and you are stealing it from me!". Charlie and his friends were very scared - they didn't know what to do. They apologized to the man and ran away as fast as their little legs could take them. Unfortunately, the man chased them, and caught up with Charlie. He grabbed Charlie by his shirt and said, "You won't get away that easily!". Charlie was very frightened and tearfully asked the man to let him go. But the man ignored him and took him away with him. Charlie's loyal friends watched helplessly as the man took him away and they never saw Charlie again.
