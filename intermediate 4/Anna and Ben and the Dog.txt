Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna and Ben and the Dog

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play with blocks. The blocks have symbols on them. Some symbols are stars, some are hearts, some are circles. One day, Anna and Ben decide to make a tower with the blocks. They are eager to see how high they can build it. They take turns to pick a block and put it on top of the other blocks. "Look, I pick a star block!" Anna says. "It is shiny and pretty." "I pick a heart block!" Ben says. "It is red and warm." They keep picking blocks and adding them to the tower. The tower grows taller and taller. Anna and Ben are happy and proud. But then, something happens. A loud noise makes them jump. It is their dog, Rex. Rex runs into the room and barks. He sees the tower and thinks it is a toy. He jumps and tries to catch a block. "Oh no, Rex!" Anna and Ben shout. "Stop, stop!" But it is too late. Rex knocks down the tower with his paw. The blocks fall and scatter on the floor. The tower is gone. Anna and Ben are sad and angry. They look at Rex. Rex looks at them. He wags his tail and licks their faces. He thinks they are playing with him. "Bad dog, Rex!" Anna and Ben say. "You broke our tower!" But then, they see the blocks. They still have symbols on them. They still can make a tower. "Maybe we can try again," Anna says. "Yes, let's try again," Ben says. They smile and hug Rex. They are not mad at him anymore. He is their friend. They pick up the blocks and start to build a new tower. They are eager to see how high they can build it this time. They hope Rex will not break it again.

One day, a pretty girl named Lily was in her room. She had a small table with paper and scissors. Lily wanted to cut the paper to make a nice picture. She started to cut the paper into shapes like a sun, a tree, and a house. While Lily was cutting, her friend Sam came to visit. He saw the pretty shapes on the table and said, "Wow, Lily! You are good at cutting! Can I try too?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, Sam! You can cut the paper to make a picture with me." As they were cutting, something unexpected happened. A small, friendly mouse came out from under the table. The mouse picked up a tiny piece of paper and started to cut it too. Lily and Sam were surprised and laughed. They all cut the paper together and made a beautiful picture for their room. And from that day, they became best friends with the little mouse.

Once upon a time, there was a big piano in a small room. A little girl named Amy loved to play the piano. She played every day and was very good at it. One day, her friend Tom came to visit her. "Hi Amy," said Tom. "Can you play the piano for me?" "Of course," said Amy with a smile. She started to play her favorite song. The music was so good that Tom started to dance. As Tom danced, he didn't see a toy on the floor. He stepped on the toy and began to fall. Amy saw him and stopped playing the piano. She ran to help him. "Are you okay, Tom?" asked Amy. "Yes, I'm fine," said Tom. "Thank you for helping me." They both laughed and Amy played the piano again. Tom danced carefully this time, and they had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly lady named Sue. She had a big television in her house. Sue liked to watch her television and talk to her friends on the phone. She had a lot of fun every day. One day, while Sue was watching her television, it started to talk! It was a big surprise. Sue didn't know what to do. She was a little scared, but she listened to what the television had to say. The television told Sue that it was magic. It could grant her one wish. Sue thought about what she wanted. She decided to wish for more friends to talk to. The television made her wish come true. Now, Sue had even more friends to talk to and watch the television with. They all had a lot of fun together.
