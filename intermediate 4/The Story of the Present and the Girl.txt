Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Present and the Girl

One day, there was a little girl. She was three years old and she was sitting on a wooden chair. She looked around the room and saw many different wooden things, like the table and the cupboard. She was thinking about one of her favourite activities - making things out of wood. The little girl decided to make something new. She got up from her chair and started looking for the wooden pieces. The little girl was so glad when she found the perfect pieces for her project. She picked up a knife, a saw and some glue. She placed the wooden pieces on the table and started to cut them into shapes. After a few minutes, she had made a little wooden house. She was so proud of her work and she was so happy. The little girl took her masterpiece and showed it to her mum. Her mum was so glad - she said it was a very special present. She hugged the little girl and said: "I'm so proud of you. You make me think of how clever you are." The little girl smiled and said: "I'm glad you like it". She hugged her mum back and ran off to go and show the wooden house to her friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big rug in a small house. The rug was very tight and had many colors. It was in the living room where a happy family played every day. One day, the family got a letter in the mail. It was an invitation to a fun party. The family was very excited. They put the letter on the tight rug and danced around it. The day of the party came, and the family put on their best clothes. They walked on the tight rug one last time before leaving the house. They went to the party, had lots of fun, and made new friends. When they came back home, they sat on the tight rug and talked about the fun they had at the party. The tight rug was always there for the family, making their house feel like a warm and happy home.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a big hill with a slide. They want to go on the slide. But there is a problem. The slide is too high for them. They cannot reach the top. They try to jump, but they fall. They try to climb, but they slip. They are sad. Then they see a big box. The box has a patch on it. The patch has a picture of a rocket. Tom has an idea. He says to Lily, "Let's lift the box and put it under the slide. Then we can stand on the box and go on the slide. It will be easy and fun." Lily likes the idea. They work together to lift the box. The box is heavy, but they are strong. They push and pull and drag the box to the slide. They put the box under the slide. They stand on the box. They are happy. They take turns to go on the slide. They go fast and slow. They go up and down. They laugh and shout. They have a lot of fun. They are glad they lifted the box. They are good friends. The end.

One day, a pink cat named Kitty went for a walk. She saw a big fan in the park. The fan was spinning fast and made a lot of wind. Kitty was curious and wanted to play with the fan. Kitty jumped and touched the fan. Suddenly, the fan stopped spinning. Kitty was surprised and didn't know what to do. She looked around to see if the fan belonged to anyone. A little boy named Timmy came running to the fan. He was happy to see Kitty and said, "Kitty, you stopped the fan! I lost my ball in the park and the fan was blowing it away. Now I can get my ball back!" Kitty was happy that she could help Timmy. The unexpected thing was that Kitty thought she made a mistake, but she really helped her new friend.
