Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Charming Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a charming little dog named Max. Max lived in a small house with his family. Max always had many ideas in his head. One day, Max had an idea to go on an adventure. He wanted to find new friends and see new things. Max walked and walked until he found a big tree. Under the tree, there was a tiny cat. Max wanted to be friends with the cat. He said, "Hello, cat! Do you want to play with me?" But the cat did not want to play. The cat was scared of Max and ran away. Max was sad and wanted to go home. But Max was lost. He looked around and could not find his way back. Max tried to ask for help, but no one was there. In the end, Max stayed lost and could not find his way back home.

Once upon a time, there was a little lamb. She was very alert, always looking out for danger. One day, she saw something that made her curious. A farmer was walking through the field with a wagon full of hay. The little lamb watched him intently, and when the farmer started to walk away, the little lamb decided to follow him. The lamb followed the farmer, who was humming to himself. The little lamb hopped and skipped along, never leaving the farmer's side. As they continued, the farmer looked down and saw the little lamb following him. He smiled and gave her a pat, inviting her to join him. The farmer and the little lamb continued walking, side by side. Eventually they made it to the barn, the wagon full of hay still in tow. The farmer showed the little lamb the hay, and showed her how to sleep on it. From that day, the lamb always wanted to go back to the barn with the farmer and sleep in the hay.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play with toys and eat snacks. One day, they find a big orange in the kitchen. They both want to eat it. "I saw it first!" Anna says. "It is mine!" "No, it is mine!" Ben says. "I am hungry!" They disagree. They pull the orange and yell at each other. They are not happy. Mom hears them and comes to the kitchen. She sees the orange and the friends. She is not happy either. "Why are you fighting?" she asks. "You can share the orange. It is big enough for both of you." "But I want it all!" Anna says. "So do I!" Ben says. Mom shakes her head. She takes the orange and cuts it in half. She gives one half to Anna and one half to Ben. "Here you go," she says. "Now you both have some orange. But you have to be patient and kind with your friends. Fighting is not nice. Sharing is nice. Do you understand?" Anna and Ben look at their orange halves. They look at each other. They feel sorry. "Yes, Mom," they say. "We understand. We are sorry." They hug and say sorry to each other. They eat their orange halves and smile. They are happy again. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Lily liked to help her mom and dad with chores. Her mom would say, "Lily, you are a big help!" One day, Lily's mom asked her to go to the store to buy groceries. Lily was happy to help. At the store, she saw a popular fruit called apples. She thought, "I will buy apples for my mom and dad." Lily went to the store lady and said, "I want to buy these apples, please." The store lady smiled and said, "Thank you, Lily. Your mom and dad will be happy." Lily took the apples home and her mom and dad were very proud of her. They all ate the apples together and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a big red barn on a farm. Inside the barn, there were many animals. There were cows, pigs, and chickens. They all lived happily in the barn. One day, it was a mild day. The sun was shining, and the sky was blue. The farmer decided to store some hay in the barn for the animals to eat. He worked hard to make sure there was enough hay for everyone. The animals were very happy with the fresh hay. They ate it all up and said, "Thank you, farmer!" The farmer smiled and went back to his work. The animals played and slept in the warm barn, knowing they had a good home.
