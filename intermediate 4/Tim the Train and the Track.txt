Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Train and the Track

Once upon a time, there was a little train named Tim. Tim was an adventurous train who loved to go on fun trips. One day, Tim decided to go on a new track that he had never been on before. As Tim went on the new track, he saw many things. He saw a big tree, a small house, and a pretty lake. Tim was very happy to see these things. Then, Tim met another train named Tina. Tina was also an adventurous train who liked to count things. Tina said, "Hi Tim! Do you want to count with me?" Tim said, "Yes, I would love to count with you, Tina!" So, Tim and Tina started to count the things they saw on their trip. They counted one big tree, two small houses, and three pretty lakes. In the end, Tim and Tina had a lot of fun counting things and going on the new track. They became good friends and went on many more adventurous trips together. And they always loved to count the things they saw on their journeys.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to record things with her toy camera. She liked to take pictures of her toys, her family, and her dog, Max. One day, she saw a big object outside her window. It was round and shiny. Mia thought it was very strange and took a picture of it with her toy camera. Mia showed the picture to her mom and dad. They told her it was a big, delicious fruit called an apple. Mia wanted to taste it, but the apple was too high up in the tree. Her mom and dad said they would help her get the apple tomorrow. The next day, Mia, her mom, and her dad went outside to get the apple. They used a long stick to reach the apple in the tree. When the apple fell, Mia caught it. She took a big bite and it was very delicious, just like her mom and dad said. Mia was happy that she recorded the strange object, because it led her to find the yummy apple.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy wand. One day, Tim found a big, expensive wand in his mom's room. He thought it was a toy, but it was a real magic wand! Tim wanted to play with the wand, so he went outside. He saw a bird in a tree and decided to shoot a spell at it. He waved the wand and said, "Shoot, bird!" The bird turned into a big, colorful balloon. Tim was so surprised and happy! Tim played with the magic wand all day. He shot spells at the flowers, the grass, and the sky. Everything changed into fun, new things. When the sun went down, Tim went back inside. He knew the expensive wand was special, so he put it back in his mom's room. From that day on, Tim always remembered the fun he had with the magic wand.

Once upon a time, in a big safari, there lived a little lion named Leo. Leo loved to play with his friends, the elephant and the monkey. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. One day, they decided to play a new game. "Let's play the dizzy game!" said the elephant. They took turns spinning around and around until they felt dizzy. When it was Leo's turn, he spun so fast that everyone laughed. "Wow, Leo, you're so dizzy!" said the monkey. After playing the dizzy game, Leo wanted to try something new. "I want to roar like a big lion!" he said. So, Leo took a deep breath and let out a big roar. His friends clapped and cheered. They all tried to roar like Leo, but no one could roar as loud as him. From that day on, they had more fun playing and roaring together in the safari.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had two toys: a red ball and a blue car. One day, he had to choose which toy to play with. He thought for a moment and picked the red ball. Tim went outside to play, but he left the blue car behind. The blue car felt sad and foolish for not being chosen. The car decided to wait for Tim to come back and play with it. While waiting, the car thought of a plan to be more fun. The next day, Tim went outside to play again. He saw the blue car had a big smile drawn on it. Tim laughed and thought the car looked very funny. He chose to play with the blue car this time. The red ball had to wait, but it knew that Tim would choose it again soon. From that day on, Tim learned to play with both toys and share his time with them. The toys were happy, and they all had fun together. And they all learned that waiting can sometimes lead to good things.
