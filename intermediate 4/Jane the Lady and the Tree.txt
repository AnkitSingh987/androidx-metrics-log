Category: Intermediate
Title: Jane the Lady and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a lady named Jane. Jane was a modern lady who liked to dare herself to do new things. One day, she saw a big tree in the park and thought, "I want to climb that tree." So Jane went to the tree and started to climb. As she climbed higher and higher, a little bird in the tree said, "Hi, lady! You dare to climb this tree? It's so high!" Jane smiled and said, "Yes, little bird. I like to try new things." When Jane got to the top of the tree, she looked around and saw the whole park. She felt happy and proud. Then, she climbed back down and said goodbye to the little bird. From that day on, Jane knew she could do anything she dared to try.

Once upon a time, there was a big, hot sun in the sky. It was so hot that all the animals were very thirsty. One day, a little boy named Tim found a long missile in his toy box. He thought it would be fun to play with. Tim went outside with the missile and saw a bird. The bird was very thirsty because of the hot sun. Tim said, "Hi, bird! I have a missile. Let's play together!" The bird was happy to play with Tim and the missile. They played all day long, and the missile helped them find water to drink. The bird and Tim became best friends. They played with the missile every day, and they stayed cool even when it was very hot outside.

Mia loved to eat cake. She thought of it every day. One day she asked her dad, "Can I have some cake?" "Sure," said her dad. "Let's go and get some now." They took a walk to the baker's. Inside, Mia saw a man with a bald head. She could tell he liked cake too – he was buying some! He gave Mia a friendly smile. Mia thought the man was nice but also a little strange. He didn't have any hair! But Mia said hi and the man smiled back. Mia and her dad left the shop with a big box of cake. Mia was excited to be eating it soon. She waved goodbye to the bald man as they left. Once again, he gave her a nice smile. Mia smiled back as they walked away. She thought it was nice to meet someone who loved cake just as much as she did.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a magazine on the floor. The magazine had many fun pictures and colors. Lily liked looking at the pictures. She saw a picture of a big, soft pillow. Lily thought it looked very comfortable. Lily's mommy saw her looking at the magazine. She asked, "Do you like that pillow, Lily?" Lily said, "Yes, it looks so soft and comfy!" Mommy smiled and said, "We can make one just like it!" Mommy and Lily went to the store. They bought some soft, fluffy stuff and a tight, stretchy cover. They put the fluffy stuff inside the tight cover. It made a big, soft pillow, just like the one in the magazine. Lily hugged the pillow. She felt so comforted and happy. Every night, Lily slept with her new pillow. It was her favorite thing in the whole world.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big box. He did not know what was in the box. Tim wanted to look inside, but he was scared. He thought, "What if there is something serious in the box?" Tim asked his friend, Lily, to come and help him. Lily was not scared. She said, "We will open the box together." Tim and Lily held hands and opened the box. Inside the box, they found many toys! There was nothing serious in the box. Tim and Lily laughed and played with the toys all day. They were happy they opened the box together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was upset because her toy car was broken. The car was made of steel, and it was her favorite toy. She wanted to solve the problem and fix her car. Sue went to her big brother Tom for help. "Tom, can you help me fix my car?" she asked. Tom looked at the steel car and said, "Sure, Sue. Let's solve this problem together." They both sat down and started to work on the car. After a while, they fixed the car together. Sue was so happy that her car was working again. She thanked Tom for his help. "Thank you, Tom! Now we solved the problem, and I'm not upset anymore!" Sue and Tom played with the steel car all day, and they had lots of fun.
