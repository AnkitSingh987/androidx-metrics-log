Category: Intermediate
Title: Lila and Ben and the Blanket

Lila and Ben were twins who liked to play in the attic. The attic was full of old things that belonged to their grandma and grandpa. Some of the things were ancient, like a big clock that did not tick, or a hat with feathers that looked like a bird. One day, they found a big blanket under a pile of books. The blanket was soft and warm, and had many colors and shapes on it. Lila and Ben liked the blanket very much. They decided to make a fort with it. They used chairs and boxes to make the walls of the fort, and draped the blanket over them. Then they crawled inside and pretended they were in a secret cave. They had a lot of fun. "Look, Ben, this shape looks like a star!" Lila said, pointing at the blanket. "And this one looks like a moon!" Ben said, pointing at another shape. They looked at the blanket and tried to find more shapes. They saw a heart, a flower, a fish, and a bird. "Where do you think this blanket came from?" Lila asked. "I don't know. Maybe grandma and grandpa made it when they were young," Ben said. "Maybe they used it to keep warm when they were cold," Lila said. "Maybe they used it to play like us," Ben said. They smiled at each other and hugged the blanket. They felt happy and cozy. They did not know that the blanket was very special. It was a magic blanket that had been passed down from generation to generation. It had the power to make people feel loved and safe. And every time someone used it, the blanket gained a new shape and a new story.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the park. She saw a soft cat sitting on a bench. Amy went to the cat and touched it. The cat felt nice and warm. Suddenly, a lady came running and said, "Stop! Don't touch my cat!" Amy got scared and said, "I am sorry, I just wanted to pet the cat." The lady looked angry and slapped Amy's hand. Amy felt sad and started to cry. A kind man saw what happened and came to help. He said to the lady, "You should not slap a child. She just wanted to play with the cat." The lady felt bad and said sorry to Amy. The kind man gave Amy a hug and she stopped crying. The lady let Amy pet the cat and they all became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his friends. They liked to see who could run the fastest. They were competitive. One day, they decided to play a game of hide and seek. Tim's friend, Sam, was the one to find everyone. Sam said, "I will count to ten, and you all hide!" All the friends ran to find a place to hide. Tim found a big tree and hid behind it. He waited for a long time. Sam looked everywhere for his friends. He found them one by one. Then, it was time to find Tim. Sam walked up to the big tree and yelled, "Boo!" Tim jumped and said, "You scared me!" They both laughed and went to find the rest of their friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a warm fireplace. A little cat sat near the fireplace. The cat felt happy and safe by the fire. One day, a little bird flew into the house. The bird was cold and helpless. The cat saw the bird and wanted to help. The cat and the bird became friends. They sat by the fireplace together to get warm. The cat and the bird played near the fireplace. They made each other laugh with their funny games. They were happy to have a warm place and a good friend. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a taxi. The taxi was fine and bright. It liked to drive around the town. One day, the taxi felt tired. It decided to take a nap under a big tree. The sun was warm, and the taxi slept for a while. When the taxi woke up, it felt fine again. It was ready to drive and help people. So, the taxi went back to work, happy and strong.
