Category: Intermediate
Title: Boo and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a small, frightened bunny named Boo. Boo loved to play with his hoop, but he was scared of the big, noisy dog that lived next door. One sunny day, Boo wanted to play with his hoop outside, but the dog was there too. Boo's friend, a kind bird named Lily, saw that Boo was sad. "Why are you sad, Boo?" asked Lily. "I want to play with my hoop, but the big dog is there," said Boo. "Don't worry, Boo. I will help you," said Lily with a smile. Lily flew over to the dog and started to sing a sweet song. The big dog listened and soon fell asleep. Boo was so happy and thanked Lily. Now, Boo could play with his hoop outside without being frightened. And from that day on, Boo and Lily played together every day, and they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time there was a jolly reindeer that liked to travel a lot. One day, he decided to venture further than ever before. So he packed his things and set off on his journey. As he was travelling, the reindeer spotted a little boy standing alone at the side of the road. He had been crying. The reindeer asked why he was so sad. The boy told him that he had lost his way. He had been on his way to his grandmother's house, but he had taken a wrong turn and had no idea how to get back. The kind reindeer was very sympathetic and offered to take the boy home. The boy agreed, so the reindeer ran as fast as he could, back to the boy's grandmother's house. The moral of this story is that it is always important to be kind and help others in need. By looking out for one another, we can make things much easier for those around us.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play outside in the snow. They make a snowman with a hat and a scarf. They give him a carrot nose and two buttons for eyes. They think he is charming. "Hello, Mr. Snowman!" Lily says. "Do you want to play with us?" "Yes, please!" Ben says in a funny voice, pretending to be the snowman. "But I am cold. Can you take me inside?" Lily and Ben laugh. They know the snowman cannot go inside. He will melt. "No, Mr. Snowman, you have to stay here," Lily says. "But we will come back tomorrow and play with you again." "OK, thank you," Ben says, still in a funny voice. "But can you give me a hug and a kiss?" Lily and Ben hug and kiss the snowman. They think he is very sweet. "Goodbye, Mr. Snowman!" they say. "We will see you soon!" They wave to the snowman and run back to their houses. They are happy. They love the snow.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy ambulance. He would make the ambulance go fast and make a "wee-woo" sound. Tim thought the ambulance was crazy fast! One day, Tim was playing with his ambulance outside. He was having so much fun. But then, Tim had to go inside to eat lunch. He put his ambulance down and went inside. After lunch, Tim wanted to play with his ambulance again, but he could not find it. Tim was sad because he missed his crazy ambulance. Tim's mom saw that he was sad. She helped him look for the ambulance. They looked and looked. Finally, they found the ambulance under a big tree. Tim was so happy! He hugged his mom and played with his crazy ambulance all day long.

Anna likes to write. She writes with a crayon on a big paper. She writes her name, and some shapes, and some animals. She writes a wolf, because she saw one in a book. She makes the wolf big and gray, with sharp teeth and a long tail. But then, her brother Tom comes. He is angry. He wants to play with the crayon. He grabs the crayon from Anna's hand. He breaks the crayon in two. He throws the paper on the floor. He says, "Your wolf is ugly. I don't like it." Anna is sad. She cries. She says, "Tom, you are mean. You ruined my writing. I like my wolf. It is not ugly. It is nice." Tom sees that Anna is sad. He feels sorry. He says, "I'm sorry, Anna. I was wrong. Your wolf is nice. It is not ugly. I was just mad. Can I help you fix your writing?" Anna stops crying. She says, "Yes, Tom. You can help me. We can use another crayon. We can tape the paper. We can write more animals. We can write a wolf and a rabbit and a bird and a fish." Tom and Anna hug. They say, "We love each other. We love writing. We love animals." They write together. They are happy.
