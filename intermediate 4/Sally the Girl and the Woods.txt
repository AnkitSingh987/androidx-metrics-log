Category: Intermediate
Title: Sally the Girl and the Woods

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. Sally wanted to find something very special and fun to do. So, one day Sally decided to go for a walk in the woods. As Sally was walking, she noticed a big and impressive tree. She found a seat on the tree and sat down. Suddenly, she heard a noise and looked around. To her surprise, she saw a bright and colourful lizard! Sally was so excited! She wanted to be friends with the lizard and take it home. The lizard did not want to go home with Sally, so she respected its decision. Instead of trying to capture the lizard, she offered to take care of it and its home. This story teaches us to respect the decisions of others, even if they are different from what we want. We should always be kind and considerate, no matter what!

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a pretty frame on the wall. A little girl named Lily lived in the house with her mom and dad. One day, the frame fell down and broke. This made Lily very sad. Lily's mom saw her sad face and said, "Don't worry, we can fix the frame together." So, they got some glue and carefully put the frame back together. When they were done, Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Mom!" The next day, Lily's friend came over to play. Lily showed her friend the fixed frame and said, "My mom helped me fix it." Her friend smiled and said, "It looks nice!" Lily was happy and thanked her friend for saying that.

Lily and Tom were playing in the garden. They saw a big jug near the fence. It was dirty and had a hole in it. "Let's wash the jug," Lily said. "Maybe we can use it for something." "OK," Tom said. They took the jug to the tap and filled it with water. They poured the water over the jug and rubbed it with their hands. But as they washed the jug, they heard a loud noise. It was a growl, like a big dog or a lion. They looked around and saw a scary animal behind the fence. It had sharp teeth and fur and eyes that glowed. "Run!" Lily shouted. She dropped the jug and ran to the house. Tom followed her. The animal jumped over the fence and chased them. They reached the door and slammed it shut. They locked it and ran to their mum. "Mum, mum, there's a scary animal in the garden!" Lily cried. "What? What animal?" Mum asked. "It was big and loud and had teeth and fur and eyes," Tom said. Mum looked out of the window and saw the animal. It was not a dog or a lion. It was a bear. "Oh no, it's a bear!" Mum said. "How did it get here?" She called the animal control and they came to take the bear away. They said it had escaped from the zoo and was looking for food. Lily and Tom were glad that the bear was gone. They hugged their mum and said they were sorry for leaving the jug outside. "It's OK, my loves," Mum said. "You are safe now. But next time, be careful when you play in the garden. You never know what you might find."

One day, a slow cat named Tim went to the gym to play. Tim liked to run and jump on the big mats. He had a lot of fun with his friends. While playing, Tim fell down and hurt his leg. He cried, "Ouch! My leg hurts!" His friend, a dog named Sam, came to help. Sam said, "Don't worry, Tim. I know how to heal your leg." Sam got a big bandage and put it on Tim's leg. Then, something unexpected happened. Tim's leg started to feel better very fast. He was not slow anymore. Tim was so happy, he said, "Thank you, Sam, for helping me heal!" Now, Tim and Sam could play at the gym together again. They had lots of fun and were very good friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big steel door. People would walk by and stare at it. They did not know what was behind the door. One day, a little girl named Lucy walked up to the door. She put her ear on it and listened. She heard a small voice behind the door. "Hello, can you help me?" said the voice. Lucy was serious and wanted to help. She tried to open the door, but it was locked. She looked around and found a small key on the ground. She put the key in the door and turned it. The door opened slowly. Behind the door, Lucy found a small dog. The dog was the one talking! The dog said, "Thank you for helping me. I was stuck here for a long time." Lucy was very surprised. She took the dog home and they became best friends. And they lived happily ever after.
