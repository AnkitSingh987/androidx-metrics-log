Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Story and the Head

Once upon a time there was a girl who loved stories. She heard a story every day and each time she nodded her head. Today was a different day though. The girl was very tired, so she curled up in her bed and closed her eyes. But then she heard a beautiful story that made her smile. The story was about a brave prince who wanted to find his princess. It was a magical tale so the girl opened her eyes and nodded her head. She was entranced by the story and soon fell asleep with her head nodding up and down. The story made her feel better. When she woke up in the morning, she felt refreshed and ready for the day. She smiled as she thought of the story and nodded her head once again.

---

Lily liked to look at the birds in the pet shop. She saw many colors and shapes. Some birds were big and loud, some were small and quiet. Some had long tails, some had short tails. Some had fancy hats, some had no hats. One day, Lily saw a new bird in the pet shop. It was in a big cage with a sign that said "Parrot". The bird was green and red and blue. It had a long tail and a fancy hat. It looked at Lily and said "Hello". Lily was surprised. She said "Hello" back. The bird said "What is your name?" Lily said "Lily". The bird said "Lily, I like you. Do you like me?" Lily said "Yes, I like you. You are a nice bird." The bird said "Lily, do you want to be my friend?" Lily said "Yes, I want to be your friend. Can I take you home?" The bird said "Yes, you can take me home. But you need to ask the man first." Lily ran to the man who worked in the pet shop. She said "Please, can I have the parrot? He is my friend. He can talk to me." The man smiled and said "The parrot is very special. He is very smart and funny. But he is also very expensive. He costs a lot of money." Lily was sad. She did not have a lot of money. She only had a few coins in her pocket. She said "But I really want the parrot. He is the best bird ever." The man said "I understand. But I can't give you the parrot for free. Maybe you can save some money and come back later." Lily thought for a moment. She said "Can I recommend something?" The man said "Sure, what do you want to recommend?" Lily said "Maybe you can give me the parrot for a little money and a trade. I can give you my coins and my teddy bear. He is very soft and cuddly. He can keep you company when you are lonely." The man looked at Lily and her teddy bear. He saw that she really loved the parrot. He said "That is a very kind offer, Lily. But I don't think it is fair. The parrot is worth much more than your coins and your teddy bear. How about this? I will give you a discount on the parrot. You can pay me half of the money now and half later. And you can keep your teddy bear. He is your friend too." Lily was happy. She said "Thank you, thank you, thank you. You are very nice. I will pay you the rest of the money soon. I promise." The man said "You're welcome, Lily. I hope you and the parrot will be very happy together. He is a very fancy bird. You need to take good care of him. Give him food and water and toys and love." Lily nodded. She said "I will, I will, I will. He is my fancy friend. I will give him everything he needs." The man gave Lily the cage with the parrot. Lily said goodbye to the man and the other birds. She took the cage and her teddy bear and went home with her mom. She was very excited to show her dad and her brother her new friend. She said to the parrot "What is your name?" The parrot said "My name is Pepe. Pepe the parrot. And you are Lily. Lily the lovely. We are friends." Lily said "Yes, we are friends. Pepe and Lily. Lily and Pepe. The best friends ever." And they were.
