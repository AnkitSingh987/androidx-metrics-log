Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Book

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a big, expensive toy store in her town. Mia loved to go there and look at all the pretty toys. One day, Mia's mom took her to the store. Mia saw a big math book on the shelf. She wanted to learn math, so she asked her mom if she could hold the book. Her mom said yes, and Mia held the book in her hands. Mia loved the math book so much that she asked her mom if they could buy it. Her mom said it was too expensive, but she would find a smaller math book that was not so expensive. Mia was happy, and they went home with a new math book to learn from.

Tom and Lily were playing in the garden when they saw some smoke coming from the house next door. They ran to the fence and looked over. They saw a fire in the kitchen and a man trying to put it out with a hose. "Fire! Fire!" Tom shouted. "We have to tell Mommy and Daddy!" Lily nodded and followed Tom to their house. They ran inside and found their parents in the living room. They told them what they saw and their parents got up quickly. "Thank you, Tom and Lily, for telling us. That is very important," Mommy said. "We have to call the firemen and help our neighbors. Stay here and don't go outside, okay?" "Okay, Mommy," Tom and Lily said. They watched as their parents grabbed their phones and ran outside. They heard sirens and saw a big red truck coming down the street. The firemen jumped out and ran to the house next door. They had helmets and coats and hoses and ladders. Tom and Lily were curious and scared. They wanted to see what was happening, but they also wanted to be safe. They went to the window and peeked out. They saw the firemen spraying water on the fire and breaking the windows. They saw the man who lived next door come out with a cat in his arms. He looked sad and tired. One of the firemen came to their house and knocked on the door. Tom and Lily opened it and saw a friendly face. "Hello, kids. I'm Mike, one of the firemen. I'm here to examine your house and make sure it's safe. Can I come in?" he asked. "Sure, Mr. Mike," Tom and Lily said. They let him in and showed him around. He checked the walls and the ceiling and the plugs and the stove. He smiled and gave them a thumbs up. "Your house is fine, kids. No damage from the fire. You're very lucky," he said. "Thank you, Mr. Mike," Tom and Lily said. They gave him a hug and a high five. "You're welcome, kids. And thank you for being brave and smart. You did the right thing by telling your parents and staying inside. That was very important," he said. He waved goodbye and went back to his truck. Tom and Lily watched as the firemen finished their work and drove away. They saw their parents and their neighbors talking and hugging. They saw the cat licking its paws. They felt happy and relieved. They learned that fire is dangerous and smoke is bad. They learned that firemen are helpful and kind. They learned that telling the truth and following the rules is important. They learned that they have a good family and good friends. They learned a lot that day.

Once upon a time was a mighty gym. It had lots of things to play on, like swings, slides and monkey bars. One day, a little girl came to the gym. She was so excited and couldn't wait to play on the swings. "Come, swing with me," the girl said to her little brother. He nodded with a smile and raced over to the swings. The girl hopped onto one swing, while the boy grabbed onto the other. They both laughed as they pumped their legs, up in the air they went, higher and higher. The air was filled with their giggles. They were having so much fun. Then, the girl said to her brother, "Let's swing together, like two mighty mountains." They both reached out and held hands, pushing off the swings as hard as they could. Around and around, their feet touched the sky, their laughter made the world bright. They stayed on the swings until the sun had set and the stars twinkled in the sky. Finally, they had to go home, but they would come back to the mighty gym again soon.
