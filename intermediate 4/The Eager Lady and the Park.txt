Category: Intermediate
Title: The Eager Lady and the Park

Once upon a time there was a very eager lady. She was excited to try something new. Every day she would try different things, and she felt so proud of herself no matter what she did. One day she decided to try and ride a bike. She had never done it before, and she was really nervous. She looked around, and saw a little girl who also had a bike. The lady asked the little girl if she could help her learn to ride. The little girl was delighted to help, and soon the lady was riding around the park. At first the lady was scared, but she tried to relax and keep riding. She was being so brave and trying so hard! The little girl was so proud of the lady, and the lady smiled with joy. She was riding a bike! The lady was so excited about her new discovery, and she kept enjoying the bike for the rest of the day. All of her tries had made it possible for her to do something she thought wasn't possible before. She was so proud of herself, and the little girl was proud too. What an amazing day.

One day, a big, strong man named Tom had a job. His job was to spend all day lifting heavy things. He liked his job very much because it made him feel important. But one day, Tom woke up feeling weak. He didn't know what to do. Tom went to work, but he couldn't lift the heavy things. His friend, Sam, saw him and said, "Tom, you look weak today. What happened?" Tom was sad and replied, "I don't know, Sam. I just can't lift anything today." Later that day, a little girl named Lily came to visit. She saw Tom and said, "I can help you, Tom. I have a magic wand that can make you strong again." Tom didn't believe in magic, but he was so sad that he let Lily try. She waved her wand and, to Tom's surprise, he felt strong again. Tom was so happy, and he thanked Lily for her help. From that day on, Tom and Lily were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his marbles. One day, Tim found a big, shiny marble that looked very special. He thought it was a magic marble. He was very happy and showed it to his mommy. Mommy said, "Be careful with your new marble, Tim!" The next day, Tim took his magic marble to the park. He played with his friends and had a yummy snack. Suddenly, a big bird saw the shiny marble and wanted it. The bird took the marble from Tim's hand and flew away. Tim was very sad and screamed, "My magic marble!" Tim's mommy heard him scream and came running. She saw the bird with the shiny marble in its beak. Mommy had an idea. She took out a shiny coin from her bag and showed it to the bird. The bird saw the shiny coin and wanted it more than the marble. It dropped the marble to take the coin. Tim was so happy to get his magic marble back. He hugged his mommy and said, "Thank you, Mommy, for saving my marble!" From that day on, Tim learned to be more careful with his special things. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park with her mom. Mia loved to play on the swings and slide. While playing, she saw a small bird with a hurt wing. The bird could not fly. Mia wanted to help the bird, so she called her mom. "Mom, can we help the bird? It can't fly," Mia asked. Her mom agreed and they took the bird home. They made a soft and warm bed for the bird. The bird felt like it was in luxury because it was so comfy. After a few days, the bird's wing was better. The bird was grateful for Mia's help. It said, "Thank you, Mia, for helping me." Mia was happy that she could help the bird. The bird flew away, but it always came back to visit Mia at the park.
