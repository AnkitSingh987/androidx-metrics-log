Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Clever Dog and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a clever little dog named Max. Max loved to play with his toy cord. One sunny day, Max went to the park to play with his friends. At the park, Max saw a new friend named Lily. Lily was a small cat who was sitting all alone. Max wanted to make Lily happy, so he went over to her. "Hi, Lily! Do you want to play with my cord?" Max asked. Lily looked at the cord and smiled. "Yes, I would love to play with your cord, Max!" she said. Max and Lily played with the cord all day long. They were very happy and had lots of fun. Soon, it was time to go home. Max offered the cord to Lily. "Here, you can keep my cord. I hope we can play again soon!" Max said. Lily hugged Max and said, "Thank you, Max! You are a very clever dog and a great friend." And from that day on, Max and Lily were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She wanted to explore, so she crawled through the house. Suddenly, she discovered something new - a big, flat piece of cardboard on the floor! She crawled over to it and started to play. She made loud noises, pretending she was a roaring dinosaur. She wandered around with the cardboard, pretending to fly it like a kite. She laughed and screamed with joy. Soon enough, she heard her mother calling her name. She crawled back inside and showed her mother what she had found. Her mother was happy to see so much fun and imagination! She thanked the little girl for bringing her such a loud and joyous discovery. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a cat named Fluffy and a dog named Spot. They were good friends and liked to play together. One day, their owner gave them a big piece of meat to share. Spot was very happy, but Fluffy felt a little jealous because she thought the meat was only for her. Fluffy tried to take the meat away from Spot, but he insisted that they share it. "We can both have some," he said. Fluffy didn't want to share, but Spot was her friend, so she agreed. They both took turns eating the meat until it was all gone. After they finished eating, Fluffy and Spot played together in the yard. Fluffy was not jealous anymore because she knew that sharing with her friend was more important. They played all day and were very happy. From that day on, Fluffy and Spot always shared everything and were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy cat. The cat liked to yawn and sleep all day. The cat lived in a big house with many shapes. One day, the cat saw a new shape. It was a big, round shape. The cat yawned and went to look at the shape. The cat thought it was just a big ball to play with. But, the big round shape was not a ball. It was a big, round dog! The dog was friendly and wanted to play. The cat was surprised but happy. They played together and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little toy. The toy loved to talk to all the other toys. One day, the toy saw a new friend. The new friend was a big, healthy apple. "Hi, apple! Do you want to talk?" the toy asked. The apple did not talk back. The toy was sad. Just then, a little girl picked up the apple. "Yum! I love healthy apples!" she said. The toy was surprised. The apple was not a toy, but food! The toy learned that not everything can talk, but that's okay. The toy was happy to watch the little girl eat the apple and play with the other toys.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to school. She had a fancy new bag. It was pink and had a big heart on it. Amy was so happy to show her friends. In school, Amy sat next to her best friend, Tom. They both gazed at the big board. The teacher wrote some math on the board. Amy and Tom liked to learn math. They liked to count and add numbers. Then, Amy said to Tom, "Look at my fancy bag! Do you like it?" Tom looked at the bag and said, "Yes, it is very nice! I like the heart on it." They both smiled and went back to learning math. They had a fun day at school.
