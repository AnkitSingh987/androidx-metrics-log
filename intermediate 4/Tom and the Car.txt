Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and the Car

Tom loves to see the zoom cars. They are very fast and loud. They have bright colors and big wheels. Tom has a zoom car toy that he plays with every day. One day, Tom's dad says, "Do you want to see the real zoom cars?" Tom is very excited. He says, "Yes, yes, yes!" They get in the car and drive to the zoom car place. Tom sees a lot of people and a lot of zoom cars. He hears them go vroom, vroom, vroom. Tom and his dad find a good spot to watch the zoom cars. They see them go around and around the track. Tom cheers for his favorite zoom car. It is red and shiny. He sees it pass the other zoom cars and win the race. Tom is very happy. He says, "Wow, wow, wow!" Tom's dad gives him a hug and says, "Did you like the zoom cars?" Tom says, "I love the zoom cars! They are the best!" They go to the zoom car shop and buy a zoom car hat and a zoom car sticker. Tom puts them on and smiles. He says, "Thank you, dad! This is the best day ever!"

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump and slide. They saw a big pond with water and ducks. They wanted to see the ducks up close. They ran to the pond and looked for the ducks. They saw some white ones and some brown ones. They saw something else too. It was long and green and had many teeth. It was a crocodile! The crocodile saw them too. It was hungry and wanted to eat them. It opened its mouth and swam fast to them. Lily and Ben were scared. They tried to run away, but the crocodile was faster. The crocodile caught Ben by his leg and pulled him into the water. Lily screamed and cried. She wanted to help Ben, but she could not. The crocodile was too strong and big. Lily ran to find help. She saw a man on a high bridge. She shouted to him, "Help, help, a crocodile has Ben!" But the man did not hear her. He was listening to music on his headphones. He did not see the crocodile or Ben or Lily. Lily was alone and sad. She had lost her friend to the crocodile. She wished she had never gone to the pond. She wished she had stayed away from the long, green, toothy thing. She wished she had listened to her mom, who had told her to be careful in the park.

Once upon a time, a girl named Lucy loved to paint. She painted all day long. One day, she went to a theater with her mom. The theater was very big. Lucy wanted to paint there too. Inside the theater, there was a deep hole. Lucy did not see the hole. She was looking for a place to paint. She walked and walked, but she did not find a place to paint. Then, Lucy fell into the deep hole. She was very scared. Her mom could not find her. Lucy cried and cried. She could not paint anymore. The end.

One day, a sweet little duck went to the lawn near the pond. The sun was shining, and the duck was happy. The duck saw a frog sitting on the grass. The duck said, "Hello, frog! Do you want to swim with me?" The frog looked at the duck and said, "Yes, I would love to swim with you!" So, the duck and the frog jumped into the pond. They swam together, splashing water and having fun. The duck and the frog were good friends. After a while, the duck and the frog got tired. They went back to the lawn and lay down on the soft grass. They looked at the sky and saw fluffy clouds. The duck and the frog smiled, happy to be friends and enjoy the day together.

One sunny day, Betty had a small notebook with her. She was very excited to see what was inside and thought it would be fun. Betty opened the notebook and saw that it was filled with lovely pictures. She smiled and ran outside, eager to share her notebook with others. While running, Betty spotted a shiny object on the ground. She quickly picked it up and found it was a beautiful bracelet. It shone in the sunlight and Betty knew she had to have it. Suddenly, she heard a voice. It was an old woman yelling at her. "That is mine! Give it back!" Betty didn't want to give it away, so she ran away with the bracelet. The old woman was very angry and shouted after her. When Betty got home, she realized the bracelet was fake. She was very sad and wished she had given it back to the old woman. Her small notebook also seemed less fun as the bracelet had shone so brightly.
