Category: Intermediate
Title: John the Boy and the Clock

Once upon a time, there was a boy named John. He was hungry and he decided to collect some food. One day, he saw a big clock in the park. John thought it would be fun to collect the clock. He tried and tried, but he couldn't get the clock. He was so hungry that he started to cry. But then, a nice lady saw him and asked what was wrong. John explained that he wanted to collect the clock, but he couldn't. The nice lady smiled and told him she could help. She picked up the clock and gave it to John. He was so happy! John thanked the nice lady and ran home. He was so excited to show his family the clock. When he arrived, John's mom saw the clock and said, "Wow! This is a great clock. Let's go and get something to eat. You must be very hungry!" John smiled and said, "Yes, I was very hungry! I'm glad I was able to collect the clock!"

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a boy named Tim. He had a toy axe that he loved to play with. Tim was a flexible boy, who could bend and twist his body in many ways. He liked to show his friends how he could play with his toy axe while being flexible. One day, Tim was playing with his toy axe near the big tree. He was not minding where he was going. He saw a big cat coming towards him. The cat looked angry and wanted to take Tim's toy axe. Tim did not want to give his toy axe to the cat, so they started to fight. Tim tried to be flexible and use his toy axe to keep the cat away. But the cat was too strong and fast. The cat took Tim's toy axe and ran away. Tim was very sad because he lost his toy axe. He learned that he should mind where he is going and be careful with his things.

One day, all the animals in the forest noticed something was strange in the sky. It was dark and a storm was coming. The animals quickly ran to seek shelter. The storm had a powerful dark sky, thunder and lightning. But then something strange happened, the storm began to paint the sky. Colors of pink and orange ended up in the sky. It was the most beautiful sight the animals ever saw. The delicate colors kept adding and little by little the sky became beautiful. Everyone started to feel peace and happiness in their hearts. After the storm ended the sky was full of amazing colors. It was a beautiful sight. The animals loved the painting in the sky and ran out of their shelters to enjoy the close view. Every night, the animals would go outside and see the painting in the sky. It was a reminder of how delicate and special the world is.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a big, wide book. She wanted to read the book, but she did not know some words. Sue went to her mom to ask for help. Her mom was happy to help her. Her mom started to explain the words in the book. Sue listened carefully. Then, her mom showed her how to spell the words. Sue tried to spell the words too. She was getting better at it. Sue and her mom read the wide book together. They had fun learning new words. Sue was happy that her mom helped her. Now, she can read more books and learn more words.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there was a small brown rabbit with soft fur. The rabbit was hopping around, looking for food. The sun was shining, and it was a hot day. The rabbit saw a big tree with a hole in it. Inside the hole, he found some yummy green leaves. He started to eat them. But then, a big black bear came and said, "Stop! Those are my leaves!" The rabbit was scared and said, "I'm sorry. I didn't know these were your leaves." The bear was kind and said, "It's okay. You can have some. But first, let's cut them into smaller pieces so we can share." So, the bear and the rabbit cut the leaves into small pieces and shared them. They became good friends and played together in the hot sun. And from that day on, the rabbit and the bear always shared their food with each other.
