Category: Intermediate
Title: The Boy Called Timmy and the Silly

Once upon a time there was a little boy called Timmy. He was very silly! One day, Timmy went to the beach with his family. While he was playing, he suddenly saw something shiny in the sand. He bent down to take a closer look and he couldn't believe his eyes - it was a razor! Timmy thought it was so cool and he decided to take it home. When he reached home, he was so excited to show everyone the razor he had found. But his mom got a bit mad when she saw it. She told Timmy that razors are dangerous and should never be touched by silly little boys. She thanked him for being so clever to find it and then put it away somewhere safe. After that, Timmy learnt an important lesson - no matter how silly he was, he should never touch razors.

One day, a charming cat named Tom found a lamp. He looked at the lamp and thought, "I want to make it work." Tom tried to turn the lamp on, but it did not work. He felt sad and asked his friend, a dog named Max, for help. Tom said, "Max, can you help me make the lamp work?" Max looked at the lamp and said, "Yes, I can help you." They tried to make the lamp work together. They pushed and pulled, but still, the lamp did not work. Just then, a little bird flew by and saw them. The bird said, "You need to put a light in the lamp to make it work." Tom and Max were surprised. They did not know that. They found a light, put it in the lamp, and it worked! They were so happy and thanked the little bird. From that day on, Tom and Max had a bright lamp to light up their home.

Jill and Jane were watching a building. It was very tall, and had many windows. But the building was boring. Jill said to Jane, "It looks like this building isn't very interesting". Jane replied, "I know. There's nothing to do. Let's go play somewhere else." So they walked away, looking for something more fun. They could see a park, full of colorful flowers and interesting things to do. Jill said, "Let's go to the park! That looks like more fun. We can swing on the swing and climb on the slide". Jane said, "Yes! Let's go play in the park instead of watching the boring building". So they ran to the park, and had lots of fun.

One sunny day, a little dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a big cat named Sam. Sam was sad because he lost his toy. Max wanted to help Sam find his toy. Max said, "Let's go look for your toy. I will follow you." They walked and looked for the toy. They saw a tree with sour apples. Max and Sam picked some apples to eat. After eating the apples, Max and Sam felt sleepy. They decided to take a nap under the tree. When they woke up, they saw the toy near the tree. Sam was happy and said, "Thank you, Max!" They played with the toy and became best friends.

One day, a boy named Tim found a mysterious toy car under his bed. It was red and shiny. Tim was very happy. He wanted to play with the car, but it needed a battery. Tim asked his mom for help. "Mom, can you help me find a battery for my car?" Tim said. Mom smiled and nodded. They looked in a box and found a battery. They put it in the car. The car started to move. Tim was excited. He played with the car all day. He made the car go fast and slow. Then, he heard a snap sound. The car stopped moving. Tim was sad. Mom came to help again. She looked at the car and saw that a small part was broken. Mom fixed the car with some glue. Tim was happy again. He said, "Thank you, Mom!" They played with the car together and had lots of fun.

Once there was a little girl named Grace. Grace was just 3 years old and she was very special. She had a voice that was so charming it made everyone smile. One day, Grace saw a shadow puppet show. It was so amazing, she clapped and applauded with joy. Her applause was so loud and cheerful, she couldn't stop. After the show, onlookers heard her applauding and gathered around the little girl. Everyone started to join in and clap along with her. "Little girl, what is your name?" one of the puppet masters asked. "My name is Grace," she answered with a smile Everyone started to laugh and everyone started to applaud. "Grace, your voice is so charming," he said. Grace beamed with pride. She was so happy to have made everyone smile with her happy voice. The puppet master gave Grace the biggest applause of all and the whole crowd joined in. Grace had made a lasting impression on the crowd with her charming voice.
