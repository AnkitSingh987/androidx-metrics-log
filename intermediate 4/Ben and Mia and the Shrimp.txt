Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben and Mia and the Shrimp

Ben and Mia like to play in the sand. They make castles and tunnels and bridges. They use shells and sticks and stones to decorate their sand creations. One day, they see a big shrimp on the shore. It is pink and shiny and has many legs. Ben and Mia are curious. They want to touch it and see what it feels like. "Hello, shrimp," Ben says. "Do you want to play with us?" The shrimp does not answer. It wiggles its tail and tries to go back to the water. But the water is too far and the sand is too hot. "Please, shrimp, don't be scared," Mia says. "We are nice. We can help you. We can join you to the water." The shrimp looks at Ben and Mia. It sees that they are kind and friendly. It nods its head and lets them pick it up gently. Ben and Mia carry the shrimp to the water. They put it down near the waves. The shrimp is happy. It thanks them with a smile and a splash. "Bye, shrimp," Ben and Mia say. "You are very strong and brave. Maybe we can play again another day." The shrimp waves its tail and swims away. Ben and Mia go back to their sand play. They are proud and happy. They have made a new friend.

Once upon a time there was a little boy named Peter. Peter loved exploring. One day, he decided to explore the sea. He waded out into the blue water and swam around. But Peter soon noticed something strange. He saw a big net in the water with fish stuck in it. He swam closer and there was a bigger fish in the big net. Peter discussed the problem with the fish. “Please help me,” begged the fish. Peter asked it why it was stuck. The fish told him that it was trapped in the net and it couldn’t get out. Peter thought for a moment and decided to try and be careful. He slowly lifted the net, and the fish was freed! The fish thanked Peter and were both very happy. They said goodbye and the fish swam away. The moral of this story is to be careful and always help those in trouble. By being brave, Peter was able to release the fish and save the day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to spin around in her orange dress. She would spin and spin until she got dizzy and fell down. Lily liked to spin so much that she wanted to win a medal for spinning. One day, Lily saw a spinning contest in the park. She was very excited and wanted to join. All the kids were spinning as fast as they could. Lily tried her best and spun very fast. She won the contest and got a shiny medal. Lily was very happy with her medal, but she noticed that some kids were sad because they did not win. Lily decided to share her medal with them. They all took turns wearing the medal and spinning together. They had lots of fun and made new friends. The moral of the story is that sharing makes everyone happy.

One day, a big red car had a flat tire. The car's owner, Tom, was sad. He talked to his friend, Sam. Tom said, "My car has a flat tire. Can you help me?" Sam said, "Yes, I can help you." Sam had a brilliant idea. He brought a big scale to weigh the car. Sam said, "Let's see how heavy your car is." They put the car on the scale. The car was very heavy. Sam said, "We need a strong tire for your heavy car." Then, something unexpected happened. A little squirrel came with a strong tire. The squirrel said, "This tire is for you, Tom." Tom and Sam were surprised. They put the new tire on the car. The car was happy, and Tom said, "Thank you, squirrel!" The squirrel smiled and ran away. Tom and Sam drove away in the big red car.

One day, a big heavy cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw another cat named Sue. They both wanted to play with the same toy. They began to quarrel and shout, "It's mine!" As they quarreled, a small bird flew down and took the toy away. The two cats stopped and looked at each other. They felt sad because the toy was gone. Tom and Sue decided to be friends and not quarrel anymore. They found another toy and played together. They had so much fun and were happy to share.
