Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Spell

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia liked to speak a lot. She talked to everyone she met. One day, Mia found a big book with many spells. She wanted to try a spell, but she did not know how. Mia asked her mom for help. Her mom said, "If you want to learn a spell, you must be kind and listen well." Mia tried to listen, but it was hard. She wanted to speak more. But she knew she had to learn, so she tried her best to listen. One hot day, Mia found a spell to make ice. She listened well to her mom and learned the spell. She made ice for her friends to cool down. Everyone was happy and thanked Mia. Mia learned that by listening and being kind, she could do great things.

One day, a funny cat named Tom wanted to succeed in catching a big red ball. Tom was very good at jumping and running. His skin was soft and fluffy. Tom saw the big red ball under a tree. He ran very fast to get it. He jumped high in the air to catch the ball. The ball went up, and Tom ran after it. He tried again and again to catch the ball. Finally, Tom was able to succeed and catch the big red ball. He was very happy and proud. He took the ball to his friends and they all played together. They had a lot of fun with the big red ball.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his red ball. One day, while playing with his ball, it rolled far away. Tim had to go find it. Tim walked and walked until he found his ball near a big tree. Under the tree, he saw a shiny buckle. He picked it up and put it in his pocket. Tim wanted to return the buckle to its owner, but he didn't know who it belonged to. Tim asked his friends if they knew who the buckle belonged to. His friend, Sally, said it belonged to Mr. Green, the healthy man who lived down the street. Tim went to Mr. Green's house and returned the buckle to him. Mr. Green was very happy and thanked Tim for being so helpful.

One day, two children named Paul and Flora were playing in the yard. They were running around and laughing very loudly. The birds and other animals ran away because it was so noisy. Suddenly, Paul and Flora saw an elephant marching by. The elephant had a big drum and was playing a cheerful tune. Paul and Flora liked the music and began to clap their hands. They applauded and smiled when the elephant thanked them for their encouragement. Paul and Flora had so much fun that day and kept playing and applauding until the sun went down. The elephant was gone, but the music stayed in their hearts.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was sad because he lost his favorite sock. It was a very cold day, and he felt miserable without it. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. While playing, he saw a big tree. He had an idea. He thought that if he could strike the tree, maybe his sock would fall down. Tim hit the tree with all his might. Suddenly, the sock fell from the tree! Tim was so happy. He put on his sock and felt warm again. From that day on, Tim always remembered to keep his socks safe.

Once upon a time, there was a crazy bird named Bobo. Bobo loved to fly high and fast in the sky. One day, he saw a big thing in the sky. It was a missile! Bobo was scared, but he had a lot of hope. Bobo knew he had to do something. He thought, "I will stop the missile and save my friends." So, Bobo flew very fast to catch the missile. He used all his strength to push it away from his friends. Bobo's friends saw him and they all helped too. They pushed and pushed until the missile went far away. Bobo and his friends were very happy. They knew that hope and teamwork saved the day. And from that day on, they all played together, knowing they could do anything.
