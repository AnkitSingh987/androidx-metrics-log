Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and Sue and the Bird

One day, a little boy named Tim and a little girl named Sue went to the park. They saw a big strawberry bush. The strawberries looked red and sweet. Tim and Sue wanted to eat them. Tim said, "I want to eat the big strawberry." Sue said, "No, I want to eat the big strawberry." Tim and Sue did not agree. They both wanted the big sweet strawberry. A big bird heard Tim and Sue. The bird flew down and took the big strawberry. Tim and Sue were sad. They did not get the sweet strawberry because they did not agree.

Once there was a big spider who lived in a small house. The spider had many cobwebs in the house. One day, a foolish cat came to the house. The cat saw the cobwebs and thought they were toys to play with. The cat said to the spider, "Can I play with your cobwebs?" The spider thought for a moment and said, "Only if you serve me some food first." The cat agreed and went to find food for the spider. The cat brought back a big bug for the spider. The spider was happy and let the cat play with the cobwebs. But as the cat played, the cobwebs became messy and tangled. The spider was sad to see the mess but then, something unexpected happened. The tangled cobwebs looked even more beautiful than before. The spider and the cat became friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a new pie. It was a big, yummy pie. Mom made the pie for her little girl, Ann. Ann loved pie so much. It was her favorite treat. One day, Ann saw the pie on the table. She wanted to eat it. But first, she wanted to kiss the pie. Ann thought that if she gave the pie a kiss, it would be even more yummy. So, Ann went to the table and gave the pie a big kiss. Then, she took a bite. It was the best pie she had ever tasted. Ann and her mom were so happy. They ate the pie together and smiled.

Lila and Ben are friends. They like to play outside. They run, jump, and laugh. They see many things. They see birds, flowers, and bugs. One day, they see something new. It is in the sky. It has many colors. It is a rainbow. Lila and Ben are happy. They point and shout. They want to touch the rainbow. They run to the rainbow. But the rainbow is far away. They run and run. But the rainbow does not get closer. They stop and look. The rainbow is still in the sky. Lila and Ben are sad. They want to play with the rainbow. They sit on the grass. They think and think. How can they play with the rainbow? Then, they have an idea. They get up and smile. They run to their house. They get some things. They get paper, crayons, and scissors. They make their own rainbow. They cut and color. They make a big rainbow. They take their rainbow outside. They hold it up. They pretend it is in the sky. They play with their rainbow. They are happy. They have a unique rainbow. They have a fun rainbow. They have a rainbow that they can touch.

Once upon a time, there was a rich man named Joe. Joe loved to jog. He loved to jog so much that he jogged every single day. One day, Joe decided to go for a jog at the harbor. The harbor was so wide and so deep that Joe could barely see the end. It was such a beautiful place. Joe kept on jogging, and eventually he reached a cliff at the end of the harbor. When Joe looked down, he saw a whole city of rich people on the beach. They were so happy and they were having so much fun! Joe was so happy that he started to jog back towards the beginning of the harbor. He couldn't help but smile, because he knew he had seen something very special. As Joe kept jogging, he promised himself that he would come back to the harbor anytime he wanted. From then on, Joe loved jogging to the harbor every day.
