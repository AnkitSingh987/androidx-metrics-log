Category: Intermediate
Title: Momo the Monkey and the Banana

Once upon a time, in a big jungle, there lived a monkey named Momo. Momo loved to eat bananas. One day, Momo found an attractive banana. It was big and yellow. Momo was very happy. Momo wanted to hide the attractive banana, so he could eat it later. He looked around and found a tall tree with many leaves. Momo climbed the tree and hid the banana in the leaves. He was sure no one would find it there. Later, Momo came back to eat his attractive banana. He climbed the tree and found his banana still hidden in the leaves. Momo was very happy and ate the banana with a big smile. And from that day, Momo always hid his bananas in the tall tree.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very honest boy. He always told the truth. One day, Tim and his mom went to the station. They wanted to ride a train. At the station, Tim saw a big, red ball. He wanted to play with it. Tim asked his mom, "Can I play with the ball?" His mom said, "No, it's not ours. We must not touch it." Tim listened to his mom and did not play with the ball. But another boy came and took the ball. His mom saw him and said, "You must be honest and not take things that are not yours. I will have to punish you." Tim felt sad for the other boy, but he was happy that he was honest. His mom gave him a hug and said, "I am proud of you for being honest." Then, they got on the train and had a fun ride.

One day, a charming girl named Lucy went to the beach with her mom and dad. They played in the sand and swam in the water. Lucy loved the beach and was very happy. While they were playing, Lucy saw a jellyfish in the water. She was scared and called her mom and dad. They came and looked at the jellyfish. Lucy asked, "What do we do?" Her mom and dad didn't answer because they didn't know. Then, something unexpected happened. The jellyfish started to talk! It said, "Don't be scared, I am a nice jellyfish. I just want to play too." Lucy, her mom, and dad were very surprised. They all played with the jellyfish and had a great day at the beach.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a lawyer named Tom. Tom was a good man who liked to encourage people. He had a dog named Max. Max was a naughty dog, but Tom loved him very much. One day, Tom and Max went for a walk in the park. Max saw a squirrel and ran after it. "Stop, Max!" Tom called out. But the naughty dog did not listen. He kept running after the squirrel. Tom chased Max and finally caught him. He looked at Max and said, "You are a naughty dog, but I still love you." Tom gave Max a big hug. They both walked back home, happy and tired.

Once upon a time, there was a happy girl named Amy. She had a big box of colorful blocks. She loved to play with them and make patterns on the floor. One sunny day, Amy's friend Tom came over to play. They were having fun making patterns with the blocks. Then Tom said, "I want to own these blocks too!" Amy didn't like that idea, but she wanted to be a good friend. Amy and Tom played with the blocks and made a big pattern on the floor. Suddenly, their pattern started to move! The blocks jumped and danced around the room. Amy and Tom were surprised and laughed. "Wow!" said Amy. "I didn't know the blocks could do that!" Tom agreed, "Me neither!" They watched the blocks dance and decided to share them. They played happily together, making more dancing patterns with their blocks.

One day, a cat and a dog were going for a walk. The cat saw a great big chair and wanted to sit on it. The cat said, "I want that seat!" The dog said, "No, I want that seat!" They both insist on sitting in the seat. Then, a big bird came and sat in the seat. The cat and the dog were sad. They did not know what to do. They wanted the seat, but the big bird was there. They thought and thought. At last, the cat and the dog had an idea. They asked the big bird if they could all share the seat. The big bird said yes! So, the cat, the dog, and the big bird all sat on the great seat together. They were happy and became good friends.
