Category: Intermediate
Title: The Gentle Swan and the Pond

Once upon a time, in a big pond, there was a gentle swan. The swan liked to swim and play all day. She had many friends in the pond, like the fish and the ducks. One day, the swan saw a big box near the pond. She swam to the box and rubbed it with her beak. When she did that, a magic frog jumped out of the box! The frog said, "Thank you for rubbing the box. I am a magic frog, and I will give you a wish." The gentle swan was very happy. She wished to be the best swimmer in the pond. The magic frog said, "Your wish is granted!" But when the swan tried to swim, she found she could only walk on the water, not swim in it. The swan was sad, but her friends still loved her. They played new games together, and the gentle swan was happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little dog named Max. Max loved to play with his red wagon. He would pull it around the yard and put his toys in it. One day, Max was playing with his wagon when he saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad because she lost her favorite toy. Max wanted to help her, so he said, "Lily, let's find your toy!" Max and Lily looked all around the yard. They looked under the trees and behind the bushes. Finally, they found the toy under a big rock. Lily was so happy that she hugged Max and said, "Thank you, Max!" Max felt proud that he could help his friend. But when they went back to play with the red wagon, they saw that it was broken. Max was sad, but Lily said, "Don't worry, Max. I forgive you. We can fix the wagon together." So Max and Lily worked together to fix the wagon. They had so much fun playing together that they became best friends forever. And they always remembered to forgive each other when something went wrong.

Once upon a time, there was a white cat named Lily. She loved to wear a shiny bracelet on her paw. Lily lived in a small house with her best friend, a girl named Emma. One day, Lily and Emma wanted to have a tea party. They put on their best clothes and set up a little table in the garden. Lily wore her shiny bracelet, and Emma wore a pretty dress. Emma said, "Lily, can you please serve the tea?" Lily nodded and served the tea with her paw. They both sipped the tea and laughed. They had a great time at their tea party, and Lily's shiny bracelet sparkled in the sun.

One day, a powerful dog named Max went for a walk. Max was big and strong. He loved to run and play with his friends. As he walked, he saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad. "Hi Lily, why are you sad?" Max asked. Lily looked up and said, "I lost my toy. I am very sad." Max wanted to help Lily find her toy. So, they looked everywhere. They looked under the tree, behind the bush, and near the pond. Finally, Max found the toy near a big rock. He gave it to Lily. Lily was so happy! She said, "Thank you, Max!" Then, Lily clapped her hands and laughed. Max was happy too. They became good friends and played together every day.

Once, there was a famous dog named Spot. He was very good at finding things. One day, he found a message in a bottle. Spot wanted to know what the message said, but he could not open the bottle. He tried to bite it, but it was too hard. Spot struggled to open the bottle all day. He tried to roll it, shake it, and even jump on it. But the bottle just would not open. Spot felt sad because he could not read the message. Then, Spot had an idea. He took the bottle to his friend, a strong bird named Flap. Flap used his beak to open the bottle. They read the message together. It was a thank you note from a faraway friend. Spot and Flap felt happy because they could read the message and share it with their friends.
