Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Sam and the Car

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to play with cars. They have many cars of different colors and sizes. They make races with their cars on the floor. "Ready, set, go!" Tom says, and pushes his red car. Sam pushes his blue car. The cars zoom across the floor. They are very fast. They make loud noises. "Vroom, vroom!" Tom's car is ahead of Sam's car. Tom is happy. He thinks he will win the race. Sam's car is behind Tom's car. Sam is sad. He thinks he will lose the race. "Quit, quit!" Sam says. He does not want to play anymore. He is angry. He picks up his car and throws it on the couch. "Your car is too fast. It is not fair." Tom stops his car. He looks at Sam. He does not understand. He likes to play with Sam. He does not want Sam to be angry. He wants Sam to be happy. "Sam, don't quit. It is just a game. We can play again. Maybe you will win next time. Or we can play something else. We can share our cars. Your car is very useful. It can do many things. Look, it can spin and jump and flip. It is fun." Tom says. He picks up Sam's car and shows him. Sam looks at Tom. He looks at his car. He sees that his car can do many things. He thinks that his car is fun. He feels sorry. He does not want to be angry. He wants to be happy. "Sorry, Tom. I was silly. I don't want to quit. I want to play with you. You are my friend. Your car is very fast. It is cool. Can we share our cars?" Sam says. He smiles. Tom smiles too. He is glad. He gives Sam his car. Sam gives Tom his car. They hug. They are friends. They play with their cars. They have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big, soft armchair in a cozy room. It was an adorable armchair with many colors. Every day, a little girl named Lily would sit in the armchair to listen to stories from her mom. One day, Lily's mom said, "Lily, let's sit in the armchair and listen to a new story today." Lily was very happy and excited. She loved to listen to her mom's stories. As they sat in the adorable armchair, Lily's mom began to tell a story about a kind bunny who helped his friends. Lily listened and smiled, feeling warm and cozy in the big, soft armchair.

Tommy was a young child who was very sleepy. He wanted to take a nap so he laid down in his bedroom. As he closed his eyes and began to relax, he suddenly heard a noise coming from outside. It was the noise of a nation marching, but it was very slow. Tommy opened his eyes and saw millions of people marching around the world. They were all singing the same song and Tommy started to sing along. Tommy stayed awake for a while, watching the nation march and singing with them. But eventually the slow pace of the march began to make Tommy sleepy and he fell fast asleep. When Tommy awoke, the nation was still marching, they were just a bit further away. Tommy smiled and went back to sleep, happy that he had witnessed such a special event.

Once upon a time, there was a big billboard that had a picture of a cat. The cat was very independent and liked to do things on its own. The cat's name was Whiskers. One day, Whiskers was walking around and saw a tree. Whiskers wanted to hang on the tree. So, Whiskers said to a friend, "I want to hang on that tree!" The friend said, "Be careful, Whiskers. The tree is very tall." Whiskers didn't listen and climbed the tree. Whiskers was hanging on a branch, feeling happy. But then, the branch broke! Whiskers fell down, but luckily, the friend was there to catch Whiskers. Whiskers learned that sometimes it's good to listen to friends, even if you are independent.

One day, a little boy was crying in a field. He felt so alone and sad. All of a sudden, he heard a sound coming from above him. He looked up to see a beautiful bird with its wings outstretched, soaring high in the sky. The boy felt a spark of joy inside. He pointed towards the bird and shouted excitedly, "Look! I can shoot a bird!" He took a moment to think, then he ran over to a nearby tree, grabbed a stick and stared shooting at the sky. He aimed the stick carefully and suddenly a loud noise echoed through the sky. The little boy was so proud when he saw the bird fly away with only one wing. He smiled and felt so happy that he had helped a bird in need. He knew his actions had made him feel proud and important. He continued to stand in the field with a big smile on his face watching the bird fly away into the distance.
