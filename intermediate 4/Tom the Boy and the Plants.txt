Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Plants

Once upon a time, in a big brown house, there lived a boy named Tom. Tom had a task to do. He needed to spray water on the plants in his yard. Tom liked to help his mom with tasks like this. One sunny day, Tom went outside with his friend, Sam. They both took their water sprayers. Tom said, "Let's spray the plants, Sam!" Sam nodded and smiled. They started to spray water on the green plants. The plants seemed happy. As they finished their task, Tom's mom came outside. She looked at the plants and said, "Good job, Tom and Sam! The plants look happy now." Tom and Sam smiled. They were proud of their work. They knew they had done a good task.

Once upon a time, there was a little black cat. The cat liked to play hide and seek with his friends. One day, he found a big box to hide in. While hiding in the box, the black cat tried to peek out. He saw his friend, a small dog, looking for him. The cat was very quiet, so the dog could not find him. The cat's lip curled up into a smile as he stayed hidden. After a while, the black cat jumped out of the box and said, "Boo!" The dog was so surprised and laughed. They played hide and seek all day long, and the black cat found many more fun places to hide.

Once upon a time, there was a tough lion. He loved to play and spend time with his family in the jungle. One day, he was walking with his family when they heard a voice mention something. The lion stopped and asked "What did you mention?" The voice said "I mentioned that I saw a big, juicy apple in the tree!" The lion was so excited! He stood up tall and said "Let's go get it!" So the family all ran to the apple tree. The lion was so excited, he almost forgot about how tough he was! He pounced up the tree and grabbed the apple with his big claws. The family all cheered and had the big juicy apple for dinner. From that day on, when the lion heard someone mention an apple, he would remember the special moment he spent with his family getting the juicy apple!

Once upon a time, there was a mighty dog named Max. Max liked to look at the sky at night. One night, Max saw a comet in the sky. Max did not recognize the comet. The comet was new to him. Max wanted to know more about the comet. Max told his friends about the comet. His friends were a cat, a bird, and a fish. They all wanted to see the comet too. Max and his friends went outside to look at the sky. The comet was big and bright. They all liked the comet. But then, the comet came closer to the ground. Max and his friends were scared. The comet hit the ground with a big boom. Max and his friends ran away. They were sad because their home was gone. The comet had made a big hole in the ground. Max and his friends had to find a new home.

Once upon a time, there was an old office. Inside, the walls were bare but there was a giant box sitting in the corner. One day, a little mouse was scurrying around the office. He stopped to look in the box and saw a scissors inside! He turned to look around the office, making sure no one was watching him, and then scampered over to it. As he reached inside to grab the scissors, he heard a voice. "Where do you think you're going?" Startled, the mouse saw a tall man in an office chair. "Sir, I was just going to cut some paper with the scissors," the mouse replied. The tall man smiled and said, "Well, I suppose that's alright. But be careful, you never know what could happen if you get too carried away." The mouse thanked the tall man and began to cut the paper. The tall man watched with a smile as the mouse made precise, careful cuts. When the mouse was finished, the tall man exclaimed, "You did a great job! You should be proud of yourself." The mouse thanked him and scurried away. The next time he came back to the office, the tall man was gone. In his place was a note that read: "I knew you had a knack for cutting paper, so I left you a surprise in the box. I hope you like it."
