Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Tom and the Test

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play and talk. They go to school together. They have a teacher. Her name is Miss Rose. She is nice and smart. One day, Miss Rose gives them a test. She says, "You have to study hard. The test is very important. It is about colors and shapes. You have to know them all." Lily and Tom are scared. They do not like tests. They do not want to study. They want to play and have fun. They think the test is boring and hard. They go home. They do not study. They watch TV. They play games. They eat candy. They forget about the test. The next day, they go to school. They see Miss Rose. She has the test papers. She says, "Are you ready for the test? I hope you studied well." Lily and Tom are not ready. They do not remember anything. They look at the test papers. They see many colors and shapes. They do not know them. They feel sad and stupid. They try to guess. They write down some answers. They hope they are right. They are not. Miss Rose collects the test papers. She looks at them. She shakes her head. She says, "Lily and Tom, you did very bad. You did not study at all. You got all the answers wrong. You do not know the colors and shapes. You have to learn them. You have to study more. You have to do the test again. You have to stay after school." Lily and Tom are very sorry. They cry. They say, "We are sorry, Miss Rose. We were lazy and silly. We did not study. We did not listen to you. We want to do better. Please forgive us." Miss Rose says, "I am sorry, too. But you have to face the consequences. You have to work harder. You have to show me that you care. You have to stay after school and do the test again. Maybe then you will learn." Lily and Tom stay after school. They do the test again. They do not like it. It is long and hard. It is bitter. They wish they had studied. They wish they had listened to Miss Rose. They wish they had done better. They are unhappy. They have a bad day.

One day, a big earthquake came to the town. It shook the ground and made the people scared. In a hidden place, there was a small dog named Buddy. Buddy was very scared of the loud noise and the shaking. Buddy ran out of his hidden place and saw people clapping their hands. They were applauding the brave helpers who were fixing the broken things. Buddy wanted to help too, so he ran to the people and barked. The people saw Buddy and smiled. They let him help by carrying small things in his mouth. When the town was fixed, everyone applauded Buddy too. He was a brave helper, and he wasn't scared of the earthquake anymore.

Once upon a time, There was a little girl named Jane who wanted to go on a vacation with her family. She asked her mom, "Mom, please can we go on a vacation?" "Yes, sweetheart," her mom said with a smile, "We will go on a nice mild vacation." Jane was so happy that she jumped up and down and clapped her hands. She loved the idea of going on a vacation. The next day, Jane packed her suitcase and waited excitedly for the car to take them away. When the car arrived, Jane got in and so did her family. They drove for a few hours and eventually arrived at the beach! It was a dream come true for Jane -- she wanted to spend the day making sand castles and collecting seashells. Throughout the day, she would run to her mom often, saying "Mom, look at this. Please can I keep it?" The vacation was even milder than Jane had imagined and she was very pleased. Jane learned a lot from that day and she looked forward to having more family vacations soon!

One day, a little girl named Lily found a gray rock. She thought it was special. Lily made a wish on the rock. She wished for a big, sweet cake. She waited for the cake to come, but it did not. Lily was sad, but she did not give up. She thought maybe the rock needed time. She put the rock under her pillow and went to sleep. The next day, she looked under her pillow, but there was still no cake. Lily remained hopeful and believed in her wish. That day, Lily went to play outside. She saw her friend Tim with a big, sweet cake. Tim said, "Lily, I found this cake on my doorstep this morning. I don't know who left it, but I want to share it with you." They both laughed and enjoyed the cake. Lily's wish came true, just not in the way she expected.
