Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Sunflowers

Lily loved sunflowers. She liked to look at their big yellow faces and feel their soft petals. She wanted to grow her own sunflower in her garden. She asked her mom for some seeds and a pot. "Here you go, Lily," her mom said. "You have to plant the seeds in the soil and water them every day. Then they will grow into sunflowers." Lily was very happy. She planted the seeds in the pot and put it on the windowsill. She watered it every day and waited for the sunflowers to come out. She believed they would be the most beautiful sunflowers ever. But nothing happened. The pot stayed brown and dry. Lily was sad. She wondered why the sunflowers did not grow. She asked her mom. "Sometimes it takes a long time for the seeds to sprout, Lily," her mom said. "You have to be patient and keep watering them. They need sun and water to grow." Lily nodded. She did not give up. She kept watering the pot and hoping for the sunflowers. One morning, she woke up and saw something green poking out of the soil. She ran to the pot and saw a tiny stem with two little leaves. She was so excited. She shouted, "Mom, mom, look! The sunflowers are growing!" Her mom came and hugged her. "That's wonderful, Lily! You did a great job. Now you have to wait for the flowers to bloom. They will be big and yellow and delicious." "Delicious?" Lily asked. "Yes, delicious. Did you know that sunflowers make seeds that you can eat? They are crunchy and tasty. You can share them with your friends or make them into butter." Lily smiled. She did not know that. She looked at the pot and imagined the sunflowers with their seeds. She could not wait to see them and taste them. She believed they would be the most delicious sunflowers ever.

Lily and Ben were playing with their toys in the living room. Lily had a purple case with many dolls inside. Ben had a red truck that made loud noises. They were having fun until Ben saw Lily's case and wanted to play with it. "Can I see your case?" Ben asked Lily. "No, it is mine. You have your truck," Lily said. "But I like your case. It is purple and shiny. Please, let me see it," Ben begged. "No, go away. You will break my dolls," Lily shouted. Ben got angry and tried to grab the case from Lily. They both pulled and pushed until the case fell on the floor and opened. All the dolls came out and some of them lost their heads or arms. Lily saw her broken dolls and started to weep. "Look what you did! You are a bad boy! You ruined my dolls!" Lily cried. "I'm sorry, I didn't mean to. I just wanted to play with you," Ben said. But Lily did not listen. She wept louder and louder. She ran to her mom and told her what happened. Her mom came to the living room and saw the mess. She was not happy. "Ben, why did you fight with Lily? You should share and be nice to each other," her mom said. "I'm sorry, mom. I was curious about her case. I won't do it again," Ben said. He felt sorry for Lily and wanted to make her feel better. He picked up one of the dolls that was not broken and gave it to Lily. "Here, this is for you. I hope you forgive me," he said. Lily looked at the doll and then at Ben. She saw that he was sorry and that he wanted to be her friend. She stopped weeping and smiled. "Thank you, Ben. I forgive you. Do you want to play with me?" she said. "Yes, I do. Can I play with your truck?" he said. "Sure, you can. And I can play with your case," she said. They hugged and played together. They learned to share and be nice to each other. Their mom was proud of them.

Once upon a time, a little infant lived in a small house. The infant had a big yard to play in. The infant loved to play hide and seek with his friends. One sunny day, the infant and his friends played hide and seek. They took turns hiding and counting. The infant found a hidden spot behind a tree. His friends looked and looked, but they could not find him. After a while, the infant came out from behind the tree. His friends were happy to see him. They all laughed and played together in the yard. The infant and his friends had a fun day playing hide and seek.
