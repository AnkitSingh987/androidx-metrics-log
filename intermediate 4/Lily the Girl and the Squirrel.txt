Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Squirrel

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a box of matches. Lily loved to play with the matches, but she knew she had to be patient and careful. One sunny day, Lily sat under a tree with her matches. She heard a tiny voice say, "Hello, can you listen to me?" Lily looked around and saw a little squirrel. The squirrel said, "I want to play with you, but I don't know how to play with matches. Can you teach me?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, I can teach you. But we have to be patient and listen to each other." The squirrel agreed, and they spent the whole day playing with matches, listening to each other, and being patient. They had so much fun and became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there were two friends named Sarah and Rob. They both wanted to go to the zoo, but they didn't know which one to go to. They decided to discuss it. "Let's go to the one with the tasty ice cream," suggested Rob. "Yes, that sounds perfect!" said Sarah. So off they went to the zoo. When they arrived, Sarah saw an x-ray machine. She was very excited and ran to it. She wanted to try it and get her own x-ray. Rob tried to stop her, but it was too late. Sarah put her hand in the machine, and suddenly there was a bright light and a loud noise. Sarah was very scared, and the machine was broken. Sarah and Rob had to call for help. The zoo security came and took Sarah for an x-ray. The doctor said it was too late and Sarah had to stay in the hospital. The two friends sadly said goodbye and never went to the zoo again.

Once upon a time, there was a kindly old man who lived in a little cottage in the woods. He was generous to everyone he met and he was known far and wide as a hero. One morning, a 3 year old girl was walking in the woods when she stumbled upon the old man’s cottage. She was curious and decided to take a closer look. The girl spotted the old man outside, kneeling in his garden. She approached him and asked, “What are you doing?” The old man smiled kindly and replied, “I am kneeling in prayer. It’s important to be generous and help out our friends.” The little girl smiled and said, “You must be a hero!” The old man laughed and said, “No, I’m just a kind old man who wants to make the world a better place.” The little girl smiled and thanked the old man for his generous advice. From that day on, she promised to always be generous, just like the old man.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy cat named Tom. Tom loved to play in the garden. One day, Tom saw a big box in the garden. He did not know what was inside. He wanted to raise the box to see what was under it. Tom tried to raise the box, but it was too heavy. A little bird saw Tom and wanted to help. The little bird was not strong, but it had a smart idea. The bird found a long stick and told Tom to use it to raise the box. Tom used the stick to raise the box. They were both very excited to see what was inside. But when they raised the box, they saw another smaller box. Tom and the little bird looked at each other and laughed. They did not expect to find another box. They worked together to open the smaller box and found a big, yummy cake inside. Tom and the little bird shared the cake and became best friends. They played in the garden every day and always helped each other.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went to the park. The park was wide with lots of room to play. Tim and Spot liked to look for things in the park. They looked under rocks and in the grass. They found a small jar of salt. "Look, Spot!" said Tim. "I found salt!" Spot wagged his tail and barked. They decided to show their friends the salt. Tim put the jar in his bag and they went to find their friends. As they walked, they saw a big bird in a tree. The bird looked at Tim and Spot. It flew down and took the bag with the salt. Tim and Spot were sad. They did not know what to do. Then, they heard a voice. "Hello, Tim and Spot!" said the bird. "I'm sorry I took your salt. I thought it was something I could eat. Here, take it back." The bird gave the bag back to Tim and Spot. They were very happy and thanked the bird. From that day on, Tim, Spot, and the big bird became good friends. They played together in the wide park and looked for more things to share. And they always made sure to keep their salt safe.
