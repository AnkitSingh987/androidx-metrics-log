Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna and the Frank

Once upon a time there were two best friends. They were two cute puppies called Anna and Frank. One day, Anna and Frank were playing in the backyard when they decided to get a parade. Anna asked Frank, "Let's get a parade! We can run fast!" Frank said, "Yes, let's get a parade! We can be the fastest ones around here. We can race each other and make it even more fun!" The two friends got ready for their parade. They ran as fast as they could and when they finished they were both so happy and excited! They had the best time sharing their parade with each other. They laughed and had so much fun. Anna and Frank were the happiest puppies around!

Lily and Max were twins who liked to play together. They had many toys, but their favorite was a big box that they could hide in. They liked to pretend that the box was a spaceship, a castle, or a secret base. Sometimes they would surprise each other by jumping out of the box and saying "Reveal!" One day, their mom called them for dinner. She had cooked a modern dish with noodles, cheese, and broccoli. Lily and Max did not like broccoli, but they knew they had to eat it. They ran to the kitchen, but they had an idea. They decided to hide some broccoli in the box and eat the rest of their dinner. They thought their mom would not notice, but they were wrong. Mom had seen them sneak some broccoli into the box. She was not happy. She told them to go back to the box and eat the broccoli. She said that hiding food was not nice, and that they should try new things. She said that broccoli was good for them, and that they might like it if they gave it a chance. Lily and Max felt bad. They knew their mom was right. They went back to the box and ate the broccoli. It was not so bad. They said sorry to their mom and thanked her for the dinner. They learned that they should not lie or waste food, and that they should be open to new experiences. They hugged their mom and went back to play with the box. They still liked to say "Reveal!", but they also said "Yum!" when they ate broccoli.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the park with her mom. Amy saw a big tunnel and wanted to play in it. She asked her mom, "Can I play in the tunnel?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Amy went inside the tunnel and saw a small, sad boy. He looked serious. Amy asked, "Why are you sad?" The boy said, "I can't find my toy car." Amy wanted to help him. Together, Amy and the boy looked for the toy car in the tunnel. They found it under a big leaf. The boy was so happy! He said, "Thank you, Amy!" They played with the toy car and had lots of fun.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a long, tall lamp near the road. It was very big and shiny. Tim liked the lamp and wanted to touch it. Tim was about to touch the lamp when a big cat jumped out from behind it. The cat was very big and scary. Tim was scared and started to shout. He shouted for his mom to help him. But when his mom came, the cat did not run away. Instead, the cat jumped up and turned off the lamp. It was now dark and Tim was even more scared. His mom tried to help, but the cat was too fast. The cat ran away and the lamp stayed off, making the night very dark and scary for Tim.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a toy truck that she loved to play with. She liked to load her toy truck with her other toys and drive it around her room. One day, Amy was playing with her toy truck and she saw a little bug. The bug had a big load of food on its back. Amy watched as the bug walked up to her lip and gave her a little kiss. The little bug was very nice to Amy. Amy smiled and decided to help the little bug. She loaded the bug's food onto her toy truck and drove it to the bug's home. The little bug was very happy and thanked Amy with a little dance. From that day on, Amy and the little bug became best friends and played together every day.
