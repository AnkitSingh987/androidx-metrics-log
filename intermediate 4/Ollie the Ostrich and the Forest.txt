Category: Intermediate
Title: Ollie the Ostrich and the Forest

One day, a big ostrich named Ollie was walking in the forest. He was very sad because his life was boring. He wanted to have fun and play with his friends, but they were all busy. Ollie felt all alone. Then, Ollie met a small bird named Bella. Bella said, "Hi Ollie, why are you sad?" Ollie told her about his boring life. Bella said, "Let's play a game to remove your sadness!" They played a game of hide and seek. Ollie was happy to play with Bella. But, when it was time for Ollie to find Bella, he could not see her. Suddenly, Bella jumped out from a bush and surprised Ollie. Ollie laughed and said, "Bella, you are so good at hiding!" They played all day and had lots of fun. Ollie learned that even when things seem boring, a good friend can make life fun and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to build things with her toy blocks. One day, she decided to build a big tower. She used red, blue, and yellow blocks to make her tower tall and strong. Lily also liked to play dress-up. She had a funny mask that she liked to wear. It was a green mask with big eyes and a silly smile. When she put on the mask, she would pretend to be a friendly monster. One day, Lily's mom asked her to clean her room. Lily put away her toy blocks and hung up her mask. She made her bed and put her toys in their special places. When she was done, her room was very clean. Lily's mom was happy, and Lily was proud of her clean room.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to visit her grandma. Grandma lived in a small house with a high tree in the back yard. Amy loved to play near the tree and look for pretty things. While playing, Amy saw something shiny near the tree. It was a small, silver key. She was very excited and showed it to her grandma. Grandma smiled and said that the key had been lost for a very long time. Grandma told Amy that the key opened a secret door in the tree. They went to the high tree and found the door. When they opened it, they found a room full of toys and games. Amy and her grandma played together all day, and they were very happy they found the secret room.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw a red seat near the slide. He wanted to sit on it, but it was wet from the rain. Tim's mom said, "Wait, let me wipe the seat for you." She took a cloth from her bag and wiped the seat until it was dry. Tim sat down and smiled. "Thank you, Mom!" he said. Then he played on the slide and the swings. While playing, Tim saw a big dog. The dog looked angry. Tim was scared and ran to his mom. She hugged him and said, "It's okay, the dog won't hurt you." They went home, and Tim felt safe again.

Once there was a little girl. She liked to explore and one day she found something in the dark. It appeared to be a piece of meat! The little girl was so excited and she quickly picked up the meat. She couldn't wait to eat it! The little girl ran back home to show her mom and dad what she had found. They were both so surprised! They told her that the meat was not safe to eat and she should put it back. The little girl was so sad but she did as her parents said. She went back to the dark and put the meat back where she found it. After that, the little girl decided to be more careful when she was exploring. She still explored each day but she always made sure to be careful and not touch anything in the dark.

Once upon a time there was a humble little girl named Jane. Jane liked to help her mommy in the kitchen. One day she asked if she could try to make a muffin. So, her mommy gave her a bowl and started to tell her how to make a muffin. Jane had to repeat each step carefully until the bowl was filled with the muffin ingredients. Then, Jane put the bowl in the oven and waited patiently. She watched the oven until the muffins were golden and ready to eat. Finally, Jane was so excited to take her muffins out of the oven. She proudly gave them to her mommy and they shared the muffins together. Jane was so proud of her accomplishment.
