Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben and the Car

Ben liked to play with his toy cars. He had many cars, but his favorite was a white car. It was very fast and powerful. He liked to make it zoom and race on the floor. One day, Ben saw a big box in the living room. It had a bow and a tag that said "For Ben". He wondered what was inside. He asked his mom if he could open it. She said no, not yet. She said it was a surprise for his birthday. Ben was curious and excited. He wanted to see his surprise. He waited and waited for his birthday. He counted the days on the calendar. He dreamed of what could be in the box. Finally, his birthday came. He ran to the living room and saw the box. He tore off the bow and the paper. He opened the box and gasped. Inside was a white car. But not a toy car. A real car. A car that he could get in and drive. A car that was very fast and powerful. Ben was so happy. He hugged his mom and thanked her. He got in the car and turned the key. The car made a loud noise and started to move. Ben felt like he was flying. He zoomed and raced on the street. He had the best birthday ever.

Once upon a time there was a little boy who loved boats. Every day he promised himself that one day he would sail in one. One day, his parents promised him a special surprise. When they arrived at the beach, the little boy saw a big yummy boat! His eyes were wide with joy as he ran across the sand to jump in the boat. On the boat, he saw many colors and he waved his hands in the air. His parents promised him lots of fun and it was even better than he ever imagined. After a while, the wind started to blow and the boat started to sail. The little boy felt like he was in a dream. He couldn't believe he was sailing in a boat! He looked around and smiled. The adventure was the best surprise ever!

Once upon a time, there was a colorful bird named Bob. Bob liked to protect his wheat. He would sit on his wheat all day and keep it safe. One day, a big wind came and blew Bob away from his wheat. Bob was sad and scared. He did not know how to find his wheat. He flew and flew, looking for it. Then, Bob saw a big, colorful tree. The tree was full of wheat! Bob was so happy. He did not know that the wind had put all the wheat in the tree. Now, Bob could protect his wheat and live in the pretty tree. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a huge steak. The steak was so big it would feed many people. Every day when the sun rose, the steak would smile. One day, two friends named Jack and Jill saw the steak and they were very hungry. Jack said to the steak, “Let’s take you home and eat you!”. But Jill said “No, we can’t do that. This steak could make many people happy.” So instead of taking the steak and eating it, Jack and Jill invited all their friends over. They each took a portion of the steak and shared it together. Everyone was very happy and the steak smiled even bigger. The moral of the story is it's better to share than to take for yourself. When you share, everyone can smile!

Once upon a time, in a big field, there was a rare flower named Poppy. Poppy was very pretty and loved to eat sun and rain to grow big and strong. One day, Poppy met a small bug named Buzz. "Hi, I am Poppy," said the pretty flower. "Who are you?" "Hi Poppy, I am Buzz," said the small bug. "I like to fly and play all day." Poppy and Buzz became good friends. They played together every day. Poppy loved to watch Buzz fly high in the sky. Buzz loved to see Poppy eat sun and rain to grow. One day, Poppy felt sad because she could not fly like Buzz. Buzz saw Poppy's sad face and said, "Don't be sad, Poppy. You are a rare and pretty flower. You can eat sun and rain to grow big and strong. I cannot do that." Poppy smiled and felt happy again. She knew that even if she could not fly, she was still special. Poppy and Buzz played together every day, and they were very happy.
