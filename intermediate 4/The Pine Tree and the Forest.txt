Category: Intermediate
Title: The Pine Tree and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a bright, sunny forest, there lived a little pine tree. The pine tree loved to play with his forest friends. One day, a squirrel came up to the pine tree and asked, "Can I play with you?" The pine tree said, "Yes, I permit you to play with me." They played and played until the sun went down. The squirrel climbed the pine tree and the pine tree shook its branches for the squirrel to jump on. They laughed and had so much fun. The next day, the squirrel brought more friends to play with the pine tree. They all played together, running around the bright forest. The pine tree was very happy to have so many friends. From that day on, the pine tree and his friends played together every day. They were the best of friends, and the bright forest was always filled with their laughter and joy.

Once upon a time, in a big field, there was a stubborn poppy. The poppy did not like to play with the other flowers. It liked to be alone. One day, a bell began to ring. The poppy heard the sound and felt curious. It wanted to know where the sound came from. So, the poppy went to find the bell. The poppy saw the bell near a big tree. The bell was pretty and shiny. The poppy liked the sound of the bell. It wanted to be friends with the bell. The bell and the poppy became good friends. They played together and the poppy was not stubborn anymore.

Once upon a time there was a prince. He had a beautiful palace and he wanted to wrap it up with a big bow before Christmas. The prince asked his assistant: "Can you help me to wrap the palace?" The assistant answered: "Yes, I will try my best!" The assistant started to wrap the palace with a long piece of shiny red ribbon. He tied a big and beautiful bow on the front door. Everyone who saw it said how beautiful it looked. Inside the palace, it was very calm. The prince smiled when he saw how beautiful the palace had become. He thanked his assistant for doing such a good job and said: "It looks so nice, now I'm ready for Christmas!"

One day, a smart boy named Tim saw a big crane. The crane was sad. Tim asked, "Why are you sad, crane?" The crane said, "I can't find my friend. I pray to find him soon." Tim wanted to help the crane, so they both looked for the friend. They looked and looked, but could not find the friend. Then, something unexpected happened. A big bird came and said, "I know where your friend is!" The big bird took them to a tree, and there was the crane's friend! The crane was so happy and said, "Thank you, Tim and big bird!" They all played together and had a great day.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a big hoop in her yard. She thought it would be fun to play with it. Sue tried to roll the hoop, but it was very stubborn and did not want to move. She tried again and again, but the hoop just would not roll. Sue's friend, Tim, came over and saw her trying to roll the hoop. He asked, "Why does the hoop not move?" Sue said, "It seems very stubborn." Tim had an idea. He went to his house and brought back a big ball. Tim and Sue pushed the ball into the hoop. To their surprise, the hoop started to roll! They laughed and played with the hoop and the ball all day long. The hoop was not stubborn anymore, and they had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a sailor named Sam. Sam had a big boat. He liked to sail on the water. One day, Sam saw a distant island. He wanted to go there. Sam sailed his boat to the island. On the way, he saw a big fish. The fish said, "Stop, Sam!" Sam stopped his boat. The fish said, "This is my home. Can you play with me?" Sam said, "Yes, I can play with you." Sam and the fish played together. They had a lot of fun. When it was time to go, Sam said, "Goodbye, fish." The fish said, "Goodbye, sailor Sam." Sam sailed back to his home, and he always remembered his new friend, the fish.
