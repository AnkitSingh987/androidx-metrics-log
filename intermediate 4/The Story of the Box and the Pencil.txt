Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Box and the Pencil

One day, a big pencil and a little pencil were in a box. They did not like each other. They started to quarrel. The big pencil said, "You are so little and stupid!" The little pencil felt sad. A girl named Lily came and took both pencils out of the box. She said, "Why are you fighting? You should be friends!" The big pencil and the little pencil stopped quarreling and listened to Lily. Lily used the big pencil to draw a house and the little pencil to draw a tree. They both looked very nice. The big pencil and the little pencil saw that they could work together. They became friends and never quarreled again.

Karen was dancing around in the playground. She felt so happy and mighty! Karen saw a pick on the ground and decided to share it with her friend. She ran over to her friend, Alice, and said: ”Hello, Alice! Look what I found! It’s a pick! Do you want to play together?” Alice smiled. ”Yes! Let’s share the pick and have fun together!” The two friends ran around the playground, taking turns to play with the pick. They made some mighty jumps and spun around with it. When it was time for the two of them to go home, Karen smiled. ”Let’s keep the pick and continue to share it with each other.” Alice agreed. ”That’s a great idea! Now we can always play together.” Karen and Alice smiled at each other and went home happy.

One day, a nosy little bird named Tim saw a big crane. Tim wanted to talk to the crane. He flew to the crane and said, "Hello, Mr. Crane! Do you believe you can help me?" The crane looked at Tim and said, "Maybe, what do you need help with?" Tim told the crane he wanted to find his friend, a little bug. The crane said, "I will help you, but you have to do something for me first." Tim agreed and the crane told him to find a shiny stone. Tim was excited and flew away to look for the shiny stone. While Tim was looking for the stone, he found his little bug friend. The bug told Tim that he had seen a shiny stone near the big tree. Tim and the bug went to the big tree, found the shiny stone, and took it to the crane. The crane was happy with the shiny stone and thanked Tim. Tim said, "Thank you for helping me find my friend, Mr. Crane!" The crane smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim. Remember, sometimes you can find what you're looking for when you help others." From that day on, Tim and the crane became good friends. They always helped each other and believed in the power of friendship.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a funny dog named Spot. Spot loved to organize his toys. He had many toys that he liked to put in a big box. Every day, Spot would take his toys out of the box and play with them. One day, Spot looked out the window and saw a beautiful view. He saw birds, trees, and a big, blue sky. Spot wanted to go outside and play with his toys under the big, blue sky. He thought it would be fun to organize his toys outside. Spot took his big box of toys outside and started to play. He organized his toys in the green grass and enjoyed the pretty view. The birds sang, and Spot felt happy. He played all day long, and when it was time to go inside, Spot put his toys back in the big box. Spot had a great day playing and organizing his toys outside with the beautiful view.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was very organized. He liked to keep all his toys in the right place. Tim had a watch. It was a gift from his mom. He loved his watch so much, he would wear it everywhere he went. One sunny day, Tim went to the park with his friend, Sam. They played on the swings and slides. Tim showed his watch to Sam. Sam said, "Wow, I like your watch! I don't own one like that." Tim smiled and said, "Thank you! My mom gave it to me." Suddenly, a little squirrel came running up to them. It took Tim's watch and ran away! Tim and Sam were shocked. They chased the squirrel, but it was too fast. They were sad and tired. Just then, the squirrel came back with the watch. It had found a shiny stone and put it on the watch. Tim and Sam laughed. The squirrel wanted to make the watch look even better. Tim thanked the squirrel, and they all played together in the park.
