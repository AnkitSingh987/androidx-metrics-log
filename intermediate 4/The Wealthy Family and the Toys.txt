Category: Intermediate
Title: The Wealthy Family and the Toys

Once upon a time there was a wealthy family that lived in a big house. One day, an emergency happened and everyone had to sort things out. Mummy asked the three year old to go to his room. The child wondered why, but mummy said it was to keep him safe. Daddy called the emergency services and sorted out the problem. The child wanted to help but he was too small. He wished he was old enough to help. Suddenly Mummy said, “Why don’t you sort the toys in your room? That way you can still help.” The child was so proud to be able to help. He quickly grabbed all the toys and started sorting them into piles. The family was so relieved when the emergency was sorted and the house was safe again. The three year old was proud to have helped make his house safe again. He knew he wasn’t quite old enough yet, but he was so pleased to have done his part to help sort out the emergency.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to bounce on her bed. One day, her friend Tom came over to play. "Let's bounce on the bed!" said Amy. Tom was a little clumsy, but he wanted to have fun too. So, they both started to bounce on the bed, laughing and playing. Suddenly, Tom fell off the bed and started to cry. Amy stopped bouncing and went to help him. "I'm sorry, Tom. I should have been more careful," she said. Tom's mom came in and hugged him. She told Amy and Tom that it's important to be careful when playing, especially on the bed. The two friends learned their lesson and promised to be more careful next time. From that day on, Amy and Tom always played safely and had lots of fun together. The moral of the story is: always be careful when playing, so everyone can have fun and stay safe.

Lily and Ben were excited to go to the park with Mom. They put on their hats and coats and ran outside. It was very cold and they could see their breath in the air. "Look, Mom, we are dragons!" Ben said, blowing out a puff of white smoke. "Rawr!" Lily said, pretending to have claws and teeth. Mom smiled and said, "You are very brave dragons, but be careful not to get too close to the pond. It is frozen and slippery." Lily and Ben nodded and ran to the swings. They pushed each other higher and higher, laughing and shouting. They felt warm and happy. After a while, they saw a big dog running towards them. It was brown and fluffy and had a red collar. It wagged its tail and barked. "Hi, doggy!" Lily said, jumping off the swing. She wanted to pet the dog and give it a hug. "Wait, Lily, don't!" Ben said, but it was too late. Lily ran to the dog and reached out her hand. The dog licked her face and knocked her down. Lily fell on the ground and slid towards the pond. "Help, help!" Lily cried, as she got closer to the edge. She was scared and cold. She shivered and wished she had stayed on the swing. Mom heard Lily's cry and ran to her. She grabbed her hand and pulled her back. She hugged her and checked if she was hurt. "Are you okay, Lily?" Mom asked, worried. "I'm okay, Mom, but I'm wet and cold," Lily said, sniffling. "I'm sorry, Lily, that was a naughty dog. He didn't mean to hurt you, he just wanted to play. But he should have been on a leash," Mom said, looking around for the owner. "Mom, I have a joke for Lily," Ben said, trying to cheer her up. "What is it, Ben?" Mom asked. "Why did the chicken cross the road?" Ben asked. "I don't know, why?" Lily asked, curious. "To get to the other slide!" Ben said, pointing to the slide on the other side of the pond. Lily and Mom laughed at Ben's joke. They thought it was funny and silly. "Good one, Ben," Mom said, smiling. "Thanks, Mom. Come on, Lily, let's go to the slide. It will be fun," Ben said, holding Lily's hand. "Okay, Ben, let's go. But no more dogs, please," Lily said, wiping her nose. They walked to the slide, holding hands. They felt warm and happy again. They had fun at the park, even if it was cold and slippery.
