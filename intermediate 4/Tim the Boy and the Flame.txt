Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Flame

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a flame. The flame was small and bright. Tim was ready to play with the flame. He thought it was pretty. Tim's big sister, Sue, saw him near the flame. She wanted to encourage Tim to be safe. Sue said, "Tim, please step away from the flame. It can hurt you." But Tim did not listen to Sue. He wanted to touch the flame. Tim touched the flame and it hurt him. He cried loudly. Sue hugged Tim and told him not to touch flames again. From that day, Tim learned that playing with flames was not a good idea.

Once upon a time, there lived a toy called Razor. Razor was the most popular toy in the whole neighbourhood. He was loved by every kid. One day, Razor decided to take a rest. He went to his favourite spot, a large tree in the park. After a few minutes, he heard a voice saying, "Please help me restore my toy!" Razor looked for the source of the voice and he found a little girl. She was crying and holding a toy that was very broken. Razor asked, "What's wrong, little girl?" She replied, "My toy is broken and I want to restore it. Can you help me?" Razor smiled and said, "Of course I can help you. I know how to restore this toy, let's get to work." The little girl was so happy and they got to work. After some hard work, Razor was able to restore the toy and the little girl was very thankful. From then on, Razor was even more popular in the neighbourhood. The end.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big slide and wanted to play on it. Tim ran to the slide and climbed up the steps. He sat down at the top and slid down fast. "Whee!" he said, laughing. In the sand near the slide, Tim found a small, harmless bug. It was green like an olive. The bug looked lost. Tim asked, "Do you need help, little bug?" The bug nodded and made a tiny sound. Tim picked up the olive-green bug and helped it find its way back to the grass. The bug said, "Thank you, Tim!" and waved goodbye. Tim smiled and went back to the slide to play some more.

Once upon a time, there was a group of friends who wanted to explore a big, old building. When they got to the door, one of they said that it was difficult to open. So they all used their strength to push and pull until the door was open. Inside was a big, dark hall. It was so dark that the friends couldn't see anything. Suddenly, one of them noticed a light coming from somewhere. So, the friends followed the light until they saw a fire burning. The friends were scared. But, then one of they said that the fire was probably their exit from the dark hall. So, the friends followed the fire until they found the exit. But it wasn't easy because the fire was burning and hot. Finally, the friends found a way out. They were very happy and could see the sun. They all happily ran outside and never went back to the old building again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who lived on a farm. One day, the girl decided to take a walk down the dirt road and count the trees she saw. She counted one tree, two and three. As she got closer to the fourth tree, she had a funny feeling inside. When the little girl reached the fourth tree, she noticed it was different than the rest. Its trunk was a strange texture and looker cleaner than the others. She counted it to make sure it wasn't missed! The little girl was excited to tell the farmer about the fourth tree. When she told him, he smiled because he knew it was special. He explained it was a fruit tree and soon it would have delicious apples on it. The farmer said they'd come back when it was ready and they would count the apples together! The little girl couldn't believe it! She had helped noticed the fourth tree and now they were going to count the apples on it! She smiled to herself, feeling proud of her discovery. She couldn't wait to come back to this special place and count the apples with the farmer.
