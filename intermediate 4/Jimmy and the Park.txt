Category: Intermediate
Title: Jimmy and the Park

Jimmy was taking a walk with his mummy in the park. He saw something strange. "Mummy, what's that?" Jimmy said. "That's an ostrich," said his mummy. "It's a very big bird." Jimmy and his mum went closer to the ostrich. It was heavy and big. Jimmy and his mum stopped and shared some food with the ostrich. The ostrich was very happy and ate the food. Jimmy thought it was very fun and told his mummy, "Let's do this again tomorrow!" Mummy smiled and said, "That sounds like a great idea!"

Once upon a time, there was a taxi driver named Tom. He was very intelligent. One day, Tom found a big sack in his taxi. He did not know what was inside. Tom asked his friend, "What do you think is in the sack?" His friend said, "I don't know. Maybe it is full of toys!" Tom decided to take the sack home and open it. That night, Tom opened the sack, and to his surprise, it was full of balloons! He remembered a party he had to go to. Tom took the balloons to the party, and everyone was happy. The balloons made the party so much fun, and Tom was glad he found the sack in his taxi.

There was a mommy who had a baby. When the baby got older, the mommy wanted to give it something to help it stay safe and not wander off. So she chose a rail. It was long and provided a safe place for the baby to play. The baby was very happy and obedient. It followed the rail around the house, playing and having fun. Mommy made sure the rail was always in the same place, so that the baby knew where it was and stayed safe. The baby stayed obedient and stayed close to the rail. It never wandered off or did anything dangerous. The rail was like a guard, watching over the baby and providing it with a safe place to play.

Once upon a time, in a normal classroom, a little boy named Tim found a big, round ball. He liked to roll the ball around the room. All the kids in the classroom loved to watch the ball roll. One day, Tim said to his friend, "Let's roll the ball outside!" His friend was excited and said, "Yes! Let's do it!" So, they took the ball outside and started to roll it on the ground. As they rolled the ball, it went too far and rolled into the street. A big car came and hit the ball. The ball popped and was not round anymore. Tim and his friend were sad. They knew they should not have taken the ball outside.

Once upon a time, there was a big, impressive fish named Bob. Bob lived in a tank with other little fish. They all loved to swim and play together. One day, Bob saw a new fish in the tank. The new fish looked sad and lonely. Bob wanted to care for the new fish and help him feel happy. Bob swam over to the new fish and said, "Hi, I am Bob. Let's play together!" The new fish smiled and played with Bob. Soon, they were best friends. The new fish was happy, and Bob was happy to care for his new friend. And they all lived happily in their impressive tank.

One day, Tom and his dad went to the woods. Tom saw a big tree. He wanted to help his dad cut it down. His dad had an axe. Tom said, "Dad, I want to use the axe." His dad smiled and said, "No, Tom. The axe is not safe for you. I insist you stay away from it." Tom was sad. He wanted to help. His dad saw this and said, "Don't worry, Tom. You can help me carry the wood when the tree is down." Tom was happy. He helped his dad carry the wood back home. They had a fun day in the woods. And Tom learned that the axe was not safe for him.

Once upon a time, in a warm land, there lived a group of animals. They liked to march and play all day. They had so much fun together. One day, a little cat named Tom joined them. He wanted to march and play too. The other animals were happy to have Tom with them. They said, "Come, Tom! Let's march and play together!" So, Tom and the other animals marched and played all day long. They marched through the warm land, laughing and having fun. They were all very happy to be friends.
