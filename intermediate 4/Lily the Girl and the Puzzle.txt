Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Puzzle

One day, a little girl named Lily found a puzzle in her room. It was a big puzzle with lots of pieces. She wanted to put it together, so she sat down and started. Her little brother, Tim, saw her playing and wanted to help. But he was very rude and took a piece from her hand. Lily did not like this, so she whispered to Tim, "Please be nice and ask if you want to help." Tim felt bad and whispered back, "I'm sorry, can I help you?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, we can do the puzzle together." They both worked on the puzzle and had fun. In the end, they finished the puzzle and were very happy.

One day, a girl named Lily and her brother Tom went on a holiday with their mom and dad. They were very happy to play at the beach. The sun was shining and the water was blue. They built a big sandcastle and found pale shells to put on it. Lily and Tom began to quarrel about who found the prettiest shell. "My shell is better!" said Lily. "No, mine is!" said Tom. Their mom and dad told them to stop fighting and share the shells. They did not want the holiday to be sad. Just then, a little crab came out of the water. It was carrying a very pretty shell, even nicer than the ones they found. The crab gave the shell to Lily and Tom. They were so surprised! They stopped quarreling and said thank you to the crab. Now they had a new friend and a beautiful shell to share. The holiday was happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy soldiers. One day, his mom gave him a new toy soldier with a big flag. Tim said to his toy soldiers, "Let's march! Be obedient and follow the flag!" The toy soldiers marched behind the flag, just like Tim asked. They marched all around the room and had a lot of fun. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. Sam wanted to play with the toy soldiers too, but he didn't want to march or be obedient. Tim said, "Sam, if we all march and follow the flag, we can have more fun together." Sam listened to Tim and they both marched with the toy soldiers. They had a great time and learned that being obedient and working together can make playtime even more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mommy and daddy. Lily loved to play with her toys in her old bathtub. Every night, she would splash and laugh in the water. One day, Lily's bathtub had a big problem. It would not hold any water. Lily was very sad. She wanted to solve the problem so she could play in the bathtub again. "Mommy, what should we do?" she asked. Her mommy said, "Let's think and solve this problem together." Lily and her mommy looked at the bathtub. They found a small hole at the bottom. They needed to fix the hole to solve the problem. Lily's mommy got some tape and put it over the hole. Lily filled the bathtub with water, and the water stayed in! Lily was so happy. She said, "Thank you, mommy! We solved the problem!" Now, Lily could play in her old bathtub again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very hungry. He wanted to eat a big apple. He asked his mom, "Mom, can I have an apple, please?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim. I will send you to the store to buy an apple." Tim went to the store. On the way, he saw a big book. The book was on the ground. Tim picked up the book. He thought, "I will give this book to my friend, Jane. She likes books." Tim bought the apple and went to Jane's house. He gave her the book. Jane was very happy. She said, "Thank you, Tim!" They both sat down and read the book together. After reading, Tim ate his apple. It was a good day.
