Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Door

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a big closet. It was very pretty. Lucy liked the closet a lot. She wanted to play in it. She opened the door and saw many nice things inside. Lucy's mom said, "Lucy, be careful! Don't go in the closet alone. I want to prevent you from getting lost." Lucy listened to her mom and did not go in the closet. Later, Lucy and her mom went into the closet together. They found a beautiful dress and a funny hat. They had a fun time playing dress-up. Lucy was happy she listened to her mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little sheep named Fluffy. Fluffy was very useful on the farm. She gave wool to keep everyone warm. One day, Fluffy went to play with her friends. While playing, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took Fluffy's wool away! Fluffy was sad and cold. Her friends wanted to help, but they didn't know how. They asked the farmer to allow them to look for the wool. The friends looked everywhere for Fluffy's wool. They found it on a tree! The wind had put it there. The friends took the wool and gave it back to Fluffy. But when Fluffy touched the wool, it turned into beautiful flowers! Fluffy was happy because she could still be useful. She gave the flowers to the farmer, and everyone lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Lisa. She was only three-years-old and was always playing in her garden. One day, she noticed that a little bird was stuck in a tree. So, Lisa ran to the tree and climbed up. She grabbed the little bird carefully and then she brought it down. When Lisa reached the ground, the bird started to behave badly. She was so scared that she started to cry. Suddenly, Lisa's mommy came out of the house wearing her red vest. She said to her daughter, "Don't be so scared, my little one. You were so brave to help the little bird." Then she added, "Let's put the bird in the vest so it can rest until its mommy comes back. She'll take care of it". Little Lisa agreed and helped her mommy to put the bird inside her vest. Then she remembered not to be stupid and to be careful with the bird. After a few minutes, the bird's mommy came back and they all said goodbye to the little bird. Lisa was so proud of her brave behaviour and she promised herself to always behave in a kind and gentle way. The end!

Anna and Ben were playing with their toys in the living room. They had a lot of toys, but their favorite was a modern board that had buttons and lights and sounds. They liked to press the buttons and make the board do different things. "Look, Anna, I can make the board say 'hello'!" Ben said, pressing a button. "Wow, that's cool, Ben! Can I try?" Anna asked, reaching for the board. "No, it's mine! You have your own toys!" Ben said, pulling the board away. Anna felt sad and angry. She wanted to play with the board too. She tried to grab the board from Ben, but he was stronger. They started to fight over the board, pulling and pushing and shouting. "Stop it, you two!" their mom said, coming into the living room. "What are you doing? Why are you fighting?" "Ben won't let me play with the board!" Anna said, crying. "Anna wants to take my board!" Ben said, frowning. Mom looked at the board and shook her head. "This board is not yours, Ben. It's a gift from your aunt. She sent it for both of you to share and enjoy. You should be kind and generous, not selfish and greedy." "But I like the board more than Anna!" Ben said. "That doesn't matter. You have to learn to share and take turns. Sharing is caring. How would you feel if Anna didn't let you play with her toys?" Mom asked. Ben thought for a moment. He remembered how much he liked Anna's teddy bear and how she always let him hug it. He felt sorry for being mean to Anna. He looked at Anna and saw that she was still crying. "I'm sorry, Anna. I was wrong. You can play with the board too. Here, I'll release it for you." Ben said, letting go of the board. Anna smiled and wiped her tears. She took the board and hugged Ben. "Thank you, Ben. I'm sorry too. You can play with my teddy bear anytime." Anna said. Mom smiled and hugged them both. "That's very nice of you, Anna and Ben. I'm proud of you for making up and sharing. Now, why don't you play with the board together and have fun?" Mom said. Anna and Ben nodded and smiled. They pressed the buttons and made the board say 'hello' and 'thank you' and 'I love you'. They learned that sharing is caring and that playing together is more fun than fighting.
