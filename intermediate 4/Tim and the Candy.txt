Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Candy

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big bag of candy. Tim loved his candy very much. He liked to share it with his friends. One day, Tim went to the park to play. He took his candy with him. At the park, Tim met a girl named Sue. She was fit and liked to run fast. Tim asked Sue, "Do you want some candy?" Sue said, "Yes, please!" So, Tim gave her some candy. They played and ate candy together. After playing, Tim saw that his candy was gone. He was sad because he lost his candy. Sue said, "Don't be sad, Tim. We can find more candy." They looked everywhere and found more candy under a tree. Tim and Sue were very happy. They ate the candy and played more games.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She liked to design things. One day, she wanted to make a shelf for her delicate toys. She asked her mom, "Can you help me make a shelf?" Her mom said, "Yes, Lily. Let's design a shelf together." They drew a picture of a shelf on paper. Then, they built the shelf with wood and paint. Lily was very happy with her new shelf. Lily put her delicate toys on the shelf. But, she put too many toys on it. The shelf broke and all her toys fell down. They broke too. Lily was sad. She learned to be more careful next time.

Once upon a time, there was a big, old cat. The old cat had a bag, or a "sack". One day, the old cat found some butter. The butter was in a little house. The old cat wanted the butter, so he put the butter in his sack. The old cat was walking with his sack. He saw a little mouse. The little mouse said, "Hello, old cat! What is in your sack?" The old cat said, "I have butter in my sack. Do you want some?" The little mouse said, "Yes, please!" The old cat gave the little mouse some butter. The little mouse was very happy. The old cat and the little mouse became friends. They shared the butter and played together. The old cat learned that sharing is good. Sharing makes you and your friends happy. And that was the moral of the story: sharing is caring.

Once upon a time, there was a big orange cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with her toy razor. One day, Kitty saw her friend, Doggy, playing outside. Kitty said, "Doggy, let's get my toy razor and play together!" Doggy was happy and said, "Yes, Kitty! I would love to play with your toy razor." So, Kitty and Doggy played with the toy razor all day. They took turns and shared the toy. They had so much fun together. At the end of the day, they were both very tired and happy. Kitty and Doggy learned that sharing and playing together made them both happy. The moral of the story is that sharing with friends is important and can make everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a white dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and lead his friends on fun adventures. One day, Spot found a peanut on the ground. He thought it was a toy, so he picked it up with his mouth and ran to show his friends. Spot met his friend, Cat, and said, "Look, I found a peanut!" Cat looked at the peanut and said, "Be careful, Spot. Some friends can get sick from peanuts." Spot did not listen and ran to find more friends to show his new toy. Spot showed the peanut to his friend Bird. Bird said, "Oh no, Spot! I can't play with that. It can make me very sick." Spot was sad but still did not listen. He ran to find more friends to play with his peanut. Finally, Spot met his friend Squirrel. Squirrel was very happy to see the peanut. "Yum!" said Squirrel, and ate the peanut right away. But then, Squirrel started to feel sick. He had never eaten a peanut before, and it made him very sick. Spot felt bad and wished he had listened to his friends. From that day on, Spot promised to be more careful and listen to his friends.
