Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Fairy and the Frog

Once upon a time, there was a little fairy named Lily. Lily was a very shy fairy. She would get embarrassed when other fairies talked to her. She liked to play alone in the woods. One day, while Lily was playing, she saw a big frog. The frog said, "Hello, fairy!" Lily felt embarrassed and tried to ignore the frog. But the frog hopped closer and closer to her. "Why do you ignore me?" asked the frog. Lily took a deep breath and said, "I'm sorry, I'm just shy." The frog smiled and said, "That's okay. I have a secret to tell you. I am not a real frog!" The frog turned into a prince! Lily was so surprised. The prince and Lily became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to play near the water. One day, while he was playing, he saw a big fish. The fish was in the water, and it looked dangerous. The fish said, "Tim, don't stand too close to the water. It can be dangerous for little boys like you." Tim listened to the fish and took a step back. He didn't want to be in danger. Tim said, "Thank you, fish. I will be careful near the water." The fish smiled and swam away. Tim played near the water, but he always remembered to be careful and not stand too close.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Jane. Jane was only three years old, and the most important thing to her was her nap. Every day she would take a nap, and it always made her so happy. One day, Jane went with her mommy to the noisy market. Jane had never been to the market before, and she was so excited. The market was full of colorful fruits and vegetables. But it was so noisy, that Jane had to cover her ears! "Mommy," she asked. "Can I take my nap here?" Her mother laughed. "No, sweetie," she said. "Let's take your nap when we get home." So Jane and her mommy went home and Jane took a long, happy nap. It was exactly what she needed to get ready for the next noisy market trip.

Once upon a time, in a small room, there was a small piano. It was red and shiny. The piano had many keys that made pretty sounds. A little girl named Lily loved to play with the small piano every day. One day, Lily's mom and dad said they wanted her to perform for them. Lily was very happy. She practiced playing her small piano all day. She wanted to make her mom and dad proud of her. When it was time to perform, Lily sat down and played her small piano. She played a nice song that made everyone smile. Her mom and dad clapped their hands and hugged her. They were so proud of Lily and her small piano.

One day, a boy named Tim found a kite. The kite was in a big tree. Tim wanted to fly the kite high in the sky. He tried to get the kite down, but it was stuck. Tim had an idea. He would count to three and jump to get the kite. Tim started to count, "One, two, three!" He jumped high and got the kite. He was so happy. Tim ran to the park with the kite. The wind was blowing just right. He knew it was time to fly the kite. At the park, Tim held the kite and started to run. The wind caught the kite and it went up, up, up. The kite flew high in the sky. Tim was so proud. He counted how long the kite stayed in the sky. The kite flew high for a very long time. Tim had a great day with his kite.

One day, a boy named Tim found a dull motorcycle in his yard. He wanted to make it shiny and new. So, he took a cloth and began to clean it. As he cleaned, he found a small box under the seat. Tim opened the box and saw a pretty stone. He wanted to hide it, so no one would take it. He thought, "I will bury it in the ground." He dug a hole with his hands and put the stone inside. Then, he covered it with dirt. Later, his friend Sam came over to play. Sam saw the motorcycle and said, "Wow! What a nice motorcycle!" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, and I found a secret stone too!" Sam asked, "Where is it?" Tim whispered, "I buried it in the ground to keep it safe." They both laughed and played with the motorcycle all day.
