Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Enthusiastic Boy and the Bikes

Once upon a time, there was an enthusiastic boy named Tim. Tim loved to ride his purple bike. He would ride it up and down the street with a big smile on his face. One sunny day, Tim met a new friend named Sue. Sue saw Tim riding his purple bike and asked, "Can I ride with you?" Tim said, "Yes, let's ride together!" So, Sue got her red bike, and they rode all around the neighborhood. As they rode, they laughed and talked. Tim and Sue became best friends. They loved riding their bikes together, and they had lots of fun. And from that day on, Tim and Sue always rode their bikes together, sharing many happy adventures.

---

One day, a fast rabbit saw a big pile of hay. The rabbit did not recognize the hay. He thought it was a big, soft bed. So, the rabbit jumped on the hay to take a nap. A horse saw the rabbit on the hay. The horse was not happy. The hay was for the horse to eat, not for the rabbit to sleep on. The horse walked to the rabbit and said, "This is my hay. You can't sleep here." The rabbit felt bad. He did not want to make the horse mad. The rabbit said, "I'm sorry. I did not know this was your hay." The horse and the rabbit became friends. They played together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little child named Tim. Tim was scared of the dark. One night, Tim's mom gave him a special hat to wear. The hat had a light on it. "Wear this hat, and you won't be scared," said Mom. Tim wore the hat to bed every night. He was not scared anymore. One day, Tim's friend, Sue, came to play. Sue saw the hat and said, "I want to wear the hat too!" Tim let Sue wear the hat, but then the light went out. Sue was scared, but Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's find a new light for the hat!" They looked and looked, and then they found a big, bright star. They put the star on the hat, and Sue was not scared anymore. Now, Tim and Sue could play in the dark together, wearing the hat with the star. And they were not scared anymore.

Once upon a time there was a little boy, who loved playing with his dad. One day the little boy decided to borrow his father's jug. He took it very carefully outside, so that he did not spill any of the liquid. The little boy enjoyed playing with the jug. He liked the way it felt in his hands, because it was so smooth. He noticed it was actually quite heavy, so he decided to fill it up with stones from the nearby river. The little boy was so proud, that he decided to take it to show his dad. But before he got to the house, the jug suddenly slipped from his hands and it fell to the ground. All the stones spilled out and the jug was broken. The little boy was so disappointed. He felt like he had made a big mistake and he suffered for it. He did not know what he was going to do tell his dad. Just then, his dad spotted him from the window. He hurried out and hugged the little boy. He said, "Don't worry about the jug, these things happen. Don't suffer for it". The little boy smiled and knew that he could always count on his dad.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly girl named Judy. She had a flexible toy called a trampoline. She was very proud of it. One day, Judy was playing on her trampoline and her voice became loud. Her mum said, "Judy, please be quiet. Shut your voice." Judy was a bit sad, but she did what her mum said and shut her voice. Judy wasn't allowed to play on the trampoline anymore, so she needed to find something else to do. She saw her mum's broom and decided to have some fun. Judy had a great time pretending she was a witch. She waved the broom around and made magical sounds. Finally, Judy's mum said, "Judy, you can be flexible. You can use the broom to clean the floor and make it shiny. That's a good job for you!" Judy was very happy. She was not only allowed to do something fun, but also something helpful. The end.
