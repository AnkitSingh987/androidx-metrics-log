Category: Intermediate
Title: Remy the Rabbit and the Song

Once upon a time, in a pretty forest, there lived a little rabbit named Remy. Remy loved to plan fun things to do with his friends. One day, he planned a big picnic for all his friends to enjoy. As they started to eat, they heard a loud thunder sound. Remy's friends got scared and wanted to run away. But Remy had an idea. He told his friends, "Let's all sit close together and sing a happy song, so we won't be scared of the thunder." So, all the friends sat close, held hands, and sang a happy song. The thunder didn't seem so scary anymore. They all enjoyed the picnic and had a great time. The moral of the story is: when we face our fears together with friends, we can overcome anything.

One day, a cool cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big box full of junk. Tom was very curious. He wanted to know what was inside. So, he decided to peek into the box. As Tom peeked into the box, he saw a small mouse named Jerry. Jerry said, "Hi, Tom! Do you want to play with me?" Tom was surprised but said, "Yes, let's play!" They played and had a lot of fun. Tom and Jerry became best friends. They learned that even though they were different, they could still have fun together. And that was the best day ever.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a smart cat named Tom. Tom loved to serve food to his animal friends. He wanted to make sure no one was hungry. One day, Tom saw a lot of food waste in the park. He had an idea. He could use the waste to feed his friends. Tom was very happy with his plan. Tom took the food waste and made a big meal for his friends. All the animals came and ate the food. They were very happy and full. Tom felt good because he helped his friends and did not let the waste go to waste.

Once upon a time, there was a great big cat named Tom. He liked to tease a little mouse named Jerry. They lived in a house with many rooms. Tom would run and chase Jerry all day long. One day, Tom and Jerry found a big book. The book was about history. They looked at the pictures and saw many great things. They saw big castles, kings, and queens. They wanted to learn more about history. Tom and Jerry stopped teasing each other. They became great friends. They read the history book together every day. They learned about great people and places. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted chicken named Chicky. Chicky could do many things that other chickens could not do. She could jump high, run fast, and even talk to people. Chicky lived on a farm with her friends. One day, Chicky met a little boy named Timmy. Timmy was sad because he lost his toy. Chicky wanted to help Timmy. She said, "Don't cry, Timmy. I will help you find your toy." So, Chicky and Timmy looked for the toy together. As they looked, a big dog came and wanted to take Chicky away. Chicky was scared, but she wanted to keep helping Timmy. She said to the dog, "Please don't take me. I want to help my friend." The dog listened to Chicky and let her go. Chicky and Timmy found the toy and they were very happy. They became best friends and played together every day.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park with her mom. Mia saw a huge tree. She wanted to climb it. Her mom said, "Be careful, Mia!" At the park, Mia saw a boy named Tim. Tim had a cone. The cone was full of ice cream. Mia wanted some ice cream too. Tim saw Mia looking at his cone. He said, "Do you want some?" Mia said, "Yes, please!" Tim offered Mia a big scoop. Mia and Tim played at the park. They ran and jumped. They had so much fun. Then, Mia saw the huge tree again. She remembered her mom's words. Mia knew she had to be careful. She climbed the tree slowly and safely. At the top, she saw a beautiful view. Mia was happy she listened to her mom.
