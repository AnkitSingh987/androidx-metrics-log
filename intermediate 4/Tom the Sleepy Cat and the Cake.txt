Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Sleepy Cat and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy cat named Tom. Tom loved to watch films on the big TV. One day, Tom found a new film to watch. He was very excited. In the film, there was a big cake. "I want to make a cake like that," said Tom. He went to the kitchen and started to mix some things in a big bowl. Tom's friend, Sam the dog, came to help. "What are you doing, Tom?" asked Sam. "I am making a big cake like in the film," said Tom. Tom and Sam put the cake in the oven. They waited and waited. The cake started to grow bigger and bigger. It increased so much that it filled the whole oven! When the cake was done, Tom and Sam shared it with all their friends. They all laughed and had a great time. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a polite boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat tomatoes. One day, he went to the market to buy some tomatoes. When he got there, he saw a big red tomato. He wanted that tomato very much. Tim bought the big red tomato and started to leave the market. On his way home, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew down and took the big red tomato from Tim's hand. Tim was very sad. But then, the little bird came back with the big red tomato. It dropped the tomato into Tim's hand. The little bird didn't want to eat the tomato after all. It just wanted to play with Tim. So, Tim and the little bird became friends and shared the big red tomato. They were both very happy.

Once there was a boy who was very jealous of his friends. He had seen them in a big raft out on the lake. He wanted to go in the raft with them but they wouldn't let him. He was feeling really bad and sad. One day the boy found his own raft and decided he would take it out on the lake himself. He wished he could take it out with his friends, but he was sure they wouldn't let him. So he grabbed the raft and ran down to the docks. When he got there, his friends were already there on their raft. He started to paddle out to them, when suddenly his paddle broke! The boy was so scared, he tried to get back to shore, but the wind was too strong. He was stuck in the middle of the lake and started to cry. His friends had seen him and paddled closer to help him, but when he tried to make it to them, the wind would push him back. Finally, his friends had to shut off their raft and turn back to get help. Unfortunately, by the time they got there the boy had been swept out to sea. The moral of the story is that jealousy will lead you nowhere. The boy wanted to join his friends, but his own jealousy and pride drove him to take risks that could have been avoided. He should have been happy with what he had and not been jealous of his friends.

One day, a little girl saw a mysterious box in her room. She did not know what was inside. She started to worry. She ran to her daddy and said, "Daddy, there is a box in my room. I don't know what's in it." Daddy smiled and said, "Don't worry, dear. Let's go see the box together." They went to her room and saw the box. Daddy opened it, and inside was a pretty doll. The little girl was happy and stopped worrying. She played with her new doll all day. Daddy watched her play and was happy too. They had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, a leopard lived in a jungle. It was a smooth leopard, and it liked to play. One day, it wanted someone to play with, so it let out a roar. The noise echoed around the jungle, so everyone knew the leopard wanted to play. An elephant heard the leopard's roar and stayed nearby. The leopard was happy and said "let's play!" The leopard ran from the elephant and swished its tail back and forth. The elephant chased the leopard around, and the leopard was happy to have a new friend. The leopard and elephant played for a long time until they got tired. Then, the leopard laid down in the sun, still feeling happy and smooth. They spent the rest of the day together, and the leopard thanked the elephant for being a great friend.
