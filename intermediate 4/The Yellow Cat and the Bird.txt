Category: Intermediate
Title: The Yellow Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a yellow cat. This cat was very fierce. It would keep its toys safe from other animals. The yellow cat lived in a big house with a kind family. One day, the yellow cat saw a little bird outside. The bird was scared of the fierce cat. But the cat just wanted to play. It did not want to hurt the bird. So, the yellow cat kept the bird safe too. The bird and the yellow cat became good friends. They would play together every day. The fierce cat was not scary to the bird anymore. They were happy and kept each other safe. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a wise old mixer. It had a big job to do every day. It had to mix all the food for the people in the town. The mixer was very happy to do its job. One day, a spoon and a fork came to the mixer. They wanted the mixer to help them. They had a big quarrel. The spoon said, "I am better at mixing!" The fork said, "No, I am better!" The wise mixer did not know what to do. The mixer said, "I will help you both. Let's mix together." But the spoon and the fork did not want to work together. They kept fighting and fighting. The wise mixer tried to stop them, but they did not listen. The spoon and the fork were so busy quarreling that they did not see the big pot of hot soup. They fell into the pot and got very hot. They cried and cried, but it was too late. The wise mixer was sad because it could not save them. From that day on, the wise mixer never saw the spoon and the fork again. It had to mix all the food alone. The people in the town were sad too. They missed the spoon and the fork. The wise mixer learned that sometimes, even wise mixers cannot stop a bad ending.

One day, a little frog named Tim went to play with his friends. They wanted to play a fun game called "quiz". They asked each other easy questions about animals and colors. Tim was very good at the game and his friends were happy. During the game, Tim saw a beautiful butterfly. He wanted to be friends with the butterfly. Tim said, "Hi, I am Tim. Do you want to play quiz with us?" The butterfly said, "Yes, I would love to play!" After the game, they all felt hungry. Tim's mom had made a delicious cake for them. They all ate the cake and enjoyed it. Tim liked the butterfly so much that he gave her a little kiss on the cheek. The butterfly blushed and they all laughed together. From that day on, they became the best of friends.

Mimi held the smooth waffle in her hand. It was big, round and yellow. She liked the way it felt. She smiled and stuck out her tongue. She couldn't wait to take a bite. Mimi's mommy said to be careful with the waffle. She warned Mimi it was hot, even though the waffle looked smooth and cool. Mimi could feel the heat coming from the waffle. She didn't want to get burned. Mimi held the waffle carefully as she lowered it down and took a bite. It tasted so yummy! She was so happy. She took another bite and smiled. She couldn't stop eating it. The smooth waffle was the best snack ever!

One day, a little girl named Sue and her dog, Max, went for a walk in the park. They loved to play and run around. But today, the park was very boring. There were no other kids or dogs to play with. Sue said to Max, "Let's go find something fun to do." They walked and walked until they found a big tree. Sue thought it would be fun to climb the tree. She started to climb, but Max barked and barked. He was trying to tell her it was not safe. Sue stopped and looked down at Max. She thought about what he was trying to say. She decided to listen to Max and climbed down the tree. Sue said, "Thank you, Max, for keeping me safe." They went back to the park and found some friends to play with. And the day was not boring anymore.
