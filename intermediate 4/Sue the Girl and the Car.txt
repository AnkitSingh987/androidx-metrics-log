Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Car

One day, a girl named Sue went to visit her friend Tom. They liked to play with their toy vehicles. Sue had a red car and Tom had a blue truck. The sun was shining and it was a great day to play outside. As they played, they saw a big, open truck on the street. It was full of toys! Sue and Tom looked at each other and smiled. They ran to the truck and asked the driver, "Can we play with the toys?" The driver laughed and said, "These toys are for all the kids in town! I need help giving them away. Will you help me?" Sue and Tom were so happy. They spent the day giving toys to all the kids and made many new friends. The day was even better than they expected.

One sunny day, a little frog named Tim went to school. He was ready for his big test. Tim wanted to show everyone how smart and useful he could be. Before the test, Tim met his friend, Sally the rabbit. Sally said, "I'm scared of the test. I don't know if I can do it." Tim smiled and said, "Don't be scared, Sally. We can do it together. Let's hop to the test room!" Tim and Sally hopped all the way to the test room. They saw their teacher, Mr. Owl. Mr. Owl said, "Today is the big test. Do your best, and show me how useful you can be." Tim and Sally looked at each other and smiled. They took the test and tried their best. When the test was over, Tim and Sally hopped back home. They knew they had done their best and were very useful. And they were happy to be such good friends.

Once there was a boy and his name was Jojo. Jojo loved balloons and everyone in the neighbourhood knew it. He was even a bit popular because of it. One day, Jojo was out playing in the park when he saw something round and colourful in the sky. He looked up and noticed what it was - a big, bright balloon! Jojo smiled and jumped up and down with excitement. He wanted the balloon so badly. So he ran to get it. He wanted to show it off to all his friends in the park. Jojo was so happy when he caught the balloon. He ran around the park holding it and showing it to everyone. Everyone he met was so impressed and smiled, too. Jojo enjoyed his new balloon so much that he couldn't stop smiling. He proudly showed it off to everyone! He was very happy and popular.

Jill and her dad were in the garden. Dad was trying to get the hose to sprinkle water on the flowers, but it had getting stuck. Dad looked angry. Jill tried to help him, but she couldn't. Dad was so stubborn. He wouldn't ask anyone else for help. Then, Jill looked up at the sky and noticed it was getting dark. She said to her dad, "Let's go to bed, Dad. We can try to fix the hose tomorrow." Dad thought it was a good idea. He smiled and said, "OK, let's go." The next morning, they came back to the garden. Dad looked around and said, "Where is the hose?" Jill smiled and said, "It's over there! I fixed it while you were sleeping!" Dad was so amazed. He hugged Jill and said, "You are so clever!" Jill was happy and proud of herself for being so stubborn and fixing the hose.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mommy and daddy. Lily loved to play outside in the sun with her friends. One day, Lily saw a pretty pony near her house. The pony was running and jumping in the grass. Lily wanted to catch the pony and play with it. She ran as fast as she could, but the pony was too fast for her. Lily felt sad, but she did not give up. She had a lucky idea. She picked some yummy apples from a tree and put them on the ground. The pony saw the apples and came to eat them. Lily was so happy. She caught the pony and they played together all day long.
