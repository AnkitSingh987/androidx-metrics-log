Category: Intermediate
Title: Sarah and the Park

Once upon a time there was a very foolish girl called Sarah. She was three years old and did not know what to do. She saw other children having fun at parties and really wanted to go. One day, Sarah saw a party outside in the park. She asked her mum if she could go. Her mum said, “No, you don’t know anyone there, you must stay here instead”. Sarah felt very sad but then she had an idea. She asked her mum, “Can I throw my own party?” Her mum replied, “Yes, if you invite your friends and tell me who is coming.” Sarah smiled and she knew it was time to start planning the party. So, Sarah ran to the park to tell all her friends about the party she was planning. Her friends were so excited and wanted to come. Sarah’s party was a success and everyone had great fun. Sarah knew she was no longer foolish. She now knew that if you want something, you can make it happen.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a healthy kangaroo named Kiki. Kiki loved to jump, play, and balance on her strong legs. One sunny day, Kiki met a little bird named Bobby. "Hi, I'm Kiki the kangaroo! I can balance very well. Can you balance too?" asked Kiki. "Hi, Kiki! I'm Bobby the bird. I can't balance like you, but I can fly!" said Bobby. Kiki and Bobby became good friends. They played together every day. Kiki would balance on one leg, and Bobby would fly around her. They laughed and had so much fun. They were the best of friends in the big green forest.

Marie was busy playing in the park when she noticed two people walking towards her. They were holding hands and smiling. Marie was curious as they smiled at her in an unknown way. She ran over to them and asked who they were. They introduced themselves as Grandma and Grandad, and Marie was so surprised. She had never heard of them before, and wasn't sure what they wanted. Grandma bent down and held Marie's hands, and told her they had a surprise. Grandma and Grandad took her to a restaurant she had never seen before. The surprise was that they had bought her lunch - a big plate of her favorite food! Marie was so happy, and thanked them for the wonderful surprise.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Joanna. She was three years old and she loved pink. Everywhere she went, she wanted to find something pink. One day, Joanna went outside and she saw a turn. It was pink! "Mine!" she shouted and she ran over to it. Then, her mom called out to her, "No Joanna, you cannot own that turn. It belongs to the park." Frustrated, Joanna looked back at the pink turn and said, "But I want it!" Her mom smiled and said, "I know you do, but you can't own it. You can turn it, or look at it, but it's not yours." Joanna paused and thought. Then she nodded, "Okay, I can turn it." And so, every day, Joanna went back to the park and turned the pink turn to her heart's content. It was her very own pink turn, even if it wasn't hers to own.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play at the park. They run, jump, and swing. They have fun. One day, they see a new slide at the park. It is big and shiny. It looks cool. Lily and Ben want to try it. "Can we go on the slide?" Lily asks Ben. "Yes, let's go!" Ben says. They run to the slide. They wait for their turn. They are excited. Lily goes first. She climbs up the ladder. She sits on the slide. She slides down. She screams. "Wow, that was fast!" Lily says. Ben goes next. He climbs up the ladder. He sits on the slide. He slides down. He screams. "Wow, that was fun!" Ben says. They laugh. They hug. They go on the slide again. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Tiny. Tiny loved to sit on his soft pillow. He was an independent dog and liked to do things by himself. One day, Tiny saw a big pillow across the room. He wanted to sit on it, but it was too far away. Tiny thought and thought about how to get to the big pillow. He saw his friend, the cat, jump from one spot to another. Tiny had an idea. He would jump to the big pillow just like the cat! Tiny took a deep breath and jumped as hard as he could. He landed on the big pillow! He felt so happy and proud. Now, Tiny could sit on his big, soft pillow all by himself. He had learned how to be even more independent, just like his friend the cat.
