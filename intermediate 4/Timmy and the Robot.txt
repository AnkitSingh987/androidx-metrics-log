Category: Intermediate
Title: Timmy and the Robot

Once upon a time there were two friends - a 3 year old called Timmy and a robot called Rusty. Timmy and Rusty lived on a planet far away. One day, Timmy and Rusty went for a walk. They saw something that caught their eye: an old spaceship! Timmy was excited and said to Rusty, ‘Let’s own it and go on a journey across the planet!’ Rusty shook his head and said, ‘No, it looks careless. We must be careful and not take any risks.’ But Timmy was too excited and didn’t listen. He opened the spaceship and said, ‘Let’s go explore the planet!’ Rusty was worried, but he followed Timmy anyway. They had a great time exploring the planet and they made many new friends. In the end, they realized that being careless can get you into trouble. But they also learned that being brave and exploring the world is really fun.

Once there was a dog named Max. Max was a deaf dog. He could not hear. Max had a job. His job was to help people. One day, Max saw a cat in a tree. The cat was scared. The cat tried to come down. The cat struggled to climb down. Max wanted to help the cat. Max barked and jumped. People came to see. They saw the cat. They got a ladder. The cat came down. Max was happy. The cat was happy. Max did a good job.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim wanted to go outside and play, but the weather was not good. It was raining, and Tim felt frustrated. He wanted to play with his friends in the park. Tim's mom saw him feeling sad. She had an idea! She said, "Let's prepare a fun game to play inside." Tim smiled and helped his mom get ready for the game. They took out colorful papers and markers to draw with. Together, they drew pictures of the sun, clouds, and rain. Tim forgot about the bad weather outside. He was happy playing with his mom inside. They laughed and had a great time. In the end, Tim didn't feel frustrated anymore.

One day, a pink cat named Kitty went to clean her house. She liked her house to be very clean. While she was cleaning, she saw the mailman coming. The mailman had a big bag of mail for everyone. Kitty went outside to get her mail. She saw a big box with her name on it. "What is in the box?" she asked the mailman. "I don't know," said the mailman, "but it is for you." Kitty was very excited and took the box inside. Kitty opened the box and found a big mess. There was dirt and mud everywhere! She was sad because her house was not clean anymore. But then, she saw a note in the box. The note said, "Surprise! We sent you a new toy to play with. But first, you have to clean the mess." Kitty smiled and started to clean. When she was done, she found a fun toy under the dirt. It was a pink ball! Kitty played with her new toy all day and forgot about the mess.

Tom and Lily were playing with a baseball in the yard. They liked to throw and catch the ball. Sometimes they missed and the ball rolled away. "Tom, you throw too hard!" Lily said. "Now the ball is under the fence. How can we get it?" Tom looked at the fence. It was very low and had a small gap. He had an idea. "I can crawl under the fence and get the ball," he said. "You stay here and watch." Tom lay down on the grass and put his head under the fence. He saw the ball on the other side. He moved his arms and legs and crawled under the fence. He grabbed the ball and crawled back. "Here, I got it!" he said, holding up the ball. "That was fun!" Lily clapped her hands and smiled. "You are very brave, Tom," she said. "That was fair. You threw the ball and you got it back. Now it's my turn to throw." Tom and Lily continued to play with the ball. They were happy and had a good time.
