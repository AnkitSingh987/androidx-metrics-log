Category: Intermediate
Title: The Great Ball of Tim and the Park

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. The sun was shining and the air was calm. Tim saw his friend, Sue, playing with a ball. He ran to her and said, "Hi Sue! Can I play too?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, Tim! Let's get the ball and play together." They played catch and had lots of fun. After a while, they got tired and sat down on the grass. Tim's mom came over with a big ice cream cone. She said, "Here, Tim. Have a taste of this sweet treat!" Tim took a bite and his eyes grew wide. "Yum!" he said. "Sue, do you want a taste too?" Sue nodded and took a bite. They both enjoyed the ice cream and felt happy on this calm and sunny day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim had a big sister named Sue. Sue was very competitive. She always wanted to be the best at everything. One day, Tim and Sue decided to have a race. They ran as fast as they could, but Sue won. Tim felt sad. Sue saw that Tim was sad and wanted to make him feel better. So, Sue offered Tim a cup of his favorite juice. Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Sue!" They drank their juice together and had a great time. From that day on, they learned to have fun together without being too competitive. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a big dog named Max wanted to provide a fun game for his friends. He thought of a game to play with a ball. Max told his friends, "We can play a game and get a score. It will be fun!" Max and his friends played the game. They had to kick the ball into a box to get a score. They were happy and felt safe while playing. Max said, "I will kick the ball now!" His friends watched as Max got ready to kick the ball. But then, something unexpected happened. A small cat ran in front of the ball and kicked it into the box. Max and his friends were surprised. The cat got a score, too! They all laughed and said, "We did not know cats could play, too!" Now, Max and his friends play the game with the cat, and they all have fun together.

One sunny morning, a bunny and her mommy were taking a walk. The bunny wanted to hop around the wild meadow, so her mommy said, "Okay, but be careful!" The bunny hopped and hopped and soon she saw a strange-looking car. Its wheels were spinning and it made crazy noises. She asked her mom, "What is that?" Her mom said, "That is a broken car, so a mechanic will have to fix it very soon." The bunny was so excited that she asked, "Mommy, can I help the mechanic fix it?" Her mom laughed and replied, "Well, sweetheart, I don't think so." The bunny continued to hop around, and soon she saw the mechanic coming to fix the car. He was carrying tools and wearing a funny hat. He got the car all figured out and when the bunny hopped up to him, he said, "Hey little bunny! You were hoping around so much, I thought you'd like to help me fix it!" The bunny was so excited that she couldn't believe it. With the mechanic's help, they got the car running in no time. After the mechanic had left, the bunny said to her mom, "I can't believe I helped! That was so fun and crazy!"

One day, a small girl named Lily went on a holiday with her mom and dad. They went to a big park with lots of trees and flowers. Lily was very happy to play there. While playing, she saw a surprise box under a tree. Lily was excited and opened the box. Inside the box, there was a small toy cat. She loved the toy cat and played with it all day. At the end of the day, it was time to go home. Lily said goodbye to the park and the toy cat. But, the toy cat jumped out of the box and followed her home! Lily was very surprised and happy to have a new friend.
