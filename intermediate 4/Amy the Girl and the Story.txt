Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to eat fruit. Her favorite fruit was a lime. She liked it because it was green and sour. Amy had a very important job. She had to give her mom a lime every day for her mom's special drink. One day, Amy forgot her important job. She did not give her mom the lime. Her mom was sad because she could not make her special drink. Amy felt bad because she forgot the lime. Amy learned that it was important not to forget things. She made a plan to remember the lime every day. She put a big green lime picture on her wall. Now, Amy never forgets the lime, and her mom is always happy. The moral of the story is to find ways to remember important things, so we don't let others down.

Lily liked to spend time with her mom. They did many fun things together, like playing, reading, and baking. One day, they decided to make a big picture with crayons. Lily chose her favorite colors: pink, purple, and yellow. Mom chose blue, green, and red. They found a big piece of paper and put it on the floor. Lily and mom started to draw with their crayons. Lily drew a flower, a sun, and a heart. Mom drew a house, a tree, and a bird. They smiled at each other and said, "Our picture is very pretty!" But then, Lily saw something that made her sad. Her yellow crayon was broken. It had a big crack in the middle. Lily loved her yellow crayon. It was the color of the sun and the stars. She did not want to lose it. She started to cry. Mom saw Lily crying and hugged her. She said, "Don't worry, Lily. Your crayon is not gone. It is still fine. You can still use it to draw. Look, I have some tape. I can fix your crayon. See? It is good as new!" Lily stopped crying and looked at her crayon. Mom had wrapped some tape around the crack. The crayon was still yellow and shiny. Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, mom. You are the best. I love you." She gave mom a big kiss and a hug. Then, they finished their picture with their crayons. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. He loved to sleep all day in the warm sun. One day, he saw a green lime on the ground. Tom had never seen a lime before, so he decided to play with it. Tom rolled the lime to his friend, Sam the dog. "Sam, I invite you to play with this green thing," said Tom. Sam was excited and barked, "Yes, let's play!" They took turns rolling the lime to each other, having lots of fun. After a while, they got tired and sat in the shade. Tom and Sam were happy they played together. From that day on, the lazy cat and his friend, the dog, always played with the lime, and they were never bored again.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a dog. The dog was naughty. It was running and jumping all over the place. Tim's mom said, "Tim, lead the naughty dog to its home." Tim took a rope and tied it to the dog's collar. He started to lead the dog to its house. On the way, they met a man. The man was holding a big plate of dessert. The dessert looked yummy. The man said, "Hello, little boy! Would you like some dessert?" Tim wanted the dessert, but he knew he had to lead the naughty dog home first. He said, "No, thank you. I need to take this dog home." The man smiled and said, "You are a good boy. Let me give you a surprise." The man gave Tim a magic wand. He told Tim to wave it and say a magic word. Tim did as he was told. Suddenly, the naughty dog turned into a cute, friendly puppy! Tim and his mom were very happy. They thanked the man and took the puppy home. And, of course, they all enjoyed the yummy dessert together.
