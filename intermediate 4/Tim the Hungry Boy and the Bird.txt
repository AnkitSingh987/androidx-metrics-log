Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Hungry Boy and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a hungry boy named Tim. He wanted to find food in the kitchen. Tim saw a big white box. It was the dishwasher. He thought maybe there was food inside. Tim said, "I will unlock the dishwasher!" He opened it and saw many clean plates. But, there was no food. Tim was sad. He did not know where to find food. Then, a little bird flew into the kitchen. The bird said, "I can help you find food, Tim!" The bird took Tim to a secret cupboard. Inside was a big cake! Tim was happy and not hungry anymore. The bird and Tim became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a big green garden, there was a little caterpillar. The caterpillar was very patient. It liked to eat leaves all day long. One sunny day, the caterpillar saw the sun shine so bright. It wanted to shine too. So, it decided to wait and be more patient. After many days, the patient caterpillar changed. It became a beautiful butterfly. It could fly high and shine with the sun. All the other bugs were happy to see it shine. The moral of the story is to be patient and good things will come.

Once upon a time, in a big, big forest, there was a powerful elephant named Ellie. Ellie loved to paint with pastel colors. She would paint trees, flowers, and her friends. One sunny day, Ellie met a little bird named Billy. Billy was sad because he couldn't paint like Ellie. Ellie said, "Don't be sad, Billy. I can help you paint too!" Ellie and Billy found a big tree to paint. Ellie used her powerful trunk to hold the pastel colors. Billy would weigh down on a paintbrush with his little beak. Together, they painted the tree with pretty pastel colors. All of their friends came to see their painting. They all said, "Wow! What a beautiful tree!" Ellie and Billy smiled and said, "Thank you! We did it together!" From that day on, Ellie and Billy painted many more beautiful things with their pastel colors. They were very happy and had lots of fun together.

Tom likes to read books with his mom. He sits on her lap and looks at the pictures. Mom reads the words and makes funny voices. Tom is eager to see what happens next. He turns the page fast. But one day, Tom is too eager. He turns the page too hard. He hears a loud snap. He looks at the book and sees a big tear. He feels sad and scared. He broke the book. Mom will be mad. He shows the tear to mom and says sorry. Mom hugs him and says it's okay. She is not mad. She says books are for reading and loving, not for snapping. She gets some tape and fixes the tear. She says they can still read the book. Tom smiles and says thank you. He turns the page gently. He is eager to see what happens next, but not too eager.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had long hair that she loved very much. One day, she met a boy named Tom. Tom was scared of many things, like big dogs and loud noises. But he was brave when he was with Lily. One day, Tom said, "Lily, I want to marry you." Lily was happy and said, "Yes, I want to marry you too!" They were both very excited about their big day. They started to plan the party and invited all their friends. On the day of the wedding, a big wind came. It blew Lily's hair into a tree. Lily was scared and cried, "Help me, Tom!" But Tom was too scared of the wind. He ran away and left Lily all alone. Lily was very sad and her hair stayed stuck in the tree.

One day, a big dog named Spot had a meeting with his friends. Spot wanted to be clean for the meeting, so he took a bath. After the bath, Spot felt happy and clean. At the meeting, Spot and his friends played with a stick. They took turns to snap the stick. Spot wanted to snap the stick too, but he had to wait for his turn. Finally, it was Spot's turn to snap the stick. He was so happy because he was clean and had a great time with his friends. Spot snapped the stick, and all his friends clapped. The meeting was fun, and Spot was glad he was clean for it.
