Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Hammer

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy hammer. One day, he went to the park with his mom and his hammer. Tim liked to hit things with his hammer, but it was harmless. At the park, Tim saw a big, red ball. He wanted to hit the ball with his hammer. He ran up to the ball and hit it. Suddenly, the ball let out a loud scream! Tim was very scared and dropped his hammer. The ball stopped screaming and started to laugh. Tim looked closer and saw that it was not a ball, but a friendly, round monster. The monster said, "I'm sorry I scared you, Tim. I was just playing a trick. Let's be friends!" Tim smiled and they played together all day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a toy car. It was very attractive. The toy car had a battery inside. The battery made the car go fast. All the kids liked the car. One day, the car did not go. The kids were sad. They did not know what was wrong. They wanted to find out. They asked their mom for help. Mom said, "Let's reveal the problem." She looked inside the car. She saw the battery. The battery was old. Mom put in a new battery. The car went fast again. The kids were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a bit nervous because today was his first day at a new school. He wanted to make new friends, but he was shy. At lunch time, Tim sat down at a big table. He had a sandwich, an apple, and a small cup of sauce. His mom had given him the sauce to make his sandwich taste better. Tim liked the sauce a lot. A girl named Sue came and sat next to Tim. She saw the sauce and asked, "Can I try some?" Tim was happy to share and gave her the sauce. Sue liked it too. They both smiled and became friends. Tim was not nervous anymore. He was happy to receive a new friend because of the sauce.

Once upon a time, there was a humble boy named Tim. He lived in a small house with his mom, dad, and dog, Spot. Tim loved to play outside with Spot. They would run and jump on the soft ground. One sunny day, Tim and Spot decided to have a race. They stood at one end of the yard. Tim's mom said, "Ready, set, go!" Tim and Spot started to run as fast as they could. They laughed as they raced across the ground. In the end, Tim and Spot reached the other side of the yard at the same time. They were both very happy. Tim hugged his humble dog and said, "Good race, Spot!" Then, they went inside to eat a yummy snack with mom and dad.

Tom and Lily were playing in the garden. They liked to pretend they were explorers, looking for new places and animals. They had a big map that they drew with crayons, and a backpack with snacks and toys. "Let's go to the valley today," Tom said, pointing to a part of the map that was green and blue. "Maybe we can find a river or a lake there." Lily shook her head. "No, I want to go to the dry place," she said, pointing to another part of the map that was brown and yellow. "Maybe we can find a dinosaur or a camel there." Tom and Lily argued for a while. Tom insisted that the valley was more fun and beautiful, and Lily insisted that the dry place was more exciting and mysterious. They could not agree on where to go. Then, they heard their mom calling them. "Tom, Lily, it's time for lunch. Come inside, please." Tom and Lily looked at each other. They did not want to stop playing, but they were also hungry and thirsty. They decided to compromise. "OK, how about this," Tom said. "We can go to the valley first, and then we can go to the dry place after lunch. Is that fair?" Lily nodded. "Yes, that's fair. But we have to hurry, or we won't have enough time to explore both places." Tom and Lily packed their backpack and ran inside. They told their mom about their plan, and she smiled. "That sounds like a great adventure," she said. "But first, you have to eat your sandwiches and drink your juice. And don't forget to wash your hands." Tom and Lily agreed. They ate their lunch quickly, and then they went back to the garden. They followed their map to the valley, and then to the dry place. They had a lot of fun, and they saw many things. They even pretended to find a river and a dinosaur, and they took pictures with their toy camera. They were happy that they had listened to each other, and that they had shared their ideas. They were the best explorers ever.
