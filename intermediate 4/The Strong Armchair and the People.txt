Category: Intermediate
Title: The Strong Armchair and the People

Once upon a time there was a strong armchair. It used to sit in a lovely home and the people who lived there were very happy. But one day, an unhappy man came to the house and he wanted to take away the armchair. He was very mean and the people who lived there were scared. The man started to drag the armchair away, but then something amazing happened. One of the people living in house ran over and grabbed the armchair, holding it tight. The strong armchair was saved! The people who lived in the house were so relieved. They thanked the person who saved the armchair, and from that day on, they kept it safe in their house.

One day, a blue bird was flying in the sky. The bird saw a girl named Lisa. Lisa was sad because she had a lot of work to do. The blue bird wanted to help Lisa. The bird flew down and said, "Lisa, can I help you with your work?" Lisa was surprised and said, "Yes, please!" The bird helped Lisa finish her work. They worked together and soon, Lisa was happy again. But then, a big wind came and blew all of Lisa's work away. Lisa was sad again. The blue bird had an idea. It requested help from its bird friends. All the birds flew together and brought back Lisa's work. They fixed it and gave it back to Lisa. The moral of the story is that when we work together and help each other, we can solve any problem.

One day, a pretty cat went for a walk. She liked to step on the soft grass. She saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. The pretty cat stepped on the tree and went up, up, up. When the pretty cat got to the top, she saw a cooler. She was hot from climbing the tree, so she wanted to see what was inside. The pretty cat opened the cooler and found cold drinks and yummy food. The pretty cat took a cold drink and some food from the cooler. She sat on a branch and enjoyed her snack. She was happy to find the cooler in the tree. After eating, the pretty cat stepped down the tree and went back home.

One day, a tall boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom and dad. The sun was bright and the water was cool. Tim loved to play in the sand and make big sand castles. Tim saw a little girl crying near the water. She was sad because she lost her toy boat. Tim wanted to help, so he decided to act like a big hero. He put on his goggles and swam in the water to find the toy boat. After a while, Tim found the toy boat and gave it back to the little girl. She was very happy and said thank you. Tim felt good because he acted like a hero and made a new friend at the beach.

One day, Maggie was feeling hot and sticky. She asked her mum if she could go for a swim. Her mum said, "Yes, but you must be careful not to get too dizzy." Maggie put on her swimming costume and went to the pool. She was having so much fun splashing and swimming around. After a while, the alarm went off. Maggie's mum called out, "Time to dry off Maggie." Maggie dried herself quickly and put her clothes back on. When she felt dry, she went out and saw her mum waiting for her with a big smile. Maggie was so happy to be dry and have had such a fun time at the pool!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a toy that could glow. The toy was a small star. Mia loved her glowing star. One day, Mia lost her star. She looked all around but could not find it. Mia felt sad. Her big sister, Lily, saw Mia's sad face. Lily asked, "Why are you sad, Mia?" Mia said, "I lost my glowing star. I miss it." Lily held Mia's hand and said, "Let's look for it together." They looked everywhere. Mia found her star under her bed. She was so happy! The star was not glowing anymore. Lily said, "Don't worry, Mia. The star just needs some rest. It will glow again soon." Mia put her star in a soft place to rest. She did not want to be harsh with it. After a while, the star began to glow again. Mia and Lily smiled and played with the glowing star together.
