Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bike and the Wheel

Once upon a time there was a big bike. It had two pedals and the biggest back wheel. It was so shiny, it almost glowed. One day, the bike decided it wanted to go for a ride, so it rode off on its own. The bike went faster and faster, until it was so fast that it could fly. It flew all around in the big sky, which made it so happy. But then it started to get tired and the bike was so very sleepy. It tried and tried to stay awake, but suddenly it started to fall asleep in the air. The bike was so sleepy it couldn't make it to the ground. The bike crashed into a big tree, where it stayed until morning. But when the sun rose, something magical happened. All the pedals on the bike started to move by themselves, and soon the bike was able to fly again. And it flew away, happy and free.

Lily and Tom were playing with their toy cars and trucks. They liked to make them go fast and crash into each other. Sometimes they pretended to fix them with their tools and stickers. One day, Tom found a big truck that had a hook at the back. He showed it to Lily and said, "Look, this truck can attach things to it. Let's try it!" They looked around for something to attach to the truck. They saw a teddy bear that was lying on the floor. It was Lily's favorite teddy bear, but it had a hole in its belly and some stuffing was coming out. "Let's attach the teddy bear to the truck!" Tom said. He grabbed the teddy bear and hooked it to the truck. He pulled the truck and the teddy bear followed. Lily was not happy. She ran after Tom and shouted, "Stop, stop! That's my teddy bear! You're hurting it!" Tom did not stop. He said, "No, it's fun! The teddy bear is dead anyway. It has a hole and no heart." Lily felt sad and angry. She caught up with Tom and snatched the teddy bear from the hook. She hugged it and said, "No, it's not dead! It's alive and it loves me. It needs an operation to fix its hole and put a heart inside." Tom looked at Lily and saw that she was crying. He felt sorry and said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I didn't know you liked the teddy bear so much. Can I help you with the operation?" Lily nodded and said, "Yes, please. You can be the doctor and I can be the nurse. We need some thread and a needle and a button for the heart." They went to the kitchen and asked their mom for the things they needed. She gave them a small needle and some red thread and a red button. She also gave them a band-aid and a kiss. They went back to their room and laid the teddy bear on the bed. They worked together to sew the hole and attach the button as the heart. They put the band-aid on the teddy bear's belly and gave it a hug. The teddy bear looked happy and cozy. Lily and Tom smiled and said, "There, the operation is done. The teddy bear is alive and well. We are good friends."

One day, a gifted boy named Tim had a toy plane. He loved to play with the plane all the time. Tim and his friends would race their toy planes to see who could win. One sunny day, Tim and his friends were playing with their toy planes outside. They decided to have a big race to see who could win. They all got ready and started to race their planes. As they raced, something unexpected happened. A real plane flew down from the sky and landed next to them. The pilot got out and said, "I saw you racing your toy planes and it looked like so much fun! Can I join in?" Tim and his friends were very surprised, but they said yes. The pilot raced his real plane against the toy planes. It was a very close race, but in the end, Tim's toy plane won. The pilot was amazed and said, "Wow, your toy plane is so fast! You are a great racer!" From that day on, Tim and his friends had a new friend to play with - the pilot and his real plane. They had many more fun races together, and Tim's gifted toy plane always won.
