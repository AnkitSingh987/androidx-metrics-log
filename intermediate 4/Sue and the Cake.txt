Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue and the Cake

Once upon a time, a pretty girl named Sue loved to design. She was very good at it. One day, Sue wanted to make a cake for her friend's birthday. She needed cream to make the cake yummy. Sue went to the store to buy cream. But when she got there, she saw that there was no cream left! Sue was sad. She didn't know what to do. She thought her friend would be sad too. Then, Sue had an idea. She could design a cake with no cream! She went home and made a pretty cake with lots of colors. When her friend saw the cake, she was so happy! Sue learned that even when things don't go as planned, she can still make something pretty and fun.

One day, a friendly dog named Spot went for a walk. He saw a big place called a stadium. Spot was very excited and wanted to go inside the stadium. As he walked closer, he saw a man standing by a sign. The sign had a picture of a dog and an arrow. Spot asked the man, "What does the sign say?" The man smiled and said, "The sign says that dogs can go inside the stadium too." Spot was so happy. He went inside the stadium and saw many other dogs playing. They were all very friendly. Spot played with his new friends all day long. At the end of the day, he went home tired but very happy.

One day, a little reindeer named Remy went to play with his friends. Remy was very loyal and always helped his friends when they needed him. They liked to play in the snow and have fun together. One of Remy's friends, a small rabbit named Ruby, was sad. She lost her toy and didn't know where to find it. Remy wanted to help her. He said, "Don't worry, Ruby! I will help you find your toy." Ruby was happy that Remy wanted to help her. As they looked for the toy, they met a big bear named Benny. Benny had found the toy and wanted to keep it. Remy said, "Please give the toy back to Ruby. It is her toy." Benny did not want to give it back. Remy did not give up. He said, "You must give it back. It is not nice to keep things that are not yours." Benny saw that Remy was loyal and kind. He gave the toy back to Ruby. Ruby was very happy and thanked Remy for his help. Remy was glad that he could help his friend. The friends learned that it is important to be loyal and to always help each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He lived on a farm with his mom and dad. They grew corn in their big field. The corn was tall and yellow, and Tim loved to play in the field. One day, Tim's mom told him not to forget to water the corn. But Tim was busy playing with his toys and he did not listen. He forgot to water the corn. The next day, the sun was very hot and the corn started to become pale. Tim saw the pale corn and felt sad. He knew he should have listened to his mom. He tried to water the corn, but it was too late. The corn did not grow anymore. Tim's mom and dad were not happy. They had no corn to eat or sell. Tim learned that he should always listen and not forget what he is told to do.

One day, a boy was looking in the garden. He saw a big wild cat. He wanted to pet it, so he slowly walked over. The cat seemed to be deaf, because it didn't move. The boy reached out and touched the cat, but it didn't move. Then he saw that it was dry. Suddenly, the cat jumped up and scratched the boy! It ran away, never to be seen again. The boy was sad, but he had learned his lesson. He knew better than to touch a wild, deaf cat.

Once upon a time, there were two polar bears, Sonny and Shade. One day, Sonny decided to go for a walk and explore. As he was wandering, he spotted something strange in the distance. He couldn't quite make it out. Shade decided to follow and see what it was. As they got closer, they realised it was a dry igloo! Sonny wanted to play in it, but Shade stopped him and said, “Let’s not mention it to anyone else.” The days went on, and Sonny couldn't stop thinking about the dry igloo. He thought they should tell somebody, but Shade kept reminding him not to mention it. After a while, Sonny became impatient and decided to tell everyone he knew. The news soon spread, and before long the dry igloo was the talk of the town. Everyone wanted to see it, and it soon became a tourist destination. The moral of the story is: think before you act. Shade had the right idea to keep the igloo a secret - but Sonny was too eager, and his actions caused the igloo to become famous.
