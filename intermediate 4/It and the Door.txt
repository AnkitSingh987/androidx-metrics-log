Category: Intermediate
Title: It and the Door

It was an alert, sunny morning. Little Jake woke up feeling eager and ready to explore. He saw a big, white dishwasher sitting all alone in the corner of the kitchen. So, he toddled over to it and opened the door. Nothing happened. But, Jake was so curious that he bravely decided to press one of the buttons. Suddenly, there was a loud rumble and the dishwasher began to churn and spin the dishes and utensils around. Jake was so surprised by what happened! He pulled out his toys and played with them, watching the dishwasher go round and round. The next day, things were much quieter. But, when Jake saw the dishwasher he knew something special was about to happen. He pushed the same button and waited. Suddenly, the door slowly opened and a big mound of bubbles tumbled out. Jake clapped his hands and grinned with delight. He had yielded a big, happy surprise!

The little girl wore a red dress every day. One day, she wanted to wear something else. She thought it would be fun to wear a duck. So, the next day, she got a yellow duck costume from her mum. She put it on and felt very funny. Her sister thought the duck was so cute! The little girl wanted to go outside and show off her duck costume. But as soon as she stepped into the garden, she started to feel very rough. Suddenly, she heard a noise in the grass. It was a real duck! The little girl was so happy. She ran over and saw that it was a big, white duck with a fluffy orange beak. The duck was so soft and nice to touch. The little girl cuddled the duck and the two of them played in the garden together. The little girl was so glad she got to make a new friend! She said goodbye to the duck and went back inside. From then on, she always wanted to wear her duck costume anytime she wanted to play with her new feathered friend.

Once upon a time, there was a kid named Tim. He was a serious kid who loved to learn. Every day, he would go to school and listen to his teacher. He would learn about numbers, letters, and colors. One day, Tim went to school and found a big, red ball in the playground. He was very excited to play with it. Tim kicked the ball very hard, and it went up in the sky. Suddenly, the ball turned into a big, red bird! The bird flew down and landed next to Tim. It said, "Thank you for helping me learn to fly!" Tim was very surprised but happy to help the bird. From that day on, Tim and the bird became best friends, and they learned many new things together.

One day, a large cactus lived in a hot, dry place. The cactus was very thirsty. It wanted water to drink. It saw a big cloud in the sky and hoped for rain. Soon, the rain came down. The cactus was so happy! It opened its mouth wide and said, "Yummy rain, come to me!" The rain heard the cactus and fell right into its mouth. The cactus drank all the water it could. As the rain fell, a little girl named Lily walked by with her red boots. She saw the big puddles and wanted to play. She jumped in the puddles and said, "Splash, splash, splash!" The cactus smiled at Lily and they both enjoyed the rain together.

One day, Ben and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to run, slide and swing. But they did not like to tie their shoes. They thought it was hard and boring. But then, something bad happened. Ben saw a big dog running towards them. It looked angry and scary. Ben wanted to run away, but his shoe came off. He tripped and fell on the grass. He hurt his knee and cried. Lily saw Ben and the dog. She was scared too, but she wanted to help Ben. She ran to him and shouted at the dog. "Go away, bad dog! Leave us alone!" The dog stopped and barked. Lily picked up Ben's shoe and threw it at the dog. The dog ran after the shoe and left them alone. Lily helped Ben get up. His knee was bleeding and filthy. She said, "Ben, are you okay? We need to go home. This is an emergency. Mom will clean your knee and make it better." Ben nodded and hugged Lily. He said, "Thank you, Lily. You are brave and kind. I'm sorry I didn't tie my shoe. I will learn how to do it. I don't want to fall again." Lily smiled and said, "You're welcome, Ben. I'm glad you're okay. I will help you tie your shoe. We can do it together. It's not so hard or boring." They held hands and walked home. They learned a lesson that day. They learned to tie their shoes and to help each other. They were happy and proud.
