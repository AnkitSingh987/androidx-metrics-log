Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob and the Flower

Once upon a time, a bee named Bob lived in a big hive. Bob loved to dive from the sky and fly around the flowers. One day, while Bob was diving, he saw a beautiful flower. He wanted to get some sweet food from it. But when Bob got close to the flower, he saw that it was fake! It was not a real flower. Bob was sad and confused. He wondered why there was a fake flower in his garden. Then, a little girl came to the garden. She saw Bob and said, "Hi, bee! I put the fake flower here to surprise you!" Bob was not sad anymore. He was happy that the girl wanted to play with him. They became friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Liza. She loved to eat olives. Whenever she saw one, she would run to pick it up. One day, Liza was playing at the park with her friends. She was running when suddenly, she fell. Her friends all gathered around her to make sure she was okay. Liza felt scared and sad but then she saw an olive. She reached out and picked it up. Suddenly, she heard her mom calling her name. "Liza, time for dinner!" she said. Liza wanted to stay and eat the olive but she knew her mom would be mad. She reluctantly put the olive down and walked away. The next day, Liza's mom took her to the store and bought her a few jars of olives. "These olives will help you stay healthy," she said. Liza was so happy and she promised herself to always be careful when she was running. She knew that eating healthy olives was important and that sometimes she must do the right thing, even if it wasn't the most fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big box called a chest. It was in a nice room. People felt comfortable when they sat near the chest. They liked it a lot. One day, a little boy named Tim found a key. He thought the key would open the chest. Tim felt excited and turned the key. The chest opened and something jumped out. It was a big, loud lion! The lion scared everyone in the room. Tim felt bad and regret that he opened the chest. The lion ran away and nobody felt comfortable in the room anymore. They were all very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy frog named Fred. He lived near a swamp. He liked to sit, eat bugs, and watch the other animals play. One day, Fred saw a big tree. He wanted to climb it. He thought it would be fun. So, he started to climb. It was hard work, but he kept going up. As Fred climbed higher, he saw something strange. There were no more bugs in the tree! Instead, there were yummy treats like cake and ice cream. Fred was so happy! He ate and ate. He was not lazy anymore. Climbing the tree was the best thing he ever did.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He wanted to cook for his mom. He tried to make a big, yummy cake. Tom mixed the cake stuff in a big bowl. He put it in the oven. But he forgot to turn the oven off. Thick smoke came out of the oven. Tom's mom came running and said, "Oh no, Tom! What did you do?" Tom felt sad. He said, "I wanted to make a cake for you, but I did not succeed. I am sorry, Mom." His mom hugged him and said, "It's okay, Tom. You can try again." Together, Tom and his mom made a new cake. This time, they watched the oven carefully. The cake was perfect. Tom succeeded, and his mom was so proud. They ate the cake and laughed together.

Tom and Lily were friends who liked to play on the beach. One day, they saw a big crab in the sand. It had red claws and black eyes. Tom and Lily wanted to invite the crab to play with them. They went closer to the crab and said hello. The crab did not say anything. It looked angry and scared. Tom and Lily thought the crab was shy. They tried to be nice and gave it a lovely shell they found. The crab did not take the shell. It snapped its claws at them. Tom and Lily were sad and scared. They ran away from the crab. They wanted to find another friend to play with. But they did not see the big wave that was coming. The wave was very fast and strong. It washed Tom and Lily away from the shore. They screamed for help, but no one heard them. The crab saw them, but it did not care. It crawled back to its hole and hid. Tom and Lily were gone. The end.
