Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Max and the Orange

Lily and Max were twins, but they liked different things. Lily liked pink and flowers, Max liked blue and cars. One day, they went to the park with their mom. Mom gave them each a tray with a sandwich and a juice. "Can I have your juice, Lily?" Max requested. "I don't like orange, I like apple." "No, Max, this is my juice. You have your own juice," Lily said. "I don't like apple, I like orange." "But I want your juice, Lily. Please, please, please," Max begged. He tried to grab Lily's tray, but Lily held it tight. "Stop it, Max. You are being mean. Mom said we have to share, but you always want my things," Lily cried. She pushed Max away, but he pushed her back. "Hey, hey, hey, what's going on here?" Mom asked. She saw Lily and Max fighting over the trays. She was not happy. She took the trays from them and put them on the bench. "Lily and Max, you are both unique and special, but you have to respect each other's choices. You can't take or force what is not yours. You have to ask nicely and accept no as an answer. Do you understand?" Mom said. Lily and Max nodded. They felt sorry for making Mom angry and for hurting each other. They hugged and said sorry. "Can we have our trays back, Mom?" Lily and Max requested. "Yes, you can, but only if you promise to be kind and share. And maybe you can try something new and different. You might like it," Mom said. Lily and Max agreed. They took their trays and sat on the grass. They each took a sip of their juice and a bite of their sandwich. They smiled and exchanged their trays. "Hey, this is not bad. I like orange juice," Max said. "Me too. I like apple juice," Lily said. They laughed and enjoyed their lunch. They learned that being unique is good, but being together is better.

Once upon a time, there was an old boot. It was worn-out, with a hole in it and beyond repair. The old boot had no home, so it sat alone on the cold concrete. "I wish I could fit my foot inside this old boot," said a small voice. A three-year-old boy had appeared from around the corner, and he was staring at the boot. He kicked it lightly with his toe, and his eyes widened in surprise. The old boot fit his foot perfectly! He looked around in delight, and said, "Now I'm never going to be barefoot again!" The old boot was now like a brand new, sturdy boot. The boy was overjoyed, as he had never had a pair of shoes before. He thanked the old boot for being his own special pair, and ran off happily down the street. The old boot felt so proud, and it was happy to have found its very own home.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red bus. This bus was perfect for taking kids to the park. One sunny day, the bus driver, named Tom, drove the bus to the park with lots of happy kids. At the park, the kids played and laughed. They ran, jumped, and had lots of fun. Suddenly, it started to rain. The kids didn't want to get wet, so they ran back to the bus. Tom opened the door and said, "Come on, kids! The bus will cover you from the rain!" The kids got on the bus, and they were all safe and dry. They thanked Tom for helping them. The rain stopped, and the sun came out again. The kids went back to the park and played some more. At the end of the day, the big, red bus took the kids back home. They all had a perfect day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a small toy called a computer. He liked to play with his computer every day. It was a fine computer, with big buttons and bright colors. One day, Tim took his computer to the park. He played catch with his friends. They had fun running and laughing. Tim threw the ball very high in the sky. But when the ball came down, it hit his computer. Tim was sad. He picked up his computer and looked at it. The computer was fine! It was not broken. Tim was happy. He put the computer in his bag and continued to play catch with his friends. They played until the sun went down and it was time to go home.
