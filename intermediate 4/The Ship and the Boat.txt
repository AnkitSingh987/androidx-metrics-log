Category: Intermediate
Title: The Ship and the Boat

Once upon a time, there was a big ship. The ship was very graceful. It moved on the water so smoothly. The ship had a captain. The captain was a kind man. He loved his ship very much. One sunny day, the ship was sailing on the water. The captain saw a little boat. The little boat was in trouble. The captain wanted to help. He said to his crew, "Let's help the little boat!" The crew agreed. The captain used a big hose to spray water. The water pushed the little boat to safety. The people on the little boat were happy. They said, "Thank you, captain!" The captain smiled. He was happy to help. The graceful ship sailed away, looking for more friends to help.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big bookshelf in his room. The bookshelf had many books and toys on it. One day, Tim saw a new book on the bookshelf. It had a picture of a big lemon on the cover. Tim liked lemons, so he took the book and started to read it. The book was about a lemon tree. The lemon tree had many sour lemons on it. Tim's friend, Sam, came to visit him. Tim showed Sam the book about the lemon tree. They both liked the story and wanted to find a lemon tree of their own. Tim and Sam went outside to look for a lemon tree. They walked and walked until they found one. They saw that the tree had many sour lemons. They picked some lemons and took them home. Tim's mom helped them part the lemons and make lemonade. Tim and Sam drank the sour lemonade and laughed. They had a fun day with the lemon tree and the new book from the bookshelf.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. He liked to play with his big red ball. One day, he saw a huge sack in his yard. Tim was very curious about the sack. He wanted to know what was inside. Tim opened the sack and saw many small, colorful balls. He was so happy and excited! He took out the balls and started to play with them. Suddenly, the balls started to move and jump by themselves. Tim was surprised! The balls were not just balls, they were little frogs! Tim was not scared, he liked the frogs. He played with the frogs all day long. At the end of the day, Tim put the frogs back in the sack and said goodbye. The next day, he went to look for the sack, but it was gone. Tim was sad, but he knew he would always remember the day he played with the magical frogs.

Once upon a time, there was a busy little girl named Sue. Sue liked to play with her toys and run around the house. One day, Sue wanted to play with her toy box, but it was locked. She needed to unlock it to get her toys. Sue looked all around the house for the key. She looked under the furniture and behind the doors. She even asked her mom and dad if they knew where the key was. But no one knew where it was. Sue was sad because she could not find the key to unlock her toy box. Then, Sue had an idea. She asked her friend, the dog, to help her find the key. The dog sniffed and sniffed, and he found the key under a chair. Sue was so happy! She used the key to unlock her toy box, and she played with her toys all day long. The busy little girl and her dog had lots of fun, and they were both very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a funny bunny. The bunny liked to play with a hammer. One day, he found a big, shiny nail. The funny bunny wanted to hit the nail with the hammer. His friend, the wise owl, said, "No, bunny! You must prevent hitting the nail. It is not safe." But the funny bunny did not listen. He hit the nail very hard. The hammer flew out of his hand and broke a window. The funny bunny was sad. The wise owl said, "You should have listened. Now the window is broken." The funny bunny learned that it is important to listen to friends who try to keep you safe.
