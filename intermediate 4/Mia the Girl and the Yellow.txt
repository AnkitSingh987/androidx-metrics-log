Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Yellow

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved the color yellow. She had a yellow dress, a yellow hat, and even a yellow bike. One day, Mia felt sad. She wanted to make herself happy by spreading the color yellow all around her room. So, she took her yellow paint and started to spread it on the walls. As she spread the paint, she started to feel happy again. Soon, her room was full of yellow. Mia was no longer sad. She played in her yellow room with her yellow toys and felt very happy. And from that day on, whenever Mia felt sad, she would spread more yellow around her and feel happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful little girl named Lily. She loved to help her mom and dad at home. One sunny day, Lily wanted to do something nice for her parents. She thought of a surprise. Lily decided to wash the dishes. She filled the sink with water and soap. Lily washed the cups, plates, and spoons. She was very careful not to break anything. She was happy to help. When mom and dad came home, they saw the clean dishes. "Wow, Lily!" said mom. "Did you wash the dishes?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, I wanted to surprise you!" Mom and dad were very proud of their thoughtful little girl. They all shared a big hug and enjoyed their clean home.

Sara and Tom were in the classroom. They liked to play with the toys and the books. But they also liked to examine things. They wanted to know how they worked and what they were made of. One day, they saw a big blackboard on the wall. It was very high and very wide. It had many colors and shapes on it. Sara and Tom wondered what they were. They decided to examine the blackboard. They found a chair and a stool. They put them next to the wall. Sara climbed on the chair and Tom climbed on the stool. They reached for the blackboard. But the blackboard was very rough. It hurt their fingers. They did not like it. "Ow, ow, ow!" Sara cried. "This blackboard is bad. It is not smooth like paper. It is not soft like cloth. It is rough like sandpaper. It makes my fingers bleed." "Me too, me too!" Tom said. "This blackboard is not fun. It is not pretty like a picture. It is not funny like a joke. It is rough like a rock. It makes my fingers hurt." They wanted to get down. But they were too high. They could not reach the chair or the stool. They were stuck. They were scared. They started to cry. "Help, help, help!" Sara and Tom shouted. "We want to get down. We want to go home. We don't like the blackboard. It is rough and mean." But no one heard them. The teacher was not in the classroom. The other kids were not in the classroom. They were outside, playing in the sun. They did not know that Sara and Tom were in trouble. They did not know that the blackboard was rough and mean. They did not know that Sara and Tom were crying and bleeding. They did not know that Sara and Tom were very, very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a mysterious big forest. In this forest, there lived a little bird. The little bird loved to whistle all day long. The other animals liked to listen to the bird's happy song. One day, a small cat came to the forest. The cat heard the bird's whistle and wanted to be friends. But the cat accidentally scared the bird. The bird stopped whistling and flew away. The cat was sad and wanted to say sorry. So, the cat found the bird and said, "Please forgive me. I didn't mean to scare you." The bird forgave the cat, and they became friends. The bird started to whistle again, and the forest was happy once more.

Once upon a time, there was a little bug. The bug was very careful. It did not want to get hurt. One day, the bug found a print on a big rock. The print was very pretty. The bug wanted to see more. At night, the bug saw something glow. It was a pretty, glowing flower. The bug was so happy! It walked carefully to the flower. The flower was next to the big rock with the print. The bug and the glowing flower became friends. They played together every day. The bug was always careful, and the flower showed the bug more prints on the rock. They were happy and had lots of fun.
