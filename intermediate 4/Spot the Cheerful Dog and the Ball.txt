Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Cheerful Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful little dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play in the big park near his home. One day, while Spot was playing, he saw a funny sight. A big, round, yellow ball was bouncing all by itself! Spot ran over to the ball and started to play with it. He pushed the ball with his nose and chased it all around the park. But then, Spot felt a tickle in his nose. He tried to hold it in, but he couldn't. Spot let out a big sneeze! The sneeze was so big that it made the ball fly high into the sky. Spot watched as the ball went up, up, up, and then came back down. He caught the ball in his mouth and brought it back to his owner. They played with the ball until it was time to go home. Spot was a very happy dog.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a yellow bird named Sunny. Sunny was a very happy bird who loved to sing and play with his friends. One day, Sunny felt sick and could not sing or play anymore. His friends were sad because they missed Sunny's beautiful songs. Sunny's friend, a wise old owl, said, "I know what will make you feel better. You need medicine." Owl went to look for the medicine in the forest. He found the medicine and brought it back to Sunny. Sunny took the medicine and soon started to feel better. As Sunny felt better, he began to sing again. His friends were very happy and gathered around him. Sunny said, "Thank you, Owl, for helping me. You have revealed the power of friendship and caring for others." The moral of the story is that friends are always there to help each other and make each other feel better.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She liked to play with rocks and wood. One day, Lily found a big rock and a long piece of wood. She thought they would be fun to play with. Lily put the rock on the wood and tried to make it fit. It was not easy, but she kept trying. Then, something unexpected happened. The rock and the wood started to move! They moved up and down, like a see-saw. Lily was so surprised! She played with her new see-saw all day long. She had so much fun with the rock and the wood. And that is how Lily made a new toy, all by herself.

Lily and Tom were going on a journey with their mom and dad. They got in the car and put on their seat belts. The car was very noisy. It made vroom vroom sounds and beep beep sounds. Lily and Tom liked to look out the window and see the trees and houses and animals. "Look, a cow!" Tom said, pointing to a big black and white animal in a field. "Moo!" Lily said, making the sound of a cow. They saw many other things on their journey. They saw a bridge, a lake, a train, and a big mountain. They admired everything they saw. They said wow and ooh and aah. "Are we there yet?" Lily asked, after a long time. "Soon, sweetie," mom said. "We are going to grandma's house. She has a surprise for us." "What is it?" Tom asked, curious. "You'll see," dad said, smiling. Lily and Tom wondered what the surprise was. They hoped it was something fun and nice. They loved their grandma. She always gave them hugs and cookies and stories. They were excited to see her. They arrived at grandma's house. They got out of the car and ran to the door. Grandma opened it and welcomed them. "Hello, my darlings!" she said. "I'm so happy to see you. Come in, come in." She hugged them and kissed them and led them to the living room. There, they saw the surprise. It was a big, fluffy, brown puppy. It wagged its tail and licked their faces. "A puppy!" Lily and Tom exclaimed. "For us?" "Yes, for you," grandma said. "He needs a good home and a lot of love. Do you want to keep him?" "Yes, yes, yes!" Lily and Tom said. They hugged the puppy and thanked grandma. They named him Spot. They played with him and fed him and cuddled him. They admired him and his cute ears and nose and paws. They had a wonderful time at grandma's house. They were very happy with their new friend. They decided that their journey was the best journey ever.
