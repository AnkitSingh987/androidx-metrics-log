Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Coat

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside, but today was very icy. His mom told him to be careful when he went out. She mentioned that he should wear his warm coat, hat, and gloves. Tim put on his warm clothes and went outside. He saw a big hill near the exit of his yard. Tim thought it would be fun to slide down the hill. He remembered what his mom mentioned about being careful on the icy day. Tim slid down the hill, laughing and having fun. He was careful not to go too fast. When he reached the bottom, he saw a big, soft pile of snow. Tim landed in the snow pile safely, just as his mom had hoped.

One day, a boy named Tim found a cool symbol on a big rock. It was a sunny day, and Tim was playing in the park. He saw the symbol and wanted to know what it meant. He thought it was a secret. Tim called his friend, Lily. Tim yelled, "Lily, come see this cool symbol!" Lily came running to see it. She looked at the symbol and smiled. She said, "I know what this is! Let's try something fun!" Lily told Tim to touch the symbol and say a magic word. Tim did what Lily said, and something unexpected happened! The symbol made a big, yummy ice cream appear! Tim and Lily laughed and enjoyed the sweet treat. The cool symbol was a magic ice cream maker!

Once upon a time there was a young girl named Sue. Sue wanted to go on an adventure and explore the world. One day she came across an unknown helicopter and decided to climb inside. As Sue sat in the helicopter she felt like she belonged there. Suddenly, out of nowhere, a voice called out, "Who are you?" Sue looked around and saw a friendly pilot. He said, "Welcome, my name is Bob. What is yours?" Sue said, "My name is Sue, and I am exploring the world." Bob smiled and said, "Well, now you belong in the helicopter." Sue was so excited and looked out the window. The world suddenly seemed so much bigger! From then on, Sue and Bob went on many adventures together in the helicopter, discovering new places and meeting new people. The world was a wonderful place!

One day, a little bird was flying in the hot sun. He was very hungry and tired. He saw a big tree and thought it was an oasis. He went closer to see if there was water. When he got there, a kind monkey was sitting in the tree. The monkey said, "Hello, little bird! You look hungry and tired. I will show you where the oasis is." The little bird was happy and said, "Thank you, kind monkey!" The monkey took the little bird to the real oasis. The bird drank water and ate some fruit. They became good friends. The little bird learned that things are not always what they seem. The big tree was not an oasis, but it brought him to a new friend. The moral of the story is to not judge things too quickly and to be kind to others.

One day, a little girl named Anna went for a walk. She saw a tiny bug on a leaf. Anna wanted to observe the bug, but she was impatient. She wanted the bug to do something fun. Anna said, "Come on, bug! Do something fun!" The bug looked at her and found a long thread. The bug began to dance on the thread. Anna was very happy to see the bug dance. She clapped her hands and laughed. But then, something unexpected happened. The thread began to glow and the bug became big! The big bug smiled at Anna and said, "Thank you for being my friend. I am a magic bug! Now, we can play together." Anna and the magic bug played all day and had lots of fun.

Lily and her mom were going to the park. They got in the car and drove on the road. But there were too many cars on the road. They had to stop and wait. Lily was bored and sad. She wanted to play on the swings and the slide. "Mom, why are there so many cars?" Lily asked. "Because it is traffic, Lily. Traffic means many cars are on the road and they cannot move fast. We have to be patient and wait for the cars to move," mom said. Lily looked out the window. She saw a big truck next to them. The truck had a hose on the back. The hose was spraying water on the road. Lily thought it was funny. She giggled. "Look, mom, the truck is making the road wet!" Lily said. Mom looked at the truck. She frowned. She did not think it was funny. She thought it was rude. Rude means not nice and not polite. "That is not nice, Lily. The truck is making the road wet on purpose. It is soaking the other cars with water. It is making them dirty and wet. That is rude," mom said. Lily did not understand. She thought the water was fun. She wanted to play with the water. She opened the window and stuck her hand out. She tried to catch the water. "Lily, no! Close the window! You will get wet and cold!" mom said. But it was too late. The truck sprayed more water. The water hit Lily's hand and face. Lily gasped. She felt the water on her skin. It was cold and wet. She did not like it. She pulled her hand back and closed the window. She started to cry. "Mom, I'm wet! I'm cold! I don't like the water!" Lily said. Mom reached for a towel. She wiped Lily's face and hand. She hugged her and kissed her. "I'm sorry, Lily. The truck was very rude. It should not have soaked you with water. You are a good girl. You did not do anything wrong. It's okay. We will be at the park soon. You can play and have fun there," mom said. Lily sniffed. She felt better. She smiled at mom. "Thank you, mom. I love you. I want to go to the park. I want to play on the swings and the slide," Lily said. Mom smiled back. She loved Lily too. She hoped the traffic would move soon. She hoped the truck would stop being rude. She hoped they would have a nice day at the park.
