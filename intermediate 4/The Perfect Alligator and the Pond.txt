Category: Intermediate
Title: The Perfect Alligator and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a very perfect alligator. He was called Alli and he was very happy. Alli was happy to live in a nice, perfect pond. Every day, he kept swimming around and enjoying the sun. One day, Alli saw some people at the edge of his pond. But Alli was not scared. He kept swimming slowly around them. He kept smiling at the people and they smiled back at him. The people kept talking to him, saying “You are so perfect, Alli!” They asked him to show them some tricks and Alli kept nodding. He kept doing flips and making them laugh! Finally, it was time to go. Alli said goodbye and kept swimming in the same pond. He kept living his perfect, happy life in that pond. Alli was a perfect alligator and he kept being happy!

Once upon a time, there was a barber who loved to cut hair. He was a happy man but sometimes he was careless. One day, a little boy came to the barber to get a haircut. The barber started to cut the boy's hair. The boy was very still and quiet. The barber was singing a fun song while he cut the hair. But he was careless and made a little mistake. He cut the boy's hair too short! The boy's mom came to pick him up. She saw his hair and was sad. The barber felt bad for being careless. He wanted to make the boy and his mom feel better. So, he gave the boy a big kiss on his head. The boy laughed and his mom smiled. They knew the hair would grow back and they all became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little red car. The car was very useful. It could go fast and slow. The car had many friends. They liked to play together. One day, the little red car was playing with its friends. They were racing around the park. The little red car was going very fast. But then, it hit a big rock. The car started to break. The little red car was sad. It could not play with its friends anymore. The car's friends wanted to help. They found a way to fix the little red car. They put it back together. The little red car was happy again. It could play with its friends. They all learned to be more careful when they race. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little vehicle named Vivi. Vivi loved to stretch her wheels in the morning. She felt happy when she did this. One day, Vivi saw a big, dark cloud. She felt frightened, but she did not know why. Vivi told her friends, "I am frightened of the big, dark cloud." Her friends said, "Do not be scared, Vivi. The cloud will go away soon." Vivi tried to be brave. She stretched her wheels and played with her friends. The big, dark cloud stayed in the sky. The next day, the big, dark cloud was still there. Vivi and her friends felt sad. Then, something unexpected happened. The big, dark cloud started to rain. The rain made a beautiful rainbow. Vivi and her friends were not frightened anymore. They learned that sometimes, things that seem scary can bring something good.

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play in the garden. They have many toys, but Sara's favorite is a pink soap. She likes to make bubbles with it. One day, Ben is jealous of Sara's soap. He wants to play with it, but Sara does not share. She says it is hers and only hers. Ben is angry. He waits until Sara is not looking. He takes the soap and runs away. He digs a hole in the garden and buries the soap. He thinks Sara will never find it. But Sara sees what Ben does. She runs after him and asks for her soap back. Ben says he does not know where it is. He lies. Sara is sad. She cries. She looks for her soap, but she cannot find it. Mama hears Sara crying. She comes to the garden and asks what is wrong. Sara tells Mama what Ben did. Mama is not happy. She scolds Ben for being jealous and lying. She makes him dig up the soap and give it back to Sara. She tells him to say sorry and to share. Ben feels bad. He says sorry to Sara and gives her the soap. He asks if he can play with it too. Sara forgives him and says yes. They make bubbles together and have fun. Mama smiles and hugs them. She tells them that sharing is caring and that lying is wrong. Sara and Ben learn their lesson. They are happy.
