Category: Intermediate
Title: Jack and Jill and the Planet

Jack and Jill were two best friends. One day they were sitting outside looking up at the sky. Jack pointed to the brightest thing he saw and said, "Look at that planet. I wish I could go there, it looks so amazing." Jill jumped up and said, "Let's go! We can step there together." Jack clapped and they started their journey. They stepped together through the sunshine and the rain, getting closer and closer to the beautiful planet they had seen. When they had almost reached the planet, they heard a loud sound and stopped in their tracks. A giant wall was blocking their way! They were so frustrated. They had come so far and now they couldn't reach their dream. Jack and Jill didn't give up. They stepped and stepped, until they both felt a shake under their feet. They had stepped onto the amazing planet! They hugged and celebrated as they looked around. They finally made it!

Once upon a time, there was a perfect marriage between a prince and a princess. They had been married for many years and were happily in love. They even had their own castle. One day, the prince decided to take a special trip. He asked the princess to come with him, but she said no because she was worried about leaving their castle. The prince said, "Don't worry, my love. It'll be perfect if we take this trip together. Let's go!" The princess finally said yes, so the prince and princess took the trip together and it was magical. They made lots of wonderful memories. When the prince and princess returned to their castle, they hugged and told each other they loved each other. Everything was perfect and they were so happy in their marriage. The end.

Lily and Ben like to play with their toys. They have many toys, but their favorite is the soldier. The soldier is big and strong and can fight bad guys. Lily and Ben take turns to play with the soldier. One day, Lily is playing with the soldier. She makes him run and jump and shoot. She is having fun. Ben is sleepy. He wants to take a nap. He sees the soldier and he wants him. He thinks Lily has played with him for too long. Ben goes to Lily and says, "Give me the soldier. It is my turn." Lily says, "No, it is not. I am still playing. Go away." Ben gets angry. He tries to take the soldier from Lily. Lily holds on tight. They pull and tug and shout. Mom hears the noise. She comes to see what is wrong. She sees Lily and Ben fighting over the soldier. She is not happy. She says, "Stop it, both of you. You have to share your toys. You can't take things from each other. That is not nice." She takes the soldier from them and puts him on the shelf. She says, "No one can play with the soldier until you say sorry and make up. Lily, you can play with something else. Ben, you can take a nap. You are sleepy." Lily and Ben feel sad. They don't want to lose the soldier. They look at each other and say sorry. They hug and make up. Mom smiles and gives them the soldier back. She says, "Good. Now you can play nicely together. And Ben, you can take a nap later if you want." Lily and Ben nod and say thank you. They play with the soldier and other toys. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy farmer named Fred. He wanted to find out what was happening in the nearby town, so he took a walk there. Fred was walking down a quiet road when he saw something strange. A small dog was stuck in a tree! Fred stopped and said, "Oh no! What can I do to help you?" He looked around and tried to come up with a plan. Suddenly, Fred had an idea. He reached into his pocket and pulled out a rope. He thought to himself, "I can use this to save the little pup!" Fred gently threw the rope over the branch, and the dog grabbed it. He then helped bring the pup down to safety. The pup thanked Fred by licking his face. Fred smiled and said, "I'm glad I was able to help you out! I'm a nosy farmer, and I'm always here to save the day!"
