Category: Intermediate
Title: The in Garden and the Bird

Once upon a time, a bright sunflower lived in a big garden. Every day, the sunflower would dance and play with the wind. The sunflower loved to catch the sun's rays and grow taller and taller. One day, a little girl came to the garden. She saw the bright sunflower and wanted to take it home. The little girl tried to catch the sunflower, but it was too tall for her. She jumped and jumped, but she could not reach the sunflower. Then, a little bird came by and saw the girl trying to catch the sunflower. The bird had an idea. The bird flew up and picked the bright sunflower with its beak. The girl was so happy! But, the bird did not give the sunflower to the girl. Instead, the bird flew away with the sunflower and planted it in another garden. The girl was sad, but she learned that some things are better left where they are. And the bright sunflower continued to dance and play with the wind in its new home.

One day, a little frog named Tim was near a pond. Tim was a slow frog, but he was very happy. The sun was shining, and the water was cool. Tim liked to play with his friends in the pond. One of Tim's friends was a big fish named Finn. Finn was fast, but he liked to play with Tim. They played hide and seek in the pond. Tim hid behind a rock, and Finn tried to find him. When Finn found Tim, they both laughed. One day, the sky became dark, and the wind blew hard. Tim started to tremble. He was scared. Finn saw Tim and swam to him. "Don't worry, Tim," said Finn. "We can hide under this big leaf until the wind stops." Tim and Finn hid under the leaf, and they felt safe. They talked and laughed until the wind stopped. When the sun came out again, they went back to playing in the pond. Tim was happy to have a good friend like Finn.

Once upon a time there was a poor panda. One day, he got stuck inside a deep hole in the forest. He was so sad and all alone. A child heard his cries for help and quickly ran to the panda. She saw that the poor panda couldn't get out by himself, so she decided to repair the hole. The child said to the panda, “I'm going to help you! I'm going to repair the hole and get you out!” The child worked quickly and carefully, digging into the dirt and rocks. Soon, the hole was repaired and the panda was free! The panda thanked the child, and said “You are very kind. Thank you for helping me repair the hole and getting me out of the forest.” The child smiled, happy that the poor panda was free and safe.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big show. Many people came to watch. A little girl named Lisa wanted to perform. She was excited but also confused. She did not know what to do in the show. Lisa thought and thought. She had an idea! She went to the market and bought many fruits. She took the fruits home and practiced her act. She learned to juggle the fruits high in the air. She was very good at it. The day of the show came. Lisa went on stage and started to juggle. The people watched and clapped. But then, something unexpected happened. One of the fruits fell and rolled away. A little boy picked it up and gave it back to Lisa. She smiled and they juggled together. The people clapped louder. The show was a big success. Lisa and the boy became good friends. They learned that when we are confused or scared, it is good to ask for help. And when we help each other, we can do amazing things.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy mechanic named Tom. He loved to fix cars and play with big tools. One day, Tom was working in his shop when he saw a little red car that needed help. Tom wanted to fix the little red car, so he tried to push it into his shop. But he was very clumsy, and he fell down. Tom got up and tried again. This time, he pushed the car into the shop and smiled. Tom worked hard to fix the little red car. He used his tools and made the car happy again. When the car was all fixed, Tom pushed it back outside. The little red car beeped, and Tom waved goodbye. He was a happy and clumsy mechanic.
