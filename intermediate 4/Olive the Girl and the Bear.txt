Category: Intermediate
Title: Olive the Girl and the Bear

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Olive. Olive was a brilliant girl who loved to play with her toys. One day, Olive found a magic wand while she was playing outside. She was very happy and excited. Olive wanted to see if the magic wand could change things. She waved the wand at her toy car, and it turned into a big, real car! Olive was so surprised and happy. She wanted to change more things with her magic wand. Then, Olive had an idea for a fun game. She decided to change her toy bear into a real bear. But when she waved the wand, the bear did not change into a real bear. Instead, the bear changed into a big, funny clown! Olive laughed and laughed at the unexpected twist. She played with the clown and had a great time. From that day on, Olive knew that her magic wand could make her life full of fun surprises.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a doll. The doll was very important to her. She took the doll everywhere she went. They were best friends. One day, Sue and her doll went to the park. They played on the swings and the slide. They had lots of fun. But when it was time to go home, Sue forgot her doll at the park. She did not know her doll was missing. At bedtime, Sue looked for her important doll. She could not find it. She was very sad. Her mom asked her where she last saw the doll. Sue remembered the park. They had to go back and find the doll. Sue and her mom went back to the park. It was dark, but they found the doll under the slide. Sue was so happy! She hugged her important doll and promised never to forget it again. They went home and had a good night's sleep.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw an enormous owl sitting on a tree. Tim was very excited and wanted to make friends with the owl. He said, "Hi, Mr. Owl! Do you want to be my friend?" The enormous owl looked at Tim and said, "Yes, I would like to be your friend, but I need your help. I lost my baby owl in the park. Can you help me find him?" Tim and the enormous owl started to look for the baby owl. They looked behind trees and under benches. Suddenly, they heard a tiny voice. It was the baby owl! But he was not an owl, he was a kitten! The enormous owl had made a mistake. The enormous owl said, "Oh, I'm sorry. I thought you were my baby owl." The kitten said, "That's okay, Mr. Owl. Can I still be your friend?" Tim, the enormous owl, and the kitten all became good friends and played in the park together.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty vase. It was in a room where everyone could see it. The vase was very reliable and never moved. One day, a cat came into the room. The cat saw the vase and wanted to play with it. The cat jumped on the table where the vase was. The cat tried to play with the vase, but the vase did not want to play. The cat got mad and pushed the vase. The vase tried to stay still, but it was hard. Then, something bad happened. The vase fell off the table and broke into many pieces. The cat was sad and ran away. The room was not the same without the pretty, reliable vase.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a best friend named Sam. They liked to play together and support each other. One day, they went on a search to find a lost toy. While they were looking, they saw a big, crazy bird. The bird was flying all around and making funny noises. Tim and Sam laughed at the bird and thought it was very funny. They kept looking for the toy, but they couldn't find it. Then, the crazy bird flew down and gave them the lost toy. Tim and Sam were so happy! They thanked the bird and went home to play. They knew that with support from their friends, they could do anything. And they all lived happily ever after.
