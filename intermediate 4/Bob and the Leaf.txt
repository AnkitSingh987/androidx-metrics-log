Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob and the Leaf

One sunny day, a purple ant named Bob was walking in the park. He saw a big tree with a yummy leaf at the top. Bob wanted to eat the leaf, so he started to climb the tree. As Bob climbed, a bird saw him and said, "Hi, ant! What are you doing?" Bob replied, "I want to eat the leaf at the top." The bird said, "I can help you. I can fly up and drop the leaf for you." The bird flew up, picked the leaf and dropped it for Bob. The leaf fell to the ground, and Bob was very happy. He said, "Thank you, bird!" Then, Bob ate the yummy leaf and went home with a big smile on his face.

One day, Tom the cat and Sam the dog were playing in the yard. They saw a big tree with a long vine hanging down. Tom had an idea. "Let's swing on the vine!" he said to Sam. Sam wagged his tail and barked, "Yes, let's do it!" Tom climbed up the tree and grabbed the vine. He swung back and forth, having lots of fun. Sam wanted to swing too, but the vine was too high for him. Tom saw Sam's sad face and said, "I'll help you!" Tom found a small stick and used it to cut the vine a little lower, so Sam could reach it. Sam was very happy and started swinging on the vine. As they played, they saw an old, deaf rabbit named Mr. Bunny sitting under a tree. He looked sad because he could not hear his friends playing. Tom and Sam wanted to cheer him up. They went to Mr. Bunny and gave him a gentle rub on his back. They played with him, making him laugh and smile. After a while, the sun began to set, and it was time for Tom and Sam to go home. They said goodbye to Mr. Bunny and promised to play with him again soon. As they walked home, Tom and Sam were happy they had made a new friend and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot liked to play outside in the sun. One day, the sky got dark and it was time for Spot to go home. But Spot did not want to go home yet. He wanted to play more. Spot went to a big tree and found a ball. He played with the ball for a long time. Then, he saw the dark sky again and knew he was in trouble. Spot was scared and wanted to go home now. Spot ran home as fast as he could. He was very sorry and felt regret for not going home earlier. When he got home, his mom hugged him and said, "Next time, listen when I say it's time to go home." Spot learned his lesson, and he never stayed out too late again.

One day, an elephant named Alejandro had been working hard, and he was tired. He yawned and decided to take a nap. Just then, a mosquito buzzed around his ear. He wanted to swat it away, but he was too tired to move. Alejandro groaned and said, "Please, leave me alone." But the mosquito kept buzzing around him. Alejandro was getting frustrated, but he tried to stay calm. He yawned again and said, "If you keep buzzing, I'm going to get angry." The mosquito still wouldn't go away. Alejandro was growing tired now and he sighed. With a loud bellow, he said, "I'm warning you! Leave me alone!" The fat mosquito ignored him and kept buzzing around his head. Alejandro had had enough! He swatted the mosquito away with one powerful flick of his trunk. The mosquito flew away, and Alejandro could finally rest.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a toy car she loved very much. One day, she saw a pretty sticker and wanted to attach it to her car. She thought her car would look even better with the sticker on it. Lily asked her mom if she could have the sticker. Her mom said they needed to check the price first. They went to the store together. Lily was anxious because she really wanted the sticker. She hoped it was not too much money. When they got to the store, they saw the price was only one coin. Lily was so happy! She gave the coin to the store man and took the sticker home. She attached the sticker to her toy car, and it looked so pretty. Lily was very happy and played with her car all day long.
