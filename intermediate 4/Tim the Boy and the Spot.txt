Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Spot

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a lucky boy. He lived in a small house with his mom, dad, and his pet dog, Spot. One day, Tim and Spot went outside to play. The wind was blowing. They played with a ball. Spot liked to run and catch the ball. Tim would throw the ball and Spot would bring it back. After playing, Tim and Spot got very dirty. Mom said, "You need to wipe your hands and face, Tim." So, Tim wiped his hands and face with a soft cloth. Spot also got wiped by Tim. They both felt clean and happy. They had a fun day playing in the wind.

Once upon a time, there was a clever little cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play and have fun. One day, Fluffy had a plan to play a game in the bathroom. Fluffy went into the bathroom and saw a big, white tub. Fluffy jumped into the tub and started to play with the water. Fluffy loved the water and how it felt on her soft fur. After playing in the water, Fluffy got out of the tub and saw a towel. Fluffy used the towel to dry her fur. Then, Fluffy went back to her bed and took a nap. Fluffy was happy with her clever plan to play in the bathroom.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved going to school every day. One day, it was very wet outside. Lily put on her rain boots and coat to go to school. On her way, Lily met a kind duck. The duck was sad because he lost his way. "Can you help me?" asked the duck. Lily said, "Yes, I will help you find your home." Lily and the duck walked together. They found the duck's home near a pond. The duck was very happy. He wanted to pay Lily for her help. Lily said, "No, thank you. I am happy to help you." Lily went to school with a big smile. She told her friends about the duck. They all laughed and played in the wet rain. The end.

Jim walked through the forest, the sun was setting and darkness was starting to fall. The trees cast long shadows, and there was a feeling of gloom in the air. Suddenly, Jim noticed some smoke in the distance. He could see it floating up into the air, and a faint smell was in the air. He decided to follow the path of the smoke until it got thicker. As Jim followed the smoke, he noticed the trees were becoming thicker and thicker - and soon enough he stumbled upon a clearing. In the clearing there was a campfire with some people around it. They were singing and having a good time. Jim smiled and he could feel the warmth of the fire. But then, Jim noticed something strange. There was some dark smoke rising into the sky, and it was tinged with a different colour. He looked up at the sky and realised it was becoming quite gloomy. Suddenly he wasn't feeling so cheerful anymore. It was time to head home. Jim turned away, and slowly disappeared back into the forest.

Once upon a time, there was a cool cat named Tom. Tom liked to play with his friend, a little mouse named Jerry. They lived in a big house with many rooms. One day, Tom found a trap in the house. He thought it would be fun to play a trick on Jerry. So, Tom put the trap near Jerry's hole, where he lived. He thought Jerry would be scared when he saw the trap. But Jerry was smart. He saw the trap and knew it was not safe. He went to Tom and said, "Tom, we are friends. Friends do not play tricks on each other. It is not nice." Tom felt sad and said sorry to Jerry. He took the trap away and they played together again. The moral of the story is that friends should be kind and not play tricks on each other.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big, hard book with a lot of colors. It was a calendar. Tim liked to look at the pictures and the fun days on it. He showed it to his mom and said, "Look, Mom! I found a fun book with colors!" His mom smiled and said, "That's a calendar, Tim. It helps us know what day it is and when special days are coming." Tim looked at the calendar again and saw a picture of a big, round moon. He was very excited and wanted to stay awake to see the moon that night. But as the night came, Tim started to yawn. He tried to stay awake, but he was so sleepy. Just as Tim was about to fall asleep, he heard a noise outside. He looked out the window and saw a big, round, shiny thing in the sky. It was the moon! The moon was so bright that it woke him up. Tim was so happy and said, "I did it, Mom! I saw the big, round moon!" His mom laughed and said, "That's great, Tim! Now let's go to bed and get some sleep." Tim went to bed with a big smile on his face, knowing he had seen the special moon.
