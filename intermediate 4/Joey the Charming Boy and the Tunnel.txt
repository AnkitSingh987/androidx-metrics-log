Category: Intermediate
Title: Joey the Charming Boy and the Tunnel

Once upon a time, there was a charming boy named Joey. He loved exploring and having adventures. One day he decided to find a tunnel. He packed a lunch and waved goodbye to his mommy and daddy. He hiked for what felt like forever but eventually he came to a shiny tunnel. He couldn't believe his eyes! He stepped inside and traveled for a long time. He soon reached the end of the tunnel and found a surprise. It was a girl named Lizzy. She had the same idea and also wanted to explore the tunnel. Joey and Lizzy smiled and hugged. Then, to everyone's surprise, Lizzy kissed Joey on the cheek! Joey was so happy he almost cried. He had never been kissed before and he found it so charming. From then on, Joey and Lizzy would explore the tunnel together and experience all kinds of fun adventures.

Once there was a powerful sport. It was so much fun that everyone wanted to play. But then one day, one of the players refused to join in. Everyone was amazed and asked why. The player told them “I don’t want to play anymore. I'm tired of always losing”. Everyone else thought the player was being silly and told them to try their best. But the player refused and went away. Everyone thought they had made the wrong choice and felt like they should be out there playing. The player went off to find a different sport, one that was powerful and gave them a chance to win. After some searching and trying, the player found a sport that was perfect for them. Now, the player is playing the sport with all their might and having lots of fun. And whenever they win, they remember to thank their lucky stars for finding the right sport.

Sara and Ben like to play with cars. They have many cars of different colors and sizes. They make roads and bridges with blocks and books. They have fun and share their cars. One day, a new boy comes to play with them. His name is Max. He has a big red car that makes loud noises. He says it is the best car ever. He does not want to share his car with Sara and Ben. He says they can only watch him play. Sara and Ben are sad and angry. They ask Max to share his car. They say they have many cars they can share with him. But Max says no. He says his car is too good for them. He is selfish. Max drives his car fast and hard. He does not care about Sara and Ben's roads and bridges. He crashes into them and breaks them. He makes a big wreck. He laughs and says his car is the strongest. Sara and Ben are very sad and angry. They do not want to play with Max anymore. They say he is mean and rude. They say he does not know how to play nice. They take their cars and go to another room. They leave Max alone with his big red car and his big wreck. He is not happy. He is lonely.

One day, a boy named Tim had a toy train. He loved to play with his train every day. Tim was a patient boy. He would wait for his mom to finish her work so they could play together. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, I have to go to the store. Be patient and wait for me to come back. Then we can play with your train." Tim said, "Okay, Mom. I will wait." While Tim was waiting, his toy train started to talk! The train said, "Hi, Tim! I can play with you until your mom comes back." Tim was very surprised. He did not know his train could talk. They played together and had lots of fun. When Tim's mom came back, the train stopped talking. Tim told his mom about the talking train, but she did not believe him. From that day on, Tim's toy train would talk and play with him whenever he was alone. Tim was happy to have a special train friend.
