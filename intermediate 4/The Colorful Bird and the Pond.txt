Category: Intermediate
Title: The Colorful Bird and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a colorful bird who lived near a pond. The bird was very sad and would cry every day. The other animals who lived near the pond did not like to see the bird cry. One day, a wise old frog saw the colorful bird crying. The frog asked the bird, "Why do you cry every day?" The bird said, "I am sad because I don't have any friends." The wise old frog told the bird, "If you want to make friends, you should be kind and help others." The colorful bird decided to listen to the wise old frog. The bird started to help the other animals near the pond. The bird helped a little fish find its family. The bird helped a baby duck learn to swim. The bird even helped a turtle flip back over when it was stuck on its back. Soon, the colorful bird had many friends. The bird was happy and did not cry anymore. The animals near the pond were happy too, because they had a kind and helpful friend. The moral of the story is: if you want to make friends, be kind and help others.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had long hair that she liked to comb every day. One day, she lost her comb and felt sad. She looked everywhere for it, but she could not find it. Amy went to her friend, Tom, for help. Together, they looked for the comb. They looked under the bed, behind the door, and even in the toy box. Suddenly, Tom saw a bad bug. He was scared and slapped it away. The bug flew far away, and they were safe. Then, Amy saw her comb on the table. She was happy to find it. She thanked Tom for helping her and for being brave with the bad bug. They played together all day, and Amy combed her hair with her special comb.

John and Sarah were happy siblings who lived in the same house. One day, they decided to go to the mall to choose some new toys. They both looked carefully and watched as other children chose their favorite playthings. Finally, they chose their own. Sarah got a bright yellow car and John got a funny teddy bear. When they got home, they asked their mom to help them wrap the toys. Mum found a clear wrap in the attic and gave it to the children. The siblings smiled and decided to wrap the toys themselves. John and Sarah carefully unwrapped the clear wrap. As they finished wrapping their toys, the wrap got stuck on the teddy bear. The siblings panicked as they heard a loud tearing sound. The wrap was not clear anymore; it had a red tear in it. John and Sarah were very sad. Mum explained that because of their mistake the bear would not look new anymore. They both chose to learn a lesson from their mistake and, from then on, took more care when wrapping things.

Once upon a time, there was a mouse named Tim. Tim loved cheese more than anything. One day, he saw a big, ugly cat. The cat was very hungry and wanted to eat Tim. Tim said, "Please, don't eat me! I can give you some cheese." The ugly cat thought for a moment and said, "Okay, give me the cheese, and I won't eat you." Tim ran to get the cheese, but he started to panic. He couldn't find any cheese. He looked and looked, but there was no cheese anywhere. Tim was very scared. The ugly cat got tired of waiting and said, "I'm going to eat you now!" Tim cried and said, "Please, no! I can't find the cheese!" But the ugly cat didn't care. The cat ate Tim, and that was the end of the story.

Lily was sad. She had a big boo-boo on her knee. She fell down when she was playing with her bike. It hurt a lot and it was red and wet. She cried and cried. Her mom came and saw her. She hugged her and said, "It's okay, Lily. Mommy is here. I will comfort you and make it better." She took her inside and cleaned her boo-boo. She put a band-aid on it and kissed it. Lily felt a little better. She smiled and said, "Thank you, mommy. You are the best." She hugged her mom back. Then she saw her brother, Ben. He was sitting on the couch with his nose wrinkled. He said, "Ew, Lily. You are smelly. You need a bath. You smell like dirt and blood." Lily felt sad again. She said, "No, I don't. You are mean, Ben. You are a bad judge. You don't know anything." Her mom said, "Ben, that's not nice. You should be kind to your sister. She had a hard day. She needs your love, not your words. Apologize to her, please." Ben said, "Sorry, Lily. I didn't mean to hurt your feelings. You are not smelly. You are brave and strong. Do you want to play with me?" Lily said, "Yes, I do. Thank you, Ben. You are a good brother." She smiled and hugged him. They played together with their toys and had fun. Lily forgot about her boo-boo and her pain. She was happy.
