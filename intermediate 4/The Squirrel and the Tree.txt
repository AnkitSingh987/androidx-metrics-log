Category: Intermediate
Title: The Squirrel and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a little squirrel. The squirrel loved to eat nuts. Every day, he would find nuts on the ground and take them to his home. One day, the squirrel found a bad nut. It was not good to eat. He was sad. So, he went to find more nuts. He found a nut tree and picked up many nuts. He was very happy. The squirrel took the nuts to his home. He put them in the sun to dry. The sun was warm and bright. After some time, the nuts were dry and good to eat. The squirrel was happy and ate the nuts with a big smile.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a heavy box with a cable on it. He wanted to see what was inside. Tim asked his friend, Sam, to help him. Sam said, "OK, let's observe and open the box." Tim and Sam tried to open the heavy box. They pulled the cable very hard. The box opened and they saw a big red button. Tim said, "I want to push the button!" Sam said, "No, don't do it. We don't know what it does." But Tim did not listen to Sam. He pushed the button anyway. Suddenly, a big rain cloud came over the park. It rained and rained. Tim and Sam got very wet. They were sad and went home.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lucy. She had a pretty veil that she loved to wear. It was an expensive gift from her mom. One day, Lucy was playing with her friends outside. They were all having fun and laughing. Lucy's mom called her to come inside and finish her lunch. Lucy ran inside and took off her veil. She put it on the table and sat down to eat. While she was eating, her cat, Fluffy, jumped onto the table. Fluffy saw the veil and thought it was a toy. Fluffy grabbed the veil and ran outside. Lucy saw this and ran after her cat. She was very worried about her expensive veil. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the veil up into a tree. Lucy's friends saw this and helped her get the veil back. Lucy was so happy to have her veil back. She thanked her friends for helping her. From that day on, she knew that her friends were always there to help her when she needed them. And she learned to keep her expensive veil safe from Fluffy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She lived in a big house with her mom, dad, and her dog, Buddy. Amy's room was upstairs, and she liked to play and study there. One day, Amy felt nervous about a test she had to take at school. Amy went to her mom and said, "Mom, I am nervous about my test. Can you help me study?" Her mom smiled and said, "Of course, Amy! Let's go upstairs to your room and study together." So, Amy and her mom went upstairs and studied for a long time. Buddy, the dog, sat with them and listened. After they finished studying, Amy felt better and not so nervous anymore. The next day, Amy took her test at school and did great. She was so happy that her mom helped her study, and she couldn't wait to tell her mom and Buddy about her good grade.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Bob. Bob had a nice house and a big car. One day, he went to the park. At the park, Bob saw a little girl named Amy. Amy was sitting on a seat, eating an ice cream. Bob wanted to sit on the seat too. So, he said, "Give me the seat, please." Amy looked up and said, "No, I was here first." Bob was not happy. He started to demand the seat. "Give me the seat now!" he said loudly. But then, something unexpected happened. A big bird came and took the seat away! It flew up high with the seat in its beak. Bob and Amy were both surprised. They looked at each other and laughed. They became friends and played together at the park all day. And they never fought over a seat again.
