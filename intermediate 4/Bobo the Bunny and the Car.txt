Category: Intermediate
Title: Bobo the Bunny and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Bobo. Bobo loved to hop all day long. One day, he hopped so much that his leg began to hurt. He felt very sad and didn't know what to do. Bobo's mom saw him and said, "Don't worry, we will go to the veterinarian." The veterinarian was a nice lady who helped animals feel better. Bobo felt ready to go and see her. He hopped into the car with his mom and they drove to the veterinarian's office. The veterinarian looked at Bobo's leg and said, "You need to rest and not hop so much." Bobo listened to her and soon, his leg felt better. He learned that it is important to take care of himself and not to do too much of one thing. Bobo was happy and ready to hop again, but this time, he knew when to stop and rest.

Once upon a time, there was a small bunny named Bob. Bob loved to hop and play all day. His favorite food was celery. One day, Bob went to the park to find some celery. As Bob hopped around, he saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was also looking for celery. Bob hopped over to Lily and said, "Hi, I am Bob. I like celery. Can we find it together?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, let's find celery together!" So, Bob and Lily hopped around the park, looking for celery. They found a big celery patch and ate it all up. They were very happy and became best friends. From that day on, they always hopped and played together.

Once upon a time, there was a big, bossy dog named Max. Max liked to tell all the other animals what to do. One day, he saw a small mouse named Tim. Max said, "Tim, go get me a firework!" Tim did not like fireworks, as they were too loud for his tiny ears. He looked at Max and said, "No, I refuse!" Max was not happy with Tim. He tried to make Tim listen, but Tim was brave. He did not want to be bossy like Max. So, he went to find his friends, a cat named Lily and a bird named Sam. Together, they told Max that it was not nice to be bossy. Max listened and said, "You are right. I will try to be kind from now on." And from that day, Max was a much nicer friend to all the animals. They all played together and had fun, without any bossy rules or scary fireworks.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a large cage in the park. They are curious. They go near the cage. Inside the cage, there is a bird. The bird is blue and yellow. It has a red ring on its leg. The bird looks sad. Lily and Ben want to help the bird. "Hello, bird," Lily says. "Why are you in the cage?" The bird speaks. It says, "Hello, kids. I am in the cage because I am lost. I flew away from my home. I miss my family." Lily and Ben feel sorry for the bird. They want to find the bird's family. "Where is your home, bird?" Ben asks. The bird speaks. It says, "My home is in the pet shop. It is near the big tree. My family is there. They are like me. They have red rings on their legs." Lily and Ben have an idea. They look for the big tree. They find the pet shop. They see many birds in the pet shop. Some of them have red rings on their legs. They go inside the pet shop. They ask the man to help them. They tell him about the bird in the cage. They show him the cage. The man is happy. He says, "Thank you, kids. You found my bird. He is very special. He can speak. He escaped from his cage. I was looking for him." The man takes the bird out of the cage. He puts him in a new cage with his family. The bird is happy. He speaks. He says, "Thank you, kids. You are very kind. You helped me find my family." Lily and Ben are happy too. They say goodbye to the bird and his family. They go back to the park. They play and have fun. They are good friends.
