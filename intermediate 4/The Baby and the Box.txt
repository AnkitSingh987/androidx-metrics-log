Category: Intermediate
Title: The Baby and the Box

Once, there was a baby. She was very quiet and always smiling. One day, she was playing alone in her room with her favourite toy when suddenly, she started to cry. She was trying to pull her toy out of the box but it was stuck. She wanted to get it, but it wouldn't come out. Suddenly, she started to tear the box apart, she ripped it up with her little hands. Finally, she got her toy. She was so happy and gave a big smile. She was so quiet, but in her room, it was filled with joy. The baby hugged her toy tight and was happy to have it back in her arms. The baby was so happy, she went off to nap in her bed, still holding her toy. Now, she knew that no matter what, she was never going to lose it again!

John was very excited for the evening. He wanted to go outside and see the night sky. As he stepped outside, something he had never seen before caught his attention. Down the street he could see a rich man's house, with a beautiful garden and a tall fence. John asked his Mom, "What is that?" Mom said, "That's a rich man's house. He lives there and he has many nice things." John said, "I want to see inside." His Mom said, "You can't, the fence is very high and we can't get past it." John was sad. He wanted to see what was inside the house. He ran to the other side of the street and circled the house, but it was too high and he couldn't see past it. That evening John was still disappointed. He looked up at the night sky and wished he had been able to see inside the rich man's house.

Lily and Sam are twins. They like to play dress-up with their clothes and toys. One day, they find a big box in the closet. It has many things that belong to their parent. Lily sees a hat with feathers and a shiny pin. She puts it on her head and says, "Look, I am a pirate!" Sam sees a coat with buttons and a scarf. He wears it and says, "Look, I am a detective!" They have fun pretending to be different people. They make noises and act out stories. They do not hear their parent coming home. Their parent opens the door and sees them. Their parent is surprised and laughs. "What are you doing, my little pirates and detectives?" their parent asks. Lily and Sam are scared. They think their parent is angry. They drop their clothes and toys and run to hug their parent. "We are sorry, we just wanted to play," they say. Their parent smiles and hugs them back. "It is okay, I am not angry. I like your imagination and creativity. But next time, please ask me before you use my things. Some of them are old and fragile. And some of them are not for children." Lily and Sam nod and say, "Okay, we will ask you. We love you, parent." Their parent says, "I love you too, my little pirates and detectives. Now, let's put these things back in the box and have some snack."

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw an alligator near the pond. The alligator looked sad and empty. Tim walked up to the alligator and asked, "Why are you so sad?" The alligator said, "I lost my toy in the pond. I can't find it." Tim wanted to help the alligator. He thought and thought. Then, he had an idea. "I can zip in the water and find your toy!" said Tim. Tim jumped in the pond and looked for the toy. Suddenly, he found it! But when he gave the toy back to the alligator, the alligator changed into a magic fairy! The fairy said, "Thank you for helping me, Tim. I was an alligator because of a spell, but you broke it. Now I can go back home!" Tim was happy he could help, and they both played in the park all day.

One day, a polite girl named Sue went for a walk. She saw a big pot in her yard. Sue thought, "I don't own this pot. Whose pot is it?" Sue asked her friend Tom, "Do you own this pot?" Tom said, "No, I don't own the pot." They both looked at the pot and wondered who owned it. Just then, a tiny frog jumped out of the pot! The frog said, "This is my pot!" Sue and Tom were very surprised. They did not know that frogs could talk. The frog thanked them for being polite and let them play with his pot.
