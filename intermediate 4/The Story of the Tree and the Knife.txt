Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Tree and the Knife

One day a wise old man was walking down the street. He enjoyed talking to the people he would pass by, asking them about their day. When he got to the middle of the street, he noticed a tiny knife lying on the pavement. He decided to pick up the knife and kept it in his pocket. He continued walking until he reached a tree. This tree had long branches, and vines that grew all the way up to the top. The wise old man thought "Maybe I can use this knife to help me see the top of the tree." So he took the knife out of his pocket, and carefully began to cut away the vines. After a few cuts he was able to climb up the tree, and see the top of it. From that spot the wise old man could see everything in the village, and all the people he had been talking to earlier. He smiled at the sight.

Once upon a time, in a tidy little house, there lived a cat and a dog. The cat was very good at cleaning, and the dog loved to cook. One day, the dog decided to make a big, yummy steak for dinner. As the dog cooked the steak, the cat saw that the pan was too hot. The cat wanted to warn the dog, so it said, "Be careful, dog! The pan is very hot!" The dog listened to the cat and turned the heat down. They both sat down to eat the yummy steak. After they finished, the cat cleaned up the mess, and the dog thanked the cat for the warning. They were happy and full, and they went to bed with big smiles on their faces.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toys, but he did not like to share. One day, his mom said, "Tim, it is important to share your toys with others." Tim did not listen. At the park, Tim met a new friend named Sam. Sam wanted to play with Tim's toy car. Tim did not permit Sam to play with it. Sam was sad and left. As a result, Tim was left alone. The moral of the story is: not sharing can make others sad and leave you alone. Sharing is important to make friends and be happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim lived in a big tree with his family. One day, it rained a lot, and Tim's tree became very wet. Tim didn't like the wet tree, so he went to find a new place to live. Tim found a big, tall fence with a nail on it. He thought the nail would be a good place to display his new home. Tim worked hard to make a new nest on the nail. He used sticks and leaves to make it cozy. Soon, Tim's family came to see his new nest. They liked the nest, but they were worried about the nail. They told Tim that the nail was not safe, and they should go back to their tree. Tim didn't want to listen, but he decided to trust his family. They all went back to their tree, and the sun came out to dry the wet tree. The moral of the story is to trust and listen to your family, as they know what is best for you.

Once upon a time, there was a little duck who loved to play in the water. One sunny day, the duck went to a pond to swim and have fun. The duck saw many pretty fish in the water and wanted to play with them. The duck jumped into the pond and started to splash and soak in the water. The water was hot, but the duck liked it. The duck made many bubbles with its beak. The bubbles floated up and popped. The little duck was happy to play with the fish and make bubbles in the hot pond. They all played together until the sun went down. Then, the duck went back home to its cozy nest and fell asleep, dreaming of more fun days in the pond.
