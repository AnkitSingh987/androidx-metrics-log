Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Treats

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play and enjoy time with his friends. One day, Tim found a big freezer in his house. He was curious and wanted to see what was inside. Tim opened the freezer and saw many cold treats. He took one and ate it. He felt happy but also a little ashamed because he did not ask his mom first. Tim decided to take more treats and share them with his friends. Tim's friends came over and they all enjoyed the cold treats from the freezer. But soon, they ate all the treats and there were none left. Tim's mom found out and was very sad. She had saved those treats for a special day. Tim and his friends felt bad and ashamed. They learned that they should always ask before taking something that is not theirs.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird. The bird lived in a cage. The cage was in a big tree. The bird liked the tree, but it wanted to come out and see different things. One day, the cage door opened. The bird came out and flew up high. It saw many different things. The bird was happy. It saw a big, red ball. The bird wanted to play with the ball. But, the big, red ball was not a ball. It was a balloon! The bird flew too close, and POP! The balloon burst. The bird was scared and flew back to the cage. The cage was safe. The bird learned to be careful with different things.

One day, a little car named Zoom went for a drive. Zoom loved to go fast and feel the wind on his rough, red paint. He would race down the street, laughing and having fun. Zoom liked to spray water from the puddles as he sped by. The water would go up in the air and make a big splash. Zoom was driving very fast when he saw a big hill. He thought it would be fun to go up the hill and then zoom back down. As he reached the top of the hill, he saw a big, gray cloud in the sky. Zoom was not scared, he thought it was just a cloud. He kept going up the hill, excited to race back down. But when he got to the top, the big gray cloud started to spray rain. The rain came down fast and hard, making the road very wet. Zoom was scared, but he knew he had to get home. So, he went down the hill very slowly and carefully. The rain made the road slippery, but Zoom made it home safe. From that day on, Zoom learned to be more careful when he saw gray clouds in the sky.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Amy. She had a lovely bracelet. The bracelet was red and blue. Amy wore it every day. She loved it so much. One day, Amy went for a walk. She saw a little dog. The dog was lost. Amy wanted to help the dog. She let the dog lead her. They walked and walked. Amy and the dog found the dog's home. The dog's family was happy. They thanked Amy for her help. Amy felt good. She made new friends. And her pretty bracelet shined brightly in the sun.

One day, a little cell named Bob was ready to go on an adventure. He lived in a big body with many other cells. They all had jobs to do, but Bob wanted to see more of the world. So, he asked his friend, the big cell named Tom, if he could part from the group and explore. Tom was not happy with Bob's idea. He said, "No, Bob, we need you here. We all have to work together to keep the body strong and healthy." But Bob really wanted to go, so he tried to think of a way to make Tom understand. Bob said, "I will be very quick, and when I come back, I will have new ideas to help us all do our jobs better!" Tom thought about it for a moment, and then agreed. So, Bob went on his adventure, and he was very careful to be quick and not get lost. As Bob explored, he saw many new things and met other cells who were doing different jobs. He learned a lot from them and was excited to share his new ideas with his friends back home. When Bob returned, Tom was happy to see him. Bob shared all the new things he learned, and the cells worked together even better than before. They were all happy that Bob had gone on his adventure, and they knew that they could learn from each other to keep the body strong and healthy.
