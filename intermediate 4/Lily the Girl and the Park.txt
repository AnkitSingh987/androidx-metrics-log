Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Park

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. In the park, Lily saw a big fountain. The water in the fountain went up and down, and it made Lily very happy. Near the fountain, Lily saw some wild birds. They were flying around and singing. Lily wanted to play with the birds, but she had to choose. Should she play near the fountain or with the wild birds? Lily thought for a moment and then she chose to play near the fountain. She splashed in the water and laughed. The wild birds flew down and played with her too. They all had a great time at the park.

One day, a little dog went for a walk. He saw a big, dirty bush. The dog wanted to play with the bush. He jumped and ran around it. He had so much fun! A little girl saw the dog and the dirty bush. She wanted to play, too. She asked the dog, "Can I play with you?" The dog just gave a little shrug. The girl smiled and joined the dog. They played and laughed near the dirty bush all day. The sun went down, and it was time to go home. The girl and the dog said goodbye. They were happy they found a new friend and a fun bush to play with.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to travel with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. Yesterday, they went on a big adventure! They walked through the forest, being very careful not to step on any bugs. Max and Lily saw many pretty flowers and tall trees. They even saw a bunny hopping around. Max wanted to play with the bunny, but Lily said, "Max, be careful! We don't want to scare the bunny." Max listened to Lily and they continued their adventure. They found a big lake and played by the water. They splashed and laughed together. At the end of the day, Max and Lily went home, happy from their fun day of traveling. They couldn't wait for their next adventure!

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man. He had many things. He lived in a big house. One day, he wanted to move to a new place. He told his friends about it. His friends came to help him move. They put his things in big boxes. Then, they moved the boxes to a big truck. The wealthy man was happy. He said kind words to his friends. They all smiled. In the new place, the wealthy man had a big party. All his friends came. They played games and ate good food. The wealthy man was very happy in his new home. His friends were happy too. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a mushroom. The mushroom lived in a big forest. It was a happy mushroom. One day, a little boy found the mushroom. The boy was lost and hurt. He sat down by the mushroom and cried. The mushroom wanted to help the boy. It told the boy, "Don't be sad. I am here to help." The boy felt better. He got up and walked with the mushroom. They found the boy's home. The boy and the mushroom were very happy.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to visit his friend, Sam. Tim put on his fit shoes and walked to Sam's house. When he got there, he saw Sam playing with a big pile of junk. Tim was excited and asked, "Can I play too?" Sam said, "Yes, let's build something fun with this junk!" They started to build a big fort. They used boxes, sticks, and old clothes. It was a lot of fun. They played in the fort all day. But then, something unexpected happened. A little dog came and jumped on their fort. The fort fell down! Tim and Sam were surprised. The little dog wagged its tail and wanted to play. They all laughed and played together. Now, they had a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely cat named Fluffy. Fluffy liked to relax under a big tree. One day, when Fluffy was relaxing, a little bird came to visit her. The little bird was not feeling well. It asked Fluffy for help. Fluffy was very patient with the bird. She listened to the bird's problem and tried to help it feel better. They talked and played together. After a while, the bird started to feel better. It thanked Fluffy for being so patient and lovely. Then, the bird flew away happily. Fluffy went back to relax under the big tree, feeling happy that she could help her new friend.
