Title: The Girl Called Lucy and the Star

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Lucy. She was three years old and loved playing with her toys. One day she took out her big, shiny lamp and decided to hold it up to the sky. Its light was so sharp and clear, it made her glad. Suddenly, something magical happened! A star descended from the sky and flew right into Lucy's arms. The star spoke to her in a tiny voice and said, "you are a brave and special girl, Lucy. I want to be your friend." Lucy was so glad her new friend was here. She promised to take care of him and together they played with her lamp all afternoon until the sun went down. Before leaving, the star smiled at Lucy and said, "Until next time, goodbye!" And Lucy felt so glad that she finally found a new friend who thought she was brave and special. They waved goodbye and the star flew back up to the sky, as sharp as ever. It was a magical day. THE

Lily and Sam are friends. They like to play outside. One day, they visit Sam's grandma. She has a big garden with many plants. "Wow, look at all the flowers!" Lily says. "They are so pretty and colorful." "Yes, they are," Sam's grandma says. "Do you want to help me water them?" Lily and Sam nod. They take a watering can and a hose. They go around the garden and give water to the plants. The plants are happy and thirsty. "Thank you, Lily and Sam," Sam's grandma says. "You are very good helpers. Do you know what these plants are called?" Lily and Sam shake their heads. They do not know the names of the plants. Sam's grandma tells them the names of some of the plants. She points to a big green plant with spiky leaves. "This is an aloe vera. It is a cool plant. It can help heal your skin if you get a cut or a burn." She points to a small purple plant with fuzzy leaves. "This is a lavender. It is a cool plant. It can make you feel calm and sleepy if you smell it." She points to a tall yellow plant with a round head. "This is a sunflower. It is a cool plant. It can follow the sun and make seeds that you can eat." Lily and Sam are amazed. They think the plants are very cool. They want to learn more about them. "Can we come back and visit you and your plants again?" Lily asks. "Of course, you can," Sam's grandma says. "You are always welcome here. I have more plants to show you next time." Lily and Sam smile. They hug Sam's grandma and say goodbye. They are happy to have a new friend and a new hobby. They love plants.

Once upon a time, there was a big hole in the ground. A little boy named Tim found the hole while he was playing outside. He was very proud of his discovery. He ran to tell his friend, Sam, about the hole. Tim said, "Sam, I found a big hole! Let's go see it!" Sam was excited and they both went to look at the hole. They stood at the edge and looked down, but they couldn't see the bottom. Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's throw rocks in the hole and listen for them to hit the bottom!" They threw rocks into the hole, but they never heard them hit the bottom. Tim and Sam got too close to the edge and fell into the hole. They were scared and cried for help. Their parents came looking for them, but they couldn't find the boys. In the end, Tim and Sam were punished for not being careful near the hole. They never got out of the hole and their parents were very sad. Tim and Sam learned that they should always be careful and listen to their parents.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy old man named Joe. Joe loved nothing more than flying around in his jet. Every day he would fly away, high up in the sky. One day, Joe was feeling extra grumpy. He asked his friends why he felt that way. "Maybe you should take a few days off to relax," they recommended. "No way! Flying is the only thing that makes me happy," replied Joe. "It's the only thing that I like to do!" Luckily, his friends had a recommendation. They told Joe to go to the beach and take a walk on the shore. "Trust us - you'll love it!" they said. Joe decided to give it a try. After a few days at the beach, he was no longer grumpy. He returned to his jet with a smile on his face. Joe realized that sometimes, it pays to take his friends' recommendations.
