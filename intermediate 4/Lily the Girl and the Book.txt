Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Book

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to read books. Her favorite book was a big, thick novel. She read it every day. One day, Lily's mom bought her a new toy. It was a big box that could be filled with anything. Lily was so happy. She decided to fill the box with her favorite things. She put her novel, her teddy bear, and her soft blanket inside. But, Lily's little brother, Tim, was spoiled. He wanted the box for himself. He took the box and dumped out all of Lily's things. Lily was sad. Her mom saw what happened and told Tim to share. Tim said sorry and helped Lily fill the box again. They both played with the box and had fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys and look at his big folder of drawings. One day, Tim wanted to find his favorite drawing of a funny cat. So, he went to his room to examine the folder. Tim's folder was high up on a shelf. He tried to reach for it, but it was too high for him. Tim did not give up. He got a chair and stood on it. Now he could reach the high shelf and get his folder. Tim opened the folder and started to look at his drawings. He saw a big red ball, a happy dog, and a pretty flower. Finally, he found his funny cat drawing. Tim was so happy that he showed it to his mom and dad. They all laughed and had a fun time looking at the drawings together.

Once upon a time, there was a king. The king had a big, shiny crown. The crown was very brilliant. It was the king's favorite thing. One day, the king went for a walk. He saw a little dog. The little dog was lost. The king wanted to help the dog. He put a leash on the dog and lead the dog to the big castle. The king and the little dog became good friends. They played together every day. The little dog liked the king's brilliant crown too. They lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Tim. Tim was an ordinary boy who liked to play with his toys. One day, he saw a big red ball in the park. He decided to go and play with it. Tim kicked the ball and it went far away. He ran after it but bumped into a tree. Tim hurt his lip and it made him sad. He sat down and cried a little bit. A nice lady saw Tim and came to help him. She put a band-aid on his lip and it made him feel better. Tim said, "Thank you!" and they both played with the big red ball. They had a fun day at the park.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the beach with her family. She was very excited to play in the sand and swim in the water. She brought her favorite toy, a small, compassionate doll named Rose. Rose had a big heart and was always kind to everyone. As Lily played in the sand, she found a small bag of salt. She wondered what it was doing there. She showed it to Rose, and they decided to sprinkle some salt on the sand to see what would happen. Suddenly, the sand began to move and change colors! Lily and Rose were amazed. The sand turned into a beautiful rainbow and started to dance around them. Lily and Rose laughed as they played with the magical rainbow sand. They had never seen anything like this before. The unexpected surprise made their day at the beach even more special. From that day on, Lily and Rose always brought a little bit of salt with them to the beach, hoping to see the magical rainbow sand again.

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play outside in the garden. They have a big box of toys. They have cars, balls, dolls, and books. One day, they receive a new toy from their grandma. It is a big globe. It shows the earth. The earth is round and has many colors. Sara and Ben are happy. They want to learn about the earth. They take the globe to the garden. They put it on the grass. They spin it and look at it. They see blue, green, brown, and white. They see land and water. They see animals and people. "Look, Sara, this is where we live," Ben says. He points to a small spot on the globe. "This is our country. It is red on the globe." "Wow, Ben, that is cool," Sara says. She points to another spot on the globe. "What is this? It is white and big. It looks cold." "That is the North Pole, Sara," Ben says. "It is very far from us. It has snow and ice. It is hard and frozen. Some animals live there, like polar bears and penguins." Sara and Ben are curious. They want to know more about the earth. They ask their grandma to tell them stories. They thank her for the globe. They love their new toy. They love the earth.
