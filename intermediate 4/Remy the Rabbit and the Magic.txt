Category: Intermediate
Title: Remy the Rabbit and the Magic

Once upon a time, there was a little rabbit named Remy. Remy was very scared of the dark. Every night, his mommy would give him a special drink with magic powder to help him sleep. The powder was made from the sweetest flowers in the forest. One day, Remy found the jar of magic powder in the kitchen. He thought if he used more powder in his drink, he wouldn't be scared anymore. So, he put a lot of powder in his cup and mixed it with water. When he drank it, his tummy started to hurt. Remy's mommy found him crying and asked what happened. Remy told her about the magic powder. She hugged him and explained that too much of anything can be bad. She told him that being scared is okay, and they can face their fears together. From that day, Remy learned not to use too much of anything and to talk to his mommy when he was scared.

Once upon a time, there was a little rubber ball. It was red and shiny. The rubber ball did not know a lot about the world. It was ignorant. But it was happy to play and bounce. One day, the rubber ball met a big rock. The rock was gray and strong. The rock did not move much. The rubber ball wanted to be friends with the rock. The rock said yes, and they played together. The rock liked to be still and watch the world. The rubber ball liked to bounce and roll. They were very different, but they were happy together. The rock and the rubber ball learned from each other and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big, round squash in his garden. Tim loved his squash very much. One day, he saw that his squash was dirty. So, he decided to clean it. Tim took a cloth and water. He began to rub the dirty squash. As he cleaned, a small bug came out. The bug looked at Tim and said, "Thank you for cleaning my home." Tim was happy to help the bug. The bug was very nice. It wanted to say thank you more. So, the bug did a bow to Tim. Tim laughed and bowed back. They became good friends. Tim and the bug played in the garden every day. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to her friend Tim's house to play. They had fun with their toys and then they got thirsty. Lucy asked Tim, "Do you want to have a tea party?" Tim said yes, and they set up a small table with cups and a tea pot. Lucy poured tea into the cups. They took a sip and made a face. The tea was bitter! They did not like it. Tim said, "Let's quit drinking this tea. Let's make it sweet." They found some sugar and put it in their cups. Suddenly, a small dog ran into the room and jumped on the table. It knocked over the tea pot and all the tea spilled out. Lucy and Tim laughed. They forgot about the bitter tea and played with the dog. The dog made their day even more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot had a pretty collar. He loved to play and run in the park. One day, Spot was a little careless. He did not see the big tree in front of him. He hit the tree and his collar fell off. A girl named Sue found the collar. Sue wanted to add something nice to the collar. She added a small bell to it. She thought the bell would make the collar even prettier. She wanted to find Spot and give him back his collar. Sue went to the park to look for Spot. She saw many dogs, but none of them were Spot. Then, she heard the bell on the collar ring. She looked around and saw a cat playing with the collar. The cat liked the bell sound. Sue was surprised, but she was happy the cat liked the collar. She decided to let the cat keep the collar. In the end, Spot got a new collar and the cat had a pretty collar with a bell too. Both Spot and the cat were happy, and Sue learned that sometimes unexpected things can make everyone happy.
