Title: Sue and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. She loved to dance. Every day, she would put on her pretty dress and dance in the yard. She was very good at it. All her friends liked to watch her dance. One day, while Sue was dancing, she heard a loud noise. It was a big, tough dog. The dog ran up to her and wanted to play. But when the dog jumped, it made a tear in Sue's pretty dress. Sue was sad. Sue's friends saw what happened. They wanted to help her. They found some cloth and fixed the tear in her dress. Sue was happy again. She put on her pretty dress and danced with her friends. The big, tough dog watched and wagged its tail.

One day, a little girl was walking home when she saw an ambulance. She looked at it carefully and saw some people inside. They looked helpful and the girl wanted to help them. The girl ran to the ambulance and asked if the people needed help. They smiled and said, "Yes. Can you please give us a hand?". She was so excited to be helpful and said, "Of course". The girl helped the people in the ambulance and they thanked her for her help. When the ambulance left, the little girl waved goodbye. She was so proud she could help people and it made her happy. From that day on she was always looking for ways to help others.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy had a pale blue telephone. She loved to play with her telephone. She would talk to her friends and her mommy and daddy on it. One day, Lucy was playing with her telephone when she felt hungry. She wanted to eat something. She looked around and saw a big, red apple on the table. But Lucy was very little, and she couldn't reach the apple. Suddenly, something unexpected happened. Lucy's telephone started to grow! It grew and grew until it was as big as the table. Lucy climbed on top of the telephone and reached for the apple. She was so happy that she could eat the apple now. And from that day on, whenever Lucy needed help, her pale blue telephone would always be there to help her.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a young boy named Tim. Tim had a big clock on the wall. He loved to watch the clock and listen to it tick-tock. Tim also liked to chew on things, like his toys and his clothes. His mom and dad told him not to chew on things, but Tim did it anyway. One day, Tim saw the clock on the wall and thought it looked fun to chew. He took the clock down and started to chew on it. The clock broke into pieces, and Tim was sad. He knew that he should not have chewed on the clock. Tim told his mom and dad what he did, and they were not happy. They told Tim that he needed to learn not to chew on things that are not food. Tim felt bad and said he was sorry. His mom and dad forgave him, but they said he must remember the lesson. From that day on, Tim never chewed on things that were not food, and he learned to listen to his mom and dad. The moral of the story is to always listen to your parents and not to chew on things that are not food.
