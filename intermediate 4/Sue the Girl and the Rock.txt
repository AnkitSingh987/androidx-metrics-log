Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a favorite sweater that she wore every day. The sweater was very flexible and soft, just like a hug from her mom. Sue loved her sweater so much. One day, Sue met a new friend named Tim. Tim saw Sue rock back and forth on her swing, wearing her flexible sweater. "I like your sweater," said Tim. Sue smiled and said, "Thank you! It is my favorite sweater." Sue and Tim played together all day. They rocked on the swings, and they shared their toys. They had so much fun, and Sue's flexible sweater kept her warm and happy. At the end of the day, Sue and Tim said goodbye and promised to play together again.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Tim. Tim loved to wear a big hat. He wore his hat every day. Tim's hat made him feel very happy. One day, Tim went to a fun show called an opera. At the opera, there were people who sang songs. They wore pretty clothes and danced around. Tim liked to watch them and listen to the music. But, during the opera, Tim's hat fell off. He felt sad and miserable without his hat. He looked everywhere for it. Then, a nice lady found Tim's hat and gave it back to him. Tim was happy again. He wore his hat and enjoyed the rest of the opera.

Anna loves to spin. She spins in her room, in the garden, and in the park. She spins so fast that she feels dizzy and happy. She likes to wear her bright dress when she spins. It has many colors and it makes a big circle around her. One day, Anna goes to the store with her mom. She sees a big tub of yogurt. It is white and creamy. She wants to try it. She asks her mom if she can have some. Her mom says yes, but she has to be careful. Yogurt can be messy. Anna takes a spoon and dips it in the yogurt. She brings it to her mouth and tastes it. It is sour and sweet. She likes it. She takes another spoonful and another. She feels full and happy. She wants to spin. She jumps off her chair and starts to spin in the store. She spins so fast that she forgets about the yogurt. The yogurt flies off her spoon and lands on the floor, on the shelves, and on the people. It makes a big mess. Anna stops spinning and looks around. She sees the yogurt everywhere. She sees the angry faces of the people. She sees her mom's face. It is not happy. It is sad and mad. Anna feels bad. She says sorry. She helps her mom clean up the mess. She learns a lesson. She can spin, but not with yogurt.

Once upon a time, in a small hut, there lived a graceful cat named Luna. Luna loved to dance and play with her friends. She had many friends like a dog, a bird, and a fish. They all liked to play together in the sun. One day, while they were playing, a big storm came. Luna warned her friends, "We need to go to the hut to be safe!" They all ran to the hut and waited for the storm to stop. They were scared, but they knew they were safe together. As they sat in the hut, they heard a knock at the door. Luna opened the door and saw a wet, shivering mouse. The mouse said, "Can I please come in? I am cold and wet from the storm." Luna let the mouse in, even though she had never been friends with a mouse before. They all became friends and played together after the storm. The unexpected new friend made them all very happy.

Once upon a time there was a friendly snowman. He loved to ride his sleigh all around the neighborhood on winter days. The snowman was always looking for someone to join him on his rides. One day, a boy named Bob who was 3 years old wanted to go for a ride on the snowman's sleigh. He asked if he could come and the snowman was so excited. "Yes, you can ride with me!" exclaimed the snowman. So, Bob and the snowman got on the sleigh. As they were riding, they could see the snow falling from the sky and the sun’s rays shining down. They both agreed that it was very beautiful in the winter. "Would you like something to eat?" asked the snowman. "I have some spicy candy." Bob said yes and the snowman passed him a piece of candy. It was so spicy that it made Bob's face red. Bob and the snowman laughed and continued their ride through the town on their sleigh while they ate their spicy candy.
