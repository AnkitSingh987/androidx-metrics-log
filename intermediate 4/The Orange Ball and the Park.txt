Category: Intermediate
Title: The Orange Ball and the Park

One day, a child saw a big orange ball in the park. The child liked the ball and wanted to play with it. The child ran to the ball and picked it up. The ball was soft and bouncy. But another child saw the ball too. The other child also liked the ball and wanted to play with it. The other child ran to the first child and said, "Give me the ball! It is mine!" The first child said, "No, it is mine! I saw it first!" The two children started to fight over the ball. They pulled and pushed and shouted. The ball did not like to be fought over. The ball wanted to be free and happy. The ball waited for a chance to move. When the two children were not looking, the ball rolled away from them. The ball rolled faster and faster, until it reached the road. The road was busy and noisy. There were many cars and trucks and buses. The ball did not see the cars and trucks and buses. The ball only wanted to move. The ball bounced on the road. But a big truck saw the ball. The truck did not want to hit the ball. The truck tried to stop, but it was too late. The truck hit the ball with a loud bang. The ball popped and broke. The ball was no more. The two children heard the bang. They stopped fighting and looked at the road. They saw the ball in pieces. They felt sad and sorry. They had lost the ball. They had lost their chance to play. They had lost their fun. They learned a hard lesson. They wished they had shared the ball.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a soft, little cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to eat sugar. Every day, she would go to her friend, a kind boy named Tim, who would give her sugar to eat. Tim knew that too much sugar was not good for Kitty, but he wanted to make her happy. One day, Tim's mom told him that he should not give Kitty so much sugar. She said that it might make Kitty sick. Tim did not understand why his mom would say that, but he decided to listen to her. He stopped giving Kitty sugar and started giving her healthy food instead. At first, Kitty was sad and missed the sugar. But soon, she started to feel better and had more energy to play with Tim. Kitty and Tim understood that sometimes, what makes us happy is not always good for us. They learned that it is important to listen to those who care about us, like Tim's mom, because they want the best for us. And so, Kitty and Tim lived happily, playing and having fun, knowing that they were making the right choices.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who loved to bounce. Every day she would go out and find somewhere new to bounce. One day she found a really good spot right next to a big tree. She started to bounce and suddenly the tree branch above her started to glow with a beautiful light. She was so amazed and wondered what could be making the light. She stopped bouncing and heard a gentle voice coming from the tree. The voice said, "Hello! You do a really good job at bouncing!" The little girl was so surprised and asked, "Who are you?". The voice said, "I am the tree fairy and I like to reward people who bounce with good things. So for you I have made this beautiful light for you to take home and enjoy." The little girl was so happy and thanked the tree fairy. From that day on she kept bouncing and the tree fairy rewarded her with different lights and good things. They became the best of friends and shared lots of fun adventures.

One day, a cool bunny named Bob went to play outside. He loved to bounce and hop all day long. He was very happy and liked to play with his friends. Bob found a big jar of honey near a tree. He wanted to share the honey with his friends. He called them to come and eat the honey with him. They all came and were very excited. But then, something unexpected happened! The jar of honey started to bounce and hop just like Bob! The honey jar was alive and wanted to play with them too. Bob and his friends laughed and played with the bouncing honey jar. They all became best friends and had lots of fun together.
