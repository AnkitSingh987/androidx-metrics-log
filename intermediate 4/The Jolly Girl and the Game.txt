Category: Intermediate
Title: The Jolly Girl and the Game

Once upon a time, there was a jolly girl. She lived in a big town with many houses. One day, the girl decided to play a game. It was a fun game that she had never played before. It was a game where you had to guess the right number. The girl was so excited that she wanted to win the game. She tried very hard to guess the right number. Finally, she guessed the number and she won the game! Everyone cheered because she was the winner. The girl was very happy. She thought the game was so much fun that she wanted to win it again. Everyone said that she was jolly and very clever. The girl went home and thought about the game. She wanted to win it once more and was determined to find the right answer the next time.

Once upon a time, in a big city, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to choose her own clothes every day. One day, she put on a red shirt and blue pants. She was ready to go out and play. Lily went to the park with her mom. They played on the swings and slides. Lily was having so much fun. She saw her friends and played with them too. They ran around and laughed a lot. After a while, Lily started to feel tired. She told her mom, "Mom, I am tired." Her mom said, "Let's go home and rest." Lily and her mom walked back to their house. Lily took a nap and had sweet dreams about her fun day in the city.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his toys. One day, Tim felt troubled. He wanted to make a new friend, but he didn't know how. Tim had an idea. He went to his mom and asked for a tomato. Mom said, "Here you go, Tim." Tim was happy to receive the tomato from his mom. He thought it would help him make a new friend. Tim took the tomato and went outside. He saw a little girl named Sue. Tim walked up to Sue and gave her the tomato. Sue smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim!" They played together all day and became best friends. Tim was no longer troubled, all because of the tomato.

One day, a little brown dog went for a walk. The dog saw a big brown mug on the ground. The dog thought, "I want to touch the mug." But the dog was too small to reach it. The dog saw a cat and asked, "Can you help me touch the brown mug?" The cat said, "Yes, I can help." The cat jumped and touched the mug with its paw. The dog was happy. But when the cat touched the mug, something unexpected happened. The brown mug started to shake and jump. It turned into a big brown frog! The frog said, "Thank you for touching the mug and setting me free!" The dog and the cat were very surprised and happy to have a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin liked to play in the big blue sea. One day, he saw a big broken shell on the sea floor. It was very pretty, so he wanted to show it to his friend, Sam the starfish. Fin swam fast to show Sam the broken shell. But suddenly, a big wave came. Fin started to panic because he didn't know what to do. The wave pushed him far away from Sam and the broken shell. Fin was sad, but he swam back to find Sam and the broken shell. When he found them, he showed Sam the pretty shell. They both smiled and played with the broken shell. And they were very happy together.

Once upon a time, there was a round ball. This ball was very popular with all the kids. They liked to play with it every day. The ball had many friends and was always happy. One day, something strange happened. The round ball got lost in the big park. It was sad and lonely. The kids looked for the ball everywhere, but they could not find it. Then, a little girl found the round ball under a big tree. She was so happy! She brought the ball back to the kids, and they all played together again. The popular round ball was happy to be back with its friends, and they all had lots of fun.
