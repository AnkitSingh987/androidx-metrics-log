Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Cloud

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a big purple cloud in the sky. The cloud looked sad. Tim wanted to help the cloud. Tim called out, "Cloud, why are you sad?" The cloud said, "I want to play with the other clouds, but they don't like me because I am purple." Tim had an idea. He urged the cloud to try again and talk to the other clouds. "Please, cloud, go talk to them. They might like you if you try." The purple cloud listened to Tim and went to talk to the other clouds. At first, the other clouds were not nice. But then, the purple cloud showed them how it could make pretty rainbows. The other clouds liked the rainbows and they all became friends. In the end, the purple cloud was happy, and Tim was happy too. They both learned that it is good to try and make new friends, even if it is hard at first.

Once upon a time, there was a small, lucky creature called Joe. Everyday, Joe would wake up in his cosy bed and look around his bedroom. He saw his favourite teddy bear and always felt so excited. One special morning, Joe woke extra early and decided he wanted to explore. He boldly opened his bedroom door, and stepped out into the hallway. Suddenly, he heard a noise coming from the kitchen. He crept closer to see who it was. To his surprise, he found a family of little mice! He watched, amazed, as they all scurried about. One of the mice noticed Joe standing there, and spoke to him. "What brings you here?" he asked. Joe replied, "I was curious to see what was under the cupboard". The mice all laughed and cheered. Then, one of the mice spoke up again. "It seems you are a lucky boy indeed. Only the most curious of adventurers stumble upon us!". Joe was so pleased that he had been lucky enough to find them. He thanked the mice, and returned to his bedroom, happier than ever.

Once upon a time there was a boy called Bob. He was very happy and had lots of fun. One day, Bob decided to go for a walk. He wore his warm coat and put on his boots, ready for a adventure. As Bob walked along, he saw something white and fluffy. He walked closer and saw snow falling lightly from the sky. Bob was so excited to see the snow. He scooped up some in his hands, enjoying the feeling of it's cold, wet texture. Bob's mum warned him to be careful as it was very cold outside. But he knew that he wanted to carry the snow home and show his mum. Bob scooped up a big ball of snow and danced with it in his arms. It felt so light and smooth, and made his heart feel sweet. But when he went to carry it back to his house, it melted away. Bob was sad but his mum said, "Don't worry, Bob. We can go to the park tomorrow and make a snowman." Smiling, Bob looked up at the sky and said, "Yes, mum! That sounds like fun!"

One day, a boy named Tom went to play with his ball. He liked to kick the ball very high. Tom saw his friend Will and asked, "Will, do you want to play with me?" Will said, "Yes, I want to play!" So, Tom and Will played with the ball. They kicked the ball back and forth. They had a lot of fun. Then, a slow turtle came by. The turtle saw the ball and wanted to play too. Tom and Will did not think the turtle could play because he was so slow. But the turtle said, "I can play too! Watch me!" The turtle kicked the ball very high, higher than Tom and Will could kick it. Tom and Will were very surprised. They all played together and had a great time. The slow turtle was not so slow after all.

Once upon a time, there was a gold swing in the park. It was so fine! Everyone wanted to swing on it. One day, a little girl named Lilly came to the park. She saw the beautiful gold swing and she wanted to give it a try. She jumped on and started to swing. Back and forth, up and down, she went. The sun shone down on Lilly and the gold swing, making it look even finer. Lilly had so much fun! She kept swinging until she was all worn out. No matter how hard she tried, she couldn't swing anymore. Lilly got off and thanked the fine, gold swing for being so much fun. Then, she said goodbye and went home.
