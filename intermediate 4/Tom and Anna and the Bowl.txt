Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Anna and the Bowl

Tom and Anna were playing with their toys in the living room. They had a lot of fun making noises and pretending to be animals. Tom was a lion and Anna was a bird. "Roar!" Tom said, jumping on the couch. "I am the king of the jungle!" "Tweet!" Anna said, flying around the room. "I am the fastest bird in the sky!" They laughed and chased each other until they heard their mom call them. "Tom! Anna! It's time for dinner!" Mom said from the kitchen. Tom and Anna ran to the table and sat down. They saw their mom had made their favorite food: spaghetti and meatballs. They smiled and said thank you. "Mom, this is yummy!" Tom said, twirling his fork. "Mom, this is delicious!" Anna said, taking a big bite. Mom smiled and said, "I'm glad you like it. And guess what? I have a surprise for you after dinner." "What is it?" Tom and Anna asked, curious. Mom winked and said, "You'll see. But you have to finish your plates first." Tom and Anna ate quickly, eager to find out the surprise. They wondered what it could be. A new toy? A book? A game? When they were done, mom brought out a big tray with four bowls. In each bowl, there was a scoop of ice cream, some whipped cream, and a cherry on top. "Dessert!" Tom and Anna exclaimed, clapping their hands. Mom nodded and said, "Yes, dessert. I made it for you because you have been such good kids today. You can choose your flavor: chocolate, vanilla, strawberry, or mint." Tom and Anna looked at the bowls and thought hard. They both liked all the flavors, but they had different favorites. "I want chocolate!" Tom said, reaching for a bowl. "I want strawberry!" Anna said, grabbing another bowl. But then they realized they had both taken the same bowls. They looked at each other and frowned. "Hey, that's mine!" Tom said, pulling the bowl. "No, that's mine!" Anna said, tugging the bowl. They started to fight over the bowl, spilling some ice cream on the table. They were upset and angry. "Stop it!" Mom said, raising her voice. "You can't have the same bowl. There are two more flavors. Why don't you trade?" Tom and Anna looked at the other bowls. They saw vanilla and mint. They didn't like them as much as chocolate and strawberry, but they knew they had to share. "Fine," Tom said, letting go of the bowl. "I'll take vanilla." "Fine," Anna said, pushing the bowl. "I'll take mint." They swapped the bowls and started to eat. They were still upset, but they didn't want to waste their dessert. They ate in silence, until they heard a knock on the door. "Who is it?" Mom asked, getting up. She opened the door and saw their dad standing there. He had a big smile and a bag in his hand. "Hi, everyone!" Dad said, coming in. "I'm home from work. And I have a surprise for you!" He opened the bag and took out two boxes. He gave one to Tom and one to Anna. "What is it?" Tom and Anna asked, excited. They opened the boxes and saw two cupcakes. One was chocolate and one was strawberry. They had frosting and sprinkles and candles on top. "Cupcakes!" Tom and Anna shouted, jumping up. Dad nodded and said, "Yes, cupcakes. I bought them for you because I love you. And because today is a special day. Do you know what day it is?" Tom and Anna thought for a moment. Then they remembered. "It's our birthday!" Tom and Anna said, smiling. Dad hugged them and said, "That's right. It's your birthday. You are four years old today. Happy birthday, my twins!" Mom joined them and said, "Happy birthday, my sweeties!" They lit the candles and sang happy birthday. Then they blew them out and made a wish. Tom and Anna wished for the same thing: to be happy and to have more dessert.

Tommy was a dog who loved to run and play. He had a red collar that his owner gave him. He was very proud of his collar and liked to show it to other dogs. One day, he saw a big park with many trees and flowers. He wanted to explore it and have fun. He asked his owner, "Can I go to the park, please?" His owner said, "Yes, you can, but be careful. Don't go too far and come back when I call you." Tommy was very excited and ran to the park. He saw many things that he liked: a pond, a bridge, a slide, a swing. He chased a butterfly, jumped over a log, slid down the slide, and swung on the swing. He was having a great time. But he did not notice that his collar was loose. When he was on the swing, his collar slipped off his neck and fell on the ground. He did not see it and kept playing. After a while, his owner called him, "Tommy, come here. It's time to go home." Tommy heard his owner and ran back to him. But when he got there, his owner looked worried. "Tommy, where is your collar?" his owner asked. Tommy felt his neck and realized that his collar was gone. He felt sad and scared. He did not know where his collar was. He said, "I'm sorry, I lost my collar. I was playing and I did not see it." His owner said, "Oh, Tommy, you should have been more careful. Your collar is important. It has your name and my phone number on it. If you lose it, someone might not know who you are or where you live. You could get lost or in trouble. You have to be more responsible and pay attention to your things." Tommy felt sorry and ashamed. He said, "I'm sorry, I was too excited and I forgot. Can we find my collar, please?" His owner said, "We can try, but it might be hard. The park is big and there are many people and dogs. Someone might have taken it or thrown it away. But we can look for it together. Maybe we will succeed." They went back to the park and searched for the collar. They asked other people and dogs if they had seen it. They looked under the slide, behind the log, near the pond, and on the bridge. They did not give up. Finally, they found the collar. It was on a bench, next to a lady who was reading a book. She had seen the collar and picked it up, hoping to find the owner. She was happy to see Tommy and his owner. She said, "Is this your collar? I found it on the ground. It's a nice collar. It has your name and your owner's phone number on it. You are a lucky dog." Tommy and his owner thanked the lady and took the collar. Tommy was very happy and relieved. He put the collar back on his neck and felt safe and proud again. He said, "Thank you, thank you, thank you. I'm so glad we found my collar. I'm sorry I lost it. I will be more careful next time. I will not forget." His owner said, "You're welcome, you're welcome, you're welcome. I'm glad too. I'm proud of you for looking for it and not giving up. You learned a good lesson today. You have to be responsible and pay attention to your things. They are part of who you are and what you love. You have to respect them and take care of them." Tommy nodded and hugged his owner. He learned a good lesson and he was happy. He still loved to run and play, but he also loved his collar and his owner. He did not want to lose them again. He was a good dog.
