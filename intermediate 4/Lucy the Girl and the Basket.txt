Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Basket

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Lucy. She had a delicate basket that she loved very much. One day, she went to the park with her basket to pick flowers. Lucy was very happy. While Lucy was picking flowers, a big dog came and wanted to play. The dog did not want to disturb Lucy, but it was excited and knocked over her basket. Lucy was sad and cried. The dog felt bad and wanted to help. The dog had an idea. It ran away and came back with more flowers than Lucy had picked. The dog put the flowers in the basket and gave it back to Lucy. She was very surprised and happy. They became friends and played together. The moral of the story is that even when bad things happen, good can come from it if we help each other.

Once, in a small house, there was a purple cat. The cat was very good at stirring things. One day, the cat found a big file. The cat knew the file was important, but did not know why. The cat showed the file to his friend, the dog. The dog said, "This file will help us find a big treasure!" The cat and the dog were very happy. They knew they had to stir something to find the treasure. The cat and the dog went to the kitchen. They found a big bowl. They put the file in the bowl and started to stir. They stirred and stirred. Suddenly, the bowl started to shake. The cat and the dog were scared, but they kept stirring. Finally, the bowl stopped shaking. Inside the bowl, there was a big treasure! The cat and the dog were so happy. They knew that the purple cat's stirring and the big file had helped them find the treasure. They shared the treasure and lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a favorite hat. The hat was red and had a big, pretty bow. Sue wore the hat every day. She loved it very much. One day, Sue was playing in the park. She saw a small, dirty dog. The dog looked sad and lost. Sue wanted to help the dog. She thought, "I will rescue the dog and take it home." Sue took off her hat and put it on the ground. The dog walked to the hat and sniffed it. Then, the dog picked up the hat in its mouth. Sue led the dog home, with the dog holding the hat all the way. At home, Sue and her mom cleaned the dirty dog. They gave it food and water. The dog was happy and wagged its tail. Sue's hat was dirty, but she didn't mind. She was glad she could rescue the dog with her hat. From that day on, Sue and the dog were best friends. They played together every day. And Sue always wore her favorite red hat, even if it was a little dirty.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a spoon. The spoon was big and shiny. Tim liked the spoon a lot. He wanted to show it to his friend, Sam. Sam was deaf, but he could talk with his hands. Tim could understand Sam's hand talk too. Tim went to Sam's house to show him the spoon. Sam looked at the spoon and smiled. Tim tried to explain why he liked the spoon. He said it was big and shiny. Sam used his hands to say he liked the spoon too. They both laughed and played with the spoon. After they played, Tim and Sam ate some food with the spoon. They had a lot of fun. Tim was happy that he found the spoon and could share it with his friend. Sam was happy too. They both knew they would always be friends, even if they talked in different ways.

Lily was a little girl who liked to play with her toys. She had a lot of toys, but her favorite was her phone. She liked to pretend she was talking to her friends and family on the phone. She also liked to take pictures with her phone and make funny sounds. One day, Lily's mom had to go to work and left Lily with her grandma. Lily's mom told her to behave and listen to her grandma. She also told her not to play with her phone too much, because it was not good for her eyes. Lily said yes, mom, and gave her a hug. But after her mom left, Lily did not behave. She did not want to play with her grandma or eat her snack. She only wanted to play with her phone. She ignored her grandma when she called her name or asked her to do something. She also turned up the volume of her phone so loud that it hurt her grandma's ears. Lily's grandma was sad and angry. She tried to be nice and patient, but Lily was very rude and selfish. She decided to take away Lily's phone and put it in a drawer. She told Lily that she could not have her phone back until she learned to behave and respect her elders. Lily was very mad and sad. She cried and screamed and kicked the drawer. She wanted her phone back. She did not care about her grandma or anything else. She felt very lonely and bored without her phone. But after a while, Lily realized that her phone was not a real friend. It did not talk back to her or hug her or play with her. It only made noises and showed pictures. She also realized that she had hurt her grandma's feelings and made her worry. She felt sorry and ashamed. She went to her grandma and said sorry. She told her that she loved her and wanted to play with her. She also said that she did not need her phone to have fun. She asked her grandma to teach her some games or stories or songs. Lily's grandma was happy and proud. She forgave Lily and hugged her. She took out her phone and gave it back to her. She told her that she could play with her phone sometimes, but not too much. She also told her that she should always behave and be kind to others. Lily learned her lesson. She thanked her grandma and promised to be a good girl. She played with her phone and her grandma, but she also shared and listened and helped. She was not lonely anymore. She was happy.
