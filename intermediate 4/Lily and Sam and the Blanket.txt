Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Sam and the Blanket

Lily and Sam were brother and sister. They liked to play games and watch movies. One day, their mom said they could watch a movie on the big TV. She gave them a big bowl of popcorn and a blanket. They looked at the movies on the shelf. Lily wanted to watch a movie with princesses and fairies. Sam wanted to watch a movie with cars and robots. They could not agree on a movie. They started to argue. "Princesses are boring!" Sam said. "No, cars are boring!" Lily said. Their mom heard them and came to the room. She was not happy. "Stop arguing, please. You can watch a movie together or not at all. How about this one?" She showed them a movie with animals and jokes. "It is funny and cute. Do you want to watch it?" Lily and Sam looked at the movie. They both liked animals and jokes. They nodded. "Okay, then. Let's watch it." Their mom put the movie in the TV and pressed play. She sat with them on the couch and covered them with the blanket. They ate popcorn and watched the movie. They laughed and smiled. They forgot about princesses and cars. They had fun.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. It was a mild day. The sun was out, and the birds were singing. Tim saw a figure on the ground. It was a toy man. Tim picked up the toy man and played with it. He ran around the park, making the toy man fly. Then, something unexpected happened. The toy man began to freeze. Tim was so surprised! The toy man turned into a real man. He was a magic man! The magic man thanked Tim for making him free. They became good friends and played in the park all day.

Once upon a time, there was a big church in a small town. Many people went to the church every day. One day, a kind man named Tom came to the church. He saw that the church was dirty. Tom wanted to help, so he decided to polish the church. Tom worked very hard to make the church clean and shiny. He thought that a clean church would make the people in the town happy and healthy. While Tom was polishing the church, he found a big box behind a door. The box was very old and dusty. Tom was curious about what was inside the box. Tom opened the box and found a lot of yummy food inside. He was very surprised! He knew that the people in the town were hungry. Tom shared the food with everyone in the town. They all ate the food and became healthy and strong. The people in the town were very happy and thanked Tom for finding the food and making the church clean.

One day, a happy boy named Tim found a big bucket. He was very enthusiastic to play with it. He ran to the park with the bucket, excited to show his friends. At the park, Tim saw a girl named Sally. Sally wanted the bucket too. They both tried to grab the bucket, but it was too big for both of them. They didn't know what to do. Then, Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's freeze the bucket and break it in half!" They put water in the bucket and left it outside in the cold. The bucket became very cold and froze. They broke the frozen bucket into two pieces. Now, Tim and Sally both had a piece of the bucket to play with, and they became good friends.

Once there was a girl called Lucy. She was very organized, and always knew exactly what she wanted to do. One day Lucy put on her sunglasses. She felt fast and ready for the day. She had a plan: she wanted to go to the park and play with her friends. Lucy's mom looked at her. "Are you sure you are organized enough, Lucy?" she asked. Lucy beamed with confidence. "Yes, I am ready. I will be fast and have lots of fun!" So Lucy grabbed her things, put her sunglasses on her head and ran off to the park. She organized an exciting game with her friends and they had lots of fun. When it was time to go home, Lucy looked at her friends and said, "Let's do this again soon!"
