Category: Intermediate
Title: Jack and Sally and the Garden

Softly, Jack and Sally walked through the garden. They admired the colorful roses and wanted to celebrate. Jack picked a rose and handed it to Sally. She thanked him with a smile and they hugged each other tight. They sat down together and began to dance. They spun around and around, clapping their hands and singing. They were celebrating their friendship and the beauty of the rose. The celebration was filled with joy. Jack and Sally laughed and cheered. They hugged and shared stories and smiled with each other. Finally, they said goodbye and promised to come back and celebrate again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Sarah. One day, Sarah's mommy said, "Let's go and pay for your dance class." Sarah was so happy! She'd been learning to dance for a few weeks, and her mommy said it was time to pay. On their way to the dance class, Sarah and her mommy stopped at a café. Sarah saw a delicious looking cake in the window and said, "Mommy, can I have a piece of cake?" "No Sarah," said her mommy. "Maybe after your dance class, if you've been a good dancer!" Sarah couldn't wait to get to the dance class! When they arrived, she put on her special shoes and began to move. She twirled and leapt, and before long the dance teacher said, "Very good Sarah, now let's pay for the class." So Sarah and her mommy paid for the lesson and walked back to the café. The cake looked even more delicious than before, and Sarah's mommy said, "Yes, Sarah, you can have a piece." Sarah was so happy! The cake was almost as fun as dancing!

One day, Tom and Sue went to play outside. It was a hot day, and they saw a big puddle of mud. They wanted to jump in the mud and have fun. Tom said, "Let's count how many jumps we can do in the mud!" Tom and Sue started to jump in the mud. They counted one, two, three, four, five jumps. They laughed and played, getting all dirty. They were having so much fun in the hot sun. But then, something unexpected happened. While they were jumping, they found a big, shiny rock in the mud. They picked it up and looked at it. It was very pretty, and they had never seen anything like it before. They showed it to their mom, and she said it was a special rock. Tom and Sue were very happy that they found the rock while playing in the mud.

Lily and Max were playing baseball in the park. They liked to hit the ball with the bat and run to the bases. Lily was good at hitting, but Max was good at catching. "Watch out, Max, I'm going to hit a home run!" Lily said, as she swung the bat. The ball flew high in the air, over the fence, and into the street. Lily and Max ran after it, but they saw a big car coming fast. "Stop, Lily, stop!" Max shouted. "The car won't yield!" Lily stopped just in time, but the car did not. It hit the ball and made a loud noise. The ball bounced back into the park, but it was flat and dirty. "Oh no, our ball!" Lily cried. "It's ruined!" They heard a honk from the car. The driver rolled down the window and looked at them. He was a nosy man with a big nose and glasses. "Hey, you kids, what are you doing?" he asked. "Don't you know you can't play baseball in the street? You could have caused an accident!" Lily and Max felt scared and sorry. They did not mean to be bad. "We're sorry, sir," Max said. "We were just playing in the park, but the ball went over the fence." "Well, you should be more careful," the man said. "And you should get a new ball. This one is no good." He threw the ball back to them and drove away. Lily and Max picked up the ball and looked at it. It was sad and ugly. "Maybe we can get a new ball from the store," Lily said. "Maybe," Max said. "But we need to ask our moms first. And we need to be more careful next time." They nodded and held hands. They walked back to the park, hoping to play again.
