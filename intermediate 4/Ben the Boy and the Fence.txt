Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben the Boy and the Fence

One day, a boy named Ben went to the zoo with his mom and dad. He liked to see all the animals, but his favorite was the alligator. He ran to the fence and looked at the big green alligator in the water. "Wow, look at his teeth!" Ben said. "He has so many teeth!" "Yes, he does," his mom said. "But he is not hungry right now. He is just enjoying the sun." Ben wanted to see the alligator open his mouth. He thought it would be cool to see all his teeth. He waited and waited, but the alligator did not open his mouth. "Please, open your mouth, Mr. Alligator!" Ben said. "I want to see your teeth!" The alligator heard Ben and looked at him. He saw that Ben was not afraid of him. He liked that. He decided to show Ben his teeth. He opened his mouth wide and let out a loud roar. "Wow, thank you, Mr. Alligator!" Ben said. "You have the best teeth ever!" The alligator smiled and closed his mouth. He was happy that Ben enjoyed his teeth. He waved his tail and went back to the water. Ben clapped his hands and laughed. He gave his mom and dad a hug and said, "That was the best day ever! I love the alligator!"

Once upon a time, a little robot lived in a big box. The little robot was very persistent. Every day, the robot tried to peek out of the box to see the world. One day, the little robot peeked out and saw a big, bright ball in the sky. The robot was so happy! The robot wanted to go outside and play with the ball. The robot tried and tried to open the box, but it was hard. The persistent robot did not give up. It kept trying to open the box. Finally, the box opened! The little robot went outside and played all day under the big, bright ball. The robot was so happy it had peeked out of the box.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Amy. She had a pet cat named Fluffy. Amy loved her cat very much. Every day, she would feed Fluffy at noon. Fluffy loved Amy too. One sunny day, Amy and Fluffy went to the park. They saw a perfect tree to play near. Amy and Fluffy played hide and seek around the tree. They had lots of fun. After playing, Amy and Fluffy went home. They were tired but happy. Amy knew it was time to feed Fluffy. So, she gave Fluffy some food. Fluffy ate all the food and purred. They both had a perfect day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a rare toy car that he loved to play with. He would push the car around the house and make it go under the table, under the chair, and under his bed. One day, Tim could not find his rare toy car. He looked under the table, under the chair, and under his bed, but it was not there. Tim started to frown because he was sad and did not know where his car was. Tim's mom saw him frown and asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim told her about his missing rare toy car. His mom helped him look for it. They found the car under the sofa. Tim was so happy, and his frown turned into a big smile. He thanked his mom, and they played with the car together.

Once there were two friends, Jenny and Flo. They went to the gym, and the gym was wild with colors and shapes. Jenny and Flo couldn't believe it! Jenny's favorite thing to do was to print things. She even had her own printing box, with all kinds of colors and papers inside. Flo thought it was so much fun to see what Jenny could print. They printed flowers and birds and even a big rocket ship. Pretty soon, the wild gym was filled with the most beautiful printed things. Jenny and Flo were so proud of themselves!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a favorite cup. This cup was very stubborn. It would not let Sue drink from it. Sue tried to drink from the cup, but it would tip over and spill. One day, Sue's friend Tim came over to play. Tim saw Sue's stubborn cup and wanted to help. They tried to make the cup let Sue drink. They asked it nicely and said "please." But the cup still would not let Sue drink from it. Then, Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's thank the cup for being our friend." Sue and Tim both thanked the cup. And guess what? The cup was happy! It let Sue drink from it. From that day on, Sue's stubborn cup was not stubborn anymore. Sue and Tim learned that saying "thank you" can make a big difference.
