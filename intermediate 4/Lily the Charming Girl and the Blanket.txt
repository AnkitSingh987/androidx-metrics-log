Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Charming Girl and the Blanket

Once upon a time, there was a charming little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside and make friends with everyone. One day, she met a big, green monster in the woods. The monster was very sad. Lily asked the monster, "Why are you sad?" The monster said, "I'm sad because I'm always cold. I can't find anything to cover myself with." Lily thought for a moment and had an idea. She ran home and got her big, warm blanket. She brought it back to the monster and said, "Here, you can use my blanket to cover yourself and stay warm." The monster smiled and wrapped the blanket around himself. But then, something unexpected happened. The monster turned into a charming prince! He had been under a spell, and Lily's kindness broke the spell. They became best friends and played together every day.

One day, a big dog and a small cat had a fight. The dog was big and strong, but the cat was fast and smart. They were fighting near a house. The cat said, "Stop! I smell smoke!" The dog stopped and sniffed too. They saw smoke coming from the house. The dog and cat felt guilty for fighting and knew they had to help. They ran to the house and barked and meowed loudly to tell the people inside. The people came out and saw the smoke. They were scared but thankful to the dog and cat. The big dog and small cat became friends and never fought again. They learned that helping others is better than fighting.

---

One day, a boy named Tim found a lamp. The lamp was very pretty. Tim took the lamp home and showed it to his mom. "Look, Mom! I found a lamp!" Tim said. Mom looked at the lamp and smiled. "That's a nice lamp, Tim. But be careful, it looks old." Tim put the lamp in his room. He tried to turn it on, but it was hard. He pushed and pushed, and then something strange happened. A big, friendly man came out of the lamp! "Hi, Tim! I can drive you anywhere you want to go," said the friendly man. Tim was so happy. He wanted to go to the park, so the man drove him there. They had a lot of fun. The next day, Tim's friend, Jane, saw the lamp. She wanted it too. But Tim told her, "We should share the lamp and the friendly man." So, they both had fun with the lamp and the friendly man. They learned that sharing is good and makes everyone happy.

Herbie and his mommy were in the kitchen. Herbie was going to help make lunch. Mommy said, "Let’s get a tray and gather the ingredients. It will be easy." Herbie was excited to help. He followed Mommy to the pantry and got some vegetables. Then he counted the eggs. He was pleased when Mommy said he had counted them right! Next, they went to the kitchen table. Mommy said, "Let’s put the vegetables, eggs and other ingredients on the tray. We can mix it all together." Herbie got to put all the ingredients on the tray. After that, something funny happened. Mommy said, "Ok Herbie, let's see what we made for lunch!" Herbie was expecting something tasty. But when he looked at the tray, he was surprised! On the tray was a delicious and colorful salad. Mommy said, "I told you it would be easy!" Herbie had fun helping to make lunch. He couldn't wait for lunchtime to happen!

Lily liked to play with her box. She made it into a house for her dolls. She cut out windows and doors and drew flowers on the walls. She was very happy with her box. One day, her brother Tom came into her room. He saw her box and laughed. He said it was a stupid box and he wanted to play with it. He grabbed the box and tore it apart. He threw the pieces on the floor and ran away. Lily was very sad. She cried and cried. She loved her box and Tom ruined it. She did not have a house for her dolls anymore. Her mom heard her crying and came into her room. She saw the mess and hugged Lily. She said she was sorry that Tom was mean and broke her box. She said she would provide a new box for Lily and help her make a new house for her dolls. Lily smiled and thanked her mom. She was glad that her mom was nice and would provide a new box for her. She hoped that Tom would learn to be nice too and not break her things. She decided to forgive him and share her box with him if he asked nicely.
