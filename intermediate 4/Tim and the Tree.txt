Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Tree

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big tree with thick branches. Tim wanted to climb the tree, but his mom said, "Wait a minute, Tim! I need to tell you something." His mom told Tim a story about a little girl who climbed a tree without asking for help. The girl fell and got hurt. Tim listened and felt sad for the girl. He didn't want to get hurt too. Tim asked his mom for help to climb the tree. His mom held his hand and they climbed together. They had fun and stayed safe. Tim learned that it's important to ask for help and listen to others.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily went for a walk with her mom. They wanted to see something new and fun. They walked down the street and saw a big building. It was a museum. Lily had never been to a museum before. They went inside the museum and saw many beautiful things. There were big paintings with pretty colors. There were statues of animals and people. Lily liked the animal statues the best. Her favorite was a big, tall giraffe. Lily and her mom walked around the museum, looking at all the beautiful things. They had a fun day together. When it was time to go home, Lily couldn't wait to tell her friends about the museum and the big, tall giraffe.

Once upon a time, there was a funny fish named Tim. Tim was a tuna who loved to laugh. He lived in a big sea with his family and friends. Every day, they would swim and play together. One day, Tim met a big, terrible shark. The shark said, "I am very hungry. I want to eat you." Tim was scared, but he had an idea. He told the shark a funny joke. "Why did the fish cross the sea? To get to the other tide!" The shark laughed and laughed. He thought the joke was so funny that he forgot about being hungry. Tim swam back to his family and friends. He told them about the terrible shark and the funny joke. They all laughed together. From that day on, the tuna and the shark became friends. They learned that laughter is a great way to make friends, even with someone who seems scary at first.

One day, in the month of March, Tim and his pale dog, Spot, went to the park. They wanted to win a big race. The race was for kids and their pets. Tim and Spot were very excited. They had practiced running fast for many days. At the race, Tim said to Spot, "We can win this!" Spot barked and wagged his tail. They lined up with the other kids and pets. The race began and they all ran as fast as they could. Tim and Spot were doing great. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind blew a hat right in front of Spot. Spot stopped running and picked up the hat. Tim was sad they didn't win the race. But then, the person who lost the hat said, "Thank you for finding my hat!" Tim and Spot felt happy because they helped someone.

Once upon a time there was a boy. He was walking around with his mom and he saw a pear that was stuck in a tree. He was very excited to get it, so he asked his mom to help him rescue the pear. His mom got a ladder and put it against the tree, but it was too tall for him to reach the pear. He was so frustrated that he couldn't get it. Suddenly, a bird flew down and grabbed the pear with its beak. The bird flew away and the boy started to cry. Just then, his mom had an idea. She asked him to close his eyes and when he opened them, the bird was back with the pear! The bird had rescued the pear and brought it back to the boy. He was so happy that he hugged his mom and thanked the bird. That day, he learned that when you want something you can't get, it doesn't mean you can't get it at all.
