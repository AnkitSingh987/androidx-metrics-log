Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Fairy and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little fairy named Lily. Lily was a very ready fairy. She always helped her friends when they needed something. One day, her friend, the bunny, had no food to eat. Lily knew she had to supply food for her friend. Lily looked everywhere for food. She looked under the big tree, by the river, and in the tall grass. But she could not find any food for the bunny. Lily felt sad because she wanted to help her friend. Then, Lily had a great idea. She flew to the flower field and found some yummy carrots. She picked the carrots and brought them back to the bunny. The bunny was so happy to see the food that Lily supplied. They ate the carrots together and played all day long. From that day on, Lily and the bunny were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a big, big park, there was a little, little cat. The cat loved to nap under a big tree. One day, while napping, the cat had a dream. In the dream, the cat met a foolish dog. The foolish dog loved to zoom around the park. The cat thought it was funny to watch the dog zoom. The dog would run fast, then stop, then run fast again. The cat would giggle and giggle. When the cat woke up from the nap, the foolish dog was there! The cat and the dog became best friends. They played together every day. The cat would nap, and the dog would zoom. They were happy friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One day, he found a treasure map. The map had a big X on it. Tim was very happy. Tim took the map to his friend, Sally. Sally was good at reading maps. They looked at the map together. The map showed them where to find the treasure. They had to walk a long way. They walked until they saw a light. The light was coming from a big tree. They dug under the tree and found a treasure box. The box was full of shiny gold coins. Tim and Sally were so happy. They wanted to share their treasure with their friends. Tim and Sally put some coins in a box. They wrote a note to their friends. The note said, "We found treasure! Have some coins to share the fun!" They put the note in the box with the coins. Then, they went to the mail place. They gave the box to the mail person. The mail person sent the box to their friends. Tim and Sally could not wait for their friends to get the treasure. They knew their friends would be happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a unique bunny named Bobo. Bobo had a magic touch. When he touched things, they would appear or change color. One day, Bobo hopped to the park. He saw a sad boy named Timmy. Timmy said, "I lost my toy car." Bobo wanted to help. He touched the ground and a toy car appeared. Timmy was happy and said, "Thank you, Bobo!" Bobo and Timmy played all day. They touched things and made them appear or change color. They had a lot of fun. At the end of the day, they hugged and said goodbye. Timmy knew that Bobo was a special friend.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to play outside. She saw a big tree and wanted to discover what was behind it. Sue walked around the tree and found a small door. She opened the door and saw a deep hole. Sue was very brave and went down the hole. Sue found a secret chamber with many toys. She saw a teddy bear, a ball, and a toy car. Sue was very happy and said, "Wow, these toys are so nice! I want to play with them all day." Sue played with the toys for a long time. Then, she heard her mom call her name. Sue knew it was time to go home. She said goodbye to the chamber and climbed out of the hole. Sue went back to her house and told her mom about the secret chamber. Her mom smiled and said, "That sounds like a fun adventure, Sue. Maybe we can play there together tomorrow."
