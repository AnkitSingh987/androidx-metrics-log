Category: Intermediate
Title: Dora and the Mommy

Dora was playing in the sand at the beach when she saw something huge swimming in the water. She ran to get her mommy and pointed at the water. "Look, mommy! It's a big huge shark!" Mommy gasped and told Dora not to go too close. She said sharks could be dangerous. Dora wanted to get closer, but she did not forget what her mommy said. She stayed on the beach and watched the huge shark swim around in the ocean. Suddenly, Dora realized she had forgotten her beach bucket. She ran back to the spot where she was playing earlier, but it was gone. "Don't worry, honey," said mommy. "Let's get you a new one, and next time we'll remember to bring it with us." Dora was so happy she hugged mommy and they went to buy a new beach bucket.

Lily and Tom are playing in the kitchen. They have some cups and spoons and pretend to make tea. Lily fills a glass with water and puts it on the table. "Tom, do you want some tea?" she asks. "Yes, please," Tom says. He picks up the glass and pretends to drink. "Listen, Tom," Lily says. "Do you hear that?" Tom stops drinking and listens. He hears a funny sound from the glass. It sounds like a whistle. "What is that?" Tom asks. Lily laughs. "It is the air in the glass. When you drink, you make a hole in the water. The air goes in and out and makes a noise." Tom tries again. He drinks and listens. He hears the whistle again. He thinks it is silly and funny. He laughs too. "Let me try," Lily says. She takes the glass and drinks. She listens. She hears the whistle. They take turns drinking and listening. They make different noises with the glass. They giggle and have fun. Mum comes in and sees them. She smiles. "What are you doing?" she asks. "We are making tea and music," Lily says. "We are using the glass and the air," Tom says. Mum thinks they are very clever and creative. She joins them and drinks and listens. They all make silly noises and laugh together.

One day, a curious little boy named Tim went for a walk. He wanted to find something fun. As he walked, he saw a big shop. The shop had a sign that said "Haircut". Tim thought that getting a haircut would be fun. He went inside the shop and asked the man, "Can I get a haircut?" The man said, "Yes, sit down and I will cut your hair." Tim was excited. He had never had a haircut before. The man started to cut Tim's hair. But Tim did not like it. It felt strange and he started to cry. The man did not stop cutting. When it was done, Tim looked in the mirror and saw that his hair was all gone. He was very sad and went home crying.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a troubled boy because his toy was broken. He wanted to fix it, but he did not know how. One day, Tim found a small screw on the floor. He thought it could help fix his toy. He put the screw in the toy and turned it. The toy was fixed! Tim was very happy. That night, Tim went to bed and prayed. He said, "Thank you for helping me fix my toy." Tim was not troubled anymore, and he slept well. From that day on, Tim always prayed before going to bed.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends who lived in a house together. One morning, they decided they wanted to do something fun. The friends went to the bedroom and made a plan. They would spin around in circles until they got dizzy. The first friend said, "Let's go!" Then they spun around and around. When they stopped, the friends were feeling very dizzy. They laughed and had a happy time! Suddenly, the bedroom became alive with colours. There were balls of every colour spinning around the room. The friends were amazed and had so much fun. When the colours disappeared, the friends were tired from all the spinning and decided to take a nap. They lay down in bed and fell sound asleep. The friends spent the afternoon playing in the bedroom and had a wonderful time. They were very happy when their fun became a wonderful memory.
