Title: Amy the Girl and the Vegetables

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to eat vegetables. Her favorite vegetable was a carrot. One day, Amy's mom had a surprise for her. Amy's mom said, "Amy, close your eyes. I have a surprise for you." Amy closed her eyes and waited. When she opened her eyes, she saw a big box of vegetables. There were carrots, peas, and even tomatoes. Amy was very happy. Amy played with the vegetables. She made a tower with the carrots. But then, she fell down and hurt her knee. "Ouch!" Amy cried. Her mom came and gave her a hug. "Be careful, Amy," her mom said. "I will," said Amy. She went back to playing with her vegetables, being more careful this time. And they all lived happily ever after.

One morning, a little girl named Sue went to the park. She saw a big sign that said, "Important! Do not go on the grass." Sue was sad because she wanted to play on the grass. Sue saw a boy named Tim. Tim said, "I want to play on the grass too, but the sign says we can't." Sue thought for a moment and said, "Let's ask the park man if we can play on the grass." The park man told Sue and Tim, "You can play on the grass today because we just put the sign up for a big surprise party tomorrow." Sue and Tim were very happy and played on the grass all morning. The next day, Sue and Tim went back to the park and saw the surprise party. They were happy they listened to the sign and asked the park man. The moral of the story is to always follow the rules and ask when you are not sure.

Once upon a time, there was a big raft. It was very dry. The raft lived by the river. One day, the raft wanted to help and serve. It wanted to make friends. A small duck came to the river. The duck was sad. It could not swim. The raft wanted to help the duck. The raft said, "I can serve you. I can take you across the river." The duck was happy. The duck got on the raft. As the raft and the duck went across the river, they saw a big fish. The big fish was stuck. The raft and the duck helped the big fish. They all became friends. The duck learned to swim. The raft was happy to serve and help. The moral of the story is: be kind and serve others, and you will make friends.

Once upon a time, there was a brown mule. He lived on a farm with other animals. The brown mule loved to play with his friends, the cows and the sheep. One day, the brown mule saw a big stage. He wanted to be on the stage and make people happy. So, the brown mule practiced a dance to show everyone. He danced and danced, and soon he was very good at it. The day came when the brown mule went on the stage. He started to dance, and everyone was amazed. They did not know a mule could dance like that! Then, something unexpected happened. The brown mule did a flip in the air! Everyone was so surprised and happy. They all clapped for the brown mule. The brown mule felt proud and knew he made everyone happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a patient cat named Tom. He loved to play with his toy model car. One day, while Tom was playing outside, his model car rolled into the street. A little bird named Sally saw the car and said, "Tom, your car is in the street! Be careful!" Tom looked and saw a big truck coming. He knew he had to save his model car. Tom ran fast and grabbed the car just in time. The truck passed by, and Tom was safe. Sally said, "Good job, Tom! You saved your car!" Tom smiled and thanked Sally for her help. They played together with the model car for the rest of the day.
