Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Robot

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big, modern robot. The robot had a big head. Tim said, "Hi, robot!" The robot said, "Hi, Tim! I need help." Tim asked, "What do you need?" The robot said, "I need a new head. My head is too big." Tim thought for a moment. He said, "I have an idea! Let's go to my house." At Tim's house, they found a small box. Tim put the box on the robot's head. Now, the robot had a small head. The robot was happy. Tim said, "You look great!" But then, something unexpected happened. The robot's big head started to talk! It said, "I am the real robot head! The small box is a trick!" Tim was surprised. He took off the small box and put the big head back on the robot. The robot said, "Thank you, Tim! I am a whole robot again." Tim and the robot played and had fun. They were happy to be friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had black fur and loved to play outside. One day, Spot went to the park with his best friend, a little girl named Sue. At the park, Spot saw a big, terrible cat. The cat was mean and always made Spot complain. Today, the cat took Sue's ball and ran away. Spot and Sue were very sad. Spot had an idea. He put on a black hat to look like the terrible cat. He went to the cat's house and took back Sue's ball. The cat did not know it was Spot and did not complain. Spot and Sue were happy and played with the ball all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a big, bald man named Tom. Tom had a big clock in his house. Tom was scared of the clock because it made a loud sound. One day, Tom's friend, Sam, came to visit. "Tom, why are you scared of the clock?" Sam asked. "I don't like the loud sound it makes," Tom said. Sam had an idea. He took the clock and put it in a box. Then, he put the box in the closet. Now, the clock's sound was not so loud. Tom was happy and not scared anymore. But then, something unexpected happened. The clock stopped making any sound at all. Tom and Sam opened the closet and saw that the clock was broken. They were sad because the clock was very special to Tom. The moral of the story is that sometimes, we need to face our fears instead of hiding from them. If Tom had not been scared of the clock, it would not have broken, and he would still have his special clock.

Once there was a little girl who loved adventure. She lived by a creek and day after day, she would go exploring. One day, she noticed something serious had happened - her favourite stick had vanished. She looked all around but couldn't seem to find it anywhere. She began to walk along the creek shore. Suddenly, there it was! A smiling fish was swimming around her stick with glee. She let out a happy cry and reached in to grab it. But it was too late. The fish was too fast and the stick soon vanished upstream. All she could do was watch as it floated away with the fish. She was disappointed, but all she could do was go back home, until the next adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a fancy gate. The gate was in a big yard. The yard had many pretty flowers. A girl named Lily lived in the house with the yard. She liked to play outside and look at the gate. One day, Lily saw a cat near the gate. The cat was sad. It had a long tail that was stuck in the gate. Lily wanted to help the cat. She went inside to find something to help the cat. She found some scissors to cut the tail free. Lily cut the cat's tail and the cat was happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The cat turned into a beautiful fairy! The fairy said, "Thank you, Lily, for helping me. I was stuck as a cat until someone kind helped me." Then, the fairy waved her wand and made the fancy gate even more beautiful. Lily and the fairy became best friends and played in the yard every day.
