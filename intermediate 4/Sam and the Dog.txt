Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam and the Dog

Sam liked to throw balls. He had many balls of different colors and sizes. He threw them in the air, on the ground, and at the wall. But his favorite thing was to throw them over the fence. The fence was tall and brown. It separated Sam's yard from the neighbor's yard. Sam did not know the neighbor. He only saw a big dog sometimes. The dog barked loud and ran fast. Sam was scared of the dog. One day, Sam found a new ball. It was unique. It was shiny and had many dots. Sam wanted to throw it over the fence. He thought it would be fun to see it fly. He threw it hard and high. But he threw it too hard and too high. The ball went over the fence and over the neighbor's house. Sam did not see where it landed. Sam was sad. He lost his unique ball. He wanted it back. He ran to the fence and looked over. He saw the big dog. The dog had the ball in its mouth. The dog looked at Sam and wagged its tail. Sam was surprised. The dog was not angry. The dog was happy. Sam smiled. He made a new friend. He called the dog. The dog came to the fence and dropped the ball. Sam reached over and took the ball. He thanked the dog and petted its head. The dog licked his hand. Sam threw the ball again. The dog caught it and brought it back. They played together for a long time. Sam was happy. He found his unique ball and a unique friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big, dark forest. In the forest, there were many animals. They were all friends. One day, they found a big squash. The squash was very big and very good to eat. The animals were very happy. The animals wanted to unite and share the squash. They wanted to have a big party. They all came together to eat the squash. The sun was going down, and it was getting darker. But the dark forest had a big, bad wolf. The wolf was hungry and saw the animals having a party. The wolf wanted the squash too. The wolf came to the party and scared all the animals away. The wolf ate the whole squash by himself. The animals were sad and did not have a good time.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to study shapes. She had a book with many shapes in it, like circles, squares, and triangles. Every day, Amy would look at her book and learn about the shapes. One sunny day, Amy went to the park with her book. She was looking at the shapes when she saw a huge shadow. Amy looked up and saw a big, friendly giant. The giant was holding a bag full of shapes. He saw Amy and smiled. The giant said, "I love shapes too! I have a surprise for you." He opened his bag and out came many shapes, but they were not normal. The circle was a square, the square was a triangle, and the triangle was a circle! Amy and the giant laughed and played with the funny shapes all day long.

Mum and Dad had an very important errand to do. "Let's go and buy the board," Mum said. Dad said, "Are you sure, it's very ugly." Mum said, "Yes, I'm sure. It's necessary." So they drove off to buy the board. When they arrived and saw the board they couldn't believe how ugly it was. Dad said, "That's it? It's terrible!" Mum said, "Yes, but it was the only one they had and it's necessary." So they bought the board and brought it home. It was so ugly! But when they put it together and saw what it was for, they couldn't help but smile. They had bought a special game board for their toddler, and it was perfect.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named May. She was very creative and loved exploring. One day she stumbled upon a roll of clear tape. She was so excited because she could use it to make something special. May held the tape in her hand and spun around, trying to think of what she could make. Suddenly, she had an idea. She quickly cut a piece of the tape with her scissors and then stuck it on a nearby bush. It made a funny shape. May looked around and found more things to attach the tape to. She stuck it to her shoes, her pet cat and even a tree! Everywhere May went she was leaving her creative mark with the clear tape. Finally, after a long day of stumbling and playing, May had used all of the tape. She smiled, feeling very proud of all the creative things she had made.
