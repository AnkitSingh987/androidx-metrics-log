Category: Intermediate
Title: The Man and His Wife and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a man and his wife. They lived in a small house near the park. The man was very selfish. He did not like to share anything with his wife. He always wanted to keep the best things for himself. One day, the man found a big, shiny coin on the ground. He was very happy and wanted to keep it all for himself. But his wife asked him to share the coin with her. The man did not want to share it, but his wife was very kind and said, "Please, let's share the coin. It will make both of us happy." The man thought about it and decided to share the coin with his wife. They went to the store and bought a big ice cream to share. Both of them were very happy. The man thanked his wife for teaching him to share. From that day on, the man learned to be less selfish. He started sharing everything with his wife and other people too. Everyone was very happy, and the man felt good inside. The moral of the story is that sharing makes everyone happy, and it is better to be kind than to be selfish.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a big tree with many lemons. The tree was very tall and impressive. Lily wanted to pick a lemon, but the lemons were too high up. Lily saw her friend, Tim, at the park. She said, "Tim, can you help me get a lemon from the tree?" Tim was taller than Lily, so he tried to reach for a lemon. But the lemons were still too high. Then, a nice man named Mr. Brown came to the park. Mr. Brown was very tall. He saw Lily and Tim trying to get a lemon. Mr. Brown said, "Do you need help?" Lily and Tim said, "Yes, please!" Mr. Brown reached up and picked a lemon for them. Lily and Tim said, "Thank you, Mr. Brown!" They were very happy to have a lemon.

Once upon a time, a unique cat named Tom found a guitar. Tom loved to play the guitar every day. The guitar was very special to him. One day, it rained a lot and the guitar got wet. Tom was sad. Tom's friend, a dog named Max, saw Tom's sad face. Max had an idea to make Tom happy. Max said, "Let's dry the guitar in the sun!" They put the guitar under the sun and waited for it to dry. The next day, the sun came up and the guitar was dry. Tom was very happy and played the guitar again. Max and Tom danced and sang together. They had a great time. From that day on, they always played the guitar and had fun together.

Once upon a time there was a little boy named Jake. Jake really liked carpets. He decided he wanted to have a carpet for his room. So Jake and his mom went to the store to pick out the perfect carpet. They looked at all the carpets and finally found one that was so soft and colorful. Jake asked his mom to buy it. His mom smiled and agreed. She bought the carpet and they took it home. Jake was so excited when they got home. He couldn't wait to put the carpet down in his room. He helped his mom to sack up the carpet and arrange it on the floor. It was a delicious new addition to Jake's room. Now Jake had the softest, most colorful carpet in his room. He was so proud of it, and it was even more comfortable to play on. Jake had a lot of fun playing on the carpet and he enjoyed it every day. And they all lived happily ever after!

Once upon a time, there was a cute little cat. She liked to talk a lot. One day, she went for a walk in the park. In the park, she met a nurse. The nurse was kind. The cat wanted to talk to her. But, the nurse did not talk back. The cat was sad. Then, something unexpected happened. The nurse turned into a big bird! The bird could talk. The cat and the bird talked and played all day. They became best friends.
