Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Smart Bird and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a smart little bird named Tim. Tim lived by the shore with his family. They loved to play in the water and eat tasty bugs. One day, Tim saw a big fish in the water. The fish said, "Hello, Tim! Do you want to follow me and see something fun?" Tim was curious and said, "Yes, I want to follow you!" So, Tim followed the big fish. They swam in the water and saw many pretty things. They saw colorful shells, tiny crabs, and other birds playing. Tim was happy he followed the big fish, and they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy was very excited because it was a holiday. She could not go to school, and she could play all day. She wanted to show her friends something interesting. Amy went outside and saw her friends, Tom and Sue. They were playing in the park. "I have something interesting to show you," Amy said. Tom and Sue looked at her and smiled. They were excited to see what Amy had to show them. Amy took Tom and Sue to her house. In her room, she showed them a big colorful box. Inside the box, there were many toys and games. Tom and Sue were very happy to see the interesting box. They all played with the toys and games, and they had a great holiday together.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a nice hunter. The nice hunter liked to help animals. One day, he saw a small, lost bird. The nice hunter knew that the bird needed help, so he took the bird home with him. The next day, the nice hunter went back to the forest. He saw a big tree that had fallen down. He knew he needed help to move the tree, but he was all alone. He thought about the small bird he had helped and wished it could help him now. Just then, the small bird came back with many other birds. They all wanted to help the nice hunter. Together, they all lifted the big tree and moved it out of the way. The nice hunter was very happy and thanked the small bird and its friends. From that day on, the nice hunter and the small bird became the best of friends, always helping each other when they needed it.

Anna and Ben like to play in the park. They like to swing on the big swings. They push each other and go high in the air. They feel happy and free. One day, they see a new swing in the park. It is different from the other swings. It has a long rope and a big hook at the end. It looks like fun. They want to try it. Anna goes first. She holds the hook with both hands and runs. She lets go of the hook and flies in the air. She swings back and forth. She laughs and shouts. She likes the new swing. Ben waits for his turn. He is ready to swing. He grabs the hook and runs. He lets go of the hook and flies in the air. He swings back and forth. He smiles and cheers. He likes the new swing too. Anna and Ben take turns on the new swing. They have a lot of fun. They swing until it is time to go home. They say goodbye to the new swing. They hope to come back soon. They love to swing.

Once upon a time, there was a woman who lived in a small house. She liked to wear fancy clothes. Every day, she would put on her fancy clothes and go to the park. One day, she met a little dog in the park. The dog could talk! The dog said, "Hello, fancy woman!" The woman was very surprised. She had never met a talking dog before. She said, "Hello, little dog!" The woman and the dog became good friends. They played in the park every day. The dog would say funny things and make the woman laugh. They were very happy together. And they lived happily ever after.
