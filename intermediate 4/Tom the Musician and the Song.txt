Title: Tom the Musician and the Song

Once upon a time, there was a musician named Tom. Tom liked to play songs on his guitar. He played every day and his songs made people happy. One day, Tom wanted to write a new song. But it was very dry outside and the sun was too bright. Tom was too hot to think of a fun song. So, he sat under a big tree to find some shade. Under the tree, Tom felt better. He started to write a happy song about the sun and the dry day. When he played his new song, everyone danced and laughed. Tom's music made the dry day more fun for everyone.

Lily liked to dream. She dreamed of many things. She dreamed of flying in the sky. She dreamed of swimming in the sea. She dreamed of having a furry friend. One day, she saw a stamp in her mom's drawer. It had a picture of a cat on it. Lily loved cats. She wanted to have a cat. She took the stamp and put it in her pocket. She went to her room and put the stamp on her pillow. She hoped that the stamp would turn into a real cat. She closed her eyes and dreamed of cuddling with the cat. But when she opened her eyes, the stamp was still a stamp. She felt sad and angry. She threw the stamp on the floor and stomped on it. "Ow!" a voice said. Lily looked down and saw a small, furry cat. It had the same markings as the stamp. It looked at her with big, green eyes. "Who are you?" Lily asked. "I'm Stampy. I was a stamp, but you made me alive with your dream. Thank you, Lily. Can I be your friend?" the cat said. Lily was surprised and happy. She picked up the cat and hugged it. She felt its soft fur and heard its purr. "Yes, you can be my friend. I'm sorry I hurt you. I didn't know you were real. I love you, Stampy." Lily said. "I love you too, Lily. You are my dream come true." Stampy said. They snuggled on the pillow and dreamed of more adventures together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Sammy who was three years old. Sammy was very eager to go explore with his dad. His dad had a map and he said to Sammy, “Let’s go explore!” Sammy was so excited, but his dad said that he had to be careful. “It’s going to be a long and rough journey”, his dad said. Sammy got a bit scared and began to worry. His dad talked to Sammy and said, “Don’t worry. You can do anything if you try your best. Believe in yourself and nothing will stop you from reaching your destination.” Sammy nodded and smiled. Sammy listened to his dad and followed the map. Eventually, Sammy and his dad were able to make it to the end of their journey. The moral of this story is if you believe in yourself and work hard, you can accomplish anything!

Once upon a time, there was a leopard who lived in a jungle. He had a big family and he was very brave. One day, the leopard heard a loud noise and he saw danger coming. He knew that he had to stay safe, so he quickly rose up into the trees. The leopard watched from his place in the trees, as the dangerous thing came closer. He was scared and he knew he had to stay hidden, so he didn't move. When the danger was gone, the leopard slowly rose down from the trees and looked all around. He was relieved that it was safe again. The leopard taught his family to rise up into the trees, when danger is near. In this way, the whole family stayed safe and happy. The end.

Once upon a time there were two sisters. Their names were Jane and Emma. Jane always had the best clothes. Today, was no exception. She showed off her new dress to Emma. Emma wanted one for herself too but Jane said no. "That's not fair," said Emma. "Why do you get to have everything?" "Because I'm older and I want them," said Jane. Emma felt sad. She thought Jane was being selfish. Jane went outside to play and Emma went to her room to get her old dress. Suddenly, she heard a knock on the door. It was Jane. She had come back with a dress for Emma. "I'm sorry I was selfish," said Jane. "I brought you this so you can show off too!" Emma smiled. She hugged Jane and they both went out to play in their new clothes.
