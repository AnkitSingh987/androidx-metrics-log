Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Clever Dog and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a clever little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball and sit on his small chair. Every day, he would play and sit, and he was very happy. One day, while Spot was playing with his ball, it rolled far away. Spot ran and ran to get it back. He saw a big tree and thought his ball was hiding there. He looked up, and there, in the tree, was a big, strange bird. The bird said, "I'll give you your ball if you yield your chair to me." Spot was surprised but wanted his ball back, so he agreed. Spot took the bird to his chair and let him sit on it. The bird was so happy that he started to sing. When the bird sang, something unexpected happened. The chair began to grow bigger and bigger! The bird was not just any bird; it was a magic bird. Spot and the bird became good friends, and they played together every day. And now, they both could sit on the big chair, and they were even happier than before.

Once upon a time there was a small girl. She was three years old and very curious. She was always looking around and exploring. One day she saw a path in the woods and wanted to see what was at the end of it. She thought to herself, "Maybe this path will have something wonderful at the end of it." The girl walked down the path and soon she came to a big door. She tried to open it but it was locked. She looked around and saw a key. She quickly grabbed it, excited to unlock the door. Suddenly, she heard a friendly voice. It said, "You can do it if you try! Don't worry, I believe in you!" She smiled and looked around. She saw a helpful rabbit hopping around the path. The rabbit said, "Here, let me help you open the door!" The girl and the rabbit worked together to unlock the door. When it opened, the girl was delighted to find a wonderful surprise. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little flea. This flea loved to jump and play all day long. He had a long way to go to find his friends. His friends were a cat, a dog, and a bird. One sunny day, the flea went to visit his friends. They all sat under a big tree. They wanted to play a fun game. The bird had an idea. "Let's have a singing contest!" said the bird. The flea, the cat, and the dog all agreed. The cat sang first. She sang a soft song. The dog sang next. He sang a loud and happy song. Then the bird sang a sweet song. Finally, it was the flea's turn. The flea sang a very tiny song, but it was the most beautiful song of all. All the friends clapped and cheered for the flea. They had a great day singing and playing together.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a compassionate man named Tom. Tom was a plumber who fixed pipes. He loved to help everyone in the village. One day, a lady named Sue came to Tom's house. She said, "Tom, please help! My pipe is broken and water is everywhere!" Tom wanted to help Sue, so he grabbed his tools and went to her house. At Sue's house, Tom saw the broken pipe. He worked hard to fix it. Sue said, "Thank you, Tom! You bring happiness to our village." Tom smiled and went back to his house, feeling happy that he could help.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. He had a big task to do. Spot wanted to invite all his friends to a fun party. He was very excited. Spot was persistent. He went to each friend's house to invite them. He invited a cat, a bird, and a squirrel. They all said "yes" and were happy to come to the party. On the day of the party, all the friends played games and ate yummy treats. They had a lot of fun. Spot was happy that he did his task well. His friends were happy too, and they all had a great time together.
