Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Jolly Girl and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a jolly girl named Sue. Sue loved to share her toys with her friends. She had a favorite toy, a mixer, that she would use to mix pretend food for her friends. One sunny day, Sue and her friends were playing in the park. They were all taking turns using the mixer to mix pretend cake. Sue was happy to share her mixer with her friends because it made everyone happy. Then, something unexpected happened. A big dog came running into the park and took the mixer in his mouth. Sue and her friends were scared, but the dog was just playing. The dog ran back to them, dropped the mixer, and wagged his tail. Sue and her friends laughed and played with the dog. They even let the dog join their game and pretended to mix food for him. They all had a fun day sharing and playing together.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Mark. He loved to build with his toy blocks. One day, he wanted to build a big tower. He thought it would be easy. But, his little sister wanted to play too. She tried to help Mark build the tower. But she was too little, and the blocks fell down. Mark was sad and mad. Mark and his sister tried again. They worked together to build the tower. This time, it was not easy. But, they did it! They were happy and hugged each other.

Once upon a time, there was a big university. It was a place where big kids went to learn and play. The university wanted to improve and become better. They needed something new and fun for everyone. One day, a very creative girl named Lucy came to the university. She had many ideas to make the university better. She thought of new games, new songs, and new ways to learn. Everyone loved her ideas and wanted to try them. With Lucy's help, the university improved a lot. The big kids were happy and had fun with the new games and songs. Lucy felt proud of her creative ideas. The university became the best place for big kids to learn and play. And they lived happily ever after.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big green slide. Anna wants to go on the slide, but Ben is scared. "Come on, Ben, let's go on the slide. It looks fun!" Anna says. "No, Anna, it is too high and too fast. I don't want to go on the slide." Ben says. "Are you a baby, Ben? I dare you to go on the slide with me. It is not hard, it is easy. You just have to sit and slide." Anna says. Anna runs to the slide and climbs up the ladder. She sits on the top and waits for Ben. Ben looks at the slide and feels nervous. He does not want to let Anna down, but he also does not want to be scared. "Ben, Ben, come on, don't be a chicken. It is just a slide, not a monster. You can do it, Ben. You are brave, Ben." Anna says. Ben takes a deep breath and walks to the slide. He climbs up the ladder slowly. He reaches the top and sees Anna smiling at him. He sits next to her and holds her hand. "Are you ready, Ben?" Anna asks. "Yes, Anna, I am ready." Ben says. They slide down together, feeling the wind and the sun. They laugh and scream and have fun. They reach the bottom and hug each other. "Wow, Ben, that was awesome! You did it, Ben, you did it! You are not a baby, you are a hero!" Anna says. "Thank you, Anna, for daring me to go on the slide. It was not so bad, it was fun. You are a good friend, Anna, and a good teacher." Ben says. They run to the slide again and again, enjoying their work and their play. They are happy and proud. They are friends.

Once upon a time, there was a smart little dog named Max. Max loved to watch the sun go up and down every day. It made him feel happy and warm. One hot day, Max saw a little boy named Tim. Tim was very sad. Max walked up to Tim and asked, "Why are you sad?" Tim looked at Max and said, "I lost my ball." Max wanted to help Tim find his ball. They looked under a big tree and behind a small bush but they could not find the ball. Finally, Max saw the ball near a pond. He ran to the pond and picked up the ball with his mouth. Tim was so happy to see the ball again. He hugged Max and said, "Thank you, Max! You are a very smart dog!" From that day on, Max and Tim became best friends. They played together every day and watched the sun go up and down. And they always felt the warm heat of the sun on their faces.
