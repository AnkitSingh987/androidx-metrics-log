Category: Intermediate
Title: The Jolly Man Sat and the Cat

One day, a jolly man sat in a big armchair. He was very happy. He liked to bow to everyone he saw. He had a big hat and a big smile. The jolly man saw a little cat. He said, "Hello, little cat! Do you want to play with me?" The little cat looked at the man and did not say anything. The jolly man bowed to the cat and the cat bowed back. Then, something unexpected happened. The little cat turned into a big, talking cat! The big cat said, "Thank you, jolly man! Your bow made me big and now I can talk!" The jolly man was very surprised but happy. They became friends and played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a fair little girl named Lily. She had a big cat named Sam. Lily and Sam were best friends. They liked to play all day. One Wednesday, Lily and Sam went to the park. They played in the mud and got very dirty. Their hands and paws were full of mud. They laughed and had lots of fun. When they got home, Lily's mom saw how dirty they were. She said, "You need to wash your hands and paws!" Lily and Sam went to the bathroom. They washed their hands and paws with soap and water. Now they were clean and ready to play again.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim lived in a comfortable nest in a big tree. Every day, he would fly around and watch the other animals play. One day, Tim saw a fire in the forest. All the animals were running away, but Tim saw a small bug stuck on a leaf near the fire. The fire was getting closer and closer to the bug. Tim knew he had to help. Tim flew down and picked up the bug just in time. They flew away from the fire and watched as the trees turned to ash. The bug thanked Tim for saving him. From that day on, all the animals knew that it was important to help others in need.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a little rabbit named Tim. Tim was a troubled rabbit. He always felt scared and worried. One day, Tim wanted to find power to be brave. Tim started to run in the forest to look for power. He ran and ran. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. As Tim ran, he saw a big tree with a hole in it. "Maybe the power is in there," he thought. Tim went inside the hole and found a little box. He opened the box and found a shiny stone. The stone was the power he was looking for! Tim felt brave and strong. He ran back home, happy and proud. From that day on, Tim was never troubled again.

Once upon a time, there was a deaf kangaroo named Kiki. Kiki could not hear, but she loved to hop and play with her friends in the big field. One sunny day, Kiki and her friends played a game. They spread out in the field and took turns hopping to each other. Kiki was very good at this game, even though she could not hear her friends call her name. In the end, Kiki won the game and all her friends clapped and cheered for her. They knew Kiki was special, and they loved her very much. Kiki was happy to have such good friends, and they played together every day.

One day, a boy went to the park. He saw a cool tree with big branches. The boy wanted to climb the tree. As he was climbing, he met a bird. "Hi, bird!" said the boy. "Do you like this tree?" The bird said, "Yes! I live here. Do you want to see my home?" The bird offered to show the boy its nest. The boy was excited and climbed higher. When they got to the nest, the boy saw something unexpected. Inside the nest, there were shiny rocks and pretty things. The bird said, "I like to collect these. Do you like them too?" The boy was happy and said, "Yes! I love them. Thank you for sharing!" They became good friends and played in the tree all day.
