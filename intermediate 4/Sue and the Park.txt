Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue and the Park

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park with her mom. She saw a big animal. It had long ears and a fluffy tail. Sue wanted to touch it, but she was scared. She asked her mom, "Can I touch the animal? I feel scared." Her mom said, "It's okay, Sue. The animal is nice. Go ahead and touch it." Sue walked closer to the animal. She reached out her hand and touched the animal's fur. "Wow, it feels fake!" Sue said, surprised. Just then, a boy came running from behind a tree. He laughed and said, "That's because it is fake! It's my toy animal. I put it here to play a joke on people." Sue and her mom laughed too. They all had fun playing with the fake animal at the park.

One day, a big ball went bounce, bounce, bounce in the rain. It was an impressive ball. It was red and shiny. The rain made the ball wet and slippery. A little girl named Amy saw the ball. She wanted to play with it. Amy ran to the ball and said, "Ball, let's play!" The ball did not say anything, but it was happy to play with Amy. They played in the rain together. Amy would throw the big ball and it would bounce, bounce, bounce. The rain made the ball go even higher. Amy laughed and said, "I like playing with you, ball!" They played until the rain stopped and they were both very happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big wardrobe in his room. He was confused because it was not there before. He opened the door and saw many pretty clothes inside. Tim heard a voice say, "Hello! I am Mr. Wardrobe. Do you want to play a game?" Tim was happy and said, "Yes, I want to play!" Mr. Wardrobe told Tim to find a toy gun and shoot at the clothes. Tim found a toy gun and started to shoot at the clothes. "Bang! Bang!" went the gun. The clothes started to dance and sing. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. He played with Mr. Wardrobe until it was time for bed. Before Tim went to sleep, he thanked Mr. Wardrobe for the fun game. Mr. Wardrobe smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim. I will be here if you want to play again." Tim went to bed with a big smile on his face, excited to play with his new friend again.

Once upon a time, there was a squirrel named Sam. Sam loved to find nuts to eat. One day, it was very cold outside. Sam put on his warm hat and went out to look for nuts. As Sam walked, he could hear the wind blowing through the trees. He looked up and saw a big nut in a tree. Sam was so happy! He climbed the tree and grabbed the nut with his little hands. Sam took the nut home and ate it. It was the best nut he ever had! The cold day didn't bother Sam anymore because he had a full belly and a warm hat. And from that day on, Sam knew that even on cold days, he could find tasty nuts if he listened to the wind.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful girl named Lily. She loved to wear her favorite skirt. It was a pretty skirt with lots of colors. Lily enjoyed twirling in her skirt and watching the colors spin around. One sunny day, Lily went to the park with her mom. She wore her favorite skirt and played on the swings. Lily enjoyed going high up in the sky. She felt like she could touch the clouds. After playing on the swings, Lily saw a group of kids playing with a ball. They asked her to join them. Lily was very happy to play with her new friends. They all had fun and enjoyed the day together. At the end of the day, Lily went home with her mom, feeling cheerful and excited to play with her new friends again.

Jimmy was so excited to wake up today because he wanted to play outside in the garden. He looked out the window and saw that it was raining. So he asked his mum if they could go play in the garden and his mum said yes. In the garden there was a lot of junk. There were so many things to explore, like an old box and some broken toys. Despite the junk, it was still a very peaceful place. The rain made everything look shiny and sparkly. Jimmy had so much fun in the garden, jumping in the puddles and catching raindrops in his bucket. He was so happy that he wanted to stay in the garden all day. But the rain was still coming down and his mum said it was time to go back inside. Jimmy went upstairs feeling so content. His mind was full of all the happy memories he had made that day, playing in the garden in the peaceful rain.
