Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Grass

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a dove on the grass. The dove was white and pretty. Tim wanted to play with the dove, so he began to rush towards it. As Tim rushed, the dove got scared and flew away. Tim was very sorry. He didn't want to scare the dove. He just wanted to be friends. Tim looked around and saw the dove sitting on a tree branch. He said, "I'm sorry, dove. I didn't mean to scare you. Can we be friends?" The dove looked at Tim and flew down to the grass near him. Tim walked slowly to the dove, and they became friends. They played in the park all day, and Tim promised never to rush at the dove again.

Once upon a time, two little friends were playing together. They were called Lily and Jack. Lily really wanted to organize something fun. She said to Jack, "Let's organize a game! It'll be so fun!" Jack was very excited and said, "Yes! What should we do?" Lily thought for a moment and then said, "Let's play tag! It'll be super duper fun!" So that's what they did. They played tag all around the park. Jack was so fast. Lily could only catch him a few times. After a while, they were both out of breath and ready for something different. Lily said, "I know! Let's have a picnic today. That will be funny!" Jack nodded and said, "Yes, let's do it! We can get all the food we need from the store and sit under the tree together!" So Lily and Jack went to the store, got all the food they needed, and sat in the park under the tree. They ate everything up and had lots of fun. The two friends had a really wonderful day organizing a game and having a funny picnic. They both knew they would remember today, forever.

One day, a boy named Tim had a toy phone. He loved his phone very much. It was a flexible phone, so he could bend it and play with it all day. Tim was very happy with his phone. Tim had a friend named Sarah. Sarah liked Tim's phone too. She asked Tim if she could play with his phone. Tim did not want to share his phone, but Sarah kept asking. She insisted that she wanted to play with the phone too. Tim thought about it and decided to be a good friend. He let Sarah play with his phone. They both had fun with the flexible phone. They bent it and talked on it. They played all day and became better friends. Tim was happy that he shared his phone with Sarah.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was sad because he could not twist his toy. He tried and tried, but it just would not twist. Tim's mom saw him and asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim told his mom, "I can't twist my toy, and it makes me sad." His mom said, "Let's try to fix it together." They tried to twist the toy, but it still would not twist. Tim's mom thought and thought. Then she said, "Maybe we can use math to help us twist the toy." They counted how many times they tried to twist the toy. They tried one more time, and the toy finally twisted! Tim was so happy, and he said, "Thank you, Mom! Math helped us twist the toy!" They played with the twisted toy all day, and Tim was not sad anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a nervous bunny. The bunny was looking for food when she stumbled upon a big, brown mushroom. She was so excited! She hopped closer to get a better look and to hop over to it. But then, a big snake suddenly appeared, slithering towards the happy bunny. The bunny was so scared. She started to hop back, but the snake said in a big voice "Please, don't go yet! I promise I won't hurt you." The bunny paused, still feeling very nervous. She replied, "Okay, I will stay. But why do you want me to stay here?" The snake smiled and replied, "I want to share the mushroom with you. I know it is a lot of food and I thought it would be more fun if we ate it together." The bunny was so happy and relieved. She thanked the snake, hopping closer and chowing down on the delicious mushroom with her new friend. They enjoyed their snack, and the bunny was no longer nervous.
