Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Become

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a folder. He liked to keep his drawings in it. One day, Tim saw a big tree. He wanted to become big like the tree. So, he thought of a foolish plan. Tim put his folder under the tree. He thought the tree would help him become big. He waited and waited. But, he did not become big like the tree. Tim felt sad. Then, Tim's mom came and told him that he would become big one day, but he had to be patient. Tim learned that it was foolish to think the tree would help him become big. He understood that he needed to wait and grow up. And that's how Tim learned the moral value of being patient.

Once upon a time, there was a humble little boy named Jason. He woke up one day and was feeling extra special. He liked the idea of doing something extraordinary. He told his mom that he wanted to sign up for a special goal. His mom was confused and asked him what it was. Jason was so excited that he couldn't contain himself. Throwing his arms up, he cried out, "I want to be a big actor one day, momma!" His mom, feeling quite proud and impressed, encouraged him and said, "That's great, sweetheart. You've got to start somewhere, and signing up for acting classes is a good place to start! Why don't we do some research on classes near us?" As they started searching, they found a great acting class. He signed up and attended every class, learning everything he could. Each day, he put forth his best effort and soon, he made many friends. He felt so happy and prepared for his goal. At the end of it all, Jason acted in a play in front of a full audience and he was a huge hit! With a humble heart, his goal was accomplished.

Once upon a time, there was a little car named Red. Red was not very fast. All the other cars would zoom past him. He wanted to improve his speed. One day, Red met a friendly truck named Big Blue. Big Blue saw that Red was sad and asked him what was wrong. Red told Big Blue that he was embarrassed because he was so slow. Big Blue wanted to help Red. Big Blue told Red to practice every day. Red did just that. He practiced and practiced. Soon, Red improved his speed. He was no longer embarrassed. Red was happy, and he thanked Big Blue for helping him. And they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a gentle bunny named Bobo. Bobo loved to read books. Every day, he would sit under a big tree and read his favorite book. One day, while Bobo was reading, a strong wind blew his book away. Bobo was very sad and began to cry. He looked everywhere for his book, but he could not find it. Then, a kind bird saw Bobo crying and asked, "Why are you crying?" Bobo told the bird about his lost book. The bird flew high and found the book in a bush. The bird brought the book back to Bobo. Bobo was so happy and thanked the bird. They became good friends and read the book together under the big tree.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He wanted to find something new. As he walked, he saw a small frightened dog. The dog had a big red ball stuck on its nose. Tim wanted to help the dog. "Hi, dog! Let me help you," Tim said. He gently took the ball off the dog's nose. The dog wagged its tail and barked happily. "Woof, woof, thank you, Tim!" said the dog. Tim and the dog played together all day. They became best friends. Tim learned that helping others makes everyone happy. The dog was no longer frightened, and Tim found a new friend. The moral of the story is to always be kind and help others when they need it.

Once upon a time, there was a bee. The bee lived in a hive. The bee loved to fly and find flowers. One day, the bee found a big flower. The bee was happy. The bee wanted to load lots of flower food to take back to the hive. The bee was very busy. A foolish bird saw the bee. The bird wanted to play. The bird flew close to the bee. The bee was scared. The bird said, "I am sorry, bee." Then, the bee and the bird became friends. They played together and the bee took the food back to the hive.
