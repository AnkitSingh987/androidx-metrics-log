Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Famous Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a famous dog named Spot. Spot loved to play in the snow. One day, Spot saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad because she lost her ball in the snow. Spot said, "I can help you find your ball!" So, Spot and Lily looked for the ball together. They dug and dug in the snow until they found the ball. Lily was very happy. Lily said, "Thank you, Spot!" Spot was happy to help. They played in the snow all day. When it was time to go home, Spot and Lily said goodbye. Spot returned to his house, and Lily returned to hers. They knew they would see each other again soon.

One day, a big bus went down the road. The driver was a nice man. He said "hello" to everyone. All the people on the bus were happy. They liked the driver. They liked to clap for him. One day, a rude man got on the bus. He did not say "hello" to the driver. The driver was sad. The people on the bus did not like the rude man. They did not clap for him. The driver said, "Please be nice and say hello." The rude man said "hello" to the driver. The people on the bus were happy again. They all clapped for the driver and the man. Now, everyone was nice on the bus.

One sunny day, Mom said, "Let's have a picnic!" Tim and Sue were very happy. They put food in a big basket. They took the basket to the park. At the park, they saw a little bug. It was an interesting bug with many colors. Tim said, "Wow, look at that bug!" Sue said, "It's so pretty!" Sue wanted to kiss the bug, but Mom said, "No, don't kiss bugs." They all laughed. They ate their picnic food and had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a normal dog named Sam. Sam loved to eat sausages. One day, Sam found a big sausage on the ground. He was very happy and ate the sausage right away. Later, Sam's tummy started to hurt. His friend, a cat named Mia, saw him and knew he needed comfort. Mia sat with Sam and patted his back. She told him that everything would be okay. After some time, Sam started to feel better. He was so thankful for Mia's help. They played together and had a fun day. Sam learned to be more careful with what he ate, and he never ate a sausage from the ground again.

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Tom. Tom was not very strong because of his thin body. He had a hard time carrying any weight. One day, Tom saw a big box. He wanted to move it, but it was too heavy. Tom had an idea. He saw a spray bottle near the box. Tom thought that if he sprayed the box, it would be easier to move. He sprayed the water on the box and tried to push it again. The box was still heavy, but Tom felt stronger. Tom kept spraying the water and pushing the box. After a while, he moved the box to where he wanted it to be. Tom was very happy. He learned that even if something is heavy, he can still move it with a little help.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Anna. She loved to play with her toys. One day, she had the idea to make something with the clay that was in her toy chest. She was so excited, she ran right up to her mom and asked, "Mommy, can I make something with the clay?" Her mom answered yes, so Anna ran off to get the clay and began making things. She made a fancy bird and all sorts of other shapes, too. After she finished all of her creations, she called out to her mom, "Mommy, look what I made!" Her mom was so amazed that she answered, "That's so fancy, Anna, I'm very proud of you!" Anna smiled and thanked her mom, then said, "Let's get some more clay so I can make some more!" Anna's mom said, "That's a great idea!" and they went to get more clay. Anna and her mom had a lot of fun creating new things with the clay. Every time Anna finished something, her mom would answer, "That looks great!" Anna was so proud of all of the things she created and was always eager to make more.
