Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a big party to welcome a new marriage. Tom and Sue were very happy. They had yummy cake and everyone was smiling. At the party, a funny dog came in. The dog wanted some yummy cake too. He jumped on the table and ate a big piece. Everyone was surprised! But then, the dog did a trick. He danced on his back legs and made everyone laugh. Tom and Sue were not mad anymore. They were happy the dog made their marriage party more fun. And everyone clapped for the funny dog.

One day, a small cat named Tim went for a walk. Tim liked to feel the smooth grass under his paws. He walked and walked until he saw a big tree. In the tree, there was a little bird. The bird looked sad. "Help me, please," said the bird. "My parent is stuck in a net. Can you save them?" Tim wanted to help. He climbed the big tree and saw the bird's parent. The net was tight, but Tim used his sharp claws to cut it. The bird's parent was free! "Thank you for saving my parent," said the little bird with a big smile. Tim felt happy and proud. He went back home to tell his friends about his adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a fish named Bob. Bob lived in a small pond. He liked to swim in the clear water. He had many friends who lived there too. They were all very happy. One day, Bob saw something new in the pond. It was a shiny, round thing. Bob and his friends did not know what it was. They all wanted to see it up close. They swam closer to the shiny thing. When they got close to the shiny thing, they saw that it had a big word on it. The word was "oxygen". Bob and his friends did not know what "oxygen" was. They were curious. They wanted to know more about it. Just then, a big frog jumped into the pond. The frog saw the shiny thing too. The frog told Bob and his friends that "oxygen" was what they needed to breathe. Bob and his friends were surprised. They did not know that the clear water had something they needed. From that day on, Bob and his friends were very thankful for the clear water and the oxygen it had. They knew that the pond was a special place for them to live. And they all lived happily ever after.

Johnnie was a chubby 3 year old boy who loved playing with trucks. He had a whole plateful of them! His favorite was a big red truck with spinning tires. Johnnie would carry it around all day long, making vroom, vroom noises as he drove it around. One day, Johnnie was in his room playing with his truck and making vroom noises. Suddenly he heard a strange noise outside. He ran to the window and saw an enormous truck parked across the street. It was much bigger than his red truck! Johnnie wanted to go and see the truck up close. Johnnie ran outside and went to stand next to the truck. He was having so much fun looking at all the details on the truck. Then he saw the driver and waved. "Do you want to play with my truck?" asked the driver. Johnnie was so excited that he shouted out, "Yes!" The driver smiled and handed Johnnie a small remote control truck. Johnnie couldn't believe his luck and quickly started racing it around the street. After a few minutes, he felt so tired that he had to surrender and sit down. The driver laughed and said, "Johnnie, you played really well with my truck." Johnnie smiled and said, "Thank you for letting me play with your truck!"

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park to play. He saw a sign that said, "Free apples here!" He walked to the table with the apples. He picked up a red apple and took a big bite. It was very spicy! Tim ran to his mom and said, "Mom, this apple is spicy!" His mom looked at the apple and saw that it was a special kind of apple. She told Tim, "This is a spicy apple, it's not like the ones we have at home." Tim wanted to try a different apple, so he went back to the table. This time, he picked up a green apple. He took a bite and it was just right. Tim was happy, and he ate the whole apple. From that day on, Tim always remembered the spicy apple from the park.
