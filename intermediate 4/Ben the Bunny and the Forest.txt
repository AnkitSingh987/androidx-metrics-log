Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben the Bunny and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a small bunny named Ben. Ben was an incredible jumper. He loved to jump all day long. One sunny day, Ben saw a big race. All the animals were there. Ben wanted to win, so he joined the race. They all ran fast, but Ben jumped even faster. At the end of the race, Ben was very tired. He found a nice spot under a tree and sat down. Ben was so happy because he won the race. All the animals cheered for Ben, the incredible jumping bunny.

One day, a good dog named Max saw a cat. The cat was running fast. Max wanted to play with the cat, so he ran after it. They ran and ran, and soon they were far from home. Max and the cat saw a police car. The police were nice and wanted to help Max find his home. They had a big book with many names. Max did not know how to read, so the police had an idea. The police used a thing to record Max's bark. They played the bark for people to hear. Soon, a little girl heard Max's bark and knew it was him. She was so happy to find Max and took him home. Max and the girl played with the cat, and they all became good friends.

Tom and Mia like to play in the creek near their house. They splash and catch fish and make boats from sticks and leaves. One day, they find a big rock with a hole in it. They wonder how deep the hole is. "Let's measure it!" Tom says. He takes a long stick and puts it in the hole. He tries to pull it out, but it is stuck. "Oh no! The stick is too tight in the hole!" Mia tries to help him. She pulls the stick with Tom, but it does not move. They pull harder and harder, but the stick is still tight. They start to cry. "Hey, what are you doing?" They hear a voice behind them. It is their big brother, Sam. He sees the stick and the hole and smiles. "You are silly. That is not a hole. That is a snake's home. The snake is holding the stick with its mouth. That is why it is tight." Tom and Mia are scared. They let go of the stick and run away from the rock. Sam laughs and follows them. He picks up the stick and shakes it. The snake lets go and slithers away. Sam throws the stick in the creek and runs after Tom and Mia. He hugs them and says, "Don't worry. The snake is not bad. It just wanted to keep its home. Next time, let's measure something else, OK?"

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to visit his friend, Kitty, who lived next door. They liked to play in the yard and chase each other. One day, Spot and Kitty did not behave well. They went into the garden and dug up the flowers. When they were caught, they both looked guilty. They knew they had done something wrong. Mommy told Spot and Kitty that they needed to learn to behave better. She showed them how to plant new flowers in the garden. Spot and Kitty promised to be good and not dig up the flowers again. From that day on, they behaved well and had lots of fun during their visits.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom liked to watch television. He watched it every day. He liked to watch fun shows with animals and colors. Tom also liked to eat yummy food when he was hungry. One day, Tom was watching television and he saw a big cake on the screen. He wanted to eat the cake, but he couldn't because it was on the television. Tom felt very hungry. He thought, "What can I do to get the cake?" Tom had an idea. He went to the kitchen and found a big spoon. He went back to the television and tried to take the cake out with the spoon. But, something unexpected happened. The television started to talk! It said, "Hello, Tom! I am a magic television. I can do something special for you." Tom was very surprised. He asked the television, "Can you give me the cake?" The television said, "Yes, but you must do something for me first. You must give me a hug." Tom hugged the television and, suddenly, the cake came out of the screen! Tom was so happy. He ate the cake and it was very yummy. From that day on, Tom knew that his television was magic and could do amazing things.
