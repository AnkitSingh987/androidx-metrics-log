Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Magic

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a girl named Mia. Mia was a very good designer. She loved to design clothes for her friends. One day, Mia met a mysterious lady who had a magic touch. The lady said, "I can give you a special massage that will make your designs even better!" Mia was curious and said, "Yes, please!" The mysterious lady gave Mia a massage, and suddenly, Mia felt a new power in her hands. She could now design clothes that were more beautiful than ever before. Mia's friends were so happy with their new clothes. They said, "Thank you, Mia! Your designs are so pretty!" But Mia knew that the mysterious lady had helped her. The moral of the story is to always be thankful for the help you receive from others, even if it comes from a mysterious source.

Once upon a time, there was a little lamp that lived in a calm room. The lamp loved to give light to the room. It made the room bright and happy. One day, the lamp saw a girl and a boy playing in the room. They were clapping their hands and laughing. The lamp wanted to join them. So, the lamp began to blink its light on and off. The girl and boy saw the lamp and began to applaud. The lamp felt very happy. It continued to blink its light, and the girl and boy kept clapping. They all had a great time together. And from that day on, the little lamp, the girl, and the boy became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She lived in a big, rich house with her mom, dad, and baby brother, Tim. Amy loved to play with her toys, and her favorite toy was a small doll named Sally. One day, while Amy was taking a bath, she saw a bottle of shampoo. She thought it would be fun to give Sally a bath too. So, she poured some shampoo on Sally's head and started to wash her hair. "Sally, you'll be so clean now!" said Amy. But when Amy tried to wash the shampoo off Sally's head, it got in her eyes. Sally started to cry. Amy felt sad and said, "I'm sorry, Sally." She quickly washed the shampoo out of Sally's eyes and gave her a big hug. From that day on, Amy made sure to be extra careful when giving Sally a bath.

One day, a big, red ball was rolling down the hill. A little boy named Tim saw the ball and wanted to touch it. But his mom said, "No, Tim! Don't touch the ball. It's stupid to touch things you don't know." Tim was sad, but he listened to his mom. He went inside the house to take a nap. While he was sleeping, a little girl named Sue saw the big, red ball outside. She thought it was so cool and wanted to touch it too. Sue touched the ball, and suddenly, it popped! It made a loud noise that woke Tim up from his nap. Tim ran outside and saw the broken ball. He told Sue, "Mom said not to touch it." Sue felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, Tim. I didn't know." They became friends and played with other toys together, being careful not to touch things they didn't know.

Once upon a time, there was a big red ball. The red ball lived in a hall with other balls. They all liked to play and have fun together. The red ball was the biggest and the most fun to play with. One day, the red ball wanted to go on a trip. It needed to pack a bag with things it would need. The red ball packed a small hat, some food, and a toy. It was ready to go on a big adventure. The red ball rolled down the hall and out the door. It rolled and rolled, having lots of fun. It met new friends and played with them. At the end of the day, the red ball was happy and tired. It rolled back to the hall, unpacked its bag, and went to sleep, dreaming of more adventures.
