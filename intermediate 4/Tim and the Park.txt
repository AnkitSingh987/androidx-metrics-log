Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Park

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went to the park. They liked to play and have fun together. Tim was very patient with Spot. He would throw a ball and wait for Spot to bring it back. At the park, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue was eating a treat. Tim asked, "Can I have a treat, too?" Sue said, "Yes, you can have one if you can catch it!" She threw the treat high up in the air. Tim and Spot both tried to catch the treat. But something unexpected happened. A big bird flew down and caught the treat in its beak! Tim, Sue, and Spot all laughed. They enjoyed the surprise and continued to play in the park.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Emma. She was feeling lonely and sad. She didn't have anyone she could talk to. So she sat down in the garden and started to cry. Suddenly a big brown bear appeared. He was very kind and he asked her why she was crying. Emma told him she was feeling lonely. The bear looked at her kindly and said it was OK. He gave her a hug to make her feel better. The bear then offered some water from a stream to help Emma feel better. She drank it and instantly felt better. The bear stayed for a while, carefully comforting Emma until she was feeling better. Finally he said it was time for him to go. Emma waved goodbye to the kind bear and thanked him for making her feel better. She never felt quite so lonely again.

One day, Tom found a trumpet in a box. He was very happy. He wanted to play the trumpet. But the trumpet was sad because it was broken. It needed something to attach to it so it could make music again. Tom looked around and found a big rubber band. He thought it might help the sad trumpet. He attached the rubber band to the trumpet. The trumpet still could not play. Tom was sad too. Tom's mom saw him and the sad trumpet. She knew what to do. She found a small piece that was missing. She attached the piece to the trumpet. The trumpet was happy now. Tom played the trumpet and they both were very happy.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little mouse. The mouse seemed very happy. He had lots of friends and played every day. But one day, he saw a big trap in his house. The mouse felt troubled. The mouse did not want to get caught in the trap. He asked his friends for help. They all thought hard about what to do. One friend said, "We can push the trap away!" So, they all worked together to push the trap out of the house. After they pushed the trap away, the mouse and his friends were not troubled anymore. They went back to playing and having fun. The little mouse was very happy that he had friends to help him. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little humble cat named Mimi was playing outside. It started to rain, and Mimi got wet. She saw a big tree and ran under it to stay dry. Mimi saw a bird sitting on a branch. The bird was wet too. "Hello, bird," said Mimi. "I am wet. Are you wet too?" "Yes, Mimi," said the bird. "I am wet too. Let's pull this big leaf down to help us stay dry." Mimi and the bird jumped up and pulled the big leaf down. They both sat under the leaf and stayed dry. They were happy to have each other's help. The rain stopped, and Mimi and the bird went back to playing in the sun.

Once upon a time, there was a white dog named Spot. Spot was very intelligent. He lived in a small house with a nice family. The family loved Spot very much. One day, Spot wanted to have a ball to play with. He looked around the house but could not find one. Spot was sad. He wanted to play with a ball so much. Then, Spot had an idea. He went to his family and barked. The family understood what Spot wanted. They gave him a big, red ball. Spot was very happy. He played with the ball all day long.
