Category: Intermediate
Title: Joe and the Present

Once there was a little boy. His name was Joe. One day Joe's dad took him to the stadium. It was much bigger than their house. Joe was so excited! He saw so many other people around him, and he felt like he was in a magical place. Then Joe's dad gave him some popcorn and a toy to play with. Joe was so grateful for his dad's present. He had so much fun and couldn't believe it was happening! At the end of the day Joe and his dad went back home. Joe hugged his dad tightly, and he was so happy. He was so grateful for the day at the stadium. Joe knew he would never forget it!

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to go fishing with her dad. One day, Lucy and her dad were out fishing. It was a hot day. Dad said, ''Let's go fishing. I bet we can carry a lot of fish home today.'' So they each grabbed their fishing poles and started fishing. Soon, Lucy caught a fish. She carried it to the boat. ''Woohoo!'' she said. Dad caught another fish. The fish were jumping around in the boat. Lucy was so excited. They carried the fish home. Dad cooked them for dinner and they enjoyed a hot and delicious dinner that night.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was very good at games. He was a successful player. One day, Tim found a big box. In the box, there was a teaspoon. Tim did not know what to do with it. Tim took the teaspoon to his mom. His mom said, "Tim, you can use the teaspoon to record how much sugar you put in your tea." So, Tim used the teaspoon to record the sugar. He put one, two, three teaspoons in his cup. But, something unexpected happened. When Tim put the third teaspoon of sugar in his cup, the sugar turned into a big, sweet candy. Tim was very happy and surprised. He shared the candy with his mom and they both had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Bobby. Bobby lived in a small tree with his family. One day, Bobby saw his friends flying around and he wanted to join them. But Bobby was scared because he had never flown before. Bobby took a deep breath and tried to fly. At first, he started to panic and felt like he was going to fall. But he remembered what his mom told him, "Don't be scared, just believe in yourself." So, Bobby tried again and this time he flew high in the sky. As Bobby was flying, he saw an empty nest on a big tree. He thought it would be fun to play in the nest. But when he got closer, he saw another bird family living there. Bobby knew it was not nice to take what belonged to others. So, Bobby flew back to his own tree and played with his family. The moral of the story is to believe in yourself and always be kind to others.

Once upon a time, there was a charming little girl named Lily. Lily had a big brain for her age. She loved to play with her toy car. One day, Lily's toy car got stuck under the sofa. Lily tried to pull the car out, but it was too hard for her. She asked her mom for help. "Mom, can you help me pull my car out?" Lily said. Her mom came and helped her pull the car out from under the sofa. After they got the car out, Lily gave her mom a big hug. "Thank you, Mom!" Lily said with a big smile. They played with the toy car together, and they had a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot lived near a big field. He liked to run and play in the field every day. Spot was a good dog. One sunny day, Spot met a new friend. It was a little cat named Fluffy. Fluffy said, "Hi Spot! Do you want to play with me in the field?" Spot said, "Yes! I love to play in the field!" Spot and Fluffy played all day. They ran, jumped, and had lots of fun. Soon, they both felt very tired. Spot yawned and said, "I am sleepy." Fluffy yawned too and said, "Me too. Let's go home and rest." They went home, happy with their good day in the field.
