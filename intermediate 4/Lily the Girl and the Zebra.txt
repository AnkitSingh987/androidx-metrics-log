Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Zebra

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small pink house with her mom and dad. One day, Lily wanted to go for a walk, so she asked her mom if she could. Mom said, "Yes, but stay close." So Lily was off and running! As Lily was walking, she saw something pink in the distance. What could it be? She went a bit closer and saw that it was a zebra! She was so excited! She asked the zebra, "Where are you going?" The zebra said, "I'm going for a stroll through the woods, would you like to come?" Lily thought it sounded like fun, so she said yes. And off they went together. Along the way, they saw all kinds of animals, trees, and flowers. It was a beautiful day! After a while they stopped to rest. Lily asked the zebra if he wanted to stay and play, and the zebra said, "Yes, let's go!" So they stayed and played hide-and-seek and all kinds of games until it was time to go home. Finally, Lily said goodbye to the pink zebra and thanked him for the fun she had that day. And they both went their separate ways, until they would meet again!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toy truck. The truck was red and rough. Lily played with her truck every day. One day, Lily saw a big rock. She wanted to lift the rock and put it in her truck. She tried and tried, but the rock was too heavy. Lily was sad for a minute. Then, Lily had an idea. She went to find her big brother, Tom. Tom was strong and could help her lift the rock. Lily asked Tom to help her, and he said yes. Together, they lifted the rock and put it in the truck. Lily was very happy, and she thanked Tom. They played with the truck and the rock all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. She had a big garden full of flowers and trees and there was a big slide in the garden too. One day, Jane's dad suggested she should play on the slide. Jane was so excited, she climbed to the top of the slide and started to slide down. But when she got to the bottom, she saw something brown! Jane went closer to investigate and saw that the brown was a snake! She was so scared that she fell over and started to cry. The snake began to speak to Jane, it suggested that she should be more careful when playing in the garden. Jane was so frightened that she ran back inside and told her dad straight away. Her dad was very worried and said he should never have suggested she play on the slide. From that day on, Jane was always very careful when playing in the garden.

Once upon a time, there was a big red jeep. It was very dependable. It would always take people where they wanted to go. One day, the jeep got very dirty. It needed a polish to be clean and shiny again. A little boy named Tim saw the dirty jeep. He wanted to help. Tim said, "Jeep, I will polish you so you can be clean and shiny again!" The jeep was happy. Tim got a cloth and started to polish the jeep. But then, a mean dog came and barked at Tim. The dog wanted to stop Tim from polishing the jeep. Tim was scared, but he knew the jeep needed his help. He said to the dog, "Go away! I need to polish the jeep!" The dog listened to Tim and went away. Tim finished polishing the jeep, and it was clean and shiny again. The jeep was very thankful to Tim, and they became good friends.

Bob and Jack were best friends. They were having a great day, playing in the park together when Bob noticed something shiny in the grass. It was a silver dime! He was so excited and asked Jack if he wanted to share it. Jack said, "No, I don’t want it. You can have it all." Bob was a bit sad and said, "That’s ok. We can keep it together." But Jack didn’t care. He started to run away with the silver dime and Bob chased after him. Suddenly, something bad happen. Jack stepped into a hole and fell down. Bob was scared, but he ran to help his best friend. But when he got there, he saw that Jack had the silver dime in his hand. Jack said, "It’s mine now! I won’t share it with you." Bob was disappointed and said, "That’s not fair. We should have kept it together like we said." But it was too late. Bob stormed away and Jack stayed in the park with his great silver dime.
