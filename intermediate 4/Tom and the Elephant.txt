Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and the Elephant

Once upon a time there was a baby elephant called Tom. He loved playing games and he always had a lot of fun. Today Tom was feeling extra adventurous, so he decided to explore in the jungle. He was hopping around on the ground, when he noticed something shiny in the bushes. It was a small colorful case. He was so delighted he couldn't help but take a closer look. When Tom opened the case he saw some beautiful, colorful jewels. Tom was about to take them but then he heard a voice. It was a big elephant coming his way. Tom started to worry. What if the big elephant wanted the jewels too? The big elephant said, "Never take something that doesn't belong to you, young one!" Tom was so scared he started to cry. The big elephant saw his tears and said, "I tell you what, you can keep one of the jewels as long as you keep it safe in the case ok?". Tom smiled and said, "ok!" He thanked the big elephant for understanding and hopped away. He took the case with the jewel inside to his home, where he kept it safe and secure. Tom was very relieved and he vowed never to worry again. From then on he only looked for fun things to do and always made sure to think wisely before making any decisions.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that he loved very much. It was a silver car that could change into a robot. Tim played with his silver car every day. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. Sam saw the silver car and wanted to play with it too. Tim didn't want to share his favorite toy. Sam asked, "Can I play with your silver car, please?" Tim said, "No, it's mine!" They started to fight over the toy, and both wanted to play with it. Tim's mom came into the room and saw them fighting. She was not happy. She told them to be patient and share the toy. But they didn't listen to her. They kept fighting, and the silver car broke in half. Now, neither Tim nor Sam could play with the silver car. They both felt sad, and they learned that not sharing can lead to bad things.

Ben and Lily were playing in the park. They saw a big tree with many leaves. They wanted to climb the tree and see what was up there. "Can we go up, Mom?" Ben asked. Mom nodded. "Yes, but be careful. Hold on to the branches and don't go too high." Ben and Lily smiled and ran to the tree. They climbed up and up, until they reached a big branch. They saw something round and brown on the branch. It was a nest! "Look, Lily, a nest!" Ben said. "Maybe there are birds inside." Lily nodded. "Let's see. Maybe we can say hello to them." They moved closer to the nest. They heard some chirping sounds. They peeked inside the nest. They saw four small birds with yellow feathers. The birds looked at them with big eyes. "Hello, birds!" Lily said. "We are Ben and Lily. We are your friends." The birds nodded. They seemed friendly. They chirped some more. "Hello, Ben and Lily!" Mom said from below. "It's time to go home. Come down now." Ben and Lily nodded. They waved goodbye to the birds. "Bye, birds!" Ben said. "We will come back and visit you again." "Bye, birds!" Lily said. "You have a nice nest." They climbed down the tree and ran to Mom. They told her about the birds and the nest. Mom smiled and hugged them. "You had a fun adventure today," Mom said. "But now it's time for a regular nap. You must be tired." Ben and Lily nodded. They were tired, but happy. They had made some new friends in the park.

Ben and Lily were going to have a picnic with Mom and Dad. They packed some sandwiches, fruits, cookies and juice in a big basket. They also brought a ball, a kite and a blanket to play and rest on the grass. They walked to the park, holding hands. They saw some ducks, squirrels and birds on the way. Ben and Lily liked to watch the animals and make noises like them. Mom and Dad smiled and joined them. When they got to the park, they found a nice spot under a big tree. They spread the blanket and put the basket on it. Mom and Dad opened the sandwiches and gave one to each child. They also poured some juice in cups. "Yum, this is good!" Ben said, taking a big bite. "I like it too!" Lily agreed, sipping her juice. They ate and talked and laughed. Then they decided to play with the ball and the kite. Ben threw the ball and Lily caught it. They ran and jumped and had fun. Mom and Dad flew the kite and watched it soar in the sky. After a while, they heard some music. They looked and saw some people wearing red and blue clothes and hats. They had drums, trumpets, flutes and flags. They were marching in a line, making loud and happy sounds. "What are they doing?" Ben asked, curious. "They are having a parade!" Mom explained. "They are celebrating something special today. Do you want to see them?" "Yes, yes!" Ben and Lily said, excited. They packed their things and followed Mom and Dad to the street. They joined the crowd and watched the parade. They saw more people with costumes, balloons, cars and bikes. They clapped and cheered and waved. "This is so fun!" Lily said, smiling. "We are lucky!" Ben said, hugging Mom and Dad. "We have a picnic and a parade!" They all hugged and smiled. They were happy and grateful for their family and their day.
