Category: Intermediate
Title: The Lady and the Elderly

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived an elderly lady. She had many books with her. She loved to read and teach little kids. One day, a small girl came to the elderly lady's house. The girl wanted to learn a new spell. The elderly lady was happy to help her. She recommended a simple spell from one of her books. The girl tried the spell and it worked! She was so happy and thanked the elderly lady. They became good friends and learned many more spells together. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved clapping her hands. One day, Lucy went to the park with her mom, and she saw a giant vegetable. It was green and big! Lucy couldn't wait to try it. Mom said, "No, Lucy, it's a vegetable and it's not for eating!" But Lucy was determined and she said, "Please Mom, I want to try it!" So, Mom said, "Okay. If you clap your hands three times, you may try it." Lucy was so happy and she clapped her hands three times. She took a bite of the giant green vegetable, but it was too sour for her. She made a sour face and said, "Ugh, it's too yucky." Mom smiled and said, "See? I told you vegetables are best for eating, not for playing." Lucy smiled and nodded. She learned her lesson and never tried to eat any veggies again.

Once upon a time, there was a messy dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his rope toy. He would run around and wag his tail while holding the rope in his mouth. One day, Spot was playing with his rope near the kitchen. His mom was cooking and she told him, "Spot, please resist playing here. It's not safe." Spot didn't want to listen, but he knew his mom was right. So, Spot took his rope outside and played in the yard. He had so much fun running and jumping with his rope. His mom was happy too, because she knew Spot was safe. And they all lived happily ever after.

Bob was a good boy. He loved to play outdoors in the sun. One day, his mommy gave him a sunflower. Bob was so happy. He wanted to keep it forever. "Mommy, can I keep the sunflower?" asked Bob. "Yes, you can keep it," said Mommy, "but you have to unlock the door first." Bob ran to the door and tried to unlock it, but it was hard. He tried and tried until finally, the door opened. Bob ran back to the sunflower. He hugged it and said, "This sunflower is good and I will keep it forever." Bob was so happy and he went back outside to enjoy the sun. He took his sunflower with him and smiled the whole time.

Once upon a time, there was a great lawyer named Sam. Sam was very good at his job. One day, Sam went to a big party with many people. At the party, Sam wanted to introduce his best friend, a big dog named Max. Max was a very smart dog. He could do many tricks. At the party, Sam and Max showed everyone the tricks Max could do. People were very happy and clapped their hands. But then, something unexpected happened. A little cat came into the party. Max saw the cat and wanted to play. But the cat was scared and ran away. Max chased the cat and made a big mess. People were very surprised. The great lawyer Sam was not happy. But then, the cat did a trick and everyone laughed. In the end, Sam introduced the cat as his new friend.

Tom and Jen are ducks who live in a pond. They like to swim and play with their friends. One day, they see a new duck in the pond. He is different from them. He has a long neck and big feet. He is a swan. Tom and Jen are curious. They swim to the swan and say hello. The swan is shy. He says his name is Sam. He tells them he came from another pond. He says he has no friends. He says the other ducks and swans do not like him. They say he is too different. Tom and Jen feel sorry for Sam. They invite him to join them. They show him their favorite spots in the pond. They share their food with him. They teach him how to chew the grass and the seeds. They make him laugh with their jokes. They have fun together. Sam is happy. He thanks Tom and Jen for being kind to him. He says he likes them a lot. He says he does not feel different anymore. He says he feels like he belongs. Tom and Jen are happy too. They say they are glad to have Sam as their friend. They say they like him for who he is. They say they do not care if he is different. They say they like to learn new things from him. The moral of the story is: Do not judge someone by how they look. Be friendly and open to others who are different from you. You can find good friends in unexpected places.
