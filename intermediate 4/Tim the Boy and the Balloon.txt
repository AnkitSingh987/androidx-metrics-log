Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Balloon

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy some food for dinner. Tim saw a big, red ball. It was expensive, but he really wanted it. He asked his mom if he could have it, but she said no. Tim was sad, but he had an idea. He thought he could pull the ball off the shelf and take it home without his mom knowing. So, he pulled the ball and tried to hide it under his shirt. As he pulled the ball, something unexpected happened. The ball was not just a ball, but it was a big balloon! It started to float up in the air. Tim was surprised and scared. His mom saw what happened and came to help him. Tim's mom was not mad, but she told him that taking things without asking was wrong. Tim said sorry and promised not to do it again. They left the store and went home, but Tim learned a big lesson that day.

Lily loved peaches. She liked how they were soft and sweet and juicy. She wanted to eat a peach every day. But peaches were not easy to find. They grew on trees that were high and far away. Lily had to wait for her mom or dad to pick them for her. One day, Lily saw a peach on the ground. It was big and round and shiny. Lily was very happy. She ran to the peach and picked it up. She wanted to bite it right away. But the peach was too big for her mouth. It was tight and hard and fuzzy. Lily tried to open her mouth wider and wider. She struggled to fit the peach in. But she could not. She felt sad and angry. She threw the peach on the ground and stomped on it. Then Lily heard a voice. It was her mom. She said, "Lily, what are you doing? Why did you ruin that peach? That was a good peach. You could have shared it with me or cut it into pieces. You should not waste food. Food is precious. You should be grateful and careful with what you have." Lily looked at her mom. She felt sorry and ashamed. She said, "I'm sorry, mom. I was greedy. I wanted to eat the whole peach by myself. But it was too big and tight. I could not eat it. I got mad and threw it away. I was wrong. I should have asked you for help. I should have shared it with you. Can you forgive me?" Her mom smiled and hugged her. She said, "Of course, I forgive you, Lily. I love you. You made a mistake, but you learned from it. Next time, when you see a peach, remember to be thankful and gentle. Peaches are gifts from nature. They are not toys. They are food. And food is for everyone to enjoy." Lily nodded and hugged her mom back. She said, "I love you, too, mom. Thank you for teaching me. Next time, I will be a good girl. I will share my peaches with you and others. And I will eat them slowly and nicely. Peaches are yummy and good."

One day, a big cat and a little dog were playing together. They found a box of powder. The big cat said, "Let's unite and make something with this powder." But the little dog was not careful. He knocked over the box, and the powder went all over the floor. The big cat was very sad. He said, "I am sorry, but we can't play with the powder now." Just then, a magic bird flew into the room. The bird saw the powder on the floor and said, "Don't be sorry. I can help you." The magic bird waved its wings, and the powder turned into a big, beautiful cake! The big cat and the little dog were very happy. They thanked the magic bird and shared the cake together.

One day, Sally and her mommy went to a restaurant. Sally was very excited. She loved eating fancy food. But when they arrived, something strange happened. Everything had vanished! It was all gone. Sally's mommy was angry and confused. She asked the restaurant workers what had happened. They said a selfish man had taken all the food away. Sally was very sad. She had been looking forward to eating all day. Luckily, another restaurant was nearby. Sally and her mommy went there and were able to have a nice meal. Sally was very happy again. She learned not to be selfish and that it was wrong to take things that didn't belong to her.
