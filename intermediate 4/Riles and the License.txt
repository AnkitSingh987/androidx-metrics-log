Category: Intermediate
Title: Riles and the License

Riles was a very smart boy. Everyday he would study very hard. He knew it was very important to keep learning. His parents were very proud of Riles. They wanted him to do his very best. His mom and dad wanted Riles to get a license when he got older. So Riles kept studying. He was determined to get his license. Everyday he worked on math and writing. He read books and practiced his spelling. Soon enough, Riles was ready for his license. Riles passed the test and he earned his license! His parents were so proud - they threw a big celebration for Riles. Riles was happy that he was able to get his license. He knew it was important and he was proud of himself for studying hard.

Once upon a time, in a peaceful little town, there was a tiny mouse named Tim. Tim was very small but very smart. Every day, Tim would play with his friends and have lots of fun. One day, Tim saw a big piece of cheese in a house. He wanted to eat it, but there was a test he had to pass. There was a big, scary cat guarding the cheese. Tim had to be brave and find a way to get the cheese without the cat catching him. Tim thought and thought. Then, he had a great idea! He found a long string and tied it to the cheese. Tim pulled the string very hard, and the cheese started to move. The cat was asleep, and Tim was able to escape with the cheese. Tim was so happy, and he shared the cheese with all of his friends. They all enjoyed the delicious treat, and Tim was proud that he passed the test.

David and Lucy were two little kids. They were the best of friends, but sometimes they would quarrel. Today they were quarreling over a toy. Lucy had the toy first and David was envious. He wanted it too! He kept asking Lucy to give it to him. But Lucy didn't want to. Their arguing got louder and soon their mommy heard them. She noticed that Lucy was holding the toy tightly in her hand. She then explained to them why it was important to share with each other and not to be envious. With a little bit of help, David and Lucy worked it out. David finally got a turn with the toy and then it was Lucy's turn again. They were both happy and continued to play together.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy tutor named Mr. Fox. He liked to help little kids learn new things. But he also liked to mind other people's business. One day, he saw a little girl named Sally playing with her toy. "Hi, Sally!" said Mr. Fox. "What are you doing?" "I'm playing with my toy," said Sally. Mr. Fox wanted to mind her play. "Can I play too?" he asked. Sally didn't like nosy people. "No, thank you," she said. "I like to play alone." Mr. Fox felt sad but he knew he should not mind her play. So he went away to find other kids to help. From that day, he tried not to be nosy and learned to let kids play alone.

Once upon a time, there was a messy little boy named Tim. He loved to play in the mud and get dirty. One day, while playing near the beach, he found a small oyster. The oyster was hiding in the sand. Tim picked up the oyster and wanted to take it home. But the oyster did not want to go with him. It wanted to escape and go back to the water with its friends. So, it tried to wiggle out of Tim's hand. Tim felt the oyster move and opened his hand. The oyster fell on the ground and rolled back into the water. It was happy to escape from the messy little boy. Tim waved goodbye to the oyster and went back to playing in the mud.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly bird. The bird had many friends. They all liked to play together. One day, the bird saw a tree. The tree was big and tall. The bird wanted to lean on the tree. So, the bird flew to the tree and leaned on it. The tree was very strong. The bird was happy. It called its friends to come and play. They all leaned on the tree too. They played all day and had lots of fun. The friendly bird and its friends loved the tree. They played there every day.
