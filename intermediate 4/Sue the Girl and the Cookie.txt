Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Cookie

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She was very adventurous. One day, Sue went outside to play. She saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Sue went up, up, up in the tree. As Sue sat in the tree, she found a cookie. She was so happy! But the cookie was dirty. Sue knew she had to wash it before she could eat it. She climbed down the tree with the cookie in her hand. Sue went inside her house and washed the cookie in the sink. She made sure it was clean. Then, she sat down and ate the cookie. It was the best cookie she ever had. And that was the end of Sue's adventurous day.

Once upon a time, there was a popular chair in a room full of furniture. The chair loved to count how many friends would sit on it each day. One day, the chair noticed that a new little table was brought into the room. The chair said to the table, "Hello, new friend! I love to count how many friends sit on me. What do you like to do?" The little table replied, "I like to hold things for my friends, like cups and toys." Days went by, and the chair noticed that more and more friends were using the little table. The chair felt sad and thought, "Maybe I am not so popular anymore." But then, the chair remembered that it loved to count and started counting how many friends used the little table too. In the end, the chair and the little table became best friends. They learned that being popular is not as important as being kind and helpful to others.

Tom and Lily were playing in the yard when they saw a big storm coming. The sky was dark and loud. They ran inside the house and closed the door. "Mom, can we mail a letter to grandma?" Lily asked. She had drawn a picture of a flower for her. "Sure, honey, but we have to wait until the storm is over. It is not safe to go outside now," Mom said. She gave Lily an envelope and a stamp. Tom looked out the window and saw a bright flash. "Wow, look, Lily! Lightning!" he shouted. He liked to watch the storm. Lily ran to the window and saw the lightning too. She was scared. "Mom, I don't like lightning. It is too bright and loud. It makes me jump," she said. Mom hugged Lily and Tom. "Don't worry, my loves. Lightning is just a big spark in the sky. It can't hurt us inside the house. It will go away soon," she said. They heard a loud boom. The lightning had hit a trash can outside. It made a big hole and a smelly smoke. "Yuck, that trash can is smelly. It needs a bath," Tom said. He made a funny face. Lily giggled. She forgot her fear. "Maybe we can mail it to grandma too. She will like it," she said. Mom laughed. "I don't think so, sweetie. Grandma likes flowers, not trash. Let's mail her your picture instead. She will love it," she said. They waited for the storm to pass. Then they put on their coats and shoes and went to the mailbox. They mailed the letter to grandma and hoped she would get it soon. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big, soft pillow that she loved to hug every night. Lily was always very careful with her pillow because it was special to her. One day, Lily found a tiny hole in her pillow. She was sad and didn't know what to do. She hugged her pillow tightly and wished it would be okay. Suddenly, a little bird came out of the hole! Lily was so surprised. The little bird told Lily that it was lost and needed a safe place to stay. Lily was happy to help and let the bird live in her pillow. They became best friends, and Lily was extra careful with her pillow now. Every night, Lily hugged her pillow and the little bird inside, knowing that she had a special friend to keep her company.
