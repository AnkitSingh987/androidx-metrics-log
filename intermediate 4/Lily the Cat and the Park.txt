Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Cat and the Park

One day, a pretty cat named Lily was walking in the park. She saw a small bug with a green skin on a leaf. The bug was lost and wanted to find his family. Lily wanted to help the bug, so she let the bug sit on her back. Lily started to lead the bug to his family. They walked and walked, looking for the bug's family. The sun was very hot, but Lily did not give up. She knew that she would find the bug's family soon. As they walked, they saw a big tree with many bugs that looked like the small bug. The small bug was so happy to see his family! Lily had led him back home. The bugs thanked Lily for her help, and they all became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a huge dog named Max. Max loved to chew on things. One day, Max found a piece of paper on the floor. He picked it up with his mouth and started to chew it. Max's friend, a little girl named Lily, saw what Max was doing. She said, "Max, don't chew the paper! It's not good for you." Max stopped chewing and looked at Lily. He did not know that paper was bad for him. Lily took the paper from Max and gave him a big hug. She said, "Let's find you something better to chew on." They went to the store and bought a chew toy for Max. From that day on, Max chewed on his toy and never chewed on paper again.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom loved to play outside under the sun. One day, while Tom was playing, he saw a little mouse named Jerry. "Hi Jerry!" said Tom. "Do you want to play with me under the sun?" "Yes, Tom!" replied Jerry. "I love to play under the sun too!" As they were playing, Tom suddenly felt a tickle in his nose. He tried to hold it in, but he couldn't. "Achoo!" Tom sneezed really loud. Jerry started to laugh. "Tom, that was a big sneeze!" said Jerry, still giggling. Tom laughed too, and they continued to play and have fun under the sun.

Once upon a time, there was a unique beetle named Bob. Bob was not like other beetles. He was very big and had a shiny shell. Bob loved to surprise his friends in the forest. One day, Bob met a little bird named Sally. Sally was sad because she lost her way. Bob wanted to help Sally. He said, "Don't worry, Sally. I will help you find your home." Bob and Sally walked and talked. They saw many trees and flowers. Suddenly, Bob hid behind a big rock. He wanted to surprise Sally. He jumped out and shouted, "Surprise!" Sally laughed and said, "You are so funny, Bob!" Bob and Sally became good friends. They played and laughed all day. In the end, they found Sally's home. Sally was happy and thanked Bob for his help. Bob smiled and said, "You're welcome! Let's play again soon!" From that day on, Bob and Sally were best friends. They had many fun adventures in the forest. And Bob always loved to surprise Sally and make her laugh.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She lived near the sea with her adorable pet cat, Mittens. They loved to play together on the beach. One day, Lucy and Mittens found a big stick near the water. Lucy said, "Mittens, let's break this stick and throw it into the sea!" Mittens meowed happily, and they broke the stick into two pieces. They threw the pieces into the water and watched the waves carry them away. Lucy and Mittens played by the sea all day. They built sand castles and chased seagulls. As the sun went down, they went back home, tired but happy. They couldn't wait for another fun day by the sea.

Once upon a time, there was a huge fish named Fin. Fin liked to dive deep in the water. One day, while diving, he met a tiny infant fish named Tiny. Tiny was lost and scared. Fin wanted to help Tiny. They swam together, looking for Tiny's family. They saw other fish, but none were Tiny's family. They kept diving and swimming until they found a big group of fish that looked like Tiny. Tiny was so happy to find his family. They all thanked Fin for being a good friend and helping Tiny. From that day on, Fin and Tiny became best friends. They loved to dive and play together in the water.
