Category: Intermediate
Title: The Girl Called Sarah and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a girl called Sarah. One day, Sarah was walking in the garden when she saw something strange. It was black and shiny, like a stone. It was coal! Sarah was a very curious girl, so she picked up the coal to see what it was like. She rolled the coal back and forth between her hands, and wondered what to do with it. Suddenly, Sarah felt a warm breeze in the air. She looked up and saw a bird, flying above her and waving its wings! The bird dipped down and waved hello to Sarah. Sarah was so excited that she waved back. Then, she looked at the coal again, feeling a little confused. What should she do with it? She could not answer this question. So, Sarah carefully put the coal back where she had found it and continued on her way.

Ben and Sam are brothers. They live on a farm with their mom and dad. They have a barn with many animals. Ben loves the animals. He likes to feed them, pet them, and talk to them. Sam hates the animals. He thinks they are dirty, noisy, and smelly. He does not want to go near the barn. One day, mom and dad have to go to town. They tell Ben and Sam to stay at home and be good. They say they will be back soon. Ben and Sam say bye to mom and dad. Ben runs to the barn. Sam stays in the house. Ben has fun with the animals. He gives them hay, water, and carrots. He tells them stories and jokes. The animals make happy sounds. They like Ben. Sam is bored in the house. He has no toys, no books, no games. He looks out the window and sees Ben and the animals. He feels angry and jealous. He decides to go to the barn and scare the animals. He thinks that will make him happy. He sneaks to the barn and opens the door. He sees Ben and the animals. He yells, "Boo!" He waves his arms and makes faces. He hopes the animals will run away and Ben will cry. But the animals do not run away. They do not like Sam. They see that he is mean and rude. They stand up for Ben. The cow moos loudly. The horse neighs and kicks. The pig snorts and charges. The sheep baa and headbutt. The chicken pecks and flaps. The animals chase Sam out of the barn. Sam is scared and sorry. He runs to the house and slams the door. Ben is surprised and proud. He thanks the animals for protecting him. He hugs them and tells them they are brave and kind. The animals make happy sounds. They love Ben. Mom and dad come back from town. They see Ben and the animals in the barn. They see Sam in the house. They ask what happened. Ben tells them the truth. Sam tells them a lie. He says he went to the barn to help Ben, but the animals attacked him. Mom and dad do not believe Sam. They know he hates the animals. They are angry and disappointed. They scold Sam and send him to his room. They praise Ben and give him a hug. They say he is modest and good. They are happy and proud.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little bunny named Bobo. Bobo loved to play with his friends. One day, he saw a small bird with a broken wing. The bird looked confused and sad. Bobo wanted to help the bird. He found a stick and offered it to the bird. "Here, use this stick to help your wing," Bobo said. The bird was happy and thanked Bobo. Soon, the bird's wing got better, and they became good friends. They played together every day. The other animals saw how kind Bobo was, and they all wanted to be friends with him too. The moral of the story is to always help others when they need it.

Once there was a cool cop. His uniform was very bright and it shone and glowed in the dark. He loved to go on night patrol and show off his glowing suit. One night another cop came to visit. He saw the bright uniform and exclaimed with amazement. He asked the cool cop, "Where did you get that suit? It looks so awesome!" The cool cop replied, "I'm so glad you asked! It was a special gift from my grandmother. She said that I should always remember to glow, no matter what time of day it is!" The second cop nodded and said, "That's very wise advice indeed!"
