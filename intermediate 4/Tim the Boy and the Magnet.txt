Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Magnet

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy magnet. The magnet was useful. It could pick up small things made of metal. Tim loved to play with his magnet. One day, Tim was playing with his magnet in the park. He saw his friend, Sue. Sue asked, "What is that, Tim?" Tim said, "It is my useful magnet. It can pick up metal things." Sue wanted to try it too. They played with the magnet together. Then, a big wind came. It blew the magnet away. Tim and Sue looked for the magnet, but they could not find it. They were sad. They did not know that the wind had blown the magnet to a faraway place where it would never be found again.

Once upon a time, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside every day. One day, he was walking and saw a harmless animal in the field. It was a small, grey creature. Tim was very excited and wanted to bring it home. He carefully crept closer to get a better look. But the creature saw him and quickly ran away. Tim decided that he needed something to help him catch it. He remembered that his mum had a pointy stick in the garden. He raced to get it and ran back to the field. Carefully, Tim crept up to the creature. He reached out with the stick, being very gentle and quiet. Suddenly, the creature jumped onto the stick and Tim knew it was time to bring it home. Tim was very happy to have found a harmless new friend. He named the creature 'Spot' and they became best friends. From that day on, Tim and Spot roamed their field together, happy as could be.

Once upon a time, there was a blue bird named Bob. Bob liked to fly and play with his friends. One day, Bob found a card on the ground. The card had a picture of a big, blue ball on it. Bob showed the card to his friend, Cat. Cat said, "Wow! I like the blue ball. Let's leave the card here and look for a blue ball to play with." Bob agreed, and they left the card under a tree. Bob and Cat looked everywhere for a blue ball. They asked their friends, "Have you seen a blue ball?" Finally, they found a blue ball near a pond. They played with the blue ball all day and had lots of fun. At the end of the day, they went back to the tree and found the card. They were happy they had found the blue ball and had a great day together.

Once there was a little boy named Joe. He wanted to join a club, but he did not know which one was right for him. Joe asked his mom if he could join a club, but she said no. Joe was sad and a little mad. He did not understand why he could not join a club. Joe asked his dad if he could join a club, but his dad also said no. Joe was even more sad and mad. He still did not understand why he could not join a club. One day, Joe saw a sign outside that said: Unknown Club. Joe asked his dad if he could join the Unknown Club and his dad finally said yes. Joe was so excited! He rushed inside and joined the Unknown Club. Joe was in the club for many hours and he had so much fun. He was so glad that his mom and dad let him join the Unknown Club.

Once there was a boy called Jack. He was very active, but he always liked to try new games. One day he decided to play the game of hide and seek. He ran and he hid behind a tall bench. It was so high that Jack could barely see the top of it. Jack waited and waited but no one came to find him. After some time Jack decided to find a better hiding spot. He scouted out many places, but every time Jack opened a door it shut before he had any chance to go in. He jumped onto a tall tree, but even if he could reach the top, the branches were too thin to support his weight. The more Jack tried to find a hidden place to hide, the more frustrated he became. Then, it dawned on Jack that he had been inside a perfect place to hide the whole time. He enthusiastically ran back to the bench and jumped back behind it. He was thrilled to be hidden once again. When Jack's friends finally came to look for him, he was found right away. Jack laughed and laughed. He had enjoyed the game so much and was sure he would do an even better job of hiding the next time.
