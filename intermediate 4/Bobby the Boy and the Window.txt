Category: Intermediate
Title: Bobby the Boy and the Window

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Bobby who liked to play outside. On this day, Bobby was very frustrated because it had been raining all week and he couldn't go outside. He sat inside and watched the rain pitter-patter on the window. Bobby thought and thought and soon came up with a plan. He went to the closet and took out an umbrella. "I can use this to keep dry!" Bobby said. Bobby ran outside with the umbrella and opened it up. He jumped in a big puddle and clicked his feet in the water. Bobby was so happy to be outside that he didn't care how he looked. Soon, Bobby's mommy came outside. "Bobby!" She said. "What are you doing? You look all wet and dry." She said with a disappointed look on her face. "Mommy," Bobby said, "I wanted to have fun, so I used my umbrella to stay dry in style." His mommy shook her head. "Bobby," she said, "you have to think of the consequences of your actions. You can't just do things without thinking. Now you're going to have to go and change into some dry clothes." Bobby was frustrated as he went inside. He had thought his plan was a good one, but mommy had shown him the importance of being wise before taking action.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a pretty bow in her hair. Lucy wanted to play outside, but she was fearful of the big dog next door. One day, Lucy was playing with her ball when it went into the dog's yard. She was scared, but she knew that she had to get her ball back. Lucy took a deep breath and went into the yard. The big dog saw Lucy and barked loudly. Lucy was so scared, but she didn't want to be punished for not getting her ball. She picked up her ball and showed the dog her pretty bow. The dog stopped barking and looked at the bow. Lucy slowly walked back to her yard, and the dog did not chase her. From that day on, Lucy was not fearful of the big dog anymore.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the farm with his mom. There, he saw a big, hairy cow. Tim had never seen a cow before. He asked his mom, "What is that?" His mom smiled and said, "That is a cow, Tim. Cows give us milk." She tried to explain how cows make milk for us to drink. Tim was very curious and wanted to know more. As they walked around the farm, Tim saw more cows. He saw a baby cow and a mommy cow. Tim asked, "Do all cows have hair?" His mom said, "Yes, cows have hair to keep them warm." Tim was happy to learn about cows and their hair. He loved the farm and the big, hairy cows.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a sad little girl named Lily. She always wore a pretty blue dress. Lily loved to watch the sunrise every morning. The colors in the sky made her smile. One day, Lily met a boy named Tom. Tom was sad too. He wore a red hat all the time. Lily asked Tom why he was sad. Tom said he had lost his favorite toy. Lily decided to help Tom find his toy. Together, they searched the village and found the toy under a big tree. Tom was happy and thanked Lily. They watched the sunrise together and became best friends. From that day, Lily and Tom were not sad anymore. They learned that helping others can make you happy too.

Tim and Lily were playing in the pool with their toys. They had a boat, a ball, and a plane. Tim liked the plane the best. He made it fly in the air and land on the water. Lily liked the boat the best. She made it sail across the pool and bump into the ball. "Look at my plane!" Tim said. "It can go fast and high!" "Look at my boat!" Lily said. "It can go far and smooth!" They both wanted to show off their toys. They did not want to share. They started to splash each other with water. They got wet and angry. "Stop splashing me!" Tim said. "You are ruining my plane!" "Stop splashing me!" Lily said. "You are ruining my boat!" They splashed more and more. They did not have fun. They had a wild fight. Mom came to the pool. She saw Tim and Lily splashing and yelling. She was not happy. She took their toys away. "Tim and Lily, you are being naughty!" Mom said. "You need to learn to share and play nice. No more toys for you today!" Tim and Lily felt sad. They realized they were wrong. They said sorry to each other and to Mom. "Mom, we are sorry," Tim said. "We will share and play nice." "Mom, we are sorry," Lily said. "We will share and play nice." Mom smiled. She gave them a hug. She said they could have their toys back tomorrow. "Good," Mom said. "Sharing and playing nice are important. They make you happy and friendly." Tim and Lily nodded. They learned their lesson. They decided to play together with the boat, the ball, and the plane. They had fun. They made peace.
