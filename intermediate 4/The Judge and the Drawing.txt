Category: Intermediate
Title: The Judge and the Drawing

Once upon a time, in a nice little town, there lived a friendly judge. The judge liked to help people complete their tasks. One day, a little girl named Lily needed help. Lily said, "Judge, I need to complete my drawing for school. Can you help me?" The judge smiled and said, "Of course, Lily! Let's do it together." They worked together and finished the drawing. Lily was very happy and thanked the judge. From that day on, they were good friends and helped each other complete many tasks.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear. The bear lived in a big, beautiful forest. He was a very wealthy bear because he had lots of yummy food to eat and a big cave to sleep in. One day, the bear went for a walk in the forest. He saw his friend, the rabbit. The rabbit was laughing at the bear. The bear felt embarrass. He didn't know why the rabbit was laughing at him. Then, the bear looked down and saw that he had a big hole in his pants! He didn't know how it happened. The bear was sad, but then he had an idea. He went back to his big cave and found a new pair of pants to wear. The bear was happy again and went back to play with his friend, the rabbit. The rabbit was happy too, and they played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a small box with a lock on it. Inside the box, she kept her favorite toy, a soft and cuddly bear named Benny. Lucy loved Benny very much and played with him every day. One day, Lucy's mom saw that Benny was hurt. His arm was torn and stuffing was coming out. Lucy's mom knew just what to do. She took Benny and gave him a gentle massage to make him feel better. Then, she sewed up his arm and made it all better. Lucy was so happy to see Benny fixed. She hugged him tight and put him back in the locked box to keep him safe. From that day on, Lucy took extra care of Benny and made sure he never got hurt again. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a woman went to the park. She looked troubled. She had a big bag with her. She sat on a bench and looked around. A little girl saw the woman and walked up to her. "Why are you sad?" asked the little girl. The woman looked at her and said, "I lost my cat. I came to the park to find her." The little girl wanted to help. They looked all around the park for the cat. Then, they heard a sound. They looked up and saw the cat in a tree! The woman was so happy! The little girl said, "I have an idea!" She ran to get a ball and threw it at the tree. The cat jumped down and ran to the woman. The woman was not troubled anymore. She thanked the little girl for her help. They all played in the park together and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a cat named Tom and a mouse named Jerry. Tom always wanted to catch Jerry, but Jerry was too fast. One day, Jerry found a big bottle of sauce in the kitchen. He thought it was important because it smelled so good. Jerry wanted to taste the sauce, but Tom saw him and tried to catch him. Tom warned Jerry, "Don't touch the sauce!" But Jerry didn't listen. He wanted to taste it so badly. As Jerry tried to get the sauce, Tom chased him around the house. In the end, Jerry got away and Tom couldn't catch him. They both learned that it was important to listen to warnings and to share with each other. From that day on, Tom and Jerry became friends and shared the sauce together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a girl named Anna. She loved to paint and play with chalk. One sunny day, she went outside to draw on the sidewalk. Anna saw her friend, Tom, walking by. She said, "Hi, Tom! Do you want to paint with me?" Tom smiled and said, "Yes, I love to paint!" They both picked up their chalk and started to draw. Anna was very organized. She put her chalk in a neat line by color. They painted a big, beautiful rainbow on the sidewalk. They were so happy and proud of their work. Their friends saw the rainbow and came over to play too. They all had a fun day painting with chalk.
