Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Frog and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a big, white swan. She lived in a pond with her baby swans. One day, a rude frog came to the pond. The frog was very loud and did not say nice things to the swan family. The momma swan said, "Please be nice to my babies. We all want to live here and be happy." The frog just laughed and said, "No, I like to be loud and rude!" One day, the momma swan had an idea. She asked her babies to count how many times the rude frog was loud. The baby swans started to count. One, two, three... they counted all day. The next day, the momma swan told the rude frog, "My babies counted how many times you were loud. You were loud one hundred times!" The frog was very surprised. He didn't know he was being so loud. The rude frog started to feel bad. He said, "I am sorry, swan family. I didn't know I was being so loud. I will try to be nice from now on." From that day on, the frog was nice and the pond was a happy place for everyone to live.

Mira was an independent girl. She liked to do things by herself. She helped her mom in the kitchen. She washed the dishes and cut the onion. She was careful not to hurt her fingers. "Good job, Mira!" her mom said. "You are a big helper. Now we can make soup for dinner." Mira smiled. She loved soup. She put the onion in a pot with water and salt. She turned on the stove. She waited for the soup to boil. She saw her teddy bear on the couch. She wanted to play with him. She forgot about the soup. She ran to the couch and hugged her teddy bear. She told him stories and sang him songs. She did not hear her mom calling her. She did not smell the smoke. She did not see the fire. The onion burned in the pot. The pot was black. The kitchen was full of smoke. "Mira! Mira! Come here!" her mom shouted. She ran to the kitchen. She saw the fire. She was scared. She grabbed Mira and ran outside. She called the firemen. "Mira, what did you do?" her mom asked. She was angry and worried. "I'm sorry, mom," Mira said. She was sad and sorry. "I forgot about the soup. I wanted to play with teddy." "Mira, you have to be careful. You can't forget about the stove. It is dangerous. You could have hurt yourself and the house." "I know, mom. I'm sorry. I won't forget again. I love you, mom." "I love you too, Mira. But next time, please ask me before you use the stove. You are independent, but you are still a little girl. You need my help sometimes." "OK, mom. I will. Can we still have soup for dinner?" "Maybe. But not onion soup. We have to buy a new onion. And a new pot."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a very comfortable cushion. She liked to sit on it and read her books. The cushion was soft, and it made her feel happy. One day, Lily could not find her cushion. She looked under her bed, but it was not there. She looked in the closet, but it was not there either. Lily felt sad and did not know what to do. She saw her big brother, Tom, and asked, "Have you seen my cushion?" Tom just shrugged. He did not know where it was. Lily and Tom looked all over the house for the cushion. Finally, they found it in the living room. It was under the big chair. Lily was so happy to have her comfortable cushion back. She sat on it and read her books with a big smile on her face. Tom was happy too, because he helped his sister find her special cushion.

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and jump all day. One day, Spot's friend, a wise cat named Whiskers, came to play. Whiskers wanted to introduce Spot to something new called yoga. Whiskers showed Spot how to do yoga. They stretched their legs and arms. They bent their bodies like a rainbow. Spot found that yoga was fun and made him feel good. Spot and Whiskers did yoga together every day. Soon, other animals saw Spot and Whiskers doing yoga. They wanted to learn too. Spot and Whiskers taught the other animals how to do yoga. All the animals became friends and felt happy. The moral of the story is that trying new things can bring friends together and make everyone happy.
