Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Window

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy lived in a small house with a big window. She loved to look out the window every day. One day, Amy saw a tiny seed on the ground outside. She picked it up and put it in a light brown pot. She put the pot near the window and gave it water. She hoped the seed would grow into a pretty flower. After some days, the seed started to grow. It grew bigger and bigger. One day, Amy saw a beautiful flower bloom. It was light pink and made her very happy. She loved to watch the flower from her window every day.

One day, a little seal was playing in the sea. The seal was very healthy and liked to swim fast. The seal had a friend, a big fish. They liked to play together. The seal and the fish had a race. "Let's see who is faster," said the seal. They swam as fast as they could. The seal was winning, but then a big wave came. The wave pushed the seal far away from the fish. The seal was scared and lost. The seal called for help, "Where are you, big fish?" The big fish heard the seal and swam very fast to find the seal. The big fish found the seal and said, "Don't be scared, I am here." The seal and the big fish swam back together. They were happy to be together again. From that day on, they always played close to each other, so they would not get lost.

One day, a little dog named Max was very tired. He wanted to relax. He found a big, soft bed and lay down. Max felt very happy and comfy. Max's friend, a cat named Lily, came to play. "Max, are you ready to play?" Lily asked. Max said, "Not now, I want to relax. Let's play later." Lily said, "Okay, Max. Have a nice rest." After Max woke up, he felt ready to play. He and Lily played and had fun. They found a treat to share. They both ate the treat and felt happy. Max and Lily were good friends who loved to play and relax together.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Mary. Mary had a pretty blouse that she loved to wear. One day, Mary was upset because she could not find her blouse. She looked and looked but it was nowhere to be seen. Little did she know that her blouse was going to cause her some trouble later on. One day, a boy named Tom asked Mary to marry him. Mary was so happy and said yes. They planned a big party for their friends and family. Mary wanted to wear her pretty blouse to the party, but she still could not find it. She was very sad but did not tell anyone. On the day of the party, Mary saw her friend Sue wearing her pretty blouse. Mary was so upset that she ran away from the party and never came back. Tom was left all alone, and everyone was sad. The pretty blouse had caused Mary to lose her chance at happiness.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside in the sun. One day, she noticed that the leaves on the trees were changing colors. It was fall, and the leaves were red, orange, and yellow. Lily thought it was important to show her friends the pretty leaves. So, she called her friends, Max and Bella, to come over and play. They had fun running and jumping in the big piles of leaves. They laughed and played all day long. As the sun began to set, Lily, Max, and Bella knew it was time to go home. They said goodbye and went back to their houses. They couldn't wait for the next fall day to play together again.

Once upon a time, in a busy little town, there was a round ball. The round ball loved to roll around and play with the kids. One day, the round ball saw a big pile of sand in a yard. The round ball thought it would be fun to roll in the sand. But, the sand pile was not for playing. The sand was for a man who was working very hard. The man did not want anyone to disturb his sand. The round ball did not know this and it rolled into the sand. The man saw the round ball and was not happy. The man picked up the round ball and threw it far away. The round ball was sad and could not play with the kids anymore. The round ball should have asked before playing in the sand. Now, the round ball is far away and the kids miss their friend.
