Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Toys

One day, a little girl named Lucy was playing with her toys. She was very happy and enthusiastic. Lucy loved to fold her toy clothes and put them away in her toy chest. Her mom called out, "Lucy, come help me in the attic!" Lucy went up to the attic and saw her mom folding old clothes. "Can you help me fold these clothes?" her mom asked. Lucy nodded and began to fold the clothes with her mom. As they folded the clothes, Lucy's mom told her stories about the people who wore them. Lucy learned that it was important to take care of things and share them with others. She felt happy and proud to help her mom. When they finished folding the clothes, Lucy's mom said, "Thank you, Lucy. You did a great job!" Lucy smiled and knew that helping others and sharing was a good thing to do. The moral of the story is to always help others and share what you have.

Once upon a time, there was a pastel. The pastel was very dangerous. It could draw things that came to life. One day, a little girl found the pastel in her room. She did not know it was dangerous. The little girl drew a big cat with the pastel. The cat came to life! At first, the girl was happy. She played with the cat. But soon, the cat became too big and too dangerous to play with. The girl wanted the cat to quit being so big and dangerous. Then, something unexpected happened. The cat started to draw with the pastel. It drew a tiny, friendly cat. The big, dangerous cat became small and nice. The girl and the cat played together and had lots of fun. The girl learned to be careful with the dangerous pastel.

Lisa was a big girl who liked to hug her friends. She hugged them when they were happy, when they were sad, or when they said hello or goodbye. She thought hugs were the best way to show love. One day, she went to the park with her mom and saw a new girl sitting on a bench. She had long, smooth hair and a pretty dress, but she looked lonely and bored. Lisa wanted to hug her and make her smile, but she was shy. She asked her mom, "Can I hug her, mom?" Her mom smiled and said, "Why don't you ask her first? Maybe she has a message for you." Lisa nodded and walked to the bench. She said, "Hi, I'm Lisa. What's your name?" The new girl looked up and said, "I'm Amy. I'm new here. I don't have any friends." Lisa felt sorry for Amy. She said, "I can be your friend. Do you like hugs?" Amy nodded and said, "Yes, I do. But I don't get many hugs. My dad is away and my mom is busy." Lisa said, "That's sad. I have a message for you. Hugs are good for you. They make you feel warm and happy. Do you want a hug?" Amy smiled and said, "Yes, please." Lisa hugged Amy and felt her smooth hair. Amy hugged Lisa and felt her soft shirt. They both felt warm and happy. They became friends and played in the park. The moral of the story is: Hugs are a simple way to show love and make friends.

Once upon a time, there was a colorful bird named Bobo. He lived in a big tree near a trail. One day, he met another bird named Lulu. Lulu was also colorful. They liked to play together. One day, they had a game. They wanted to see who could match the most things. Bobo found a red flower and said, "Look, I found a red flower. It matches my red feathers!" Lulu found a blue rock and said, "I found a blue rock. It matches my blue feathers!" But then, they saw a big rainbow in the sky. It had all their colors! They both wanted to match the rainbow. Bobo said, "I want to match the rainbow!" Lulu said, "No, I want to match the rainbow!" They did not know what to do. They asked a wise old owl for help. The owl said, "You can both match the rainbow. You are both colorful birds. Play together and share the rainbow." Bobo and Lulu were happy. They played together under the big rainbow and were friends forever.
