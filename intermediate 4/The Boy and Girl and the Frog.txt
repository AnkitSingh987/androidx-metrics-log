Category: Intermediate
Title: The Boy and Girl and the Frog

One day, a boy and a girl went to play near a creek. The creek had large rocks around it. They liked to jump from rock to rock. The water in the creek was very clear. The boy saw a large frog near the water. He said to the girl, "Look at that big frog! Let's try to catch it!" The girl was scared, but the boy urged her, "Come on, it will be fun!" They tried to catch the frog, but it jumped away. They kept chasing it until they were very far from the creek. Then, they got lost in the woods. They were sad and scared because they could not find their way back home.

One day, a brown dog named Spot wanted to have a parade. He asked his friend, a cat named Fluffy, "Will you help me decorate for the parade?" Fluffy said, "Yes, I will help you, Spot!" They used pretty colors to decorate the street. They were very excited. Many friends came to see the parade. Spot and Fluffy walked and danced in the parade. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew away all the decorations. Everyone was sad. Spot said, "Don't be sad, friends. We can still have fun!" All the friends played games and laughed. The parade was still fun, even without the decorations.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Mark. He was a gifted child who loved to polish his shoes. Every day, he would sit outside and make his shoes shine. One day, a little bird came to visit him. The bird said, "Hello, Mark! You are very good at making your shoes shine. Can you help me polish my nest?" Mark was happy to help the bird. He took a soft cloth and gently polished the bird's nest until it was shiny and clean. The bird was very thankful and said, "Thank you, Mark! Now my nest looks beautiful." From that day on, Mark and the little bird became best friends. They spent their days together, polishing things and making them look nice. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Amy was playing in her room. She heard a noise in the hall. She was curious and went to see what it was. When she walked into the hall, she saw her mom with a big box. Amy felt a little uncomfortable because she didn't know what was in the box. Amy asked her mom, "What's in the box?" Her mom smiled and said, "It's a surprise for you!" Amy was so excited. She couldn't wait to see what was inside. Her mom opened the box, and Amy was so surprised to see a new toy inside. It was a big, soft teddy bear. She hugged her new teddy bear and felt happy. Her uncomfortable feeling was gone. Amy and her teddy bear played together all day in the hall.

Lily and Ben are playing in the park. They see a big bird on a tree. Lily wants to take a picture of the bird. She has a camera that her dad gave her. She is very careful with the camera. "Ben, look at the bird! It is so pretty!" Lily says. "I want to take a picture of it. Can you help me?" Ben nods. He likes the bird too. He holds Lily's hand and they walk closer to the tree. They are very quiet. They do not want to scare the bird. Lily holds the camera and points it at the bird. She presses the button. But nothing happens. The camera does not make a sound. The camera does not take a picture. Lily looks at the camera. She sees a red light. The camera has no power. The camera needs new batteries. Lily is sad. She wants to take a picture of the bird. She looks at Ben. She has an idea. "Ben, can you go and ask mom for new batteries?" Lily says. "Please, Ben. I really want to take a picture of the bird." Ben shakes his head. He does not want to go. He wants to stay and watch the bird. He likes the bird too. "No, Lily. I do not want to go. You go and ask mom for new batteries." Ben says. "It is your camera. You go." Lily is angry. She does not like Ben's answer. She wants Ben to help her. She wants Ben to be nice. "Ben, you have to go and ask mom for new batteries!" Lily says. "I am your sister. You have to do what I say. I demand it!" Ben is angry too. He does not like Lily's tone. He does not like Lily's demand. He wants Lily to be polite. "Lily, you do not have to yell at me. You do not have to demand anything." Ben says. "You have to ask nicely. You have to say please and thank you." Lily and Ben start to argue. They raise their voices. They forget about the bird. The bird hears them. The bird does not like the noise. The bird flies away. Lily and Ben stop arguing. They look at the tree. They see no bird. They miss the bird. They look at each other. They feel sorry. They say sorry. They hug each other. They decide to share the camera. They decide to be careful with the camera. They decide to be nice to each other. They go and ask mom for new batteries. They say please and thank you. Mom gives them new batteries. They put them in the camera. They look for another bird. They find one. They take a picture of it. They are happy.
