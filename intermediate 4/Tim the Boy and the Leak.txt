Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Leak

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a smart kid who loved to solve problems. One day, Tim found a leak in his house. Water was coming out of a pipe. He thought, "I must fix this leak!" Tim tried to stop the leak with his hands, but it did not work. He felt a bit stupid. Then, he saw a big toy car. He had an idea. He put the car under the leak to catch the water. Tim thought he had solved the problem, but then something unexpected happened. The toy car filled up with water and started to float. It floated out of the house and down the street. Tim followed the car, amazed. Suddenly, the car hit a rock and the water came out. The leak was gone! Tim learned that sometimes, even when we feel stupid, we can still solve problems in a new and fun way.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved her soft, warm blanket very much. Every night, she would choose her blanket to sleep with. One day, Lily found a little bird outside her house. The bird was not moving. She knew the bird was dead. She felt sad for the bird and wanted to help. Lily took her blanket and wrapped the bird in it. She made a nice, warm bed for the bird. The next day, she saw the bird was still dead. But she knew she did a good thing by giving the bird her blanket. And that made her feel happy.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the kitchen. He wanted to get ice cream from the freezer. But the freezer door was very hard to open. Tim started to struggle with the door. He pulled and pulled, but it would not open. Then, Tim's mom came into the kitchen. She saw Tim trying to open the freezer. "What are you doing, Tim?" she asked. "I want ice cream, but the door is stuck!" Tim said. His mom smiled and said, "Let me help you." Tim's mom opened the freezer door with a big pull. Tim was glad that his mom helped him. They took out the ice cream and ate it together. Tim said, "Thank you, Mom!" And they both enjoyed their ice cream treat.

Once upon a time, there was a brave bear who lived in the woods. One day, while he was walking around, he saw a delicious berry. He got excited and wanted to try it. “I want to eat that berry,” he said. But then another bear appeared and told him that the berry is dangerous. “No, no! Don’t eat it,” said the other bear. “It’s dangerous” The brave bear was disappointed but he listened to the other bear and decided not to eat the berry. He knew that it was better to be safe than sorry. The End.

One day, a girl named Lily found a pretty bracelet on the ground. It was shiny and original. She put it on her wrist and smiled. Lily saw a small spray bottle nearby. She picked it up and filled it with water. She wanted to show her friends her new bracelet and play with the spray bottle. She went to the park and found her friends. They all loved her original bracelet and had fun spraying water at each other. They laughed and played until the sun went down.

Lily and Tom were best friends. They liked to play with toys, draw pictures and read books. One day, they found a big box in the attic. It was full of old things that belonged to their grandma. "Look, Tom, what is this?" Lily asked, holding a shiny thing with a button. "It is a camera, Lily. You can use it to take photos. Like this." Tom said, pressing the button. The camera made a click sound and a photo came out. "Wow, that is cool! Can I try?" Lily said, taking the camera from Tom. "Sure, but be careful. The camera is fragile. It can break easily." Tom said, handing her the camera. Lily smiled and pointed the camera at Tom. She pressed the button and another photo came out. She looked at it and saw Tom's face. He was making a funny face and sticking out his tongue. "Ha ha, you look silly, Tom!" Lily said, laughing. "Let me see, let me see!" Tom said, reaching for the photo. But Lily was too fast. She ran away with the photo, giggling. "Come back, Lily! Give me the photo!" Tom said, chasing her. They ran around the attic, dodging the old things. They had fun, but they did not see the loose board on the floor. Lily stepped on it and tripped. She fell down and dropped the camera and the photo. "Oh no, Lily, are you okay?" Tom said, running to her. Lily was not hurt, but she was scared. She saw the camera on the floor. It was broken. The photo was torn. "I'm sorry, Tom. I broke the camera. I tore the photo. I was not careful." Lily said, crying. Tom hugged her and said, "It's okay, Lily. Don't cry. I'm not mad at you. I trust you. You are my best friend." "Really? You are not mad?" Lily said, wiping her tears. "Really. I'm not mad. The camera and the photo are not important. You are important. I trust you." Tom said, smiling. "Thank you, Tom. You are a good friend. I trust you too." Lily said, smiling back. They hugged again and said, "We are best friends forever."
