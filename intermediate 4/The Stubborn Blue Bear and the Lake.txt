Category: Intermediate
Title: The Stubborn Blue Bear and the Lake

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn blue bear. He liked to do things his own way and never wanted to listen. One day, he went on an adventure to a far away land. Everywhere he went, he saw happy smiles. He would not smile though, because he was too stubborn. He continued his journey, but he found himself getting more and more tired. After a while, he saw something blue in the distance. He was so excited, and he smiled! As he got closer, he saw a beautiful lake. He went to the lake and admired its blueness and peacefulness. He decided to take a rest, so he sat and watched the lake while the soft breeze made him smile again. Smiling made him feel better, and he even started to feel happy. He enjoyed himself so much that he decided to stay a while and not be so stubborn. The bear jumped in the lake and explored the beautiful blue place with his new friends. From that day on, he embraced every adventure with a smile.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was a very independent girl. She loved to do things all by herself. One sunny day, Mia went to the park with her mom. She saw a big tree with pretty flowers. Mia wanted to pick some flowers for her mom. She found a small basket near the tree. Mia put the flowers in the basket. She was very happy with her pretty flowers. She wanted to show her mom how independent she was. Mia walked back to her mom and offered her the basket of flowers. Her mom was so happy and proud. Mia learned that being independent and helping others can make everyone happy.

Tom and his loyal dog Spot like to play in the garden. They have a hose that can spray water. They spray the flowers, the grass and each other. They laugh and run and have fun. One day, they see a frog in the pond. The frog is green and slimy. It has big eyes and a long tongue. Tom and Spot want to play with the frog. They go near the pond and say hello. "Hello, frog. Do you want to play with us?" Tom asks. The frog does not answer. It jumps into the water and hides. Tom and Spot wait for the frog to come out. They are curious and friendly. They see the frog's head pop out of the water. They smile and wave. The frog opens its mouth and sprays water at them. Tom and Spot are surprised and wet. They blink and shake their heads. "Why did you do that, frog? That was not nice!" Tom says. The frog does not say sorry. It sprays water at them again. Tom and Spot are angry and sad. They do not want to play with the frog anymore. They turn around and walk away. "Come on, Spot. Let's go play somewhere else. The frog is not a good friend." Tom says. Spot barks and follows Tom. They are loyal to each other. They find a new game to play in the garden. They forget about the frog. They are happy again.

One day, a cheerful bunny named Bobo was hopping in the woods. He saw a hoop on the ground. Bobo thought it would be fun to play with the hoop. "Hi, hoop! Let's play," said Bobo. He put his front paws on the hoop and started to spring up and down. The hoop began to roll, and Bobo chased after it. They were having so much fun! As they played, the hoop rolled into a big hole. Bobo was sad. He didn't know how to get the hoop out. Then, a wise old owl came by and said, "Bobo, if you work together with your friends, you can get the hoop out of the hole." Bobo called his friends, and they all worked together to get the hoop out. They were happy and played with the hoop again. The moral of the story is that working together with friends can help you solve problems.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a cart that he loved to play with. He would push the cart fast around his house and yard. His cart was his favorite toy. One day, Tim saw a big dog in his yard. The dog was playing with Tim's cart. The dog was biting it and pushing it around. Tim was very sad. He did not want the dog to break his cart. He felt hate for the dog for trying to ruin his toy. Tim ran to his mom and told her what was happening. His mom went outside and told the dog to go away. The dog left, and Tim's cart was safe. Tim was happy again. He played with his cart fast and had fun.
