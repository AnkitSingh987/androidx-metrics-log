Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Bald Man and the Laser

One day, a bald man named Tom found a big toy. It was a laser. Tom liked the laser a lot. He played with it every day. The laser made pretty lights. Tom was happy. Tom had a friend named Jim. Jim came to play with Tom. They played with the laser together. They made the lights go all around the room. They laughed and had fun. One day, they found a button on the laser. The button said "reverse". They pushed the button. The laser changed. The lights went back. Tom and Jim were surprised. They played with the laser more. They liked the new game. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a nice dog named Spot. Spot loved to play near the water. One day, he saw a raft by the water. Spot was excited and wanted to go on the raft. Spot tried to lift the raft into the water, but it was too heavy. He tried and tried, but he could not lift it. Spot felt sad. He wished he could find a way to get the raft into the water. The next day, Spot went to the water again. This time, he saw a big stick. He had an idea! Spot used the stick to push the raft into the water. Spot was happy that he could finally play on the raft. It was a fun day for Spot, and he learned that sometimes, you just need a little help.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily loved to make things better. She wanted to improve everything around her. One day, she saw her mom feeling helpless. Her mom had a bottle of perfume that she loved, but it was almost empty. Lily wanted to help her mom. She thought, "I can make more perfume for her!" So, she went to the garden and picked many pretty flowers. She put the flowers in a big bowl and mixed them with water. She worked hard to make the best perfume ever. When Lily was done, she gave the new perfume to her mom. Her mom smiled and hugged her. She was so happy! Lily felt proud that she could improve her mom's day. They both smelled the sweet perfume and felt very happy together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Paul. He liked to go outside and collect things. One day, he found a pie! He was really excited, and he carefully picked it up. He brought it back to his house and showed it to his mom. "Mommy, I found a pie!" he said, holding it up and smiling big. "That's great, Paul!" she said, "Let's cut it up and see how it tastes." Paul and his mom carefully cut the pie into four clean slices. They took a bite at the same time. It was the most delicious pie Paul had ever tasted! He was so happy that he wanted to go out and collect more pies. His mom said, "Go ahead, Paul! Just make sure to stay clean while you're looking." So, Paul went out and collected as many pies as he could carry. He and his mom ate lots of delicious pies that day. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Bob. Bob was a loyal dog. Every day, Bob would jog with his friend, Tom. Tom was a boy. They liked to jog to the market. One day, while they were jogging, Bob saw a cat. Bob liked to chase cats. He forgot about Tom and ran after the cat. Tom was sad and went to the market alone. At the market, Tom looked for Bob but could not find him. Tom went home and cried. Bob never came back. Tom was very sad and missed his loyal friend.

Mommy and Daddy took Johnny on a camping trip. They decided to stay the night and put up their tent. Mommy and Daddy worked together to attach the tent. First, they spread out the orange fabric. Then, Daddy grabbed the poles and Mommy helped him attach them to the tent. Finally, the tent was ready! Johnny was so excited and ran inside to explore. He stretched his arms wide and said "This is MY tent!" Mommy smiled and said, "Yes, this is your tent for the night. Let's all make our beds and get ready for bed." Johnny was so excited to sleep in his orange tent. He ran out and grabbed his sleeping bag and quickly made his own bed. He couldn't wait to see what the night had in store.
