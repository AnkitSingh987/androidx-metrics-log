Title: Sally the Girl and the Orders

Once, there was a little girl named Sally. She was three years old and very spoiled. Sally was always making orders at home. One day, her parents put their foot down and said that she had to stop making orders. Sally didn't like this and she stuck out her nose. Sally's dad said to her, "Sally, your orders have to stop. You can't always get what you want." Sally was very sad. She said, "But why can't I order whatever I want?" Sally's mom said, "Sweetheart, being spoiled means that you need to learn to be content with what you have. Sometimes, ordering too much doesn't make us happy." Sally thought about what her mom said and decided to try to be content with what she had. She hugged her mom and dad and smiled. She knew that it was better to be content than to always be ordering more and more.

Bob was playing with his favourite toy, a flute. He was trying to count how many notes he could play. Suddenly, his friend Debbie came over. Debbie saw the beautiful flute and asked if she could play it. "No!" said Bob. "It’s my flute!" Debbie was sad. She asked, "Please can I just play one note?" Bob thought for a moment. He said, "Okay, you can count to three, and then you must give it back to me." Debbie placed the flute to her lips and started counting. She counted one, two and on three, she played the most beautiful note Bob had ever heard. Bob smiled. He said, "That was so beautiful, can you play just one more?" Debbie smiled. She counted to three and played the same beautiful note again. Bob and Debbie were both very happy. They decided to play the flute together and share their music with the world.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She heard that there was a movie she wanted to watch, so she asked her mom to take her to the theatre. But when they arrived, the theatre was too hot. Sue started to cry and said, "It's too hot, mommy!" Her mom said, "Don't worry, baby. Let's stretch our arms and legs to make the theatre cooler." So Sue and her mom stretched and it felt good! Suddenly, the theatre was cool enough for them to enjoy the movie. After the movie, Sue learned an important lesson. With the help of her mom, she figured out that stretching can help when things are hot. The moral of this story is that with a little stretch, we can solve any problem.

Once upon a time there was a girl called Molly. Molly really liked making things. One day she was crafting something special on her shelf when her brother, Kevin, decided to try and grab it. Molly was very cross and shouted "no!" Kevin said to her, "Molly, why don't you tell me what it is?" Molly shook her head and said, "It's a surprise... you'll just have to wait." Kevin was not very happy and he told her, "Come on, Molly! I just want to see it. Tell me what it is!" Molly said, "No! You have to be patient. If I tell you, it won't be a surprise anymore." Kevin looked very unhappy. Suddenly, an idea popped into Molly's head. She smiled and said, "I know! I'll give you a hint. It's very cheerful and it's going to make someone very happy." Kevin thought for a moment and then he said, "Oh! I know what it is now!" Molly smiled and said, "That's what happens when you wait patiently. It's much more fun that way!"

Once upon a time, there was a blue boat. The boat had a big sail. The boat liked to go fast in the water. One sunny day, the blue boat saw a little boy on the beach. The little boy was sad. The blue boat wanted to help him. So, the boat went close to the beach and shouted, "Hi, little boy! Do you want to sail with me?" The little boy heard the shout and looked at the blue boat. He smiled and said, "Yes, I want to sail with you!" The little boy got on the blue boat, and they sailed away. They had a fun day together, and the little boy was not sad anymore.
