Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Book

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to read books. She had many books with many pages. But her books were all mixed up. She wanted to sort them to make it easy to find her favorite ones. One day, Lucy had an idea. She would sort her books by color. She put all the red books together, all the blue books together, and all the green books together. Now, it was easy to find her favorite books with her favorite color pages. The next day, Lucy went to her red book pile to read her favorite story. When she opened the book, she saw that the pages were not red like she thought. They were yellow! Lucy had sorted her books by the color of the cover, not the color of the pages. But now, she knew how to sort her books even better. She sorted them by the color of the pages, and it was easy to find her favorite stories. And they all lived happily ever after.

Tom and Anna were playing in the kitchen. They saw a big yellow fruit on the table. Tom wanted to try it. He picked it up and bit it. "Ow!" he said. "This fruit is sour and hurts my mouth!" He spit it out and started to cry. Anna saw Tom crying and felt sorry for him. She knew the fruit was a lemon. She had seen Mom use it to make juice. She said to Tom, "Don't cry, Tom. That fruit is not for eating. It is for making juice. Here, have some water. It will make you feel better." She gave Tom a glass of water and hugged him. Tom drank the water and stopped crying. He said to Anna, "Thank you, Anna. You are very helpful and kind. I'm sorry I took the lemon. I didn't know it was sour and hurtful." Anna smiled and said, "It's okay, Tom. I'm glad you are not sad anymore. Do you want to play with me? We can make some juice with the lemon and some sugar. Mom will be happy to see us." Tom nodded and said, "Yes, let's do that. That sounds fun and yummy. You are the best sister ever, Anna." They washed their hands and took the lemon and some sugar to the juicer. They squeezed the lemon and added some sugar and water. They made a pitcher of lemonade and poured some in two cups. They tasted it and smiled. It was sweet and refreshing. They said, "Cheers!" and clinked their cups. They were happy and proud of their juice. They waited for Mom to come home and share it with her.

Once upon a time, there was a little car with four wheels. It liked to drive around and play with its friends. One day, the little car saw a big sign with a picture of a wheel on it. The sign was far away, but the little car wanted to see it up close. The little car went to the distant sign and saw that it had words printed on it. The words were simple: "Be kind and share." The little car thought about the words and decided to share its toys with its friends. It went back to its friends and gave them each a toy. The friends were very happy and they all played together. They took turns with the toys and had lots of fun. The little car learned that sharing made everyone happy. The moral of the story is to be kind and share with others.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play with her toys and eat yummy food. One day, she found an interesting bowl in the kitchen. It was big and blue, with pretty pictures on it. Lily wanted to use the bowl for her snack. She put some sweet fruit inside and went to play. But she forgot to cover the bowl. While she played, a sneaky cat came into the kitchen. The cat saw the bowl and jumped up to eat the fruit. When Lily came back, she saw the cat eating her fruit. She knew she should have covered the bowl. Next time, she will remember to cover her interesting bowl to keep her food safe. And that day, Lily learned a good lesson.
