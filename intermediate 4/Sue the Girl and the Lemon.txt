Title: Sue the Girl and the Lemon

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to eat all kinds of food. One day, she found a big, round fruit. It was a lemon. She did not know what it was, but it looked so fun and bright. Sue took a big bite of the lemon. "Oh no!" she said. The lemon was very sour. Her face went all funny. She did not like the sour lemon. But she wanted to finish it, so she took another bite. The sour taste was still there, but she was brave. Sue saw her friend Tom. She gave him the lemon and said, "You try!" Tom took a bite and made a funny face too. They laughed and laughed. They did not like the sour lemon, but they had fun. They played with the lemon and took turns making funny faces. At the end of the day, they had finished the lemon and had a great time. They went home and told their moms and dads about the sour lemon and the fun they had at the pass.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved strawberries. One day, she went to the park and saw a big, red strawberry. She was very happy. Sue saw a boy named Tom at the park too. Tom was feeling selfish and wanted the strawberry all for himself. Sue and Tom both tried to match who could pick the strawberry first. "Let's share the strawberry," said Sue. But Tom said, "No, I want it all for me!" Sue felt sad, but she had an idea. "If we match and work together, we can find more strawberries for both of us!" Tom thought about it and agreed. So, Sue and Tom worked together and found many strawberries. They shared them with each other and had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a large east wind. It blew and blew and blew. Every day, the wind would howl and moan. The big trees in the east would sway, then sigh in the wind. The wind was so strong, it covered the whole east side of the world. One day, a small girl named Rose noticed the big east wind. She wanted to see what it was all about, so she decided to take a walk to the east side of her town. As she walked, she saw the large trees waving in the wind. She heard them sighing too. Rose smiled, feeling happy and curious. When Rose reached the east side of the town, she was shocked by what she saw. The large east wind was much bigger and stronger than she expected. She thought it felt like a big hug! Rose stood still, feeling the east wind in her hair. She smiled and sighed with the wind. Then she realized how lucky she was to feel the big east wind and went back home, knowing she would never forget it.

Once upon a time, in a big jungle, lived a tiny monkey named Momo. Momo loved to play with his friends. They played games like tag and hide and seek. Momo was very good at playing games. He was fast and smart. One day, Momo met a new friend named Lulu. Lulu was a competitive bird who also loved to play. Momo and Lulu decided to play a game of tag. Momo said, "Lulu, let's play tag. I will be it first." Lulu agreed and they started to play. Momo and Lulu had so much fun playing tag. They ran and jumped all around the jungle. Momo was very fast, but Lulu was faster. At the end of the game, they were both tired but happy. They sat down and talked. Momo said, "Lulu, you are a good friend. Let's play again tomorrow." Lulu smiled and said, "Yes, Momo. I would like that." And from that day on, Momo and Lulu played together every day in the big jungle.

Once upon a time, there was a naughty boy named Tim. He liked to create big messes in the house. One Wednesday, Tim had a new idea. He found a big box of paint and started to paint everywhere. The walls, the floor, and even the dog! Tim was having so much fun being naughty. But then, Tim's mom came home. She saw the big mess and was very sad. Tim felt bad too. The bad ending was that Tim had to clean the whole house. The moral value is: being naughty can make others sad, so be good instead.
