Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bald Man and the Toys

One day, a bald man went to the park. He had a big bag with him. He wanted to unpack his bag and play with his toys. The bald man liked to play with toys even though he was big. In his bag, he found a long chain. The chain was shiny and fun to play with. He swung the chain around and around. The bald man laughed and smiled as he played with his chain. A little girl saw the bald man playing with the chain. She wanted to play too. The bald man shared his chain with the girl. They both had fun playing with the chain. The bald man and the little girl became good friends.

One day, a little bunny named Bob wanted to hop. He hopped all around the yard. He saw a rake lying on the ground. Bob thought it would be fun to hop over the rake. As he hopped, he heard a sad sound. "Why are you so miserable?" Bob asked. It was a little bird named Tim. Tim said, "I can't find my nest. I am lost." Bob wanted to help Tim. They hopped around together, looking for the nest. Then, something unexpected happened. The rake started to move! It stood up and said, "I know where the nest is. I will take you there." The rake took Bob and Tim to the nest. Tim was so happy. He said, "Thank you, Bob and Mr. Rake!" They all became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a cool room. In the room, a little girl named Lily and her doll, Daisy, had fun playing games. One day, they decided to build a big tower with their toy blocks. Lily and Daisy worked together to make the tower taller and taller. They tried to support each other as they put more blocks on top. But then, they saw a big shadow on the wall. It looked like a huge monster! They were scared and stopped building the tower. Suddenly, the shadow moved, and they saw it was just their cat, Fluffy, walking in the room. Lily and Daisy laughed and felt happy again. They finished building the tower, and it was the tallest one they ever made. They learned that sometimes, things that seem scary aren't so scary after all.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to build things with his toy blocks. One day, Tim saw a magazine with a big tower on the cover. He wanted to build a tower just like it. Tim asked his mom, "Can you help me build the tower?" His mom said, "You can do it, Tim. Be independent and try it by yourself." Tim felt brave and started to build the tower with his blocks. As Tim built the tower, it got taller and taller. He was proud of his work. When he finished, he showed his mom the tower. She was happy and said, "Good job, Tim! You did it all by yourself." Tim learned that he could do things on his own if he tried. The moral of the story is to believe in yourself and be independent.

Once upon a time, there was a small group of animals. In this group, there was a dog, a cat, and a bird. They all lived together in a big, happy forest. One day, the dog heard a cry coming from a tree. The dog ran to the tree and saw a baby bird. The baby bird was scared and crying because it fell from its nest. The dog, the cat, and the bird wanted to help the baby bird. The dog used its strong legs to jump up and catch the baby bird. The cat and the bird cheered for the dog. They were all very proud of their teamwork. They took the baby bird back to its nest, and the baby bird stopped crying. The group of animals were happy they could help their friend.

One day, a little girl named Amy found a pair of scissors. She thought they were perfect. Amy wanted to use the scissors to cut pretty shapes from paper. She told her mom, "Look, Mom! I found perfect scissors!" Her mom smiled and said, "Be careful, Amy. Scissors can be sharp." Amy nodded and gazed at the scissors. She knew she had to be careful. The next day, Amy played with her friend, Tim. They used the perfect scissors to cut out shapes. They made a sun, a moon, and stars. Amy remembered her mom's words and was very careful. Tim was careful too. They had lots of fun with the perfect scissors.
