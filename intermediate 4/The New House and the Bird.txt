Category: Intermediate
Title: The New House and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a new house in a big field. The new house was very pretty and had many rooms. One day, it began to rain. The rain came down from the sky and made the field wet. The new house wanted to play with someone, but no one was around. A little bird flew by and saw the new house. The bird said, "Hello, new house! Why are you all alone?" The new house replied, "I don't know. I wish I had a friend to play with on this rainy day." The little bird thought for a moment and then said, "I can be your friend, new house!" So, the little bird and the new house played together in the rain. They laughed and had lots of fun. The rain made puddles, and the little bird splashed in them. The new house was happy to have a friend to play with. And from that day on, the little bird and the new house were best friends, even when the sun came out.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Sarah and Jake. They liked to have lots of fun together, but Jake was always getting into trouble! One day, Sarah was playing with a knife. She wasn't being very careful, and it slipped and cut her lip. Sarah started to cry and Jake felt really bad. He told Sarah he could fix it. He held her lip open with his fingers and then looked around for something to make it better. He saw a pink flower nearby, so he cut a piece off and put it on Sarah's lip. The flower worked. It made Sarah's lip feel much better and prettier too! From then on, Sarah and Jake were both extra careful when playing with knives.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy boat. One sunny day, he decided to sail his boat in the big pond near his house. As Tim played, a careless march of ants came by the pond. They were very noisy and did not watch where they were going. They bumped into Tim, and he dropped his boat into the water. The boat sailed away, far from Tim. He tried to catch it but he couldn't. The boat sailed too far and was lost. Tim was very sad and went back home without his boat.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a big flag on a tall pole. The flag had many colors and it made Tim feel comfortable and happy. He wanted to know more about the flag, so he asked his mom a question. "Mom, what is that flag for?" Tim questioned. His mom smiled and said, "That flag is for a special day. Just wait and see." Later, Tim saw other kids playing with their flags. He wanted to play too, but he didn't have one. Tim felt sad and a little angry. He went to his mom and asked if he could have a flag too. His mom looked at him and said, "Remember the special day I told you about? That day is today! I have a surprise for you." She took out a small flag from her bag and gave it to Tim. He was so happy and excited! Tim played with his new flag and made friends with the other kids. They all waved their flags together, celebrating the special day. Tim learned that asking questions can lead to happy surprises.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She liked to play in the park. One day, she saw something that seemed very nice. It was white and looked like an angel. Lily wanted to be friends with it. Lily walked up to the angel. It was very pretty and looked harmless. She said, "Hello, angel! Do you want to play with me?" The angel smiled and said, "Yes, Lily! I would love to play with you." So, Lily and the angel played together. They ran, jumped, and laughed. They had so much fun. After that day, Lily would always remember her new friend, the kind and harmless angel.
