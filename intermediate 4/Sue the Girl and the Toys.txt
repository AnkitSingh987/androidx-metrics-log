Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Toys

One day, a little girl named Sue was helping her elderly grandma pack things for a trip. They were putting clothes, toys, and food into a big bag. Sue was very excited to go on the trip with her grandma. As they were packing, Sue found a small, old toy in the room. She asked her grandma, "What is this thing?" Grandma smiled and said, "That was my toy when I was a little girl like you. I played with it all the time." Sue wanted to pack the toy, but her grandma said, "No, we have to leave it here. It is too old and might break." Sue was sad, but she listened to her grandma. They finished packing and went on their trip. On the trip, Sue and her grandma had lots of fun. They played games, ate yummy food, and saw new places. When they came back home, Sue found the old toy still in the room. But now, the toy looked new and shiny. Sue learned that sometimes it is good to listen to the elderly because they know what is best. The old toy was now safe and new, and Sue could play with it. The trip with her grandma made Sue very happy, and she learned to always listen to her wise grandma.

Once upon a time, there was a big, smooth rock. It was in the middle of the woods. A little boy named Tim found the rock. He thought it would be a nice place to play. One day, Tim brought his tent to the rock. He wanted to sleep there at night. He put the tent on the smooth rock. It was hard to make the tent stay, but he did not give up. He pushed and pulled until the tent would not move. The rock did not yield. That night, Tim slept in his tent on the smooth rock. He was happy and cozy. When the sun came up, he woke up and played all day. Tim loved his special tent on the smooth rock.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had big eyes and was very careful with everything she did. Lily loved to watch the sun rise in the morning. One day, Lily saw a big bird in her yard. The bird had hurt its wing and could not fly. Lily wanted to help the bird, so she was very careful when she touched it. She took the bird inside her house and made a soft bed for it. Days went by and the bird started to feel better. One morning, the bird started to rise and fly around the room. Lily was so happy! But then, something unexpected happened. The bird turned into a fairy! The fairy thanked Lily for being so careful and kind. She gave Lily a special gift: a magic wand that could make her dreams come true. The moral of the story is to always be kind and careful with others, because you never know what magic can happen.

Once upon a time, there was a big maze. In the maze, there was a sad little mouse. The mouse was sad because it was lost. It wanted to go home, but it did not know how. One day, a friendly cat came to the maze. The cat saw the sad little mouse and wanted to help. The mouse did not like the cat because it was scared. The cat insisted on helping the mouse. It said, "I will help you find your way home." The cat and the mouse went through the maze together. They walked and walked, and the mouse started to feel better. The cat was very nice and did not make the mouse feel scared. They found the way out of the maze, and the mouse was happy again. Now, the mouse was not miserable anymore. The cat and the mouse became best friends. They played together and had lots of fun. The mouse was not scared of the cat anymore, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy boat in the sink. One day, he saw something disgusting in the sink. It was a big, yucky bug! Tim did not like the bug at all. Tim wanted to get the bug out of the sink, so he could play with his toy boat. He tried to think of a way to make the bug go away. Then, he had an idea! He would strike the bug with a spoon. Tim picked up a big spoon and struck the bug. The bug fell out of the sink and onto the floor. Tim was so happy that the bug was gone. Now, he could play with his toy boat in the sink without the disgusting bug. And so, Tim played with his boat all day long, and he had lots of fun.
