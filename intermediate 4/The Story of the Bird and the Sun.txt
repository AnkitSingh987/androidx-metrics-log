Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Bird and the Sun

One morning, a little bird woke up. The bird was delicate and small. The sun was bright and warm. The bird wanted to fly high in the sky. The bird tried to fly, but it was hard. The bird did not give up. It tried again and again. The bird wanted to succeed. Finally, the bird succeeded! It flew high in the sky. The bird was happy. It sang a beautiful song for all to hear.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a nosy cat named Max. Max liked to look at everything in the house. One day, Max saw a lamp on a table. He jumped up to see it better. When Max jumped, the lamp fell down and broke. Lucy saw what happened and felt sad. She wanted to fix the lamp. So, she asked her dad for help. Her dad was good at fixing things. Lucy and her dad worked together to fix the lamp. Max watched them, being nosy as always. When they were done, the lamp was like new again. Lucy was happy, and Max learned to be more careful around things. They all lived happily ever after.

Once there was a white bulb who wanted to be dry. Every day it would wake up and it would look across the room, feeling the moisture. The bulb felt so sad and lonely. One day, a little girl named Sally found the bulb while she was playing. She was very kind and saw the bulb wanted to be dry. "Hello, little bulb," she said cheerfully. "Do you want to be dry?" The bulb nodded its head and smiled. Sally found some cloth and carefully dabbed at the bulb until it was dry. The bulb was so happy and thanked Sally for being so kind. From then on, the bulb and Sally were friends. Whenever the bulb was feeling damp, Sally would always do her best to make the bulb dry again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big parade. Everyone was happy and excited. They all wanted to see the parade. There were big balloons, pretty cars, and funny clowns. A little boy named Timmy wanted to see the parade too. But his mom warned him, "Timmy, don't go too close to the street." Timmy was upset. He wanted to see everything better. So, when his mom was not looking, he went closer and closer to the street. Suddenly, a big balloon came too close to Timmy. It scared him, and he started to cry. He was so upset that he ran away from the parade. Timmy got lost and couldn't find his mom. The parade was not fun for Timmy anymore.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys. But sometimes they fight over them. One day, they both want the same toy. It is a navy boat that can float in the water. "Give me the boat!" Lily says. "I saw it first!" "No, it is mine!" Ben says. "I like it more!" They pull the boat from each other. They are very upset. They make loud noises. Their mom hears them and comes to see what is wrong. "What is going on here?" Mom asks. "Why are you fighting?" "Lily and Ben point at each other. They say, "He/she wants my boat!" Mom looks at the boat. She sees that it has two stickers on it. One says Lily and the other says Ben. "This boat is for both of you," Mom says. "It has your names on it. You can share it and take turns. How about you play together and make the boat go in the bathtub?" Lily and Ben think about it. They like the idea of playing in the water. They say sorry to each other and hug. They take the boat to the bathroom and fill the tub with water. They make the boat sail and splash. They have fun and forget their fight. Mom smiles and watches them. She is happy that they are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with his toy triangle. One sunny day, Tom went outside to play with his triangle. As Tom played, he saw a little mouse named Jerry. Jerry was scared of Tom, so he tried to run away. But Tom wanted to play, so he shouted, "Jerry, don't go! Let's play together!" Jerry stopped and looked at Tom. He was scared, but he wanted to be friends. So, Jerry and Tom played with the triangle together. They had lots of fun, and they became best friends. From that day on, Tom and Jerry always played together. They learned that it is good to share and be kind to others. And they lived happily ever after.
