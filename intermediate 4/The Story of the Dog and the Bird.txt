Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Dog and the Bird

One day, a smelly dog found a piece of leather. The dog liked the leather a lot. He played with it all day. He ran and jumped with the leather in his mouth. The dog had a friend, a little bird. The bird saw the dog with the leather. The bird said, "Dog, why do you play with that smelly thing?" The dog said, "I like it, it is fun to play with." The bird wanted to help the dog. The bird said, "I urge you to clean the leather. It is too smelly." The dog listened to the bird. They took the leather to a big puddle of water. The dog put the leather in the water and the bird helped him clean it. After the leather was clean, it was not smelly anymore. The dog and the bird played with the leather together. They were very happy and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a pastel cat named Blue. Blue liked to bathe in the sun. One day, while Blue was bathing, an angry bird named Red came to the same spot. Red wanted to bathe in the sun too. Red said, "Go away, Blue! I want to bathe here." Blue did not want to leave. Blue said, "No, Red! I was here first." This made Red very angry. Red and Blue did not know what to do. Then, a big, wise tree nearby said, "Why don't you both bathe in the sun together?" Red and Blue thought that was a good idea. They stopped being angry and bathed in the sun together. They became good friends and shared the sunny spot every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a favorite hat that he loved to wear. One day, his hat was gone. Tim was sad. He looked everywhere for his hat. He saw his mom washing clothes. Tim saw his hat in the washing machine. His hat was shrinking! The hat was fragile, so it could break easily. Tim was worried. Tim told his mom about the hat. She knew what to do. She took the hat out of the washing machine. She put it on the sun to dry. The hat stopped shrinking. Tim was happy. He learned to be careful with his fragile hat.

Once, there was a shark. The shark was very interesting. It had big teeth and a long tail. It lived in the deep sea. One day, the shark met a small fish. The fish said, "Hello, Mr. Shark. You look interesting. Can we be friends?" The shark thought for a moment and said, "Yes, we can be friends." They played together for a while. The shark was very strong. It could break big rocks with its tail. The fish was amazed. But one day, the shark was not careful. It broke something very important. The fish got very sad and swam away. The shark was left all alone.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy raft that he loved to play with in the water. One day, he decided to take his raft to the big pond near his house. As Tim played with his raft, he saw a hurt bird sitting by the pond. The bird looked sad and could not fly. Tim wanted to help the bird feel better. He thought maybe the bird would like to ride on his raft. Tim gently picked up the bird and put it on his raft. He pushed the raft around the pond, and the bird seemed to enjoy the ride. After a while, the bird started to feel better and could fly again. Tim was happy that he could help his new friend. They played together at the pond every day, and Tim always remembered to take his raft with him.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tweet. Tweet lived in a big tree. Every morning, when the sun would rise, Tweet would wake up and sing a happy song. One day, a brilliant butterfly named Flutter came to visit Tweet. Flutter said, "Hello Tweet! I love your song. Can I sing with you?" Tweet was happy and said, "Yes, Flutter! Let's sing together!" So, every morning, when the sun would rise, Tweet and Flutter would sing their happy song. All the animals in the forest would wake up and listen to their beautiful music. And they all lived happily ever after.
