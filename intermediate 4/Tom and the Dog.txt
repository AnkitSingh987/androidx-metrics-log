Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and the Dog

One day, Tom went to the park with his mom. They saw a big display with many colors. Tom felt happy and wanted to see more. His mom said, "Tom, let's go closer to look at the display." As they walked, they heard a loud sound. Tom felt uncomfortable. He asked his mom, "What is that sound?" His mom said, "I don't know, let's find out." They walked towards the sound and saw a big dog. The dog was playing a big drum. Tom and his mom were surprised. They laughed and clapped. The big dog stopped playing and said, "Thank you! I like to make people happy with my drum." Tom and his mom had a fun day at the park with the big dog.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom had a flashlight that he loved very much. He would use it to look at things in the dark. Tom was always careful with his flashlight because he did not want to break it. One day, Tom's friend, Sam, came over to play. Sam saw the flashlight and wanted to play with it too. Tom was scared that Sam would not be careful with his flashlight. He did not want to share it with Sam. Sam was sad because he wanted to play with the flashlight too. Tom thought about it and decided to let Sam play with it. He told Sam to be very careful. They played together and had lots of fun. In the end, Tom was happy that he shared his flashlight with Sam.

Once there was a little girl who loved winter. She would spend hours looking out the window of her house and watching as the snowflakes twirled around in the cold air. One morning, the little girl woke up to find the sky closed and the snow was falling. She was so excited and ran to get dressed for the day. "Daddy, can I go and play in the snow?" she pleaded. "Of course you can, my little one," he replied, "But don't forget to put on your warm clothes. The forecast said it's going to be a fine winter today." The little girl couldn't contain her excitement and ran outside, where she was met by a beautiful snow-covered landscape. Hours passed, and the skies became darker and darker. The little girl's father had been watching her from the window, and he knew that time was running out. He ran outside and yelled "It's time to come inside now! The storm is getting closer." The little girl reluctantly followed him back to the house and closed the door. As they watched out the window, a powerful winter storm hit. They were so glad that they had gotten inside in time. The little girl could hardly wait for next winter, when she would be able to get outside and play in the snow again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a muffin that she loved very much. One day, she met a boy named Tim. Tim did not have a muffin. Sue felt sad for Tim and wanted to help him. Sue said, "Tim, I want to give you my muffin. We can both own it and share it together." Tim was very happy and said, "Thank you, Sue!" They were both calm and happy to share the muffin. But, when they broke the muffin in half, they found a surprise inside! There was a little toy! Sue and Tim were so happy. They learned that sharing can bring good things and make friends happy. They played with the toy and ate the muffin together, and they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim had a big goal. He wanted to reach the top of the tall hill near his home. The hill was unknown to him because he had never been there before. One sunny day, Tim put on his shoes and started to walk up the hill. He saw pretty flowers, green grass, and happy birds. The hill was steep, but Tim was brave and strong. He kept going up and up. Finally, Tim reached the top of the hill. He looked around and saw his town below. He felt very happy and proud to reach his goal. Tim knew that even unknown places can be fun and exciting.
