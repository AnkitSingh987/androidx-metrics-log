Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim Finds the Rock

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went for a walk in nature. He saw pretty flowers and big trees. Tim loved to play in nature. He found a shiny, expensive rock on the ground. Tim thought it was very special. Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue saw the expensive rock and wanted it. She said, "Tim, can I have the rock? I will give you my toy." Tim thought about it and said, "No, I want to keep the rock. It is special to me." Sue was angry. She called her big brother, Sam. Sam was mean. He came and took the expensive rock from Tim. Tim was very sad. He went home without the special rock. The day was bad for Tim because he lost his expensive rock.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved the color orange. Lily had an orange dress, an orange hat, and even an orange cat. One day, she saw a beautiful butterfly with an orange pattern on its wings. She wanted to ask the butterfly a question. Lily asked the butterfly, "Why are your wings orange?" The butterfly said, "My wings are orange because I love the color, just like you!" Lily was happy that she and the butterfly both liked the color orange. The butterfly then showed Lily more orange things in the garden. They saw orange flowers, orange bugs, and even an orange bird. Lily was so happy to see all the orange things. She thanked the butterfly for helping her find them. From that day on, Lily and the butterfly were the best of friends.

Bella was a dog who liked to run and play. She had a big yard with many toys and a soft bed. But one day, she felt something itchy on her fur. She scratched and scratched, but it did not help. She looked at her fur and noticed a tiny bug. It was a flea! "Go away, flea!" Bella barked. "You are not my friend. You make me itchy and grumpy." But the flea did not listen. It jumped from one spot to another, biting Bella's skin. Bella tried to catch it with her teeth, but it was too fast. She whined and whimpered, feeling unhappy. Her owner, Sam, heard her noise and came to see what was wrong. He saw Bella scratching and biting her fur. He saw the flea hopping around. He felt sorry for Bella. "Poor Bella," Sam said. "You have a flea. Don't worry, I will help you. I have a special shampoo that will make the flea go away." He took Bella to the bathroom and filled the tub with warm water. He poured some shampoo on Bella's fur and rubbed it gently. He rinsed it off and dried her with a towel. He checked her fur and saw no more fleas. "Bella, you are flea-free!" Sam said. "How do you feel?" Bella wagged her tail and licked Sam's face. She felt much better. She was not itchy or grumpy anymore. She was happy and thankful. "Thank you, Sam," Bella said. "You are my best friend. You make me feel good." Sam smiled and hugged Bella. He was happy too. He loved his dog. They went to the yard and played with the toys. They had a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a famous duck. This duck was famous for making soap. Every day, the duck would make soap for all his friends. The duck's friends loved the soap. They used it to wash and play. One day, the duck made a big soap. It was so big that it could make many bubbles. The duck's friends came to see the big soap. They were very happy. They started to clap and cheer. The famous duck and his friends played with the big soap all day. They made many bubbles and had lots of fun. At the end of the day, they all clapped for the famous duck. They knew he was the best soap maker ever.

Once upon a time, in a small park, there was a nice bench. On this bench, a group of toys lived. They were very organized. They liked to display their colors and shapes for all to see. One day, a little girl named Sue came to the park. She saw the bench with the toys. Sue liked how they were organized. She liked their display of colors and shapes. Sue wanted to play with the toys. Sue played with the toys all day long. She was very happy. The toys were happy too. They liked to be played with. When it was time to go home, Sue put the toys back on the bench. She made sure they were organized and their display was nice. Sue said goodbye to the toys and went home. The toys on the bench were happy and ready for the next child to play with them.
