Category: Intermediate
Title: The Empty Room and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a big empty room. Everyone thought it was too dusty and lonely. So one day a mummy, a daddy and their little baby decided to transform the room. They took out the dust and filled the room with lots of cushions and blankets. They put up wide walls and painted them many colours. Then, the mummy and daddy put lots of toys in the room. The little baby smiled with joy. The baby loved his new room. Every day he tried something new. He climbed on the pillows, rolled on the blankets and played with the toys. He felt excited and happy. One day, the mummy and daddy heard the baby talking. They stopped to listen. He said: "I love this room. I love it so much! Can I share it with my friends?" The mummy and daddy smiled. They understood the baby's wish. They slowly nodded. They decided to open the room to all the children in the neighbourhood. Together, the mummy and daddy turned the room into a playground for everyone. The baby's friends became so happy. Everyone loved the big, wide playground. The baby's simple wish had become the source of love and joy for the neighbourhood. This story teaches us to share our love and happiness with people around us. That's how a big, empty room became a wide and wonderful playground.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was not happy because he felt uncomfortable in his new shoes. He wanted to take a step, but he did not trust his shoes. He thought they were too tight and would hurt his feet. One day, Tim met a talking frog. The frog said, "Tim, trust your shoes and take a step. They will not hurt you." Tim was scared, but he decided to trust the frog. He took a step and to his surprise, his shoes did not hurt him at all. But then, something unexpected happened. With each step Tim took, his shoes began to grow bigger and bigger. Soon, they were so big that Tim could not walk anymore. He fell down and started to cry. The talking frog laughed at him and hopped away. Tim was stuck with his big shoes, and he realized that he should not always trust what others say.

Once upon a time there was a gifted sunflower named Lily. Every morning, Lily would open her petals to welcome the morning sun. It made her feel happy and strong. One morning, Lily spots a little girl in the garden. She bobs up and down to get the girl's attention. Finally, the girl notices her. "My, you are a beautiful sunflower!" the little girl exclaims. She looks so happy that Lily continues to bloom just for her. But then, tragedy strikes! It suddenly starts to rain, and the little girl has to leave the garden. Poor Lily stands in the rain, feeling lost. The rain just keeps on pouring, and soon Lily begins to feel weak. The rain has ruined her petals and kept her from blooming again. The little girl never came back, and Lily never bloomed again. But even on a rainy day, she is still a gifted sunflower.

Tom and Sam were brothers. They liked to read stories. One day, they found a big book of stories in the attic. They opened it and saw many pictures of animals and people. "Let's read this story," Tom said. He pointed to a picture of a lion and a mouse. "It looks fun." "OK," Sam said. He sat next to Tom and listened. Tom read the story. It was about a lion who caught a mouse. The mouse begged the lion to let him go. He said he would help the lion someday. The lion laughed and said he did not need the mouse's help. But he was not hungry, so he let the mouse go. The mouse thanked the lion and ran away. He said he would not forget the lion's kindness. Tom and Sam liked the story. They turned the page and saw another picture. It was of the same lion and mouse, but this time the lion was in trouble. He was caught in a net. He roared and struggled, but he could not escape. "Look, Sam," Tom said. "The lion is in trouble. Do you think the mouse will help him?" "I don't know," Sam said. "Maybe he is scared of the lion. Maybe he is angry at the lion. Maybe he forgot the lion." They read on. The mouse heard the lion's roar. He remembered the lion's kindness. He ran to the net and saw the lion. He was not scared. He was not angry. He did not forget. He said to the lion, "Don't worry, I will help you. You were kind to me, and I will be kind to you." He bit the net with his sharp teeth. He made a hole in the net. He told the lion to crawl out. The lion did. He was free. He thanked the mouse and said, "You are a true friend. You helped me when I needed you. You are brave and smart. I was wrong to laugh at you. I am sorry." The mouse said, "You are welcome. You were kind to me when I could not help you. You are strong and generous. I am glad to be your friend." They hugged and said goodbye. They went their own ways, but they never forgot each other. Tom and Sam closed the book. They smiled and said, "That was a good story. We learned something." "What did we learn?" Tom asked. "We learned that kindness is important. We learned that size does not matter. We learned that friends can help each other. We learned that everyone can escape from trouble with a little help," Sam said. "Yes, we did," Tom said. "Let's read another story tomorrow." They put the book back in the attic. They went downstairs and had dinner. They were happy and hungry.
