Category: Intermediate
Title: Sophie the Girl and the Squirrel

Once upon a time, there was a young girl named Sophie. She had a playful hut in the middle of the forest. On the outside of the hut, there were lots of colorful flowers. One day Sophie decided to explore the forest. Suddenly, she heard a voice from a nearby tree. It said: "hi Sophie, would you like to play with me?" Sophie turned around and saw a squirrel at the bottom of the tree. Sophie replied: "Hi Squirrel, I would love to play with you!" The squirrel was so delighted that it ran back to its tree and grabbed a nut. "Here you go", it said, "this nut is for you to enjoy". Sophie took the nut and thanked the squirrel, who said: "my pleasure". Sophie ate the nut and then the squirrel said: "I have a surprise for you". The squirrel jumped back to the ground and gave Sophie a piece of cut grass. It said: "this piece of grass will help you make a bed for your hut." Sophie thanked the squirrel and thanked it for its playful gifts. The squirrel nodded and said: "it was my pleasure". The squirrel waved goodbye and soon afterwards, Sophie was back in her hut, content and warm.

Once upon a time there was a family of ducks. They loved to march around the pond they lived in. One day they decided to go on a journey. First, they had to march over the green grass. Then, they had to march through a muddy field. After a long march, the ducks were very tired. They looked for a comfortable spot to rest. Suddenly, they found the perfect place to take a nap—some soft autumn leaves on the ground. The ducks tucked themselves into the leaves and fell asleep. The ducks were happy and comfy in the leaves. But, when the sun went down, the leaves began to fall from the trees. The ducks quickly spread their wings and flew away before the leaves could touch them. That night, the ducks came back to their pond, safe and sound. They slept in their own comfy nest, surrounded by their family.

Mommy was in the kitchen organizing the onions. She was preparing dinner for later that night. The young girl watched her mother’s every movement, mesmerized by the process. She had never seen onions before, and couldn’t understand why Mommy used them. "Mommy, what's that?” the girl said pointing to the onion. “That’s an onion, sweetheart. Don’t touch it! It’s very disgusting,” Mommy said determinedly. The girl was confused. “But why is it so disgusting?” Mommy sighed. “It just tastes bad. Now go and play in your room for a little bit." The girl went off to her room to play, unsure of why onions were so disgusting. Mommy finished organizing the onions and started preparing dinner.

Once upon a time, there was a big, impressive sheep. This sheep had a lot of soft wool. The sheep lived on a farm with many friends. One day, the sheep heard a little voice. The voice said, "I am hungry." The next day, the sheep saw a small bug. The bug was chewing on a leaf. The sheep thought, "Maybe this is the hungry friend." The sheep went to the bug and said, "Do you want some of my wool to chew?" The bug said, "No, thank you. I like leaves." The sheep felt sad. It wanted to help the hungry friend. The next day, the sheep saw a little bird. The bird was chewing on a worm. The sheep asked the bird, "Do you want some of my wool to chew?" The bird said, "No, thank you. I like worms." Finally, the sheep found a tiny mouse. The mouse was chewing on a small piece of cheese. The sheep asked the mouse, "Do you want some of my wool to chew?" The mouse said, "Yes, please! I love soft wool to make my bed warm." The sheep was very happy. It gave the mouse some of its wool. The mouse was not hungry anymore, and they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had an old toy car that he loved very much. He played with the car every day and took it everywhere he went. One day, while Tim was playing in the park, a big dog came up to him. The dog saw the car and wanted to have it. Tim was scared of the big dog and thought the dog was a threat. He did not want the dog to take his old toy car. So, Tim held the car close to him and yelled for help. His mom heard him and came running to see what was wrong. When Tim's mom saw the big dog, she knew what to do. She picked up a stick and threw it far away. The big dog saw the stick and ran after it, forgetting about Tim's old toy car. Tim and his mom were happy that the threat was gone, and they went home to play with the car some more.
