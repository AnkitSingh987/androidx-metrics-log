Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bird and the Cage

Once upon a time, there was a bird in a cage. The bird was sad. He wanted to leave the cage and be independent. One day, a girl came to the cage. She said, "Bird, I will help you leave." She opened the cage and the bird flew out. The bird was happy to be free and independent. But then, something unexpected happened. The bird flew back to the cage and said, "I want to stay here. I feel safe in my cage." The girl was surprised but she understood. The bird and the girl became friends and they were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a pie. The pie was very dry. It lived in a big house with a nice girl named Lily. Lily loved the pie, but she did not want to eat it because it was so dry. One day, Lily had an idea. She believed she could make the dry pie yummy. She went to the kitchen and found some sweet red fruit. She put the fruit on the pie and it became a yummy pie. Lily was so happy. She shared the pie with her family and friends. They all loved the pie now. Lily learned that if you believe, you can make things better. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a wild bird in his yard. The bird was up high in a tree. Tim wanted to help the bird come down. He got a ladder from his house. Tim put the ladder near the tree. He started to climb up. His mom saw him and called out, "Tim, do you remember what I told you about climbing ladders?" Tim stopped and thought. He remembered his mom said, "Do not climb ladders without me." Tim felt sad. He wanted to help the bird, but he did not want to break the rule. Tim's mom saw the wild bird too. She said, "I will help you, Tim." Together, they climbed the ladder. They helped the bird come down from the tree. Tim was happy. He remembered to follow the rule and still helped the bird.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his yellow ball. One day, while playing, he saw a big stick on the ground. Tim thought it would be fun to play with the stick too. So, he picked it up and started to run around. Tim's dog, Spot, saw him playing with the stick. Spot wanted to play too! He tried to take the stick from Tim, but Tim said, "No, Spot! This is my stick!" Spot was sad, but he did not give up. He tried again and again to get the stick, but Tim refused to share. Finally, Tim saw how sad Spot was. He felt bad for not sharing the stick with his best friend. So, Tim threw the stick, and Spot ran after it. They played together all day, taking turns with the stick and the yellow ball. And they both were very happy.

One day, a musician went to play music in the park. He had a big drum. He wanted to make people happy with his music. But when he got to the park, it was empty. No one was there. The musician felt sad because he wanted to play for people. Then, a little bird came and asked, "Why are you sad?" The musician said, "I want to play music for people, but the park is empty." The bird had an idea. "I can help you do that," it said. The bird flew away and came back with many friends. The musician started to play his drum, and the birds started to sing. They made beautiful music together. People heard the music and came to the park. They danced and clapped. The musician was happy, and the park was not empty anymore. The birds and the musician did something unexpected - they made music together, and it made everyone happy.

One day, a small boy marched down the street. He carried a folder in his hands, and it made his arms feel tired. But he didn't give up! As he marched around the corner, he saw a tall building. His eyes grew wide and he knew he wanted to explore it. Quickly, he marched inside and saw a lot of staircases. He knew he wanted to go up, but he had to be careful because the stairs were tall. Still marching, he climbed up the stairs. He was so excited to see what else was in the tall building. When he reached the top, he realized that the folder he had been carrying fit inside a small door that he had seen! He opened the folder and saw a lot of colorful toys inside. He was so happy that he had marched all the way up the stairs. It was worth it! Now he could play with the toys.
