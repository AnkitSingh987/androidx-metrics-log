Category: Intermediate
Title: The Colorful Cactus and the Boy

Once upon a time, in a big desert, there was a colorful cactus. The cactus was very pretty and had many colors. It was the only colorful thing in the desert. One day, a little boy saw the colorful cactus. He wanted to touch it. His mommy said, "No, don't touch the cactus!" But the little boy didn't listen. He felt a strong urge to touch the pretty cactus. The little boy touched the colorful cactus, and it hurt him. He cried and cried because it was very painful. His mommy tried to help him, but it was too late. The little boy learned that he should always listen to his mommy.

One day, a little girl named Lily was helping her mom with folding clothes. Lily liked to help her mom. Her mom gave her a pile of clothes to fold. In the pile, there was a fragile dress made of soft, thin fabric. Lily's friend Tom came over to play. Tom saw the pile of clothes and said, "Let's play with the clothes!" Lily said, "We have to be careful with the fragile dress." Tom didn't listen. They started to play with the clothes. They threw them in the air and laughed. But then, Tom grabbed the fragile dress and pulled it hard. The dress ripped in half. Lily started to cry. Her mom came into the room and saw the ripped dress. She was sad too. She said, "You should have been careful with the fragile dress." Tom felt bad for not listening to Lily. Now, the dress was broken and they couldn't fix it.

One day, Jack was walking in the park with his parents. Suddenly, he noticed a zip. He was so excited because he had never seen a zip before. His parents told him that it was a shelter. "Wow, that's so cool," said Jack. Jack asked his parents if he could go inside. His parents said that he couldn't because it was very expensive. Jack was so disappointed. He wanted to experience the shelter so badly. But then his parents reminded him that he could play zip at home! "That's right!" Jack exclaimed. He happily ran off to play zip at home with his parents.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a piece of leather on the ground. He picked it up and thought it was very special. He showed it to his friend, Sally. Sally said, "Wow! That is a nice piece of leather!" Tim and Sally played with the leather all day. They made it into a cape for their toy bear. They had so much fun. But then, Tim's mom called him for dinner. Tim was so hungry that he forgot the leather outside. The next day, Tim and Sally went to find the leather. But it was gone! They looked everywhere. They were very sad. Then, they saw a bird with the leather in its beak. The bird dropped it into a puddle. Tim picked it up, but it was wet and bitter. "Don't worry, Tim," said Sally. "We can clean it and play with it again." Tim smiled and said, "Yes, we will never forget our special leather again."

Once upon a time, there was a big black car. It liked to drive fast. One day, the car wanted to go on a trip to the port. The port was by the water, where the big boats lived. The car started to drive to the port. On the way, it saw many friends. It saw a red ball, a blue bike, and a yellow dog. They all wanted to go to the port too. So, they all went together. When they got to the port, they saw the big boats. The boats were so big and pretty. The black car, the red ball, the blue bike, and the yellow dog played all day by the water. They had so much fun at the port.

Once upon a time, there was a messy room. In the room, there was a big chest. The chest was full of toys. A little boy named Tom lived in this room. Every day, Tom played with his toys and made the room even messier. One day, Tom's mom said, "Tom, you need to clean your room." Tom did not want to clean, but he knew his mom was right. So, he started to pick up his toys and put them back in the chest. While Tom was cleaning, he found a toy alligator. The alligator could snap its mouth open and closed. Tom thought it was fun to make the alligator snap at his other toys. But, he remembered that he needed to clean his room. So, Tom put the alligator in the chest and finished cleaning. When he was done, his room was nice and neat. Tom's mom was happy, and Tom was proud of his clean room.
