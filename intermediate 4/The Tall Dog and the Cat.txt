Category: Intermediate
Title: The Tall Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a tall dog. The tall dog had an owner. The owner loved the tall dog very much. One day, the owner took the tall dog to the park. The tall dog saw a small cat. The tall dog wanted to play with the small cat. The owner urged the tall dog to be nice to the small cat. The tall dog and the small cat played together. They had fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The small cat grew very big. The tall dog was not tall anymore. The small cat was now the tall cat, and the tall dog was now the small dog. The owner was surprised, but they all played together and had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to nap in the sun. One day, Max was napping under a big tree. He was dreaming about his favorite treat. Max's friend, a little bird named Lily, was worried about Max. Lily said, "Max, wake up! I have a surprise for you!" Max woke up and said, "What is it, Lily?" Lily told Max that she found a big treat for him. Max was very happy and followed Lily to the treat. When they got to the treat, Max saw that it was not a treat at all. It was a big, scary cat! Max was scared, but he remembered that he had a friend in Lily. Together, they scared the cat away. After that, Max and Lily found a real treat to share. The moral of the story is to always be brave and help your friends, even when things are not what they seem.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily liked to play with her toys all day. She had a big box of toys, but her favorite toy was a silly, soft teddy bear. One day, Lily was playing with her teddy bear when she heard a noise. She looked around and saw a big, shiny box in her room. Lily was surprised, because the box was not there before. She thought it was a gift, so she opened the box. Inside the box, Lily found a toy race car. She was very excited to play with it. She pushed the car around her room, and it was very fast. But when Lily tried to finish the race with her car, something unexpected happened. The car turned into a big, real race car! Lily was so amazed and couldn't believe her eyes. She knew she had found a magical toy. And from that day on, Lily had even more fun playing with her new, magical race car and her silly teddy bear.

Once upon a time, in a big purple house, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play catch with his dog, Spot. One day, Tim found a map in his room. The map was old and had a big red "X" on it. Tim was very excited to see what the "X" would lead to. Tim and Spot went outside to play and follow the map. They walked around the big purple house and found the "X" under a big tree. Tim started to dig and dig. Spot helped him by digging too. After a while, they found a big box. Tim was very happy and thought it was a treasure. Tim opened the box and saw a big purple ball inside. He was so excited to play catch with it. But when Tim threw the ball, it did not come back. The ball went up, up, up into the sky and did not come down. Tim was very sad because he could not play catch with the purple ball. Spot was sad too. They went back to the big purple house, but they could not play catch anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Tim. Tim was a generous dog who loved to share his toys with his friends. One day, Tim went to the park to play with his friends. In the park, there was a big fountain. Tim and his friends liked to play near the fountain. One day, the fountain started to tremble. Tim and his friends were scared. They did not know what to do. Then, a kind old lady came to help. She told Tim and his friends that they should not be scared. She showed them how to be brave and not to tremble. Tim and his friends learned to be brave like the kind old lady. The moral of the story is to be brave and help others when they are scared.
