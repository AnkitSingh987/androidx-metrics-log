Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Bird and the Leaves

One day, a big bird named Bob saw a small tree with dry leaves. Bob felt sad for the tree. He wanted to help it. He thought, "I will provide water for the tree to make it happy." Bob flew to a river to get water in his beak. On his way back, he saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was trying to pick apples from a tall tree. She was too small to reach them. Bob had an idea. He said, "Sue, can you help me give water to the small tree with dry leaves? Then I will help you pick apples." Sue agreed to help. They went to the river and filled a bucket with water. They gave the water to the small tree. The tree became happy and grew bigger. The dry leaves fell down and new green leaves grew. Now it was time for Bob to help Sue. Bob picked the apples from the tall tree and gave them to Sue. Sue was very happy. She said, "Thank you, Bob!" In the end, they both learned that helping each other made them feel good. And the small tree with dry leaves became a big, strong tree with many green leaves.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a thin, red thread that she loved very much. Amy would play with her thread every day. One day, Amy's mom asked her to clean her room. "Amy, please clean your room," her mom said. Amy nodded and started to clean. While cleaning, she found her thin, red thread under her bed. She was so happy to see it again! Amy went to her mom and showed her the thin, red thread. "Look, Mom! I found my thread when I cleaned my room!" she said with a big smile. Her mom was proud of Amy for cleaning her room and finding her thread. They both laughed and played with the thread together. And from that day on, Amy always remembered to clean her room.

Once upon a time there was a small mouse who loved to play. One day, he saw something shiny and he ran closer to take a look. He realized it was a large, metal trap that seemed to have been set on the ground. He felt a bit scared, but he was brave. He put one paw forward and carefully tapped the trap. Then he stepped back to see what would happen. Nothing did, so he courageously tried again. He realized the trap wasn't real and only pretended to be a danger. So he boldly jumped right in the middle and started to explore. He looked around, but he couldn't find anything to eat or drink. He got a bit worried, but he soon saw a way out. He bravely went over to the edge and realized the trap was no longer a trap at all. The mouse was so happy and he ran off in search of a new adventure. The end!

Once upon a time, there was a shy bird named Tim. Tim lived on a cliff near the sea. Every day, Tim would fly away from the cliff to find food for his family. One day, Tim saw a big, yummy worm on the ground. He was so excited! But when he tried to pick it up, the worm turned into a big, scary cat! Tim was so scared, he flew back to the cliff as fast as he could. When Tim returned to the cliff, he told his family about the scary cat. But then, the cat came to the cliff too! The cat said, "I'm sorry I scared you, Tim. I'm a magic cat, and I can change into anything." Tim and the cat became friends, and they all lived happily together on the cliff.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Susie. One day Susie was out walking in the park when she arrived at a large tree. She looked up and saw a bird sitting on the branches. Susie looked around and noticed a small worm crawling on the ground. She said to the bird, "Hello, look at the fair worm!" The bird replied, "Yes, that's a very fair worm. I have never seen one like it before." Susie was excited, so she carefully picked up the worm and put it in a jar. She wanted to take it home with her. Suddenly, Susie heard a loud noise. It was her mother arriving to pick her up. Susie quickly put the jar in her bag and ran to the car. After she arrived home, Susie took the worm out of the jar and put it safely back into the ground. The end.
