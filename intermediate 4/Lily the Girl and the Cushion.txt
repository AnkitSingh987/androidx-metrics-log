Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Cushion

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had a light blue cushion that she loved very much. It was soft and nice to hug. Lily took her cushion everywhere she went. One day, Lily's mom said, "We need to sell some things to make room in our house." Lily thought about it and decided to help her mom. She took her light blue cushion and went to the front yard. She put the cushion on a table. A boy named Tim came by and saw the cushion. He said, "I like your light blue cushion. Can I buy it?" Lily thought about it and said, "Yes, you can buy it. But you have to promise to take good care of it." Tim promised to take good care of the cushion. He gave Lily some coins, and Lily gave Tim the light blue cushion. They both were happy, and Lily knew her cushion would be loved by Tim.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big, modern statue. The statue was of a man holding a flower. Every day, people would go to see the statue and talk about how nice it was. One day, a little girl named Amy went to see the statue with her mom. Amy looked at the flower in the statue's hand and asked her mom, "Will the flower bloom?" Her mom smiled and said, "No, dear. It's just a statue. Statues don't have real flowers." The next day, something unexpected happened. The flower in the statue's hand started to bloom! People from all over the town came to see the blooming flower. They were all amazed and couldn't believe their eyes. Amy and her mom went to see the statue again. When they saw the blooming flower, Amy said, "Wow, the flower did bloom!" Her mom looked at her and said, "Yes, it did. This teaches us to never say never, as sometimes even the impossible can happen." From that day on, everyone in the town learned the value of believing in the unexpected and never giving up hope. And the statue with the blooming flower became a symbol of hope and wonder for all who saw it.

One day, a girl named Lucy went to play outside with her spade. She wanted to dig in the dirt and make a big hole. Lucy loved to play with her spade, and she liked to pretend she was a cook making a big cake in the dirt. As Lucy was digging, she found a big, shiny rock. She thought it was a magic rock that could help her cook. She ran to her mom and showed her the rock. Lucy's mom said, "Be careful, Lucy. That rock might be hot and hurt you." Lucy didn't listen to her mom. She took the rock and put it in a pot on the stove. She turned on the heat and waited for the rock to cook. Suddenly, the rock started to change color and got very hot. Lucy was scared and didn't know what to do. Just then, Lucy's mom came in and saw the hot rock. She quickly turned off the stove and took the rock out with a big glove. She told Lucy, "I told you it might hurt you. We need to be careful with things we find outside." Lucy learned her lesson and promised to listen to her mom next time. And from that day on, Lucy only cooked with her spade and dirt, making the best dirt cakes ever.

Once there was a little boy. His name was George. He wanted to raise his own spark. One day, he asked his Mom: "Mom, can I raise a spark?" His Mom said, "Yes, George! Where do you want to raise it?" George thought for a moment and said, "Let's raise it in the deep forest." So, they packed some food into a basket and went to the forest. George found a deep, dark spot and put down his spark. Then he and his Mom watched as the spark slowly grew and grew. At the end of the day, the spark had grown into a huge fire. George and his Mom sat and watched for a long time. They were both very happy and George said, "Mom, I did it! I raised my own spark!"
