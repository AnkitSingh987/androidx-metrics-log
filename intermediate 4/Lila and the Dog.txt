Category: Intermediate
Title: Lila and the Dog

Lila was an adventurous girl who liked to explore new places. One day, she found a big whip in her dad's closet. She wanted to play with it and pretend to be a cowboy. She took the whip and ran outside. She saw a big dog in the yard. The dog was friendly and wagged his tail. Lila thought the dog would be a good horse for her game. She tried to put the whip around the dog's neck, but the dog did not like it. He barked and shook his head. He refused to let Lila use him as a horse. Lila got angry and pulled the whip harder. The dog yelped and bit the whip. He ran away with the whip in his mouth. Lila chased him, but he was faster. She lost the whip and the dog. Lila was sad and sorry. She realized she had hurt the dog and made him scared. She wished she had been nicer to him and played a different game. She learned that she should not use things that are not hers and that she should respect animals. She hoped the dog would forgive her and come back. She decided to be more careful and kind in her adventures.

Mary and Bob were best friends. They both liked playing with their dolls and going to the park. On one sunny day, they went to the park together. They sat on the soft grass and watched the trees. Then they heard a strange sound. Mary asked: "What's that?" Bob said: "I think it's laughing!" They looked around and soon saw a mark. It was actually a little squirrel, laughing and having fun. Mary and Bob started laughing too! The squirrel was not scared. It stayed there and let Mary and Bob get closer. Mary even got to touch it. The squirrel was very soft and Mary laughed again. Bob said: "This is the best day ever!" Mary and Bob laughed together and kept playing with the little squirrel.

Once upon a time, there was a large, friendly bear named Ben. Ben had a favorite sock that he loved to wear. One day, Ben lost his sock and he felt very sad. Ben asked his friend, a small bird named Tim, "Have you seen my sock?" Tim replied, "No, but let's look for it together!" So, Ben and Tim searched high and low for the sock. They looked near the trees, by the river, and in the bushes. Finally, they found the sock under a big rock. Ben was so happy and he thanked Tim for helping him. Tim said, "You're welcome, Ben! Friends are here to comfort each other." From that day on, Ben and Tim always helped each other when they needed it. And they learned that being kind and comforting friends is what makes life better.

Sara loves to watch cartoons on TV. She likes the ones with animals that talk and sing and do funny things. Her favorite cartoon is about a graceful cat that wears a pink bow and a blue dress. The cat is always nice and helps her friends. One day, Sara wants to watch her cartoon, but her mom says no. Mom says Sara needs to do her homework first. Sara does not like homework. She thinks it is boring and hard. She wants to have fun and see the cat. "Please, mom, can I watch my cartoon?" Sara asks. "It is almost over. I will do my homework later." "No, Sara, you need to do your homework now," mom says. "You can watch your cartoon after you finish. It is not going anywhere. Homework is important. It helps you learn and grow." Sara feels sad and angry. She does not want to learn and grow. She wants to watch her cartoon. She thinks mom is mean and does not understand. She stomps her feet and cries. Mom hugs Sara and wipes her tears. She says, "I know you love your cartoon, Sara, but you need to do your homework first. It is not fair to watch TV when you have work to do. How would you feel if the cat did not do her work and just watched TV all day? She would not be graceful or nice. She would be lazy and rude." Sara thinks about what mom says. She does not want to be lazy and rude. She wants to be graceful and nice like the cat. She says, "I'm sorry, mom. You are right. I will do my homework first. Then I can watch my cartoon and be happy." Mom smiles and says, "That's a good girl, Sara. I'm proud of you. I love you very much." Sara hugs mom and says, "I love you too, mom. Thank you for helping me." Sara goes to her room and does her homework. She works hard and finishes quickly. Then she goes to the living room and watches her cartoon. She sees the cat and smiles. She thinks the cat is graceful and nice. She thinks she is graceful and nice too. She is happy.
