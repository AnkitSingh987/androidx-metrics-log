Category: Intermediate
Title: The Blue Bird and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a blue bird. The blue bird lived in a big tree. The blue bird had a friend, a silver fish. The silver fish lived in a pond near the tree. One day, the blue bird was sad. The silver fish was not in the pond. The blue bird did not know where the silver fish went. The blue bird wanted to find the silver fish. The blue bird closed its eyes and started to pray. It prayed for help to find the silver fish. Then, the blue bird saw the silver fish in another pond. The blue bird was happy to see the silver fish again. They played together and were happy friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his best friend, a cat named Max, went to play outside. They liked to play in the tight spaces between the trees. As they were playing, Max found a big box. Tim asked Max, "What is in the box?" Max didn't know, so they decided to open it. When they opened the box, it revealed a secret map. The map had a big red X on it. Tim and Max looked at each other and knew they had to find the treasure. They followed the map, walking over hills and through the woods. As they got closer to the big red X, they saw a big hole in the ground. Tim and Max looked at each other and said, "This must be where the treasure is!" They dug and dug until they found a smaller box. When they opened the smaller box, they found a note that said, "The real treasure is the fun you had together." Tim and Max smiled, knowing they had a great adventure and the best treasure of all – their friendship.

Once upon a time, there was a happy cat named Jazz. Jazz loved to cook. Every day, Jazz would cook healthy food for all his friends. One day, Jazz made a big pot of soup. He put in lots of veggies, like carrots and peas. His friends came to eat the soup. They all said, "Yummy! This is very good!" After they ate, Jazz and his friends danced and played. They had so much fun. They were happy and healthy because of the yummy food Jazz cooked. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a small girl who wanted to go on an adventure. She followed a path deep into the woods. When she reached a swamp, she stopped and looked around. She was amazed by how different it was. The water was calm and the trees made a tall wall around her. In the middle, she could see a modest wooden house. She stepped carefully into the marsh and walked up to the front steps. When she opened the door, she was amazed at what was inside. There were all sorts of animals, plants, and furniture. She felt like she had found paradise! She spent hours exploring and talking to the animals. She even made some new friends. When it was time to leave, she said goodbye to her friends and walked back home, smiling all the way. The small girl will never forget her wonderful adventure in the swamp.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big fountain. The water in the fountain was dirty. Tim was sad because he wanted to play in the water. He said, "I wish the water was clean." A nice man heard Tim and had an idea. He told Tim to close his eyes. Tim did as the man said. The man then quickly cleaned the fountain. When he was done, he told Tim to open his eyes. Tim opened his eyes and got a big surprise. The water in the fountain was now clean! "Wow, thank you!" Tim said to the nice man. They both laughed and played in the clean fountain water. Tim had a great day at the park, and he was very happy that he got a surprise.

Jill had to pack her luggage for her trip. She spoke to her mum as she put her clothes in her bag. "I like going on trips," she said. The wind blew outside, and the sun shone in the sky. It was a dry day. Mum helped Jill pack her luggage. They put in some books, toys, and snacks. Jill was very excited to go. "We are all ready to go," said Mum. "Let's get going!" Jill said goodbye to her room before she picked up her dry luggage. She waved to Mum as they left for their trip.
