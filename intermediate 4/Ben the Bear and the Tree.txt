Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben the Bear and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a cute little bear named Ben. Ben loved honey very much. One day, it was very wet and rainy outside, but Ben still wanted to find some honey to eat. He put on his rain boots and went out to look for honey. Ben walked for a long time and saw a big tree. He thought, "I hope there is honey in this tree!" Ben climbed up the tree and found a beehive full of honey. He was so happy! But he also saw a sad bee sitting on a branch. "Why are you sad?" Ben asked the bee. The bee said, "I am sad because my wings are wet and I can't fly home." Ben wanted to help the bee. He said, "I will help you get home, and then we can share the honey together!" The bee smiled, and they became friends. Ben carried the bee on his back, and they went to the bee's home. When they got to the bee's home, the bee's family was so happy to see her. They thanked Ben for helping her. The bee's family gave Ben lots of honey to take home with him. Ben and the bee were both very happy, and they knew they would be friends forever. From that day on, Ben and the bee always shared honey together, even on wet and rainy days. And they always remembered to help each other when they needed it.

Once upon a time, there was a big red barn. In the barn, there were many animals. They liked to play and have fun. But, there was a little cow who was scared of the dark. The cow would not go into the barn at night. One day, the other animals tried to help the scared cow. They said, "Come into the barn with us. It is not so bad." But the little cow refused. It was too scared to go in. So, the other animals came up with a plan. They put a small, soft light in the barn. This made the barn less dark and scary. The little cow saw the light and felt better. It went into the barn with the other animals and was not scared anymore. They all played and had fun together in the big red barn.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the beach with her mom. They saw a large jellyfish in the water. Lucy was scared and asked her mom, "What is that?" Her mom tried to explain, "That is a jellyfish. It lives in the water. But we should not touch it, because it can hurt us." As they watched, something unexpected happened. The jellyfish started to talk! It said, "Don't be scared, Lucy. I am a friendly jellyfish. I will not hurt you." Lucy and her mom were very surprised. They never thought a jellyfish could talk! From that day on, Lucy and the jellyfish became good friends, and they played together at the beach every summer.

One day, a little girl named Amy found a big box with a zip. She wanted to open it, but she could not. She asked her friend, Tom, "Can you help me open the zip?" Tom tried, but he could not open it too. They both felt envious when they saw other kids playing with their toys. They wanted to play with the toy in the box too. They decided to ask their teacher, Miss Lee, for help. Miss Lee saw the zip and said, "Let me try." She pulled and pulled, and the zip opened. Amy and Tom were so happy. They said, "Thank you, Miss Lee!" Miss Lee smiled and said, "You're welcome. Now you can play with your toy." Amy and Tom took out the toy and played together. They were not envious anymore. They learned to accept help from others when they could not do something by themselves. From that day on, Amy and Tom always helped each other and their friends whenever they needed it. They knew that it was good to accept help and to help others too.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a boy named Tim. He had a toy named Mr. Bear. Tim loved to play with Mr. Bear. One day, Tim's mom said, "We have a new friend coming to play. Let's introduce Mr. Bear to her." When the new friend came, her name was Lily. Lily had a toy too. It was a fake bird. Tim and Lily played with Mr. Bear and the fake bird. They had so much fun. They opened a door with a big knob and made a house for their toys. After playing, Tim and Lily learned that sharing toys makes playtime more fun. They became good friends. The moral of the story is: sharing is caring, and it brings friends closer.
