Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Ghost and the Play

Once upon a time, in a humble little house, a ghost lived. This ghost liked to play and have fun with the kids. One day, a new family moved into the house. The ghost wanted to play with them, but they did not know he was there. The ghost tried to show them he was there, but they only got scared. They began to quarrel with each other because they were scared. The ghost did not like to see them fight. He wanted them to be happy and play with him. Then, one day, something unexpected happened. The ghost found a way to make the family laugh. He made funny faces and danced around. The family saw the ghost and laughed! They were not scared anymore. They played with the ghost every day and they all became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom was very excited one day because his mom promised him a special cube. When Tom finally got the cube, he was so happy! It was bright and colorful with many sides to explore. Tom wanted to try out his cube right away, but his mom warned him to be careful--the cube was fragile. Tom promised he would be careful, and slowly started exploring his cube. He slowly turned it around, looking at each colorful side. Tom liked his cube very much, and continued playing with it each day. Whenever his mom saw him playing with it, she reminded him to be careful because the cube was fragile. As time passed, Tom started manipulating the cube like a pro and enjoyed himself even more. He and his cube were now best friends.

Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to look for bugs and flowers. They saw a lizard on a rock. It was green and brown and had a long tail. "Wow, look at the lizard!" Lily said. "It is so cool. Can we touch it?" Ben was scared of the lizard. He thought it might bite or scratch them. He shook his head. "No, Lily, we can't touch it. It is not a toy. It is a wild animal. It might run away or hurt us." But Lily did not listen to Ben. She wanted to see the lizard up close. She had an urge to touch it. She moved slowly towards the rock. "Come on, Ben, don't be a baby. It is just a lizard. It is not a lion or a snake. It is cute and small. I want to pet it." Ben tried to stop Lily. He grabbed her arm. "Lily, stop! You are being silly. The lizard does not want to be petted. It wants to be left alone. You are scaring it. Come back here!" But Lily pulled her arm away from Ben. She reached out her hand to the lizard. The lizard did not like Lily's hand. It felt threatened. It opened its mouth and showed its teeth. It hissed and jumped at Lily. "Aaaah!" Lily screamed. She was surprised and scared. She fell back and dropped her hand. The lizard ran away and hid under a bush. Ben ran to Lily. He hugged her and checked her hand. She had a small scratch on her finger. It was bleeding a little bit. "Are you okay, Lily?" Ben asked. "Did the lizard hurt you?" Lily nodded. She was crying. She felt sorry and stupid. "Yes, Ben, I am okay. But the lizard hurt me. It was angry at me. I should have listened to you. You were right. I was wrong. I was silly. I am sorry, Ben." Ben smiled and kissed her cheek. He took her hand and wrapped it with a leaf. "It's okay, Lily. I forgive you. You are not silly. You are curious. But you have to be careful. Some animals don't like to be touched. They need their space. Next time, we can look at the lizard from far away. Okay?" Lily smiled and nodded. She hugged Ben and said thank you. "Okay, Ben. Thank you for being my friend. You are very smart and brave. And tall. I like you." They walked back to the house. They saw their mom and dad. They told them what happened. Their mom and dad were glad they were okay. They gave them a hug and a cookie. They said they were proud of them for being honest and kind. They said they loved them very much.
