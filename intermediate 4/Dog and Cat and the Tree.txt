Category: Intermediate
Title: Dog and Cat and the Tree

One day, Dog and Cat had a quarrel. Dog said, "You are bad! You took my toy!" Cat said, "No, I did not take your toy!" They were both very mad. Then, a big wind came and blew the toy away. Dog and Cat saw the toy fly away with their own eyes. They both felt sad. They did not know what to do. Dog and Cat looked at each other and said, "Let's find the toy together!" They used their paws to look for the toy. In the end, they found the toy stuck in a tree. Dog and Cat were happy again. They learned that it is better to be friends than to quarrel.

One sunny day at the beach, Tina was swimming with her best friend, Jake. Suddenly, Jake saw a jellyfish floating by and shouted, "Look! A jellyfish!" Tina ran over. She was so excited to see the cool jellyfish. It had powerful, long tentacles moving in the waves. Jake didn't want to get too close, so he said, "Careful Tina, it could sting you!" Tina replied, "Don't worry, I have a towel to wipe it off if it does." Jake laughed, "You don't have to worry about that. Jellyfish can't sting us!" They both watched and admired the jellyfish for a few minutes before it floated away and disappeared. They stayed at the beach for hours playing in the sand and surfing in the waves. They had so much fun and tina was thankful that she wiped the jellyfish with her towel.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his purple ball. One day, while playing, he saw a big cat. The cat wanted to take Spot's ball away. Spot was sad and scared. Spot thought hard and decided to be careful. He hid the ball behind a tree. Then, he went to the cat and asked if they could share the ball. The cat agreed, and they both played together. After playing, Spot and the cat were very tired. They needed to rest. They lay down under the tree and slept. When they woke up, they were happy to be friends. Spot learned that sharing and being careful can solve problems.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy king. He lived in a big castle with many chambers. The king had a plan to find the best toy for his little prince. One day, the king went to a room filled with toys. He looked at all the toys and picked a big, red ball. The king knew the little prince would love it. He hid the ball in a secret chamber, waiting for the right time to give it to the prince. The little prince knew his dad had a plan. He looked all around the castle for the secret chamber. One day, he found the room with the big, red ball. The prince was so happy! The king's plan had worked, and the prince loved his new toy.

Once upon a time there were two friends. One was called Tom and he was a frog. The other was called Joe and he was a mouse. Tom wanted to go for a swim, but he had no pond nearby. So he asked Joe if he could borrow his swamp. Joe said yes and so Tom hopped off to the swamp. When he arrived, it was very colorful. He could see all sorts of bugs and plants. He happily swam around until he was tired. Then he thanked Joe and hopped back home. The two friends had a lot of fun that day. Tom was very happy to borrow Joe's swamp and he couldn't wait to go back.

Once upon a time there was a bee named Buzz who was very eager to make friends. He had been living alone in the forest and he wanted someone to share his honey with. One day he decided to go find a buddy, so he flew around looking for someone to unite with. While he was searching, Buzz noticed a butterfly floating in the air, and he decided to give it a try. Buzz flew up to the butterfly and said, “Hi! I’m Buzz. Could we be friends? I have lots of honey and I’d like to share it.” The butterfly was excited to hear this and said, “Yes! I’m Flutter and I’d love to unite with you!” So, Buzz and Flutter went to Buzz’s favorite spot in the forest and shared the honey. Since that day, Buzz and Flutter have been the best of friends and look forward to sharing honey every day.
