Category: Intermediate
Title: The Box and the Car

Once upon a time, in a bright sunny land, there was a big box. In that box, there were many toys. The toys loved to play all day long. One day, a new toy came to the box. It was a small car. The car was very happy to meet new friends. "Hello, I am new here," said the small car. "Can I play with you?" The other toys said yes, and they all played together. They played many fun games. But the small car was sad because it could not fit in some places. The other toys tried to help, but they did not know what to do. One day, a wise old toy said, "I have an idea. We can all work together to make more space for the small car." All the toys agreed, and they started to work. They moved things around and made the box bigger. Soon, the small car could fit everywhere. The small car was very happy, and they all played together forever.

Once upon a time, there was a big, fierce bear. The bear lived in a forest near a small tent. In the tent, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim and the bear were friends. One day, Tim and the bear were playing near the tent. Tim was hungry, so he said, "Bear, I want to eat. Can you help me find food?" The bear nodded and they both went to look for food in the forest. They found some yummy berries and ate them together. After they ate, they went back to the tent and played some more. Tim and the bear were very happy and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big cage. Inside the cage, there was a very smart bird. The bird was very intelligent. One day, the bird saw a big tree shake. The bird knew something was going to happen. The next day, the bird saw the tree shake again. This time, the bird saw a little squirrel come out of the tree. The squirrel was very fast. The bird knew the squirrel was the one that made the tree shake. The bird wanted to be friends with the squirrel. The bird and the squirrel became friends. They played together every day. One day, the squirrel shook the tree very hard. The cage door opened! The bird was free! The bird and the squirrel were very happy. They played together in the big tree every day.

Harry and his mommy were going on an adventure. They drove in the car and passed a lot of interesting things. Harry asked his mommy what they were doing and she mentioned they were going to the store to get some gear. At the store, there were lots of different things to choose from. There were big, expensive things and small, cheap things. Harry was so excited to pick a gear for their adventure. He picked up a big, shiny, and expensive gear. Harry and his mommy then went and put the gear in their car. They drove home and Harry couldn't wait to use his new gear. He had such a great day with his mommy and was so excited for their next adventure!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a big box that she wanted to open. She hoped that there was something nice inside. Sue tried to open the box, but it was very hard. Sue asked her friend, Tom, for help. Tom was not very smart, and he did something stupid. He tried to open the box by jumping on it. This made Sue laugh, but it did not open the box. Finally, Sue's mom came and helped them open the box. Inside, they found a beautiful toy. Sue and Tom were very happy. They played with the toy all day, and they learned that it is better to ask for help than to do something stupid.

One day, a girl named Sue wanted to design a toy. She thought about what she could make. She wanted the toy to be fun and flexible. Sue had an idea! She would make a snake with a big knob for its head. Sue found some clay and began to design her snake. She made a long, flexible body and a big knob for the head. When she was done, she showed her snake to her friend, Tim. "Look at my snake, Tim!" Sue said. "It has a big knob head and a flexible body!" "Wow, Sue!" Tim said. "That's so cool! I want to play with it too!" Sue and Tim played with the snake all day. They made it twist and turn. They even made it dance! They had so much fun with Sue's flexible snake with a big knob head.
