Category: Intermediate
Title: The Fisherman and the Boat

Once upon a time, there was a fisherman. He had a big boat. He was very reliable. He caught many fish every day. One day, his friend needed help. His friend asked, "Can you lend me your boat?" The fisherman said, "Yes, I will lend you my boat." His friend was very happy. The friend caught many fish too. He gave some fish to the fisherman. They were both happy. They shared the fish with their families. The fisherman was a good and reliable friend.

Once there was a man. He was very popular. He liked beef for his meals. One day he wanted to fix his car. But he didn't know how. He looked for someone to help him. Finally he found a repairman who helped him fix his car. He was very happy. He thanked the man and gave him some beef as a reward. The repairman was very pleased. Afterwards, people knew the man was a helpful and popular man. Everyone liked him for his kindness. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy loved to play hockey. She had a shiny, blue hockey stick and a brand new puck. One day, when she was playing hockey, the ground outside turned icy. Lucy stopped and looked around. "It's so cold," she whispered. Just then, Lucy's mom came outside. "Let's go inside and find something warm to do," she said. But Lucy shook her head. "I want to keep playing hockey," she said. "I hope this icy ground will keep my puck sliding well." Lucy's mom smiled. She put her hand on Lucy's shoulder. "I hope so, too," she said. And Lucy did keep playing hockey. The icy ground made her puck go really fast. Lucy smiled and spun around with her stick. She was so happy and proud!

Lily and Ben were playing in the snow. They made a big snowman with a hat, a carrot nose and two buttons. Lily wanted to give the snowman a scarf, but she could not find one. "Look, Ben!" she said. "I have an idea. We can use your scarf for the snowman. It is gray and warm." Ben shook his head. "No, Lily. I need my scarf. It is very cold and I have a secret under my scarf." Lily was curious. "What secret, Ben? Please tell me. I am your sister. I will not laugh." Ben looked around. He did not see anyone else. He whispered to Lily. "OK, Lily. But you have to promise not to tell anyone. It is a big secret." Lily nodded. "I promise, Ben. Cross my heart." Ben lifted his scarf and revealed his secret. He had a big red spot on his chin. He had fallen on the ice yesterday and hurt himself. Lily gasped. "Oh, Ben. That looks sore. Does it hurt?" Ben nodded. "Yes, Lily. It hurts a lot. That is why I wear my scarf. I do not want anyone to see it. They might tease me or call me names." Lily hugged Ben. "I am sorry, Ben. You are very brave. I will not tell anyone your secret. And I will not take your scarf. You can keep it." Ben smiled. "Thank you, Lily. You are very kind. And you are the best sister ever." They hugged again and then went back to playing with the snowman. They decided to use a branch for the scarf instead. They had fun in the snow and forgot about the spot. They were happy.

Once upon a time, in a small house near the woods, lived a boy named Tim. Tim liked to listen to the sounds of the birds and the wind. One day, it was very gloomy outside, and the sky was full of dark clouds. Tim felt a little sad because he could not play outside. While Tim was inside, he heard a strange sound coming from the woods. He thought it might be a threat, like a big, mean animal. Tim was scared, but he wanted to be brave and help his friends in the woods. Tim went to the woods and listened very carefully. He saw a small, lost bird that was making the strange sound. The bird was not a threat at all! Tim felt happy that he could help the bird find its way back home. The gloomy day turned bright and sunny, and Tim played outside with his new bird friend.
