Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max had a job. His job was to help his friend, a big dog named Sam. Max was a very good helper. One day, Max and Sam had a big job to do. They needed to find a lost ball. Max was impatient. He wanted to find the ball fast. Max and Sam looked in the yard, under the tree, and behind the house. Max did not give up. He tried very hard to succeed. Finally, Max found the ball under a bush. Max and Sam were happy. They did their job and found the ball. Max learned that being patient helps you succeed.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that he loved to play with. One day, while playing with his car, it broke. Tim was very sad because his car was broken. Tim's mom saw the broken car and had an idea. She found a piece of paper and drew a sign on it. The sign had a picture of a car with a big red "X" on it. She showed the sign to Tim and said, "This sign means no more broken cars." Tim didn't understand, but he smiled anyway. The next day, Tim's mom took him to the store. Tim saw a new toy car that he liked. His mom looked at the sign she made and then bought the new car for Tim. Tim was so happy and surprised! From that day on, Tim took good care of his new car, and it never had broken skin. And every time he saw the sign, he remembered to be careful with his toys.

One day, a little girl named Amy was playing with her toys. She had a toy cat named Mr. Whiskers. Amy loved Mr. Whiskers and took him everywhere she went. Mr. Whiskers had a special style with a big bow on his neck. Amy was very grateful for her toy cat. One day, Amy could not find Mr. Whiskers. She started to search everywhere. She looked under her bed, in the toy box, and in the living room. Amy was very sad and asked her mom, "Have you seen Mr. Whiskers?" Her mom said, "No, but let's search together." They looked in the kitchen, the bathroom, and even outside. Suddenly, Amy's little brother came running in with a big smile. He had found Mr. Whiskers! He had been hiding in the laundry basket. Amy was so happy and grateful to find her toy cat. "Thank you for finding Mr. Whiskers!" Amy said to her brother. They both laughed and played with Mr. Whiskers together. From that day on, Amy made sure to always keep her toy cat close by. And she learned that sometimes, help can come from the most unexpected places.

Once upon a time in a small town, there was a smart little girl named Lily. Lily loved to ask questions. She asked questions about everything she saw. One day, she went for a walk with her mom down the road. As they walked down the road, Lily saw a big tree. She asked her mom, "Why is the tree so big?" Her mom said, "The tree is big because it has been growing for a long time." Lily thought that was very interesting. Next, Lily saw a cat sitting by the road. She asked her mom, "Why does the cat have a tail?" Her mom said, "The cat has a tail to help it balance when it walks and jumps." Lily liked learning new things and felt very smart.

One day, a boy named Tim found a flute in his toy box. He did not know how it got there. Tim really wanted to play the flute. He picked it up and started to play. It made a pretty sound. Tim was happy. Tim had a friend named Sam. He urged Sam to come over and see the flute. Sam liked spicy food, so he brought some spicy chips to share. They played with the flute and ate spicy chips. They were having a lot of fun. Then, something unexpected happened. The flute started to make a different sound. The sound was like magic. Suddenly, their toys started to dance! Tim and Sam were so surprised. The flute made their toys come to life! They laughed and played with their dancing toys all day long.
