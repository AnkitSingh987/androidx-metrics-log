Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Player and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little player named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys. One day, he got a new toy in a big box. He was very excited to see what was inside. He felt a little nervous, but he knew he had to unpack the box to find out. Tim sat down and started to unpack the box. He pulled out a big, shiny toy car. Tim's eyes got big and he smiled. He knew this toy car would be so much fun to play with. He pushed the car around the room and laughed. As Tim played with his new toy car, he felt happy. He was not nervous anymore. He knew that sometimes good things come in big boxes. And when it was time to unpack another box, he would be brave and excited.

Once upon a time, there was a charming little girl named Emma. She lived in a small village near the woods with her mom and dad. One day, Emma had an urge to explore the woods. She ran to the kitchen, grabbed a large jug and set off. She walked through the woods, marveling at all the beauty. Suddenly, she heard a noise. It was a strange sound coming from a tall tree nearby. When she got closer, she realized that the sound was coming from a single bird that was perched at the top of the tree. It seemed to be calling to her and it's voice was so lovely and strong. Emma wanted to get closer, but her mom had warned her never to go too near strange animals in the woods. She was scared but the bird's voice urged her closer. Finally, the bird flew away but Emma was determined to find it's nest. She searched and searched until she finally spotted it. Inside there were three little eggs and Emma was delighted. She looked around her and realized that the woods were full of charming creatures. Smiling, Emma filled her jug of water and carried it back home. She had learned an important lesson that day: never ignore those little urges, you never know what you might find!

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had white fur and loved to play outside. One day, while playing, Spot got very dirty. His white fur was covered in mud. Spot felt ashamed because he didn't look nice anymore. Spot went to his friend, Cat, and asked for help. "Cat, can you help me clean my white fur?" Spot asked. Cat agreed to help and they went to find water. They found a small pond and Cat showed Spot how to wash off the mud. After a while, all the mud was gone and Spot's fur was white again. Spot thanked Cat for helping him and they played together for the rest of the day. Spot was happy and not ashamed anymore. From that day on, Spot always asked for help when he needed it.

Ben likes hockey very much. He has a hockey stick and a ball. He plays hockey with his dad in the yard. He wants to be a good hockey player. One day, Ben sees a sign at the park. It says: "Hockey Test for Kids". Ben is curious. He asks his dad: "What is a hockey test?" His dad says: "It is a fun game. You can show how well you can play hockey. You can meet other kids who like hockey too." Ben says: "I want to do the hockey test. Can we go to the park?" His dad says: "Sure, we can go. But you have to be independent. You have to play by yourself. I can watch you, but I cannot help you. Do you understand?" Ben says: "Yes, I understand. I can play by myself. I am independent." They go to the park. Ben sees many kids with hockey sticks and balls. He joins them. He listens to the rules. He tries his best. He has fun. He makes new friends. He is happy. He did the hockey test. He is a good hockey player. He is independent.

Once upon a time, there was a little ant named Tim. Tim was a harmless ant who liked to help his friends. He lived in a big garden with many other ants. They all worked together to serve food to their queen ant. Tim was very good at his job. One day, Tim met a new friend named Sam. Sam was a caterpillar who lived on a big green leaf. Sam said, "Hi, Tim! I like the warm season. The sun is nice and bright." Tim agreed and said, "Yes, I like this season too. It's great for finding food." Tim and Sam played together in the garden every day. They had lots of fun in the warm season. Soon, it was time for Sam to change into a butterfly. Tim was sad to see his friend go, but he knew Sam would be back to play again next season.
