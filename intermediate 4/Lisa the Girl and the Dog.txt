Category: Intermediate
Title: Lisa the Girl and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lisa. She had a small, harmless dog named Max. Lisa and Max had a lot of love for each other. They played all day and were very happy. One day, Lisa and Max were playing in the park. They saw a big boy with a big dog. The big boy said, "My dog is better than your dog." Lisa did not like this. She said, "No, my dog is the best." They started to disagree. Lisa and the big boy had an idea. They would have a race to see whose dog was the best. Max and the big dog ran very fast. Max was small, but he tried very hard. In the end, Max won the race. Lisa was very happy, and the big boy was sad. They learned that even small, harmless dogs can be the best.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin lived in an icy cold pond. He had many friends who lived there too. They liked to play games and swim all day. One day, while playing, Fin noticed something shiny on the bottom of the pond. He swam down to see what it was. It was a shiny scale from another fish. Fin thought it was very pretty. Fin showed the shiny scale to his friends. They all thought it was beautiful too. They took turns holding the scale and playing with it. The icy pond had never been so fun. And from that day on, Fin and his friends had a special treasure to play with and share.

One day, a little bee was flying in the garden. She saw many flowers and smelled their sweet nectar. She wanted to take some nectar back to her hive, where her family and friends lived. She flew from flower to flower, filling her belly with nectar. But as she was flying, she saw a big bird. The bird was hungry and wanted to eat the little bee. The bird flew fast and chased the little bee. The little bee was scared and tried to fly away. She flew over the fence, over the road, over the trees, but the bird was still behind her. She saw a big house with a window. She flew inside the window, hoping to hide from the bird. But inside the house, there was a little girl. The little girl was playing with her toys. She saw the little bee and smiled. She liked bees and knew they were good for the flowers. She was compassionate and wanted to help the little bee. She gently picked up the little bee and put her in a jar. She poked some holes in the lid so the little bee could breathe. She gave the little bee some water and some honey. She said, "Don't worry, little bee. I will keep you safe until the bird is gone. Then I will let you go back to your hive." The little bee was grateful and happy. She drank the water and the honey and rested in the jar. She saw the bird fly away, looking for another snack. She saw the little girl smile and wave at her. She said, "Thank you, little girl. You are very kind and compassionate. You saved my life. I will never forget you." The little girl opened the jar and let the little bee go. The little bee flew out of the window and back to the garden. She flew to her hive, where her family and friends were waiting for her. She told them what happened and how the little girl helped her. They all cheered and thanked the little girl. They said, "The little girl is our friend. She is compassionate and brave. We will always remember her." The moral of the story is: Be compassionate and help those in need. You may make a friend for life.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a toy named Mr. Brain. Mr. Brain was very smart but also very fragile. One day, Amy wanted to play with her friends outside. She didn't want Mr. Brain to feel lonely, so she asked her mom, "Can Mr. Brain come outside and play with us?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful. Remember, Mr. Brain is fragile." Amy and her friends played outside all day. They were very careful with Mr. Brain. They knew he was fragile, so they didn't throw him or let him fall. They asked Mr. Brain many questions and he helped them learn new things. At the end of the day, Amy took Mr. Brain back inside the house. She was happy that she had asked her mom if he could come outside. Mr. Brain was still in one piece because everyone had been careful with him. Amy learned that it's important to ask questions and be careful with fragile things. And Mr. Brain was always there to help her learn and play.
