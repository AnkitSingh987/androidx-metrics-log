Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue liked to make things. One day, she found a bottle of ink. It was not an ordinary ink. It was a magic ink! Sue wanted to make a picture with the magic ink. She drew a cat on a piece of paper. Suddenly, the cat jumped off the paper! Sue was very surprised. The cat was now real and alive. Sue and the cat became best friends. They played and had fun together. But one day, the cat found the magic ink. The cat played with the ink and made a big mess. Sue was sad, but then she saw that the ink made more magic animals. Now, Sue and the cat had even more friends to play with. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was a fearful girl who liked to mail letters to her grandma. One day, she was going to mail a letter when she saw a big dog. Sue was scared of the big dog, but she wanted to mail her letter. Sue put her lunch in her bag and walked to the mailbox. The big dog followed her. Sue was very scared, but she had an idea. She took her lunch out of her bag and gave it to the big dog. The big dog ate the lunch and wagged its tail. Sue was not scared anymore. Sue and the big dog became friends. They played together and mailed letters to grandma. The big dog was not scary, but a nice dog. Sue learned that sometimes things that seem scary can be nice.

Ben and Mia were having a tea party with their stuffed animals. They poured water from a plastic pot into tiny cups and pretended it was tea. They gave some to their animals and said, "Here you go, Mr. Bear. Here you go, Mrs. Bunny. Drink your tea and be polite." Suddenly, they felt the floor shake and heard a loud noise. It was an earthquake! The cups and the pot fell over and spilled water everywhere. The animals tumbled down from the chairs. Ben and Mia were scared and hugged each other. "Mommy! Daddy!" they cried. Their parents came running into the room and saw the mess. They picked up Ben and Mia and held them tight. "Don't worry, sweeties. It's over now. You're safe with us," they said. They checked the house for any damage and found none. They cleaned up the water and the toys and put them away. "Can we have a real tea party now?" Ben asked. "Sure, why not?" their mom said. "But first, let's thank God for keeping us safe and pray for those who might be hurt by the earthquake." They all bowed their heads and said a prayer. Then they went to the kitchen and made some real tea and cookies. They sat around the table and enjoyed their snack. They were polite and shared with each other. They were happy and grateful. They forgot about the earthquake and had fun.

Once upon a time there was a young boy called Tim. Every day Tim liked to go for a walk around his neighbourhood. One afternoon on his daily walk, Tim met an old man. The old man smiled at Tim and introduced himself as Mr Jones. He was very friendly and asked Tim if he wanted to come for a walk with him. Tim liked the old man and he agreed to go for a walk. As they were walking around, Mr Jones was telling Tim all about his adventures when he was a young boy. Later on in the walk, Mr Jones took Tim to a pond. He told Tim that when he was young he loved to feed the ducks and he showed Tim how to do it. When it was time to go home, Tim asked Mr Jones if he wanted to come with him. To his surprise the old man accepted, and the two of them started walking together towards Tim's house. From then on, Tim and Mr Jones became good friends. They would go for a walk every day, and Tim loved to hear all the stories Mr Jones would tell him.
