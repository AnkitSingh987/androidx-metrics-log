Category: Intermediate
Title: The Modest House and the Car

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a modest house. In this house, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was a good girl who always listened to her mom and dad. One day, Sue's mom asked her to go to the store. On her way, she saw a lot of traffic on the road. Sue thought it was fun to watch the cars go by. She saw a small, quiet spot next to the road and decided to lie down and watch the cars. As Sue lay there, a kind man saw her and stopped his car. He told Sue that lying next to the road was not safe. Sue realized she had made a mistake and thanked the man for helping her. She went to the store and back home safely. From that day on, Sue learned the importance of being careful and never lay near traffic again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to pretend. One day, he found a big, clean package in his room. He was very happy. Tim called his friend, Sue. "Sue, come and see the big, clean package!" he said. Sue came running to Tim's room. They both looked at the package and smiled. "What do you think is inside?" asked Sue. Tim thought for a moment. "Let's pretend it's full of toys!" he said. They both opened the package and played with their pretend toys all day long. They had so much fun.

Once upon a time, a robot had a big job to do. He had to deliver a box to a far away house. The road was rough, but the robot was strong. He walked and walked, carrying the box. As the robot walked, he met a cat. The cat wanted to see what was inside the box. The robot said, "No, I must deliver the box." The cat was sad, but the robot kept going. Finally, the robot got to the house. He gave the box to the person who lived there. The person opened the box, and guess what? The cat was inside! The robot and the person laughed. The cat had been in the box all along. The robot was happy he delivered the box, and the person was happy to have a new friend.

One day, a little ant was walking in the forest. He was an honest ant who always told the truth. As he walked, he met a big bear. The bear was sad and lost. The ant said, "Hi, bear! I know the way home. I can help you." The bear looked down and saw the tiny ant. "How can you help me? You are so small!" said the bear. But the ant was smart. He climbed on the bear's back and showed him the way. As they walked, they saw a big wall in front of them. The bear was scared. But the honest ant knew a secret door in the wall. He showed it to the bear, and they went through. The bear was very happy and thanked the little ant. From that day on, they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to dig and bury his toys in the yard. One day, it was very wet outside because of a big storm. Spot wanted to play, but he knew it was too wet to go outside. Spot looked out the window and saw his friend, Birdie. Birdie was sitting on a branch, getting wet from the rain. Spot opened the door and called out, "Birdie, come inside! It's too wet to play out there!" Birdie flew inside and they played together all day. They played hide and seek, and Spot even let Birdie help bury his toys in a big pile of blankets. When the storm was over, they went outside to play again. Spot and Birdie were very happy to have each other as friends, no matter if it was wet or sunny outside.

Adam was only three years old and he loved to play. One day, his parents invited his best friend Rob over to their house. When Rob arrived, he had a big bag with him that Adam wanted to look at. Adam asked, "What is in the bag Rob?" Rob smiled, "It's a surprise. I have something special for you!" Adam was excited. He quickly opened the bag and pulled out a bright yellow port! Rob had remembered Adam's favorite color. Adam was happy and proud. He turned to his parents and said, "Look what Rob brought me!" His father smiled and said, "Let's invite Rob inside and have some lunch." Adam was happy to have his friend inside the house and he felt embarrassed. He said, "Don't worry Rob, you don't have to be embarrassed. We'd love to have you here with us." Rob smiled and thanked Adam. They all had a great time together, playing and eating lunch. Adam showed Rob all his other toys and they had a wonderful time.
