Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Sam and the Door

Tom and Sam were brothers. They liked to play in the big building where they lived. One day, they saw a new elevator. It was shiny and fast. They wanted to try it. "Let's go inside!" Tom said. "OK!" Sam said. They pushed the button and the door opened. They ran inside and pushed more buttons. The elevator went up and down very fast. They laughed and screamed. "Stop it!" a voice said. It was the man who worked in the building. He was angry. "You are making too much noise! You are breaking the elevator!" Tom and Sam were scared. They stopped pushing buttons. They wanted to get out. But the elevator did not stop. It made a loud noise and shook. Then it stopped in the middle of the floor. The door did not open. The lights went out. It was dark and quiet. "Help!" Tom and Sam cried. "We are stuck!" They banged on the door and shouted. But no one heard them. They waited and waited. They were hungry and thirsty and cold. They were sorry. They regretted playing with the elevator. They wished they had listened to the man. The moral of the story is: Do not play with things that are not yours. You might get in trouble or hurt yourself. Always listen to the adults who know better.

One day, a cute little bird was flying around. It saw a fountain with water. The bird was very happy. It wanted to bathe in the water. The bird went to the fountain and said, "Hello, fountain! Can I bathe in your water?" The fountain said, "Yes, little bird, you can bathe in my water." The bird bathed in the water and felt very clean. A big bird came and saw the little bird. The big bird said, "You look clean and happy, little bird." The little bird said, "Yes, I am. I bathed in the water from the fountain." The big bird wanted to be clean too. So, it also bathed in the fountain. Both birds were clean and happy. They became friends and played together. The moral of the story is: Sharing good things with others can make you happy and make new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Anna. Anna wanted to go on an adventure. So, she set off to find a port. When she arrived at the port, a man was working. Anna thought it looked like fun, so she asked if she could help him. The man said yes and showed Anna exactly what to do. She was very excited to be working with him. Together, they worked hard all day long. When the job was finished, the man gave Anna a surprise - a delicious treat! Anna was so happy to be rewarded for her hard work. She said goodbye to the man and then headed home. Even though it was a lot of work, Anna had a lot of fun and was very proud of herself for helping out.

Once upon a time there were two friends. They were called Sandy and Duke. They both lived on the same big garden and they became good friends. They loved to play on the lawn together. They enjoyed running and jumping on the soft grass. One day, a huge storm came. It destroyed the lawn. Sandy and Duke were very sad. They did not know what to do. One day, an idea came to them. They decided to unite and fix the lawn together. They worked hard to clear the mess and replant the grass. After two days, the garden was back to looking beautiful. Sandy and Duke were so proud of what they achieved together. The lawn was now safe, and they were the happiest friends in the world.

Once upon a time, there was a big boat. The boat had a big anchor. The boat liked to go to different places. The boat had many friends. One day, the boat met a new friend, a little fish. The little fish was different. The little fish wanted to play with the boat. The boat was happy to have a new friend. The little fish said, "Let's play a game!" The boat insisted, "Yes, let's play!" They played all day and had lots of fun. The boat and the little fish were very happy together. And they stayed friends forever.
