Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Candy

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved candy. One day, Tim saw a big jar of candy on the table. He wanted to eat some, but he was too short to reach it. He called for his friend, Sam the dog. "Sam, can you help me get the candy?" Tim asked. Sam wagged his tail and jumped up to knock the jar off the table. The candy fell all over the floor. Tim was so happy but also a little careless. He started to eat the candy from the floor. His hands and face got very sticky. Mom saw Tim and said, "Tim, you must wipe your hands and face before you eat more candy. It is not clean to eat from the floor." Tim listened to Mom and wiped his hands and face. He learned not to be careless and to always keep clean when eating candy. From that day on, Tim and Sam always made sure to wipe their hands and face before eating their favorite treat.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max had a job to do. He had to work hard every day. He had to help the doctor with an operation. Max was very anxious. He wanted to do a good job. One day, Max went to work with the doctor. The doctor said, "Max, you must help me with this operation. We need to make the sick cat feel better." Max nodded and tried to help the doctor. He held the tools for the doctor and watched him work. But Max was too anxious. He dropped the tools and they fell on the floor. The doctor looked at Max and said, "Oh no, Max! We can't finish the operation now. The cat won't get better." Max felt very sad. He knew he had not done a good job. Max went home that day feeling very bad. He knew that he had let the doctor and the cat down. The cat did not get better, and Max was very sad. He wished he could have done a better job, but it was too late. The cat stayed sick, and Max felt like he had failed.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom had a toy rubber duck that he loved very much. He would use it to play in the water every day. His friend, Sam, saw the rubber duck and felt envious. Sam wished he had a rubber duck too. One day, Tom went to the park with his rubber duck. He played near the pond with the duck, and Sam watched him. Sam thought of a plan to get the rubber duck. He knew that Tom would go to the park again tomorrow. Sam decided he would try to get the rubber duck the next day. The next day, Tom came to the park with his rubber duck again. Sam was hiding behind a tree, waiting for the right time to take the duck. But when he saw Tom's big smile, he felt bad. Instead of taking the duck, Sam decided to talk to Tom. He told Tom how much he liked the rubber duck and how envious he was. Tom smiled and said, "We can share the rubber duck and play together!" From that day on, Tom and Sam played together with the rubber duck and became the best of friends.

Harry and his mommy were walking to the stadium. It was a hot day and the sun was melting the ice cream they were carrying. Harry was very worried. Mama said, "Don't be worried, Harry. We'll get to the stadium soon!" Harry smiled as he skipped and hopped along the way. Along the way, his mama stopped and said, "Let's have some of that melting ice cream before it melts away." So Harry took a lick of the sweet, cold ice cream. He smiled and said,"Yum, mama! That was delicious!" Mama smiled and said, "Let's hurry up so we can get to the stadium before the ice cream melts away!" Harry smiled and they kept walking until they arrived at the stadium. Harry was so excited! He couldn't wait to explore the stadium and watch the game!
