Category: Intermediate
Title: The Jolly Man and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a jolly man. He had a big box of nails. He liked to help people fix things. He was very good at it. One day, a little girl needed help. Her toy was broken. The jolly man used a nail to fix it. The little girl was very happy. She said, "Thank you, jolly man!" The jolly man knew that helping others was important. He knew that the nails had value. He was always ready to help with his nails. And everyone loved the jolly man with his box of nails.

Lily and Sam were at the zoo with their mom. They wanted to see the elephants, but there was a long line of people waiting. Lily and Sam were impatient. They did not like to wait. "Mom, can we go see the elephants now?" Lily asked. "Not yet, sweetie. We have to wait for our turn," Mom said. "But we have been waiting for a long time!" Sam said. "I know, honey. But we have to be polite and patient. The elephants are very popular and everyone wants to see them," Mom said. Lily and Sam sighed. They looked at the other animals, but they were not as interesting as the elephants. They saw monkeys, giraffes, zebras, and lions, but they did not care. They only wanted to see the elephants. Finally, it was their turn. They ran to the fence and looked at the big gray animals. They were amazed by their long trunks, big ears, and strong legs. They watched them eat, drink, and play with each other. They smiled and laughed. "Look, Mom, that one is spraying water with its trunk!" Lily said. "Wow, that one is lifting a log with its trunk!" Sam said. "They are so cool, Mom!" Lily and Sam said. Mom smiled and took a picture of them. She was happy to see them enjoy the elephants. "Mom, can we feed them?" Lily asked. "Sorry, sweetie, we can't. The zookeepers prevent us from feeding them. They have a special diet and we might make them sick if we give them our food," Mom said. "Oh, I see. That makes sense," Lily said. "Can we touch them?" Sam asked. "Sorry, honey, we can't. The zookeepers prevent us from touching them. They might be scared or angry if we get too close and they might hurt us with their tusks or feet," Mom said. "Oh, I see. That makes sense," Sam said. Lily and Sam nodded. They understood that the zookeepers were trying to protect them and the elephants. They decided to just watch them and wave at them. They thanked Mom for taking them to the zoo. They had a lot of fun. They learned a lot about the elephants. They were not impatient anymore. They were happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a magic top that could spin. He was so excited to play with it. He would spin the top and watch it go round and round. The top had a special power that made it spin for a very long time. Tim would spin the top every day and show it to his friends. One day, Tim was worried because he could not find the magic top. He looked everywhere for it, but it was gone. He was sad and did not know what to do. His friends tried to help him find the top, but they could not find it either. Tim felt very sad and missed his magic top. Then, something unexpected happened. Tim's little sister, who was always watching him play with the top, found it under her bed. She had been playing with it and forgot to give it back to Tim. She gave the top back to Tim, and he was so happy. Tim and his sister spun the top together, and they both had fun. The magic top made them both smile and forget their worries.

One day, a restless little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a man in a suit who looked important. Tim asked his mom, "Who is that man?" His mom said, "That is a lawyer, he helps people with rules." Tim wanted to play with the lawyer, so he went up to him and said, "Hi, do you want to play with me?" The lawyer smiled and said, "Not now, I have work to do. But, let's play later, okay?" Tim was sad, but he said okay and went to play on the swings. After some time, the lawyer finished his work and came to Tim. He said, "Now, I can play with you! Let's have fun on the slide." Tim was happy and they both played together. The lawyer had lots of fun and Tim made a new friend.
