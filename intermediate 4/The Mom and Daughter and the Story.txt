Category: Intermediate
Title: The Mom and Daughter and the Story

Once there was a mom and a daughter. The daughter was very young, only three years old. The mom wanted to teach her daughter a very important lesson. The mom said to her daughter, “My love, I want to show you something. I want you to look at this symbol. It’s a very special symbol.” The daughter asked, “What does it mean?” The mom replied, “It’s the symbol of patience. Patience means that even when things are hard, you can stay calm and wait. It’s very important to be patient. You need to take some time to rest and relax.” The daughter nodded and said, “Okay. I understand. I will be patient.” The mom smiled and hugged her daughter. The moral of the story is that patience is a virtue. Taking the time to rest and relax can help us be more patient when hard times come our way.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park with her mom. She saw a big tree with yummy pears on it. Mia wanted to eat a pear, so she asked her mom to help her get one. Her mom said, "Be careful, do not drop the pear or it will hurt." Mia tried to reach for a pear, but she was too short. Then, a nice boy named Tom came by and helped her get a pear. Mia was happy and said, "Thank you, Tom!" But when Mia held the pear, she dropped it. The pear fell on her foot and it hurt a little. Mia felt sad and thought she could not eat the pear anymore. But Tom said, "Do not worry, we can wash it and it will be good to eat." They washed the pear and ate it together. Mia learned that even if you make a mistake, you can fix it with the help of a friend.

Once upon a time, there was a princess. She lived in a big castle. The princess liked to play outside. One day, it started to rain. The rain made everything wet. The princess put on her boots and coat. She went outside to play in the rain. The princess jumped in the wet puddles. She danced and sang in the rain. She had so much fun. When the rain stopped, the princess went back to her castle. She was happy and loved the wet rainy day.

Once upon a time, there was a great teacher. He had a special job. He taught people how to use a razor. One day, the teacher called a 3-year-old boy to him. The boy was excited, but also a bit scared. The teacher showed the boy a razor, and said it was very special. He said it was very important to be careful, and explained how to use it. Then he gave the boy a practice razor and let him try it. The boy was scared at first, but the teacher was very patient and helped him every step of the way. The teacher praised the boy for his great work, and soon he was a razor expert! From that day on, the 3-year-old boy used his razor with confidence. He was very proud of what he had learned, and he thanked the great teacher for teaching him.

One day, a white cat named Lily went outside to play. She saw a post near the big tree. Lily wanted to play with the post, so she thought of a fun game. Lily found a long string and said, "I will tie this string to the post and play jump rope!" She tied the string to the post and started to play. She jumped and laughed as she played her game. Soon, her friend, a little dog named Max, came over. Max saw Lily playing and asked, "Can I play too?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes! Let's play together!" So, Lily and Max played jump rope with the string tied to the post. They had lots of fun and played all day long.

One day, a cat named Tom found a long, yummy ice cream in the kitchen. He wanted to eat it all by himself. But he knew that his friend, a dog named Jerry, was feeling sad. Tom thought of a plan to make Jerry happy. Tom took the ice cream outside and called Jerry to play with him. They ran around and had lots of fun. But Tom forgot about the ice cream. When they came back, the ice cream had started to melt. Tom felt bad, but Jerry smiled and said they could still share it. They both ate the ice cream together, even though it was a little melted. Tom and Jerry learned that sharing with friends is more fun than keeping things for yourself. And they knew that next time, they would share from the start, so their ice cream wouldn't melt away.
