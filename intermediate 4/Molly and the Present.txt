Category: Intermediate
Title: Molly and the Present

Once upon a time, there were two best friends named Molly and Jack. Molly had a very important job - she needed to create something special for her family. But it was tricky. She was not sure what to do. Jack had a good idea. "Let's wrap a present," he said. Molly thought it was a great idea and they crafted the perfect present. Molly gathered colourful paper and Jack helped her tie a nice bow. Together they wrapped the present with a big hug. When they were finished, Molly was so proud of what they had created - it was a beautiful present. Later that day, her family was so happy when they opened their gift. They said Molly and Jack did a very important job. Molly and Jack looked at each other and smiled - they felt happy that they could share the special moment with her family.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. He had a big dog named Max. Max was very strong and liked to run fast. Tom used a leash to walk Max so he would not run away. One day, Tom and Max went for a walk. They saw a big door. The door was open. Tom thought it would be fun to see what was inside. He did not shut the door behind him. Inside, they found a big cat. The cat did not like Max. The cat hissed and jumped on Max. Max got scared and pulled on the leash. Tom fell down and the leash broke. Max ran away and Tom could not find him. Tom was very sad because he lost his best friend.

Anna and Ben are playing in the park. They see a big stage with many chairs. They run to the stage and climb up the stairs. They see a microphone on a stand. Anna wants to try it. "Hello, hello, can you hear me?" Anna says into the microphone. Her voice is very loud and echoes in the park. Ben laughs and claps his hands. But then, a man comes to the stage. He looks angry and has a big hat. He shouts at Anna and Ben. "Hey, you kids, get off the stage! This is not a toy! This is for the concert tonight!" Anna and Ben are frightened. They drop the microphone and run down the stairs. They look for their mom and dad. They find them on a bench and hug them. "Mom, dad, we are sorry. We saw a microphone and wanted to play. But a man yelled at us and scared us," Anna says. "It's okay, sweetie. You didn't know. But you have to be careful. Some things are not for playing. They are for working or for special events. The microphone is for the singers who will perform tonight. They need it to sing to the people," mom says. "Oh, I see. I like singing, but I don't need a microphone. I can sing with my own voice," Ben says. "That's right, Ben. You have a beautiful voice. And you can sing anytime you want. But not on the stage. That's for the professionals," dad says. Anna and Ben nod and smile. They are not frightened anymore. They are happy. They ask their mom and dad if they can watch the concert tonight. Mom and dad say yes. They think it will be fun. They hope the man with the big hat will not be angry anymore. They hope he will like their singing too.

One day, a big machine was in a small room. It was not fit. The machine wanted to escape. It did not like the small room. The machine was sad. A little girl came into the room. She saw the machine. The girl said, "Why are you sad, machine?" The machine said, "I am too big for this room. I want to escape." The girl wanted to help the machine. She tried to push the machine out of the room. But the machine was too big. It could not escape. The girl and the machine tried and tried. Then, a big wind came. It was a big surprise! The wind pushed the machine out of the room. The machine was happy. But the wind was too strong. The wind pushed the machine far away. The girl was sad. She could not see the machine anymore. The machine was lost. The girl went back to the small room. She missed the machine. The machine missed her too. They could not be friends anymore.
