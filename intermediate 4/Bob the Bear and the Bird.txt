Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Bear and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big bear named Bob. Bob wanted to be successful. He had a plan to make a special suit to help him do many things. One day, Bob went to his friend, a small bird named Sue. He asked her, "Sue, can you help me wrap my suit?" Sue said, "Yes, I can help you!" So, they worked together to wrap the suit with pretty cloth. When the suit was done, Bob put it on. He felt very happy and successful. Bob said, "Thank you, Sue!" And they played together all day long.

Once upon a time, there lived a silly queen in a big castle. Every day, the queen would take walks around the castle and greet all of her subjects. One day, a little girl saw the queen and immediately ran up to her and gave her a big hug! The Queen was amazed! She had never seen such a brave little girl before. Queen: "My child! What is your name?" Little Girl: "My name is Maria!" Queen: "It's lovely to meet you, Maria! Would you like to come have some tea with me?" Little Girl: "Yes please!" So, Maria and the queen had tea together. They talked and laughed for hours! When it was time for Maria to go home, the queen hugged her one last time. Queen: "Goodbye, Maria! It was so much fun having tea with you!" Little Girl: "Goodbye, Queen! I had so much fun!" And that's how a silly queen and a little girl became friends!

Once upon a time, there was a hairy lawyer named Bob. He loved to help people. Bob had a big, funny mustache that made everyone laugh. One day, Bob was very thirsty. He wanted to drink some water. So, he went to the park with his little cup. At the park, he saw a big water fountain. He was so happy! Bob drank the water from the fountain. It was cold and yummy. He felt much better. Then, he saw a little girl who was also thirsty. Bob showed her the water fountain, and they drank together. They both laughed and played at the park. And from that day on, they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big boat. This boat was part of the navy. The boat had a nice man named Tom. Tom liked to polish the boat. He made it shine and look very pretty. One day, Tom saw a small, harmless fish. The fish said, "Hello, Tom! Can I help you polish the boat?" Tom smiled and said, "Yes, little fish. You can help me make the boat shine." So, the little fish and Tom worked together. They polished the boat all day. The boat looked very pretty. The fish and Tom became good friends. They were happy and played together every day.

Ann and Ben were playing in the park one day. Suddenly, they spotted a large, green frog in the pond. "Oh wow!" exclaimed Ben. "Can I pick it up, Ann?" Ann shook her head, and warned Ben. "No, no! It could be dangerous. We should keep away from it." Ann also saw a tasty-looking salad on the picnic table. "Let's have the salad," said Ann. "It looks yummy." Ben was very excited, and eagerly grabbed the salad. They shared it, and it was easy to eat. Afterwards, Ann warned Ben again. "Remember, never ever pick up the frog! Ok?" Ben smiled and agreed. They went back to playing in the park, making sure to stay far away from the frog this time.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was scared of big storms with loud thunder and bright lightning. He would hide under his bed when the storms came. His mom wanted to help him feel better. One day, Tim's mom had an idea. She told Tim about a special friend, Mr. Reliable, who could improve his fear of storms. Mr. Reliable was a big, strong tree that could protect Tim from the lightning. Tim was curious and wanted to meet Mr. Reliable. Tim and his mom walked to the park and found Mr. Reliable. They sat under the tree and talked about the storms. Tim felt safe with Mr. Reliable, and his fear started to improve. The next time a storm came, Tim thought of Mr. Reliable and felt brave. The conflict in his heart was gone.
