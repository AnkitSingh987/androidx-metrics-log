Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Tree and the Forest

One day, a big thick tree and a small thin tree were talking in the forest. The big thick tree said, "I am so strong, no one can hurt me." The small thin tree felt sad because it was not as strong as the big thick tree. A mean man came into the forest with a thing to shoot. He saw the big thick tree and the small thin tree. He wanted to hurt the trees. The small thin tree was scared, but the big thick tree was not scared. The big thick tree said, "Do not worry, I will protect you." The mean man tried to shoot the big thick tree, but the tree was too strong. The shoot did not hurt the big thick tree. The mean man got very angry. He tried to use poison to hurt the big thick tree. But the big thick tree was still too strong for the poison. The mean man gave up and left the forest. The small thin tree was very happy and thanked the big thick tree for protecting it. The big thick tree was happy to help its friend.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his toy, which was a big rock. He would run and jump with the rock in his mouth, all day long. One day, Spot found a new toy. It was a small battery. He did not know what it was, but he thought it was fun to play with. He rolled it on the ground and chased it around. But then, Spot missed his rock. He looked for it everywhere, but he could not find it. Spot felt sad. He decided to be loyal to his rock and not play with the battery anymore. He searched and searched, and finally found his rock under a bush. Spot was so happy to have his rock back, and he promised to never leave it again.

Once upon a time there was a very happy family. The mother had a baby and the older brother was a 3 year old. The 3 year old brother was very excited to have a baby in the family, but he was also a bit scared. One day, the mother said to the 3 year old: "Why don't you be nice to the baby? Would you like to comfort it?" The 3 year old thought about it and after a while he smiled and said yes. The 3 year old came near the baby and started to smile, hug and talk to him. The baby felt warm and safe and the 3 year old was very proud of himself. After that, the 3 year old played a game with the baby. He sang songs, made funny noises and tickled the baby with a feather. The baby laughed so much! It was healthy and fun. The 3 year old was so happy to see that the baby was happy. He would always comfort the baby and play games together.

One day, a big red car was driving down the road. The car saw a little blue car coming from the other way. The big red car wanted to say hello to the little blue car, so it stopped. The little blue car stopped too. They were having a meeting. The big red car said, "Hello, little blue car! Let's play a game. We can reverse and see who goes back the fastest!" The little blue car was alert and said, "Okay, but we must be careful not to hit anything!" They both started to reverse. The big red car was going very fast, but the little blue car was more alert and saw a tree behind it. The little blue car stopped just in time, but the big red car hit the tree. The little blue car said, "Oh no, big red car! Are you okay?" The big red car was sad and said, "Yes, I am okay, but I should have been more alert like you. Thank you for being careful." From that day on, the big red car and the little blue car were always alert when they played together. They learned that being careful and looking out for each other was the best way to have fun.
