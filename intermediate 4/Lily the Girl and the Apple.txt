Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Apple

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys and eat yummy food. Lily was always happy when she played and ate. One day, Lily's mommy gave her a big, red apple. Lily took a big bite and put the apple in her stomach. The apple made her stomach feel good and full. She was very happy. Later, Lily played with her toys and shared her apple with her teddy bear. They both had full stomachs and were very happy. And they lived happily ever after.

---

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the park. They wanted to discuss something. They talked about the air they breathe. The air has something called oxygen. Oxygen is important for all living things. The cat and the dog wanted to make sure the air was safe. They knew that safe air meant they could play and have fun. They asked their friend, the bird, to help them. The bird could fly high and check the air. The bird flew up and looked around. It came back and told the cat and the dog that the air was safe. The cat and the dog were happy. They played in the park all day, knowing they could breathe the safe oxygen.

---

One day, a sweet human named Lily went out to play. She met her friend, Tom, who was sad. Tom had lost his toy and didn't know where to look. Lily wanted to help Tom feel happy again. Lily said, "I recommend we look for your toy by the big tree." They went to the big tree and began to search. They looked high and low, but they could not find the toy. They felt sad and tired. Just then, a big mean dog came and took the toy in its mouth. The dog ran away very fast. Lily and Tom could not catch the dog. They were very sad and cried. The toy was gone forever.

Once upon a time, there was a generous pumpkin. All the other pumpkins in the patch liked her very much. She was always so kind and thoughtful. One day, the pumpkins decided to have a party. The generous pumpkin was very excited. She talked to all the other pumpkins about what fun things they could do. They agreed that they would have a race and dance in the moonlight. When the moon came out, the pumpkins began to race around the patch. The generous pumpkin was the slowest, but it didn't matter. As the others cheered for her, she smiled. When the race was done, the pumpkins danced in the moonlight. All of them sang and twirled around. The generous pumpkin smiled and laughed, feeling so happy to be surrounded by friends. The next morning, the pumpkins parted ways with hugs and happy chatter. The generous pumpkin said goodbye feeling full of joy. All the other pumpkins knew they would see her again soon.

Ben is a bunny who likes to hop. He hops in the grass, he hops in the dirt, he hops in the sun. He hops with his friends, he hops with his mom, he hops with his dad. He hops all day long. One day, Ben hops too far. He hops to the big road, where the cars go fast. He does not see the cars, he only sees the flowers. He hops to the flowers, but he hears a loud noise. It is a car, and it is coming to him. Ben is scared. He tries to hop away, but it is too late. The car hits him, and he feels a lot of pain. He cries out, and his mom hears him. She runs to him, and picks him up. She hugs him, and tells him it is okay. Ben is hurt. He has a big cut on his leg, and it bleeds. His mom takes him to the vet, who helps him. The vet gives him a bandage, and some medicine. He tells Ben he will be fine, but he has to rest. Ben learns a lesson. He learns that the big road is dangerous, and he should not hop there. He learns to listen to his mom, and stay close to her. He learns to be careful, and watch where he hops. He is happy to be alive, and he hopes to hop again soon.
