Category: Intermediate
Title: Kitty the Clumsy Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with her toy motor car. One day, she went to the park to play with her friends. At the park, Kitty saw her friend, a dog named Spot. Spot was playing with a ball. "Kitty, can you send the ball to me?" asked Spot. Kitty tried to kick the ball, but she was clumsy and missed it. Kitty felt sad, but then she had an idea. She went to her toy motor car and put the ball on it. She sent the motor car to Spot with the ball on top. Spot was happy and they played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly mixer named Mixy. Mixy loved to mix things up. Every day, Mixy would wake up and find new things to mix. One day, Mixy found some colors to mix. Mixy mixed red and blue, and made a pretty purple. Mixy mixed yellow and blue, and made a bright green. Mixy was very happy with the new colors. Mixy then found some toys to mix. Mixy mixed cars and trains, and made fun new toys to play with. Mixy mixed dolls and blocks, and made a big tower. Mixy had a great day mixing things up and making new fun things.

Sally had a gloomy day. She was bored and sad. She didn't want to do anything. Her mom suggested they play a game. Sally looked around the room and noticed the ashtray on the table. She remembered that she had a puzzle at home. She said to her mom, "can we replace the ashtray with the puzzle?". Her mom smiled and said, "yes". They went to Sally's room and got the puzzle. Sally carefully held the puzzle and brought it back to the living room. She and her mom replaced the ashtray with the puzzle. Now the room looked much brighter. Sally and her mom sat down and solved the puzzle together. This was much more fun than being gloomy.

One day, a grumpy pigeon named Pete lived in a park. He was always sad and did not like to play with the other birds. Pete would sit on a bench and watch the kids play. One day, a little girl named Sue came to the park. She saw Pete the grumpy pigeon and wanted to be his friend. Sue gave Pete some bread to eat. Pete liked the bread and started to feel happy. Sue came to the park every day to play with Pete. They would run and jump together. Pete was not grumpy anymore. He was happy and did not want to quit playing with Sue. Pete the pigeon and Sue became best friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the sea with his mom and dad. They saw a big yacht. The yacht was white and blue. Tim liked the yacht very much. He wanted to find things that match the yacht. Tim looked around and found a white and blue ball. "Look, mom! The ball matches the yacht!" he said. Mom smiled and said, "Yes, it does!" Tim was very happy. Then, a big wave came and made a loud noise. Tim felt frightened. His mom hugged him and said, "Don't worry, Tim. The wave can't hurt you. We are safe." Tim felt better and continued to play with his ball near the beautiful yacht.

Once upon a time, in a small village, winter came. The snow fell and covered everything. It was very cold, but very pretty. The people in the village wore warm clothes to play outside. They loved this original winter. One day, a little girl named Mia met a talking snowman. The snowman said, "Hello, Mia! I am very cold. Can you help me find a warm hat?" Mia thought for a moment and then ran to her house. She found a big, warm hat for the snowman. Mia put the hat on the snowman's head. The snowman was very happy. He said, "Thank you, Mia! You are very kind." Mia smiled and said, "You're welcome, Mr. Snowman! Let's play together in this beautiful winter." They played all day in the snow. Mia and the snowman had so much fun. When it was time to go home, Mia gave the snowman a big hug. The snowman said, "Thank you for being my friend, Mia." Mia said, "Thank you too, Mr. Snowman. See you tomorrow!" And so, Mia and the snowman became the best of friends. They played together every day during that special winter. And they always remembered to say "thank you" to each other for all the fun they had.
