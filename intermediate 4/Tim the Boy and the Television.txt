Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Television

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was an intelligent boy who loved to try new things. One day, he found a big television in his house. He wanted to see if he could make it work by himself. Tim asked his friend, Sam, "Do you know how to turn on the television?" Sam shook his head and said, "No, but we can try together." They tried to push different buttons, but nothing happened. Tim and Sam felt sad because they could not make the television work. After a while, they tried one more time. This time, the television turned on! But, it was too loud and it scared them. The television fell on the floor and broke into pieces. Tim and Sam were very sad because they broke the television. They knew they should have asked for help instead of trying by themselves.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toys. One day, Tim found a pretty glass. The glass was shiny and colorful. Tim wanted to play with the glass, but he was afraid his friends would want to play with it too. Tim was feeling selfish. He didn't want to share the pretty glass with his friends. So, he hid the glass in his room and played with it when no one was looking. But soon, Tim started to feel lonely. He missed playing with his friends. One day, Tim's friend Sue came over. She saw the pretty glass in Tim's room and said, "Wow, that's a beautiful glass! Can we play with it together?" Tim thought for a moment and decided to share his pretty glass with Sue. They played together and had lots of fun. Tim learned that sharing and playing with friends was much better than being selfish.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sarah. She was three years old and was very curious. One day, she decided to go searching around the house. She looked in all the corners and under all of the cushions. She even looked behind the big couch. Finally, Sarah found something amazing. It was a vase! She had never seen anything like it before. It was a beautiful bright blue colour and had some pretty decorations on it. Sarah picked up the vase and felt something inside it. She wanted to know what was inside it. She held onto it and asked her mom. "Mommy, what's inside the vase?" Her mom smiled and replied, "Why don't you search and find out?" Sarah tried to look inside the vase, but it was too small and she felt uncomfortable. Finally, she shook the vase and heard something rattling inside. She opened the vase and found a shiny penny inside! Sarah was so excited and showed her mom. Her mom said, "Good job searching, Sarah!"

Once there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that he loved to play with. One day, the car stopped moving. Tim was sad. His mom told him that the car needed a new battery. Tim didn't know what a battery was, but he wanted to help his car. Tim's mom showed him a small battery. She said, "We use this to make the car go." Tim was happy that he could help his car. He put the battery in the car and it started moving again. Tim and his mom played with the car and had fun. One day, Tim saw a small bug that was lost. Tim wanted to help the bug. He was a compassionate boy. He picked up the bug and put it in a safe place. Tim's mom was proud of him for being kind to the bug. Tim felt good for helping the bug, just like he helped his car.

Sara and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to pretend they were a queen and a king. Sara wore a crown made of flowers and Ben had a stick for a sword. "Look, Ben, I found a big snail!" Sara said, holding the slimy creature in her hand. "He can be our pet. He can stay with us in our castle." Ben wrinkled his nose. "Ew, Sara, snails are smelly. And they are slow and boring. I don't want a snail for a pet. I want a dragon or a lion. They are strong and fast and brave." Sara frowned. "Snails are not smelly. They are cute and shiny. And they are smart and friendly. They can make trails and hide in their shells. I like snails. You are mean, Ben." Ben shook his head. "No, I'm not mean. I'm the king. And I say we don't need a snail. We need a better pet. Come on, Sara, let's go find one." He dropped his stick and ran away, leaving Sara alone with the snail. Sara felt sad and angry. She hugged the snail and said, "Don't worry, snail. You can stay with me. I'm the queen. And I say you are the best pet ever."
