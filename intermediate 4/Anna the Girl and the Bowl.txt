Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna the Girl and the Bowl

One day, a little girl named Anna was playing outside. It was a wet day, and she was jumping in puddles. She had so much fun, but she got very cold and wet. Anna went inside her house to get warm and dry. Anna's mom saw her shivering and said, "Oh no, you're all wet! Let me get you some warm clothes and make you some hot soup to help you feel better." Anna smiled and said, "Thank you, Mommy!" After putting on dry clothes, Anna sat at the table. Her mom gave her a big bowl of hot soup. The soup made her feel warm and comforted. Anna's mom hugged her and said, "I'm glad the soup made you feel better. Next time, wear a raincoat when it's wet outside." Anna nodded and promised to do so.

One day, a little shark was swimming in the sea. He was ready to find a new friend to play with. He swam and swam, looking for someone to have fun with. As he swam, he saw a little girl playing with a ball on the beach. The little shark thought, "Maybe she would like to play with me!" So, he swam closer to the shore and said, "Hi! I am a shark. Do you want to play with me?" The little girl was not scared. She said, "Hello, shark! Yes, I want to play with you!" The shark was very happy. The little girl held the ball and threw it into the water. The shark swam fast and caught the ball with his mouth. He swam back to the girl and gave the ball back to her. They played together all day long, laughing and having fun. The little shark and the girl became best friends, and they were always ready to play together.

Once upon a time there lived a nice boy. His name was Jack. Jack had a pet. He loved his pet very much. One day, Jack decided to organize his pet's toys. He wanted to keep them tidy and have them ready to play with. However, Jack's pet didn't like this. It didn't like the plan at all. Jack's pet meowed and tried to make him stop. Jack said firmly, "No. I need to organize your toys. That way they will be nice and neat." But his pet meowed again and wouldn't listen. Then, Jack's father walked in the room. "What's going on?" he asked. Jack explained how he wanted to organize his pet's toys and his pet was objecting. Jack's father said, "Jack, I understand how important it is to organize things. But your pet also needs to feel respected and valued. Otherwise, it will never learn to listen to you. So, try to find balance between organizing and letting your pet be happy." Jack nodded and thought deeply about his father's wise words. Then, he gave his pet a hug and offered it a treat. Jack and his pet worked together to organize the toys in a way that was both nice and respected. Once upon a time there was a little girl called Sarah. She was three years old and always very clumsy. One day Sarah was outside playing ball when it rolled away from her. She watched it roll all the way down the hill and disappear into the park. She knew it was time to go get it. Sarah walked down the hill and the view of the park made her smile. She loved playing in the park, but today there was something special. There, right in the centre, was a big red ball, just like her own! Sarah walked towards the ball and said hello. To her surprise, the ball started talking too. It said, "Hello, my name is Bob the ball. Would you like to play with me?" Sarah was excited and said "Yes!" and they started to play. They rolled around the park, finding lots of new places and then rolled all the way back up the hill again. Sarah was tired, but also very happy. Bob the ball gave Sarah a big hug goodbye and Sarah waved as he rolled away. She smiled and knew she would never forget the day she spent playing with Bob the ball.
