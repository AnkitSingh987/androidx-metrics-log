Category: Intermediate
Title: Fred and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a little boy whose name was Fred. On warm summer days, Fred would remember walking with his mom and dad in the lovely fields. The sight of the blooming flowers and lush green grass would fill Fred with joy. Every night before bedtime, Fred's mom would tell him the story of the magic pond at the edge of the woods. "Someday," she said, "You'll get to see the pond for yourself. It's the most lovely thing you've ever seen!" Fred eventually grew up, and one sunny day his parents took him to the edge of the woods. To his amazement, he found the pond of his mom's stories. The water was the bluest of blues and the trees around it were the greenest of greens. This was the beautiful sight his mom had promised him. Fred smiled and suddenly remembered his mom's words from so many years ago. She knew he would love this lovely sight, and as he stood there gazing at the pond, he felt nothing but love.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One day, he put on his favorite pants and went out to play. While playing, Tim saw a big rock. He wanted to climb it. As he climbed, he felt a little weak. He tried hard to hold on, but his hands were tired. Tim did not give up. He kept going. When Tim was almost at the top, he stumbled. He was scared, but he did not fall. He held on tight and made it to the top. Tim was happy and proud of himself. He had fun climbing the big rock, even if it was hard.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom had a dream. In his dream, he saw a big, gloomy tree. The tree was sad because its leaves were all mixed up with other trees' leaves. Tom wanted to help the tree. One sunny day, Tom went to the park. He saw the big, gloomy tree from his dream. He knew he had to help the tree. Tom thought of a plan. He would separate the leaves from the other trees' leaves. He started to pick up the leaves and put them in different piles. As Tom worked, the tree started to look happy. The tree was not gloomy anymore. Tom felt good because he helped the tree. The tree thanked Tom and gave him a big hug. From that day on, Tom and the tree were best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to watch her mom sew clothes. One day, Sue found a pin on the floor. She knew it was sharp and could hurt someone. Sue wanted to be an obedient girl, so she decided to give the pin to her mom. Sue walked to the kitchen where her mom was cooking. She held the pin tight in her hand so she would not lose it. But, on her way, she saw her little brother, Tim. Tim wanted the pin because it was shiny. Sue knew it was not safe for Tim to have the pin, but Tim started to cry. Sue did not want her brother to be sad, but she also wanted to be obedient. She thought hard and had an idea. She told Tim, "Let's go to mom and give her the pin together." Tim stopped crying and they both went to their mom. Sue gave the pin to her mom and her mom was very happy. Sue and Tim felt good because they were obedient and kept each other safe.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to perform. He would jump and run fast on a big stage. Spot made many friends who liked to watch him play. One day, a big bird named Flap came to the stage. Flap was very fast too, but he could not perform like Spot. Flap was sad and wanted to learn. So, Spot said, "I will help you, Flap!" They practiced together. Spot and Flap became very good friends. They were so happy to perform on the stage. But one day, something unexpected happened. Flap jumped very high and fast, and he started to fly! Spot was so surprised. He did not know Flap could fly. Spot and Flap were very happy. Now they could perform together, with Spot running fast on the stage and Flap flying high in the air. Their friends loved to watch them play even more. And they all lived happily ever after.
