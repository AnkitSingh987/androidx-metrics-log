Category: Intermediate
Title: The Red Bus Was and the Car

One day, a big red bus was in a race. The bus wanted to win. It was a very proud bus. It went fast down the road with a big smile. The bus raced with a car, a bike, and a truck. The bus went faster and faster. The car, bike, and truck tried to go fast too. But the bus was too fast for them. At the end of the race, the bus won. The bus was very happy and proud. The car, bike, and truck clapped for the bus. They all had fun in the race.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big bottle that was very important to him. He took it with him everywhere he went. One day, Tim saw a tree and wanted to climb it. He thought it would be fun to take his important bottle up the tree with him. As Tim started to climb the tree with his bottle, he met a little bird. The bird said, "Hi, Tim! What are you doing with that bottle?" Tim said, "I am climbing the tree with my important bottle. I take it everywhere." The bird thought it was a fun idea and decided to climb with Tim. When they got to the top of the tree, something unexpected happened. Tim's important bottle started to glow! Tim and the bird looked at the bottle and saw a tiny fairy inside. The fairy said, "Thank you for bringing me up here, Tim. I was stuck in the bottle, but now I am free!" Tim and the bird were very happy that they could help the fairy. From that day on, they all became best friends and had many more fun adventures together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Melissa who loved to smile. Her smile was so big and bright that it made everyone around her feel so happy. One day, Melissa's mom told her that there was a special event coming up that she was going to get to go to. Melissa was so excited that she started to smile even bigger. Melissa put on her brown dress that matched with her beautiful brown shoes. She couldn't wait to go to the event. Just then, Melissa's mom said, "Let's go! The event is starting soon. Are you ready?" Melissa smiled and said, "Yes! I'm definitely ready!" So, Melissa and her mom went to the event. As soon as they got there, Melissa noticed all the happy people smiling and she smiled too. She had the best time at the event. It was such a fun day and Melissa couldn't stop smiling.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had long, pretty hair. She liked to look at the sky at night. She saw many stars in the sky. One star was her favorite. It was the biggest and brightest star. Every night, Sue would pray to the big, bright star. She would ask the star to help her have good dreams. She wanted to dream about playing with her friends and eating yummy food. Sue felt happy when she talked to the star. One night, Sue had a dream about the big, bright star. The star came down from the sky and played with her. They played games and ate ice cream together. When Sue woke up, she knew her prayer to the star had worked. She was very happy and knew the star was her special friend.

Once upon a time, there was a farm. On the farm, there lived a cow, a pig, and a sheep. They were good friends. They liked to talk and play all day. One day, a scary noise came from the woods near the farm. The cow, the pig, and the sheep were scared. They did not know what the noise was. The cow said, "What is that scary noise?" The pig said, "I don't know, but I am scared too." The friends went to look for the scary noise. They walked and walked until they found a little bird. The bird was hurt. The cow, the pig, and the sheep helped the bird. The bird said, "Thank you for helping me." The friends were happy. They were not scared anymore. They all went back to the farm and played together.
