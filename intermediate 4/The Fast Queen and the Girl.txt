Category: Intermediate
Title: The Fast Queen and the Girl

Once upon a time there was a fast queen. She was so fast that everyone else in the land was jealous of her. Everywhere she went, everyone would stop and watch her run. As the queen ran, she sang loudly. One day, the queen went to a special event. All the people in the kingdom were there, including a young girl. The girl asked the fast queen what she liked to do most. The queen said, "I like to clean!" The young girl thought this was strange. She couldn't imagine why the fast queen would want to clean. The queen looked at the young girl and said, "You may not understand why I like to clean now, but one day you'll see why it's important. Now, watch me!" The fast queen then showed the young girl how to clean quickly and efficiently. The young girl was amazed at how fast the queen could clean. Now, when the queen needs help, the young girl is always there to help her clean.

One day, a clumsy dog named Tim went for a walk. It was a hot day and the sun was shining bright. Tim saw a big ice cream cone on the ground. It was melting because of the heat. Tim wanted to eat it, but he knew he had to be quick. Tim tried to pick up the ice cream cone, but he was too clumsy. His big paws kept making it slip and fall. He started to feel sad. Just then, a little girl named Sue saw Tim and wanted to help him. "Hi, Tim! Let me help you," said Sue. She picked up the cone and gave it to Tim. He was so happy! They walked together, sharing the ice cream cone as it melted. They came to a pass in the road and decided to go left. Tim and Sue had a great day playing and eating ice cream, and they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to spin around in circles. One day, Kitty saw a shower. The shower was big and had lots of water. Kitty was curious and walked closer to the shower. She saw a toy inside the shower. Kitty wanted to play with the toy, so she jumped into the shower. The water was cold and wet, but Kitty didn't mind. Kitty started to spin around with the toy in the shower. She had so much fun spinning and playing with the toy. After a while, Kitty got tired and stopped spinning. She jumped out of the shower and went to take a nap. Kitty had a great day playing in the shower.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Lily. She had a pet bee named Buzz. Buzz could make honey that would glow. Lily loved Buzz and his glowing honey. One day, Lily and Buzz went to the market. They saw a man selling cheap toys. Lily asked the man, "Can I have a toy?" The man said, "Yes, but you have to give me something in return." Lily thought about what she had, and she remembered the glowing honey. Lily told Buzz to make some glowing honey for the man. Buzz made the honey, and Lily gave it to the man. The man was so happy with the glowing honey that he gave Lily a toy. Lily and Buzz went back home, happy with their new toy, and the man was happy with his glowing honey.

Lily was a little girl who liked to play outside. She liked to run, jump, and pick flowers. But one day, she felt a tickle in her nose. She tried to rub it, but it did not go away. She felt a big sneeze coming. She opened her mouth and said, "Achoo!" The sneeze was so loud that it scared a bird in a tree. The bird flew away, making a noise. Lily looked up and saw the bird. She thought it was pretty, but she was also frightened. She did not like loud noises. She ran back to her house, where her mom was waiting. She hugged her mom and said, "Mom, I sneezed and scared a bird!" Her mom smiled and said, "It's okay, Lily. Sometimes we sneeze when we have dust or pollen in our nose. It's not your fault. The bird is not angry with you. It just flew away to find another place to stay." Lily felt better. She said, "Can I go outside and play again?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can. But first, let me give you a tissue and some water. And remember to cover your mouth and nose when you sneeze, okay?" Lily nodded and said, "Okay, mom. Thank you." She took the tissue and the water and went outside again. She hoped to see the bird again and say sorry. She also hoped to not sneeze again.
