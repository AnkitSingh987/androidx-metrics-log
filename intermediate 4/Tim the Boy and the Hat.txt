Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Hat

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a light bird in the sky. The bird was lost and needed help. Tim wanted to provide help to the bird. Tim and the bird went on a walk to find the bird's home. On their way, they met a stranger. The stranger was a nice man with a big hat. The stranger said he knew where the bird's home was. They followed the stranger to a big tree. Tim thought this was the bird's home, but it was not. The light bird was a magic bird! The bird said "Thank you" to Tim and the stranger and flew up into the sky. The bird turned into a bright star, and Tim knew he had a new friend in the sky.

There was an ancient tree in the forest. It was very, very old. Birds and squirrels lived in the tree. One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the forest. She wanted to see the tree. Lucy walked and walked until she found it. When Lucy saw the tree, she had a question. She asked, "How did you get so old?" The tree did not say anything. Lucy thought maybe the tree did not hear her. She asked her question again, louder this time. Just then, Lucy felt something on her toe. It was a tiny ant. The ant looked at Lucy and said, "I can tell you how the tree got so old." Lucy was happy. She listened to the ant's story. The ant told her that the tree was loved by all the animals and people. That is why it grew so big and old. Lucy smiled and thanked the ant. She went home to tell her friends about the ancient tree and the ant.

Once upon a time, there was a big race. All the kids wanted to win a shiny medal. They ran very fast, but one little girl named Mia had a hidden trick. She knew a secret path that was shorter. Mia took the secret path and was almost at the finish line. But then, she saw a little puppy who was lost. Mia knew she had to help the puppy, even if it would delay her. She picked up the puppy and carried it with her. At the finish line, Mia came in last, but she was happy. She had helped the puppy find its way home. All the kids cheered for Mia. They knew she was the real winner. Mia got a special medal for being kind and helpful.

Once upon a time, two friends, Tom and Lucy, were walking across a field. Tom said to Lucy, "Let's bury something silly!" Lucy and Tom each got a special little thing they wanted to bury. Tom grabbed a stick and said, "I'll bury this!" Lucy found a rock and said, "I'm burying this!" Together, they dug a big hole in the ground and put the items inside. Then they filled the hole with dirt and made sure it was all covered up. Suddenly, Lucy fell down and laughed. Tom laughed too and said, "You are so silly Lucy!" Then they went back home with a smile on their faces. They knew that their special things were buried safely in the ground.

Jimmy was at the baseball field with his dad. He watched the older kids playing a game and wished he could join them. Dad said, "Soon enough you'll be playing, son. Now watch and learn." Suddenly, a filthy old baseball rolled over to Jimmy and he picked it up. He wanted to show his dad he could shoot it like the older kids. But when Jimmy threw the ball, it hit a tree. Dad scolded him, "Jimmy, I said to watch, not touch!" Jimmy was disappointed his dad was mad. He sadly opened his hand to return the ball, but it was gone. He looked around, but the ball was nowhere to be seen. "Oh, Jimmy!" Dad said. "How will we ever find it?" Dad and Jimmy searched around the field until finally they found the filthy old baseball hiding in the grass. Jimmy was so excited, he grabbed the ball and gave it a big hug. Dad smiled, giving his son a pat on the back. They were both happy the ball was found!
