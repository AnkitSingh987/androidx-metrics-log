Category: Intermediate
Title: Kitty the Cat and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a cat named Kitty. Kitty liked to play and pretend. One day, Kitty saw a big dog. The dog looked nice, but it was a little stupid. Kitty said, "I will pretend to be a dog and play with the big dog." Kitty walked up to the dog and said, "Woof! I am a dog, let's play!" The big dog looked at Kitty and said, "You are not a dog, you are a cat. But okay, let's play." They played and had fun. But then, the big dog got too excited and played too rough. Kitty got hurt and cried. The big dog felt bad and said, "I am sorry, Kitty. I didn't mean to hurt you." The moral of the story is to be careful when you pretend to be something you are not.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was hungry yesterday. She went to her mom and said, "Mom, I am hungry." Her mom gave her a big apple to eat. Sue was very happy. Later, Sue went outside to play. She saw a little bird on the ground. The bird was also hungry. Sue stared at the bird and thought, "I can help the bird too." She went back inside and asked her mom for some bread. Sue's mom gave her a small piece of bread. Sue went back outside and gave the bread to the little bird. As the bird ate the bread, something unexpected happened. The bird grew big and turned into a magical talking bird! The bird said, "Thank you, Sue, for being so kind. I am a magic bird and I will be your friend forever." Sue was very surprised but happy to have a new friend. They played together every day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time in a small village, there lived a charming little girl named Grace. Grace was only three years old and loved to explore her village. One day, Grace was playing in the garden when she discovered a pile of white powder. She bent down to take a closer look. Just then, her mum walked into the garden. "Grace, what have you found?" her mum asked. "It's powder!" said Grace. Her mum smiled. "That's flour," she said. "It's used for baking cakes." Grace's mum explained that the powder was very special and she asked Grace to take care of it. Grace said she would and she and her mum returned the powder to the kitchen cupboard. Grace noticed that her mum seemed sad. She wanted to make her feel better, so she gave her a big hug and said, "It's ok mum. I will comfort you." Grace's mum smiled. She was touched that her little girl was so understanding. She hugged Grace back and thanked her for being so charming. Grace and her mum spent the rest of the day in the garden playing and laughing together. They had a wonderful time!

One day, a creative little girl named Sue went for a walk. She saw a pretty flower by the road. Sue wanted to take the flower home. She picked the flower and held it in her hand. On her way home, Sue met a small bug. The bug said, "Please, give me back my flower. It is my home." Sue was very surprised. She did not know that bugs can talk. Sue was kind and wanted to help the bug. She put the flower back where she found it. The bug was happy and thanked Sue. Sue went home and told her mom about the talking bug. Her mom was surprised too. They both learned something new that day.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable dog named Max. Max was a fast dog who loved to run. He had a lot of speed when he played with his friends. Max was always happy to help others. One day, Max saw a little bird who was lost. The little bird asked Max for help. Max wanted to play with his friends, so he ignored the bird at first. But then, Max thought about how he was a dependable dog and decided to help the little bird. Max and the bird went on a long walk to find the bird's family. They met many new friends along the way. Finally, they found the bird's family, and everyone was so happy. Max learned that helping others is more important than playing. The moral of the story is to always be dependable and help others, even when it's not easy.
