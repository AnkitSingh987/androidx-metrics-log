Category: Intermediate
Title: Simmo and the Rainbow

Once upon a time a bird called Simmo felt hurt. He was feeling sad and alone so he flew up high in search of some help. After what felt like forever he saw a rainbow, and that made him feel better. Simmo fluttered down towards the rainbow and came to a rest in a meadow full of lovely flowers. As he settled, he heard a voice calling to him! The voice belonged to a friendly mouse called Mario. Mario asked Simmo a question. He wanted to know why Simmo was so hurt. Simmo explained that he had been feeling lonely and was sad that no one was paying him any attention. Mario comforted Simmo and told him he was not alone. The two friends laughed and talked for a long time in the meadow. By the time they were finished talking, Simmo felt much better. His hurt had settled and Mario stayed by his side in times of need. Simmo had made a loyal friend and the two of them were so very happy. They both settled into their new friendship and shared many more adventures from that day on.

Once upon a time, there was a gentle rabbit named Bob. Bob lived in a big forest with his friends. One day, Bob found a big jar of jam. He wanted to spread it on his bread, but he didn't know how. Bob asked his friend, Cat, "How do I spread jam on my bread?" Cat said, "I don't know, let's ask Dog." So, they went to Dog's house. Dog was not home, so they could not ask him. Bob was sad because he really wanted to eat his jam. Finally, they found Dog at the park. Dog said, "I can help you spread the jam, Bob!" Dog took a big knife and spread the jam on Bob's bread. Bob was so happy! He shared his jam bread with Cat and Dog, and they all enjoyed a yummy snack together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had an amazing aunt named Lucy. Aunt Lucy loved to spoil Mia with fun toys and yummy treats. One day, Mia and Aunt Lucy went to the park. They played on the swings and the slide. Mia was very happy. Aunt Lucy said, "Mia, you are so good at playing! Let's go get ice cream to spoil you more." Mia smiled and said, "Yes, please, Aunt Lucy! I love ice cream!" They went to the ice cream shop and Mia picked her favorite flavor. Mia and Aunt Lucy sat on a bench and ate their ice cream. They laughed and talked, and Mia knew she was very lucky to have such an amazing aunt.

Once upon a time, there was a hairy dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball and watch videos with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. They had lots of fun together, laughing and playing all day long. One day, while they were watching a video, Spot's ball suddenly vanished! Spot and Tim looked everywhere for the ball, but they could not find it. They were very sad because they loved to play with the ball. Then, Tim had an idea. He and Spot went outside and looked under a big tree. There, they found the ball! Spot and Tim were so happy that they found the ball. They played with it all day and watched more videos together. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a player named Tim. Tim loved to play with his ball. One day, Tim saw a big tree and wanted to kick his ball over it. "Watch me!" Tim said to his friend, Sam. But Tim did not know the word "regret". He was ignorant. He kicked the ball very hard. It went up, up, up, and got stuck in the tree. "Oh no!" said Tim. "I am sad. I miss my ball." Sam looked at Tim and said, "Next time, we will be more careful. We do not want to feel regret again." From that day on, Tim and Sam learned a new word: "regret". They played more carefully and never lost their ball in the tree again.
