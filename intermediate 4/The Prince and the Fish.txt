Category: Intermediate
Title: The Prince and the Fish

Once upon a time there was a prince who was very proud. Everywhere he went people bowed and praised him. One day, the prince was walking in the woods when he came across a bush with the most wonderful fruits. He picked the fruits but then, he noticed something strange. He felt ashamed. The prince said to himself, "Why am I so ashamed? I'm a prince, I can do whatever I want!" He started to cry, and his tears turned into a golden pond. Suddenly, a fish jumped out of the water and spoke to the prince. Fish said, "Prince, why are you so ashamed? You have done nothing wrong." The prince replied, "I picked the fruit without permission and now I'm embarrassed!" The fish smiled and said, "Right now you might feel embarrassed, but why not become the guardian of this forest? You can protect these wonderful fruits and all creatures living here with honor and pride." The prince was inspired. He put on a golden crown and became the Guardian of the Forest. From then on, he always felt proud, never ashamed.

One day, a gifted dog named Spot found a tire. He wanted to play with it. He tried to pick it up, but it was too big. Spot felt sad. Then, Spot had an idea. He asked his friend, a bird named Ring, to help him. Ring said, "I can help you, Spot. Let's work together!" They both tried to lift the tire. It was still hard, but they did not give up. At last, they lifted the tire. They were happy and played with it all day. Spot and Ring learned that when they work together, they can do anything.

One day, a cute bird named Bob lived in a bush. The bush was green and had many leaves. Bob liked to play with his friends in the sky. He loved to dive down and up in the air. One morning, Bob told his friend, "I will dive very fast today!" His friend said, "Be careful, Bob! You should look where you dive." But Bob was too excited to listen. Later, Bob dived very fast. He did not look where he was going. He dived right into his bush! All the leaves fell down. Bob was sad, but he learned to be more careful when he plays.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy who loved to have fun. One day, he started playing with his toy trucks in his room. He pushed the trucks around and made cool noises. They were so fun! Then, he heard his mom calling him. She said there was a special treat waiting in the kitchen. He quickly ran to the kitchen and saw it was a tasty ice cream sundae! He was so excited and started eating it right away. The ice cream was delicious. After a while, he licked the last drops of ice cream from the bowl. He felt very happy and his tummy was full. What a tasty treat! The little boy thanked his mom for the special treat. Then, he ran back to his room to continue playing and having fun with his toy trucks.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful dog named Max. Max loved to play at the park with his friends. They liked to run, jump, and play fetch with their ball. Max was very good at finding the ball and bringing it back to his friends. One day, Max and his friends went to the park to play. They were having so much fun when something unexpected happened. Max found a big, red ball near a big tree. Max thought it was a new toy to play with. He tried to pick it up with his mouth, but it was too big! Max's friends saw him trying to get the big, red ball. They knew it was not a toy, but a part of an operation to fix the park. Max's friends told him to leave the ball alone, but Max was very helpful and wanted to help with the operation. Max pushed the big, red ball with his nose, and it rolled down a hill. The ball hit a switch, and all of a sudden, the park's broken swings and slide were fixed! Max and his friends were so happy that they could play on the swings and slide again. Max was a helpful hero, and they all played at the park until the sun went down.
