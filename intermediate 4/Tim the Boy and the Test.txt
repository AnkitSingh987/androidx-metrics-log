Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Test

One day, a boy named Tim had a boring test at school. He did not want to take the test. Tim thought it was not fun. So, he played with his toys at home instead. The next day, Tim went back to school. His friends said, "We took the test yesterday, but you were not there." Tim felt sad because he missed the test. He did not know what to do. Tim talked to his teacher. The teacher said, "Do not worry, you can take the test today." Tim took the test and it was not so bad. He was happy to finish it. Tim learned to not miss tests and to try even if it is boring.

Once upon a time, there was a young engine named Speedy. Speedy loved to go fast. He lived in a big town with many other engines. One day, Speedy saw a big hill. He wanted to speed up the hill and see how fast he could go. So, he started to go up the hill. He went faster and faster, and soon he was at the top. Speedy was so happy that he could go so fast. He went down the hill and back to the town. All the other engines were happy for him too. They all played together, and Speedy was the fastest of them all.

Billy woke up in the middle of the night and heard a noise. He was scared, so he peeked out of his bedroom door to see what it was. In the hallway he saw a big monster! It was open with lots of eyes and more arms than he had ever seen before. Billy was so scared he didn't know what to do. He wanted to run away, but he was too scared to move. Suddenly, he heard his mama and papa's voices coming from the other room. They were coming to help him! Billy was so brave that he ran to the monster and opened the door. His parents were so happy to see him. They hugged him tight and took him away from the scary monster.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to eat beans. She had a big box where she kept all her beans. The box was very organized. Sue made sure that all the beans were in the right place. One day, Sue went to the store with her mom. She saw a new kind of bean that she had never seen before. Sue was so excited! She asked her mom to buy the beans for her. Her mom said yes, and they took the beans home. When they got home, Sue put the new beans in her box. She shut the box and went to play outside. Later, Sue came back inside to eat her beans. She opened the box and saw that her new beans were now friends with her old beans. Sue was so happy that all the beans were getting along. She ate her beans with a big smile on her face.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a pretty flower and wanted to pick it. But her mom said, "No, Lily. We must not pick flowers from the park." Lily felt sad and asked her mom, "Why can't I pick the flower?" Her mom replied, "If everyone picks the flowers, there will be no more flowers for others to see and enjoy." Lily thought about it and said, "I don't want to be silly and selfish. I will not pick the flower." She refused to pick the flower and let it stay in the park. Soon, a little boy came to the park and saw the pretty flower. He smiled and said, "Look, mom! A beautiful flower!" Lily felt happy that she didn't pick the flower. The moral of the story is to think about others and not be selfish.

Once upon a time, there was a lively little cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to run and play all day with her friends. One day, Kitty got an invite to a big party at the airport. Kitty was very happy to go to the party. She put on her best dress and went to the airport. When she got there, she saw lots of her friends. They were all dancing and playing games. Kitty danced and played with her friends all day long. They had so much fun at the airport party. When it was time to go home, Kitty was tired but very happy. She couldn't wait for the next fun party with her friends.
