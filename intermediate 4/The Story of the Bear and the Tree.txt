Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Bear and the Tree

One day, a mighty bear was walking along. He was looking for a place to have dinner. Suddenly, he saw an apple tree. It was far away, but he moved quickly. The bear reached the tree and started to eat the apples. He was so happy! Then, he moved on and found a sweet little stream. He could see some tasty fish swimming inside. He moved closer and started to eat the fish. When he was done, he was full and happy. It had been a mighty dinner!

Tom and Lily were going to church with their mom and dad. They liked church because they could sing songs, listen to stories, and play with their friends. They also liked the big bell that rang when church started and ended. On the way to church, Tom saw a red car that he liked. He pointed at it and said, "Look, mom, a red car! Can I have a red car?" Mom smiled and said, "Maybe when you are older, Tom. Red cars are very fast and fun, but they are also very expensive and need a lot of care." Lily saw a yellow flower that she liked. She pointed at it and said, "Look, dad, a yellow flower! Can I have a yellow flower?" Dad smiled and said, "Maybe later, Lily. Yellow flowers are very pretty and smell nice, but they also need water and sun to grow." Tom and Lily wanted the red car and the yellow flower, but they knew they had to go to church. They trusted their mom and dad, because they were dependable and always kept their promises. They held their hands and walked to church. When they got to church, they heard the big bell ring. They ran to the door and saw their friends waiting for them. They said hello and went inside. They sang songs, listened to stories, and played with their friends. They had a lot of fun. When church ended, they heard the big bell ring again. They said goodbye to their friends and went outside. They saw their mom and dad waiting for them. They hugged them and said, "We love you, mom and dad. You are dependable and always keep your promises." Mom and dad smiled and said, "We love you, too, Tom and Lily. You are good and obedient children. And guess what? We have a surprise for you." They took them to the car and opened the trunk. Inside, there was a toy red car for Tom and a plastic yellow flower for Lily. Tom and Lily were very happy and thanked their mom and dad. They played with their new toys all the way home.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a big dog named Max. Sue and Max liked to play together. One day, they went to the park. At the park, they saw a man with a big box. The man said, "I have a rich rub for dogs. It makes dogs feel good." Sue wanted Max to try it. She asked Max to nod if he wanted to try the rub. Max nodded his head up and down. The man put the rich rub on Max. Max was very happy. Sue and Max played in the park all day. They went home and told their friends about the rich rub. Sue and Max had a good day.

The three year old girl had an important day ahead of her and she needed to plan carefully. She found her favourite dress and put it on. She looked at herself in the mirror and felt excited, but also a little confused. She had a lot to do and not much time to do it in. The girl planned her day carefully. She knew she had to brush her teeth, feed the cat and put her shoes on. And finally, she had to pick a beautiful flower for Auntie. It was all a bit confusing, but she was sure she could do it. When she was all ready, the little girl smiled at herself in the mirror. She was wearing her favourite dress and was feeling excited again. She grabbed her satchel and headed out the door. She was ready to face the day.
