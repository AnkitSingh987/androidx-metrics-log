Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Yawn

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim was very impatient. He always wanted things to happen fast. One day, Tim's mom told him a story about a big yawn. "Mom, what is a yawn?" Tim asked. "A yawn is when you open your mouth wide because you are tired or bored," Mom said. One day, Tim was playing with his friend, Sam. They were waiting for the ice cream truck. Tim was very impatient and wanted the truck to come fast. He started to yawn because he was bored. "Tim, you should learn to be patient," Sam said. "Good things come to those who wait." Tim thought about what Sam said and knew it was the truth. He decided to be more patient from that day on. The ice cream truck finally came, and they enjoyed their ice cream together. Tim learned that being patient was important and that good things happen when you wait.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toys in the deep sand near his house. One day, he found a shiny saw. He picked it up and went to show his mom. "Look, Mom! I found a saw!" Tim said. His mom smiled and said, "That's great, Tim! But remember, don't use it without me. It can be dangerous." Tim nodded and said, "I will remember, Mom." He put the saw in his room. The next day, Tim was playing with his friend, Sam. Sam saw the shiny saw and wanted to use it. Tim remembered what his mom said and told Sam, "We can't use it without my mom. It's dangerous." Sam listened to Tim and they played with other toys instead. Later, Tim's mom saw them playing safely and was very proud of Tim. She knew he had listened and learned to follow rules. The moral of the story is to always listen to your parents, as they know what's best for you.

Once upon a time, there was a big, grumpy cat. The cat liked to lay in the sun all day. He did not like to play with the other cats. He just liked to be alone. One day, the cat heard a loud noise. It was a police car. The police car had bright lights and made a lot of noise. The cat was scared, so he ran away and hid under a tree. After a while, the police car went away. The grumpy cat came out from under the tree. He went back to his sunny spot and lay down again. The cat was happy to be alone in the sun once more.

Once there was a little girl called Jo. Jo was only 3 years old, but very smart! She loved to play with her friends at school. One day, Jo saw some of her friends having a meeting in an open field. She wondered what a meeting was, so she walked over to join them. The other kids were very friendly and welcomed her. They told her that a meeting was an opportunity to talk, share ideas, and help each other out. Jo listened thoughtfully and felt very peaceful. At the end of the meeting, Jo had learned an important lesson: teamwork makes great things happen! She was happy that she had joined the meeting and enjoyed spending time with her friends. Jo had learned that working together in a peaceful way can help everyone achieve their goals. She knew that she would remember the important lesson from the meeting for a long time.

Once upon a time, a little blue truck lived in a small town. The blue truck loved to play with his friends. They would race and have fun all day long. One day, the blue truck's friends went to a big race in another town. The blue truck was sad because he had to stay home. He missed his friends very much. While the blue truck was alone, he saw a big hill. He thought, "I can race down this hill and have fun too!" So the blue truck went up the hill and started to race down. But as he raced, he went too fast! The blue truck couldn't stop and was scared. Just then, a big, strong tree saw the blue truck and wanted to help. The tree put out its long branch and caught the blue truck just in time. The blue truck was safe and happy. He thanked the tree for saving him. The tree smiled and said, "You're welcome, little blue truck. I missed my friends too, and now I have a new friend in you!" From that day on, the blue truck and the tree were best friends, and they never felt alone again.
