Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Crayon

Lily liked to draw with her crayons. She had many colors and shapes to make on her paper. But she did not like to share her crayons with her brother Ben. Ben was younger and he did not know how to draw well. He broke the crayons and made them dull. One day, Lily found a sharp crayon in her box. It was red and shiny and made a thin line on the paper. Lily was happy and wanted to draw a big heart with it. She did not want Ben to see it or touch it. But Ben saw the sharp crayon and wanted to try it. He reached for it and said, "Me want red!" Lily pulled the crayon away and said, "No! Mine! Go away!" Ben cried and tried to grab the crayon. Lily pushed him and he fell on the floor. He bumped his head and it hurt. He cried louder and louder. Mom heard the noise and came to see what was wrong. She saw Ben on the floor and Lily with the sharp crayon. She was angry and said, "Lily, what did you do? Why did you hurt your brother?" Lily felt bad and said, "Sorry, Mom. I did not mean to hurt him. I just wanted to keep my sharp crayon. It is rare and I like it." Mom said, "Lily, I know you like your crayon, but you have to share with your brother. He is learning how to draw and he needs your help. You can teach him how to use the crayon gently and make nice pictures. Sharing is caring, Lily. Do you understand?" Lily nodded and said, "Yes, Mom. I understand. I will share with Ben and teach him how to draw. I am sorry, Ben. Do you want to draw with me?" Ben stopped crying and said, "Yes, Lily. Thank you. Me want to draw with you." Mom smiled and said, "That's good, Lily and Ben. I am proud of you. Now, let's go and get a band-aid for Ben's head and some cookies for both of you."

Once upon a time, there was a chubby raccoon who lived in the woods. He was always very curious and loved exploring around the forest. One morning he was walking when he accidentally stumbled upon a strange place. Everything was so quiet and he couldn't hear any birds chirping or animals calling. The raccoon was stunned and all he could do was stare. He noticed a large squirrel nearby and he said "hi". The squirrel looked at him and said, "What do you want here?". The raccoon said, "I just wanted to know what this place is. It's so quiet and I've never been here before". The squirrel explained, "This is the silence forest. No animals ever make any noise. Everyone must always be quiet". The raccoon thought for a moment before nodding in understanding. He thanked the squirrel and waved goodbye before heading back home, but every time he walked through the forest, he thought of the silence and felt so peaceful.

Once upon a time, there was a little lizard. He lived in a big forest. The lizard had a powerful friend, the sun. They liked to play together every day. One day, the sun was sad. Big clouds were in the sky. The sun could not spread its light on the forest. The lizard wanted to help his friend. So, the lizard went to find the big clouds. The lizard climbed a tall tree. He talked to the big clouds. "Please go away," he said. "My friend, the sun, wants to play." The big clouds listened to the little lizard. They moved away and let the sun shine. The sun was happy again. It spread its light all over the forest. The lizard and the sun played together all day. They were the best friends in the big forest. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a big horse lived in a stable. The stable was wide and warm. The horse had a friend, a small bird. They liked to play and talk together. The bird asked the horse, "Why is your home so wide?" The horse tried to explain, "It is wide so I can move and sleep well." The bird was happy with the answer. One day, when the horse came back to the stable, he saw a big elephant in his home! The elephant said, "I needed a place to sleep, and your stable is so wide and nice." The horse was surprised but let the elephant stay. They became good friends and played together with the bird.
