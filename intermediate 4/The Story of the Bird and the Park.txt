Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Bird and the Park

One day, a funny bird found a journal in the park. The bird liked to rock on the swings, so it took the journal to the swings to read. The bird read about a big party that was going to happen in the park. The bird was very happy and wanted to go to the party. The funny bird told all its friends about the party. They all went to the park to have fun. They played games, ate yummy food, and rocked on the swings together. The bird noticed that the journal said to be nice to others and share at the party. At the party, the funny bird shared its swing with a small bug who also wanted to rock. The bug was so happy that the bird shared the swing. All the other animals saw this and started to share their games and food too. The party was a big success and everyone had a great time. The moral of the story is to always be nice and share with others. When you share, everyone can have fun and be happy together.

Once upon a time, there was a very useful lab. In the lab, there were many things to look at. There was a big dog named Max. Max liked to help in the lab. One day, Max saw a small box on the floor. He did not know what was in the box, but he wanted to look inside. Max took the box to his friend, Sam. Sam was a little boy who liked to play with Max. Max showed Sam the box. Sam wanted to look inside too. They both thought there would be a toy inside the box for them to play with. But when they opened the box, they found a small key. They did not know what the key was for. They looked around the lab, and they saw a little door. They put the key in the door and turned it. The door opened, and they found a room full of big, colorful balloons. Max and Sam were very happy. They played with the balloons all day long. The little key in the box was a big surprise for them.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to find a book on her bookshelf. She saw a big, dead bug on the floor. Amy was scared. She went to her mom and said, "Mom, there is a dead bug near the bookshelf." Mom thanked Amy for telling her. She went to the bookshelf to clean up the dead bug. But when she got there, the bug was gone. Mom was confused. She looked around and saw the bug on the wall. It was not dead after all! It was a trick! Amy and her mom laughed. They were happy the bug was not dead. The bug flew out the window and they waved goodbye. Then, Amy picked a book from the bookshelf to read with her mom. They had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby boy called Jack. He had a wagon and loved to use it as his mode of transport. One day, Jack rode his wagon to the supermarket. He was so happy when he saw all the yummy food! He wanted to cook something special for dinner. So, Jack loaded up his wagon with all sorts of delicious ingredients-- carrots, butter and flour. Then, he rode his wagon all the way back home. Jack spent all afternoon cooking in the kitchen. He chopped, mixed and stirred the ingredients together until it smelled wonderful. He served the finished meal to his family. Everyone had a big smile, and said it was the best dinner ever! Jack loved to cook and was already planning what to make next. With a big grin, he jumped onto his wagon and rode off.

Lily and Tom were playing with a big box of toys. They liked to find different things and see if they could fit them together. Lily found a yellow egg that was soft and squeaky. She tried to fit it into a hole in a wooden puzzle, but it was too big. "Look, Tom, this egg does not fit here!" she said. Tom came over and looked at the egg. He thought it was funny and he squeezed it. It made a loud noise and he laughed. "That is not an egg, Lily, that is a duck!" he said. Lily was confused. She looked at the egg and then at Tom. She did not see a duck. She only saw a yellow egg with a beak and eyes. "No, Tom, this is an egg. See, it has a shell and a yolk inside," she said. She showed him the egg and pointed at the parts. Tom shook his head. He thought Lily was ignorant. He did not know what a shell and a yolk were. He only knew what a duck was. "No, Lily, this is a duck. See, it has a beak and eyes and it quacks," he said. He showed her the egg and made it quack again. Lily frowned. She thought Tom was ignorant. He did not know what an egg was. She only knew what an egg was. They argued for a while, each trying to prove their point. They did not listen to each other. They did not learn from each other. They did not have fun with each other. They were both ignorant and stubborn. Then, their mom came in and saw them fighting. She was not happy. She took the egg from them and explained what it was. "This is not a real egg, Lily, and this is not a real duck, Tom. This is a toy that looks like both. It is an egg-duck. You can squeeze it and it quacks, or you can open it and see the yolk. It is meant to be fun and silly, not to cause trouble. You should share it and play nicely, not fight over it. Do you understand?" she said. Lily and Tom looked at the egg-duck and then at each other. They felt ashamed. They realized they were both wrong and they were both right. They apologized to each other and to their mom. They decided to share the egg-duck and try to fit it into different places. They learned something new and they had fun together. They were not ignorant and stubborn anymore. They were smart and friendly.
