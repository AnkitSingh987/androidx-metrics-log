Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bear and the Cave

Once upon a time, there was a kind bear who lived in a cave. He looked out over a great valley where all the creatures of the woods lived. One day, the bear was feeling worried. In the sky, dark clouds were gathering and the weather did not look good. He wanted to protect all the creatures so he called out to them with a loud voice. "Friends, come to my cave. I will keep you safe from the bad weather!" The animals quickly came to the bear's cave. They huddled together and the bear made sure they were all snug and warm. Then, from outside the cave, they heard the sound of thunder. Lightning lit up the sky, but the bear's kind heart made sure that every creature was safe from the storm. The bear smiled as he watched over his friends until the storm passed, and then he said, "Thank you all for trusting me. Now let's go back outside and enjoy the sunshine."

Once upon a time, there was a taxi. The taxi was yellow and shiny. The taxi liked to drive around the town all day. It would stop and pick up people who needed a ride. The taxi was very happy to help. One day, a deaf man needed a ride. He could not hear, but he could see the taxi coming. The taxi stopped and the deaf man got in. The taxi knew the deaf man could not hear, so it drove very carefully. The deaf man smiled and gave a thumbs up to the taxi. As they drove, they saw a big puddle. The taxi did not want the deaf man to get wet. So, the taxi took a big step over the puddle. The deaf man was safe and dry. They reached their destination and the deaf man got out. He waved goodbye to the taxi. The taxi was very proud and happy to help the deaf man.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was very anxious to go to the store. She wanted to buy a new toy with her saved up money. So, she asked her mom, "Can we go to the store, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, but first you have to put on your shoes and coat." Sue knew that her mom was going to say that. She had her shoes and coat ready by the door. Sue put them on and was ready to go. Sue and her mom started to walk to the store. Sue was so happy that she took big steps. She counted each step she took. When they got to the store, Sue found the toy she wanted. She was so excited that she had enough money to buy it. Sue and her mom walked back home, and Sue played with her new toy all day long.

One day, a little sheep named Billy was playing with his wool. He loved how soft and warm it was. He saw a big ball of wool rolling down the hill. He wanted to play with it, so he ran to catch it. As Billy ran, he saw a small girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she had lost her cheap toy. Billy wanted to help, so he pushed the big ball of wool to Sue. "Here, play with this," said Billy. Sue looked at the wool and smiled. "Thank you, Billy!" she said. But then, another sheep named Timmy came running. Timmy was angry because the big ball of wool was his. He wanted it back. "That's my wool!" he said. Billy and Sue didn't want Timmy to be sad, so they pushed the ball of wool back to him. "We're sorry, Timmy," they said. Timmy smiled and let them play with the wool too. They all played together and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a lively little bunny named Ben. Ben loved to hop and play with his friends in the sunny meadow. One day, Ben decided to have a tea party with all his friends. Ben's friends were excited for the tea party. They all helped to cut up yummy treats like apples, carrots, and bread. They put the treats on a big plate for everyone to share. Ben poured tea into little cups for each of his friends. At the tea party, Ben and his friends laughed and talked. They ate the yummy treats and drank the tasty tea. Everyone had a fun time at Ben's lively tea party. And from that day on, they had tea parties often, enjoying each other's company in the sunny meadow.
