Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and Tom and the Mom

Sara and Tom were playing in the kitchen. They liked to pretend they were chefs and make yummy food. They found a big yellow lemon on the counter. Sara wanted to squeeze it and make lemonade. Tom wanted to cut it and make lemon pie. "Let me have the lemon!" Sara said. "No, it's mine!" Tom said. They both pulled on the lemon, but it was too hard. They heard a loud crack. The lemon broke in half and squirted juice everywhere. Sara and Tom got juice in their eyes and mouths. It was sour and stingy. They dropped the lemon and cried. Mom heard the noise and came to the kitchen. She saw the mess and the sad children. She was not happy. "What did you do?" Mom asked. "We broke the lemon," Sara and Tom said. "Why did you break the lemon?" Mom asked. "We wanted to make food," Sara and Tom said. Mom sighed. She wiped their eyes and mouths with a soft cloth. She hugged them and said, "I know you like to play, but you have to be careful. Lemons are not toys. They are for eating. Next time, ask me before you use something from the kitchen. OK?" "OK," Sara and Tom said. Mom smiled. She said, "How about we make some lemonade together? I have another lemon and some sugar and water. It will be sweet and refreshing. Do you want to help me?" "Yes, please!" Sara and Tom said. They followed Mom to the sink. Mom washed the lemon and cut it with a knife. She gave Sara and Tom each a half and a squeezer. They squeezed the lemon juice into a pitcher. Mom added some sugar and water and stirred it. She poured some lemonade into cups and gave them to Sara and Tom. They tasted the lemonade. It was delicious. They smiled and said, "Thank you, Mom!" Mom said, "You're welcome, my loves. You know, you are very dependable. You always help me when I ask you. I'm proud of you." Sara and Tom felt happy. They hugged Mom and said, "We love you, Mom!" They drank their lemonade and enjoyed their playtime. They learned not to break lemons again.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Spot. Spot was a very unusual dog. He loved to stretch and play golf. Every day, he would go to the park with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. One sunny day, Spot and Tim went to the park to play golf. They saw a little girl named Sue who was sad. She lost her toy and could not find it. Spot and Tim wanted to help Sue feel happy again. Spot used his long legs to stretch and look for the toy. He found it behind a tree. Sue was so happy, she hugged Spot and Tim. The three friends played golf together and had lots of fun. The moral of the story is that helping others can make you happy too.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to clap her hands and play with her toys. One day, she found a big wheel in her backyard. She was very happy and started to play with it. Amy wanted to play with her friends, so she organized all her toys in a line. She pushed the big wheel with her friends, and they all clapped their hands. They were having a lot of fun. But soon, they saw that the wheel was rolling too fast and going towards a big tree. Amy and her friends ran to stop the wheel before it hit the tree. They all worked together and stopped the wheel just in time. They clapped their hands and were very happy. They learned that by working together and being organized, they could do anything. And from that day on, they always helped each other and played together.

Once upon a time, there was a clever octopus named Ollie. Ollie had many friends in the sea. One day, Ollie and his friends wanted to play a game. Ollie said, "Let's play hide and seek!" Ollie was very good at hide and seek. He could change color to match the rocks and plants. Ollie's friends looked and looked, but they could not find him. Ollie thought, "I will stay here and wait for my friends to find me." As Ollie stayed hidden, he saw a big fish coming. The big fish was hungry and wanted to eat Ollie's friends! Ollie knew he had to do something. He changed color again and swam fast to scare the big fish away. Ollie's friends were so happy that he saved them. They said, "Ollie, you are so clever! Thank you for keeping us safe!" From that day on, Ollie and his friends played many games, but they always stayed close to each other to stay safe.
