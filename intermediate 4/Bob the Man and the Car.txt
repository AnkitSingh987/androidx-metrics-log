Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Man and the Car

Once upon a time, there was an elderly man named Bob. He had a red car with four big tires. Bob loved to drive his car around the town. One day, he saw a little girl named Sue sitting on the side of the road. She looked sad. Bob stopped his car and asked Sue, "Why are you sad?" Sue looked at him and said, "I am tired and my legs hurt. I can't walk home." Bob thought for a moment and said, "I can help you. Please seat in my car, and I will take you home." Sue smiled and said, "Thank you!" She sat in the car on the soft seat. As they drove, Bob told Sue stories about when he was young. Sue listened and laughed. The big tires on the car went round and round, taking them closer to Sue's home. When they arrived at Sue's house, she said, "Thank you, Bob, for the ride and the stories." Bob smiled and said, "You're welcome. I'm glad I could help you." Sue waved goodbye as Bob drove away, his big tires taking him on more adventures. From that day on, whenever Sue saw Bob's red car with the big tires, she would wave and smile. Bob would always wave back, happy that he had helped a friend. And they both knew that sometimes, all it takes is a soft seat and a kind heart to make someone's day better.

Once upon a time there was a duck named Quack. Quack lived near a pond which was always very warm and inviting. One day Quack's best friend, a little frog called Hop, came to visit. "Hi Quack, I've been invited to a special party tonight. Would you like to come?" said Hop. "Sure! I would love to go," said Quack. So the two friends set off and they had a wonderful night at the party. Quack and Hop danced, laughed, and ate some delicious food. After the party, Quack noticed that one of Hop's friends had left a pile of waste in the pond. That didn't seem very inviting! "Hey Hop, why did your friend leave all that waste in the warm pond? That's not very nice," said Quack. "I'm sorry Quack," said Hop. "My friend must have forgotten to take it away with him. Let's get rid of it and make the pond inviting again." So the two friends worked together to take out the waste and throw it away in a bin. Soon, the pond was clean and warm again, just as inviting as before!

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a box full of toys. One day, Tim found a piece of tape in his toy box. He did not know where it came from. It was unknown to him. Tim wanted to find a match for the tape. He looked around his room and saw many things. He saw his toy cars, his stuffed animals, and his blocks. Tim tried to match the tape with his toy cars, but it did not fit. He tried to match the tape with his stuffed animals, but it did not fit either. Finally, Tim tried to match the tape with his blocks. The tape stuck to the blocks and held them together. Tim was happy that he found a match for the unknown tape. He played with his blocks and tape all day, building tall towers and big bridges. And from that day on, Tim always knew that the tape was a great match for his blocks.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily went to her garden to play. She liked to discover new things. Today, she saw something red hiding behind the green leaves. She looked closer and found a big, round tomato! Lily ran to her mom and said, "Look, Mom! I found a tomato!" Her mom smiled and said, "Good job, Lily! That's a very fair tomato you found!" Lily felt proud and happy. The next day, Lily and her mom made a yummy salad with the tomato. They shared it with their family. Everyone loved it and said, "Thank you, Lily, for discovering the tomato!" Lily felt happy and proud to help her family.
