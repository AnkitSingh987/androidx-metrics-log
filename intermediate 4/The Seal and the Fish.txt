Category: Intermediate
Title: The Seal and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a little seal. The seal lived in the big, wet sea. The seal liked to play all day. One day, the seal met a new friend. The friend was a fish. The fish was wet too. They wanted to play together. The seal and the fish played a game. They would shake their bodies in the water. They laughed and had fun. They were happy to be wet friends in the sea.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play on the swing at the park. One sunny day, she went to the park with her mommy and saw a new swing. It was big and colorful. Lily was very excited to try it. As Lily played on the new swing, she saw a little boy named Timmy. Timmy was making silly faces and being very loud. Lily thought Timmy was funny, but she also felt that he was not being nice to others. Timmy was not sharing the swing and kept making silly faces at everyone. Lily decided to display kindness to Timmy. She asked him if he wanted to play together on the swing. Timmy was surprised because no one had ever asked him to play before. They took turns on the swing and had lots of fun. Timmy learned that sharing and being kind was better than being silly and loud. And from that day on, Timmy and Lily became good friends who always shared and played nicely together. The moral of the story is that kindness and sharing can turn a silly person into a good friend.

One day, a big cat named Leo the Leopard was playing in the woods. He saw a mysterious box under a tree. Leo was very curious and wanted to know what was inside the box. He tried to open it, but it was closed very tight. Leo went to his friend, Sam the Squirrel, for help. Sam said that they needed a special key to open the box. They both went to find the key. After a long time, they found the key under a big rock. They were very excited and happy. Leo and Sam went back to the mysterious box. They used the key to open it. Inside the box, they found a beautiful part to play with. It was a fun game with lots of colors. They played with it all day and had a great time. The mysterious box had brought them a new and exciting game to enjoy together.

Once upon a time, there was a baby who loved to play with his toys. He had a big, red ball and a soft, blue bear. The baby and the bear were best friends. They played together every day. One day, the baby and the bear were playing in the kitchen. The baby saw a spicy pepper on the table. He thought it was a toy and picked it up. The baby said to the bear, "Look, I found a new toy!" The bear knew the pepper was not a toy, but he didn't want the baby to feel sad. So, the bear said, "Let's show it to Mommy first." The baby and the bear went to Mommy, who was cooking dinner. She saw the spicy pepper and said, "Oh no, baby! That's not a toy. It's a spicy pepper for our food!" The baby gave the pepper back to Mommy. She gave him a big hug and said, "Thank you for being so helpful!" The baby and the bear went back to playing with their toys, happy to have helped Mommy.

Anna and Ben like to play on the beach. They pretend they are pirates and look for treasure. They dig holes in the sand and find shells and stones. They put them in a bucket and say, "Arrr, we are rich!" One day, they play too close to the water. They do not see the tide coming in. The tide is the water that goes up and down. Sometimes it is low and sometimes it is high. When it is high, it covers more of the beach. "Look, Ben, I found a big shell!" Anna says. She holds it up and smiles. "Wow, that is a nice shell, Anna!" Ben says. He runs over to see it. But then, a big wave comes and splashes them. It is cold and salty. It takes their bucket and their shells and stones. It also takes Anna's shell. They are wet and sad. "Hey, give us back our treasure!" Ben shouts at the water. He tries to chase the wave, but it is too fast and too strong. "Ben, come back!" Anna cries. She is scared. She does not want to lose Ben too. But Ben does not hear her. He runs into the water and tries to grab the bucket. He does not see the next wave coming. It is bigger and stronger than the first one. It knocks him down and pulls him under. He cannot breathe. He cannot swim. He cannot scream. Anna watches in horror. She screams for help, but no one hears her. She is alone on the beach. She wishes they had not played so close to the water. She wishes they had not pretended to be pirates. She wishes Ben was still with her. She wishes the water was clean and not dirty. She wishes the tide would go away and never come back.
