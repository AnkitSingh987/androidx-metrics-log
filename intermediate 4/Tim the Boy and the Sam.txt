Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Sam

Once upon a time, in a normal little town, there lived a young boy named Tim. Tim was a happy youth who loved to play with his friends. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. One sunny day, Tim and his friend, Sam, found a big, red ball. They both wanted to play with it, but they couldn't agree on what game to play. Tim wanted to kick the ball, but Sam wanted to throw it. They began to quarrel. "Why don't we take turns?" asked Tim. "I'll kick it first, then you can throw it after." Sam thought about it for a moment and then smiled. "That's a great idea!" he said. They stopped quarreling and started playing. They took turns and had lots of fun. And from that day on, Tim and Sam always shared and played happily together.

One day, Jack and his family went to the park. It was sunny outside, and the sun was bright yellow. Jack and his family were having fun when Jack's mom heard a ringing sound. It was her phone. She answered it and realized that she had to pick up some medicine urgently. Jack's mom said, "Jack! We have to go now! We have to get some medicine!" Jack said, "Why do we need medicine?" "We need it because we have to be very careful about keeping ourselves healthy," his mom replied. So Jack and his family hopped in the car and drove to the pharmacy. They picked up the medicine and soon were back in the park. The sun was still shining bright yellow, and they had lots of fun.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a pretty bottle. It was a bottle of perfume. Mia was very curious about the bottle. She thought it was juice and wanted to drink it. Mia's mom saw her and said, "No, Mia! Don't drink that! It's perfume, not juice." Mia was stubborn and didn't listen to her mom. She thought, "It looks like juice, so it must be juice." When her mom went to cook, Mia opened the bottle and tried to drink it. But, the smell was very strong, and she didn't like it. She felt a little sick. Mia's mom came back and saw Mia's face. "Mia, did you try to drink the perfume?" she asked. Mia nodded, feeling sad. Her mom hugged her and said, "It's okay. Now you know that perfume is not for drinking. We should always listen to our mom and dad. They know what's best for us." Mia learned her lesson and never tried to drink perfume again.

Once upon a time, there was a gray bunny. The bunny lived in a small, cozy hole. The bunny had a favorite food: apples! One day, the gray bunny went on a walk to find apples. He hopped and hopped until he saw a big apple tree. The tree had many big, red apples. The gray bunny was very happy. The gray bunny started to pick apples. He ate one, and then another. The apples were yummy! After eating, the gray bunny went back to his cozy hole. He was full and happy. The end.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little squirrel named Tim. Tim loved to find acorns and play with his friends. One day, he found a big acorn under a tall tree. He was so happy to find it! But then, Tim saw his friend, Sam the squirrel, frown. Sam was angry because he could not find any acorns. Tim wanted to make Sam happy, so he gave Sam the big acorn he found. Sam's frown turned into a big smile! From that day on, Tim and Sam played together and shared their acorns with each other. They were the best of friends and always had fun in the big forest. And they never let an angry frown come between them again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a mask that was very important to her. She would mind her mask and keep it safe. One day, Sue went to the park with her mask. At the park, Sue played with her mask. She put it on and pretended to be a superhero. She was having lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A dog came running and took Sue's mask away. Sue was sad and scared. Sue ran after the dog to get her mask back. The dog stopped and looked at Sue. It was not a real dog, but a boy in a dog costume! The boy took off the dog head and said, "I'm sorry, I was just playing." Sue was surprised, but happy to get her mask back. They both laughed and became friends.
