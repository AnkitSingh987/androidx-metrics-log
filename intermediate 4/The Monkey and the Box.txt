Category: Intermediate
Title: The Monkey and the Box

One day, a monkey went to a modern house. The monkey was very happy. He saw a big box in the house. The monkey was curious. The monkey said, "What is in the box?" He wanted to reveal what was inside. So, the monkey opened the box. Inside the box, there was a big, shiny ball. The monkey was very surprised. Then, the ball started to talk. "Hello, monkey! I am a magic ball. I can make your wishes come true." The monkey was very excited. He wished for a big banana tree. The magic ball made the wish come true, and the monkey was very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a fancy belt. It was very pretty and shiny. The belt lived in a big box with other belts. But the fancy belt was the most special one. One day, a little girl named Amy found the fancy belt. She said, "Wow, what a pretty belt! I want to wear it." So, she put the belt around her waist. The belt was so happy to be worn by Amy. Amy wore the belt to a party. She met a boy named Tim. Tim said, "I like your belt. It's very nice." Amy smiled and said, "Thank you. I trust my belt to make me look good." They played together and had lots of fun. The fancy belt was happy to help Amy make a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. She loved to decorate her room with pretty things. One day, she found a big box in her room. She was so excited to see what was inside! Sally opened the box and found a little frightened kitten. "Don't be scared," she said to the kitten. "I will give you a hug!" The kitten felt better and started to purr. Sally and the kitten became best friends. One day, while they were decorating the room, the kitten jumped on a chair and knocked over a big vase. It broke into many pieces. Sally was upset, but then she saw something shiny in the broken vase. It was a beautiful necklace! Sally and the kitten were so happy. They knew that they would always be friends and have fun decorating together.

Once upon a time, there was a big, heavy rock. The rock could not move. One day, a small bird came to the rock. The bird said, "Hello, rock! Can you peek and see what is behind you?" The rock said, "I cannot peek. I am too heavy. Can you help me?" The bird tried to push the rock, but it was too hard. The bird had an idea. "I will ask my friends for help!" said the bird. The bird went to find her friends. She found a cat, a dog, and a rabbit. They all came to help the heavy rock. Together, they pushed the rock. The rock moved a little. They could peek behind the rock. They saw a big tree with a sign. The sign had a new language on it. The bird, the cat, the dog, and the rabbit all learned the new language together. They were very happy.

One day, a girl named Lucy and her daddy went to the park. They saw a big, impressive slide. Lucy wanted to go on the slide, but her daddy said it was too big for her. Lucy did not like this. She started to disagree with her daddy. She said, "I am big enough to go on the slide!" Her daddy said, "No, Lucy, it is too big for you." But Lucy did not listen. She ran to the slide and climbed up. Her daddy was worried, but he watched her closely. Lucy went down the slide very fast. When she reached the bottom, she was happy. Her daddy saw that Lucy was okay and smiled. He was proud of her for being brave. They played at the park for a long time and had lots of fun together.

Lily liked to hide. She hid under the bed, behind the sofa, in the closet. She hid from her mom, her dad, her brother. She hid when it was time to eat, time to sleep, time to play. She thought hiding was fun. One day, Lily saw a rainbow in the sky. It was big and bright and had many colors. Lily wanted to see it closer. She ran outside and looked for a place to hide. She saw a big tree with a hole in it. She thought it was a good hiding spot. She crawled into the hole and waited. She hoped the rainbow would come to her. She waited and waited and waited. She heard a voice. It was not her mom, her dad, or her brother. It was a small and squeaky voice. "Hello, who are you?" the voice said. Lily was scared. She peeked out of the hole. She saw a tiny mouse with a red hat. The mouse looked at her and smiled. "I'm Lily. Who are you?" Lily said. "I'm Max. I live here. This is my home. Do you want to be my friend?" Max said. Lily nodded. She liked Max. He was cute and friendly. He showed her his home. He had a bed, a table, a chair, and a book. He also had a picture of a rainbow. "That's a rainbow. I like rainbows. Do you like rainbows?" Max said. "Yes, I do. I came here to see the rainbow. It's in the sky. It's big and bright and has many colors." Lily said. "Really? Can I see it too?" Max said. "Sure. Come with me. We can go outside and see the rainbow." Lily said. Lily and Max crawled out of the hole. They looked up at the sky. They saw the rainbow. It was still there. It was still big and bright and had many colors. Lily and Max smiled. They held hands. They were happy. They did not know that the rainbow was famous. It was the most beautiful rainbow in the world. Many people came to see it. They took pictures and videos. They posted them on the internet. They said it was a miracle. They said it was a sign. They said it was a wonder. But Lily and Max did not care. They only cared about each other. They were friends. They had fun. They hid under the rainbow.
