Category: Intermediate
Title: The Gray Bunny and the Flower

Once upon a time, there was a little gray bunny. The bunny loved to play with his friends in the big green field. One day, they found a pretty flower growing near a tree. The flower was red and smelled very nice. The little gray bunny and his friends liked the flower a lot. The bunny and his friends wanted to take the flower home. But the wise old owl said, "No, let the flower stay here and grow. It will make the field even more beautiful." The little gray bunny and his friends listened to the wise old owl and left the flower there. Days went by, and the flower grew bigger and more beautiful. The little gray bunny and his friends were happy they listened to the wise old owl. They danced around the flower and started to applaud. The moral of the story is to listen to wise advice and let beautiful things grow.

Once upon a time there was a tiger who loved to zip. He enjoyed the feeling of the wind in his face and the speed of the ride. One day, he was so excited about the idea of zipping he didn't pay attention to where he was going. Suddenly, he heard a loud "MEOW!" He realized he had zipped right up to a very busy cat. "What do you want?!" said the cat in a grumpy voice. "I just wanted to zip," said the tiger apologetically. The cat smiled and said, "Well why don't you zip around here instead? We have lots of places to zip right here in my backyard!" The tiger thanked the cat, and the two of them zipped around the backyard having lots of fun. The tiger loved zipping around with the busy cat and they had many happy adventures!

Anna and Ben like to play in the park. They see many flowers and trees and birds. They also see a big bee. The bee is yellow and black and has a long sting. "Look, a big bee!" Anna says. "I want to touch it. It is pretty." "No, Anna, don't touch it!" Ben says. "The bee can hurt you. It can sting you and make you cry." But Anna does not listen to Ben. She runs to the big bee and tries to catch it. The big bee does not like that. It flies away from Anna and then comes back. It stings Anna on her hand. "Ow, ow, ow!" Anna screams. "The bee stung me! It hurts!" Ben runs to Anna and sees her hand. It is red and swollen. He feels sorry for Anna. He offers her his toy car. He hopes it will make her feel better. "Here, Anna, you can have my toy car," Ben says. "It is fast and fun. Maybe it will make you forget the bee." But Anna does not want the toy car. She is too sad and angry. She throws the toy car on the ground and breaks it. She does not say thank you to Ben. She only cries and cries. Ben is shocked and hurt. He likes his toy car. He was nice to Anna and she was mean to him. He does not understand why she did that. He picks up his broken toy car and walks away. He does not want to play with Anna anymore. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved candy more than anything else. One day, he saw a big jar of candy on the top shelf in the kitchen. Tim was very excited and wanted to get the candy. Tim tried to mind his mom and not climb on the furniture, but he was too tempted by the candy. So, he climbed up the shelf to get the candy jar. But, oh no! The jar fell and broke on the floor. All the candy was gone, and Tim felt very sad. Mom came into the kitchen and saw the mess. She was not happy, but she knew Tim was sad. She hugged Tim and said, "Next time, just ask for help, and we can get the candy together." Tim learned his lesson and promised to mind his mom from then on.
