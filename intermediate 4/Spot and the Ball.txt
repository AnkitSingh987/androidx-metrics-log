Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot liked to play outside, even when it was hot. One hot day, Spot saw a big red ball in the yard. He wanted to play with the ball, but it was too hot to touch. Spot paused and thought, "How can I play with the ball if it's so hot?" He had an idea. He would ask his friend, the cat named Whiskers, for help. Spot ran to Whiskers and said, "Whiskers, the ball is hot. Can you help me?" Whiskers thought for a moment and said, "Yes, I can help you." Whiskers found a long stick and used it to rub the hot ball until it was cool enough for Spot to play with. Spot was so happy, and he and Whiskers played with the ball all day long.

One day, a colorful fish named Bob wanted to swim. Bob loved to swim in the big blue sea. He liked to see all the other fish and play with them. One morning, Bob found a waffle floating in the water. It was a big waffle, with lots of squares. Bob was very hungry, so he took a bite of the waffle. It was yummy! After eating the waffle, Bob felt happy and full. He swam all day with his fish friends, playing and having fun. The colorful fish and the waffle were a big part of Bob's happy day.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree in a big park. A little boy named Timmy loved to play near the tree. One day, Timmy saw a big wheel near the tree. He was very happy and wanted to play with it. Timmy's friend, Sally, came to play with him. She saw the wheel and said, "Let's play with the wheel, Timmy!" Timmy agreed, and they started to play. They rolled the wheel and ran after it, laughing and having fun. But then, the wheel rolled too fast and went near a big road. Timmy and Sally tried to stop it, but they could not. The wheel rolled onto the road, and a big car hit it. The wheel broke into many pieces. Timmy and Sally were very sad. They should have been more careful with the wheel.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Sam. He was a very dependable boy, and he always obeyed his mom. One day his mom asked him to help her make spaghetti. Sam said yes, and he excitedly went to the kitchen with her. His mom showed him how to mix the noodles and the sauce. Sam worked hard and mixed everything up as his mom said. Then it was time to answer the door. There was a big surprise - friends were coming to eat the spaghetti with them! Sam felt a little scared, but he knew his mom was dependable and everything would be alright. He said good-bye to his mom and opened the door. Sam was so happy to see all his friends. They all sat down and ate the spaghetti that Sam made with his mom. Sam was more dependable than ever after that day. He knew that if he helped around the house and did as his mom said, he could have fun surprises!

Once upon a time, there was an elderly frog named Mr. Hop. He lived in a swamp with his best friend, a little bird named Chirpy. They loved to play and have fun together. One sunny day, Mr. Hop said to Chirpy, "Let's record our song!" Chirpy liked the idea. They found a big leaf to use as a stage. Mr. Hop jumped and croaked, while Chirpy sang and danced. They had a great time making their song. When they finished, Mr. Hop and Chirpy showed their song to all their swamp friends. The other animals clapped and cheered. They were so happy to see their friends having fun. Mr. Hop and Chirpy smiled and knew they would remember this day forever.

One day, a big lion was walking slow in the forest. He was very hungry and wanted a snack. He looked around and saw a small rabbit. "Hello, rabbit," said the lion with a big roar. "I am hungry and you look like a good snack." The rabbit was scared. "Please, Mr. Lion, don't eat me! I can show you where to find a better snack." The lion thought for a moment and said, "Okay, show me." The rabbit led the lion to a big tree with lots of yummy fruit. The lion ate the fruit and was very happy. He thanked the rabbit and they became good friends. From that day on, the lion never roared at the rabbit again.
