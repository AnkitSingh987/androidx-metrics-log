Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Chimney

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was adventurous and liked to spend her days outside. One day, she saw a big chimney on a house. She wanted to see what was inside. Sue climbed up the chimney and found a small, friendly bird. The bird said, "Hello! Do you want to spend the day with me?" Sue was very happy and said, "Yes! Let's play together." They spent the whole day playing and having fun. Sue and the bird became best friends. At the end of the day, Sue climbed down the chimney and went home. She couldn't wait to tell her mom about her adventurous day with her new bird friend.

Once upon a time, in a big, green forest, there was a small, black cat. The black cat loved to lie down under the tall trees. One sunny day, a big, brown dog came into the forest. The dog was loud and harsh. The black cat did not like the big, brown dog. The big, brown dog saw the black cat lying under the tree. The dog wanted to play with the black cat, but the black cat did not want to play. The black cat ran away from the dog. The big, brown dog chased the black cat through the forest. The black cat was fast and hid behind a big rock. The big, brown dog could not find the black cat. The dog felt sad and walked away. The black cat came out from behind the rock and went back to lie under the tall tree. Now, the black cat could rest in peace again.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw a delicious ice cream truck and asked his mom, "Can I have ice cream, please?" His mom said, "Yes, you can have one." Tim got a big ice cream cone with his favorite flavor, chocolate. He was so happy and started to eat it. As he ate his ice cream, he saw his own image in a big mirror near the ice cream truck. He looked at his face and saw ice cream all over his mouth. He laughed and thought it was funny. A little girl named Sue saw Tim and wanted ice cream too. She asked her dad, "Can I have ice cream like Tim?" Her dad said, "Of course, let's go get some." Sue got a strawberry ice cream cone and they all sat together in the park, enjoying their delicious ice cream on a sunny day.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big, purple ball. He wanted to play with it, but he did not know who it belonged to. Tim looked around and saw a girl named Sue. He asked her, "Is this your purple ball?" Sue shook her head and said, "No, it's not mine." Tim realized the ball was all alone. He decided to take it home and play with it. He and Sue had lots of fun playing together with the purple ball at the park. And from that day on, Tim and Sue became best friends.

Once there was a naughty boy. He was always getting into trouble. He didn't like to obey the rules. He thought he could do whatever he wanted. One day, the boy went underground. He was not allowed to go underground because it was dangerous. But the boy didn't care. He just kept going deeper and deeper. Finally, the boy got to a place he had never been before. Everything there seemed very strange. It wasn't normal. All of a sudden, the boy realized he was in deep trouble. The boy heard a deep voice and felt something grabbing his arm. He was so scared. It was the King of the underground! The King was angry that the boy had disturbed his kingdom and he decided to punish him. The boy was never seen again. His parents were so worried but they never found him. They had to accept that the naughty boy was gone forever.

One day, a little dog named Spot went for a wander in the woods. He saw a big, strong tree with a frame hanging from it. Spot was very curious about the frame, but he could not reach it. He tried to jump, but it was too high. He thought, "I need to find a way to get that frame." In the woods, Spot met a tall giraffe named Gerry. Gerry was very kind and wanted to help Spot. They went back to the big, strong tree together. Spot asked Gerry to lift him up so he could reach the frame. Gerry agreed and lifted Spot with his long neck. Spot finally reached the frame and saw a beautiful picture inside. It was a picture of a happy dog just like him, playing with a ball. Spot was so happy he found the frame, and he thanked Gerry with a big hug. They both went home, feeling happy and proud of their new friendship.
