Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to walk with his dog, Zip. One day, they went for a walk in the park. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. Tim and Zip were very happy. As they walked, they saw a big girl named Sue. Sue looked upset. Tim asked her, "Why are you upset?" Sue said, "I lost my toy. Can you help me find it?" Tim and Zip wanted to help Sue, so they all walked together to look for the toy. They looked and looked, but they could not find Sue's toy. Sue became more upset, and even Tim and Zip felt sad. In the end, they had to go home without finding the toy. Tim, Zip, and Sue were all very sad, and it was a bad day for everyone.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly lady named Sue. Sue was very kind and loved to help others. One day, she found a small box with a label on it. The label said, "For Tim." Sue knew Tim was a young boy who lived nearby. Sue decided to take the box to Tim's house. On the way, she met a cat. The cat said, "Meow, can you help me find food?" Sue replied, "Of course, little cat. Let's find some food for you." So, Sue and the cat looked for food together. As they were walking, Sue accidentally dropped the box. The label fell off, but Sue picked it up and put it back on. They found some food for the cat and continued to Tim's house. When they arrived, Sue gave the box to Tim, and he was very happy. The cat was happy too, because it had found a new friend in Sue. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a green frog named Bob. Bob loved to eat strawberries. One day, Bob went to find some strawberries to eat. Bob saw a big strawberry under a tree. He was very happy. He picked up the strawberry and started to eat it. It was very yummy. Bob ate and ate until he was full. After eating the strawberry, Bob felt sleepy. He yawned a big yawn. He found a soft spot under the tree and lay down. Bob closed his eyes and went to sleep, dreaming of more yummy strawberries.

Once upon a time, there was a charming cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with his net. He liked to catch bugs and leaves with it. One day, Tom went to the park with his net. While Tom was playing, he heard a sound. It was a tiny bird stuck in a tree. The bird was scared and couldn't fly. Tom wanted to help the bird. So, he used his net to catch the bird and set it free. But, something unexpected happened! When Tom caught the bird, it changed into a big, pretty butterfly. The butterfly thanked Tom for helping and they became best friends. From that day on, Tom and the butterfly played together in the park with the net.

One day, a big billboard stood tall next to the road. It had a clear picture of a happy cat. Sam and his dad walked by the billboard every day. Sam loved the happy cat and smiled every time he saw it. One morning, Sam and his dad walked by the billboard again. But the happy cat was gone! The billboard had changed. Now, it showed a big red ball. Sam asked his dad, "Where did the happy cat go?" His dad said, "The billboard changed, Sam. Sometimes, things change and we see new pictures." Sam looked at the big red ball and started to smile. He liked the new picture too. From that day on, Sam learned that change can be fun and exciting.

Once upon a time, in a calm meadow, there was a little daisy. The daisy loved the sun, and every day it would open up its petals to feel the warm light. One day, a tiny bug came to visit the daisy. "Hello, little daisy!" said the bug. "You look so happy in the sun." "Hi, bug!" said the daisy. "I love the sun. It makes me feel good." The bug smiled and played with the daisy for a while. They laughed and had fun. But then, the sky grew dark and the wind began to blow. "Oh no!" said the bug. "A storm is coming. You should shut your petals, daisy, to stay safe." The daisy listened to the bug and shut its petals tight. The storm came and went, and the daisy stayed safe and dry. When the sun came back out, the daisy opened its petals once again. "Thank you, bug," said the daisy. "You helped me stay safe." The bug smiled and said, "You're welcome, daisy. Friends help each other." From that day on, the daisy and the bug were the best of friends, and they played together in the calm meadow every day.
