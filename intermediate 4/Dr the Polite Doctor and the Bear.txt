Category: Intermediate
Title: Dr the Polite Doctor and the Bear

One day, a polite doctor named Dr. Brown was walking in the park. He saw a little girl named Lily. She was sad. Lily's toy bear was too big to play with. "Can you help me, Dr. Brown?" Lily asked. "Can you make my bear shrink?" Dr. Brown thought about it. "Yes, I can help you," he said. He took out a magic wand and waved it over the bear. The bear started to shrink! But then, a big dog came and took the bear away. Lily and Dr. Brown ran after the dog. They wanted to get the bear back. "Please, dog, give us the bear," Dr. Brown said. The dog was a good dog and gave the bear back. Lily was happy. She thanked Dr. Brown and they played with the bear together.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was very excited. He wanted to win a big race. If he won, he would get a holiday. The day of the race came. Tim ran very fast. He had a friend named Sam. Sam wanted to win too. They both ran and ran. But then, they started to fight. They both wanted to win so bad. Because of the fight, they both stopped running. They did not win the race. They did not get the holiday. Tim and Sam were both very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was confused because he wanted to eat something yummy. He asked his mommy to help him find a snack. His mommy said, "I recommend a melon." Tim went to find the melon in the kitchen. But when he got there, he saw a big cat. The cat was angry and did not want Tim to take the melon. Tim was scared, but he really wanted the melon. Tim tried to get the melon, but the big cat jumped and took the melon away. Tim was very sad and cried because he did not get the yummy melon. The big cat ate the melon and ran away.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim found a big package in his room. He was very happy and excited. He gazed at the package and thought about what could be inside. He wanted to open it right away. Tim's mom came into the room and saw him with the package. She said, "Tim, before you open the package, can you help me clean the house?" Tim thought for a moment. He knew he should help his mom, but he really wanted to open the package. Tim decided to help his mom first. They cleaned the house together and had fun. After they finished cleaning, Tim's mom said, "Thank you for your help, Tim. Now you can open your package." Tim opened the package and found a deep blue toy car inside. He loved it! Tim learned that when you help others, good things will come to you.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play outside. The sun was bright and the ground was dry. Tim saw his shadow on the ground and thought it was funny. He started to dance and his shadow danced too. Tim was very happy. As Tim danced, something unexpected happened. His shadow started to dance on its own! Tim was surprised and a little scared. He stopped dancing, but his shadow kept dancing. Tim wondered what was happening. Then, Tim saw a little bug on the ground. The bug was making the shadow dance! The bug was playing with Tim's shadow. Tim laughed and started to dance again. Now, Tim, his shadow, and the little bug were all dancing together. They had a fun day in the sun.

Once upon a time, there was a foolish dog named Spot. Spot loved to wear his bathrobe all day long. One day, Spot met a little girl named Lucy. Lucy asked, "Why do you wear a bathrobe all the time?" Spot said, "I like it. It is soft and warm." Lucy said, "You should not wear it all the time. Something bad could happen." But Spot did not listen to Lucy. He kept wearing his bathrobe everywhere he went. One day, Spot went to a park. He ran and played with other dogs. Spot was having so much fun. But then, Spot's bathrobe got stuck on a tree. Spot tried to get free, but he could not. He was stuck and felt sad. Lucy saw Spot and said, "I told you something bad would happen." The moral of the story is: Always listen to good advice from friends, or something bad might happen.
