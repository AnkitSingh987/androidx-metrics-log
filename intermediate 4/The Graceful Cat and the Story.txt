Category: Intermediate
Title: The Graceful Cat and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a graceful cat. She liked to play outside with her friends. One day, while playing, she got a small cut on her leg. Her mom saw the cut and said, "Let's clean it and put a bandage on it." The graceful cat did not want to stop playing, so she said no. Her mom told her it was important, but the cat still said no. The next day, the cut got worse. The graceful cat was sad and in pain. She should have listened to her mom. The moral of the story is to always listen to your mom when she wants to help you.

Once upon a time, there was a popular toy named Zip. Zip was a small car that all the kids liked. Zip had a passport. The passport let Zip go to fun places with the kids. One day, Zip's passport was lost. Zip was sad and the kids were sad too. They could not take Zip to fun places. The kids looked for the passport. They looked under the bed, in the toy box, and in the closet. But they could not find it. Then, a little girl named Amy had an idea. She asked her dog, Spot, to help find the passport. Spot sniffed and sniffed. He found the passport under a big pillow. Zip and the kids were happy again. Now, they could all go to fun places with Zip's passport. And they all lived happily ever after.

"Look at the patch!" said Ma. Little Jake looked up from the ground. He saw something green on the wall above him. Ma pointed to the patch. It was big and heavy. "What is it?" asked Little Jake. Ma smiled. "It is something very special. It will save us." Little Jake looked closely at the patch. It had something written on it. Ma read it aloud. "Help is here." Ma put her arm around Little Jake. "Let's wait. The patch will save us." Little Jake didn't understand why the patch would save them, but he held onto Ma's arm and waited. Suddenly, a loud noise filled the air. Ma and Little Jake looked up and saw big men coming to help. Little Jake smiled. Ma had been right. The patch had saved them.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a huge tree. The tree was a pine tree. It was very tall and strong. The tree had many green needles. The tree liked to supply food for the little animals. One day, a small bird came to the huge pine tree. The bird was hungry. The tree wanted to help the bird. The tree gave the bird some food. The bird was happy. But then, something unexpected happened! The small bird changed. It became a big, beautiful bird! The huge pine tree was surprised. The big bird said, "Thank you, tree, for your help." The big bird flew away, and the huge pine tree felt proud to have helped.

Once upon a time there was a small girl who loved to wear dresses. She had many dresses in her cupboard, but one day she noticed she was missing a special dress. She went to her mom and asked, "Mom, where is my white dress?" Her mom smiled and told her that the dress had been given to her brother. The little girl was sad and asked, "Why? I want to wear the white dress too!" Mom said, "Your brother wanted to look his best for grandpa's birthday party, so he borrowed the dress. Don't worry, you can wear it once he is done. He won't need it anymore after he wears it to the party." The little girl was happy again and thanked her mom for understanding. The girl waited patiently for her brother's return and when he brought the dress back, she put it on proudly. She wore the white dress to many special occasions, just like her brother!

Lorraine and her little brother, Rufus, were playing in the park. Rufus was very excited and started to jog around the park. He ran around trees, over hills and even around the pond. Lorraine watched her little brother with pride. She wanted him to have fun and enjoy his time in the park. Suddenly, Rufus felt a sharp pain in his foot. He had stepped on a spike! The spike was very sharp and it hurt him a lot. Rufus started crying. Lorraine saw what had happened. She said: "Rufus, let's go back to the house and put some cream on the sore spot. If we do not treat it, it will get worse and make you very unhappy." Rufus nodded. He learned a valuable lesson that day. He should be careful where he is running and stay away from sharp objects. The moral of the story is that it is very important to be careful and look out for dangers. It is better to be safe than to be sorry.
