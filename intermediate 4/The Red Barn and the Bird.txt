Category: Intermediate
Title: The Red Barn and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big red barn. In the barn, there were many animals. They were all friends. They liked to play and talk to each other. One day, a new animal came to the barn. It was a small bird. The bird was very glad to see the big red barn. The bird said, "Hi, I am new here. Can I play with you?" The other animals were happy to hear the bird. They said, "Yes, come and play with us!" The bird was very glad. They all played together and had fun. One day, the bird heard a loud noise. It was a big storm. The wind was strong, and the rain was heavy. The bird was scared. The other animals in the barn saw the bird was scared. They said, "Do not worry, we will help you." The animals all worked together to keep the bird safe. They made a warm, dry place for the bird to stay. The bird was very glad for their help. The storm went away, and the sun came out. The animals learned that it is good to help each other. When we work together, we can do big things. And the bird was very happy to have new friends in the big red barn.

Once upon a time, there was a humble mechanic named Tom. He loved to fix things in his big, red shop. One day, a little girl named Amy came to his shop with her broken toy car. "Can you fix my car, please?" Amy asked Tom. Tom nodded and said, "Yes, I can help you." Tom worked hard and soon, the toy car was as good as new. Amy was very happy and thanked Tom. Near Tom's shop, there was a small garden with a tiny flower that was about to bloom. Every day, Tom took care of the flower and gave it water. He waited for the flower to grow and bloom. One sunny day, the flower finally bloomed and it was the most beautiful flower in the garden. People came to see the flower and Tom's shop became very famous. But Tom stayed humble and always helped others. The moral of the story is to be kind and humble, and good things will happen to you.

Once upon a time, there was a big maze. In the maze lived a dog named Max and a cat named Luna. Max and Luna were friends, but sometimes they felt jealous of each other. One day, they heard a little bird singing. "I wish I could sing like that," said Max. "Me too," said Luna. They decided to ask the bird to teach them to sing. The bird agreed, and they began to practice. Max and Luna tried very hard, but they could not sing like the bird. They felt sad and jealous. The bird told them, "You don't need to be jealous. You both have your own special things you can do. Max, you can bark and protect your friends. Luna, you can climb trees and catch mice. Be happy with what you can do." Max and Luna learned that being jealous was not good. They should be happy with what they can do and not wish for what others can do. And from that day on, they were happy and played together in the maze, using their own special skills.

Once upon a time, there was a huge dog. His name was Barkley. Barkley enjoyed going to the park and playing with the other dogs. One day, Barkley was at the park. Suddenly, a boy walked up to him and started to throw a ball. Barkley was very excited. He ran and fetched the ball. He loved playing fetch. The boy started to throw the ball farther and farther. Soon, Barkley had to run to get the ball. He ran and ran and ran until he could not see the boy anymore. But Barkley did not stop running. He kept running and running until he could no longer see the park. Barkley was very far from home. He was scared and did not know what to do. All of a sudden, he spotted a huge, dark tree. He thought he should hide under the tree and wait for someone to come and find him. So he ran to the tree and hid under the branches. After a few hours, he heard a voice calling out his name. It was the boy from the park! Barkley ran out from under the tree as fast as he could. He was so happy to see the boy and he couldn't stop wagging his tail. The boy took Barkley home and promised to never throw the ball too far again. From then on, Barkley always stayed close to the boy and they were the best of friends.
