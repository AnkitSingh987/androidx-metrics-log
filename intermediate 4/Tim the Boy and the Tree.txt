Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Tree

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big, scary tree. Tim was scared, but his mom said, "Don't worry, it's just a tree. It can't hurt you." Tim thought about it and said, "Okay, mom. I want to play by the tree." His mom let him play near the tree. Tim had fun running around and playing with his ball. Then, something unexpected happened. The scary tree started to talk! It said, "Hello, little boy. I'm not scary. I just look different. Will you be my friend?" Tim was surprised but happy. He said, "Yes, tree, I'll be your friend!" And they played together all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim had a big dream. He wanted to open a restaurant. He loved to cook and share food with his friends. One day, Tim asked his friend, Sally, "Do you want to help me open my restaurant?" Sally said, "Yes, I would love to help!" So, they began to work together. They made a small restaurant in Tim's backyard. Tim and Sally cooked yummy food for everyone. People came to eat and said, "This is the best food ever!" They were very happy, and Tim's dream came true. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a sleepy boy. He liked to play with his friends at the club. The club was a fun place to be. Tim and his friends liked to run, jump, and laugh together. One sunny day, Tim and his friends went to the club. They saw a big slide there. Tim was excited to slide down. He climbed up and sat at the top. Tim was a little scared, but he knew it would be fun. Tim slid down the slide. He went fast and laughed as he went down. His friends clapped and cheered for him. Tim felt happy and proud. They all took turns sliding down the slide. After a fun day at the club, Tim went home feeling sleepy and happy.

One day, a big ball and a small ball had a race. They wanted to see who could roll the longest. The big ball said, "I will win because I am big!" The small ball said, "I will try my best!" They started to roll down a long hill. The big ball rolled very fast. The small ball tried to roll fast too, but it was hard. The big ball was far in front. The small ball said, "Wait for me!" But the big ball did not wait. It rolled faster and faster. Then, the big ball saw a big hole. It was too late to stop. The big ball fell into the hole. The small ball stopped and looked at the hole. It was sad. The big ball did not win, and the race had a bad ending.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his dad. They saw a delicate butterfly in the garden. Tim wanted to catch it, but it flew away. "Oh no!" he said. His dad told him, "Don't worry, Tim. We can look for more butterflies." As they walked, Tim saw a big tree. He wanted to climb it, but he struggled. His dad helped him up, and they sat on a branch. They looked around and saw many pretty flowers and birds. They even saw a squirrel. Suddenly, the squirrel started to talk! It said, "Hello, Tim and Dad! I have a gift for you." Tim and his dad were very surprised. The squirrel gave them a magic saw. "This saw can help you make anything you want," said the squirrel. They thanked the squirrel and went home. From that day on, Tim and his dad made many beautiful things with their magic saw.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely bicycle. The bicycle lived in a small house with a big family. The family had many bicycles, but they never used the lonely bicycle. One sunny day, a little girl named Anna saw the lonely bicycle. She wanted to ride it and make it happy. Anna asked her mom if she could ride the lonely bicycle. Her mom said yes, and Anna was very excited. Anna took the bicycle to the park. She waved to her friends as she rode the bicycle. The bicycle was not lonely anymore. It was very happy to be with Anna. They rode together every day and had lots of fun.
