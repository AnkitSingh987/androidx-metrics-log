Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and Tom and the Garden

Once upon a time, there were two best friends, Tim and Tom. They were playing together in the garden when they noticed something strange. It was a completely empty skull! Tim said, "Wow, look at the skull! Do you want to play with it?" Tom nodded and they both started picking it up and examining it carefully. They baked in the sun and played with the skull until it was time to go inside. The next day, they went back to the garden. To their surprise, the empty skull was gone! Tim asked Tom, "Where could it have gone?! Let's find it!" They searched high and low but couldn't find it. Finally, they decided to look in the tall trees. And there it was, the empty skull! "Yay we found it" shouted Tom. The two friends played with the skull for hours, until it was time for them to go home. They said goodbye and went back to their homes, happy that they found the empty skull.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a girl named Lucy. She loved to gather flowers and put them in her vest. One day, Lucy went to the park to look for flowers. She was very happy and excited. As she walked, she found many pretty flowers. She put them in her vest, one by one. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all the flowers out of her vest! Lucy was very upset. She did not know what to do. Suddenly, a kind squirrel came to help Lucy. The squirrel had seen the flowers fly away and had gathered them for her. Lucy was so surprised and happy! She thanked the squirrel and put the flowers back in her vest. From that day on, Lucy and the squirrel were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to talk and tell stories. One day, Lucy felt nervous. She had to tell a story in front of her friends at school. She did not want to mess up. At school, Lucy's teacher said, "Okay Lucy, it's your turn to tell a story." Lucy stood up and took a deep breath. She started to talk, "This story is about a big, friendly dog who goes on an adventure." As she talked, her friends listened and smiled. Lucy felt less nervous as she told her story. Her friends loved the story and clapped when she was done. Lucy smiled and said, "Thank you for listening to my story." She was happy that she talked and shared her story with her friends.

Sam liked to watch videos on his tablet. He watched videos of cars, animals, and songs. He watched videos all day and did not play with his toys or his friends. He did not listen to his mom or his dad when they asked him to do something. He only wanted to watch videos. One day, Sam's tablet ran out of power. He tried to plug it in, but it did not work. He looked for another plug, but he could not find one. He was very sad and angry. He cried and shouted. He did not know what to do without his videos. His mom came to see what was wrong. She saw Sam's tablet and his tears. She felt sorry for him, but she also wanted to teach him a lesson. She said, "Sam, you watch too many videos. You need to do other things, like play, read, and help. Videos are fun, but they are not everything. You need to balance your time and your activities. Do you understand?" Sam nodded, but he still felt unhappy. He did not like to hear his mom's words. He felt uncomfortable. He wanted his videos back. He said, "But mom, I love videos. They make me happy. Can I have my tablet back, please?" His mom said, "No, Sam. Not today. Today, you will do something else. You will help me dry the dishes. It is easy and fun. You will see." She took Sam to the kitchen and gave him a towel. She showed him how to dry the dishes and put them away. She smiled and praised him when he did a good job. Sam did not like it at first. He thought it was boring and hard. He missed his videos. But as he dried the dishes, he started to enjoy it. He liked to see the dishes shine. He liked to hear his mom's voice. He liked to feel useful and proud. He realized that drying the dishes was not so bad. It was actually fun. He finished drying the dishes and hugged his mom. He said, "Thank you, mom. I'm sorry I was rude. I learned my lesson. I will not watch videos all day. I will do other things, too. I will play, read, and help. I will balance my time and my activities. I understand now." His mom hugged him back and said, "I'm proud of you, Sam. You are a good boy. You learned a valuable lesson. Videos are fun, but they are not everything. There is more to life than videos. You need to explore, learn, and grow. You need to be happy and healthy. You need to be yourself." Sam smiled and said, "I love you, mom. You are the best. Can I watch one video now?" His mom laughed and said, "Okay, Sam. Just one. But only after you eat your lunch. Deal?" Sam agreed and said, "Deal. Thank you, mom. You are the best." The end.
