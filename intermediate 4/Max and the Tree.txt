Category: Intermediate
Title: Max and the Tree

Max and his dad were in the park and watched the aeroplane flying up in the sky. Max asked his dad excitedly, "What is that?" Dad explained, "That's an aeroplane, my boy. It's flying high up in the sky." Max said, "I want to fly in an aeroplane!". Dad smiled, showing his compassionate side, and said, "Maybe one day, son". Max was eager to fly. He then saw a tree in the park. He pointed to the tree and said, "I can show you something". Dad looked interested and said, "What is it?". Max then replied, "I will hang upside down in the tree, like an aeroplane!". Dad laughed and said, "That's a good idea, let's try it out". Max climbed the tree and was hanging like an aeroplane. Dad was impressed and smiled at his son's creativity. Max laughed and said, "I am flying like an aeroplane!". His dad smiled lovingly, feeling his compassionate heart swell. Max ran to his dad and hugged him tightly. Dad said, "That was amazing, my son". Max smiled and said, "I love flying like an aeroplane!"

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play outside. Tom has a green wagon. He puts his toys in the wagon. Lily has a doll. She puts her doll in the wagon too. "Let's go for a ride!" Tom says. He pulls the wagon. Lily sits in the wagon. They go around the yard. They see a big tree. They see a bird. They see a dog. "Look, a dog!" Lily says. She points to the dog. The dog is brown and white. He runs to them. He barks. He wants to play. "Hi, dog!" Tom says. He stops the wagon. He stands up. He pets the dog. The dog licks his hand. He wags his tail. "Can I pet him too?" Lily asks. She stands up. She gets out of the wagon. She walks to the dog. She pets the dog. The dog licks her face. She giggles. The dog is happy. Tom and Lily are happy. They play with the dog. They forget about the wagon. The wagon is green. It waits for them.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to weigh his cat, Fluffy. He put Fluffy on a big thing that tells how heavy things are. Tim saw that Fluffy was very heavy. He thought that if Fluffy got lighter, he would give her a reward. Tim was a bit ignorant, so he did not know that cats like Fluffy are just big. He tried to help Fluffy get lighter by taking her for walks and giving her less food. Fluffy did not like this, but she wanted the reward. One day, when they were walking, Fluffy saw a big cake on the ground. Fluffy ate the whole cake very fast. Tim was sad because he thought Fluffy would not get lighter now. But, something unexpected happened. Fluffy got lighter because the cake was magic. Tim was happy and gave Fluffy her reward, a big hug.

Once upon a time, there was a large hut in the woods. Many animals lived in the hut. They were all friends. They played and ate together. One day, the hut started to tremble. All the animals were scared. They did not know what was happening. They ran out of the hut to see what was making it tremble. Outside, they saw a big, friendly giant. The giant was just playing with a ball. He did not mean to make the hut tremble. The animals were not scared anymore. They asked the giant to play with them. The giant was happy to have new friends. They all played together and had lots of fun.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk down the road. The sun was shining and the road was long. Tim liked the road because it was flexible and bumpy. He liked to jump on the bumps and watch them move. As Tim walked, something strange happened. The road began to shrink! It got smaller and smaller. Tim was very surprised. He didn't know what to do. He thought maybe he was too big for the road now. Then, Tim had an idea. He jumped up and down on the flexible road. With each jump, the road grew bigger and bigger! Soon, it was back to its normal size. Tim was happy again. He continued walking down the road, jumping on the bumps and having fun.
