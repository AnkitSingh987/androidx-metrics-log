Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Dog and the Sun

Once upon a time, in a small humble town, there lived a little girl. She was a pupil at a school near her house. Every day, she would go to school and learn new things. One day, when the sun began to rise, she went to school as usual. But today, something unexpected happened. A big, friendly dog came into her class. All the kids were very excited and happy to see the dog. The teacher said, "This dog is lost, and we need to find its home." The little girl and her friends decided to help the dog. They searched and searched, and finally found the dog's home. The dog's family was so happy and thankful. The little girl and her friends felt very proud of themselves for helping the dog find its way home.

One day, a boy named Tim found a long thing in his room. It was very long and he did not know what it was. Tim asked his mom, "Mom, what is this long thing?" His mom said, "It is a long stick to wipe things." Tim wanted to try the long thing. He went to the kitchen and saw a dirty table. He took the long stick and wiped the table. The table was clean! Tim was happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The long thing began to talk! It said, "Thank you, Tim, for using me to wipe the table. I am a magic stick and I can talk!" Tim was surprised, but he was happy to have a new friend. They wiped and cleaned many things together and had lots of fun.

Kyle loved going to the museum. He saw so many wonderful things there. But one day, he went to the museum, and it was terrible. Everything was in disarray, and the museum looked old and worn. Kyle was very sad. He wanted the museum to be nice again. So, he decided to try to improve it. He started cleaning up, and soon the museum was looking much better. He found some paint, and he brightly painted the walls and the floors. All of a sudden, it looked like a brand new museum. Everyone was thrilled and they thanked Kyle for making it better. From then on, Kyle continued to help out at the museum, and it just kept improving. He was very proud of what he could do.

Once upon a time, in a quiet little town, there lived a girl named Sue. Sue loved to fix things. She had a small box filled with her tools. Sue was always happy when she could help her friends and family. One day, Sue found a broken toy yet. It was very sad and could not play with the other toys. Sue knew she had to fix it. She took out her tools and began to work. She was very quiet as she worked, not wanting to wake up her baby brother. After some time, Sue fixed the yet. It was so happy that it could play with the other toys again. Sue felt proud of her work. The quiet town was filled with happy laughter as the children played with their fixed toys. And Sue knew she would always be there to help fix things for her friends and family.

One day, a little girl named Mia was eager to solve a big problem. She wanted to make more space in her room for her toys. She thought and thought about how to do it. Mia asked her friend, Tom, "How can I make more space in my room?" Tom said, "Maybe you can put some toys in a box and give them away. Then you will have more space." Mia liked the idea. She found a big box and put some toys in it. Then she gave the box to a friend who needed toys. Now, Mia had more space in her room for her toys, and she was happy.

Sam was a 3 year old boy and today he brought a lovely toy to the park. He hugged it close as he played with his friends. At one point, he got very tired and thought it would be a good idea to take a nap. His friends were fearful, but they let him lie down under a tree, promising to take care of his toy. Still, Sam was a bit worried so he asked his friends to stay close by while he napped. They nodded their heads and he drifted off to sleep. Suddenly, he awoke to a horrible sound. He saw that his friends were gone and his toy was gone too! This made Sam so scared that he started to cry. With no one around to help him, Sam was stuck in the park. He tried to find his toy, but he was scared of the dark and the animals he could hear in the distance. He gave up and trudged back home, without his toy.
