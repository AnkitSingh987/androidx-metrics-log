Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Story

One warm day, Tim went to get a haircut. Tim did not like haircuts. They made him feel scared. He sat in the big chair and the nice lady put a cape on him. Tim's mom was there to tell him it would be okay. The nice lady started to cut Tim's hair. He felt the scissors near his ear and got more scared. Tim's mom held his hand and told him a story about a brave boy who also got a haircut. Tim listened to the story and tried to be brave like the boy. In the end, Tim's haircut was done. He looked in the mirror and saw his new haircut. Tim felt proud and happy. His mom told him he was very brave. They left the haircut place and went to get ice cream to celebrate.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red parrot named Polly. Polly lived in a small house with a nice family. They were very careful with Polly and always made sure she was happy. Every day, Polly would sit by the window and watch the kids play outside. One day, Polly saw the kids cleaning their toys. They were having fun and laughing. Polly wanted to help, so she tried to clean her cage. She was very careful not to break anything. She used her beak to pick up the small things and put them in the right place. The next day, when the family saw how clean Polly's cage was, they were so happy! They knew Polly had been watching them and learned how to clean. They gave Polly a big hug and a special treat. From that day on, Polly always helped the family clean. They all lived happily ever after.

One day, a good cat named Tom lay in the sun. He wore his favorite pants. He felt very happy. But he saw his friend, the dog named Max, looking sad. Max lost his pants while playing in the park. Tom wanted to help Max find his pants. They went to the park and looked everywhere. They looked under the slide, by the swings, and even in the sandbox. But they could not find Max's pants. They felt very tired and sad. But then, Tom saw something in a tree. It was Max's pants! How did they get up there? Tom had an idea. He jumped and climbed the tree. It was hard, but Tom was a good climber. He got Max's pants from the tree and gave them to Max. Max was so happy! They both laughed and played in the park all day. They learned that good friends always help each other.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom had a toy guitar. He liked to play it every day. It was a fun thing to do. One day, Tom saw a big guitar in a store. It was very expensive. Tom wanted it, but he did not have enough money. Tom's mom told him to save his money. So, Tom saved his money for a long time. One day, he had enough money to buy the big guitar. He was very happy. Tom went to the store with his mom to buy the guitar. But when they got there, Tom did not recognize the store. It looked different. Inside, Tom found out the store had changed. They did not sell guitars anymore. They only sold food. Tom was sad, but he used his money to buy ice cream for him and his mom. They went home and played with his toy guitar. Tom realized that his toy guitar was special too.

Once upon a time, there was a normal boy named Tom. Tom had a toy motorcycle. He loved to play with it all day. One sunny day, Tom took his motorcycle outside to play. He made the motorcycle speed on the ground. It went very fast. Tom was so happy to see it go. Then, Tom's friend, Tim, came to play with him. Tim had a toy car. They raced their toys together. The motorcycle and the car went speed, speed, speed. Tom and Tim laughed and played until it was time to go home. They had a fun day with their toys.
