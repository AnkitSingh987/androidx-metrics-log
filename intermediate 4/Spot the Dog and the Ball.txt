Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Ball

One day, a little dog named Spot was playing in the park. He liked to run and grab his ball. Spot was very fit and fast. He played with his ball all day long. Spot saw a big cat and wanted to play with it. The cat did not want to play and got mad. They started to fight. Spot and the cat made loud noises. Then, something unexpected happened. A nice bird flew down and dropped a bandage on the ground. Spot and the cat stopped fighting. They looked at the bandage and then at each other. They decided to be friends and play with the ball together. The bird helped them learn to share and have fun.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly lady named Grandma Sue. She lived in a small house with her cat, Fluffy. Yesterday, she went to the market to buy some fruits. At the market, she met a little boy named Tim. Tim said, "Hi, Grandma Sue! Can you help me fill my basket with fruits?" Grandma Sue smiled and said, "Of course, Tim!" Together, they filled Tim's basket with apples, bananas, and oranges. Then, they went back to Grandma Sue's house and shared the fruits with Fluffy. They all had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy dog. Every morning he would wake up late. One day, his owner said to him, "Hey, why don't you get up early a change? It's more fun that way!" But the lazy dog just went back to sleep again. He didn't want to change his ways. The next morning the lazy dog woke up late again. But this time, all the other dogs were already playing and running around, while the lazy dog was still sleeping. He wished he had listened to his owner, so that he could be out playing with the other dogs. The moral of the story is to listen to the wise words of others. If you don't, you might miss out on things that could be fun and exciting.

Once there was a little boy named Tommy. Tommy was 3 years old and he had an idea. He wanted to go on a special trip! Tommy went to the store to get a passport. It was a very important piece of paper that showed he could go on his special trip. He wanted to be honest when he filled out his passport. He asked his Mom to help him fill out the form. He was careful when he touched the paper. Mom helped Tommy be honest and soon he had his passport. Tommy was so excited! Tommy's Mom drove him to the airport with his passport. It was an exciting adventure! On the airplane Tommy could look at his passport with pride. His honest work paid off and soon he was on his special trip!

Once upon a time there was a person named Ben. Ben was brave and curious. He liked to explore the world around him. One day Ben met a dangerous person. When he saw the person he was scared but he did not back away or run away. "Who are you?" asked Ben. The person laughed. "I am from the far away kingdom," he said. "I am here to explore the world." Ben thought for a moment and then he asked, "Are you dangerous?" The person smiled and said, "Not really. I am nice and kind. But I can be dangerous if I want to be." Ben thanked the person and then he went on his way. From that day forward he knew to be a bit more careful when he met new people.

Once upon a time, there was a giant. The giant was very silly. He liked to play with his friends, the little birds. One day, the giant saw a big, red ball. He wanted to play with the ball. The giant kicked the ball and it went zoom! The ball went so far that it went over the hills. The little birds saw the ball and they went zoom too. They wanted to bring the ball back to the giant. The little birds found the ball and pushed it back to the giant. The giant was so happy to see his friends and the ball. They all played together and had a fun day. The giant learned that even though he was big, he could still have fun with his little friends.
