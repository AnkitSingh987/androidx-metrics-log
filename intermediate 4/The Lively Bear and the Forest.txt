Category: Intermediate
Title: The Lively Bear and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a lively bear. He lived in a big forest with many trees. The bear loved to play with his friends, the birds and the rabbits. One day, the bear wanted to do something new. He wanted to climb the tallest tree in the forest. He tried and tried, but he could not succeed. The bear felt sad and asked his friends for help. His friends, the birds and the rabbits, came to help him. They cheered for the bear and showed him the best way to climb. With their help, the bear tried again. This time, he did it! He climbed the tallest tree and succeeded. The bear was so happy, and so were his friends. They all played together and had a great day.

Lily and Sam liked to build with blocks. They had many blocks of different colors and shapes. They could make houses, towers, bridges, and anything they wanted. They had fun playing with their blocks every day. One day, they decided to build a big castle with their blocks. They worked hard to make the walls, the doors, the windows, and the towers. They used blue blocks for the water, green blocks for the grass, and yellow blocks for the sun. They were very proud of their castle. But then, their cat, Max, came into the room. Max was a playful cat. He liked to chase things and knock them over. He saw the castle and thought it was a toy. He jumped on the castle and made it fall down. The blocks went everywhere. Lily and Sam were sad and angry. They shouted at Max and told him to go away. Max felt sorry. He did not mean to ruin their castle. He wanted to make them happy. He walked up to them and rubbed his head on their legs. He looked at them with his big eyes and gave them a wink. He tried to tell them he was sorry and he loved them. Lily and Sam saw Max's wink. They knew he was not a bad cat. He was just a playful cat. They forgave him and hugged him. They decided to build another castle, but this time, they made a space for Max to join them. They used red blocks for a bed, brown blocks for a bowl, and white blocks for a mouse. They invited Max to sleep in the bed, eat from the bowl, and play with the mouse. Max was happy and so were Lily and Sam. They all played together with their blocks and had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big piano in a small room. A little girl named Lucy loved to play it every day. She liked to make happy songs and sing along. Lucy's cat, Mittens, would sit and listen. One day, Lucy found a tasty treat on the piano. She did not know where it came from. She wanted to solve the mystery. Lucy looked around and saw Mittens with a big smile. Mittens had put the treat there as a surprise! But then, something unexpected happened. The piano started to play by itself! Lucy and Mittens were scared. They hid behind a chair and watched. The piano played a happy song, just like the ones Lucy played. Then, they saw it was not the piano playing by itself. It was Lucy's mom playing a trick on them! They all laughed and enjoyed the tasty treat together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his ball. One day, he saw a cheap toy in a shop. It was a ball that could make a score when you threw it into a hoop. Tim wanted to play with it so much! Tim got the cheap toy and played with it all day. He threw the ball into the hoop and made many scores. He was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The ball changed into a big, colorful bird! Tim was very surprised. The bird said, "Thank you, Tim! I was a bird stuck in that cheap toy. You helped me by making so many scores!" Tim was happy that he helped the bird. They became best friends and played together every day. And Tim still loved to make scores with his new friend.
