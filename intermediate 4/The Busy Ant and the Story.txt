Category: Intermediate
Title: The Busy Ant and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a busy ant. The ant loved to eat toast. One day, while the ant was eating toast, a big, mean witch saw the ant. The witch did not like the ant. She wanted to make the ant very small. The witch waved her wand and said, "Shrink!" The ant became very, very small. The ant was now so tiny that it could not eat the toast anymore. The ant was sad because it loved to eat toast so much. The ant tried to find help, but it was too small for anyone to see. In the end, the ant was so tiny that it got lost and could not find its way home. The moral of the story is: even if you are busy, always be kind to others and watch out for danger.

One day, a lazy cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big tree with a hook on it. Tom thought it was a fun toy to play with. He jumped up and tried to catch the hook with his paw. Tom's friend, a dog named Max, saw him playing with the hook. Max said, "Tom, be careful! That hook can hurt you!" But Tom didn't listen. He was having too much fun. He blinked his eyes and tried to catch the hook again. This time, Tom caught the hook with his paw. But, oh no! The hook was stuck in his paw and it hurt a lot. Tom cried and cried. Max tried to help, but he didn't know what to do. In the end, Tom had to go to the doctor to get the hook out. He learned to be more careful and listen to his friends when they tell him something is not safe.

The sun shone brightly on the road as Jack and his dad drove down the street. Jack was happy as he watched his dad as he hummed a cheerful tune. They soon arrived to a small store and Jack's dad parked the car. When they went inside, Jack's eyes lit up. He saw shelves of bright and shiny toys. His dad told him he could choose one toy and with delight he rushed to the back of the store. He ran his hand through the soft plushies and saw a cart. Jack was so excited he leaped and shouted gleefully, “I want this!” His dad, however, shook his head and told him, “no, Jack. This is too expensive. I’m sorry. We will have to look for something else.” Jack felt a bit upset and refused to look for anything else. His dad sighed and said, “Alright. Let’s try it out for a bit and then we will decide.” Jack happily grabbed the handle of the cart and his dad pushed it around the store. As Jack peered inside the cart, he noticed it had a bitter smell. Then, all of a sudden, the cart started to shake a little and rattle. Jack’s dad looked down and saw a loose wheel coming out of the cart. He quickly realized that if they bought the cart, it would be hard to move it

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Sarah. She was only 3 years old. One day, she was left alone in the house with her pet rabbit, Fluffy. Suddenly, Sarah saw the stove near her. She was very curious and wanted to explore it. She wanted to see what was inside. So, Sarah walked to the stove and tried to open the door, but it was hard. She tried again and again, until the door opened. Sarah: "Look Fluffy, a stove! I want to see what is inside," Fluffy: "Bunny, no! Don't do it!" But Sarah didn't listen to her pet and she closed the door to the stove. Suddenly, there was a big noise and Sarah started to cry. Sarah: "It's too hard and loud!" She had not understood that there was something dangerous inside the stove. The stove was very hot and Sarah had gotten a very bad burn. The moral of this story is that children should not play around dangerous things, no matter how curious they may be. Always keep children away from danger and teach them to be careful.
