Category: Intermediate
Title: Bethany and the Park

Bethany was walking through the park one sunny day. She saw another girl, who was about her age, and asked her, "Will you come and dance with me?". The other girl replied, "Yes, I'd love to!". So the two girls began to dance together. Bethany twirled round and round, while the other girl jumped and jumped. They laughed and giggled in delight as they spun and skipped. As they finished, they hugged each other and shouted, "We did it! That was a successful dance!". Bethany said, "Let's do it again!". So they did. The girls agreed to meet every day in the park to practice their dances. After a few weeks, they had become very successful dancers and everybody admired their skill and style. The two girls beamed with pride as they continued to dance together. Nothing would stop them from enjoying their wonderfully successful friendship.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had a toy that she loved. It was a soft, cute dog. But Lily's friend, Max, did not have a toy. Max was envious of Lily's toy dog. One day, Lily and Max were playing with the toy dog together. Max wanted to make the toy dog look cool. He took scissors and cut the toy dog's hair. But, oh no! The toy dog's hair looked bad. Lily was full of worry. Then, something unexpected happened. Lily's mom came in and saw the toy dog's hair. She smiled and said, "Don't worry, kids. I can fix it." Lily's mom took the toy dog and sewed a new, pretty hat on its head. Now, the toy dog looked even better than before! Max was happy, and Lily was not worried anymore. They played with the toy dog and had lots of fun.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a box of chalk. Sue was very creative. She loved to draw and make things. She thought, "I can use this chalk to make a big surprise for my friends!" So, she took the chalk and went outside. Sue started to draw on the ground. She drew a big sun, a happy face, and a pretty flower. Then, she drew a long line for her friends to follow. Sue smiled and said, "My friends will love this surprise!" Later, her friends came to play. They saw the drawings on the ground and followed the line. They found Sue waiting for them. "Surprise!" Sue said. Her friends laughed and clapped. They all took turns drawing with the chalk. They had so much fun being creative together.

Sara likes to look at the stars at night. She has a book with pictures of stars and planets. She likes to open the book and learn their names. She also has a star lamp that makes star shapes on her wall. One night, Sara hears a knock on her window. She gets up and sees a little star outside. The star is shiny and yellow. It smiles at her and says, "Hello, Sara. Can I come in?" Sara is surprised and happy. She opens the window and lets the star in. The star floats around her room and looks at her things. It likes her book and her lamp. It says, "You have a very tidy room, Sara. You must love stars a lot." Sara nods and says, "Yes, I do. Stars are beautiful and bright. They make me feel happy. Do you live in the sky with the other stars?" The star nods and says, "Yes, I do. But sometimes I like to visit the earth and make new friends. You are my new friend, Sara. Do you want to come with me and see the sky?" Sara thinks for a moment. She wants to see the sky with the star, but she also knows that it is late and she has to sleep. She says, "Thank you, star. But I have to stay here. Maybe you can come again another night." The star understands and says, "Okay, Sara. I will come again another night. But before I go, I have a gift for you." The star touches Sara's hand and gives her a little star necklace. It sparkles on her neck. The star says, "This is for you, Sara. So you can always remember me and our friendship." Sara hugs the star and says, "Thank you, star. You are very kind and sweet. I will always remember you and our friendship." The star hugs Sara back and says, "Good night, Sara. Sleep well and dream of stars." The star flies out of the window and waves goodbye. Sara waves back and closes the window. She puts on her star lamp and looks at her star necklace. She feels happy and warm. She says, "Good night, star. I love you." She closes her eyes and falls asleep.
