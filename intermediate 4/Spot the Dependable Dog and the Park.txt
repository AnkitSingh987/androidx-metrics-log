Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dependable Dog and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a dependable dog named Spot. Spot loved to play in the park with his best friend, a little girl named Sue. Sue and Spot were always happy when they were together. One day, Sue and Spot found a big, shiny mug in the park. They decided to take it home and show it to Sue's mom. But on the way home, Spot saw a big, muddy puddle. Spot loved to jump in puddles, but he didn't want to spoil the shiny mug. So, Spot put the mug down very carefully and jumped in the puddle. He played and splashed until he was all muddy. Then, he picked up the mug with his mouth and carried it home to Sue. Sue's mom was so happy to see the shiny mug and gave Spot a big hug. And that is how Spot learned to be dependable and take care of things for his friend Sue.

One day, a little girl named Sue got a small hurt on her arm. She was sad and cried a little. Her mom said, "We need to go see a doctor for your arm." Sue was scared, but her mom told her the doctor would help her feel better. At the doctor's office, the doctor looked at Sue's arm. He said, "We need to take an x-ray to see the inside of your arm." Sue didn't know what an x-ray was, but she was brave. The doctor took a picture of her arm with a big machine. The x-ray showed that Sue's arm was delicate and needed a cast. The doctor put a cast on Sue's arm and told her to be careful. Sue's mom was so proud of her for being brave. They went home and Sue's mom sent a picture of Sue's x-ray to Sue's dad. Sue felt better and knew that she could be brave even when she was scared.

Once there were two brothers named Jack and Will. Jack was always complaining that Will wasn't creative. One day, Jack turned to Will and said, "You are never creative! All you do is play video games!" Will frowned and replied, "I am creative, I just like video games too." Jack shook his head, "No you're not!" he complained. Will smiled and said, "Okay, watch this! I will build a castle out of lego blocks!" Will put his hands to work and sure enough, he was very creative. He built the most amazing castle that Jack had ever seen. Jack looked in awe and said, "Wow! That is really cool Will!" Will smiled and replied, "See, I told you I was creative!"

Once upon a time, there was a gentle cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play with her ball and climb on her seat. One day, while Fluffy was playing, she saw a little bird outside her window. Fluffy went outside to meet the bird. The bird was sitting on a seat in the garden. Fluffy said, "Hello, bird! Can I sit on the seat too?" The bird replied, "Yes, Fluffy! Come sit with me." As they sat on the seat, Fluffy started to demand, "Bird, can you sing a song for me?" The bird agreed and sang a beautiful song. Fluffy and the bird became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy was a good girl who liked to play and have fun. One day, Amy found a big book about a place called "university." She didn't know what it was, but it looked fun. Amy wanted to go to the university, but she was too small. So, she asked her big sister to take her. Her sister said, "No, Amy. You are too little." Amy felt bad and wanted to cry. She didn't like being too little. Amy didn't want to feel bad anymore. So, she decided to be a big girl and try to go to the university by herself. But she got lost and was scared. Then, her big sister found her and took her home. Amy said, "I'm sorry. I regret going alone." They hugged, and Amy was happy to be home.
