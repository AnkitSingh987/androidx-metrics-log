Category: Intermediate
Title: Jack the Boy and the Dream

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Jack. Jack was three years old and he was an honest boy. He liked to nap every day in the afternoon. It was his favorite thing to do. One day, Jack went to his room and took a nap. While he napped, he had a dream. In his dream, he was able to walk around inside of his own brain. Inside his brain, he saw a lot of strange things. Jack woke up from his nap and told his mom about his dream. She laughed and said, "That's a very interesting dream, Jack! You have a very creative brain!" She gave him a hug and told him that being honest is important. Jack smiled and said, "I will always be honest." He knew that his brain was full of some very unique ideas.

Once upon a time, there was a great big rock. This rock had a secret. Inside the rock, there was a special mineral that could make people happy. The rock liked to hide its mineral, so no one could find it. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the rock. He saw the rock and said, "Wow, you are a great big rock! I want to play with you." The rock liked Tim and wanted to share its secret. So, the rock said, "Tim, I have a special mineral inside me. If you find it, it will make you happy." Tim was excited and started to look for the mineral. He looked and looked, but could not find it. The rock saw Tim was sad and said, "Don't be sad, Tim. I will help you find the mineral." Together, they looked and looked until they found the special mineral. When Tim found the mineral, he was so happy. He said, "Thank you, great big rock! You made me happy!" The rock was happy too because it made a new friend. From that day on, Tim and the rock played together every day, and they were always happy.

Lily loved to play in the garden. She liked to smell the flowers, chase the butterflies and dig in the dirt. She had a small shovel and a bucket that she used to make mud pies. She was very happy in the garden. One day, Lily saw something shiny in the dirt. She used her shovel to dig it out. It was a ring with a big stone that sparkled in the sun. Lily thought it was very pretty. She put it on her finger and felt like a princess. She wanted to show it to her mom. But when she ran to the house, she saw her mom talking to a man. The man looked angry and sad. He was holding a picture of a woman. The woman was wearing the same ring as Lily. Lily heard the man say, "This ring was very expensive. It was a gift from my wife before she died. She loved this garden. I don't know how it got lost. Please help me find it." Lily felt scared and guilty. She realized that the ring was not hers. It belonged to the man and his wife. She had taken something that was very important to them. She did not want to make them sad. She wanted to make them happy. She took off the ring and ran to the man. She said, "I'm sorry, I'm sorry. I found this ring in the dirt. I did not know it was yours. Here, take it back. It is very pretty. I hope it makes you happy." The man looked at Lily with surprise and gratitude. He took the ring and hugged her. He said, "Thank you, thank you. You are very kind and honest. You have made me very happy. You have given me back a part of my wife. She would have liked you. You are a good girl." Lily smiled and felt proud. She had done the right thing. She had discovered something new in the garden, but she had also discovered something in her heart. She had learned to share and to care. She had made a new friend. She was very happy in the garden.
