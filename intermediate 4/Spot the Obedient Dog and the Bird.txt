Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Obedient Dog and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was an obedient dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and have fun. One day, his friend, a little bird, came to visit. They wanted to play together in the yard. Spot and the bird saw a big paper with a picture of peace on it. The bird had an idea. "Let's cut the paper and make something fun!" So, they found some safe scissors and started to cut the paper. They cut out the peace sign and hung it on the tree. Spot and the bird were very happy. They played under the tree with their new peace sign all day long. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a wild dog named Spot. Spot liked to run and play with his friends. One day, they decided to have a big party in the park. They made a list of things to include in the party, like food, games, and music. During the party, Spot saw a big pool of water. He thought it was a leak from the water hose. Spot wanted to help fix the leak, so he asked his friends to come with him. They all ran to the pool of water, ready to fix the problem. But when they got there, they saw that it wasn't a leak at all! It was a surprise water slide that their friends had made for the party. The wild dog and his friends were so happy. They played on the water slide all day long, and it was the best party ever.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who was going to the beach. She had been looking forward to playing in the sand and going swimming all day! When she got to the beach, she quickly ran into the water and started splashing around. The water was so cold and wet, she just couldn't help smiling and having fun. But then, all of a sudden, the little girl's foot got stuck in the mud and she couldn't move. No matter how hard she tried, her foot wouldn't come loose! Her muscles felt so helpless and all she could do was soak in the cold water. Just then, someone came to help the little girl. It was her mom who had been watching nearby. With gentle hands, her mom pulled her out of the mud and hugged her tight. The little girl was relieved and so very happy that her mom had come to help.

One sunny day, a girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. The sky was bright and blue. Lily felt very happy. She wanted to play with her ball and take a photo with her mom. "Mom, can we take a photo together?" Lily asked. Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, of course!" They stood near a big tree and smiled. Click! The camera took a nice photo of them. After that, Lily played with her ball. She threw it high in the air and caught it. She felt very proud of herself. Her mom clapped her hands and said, "Good job, Lily!" When they went home, Lily looked at the photo with her mom. They both felt very happy. They put the photo on the fridge so they could see it every day. And every time they looked at it, they remembered the bright, sunny day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a big, powerful dog named Max. He lived near a place called a university. The university was where big people went to learn and play. One day, Max saw a display at the university. The display had many colors and shapes. Max liked the display because it was fun to look at. He wanted to show his friends, so he barked and wagged his tail. Max's friends came to see the display. They all liked it too. They played near the university and looked at the display all day. The powerful dog and his friends were happy, and they had a great day together.

Lily and Sam like to look at the stars. They have a big book with pictures of space. They see the moon, the sun, and many other things. They wonder what is out there. One night, they ask their dad if they can go outside and look at the sky. He says yes, but they have to be honest and careful. He gives them a flashlight and a blanket. They go to the backyard and spread the blanket on the grass. They turn off the flashlight and look up. They see many bright dots and some big shapes. They try to find the ones they know from the book. They see the Big Dipper, the Little Dipper, and the North Star. They see a shooting star and make a wish. They see a round thing that looks like a smile. They think it is the moon, but it is not. It is a planet. They learn a new word: Venus. They are happy and amazed. They talk and giggle. They ask their dad many questions. He comes and joins them. He tells them more stories about space. He says he is proud of them for being honest and curious. He says he loves them. They hug him and say they love him too. They stay outside until they are sleepy. Then they go inside and dream of space.
