Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Box

One day, a little girl named Sue was in her room. She saw her mom's big red dress in the closet. Sue liked the dress a lot. She wanted to wear it, but it was too big for her. Sue had an idea. She thought she could lend the dress to her friend, Jane, who was bigger than her. Sue went to Jane's house with the red dress. She asked Jane if she wanted to try it on. Jane said yes and put on the dress. It was still a little big for Jane, but they had fun playing dress-up. They laughed and danced around the room. While they were playing, Sue found a hard box in Jane's room. She opened it and found a beautiful small dress inside. Jane said, "I never wear this dress, do you want to try it on?" Sue was so happy and tried on the small dress. It fit her perfectly. They played and danced some more, wearing the dresses they had lent each other. Sue learned that sharing with friends is fun and can make everyone happy.

One sunny day, a pale girl named Lily went to school. It was a special day called Wednesday. On Wednesday, her teacher, Mr. Fox, would teach them something new and fun. In the morning, Mr. Fox said, "Today, I will teach you all how to make a paper boat." The kids were happy and clapped their hands. They all sat down and listened to Mr. Fox. Lily tried to make a paper boat. She asked her friend, Sam, "Can you help me?" Sam said, "Yes, let's make it together!" They made a boat and put it in a small pool of water. The boat floated, and the kids laughed and played. They all had a great Wednesday.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play at the park. His favorite thing to do was to slide down the big blue slide. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. When they got to the park, Tim saw his friend, Sue. Sue was a proud little girl who liked to play with Tim. They both ran to the big blue slide and climbed up the stairs. "Ready, Sue?" Tim asked. "Yes!" Sue said with a smile. They both slid down the slide, laughing and having fun. They played on the slide all day, and Tim felt very happy.

Once upon a time, there was an adorable youth named Sarah. She was so sweet and she loved to sing and dance and play. Every day, Sarah would take a walk in a big park. One day, Sarah noticed a stranger in the park. He looked a bit strange but Sarah wasn't scared. She decided to walk closer to him and asked: "Hello, who are you?". The stranger answered: "My name is Jim. I'm here to ask you some questions, would you like to answer them?". Sarah was excited and said yes. However, the questions were very strange and Sarah couldn't answer them. After struggling to answer all the questions, the stranger got frustrated. He shouted angrily and ran away. The night came and Sarah felt sad. She wished she hadn't talked to the stranger and had just gone home. She now felt scared every time she walked in the park. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a unique parrot named Polly. Polly loved to zip around in the sky and play with her friends. One day, while Polly was zipping high up, she saw a big tree with lots of other birds. Polly flew down to the tree and said, "Hi! I'm Polly, the unique parrot. Can I play with you?" The other birds looked at her and said, "You're different. We don't want to play with you." Polly was sad and flew away. As she zipped through the sky, she saw a big rainbow. Suddenly, something unexpected happened! Polly started to change colors just like the rainbow. Now, she was even more unique. Polly went back to the tree and showed the other birds her new colors. They were amazed and said, "Wow! You are so beautiful and unique, Polly. We want to play with you now." From that day on, Polly and the other birds played together every day, and they all learned that being unique is a good thing.
