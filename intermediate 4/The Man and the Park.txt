Category: Intermediate
Title: The Man and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a man. The man wanted to play with the kids in the park. The kids did not permit the man to play with them. The man felt sad and ashamed. The man went to the park again the next day. He tried to play with the kids again. The kids still did not permit the man to play with them. The man felt even more sad and ashamed. The man thought of a plan. He took a big toy to the park. The kids saw the toy and wanted to play with it. They let the man play with them. But the man did not share the toy. The kids got mad and went away. The man was left all alone. The bad ending made the man very sad. The moral of the story is to always be kind and share with others.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing outside. They saw a wet letter on the ground. The cat wanted to take the letter to their house. The dog wanted to leave it outside. The cat and dog started to disagree. The cat said, "The letter is wet, we need to take it inside to dry." The dog said, "No, it is wet, it will make our house messy." They could not agree on what to do with the letter. Then, a big bird came and picked up the wet letter. The bird took the letter to a tree and put it in its nest to dry. The cat and dog stopped disagreeing and went back to playing together. They were happy the bird took care of the wet letter.

Once upon a time, there was an intelligent dog named Max. Max loved to sit on a bench in the park with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. They would sit on the bench and watch the birds fly in the sky. One day, while sitting on the bench, a big, mean cat came to the park. The cat wanted to take the bench from Max and Lily. Max said, "No, cat! This is our bench. Go away!" Max wanted to protect Lily and the bench from the mean cat. But the cat did not listen. The cat jumped on the bench and pushed Max and Lily off. Max tried his best to protect the bench, but the cat was too strong. Max and Lily were sad that they could not sit on their favorite bench anymore. The mean cat had won.

Once upon a time there was a brave little rabbit who lived in a big forest. Every day she would explore, looking for adventures. One day, she decided to go on a big zoom! She got ready by hopping up and down to prepare for the journey. She tidied up her fur and hopped off, ready for her zoom. The rabbit went far and wide, hopping and jumping through the forest. She was so happy to explore unknown places. After a long day of hopping, she came back home. She was so brave and proud of all the discoveries she had made. The little rabbit prepared herself for her next journey the next day. She was so excited to keep zooming and discovering her big forest.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big, colorful square. Many kids liked to play there. One sunny day, two friends, Tom and Lily, went to the square to play. Tom had a red car, and Lily had a blue car. They wanted to race their cars. Tom said, "Let's race from this side of the square to the other side." Lily agreed and said, "Okay, let's see who is faster!" They put their cars on the ground and started to race. The cars went very fast. Tom's red car was in the front, but Lily's blue car was close behind. The kids and their friends cheered as the cars raced across the colorful square. In the end, Tom's red car won the race. Tom was very happy and said, "I won! My car is the fastest!" Lily smiled and said, "Good job, Tom! Let's race again tomorrow." From that day on, Tom and Lily raced their cars in the colorful square every day. They had lots of fun, and all the kids in the town loved to watch their races.
