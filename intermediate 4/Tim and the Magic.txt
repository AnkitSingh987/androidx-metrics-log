Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Magic

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to gather rocks in his big red bag. He would look for amazing rocks all day long. Tim's best friend was a fast car named Zoom. Zoom could go very fast and they would play together every day. One sunny day, Tim and Zoom went to the park to gather more amazing rocks. They found big rocks, small rocks, and shiny rocks. They were having a lot of fun. Suddenly, they saw a big rock that was different from the others. It was very big and very shiny. Tim and Zoom decided to take the big shiny rock home. But when they touched the rock, something unexpected happened. The rock started to talk! The rock was not a rock, but a magic rock. The magic rock told Tim and Zoom that it could make their wishes come true. Tim and Zoom were very happy and made lots of fun wishes together. And they all lived happily ever after.

Marianne and her mommy went to the park. Marianne saw a big symbol in the middle of the park. It was incredible! "Look Mommy, what is that?" Marianne asked. "It's a symbol of hope," said her Mommy. Marianne didn't understand, so her Mommy explained. "It's a sign that things will get better. We must urge everyone to keep believing." Marianne was inspired by the symbol. She wanted to urge everyone to keep believing, too. She ran up to a group of children playing in the park and shouted with excitement, "Look at the symbol! Believe even when it's hard!" The other children looked at the symbol and cheered. Everyone was encouraged to keep believing. Marianne and her mommy smiled. It was an incredible moment.

Once upon a time, in a calm forest, there lived a little bug named Buzzy. Buzzy loved to play with his friend, Leafy the leaf. They would dance and sing all day long. One sunny day, Buzzy and Leafy saw a big spray of water coming from a flower. "Wow!" said Buzzy. "Look at that! Let's play in the water!" Leafy nodded and smiled. They ran towards the water and started to play. As they played, a big frog named Hoppy came by. "Hi, Buzzy and Leafy! Can I play too?" asked Hoppy. Buzzy and Leafy said, "Yes! Come and play with us!" So, Hoppy jumped into the water and they all played together. They splashed and sprayed water on each other, laughing and having fun. After a while, they were all tired but happy. "That was so much fun!" said Buzzy. "Let's play together again soon!" From that day on, Buzzy, Leafy, and Hoppy became the best of friends. They played together in the calm forest every day, always finding new ways to have fun. And they lived happily ever after.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big red ball on the grass. They run to the ball and want to play with it. "Look, a red ball!" Lily says. "Can we play with it?" "Yes, let's play!" Tom says. They kick the ball and throw the ball and catch the ball. They have fun. But then, a big dog comes to the park. The dog sees the red ball and thinks it is a toy. The dog runs to the ball and grabs it with his mouth. He starts to chew the ball. "Hey, that's our ball!" Tom says. He tries to get the ball from the dog, but the dog growls and runs away. "Stop, dog, stop!" Lily says. She chases the dog, but the dog is too fast. He runs out of the park with the ball. Lily and Tom are sad. They lost their red ball. They sit on the grass and cry. A nice lady sees them and comes to them. She has a basket with cookies. "What's wrong, children?" she asks. "A dog took our red ball and chewed it!" Lily says. "That's too bad!" the lady says. "But don't cry. I have something for you. Do you like cookies?" "Yes, we like cookies!" Tom says. The lady gives them each a cookie. They are sweet and crunchy. Lily and Tom smile and say thank you. They eat their cookies and feel better. They decide to play something else. They look for flowers and bugs and rocks. They forget about the red ball and the dog. They are happy again.
