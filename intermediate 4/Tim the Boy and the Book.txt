Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Book

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to learn new things. One day, he found a small, harmless bug in his yard. The bug had a pretty stamp on its back. Tim wanted to know more about the bug. He asked his mom about the bug with the stamp. She told him it was a ladybug. Ladybugs are good bugs and help the plants. Tim wanted to learn more about ladybugs and how they help. Tim and his mom went to the library to find a book about ladybugs. They read the book together and learned that ladybugs eat other bugs that hurt plants. Tim was happy to know that the harmless bug with the stamp was a helper.

Lily liked to store her toys in a big box. She had many toys, but her favorite was a bow. It was pink and shiny and had a ribbon. She liked to pretend she was a princess with a bow. One day, Lily saw a huge bow in the window of a store. It was bigger than her and had many colors. She wanted to have it, but it was too expensive. She asked her mom if she could buy it, but her mom said no. Lily was sad. She went home and played with her own bow. She tried to make it look like the huge bow. She used crayons and stickers and glitter. But it was not the same. She wished she had the huge bow. The next day, Lily's mom surprised her. She had a gift for her. It was the huge bow from the store! Lily was so happy. She hugged her mom and thanked her. She put the huge bow on her head and felt like a princess. She stored her old bow in the box and played with her new bow all day. She loved her huge bow.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to imagine big adventures. One day, she saw a tiny bug. The bug was only an inch long. Lily was very enthusiastic about meeting this new friend. "Hi, little bug!" Lily said. "Do you want to play with me?" The tiny bug nodded its head. So, Lily and the bug went on an adventure. They played in the grass and climbed up trees. They had so much fun together. At the end of the day, Lily and the tiny bug were tired. They said goodbye to each other. Lily went home and told her mom about her new friend. She couldn't wait to imagine more adventures with the tiny bug.

Once upon a time there was a painter called Bob who was driving his car. He was driving very fast and the roads were tight. Suddenly Bob hit a rock in the road and his car stopped. Bob was in pain and he started to cry. He tried to get his car to drive again but nothing seemed to work. He looked around and he spotted a kind old man walking by the side of the road. Bob asked the old man for help. The old man went up to Bob's car and tapped it with a hammer. He said, "You just need to tighten the bolts and then it'll work again." He tightened each of the bolts and suddenly Bob's car started to drive again. Bob was so thankful to the old man and he drove off into the sunset.

Once upon a time, there was a big cloud in the sky. The cloud was a little bit weird. It was not like the other clouds. It was pink and had a big smile on its face. The cloud was always happy and loved to play with the wind. One day, the wind said, "Let's play a game. I will try to separate you into two clouds, and you have to try to stay together." The cloud agreed, and they started to play. The wind blew very hard, but the cloud stayed together. They were having so much fun. Then, something unexpected happened. The cloud started to change colors. It turned from pink to blue, then green, and finally to a bright rainbow. The wind and the cloud were both very surprised. The cloud said, "Wow, that was fun! I didn't know I could change colors like that!" The wind replied, "You are a very special cloud, and we can play together every day." From that day on, the weird cloud and the wind became best friends, and they played together all the time.
