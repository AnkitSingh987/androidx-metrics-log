Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a fun toy car with a handle. Lily liked to play with her toy car all day. One day, she went outside to play with her car in the busy park. At the park, Lily met a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy truck. They played together, pushing their toys on the ground. They laughed and had a lot of fun. But then, Lily's toy car got stuck in the mud. She tried to pull the handle, but it would not move. Lily yelled, "Help, Tim! My car is stuck!" Tim came over and helped her. He grabbed the handle and pulled hard. The toy car came out of the mud. Lily said, "Thank you, Tim!" They played together until the sun went down and it was time to go home.

One day, Sam went to the park with his mom. They took a big basket with them. In the basket, there was yummy food to eat. Sam was very happy. At the park, Sam saw a little girl. She looked sad. Sam thought, "I want to make her happy." So, he went to the girl and said, "Hi, I am Sam. Do you want to play with me?" The little girl smiled and said, "Yes, I would like that. My name is Lily." They played together all day. When it was time to eat, Sam's mom said, "We have some extra food in the basket. Would you like to eat with us, Lily?" Lily was happy and said, "Yes, thank you!" They all sat down and enjoyed the food from the basket. After they finished eating, Sam and Lily played some more. They had a great day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Tom. Tom had many things, but he was not happy. He wanted to receive a special gift to make him happy. One day, Tom went for a walk. He saw a big puddle on the ground. He jumped in the puddle and got all wet. Tom laughed and felt happy. From that day on, Tom knew that the best gifts were not things he could buy. The best gifts were the simple things, like jumping in a puddle. Tom was happy and shared his story with others.

Once upon a time there was a family of five. Mommy and Daddy, big brother and little sister, and their pet rabbit. One day in the spring, the family decided to plant some corn. Mommy, Daddy and the kids grabbed their gardening tools and set to work. Big brother worked hard, planting the seeds in neat little rows. Little sister carefully covered them up with dirt. Then they took turns watering the ground. Finally, the corn was growing! As it grew, it started to act like an independent little plant. It reached for the sun and swayed in the breeze. The family watched it each day and smiled. As time went by, the corn grew even bigger and bigger. Finally the day arrived when it was big and juicy enough to pick! The family gathered around the patch of corn and clapped their hands in excitement. Big brother spoke up. "Let's pick it now! I'm so hungry I could eat a whole ear of corn!" Momma laughed. "Let's eat some for dinner tonight. It looks like the perfect time to enjoy our corn!"

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a charming little cat. The cat liked to play with a ball. She was happy and loved her home. One day, the cat saw a chimney. The cat was curious and wanted to see what was inside. She climbed up the chimney and got very dirty. The cat's fur was all black from the soot. The cat wanted to become clean again. She found a bowl of water and washed herself. The cat was happy and charming once more. She played with her ball and never went in the chimney again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a small cat named Tilly. One day, Tilly got hurt and Lily wanted to help her cat heal. Lily felt sad and frustrated because she did not know how to make Tilly feel better. Lily's mom saw that she was sad, and she had an idea. She gave Lily a message from a wise old owl. The message said, "To help Tilly heal, give her love and care." Lily was happy to know how to help her cat. So, Lily took care of Tilly with lots of love. She gave her soft pets and warm hugs. Soon, Tilly started to feel better. The love and care from Lily helped Tilly heal. They played together and were happy again.
