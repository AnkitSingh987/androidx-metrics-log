Category: Intermediate
Title: The Boat and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a little boat that loved to sail on the water. One day, the boat met a regular pebble near the shore. The pebble said, "Hi, boat! Can you take me for a sail?" The boat was happy to have a new friend. "Of course! Hop on," the boat said. So the pebble jumped on the boat, and they sailed together on the big water. As they sailed, they saw many fun things. They saw fish, birds, and other boats. The pebble had a great time. When the sun was going down, the boat took the pebble back to the shore. The pebble said, "Thank you, boat! That was so much fun!" The boat smiled and said, "You're welcome, pebble. Let's sail again soon!" And they became the best of friends.

Grandma and John went on a walk in the woods. John held onto Grandma's hand and they saw all sorts of wonderful things. They saw birds, rabbits, and a squirrel. As they were walking, Grandma said, "Listen John. Do you hear that noise?" John perked up his ears and listened carefully. Then he said, "Yes, Grandma. I hear it." It was the sound of an acorn falling from a high tree. Grandma and John looked up and saw the tree had acorns everywhere! John was so excited. He said, "Can I pick one, Grandma?" Grandma said, "Of course!" John found one that looked just perfect. He held it up to the light and said, "It's so shiny!" Grandma smiled and said, "You have the best acorn I've ever seen." John smiled back and said, "I'm going to keep it forever." Grandma hugged him and said, "That's a great idea." John put the acorn in his pocket and they went off to explore the rest of the woods.

Once upon a time, there was a generous bird named Blue. Blue was very kind and liked to help others. One day, Blue found a soft feather on the ground. He decided to keep it for a special reason. Blue saw his friend, Bunny, who was very sad. Bunny lost her favorite toy. Blue wanted to comfort Bunny and make her feel better. He thought about the feather he found and got an idea. Blue gave the feather to Bunny. Bunny felt the soft feather and smiled. The feather made her feel better. Blue was happy that he could help his friend. From that day on, Bunny always kept the feather with her to remember Blue's kindness.

One day, a colorful dolphin named Dolly lived in the sea. She liked to play with her friends. They would swim and jump together. But one day, Dolly and her friend, Danny, had a quarrel. "Why did you take my toy?" asked Dolly. Danny said, "I did not take it. I found it in the sea." Dolly was sad and angry. She did not want to play with Danny anymore. Later, another friend found the toy. They showed it to Dolly and Danny. They both said sorry to each other. They were happy again. They played together and had fun with their friends in the sea.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play together. Tomorrow is Lily's birthday. Tom wants to make a present for Lily. He thinks hard. What does Lily like? Tom remembers that Lily likes to draw. He has some paper and crayons. He can make a picture for Lily. He takes the paper and crayons and goes to his room. He draws a big sun, a green tree, and a blue bird. He writes "Happy Birthday Lily" on the bottom. He is happy with his picture. He folds the paper and puts it in an envelope. He writes "To Lily" on the front. He runs to Lily's house. He knocks on the door. Lily's mom opens the door. She smiles and says, "Hello, Tom. What do you have there?" Tom says, "It's a present for Lily. Can I give it to her?" Lily's mom says, "Of course. Come in. Lily is in the living room." Tom follows Lily's mom. He sees Lily sitting on the couch. She is playing with her doll. She looks up and sees Tom. She smiles and says, "Hi, Tom. What are you doing here?" Tom says, "I came to say happy birthday. I made a present for you. Here you go." He hands the envelope to Lily. Lily opens it and sees the picture. She gasps and says, "Wow, Tom. This is so beautiful. You drew a sun, a tree, and a bird. And you wrote my name. Thank you so much. This is the best present ever." She hugs Tom and says, "You are a very good friend. I love your picture." Tom feels happy. He says, "You're welcome, Lily. I'm glad you like it. I love you too." They hug again and then play with the doll and the picture. They have a lot of fun. They are very happy.
