Category: Intermediate
Title: Jazz the Bug and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a little bug named Jazz. Jazz liked to crawl all day long. He crawled in the hot sun, and he crawled in the cool shade. He loved to explore the big world around him. One hot day, Jazz met a new friend named Bee. Bee was flying near some pretty flowers. Jazz said, "Hi, I'm Jazz! I like to crawl. What do you like to do?" Bee said, "Hi Jazz! I'm Bee. I like to fly and find sweet things to eat." Jazz and Bee played together all day long. They crawled and flew under the hot sun, and they had lots of fun. When the day was over, they said goodbye and went to their homes. Jazz knew he had made a great new friend, and he couldn't wait to play with Bee again.

Once upon a time, there was a potato. He was a very sad potato. Every day he used to sit at the edge of the garden, on his own. He never had any friends and would just watch the other vegetables playing together. One day, the other vegetables noticed the potato and asked why he was so sad. The potato yawned and said, "No one ever plays with me." The vegetables felt sorry for the potato and said, "Come and play with us". So the potato joined the vegetables and they all had lots of fun playing games. The potato laughed and said, "Now I'm happy!" From then on, the potato had lots of friends to play with and never felt sad anymore! The End.

Once upon a time, in a big green park, there was an intelligent bird named Bluey. Bluey loved to question everything around him. One day, he saw a small boy named Timmy eating something round and purple. Bluey had never seen this before. Bluey flew down to Timmy and asked, "What is that in your hand, Timmy?" Timmy looked at Bluey and said, "This is a grape! It is yummy and sweet. Do you want to try one?" Bluey nodded his head, excited to try something new. Timmy gave Bluey a grape, and he took a small bite. Bluey loved it! He felt happy and thanked Timmy for sharing. From that day on, Bluey and Timmy became best friends, and they always shared grapes when they met in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue and her baby brother named Tim. They lived in a small house with their mom and dad. Sue loved to play with Tim and help him learn new things. One sunny day, they were playing outside in the yard. Sue saw her dad hang some clean clothes on a line. She wanted to help her dad, so she asked, "Dad, can I help you hang the clothes?" Her dad smiled and said, "Of course, Sue! You can help me with the socks." Sue was happy to help her dad and make the clothes clean and dry. While Sue was helping her dad, Tim was playing with his toy car. He saw Sue and their dad hanging the clothes and wanted to help too. Tim tried to reach the clothes, but he was too small. Sue saw Tim trying to help and said, "Don't worry, Tim! You can help when you get bigger. For now, let's play together." Sue and Tim played with their toys in the sun, and they had a fun day with their family.

One day, a little boy named Mark went to the park with his mom. It was a hot day, so they put on their hats and took a big bottle of water. Mark liked to play on the swings and slide, and his mom liked to watch him have fun. At the park, Mark met a new friend. Her name was Lily. They played together on the swings, and then they decided to play a game of tag. Mark said, "Lily, I promise I will not run too fast, so you can catch me." Lily smiled and said, "I promise too, Mark." They played tag for a long time, and they were both very hot and tired. Mark's mom called them over and said, "You two look hot! Let's take a break and drink some water." Mark and Lily drank the cold water and felt much better. They promised to play together again next time at the park.
