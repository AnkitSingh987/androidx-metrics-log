Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Swings

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went to camp. At camp, Tim had a lot of fun. He liked to swing on the swings with his friends. One day, Tim met a smelly dog. The dog was very dirty and needed a bath. Tim said to the dog, "You are smelly. Let's find some water to make you clean." The dog wagged its tail and followed Tim. They walked and walked until they found a small pond. Tim helped the dog get into the pond. The dog splashed and played in the water. Soon, the dog was clean and not smelly anymore. Tim and the dog became best friends. They played together every day at camp, and they both loved to swing on the swings.

Once upon a time, there was a normal dog named Bobo. Bobo loved to sing. One day, he went to an opera with his mom. The opera was a big show where people sang songs on a stage. Bobo was very excited. When they got to the opera, Bobo and his mom found their seats. The show started and Bobo watched the people sing. He liked the songs a lot. Then, Bobo had an idea. He wanted to sing too. Bobo got out of his seat and went to the stage. He kneeled down and started to sing. His voice was loud and happy. Everyone in the opera clapped for Bobo. His mom was proud of him. From that day on, Bobo knew he loved to sing more than anything.

Once upon a time there was a princess. She liked to wear purple dresses. Every day she went out to explore the world and see new places. One day, she came across a small pond. It looked very quiet. She decided to go over and take a look. Suddenly, she heard a noise. She stopped and tried to guess what it was. Then, she spotted a little frog sitting by the edge of the pond. The princess said: "Hi little frog! What are you up to?" The frog looked up. "I was just looking for something to eat," he said. The princess smiled. "I have an idea. Why don't you come with me? I have a nice garden with lots of delicious things to eat." The frog hopped up and followed the princess. They had a great time together. The purple dresses of the princess made the frog hop with happiness. The End.

Once upon a time, in a big field, there was a lot of green grass. The grass was amazing and soft. Every day, a big truck came to load the grass. The people in the town loved the grass. One day, a little boy named Tim saw the truck loading the grass. He wanted to help. Tim tried to load the grass, but it was too heavy. He felt sad. Then, something unexpected happened. A big, friendly dog came to help Tim. The dog was strong and could load the grass on the truck. Tim was so happy! They worked together and loaded all the grass. The people in the town were amazed. Tim and the dog became best friends, and they helped load the grass every day.

One day, a little girl named Amy was playing outside. She was eager to find a surprise. Amy looked under a big tree and saw a small box. "What's in the box?" she asked herself. Amy opened the box and found a tiny jacket. She picked it up and said, "Wow, a jacket! But it's too small for me." Just then, a little bird flew down from the tree. The bird looked cold. "Is this your jacket?" Amy asked the bird. The bird nodded and said, "Yes, thank you for finding it!" Amy put the jacket on the bird, and the bird felt warm and happy. Together, they played and laughed in the sun. The bird was very thankful for Amy's help. From that day on, Amy and the bird became the best of friends. And Amy learned that sometimes, the best surprises can be found in the most unexpected places.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max and a small cat named Lily. They lived in a house with a nice family. Max and Lily liked to play, but sometimes they were quiet too. One day, Max had a big bone to chew. Lily was jealous because she wanted something to play with too. She looked at Max with sad eyes. Max saw Lily was not happy and wanted to help. Max decided to offer Lily his bone. He picked it up and took it to her. Lily was very happy and they both played with the bone together. Now, they were not jealous anymore and enjoyed their quiet playtime. And they were the best of friends.
