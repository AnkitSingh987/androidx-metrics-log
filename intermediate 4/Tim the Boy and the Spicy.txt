Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Spicy

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. One day, Tim went for a walk and saw a big wall. He wanted to see what was behind the wall, so he found a way to climb it. When he got to the top, he saw a big, spicy pepper. "Wow! That's a big pepper!" Tim said. He wanted to take the spicy pepper home, but he was scared. He thought the pepper might be too spicy for him. As he stood on the wall, a friendly bird flew by and saw Tim. "Hi, Tim! Why are you scared?" the bird asked. "I want to take this spicy pepper home, but I'm scared it's too spicy for me," Tim said. "Don't worry, Tim! If you think it's too spicy, just share it with your friends. They can help you eat it," the bird said. Tim smiled and thanked the bird. He took the spicy pepper home and shared it with his friends. They all had fun eating the spicy pepper together, and Tim wasn't scared anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim liked to play near the creek by his house. One day, while playing, he saw a little frog. The frog was sad. Tim asked the frog, "Why are you sad, little frog?" The frog said, "I lost my way to my home. I can't find my family." Tim wanted to help the frog. They looked and looked for the frog's home. Soon, they realized that the frog's home was on the other side of the creek. Tim helped the frog hop across the creek. When they got to the other side, the frog's family was there. They were very happy to see the little frog. The frog said, "Thank you, Tim, for helping me find my family." Tim smiled and went back to his house, feeling good that he helped his new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big library. It had many books for kids to read. In this library, there was a nice lady who would supply the books to the kids. She would give them books with fun stories and colorful pictures. One day, a new boy came to the library. He was not happy. He said the sun outside was too harsh. The nice lady wanted to help him. She gave him a book with a story about a magic umbrella that could make the sun less harsh. The boy read the book and smiled. He wished he had a magic umbrella too. Then, something unexpected happened. The boy found a real magic umbrella in the library! He opened it and the sun outside became less harsh. The boy was very happy and everyone in the library was surprised. The library became a magical place where kids could find not only fun stories but also real magic.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Bobo. Bobo loved to soar high in the sky. One day, while flying, Bobo saw a small boy named Timmy. Timmy was slow and sad because he lost his ball in a tree. Bobo flew down and asked, "Why are you sad, Timmy?" Timmy looked up and said, "My ball is in the tree, and I can't get it." Bobo wanted to help, so he had an idea. "Hold your thumb up high, and I will fly up and get your ball," said Bobo. Timmy held his thumb up, and Bobo soared up the tree. With a little push, the ball fell down. Timmy was so happy, and he thanked Bobo. From that day on, Bobo and Timmy became best friends, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Ann. She had a big jug that she loved very much. One day, Ann was upset because her jug was empty. She wanted to fill it with water but she was too small to reach the tap. Ann's friend, Tom, saw that she was upset. He wanted to help her. Tom was taller than Ann, so he could reach the tap. He filled the jug with water for her. Ann was very happy and smiled at Tom. Ann said, "Thank you, Tom!" She was not upset anymore. They played with the jug of water and had lots of fun. From that day on, Ann and Tom were the best of friends.
