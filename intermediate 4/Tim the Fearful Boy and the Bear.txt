Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Fearful Boy and the Bear

Once upon a time, there was a fearful boy named Tim. He had a toy bear that he loved very much. One day, Tim lost his bear and he was very sad. Tim's mom and dad knew that he was sad, so they had an idea. They told Tim that they would do an "operation" to find his bear. Tim did not know what an "operation" was, but he was happy that his mom and dad wanted to help him. They all looked for the bear together. They looked under the bed, in the closet, and behind the chair. Then, Tim's mom found the bear in the toy box! Tim was so happy that he hugged his bear and said, "Thank you, mom and dad, for the operation!" Tim's mom and dad smiled and hugged Tim back. They were all happy that the operation had worked, and they had found the bear.

Once there was a little girl who loved measuring things. One day she found a special card. She asked her Mom, "What is this card?" Her Mom replied, "This is a measuring card. It can help you to measure things." The little girl got very excited. She took out her measuring card and started measuring the things around the house. She measured the couch, the wall and even measured the floors. When she was done, she saw how peaceful her house looked. Everything she had measured was perfect and fit well into the house. She looked at her card and smiled. She was still a very young girl, but measuring things had brought her so much joy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to collect things. He had a big bin full of toys. One day, Tim saw a beautiful rock on the ground. He wanted to add it to his bin. Tim was so excited that he became careless. He tripped and fell. His bin of toys spilled all over the ground. Tim's friend, Sue, saw what happened and came to help. Sue said, "Oh no, Tim! Your toys are everywhere! Let's pick them up together." Tim and Sue picked up all the toys and put them back in the bin. Tim thanked Sue and they both laughed. Then, Tim put the beautiful rock in the bin too. From that day on, Tim was more careful when he collected things.

One day, a little clown was feeling dizzy. He didn't know why, but his head was spinning. So, he decided to sit down and rest under a big tree. While he was sitting, he saw a little girl named Lily. Lily saw the clown and said, "Hi, clown! Why are you dizzy?" The clown replied, "I don't know, but my head is spinning." Lily wanted to help the clown feel better. She thought of a fun game they could play. Lily said, "Let's play hide and seek! It will make you feel better!" The clown agreed, and they started to play. Lily closed her eyes and counted, while the clown went to hide. He found a good spot behind a bush. When Lily was done counting, she looked for the clown. She found him quickly, and they both laughed. The clown didn't feel dizzy anymore. They played hide and seek all day, and the clown and Lily became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a huge tree in a big forest. In the tree, there was a small cell where a tiny bird lived. The bird loved to sing every day. One day, a little girl named Amy walked by the huge tree. She could hear the bird singing. "Hello, bird!" she said. "I hear your song. It is very pretty." The bird was happy and sang even more. Amy came to the huge tree every day to hear the bird sing. They became good friends. One day, Amy saw another kid being mean to a small bug. She told the kid, "We should be nice to all, big or small." The kid learned to be kind, and they all played together. The moral of the story is to always be kind to everyone, no matter their size.
