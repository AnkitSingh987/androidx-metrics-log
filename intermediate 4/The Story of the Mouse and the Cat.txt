Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Mouse and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a big cat and a little mouse. They were not friends, but one day, the little mouse said, "Big cat, I think I can teach you something new." The big cat was very surprised and said, "What can you teach me, little mouse?" The little mouse said, "I can teach you to be careful. You are always too fast and too loud." The big cat laughed and said, "Okay, little mouse, show me how to be careful." So, the little mouse showed the big cat how to walk softly and not make a lot of noise. One day, while the big cat and the little mouse were walking carefully, they saw a big dog. The big dog did not see them because they were being so careful. The big cat and the little mouse were able to run away and hide. The big cat said, "Thank you, little mouse, for teaching me to be careful. You are a good friend." The little mouse smiled and said, "You are a good friend too, big cat." The big cat and the little mouse became best friends, and they learned that even unlikely friends can teach each other important lessons. The moral of the story is to be open to learning from others, even if they are different from you.

Once upon a time, there was a furry dog named Max. Max lived in a big country with lots of trees and flowers. He loved to run and play all day long. One day, Max saw a little girl named Lily. She was sad. Max wanted to make her happy. So, he ran up to her and said, "Woof! Let's play and have fun!" Lily looked at Max and smiled. She said, "Okay, let's play!" Max and Lily played all day. They ran and jumped in the grass. The sun was shining, and they were very excited. They became the best of friends. From that day on, Max and Lily played together every day, and they were always happy.

Once upon a time, there was a naughty bee. He flew around everywhere, never settling in one spot. One day, he heard a voice calling to him from the ground. He flew down and saw an old ant. The ant urged him to stay with him for a rest. The bee didn't want to listen and so he kept flying away. As the days went on, he got more and more tired from never staying in the same spot. He could feel himself getting weaker and weaker. Eventually, he spotted the ant again and decided to take him up on his offer. The ant was happy to see the bee and brought him lots of food to eat. The bee enjoyed his rest and slowly got back his strength. Once rested, the bee understood how important it was to stay in one spot and not be too naughty and fly around too much. And so the moral of the story is that it is important to rest and stay in the same spot sometimes.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to play a sport. He liked to run and kick. He asked his friend, Sam, to play with him. Sam was a reliable friend. He always played with Tim. Tim questioned Sam, "Do you want to play a sport with me?" Sam said, "Yes, let's play!" They went to the park to play. But when they got there, they saw other kids playing on the field. They could not play their sport. Tim and Sam were sad. They wanted to play, but the field was full. So, they asked the other kids, "Can we play with you?" The other kids said, "Yes! Come play with us!" Tim and Sam were happy. They played the sport together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived near a big wood. One day, he went into the wood to play. He saw a squirrel sitting on a tree branch. The squirrel looked sad. "Hi, squirrel," said Tim. "Why are you sad?" "I am sad because my friend, the rabbit, is being selfish. He won't share his food with me," said the squirrel. Tim wanted to help, so he went to find the rabbit. He saw the rabbit eating carrots. Tim reminded the rabbit that it was good to share. "Rabbit, it is not nice to be selfish. You should share your food with your friend, the squirrel," said Tim. The rabbit listened to Tim and decided to share his food with the squirrel. The squirrel was very happy, and they both became good friends. Tim felt proud that he could help his new friends in the wood.
