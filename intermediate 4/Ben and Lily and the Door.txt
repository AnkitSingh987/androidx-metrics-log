Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben and Lily and the Door

Ben and Lily were twins who liked to play games. They were very competitive and always wanted to win. One day, they decided to play hide and seek in the house. Ben was the seeker and Lily was the hider. Lily ran to the kitchen and looked for a good hiding spot. She saw the oven and thought it was a good idea. She opened the oven door and crawled inside. She closed the door behind her and waited for Ben to find her. Ben wandered around the house and looked for Lily. He checked the living room, the bedroom, the bathroom, but he could not find her. He was getting frustrated and bored. He went to the kitchen and saw the oven. He thought maybe Lily was hiding there. He opened the oven door and saw Lily inside. "Ha! I found you!" he shouted. "You are the loser!" Lily was scared and angry. She did not like being in the oven. It was dark and hot and smelly. She did not like being called a loser. She pushed Ben and tried to get out of the oven. "Hey, stop it!" Ben said. "You are not fair!" They started to fight and yell in the oven. They did not hear their mom coming into the kitchen. She saw the oven door open and heard the noise. She was shocked and worried. She quickly pulled Ben and Lily out of the oven and hugged them. "What are you doing in the oven?" she asked. "That is very dangerous! You could have burned yourself or started a fire!" Ben and Lily felt sorry and ashamed. They realized they had made a bad choice. They apologized to their mom and to each other. They promised to never hide in the oven again. They decided to play a different game that was safer and more fun.

One day, a little boy named Tim was very impatient. He wanted to play with his friend, Sam. Tim's mom said, "Wait, I need to fix the curtain first." Tim didn't listen and ran to Sam's house. They played and had fun. But when Tim came home, he saw his mom was sad. She said, "The curtain is still not fixed. I needed your help, Tim." Tim felt regret. He said, "I'm sorry, Mom. I will help you now." They fixed the curtain together, and his mom was happy. Tim learned to be patient and help his mom.

One day, a little girl named Amy was eager to play outside. She put on her shoes and ran into the garden. She saw a big tree with a lemon hanging from it. Amy wanted to pick the lemon, but it was too high up. Amy asked her friend, Tom, to help her. Tom said, "I have an idea! We can use a stick to make the lemon fall." They found a long stick and tried to reach the lemon. But the stick was not long enough. Just then, Amy's mom came outside and said, "Stop! I can help you." She picked up Amy and lifted her high in the air. Amy reached out and picked the lemon. They all smiled and said, "Thank you!"

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. The sun was high in the sky, and the sand was warm under their feet. They saw many people at the beach, but they found a nice spot that was available. Tim's mom said, "Tim, please repeat after me. I will stay close to you at the beach." Tim said, "I will stay close to you at the beach." They played in the sand and built a big sandcastle together. As they played, a girl named Lily came over. She asked, "Can I play with you?" Tim and his mom said yes. They all had fun at the beach together, and Tim remembered to stay close to his mom.

Jake was a lazy boy. He rarely wanted to do anything. All he did was watch TV. But one day his mom had an idea. She gave Jake a special case. It was filled with fun things. Inside the case were toys and books for Jake to play with. There were also markers, crayons, and a coloring book. Jake was so excited! He opened the case and started to play. He drew pictures and colored them in with the different markers. He also built a tower with the blocks inside the case. Jake loved the case and played with it all day. At the end of the day Jake was tired, but he had had so much fun. Jake's mom was happy that he had found a new activity to do. From then on, Jake stopped being so lazy, and made sure to watch the case every day.
