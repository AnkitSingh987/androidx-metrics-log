Category: Intermediate
Title: The White Cat and the Box

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a white cat. The cat had a friend, a little boy. They liked to play together every day. One day, a big box arrived at the house. The little boy and the white cat were very excited. They wanted to see what was inside the box. The little boy opened the box. Inside, they found a lot of colorful balls. The white cat and the little boy played with the balls all day long. They were very happy and had a lot of fun. And from that day on, the white cat and the little boy had even more fun playing together with the colorful balls.

Once upon a time, there was a small, honest dog named Ben. Ben liked to sit in the park and watch the people play. One sunny day, while Ben was sitting, he saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was playing with her ball. She threw the ball and it went far away. Ben wanted to help Sue, so he ran fast to get the ball. Just as Ben was about to pick up the ball, a big cat jumped out from behind a tree. The cat wanted the ball too. Ben did not want to fight, so he thought of an idea. He asked the cat to play a game of pass with him and Sue. The cat agreed to play and they all had fun passing the ball to each other. Sue was happy to have new friends. The cat learned that it was better to share and play together. In the end, they all sat down in the park, tired but happy. The moral of the story is that being honest and kind can bring people and animals together.

Once upon a time, there was a girl. She brought a label with her every day. The label was light and small, and she kept it in her pocket. She wanted to give it away. One day the girl went to a special place. There she gave her label to a tall person. The girl felt happy and excited. The tall person said to the girl “This label will be yours forever.” She thanked him and smiled. The girl was very surprised when the tall person put a big hat onto her head. He said, “This hat will make you extra special.” The girl was thrilled! The special hat was made from the label which she gave away. That is why the label was important.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Amy. Amy liked to sort her toys after she played with them. She had a big box for her dolls and a small box for her cars. Amy knew this was good work to do. One sunny day, Amy found a thin stick in her yard. She did not know where it came from. She thought it was special, so she picked it up and took it inside. She wanted to find a place to keep her thin stick safe. Amy looked at her boxes of toys and thought about where to put the thin stick. She decided to put it in the big box with her dolls. Now, the thin stick had a home. Every day, Amy would play with her dolls and the thin stick, and she was happy to have a new friend.

Once upon a time there was a good shark who wanted to go for a swim. He had been swimming near the beach and now wanted to take a break. So the shark asked the ocean, "Can I please have a rest?" The ocean said, "Yes of course you can have a rest, but you must ask nicely and please don't stay too long." The shark understood and he said, "Thank you very much. I promise to not stay too long." The ocean was happy and said, "That's very kind of you. You can have a rest, please come back soon!" So the good shark had a rest and when he was ready to go again he thanked the ocean and said goodbye. Then he swam away with a smile.
