Category: Intermediate
Title: Eddie and the Park

Little Eddie was having the best day ever. He was on a swing in the park with his big brother. The wind blew against his hair and he let out a big laugh. "This is awesome!" he shouted. His brother gave him a thumbs up. "You sure are having fun, Eddie," he said. Eddie pumped his legs harder and he flew higher and higher. He loved the feeling of the air on his face. But soon he felt sleepy and he stopped swinging. He yawned and rubbed his eyes. "Time for a nap," his brother said. He lifted Eddie off the swing and put him on the grass. Eddie smiled and cuddled up into a cozy blanket. He watched his brother swing high in the sky as he drifted off to sleep.

One day, a girl named Lily went to the store with her mom. They bought many things to unpack at home. One of the things they bought was an avocado. Lily had never seen an avocado before. She thought it was very green and a little funny-looking. When they got home, Lily helped her mom unpack the bags. She found the avocado and said, "Mom, what is this?" Her mom said, "That is an avocado, Lily. We will eat it later." Lily was excited to try something new. Later, Lily and her mom cut the avocado open. Inside, they found an enormous seed! Lily was so surprised. She said, "Mom, I did not know there was something so big inside the avocado!" Her mom said, "Sometimes, we find big surprises in small things, Lily. It's important to be curious and try new things." Lily smiled and knew her mom was right. She was happy she tried the avocado and learned something new.

---

Lily and Tom were playing in the garden. They found a big bottle under a bush. It was green and shiny. They wanted to see what was inside. "Let's cut it open!" Tom said. He had a pair of scissors in his pocket. "No, don't!" Lily said. She was scared. "It might be dangerous. Maybe it has something unknown in it. Something bad." Tom did not listen. He took the scissors and tried to cut the bottle. But the bottle was too hard. The scissors slipped and cut his finger. Tom cried out loud. He dropped the bottle and the scissors. Lily ran to him. She saw blood on his finger. She felt sorry for him. She took a tissue from her pocket and wrapped it around his finger. "Are you okay?" she asked. "It hurts!" Tom said. He was angry and sad. He looked at the bottle. It was still closed. He did not want to see it anymore. "Let's go inside," Lily said. "We can tell mom. She will make it better." They left the bottle in the garden. They did not know that inside the bottle there was a fairy. She was sleeping. She had been trapped in the bottle for a long time. She hoped that someone would free her. But no one did. She kept dreaming of the sky and the flowers. She did not hear Tom and Lily. She did not see them go. She was alone in the unknown bottle.

Anna and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump and slide. They saw a big tree with many branches. They wanted to climb the tree and see the birds. "Can we climb the tree, Anna?" Ben asked. "Yes, let's climb the tree, Ben. It looks fun," Anna said. They climbed the tree carefully. They held on to the branches and moved up. They saw a nest with three eggs. They saw a squirrel with a nut. They saw a butterfly with pretty colors. They were very happy. They wanted to see more. They climbed higher and higher. They did not see their mom and dad. They wandered too far. They got lost. They did not know how to go down. They were frightened. "Anna, I want to go down. I want to see mom and dad," Ben said. "Me too, Ben. I don't like it here. It is too high and too scary," Anna said. They cried for help. They hoped someone would hear them. They hugged each other. They wished they had not climbed the tree.
