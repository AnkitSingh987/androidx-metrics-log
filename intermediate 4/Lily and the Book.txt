Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Book

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Lily. She loved to read books every day. Yesterday, she went to the park to read a new book. The sun was bright, and the birds were singing. While Lily was reading, a big wind came and blew her book away. She was sad and didn't know what to do. Then, a nice boy named Tom saw Lily and wanted to help her. Tom and Lily looked everywhere for the book, but they could not find it. Then, they saw a little dog with the book in its mouth. The dog was playing with the book like it was a toy. Tom and Lily ran to the dog and asked nicely if they could have the book back. The dog gave the book back, and they all became good friends. The moral of the story is that when we help each other, we can solve problems and make new friends.

Once upon a time there was a very adorable bunny. The bunny's fur was soft and fluffy, just like the clouds in the sky. The bunny adored the feel of everything around her and loved to touch things to discover their texture. One day she decided to explore a new area and she hopped off into a dark forest surrounding her home. Suddenly, the bunny heard a whisper coming from the shadows. She quickly hid behind a tree and squeaked in curiosity. She peeped around the tree and saw a small fox looking around and whispering. The fox kindly explained that she was lost and was looking for her family. The bunny felt bad for the fox and wanted to help her. After a few minutes of searching, the fox found her family and ran off happily. The bunny whispered goodbye and waved. The bunny was so happy that she could help the fox that she ran back home. From then on, she wanted to help all the animals in the forest and make the forest a happy place. She was an adorable bunny and her friends loved her very much!

Once upon a time, there lived a gifted bear called Mark. Mark was very special and loved by everyone in the forest. Every day he would go out exploring, looking for new adventures. One day, while exploring, Mark found a magical hat. He quickly put it on and began to feel its power. His friends were amazed and started talking to the hat. "What’s your name?" asked Mark's friend, Joe. "My name is Mr. Hat," said the hat. Joe was very excited. He asked Mr. Hat if he could make an order. "Of course," said Mr. Hat. "What can I do for you?" Joe thought for a moment. Then he asked,"Can you help us find our way back home?" "I can do that!" said Mr. Hat with a smile. He waved his magic wand and suddenly a path appeared in the forest. Mark and all his friends followed it, and soon enough they were back home. Everyone was very thankful for the help of Mr. Hat. Mark thanked Mr. Hat and returned the magical hat. Mr. Hat said he was happy to help before he vanished. Everybody in the forest knew that Mr. Hat was very gifted indeed!

Tom and Sam were friends who liked to play outside. One day, they saw a big cloud in the sky. It was white and fluffy, like cotton candy. "Look, a cloud!" Tom said. "I want to eat it. It must be tasty." Sam shook his head. "You can't eat a cloud, Tom. It's too high and too far. And it's not candy, it's water." But Tom did not listen. He wanted to eat the cloud. He ran to the hill and found a long stick. He tried to poke the cloud with the stick, but he could not reach it. "Come down, Tom. It's not safe," Sam said. "The stick is too heavy and the hill is too steep. You might fall and get hurt." But Tom did not yield. He wanted to eat the cloud. He climbed higher and higher, until he was at the top of the hill. He swung the stick with all his might, but he missed the cloud. He lost his balance and fell down the hill. He rolled and rolled, until he hit a rock. He cried out in pain. Sam ran to help him. He saw that Tom had a big cut on his head and a broken arm. He was bleeding and crying. Sam felt sorry for him, but he was also angry. "Tom, why didn't you yield? Why didn't you listen to me? You were greedy and foolish. Now you are hurt and sad. And you still can't eat the cloud." Tom realized that Sam was right. He had been greedy and foolish. He wished he had listened to him. He wished he had not tried to eat the cloud. He learned his lesson. The moral of the story is: Don't be greedy and foolish. Listen to your friends. And don't try to eat clouds.
