Category: Intermediate
Title: The Lime Tree and the Ball

Once upon a time, in a lively green garden, there was a little lime tree. Every day, the sun would rise, and the lime tree would grow a bit more. The lime tree was very happy. One day, a little boy came to the garden. He saw the little lime tree and wanted to play with it. The boy picked a lime from the tree and threw it high up in the sky. The lime flew up and up, and something unexpected happened! As the lime touched the sky, it turned into a big, round, green ball. The boy was so surprised and happy. He played with the big green ball all day long. When the sun began to set, the ball turned back into a lime. The boy put the lime back on the tree and said goodbye. From that day on, the little lime tree and the boy became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely tree. It was very tall and had many leaves. The tree was sad because it could not reach the other trees. They were too far away. One day, a little bird came and sat on the tree. The bird saw that the tree was lonely and wanted to help. So, the bird began to sing a beautiful song. The song made the tree feel better. After that, more birds came to visit the tree. They would rub their beaks on the tree's bark and play in its leaves. The tree was not lonely anymore. It was happy because it had made many new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big sunflower. It lived in a pretty garden. The sunflower was restless. It wanted to see the world. One day, a bird came to the garden. The bird wanted to hang a nest in the sunflower. The sunflower was very happy. It knew the bird could help it see the world. The bird built the nest and had baby birds. The sunflower took care of them. The baby birds grew and flew away. The sunflower knew it was time to see the world. The wind blew, and the sunflower's seeds went far away. The sunflower was happy. Now, it could see the world and make new friends.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, lived a brilliant dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his friends. They played ball, ran around, and had fun all day. One day, Spot found a big, red ball. He wanted to share it with his friends. He went to his friend, the little cat, and said, "Look, I found a big, red ball. Let's play with it!" The little cat was happy and they started to play. As they played, more friends joined them. They all took turns and put the ball in different places. They laughed and played together. Soon, it was time to go home. Spot said, "I had so much fun today. I'm glad I shared my ball with everyone." The little cat smiled and said, "Thank you, Spot. Sharing is a good thing to do. When we share, everyone is happy." And so, Spot learned that sharing with friends is important. He promised to always share his toys and have fun with his friends. The moral of the story is: sharing makes everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She was very obedient and always listened to her mum and dad. One day, her mum and dad took her to the doctor. The doctor said, "I'm going to check your lungs. I'm going to measure them and they might shrink." The little girl was scared. "Will it hurt?" she asked. The doctor said, "Not at all. I'm just going to measure your lungs and if they shrink, don't worry." So the little girl obediently lay down and the doctor placed a measuring tape on her chest. After a few minutes, the doctor said, "Your lungs have indeed shrunk a little, but don't worry, that's normal." The little girl was relieved. She thanked the doctor and went home with her mum and dad. She was happy to know that she was still healthy, even though her lungs had shrunk a little. The End.
