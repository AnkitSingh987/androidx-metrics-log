Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Cool Cat and the Dog

One day, a cool cat named Tom found a big bag of sugar. He wanted to eat it all by himself. But his friend, a little dog named Tim, saw the sugar too. They both wanted the sugar. Tom said, "I found it first, so it's mine!" But Tim did not like that. He wanted some sugar too. So, they started to fight. Tom tried to kick Tim, but Tim was too fast. Then, a wise old owl saw them fighting. He said, "Stop! You should not fight. If you share the sugar, you both can be happy." Tom and Tim thought about it. They knew the wise old owl was right. So, they stopped fighting and shared the sugar. They both were happy, and they learned that sharing is better than fighting.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there lived a mysterious bug. This bug was special because it could shrink. It was very tiny and loved to play with its friends. One day, the bug decided to play a game with its friends. It wanted to see who could find the most colorful flowers. They all ran around the garden, looking for the prettiest flowers they could find. While they were playing, the mysterious bug found a special flower. When it touched the flower, it started to shrink! It became smaller and smaller. The bug's friends were amazed and they all laughed and clapped at the fun trick. The mysterious bug then touched the flower again and grew back to its normal size. They all continued to play and had a great day in the garden.

One day, a big, wide river wanted to fill up with water. The river was very graceful and pretty. The river asked the little rain cloud, "Can you help me fill up with water?" The little rain cloud said, "Yes, I will help you." So, the little rain cloud rained and rained. The river filled up with water, but it was not enough. The river was still not full. The river asked the big rain cloud, "Can you help me fill up with water?" The big rain cloud said, "No, I am too busy making rain for the plants." The river was sad and did not know what to do. Then, the sun came out and shined bright. The sun told the river, "Don't worry, I will help you." The sun shined so bright that it made the little rain cloud grow big. The big rain cloud rained and rained. The river filled up with water and was very happy. The river thanked the sun and the rain cloud for helping it fill up with water. The river was full, wide, and graceful again.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved dessert. His favorite dessert was ice cream. One day, he went to the store with his mom to buy some ice cream. At the store, Tim picked a big ice cream. His mom said, "We need to pay for the ice cream." They went to the man at the front to give him money. Tim was happy because he was going to eat his ice cream soon. When they got home, Tim ate his ice cream very fast. His tummy started to hurt. Tim felt ashamed because he knew he should have eaten it slower. But his mom hugged him and said, "Next time, eat your dessert slow and you will feel better."

Once upon a time there was a little girl. She was three years old and very, very brave. She was on an adventure with her mum in the forest. She looked around and saw lots of big trees and colorful flowers. The little girl wanted to explore more, so she went further into the forest. Suddenly, she discovered a big big sack. It was easy to open, so she peeped inside and saw lots of shiny gems! The little girl was very excited. She quickly ran back to her mum and said, "Mummy, mummy, look at what I found!" But her mum was worried. She said, "That's not yours! You must put it back where you found it." The little girl was so sad, but she knew her mummy was right. She put it back and promised to tell nobody about the sack of gems. She was brave and did the right thing. The end.
