Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Telephone

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy was very independent. She liked to do things by herself. One day, Lucy found a broken telephone in her house. She wanted to repair it. Lucy got her tools and started to fix the telephone. She worked very hard. Her mom and dad watched her and were happy to see her being independent. They knew she could do it. At last, Lucy repaired the telephone. She was so proud of herself. Her mom and dad clapped and cheered for her. From that day on, Lucy knew she could do anything she set her mind to. And she lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little mouse. The mouse was worried because he was very dirty. He wanted to be clean and nice, but he did not know how to wash himself. One day, the mouse saw a big cat. The cat was washing her face with her paw. The mouse said, "Hello, cat! Can you help me? I am worried because I am dirty. How do you wash yourself?" The cat looked at the mouse and said, "Of course, little mouse! I can help you. Just use your paws like me. Wet them and rub them on your face and body." The mouse did what the cat said. He wet his paws and rubbed them on his face and body. Soon, the mouse was clean and not worried anymore. He thanked the cat and they became good friends. From that day on, the mouse always knew how to wash himself and was never worried about being dirty again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy called Jack. He didn't like it when his mum wiped his face with a cloth because it tickled! But his mum said it was to make him clean and fresh. One day, Jack was playing with his toys when his mum said it was time to clean his brain. Jack wasn't sure what she meant, but she handed him a cloth. She said the cloth was to wipe away all the boring thoughts, so Jack started to use it. He wiped away his worries and his bad ideas. When he finished, he felt so happy and light. Jack had learned that sometimes it was good to wipe away the old thoughts and start anew. The End.

Once upon a time, there were two friends called Jack and Millie. Jack had a cool blue pipe that he loved playing with. One day, Jack asked Millie to bring his pipe to the park with her. "No!" Millie said. "I'm not going to bring your pipe anywhere!" Jack was disappointed. He didn't know what to do. He had a plan to play with his cool pipe in the park. "Please, Millie," Jack pleaded. "I'll be really careful with it, I promise!" Millie thought for a moment, then finally said, "Okay, I'll bring it - but you have to be super careful!" So Jack and Millie went to the park, and had a lot of fun playing with the cool pipe. Jack promised to always look after his pipe, and Millie was glad she'd brought it with her. The two friends had a wonderful day!

Once upon a time, there was a helpful frog named Sam. Sam lived by a big pond. The pond had a stage where all the animals liked to play and sing. One sunny day, Sam saw that the stage was very dirty. He wanted to make it clean for his friends. So, he jumped into the pond and got his body all wet. Then, he jumped onto the stage to soak it with water. Sam did this many times. Soon, the stage was clean and shiny. His friends were so happy. They all played and sang on the stage together. And that is how helpful Sam made the stage nice and clean for everyone.

Once upon a time, in a small park, there was a big statue. The statue was of a kind man who would tell stories to kids. Every day, children would come to the park and sit near the statue to hear the stories. One day, a new girl came to the park. She was a spoiled girl who always wanted things her way. She wanted the statue to tell her a special story. But the statue could not speak to her. The girl got very sad and started to cry. Soon, the other children came to the park. They saw the spoiled girl crying and asked her what was wrong. She told them she wanted the statue to tell her a special story. The children smiled and said they could all tell her a story together. The spoiled girl felt happy and they all sat near the statue, telling stories and having fun.
