Category: Intermediate
Title: The Great Park of Tim and the Ball

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Max, went on a trip. They wanted to have an interesting time. They walked and walked until they found a big park. In the park, they saw a girl named Lily. Lily was playing with a ball. Tim said, "Hi, can we play too?" Lily said, "Yes, let's play together!" They played with the ball and had a lot of fun. They ran and laughed. It was an interesting trip for Tim and Max. They made a new friend and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a brave little rabbit named Lucky. Luckily, he had many friends who would encourage him on his adventures. One day, Lucky's friends suggested they go explore a maze together. "Let's see who can make it out first!" they said. All of them were excited to take on the challenge. Lucky was a bit nervous, but his friends encouraged him and cheered him on. So he took a deep breath and and stepped into the maze. He started exploring and soon found himself stuck at a dead end. Suddenly, one of his friends shouted from afar. "Lucky! Follow my voice and it will help you find your way out." Lucky did as he said and followed the voice until he found the exit. "Yippie!" he shouted as he came out. All of his friends cheered for Lucky and congratulated him for being so brave. He was certainly very lucky!

One day, a noisy bird and a quiet bunny were in the woods. They were good friends. The bird liked to sing and the bunny liked to eat berries. They saw a big berry bush and went to it. The bird sang, "I will slap the bush and the berries will fall for you to eat." The bunny was happy. The bird slapped the bush and the berries fell. The bunny ate them and they were very yummy. But then, a big bear came! The bear was mad because the bird was noisy and the bunny ate his berries. The bird and the bunny were scared. The bear slapped the bush, and more berries fell. The bear said, "Now we can all share!" They all ate the berries together and became friends.

Billy was three years old and he loved playing with his toy hammer. He was looking for something to hit when he saw a nail in the wall. He grabbed his toy hammer, ready to hit it, when his mom stopped him. "Billy!" his mom said. "We don't hit nails with our toys, they are too sharp. That might make you hurt." But Billy didn't listen. He wanted to hit the nail with his hammer, so he decided to do it. He hit the nail with his hammer and the nail came out of the wall. Suddenly, the nail started moving and a big, dead spider popped out. Billy was terrified and ran away, screaming. His mom came over and saw the dead spider. "That's why you don't hit nails with toys," she said. "You could have been bitten by the spider and it could have been dangerous." Billy learned his lesson and never decided to hit a nail with a toy hammer again.

Once upon a time, there was a naughty seal. Every day, the seal liked to get very messy. He would play in the sand and try to catch fish with his paws. One day, the seal decided to go for a swim. But no matter how hard the seal tried, he just couldn't seem to get his fur clean. So he climbed out of the water and looked for help. At that moment, a little girl was walking nearby. She saw the seal and knew she had to help him. The little girl took the seal to a nearby pond. Then she took a bucket of soapy water and started to wash the seal. The seal was so happy! He jumped up and down in the water and began to splash around. Soon, the seal was sparkly clean and ready to go back home. The naughty seal thanked the little girl and then ran off happily.

Once upon a time, there was a big, thick tree. It was in a nice park with many kids. The kids loved to play near the tree. One day, a small boy named Tim found a hidden cupboard in the tree. He was very happy and excited. Tim opened the cupboard and saw a small, cute cat inside. The cat was trembling because it was cold and scared. Tim felt sad for the cat and wanted to help it. He took the cat out of the cupboard and hugged it. The cat felt warm and safe in Tim's arms. Tim took the cat home and gave it some food and a warm bed. The cat stopped trembling and started to purr. Tim and the cat became best friends. They played together every day, and the cat always slept in Tim's room. And they all lived happily ever after.
