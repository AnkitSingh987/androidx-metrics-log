Category: Intermediate
Title: The Musician and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a musician. He loved to play music all day. He played music with a big box that made sounds. He liked to lift the box and take it with him. One day, the musician went to the park. He wanted to play music for all the people. But he was frightened. He was scared that the people would not like his music. The musician took a deep breath and started to play. The people in the park loved his music. They clapped and danced. The musician was happy. He played music all day and was not frightened anymore.

Once upon a time there was a sheep called Molly. She was very quiet and loved to stay still and chew her wool. She was always very content with her life and enjoyed the peacefulness of the humble meadows. One day, Molly was not alone in the meadow. A big, mean wolf came by and decided to stay in the meadow with Molly. Molly was very scared. The wolf tried to be nice and asked Molly to be friends, but she just couldn't bring herself to trust the wolf and stayed quiet. The wolf realized that Molly wasn't going to be easy friends and became angry. Still, he wanted stay in the meadow so he locked Molly in a box and took all her wool away. Molly was so scared and so sad and she started to cry. Molly had to stay in the box for the rest of her life, without any wool to chew, and she was never able to find her peace again. The end.

Once upon a time there was a girl. She loved going on adventures. One day, she found a hidden door. She wondered what was inside, so she opened it. Inside, she saw a zigzag path. She followed it until the path took her to a big room. In the room was a funny looking creature. He had a big smile on his face. The creature asked the girl a question. She wasn't sure what to do, so she just smiled back. The creature said, "I will give you a sweet treat if you answer my question." The girl was very excited. She thought hard and answered the question correctly. The creature smiled and gave her a big surprise. He had hidden a basket full of candy on the zigzag path! The girl was so happy that she thanked the creature and went home with the treats. She had a wonderful day and the adventure she found was more than she could have hoped for!

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Jack and Jill. They both wanted to go on a special trip, but they didn't know where to go. One day, Jack and Jill decided to unite for a special journey. They packed up their bags and started walking. But then, the sky became very dark and gloomy. They were scared and didn't know what to do. Suddenly, they saw a magical light in the sky. It was a rainbow! Jack and Jill united together and started running towards the rainbow. As they ran, the sky cleared and they saw a beautiful garden. Inside the garden, they found the perfect place for their trip. They laughed and played until it was time to go home. Jack and Jill were so happy to have united for this trip. Together, they had conquered the gloomy sky and made it to the magical garden! They would never forget this special journey.

Lily had a brown head with many curls. She liked to play with her toys and books in her room. But she did not like the dark. When it was night, she wanted a light to glow near her bed. One night, her mom said, "Lily, it is time to sleep. Turn off your light and close your eyes." Lily did not want to do that. She said, "No, mom, I need my light. The dark is scary. Please let me keep my light on." Mom said, "Lily, you are a big girl now. You do not need a light to sleep. The dark is not scary. It is just quiet and calm. You have your teddy bear and your blanket to keep you company. Trust me, you will be fine." Lily did not like what mom said. She felt sad and angry. She said, "No, mom, you do not understand. I want my light. I need my light. Please do not take it away from me." Mom sighed and said, "Lily, I love you, but you have to listen to me. The light is not good for your eyes and your sleep. It will make you tired and grumpy in the morning. You have to learn to sleep without it. I will give you a hug and a kiss, and then I will turn off your light. OK?" Lily did not say anything. She hugged her mom and gave her a kiss. She hoped mom would change her mind. But mom did not. She turned off the light and said, "Good night, Lily. I will see you in the morning. Sweet dreams." Lily felt scared and alone. She could not see anything in the dark. She held her teddy bear and her blanket tight. She wished her light would glow again. She did not want to sleep. She wanted to cry.
