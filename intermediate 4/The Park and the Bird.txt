Category: Intermediate
Title: The Park and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a park. This park was very old. It had big trees and a fun swing. Many kids liked to play there. One sunny day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He played on the swing and climbed the trees. He saw a big tree that looked very old. Tim wanted to climb it. Tim climbed the old tree. But when he got to the top, he started to fall. He was scared. Just then, a big bird saw him and helped him. The bird flew Tim back to the ground. Tim was happy and said thank you to the bird. He played in the park all day and had lots of fun.

---

Once upon a time, there was a happy little girl named Lucy. One day, Lucy went outside to play in the snow. She wore a big coat, a hat, and mittens to keep her warm. Lucy met her friend, Tim, outside. Tim looked at Lucy's mittens and asked, "What are those?" Lucy wanted to explain to Tim. She said, "These are mittens. They keep my hands warm in the snow." Tim looked at his own hands and said, "I want mittens too!" So, Lucy and Tim went inside and asked Lucy's mom for another pair of mittens. Lucy's mom gave Tim some mittens and they both went outside to play again. They were so happy and warm with their mittens on. They played in the snow all day long.

---

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a big, red ball. One day, Lucy's mommy told her, "Lucy, be a good girl and don't play with the ball inside the house." But Lucy was feeling naughty. Later, Lucy played with the ball in the living room. She kicked it hard, and it hit a big box. The box had a pretty wrap on it. "Oh no!" Lucy said. "I broke the wrap on the box. Mommy will be sad." Lucy ran to her mommy and told her what happened. Mommy said, "It's okay, Lucy. We can fix it." They went to the living room and saw the wrap had a small cut. Mommy took out some tape and fixed the cut on the wrap. Lucy said, "Thank you, mommy! I won't be naughty again." From that day on, Lucy played with her ball outside and never broke anything in the house again. And when she saw a pretty wrap, she remembered to be a good girl.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. She lived in a big house with her mom and dad. One day, Sally saw her dad go into the attic. She was very curious about what was inside, so she decided to go up there too. Sally climbed the stairs and leaned on the attic door. It opened with a creak, and she saw many delicate things inside. There were old toys, pretty dresses, and dusty books. Sally was excited and wanted to play with everything. Sally's mom had told her to be careful with delicate things, so she played gently. She found a beautiful doll and a toy car. Sally played with them all day, and when it was time to go back downstairs, she carefully put them back where she found them. The next day, Sally's mom found the doll and the toy car, and she knew Sally had been in the attic. She smiled and decided to let Sally keep the toys because she had been so careful with them.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big, interesting rock. It was purple and shiny. He picked it up and stared at it. He thought it was the most beautiful rock he had ever seen. Tim showed the rock to his friend, Sally. "Look at my purple rock," he said. Sally stared at the rock too. She thought it was very interesting. "Can I hold it?" she asked. Tim said yes, and they both played with the rock. As they played, the sky got dark and it started to rain. The rain made the rock slippery. Tim tried to hold on to it, but it fell out of his hands. The purple rock rolled away and fell into a big puddle. Tim and Sally were sad. They couldn't find the interesting rock anymore. The rain had washed it away.
