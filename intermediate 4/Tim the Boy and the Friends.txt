Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Friends

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a brave boy named Tim. He loved to play with his friends every day. They would run, jump, and have lots of fun. One day, Tim and his friends had a quarrel. They were mad at each other for a minute. But then, they all said sorry and hugged. They knew it was not good to fight. After that, they played together again. They were happy and had lots of fun. Tim and his friends learned to be nice and not to quarrel. They stayed brave and good friends forever.

---

One day, a cat named Tuna lived in a fancy house. Tuna was a very happy cat. She liked to play with her toys and eat yummy food. She had a friend named Birdy who lived in a tree outside her house. Tuna and Birdy liked to play together. One day, Tuna and Birdy wanted to play, but it was raining. The rain made them sad. They had to delay their playtime. They waited and waited for the rain to stop. Finally, the rain stopped, and the sun came out. Tuna and Birdy were so happy! But then, something unexpected happened. When Tuna and Birdy went to play, they found a big rainbow in the sky. The rainbow was so pretty! Tuna and Birdy decided to follow the rainbow to see where it went. At the end of the rainbow, they found a big box of toys! It was a surprise gift from the rainbow. Tuna and Birdy were so happy that they had to delay their playtime because now they had even more toys to play with!

---

Once upon a time, there was a fisherman who liked to go fishing in his boat. One day, the fisherman wanted to go fishing in the forest. He got into his boat and sailed away. When the fisherman was almost there, he heard a loud noise. He was very frightened. Suddenly, a big fish came up from the water. It looked like it was weighing a lot! The fisherman was so scared that he started to shake. He dropped his fishing rod and paddles in the boat. He was so frightened that he decided to turn around and go home. He sailed back home, but he wouldn't forget the big fish he saw. He was glad he was safe at home!

---

Anna and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to run, jump and slide on the grass and the swings. But it was a very hot day and the sun was bright. They felt tired and thirsty. "Let's find some shade," Anna said to Ben. "We can rest and drink some water." They looked around and saw a big tree with many green leaves. They ran to the tree and sat under it. The shade was cool and nice. They drank some water from their bottles and felt better. "Look, what is that?" Ben pointed to something shiny on the ground. It was a small metal ring with a red stone in it. It sparkled in the sun. "Maybe it is a treasure," Anna said. She picked up the ring and examined it. She turned it around and looked at the stone. It was smooth and bright. "Can I see it?" Ben asked. He reached out his hand. "OK, but be careful," Anna said. She gave the ring to Ben. He examined it too. He rubbed the stone and felt its warmth. "It is very pretty," Ben said. "Do you think someone lost it?" "Maybe," Anna said. "Or maybe it is a magic ring. Maybe it can make wishes come true." "What would you wish for?" Ben asked. Anna thought for a moment. She looked at the ring, then at Ben, then at the park. She smiled. "I would wish for more days like this," she said. "More days to play with you and have fun." Ben smiled too. He nodded. "Me too," he said. He put the ring on his finger. It fit perfectly. They held hands and closed their eyes. They made their wish together. Then they opened their eyes and laughed. They did not know if the ring was magic or not. But they felt happy and lucky. They decided to keep the ring as a secret. They put it in their pocket and got up. "Let's play some more," Anna said. "OK, let's go," Ben said. They ran to the slide and slid down. They felt the wind in their hair and the sun on their skin. They were not tired anymore. They were having the best day ever.
