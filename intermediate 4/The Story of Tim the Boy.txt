Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of Tim the Boy

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a wander. He walked on a path in the big park. It was a sunny day and the birds were singing. Tim saw many pretty flowers and green trees. He was very happy. But then, Tim saw something unusual. A big, scary dog was in the park too. The dog barked very loud. Tim was scared and didn't know what to do. He wanted to go back home, but the dog was in his way. Tim thought for a moment, and then he had an idea. He picked up a stick and threw it far away. The big, scary dog ran after the stick. Tim quickly walked back on the path to his home. He was safe and not scared anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot was a filthy dog because he loved to play in the mud. One day, Spot saw a pretty cat named Fluffy. Fluffy was very clean and did not like mud. Spot said, "Hi Fluffy, do you want to play in the mud with me?" Fluffy shivered and said, "No, thank you. I don't like mud." Spot looked sad because he wanted to play with Fluffy. Spot had an idea. He jumped into a big pool of water and got all clean. Then, he asked Fluffy to play again. This time, Fluffy said yes. They played all day and became best friends. Soon, they had a happy marriage and lived together in a nice, clean house.

One day, a little cat named Mimi went for a walk. She saw a big dog named Max. Max was a bit rude. He did not say "hi" to Mimi. Mimi felt sad but she did not want to be rude too. So, she just gave a little shrug and kept walking. Later, Mimi saw her friend, a bird named Bob. Bob was very nice to Mimi. He said, "I have a treat for you!" Mimi was happy. The treat was a yummy fish. Mimi said, "Thank you, Bob!" In the end, Mimi learned that it is better to be nice, like Bob. Being rude, like Max, did not make anyone happy. Mimi and Bob played together and had fun. They were good friends and shared treats with each other.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a little girl named Mia. Mia had a toy cat that she loved very much. One day, Mia and her toy cat went out to play in the yard. The sun was shining, and it was a beautiful day. While playing, Mia saw a dangerous bug. She was scared and started to weep. Suddenly, the toy cat began to glow with a bright light. The light made the bug go away, and Mia stopped crying. She was so happy that her toy cat had saved her from the bug. But then, something unexpected happened. The toy cat turned into a real cat! Mia was so surprised and happy. She hugged her new friend, and they played together all day long. From that day on, Mia and her real cat were always together, and they never saw the dangerous bug again.

One day, a big truck arrive in the town. The truck was full of olives. The olives were not normal. They were very big and very green. The people in the town were very happy. A girl named Lily went to the truck. She said, "I want to see the big olives!" Her mom said, "Okay, let's go!" They walked to the truck and saw the big green olives. Lily was very happy. But then, something strange happen. The big green olives started to talk! The olives said, "Hello! We are not normal olives. We are from a far away place." The people in the town were very surprised. They had never seen talking olives before. The talking olives became friends with the people in the town, and they all lived happily together.

One day, a strong bird with blue feathers wanted to fly high in the sky. The bird loved to fly and look at the big world below. But there was a problem. A mean, big cat always tried to catch the bird when it flew. The strong, blue bird was sad and scared. It wanted to fly without the mean cat trying to catch it. So, the bird thought of a plan to stop the cat. The bird found a big, red ball. It used the ball to play with the cat. The cat liked the ball and played with it a lot. Now, the strong, blue bird could fly high in the sky without the mean cat trying to catch it. The bird was happy and free to fly.
