Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Lollipop

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the store with her mom. Amy saw a big, round lollipop. She wanted it very much. She asked her mom, "Can I have the lollipop, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, but it is important to share with your brother, Tom." When they got home, Amy showed the lollipop to Tom. They both took turns licking the lollipop. It was yummy! But then, Tom accidentally dropped the lollipop on the floor. Amy was sad and yelled, "No, our lollipop!" Mom came into the room and saw the dirty lollipop. She said, "Don't worry, we can go back to the store and get a new one. But this time, we will get two lollipops, one for each of you." Amy and Tom were happy. They went back to the store with their mom and got two new lollipops. They learned that sharing is important, but having their own lollipop was even better.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a plastic toy car that he loved to play with. One day, while he was playing, the car broke. Tim was very sad and didn't know what to do. He went to his mom and showed her the broken car. His mom said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can fix it." She took the car and was very gentle with it. She used glue to put the pieces back together. While they waited for the glue to dry, Tim's mom told him, "Sometimes things break, but we can fix them if we are careful and gentle." Tim learned that being gentle is important when fixing things. He also learned that it's okay if things break because they can be fixed. From that day on, Tim was always gentle with his toys and helped his friends fix their toys too.

One day, a tidy mechanic named Tom was working in his shop. He liked to fix cars and make them go "zip" fast. Tom was always happy when he could help people with their cars. A little girl named Sue came into the shop with her toy car. She looked sad. "My car won't zip," she said. Tom smiled and said, "I can help you." Tom looked at the toy car and saw that it needed a new wheel. He found a small wheel and put it on the car. He gave the car back to Sue. "Now your car will zip again," he said. Sue was so happy. She thanked Tom and went outside to play with her toy car. It zipped very fast, just like Tom said it would. Sue knew that if her car ever needed help again, she could go to the tidy mechanic named Tom.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a modern toy named Twisty. Twisty was a purple toy that could twist and turn in many ways. Twisty had many friends who lived in the house with him. One sunny day, Twisty and his friends went outside to play. They played fun games like hide and seek, tag, and catch. Twisty was very good at these games because he could twist and hide in small places. His friends loved playing with him. As they played, Twisty met a new friend named Tiny. Tiny was a small, round toy. Twisty said, "Hi, I am Twisty! I can twist and turn. What can you do?" Tiny replied, "Hello, Twisty! I can roll very fast!" Twisty and Tiny played together, and they became best friends. They had a great day playing with their friends in the warm sun.

Once upon a time, there was a log in the forest. It was all alone. Nothing stirred nearby. One day, a little girl found the log. She was very brave. She wanted to make the log less lonely. The girl grabbed a big stick from the ground. She started to stir the log. It made a lot of noise. Suddenly, a lot of bugs and small animals came out of the log. The little girl was very happy. The log was no longer lonely. The little girl and the log were very happy. They played together all day long. From that day on, they were the best of friends.
