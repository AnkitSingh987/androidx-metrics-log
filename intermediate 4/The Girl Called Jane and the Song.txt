Category: Intermediate
Title: The Girl Called Jane and the Song

Once upon a time there was a girl called Jane. Jane was a very anxious girl, but she had a brave spirit. Every day, she would walk to school by herself. One day, she noticed a strange man watching her. She was embarrassed and started to run to school. When she got to school, the strange man was there too! He gave her a big smile and asked her for a dance. Jane was so shocked that she could not move. She was so embarrassed! But then, the strange man waved his hand in the air and a beautiful song started playing. Jane's anxiousness started melting away and she finally smiled. She stepped forward and started to dance. The strange man was actually an angel! He wanted to show Jane that no matter how anxious she felt, she still had the spirit to keep on going and dancing, no matter what. Jane was so happy and thanked the angel for his help.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a wise old owl. The owl lived in a tall tree near a trail. Every day, the owl would sit on a branch and watch the animals play. One sunny day, a little rabbit was hopping along the trail. The rabbit saw a big, tasty carrot. He wanted to eat it, but it was stuck in the ground. The rabbit pulled and pulled, but he could not get the carrot out. The wise owl saw the rabbit and wanted to help. He flew down and snapped the carrot out of the ground with his strong beak. The rabbit was so happy! He thanked the owl and shared the carrot with him. From that day on, the wise owl and the little rabbit became the best of friends, and they played together on the trail every day.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to write a letter to his friend. He was anxious because he had never written a letter before. He found a pen and paper and started to write. While Tim was writing, he saw a prune on the table. He thought it was a fun toy, so he picked it up and played with it. He put the prune on the paper and rolled it around. He laughed because the prune made funny marks on the paper. Suddenly, the prune started to grow! It grew bigger and bigger until it was as big as Tim. Tim was not scared, but surprised. He looked at the big prune and smiled. The big prune smiled back at him. They became friends and played together all day. Tim learned that sometimes, unexpected things can bring new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her toys. Her toys were her equipment. She had a comfortable chair where she would sit and play all day. One day, Sue found a new toy. It was a small, cute bug. The bug could blink its eyes. Sue liked to watch the bug blink. She would laugh and clap her hands. Sue showed her mom the blinking bug. Her mom smiled and said, "What a fun toy!" Sue played with the bug and her other equipment in her comfortable chair. They all had a happy day together.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly dog named Max. Max liked to wander around the park every day. He loved to visit his friends and play with them. One day, Max went for a visit to see his best friend, Lucy, the cat. They played with a ball and ran around the park. They were very happy to see each other. After playing, Max and Lucy went to visit their elderly friend, Mr. Owl. Mr. Owl liked to tell stories. They listened to his stories and had a fun day together.

Lily and Max were twins who liked to play hide and seek. One day, they decided to play in their room while their mom was cooking. Lily counted to ten and then looked for Max. She checked under the bed, behind the curtains, and in the closet, but she could not find him. "Where are you, Max?" she called out. "I give up!" She heard a giggle from the corner of the room. She saw a pile of pillows and blankets on the floor. She walked over and lifted one of the pillows. She saw Max's face peeking out. He had a big smile on his face. "Boo!" he shouted. "You found me!" Lily laughed and hugged him. "You are good at hiding, Max. But you should not make so much noise. Mom might hear us." They put the pillows and blankets back on the bed and switched roles. Max counted to ten and then looked for Lily. He checked under the bed, behind the curtains, and in the closet, but he could not find her. "Where are you, Lily?" he called out. "I give up!" He heard a beep from the corner of the room. He saw a red alarm clock on the nightstand. He walked over and picked it up. He saw Lily's face peeking out from behind the alarm clock. She had a scared look on her face. "Help!" she whispered. "I'm stuck!" Max was confused. "How did you get stuck, Lily?" Lily explained that she had tried to hide behind the alarm clock, but she had pushed it too hard and it had fallen on her head. She could not move it because it was too heavy. She felt helpless and scared. Max felt sorry for her. He put the alarm clock down and helped her get out. He hugged her and said, "I'm sorry, Lily. Are you okay?" Lily nodded and said, "I'm okay, Max. Thank you for helping me. You are a good brother." They decided to stop playing hide and seek and play something else. They heard their mom call them for dinner. They ran downstairs and told her what had happened. She was glad that they were okay and that they had helped each other. She gave them a kiss and said, "You are good twins. I love you both."
