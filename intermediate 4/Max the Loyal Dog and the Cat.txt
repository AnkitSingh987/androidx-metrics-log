Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Loyal Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Max. Max loved to play with his hose in the yard. Every day, Max would run and jump in the water from the hose. One day, Max went to visit his friend, the cat named Luna. Luna also liked to play with the hose. Max and Luna had lots of fun playing with the hose together. They laughed and splashed in the water. After a while, Max and Luna were tired from playing with the hose. They sat down and took a rest. Max was happy to have a loyal friend like Luna. They promised to visit each other and play with the hose again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty dog named Max. Max loved to pick things up with his mouth. One day, Max found a big box. The box had something very special inside: chocolate! Max knew he should not eat the chocolate. But Max wanted to share the chocolate with his friends. So, Max picked up the box with his mouth and went to find his friends. Max found his friends, and they all shared the chocolate. Everyone was happy and thanked Max for being a mighty dog. Max was proud of himself for sharing the chocolate with his friends. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a very ignorant lawyer. He had read all of the laws, but he did not understand them. The lawyer went to court and tried to defend his client, but he didn't know what to say. The judge was very angry and said, "You are so ignorant!" Then the judge sentenced the client to a long time in prison. The lawyer was sad. He felt dry and wanted to hide in a corner. He did not know how to help the client. He had failed them. The lawyer never went back to court. He was too ashamed and afraid of being punished for his ignorance. He just hoped his client would forgive him. But in the end it was too late, the damage had been done.

Once upon a time, there was a little frog. This frog was different from the other frogs. He had a big red spot on his back. The other frogs did not have this spot. One day, the little frog asked his friend, "Why am I different?" His friend said, "I don't know, but it's okay to be different. We all value you." The little frog felt happy that his friend liked him. But some other frogs did not like the little frog's red spot. They said mean things to him. This made the little frog sad. He went to his friend and told him what happened. His friend said, "Don't worry. I will talk to them." His friend talked to the mean frogs. He told them that it's not nice to say mean things. The mean frogs said sorry to the little frog. They all played together and had fun. The little frog learned that being different is okay and that his friends value him.

Once upon a time, there was a tall boy named Tim. Tim had a spoon that he loved very much. He would take his spoon with him everywhere he went. He liked to eat with it and play with it too. One day, Tim went to the park with his spoon. He saw a girl named Sue. Sue had a big, pretty hat. Tim liked the hat a lot. He started to admire Sue's hat. He thought it was the best hat he had ever seen. Tim wanted to be friends with Sue. He walked up to her and said, "Hi, I like your hat." Sue smiled and said, "Thank you, I like your spoon." They became friends and played together all day. Tim and Sue were very happy.

One summer day, a cat named Tom went outside to play. Tom was a little foolish sometimes, but he loved to have fun. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. Tom was very happy. Tom saw a water hose in the garden. He had a fun idea. He put his paw on the hose and started to spray water everywhere. The water went up and down, left and right. Tom laughed and jumped in the water. Tom's friend, a dog named Max, came to play too. Max saw Tom spraying water and thought it was funny. They both played in the water, laughing and having a great time. Even though Tom was foolish, they had a fun summer day together.
