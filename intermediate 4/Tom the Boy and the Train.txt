Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Train

One day, a nice boy named Tom went to play outside. He saw a big train with lots of steam coming out. Tom was very happy to see the train. He wanted to ride on it. Tom asked the train driver, "Can I ride the train, please?" The train driver said, "Yes, you can! But first, you have to find the magic key to open the door." Tom looked and looked for the key. He looked under rocks and in the trees. Then, Tom realized the key was in his pocket all along! He was so surprised. He used the key to open the door and got on the train. The train went fast and Tom had a great time. The train driver smiled and said, "Tom, you had the key to fun in your pocket the whole time!"

One day, a big red telephone sat on a table. It was very quiet. A little girl named Lucy was playing with her toys in her room. Suddenly, the telephone made a loud noise. It was time for the telephone to wake up! Lucy heard the noise and ran to the telephone. She picked it up and said, "Hello?" "Hi Lucy! This is a serious call!" said a voice on the other side. Lucy did not know who it was. She was confused. She asked, "Who are you?" The voice laughed and said, "It's me, your friend Tim! I am just playing with you. I wanted to surprise you." Lucy laughed too. She was happy it was just her friend Tim and not a serious call. They talked and played on the telephone for a long time. In the end, the big red telephone went back to sleep, and Lucy and Tim had a fun day.

One day, a cat and a dog were best friends. They liked to play and run in the yard. The cat had a task to spread food for the birds. The dog had a task to spread water for the flowers. They did their tasks every day. One day, the cat said, "I am hurt. I can't do my task today." The dog said, "Don't worry, I will help you." The dog spread food for the birds and water for the flowers. The cat was happy and said, "Thank you, dog." The next day, the cat felt better. They went to the yard to do their tasks. But, something unexpected happened! The birds and flowers could talk! The birds said, "Thank you, cat and dog, for the food and water." The flowers said, "We have a surprise for you!" Then, the flowers changed into beautiful butterflies. The cat and the dog were so happy and played with the butterflies all day.

Once upon a time there was a little boy called Jack. He was only three years old and he really loved exploring. One day he decided to go and explore his garden. He opened the back door and entered the garden. As he walked around the garden, he noticed a new insect. This insect was see-through and had wings. It was beautiful. Jack wanted to get close to the insect so he reached out his hand to touch it. Suddenly, the insect flew away! Jack was sad and called the insect "stupid". He thought it was mean that the insect would not let him see it. Jack decided to try and catch the insect. He ran around the garden trying to spot it in the trees and bushes. But no matter how hard he tried, he couldn't catch the insect. It was too fast! Jack eventually got tired so he went back inside. He was still feeling sad and upset, but he knew that he would never give up trying to catch that stupid insect.

Once upon a time, there was a charming little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play outside in the sun. One day, she saw a tiny flea on a leaf. She thought it was cute and wanted to be its friend. Lily had an idea. She wanted to create a small house for her new flea friend. She used sticks, leaves, and grass to make the house. Lily was very happy with her creation. She knew her flea friend would love it too. Lily put the flea inside the house she created. The flea was happy in its new home. They played together every day. Lily and the flea became the best of friends. They had lots of fun in their charming little world.
