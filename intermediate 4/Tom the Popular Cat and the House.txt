Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Popular Cat and the House

Once upon a time, there was a popular cat named Tom. Tom liked to travel to many places. He had a lot of friends because he was nice and fun. One day, Tom went to a big house. In the house, there was a lot of toast. Tom loved toast, so he ate it all up. He was very happy. But then, the owner of the house came home. The owner was mad that Tom ate all the toast. The owner chased Tom away, and Tom was sad. He could not travel to the big house anymore.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a polite boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars. His favorite car was a big red one that could drive very fast. One day, Tim's mom gave him a new bottle of lotion. She told him to use it on his hands to make them soft. Tim had an idea. He thought that if he put the lotion on the wheels of his big red car, it would drive even faster. So, he put some lotion on the wheels and started to play with it. Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play too. Sam saw the big red car and asked, "Can I drive it too, please?" Tim said, "Sure, Sam, but be careful. I just put lotion on the wheels." Sam took the big red car and started to drive it. But because of the lotion on the wheels, the car slipped and crashed into the wall. The big red car broke into pieces. Tim and Sam were very sad. They learned that putting lotion on the wheels of the car was not a good idea. The big red car was gone forever, and they couldn't play with it anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Kitty. Kitty lived in a big house with her best friend, a little girl named Lucy. They played together every day. One sunny day, Kitty and Lucy went for a walk in the park. They saw a stand with many toys. Lucy saw a pretty doll and said, "This doll belongs to me!" Kitty saw a soft ball and said, "This ball belongs to me!" They played with their new toys all day. The sun went down, and it was time to go home. Kitty and Lucy were very happy. They knew they belonged together, just like their toys. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a small cabin. In the cabin, there lived a helpful girl named Lily. She liked to help her mom and dad with their work. Lily had a big heart and a big smile. One day, Lily saw a lost dog near the cabin. The dog was sad and hungry. Lily wanted to help the dog. She decided to take the dog to her cabin. She gave the dog food and water. The dog was very happy. Lily and the dog became best friends. They played and had fun together. The dog was helpful too. It helped Lily with her chores. They lived happily in the little cabin in the big forest. And they were always ready to help others who needed it.

Once upon a time, there was a humble panda. The panda loved to eat bamboo and play with his friends. He lived in a big forest with lots of trees and flowers. One day, the panda was very tired. He yawned and said, "I need to sleep." So, he looked for a nice place to rest. He found a soft bed of leaves under a big tree. The panda lay down and closed his eyes. Soon, the panda was fast asleep. He had happy dreams about playing with his friends and eating bamboo. When he woke up, the sun was shining, and the panda felt good. He was ready to play and have fun again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big sister named Sue. He would always admire Sue because she could do many things that he could not do. Sue could run fast, climb trees, and even read books. One day, Tim and Sue went to play outside. They saw a cat stuck in a tree. The cat was in trouble. Sue tried to help the cat, but it was difficult for her to reach it. Tim wanted to help too, but he was too small. Sue had an idea. She found a long stick and used it to help the cat come down from the tree. The cat was safe and happy. Tim and Sue felt very proud that they could help the cat. From that day on, Tim learned that even though some things are difficult, he could still help if he tried his best.
