Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Helpful Dog and the Cat

One day, a helpful dog named Max went for a walk. He liked to find treats and share them with his friends. Max was a very good dog. As he walked, he saw a cat. The cat seemed sad. Max asked, "Why are you sad, cat?" The cat said, "I lost my treat. Can you help me find it?" Max said, "Yes, I can help you!" Max and the cat looked for the treat. They found it under a tree. The cat was happy. Max and the cat shared the treat. They became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max was very restless. He did not like to stay in his yard. He wanted to go out and play with his friends. One day, Max saw a hole in the fence. He thought, "I can escape through this hole and go play with my friends!" So, he squeezed through the hole and went to find his friends. They played all day long. But then, something unexpected happened. Max saw a little girl crying. She was lost and could not find her way home. Max wanted to help. He let the little girl hold his collar and he led her back to her house. The girl's family was so happy and thanked Max. Max realized that sometimes, staying home is not so bad after all.

Once upon a time, there was a race. In this race, there were two friends, a dog and a cat. The dog was big and strong, while the cat was small and weak. They both wanted to win the race, but they had different styles. The dog liked to run very fast all the time. The cat liked to run fast too, but he also liked to jump high and climb trees. The race started, and the dog and the cat began to run. They ran through the park, past the pond, and up the hill. At the end of the race, the dog and the cat were both tired, but they were happy. They learned that even though they had different styles and one of them was weak, they could still have fun together. And that is what made them the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big garden. In the garden, there were many pretty flowers. A girl named Amy liked to sell the flowers. Amy had a friend named Tim. Tim liked the garden too, but he did not have one. Tim was envious of Amy's garden. One day, Amy said to Tim, "I will sell you some flowers for your own garden." Tim was very happy. He bought the flowers and planted them in his yard. The flowers started to grow. The next week, Amy went to visit Tim's garden. She saw Tim's flowers were bigger and prettier than hers. Now, Amy was envious of Tim's garden. She asked Tim, "How did your flowers grow so big?" Tim said, "I gave them lots of love and care." Amy learned that love and care can make a garden grow better. From that day on, both friends took care of their gardens and had the prettiest flowers in town.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely little girl named Lily. She had a pretty bow in her hair. She loved to play with her friends in the park. But there was a boy named Tim who did not like Lily. Tim would always say, "I hate your bow!" One day, Lily was playing with her friends when a big wind came. The wind blew Lily's bow out of her hair and into the sky. All the kids were sad, but Tim was happy. He thought it was funny that Lily's bow was gone. But then, something unexpected happened. A bird saw the bow flying in the sky and caught it. The bird brought the bow back to Lily and put it back in her hair. Tim was surprised and felt bad for being mean. He said sorry to Lily and they became friends. The moral of the story is to be kind to others, even if they are different from you.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty house with a big fireplace. In this house, there lived a nice family. They had a mom, a dad, a boy named Tim, and a girl named Lily. They liked to sit by the fireplace to get warm and talk. One cold day, Mom said, "Let's make a fire." Dad took a stick and struck it on the fireplace. The fire started and the family felt warm. They all sat around the fireplace and looked at the pretty fire. Tim and Lily played near the fireplace. They liked the pretty colors of the fire. Tim said, "I like the red and orange." Lily said, "I like the blue and yellow." They both agreed that the fire was very attractive. They stayed warm and happy together by the fireplace.
