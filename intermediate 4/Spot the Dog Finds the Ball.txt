Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog Finds the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had a rough, brown fur. He loved to play with his friends in the park. One day, while playing, Spot lost his favorite ball. He began to worry because he didn't know where it was. Spot looked everywhere for his ball. He asked his friends to help him find it. They searched high and low, near and far. Spot was very sad and worried that he would never find his ball again. Then, a kind girl named Lucy found Spot's ball under a bush. She gave it back to him with a big smile. Spot was so happy that he wagged his tail and jumped around. As a reward for helping him, Spot gave Lucy a big, wet kiss on her cheek. They became best friends and played together every day.

Sam and Lily were twins who liked to play outside. One day, they saw a big hole in the fence near their house. They wanted to see what was on the other side. "Let's go, Sam!" Lily said. "Maybe there is a fun place to explore!" "No, Lily, we can't!" Sam said. "Mom and Dad warned us not to go near the fence. They said it is dangerous and there are bad people there." "But it is so boring here!" Lily said. "We always play with the same toys and the same games. I want to see something new!" Lily did not listen to Sam. She ran to the hole and squeezed through. Sam followed her, because he did not want to leave her alone. They found themselves in a dirty and noisy place. There were cars and trucks and bikes and people everywhere. They smelled smoke and trash and gas. They heard horns and sirens and shouts. They felt scared and lost. "Sam, I don't like this place!" Lily said. "It is not fun at all! It is scary and ugly and loud! Let's go back!" "OK, Lily, let's go back!" Sam said. "But how do we find the hole?" They looked around, but they could not see the fence or the hole. They had gone too far. They started to cry. A kind man saw them and asked them what was wrong. He helped them find the hole and go back to their side. He told them to be careful and not to go near the fence again. Sam and Lily thanked the man and ran to their house. They hugged Mom and Dad and told them what happened. Mom and Dad were angry and worried, but also happy that they were safe. They gave them a big lecture and a big hug. Sam and Lily learned their lesson. They never went near the fence again. They realized that their home was not boring, but cozy and happy. They played with their toys and games and each other, and had fun.

One day, a little ball named Rollie went on a walk. He saw a magnet on the ground. "Hi, I am Rollie. What is your name?" he asked. The magnet said, "I am Maggie the Magnet. Nice to meet you!" Rollie and Maggie became friends. They played all day. Then they found a spicy pepper. "What is this?" asked Rollie. "It is a spicy pepper," said Maggie. They thought it was fun to play with the spicy pepper. Rollie and Maggie played a game with the spicy pepper. They would roll the pepper to each other. But one time, when Rollie rolled the pepper to Maggie, the magnet pulled the pepper to her very fast! Rollie and Maggie did not know that magnets can pull some things. They laughed and played with the spicy pepper and the magnet all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a big crocodile who lived in a pond. One day, he saw a boat sail by. The crocodile was worried because he had never seen a boat before. He swam closer to the boat to see who was inside. In the boat, there was a little girl named Lily. She saw the worried crocodile and said, "Hello, Mr. Crocodile! Why are you worried?" The crocodile replied, "I am worried because I have never seen a boat before. What is it?" Lily smiled and said, "This is a boat. It helps me sail on the water." The crocodile felt better and thanked Lily for teaching him about boats. They became good friends and sailed together on the pond. The moral of the story is that talking to someone can help you feel better when you are worried or scared.
