Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a big forest, a little girl named Lily went out to play. She wanted to discover new things. While walking, she found a small tent. This tent was in an unknown part of the forest. Lily was curious and went inside the tent. Inside, she found a big box. She opened the box and saw many toys. Lily was very happy and played with the toys. But then, she heard a small cry. It was a little boy, who was sad because his toys were gone. Lily felt bad and decided to give the toys back to the boy. They played together and became good friends. The moral of the story is to always be kind and share with others.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. They wanted to pack a fun picnic. Tim's mom let him choose what to pack. He picked a big red apple, a yummy sandwich, and a rare blue jellyfish toy. At the beach, Tim met a new friend named Sarah. They played with the rare blue jellyfish toy in the sand. Tim said, "I like your hat, Sarah!" Sarah said, "Thank you, Tim! I like your jellyfish toy!" After playing, Tim and Sarah sat down to eat their picnic. Tim's mom shared the big red apple and yummy sandwich with Sarah. They all had a great day at the beach with the rare blue jellyfish toy.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside all day. One day, Spot found a big box. He was very excited. The box was a mystery. Spot's mom saw the box and was worried. She warned Spot to be careful. She said, "Spot, don't open the box. It could be bad." But Spot wanted to see what was inside the box. Spot opened the box, and out came a lot of balloons. They were all different colors. Spot's mom was happy that it was not bad. They played with the balloons all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim and his loyal dog, Spot, went for a walk. They saw a big apple tree. Tim said, "I want to eat an apple, but I can't reach." Spot barked and jumped up to get an apple. He got one and gave it to Tim. Tim said, "Thank you, Spot!" They both ate the apple and it was yummy. Then, they saw a man. The man asked, "Did you take an apple from my tree?" Tim said, "Yes, but I forget to ask." The man was angry and said, "You can't take apples without asking!" He took Spot away from Tim. Tim was very sad and learned not to take things without asking.

Once upon a time, there was a generous girl named Amy. She loved to share her toys with her friends. One day, Amy found a big basket of fruit. There were apples, oranges, and bananas. She was so happy and wanted to keep all the fruit for herself. Amy thought about her friends and how they would also like the fruit. She decided to be generous and share the fruit with them. Amy took the fruit to the park where her friends were playing. When they saw the fruit, they were very happy. Amy and her friends ate the fruit together. They all said thank you to Amy for sharing. They played and had fun all day. The moral of the story is that sharing makes everyone happy.

Max was playing in his garden when it started to rain. He ran to the house to get away from the wet weather. He opened the big cupboard in his bedroom, hoping to find something to play with. Suddenly, out of the cupboard popped a tiny, harmless creature - it looked like a mouse. Max jumped and screamed, startled by the surprise. The mouse looked at Max and said "Don’t be scared, I just want to be friends. We can be careful and stay away from the rain." Max smiled and said "okay" and the pair spent the day playing and having fun. But when day turned to night and the rain was still pouring, Max decided it was time for bed. Sadly, the mouse couldn't go back into the cupboard, so Max had to leave him out in the rain. The mouse looked sad and said "I'll miss you". Max felt terrible, but there was nothing he could do. He just had to leave the mouse in the rain, harmless and all alone.
