Category: Intermediate
Title: Kitty the Cat and the Mouse

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Kitty. Kitty was very hungry, so she wanted to have a big party with her friends. She decided to invite all her friends to her house to eat and play. Kitty was very excited about her party. Kitty got a big glass to put juice in for her friends. She put the glass on a table and started to make some yummy food. Her friends came to the party and they were all very happy. They played games and laughed a lot. But then, something unexpected happened. A little mouse came to the party too! The mouse was also hungry and wanted to join the fun. Kitty and her friends were surprised, but they decided to let the mouse stay. They all shared the yummy food and juice. In the end, Kitty and the mouse became good friends, and they all had the best party ever!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to count things. One day, Sue found a big, heavy box in her room. She opened the box and saw many sheets of paper inside. Sue wanted to count the sheets. She started to count the sheets, one by one. Suddenly, as she picked up a sheet, a tiny mouse jumped out from under it. Sue was so surprised! The mouse looked at Sue and said, "Hi! I'm Mike, the magic mouse. Thank you for finding me!" Now, Sue and Mike became good friends. They played and counted things together every day. And they lived happily ever after.

Once there was a boy named Joe. He loved to play outside. One day he found a stretchy plant in his garden. He was so excited and tried to pull it back and forth. He pulled it so tight that it wouldn't move. Joe was so frustrated, he didn't know what to do. Then his dad walked past and saw what was happening. He said, "Joe, to make this plant move, you need to stretch it, but not too tight!" Joe tried what his dad said and it worked! He could now play with the stretchy plant. Joe was so excited and now he could play with the plant all day.

One day, a silly cat named Tom found a big onion in the kitchen. He didn't know what it was, but he liked how it smelled. Tom thought it would be fun to play with the onion, so he pushed it around the floor. Tom's friend, a little girl named Lucy, saw the cat playing with the onion. She had a fun idea. Lucy took out her toy camera and started to record Tom playing with the onion. She thought it would be a funny video to show her friends later. As Lucy recorded, Tom pushed the onion into the living room. Suddenly, the onion rolled under the couch. Tom tried to get it, but it was stuck. Lucy stopped recording and helped Tom get the onion. They both laughed and had a great time playing with the silly onion. Later, Lucy showed the video to her friends, and they all laughed at the silly cat and his onion adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a dog named Spot. They loved to play outside. One day, they found a dirty ball. The ball was under a big tree. Tim and Spot were very happy. Tim's mom saw them play. She said, "Tim, the ball is dirty. You should wash it." Tim did not mention the dirty ball to his mom. He just played with Spot. They had so much fun. Later, Tim's mom came outside. She saw the dirty ball under the tree. She said, "Tim, I told you to wash the ball." Tim was sorry. He washed the ball with Spot. They played with the clean ball all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Ben. Ben loved to play in the spring. The sun was warm, and the flowers were pretty. Ben liked to stretch his legs and hop around the green grass. One day, Ben saw a big, slow turtle named Tim. Tim was walking in the grass, enjoying the spring day. Ben wanted to be friends with Tim, so he hopped over to him. "Hi, Tim! Do you want to play?" asked Ben. Tim was happy to play with Ben. They played together all day long. They hopped, stretched, and walked in the warm spring sun. They became best friends and enjoyed many more slow, sunny days in the spring.
