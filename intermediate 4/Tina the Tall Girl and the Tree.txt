Category: Intermediate
Title: Tina the Tall Girl and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a tall girl named Tina. Tina loved to wear her favorite blouse. It was blue with pretty flowers. She liked to imagine that she was a big princess in a far away land. One day, Tina saw a big tree. It was very tall and had many branches. Tina had an idea. She wanted to climb the tree and imagine she was a princess in a tall tower. Tina started to climb the tree, feeling excited. As she climbed up, her blouse got caught on a branch. She tried to get free, but the blouse ripped. Tina was sad and scared. She couldn't climb down because her blouse was stuck. She cried and cried, wishing she didn't climb the tree. The tall tree was not a fun place for a princess after all.

Once upon a time, there was a fast rabbit named Bob. One day, a big storm came to his home. Bob was scared and wanted to find a safe place to keep away from the storm. Bob saw a big tree and said, "I can keep safe under this tree." So, he went under the tree and waited for the storm to go away. But the storm was very strong and the tree started to shake. Then, Bob met a slow turtle named Tim. Tim said, "Bob, let's keep together and find a better place to hide." But Bob didn't listen to Tim. He wanted to stay under the tree because he thought it was safe. Tim went away to find a safer place. The storm got worse and the tree fell down. Bob was not safe anymore. If only he had listened to Tim, he would have been safe. But now, it was too late.

One day, a furry ostrich named Ollie was walking in the woods. He saw a big, round ball. Ollie was curious and wanted to play with the ball. He used his long neck to squeeze the ball, but it did not move. Ollie saw a little girl named Lily. She was playing near the ball. "Hi, Lily!" said Ollie. "Can you help me squeeze this ball?" Lily looked at the ball and said, "Okay, Ollie! Let's try together." They both squeezed the ball, but it still did not move. Suddenly, the ball began to shake and wiggle. Ollie and Lily stepped back, surprised. The ball opened up like a big flower, and out came a tiny, magical fairy! The fairy said, "Thank you for helping me! I was stuck inside the ball. As a thank you, I will grant you both one wish!" Ollie and Lily were so happy and excited. They knew that their new friend, the fairy, would make their wishes come true.

Once upon a time, there was a funny queen. She liked to play and laugh all day. The queen lived in a big castle with many friends. They all liked to have fun together. One day, the queen told her friends to stay in the castle. She wanted to go for a walk alone. As she walked, she found a big box. The queen was very curious and opened the box. Inside the box, there was a magic hat. The queen put the hat on her head. Suddenly, she turned into a funny bird! The queen was surprised but she liked being a bird. She flew back to the castle and played with her friends. They all laughed and had a great time with the funny bird queen.

Once upon a time, there was a wide bowl full of yummy pasta. It was in a kitchen on a big table. A little girl named Lily loved pasta very much. She wanted to eat the pasta in the wide bowl. She was so happy to see it. But one day, Lily lost her pasta. She looked in the kitchen, but it was not there. She looked in her room, but it was not there. Lily was very sad. She wanted her pasta back. Lily asked her mom for help. Her mom found the pasta in the fridge. Lily was so happy! She ate the pasta from the wide bowl and smiled. She knew she should always put her pasta in the fridge so she would not lose it again.
