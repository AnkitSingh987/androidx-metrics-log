Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Gifted Boy and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a gifted boy named Tim. He loved to play at the park. His favorite thing to do was swing on the swings. One day, Tim found a big stick at the park. He called it his "staff". He liked to hold it while he played. He would swing with his staff in his hand. Tim and his staff had many fun days at the park. They would swing higher and higher. Tim felt like he could touch the sky. He was very happy. And that is the story of Tim, his staff, and the swings.

---

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing outside. He loved to play in the sun with his toys. But suddenly, he saw dark clouds in the sky. Tim was scared of storms, so he ran inside his house. His mom saw him and said, "Don't worry, Tim. You are safe inside the house." Tim felt better when he heard his mom say that. They sat by the window and watched the rain fall outside. As they sat, Tim could hear the lightning. It was loud and bright. But he knew he was safe with his mom. They talked and laughed until the storm was over. Then, Tim went back outside to play in the sun again.

Once upon a time, there was a big party. Many kids came to the party. They played games, ate cake, and had fun. But two kids, Tim and Sue, did not play together. They had a quarrel. Tim said to Sue, "You are stupid!" Sue said back, "No, you are stupid!" They were both very upset. Their friends did not like the quarrel. They wanted Tim and Sue to play and have fun at the party. A friend named Sam came to Tim and Sue. Sam said, "Stop the quarrel! Let's play together and have fun!" Tim and Sue thought about it. They said sorry to each other. They all played together and had a great time at the party. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little, frightened mouse named Mimi. Mimi lived in a tiny cell under the ground. One day, Mimi decided to clean her cell because it was very dirty. While she was cleaning, Mimi found a shiny, magic stone. She said to herself, "What a pretty stone!" Mimi touched the stone and, suddenly, she was not frightened anymore. She felt brave and strong. Mimi went outside and saw a big cat. The cat said, "I am going to eat you, little mouse!" But Mimi was not scared. She showed the cat her magic stone and said, "I am not frightened anymore!" The cat saw how brave Mimi was and decided not to eat her. They became friends and played together every day. The moral of the story is that being brave can turn your enemies into friends.

Once upon a time, there was a weird magnet. It was not like other magnets. It could lead things to it, even if they were not metal. One day, the weird magnet was in a big park. In the park, many kids played with their toys. The weird magnet felt lonely and wanted to play too. It started to lead the toys to it. The toys moved closer and closer to the weird magnet. The kids saw their toys move and were so happy! They played with the weird magnet all day. The weird magnet was not lonely anymore. It had many friends and fun in the park. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a wealthy man named Bob. Bob had many things, but he was not happy. He wanted to help others and make friends. So, Bob had a task for himself to supply food to people who did not have any. One day, Bob saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was hungry and sad. Bob walked up to Sue and said, "Hi, I am Bob. I want to help you. I can supply food for you." Sue looked up and smiled. She said, "Thank you, Bob. You are very kind." Bob and Sue went to the store and bought lots of food. They then gave the food to people who were hungry. Everyone was happy and grateful. Bob felt good about his task. He had made many new friends, and he knew he had done a good thing.
