Category: Intermediate
Title: John and the Taste

John liked to taste lots of things. One day he was standing in front of a store. He could see lots of yummy things in there. He wanted to try them all! John saw some candy that looked delicious. He wanted to try them but it was too expensive. He was sad because he couldn't try the candy. Then he saw something interesting. He saw some palms right by the store. He was curious to taste them. He bent down and picked one up. It was cheap and it tasted sweet! John was so happy that he could finally get something to eat. He ate the palm and smiled. From then on, he could always taste nice things when he was out.

Tom and Sue like to play with their toys. They have cars, dolls, blocks and books. But one day, Tom's car broke. The wheel came off and the car could not move. Tom was sad and cried. Sue saw Tom crying and wanted to help. She said, "Don't cry, Tom. We can repair your car. I have a cell in my pocket. It is like a small box with a light. We can use it to fix your car." Tom stopped crying and looked at Sue's cell. He said, "How can we use your cell to repair my car? It is not a tool. It is a toy." Sue said, "No, it is not a toy. It is a magic cell. It can do many things. It can make sounds, show pictures, and talk to people. And it can also repair things. Watch me." Sue took the cell and touched the screen. She said, "Cell, please repair Tom's car. Make the wheel stick to the car again." The cell made a beep and a flash. Then, the wheel was back on the car. The car could move again. Tom and Sue were happy and clapped. They played with the car and the cell for a long time. But they forgot to clean their toys. The car and the cell became dirty. They had dust, mud, and grass on them. Mom saw their dirty toys and was not happy. She said, "Tom and Sue, you have to clean your toys. They are dirty and can get sick. You have to take care of them." Tom and Sue said, "Sorry, Mom. We will clean our toys. We love them and don't want them to get sick. We will use water, soap, and a cloth to make them clean." They took their toys to the bathroom and washed them. They made them shiny and bright. Mom saw their clean toys and was happy. She said, "Good job, Tom and Sue. You are good children. You know how to repair and clean your toys. You can play with them again. But remember to put them away when you are done." Tom and Sue said, "Thank you, Mom. We will remember. We are happy to have our toys. They are fun and magic. We will take care of them." They hugged Mom and went to play with their toys again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had an original bracelet. Every morning, she wore it on her arm and it sparkled in the sunshine. One day, Lucy wanted to follow a bright, colourful butterfly. She followed the butterfly deep into the forest, away from her home. The butterfly flew in and out of the trees. Lucy ran after it. Everything looked so different, she got a little bit scared. But she kept going, following the butterfly with her bracelet glimmering in the sun. The butterfly stopped at a big, old tree. Lucy looked around and saw a secret cave. She went inside and, whoa! there was the most beautiful, sparkling bracelet. It was exactly like her original bracelet, but this one had colourful gems all around it. Lucy was very happy. She put the new bracelet next to her original bracelet, and ran out of the secret cave to show all her friends. From then on, she always wore both the original bracelet, and the secret one with the gems. Lucy had lots of fun playing and she never forgot to follow her dreams.
