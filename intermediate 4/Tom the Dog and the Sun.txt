Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Dog and the Sun

Once upon a time, on a charming Tuesday, there was a big dog named Tom. Tom loved to play with his little boy, Tim. They played all day long in the sun. One day, Tim had an idea. He wanted to measure Tom to see how big he was. Tim got a long string and put it next to Tom. Tom was very big! Tim and Tom were happy. They played more and more. They had a fun and charming Tuesday. They knew they would always be best friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big piece of cardboard. He wanted to make something fun with it. Tim had an idea! He could make a car from the cardboard. He worked hard all day, cutting and folding the cardboard. After a while, Tim started to feel tired. But he did not want to stop. He wanted to finish the car. Tim's mom saw him working hard. She said, "Tim, you look tired. Why don't you take a break?" Tim smiled and said, "I want to finish my car first. Then I can play with it." He kept working on the cardboard car. Soon, the car was done. Tim was so happy. He had gained a new toy to play with. He forgot he was tired and played with his cardboard car all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Lily. She had a notebook that she loved very much. The notebook was blue and had many pages. One day, Lily wanted to complete her notebook by drawing a big, happy sun. She took her yellow crayon and started to draw. She made a big circle and then added lines all around it. When she was done, Lily showed her mommy the picture. Her mommy smiled and said, "Wow, what a pretty sun!" Lily felt happy and proud that she completed her notebook with a beautiful drawing.

One day, Jake and his mommy were driving down the road. Jake looked out of the window and saw a huge truck filled with fuel. Jake asked his mommy, "What is that truck for?" "That truck is for carrying fuel, honey," replied Mommy. Suddenly, the truck's engine stopped and it came to a halt in the middle of the road. Mommy started to worry when she realized they had no fuel in their car. Mommy said to Jake, "I'm going to ask those kind people if they can help us." She then left the car and approached the truck. Mommy asked the people if they had enough fuel to give them, but the people told her that their truck was out of fuel. Mommy thought for a moment and then realized that a gas station wasn't too far away. Mommy thanked the people for their kindness and promised to come back and repay them when they got their car filled up with fuel. Mommy and Jake made it to the gas station safely and had their car filled with fuel. On the way back home, Mommy promised Jake that she would never let their car run out of fuel ever again. Jake smiled and said, "Thank you, Mommy for your kind help."

Once upon a time, in a big green field, there was a little bird. The bird had a yellow wing and a blue wing. It liked to fly high in the sky and sing songs. The bird was very happy. One day, the bird met a small red bug. The bug was very loud. The bug liked to point at things with its tiny legs. The bird and the bug became friends. They played together in the big green field. The bird showed the bug how to fly with its yellow and blue wings. The bug showed the bird how to point with its tiny legs. They had a lot of fun together. The bird and the bug were the best of friends in the big green field.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had long hair and loved flowers. One day, she saw a sad flower in her garden. It did not bloom like the other flowers. Lily felt sad too. Lily said to the flower, "Why are you so sad? I want to help you bloom like the other flowers." The flower looked up at her and said, "I need more water and sun to grow big and strong." Every day, Lily took care of the flower. She gave it water and made sure it had enough sun. Slowly, the flower started to grow. It grew taller and taller. One day, the flower bloomed! It was big and beautiful. Lily was so happy to see the flower bloom. She danced around the garden with her long hair flowing in the wind. The flower was happy too. It thanked Lily for her help. From that day on, they were the best of friends.
