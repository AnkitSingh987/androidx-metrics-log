Category: Intermediate
Title: The Cat and the Tree

One day, a big cat went for a walk. The big cat was very tired, so it wanted to rest. The big cat saw a nice tree and sat down under it to rest. A little girl saw the big cat and said, "Hello, big cat! Are you hungry? I have some soup." The big cat said, "Yes, I want soup! Thank you, little girl." The little girl gave the big cat some soup to eat. As the big cat ate the soup, something unexpected happened. The big cat started to shrink! The big cat was now a small cat. The little girl laughed and said, "Now you are a small cat, just like my pet!" The small cat was happy and played with the little girl all day.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to play in her yard. She found a big black rock. It was very heavy. Lily wanted to show her friend, Tim, the black rock she found. "Look, Tim!" Lily said. "I found a big black rock! Isn't it cool?" Tim looked at the rock and said, "Wow, that's a big rock! Let's see what's inside!" They took a hammer and hit the rock. The rock broke into pieces and a lot of black powder came out. Lily and Tim were surprised. They did not know what the black powder was. They played with the powder and made funny shapes. Then, a little bird came and ate some of the black powder. The bird turned into a big, colorful bird! Lily and Tim were so happy. They knew they found magic black powder. They played with the bird and the magic powder all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved to go outside and play in the snow. One day, she wanted to go out and play, but she didn't put on any warm clothes. She ran outside and played in the snow for hours and hours, but after a while she started to feel very cold. She snapped herself out of her game and hurried back inside, but not before she realized she had forgotten something very important - her delicate scarf. She remembered how pleased her mum had been when she had taken the time to knit it. The little girl was filled with regret when she thought of how her mum would react to her not wearing her scarf. She wanted to surprise her mum with something else, so she decided to make her a card. It took the little girl many days of planning and drawing to make the perfect card. When she was done, it was full of glitter, stickers, and a big heart. She presented it to her mum and told her what had happened. To her surprise, her mum hugged her and told her that the card was a wonderful gift. This experience taught the little girl the value of appreciating what she already has and to always take the time to prepare warm clothes whenever she plays outside in the snow. She will never forget this lesson and always make sure to do the right thing.

Tom and Mia were friends who liked to play in the woods. One day, they found a big cabin that looked old and scary. They wanted to see what was inside, so they opened the door and went in. The cabin was dark and messy. There were many things on the floor and on the shelves. Tom and Mia saw books, toys, clothes, dishes, and more. They thought it would be fun to organize the things and make the cabin look nice. Tom said, "I will put the books on the shelf. You can put the toys in the box." Mia nodded and said, "OK. And we can put the clothes in the closet and the dishes in the sink." They started to organize the things and had fun. But then, they heard a loud noise. It was a growl. They turned around and saw a big bear in the corner. The bear was angry and hungry. It saw Tom and Mia and wanted to eat them. Tom and Mia were very scared. They ran to the door and tried to open it. But it was locked. They screamed for help and banged on the door. The bear came closer and closer. Will Tom and Mia escape from the scary cabin? Will someone hear them and save them? What will happen to the things they organized? You can imagine the end of the story.
