Category: Intermediate
Title: Daisy the Girl and the Flower

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Daisy. Daisy loved to play in her garden. It had deep, soft dirt that felt nice on her hands. She liked to dig and plant new flowers. One sunny day, Daisy found a special seed. She knew it would grow into a very pretty flower. So, she dug a deep hole and put the seed in it. Every day, Daisy would add water to the seed. She would talk to it and sing songs. She hoped that the seed would grow big and strong. The seed started to grow, and Daisy was very happy. She could not wait to see what kind of flower it would be. One morning, Daisy went to her garden and saw that the seed had grown into a big, beautiful flower. It had bright colors and a sweet smell. All her friends came to see it. They all loved the flower and played in the garden together. Daisy knew that adding water and love to the seed made it grow into the most amazing flower ever.

Billy and his mom were taking a walk. As they walked, they noticed a car parked by a fence. Suddenly, they heard a loud sound coming from the vehicle. They looked in and saw a happy man washing his car. He was glad to see them, so he said hello. Billy's mom said hello too and asked him, "What are you doing?" The man said, "I'm drying my car. I want to make it look nice." He smiled as he said it. Billy was very glad to see him so happy. They watched for a few more minutes as the man dried the car, then moved on. As they continued their walk, Billy smiled and said, "I'm glad the car is drying. Now, it will look really nice!" His mom smiled too and said, "Yes, it will."

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Lucy. She loved to play in her garden with her friends. They liked to dig in the dirt and find all kinds of things, like worms and rocks. But Lucy's favorite thing to find was a radish. She thought they were so pretty and red. One day, Lucy found a big radish and wanted to show it to her friends. She picked it up and ran to them. But as she ran, she tripped and fell. The radish flew out of her hand and landed in the mud. Lucy felt very embarrass. She did not want her friends to see her like this. But then, something unexpected happened. The muddy radish started to wiggle and shake! It turned into a pretty butterfly! The butterfly flew up and landed on Lucy's hand. Her friends saw it and smiled. They all thought it was so pretty and amazing. In the end, Lucy was happy and not embarrass anymore. They played with the pretty butterfly and had a fun day in the garden.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He had a new toy made of wood. It was a car. Tom loved his car very much. He played with it every day. One day, Tom met a girl named Sue. Sue saw Tom's car and said, "Wow, your car is new and made of wood! Can I play with it too?" Tom said, "Yes, you can have a turn." Sue and Tom played with the car together. They had so much fun. They became good friends. From that day on, they always played with the new wood car and shared many happy times.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to play outside. But it started to rain, so he could not go out. The rain made him sad. He wanted to find a way to play outside and stay dry. Tim had a good idea. He asked his dad for help. They worked together to build a big umbrella. The umbrella was so big, it could cover Tim and his toys. They worked hard and had fun. When they finished, Tim went outside with the big umbrella. The rain did not make him wet. He played with his toys and had a great time. Tim was very happy. The rain did not stop him from playing.
