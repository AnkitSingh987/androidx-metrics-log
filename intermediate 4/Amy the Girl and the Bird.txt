Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a fragile toy bird that could sing. Amy loved her bird and would keep it with her all the time. She liked to hear its pretty voice. One day, Amy took her toy bird to the park. She showed her friend, Tim. "Look at my bird," she said. "It can sing." Tim was very happy to see the bird. They played with the bird and listened to its voice. But then, something unexpected happened. A real bird flew down and landed next to Amy's toy bird. The real bird began to sing too! Amy and Tim were so surprised. They laughed and listened to the real bird's voice. Now, Amy had two birds to keep her company and sing for her.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Tom and Sarah, who loved to go to the beach. Every day they grabbed their beach bags and ran to the shore. Today they stopped near a big reef. Tom said, "Wow, this is the perfect spot, let's go in the water!" Sarah said, "Yes, let's go!" They took off their clothes and zipped into the sea. They swam to the reef, which was full of coral and fish. They both had so much fun playing together in the reef. Once they were too tired, they decided to go back to the shore. Tom said, "That was perfect, I can't wait to come back here tomorrow!" Sarah laughed, "Me too!" They put their clothes back on and ran back to the beach.

Once upon a time there was a dog called Spot who lived in a cozy home. Spot was a happy dog who loved to sleep all day, every day. One day, a thief tried to break into Spot's house. Spot was very scared and started to bark loudly. The thief was so scared and ran away. Spot was very brave and kept his home safe. He learned that it was important to stay awake so the thief couldn't hurt him or his home. Spot was very sad that the thief was so mean. But he felt proud of himself for being so brave and protecting his home. Spot learned that it's important to not be afraid and do what's right, even if you're scared.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was very filthy because he loved to play in the mud. One day, Spot saw a big, red ball stuck in a tree. He wanted to rescue the ball, so he tried to jump high to get it. As Spot tried to jump, he felt sick. His tummy hurt, and he knew he needed medicine to feel better. Spot's friend, a kind bird named Tweet, saw him feeling sick. Tweet wanted to help Spot, so she flew away to find some medicine. Tweet found the medicine and brought it back to Spot. But when they looked up at the tree, the big, red ball was gone! The wind had blown it away. Spot took the medicine and felt better. Then, they saw the ball rolling down the hill. Spot and Tweet chased the ball and caught it just in time. They were so happy to rescue the ball and play together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw something in the bushes. It was a fragile skeleton. Tim was surprise. He said, "Oh! What is this?" The fragile skeleton said, "Hi, I am Skelly. I am surprise to see you too. Can you help me find my friends?" Tim said, "Yes, I can help you." Tim and Skelly went to look for Skelly's friends. They looked behind trees and under rocks. Then, they found a big box. They opened the box, and surprise! Skelly's friends were inside. They were all happy and thanked Tim for his help.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a clever girl named Lily. She loved to read and write. One day, she found a book with a poem inside. The poem was about being kind and helping others. Lily wanted to solve a problem for her friends. She thought that if she read the poem to them, they would learn to be kind too. So, she gathered her friends and read the poem out loud. They listened carefully to her words. After Lily read the poem, her friends felt happy and wanted to be kind like the poem said. They started to help each other and share their toys. Everyone in the town learned to be kind because of Lily and her poem. The town became a happier place to live. The moral of the story is that being kind and helping others can make the world a better place.
