Category: Intermediate
Title: The Man and the Magic

Once upon a time, there was an elderly man. He had magic. He could make things change with just a wave of his hand. People in the town were scared of him because they did not understand his magic. One day, a little girl came to the elderly man's house. She was not scared. She asked him to show her the magic. The elderly man was happy. He showed her how he could make flowers grow and animals talk. The little girl loved it. The people in the town saw the little girl with the elderly man. They saw that she was not scared. They thought maybe they should not be scared too. The elderly man decided to surrender his magic to the town. He taught everyone how to use it. The town became a happy place, full of magic. The moral of the story is not to be scared of things you do not understand, but to learn about them and share with others.

Sara was a poor girl who lived with her mom in a small house. They did not have much to eat or wear, but they loved each other very much. Sara liked to read books, but she only had a few old ones on a shelf in her room. One day, Sara saw a big truck outside her house. It was full of boxes with new books for the library. Sara ran to the truck and asked the man, "Can I have a book, please?" The man smiled and said, "Sure, little girl. You can have one book. But you have to give it back when you are done, okay?" Sara nodded and thanked the man. She looked at the boxes and picked one that had a picture of a princess on the cover. She was so happy that she hugged the book and ran back to her house. She showed the book to her mom and said, "Look, mom, I have a new book! The man gave it to me. He said I have to give it back, but I can read it first." Her mom hugged her and said, "That's very nice of him. And it's very nice of you to share the book with the library. You are a good girl, Sara. Now, let's read the book together, shall we?" Sara and her mom sat on the bed and opened the book. They read about the princess and her adventures. Sara felt like she was in a fairy tale. She forgot about being poor and hungry. She was just happy to have a book and a mom who loved her.

Once upon a time, there was a normal boy named Tom. Tom liked to visit his friend, Sue. Sue had a toy that Tom liked a lot. It was a small violin. One day, Tom went to visit Sue to play with the violin. They had fun making music together. They played and laughed all day long. When it was time for Tom to go home, he said goodbye to Sue and the violin. He knew he would visit again soon. Tom and Sue were happy to have each other as friends.

One day, a little fish saw a jellyfish in the sea. The jellyfish could wave its long arms to swim. The little fish was envious. It wanted to wave and swim like the jellyfish. The little fish asked the jellyfish, "Can you teach me how to wave like you?" The jellyfish said, "Yes, but you must be careful." The little fish tried to wave, but it could not. The little fish was sad. It asked a big fish for help. The big fish said, "You are a fish, not a jellyfish. You can swim fast with your tail." The little fish tried and found out it could swim fast too. The little fish was happy. It learned that it was good to be a fish. The moral of the story is to be happy with who you are and not to be envious of others.

Once upon a time, there was a dull stable. In the stable, there was a big horse named Bob. Bob was not happy because he was all alone in the stable. One day, a little girl named Mary came to the stable. She saw Bob and wanted to make him happy. So, she started to clap her hands and sing a song. Bob liked the song and started to feel better. Every day, Mary would come to the stable and clap her hands. Bob and Mary became best friends. They played and laughed together. The stable was not dull anymore. It was now a happy place for Bob and Mary.
