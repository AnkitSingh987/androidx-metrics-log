Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Gift

Once there was a little girl named Mia. She was three years old and loved to play with friends. One day, she was sad because her friends had ignored her at the park. She felt angry and upset. Mia went to find her mom. "Mom, my friends ignored me at the park," she said. Her mom smiled and said, "I have something special for you. It's a gift." Mia was so excited to open the gift. Inside was a beautiful doll. She hugged the doll. Now that she had her special gift, she wasn't angry anymore. The next day at the park, Mia was so happy to see her friends. She wanted to show them her new doll. But this time, her friends had something special for her — they had brought her a surprise gift! Mia was so happy and couldn't believe how much her friends cared. She was grateful that they had not ignored her anymore. From that day on, Mia had lots of laughter and fun with her friends, and never felt angry or ignored again.

Once upon a time, there was a little rabbit named Tim. Tim had a big, envious friend named Ellie the elephant. One day, Tim and Ellie went to play near the big tree. Tim saw a small, shiny thing on the ground. It was a razor. Tim did not know what it was, but he knew it was special. Tim showed the razor to Ellie, and she wanted it too. They both decided to play a game. The winner would get the razor. They played hide and seek. Tim was very good at hiding, but Ellie was big and could not hide well. Tim won the game and got to keep the razor. The next day, Tim and Ellie went to the big tree again. This time, they found a big, shiny box. They opened it and found many razors inside. Ellie was so happy that she trembled with joy. Now, both Tim and Ellie had their own razors, and they were not envious of each other anymore. They played with their new toys and stayed best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there lived a boy named Bob. He was three years old. Every day, Bob would play in his house and have a lot of fun. One day, Bob's mom brought home a new computer. Bob was so excited! He wanted to try it out right away. Bob's mom showed him how to use the computer. She showed Bob how to sign onto the computer by pushing buttons. Bob thought it was really cool! At first, Bob enjoyed playing on the computer. But soon, he found it boring and stopped playing. He would rather be outside running around and having adventures instead of staying in and being bored. So Bob decided to tell his mom that he didn't want to play on the computer anymore. "Mommy," Bob said, "can I play outside instead? Playing on the computer is too boring!" His mom nodded her head and said, "Yes, of course you can, Bob!" Bob smiled, happy that he could now return to his fun outdoor adventures.

Once upon a time, there was a little worm. The worm was very smooth. He lived in a big garden with many friends. One day, the worm wanted to play with his friends. He asked his friends, "Will you let me play with you?" His friends said, "Yes, let's play hide and seek!" The worm was very happy. They started to play, and the worm hid under a smooth rock. He waited for his friends to find him. But, they didn't find him for a long time. Then, something unexpected happened. The worm heard a big sound. It was a bird! The bird picked up the smooth rock to find food. The worm was scared, but then the bird saw him and said, "Hello, little worm! Are you playing hide and seek? I will help you hide better." The bird put the worm in a tall tree. The worm was safe and his friends found him. They all laughed and played together. The worm had a new friend, the bird, and they all lived happily ever after.
