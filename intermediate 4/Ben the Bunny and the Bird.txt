Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben the Bunny and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a large forest, there lived a little bunny named Ben. Ben loved to hop and play all day. One sunny day, while Ben was hopping, he saw a small bird stuck in a gate. The bird chirped, "Help me, please!" Ben wanted to rescue the bird, but he was too small. He thought of his friend, Ellie the elephant. Ellie was large and strong. Ben hopped to find Ellie and told her about the bird in the gate. Ellie said, "Let's go rescue the bird!" Together, they went to the gate. Ellie used her large trunk to gently open the gate. The bird was free! The bird said, "Thank you, Ben and Ellie!" They all smiled and played together for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, there was a polite cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to gather things from around the house. One day, she found a dish in the kitchen. Kitty saw her friend, Doggy, and said, "Look, I found a dish! Let's gather more things and play together!" Doggy agreed and they started to gather more things. But, as they were playing, they knocked over a big cup of water. It made a big mess! Mommy came in and saw the mess. She was not happy. She said, "Kitty and Doggy, you made a big mess! You must clean it up now." Kitty and Doggy felt sad, and they had to clean up the mess instead of playing.

Once upon a time, there was a little car named Zoom. Zoom loved to go fast. He would race down the street and make all the other cars turn their heads. One day, Zoom found a long cable on the ground. He had a fun idea. Zoom tied the cable to a big tree and the other end to his back. He wanted to see how fast he could go with the cable. Zoom started to go faster and faster. He felt fierce, like a big lion. All the other cars and people cheered for him. Zoom was so happy that he could make everyone smile with his fun game. And from that day on, Zoom and the cable were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to sit in the garden and look at the pretty flowers. Her favorite flower was the poppy. The poppy was red and fragile. One sunny day, Lily went to sit by the poppy. She was very careful not to touch the fragile flower. She knew it could break easily. As she sat, a small butterfly came to visit the poppy. The butterfly was yellow and soft. Lily watched the butterfly sit on the poppy. She smiled and felt happy. The butterfly, the poppy, and Lily all enjoyed the sunny day together. And from that day on, Lily knew she would always love the fragile poppy and her garden friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved the color pink. Lily had a pink dress, pink shoes, and a pink room. She was very happy. One day, Lily saw a weak bird in her yard. The bird could not fly. Lily wanted to help the bird. She gave it food and water. The bird started to feel better. Lily wanted to learn how to make the bird strong again. She asked her mom for help. Her mom showed her how to take care of the bird. Soon, the bird was strong and could fly. Lily was so happy to see the bird fly away.

Once upon a time, there was a big fish and a small fish. They were good friends. They lived in a pond. The big fish had a shiny scale. The small fish did not know what a scale was. He was ignorant. One day, the big fish said, "Let's separate for a while and play hide and seek." The small fish agreed. The big fish went to hide, and the small fish started to count. When the small fish finished counting, he started to look for the big fish. The small fish found the big fish behind a rock. He saw the shiny scale and asked, "What is that?" The big fish replied, "It's a scale. All fish have them." The small fish looked at his body and saw his own scales. He was happy to learn something new. From that day on, they played together and learned more about each other.
