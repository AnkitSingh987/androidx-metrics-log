Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam and the Dan

Once upon a time, there were two friends named Sam and Dan. Sam was a three year old little boy with wild brown hair. And Dan was an old museum guard. One day, Sam decided to visit the museum with Dan. As they walked through the museum, Dan showed Sam all of the wonderful objects inside. Suddenly, Sam saw a huge sword on the wall. He asked Dan if he could take it down. "That's much too big," said Dan. "Come with me. I have something that you can cut." Dan led Sam to his office and showed him a small piece of paper. He handed Sam a small pair of scissors and said, "You can use the scissors to cut the paper if you'd like." Sam happily accepted the scissors and began cutting the paper into wild shapes. Dan watched with a smile on his face. Once Sam was done and the paper was completely cut, Dan said, "That's amazing! You have made a unique and wild creation, Sam. Maybe you could become a great artist one day." Sam beamed with delight. He couldn't wait to go back to the museum to show Dan his next masterpiece.

Once upon a time, there was a clever little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys and eat sweet treats. Her favorite treat was a big, yummy lollipop. One day, Lily went to the store with her mom. She saw a lollipop and asked her mom if she could have it. Her mom said yes, and Lily was very happy. She held the lollipop in her hand and couldn't wait to eat it. When they got home, Lily started to open the lollipop. But, oh no! The lollipop fell on the floor and it break. Lily was very sad. Her mom saw what happened and gave her a hug. Then, her mom had a clever idea. She took the broken lollipop and put it in a bowl. She added ice cream and mixed it all up. Lily smiled as she ate her new treat. It was even better than before!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Marie. She always enjoyed playing in the park. She liked to run and twist around in the grass. One day Marie was exploring by the lake when she came upon a peaceful stone. She picked it up, feeling its smooth surface. Marie's friend, Sarah, saw her and asked, "What do you have there?" Marie smiled and said, "This is a peaceful stone. I found it over by the lake. I'm going to keep it." Sarah smiled and said, "You can use it for twisting around. It looks like a lot of fun." Marie nodded and smiled. She knew that she would have lots of fun playing with her peaceful stone.

One day, a boy and his dog went for a walk. They saw a big bull in a field. The bull was very big and strong. The boy and his dog were a little scared, but they wanted to see the bull up close. As they got closer, they saw that the bull was not moving. The boy thought the bull was dead. He was sad and did not know what to do. The dog barked at the bull, trying to wake it up. Suddenly, the bull opened its eyes and stood up. The boy and his dog were very surprised! The bull was not dead, it was just taking a nap. The boy and his dog were happy and ran back home to tell their friends about the big bull they saw.

One day, a modest cat and a dog went for a walk. They liked to admire the trees and flowers. The cat said, "I like the big trees." The dog said, "I like the pretty flowers." As they walked, they saw a box. The box was a shock. They did not know what was in it. The cat said, "Let's open the box." The dog said, "Okay, let's see what's inside." When they opened the box, they found a small bird. The bird was also a shock. The bird said, "Thank you for finding me." The cat and dog were happy to make a new friend. Now, they admired the trees, flowers, and their new friend together.
