Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben and Lily and the Dog

Ben and Lily liked to play in the park. They liked to slide, swing and run. But they did not like the big dog that lived near the park. The big dog was scary. It had sharp teeth and loud bark. Sometimes it chased Ben and Lily when they walked by. One day, Ben and Lily saw something shiny in the grass. They went to look. It was a copper coin. Ben picked it up and showed it to Lily. "Look, Lily, I found a copper. It is pretty and round. Maybe we can buy candy with it," he said. Lily smiled and reached for the copper. But then they heard a growl. The big dog was behind them. It wanted the copper too. It jumped at Ben and Lily. They screamed and ran. The big dog ran after them. "Help, help!" Ben and Lily shouted. "The big dog is chasing us!" A man heard them. He was a policeman. He wore a blue uniform and a hat. He had a whistle and a badge. He saw the big dog and blew his whistle. The big dog stopped and looked at him. The policeman called the big dog. "Here, boy, come here. You are a good dog, aren't you? You don't want to hurt the children, do you?" he said. The big dog wagged its tail and went to the policeman. The policeman petted the big dog and put a leash on it. He smiled at Ben and Lily. "Don't be afraid, kids. This is my dog, Rex. He is not scary. He just likes to play. He thought the copper was a toy. I'm sorry he scared you. Here, you can have the copper back. It is yours," he said. Ben and Lily took the copper and thanked the policeman. They were not scared anymore. They liked Rex now. They petted him and gave him a treat. Rex licked them and wagged his tail. Ben and Lily smiled and went to buy candy with the copper. They were happy. They had a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a rabbit. The rabbit lived in a distant place, far from other animals. The rabbit liked to play, but no one was around to play with him. One day, a big bird came to the rabbit's home. The rabbit asked, "Can I play with you?" The big bird said, "I will allow you to play with me, but only if you can catch me!" The rabbit was very happy and started to chase the big bird. But then, something unexpected happened. The big bird stopped and let the rabbit catch him! The big bird said, "I was lonely too, and I wanted a friend. Now, we can be friends and play together!" The rabbit and the big bird became best friends and played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to play with her toy tea set. One day, she wanted to make her tea set more fun. She had an idea to fill the cups with something new. Amy saw her mom's coffee on the table. She thought it looked like fun to play with. She did not know it was not fake. She filled her toy cups with the coffee. Amy played with her tea set and the coffee. But then, she spilled the coffee on her dress. She was very sad. The coffee made a big mess and her dress was dirty. The fun day turned bad.

Once upon a time there was a tiger who loved candy. All day he would dream about eating candy. One day he decided to go on a hunt to find some. He searched and searched, and then he heard a loud roar. He knew it was a big monster, and he was scared. The monster roared again, and the tiger stepped closer. He saw that the monster was made of candy. It was all wrapped up in a tight package. The tiger was so excited, he couldn't believe his eyes! He carefully opened the package and started to eat the candy. He was so happy, he couldn't stop smiling. The monster roared one more time, but this time in a friendly voice. He said thank you for finding me and eating my candy. The tiger and the monster became friends, and every day the tiger would roar and the monster would give him as much candy as he wanted. They lived happily ever after.
