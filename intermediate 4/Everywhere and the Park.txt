Category: Intermediate
Title: Everywhere and the Park

Once upon a time there was a girl who felt very lonely. Everywhere she looked, she saw people but no one wanted to be her friend. One day she went to the park and saw a doggy in the grass. She went up to the doggy and gave him a gentle touch. The doggy looked at her with kind eyes. The girl gave him a big hug and right away, the doggy understood how she was feeling. From that moment on, they were the best of friends. Whenever the girl felt lonely, she would go to the park and her doggy friend would always be there to keep her company. The girl and doggy would play in the park together, take long walks, and share special moments of cuddles. The girl was so happy to have a friend who could give her the love and care she needed. That was the day the loneliness in her heart disappeared!

Once there was a little girl who didn't understand the world around her. She was just three years old and she wanted to know why things worked the way they did. One day she was out in the garden, and she saw a stone. She had never seen the stone before, and it made her curious. She picked it up and held it in her hands. It felt smooth and warm. She sat down in the garden and tried to understand what the stone was all about. She thought and thought, but she couldn't make sense of it. Then she remembered her mom's advice, to be calm. So the little girl closed her eyes, and sat with the stone. She noticed how calm it made her feel and soon, without really knowing why, the little girl understood what the stone was. It was a special stone that offered her comfort and peace anytime she held it, and she always kept it with her. The little girl smiled, feeling happy that she could now understand the stone.

One day, a small cat named Tim went to play in the park. Tim loved to climb trees. He saw a big tree and thought it would be fun to climb. But Tim was a bit careless. He did not look where he was going. As Tim started to climb, he met a wise old owl. The owl gave Tim a wink and said, "Be careful, little cat. The tree has a secret." Tim did not listen to the owl. He just wanted to climb higher and higher. At the top of the tree, Tim found a big nest with pretty eggs. He wanted to touch them. But then, the mama bird came back. She was mad at Tim for being near her eggs. Tim was scared and climbed down fast. He learned to be more careful and listen to wise friends.

One day, a little gray cat named Tom went outside to play. He saw a long chain on the ground. Tom had an idea. He wanted to design a necklace with the chain. Tom asked his friend, a little bird named Sue, "Do you want to help me design a necklace?" Sue said, "Yes, I would love to help you!" They started to look for pretty things to put on the chain. While they were looking, they found a big shiny key. They put the key on the chain and made it into a necklace. Just then, the key started to glow. It was a magic key! The key made their wishes come true. Tom and Sue were so happy and surprised. They played with the magic key and had lots of fun.

Once, there was a powerful doctor. He could make sick people feel good again. One day, a little boy came to see him. The boy had a big red ball. But the ball was stuck to his hand. The boy could not separate the ball from his hand. The doctor said, "Let me help you." He tried to separate the ball from the boy's hand. But the ball did not come off. The doctor was surprised. This never happened before. The doctor thought and thought. Then, he had an idea. The doctor asked the boy, "Did you say 'please' when you asked the ball to separate?" The boy shook his head. "No, I did not say 'please'." The doctor told the boy to say "please" to the ball. The boy said, "Please, ball, separate from my hand." And just like that, the ball came off. The boy and the doctor were very happy. They learned that saying "please" is very powerful.
