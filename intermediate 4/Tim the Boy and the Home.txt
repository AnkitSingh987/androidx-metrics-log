Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Home

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was an adventurous boy who loved to go on walks. One day, he decided to explore a new road near his house. As Tim walked down the road, he saw a big tree. A little bird was sitting on a branch. The bird said, "Hi, I'm lost. Can you help me find my way home?" Tim said, "Yes, I will help you." They walked together, looking for the bird's home. After a while, they found a small house made of sticks. The bird said, "This is my home! Thank you for helping me!" Tim felt happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The little bird's house started to shake and a big bird came out. The big bird was the little bird's mom! She said, "Thank you for helping my baby come home!" Tim was surprised, but he felt even happier. The birds and Tim became good friends, and they all had many more adventures together.

Once upon a time, there was a big ostrich named Ollie. Ollie lived in a hot place with lots of sun. He had many friends who liked to play with him. They would run and jump all day long. One day, Ollie found a small ball. It was too hot to hold, so he used his big feet to kick it. He showed his friends the hot ball and they all wanted to play with it. But they could not hold it because it was too hot. Ollie's mom saw them and said, "Let the hot ball cool down before you play with it." So they waited, and soon the ball was cool enough to hold. They played with the ball all day and had lots of fun. The moral of the story is to be patient and listen to the advice of others, especially when it comes to safety.

Once upon a time, there was a big whale. The whale lived in a fancy house in the sea. He liked to wear a fancy hat on his head. One day, the whale met a little fish. The fish was lost and needed help. "Excuse me, Mr. Whale," said the fish. "Can you help me? I am lost and I cannot find my mom." The whale wanted to help the fish, so he asked, "Where did you last see her?" The fish tried to answer, but he could not remember. The whale thought for a moment and said, "Let's look for your mom together." They swam around the sea, asking other sea animals if they had seen the fish's mom. Finally, they found her near a big rock. The fish was so happy to see his mom again. The whale felt happy too, because he helped his new friend. From that day on, the whale, the fish, and his mom became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, an old man was in his kitchen. He wanted to create something special. He looked through the cupboards and he grabbed a bag of flour and a jar of sugar. He also found an egg and a piece of butter. The old man started to mix up the ingredients. He felt satisfied with his experience. Suddenly, the old man's daughter walked in. She saw what the old man was making and she said, "Daddy, you are making cookies!" The old man said, "Yes, I thought I would make something for us to share". The daughter felt sad because she had wanted to create something special too. She said to her father, "But I wanted to make the cookies". The old man didn't know what to do. He looked at his daughter and he said, "That's okay. I will make the cookies, but tomorrow we can both make cookies together". He smiled and his daughter smiled back. They both worked together to create the most delicious cookies ever.

One day, a fast dog named Max wanted to race. Max was famous for being fast. He asked his friend, a small cat named Sam, if he wanted to race too. Sam said, "Yes, I want to race!" They started the race, and Max ran very fast. Sam was not as fast as Max, but he tried his best. Then, something unexpected happened. Max saw a stamp on the ground. He stopped to look at it because it was shiny and pretty. Sam kept running. When Max picked up the stamp, he saw Sam cross the finish line. Sam won the race! Max was sad, but Sam said, "It is not about winning. It is about having fun and trying your best." Max learned that being famous and fast is not everything. He was happy to have a friend like Sam who taught him a good lesson. From that day on, Max and Sam raced for fun and always tried their best.
