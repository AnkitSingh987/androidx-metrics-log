Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Cheerful Girl and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful girl named Lily. She loved to paint and listen to jazz music. Every day, she would paint beautiful pictures while the jazz music played. One day, Lily painted a big, happy sun in the sky. She painted green trees and blue birds too. As she painted, the jazz music made her dance and smile. She was so happy. In the end, Lily showed her family the pretty painting. They all smiled and clapped for her. They listened to the jazz music together and danced. Everyone was cheerful and had a great time.

Once upon a time, in a big nation, there was a smart cat named Sam. Sam loved to play with his friend, a little girl named Lily. They played hide and seek, tag, and catch the ball. One day, Sam found a magic hat. When he put the hat on his head, he could vanish! Sam went to Lily and said, "Look, I can vanish now!" He put the hat on and disappeared. Lily looked around, but she could not see Sam. "Wow, Sam! That's so cool!" she said. Sam took off the hat and came back. They both laughed and clapped their hands. Sam and Lily decided to play a new game with the magic hat. They took turns wearing the hat and vanishing. Each time one of them vanished, the other had to find them. It was so much fun! They played all day long and had the best day ever. And from that day on, Sam and Lily had many more magical adventures together.

Once upon a time, there was a little crab named Tim. Tim lived near the sea with his family. Every day, the tide would go in and out, and Tim would play on the beach. One day, Tim saw a girl with a big hat. The girl waved to Tim. Tim felt nervous because he had never seen a girl with a big hat before. He hid behind a rock and watched the girl. The girl came closer to Tim and said, "Hi, little crab! Would you like to play with me?" Tim felt braver and waved his claw back. They played in the sand and had fun until the tide came in. Then, they said goodbye and went home.

Tom and Jen were hungry. They wanted to make some soup. They asked their mom if they could use the stove. Their mom was busy on the phone. She said yes, but she told them to be careful and not to touch the hot pot. Tom and Jen went to the kitchen. They found a can of soup and a pot. They opened the can and poured the soup into the pot. They put the pot on the stove and turned it on. They waited for the soup to boil. They heard a loud noise from the living room. They ran to see what it was. Their dad had come home from work. He was deaf, so he did not hear the phone or the stove. He was angry because he saw their mom talking on the phone. He thought she was talking to someone else. He yelled at her and threw the phone on the floor. Tom and Jen were scared. They wanted to stop their dad. They forgot about the soup. They did not hear the soup bubbling and spilling over the pot. They did not smell the soup burning and smoking. They did not see the fire starting on the stove. The fire grew bigger and bigger. It reached the curtains and the cabinets. It filled the kitchen with smoke and flames. Tom and Jen did not notice. They were too busy trying to calm their dad. Their mom did not notice. She was too busy crying and covering her ears. Nobody noticed until it was too late. The fire alarm did not help. It was broken. Nobody heard it. Nobody reminded them of the stove. Nobody saved them. The fire burned them all.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to dance. She would dance on the ground all day long. One day, Sue went to the park to dance. She felt very happy. While Sue was dancing, she saw a weak, little bird on the ground. The bird could not fly. Sue stopped dancing and went to help the bird. She picked it up and hugged it. The bird started to feel better. Then, something unexpected happened. The weak bird started to dance too! Sue and the bird danced together on the ground. They became best friends and danced happily ever after.
