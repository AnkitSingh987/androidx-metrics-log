Category: Intermediate
Title: Trusty the Light and Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a light and happy bird named Trusty. Trusty loved to fly high in the sky and sing sweet songs. Every day, Trusty would fly and play with his friends. He always had a good time. One day, Trusty saw a big tree with a hole. He thought it would be a fun place to play. Trusty told his friends, "I will go in the tree and see what is inside." His friends were scared, but they trusted Trusty to be brave and safe. Trusty went into the tree and found a shiny, pretty thing. It was a little bell. Trusty took the bell and showed it to his friends. They all loved the bell and took turns playing with it. Trusty was happy he went into the tree, and his friends were happy they trusted him. From that day on, they had even more fun with the bell, and they knew they could trust Trusty to find more good things to play with.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom liked to collect things. One day, he found a bean. The bean was small and green. Tom thought it was special. Tom wanted to find more beans. He looked in the garden. It was difficult to find them. But Tom did not give up. He looked under the leaves and behind the flowers. Soon, he found more beans. Tom was happy with his beans. He put them in a jar. He showed his mom and dad. They were proud of him. Tom learned that even when things are difficult, he can still do them if he tries hard.

Once upon a time, there was a graceful bird named Bella. She lived in a big tree with many other birds. They all loved to fly and sing together. They were a happy network of friends. One day, Bella saw a small bird who was sad. This bird was not part of their network. Bella asked, "Why are you sad?" The small bird said, "I am not good at flying like you, and I have no friends." Bella felt bad for the small bird. Bella decided to help the small bird. She taught the bird how to fly better and introduced her to the network of friends. Soon, the small bird was happy and loved by all. The moral of the story is to always help others and share your love with them.

One day, a green frog went to the park. He saw a bike with a pedal. The frog wanted to ride the bike. He jumped on the bike and started to pedal. A little girl saw the frog on the bike. She stared at the frog and said, "Hi, frog! Can I ride with you?" The frog nodded and the girl got on the bike too. They pedaled together, going fast and having fun. As they rode, they saw a big hill. The frog and the girl pedaled up the hill. When they got to the top, they saw a big surprise. A party was happening with all their friends! The frog and the girl joined the party and had a great time.

Once there was a family of birds. They were looking for a new home and flew over a big field. Then, they spotted a chimney! The birds flew closer and saw that the chimney was empty. "We can live here!" exclaimed the mum bird. The three little birds were excited and started to fly around in circles. They were so happy! The birds quickly started to build their new home. They carried twigs in their beaks and speed around the chimney. "I'm the fastest!" shouted the youngest bird. "No, I'm the fastest!" said the middle bird. The mum and dad watched proudly as their little birds raced around. It was wonderful to see their enthusiasm. Eventually, their nest was complete! They cosied up inside and were so warm and content. They had found the perfect place to call home.

Once upon a time, there was a boy called Jack. Jack was very serious about growing his muscles. Every day, he woke up and ate yummy food to make his muscles strong. Then he played outside, running and jumping to make them bigger. When the sun went down, he would do special exercises just for his muscles. Jack worked hard every single day, and his muscles started to grow. They got bigger and bigger, until he was really strong. His friends were so impressed and always asked him how he could make his muscles so big. He would just smile and tell them that he was being very serious and working hard. Soon all of his hard work paid off. Jack's muscles were now so strong that he could do things that he never thought he could do before. He could lift heavy things, play harder games and run faster than ever before. Jack was now so proud of his muscles, and his friends all loved his muscles too! The end.
