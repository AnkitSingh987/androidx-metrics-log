Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Seat

One day, a girl named Amy went to the store with her mom. They wanted to buy a new seat for their home. Amy was very happy because she liked to sit on new things. At the store, they saw a modern seat that was very pretty. Amy said, "Mom, I like this seat. Can we buy it?" Her mom said, "Yes, we can buy this seat. It will look nice in our home." When they got home, they put the new seat in the living room. But then, something unexpected happened! The seat started to talk! It said, "Hello, I am a talking seat. I can tell you stories and sing songs." Amy and her mom were very surprised, but they liked their new talking seat. They all became good friends and had lots of fun together.

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk in the woods. The sun was shining and the sky was clear. Mia saw a big tree and wanted to play hide and seek. She hid behind the tree and started to peek out. Mia saw a witch walking in the woods. The witch was looking for her lost cat. Mia wanted to help the witch, so she said, "I can help you find your cat!" The witch smiled and said, "Thank you, Mia!" They looked and looked, but they could not find the cat. Mia felt sad. Then, they heard a soft "meow" from the big tree. Mia peeked behind the tree and saw the cat! Mia and the witch were very happy. They became good friends and played together in the woods.

Once there was a boy who lived in a small house with his parents. He was three years old and loved playing in his house. Every now and again, his parents put him in a small chamber which was very dark and ugly. He hated going into the chamber, and would cry whenever his parents made him. One day, his parents were talking to him and said "Every time you go into that chamber, you must remember how much we love you. Even when it's dark and ugly, we love you and want the best for you." The boy was sad but he remembered his parents' words and stopped crying. The next time he went into the chamber, he didn't cry, and instead thought about the love his parents had for him. The moral of this story is that even in dark and ugly places, it's important to remember that family and friends always love you.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to bake with her mom. One day, Mia's mom said, "We need to make a cake for your dad's birthday. It is important." Mia was very excited to help. In the kitchen, Mia's mom showed her the recipe. Mia saw that they needed a teaspoon of something. She had to find the teaspoon to complete the cake. Mia looked and looked, but she could not find it. She felt sad. Then, Mia remembered that she had seen the teaspoon in her toy box. She ran to her room and found it. Mia brought the teaspoon back to her mom, and they completed the cake. When dad came home, he was so happy to see the cake. Mia felt proud that she had helped make the important cake for her dad.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his aunt. They played with a big red ball. They had a lot of fun. But then, Tim kicked the ball too hard and it went far away. Tim was sad because he thought he might lose the ball. Tim's aunt said, "Don't worry, I will find the ball for you." She looked and looked, but she could not find the ball. Tim felt sad. Then, something unexpected happened. A dog came running with the ball in its mouth. The dog was very big and looked dangerous. But the dog was not dangerous at all. It was very friendly. The dog gave the ball back to Tim and his aunt. They were so happy! Tim and his aunt played with the dog and the ball for the rest of the day. They had even more fun than before. And Tim learned that sometimes, things that look scary can be very nice.
