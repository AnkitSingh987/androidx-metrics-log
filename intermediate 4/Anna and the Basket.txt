Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna and the Basket

Anna liked to help her mom with the laundry. She learned how to fold the clothes and put them in the drawers. She was very good at folding blouses, pants, socks and shirts. She felt happy when her mom smiled and said, "Thank you, Anna, you are a big help!" One day, Anna saw a new blouse in the laundry basket. It was pink and had flowers on it. Anna thought it was very pretty. She wanted to try it on and see how it looked on her. She took the blouse out of the basket and put it over her dress. She looked in the mirror and smiled. She felt like a princess. She forgot about the laundry and went to play with her toys. She had fun with her dolls, her blocks and her books. She did not hear her mom calling her name. She did not see her mom looking for the blouse. She did not notice her mom getting angry and sad. When her mom found her in her room, she saw the blouse on Anna. She was very upset. She said, "Anna, what did you do? This is not your blouse. This is a gift for your aunt. You ruined it with your dress and your toys. How could you be so selfish and careless?" Anna felt very bad. She did not mean to make her mom angry and sad. She did not know the blouse was a gift. She just wanted to look pretty. She said, "I'm sorry, mom. I did not know. I just liked the blouse. I wanted to help you with the laundry. I can fold the blouse and put it back in the basket." Her mom said, "No, Anna, you can't. The blouse is dirty and wrinkled. You can't give it to your aunt like this. You have to learn to respect other people's things. You have to ask before you take something that is not yours. You have to be careful with your clothes and your toys. You have to be responsible and honest." Anna nodded. She understood. She said, "I'm sorry, mom. I will be more careful. I will ask before I take something. I will be responsible and honest. I love you, mom." Her mom hugged her. She said, "I love you, too, Anna. I forgive you. But you have to help me fix the blouse. We have to wash it and iron it and wrap it. We have to make it look nice for your aunt. Will you help me?" Anna said, "Yes, mom. I will help you. I want to make you happy. I want to make aunt happy. I want to be a big help." They went to the laundry room and worked together. They washed the blouse and ironed it and wrapped it. They made it look nice for aunt. They felt happy and proud. They learned a lesson. They learned to be careful, respectful, responsible and honest. They learned to say sorry and to forgive. They learned to love each other.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who liked to whistle. One day, the girl went outside to play. She found a bean and, looking up at the sky, she started to whistle a happy tune. The sun was out and the sky was so blue and the girl felt peaceful. As she whistled, something exciting happened! A bird flew above her. It was whistling too! The little girl smiled and kept on whistling. The bird flew away, but the girl was still whistling happily with the sun on her face. The girl then put away the bean and decided to go for a walk in the park. Everywhere she walked, she heard the birds whistling and it sounded lovely. The girl was so happy that she started to whistle again. The girl walked until she was tired and decided to sit down. She found a grassy spot and, looking up, watched the birds fly in the sky. She closed her eyes and sat still in the peaceful sunshine. She listened to the birds whistling and felt content and very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big billboard in the town. The billboard had a picture of a happy man with a big smile. One day, a little boy named Tim saw the billboard and wanted to write on it. Tim asked his friend, Sam, "Can I write on the billboard?" Sam, who was ignorant, said, "Yes, go ahead!" So, Tim took his crayon and wrote his name on the billboard. Suddenly, the happy man on the billboard came to life! He looked at Tim and said, "Thank you for writing on me. Now, I will give you a surprise!" The happy man gave Tim a big bag of candy. Tim shared the candy with Sam, and they both learned that it is important to be kind and share with others. The moral of the story is to always be kind and share, even when you don't know what will happen next.
