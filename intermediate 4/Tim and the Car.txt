Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Car

One sunny day, a little boy named Tim was troubled. Today, he had to choose a toy to bring to school. He looked at his toy box and saw many toys. There were cars, dolls, and balls. He did not know which one to pick. Tim asked his mom, "Mom, can you help me choose a toy?" His mom smiled and said, "Why not take the car? You love to play with it." Tim thought for a moment and said, "Okay, I will take the car." At school, Tim showed his friends the car he chose. They all played together and had lots of fun. Tim was happy he picked the car. Today was a good day, and Tim was not troubled anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to decorate his room with his favorite things. He had a big number on his wall because he was three years old. One day, Tim found a naughty cat in his room. The cat was making a mess and tearing down Tim's decorations. Tim was sad and didn't know what to do. He tried to make the cat go away, but the cat just kept being naughty. Then, something unexpected happened. The naughty cat found a toy and started to play with it. The cat wasn't naughty anymore! Tim was happy, and they became friends. They played together and decorated the room with even more fun things. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a nice bird named Betty. Betty was an attractive bird with pretty blue feathers. She lived in a big tree in a beautiful park. Betty liked to eat fruits, and her favorite fruit was an orange. One day, Betty met another bird named Bobby. Bobby was a friendly bird with bright red feathers. They talked and played together. "Do you like oranges?" asked Betty. "Yes, I do!" said Bobby. They both laughed and shared a yummy orange. Betty and Bobby decided to settle in the big tree together. They built a warm nest and lived happily. Every day, they ate oranges and played in the park. They became best friends and enjoyed their simple life.

One day, Jenny went outside and noticed a big, fluffy cloud in the sky. She was excited, and wanted to explore it. She grabbed her dad's hand and asked him if they could go up to the cloud. Her dad smiled and said, "No, Jenny. We can't go to the cloud." Jenny frowned and asked, "Why not, Daddy?" Her dad looked guilty, and said, "Well, the cloud is far away, and we can't reach it." Jenny felt sad, but then she noticed another cloud, close by. She gave her dad a huge hug and said, "Look, Dad! This cloud is close by. Let's go look at it!" Her dad smiled, and said, "Yes, let's go look at the other cloud." The two of them ran to the nearby cloud and enjoyed the view from below. They were both so happy!

Anna and Ben like to play on the beach. They run and jump and make sand castles. They also look for shells. Shells are pretty and shiny and have different shapes and colors. One day, Anna finds a big shell. It is white and pink and has a spiral. She is very happy and shows it to Ben. Ben likes the shell too. He wants to take it and play with it. He says, "Can I have the shell, Anna? Please?" Anna thinks for a moment. She loves the shell, but she also loves Ben. She says, "OK, Ben. You can have the shell. But you have to be careful. The shell is fragile and precious. Don't break it or lose it." Ben smiles and says, "Thank you, Anna. You are very kind. I will be careful. I promise." He takes the shell and holds it gently. He feels the smooth and bumpy surface. He puts it to his ear and listens. He hears a soft sound, like the sea. Anna and Ben play with the shell. They pretend it is a boat, a hat, a horn, and a treasure. They have a lot of fun. They also share the shell with their friends. They show them how graceful and beautiful it is. They all admire the shell and say, "Wow!"
