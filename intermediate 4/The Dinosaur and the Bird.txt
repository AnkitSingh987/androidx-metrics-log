Category: Intermediate
Title: The Dinosaur and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big dinosaur. The dinosaur was green and had a long tail. The dinosaur was very patient. It liked to wait and watch the other animals play. One day, the dinosaur met a little bird. The bird said, "Hello, Mr. Dinosaur! Do you want to play with me?" The dinosaur was happy and said, "Yes, I want to play with you." They played together all day. The bird showed the dinosaur how to bow. They bowed to each other and laughed. They had so much fun. At the end of the day, the dinosaur and the bird were very tired. They said, "Goodbye" and went to sleep.

Once upon a time, there was an incredible octopus named Ollie. Ollie lived in the big blue sea. He had many friends, but his best friend was a little fish named Fin. One day, Fin was very sad. A big mean shark took his favorite toy. Ollie saw his friend cry and said, "Do not cry, Fin. I promise I will help you get your toy back." Ollie and Fin swam to the shark's home. They saw the toy inside. Ollie used his long arms to grab the toy. The shark saw them and was angry. "Why did you take my new toy?" he asked. Ollie said, "This toy is not yours. It is Fin's toy. You made him sad. Promise you will not take things that are not yours." The shark thought for a moment and said, "I promise." From that day on, the shark was nice to everyone. Ollie, Fin, and the shark became good friends. They played together in the big blue sea, and everyone was happy.

One day, a girl named Sue went for a walk. She saw a beautiful sunrise. The sky was full of pretty colors like pink, orange, and yellow. Sue felt happy looking at the sunrise. On her walk, Sue met a silly dog. The dog was jumping and barking. Sue said, "Hi, silly dog! Why are you so loud?" The dog said, "I lost my toy. I am sad." Sue wanted to help the dog find his toy. Sue and the silly dog looked for the toy together. They looked under the trees and behind the bushes. Finally, they found the toy near a big rock. The dog was happy and said, "Thank you, Sue!" They played together until the sun went down.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived an adorable little girl named Lily. She had a pet cat named Snowy. They loved to play and have fun together. Lily liked to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, Lily's mom asked her to help prepare dinner. Lily was so excited! She put on her apron and got ready to cook. Her mom showed her how to make rice. Lily watched carefully and learned how to wash the rice and put it in a pot. Lily and her mom cooked the rice together. When it was done, they put it on plates for the family to eat. Lily felt so proud of what she had done. She knew that she had helped her mom and learned something new. And the rice was very yummy!

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy magnet. The magnet was not big and it was cheap. Tim liked to play with the magnet. He would clap when the magnet picked up things. One day, Tim saw a big tree. There was a shiny thing on the tree. Tim wanted the shiny thing. He tried to reach it, but he was too small. He was sad. Tim thought about his magnet. He had an idea. He went to get his magnet. He threw the magnet to the shiny thing. The magnet stuck to it. Tim pulled the magnet and the shiny thing came down. Tim was so happy that he clapped. The magnet had helped him get the shiny thing from the tree.

Once upon a time, there was a clever dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his toy ball. One day, he lost his ball in a big bush. Spot was very sad. Spot had an idea. He found some tape in his house. He put the tape on a stick. He called his friend, the cat, to help him. The cat was named Mittens. Spot and Mittens used the tape on the stick to get the ball out of the bush. They were very happy. Spot and Mittens played with the ball all day. They were very clever friends.
