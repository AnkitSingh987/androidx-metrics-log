Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Toys and the Jack

Once upon a time, there was a big, cheerful hall. In the hall, there were many kids playing and having fun. One day, a little girl named Lily came to the hall to play with her friends. Lily saw a boy named Jack who was sad. She went to him and said, "Hi Jack, do you want to play with me?" Jack looked at her and smiled. "Yes, Lily, I would love to play with you." Lily and Jack played together all day. They laughed and had a great time. Lily was very kind and always offered to share her toys with Jack. They became best friends and the hall was always full of joy and laughter. From that day on, Jack was never sad again.

Once upon a time, there was a colorful bird. The bird loved to work in the sun. One day, the bird saw a big box of chocolate. The bird wanted to eat the chocolate, but a big cat saw it too. The cat said, "I want the chocolate!" The bird said, "No, I saw it first!" They did not want to share. They started to fight over the chocolate. Then, a big wind came. The wind blew the box of chocolate away. Both the bird and the cat were sad. They learned that fighting is not good.

One day, a pilot named Tom went for a walk. He liked to wander in the park. The park was big and fun. Tom saw many trees, flowers, and kids playing. In the park, Tom met a tough dog. The dog was big and strong. The dog said, "Woof! Woof!" Tom was not scared. He said, "Hello, dog! Do you want to play?" The dog liked Tom. They played all day. They ran, jumped, and laughed. The sun was bright and warm. Tom and the dog were happy. They became good friends. And that is how the pilot named Tom met his new tough dog friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was very obedient. One day, Lucy was feeling happy and so she decided to skip around the house. She skipped over the couch and spun around in circles. Lucy's mom saw her and said, "Lucy! You are so obedient." Lucy smiled and said, "I am!" Then she kept skipping around the room. Lucy's mom smiled and gave her a big hug. "Go ahead, keep skipping! It's so much fun!" Lucy was delighted and kept skipping around the room. She jumped over the couch and spun around in circles. Lucy felt so happy and she knew she was being very obedient. She could feel the love of her mom all around her and when she was finished, she hugged her mom and said, "Thank you!"

Once, there was a little girl named Laura. She lived on a small farm with her family. On the farm, there was a big field filled with beautiful, green grass. One day Laura decided to take her tricycle and ride it around the field. She was so happy to be riding that she laughed and sang. As she was riding in the middle of the field, she saw something shiny out of the corner of her eye. She stopped and looked closer. It was a huge bubble! Laura was excited and ran over to the bubble. She tried to touch the bubble, but it kept popping away. Finally, she was able to grab it! It felt dry and smooth in her hands. She then shouted "I caught a bubble!" Her mom heard and came running out. She said, "Oh, that's so nice! Now, why don't you go back and ride your tricycle around the field?" And so Laura did. She enjoyed riding around the field in the sunshine. She was so happy that she had caught a bubble, too.

Once upon a time, there was a large dog named Bob. Bob loved to play catch with his friends. One day, Bob and his friends found a flag in the park. The flag was red and blue. They decided to play a new game with the flag. Bob and his friends made two teams. The first team had to catch the flag from the other team. The team that caught the flag would win. They all played and had fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A small cat came and took the flag away from them. The cat ran very fast and Bob and his friends could not catch her. They all stopped and looked at each other. They decided to work together to get the flag back. They made a plan and followed the cat. Finally, they caught the cat and got the flag back. The moral of the story is that when we work together, we can solve problems and have more fun. Even though something unexpected happened, Bob and his friends learned the value of teamwork. And from that day on, they always played together and helped each other.
