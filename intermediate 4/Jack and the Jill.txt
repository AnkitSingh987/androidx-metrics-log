Category: Intermediate
Title: Jack and the Jill

Once upon a time, there were two friends named Jack and Jill. They were happy and healthy, and spent every day together. However, one day they started to quarrel. "You take the axe and chop the wood," said Jack, pointing at the axe. Jill was angry. "No, you do it!", she said. They argued for hours, until neither of them wanted to do it. Suddenly, they heard a voice from behind them. "You can both chop the wood together," said the voice. It was Jack and Jill's mum. Jack and Jill looked at each other, and then started to laugh. "Yeah, let's chop the wood," said Jill. So Jack and Jill grabbed the axe and chopped the wood together. They were happy and healthy, and were best friends again.

One day, Tom and his mom went on a safari. They saw many big animals like lions, elephants, and giraffes. Tom was very excited. Before they went into the deep part of the safari, Tom's mom said, "Tom, let me tie your shoes so you don't trip." Tom let his mom tie his shoes, and then they went deeper into the safari. As they walked, they met a friendly monkey. The monkey said, "Hello, Tom! Welcome to the safari!" Tom and his mom talked with the monkey and had a fun day on their safari adventure.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim went to the zoo with his mom. They saw many animals like lions, monkeys, and bears. Tim liked the zoo a lot. Tim saw a big, tough monkey. The monkey looked angry. Tim's mom said, "Tim, be careful. Don't go too close." But Tim didn't listen. He wanted to be friends with the monkey. Tim went close to the monkey and said, "Hi, monkey! Let's be friends." The monkey didn't like that. The monkey didn't want to surrender and be friends. The monkey was too tough. The monkey got mad and threw a banana at Tim. Tim cried and was very sad. His mom was sad too. They had to leave the zoo. Tim learned that he should listen to his mom and not go too close to the tough animals.

Lily is a little girl who likes to play outside. She likes to run, jump, and swing on the playground. She likes to see the birds, the flowers, and the sun. She is happy when she plays outside. But one day, Lily feels sick. She has a cough, a fever, and a sore throat. She does not want to play outside. She wants to stay in bed. Her mom says she needs to rest and drink water. She says Lily has a cold and needs to get healthy. Lily is sad. She misses playing outside. She misses her friends. She asks her mom when she can go out again. Her mom says she needs to wait until she feels better. She says playing outside is good, but staying inside is good too. She says staying inside helps Lily heal and helps other people not get sick. Lily understands. She knows her mom loves her and wants her to be healthy. She knows she needs to stay in bed and rest. She hugs her mom and says thank you. She says she loves her mom and hopes she gets well soon. Her mom smiles and says she loves her too. She says they can play outside together when Lily is healthy. Lily smiles and closes her eyes. She dreams of playing outside.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had a pet dog named Max. They loved to play together. Lily would throw a ball, and Max would run to get it. They had so much fun. One day, Lily wanted to have a party with her friends. She made a list of things to include for the party. She wanted to have cake, balloons, and games. Lily was very excited to have her friends come over. But, there was a terrible rain storm on the day of the party. Lily was sad because her friends could not come. But then, she had an idea. She decided to have a party with her pet dog, Max. They played games and ate cake together. Even though it was a rainy day, Lily and Max had a great time.
