Category: Intermediate
Title: The Great Tree of Tim and the Bird

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk in the woods. He saw a big, thick tree. Tim wanted to climb the tree, so he began to climb up. As he climbed, Tim met a talking bird. The bird said, "Hi, Tim! I urge you to go back down. This tree has a secret." Tim was scared, but he wanted to know the secret. He kept climbing up the tree. When Tim got to the top of the tree, he saw a big nest. Inside the nest were shiny, gold eggs! The bird flew up and said, "These are magic eggs. They can make your wishes come true. But you must share them with others." Tim was happy and he promised to share the magic eggs with his friends.

Once upon a time there were two friends who were walking through a forest. It was very peaceful and the trees and leaves were whispering in the wind. Suddenly, one of the friends saw a picture on the ground and said, "Look! What a beautiful picture!" The other friend asked, "What does it show?" The first friend bent down and picked up the picture. It was a beautiful painting of a lake, with trees reflecting in the water. The other friend couldn't wait to see the picture so he grabbed it from the first friend. But in his enthusiasm he pinched the corner of the picture and caused a tear in it! The first friend was so sad. He said, "Now the picture is ruined and it can never be the same again." But the other friend knew just what to do. He found a patch of grass by the lake and the two of them carefully glued the picture onto the grass. Then, they admired the picture reflecting in the peaceful lake.

One day, a little girl named Amy went for a walk. She saw a pretty rose in a garden. The rose was red and smelled nice. Amy wanted to become friends with the rose. She said, "Hi, Rose! Can we be friends?" The rose said, "Yes, we can be friends, Amy." So, they played together every day. They had fun in the sun, and when it was cool, they would sit and talk. One day, it rained very hard. Amy couldn't go outside to see the rose. She was sad and missed her friend. The rain didn't stop for many days. When the rain stopped, Amy went to see the rose. But the rose was not there. It had become all brown and broken. Amy cried and said, "I miss you, Rose." Amy learned that sometimes, things don't last forever. She was sad, but she knew she would always remember her friend, the rose.

One day, a big bird had a basket. The basket was high in a tree. The bird liked the basket a lot. It was her home. A little cat saw the basket. The cat wanted to play with it. The cat tried to climb the tree, but the tree was too high. The cat was sad. Then, a nice dog appeared. The dog wanted to help the cat. The dog had an idea. The dog stood on its back legs, and the cat climbed on the dog. Now, the cat could reach the basket. They were happy and played together. The bird was happy too because she made new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a brilliant little bird. This bird loved to fly high in the sky. One day, the bird saw a shiny metal on the ground. The bird wanted to show it to all its friends. The bird picked up the metal with its beak and flew to find its friends. The bird's friends saw the shiny metal and were very happy. They all wanted to play with it. They encouraged each other to take turns holding the shiny metal. At the end of the day, the brilliant bird and its friends were tired but happy. They had a lot of fun playing with the shiny metal. They knew that they would always encourage each other and be the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy who was very adventurous. He liked doing new things and exploring new places. One night, he was looking up at the sky and he saw a star twinkling in the night sky. He got an idea. He said to himself, "I'm going to release the star!" So he quickly started packing a little bag with all the things he would need to make it happen. When he was ready, he walked up to the star and said, “I'm ready, star! Let's go!” He then reached into his pocket and pulled out a string. He tied it around the star and then stepped back. With a really big smile, he released the star. The star flew away and the little boy felt really happy. He had just done something really adventurous which not many people get to do. The end.
