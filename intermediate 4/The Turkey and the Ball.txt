Category: Intermediate
Title: The Turkey and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a big turkey. The turkey lived on a farm with many friends. The turkey was very soft and kind to everyone. All the animals liked the soft turkey. They played together every day. One day, the soft turkey found a ball. The ball was big and round. The turkey and the other animals wanted to play with the ball. They kicked the ball and ran after it. They were all very happy. But then, the ball hit a tree. The tree was very big and strong. The ball broke into pieces. The soft turkey and the other animals were sad. They could not play with the broken ball anymore. But they knew they could still play other games together. So they all hugged the soft turkey and went to play a new game. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a barber named Tom felt very tired. He yawned a big yawn. His friend, the baker named Sam, saw him yawn. Sam felt jealous. He wanted to yawn too, but he could not. Sam thought, "Why can't I yawn like Tom?" He felt sad. He wanted to find a way to yawn. So, Sam went to Tom and asked, "How do you yawn?" Tom said, "I yawn when I am sleepy. Maybe you can try to be sleepy too." Sam tried to be sleepy, but he could not yawn. He was still jealous of Tom. One day, Sam worked very hard. He baked many cakes and bread. At the end of the day, Sam felt very tired. He yawned a big yawn. He was not jealous of Tom anymore. They both laughed and yawned together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to paint. She had many colors of paint to use. Sue was very patient when she painted. She liked to make pretty pictures. One day, Sue decided to paint a picture of her cat, Tom. She took out her paint and started to paint Tom. Tom was a very patient cat. He sat very still while Sue painted him. While Sue was painting, she heard a sound. She looked around and saw a little bird peek through the window. The bird was very curious about Sue's painting. Sue smiled and kept painting. The bird watched Sue paint Tom. When Sue finished her painting, the bird flew away, happy to have seen such a pretty picture.

One day, a big orange pumpkin sat in the yard. It was icy and cold outside. The pumpkin felt very cold too. A little boy named Tim saw the pumpkin and said, "Brrr, you must be very cold, pumpkin!" Tim had an idea. He went inside his house and got a warm hat for the pumpkin. He put the hat on the pumpkin and said, "There you go, now you won't be cold anymore!" Just then, a big wind came and the pumpkin started to roll. It went zoom, zoom, zoom down the hill! Tim ran after the pumpkin, but it was too fast. The pumpkin hit a big rock and it broke into many pieces. Tim was sad, but then he saw something shiny inside the pumpkin. It was a key! Tim knew this was a special key and he was excited to find out what it would unlock.

One day, Tim saw a big box in the living room. He did not recognize it. It was a television! His mom said, "We have a new thing to watch fun shows on." Tim and his mom sat down to watch a show. The television was very loud! Tim's mom turned down the sound. "Is that better, Tim?" she asked. Tim nodded and smiled. They watched a funny show about a dog and a cat who were friends. The next day, Tim's friend Sam came over to play. Tim told Sam about the television. Sam got very excited. "Can we watch the dog and cat show?" he asked. Tim and Sam watched the show together, but this time, it was not too loud. They laughed and had fun.

Lily was a big girl who liked to do things by herself. She was independent and did not need help from anyone. She could dress herself, brush her teeth, and make her own toast for breakfast. One morning, she went to the kitchen and put some bread in the toaster. She waited for it to pop up, but it did not. She pushed the lever down again, but still nothing happened. She looked at the toaster and saw that it was not plugged in. She tried to reach the plug, but it was too high for her. She decided to ignore the toaster and find something else to eat. She opened the fridge and saw some cheese and jam. She took them out and looked for a knife. She could not find one, so she used her fingers to spread the cheese and jam on the bread. She made a big mess, but she did not care. She was hungry and proud of her toast. She took a bite and smiled. It was yummy. She heard her mom coming down the stairs and ran to show her. She said, "Look, Mom, I made toast by myself!" Her mom saw the toast, the cheese, the jam, and the unplugged toaster. She was not happy. She said, "Lily, what did you do? You made a big mess and you wasted the cheese and jam. And you ignored the toaster. You could have burned yourself or the house. You need to ask for help when you do things like this. You are not as independent as you think." Lily felt sad and sorry. She did not mean to make her mom angry. She said, "I'm sorry, Mom. I just wanted to make toast by myself. I did not know the toaster was not working. I will ask for help next time." Her mom hugged her and said, "It's okay, Lily. I know you are a big girl and you want to do things by yourself. But sometimes you need to listen and learn from others. And you need to be careful and clean up after yourself. I love you, Lily. Now, let's make some real toast together."
