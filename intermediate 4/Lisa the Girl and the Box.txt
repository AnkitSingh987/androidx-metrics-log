Category: Intermediate
Title: Lisa the Girl and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lisa. Lisa loved to listen to her mom read stories. One day, Lisa found a filthy old box in her room. "Mom, what is this?" she asked. Her mom looked at the box and said, "Oh, that is a box of stamps. We can use them to make pretty pictures." Lisa was excited and they started to make pictures with the stamps. As they were playing, one of the stamps started to talk! "Hello, Lisa and Mom!" it said. Lisa and her mom were very surprised. The talking stamp helped them make the most beautiful pictures ever. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, Tom and his dad went on a trip. They needed to drive to the airport. Tom was happy, but his dad was grumpy. Dad did not like long drives. On the way, they saw a big cow. The cow was in the road. "Moo!" said the cow. Dad was grumpy and said, "Get out of the way, cow!" Tom laughed and said, "Hello, cow!" The cow moved, and they kept driving. Tom's dad was still grumpy. Tom wanted to make his dad happy. He sang a song. Dad smiled and sang with him. They had fun and soon, they were at the airport.

Once upon a time, in a land of different fruits, there lived a peach named Pippa. Pippa was a happy peach who loved to play with her friends in the sun. One day, Pippa heard a little voice. "Hello, Pippa!" said the voice. Pippa looked around and saw a tiny ant. "Hi, ant!" Pippa said with a big smile. The ant smiled back and said, "I love your peach tree. Can I play with you?" Pippa replied, "Yes, of course! Let's play!" Pippa and the ant played all day. They laughed and had fun. When the sun went down, they said goodbye. The ant went back to his ant hill, and Pippa went to sleep in her peach tree. The next day, Pippa saw the ant again. "Hi, ant!" Pippa said. "Hi, Pippa!" the ant replied. "Let's play again today!" They played and played, and Pippa was very happy to have a new friend. From that day on, Pippa and the ant were best friends. They played together every day, and they learned that even though they were different, they could still be friends and have lots of fun.

One morning, Mommy wanted to encourage Jenny to try something new. "Jenny," she said, "how about we dance to some jazz? It's so fun!" Jenny smiled and said, "Yes, Mommy!" They put on some music and Jenny began to move her feet. "Wiggle your arms," said Mommy. Jenny tried it and laughed. She was having so much fun! Suddenly, a deaf cat came in and joined the dance. "Meow!" it said. Jenny smiled and said hi. She even moved her body slowly to encourage the cat to join the dance. Mommy and Jenny laughed, and the cat danced around them like a little jazz musician. It was so joyful that the three of them ended the dance with a big hug!

Once upon a time, there was a little bug who loved to crawl. One day, he found a big, round, yellow lemon. The bug was very happy and wanted to play with the lemon. The bug crawled on the lemon and rolled it around. He had so much fun! But then, he fell off the lemon and hurt his leg. The bug was sad because it hurt a lot. A nice bird saw the bug and helped him. The bird picked up the bug and put him back on the lemon. The bug was happy again and thanked the bird. They played together and became good friends.

Once upon a time there was a new mountain. It was like none other and it was special. Every day a little girl would go and explore the mountain. The little girl was so happy but she was always hungry! One day she decided to go and eat something on the mountain. She saw many things to eat. She saw an apple and it smelled so nice. She ate the apple and she was so full. The little girl explored the mountain for hours and she found something new. It was a small lake near the top of the mountain. She decided to stay and eat her lunch by the lake. The little girl sat and ate her lunch and she was so happy. She couldn't believe she was exploring a new mountain. She learned so many things about the mountain that day and enjoyed being alone in nature.
