Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Lily and the Firemen

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play outside. One day, they saw a big fire truck with red lights and loud noises. They wanted to see the fire truck up close, so they ran after it. The fire truck stopped near a house that had smoke and flames. The firemen got out and put on their helmets and coats. They looked brave and strong. Tom and Lily wished they could wear helmets and coats like the firemen. They saw a pile of helmets and coats near the fire truck. They thought no one would mind if they tried them on. They put on the helmets and coats and pretended to be firemen. They felt fine and happy. But then, a fireman saw them and shouted. He was angry and scared. He told them to take off the helmets and coats and go away. He said the helmets and coats were not for playing. They were for protecting the firemen from the flames. He said the flames were very hot and dangerous. He said Tom and Lily could get hurt or burned if they stayed near the flames. Tom and Lily felt bad and sorry. They took off the helmets and coats and ran back to their mom. They said they were sorry for being naughty and curious. They said they learned their lesson. They said they would never play with fire or firemen's things again. They said they would respect and thank the firemen for their work. Their mom hugged them and said she was glad they were fine and safe. She said she was proud of them for telling the truth and being sorry. She said they should always listen to the firemen and stay away from the flames. She said the firemen were heroes who saved people and houses from the flames. She said Tom and Lily could be heroes too, by being good and kind.

Once upon a time, there was a large boot. The boot could run very fast. It lived in a big house with a little boy named Tim. Tim and the boot were best friends. They loved to play and run outside in the sun. One day, Tim and the boot went to the park. They ran and played all day. But then, something unexpected happened. While they were running, the boot jumped high into the air and landed on a big ball. The ball started to roll with the boot on top! Tim chased after the boot and the ball. They rolled faster and faster. Suddenly, the ball hit a tree and stopped. The boot flew off the ball and landed in a big puddle of mud. Tim laughed and helped the boot out. They were both very dirty, but they had so much fun. From that day on, Tim and the large boot had a new game to play at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a big dream. She wanted to ride a fancy bike with pedals. She saw other kids ride their bikes and wanted to be like them. One day, Lucy's mom and dad got her a fancy bike with pedals for her birthday. Lucy was so happy. She could not wait to ride it. But first, she had to learn how to pedal and balance. Lucy practiced every day with her mom and dad. They held the bike while Lucy pedaled. Soon, Lucy could ride the fancy bike all by herself. She was so proud. Her dream came true, and she could ride her bike with her friends.

One day, a colorful bird named Bobo was flying in the sky. He liked to see all the pretty things in the world. Bobo saw a big cloud in the sky. The cloud was a hurricane. It was very big and scary. Bobo was scared, but he wanted to help his friends. He went to his friend, the little mouse. "Mouse, there is a big hurricane coming! We need to hide!" said Bobo. The little mouse was scared too. They both went to find a safe place to hide from the hurricane. They found a small hole under a big tree. They went inside the hole and waited for the hurricane to pass. They were safe and sound. After the hurricane was gone, they came out of the hole. "Thank you, Bobo, for helping me," said the little mouse. Bobo was happy that he could help his friend. They both went back to playing and seeing the colorful world around them.
