Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and Lily and the Hill

Tim and Lily were playing in the garden. They liked to look at the bugs and flowers. They saw a big ant hill with many ants. Tim had a magnifying glass. He wanted to see the ants better. "Look, Lily, I can make the ants bigger with this," he said, holding the glass over the hill. Lily was thoughtful. She knew that the glass could also make the sun hotter. She remembered that ice could melt when it was hot. She did not want the ants to melt. "Tim, that is not nice. The ants will get too hot and hurt. Put the glass away," she said. But Tim did not listen. He thought it was fun to make the ants bigger. He moved the glass closer to the hill. He did not see the smoke and the smell. Suddenly, he heard a loud hiss. He dropped the glass and jumped back. The ant hill was on fire. The ants were running away. Some of them were burned and melted. Tim was scared and sorry. He did not mean to hurt the ants. He cried and said, "I'm sorry, I'm sorry, I'm sorry." Lily ran to get some water. She poured it over the fire. She helped Tim to calm down. She said, "It's okay, Tim. You made a mistake. But you have to be careful with the glass and the sun. They can be dangerous. Next time, let's just watch the ants, okay?" Tim nodded. He hugged Lily and said, "Okay. Thank you, Lily. You are a good sister. And a thoughtful one."

One day, a little girl named Lily went to play in the big room. She saw a cabinet. The cabinet was very big. Lily wanted to see what was inside the cabinet. She opened the door and found a powerful drum. Lily thought it would be fun to play the drum. She hit the drum and clapped her hands. The sound was very loud. Suddenly, the room started to shake. Lily was scared, but she kept playing the drum and clapping. Then, something unexpected happened. The big cabinet started to dance! Lily was surprised, but she kept playing the drum and clapping. The cabinet danced and danced. Lily laughed and played the drum. They had so much fun together.

Lily and Ben were bored. It was a gloomy day and they could not go outside to play. They looked at their toys, but they did not want to play with them. They wanted to do something new and fun. They went to the kitchen and saw their mom. She was making a cake. She had a big bowl, a whisk, some eggs, some flour, some sugar, and some butter. She also had a teaspoon. She was measuring some vanilla with it. "Mom, can we help you?" Lily asked. "Sure, you can help me. You can be my little helpers," Mom said. She smiled and gave them each an apron. Lily and Ben put on their aprons and washed their hands. Mom showed them how to crack the eggs and whisk them. She let them take turns to add some flour, some sugar, and some butter to the bowl. She told them to be careful and not spill. "Can I use the teaspoon?" Ben asked. "Yes, you can use the teaspoon. You can add some vanilla to the batter. It will make the cake smell good and taste yummy," Mom said. Ben took the teaspoon and poured some vanilla into the batter. He was very proud. He gave the teaspoon to Lily and she did the same. "Good job, you two. You are very good helpers. Now we can put the batter in the pan and bake the cake," Mom said. Lily and Ben helped Mom put the batter in the pan and put the pan in the oven. They set the timer and waited for the cake to be ready. They were not bored anymore. They were happy. They helped Mom make a cake. They could not wait to eat it. It was going to be delicious.

Once upon a time, there was a small glue. Little Jimmy was so excited to play with it. He tried to make a picture with it, but the glue wouldn't stick. Frustrated, he started to frown. He asked his mom for help. His mom said she'd help, but only if he was really careful. Jimmy nodded and smiled, and then started to glue the pieces together. Everything seemed to be going well, until suddenly, one piece of the paper ripped in two. Jimmy started to frown again. He grabbed the glue and tried to make it better, but the glue just wouldn't stick. No matter how many times he tried, the glue just wouldn't work. Finally, Jimmy gave up. He looked at all the pieces he had tried to make with the glue, and then sighed. He just couldn't make it work, no matter how hard he tried. With a sad look on his face, he put the glue away and walked away.
