Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and the Rock

Tom was a boy who liked to talk to animals. He wanted to know what they did and how they felt. One day, he went to the beach with his mom and dad. He saw many animals there, like crabs, seagulls, and fish. He saw a big rock with many oysters on it. He wondered if the oysters could talk. He picked one up and said, "Hello, oyster. Can you talk to me?" The oyster did not say anything. It was closed tight. Tom tried to open it, but it was too hard. He asked his mom for help, but she said, "Oysters don't talk, Tom. They are not like us. They are very different." Tom was sad. He wanted to talk to the oyster. He put it back on the rock and said, "Sorry, oyster. I hope you are happy." Then he heard a small voice. It said, "Thank you, Tom. You are very kind. I can talk, but only a little. I don't talk to many people, because they want to eat me. You are different. You are clever and curious. I like you." Tom was surprised. He looked at the oyster and saw that it had opened a little. He saw a shiny pearl inside. The oyster said, "This is my gift to you, Tom. A pearl of friendship. You can keep it and remember me." Tom was happy. He thanked the oyster and took the pearl. He showed it to his mom and dad. They were amazed. They said, "What a lucky boy you are, Tom. You found a talking oyster and a beautiful pearl. You are very clever and curious indeed."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy was very excited because she was going on a holiday with her family. They were going to the beach, and Amy loved to play in the sand and water. While playing on the beach, Amy found a pretty shell. She wanted to show it to her mom and dad. But when she picked it up, she accidentally tore a hole in her bag. "Oh no!" said Amy. "My bag has a hole!" She felt sorry for her bag. Amy's mom saw what happened and came to help. "Don't worry, Amy," her mom said. "We can fix your bag." Amy's mom took a piece of cloth and tied it around the hole. "There, now your bag is as good as new." Amy was happy and thanked her mom. They continued to enjoy their holiday at the beach.

One day, a little girl named Mia was playing in her room. She saw a big bookcase with many books. The bookcase looked mysterious. Mia wanted to know what was behind the bookcase. Mia had a toy gun that could shoot soft balls. She decided to shoot the bookcase with her toy gun. When she shot the bookcase, something unexpected happened. The bookcase started to move! The bookcase moved away from the wall, and Mia saw a secret door. She opened the door and found a hidden room. Inside the room, there were fun toys and games. Mia was very happy and played there all day.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Bob. He lived in a big house with many toys. One day, Bob saw a cat stuck in a tree. The cat was sad and scared. Bob wanted to help the cat, but he could not reach it. Bob had an idea. He called the police for help. The police came with a long ladder. They climbed the ladder to reach the cat in the tree. The cat was very happy to be saved. The police and Bob became friends. They played with the toys in Bob's big house. The cat lived with Bob and they were all very happy. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim found a raft near the icy water. The raft was big and round. Tim wanted to play with the raft. He pushed the raft into the water and jumped on it. He felt happy and excited. As Tim played on the raft, he saw a little bird. The bird was cold and wet. Tim cared for the bird and wanted to help it. He put the bird on the raft with him. They became friends and played together on the raft. Suddenly, the raft started to move fast. Tim and the bird were scared. But then, they saw a big, friendly whale pushing the raft. The whale wanted to play too. Tim, the bird, and the whale had a fun day playing together on the icy water.
