Category: Intermediate
Title: The Wolf and the Wrap

Once upon a time, there was a chubby wolf. He lived in a big forest. The wolf had many friends. He liked to play and run with them. One day, the wolf found a big, soft thing. It was a wrap. The wolf put the wrap on the ground. He sat on it and felt very cozy. He liked the wrap a lot. The chubby wolf took the wrap to show his friends. They all liked it too. They took turns sitting on the wrap. They played on it all day. The wrap made them all happy.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom liked to play with his toys. His favorite toy was a big, round ball. This ball was very special because it was the original ball from a far away universe. One day, Tom went outside to play with his ball. He threw it up in the sky, and it went very high. But when the ball came back down, it did not look like the same ball. It was now a square! Tom was very surprised. He did not know that his ball could change shape. He played with the square ball all day, and it was fun. But then, something even more unexpected happened. The square ball turned into a triangle! Tom laughed and played with his triangle ball. He knew that his ball from the universe was very special, and he loved it even more. The end.

Once there was a brave mechanic called Bob. He was strong and fast and he loved fixing things. Every day Bob would go to the garage and help people with their cars. His customers were always very happy with his work. One day, a customer came in who was in a real hurry. He needed Bob to fix his car and fast! Bob knew he could fix it and so he said “I will do my best to help you”. Bob worked really hard to fix the car quickly. He was brave and worked late into the night to get it done. Finally, he finished, and the customer was very pleased. The customer thanked Bob for his help and said, “Thank you for protecting me and my car. I don’t know what I would have done without you.” Bob smiled and replied, “It was my pleasure!”

Once upon a time, there was a thin couch. Every day, it waited patiently for someone to come and sit on it. One day, a little girl, named Amy, came up to the couch. She wanted to complete a project. She had so many colors and glue and glitter spread out on the couch. She worked and worked until she was done. Finally, Amy smiled because she had completed her project. She was so happy that she hopped on the thin couch, enjoying her work. Then she curled up, closed her eyes and fell asleep. All was good in the world. The End

Molly knew she was sleepy. She looked at her pillow and thought it looked very easy and soft. She laid down on her pillow, closed her eyes and hugged it tight. Soon Molly was fast asleep. Her dreams were of a place full of fun. She was playing with her friends, running and laughing. Molly knew this was a happy place and she wanted to stay forever. But, she did not forget that her pillow was still waiting for her. Molly woke up and knew that she had to get up. She remembered how soft her pillow was and how it made her feel safe. So, she got up and hugged her pillow tight before she started her day. Molly knew that when it was time to go to bed again, she would always come back to her pillow so she could feel happy and sleepy.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went to the park. They saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Tim felt nervous, but he wanted to try. Spot barked and wagged his tail. Tim started to climb the tree. He went up and up. Spot barked louder. Tim's mom saw him and raised her voice, "Tim, come down now!" But Tim did not listen. He wanted to go higher. Suddenly, Tim slipped and fell. He cried as his mom ran to help him. Tim was hurt and sad. He should have listened to his mom. The day ended badly, and Tim learned to be careful and listen to his mom.
