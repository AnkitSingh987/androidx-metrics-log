Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Squirrel

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing outside. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. As he started to climb, he slipped and hurt his leg. Tim felt very sorry and started to cry. A kind squirrel saw Tim and asked, "Why are you crying, little boy?" Tim said, "I hurt my leg when I tried to climb the tree." The squirrel wanted to help and found a bandage for Tim. The squirrel put the bandage on Tim's leg. After the bandage was on, Tim felt better. He leaned on the tree and said, "Thank you, squirrel." The squirrel smiled and said, "You're welcome! Be careful next time." Then, something unexpected happened. The tree started to talk! The tree said, "I'm sorry I made you fall, Tim. I will be more careful too." Tim and the squirrel were very surprised, but they were happy to have a new friend.

One day, a little boy went to the park with his mom. They saw a vendor selling drinks. The boy was nervous because he had never seen a vendor before. He told his mom that he was thirsty and wanted a drink. His mom said, "Okay, let's get a drink from the vendor." They walked up to the vendor and the boy picked a red drink. The vendor gave it to him with a smile. The boy was still nervous, but he said thank you and took a sip of the drink. As he drank, something unexpected happened. The boy started to laugh and jump around. His mom looked at him and started to laugh too. The vendor smiled and said, "That drink is special. It makes you happy and want to play." The boy and his mom laughed and played in the park all day. They were so happy that they forgot all about being nervous. And from that day on, every time they went to the park, they would visit the vendor and get a special drink to make their day even more fun.

Nina was in her room playing with her dolls when her mom called her to come downstairs. When Nina got to the kitchen, she saw that mom was holding a pink fan. "Mom, what is this?" asked Nina. Mom smiled at her and said, "This is a fan, my little one. I brought it for you!" Nina was so excited. She grabbed the fan and raced around the house, feeling the cool air on her face. She laughed with joy. Later in the evening, mom said, "Nina, it's time to put away your pink fan." Nina grumbled, but she knew that mom was right. She carefully put the fan in the corner of her room and said goodnight. Nina was so happy that her mom had brought her the pink fan. She fell asleep with a big smile on her face.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived near a big wood. He liked to play with his friends in the wood. One day, Tim and his friends were playing hide and seek. Tim hid behind a big tree. His friend, Sam, found him and said, "I found you, Tim! You embarrass me!" Tim felt sad, but he was grateful that Sam was his friend. Then, they saw a big dog. The dog was angry and chased them. Tim and Sam ran away, but they could not find their way out of the wood. They were lost and scared. They called for help, but no one heard them. In the end, they had to stay in the wood all night.

Once there was a small boy named Tom. Tom was very excited because it was his birthday. He eagerly opened the presents his parents gave him. The last one was a special gift he didn't recognize. Tom opened the gift and realized it was a toy car. He was so happy he jumped around the room. He screamed, ''It's my favorite thing! I love it!'' Tom hugged his parents and thanked them for the gift. They both looked very happy. Tom played with the toy car for hours, but soon he felt miserable. He realized that he was the only one in the entire neighborhood who didn't have a real car. Tom said to his parents, ''Why don't I have a real car like everyone else?'' His parents said, ''We are sorry that you are unhappy, but it is time for dinner. Maybe we can make you a real car when you are older.'' Tom smiled. He felt much better now. He was glad he realized that getting a real car soon would be a great gift.
