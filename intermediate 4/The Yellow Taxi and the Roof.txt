Category: Intermediate
Title: The Yellow Taxi and the Roof

Once upon a time, there was a big yellow taxi. It liked to help people go to different places. One day, it was very cold outside. The wind was bitter, and everyone was shivering. A little girl and her mom needed a ride. They saw the taxi and waved. The taxi stopped, and they got in. The girl had a big toy that she wanted to bring with her. But it was too big to fit inside the taxi. The taxi driver had an idea. He helped the girl put the big toy on the roof. It fit! The girl and her mom were very happy. They went to their friend's house and had a fun day. The taxi was happy to help them, even on a cold, bitter day.

Once upon a time, there was a purple car. It arrived late in the night and nobody knew why it came. The car stopped and the door opened. Out came mama with a big bag of waste. She looked a bit sad. "Are you ok mama?" asked the child. "Yes, everything is fine," said mama, but her voice sounded a little funny. The child looked inside the car. Inside, he saw lots of colourful toys. He wanted to play with them right away. "Can I have a go? Can I? Pleeease," said the child excitedly. Mama smiled and said, "Yes, of course!" The child was so happy. He ran around the car and quickly put the toys away. From now on, the purple car would always remind the two of them of this lovely moment.

One day, a little boy named Tim be in his room. He be playing with his toys. Tim be a silly boy who loves to laugh and have fun. Tim has a toy brain. He talks to the toy brain and asks, "Why are you so silly, brain?" The toy brain answers, "Because I like to make you laugh, Tim!" Tim and the toy brain play together all day. They be very happy and silly. They make each other laugh a lot. And they become best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a colorful raft. The raft was very pretty. It had many colors like red, blue, and yellow. Tom and Tim, two little boys, found the raft near the river. "Let's go on the raft!" said Tom. Tim was scared, but he agreed. They got on the raft and started to float down the river. The boys saw many fun things like fish, birds, and big rocks. They were having so much fun that they did not see the big waterfall up ahead. Tim heard the loud sound of the waterfall and said, "Tom, I hear something! We must go back!" But Tom did not listen. He just wanted to repeat the fun they had. They got closer and closer to the waterfall. As they got closer, the sound got louder. Tom finally heard it too. "Oh no!" he said. "We need to get off the raft!" But it was too late. The colorful raft went over the big waterfall, and the boys were very scared. The colorful raft was now gone, and the boys were sad. They wished they had listened to Tim when he first heard the waterfall. Now they had no raft, and they had to find their way back home.

Lily was scared of the monster under her bed. She hated it very much. Every night, she heard it growl and scratch. She wanted it to go away. One night, she decided to be tough and face the monster. She took her flashlight and crawled under the bed. She saw a big, hairy, green thing with sharp teeth and claws. It looked mean and scary. "Who are you?" Lily asked, trying not to shake. "I'm the monster under your bed," the thing said. "And I'm hungry." "What do you eat?" Lily asked, hoping it was not her. "I eat little girls who are brave enough to come under the bed," the monster said, licking its lips. Lily was very afraid, but she did not want to show it. She had an idea. She pointed her flashlight at the monster's eyes and said, "You can't eat me. I'm not a little girl. I'm a super hero. And I have a super power. I can make you go blind with my light." The monster blinked and squinted. It did not like the bright light. It felt a pain in its eyes. It started to whimper and cry. "Please, stop. You're hurting me. I'm sorry. I won't eat you. I won't bother you. Just let me go," the monster begged. Lily felt sorry for the monster. She realized it was lonely and scared too. She turned off her flashlight and said, "OK, I'll let you go. But you have to promise me something. You have to promise me that you'll be nice and friendly. And that you'll find a new home where you can be happy and safe. And that you'll never come back under my bed." The monster nodded and said, "I promise. Thank you for being kind. You're a good super hero. And a good friend." Lily smiled and said, "You're welcome. Goodbye, monster." The monster crawled out from under the bed and ran away. Lily went back to her bed and slept peacefully. She was not scared of the monster anymore. She was proud of herself for being tough and brave. And she was happy that she made a new friend.
