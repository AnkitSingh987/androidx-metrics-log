Category: Intermediate
Title: Max and Lily and the Frog

One day, a big dog named Max and a small cat named Lily went for a walk. They saw a dull bridge. The bridge went over a little river. Max and Lily wanted to play on the other side of the river. As they walked on the bridge, they met a big frog. The frog said, "You can't go over my bridge!" Max and Lily were scared. But then, the frog began to laugh. "I'm just joking," said the frog. "You can go over my bridge, but first, let's play a game." The game was to slap hands with the frog. Max went first. He slapped the frog's hand very hard. The frog was not happy. Lily went next. She slapped the frog's hand very softly. The frog was happy. Max and Lily learned that being kind is better than being mean. They played with the frog and then went to the other side of the river. They had a great day and made a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy who loved to wear his favorite dark blue t-shirt. One day, he found a bar of chocolate. He was very excited and he quickly put it in his pocket. When his mother saw him, she said, "Where did you find that chocolate?" The little boy replied, "I found it on the ground!" His mother said, "No, you must not eat it. You are not sure if the chocolate is safe. It is better to wear clean and safe clothes." The little boy learned his lesson and he never touched that dark chocolate again. He was always careful and he always wore clean and safe clothes. The moral of the story is: It is better to wear clean and safe clothes than take risks with things that you don't know.

One day, a big red ball was playing outside. It was a warm and sunny day. The big red ball was very happy. It liked to roll around and play with its friends. The big red ball was also very intelligent. It could do many fun tricks. As the big red ball was playing, it saw a cooler. The cooler was blue and had cold drinks inside. The big red ball wanted to play with the cooler too. So, it started to roll faster and faster towards the cooler. Suddenly, the big red ball began to fall. It fell into a big hole in the ground. It was very dark in the hole. The big red ball was scared. But then, it saw something shiny. It was a magic wand! The big red ball used the wand to fly out of the hole. It was very happy. The big red ball went back to play with the cooler. The cooler was happy to see the big red ball again. They played together all day long. And the big red ball used the magic wand to do even more fun tricks. From that day on, the big red ball and the cooler were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a wise old owl. The owl loved to sail on the big blue sea. One day, the owl wanted to go on a long trip. He packed his luggage with food and toys. On the sea, the owl met a big, mean fish. The fish wanted the owl's luggage. The owl did not want to give it to him. They both got very mad. The wise owl thought of a plan. He said, "Let's have a race. If you win, you get my luggage." The fish agreed. They raced in the water. The owl was very fast. The owl won the race and kept his luggage. The fish went away, and the owl sailed happily on his trip.

Alice loved to wrap things. She loved wrapping presents for her family and friends. Today, Alice had found a big project to wrap. She wanted to wrap her mattress. Alice had seen her parents make the bed, so she knew it should be wrapped like a big long gift. Alice grabbed a long sheet of blue wrapping paper. She carefully laid it on the mattress and tried to tuck it in so that it would be tight. "Almost done," she said. She kept tucking and wrapping until the mattress was completely covered in the blue wrapping paper. Alice stepped back to see her work. "Wow, it looks so deep with the blue wrapping paper," she said. She made sure to give it a few extra tugs so that it was nice and tight. Lastly, she grinned with satisfaction and said, "Perfect!"
