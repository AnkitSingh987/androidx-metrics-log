Category: Intermediate
Title: Bobo the Bear and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a bear named Bobo. Bobo loved honey. He would eat honey all day. Bobo lived in a big forest with many thin trees. One day, Bobo's friend, Bee, came to visit. Bee said, "Bobo, I need help! Our honey is stuck in a high tree!" Bobo wanted to help his friend. So, Bobo and Bee went to the thin tree with the honey. Bobo tried to send a long stick up to get the honey. But the stick was too short. Then, Bobo had an idea. He climbed the thin tree and got the honey. Bee was very happy. Bobo and Bee shared the honey. They were very happy. From that day, they were the best of friends. And they always helped each other when they needed it.

One day, a big dog named Max went for a jog in the park. Max was a healthy dog with a lot of energy. As he jogged, he saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she lost her toy. Max asked, "Why are you sad?" Sue said, "I lost my toy and it has a lot of value to me. It was a gift from my mom." Max wanted to help Sue, so they looked for the toy together. After a while, they found the toy under a big tree. Sue was so happy and thanked Max. They became good friends and played together every day. The moral of the story is to help others when they are in need, and you will make great friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Lucy. She liked to dress up in her favourite clothes and play pretend games. One day, when she was playing, she saw a furry mask on the floor and decided to pick it up. Lucy tried it on, but when she looked at herself in the mirror, she realised something was wrong – the mask was hairy on the inside, and smooth on the outside! Lucy felt silly and so she decided to reverse the mask. She felt much better when she put it on the right way and saw how beautiful it looked. The moral of this story is that sometimes, when faced with a problem, it's important to not give up, but to think outside of the box. Lucy reversed the mask and she was very proud of herself for solving the problem.

Lily likes to balance on one toe. She thinks it is fun and hard. She can do it for a long time. She likes to wear her pink shoes with sparkles. They make her feel like a fairy. One day, Lily goes to the park with her mom and dad. She sees a big slide. She wants to go on it. She runs fast to the slide. She climbs up the steps. She balances on one toe at the top. She smiles and waves to her mom and dad. But then, a big wind comes. It blows Lily's hair and dress. It makes her lose her balance. She falls off the slide. She lands on the grass. She cries. Her mom and dad run to her. They hug her and kiss her. They check her toe. It is not hurt. It is only dirty. Lily stops crying. She is brave. She says she wants to try again. She goes back to the slide. She does not balance on one toe this time. She sits down and slides down. She laughs. She likes the slide. She likes to balance on one toe, but not on the slide. She learns something new.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom loved to watch birds. Every day, he went to the park and watched the birds fly. The birds were healthy and happy. One day, Tom saw two birds have a race. The birds flew very fast. Tom watched and cheered for the birds. He wanted to see who would win the race. The birds flew faster and faster. At last, one bird won. The bird was so happy with its victory. But then, something unexpected happened. The winning bird shared its victory with the other bird. They both became friends and flew together. Tom learned that sharing and being friends is more important than winning.
