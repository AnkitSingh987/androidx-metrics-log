Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna and Ben and the Crown

Anna and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to dig in the sand and look for shiny things. One day, they found a big, bright gem. It was red and sparkly, like a star. "Wow, look at this!" Anna said. "It is so pretty!" "Let me see!" Ben said. He tried to grab the gem from Anna's hand. "No, it is mine!" Anna said. She held the gem tight. "I found it first!" They started to pull and tug on the gem. They were both angry and loud. A man was walking by. He heard the noise and saw the gem. He smiled and came closer. "Hello, children," he said. "What do you have there?" Anna and Ben stopped fighting. They looked at the man. He was tall and wore a hat and a coat. He had a bag on his shoulder. "It is a gem," Anna said. "We found it in the sand." "It is very beautiful," the man said. "Do you know what it is?" Anna and Ben shook their heads. "It is a ruby," the man said. "It is very rare and valuable. Do you want to see something amazing?" Anna and Ben nodded. The man opened his bag. He took out a big book. He opened it and showed them a page. It had a picture of a crown. It was gold and had many gems on it. One of them was a ruby, just like theirs. "This is the crown of a king," the man said. "He lived in a distant land, long ago. He wore this crown on his head. He had many rubies like yours. He liked to display them and show off his power." Anna and Ben looked at the picture. They were amazed. They had never seen anything like it. "Can we keep our ruby?" Anna asked. "Of course you can," the man said. "It is yours. You found it. But you have to share it. It is too precious to fight over. Can you do that?" Anna and Ben looked at each other. They felt sorry for being mean. They nodded. "Yes, we can," Ben said. "Good," the man said. He smiled and closed his book. He put it back in his bag. He stood up and tipped his hat. "Have fun, children. And take good care of your ruby. It is a special gem." He walked away. Anna and Ben watched him go. They held the ruby together. They felt happy and proud. They decided to display it in their room, where they could see it every day. They also decided to be nice to each other, and to share their other shiny things. They learned a lesson from the ruby and the man. They were friends again.

The little boy was running around in circles. He was so happy and alive. But suddenly, he heard loud noises. It frightened him and he started to cry. He wanted his mommy to forgive him and make everything alright again. But the noises kept getting louder, and the little boy was so scared that he curled up into a ball. He couldn't see anything, and he was shaking. The noise suddenly stopped, and the little boy slowly opened his eyes. But it was not the same world as before. The circle was gone and everything around him was dead and grey. He was too afraid to move. The little boy had his mommy's love, but he could not find her now. He had lost in the world. He never got a chance to be forgiven.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Daisy. Daisy loved to dream. Every night, she would dream of fun things like playing with her friends, eating ice cream, and running in the park. One day, Daisy was playing outside, and she saw a big, pretty butterfly. Daisy wanted to catch it, so she ran after it. She was very alert and watched the butterfly closely. The butterfly flew up, down, and all around, but Daisy did not give up. Then, something unexpected happened. The butterfly stopped flying and turned into a fairy! The fairy said, "Daisy, you are so kind and alert. I will give you one wish. What do you want?" Daisy thought for a moment and then said, "I want to be able to dream all the time, even when I'm awake!" The fairy smiled and granted her wish. From that day on, Daisy could dream all the time, and her life was full of fun and magic.
