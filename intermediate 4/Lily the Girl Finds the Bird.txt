Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl Finds the Bird

One day, a little girl named Lily found a dirty paper on the ground. She picked it up and saw that it had a poem written on it. Lily liked poems, so she took the paper home to show her mom. "Mom, look what I found!" Lily said, holding the dirty paper. Her mom smiled and said, "Let's clean it up and read it together." They washed the paper and read the poem. It was about a bird who wanted to be free. The next day, Lily and her mom went to the park. They saw a bird trapped in a cage. Lily remembered the poem and felt sad for the bird. She asked her mom, "Can we release the bird, so it can be free like in the poem?" Her mom agreed. Lily opened the cage and the bird flew away, happy to be free. The bird sang a beautiful song as it flew into the sky. Lily and her mom smiled, knowing they had done a good thing.

Once upon a time, there was a bright kid named Tim. He liked to play with his toy cars. His favorite car was red and it could speed very fast. One day, Tim took his toy car to the park. He met his friend, Lily, who had a blue car. They decided to race their cars to see which one was faster. Tim and Lily said "Ready, set, go!" and their cars began to speed. The red car went very fast and the blue car tried to catch up. In the end, Tim's red car won the race. They both had fun and played together until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a big, hairy dog named Max. Max loved to play with his ball. One day, Max could not find his ball. He looked and looked, but it was not there. Max was sad. Max's friend, Tim, saw him looking sad. Tim asked, "Max, why are you sad?" Max said, "I can't find my ball." Tim thought for a moment and said, "Did you remember to look in the attic?" Max did not remember to look in the attic. He went up the stairs to the attic. There, in the corner, he found his ball. Max was so happy! He thanked Tim for helping him remember. Max and Tim played with the ball all day. They were very happy. They knew that they would always remember to look in the attic if they ever lost something again.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play in the garden. They have a big slide, a swing, and a sandbox. Sometimes they share, but sometimes they fight. One day, they want to play on the slide. But they both want to go first. They push and pull and shout. "I want to go first!" Lily says. "No, I want to go first!" Ben says. They are very competitive. They do not listen to each other. They ignore their mom, who tells them to take turns. Mom is not happy. She goes to the garden and takes them by the hand. She brings them to the sandbox. "Look, there are two shovels and two buckets. You can both play here. You can make sand castles and dig holes. But you have to be nice and share. Do you understand?" Mom says. Lily and Ben nod. They are sorry. They do not want to make Mom sad. They say sorry to each other. They play in the sandbox. They have fun. They make a big sand castle together. They share the shovels and the buckets. They do not fight. They do not ignore Mom. They are happy. Mom is happy too. She smiles and gives them a hug.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Bobo. Bobo loved to eat. His favorite food was radish. One day, Bobo went to the garden to find some radish. In the garden, Bobo saw many radishes. He was very happy. Bobo began to eat the radishes. He ate one, then another, and another. Soon, his tummy was full. But Bobo did not stop. He kept eating the radishes. The garden became empty. There were no more radishes left. Bobo felt sad. Next time, he promised to save some radishes for later.
