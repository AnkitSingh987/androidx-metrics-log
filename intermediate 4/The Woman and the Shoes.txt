Category: Intermediate
Title: The Woman and the Shoes

Once upon a time, there was a woman. She loved to polish her red shoes. She was eager to make them shine. One sunny day, the woman went for a walk. She wore her shiny red shoes. She saw a big tree and sat under it. As she sat, a little bird came by. The bird liked her shiny red shoes, too. They became friends and played all day. The woman and the bird were happy.

---

Once there was a whale who lived in the ocean. He was so lonely and wished he had someone to play with. So one day he decided to create something. He closed his eyes and used all his imagination. Then he opened his eyes and was surprised by what he saw. He had created a magical fish! Its scales shone brightly and its eyes sparkled. The whale was so excited and said "Hello, magical fish! We will be friends." The fish was a bit frightened though. It had never seen such a big creature and it didn't know what the whale wanted. It said, "Whale, what do you want?" The whale smiled and said, "I just wanted a friend to play with! Do you want to be my friend?" The fish was still a bit scared, but it said, "Yes, I will be your friend." The whale and the magical fish became the best of friends. They played together in the ocean, and the whale was never lonely again.

There once was a boy named Tim. Tim was a very reliable boy. He always did what he was told. One day, Tim's mom asked him to help her with a big job. "Tim," she said, "I need you to order some food for our dinner tonight. Can you help me?" Tim nodded and said, "Yes, Mom. I can help." Tim went to the store with a list of food. He found all the things on the list and put them in the cart. Then he went to the man at the store and gave him the list. The man looked at the list and said, "Good job, Tim. You are very reliable." Tim went home and showed his mom all the food he got. She was very happy. They made a big dinner with the food Tim ordered. Everyone ate and had a good time. Tim was proud that he could help his mom.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the mall with his mom. They wanted to buy a sack for Tim's toys. Tim was very excited. He loved to go to the mall. At the mall, they saw a big, bad wolf. The wolf said, "I want your sack!" Tim was scared, but he said, "No, Mr. Wolf, this is for my toys." The bad wolf did not like that. Tim's mom said, "Go away, Mr. Wolf. You cannot have our sack." The bad wolf was sad, but he left them alone. Tim and his mom bought the sack and went home. Tim was happy to have a new sack for his toys.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big sister named Sue. Tim and Sue both loved cookies. One day, Mom made some yummy cookies for them. Tim was very happy. Sue got a big cookie, but Tim got a small cookie. Tim was envious. He wanted a big cookie like Sue. Tim said, "I hate my small cookie. I want a big cookie, too!" Sue looked at Tim and felt sad for him. Sue broke her big cookie in half and gave one half to Tim. Now, they both had the same size cookies. Tim was not envious anymore. He said, "Thank you, Sue!" They both ate their cookies and smiled. They were happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Ella. Ella loved to play in her garden. She especially loved to pick the bright pastel flowers that she could find. One day, Ella saw something very different in the garden. It was a little fairy, flying around the garden with her wings! Ella couldn't believe her eyes. The fairy stopped when she saw Ella and said, "Please do not pick any more flowers Ella. They need to stay in the garden." Ella was very sad, she liked picking the pretty pastel flowers. But she could see that the fairy was serious, so she knelt down and promised not to pick any more. But the fairy was sad too, she wanted Ella to have lots of fun in the garden. So she said, "I understand why you want to pick the flowers, I have an idea. How about you pick one flower, but you must look after it, and keep it safe?" Ella was so happy now. She picked the prettiest pastel flower and promised to look after it forever. The little fairy smiled and then flew away. Ella and the fairy were very different, but they were also very happy together.
