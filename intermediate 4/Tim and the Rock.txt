Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Rock

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big rock in his yard. He was very confused. He had never seen a rock this big before. Tim decided to show the rock to his friend, Sam. "Look at this big rock, Sam!" said Tim. Sam looked at the rock and was confused too. They both thought about what to do with the rock. Then, they had an idea. They would use the rock to make a fun game. Tim and Sam found a pedal from an old bike. They put the pedal on the rock and started to rock back and forth on it. They laughed and played on the rocking rock all day. It was the best day ever.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with his mom, dad, and his dog, Spot. In the house, there was a big fireplace. Tim liked to sit by the fireplace to feel warm. One day, Tim was playing with Spot near the fireplace. He was very careless and almost fell into the fire. "Be careful, Tim!" said his mom. "The fireplace is hot, and you could get hurt." Tim listened to his mom and moved away from the fireplace. Tim's dad saw him feeling sad and wanted to encourage him. "Tim, let's play a game away from the fireplace," said his dad. "That way, we can be safe and have fun." Tim smiled, and they played with Spot in the living room. Tim learned to be more careful and had a great day with his family.

Once upon a time, there was a little boat named Bob. Bob loved to sail to the port. One day, while going to the port, Bob met a big, rude boat. The rude boat said, "You can't get to the port. It's only for big boats like me!" Bob felt sad, but he didn't give up. He thought of a plan to get to the port. He saw a nice fish and asked for help. The fish said, "I can help you, Bob. Just follow me!" So, Bob followed the fish, and they went a different way to the port. When they got to the port, the rude boat was surprised to see Bob. The rude boat said, "How did you get here?" Bob smiled and said, "I had help from my new friend, the fish!" From that day on, Bob and the fish played together at the port, and the rude boat learned to be nice to everyone, no matter how big or small they were.

Once upon a time, there was a gloomy day. The sun was hiding behind the clouds. In a small house, a girl named Sue was sad. She wanted to play outside, but it was too cold and wet. Her mom had an idea. "Let's make yummy food on the grill!" she said. Sue and her mom went outside with their coats on. They put some food on the grill. They waited and watched. The food began to smell good. Sue started to feel happy. The gloomy day was not so bad after all. While they were grilling, something unexpected happened. The sun came out from behind the clouds. The day was no longer gloomy. Sue's mom said, "See, when we do something fun, even a gloomy day can yield a happy time." Sue learned that even when things seem sad, doing something fun can make it better.

Once there was a little girl named Janie who loved to explore. One day, she went for a walk and ended up at a lake. She had never seen anything like it before. The lake was surrounded by rare pines, and Janie couldn't believe her eyes! When Janie reached the lake, she went down to the shore and picked up a rock. She skipped it across the lake and it made a beautiful sound as it jumped along the surface. Janie continued to explore, enjoying the sights and sounds around her. As she did, she spotted something near the pines. It was a beautiful grey bird! Janie watched in wonder as the bird flew in and out of the rare pines. Janie was so excited by her discovery that she ran over to the bird. She exclaimed, "Hello. What kind of bird are you?" The little bird chirped back, "I'm an owl! It's so rare to see me here in the pine!" Janie and the owl enjoyed their time together. She watched it fly between the pines, and the bird seemed so content. Janie left the lake feeling lucky for such a rare experience.
