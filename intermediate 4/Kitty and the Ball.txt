Category: Intermediate
Title: Kitty and the Ball

One day, a little cat named Kitty was walking down the road. She saw a big dog named Spot. Spot was sad and ashamed because he lost his toy ball. Kitty said, "Spot, why are you sad?" Spot said, "I lost my ball." Kitty wanted to help, so she said, "Let's find your ball. Come with me." They walked down the road together. They looked under a tree and found the ball. Spot was happy and not ashamed anymore. Kitty and Spot played with the ball and had fun. They became good friends and always played together.

One day, a noisy man went to the park. He liked to talk and laugh very loud. He sat on a bench and stared at the birds in the trees. A little girl was playing near the man. She heard the noisy man and walked over to him. She stared at him and asked, "Why are you so loud?" The man smiled and said, "I am happy and I want everyone to know." The little girl stared at the birds and said, "But the birds are quiet and they are happy too." The noisy man thought about what the little girl said. He decided to be more quiet and enjoy the birds. They both sat on the bench, stared at the birds, and had a happy day at the park.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim had some stuff. He loved his stuff so much. He wanted to travel with his stuff. One day, he put his stuff in a big bag. He was so happy to go on a trip with his stuff. Tim walked and walked with his stuff. But soon, he felt weak. He could not carry his stuff anymore. He sat down and cried. Then, a big bird saw Tim. The bird wanted to help him. The bird picked up Tim's stuff with its beak. Tim was surprised and happy. The bird was strong, not weak like Tim. They traveled together. The bird carried Tim's stuff, and they had fun on their trip. And that's how Tim and the bird became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy called Mark. One day Mark went to the park to play with his best friend Andy. Mark and Andy played lots of games and had lots of fun. Suddenly, they met a girl called Isabella. Isabella said, “Hi, I want to play with you two!” Mark and Andy were excited to meet a new friend. They started playing a game which Isabella suggested. She was very organized and knew how to play the game very well. But then, Mark started to feel left out. He wanted to join the game but Isabella was not letting him. Mark got very sad and started to cry. Andy was worried about Mark and wanted to help him. So Andy quickly suggested they play a game they all could play together. Isabella smiled and agreed. They had lots of fun playing the game together and Mark was very happy. The three friends started to play together every day in the park. They became very good friends and always had lots of fun. They realized the best way to have fun is when everyone joins in.

Once upon a time, there was a big bird named Ollie. Ollie was an ostrich who lived on a farm. He had many friends on the farm, like cows, pigs, and chickens. They all liked to play together in the mud. One day, Ollie got very dirty. He was filthy from playing in the mud with his friends. His feathers were brown and yucky. Ollie's friends laughed, but Ollie did not like being so dirty. Ollie's mom saw him and said, "Ollie, you need a bath." Ollie did not want to take a bath, but he did not want to suffer with the dirt on him anymore. So, Ollie took a bath and got all clean. His feathers were white and pretty again. Ollie felt happy and went to play with his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a famous bear named Bobo. Bobo lived in a cold place with lots of snow. He had a big, round igloo made of ice. One day, Bobo's friend, a little bird named Lulu, came to visit. Lulu was cold and tired. She asked Bobo, "Can I borrow your igloo to rest?" Bobo said, "Yes, Lulu, you can borrow my igloo. It will keep you warm and safe." Lulu was happy and went inside the igloo to rest. After Lulu had a good rest, she thanked Bobo for letting her borrow the igloo. They played in the snow and had lots of fun together. From that day on, Bobo and Lulu were best friends.
