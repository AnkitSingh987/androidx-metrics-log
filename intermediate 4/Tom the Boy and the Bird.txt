Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Bird

Once, there was a boy named Tom. He had a big apple tree in his yard. Tom would keep the apples to eat. One day, he saw a weak bird under the tree. The bird could not fly. Tom wanted to help the bird. Tom gave the bird some apple to eat. The bird ate the apple and started to feel better. Tom was happy to see the bird get strong. But then, something unexpected happened. The bird grew really big! The big bird picked up Tom and took him for a ride in the sky. Tom was scared at first, but then he had fun. They flew over the town and saw many things. When they came back, the bird was small again. Tom and the bird became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly man named Tom. Tom was very good at fixing things. One day, he found a broken toy car in the park. Tom wanted to repair the toy car for the child who lost it. Tom took the toy car home and started to fix it. He used his fingers to put the pieces back together. With a little glue and some tape, the toy car looked like new again. The next day, Tom went back to the park with the fixed toy car. He saw a little boy looking sad. Tom asked him if he lost a toy car. The boy said yes, and Tom gave him the repaired car. The boy was very happy, and Tom felt good that he could help.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to explore her neighbourhood. One day, while she was out exploring, she noticed something unusual on the lawn of her neighbour's house. She approached it and saw it was a giant furry animal! She ran to the door of the house and knocked. A friendly old lady opened the door. Lucy asked, "What is that furry thing on your lawn?" The old lady said, "It's an elephant! I ordered it all the way from Africa!" Lucy thought it was so cool. She thanked the old lady and went back home to tell her friends about the unusual elephant. She couldn't believe something so big and furry was in her neighbourhood!

One day, there was a rare bookshelf in the forest. It was so special that everyone wanted to come and look at it. So, lots of animals came to the forest. They saw the bookshelf and thought it was so cool. Then a little rabbit hopped up to the bookshelf. He wanted to take a closer look. He looked and looked but couldn't find anything. Then, suddenly, he noticed something. It was a rare book! The rabbit was so excited that he jumped up and down. He ran over to all of his friends and told them about the special book he had found. Soon, all of the animals were coming to the forest to see the rare bookshelf. They looked at it and read the special book that the little rabbit had found. It was the best day ever!

Once upon a time, there was a dog called Spot. Spot was eager to find a friend. One day, Spot met a little boy in the park. The boy said, "Hi Spot! Can I give you a pat?" Spot was so happy and eager to be friends. He licked the boy's hand. The boy said, "Let's go for a walk together!" Spot ran around, excited to be with his new best friend. Suddenly, the boy's mom came towards them. "No pets allowed in the park! Get away from my son!" she shouted. She looked angry and Spot ran away. The boy was sad that his new friend was gone. He had been eager to be friends with Spot, but he didn't understand why his mom had said no. Moral of the story: We must respect other people's rules, even if we don't understand why they are in place.

One day, a boy named Tim had a baseball. He liked to play with it a lot. Tim's room was very tidy. He always put his toys away when he was done playing with them. His mom was very happy about this. One day, Tim played with his baseball outside. He threw it very high in the sky. But, when it came down, it broke a window. The window was part of his dad's car. Tim was very sad and scared. He knew that his dad would not be happy. Tim wanted to make things right. He asked his mom to help him repair the window. They worked together to fix it. When they were done, the window looked good as new. Tim's dad came home and saw the fixed window. He was proud of Tim for fixing his mistake. From then on, Tim was more careful when playing with his baseball.
