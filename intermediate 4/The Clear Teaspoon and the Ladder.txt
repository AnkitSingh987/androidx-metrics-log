Category: Intermediate
Title: The Clear Teaspoon and the Ladder

Once upon a time, in a small kitchen, there was a clear teaspoon. The teaspoon was very happy. It had many friends like the fork, the knife, and the plate. They all worked together to help people eat their food. One day, a little boy named Tim came into the kitchen. He wanted to get some honey for his toast. But the honey jar was too high for him. The clear teaspoon saw Tim and wanted to help. The teaspoon asked its friends, "How can we help Tim get the honey?" The fork, the knife, and the plate thought for a moment. Then, they had a great idea! They all stood on top of each other to make a tall ladder. The clear teaspoon climbed to the top and scooped out some honey for Tim. Tim was very happy and thanked the teaspoon and its friends. From that day on, Tim always used the clear teaspoon for his honey. He also learned to ask for help when he needed it. The moral of the story is that it's good to ask for help and work together with friends.

Once upon a time, there was an ordinary cloud named Fluffy. Fluffy liked to carry rain to all the flowers and trees. One sunny day, Fluffy met a little bird named Chirpy. Chirpy was very sad because she was thirsty and wanted water. "Hi Fluffy, can you carry rain to my tree? I am very thirsty," Chirpy asked. Fluffy wanted to help Chirpy, so he tried to carry rain to her tree. He tried and tried, but the sun was too strong, and Fluffy could not carry the rain. In the end, Fluffy could not carry the rain to Chirpy's tree. Chirpy was still very thirsty and sad. Fluffy felt bad because he could not help his new friend. That day, the sun shone bright, and there was no rain for Chirpy.

Tom and Lily liked to play in the dark. They had a flashlight that made bright light. They took turns to shine the flashlight on the walls and make funny shapes with their hands. One night, they heard a loud noise outside. They looked out the window and saw a big storm. The wind was blowing hard and the rain was falling fast. They saw flashes of light in the sky and heard booms of thunder. "Wow, look at that!" Tom said. "It's so lively!" "I'm scared," Lily said. "It's too loud and too dark." "Don't worry, Lily," Tom said. "We have our flashlight. It will keep us safe and warm. We can play with it until the storm is over." He gave the flashlight to Lily and hugged her. Lily smiled and hugged him back. She felt better with the flashlight in her hand. She shone it on Tom's face and made him laugh. They valued their flashlight very much. It was their favorite toy and their best friend. They played with it until they fell asleep in each other's arms. The storm passed and the sun came out. They woke up and saw a rainbow in the sky. They were happy and grateful. They loved their flashlight and each other.

Sam and Lily went to the shop with Mom. They wanted to buy a cake for Dad's birthday. Mom gave them some money and said they could pick any cake they liked. Sam and Lily looked at all the cakes. There were big cakes and small cakes, round cakes and square cakes, chocolate cakes and vanilla cakes. Some cakes had fruits or nuts on top, some had cream or icing. Sam and Lily liked different cakes. Sam liked the chocolate cake with nuts, Lily liked the vanilla cake with fruits. They asked the shop lady how much each cake was. The shop lady told them the price of each cake. Sam and Lily had to add the price of the cake they liked to the price of the candles and the card. They had to make sure they had enough money. Sam and Lily did not know how to add very well. They used their fingers and toes to count. They tried to add the price of the chocolate cake with nuts, the candles and the card. They tried to add the price of the vanilla cake with fruits, the candles and the card. They got confused and mixed up the numbers. They saw another cake in the shop. It was a big cake with a picture of a car on it. Sam and Lily both liked cars. They asked the shop lady how much the car cake was. The shop lady said it was a special cake and the price was unknown. She said they could have it for free if they wanted. Sam and Lily were very happy. They thanked the shop lady and took the car cake, the candles and the card. They ran to Mom and showed her their cake. Mom was surprised and smiled. She said they were very lucky and clever. They went home to surprise Dad with their cake. Dad was very happy and hugged them. They all ate the cake and had a fun party.
