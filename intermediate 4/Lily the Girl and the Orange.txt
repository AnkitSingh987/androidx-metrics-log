Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Orange

One day, a little girl named Lily found a small orange box. She opened the box and saw pretty jewelry inside. There were shiny necklaces, bracelets, and rings. Lily wanted to keep the jewelry, but she did not know who it belonged to. Lily had an idea. She took a piece of paper and made a mark on it. The mark was a picture of the orange box and the jewelry. She showed the picture to her friends and asked if the box belonged to any of them. But no one knew who it belonged to. Finally, Lily met a kind old lady. The lady saw the mark on the paper and smiled. She said the orange box and jewelry were hers. She was so happy that Lily found it. The kind old lady gave Lily a pretty necklace from the box to say thank you. Now, Lily and the old lady were friends and they both had pretty jewelry to wear.

Once upon a time, there was a big, hairy dog. The dog liked to run and play all day long. One day, the dog ran to the east, where the sun comes up. He wanted to see what was there. As he ran, the hairy dog got very tired. He thought, "I should rest for a bit." So, he found a big tree and lay down under it. The dog closed his eyes and took a long nap. While the hairy dog was resting, a mean cat came by. The cat saw the dog sleeping and thought it would be fun to play a trick. The cat took the dog's favorite toy and ran away. When the hairy dog woke up, he was very sad to see his toy was gone. And he never found it again.

One day, a tired little bird named Blue was flying in the sky. Blue saw a soft spot to lay down and take a rest. The soft spot was green grass under a big tree. As Blue lay under the tree, a small ant named Tiny walked by. Tiny was on a march with his ant friends, but he was also tired. Tiny saw Blue and said, "Hi, can I lay down too?" Blue said, "Yes, come lay down with me." So Tiny lay down next to Blue, and they both took a nice nap under the big tree. When they woke up, they felt happy and not tired anymore. Blue and Tiny became good friends, and they played together in the warm sun.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a shoe in her room. It was a flexible shoe that she could bend and twist. Sue was very happy with her new shoe. Sue went to her friend Tom and said, "Look at my shoe! It is so flexible!" Tom looked at the shoe and said, "Wow, that is a cool shoe!" They played with the shoe all day, bending it and twisting it. Later, they got tired and decided to rest under a big tree. While they were resting, a bird came and took the shoe from them. Sue and Tom were very surprised. They looked at each other and said, "Oh no! The bird took our flexible shoe!" They laughed and knew they had a new adventure to get their shoe back.

Once upon a time, there was a big boat that could sail on water. On the boat, there was a lot of furniture. The boat had a captain, and he loved to sail it. One day, a little boy came to the captain. The boy said, "I want to sail the boat, but I don't know how." The captain said, "It's okay. I will teach you." The boy was very happy, and they started to learn together. As they sailed, the boy learned many things. He was not ignorant anymore. They had a lot of fun, and the boy became a good sailor. The moral of the story is that it's okay to ask for help when you don't know something.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to go to the store with his mom. They would buy many things and put them in bags. Tim was always excited to help his mom. One day, they went to the store and bought many things. When they got home, Tim was ready to help his mom unpack the bags. They took out fruits, toys, and clothes. Tim was very happy. But as they unpacked, they found a filthy toy. It was dirty and not nice to touch. Tim and his mom washed the toy with soap and water. After that, the toy was clean and shiny. Tim played with the toy and had lots of fun.
