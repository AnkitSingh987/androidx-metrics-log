Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Cat and the Castle

One day, a chubby cat named Tom found a folder in the park. He was very happy. Tom loved to pretend he was a king. He took the folder home and made a plan. Tom put on his king hat and said, "I will use this folder to make a big castle. Then, I will be the best king ever!" He started to work on his castle. Tom's friend, a little bird named Sue, saw him and asked, "What are you doing, Tom?" "I am making a castle with this folder. I am King Tom!" he said. Sue wanted to help, so she pretended to be a queen. They both worked hard on the castle. Tom used the folder to make the walls, and Sue helped him put them up. When the castle was done, Tom and Sue played in it all day. They had so much fun being King Tom and Queen Sue. The folder had helped them make their dream come true.

Once there was a little boy who lived in a big house. He was very brave and always wanted to do new things. One day he decided to do something very difficult. He wanted to get some coal for the family fire. He looked around his house but he couldn't find any coal. So he decided to go on a journey. He went to the woods, looking for coal. He searched high and low, but he couldn't find anything. He decided to try digging. He found lots of dirt, but not any coal. He kept digging, searching for long time, but nothing. He decided he had to try something else. So he decided to ask for help. He looked for an old man and asked if he could help him. The old man smiled, and said he knew where to get coal. He took the little boy to a special place. There was coal everywhere! The little boy was so happy! He filled his sack with the coal and went back home. He was very proud of himself! He had done something difficult, but he had done it! He filled the fire and his family was so grateful.

Mum and Dad took their three year old daughter to the movies. She was so excited to watch her favourite cartoon, and couldn't wait to crunch on the popcorn. When they reached the theatre, the little girl grabbed a big bucket of popcorn and took a seat in the front row. She was just about to take a bite when she heard a loud sound. Mummy asked, "what was that sound?" Dad replied, "it sounds like the projector broke". Mummy quickly said, "oh no! That's too bad, we'll have to go home". The little girl was so upset. She was so looking forward to the movie, and now it was ruined. Mum had an idea. She said, "I have a better idea. Let's not go home, let's go back to the kitchen and watch a movie on the laptop. We can replace the popcorn with lollipops." The little girl was very surprised, but excited. She loved lollipops! They all went back to the kitchen, put on a movie and enjoyed the lollipops. The little girl was no longer upset. She was very happy and smiled the whole time.

Lily liked to follow her big sister, Anna, everywhere. Anna was very bossy and told Lily what to do and what not to do. Lily did not mind, because she wanted to be like Anna. One day, Anna said, "Let's go to the park and play with the white ducks." Lily nodded and ran to get her coat and shoes. Anna took Lily's hand and they walked to the park. At the park, they saw many white ducks swimming in the pond. Anna said, "Look, Lily, how pretty they are. Let's feed them some bread." She took out a bag of bread from her pocket and tore off some pieces. She threw them to the ducks and said, "Here, duckies, come and eat." Lily wanted to feed the ducks too, but Anna said, "No, Lily, you are too small. You might fall in the water or scare the ducks. You stay here and watch me." Lily felt sad and left out. She sat on the grass and watched Anna feed the ducks. But then, she saw something shiny on the ground. It was a silver coin. Lily picked it up and smiled. She thought, maybe I can buy something nice with this coin. She looked around and saw a man selling balloons. He had many balloons of different colors. Lily liked the red one best. She got up and ran to the man. She gave him the coin and said, "Please, can I have the red balloon?" The man smiled and gave her the red balloon. He tied a string around it and said, "Here you go, little girl. Enjoy your balloon." Lily thanked him and held the string tightly. She felt happy and proud. She looked for Anna and said, "Look, Anna, look what I have. A red balloon. It is so pretty." But Anna did not look happy. She looked angry and jealous. She said, "Lily, where did you get that balloon? How did you pay for it? Did you steal it?" She tried to grab the balloon from Lily, but Lily pulled it away. She said, "No, Anna, I did not steal it. I found a coin and I bought it. It is mine. You are so bossy and mean. You did not let me feed the ducks. You can't have my balloon." Anna and Lily started to fight over the balloon. They pulled and tugged and shouted. The ducks got scared and flew away. The man selling balloons shook his head and said, "Oh, dear, what a mess. Why can't they share and be nice?" The end.
