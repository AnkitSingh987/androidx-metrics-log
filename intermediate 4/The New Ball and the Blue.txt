Category: Intermediate
Title: The New Ball and the Blue

Once upon a time, there was a new ball. The ball was red and loved to roll. One day, the ball met a blue ball. The blue ball said, "Hi, I like to roll too!" The red ball and the blue ball rolled together. They rolled up and down hills. They rolled fast and slow. They had so much fun. Then, the red ball saw a leak in the blue ball. The blue ball was sad. The red ball said, "Don't be sad. We can fix the leak." They found tape and put it on the leak. The blue ball was happy again. They rolled together all day long.

Lily and Sam were young twins who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big statue of a lion in the park. The statue was very old and had cracks and moss on it. Lily and Sam thought it looked scary and fun. They ran to the statue and climbed on its back. They pretended they were riding the lion and shouted, "Roar! Roar! We are the king and queen of the jungle!" They laughed and had a good time. But then, a man came to the statue. He was angry and had a broom in his hand. He shouted, "Hey, you kids! Get off the statue! You are ruining it! This is a work of art, not a toy!" Lily and Sam were scared and jumped off the statue. They ran to their mom, who was sitting on a bench nearby. She hugged them and said, "Don't worry, my loves. The man was just doing his job. He has to take care of the statue and keep it clean. But you should not climb on it. It is not safe and it might break." Lily and Sam said they were sorry and they understood. They asked their mom if they could play somewhere else. She said yes and took them to the swings. They forgot about the statue and had fun again.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly lady who lived in a small house. She had a big heart and loved to help her friends. Every day, she would go to her garden and pick flowers for her friends. One sunny day, she saw a little girl crying near her garden. The elderly lady went to the girl and kneeled down to talk to her. The girl said she lost her toy in the garden. The elderly lady wanted to help the girl find her toy. The elderly lady and the girl looked in the garden. They found the toy under a big flower. The girl was very happy and hugged the elderly lady. From that day on, they became good friends and played in the garden together.

Once upon a time, there was a useful dishwasher. It lived in a big house with a happy family. The dishwasher loved to help the family by cleaning all their dirty plates and cups. One day, a little boy named Tim was playing with his ball inside the house. He was having a lot of fun, but his mom told him to be careful. Tim did not listen and he kicked the ball very hard. The ball flew across the room and hit the useful dishwasher. The dishwasher was sad, but it did not break. Tim's mom came into the room and saw what happened. She told Tim to say sorry to the dishwasher. Tim said sorry and gave the dishwasher a big hug. The useful dishwasher was happy again, and it kept on cleaning the family's plates and cups. And Tim learned to be more careful when he played inside the house.

Once upon a time, there was a playful pony named Poppy. Poppy liked to run, jump, and play in the big, green field. Every day, Poppy would play with her friends, the birds and the rabbits. One sunny day, Poppy found a big box. She looked at the box and said, "I want to fit in the box!" She tried to fit in the box, but she was too big. Poppy felt sad because she could not fit. Then, a little bird came and said, "Poppy, you are too big for the box, but we can play with it!" The bird and the rabbits helped Poppy push the box down the hill. The box rolled and rolled, and it hit a tree. When it hit the tree, the box opened, and lots of colorful balls came out! Poppy and her friends played with the balls all day long, and they were very happy.
