Category: Intermediate
Title: Jane the Girl and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. Jane loved to play with her toy car. The car was made of metal and was very pretty. Jane wanted to make her car shiny, so she decided to soak it in water. Jane put her car in a big bowl of water. She thought this would make her car successful and win all the races. But something unexpected happened. Instead of becoming shiny, the car began to change color. It turned from silver to a beautiful rainbow! Jane was so happy with her new rainbow car. She knew that even though it didn't become shiny, it was still successful. Now, her car was special and different from all the other cars. And that's how Jane's metal car became a magical rainbow car.

Lily and Ben were playing with their toys in the living room. They had a lot of fun making noises and pretending to be animals. But then, Lily saw a shiny red car on the shelf. She wanted to play with it. "Ben, can I have the car?" she asked. "No, it's mine. You can play with the doll," Ben said. "But I don't like the doll. I like the car. Please, Ben, let me have it," Lily begged. "No, no, no. Go away, Lily. The car is mine," Ben shouted. He pushed Lily away and grabbed the car. Lily felt angry and sad. She ran to the kitchen and saw the sink. She had an idea. She climbed on a chair and turned on the faucet. She filled the sink with water and threw the doll in it. She smiled and said, "Now you can't play with the doll, Ben. It's wet and dirty." But then, she heard a loud noise. It was Ben. He had followed her to the kitchen and saw what she did. He was furious. He threw the car at Lily and hit her in the head. Lily cried out in pain and fell off the chair. She hit her head on the floor and blood came out. "Mama, mama, help!" Ben screamed. Mama came running and saw the mess. She saw the water, the doll, the car, and Lily. She was shocked and scared. She picked up Lily and hugged her. She called the doctor and said, "Please, come quickly. My daughter is hurt." The doctor came and took Lily to the hospital. He said she had a big cut and a concussion. He said she needed stitches and medicine. He said she would be okay, but she had to stay in the hospital for a few days. Mama was sad and angry. She looked at Ben and said, "What have you done, Ben? Why did you hurt your sister? Why did you quit playing nicely? You are a bad boy. You have to say sorry to Lily and to me. You have to learn to share and be kind. You have to go to your room and think about what you did. You are in big trouble." Ben felt sorry and scared. He wanted to hug Lily and say sorry. He wanted to hug Mama and say sorry. He wanted to play with Lily and the car and the doll. But he couldn't. He had to go to his room and stay there. He had no toys, no friends, no fun. He felt lonely and gloomy. He wished he had never seen the car. He wished he had never quit playing with Lily. He wished he had never hurt her. He wished he could start over. But he couldn't. He had made a big mistake. And he had to pay for it.

One day, a boy named Tim saw a large box in the kitchen. He wanted to know what was inside. He asked his mom, "What is in the big box?" His mom smiled and said, "Let's open it and reveal what's inside." They opened the box together. Inside was a big refrigerator. It was very shiny and new. Tim was excited to see it. He asked his mom, "Can we put our food in it?" His mom said, "Yes, we can put all our food in this big refrigerator." Tim and his mom filled the refrigerator with food. They put apples, milk, and many other things inside. Tim was happy to have a big refrigerator in their kitchen. Now they had a nice place to keep their food cold and fresh.
