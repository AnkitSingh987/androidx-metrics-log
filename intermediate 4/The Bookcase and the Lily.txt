Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bookcase and the Lily

Once upon a time, there was a big bookcase in a small room. The bookcase was full of many colorful books. There was a little girl named Lily who loved to read the books every day. One day, it was very hot and dry outside. Lily wanted to relax and read her favorite book, but she could not find it. She looked everywhere in the room, but the book was not there. Lily felt sad and did not know what to do. Lily's big brother, Tom, saw her looking sad. He asked her what was wrong. Lily told him about her missing book. Tom decided to help her look for it. Together, they searched the room and found the book under the bookcase. Lily was so happy, and she hugged her brother. Now, she could relax and read her favorite book on the dry, hot day.

Once upon a time, there was a poor little bunny named Ben. Ben lived in a small hole near a big farm. One day, he went out to find some hay to eat. He saw a big pile of hay near the farm. As Ben hopped closer to the hay, he met a big, strong horse named Harry. Harry looked at Ben and said, "Hello, little bunny! What are you doing here?" Ben replied, "I am looking for some hay to eat. I am very hungry." Harry felt sad for the poor little bunny. He decided to help Ben. "I will protect you and help you get some hay," said Harry. Together, they went to the big pile of hay. Ben ate as much hay as he wanted, and his tummy was full and happy. From that day on, Ben and Harry became best friends. They played together and helped each other. Harry always protected Ben, and Ben always shared his hay with Harry. They were happy and lived together on the farm forever.

Mommy was in the kitchen. She was making dinner and wanted it to be special. She was so busy that she almost forgot something. She grabbed a spoon and looked around. "Where is my little one?" She said. Then, there was a small voice from behind her. "Here I am!" Mommy turned around and saw her 3 year old child with a big, impatient smile. Mommy smiled and said, "Ah, there you are. I almost forgot something. Come join me!" Mommy handed the spoon to her little one and they both went to work. She taught her a new way to use the spoon. Her little one was so happy. After a while, Mommy said, "Let's have a taste test." The little one was so impatient. She took the spoon and had a taste. She was delighted. Mommy smiled and said, "I am so glad you like it. I'm glad I didn't forget to get you!"

One day, a shy cat saw a big jar of oil. The cat wanted the oil, but it was not his. He thought, "I can steal the oil and no one will know." So, the shy cat took the jar and ran away. In the woods, the shy cat met a big dog. The dog said, "Hey cat, what do you have there?" The cat said, "It is my oil." The dog did not believe the cat. He said, "I think you stole it. Give it to me or I will tell on you." The cat was scared, so he gave the oil to the dog. The dog took the oil and ran away. But he did not see a big hole in the ground. He fell in the hole with the oil. The jar broke and the oil was all gone. The shy cat saw this and felt bad. He knew that stealing was not right. In the end, both the cat and the dog lost the oil and learned a hard lesson.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Amy. Amy had a journal. She liked to write about her day in her journal. One day, she wrote about how she was embarrass when she fell down in front of her friends. Amy had a friend named Tom. Tom was distant because he lived far away. One day, Tom came to visit Amy. They played and had fun. Then, Amy showed Tom her journal. She read the story about how she fell down. Tom said, "I will help you not be embarrass." Amy was happy. They practiced walking and not falling down. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew Amy's journal away. It was gone forever. Amy and Tom were sad. They could not find the journal. They looked and looked, but it was gone. The wind had taken it far away. Amy was very sad because she lost her journal, and Tom could not help her find it. The end.
