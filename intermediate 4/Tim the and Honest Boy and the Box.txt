Title: Tim the and Honest Boy and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a kind and honest boy named Tim. Tim had a big friend, a tiger named Tom. They lived in a small house in the woods. One day, Tim and Tom were playing when they found a big box. Tim said, "Let's send this box to a friend." They put the box on a cart and started to push it to their friend's house. As they pushed the cart, the box opened, and many balloons came out. Tim and Tom were very surprised. They did not know the box was full of balloons. The balloons lifted them up into the sky, and they had a fun ride. They laughed and played in the sky, and their friend saw them and joined the fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big, intelligent dog named Max. Max lived with a nice family in a little house. The little house had a yard with a fence around it. One day, Max saw a small cat outside the fence. The cat was rubbing her head on the fence. Max said, "Hi, cat! What are you doing?" The cat said, "I'm trying to get through the fence to play with you, Max." Max wanted to help the cat. He thought and thought. Then, he had an idea! Max went to the fence and started to rub his big head on it too. Suddenly, the fence door opened! The cat and Max were very happy and played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a magic finger that could do unknown things. She loved to play with her magic finger and show her friends what it could do. One day, Lily was playing with her magic finger and something unexpected happened. She pointed her finger at a toy car, and it started to move! Lily was so happy and excited. She showed her friends, and they were amazed too. They all wanted to play with the magic finger. But then, Lily's magic finger did something that made her feel embarrass. She pointed her finger at her friend's hat, and it flew away! Lily didn't know what to do. She was so sorry and didn't want to make her friend sad. So, she used her magic finger one more time to bring the hat back. Everyone was happy again, and Lily learned to be more careful with her magic finger.

One day, a big dog named Sam went for a walk. He saw a little boy named Tim playing with a toy. Tim looked at Sam and said, "Hi, big dog! Do you want to play?" Sam thought for a moment and said, "Yes, I want to play! What do you have?" Tim showed Sam a video on his little screen. The video had funny cats and dogs playing together. Sam and Tim laughed and laughed. They played all day, watching the video and running around. When the sun went down, Sam said, "Thank you for playing with me, Tim." Tim smiled and said, "You're welcome, big dog Sam. Let's play again tomorrow!" And they did.

Once upon a time there was a gear the gear was very attractive and it shone in the sun. One day the gear started to blink and it kept blinking until it was night. The gear was so attractive that a person came to take it while it was still blinking. Once the gear was gone, the person made some bad things with it. Everyone saw what he did and felt very sad. They couldn't help the gear and for that, the person laughed very darkly. The gear never blinked again and it was never seen. Everyone felt so sad and wished that the gear was back in its place. But it was too late, the gear was gone forever and the person never got punished for what he had done.

Once upon a time, there was a big, useful gate in a small town. This gate was very special because it could talk. One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to ride his bike to the park. He had to go through the gate to get there. As Tim got close to the gate, it said, "Hi, Tim! Do you want to ride your bike to the park?" Tim was very surprised that the gate could talk. He said, "Yes, Mr. Gate! I want to ride my bike to the park. Can you let me through?" The gate opened wide, and Tim started to ride his bike. But then, something unexpected happened. The gate turned into a big, friendly dog! The dog said, "Hi, Tim! I am not just a gate, I am also a dog! Let's ride to the park together!" Tim was very happy and said, "Wow! I did not know you could turn into a dog, Mr. Gate! Let's go to the park together!" So, Tim and the big, friendly dog rode to the park and played all day. They had lots of fun, and Tim knew that the useful gate was his new best friend.
