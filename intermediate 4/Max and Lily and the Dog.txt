Category: Intermediate
Title: Max and Lily and the Dog

One day, a big dog named Max saw a small cat named Lily. Lily was chewing on a toy. Max was envious because he wanted the toy too. He said to Lily, "Can I chew the toy too?" Lily said, "Yes, we can share the toy. But first, let's fix it with glue because it's broken." Max was happy and wagged his tail. They found glue and fixed the toy together. After the glue dried, Max and Lily chewed the toy together. They had a lot of fun and became good friends. They learned that sharing is better than being envious.

Once upon a time, there was a cat named Velvet. Velvet was a very foolish cat. She liked to play in the water, even though most cats do not like water. One day, Velvet was playing in a big puddle. She splashed and jumped, making a big mess. Soon, she was all wet and cold. Velvet did not like being wet and cold, but she had so much fun in the water. Velvet's friend, a dog named Max, saw her shivering. Max knew what to do. He told Velvet to lay in the sun to dry. Velvet listened to Max and laid down in the warm, sunny spot. Soon, she was all dry and warm again. Velvet learned that day that playing in the water can be fun, but it is important to dry off after. And she was not so foolish anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She loved to play hide and seek with her friends. One day, Mia found a hidden spot behind a big tree in her backyard. It was the perfect place to hide! Mia's mommy called her inside to take a bath. After the bath, Mia put on her soft, pink bathrobe. She felt so warm and cozy in it. Mia thought it would be fun to play hide and seek with her bathrobe on. So, she went back outside to find her friends. Mia hid behind the big tree again, and her friends started to look for her. They looked and looked but could not find Mia. Then, one of her friends saw the bathrobe's belt hanging out. He decided to pull it gently. Mia giggled, and her friends found her. They all laughed and played more hide and seek until it was time to go inside.

Once upon a time, there was a charming little cat. The cat loved to play with a green ball. One day, the cat went to the park to play with her friend, a dog. The dog said, "Let's play with the green ball!" As they played, the cat hit the ball very hard. It went far away into the woods. The cat and the dog looked for the green ball, but they could not find it. They were very sad. Then, a big bird saw the green ball. The bird thought it was food and took it away. The cat and the dog were sad because they lost their green ball. The moral of the story is to be careful when you play, or you might lose your toys.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Katie and Sam. Katie and Sam liked to joke and laugh together. One day they made a game. They would number their jokes and whoever had the funniest one would win the game. Katie was sure she had the best joke and began to laugh because she thought she was sure to win. But when Sam told his joke, everyone started roaring with laughter. Katie was very troubled. She quickly thought of a new joke, but it wasn't nearly as good. When it was time to decide the winner, Sam was chosen as the best joke teller. Katie was so troubled that she started to cry. She was so sad that she ran away and never returned to play with Sam again.

Tom and Lily were very tired. They had played all day with their toys, their books and their dog. They had also helped Mom and Dad with some chores, like putting away their clothes and setting the table. Now it was time for bed, but they wanted to stay up a little longer. "Can we have a snack, Mom?" Tom asked, rubbing his eyes. "Me too, me too!" Lily said, yawning. Mom smiled and said, "Okay, but only a small one. How about some peanuts and raisins?" Tom and Lily nodded and followed Mom to the kitchen. They sat on their stools and Mom gave them each a small bowl of peanuts and raisins. They munched happily and thanked Mom for the snack. "These are yummy, Mom," Tom said. "I like peanuts, they are crunchy," Lily said. Mom said, "I'm glad you like them. Do you know where peanuts come from?" Tom and Lily shook their heads. Mom said, "Peanuts grow under the ground, like potatoes. They are seeds of a plant that has yellow flowers. Farmers dig them up and dry them and then we can eat them." Tom and Lily were amazed. They had never seen peanuts growing under the ground. They wondered what other things grew under the ground. Mom said, "It's time to brush your teeth and go to bed. You can learn more about peanuts and other plants tomorrow." Tom and Lily agreed and gave Mom a hug and a kiss. They said, "We love you, Mom. We are grateful for you and for the peanuts and raisins." Mom said, "I love you too, Tom and Lily. You are my sweet peanuts. Now off you go to bed." Tom and Lily brushed their teeth and went to their room. They put on their pajamas and climbed into their beds. They said good night to each other and to their dog, who was sleeping on the floor. They closed their eyes and fell asleep, dreaming of peanuts and flowers.
