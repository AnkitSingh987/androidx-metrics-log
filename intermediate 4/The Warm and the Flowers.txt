Category: Intermediate
Title: The Warm and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a warm, sunny day. A little boy named Tim went outside to play with his toys. He liked to play with his toy cars and trucks. Tim was very happy. Tim's mom was doing work in the garden. She needed help to support the big flowers that were growing very tall. Tim wanted to help his mom. He took his toy truck and put the big flowers in the back. Tim's mom smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim!" Together, they supported the big flowers and finished the work in the garden. After they were done, Tim and his mom sat in the warm sun. They were both very happy to have helped each other.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little bunny named Bobo. Bobo loved to play with his friends, but sometimes he would pick things up that he should not touch. One day, Bobo saw something shiny on the ground. He wanted to pick it up, but he remembered his mom saying, "Do not touch things you do not know, Bobo." The next day, Bobo and his friends were playing near the shiny thing. His friend, Lulu the squirrel, did not know about the harsh rule. Lulu saw the shiny thing and picked it up. Suddenly, Lulu felt a big ouch! She dropped the shiny thing and cried. Bobo felt sad for his friend. Bobo told his mom about what happened to Lulu. His mom hugged him and said, "You did a good job, Bobo. You remembered the rule and did not touch the shiny thing. Now you know why we have that rule." From that day on, Bobo and his friends learned to be careful and always listen to their moms and dads. The moral of the story is to listen to the wise words of our parents and not touch things we do not know.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to watch cartoons on his TV. His favorite cartoon was about a slow turtle who had many friends. One day, Tim was watching his favorite cartoon when his mom said, "Tim, it's time to change your clothes and get ready for bed." Tim didn't want to stop watching his cartoon, but he knew he had to listen to his mom. So, Tim changed into his pajamas and got into bed. As he closed his eyes, he imagined he was friends with the slow turtle from his favorite cartoon. Together, they went on fun adventures and played with all the other animals in the forest. And every night, Tim would dream of new adventures with his turtle friend.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy little house near a stream. In the house lived a kind girl named Lily and her mom. They loved to sit by the stream and watch the fish swim by. One sunny day, Lily and her mom went to the stream. They found a big rock to sit on. "Mom, let's seat here and watch the fish," said Lily. "Yes, Lily, this is a nice spot," her mom replied. As they sat on the rock, they saw a little duck swimming in the stream. The duck was so happy, splashing water with its wings. Lily and her mom laughed and clapped their hands. They had a fun day by the stream.

One day, a lady went for a walk. She saw a wild cat. The cat was big and had pretty spots. The lady wanted to be friends with the wild cat. She said, "Hi, cat! Do you want to play?" The wild cat did not yield. It did not want to play. It said, "No, lady. I am wild. I do not play with people." The lady was sad. She walked away from the wild cat. She saw a little dog. The dog was happy and wanted to play. The lady played with the dog all day. They had fun and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a humble fixer named Tom. He loved to repair things. One day, he found a broken toy laser. It was sad and did not work. Tom said, "Don't worry, little laser. I will fix you!" He worked hard to repair the toy laser. He put new parts and made it all better. The toy laser was very happy. The toy laser said, "Thank you, Tom! You are the best fixer!" Tom smiled and felt proud. He knew that helping others was a good thing to do. And they played together all day long.
