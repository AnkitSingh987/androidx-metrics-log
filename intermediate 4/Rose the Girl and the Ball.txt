Category: Intermediate
Title: Rose the Girl and the Ball

Once there was a little girl named Rose. She enjoyed playing with her toys and singing her favourite songs every day. One day, Rose heard a very special ringing sound coming from outside. It was a bell. She was curious and decided to follow the sound. As she did, she arrived at a fun fair. Rose spent a long time playing the games and going on rides. Finally, she ended up at the prize-winning stall. Here, you had to throw a ball at the cans to win a prize. At first, Rose had not been so successful. But then, after five tries, something remarkable happened. She threw her ball and it hit the cans perfectly. She was so excited to win the prize! Rose realised that with a bit of practice and perseverance, it was possible to achieve perfect results. She took home the prize and felt very proud of herself. The moral of this story is that with hard work and determination, anything is possible.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a pale blue dress and loved to play outside. One day, she wanted to help her mom make a yummy cake. So, she went outside to gather pretty flowers to put on the cake. Lucy found many flowers in the garden. She picked red, yellow, and blue ones. She was very happy with her flowers. She ran back to the house and showed her mom the flowers she gathered. Her mom smiled and said, "Thank you, Lucy!" Together, they put the cake in the oven to bake. They waited and waited. When the cake was done, they took it out of the oven. They put the pretty flowers on the cake. Lucy and her mom were so happy with their beautiful cake. They shared it with their family and everyone loved it.

One day, a little bug went for a walk. The bug was an insect with wide wings. It liked to fly and play in the sun. The insect had many friends in the garden. They liked to play together. One morning, the insect saw a big flower. It was very pretty. The insect wanted to sit on the flower. It flew up and landed on the flower. The flower was so soft. The insect was very happy. But then, the sky got dark. It started to rain. The insect tried to fly away, but its wings were wet. The insect sighed, feeling sad. It could not fly home. The rain did not stop, and the insect stayed on the flower. The insect was cold and wet and could not go back to its friends.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys. They have a big box of toys in their room. Sometimes they share, sometimes they fight. One day, they find a new toy in the box. It is a rock. It is not a normal rock. It is a shiny rock. It has many colors. It sparkles in the light. Anna and Ben both want the rock. They say, "It is mine!" They pull the rock from each other. They are angry. They are loud. Mom hears them. She comes to their room. She sees the rock. She says, "This is not a toy. This is a special rock. It is from the university. Dad brought it home. He is a scientist. He studies rocks. He will be very sad if you break it." Anna and Ben are scared. They do not want to make Dad sad. They do not want to break the rock. They say, "Sorry, Mom. Sorry, Dad. We did not know. We will give it back." Mom smiles. She says, "It is okay. You did not know. But you have to be careful with things that are not yours. You have to ask before you take them. You have to share with each other. Do you understand?" Anna and Ben nod. They say, "Yes, Mom. We understand. We will share. We will ask. We will be careful." Mom hugs them. She says, "Good. I love you. Now, let's put the rock back in the box. Dad will show it to you later. He will tell you more about it. He will tell you why it is special. He will tell you where it came from. He will tell you what it is called." Anna and Ben are curious. They want to know more about the rock. They say, "Okay, Mom. Thank you. We love you too. We will wait for Dad. We will listen to him. We will learn from him." They put the rock back in the box. They play with their other toys. They share. They do not fight. They are happy.
