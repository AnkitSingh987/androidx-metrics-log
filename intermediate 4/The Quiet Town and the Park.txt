Category: Intermediate
Title: The Quiet Town and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a quiet little town. In this town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy trumpet. He loved his trumpet very much. One day, Tim went to the park to play. He saw his friend, Sue. Sue asked, "Can I play with your trumpet?" Tim said, "Yes, but please be careful. I value my trumpet a lot." Sue played with the trumpet, but she was not quiet. She played it very loud. Tim said, "Please play it quiet, Sue." Sue tried to play it quiet, but she did not know how. Tim showed Sue how to play the trumpet quiet. They played together and had fun. They both valued the quiet trumpet and played it all day long.

Once upon a time, there were two children. One was named Sam and the other was named Keith. Sam wanted to be a very smart whale, so he decided to practice every day. One day, Keith said to Sam, "How can you improve to be a smart whale?" Sam replied, "I can practice every day and listen to my parents. With practice, I will get better and better at swimming and learning about the ocean." So every day, Sam followed his advice and practiced swimming and learning more about whales. Soon enough, Sam became even smarter and he was excited to show off his new knowledge to his parents and friends. Sam was very proud of himself for improving and becoming a smart whale.

Ben was sad. He did not get an invitation to Sam's birthday party. Sam was his best friend. They liked to play with cars and trucks. Why did Sam not want him to come? He asked his mom. She said, "Maybe Sam's mom forgot to give you one. Or maybe she did not have enough invitations. Or maybe Sam is mad at you for something. You can ask him tomorrow at school." Ben sighed. He did not like any of those reasons. He wanted to go to Sam's birthday party. He wanted to eat cake and play games and get a goodie bag. He did not want to stay home and be bored. The next day, he saw Sam at school. He walked up to him and said, "Hi, Sam. Can I talk to you?" Sam looked at him and smiled. He said, "Hi, Ben. Sure, what do you want to say?" Ben felt nervous. He said, "I did not get an invitation to your birthday party. Did you forget me? Or are you mad at me?" Sam looked surprised. He said, "No, I did not forget you. And I am not mad at you. You are my best friend. I gave you an invitation yesterday. I put it in your backpack. Did you not see it?" Ben felt confused. He said, "No, I did not see it. I did not look in my backpack. I was too sad." Sam said, "Well, look now. It is there. I promise. And please come to my birthday party. It will be fun. We will have a fierce dinosaur theme. And you can wear a dinosaur hat. And we can roar like dinosaurs." Ben looked in his backpack. He found the invitation. It had a picture of a fierce dinosaur on it. It said, "You are invited to Sam's dinosaur birthday party. Roar!" Ben felt happy. He said, "Thank you, Sam. I am sorry I did not see it. I will come to your birthday party. It sounds awesome. I love dinosaurs. And I love you. You are my best friend." Sam said, "I love you too, Ben. You are my best friend. And I am sorry you were sad. But now you can be happy. And we can roar like dinosaurs." They hugged and roared. They were happy. They were best friends. They were fierce dinosaurs.

One day, a pirate named Tom went on a big boat. Tom was an honest pirate. He liked to joke and make his friends laugh. He had a big hat and a funny bird on his shoulder. Tom and his friends went to find a treasure. They looked at a map to see where to go. The map showed them an island with a big X. Tom and his friends sailed the boat to the island. They saw trees, sand, and water. They were happy and excited. Tom and his friends dug in the sand where the X was. They found a big box. Inside the box, there were shiny coins and pretty jewels. They laughed and shared the treasure. Tom made a joke and everyone laughed. They were happy because they were honest pirates.
