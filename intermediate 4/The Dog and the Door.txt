Category: Intermediate
Title: The Dog and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a little dog. The dog was very poor. He had no home. He walked and walked to find a place to sleep. One day, he found a small house. The house had a broken door. The dog saw a nail on the ground. He had an idea. He wanted to fix the door. He asked a big man for help. The man was nice. He nodded and smiled. The man used the nail to fix the door. The dog was happy. Now, he had a home.

Jimmy and Mommy were playing on the beach. The morning sun shone brightly and the waves were quiet. "Let's look for shells," said Mommy. Jimmy smiled and started running along the edge of the surf. "Be careful, sweetheart," Mommy said. "Don't go too far." Jimmy giggled and scampered farther down the beach. Mommy spotted something glimmering in the sand. As she walked closer, she saw a lot of tiny crabs scuttling around. She smiled and said, "Look, Jimmy! The crabs are scattering!" Jimmy stopped and looked. He watched the little crabs and smiled. Then he started running through the sand, chasing the crabs. After a bit he stopped and looked back at Mommy. She winked and said, "I'll join the fun in a minute." So, Mommy and Jimmy played together until they were both tired out. It was a wonderful morning, full of quiet beauty. Jimmy and Mommy hugged good-bye, and scampered off, ready for another day of beach fun.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a small, orange cat. The cat liked to play with his black ball. One sunny day, the cat met a big, brown dog. The dog saw the cat's black ball and wanted to play too. "Can I play with your black ball?" the dog asked the orange cat. The cat was happy to have a new friend to play with. So, they played together, displaying how much fun they had. As they played, a small, green bird watched them from a tree. The bird liked the orange cat and the brown dog. The bird flew down and said, "You both play so well together!" The cat and the dog smiled, happy to have a new friend to play with. From that day on, the orange cat, the brown dog, and the green bird played together every day, showing their happy friendship.

Once upon a time, in a big, big forest, there was a nice cave. In the cave, a pretty bird lay. The bird had many colors. The bird was very attractive. The bird had a friend, a small, small mouse. The mouse lived in the cave too. One day, a big, big bear came to the cave. The bear saw the pretty bird and the small, small mouse. The bear said, "This is my cave now. You need to go away." The pretty bird and the small, small mouse were very sad. The pretty bird said to the small, small mouse, "We need to make the bear go away. Let's think." The small, small mouse had an idea. They went to find help from their friends. The friends were a fast, fast rabbit, and a wise, wise owl. The fast, fast rabbit ran around the big, big bear. The wise, wise owl made a loud noise. The big, big bear got scared and ran away. The pretty bird and the small, small mouse were happy. They got their nice cave back. They thanked their friends, the fast, fast rabbit, and the wise, wise owl. They all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a lively dog named Spot. Spot loved to run, jump, and play with his friends. One day, Spot found a shiny copper ball in the park. He wanted to learn how to play with it, so he asked his friend, a wise old cat named Whiskers, to teach him. Whiskers showed Spot how to roll the copper ball with his nose and chase after it. Spot and Whiskers had a lot of fun playing with the ball. They laughed and ran around the park together. Other kids saw them and wanted to play too. Soon, the whole park was full of kids playing with the copper ball. They took turns rolling it and chasing after it. Spot and Whiskers were happy to teach everyone how to play with the ball. The day was full of fun, laughter, and new friends. And Spot learned that sharing and teaching others can make a day even more special.
