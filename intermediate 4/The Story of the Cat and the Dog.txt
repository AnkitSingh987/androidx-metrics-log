Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Cat and the Dog

One day, a yellow cat and a brown dog were good friends. They liked to go and play in the park. The cat said, "Let's go to the park today!" The dog said, "Yes, that is a good idea!" When they got to the park, they saw a big yellow ball. They both wanted to play with the ball, but they could not share. The cat said, "I saw it first!" The dog said, "But I want to play too!" They were not happy. Then, a big wind came and took the yellow ball up in the sky. The cat and the dog looked up and saw the ball go far away. They were sad, but then they saw a new toy on the ground. It was a big yellow rope. They both grabbed the rope and played together. They were happy and learned to share.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big tree in her yard. The tree had many round things called olives. Lily thought the olives were interesting. One day, Lily's mom told her to pick the olives from the tree. Lily tried to get the olives, but they were too high up. So, she asked her friend, a tall bird named Ben, for help. Ben was happy to help Lily. Ben flew up to the tree and picked the olives for Lily. Together, they got many olives. Lily's mom was happy with the olives they brought. She said, "Thank you, Lily and Ben! The tree has given us many olives. It has yielded a lot!" Lily and Ben smiled, happy to help.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Sue went for a walk. She saw a big tree with a hole under it. Sue wanted to touch the hole, but she was scared of the unknown. Sue was very brave, so she put her hand in the hole under the tree. She felt something soft and warm. Sue thought it was a small animal. But then, something unexpected happened! The soft and warm thing started to move! Sue was surprised and pulled her hand out. She saw it was not an animal, but a lost teddy bear. Sue was so happy to find the teddy bear, and they became best friends.

Sara was a little girl who liked to dream. Every night, she dreamed of different things. Sometimes she dreamed of flying in the sky with the birds. Sometimes she dreamed of swimming in the sea with the fish. Sometimes she dreamed of playing in the park with her friends. One night, she dreamed of a pink castle. It was very big and pretty. She wanted to go inside and see what was there. She walked to the door and knocked. A voice said, "Who is it?" "It's me, Sara. Can I come in?" she said. The voice said, "Yes, you can. But be careful. There is poison in the castle. It can make you sick or hurt you. Do not touch anything or eat anything." Sara was curious. She opened the door and went in. She saw many rooms and things. She saw a pink bed, a pink dress, a pink cake, a pink book, and a pink cat. She wanted to try them all. She forgot what the voice said. She jumped on the bed, but it was too soft and she fell down. She put on the dress, but it was too tight and she could not breathe. She took a bite of the cake, but it was too sweet and she felt sick. She opened the book, but it was too boring and she fell asleep. She petted the cat, but it was too angry and it scratched her. Sara woke up and cried. She wanted to go home. She ran to the door, but it was locked. She was trapped in the pink castle. She wished she had listened to the voice. She wished she had not dreamed of the pink castle. She wished she had dreamed of something else.

Once upon a time a gifted little girl named Daisy was playing in the park. Suddenly she heard a loud cheer coming from across the park. Daisy ran over and saw a group of children playing a game. "What are you playing?" Daisy asked excitedly. "We're playing a game to see who can throw their ball the furthest," one of the children replied. The children spread out to give Daisy a turn. Daisy grinned and tossed her ball. Everyone watched as it landed further than any of the others. "You win!" the children shouted. Daisy was overjoyed. She ran towards the children, who all cheered. She felt so proud and amazed by her own talent. The children spread out to try the game again - but this time they were all determined to beat Daisy!
