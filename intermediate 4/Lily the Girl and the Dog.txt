Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Dog

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a little girl named Lily. Lily had a toy dog that she loved very much. The toy dog was amazing and had a tape on it. Lily liked to play with the toy dog all day. One day, Lily's mom asked her to clean her room. Lily did not want to clean her room. She wanted to play with her amazing toy dog. But Lily's mom said she had to clean her room first. Lily started to clean her room. But she was not careful. She stepped on her amazing toy dog and broke the tape. The toy dog was not amazing anymore. Lily was very sad and cried. She wished she had been more careful while cleaning her room.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Kitty. Kitty had a friend named Doggy. Kitty was jealous because Doggy could run fast. Kitty wanted to run fast too. One day, Kitty and Doggy went to play in a big house. They ran and played all day. Then, they got lost. They could not find the exit. Kitty and Doggy were scared. They knew they needed to find the exit. Kitty thought hard. She had an idea. She climbed up high and saw the exit. Kitty showed Doggy the way. They were not lost anymore. Kitty was happy she could help. Doggy was not jealous of Kitty. They were best friends.

Lila loved butterflies. She liked to watch them fly in the garden. She liked their pretty colors and their soft wings. She wanted to catch one and hold it. One day, she saw a big yellow butterfly on a flower. She ran to it and tried to grab it. But the butterfly was fast and flew away. Lila chased it around the garden, but she could not catch it. She felt sad and tired. She sat down on the grass and looked at the sky. She saw a cloud that looked like a butterfly. She smiled and closed her eyes. She felt the sun on her face and the breeze on her hair. She fell asleep. When she woke up, she saw something on her hand. It was the yellow butterfly! It had landed on her while she was sleeping. It was still and quiet. Lila was very happy and surprised. She gently touched its wing with her finger. It felt soft and tickly. She said, "Hello, butterfly. Thank you for coming to me. You are very pretty and nice. I am sorry I chased you. I just wanted to be your friend." The butterfly moved its wings and flew up. It circled around Lila's head and then flew away. Lila waved goodbye and watched it go. She felt proud and happy. She had made a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a modern fireman named Tom. He loved to sing while he worked. Tom had a big red truck, and he would drive it to help people when there was a fire. One day, Tom was singing in his truck when he saw a cat stuck in a tree. He stopped the truck and climbed the tree to save the cat. But, when he got to the top, he found out the cat could sing too! Tom and the cat sang a happy song together. They became best friends and went on many adventures. From that day on, Tom the fireman and the singing cat worked together to help people and make them smile.

Once upon a time, there was a big fish named Fin. Fin lived in the dark ocean. He had many friends. One day, Fin saw a little girl named Lily by the ocean. She looked sad. "Hi, Lily. Why are you sad?" Fin asked. Lily sighed and said, "I lost my toy in the ocean. I can't find it." Fin wanted to help Lily. He told her, "I will find your toy for you." Fin swam deep into the dark ocean. He looked for the toy everywhere. Suddenly, he saw a shiny thing. It was Lily's toy! But, it was stuck in a big shell. Fin pushed the shell with all his strength. The shell opened, and out came the toy and a beautiful pearl. Fin took the toy and the pearl back to Lily. She was so happy to see her toy. She hugged Fin and said, "Thank you, Fin! You are the best friend ever!" Fin smiled and gave her the pearl too. Lily and Fin became best friends forever.
