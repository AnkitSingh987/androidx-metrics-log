Category: Intermediate
Title: The Parrot and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a big parrot. The big parrot lived in a tree. The tree was near a house. The big parrot liked to talk a lot. It said many words. The big parrot was very happy. One day, a cat came to the tree. The cat saw the big parrot. The cat wanted to catch the big parrot. The big parrot saw the cat and began to panic. The big parrot did not know what to do. It was very scared. The big parrot tried to fly away, but it was too late. The cat was too fast. The cat caught the big parrot. The big parrot was very sad. The cat took the big parrot away. The big parrot was never seen again.

John and his mom were at the park. John wanted to explore and his mom said he could if he was careful. John ran quickly towards a big structure. It was round and made of wood. He could see it was deep, so it would be fun to explore. John's mom asked him to hurry. She wanted to make sure he didn't get too close and peek inside it. John was excited and ran quickly. He was getting close and his mom started to get worried. John's mom said, "John, you need to hurry! That structure might be too deep for you!" John quickly ran away from the structure and back to his mom's side. He was happy that he followed his mom's orders and listened to her.

Once upon a time, there were two little boys. One day, they wanted to choose something for a snack. The first little boy looked in the cupboard and he chose a bowl. The second little boy looked in the cupboard and he chose a bowl too. The two little boys went outside with their bowls. They wanted to fill them with something yummy. They looked around and saw a tree with lots of little apples. They chose the little apples and filled their bowls. The two little boys were so happy! They ran back inside and ate their apples with big smiles. As they ate, they realized how much fun it was to choose things together. They decided to choose things together all the time.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with a porch. Lily loved to sit on the porch and look at the flowers in her garden. She liked to watch them bloom and grow. One sunny day, Lily saw a tiny seed on the ground. She picked it up and planted it in a pot on the porch. Every day, she gave the seed water and talked to it. She wanted it to grow big and strong. Soon, a little green plant came out of the seed. Lily was so grateful and happy. The plant grew bigger and bigger. One day, a beautiful flower bloomed on the plant. Lily smiled and knew that her love helped the flower grow.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a magic hand. This hand was incredible. It could do many things. Tim loved his hand very much. One day, Tim went to the park. He saw a cat stuck in a tree. The cat was scared. Tim wanted to help the cat. He used his incredible hand to reach up high. He took the cat out of the tree. Tim's mom told him to always mind his hand. She said, "Use your hand to help others, Tim." So, Tim did. He helped people and animals with his incredible hand. Everyone was happy. And Tim was happy too.

Once upon a time there were two friends. One was a brave rabbit and the other was a small bird. The rabbit wanted to fly to the land across the sea, but the bird thought this would be too hard. Rabbit didn't listen and started swimming out to sea. The bird ran to the shore and shouted out, "Come back, Rabbit! It is too far away!” But Rabbit paid no attention and kept swimming. The rabbit swam farther and farther away, until the bird could not see Rabbit any more. The bird sighed and thought, 'Rabbit will never make it'. But Rabbit kept swimming and swimming. Eventually, Rabbit reached the land. Rabbit thought the journey was easy. But the journey was much too hard for Rabbit. Rabbit never made it back home and was lost forever. The moral of the story is to always listen to your friends. Don't take on too much that is too hard for you. If you do, bad things might happen.
