Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Cat and the Spaghetti

One day, a crazy cat found some spaghetti. The cat loved to eat, so he ate the spaghetti very fast. He was very happy with his yummy food. The next day, the cat found more spaghetti, but it was not good. It was old and had started to rot. The cat did not want to eat it because it smelled bad. The cat learned that good spaghetti is yummy, but old spaghetti can rot and be yucky. So, he only ate fresh spaghetti from then on. The crazy cat was happy and healthy, and he loved his spaghetti.

Once upon a time, there was a blue cat named Bob. Bob liked to act in a play with his friends. They would all dress up and pretend to be different animals. One day, they were acting in a play about a big race. Bob was playing a fast dog, and his friends were playing a slow turtle and a silly rabbit. They all raced to see who could get to the exit first. In the end, the slow turtle won the race. Bob, the blue cat, was happy for his friend. They all laughed and had fun, and they couldn't wait to act in another play together.

Once upon a time, a little dog named Max was playing with his ball. Max loved his ball so much. He would throw it and run to catch it. Max was very good at not letting the ball get away from him. But one day, Max did something he did not want to do. He lost his ball. Max was very sad. Max looked for his ball all day. He was a persistent dog and did not want to give up. He looked under the trees and behind the bushes. Max even climbed up a small hill to look for his ball. Then, Max saw a big cat named Bella holding his ball. Max wanted his ball back, but Bella was not nice. She did not want to give the ball back. Max and Bella started to fight over the ball. They both wanted it so badly. During their fight, Max and Bella did not see that the ball was rolling away. It rolled down the hill and into a big hole. The hole was so deep that Max and Bella could not see the bottom. They both tried to get the ball, but it was too late. The ball had a big fall, and Max and Bella could not get it back. Max and Bella were both very sad, and they learned that fighting does not solve anything.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play on the street with her friends. They liked to run, jump, and chase each other all day long. One sunny day, Kitty and her friends were playing when a ball came rolling down the street. Kitty saw the ball and wanted to strike it. She ran as fast as she could and hit the ball with her paw. The ball flew high up into the sky. Kitty's friends clapped and cheered. They took turns striking the ball and playing together. They had lots of fun until the sun went down. Then, they all went home to their warm beds, happy and tired from their day on the street.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Will. He had a long, red ball that he loved to play with. Every day, he would take his ball outside and kick it high into the sky. One sunny day, Will went to the park with his mom. He took his long, red ball with him. He wanted to kick it so high that it would touch the clouds. Will ran fast and kicked the ball with all his might. The ball went up and up. It went so high that Will couldn't see it anymore. He was sad, but then he saw the ball come back down. It bounced and rolled right back to him. Will was so happy that he hugged his long, red ball and promised to always play with it.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy dove named Dolly. Dolly liked to fly around and see what her friends were doing. One day, Dolly saw her friend, a big bear named Benny. "Hi Benny!" Dolly said. "What are you doing?" Benny said, "I am trying to squeeze my big body into this small cave." Dolly watched as Benny tried to fit into the cave. Benny's body was too big, and he could not get in. Dolly had an idea. She flew away and came back with a long rope. "Let's use this rope to help you squeeze in," Dolly said. Benny tied the rope around his body and Dolly helped to pull. With a little help from Dolly, Benny was able to squeeze into the cave. Benny was very happy and said, "Thank you, Dolly!" From that day on, Dolly and Benny were the best of friends. They played together every day and had lots of fun. And Dolly learned that being nosy can sometimes be helpful.
