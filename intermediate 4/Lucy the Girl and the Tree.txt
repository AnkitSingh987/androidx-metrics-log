Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Tree

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to play outside. She saw a big tree with a hidden door. Lucy was very surprise. She opened the door and went inside. Inside the tree, Lucy found a big room. The room had a table with a big bowl on it. The bowl was full of yummy fruit. Lucy took a bite of an apple and it was very sweet. Lucy heard a small sound behind her. She turned around and saw a little squirrel. The squirrel was eating a nut from the bowl. Lucy and the squirrel became friends and played together all day. They were very happy in their hidden tree room.

One day, a little frog named Tim wanted to spring high. He saw a big tree and thought it was a good place to jump. He tried to spring up, but he could not reach the top. Tim asked his friend, a wise old owl, for help. The owl said, "I will give you a lecture on how to spring high." Tim listened carefully to the owl's words. The owl told him to bend his legs and push hard on the ground. The next day, Tim tried again. This time, he remembered the owl's lecture. He bent his legs and pushed hard on the dry ground. With a big jump, Tim sprang high and reached the top of the tree. He was so happy that he could finally spring high. "Thank you, wise owl!" Tim said. "Your lecture helped me a lot!" "You're welcome, Tim," the owl said. "Now you can spring high and see the world from up here!"

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved to dance. She was so graceful, like a fairy. One day, she wanted to practice her dance moves in her backyard, so she went outside. She wanted to look in the mirror, so she went inside to grab the telephone. She put the telephone up to her face and spun gracefully around, admiring how she looked. Suddenly, she heard a voice coming from the telephone. "Hello?" said the voice. The little girl was surprised and dropped the telephone. "Who's there?" she asked with a scared look on her face. The voice chuckled and said, "It's just me. I'm playing a game with all the children around the world." The little girl smiled and grabbed the telephone again. She said, "Okay, I'm ready!" The voice explained the game to her and she enjoyed playing it. When the game was over, the voice said goodbye and the little girl thanked him before hanging up. She danced back outside with a smile on her face, feeling so graceful.

Once upon a time, there was a graceful cat named Bella. She loved to play with her cable toy. Bella would jump and run all day with her toy. One day, she wanted to serve her friends a nice treat. Bella's friends came to her house. They were very happy to see her. They all played with the cable toy and had a lot of fun. Then, Bella went to the kitchen to serve her friends some yummy snacks. But when Bella came back, she saw something unexpected. A big, friendly dog was playing with her friends and the cable toy! At first, Bella was scared, but soon she saw that the dog was nice. They all played together and had a great time. From that day on, Bella, her friends, and the big, friendly dog were the best of friends.

One day, a parent and a child went for a walk. They saw a bright red car. The car was broken. The parent said, "I can repair it." The parent fixed the car. The child was happy. They got in the car to go for a ride. The car went fast! The child said, "This is fun!" But then, the car started to talk! It said, "Thank you for fixing me!" The parent and the child were surprised. They made a new friend, the talking car. They all went for a fun ride together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was a tough boy who liked to run and play all day. One day, Tim was playing with his favorite toy near the toilet. He was having so much fun! But then, Tim made a mistake. He accidentally dropped his toy into the toilet. He was very sad and didn't know what to do. He tried to reach for the toy, but it was too far down. He thought about tearing a piece of paper to get it out, but he knew that would not work. Tim decided to ask his mommy for help. She came to the rescue and got the toy out of the toilet for him. Tim was so happy! He learned that it is always good to ask for help when you need it. And from that day on, he made sure to play with his toys far away from the toilet.
