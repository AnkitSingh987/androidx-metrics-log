Category: Intermediate
Title: The Man and His Mule and the Cave

One day, a man and his mule went on a trip. The man had to pack a lot of things on the mule's back. The mule was very strong and could carry many things. They walked and walked until they found a mysterious cave. Inside the cave, they saw a big box. The man was curious and opened the box. He found many colorful balls. The mule was scared, but the man was happy. He thought he could play with the balls. The man picked up a ball and threw it. Suddenly, the ball turned into a big cake! The man was surprised and the mule was happy. They found out that the balls in the mysterious cave could turn into yummy food. They had a fun party in the cave with lots of food and laughter.

Once upon a time, in a small school, there was a class of little kids. They were all friends and liked to play together. In the class, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a slow boy, but he had a big heart. One day, the class went to the park for a picnic. They played games, laughed, and had fun. When they got thirsty, their teacher gave them juice to drink. Tim was slow at drinking his juice, but he didn't mind. As the day went on, Tim's friends helped him to catch up with the games. They played together and had a great time. In the end, the whole class learned that being slow was okay, as long as they were kind and helped each other.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable dog named Spot. Spot liked to do yoga with his friend, a little girl named Lisa. They did yoga every day in the big, green park. One day, a big, funny bird came to the park. The bird saw Spot and Lisa doing yoga. The bird stared at them. He wanted to do yoga too. Spot and Lisa saw the bird and smiled. They all did yoga together. Spot, Lisa, and the funny bird had a lot of fun. They became good friends. From that day on, they always did yoga together in the big, green park.

One day, a boy named Tim saw his shadow on the ground. He thought it was a fake friend. He said, "Hello, shadow. Can you help me find my toy?" The shadow did not talk, but it moved when Tim moved. Tim walked and looked for his toy. The shadow followed him. They looked under the bed, behind the door, and in the toy box. But they did not find the toy. Then, Tim's mom came into the room. She saw Tim talking to his shadow. She smiled and said, "Tim, the shadow cannot talk. It is not a real friend." Tim was sad, but then his mom showed him where his toy was. It was in the closet! Tim was happy and said, "Thank you, mom! You are the best helper!"

One day, a big race was going to happen in the town. All the animals were very excited. A fast rabbit, a strong bear, and a silly monkey were going to run in the race. Everyone wanted to see who would win. The race started and all the animals ran as fast as they could. The rabbit was in the lead, but then something unexpected happened. A disgusting smell arrived, and the rabbit stopped to see what it was. The bear and the monkey kept running. The rabbit said, "Ew, what is that smell?" A skunk appeared and said, "Sorry, that was me!" The rabbit laughed and they both watched the race together. In the end, the bear won the race, but the rabbit and the skunk became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside. One day, she saw an amazing slide in the park. It was big and shiny. Lily wanted to go on the slide. Lily climbed up the ladder and sat at the top of the slide. She looked down and saw her friend, Tommy, at the bottom. Tommy was holding a big bag of corn. He was going to feed the ducks at the pond. Lily slid down the slide very fast. She laughed and had so much fun. When she got to the bottom, she and Tommy went to the pond. They fed the corn to the ducks. The ducks were happy, and Lily and Tommy had a great day.
