Category: Intermediate
Title: Missy the Cat and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was an elderly cat named Missy. Missy lived in a small house with her friend, a tiny mouse named Tim. They liked to play and talk all day long. One day, Missy said to Tim, "I hear a wonder in the big room. Let's go see!" Tim was scared, but he followed Missy to the big room. They saw a big box in the room. Missy and Tim were very curious. Missy and Tim opened the box and found a big, loud dog inside. The dog barked and chased them. They ran and ran, but the dog was too fast. In the end, the dog caught Tim and Missy, and they were never seen again.

One day, a boy named Tim found a coin on the ground. The coin was shiny and round. He picked it up and put it in his pocket. Tim was happy to find the coin. Tim saw a ball in the park. He wanted to kick it. He ran to the ball and gave it a big kick. The ball went high in the sky. Tim was good at kicking the ball. Tim's friend, Sam, came to play. Sam was reliable and always played with Tim. They kicked the ball and found more coins. They had a fun day at the park together.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Lucy. She always dreamed of going on a tour around the world. One day, when Lucy was walking through the park, she saw an old bus waiting and a man standing next to it. "This is for you," said the man with a smile, "come and join me for a tour of the world!" Lucy was very excited. She hopped into the bus and suddenly noticed how dirty it was. She reminded herself to stay brave and she looked out the window. "Welcome aboard," said the man. He pointed to a map on the wall and began to explain all the places they would be visiting. The two of them were soon off, zooming away in the old, but clean bus. After a while they stopped at a big, beautiful palace. "Wow," said Lucy. "Welcome to the kingdom," said the man. Lucy stepped out, she felt so lucky to experience something so incredible. She took a deep breath, thankful for this amazing tour.

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a walk. She wanted to find a new place to play. Sue walked and walked, but she could not find a good place. She started to feel sad. Sue saw a boy named Tom. Tom asked, "Why are you sad?" Sue said, "I can't find a new place to play." Tom said, "I think I know a place. Follow me!" They walked to a normal-looking tree. Sue looked at the tree and said, "But this is just a normal tree." Tom smiled and said, "This is not just a normal tree. It is magic!" Tom touched the tree and it opened a secret door. They went inside and found a big, fun place to play. Sue was happy, and they played all day. The magic tree was their special secret.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. Jane loved to pour water on her plant every day. She was very proud that it was so green and healthy. One day, Jane did not want to pour water on her plant. It was a warm day, so she thought that it would be alright if she skipped this one day. The next day, when she looked at her plant, she knew that she had made a mistake. All the leaves were withered and dry. She had not given it water, so it had not been healthy. Jane was very sad, but she knew that she could fix it. She got out her watering can and started to pour water on her plant. Soon enough, it had plenty of water. The leaves were not withered anymore. Jane had learned her lesson and was now very careful to give her plant lots of water every day. Moral of the story: It's always good to take care of things so that they stay healthy.
