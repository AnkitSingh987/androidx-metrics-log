Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Cup

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved the color blue. She had a blue dress, blue shoes, and even a blue room. One day, Sue went on a walk with her mom. They wanted to find something new and fun. As they walked, Sue saw a blue tea cup on the ground. "Look, mom!" she said, "I found a blue tea cup!" Sue picked it up and showed it to her mom. Her mom smiled and said, "What a pretty blue tea cup! Let's take it home and have some tea." When they got home, Sue and her mom made some tea. They poured the tea into the blue tea cup and another cup. Sue and her mom sat down and had a tea party. They laughed and talked, and Sue was so happy she found the blue tea cup.

Once upon a time, there was a young girl named Daisy. She was very lively and loved to explore the world. One day, Daisy decided to raise some animals. She gathered some eggs and put them in a basket. The eggs hatched and out came some little chickens. Daisy was so happy and she took care of them every day. She fed them and made sure they had a safe place to sleep. One day, Daisy noticed that the chickens were not feeling well. She asked them what was wrong and one of they said, "Someone has put some poison in our food." Daisy was very worried and quickly took them away from the food. Daisy then went to get help from her wise neighbor. The neighbor said, "You must be more careful next time. Do not forget to check the food for poison and keep a close eye on the chickens!" From then on, Daisy was very careful and took extra care of her lively friends. They were very happy and healthy. The end!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toys and eat yummy snacks. One day, Tim felt hungry and decided to find a snack in the cupboard. The cupboard was very big and high, so he had to stand on his toes to reach it. As Tim opened the cupboard, he saw a scary shadow on the wall. He was scared and wanted to run away. But then, he saw it was just a funny hat that his mom put on the top shelf. Tim laughed because he was not scared anymore. Tim found a yummy snack in the cupboard and ate it all up. After eating, he put the funny hat on his head and played with his toys. He was very happy that he decided to be brave and not scared of the shadow. And from that day on, Tim always remembered to be brave and not let scary things stop him from having fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little dragon. He was a nervous dragon. He had big wings, but he was scared to fly. One day, the little dragon met a kind bird. The bird said, "Don't be scared. I will help you fly." The dragon felt happy and wanted to try. The bird and the dragon went to a big hill. The bird showed the dragon how to flap his wings. The dragon tried, and he started to fly! He was not nervous anymore. They both flew together in the sky, and the dragon was very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very naughty boy. He liked to play tricks on his friends and family. One day, Tim saw his mom cooking in the kitchen. He noticed the steam coming out of the pot. Tim thought it would be fun to make his friends think there was a cloud inside the house. So, he took the pot off the stove and ran to show his friends. But Tim's mom saw him and said, "Tim, you are being naughty! The steam is hot and can hurt you and your friends." Tim felt bad and said sorry. He learned that it's not good to be naughty and play with things that can hurt others.
