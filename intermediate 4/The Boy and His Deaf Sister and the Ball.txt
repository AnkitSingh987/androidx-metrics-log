Category: Intermediate
Title: The Boy and His Deaf Sister and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a boy and his deaf sister. They liked to play in the park. One day, they went to the park to play with their ball. The boy and his sister started to count how many times they could throw the ball to each other. They were very happy to play together. The boy threw the ball to his sister, and she threw it back. They did this many times. But then, a big dog came to the park. The dog saw the ball and wanted to play too. The dog ran very fast and took the ball away. The boy and his sister were sad. They tried to get their ball back, but the dog was too fast. They went home with no ball and a bad day.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there was a small yellow bird. The bird loved to take little things to its nest. It was very careful when it did this, because it did not want to drop anything. One day, the yellow bird saw a shiny red ball. It wanted to take the ball to its nest. So, it tried to be very careful. But the ball was too big for the bird to hold in its beak. The bird tried and tried, but it could not take the ball. Later, the bird saw a little girl crying. She had lost her shiny red ball. The yellow bird knew where the ball was! It flew to the ball and tried to take it again. This time, the bird was more careful. It pushed the ball with its beak, all the way to the little girl. She was so happy to have her ball back, and they became friends. The yellow bird learned that being careful and helping others can make new friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a rare gold rock. It was very pretty and shiny. All the people in the town liked to look at the gold rock. One day, a big man came to the town. He wanted to take the gold rock away. The people did not want the big man to take their pretty rock. They said, "Stop! You can't take our gold rock!" The big man tried to take the gold rock, but the people did not let him. They all worked together to stop the big man. They were very happy to keep their rare gold rock. The people in the town lived happily ever after with their pretty gold rock.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily got an invitation to a party. She felt happy but also a little unknown because she did not know who sent it. Lily went to the party and saw many kids. They were all playing and laughing. A boy named Tom came up to her and said, "Hi, I am Tom. I sent the invitation. Do you want to play with us?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, I would like that." Before they played, Tom's mom said, "Everyone, let's wash our hands first." All the kids washed their hands and then played together. Lily made new friends and had lots of fun at the party.

One day, a little independent cat named Tim went for a walk. The sky was gray and dark. Tim saw his friend, a dog named Sam. Sam said, "Look, it will rain soon!" Tim and Sam decided to play before the rain came. They ran and jumped in the park. After a while, the rain began to fall. Tim and Sam were wet but happy. As they ran home, they saw a big box. It was a surprise! Inside the box, there were toys and treats. Tim and Sam shared the surprise and played all day, even in the rain.

John valued his lively goat, but knew it was time for a change. He took his goat to the market and talked to many people to find the perfect home for it. Finally, he found a family who was very excited to take the goat. They had already prepared a nice home for the goat, and John could tell that the family would take great care of it. John was sad to say goodbye to his goat, but he was also happy. He felt that he was doing the right thing. After a few moments of sadness, he was feeling better and was ready for the next step in his life. John's life had changed for the better because of the goat. Not only did the goat bring him joy, but it also taught him to be more grateful for what he had. He would never forget the amazing memories he had with the lively goat.
