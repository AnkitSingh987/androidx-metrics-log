Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Clumsy Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball. He would run and jump and shoot the ball into a big box. Spot wanted to get a high score. One sunny day, Spot went to the park. He saw his friend, a little bird named Chirpy. Chirpy wanted to play with Spot. They took turns to shoot the ball into the box. Chirpy was very good at it. Spot was clumsy, but he tried his best. Spot and Chirpy played all day. They laughed and had fun. Even though Spot was clumsy, he got a high score. Spot and Chirpy were happy. They went home tired but excited to play again another day.

Once upon a time, there was a purple cat named Mimi. Mimi loved to take walks in the park. One day, Mimi found a bridge in the park. She had never seen it before. Mimi was excited to cross the bridge. She walked onto the bridge and looked down at the water. She saw some fish in the water. The fish were also purple, just like Mimi. After Mimi crossed the bridge, she found a new friend. The new friend was a purple dog named Max. Mimi and Max played together all day. They had so much fun and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree in a big park. Many kids liked to play near the tree. One day, a man came to the park and put up a sign. The sign said, "No playing near the tree." The kids were sad. They wanted to play near the tall tree, but the sign told them not to. They did not know what to do. The big kids tried to order the little kids to play somewhere else, but the little kids did not want to listen. One day, a kind mom saw the kids looking sad. She walked up to the sign and read it. She knew the kids loved playing near the tall tree. So, she went to talk to the man who put up the sign. The man listened to the mom and saw how sad the kids were. He took down the sign and let the kids play near the tall tree again. Now, the kids were happy and played near the tree every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little yellow bird. The bird lived in a big tree. The bird loved to fly and sing. One day, the bird saw something on the ground. It was a piece of plastic. The bird was curious. It picked up the plastic with its beak. The bird tried to taste the plastic. It did not taste good. The bird knew it should not eat the plastic. So, the bird put the plastic in the trash. It flew back to its tree and sang a happy song.

There once was a little girl on the playground. She was having a lot of fun playing but she started to get a bit naughty. She took somebody else's toy and wouldn't give it back. Then her mommy came over. "You must behave," said Mommy. The girl felt a bit guilty and put the toy back. Then they went home and Mommy said, "You can have food after you behave nice." So the girl cleaned up her room and was very polite to her sister. The mommy was happy and gave her a big plate of food. The little girl was very happy and learned that when she behaves nice, good things happen.

Once upon a time, there was a big tower. It was very tall and had many windows. Inside the tower, there lived a little girl named Sally. Sally was very sad because she was all alone in the tower. Her toys were spoiled, and she had no one to play with. One day, a brave boy named Tom came to the tower. He saw Sally in the window and shouted, "Hi! I am Tom. Why are you sad?" Sally said, "I am sad because I am alone and my toys are spoiled." Tom wanted to save Sally and make her happy. He climbed the tower and went inside. Tom fixed Sally's toys and played with her. They had lots of fun together. Sally was not sad anymore. In the end, Tom and Sally became best friends. They played in the tower every day. Sally was happy because Tom saved her from being alone. And they lived happily ever after.
