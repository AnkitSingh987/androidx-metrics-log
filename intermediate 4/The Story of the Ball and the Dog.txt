Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Ball and the Dog

Once upon a time there was a group of little children. They were all having fun playing together, but there was one little boy who was feeling very frightened. He was the only one who was feeling scared and he did not want to join the group. The other children noticed how scared the little boy was and they decided to calm him down. So they started playing a game where they were pretending to kick a ball around. The boy still seemed scared, but he started to join in the game. Just when the little boy was starting to feel less frightened, a big scary dog came running around the corner! The little boy was so scared that he started to cry. He ran over to the group of children and they all hugged and comforted him. Suddenly one of the children had an idea. He said, "We can chase the puppy away together by kicking at it. We're a group and we can do it!" The other children agreed and so they all kicked at the puppy together. It worked! The puppy ran away and the little boy was no longer frightened. He didn't feel scared anymore and he was happy to be playing and laughing with the group of children.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big, mysterious box. Tim was curious and wanted to find out what was inside the box. He tried to open it, but it was too heavy. Then, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue was playing with a basketball. Tim asked, "Can you help me open the box?" Sue said, "Yes, let's try together!" They both pushed and pulled, and the box opened. Inside the box, there were many toys. But there was a problem. There were too many toys for just Tim and Sue. They decided to share the toys with other kids at the park. Everyone was happy and had fun playing together.

Ben and Lily were twins who liked to play in the farm. They liked to see the cows, the pigs, the sheep and the chickens. One day, they saw a big chicken with white feathers and a red comb. They wanted to touch it and play with it. "Can we ask the farmer if we can play with the chicken?" Ben said. "OK, let's go ask him," Lily said. They ran to the farmer, who was busy with his tractor. They waited until he saw them and smiled. "Hello, Ben and Lily. What do you want to ask me?" the farmer said. "Can we play with the big white chicken, please?" Ben and Lily said together. The farmer looked at the chicken and frowned. He shook his head. "No, you can't play with that chicken. That chicken is not a nice chicken. That chicken is a rooster. He is very loud and mean. He can peck you and hurt you. You should stay away from him," the farmer said. Ben and Lily felt sad. They really wanted to play with the chicken. They thought he looked soft and pretty. They did not believe the farmer. They thought he was lying. "Let's go play with the chicken anyway. The farmer is busy. He won't see us," Ben whispered to Lily. "OK, let's go. But be careful," Lily whispered back. They sneaked away from the farmer and went to the chicken coop. They saw the big white chicken sitting on a nest. He looked sleepy. Ben and Lily smiled. They thought he was cute. They reached out their hands to touch him. But as soon as they did, the chicken woke up. He saw them and got angry. He opened his beak and crowed loudly. He flapped his wings and jumped at them. He pecked them and scratched them with his claws. Ben and Lily screamed. They felt pain and fear. They ran away from the chicken. They cried and called for the farmer. The farmer heard them and came to help them. He saw their wounds and blood. He scolded them and took them to the house. He cleaned their wounds and put bandages on them. He told them they were very naughty and foolish. He told them they should have listened to him and stayed away from the chicken. He told them they could not play in the farm anymore. He told them they had to go home and stay in their room. Ben and Lily felt sorry and ashamed. They wished they had not played with the chicken. They wished they had obeyed the farmer. They learned their lesson. They never played with the chicken again.
