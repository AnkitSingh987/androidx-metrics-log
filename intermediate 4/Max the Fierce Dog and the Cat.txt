Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Fierce Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a fierce dog named Max. Max liked to cook food for his friends. One day, Max was cooking a big meal for everyone. He was very excited. While Max was cooking, he saw a small cat named Luna. Luna was playing near the toilet. Max did not like Luna playing near the toilet. He thought it was not safe. So, Max stopped cooking and went to talk to Luna. Max said, "Luna, do not play near the toilet. It is not safe." Luna did not listen to Max. She kept playing. Max was sad, but he had an idea. Max went back to cooking and made a toy for Luna. When Luna saw the toy, she stopped playing near the toilet. Max and Luna played together and had fun. Max was happy that Luna was safe, and they all enjoyed the big meal.

Once upon a time there was a boy who wanted a real Furniture to sit on. But all he had was a fake one! He was so sad. Suddenly, he heard a tiny voice speak. It was coming from his furniture! "I will help you," it said. The boy was so surprised! He couldn't believe it! But he trusted the furniture and believed it would help. The next day, the boy woke up and the furniture was gone! But then, in the corner of his room, he saw a beautiful real furniture that he could sit on. He was so happy! He couldn't believe it! He looked around the room, but he couldn't see the fake furniture anywhere. The boy knew that the furniture had kept its promise and had helped him get something real! He thanked the furniture and said goodbye.

One day, a little fish was swimming in the sea. The fish saw a big shark. The shark was not happy. The shark was frustrated. The little fish was scared. The shark said to the fish, "Give me your food!" The shark demanded the fish's food. The little fish did not want to give the shark his food. The fish said, "No, it is my food!" The shark got angry. The shark and the fish started to fight. They pushed and hit each other. The little fish tried to swim away, but the shark was too fast. The shark caught the little fish. The little fish was sad. The shark ate the fish and his food. The fish was gone, and the shark was still frustrated. It was a bad ending for the little fish.

One day, a girl named Lily went to her grandpa's house. She saw a grill in the backyard. She asked her grandpa, "What is this?" He said, "This is a grill, Lily. We cook food on it." Lily wanted to help her grandpa cook. He said, "Okay, but first we need to unlock the grill." Lily used a key to unlock the grill. They put food on it. They made spicy chicken. While they were eating, a big bird came down. It took the spicy chicken! Lily and her grandpa were sad. But then, the bird came back. It brought a big fruit. They ate the fruit and were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had big eyes and thick, soft fur. He loved to play with his ball and run in the park. One sunny day, Spot saw a little bird in the sky. The bird was singing a sweet song. Spot wanted to be friends with the bird, so he decided to follow it. He watched the bird with his big eyes as it flew from tree to tree. As Spot followed the bird, he wagged his tail and barked happily. The bird saw Spot and flew down to say hello. They played together all day, running and jumping in the thick grass. Spot and the bird became the best of friends, and they had many fun adventures together.

Once upon a time, there was a rich boy who lived in a great big house. Every day the boy went outside to play and explore. One day, the boy was looking for something new and exciting to do. He went to a big field and found an old story book on the ground. He picked it up and opened it. To his surprise, the book was about a magical kingdom with beautiful castles, kings and queens. The boy was so excited to read the story. He continued to read and soon learned that the magical kingdom was filled with riches. He couldn't believe it! He had discovered a rich surprise. The boy was so happy that he had found the story. He kept the book and read it to his friends. They were all amazed by the surprise and they all loved the story.
