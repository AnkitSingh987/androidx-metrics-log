Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Pants

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim was wearing his favorite pants with big red and blue stripes. He loved to play with his friends and run around the park. While Tim was playing, a big wind came and blew off his pants! He was very ashamed and didn't know what to do. His friends saw this and started to laugh. Tim wanted to cry, but then he remembered to be brave. Tim's mom saw what happened and came to help. She said, "Don't worry, Tim. I have your extra pants in my bag." Tim was surprised and happy. He quickly put on his extra pants and went back to play. His friends stopped laughing and asked, "How did you have extra pants?" Tim replied, "My mom always has a plan." From that day on, Tim was never ashamed, and he knew his mom would always be there to help him.

One day, a little girl named Amy saw a weird insect in her garden. She had never seen such a bug before. It had big eyes, tiny legs, and a funny face. Amy was scared and didn't know what to do. Amy's mom saw her looking at the insect and asked, "What's wrong, Amy?" Amy pointed at the bug and said, "Look, Mom! A weird insect!" Her mom smiled and suggested, "Why don't you say hello to the insect? Maybe it's friendly." Amy thought about it and said, "Okay, Mom." She went closer to the insect and said, "Hello, weird insect!" To her surprise, the insect smiled back and said, "Hello, Amy! I'm a friendly bug. My name is Buzz." They started to play together and had a lot of fun. As they played, Amy learned that even if something looks weird, it can still be nice and friendly. She was happy that she listened to her mom's suggestion and made a new friend. The moral of the story is: don't judge someone or something by their looks.

Once upon a time, there was a soft cat named Mimi. Mimi loved to play with her ball. One day, Mimi saw a big box. The box had a sign that said, "Do not touch." Mimi did not mind the sign. She wanted to see what was inside the box. Mimi jumped on the box and opened it. Inside the box, there was a shiny thing. Mimi thought it had value. She wanted to play with the shiny thing. Mimi took it out and played with it all day. At night, Mimi's mom found out about the shiny thing. She was very sad. Mimi did not know that the shiny thing was very important to her mom. Mimi's mom had to give the shiny thing back to the person who owned it. Mimi felt bad for not minding the sign. From then on, Mimi learned to always mind what the signs say.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. Sally was feeling sick, so her mom made her some warm soup. Sally thought the soup was boring. She wanted to eat cake instead. But her mom said, "No cake, Sally. You need soup to feel better." One day, Sally's friend Tim came over to play. Sally still felt sick and couldn't play with Tim. Tim saw Sally eating the boring soup. He wanted to make the soup fun for Sally. So, Tim put a big, red nose on and pretended to sneeze really loud. "Achoo!" he said, and he made a funny face. Sally laughed and laughed. She didn't think the soup was boring anymore. She ate all the soup and started to feel better. Sally learned that sometimes, things that seem boring can be fun with friends. And Tim learned that helping a friend can make you feel good inside.

Once upon a time, there was a wild bird named Tom. He loved to fly and sing all day. Tom had many friends in the big tree. One day, Tom saw a little car that needed help. The car was sad because it could not go. Tom wanted to care for the car. He thought it needed oil to go fast. So, he flew to find some oil. Tom found some oil and gave it to the car. The car was happy and could go fast again. But then, something unexpected happened. The car turned into a big, friendly bear! The bear said, "Thank you, Tom, for caring and helping me." The bear was a friend, too. Now, Tom, the bear, and all the friends in the big tree played together every day.
