Category: Intermediate
Title: Kiki the Kangaroo and the Mom

Once upon a time, there was a kangaroo named Kiki. Kiki loved to jump and play all day. But one day, Kiki got very filthy. She was covered in dirt and mud from playing outside. Kiki wanted to go inside her house to eat her dinner, but her mom would not allow her. Her mom said, "You are too filthy, Kiki! You must take a bath first." Kiki was sad, but she knew her mom was right. So, Kiki took a bath and scrubbed all the dirt and mud off. She was clean again! Her mom was happy and allowed Kiki to come inside. They had a yummy dinner together and Kiki promised to stay clean next time.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went for a walk. They saw a big mud puddle. Tim said, "Look, Spot! Mud!" Spot barked and wagged his tail. The sky was dark, and it looked like rain would happen soon. Tim and Spot played in the mud. They got very dirty. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise. A big, sleepy bear came out of the woods. The bear looked at Tim and Spot and said, "You are making too much noise. I am trying to sleep!" Tim and Spot were scared. Tim said, "I'm sorry, Mr. Bear. We will be quiet now." The bear went back to sleep, and Tim and Spot left the mud puddle. They went home to clean up before the rain happened. They were happy they made a new friend, even if he was a big, sleepy bear.

Jill was curious. Every day she watched her mother pick up things that were down the stairs. When she asked her mother what she was doing, her mother said "you will find out soon". Jill wanted to know what the secret was, but her mother wouldn't tell her. It wasn't long until she got the chance to find out. One day, her mother asked if she wanted to come down the stairs with her to pick something up. Eagerly, she said yes. When she got to the bottom of the stairs, she noticed a box. Her mother told her that it was time for her to learn to pick something up on her own. Jill ran fast to the box and carefully picked it up. She looked inside the box, and her face lit up. She couldn't believe it - it was a present for her! Jill realized then what a secret picker-upper her mother had been all along. From now on, she would always run fast down the stairs when her mother asked her to join her.

One day, a little girl named Issy was walking in the park. She saw a coat on the ground so she picked it up and put it on. She felt so high wearing it! She decided to record the moment and wrote it in her journal. The next day, she went back to the park with her journal. When she was writing in her journal, she heard someone calling her name. She looked up and saw the coat's owner. It was the King! He thanked Issy for finding his coat and invited her to the castle. Issy was over the moon! She knew she had to record this amazing moment. When she got to the castle, Issy got a surprise. The King said that she could pick out any coat she wanted in the castle. He said that she could keep the coat she found as a special souvenir. Issy was thrilled! She chose a beautiful high coat and put it on. She couldn't believe it. She recorded the special moment in her journal and thanked the King. Issy enjoyed wearing the high coat and felt so proud. She knew that this was a moment she'd never forget!

Once upon a time, there was a calm teacher named Mr. Fox. He loved his classroom very much. The classroom was big and full of kids. Mr. Fox wanted to improve the classroom for the kids. One day, Mr. Fox asked the kids, "What can we do to make our classroom better?" The kids thought and thought. They all said, "We need more colors!" Mr. Fox smiled and said, "Let's make a plan." The next day, Mr. Fox and the kids started to paint the classroom. They painted the walls with bright colors. They were so happy! But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind blew through the open window and knocked over a paint can! All the kids gasped. The paint was all over the floor! But Mr. Fox stayed calm. He said, "Let's turn this into something fun!" They all dipped their hands in the paint and made handprints on the wall. It was a big surprise, but the classroom looked even better! In the end, Mr. Fox and the kids improved their classroom. They learned that when something unexpected happens, they can still make the best of it. And their classroom was the happiest and most colorful one in the school.
