Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Silly Cat and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a silly cat named Tom. Tom loved to supply his friends with fun toys to play with. He would bring balls and blocks for everyone to enjoy. One day, Tom's friends asked him to tell a story. Tom thought and thought about what story to tell. He decided to make up a silly story about a big red ball that could talk. In the story, the big red ball went on adventures with its friends. They played in the park, jumped in puddles, and even went to the moon! All the friends laughed and clapped as Tom told the silly story. After the story, Tom and his friends played with the big red ball. They pretended it could talk just like in the story. They had so much fun and couldn't wait for Tom to supply more toys and stories next time.

One day, a little boy named Tom went for a walk. He wanted to observe things around him. He saw a big tree with a loop on it. Tom thought it was very interesting. Tom saw a tough-looking dog near the tree. The dog had a big loop around its neck. The dog said, "Hi, I am Sam. Do you want to play with me and my loop?" Tom said, "Yes, I want to play with you and your loop!" They played together and had lots of fun. Tom learned that even tough-looking dogs could be nice friends.

One day, a black cat named Tom went out to play. He liked to play with his toy laser. Tom's friend, a little boy named Tim, also loved to play with the laser. They played together every day. One sunny day, Tom and Tim went to the park to play with the laser. They laughed and had fun, running after the red dot. The black cat jumped high and tried to catch it. Tim giggled as he watched Tom play. But then, the laser stopped working. Tom and Tim felt sad. They tried to fix it, but they couldn't. The black cat and the little boy had to go home without playing more. They were not happy, and they missed their fun laser game.

Once upon a time, there was a brave girl who had a special secret. Every morning she would go to the beach and pick up a handful of sand. She wasn't sure why, but the sand felt so nice and sharp in her hands. Every year, the girl would save up her pocket money and use it to buy things from the market. She was always looking for cheap items that she could fix up and make even better. One day, the girl's secret was revealed - she had been using the sharp sand to create something special on her own. She had used it to carve beautiful art pieces and sell them at the market. Unfortunately, the girl had caught the attention of some greedy people. They wanted to get their hands on the girl's special sand. They devised a plan to take it from her and sell it at a much higher price. The girl knew that she had to protect her special sand. She devised a plan and came up with a clever solution. In the end, she managed to stay one step ahead and keep her secret safe.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his dog, Spot. They walked to the park and saw a wet stand with a kind lady selling ice cream. Tim wanted to gain a new friend, so he said "hi" to the lady. "Hi, Tim!" said the lady. "Would you like some ice cream?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, please!" The lady gave Tim a big ice cream cone. Spot wagged his tail and barked happily. Tim and Spot sat under a tree and enjoyed their ice cream. They watched as the sun dried the wet stand. Tim was happy that he gained a new friend at the park. They walked home together, excited to come back and visit the kind lady again.

Once upon a time, there was a princess. She was very smart. She lived in a big castle with her mom and dad. They were the king and queen. The princess had many friends in the kingdom. One day, the princess wanted to play with her friends. She thought of a fun game. The game was to unite all her friends and make a big circle. They held hands and danced around. Everyone was happy and having fun. The princess was so intelligent, she made more games for her friends. They played all day long. The king and queen were proud of their smart princess. The princess and her friends were happy, and they all stayed friends forever.
