Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Horse

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived on a ranch with his mom, dad, and his dog, Spot. They had many animals on the ranch like cows, horses, and chickens. Tim loved to play with Spot and help his mom and dad with the animals. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, we have a surprise for you!" Tim was very excited and couldn't wait to see what the surprise was. He looked around the ranch, but he could not find the surprise. Tim felt frustrated, but he kept looking. Finally, Tim's dad called him to the barn. When Tim walked in, he saw a big, beautiful horse with a bright red bow. "Surprise!" said his mom and dad. Tim was so happy and couldn't wait to ride the horse. He thanked his mom and dad and gave them a big hug. From that day on, Tim and the horse became the best of friends, and they had many fun adventures on the ranch together.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red ball. It was amazing! All the kids in the park loved to play with it. One day, a little boy named Timmy went to the park with his mom. He saw the big, red ball and wanted to kick it. So, he ran up to the ball and gave it a big kick. The ball flew high into the sky. As the ball came back down, a nice cop saw it. He was walking in the park. The cop caught the ball and gave it back to Timmy. Timmy was so happy. He said "thank you" to the cop and played with the ball all day.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a broken firework in the park. He did not know what to do with it. Tim saw his friend, Sue, and said, "Look what I found! What do you think we should do with it?" Sue looked at the broken firework and thought for a moment. She then said, "I suggest we show it to my big brother, Tom. He knows a lot about fireworks." So, Tim and Sue went to Tom's house. They showed him the broken firework. Tom looked at it and smiled. "I have an idea," he said. "Let's fix it and surprise our friends at the park!" Tim and Sue were excited about the plan. Tom fixed the broken firework and they all went to the park. They gathered their friends and told them to watch the sky. Tom lit the firework and everyone looked up. But instead of a big, loud firework, it made a soft, pretty rainbow in the sky. All the friends were surprised and happy. They clapped and cheered, and they all agreed it was the best firework they had ever seen.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had a very ugly fur. The texture of his fur was not like other dogs. It was rough and not soft. People did not like to pet Spot because of his ugly fur. But Spot was a very nice dog. One day, Spot saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was lost and scared. Spot wanted to help her. He went to her and wagged his tail. Lily saw that Spot was a nice dog, even with his ugly fur. She decided to stay with Spot and not be scared. Spot and Lily walked together until they found Lily's home. Her family was so happy to see her. They thanked Spot for helping Lily. From that day on, they learned that it's not how you look on the outside, but how kind you are on the inside that matters. The moral of the story is to not judge others by their looks, but by their actions and kindness.

Once upon a time, there was a sad crocodile. He lived by a big river. His name was Tim. Tim had no friends. He was very lonely. One day, a small bird came to the river. The bird saw Tim and asked, "Why are you so sad, crocodile?" Tim said, "I have no friends. No one wants to play with me." The bird wanted to help Tim. So, she went to find friends for him. But then, something unexpected happened. The bird found a big group of animals that were all trembling. They were scared of Tim because they thought crocodiles were mean. The bird told the animals, "Tim is not mean. He is just sad and wants friends." The animals decided to give Tim a chance. They all went to the river to play with him. Tim was so happy that he was not sad anymore. And that is how the sad crocodile found friends and became happy.
