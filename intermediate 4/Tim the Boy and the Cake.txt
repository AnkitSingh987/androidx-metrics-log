Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Cake

One day, a little boy named Tim went to a big party with his mom and dad. There were many kids playing and a lot of yummy food to taste. Tim saw a big cake on a table and wanted to taste it. He took a small piece of cake with his fingers and wiped them on a napkin after eating. Near the table, there was a kind old man named Mr. Smith. Mr. Smith was deaf, so he could not hear very well. He saw Tim and smiled. Tim said, "Hi, Mr. Smith! The cake is very good. You should taste it too!" Mr. Smith did not hear Tim, but he saw the cake and took a piece. Suddenly, the lights went out and everyone was quiet. Then, the lights came back on and there was a big surprise! The cake was not just a cake, but a big pinata full of toys and candy. Everyone laughed and clapped as the kids took turns hitting the pinata. Tim and Mr. Smith looked at each other and laughed. They were happy they tasted the cake before the big surprise. Tim gave Mr. Smith a napkin to clean his hands, and they both joined the fun with the other kids.

One day, a little girl named Amy went for a walk. She saw a big tree. The tree was very useful. It gave shade to the animals and birds lived in it. Amy loved the tree. A small bird flew down and said, "Help! My friend is stuck in the tree." Amy looked up and saw another bird caught in the branches. She wanted to help the bird. She thought about how she could get the bird out. Amy had an idea. She found a long stick. She used the stick to free the bird. The bird was not a bird at all! It was a butterfly. The butterfly said, "Thank you for your help, Amy!" The butterfly and the bird became friends with Amy. They played under the useful tree every day. And the tree was happy to help them have fun.

One day, a kind bird went to a temple. The temple was big and pretty. The bird liked to play near the temple. One day, while the bird was playing, it saw a big bug. The bug could zoom very fast. The bird and the bug started to play together. They zoomed around the temple and had fun. Then, something unexpected happened. The bug zoomed into the temple and turned into a beautiful fairy. The fairy said, "Thank you for being kind and playing with me. I was a bug, but now I am a fairy again." The bird and the fairy became best friends and played near the temple every day.

One day, a boy named Tom went to play outside. He saw a distant hill and wanted to go there. He asked his mom, "Can I go to the hill?" His mom said, "Yes, but be back soon." Tom walked to the hill with his toy car. He found a spot to play and decided to bury the car in the dirt. He dug a hole, put the car in, and covered it with dirt. Tom was happy with his buried car. After playing, Tom went back home. His mom made him toast for a snack. Tom told his mom about burying the car. His mom smiled and said, "That sounds fun!" Tom enjoyed his toast and thought about his fun day on the distant hill.

Once upon a time there was a king who was very unhappy. He had spent all of his money and he was very poor. One day, the king called all of his servants to his castle. He said, "I have a plan to make us all wealthier. The plan is to release a cheap item into the market." The servants were confused but they did not dare say anything. Finally one brave servant spoke up. "Your Majesty, what item do you wish to release?" The king thought for a moment and then said, "I will release my golden crown. Everyone will want to buy it and I can make lots of money!" The servants all cheered and the king was very pleased. The servants released the cheap golden crown and it was a hit! Soon the king had plenty of money and he was very happy. The end.
