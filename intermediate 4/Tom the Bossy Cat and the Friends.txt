Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Bossy Cat and the Friends

Once upon a time, there was a bossy cat named Tom. Tom liked to tell all his friends what to do. One day, Tom found a big, shiny thing made of steel. He thought it was very special. Tom told his friends, "I will not yield this steel thing to anyone. It is mine!" His friends did not like this. They thought Tom was being too bossy. In the end, Tom's friends stopped playing with him because he was not nice. Tom learned that being bossy was not good. He gave the steel thing to his friends and they all played happily together. Tom learned to share and be kind to his friends.

Once upon a time there was a smart bear named Bob. Bob was a bear who never forgot anything. Whenever he wanted to remember something he would always remind himself. One day while walking in the forest, he saw a mama duck and two ducklings. The little ducklings were scared and couldn’t find their way home. Bob said, “Do not worry”, and offered to help. He took the ducklings under his arm and they continued walking until they reached the duck pond. The mama duck was happy. Then, Bob asked the mama duck, “How can I remind myself of the importance of helping others?” The mama duck answered, “Every morning, when you have your shower, think of us and remind yourself that it's nice to help others.” Bob thanked her and the mama duck left with her ducklings. Bob learned that when he was smart and helped others, it made him even smarter.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy hen named Henny. She lived on a farm with many other animals. Henny had a special job, she laid eggs every day. One day, Henny laid a very big egg. All the other animals were amazed. But Henny was still grumpy. She did not value the egg and thought it was not important. A wise old owl saw Henny and told her, "Henny, you must value your egg. It is a gift and can bring happiness to others." Henny listened to the owl and started to care for her egg. She saw how her egg made people happy and she became less grumpy. Henny learned that valuing what we have can make us and others happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big house. In the house, there was a family with a mommy, a daddy, and a little girl. The little girl had a room upstairs. She was very proud of her room because it was pretty and clean. One day, the little girl wanted to have a tea party with her toys. She asked her mommy to provide some snacks for her tea party. Her mommy said yes and went to the kitchen to get some cookies and juice. The little girl took the snacks upstairs to her room. She shared the cookies and juice with her toys at the tea party. Everyone had a lot of fun. The little girl felt happy and proud of her tea party. The end.

Tom and Anna are twins. They like to play together. They have many toys. They make a lot of noise. They make a lot of mess. One day, Mom and Dad say they have to go to a hotel. They say it is for work. They say Tom and Anna can come too. They say they have to pack their bags. Tom and Anna are excited. They want to see the hotel. They want to see the pool. They want to see the big bed. They do not want to pack their bags. They say they want to take all their toys. Mom and Dad say no. They say they can only take a few toys. They say they have to share a suitcase. They say they have to hurry. Tom and Anna are sad. They do not want to share. They do not want to hurry. They start to fight. They pull and push each other. They throw their toys. They make a bigger mess. Mom and Dad are angry. They say Tom and Anna have to stop. They say they have to unite. They say they have to help each other. They say they have to be nice. Tom and Anna look at each other. They look at the mess. They look at Mom and Dad. They feel sorry. They say they are sorry. They hug each other. They pick up their toys. They pack their bags. They unite. Mom and Dad are happy. They say Tom and Anna are good. They say they are proud. They say they are ready. They take the bags. They go to the car. They go to the hotel. They have fun.
