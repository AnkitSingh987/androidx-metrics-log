Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and Ben and the Toys

Sara and Ben were twins who liked to play with their toys. But they did not like to clean their room. They left their toys everywhere and made a big mess. One day, their mom said, "Sara and Ben, you have to clean your room. It is too messy. You cannot play until you do it." Sara and Ben did not listen. They said, "No, mom. We want to play. Cleaning is boring. We will do it later." Mom was not happy. She said, "If you do not clean your room, you will not get a surprise. I have a surprise for you, but you have to earn it." Sara and Ben were curious. They asked, "What is the surprise, mom?" Mom said, "It is a secret. But it is very special. It is an angel." Sara and Ben loved angels. They had seen pictures of angels in books and on TV. They wanted to see a real angel. They said, "OK, mom. We will clean our room. We want to see the angel." Sara and Ben worked hard to clean their room. They put their toys in boxes and baskets. They threw away the trash. They made their beds. They dusted and swept. They made their room look nice. Mom came to see their room. She smiled and said, "Good job, Sara and Ben. You have cleaned your room very well. I am proud of you. Now you can see the surprise." Mom took them to the living room. There was a big box with a bow on it. Mom said, "This is your surprise. It is an angel. But you have to be gentle and kind with it. It is a gift from God." Sara and Ben opened the box. Inside was a fluffy white kitten with blue eyes and a pink collar. It looked like an angel. It meowed and licked their faces. Sara and Ben were so happy. They hugged and kissed the kitten. They said, "Thank you, mom. Thank you, God. We love our angel. It is the best surprise ever." Mom said, "You are welcome, Sara and Ben. You have learned a lesson today. Cleaning is not boring. It is a way to show respect and gratitude. And when you do good things, you get good things. That is how God rewards you. He sends you angels to love and protect you." Sara and Ben nodded and said, "We understand, mom. We will always clean our room and do good things. We want to make God and you happy. And we want to take care of our angel. It is our new friend." Mom hugged them and said, "I love you, Sara and Ben. You are my angels too."

One day, Little Luke was playing in the meadow with his toys. It was a beautiful day and he was enjoying the sun on his face. Suddenly, he heard a loud rushing noise coming from the distance. He looked up and saw a mighty wild horse coming his way. He was frightened and ran back to his mom. “Mom, there is a wild horse!" His mom took his hand and said, “Don’t worry, Luke. The horse is not here to hurt us. He is just rushing about.” Little Luke was brave and decided he wanted to see the mighty wild horse. He held on to his mom’s hand and they went back to the meadow. The horse was so close and Little Luke reached out to stroke his nose. The horse was soft and gentle, not wild after all. In the end, Little Luke learned an important lesson - not to be scared of strange things out in the wild.

Once upon a time, there was a little caterpillar named Bob. Bob was very flexible. He could move and bend in many ways. He lived in a beautiful garden with his friends. One sunny day, Bob met a happy butterfly named Lily. Lily was flying around, laughing and having fun. Bob looked at her and said, "Why do you laugh so much?" Lily replied, "I laugh because I am happy, and it makes me feel good." Bob thought about what Lily said and decided he wanted to laugh too. He practiced bending and twisting his flexible body in funny ways. He made silly faces and wiggled around. Soon, all his friends were laughing with him. The next day, Bob and his friends played in the garden. They laughed and had fun together. They knew that laughing made them feel happy and good inside. And as they laughed, Bob felt a change coming. He started to feel different. Bob didn't know it yet, but soon he would become a beautiful butterfly, just like Lily. And they would laugh and fly together in the garden, making everyone around them happy too.
