Category: Intermediate
Title: The Cake and the Toys

Once, there was a big cake. It was a very yummy cake. A little boy named Tim wanted to eat the cake. But, his mom said, "No, not now. You can eat the cake later, after dinner." Tim tried to act like a good boy. He played with his toys and waited. But, he kept thinking about the cake. He was very persistent. He asked his mom again and again, "Can I eat the cake now?" His mom said, "No, Tim. You have to wait." Tim was sad, but he tried to be patient. He played some more and soon, it was time for dinner. Tim ate all his food, and then his mom said, "Now you can eat the cake!" Tim was so happy. He took a big bite of the cake. It was the best cake ever. Tim learned that waiting can be hard, but sometimes, it is worth it.

Once upon a time, there was a town. In the town, there was a big top. The big top could spin. One day, a small boy named Tim went to see the big top. Tim saw the big top spin and spin. He was frightened. He said, "Mom, I am scared!" Mom said, "Do not be scared, Tim. It is fun to watch the big top spin." As they watched, something unexpected happened. The big top stopped spinning! The people in the town were sad. Tim was not frightened anymore. He said, "Mom, I can help the big top spin again!" Tim pushed the big top, and it started to spin again. The people in the town were happy. The moral of the story is: Do not be scared to try new things. Sometimes, you can help others by being brave.

Once upon a time, in a tidy little house, there lived a girl named Lily. She loved to hang cards on the wall. One day, she found a very pretty card with a big, red heart on it. She wanted to hang it in her room. Lily called her friend, Tim. "Tim, come see my pretty card!" she said. Tim came over and looked at the card. "Wow, that's a nice card!" he said. They both smiled and were happy. Lily and Tim hung the card on the wall. They stood back and looked at it. "Our room looks so tidy now," said Lily. Tim nodded and said, "Yes, it does!" They laughed and played together, proud of their tidy room with the pretty card on the wall.

One day, a clumsy little boy named Tim wanted to build a tower with his blocks. He tried very hard to make it tall and strong. But every time he put a block on top, the tower would fall down. Tim felt sad. Then, Tim had an idea. He asked his friend, the wind, to help him build the tower. The wind blew and blew, and together they made a big tornado. The tornado picked up all the blocks and put them on top of each other. The tower was very tall. But the tornado was too strong, and it knocked the tower over again. Tim laughed and said, "Thank you, tornado, but I will try again on my own." And with a big smile, Tim started to build his tower once more.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had many toys, but he did not like to share. He was a selfish boy. One day, Tim went to the park to play with his toys. He had a big bag that included all his favorite things. At the park, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue wanted to play with Tim and his toys. But Tim did not want to share. He said, "No, these are my things!" Sue was sad and walked away. A wise, old bird saw everything. The bird flew down and talked to Tim. The bird said, "Sharing is good. Friends are better than things." Tim thought about it and decided to share. He called Sue back and they played together. Tim learned that sharing was fun and he was not selfish anymore.

The sun was shining brightly in the sky and it was the perfect day for a picnic. Maggie and her mom had packed a delicious lunch, and grabbed a wagon full of fun toys to bring to the park. As they approached the park, Maggie could see all the other children running around and playing. "Let's go!" said Maggie, pulling her mom's arm towards the park. Her mom laughed and followed Maggie towards the jungle gym. As she climbed and explored, Maggie noticed the most amazing thing - the ground was spongy and bouncy, like a trampoline! She bounced and leaped for what felt like hours. Suddenly she realized that she hadn't stopped, she was still bouncing and sopping wet! "Oh no!" said Maggie's mom. "You've gone and stepped in a big puddle of shampoo. Don't worry, we have some towels in the wagon. We can dry you off, no problem." Maggie smiled and grabbed a towel from the wagon and began to dry herself off. Just then, her mom reached for the bottle of shampoo and squirted it on the ground. To Maggie's amazement, the shampoo formed into a big wonderful bubble that floated above the ground! Maggie screamed in delight and jumped into the bubble. As she enjoyed her perfect ride through the air, she yelled to her mom, "That shampoo really yields!"
