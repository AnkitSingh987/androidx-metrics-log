Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Bird and the Gift

One day, a little bird named Tim wanted to deliver a gift to his friend, Sam. Sam lived far away, near a big volcano. Tim was scared, but he wanted to be a good friend. As Tim flew, he saw the distant volcano. He got closer and closer. He saw Sam's house and called out, "Sam, I have a gift for you!" Sam came out and said, "Thank you, Tim! But be careful, the volcano is not safe." Tim didn't listen. He wanted to see the volcano up close. But the volcano was angry. It made a big boom and hot stuff came out. Tim was too close and got hurt. Sam was sad, and they both learned to be careful around volcanoes.

One day, a big cat and a little dog were friends. They liked to play and have fun. The big cat said, "I want to bake a cake today." The little dog was happy and said, "Yes, let's bake a cake!" They went to the kitchen and found a large bowl. They put flour, eggs, and sugar in the bowl. They mixed it all together. The big cat said, "We need to be safe when we use the oven." The little dog nodded and said, "Yes, safe is good." The big cat and little dog put the cake in the oven. They waited and waited. Then, they heard a ding! The cake was ready. They were very careful when they took the cake out of the oven. They were safe and the cake was large and yummy. They shared the cake with their friends and everyone was happy.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. It was an icy day, so she put on her warm coat. She saw her friend, Tom, and they walked together. They both liked to understand things and learn about the world. As they walked, they saw a big hill with snow. They wanted to play on the hill. They ran up the hill and started to slide down. It was so much fun! They laughed and played on the icy hill. But then, something unexpected happened. When they slid down the hill, they found a secret door under the snow! They opened the door and saw a warm room with many coats. The room was full of people who also liked to understand things. Lily and Tom made new friends and learned many new things together. They were so happy they found the secret door on the icy hill.

Once upon a time, there was a big truck named Tom. Tom was very strong and could support many things. One day, Tom was driving down the road when he saw a wreck. A small car named Tim was in the wreck and was very sad. Tom wanted to help Tim, so he used his strong back to support the little car. Tim was happy that Tom was helping him. But there was an angry bird named Bob who did not like that Tom was helping Tim. Bob tried to stop Tom from helping Tim, but Tom and Tim worked together to make the angry bird go away. They showed Bob that they could be nice and support each other. In the end, Bob became friends with Tom and Tim, and they all helped each other when they needed it. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a girl called Sarah who was three years old. She was having a lovely day in the sunny park. Sarah was looking around the park for something special to find today. Suddenly, she saw a tree with a big fluffy nest in it. She climbed up the tree and saw four little eggs in the nest. Sarah smiled and said, "Hello! Who lives here?" Then there was a voice from the nest. "We do!" Sarah looked closely and saw two little birds had come out of the eggs. They were very comfy in their nest. Sarah was so excited and asked, "Can I play with you?" Little birds said, "Yes please, we would like that." So Sarah and little birds played tag and hide-and-seek all afternoon. Sarah had a lot of fun finding new games. When the sun started to go down, Sarah hugged the little birds goodbye and went home with lots of happy memories from the day.
