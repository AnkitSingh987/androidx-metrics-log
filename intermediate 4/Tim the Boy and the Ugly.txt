Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Ugly

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big field. In the field, there was an ugly bug. The bug was very sad. Tim walked up to the bug and asked, "Why are you sad, ugly bug?" The ugly bug looked up and said, "I am sad because no one wants to play with me. They all say I am too ugly." Tim thought for a moment and said, "I will play with you, ugly bug. I don't think you are too ugly. We can be friends." The ugly bug was very happy. So, Tim and the ugly bug played in the field all day. They had so much fun together. The ugly bug was not sad anymore. And Tim learned that it is nice to be kind to everyone, even if they look different.

Once upon a time, there was a shy cat named Tim. Tim loved to play with his rope toy. One day, Tim was playing with his rope when something unexpected happened. The rope got very dirty. Tim wanted to clean the rope, so he took it to a big bowl of water. He put the rope in the water and swished it around. The rope got cleaner and cleaner. Tim was very happy. But then, the rope started to move! It wasn't a rope at all. It was a long, skinny snake! The snake was not shy like Tim. It said, "Thank you for cleaning me!" Tim was scared at first, but the snake was nice. They became friends and played together every day.

Once there was a good clown. He liked to do fun things to make kids laugh. Every day he put on his big red shoes and bright costume. Then he drove to the park with his box of toys. When he got to the park, he spread out his big blanket and opened his toy box. There were lots of toys inside. He had a big yellow ball, a stuffed animal for the kids to hug, and colorful bubbles to blow. The clown smiled and made funny faces. Soon, lots of children gathered around him and laughed. He ran from one kid to the next, doing silly things to make them smile. The children had a great time playing with the clown. They played tag, jumped on the blanket, and chased bubbles. Everyone laughed and was happy. When it was time to go home, the children thanked the clown for the good time. The clown smiled and waved goodbye. The kids left feeling happy.

One day, a jolly dog named Max went to the stadium with his friend, a little boy named Tim. They wanted to watch a big game. The stadium was full of people, and Max and Tim were very excited. During the game, Max started to get tired. He said to Tim, "I need to relax." But Tim was too busy watching the game to listen. Max tried to relax, but the noise from the people made it hard. Max felt sad. Finally, Tim saw that Max was sad. He said, "Let's find a quiet place for you to relax, Max." They walked around the stadium until they found a nice, quiet spot. Max laid down and relaxed, while Tim sat with him. They both felt happy again.

One day, Ben and his grumpy cat, Tom, went to the park. They saw a big tree with lots of grapes. Ben wanted to pick some grapes to eat, but Tom did not like grapes. Ben said, "Tom, let's sort the grapes by color. I will pick the purple ones, and you can pick the green ones." Tom did not want to sort grapes, but he helped Ben anyway. They picked a lot of grapes and put them in two big bags. Ben was very happy, but Tom was still grumpy. Suddenly, a little bird came and took one of the green grapes from Tom's bag. Tom was surprised and started to laugh. The bird came back and took more grapes, and Tom laughed more and more. Ben said, "See, Tom? Helping others can make you happy, even if you don't like what you are doing." And from that day on, Tom was not grumpy anymore. He learned that helping others can make you happy, and he always helped Ben, even when he did not like what they were doing.
