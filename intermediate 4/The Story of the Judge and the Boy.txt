Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Judge and the Boy

Once upon a time there was a little boy who was very smart. He liked to count things and count all day long. One day while he was counting, he saw a judge. The judge was very tall and he was wearing a big robe. The boy said hello to the judge. The judge said, "Hello, young one. What are you doing?" The boy said, "I'm counting things, judge." The judge asked, "Can you count to ten?" The boy proudly said, "Yes, I can count to ten!" The judge smiled at the boy and said, "That's very smart of you. Keep up the good work!" The boy thanked the judge and carried on counting things. He felt very proud that the judge thought he was so smart.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play sport. He was very good at it. One day, Tim saw a big box. It was very creative. He wanted to play with it. But first, he had to finish his sport game. Tim's friend, Sam, said, "We can play with the box after the game." Tim was happy. He knew that he had to delay playing with the box. He was excited to see what was inside. When the game was over, Tim and Sam went to the box. They opened it. Inside was a big, colorful ball! They did not expect that. They thought it would be something else. Tim and Sam played with the ball all day. They had so much fun. The end.

Lily and Tom are playing with their toys in the living room. They have a lot of fun making noises and building things. But then, they hear a knock on the door. They wonder who it is. Mom opens the door and says hello to a person they do not know. The person is wearing a big coat and a hat. He has a big bag with him. He smiles and says, "Hello, I am your uncle Jack. I came to visit you for a few days." Lily and Tom stare at the person. They do not remember having an uncle Jack. They feel shy and scared. They hide behind Mom and do not say anything. Mom sees that they are nervous and says, "Don't be afraid, Lily and Tom. Uncle Jack is very nice and he loves you. He brought you some gifts from his travels. Why don't you say hello and thank him?" Lily and Tom look at each other. They do not want to be rude, but they do not want to talk to the person either. They think it is difficult to be friendly with someone they do not know. They whisper to each other, "What should we do?" Uncle Jack hears them and says, "I know it is hard to meet new people, but I am not a stranger. I am your mom's brother and your family. I have some stories and pictures to show you. Maybe we can play together later. Would you like that?" Lily and Tom feel a little curious. They wonder what stories and pictures he has. They wonder what gifts he brought. They decide to give him a chance. They come out from behind Mom and say, "Hello, Uncle Jack. Thank you for the gifts. We are Lily and Tom. Nice to meet you."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to play outside with her dog, Max. One day, they went on a walk to discover new things. They saw trees, flowers, and a big, smelly pond. Sue asked Max, "Do you like the smelly pond?" Max barked, "No, it's too smelly!" They both laughed and continued their walk. They had not found the best thing yet. As they walked, they saw a big, rainbow slide. Sue said, "Max, we found it! The best thing yet!" They played on the slide all day, forgetting all about the smelly pond. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to lift things. He would lift toys, chairs, and even his pet cat. One day, Tim found a cauliflower in the kitchen. He thought it was a fun toy to lift. As Tim played with the cauliflower, he noticed that it was very persistent. It would always try to roll away from him. He thought this was funny and it made him laugh. Tim chased the cauliflower around the house, trying to lift it every time it rolled away. Then, something unexpected happened. The cauliflower suddenly stopped rolling and started to grow! It grew and grew until it was as big as Tim's house. Tim was amazed and didn't know what to do. But then, the big cauliflower opened up, and inside was a big toy room full of new toys for Tim to lift and play with. The cauliflower was not just a cauliflower, but a magical toy room! And Tim played happily ever after.
