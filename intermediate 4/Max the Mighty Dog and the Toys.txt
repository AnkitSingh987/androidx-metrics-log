Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Mighty Dog and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a mighty dog named Max. Max was big and strong, and he loved to play with his friends. One day, Max found a bag on the ground. He was very curious about what was inside the bag. Max tried to open the bag with his paws, but it was too hard. He called his friend, a little bird named Lucy. Lucy flew down and used her beak to help Max open the bag. Together, they pulled and pulled, and finally, the bag opened! Inside the bag, they found many toys and treats. Max and Lucy were so happy. They shared the toys and treats with all their friends. Everyone played and had fun. Max and Lucy learned that working together made them even mightier. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once, there was a grumpy bear. He was walking in the woods when he came to a puddle. The puddle was big and deep. He looked at it and thought, "I don't want to get wet!" He pushed a stick in the puddle and it made a big splash. He laughed and he pushed the stick in the puddle again. But this time, something poked back at him. He was surprised and scared. He looked in the puddle and saw a grumpy frog inside. The frog didn't want the bear to play. He croaked loudly and swam away. The bear was sad and grumpy. He had wanted to play in the puddle with his stick. He turned and walked away. But something told him to come back to the puddle soon.

Jim was in a hurry. He was going to soccer practice and he wanted to be on time. He saw that his soccer uniform was not in his bag. His mom could not find it anywhere. Luckily, Jim saw his dad coming back from the store. He had a brand new uniform for Jim. Jim was so excited. He quickly put on the new uniform and he was ready for soccer practice. He ran out of the door and hurried to the field. Jim was happy. He was ready to have lots of fun at soccer practice.

Once upon a time, there were two brothers, Joe and Max, who wanted to build something special. Joe said, “Let’s build a quiz!” Max replied, “What kind of quiz?” “A special quiz” Joe said, “A quiz made out of fragile objects.” Max and Joe then began to gather some bits and pieces they could use to build their quiz. They found toy blocks, a glass jar, and a pile of sticks. They began to carefully build their special quiz. When they finished, there were lots of fragile pieces that made it look like a wonderful puzzle. Max and Joe were so proud of their special quiz. They couldn't wait to try it out! They hugged each other and said, “Let’s do the quiz!”

One day, a little boy named Tim found a long string in his toy box. He wanted to play with it, but he did not know what to do. He asked his mom, "Mom, can I play with this string?" His mom said, "Yes, you can play with the string, but be careful." Tim took the string and started to play. He tied the string to his toy car and pulled it around the room. He had a lot of fun with the string. Then, he saw his cat, Fluffy, watching him. Tim thought Fluffy would like to play too. He held the string in front of Fluffy, and she started to play with it. They played together for a long time. Later, Tim's dad came home with a bag of spicy chips. Tim asked, "Dad, can I have some spicy chips?" His dad said, "Yes, but just a little. They are very spicy." Tim tried the chips and they were spicy, but he liked them. He shared the chips with his dad, and they both had a fun day.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a harmless object in her room. It was a big, soft, and colorful thing. She did not know what to do with it. So, she asked her mom, "Mom, what is this?" Her mom smiled and said, "That is a blanket, Lily. You can fold it and use it to keep warm when you sleep." Lily liked the idea. She tried to fold the blanket, but it was too big for her small hands. She asked her mom for help. Her mom showed her how to fold the blanket into a small square. They did it together, and Lily felt happy. That night, Lily used the folded blanket to keep warm in her bed. She felt proud that she learned something new. The harmless object became her favorite thing, and she took it everywhere she went.
