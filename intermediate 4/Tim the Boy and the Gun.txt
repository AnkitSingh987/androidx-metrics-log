Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Gun

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He was restless and always running around. One day, Tim found a toy gun in his room. He hoped to play with the gun and have fun. Tim took the toy gun outside and played with his friends. They had a lot of fun pretending to be heroes. But then, something unexpected happened. A big dog came running towards them. The dog was not happy to see the toy gun. It barked and growled at Tim and his friends. Tim was scared, but he had an idea. He threw the toy gun away from them. The dog ran after the toy gun and left them alone. Tim and his friends were safe. They learned that sometimes it's better to let go of things to solve a problem. They played different games and had fun together without the toy gun.

Once there was a clumsy person walking by the sea. The person got close to the edge of the water and the waves started to splash. The water was coming up higher and higher. The clumsy person slipped and tumbled in. Everyone shouted for help, but nobody could reach him in time. The currents of the water made the person disappear. Splash! He was gone.

One day, a girl named Lucy wanted to mail a letter to her friend, Tom. She put the letter in a big box and went to the mail place. On her way, she saw a radio on the ground. It was dirty and looked disgusting. She picked it up and tried to clean it. Lucy turned the radio on and heard a voice. The voice said, "I am a magic radio. If you help me, I will give you a gift." Lucy was excited and asked, "What do you want me to do?" The radio said, "I need a new home. Can you find one for me?" Lucy thought about where she could find a new home for the radio. She walked around and saw a boy named Jack. Jack was sad because he lost his radio. Lucy asked, "Is this your radio?" Jack said, "Yes, it is! Thank you for finding it!" Lucy was happy to help Jack and the radio. In the end, the magic radio gave Lucy a gift for being so kind. Lucy and Jack became good friends and played together every day. And they never forgot the day they met because of the disgusting radio.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big tap. The water from the tap was harsh, but Tim liked it. He played with the water, and his hands got wet. A mean man came to the park. He saw the big tap too. The man wanted to steal the tap. He thought it would be fun. Tim saw the man and got scared. Tim said, "No, don't steal the tap!" The man looked at Tim and felt bad. He said, "I am sorry, little boy. I will not steal the tap." Tim was happy, and they both played with the water from the tap.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to pretend he was a big man with a mustache. He would draw a mustache on his face using his mom's makeup. Tim was very patient when he drew his mustache. He wanted it to look just right. One day, Tim went outside to play with his friends. He said, "Hi, friends! Look at my mustache!" His friends laughed and said, "Wow, Tim! You look like a big man!" They all started to play a game of catch. While they were playing, a real big man with a mustache came to the park. He saw Tim and said, "Hey, little boy! I like your pretend mustache. It looks just like mine!" Tim was so surprised and happy. The big man with the mustache played catch with Tim and his friends. They all had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, there was a painter. He liked to paint all day. He was a brilliant painter. People loved his work. One day, the painter met a little girl. She did not understand his paintings. The painter felt sad. He wanted to make her happy. Then, the painter made a new painting. It was a big heart. The little girl saw it and smiled. She understood the painting now. The painter learned that simple things can make people happy too. And they all lived happily ever after.
