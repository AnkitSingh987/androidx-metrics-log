Category: Intermediate
Title: The Restless Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a restless bird in a birdcage. The birdcage was set high up in a tree. The bird wanted to fly and play with his friends. He felt very sad in the cage. One day, a big wind came and blew the birdcage down from the tree. The door of the birdcage opened and the bird was free. The bird was so happy and he flew up in the sky. But soon, the bird got very hungry. He did not know how to find food. Then, the bird saw his friends. They helped the bird find food. The bird was happy, but he learned that being free is good, but having friends to help you is even better.

Once upon a time, there was a big stadium. In the stadium, people played many games. One day, a sweet little boy named Tim went to the stadium with his mom. While they were at the stadium, Tim saw a big ice cream stand. He asked his mom if he could have some. His mom said he could have one scoop. But Tim wanted more. He wanted to spoil himself with lots of ice cream. Tim did not listen to his mom. He took many scoops of ice cream. Tim felt happy at first, but then he started to feel sick. His mom told him that too much of a sweet thing can be bad. In the end, Tim learned that it is important to listen to his mom. He also learned that spoiling himself with too many sweet things can make him feel sick. From that day on, Tim only had one scoop of ice cream when he went to the stadium.

Once upon a time, there was a big red truck. The truck was not happy. It had a terrible noise coming from its wheels. The truck wanted to find help to fix the noise. One sunny day, a kind boy named Tom saw the truck. Tom wanted to help the truck. He took the truck's hand and lead it to his dad. Tom's dad was good at fixing things. Tom's dad looked at the truck and found the problem. He fixed the terrible noise. The truck was so happy! Now, the truck could drive without any noise. Tom and the truck became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom had a best friend, a doll named Sally. They liked to play together all day long. One day, they went to the park to play. "Look, Sally!" said Tom, "I can climb this tree!" Tom climbed the tree, but then he got stuck. "Oh no, I can't get down!" Tom cried. Sally wanted to help Tom, so she tried to remind him how to climb down. "Tom, remember to use your paws and go slow," said Sally. But Tom was still scared. Suddenly, a friendly bird flew by and heard Tom's cries. The bird had an idea to help Tom get down. The bird picked up Sally with its beak and flew her up to the tree. The bird put Sally on a branch near Tom. Seeing Sally with him in the tree made Tom feel brave. Together, they climbed down the tree safely. Once they were back on the ground, Tom and Sally thanked the bird for its help. They learned that friends can help each other in unexpected ways. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a little bunny who was very reliable. Everywhere he went, he was considered a faithful friend. One day, the bunny was kicking a soccer ball around with his friends. As he was playing, he noticed a little duck eating lunch alone. The bunny hopped over to the duck and asked, "Do you want to play with us?" The little duck nervously shook its head and said, "No thank you." The bunny kept smiling, and said, "You can trust me, I won't let anyone hurt you." The duck then nodded his head and joined them in the game. The bunny was showing the duck that although he was small, he was dependable and could be trusted. The moral of this story is that being reliable and trustworthy are important qualities to uphold. It doesn't matter how small you are, you can still be a dependable friend.
