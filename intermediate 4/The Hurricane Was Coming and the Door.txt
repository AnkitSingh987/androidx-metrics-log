Category: Intermediate
Title: The Hurricane Was Coming and the Door

One day, a big hurricane was coming. It was very strong and fast. The wind blew hard, and the rain fell heavy. People were scared and wanted to go to a safe place. A little boy named Tim was playing outside. He was careless and did not know about the hurricane. He saw the dark clouds and felt the strong wind. But he kept playing, thinking it was just a fun game. Tim's mom saw him and shouted, "Tim, come inside now!" Tim heard his mom and ran to her. They went inside their house and closed the door. The hurricane went by, and they were safe. Tim learned not to be careless and to listen to his mom when she says to go inside.

One day, a little girl named Lily and her daddy went to the park. They played catch with a ball. Lily felt warm and happy. She loved playing with her daddy. While playing, Lily threw the ball too far. It went into the woods. Lily felt bad and said, "I regret that." Daddy said, "It's okay, we will find it." They went into the woods to look for the ball. As they looked, they found a big, warm, and furry friend. It was a bear! But the bear was nice and gave them the ball. Lily and daddy were surprised. They thanked the bear and went home. They never expected to meet a nice bear in the woods.

Once upon a time, there was a big race for little kids. All the kids wanted to win the race and get the trophy. The trophy was very shiny and pretty. Everyone wanted to take it home. One little boy, named Tim, was very dependable. He always helped his friends and family. Tim wanted to win the race too. He practiced running every day. His friends cheered for him and he felt happy. On the day of the race, Tim ran very fast. He heard his friends and family shout, "Go, Tim, go!" He ran as fast as he could. At the end, Tim won the race. He was so happy. He went to get the trophy. He held it up high and said, "Thank you!" His friends and family were so proud of dependable Tim.

One day, a serious man went to the park. He had a big box with him. The man sat down and looked at the box. A little boy came up to him and asked, "What's in the box?" The man smiled and said, "I will reveal it soon." The boy was excited and told all his friends. They all waited to see what was inside the big box. The man opened the box, and out came a lot of balloons. The kids were so happy! But then, the wind blew really hard. The balloons went up, up, up into the sky. The kids were sad, but the serious man had a surprise. He revealed another box, and inside it were even more balloons! The kids were happy again, and they all played with the balloons in the park.

Once there was a little girl named Sarah. She had a post in her garden. One day, it started raining and the post became wet. Sarah felt sad, because the rain would make the post rot. Sarah thought of a plan. She collected some stones and gathered them around the post. Then she went out in the rain and began to get the post dry with her hands. She worked hard until the post was all dry. When she had finished drying, Sarah was very happy. She looked around and noticed that her hands were rough. She realized that the post was not the only thing she had to be careful of, she also had to take care of herself. Sarah smiled. She understood the moral of her story: to take care of not just her post but also her own body.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a different tree. This tree was not like other trees. It was a pine tree with many colors. The pine tree had red, blue, and yellow leaves. All the other trees were green. The animals in the forest liked the different pine tree. One day, a little bird came to the pine tree. The bird was sad. "Why are you sad?" asked the pine tree. The bird said, "I am not like other birds. My wings are too small, and I can't fly." The pine tree said, "I will help you. I will mind you and keep you safe." The next day, something unexpected happened. The little bird tried to fly again. This time, the bird could fly! The bird was so happy. The pine tree was happy too. The bird said, "Thank you, pine tree, for minding me and helping me believe in myself." The pine tree and the bird became best friends. They knew that being different was not bad. It made them special.
