Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a big dog and a little cat. They lived in a house with a girl named Lily. Lily loved the dog and the cat very much. But the dog and the cat did not like each other. They would always separate from each other and not play together. This made Lily frustrated. One day, Lily said to the dog and the cat, "You need to be friends. I love you both." The dog and the cat looked at each other and thought about what Lily said. They did not want to make Lily sad. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the cat up into a tree. The cat was scared and could not get down. The dog saw the cat in the tree and felt bad. The dog said, "I will help you, cat." The dog used its strong legs to push the tree, and the cat fell down into the soft grass. The cat said, "Thank you, dog." From that day on, the dog and the cat became friends. They played together and did not separate anymore. Lily was so happy to see her pets getting along. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big box. In the box, there were many different gems. One day, a boy named Tom found the box. He was very happy. He took the box and set it on the table. Tom called his friend, Sue. "Sue, come see the gems!" he said. Sue came and saw the box. She was very happy too. They looked at the gems and saw that each one was different. Some were red, some were blue, and some were green. Tom and Sue wanted to play with the gems. They set the gems on the floor and made a game. They took turns picking a gem and saying its color. They had so much fun with the different gems. And they played until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a cool toy named Mr. Brush. Mr. Brush was a fun toy that could help Amy keep her room clean. She liked to play with Mr. Brush every day. One day, Amy's room was very messy. Her mom asked her to clean it up. Amy took Mr. Brush and started to clean. She tried very hard to manage the mess. She picked up her toys and put them away. Mr. Brush helped her sweep the floor. After a while, Amy and Mr. Brush made the room look nice and clean. Amy's mom was very happy. She gave Amy a big hug and said, "Good job, Amy! You and Mr. Brush did a great job!" Amy felt proud and gave Mr. Brush a hug too. They had managed to clean the room together.

Once upon a time there was a brave little girl named Jada. She lived in a small, bright house with her mom and dad. One night Jada woke up because she heard a strange noise coming from the closet. She was very scared. She was so scared that she didn't want to go to the closet to find out what it was. But Jada was brave, so slowly she walked toward the closet. She slowly opened the door, and to her surprise, she saw a tiny mouse. The mouse was just as scared as Jada! "Please don't be scared," Jada said softly with a reassuring smile. The mouse nodded, and Jada let him out of the closet. They started to talk and Jada soon realized that the mouse was lost. He had been trying to find his way out of the closet for hours. Jada and the mouse became friends. Jada was the leader and the mouse kept close beside her. She showed him the way out of the house and the mouse thanked her. Before the rodent left, Jada had one last piece of advice for him. "Remember to never be scared to ask for help, even if you think it is far away," she said to him. The moral of this story is that it is important to rely on others for help, even if it doesn't seem close. Sometimes a little courage is needed to take the first step
