Category: Intermediate
Title: The Lonely Lamp and the Tim

Once upon a time, there was a lonely lamp. It lived in a small room all by itself. The lamp was sad because it had no friends to play with. One day, a little boy named Tim came into the room. He saw the lonely lamp and said, "Hi, lamp! I'm Tim. Do you want to be my friend?" The lamp was very happy and said, "Yes, I would love to be your friend, Tim!" From that day on, Tim and the lamp became best friends. They played together every day. Tim would turn the lamp on and off, and the lamp would light up the room for Tim. They were both very happy to have each other, and the lonely lamp was lonely no more.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom was a competitive boy. He liked to win games. One day, Tom found a shield. It was a big, round shield. He was very happy to have it. Tom showed his shield to his friends. They all wanted to have a shield too. So, they decided to make their own shields with paper and colors. Now, each friend had a shield too. They were ready to play a game. Tom and his friends played a game with their shields. They pretended to be knights. They had so much fun. Tom was happy to share his shield idea with his friends. They learned that playing together was more fun than just winning.

Mummy was walking in the park with her little daughter. It was a beautiful day, but a little cold. The little girl was shivering. Mummy saw a big bull in the field. She warned her daughter not to go near it, as it might be naughty. The girl nodded and replied, “Yes, mummy. I know what you mean. Bulls can be naughty.” Mummy smiled and said, “That's right. We should keep our distance from naughty bulls.” They continued walking and soon reached the playground. The little girl was excited and forgot all about the bull. She ran around and played, not worrying about the cold anymore.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the snow. They make a big snowman with a hat and a carrot nose. They give him a jolly smile with stones. "Look, our snowman is so happy!" Anna says. "Yes, he is! But he is cold, too. He needs a scarf," Ben says. They look around for a scarf. They see a red one on a fence. It belongs to Mrs. Lee, who lives next door. "Can we borrow your scarf, Mrs. Lee?" Anna asks. "Of course, dear. You can use it for your snowman. He looks very nice," Mrs. Lee says. "Thank you, Mrs. Lee!" Ben says. They take the scarf and wrap it around the snowman's neck. They point at him and laugh. "Now he is warm and jolly!" Anna says. "Yes, he is! And he is our friend, too," Ben says. They hug the snowman and each other. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She was only three years old. Every day, she would rock in her rocking chair. She would rock and rock and rock, until she felt nice and sleepy. One day, the little girl was not feeling well. So her mommy took her to see the doctor. The doctor shined a light on the little girl, and made her feel better. The doctor gave the little girl some medicine. She didn't like it very much, so the doctor said she could rock in the rocking chair while she ate it. The little girl rocked in her chair. After she finished her medicine, she felt much better! She was so grateful to the nice doctor.

Once there was a bald man. He loved dolphins. Every day he went to the beach and looked out at the ocean. One day, the man saw a dolphin. He was so excited! He watched the dolphin for a long time and the dolphin stayed near the shore. The man went back to the beach every day. Every day the dolphin was still there. The man started talking to the dolphin and they became friends. Every day the dolphin stayed a little bit longer. Soon the dolphin was with the man all day long! The man was so happy. But then something strange started happening. The man's hair began to disappear. He was getting bald! And the longer the dolphin stayed, the more hair the man lost. The man was happy though, because he always had a friend. And every day the friendship between the man and the dolphin increased.
