Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben and Mia and the Park

Ben and Mia are friends. They like to play in the park. They enjoy the swings, the slide and the sandbox. One day, they find a big nail in the sandbox. It is shiny and hard. "Look, Ben, a nail!" Mia says. "What can we do with it?" "Maybe we can make a picture with it," Ben says. "We can use it to draw in the sand." They take the nail and start to make shapes and lines in the sand. They make a sun, a flower, a house and a car. They are happy and proud of their picture. But then, a big boy comes to the sandbox. He is mean and rude. He sees Ben and Mia's picture and he does not like it. "That is a silly picture," he says. "I can make a better one." He takes the nail from Ben's hand. He tries to break their picture with the nail. But the nail is too weak. It bends and snaps in half. "Ow!" the big boy says. He drops the nail and runs away. He is angry and hurt. Ben and Mia look at the broken nail. They are sad and scared. "Are you okay, Ben?" Mia asks. "Yes, I am okay, Mia," Ben says. "But the nail is broken. We can't use it anymore." They hug each other and go to find another toy. They leave the broken nail in the sand. They do not want it anymore. They still enjoy playing in the park.

One day, a little boy named Tom was playing in his messy room. He loved to perform shows with his toys. He had a toy robot made of steel. The robot was his favorite toy. Tom would make the robot dance and sing for his shows. One sunny day, Tom was performing a show with his robot. He made the robot dance around the room. Suddenly, the robot stopped moving. Tom was sad because he did not know how to fix his steel robot. He tried to make the robot dance again, but it did not work. Then, something unexpected happened. Tom's toy dog started to move! The toy dog danced around the room and joined the show. Tom was very happy and surprised. He did not know his toy dog could dance too. Now, Tom and his toy dog performed shows together, and they had lots of fun.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Jill. She was brave and strong. Every day she liked to wander around her village and to explore the land around her. One day, she met a fox in the woods. The fox was happy and smiling. “Where are you going my little one?” asked the fox. “I want to find the path to freedom. Can you help me?” asked Jill. The fox smiled and said “Yes, follow me! I will show you the way to victory”. So, Jill and the fox began their journey, walking through the forest. They walked for hours, until they eventually found a safe path that led to freedom. Jill was so happy, she had found the way to safety and victory. The fox smiled and said “Remember, brave and strong little ones always find a way to succeed”. Jill thanked the fox, and went on her way, feeling safe and victorious.

One day, a pink cat named Kitty wanted to become strong. She thought doing exercise would help her. So, she went to the park to play. At the park, Kitty saw a big dog named Max. Max was jumping and running. Kitty asked Max, "How do you become strong?" Max said, "I do exercise every day. I run and jump a lot." Kitty liked the idea. She started to exercise with Max. They ran, jumped, and played together. Soon, Kitty became strong and happy. And they became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a hot place, there was a thoughtful camel named Hope. Hope loved to walk and find new things. One day, while walking, she saw something far away. She thought it might be an oasis, a place with water and trees. Hope was thirsty and wanted to see if it was true. As Hope walked closer, she saw that it was not an oasis, but a big, shiny rock. She was sad because she was still thirsty. But then, something unexpected happened. The shiny rock started to shake and move. Hope was scared, but she did not run away. The shiny rock opened up, and out came a kind, talking bird. The bird said, "Thank you, Hope, for not running away. I was stuck in that rock, and you set me free!" The bird then showed Hope a secret path to a real oasis. Hope and the bird became best friends, and they had lots of fun together.
