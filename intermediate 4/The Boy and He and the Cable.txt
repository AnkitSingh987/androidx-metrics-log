Category: Intermediate
Title: The Boy and He and the Cable

Once there was a boy and he had a lovely cable. He squeezed it tight and it made a funny noise. He loved the sound. He would hug the cable and play with it. He thought the cable was special and he hugged it every day. One day, he noticed something bad happening. His cable was starting to break in the middle. He was so sad and he couldn't understand why it was happening. He didn't want his special cable to break and he was so worried. But then, he realised the reason why it was breaking. He had been squeezing the cable too hard. He felt so silly and he understood what he had done. He tried to be gentle with the cable and he hugged it even tighter. He realised that by squeezing the cable too hard, he had made it stronger. He smiled, happy that his lovely cable was intact. From then on he squeezed gently and hugged lovingly, never forgetting the lesson he had learnt.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max liked to jog in the park. He loved to feel the sun on his fur and the wind in his face. One day, while Max was jogging, he saw a pile of junk. He thought it looked fun to play with. So, Max ran to the junk and started to dig. As Max dug through the junk, he found a big, red ball. He was so happy! Max took the ball and jogged back home. From that day on, Max and the big, red ball were the best of friends.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the field near their house. The field has many flowers and bugs and birds. Lily and Ben run and jump and laugh in the field. One day, Lily sees a big, brown cow in the field. She thinks the cow is funny. She runs to the cow and says, "Hello, cow! Do you want to play with us?" The cow looks at Lily and says, "Moo!" Ben hears Lily and the cow. He thinks Lily is joking. He runs to Lily and says, "Lily, that is not a cow. That is a horse!" He points to the cow and laughs. Lily looks at Ben and says, "Ben, that is not a horse. That is a cow!" She points to the cow and laughs. The cow looks at Lily and Ben and says, "Moo!" Lily and Ben look at the cow and say, "Moo!" They all laugh together. They think they are funny. They play in the field until the sun goes down.

Bobbie was a tall little boy who loved to gaze at the world around him. He especially liked to play near the old metal rail that ran along the edge of the park. Every day, Bobbie would spend lots of time around the rail, sitting and playing and looking around. One special day, Bobbie noticed something sparkly when he was gazing at the rail. He walked over to take a closer look. It was a tiny, glittery beetle, walking along the rail. Bobbie gasped and smiled in delight. The beetle saw Bobbie and waved one of its antennae. Bobbie waved back, and the beetle kept walking along the rail. Bobbie kept gazing at it until the beetle scurried out of sight. He had had such a wonderful surprise! As Bobbie walked away, he looked at the rail one more time and smiled. He would always remember the tall metal rail, and the beetle he had seen that day.

One day, a little boy named Tim was very impatient. He wanted a cookie from the big jar on the shelf. He asked his mom for help, but she was busy. Tim had an idea. He saw a chair and pushed it to the shelf. He climbed up and got the cookie jar. Tim was so happy, he started to clap. His mom heard the clapping and came to see. She saw Tim with the cookie jar and smiled. She helped him get down and they ate cookies together.

Once upon a time, in a gloomy forest, there lived a big, strong bear named Bobo. Bobo loved to play and have fun. One day, he found a big, tall tree that he wanted to climb. As Bobo climbed the tree, he saw a little bird named Chirpy sitting on a branch. Chirpy looked sad. "Why are you sad?" asked Bobo. Chirpy replied, "A fire destroyed my nest and now it's just ash." Bobo wanted to help Chirpy. He said, "Let's build a new nest for you!" Chirpy smiled and together they found sticks and leaves to make a new nest. Bobo put the nest high up in the tree, safe from any harm. Chirpy was very happy in her new nest. She thanked Bobo and they became best friends. They played and had fun in the gloomy forest every day, and they always looked out for each other. And they lived happily ever after.
