Category: Intermediate
Title: The Building and the Tim

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big building. The building was very pale. Every day, many people went inside the building to do their work. But at night, the building was very quiet. No one was there, and it would remain empty until morning. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the building with his mom. They had to do some work there. Tim looked at the pale building and felt a little scared. But his mom held his hand and said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will be with you the whole time." They went inside the building and did their work. Tim started to feel better. He saw that the building was not so scary after all. When they were done, they went back home. From that day on, Tim was not scared of the pale building anymore. He knew that as long as his mom was with him, everything would be okay.

One day, a little dog named Spot felt anxious. He had a message to give to his friend, Cat. The message was from their friend, Bird. Spot did not know how to tell Cat the message. He was scared. Spot went to Cat and said, "Cat, I have a message from Bird." Cat asked, "What is the message, Spot?" Spot was too anxious to say it. He thought Cat would be mad. So, Spot did not tell the message. Later, they all went to a party. Bird wanted Spot to serve the cake. When Spot served the cake, he felt brave. He said, "Cat, the message is that Bird is moving away." Cat was sad, but not mad. They all hugged and said they would stay friends.

Once upon a time, there was a fat rabbit named Bob. Bob loved to eat carrots. One day, he went to his friend, Tim the turtle, to borrow some carrots. "Can I borrow some carrots, please?" asked Bob. Tim said, "Yes, you can. But you need to bring them back tomorrow." The next day, Bob went to the garden to get more carrots. But when he got there, he found something unexpected. The carrots were gone! Bob was sad and worried. He didn't know what to do. He went back to Tim and told him what happened. Tim said, "Don't worry, we can find the carrots together." They looked everywhere and finally found the carrots. A little mouse had taken them to share with her family. They all became friends and shared the carrots happily.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to clap his hands. Every day, Tim would find something to clap about. He was always enthusiastic and happy. One day, Tim saw a newspaper on the floor. He picked it up and started to dance around with it. He clapped his hands and laughed as he waved the newspaper in the air. His mom and dad saw him and started to clap too. They were all having so much fun. Tim decided to make a game with the newspaper. He would throw the newspaper in the air and clap his hands as many times as he could before it landed on the ground. Tim, his mom, and his dad took turns playing the game. They clapped, laughed, and had a great day together.

Once upon a time, there was a smart cat named Tom. Tom liked to play with his friend, a little bird named Tim. They would play hide and seek all day. One day, Tim hid very well, and Tom could not find him. Tom looked and looked, but Tim was out of sight. Tom started to feel sad and said, "I miss you, Tim! Where are you?" Tim heard Tom and felt bad. He flew out of his hiding spot and said, "I'm here, Tom! I didn't want to make you sad. Let's play a different game." Tom was happy to see Tim again. They decided to play tag instead. They ran and laughed together, and they were the best of friends. They knew they would always have fun, no matter what game they played.

Once upon a time, there were two boys, Joe and Bob. Joe was playing on the playground and he noticed something very round. He shouted to Bob saying. "Look! I can see something round!" Bob hopped off the swing to come look. "Let's go see, Joe!" When they got close to the round thing they couldn't believe it, it was a big red ball! Joe said, "Let's play!" But Bob said, "No, that's a stupid thing to do." Joe was so surprised at what Bob said, he replied, "Why is it stupid?" Bob said, "Because if we play with it, it could roll away and we won't be able to find it." Joe thought about it, and he agreed that it was a good idea to observe the ball and not play with it. So they both sat back and observed the round ball from a distance, until eventually it got dark and they had to go home.
