Category: Intermediate
Title: The Witch and the Park

Once upon a time there was a witch. She was so boring that she wanted to take something special away. So she went to the park and found a little girl. She asked the girl if she wanted to take a ride on her broomstick. The girl was so excited! She quickly hopped onto the witch’s broomstick, and they flew up and up into the sky. The girl had the best time ever! They flew over cities and seas, houses and mountains. Everywhere they looked, there were new things to explore. They eventually had to come down. When the witch landed back on the ground, the girl said goodbye and thanked the witch for the fun ride. The witch said she would take her on a ride again soon. The little girl went home with a huge smile on her face, happy to have made an exciting and special memory with the witch.

Tammy was sitting on the wagon gazing up at the sky. She watched the clouds float by as she sang happily to herself. All of a sudden, a loud thunderclap startled her. She was so frightened she started to cry. Her mom heard her and quickly rushed to her side. She wrapped her arms around her daughter and said “There, there, it’s just a bit of thunder, it won’t hurt you.” Tammy looked up at her mom and said “But it’s scary!” Her mom smiled and said “Let’s go find some rainbows, shall we?” Tammy wiped away her tears and nodded. Her mom pulled the wagon and they both scanned the sky for a rainbow. Suddenly, they saw a beautiful one arching across the sky. Tammy’s face lit up and she said “Oh, wow! It’s so pretty!” The two of them gazed up in amazement, and soon the frightened feeling was replaced by a feeling of awe. Tammy smiled and said “Let’s come back tomorrow and look for more rainbows!” Her mom smiled and gave her a big hug. “Yes, let’s do that.”

Once upon a time, in a small garden, there was a gentle plant. It had green leaves and a big, pretty flower. The plant was friends with a little girl named Lily. They liked to talk and laugh together. One sunny day, Lily went to the garden and said, "Hello, gentle plant! Let's laugh and play together." The plant shook its leaves and laughed with Lily. They had a lot of fun in the sun. But one day, a big storm came. The wind blew very hard, and the rain fell down. The gentle plant tried to be strong, but the wind was too much. The plant fell down and could not get up. Lily came to the garden, but the plant was gone. She was very sad and cried. The gentle plant and Lily could not laugh and play together anymore.

Once upon a time there was a small boy called John. He went for a walk outside with his friends. They came across a big purple box on the ground. It looked so fit that the boys wanted to explore what was inside. John was very curious and he asked his friends to help him open the box. When they opened it, they saw a lot of bright and shiny toys. John was so happy and he couldn't help but take some of them home. But his friends were not happy and they told him not to take the toys, but John didn't mind. So he took some of the toys and put them in his pocket. When John arrived home, his mother was very angry. He had taken something that didn't belong to him. She scolded him for not using his head and for not minding what his friends had said. John was very sorry and he promised to never take something that didn't belong to him again. But it was too late. He had to face the bad ending of not minding.

One day, a boy named Tim found an axe in the woods. He felt very big and strong with it. He walked back to his house, swinging the axe. His mom saw him and asked, "Tim, where did you get that axe?" Tim just shrugged and said, "I found it in the woods." His mom looked worried and told him, "You must be careful with it, Tim. It can be dangerous." The next day, Tim went to play with his friend, Sam. He brought the axe with him. Sam looked at the axe and said, "Wow! That is cool!" They played with the axe for a while, but then Sam got a cut on his hand. Sam cried, and Tim felt ashamed. Tim took the axe back to the woods and left it there. He didn't want to hurt anyone again. The next day, he found a toy axe in the same spot. It was a gift from his mom. Tim smiled and knew that he could play safely with his new toy axe.
