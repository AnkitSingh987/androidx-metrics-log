Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bird Felt Dizzy and the Tree

One day, a little bird felt dizzy. It could not fly very well. The bird saw a big tree and sat on a branch to rest. The bird was sad because it could not play with its friends. A kind girl named Lily saw the dizzy bird. She wanted to help it feel better. Lily said, "Little bird, why are you dizzy?" The bird told her that it bumped its head while flying. Lily knew what to do. She took a soft cloth and wiped the bird's head gently. The bird started to feel better. It thanked Lily and flew away to play with its friends. Lily was happy she could help the bird.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily loved to collect pretty rocks. She had a big box where she kept all her special rocks. Lily was very careful with her box because the rocks were fragile and could break easily. One sunny day, Lily went to the park to find more rocks. She saw a beautiful blue rock near the swings. Lily was so happy and picked it up with a big smile. As she walked, she saw a funny squirrel making a lot of motion. The squirrel was jumping and running all around the park. Lily laughed and decided to follow the squirrel. She hoped it would lead her to more rocks. The squirrel stopped near a tree and Lily found a shiny red rock. She was so excited and put it in her pocket. At the end of the day, Lily went home and put her new rocks in the fragile box. She couldn't wait to tell her friends about her adventure at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a brave girl called Grace. Grace had beautiful, curly purple hair. She lived with her mum and dad who always gave her tasks to do. One day, Grace was given a difficult task. She had to clean all the windows in her house. Grace was sad and scared, but she knew she had to try and accept this task. Grace got to work. She cleaned all the windows, from top to bottom. She even found a way to reach the highest window! Finally, after a long day of work, she was finished. Grace was so happy. She was proud of herself that she had accepted the task and done a good job. Her mum and dad were so proud of her too. Grace smiled and said, "purple is my favourite colour, and I love a good task!"

Once upon a time, in a big world, there were many animals. They lived in a big forest. They were all friends and played together. One day, they decided to have a party. The party would include all the animals in the forest. But there was one bad animal, a big bad wolf. The other animals did not want the big bad wolf at the party. They were scared of him. The big bad wolf was sad because he wanted to play too. The big bad wolf decided to be nice. He helped the other animals set up the party. The other animals saw that the big bad wolf was not so bad. They let him join the party. They all had fun together, and the big bad wolf was not bad anymore.

Tom and Lily were playing in the living room. They liked to look at books and learn new words. Tom found a big book with many words. It was a dictionary. "Look, Lily, this book has all the words in the world!" Tom said. He opened the book and pointed at a word. "This word is delicious. It means something is very good to eat. Like cake or ice cream." Lily smiled and nodded. She liked delicious things. She wanted to see more words. She reached for the book, but Tom pulled it away. "No, this is my book. You can't have it. Find your own book," Tom said. He was selfish and mean. Lily felt sad and angry. She wanted to share the book with Tom. She looked around and saw a ball. She had an idea. She picked up the ball and threw it at Tom. "Hey, stop that!" Tom shouted. He dodged the ball, but he dropped the book. The book fell on the floor and made a loud noise. Mom heard the noise and came to the living room. She saw the book on the floor and the ball near Tom. She was very upset. "What are you doing? This book is very important and expensive. You can't throw things at each other. You are both in big trouble!" Mom said. She took the book and the ball and put them away. She sent Tom and Lily to their rooms. They had to stay there until dinner. Tom and Lily were sorry and bored. They wished they had played nicely and shared the book. They learned that being selfish and mean was not fun. They missed the delicious words.
