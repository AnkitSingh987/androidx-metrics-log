Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was very excited because her mom was taking her to the park. Lily loved the park because it had swings and slides. When they got to the park, Lily's mom put a big blanket on the grass. Lily liked to sit on the blanket and eat her snack. But today, a naughty dog came running and jumped on the blanket. The dog wanted to play with Lily. Lily laughed and played with the naughty dog. They ran around the park, played on the swings, and went down the slide. After a while, the dog's owner came and took the dog home. Lily was tired but happy. She went back to the blanket, ate her snack, and then went home with her mom.

Once there was a little girl called Sandy. She was three years old and she loved going outside to play. One day, Sandy decided to go to the park. When she arrived, she saw her friend, Jimmy. He was holding a big, fluffy toy and running around. Sandy wanted the toy for herself, so she went over to him and demanded it. Jimmy told her: "No, this is my toy. I won't give it to you". Sandy wasn't happy. She started stamping her foot and saying: "Give it to me now!" Jimmy just laughed. "Come on, Sandy", he said. "Let's have some fun and play a game with it". Sandy wasn't impressed. She was sure that Jimmy was being funny, but she was determined to get what she wanted. She stamped her foot again and said: "I want the toy!" Jimmy thought for a moment and then he finally agreed. He handed over the toy and they both went off to play. Sandy had won, and they had a great time together in the park.

One day, a girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. She saw a big pond with fish in it. Lily thought it was the perfect day to feed the fish. She asked her mom if they could feed them, and her mom said yes. Lily was very happy. Lily and her mom went to the store to buy fish food. They found a big bag of fish food and bought it. Lily could not wait to go back to the pond to feed the fish. She held the bag very tight, so she would not drop it. When they got back to the pond, Lily opened the bag and started to feed the fish. The fish were very hungry and came to eat the food. Lily was so happy to see the fish eat. She knew that she had made the fish happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a wise old owl. The owl lived in a big tree. The owl saw a race one day. The race was between a fast rabbit and a slow turtle. The rabbit said, "I will win the race!" The turtle said, "I will try my best." The race began, and the rabbit ran fast. The turtle walked slow. The rabbit was far ahead and thought he would win. But then, something unexpected happened. The rabbit saw a big cake. He stopped to eat the cake. The turtle kept walking. The rabbit ate too much cake and felt sleepy. He fell asleep under a tree. The wise old owl saw the rabbit sleeping. The owl frowned and said, "Oh no, rabbit, you should keep going!" But the rabbit did not hear the owl. The turtle walked past the rabbit and won the race. The rabbit woke up and was sad, but he learned a lesson. Next time, he would not stop until the race was done.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy cat named Max. Max lived in a house with a girl named Lily. Max liked to play with yarn. One day, Max found a big ball of yarn. He was very happy. Max played with the yarn all day. He rolled it and jumped on it. Then, he heard a voice. "Hello, Max!" said the voice. Max looked around but did not recognize who was talking. He was confused. "Who are you?" Max asked. "I am a magic yarn!" said the voice. Max was surprised. The magic yarn told Max that it could make his grumpy feelings go away. Max thought about it and then said, "Okay, I will try." The magic yarn wrapped around Max and he felt happy. Max and Lily played together and they all lived happily ever after.
