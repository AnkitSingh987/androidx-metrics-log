Category: Intermediate
Title: Sarah the Girl and the Theater

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Sarah. She felt so excited because her mommy had taken her to the theater. It was a fair day and the theater was really big and beautiful. Sarah was so happy and felt warm inside. When Sarah and her mom reached the theater, all the lights were so bright that it lit up the room. There were lots of people at the theater and everyone was very happy and excited. The lights and the music made Sarah feel even more special. When the show was over, Sarah was sad that it had ended. But then she found out that she could go to the theater again very soon and she was overjoyed! Sarah was so lucky and felt so happy that she would be able to go back to the theater. She loved the theater and it was such a fair place to be.

Once upon a time, there was a little monster. The monster was very angry. He wanted to play, but all the other animals were scared of him. They did not want to play with the angry monster. One day, the monster met a little girl. The girl was not scared of the monster. She asked the monster if he wanted to play with her. The monster was so happy. They played all day long. As they played, the monster became less angry. He was having so much fun with the little girl. The other animals saw that the monster was not angry anymore. They all came to play with the monster and the little girl. They all played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a smart girl named Mia. She loved to look at the moon every night. The moon was big and bright, and it made her happy. One night, Mia looked up at the sky, but the moon was not there. She was sad and did not know where the moon went. She started to cry. Her mom and dad tried to help her find the moon, but they could not see it too. Then, a big cloud moved away, and there was the moon! It was hiding behind the cloud all the time. Mia was so happy to see the moon again. She knew the moon loved her too. From that day on, Mia and the moon were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. Tom liked to play a game called chess. Chess was a game with black and white pieces that you moved on a board. One day, Tom wanted to organize his chess pieces. He put all the black pieces on one side and all the white pieces on the other side. He was very happy because now he could find his pieces easily. Tom invited his friend, Sam, to play chess with him. They played the game together and had a lot of fun. Tom was good at chess, but Sam was even better. They laughed and played until it was time for bed. The end.

Once upon a time there was a cat. The cat was very dirty. He had been rolling in the mud all day long. The cat walked into a room and saw a blanket on the floor. He liked the blanket and decided to fold it up. Just then, a little girl walked into the room. She saw what the cat was doing and said, "No, kitty! That's my blanket! Don't fold it." The cat hissed at the little girl and kept folding the blanket. The girl was angry that the cat was getting her blanket dirty. "Bad kitty!" Screamed the little girl. The cat quickly unfolded the blanket and ran out of the room. The girl was happy that her blanket was safe but still mad that it was now even dirtier than before. The little girl knew she had to clean the blanket but she still couldn't believe the naughty cat had been so mischievous.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lucy loved to wear her red dress. She wore it every day. One day, she went to the park to play with her friends. They played catch, and they had fun. Lucy was happy. As they played, a big wind came. It blew Lucy's red dress away! Lucy was sad. She looked for her dress, but she couldn't find it. Her friends helped her look too. They looked under the trees and behind the bushes. Then, they saw a big red ball. It was not Lucy's dress, but it looked like it. The wind had blown the red dress into a ball! Lucy laughed, and her friends laughed too. They played with the red ball, and Lucy was happy again. The result was a fun day at the park.
