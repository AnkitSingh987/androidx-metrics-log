Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Lily. Lily had long, soft hair. Every day, she would take her brush and make her hair nice and smooth. One day, Lily went for a walk in the park. She saw a little bird with messy feathers. The bird looked sad. Lily wanted to help the bird. She took her brush and gently brushed the bird's feathers. After Lily brushed the bird's feathers, the bird was very happy. The bird's feathers were now pretty and smooth. Lily and the bird became good friends. They played and laughed together in the park. And from that day on, Lily always took her brush with her, just in case someone needed her help.

Once upon a time, there was a reliable dog named Max. Max was a very good dog who liked to help his family. One day, Max heard an alarm. The alarm was very loud. Max knew he needed to do something. Max ran to his family and yelled, "Alarm! Alarm!" His family looked at him and saw that he was worried. They all ran outside to see what was going on. They saw a big red truck with flashing lights. The truck was loud and scary. But then, something unexpected happened. The truck stopped, and out came a big, friendly man. He was holding a big cake with the words "Happy Birthday" on it! Max's family laughed and clapped. They had forgotten that it was Max's birthday. The big, friendly man was there to surprise Max with a birthday party. Max wagged his tail and barked happily. It was the best birthday ever.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin lived in a perfect stream with his family. The water was clean and full of yummy food for Fin and his friends. Every day, Fin would swim and play with his friends in the stream. One day, Fin saw a big fish who could swim very fast. Fin wanted to swim just as fast as the big fish. He tried and tried, but he could not swim as fast. Fin felt sad and wanted to quit trying. His mommy saw him and asked, "Why are you so sad, Fin?" Fin told his mommy he wanted to swim as fast as the big fish, but he could not do it. His mommy smiled and said, "Fin, you are a little fish, and you will grow up to be a big fish one day. Do not quit trying. Keep swimming and practicing, and you will get better." Fin listened to his mommy and did not quit. He kept swimming and practicing every day. As time went by, Fin grew bigger and stronger. He swam faster and faster. One day, he finally swam as fast as the big fish. Fin was so happy, and his friends and family were proud of him. The moral of the story is: never quit trying, and you will get better at what you want to do.

Sandy went for a walk. She saw something in the grass. It was a cell. It looked very old and was really filthy. She picked it up and tried to clean it. But it was still really dirty. Sandy decided to keep the filthy cell in her pocket. She kept on walking until she got home. When she got back, she showed her mom the old cell. Her mom smiled and said it was very special. She told Sandy that they had enough money to buy a special phone to use the cell. Sandy was so excited and couldn't wait to try the phone. The next day, Sandy and her mom went to the store together. They picked out a special phone and put the filthy cell in it. They took it home and Sandy carefully pressed the buttons to turn it on. It worked! Sandy was so happy to have a phone! She spent all day running around and having fun with her new phone. Even if it was filthy, the old cell was still very special.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys and their dog, Spot. Spot is a big, brown dog who likes to run and jump and bark. One day, Anna and Ben have a plan. They want to surprise their mom with a gift. They find some paper and crayons and draw a picture of their family. They write their names and Spot's name on the picture. They are very proud of their work. They hide the picture under their bed and wait for their mom to come home. They hear her car in the driveway and run to the door. They hug their mom and say, "Hi, Mom! We have a surprise for you!" Their mom smiles and says, "You do? What is it?" Anna and Ben say, "Come with us! We'll show you!" They take their mom's hand and lead her to their bedroom. Spot follows them, wagging his tail. They lift the bedsheet and show their mom the picture. Their mom gasps and says, "Wow! This is beautiful! Thank you, Anna and Ben! You are so sweet and talented!" Anna and Ben beam and say, "You're welcome, Mom! We love you!" Their mom hugs them and says, "I love you too! This is the best surprise ever!" Spot barks and jumps on the bed. He wants to join the hug. He is a persistent dog. He licks their mom's face and makes her laugh. Anna and Ben laugh too. They are a happy family.
