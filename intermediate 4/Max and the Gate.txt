Category: Intermediate
Title: Max and the Gate

One day, a big, intelligent dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a gate. Max wanted to go through the gate to play with his friend, a little bird named Lily. Max pushed the gate with his paw, but it did not open. He called out, "Lily, can you help me open the gate?" Lily flew down and tried to push the gate with her small wings. But the gate still did not open. Then, Max had an idea. He used his paw to slap the gate hard. To their surprise, the gate swung open! Max and Lily were very happy. They went through the gate and played together all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat named Tom. Tom was very tired. He let out a big yawn. He wanted to sleep, but he could not find a cozy place to rest. Tom walked and walked. He saw a distant house. The house was far away, but Tom was brave. He walked to the house and saw a warm light inside. Tom thought, "Maybe I can find a cozy place to sleep there." Tom went inside the house. He smelled something yummy. It was cocoa! A kind girl saw Tom and said, "You look tired. Would you like some cocoa?" Tom said, "Yes, please." The girl gave Tom some cocoa. It was warm and sweet. After drinking the cocoa, Tom felt sleepy. The girl said, "You can sleep here, Tom." She showed him a soft bed. Tom lay down and closed his eyes. He was happy to find a cozy place to sleep. And Tom slept all night long.

Once upon a time, there was a bear named Bobo. Bobo loved honey very much. Every day, he would go to the big tree where the bees lived to get honey. Bobo was a very flexible bear, so he could bend and reach the honey easily. One day, the bees asked Bobo for help. They needed to deliver honey to their friend, the butterfly. Bobo said yes, he wanted to help his bee friends. So, Bobo took the honey and went to find the butterfly. Bobo found the butterfly in the flower garden. He gave the honey to the butterfly, and the butterfly was very happy. The bees were happy too, and they thanked Bobo for his help. From that day on, Bobo and the bees were very good friends. They learned that helping each other made them all happy. The moral of the story is that it is good to help your friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little monkey named Momo. Momo loved to have bananas. He would eat bananas all day long. He was a very happy monkey. One day, Momo saw a big banana on a tree. He wanted to have it. But there was a naughty bird sitting on the tree. The bird did not want Momo to have the banana. Momo tried to be nice to the naughty bird. He said, "Please let me have the banana." The bird thought for a moment and then said, "Okay, you can have the banana." Momo was so happy! He ate the banana and shared some with the bird. They became good friends and played together every day.

One day, a little girl named Jane went for a walk. She wore her red mittens to keep her hands warm. As she walked, she saw a big, scary tree. The tree had a big hole in it. Jane was scared, but she wanted to see what was in the hole. She walked up to the tree and looked inside. There was a little cat. The cat let out a big yawn. Jane was not scared anymore. She wanted to help the cat. She took off her red mitten and put it in the hole. The cat looked at the mitten and then looked at Jane. It jumped out of the hole and onto her shoulder. Jane was surprised! She thought the cat was scary, but it was just a nice little cat. They walked home together, and Jane had a new friend.

Tom and Mia like to go to the zoo with Mom and Dad. They see many animals there. They see lions, monkeys, elephants, and more. But their favorite animal is the ostrich. The ostrich is a big bird with long legs and a long neck. It can run very fast, but it cannot fly. One day, Tom and Mia see a sign that says: "Come and feed the ostriches!" They are very excited. They ask Mom and Dad if they can do it. Mom and Dad say yes, and they buy some food for the ostriches. They follow the sign to a big fence. There are many ostriches inside. Some are eating, some are sleeping, and some are looking at the people. Tom and Mia hold the food in their hands and walk to the fence. They see a friendly ostrich that comes to them. It has big eyes and a small beak. It makes a funny sound. "Hello, ostrich!" Tom says. "Do you want some food?" He holds out his hand with the food. The ostrich pecks at the food gently. It likes it. It makes another sound. "Thank you, ostrich!" Mia says. She holds out her hand with more food. The ostrich pecks at it too. It is happy. But then, another ostrich comes. It is bigger and meaner than the friendly ostrich. It wants the food too. It pushes the friendly ostrich away. It pecks at Tom's hand hard. It hurts. Tom drops the food and cries. "Ow, ow, ow!" he says. "Bad ostrich! Go away!" Mia is scared. She drops her food too. The mean ostrich eats it all. It makes a loud sound. "No, no, no!" Mia says. "You are not nice! You are greedy!" Mom and Dad hear Tom and Mia cry. They run to them and hug them. They check their hands. They are not bleeding, but they are red and sore. Mom and Dad say sorry to Tom and Mia. They say the mean ostrich was rude and selfish. They say the friendly ostrich was sweet and gentle. They say they are proud of Tom and Mia for being brave and kind. They say they love them very much. Tom and Mia feel better. They smile and hug Mom and Dad back. They say they love them too. They say they still like the ostriches, but not the mean one. They say they want to see the other animals now. Mom and Dad agree. They leave the fence and walk to the next exhibit. They have a fun day at the zoo.
