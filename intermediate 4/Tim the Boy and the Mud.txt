Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Mud

One day, a boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a big pile of thick mud. Tim had an idea. He wanted to make the mud even more fun to play with. Tim ran to his mom and asked, "Can I add water to the mud?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful not to make a big mess." Tim was happy and went to get a bucket of water. He poured the water into the thick mud and mixed it all up. The mud became soft and gooey. Tim jumped into the mud and had a lot of fun playing in it. His mom came out and laughed, "You made a great mud puddle, Tim!" They both smiled and enjoyed the muddy day.

Once there was a little girl who had a special chest. It was green and shiny and filled with lots of colourful treasures. Inside the chest, her things were very organized. She arranged some of the objects in one corner of the chest, some at the centre and some at the other corner. One day, she decided to take out all the items from the chest and put them back in reverse. She had a lot of fun arranging the objects in different ways. When she was done, she had created a brand new, more organized layout! She was so proud of herself for doing such a big job. She smiled as she saw all her things in a different order. It was like a completely new chest! She promised to reverse the things in her chest often to keep it fresh and fun.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big, old toy jeep. It was the first one he had ever seen. It was not like the other toy cars he had. This jeep was original and very special. Tim loved to play with it all day long. One sunny day, Tim was playing with his jeep outside. He made the jeep go fast and jump high. Suddenly, his big brother Sam came out to play too. Sam saw the jeep and said, "Wow, that's a cool jeep! Can I play with it too?" Tim said, "Yes, but be careful. It's my favorite toy." Sam took the jeep and started to play with it. He made it go even faster and jump even higher than Tim did. But then, something unexpected happened. The jeep hit a big rock and broke into many pieces. The jeep was destroyed. Tim was very sad and cried. Sam felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, Tim. I didn't mean to break your jeep." Tim looked at the broken jeep and said, "It's okay, Sam. We can fix it together." So they worked together to fix the jeep, and it became even more special to Tim because now it had a story to tell.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. They saw a big tree with sweet, red apples. Lily wanted to pick an apple from the tree. Her mom helped her reach it. The apple was very pretty and had a nice texture. Lily saw a boy named Tim at the park. He was sad because he had no apple. Lily wanted to make Tim happy. So, she offered him her sweet apple. Tim smiled and said thank you to Lily. Now, Tim and Lily were both happy. They played together at the park and shared the sweet apple. The apple had a good texture and they both liked it a lot. They became good friends and always shared with each other.

One day, a big dog named Max felt an enormous pain in his leg. He didn't know what to do, so he went to find his friend, a little cat named Lily. Max urged Lily to help him because the pain was so bad. Lily thought of a plan to help Max. They went to the park and found a big tree. Lily told Max to hit his leg on the tree to make the pain go away. Max was scared, but he trusted Lily. Max hit his leg on the tree, and something unexpected happened. The pain went away! But, it was not because of the tree. Max saw a small rock fall out of his shoe. The rock was what made his leg hurt. Max and Lily laughed and played in the park, happy that the pain was gone.
