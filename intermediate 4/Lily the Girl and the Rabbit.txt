Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Rabbit

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk in the woods. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. Lily liked to listen to the sweet songs of the birds. As she walked, she saw a little rabbit. The rabbit was scared and said, "I can't find my way home. Can you help me?" Lily said, "Of course, I will help you find your home." They walked and walked, looking for the rabbit's home. The sun started to set, and it became night. Lily and the rabbit were still in the woods. They were both scared, but they kept walking. Suddenly, they saw a light. They walked towards the light and found a tiny house. The rabbit said, "This is not my home, but let's see who lives here." They knocked on the door, and a little mouse opened it. The mouse said, "Hello, I am Momo. My friends are lost too. Can you help us find our way home?" Lily, the rabbit, and Momo decided to help each other. They walked together, and soon they found all their homes. They became best friends, and they never got lost again.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. He had a hidden toy guitar. He loved to play it every day. Tom would sing and dance with his guitar. One day, Tom met a girl named Sally. She liked to sing and dance too. They became good friends. They played with the guitar and sang songs together. Tom and Sally grew up and decided to marry. At their wedding, they played the hidden guitar and danced. They were very happy and lived happily ever after.

One day, Roxy the cow was having trouble. Her hoof felt very tough, so she couldn't walk. She rolled over to her best friend, Freddy the pig. "What's wrong, Roxy?" asked Freddy. "My hoof is really tough and I can't walk," replied Roxy sadly. "Don't worry! I think I know how to help," said Freddy. He rolled over to the kitchen and grabbed some cold glass of milk. "Here, drink this," he said, handing it to Roxy. Roxy drank the milk and after just a few sips, she felt much better. She rolled around the barnyard, feeling better and better. The tough hoof was now feeling much softer and she was able to walk again. "Thank you, Freddy!" said Roxy. "You're very welcome," smiled Freddy.

One day, a smart cat named Tom went for a walk. It was a nice season, with lots of sun and warm air. Tom liked this time of year because he could play outside with his friends. Tom saw his friend, a dog named Max. Max was playing with a ball. "Hi Max, can I play too?" Tom asked. "Yes, come and play!" Max said. They played with the ball and had lots of fun. As they were playing, the ball went too far and hit a big tree. The tree did not like it and gave the ball a big slap. The ball flew back to Tom and Max. They laughed and played some more.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy phone. One day, he played with his toy phone and said, "Ring, ring!" He pretended to talk to his friend, Sam. Tim told Sam about the enormous jelly he saw at the store. It was so big and wiggly! The next day, Tim and Sam went to the store together. They wanted to see the enormous jelly. They walked and walked until they found the jelly aisle. Tim said, "Look! There it is!" The jelly was so big, it was hard to carry. But they wanted to show their friends, so they carried it together. They brought the enormous jelly to the park. All their friends were amazed. The jelly wiggled and jiggled as they played with it. Suddenly, the jelly fell and broke into many small pieces. Tim and Sam were sad. But then, they had an idea. They shared the small pieces of jelly with all their friends. Everyone was happy and had a great time eating the tasty jelly.
