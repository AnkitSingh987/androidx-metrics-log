Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Bird and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Bob. Bob liked to fly and sing. One day, Bob saw a big jail. He flew near the jail and saw a sad dog inside. The dog looked at Bob and said, "I am sad. I want to go out and play." Bob felt bad for the dog. He thought, "I wonder how I can help the dog." Then, he had an idea. He found a key on the ground. Bob picked up the key with his beak and flew to the jail. He put the key in the lock and opened the door. The dog was very grateful. He said, "Thank you, Bob! Now I can go out and play!" The dog and Bob played together and became good friends. They were happy and never went near the jail again.

Ben and Lily like to play hide and seek. They take turns to hide and wait for the other to find them. They have many good spots to hide in the house and the yard. One day, Ben is the seeker and Lily is the hider. Lily runs to the shed and finds a big box. She thinks it is a good spot to hide. She climbs into the box and closes the lid. She waits for Ben to come. Ben looks for Lily everywhere. He checks the closet, the bed, the sofa, the tree, the swing, but he cannot find her. He starts to feel sad. He wonders where Lily is. He calls her name, but he hears no answer. Lily hears Ben calling her name. She wants to say something, but she cannot. The box is too tight and dark. She starts to feel scared. She tries to open the lid, but she cannot. The box is too heavy and stuck. She starts to cry. Ben hears Lily crying. He follows the sound to the shed. He sees the box and pushes it. He opens the lid and sees Lily. He hugs her and says, "I found you! Are you okay?" Lily hugs him back and says, "I'm okay. I'm lucky you found me. I was stuck in the box. It was not a good spot to hide." Ben says, "I'm sorry you were stuck. It was a hard spot to find. Next time, let's hide somewhere else." Lily says, "Okay. Next time, you hide and I seek." They smile and go back to the house. They are happy to play together.

Once upon a time, there was an old man who lived in an ancient forest. He was a wise man who was known to have many stories to tell. One day, a curious three year old girl named Clara went walking in the forest and saw the old man. She asked him if he had any stories to tell. The old man smiled and said, "Yes, I do." Clara sat down and listened intently as the old man spoke. He told stories about the forest, animals, and things that happened in the distant past. He even taught her some words and many of their meanings. Clara listened for hours as the old man talked. When he finished, she thanked him for his wonderful stories. He gave her a wink and said, "That's what old men like me are here for."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sofia. She was only three years old and liked to play outside all the time. One day, while she was out playing, she suddenly noticed something in the bushes. It was a bright, green cricket! Sofia was so excited, she ran back home to show her mom. When she arrived, her mom asked her what she had found. Sofia said she found an eye. Her mom couldn't believe it and started to laugh. Sofia didn't understand what was so funny, so she asked why everyone was laughing. Her mom replied, "That's not an eye, it's a cricket. You made a mistake and that's okay. It's nothing to be embarrassed about". Sofia realised her mistake and smiled. She learnt that it's alright to make mistakes, big or small, because everyone does! Knowing this made her feel much better. From then on, she knew to be brave enough to admit she was wrong, even if it felt a little hidden. The moral of this story is that it is okay to make mistakes. Everyone makes them, so it's nothing to be embarrassed about and it's good to be brave enough to admit when you're wrong.
