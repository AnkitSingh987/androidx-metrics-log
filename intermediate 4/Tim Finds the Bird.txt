Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim Finds the Bird

Once upon a time, in a big green park, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a huge tree with many apples on it. Tim thought to himself, "I want to get a big apple to eat." Tim saw a tall ladder near the tree. He climbed up the ladder to get the apple. When he reached the top, he discovered a tiny bird sitting on a branch. The bird said, "Hello, Tim! Can you help me find my family?" Tim was surprised to hear the bird talk, but he wanted to help. They looked around the tree and found the bird's family hiding in a hole. The bird was so happy that it gave Tim the biggest apple on the tree as a thank you gift. Tim and the bird became good friends and shared the huge apple together.

Once upon a time, there was an enormous maze. In the maze lived a little bunny named Bobo. Bobo loved to play and hop around in the maze. But one day, he went too far and got lost. Bobo felt regret for going too far. He wanted to find his way back home. As he hopped around, he met a big, kind bear. The bear saw that Bobo was sad and asked, "Why are you sad, little bunny?" Bobo told the bear that he was lost and wanted to go home. The big bear knew the maze very well. He said, "I can help you, Bobo!" They walked together, and the bear showed Bobo the way. But then, something unexpected happened. The bear and Bobo found a secret door in the maze. They opened the door and found a beautiful garden with lots of yummy food and fun games. Bobo and the bear decided to stay and play in the garden, and they became the best of friends.

One day, Tom and Sue decide to play. They go to the park. They see a big hill. "Let's roll down the hill!" says Tom. Sue nods and they both go to the top. They start to roll down the hill. They go round and round, getting dizzy. They laugh and have fun. But then, something unexpected happens. Tom rolls too fast and bumps into a tree! "Ouch!" says Tom, rubbing his head. He looks at the tree and sees a big tip jar. It is full of shiny coins. "Look, Sue! We found a treasure!" he says. Sue runs to Tom and looks at the jar. "Wow! That's a lot of coins!" she says. They decide to take the jar and share the treasure with their friends. They all have a big party with the coins, and everyone is happy.

Tom was a spider who liked to make cobwebs. He made them in the corners of the house, where he could catch flies and bugs. He was proud of his cobwebs, because they were big and strong and shiny. One day, he saw a new spider in the house. Her name was Lily, and she was very different from Tom. She was smaller and faster and had bright colors on her back. She also made cobwebs, but they were not like Tom's. They were small and round and had patterns on them. Tom was curious about Lily and her cobwebs. He wanted to talk to her and see how she made them. But he was also shy and afraid. He thought Lily might not like him or his cobwebs. He thought she might laugh at him or run away. But Lily was not like that. She saw Tom watching her and smiled. She said hello and asked him his name. She told him she liked his cobwebs and asked him how he made them. She said she was new in the house and wanted to make friends. Tom felt happy and surprised. He said hello and told her his name. He told her how he made his cobwebs and asked her how she made hers. He said he was glad to meet her and wanted to be friends. They talked and laughed and showed each other their cobwebs. They found out they had many things in common, like their favorite foods and places to hide. They also learned from each other, like how to make different shapes and colors with their silk. They decided to join their cobwebs and make a big one together. They were happy and proud of their cobweb, because it was different and beautiful and theirs.
