Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Flower

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to pick flowers with her daddy. They would go to the park and find the most beautiful flowers to bring home. One day, Lily and her daddy found a big, creative flower. It was so big that Lily could not pick it. Daddy helped her, and they both smiled. They were so happy to find this special flower. They took the creative flower home and put it in a vase. Every day, Lily and her daddy would look at the flower and remember their fun day at the park. They loved to pick flowers together and make their home beautiful.

Once upon a time, there was a pencil who lived in a big box. The pencil was very sad because it was boring. It did not have any colors like the other pencils. The other pencils were red, blue, and green. The boring pencil was just gray. One day, the boring pencil went to work with a little girl. The little girl liked to draw with all the pencils. She drew a big sun with the red pencil, a big tree with the green pencil, and a big sea with the blue pencil. The boring pencil did not have any work to do. It felt very sad. Then, something unexpected happened. The girl picked up the boring pencil and started to draw something new. It was a big, gray elephant with a long nose. The boring pencil was so happy! It was not boring anymore. It could draw big, gray elephants, and it was special too.

Ben and Lily wanted to go to the zoo. They asked Mom and Dad for tickets, but Mom and Dad said no. They said the zoo was too far and too expensive. Ben and Lily were sad and angry. They had a plan. They waited until Mom and Dad were busy. Then they took their piggy banks and ran to the bus stop. They saw a big bus with a picture of a lion on it. They thought it was the zoo bus. They gave the driver some coins and got on. The bus was full of people. Ben and Lily looked for seats. They saw a nice lady with a big bag. They asked her if they could sit next to her. She said yes and smiled. She opened her bag and showed them something. It was a snake! Ben and Lily screamed. They did not like snakes. They were scared. The lady said, "Don't worry, he is my pet. His name is Slither. He is very friendly. Do you want to touch him?" Ben and Lily said no. They wanted to escape. They jumped out of their seats and ran to the front of the bus. They asked the driver to stop. The driver said, "What's wrong? We are almost at the zoo." Ben and Lily said, "This is not the zoo bus. This is the snake bus. We want to go home. Please let us off." The driver said, "OK, OK, calm down. I will stop at the next stop. But you need to pay me another ticket. You only paid for one way." Ben and Lily looked at their piggy banks. They were empty. They had no more coins. They had no more tickets. They were stuck on the snake bus. They were not successful. They wished they had listened to Mom and Dad.

Once upon a time there was a fat clown. He was so big and happy. He carried his bag full of things everywhere he went. One day, the clown was walking down the street and he spotted a little girl. She was only 3 years old. The clown was so excited to see her. He asked the little girl: "Would you like to come with me?". The girl nodded and smiled. The clown picked her up and carried her on his shoulders. He made funny sounds and sang silly songs as they walked. The little girl laughed and giggled with joy. The clown carried the little girl all the way to her house. Then he said goodbye and she waved as he walked away. The fat clown was so happy to have met the little girl. He knew that she would remember him forever.
