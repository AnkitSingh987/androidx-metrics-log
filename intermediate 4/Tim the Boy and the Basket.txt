Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Basket

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was looking for a job. He wanted to help his mom and dad. They needed his help because they were very busy. One day, Tim found a job in an unknown place. He had to pick up apples. Tim was happy to have a job. He worked very hard every day. He picked up many apples and put them in a big basket. Tim's mom and dad were so proud of him. They didn't need to worry about their work anymore. Tim's job made everything better. And the unknown place became a happy place for everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy dog named Spot. Spot had a red cap. He wore it every day. Spot loved to follow his friends in the park. One day, Spot and his friends played a fun game. They ran and jumped while Spot followed them. But, Spot tripped on a rock and lost his red cap. He looked everywhere, but could not find it. Spot was sad and wanted his cap back. A little bird saw Spot and wanted to help. The bird flew up high and found the red cap. The bird brought the cap back to Spot. Spot was very happy and thanked the bird. From that day, Spot learned to be more careful and help others too. And Spot and the bird became best friends.

The little boy was so enthusiastic when he saw the ice cream stand. He begged his mom to buy it for him. “Mommy, can I have some ice cream? Please!” the little boy said eagerly. His mom looked at the ice cream with a potential expression.“Alright, but we must prevent you from eating too much of it. Remember, too much of a good thing can make you sick,” she said with a smile. The boy was so excited when his mom handed him the ice cream. He wanted to take a bite right away, but he wanted to savor the taste first. He took a deep whiff and smiled. “It tastes so good!” the little boy exclaimed. “Yes, it does,” his mom agreed. She then looked at him with a stern expression. “But still, remember to prevent yourself from eating too much. We don’t want you to get too sick, okay?” The little boy knew his mom was right. He savored the taste in small bites and enjoyed every morsel of it.

One day, a curious boy named Tom found a map in his room. The map was big and had many colors. Tom did not know what the map was for. He showed the map to his friend, Sam. Sam looked at the map and said, "I think this map can help us find something fun!" Tom and Sam went outside to follow the map. They walked and walked until they saw a big tree. The map suggested they should dig under the tree. Tom and Sam were very excited. They found a box under the tree. They thought it would have toys or candy inside. But when they opened the box, they found a note. The note said, "You found my secret box! Now, it is your turn to hide it and make a new map for someone else to find." Tom and Sam were surprised, but they smiled. They knew they could make a fun game for someone else to play.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had many blocks to play with. Lily was a very obedient girl and always listened to her mom. One day, Lily's friend, Tim, came to play with her. They built a big tower with the blocks. Tim said, "Wow, our tower is so tall!" Lily agreed, "Yes, it's amazing!" Suddenly, the tower started to shake. Lily and Tim began to panic. "Oh no, it's going to fall!" cried Lily. Tim quickly said, "Don't worry, let's hold it together." They held the tower and stopped it from falling. In the end, Lily and Tim felt proud of their teamwork. They learned that when they panic, they can still work together to fix things. And they continued to play happily with the blocks.
