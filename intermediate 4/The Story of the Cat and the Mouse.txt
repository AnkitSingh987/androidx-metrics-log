Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Cat and the Mouse

Once upon a time, there was a big, bossy cat. The cat liked to tell everyone what to do. One day, the cat saw a small mouse. The mouse was very little and was working hard to clean up some dust. "Hey, mouse!" said the bossy cat. "You need to work harder to clean up all the dust!" The little mouse was sad but kept working. He tried his best to make the cat happy. But the bossy cat was never happy. He always wanted more work done. In the end, the little mouse was too tired and sad. He could not clean up all the dust for the bossy cat. The bossy cat was still not happy, and the little mouse did not have any fun.

Once upon a time, in a little house, there lived a boy named Tim. He loved to eat bread with butter. Every day, he would ask his mom for more bread and butter. One day, Tim wanted to learn how to put butter on his bread. His mom was very gentle and showed him how to do it. She said, "First, you take the knife, then you put it in the butter, and then you spread it on the bread." Tim tried to follow her steps. Tim had to repeat the steps many times to get it right. He would put the knife in the butter and then spread it on the bread. Each time, he got better at it. Finally, he could do it all by himself. Tim was so happy and proud. Now, he could eat bread with butter anytime he wanted.

One day, a cute bunny was hopping in the garden. He saw many vegetables. He liked to eat them. But he knew he should ask first. The bunny went to the farmer. He asked, "Can I eat some vegetables?" The farmer said, "No, they are not for you." The bunny was sad. He wanted the vegetables so much. He thought of a plan. He would do a dance for the farmer. The bunny danced and hopped. The farmer laughed and clapped. He said, "Okay, you can have some vegetables." The cute bunny was happy. He ate the vegetables and said, "Thank you!" The farmer and the bunny became friends. They shared the vegetables and had fun together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went on a journey with his mom. They walked and walked until they found a big tree. Tim saw a squirrel on the tree and wanted to play with it. His mom told him to be careful. "Tim, don't go too close. The squirrel might get scared and hurt you," Mom said. But Tim could not resist. He wanted to touch the squirrel's soft fur. Tim ran to the tree and tried to climb it. But the squirrel got scared and jumped on Tim's face. Tim fell from the tree and got hurt. He cried and cried. Mom came to help him. "Tim, I told you not to go close to the squirrel. Now you are hurt, and we have to go back home," Mom said sadly. Tim felt bad for not listening to his mom. They had to end their journey early, and Tim had a big bruise on his face.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play outside in the big landscape. He liked to run, jump, and laugh with his friends. The sun was always shining, and the sky was always blue. One day, Tim found a little dog. The dog was lost and sad. Tim wanted to help the dog become happy again. He took the dog home and gave it food and water. The dog was very obedient and listened to Tim. Tim and the dog played together every day. They ran, jumped, and laughed in the big landscape. The dog was happy, and Tim was happy, too. They were best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a small, sweet bunny. The bunny lived near a big hill. One sunny day, the bunny went out to play. As the sweet bunny hopped up the hill, it saw a pretty flower. The bunny wanted the flower, but it was high up on the hill. The bunny tried to jump, but it could not reach the flower. Then, the sweet bunny had an idea. It hopped back and ran fast up the hill. The bunny jumped and got the flower! But, it started to fall down the hill. The bunny closed its eyes and hoped it would be okay. At the bottom of the hill, the bunny opened its eyes and saw that it was safe. It held the pretty flower and smiled. The sweet bunny was happy.
