Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Barky

Once upon a time, in a cold land, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a small igloo with his noisy dog named Barky. They loved to play and laugh all day long. One day, Tim and Barky wanted to build a big snowman. They worked very hard, but they could not make the snowman stand up. Tim was sad, but Barky said, "Woof! We can do it! We will succeed!" Tim and Barky tried again. They rolled a big snowball and a small snowball. They put the small one on top of the big one. This time, they succeeded! They made a big snowman, and Tim was very happy. Barky barked and jumped around the snowman, making lots of noise. From that day on, Tim and Barky knew that if they worked together, they could succeed at anything. They lived happily in their little igloo, always ready for their next big adventure.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a small orange bird named Kiki. Kiki loved to fly and sing. One day, Kiki saw a big tree with a hole in it. "This is a good start for my new home!" she said. Kiki worked hard to make her new home nice and cozy. Soon, she had a friend named Buzzy, the bee. Buzzy was sad because he couldn't find any flowers. Kiki said, "Don't worry, Buzzy! I will help you find flowers." Kiki and Buzzy looked all around the forest for flowers. They saw a big flower far away. "Let's go there!" said Buzzy. When they got to the flower, Buzzy gave Kiki a big kiss. "Thank you, Kiki!" he said. Together, they found more flowers and became best friends. They learned that helping each other made them happy. And that was the start of a beautiful friendship. The moral of the story is: when you help others, you will find happiness too.

Once upon a time, there was a playful mule named Max. Max lived on a farm with many friends. Max loved to help his friends every day. One day, Max saw his friend, the duck, in trouble. The duck's toy was stuck in a tree. Max wanted to help. So, Max used his long legs to reach the toy. The duck was very happy. Max felt good when he helped his friends. All the animals on the farm loved Max. They played together every day. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy some meat for dinner. Tim was very excited because he liked going to the store with his mom. It was important for him to help her. At the store, Tim saw a big dog. He pointed at the dog and said, "Look, Mom! A dog!" But the dog wanted the meat too. The dog barked and tried to take the meat from Tim's mom. Tim was scared, but he knew he had to help his mom. Tim said, "No, dog! This is our meat!" He stomped his foot and made a loud noise. The dog was surprised and ran away. Tim's mom was so proud of him. She said, "Good job, Tim! You are so brave and important." They took the meat home and had a yummy dinner.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved the color purple. One day, she found a big purple paper in her room. Lily was very happy and wanted to show her friend, Tim. Lily ran to Tim's house with the purple paper. She knocked on the door and said, "Tim, look at my big purple paper! Let's draw on it!" Tim was excited and said, "Yes! I will get my crayons." As they started to draw, a little dog came into the room. The dog saw the purple paper and thought it was a toy. The dog jumped and grabbed the paper in his mouth. Lily and Tim were surprised and said, "Oh no! Our paper!" The dog ran away with the purple paper. Lily and Tim chased after the dog, laughing. They finally caught the dog and got their paper back. They decided to draw a picture of the dog on the purple paper, and it became their favorite drawing ever.
