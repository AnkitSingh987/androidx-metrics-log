Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Bald Man and the Picture

One day, a bald man named Tom went to a room to print a big picture. He liked to print pretty pictures for his friends. The room had a big printer and a lot of colors. Tom was happy to be there. As Tom started to print, he saw that the picture was not coming out right. It was all mixed up! The colors were not where they should be. Tom was sad and did not know what to do. Then, something unexpected happened. The printer started to fix itself! The colors went back to the right places, and the picture came out perfect. Tom was so happy and surprised. He took the pretty picture and showed it to all his friends. They all loved the picture and could not believe the printer fixed itself.

Once upon a time, there was a small, unique shrimp named Sam. Sam lived in a big sea with many fish friends. Sam was the only shrimp who could load things onto his back. One day, Sam and his friends were playing near a big ship. Sam saw the ship had many things to load. He wanted to help. Sam told his friends, "I will help the ship load its things." Sam worked hard to load the things on the ship. His friends watched and cheered for him. After a long time, Sam finished loading the ship. The people on the ship were very happy. Because Sam helped the ship, the people gave him a special gift. It was a shiny, new shell. Sam put on the shell and felt very proud. His friends clapped and cheered. Sam was a unique and helpful shrimp.

Once upon a time, there was a clever little girl named Amy. Amy loved to fold paper into fun shapes like birds, stars, and flowers. She would give her folded paper gifts to her friends and family, and they all loved them. One day, Amy saw a young boy named Timmy who was sad. He couldn't fold paper like Amy could. Amy wanted to help Timmy, so she taught him how to fold paper into fun shapes too. Timmy was very happy, and he thanked Amy for helping him. The moral of the story is that when we help others and share our skills, we can make others happy and feel good too.

Once upon a time, in a very icy land, there lived a little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside with her friends. One day, while they were playing, they found a big chain on the ground. They didn't know what it was for, but they thought it was fun to play with. The next day, the sun was shining, and the ice began to melt. Lily and her friends were playing near a big hill. They saw a big rock start to roll down the hill towards them. They were scared and didn't know what to do. Then, Lily remembered the chain they found the day before. She ran to get it and told her friends to help her. They used the chain to stop the big rock from rolling down the hill. They saved themselves and their town from the big rock. Everyone was very happy and thanked Lily and her friends for their help.

Once upon a time, there was a famous ostrich named Ollie. Ollie loved to run and play with his friends. He was very fast and could rush from one place to another quickly. One sunny day, Ollie and his friends played a game of tag. Ollie was "it" and started to rush after his friends. He ran so fast that he caught all of them in no time. Everyone cheered for Ollie, the famous ostrich. They all had fun playing together and Ollie felt happy to have such good friends. The end.

Once upon a time, in a big pond, there was a tough alligator named Al. Al had many friends, but he loved to count. One sunny day, Al and his friends were playing by the pond. They were having lots of fun. But then, they saw a big cloud coming. They knew it would rain soon. Al had an idea. He told his friends, "Let's count how many times the rain falls in the pond!" They all agreed and waited for the rain to come. They knew that the one who would count the most raindrops would be the winner. Al was excited because he was good at counting. The rain came and all the friends started to count. Al was counting very fast. Suddenly, he saw a little bird who was wet and cold. Al stopped counting and helped the bird. He took the bird to a warm and dry place. Al didn't win the counting game, but he was happy because he helped a friend. In the end, Al was the real winner.
