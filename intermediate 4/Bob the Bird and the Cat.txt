Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Bird and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Bob. Bob was very patient. Every day, Bob would sit on a wire and watch the other birds fly around. One day, a big bird named Max came up to Bob and said, "Hey, Bob, why are you always sitting on the wire? Why don't you fly with us?" Bob looked at Max and said, "I am waiting for my friend, Sam. He is trapped in a cage. I want to help release him." The next day, Max came back with a key he found. Max said, "I found this key. Maybe it can open the cage and release Sam." Bob was very happy and they went to find Sam's cage. They put the key in the lock and turned it. The door opened, and out came... a cat! The cat had been hiding in the cage all along. The cat said, "Thank you for releasing me. I am sorry I scared Sam away." Bob and Max were surprised but they forgave the cat. They all became friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time there was a bunny who loved to jump. The bunny was fast and always hopped through the woods with joy. One day the bunny was feeling especially happy so it jumped to the top of the hill. When he reached the top, he noticed a well. He gulped and went to investigate. The bunny peered over the edge of the well and tasted the water. "It's delicious!", he exclaimed as he hopped around in delight. Suddenly, he heard a voice. "Do you like the taste of this water, little bunny?", the voice said. The bunny felt excited and nodded. He jumped closer to the voice and saw a little old man, who was smiling. "Well then, I'll give you a magical gift," the old man said. He reached into his pocket and gave the bunny a gumball. The bunny loved the sweet taste of the gumball and thanked the old man. Since then, the bunny has never forgotten the magical taste of the gumball from the old man by the well.

Tommy liked to play in the garden with his toy truck. He dug holes and filled them with dirt and rocks. One day, he saw a small, brown animal with a long nose and tiny eyes. It was a mole. The mole was digging a hole too, but much faster than Tommy. Tommy was curious. He wanted to see what the mole was doing. He followed the mole with his truck, but the mole was very shy. It hid under the ground and popped up in different places. Tommy tried to repeat what the mole did, but he could not dig as deep or as fast. The mole was mysterious. Tommy wondered why it dug so many holes. Was it looking for food? Was it making a home? Was it playing a game? Tommy wished he could talk to the mole and ask it questions. He decided to leave a gift for the mole. He put a shiny rock and a juicy worm in one of the holes. He hoped the mole would like them. The next day, Tommy came back to the garden. He looked for the hole where he left the gift. He was happy to see that the rock and the worm were gone. He saw a new hole nearby. He peeked inside and saw something shiny. It was a button. The mole had left a gift for Tommy too. Tommy smiled. He felt like he had made a new friend. He put the button in his pocket and went to look for the mole. He wanted to say thank you and play with it some more.

One day, a helpful boy named Tim wanted to go on a trip. He began to pack his bag. He put in toys, clothes, and a big buckle. The buckle was shiny and pretty. Tim loved to wear it. As Tim was packing, his friend Sue came to visit. "What are you doing?" asked Sue. "I am packing for a trip!" said Tim. "Can I come too?" asked Sue. Tim said, "Yes, let's go together!" On their trip, they found a big box. They opened it and, to their surprise, it was full of more shiny buckles! They were so happy and decided to share them with all their friends. And that's how Tim and Sue's trip became the best trip ever.
