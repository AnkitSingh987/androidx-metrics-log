Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Cat and the Mouse

One day, a friendly cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a long strip of grass and decided to play on it. Tom met a little bird on the grass. The bird said, "Hi, I am Bob. Do you want to play with me?" Tom and Bob played together on the strip of grass. They had a lot of fun. They ran, jumped, and laughed. Suddenly, they heard a scream. They looked around and saw a tiny mouse trapped in a hole. Tom and Bob ran to help the mouse. The mouse was scared and said, "Please help me! I am stuck!" Tom and Bob worked together to get the mouse out of the hole. The mouse was very happy and thanked them. From that day on, Tom, Bob, and the mouse became best friends. They played on the strip of grass every day and always helped each other when they needed it. The three friends were always happy and loved to play together.

One day, a boy named Tim went to help his mom with the laundry. The laundry was very long, and it took a long time to finish. Tim's mom called to him, "Tim, can you help me fold the laundry?" Tim said, "Yes, Mom!" and he ran to help her. They folded the laundry together. Tim liked to help his mom because it made her happy. After they finished the laundry, Tim's mom gave him a big hug. She said, "Thank you, Tim, for helping me with the long laundry." Tim smiled and hugged her back. He knew that helping his mom was a good thing to do.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy loved to wander around her house. One day, she found a big box of cereal in the kitchen. Lucy was very happy because cereal was her favorite food. Lucy's mom saw her with the cereal and said, "Lucy, you can have some cereal if you can find a bowl and a spoon." Lucy looked and looked but she could not find a bowl and a spoon. She was sad because she really wanted to eat the cereal. Then, Lucy's dad came into the kitchen. He saw Lucy looking sad and asked, "Why are you sad, Lucy?" Lucy said, "I can't find a bowl and a spoon to eat my cereal." Lucy's dad smiled and said, "I will help you find a bowl and a spoon." They looked and looked, and they found a bowl and a spoon. Lucy was very happy and felt successful. She ate her cereal with a big smile on her face.

One day, a little caterpillar was in the garden. It was a tough caterpillar. It wanted to find a good place to sleep. The caterpillar met a big dog. The dog said, "What are you doing, little caterpillar?" The caterpillar said, "I am trying to find a place to sleep." The dog wanted to help. It looked around and saw a big leaf. The dog said, "Look, little caterpillar! This leaf will be a good place for you to sleep." The caterpillar was happy. It went to the leaf and started to sleep. While the caterpillar slept, it changed. When it woke up, it was not a caterpillar anymore. It was a beautiful butterfly! The butterfly said, "Thank you, big dog, for helping me find a good place to sleep." The dog and the butterfly played together in the garden. They were very happy. The butterfly revealed its colorful wings and flew high in the sky. The dog watched and smiled.

John and his Dad were outside playing in the park. John liked to ask his Dad lots of questions and he had one he wanted to ask him now. John asked, "Dad, what is that?" He pointed at a big black object on the ground. His Dad answered, "That is a gun, John." John asked, "What is a gun?" His Dad answered, "A gun is a big and tight toy. We don't play with it." John asked, "Why not?" His Dad said, "It's too tight, and it's not a toy, so we don't play with it." John nodded his head and said, "Okay, Dad." He thought about the gun and wondered what it was for. His Dad had the answer. "Guns are made to protect us," he said. John smiled and said, "That's cool!"
