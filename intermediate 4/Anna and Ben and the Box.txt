Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna and Ben and the Box

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to watch movies on the TV. Sometimes, they go to the movie place with Mom and Dad. The movie place has a large screen and many seats. Anna and Ben can see and hear the movies very well. Today, Mom and Dad say they will buy a movie for Anna and Ben. They can choose any movie they want. Anna and Ben are very happy. They look at the movie boxes on the shelf. There are many movies. Some have animals, some have cars, some have princesses, some have superheroes. Anna likes princesses. She picks a movie with a pink box and a princess on the cover. She says, "I want this movie, Mom. It has a princess and a castle and a horse." Ben likes superheroes. He picks a movie with a blue box and a superhero on the cover. He says, "I want this movie, Dad. It has a superhero and a robot and a spaceship." Mom and Dad smile. They say, "OK, Anna and Ben. You can have these movies. They look fun. Let's go and pay for them. Then we can go home and watch them on the TV." Anna and Ben are very excited. They hug Mom and Dad and say, "Thank you, Mom and Dad. You are the best. We love you and we love movies."

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went on a trip with his mom and dad. They had to unpack their bags when they got to the big house. Tim was patient and waited for his mom and dad to finish. Tim found a glove in his bag. It was a magic glove! He put it on and went outside to play. He saw a cat stuck in a tree. Tim wanted to help the cat. He waved his magic glove and the cat came down from the tree. The cat was happy, and Tim was happy too. But then, something unexpected happened. The cat turned into a big, friendly dog! Tim was surprised, but he liked the dog. The dog could talk! The dog said, "Thank you for saving me, Tim. I was a dog stuck in a cat's body. Your magic glove helped me become a dog again." Tim and the dog became best friends and had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was an enormous motorcycle. It was very big and shiny. The motorcycle liked to ride fast and make loud noises. One day, the motorcycle met a little boy named Tim. Tim was very small and liked to play with his toys. "Hi, motorcycle!" said Tim. "You are so big! Can I ride on you?" The motorcycle was happy to meet Tim. "Yes, you can ride on me," said the motorcycle. "But you must be very careful. I am very big and fast." Tim climbed on the motorcycle and they went for a ride. They went up and down the hills and through the park. Tim was very happy and laughed as they rode. The motorcycle was happy too because it had a new friend. At the end of the day, Tim and the motorcycle went back home. Tim's mom and dad were happy to see them. They all had a big dinner together and the motorcycle stayed with Tim. From that day on, they were best friends and had many more fun adventures together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars. They were very noisy, but he didn't mind. One day, Tim found a big map in his room. The map had many colors and lines on it. Tim was very excited to find out what the map was for. Tim showed the map to his mom. "Mom, what is this?" he asked. "That is a map, Tim," she said. "It shows us how to get to places." Tim thought that was very cool. He wanted to use the map to go on an adventure. So, Tim and his mom went outside. They followed the map to a big park. The park had a playground, and Tim was so happy. He enjoyed playing on the swings and the slides. "I like the map, Mom!" said Tim. "It took us to a fun place!" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, maps can take us on great adventures." And they played at the park all day long.
