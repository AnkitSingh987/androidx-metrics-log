Category: Intermediate
Title: Bobo the Ugly Dog and the Girl

Once upon a time, there was an ugly dog named Bobo. Bobo was not like other dogs. He was big, and his fur was not soft. But Bobo was a good dog. One day, Bobo saw a little girl crying. She was sad because her ice cream fell on the ground. Bobo wanted to help, so he did a funny motion with his tail. The girl stopped crying and started to laugh. Bobo was happy he made the girl feel better. Then, Bobo did something unexpected. He used his big paw to wipe the ice cream off the ground. The girl was amazed! She gave Bobo a big hug, and they became best friends. From that day on, everyone saw that Bobo was not just an ugly dog, but a very special and kind friend.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play all day long. One day, he saw something on the ground. It was a paper with a picture on it. It was a license! Spot picked up the license in his mouth and started to run. He wanted to find the person in the picture and give it back. As Spot ran, he saw a big tree. There was a kitten up in the tree, and it was trembling. Spot wanted to help the kitten, so he barked and jumped. The kitten got scared and jumped down from the tree. The kitten was happy to be on the ground again. Spot showed the kitten the license, and they both went to find the person in the picture. At last, they found the person, and he was very happy to get his license back. He thanked Spot and the kitten for their help. From that day on, Spot, the kitten, and the person became best friends and had many more jolly adventures together.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin lived in a small tank in a big house. One day, Fin saw a tall tree outside the window. He wanted to see the tree up close, so he thought of a plan. Fin asked his friend, Birdie, for help. Birdie was a happy bird who lived in a cage near Fin's tank. Fin said, "Birdie, can you help me see the tree? I am worried I can't do it alone." Birdie agreed to help Fin. They waited for the window to open. When the window opened, Birdie leaned on Fin's tank and picked him up with her beak. Then, she flew to the tree. Fin was so happy to see the tree, but he missed his tank. So, Birdie took him back home. The moral of the story is that it's okay to ask for help when you're worried or scared. Friends are there to help and support each other.

Once upon a time, there was a powerful pink cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play and help her friends. One day, Kitty's friend, Bunny, got stuck in a tree. Kitty saw Bunny in the tree and said, "Bunny, don't worry! I will rescue you!" Kitty used her powerful legs to jump high and reach Bunny. She grabbed Bunny and they both landed safely on the ground. Bunny said, "Thank you, Kitty! You are a great friend!" Kitty and Bunny were very happy. They played together all day long. And from that day on, they knew they could always count on each other.

Once upon a time, there was a big gray rock. The rock was very angry. It did not like it when anyone came near it. One day, a small boy saw the rock. He was very curious. He wanted to touch the rock. His mom said, "No, don't touch the angry rock!" But the boy did not listen. The boy touched the gray rock. The rock got very angry. It started to shake and jump. The boy was scared and ran away. But the rock kept jumping and shaking. The boy's house broke, and everyone was sad.

Once upon a time, there was a smart cat named Tom. Tom was very good at giving massages. One day, Tom met a little dog named Max. Max was sad because his back hurt. Tom said, "Max, I can give you a massage to help you feel better. But please don't spoil it by moving too much." Max agreed and sat very still. Tom started to give Max a gentle massage. As Tom gave Max the massage, Max felt better and happier. He said, "Thank you, Tom! You are a very smart cat and a good friend." From that day on, Tom and Max were the best of friends, and they played together all the time.
