Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Bird and the Cloud

One day, a little bird named Tim was flying in the sky. He saw a big, soft cloud. The cloud was very nice. Tim wanted to carry the cloud to his friends. Tim asked the cloud, "Can I carry you to my friends?" The cloud said, "Yes, you can try." So, Tim tried to carry the cloud. He pushed and pulled, but the cloud was too big. Tim did not give up. He asked his friend, a strong wind, for help. The wind blew the cloud to Tim's friends. They were all happy and played with the cloud. Tim felt successful because he carried the cloud with help from his friend.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was deaf, but he was very smart. He loved to learn new things. One day, he went to a big building called a university. He wanted to see what was inside. Tim met a kind teacher at the university. The teacher said, "Hello, Tim! I can show you around." Tim couldn't hear her, but he could read her lips. He nodded and smiled. The teacher showed Tim many rooms with books and pictures. Tim liked to look and examine everything. In one room, there were kids playing with toys. Tim wanted to join them. He picked up a toy car and showed it to a little girl. The girl said, "I like your car!" Tim couldn't hear her, but he saw her smile. They played together and had a lot of fun. Tim loved his visit to the university and made new friends.

Once there was a man who was intelligent and kind. He always wanted to help those in need. Every day he prayed by the river for them. One day, as he was praying he heard a small voice. He was puzzled and looked around. There was a three-year-old child sitting by the river. The man asked her, “Why are you praying?” The child said, “I am praying that my family has enough to eat.” The man was moved by her simple faith and decided to help her. He gave her food and hugged her tightly. The man said, “Keep praying, sweetheart. God will help you.” The girl smiled and thanked him. As she walked away, the man looked at her with admiration for her strength and intelligence.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lisa. Lisa had a magic carpet in her room. This carpet was very special. It could fly! Lisa loved to fly on her carpet every day. But one day, the carpet was empty. Lisa could not find her carpet anywhere. Lisa was very sad. She looked for her carpet all day. She asked her mom and dad if they had seen her carpet. They said no. Lisa was worried that her carpet had escaped. She did not know what to do. Then, something unexpected happened. Lisa's little brother, Tim, came into her room. He was holding her magic carpet! Tim had taken it to play with his toy cars. Lisa was so happy to see her carpet. She told Tim that they could play with the carpet together. They both had a great time flying on the magic carpet, and Lisa learned to always look for her brother when something went missing.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a blue dress that she loved very much. Lily liked to play in the park with her friends. One day, Lily saw a big dog in the park. She wanted to play with the dog, but her mom warned her to be careful. "Lily, be careful with the big dog," her mom said. Lily listened to her mom and did not go near the dog. Later, Lily found a blue ball and played with her friends. They had so much fun together. Lily was happy that she listened to her mom's warning. And at the end of the day, she went home with her blue dress and a big smile on her face.

Once upon a time, there was a big, hairy dog named Spot. Spot liked to lie down in the sun and take naps. One day, while he was sleeping, a little girl named Sue found a pretty dress. Sue wanted to play dress-up with Spot. So, she put the dress on him. Spot woke up and saw the dress. He was very happy and wagged his tail. Sue and Spot played all day in the dress. They ran, jumped, and laughed. When the sun went down, Sue took the dress off Spot. They were both tired and happy. They lay down together and fell asleep.
