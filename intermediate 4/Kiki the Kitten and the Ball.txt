Category: Intermediate
Title: Kiki the Kitten and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little kitten named Kiki. Kiki was a normal kitten who liked to play and have fun. She had a big family who loved her very much. One day, Kiki was playing with her ball when it rolled under a heavy box. She tried to get the ball, but the box was too heavy. Kiki's big brother, Tom, saw her and came to support her. Together, they pushed the box and got the ball back. Kiki was so happy that Tom helped her. From that day on, they played together all the time. Kiki learned that it was good to have someone to support her. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Tim thought it would be interesting to see what he could find up there. As Tim climbed the tree, he found a small box. He thought, "I wonder what is inside?" Tim opened the box and saw a shiny key. He was very surprised. Tim climbed down the tree with the key. He saw a little door on the ground that he had never seen before. Tim used the key to open the door. Inside, he found a room full of toys! It was a secret playroom just for him.

Once upon a time, a big tiger lived in the forest. The tiger had a friend named Bird. They played together every day. One day, the tiger saw Bird fly high up in the sky. The tiger felt jealous. He wished he could fly too. "Hey Bird," said the tiger, "I want to fly like you. Can you teach me?" Bird smiled and said, "I can try, but you are a tiger. Tigers can't fly. But I can show you how to jump high and have fun." The tiger and Bird practiced jumping high. They had so much fun. After a while, the tiger felt tired. Bird said, "Let's rest under this big tree." The tiger agreed and they both sat down to rest. While they rested, the tiger thought about how lucky he was to have a friend like Bird. He was not jealous anymore. The tiger and Bird were happy to play together, even if they were different. They knew that being friends was more important than flying or jumping high.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Kate. She liked to go exploring in the woods. She liked to hunt for treasures. One sunny day Kate went out to the woods looking for treasures. She saw a mine with a big door in the side. She opened the door and went inside. As she walked inside it started to get cold and she began to shiver. Suddenly she heard a voice. It said, "What are you doing here?" Kate jumped and said, "I was just looking for treasures." The voice said, "This mine is honest. You can find some treasures here if you look." Kate was so excited. She began to look around and soon found lots of shiny things. They were all hers! Kate thanked the voice and said goodbye. She took her treasures and ran home with a big smile on her face. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big, scary lion. He liked to roar very loud. One day, he felt hot and not good. He wanted to know if he was sick. The lion went to his friend, the monkey. The monkey had a thermometer. The monkey put the thermometer in the lion's mouth. They waited to see if the lion was sick. After a short time, the thermometer showed that the lion was not sick. The lion was happy. He roared a big, happy roar. The scary lion and the monkey played together all day. They were best friends.

Once upon a time, in a small home, there was a messy boy named Tim. Tim never liked to clean up. His room was always full of toys, clothes, and dirt. His mom always told him to clean up, but Tim did not listen. One day, Tim's mom gave him a bar of soap. She said, "Tim, you must wash your hands to be clean." Tim took the soap, but he did not want to use it. He thought it was more fun to play with the soap than to wash his hands. Tim played with the soap all day. He slid it on the floor, threw it in the air, and even tried to draw with it. The soap made everything in the house messy. Tim's mom was not happy. She said, "Tim, you did not use the soap to wash your hands. Now the house is a big mess." At the end of the day, Tim's hands were still dirty. His mom did not provide him with more soap. Tim got sick because he did not wash his hands. He was sad and wished he had listened to his mom.
