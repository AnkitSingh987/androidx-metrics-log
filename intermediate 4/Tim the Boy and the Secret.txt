Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Secret

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to eat all kinds of food. One day, his mom gave him a new food called cauliflower. Tim looked at it and thought it was strange. His mom said, "I recommend you try it, Tim. It's good for you." Tim took a slow bite of the cauliflower. To his surprise, it tasted really good! He ate it all up and asked his mom for more. His mom was happy that Tim liked the new food. The next day, Tim went to play with his friend, Lily. He told her about the cauliflower and said, "I recommend you try it, Lily. It's so yummy!" Lily agreed to try it, but when she took a bite, something unexpected happened. The cauliflower turned into candy in her mouth! Tim and Lily laughed and enjoyed their magical cauliflower candy. And from that day on, they always shared their special secret with each other.

One day, a smelly dog named Spot went for a walk. He saw a big hill and thought, "I want to go up there!" As he climbed the hill, he found a small store. The store had many toys and treats for dogs. "Wow, look at all these fun things!" Spot said to the store owner, a nice lady. She smiled and said, "You can pick one toy and one treat, Spot." Spot was very happy and excited. He picked a ball and a bone. Spot went back down the hill with his new ball and bone. He played with the ball all day and ate the bone when he was hungry. At the end of the day, he went home and told his mom all about the store on the hill. His mom said, "That's great, Spot! Maybe we can go back to the store together next time." Spot wagged his tail and knew that he would have more fun adventures at the store on the hill.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the store with her mom. They wanted to buy a surprise for Amy's dad. It was his birthday. Amy was very excited to pick out the surprise. At the store, Amy saw a big, fierce dog. The dog had a number on its collar. The number was three. Amy was scared. The dog's owner said, "Don't worry, he's friendly!" Amy felt better. Amy and her mom found the perfect surprise for dad. It was a big cake with the number three on it, just like the dog's collar. When they gave the surprise to dad, he was very happy. "What a great surprise!" he said. And they all laughed and had a good time.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. Tom loved to play his guitar. Every day, he would sit under a big tree and sing happy songs. One day, Tom saw a little bird trying to balance on a branch. The bird was not very good at it. Tom wanted to help the bird, so he played a soft song on his guitar. The little bird listened to Tom's guitar and started to dance. It danced and danced, and soon it was able to balance on the branch. The bird was so happy, it sang along with Tom. Now, Tom and the little bird were friends. They played and sang together every day under the big tree. And they both lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a little boy named Jack. He lived with his mom and dad and he had a pet dog named Bob. Jack loved to feed Bob special treats. Every day he would get a treat out of the cupboard and give it to Bob. One day, Jack put on his special cap. It was very tight on his head, but he liked it so much he didn’t want to take it off. He then grabbed some treats to feed Bob. He knew that the treats were special and Bob would really like them. Jack went outside to the garden to feed Bob. He found Bob in the corner, eagerly wagging his tail. He fed Bob the treats and watched as Bob happily ate them. They both had a great time. When it was time to go back inside, Jack put on his tight cap again and said goodbye to Bob. He could not wait to play with Bob the next day.
