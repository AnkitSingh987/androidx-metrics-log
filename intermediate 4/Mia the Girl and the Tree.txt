Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to do yoga. Every day, she would stay in her room and do yoga. It was her favorite thing to do. One sunny day, Mia wanted to find the perfect place to do her yoga. She went outside and saw a big, green tree. "This is it!" she thought. "This is the perfect spot for my yoga!" Mia put her mat under the tree and started to do her yoga. As Mia did her yoga, a small, cute cat came by. The cat watched Mia and wanted to join her. So, the cat tried to do yoga too. They both stayed under the tree, doing yoga together. Mia and the cat became best friends, and they did yoga under the tree every day. They were very happy.

One day, a boy named Tim found an old basketball. It was big and round. Tim liked to play with it. He would throw it and it would bounce back to him. Tim and the basketball were always together. One sunny day, while Tim was playing with the basketball, something unexpected happened. The basketball started to talk! It said, "I am hungry. Can you feed me?" Tim was very surprised. He had never heard a basketball talk before. Tim thought for a moment and then he had an idea. He went into the kitchen and got some food. He put the food in a bowl and placed it next to the basketball. The basketball ate all the food! It was happy and said, "Thank you, Tim. I feel much better now." From that day on, Tim and the basketball were not just friends, they were best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to have fun with her toys. She had a toy box full of useful things. One day, Sue found a new toy. It was a box with numbers on it. Sue played with the numbers. She put them on the floor in order. One, two, three, four, and five. Sue's dog, Spot, came to play too. Spot liked the numbers. He picked up the number three with his mouth. Sue and Spot played with the numbers all day. They had a lot of fun. The numbers were very useful. They helped Sue learn to count. Sue and Spot were happy with their new toy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big, impressive tree. Many kids loved to play near the tree. One day, a little boy named Tim saw a cat stuck in the tree. He knew he needed to help the cat. Tim thought, "This is a big task for me, but I will try." Tim tried to climb the tree, but he was too small. He felt sad. Then, something unexpected happened. A little bird came to help. The bird talked to the cat and told it to jump onto its back. The cat did what the bird said. The bird flew down with the cat on its back. Tim was so happy. The cat was safe now. The moral of the story is that even when a task seems too big, you can still find help in unexpected places.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a little girl named Lily. She loved to keep her room clean. She would put away her toys and make her bed every day. Lily also liked to help her mom and dad with chores around the house. One day, there was a big earthquake in the town. Many houses shook and things fell down. Lily's house shook too. But because her room was clean, nothing broke. Lily's mom and dad were very happy that she had kept her room clean. After the earthquake, Lily went outside to see if her friends were okay. She saw that their houses were messy and some of their things had broken. Lily knew she could help. She started to help her friends clean up their rooms and put things away. Soon, all the rooms were clean and safe again. The other kids in the town saw how Lily's clean room had helped her during the earthquake. They decided to keep their rooms clean too. They learned that keeping their rooms clean was important for their safety. The moral of the story is that being clean and organized can help keep you safe and make it easier to help others in times of need.
