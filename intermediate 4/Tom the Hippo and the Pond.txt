Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Hippo and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a big hippo named Tom. Tom lived in a pond with many fish. He had a lot of friends, but he liked to play with his best friend, a little bird named Sue. One day, they played a game called "catch". Tom tried to catch Sue, but it was not easy. Sue was fast, and she could fly high in the sky. Tom was slow, but he did not give up. He wanted to win the game. Sue flew down to help Tom catch her. She let him get close and then flew away. Tom had fun trying to catch Sue, and they played all day. In the end, Tom caught Sue, and they both laughed. They were happy to play together, and they knew that their friendship was more important than winning the game.

One day, Jack and Jill went out to the beach. The sun was shining and the sand was warm between their toes. But what Jack and Jill didn't know is that a huge storm was coming. When they arrived, Jack and Jill started to gather shells along the shore. With each step, they found more and more shells, until there was a whole row of them. The storm was getting closer and closer. Suddenly, dark clouds started to cover the sky and strong winds started to blow. Jill gathered up the shells quickly and wanted to take them back to their home. But it was too late. When they looked back at the beach, there was a huge wave coming towards them. All of the shells were washed away. Jill was so sad. The wave was heavy and before Jack and Jill had a chance to run away, the wave crashed onto them. The tide was too strong and Jack and Jill were swept away. Jack and Jill were never seen again.

Jenny was a three year old who loved to explore different places. One day, she saw an airplane in the sky and asked her mom, "Where is it going?" Her mom replied, "It is going to a far away place". Jenny was very curious and asked, "Can I go?" Her mom smiled and said, "Not right now, but maybe someday". Jenny hoped to fly the plane one day. On another day, Jenny went to the library and saw many books. She asked her dad, "What are all those books about?" Her dad said, "They are books in different languages. People use language to communicate what they feel and think." Jenny was excited and asked, "Can I learn?" Her dad said, "Of course! You can learn any language you want." Jenny was nervous, but she hoped she could learn enough to communicate with others.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a sleepy cat named Tom. Tom loved to nap all day long. One day, Tom was taking a nap when he heard a loud sound. It was thunder! Tom woke up and looked out the window. The sky was dark, and the rain was falling. Tom saw something glow in the sky. It was a bright star! The star was glowing so much that it made Tom feel happy. Tom liked to watch the star and listen to the thunder. He thought it was fun. After a while, the thunder stopped, and the rain went away. The star was still glowing in the sky. Tom felt sleepy again, so he went back to his warm bed. He closed his eyes and dreamed of the glowing star and the thunder. Tom was a happy, sleepy cat.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a friendly man named Tom. Tom had many tools. He used his tools to fix things for his friends. One day, a little girl named Lily came to Tom's house. She needed help to fix her toy. Tom was happy to help. He lent Lily a small tool to fix her toy. Lily used the tool and fixed her toy. She was very happy. Tom was happy too. They played together and became good friends. From that day on, Tom always lent his tools to his friends, and they all loved him for being so friendly and helpful.
