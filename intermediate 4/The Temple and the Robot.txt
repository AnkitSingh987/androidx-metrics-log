Category: Intermediate
Title: The Temple and the Robot

Once upon a time, in a land far away, there was a big temple. Many people went to the temple to choose their favorite toy. One day, a boy named Tom went to the temple with his mom to choose a toy. Inside the temple, Tom saw a tough-looking toy. It was a big, strong robot. Tom said, "Mom, I want this robot. It looks tough and strong!" His mom said, "Okay, Tom. You can have the robot." But when Tom picked up the robot, something unexpected happened. The robot came to life and said, "Hello, Tom! I am your new friend. Let's play!" Tom and the robot played all day long, and they became best friends. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little chick named Charlie. Charlie lived on a farm with many other animals. One day, while playing with his friends, Charlie found a big egg. He had never seen an egg this big before. Charlie was very serious about taking care of the egg. Charlie went to his mom and requested, "Mom, can I keep the egg? I promise to take good care of it." His mom agreed and said, "Okay, Charlie, but you must be responsible and look after it." Charlie was very happy and took the egg to his little house. He made a soft bed for the egg and kept it warm. Every day, Charlie would talk to the egg and tell it stories. He was very excited to see what was inside. One sunny morning, the egg started to crack. Charlie watched as a big, beautiful bird came out of the egg. The bird was so happy to see Charlie and thanked him for taking care of her. Charlie and the bird became best friends and played together on the farm every day. From that day on, Charlie learned the importance of being serious and responsible. He was very proud of himself and his new friend. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play outside with his red ball. One sunny day, Tim went to the park with his mommy. At the park, Tim saw a big tree. He wanted to have a yummy sandwich under the tree. His mommy made a sandwich with cheese and ham. Tim was very happy. Then, a funny squirrel came and took Tim's sandwich. Tim was surprised! The squirrel ran up the tree with the sandwich. Tim laughed and his mommy made him another yummy sandwich. They had a fun day at the park.

Ben and Mia like to bounce on the big bed. They jump up and down and laugh. They count how many times they bounce. "One, two, three, four, five!" Ben says. "Six, seven, eight, nine, ten!" Mia says. They bounce higher and higher. Mom hears them from the kitchen. She is making lunch. She does not like the loud noise. She goes to the bedroom and opens the door. "Ben and Mia, stop bouncing on the bed!" she says. "You will break it or hurt yourself. Come and eat your lunch." Ben and Mia stop bouncing. They look at Mom. They are not happy. They like to bounce. They do not want to eat. "But Mom, bouncing is fun!" Ben says. "We are counting our numbers!" Mia says. Mom smiles. She knows they are learning and playing. But she also knows they need to eat and rest. "I know, bouncing is fun and counting is good. But you also need to eat your food and take a nap. How about this: after lunch and nap, you can bounce on the trampoline in the backyard. You can count your numbers there too. But no more bouncing on the bed, okay?" Ben and Mia think about it. They like the trampoline too. They nod. "Okay, Mom," they say. They get off the bed and follow Mom to the kitchen. They eat their lunch and take their nap. Then they go to the backyard and bounce on the trampoline. They count their numbers and have fun. Mom watches them and smiles. She is happy they are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max loved to protect his family. He would bark with his loud voice when he saw something strange. One day, Max saw a little cat in the yard. He barked and barked to protect his family. But the cat did not run away. The cat walked up to Max and said, "I am lost. Can you help me find my home?" Max was surprised. He did not know cats could talk! Max and the cat walked and walked. They found a tasty treat on the ground. They shared the treat and became friends. In the end, Max helped the cat find its home. The cat's family was so happy, they gave Max a big hug. Max learned that sometimes, the best way to protect his family was to make new friends.
