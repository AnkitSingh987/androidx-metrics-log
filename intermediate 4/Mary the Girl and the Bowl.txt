Category: Intermediate
Title: Mary the Girl and the Bowl

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mary. Mary was only three years old and loved to do one thing more than anything else in the world – eat! One day, Mary was walking through the kitchen when she saw something very yummy in a bowl. She couldn't help herself and reached out to take the food. Suddenly, a voice said, “Oh no! Mary, listen to me! That food is for someone else!" But it was too late. Mary had already taken the food and was about to eat it. The voice said again, “Now, you will have to stay in your room until you have thought about what you have done.” And that is what the little girl had to do until the end of the day. Instead of eating yummy food, she had to stay in her room and think about her bad behaviour.

Once upon a time, there was a little town. In this town, there was a shop. The shop was very boring. It had old things like an ashtray. No one liked to go there. One day, a girl named Lily went into the shop. She saw the ashtray and thought it was not boring. She wanted to save it from the boring shop. So, she took it home. Lily cleaned the ashtray and put it in her room. Her friends saw it and thought it was nice. They all went to the shop and found more things to save. Soon, the shop was not boring anymore. The moral of the story is that even things that seem boring can be special if we give them a chance.

Once upon a time, there was a little seal named Sam. Sam loved to play in the water all day. He was very good at swimming and jumping. One day, Sam saw a new friend in the water. It was a big fish. Sam swam over to the fish and said, "Hello, do you want to play with me?" The big fish looked at Sam and did not recognize him. The big fish was impatient and said, "I don't know you. I have to go." Sam was sad and swam back to his family. He told his mom and dad about the big fish. They told him, "Sometimes, friends need time to get to know you. Be patient and try again later." The next day, Sam swam back to the big fish. This time, the big fish recognized him and said, "Hello, Sam. I am sorry I was impatient yesterday. Let's play together!" Sam and the big fish played all day and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big, soft pillow. It was in a room with a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play catch with his pillow. He would throw it up high and then try to catch it before it fell. One day, Tim threw the pillow up, but it did not come back down. He looked all around, but he could not find it. Tim was sad and felt a bitter taste in his mouth. He missed his pillow very much. Then, he heard a noise from the window. He saw a big bird with his pillow in its mouth. The bird tried to fly away with it, but Tim was quick. He ran and jumped to catch the pillow from the bird. As Tim caught the pillow, the bird spoke. It said, "I am sorry, Tim. I thought your pillow was food for my babies." Tim was surprised that the bird could talk, but he was not mad. He told the bird, "It's okay. We can share my pillow to keep your babies warm." So, Tim and the bird became friends, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a distant land, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to walk on the path by his house. He liked to see the trees, flowers, and animals. One sunny day, Tim saw a big cat on the path. The cat was very pretty. The cat liked to tease Tim. It would hide behind a tree and then jump out to surprise him. Tim laughed and played with the cat. As they played, they walked further down the path. They saw many things and had a lot of fun. At the end of the day, Tim and the cat went back to their homes. They were happy they had a fun day together.
