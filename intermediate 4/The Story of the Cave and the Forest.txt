Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Cave and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a girl. She liked to explore. One day, she decided to go look for something special. She went out and explored and explored. She went in the forest and look down inside a cave. Inside she found something special - a silver thing! She was very happy because she found it. But then, something went wrong. She was being careless and she left the silver thing inside the cave. When she came back, it was missing. She was sad. She started to look and look and look, but she couldn't find it. She asked the birds and the trees, but nobody knew where it was. She looked for a long time, until finally, she found it! The silver thing was hidden in a tree! She was so happy. She carefully took it and was happy that she found it. She was more careful with the silver thing this time. She brought it home and was very happy.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing near a house. The house had a big chimney. The cat was very rude. The dog did not like it when the cat was rude. The cat said, "I can hang on the chimney." The dog said, "No, you can't." The cat tried to hang on the chimney, but it was too hard. The dog laughed at the cat. The cat got mad and said, "You are rude, too!" The dog said, "I am sorry." They both said sorry to each other. Then, they played together and had fun.

Once upon a time, there was an ancient car. The car was very old and slow. It did not have much fuel. The car wanted to go far away. One day, the car met a big truck. The truck had a lot of fuel. The car asked the truck, "Can I have some fuel, please?" The truck said, "Yes, you can have some of my fuel." The car was happy. It got the fuel from the truck. Now it could go far away. The ancient car went on a long trip and saw many fun things. And they both became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big girl named Lily. She had an enormous toy box filled with many toys. One day, she wanted to play "marriage" with her dolls. She took out her two favorite dolls, Tom and Sue, and started to play. As Lily played, her little brother, Ben, came into the room. Ben wanted to play with the dolls too. But Lily didn't want to let him play. She thought he was too little and would break her dolls. This made Ben very sad and he started to cry. Ben went to their mom and told her what happened. Mom said they should share the toys. Lily didn't want to, but she let Ben play with her dolls. They played "marriage" together, but they started to fight over who would hold the dolls. The fight got bigger and bigger until they broke both dolls, Tom and Sue. Lily and Ben were both very sad. They knew they should have shared and played nicely together. Now, their favorite dolls were broken, and the marriage game was over. It was a bad ending for both of them, and they wished they had been nicer to each other.

Once upon a time, there was a bee named Buzz. Buzz lived in a hive with his bee friends. The hive was their home, and they were glad to live there. Buzz liked to make honey with his friends. They all worked together in the hive. One day, a new bee named Buzzy came to the hive. Buzz and his friends were happy to meet Buzzy. They said, "Welcome, Buzzy!" They were glad to have a new friend. Buzzy was also happy to be in the hive with Buzz and his friends. Buzz, Buzzy, and their friends made honey together in the hive. They played and laughed. They were all glad to be friends and to live in the hive. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was an enormous bell. This bell would ring every day to tell the people when to wake up and when to go to bed. The people loved the bell and listened to it every day. One day, a little boy named Tim went to see the enormous bell. He wanted to be the one to ring it. He climbed up the tall tower where the bell was. When he got to the top, he found a big rope. Tim knew that this rope made the bell ring. Tim waited for the right time to ring the bell. He knew it was almost time for the people to wake up. He held onto the rope tightly and pulled it down. The enormous bell rang loudly, and the people in the village woke up with a smile. They knew it was time to start their day. Tim felt happy and proud that he could help his village.
