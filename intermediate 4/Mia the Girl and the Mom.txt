Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Mom

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was a very persistent girl who loved to help her mom. One day, Mia saw her mom washing the table. She wanted to help too. Mia asked her mom, "Can I wash the table too?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, you can help me." Mia was very happy. She got a cloth and started to wash the table. She washed and washed, making sure the table was very clean. Mia's mom was very proud of her persistent little girl. She said, "Good job, Mia! The table is so clean now!" Mia smiled and felt very happy that she could help her mom. From that day on, Mia always helped her mom wash the table.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a small girl named Lily. Lily loved to spin around and around in her room. She would spin so fast that she would get dizzy and fall on her soft bed, laughing. One day, Lily found a small prune in the kitchen. She thought it was a funny-looking fruit. She took the prune to her room and decided to play with it. She put the prune on the floor and tried to spin it like a top. The prune started to spin very fast. It spun so fast that it made a little whirlwind in Lily's room. Lily was very happy and laughed as she watched the prune spin. She clapped her hands and danced around the spinning prune. After a while, the prune stopped spinning. Lily picked it up and hugged it. She knew it was a special prune. From that day on, Lily and the spinning prune were the best of friends. They played together every day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a shy cat named Fluffy. Fluffy did not like to play with other cats because he was too shy. He would hide behind a big tree when other cats came to play. One day, he decided he wanted to become brave and make friends. Fluffy walked to a new place where many cats were playing. He saw a small cat named Tiny playing with a ball. Fluffy slowly went to Tiny and asked, "Can I play with you?" Tiny smiled and said, "Yes, let's play together!" As they played, Fluffy became less shy. He made many friends in the new place. Fluffy was happy that he became brave and found new friends to play with. From that day on, Fluffy was not shy anymore and loved to play with his friends.

One day, a little bee went out to look for flowers. The sun was hot and the day was dry. The bee saw a big tree and thought it might find some flowers there. The bee flew around the tree and saw a big hole. The bee went in to use the hole to rest. But inside the hole, the bee found a big, dry leaf. The bee thought it was a flower, so it tried to get some food from it. But nothing came out. Just then, the dry leaf started to move! The bee was scared. The leaf was not a flower, it was a big bug! The bug was nice and said, "I can help you find real flowers." The bee and the bug became friends and found lots of flowers together. The bee was happy and thanked the bug for being a good friend.

Once upon a time, in a big universe, there was a clear sky. The bright sun was shining, and a little girl named Lucy went outside to play. She loved to run and jump in the warm, soft grass. One day, while Lucy was playing, she saw a big, shiny rock. She ran to it and touched it. Suddenly, she started to slip, and she fell into a hole under the rock. Lucy was scared, but she saw a light at the end of the hole. Lucy walked towards the light and found herself in a new place. The sky was still clear, but the sun was different. Instead of one sun, there were two suns in this universe! Lucy was surprised and excited. She knew she had found a special place. In the end, she found her way back home and told her friends about the two suns. They all wanted to see them too.
