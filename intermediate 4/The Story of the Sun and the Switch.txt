Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Sun and the Switch

Once upon a time, there was a big room with a special switch on the wall. This switch could make a fake sun come out and play. A little boy named Tim and a little girl named Sue both wanted to push the switch at the same time. They both loved the fake sun and wanted to see it shine. Tim and Sue started to fight over who would push the switch first. They both pulled and pushed each other, trying to get to the switch. They both wanted to be the one to make the fake sun come out. They were not happy and could not agree. Then, Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's push the switch together." Sue liked this idea, and they both walked up to the switch. They put their hands on the switch and pushed it at the same time. The fake sun came out, and they were both happy. They played together under the fake sun, and they learned that sharing was more fun than fighting.

One day, Tim had an ugly mixer. The mixer was big and green. Tim wanted to make a cake. He put flour, eggs, and milk in a bowl. The ugly mixer mixed it all up. Tim was happy. Tim's little sister, Sue, wanted to help too. She saw a piece of paper on the table. Sue tried to tear the paper. It was hard, but she did it. Sue was proud. Tim and Sue put the cake in the oven. They waited and waited. When the cake was done, they took it out. It was a big, yummy cake. Tim and Sue shared the cake with their friends. Everyone was happy.

Once upon a time there was a young girl named Sarah. She was very wealthy and she lived in a big room all by herself. Every night, Sarah would go to sleep in the room. One night, Sarah heard a noise coming from outside her door. She got out of bed and opened her door. Standing in the doorway was a big bear! The bear said, "I want your money. Give me all your wealth." But Sarah was very brave and she said, "No. I won't give you my money." The bear was very angry and said, "If you don't give me your money, I will eat you!" Sarah ran to her room, but the bear followed her. In the end, the bear ate Sarah and she never woke up again.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted little girl called Clara. Everywhere she went, she knew just what to do. One day, Clara saw a cupboard in the corner of a room. She couldn't help but peak inside. When she opened up the cupboard, a voice from inside spoke to her. "Hello little girl." It said. "I am in need of help. Could you kindly supply me with something?" Clara thought for a moment, and then replied "Yes, of course." She reached into her pocket and pulled out a small, silver key. The voice inside thanked her kindly, and said "This is the key to my freedom. Thank you, little one." The next day, Clara returned to the cupboard. To her surprise, the door had been left wide open and the voice was gone. All that was left was a small slip of paper. On it, it said: "My escape was made possible thanks to your kindness. You have done a great deed today, but be warned: not everyone is as gifted as you. Maybe it would be best not to show your generosity so openly in the future." Clara was horrified. She knew that her kind act of kindness had led to the escape of a wanted criminal. From that day on, she was extra cautious with the help she supplied.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful little boy named Tim. He loved to play outside his home with his red ball. Every day, he would push the ball around the yard and laugh as it rolled away. One sunny day, Tim was playing with his ball and he pushed it too hard. The ball rolled far away from his home and into the woods. Tim was sad and wanted his ball back, so he went into the woods to find it. Tim walked and walked until he found his red ball near a big tree. He was so happy! He picked up the ball and started to go back home. He pushed the ball all the way back to his yard, and played with it until the sun went down. Tim felt cheerful again, and he knew he would always be careful when he pushed his ball.
