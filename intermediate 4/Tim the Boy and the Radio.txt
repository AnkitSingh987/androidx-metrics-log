Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Radio

One day, a little boy named Tim found a radio. It was not easy to use, but Tim wanted to try. He turned the radio on and music started to play. Tim was very happy and started to dance. Then, something funny happened. A voice came from the radio. It said, "Hello, Tim! I am a talking radio!" Tim was surprised and said, "Hello, radio! I did not know you can talk!" The radio and Tim became good friends. They played together and talked every day. Tim was glad he found the radio. And the radio was happy too, because it now had a friend.

Once upon a time, in a big sea, there lived an oyster. The oyster had many friends. They liked to play and swim all day. But one day, the oyster felt upset. The oyster's friends wanted to make the oyster feel better. They tried to make funny faces and tell funny jokes. But the oyster was still upset. Then, a wise old fish came to help. The fish told the oyster, "To feel better, you must share your feelings with your friends." The oyster talked to its friends about why it was upset. The friends listened and helped the oyster feel better. The moral of the story is: when you are upset, talk to your friends and they will help you feel better.

One day, a little girl named Mia was playing in the park. She saw a big tree and wanted to settle under it. So, Mia sat down under the tree to rest. She felt happy and safe there. Mia had a piece of gum in her pocket. She took it out and started to chew it. She blew a big bubble, but it popped on her face. Mia felt frustrated. She tried again and again, but the bubble kept popping. A little boy named Tim saw Mia and asked, "Why are you frustrated?" Mia said, "I can't blow a big bubble with my gum." Tim smiled and said, "I can show you how." Mia and Tim practiced together, and soon Mia could blow big bubbles too. They both laughed and played under the tree, happy with their gum.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty flower. The flower liked to bloom. It bloomed every day at sunset. The sky was orange and pink at sunset. The flower thought it was interesting. One day, a little boy saw the flower. The boy said, "Hi, flower! You are so pretty. I like to watch you bloom at sunset." The flower was happy. The boy came to see the flower every day. The boy and the flower became good friends. They loved to watch the sunset together. The boy thought the flower was interesting. The flower thought the boy was interesting too. They lived happily ever after.

Tim and Kim were twins who liked to play with toys. One day, they found a big boat in the garage. It was red and shiny and had a sail and a wheel. They wanted to take it to the pond and make it go on the water. "Let's go, let's go!" said Tim, who was very excited. He grabbed the boat and ran to the door. "Wait, wait!" said Kim, who was more careful. She saw a note on the boat that said: "This boat is very old and fragile. Do not use it without asking Dad first. He will recommend how to handle it safely." Tim did not listen to Kim. He thought he knew how to play with the boat. He opened the door and ran outside. Kim followed him, trying to stop him. They reached the pond and Tim put the boat on the water. He pushed it with his hand and made it go fast. He was very cheerful and laughed. But then, a strong wind came and blew the sail. The boat tipped over and sank into the water. Tim was very sad and cried. Kim felt sorry for Tim. She hugged him and said: "I'm sorry, Tim. I know you wanted to have fun. But you should have listened to the note. Dad knows best. He would have recommended how to play with the boat without breaking it. Maybe he can fix it for us." Tim nodded and said: "You're right, Kim. I was wrong. I should have listened to you and Dad. I'm sorry I ruined the boat. I hope Dad can fix it. I will ask him first next time." They went back to the garage and waited for Dad to come home. They told him what happened and said they were sorry. Dad was not angry. He was glad they were honest and learned their lesson. He said he would try to fix the boat and teach them how to use it properly. He also gave them a hug and said he loved them. The moral of the story is: Listen to your parents and follow their advice. They want you to be safe and happy.
