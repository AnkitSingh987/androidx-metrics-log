Category: Intermediate
Title: Jamie and the Garden

Jamie was a brilliant three-year-old. One day, she was playing with her favorite pen in her garden. She spun it between her fingers as she sat down on the grass. "Hey, pen," she said. "Let's go for a spin!" The pen was so excited that Jamie was taking it for a spin. It smiled and held on tight as Jamie went round and round in circles. As she spun, Jamie could see the beautiful flowers and the big trees in her garden. She spun faster and faster until her head was spinning too. She laughed and forgot all her troubles. Just then, Jamie's mom came out of the house and saw Jamie spinning with the pen. She laughed and said, "Jamie, that was brilliant! I'm so glad you're having fun." Jamie smiled and said, "I love spinning pen! It's so much fun!" Jamie's mom watched her spin and play with the pen until she was tired. Then they both said goodbye to the pen and went inside for a snack.

Once upon a time, there was a little ball named Rolly. Rolly loved to roll all day in the sand. One day, Rolly met a big, fearful dog named Barky. Barky was scared of the sand because he thought it was dirty. Rolly said, "Hi Barky! Why are you scared of the sand? It's fun to roll in!" Barky looked at Rolly and said, "I don't want to get dirty. I am a clean dog." Rolly wanted to help Barky have fun. So, Rolly rolled in the sand and showed Barky how fun it was. Barky saw Rolly having fun and decided to try it too. They both rolled in the sand and had a great time. In the end, Barky wasn't fearful of the sand anymore, and they became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Sam. He was a troubled youth. Sam did not have many friends. He wanted to play with other kids, but they did not want to play with him. This made Sam very sad. One day, Sam was walking in the park. He saw a nice girl named Lily. Lily was playing with a ball. Sam wanted to play with her. He called out to her, "Hi! Can I play with you?" Lily looked at him and said, "Yes, come play with me!" Sam was very happy. Sam and Lily played together all day. They had so much fun. Sam was not troubled anymore. He had a new friend in Lily. From that day on, Sam and Lily played together every day. They became best friends and were never sad again.

One day, a big crocodile went to a fancy party. He wore a nice hat and a pretty bow tie. All his friends were there, and they played games and ate yummy food. The crocodile was very happy. But then, the crocodile started to cry. His friends asked him, "Why are you crying?" The crocodile said, "I lost my pretty bow tie." His friends wanted to help him, so they all looked for the bow tie together. After a while, one of the friends found the pretty bow tie. The crocodile was so happy, he stopped crying. He said, "Thank you, friends, for helping me find my bow tie." The friends were happy too. They learned that when we help each other, we can solve problems and be happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a big grill. It was very expensive. It lived in a house with a nice family. The family loved to cook yummy food on the grill. One day, the grill wanted to try something new. It wanted to cook a big, tasty cake. The grill asked the family if they could help. The family said, "Yes, we will try to make a cake with you." The family and the grill tried very hard to make the cake. They mixed and mixed the cake stuff. Then, they put the cake on the grill. The grill was very hot, and it cooked the cake. The family was so happy. They ate the cake, and it was very good. The grill was happy too, because it tried something new with the family.
