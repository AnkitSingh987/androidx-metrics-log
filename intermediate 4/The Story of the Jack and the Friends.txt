Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Jack and the Friends

Once upon a time, in a land far away, there was a big, loud temple. Many people went there to pray and play. One day, a boy named Jack went to the temple with his friends. Jack liked to tease his friends and make them feel bad. At the temple, Jack and his friends played a game. Jack was very loud and teased his friends a lot. His friends did not like it when Jack teased them. They felt sad and asked Jack to stop. But Jack did not listen to his friends. The loud noise made the temple angry. The temple did not like Jack's teasing and loud voice. So, the temple started to shake and fall down. Jack and his friends tried to run away, but they could not. In the end, the temple fell on Jack and his friends. They were all very sad and scared.

Anna and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to swing, slide and run. But today the sky was gloomy. It was dark and gray. They heard a loud noise. It was thunder. "Let's go home," Ben said. "I don't like thunder. It is scary." "OK," Anna said. "But first, let's say goodbye to the ducks. They are our friends." They walked to the pond. They saw the ducks swimming and quacking. They threw some bread crumbs to them. "Goodbye, ducks," Anna said. "We will come back soon." "Goodbye, ducks," Ben said. "Thank you for being nice to us." The ducks came closer to the shore. They nodded their heads. They made a symbol with their wings. It was a heart. "Look, Ben," Anna said. "The ducks are saying they love us. They are welcoming us to their pond." "That is so sweet," Ben said. "I love them too. They are not afraid of thunder. They are brave." Anna and Ben smiled. They felt happy. The ducks made them forget the gloomy sky. They waved to the ducks and ran to their home. They hoped the sun would come out tomorrow. They wanted to play with the ducks again.

Once upon a time there was a shy girl named Mary. Mary was walking around the store when she saw something that made her frown: the ice-cream stand. Everyday the ice-cream stand was there, but today something didn't seem quite right. So Mary asked the man behind the counter, "Why do you look so sad?" The man replied, "I'm sad because this is the last day for this stand. I'm moving away tomorrow." Mary was sad to hear this. She wanted to make the man smile, so she said, "Can I have one last ice-cream?" The man smiled and said, "Of course, one last ice-cream on the house." Mary smiled, and the man finally said, "Thank you. You made me forget my sadness and remember the happy moments I've had here". Mary was happy to see the man smile. She waved goodbye as she started to walk away, already looking forward to her tasty treat.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lila. She was happy and excited because it was festival! Every year, festival happened and the whole village would come together. Lila loved festival because there was so much to see and do. Everywhere, people were smiling and laughing. Lila wanted to take part in the festival, so she decided to kneel. As she knelt, she looked around. Everyone was busy, having fun and being safe. Seeing this made Lila happy, so she stayed there and kept knelling. The festival was a special day and Lila was happy to be part of it. When the festival was over, she walked back home feeling happy and safe. And she couldn't wait for the next festival!

One day, a big lion named Leo went for a walk. He had a long leash tied to his neck. The sun was shining and the day was light and happy. Leo liked to walk and see his friends in the park. In the park, Leo saw his friend, a small dog named Max. Max was happy to see Leo. Max said, "Hi, Leo! Let's play together!" Leo agreed and they started to play. They ran, jumped, and had lots of fun. After playing, Leo and Max were tired. Leo looked at Max and said, "Max, do you want to hear me roar?" Max was excited and said, "Yes, please!" So Leo took a deep breath and roared very loud. Max and all the other animals in the park were amazed. They clapped and cheered for Leo. After that, Leo and Max walked back home, feeling happy and proud.
