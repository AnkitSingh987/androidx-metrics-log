Category: Intermediate
Title: The Race and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a big race in the park. Many animals joined the race. There was a fast rabbit, a slow turtle, and a foolish dog. The foolish dog had very big ears. He liked to show off and make others laugh. He thought he could win the race just by being funny. So, the dog did not run fast like the rabbit or slow like the turtle. Instead, he jumped around, making his big ears flap in the wind. In the end, the rabbit won the race because he was very fast. The turtle came in second place because he never stopped trying. The foolish dog did not win, but he made everyone laugh with his big ears. And that day, all the animals had fun in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside and wonder about the world. One sunny day, she saw a big tree with a delicious red jelly hanging from it. Lily had never seen a jelly tree before. Lily called her friend, Tom, and said, "Tom, come see the jelly tree!" Tom ran over and looked at the tree. "Wow!" he said. "Can we eat the jelly?" Lily and Tom picked the red jelly from the tree. They took it to Lily's mom, who said, "This jelly looks delicious! Let's share it with our friends." So, Lily, Tom, and their friends all enjoyed the delicious red jelly from the jelly tree. They laughed and played, and had a great time together. From that day on, Lily and Tom always wondered what other amazing things they might find in their world.

Once upon a time, in a small home, there was a humble girl named Amy. Amy loved to eat jelly. One day, Amy's mom made a big jar of jelly and put it on the top shelf. Amy saw the jar and wanted to eat some jelly. Amy was too short to reach the jar on the top shelf. She tried to jump, but she couldn't reach it. She tried to climb, but she fell down. Amy was sad. She thought she would never get the jelly. But she didn't give up. She remembered that her dad had a big ladder in the garage. Amy went to the garage and found the big ladder. She carried the ladder to the kitchen and set it up. She climbed the ladder and finally reached the jar of jelly. She was so happy! Amy got the jelly and shared it with her family. In the end, Amy learned that if you try hard and never give up, you can do anything. And that's how the humble girl named Amy got her favorite jelly.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a proud boy who liked to help his mom. One day, his mom asked him to step on a towel and help her clean the house. Tim said, "Yes, Mom! I will step on the towel and help you." He was very excited to help his mom. Tim stepped on the towel and began to slide around the floor. He went from one room to another, cleaning the floor with the towel under his feet. As Tim was sliding around, he saw a big glass on the table. He thought it would be fun to slide over and touch it. But when he did, the glass fell and broke into many pieces. Tim was scared and sad. He knew he had made a big mess and his mom would not be happy. His mom came into the room and saw the broken glass. She said, "Oh no, Tim! You should have been more careful. Now we have a big mess to clean up." Tim felt very bad and knew he had not been a good helper. From that day on, Tim learned to be more careful when helping his mom. He never stepped on a towel to clean the floor again, and he always made sure to stay away from things that could break.

Once upon a time, there was a fat pig named Paul. He had four feet, but was too lazy to use them. One day, Paul had an idea to trick his friends. So he lied down on the ground and shut his eyes. His friends the sheep and cow came to check on him. The cow said, "Paul, why are you lying on the ground?" Paul replied, "I can't walk. My feet are too fat." The sheep said, "Don't worry, Paul. I can help you. I'll carry you!" The sheep then carried Paul on her back until he found a comfortable spot near the pond. Paul thanked the sheep and hopped off her back. He thanked her for helping him and they all were happy. The End.
