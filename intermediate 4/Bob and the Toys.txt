Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob and the Toys

Bob was a very popular boy in his neighbourhood. He had lots of friends who loved to play with him all day long. One day, Bob and two of his friends were playing with toys in the park. Suddenly, Bob decided to poke one of his friends with a stick. His friend felt quite scared and ran away. Bob's other friend, Jessie, thought it wasn’t very nice to poke other people. She decided to poke Bob back. She ran towards him and lightly poked his leg. Bob felt surprised. Jessie ran away and laughed. Bob chased after her, but he couldn’t catch up. Everyone thought it was very funny. Bob and Jessie became even more popular after that day.

Sara and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big bush with many blueberries. They are hungry and want to eat some. They go to the bush and pick some blueberries. They are sweet and juicy. Sara and Ben are happy. They eat more and more blueberries. They do not see the sign that says "Do not pick the blueberries. They belong to the farmer." The farmer comes to the park. He sees Sara and Ben eating his blueberries. He is angry and sad. He says "Why are you eating my blueberries? I work hard to grow them. I need them to sell and make money. You are stealing from me." Sara and Ben are scared and sorry. They say "We did not know. We are hungry and like blueberries. We are sorry. Please do not be mad." The farmer says "You should not take what is not yours. You should ask before you eat. You should be honest and clear. That is the right thing to do. Do you understand?" Sara and Ben nod and say "Yes, we understand. We are sorry. We will not do it again. We will be honest and clear. Can we be your friends?" The farmer says "Yes, you can be my friends. But you have to help me pick the blueberries and take them to the market. And you can have some as a treat. But only some. Do you agree?" Sara and Ben say "Yes, we agree. We will help you. And we will share the blueberries. Thank you for being kind." The farmer smiles and says "You are welcome. Come, let us go to the market. And remember, be honest and clear. That is the best way to be."

Once upon a time, there was a big blackboard in a small room. The blackboard was very old and mighty. It was so big that all the kids could draw on it at the same time. One day, the sun was shining very bright. It was so hot that the blackboard started to feel like it was going to melt. The kids were worried and wanted to help the blackboard. So, the kids found a big fan and turned it on. The fan blew cool air on the blackboard, and it did not melt. The blackboard was happy and the kids were happy too. They all played and drew on the blackboard all day long.

Harry was feeling restless. He was riding on his tricycle and he didn't feel like stopping. He was going faster and faster, but he knew he shouldn't go too fast. Suddenly, he heard his mama calling from the door. "Harry," she said, "It's time for dinner!" Harry started to slow down his tricycle and he turned the wheel so he could go to the door. He could see his mama smiling as he parked his tricycle. "Come here, my darling," she said. Harry scooted closer to his mama and she pulled him into a big hug. She squeezed him tight and kissed his cheeks. "Do you like hugs, little one?" She asked. Harry smiled and nodded. He loved when his mama cuddled him close. He felt safe and loved when she gave him hugs. "That's good," Mama said, with a smile. "Let's go eat dinner now, OK?" Harry said goodbye to his tricycle and followed his mama inside. He knew that even though he felt restless, his mama's hugs could always make him feel better.
