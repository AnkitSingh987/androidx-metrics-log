Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was very adventurous. One day, Lucy went on a walk with her mom. They saw many fun things like trees, birds, and a big, blue sky. On their walk, they found a door with a shiny knob. Lucy wanted to learn how to open it. Her mom decided to teach her. She said, "Lucy, hold the knob and turn it." Lucy did just that, and the door opened. Behind the door, they found a beautiful garden. There were flowers, butterflies, and a little pond. Lucy and her mom had a great time playing in the garden. They laughed and played until the sun went down. Then they went back home, happy and tired from their adventurous day.

Tom was a little mouse who lived in a big field of wheat. He liked to eat the wheat and play with his friends. He was happy and safe. One day, he saw a big bird flying over the field. The bird was black and red and had a long tail. Tom thought the bird was beautiful and powerful. He wanted to see it up close. He decided to follow the bird and see where it went. He ran out of the wheat and into the open. He did not listen to his friends who told him to stay in the wheat. He did not see the danger. The bird saw Tom and swooped down. It opened its sharp beak and grabbed Tom in its claws. Tom was scared and hurt. He wished he had stayed in the wheat with his friends. The bird flew away with Tom and ate him. Tom was gone. His friends were sad and missed him. The moral of the story is: Do not follow strangers who look powerful. They might be dangerous and hurt you. Stay with your friends who care for you and protect you.

Tom and Lily are twins. They like to play in the living room. There are many toys and books and magazines there. Sometimes they share, sometimes they fight. One day, Tom finds a magazine with big pictures of cars and trucks. He likes cars and trucks very much. He sits on the floor and looks at the magazine. He makes noises like vroom and beep. Lily sees the magazine and wants to look at it too. She likes cars and trucks as well. She walks over to Tom and says, "Tom, can I see the magazine?" Tom says, "No, Lily, this is my magazine. Go away." Lily says, "No, Tom, this is our magazine. We can share." Tom says, "No, Lily, this is mine. You have your own toys." Lily gets angry and tries to grab the magazine from Tom. Tom holds it tight and pushes Lily away. Lily falls on the floor and cries. Mom hears the noise and comes to the living room. She sees Tom and Lily fighting over the magazine. She is not happy. She says, "Tom, Lily, what are you doing? Why are you fighting?" Tom says, "Mom, Lily wants my magazine. She can't have it." Lily says, "Mom, Tom won't share the magazine. He is mean." Mom says, "Tom, Lily, this is not your magazine. This is Dad's magazine. He left it here by mistake. You can't play with it. It is not for kids." Tom and Lily are surprised. They look at the magazine and see small words and numbers that they don't understand. They say, "Oh." Mom says, "Come on, Tom, Lily, let's put the magazine back on the table. Then you can choose another toy or book to play with. But you have to share and be nice to each other. OK?" Tom and Lily say, "OK, Mom." They stand up and put the magazine back on the table. Then they hug each other and say, "Sorry, Tom. Sorry, Lily." Mom smiles and says, "That's better. Now, what do you want to play with?" Tom and Lily look around and see a big box of blocks. They say, "Blocks!" Mom says, "OK, blocks. But remember, share and be nice." Tom and Lily say, "Yes, Mom." They run to the box of blocks and start to build a big tower. They are happy and have fun. The end.
