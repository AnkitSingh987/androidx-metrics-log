Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Magic

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim went to the mall with his mom. At the mall, Tim saw a magic man. The magic man could make things disappear. Tim thought that was fun. One day, Tim found a magic wand. He remembered the magic man at the mall. Tim wanted to make things disappear too. But Tim was a little selfish. He did not want to share the wand with his friends. Tim waved the magic wand at his toys. The toys disappeared! Tim was happy. But soon, he felt lonely. He missed playing with his friends and sharing his toys. Tim learned that being selfish was not fun. Tim wished for his toys to come back. He waved the magic wand, and the toys appeared again. Tim was happy. He decided to share the magic wand with his friends. They all had fun making things disappear and appear again. Tim was not selfish anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a big log. The log was empty inside. It was in the woods. Many animals lived near the log. One day, a man came to repair the log. He wanted to make it a home for the animals. The animals were happy. They liked the man. Then, something unexpected happened. The man found a hidden door in the log. He opened the door. Inside the log, there was a big, beautiful room. The room was not empty. It was full of food and toys for the animals. The animals were very happy. They lived in the log with the man. They all became good friends.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to a festival with her mom and dad. There were many fun things to do. Mia saw a big, rough dog. She wanted to pet it. "Can I pet the dog, mom?" Mia asked. Her mom said, "Yes, but be gentle." Mia blinked her eyes and went to pet the dog. The dog was very nice and licked her hand. Just then, something unexpected happened. The rough dog started to talk! "Thank you for petting me," said the dog. Mia was very surprised. She blinked her eyes again and smiled. The dog and Mia became friends and played together at the festival.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play in the dirt. One sunny day, Spot went outside to play with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. Tim and Spot played in the dirt together. Tim used his finger to dig a small hole. Then, Spot helped Tim bury a shiny stone in the hole. They covered the hole with dirt and patted it down with their hands and paws. After they buried the shiny stone, Tim and Spot played some more. They ran around the yard, laughing and barking. They were very happy. When it was time to go inside, Tim gave Spot a big hug. They were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a new little cat. Her name was Lucy. Lucy was very quiet. She loved to play and find new places to hide. One day, Lucy found a big box. She thought it was a great place to hide. She jumped in the box and it was very quiet inside. Lucy liked the quiet box a lot. Lucy's friend, Ben the dog, was looking for her. He wanted to surprise Lucy. Ben found the box and opened it. "Surprise!" he said. Lucy was happy to see Ben. They played together all day long.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a wealthy man named Mr. Brown. He had a big house and many toys. One day, he wanted to prepare a big meal for his friends. So, he went to the grocery store to buy food. At the grocery store, Mr. Brown met a little girl named Lily. She was looking for food too. Mr. Brown asked her, "What are you buying today, Lily?" Lily said, "I need bread and milk for my family. We don't have much money." Mr. Brown felt sad for Lily. He wanted to help her. So, he bought all the food she needed and gave it to her. Lily was very happy and thanked Mr. Brown. They both went home with their groceries. That night, Mr. Brown prepared a big meal for his friends. They all enjoyed the food and had a great time. Mr. Brown was happy because he helped Lily and had fun with his friends. From that day on, Mr. Brown always helped others who needed it. And everyone in the town loved him.
