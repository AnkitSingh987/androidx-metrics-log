Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Mia and the Hill

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they wander away from the swings and slides. They see a big hill with many trees and rocks. They want to climb the hill and see what is on the other side. They hold hands and walk up the hill. They see many birds and flowers and bugs. They hear a stream and a wind. They are happy and curious. They reach the top of the hill and look down. They see a big hole in the ground. It is dark and scary. "What is that?" Tom asks. "I don't know. Maybe it is a cave. Do you want to go inside?" Mia asks. Tom shakes his head. He is afraid. He sees something shiny in the hole. It is a skull. It is yellow and old and has no eyes or nose or teeth. It looks like a monster. "Look! A skull!" Tom shouts. Mia sees the skull too. She screams and runs away. Tom follows her. They run down the hill and back to the park. They find their moms and hug them. They tell them about the skull. Their moms say it is not a monster. It is a bone. It is from a long time ago. It is not scary. It is interesting. Tom and Mia are not sure. They still think the skull is scary. They do not want to wander away from the park again. They want to stay with their moms and play with the swings and slides. They are safe and happy.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a brilliant red carpet. A boy named Tim and his friend, Sam, loved to play on it. They liked to pretend it was a magic carpet that could fly. One day, Tim's mom said she would lend the carpet to their neighbor, Mrs. Brown. Tim and Sam were sad because they loved playing on the carpet. They decided to play with it one last time before it went away. They put on their hats and climbed on the carpet, pretending to fly to faraway lands. They had a lot of fun. The next day, Mrs. Brown came to take the carpet. Tim and Sam said goodbye to their magic carpet. But then, Mrs. Brown said she had a surprise. She brought out a new, even more brilliant blue carpet! She had bought it for the boys to play on. Tim and Sam were so happy! They thanked Mrs. Brown and continued their magical adventures on their new carpet.

Once upon a time, in a cool forest, there lived a little boy named Tim. He liked to play with his red ball. One day, while playing, he saw a big cat. The cat wanted to play with Tim's red ball too. Tim and the cat played together, but then the cat dropped the ball near a sharp cut on a tree. The ball got stuck! Tim and the cat tried to get the ball out, but they couldn't. They felt sad and didn't know what to do. Just then, a friendly bird saw them and wanted to help. The bird flew up to the cut in the tree and pushed the red ball out. Tim and the cat were so happy! They thanked the bird and continued to play together in the cool forest.

Lily and Ben were twins who liked to play together. One day, they saw a big automobile parked outside their house. It was shiny and red, and had four doors and four wheels. "Let's pretend we are driving the automobile!" Lily said to Ben. "We can go to the park and see the ducks!" Ben nodded and followed Lily to the automobile. They opened the doors and climbed inside. They sat on the seats and held the steering wheel. "Vroom, vroom!" Lily said, making noises with her mouth. "We are going so fast!" "Beep, beep!" Ben said, pressing the horn. "Watch out for the other automobiles!" They had fun pretending to drive the automobile, until they heard a loud voice. "Hey, what are you doing in my automobile?" It was the owner of the automobile, who had come out of his house. He looked very upset and angry. Lily and Ben were scared and tried to get out of the automobile. But they were too small and the doors were too heavy. They struggled to open them, but they could not. "Help, help!" Lily cried. "We are stuck!" "Sorry, sorry!" Ben said. "We were just playing!" The owner of the automobile came closer and opened the doors for them. He took them out of the automobile and scolded them. "Don't you ever touch my automobile again!" he said. "It is not a toy! It is very expensive and dangerous! You could have broken something or hurt yourselves!" Lily and Ben felt ashamed and sorry. They nodded and said they were sorry again. They ran back to their house and told their mom what happened. She hugged them and said they should not play with things that did not belong to them. She also said they should apologize to the owner of the automobile and be more careful next time. Lily and Ben learned their lesson and never played with the automobile again. They found other things to play with, like their bikes and their dolls. They still liked to pretend, but they did not cause any trouble.
