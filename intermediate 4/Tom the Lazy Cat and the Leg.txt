Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Lazy Cat and the Leg

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. Tom had a leg that he liked to admire. He would look at his leg and think, "Wow, I have a nice leg!" One day, Tom went outside to play with his friends. They saw his leg and they all wanted to admire it too. They said, "Tom, your leg is so nice! We want to have a leg like yours!" Tom felt happy that his friends liked his leg too. They played together all day, and when they were tired, they all lay down to rest. Tom looked at his leg one more time and thought, "I may be lazy, but I have a great leg!" And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was sad because he could not find his toy. He looked everywhere in his house but could not find it. One day, Tim went to the kitchen and saw water coming out of the faucet. He realized that his toy might be near the water. Tim looked around and saw his toy hiding behind the faucet. Tim was very happy to find his toy. He played with it all day and was not sad anymore. The faucet had helped Tim find his toy and now he was a happy little boy.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lucy wandered in her house. She was looking for her favorite toy. She looked under her bed, behind the door, and on the shelf. But she could not find her toy anywhere. Lucy felt sad and asked her mom, "Mom, where is my toy?" Her mom said, "I don't know, but I think it might be hidden somewhere. Let's look together." So Lucy and her mom looked in the living room, the kitchen, and even the bathroom. But they still could not find the toy. Suddenly, Lucy's little brother, Tommy, came into the room. He was holding Lucy's toy! Tommy said, "I found it in the garden! I wanted to play with it too." Lucy was so happy to see her toy again. She hugged Tommy and said, "Thank you, Tommy! Next time, let's play together." And they all played happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim found a dark box in his room. He did not know where it came from. He wanted to know what was inside, so he tried to open it. But the box was locked. Tim felt sad and went to ask his mom for help. "Mom, can you help me open this dark box?" Tim asked. His mom looked at the box and said, "I can help you, but first we need to take it to the kitchen." So Tim and his mom took the box to the kitchen. They found a key and opened the box together. Inside the box, they found many toys and a note. The note said, "These toys are for you, Tim. Have fun and share with your friends." Tim was very happy and played with the toys all day. He also shared the toys with his friends, and they all had fun together.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a white letter on the floor. It had her name on it. She opened the letter and read it. The letter said, "Mia, you need to measure the table. It will be fun. Love, Mom." Mia was excited to help her mom. She went to the kitchen and saw a big ruler on the table. Mia picked it up and started to measure the table. She said, "The table is very long." Her mom heard her and smiled. She knew Mia was doing a good job. Later, Mia's mom came into the kitchen with a big surprise. It was a giant cake! She said, "Thank you for measuring the table, Mia. Now we have enough space for the cake." Mia was so happy. She knew that measuring the table was important. They enjoyed the cake together and had a great day.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a race. All the animals wanted to win. A graceful deer, a strong bear, and a small ant were in the race too. The animals were very excited. The deer was very fast and the bear was very strong. But the ant was small and slow. The ant had a plan. The ant would carry a leaf during the race. The other animals laughed at the ant. They thought the ant could not win. The race started. The deer ran fast and the bear was strong. The ant carried the leaf and was slow. The sky became dark and it started to rain. The rain made the ground wet and slippery. The deer slipped and fell. The bear did not see the deer and fell too. The ant walked slow and steady. The ant did not slip because the leaf kept the ant safe. The ant was close to the finish line. But the ant stopped to laugh at the deer and the bear. The ant forgot to finish the race. The ant lost the race. The moral of the story is: do not laugh at others when they are down, or you may lose too.
