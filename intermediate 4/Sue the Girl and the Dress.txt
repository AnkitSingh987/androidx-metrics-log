Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Dress

One day, a girl named Sue found a pretty dress in her closet. She loved the dress and wanted to wear it to her friend's party. But her mommy said, "Sue, that dress is too spicy for the party. You may regret wearing it." Sue did not listen to her mommy and wore the dress anyway. At the party, Sue's friends laughed at her dress. They said, "Your dress is so spicy!" Sue felt sad and wished she had listened to her mommy. She knew she made a mistake. Sue went home and said sorry to her mommy. She said, "Mommy, I regret wearing the spicy dress. Can you help me pick a better dress next time?" Her mommy hugged her and said, "Of course, Sue. We will find a nice dress for the next party." Sue learned to listen to her mommy and never wore the spicy dress again.

One day, a mighty dog named Max went to play outside. He saw a sink in the yard. Max was very curious about the sink. He thought, "I want to play with the sink." Max went to his friend, a little bird named Tia. "Tia, can you help me play with the sink?" he asked. Tia was happy to help. They went to the sink and saw a big pile of leaves. Tia had an idea. "Let's scatter the leaves and see what happens," she said. So, they scattered the leaves all around the sink. As they scattered the leaves, they heard a sound. It was a little kitten hiding under the sink! The kitten was scared of the leaves, but Max and Tia were kind. They played with the kitten and had lots of fun. After that day, the three friends played together all the time.

Once upon a time, two friends were walking in the forest. One of them was named Mary and the other one was called John. Mary had a candle in her hand and she said to John, "Look at my candle, it's very fragile!" John twisted the candle around with his fingers. But suddenly, something strange happened. The candle cracked, and Mary said, "Oh no! Be careful, my candle is too fragile!" John said, "Why don't we try to fix it? I have some tape in my bag." So together, the two friends twisted and taped the candle back together. When it was fixed, Mary said, "Thank you so much for helping me. Now the candle is as good as new!" John smiled, and said, "You're welcome. We make a good team!" The friends hugged and each went their own way. From then on, Mary was always careful not to twist her candle too much, because she knew it was fragile.

Ben and Mia were playing in the park with their mom. They saw a big truck with a picture of a lion on it. The truck was making loud noises. "Look, mom, a lion!" Ben said, pointing at the truck. "That's not a real lion, Ben. That's a picture of a lion. Do you know what a real lion looks like?" mom asked. Ben nodded. He had seen a real lion at the zoo with his dad. He remembered the lion had a big mane, sharp teeth and a long tail. Mia shook her head. She had not seen a real lion yet. She was curious. "Can we go see the real lion, mom?" Mia asked. "Maybe another day, Mia. The zoo is far from here. And the truck is not going to the zoo. It is going to the circus," mom explained. "What is the circus, mom?" Ben asked. "The circus is a place where people and animals do tricks and make people laugh. Sometimes they have lions, but they are not wild. They are trained," mom said. "Can we go see the circus, mom?" Mia asked. "Maybe, Mia. But we have to see if we have enough money and time. And we have to see if the circus is fit for you. Some circuses are not very nice to the animals. They make them do things they don't like," mom said. Ben and Mia looked at the truck again. They wondered what the lion inside was like. They hoped the lion was happy and safe. They recognized the lion was a beautiful and powerful animal. They wanted to learn more about lions and other animals. They thanked mom for taking them to the park. They had fun playing and learning.
