Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Three and the Pizza

One evening, a humble family of three were sitting around a table in their small kitchen. The father was cutting a pizza into neat slices with a sharp knife. The three year old asked, "What are you doing, Daddy?" "Making dinner," the father replied. The three year old was so excited; that meant they were having pizza for dinner. But then the father raised the knife in the air and said, "If you are a good boy, you can have an extra slice!" The three year old's mouth dropped open in excitement. He couldn't wait to eat the extra slice of pizza. The night passed quickly, and soon the family was done eating. Daddy raised the sharp knife high in the air and said, "You have been so good tonight, and I have a special treat. Here is your extra slice of pizza!" The three year old smiled in delight and quickly took the slice from his father. This was the best pizza of his life because it was made with a lot of love.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog. His name was Bob. Bob had a toy he loved a lot. The toy was old and ugly. But Bob did not care. He played with the toy every day. One day, the toy got very dirty. Bob's mom said, "We need to wash the toy." Bob did not want to let go of his toy. But he knew it was dirty. So, he let his mom wash the toy. After the toy was clean, Bob was happy. The toy was still ugly, but it was clean. Bob played with the toy again. He loved his toy very much. And they lived happily ever after.

Lily was a musician. She liked to play her drum and sing songs. She had a big show today. She wanted to show everyone her drum and songs. But Lily had a problem. She was too small to go on the stage. The stage was high and had stairs. Lily could not walk up the stairs. She could only crawl. She tried to crawl up the stairs, but it was hard and slow. "Come on, Lily, hurry up!" said Ben, her friend. Ben was also a musician. He played the guitar. He was on the stage already. He was impatient. He wanted to start the show. "I'm trying, Ben, I'm trying!" said Lily. She crawled faster, but she was still far from the stage. She saw many people waiting for the show. They looked bored and tired. Lily felt sad and scared. Then she had an idea. She saw a big box near the stairs. The box had wheels. Lily crawled to the box and got inside. She pushed the box with her feet. The box rolled down the floor and up the ramp to the stage. Lily was happy. She made it to the stage! "Wow, Lily, that was cool!" said Ben. He helped Lily out of the box. He gave her the drum. They smiled at each other. They were ready to play their music. The people clapped and cheered. Lily and Ben were happy. They were musicians.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lila. She was three years old and desired to go to the park. One day after lunch, her mom took her. At the park, Lila shouted with joy. Everything seemed so big to her! She ran to the swings and she asked her mom, “Can I go on the swing?” Her mom smiled. She said, “Yes, you can. Just make sure to swing slow and carefully.” Lila sat in the swing and began to sway. Up and down and back and forth. She felt incredible, like she could fly! Suddenly, a voice from behind she said, “Do you want me to push you?” Lila swiveled around and saw a friendly face peering at her. He said, “Hi, my name is Joseph. I would like to serve and push you.” Lila said, “Yes please!” and then she felt the swing take-off. Joseph pushed her so high and so fast. Lila shouted with joy, feeling incredible. Soon she was back on the ground, full of excitement and joy. Lila thanked Joseph for pushing her. Then she said goodbye and ran to find her mom. Everything was incredible! She thought to herself, “I can’t wait to come back to the park again.
