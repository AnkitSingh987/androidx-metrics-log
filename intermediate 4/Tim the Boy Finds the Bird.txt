Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Bird

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big tree and wanted to play. He thought he saw something shiny behind the tree. Tim decided to peek. Tim saw a spear behind the tree! He picked it up and said, "Wow, this is cool!" Just then, a silly bird flew by and took the spear from Tim. The bird said, "You are stupid, this is mine!" Tim was sad and didn't know what to do. Then, something unexpected happened. The bird flew back and gave the spear to Tim. The bird said, "I'm sorry, let's be friends." Tim was happy and they played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man. He had a big house and many toys. He liked to play with a ball. One day, he wanted to play with water. He went outside to the tap. The tap was shiny and new. The wealthy man turned the tap. Water came out. He put his hands under the water. It was cold and wet. He liked it. He thought of a fun game to play. He wanted to shoot the water into the air. The wealthy man took a cup. He filled it with water from the tap. He threw the water up high. It looked like rain. He laughed and clapped. He played this game all day. The wealthy man was happy.

One day, Sally was exploring the forest. She came across a strange oak tree that seemed to be growing all by itself. She cautiously touched its rough bark. It was noon and Sally was getting hungry. Suddenly, she noticed something growing on the tree. With a big smile, she plucked it off and held it in her hand. It was a big, ripe berry. She couldn't wait to taste it. Sally took a bite of the sweet berry. She laughed with delight as the juice tickled her tongue. She quickly ate the rest of the berry and looked around for more. She soon spotted a bush filled with berries. She ran over to the bush and grabbed as many as she could. As she looked at the pile of berries in her hands, a feeling of excitement filled her heart. She couldn't wait to taste them all. The rest of the day, Sally roamed the forest tasting the delicious berries. One by one, she tasted various shapes and sizes of the berries and savoured their sweet flavour. Finally, she was done! What a day she had in the forest!

Once upon a time, there was a bird who wanted to build a nest. She searched high and low, but couldn't find anything special to use. She looked to the sky and saw a big tree. With that in sight, the bird began to fly towards it. When she reached the tree, she was nervous. The branches of the tree were high and the bird was scared of not being able to reach them. But she tried her best and finally flew up the tree. The bird found the perfect spot to build her nest. She built it with a lot of care and it looked so pretty. Soon, she laid two eggs in it and waited for them to hatch. One day, a small bird came over to her nest and said, "You should take care of your nest and your eggs, because if you don't, you might reach a point where you won't be able to protect it anymore." The bird was grateful for the advice, and she worked hard to make sure her nest and eggs were safe. She reached her goal of having a beautiful nest and a family to go with it. Moral: Hard work and determination can help you reach your goals.

There were two children in the park. Their names were Tip and Tops. They were playing together and having lots of fun. Suddenly, an impressive white bird appeared in the sky. Tip was amazed and shouted, "Wow, look at that! Isn't he amazing!" The bird flew closer and then Tip realized it was an eagle. Tip said "Let's protect him!" Tops agreed, but then asked, "But how do we protect him?" Tip replied, "We just need to make a big noise to scare him away and keep him safe." The two children made a lot of noise until the white eagle flew away. They felt proud of themselves for protecting the impressive eagle.
