Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Frog

One day, a boy named Tim found a deep hole in the ground. He saw a lollipop inside the hole. Tim wanted the lollipop very much. He tried to pick it up, but his arm was too short. His friend, Sam, saw him and came to help. Sam said, "I can help you get the lollipop!" Tim was happy. Sam urged Tim to hold his legs while he went down into the hole. They worked together to get the lollipop. But when Sam came out of the hole, something unexpected happened. Instead of a lollipop, Sam had a frog in his hand. The frog jumped away, and the friends were sad. They did not get the lollipop they wanted. They learned that sometimes things do not go as planned, and they walked away with a bad ending to their day.

Once upon a time, there was a white cat named Snow. Snow liked to play in the garden. One day, Snow saw a red rose. The rose was very pretty. Snow wanted to give the rose to her friend, Bunny. Bunny was a nice friend who always helped Snow. The rose would remind Bunny of their fun times in the garden. Snow took the rose to Bunny's house. Bunny was very happy to see the rose. They played together all day in the garden. The red rose made them both smile.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, ugly helmet. He wore it every day. He hoped it would keep him safe when he played outside. One day, Tim met a new friend, Sue. Sue looked at Tim's helmet and said, "Why do you wear that ugly helmet?" Tim said, "I hope it keeps me safe when I play." Sue thought about it and said, "Can I try it on?" Sue put on the ugly helmet and played with Tim. They had lots of fun together. At the end of the day, Sue gave the helmet back to Tim. She said, "Thank you for letting me try your helmet. I hope it keeps you safe too." Tim smiled and they both went home, happy to have a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball. One day, Spot saw a newspaper on the floor. Spot thought, "Hmm, maybe I can play with that too!" So, Spot started to play with the newspaper. As Spot played, he saw a dangerous bug on the newspaper. Spot was scared and didn't know what to do. He thought, "I need to remove the bug, but how?" Then, Spot had an idea. He would use his ball to make the bug go away. Spot rolled his ball to the bug, and something unexpected happened. The bug jumped onto the ball and started to play with it too! Spot was surprised, but he realized that the bug wasn't dangerous after all. They both played with the ball and the newspaper, and they became good friends. From that day on, Spot and the bug played together every day, and they were very happy.

Lily and Sam were playing in the snow. They made a big snowman with a hat and a scarf. They gave him two buttons for eyes and a carrot for a nose. They were very happy. But then a big dog came. He was not a nice dog. He barked and ran at the snowman. He wanted to take the carrot. He knocked down the snowman and made a mess. Lily and Sam were sad and scared. They ran away from the dog. Lily dropped one of her mittens in the snow. She did not see it. A boy saw what happened. He was polite and brave. He chased away the dog with a stick. He picked up Lily's mitten and ran after her and Sam. "Hey, wait!" he called. "You forgot your mitten!" Lily and Sam stopped and turned around. They saw the boy with the mitten. They smiled and thanked him. "Thank you for rescuing our mitten and our snowman!" Lily said. "You're welcome," the boy said. "I'm Tom. Do you want to be my friends?" "Yes, please!" Sam said. They all went back to the snowman. They fixed him and gave him a new carrot. They played together until it was time to go home. They were very happy.
