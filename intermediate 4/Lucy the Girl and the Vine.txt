Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Vine

One day, a little girl named Lucy went outside to play. She saw a big tree with a long vine hanging down. Lucy wanted to climb the tree, but she thought the vine looked tough. She was not sure if she could do it. Lucy's friend, Tim, came over and said, "I can help you climb the tree. We can study the vine together and find the best way to climb it." Lucy was happy to have help from her friend. They looked at the vine and found a good spot to start. Tim said, "Hold on tight and be strong!" Lucy held on to the vine and climbed up the tree with Tim's help. They both felt very proud when they reached the top. They sat on a big branch and laughed together, happy that they had climbed the tough vine.

One day, a boy named Tim found a rake in his yard. The rake was big and had a long handle. Tim thought it would be fun to play with the rake. Tim met his friend, Sue, who was very bossy. Sue said, "Let me play with the rake!" Tim didn't want to give her the rake, but Sue screamed, "Give it to me now!" Tim was scared and gave her the rake. Sue played with the rake, but she didn't know how to use it. She swung it around and hit Tim by accident. Tim cried and went home. Sue felt bad, but she didn't say sorry. They didn't play together again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Mary. She had a pet rabbit that she loved to play with. They would run around in her garden and have lots of fun. One day Mary and her rabbit were running around the garden when Mary heard a loud noise. She looked up to see what it was and noticed a noisy plane flying high above her. She watched it as it flew above and her rabbit started hopping faster and faster as the plane sped off in the distance. As Mary watched the plane, she noticed something interesting – a plant! She hadn't seen it before, so she wanted to see what it was. She picked it up, examined the leaves, and smelled the flowers. It was beautiful. Mary thought to herself, "I know what I can do with this plant. I will put it in my garden and I can watch the planes fly over it and enjoy the noise they make. That way, my rabbit and I can enjoy the noise and still have a peaceful garden.” Mary planted the beautiful plant in the garden and went for a walk with her rabbit. They watched the planes fly over and the plant swayed in the breeze created by the flyers. The moral of this story is that it's ok to enjoy the special moments of life, like watching planes fly over, while still maintaining peace in our lives and/or surroundings.

Lily was cold. She had to wait for Mommy to come back from the store. She wore a coat and a hat, but she still shivered. She wanted to go inside the house, but Mommy had the key. She looked at the phone in her hand. Mommy said she could call her if she needed anything. But Lily did not know how to use the phone. She pressed some buttons, but nothing happened. She felt impatient. She wanted to hear Mommy's voice. She saw a car coming down the street. She hoped it was Mommy's car. She ran to the curb and waved. But it was not Mommy's car. It was a red car. Mommy's car was blue. Lily felt sad. She went back to the porch and sat down. She hugged the phone and shivered some more. She wished Mommy would come soon. Then she heard a familiar sound. It was Mommy's car. She saw it turn into the driveway. She jumped up and ran to meet Mommy. Mommy got out of the car and smiled. She opened her arms and hugged Lily. She said, "I'm sorry, sweetie. The store was very busy. I got you some cookies and milk. Are you okay?" Lily nodded. She felt warm and happy. She showed Mommy the phone and said, "I tried to call you, but I don't know how." Mommy said, "That's okay. You did a good job waiting for me. Let me show you how to use the phone. Then we can go inside and have a snack." Lily smiled. She liked the phone. She liked Mommy more.
