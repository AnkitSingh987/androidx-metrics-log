Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Football

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his football. He played with it every day in his yard. One day, a big, impressive dog came to Tim's yard. The dog wanted to play too. Tim was scared of the big dog, but he didn't want to surrender his football. Tim thought for a moment. Then, he had an idea. He threw the football far away. The big dog ran and got it. The dog brought it back to Tim. They played together all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a broken helicopter. It was very old, and it couldn't fly anymore. One day, a little boy found the helicopter and was very excited to see it. He wanted to play with it, so he tried to make it work. But no matter how hard he tried, it just wouldn't fly. Finally, he had a bright idea. He asked his dad to help him fix it. His dad was surprised to see the broken helicopter, but he said he could fix it. Together, they worked hard and fixed the helicopter. When they turned it on, the helicopter started to spin around and fly like new! The little boy was so excited and ran around the yard, chasing the helicopter. His dad was happy too and watched his son enjoy the helicopter. They both had a great day!

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a walk with her mom. They saw a big rock. Sue's mom said, "Wow, that's an impressive rock!" Sue wanted to know more. Her mom told her that the rock was a special kind of rock called a mineral. As they got closer to the mineral, Sue started to feel her nose tickle. She tried to hold it in, but she couldn't. "Achoo!" Sue sneezed so hard that the impressive mineral started to shake. Then, something unexpected happened. The mineral cracked open and a tiny, friendly dragon popped out! The dragon said, "Thank you for waking me up with your sneeze, Sue!" Sue and her mom were very surprised. They became friends with the dragon and had a fun day playing together.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a smooth rock. On the rock, a little bird made a home. The bird had a friend, a squirrel. They liked to play and talk. One day, they found a big branch. The bird said, "I want to make a swing." The squirrel said, "No, I want to make a house." They did not agree. They were sad. Then, a nice wind came. It blew the branch up into a tree. The wind made the branch into a swing and a house! The bird and the squirrel were happy. They played together and lived in the tree.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a piece of cake on the table. He wanted to eat it, but he knew his mom would be upset if he ate it before dinner. Tim tried to resist the cake, but it was very hard. Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. Sam saw the cake and said, "Let's eat the cake! It looks yummy!" Tim thought it was a foolish idea, but he didn't want Sam to eat the cake alone. So, they both ate the cake together. When Tim's mom came home, she saw the empty plate and asked, "Did you eat the cake before dinner?" Tim and Sam looked at each other and said, "Yes, we did." Tim's mom was not happy, but she knew they were just being kids. She told them, "Next time, please wait until after dinner to eat dessert." Tim and Sam promised they would.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom loved to play with his ball. One day, he saw a big goal in the park. He wanted to kick his ball into the goal. Tom dared to try. He kicked the ball very hard. But the ball was heavy. It did not go far. Tom was sad. He tried again and again. Each time, the ball did not go into the goal. Then, a big dog came. The dog saw Tom and the heavy ball. The dog helped Tom. It pushed the ball with its nose. Together, they made the ball go into the goal. Tom was happy. He thanked the dog, and they played together all day.
