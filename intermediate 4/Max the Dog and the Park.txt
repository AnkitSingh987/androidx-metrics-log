Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Park

One day, a little dog named Max was very excited. He heard his mom mention a fun park. Max wanted to go to the park and play with his friends. He jumped up and down and wagged his tail. When they got to the park, Max saw his best friend, a cat named Luna. They played together, running and chasing each other. Max was happy to be with Luna. They took a break and Max got a nice rub from Luna's mom. It felt good and made Max even more excited. After playing all day, Max and Luna were tired. They said goodbye to each other and went back to their homes. Max couldn't wait to tell his mom about his fun day at the park with Luna. He was excited to go back and play again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a pretty sweater that her mom made for her. Amy loved her sweater so much, she wore it every day. One day, Amy went to the park to play. She saw a little boy sitting alone. He looked sad, so Amy went to talk to him. The little boy's name was Tom. His mom did not have any available time to make him a sweater. He was cold and upset. Amy wanted to help Tom. She gazed at her sweater and thought of what to do. She decided to give her sweater to Tom. Tom was so happy and warm now. Amy and Tom played together at the park. They became best friends. The moral of this story is to be kind and share with others. When we share, we make others happy and we feel good too.

One day, a new lizard went to the park. He saw a big ball. He wanted to play with the ball. So, he kicked the ball. The ball went far away. Another lizard saw the new lizard kick the ball. He went to the new lizard and said, "Hey, that's my ball!" The new lizard was sad. He did not know it was his ball. They both wanted to play with the ball, but they did not want to share. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took the ball away. The two lizards were sad. They looked at each other and said, "Let's find the ball together." They became friends and learned to share.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was a happy boy, but he was a little fat. One day, Tom went to the store with his mom. He saw a shiny penny on the ground and picked it up. Tom was very happy to find the penny. In the store, Tom saw a girl from his school. Her name was Sue. Sue saw Tom and said, "You are fat and have a penny. I don't want to play with you." Tom felt very sad and embarrass. He didn't know what to do. Just then, an old man walked up to Tom. The old man was very kind. He said, "Don't be sad, Tom. You found a magic penny! It can make one wish come true." Tom was surprised and happy. He wished for everyone to be nice to each other. Suddenly, Sue smiled and said, "I'm sorry, Tom. Let's be friends and play together." Tom was very happy, and they all played together. The moral of the story is that being kind is better than being mean, and magic can come from unexpected places.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Timmy. Timmy loved to play dress-up with his costume box. One day, he found a big, red costume in the box. He put it on and felt very happy. Timmy wanted to show his friends his new costume. He went outside and saw his friend, Susie. "Look at my red costume!" Timmy said, holding his arms out wide. Susie looked at Timmy and started to laugh. "You look funny!" she said. Timmy felt embarrassed and ran back inside his house. Timmy's mom saw him and asked, "Why are you sad?" Timmy told her what happened. His mom hugged him and said, "It's okay to be different. Your costume is special, just like you." Timmy felt better and went back outside. He played with Susie and his other friends, wearing his red costume. They all learned that it's okay to be different and to be proud of who you are.
