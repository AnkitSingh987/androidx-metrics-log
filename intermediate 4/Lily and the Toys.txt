Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Toys

Lily likes to organize her toys. She has a box for dolls, a box for cars, and a box for blocks. She puts each toy in the right box. She is very neat and tidy. One day, she has a friend over to play. His name is Ben. Ben likes to play with all the toys. He takes them out of the boxes and mixes them up. He makes a big mess. Lily is not happy. She says to Ben, "Ben, please do not make a mess. I like to organize my toys. Can you help me put them back in the boxes?" Ben says, "But Lily, it is more fun to play with all the toys. They are interesting. Look, I can make a doll drive a car. And I can make a block tower with the cars. And I can make the dolls talk to each other." Lily looks at what Ben is doing. She thinks it is interesting too. She says, "OK, Ben, you can show me how you play with the toys. But after we play, can we organize them again?" Ben says, "OK, Lily, we can organize them again. But first, let's play. You can be my friend and we can have fun." Lily and Ben play with the toys. They have fun. They make up stories and games. They laugh and smile. After they play, they organize the toys together. They are good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a velvet cat named Softy. Softy loved to count. She would count all day long. Softy had many friends who liked to count too. They would have fun counting together. One day, Softy and her friends had a competitive counting game. They wanted to see who could count the highest. Softy was very good at counting, but her friend, Tiny, was also good. They counted and counted, trying to be the best. As they kept counting, Softy and Tiny got very tired. They did not want to stop because they both wanted to win. They did not see the big dog coming. The big dog was not nice. Softy and Tiny were so busy counting, they did not hear their friends calling to warn them. The big dog came and scared them away. Softy and Tiny learned that being too competitive can be bad.

One day, a little dog named Lucky went for a walk. He liked to run and jump in the park. The sun was bright and the grass was green. Lucky was very happy. While playing, Lucky found a big pile of dust. He thought it was fun to roll in the dust. He got very dirty, but he did not care. Lucky was having a great time. After playing, Lucky felt tired. He found a nice tree and lay down to rest. He closed his eyes and took a nap. When he woke up, he felt better and went home. Lucky had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a kind man named Tom. Tom loved to help people. One day, he found a big bottle of wine. He knew it was not for kids, but he had an idea. Tom had a big kite. He wanted to fly the kite very high. He thought, "I can attach the bottle of wine to the kite. It will make the kite fly higher." So, he tied the bottle to the kite with a long string. He went to the park on a mild day to fly the kite. As the kite went up, everyone saw the bottle of wine. They asked Tom why he did that. Tom said, "I want the kite to fly high, and I want everyone to remember that we should not drink wine. It is not good for us." The people smiled and clapped. They learned a good lesson that day.

Once upon a time, there was a messy room. In the room, there was a little animal. The animal was sad because no one would care for him. He would look out the window and wish for a friend. One day, a kind girl named Lisa came to the room. She saw the messy room and the sad animal. Lisa knew she could help. She started to clean the room and care for the animal. The animal felt happy and began to wag its tail. As Lisa cared for the animal, they became best friends. The animal was not sad anymore. The messy room became clean and bright. The animal knew that Lisa was the friend he had been waiting for. They lived happily ever after.
