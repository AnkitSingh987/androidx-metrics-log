Category: Intermediate
Title: Mark and the Ball

Mark is a little boy who loves to play. One day, he finds an incredible big ball in his yard. He is so happy and wants to show it to his friend, Sue. He runs to her house and says, "Sue, look at my incredible big ball!" Sue loves the ball too. They play with it all day. They throw it, kick it, and roll it around. They have lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happens. The ball starts to get bigger and bigger! Mark and Sue look at each other and say, "Wow, this is so strange!" The big ball keeps growing until it is as big as a house. Mark and Sue are very surprised. They climb on top of the ball and ride it down the street. All the other kids see them and say, "Wow, that's an incredible big ball!" Mark and Sue smile and say, "We love our incredible big ball!"

Jeff was a brave little boy. One day he wanted to do something daring. He decided to try to get a cooler from the beach. He bravely marched over to the beach and looked around. He saw a cooler that was perfect for his plans. He ran over and daringly opened the cooler. Inside were some cool treats and his favorite drink. He proudly took it and marched back to his house. He was so excited to share his perfect cooler with his family. Everyone loved it so much they decided to take it to all their outdoor adventures. Jeff was brave, daring and he made the perfect choice.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big closet in his room. He liked to play in the closet and hide his toys there. One day, Tim was playing with his ball. He threw the ball very high and it went into the closet. He heard a loud noise. "Oh no!" Tim said. "Did I break something?" He went to the closet and saw that he broke a cup. Tim was sad that he broke the cup. He told his mom about it. "Mom, I broke a cup in the closet," he said. His mom looked at the broken cup and said, "It's okay, Tim. I'm glad you told me. We can clean it up together." They cleaned up the broken cup and Tim felt better. From that day on, he was more careful when playing with his ball near the closet. And his mom was always glad that Tim learned to be careful and honest.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily was going to have a birthday party. She was very excited. She wondered who would come to her party. She wanted to have a lot of friends there. Lily's mom said, "Lily, you can invite five friends to your party." Lily thought about her friends. She wanted to invite her honest friend, Sam. Sam always told the truth. Lily liked that about him. She also invited four more friends who were nice and kind. At the birthday party, everyone had a lot of fun. They played games and ate cake. Lily was very happy. Then, it was time to open presents. Lily opened Sam's present first. It was a beautiful toy. "Thank you, Sam," said Lily. "I love it!" Sam smiled and said, "I am glad you like it. I picked it just for you." The moral of the story is that having honest and kind friends is very important. They make us happy and help us have fun.

Once upon a time, in a cute little town, there lived a big bear and a small bear. They were best friends. One day, they found a box. The big bear said, "I want to fit in the box!" The small bear said, "No, I want to fit in the box!" The big bear and the small bear started to fight. They pushed and pulled, trying to fit in the box. Their fight became a big war. The other animals in the town saw them and were sad. They did not like the war. Then, a wise old owl came to the bears. She said, "Stop fighting! The box is not for you." The bears listened and stopped the war. The owl showed them what was inside the box. It was a mirror! The bears looked in the mirror and saw how cute they were when they were friends. They learned that being friends is better than fighting. And they lived happily ever after.
