Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Sweet Dog and the Box

One day, a sweet dog named Max was playing in the yard. He saw a big hole in the earth. Max was very curious and wanted to know what was inside the hole. So, he put his nose in the hole and started to dig. As Max was digging, he found a small box. He picked it up with his mouth and ran to his friend, a little girl named Lily. "Look, Lily! I found a box in the earth!" said Max. Lily was excited and wanted to help Max solve the mystery of the box. Together, they opened the box and found a pretty necklace inside. Lily put the necklace on, and they both felt very happy. They had solved the mystery of the box in the earth. From that day on, Max and Lily loved to play and solve mysteries together.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful bird named Blue. Blue loved to soar high in the sky every evening. One day, while flying, Blue saw a little girl named Amy. Amy looked sad. Blue flew down and asked, "Why are you sad, Amy?" Amy said, "I lost my toy. Can you help me find it?" Blue nodded and said, "I will help you!" Blue soared high in the sky and looked for the toy. Soon, Blue found the toy near a big tree. Blue brought the toy back to Amy. Amy was very happy and thanked Blue. From that day, Blue and Amy became best friends. They played together every evening.

One day, a squirrel went to the park. The squirrel wanted to find the perfect nut. He looked under the trees and in the grass. He found many nuts, but they were not perfect. The squirrel saw a big tree with a lot of nuts. He thought, "This tree must have the perfect nut!" He climbed up the tree and found a nut. It was not perfect, but he took it anyway. The squirrel's mom saw him with the nut. She said, "That nut is not perfect. You must keep looking." The squirrel did not listen. He ate the nut and got a tummy ache. His mom told him, "Next time, listen when I tell you something." The squirrel learned to listen to his mom and find the perfect nut.

Once upon a time there was a little boy named Jake. Jake liked to create things. He used all sorts of things, like blocks and clay, to make things. One day, Jake's mom gave him some wood and some string. She said, "Can you help me make a hanger?" Jake was so excited! He took the wood and string, and began to work. He worked really hard, making sure the hanger was strong. After a few minutes, he was done. He proudly showed his mom the hanger he had created. His mom said, "This hanger looks really strong - well done!" Jake was so proud of himself. He had made something all on his own and it was strong. He smiled and said, "I like to create!"

Once upon a time, there was a big world with many different places. In one of these places, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to carry her toy bear everywhere she went. One day, Mia and her bear went for a walk. As they walked, they met a little boy named Tom. Tom said, "Hi, Mia! I see you carry your bear. I carry my toy car." Mia smiled and said, "Yes, I love my bear. We can play together with our toys!" So Mia, Tom, and their toys played in the different places of the big world. They climbed hills, jumped in puddles, and ran through the grass. They had so much fun together, and they became the best of friends. And that is how Mia and Tom learned that even though they were different, they could still have fun and be friends in the big world.

Once upon a time, there was a dull lung. It was brown all over and very soft. But one day, the sun was shining so brightly that it started to melt the dull lung. The dull lung started to melt faster and faster and soon it was a puddle on the ground. Then, a little boy came running to try and save it. He put his tiny hands in the puddle and tried to put the dull lung back together again. But it was too late. The dull lung had melted completely and there was nothing the little boy could do. The little boy was so sad that his dull lung was gone. He wished it hadn't melted so quickly, but there was nothing he could do to stop it. So the little boy sadly trudged away, leaving the puddle behind. He would never play with his dull lung again.
