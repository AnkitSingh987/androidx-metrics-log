Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Doll

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a doll that she loved very much. The doll had a pretty dress and long hair. Lucy played with the doll every day. One day, a mean boy named Tom saw Lucy's doll. He wanted the doll for himself. So, he decided to steal the doll from Lucy when she was not looking. Lucy was very sad and miserable when she found out her doll was gone. Lucy's friend, Sam, saw Tom take the doll. Sam went to Tom and told him that stealing was bad. Tom felt sorry and gave the doll back to Lucy. Lucy was so happy to have her doll back, and she thanked Sam for helping her. From that day on, Tom learned not to steal, and they all played together happily.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little dog named Spot. He liked to know what everyone was doing. One day, Spot saw a big bird with a message in its beak. The bird was going to provide the message to someone. Spot wanted to know what the message said. He ran up to the bird and asked, "What is the message?" The bird replied, "It's a secret! I cannot tell you." But Spot was very nosy and did not give up. He followed the bird all day until they reached a house. The bird gave the message to a little girl. She read it and smiled. Spot was happy that the girl was happy, but he still wanted to know the secret. The girl saw Spot and said, "Don't be nosy, Spot! The message was a nice surprise from my friend. It's not for you to know." And from that day on, Spot learned not to be nosy.

Once upon a time, there was a Mommy and her little girl, Rose. They were best friends and did everything together. One day, Mommy said to Rose, "Let's go to the movie theater, shall we?" Rose excitedly clapped her hands and cried, "YAY! A movie! I can't WAIT!" So off they went. The movie was amazing and both Mommy and Rose had a great time. But alas, on their way home, they got lost. Mommy started getting worried, and Rose recognized that she was feeling helpless. Rose said to her Mommy, "Don't worry! We'll find our way! I will take care of you!" Mommy smiled and hugged Rose. As they kept walking, they eventually found the way home. From then on, Mommy knew she could always count on her little girl to care for her.

Once upon a time, there was a big, old house. In the house, there was a small door. The door went to a secret place called the attic. The attic was a fun place to play. One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the attic. She found a big, soft seat. The seat was very old, but it was still nice to sit on. Lucy liked to sit on the seat and read her books. One day, Lucy found a magic book in the attic. When she read the book, her toys came to life! Lucy and her toys had a big party on the old seat. They laughed and played all day. The attic was the best place to have fun.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went for a wander in her big backyard. She felt calm as she walked on the soft grass. She saw pretty flowers, tall trees, and heard birds sing. Lucy loved her backyard. As she walked, she found a small table with a plate of toast on it. Lucy was very hungry, so she picked up a piece of toast and took a big bite. It was yummy! She ate all the toast on the plate. Just then, a tiny mouse came out from behind the table. The mouse looked sad and said, "That was my toast." Lucy felt bad for eating the mouse's toast. She said sorry and shared her lunch with the mouse. From that day on, Lucy and the mouse were best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a great raft. It was perfect for the river, and a boy and his sister loved to sail it every day. One day, the boy and his sister took the raft out and the river was really calm. But then it started to rain really hard. The boy said to his sister: "We need to dry the raft quickly!" But the rain was too strong and they could not dry the raft. The river started to get bigger and bigger. The raft began to fill up with water and started to sink. The boy and his sister started to shout and cry for help, but it was too late. The raft was lost! The End.
