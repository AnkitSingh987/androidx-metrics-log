Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Polite Cat and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a polite cat named Tim. Tim had a friend, a dog named Sam. They played together every day. One day, Tim saw a symbol on a tree. It looked like a big "X". Tim called Sam and said, "Look at the tree! What is that?" Sam said, "I don't know. Let's find out!" They followed the symbol and found a big box. They opened the box, and inside was a big cake! They were so happy and shared the cake with their friends. The end.

Mommy was walking in the park with Bobby, her three-year-old son. Bobby saw a bald man walking towards them with a plant in his hands. "Hello," said the bald man, smiling. He held the plant out to Bobby. "Can I bring it home?" asked Bobby. The bald man nodded. "Yes," he said. "You can bring it home with you. It's a very special plant." Bobby smiled. He thanked the bald man and ran ahead to his mommy, holding the plant carefully with both hands. When Mommy saw the plant, she smiled. "What a wonderful gift!" she said. She gave the bald man a polite nod as they walked away. Bobby carried the plant all the way home, admiring its green leaves. When they arrived, Mommy helped him put it in the garden. Bobby was so happy to have his own special plant. He always smiled when he saw it. They both enjoyed taking care of it.

Once upon a time, there was a lucky little boy named Tim. Tim had a lime tree in his yard. One day, he wanted to make a drink with the limes. He asked his mom, "Can I squeeze the limes to make juice?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful, the limes can be tricky." Tim did not listen to his mom. He thought he was lucky and nothing bad would happen. He started to squeeze the limes, but they were very slippery. One lime rolled away and hid under the table. Tim looked under the table and saw the lime. He tried to grab it, but it kept rolling away. Tim felt sad and thought he was not lucky anymore. Then, his mom came and helped him. She said, "You are still lucky, but you need to learn to listen and be careful." From that day on, Tim learned to listen and be careful. He knew that being lucky was not enough. He had to be smart and listen to his mom. And they made the best lime juice together.

One day, a little ball named Bouncy was very tired. Bouncy wanted to sleep but could not find a good place. Bouncy saw a big curtain and thought, "Maybe I can sleep there." Bouncy went to the curtain and tried to climb it. But it was too hard. Bouncy asked a big cat for help. The cat said, "I can help you, but you must bounce very high for me first." Bouncy was very tired, but wanted to sleep on the curtain. So, Bouncy tried to bounce high for the cat. Bouncy jumped and jumped, but could not bounce high enough. The cat got mad and said, "You cannot bounce high. I will not help you." Bouncy was sad and still very tired. Bouncy tried to find another place to sleep, but could not. In the end, Bouncy could not sleep and was very tired all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was a very great dog. He loved to go on walks and play with his friends in the park. One day, Max went on a walk with his best friend, Sam. They were very happy to start their walk. As they walked, the sky got dark and cold. The wind started to blow, and Max began to shiver. Sam saw Max shiver and felt sad. Sam wanted to help Max feel better, so they decided to find a warm place to hide from the cold wind. They found a big tree with lots of leaves. Max and Sam went under the tree and cuddled together. The leaves kept them warm and safe from the cold wind. Soon, Max stopped shivering, and they both felt great again. They played under the tree until the wind stopped, and then they went home, happy and warm.
