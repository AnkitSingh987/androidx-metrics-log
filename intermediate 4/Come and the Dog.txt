Category: Intermediate
Title: Come and the Dog

One day, a big, hairy dog was walking down the street. He saw a sign that said, "Come to the park for fun!" The hairy dog wanted to play, so he went to the park. At the park, the hairy dog met a little girl named Sue. Sue said, "Hi, hairy dog! Do you want to play with me?" The dog wagged his tail and they played together. They ran, jumped, and laughed all day. But then, something unexpected happened. The hairy dog started to talk! He said, "Thank you, Sue, for playing with me. I am really a boy who was turned into a dog by a mean witch. Your love and laughter broke the spell!" Sue was very surprised, but happy to have a new friend. They played together every day after that.

Once upon a time, there was a prince. The prince liked to run very fast. Every day, he would run in the big park near his home. One day, while the prince was running fast, he saw a big pile of rocks. He thought it would be fun to add more rocks to the pile. So, he picked up a rock and added it to the pile. He did this again and again, making the pile bigger and bigger. Soon, the prince had a new game. He would run fast, find a rock, and add it to the pile. He played this game every day and was very happy. His big pile of rocks grew and grew, and everyone in the town knew about the fast prince and his big pile of rocks.

Once upon a time, in a small cabin in the woods, there lived a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play with her dog, Max. One day, Lucy's mom told her to leave the cabin and go pick some berries. Lucy felt a little helpless, but she took Max with her and they went off to find berries. As they walked, Lucy and Max saw a big tree. Lucy thought it would be fun to climb the tree. She left Max at the bottom and climbed up, up, up. But then, she looked down and saw how high she was. Lucy felt helpless and scared. She didn't know how to get down. Max saw Lucy was in trouble and ran back to the cabin. He barked at Lucy's mom, and she knew something was wrong. They both went back to the tree and saw Lucy high up in the branches. Lucy's mom helped her down and they all went back to the cabin together. From that day on, Lucy knew she wasn't helpless, because she had her family and Max to help her.

Once upon a time there was an ancient man. He was walking down the road carrying a basket of carrots. The man carefully placed each carrot down the road. Suddenly, a little girl came running up to him. She asked the man what he was doing. The man smiled and replied, "I am spending my carrots. I want to turn them into something special." The girl looked puzzled and asked, "What are you going to turn them into?" The man patted the little girls head and replied, "I'm going to turn each carrot into a wish. See that carrot over there? Well, I just wished for a new car." The little girl was so excited she ran off to try and make wishes of her own. The man smiled and started walking on. He tried to make the most of his ancient wishes by spending each carrot carefully.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy baby named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with his mom and dad. He had a crib in his room where he would sleep every night. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, it is time to yield to sleep." Tim was very sleepy, but he did not want to go to his crib. He wanted to play with his toys. His mom said, "You can play tomorrow, but now you need to sleep." So, Tim went to his crib and lay down. He closed his eyes and soon fell asleep. He had a dream about playing with his toys and having fun. When he woke up the next day, he was happy and ready to play.
