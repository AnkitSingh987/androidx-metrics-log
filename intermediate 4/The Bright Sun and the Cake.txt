Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bright Sun and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a bright sun in the sky. It was very hot. Mom was in the kitchen. She was making a cake. She put the cake in the oven. The oven was hot too. A little boy named Tim saw the bright sun. It reminded him of the cake in the oven. He wanted to eat the cake. He waited and waited. He was very hungry. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the bright sun away. The sky became dark. Tim was sad. But then, the wind blew the oven door open. The cake was ready! Tim was very happy. The wind had brought him a surprise.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his red shoes. One day, while playing outside, his shoes came untied. Tim did not know how to tie his shoes. He saw his friend, Sue. Sue knew how to tie shoes. Tim asked her, "Sue, can you help me tie my shoes?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, Tim, I can help you. It is easy and fun!" Sue showed Tim how to make loops and tie his shoes. Tim tried to do it too. At first, it was hard, but then it became easy. Tim and Sue played all day with their shoes tied. They had lots of fun. Tim was happy that he learned to tie his shoes. He knew that if he kept trying, he could do it. The moral of the story is: "Don't give up, keep trying, and you will learn new things."

Once upon a time, there was a big, impressive cat named Tom. Tom lived in a small house with his best friend, a little girl named Lucy. One day, Lucy and Tom were in the kitchen. Lucy wanted to cook some eggs in a pan for breakfast. "Tom, can you help me with the pan?" Lucy asked. Tom was happy to help. He jumped up and grabbed the pan with his paws. But as they were cooking, something unexpected happened. A loud noise came from outside, and it disturbed them. "What was that?" Lucy asked. Tom went to the window to see. He saw a tiny bird who had knocked over a trash can. The bird was trying to find food. Tom felt sorry for the bird, so he and Lucy decided to share their breakfast with it. They all ate together, and the bird was very thankful.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a blue calendar on the wall. It had many pictures of animals on it. A little girl named Lily lived in the house. She loved to look at the animals on the calendar every day. One sunny day, Lily's friend, Max, came over to play. Max saw the blue calendar and said, "Wow, I like the animals on your calendar!" Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Max. I love them too." Lily wanted to be kind to Max, so she decided to offer him a gift. She found a blue pencil and said, "Max, would you like this blue pencil as a gift? You can draw your own animals with it!" Max was very happy and said, "Thank you, Lily! I will draw a big blue elephant!" And they both laughed and played together, drawing animals with the blue pencil.

Once upon a time, there were two little friends called Jack and Jill. One day when they were walking, it started to snow. Jack said, “Let’s make a snowman!” Jill said, “That sounds like fun!” They started to make the snowman, making a big snowball for the head and smaller ones for the body and arms. But when they were done, Jack said “I’m going to wipe my snowball for the head, so our snowman looks the best!” Jill was jealous that Jack was doing that, so she said “Me too! I don’t want your snowman to look better than mine.” So both Jack and Jill wiped their snowballs so they could build the best snowman. When they were done they had their very own snowman – and they both agreed it was the best one ever!
