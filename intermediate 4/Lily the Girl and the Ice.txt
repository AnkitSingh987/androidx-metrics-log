Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Ice

One day, a girl named Lily found an amazing bracelet. It was shiny and pretty. She put it on her arm and went to show her friend, Sam. Sam thought it was very nice and wanted to play with it. Lily and Sam went to the park to play. They saw a little shop that had a sign that said "serve." They went inside and found a lady who made ice cream. They asked her for some ice cream to eat. The lady said, "I can serve you ice cream, but first you must give me something special." Lily thought about it and decided to give the lady her amazing bracelet. When she did, something unexpected happened. The lady turned into a magical fairy! She said, "Thank you for giving me the bracelet. Now, I can serve ice cream to everyone in the park for free!" Lily and Sam were very happy and enjoyed their ice cream with all their friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big, deep blue ball. She loved to play with it every day. Lily liked to throw the ball high up in the sky and catch it with her hands. One day, Lily threw the ball too hard. It went up and up, and then it came down and hit her on the head. "Ouch!" she cried, holding her head. Her mom heard her cry and came running to her. "Lily, what happened?" she asked. "I threw the ball and it hit my head," Lily said, tears in her eyes. Her mom hugged her and said, "Don't worry, I will comfort you." She took Lily inside the house and put ice on her head. The ice made her feel better. Later, Lily and her mom went outside again. They played with the ball together. This time, they threw it gently and laughed as they caught it. Lily learned to be careful when playing with her deep blue ball, and she always remembered how her mom comforted her when she was hurt.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy liked to play with her friends. One day, Amy and her friends were playing with a ball in the park. They were having a lot of fun together. Amy saw a cat sitting on a wall. The cat was very pretty. Amy wanted to pet the cat. She walked up to the cat, but she tripped on a rock. She fell down and got a big stitch in her leg. It hurt a lot. Amy felt foolish for not looking where she was going. Her friends came to help her. They got a bandage for her stitch. They all went home and had some ice cream. Amy learned to be more careful when playing.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play outside in the dirt. One day, she found a dirty toy car in the yard. It was covered in mud and leaves. Lucy wanted to make the car clean and shiny again. Lucy took the dirty toy car inside her house. She found a cloth to wipe the dirt off. She wiped and wiped the car until all the mud and leaves were gone. The toy car looked like new again. Lucy was very happy with her clean car. After playing with the clean car for a while, Lucy saw a waste bin. She knew that the dirty mud and leaves she wiped off the car should go in the waste bin. So, she picked up the dirty cloth and put all the dirt in the bin. Now, Lucy's toy car was clean, and there was no more mess.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim had a camera to take pictures of pretty things. His mom had a big blanket to sit on. She started to fold the blanket to make it nice and neat on the grass. They both sat down and Tim began to take pictures of the trees and flowers. A loud dog came running by and barked, "Woof! Woof!" Tim was scared, but he wanted to take a picture of the dog. He held up his camera and said, "Say cheese, doggy!" The dog stopped barking and smiled at the camera. Tim took the picture and the dog said, "Thank you, Tim!" Tim and his mom were so surprised. They didn't know dogs could talk! The dog told them, "I am a magic dog. I can do many fun things!" Tim and his mom laughed and played with the magic dog all day. When it was time to go home, Tim's mom folded the blanket and they said goodbye to their new friend. They couldn't wait to tell everyone about the magic dog they met at the park.
