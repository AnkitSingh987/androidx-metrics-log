Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and the Door

Sara was a gifted girl who liked to draw and sing. She had a big box of crayons and a book of songs. She also had a friend, Tom, who lived next door. Tom liked to play with Sara, but he was not very good at drawing or singing. He often copied Sara's pictures and repeated Sara's words. One day, Sara and Tom were playing in the garden. Sara had a new dress with a pretty patch on it. She was very happy and showed it to Tom. Tom liked the patch too, and he wanted one for himself. He asked Sara if he could have her dress. "No, Tom, this is my dress. You can't have it. It's mine," Sara said. "But I like the patch. It's so nice and colorful. Please, Sara, let me have it. Just for a little while," Tom begged. Sara shook her head. She did not want to share her dress. She liked it too much. She turned away from Tom and started to sing a song from her book. Tom felt angry and sad. He wanted the patch more than anything. He decided to take it from Sara. He waited until Sara was busy singing, and then he sneaked behind her. He grabbed her dress and pulled it hard. He tried to rip off the patch, but he only made a big hole in the dress. Sara felt a tug and a tear. She stopped singing and looked down. She saw her dress ruined and Tom holding the patch. She screamed and cried. "Tom, what did you do? You ruined my dress! You stole my patch! You are a bad friend! Go away, go away!" Sara yelled. Tom felt scared and sorry. He did not mean to hurt Sara. He only wanted the patch. He tried to give it back to Sara, but she pushed him away. She ran into the house and slammed the door. Tom was left alone in the garden, holding the patch. He felt very sad and lonely. He wished he had not been so greedy. He wished he had been a better friend. He wished he could say sorry to Sara. But it was too late. Sara did not want to play with him anymore.

Once upon a time, there was an old red cell that lay sleeping in the corner of a room. Every day, a little three year old girl named Sarah passed the cell on her way to the kitchen. One day, Sarah stopped at the cell and asked "What are you?" The old red cell opened its eyes and replied in a deep, sleepy voice "I am a cell." Sarah's eyes lit up. She was so excited to have made a new friend. She asked the old red cell, "Will you come home with me?" The old red cell smiled. "Of course I will. But we must go to sleep now." So Sarah and the old red cell said goodnight and curled up together in a comfy bed in Sarah's bedroom. They both fell asleep for hours and hours. The next morning, Sarah woke up with the red cell right beside her. She was so happy that her new friend had come to stay.

Once there was a little girl called Sarah who loved to slide. Every day, she would slide down the slide in her garden and laugh with delight. One day, Sarah's mommy told Sarah they were going to the festival. Sarah was so excited and asked if she could bring her slide. Her mommy nodded and smiled. When they got to the festival, Sarah set up her slide and started to slide. Everyone laughed with delight when Sarah went down the slide. But suddenly some children came and took Sarah's slide away. Sarah began to cry and her mommy was very concerned. She took Sarah away and told her something important. She said, "It's important to be modest when other people have something we want. We must remain kind, even if it takes effort." Sarah nodded and said she understood. From that day on, Sarah tried to be modest and to keep kindness in her heart.

One day, a little girl named Lily went outside to play. She saw a big puddle on the ground. Lily wanted to jump in the puddle. But her mom said, "No, Lily. You will get wet and dirty." Lily was sad, but she had an idea. She went inside and found a big towel. Lily said, "I will wrap the towel around me, so I don't get wet." Her mom smiled and said, "That's a good idea, Lily." Lily wrapped the towel around her and went back outside. She jumped and splashed in the puddle. The towel kept her dry. But then, Lily saw a spoiled apple on the ground. She didn't like the spoiled apple, so she picked it up and threw it away. Then, Lily played with her friends in the puddle until it was time to go inside for dinner.
