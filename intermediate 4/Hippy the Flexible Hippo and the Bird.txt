Category: Intermediate
Title: Hippy the Flexible Hippo and the Bird

Once, there was a flexible hippo named Hippy. Hippy lived near a big pond. One day, he saw a bird folding its wings. Hippy wanted to fold something too. He looked around and found a big leaf. Hippy said, "Hi, bird! I want to fold like you. Can you help me?" The bird flew down and said, "Sure, Hippy! Let's fold the big leaf together." Hippy and the bird folded the big leaf. They were happy to fold together. They played near the pond all day. And from that day, Hippy and the bird became good friends.

Lily loved to read books. She had many books in her room. Some books had pictures, some books had stories, some books had words. Lily liked to learn new words. She had a big book with many words. It was a dictionary. One night, Lily wanted to read her dictionary. She took it from her shelf and went to her bed. She turned on her lamp and opened the book. She saw many words and their meanings. She tried to read them, but some words were hard. She did not know how to say them or what they meant. She asked her mom for help. Her mom came to her room and looked at the book. She smiled and said, "Lily, this is a very good book, but it is not for bedtime. This book is for when you want to study or write. It has many words that you will learn when you are older. But now, you need to sleep. Why don't you choose another book, a book with a story or a picture?" Lily did not want to quit. She wanted to read her dictionary. She said, "No, mom, I want to read this book. I want to learn new words. Please, help me read this book." Her mom said, "Lily, I know you are curious and smart, but you also need to rest. Reading this book will not help you sleep. It will make your head hurt and your eyes tired. You can read this book tomorrow, when the sun is up. But now, it is dark and you need to sleep. Please, put this book away and choose another book." Lily was sad and angry. She did not want to put her dictionary away. She wanted to read it. She said, "No, mom, I don't want to sleep. I don't want another book. I want this book. Please, let me read this book." Her mom said, "Lily, I am sorry, but you have to listen to me. This is not a good time to read this book. You are not being nice or respectful. You are being stubborn and rude. If you don't put this book away and go to sleep, I will take it away from you and you will not see it again." Lily was scared and sorry. She did not want to lose her dictionary. She liked her dictionary. She also liked her mom. She did not want to make her mom angry or sad. She realized that she was wrong. She said, "Mom, I am sorry. I was not nice or respectful. I was stubborn and rude. I will put this book away and go to sleep. I will read it tomorrow, when the sun is up. Can you please forgive me and hug me?" Her mom smiled and said, "Of course, Lily, I forgive you and I love you. You are a good girl and a good reader. I am proud of you for wanting to learn new words. But you also need to learn when to stop and when to listen. Reading is fun and important, but so is sleeping and obeying. You can read your dictionary tomorrow, but now, you need to sleep. Here, let me hug you and tuck you in. Good night, Lily." Lily hugged her mom and said, "Good night, mom. I love you. Thank you for helping me and teaching me. I will sleep well and dream of new words." She closed her eyes and fell asleep. She was happy and peaceful. The end. Moral value: The story teaches the value of moderation, obedience and respect. It shows that reading is good, but not at the expense of sleep and health. It also shows that listening to parents and following their rules is important and respectful. It also shows that apologizing and forgiving are signs of love and kindness.
