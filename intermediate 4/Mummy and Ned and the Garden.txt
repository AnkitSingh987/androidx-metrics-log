Category: Intermediate
Title: Mummy and Ned and the Garden

Mummy and Ned were at the swimming pool. The water was blue and sparkly. A big yellow diving board stretched over the water. Ned wanted to dive. He climbed up the ladder and stepped onto the board. It wobbled under his feet. Then he jumped, sliding through the air. He stretched his arms out and splashed into the water. Ned was very proud of himself. He looked up and saw Mummy smiling. He swam over to her and they splashed each other. When they had finished, they went in the garden. There were lots of pretty orange flowers growing in the garden. Mummy and Ned saw a huge orange bulb growing in the ground. They dug it out of the ground and gave it a big hug. Then they planted it in a pot and watered it. They put it in the sun, where it could grow big and strong.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play in the big hall. He saw a fragile toy house on a table. Tim wanted to touch it, but he was scared it might break. Tim's friend, Lily, came over and asked, "Do you want to play with the toy house?" Tim nodded, but he was still scared. Lily said, "We can be careful. Let's play together." As they played, Tim and Lily had a small fight. Tim wanted to put a toy car inside the house, but Lily said it was too big. Tim got sad and didn't want to play anymore. Lily said sorry and gave Tim a hug. They decided to find a smaller toy car to put in the house. Tim and Lily played happily together and took care of the fragile toy house.

---

One day, a mop was in the kitchen. The mop was sad because it was dry. It wanted to soak in water and be wet. The mop had a friend, a sweet cat. The sweet cat saw the mop and wanted to help. The sweet cat had an idea. It went to the sink and tried to turn on the water. But the sweet cat was too small. It could not turn on the water. The sweet cat was sad too. It wanted to help the mop soak. Just then, a big dog came into the kitchen. The dog was thirsty and went to the sink. The dog turned on the water to drink. The water splashed on the floor. The mop jumped into the water and started to soak. The mop was happy and wet. The sweet cat was happy too. The dog, cat, and mop played in the water and had a fun day.

Tom and Anna like to play pretend. They have a big box of toys and costumes. Today, they want to pretend they are a teacher and a student. Tom puts on a hat and glasses. He holds a book and a stick. He is the teacher. Anna puts on a dress and a backpack. She holds a pencil and a paper. She is the student. Tom says, "Hello, class. Today we will learn about furry animals. Furry animals have soft hair on their skin. Can you name some furry animals?" Anna thinks hard. She says, "I know some furry animals. A cat is a furry animal. A dog is a furry animal. A bear is a furry animal." Tom says, "Very good, Anna. You are a smart student. Here is a sticker for you." He gives her a sticker with a star on it. Anna smiles and says, "Thank you, teacher." They hear a noise outside. They look out the window and see a big furry animal. It is a lion. It has a mane and a tail. It roars loudly. Tom and Anna are scared. They drop their toys and costumes. They run to their mom and dad. They hug them and say, "Help! There is a lion outside!" Mom and dad look out the window and laugh. They say, "Don't worry, kids. That is not a real lion. That is Mr. Jones, our neighbor. He is wearing a lion costume for a party. He is just pretending, like you." Tom and Anna feel silly. They laugh too. They say, "Oh, we were just pretending too. We were playing teacher and student. We learned about furry animals." Mom and dad say, "That sounds like fun. You are very creative and clever. We are proud of you." Tom and Anna hug their mom and dad. They say, "We love you. Can we play pretend again tomorrow?"
