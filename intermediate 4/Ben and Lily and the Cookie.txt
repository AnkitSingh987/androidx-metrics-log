Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben and Lily and the Cookie

Ben and Lily are twins. They like to play outside in the snow. They make snowmen and snowballs and snow forts. They have fun. One day, they see a big billboard on the road. It has a picture of a hot chocolate and a cookie. It says: "Warm up with a treat at Joe's Cafe". Ben and Lily want to go to Joe's Cafe. They are cold and hungry. They ask their mom if they can go. Their mom says: "Yes, but you have to use your coats and hats and gloves. It is very icy outside. Be careful." Ben and Lily use their coats and hats and gloves. They hold hands and walk to Joe's Cafe. They are careful not to slip on the ice. They see the billboard again. It makes them smile. They reach Joe's Cafe. They go inside. It is warm and cozy. They order hot chocolate and cookies. They say: "Thank you, Joe." Joe says: "You're welcome, Ben and Lily. Enjoy your treat." Ben and Lily enjoy their treat. They sip their hot chocolate and nibble their cookies. They feel happy and warm. They look at each other and say: "This is the best day ever."

Once upon a time, there was a small car. The car wanted to go on an adventure, but it had one problem—its engine was broken. The car tried and tried, but no matter how hard it tried, it couldn't get the engine to work. Now the car was feeling very frustrated. Then one day, the car had an idea. It drove over to the garage and said, "Can you help me? My engine is broken!" The mechanic smiled and said, "Yes, I can help you. Just give me some time." The car waited patiently. After a few minutes, the mechanic said, "It's fixed! Now you can go on your adventure." The car was very happy and drove off, adventure in its sights.

Once there was a little girl who loved to play games. One day, she and her friends played a game to see who could win the most berry treats. The little girl was very lucky, and she won the most treats. She smiled with glee and collected all the berry treats in her basket. All her friends were so happy for her and they clapped and cheered to congratulate her. She enjoyed the day and was so proud of herself. When she got home, she celebrated her win by making a berry treat and eating it with her family. She was so lucky, and it made her day.

One day, a boy named Tim found a zipper on the floor. He wanted to design something fun with it. He thought about what to make. Tim decided to make a bag with the zipper. Tim's friend, Sam, saw the bag and said, "Wow, that is a nice bag! Can you make one for me too?" Tim felt happy that Sam liked his bag. He said, "Yes, I can make one for you." Sam's sister, Lily, saw the bags and felt jealous. She wanted a bag too. She asked Tim, "Can you make a bag for me too, please?" Tim said, "Of course, Lily! I will make one for you too." Now, all three friends had a fun bag with a zipper. They played together and had a great time.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park. Sue saw a long stick on the ground. She picked up the stick and started to mix the dirt with it. She was having fun making a big mess. All of a sudden, a big monster came out of the bushes! Sue was scared, but the monster was friendly. He had a big smile and said, "Hi! I'm Max, the Mud Monster. Can I play with you?" Sue was surprised but happy. She and Max played together, mixing the dirt with the long stick. They made a big mud castle, but it was not a scary castle. It was a fun castle for them to play in. They had a great day at the park, and Sue made a new, unexpected friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Billy. Billy loved to sing songs all day long. One day, he heard that a big party was happening in the park. He was very excited and wanted to go. At the party, all the animals were having fun. They were playing games and eating yummy food. Billy wanted to sing a song for his friends, but he felt ashamed. He was scared that they would not like his song. But then, a wise old owl told him to be brave and just try. Billy started to sing, and something unexpected happened. All the animals began to dance and clap. They loved his song so much that they wanted to pay him with hugs and treats. Billy was so happy that he sang even more songs. He learned that it's okay to try new things, even if you're scared. And that's how Billy became the most famous singing bird in the park.
