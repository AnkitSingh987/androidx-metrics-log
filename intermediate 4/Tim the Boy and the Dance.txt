Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Dance

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to dance. He would dance all day long. Tim wanted to raise his hands and move to the rhythm of the music. But Tim did not know how to dance well. He was ignorant about dancing. One day, Tim met a kind teacher. The teacher said, "I can teach you to dance better." Tim was very happy. He wanted to learn. The teacher showed Tim how to raise his hands and move to the rhythm of the music. Tim listened and practiced. After some time, Tim was not ignorant about dancing anymore. He could raise his hands and move to the rhythm very well. Tim was very happy. He thanked the kind teacher. Now, Tim could dance and have fun with his friends. And they all lived happily ever after.

Lily had a new wagon. It was big and red. She wheeled it around the garden, laughing with joy. Then she spotted her dad. He had something on the ground. "What's that?" Lily questioned. "It's a wheel for your wagon," her dad said. Lily was excited. She helped him to fit the wheel onto the wagon. But the wheel wobbled. Her dad sighed and looked frustrated. He tried to fix it, but it wouldn't stay in place. He tried again and again, but nothing worked. Finally, he gave up. He shrugged and said, "Let's go and get a new wheel." Lily cheered and said, "Yay!" They carried the old wheel back to the shed and wheeled the wagon to the store. They found a brand new wheel. It only took a few minutes to fit it onto the wagon. It worked perfectly. "Yay!" Lily beamed. She climbed into the wagon and prepared for an adventure.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a furry bunny named Ben. Ben loved to hop all day long. He would hop from one end of the garden to the other. One day, while hopping, Ben saw a big squash. He got very excited and called his friend, Tom the turtle. "Tom, come see this big squash I found!" said Ben. Tom slowly came to see the squash. They both looked at it and wondered how it got so big. Just then, the squash began to move! Ben and Tom were very surprised. The squash opened up, and out came a little fairy. The fairy said, "Thank you for finding my home! As a gift, I will make your garden the best place to live." With a wave of her wand, the garden became even more beautiful. Ben and Tom were so happy and continued to play together in their wonderful garden.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very hungry. He wanted to eat a big apple. He looked at the apple tree in his yard. There were many apples on the tree. Tim found a long cloth. He thought, "I can use this cloth to get an apple." He threw the cloth up to the tree. The cloth caught an apple. Tim pulled the cloth down. The apple came down too. Tim was happy. He said, "I did it! I got an apple!" He took a big bite. His hungry tummy was now complete. Tim shared the apple with his friend. They both smiled and played together.

One day, Jack and his Mommy was walking down the street. They could hear something jolly roaring from far away. Jack asked his Mommy what it was, but she didn’t know. So, they followed the sound and found the biggest, jolly lion Jack had ever seen. Jack was very scared and ran away, but his Mommy stayed with the lion. The lion said it felt sick and needed help. Jack’s Mommy said they should take the lion to the hospital. They helped the lion into a car and drove to the hospital. The doctors said the lion was all better after a few days of rest. Before they left, the lion thanked Jack and his Mommy and roared a jolly goodbye!

One day, a cute little bear went for a walk in the woods. He was happy and singing a song. While he was walking, he saw a big, mean bear. The big bear looked angry. The big bear said, "This is my part of the woods! You need to leave!" The little bear was scared but did not want to surrender his walk. He thought of a plan. He said, "I will give you my honey if you let me stay." The big bear was surprised by the offer. He thought for a moment and said, "Okay, I will let you stay if you give me your honey." The little bear smiled and went to get his honey. But when he came back, he brought a big group of bees with him. The bees buzzed around the big bear, making him run away in fear. The little bear had tricked the big bear and saved his walk in the woods. From that day on, the big bear never bothered the little bear again, and they both enjoyed the woods together.
