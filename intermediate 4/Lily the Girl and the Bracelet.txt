Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Bracelet

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to collect pretty things. Her favorite thing to collect was bracelets. She had many bracelets in all colors. One sunny day, Lily saw a very pretty bracelet on the ground. She wanted to add it to her collection. But when she tried to pick it up, a little boy named Timmy saw her. Timmy was angry because he thought the bracelet was his. Lily and Timmy both wanted the bracelet. They did not want to share. They both pulled on the bracelet, but it did not break. Then, Lily had an idea. She said they could take turns wearing the bracelet. Timmy liked the idea, and they both were happy. They learned to share and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a foolish rat named Remy. Remy lived in a big house with many rooms. He loved to play and run around all day. One day, Remy met a cat named Tom. Tom said, "Hello, little rat. I am hungry. Can you give me some food?" Remy thought for a moment and said, "I can give you some cheese. It is in the kitchen." Remy and Tom went to the kitchen together. Remy gave Tom the cheese, and Tom was happy. They became friends and played together every day. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tom found some gum. The gum was incredible! It was big and pink. Tom loved gum, so he put it in his mouth. He chewed it and blew a big bubble. The bubble was so big that it lifted him up into the sky. Tom was scared. As Tom went up, he saw a big bird. The bird did not like Tom in the sky. The bird said, "You should not be here!" The bird tried to pop the bubble. Tom was scared and said, "Please, do not pop my bubble!" The bird thought about it and said, "Okay, I will not pop your bubble, but you must go back down." Tom did not want to fall, but he knew he had to go back down. He let some air out of the bubble. The bubble got smaller and Tom started to fall. He fell slowly and safely back to the ground. Tom was happy to be back on the ground, but he knew he would never forget his incredible gum adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a little duck. The duck was pale yellow. It lived near a big pond. The duck liked to swim in the water. One day, a girl came to the pond. She saw the pale duck swimming. The girl sat down and began to watch the duck. She liked how it moved in the water. The duck saw the girl watching. It swam over to the girl. The girl smiled and pet the duck. They became friends. The girl came to watch the duck every day, and they played together by the pond.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a toy box full of fun things to play with. One day, she found a big cube in the box. The cube was new, and she did not know what it was for. She decided to play with it and see what would happen. Lucy played with the cube all day long. She rolled it, threw it, and even sat on it. As she played, she started to feel hungry. She remembered that her mom had put a small, round fruit in her lunch box. She decided to eat the fruit to make her tummy happy. Lucy took a big bite of the fruit. But, oh no! The fruit was very sour! Her face went all funny, and she did not like it at all. She knew that she should not play with the cube and eat at the same time. From that day on, Lucy decided to play with her toys and eat her food only when it was time to eat. And she never forgot the sour fruit that taught her a lesson.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy alarm that he loved very much. He would play with it all day, making it beep and light up. At night, he would take it to bed with him so he could feel safe. One day, Tim's mom wanted to add something new to his toy alarm. She found some colorful stickers and put them on the alarm. Tim was so happy to see his alarm looking even more fun. He couldn't wait to show it to his friends. Tim took his alarm with the new stickers to the park. He showed it to his friends, and they all thought it was very cool. They played with the alarm, making it beep and light up. Tim felt safe and happy because his toy alarm was with him, and he had his friends by his side.
