Category: Intermediate
Title: Mom and Tim and the Hat

One day, Mom and Tim went to the market. The market was big and impressive. There were many things to see and buy. Tim was very excited. Mom said, "Tim, can you help me bring the big bag?" Tim nodded and took the big bag. They walked around the market and put fruits and vegetables in the bag. At the market, Tim saw a man with a big hat. The man said, "Hello, Tim! I like your big bag." Tim smiled and said, "Thank you! I am helping Mom." The man laughed and gave Tim a red apple. Tim and Mom went home, happy with their day at the market.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Lily loved to play outside and look at the beautiful landscape. There were big trees, pretty flowers, and a little pond. One day, Lily was playing near the pond when she saw a tiny frog. The frog looked sad and uncomfortable. It had lost its way and could not find its family. Lily wanted to help the little frog, so she picked it up and held it in her hands. Lily and the frog walked around the pond, looking for the frog's family. The frog began to weep because it missed its family so much. After a while, they found the frog's family near a big rock. The frog was so happy that it stopped weeping. Lily felt good that she had helped the frog, and they all played together by the pond.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, plan to go to the station. They want to see the big trains. Tim puts on his coat and hat. Spot wags his tail. They walk together to the station. At the station, they see a big, scary train. Tim says, "Wow, that train is scary!" Spot barks at the train. Tim holds Spot's leash tight. They watch the scary train go by. The train makes a loud noise. Tim and Spot cover their ears. After the scary train leaves, Tim and Spot see a nice train. They go close to the nice train. The nice train has a smiling face. The train says, "Hello, Tim and Spot! I am not scary. I am a happy train!" Tim and Spot smile and wave goodbye to the nice train as it goes away. They had a fun day at the station.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a polite soldier named Sam. Sam was very nice and always said "please" and "thank you." He wanted to help everyone in the town. One day, Sam met a little girl who was sad. "What's wrong?" Sam asked her. "I lost my toy," the girl said with a frown. Sam wanted to help her, so he said, "Don't worry, I will find your toy." Sam looked everywhere for the toy. He looked under rocks, in trees, and behind houses. Finally, he found the toy under a big bush. Sam was happy that he could succeed in finding the girl's toy. He gave the toy back to the girl, and she was so happy. She said, "Thank you, Sam!" Sam felt good that he could help someone. The people in the town learned from Sam's actions. They learned that being polite and helping others can make everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a kind tutor. The tutor liked to help kids learn. One day, a little girl named Lily came to the tutor with a question. She asked, "Why do birds fly?" The tutor thought about it and gave her a silly answer, "Because they have wings and like to dance in the sky." Lily laughed and said, "That's a funny answer, but I want to know more." The tutor thought again and said, "Birds fly to find food and go to their homes." Lily was happy with the answer and went to tell her friends. The next day, Lily came back to the tutor with another question. She asked, "Why do fish swim?" The tutor smiled and said, "Fish swim because they like to play in the water." Suddenly, a fish jumped out of the water and said, "That's not true! We swim to find food and go to our homes too!" The tutor and Lily were very surprised. They never thought a fish could talk! From that day on, the tutor and Lily became friends with the talking fish, and they all learned new things together.
