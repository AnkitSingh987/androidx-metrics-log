Category: Intermediate
Title: Bobo the Bunny and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a funny bunny named Bobo. Bobo had a big pocket on his shirt. He liked to put things in his pocket. One day, Bobo found many colorful stones in the park. He was very happy and put all the stones in his pocket. Bobo's pocket was very full. It was hard for him to walk. He wanted to show his friends the stones but he could not find them. The stones were all mixed up. Bobo's friend, Lulu the bird, saw him and said, "Bobo, you should organize your pocket. Then you can find the stones you want." Bobo listened to Lulu and started to organize his pocket. He put the red stones together, the blue stones together, and the green stones together. Now, his pocket was neat and he could find the stones he wanted. Bobo was very happy and thanked Lulu. Bobo and Lulu played with the colorful stones in the park. They made a beautiful picture with the stones on the ground. All their friends came to see and they all loved it. From that day on, Bobo always organized his pocket and everything was easy to find. The moral of the story is that when we organize our things, we can find them easily and have more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Fashion. She loved to find things that match. One day, she saw a big box in the corner of her room. She was very curious about what could be inside the box. Fashion opened the box and found many colorful socks. There were red socks, blue socks, and yellow socks. She decided to play a game and match the socks by their colors. She had a feeling that something fun would happen if she matched all the socks. After matching all the socks, Fashion saw a secret note at the bottom of the box. The note said, "If you match all the socks, you will find a hidden toy!" Fashion was very excited and searched for the toy. She found a new ball to play with. Fashion was happy that her love for matching things led her to a fun surprise.

Lily wanted to wear her big sister's dress. It was pink and shiny and had many flowers on it. Lily loved flowers. She took the dress from the closet and put it on. It was too big for her. The dress dragged on the floor and the sleeves covered her hands. She went to the kitchen to show her mom. Mom was making soup. She had a big spoon in her hand. She saw Lily and smiled. "Wow, Lily, you look very pretty in that dress," Mom said. "But isn't it too big for you?" Lily shook her head. She felt happy. She wanted to help Mom with the soup. She reached for the spoon. But the dress was in the way. She tripped over the dress and fell on the floor. The spoon flew out of Mom's hand and landed on Lily's head. The soup splashed everywhere. Lily started to cry. She felt hot and wet and hurt. Mom ran to her and hugged her. "Oh, Lily, I'm sorry. Are you okay?" Mom asked. Lily nodded. She felt sorry too. She was foolish to wear the big dress. She wanted to take it off. "Mom, can I wear my own dress?" Lily asked. "Of course, sweetie. Let's go change. And then we can clean up this mess together," Mom said. Lily smiled. She loved Mom. She loved her own dress too. It was yellow and soft and had one flower on it. Lily loved flowers.

Once upon a time, there was a small hut by a big sea. In the hut, there lived a kind boy named Tim. Tim loved to sail his little boat on the sea. He would sail every day, and he was very happy. One sunny day, while Tim was sailing, he saw a big fish. The fish was frightened and needed help. Tim wanted to help the fish, but he was scared too. He thought about it and decided to be brave. He went near the fish and helped it. The fish was so happy and thanked Tim. The big fish had a secret. It could grant wishes. As a thank you, the fish granted Tim a wish. Tim wished for more friends. The next day, many kids came to the hut to play with Tim. They all sailed together and had fun. The moral of the story is to be brave and help others, and good things will come to you.
