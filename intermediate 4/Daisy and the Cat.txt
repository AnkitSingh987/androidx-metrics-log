Category: Intermediate
Title: Daisy and the Cat

Once there was a little girl called Daisy. Daisy had a lovely plate with a picture of her favourite cat on it. She always liked to tell her friends about her special plate. One day, Daisy was in the kitchen and saw her plate on the counter top. She grabbed it and ran to find her friends. When she found them, Daisy said proudly "Look at my plate. It's so lovely! It has a picture of my favourite cat on it". Her friends all smiled and said "It looks really nice, Daisy!". Suddenly, Daisy's mum appeared in the doorway. She said "Daisy, would you like to show your friends your plate?". Daisy beamed and said "Yes, Mum!" Daisy's mum then asked Daisy's friends why they were admiring her plate. Daisy's friends explained how lovely it was and Daisy said, "My mum gave it to me, and I love it so much". Daisy's mum smiled and said "That's so lovely, Daisy". She then told Daisy and her friends to get ready for dinner, as she had cooked a special meal. Daisy gave her mum a big hug, and then went off to the kitchen to set her special plate on the dinner table.

Lily and Max were playing in the park. They saw a big oak tree with many leaves. They ran to the tree and hugged it. "Hello, tree!" Lily said. "You are so big and strong!" The tree did not say anything. It just stood there. Max looked up and saw a squirrel on a branch. He pointed at it and said, "Look, Lily! A squirrel!" Lily looked up and saw the squirrel too. She smiled and said, "Hello, squirrel! Do you want to play with us?" The squirrel did not say anything. It just ran away. Lily and Max felt sad. They wanted to make friends with the tree and the squirrel. Then they heard some music. It was coming from a radio near a bench. A man and a woman were sitting on the bench. They were holding hands and smiling. Lily and Max ran to the radio. They liked the music. It was fast and happy. They started to dance. They jumped and spun and clapped. They had fun. The man and the woman saw them and laughed. They liked the children. They said, "You are very good dancers!" Lily and Max stopped dancing. They looked at the man and the woman. They felt confused. They did not know them. They did not know why they were laughing. They said, "Who are you?" The man and the woman said, "We are your parents!" Lily and Max gasped. They did not remember them. They had been lost for a long time. They ran to the man and the woman. They hugged them. They felt happy. They said, "We missed you!" The man and the woman said, "We missed you too!" They all hugged and cried. They were a family again.

Once upon a time, there was a large cardboard box in the yard. A little boy named Tim and his dog, Max, loved to play with it. One day, they decided to prepare the box for a big adventure. Tim said to Max, "We will make a big boat with this cardboard." Max barked happily. They worked together, pushing and pulling the box until it looked like a boat. Then, they got inside the boat and pretended to sail on the sea. As they sailed, something unexpected happened. The wind blew very hard and the large cardboard boat started to move! Tim and Max were surprised and excited. They sailed all around the yard, laughing and barking. When the wind stopped, they knew they had the best adventure ever.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to wrap a gift for his mom. He did not know how to wrap it. He asked his dad, "How do I wrap the gift?" His dad said, "I will show you how." They got paper and tape. They tried to wrap the gift, but it was hard. Tim was persistent. He did not give up. They kept trying and trying. Finally, they finished wrapping the gift. Tim gave the gift to his mom. She was so happy! She opened it, and it was a big toy car. Tim was surprised. He thought he bought a necklace for his mom. They all laughed. Tim's dad said, "I guess we wrapped the wrong gift!" They all had fun playing with the toy car together.
