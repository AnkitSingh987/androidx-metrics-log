Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Toys and the Window

Once upon a time there was a girl. She had long brown hair and always wore a big smile. Today the girl was very tired. She had been playing with her toys all day, and now she was ready for bed. She walked over to her bed and sat down. The bed felt nice, the texture was soft and comfortable. She laid down and closed her eyes. Suddenly she heard a loud clapping sound. It was coming from outside her window. She opened her eyes and sat up in her bed. She smiled and hopped off her bed and ran outside. There was a huge crowd in front of her house. The girl was confused at first, but then she realized that they were all clapping for her. She was so happy and proud. She smiled and waved to the people and clapped her hands too. The girl was so happy and so excited that she had to go inside and tell her mom. She ran back into her bedroom, gave her bed one final sleepy hug and said goodnight. She was no longer tired, but full of energy!

One night, while snuggled into his bed, a little boy gazed out his bedroom window. The boy saw something big, grey and wide eyed. It was an owl, perched on top of the tree in his backyard. The little boy was curious. He knew the owl must have something interesting to say. He decided to go outside and find out. As soon as he stepped onto the grass, the owl let out a loud hoot. The little boy's eyes lit up. He said, "Hello!" The owl didn't answer. The little boy said, "Please talk to me!" Again, the owl kept still and quiet. The little boy couldn't help but feel sad and disappointed. But still, he had a feeling that the owl would talk someday. He knew it was going to take patience and that the owl was stubborn. The little boy shook his head and went back into the house. But he could feel the owl's gaze on him, so he knew that the owl was watching and waiting. The little boy waited with anticipation, day after day, until one night, the owl finally spoke. The little boy rushed back outside to find out what the owl had to say.

Once upon a time, there was a man who wanted to go for a drive in his automobile. He split the delicious fruits into two bowls and invited his three year old daughter to join him. The daughter smiled and said “Yes! Papa, please can I join you?” The Papa said “Yes, of course. Let’s go!” They got in the car and drove off. Along the way, they stopped to pick some tasty fruits from a tree. The Papa split the fruits into two bowls. The daughter said to Papa “Thank you Papa! The fruits looks so yummy!” The Papa replied “Yes, they are really tasty! We can take the bowls home and share them with your brothers. Now let’s get going!” They continued their drive in the automobile and enjoyed the beautiful scenery. When they got home, the daughter shared the tasty fruits with her brothers. Everyone had a wonderful time as a family.

Ben and Lily were twins who liked to act. They acted like animals, like pirates, like superheroes. They had a lot of fun making up stories and playing with their toys. One day, their mom said, "I made spaghetti for lunch. Come and eat, my little actors." Ben and Lily ran to the kitchen. They loved spaghetti. It was their favorite food. But when they saw their plates, they were surprised. The spaghetti was not red, like usual. It was green, and it had little pieces of broccoli and cheese on it. "What is this?" Ben asked. "This is not spaghetti. This is yucky." Mom smiled. "This is a special spaghetti. It is good for you, and it tastes yummy. Try it, you might like it." Lily was adventurous. She liked to try new things. She picked up a fork and twirled some green spaghetti. She put it in her mouth and chewed. "Mmm, this is good," she said. "It is cheesy and crunchy. I like it." Ben was not adventurous. He did not like to try new things. He pushed his plate away and crossed his arms. "No, I don't want it. I want red spaghetti. This is not good." Mom sighed. "Ben, don't be so picky. You should be more like your sister. She is brave and curious. She likes to act and to eat new things. You are missing out on a lot of fun." Ben looked at Lily. She was smiling and eating her green spaghetti. She looked happy and satisfied. He felt a little jealous. Maybe he was missing out on something. Maybe he should be more adventurous, too. He picked up his fork and twirled some green spaghetti. He put it in his mouth and chewed. He made a face. He did not like it. It was cheesy and crunchy, but it was also bitter and weird. He spit it out and said, "Blech, this is bad. I don't like it. I want red spaghetti." Mom shook her head. "Ben, you are not acting. You are being rude. You should at least try to be polite and say thank you. And you should not waste food. There are children who have nothing to eat." Ben felt ashamed. He knew Mom was right. He was not acting. He was being rude. He did not want to be rude. He wanted to be a good actor. He wanted to make Mom happy. He picked up his fork again and twirled some green spaghetti. He put it in his mouth and chewed. He smiled and said, "Mmm, this is good. Thank you, Mom. You are the best cook." Mom smiled back. "You are welcome, Ben. You are a good actor. And a good son." Lily winked at Ben. She knew he was acting. She was proud of him. He was adventurous, after all.
