Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Cloud

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a friend named Shadow. Shadow was a deep, dark shape on the ground. Shadow liked to follow Lily everywhere she went. One sunny day, Lily and Shadow went to play outside. They played with a ball and had lots of fun. But then, a big cloud came and covered the sun. Shadow became very small and Lily could not see him well. Lily was sad and wanted her friend back. Lily had an idea. She asked the cloud to send the sun back out. She said, "Please, cloud, send the sun back so I can see my friend Shadow." The cloud heard Lily and moved away. The sun came out and Shadow became big and deep again. Lily was happy to have her friend back and they played together all day long.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little gray rabbit. The rabbit loved to play and balance on logs. One day, while he was playing, he met a big, fierce bear. "Hello, little rabbit," said the bear. "I see you can balance very well. Can you teach me how to balance too?" The rabbit was scared of the fierce bear, but he wanted to help. So he said, "Okay, big bear. I will teach you how to balance." The rabbit showed the bear how to balance on a log. The bear tried and tried, but he kept falling off. The bear got angry and roared, "I can't do it! This is too hard!" The rabbit knew the bear was upset, but he didn't want to give up. He said, "Don't worry, big bear. We can keep trying together. I know you can do it!" The bear listened to the rabbit and tried again. With the rabbit's help, the bear finally learned how to balance. They became good friends, and they played in the forest together every day.

One day, a little girl named Lily saw a big cloud in the sky. The cloud was dark and scary. Lily was scared, but she tried to ignore it. She wanted to play outside with her ball. As Lily played, the wind started to blow. The dark cloud got bigger and closer. Lily's mom came outside and said, "Lily, come inside now. There is a storm coming." But Lily ignored her mom and kept playing with her ball. Suddenly, there was a bright lightning in the sky. It was very loud and scary. Lily's ball rolled away and landed next to a sour lemon tree. Lily ran to get her ball, but she slipped on a sour lemon and fell down. She started to cry. Her mom came running to help her. "I told you to come inside, Lily," her mom said. "Now you are hurt and your ball is lost." Lily was sad and wished she had listened to her mom.

Once upon a time, there was a mysterious spider. The spider lived in a big tree. One day, the spider wanted to send a letter to his friend, the ant. The spider said, "Ant, can you help me send this?" The ant said, "Yes, I can help!" So, the ant took the letter and went away. The spider was happy. But then, something unexpected happened! The wind blew the letter away from the ant. The ant was sad. But the wind was really a magic bird! The magic bird took the letter and gave it to the ant's friend. The spider and the ant were very happy. And they all became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful rabbit named Bob. Bob loved to help his friends in the forest. One day, he heard his friend, the bird, cry. "Help me, please!" the bird requested. Bob hopped over to see what was wrong. The bird's wing was hurt, and he could not fly. Bob wanted to help him. Bob had an idea. He found a big leaf and some vines. He put the leaf on the bird's wing and tied it with the vines. The bird tried to fly, but he still could not. Just then, a big wind came and blew the leaf away. Under the leaf, the bird's wing was all better! The bird was so happy and thanked Bob for being so helpful. They both laughed and played together in the forest.
