Category: Intermediate
Title: The Curious Girl and the Rock

Once upon a time there was a curious girl. She was excited to go to the stadium for the first time. The stadium was full of people and the girl couldn't believe how many people were there. The girl noticed that everyone was rocking to the music. She loved the feeling and started to rock too. She rocked longer and longer and before she knew it, she was the only one rocking. Everyone around her had stopped. The girl felt silly and embarrassed. She wanted the ground to swallow her up. Everyone was staring at her and the girl started to cry. The girl vowed to never go to the stadium again. She was scared of feeling embarrassed like that ever again.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside. One sunny day, he went to the park to find his friends. As Spot walked, he saw a distant patch of grass. He thought, "That looks like a fun place to play!" So, he ran to the patch and found his friend, Bunny. "Hi, Bunny!" said Spot. "Do you want to play with me?" "Yes, Spot!" said Bunny. "Let's play!" So, Spot and Bunny played on the patch until the sun went down. They were very happy and tired. They went home, excited to play again the next day.

Once upon a time, there was an amazing queen. She lived in a big, pretty castle. The queen had a magic flower. The flower could talk when you touch it. One day, a small girl came to see the queen. The girl saw the magic flower. She wanted to touch it. The queen said, "Be careful. The flower can do something unexpected." The small girl touched the flower. The flower started to talk. But then, something unexpected happened. The flower turned the girl into a queen too! Now, there were two amazing queens in the big, pretty castle. They became best friends and had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved ice-cream more than anything in the world. One day, his mom told him that something incredible would happen tomorrow. The next day, Tim woke up very excited. He put on his shoes and ran outside to see what would happen. He saw a big, colorful truck driving down the street. The truck stopped right in front of his house. Tim's mom came outside with a big smile on her face. The truck door opened, and out came a man with lots of ice-cream! Tim's mom had invited the ice-cream man to their house. Tim and his friends got to eat all the ice-cream they wanted. The incredible thing his mom told him about had happened, and it was the best day ever!

Tim and Sam were best friends. They liked to play with cars and make zoom sounds. One day, they found a big cake on the table. It looked yummy and sweet. "Let's taste the cake," Tim said. "No one will know." Sam nodded. He was hungry and curious. They climbed on the chairs and took big bites of the cake. It was soft and creamy. They smiled and ate more. But then, they heard a loud voice. It was Mom. She was angry and sad. "What did you do?" she asked. "That was my birthday cake. I made it for myself. I was proud of it. Now it is ruined. You are naughty and selfish." Tim and Sam felt bad and scared. They tried to say sorry, but Mom did not listen. She sent them to their rooms without any toys or stories. They cried and wished they had not tasted the cake. They had lost their best friend and their mom's love.

Anna and Ben are friends. They go to the same school. They wear the same uniform. It is blue and white. One day, Anna falls down on the playground. She scrapes her knee. It is red and hurt. She cries. Ben runs to her. "Are you okay, Anna?" Ben asks. He is worried. "No, it hurt a lot," Anna says. She shows him her knee. "I have a band-aid in my backpack. I can lend it to you," Ben says. He is kind. "Thank you, Ben. You are a good friend," Anna says. She smiles. Ben goes to his backpack. He takes out a band-aid. He gives it to Anna. Anna puts it on her knee. It feels better. "Can I hug you, Ben?" Anna asks. "Of course, Anna. I like hugs," Ben says. They hug. They are happy. They go back to play. They are still friends.
