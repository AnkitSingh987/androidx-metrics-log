Category: Intermediate
Title: The Purple Cat and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a big purple cat. The cat was very selfish. It did not like to share its toys with other animals. One day, the purple cat met a little dog. The dog wanted to play with the cat's ball. The dog said, "Can I play with your ball, please?" But the selfish purple cat did not let the dog play with the ball. The cat wanted to keep the ball all to itself. Later, the purple cat saw the dog with a new friend, a small bird. The dog and the bird were playing together and having fun. The cat felt sad and alone. It decided to stand up and go to the dog and the bird. The purple cat said, "I am sorry for being selfish. Can I play with you?" The dog and the bird let the purple cat play with them. They all had fun together. The purple cat learned that sharing is good and being selfish is not nice. They all became good friends and played together every day.

Lily and Tom were at the park with their mom. They saw a truck that sold ice cream. Lily and Tom loved ice cream. They ran to the truck and asked their mom for some money. "OK, but only one cone each, and be careful not to drop it," their mom said. Lily and Tom each got a cone with two scoops of ice cream. Lily chose chocolate and vanilla. Tom chose strawberry and mint. They licked their cones happily. But then, a big dog came running towards them. It barked loudly and jumped on Tom. Tom was scared and dropped his cone. The dog ate his ice cream and ran away. Tom started to cry. He had no ice cream left. He felt helpless. He looked at Lily's cone. She still had her ice cream. "Can I have some of your ice cream, Lily?" Tom asked. Lily looked at her brother. She felt sorry for him, but she also liked her ice cream. She did not want to share. "No, this is mine. You should have been more careful," Lily said. Tom cried harder. He was hungry and sad. He wished he had his ice cream. Lily licked her cone. She felt happy for a moment, but then she felt bad. She saw Tom's face. He looked so unhappy. She remembered how they always played together and shared their toys. She felt something in her heart. She felt regret. She decided to do something nice. She broke her cone in half and gave one half to Tom. "Here, you can have this. I'm sorry I was mean," Lily said. Tom stopped crying. He smiled and took the cone. He thanked Lily and hugged her. They ate their cones together. They felt happy again. They learned to be kind and share. Their mom saw them and smiled too. She was proud of them.

John was walking in the park when he noticed a tall man with a ruler in his hand. The man was measuring the flowers in the garden. Intrigued, John ran over to him. John: "What are you doing, sir?" The man smiled. "I'm trying to find the truth, John. Every flower is unique, so I'm measuring them to find out which is the best one." John thought this was a wonderful idea. He ran off to gather some flowers. He carefully measured each one, using the ruler the man had given him. Finally, when John had measured all the flowers, he came back to the man. The man said, "Now, let's see which flower has the most unique measurement!" Together, they announced the winner. It was a beautiful yellow daffodil flower! John was so proud of his findings. The man smiled. "You have found the truth with your measuring. That is a wonderful gift!"

Tom and Lily liked to play near the river. They saw a big crane that lifted things from a boat. They wanted to see it better, so they walked closer to the water. "Be careful," said Lily. "The water is deep and fast. Don't go too near." "I promise," said Tom. "I just want to look at the crane. It is so big and strong." But Tom did not keep his promise. He went too near the water and slipped on a wet rock. He fell into the river and screamed for help. Lily ran to get help, but it was too late. The water was too deep and fast. Tom could not swim. He sank under the water and did not come up again. The crane did not see him. It kept lifting things from the boat. It did not know that Tom was gone.
