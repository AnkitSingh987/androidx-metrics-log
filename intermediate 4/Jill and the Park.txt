Category: Intermediate
Title: Jill and the Park

Jill was on a stroll through the park, when she spotted something interesting. As she looked around, she noticed a pigeon with a very colorful feather. She was so excited, she ran over to take a closer look. She asked the pigeon, "Are you lost?" The pigeon replied, "No, but I sure am pretty!" Jill was so taken aback by the bird's reply, she wondered what it could mean. The next day, Jill returned to the park with a bag of birdseed. She placed the seed in front of the pigeon and watched as more and more birds flew in to eat. Jill had had many pleasant conversations with the pigeon and noticed that it was now surrounded by a number of its feathered friends. She now realized what the pigeon meant when it said it was pretty; it was referring to the beauty of friendship. Jill smiled, pleased she could bring the pigeon such joy.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful farm. The farm had many animals. There was a kind farmer named Tom. He loved his animals very much. One day, a new animal came to the farm. It was a little duck. The duck was lost. Tom said, "Welcome to our farm!" The duck was happy. Tom gave the duck a tour around the farm. They saw cows, pigs, and chickens. The duck made new friends. The duck said, "Thank you, Tom! I love this beautiful farm." Tom was happy too. The animals played all day. They were all very happy. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a wise old frog lived near a big pond. He liked to keep the pond clean and safe for his friends. One day, a mosquito came to the pond. The mosquito was not nice. He liked to bite the other animals. "Please go away," said the wise old frog to the mosquito. "You are not nice to my friends." The mosquito did not listen. He kept biting the animals. The wise old frog had a plan. The wise old frog went to his friend, the big fish. "Can you help me?" asked the frog. The big fish said, "Yes, I can help you." The big fish swam fast and jumped out of the water. He caught the mosquito in his mouth. The other animals were happy and thanked the big fish. The wise old frog knew that sometimes, you need a friend to help you.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very good at cleaning his room. One day, while Tim was cleaning, he found a big bin. The bin was full of old toys. Tim decided to kneel down and look inside the bin. Tim saw a small, persistent dog toy. The dog toy would always come back when Tim threw it away. Tim thought it was fun. Tim said to the dog toy, "You are so persistent! You always come back to me." Tim and the dog toy played all day. They were very happy. At the end of the day, Tim put the dog toy back in the bin. Tim knew that the next time he cleaned his room, the persistent dog toy would be there to play with him again.

Once upon a time, there was a white cat named Fluffy. Fluffy liked to play in the yard. One day, Fluffy saw a row of pretty flowers. The flowers were red, blue, and yellow. Fluffy thought they were very nice. Fluffy's friend, a brown dog named Buddy, came to play. Buddy said, "Let's play near the flowers. But we must not spoil them." Fluffy agreed, and they played together near the row of pretty flowers. They had lots of fun. As they played, a little girl named Sue came outside. Sue saw Fluffy and Buddy playing near the flowers. She said, "Thank you for not spoiling my flowers. They are very pretty, and I like them a lot." Fluffy and Buddy were happy that they did not spoil the flowers, and they all played together in the yard.

One day, a little boy named Tim and a little girl named Sue went to play. They saw a big ball on a shelf. They wanted to play with the ball, but it was too high. Tim tried to reach the ball, but he was too small. "Let's help each other," said Sue. They stood on the floor, one on top of the other, and tried to reach the ball. They were calm and careful. But still, they could not reach the ball. Just then, a big dog named Max came in. He saw Tim and Sue trying to reach the ball. Max wanted to help. He jumped up and pushed the ball off the shelf. But, surprise! The ball was full of candy! The candy fell all over the floor. Tim, Sue, and Max laughed and ate the candy together. They had a fun day.
