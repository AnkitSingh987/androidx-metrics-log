Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Girl and the Father

Once upon a time there was a little girl who was very excited. Her father went to the market to buy a crocodile. When they arrived at the market, the little girl was so amazed. She saw so many crocodiles! Father asked one of the sellers, "How much do you sell crocodiles for?" The seller answered, "This one costs 5 coins." Father smiled and said, "I'll take it!" The little girl was so excited and she hugged the crocodile. She said, "I will take good care of it!" Father paid the money and they went home with the new crocodile. The little girl was the happiest she had ever been!

Once upon a time, there was a little rabbit named Bob. Bob loved to eat salad. Every day, he would hop to the garden and find the best salad to eat. One day, Bob felt uncomfortable. His tummy hurt because he ate too much salad. He wanted to rest, but he didn't know where to go. So, he asked his friend, the bird, for help. The bird showed Bob a soft and cozy place to rest under a big tree. Bob lay down and soon felt better. From that day on, Bob didn't eat too much salad and always knew where to rest when he was tired.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy old cat named Mr. Whiskers. He liked to complain a lot. He would say, "I don't like this toy" or "I don't want that food." One day, Mr. Whiskers heard a story about a magic fish that could make anyone happy. Mr. Whiskers wanted to find the magic fish, so he went on a long walk. He looked in the pond, under a big rock, and even up in a tree. After a long time, he found the magic fish in a small puddle. The fish said, "I can make you happy, but you must do something nice for someone else first." Mr. Whiskers thought about it and decided to help a little girl find her lost dog. When he found the dog, the girl was so happy! Mr. Whiskers felt good about helping her. He went back to the magic fish and told him the story. The magic fish smiled and said, "You see, you don't need my magic. You can be happy by doing good things for others." Mr. Whiskers learned his lesson and never complained again.

One day, Tim and Sam were playing in the park. They wanted to build something fun. Tim said, "Let's build a big toy gun!" Sam thought it was a great idea. They found sticks, leaves, and mud to make the toy gun. They worked together and made a big, healthy-looking gun. It was strong and they were proud of it. Tim held the gun and said, "Bang, bang!" Sam laughed and pretended to fall down. They played with their toy gun all day, having lots of fun in the park.

Once upon a time, a wealthy man named Tom lived in a big house. Tom had so many things that he wanted to see the whole universe. One day, he met a little bird named Blue. "Hi Blue," said Tom. "I want to see the universe. Can you help me escape this place?" "Sure, Tom," said Blue. "I can fly high and show you the sky." So, Tom and Blue went on an adventure. They saw the sun, the moon, and the stars. Tom was so happy to see the universe with his new friend. Blue was happy too because he could help Tom. The moral of the story is that even a wealthy person needs friends to help them escape and see the world. Friends make life better and more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was an original dog with a big round spot on his back. He loved to play with his friends in the park. One day, Spot saw a new dog in the park. The new dog was big and had no spots. Spot did not like the sight of the new dog. He thought the new dog was not like him, so he did not want to play with him. Spot told his friends that he hated the new dog. His friends did not understand why Spot felt this way. They wanted to play with the new dog too. Spot got very mad and ran away from his friends. He did not want to share his park with the new dog. As time went by, Spot stayed away from the park. He missed his friends and the fun they had together. One day, Spot saw his friends playing with the new dog. They were all happy and having a good time. Spot felt sad and alone. He realized that hating the new dog was not right. The moral of the story is that we should not hate others just because they are different from us. If we do, we might miss out on making new friends and having fun.
