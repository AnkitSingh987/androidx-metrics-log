Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Elevator

Once upon a time, there was a big building with an elevator. A boy named Tim was very enthusiastic about going on the elevator. He loved to watch the doors open and close. One day, Tim found a spray bottle near the elevator. He thought it would be fun to spray water on the buttons. "I will spray the buttons and see what happens," he said to his friend, Sam. As Tim sprayed the buttons, the elevator started to shake. The doors opened and closed very fast. Tim and Sam were scared. The elevator did not work right anymore. Tim was sad that his fun idea made the elevator break.

Once upon a time, there was a mild cat named Tom. Tom loved to visit his friends. One day, he went to visit his friend, a dog named Max. At Max's house, they played fun games. They ran and jumped outside. Then, they went inside to eat. Max's mom set a big table with yummy food. Tom and Max sat at the table. They ate and laughed. They had a great time. After they finished eating, they played more games. Tom was happy he visited Max.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was eager to find a gift for her best friend, Max. Max was a kind and funny dog who always made Sue laugh. They loved to play together. One day, Sue found a big, shiny ball. She thought Max would love it. She ran to Max and said, "Look, Max! I found a gift for you! Let's play with it!" Max wagged his tail and barked happily. He was excited to play with the new ball. Sue threw the ball and Max ran after it. They played all day long. Max knew he could trust Sue, and Sue knew she could trust Max. They were the best of friends, and they always had fun together. And they both loved the special gift Sue found.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had soft, brown fur and a big, happy smile. Every day, Spot would go with his friend, Sam, to collect the mail. One sunny day, Spot and Sam walked to the mailbox. Sam said, "Spot, let's get the mail together!" Spot barked happily and wagged his tail. They opened the mailbox and found a big, blue envelope. Sam took it out and they went back home. When they got home, Sam opened the envelope. Inside, there was a soft, fluffy toy for Spot! Spot was so happy and excited. He played with his new toy all day long. And from that day on, Spot and Sam always collected the mail together, hoping for more surprises.

Tom and Mia are friends who like to play outside. One day, they see a big hill with a tall tree on top. They want to climb the hill and see the tree. "Let's go, Tom!" Mia says. "Maybe we can see the whole town from up there!" "OK, Mia!" Tom says. "But be careful, the hill is steep and the tree is high!" They start to climb the hill, holding hands and helping each other. They are happy and excited. They hear a distant bell ringing from the church. They wonder what time it is. They reach the top of the hill and see the tree. It has green leaves and red apples. It looks yummy and pretty. "Wow, look at the tree, Mia!" Tom says. "Can we pick some apples?" "Sure, Tom!" Mia says. "But we have to stretch our arms and be gentle. We don't want to hurt the tree or fall down!" They stretch their arms and try to reach the apples. They pick some and put them in their pockets. They thank the tree and smile. They sit under the tree and eat the apples. They are sweet and juicy. They share and enjoy. They look at the town and see the church, the school, the park and their homes. They feel happy and grateful. They hear the bell ringing again. It sounds louder and closer. "Tom, Mia, it's time to go home!" They hear their moms calling from the bottom of the hill. "Come on, hurry up!" "OK, moms, we're coming!" Tom and Mia say. "We had a lot of fun!" They hold hands and run down the hill. They wave to the tree and say goodbye. They hug their moms and show them the apples. They tell them about their adventure. Their moms are proud and happy. They praise them for being brave, kind and respectful. They tell them they love them and take them home. The end. Moral value: The story teaches the value of friendship, curiosity, gratitude and respect for nature.
