Category: Intermediate
Title: The Man and the Boy

Once upon a time, there was an elderly man who lived in a small house. He had a big tree in his yard. One day, the elderly man saw a little boy trying to climb the tree. The little boy wanted to reach the top, but he was scared. As the little boy tried to climb higher, he slipped and started to fall. The elderly man ran to catch him. The boy landed in the man's arms, and they both fell down. The elderly man hurt his thumb, but he was happy he saved the little boy. The little boy said "Thank you" to the elderly man. They both learned that it is important to be careful when climbing trees, and to always help others when they are in trouble. The elderly man and the little boy became good friends, and they never forgot the day they learned to be careful and kind.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They took a big bag with them. Inside the bag, there was Tim's lunch. Tim was very hungry and could not wait to eat his lunch. At the park, Tim saw a big tree. He asked his mom, "Can I shake the tree? Please, Mom!" His mom said, "Okay, but be careful." Tim shook the tree, and something unexpected happened. A modern toy robot fell from the tree! Tim was very surprised. Tim picked up the robot and showed it to his mom. "Look, Mom! A robot was hiding in the tree!" Tim's mom smiled and said, "What a fun surprise! Now, let's eat your lunch." They sat under the tree, ate lunch, and played with the robot. Tim had the best day ever at the park.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big slide there. Tim was so happy! He wanted to slide down it all day. His mom said, "Be careful, Tim. You don't want to get hurt." Tim went up the slide and slid down fast. He felt like he was flying! But then, he saw a harmless bug. The bug was sitting on the slide, and Tim did not want to hurt it. So, he tried to stop, but he couldn't. He was going too fast! He closed his eyes and waited for the crash. But something unexpected happened. Instead of hitting the bug, Tim felt a weight on his head. It was a big hat! His mom had thrown it to him just in time. The hat covered the bug, and Tim slid right over it. The bug was safe, and Tim was happy. He learned that sometimes, even little things can make a big difference.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Billy. Billy lived in a small hedge near a big house. He loved his home, but he had a problem. There was a big, angry cat who lived in the big house. The cat would always demand that Billy leave the hedge. One day, Billy decided he had enough. He did not want to leave his home. So, he thought of a plan to make the cat go away. He asked his friend, the squirrel, for help. The squirrel agreed to help Billy. The next time the angry cat came to demand that Billy leave, the squirrel dropped nuts on the cat's head. The cat was so surprised, it ran away! From that day on, the cat never bothered Billy again. Billy was happy, and he lived in his hedge with no more problems.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his friends in the garage. They had many toys and games they would play together. Tim's favorite game was racing toy cars with his friends. One day, Tim and his friend, Sam, were having a competitive race with their toy cars. They would grab their cars and make them go fast. They laughed and had so much fun as they raced their cars around the garage. In the end, Tim's car won the race, but they both had a great time. They put their cars away and played with other toys. Tim and Sam knew that it was not about winning, but about having fun with friends in the garage.
