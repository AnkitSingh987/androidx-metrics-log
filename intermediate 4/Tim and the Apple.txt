Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Apple

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy switch. He would flip it up and down, making funny sounds. One day, Tim took his switch to the park to play. At the park, Tim saw a big tree with a delicious red apple. He wanted the apple so much. He tried to climb the tree, but he could not reach the apple. Tim was sad and sat down under the tree. Then, a friendly bird saw Tim and wanted to help. The bird flew up to the apple and gave it a little push. The apple started to fall down from the tree. Tim caught the apple and was very happy. He shared the delicious apple with the bird, and they became best friends. Tim and the bird played with the switch together and had a fun day at the park.

One day, a little boy named Tim got a fancy gift. It was a big box with a bow. He was so happy to receive it. But, his friend Sue did not get a gift. Sue was sad. Tim wanted to make Sue happy. He thought and thought. Then, he had an idea. He put the fancy gift under a tree. He told Sue to look under the tree. Sue found the gift and was happy. Tim and Sue played with the fancy gift together. They had lots of fun. The gift was not just for Tim, but for both of them. The moral of the story is to share with others and make them happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little monkey named Momo. Momo liked to play in the sun. One day, it rained and Momo got all wet. Momo did not like being wet. Momo wanted to be dry. Momo saw his friend, the big tree. "Tree, can you help me be dry?" asked Momo. The big tree said, "Yes, Momo! Come under my big leaves. The sun will make you dry here." Momo was happy and said, "Thank you, big tree!" After Momo was dry, he found a yummy banana. Momo ate the yummy banana and shared it with the big tree. They both were happy and had a fun day in the sun.

Once upon a time, there was a polite cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to help her friends. One day, her friend, Bunny, was not feeling well. Bunny had a big sneeze and a red nose. Kitty wanted to help Bunny feel better. Kitty said, "Bunny, you need a pill to feel good." Bunny nodded and said, "Yes, but I can't reach it. It's up high on the shelf." Kitty had an idea. She used her long tail to reach the pill on the shelf. Kitty gave the pill to Bunny. Bunny took the pill and said, "Thank you, Kitty. You are a good friend." Soon, Bunny felt better. They played and laughed all day. And from that day on, they were the best of friends.

Billy and his dad drove to a big park. Dad said, "Let's get out and go for a walk". Billy said, "Okay!" Billy and his dad stopped when they heard a loud sound. Dad said, "That must be an engine!" Billy asked, "What is an engine?" Dad said, "It is a nosy machine that rocks." They went to the playground to see the engine. Billy saw it rocking back and forth. He was so excited to see it. It was noisy and dad said it was too loud. Billy tried to get closer but he tripped on a rock and fell hard into the engine. Dad rushed over to help him but it was too late. Billy was hurt bad and they called the ambulance. The ambulance whisked Billy away to the hospital and Dad followed. Later, the doctors told Dad that Billy had broken his arm. Dad was very sad and wished he had been more nosy about the engine.

One day, a sweet cat named Kitty went to the library. She loved to look at the books with numbers. Kitty wanted to learn how to count. In the library, Kitty met a dog named Doggy. Doggy also loved numbers. They both wanted the same book about numbers. This made them sad because they both wanted to learn. Kitty and Doggy decided to share the book. They sat down and read the number book together. They learned how to count and became good friends. They went to the library every day to learn more numbers and read other books.
