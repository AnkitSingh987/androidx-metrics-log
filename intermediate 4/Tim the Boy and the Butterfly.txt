Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Butterfly

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a light, pretty butterfly. Tim wanted to catch it. He tried and tried, but he could not catch it. Tim saw his friend Sue. Sue said, "I can help you catch it! We need a net." So, they went to find a net. They looked and looked, but they could not find one. Then, they had an idea. They would make a net. They made a net out of sticks and string. They were ready to catch the butterfly. They went back to the park. They saw the butterfly again. They were very quiet. They got close. They got ready to catch it. "One, two, three!" said Sue. They tried to catch the butterfly with the net, but surprise! It was not a butterfly. It was a bee! The bee was not happy. It stung Tim and Sue. They cried and ran home. They learned that not all surprises are good, and they should be more careful next time.

Once upon a time, there was a prince who loved to spin. He would spin all day and all night. One day, he found a rare, shiny stone in his garden. "Look at my rare stone!" said the prince to his friend, the cat. The cat looked at the stone and said, "Let's spin it and see what happens!" So, the prince and the cat started to spin the rare stone. As they spun it, something unexpected happened. The stone turned into a big, colorful ball. "Wow!" said the prince. "I didn't know my rare stone could do that!" The prince and the cat played with the big ball all day, and they were very happy. And from that day on, they knew that sometimes, when you spin something rare, magical things can happen.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved playing outside in the dirt. One day, he found a dirty envelope on the ground. Tim was curious and wanted to know what was inside the envelope. He took the envelope to his mom and asked her to help him unlock the secret inside. His mom carefully opened the envelope and found a pretty picture of a rainbow. Tim was so happy to see the colorful rainbow. From that day on, Tim always looked for more envelopes when he played outside. He hoped to find more surprises and unlock more secrets. And every time he found one, he would run to his mom, and they would open it together.

Lily was a shy girl who liked to play with her dolls and draw pictures. She did not like to talk to other kids or try new things. One day, her mom told her they were going to visit her aunt and uncle, who had a big farm with many animals and plants. Lily was scared and did not want to go. She hid under her bed and said, "No, no, no! I want to stay home!" Her mom said, "Come on, Lily, hurry up! We have to go now. It will be fun, I promise. You will see many things and make new friends." Lily did not believe her mom, but she had no choice. She packed her bag and got in the car. She felt sad and nervous. When they arrived at the farm, Lily saw a big dog, a fluffy cat, a noisy rooster, and a friendly cow. She also saw a garden with many fruits and vegetables, and a tree with green and black olives. Her aunt and uncle welcomed them with hugs and smiles. "Hello, Lily, we are so happy to see you!" they said. "Do you want to see the farm and help us with some chores?" Lily shook her head and clung to her mom. She did not want to see anything or help anyone. She wanted to go back home. Her mom said, "Lily, don't be shy. Your aunt and uncle are very nice and they have many things to show you. Why don't you try one of these olives? They are delicious and healthy." Lily looked at the olives and wrinkled her nose. She did not like how they looked or smelled. She said, "No, thank you. I don't like olives." Her aunt said, "That's okay, Lily. You don't have to eat them if you don't want to. But maybe you can help me pick some for our salad. It's easy and fun. You just have to reach up and twist them off the branch. Do you want to try?" Lily was curious and a little bit brave. She nodded and followed her aunt to the olive tree. She reached up and picked an olive. It felt smooth and soft in her hand. She smiled and picked another one. She liked how they looked and felt. Her aunt said, "Good job, Lily! You are a great helper. Do you want to taste one now?" Lily thought for a moment and decided to try one. She bit into it and tasted the salty and sour juice. She liked it and ate the whole olive. She said, "Mmm, this is good. Can I have another one?" Her aunt said, "Of course, Lily. You can have as many as you like. I'm glad you like them. You see, sometimes trying new things can be fun and yummy. You just have to be brave and open-minded." Lily agreed and thanked her aunt. She felt happy and proud. She realized that her mom was right. It was fun to visit the farm and see new things and make new friends. She decided to try more things and be less shy. She learned that being brave and open-minded can make her life more exciting and enjoyable.
