Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Elephant

One day, a girl named Mia went to the zoo with her mom. They saw many animals like big lions, tall giraffes, and funny monkeys. Mia was very happy to observe all the animals. She saw an enormous elephant and said, "Mom, look at that big elephant! Can we go see it?" Her mom said, "Yes, let's go!" They walked to the elephant place. But when they got there, the enormous elephant was gone! Mia and her mom were very surprised. They looked around and saw that other animals were gone too. They saw a zoo worker and asked, "Where are the animals?" The zoo worker smiled and said, "Today is a special day. The animals are having a big party in a secret place. They will come back soon." Mia and her mom laughed and went to see the other animals. They had a great day at the zoo, and Mia couldn't wait to tell her friends about the special animal party.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a favorite tie that he loved to wear. But one day, the tie got a big rip. Tim was very sad. Tim's mom saw how sad he was and thought of a way to make him happy again. She told Tim that she would try to fix his tie. Tim was hopeful that his mom could restore his tie. Tim's mom worked very hard to fix the tie. She sewed and sewed until the rip was gone. When she was done, she gave the tie back to Tim. He was so grateful to have his favorite tie back. He wore it all the time and always remembered how his mom helped him. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a charming boy named Tim found a big box. The box had a picture of a rocket on it. Tim was very happy. He wanted to play with the rocket. He asked his mom, "Can I play with the rocket, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but we have to order the parts first." Tim and his mom ordered the parts for the rocket. They waited for the parts to come. A few days later, the parts came in a big truck. Tim and his mom opened the box. But, surprise! There were no rocket parts inside. Instead, there were lots of balloons and a note. The note said, "We sent you balloons to make you smile. Enjoy!" Tim and his mom laughed. They played with the balloons all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a big ball that she liked to roll. One day, Sue found a sheet in her room. The sheet was tough and strong. Sue had an idea. She asked her friend Tom, "Do you want to play with me?" Tom said, "Yes!" So, they went outside with the ball and the sheet. Sue and Tom put the sheet on the ground and rolled the ball on it. They laughed and had fun. But then, the ball rolled too fast and went into the street. Sue and Tom ran after it without looking. A car came and honked, but it was too late. The ball was flat and Sue and Tom were sad. The moral of the story is to always be careful and look before you run into the street.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot liked to sit in the sun all day. He would sit and sit and sit. One day, it was very hot outside. Spot did not know it was so hot. Spot went outside to sit in the sun. After a while, he started to feel terrible. He was too hot from the sun. Spot did not like this feeling. He wanted to find a way to feel better. Spot saw a big tree with lots of shade. He thought, "If I sit under the tree, I will not feel so hot." So, Spot went to sit under the tree. The shade made him feel much better. Now, Spot knew that on hot days, he should sit under the tree instead of in the sun.
