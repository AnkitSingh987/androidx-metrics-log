Category: Intermediate
Title: Speedy the Car and the Cone

One day, a little car named Speedy was driving down the wet road. He saw a big cone in the middle of the road. Speedy thought it was a fun toy, so he wanted to play with it. He drove faster and faster, wanting to hit the cone. Just then, a big truck named Biggie saw Speedy racing towards the cone. Biggie said, "Speedy, stop! The cone is there to keep us safe. You must not hit it." Speedy slowed down and stopped just before hitting the cone. He felt sad and said, "I'm sorry, Biggie. I didn't know the cone was there to help us. I will be more careful next time." Biggie smiled and said, "It's okay, Speedy. We all make mistakes. The important thing is to learn from them and do better next time." From that day on, Speedy always followed the rules and was more careful on the wet road. He learned that it's important to listen to others and follow the rules to stay safe.

One day, a little girl named Mia was very confused. She did not know what to do for lunch. She asked her mom, "Mom, what can we have for lunch?" Her mom said, "Let's perform a lunch dance, and maybe we will get an idea!" Mia and her mom danced around the kitchen, laughing and spinning. They danced until they were tired. After the dance, Mia's mom had an idea. "Let's make a yummy lunch with bread, cheese, and fruit!" So they made their lunch together and had a fun time eating it. Mia was not confused anymore, and she was happy that they performed the lunch dance.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy cat named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with his best friend, a little girl named Sue. One sunny day, Sue wanted to play outside with Tim. "Tim, let's play ball!" Sue said. They went outside and Sue gave Tim a big, soft ball. Tim was so sleepy, but he wanted to play with Sue. He tried to kick the ball with his little paw. The ball went up and down, up and down. Sue laughed and clapped her hands. "Good job, Tim!" she said. They played until the sun went down. Tim was very sleepy now. Sue picked him up and gave him a hug. "Time for bed, Tim," she said. They went inside, and Tim fell asleep with a happy look on his face.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big red ball. One day, she went to the park to play with her ball. At the park, Lily met a confused dog named Max. Max saw Lily's ball and wanted to play too. "Can I play?" asked Max. "Welcome!" said Lily. They played together and had lots of fun. After a while, Lily wanted to go home. She tried to carry her ball, but it was too big for her. Max wanted to help, but he was too small. They tried and tried, but they could not carry the ball together. Lily was sad, and Max was sad too. They had to leave the big red ball in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to count things. She would count her toys, her books, and even the steps she took. One day, Amy's mom asked her to help with something. She wanted Amy to count all the thin sticks in the big box. Amy was very excited to help. Amy's mom told her that they would use the thin sticks to make a big surprise. Amy started to count the sticks. One, two, three, four, and so on. When she was done, she told her mom that there were twenty thin sticks. Her mom was very happy with her help. The next day, Amy's mom showed her the big surprise. She had used the thin sticks to make a big art piece for the refrigerator. It was a picture of Amy and her family. Amy was so happy to see the surprise. She knew that her counting had helped her mom make the beautiful art piece. And from that day on, Amy loved to count even more, knowing that her counting could help make wonderful things.
