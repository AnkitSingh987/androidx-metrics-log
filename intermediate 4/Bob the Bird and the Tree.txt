Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a bird named Bob. Bob lived in a nest high up in a tree. One day, Bob saw that his nest was too tight. He wanted to make it bigger and better. So, he flew around to find more sticks and leaves. As Bob was looking for sticks, he saw his friend, Sally the squirrel. Sally was sad because she had no one to celebrate her birthday with. Bob had an idea. He asked Sally to help him make the nest bigger, and then they could celebrate her birthday together in the new nest. They worked hard and made the nest bigger. But then, something unexpected happened. The wind blew very strong and took the nest away. Bob and Sally were sad. But they learned that it is more important to celebrate and be happy with friends than to have a big nest. They hugged each other and celebrated Sally's birthday under the tree, and they were happy.

Once upon a time, there was an intelligent boy named Tim. He loved to look at tiny things with his toy called a microscope. He could see things that were too small for his eyes alone. He liked to learn about the world around him. One day, Tim found a piece of paper on the ground. He decided to fold the paper into a little boat. He took the boat to the pond and watched it float on the water. As it floated away, a little frog jumped onto the boat and rode with it. Tim was very happy to see the frog enjoying the boat he made. Soon, other kids saw what Tim did and started folding their own paper boats. The pond became a happy place for all the kids and the frogs. The moral of the story is that when we use our intelligence to create something, we can bring joy to others and ourselves.

One day, a small dog named Max was playing outside. Max saw a big shower of rain coming. He felt confused. He did not like to get wet. Max ran to his friend, the big tree. Max said, "Tree, can you help me? The rain is coming, and I do not want to get wet." The tree did not say anything. Max was sad. He did not know what to do. Then, Max heard a snap. He looked around and saw a big branch fall from the tree. The branch hit Max, and he fell down. Max was hurt and wet from the rain. The rain did not stop, and Max stayed wet and sad.

Anna liked to play with her toys in her room. She had many toys, but her favorite was a red rocket. She liked to make it fly in the air and make zoom noises. She also liked to keep her room tidy, because her mom said that was good. One day, Anna had a visitor. It was her cousin Leo, who came from far away. He was three years old, like Anna, but he had never seen a rocket before. He knocked on Anna's door and said hello. "Hi, Leo," Anna said. "Do you want to play with me?" "Yes, please," Leo said. "What do you have?" Anna smiled and showed him her rocket. "This is my rocket. It is red and shiny. It can fly very fast and go to the moon. Do you like rockets?" Leo looked at the rocket with big eyes. He had never seen anything like it. He reached out his hand and touched it gently. "Wow, it is cool. Can I hold it?" "Sure, you can," Anna said. "But be careful. It is fragile. And don't make a mess. I like my room tidy." Leo nodded and took the rocket. He felt its smooth surface and its pointy tip. He lifted it up and pretended to fly it. He made zoom noises like Anna. He liked the rocket very much. He said thank you to Anna. "You're welcome, Leo," Anna said. "I'm glad you like my rocket. Do you want to be my friend?" "Yes, I do," Leo said. "You are very nice. And you have a cool rocket. Can I introduce you to my teddy bear? He is brown and soft. He likes rockets too." Anna nodded and smiled. She liked Leo too. He was very polite and fun. She wanted to meet his teddy bear. She said yes to Leo. They went to Leo's room and played with the rocket and the teddy bear. They had a lot of fun. They introduced their toys to each other and made up stories. They became good friends. They shared their rocket and their teddy bear. They also kept their rooms tidy. Their moms were proud of them.
