Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a good boy who liked to help his friends. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, had to take an exam. Sam was very scared and asked Tim for help. Tim said, "Don't worry, Sam! I will help you part the things you need to learn. We will make a useful plan together." So, Tim and Sam sat down and made a plan. They talked about the things Sam needed to learn for the exam. They worked hard and had fun. On the day of the exam, Sam was not scared anymore. He knew that he had worked hard and had a good friend like Tim to help him. Sam did very well on the exam. The moral of the story is that when you help your friends, good things can happen.

Once there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat spaghetti. One day, Tim was very sad because there was no spaghetti in his house. Tim's mom saw that he was sad. She said, "Let's go to the store and buy some spaghetti!" Tim was happy again. They got in the car and drove to the store. At the store, Tim and his mom found the spaghetti. They put a lot of spaghetti in the cart. They also found sauce and cheese. They paid and went back to the car. Tim's mom helped him load the spaghetti into the car. They went home and cooked the spaghetti. Tim was not sad anymore. He was very happy to eat his favorite food.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a boy named Tim. Tim was very creative. He loved to draw and play with his toys. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, we are going to a new place to eat today. They have a big menu with lots of yummy food!" When they got to the new place, Tim looked at the menu. He saw many things he wanted to try, but he could not resist the big, yummy ice cream. "Mom, can I have the ice cream, please?" Tim asked. His mom said, "Yes, but you have to eat your food first." Tim ate his food very fast. He was so excited for the ice cream. But when the ice cream came, it was not like any ice cream Tim had ever seen. It was green and blue and had a funny shape. Tim was surprised, but he tried it anyway. It was the best ice cream he had ever had! He learned that sometimes, new and different things can be very fun and yummy.

Once upon a time there was a girl named Lucy. She was very happy and she loved to play. One morning Lucy went outside to play in the yard. Suddenly she saw a big cloud of dust in the air. It made Lucy sneeze and cough. Frightened, she ran inside to find her mom. "Mom, there's so much dust outside," said Lucy. Mom quickly looked outside. Then she told Lucy to stay inside and not leave until the dust was gone. Lucy was sad, but she did as she was told. Every day she stayed inside, waiting for the dust to go away. She was so grateful when it finally did. When Lucy left the house, she saw a big mess. The dust had made her backyard look dirty and sad. Lucy started to clean up all the dirt. Suddenly she heard a loud noise. She looked around and saw a big monster. It said, "I told you to leave, but you didn't listen. Now it's too late!" The monster chased Lucy back home. Lucy was scared and ran inside. She never went outside again.

Once upon a time, there was a kind girl named True. She loved to study her big book of animals. One day, she saw a small bird on the ground. It was not moving. True felt sad and said, "Oh no, the bird is dead." True's friend, Ben, came over and saw the bird too. He asked, "What happened, True?" True told him, "The bird is dead. I want to help it, but I don't know how." Ben thought for a moment and said, "Let's study our books and find a way to help the bird." True and Ben studied their books and found a way to make a cozy nest for the bird. They put the bird in the nest and waited. After a while, the bird started to move! True and Ben were very happy. They said, "Goodbye, bird! We are glad we could help you!" The bird flew away, and True and Ben went back to studying their books, proud of their good deed.
