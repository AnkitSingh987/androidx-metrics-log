Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Bear

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play with her toys. One day, Lucy went to visit her aunt's house. Her aunt lived in a big, red house. Lucy was very excited to see her aunt. When Lucy got to her aunt's house, her aunt said, "Hello Lucy! How are you?" Lucy replied, "I am good, auntie! Can I borrow a toy?" Her aunt smiled and said, "Of course, Lucy! You can play with any toy you like." Lucy found a big, soft teddy bear to play with. But the teddy bear was uncomfortable to hold. Lucy said, "Auntie, this bear is not nice to hold. Can I borrow another toy?" Her aunt said, "Sure, Lucy. Let's find a better toy for you." They looked for a new toy together. Lucy found a small, soft bunny that was very comfortable to hold. She was very happy with her new toy. Lucy and her aunt played together all day long. They had so much fun at the big, red house.

Jack ran into the garden and there was much joyousness. Everything around him seemed full of delight. He saw a big messy bulb on the ground and he demanded to bring it home. His mom saw him with the bulb and said "No Jack, you can't take it home. It's too big and messy". Jack kept demanding to take it home, so his mom went looking for a smaller bulb to replace it with. She searched and searched and eventually found one she thought was perfect. Jack was delighted and took it home. As he cleaned it off, he imagined all sorts of things he could do with it. He was a happy boy!

Once upon a time, there was a gray cat named Tom. Tom lived on a big hill. Every day, Tom would play and run up and down the hill. He liked to wrap himself in a soft, red blanket when he was tired. One day, while Tom was playing on the hill, he saw a big, gray box. Tom was curious, so he went to look at the box. He tried to wrap his paws around it, but it was too big. Tom pushed the box down the hill to his house. When Tom got the box to his house, he found a way to open it. Inside the box, he found a big, gray elephant! The elephant was very friendly, and Tom was happy to have a new friend. They played together on the hill and wrapped themselves in Tom's red blanket when they were tired. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby shrimp. The shrimp lived in a big blue sea. The chubby shrimp liked to swim and play with his friends, the little fish. One day, the chubby shrimp saw a big fish. The big fish said, "Hello, chubby shrimp! Can you repeat what I say?" The chubby shrimp said, "Yes, I can repeat. What do you want me to say?" The big fish said, "Swim with me and be my friend." The chubby shrimp was happy to have a new friend. He repeated, "Swim with me and be my friend." But then, something unexpected happened. The big fish started to chase the chubby shrimp! The chubby shrimp was scared and swam away fast. The little fish saw what happened and came to help. They all swam together and made the big fish go away. The chubby shrimp learned that sometimes big friends can be scary. From that day on, the chubby shrimp only played with his little fish friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a dry computer in his room. It was standing on a table. Tim didn't know what to do with it. So, he called his friend, Sam. "Sam, I found a dry computer. What do I do?" Tim asked. Sam said, "Let's put it on the floor and stand next to it. Maybe it will work." Tim and Sam stood next to the computer. They pressed some buttons, but it didn't work. They were sad. Then, Tim's mom came into the room. She saw the dry computer and smiled. "Don't worry, boys. We can fix it," she said. Tim and Sam were happy. They knew they would have fun with the computer soon.
