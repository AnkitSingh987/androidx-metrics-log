Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Lovely Girl and the Tim

Once upon a time, there was a lovely little girl named Sue. She had a best friend named Tim. They liked to play together every day. Sue and Tim were very happy friends. One day, Sue found some gum. She liked the gum a lot. Sue said to Tim, "Look, I have gum! I want to share with you." But Tim wanted all the gum for himself. Sue and Tim began to quarrel. They were both sad. Then, Sue's mom came and said, "Why are you quarreling? You should share the gum and be happy friends again." Sue and Tim agreed. They shared the gum and played together. They were best friends again, and they were very happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a red wagon in his yard. He wanted to make it look silly. So, he put a big hat on the wagon and drew a funny face on it. Tim was very happy with his silly wagon. Tim pulled the silly wagon around the yard. He saw his friend, Sue, and said, "Hi Sue! Look at my silly wagon!" Sue laughed and said, "That's very silly, Tim! Can I help you make it even sillier?" Tim said, "Yes, let's make it the silliest wagon ever!" Tim and Sue put more funny things on the wagon. They added a long scarf, big sunglasses, and a toy bird on top. They laughed and played with the silly wagon all day long. The silly wagon made them very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She wanted to go for a walk and so she put on her favourite pink shoes and marched outside. As she marched down the street, she saw a strange figure up ahead. It looked a little like an animal, but it was made from sticks and twigs. She couldn't tell what it was. She plucked up the courage to march closer and when she did, she saw that the figure was dead. It was a poor little bird that had flown too near the window and hit it. The girl was so sad that she started to cry. She said to the poor bird, "Why did you have to leave so soon?". But the bird didn't answer. The only sound was the wind blowing through the trees. The little girl felt brave again and marched away with a heavy heart. She knew that she'd always remember the poor bird, as sad as it was.

Lily and Ben were going on a big trip with Mom and Dad. They had to pack their bags and take their passports. A passport was a small book with their names and pictures in it. They needed it to go on the plane and see new places. The day they left, it was raining hard. Lily and Ben looked out the window and saw big drops of water falling from the sky. They heard thunder and saw lightning. They were scared and excited at the same time. "Will the rain stop soon?" Lily asked Mom. "I hope so, honey. We don't want to miss our flight," Mom said. "Can we play in the rain?" Ben asked Dad. "Maybe later, buddy. We have to get to the airport first," Dad said. They put on their coats and hats and got in the car. Mom drove carefully and Dad checked the time. They listened to music and sang along. They hoped the rain would stop soon. When they got to the airport, the rain had stopped. They saw a big rainbow in the sky. It had many colors and looked incredible. Lily and Ben pointed and smiled. "Wow, look at the rainbow!" Lily said. "It's so pretty!" Ben said. "It's a good sign. It means we'll have a wonderful trip," Mom said. They got out of the car and took their bags and passports. They walked to the big building where the planes were. They were ready for their big trip. They couldn't wait to see new places and have fun. They were happy and curious. They thanked the rainbow for the incredible gift.

Once upon a time there was a family of bears who lived happily in the woods. One day, the father bear could hear a noise outside. The noise was coming from the icy cold war that had just begun. The father bear opened the door and said to the other bears, "We must help the others and enjoy the war". The mother bear said, "But why should we enjoy something so icy and cold?". The father bear replied, "Because by helping, we are keeping everyone safe from harm." The bears all nodded in agreement and went out to join the war. Working together, the family soon saw that their efforts had made a difference. The bears returned home in the evening, exhausted but happy that they had enjoyed their icy cold mission. And ever since that day, the bears were remembered as heroes with noble hearts.
