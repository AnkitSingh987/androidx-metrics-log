Category: Intermediate
Title: Lila and Tom and the Fish

Lila and Tom went to the lake with their dad. They wanted to catch some fish. Dad gave them each a rod and a hook. He showed them how to tie a worm on the hook. "Be careful with the hook," he said. "It can hurt you if you touch it." Lila and Tom nodded. They threw their lines into the water and waited. They saw some fish swimming near the surface. They hoped the fish would like their worms. Suddenly, Tom felt a tug on his line. He pulled hard and lifted his rod. He had caught a big fish! "Look, Lila, look!" he shouted. "I got a fish!" Lila looked and clapped. She was happy for Tom. She wanted to catch a fish too. But then, Tom saw something wrong. The hook had gone through the fish's eye. The fish was bleeding and flapping. It looked hurt and scared. Tom felt sorry for the fish. He did not want to hurt it. He wanted to let it go. "Dad, dad, can I let the fish go?" he asked. "It is hurt. I don't want it." Dad came over and saw the fish. He nodded. "Okay, Tom, you can let the fish go," he said. "You are a kind boy. Sometimes fishing can hurt the fish. We have to be gentle with them." He helped Tom take the hook out of the fish's eye. He put the fish back in the water. The fish swam away. Tom felt better. He smiled at Lila. "Maybe we can catch another fish," he said. "But not hurt it." Lila smiled back. "Okay," she said. "Let's try again."

Once upon a time, there was a big, hairy dog. The dog liked to run and play with his friends, but he had a problem. His skin was very itchy, and he was always trying to turn and scratch it. One day, the dog met a cat. The cat was not nice. The cat saw the dog's itchy skin and thought it was funny. The cat said, "I will help you, but you have to give me your favorite toy." The dog was sad, but he wanted to stop the itch, so he gave the cat his toy. The cat did not help the dog. The cat took the toy and ran away. The dog was very sad and still itchy. The dog learned that not everyone is nice and that he should be careful who he trusts.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the market with her mom. She was excited because she loved to see all the colorful fruits and vegetables. Lily always wanted to help her mom pick the best ones. At the market, Lily saw a man selling seeds. "Mom, can we buy some seeds?" she asked. "Sure," her mom said, "What do you want to plant?" Lily thought for a moment and said, "I want to plant flowers!" When they got home, Lily and her mom went to the backyard. "Now, we need to bury the seeds in the ground," her mom told her. Lily carefully dug small holes and dropped the seeds in. She covered them with dirt and watered them with a big smile. Every day, Lily checked her flowers. She was so happy when they started to grow. Soon, the backyard was full of beautiful, colorful flowers. Lily knew that she helped make something amazing.

Once upon a time, there was a regular cat. The cat lived in a small house with a nice girl. The girl loved the cat very much. They played together every day. One day, the girl wanted to surprise the cat. She hid behind a big chair and waited for the cat to come. When the cat walked by, the girl jumped out and said, "Surprise!" The cat was so happy and they both laughed. The girl and the cat had lots of fun together. They learned that making others happy is a good thing to do. The moral of the story is to always be kind and make others smile.

One day, a charming little cat named Mimi found a big frame in her yard. Mimi was very curious and wanted to know what was inside the frame. She tried to push it, but it was too heavy for her. Mimi was sad and asked her friend, a small bird named Chirpy, for help. Chirpy said, "Don't worry, Mimi! I will help you release what's inside the frame." Chirpy pushed the frame with all her might, and suddenly, the frame opened. They were both very excited to see what was inside. To their surprise, a big, colorful ball came out of the frame. Mimi and Chirpy were so happy to see the ball. They played with the ball all day long, forgetting about their earlier conflict. The ball brought them closer together, and they became best friends forever.
