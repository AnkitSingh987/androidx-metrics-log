Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Shoes

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Lily. She was three years old and loved the beach. Lily went to the beach with her mom and dad. When she got there, Lily saw the waves crashing against the shore. She kicked off her shoes, jumped over the waves, and splashed into the water. “Wow, this is so much fun!” Lily said. Lily’s mom smiled and said, “That’s fantastic! Now, why don’t you make a poem about the beach?” Lily thought for a moment and then she started to write. “The waves are big and the sand is tidy…” Lily said as she wrote. When Lily finished the poem, she showed it to her mom. Her mom smiled and said, “What a lovely poem, Lily! You are so creative!” Lily was very happy. She continued to play in the sand and the water. She made castles, splashed around, and enjoyed the day. At the end of the day, Lily was all cleaned up and ready to go home. It had been a perfect day at the beach.

James was worried about jumping in the closet. He didn't want to do it but his friend said it would be fun. He asked his mum if he could jump in the closet and when she said yes, he got very excited. James ran over to the closet and put his hands on the handle. He paused a moment, feeling nervous. Then he took a deep breath and opened the door. He looked hesitantly inside but saw nothing but darkness. He was scared, but his friend said it would be fun so he mustered up his courage and jumped. He was shocked when he felt something soft under his feet. He reached into the darkness and pulled out a warm, furry blanket. It was a new blanket his mum had just bought for him! He jumped from the closet and hugged the blanket. He giggled with delight and thanked his mum for always taking care of him. From then on, James was never worried about the closet again.

Once upon a time, in a little house, there lived a girl named Sue. Sue loved to eat waffles. One day, she had a creative idea. She wanted to decorate her waffles to make them look fun and tasty. So, she asked her mom for help. Together, they used yummy things like fruit, syrup, and whipped cream to make the waffles look pretty. They made faces, flowers, and even a little dog on the waffles. Sue was so happy with her creative waffles. Sue shared her decorated waffles with her friends. They all loved them and had fun making their own creative waffles too. The moral of the story is that when you share your creative ideas with others, it brings happiness and fun to everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a big, white sheep. He had lots of wool. Every day, the sheep enjoyed eating grass and running around the farm. A little boy saw the sheep and thought it was great. He wanted to play with it. One day, the boy went up to the sheep and said, “Hello, sheep! Let’s have some fun.” The boy started to tease the sheep with his hand. The sheep laughed and the boy laughed too. They played together for hours. The boy thought it was so much fun that he wanted to take some of the sheep's wool. He pulled some off with his hands and ran away, laughing. The sheep didn't like that, so he followed the boy and tried to get his wool back. The boy laughed and kept running. Finally, the sheep gave up. The boy kept the wool and made a great scarf for his mother. She was so happy! She said, “Thank you for the lovely wool, my boy.” The boy smiled, glad that he had made his mom so happy. And he never teased the sheep again.

One day, a brown dog named Spike went for a walk. He saw a little girl with a big red ball. The girl said, "Hi Spike, do you want to play with me?" Spike was happy and wagged his tail. They played with the ball all day. The sun was hot and they were tired. The girl said, "Spike, I am thirsty. Let's go find some water." Spike barked and they went to look for water. They found a small pond. The girl drank the water and said, "Now I am not thirsty. Thank you, Spike!" Spike was happy that he could help the girl. They played some more and had a fun day.
