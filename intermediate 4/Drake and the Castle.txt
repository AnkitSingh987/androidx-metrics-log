Category: Intermediate
Title: Drake and the Castle

Drake was very excited to go to the beach. He was 3 years old, and never been to the beach before. He was so thrilled, he ran around his house, singing and hopping with joy. Mama, who was also excited, said: "Drake, let's pack your stuff and go!" They put all the things in the car, and drove together to the beach. When they arrived, the sun was already shining high, and the water was sparkling. "It's so cool here, Mama!" Drake said, amazed. Mama smiled and gave him a big hug. She said: "You know what? You can take your toys and build a sand castle." She gave Drake a small tip and he ran away, heading for the water. He was laughing and shouting, as he made the biggest castle he ever seen. At the end of the day, Drake's castle was still standing, and it was even cooler than it was in the morning. Mama and Drake enjoyed the beach until the sun started to set. They hugged each other and went home, with many nice memories.

Jim had a tent in the forest. He was happy living out there by himself. One day, he was feeling tired, so he lay down in the tent for a nap. Suddenly, he heard a loud shout. He opened his eyes and looked out, and he saw a pale man who was looking right at him. The pale man shouted again. He said, "What are you doing in my tent?" Jim was scared, but he was also angry that someone had disturbed his rest. He shouted back, "This isn't your tent! It's mine!" The pale man was surprised. He looked around and said, "This is my forest! All the tents here belong to me!" Jim was even more angry now and he shouted louder than ever, “I don’t care! This tent is mine and I’m not leaving!” The pale man frowned. He said in a low voice, “We’ll see about that.” Then he walked away. Jim breathed a sigh of relief and closed his eyes, hoping the man would stay away.

One day, a nosy cat went for a walk. The cat liked to tell stories to his friends. Today, he saw a big tree with a lot of shade under it. He thought it was a good place to rest and tell a story. In the shade, the cat saw a little bird. The cat said, "Hello, bird! I want to tell you a story." The bird was happy to listen. The cat told a story about a big, strong cat who was friends with all the animals. While the cat was telling the story, a big wind came. The wind blew the cat and the bird up into the sky. They were scared, but then something unexpected happened. They started to fly together, just like in the cat's story. The cat and the bird became best friends. They flew all around, telling stories to each other. They learned that even nosy cats and little birds can have big adventures together. And from that day on, they always shared their stories under the big tree in the shade.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. They saw the blue water, the big sky, and lots of sand. Tim was very happy to play in the sand. He made a little hill with his hands and then he saw something shiny in the sand. Tim pointed at it and said, "Look, Mom!" His mom came over and saw the shiny thing too. She picked it up and it was a small, old lamp. She rubbed the lamp and a mighty genie came out! The genie was big and blue, and he could do magic. Tim and his mom were very surprised to see the genie at the beach. The genie said, "I will give you one wish because you found my lamp." Tim thought about what he wanted. He looked at the sand, the water, and the sky. Then he had an idea. He wished for a big, fun sand castle to play in. The genie made a huge sand castle with his magic. Tim and his mom played in the sand castle all day and had lots of fun. They thanked the genie for the best day at the beach ever.
