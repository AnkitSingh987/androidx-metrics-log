Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved ice-cream very much. One day, he went to the park with his mom. At the park, there was a big ice-cream truck. Tim was very happy. Tim wanted to pick the best ice-cream. He saw a girl named Sue. Sue also wanted the best ice-cream. Tim and Sue were competitive. They both wanted to win. Tim said, "I will get the biggest ice-cream!" Sue said, "No, I will get the biggest ice-cream!" Then, something unexpected happened. The ice-cream truck man said, "I have a surprise!" He showed Tim and Sue a big, special ice-cream. It was big enough for two kids to share. Tim and Sue looked at each other and smiled. They decided to share the big ice-cream. Tim and Sue learned that sharing is better than competing. They became friends and enjoyed the ice-cream together. And they lived happily ever after. The end.

Once upon a time there was a lazy earth. It didn't like to work or do anything. All it wanted to do was sleep. One day something scared the earth. It was a giant monster with big eyes and sharp teeth. The monster was very scary and it wanted to eat the earth. The earth didn't know what to do. It was too lazy to run away. Then it had an idea. It started to shake and move quickly. The monster was very surprised and started to jump up and down. The earth was shaking so much that it scared the monster. The monster was so scared that it ran away without looking back. The earth was so happy. It stopped shaking and lay down. From then on it was no longer lazy. It worked hard and had lots of fun adventures.

One day, a little girl named Amy was playing in her room. She was very nervous because she wanted to measure her floor to see how big it was. She asked her mom for help. "Mom, can you help me measure the floor?" she said. Her mom said, "Of course, Amy! Let's do it together." As they started to measure the floor, they heard a strange noise. It was coming from under the floor! They were very surprised. "What is that noise?" Amy asked her mom. Her mom didn't know. They decided to find out what was making the noise. They lifted the floor and found a small, friendly mouse. The mouse said, "Hi, I'm Mimi. I was just playing under the floor. I'm sorry if I scared you." Amy and her mom were not scared anymore. They were happy to meet Mimi. They all became friends and measured the floor together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to play with his smooth, round ball. Tim had a lot of fun when he played with his ball. But sometimes, Tim did not behave well. He would throw the ball at his friends and make them cry. One day, Tim's mom told him that he needed to behave better. She said, "Tim, you must be nice to your friends. If you are not, they will not want to play with you." Tim thought about this and felt sad. He wanted to have fun with his friends, but he did not want to make them cry. The next day, Tim played with his smooth ball and his friends. This time, he behaved well. He did not throw the ball at them. Instead, they all took turns playing with the ball. They had so much fun together. Tim learned that when he behaved well, everyone could have fun and be happy.

One day, a little cat named Kitty wanted to get a toy. She had zero toys to play with. Kitty felt sad and not comfortable. She had an idea to find a toy outside. Her friend, Doggy, wanted to help too. So, they went out to find a toy for Kitty to play with. Kitty and Doggy looked and looked for a toy. They saw a big tree with something shiny up high. They thought it could be a toy. Kitty tried to climb the tree, but she could not reach it. Doggy had an idea to get the shiny thing. Doggy found a long stick and used it to get the shiny thing from the tree. It was a toy! Kitty was so happy and comfortable now. They played together with the toy all day. From that day on, Kitty and Doggy were best friends and always helped each other.
