Category: Intermediate
Title: Kitty the Clever Cat and the Dog

One day, a clever cat named Kitty went to a big place called University. She wanted to hear and learn many things. The University was full of big people who talked a lot. Kitty met a nice dog named Doggy at the University. Doggy said, "I like to hear the big people talk. They say smart things." Kitty said, "I want to be clever like them too." They both listened and learned together. One day, Kitty and Doggy heard the big people talk about a big surprise. They were very excited. They learned and listened more to find out what the surprise was. At last, they found out that the big surprise was a big party with yummy food and fun games. Kitty and Doggy were so happy that they became clever and had lots of fun at the University.

Once upon a time, a shy little dog named Spot lived in a small house. Spot loved to play with his red ball. One day, he saw a big box near the door. He was very curious. "What is inside?" he thought. Spot was excited. He had a feeling it was a surprise for him. Spot's friend, a bird named Tweet, came by to play. Spot showed Tweet the big box. "What do you think is inside?" Spot asked. Tweet did not know the answer. They both decided to open the box together. As they opened the box, they saw a big, bouncy castle! They were so happy! Spot said, "Wow! It's a surprise for us!" They played in the bouncy castle all day and had lots of fun. Spot was not shy anymore. He and Tweet became best friends forever.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily and her dog, Spot, wanted to have a picnic. They thought it would be fun to eat outside under a big tree. They took a soft blanket, yummy food like apples, sandwiches, and cookies, and a big bottle of water. Lily and Spot found a nice tree with lots of shade. They spread the blanket on the ground and put the food on top. "This will be a fun picnic, Spot!" said Lily. Spot wagged his tail and barked, "Woof!" But then, some ants came and started to crawl on the blanket. Lily felt uncomfortable and said, "Oh no, the ants are taking our food, Spot! We need to move!" So Lily and Spot picked up the blanket and moved to another tree. They put the blanket down and enjoyed their picnic with no more ants.

Once upon a time, there was a little crab. The crab was troubled. It wanted to find a new home. It lived by the sea, but it was not happy there. One day, the crab saw a girl playing near the water. She was imagining she was a princess. She wore a pretty dress and a shiny crown. The crab liked the girl and wanted to be her friend. The crab went to the girl and said, "Can I be your friend?" The girl was happy to have a new friend. She picked up the crab and put it in her pocket. They played and imagined all day long. The crab was not troubled anymore. It found a new home and a new friend. They lived happily ever after.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play and learn together. They have a big test tomorrow. They want to be successful. "Let's study for the test," Lily says. "We can look at the pictures and say the words." "OK," Ben says. "I like pictures. They are fun and pretty." They sit on the floor and open their books. They see a picture of a blue sky. They say "sky". They see a picture of a blue bird. They say "bird". They see a picture of a blue car. They say "car". They are happy. They think they know all the words. They close their books and go to sleep. The next day, they go to school. They are excited for the test. They sit at their desks and wait for the teacher. The teacher gives them the test. They look at the paper. They are confused. They do not see any pictures. They only see letters and numbers. They do not know what to do. They try to guess, but they are wrong. They feel sad and scared. They do not want to fail. The teacher collects the test. She looks at their answers. She shakes her head. She is not happy. "Lily and Ben, you did not study well," she says. "You only looked at the pictures. You did not learn the letters and numbers. You are not successful. You have to do the test again." Lily and Ben cry. They wish they had studied better. They do not like the test. They do not like the teacher. They do not like school.
