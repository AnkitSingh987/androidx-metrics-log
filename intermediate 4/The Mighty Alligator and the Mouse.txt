Category: Intermediate
Title: The Mighty Alligator and the Mouse

Once upon a time, there was a mighty alligator who was always very busy. He was always carrying things around with him. One day, there was a little mouse who wanted to go on an adventure. She asked the alligator if he could help her. The alligator looked at the little mouse and said, "Right, I'll help you! What do you want me to carry?" The mouse smiled and said, "Carry me on your back so I can explore the world!" The alligator smiled and said, "Hop on! Let's go!" The mighty alligator carried the little mouse all around the world. They went on many adventures and saw many wonderful sights. The alligator stopped to take a rest and the mouse hopped off to explore the area. The mouse thanked the alligator for the wonderful adventure. The alligator smiled, said goodbye and went off in search of the next adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a musician named Tom. Tom loved to make music. He played the guitar and sang songs. One day, Tom wanted to design a new song. He sat under a big tree and thought about what to make. While he was thinking, a little bird flew down and sat next to him. The bird said, "Hello, Tom! I can help you make a new song." Tom smiled and said, "Thank you, little bird!" Together, they started to design a beautiful song. When they finished the song, Tom felt embarrassed to sing it in front of others. But the little bird said, "Don't be scared, Tom. Your song is great!" Tom took a deep breath and started to sing. Everyone loved the song and clapped for Tom and the little bird. They were very happy and continued to make more songs together.

Once upon a time, in a dry land, there was a big temple. Many people lived near the temple. They were sad because they had no water. They wanted to raise their plants, but they could not. The people tried to find water, but they could not find any. One day, a little girl named Lily thought of a plan. She told everyone to dig a big hole near the temple. They dug and dug, but still, there was no water. The people were very sad and thought they would never raise their plants. Then, something unexpected happened. A big rain came! The rain filled the hole with water. The people were so happy! They could now raise their plants and have water to drink. The temple became a happy place, and the people thanked Lily for her idea.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big box of crayons. He loved to draw and color. He took the crayons to the park to draw a picture. While he was there, he saw a boat sail on the pond. Tim thought it was so cool! Tim met a girl named Sue who also liked to draw. They decided to draw together. Tim said, "Let's draw the boat that sails on the pond!" Sue agreed, and they started to draw. They were having so much fun. But then, a rude boy came and took Tim's crayons. He said, "These are mine now!" Tim and Sue were sad. They did not know what to do. Suddenly, the boat on the pond sailed very fast and splashed water on the rude boy. He dropped the crayons and ran away. Tim and Sue were so surprised! They got their crayons back and continued to draw, happy that the boat helped them.

Anna and her dog Spot were going on a trip with Mom and Dad. They had to pack their things in a big bag. Anna put her clothes, her books, and her teddy bear in the bag. Spot helped by wagging his tail and licking Anna's face. "Good boy, Spot!" Anna said. "You are my best friend. Do you want to pack something too?" Spot barked and ran to his toy basket. He picked up his favorite ball and his squeaky bone. He brought them to Anna and dropped them in the bag. "Good job, Spot!" Anna said. "Now we have everything we need. But wait, we forgot something important. Where is your collar?" Spot looked around and saw his collar on the floor. He had taken it off when he was playing. He ran to get it and put it on his neck. He felt proud and happy. "Good boy, Spot!" Anna said. "Now you are ready to go. Let's go find Mom and Dad. They have a surprise for us." Anna and Spot went to the car, where Mom and Dad were waiting. They put their bag in the trunk and got in the back seat. Mom and Dad smiled and gave them a hug. "Are you excited, Anna and Spot?" Mom asked. "We are going to Grandma's house. She has a big yard, a cozy couch, and yummy cookies for you." Anna and Spot cheered and licked Mom's face. They loved Grandma and her cookies. They couldn't wait to get there and have fun. They looked out the window and saw the trees and the birds. They sang a song and laughed. They were happy.
