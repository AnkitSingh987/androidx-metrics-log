Category: Intermediate
Title: The Worm and the Apple

Once upon a time, there was a little worm. The worm liked to play in the dirt. One day, the worm found a big red apple. The apple was sour, but the worm did not care. The worm was happy to have a new home. The sun was very hot. It started to burn the apple. The worm was scared. The worm did not want to leave the apple. The worm liked the sour apple too much. The worm stayed in the apple. The sun burned the apple more and more. The worm was very sad. In the end, the worm and the apple were burned by the sun. The worm should have left the apple when it started to burn.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red truck. The truck was very good at its job. It would carry lots of things from one place to another. One day, the truck driver saw a disgusting mess on the road. It was all dirty and smelly. The driver did not want to drive through it, but he had to. So, the driver had an idea. He wanted to design a new truck. This truck would be able to clean up the disgusting messes on the road. The driver worked very hard to make his idea come true. He drew pictures and talked to other people to help him. One day, the new truck was ready. It was big, green, and had special tools to clean up the messes. The driver was so happy with his new truck. But, something unexpected happened. When the new truck started to clean up the disgusting mess, it turned into beautiful flowers! The driver was surprised and very happy. He learned that sometimes, even the most disgusting things can turn into something beautiful if we work hard and use our ideas.

Tom and Jane were very good friends. One day, they decided to go for a drive. Tom had his dad's car, so they drove off together. Tom was driving very fast and Jane was enjoying the breeze. Suddenly, they stopped in front of a basketball court. Jane asked excitedly, "Can we play?" Tom replied, "Sure! Let's play basketball." They began to play and had such a great time. However, after some time, Tom started to tire as the game was getting boring. He said to Jane, "This game is boring. Can we do something else?" Jane was disappointed and did not want to do anything else. She said, "No! This is fun. Let's keep playing!" Tom and Jane had a conflict about whether to keep playing or not. In the end, Tom gave in and they continued the game. They had so much fun that they decided to come back the next day.

Anna and Ben were friends who liked to play with blocks. They had many blocks of different colors and shapes. They could build houses, towers, cars and animals with their blocks. One day, they wanted to build a big castle with all their blocks. But they had a quarrel about how to do it. Anna wanted to use the red and yellow blocks for the walls, and the blue and green blocks for the roof. Ben wanted to use the blue and green blocks for the walls, and the red and yellow blocks for the roof. "No, no, no!" Anna said. "My way is better. Red and yellow are the colors of the sun and fire. They make the castle look strong and bright." "No, no, no!" Ben said. "My way is better. Blue and green are the colors of the sky and grass. They make the castle look cool and calm." They could not agree. They started to pull and push the blocks from each other. They made a big mess. They broke some of the blocks. They made each other cry. They stopped playing with blocks. They did not talk to each other. They did not look at each other. They were very angry and sad. They had a bitter memory of their quarrel. The next day, they saw each other in the playground. They wanted to play with other toys, but they could not find any. All the other children had taken them. The only toys left were the blocks. They looked at the blocks. They looked at each other. They remembered their quarrel. They felt sorry. They wanted to be friends again. They walked towards each other. They smiled. They said sorry. "Sorry, Anna. I was mean to you. I like your way of building the castle. Can we try it?" "Sorry, Ben. I was mean to you. I like your way of building the castle. Can we try it?" They hugged. They took the blocks. They started to build the castle together. They used the red and yellow blocks for the walls, and the blue and green blocks for the roof. They mixed the colors. They made the castle look beautiful. They were happy. They had a good memory of their friendship.
