Category: Intermediate
Title: The Vehicle and the Silly

One day, a big vehicle went down the road. The vehicle was very silly. It had a big smile on it. The smile made everyone who saw it happy. The vehicle liked to make people happy. One day, the smiling vehicle saw a little girl. She was sad. The vehicle wanted to make her happy. So, it did something unexpected. The vehicle turned into a big, funny clown! The clown was silly and made the little girl laugh. But then, something even more unexpected happened. The silly clown turned back into the smiling vehicle. The little girl was so surprised! She laughed and laughed. Now, she was happy. The smiling vehicle was happy too. They both knew that they had made a new friend.

Once upon a time, in a small garden, there was a red carrot. This carrot was very big and bright. All the other carrots in the garden were orange, but this one was special. One day, a rabbit came to the garden. He saw the red carrot and said, "You are not a carrot! You are red, not orange!" The red carrot did not like this. It said, "I am a carrot, even if I am red." The rabbit and the carrot did not agree. Later, a kind bird flew into the garden. The bird saw the red carrot and the rabbit. The bird said, "You are both right. You are a carrot, and you are red. That makes you special." The rabbit and the red carrot became friends. They learned that it is okay to be different and not always agree.

Once upon a time there was a bird that wanted to soar high in the sky. She was so excited but had no idea how she would get up high. Then she saw a bowl in her nest. She flew over to it and hopped in. She spread out her wings and began flapping them. Up, up and up she went into the sky. The bird was so happy; she felt free and healthy. She spread her wings wide and soared all around the sky. She flew above the clouds and as far as she could see. She looked down, and there was her nest with the bowl still in it. Soon it was time to come back home. She flapped her wings and glided down to the nest. She hopped out of the bowl and rested in it for a bit. The bird thanked the bowl for the wonderful flight and knew she would soar again soon.

Tom and Lily were playing in the living room. They had many toys, but they liked to pretend with other things. Tom found an iron on the shelf. He said, "Look, Lily, this is a spaceship. It can fly to the moon and the stars." Lily wanted to play too. She said, "Can I be the pilot? I can press the buttons and make noises." She took the iron from Tom and made it zoom in the air. "Vroom, vroom, we are going to the moon!" Tom was not happy. He said, "No, Lily, give it back. It is my spaceship. You can have something else." He tried to grab the iron from Lily, but she held it tight. They pulled and pushed, and the iron fell to the floor. It made a loud noise and broke into pieces. Tom and Lily paused. They were scared. They knew the iron was not a toy. It was something for grown-ups. It was fragile and could hurt them. They looked at the broken iron and felt sorry. They heard their mom coming. She said, "What was that noise? What are you doing?" She saw the iron on the floor and was angry. She said, "Tom, Lily, how could you do this? This iron is not a toy. It is dangerous and expensive. You could have burned yourselves or the carpet. You are in big trouble." Tom and Lily said, "We are sorry, mom. We were playing. We did not mean to break it. We will not do it again." They hugged their mom and said, "We love you, mom. Please forgive us." Mom was still angry, but she loved them too. She said, "I love you too, but you have to be careful. You have to respect the things that are not yours. You have to learn from your mistakes. Now, go to your room and think about what you did. And no more playing with the iron." She took them to their room and closed the door. She sighed and picked up the pieces of the iron. She hoped it could be fixed.
