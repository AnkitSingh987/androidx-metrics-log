Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Treasure

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his friends. One day, Tim and his friends found a map. The map showed the way to a treasure. Tim and his friends wanted the treasure. They walked and walked. It was not easy, but they did not give up. They saw a big tree, and the treasure was under it. They were so happy. But then, a big bird came and took the treasure. Tim was brave. He climbed the tree and saved the treasure. Tim and his friends were very happy. They shared the treasure and played all day.

---

One day, a little bird named Tim was flying in the sky. It was icy cold outside, but Tim was happy because he saw a big peach tree. The tree had many big, sweet peaches on it. Tim wanted to eat a peach, so he flew down to the tree. When Tim got to the tree, he saw a small squirrel named Sally. Sally was holding a peach in her hands. Tim said, "Hi Sally! Can I have a peach too?" Sally smiled and said, "Yes, Tim! You can have a peach. Let's eat them together." Tim and Sally sat on a branch and ate their peaches. They were happy because the peaches were so sweet and juicy. After they ate, they decided to display the peach pits on the ground, so other animals could see how good the peaches were. Tim and Sally had a fun day and became good friends.

---

One day, a famous cat named Tom went for a walk. He liked to walk in the sun and look at the trees. Tom was very happy. While walking, Tom noticed a big shield on the ground. The shield was shiny and pretty. Tom thought it was fun to play with the shield. Tom took the shield home and showed it to his friends. They all thought it was cool. From that day on, Tom and his friends played with the shield every day. They were very happy.

---

One day, a big engine went crazy. It made loud sounds and could not stop. The engine was very sad and scared. It wanted to go back to being a good engine. A little girl saw the engine and wanted to help. She did not know what to do. She closed her eyes and started to pray. She prayed for the engine to be happy and not crazy anymore. As she prayed, something unexpected happened. The engine stopped being crazy and loud. It became calm and happy. The little girl and the engine became best friends. They played together every day and had lots of fun.

---

One day, a playful cat named Sam went outside to play. Sam saw his friend, a dog named Max, doing yoga in the yard. Sam wanted to try yoga too. So, Max showed Sam how to do a fun yoga pose. They both laughed and had a great time. While they were doing yoga, Sam's little sister, a kitten named Lily, found a spray bottle. She thought it would be fun to spray Sam and Max. Lily sneaked up to them and sprayed water on them. Sam and Max were surprised, but they laughed. Sam, Max, and Lily played together all day. They did more yoga, sprayed water, and had lots of fun. They loved being playful and spending time together.

Once upon a time, there was a noisy dog named Spot. Spot loved to bark and play all day. One day, Spot found a big box in the yard. He was very excited and wanted to see what was inside. Spot used his paw to open the box. He pushed and pushed, and finally, the box opened to reveal a big ball. Spot was so happy! He took the ball with his mouth and ran around the yard. A little girl named Sally saw Spot playing with the ball. She wanted to play too. Sally used her finger to throw the ball far away. Spot ran fast to get the ball and bring it back to Sally. They played together all day and became best friends.
