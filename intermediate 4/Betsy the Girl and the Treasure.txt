Category: Intermediate
Title: Betsy the Girl and the Treasure

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Betsy. She was three years old and very modest. One day, Betsy decided to go on an adventure. She arrived at a big old mine and was thrilled. She had never been to a mine before! Betsy started searching through the mine, looking for a special surprise. As she roamed around, she found lots of shiny stones. Before long, Betsy stumbled upon a chest full of delicious treats. She couldn't believe her luck! Betsy opened the chest and started to spoil herself with yummy candy and chocolate. Her eyes sparkled with excitement as she ate her treats. She enjoyed her special surprise so much! When Betsy was finished eating, she happily thanked the mine for her adventure. She said goodbye and went home. Betsy sat dreaming about her treasure for days, remembering the way it felt to spoil herself.

One day, a cheerful sun was shining in the sky. It was very hot. A boy named Tim went to the pool to play. He saw his friend, Sam, there too. The pool water was cool and nice. Tim said, "Hi, Sam! The sun is shining today. Let's play in the pool!" Sam smiled and said, "Yes, Tim! Let's play and have fun!" They jumped into the pool and splashed water on each other. They laughed and played all day. As the sun began to set, the pool started to shine like gold. Tim and Sam looked at the pretty water and smiled. They knew they had a fun day at the pool. The cheerful sun waved goodbye as it went to sleep.

Once upon a time, there was a fast dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to run and play with his friends. One day, he found a thing in the yard. It was a toy, and it was his favorite thing to play with. Buddy played with the toy all day long. He would throw it up in the air and catch it. But then, a big cat came into the yard. The cat wanted the toy too. Buddy did not want to give it up, but the cat was very big and scary. Buddy knew he had to be brave. He thought about what his mommy told him, that sometimes it's okay to surrender. So, he gave the toy to the big cat. The cat was happy and left the yard. Later that day, Buddy's mommy gave him a new toy, and he was so happy. Buddy learned that it's okay to give up something for a friend, and that good things can happen when you do.

Once upon a time, in a small forest, there lived a bird and a squirrel. They were good friends. One day, they saw a big twig on the ground. The bird and the squirrel both wanted the twig to make their homes better. They started to quarrel. "Stop being foolish!" said the bird. "I saw the twig first, so it is mine!" The squirrel did not agree. "No, I saw it first! I need it for my nest!" They were both very upset. Just then, a wise old owl heard their quarrel. He flew down to them and said, "Why are you fighting? You can both use the twig. You can share it and make your homes better together." The bird and the squirrel stopped quarreling. They thanked the owl and worked together to share the twig. They both made their homes better and stayed good friends.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a small heart-shaped rock. It was dark and shiny. Mia loved it and wanted to show it to her friend, Tom. Mia went to Tom's house and said, "Look, Tom! I found a dark heart rock. Do you want to see?" Tom said, "Yes, please!" He looked at the rock and thought it was very nice. Mia told Tom, "I want to keep it, but I will let you hold it for a little bit." Tom held the rock and thought about how pretty it was. Then, something unexpected happened. The dark heart rock started to glow! It became very bright and warm. Mia and Tom were surprised. They did not know what to do. Mia said, "Let's repeat what we did before. Maybe it will stop glowing." They repeated what they did, but the rock kept glowing. Mia and Tom decided to show the glowing rock to their parents. Their parents told them that the rock was special and that it was a sign of love and friendship. The moral of the story is that when you share your heart with others, it can create a special bond that lights up the world.
