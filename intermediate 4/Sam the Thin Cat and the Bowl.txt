Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam the Thin Cat and the Bowl

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Sam. Sam had a big love for his own bowl of food. He would eat from it every day. One day, Sam saw that his bowl was empty. He was sad and did not know what to do. Sam went to look for food. He walked and walked until he found a big bag of food. Sam was so happy that he jumped and danced. But then, something unexpected happened. The bag of food started to move! Sam was very scared and jumped back. Out of the bag came a small mouse named Tim. Tim was also thin and hungry. Sam and Tim looked at each other and decided to share the food. They became best friends and ate from the same bowl every day. They learned that love and sharing were better than owning things alone.

Once upon a time, there was a big, attractive car. The car could drive very fast. A dog with a hurt heel lived near the road. The dog liked to watch the car drive by every day. One day, the dog wanted to see the car up close. The dog limped on its hurt heel to the road. The dog saw the attractive car driving very fast. The dog tried to cross the road to see the car better. But the car was driving too fast. The car could not stop in time. The dog was hit by the car. The dog's hurt heel was not the only problem now. The dog was very sad and hurt, and the car drove away.

Once upon a time, there was a child named Tom. Tom lived with his mom and dad in a small house. They were a happy family. Tom's mom and dad loved him very much. They wanted to raise him to be a good person. One day, Tom and his mom went to the park. The sky was clear and blue. They played with a big red ball. Tom laughed and had lots of fun. His mom was happy to see him smile. As Tom grew up, he learned many things from his mom and dad. They taught him to be kind and helpful. Tom became a good child, and his mom and dad were proud of him. They knew that they had raised him well.

One day, a little girl named Mia was playing in her room. She saw a long cord behind her toy box. Mia felt curious and wanted to find out what was hiding behind it. Mia moved her toy box and peeked behind it. There, she found a tired, old teddy bear. It looked sad and lonely. Mia picked it up and said, "Hi, teddy bear! Do you want to play with me?" The tired, old teddy bear looked at Mia and said, "Yes, please! I have been alone for a long time. I want to play with you and be your friend." Mia and the teddy bear played all day long. They had so much fun together. At the end of the day, Mia was very tired. She hugged the teddy bear and said, "Now, you will never be alone again. You can sleep with me every night." The tired, old teddy bear smiled and gave Mia a big hug. They both went to sleep, happy to have found each other.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a hungry dog named Buddy. Buddy liked to play with his friends and look at the pretty sunset. One day, Buddy met a cat named Whiskers. Whiskers was sitting under a tree, also looking at the sunset. Buddy said to Whiskers, "Can I sit with you and look at the sunset?" Whiskers said, "Yes, you can sit with me." So, Buddy sat down next to Whiskers, and they both looked at the sunset together. It was very pretty, with colors like red, orange, and pink. After a while, Buddy's tummy rumbled. He was still very hungry. So, Buddy asked Whiskers, "Can you help me find some food?" Whiskers said, "Yes, I can help you." They both went on a walk to find some food. They found a nice lady who gave them some food to eat. Buddy and Whiskers were very happy and not hungry anymore. They went back to their tree and looked at the sunset again, feeling very full and happy.
