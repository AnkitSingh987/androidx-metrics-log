Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Poor Girl and the New

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a poor girl named Lily. She had no pretty clothes or style. Lily had a big heart and loved to invite friends to play. One sunny day, Lily saw a new girl in town. She wanted to invite her to play, too. So, Lily went to the new girl and asked, "Do you want to play with me?" The new girl said, "Yes, I would love to play with you!" While they were playing, something unexpected happened. The new girl waved her hand, and pretty clothes appeared on Lily. The new girl was a fairy! She said, "I like your kind heart, Lily. Now you have pretty clothes and style, too." From that day on, Lily and the fairy were best friends. They played together every day, and Lily always invited more friends to join. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a big and intelligent dog named Max wanted to test his new toy. He asked his friend, a little cat named Lily, to play with him. "Lily, let's test my new toy!" Max said. Lily was happy to play with Max and his new toy. Max and Lily played with the toy and had lots of fun. They ran and jumped, and the toy went up and down. Then, something unexpected happened. The toy fell into a big cup of water! The toy was all wet and did not work anymore. Max and Lily felt sad, but they learned something important. They should be more careful when playing with their toys. The next time they played, they made sure to keep their toys away from water. Max and Lily became more intelligent and careful friends.

Once there was a naughty girl named Janet. She loved playing tricks on her parents and friends. One day she had the idea to make a veil out of an old piece of fabric. She went to her mom and asked for the fabric. "Mom, can I have the fabric to make a veil?" Her mom was confused. "What do you want to do with it?" "I want to make a veil!" Janet said excitedly. Her mom smiled and said, "Okey-dokey". She gave Janet the fabric and Janet ran off to make her veil. When Janet was finished, she tried it on and ran to show her mom. "Look Mom, I made it!" Her mom looked surprised. "That's really beautiful," she said. "Where did you learn to make something like this?" "I don't know," Janet said, looking proud. "Well, you did a great job," her mom said as she gave Janet a hug. From then on, Janet wore her veil everywhere she went, to show off her special creation. Even though she was a naughty girl, her mom was always so proud of her.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a shy boy named Tim. Tim was always scared to talk to people. One day, Tim went on a trip with his family. They took a bus and brought their luggage with them. While on the bus, Tim saw two big men fight. They were very loud and scary. Tim wanted to help, but he was too shy. Suddenly, the bus stopped and the men fell down. The luggage fell on top of them and they stopped fighting. Tim was very happy that the fight was over. He realized that even though he was shy, he could still help. The other people on the bus thanked him for his help. Tim felt brave and proud. From that day on, he was not as shy as before.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Josh. He was only three years old. One day, his mom said to him, "Josh! Let's go get a surprise!" Josh was so excited he jumped up and down with joy. Josh and his mom got in the car and drove to a big store. Josh looked out the window and saw lots of cool things. He couldn't wait to see what the surprise was going to be! Once they arrived at the store, Josh wandered around with his mom while she looked for his surprise. Then, at last, they found it! It was a big, red toy truck! Josh was so happy and he said, "It's cool, mommy!" Josh and his mom went back home with the toy truck and Josh couldn't wait to show it to his friends. He was sure they would be surprised at the cool new toy! And he was right—his friends loved it!
