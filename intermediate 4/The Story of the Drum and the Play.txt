Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Drum and the Play

Once upon a time, there was a big, heavy drum in a small village. The drum was very loud. All the kids in the village liked to play with the drum. But they did not dare to play it too loud because it could make the grown-ups angry. One sunny day, a little boy named Tim went to the drum. He wanted to play it, but he was scared. His friend, Sue, saw him and said, "Tim, do you dare to play the drum?" Tim looked at the drum and then at Sue. He said, "I don't know. What if the grown-ups get mad?" Sue smiled and said, "Let's play it together. If we play it soft, they won't be mad." So, Tim and Sue played the drum together. They made sure not to play it too loud. They had so much fun and the grown-ups did not get mad. From that day on, Tim and Sue played the drum every day and were very happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a wealthy man named Tom. He had a big, green garden where he liked to play. In his garden, there were many pretty flowers and tall trees. One sunny day, Tom saw something shine in the sky. It was a little star, shining bright and happy. Tom was excited and wanted to show his friends the shiny star. So, Tom called his friends to come and see the shiny star in the sky. They all looked up and saw the star shining brightly. They were very happy and played in the green garden all day long. The star watched them, shining and smiling from the sky.

Once upon a time in a small house, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a toy robot made of steel. The robot was very obedient and always listened to Lily. They were best friends and played together every day. One sunny day, Lily and her robot went outside to play. They saw a big tree with fruits hanging from it. Lily wanted to taste the fruit, so she asked her robot friend to help her get one. The obedient robot reached up and picked a fruit for Lily. Lily took a bite of the fruit and it tasted very sweet. She shared the fruit with her robot friend, even though he could not taste it. They laughed and played under the tree until the sun went down. Lily and her robot friend had a great day, and they went back home to their small house, ready to have more fun tomorrow.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Kitty. She lived in a small shelter made of wood. Kitty loved to sleep all day and play all night. One day, Kitty met a friendly dog named Max. Max was very happy and liked to run and play. Max asked Kitty, "Do you want to play with me?" Kitty said, "Yes, let's play!" They became good friends and played together every day. As Kitty and Max grew up, they decided to marry each other. They had a big party with all of their friends. Everyone danced and had fun. Kitty and Max were very happy together, and they lived in their cozy shelter for the rest of their lives.

Once upon a time, there was a wide igloo. It was in a cold place with lots of snow. In the igloo, there lived a happy penguin named Pip. Pip loved to play with his friends and count numbers. One day, Pip met a new friend named Pup. Pup was a little seal who lived near the wide igloo. "Hi, I'm Pip! I love numbers!" said Pip. "Hi, I'm Pup! I love to play!" said Pup. Pip and Pup played all day, and Pip taught Pup how to count numbers. As they played, Pip said, "I have a big surprise for you, Pup!" Pup was excited and couldn't wait to see the surprise. They played more and counted numbers until it was time for the surprise. Pip took Pup to the wide igloo and showed him a big ice slide. "Wow! This is the best surprise ever!" said Pup. They slid down the ice slide and had so much fun, laughing and counting numbers together.
