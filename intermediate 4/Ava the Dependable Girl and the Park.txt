Category: Intermediate
Title: Ava the Dependable Girl and the Park

Once upon a time there lived a dependable little girl named Ava. She loved playing with her pet dog, Brownie. One day, Brownie and Ava were playing in the park. Suddenly, Ava heard a loud noise. She screamed. It was a trumpet! Ava was really scared. Brownie barked and tried to comfort her. Then a kind man came walking towards them. He was playing the trumpet. He said to Ava, "Do not be scared! I am here to bring you joy." Ava smiled and felt safe. From then on, Ava and Brownie went to the park every day so they could listen to the man playing his trumpet. Ava was so happy, knowing that her friend the kind man was dependable.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear named Ben. Ben loved to eat honey. One day, he found a big jar of honey near a tree. Ben was very happy and wanted to eat it all. Ben saw his friend, a little bird named Sally. Sally said, "Ben, don't eat all the honey. It is spoiled. It will make you sick." Ben did not want to get sick, so he listened to Sally. He decided to cover the jar and save it for later. Later, Sally and Ben found new honey that was good. They ate the good honey together and were very happy. They played all day and were glad they did not eat the spoiled honey.

One day, a chubby cat named Tim went outside to play. Tim liked to study the birds in the sky. He saw a big bird that looked like a helicopter. Tim wanted to be friends with the helicopter bird. Tim jumped high to reach the helicopter bird. But he could not reach it. Tim was sad. Then, something unexpected happened. The helicopter bird came down to Tim. It was not a bird; it was a real helicopter! The helicopter smiled at Tim. It asked, "Do you want to be friends?" Tim was happy and said, "Yes!" They played together all day. Tim learned that sometimes, surprises can be the best friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a purple cat. The cat was very friendly and liked to play with all the other animals. One day, the cat found a big piece of meat in a garden. The cat thought, "I can eat this meat, but I want to share it with my friends." So, the purple cat went to find her friends. She found a dog, a bird, and a mouse. They were all very happy to see the cat and the meat. The purple cat said, "Let's share this meat together!" The dog, the bird, and the mouse agreed. They all sat down and ate the meat. They were very happy to share the food with each other. From that day on, the animals always shared with each other and they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a curious cat named Tom. Tom liked to choose new places to play every day. One day, he saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Tom climbed the tree and saw a big bird's nest. The birds did not like Tom being so close to their nest. They were mad and scared. Tom did not know what to do. Tom was brave and jumped off the tree. He ran back to his home. The birds were happy, and Tom learned that he should not climb trees with bird nests. From that day, Tom chose to play in safer places.

Sara liked to bathe in the big tub with lots of bubbles. She had a yellow duck and a blue boat to play with. She made the duck swim and the boat sail in the water. She sang a song about the duck and the boat. One day, she saw something shiny in the water. It was a buckle. It was silver and had a star on it. Sara wondered who it belonged to. She picked it up and looked at it. It was very pretty and mysterious. She wanted to keep it. She put the buckle in her pocket and got out of the tub. She dried herself with a towel and put on her pajamas. She said good night to her mom and dad and went to her bed. She took out the buckle and put it under her pillow. She thought maybe it was a magic buckle that could make her dreams come true. She closed her eyes and wished for a pony.
