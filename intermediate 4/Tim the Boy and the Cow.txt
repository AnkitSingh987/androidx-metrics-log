Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Cow

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a friend named Cow. Cow was very useful because she gave Tim milk every day. Tim loved Cow and Cow loved Tim. One day, Cow asked Tim, "Can you allow me to eat some beef?" Tim was sad. He said, "No, Cow. Beef is not good for you. It is not useful." Cow was sad too, but she knew Tim was right. So, Tim and Cow played together in the field. They were happy because they knew they were useful to each other. They did not need beef to be happy. They had each other and that was enough.

Once upon a time, there was a smart cat named Tom. Tom lived in a big house with a little girl named Sue. Sue loved Tom and they played together every day. One day, Sue was running around the house and saw a big chair. She wanted to climb on it. Tom knew that Sue could fall and get hurt, so he wanted to prevent her from climbing. Tom jumped on the chair and said, "Meow, meow! Don't climb, Sue!" Sue listened to Tom and didn't climb the chair. She said, "Thank you, Tom! You are a very smart cat!" They played together on the floor and had a fun time.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park. The sun was shining, and the park was calm. Lucy loved to play on the swings and slide. She saw a small kitten near a tree. The kitten was soft and cute. Lucy said to the kitten, "Hi, little kitten! Do you want to play with me?" The kitten looked at her and meowed. Lucy and the kitten played together in the park. They ran and jumped and had lots of fun. After a while, Lucy and the kitten were tired. They sat down under the tree. Lucy petted the kitten, and they both felt calm and happy. They had a great day at the park together.

One day, a girl named Mia went to the park with her mom. Mia loved to play on the swings and slide. She saw her friend Tom at the park, too. They were both very happy to play together. Mia and Tom played a game where they would smile and touch their shoulders. "Touch your shoulder and smile!" said Mia. "OK!" said Tom, as he touched his shoulder and smiled. Mia's mom had made a spicy snack for them to eat. Mia and Tom sat down to eat the spicy snack. But when they took a bite, they both made a funny face. The spicy snack was too hot for them! They didn't know that Mia's mom had put a lot of spicy stuff in it. Suddenly, a small dog came running to them. The dog ate the spicy snack very fast. Mia and Tom were surprised that the dog liked the spicy snack. They laughed and watched the dog eat. Then, they went back to playing their shoulder and smile game. The dog joined them, and they all had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Daisy. Daisy liked to dress up in pretty clothes. She had a big, fun box full of dresses, hats, and shoes. Every day, Daisy would play dress up and have a great time. One day, Daisy found a new dress in her box. It was big and green. Daisy did not know where it came from. She was ignorant about it. But she put on the dress anyway. It was fun to wear something new. Then, something unexpected happened. When Daisy put on the green dress, she turned into a big, green frog! She was surprised and scared. But then, she found out she could jump very high and swim very fast. Daisy had a lot of fun being a frog for a day. When she took off the green dress, she turned back into a little girl. Now, Daisy knew that the green dress was magic and she could have even more fun playing dress up.

Once upon a time, there was a small bird named Jimbo. Jimbo loved to explore and make friends. He hopped from tree to tree and said hello to all the animals he saw. One day, he saw a nest in a nearby tree and decided to take a closer look. As soon as he landed, he heard a voice. "Come inside my nest," said the voice. Jimbo hopped through the door and was surprised by what he saw. Inside the nest was a larger bird who was deaf. The deaf bird spoke in sign language. She said, "Welcome to my nest. I am Mary. Why have you come here?" Jimbo told Mary that he came because he wanted to make new friends. Mary gladly welcomed him despite the delay. Jimbo was very happy and thanked Mary. They became great friends and enjoyed many fun activities together.
