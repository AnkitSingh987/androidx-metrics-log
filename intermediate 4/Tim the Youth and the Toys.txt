Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Youth and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a youth named Tim. Tim loved to sort his toys. He had many toys like cars, balls, and blocks. Tim was very good at sorting his toys by color and size. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came to play. Sam saw all of Tim's toys and felt envious. He said, "Wow, Tim! You have so many toys! Can I play with them too?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, Sam! Let's sort the toys together." Tim and Sam had lots of fun sorting the toys. They sorted the cars by color, the balls by size, and the blocks by shape. They played all day and were very happy. In the end, Sam was no longer envious because he learned that sharing and playing together was more fun than having many toys alone.

Once upon a time, there was a warm and sunny day. A little boy named Tim went to see his tutor. His tutor was a kind lady who helped him learn new things. They sat under a big tree to read a book together. As they read, Tim started to feel tired. He told his tutor he needed to rest. So, they both lay down on the soft grass under the tree. They closed their eyes and listened to the birds sing. While they were resting, something unexpected happened. A little puppy came running up to them. The puppy licked Tim's face, and he woke up with a big smile. The tutor opened her eyes and laughed. They never thought a new friend would come and find them under the tree. From that day on, the puppy joined Tim and his tutor for their lessons under the big tree.

Once upon a time, there was a delicate bird named Bella. She liked to fly and sing. Bella had a birthday every year. One day, Bella met a big dog named Max. Max liked to run and play. Bella said, "Hi, Max! Let's be friends." Max said, "Yes, Bella! Let's play together." So, Bella and Max played all day. They had lots of fun. Every year, they celebrated their birthdays together. And they were always happy.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful man named Tom. Tom had a big axe. He used the axe to cut wood for his friends. One day, Tom got very tired. He wanted to quit cutting wood. His friends saw him and wanted to help. They all came to Tom and said, "We will help you cut wood." Tom felt happy. He did not quit. He and his friends cut wood together. They all had fun and worked as a team. The moral of the story is that when you feel like quitting, ask for help. Friends can make hard work easier and more fun.

Little Susie and her mommy were outside playing. Susie wanted to add something to the sky. Susie asked her mommy, "Mommy, can I add something to the sky?" Mommy replied, "You can try, my love." So Susie took a deep breath and held her hands to the sky. Little by little she slowly added the most beautiful clouds to the sky. They were especially soft and white, and the colors made Susie smile. Every time she added a cloud, it gave the sky a delicious sweet and sour flavor. Mommy was so proud of Susie and watching her create something so special. What a wonderful day!

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to chew on things, especially socks. One day, Spot saw a big sock on the floor. He was very happy and thought, "This is the best day ever!" Spot started to chew on the big sock. But then, his friend Tom the cat came into the room. Tom was very tired and said, "Spot, that is my sock! Please give it back." Spot did not want to give the sock back, and they started to fight over it. This made both Spot and Tom very sad. As they were fighting, a little mouse came into the room. The mouse picked up the big sock and ran away with it. Spot and Tom stopped fighting and looked at each other. They knew they had to work together to get the sock back. They chased the mouse and got the sock back. In the end, Spot and Tom decided to share the sock and became best friends.
