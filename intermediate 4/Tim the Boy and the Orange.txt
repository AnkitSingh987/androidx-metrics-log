Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Orange

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big orange ball on the ground. Tim wanted to play with the ball, so he tried to move it. But the ball did not move. It was stuck! Tim asked his friend, Sam, "Can you help me move the orange ball?" Sam said, "Yes, let's try together!" They both pushed and pushed, but the ball still did not move. Then, Tim had an idea. He found a bitter lemon on the ground. Tim and Sam used the lemon to make the ball slippery. They pushed the ball again, and it started to move! Tim and Sam were so happy that they could play with the orange ball together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim went on a safari with his mom and dad. They saw many animals like lions, elephants, and monkeys. Tim was very happy. One day, Tim saw a little monkey. The monkey seemed sad. Tim asked the monkey, "Why are you sad?" The monkey said, "I lost my mom. Can you help me find her?" Tim said, "Yes, I will help you." Tim, his mom, his dad, and the little monkey looked for the monkey's mom. They looked and looked, but they could not find her. Then, something unexpected happened. The little monkey's mom was hiding behind a tree! She jumped out and said, "Surprise!" The little monkey was so happy to see his mom. Tim and his family were happy too. They all had a great time on their safari.

Once upon a time, there were two children named Graham and Kate. They were playing outside when they spotted a kayak on the beach. Graham said to Kate, "Let's go explore the kayak. I bet it's fun!" And Kate said, "Yes, let's go!" They hopped into the kayak, and Graham became the captain. As the captain, he got to decide when to paddle and where to go. They left the beach and paddled to a nearby island. It was a lazy day, so they just relaxed and enjoyed the view. Suddenly, they heard a voice calling, "Hey, kids! What are you doing?" It was an old, wise fishermen. The children said, "We're exploring!" The fishermen smiled and said, "That's wonderful. I hope you have a great time." And that's what the children did. They had a wonderful time exploring the island together. They knew it was the best kayak adventure ever.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was very adventurous. She loved to play with her hoop. One day, Mia went to the park with her hoop. At the park, Mia met a boy named Tom. Tom wanted to play with Mia's hoop too. "Can I play with your hoop?" Tom asked. Mia said, "Yes, we can play together!" They both played with the hoop and had a lot of fun. But then, the hoop become stuck in a tree. Mia and Tom were sad. They both tried to get the hoop down. They jumped, but they could not reach it. Mia had an idea. "Let's ask for help!" she said. They found a tall man and asked him to help. The man reached up and got the hoop out of the tree. Mia and Tom were happy again. They played with the hoop and had a great day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a red car named Remy. Remy loved to race on the stage. One day, Remy was racing with his friend, a blue car named Benny. "Let's complete the race together!" said Remy to Benny. Benny agreed, "Yes, let's do it!" They raced and raced, and Remy and Benny were very fast. They went around the stage, and all the people clapped for them. Remy and Benny were so happy to complete the race together. They were the best friends, and they loved to race on the stage.

Once there was a girl named Ruby. She was three years old and loved to explore. One day, Ruby found a big, empty stage. She was curious and wanted to check it out. She hopped on the stage and pretended it was her very own show! Ruby danced across the stage, enjoying the feeling of the wooden floor under her feet. She sang songs and spun around in circles, letting her imagination take her to a whole new world. As the day went on, Ruby got more and more sleepy. She decided it was time to go home and make dinner. But she was glad that she had such a fun day exploring and enjoying the stage.
