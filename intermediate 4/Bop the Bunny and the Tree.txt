Category: Intermediate
Title: Bop the Bunny and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little bunny named Bop. Bop loved to skip and play all day long. One day, Bop found a thick rope on the ground. He thought it would be fun to play with, so he picked it up with his mouth and started to skip with it. Bop skipped and skipped with the thick rope around his neck. He was having so much fun that he didn't see a big tree in front of him. Bop skipped right into the tree and the thick rope got stuck. He tried to pull it off, but it was too tight around his neck. Just then, Bop's friend, a wise old owl, saw him and knew he was in trouble. The owl quickly flew down and used his sharp beak to cut the thick rope. Bop was free and so happy that his friend saved him. From that day on, Bop learned to be more careful when he played and always remembered to look where he was skipping. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a mole who lived in a hole. One day, Mole felt uncomfortable in his hole. He wanted to go out and play. He saw a big pond and wanted to have fun. Mole said to himself, "I will go splash in the pond!" Mole went to the pond and saw a duck. Duck was splashing in the water. Mole asked Duck, "Can I splash with you?" Duck said, "Yes, come in! The water is nice." Mole was happy and jumped in the water to splash with Duck. But then, something unexpected happened. Mole didn't know how to swim! Duck saw Mole was in trouble and quickly helped him. Duck taught Mole how to swim, and they splashed together all day. From that day on, Mole and Duck were best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boat that liked to sail on the water. The boat was harmless and friendly to everyone. It liked to play with the fish in the water and the birds in the sky. One day, the boat found an onion floating on the water. It was a big, round onion. The boat thought the onion was a toy, so it played with the onion. The boat pushed the onion with its nose, and the onion rolled on the water. The boat and the onion became best friends. They sailed together on the water every day. The birds and the fish liked to play with the boat and the onion too. They all had lots of fun and lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a brave little boy named Tim. Tim liked to act in plays at school. He had a big part in a play about a king and his friends. One day, Tim had to say a long line in the play. He was scared, but he wanted to be brave. He practiced his line over and over again. "I am the king's friend, and I will help him," he said. The day of the play came, and Tim was ready. He stood on the stage and said his line, "I am the king's friend, and I will help him." Everyone clapped and cheered for Tim. He was so brave and did a great job in the play.

One day, Lily saw something delicious in the park. She ran up to it and found a huge plate of candy and cookies. She was so excited she ate as much as she could. Lily thought that was the most delicious thing she had ever tasted. She forgot all about her parents telling her to value peace and be kind. She kept eating and eating until all the food was gone. But soon, people started arguing nearby. They were shouting and fighting. Lily felt scared and didn't know what to do. Then the police came and arrested the bad men. The police said the men were fighting over the delicious food Lily had eaten. They said if she had been patient and valued peace, the bad men wouldn't have fought. Lily was sad that her greed had caused all this trouble. From then on, she tried to remember to value peace and be kind.
