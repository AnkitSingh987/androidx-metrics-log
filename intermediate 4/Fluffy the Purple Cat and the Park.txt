Category: Intermediate
Title: Fluffy the Purple Cat and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a purple cat named Fluffy. Fluffy liked to play with his toy missile. He would throw it and run after it all day long. One day, Fluffy met another cat named Whiskers in the park. Whiskers wanted to play with Fluffy's toy missile too. Fluffy didn't want to share, so they started to fight over the toy. After a while, Fluffy and Whiskers got tired of fighting. They decided to take turns playing with the toy missile. They became good friends and played together every day. And they both learned that sharing was more fun than fighting.

Once upon a time, there was a big brown dog named Max. Max liked to help his mom in the kitchen. One day, his mom gave Max a little apron to wear. Max was very happy. Max wore his apron and helped his mom cook. They made yummy food together. Max liked to serve the food to his family. Everyone loved Max's cooking. One day, Max made a big cake. It was a special day. Max wore his apron and served the cake to everyone. They all clapped for Max. Max was very proud.

Lily likes to chew. She chews her food, her toys, her clothes, and sometimes her hair. She thinks chewing is fun and makes her teeth strong. But chewing can also get her in trouble. One day, Lily found a teaspoon in the kitchen. She thought it was shiny and pretty. She wanted to chew it. She put it in her mouth and bit hard. But the teaspoon was not soft like food or toys. It was hard and metal. It hurt Lily's teeth and tongue. She cried out loud. Mom heard Lily cry and ran to the kitchen. She saw Lily with the teaspoon in her mouth. She was worried and angry. She took the teaspoon out and checked Lily's mouth. She said, "Lily, why did you chew the teaspoon? You could have broken your teeth or cut your tongue. Teaspoons are not for chewing. They are for measuring and stirring. You are lucky you are not hurt. Don't ever chew teaspoons again." Lily felt sorry and scared. She said, "I'm sorry, Mom. I didn't know. I just wanted to chew something shiny. I won't chew teaspoons again. Can I chew something else?" Mom hugged Lily and said, "I know you like to chew, but you have to be careful. You can chew your food, your toys, or your gum. But don't chew anything that is hard, sharp, or dirty. And don't chew too much or too fast. You might choke or get a tummy ache. Chewing is good, but only in moderation." Lily nodded and said, "Okay, Mom. I understand. I will chew only good things and not too much. Can I have a cookie?" Mom smiled and said, "Yes, you can have a cookie. But use a teaspoon to scoop some milk first. And don't chew the teaspoon."

Once upon a time, in a small pond, there was a lazy frog. He liked to sit and watch the water all day. One day, the frog saw something new in the pond. It was a boat! The frog hopped into the boat and started to discover the pond. He saw fish, birds, and bugs. The frog was happy to see all the new things. He liked the boat a lot. After a long day of discovering, the lazy frog went back to his spot. He was tired but very happy. From that day on, the frog and the boat became best friends. They discovered the pond together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a furry dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his favorite toy, a ball with a handle. One day, Spot saw a little bird lay on the ground. The bird could not fly because its wing was hurt. Spot wanted to help the bird, so he gently picked up the bird with the handle of his toy. He carried the bird to his human friend, who knew how to make the bird feel better. Spot's human friend put a soft bandage on the bird's wing and gave it some food. After some days, the bird could fly again. It was so happy and thanked Spot for his help. Spot learned that it is good to help others when they are in need. He felt proud and happy for being kind and helpful. The moral of the story is that helping others can make you feel good too.
