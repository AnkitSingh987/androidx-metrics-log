Category: Intermediate
Title: Blue the Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a bird named Blue. Blue loved to fly high in the sky. He was very glad when he could fly. He had many friends who liked to fly too. One day, Blue made a promise to his friends. He said, "I will fly higher than any bird has ever flown!" His friends were excited to see him try. They all went to the big tree to watch Blue fly. Blue started to fly higher and higher. But then, something unexpected happened! A big wind blew Blue to a new place he had never seen before. There, he met a kind frog who could also fly. Blue was so surprised! They became friends and flew together. Blue was glad he made a new friend, even if he did not fly the highest.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy radio that he loved. He would carry it everywhere he went. One day, Tim was playing with his radio in the park. He felt very glad and happy. While Tim was playing, he saw a big tree. He wanted to see what was behind it. So, he went to the tree and took a peek. To his surprise, he saw a big, colorful bird. The bird looked at Tim and started to talk! "Hello, Tim!" said the bird. Tim was so surprised that he dropped his radio. The bird picked up the radio and started to sing. Tim was happy and danced with the bird. From that day on, Tim and the bird became best friends, and they played with the radio together.

Tommy was walking through the forest with his mommy. He saw a lamp peeking out from behind a big tree. He knew that he was not supposed to take things and he was not allowed to go near strange things, so he stopped and asked his mommy. "Mommy, what is this strange thing?" "It looks like a lamp," his mommy said. "But we don't know what it is doing here, so be careful and don't touch it." But Tommy was curious so he inched a bit closer to take a better look. The lamp seemed quite interesting and he wanted to pick it up but he knew he should listen to his mother. So he stepped a bit closer and then he heard a loud crackling noise. Suddenly, a strange creature appeared from behind the tree. It seemed angry and it charged towards Tommy! It seemed to want to attack him but then his mother stepped in front of him. She threw the lamp at it and it ran away. But it was too late. Tommy's mother was hurt and he could see that she was in pain. She looked at him and said "I told you to be careful. Now this happened because you didn't listen."

Once upon a time, there was a boy. The boy felt miserable one day. He thought going to the park would make him feel better. So, he went to the park to play. At the park, the boy saw a swing. He loved to swing. He ran to the swing and sat on it. He began to swing back and forth. The boy felt a little better as he swung higher and higher. But then, the boy swung too high. He fell off the swing and got hurt. The boy felt even more miserable than before. He went back home, sad that his day at the park did not have a happy ending.

One day, a little girl named Sue got an invitation to a fun party. She felt very lucky and happy. Sue put on her pretty dress and went to the party with a big smile. At the party, there were many friends, toys, and yummy food. Sue played with her friends and had a great time. Suddenly, a big dog came into the room. Everyone was scared and ran away, but Sue did not run. She kneeled down and looked at the dog. The dog came closer to Sue, and she saw that it had a little note on its collar. Sue read the note and found out that the dog was a surprise gift for her from her mom and dad. Sue was so happy and felt even more lucky. She hugged the dog and named it Lucky. From that day on, Sue and Lucky were the best of friends.
