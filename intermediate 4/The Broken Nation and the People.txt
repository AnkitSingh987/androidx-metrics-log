Category: Intermediate
Title: The Broken Nation and the People

Once upon a time, there was a broken nation. It was so broken that the people that lived in it were sad. Nobody knew what to do to make it better. One day, a special kind of person arrived. His name was Fred. He was a very smart person. He was able to see the problems and knew what to do to make them better. He talked to the people in the nation and taught them how to manage the broken things in their lives. He showed them how to work together and make things better. At first, it wasn't easy for the people of the nation. They had to learn a lot of new things. But under Fred's guidance, the people in the nation slowly began to manage their problems and their sadness slowly went away. They all worked hard and eventually the broken nation was fixed. Everyone in the nation was happy and celebrated. They all thanked Fred for helping them manage the broken things in the nation.

One day, a little girl named Sally went to the park. She saw a rough rock and wanted to play on it. Sally tried to balance on the rock, but it was hard. "Look, Mom!" Sally said. "I can balance on the rock!" Her mom smiled and said, "Be careful, Sally. The rock is rough." Sally's friend, Tim, came to the park too. He had a letter in his hand. "Sally, I got a letter from my grandma!" Tim said. "She sent me a picture of her cat." Sally and Tim played on the rough rock together. They took turns trying to balance on it. They had a lot of fun at the park. After playing, they sat down and looked at the letter from Tim's grandma. They laughed at the funny cat picture and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play games with his friends. They were very competitive. They always wanted to win. One day, Tim and his friends found a map. The map showed them where to find a big prize. They decided to have a race to see who could get to the prize first. They all ran as fast as they could. Tim was in the lead, but he saw his friend, Sam, fall down. Tim stopped and thought about what to do. Tim decided to give Sam a hand. They walked together to the prize. In the end, all the friends shared the prize. They learned that helping each other is more important than winning.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a big, purple ball. Lily wanted to play with the ball. But there was a boy named Tim who also wanted to play with the ball. They both looked at the ball and then at each other. Lily said, "I saw it first, so I will play with it." Tim looked sad and said, "But I want to play too." Lily thought for a moment and then had an idea. "We can play together!" she said. Tim smiled and nodded. They started to play with the purple ball. They threw it and waved at each other. The sun was pale in the sky, but they were having so much fun. They played until it was time to go home. Now, Lily and Tim were friends and they shared the purple ball.

Once upon a time, there was a tiny mouse named Tim. He lived in a small house made of cheese. Tim loved to eat his cheese, but he wanted to try something new. He had heard about a tasty treat called "mild soup" from his friend, the big cat named Max. One day, Tim decided to make the mild soup. He asked Max for help. Max said, "To make mild soup, you need to gain some vegetables and put them in a pot with water." Tim was excited and went to gather the vegetables. He found some carrots, peas, and corn. Tim and Max put the vegetables in a pot with water. Max told Tim, "Now, you need to add a teaspoon of salt to the soup." Tim took a small spoon and added the salt. They cooked the soup and shared it together. The mild soup was delicious, and Tim was happy he tried something new. From that day on, Tim and Max enjoyed making and eating mild soup together.
