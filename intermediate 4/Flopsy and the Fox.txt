Category: Intermediate
Title: Flopsy and the Fox

Once upon a time there was a hairy rabbit called Flopsy. Flopsy was very proud of her long fur and she loved to flaunt it. One day, she wanted to explore the world outside her burrow. She spread her furry paws and hopped off. Flopsy hopped past the long grass and potential friends. But Flopsy didn't want any friends; she wanted to value her independence and keep exploring. After a while, Flopsy noticed a carrot in the distance. "Mmm, a carrot", she thought, "How delicious". She spread her fluffy tail and hopped towards it. But before she could get to it, a fox pounced in front of her. The fox said, "Give me that juicy carrot before I eat you!" Flopsy spread her ears in fear, but she refused to back down. She said, "No! It's mine. You can't have it!" The fox was taken aback. "Wow, you'll stand up for what you believe in? I respect that. That value is worth more than any carrot." He smiled, spread his bushy tail and hopped away. Flopsy had won the battle with the fox, and she now valued her independence even more. She smiled and hopped away with her carrot in hand.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat rice. One day, his mom made a big bowl of rice for lunch. The rice was white and clear, just the way Tim liked it. Before Tim could eat the rice, he had to ask his mom for permission. "Mom, can I eat the rice now?" he asked. His mom looked at him and smiled. "Yes, Tim, I permit you to eat the rice," she said. Tim was so happy. He sat down at the table and ate the clear rice. It was so yummy. He ate and ate until there was no more rice left in the bowl. Tim's mom was proud of him for asking permission and for eating all of his rice. And they lived happily ever after.

There was a fat object that everyone was scared of. One day, two small children were talking about the object. "Do you think this object is scary?" asked the first child. "Oh yes, it's so fat," answered the second child. Suddenly, the object moved and it scared the two children. They screamed and ran away as fast as they could. The next day, the two children came back to try and get a closer look. "Let's get closer," the first child said bravely. The second child was too scared, but slowly they inched closer to the object. It was so fat that it couldn't even move, and it was only an old toy! The children realised they weren't scared anymore so they started to laugh and play with the object. From then on, they played with the fat object every day.

One day, a little girl named Mia was playing with her toys. She had a big, hairy teddy bear named Mr. Bear. She loved Mr. Bear very much. They liked to watch television together. They would sit on the floor and watch their favorite show, "The Happy Friends." One day, while they were watching television, Mr. Bear started to vanish! Mia was very scared. She didn't want her friend to go away. She cried, "Mr. Bear, please don't go!" Just then, Mia's mommy came into the room. She saw Mia crying and asked, "What's wrong, Mia?" Mia told her mommy about Mr. Bear vanishing. Her mommy smiled and said, "Don't worry, Mia. Mr. Bear is just hiding. Let's find him together." Mia and her mommy looked around the room. They found Mr. Bear hiding behind the television! Mia was so happy to see him. She hugged him tightly and said, "I'm so glad you didn't vanish, Mr. Bear! Let's watch more television together." From that day on, Mia, Mr. Bear, and her mommy would watch television together. They were a happy family, and Mr. Bear never tried to vanish again.

One day, a boy named Tim found a rare cup. It was red and shiny. Tim was very happy. He wanted to show the cup to his friend, Sue. "Look, Sue!" Tim said. "I found a rare cup. It is so pretty!" Sue looked at the cup and saw a mark on it. "Wow, Tim! There is a mark on the cup. What does it mean?" Tim and Sue went to ask Mom. Mom looked at the mark and smiled. "This mark means that the cup is very special. It is a rare cup." Tim and Sue were very excited. They played with the rare cup all day. They were very careful not to break it. The rare cup with the mark made them very happy.
