Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Smart Cat and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a smart cat named Max. Max liked to relax in the sun. He was an intelligent cat who knew many things. One day, Max found a bottle of juice called wine. Max knew it was for grown-ups, not for cats or kids. Max wanted to help, so he took the wine to his mom. Max's mom was happy he brought the wine to her. She said, "Thank you, Max, for being so smart and helpful." Max was proud of himself for doing the right thing. From that day on, Max continued to relax in the sun and be the intelligent cat he was.

Once upon a time, there was a wide bowl. It was so big that a small cat could sit inside. One day, a little girl named Mia found the wide bowl in her kitchen. She wanted to use it to make a big cake. Mia asked her mom, "Can I use the wide bowl to make a big cake?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful not to drop it." Mia was very happy and started to make the cake. She put all the things she needed into the wide bowl. While Mia was making the cake, the small cat jumped into the wide bowl. Mia didn't see the cat and closed the bowl with a lid. The cat was stuck inside! Mia's mom heard the cat meow and quickly opened the bowl. The cat jumped out, and everyone laughed. Mia learned to always look inside the wide bowl before closing it.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had a very unusual bracelet. It was big and shiny. Lily loved her bracelet very much. One day, Lily was playing with her friends. They were passing a ball. Lily wanted to pass the ball too, but her bracelet was too big. It made it hard for her to play. Lily felt sad. Lily's mom saw her and said, "Lily, you can give your bracelet to someone who needs it more. Then you can play with your friends." Lily thought about it and decided to give her bracelet to a little girl who was sick. The little girl was very happy, and Lily felt good. She could now play with her friends, and she learned that sharing can make everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly machine. The machine was big and red. It liked to surprise people with fun things. One day, it met a little boy named Tim. "Hi, Tim!" said the friendly machine. "I have a surprise for you!" Tim was very happy. He loved surprises. "What is it?" Tim asked with a big smile. The machine told Tim to close his eyes and wait. The machine made a loud noise. Tim opened his eyes and saw a big, shiny ball. He was so happy and played with the ball all day. But soon, the sky turned dark, and Tim knew it was time to go home. Tim said goodbye to the friendly machine and took the shiny ball home. But when he got there, his mom was not happy. "That ball is too big for our house, Tim," she said. Tim was sad, but he knew his mom was right. He had to take the ball back to the friendly machine. The next day, Tim went back to the machine and told it what happened. The machine was sad, too. It wanted to make Tim happy, but it didn't know that the surprise was not good for Tim's home. From that day on, the friendly machine learned to think more about the surprises it gave to people. And Tim learned that not all surprises are good, even if they seem fun at first.

Tom and Lily were playing in their room. They had many toys and books and they liked to make fun games. But they also liked to make a lot of noise. They shouted and laughed and banged their toys on the floor. Their mom was in the hall. She was working on her laptop. She had to write an important email for her job. She needed quiet and peace. But Tom and Lily were too loud. They disturbed her work. She could not think or type. She went to their room and opened the door. She looked at them with a stern face. She said, "Tom and Lily, you are being too loud. You are disturbing me and the neighbors. You need to play quietly or go outside. Do you understand?" Tom and Lily nodded. They felt sorry for their mom. They did not want to make her angry or sad. They said, "We are sorry, mom. We will play quietly or go outside. We love you, mom." Their mom smiled. She said, "I love you too, Tom and Lily. Thank you for being good and respectful. Now, please, play quietly or go outside." She closed the door and went back to the hall. She finished her email and felt happy. Tom and Lily played quietly or went outside and had fun. They did not disturb anyone. They were good and respectful.
