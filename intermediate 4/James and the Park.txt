Category: Intermediate
Title: James and the Park

James was a compassionate little boy. He loved helping others and playing outside. Today, he decided to take a walk in the park near his neighborhood. When he arrived, he noticed a lot of people from all over the nation. They were all speaking different languages and were laughing together. James had a great time meeting all the different people. Suddenly, he saw an elderly lady sneezing. He quickly ran towards her and asked, "Are you okay?". The lady replied back, "Oh, yes thank you. I just needed to sneeze." James smiled and said, "Let me help you". He offered the lady his hand and he escorted her to a nearby bench. The lady was very appreciative and thanked James for his compassion. James beamed proudly from the exchange and continued to explore the park.

One day, Tom and Sue went to the store. They wanted to buy a red ball. Inside the store, they saw a big freezer. The freezer was cold and had ice on it. Tom touched the freezer and said, "Brr! It's so cold!" Sue looked at the freezer and frowned. "Why is the red ball in the freezer?" she asked. Tom frowned too. They didn't know how to get the ball out. Then, a store man came and opened the freezer. He took out the red ball and gave it to Tom and Sue. They were very happy and said, "Thank you!" They played with the red ball all day and forgot that it was once in the freezer.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. It was a cold day, but he wore a big coat to stay warm. Tim loved to play with his friends in the park. They liked to run, jump, and laugh together. As Tim played, he found a potato on the ground. He picked it up and showed it to his friend, Sam. "Look, Sam! I found a potato!" said Tim. Sam looked at the potato and said, "Wow! Let's play with it!" They took turns throwing the potato to each other, pretending it was a ball. After a while, Tim and Sam started to feel cold. They decided to go home and show their moms the potato they found. Both moms smiled and said, "Good job, boys! Now we can cook the potato and eat it for dinner." Tim and Sam were happy that their day of play ended with a warm meal.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big purple tree. This tree had many lemons on it. A little boy named Tim lived near the tree. He loved to eat the lemons, but he could not reach them. One day, Tim met a nice bird. The bird could fly high and get the lemons for him. Tim said, "Please, bird, can you get a lemon for me?" The bird said, "Yes, I will help you. But first, you must pray to the tree." So, Tim closed his eyes and prayed. The bird flew up and got a lemon for Tim. But when Tim tried to eat the lemon, it was very bad. It made Tim's mouth hurt. The bird said, "I am sorry, Tim. The lemons from the purple tree are not good to eat." Tim was sad, but he learned that not all lemons are good.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the shore. He had a little boat. The boat was fit and strong. Tim wanted to sail on the water. He was very happy. As Tim sailed, he saw a big fish. The fish was swimming fast. Tim was scared. The fish came close to his boat. Tim thought the fish would eat his boat. But then, something unexpected happened. The big fish started to talk. It said, "Don't be scared, Tim. I am a nice fish. I just want to be your friend." Tim was surprised. He never heard a fish talk before. Tim and the fish became good friends. They sailed together and had fun at the shore.

Once upon a time there was a boy who was very creative. One day, he thought it would be fun to cut the shelf in his bedroom. He grabbed a saw from the toolbox and started sawing away. Unfortunately, the shelf was too big and it was too difficult for him to cut. The boy, in his creative way, decided to get help from his mom, who was in the kitchen. He shouted, "Mom, help me cut the shelf!" But before his mom could come, the shelf suddenly broke and lots of things on it spread everywhere. The mom came and was very unhappy when she saw the mess. She was angry with the boy and scolded him. She said, "No more cutting shelves!" The boy was upset and sorry, but it was too late. The shelf was broken and the things were spilled all over the bedroom.
