Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Thoughtful Boy and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful boy named Tim. Tim loved to play football with his friends. They would work hard to get better at the game. One day, Tim saw a big tree with a hole in it. He thought, "Maybe I can put my football in there for safekeeping." So, he put the football in the hole and went to eat lunch. After lunch, Tim and his friends went to play football. Tim remembered the tree and went to get the football. When he took it out, he found a note inside. The note said, "Thank you for being thoughtful and sharing your football. Here is a new ball for you!" Tim and his friends were very happy and played with both footballs all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a big, soft blanket that she loved very much. She was always very careful with her blanket. One day, Mia's friend Sam came over to play. "Can I see your blanket, Mia?" asked Sam. Mia said, "Yes, but be careful with it." Sam took the blanket and played with it. They had lots of fun. But then, Sam was not careful. He spilled juice on Mia's blanket. Mia was sad and upset. She did not receive a new blanket. Now, Mia's blanket was not as nice as before.

Tom and Lily were twins. They liked to do everything together. They liked to play with their toys, read their books, and sing their songs. They also liked to share their things with each other. They were very generous. One day, they went to the park with their mom. They saw a big yellow taxi on the street. They wanted to ride in the taxi. They asked their mom if they could. Their mom said yes, but they had to split the fare. She gave them some coins to pay the driver. Tom and Lily got in the taxi. They were very excited. They looked out the window and saw many things. They saw cars, buses, bikes, and people. They saw shops, houses, trees, and flowers. They pointed and named everything they saw. The taxi stopped at the park. The driver told them how much they had to pay. Tom and Lily counted their coins. They had enough. They gave the driver the coins and said thank you. The driver smiled and said they were very polite. He gave them a candy each. He was very generous. Tom and Lily got out of the taxi. They ran to the playground. They had a lot of fun. They played on the swings, the slide, the seesaw, and the sandbox. They shared their candy with their mom and each other. They were very happy. They were very generous.

One day, a little dog named Spot was playing in the yard. He saw a long strip of cloth on the ground. Spot was scared because he thought it was a snake. He ran to his friend, the cat named Fluffy. "Fluffy, help! There is a snake in the yard!" Spot said. Fluffy went to look and saw the strip of cloth. "That's not a snake, Spot. It's just a strip of cloth," Fluffy said. Later, Spot's owner, a girl named Lucy, came outside. She saw the strip of cloth and had an idea. "I can replace my old jump rope with this," she said. Lucy tied the ends of the strip together and started to jump. Spot and Fluffy played together while Lucy jumped with her new rope. They all had a fun day, and Spot was not scared anymore.

Once upon a time there was a hairy bear. He loved to eat sweet things and listen to stories. One day, he smelled something delicious, so he followed it. He came to a big tree with yummy honey dripping off the trunk. The bear couldn't resist the tempting smell and taste. He licked and licked away, until he was so full, he felt like he'd explode. Just then a little mouse scurried out of the tree. The bear asked the mouse if he could taste the honey too. The mouse gave the bear a stern look and said, "No way! This is my secret honey, and I don't want to share it." The bear was so mad, that he grabbed the little mouse and threw him into the air. The mouse cried out, "Help!" but it was too late. The bear ate the mouse and all the honey he could. The bear was feeling very full and he thought the story was over until he heard something moving inside his tummy. It was the mouse, who was still alive and trying to get out. The mouse managed to escape through the bear's hairy fur and ran away as fast as he could. The bear felt so sick and was ever so sorry. The moral of the story is, always be kind and sharing!
