Category: Intermediate
Title: Rufus the Dog and the Pond

Once upon a time there was a dog named Rufus. He had a collar around his neck and he was always so noisy! Everyday, the little boy from down the street would take Rufus outside and wash him in the pond. One day, the little boy decided he didn't want to wash Rufus anymore. He stopped taking him outside to the pond. Rufus was so sad. He wanted to be washed, but the little boy just kept ignoring him. He scratched at his collar in frustration. One day, Rufus got so frustrated that he took off his collar and ran away. Even though the little boy chased after him, Rufus was never seen again. The end.

One day, a boy named Tim found a map. The map showed a big red X. Tim wanted to find the red X. He asked his friend, Sam, to help him. Sam said, "Yes! Let's go find the X!" They walked and walked until they saw a big rock. The map said the X was under the rock. Tim and Sam tried to push the rock, but it was too heavy. They felt frustrated. Tim said, "We need help to push the rock!" Just then, a big, friendly dog came to help them. The dog pushed the rock with his strong legs. The rock moved, and they saw the red X! But, under the X, there was a note. The note said, "Surprise! The real treasure is in your heart." Tim, Sam, and the dog laughed. They didn't find gold or toys, but they found a new friend. They were happy because they learned that friendship is the best treasure.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a red cap that he loved very much. He wore it every day. One day, he decided to play outside with his friends. They wanted to see who was the fastest. Tim and his friends started to run. They ran very fast. Tim was the fastest of all. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took Tim's cap away. It flew up in the sky. Tim was very sad. Tim and his friends decided to look for the cap. They looked everywhere. Then, they saw a big tree. In the tree, they saw a bird wearing Tim's cap! The bird liked the cap too. Tim asked the bird if he could have his cap back. The bird said yes, but only if Tim would be his friend. Tim agreed and they became friends. From that day on, Tim and the bird played together with the red cap.

Once upon a time, in a fair land, there lived a little girl named Lily. Lily had a soft voice that everyone loved. One day, she went to play near the big tree. While playing, Lily heard a voice coming from the tree. The voice mentioned a hidden treasure. Lily got very excited and wanted to find the treasure. Lily followed the voice to a dark cave. She was scared but wanted the treasure. Inside the cave, she found a big box. But when she opened the box, a mean monster jumped out and scared her away. Lily ran home, sad that she did not get the treasure.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One day, he saw a big mountain. He wanted to go to the top. Tim asked his friends to join him. They all said yes. They were fit and ready to go. They walked and walked until they got to the mountain. It was so big! They started to climb up. It was hard, but they were having fun. Tim and his friends helped each other when it got tough. At last, they reached the top of the mountain. They were so happy! They looked around and saw their home far away. They played on the mountain for a while. Then, they went back down. When they got home, they told everyone about their big adventure.

One day, a little girl decided to go for a walk in the park. As she walked along, she wanted to see who she could watch. Suddenly, she saw a little bird flying in the sky. It was so delicate! She watched the bird go higher and higher until it disappeared. The little girl was very happy and started to run. She felt like she was running to victory! Finally, she reached the end of the park and stopped. She looked behind her and saw a rainbow in the sky. She watched it with delight. The little girl was so excited that she started to jump around. She felt like she had achieved victory! She proudly said, "I watched the delicate bird and a beautiful rainbow." She smiled as she went back home.
