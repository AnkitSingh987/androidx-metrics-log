Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot and Amy and the Cat

Once upon a time, a loyal dog named Spot lived with a little girl named Amy. They loved to play together. One day, Amy and Spot went to the mall with Amy's mom. At the mall, they saw a big machine that could print pictures. Amy wanted to print a picture of her and Spot. So, Amy's mom helped her put the picture in the machine. They waited for the picture to come out. But, something unexpected happened! Instead of their picture, the machine printed a picture of a cat. Amy and Spot were surprised. They looked at the cat picture and then at each other. They laughed and decided to keep the cat picture too. This funny moment taught them that sometimes things don't go as planned, but that's okay. They learned to find joy in unexpected surprises and always stayed loyal to each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jill. She wanted to explore the town, but she was a bit scared. Mother said, "Don't worry, sweetie. Everything will be okay. Hold my hand and I'll show you the town." So, Jill held her mother's hand and they walked around the town. Jill saw lots of streets and people. She saw tall buildings and bridges. Everything looked so new. Jill also saw a tightrope on the side of the street. She asked, "What is that?" Mother said, "That's a tightrope. You can walk on it if you want to, but it's very tight." Jill was brave and she wanted to try it. With her mother's help, she stepped onto the tightrope. The rope felt taut and tight - she had to be very careful with her steps. But with practice, she was able to make it to the other side. Jill was so happy and proud of herself. The tightrope was one of Jill's favorite things that she saw in the town. She was able to explore even without being scared.

Anna and Ben were best friends. They liked to play in the park and share their toys. One day, they found a shiny coin on the ground. Anna picked it up and said, "Look, Ben, a coin! We can buy candy with it!" Ben smiled and said, "Or we can buy a ring and marry each other!" Anna giggled and said, "Okay, let's do that! You are my best friend and I love you!" They ran to the store and bought a ring with the coin. It was a plastic ring with a big red stone. They thought it was very pretty. They put the ring on Anna's finger and said, "I do!" They hugged and kissed and went back to the park. They saw a big dog with a long nose and a curly tail. It was barking and growling at them. They were scared and ran away. The dog chased them and tried to bite them. They saw a man with a stick and a hat. He was very ugly. He had a big nose and a bushy beard. He looked angry and mean. He shouted at the dog and hit it with the stick. The dog ran away and left them alone. Anna and Ben thanked the man and said, "You are very brave and kind! You saved us from the dog!" The man smiled and said, "You are welcome, kids. You are very cute and happy! You are married?" Anna and Ben nodded and showed him the ring. They said, "Yes, we are married! We love each other!" The man laughed and said, "That's wonderful! I wish you all the best! Here, take this coin. It's a lucky coin. It will bring you good things!" He gave them a shiny coin and waved goodbye. Anna and Ben thanked him and said, "Thank you, sir! You are very nice!" They ran to the store and bought candy with the coin. They shared the candy and ate it. They were very happy. They said, "We are married and we have a lucky coin! We are the luckiest kids in the world!"

Once upon a time, in a beautiful forest, there lived a little bunny. The bunny had many friends who played with him every day. They had so much fun running, jumping, and laughing together. One day, the bunny's friend, a squirrel, told him a secret. The bunny wanted to know if the secret was the truth. He asked his friend, "Is this true?" The squirrel did not answer. The bunny was not happy. He wanted to know the truth. The bunny went to another friend, a bird, and told her about the secret. The bird knew the truth. The bunny asked, "Please tell me the truth." The bird said, "You must be nice and ask me kindly." The bunny said, "Okay, I will be nice. Can you please tell me the truth?" The bird told the bunny the truth, and the bunny was happy. From that day on, the bunny always asked for things nicely and never demanded the truth again.
