Category: Intermediate
Title: The Great Bear and the Mouse

Once upon a time, there was a great big bear. The bear liked to walk on the earth. One day, the bear went for a walk in the woods. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. As the bear walked, he saw a big rock. But he did not see the small rock by it. The bear stumbled on the small rock and fell down. He felt sad and hurt. Just then, a tiny mouse came by. The mouse saw the great big bear on the earth. The mouse wanted to help the bear. The bear thought the mouse was too small to help. But the mouse was strong. The mouse pushed and pushed, and the great big bear was up again. The bear and the mouse became good friends. They learned that even the smallest friend can help in a big way.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to eat pastry. One day, she saw a thick, yummy pastry in the shop. She wanted to eat it so bad. Amy's mom told her, "If you win the race at school, I will buy you the pastry." Amy was very happy. She wanted to win the race and eat the yummy pastry. On the day of the race, Amy ran very fast. She tried her best and won the race. Her mom was so proud of her. They went to the shop and bought the thick pastry. Amy ate the pastry and it was very yummy.

One day, a small boy named Tim found a big, unusual bottle. It was not like other bottles he saw before. It had a funny top and strange pictures on it. Tim was very curious, so he decided to grab the bottle and show it to his mom. "Mom, look what I found!" said Tim, holding the bottle up high. "What is it?" His mom looked at the bottle and smiled. "That's glue, Tim! It's for sticking things together. But be careful, it can be very sticky!" Tim wanted to try the glue. He opened the bottle and put some on a piece of paper. He then put another piece of paper on top. The glue did its job, and the papers stuck together. Tim was so happy and showed his mom his new creation. From that day on, Tim used the unusual glue to make many fun things. He made a big tower with paper, a funny hat for his dog, and even a little house for his toys. Tim and his mom had a great time playing and creating with the glue. And they always remembered to be careful, because the glue was very sticky!

Once upon a time, there was a hero named Tim. Tim was an eager boy who always wanted to help others. He lived in a small village with his family and friends. One day, a big race was happening in the village. Tim wanted to win the race very much. So, he put on his fast shoes and went to the race. All his friends were there, and they were so excited to see who would win. The race started, and Tim ran very fast. He was eager to be the hero of the village. As he ran, he saw a friend who fell down. Tim stopped and helped his friend stand up. They ran together and crossed the finish line. Tim did not win the race, but he was still the hero because he helped his friend. Everyone was happy, and they all cheered for Tim, the eager hero.

Once upon a time there was a boy named Jack. He was very excited because he was going on an adventure. He went to the car with his mom and dad, and his dad told them he was going to get gas for the car. When Jack saw the gas station, he was so excited! Jack's dad got out of the car and told Jack to stay inside. But Jack wanted to explore! So he opened the door and ran to the gas station. He saw lots of cool things there and wanted to touch everything. After a little while, Jack's dad came back and told Jack to come back to the car. Jack was so happy that he had gone to the gas station. He told his mom and dad all about everything he had seen. Jack was so excited that he couldn't wait to tell his friends about his adventure.
