Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Balloon

One day, a little girl named Sue was very impatient. She wanted to go to the big festival in her town. Her mom and dad tell her they will go soon. Sue was so happy to hear that! On the day of the festival, Sue and her family went to the park. There were many games, food, and happy people. Sue saw a big, red balloon and wanted it. Her mom and dad tell her she can have it. Sue smiled and held her balloon tight. Sue had a great time at the festival. She played games, ate yummy food, and danced to fun music. When it was time to go home, Sue was tired but happy. She couldn't wait to tell her friends about the fun day at the festival.

Once upon a time, there was a great big cat. The cat liked to play with a toy arrow. One day, the cat saw a little bird. The cat wanted to play with the bird. So, the cat got ready to start the game. The cat threw the arrow up in the sky. The cat thought the bird would fly away. But the bird did not fly away. The bird caught the arrow in its beak. The cat was very surprised. The bird and the cat started to play with the arrow together. They became good friends. From that day on, the cat and the bird played with the arrow every day. They had lots of fun and were very happy.

One day, a pale cat named Tim went to the market. He wanted to buy some meat for his dinner. Tim saw his friend, a dog named Sam, at the market too. "Hi Sam!" said Tim. "Can you help me use my money to buy meat?" "Sure, Tim!" said Sam. They went to the meat shop together. Tim gave his money to the shop man and got some meat. Tim and Sam walked home. They were very happy. Tim cooked the meat and shared it with Sam. They ate and had a fun time. They were good friends.

Once upon a time, there were two friends named Frank and Fred. One day, Frank had an idea. He wanted to make butter. He set up a fancy machine in his backyard and collected some cream. But Fred thought it was foolish. "Why do you bother," he said. "It will never work!" Frank was determined to prove him wrong and he kept trying. He was careful and patient as he followed instructions. He added a pinch of salt and waited. Finally, the cream turned to butter! Frank was so excited, he ran to find Fred. But Fred was still ignorant. "How can that be butter?" he asked. Frank explained the process to him and pushed the warm butter into Fred's hands. He couldn't believe his eyes. "It's amazing," said Fred. "You are so clever!"

Once upon a time, there was a painter named Tom. He loved to paint all day. One day, he wanted to get new paint for his pictures. So, he went to the store. At the store, Tom met a man who was a little bit stupid. The man wanted to help Tom get paint. But he got the wrong paint. The paint was magic! Tom did not know that. Tom went home and used the magic paint. When he painted, something unexpected happened. The pictures came to life! Tom was so surprised. He and his new friends had a lot of fun. And the stupid man at the store became a hero for giving Tom the magic paint.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty vase in a big house. The vase was very happy because it had many flowers in it. Every day, the vase would talk to the flowers and they would discuss fun things. They all loved being together. One day, a little boy came to the house. He liked to play with his ball. The vase and the flowers saw the boy and knew that he might hit the vase with the ball. They were upset and worried. They talked about what they could do to stay safe. The next day, the little boy played with his ball near the vase. The vase and the flowers were very scared. But then, the boy's mom came and moved the vase to a higher place. Now, the vase and the flowers were safe and happy again. They were not upset anymore and continued to discuss fun things together.
