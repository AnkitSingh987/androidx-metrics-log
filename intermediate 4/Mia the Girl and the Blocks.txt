Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Blocks

One day, a little girl named Mia was playing with her blocks. She liked to stack them up high and make tall towers. Mia had many colors of blocks - red, blue, yellow, and green. She liked to mix the colors when she built her towers. Mia's mommy called her for lunch. She had made Mia's favorite food, a tasty sandwich. Mia took a big bite and said "Yummy!" She ate her sandwich very fast. Mia's mommy smiled and gave her a glass of milk to drink. After lunch, Mia went back to her blocks. She wondered how high she could stack them. She put one block on top of another, and then another, and another. The tower got very tall! Mia was happy and clapped her hands. She loved playing with her blocks.

Lily was a popular girl in her class. She had many friends who liked to play with her. She was good at sharing, singing and drawing. She always wore pretty dresses and ribbons in her hair. One day, Lily found a magic ring in the park. It was shiny and had a big red stone. She put it on her finger and felt very happy. She showed it to her friends and they all said wow. They wanted to try it too, but Lily said no. She wanted to keep it for herself. But the ring had a secret. It was a shrinking ring. Every time Lily said a word that started with S, she became smaller and smaller. She did not notice at first, but soon she was as small as a mouse. Her friends were scared and tried to help her. They called for a teacher and an ambulance. The ambulance came and took Lily to the hospital. The doctor looked at her and said she had to take off the ring. But Lily did not want to. She liked the ring and thought it was hers. She said no and shrunk even more. She was now as small as a bug. The doctor was very clever. He knew how to make Lily take off the ring. He told her a story about a princess who had a magic ring that made her popular. But the ring also made her shrink and lose her friends. The princess was very sad and lonely. She wished she could be big and happy again. She realized that the ring was not good for her and threw it away. Then she grew back to her normal size and hugged her friends. Lily listened to the story and felt sorry for the princess. She did not want to be sad and lonely. She wanted to be big and happy again. She realized that the ring was not good for her and took it off. Then she grew back to her normal size and hugged her friends. The doctor smiled and gave Lily a sticker. He said she was very brave and smart. He told her to share her toys and not to be greedy. Lily said sorry and thanked him. She gave the ring to the doctor and said he could keep it. She did not need it anymore. She had her friends and they liked her for who she was.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to wear an attractive bow in her hair. One day, she put on her favorite bow and went to play outside. Amy was playing hide and seek with her friends. She found a big tree to hide behind. Amy tried to peek out from behind the tree to see if her friends could find her. She was very quiet and still. Finally, her friend Tom found her. "I see your pretty bow!" he said. Amy laughed and they all played together until it was time to go home. Amy's attractive bow made her easy to find, but she didn't mind. She loved playing with her friends and wearing her favorite bow.

One day, a girl named Sue wanted to create a big art on her wall. She had many colors and papers to make it pretty. Sue was very happy to start her work. Sue used a pin to put her art up on the wall. Suddenly, the pin fell down and her art fell too. Sue was upset. She did not know what to do. She looked around and saw her dog, Max, with the pin in his mouth. Max gave the pin back to Sue. She was surprised! She did not know Max could help her. Sue was happy again. She put her art back up with the pin. Now, Sue and Max create art together all the time.
