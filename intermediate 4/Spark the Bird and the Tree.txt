Category: Intermediate
Title: Spark the Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Spark. Spark lived in a high tree with his family. He loved to fly and tell stories to his friends. One day, Spark's mom told him a secret. She said that there was a big surprise waiting for him, but he had to be patient. Spark was very excited and couldn't wait to find out what the surprise was. Days went by, and Spark kept looking for the surprise. He flew high and low, but he couldn't find it. Then one day, Spark found a big box under the high tree. He opened the box and saw a beautiful, shiny toy inside. Spark was so happy! His mom's secret was true, and the surprise was worth the wait.

Once upon a time, there was a loud cat named Tim. Tim liked to test his loud meow every day. One day, Tim said to his friend, "I will test my meow in the big park." The next day, Tim went to the big park. He saw many kids and pets. Tim thought, "This is a good place to test my loud meow." He took a deep breath and got ready. Just as Tim was about to meow, a big dog ran up to him. The dog said, "I heard you are loud. Let's test who is louder, you or me!" They both tested their loud voices. The big dog was louder than Tim. Tim was happy to find a new friend who was loud too. They played together at the park every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big, strong dog named Max. Max had big muscles and loved to run fast. One day, Max saw a little cat stuck in a tree. The cat looked scared and needed help. Max wanted to help the cat, but it was difficult for him to climb the tree because of his big muscles. Then, something unexpected happened! A tiny bird came to help Max. The bird was small, but it was very smart. The bird told Max to go find a long stick. Max did as the bird said, and the bird used the stick to help the cat escape from the tree. The cat was very happy and thanked Max and the tiny bird. Max learned that even though he had big muscles and was strong, he could not do everything alone. Sometimes, it takes friends with different skills to help each other. From that day on, Max, the cat, and the tiny bird became best friends, and they always helped each other when things were difficult.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play with toys and books. One day, they go to the library with their moms. They see a big book with many pictures of small things. The book says "microscope". "What is a microscope?" Anna asks. "A microscope is a thing that makes small things look big," Ben says. "Can we see a microscope?" Anna says. "Yes, come with me," Ben says. He takes Anna to a table where there is a microscope and some slides. He puts a slide under the microscope and looks through the eye-piece. "Wow, this is a leaf, but it looks like a green net," he says. "Can I see?" Anna says. "Sure, here you go," Ben says. He lets Anna look through the eye-piece. "Wow, this is amazing," Anna says. She sees many tiny cells and veins on the leaf. They take turns looking at different slides. They see a hair, a feather, a bug, and a flower. They are very happy and curious. "Look, this is a joke," Ben says. He puts a slide with a smiley face under the microscope. Anna looks and laughs. "That is funny," she says. They hear their moms calling them. "It is time to go, kids," they say. "Okay, mom," Anna and Ben say. They put the slides and the microscope back. They hug and say goodbye. "Thank you for showing me the microscope, Ben," Anna says. "You are welcome, Anna. It was fun," Ben says. They leave the library with their moms. They think the microscope is a fair and cool toy. They want to see more small things. They are still friends.
