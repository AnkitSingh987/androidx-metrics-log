Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a happy girl named Lily. She had a favorite mask. She liked to wear it when she played with her toys. One day, she wanted to play outside with her mask. But, oh no! Lily could not find her mask. She looked around her room. She looked under her bed. She looked in her toy box. Lily was sad. She thought she had lost her mask forever. Then, something unexpected happened. Lily's mom came into her room. She had the mask! Lily was so happy. She had just forgotten where she put it. Now, Lily could play outside with her mask and have fun. She learned to always remember where she put her things. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy who loved to draw. One sunny day, she went outside to play with her best friend, Tom. They decided to draw a big line on the ground with their chalk. Lucy said, "I love drawing lines! Let's make a long line together!" Tom smiled and agreed. They started at one end of the yard and drew a straight line all the way to the other end. They were very proud of their line. As they were drawing, they found a bitter lemon on the ground. Tom picked it up and said, "This lemon is very bitter! Let's not eat it." Lucy agreed and they continued to draw their line. When they finished, they looked at their line and felt happy.

Once upon a time, there was a mechanic named Tom. Tom was a very nice man who helped fix cars. He always tried to behave well and be kind to everyone. One day, Tom found a big, shiny coin on the street. He picked it up and took it home. The next day, he found out that the coin was very special. It made him very rich! Tom was so happy that he could buy lots of nice things. But he never forgot to behave well and be kind to others. He used his rich money to help people and fix their cars for free. Everyone loved Tom, the kind and helpful mechanic.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had a pretty pale dress that she loved to wear. One day, Lily's mom told her they were going to a big party. Lily was so happy and knew she wanted to wear her pale dress. Before the party, Lily had to help her mom work in the garden. Lily was careful not to get her dress dirty. She saw a big rock and thought it would be a good place to hide her dress. Lily put her dress behind the rock and went to work with her mom. After they finished working, it was time to get ready for the party. Lily ran to the rock to get her dress, but it was gone! She looked around and saw a bird flying away with her dress in its beak. Lily was sad, but her mom had an idea. They followed the bird to its nest and found the dress. Lily was so happy to wear her pale dress to the party, and they all had a great time.

Once upon a time, there was a lively sailor. He had a big boat. The sailor liked to kick a ball when he was not on the boat. One day, the sailor went to the park to kick his ball. He saw a little dog. The dog liked to kick the ball too. They played together and had fun. After they played, the sailor went back to his boat. He waved goodbye to the little dog. The sailor and the dog were happy. They knew they would play again soon.

One day, a little boat was in the water. The boat liked to float and play with the fish. The sun was shining and the water was calm. The boat was happy. Then, the sky turned dark and the wind began to blow. The waves got big and the boat started to drop up and down. The boat was scared. The fish swam away and the boat was all alone. An angry bird came and saw the boat. The bird didn't like the boat in the water. The bird tried to push the boat away. But the boat didn't want to go. The sun came back and the water got calm again. The angry bird flew away and the boat was happy. The fish came back and played with the boat once more.
