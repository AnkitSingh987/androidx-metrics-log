Category: Intermediate
Title: The Farm and the Mouse

Once upon a time, there was a big farm. On this farm, there were many animals. They were all friends. One day, a little adorable mouse met a big, strong horse. The mouse said, "You are so big, and I am so small. But I think we can be friends." The horse said, "Yes, let's be friends." After some time, the mouse and the horse decided to marry. They had a big party in the wheat field. All their friends came. They ate lots of yummy food and danced. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a healthy boy who loved to play outside with his friends. One day, Tim saw the mail truck come down his street. He was very excited because he loved getting mail from his grandma. Tim ran to the mailbox and found a letter from his grandma. He opened it and saw that she requested him to draw a picture of his favorite animal. Tim loved to draw, so he went inside and got his crayons and paper. Tim drew a big, happy dog and showed it to his mom. She said it was a great picture and helped him put it in an envelope to send back to his grandma. Tim was so happy that he could share his drawing with his grandma through the mail.

Ben and Lily were brother and sister. They liked to play together in their bedroom. They had many toys and books and games. But sometimes they also fought and made noise. One day, they were playing with their cars and dolls. They pretended they were driving and talking on the phone. They made loud sounds and laughed. But they did not hear their mom calling them for lunch. "Ben! Lily! Come downstairs now! It's time to eat!" Mom shouted from the kitchen. But Ben and Lily did not listen. They kept playing and having fun. Mom was worried. She wondered why they did not answer. She went upstairs to check on them. She opened the door of their bedroom and saw them playing. She was angry. "Ben! Lily! How many times do I have to call you? Why are you not listening to me?" Mom asked. She looked at their toys and books and games. They were everywhere. The bedroom was messy. "Sorry, Mom. We did not hear you. We were playing," Ben said. "We were having fun, Mom. We like our toys and books and games," Lily said. Mom shook her head. She was not happy. She said, "You have to listen to me when I call you. And you have to clean up your bedroom. It's too messy. You have to learn to be tidy and respectful. Now, go downstairs and eat your lunch. And after that, you have to come back and put away your toys and books and games. Or else, I will send them away." Ben and Lily were scared. They did not want to lose their toys and books and games. They loved them. They said, "No, Mom. Please don't send them away. We will listen to you. We will clean up our bedroom. We promise." Mom nodded. She said, "OK. But you have to keep your promise. And you have to be quiet and nice to each other. No more fighting and making noise. Do you understand?" Ben and Lily nodded. They said, "Yes, Mom. We understand. We are sorry. We will be good." Mom smiled. She said, "Good. Now, go and eat your lunch. And then, come back and clean up your bedroom. I will help you." Ben and Lily hugged Mom. They said, "Thank you, Mom. We love you." Mom hugged them back. She said, "I love you too. Now, hurry up. Your lunch is getting cold."

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Jenny. She wore an ordinary belt around her waist. Today she was feeling brave, so she decided to try something new. “Mama, I want to quit my belt,” she said. Her mother looked at her and smiled. “Okay, sweetheart, go ahead,” her mother said. Jenny carefully undid her belt and felt the buckle loosen in her hands. She smiled with excitement and held out her belt. “Look Mama, I quit my belt!” she exclaimed. Her mother smiled and gave her a thumbs up. “Good job, Jenny! You did something ordinary in a brave new way!” she said. Jenny was so proud of herself. She knew she could do anything if she put her mind to it. She smiled and hugged her belt close.
