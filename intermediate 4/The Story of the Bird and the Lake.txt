Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Bird and the Lake

One day, a big bird and a small bird were playing near a deep lake. The big bird saw a shiny thing in the water. It wanted to get it, but the big bird's neck was not long enough. The small bird had a long neck and said, "I can help you get the shiny thing." The big bird and the small bird started to argue. "No, I want to get it myself!" said the big bird. The small bird felt sad but still wanted to help its friend. So, it waited for the right time. Soon, it started to rain. The rain made the lake water rise. The small bird saw the shiny thing getting closer to the top. It said to the big bird, "Look, the rain is helping us! Now you can reach the shiny thing!" The big bird smiled and said, "Thank you, small bird, for being a good friend." They both got the shiny thing together and played happily in the rain. The big bird learned that it is good to have friends who help each other.

Once upon a time there was a girl named Sally. She was three years old and loved receiving mail. Every day she would wait by the mailbox in anticipation of a letter. One day, Sally dreamt of receiving a mysterious package. It would be filled with something magical! She pictured it in her head and it made her smile. The very next day, Sally excitedly checked the mailbox. To her surprise, there was a mysterious package waiting for her. She opened it to find a beautiful toy unicorn inside! Sally was overjoyed and hugged it close to her heart. She thanked the mail for bringing such a wonderful mysterious gift to her and ran off to show her parents. From that day on, Sally always looked forward to opening her mail.

One day, a strong doctor named Sam went for a walk. He saw a big tree and wanted to turn around it. As he walked, he met a small dog. The dog said, "I don't feel good. Can you help me?" Doctor Sam looked at the dog and said, "I can help you. Let's go to my house." They walked together to the doctor's house. Doctor Sam gave the dog some water and a soft bed to lie on. The dog started to feel better. The next day, the dog felt much better. He jumped up and said, "Thank you, Doctor Sam!" Just then, the dog started to turn into a big, strong lion! Doctor Sam was very surprised. The lion said, "I am a magic lion. I wanted to see if you would help me when I was small and weak. You are a kind doctor." Doctor Sam and the lion became good friends, and they helped each other every day.

Sara liked to go to the beach with her mom and dad. She liked to play in the sand and look for shells and rocks. Sometimes she found oysters, the big shells that had a pearl inside. She hoped to find a pearl one day, because they were shiny and pretty. One day, she saw a big oyster on the shore. She ran to pick it up, but it was too heavy for her. She asked her dad to help her. He lifted the oyster and opened it for her. Inside, there was a pearl! Sara was so happy. She thanked her dad and hugged the oyster. She wanted to keep the pearl, but then she saw the oyster was still alive. It had a mouth and eyes and looked sad. Sara felt sorry for the oyster. She remembered that the pearl was its home and its treasure. She decided to be compassionate and give the pearl back to the oyster. She put the pearl inside the oyster and closed the shell. She said goodbye to the oyster and put it back in the water. She hoped the oyster would be happy and safe. She smiled and went back to play with her mom and dad. She knew she had done a good thing.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a big dog with a tough ball in its mouth. Tim wanted to play with the dog and the ball. He asked his mom, "Can I play with the dog?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful. The dog might bite." Tim went to the dog and asked, "Can I play with your ball?" The dog wagged its tail and dropped the ball. Tim picked up the ball and threw it. The dog ran to get it and brought it back. They played together and had fun. But then, something unexpected happened. When Tim tried to get the ball from the dog's mouth, the dog accidentally bit Tim's lip. Tim cried and ran to his mom. His mom hugged him and said, "I told you to be careful." From that day, Tim learned to be more careful when playing with dogs.
