Category: Intermediate
Title: The Noisy Clown and the Shoes

Once upon a time, there was a noisy clown. He liked to make people laugh. He had big shoes and a red nose. The clown lived in a fun town with many friends. One day, the clown went to a party. At the party, he could hear children laughing. They liked his funny tricks. He made balloon animals and did a silly dance. Everyone was happy. As the sun went down, the noisy clown said goodbye to his friends. He went home with a big smile on his face. He knew he made the children happy. The clown went to sleep, excited for the next fun day.

Tom and Sue like to play with blocks. They make towers and houses and bridges. They have many blocks of different colors and shapes. One day, they see a big box in the living room. It has a picture of a big block set on it. They run to the box and open it. Inside, there are many new blocks. They are bigger and brighter than their old blocks. They also have numbers and letters on them. "Wow, look at these blocks!" Tom says. "They are so cool! Let's play with them!" "OK, but we have to share them," Sue says. "We can take turns to pick one block and add it to our tower." They agree and start to build a big tower with the new blocks. They count the numbers and say the letters as they add them. They have fun and learn new things. But then, they see a small block at the bottom of the box. It is ugly. It is brown and has no number or letter on it. It also has a sticker that says "prize". "What is this?" Tom asks. "It is ugly. It is not like the other blocks." "Maybe it is a prize," Sue says. "Maybe it does something special. Let's add it to our tower and see what happens." They add the ugly block to the top of their tower. Suddenly, the block makes a loud noise. It says "Surprise! You win a prize! Press the button and see what you get!" Tom and Sue are surprised and excited. They press the button on the ugly block. The block opens and a small toy comes out. It is a cute puppy that barks and wags its tail. "Wow, it is a puppy!" Tom says. "It is so cute! I love it!" "Me too!" Sue says. "It is the best prize ever! The ugly block was not so bad after all!" They hug the puppy and play with it. They also play with their new blocks. They are happy and share their prize. They learn that sometimes, ugly things can be good.

Once upon a time, there was a cat named Kitty. Kitty was very patient. She liked to sit and wait for her friend, a little girl named Emma. Emma loved playing with Kitty. One day, Emma found a box. She thought it would be fun for Kitty to play in it. "Kitty, do you want to play in the box?" Emma asked. Kitty meowed and went to the box. But the box was too small. Kitty tried to fit, but she could not. Emma saw that Kitty could not fit in the box. She looked for a bigger box. Emma found a big box and said, "Kitty, try this box!" Kitty tried the big box, and she fit! Kitty and Emma played with the box all day. They were very happy.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Lily loved the color orange. She had orange clothes, orange toys, and even an orange cat named Max. One sunny day, Lily and Max went for a walk. They saw a big orange barrel in the park. Lily was so excited! She wanted to take the barrel home. So, Lily and Max pushed the barrel all the way to their house. When they got home, Lily's mom and dad were surprised to see the big orange barrel. They helped Lily clean it and put it in the backyard. Lily and Max played with the barrel every day. They would hide in it, roll it around, and even pretend it was a spaceship. And they all lived happily ever after.
