Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Dog

Once there was a little girl named Amy. She had a big network of friends. They all liked to play together. One day, they decided to celebrate Amy's birthday. They wanted to eat healthy food and have fun. Amy's friends were talking about the party. "Let's get healthy snacks," said one friend. "Yes, and we can play fun games too," said another friend. They were all very excited. On the day of the party, Amy's friends set up everything. But then, something unexpected happened. A big, funny dog came to the party. The dog was wearing a party hat! Everyone laughed and played with the dog. The party was even better than they planned.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. It was very cold and bitter outside. Tim wore a big coat, a warm hat, and mittens. His mittens were red and soft. As Tim walked, he saw a cat. The cat was cold too. The cat said, "Meow, can you help me?" Tim wanted to help the cat. He asked, "What do you need?" The cat said, "I need a warm place to stay." Tim thought for a moment. He took off one of his mittens and put it on the cat. The cat was happy and warm. Together, they walked back to Tim's house. They played and had fun all day.

One day, two friends decided to go on a special journey together. They brought a small boat and sailed away. As they sailed, they began to quarrel. They started to yell and raise their voices. The boat was very fragile and when they quarreled, it began to shake. Suddenly, it cracked right in the middle and started to sink. The two friends tried to grab pieces of driftwood to keep afloat, but the waves were too strong and they were pulled apart. The two friends were so far away from each other, calling each other's names. But their voices were drowned out by the sound of the waves. It was a sad ending for the two friends who had started their voyage together. They drifted away from each other, never to be seen again.

Lily loved to paint. She had many colors of paint and many brushes. She liked to paint flowers and trees and animals on paper. She always showed her paintings to her mom and dad, who said they were very pretty. One day, Lily found a new paint in her box. It was white. She wondered what she could paint with white. She looked around her room and saw her doll. Her doll had a delicate dress with lace and ribbons. Lily thought her doll would look nice with some white paint. She took her doll and her white paint and a brush. She started to paint her doll's dress. She painted white dots and stripes and swirls. She thought her doll looked like a snowflake. "Look, Mommy, look what I did!" Lily said, holding up her doll. "I painted her dress white. Do you like it?" Mommy came to see Lily's doll. She smiled, but she also looked a little sad. She said, "Lily, your doll is very beautiful, but you know you shouldn't paint on your toys. The paint might ruin them. And this dress was very delicate. It was a gift from Grandma. She made it for you with love." Lily felt sorry. She didn't know she did something bad. She said, "I'm sorry, Mommy. I just wanted to make her pretty. I love Grandma and I love her dress. Can we fix it?" Mommy hugged Lily and said, "It's okay, sweetie. I know you didn't mean to hurt your doll. We can try to wash the paint off, but it might not come off completely. Next time, if you want to paint something white, you can use paper or canvas. They are made for painting. And you can still paint flowers and trees and animals with your other colors. They are very pretty, too." Lily nodded and said, "Okay, Mommy. Thank you. I love you." Mommy said, "I love you, too, Lily. Now let's go and see if we can clean your doll."

Once upon a time, there was a successful dad who loved to build things. He had a daughter named Lily. Lily liked to watch her dad build. One day, Lily asked her dad, "Can I help you build?" Dad said, "Yes, Lily! Let's build a big house for your dolls." So, they started to build the house. They used toy blocks to make the walls and a big piece of paper for the roof. Lily was very happy. When they finished building the house, Lily said, "Thank you, Dad! I love our house." Dad smiled and said, "You did a great job, Lily! I am proud of you." They played with the dollhouse all day, and Lily knew she could build anything with her dad's help.
