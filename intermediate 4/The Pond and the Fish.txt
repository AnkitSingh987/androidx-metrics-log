Category: Intermediate
Title: The Pond and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a small pond. In the pond, there were fish, frogs, and ducks. They all played together and had fun. They liked to include everyone in their games. One day, a man came to the pond. He wanted to catch the fish and sell them for cheap. The fish did not want to be caught and sold. They were scared and did not know what to do. The man caught many fish and took them away. The pond was sad and quiet. The fish friends were gone, and the frogs and ducks missed them. The pond was not a happy place anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a smelly dishwasher. It was very sad because it made all the dishes smelly too. No one wanted to use the smelly dishwasher. They wanted to find out why it was smelly. One day, a little girl named Sue saw a sign on the dishwasher. The sign said, "Help! I am smelly because I am dirty inside." Sue wanted to help the smelly dishwasher. She asked her mom to help her clean it. Sue and her mom cleaned the smelly dishwasher. They took out all the dirty things that were stuck inside. The dishwasher was not smelly anymore. Everyone was happy to use the clean dishwasher. The dishwasher was happy too, and it thanked Sue and her mom with a big smile.

One day, a boy named Tim found a rare vest in a box. He liked the vest a lot. It was a bit dirty, so he thought he should wash it. He asked his mom, "Can I wash this vest, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful. It is very old." Tim washed the vest with soap and water. He was very happy to see it clean. He wore the vest and went to play outside with his friends. They all liked his vest and wanted to know where he found it. While they were playing, a bird came and took the vest from Tim. Everyone was surprised. The bird flew away with the vest. Tim was sad, but his friends told him, "Don't worry, we will help you find another one." They all looked for a new vest, but they could not find one. Tim learned that some things are rare and cannot be replaced. He also learned that his friends were always there to help him.

Once upon a time, there was a rose. The rose was very old. It was in a big garden. The rose was red and pretty. One day, the rose saw a little girl. The girl came to the garden. She wanted to play with the rose. The rose was happy to have a friend. The girl and the rose played together. They had fun. Then, it was time for the girl to go home. The rose was sad to see her friend leave. But they knew they would play again soon.

One day, a boy named Tim found a perfect shirt. It was red and blue, and it made him very happy. He wanted to show his friend, Sam. So, he ran to Sam's house. "Look at my perfect shirt!" said Tim to Sam. Sam liked the shirt too. They decided to play a game outside. They played with a big ball, and they had a lot of fun. As they played, the ball went into the bushes. Tim went to get the ball, but something bad happened. His perfect shirt got stuck on a branch. The shirt started to fill with air, and it got bigger and bigger. "Oh no!" said Tim, as the shirt filled with so much air that it lifted him off the ground. Sam tried to help, but it was too late. Tim floated away in his perfect shirt, and Sam was very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max loved to eat sausages. One day, Max saw a sausage on the ground. It was the biggest sausage he had ever seen! Max was very happy. Max picked up the big sausage and carried it home. He met his friend, a small cat named Luna. Luna saw the big sausage and said, "Wow, Max! That is a big sausage! You should value it and save it for later." Max thought about it and decided to hide the sausage under his bed. Later that day, Max and Luna played outside. They had so much fun! But Max forgot about the big sausage under his bed. When they went back inside, they found the big sausage was gone! Max was sad, but Luna said, "Don't worry, Max. Next time, we will value the sausages more and eat them right away." From that day on, Max and Luna always valued their sausages and enjoyed them together. And they lived happily ever after.
