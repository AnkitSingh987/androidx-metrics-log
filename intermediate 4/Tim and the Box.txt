Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very dull boy who didn't like to play with his friends. One day, Tim saw a big box with a turn on it. He was very curious about what was inside the box. Tim went to the box and tried to open it. But the box was shut very tight. He tried and tried but he could not open it. Then, Tim asked his friends for help. His friends came and they all tried to open the box together. With their help, the box opened and they saw many colorful toys inside. Tim and his friends were very happy. They played with the new toys all day long. Tim learned that when you work together with your friends, you can do anything. From that day on, Tim was not a dull boy anymore. He played with his friends every day and they had lots of fun. The moral of the story is that teamwork makes things easier and more fun.

Once upon a time, there were two friends called Andy and Jack. They were playing together in the park. The park was wide, and there were lots of trees. Andy and Jack went to look at the big pond, where they saw some ducks swimming around. Suddenly, Andy slipped and fell into the pond! He started to scream for help, but Jack couldn't get him out. Suddenly, Jack saw a saw in the park. He grabbed the saw and sawed the trunk of a big tree. The tree trunk fell over and made a bridge to where Andy was in the water. Jack quickly grabbed Andy and got him out of the water. But when they looked up, they noticed something moving in the branches of the tree. It was a big, angry bear! Andy and Jack were so scared that they ran away as fast as they could. Andy and Jack learned a lesson that day - never mess around with a bear!

Once upon a time, there was a king who loved to fly. He had big wings that let him fly high in the sky. The king was very calm and kind to everyone in his land. One day, a little bird came to the king. The bird said, "Oh, King, can you teach me to fly like you?" The king smiled and said, "Of course, little bird. I will help you fly." The king and the little bird went to the top of a hill. The king showed the bird how to spread its wings and jump into the wind. The little bird tried to fly but fell down. The king said, "Don't worry, little bird. You can try again." The little bird tried again and again. Each time, the king was calm and kind, helping the bird learn to fly. Finally, the little bird flew high in the sky, just like the king. The king and the little bird became good friends. They flew together every day, making everyone in the land happy. And the little bird always remembered the calm and kind king who taught it to fly.

One day, a little fish named Fin swam in a big pond. Fin was not a big fish, but he was a mighty fish. He liked to play with his friends and swim fast. One day, Fin saw a big fish stuck in a net. The big fish said, "Help me, please!" Fin knew he had to hurry to save his new friend. He swam fast and used his mighty tail to break the net. The big fish was free! "Thank you, Fin!" said the big fish. They swam and played together. The other fish saw how kind and strong Fin was. They learned that even little fish can do big things when they help others. The moral of the story is that you don't have to be big to be mighty and help others.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to joke and play with his friends. One day, he saw a big package near his house. It was a brilliant, shiny box. Tim was very excited to see what was inside. He called his friends to come and look at the package. They all gathered around and started to guess what could be inside. Tim thought it was a big toy, and his friends thought it was a cake or a new ball to play with. They all laughed and joked as they tried to open the package. As they opened the package, they found a big, shiny mirror inside. They all looked at the mirror and saw their happy faces. But then, something unexpected happened. Their faces in the mirror started to joke and play, just like them! Tim and his friends were surprised, but they laughed and played with their new mirror friends. And from that day on, they had even more fun together.
