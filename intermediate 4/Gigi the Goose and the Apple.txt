Category: Intermediate
Title: Gigi the Goose and the Apple

Once upon a time, there was a goose named Gigi. Gigi lived on a farm with her friends. She had fun playing in the mud all day. One day, Gigi got very dirty. She went to her friend, a cow named Cici. Gigi said, "Cici, I am dirty. Can you help me wipe the mud off?" Cici said, "Yes, Gigi. I can help you." Cici used her tail to wipe the mud off Gigi. Gigi was happy and said, "Thank you, Cici." After that, Gigi and Cici went to eat some food. They found a big, sour apple on the ground. They shared the apple and laughed. They were happy to be clean and full.

One day, a funny bird wanted to fly. The bird had big, pretty wings. It was a sunny day, perfect for flying. The bird was very happy. The funny bird flew high in the sky. It saw water and a shore. On the shore, the bird saw a little girl. The girl was playing with a ball. The bird wanted to play too. The bird flew down to the shore. It said, "Hi, can I play with you?" The girl smiled and said, "Yes, let's play!" They played with the ball and had a lot of fun. The funny bird and the girl became good friends.

Lily was a smart girl who liked to help her mom at the hospital. She admired the doctors and nurses who made people feel better. Sometimes, she got to watch them do an operation, where they opened up someone's body and fixed what was wrong. One day, she saw a doctor do an operation on a boy who had a bad heart. The doctor used a special machine that made the boy's heart stop and start again. Lily was amazed by how the doctor knew what to do. "Wow, you are so brave and smart, doctor!" Lily said to him after the operation. "How did you learn to do that?" The doctor smiled and thanked Lily for her kind words. He told her that he studied a lot of books and practiced a lot of times. He said that he loved his job because he could help people like the boy. Lily nodded and said, "I want to be like you when I grow up. I want to help people too." The doctor hugged Lily and said, "I'm sure you will, Lily. You have a good heart and a curious mind. Just keep learning and trying new things. And always admire those who do good in the world."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Emma. She had a regular toy named Mr. Bunny. They played together every day and had lots of fun. One day, Emma fell down and hurt her leg. Her mom took her to the hospital. Emma was scared, but Mr. Bunny was with her. They met a nice doctor who looked at her leg. The doctor told Emma to lie down on the big bed. She held Mr. Bunny tight. The doctor put a bandage on her leg. Soon, Emma felt better. She went home with her mom and Mr. Bunny. They were all happy, and Emma could play again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a little girl named Lily. One day, she found an unknown toy in her room. It was a small, blue robot. Lily was very happy and wanted to play with it. Lily asked the robot, "What can you do?" The robot said, "I can help you organize your toys." So, they started to put all the toys in their right places. The robot was very helpful, and Lily's room was soon very neat. The next day, Lily found a message on a piece of paper. It said, "Thank you for finding my robot. Can I have it back? - Your friend, Tim." Lily was sad, but she knew she had to give the robot back. She took the robot to Tim's house, and they became good friends. They played with the robot together, and it helped them organize their toys.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat tasty food. One day, Tim's mom made him a tasty cake. Tim was very happy. Tim wanted to eat the cake, but first, he needed to open the big box where the cake was. He tried to open it, but it was hard. Then, Tim had an idea. He would find a match to help him open the box. Tim found a match and used it to open the box. The box opened, and Tim saw the tasty cake inside. He was so happy that he shared the tasty cake with his friends. They all enjoyed the cake and had a fun day.
