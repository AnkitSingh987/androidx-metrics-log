Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Skin

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had an important job. He had to spread lotion on his skin. His mommy told him it would keep his skin soft and nice. One day, Tim went to play outside. He forgot to spread the lotion on his skin. The sun was very hot, and Tim played for a long time. When Tim came back inside, his skin was red and hurt a lot. Tim was sad because he did not do his important job. Now he knows that he should always spread lotion on his skin before playing outside.

One day, Lilly was looking up at the sky. She saw the bright stars twinkling and twirling. She imagined they were fairies dancing in the night sky. She asked her Mom, "Mom, what is that?" Her Mom replied, smiling, "That is the universe. It's very big and it's very far away." Lilly looked at the stars and thought about how enormous it was. She asked, "What is the universe?". Her Mom explained, "It's a very large and difficult thing to understand. It's made up of the stars, planets, moons, and all the galaxies. It's the world, Lilly. Everything and everyone is all part of it." Lilly felt really small compared to the huge universe and she said, "Wow. That's really amazing." Lilly and her Mom stayed outside and watched the stars until it was time to sleep. Lilly kept thinking about the universe as she drifted off to sleep.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big pocket on his shirt. One day, he found a small bird with a hurt wing. The bird could not fly, so Tim wanted to help it. Tim put the bird in his pocket to keep it safe. He took the bird to his mom. She knew how to rescue hurt animals. She took care of the bird and made it feel better. Tim was happy to see the bird getting better. After a few days, the bird's wing was all better. It was time for the bird to go back to its family. Tim took the bird outside and opened his pocket. The bird flew out and up into the sky. Tim's pocket was empty, but he was happy because he had helped rescue the bird.

Once upon a time, there was a big, reliable car named Tom. Tom had a loud horn that he loved to honk. One day, Tom saw a sign that said, "Big Race Today!" Tom wanted to join the race and celebrate with his friends. Tom went to the race and saw many cars. Some were fast, some were slow, but Tom knew he was reliable. Before the race started, Tom honked his horn very loud. All the cars looked at him and smiled. They knew that Tom's horn was special. The race began and all the cars zoomed off. Tom was not the fastest, but he did not stop. He remembered his loud horn and honked it as he passed each car. At the end of the race, Tom won! All his friends cheered and celebrated with him. Tom was so happy that his reliable horn helped him win the race.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a pretty, orange dress that she liked to wear. Mia loved to play with her toys every day. One day, while playing, Mia found a big, shiny lock on a small box. She wanted to open the box, but she didn't have a key. Mia tried to pull the lock, but it was too strong. Mia didn't give up. She tried to tear the lock using her hands. It didn't work, but she kept trying. After a while, the lock opened! Inside the box, she found a beautiful toy. Mia was very happy and played with her new toy all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a naughty little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy wheel. He would roll it all around the house and make it go fast. One day, Tim's mommy asked him to help her number the cups for a party. "Tim, can you help me put the numbers on the cups?" she asked. Tim said, "Yes, mommy!" He took his wheel and rolled it to the table. But instead of helping, he started playing with his wheel. He rolled it over the cups and knocked them down. "Oh no, Tim!" said mommy, "That's very naughty! We need to number the cups for the party." Tim felt sorry and said, "I'm sorry, mommy. I'll help now." So Tim and his mommy numbered the cups together. They counted one, two, three, and so on. When they were done, Tim's mommy was very happy. "Thank you, Tim!" she said with a smile. From that day on, Tim learned to be helpful and not so naughty. He still loved to play with his wheel, but he also learned when it was time to help his mommy. And they had a wonderful party with all the numbered cups.
