Category: Intermediate
Title: The in Town and the Star

Once upon a time, a huge blueberry lived in a small town. The blueberry was very big and round. Everyone in the town loved the blueberry because it was so big. One day, the town had a big party to display the huge blueberry. All the people came to see it. They danced, played games, and had fun. The huge blueberry was the star of the party. But then, something unexpected happened. The huge blueberry started to roll away! It rolled faster and faster. The people were scared and ran after it. The blueberry rolled into a big pond and splashed water everywhere. The people laughed and clapped. The huge blueberry was now a fun water play thing for the town.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a long cord in his room. He did not know what to do with it. So, he went to his big sister, Sue. Sue was smart and always knew what to do with things. "Hello, Sue," said Tim. "I found this cord. What should I do with it?" Sue looked at the cord and thought for a moment. "I recommend we use it to make a scary mask," she said with a smile. Tim liked the idea and they both started to work on the mask. They cut out some eyes and a mouth from the cord. When they were done, Tim put the mask on. It looked very scary! Tim and Sue laughed and played with the mask all day. They had lots of fun, and Tim was happy that he had found the cord.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly cat named Bob. Bob loved to eat. Every day, Bob would eat and play with his friends in the big network of trees. One day, while Bob was eating his favorite food, he saw a big bird in the network. The bird was not like the other birds. It was big and had a long neck. Bob was curious and wanted to play with the bird. Bob jumped up to the network to meet the bird. But, the bird was not a bird at all! It was a big balloon with a long string. Bob was surprised, but he was happy to have a new friend. From that day on, Bob and the big balloon played together in the network of trees.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely toy. This toy was a little snowman. He lived in a cold room all by himself. He wished he had a friend to play with. One day, the sun came out and shone through the window. The snowman saw the sun and felt happy. He thought maybe he could find a friend today. The snowman did not know that when the sun shines on snow, it can melt. As the sun kept shining, the snowman started to feel smaller. He did not know what was happening. He was scared but still hoped to find a friend. As the snowman melted, a little girl came into the room. She saw the melting snowman and felt sad. She quickly put him in the cold freezer. The snowman froze again and felt better. The little girl played with the snowman every day. The lonely toy was not lonely anymore. The snowman was happy to have a friend who saved him from melting.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim. He loved dessert. His favorite was ice cream. One day, his mom made a big bowl of ice cream for him. Tim's friend, Sam, came to play. They played with their toys and had lots of fun. But, they made a big mess in the room. Tim's mom saw the mess and said, "You must restore the room and make it clean again." Tim and Sam worked together to put the toys back. They found out that some toys were useful to help them clean up. A toy truck helped carry the small toys back to their place. After they restored the room, Tim's mom was happy. She gave them the ice cream as a treat for their hard work. They enjoyed the dessert and learned that it was good to clean up after playing.
