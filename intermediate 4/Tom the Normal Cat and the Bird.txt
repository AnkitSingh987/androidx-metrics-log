Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Normal Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a normal cat named Tom. Tom had a big job. He was a guard for a big house. Every day, he would watch the house and make sure it was safe. One day, Tom saw a little bird named Tim. Tim was very hungry. Tom felt bad for Tim and wanted to help. Tom said, "I will serve you some food, Tim." Tim was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tom!" But, then something strange happened. Tim grew very big! He was not a normal bird anymore. Now, Tom and Tim were both guards for the big house. They worked together and were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big pumpkin. It was very tight. It lived in a small house. The pumpkin had a friend. The friend was a little boy. The little boy liked the pumpkin very much. One day, the little boy saw that the pumpkin was sad. The pumpkin said, "I need help. I am too tight." The little boy wanted to help the pumpkin. He thought and thought about how to help the pumpkin. The little boy tried to make the pumpkin less tight. He pushed and pulled the pumpkin. But the pumpkin stayed tight. The little boy was sad. The pumpkin was still tight and sad. The little boy could not help the pumpkin. The pumpkin stayed tight forever.

One day, Lucy was sitting in her garden when she noticed something big and black in the bushes. She got closer and peered in to try and find out what it was. She shouted out "Whats in there?" Suddenly, out from the bushes popped a big black hat. Lucy jumped with surprise and said "Wow! Where did you come from?" The hat replied, "I fell off the market stall down the road. I've been stuck here for days". Lucy said "Wow! Why don't I take you home with me?" The hat said "That sounds great - but I'm afraid someone might be looking for me!" Lucy said "Don't worry! I will guess what happened to you and try to help you find your owner. In the mean time, you can stay with me. What do you say?" The hat smiled and said "That sounds like a good plan". And so the hat ended up in Lucy's bedroom and the two of them soon became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy ostrich. Every day she would just sit and do nothing. One day, something happened. The ostrich decided to go on an adventure! She started walking in the sun, with nothing but her lazy steps taking her wherever they wanted to go. Suddenly, she saw something shiny in the grass. It was a sparkly bracelet! The ostrich was so excited; it was her favourite colour! She picked it up so she could wear it. Then, all of a sudden, the bracelet became too tight on her foot. She had to remove it. Gently, she carefully used her beak and claws to take the bracelet off her foot. With a bit of effort and a few tries, she was finally able to remove it. Now, the lazy ostrich was free to go on her adventure without the bracelet slowing her down. She walked off, happy and full of excitement.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy little girl named Lily. She liked to count things. One day, while playing in her room, she saw a funny bug on her shoulder. "Hello bug!" said Lily. "Let's count together!" The bug nodded its head. So, Lily and the bug started to count toys in the room. They counted one, two, three, four, five toys. But then, the bug started to feel sick. It fell off Lily's shoulder and onto the floor. "Oh no, bug!" cried Lily. "What can I do to help you?" The bug didn't say anything. It just lay there, not moving. Lily tried to help the bug, but it was too late. The bug didn't get better, and it didn't wake up. Lily was very sad. She had lost her new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little hidden house made of bricks. In this house lived a kind boy named Tim and his small cat, Mimi. They liked to play fun games together. One sunny day, Tim and Mimi went outside to play. They saw a big pile of bricks. Tim said, "Let's build a tower with these bricks!" Mimi meowed happily and they started to build. They put one brick on top of another until the tower was very tall. As they were playing, a tiny bug came to watch. The bug wanted to play too, but it pinched Tim's finger. "Ouch!" Tim cried. Mimi saw the bug and said, "Don't pinch my friend!" The bug felt sad and said, "I'm sorry, I just wanted to play too." Tim and Mimi let the bug join them, and they all played together. They built an even bigger tower with the bricks. And from that day on, they were the best of friends.
