Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Shoes

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a lot of shoes. He was selfish and did not like to share his shoes with others. He loved his shoes so much that he would not provide any to his friends. One day, while Tim was playing with his shoes, a magic shoe appeared. The magic shoe could talk! It said, "Tim, you need to learn to share your shoes with others." Tim was not happy about this, but he listened to the magic shoe. The next day, Tim decided to provide his shoes to his friends. As he shared his shoes, something unexpected happened. The magic shoe turned all of his shoes into fun toys! Tim and his friends played with the toys and had a great time. Tim learned that sharing is more fun than being selfish.

One day, a smelly dog named Spot wanted to play with his friends. He went to the park and saw his friends playing a game. They were trying to do a score by kicking a ball into a net. Spot wanted to join them and have fun. Spot ran up to his friends and said, "Can I play too?" His friends smiled and said, "Yes, let's all play together!" Spot was very happy. They let him do a score by kicking the ball. He ran and kicked the ball with his paw. The ball went into the net and everyone cheered. After playing, Spot and his friends were tired and smelly. They all went to the pond to wash off the dirt. They splashed and played in the water. Spot was very happy to have such good friends. They all went home and took a nap, dreaming of the fun they had.

---

One day, a big group of friends went on a tour. They walked and looked at many fun things. They saw big trees, pretty flowers, and small animals. They were very happy and excited to be on the tour together. As they walked, they found a big ice house. They wanted to go inside and see what was there. When they went in, they saw a big ice room. The room was so cold that they began to freeze. Their noses turned red, and their hands felt like ice. Then, a guilty-looking small dog came out from behind the ice. The dog had a magic stick. He said, "I made this ice house, but I didn't mean to make you freeze!" He waved his magic stick, and the ice house turned into a warm, cozy house. The friends were not cold anymore, and they all played with the dog. They had a great time and were happy that something unexpected happened on their tour.

Once upon a time, there was a little frog named Tim. Tim had big eyes that were always wet, because he lived in a pond. He liked to jump and play all day with his friends. One sunny day, Tim was playing with his friend, a yellow duck named Lucy. They were having so much fun splashing in the water. But then, a big cloud came and covered the sun. Tim and Lucy looked up and saw the cloud was very dark. Soon, rain started to fall. Tim and Lucy felt the rain on their wet skin. They decided to go home. As they swam back to their homes, Tim closed his eyes and smiled. He liked the rain on his face. When they reached their homes, they said goodbye, and Tim closed his eyes to sleep. He dreamed of more fun days with his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a huge tree. The tree had many leaves. One day, a man came and took some leaves. He made paper from the leaves. The paper was very pretty. A little girl found the paper. She liked it a lot. She knew the paper had value. She wanted to make something with it. So, she started to fold the paper. She made a big bird. The bird was very pretty too. One day, something unexpected happened. The big bird came to life! It could fly and talk. The little girl was very happy. She played with the bird every day. They became best friends. And they lived happily ever after.
