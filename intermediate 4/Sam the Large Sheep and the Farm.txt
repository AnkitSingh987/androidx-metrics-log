Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam the Large Sheep and the Farm

Once upon a time, there was a large sheep named Sam. Sam had lots of wool on his body. He lived on a farm with his friends. One day, a little girl named Lily came to the farm. She saw Sam and said, "Hello, big sheep! Can I have some of your wool?" Sam liked Lily and wanted to help her. So, Sam said, "Yes, you can have some of my wool." Lily took some wool from Sam and said, "Thank you, Sam!" Sam was happy to help Lily. He felt good when he gave his wool to her. From that day on, Sam and Lily became best friends, and Sam always gave his wool to Lily when she needed it.

One day, a bunny named Bob wanted to play. He saw a scooter near a tree. Bob wanted to ride the scooter. So, he hopped on it. He was very happy. Bob hopped on the scooter and rode it fast. He saw a dead bug on the ground. Bob stopped the scooter and looked at the bug. He felt sad for the bug. Bob hopped back on the scooter and rode it slow. He saw his bunny friends. They all hopped on scooters too. They rode together and had fun.

Once upon a time there was an ordinary ghost. The ghost was very sad because nobody ever gave him a big kiss. One day he thought he should find someone who could give him a kiss. So he floated around looking for someone special. He soon saw a little girl. She had pink cheeks and a lovely smile. The ghost smiled back at her and said “Do you want to give me a kiss?” The little girl was surprised to see a ghost. But she liked him and said “Yes, I'll give you a kiss!” The ghost was delighted and thanked her. He was so happy to get a big kiss from the little girl. They hugged each other and said goodbye. The little girl was happy that she had given the ghost a kiss. She knew that she had made him very happy. From then on, the ghost was no longer a sad and ordinary ghost.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside under the big blue sky. One day, Lily met a new friend named Tom. Tom was a mild boy who liked to play with Lily. One day, Lily and Tom were playing outside. Lily looked up at the sky and said, "Look, Tom! The sky is so big and blue!" Tom looked up and said, "Yes, Lily, it is very pretty!" They played all day long under the sky. They laughed and had so much fun. As the sun went down, Lily and Tom said goodbye. They knew they would play again under the big blue sky soon.

Once upon a time, there was a small valley. In the valley, many animals lived. They were all friends and played together every day. One day, a crazy bird came to the valley. The bird was loud and did not play nice with the other animals. A little bunny saw the crazy bird and did not like how it acted. The bunny wanted the valley to be happy again. So, the bunny went to talk to the bird. The bird did not listen to the bunny and kept being loud and mean. The little bunny was sad and began to weep. The other animals saw the bunny weep and came to help. They all talked to the crazy bird together. The bird saw how sad it made the bunny and the other animals. The bird said sorry and promised to be nice. The valley was happy again, and all the animals played together, even the crazy bird.

Once upon a time, there were two furry friends called Sam and Sophie. Sam and Sophie wanted to explore the world together, so one day they set off on their journey. Sam and Sophie came across a gentle cliff. They got closer and noticed there were lots of things to do at the cliff. Sophie said to Sam, "Let's use the cliff! We can slide down it and go exploring." Sam nodded happily and said, "Yeah, let's use it!" So, they used the cliff with great care. First they slid down it, then they ran around and explored. When they were done, they both agreed that the gentle cliff was lots of fun!
