Category: Intermediate
Title: The Barrel and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a big barrel. It was in a small town. A man wanted to order a new barrel. He needed it for his apples. The man went to the store. He said, "I want to order a big barrel, please." The store lady said, "Okay, it will come soon." The man was happy. But when the big barrel came, it was not empty. There were many toys inside! The man was surprised. He shared the toys with all the kids in the town. Everyone was happy and played together.

---

One day, a little whale named Wally was swimming in the big sea. He was a frightened whale because he was scared of the dark. Wally had a friend named Sally the fish. Sally was very good at counting. One day, Wally and Sally were playing a game. They had to count how many rocks they could find. Wally found four rocks, but Sally found ten rocks. Wally was sad because he could not count as well as Sally. Sally said, "Don't be sad, Wally. I can teach you how to count better." The next day, Wally and Sally saw a big, dark cave. Wally was scared, but Sally said, "Let's go in and count the rocks inside the cave." Wally was still scared, but he wanted to learn how to count better. So, they went inside the cave together. Inside the cave, it was very dark. Wally was frightened, but Sally helped him count the rocks. They counted one, two, three, and so on. Soon, Wally was not scared anymore because he was having fun counting with Sally. The moral of the story is that friends can help you face your fears and learn new things. And, with a little help, even a frightened whale can learn to count and be brave.

Once upon a time, there was a regular boy named Tim. Tim had a toy shield that he loved to play with every day. He would run around the house, pretending to be a brave knight. One day, while Tim was playing, he saw a big cat outside. The cat looked at him and nodded its head. Tim was very surprised because cats don't nod like people do. He thought the cat must be a magic cat. Tim took his shield and went outside to meet the magic cat. But when he got closer, he saw that the cat was just a regular cat with a leaf stuck on its head. The cat was trying to shake the leaf off, and that's why it looked like it was nodding. Tim laughed and helped the cat get the leaf off. And from that day, Tim and the cat became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a brave dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to joke with his friends. One day, Buddy saw his friend, Kitty the cat, sitting by a tree. Buddy said, "Kitty, why are you sad? Let me tell you a joke to make you laugh." Kitty looked at Buddy and said, "Okay, tell me a joke." Buddy told a funny joke about meat, and Kitty laughed so hard. Later, Buddy and Kitty found a big piece of meat. They shared it and had a great time. They were happy to be brave and silly friends.

One day, a little pink bunny went for a walk in the big green forest. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. The pink bunny was very happy. The pink bunny wanted to find some yummy food to eat. So, she started to search for food. She looked under the big rocks and inside the small bushes. She found some tasty berries and ate them. As the pink bunny was eating the berries, she heard a loud noise. The noise made her tremble with fear. She hid behind a big tree and saw a big, friendly bear. The bear said, "Don't be scared, little bunny. I just want to be friends." The pink bunny smiled and they played together in the forest.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to reach for things. He reached for toys, food, and even his cat. Tim had a style when he reached. He would jump, stretch, and grab. One day, Tim saw a hard ball on a high shelf. He wanted to play with it. He tried to reach it, but it was too high. Tim thought and thought. He had an idea. He would use a chair to stand on. Tim stood on the chair and reached for the hard ball. But the chair was not strong. It fell and Tim fell too. Tim was sad and hurt. The hard ball stayed on the shelf. The end.
