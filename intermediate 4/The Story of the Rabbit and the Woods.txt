Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Rabbit and the Woods

One day, a little soldier was walking in the woods. He was frightened because he heard a loud noise. He did not know what it was. He thought it might be a big animal that would hurt him. He saw a small rabbit and asked, "Did you make the loud noise? Did I disturb you?" The rabbit said, "No, it was not me. I am too small to make such a loud noise." As they talked, a big wind came and blew away the trees. The soldier and the rabbit were very surprised. The loud noise was just the wind, and it did not want to hurt them. They laughed and played together, happy that they were not in danger.

One day, a little seal was very nervous. It was time for the big show. The seal wanted to clap its fins and make everyone happy. But the seal was scared. What if no one liked the show? The seal went to the stage. It started to clap its fins. The people watched and waited. Then, something unexpected happened. The seal clapped so hard that it jumped very high into the air! Everyone was surprised. When the seal came back down, it landed on a big ball. It rolled on the ball and clapped its fins again. The people laughed and clapped too. The seal was not nervous anymore. It was happy because it made everyone smile.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside with his dog, Spot. They loved to run and jump in the sun. One day, Tim found a bulb in the ground. He showed it to Spot. Spot sniffed it and made a disgusting face. Tim laughed and decided to follow Spot to see where they could plant the bulb. They found a nice spot under a big tree. Tim dug a hole and put the bulb in it. They covered it with dirt and watered it. Tim and Spot were very happy. They couldn't wait to see what would grow from the bulb.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to eat delicious cookies. One day, she wanted to make cookies all by herself. She asked her mom for help. Her mom said yes and they started making cookies together. Lily and her mom worked very hard. They mixed and rolled the dough for many minutes. After a while, they had made lots of cookies. Lily was very happy. She knew she could succeed with her mom's help. When the cookies were done, Lily and her mom took them out of the oven. They smelled so good! Lily and her mom shared the cookies with their friends. Everyone said the cookies were the most delicious they had ever tasted. Lily was proud that she succeeded in making cookies with her mom.

Lila and Ben are friends. They like to play together. Sometimes they play with toys, sometimes they play with books, sometimes they play with music. One day, they find a big box of music things. There are drums, guitars, flutes, and more. Lila and Ben are happy. They want to try them all. Lila picks up a flute. She blows into it. It makes a high sound. Ben picks up a guitar. He strums it. It makes a low sound. They like the sounds. They play together. But soon, they want to change. Lila wants to play the guitar. Ben wants to play the flute. They ask each other. "Can I have the guitar, please?" Lila says. "Can I have the flute, please?" Ben says. They are flexible. They share. They swap the music things. They say thank you. They play again. They make new sounds. They have fun. They are good friends. They like music.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. Tom liked to sleep all day and never wanted to play. One day, Tom's friend, a little mouse named Jerry, came to visit. "Tom, let's go for a walk!" said Jerry. Tom looked at Jerry and yawned. "I'm too lazy to walk," he said. Jerry had an idea. He found a pedal car and said, "Let's use this! You can sit and I will pedal." Tom liked the idea, so they got into the pedal car. Jerry pedaled fast and Tom enjoyed the ride. They went up and down the hills, and all around the town. Tom started to feel happy and not so lazy anymore. At the end of the day, Tom thanked Jerry for the fun ride. "I had a great time," said Tom. "Maybe I should not be so lazy all the time." From that day on, Tom and Jerry went for walks and played together every day. And Tom was not a lazy cat anymore.
