Category: Intermediate
Title: Spike the Hedgehog and the Ball

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little hedgehog named Spike. Spike was very adorable. He had many friends, but sometimes they would disagree on things. One day, Spike and his friends were playing near a big tree. They found a shiny ball. They all wanted to play with it, but they could not agree on how to play. Some wanted to roll it, some wanted to throw it, and some wanted to hide it. They started to disagree. Suddenly, the shiny ball started to move on its own! Spike and his friends were surprised. They stopped disagreeing and watched the ball. The ball opened up, and out came a friendly fairy! The fairy said, "Thank you for finding my home. I was lost!" Spike and his friends were happy to help. They learned that it is okay to disagree sometimes, but it is important to work together. From that day on, they all played happily together in the big forest.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue had a twin sister named Lucy. They liked to play together. One day, they found a fancy box. The box was big and pretty. Sue and Lucy wanted to see what was inside. Sue and Lucy tried to open the box. They tried to add more power to open it. But the box would not open. They were sad. They wanted to see what was inside the box. Sue had an idea. She asked her twin sister to help her. Together, Sue and Lucy added more power. They pushed and pushed. The fancy box opened. They looked inside. They saw another twin! It was a toy twin. The toy twin looked like Sue and Lucy. They were so happy. They played with the toy twin all day. The fancy box was a big surprise.

One day, a boy named Tim went to a big church with his mom. It was a hot day, so they wore hats to stay cool. They walked into the church and saw many people. Tim saw a girl named Sue. She had a toy in her hand. "Hi, Sue!" Tim said. "What do you have?" Sue said, "Hi, Tim! I have a toy car. It is fun to play with." Just then, Sue's hand got tired, and she dropped her toy car. It made a loud noise. Tim and Sue looked at each other and laughed. They picked up the toy car and played with it together. They had a great time at the big church on that hot day.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Bobo. He loved to play with his friends in the big green field. One sunny day, Bobo and his friends were playing a game. They had to find the number zero. They looked everywhere, but they could not find it. They were very sad. Bobo's mom saw them and said, "Don't be sad, little ones. I will help you find the number zero. But you must hurry, because it will be dark soon." So Bobo and his friends hurried to look for the number zero with Bobo's mom. They looked under rocks, behind trees, and even in the pond. Finally, they found the number zero hidden in the tall grass. Bobo and his friends were so happy! They thanked Bobo's mom and played with the number zero until the sun went down. The moral of the story is that when you are sad or need help, don't be afraid to ask someone you love. They will always be there to help you.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat named Tom. Tom liked to play with a red ball. He would get the ball and run all around the yard. Tom was very happy. One day, Tom met an old dog named Max. Max said, "Hi Tom, do you want to play with me? It's easy!" Tom was not sure, but he said, "Okay, let's play!" They started to play, but suddenly, the red ball changed into a big cake! Tom and Max were very surprised. They looked at each other and laughed. Then, they ate the cake together and became good friends.
