Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Cat and the Ball

One day, a cat with soft skin was walking in the woods. The cat saw a big, red ball. The cat wanted to play with the ball, but the ball was not his. The cat felt guilty but still played with the ball. The cat said to the ball, "You are fun to play with, but I am guilty because you are not mine." The ball did not say anything back. The cat played with the ball all day long. At the end of the day, the cat was very tired. He wanted to go home, but he could not find the way. The cat was lost and scared. He cried for help, but no one came. The cat was all alone in the woods, and he never found his way home.

Lola was a brave girl. She was only three years old but she liked to take on dares. One day her mom showed her a picture of a tutoring school. It was painted blue and it looked very big. "If you dare go inside, I will give you a special treat," her mother said. Lola was excited. She thought about it for a moment and then she said, "I dare!" They walked up to the blue tutoring school. Lola was a bit scared but she opened the door and walked inside. As soon as they did, they heard the sound of kids laughing and playing. Lola smiled and relaxed. Her mother said, "See, it's not so scary after all. Now we can go have that special treat!" Lola was so happy. She had taken on the dare and conquered her fears. She had defeated the blue tutoring school!

Once upon a time, there was a little seed. The seed wanted to grow big and strong. So, it asked its friend, the sun, for help. The sun said, "You need a bucket with dirt and water to grow." The seed found a bucket and asked the rain to fill it with water. The rain said, "I will help you, but you must be brave." The seed was brave and jumped into the bucket. The rain filled the bucket with water, and the seed got wet. Every day, the sun shined on the seed, and the rain gave it water. The seed started to grow. It grew up and up until it was a big, strong tree. The tree was happy and thanked the sun and the rain for helping it grow. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked going to the restaurant with his dad. They would eat yummy food and have fun. Tim was very happy when they went to the restaurant. One day, while they were eating, a rough dog came into the restaurant. The dog was big and had rough fur. Everyone was scared. Tim's dad told him to mind the dog and not to touch him. But Tim was not scared. He knew the dog just wanted to play. Suddenly, the rough dog started to dance! It was a very unexpected thing. Everyone in the restaurant laughed and clapped. The dog was not scary at all. He was just a dancing dog who wanted to have fun. Tim and his dad smiled and enjoyed the show.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park. She saw a pretty red flower called a poppy. Lucy felt anxious because she wanted to include the poppy in her flower collection at home. She thought, "I will take this poppy home with me." Lucy asked her friend, Tom, "Can I take this poppy home?" Tom said, "No, Lucy. It is not good to take things that are not yours." But Lucy did not listen to Tom. She took the poppy home anyway. At home, Lucy put the poppy in a vase. But soon, the poppy started to look very sad. It was not happy being away from its friends in the park. Lucy felt bad for taking the poppy. In the end, the poppy wilted and died. Lucy learned that it is not good to take things that are not yours.

Once upon a time, there was a big, wide house. It had a red roof. In the house, there was a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot. They liked to play together all day. Tim would throw a ball, and Spot would run to get it. They were very happy. One day, there was a loud noise. Tim and Spot heard it. "What was that?" Tim asked. It was a strike! A tree had hit the roof! Tim and Spot were scared. They did not know what to do. They hid under the bed together. Then, something unexpected happened. Tim and Spot saw a little bird come through the hole in the roof. The bird was lost and scared too. Tim and Spot became friends with the bird. They were not scared anymore. They all played together, and they were happy. The end.
