Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Door

One day, a big dog named Max saw a suit. The suit was red and blue. Max was glad. He wanted to wear the suit and look nice. Max put on the suit and went for a walk. As Max walked, he saw a door. Max wanted to enter the door. He went in and saw many animals wearing suits too. Max was very happy to see them all looking nice. But then, something unexpected happened. All the animals took off their suits. They were not animals, but kids dressed as animals! Max was surprised, but he was still glad. Now, Max played with the kids and they all had fun together.

One day, a quiet cat and a loud dog had a race. The cat said, "I will win because I am fast." The dog said, "No, I will win because I am strong." They ran and ran. The cat was fast, but the dog was strong. The cat was ahead, but the dog was close behind. Their friends, a bird and a frog, watched with big eyes. At the end, the cat and the dog crossed the finish line at the same time. They both won! The bird and the frog clapped their hands. The cat and the dog were happy and hugged each other. The quiet cat and the loud dog were now best friends.

One day, a little girl was walking around her garden. Suddenly, the sky turned dark and the wind started to blow. Suddenly, there was a loud noise and a fire came! It was a weird fire, with colours that the girl had never seen before. She ran inside to get her mom. "Mom, come see!" the little girl said. Mom ran outside to see what was happening. When she saw the fire she said, "That's weird. We should call the fire department to help put it out." The little girl and her mom waited for the fire department to come. When they arrived, the firefighters used their hoses and soon the weird fire disappeared. The little girl was glad the fire was gone. She and her mom hugged and thanked the firefighters for their help. The little girl learned that day that we must always be careful with fire.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a wise old owl. The owl lived in a big tree. The owl was very kind and helped the village animals when they had a mystery. One day, a little mouse came to the wise owl. The mouse said, "Owl, I have a mystery. I lost my toy. Can you help me find it?" The wise owl said, "Yes, little mouse, I will help you." The wise owl and the little mouse looked everywhere. They poked under rocks, and they poked in holes. At last, they found the toy under a big leaf. The little mouse was so happy. The mouse said, "Thank you, wise owl, for helping me." The wise owl said, "You're welcome, little mouse. Remember, it's always good to help your friends when they need it." The little mouse learned that helping others is important, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to draw with her powerful crayons. One day, she drew a beautiful picture of a big, happy sun. Mia wanted to share her picture with her best friend, Max. So, she decided to mail it to him. She put the picture in an envelope and walked to the mailbox with her mom. Mia said, "Mom, can you help me mail my picture to Max?" Her mom smiled and said, "Of course, Mia!" They put the envelope in the mailbox and raised the red flag. A few days later, Max got the picture in the mail. He was so happy to see Mia's beautiful sun. He called Mia and said, "Thank you for the pretty picture, Mia! I love it!" Mia felt happy that she could share her powerful crayon drawing with her friend.

Once upon a time, in a lively forest, there lived a little bird named Tim. Tim had many friends in the forest. One day, Tim met a big bear. The bear was sad. Tim asked the bear, "Why are you sad?" The bear said, "I lost my way home." Tim wanted to help the bear. He used his brain to think. Tim asked his friends, the ants, to help find the bear's home. The ants were very smart. The ants found the bear's home. Tim and the bear were very happy. The bear said, "Thank you, Tim!" Tim and the bear became good friends. And they all lived happily in the lively forest.
