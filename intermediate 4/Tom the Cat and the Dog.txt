Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Cat and the Dog

One day, a cat named Tom found a big newspaper. He looked at it and let out a deep sigh. He did not know what the newspaper said. Tom wanted to learn what the words meant. Tom went to his friend, a dog named Max. Max knew how to read the newspaper. Tom asked Max to help him learn. Max was happy to help his friend. They sat down and Max started to teach Tom. After a while, Tom began to understand the words. He was so happy that he could read the newspaper. Tom thanked Max for helping him. They both smiled and read the newspaper together.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Lily and her adorable cat, Tippy. They loved to play together all day. One sunny day, Lily's mom came home with a big box. Lily and Tippy were very excited to see what was inside. "Can we unpack the box, Mom?" Lily asked. Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, let's unpack it together!" Lily and Tippy helped her mom open the box. Inside, they found many colorful toys and books. Lily and Tippy were very happy to see all the new things to play with. Lily picked up a toy car and said, "Look, Tippy! This car is so much fun!" Tippy looked at the car and batted it with her paw, making it roll across the floor. They played with the car and the other toys for hours, laughing and having a great time. At the end of the day, Lily and Tippy were tired but very happy with their new toys.

Once upon a time, there was a creative little bunny named Bob. Bob loved to jump all day long. One day, Bob found a pedal near his home. He didn't know what it was, but he thought it looked fun. Bob went to his friend, the wise owl named Ollie. "Ollie, what is this?" asked Bob. Ollie looked at the pedal and said, "Oh, that is a pedal. It can make things go fast!" Bob was excited and wanted to try it. He put the pedal on the ground and jumped on it. Suddenly, the pedal started to move! Bob was going so fast, he couldn't believe it. He zoomed past all his friends in the forest. As Bob was having fun, he didn't see a big rock in his way. He hit the rock and went flying through the air. But instead of falling, Bob started to fly! He had become a flying bunny! All his friends cheered for him, and from that day on, Bob was the most creative flying bunny in the whole forest.

Once upon a time, there was a strong monkey named Max. Max lived in a big tree with many green leaves. He loved to play with his friends in the tree. One day, Max saw a palm tree with big yellow fruits. He wanted to share the fruits with his friends. Max jumped and climbed to the top of the palm tree. He saw the fruits and got very happy. Max used his strong arms to shake the palm tree. The yellow fruits fell down to the ground. Max and his friends ate the fruits and they all smiled. They were very happy because Max was strong and kind. They played in the tree until the sun went down.

One day, a big, hairy dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a cable on the ground. Max wanted to play with the cable, so he picked it up with his mouth. Max walked with the cable in his mouth. He did not see a man coming. The man was sad because he needed the cable to pay for his food. Max saw the man and knew he had to help. Max gave the cable to the man. The man was happy and gave Max some food too. They both ate and became friends. The man did not need the cable to pay after all. They found a new way to pay and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Fluffy. Fluffy had very long hair. She loved to sleep all day and play all night. One day, Fluffy's friend, a little bird named Chirpy, came to visit. Chirpy said, "Fluffy, remember to clean your hair today!" Fluffy just yawned and said, "Okay, Chirpy, I will do it later." But Fluffy did not clean her hair. Instead, she went to play with her toy mouse. As she played, her hair got messier and messier. Chirpy saw this and said, "Fluffy, your hair is very messy now. Please clean it!" Fluffy just yawned again and said, "I am too lazy, Chirpy. I will do it later." The next day, Fluffy finally remembered to clean her hair. She felt much better and thanked Chirpy for reminding her. From that day on, Fluffy promised to always remember to clean her hair, even if she was feeling lazy.
