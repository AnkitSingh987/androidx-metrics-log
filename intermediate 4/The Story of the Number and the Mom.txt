Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Number and the Mom

Once there was a little girl who was very honest. She loved wearing her prettiest dress every day. One day, her mom gave her a number to remember. She was very excited and kept repeating the number over and over. `Mommy, what does this number mean?` She asked. `It is important for you to remember it.` her mom said. She was happy and went to play, always counting the number. One day, she decided to go out of the house with her dress on. She saw a group of boys and ran towards them. `What is this number?` She asked one of them. The boys looked at each other and laughed. One of they said, `It's nothing.` The little girl felt sad because she was not being honest. She quickly ran home and never looked back. Unfortunately, she had forgotten the number her mom had given her and never found out what it meant.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the park with her mom. At the park, Amy saw a cherry tree. The cherries were red and yummy. Amy wanted to eat some cherries, so she asked her mom, "Can I have some cherries, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, but only take a few." Amy saw a boy named Ben near the cherry tree. Ben was rude. He took many cherries and threw them at the birds. The birds were sad. Amy did not like this. She told Ben, "You should not throw cherries at the birds. It is not nice." Ben felt bad. He said, "I am sorry, Amy. I will not do it again." Amy and Ben became friends. They clapped their hands and played together at the park. The birds were happy too. The moral of the story is to be kind to others and share.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to look at the stars in the sky. Every night, he would look out his window and see the distant stars. One night, Tim saw a very big star. It was the biggest star he had ever seen. Tim said to his mom, "I want to complete a picture of the big star." His mom gave him paper and colors. Tim drew a big circle and lots of little circles around it. He was very happy with his picture. One day, while Tim was playing outside, he saw the big star in the sky. It started to get bigger and bigger. Tim was scared. He ran to his mom and said, "The big star is coming!" His mom looked out the window and saw the big star too. They hid under the table, but the big star kept getting closer. The big star came to their house and broke it. Tim and his mom were very sad. They lost their home because of the big star. They had to find a new place to live. Tim learned that sometimes, the things we love can surprise us and make us sad.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived many animals. They were all friends and played together every day. One day, a little bird was flying high in the sky when it fell down and hurt its wing. All the animals saw this and were very sad. A kind and patient bear saw the little bird and wanted to help. The bear knew how to fix things, so he went to the little bird and said, "Don't worry, I will fix your wing." The little bird was happy to hear this and let the bear help. The bear carefully fixed the little bird's wing. It took some time, but the bear was patient and gentle. Soon, the little bird's wing was all better. The little bird thanked the bear and flew up into the sky again. All the animals in the forest were happy to see their friend flying again, and they continued to play together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red ball. The ball had a loop on it. The loop was blue. The ball liked to keep going and going. It rolled all day and all night. One day, the ball met a spicy pepper. The pepper was green and small. The ball and pepper became friends. They played together all the time. They liked to keep playing. One day, the ball and pepper found a hill. They had an idea. The ball rolled down the hill fast. It went through the loop. The pepper jumped on the ball and went through the loop too. They both laughed and were happy. They kept playing all day long.
