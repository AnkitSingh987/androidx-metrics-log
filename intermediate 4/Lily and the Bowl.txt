Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Bowl

Lily liked to bake. She baked cakes and pies and cookies with her mom. She liked to mix and stir and pour and taste. She liked to make things sweet and yummy. One day, Lily went to visit her dad at his work. He worked in a lab. He did not bake. He did experiments with tubes and beakers and machines. He liked to make things fizz and pop and change color. He liked to learn new things. Lily wanted to help her dad in the lab. She saw a big bowl with some white powder in it. She thought it was flour. She saw a bottle with some red liquid in it. She thought it was juice. She poured some juice into the bowl and stirred it with a spoon. She thought she was making a cake. But she was not making a cake. She was making a mess. The powder and the juice reacted and made a big foam that spilled over the bowl and onto the table and the floor. It made a loud noise and a bad smell. Lily was scared and surprised. Her dad was angry and troubled. He had to clean up the mess and fix his experiment. Lily said she was sorry. She did not know it was not flour and juice. She just wanted to bake with her dad. Her dad said he was sorry too. He did not mean to yell at her. He just wanted to do his work. He hugged her and said he loved her. He said he would show her some fun experiments that were safe and easy. He said they could bake a real cake later at home. Lily smiled and said she liked that. She liked to bake and she liked to learn. She liked her dad and his lab.

Once upon a time, there was a family who lived in a big, old, ancient house. In this family, there was a mom, a dad, and a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy car and follow his cat, Whiskers, around the house. One day, Tim saw Whiskers go through a tiny door he had never seen before. Tim wanted to follow Whiskers, so he opened the door and found a secret room! Inside the room, there was a big, shiny box. Tim was very excited and called his mom and dad. "Mom, Dad, come see!" Tim said. His mom and dad came running and saw the secret room and the shiny box. They all opened the box together and found a treasure map! The family decided to follow the map to see where it would take them. As they followed the map, they found a beautiful garden with lots of pretty flowers and a big tree. The family was very surprised and happy to find this special place. They decided to have a fun picnic under the big tree and play games all day. From that day on, the family would visit the secret garden every week. They would follow the treasure map and have lots of fun together. And it was all thanks to Tim and Whiskers, who found the secret room in their ancient house.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a bike ride. He liked to ride his bike with thick wheels. Tim loved to pedal fast and feel the wind in his hair. He saw his friend, Sue, and they started to talk. While they were talking, a big dog came up to them. The dog was very friendly and wagged its tail. It started to follow Tim and Sue as they rode their bikes. They thought the dog was lost and wanted to help it find its home. They pedaled and pedaled until they found a house with a big yard. The dog ran into the yard and started to bark. Tim and Sue saw a man come out of the house. They thought the man would be happy to see the dog, but he looked surprised. The man told them that the dog was not his, but he would take care of it until they found its real home. Tim and Sue were happy that the dog had a safe place to stay, and they made a new friend too.
