Category: Intermediate
Title: Ronny and the Mom

One night, Ronny and his mom were sitting on the couch. Ronny was feeling very guilty. His mom saw the sadness in her son's eyes. "Ronny, why don't you invite your friends over for dinner?" Mom asked. Ronny smiled and said "Mom, that would be great!" So Ronny invited his friends over for dinner on the night. All of his friends were delighted to come over. They had lots of fun playing together and eating dinner. At the end of the night, Ronny's mom said "I'm so happy you invited your friends over. You were looking so guilty earlier, it was nice to see you smiling!" Ronny gave his mom a big hug and smiled.

One day, a cat and a dog were walking down a path. They saw a row of trees with big, red apples. The dog said, "Look! Yummy apples! Let's get some." The cat was glad and agreed. The dog tried to jump and grab an apple, but he was not tall enough. Then, the cat climbed the tree and pushed an apple. The apple fell to the ground, and the dog picked it up. The cat said, "Here you go, friend!" The dog was happy and wagged his tail. They both sat under the tree and ate the apples together. They were very glad to share the yummy apples. After eating, they played and ran around the trees. They had a fun day and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big red bus. The bus liked to go fast on the road. One day, the bus wanted to borrow a toy from a little boy. The little boy said, "Okay, you can borrow my toy, but you must be careful." The bus took the toy and went on the road. But the bus found it difficult to hold the toy and drive at the same time. The bus went too fast and did not look where it was going. The bus hit a big rock and the toy flew out of the bus's hand. The toy broke into many pieces. The bus felt very sad because it broke the little boy's toy. The little boy was sad too, and the bus knew it could not fix the toy. The bus learned that it should be more careful when borrowing things from others.

One day, a big boy named Tim went to play near the river. He saw an enormous fish in the water. Tim thought the fish would be fun to catch, so he ran home to get his spear. When he came back, he saw the fish was still there. He threw the spear into the water, but the fish was too fast. Tim got wet and started to soak. "Oh no!" he said, "I am all wet now!" Just then, another boy named Sam came by. He saw Tim and the enormous fish. "I can help you catch the fish," Sam said. They tried again together, but the fish jumped out of the water and turned into a bird. The bird flew away, and the boys were very surprised. They laughed and played together, even though they did not catch the fish-bird.

Once upon a time, there lived a little boy named Sam. He was three years old and very adventurous. One day, Sam heard a noise in the dark night. He wanted to listen closely to try and figure out what it was. He heard a cheerful voice calling out his name! Sam tiptoed outside and saw an old friend named Jill. "Hi Sam! Long time no see!", Jill said. Sam was so happy to see her. He jumped and said, "Hi Jill! What are you doing here?" Jill smiled and said, "I've come to play! Let's have some fun!" Sam and Jill ran around in the dark and laughed and giggled until they were both tired. They said goodbye and Sam watched Jill walk away until she disappeared into the night. Sam went back inside and fell asleep with a cheerful smile on his face. He felt content knowing that his old friend was still looking out for him.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear. He was happy and calm. He liked to feel different textures with his big paws. One day he was walking in the forest when he found a strange thing. It felt furry, like a paw but much smaller. He pinched it gently with two of his fingers and realised it was a little mouse. The bear was very gentle and he smiled as the little mouse scampered away. He was happy to have made the mouse feel calm. The bear continued to explore the forest and feel all the different textures he found. He felt rough tree trunks, soft wings of birds and the fur of rabbits. At the end of the day, the bear was feeling content. He had enjoyed pinching the little mouse and feeling the textures in the forest. He smiled and hugged himself before wandering back home.
