Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was only three, but she was very brave. One night, Lucy was walking in the forest. Suddenly, she heard something. She looked up in the sky, and saw a big, white owl. The owl was calling out "who-who-who". Lucy was so scared, but she was also brave. She slowly walked closer to the owl and said "hi owl, what are you doing?". The owl said "I am looking for something to eat. I am very hungry!". Suddenly, Lucy saw a big, hard nut. She picked up the nut and said "here owl, why don't you have this?". The owl was so happy! He grabbed the nut with his beak and flew away. Lucy was proud that she had helped the owl. She went home with a big smile on her face.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty butterfly. The butterfly loved to fly in the sun. One day, the butterfly saw a boy. The boy was digging a hole. He wanted to bury something. The butterfly was curious and wanted to see. The butterfly flew close to the boy. The boy was persistent. He dug and dug the hole. The butterfly thought the boy would bury a toy or a treasure. But then, something unexpected happened. The boy took out a small plant and put it in the hole. The butterfly was surprised. It was not a toy or a treasure. The boy was burying a plant to help it grow. The butterfly was happy. It flew around the boy and the new plant. The boy smiled and knew that the butterfly liked what he did. They both became friends and helped the plant grow big and strong.

Lily and Max were best friends. They liked to play in the park every day. Lily had a dog named Spot. Spot was very friendly and liked to run and jump. Max had a cat named Fluffy. Fluffy was very shy and liked to hide and nap. One day, Lily and Max went to the park with Spot and Fluffy. Lily put a leash on Spot, so he would not run away. Max put Fluffy in a basket, so he would not get scared. They walked to the swings and took turns to swing high and low. They had a lot of fun. "Look at me, Lily! I can swing higher than you!" Max said. "No, you can't, Max! I can swing higher than you!" Lily said. They laughed and swung faster and faster. They did not see a big hole in the ground. Spot saw the hole and barked. He wanted to warn them. But Lily and Max did not hear him. They were too busy swinging. Suddenly, Lily's swing broke. She fell off and landed in the hole. She hurt her knee and cried. Max stopped swinging and ran to help her. He saw Spot pulling on the leash. He understood that Spot was trying to help. He thanked Spot and gave him a hug. "Are you okay, Lily?" Max asked. "It hurts, Max. I want to go home." Lily said. "I'm sorry, Lily. It was my fault. I should have listened to Spot. He is a good dog." Max said. "It's okay, Max. You are a good friend. You came to help me. Spot is a good dog, too. He saved me." Lily said. They got out of the hole and walked back to the basket. Fluffy was still sleeping. He did not know what happened. They picked up Fluffy and Spot and went home. They learned a lesson. They learned to pay attention to their surroundings and to their pets. They were lucky that nothing worse happened. They were lucky to have each other and their pets. They hugged and said sorry and thank you. They were still best friends.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to play in the park. She saw a big, impressive tree and started to climb it. When she got to the top, she looked around and saw a big, green monster. The monster saw Lily and started to walk towards her. Lily was scared and didn't know what to do. But then, something unexpected happened. The monster started to dance and sing a funny song. Lily started to laugh. The monster was not scary at all. He was a friendly monster who just wanted to play. Lily and the monster became good friends and played in the park all day. They had so much fun together, and Lily learned that not all monsters are scary.
