Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bear and the Rainbow

Once there was a little bear who wanted to have a party. He asked all his friends to come, but they all said they were too busy. Standing there alone, the little bear had an unusual idea. He went up to the clouds and asked them, “Do you want to come to my party?” The clouds were so excited! They said, “Yes, we would love to come!” The clouds brought a colorful rainbow and a happy sun for the party. The little bear and clouds laughed, sang and talked the whole day. Everyone had so much fun at the party! At the end the little bear said, “I learned something today. Even if it’s unusual, when you ask people for help, they might surprise you in a very good way!”

One day, a boy named Tim found an envelope on the floor. He picked it up and saw that it had a pretty stamp on it. Tim wanted to know what was inside the envelope, but he knew he should not open it. Tim went to his friend, Sara, who lived next door. Sara was deaf, but they could still talk using their hands. Tim showed Sara the envelope and asked, "Who does this belong to?" Sara looked at the envelope and pointed to the house across the street. Tim walked to the house and knocked on the door. A kind lady opened the door and smiled. Tim showed her the envelope and said, "I found this. Is it yours?" The lady nodded and thanked Tim for bringing it to her. Tim felt happy that he could help the lady. He went back to his house and told his mom about the envelope. His mom was proud of him for being a good helper. From that day on, Tim and Sara became even better friends, always ready to help others.

Once upon a time, there was a big, heavy plant. It lived in a small garden with many friends. The plant was very happy, but it needed help to grow. One day, a kind girl came to the garden. She saw the heavy plant and knew it needed water. So, she got a big cup and filled it with water. The girl gave the water to the plant. The water helped supply the plant with what it needed to grow. As the days passed, the girl and the heavy plant became good friends. The girl would visit the plant every day and supply it with water and love. The plant grew big and strong, and the garden was full of happiness.

Once upon a time, a sad little girl named Lily went for a walk. She had no friends to play with. She saw a big puddle on the ground. Lily loved puddles, so she jumped in it. As she jumped, something unexpected happened. A small frog came out of the puddle. The frog could talk! He said, "Hello, I am Fred the Frog. I own this puddle. Do you want to be friends?" Lily was very happy and said, "Yes, I want to be friends!" Lily and Fred played in the puddle all day. But then, the plot twist came. Fred was not just a frog, but a prince! A witch had turned him into a frog. When Lily became his friend, the spell was broken. Fred turned back into a prince. Lily and Prince Fred were best friends forever.

Anna and Ben were twins who liked to play with their toys. They had many toys, but their favorite was a big ball that looked like the world. The ball had many colors and shapes, and they liked to point at them and say their names. One day, they were playing with the ball in the living room, when their mom called them for lunch. "Anna, Ben, come and eat, please!" she said. "We have soup and bread and cheese." Anna and Ben did not want to stop playing. They were having fun with the ball. They were impatient. They did not listen to their mom. They kept rolling the ball and laughing. Their mom called them again. "Anna, Ben, come and eat, now!" she said. "The soup is getting cold and you need to eat to grow big and strong." Anna and Ben still did not want to stop playing. They were very impatient. They did not listen to their mom. They kept bouncing the ball and shouting. Their mom came to the living room. She was not happy. She saw the ball on the floor and picked it up. "Anna, Ben, you are being naughty!" she said. "You did not listen to me when I asked you to come and eat. Now you have to wait until after lunch to play with the ball again." Anna and Ben were sad. They liked the ball. They did not want to lose it. They looked at their mom and said, "Sorry, mom. We love the ball. Can we have it back, please?" Their mom looked at them and said, "You can have it back, but only if you promise to listen to me next time I request you to do something. Do you promise?" Anna and Ben nodded. They said, "Yes, mom. We promise. We will listen to you." Their mom smiled. She said, "Good. Now come and eat your lunch. Then you can play with the ball again. But remember, the ball is not the only thing in the world. There are many other things to learn and enjoy." Anna and Ben hugged their mom. They said, "Thank you, mom. We love you. And we love the world." They followed their mom to the kitchen and ate their lunch. Then they played with the ball again, but this time they listened to their mom when she requested them to do something. They were happy.
