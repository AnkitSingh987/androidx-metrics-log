Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Garden and the Tree

One sunny day, a little girl was playing in the garden. She was wearing a pink dress and smiling happily. Suddenly, she saw a sandwich under a tree. The sandwich was pink! She quickly ran over and picked it up. She said excitedly, "Oh, look! A pink sandwich!". She sat down on the grass, and ate her sandwich. When she had finished, she said contentedly, "Yum! That was so good!". She then went off to find something else to do. Later, the little girl went to the park with her parents. She spotted a swing with a pink seat. She jumped over and said, "Mommy, can I sit in this pink seat?". Her mom said, "Of course, sweetheart!". So the little girl sat down in the pink seat, and smiled a big smile. She said, "This seat is perfect!". The little girl had a lovely day, thanks to her pink sandwich and pink seat.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a messy circle on the floor. It was made of many colors. Mia wanted to fix the messy circle, so she went to find her friend, Tom. Tom came to help Mia. They tried to fix the messy circle by using a big brush. They painted the circle with blue and red. But the circle was still messy. Mia and Tom thought hard. Then, they had an idea! They used a big cloth to clean the messy circle. They worked together and soon the circle was clean and pretty. Mia and Tom smiled and were happy that they fixed the messy circle.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tom. Tom wanted to be famous. He liked to polish things to make them shiny. One day, Tom found a big, old lamp. He thought that if he could polish it and make it shiny, maybe he could be famous. Tom started to polish the lamp. He rubbed it and rubbed it until it was very shiny. Suddenly, a big genie appeared. The genie said, "Thank you for making my lamp shiny. I will give you one wish. What do you want?" Tom thought for a moment and then said, "I want to be famous!" The genie nodded and with a big poof, Tom became famous. Everyone knew Tom and his shiny lamp. Tom was very happy. From that day on, Tom and the genie were best friends. They went on many fun adventures together. And every time Tom found something that needed polishing, he would make it shiny and remember how he became famous.

Once upon a time, there was a small poppy named Poppy. Poppy lived in a big garden with her friends. She was very happy, but she had a problem. Poppy was so small that no one could see her. One day, a big wind came and started to pass through the garden. All the flowers were scared. They held on tight to the ground so they would not fly away. Poppy was scared too, but she wanted to help her friends. Poppy thought of a plan. She asked the wind to help her. The wind listened and started to pass gently. Poppy became a small kite, flying high in the sky. All her friends could see her now. They were happy and not scared anymore. Poppy was small, but she was very brave. And from that day on, everyone in the garden knew and loved Poppy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big bell. The bell would ring every day in March. One day, a filthy dog named Spot came to the town. Spot did not like the bell sound. He wanted to stop the bell from ringing. Spot went to his friend, a little girl named Sue. "Sue, help me stop the bell from ringing," said Spot. Sue did not want to help at first, but Spot was her friend. So, she said, "Okay, let's try." They climbed up the tower where the bell was. Sue and Spot tried to stop the bell from ringing, but they could not. They made a big mess, and the bell rang louder than ever. The town people were not happy. In the end, Spot and Sue were in trouble. The town people were mad at them for making the bell ring so loud. The moral of the story is that sometimes, it is better to let things be and not try to change them.
