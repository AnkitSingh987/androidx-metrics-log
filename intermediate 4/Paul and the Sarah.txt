Category: Intermediate
Title: Paul and the Sarah

Once upon a time, there were two friends named Paul and Sarah. They were very friendly, and they always wanted to have fun together. One day, Paul said to Sarah, "Let's write something on the wall!" Sarah thought it sounded like a lot of fun and she said, "Yes! Let's do it!" So Paul got a big piece of paper and some markers and asked Sarah, "What should we write?" Sarah thought for a minute, then said, "Let's write 'Friends forever!' That way we can remember how friendly we are!" Paul smiled and said, "That's a great idea! Let's do it!" So they both wrote "Friends forever!" on the wall. When they were done, they both smiled. Then Paul said, "That was fun! Let's do it again sometime!" Sarah nodded and said, "Yes! That was really fun!" And that's how Paul and Sarah had fun writing together.

Once upon a time there was a girl who wanted to meet her friend. Her name was Emily and it was Tuesday. Emily wanted to wear her very best clothes, so she got dressed in a white dress. She looked pretty. She skipped outside and down the street until she saw her friend. When she saw him, her face lit up with a smile. Then, Emily ran to meet her friend. They hugged each other tightly and played together. They laughed, sang songs, and played tag. Emily and her friend had so much fun together. Emily was so happy when the day ended. She said goodbye to her friend, wearing the same white dress. She waved until she could not see him anymore. Then, Emily skipped happily home. It had been a magical Tuesday!

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a hot day. The sun was shining, and all the kids wanted to play. They went to a big pool to have fun and cool down. As they played in the pool, a little crab walked by. He was very small and liked to pinch things with his claws. The kids saw the crab and wanted to play with him too. They were very careful not to get pinched by his claws. They played all day in the hot sun and the cool pool. The little crab pinched a beach ball and made everyone laugh. At the end of the day, they were all happy and tired. They said goodbye to the little crab and went home to rest.

Once upon a time, there was a wild dog named Spot. Spot liked to run, jump, and play all day in the big park. He had many friends, like the big tree, the funny duck, and the small bug. One sunny day, Spot saw a new sign in the park. The sign had many words on it. Spot did not know what the words said, but he wanted to find out. He asked his friend, the big tree, but the tree did not know. He asked the funny duck, but the duck did not know either. Spot even asked the small bug, but the bug did not know what the sign said. Spot felt sad that he could not read the words on the sign. But then, a little girl named Lily came to the park. Lily saw Spot looking at the sign and asked if he needed help. Spot wagged his tail and barked. Lily read the words on the sign to Spot. The sign said, "Be kind to all the wild animals in the park." Spot was happy that he knew what the sign said, and he thanked Lily. From that day on, Spot and Lily became the best of friends, and they played together in the park every day.

Once upon a time, there was a loud turkey. The turkey loved to make noise all day. He would gobble and gobble, making everyone laugh. One day, the turkey found a big bell. He thought it would be fun to attach the bell to his neck. So, he asked his friend, the cat, to help him. The cat tied the bell to the turkey's neck with a soft string. Now, the loud turkey was even louder! He walked around, gobbling and ringing the bell. Everyone in the farm could hear him. The other animals laughed and clapped. The loud turkey was very happy, and he made everyone smile.
