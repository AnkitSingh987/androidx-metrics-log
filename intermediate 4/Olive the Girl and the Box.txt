Category: Intermediate
Title: Olive the Girl and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Olive. Olive liked to clean her room. She had a comfortable bed and a big toy box. One day, Olive was cleaning her room. She found a small box under her bed. The box was hidden and Olive never saw it before. She opened the box and found a magic wand. Olive waved the magic wand and something unexpected happened. Her toys started to clean the room by themselves! Olive was so happy. Now, she could play and have a clean room too.

Once upon a time there was a boy who was playing in his modern bedroom. He liked to run around and poke his pillow. One day, the boy got a bit too excited and started to poke the pillow very hard. But he didn't realize that the pillow had a hidden, old button at the back. Suddenly, the button began to beep and the whole room started shaking! The boy was so scared that he started crying. Suddenly, a big, old man appeared in the room and scowled at the boy. the boy was trembling and the old man asked him why he had been poking the pillow so hard. The boy apologized and said that he just wanted to have some fun. The old man smiled and then he said, "as punishment, I have to take your modern bedroom away from you! Goodbye!" The boy started to sob and he tried to plead but the old man had already gone. The boy was sad and upset. He never got to play and poke his pillow in his modern bedroom ever again.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a deep hole in her backyard. She wanted to examine it, so she looked inside. She saw a shiny lock at the bottom of the hole. Sue was very curious. Sue asked her friend, Tom, "What do you think is in the hole?" Tom said, "I don't know, but let's find out!" They both looked at the lock and tried to open it, but it was too hard. Sue and Tom decided to ask their moms for help. Their moms came and looked at the lock. They told Sue and Tom that the lock was very old, and they should leave it alone. Sue and Tom listened to their moms and went to play in the yard. They always remembered the deep hole with the shiny lock, and it became a fun secret between them.

Lily and Ben were playing in the snow. They made a big snowman with a hat and a scarf. They gave him a carrot nose and two rocks for eyes. They were very happy. "Look at our snowman!" Lily said. "He is so big and nice!" "Yes, he is!" Ben said. "But he is also messy. He has snow all over him. We should put some snow away." Lily and Ben started to put some snow away from the snowman. They made a pile of snow next to him. They worked hard and fast. But then, they heard a loud noise. It was their dog, Max. He ran out of the house and jumped on the pile of snow. He barked and wagged his tail. He made the snow fly everywhere. "Max, no!" Lily and Ben shouted. "You are ruining our snowman!" Max did not listen. He kept playing with the snow. He knocked off the hat and the scarf. He bit the carrot nose. He made the snowman fall down. Lily and Ben were very sad. They looked at their snowman. He was not big and nice anymore. He was small and messy. "Bad dog!" Lily said. "You made our snowman go away!" "Sorry, kids," their mom said. She came out of the house and saw the mess. "Max was too excited. He wanted to play with you." She hugged Lily and Ben and gave them a kiss. "Don't worry. You can make another snowman tomorrow. It is still winter. There is plenty of snow." She took Max inside the house. She gave him a treat and a toy. She told him to be good. Lily and Ben smiled. They were not sad anymore. They knew they could have more fun in the snow. They went inside the house too. They had some hot chocolate and cookies. They told their mom about their snowman. They laughed and cuddled. They were happy.
