Category: Intermediate
Title: Max and Millie and the Garden

Max and Millie were best friends and they always liked to play together. Today they wanted to fight with each other but mum said no. "You must play nice and not fight," she said. Max and Millie still wanted to fight, so when mum wasn't looking they ran outside into the back garden. Max thought he was the strongest and Millie thought she was the toughest. So they decided to have a fight. Max got ready to attack and Millie stepped back onto the ground. Max made a big lunge at Millie and before she could run away he caught her by the arm. Max tried to look tough but his punches didn't hurt Millie. She squirmed and wriggled free, running away as fast as she could. "You can't fight me!", she shouted. Max chased after her around the garden but Millie was too fast. She jumped over the fence and ran away, leaving Max behind. He was so angry he punched the ground hard and hurt his hand. Mum saw what happened and she came outside to tell Max off. "I told you not to fight," she said. "Fighting isn't the answer. You have to learn to understand each other, not fight." Max thought about what mum said and he knew she was right. He learned his lesson and he and Millie became best friends again. They have never fought since.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys and watch her daddy work. Her daddy was a builder, and he made big houses for people to live in. Lily liked to watch him work from the window, and she would clap her hands when he finished a house. One day, Lily saw that her daddy was sad. He looked at the broken chair in their house, and he didn't know how to fix it. Lily wanted to help her daddy feel better, so she had an idea. She ran to her room and grabbed her toy hammer and some tape. Lily went to her daddy and said, "Daddy, I can help you fix the chair!" Her daddy smiled and said, "Thank you, Lily. Let's work together." They worked side by side, with Lily using her toy hammer and her daddy using his big hammer. Soon, the chair was fixed and strong again. Lily's daddy was so happy that his little girl helped him. He picked her up and gave her a big hug. "You did a great job, Lily," he said. "I'm so proud of you." From that day on, Lily and her daddy worked together to fix things around the house. And they were never sad again.

There was once a little girl who had a cheap toy car. She loved to play with it and zip around with it's wheels. One day, she wanted to use her car outside but she couldn't find it. She searched the house high and low until she found it in the freezer. She asked her mom, "Mom, What happened to my car?" Her mom said "Oh, it got so hot outside that it melted a little. I put it in the freezer so it would be safe". The little girl was so happy and excited to play with her car again. She said,"Now it's even cooler, let's go outside and play!" So the little girl grabbed her melted car and raced outside with it's squeaky little wheels.

Once upon a time, there was a powerful car named Zoomy. Zoomy loved to go fast and help people. One day, Zoomy saw a little boy named Timmy who was lost. Zoomy said, "Hi, I am Zoomy. I am a powerful car. I can help you find your way home." Timmy replied, "Thank you, Zoomy! I am lost and need help." Zoomy and Timmy went down the road and saw a sign. The sign showed the way to Timmy's house. They followed the sign and soon found Timmy's house. Timmy said, "Thank you, Zoomy, for helping me find my way home!" Zoomy smiled and said, "You're welcome, Timmy! I am always here to help." And with that, Zoomy zoomed away, ready to help someone else.
