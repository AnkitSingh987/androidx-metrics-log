Category: Intermediate
Title: Sally the Famous Alligator and the Bird

Once upon a time there was a famous alligator named Sally. She lived in the swamp, where she swam and rested. One day Sally and her friends were swimming in the swamp when a little bird stopped by. "Hello friends!" chirped the bird. "Where are you all going?" "We're going to rest," replied Sally in her deep voice. The bird laughed. "Resting? That sounds fun! Can I join you?" Sally smiled and nodded. "Yes, of course!" Soon they were all resting, taking a nap under the sun. Sally enjoyed the rest, it made her feel much better. "That was so much fun," said the bird when they were done. Sally and her friends all agreed. From that day forwards, resting became a famous event for the alligators in the swamp!

One day, a girl named Mia found a unique flute. It was a pretty flute with many colors. Mia wanted to play the flute. She blew into it and made a sound. "Wow!" said Mia, "I like this flute!" Mia played the flute every day. She got better and better. One day, she played a special song. When she played the song, a big bird came. The bird said, "Hello Mia, I am here to help you." Mia was surprised. She asked the bird, "Can you take me to a fun place?" The bird said, "Yes, but first you must release the other animals from the flute." Mia played the special song again. She released many animals from the flute. There were cats, dogs, and even a small elephant. Mia and the bird went on a fun trip with all the animals. They played games and had a big party. The animals were happy, and Mia was happy too. She knew she had found a very special flute.

Once upon a time, there was a big boat called a yacht. Many friends liked to play on the yacht. They were very competitive. They always wanted to win games. One day, they played a game to see who could jump the highest. They all tried very hard. Then, a little bird came. The bird wanted to play too. The friends did not want the bird to play. They said the bird was too small. But the bird said, "Please, let me play!" The bird insisted. So, the friends let the bird play with them. The bird jumped very high. It jumped higher than all the friends! The friends were very surprised. They did not think the little bird could win. But the bird was very happy. It showed the friends that even small birds can do big things. The friends learned to be kind and let everyone play.

Once upon a time there was a little girl. She was very happy and loved to play. One day she wanted to try something new. Her mommy gave her a needle and told her to be careful. The little girl started to try to use the needle, but it was too hard. She tried and tried but she couldn't do it. Soon she got frustrated and started to cry. Her mommy came over and hugged her. She told her that it was okay to try something new, but it was okay to not be able to do it too. The little girl wiped away her tears. She smiled, thanked her mommy and said she would try again some time. Together they put the needle away and went off to play something else. The little girl was so happy that she tried something new, even if it was hard. She knew that she just needed a little help and she could try again.

One day, a small bird named Bob wanted to sit on an island. The island was brilliant and had many pretty trees. Bob told his friend, Cat, about the island. Cat wanted to sit on the island too. Bob and Cat went to the island. But they saw a big dog on the island. The dog did not want them to sit there. Bob and Cat were sad. They thought of a plan to make the dog go away. Bob found a stick and gave it to Cat. Cat threw the stick far away. The big dog ran to get the stick. Now, Bob and Cat could sit on the brilliant island. They were happy and played all day.
