Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Vase and the Girl

Once there was a little girl who was three years old. She had a cool vase that she loved. Every day she would take it out and admire how sparkly and pretty it was. But then one day, a mean boy came along. He hated the vase and so he kicked it over and broke it. The little girl was so sad. She cried and cried. But then her big sister came to the rescue. She had a cool idea. She took all the pieces of the broken vase and glued them back together. When it was finished, the vase looked even cooler than before! The little girl was so happy, she thanked her big sister for fixing the vase. From then on, she loved the vase even more than before. And she made sure no one ever tried to break it again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy hook. He would pretend to be a pirate, and he would say, "I am a pirate, and this is my hook!" One day, Tim was playing in his room when it started to get dark outside. Tim did not like the dark, so he asked his mom, "Mom, can you turn on the light? It is dark, and I do not like it." His mom said, "Of course, Tim. I will turn on the light for you." She turned on the light, and Tim felt better. He went back to playing with his hook and pretending to be a pirate. He said, "Now I can see and play with my hook!" Tim and his mom played pirates together until it was time for bed. Tim put his hook away and went to sleep, knowing that he could play again tomorrow.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red ball. It was under a tree. A little girl named Lily wanted to play with the ball. But the ball did not seem to be available. It was stuck in the tree. Lily tried to get the ball. She jumped, but she was too small. She looked around for help. She saw her dog, Max. Max was good at getting things. Lily called Max over to the tree. She pointed at the ball. Max looked at the ball and then at Lily. He knew what to do. Max jumped and got the ball. The ball was now available for Lily to play with. Lily and Max played with the ball and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Lily. She was very curious and liked to explore. One day, she went for a walk in the woods and saw a big tree. She kneeled down next to it and wondered what it was like inside. She saw a crack in the bark and peeped through it! All of a sudden, a grumpy old owl appeared in the tree. He asked, "What are you doing here?" Lily said, "I'm just wondering what it looks like in there!" The owl flew out of the tree and said, "You must never enter another creature's home without permission. Now kneel down and apologize.” Lily bowed her head and said sorry. The owl said, "That's better. Now I can go back to my home in peace." Lily waved goodbye and walked home with a newfound respect for nature. She learned an important lesson: never be too nosy!

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to go for a walk every day. One day, while walking, he saw a scary, broken toy. Tim wanted to repair the toy and make it nice again. Tim took the toy home and said to his mom, "Mom, I found this scary toy. Can we repair it?" His mom looked at the toy and said, "Yes, Tim, let's try to repair it together." They worked hard to repair the toy, but it was still scary. The toy started to move and talk all by itself. Tim and his mom were scared. The toy chased them around the house and they could not stop it. In the end, the toy broke more things in the house and Tim and his mom were very sad.
