Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Sue and the Toys

Tom and Sue were twins who liked to play together. They had a big box of toys in their room, but they wanted to share some with their cousin Amy, who lived far away. They decided to mail some toys to Amy as a surprise. They picked out some toys that they thought Amy would like: a ball, a doll, a car, and a book. They put them in a big envelope and wrote Amy's name and address on it. Then they asked their mom to take them to the post office. At the post office, they gave the envelope to the man behind the counter. He put a stamp on it and said, "This will take a few days to get to Amy. She will be very happy when she opens it." Tom and Sue were impatient. They wanted Amy to get the toys right away. They asked their mom, "Can we call Amy and tell her we mailed her something?" Their mom said, "No, that would spoil the surprise. You have to wait until she gets it and calls you back. That will be more fun." Tom and Sue were not happy. They wanted to talk to Amy now. They pouted and whined all the way home. When they got home, they saw a package on the porch. It had their names on it. They ran to open it. Inside, they found a letter and a jar of jelly. The letter said, "Dear Tom and Sue, thank you for being such good cousins. I miss you a lot. I made this jelly for you with grandma. I hope you like it. Love, Amy." Tom and Sue were very surprised and happy. They hugged the jar of jelly and ran to show their mom. They said, "Look, mom, Amy mailed us something too! She made us jelly! Can we eat some now?" Their mom smiled and said, "Yes, you can. That was very nice of Amy. Why don't you call her and say thank you?" Tom and Sue agreed. They called Amy and thanked her for the jelly. They told her they mailed her some toys. They chatted and laughed for a while. They felt closer to Amy, even though she lived far away. They learned that mailing and receiving things can be fun, but also that they have to be patient sometimes.

One day, a wise cat named Tom found a laser. The laser was red and shiny. Tom thought it was fun to play with. Tom went to his friend, a dog named Max. Tom said, "Max, let's play with my new laser." Max was very happy and said, "Yes, let's play!" Tom and Max played with the laser all day. They ran and jumped to catch the red dot. They had so much fun together. At the end of the day, they were tired but happy. They knew they would play with the laser again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a restless rabbit. The rabbit liked to run and jump all day. It never wanted to stop. The rabbit was always having fun. One day, the rabbit met a big tree. The tree said, "Little rabbit, you should stop and rest." But the rabbit did not want to stop. It wanted to keep playing. Finally, the rabbit got tired. It stopped and sat under the tree. The tree was happy. The rabbit learned that it is good to stop and rest sometimes. And they all lived happily ever after.

Tommy liked to play with his toy trumpet. He liked to make loud noises and pretend he was in a band. He did not care if his sister Lily was trying to sleep or read. He did not care if his mom was on the phone or cooking. He was ignorant of how his trumpet bothered them. One day, Lily had an idea. She took her dad's phone and hid behind the couch. She waited for Tommy to start playing his trumpet. Then she pressed the record button. She wanted to show Tommy how annoying he was. "Tommy, stop playing your trumpet!" Mom shouted from the kitchen. "You are making too much noise!" But Tommy did not stop. He blew harder and louder. He thought he was very good. Lily smiled and stopped the record. She ran to Tommy and showed him the phone. "Look, Tommy, this is you!" she said. She played the record for him. Tommy heard his trumpet and his mom's voice. He heard how loud and bad he sounded. He felt ashamed and sorry. He looked at Lily and said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I did not know I was so noisy. Can you forgive me?" Lily nodded and hugged him. "It's okay, Tommy. I forgive you. But please, play your trumpet only when we are not busy or sleeping. Okay?" Tommy nodded and hugged her back. "Okay, Lily. I will be more careful. Thank you for showing me the record. You are a good sister." They put away the phone and the trumpet and went to play something else. Mom smiled and was happy. She was proud of her children. They learned to be kind and respectful.
