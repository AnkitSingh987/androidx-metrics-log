Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She lived in a house with an upstairs. Amy loved to play upstairs with her toys. One day, she felt anxious because she could not find her favorite toy, Mr. Bunny. Amy called out to her mom for help. "Mom, I can't find Mr. Bunny!" she said. Her mom came upstairs to help her look. They looked under the bed, in the toy box, and even behind the curtains. But Mr. Bunny was nowhere to be found. Just when Amy was about to give up, she heard a noise coming from the closet. She opened the door, and there was her little brother Tom, holding Mr. Bunny! Tom had been hiding in the closet with Mr. Bunny the whole time. Amy and her mom laughed, and they all went downstairs to play together.

John was walking through the park, gazing at the trees. He suddenly spotted something on the ground. It was a peanut! Thinking it would make a tasty snack, he bent down to pick it up. But then he heard a loud noise. It was coming from right beside him. It was a big, stubborn bulldog who did not want John to take the peanut. The bulldog stood up and barked loudly. John was scared and jumped back. He shook his head. "No, no peanut for you," he said firmly. The bulldog gave him a long, stubborn stare. But then he slowly sat back down and let John have the peanut. John smiled, and thanked the bulldog. He smiled as he ate the delicious peanut. He was glad that the bulldog was so stubborn, for it had allowed him to enjoy a tasty treat.

One day, a curious little girl named Mia went for a walk. She wanted to observe the world around her. She saw birds, flowers, and trees. Mia loved to learn new things. At lunch time, Mia sat down under a big tree. She opened her lunch box and saw a yummy sandwich. But then, something unexpected happened. A little squirrel came and took her sandwich! Mia was sad, but she did not get mad. She watched the squirrel run away with her lunch. She followed the squirrel to see what would happen next. The squirrel took the sandwich to a small nest in a tree. Mia saw baby squirrels in the nest. They were very hungry. She understood that the squirrel took her lunch to feed its babies. Mia felt happy that her sandwich could help the baby squirrels. She went home and told her mom the story. They both smiled and made another sandwich for Mia's lunch.

There were two friends walking down the road. As they were walking, they saw something weird. "What is that?" The first friend asked. "It looks like a plate," the second friend replied. The two friends then went over to the plate. It was really weird! The friends looked closely and saw some letters written on the plate. Suddenly, the plate started to shake. The two friends got scared and ran away. They stopped a few yards away. They turned back and could see the plate still shaking. The plate then spoke. It asked, "Who are you?" The two friends were very surprised. The first friend replied, "We're just two friends who were walking by." The plate then said, "That's nice. Have a good day!" The two friends stood in shock. They said goodbye and left quickly. They were still confused about what just happened. As they walked away, they agreed that it was a very weird day.

Sam and Lily were playing with their toys in the living room. They liked to make up stories with their dolls and cars. Sam had a blue car and Lily had a pink doll. They pretended that the car and the doll were friends and went on adventures. "Let's go to the park, car," said Lily, making her doll talk. "We can play on the swings and slide." "OK, doll," said Sam, making his car talk. "But we have to be careful of the dog. He is big and scary and likes to chase us." They moved their toys across the floor, making noises and sounds. They had fun playing together. But then they saw something on the table. It was a round thing with holes and a lot of dirty stuff inside. It smelled bad and looked ugly. "What is that?" asked Lily, pointing at the thing. "That is an ashtray," said Sam. "Mommy uses it to put her cigarettes. She smokes a lot and it makes her cough. I don't like it." "Me neither," said Lily. "It is nasty and gross. Why does she smoke?" "I don't know," said Sam. "She says it helps her relax. But I think it is lazy. She should play with us instead." They looked at the ashtray and felt sad. They wished their mommy would stop smoking and finish with the ashtray. They wanted her to be healthy and happy. "Maybe we can help her," said Lily. "Maybe we can make her a card and tell her we love her and we want her to quit." "That is a good idea," said Sam. "Let's do it. Maybe she will listen to us." They left their toys and got some paper and crayons. They drew a picture of their mommy with a big smile and a heart. They wrote "We love you, Mommy. Please finish with the ashtray and play with us." They put the card on the table, next to the ashtray. They hoped their mommy would see it and be proud of them. They hugged each other and went back to their toys. They continued their story with the car and the doll. They were happy and hopeful.
