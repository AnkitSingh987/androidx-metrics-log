Category: Intermediate
Title: The Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a playful cat. The cat liked to play on the porch. The cat liked to smile. One day, the cat saw a big ball. The cat jumped and played with the ball. The cat was happy and smiled more. The cat played on the porch all day. The sun was warm and the cat was happy. The cat smiled and played until it was time to sleep.

Once upon a time there was a bunny named Floppy who loved to build things. One day he decided to build a house out of carrots. He gathered up lots and lots of tasty carrots from the nearby garden. He started to build the house, but soon discovered it was too hard to do all on his own. Suddenly, he noticed a little girl nearby. She was very kind and compassionate, and smiled at Floppy. "Will you help me build my carrot house?" asked Floppy. The girl happily agreed and the two of them set off to work. Together, they managed to build the house in no time at all. When they finished, Floppy thanked the girl for helping him out. She smiled and said, "It was my pleasure." The two of them became fast friends, always building fun and creative things together. Whenever they needed help, they were always there for each other, showing an endless amount of compassion. And they all lived happily ever after!

Once upon a time, there was a little engine named Tim. Tim loved to dress up in colorful clothes. One day, Tim put on a big red hat and a long blue scarf. He looked very funny. Tim went to see his friend, Sue. Sue was a big engine. Sue saw Tim and said, "Why do you dress like that? You look silly!" Tim felt sad, but he still liked his clothes. The next day, Tim and Sue were working together. Suddenly, a big wind came and blew away Sue's hat. Tim used his long blue scarf to catch it. Sue said, "Thank you, Tim! I was wrong. Your clothes are not silly. They are helpful!" Tim was happy, and they both laughed.

Lily and Tom are playing in the park. They like to run and jump and slide. The sun is bright and warm. They see their shadows on the ground. Their shadows are long and funny. "Look, Tom, my shadow is bigger than you!" Lily says. She holds her arms up high. "No, look, Lily, my shadow is bigger than you!" Tom says. He stands on his toes. They laugh and try to make their shadows bigger and bigger. They have fun. But then, they hear a loud roar. They turn around and see a huge shadow behind them. It is dark and scary. It has big teeth and claws. It is a bear! "Help, help, a bear!" Lily and Tom scream. They run away from the shadow. They are very afraid. But then, they see a man. He is holding a camera. He is smiling. "Don't worry, kids, it's not a real bear. It's just a picture. I was taking photos of the park. See?" He shows them the camera. On the screen, they see the bear. It is not a shadow. It is a photo. Lily and Tom feel silly. They are not afraid anymore. They are glad it is not a real bear. "Sorry, I didn't mean to scare you. Do you want to see more pictures?" The man asks. "OK, but no more bears!" Lily and Tom say. They look at the camera. They see pictures of flowers, birds, and clouds. They are pretty and nice. They forget about the shadow. They have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a happy mechanic named Tom. Tom loved to paint. He painted cars all day long. His favorite color was blue. He painted blue cars, blue trucks, and blue buses. Tom was the best painter in town. One day, Tom found a big box outside his shop. He was very excited. Tom opened the box and saw a lot of paint. There were red, green, yellow, and pink paint cans. Tom was very happy. He could paint more cars with these new colors. Tom started to paint a car with the new paint. But something unexpected happened. When he painted the car, it turned into a big, blue bird. The bird flew away, and Tom was very surprised. He didn't know that the new paint was magic. Now, Tom could paint cars that turned into birds. He had more fun painting than ever before.
