Category: Intermediate
Title: The Park of Tim the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He loved to play with his new baseball. One sunny day, Tim went to the park with his mom and dad to play. Tim threw the baseball high up in the sky. He would pause and wait for the ball to come back down. Then, he would catch it with a big smile on his face. His mom and dad clapped and cheered for him. After playing for a long time, Tim got tired. He paused to rest under a big tree. Tim, his mom, and his dad had a fun day at the park with the new baseball. They went home and Tim slept well that night, dreaming of more fun days with his baseball.

Once upon a time, there was a musician named Tim. He loved to play music for everyone. Tim was wealthy and had a big house. He liked to relax and play songs on his guitar. One day, he heard about a big music show in the town. He wanted to go and play his music there. Tim practiced a lot for the big day. He knew that something special would happen at the show. He played his guitar every day to get ready. His friends and family came to listen and they all loved his music. The day of the big show came, and Tim was very excited. He played his guitar for the people in the town. Everyone loved his music and clapped their hands. As he played, a big music man came to listen. The music man liked Tim's music so much that he asked Tim to join his band. Tim was so happy, and his practice had paid off. Now, he could play his music for even more people.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside. One day, Spot saw a big ball. He had the urge to play with it. The ball was red and available to play with. Spot went to his friend, Cat. Spot asked, "Cat, do you want to play with the ball?" Cat looked at the ball and said, "No, I refuse to play with the ball. I want to nap." Spot was sad, but he still wanted to play. Spot went to his other friend, Bird. Spot asked, "Bird, do you want to play with the ball?" Bird looked at the ball and said, "Yes, I will play with you!" Spot and Bird played with the ball all day long. They had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a big red barn on a farm. This was not a normal barn. People believed that the barn could talk. One sunny day, a little girl named Lily went to visit the barn. She walked up to the barn and said, "Hello, Mr. Barn!" To her surprise, the barn said, "Hello, Lily!" Lily could not believe her ears. From that day on, Lily and the talking barn became best friends. They would talk and play all day long. And even though the barn was not normal, Lily loved it just the way it was.

Once upon a time, in a small garden, there was a big, round squash. The squash had a friend, a reliable dog named Spot. Spot always watched over the squash to make sure it was safe. One day, a naughty bird came and tried to spoil the squash. Spot saw the bird and barked very loud. The bird got scared and flew away. Spot had saved the squash from being spoiled. From that day on, Spot and the squash were the best of friends. They played together in the garden every day. The squash knew that Spot was a reliable friend who would always keep it safe.

Mama was drinking a hot coffee at the kitchen table. She sighed and said, "I wish I could see the sun today." Just then, the sun started to shine through the window. "Look!", mama said, pointing. Little brother clapped his hands and said, "shine, shine!" He ran outside to play in the sun. Mama ran outside, too. She wanted to feel the sunshine on her face. Mama and little brother lay on the grass and looked up at the bright sky. Mama smiled, looking at her little boy. Suddenly, Mama's hot coffee cup started to tip, spilling coffee on the grass. Mama quickly grabbed the cup and said, "oops!" She laughed. Little brother smiled and said, "shine, shine!"
