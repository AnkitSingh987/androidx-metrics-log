Category: Intermediate
Title: The Hippo and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big hippo. The hippo was very filthy. He lived in a small pond with his friends. They liked to play in the mud all day. The filthy hippo was happy. One day, a small bird came to the pond. The bird saw the filthy hippo and said, "Hello, Mr. Hippo! Why are you so filthy?" The hippo looked at the bird and said, "I like to play in the mud. It is fun!" The small bird thought for a moment. Then, it said, "Can I play in the mud with you?" The hippo smiled and said, "Yes, you can!" So, the small bird and the filthy hippo played in the mud together. They had lots of fun and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was an obedient child who always listened to his mom and dad. Tim had a big, happy smile on his face most of the time. He loved to play with his toys, run in the park, and eat his favorite meal, which was pizza. One day, Tim saw a little girl named Lily in the park. She had a frown on her face because she was sad. Tim wanted to make her happy, so he shared his toys with her. They played together, and soon Lily's frown turned into a big smile. Later, Tim's mom called him for dinner. It was time for their meal, and Tim was excited to eat pizza again. Lily joined them, and they all ate their meal together. Tim, Lily, and their parents were happy, and there were no more frowns on their faces. They became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a quiet little girl named Lily. She loved to dress up in costumes. Her favorite costume was a big, red cape that made her feel like a superhero. One day, Lily saw a cat stuck in a tree. She put on her red cape and said, "Don't worry, cat! I will save you!" She tried to climb the tree, but it was too tall. Lily didn't give up. She found a long stick and used it to help the cat. "Come on, cat, grab the stick!" she said. The cat grabbed the stick and Lily pulled it down. She was so happy she could help the cat. Lily's mommy saw what she did and said, "Lily, you really are a superhero! You succeeded in saving the cat!" Lily smiled and felt proud. She knew that even a quiet little girl could do big things when she tried.

Once upon a time, in a cold, quiet forest, there lived a little rabbit named Remy. Remy had many friends, but he was very shy. One day, Remy's friends decided to play a game called hide and seek. Remy was excited, but he was also scared. He did not want his friends to find him too fast. Remy's friend, Benny the squirrel, said, "Remy, don't worry! Just try your best to hide. We will all have fun!" Remy nodded and decided to try his best. All the friends closed their eyes and started to count. Remy ran as fast as he could to find the best hiding spot. As Remy was running, he saw a big, cold rock. He thought it would be a great place to hide. So, Remy hid behind the rock and stayed very quiet. His friends looked and looked, but they could not find Remy. They started to get worried. Finally, Benny the squirrel found Remy behind the big, cold rock. "Remy, you did a great job hiding!" Benny said with a big smile. Remy felt happy and proud that he tried his best. From that day on, Remy was never scared to play hide and seek with his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max was a very good dog. One day, Max went to the airport with his friend, a little boy named Tim. Tim's mom and dad were going on a trip, and they needed Max to help them. At the airport, Max had a very important job. He had to lead Tim, his mom, and his dad to the right place. Max was very good at leading. He walked in front, and Tim, his mom, and his dad followed him. They walked and walked until they found the right place. But then, Max saw something dangerous. A big truck was coming very fast. Max knew he had to keep Tim and his family safe. So, he barked and barked to tell them to stop. They stopped just in time, and the truck went by. Tim and his family were safe, and they thanked Max for being such a good dog.
