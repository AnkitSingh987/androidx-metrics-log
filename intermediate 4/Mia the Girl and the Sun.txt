Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Sun

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk with her mom. They wore their big hats to keep the sun away. Mia loved to walk with her mom in the park. They saw many pretty flowers and birds. As they walked, Mia saw a big, tasty ice cream shop. "Mom, can we get some ice cream? It looks so tasty!" Mia asked. Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, we can have some ice cream." They went into the ice cream shop and picked their favorite flavors. Mia chose chocolate and her mom picked vanilla. They sat outside, enjoying their tasty treats. Mia was very happy to wear her big hat and go for a walk with her mom.

Once upon a time, there was a fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim all day with his friends in the big pond. They would play games and splash each other with their tails. One day, Fin saw a bird up in the sky. The bird could fly high and fast. Fin felt envious of the bird because he wanted to fly too. He wished he could be up in the sky and see the world from high above. Fin decided to ask the bird how to fly. The bird told Fin that he could not fly because he was a fish, but he could swim fast and far. Fin felt sad, but then he realized that he was special too. He could swim and explore the deep pond with his friends. Fin was happy being a fish and continued to swim and play with his friends every day.

Once there was an old man who had a thoughtful sword. He was very proud of it, and he looked after it very carefully. The old man took the sword outside one day and decided to have a picnic. He put down a large blanket and laid the sword beside him. As the old man was eating, a little boy started to admire the sword. The boy asked the old man if he could borrow the sword. The old man said, "Yes, but you must be sure to keep it clean and dry. Otherwise, it can get rusty." The little boy nodded, took the sword and ran off to play. In his enthusiasm, he splashed water on the sword and left it out in the rain. When the little boy returned with the sword, it was all rusty. The old man shook his head sadly. The little boy was very sorry and said, "I'm sorry. I didn't mean to let the sword get rusty." The old man said, "I understand. But next time, think before you act. Being thoughtful can help avoid a lot of trouble.” Moral: Being thoughtful can help avoid a lot of trouble.

Once upon a time, there was a gentle cat named Lucy. Lucy lived in a small house with her best friend, a dog named Max. They played together every day and were very happy. One day, Lucy met a handsome cat named Tom. Tom and Lucy liked each other very much. They decided to marry and have a big party with all their friends. Max was excited to help them plan the party. At the party, Lucy and Tom were dancing when they heard a small cry. They looked around and saw a little mouse stuck in a drain. "Help me, please!" the mouse squeaked. Lucy, Tom, and Max worked together to save the mouse from the drain. The little mouse was so grateful, and they all became friends. The party became even more fun, and everyone danced the night away. In the end, Lucy and Tom were married, and they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a girl named Mia wanted to buy a modest kayak. She went to the store with her mom to find one. Mia saw a blue kayak that she liked very much. She was very excited to take it to the lake and play. At the lake, Mia saw a duck near her kayak. She wanted to be friends with the duck. So, Mia got into her kayak and paddled closer to the duck. But, the duck was not a real duck. It was a toy duck! Mia was surprised and laughed. She took the toy duck and put it in her kayak. Now, Mia had a new friend to play with at the lake. She had fun with her modest kayak and her toy duck.
