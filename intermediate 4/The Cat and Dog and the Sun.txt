Category: Intermediate
Title: The Cat and Dog and the Sun

One day, a cat and a dog went to play. They had fun in the sun. The cat said, "I want to introduce you to something new." The dog said, "What is it?" The cat took the dog to a big shop. In the shop, there was a smell. It was a good smell. The cat said, "This is coffee. It is delicious." The dog wanted to try it. They both had a cup of coffee. But, the coffee was not for cats and dogs. It made them jump and run fast. They ran all around the shop. The people in the shop were surprised. The cat and dog did not know that coffee was for people, not for them. They laughed and went back to play outside.

Mama led little Micah to the barn. He held her hand, trembling with excitement. Inside, there were all kinds of animals. Mama walked to a small, hidden corner of the barn. There, in the shadows, was a beautiful leather couch. Mama said: "Sit on the couch, my little one." Micah did, trembling with curious anticipation. He wondered what was going to happen. Suddenly, a large, fluffy rabbit hopped out from under the couch! Micah clapped happily and ran to pet the rabbit's soft fur. He had never seen such a thing. Mama smiled and said: "See? I told you that hidden surprises can be the most wonderful thing."

Once upon a time, there was a monkey named Momo. Momo was very intelligent. He lived in a big tree with his family. They played and ate bananas all day. Momo loved to turn around and jump from branch to branch. One day, Momo saw a big box under the tree. He was curious, so he went down to look at it. Momo turned the box around and found a door. He opened the door and went inside. Momo found a room full of bananas! He was so happy. Momo decided to share the bananas with his family. But when he tried to leave the box, the door was gone! Momo was scared. Then, he had an idea. He turned around three times, and the door appeared again! Momo was so intelligent that he found a way to open the door. He took the bananas and shared them with his family. They were all very happy and surprised.

Sara and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a big slide. The slide is high and shiny. Sara and Ben want to go on the slide. "Can I go first?" Sara asks. "No, I want to go first!" Ben says. They both run to the slide. They push and pull. They are not happy. A man sees them. He has a bag of corn. The corn is yellow and sweet. The man smiles. "Hello, children. Do you like corn?" he asks. "Yes, we like corn!" Sara and Ben say. "Then why don't you share this corn with me? And then you can share the slide too. Sharing is good. Sharing makes you happy." the man says. He gives them some corn. Sara and Ben eat the corn. They say thank you. They feel better. "Can I go first?" Sara asks. "OK, you can go first. And then I will go." Ben says. They share the slide. They go up and down. They laugh and have fun. They are happy. They are friends. They share.

Once upon a time, there was a brave knight. He was very loyal to his kingdom. One day, he went out on a quest to save his kingdom. He took his horse and rode into the forest. When he got to a lake, he noticed a board floating on the water. He wondered what it was and decided to take a closer look. He reached out to take the board, when suddenly a small voice said, "I offer you this board as a gift brave knight!" The knight looked around and saw a small creature. It was a friendly elf. The elf smiled at the knight and said, "I am here to help you! I promise that if you accept my offer, I will be a loyal companion on your quest!" The knight was grateful for the elf's offer and took the board. He thanked the elf for his help and together, the elf and the knight went off on the quest together. The knight knew that was sure to be successful with the elf's help. The end!
