Category: Intermediate
Title: A Tale of the Cat

One day, a cat and a dog were playing outside. They saw a big cup of milk on a strip of grass. The cat said, "I want to drink the milk!" The dog said, "No, I want to drink the milk!" They both looked at the milk and felt guilty because they wanted it all. Then, the cat had an idea. "Let's pour the milk into two cups, so we can both have some!" The dog agreed. They found two cups and poured the milk into them. They were happy to share. But suddenly, a bird came and took one of the cups. The cat and the dog were surprised! They never thought a bird would take their milk. They looked at each other and laughed. They learned that sometimes unexpected things happen, and it's better to share and be happy together.

Once upon a time, there was a rat. The rat was not happy. The rat thought life was difficult. He had no friends. He lived in a small hole in a big house. One day, a cat came to the house. The cat saw the rat and said, "I want to eat you." The rat was scared. He thought, "I need to find a way to not get eaten." The rat had an idea. He said to the cat, "If you don't eat me, I can help you. I can find food for you." The cat thought about it and said, "Okay, I will not eat you. Let's be friends." The rat and the cat became friends. They helped each other find food. The rat was not scared anymore. He was happy because he now had a friend. Life was not difficult anymore.

Once upon a time, in a thick forest, there was a big pine tree. Many animals lived around the tree. One day, a bird named Bob came to the forest. Bob was looking for a new home. Bob saw the big pine tree and said, "I want to attach my nest to this tree." A squirrel named Sam heard Bob and came to help. Sam said, "I can help you attach your nest to the tree." Bob was happy and said, "Thank you, Sam!" Together, Bob and Sam attached the nest to the pine tree. They became good friends and played together every day. All the animals in the forest saw their friendship and learned that helping each other is a good thing. And they all lived happily ever after. The moral of the story is to always help others and be kind.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there was a tall tree. On one of the tree's strong branches, a little bird sat. The little bird was very patient as it waited for its mommy to bring food. The mommy bird came back with a yummy worm. The little bird was so happy! It stretched its neck to reach the worm. The mommy bird dropped the worm into the little bird's mouth. The little bird ate the worm and felt full. The sun began to set, and the sky turned pink and orange. The mommy bird and the little bird cuddled on the branch. They felt warm and safe. The little bird was so thankful for the yummy worm and the strong branch. The mommy bird was happy to take care of her baby. They both fell asleep as the stars came out.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a regular toy named Stitch. Stitch was a small, blue dog. Sue and Stitch did everything together. They played, ate, and even slept together. One day, Sue and Stitch went to the park. They had fun playing on the swings and the slide. They laughed and played all day long. But when it was time to go home, Stitch was nowhere to be found. Sue was very sad. Sue looked everywhere for Stitch. She asked her friends if they had seen him. Finally, she found Stitch under a big tree. Sue was so happy to have Stitch back. From that day on, Sue and Stitch were never apart again.

One day, a boy named Tim was eager to play in the snow. He put on his coat, hat, and boots. He ran outside and saw his friend, Sue. Sue was making a big snowman. Tim wanted to help her. "Can I help you make the snowman?" Tim asked. Sue did not want help. She ignored Tim. Tim was sad. He walked away and started to make his own snowman. He made a small snowman with a big smile. Suddenly, something unexpected happened. The snowman came to life! The snowman said, "Hello Tim, I am your friend now!" Tim was very surprised and happy. He ran to Sue and said, "My snowman is alive! Come see!" Sue stopped ignoring Tim and they played with the snowman together. They all had fun in the snow and became good friends.
