Category: Intermediate
Title: Sally the Girl and the Magic

One day, a little girl named Sally went for a walk with her mother. Sally was already very excited to go on an adventure. As they walked, Sally noticed a cord. It was an ordinary cord, just lying on the ground. Sally wanted to pick it up and start playing with it. Her mom warned her against it and asked her to stay away from the cord. Sally obeyed, but still wanted to play with it. Suddenly, a light flashed from inside the cord. It wasn’t an ordinary light, but a magical one! Sally's eyes widened and she cried out with joy. She picked up the cord, the light kept shining. Sally was so curious about its power. The mom couldn't believe her luck! She had no idea how Sally noticed the magic cord but was grateful that she did. She promised to take care of it. From then on, Sally and mom cherished the cord. They kept it safe in their home and every now and then, Sally used its magical light.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big steel chair. It was a very fierce chair with sharp teeth. Tim thought it was a fun chair to seat on. He called his friend, Sam, to come see the chair. "Look, Sam!" Tim said. "I found a fierce chair. Let's seat on it!" Sam was scared of the chair but did not want to be a baby. So, Sam said, "Okay, I will seat on it too." When Sam and Tim were on the chair, something unexpected happened. The chair started to move! It was not just a chair, but a hungry monster! The monster ate Tim and Sam. They were very sad and scared inside the monster's belly. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a box. The box was very filthy. It sat in the dirt all day and all night. No one wanted to play with the filthy box. The box felt very sad. One day, a little car came by. The car saw the filthy box and wanted to be friends. The car said, "Don't be sad, box. Let's play together!" The car went zoom, zoom, zoom around the box. The box started to feel happy. The car and the box played all day. They zoomed through the dirt and had so much fun. The filthy box didn't feel lonely anymore. The car and the box became best friends, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little frog named Jump. Jump loved to play with his friends in the deep pond. They would swim and jump all day long. One sunny day, Jump and his friends found a big plate near the pond. They were very curious and wanted to play with it. Jump had an idea. He said, "Let's use the plate to jump even higher!" Jump and his friends took turns jumping on the plate. Each time they jumped, they went higher and higher. They laughed and had so much fun. At the end of the day, Jump and his friends were tired but very happy. They put the plate near the deep pond, so they could use it to jump high again the next day. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went for a walk. She saw a pastry shop and went inside. Lucy saw many yummy pastries. She saw a wet pastry and thought it was strange. Lucy wanted to observe it more. Lucy looked at the wet pastry and saw it move. She was surprised! The pastry moved again and again. Lucy watched it closely. Then, something unexpected happened. The wet pastry started to grow! The pastry grew bigger and bigger. It turned into a big, wet, pastry friend! Lucy and the pastry friend played and had fun. They shared other yummy pastries. The twist was that the wet pastry was a magical friend.

One day, a little dog named Spot was walking on a path. He saw a small bird with a hurt wing. The bird was very sad and anxious. Spot wanted to help the bird, so he said, "Don't worry, I will rescue you." Spot ran to find his friend, Sue the cat. He told her about the bird. Sue said, "We can help the bird feel better." They went back to the bird and gently picked it up. They took the bird to their home and gave it food and water. After a few days, the bird's wing was better. The bird was happy and said, "Thank you for rescuing me." Spot and Sue were happy they could help their new friend.
