Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam the Cat and the Bird

One day, a kind cat named Sam went for a walk. Sam was a black cat with big green eyes. He liked to play and sleep in the sun. Sam met a little bird and they became friends. The bird said, "Sam, let's play a game. I will hide and you will find me." Sam agreed and started to look for the bird. He looked under a tree, behind a big rock, and in a small bush. Finally, Sam found the bird sleeping in a nest. Sam said, "I found you, bird!" The bird woke up and said, "You are good at this game, Sam!" They both laughed and played more games until the sun went down. Then, they said goodbye and went to sleep, happy to have a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to collect pretty rocks. Every day, she would take her empty bucket and go out to find more rocks for her collection. One sunny day, Sue met a boy named Tim. Tim saw Sue's bucket and asked, "What are you doing with that empty bucket?" Sue replied, "I am collecting pretty rocks. Do you want to help me?" Tim nodded and they both started looking for rocks together. As they were collecting rocks, they saw a big shiny rock near the edge of a deep hole. Sue and Tim both wanted the shiny rock, but they didn't think about how dangerous it was to get it. They tried to reach the rock, but they both fell into the hole. They were stuck and couldn't get out. Their parents found them later and helped them out of the hole. Sue and Tim were very sad because they lost their pretty rocks and got hurt. The moral of the story is to always think about safety before trying to get something you want.

Once upon a time, there was a famous dog named Spot. Spot loved to walk and play with his friends. He was famous because he could walk on two legs like a person. Everyone in the town knew Spot and liked to watch him walk. One day, Spot went for a walk with his best friend, a little girl named Sue. They walked through the park, and Spot walked on two legs the whole time. Sue laughed and clapped her hands. They had so much fun. As the years went by, Spot and Sue walked and played together every day. They were the best of friends. Spot was still famous, and everyone loved to see him walk. Spot and Sue were happy, and they stayed friends for many years.

Once upon a time, there was a clever little dog named Spot. Spot lived in a small house with his best friend, Tom. They were very happy together. Spot liked to play with Tom all day long. They had a neighbor, a big cat named Whiskers. Whiskers did not like to play or behave well. One day, Spot said to Tom, "Let's go play with Whiskers, our neighbor." Tom said, "Okay, but we must be nice and behave well." They went to Whiskers' house and asked, "Can you play with us?" Whiskers said, "Yes, but I will not behave well." They played together, but Whiskers was not nice. He took Tom's toy and ran away. Spot and Tom were sad. They tried to get the toy back, but Whiskers was too clever. He hid the toy in a place they could not find. Spot and Tom went back home without the toy. In the end, Spot and Tom learned that playing with someone who does not behave well is not fun. They decided to stay away from Whiskers and only play with friends who are nice and behave well. They were sad about losing the toy, but they knew it was better to play with good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a tiger. He was big and strong, and he had a majestic roar! Everyone in the jungle was scared of his roar. One day, the tiger was walking through the jungle when he came upon a strange trunk. It was big and clear, and the tiger was very curious. He wanted to see what was inside. He opened the trunk and stepped inside. Suddenly, the trunk began to close up! The tiger couldn't get out, no matter how hard he tried! He roared and roared, but nobody was there to hear him. He was stuck in the trunk forever - or at least until it rotted away. The End.
