Category: Intermediate
Title: The Mighty Deer and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a mighty deer. The deer had big strong legs and could run very fast. All the little animals in the forest loved to watch the deer run and play. One sunny day, the deer was running through the forest when it heard a tiny voice say, "Help! Help!" The deer stopped and looked around. It saw a little bird stuck in a tree. The bird said, "Please, mighty deer, help me get out of this tree!" The mighty deer wanted to help the little bird. It used its strong legs to jump and reach the bird. With a gentle touch, the deer set the bird free. The little bird was so happy and said, "Thank you, mighty deer, for saving me!" From that day on, the mighty deer and the little bird were the best of friends.

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a big, interesting toy. It was soft and round, like a ball. She wanted to play with it, so she picked it up and gave it a squeeze. When she squeezed the toy, it made a funny noise. Lucy laughed and squeezed it again. Lucy took the toy to her friend, Tim. They played with the toy and squeezed it to make the funny noise. They were having so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. When they squeezed the toy one more time, a little man popped out! The little man looked worried. He said, "I am the emergency helper. I come out when someone needs help. But you both look happy and safe. I think I came out by mistake." Lucy and Tim looked at each other, surprised. They didn't know the toy had a helper inside. The emergency helper said, "Since I am here, let's play together!" Lucy, Tim, and the helper played and laughed all day. They had so much fun with their new friend. In the end, the toy was not just interesting, but also full of surprises.

Once upon a time, there was a clever cat named Tom who loved to run. He had four strong feet that helped him run very fast. One day, Tom saw a little bird in the tree. He wanted to talk to the bird, so he ran as fast as he could to the tree. "Hi, bird!" said Tom. "I love to run with my fast feet. Do you want to play with me?" The bird looked at Tom's feet and smiled. "I like your feet, Tom. But I can fly with my wings. Let's play a game. I will fly and you will run. Let's see who is faster." Tom and the bird started their race. The bird flew high in the sky, and Tom ran fast on the ground. They both loved to play this game. As they raced, Tom saw a big rock in front of him. He knew he had to be clever to win the race, so he jumped over the rock with his strong feet. In the end, Tom and the bird reached the finish line at the same time. They were both happy and laughed. "You are a clever cat, Tom," said the bird. "And your feet are very fast. I loved playing with you today." Tom smiled and said, "I loved playing with you too, bird. Let's be friends forever." And from that day on, Tom and the bird were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there were two siblings, Jack and Jill, who were very independent. They loved to explore the world and never needed anyone’s help. One day, they wanted to watch a TV program but could not find the remote control. Jack said to Jill, “Let’s try to find the remote and turn on the TV again.” Then, they started searching everywhere but still could not find it. Suddenly, Jill remembered something and said, “I know! Why don’t we try to turn on the TV without the remote?” Jack was surprised and said, “It is possible? Let’s try it out.” So, they went to the TV and worked together to turn on the TV. Finally, with their hard work, the TV turned on! Both of them were so happy that they smiled happily and hugged each other. The moral of the story is: Even if we are independent, it doesn’t hurt to ask for help from someone. Sometimes, two can accomplish a goal together more easily than just one alone.
