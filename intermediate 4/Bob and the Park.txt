Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob and the Park

Bob was a strong boy. He had lots of toys but his favorite was his skipping rope. One day he decided to go to the park to see if anyone wanted to play with him. On the way, Bob saw a big desk by the side of the street. He thought it must be really strong! When Bob arrived at the park, he saw some other children playing on the swings. He quickly joined them, skipping around the playground. As he skipped, his rope got caught in the desk he had seen earlier. He stopped to untangle it and noticed something shiny glinting in the sun. It was a big, shiny coin! Bob was so excited. He picked up the coin and quickly ran to a nearby shop to find out how much it was worth. The shopkeeper said it was a silver dollar and it was worth a lot of money. Bob was so happy, and he skipped all the way home. He couldn't wait to show his parents what he had found. He was sure that they would be so excited, and they'd be able to buy all the things they needed with the money from the coin.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful bird named Bobby. Bobby had many beautiful feathers. One day, Bobby met a little girl named Lily. "Hi, I am Lily. Can you help me?" asked Lily. Bobby nodded and said, "Yes, I can help you. What do you need?" Lily said, "I want to taste the yummy fruit on that tall tree, but I can't reach it." Bobby flew up and picked a big, red fruit for Lily. He gave it to her, and she took a big bite. "Thank you, Bobby! This fruit tastes so good!" said Lily with a happy smile. Bobby was happy too because he could help his new friend. From that day on, Bobby and Lily played together every day. They shared many tasty fruits and had lots of fun. And Bobby's helpful feathers always made their playtime even more special.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a small red ball and wanted to play with it. But when he picked up the ball, he found a tiny pill under it. Tim didn't know what the pill was, so he put it in his pocket. Tim went to sit on the grass and play with the ball. A girl named Sally came to play too. They had fun playing with the ball, but Tim forgot about the pill in his pocket. When they were tired, they sat down to rest. Tim remembered the pill and showed it to Sally. She didn't know what it was either. While they were looking at the pill, a small bird flew down and took it from Tim's hand. The bird flew away and they couldn't see it anymore. Tim and Sally were surprised and didn't know what to do. But they learned that they should not play with things they don't know about.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy little house in the woods. In the house lived a big bear and a little bear. They liked to keep their home nice and clean. One day, the big bear felt a weight on his back. "Oh no!" he said. "I have a weight on my back!" The little bear saw the big bear tremble. The little bear asked, "What's wrong, big bear?" The big bear replied, "I have a weight on my back, and it makes me tremble!" The little bear wanted to help the big bear. So, the little bear climbed on the big bear's back and pushed the weight off. The big bear was so happy! He stopped trembling and thanked the little bear. "Thank you, little bear, for helping me!" The two bears hugged and went back to keeping their tidy little house clean and happy.

One summer day, a gifted girl named Lily was walking by a lake. She saw a small bundle that seemed to be moving. When she got closer, she noticed it was a baby! She was so excited she scooped it up and hugged it tightly. Gently, she wrapped the baby in her scarf and walked to find its parents. Suddenly, she heard someone call out, “Give me back my baby!” It was a wicked old man who seemed to have come out of nowhere. Lily tried to explain that she was only trying to help the baby, but the man wouldn’t listen. He yelled at her and told her to go away. Lily knew something was wrong, so she tried to run away with the baby. But the man was too fast. He caught up with her and snatched the baby away. Lily was sad and helpless. She watched as he ran away towards the lake and disappeared. In the distance, Lily could hear the old man cackling. For Lily, the lake was now a reminder of her bad ending. She will never forget that day, when she wrapped the baby in her scarf hoping to help - only to have it snatched away by a wicked old man.
