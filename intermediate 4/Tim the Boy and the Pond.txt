Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very adventurous. He loved to play outside and explore. One day, he found a big rock. He thought it was special, so he put it in his pocket. Tim's mom told him that the rock might be dirty and not to put it in his pocket. But Tim insisted. He wanted to keep the rock with him always. So, he carried the rock in his pocket everywhere he went. One day, Tim was playing by a pond. He forgot about the rock in his pocket. He went to play near the water and slipped. The heavy rock in his pocket made it hard for him to get up. Tim's mom came running and helped him, but Tim's pocket got wet and ripped. The rock was lost forever. Tim was sad, but he learned to listen to his mom next time.

One day, an elderly man named Tom went for a jog. He liked to jog in the park every day. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. Tom felt happy when he jogged. In the park, Tom saw a police car. The police were looking for a lost dog. They asked Tom if he saw the dog. Tom said he did not see the dog, but he would help look for it. Tom jogged around the park and found the lost dog. He took the dog to the police. The police were happy, and the dog was happy, too. Tom felt good because he helped the police and the dog.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to create things with his hands. He made toys, pictures, and many other things. One hot day, Tim had an idea. He wanted to make a fan to cool himself down. Tim found some paper and sticks to make his fan. He worked very hard and soon, he had a beautiful fan. But, he wanted to make his fan special. He thought making it dangerous would be fun. So, he put sharp points on the edges of the fan. Tim went outside to show his friends his new fan. He said, "Look what I made! It is a dangerous fan!" His friends were scared and said, "That is not safe, Tim. Someone could get hurt." Tim didn't listen and started playing with his fan. But as he played, the fan cut his hand. Tim cried and his friends helped him. They told him, "You should not make dangerous things. It is better to create safe and fun things for everyone." Tim learned his lesson and promised to create only safe things from then on. And they all played happily together.

Once upon a time, there was a charming little boy called Joe. He wanted to go to the lake to have fun. He asked his mummy, "Mummy can I take my ball to the lake?" Mummy said, "Yes you can, but don't forget to be careful." So Joe went to the lake with his ball. When he arrived, he saw many animals and lots of trees. Joe was very happy. He walked to the edge of the lake and threw his ball in. He laughed and shouted, "Jump, ball, jump!" The ball jumped across the lake and Joe jumped up and down with excitement. He watched as the waves splashed against the lake and the wind blew through the trees. Joe knew he had found a special and charming lake!

One day, a little girl named Lily went to her room. She wanted to lay down on her bed. But first, she had to put a sheet on it. Lily found an amazing sheet with many colors. She was very happy. Lily's friend, Tim, came to play. "Wow!" said Tim, "That sheet is amazing!" They played on the bed with the colorful sheet. They had so much fun. They laughed and jumped on the bed. Then, something unexpected happened. The sheet started to move! The sheet wrapped around Lily and Tim, and it lifted them up. They flew around the room! "This is amazing!" said Lily. They laughed and had even more fun. The amazing sheet took them on a fun adventure.
