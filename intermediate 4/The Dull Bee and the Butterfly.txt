Category: Intermediate
Title: The Dull Bee and the Butterfly

Once upon a time, there was a dull bee. The bee lived in a big hive. The bee had many friends in the hive. They all worked hard to make honey. But the dull bee was not good at making honey. One day, the dull bee decided to go out and look for flowers. The bee flew far away from the hive. The bee saw a big flower and wanted to turn around. But something unexpected happened. The dull bee turned into a bright, colorful butterfly! The butterfly was so happy with the change. It flew back to the hive to show its friends. The other bees were surprised and could not believe their eyes. They all cheered for their friend who was once a dull bee but now a beautiful butterfly. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a white vehicle. This vehicle was very special because it could talk. One day, the white vehicle was driving around the town when it saw a little boy named Timmy. Timmy was trying to gather some pretty stones in the park, but he could not reach them all. The white vehicle saw this and wanted to help. So, it drove up to Timmy and said, "Hi, I am a white vehicle. I can help you gather the pretty stones." Timmy was amazed and said, "Wow, thank you, white vehicle!" Together, they gathered all the pretty stones in the park. Timmy was very happy and thanked the white vehicle again. From that day on, Timmy and the white vehicle became best friends. They played and gathered things together every day, and they lived happily ever after.

Lily and Max were playing in the yard. They liked to make piles of leaves and jump on them. Lily had a blue rake that she used to gather the leaves. Max had a red rake that he found in the shed. "Look, I made a big pile!" Lily said, pointing to her leaves. "It's bigger than yours!" "No, it's not!" Max said, looking at his pile. "Mine is bigger and better!" They ran to each other's piles and started to examine them. They counted the leaves and compared the colors and shapes. "See, I have more leaves!" Lily said. "And they are prettier!" "No, you don't!" Max said. "And they are not!" They began to argue and pull each other's leaves. They got angry and sad. "Stop it!" a voice said. It was Mom, who came out of the house. "What are you doing?" "Lily said her pile is bigger and better than mine!" Max said. "Max said his pile is bigger and better than mine!" Lily said. Mom sighed and shook her head. She walked to the piles and looked at them. "They are both nice piles," she said. "But they are not for fighting. They are for having fun. Why don't you share your rakes and make one big pile together?" Lily and Max thought about it. They looked at each other and nodded. "OK, Mom," they said. They gave each other their rakes and started to work together. They moved the leaves and made one big pile. They smiled and laughed. "Look, we made a huge pile!" Lily said. "It's the biggest and best pile ever!" Max said. They hugged each other and ran to the pile. They jumped on it and had fun. Mom watched them and smiled. She was happy.

One day, a cat named Tom went to the park to play. He saw a rail that he wanted to climb. But he was too small to reach it. He saw a big, tall dog named Max. Tom asked Max if he could borrow his back to get on the rail. Max said yes. Tom climbed on Max's back and then onto the rail. He was very happy. But then, a rude bird came and sat on the rail too. The bird started to push Tom off the rail. Tom was scared. Just when Tom thought he would fall, the unexpected happened. Max jumped up and caught Tom in his mouth. The rude bird flew away. Tom was safe, and he thanked Max for saving him. They became best friends and played together in the park every day.
