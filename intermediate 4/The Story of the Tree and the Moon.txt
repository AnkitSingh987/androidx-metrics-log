Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Tree and the Moon

One day a little boy wanted to go exploring. He was very curious and excited. He decided to climb the tall tree near his house. He was a bit scared, but also very brave. He looked up at the tree and began to climb. He went slowly and carefully, and soon he reached the top. From there, he could see for miles! From the top of the tree, the little boy could see a new land. It was full of trees and animals, and it looked so exciting! He wanted to explore the land, but he realized it was getting dark. He was feeling a bit sad, but then he noticed a big light in the sky. It suddenly became very bright, and illuminated the whole land. It was the moon! The little boy had been so serious about exploring, that he hadn’t noticed the moon had risen. He watched the beautiful moonlight and became very excited. Maybe tomorrow he could explore the land. He climbed back down the tree and went home, filled with anticipation and excitement.

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing in her room. She had a task to clean her toys, but she didn't want to do it. She wondered what would happen if she didn't finish her task. Lily's mom called her from the kitchen, "Lily, come here! I have a yummy treat for you!" Lily ran to the kitchen, excited for the treat. But her mom looked at her and said, "Did you finish your task?" Lily looked down and said, "No, I didn't clean my toys." Her mom said, "You need to finish your task before you can have the yummy treat." Lily felt sad, but she knew her mom was right. She went back to her room and cleaned her toys. When she was done, she ran to her mom and said, "I finished my task!" Her mom smiled and gave her a yummy cookie. Lily felt happy and proud that she finished her task.

One day, a pale boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big sign. The sign had a picture of a trumpet on it. Tim liked the trumpet. He wanted to play it. Tim went to his mom and dad. He asked them if he could play the trumpet. They said yes. They went to the store and bought a small trumpet for Tim. Tim played the trumpet every day. He was very happy. He played it at home, at the park, and even at school. Everyone liked to hear Tim play the trumpet.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a brave bunny named Bob. Bob loved to spring and play with his friends. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. One day, while Bob and his friends were playing, they found a piece of cake on the ground. They were very hungry and wanted to eat the cake. But they did not know who the cake belonged to. Bob, being the brave bunny, decided to find out who the cake belonged to. He asked all his friends if they knew, but no one did. So, he went on a journey and asked all the animals in the forest. Finally, he found a little girl who was looking for her lost cake. Bob gave the piece of cake back to the girl, and she was very happy. The little girl thanked Bob for being so brave and kind. From that day on, they became the best of friends.

One day, a big dark cloud came to the sky. The sun was hiding behind it. Many raindrops fell down from the cloud. The rain made a big wave in the river. The wave was fast and strong. Near the river, there were two friends, a dog and a cat. The dog said, "We need to unite to save our toys from the wave." The cat agreed, "Yes, let's work together." They both ran to get their toys. The dog and the cat picked up their toys and moved them away from the river. The wave came and went. Their toys were safe. The dark cloud went away, and the sun came back. The dog and the cat were happy they united to save their toys. They played together under the sun.
