Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and the Book

Sara likes to read books. She has many books with pictures and words. She likes to turn the pages and look at the pictures. Sometimes she reads the words too. One day, Sara finds a new book on the shelf. It is a big book with a green cover. She opens the book and sees a picture of a lion. The lion has a big mane and a long tail. Sara thinks the lion is beautiful. "Hello, lion," Sara says. "What is your name?" The lion looks at Sara and smiles. He says, "Hello, little girl. My name is Leo. I am a friendly lion. I live in the book. Do you want to be my friend?" Sara is surprised. She did not know that the lion can talk. She says, "Yes, I want to be your friend. You are very nice. Can I read your book?" Leo says, "Of course you can. This book is about me and my friends. We have many adventures in the jungle. Come, I will show you." Sara and Leo turn the pages together. They see pictures of elephants, monkeys, zebras, and giraffes. They read the words and learn new things. Sara and Leo have fun. They are happy to meet each other. They are happy to share the book. The book is very old. Some of the pages are dry and torn. Sara is careful not to break them. She says, "Leo, your book is very old. Do you want a new book?" Leo says, "No, I do not want a new book. This book is my home. I love this book. It has all my memories and stories. It is very special to me." Sara understands. She says, "I see. This book is very special to you. I will not hurt it. I will be gentle with it. I like this book too. It is very interesting and fun." Leo says, "Thank you, Sara. You are very kind and smart. I am glad you like my book. You are a good friend. I hope you will come back and visit me again." Sara says, "I will, Leo. I will come back and visit you again. You are a good friend too. I hope you will be happy in your book." Sara and Leo hug each other. They say goodbye. Sara closes the book and puts it back on the shelf. She smiles and thinks of Leo. She is happy to meet him. She is happy to read his book.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a chubby cat named Mimi. Mimi loved to eat sugar. One day, Mimi went to the park to play with her friends. At the park, Mimi met a big dog named Max. Max saw that Mimi had some sugar with her. Max said, "Can I have some sugar, please?" Mimi was happy to share and said, "Yes, you can have some. Let's part it in two." So, they both had some sugar to eat. After they ate the sugar, Mimi and Max played together all day. They ran, jumped, and laughed. They had so much fun. When it was time to go home, Mimi and Max said goodbye. They were now best friends and couldn't wait to play again tomorrow.

Once upon a time, there was a mommy and a little girl. The mommy was very thoughtful. She wanted to create something special for the little girl. So, she made a surprise dinner. The little girl was happy. She saw that the dinner was very nice. The mommy had made her favourite food and special desert. She was so happy with the nice surprise. The little girl thanked the mommy for being so thoughtful. She also said thank you for creating the special dinner. The little girl felt so lucky. She ate the dinner and enjoyed every bite. The mommy was happy to see the little girl enjoying her surprise dinner. After the dinner, the mommy gave the little girl a big hug. The little girl hugged her mommy back and thanked her again for the thoughtful dinner. The End.

One day, a charming cat named Tom went for a walk. As he walked, he saw a big box. Tom was very curious, so he decided to peek inside. Inside the box, he found a pretty toy. Tom liked the toy very much and wanted to take it home. As Tom was playing with the toy, a thief named Rob came by. Rob saw Tom with the toy and wanted it too. Rob said, "Give me the toy, it is mine!" Tom was scared and didn't know what to do. Then, something unexpected happened. A big dog named Max came running. Max saw the thief and started to bark very loud. Rob got scared and ran away. Tom was happy and thanked Max. Tom and Max became best friends and played with the toy together.
