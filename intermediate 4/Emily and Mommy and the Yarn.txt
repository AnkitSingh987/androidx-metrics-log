Category: Intermediate
Title: Emily and Mommy and the Yarn

One day, Emily and Mommy were playing together. Emily wanted to do something special, so Mommy said they could tie some yarn. Emily was very excited! Mommy told her that tying yarn was an important task. Emily was determined to do it all by herself. Mommy asked Emily if she wanted help. She said "no," and got to work. Emily grabbed the two pieces of yarn, held them tight, and started to twirl them together. Then, she tugged and pulled until it was all tied up. Mommy was so proud of Emily. She smiled and said, "You did it! That was important work!" Emily couldn't believe it. She had tied her own yarn!

Once upon a time, there was a dark night. In that night, there was a helicopter flying in the sky. It was very noisy. Suddenly, it went lower and lower until it stopped in the middle of the night. The people in the helicopter started to poke the sky with their hands. Everyone was very surprised by that. What did they mean by poking the sky? No one knew. Everyone kept looking at the sky and the helicopter kept flying high and low. After some time, the helicopter moved away and everything was quiet again. The mystery of that night was never solved. But everyone knew that the helicopter came to poke the sky for some special reason.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in the mud. One day, he made a big mess. His hands were dirty, and his clothes were dirty too. Tim's mom saw the mess and said, "Tim, you need to wash!" Tim did not like to wash. He wanted to keep playing. But his mom said, "No more play until you are clean." So, Tim went to the bathroom. He turned on the water and made a motion with his hands. The soap and water made bubbles. Tim liked the bubbles. When Tim was all clean, he went back to his mom. She smiled and gave him a big hug. Then, she gave him a delicious snack. Tim was happy that he washed. Now, he could play again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to watch his favorite show on the screen. One day, the screen went bad and Tim could not see his show. Tim asked his mom, "Please, can you fix the screen?" His mom tried to fix it, but it was still bad. Tim felt sad. Then, Tim had an idea. He asked his dad, "Please, can you help fix the screen?" His dad tried and it worked! The screen was not bad anymore. Tim was happy and said, "Thank you!" Now, he could watch his favorite show again.

Mama and Papa were getting ready for a trip. They filled their big luggage with all their clothes, books and food. "Let's create something new for this trip," Mama said. Papa looked around, grabbed some paper and scissors and said, "Yes! Let's make something special." Mama and Papa then began to create a fierce lion out of the paper. They cut and snipped, drew and colored. After an hour of hard work, the fierce lion was done! Mama and Papa placed the fierce lion in their luggage and smiled. "Now we are ready for the trip," Papa said. Mama nodded and said, "Yes, with our fierce lion in our luggage, let's go!"

Tom and Jen are twins. They like to play with toys and watch TV. But they do not like to share. They always fight over who gets what. One day, Mom buys a new TV for the living room. It is very big and tall. Tom and Jen are happy. They want to watch their favorite shows. But they do not agree on what to watch. Tom likes cars and trucks. Jen likes dolls and animals. They start to yell at each other. "I want to watch cars!" Tom says. "No, I want to watch dolls!" Jen says. They pull the remote from each other. They tug and tug. Then, snap! The remote splits in two. The TV goes off. Tom and Jen are shocked. They look at the broken remote. They look at the dark TV. They look at each other. They start to cry. They are sorry. They did not mean to break the remote. They did not mean to fight. Mom hears them crying. She comes to the living room. She sees the broken remote and the dark TV. She is not happy. "What happened here?" Mom asks. Tom and Jen tell her what happened. They say they are sorry. They hug Mom. Mom hugs them back. She is not angry. She is glad they are sorry. She says they can watch TV again, but they have to share. They have to take turns. They have to be nice. Tom and Jen nod. They agree. They say they will share. They say they will be nice. Mom smiles. She fixes the remote with tape. She turns on the TV. She lets Tom and Jen watch their favorite shows. They take turns. They are nice. They are happy.
