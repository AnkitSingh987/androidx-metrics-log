Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Car

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy that he loved very much. The toy was a small car. Tim played with the car every day. One day, Tim saw a girl named Sue. Sue did not have a toy to play with. Tim wanted to be generous, so he said, "Sue, you can play with my toy car." Sue was happy and said, "Thank you, Tim!" But then, Sue played with the toy car too hard. The toy car broke into pieces. Tim was sad and said, "My toy car is broken." Sue said, "I am sorry, Tim." Now, Tim did not have a toy car to play with anymore.

Once upon a time there was a Baby Bird. She was the cutest bird in the nest. One day, she was flying around looking for something special. Suddenly a big wind came and she nearly got pinched by a giant claw! She was very scared. The big wind brought her to a big tree with a big nest in it. Baby Bird was delighted and hopped right into the nest. She felt so safe and snug in the warm nest. Then, an owl came into the tree. Baby Bird was very scared and hid in the corner of the nest. The owl swooped closer and closer with his big wings and big eyes. But he just said "Hello little one, how are you today?". Baby Bird was so relieved and replied "I'm normal. Thank you for asking!". The owl smiled, nodded and flew away. Baby Bird was now safe from any more pinches from the giant claw!

Once upon a time, two best friends were walking along a street together. They were so excited to go on an adventure. Suddenly, they arrived at a big park. It was filled with strips of colourful grass and lots of fun things to do. They were overwhelmed by all the creative possibilities. "What should we do first?" asked one of the friends. The other friend took a look around and said, "Let's jump on the strips and play a game! It'll be so much fun!" So, they both ran to the strips, laughing and playing all day long. They had so many adventures and got to use their imaginations in so many creative ways. By the end of the day, they were exhausted but so happy to have arrived at such an amazing place.

One day, a lively dog named Spot was playing in the yard. He liked to run and jump all day long. Spot saw a box with a lot of things inside. The box included a ball, a toy, and a razor. Spot's friend, Tom the cat, came over and said, "Let's play with the ball!" Spot agreed and they played with the ball for a while. Then Tom said, "What is that other thing in the box?" Spot replied, "I don't know, let's find out!" When they tried to play with the razor, it made a loud noise and scared them. They did not know what it was for. Just then, Spot's mom came out and said, "That is not a toy, it is for grown-ups only!" She put the razor away and gave them a new toy to play with. Spot and Tom were happy to play with the new toy and knew not to play with the razor again.

Once upon a time there was a silly old bear named Bob. He was very anxious about the world around him. One day he was walking around and he saw a little rabbit. Bob said to the rabbit, "Hi there, little bunny! How are you today?". The rabbit furrowed its brow and replied, "I'm a bit anxious. Why are you so anxious, Mr. Bear?". Bob was a bit taken aback and he scratched his ear thoughtfully. Then, he told the rabbit that he was anxious because he felt like the world was changing too quickly. The rabbit felt sympathetic and replied, "It's ok to be anxious sometimes, Mr. Bear. We all feel it from time to time." Bob was moved by the rabbit's words and felt grateful for their kindness. With a big smile, Bob said, "Thank you, little bunny. I feel a bit better now." The rabbit smiled back and said, "Don't worry, Mr. Bear. You'll be ok." And with that, Bob and the rabbit said goodbye and went on their separate ways.
