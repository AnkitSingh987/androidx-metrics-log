Category: Intermediate
Title: The Dog of Tim the Boy

One day, a boy named Tim went for a jog in the park. He liked to run fast and feel the wind on his head. The park was big and had many trees and flowers. Tim was happy when he was in the park. As Tim was jogging, he saw a big, scary dog. The dog looked at Tim and said, "Why are you running in my park?" Tim was surprised that the dog could talk. He said, "I like to run and play here. Can we be friends?" The scary dog thought for a moment and said, "Okay, we can be friends. But you must be kind to everyone in the park." Tim agreed, and they played together all day. The scary dog wasn't scary anymore, and they became best friends. The unexpected thing was that the scary dog could talk. The moral of the story is to be kind to everyone, even if they look scary.

One day, a big cow with wide horns was in a field. The cow saw a little boy. The boy was near a tree. The tree had a red ball stuck in it. The boy was sad. The cow said, "Why are you sad?" The boy said, "My red ball is stuck in the tree." The cow wanted to help. The cow had an idea. The cow said, "I will use my wide horns to get your ball." The cow tried to pull the red ball out with its wide horns. But something unexpected happened. The tree started to shake. All the leaves fell down. The boy and the cow were surprised. Then, the red ball fell down too! The boy was very happy. The cow was happy too. The boy said, "Thank you, cow!" The cow and the boy became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy dog named Spot. Spot loved to wander and play outside. One day, he saw a big aeroplane in the sky. Spot was very excited and wanted to see the aeroplane up close. He wandered far away from his home to find the aeroplane. He saw the aeroplane land in a big field. Spot was so happy to see the aeroplane. He played near it and had lots of fun. At the end of the day, Spot wandered back home, tired but happy.

Once upon a time, there was a tiny caterpillar. He was very hungry. He wanted to find food to eat. He saw a huge leaf on a big tree. The caterpillar thought the leaf looked yummy. The caterpillar climbed up the tree to get to the huge leaf. He was so excited to eat it. When he reached the leaf, he started to chew on it. The leaf was tasty and made him happy. The caterpillar chewed and chewed on the huge leaf. Soon, the leaf was all gone. The caterpillar was full and not hungry anymore. He was very tired from all the chewing. So, he found a cozy spot on the tree and took a long nap.

James and Mary were best friends. Mary loved to share her toys but James was always too shy to share his. One day, Mary wanted to show James her newest toy but he was too embarrassed to look. She said, "Hey James, welcome to my house. Would you like to see my toy?" James hesitated, feeling helpless. Finally he replied, "Yes, maybe." Mary was excited and with a big smile, she spread out all of her toys on the floor. James was amazed! He chose one of her cars and she said, "You can share it with me!" James couldn't believe it. He had never shared something with a friend before. He smiled and said, "Thank you, Mary!" Mary was so happy and said, "Welcome to my house, James!" and they played together for hours. From that day on, James always shared his toys with Mary.

One day, a gray cat named Tom went out to play. He saw a shiny red ball in the park. Tom wanted the ball, so he decided to steal it. He looked around to make sure no one was watching. Then, he took the ball and ran away. At noon, Tom was playing with the red ball when a little girl named Lily came up to him. "Hey, that's my ball!" she said. Tom felt bad for stealing the ball, so he gave it back to Lily. They started to play together and became friends. As they played, a big dog came up to them. The dog wanted to steal the ball too! But instead of running away, the dog asked if he could play with them. Tom, Lily, and the dog played together all day. They learned that it is better to share and make friends than to steal.
