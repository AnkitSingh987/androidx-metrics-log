Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam the Sheep and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a sheep named Sam. Sam was a different sheep because he had blue wool instead of white. Sam lived on a farm with many other sheep. They all played and ate grass together. One day, Sam saw a little bird with a broken wing. The bird could not fly. Sam wanted to help the bird. He said, "Don't worry, little bird. I will support you." Sam let the bird sit on his back, so she could rest her broken wing. The other sheep saw Sam and the bird. They said, "Sam, you are a good friend." Sam smiled and knew that even though he was different, he could still help others. The bird got better, and they all played together on the farm.

One day, a cat named Tom was very tired. He wanted to relax under a big tree. The tree was in the park where all the kids played. Tom liked the park because it was not boring. While Tom was walking to the park, he found a paper on the ground. It was a license! Tom did not know what a license was, but he thought it was special. He carried the license in his mouth to the park. When Tom got to the park, he showed the license to his friends. They did not know what it was either. Suddenly, a big wind came and blew the license away! Tom and his friends chased after it. The wind stopped and the license landed on the ground. Tom picked it up and saw that it changed! Now, it was a map to a fun new park. Tom and his friends followed the map and found the new park. They played all day and were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little red bird. The bird lived in a big tree near a pond. The bird loved to fly and play all day. One day, the bird saw a frog in the mud. The frog was waving to the bird. The bird flew down to the frog and said, "Hi, frog! Do you want to play with me?" The frog said, "Yes, bird! Let's play in the mud!" The bird and the frog played in the mud all day. They waved to their friends who came to join them. The sun was shining, and they all had a fun day playing in the mud together.

Once upon a time there was a mommy and a daddy. They had a happy home, with a backyard full of nice things to do. One day, a baby arrived. The family was so very happy and the baby was very healthy. Everyone noticed that the backyard became a big part of their life. Mommy and Daddy took the baby out to play everyday, and the baby loved to crawl around and explore. Every day the baby was getting stronger and stronger. One day Mommy and Daddy told the baby it was important to be strong and healthy to stay safe, and that was why they were always happy to see the baby out in the backyard. The moral of the story is that it is important to be healthy in order to stay safe and have a happy home.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a special cup. The cup was very useful. Lily took the cup everywhere she went. One day, Lily met a tiny bird. The bird was thirsty. "Help me, please," said the bird. Lily wanted to help the bird. She filled her cup with water from a small puddle. The bird drank the water. "Thank you," said the bird. Lily had an idea. She would teach the bird to use the cup. She showed the bird how to hold the cup with its beak. The bird learned fast. Now, the bird could drink water from the cup all by itself. Lily and the bird became good friends. They played together every day, and the bird always had water to drink from the useful cup.

One day, a boy named Tom went to the sea with his dad. They went on a big boat called a yacht. Tom's dad was a good and reliable captain. Tom loved to help his dad on the yacht. They would cover the yacht with a big cloth when it rained. As they sailed, they saw a big fish jump out of the water. "Wow, dad! Did you see that?" Tom shouted. His dad smiled and said, "Yes, that was a big fish! Let's try to find more." They sailed on, looking for more big fish. Suddenly, the sky turned dark and it started to rain. Tom and his dad quickly covered the yacht with the big cloth. They waited for the rain to stop. When the rain stopped, they uncovered the yacht and saw something unexpected. A little bird was sitting on the yacht with them! The bird looked wet and cold. Tom and his dad decided to help the bird. They gave it some food and made a warm place for it to rest. The little bird became their friend and stayed with them on the yacht. Tom and his dad were happy to have a new friend on their sailing adventure. They knew that they would always remember the day they found the little bird on their yacht.
