Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam the Flexible Snake and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a flexible snake named Sam. Sam loved to eat berries. One day, Sam went to find some yummy berries to eat. He slithered and twisted through the trees and bushes. While looking for berries, Sam met a big bear named Ben. Ben also loved to eat berries. They saw a big berry bush with lots of sweet berries on it. Sam and Ben both wanted to eat all the berries. They did not want to share. Sam and Ben started to fight over the berries. They pushed and pulled, trying to get to the berry bush. But then, they heard a little voice. It was a tiny bird named Tina. Tina said, "Please stop fighting! You can both have some berries if you share." Sam and Ben looked at each other and felt bad. They knew Tina was right. They decided to share the berries and became friends. From that day on, Sam, Ben, and Tina all ate berries together and played in the big forest. They were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, red rubber ball. He loved to play with it every day. It was easy to bounce and catch. Tim thought his ball was the best. One day, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue had a pretty, blue rubber ball. Sue said, "I like your ball, Tim. Do you want to play with mine?" Tim said, "Yes, I like your ball too, Sue. Let's play together!" Tim and Sue played with their rubber balls all day. They learned the value of sharing and being friends. They knew that playing together was more fun than playing alone. From that day on, Tim and Sue were the best of friends, and they always played with their rubber balls together.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a big flood. The water was everywhere, and it was blue. The people in the village were scared. They needed help to get away from the flood. One day, a kind man came to the village. He saw the blue flood and knew he had to do something. He had a big boat, and he wanted to catch everyone in the village and take them to a safe place. So, he started rowing his boat in the flood, catching the people one by one. As the kind man rowed his boat, he saw a little girl standing on a roof. The girl was scared and crying. The man rowed closer to her and said, "Don't worry, little girl. I will catch you and take you to a safe place." The little girl smiled and said, "Thank you, kind man." The man caught the girl and took her into his boat. The kind man rowed his boat and caught more people. Soon, the boat was full of people from the village. They all thanked the kind man for saving them from the blue flood. The man took them to a safe place, far away from the water. After the flood was gone, the village people were happy again. They all thanked the kind man for catching them and taking them to a safe place. They lived happily ever after, and they never forgot the man who saved them from the blue flood.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved balloons more than anything else. One day, he saw a big, red balloon in the store. Tim asked his mom to get it for him. "Please, can I have the balloon?" he requested. His mom said yes and bought the balloon for Tim. He was so happy! Tim played with the balloon all day, running and spinning around with it. He felt dizzy from all the spinning, but he didn't mind because he loved the balloon so much. But one day, Tim was playing with the balloon outside and it started to rain. The wind was very strong, and it blew the balloon away from Tim's hands. He tried to catch it, but he was too dizzy to run fast. The balloon flew far away, and Tim could not find it. He was very sad and cried all the way home.
