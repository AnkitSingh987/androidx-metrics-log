Category: Intermediate
Title: The Modest Theater and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a modest theater. In this theater, there were many fun plays. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the theater with his mom. Before the play started, Tim saw a big, red ball on the stage. He wanted to play with it. So, he ran up to the stage and said, "Can I play with the ball?" A nice lady on the stage said, "Yes, you can play with the ball, but you must run fast and bring it back before the play starts." Tim ran fast, grabbed the ball, and had fun playing with it. He ran back to the stage and gave the ball to the nice lady. Then, he sat with his mom and watched the play. Tim was happy, and he loved the modest theater.

One day, a mild cat named Sam found a big sack. Sam was very happy. He wanted to show his friend Tim the sack. Tim was a small dog. Sam went to Tim's house with the sack. Sam said to Tim, "Look, I found a big sack! Let's play with it." Tim was excited. They both played with the sack. They put toys in the sack and took them out. They had fun. Then, Sam found a long stick. He said to Tim, "Let's play with the stick too!" Tim liked the idea. They took turns holding the stick and hitting the sack. They laughed and played all day with the sack and the stick.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She loved to play in the winter snow. One day, Lily saw her friend Tom with a big red ball. Lily felt jealous because she wanted a big red ball too. Lily went home and told her mom, "I want a big red ball like Tom." Her mom said, "We can mail a letter to your grandma and ask her to send you one." Lily and her mom wrote a letter and put it in the mailbox. They waited for a few days. One day, the mailman came and gave Lily a big box. She opened it and found a big red ball inside! Lily was so happy. Now, Lily and Tom could play together with their big red balls in the winter snow. They were both happy and no one was jealous anymore. They played all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Max. Max loved to smile and play with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. They played together every day in the big yard. One day, Max and Tim found a secret chamber in their house. The chamber was small and had many toys. They were so happy to find this new place to play in. Max and Tim spent hours playing in the secret chamber. They smiled and laughed as they shared their toys. They knew they would always be best friends and have fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Amy. Amy liked to read books. She had a network of friends who also liked to read. They would sit under a big tree and read books together. The tree had many delicate leaves that would dance in the wind. One day, Amy and her friends found a new book. The book was big and had many colors. They sat under the tree to read the new book. As they read, they heard a sound. It was a sound they never heard before. The sound came from the big tree. The tree started to shake. The delicate leaves fell down. Then, the tree talked! It said, "Hello, friends!" Amy and her friends were very surprised. The tree could read the book with them! Now they had a new friend in their network. And they all read books together, under the talking tree.

Mandy, a 3 year old, was walking by herself in the park. All around her, the trees had leaves of different colors. This made the park look so alive and wonderful! Mandy came upon a mysterious gray box. On the box was a bright lock. Out of curiosity, she reached out to unlock the box. To her surprise, she succeeded! After the box was unlocked, she opened the lid and found an exciting new game. Mandy was so happy to discover the new game. She held it close to her chest and ran home to show it to her family. When she got home, she exclaimed to her mom, "Look what I found! Can we play together?" Her mom smiled and said, "Oh Mandy, what a wonderful game! Let's play!" Mandy happily accepted the offer and the two of them enjoyed a wonderful afternoon playing the game together.
