Title: Tim the Boy and the Puddle

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play in the mud. One day, Tim saw a big puddle. He jumped in it and got very filthy. Tim loved how the mud squished between his toes. Tim wanted to play in the puddle again. So, he took a step back and jumped in the puddle. He did this over and over. Tim would step and jump, step and jump. He liked to repeat this game. Soon, it was time to go home. Tim's mom saw how filthy he was. She said, "Tim, you need a bath!" Tim smiled and nodded. He had so much fun playing in the puddle. Tim knew he would play in the mud again soon.

Once upon a time there was a boy who had a camera. He wanted to take pictures of his friends, so he was very excited whenever he opened the camera. One day, he asked his mom for help to open the camera but his mom said she was busy. The boy had to open it by himself. He struggled a little bit but he eventually managed to open it. The next day, his mom brought a new camera for him. It was a very reliable camera and it was easy to open. The boy was so happy! He immediately showed it to his friends and they were all very excited to take pictures with the camera. From that day onwards, the boy learnt an important lesson that he should always take the time to find reliable things to make his life easier. He values the things he can open easily, such as the camera, and he takes his time to find reliable things.

Molly was playing with her stuffed bear and she wanted to open the door to the garden. She was only 3 but she had a plan! She put her bear down, went over to the door and grabbed the knob. She twisted and pulled but nothing happened. Suddenly her Mom came running into the room. "What are you trying to do?" she asked. Molly said “I want to go into the garden. I can't open the door, it won't open!” Molly's Mom said "Let me try". She grabbed the knob and twisted it. But just like before, nothing happened. "That's weird," she exclaimed. Just then, Molly's Dad came in. "What's going on in here?" he asked. Molly's Mom said "Molly wanted to go in the garden but the door won't open!" Molly's Dad said "Let me take a look". He grabbed the knob and twisted, but the door still wouldn't open. He said, "Hmm, this is strange, I think the knob is stuck." He got his tools and soon he was unscrewing the knob. Then he said, "Ah, there it is, looks like something got stuck in the knob and it wouldn't turn". Molly was so relieved and she finally got to go into the garden. She had a great day playing in the garden

Once upon a time, there was a big, noisy dog named Max. Max loved to play with his friends in the park. One day, Max saw a little girl with a box of matches. She was trying to stir them in a bowl. Max knew that matches were not for playing. Max wanted to help the little girl learn the right way to play. He ran to her and took the matches away. The little girl was sad, but Max showed her how to play with a ball instead. They had lots of fun together. At the end of the day, the little girl's mom came to pick her up. She saw Max and the little girl playing and was very happy. She thanked Max for teaching her daughter the right way to play. The moral of the story is that we should always help others learn the right way to do things, even if it means taking away something they like.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to read. She read books all day. One day, while reading a book, she heard a noise. It made her feel fearful. Sue looked around and saw a big cat. The cat had a big head. Sue asked the cat, "Why is your head so big?" The cat said, "I like to read too. I read a lot of books and my head grew big." Sue was not fearful anymore. She said, "Let's read books together!" The cat agreed, and they read books all day. Sue and the big head cat became good friends. They were happy and not fearful anymore.
