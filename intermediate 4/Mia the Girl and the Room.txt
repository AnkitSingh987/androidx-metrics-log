Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Room

One day, a little girl named Mia wanted to decorate her room. She found a long strip of paper with many colors. She was very happy and called her mom. "Mom, can I use this strip to make my room pretty?" Mia asked. Her mom said, "Yes, you can, Mia. Let's make your room look nice together." Mia and her mom started to decorate the room. They put the strip on the wall. It made the room look very pretty. The sky was clear outside, and the sun made the colors shine bright. Mia's friend, Tim, came to play. He saw the strip on the wall and said, "Wow, Mia! Your room is so pretty!" Mia smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim! My mom and I made it pretty together." They played all day in Mia's room. They had so much fun with the pretty strip on the wall. Mia was happy that her room was now nice and bright.

One day, a boy named Tim and his friend, Sue, were playing with blocks. They were making a big tower. Tim was very serious about making the tower tall and strong. Sue was also serious and wanted the tower to be the best one ever. As they put more and more blocks on the tower, it started to shake. Tim and Sue looked at each other and shouted, "Oh no! Be careful!" They didn't want their tower to fall down. They worked together to make it strong. Just then, their mom came into the room and saw the tower. She said, "Wow, that's a big tower! Good job, Tim and Sue!" Tim and Sue smiled and shouted, "Thank you, Mom!" They were very proud of their tower and happy that they worked together.

Once upon a time, there was a unique little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside with her friends. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. One day, Lily found a big, red ball in her backyard. She was very excited to play with it. Lily and her friends decided to play a game with the big, red ball. They would take turns kicking the ball as hard as they could. When they kicked the ball, it would make a lot of dust fly up into the air. The dust tickled their noses and made them sneeze. As they played, Lily saw a small bug trapped in a spider web. She felt sad for the bug and wanted to help it. Lily carefully picked up the bug and released it from the web. The bug was now free and flew away happily. Lily felt very proud and happy that she could help the bug. She went back to playing with her friends and the big, red ball, having a fun day full of laughter and joy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He did not like to eat avocado. Every day, he would complain when his mom gave him avocado. His mom would say, "But it is very rich and good for you!" Tim would still not eat it. One day, Tim was walking in the park. He saw a big, green ball on the ground. He picked it up and started to play with it. He loved his new green ball. He played with it all day long. At dinner, Tim's mom gave him avocado again. This time, he looked at the avocado and thought of his green ball. He decided to try the avocado. To his surprise, he liked it! It was just as fun to eat as his green ball was to play with. From that day on, Tim never complained about avocado again.

Once, there was a polite goose. One day, he decided to peek around the pond to see what he could find. As he looked around he saw a carrot in the far corner of the pond. He quickly hopped over to said it. He was about to take a bite when he realized that he wasn't alone. He turned around and saw a small frog looking back at him. The frog said, “Hello! That carrot looks delicious! Please, can I have some?” The goose, being very polite, said, “Yes, of course, take some. I don't mind sharing.” The frog thanked the goose and the two became friends. They decided to share the carrot and chat about the pond for the rest of the day.
