Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toy phone. One day, she went to the park with her mom. At the park, she saw a boy named Tim who was upset. Lily wanted to help him feel better. Lily went to Tim and said, "Hi, I'm Lily. Do you want to play with me and my phone?" Tim looked at her and said, "Yes, I want to play. I'm upset because I lost my ball." As they played with the toy phone, something unexpected happened. A big dog came running towards them. The dog had Tim's ball in its mouth! The dog dropped the ball and wagged its tail. Tim was so happy to see his ball again. Lily and Tim played with the dog and the toy phone. They became good friends and were not upset anymore. From that day on, they always met at the park to play together with the dog and the toy phone. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big red dinosaur. The dinosaur lived in a green forest. He had many friends like birds, rabbits, and squirrels. But he was the only dinosaur in the forest. One day, the red dinosaur met a little girl named Lily. Lily was very good at creating things. She could make animals out of clay, and she could draw pictures of flowers and trees. The red dinosaur asked Lily, "Can you create a friend for me? I want to play with another dinosaur." Lily thought for a moment and then said, "Yes, I can create a friend for you!" She took some clay and made a small blue dinosaur. The red dinosaur was so happy! He now had a new friend to play with. They played all day long, and all the animals in the forest joined them. From that day on, the red dinosaur and his blue friend had lots of fun together in the green forest.

One day, a silly cat named Tom went to the park. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Tom saw a bird up in the tree and thought, "I want to play with the bird!" Tom said to the bird, "Hello, bird! Can you come down and play with me?" The bird looked at Tom and said, "I will come down, but only if you bring me a sandwich." Tom was happy and ran to get a sandwich for the bird. Tom found a sandwich and went back to the tree. He gave the sandwich to the bird, and they started to play. They played all day long, and when they were done, they both clapped their hands (and wings) in joy. Tom and the bird became very good friends and had many more fun days at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little chubby cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with her ball. One day, the ball rolled far away and went behind a post. Kitty asked her friend, Birdie, "Can you help me get my ball back?" Birdie said, "Yes, I can help you." So, Birdie flew behind the post and got the ball for Kitty. After they returned the ball, Kitty and Birdie played together. They had a lot of fun. Then, they sat under a tree and shared a yummy snack. They were very happy and became best friends.

Tim and Lily liked to play in the park. They liked to swing, slide and run. But they did not like to listen to Mom. Mom told them to stay near her and not to go to the wild part of the park. The wild part had many trees and bushes and unknown things. One day, Tim and Lily saw a big butterfly. It was yellow and blue and very pretty. They wanted to catch it. They ran after it and forgot about Mom. They followed the butterfly to the wild part of the park. They did not see Mom calling them. In the wild part, they saw many things. They saw a bird, a squirrel, a frog and a snake. They were scared of the snake. It was black and long and hissed at them. They screamed and ran away. They got lost and did not know how to go back. Mom heard them scream and ran to find them. She was very worried and angry. She found them crying and hugged them. She was happy they were safe, but she was also mad. She said they were very naughty and did not listen to her. She said they had to punish them. She said they had to go home and not play in the park for a week. Tim and Lily were very sorry and said they would never do it again. They learned their lesson and never went to the wild part of the park again.
