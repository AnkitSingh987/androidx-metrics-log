Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Leaf and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a big open field, there was a small tree with a big green palm leaf. The leaf was very happy because it could dance in the wind and play with the sun. One day, a little bird came to sit on the palm leaf. The bird wanted to be friends with the leaf, but the leaf decided to ignore the bird. The leaf thought it was too busy dancing and playing to make friends. The bird felt sad and flew away. The next day, the bird came back with a friend. They both sat on the palm leaf and sang a beautiful song. The leaf realized it was more fun to have friends than to ignore them. From that day on, the leaf, the bird, and the bird's friend played together in the big open field.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim wanted to help his mom at the market. Mom said, "Yes, you can help me, Tim." At the market, Tim saw a helpless kitten. The kitten was sad and hungry. Tim wanted to help the kitten. He gave the kitten some food. The kitten was happy. But then, a big dog came. The dog was mean. The dog took the food from the kitten. Tim tried to help, but he was too small. The dog ate the food and ran away. Tim and the kitten were sad. The end.

Once upon a time, there were two friends called Tom and Bill who loved exploring together. One day, they decided to go to the wood to find some pick. Tom was quite shy, so he stayed in one spot and watched as Bill jumped about looking for the pick. Suddenly, Tom noticed something sparkling beneath a bush and called out to Bill. Tom smiled and said, "I think I've found something! I'm going to try to match it up." Bill ran over to Tom and together, they carefully pulled away the leaves to uncover a beautiful pick. Tom grabbed it and shouted, "Bill, I'm so happy I found it! Now we can finish our adventure!" Bill jumped up and down in excitement, and the two friends had a special hug to celebrate Tom's find.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big, impressive tree. Every day, birds would receive food from the tree. They were very happy. One day, a new bird came to the town. He did not know the law of the tree. The law was that all birds must share the food. The new bird did not share. He took all the food for himself. The other birds were sad. They told the new bird about the law. The new bird said sorry and shared the food. Now, all the birds were happy again. They all received food from the impressive tree and followed the law.

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse. The mouse lived in a small, colorful house. The house was red, blue, and yellow. The mouse loved his house very much. One day, the mouse wanted to mail a letter. He wrote a letter to his friend, the cat. The letter said, "Hello, friend! I hope you are happy. Love, Mouse." The mouse put the letter in an envelope and walked to the mailbox. The mouse put the letter in the mailbox and went back to his colorful house. He waited for the cat's letter to come. Soon, the cat's letter came in the mail. The mouse was very happy to read the cat's letter. They stayed friends and wrote letters to each other all the time.

One day, a little girl named Amy wanted to read a book. She looked in her room and found a big box. It was full of books! Amy was very happy and ready to read. Amy picked a book about a big wreck. In the story, a car and a truck had a crash. They both were sad, but they said sorry and became friends. They helped each other get better. Amy liked the story a lot. She read it to her mom, dad, and little brother. They all liked the story too. They were all ready for bed and said good night. Amy put the book back in the box, and they all went to sleep.
