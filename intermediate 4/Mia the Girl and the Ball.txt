Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She liked to play outside in her yard. One sunny day, she was playing ball with her dog, Spot. While they were playing, the ball went into a bush. Mia went to get the ball out of the bush, but it was stuck. She pulled and pulled, but the ball would not come out. Mia tried to break the bush to get the ball. She pushed and pulled some more. Finally, the ball came out of the bush. Mia and Spot were very happy. They played with the ball all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy cat named Tom. Tom liked to wear a big red hat. He lived in a small house with his friend, a little girl named Sue. One day, Sue said, "Tom, let's play a game. I will hide, and you try to find me." Tom agreed and started to look for Sue. While he was looking, a big wind came and blew his hat away. Tom was sad and worried. He knew he had to find his hat before it could escape. Tom looked high and low for his hat. He asked a bird, "Have you seen my hat?" The bird said, "Yes, I saw it fly away with the wind." Tom kept looking and finally found his hat stuck in a tree. But when he climbed up to get it, he found Sue hiding in the tree too! Tom was surprised and laughed, "Sue, I found you and my hat!" Sue giggled and said, "I hid in the tree to make sure your hat didn't escape." They both laughed and went back to their small house, happy to have found each other and the hat. From that day on, Tom and Sue always played together and made sure to keep their things tidy. They knew that if they worked together, they could always find what they were looking for.

Once upon a time, there was a queen. She was a nice and harmless queen. She liked to play games with her people. One day, she wanted to play a new game called "match". The queen asked her people to find things that match. They looked around and found two red balls. "Look, queen! These balls match!" they said. The queen was happy and clapped her hands. Next, they found two big, green trees. "Look, queen! These trees match!" they said. The queen smiled and nodded. She loved her new game, and everyone had fun finding things that match.

Mama and Papa were poor, but they had a baby. The baby was so happy and loved to stir something in her hands. Mama had a special pillow for the baby. Every day Mama gave the pillow a big hug and said she loved it. One day Mama and Papa had to go away. Before they left, Mama tucked the baby into bed and put the pillow close to the baby's heart. She then said goodbye and left. The baby was sad and alone. He started to stir the pillow in her hands. But then something bad happened. The pillow fell apart and the stuffing flew all over the room. The baby started to cry but it was too late. Mama and Papa could not help her anymore. The baby was very sad and poor without her special pillow. She would never be happy again.

Once upon a time, there lived a 3-year-old girl named Chloe. She lived next to a park and she loved playing there during her free time. One day, Chloe went out for a picnic with her parents. As they were walking around the park, she saw a long tunnel. Chloe was scared when she saw the darkness inside the tunnel. She started to blink as she was too scared to step in. Suddenly, an old man standing by the entrance of the tunnel, said to Chloe, “We all must face our fears and we can do that by taking small steps." Chloe gradually took small steps and walked inside the tunnel. She was relieved to find that there was nothing to be scared of. When she came out of the tunnel, Chloe smiled and thanked the old man. She realized that it is important to face our fears and take small measures to achieve our goals. The moral of the story is that we should not be afraid to face our fears and always take small steps to achieve our goals.
