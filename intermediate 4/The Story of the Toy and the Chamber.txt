Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Toy and the Chamber

Once upon a time, there was a bright room called the chamber. In this chamber, there was a big, round toy that could spin. Many kids would come to play with the spinning toy. One day, a little girl named Sue came to the chamber to play. She saw the spinning toy and thought it was fun. Sue pushed the toy, and it started to spin fast. She laughed and clapped her hands. Sue played with the spinning toy all day. She made friends with other kids who came to the chamber too. They all had fun spinning the toy together. At the end of the day, Sue went home with a big smile on her face.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. He loved to travel with his interesting flute. One day, he went to a big forest. In the forest, he met a girl named Sue. Sue was sad. "Why are you sad?" Tom asked. "I lost my toy," Sue said. "I can't find it." Tom wanted to help Sue. He played his interesting flute. The music was so nice. All the animals in the forest came to listen. A squirrel heard the music and saw Sue's toy. The squirrel picked up the toy and gave it to Sue. "Thank you, Tom," Sue said with a big smile. "You and your flute are very interesting." From that day on, Tom and Sue became best friends. They traveled together and played with their toys in the big forest. And whenever someone was sad, Tom would play his interesting flute to make them happy.

One day, a little fox went to visit his friend, the big bird. The big bird lived in a high tree. The little fox looked up and saw the big bird in her nest. "Hi, big bird! Can I come up?" asked the little fox. The big bird flew down and said, "Yes, little fox! Let me help you." The big bird picked up the little fox with her beak and took him up to the high tree. They played all day in the high tree, and the little fox was very happy. When it was time to go home, the big bird helped the little fox get down from the high tree. The little fox said, "Thank you, big bird! I had fun visiting you today!" The big bird smiled and said, "You're welcome, little fox! Come visit me again soon!"

Once upon a time, there was a boring garden. In this garden, there were many flowers that did not bloom. One day, a girl named Lily found a magic powder. She thought, "Maybe this powder will help the flowers bloom!" Lily put the powder on the flowers in the boring garden. She waited and waited. Then, something unexpected happened. The flowers started to bloom, but they were not normal flowers. They were big and colorful, and they could talk! The flowers thanked Lily for helping them bloom. They were not boring anymore. They were happy and full of life. The garden became a fun place for Lily to play and make new friends. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a huge farm. On the farm, there were many vegetables. Tom, the farmer, took care of them every day. He loved his vegetables very much. One day, Tom saw that some vegetables were missing. He was very sad. Tom wanted to replace the missing vegetables. He went to the store and bought more seeds. He planted the seeds in the ground and waited for them to grow. But Tom did not learn his lesson. He did not watch his vegetables carefully. The animals came and ate the new vegetables too. Tom was very sad again. The farm did not have many vegetables left. The moral of the story is to always watch and protect what is important to you.

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big ball with spikes. It is green and yellow. Sara wants to touch it. "Look, Ben, a spiky ball!" Sara says. "Can I hold it?" Ben shakes his head. "No, Sara, it is cold and hard. It might hurt you." Sara does not listen. She runs to the ball and holds it with both hands. She feels the spikes prick her fingers. She drops the ball and cries. "Ow, ow, ow! The spiky ball is bad!" Sara says. Ben runs to Sara and hugs her. He helps her wash her hands with water. He gives her a band-aid and a kiss. "I'm sorry, Sara. Are you okay?" Ben says. Sara nods. She smiles at Ben. "Thank you, Ben. You are a good brother. I love you." "I love you too, Sara. Next time, listen to me, okay?" Ben says. Sara nods again. They hold hands and go back to their mom. They learn to be careful with spiky things.
