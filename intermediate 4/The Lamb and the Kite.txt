Category: Intermediate
Title: The Lamb and the Kite

Once there was a little lamb who was very flexible. Her wool was soft and bouncy, and she liked to stay close to her mom. One day, she decided to explore away from her mom, and she found a big, wide meadow. Suddenly, the lamb saw a kite flying in the sky! She was so excited and tried to jump up and reach it. She might not have been able to touch it, but she stayed flexible and jumped almost as high as the kite. She stayed until the kite disappeared and then went back to her mom. She had lots of fun, stretching her legs and playing with her friends in the meadow, always staying flexible!

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was only three years old but he was very brave. One day, he decided to go to the park by himself. When he arrived, Tim noticed that someone was having a surprise party. He watched as all his friends had a great time. Suddenly, he felt a bit worried. "What can I do to help?" thought Tim. He smiled and came up with an idea. Tim took out his box of crayons and some paper. He made a colorful banner with the words 'Happy Birthday!' and some balloons. He saved some of the crayons to make a happy birthday card. Tim happily ran up to the surprise party and gave the card and banner to the surprised guest. Everyone cheered for Tim and thanked him for helping out. Tim was glad that he could make his friends happy. He also felt better because he wasn't worried anymore. He had taught himself that he didn't need to be scared when he wanted to help. Tim will always remember this wonderful surprise birthday party.

Once there was a man who was bald. He was a kind man who always wanted to lend his things to others. One day, he saw a lady looking up at the ceiling. He asked her what she was doing and she told him she wanted to paint the ceiling but didn't have a ladder. So, the man thought and thought, and soon he said that he could lend her his ladder so she could reach up to the ceiling. The lady was very happy and thanked the man for his kindness. She used his ladder and painted the ceiling. When she was done, she gave the ladder back and thanked the man again. He was so happy to see the lady smile, and he felt proud of himself that he had been able to lend something to someone in need.

Ben likes to fold paper. He can make many things with paper. He can make a hat, a boat, a bird, and a star. He likes to show his paper things to his mom and dad. They give him a smile and a hug. One day, Ben finds a big paper in the closet. It is very interesting. It has many colors and pictures on it. Ben thinks he can make a big paper thing with it. He takes the paper and goes to his room. He starts to fold the paper. But the paper is not a normal paper. It is a map. It is a very old and important map. It belongs to his dad. His dad is a teacher. He needs the map for his work. He has borrowed the map from the library. He has to give it back soon. Ben does not know this. He folds the paper many times. He makes a big paper ball. He is very happy. He runs to his mom and dad. He wants to show them his paper ball. But when his mom and dad see the paper ball, they are not happy. They are very angry and sad. They see that the paper ball is the map. They see that the map is torn and wrinkled. They see that the map is ruined. They yell at Ben. They tell him he is a bad boy. They tell him he has to pay for the map. They take away his paper things. They send him to his room. Ben is very sad. He does not understand. He does not know why his mom and dad are so mad. He does not know why his paper ball is bad. He cries in his room. He wishes he never folded the paper. He wishes he never saw the map. He wishes he could have a smile and a hug.
