Category: Intermediate
Title: The Building and the Elevator

Once upon a time, there was a big building. In the building, there was an elevator. People used the elevator to go up and down. One day, a man wanted to sell things in the elevator. The man was angry because no one wanted to buy his things. He thought of a plan. He would make the elevator stop, so people would have to buy his things. He did not know that a little girl was watching him. The little girl saw the angry man's plan. She knew she had to do something. When the man was not looking, she pushed a button. The elevator did not stop. The angry man was surprised. The little girl saved the day, and everyone was happy.

Once upon a time, in a big gym, there was a very impressive dog. The dog could hear very well. One day, the dog heard a loud noise. He went to see what it was. When the dog got there, he saw a big cat. The cat was jumping and making the noise. The dog was scared of the cat. But the cat was not scary. The cat was just having fun. Then, the dog and the cat became friends. They played together in the gym. The dog was happy he found a new friend. The cat was happy too. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a brave little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his toys. He had a big box filled with toys. One day, a mean cat named Whiskers came to Spot's house. Whiskers wanted to take all of Spot's toys away. Spot did not want to lose his toys, so he decided to be brave and stand up to Whiskers. Spot said to Whiskers, "You cannot take my toys, they are mine!" Whiskers laughed and said, "I am bigger and stronger, I can take what I want!" Spot was scared, but he knew he had to be brave. He thought of a plan to get his toys back. Spot knew Whiskers did not like loud noises, so he found a big file in his dad's tools. He started to scrape the file on the ground, making a loud noise. Whiskers was scared of the noise and said, "Stop! I don't like that noise!" Spot said, "I will stop if you promise not to take my toys." Whiskers agreed, and Spot put the file away. From that day on, Spot and Whiskers learned to share and play together. And Spot learned that being brave can help you face your fears and solve problems.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a girl named Lily. She loved to create things with her hands. One day, she saw a fragile bird in the rain. The bird was sad and wet. Lily wanted to help the bird. She had an idea! Lily would create an umbrella for the bird. She worked hard to make the umbrella. It was small and just right for the bird. Lily was very happy with her work. She knew the umbrella would keep the bird dry. The next day, it rained again. Lily went outside and saw the fragile bird. She gave the bird the umbrella she created. The bird was so happy! It stayed dry and safe in the rain. Lily felt good that she could help her new friend.

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play in their room. They had many toys and books and games. But they also had a big closet that was always closed. Their mom and dad told them not to open the closet, because it was dark and scary inside. They said there were monsters and spiders and ghosts in the closet. One day, when mom and dad were not home, Tom and Lily were bored. They had played with all their toys and books and games. They wanted to do something new and fun. Tom had an idea. He said to Lily, "Let's open the closet and see what is inside. Maybe we can find some treasures or secrets. I am not afraid of the dark or the monsters or the spiders or the ghosts. Are you?" Lily was not sure. She was a modest girl who liked to follow the rules. She said to Tom, "No, I don't want to open the closet. Mom and dad said it was bad and dangerous. I am afraid of the dark and the monsters and the spiders and the ghosts. They might hurt us or take us away. Please, let's not open the closet." But Tom was not modest. He was a curious boy who liked to break the rules. He said to Lily, "Don't be silly. Mom and dad just said that to scare us. There is nothing in the closet but clothes and shoes and boxes. Come on, it will be fun. I will go first and you can follow me. Trust me, it will be okay." Tom and Lily argued for a while. Tom tried to convince Lily to open the closet. Lily tried to convince Tom to leave the closet alone. They raised their voices and pushed each other. They were not nice to each other. They were angry and rude. Then they heard a loud noise. It was the doorbell. Mom and dad were back. They heard their footsteps and voices. They stopped arguing and looked at each other. They were scared. They knew they were in trouble. They ran to their beds and pretended to be asleep. They hoped mom and dad would not notice the closet. They hoped the closet would not open by itself. They hoped the dark and the monsters and the spiders and the ghosts would not come out. They wished they had been more modest and obedient. They learned their lesson.
