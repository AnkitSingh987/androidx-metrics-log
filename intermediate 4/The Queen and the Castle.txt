Category: Intermediate
Title: The Queen and the Castle

Once upon a time, there was a queen who lived in a big castle. The queen loved to dance and sing. One day, she decided to have a big party for all the people in her kingdom. At the party, everyone was having fun. The queen danced and sang on a stage. The people clapped and applauded. They were very happy. Then, a rough-looking man came into the party. He looked very different from the others, and some people were scared. The man walked up to the queen and said, "I want to dance too." The queen looked at the man and smiled. She said, "Of course, you can dance with us." The man was surprised and happy. He danced with the queen, and everyone applauded him too. The rough-looking man was not scary at all. He was just a kind man who wanted to have fun. From that day on, the queen and the man were good friends, and they danced together at every party.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Max. Max lived in a lab. He was a very tired dog. One day, Max saw a cat. The cat was playing with a toy. Max wanted the toy too. Max went to the cat and said, "Give me the toy!" The cat did not want to give it to Max. Max and the cat started to fight. They both wanted the toy. They did not want to share. They were very mad at each other. A wise old bird saw them fighting. The bird told Max and the cat, "You both can play with the toy. You must share and be nice." Max and the cat listened to the bird. They played with the toy together. They were happy and not mad anymore. The moral of the story is to share and be nice to others.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to imagine he was a big hero. One day, he saw a glass on the table. The glass was full of juice. Tim wanted to drink the juice, but he was too small to reach it. Tim had a naughty idea. He thought he could climb on a chair to get the glass. He was very careful, but the chair was not strong. The chair fell and the glass broke. The juice was all over the floor. Tim's mom came into the room and saw the mess. She was not happy. Tim was sad because he did not get to drink the juice and he made his mom mad. The chair was broken, and Tim learned that being naughty can lead to bad things happening.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amanda. She was only 3 years old and she loved animals. One day, she went to the pet shop and saw a very pretty birdcage with a blue bird inside. She said to her mom, "I want it, please!" Her mom said, "But why, sweetheart? That bird looks so lonely." Amanda said, "No, it's not lonely. We can mark it and it will be happy to have a family." So her mom said, "Very well," and they brought the birdcage home. Amanda immediately began to take care of the blue bird and mark its cage with lots of colors. She even put a swing in the cage, so that the bird could have fun. The blue bird was very happy with Amanda and her family, and it was never lonely again. Amanda and the blue bird were best friends and enjoyed spending lots of time together. The End.

One day, a little fish woke up near the shore. The fish was upset because it was not in the deep water. It wanted to go back to its friends in the sea. The fish saw a big bird and said, "Bird, please help me! I am upset and want to go back to the sea." The bird looked at the fish and nodded. It picked up the fish with its beak and flew over the water. As they flew, the fish saw its friends below. The bird let the fish go, and it splashed into the sea. The fish was happy to be back with its friends. They all swam and played together, and the fish thanked the bird for helping it. From that day on, the fish and the bird were good friends.
