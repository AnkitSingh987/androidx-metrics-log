Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom liked to search for different things in his house. One day, he found a big pocket on his dad's coat. He wanted to see what was inside. Tom put his hand in the pocket and felt something soft. He pulled it out and saw it was a red ball. He was happy and played with it all day. Then, Tom wanted to search for more things in the pocket. The next day, Tom went back to the pocket. This time, he found a small, green car. He played with the car and the ball together, making them race. Tom loved the different things he found in the pocket and couldn't wait to search for more.

Once upon a time, there was a big girl named Sue. Sue loved to imagine things. She would imagine big castles, funny animals, and beautiful flowers. One day, Sue imagined an enormous tree. The tree was so big that all her friends could play on it. They climbed the tree, laughed, and had fun together. Sue was very happy. Sue also imagined a marriage between two cute animals, a cat and a dog. They had a pretty party with all their animal friends. They danced, ate yummy food, and played games. In the end, everyone was happy and Sue loved to imagine more fun things.

Once upon a time, there was a kind and reliable dog named Max. Max always helped his friends, and everyone loved him. One day, Max saw a thief take a toy from his friend, a little girl named Lily. Max went to the thief and said, "Give the toy back to Lily, please." The thief felt bad and gave the toy back to Max. Max returned the toy to Lily, and she was very happy. Lily said, "Thank you, Max! You are the best friend." Max felt proud and happy. Everyone admired Max for being brave and helpful. From that day on, the thief learned to be good and never took things from others again.

---

Once upon a time, there was a wild cat named Tom. Tom liked to run and play in the sun. One day, Tom saw a big green cactus. He thought it was a fun toy to play with. Tom tried to part the cactus with his paws. But the cactus was very prickly. It hurt Tom's paws a lot. Tom cried and ran to his mom for help. Mom cat tried to help Tom, but she could not fix his paws. Tom felt sad and learned that not everything can be a toy. From that day on, Tom stayed away from the cactus and played with safe toys instead.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big, red ball in the sky. The ball was starting to rise. Tim felt lucky to see it. He wanted to show his friend, Sam. Tim ran to Sam's house. He knocked on the door. Sam came out and said, "Hi, Tim! What do you want to show me?" Tim said, "Look at the big, red ball in the sky. It is rising!" Sam looked up and saw it too. They both felt lucky. After a while, Sam said, "I am tired. I need a nap." Tim said, "Me too. Let's take a nap under the tree." So, they both lay down and took a nap. While they were sleeping, the sky turned dark. It started to rain. The rain woke them up. They were wet and cold. Tim said, "We are not lucky now." Sam said, "No, we are not." They ran back to their homes, feeling sad about the bad ending to their day.

Sara was a wealthy girl who had many toys and clothes. She liked to play with her dolls and dress them up. She did not like to share or help others. She thought she was better than everyone else. One day, she went to the history museum with her class. She saw many things from the past, like old cars, clothes, and tools. She was bored and did not listen to the teacher. She wanted to go home and play with her dolls. She wandered away from the group and found a room with a big painting. The painting showed a poor family who lived long ago. They had no toys or clothes. They had to work hard and help each other. Sara felt sorry for them. She wondered how they could be happy. She heard a voice behind her. It was an old lady who worked at the museum. She smiled and said, "Do you like the painting? It shows my great-grandparents. They were not wealthy, but they were kind and generous. They taught me to help others and be grateful for what I have." Sara was surprised. She asked, "How can you help others when you have so little?" The old lady said, "You can help others in many ways. You can share, listen, comfort, and learn. You can also help by being respectful and polite. You can help by being a good friend. Helping others makes you happy and rich in your heart." Sara thought about what the old lady said. She realized that she had been selfish and rude. She felt ashamed and sorry. She said, "I'm sorry. I have been a bad girl. I want to help others and be a good friend. Can you teach me how?" The old lady nodded and hugged her. She said, "Of course, I can. Come, let's go back to your class. I'll show you some more things from the past. Maybe you'll find something that interests you. And maybe you'll find some new friends who need your help." Sara smiled and followed the old lady. She learned a lot from the history museum. She also learned a lot from the old lady. She learned to be more helpful and humble. She learned that being wealthy is not only about having things, but also about having a good heart.
