Category: Intermediate
Title: Charlie the Boy and the Car

Once there was a young boy named Charlie. He was ready to go on an adventure. So Charlie and his mum got in the car and drove to the beach. It was a sunny day and they could hear the ocean waves crashing against the shore. Charlie loved the beach and quickly began to explore. At one point, he found a tube in the sand. He excitedly showed it to his mum, but then he noticed that his mum was starting to weep. Charlie didn't understand why his mum was so sad. He reached out and hugged her tightly. He asked, "Are you ok?". His mum smiled and said, "Yes, I'm ok. This tube reminds me of a time when I was little, like you". It was then that Charlie realised that his mum had been missing someone. He decided to do something special for her. He took the tube and began to fill it up with all the colourful shells he had found. Then, he presented it to his mum. His mum was so happy and hugged him tightly. She said, "Thank you. This tube is now full of memories of us together." Charlie smiled and said, "That's ok, Mum. I'm always ready for a new adventure!"

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They bought a box of cereal. Tim felt lucky because it was his favorite kind. He could not wait to eat it. When they got home, Tim helped his mom unpack the bags. He found the cereal box and showed it to his mom with a big smile. She smiled too and said, "You are a good helper, Tim!" The next morning, Tim ate his lucky cereal for breakfast. It made him very happy. He told his mom, "I am so lucky to have this cereal and you!" His mom hugged him and they had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very alert. He liked to look at the sky and see the stars. One day, Tim saw a new star. It was a new planet! Tim wanted to go to the planet. He asked his mom, "Can I go to the planet?" His mom said, "No, it's too far away." Tim was sad. He really wanted to go to the planet. Then, Tim had an idea. He would change into a big rocket ship! So, with a little help from his mom, Tim became a rocket ship. He flew up, up, up into the sky. Tim went to the new planet and made new friends. He was happy.

One day, a little girl named Mia saw a big billboard. It had many different colors on it. Mia was so happy to see it. She ran to her friend Tom and said, "Look at the big sign! It has so many colors!" Tom looked at the billboard too. He saw that it was not like the other signs. It was very different. He said, "Wow, Mia! You are right. This sign is not like the others. It is special!" Mia and Tom decided to act like the billboard. They put on clothes with many colors. They danced and laughed together. Their friends saw them and joined in the fun. Everyone was happy, just like the colorful billboard.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy called John. He loved looking up at the night sky. One day, he peered up and said to his mum, "Mum, what is that incredible thing there?" Mum said, "That's a planet. It moves around the sun in space." John's eyes widened in amazement. "Really?" he asked. Mum smiled and said, "Yes, isn't it incredible? It's like a big ball of gas spinning around a star." John couldn't believe it. He asked, "Can I move faster than a planet one day? Mum said, "Yes, I'm sure you can. Nothing is impossible for you! Now let's go inside and have dinner". John smiled and ran inside. He was so excited that one day he would be able to move faster than the incredible planet up in the night sky.

Once upon a time there was a little boy called Tim. Tim liked to make a mess when he played. His mom was always suggesing to him to keep his room tidy, but he never listened. One night, Tim and his mom were watching TV together. Tim wanted to watch a cartoon, but his mom suggested a movie. Tim started to get frustrated and said, "I don't want to watch a movie. I want to watch a cartoon!" His mom said, "We can watch a movie first, and then you can watch a cartoon later." Tim was not very happy with this suggestion and said, "Fine. But the room has to be tidy before I watch the cartoon." Tim's mom agreed, so Tim rushed to his room to tidy up the mess. He picked up all his toys and put them away neatly. Finally, the room was tidy and Tim's mom looked very proud of him. So, as promised, Tim was allowed to watch the cartoon that he wanted. He settled down and enjoyed the show, but he always remembered to keep his room tidy and listen to his Mom's suggestions.
