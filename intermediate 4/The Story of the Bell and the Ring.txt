Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Bell and the Ring

Once upon a time, there was a young girl who had a very special scooter. It was blue and shiny, and it had a bell which would ring when she rode it. Everywhere she went, she liked to ring that bell. One day, the girl was in the park with her scooter and she met some other kids. They all wanted to try her scooter because it was so popular. So she let them ride it and also helped them to ring that bell. The bell sounded loud and clear, and everyone was so happy. The girl and her friends had so much fun that day, and they all decided to meet again the next day to play with the scooter. From that day on, the girl was one of the most popular kids in the park. She never stopped ringing that special bell on her scooter.

David was playing outside. Suddenly, he slipped and fell onto the dry grass. He wanted to get up but he felt so tired. "Mama, I fell," David said, while trying to get up. Mama came outside to check on him. She saw that he was okay. She helped David up and then asked, "Where is your computer?" David remembered he was playing with the computer. He had left it in the living room! He ran to get it, with Mama following behind him. When they reached the living room, David couldn't find it anywhere. He asked, "Where did the computer go?" Mama pointed to the floor. The computer had fallen off the sofa and landed on the rug. David was very happy to see the computer safe and sound. He picked it up and hugged it tight. Mama said with a smile, "Let's put the computer back on the sofa."

Once upon a time there was a dove. She was very alert, always looking around. One day she split away from her family. She wanted to explore the world. The dove flew far and wide. She saw lots of things - big hills, tall trees and lots of animals too. Some were very fast, some were very slow. In the evening the dove saw a family of ducks. She said hello and they said hello back. The dove was happy and smiled. The ducks asked the dove if she wanted to join them for dinner. She said yes and they had lots of fun. The dove was so happy that she split away from her family to explore the world.

---

Lily and Sam are friends. They like to play at the park. They gather rocks and sticks and leaves. They make a big pile. They say it is their castle. They see a slide. It is long and shiny. They want to go on it. They run to the slide. They take turns. Lily goes first. She sits on the slide. She slides down fast. She laughs. Sam goes next. He slides down too. He says it is fun. They see a dog. It is big and brown. It runs to them. It barks. Lily and Sam are scared. They drop their rocks and sticks and leaves. They run away from the dog. They hide behind a bench. They hug each other. The dog sniffs their pile. It likes their rocks and sticks and leaves. It takes some in its mouth. It runs away with them. It is happy. Lily and Sam peek from behind the bench. They see the dog. They see their pile. It is smaller now. They are sad. They cry. They want their rocks and sticks and leaves back.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy tasty food for dinner. Tim liked going to the store because he could see all the different things they had. While they were at the store, Tim saw a big tub of water. He wanted to play in it. Tim asked his mom, "Can I play in the water, please?" His mom said, "Okay, but don't get too wet!" Tim went to the tub and started to splash in the water. He was having so much fun! Other kids saw Tim and joined him. They all splashed and laughed together. Tim was happy he got to play in the water at the store. After they finished playing, Tim and his mom bought the tasty food they needed. They went home and cooked a yummy dinner. Tim told his dad about the fun he had at the store, and they all enjoyed the tasty meal together.
