Category: Intermediate
Title: The Oven and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a big oven. The oven was very angry. It did not like to cook food. One day, a little girl named Sue came into the kitchen. She saw the angry oven and said, "Why are you so angry, oven?" The oven replied, "I don't want to cook food. I want to glow like the stars in the sky." Sue thought for a moment and said, "I have an idea! Let's make a cake with lots of shiny sprinkles on it. When we cook the cake, you will glow like a star." The oven liked the idea. Sue made the cake and put it in the oven. When the cake was done, the oven glowed like a star. The angry oven was now happy. Sue and the oven learned that by working together, they could make each other happy. And that was the moral of the story: when we help each other, we can find happiness.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a restless little dog named Max. Max liked to play and jump all day. One day, Max saw a cobweb in the corner of the room. He thought it was a toy and wanted to play with it. Max jumped up and tried to grab the cobweb. He heard a snap sound as he pulled it down. Max was scared, but then he saw a tiny spider. The spider looked sad because its home was gone. "Sorry, spider," said Max. "I did not know it was your home. I will help you make a new one." So Max and the spider worked together to make a new cobweb. And they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a crazy idea. He wanted to borrow a big ball from his friend, Sam, to play a new game. Tim thought it would be fun to play catch with the ball in his palm. So, Tim went to Sam's house to ask if he could borrow the big ball. Sam said yes, and they went outside to play. They laughed and had fun as they threw the ball back and forth. The ball fit just right in Tim's palm. After a while, they got tired and sat down under a tree. Tim thanked Sam for letting him borrow the big ball. They both agreed that it was a crazy and fun game. From that day on, Tim and Sam played with the big ball all the time, and they became best friends.

One day, a modern pirate named Sam went to the park. He saw a big tree and wanted to play. Sam had a toy gun that could shoot soft balls. He got ready to shoot the balls at the tree. A little boy named Tim saw Sam and wanted to play too. Tim said, "Can I play with you?" Sam smiled and said, "Yes, let's shoot the soft balls at the tree together!" As they were playing, a big wind came and blew all the soft balls away. Sam and Tim were sad. But then, a big dog came running with all the soft balls in his mouth. The dog dropped the balls and wagged his tail. Sam and Tim laughed and played with the dog, shooting the soft balls and letting the dog bring them back. The day became fun and happy again.

Lily was a big girl who liked to wander around the house. She wanted to see everything and touch everything. Sometimes she made a mess, but she always tried to help mommy clean up. One day, Lily wandered into the bathroom. She saw the toilet and remembered what mommy had told her. Big girls use the toilet, not the diaper. Lily wanted to be a big girl, so she decided to try the toilet. She pulled down her pants and sat on the toilet. She waited and waited, but nothing happened. She heard mommy calling her name. "Lily, where are you?" Mommy sounded worried. Lily wanted to answer, but she did not want to leave the toilet. She hoped something would happen soon. Mommy opened the door and saw Lily on the toilet. She smiled and said, "Lily, what are you doing?" Lily looked at mommy and said, "I'm trying to use the toilet, like a big girl." Mommy praised her and said, "That's very good, Lily. I'm proud of you. But you don't have to sit on the toilet for so long. You can get up and try again later, when you feel like you need to go." Lily nodded and got up. She pulled up her pants and flushed the toilet. She washed her hands and hugged mommy. Mommy hugged her back and said, "You're a very brave and smart girl, Lily. I love you." Lily felt happy and proud. She said, "I love you too, mommy. Can I wander some more?" Mommy laughed and said, "Sure, but stay close to me, okay?" Lily agreed and followed mommy out of the bathroom. She was ready for more adventures.
