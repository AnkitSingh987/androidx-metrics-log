Category: Intermediate
Title: Mimi the Cat and the Mouse

One day, a small cat named Mimi was very cold. She began to shiver. She looked around for a warm place to sit. Mimi saw a big tree with a hole in it. "That looks warm," she said. Mimi went inside the tree hole and saw a big owl. The owl had big eyes. "Hi, Owl! Can I stay here?" Mimi asked. Owl said, "Yes, but you must be nice to everyone." Mimi agreed. One day, a cold mouse came to the tree. Mimi wanted to catch the mouse, but she remembered the owl's words. Instead, she let the mouse stay. The mouse was very thankful. From that day on, Mimi, the owl, and the mouse lived happily together, always being nice to each other.

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to play with his ball in the park. He would run and jump and catch the ball all day long. One day, Buddy found a big box. The box had a price tag on it. Buddy did not know what the price was, but he wanted to fill the box with fun things. So, he went to find his ball and put it in the box. Buddy also found a nice stick and some soft grass to put in the box. He was very happy with his box. Buddy played with his ball, stick, and grass all day. And every day after that, Buddy would fill the box with new things to play with.

Once upon a time, there was a thin bird named Bella. Bella loved to sing. Every day, she sang on a big tree. She had many friends who loved to hear her sing. One day, Bella found a card on the ground. It had pretty colors and a picture of a cake. She showed the card to her friend, Max, the cat. Max said, "Oh, it's a birthday card! Let's give it to someone who has a birthday today." Bella and Max went to find their friend, Lucy, the dog. They gave her the card and said, "Happy birthday, Lucy!" Lucy was very happy. She said, "Thank you, Bella and Max! Can you sing for me today?" Bella sang a beautiful birthday song for Lucy. All their friends came to listen. They all clapped and cheered. From that day on, Bella, Max, and Lucy were the best of friends, and they always celebrated their birthdays together.

One day, a big turkey named Tom lived on a farm. Tom was a very patient turkey. He had many friends, like the cow, the pig, and the duck. They all liked to play together. One morning, Tom saw a big truck come to the farm. The farmer put all the turkeys in the truck. Tom was sad and said, "I don't want to go." His friend, the duck, heard him and said, "Don't worry, Tom. We will help you." The cow, the pig, and the duck made a plan to save Tom. They all worked together to open the truck door. Tom saw the door open and jumped out. The turkey sighed, "Thank you, friends!" The farmer saw Tom run away and tried to catch him. But Tom was fast and hid behind the big tree. The farmer could not find him. Tom was safe with his friends again. From that day on, Tom and his friends played together on the farm. They were all very happy. And Tom, the patient turkey, was very thankful for his good friends.

Once upon a time, in a small room, there was a sock. This sock was unknown to everyone. It was all alone under the bed. The sock was sad and wanted a friend. One day, a small toy bear came close to the sock. The bear said, "Hello, sock! I have never seen you before. Do you want to be friends?" The sock was very happy and said, "Yes, I want to be friends with you!" The sock and the bear played all day. They became very good friends. They knew they would be friends forever. And they lived happily ever after.

Lily and Ben were best friends. They liked to play with blocks and make towers. One day, they had a big box of blocks to share. They decided to see who could make the highest tower. Lily started to add blocks to her tower. She was careful and balanced them well. She wanted her tower to be graceful and strong. Ben also started to add blocks to his tower. He was fast and piled them up. He wanted his tower to be big and tall. Soon, their towers were very high. Lily and Ben looked at each other's towers. They both thought their own tower was better. They started to say mean things to each other. "Your tower is too slow and boring. Mine is faster and more fun!" Ben said. "Your tower is too messy and shaky. Mine is more graceful and stable!" Lily said. They kept arguing and did not notice that their towers were getting too high. Suddenly, both towers fell down with a loud crash. The blocks scattered everywhere. Lily and Ben were sad and angry. They had no more blocks to play with. They sat on the floor and looked at the mess. They realized that they had been silly and rude. They had wasted their blocks and their fun. They felt sorry and wanted to be friends again. They looked at each other and said, "I'm sorry. I was wrong. Your tower was good too. Can we play together again?" They hugged and smiled. They decided to work together and make a new tower. They used all the blocks and helped each other. They made a tower that was high and graceful and fun. They had more fun than before. They learned that sharing and being kind is better than fighting and being mean.
