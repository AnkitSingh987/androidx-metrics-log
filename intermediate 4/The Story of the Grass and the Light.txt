Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Grass and the Light

Once upon a time there were two friends who liked to play together. One day they had a fight. They ran and ran until they came to a yellow light. They stopped to look around and noticed something. Instead of being angry, they had big smiles on their faces. The yellow light made everything look different. They decided to play a game of tag. They laughed and chased each other around the light until they were both so tired they decided to take a break. They lay in the grass, looking up at the yellow light, and decided that no matter what happened, they would always be friends.

One day, a creative little mouse named Max saw a big piece of cheese in a trap. Max knew the trap could snap and hurt him, but he wanted the cheese very much. Max had a smart idea. He asked his friend, a strong bird named Ben, to help him get the cheese. Max said, "Ben, can you help me get the cheese from the trap? You are big and strong, and the trap will not snap on you." Ben agreed to help Max. He used his beak to lift the cheese from the trap. Just as Ben got the cheese, the trap snapped shut, but it did not hurt Ben. Max and Ben were very happy that they got the cheese without getting hurt. Max and Ben shared the cheese and had a great time. They knew that by working together and being creative, they could solve any problem. And from that day on, Max and Ben were the best of friends.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a messy book. The book was in a box. Sue liked the book. She wanted to read it. She took the book to her mom. "Mom, can you read this book to me?" Sue asked. Sue's mom looked at the book. "This book is very messy," she said. "Let's clean it first." Sue and her mom cleaned the book. They used a cloth to wipe the dirt off the pages. The book looked better now. Sue's mom set Sue on her lap. She opened the book and began to read. Sue listened to the story. She was happy. The messy book was now a clean book, and Sue loved it.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. They had a modern kitchen, where Lily loved to help her mom cook. One day, Lily's mom asked her to stir the soup. Lily was so happy and proud. Behind their house, there was a big garden with many plants. Lily's favorite plant was a long vine that grew on the fence. She liked to watch the vine grow and play with the leaves. Sometimes, she would pretend that the vine was a magic rope that could take her on adventures. One sunny day, Lily was playing with the vine when she heard a tiny voice. "Hello," said the voice. Lily looked around and saw a little bug on the vine. "Hi," said Lily. "I am a magic bug. I live on this vine, and I can make your wish come true." The little bug asked Lily what she wanted. Lily thought for a moment and said, "I wish for a friend to play with." The magic bug smiled and, with a wave of its tiny wings, a cute puppy appeared. Lily and the puppy played all day long, and they became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a happy boy named Tim. Tim loved to spread butter on his bread. One day, his mom gave him a new food to try. It was called cauliflower. Tim looked at the cauliflower and thought it looked like a tiny tree. "Mom, what is this?" Tim asked. His mom replied, "It's cauliflower, Tim. It's very yummy. Try it!" Tim took a small bite and found that he liked it. He was excited to eat more cauliflower. The next day, Tim's mom made a big surprise for him. She made a whole forest of cauliflower trees! Tim was so happy. He spread butter on his bread and ate it with the cauliflower. They all had a great time eating the yummy forest together.
