Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Tough Boy and the Sue

One day, a tough boy named Tim found a big umbrella. He liked the umbrella very much. He knew it was important to value things he found. Tim saw a girl named Sue. Sue was wet from the rain. Tim said, "Sue, do you want my umbrella?" Sue said, "Yes, thank you, Tim!" Tim and Sue shared the umbrella. They were happy and dry. They learned to value things they had and to help each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little shrimp who lived in the sea. The shrimp had many friends who liked to play and laugh with him. They played hide and seek, and tag, and had a lot of fun together. One day, while they were playing, a big fish came near them. The shrimp and his friends were scared. They did not want the big fish to eat them. So, they swam away as fast as they could. They swam and swam until they found a safe place to hide. In the safe place, they laughed and played again. They were very happy that they were safe from the big fish. The little shrimp and his friends learned that it was important to stay safe while having fun. And they lived happily ever after.

Once a long time ago there was a family that lived together on a farm. The mother had a baby infant and she loved it very much. She was always wiping its face and hands. One night a storm comes and it is so loud and long that it scares the infant. The mother decides to wrap her infant up in a blanket and take it outside. She wipes away some of her tears, looks up at the sky and makes a special wish. The next day the sky is light and clear. The family goes out and looks at the warm sun and the mother holds the infant close. In the field there is something special, a rainbow! The mother wipes away her tears and wonders if the rainbow is the answer to her wish. In the sky the rainbow fades away and the mother knows her wish has come true. From then on the mother was always wiping away the tears of joy, knowing that love was very strong on the farm.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a creative cop named Bob. He loved to paint and draw when he was not working. One day, while Bob was painting a big, pretty tree, a little boy named Tim came to him. "Hi, I am Tim. What are you doing?" asked Tim. Bob said, "I am painting a tree. Do you want to try?" Tim was excited and started to paint with Bob. They had a lot of fun together. But then, Tim's mom called him to go home. Tim didn't want to quit painting, but he had to listen to his mom. He said, "Bye, Bob. I had fun painting with you." Bob smiled and said, "Bye, Tim. We can paint together again soon." And they both went their separate ways, happy to have made a new friend.

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a lonely pear. The lonely pear wanted a friend. It looked around, but there were no other pears on the tree. The lonely pear was very sad. One day, a little bird came to the tree. The bird saw the lonely pear and wanted to help. The bird flew away to find a friend for the lonely pear. The bird looked high and low, but could not find another pear. The bird did not give up. It kept looking until it found a small pear tree with many pears. The bird picked one pear and brought it back to the lonely pear. The lonely pear was so happy to have a friend. They talked and laughed together, and the lonely pear was lonely no more.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a watch that he loved very much. But there was one thing that Tim did not like. He did not like being restless. He would always say, "I hate being restless!" One day, Tim went to the park with his watch. He was feeling restless and did not know what to do. He walked and walked until he found a big tree. Tim sat under the tree, hoping to feel better. But he still felt restless. Just then, a little bird flew down from the tree. The bird looked at Tim's watch and said, "I can help you feel better! I will take your watch and fly up high. When I drop it, you must catch it. This will help you not feel restless." Tim was scared, but he agreed. The bird took the watch and flew up high. Tim watched as the bird dropped the watch. Tim jumped and caught the watch just in time. He felt so happy and not restless anymore. From that day on, Tim and the little bird were best friends. They played together every day, and Tim never felt restless again.
