Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Garden

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy had very long hair. She liked to comb her hair every day. One day, she lost her comb. Lucy was sad. She had to find her comb. Lucy started to think. She looked under her bed. She looked in the kitchen. She looked in the garden. There, she saw a weak, little bird with her comb. The bird was using the comb to make a nest. Lucy felt happy that the bird found a good use for her comb. Lucy decided to let the bird keep the comb. She learned that sometimes, it is good to share things with others who need them more. Lucy felt happy and proud. She told her friends and family about the bird and her comb. They all learned that sharing can bring happiness to everyone.

Sara liked to play with her toys in her room. She had many toys, but her favorite was a clock. The clock had a face with two hands and numbers. Sara liked to move the hands and make the clock say different times. She also liked to pretend that the clock was her friend and talk to it. One day, her brother Tom came into her room. He saw the clock and wanted to play with it too. He said, "Give me the clock, Sara. I want to see it." Sara did not want to give him the clock. She said, "No, Tom. This is my clock. You have your own toys. Go away." Tom did not listen. He tried to grab the clock from Sara. Sara held on to the clock and shouted, "Stop it, Tom. You are being mean. Leave me alone." They pulled and pushed the clock until it fell on the floor. The clock broke into pieces. Sara and Tom were both shocked and sad. They heard their mom coming up the stairs. She had told them to keep their room tidy and not to fight. Sara and Tom knew they were in trouble. They quickly tried to hide the broken clock under the bed. They hoped their mom would not see it. They pretended to be nice to each other and smiled at their mom. But their mom was not fooled. She saw the mess on the floor and the clock under the bed. She said, "What have you done, Sara and Tom? You have broken the clock. That was a gift from your grandma. How could you be so careless and rude?" Sara and Tom felt sorry and ashamed. They said, "We are sorry, mom. We were playing and we had a fight. We did not mean to break the clock. We love the clock and we love grandma." Their mom was angry and disappointed. She said, "You need to learn to share and respect your things and each other. You will have to fix the clock or buy a new one with your own money. And you will have to clean your room and apologize to grandma. Now go and do it." Sara and Tom nodded and said, "Yes, mom." They went to pick up the pieces of the clock and the other toys. They wished they had not fought over the clock. They learned their lesson. They decided to be more tidy and more kind.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big billboard. The billboard was charming and had a picture of a happy family. The family was smiling and having fun. The people in the town liked to look at the billboard because it made them feel happy. One day, a naughty boy named Tim wanted to disturb the charming billboard. He threw stones at it and made the picture look bad. The people in the town were sad because they could not look at the happy family anymore. A wise old man saw what Tim had done and told him that it was not nice to disturb things that make others happy. Tim felt bad and said sorry. He helped to fix the charming billboard so the people in the town could be happy again. The moral of the story is that we should not disturb things that make others happy.
