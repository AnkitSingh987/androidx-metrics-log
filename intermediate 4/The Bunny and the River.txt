Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bunny and the River

Once upon a time, there was a bunny who wanted to explore the world. He put on his green shoes and stepped out of his burrow. As he adjusted to the bright sunlight, he spotted a shiny thread leading away from his home. Excited, he followed the thread and it took him to many new places. The thread wound through fields, over hills and through forests. The bunny was so excited and hopped place to place. He traveled for days and days until he reached the banks of a sparkling river. He stopped to rest and refresh himself. Suddenly, he noticed a big, green apple beside him. He had never seen anything like it before! He took a big bite and it tasted sweet and delicious. He gathered some more of the green apples and tucked them into his pocket. Then the bunny was ready to explore some more. He followed the green thread away from the river and soon he could see his home in the distance. He hopped all the way back, sure that he had many more wonderful adventures on the way!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Sue. She was walking through a forest when she found a beautiful jewel. She wanted to take it home with her, so she smiled and nodded. But then she saw a selfish lion. The lion wanted the jewel for himself. He walked up to Sue and said, "Give me the jewel!" Sue shook her head and said, "No, I found this jewel first. It’s mine!". The lion roared and stepped closer, but Sue stood her ground. The lion thought for a moment and then nodded. He said, "I will let you keep the jewel. But don't forget to share it with your friends." Sue nodded and said she would never be selfish with her new treasure. The lion wandered away, and Sue returned home with the sparkling jewel. She showed it to her friends and they all said it was the most beautiful jewel they had ever seen!

One day, a happy fish named Sam lived near a reef. The reef was big and pretty with many colors. Sam loved to play with his friends in the water. Sometimes, Sam would tease his friends by hiding behind the rocks. He would jump out and say "Boo!" His friends would laugh and chase him around the reef. At the end of the day, all the happy fish would swim home to their families. They would tell stories about their fun day at the reef. Sam and his friends loved their life in the sea.

Once upon a time, there was a polite boy named Tim. He loved to watch videos on his mom's big phone. One day, his mom showed him a new video. It was about a funny dog that could attach its toy to its tail. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. "Mom, can I watch the funny dog again?" Tim asked, with a big smile on his face. "Of course, Tim," his mom said. "But first, let's attach your toy car to your shirt, so you won't lose it while you watch the video." Tim was very happy. His mom helped him attach the toy car to his shirt. Then, he sat down and watched the funny dog video again. He laughed even more this time. After watching the video, Tim went outside to play with his toy car. He was still laughing about the funny dog. He wanted to show his friends the video too. So, he ran back inside and asked his mom if he could share the video with his friends. His mom said, "Yes, you can show your friends the funny dog video. Just remember to be polite and say 'please' and 'thank you'." Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Mom!" Then, he went outside to share the funny dog video with his friends. They all laughed and had a great time.

Lily and Ben were playing with their toy trucks in the garden. They liked to load the trucks with dirt, leaves and rocks and drive them around. Lily had a big red truck and Ben had a small blue one. "Look, Lily, I have more dirt than you!" Ben said, showing his truck. "No, you don't, Ben. My truck is bigger, so I can load more dirt!" Lily said, scooping up some more dirt with her shovel. They both laughed and kept loading their trucks. They did not see the jar of jam that Mom had left on the table. It was Mom's special jam that she made from the berries in the garden. She was going to make sandwiches for lunch. Soon, Lily and Ben had filled their trucks with dirt. They decided to drive them to the other side of the garden, where there was a small hill. They wanted to see who could go faster. "Ready, set, go!" Lily shouted, and they both pushed their trucks. Lily's truck went very fast, but Ben's truck was slower. He tried to catch up with Lily, but he bumped into the table. The jar of jam fell off the table and landed on Ben's truck. The jam spilled all over the dirt and the truck. "Oh no!" Ben cried, looking at his messy truck. Lily stopped her truck and ran back to Ben. She saw the jam and the dirt and the broken jar. "I'm sorry, Ben. Are you okay?" she asked, being gentle. Ben nodded, but he looked sad. He liked his truck and he did not like the jam. Mom heard the noise and came out of the house. She saw the mess and the kids. "What happened here?" she asked, surprised. Lily and Ben told her what happened. They said they were sorry for breaking the jar and spilling the jam. Mom smiled and hugged them. She said it was okay, accidents happen. She said she had more jam in the house and she could clean the truck. She took them inside and made them sandwiches. She gave them some jam to put on their bread. They ate their sandwiches and thanked Mom. They learned to be more careful with their trucks and the jam. They also learned that Mom was very kind and gentle. They loved Mom and they loved each other.
