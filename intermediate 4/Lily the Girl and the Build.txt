Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Build

Once upon a time, in a tidy little house, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily loved to make models with her toy blocks. She would build tall towers, big houses, and even little cars. One day, her friend Tom came to play with her. "Let's build a big model together!" said Lily. Tom nodded, and they started to build. They used red, blue, and yellow blocks to make their model. It was so much fun! Suddenly, Tom felt a tickle in his nose. "Achoo!" he sneezed, and the model fell down. Lily looked sad, but Tom said, "I'm sorry, Lily. Let's build it again!" They laughed and started to build a new model, even bigger than before. And they played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went to the doctor with his mom. The doctor said Tim needed an x-ray. Tim did not know what an x-ray was, but his mom said it was a picture of his insides. Tim was scared, but his mom told him it was important. The x-ray was expensive, so Tim's mom had to plan and save money to pay for it. Tim saw his mom work hard to save money. She put coins in a jar every day. Tim wanted to help, so he did chores to earn coins too. They both worked together to save for the x-ray. Finally, they had enough money for the x-ray. Tim was brave and got the x-ray done. The doctor said Tim was okay. Tim and his mom were happy. They learned the value of planning and working together to achieve their goal. And Tim was not scared of x-rays anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a big fish named Bob. Bob loved to dive deep in the water. One day, while diving, he saw a little fish named Tim. Tim looked sad. Bob asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim said, "I want to hear an interesting story, but I don't know any." Bob thought for a moment and said, "I know a story! Let's go to the top of the water, and I will tell you." As they swam up, they saw a big bird named Sue. Sue heard them talking about the story. She wanted to hear it too. So, they all sat down to listen to the interesting story. Bob began to tell the story, but suddenly, a big wave came and splashed them all! The wave made them laugh and forget about the story. They played in the water and had fun. From that day on, Bob, Tim, and Sue were the best of friends. They learned that sometimes the best stories are the ones you make together.

Once upon a time, there was a happy reindeer named Remy. Remy lived in a big forest with his friends. They loved to run and play all day long. One day, Remy's friend, a little bird named Bella, came to play with him. Bella liked to tease Remy by flying high up in the sky where Remy could not reach. "You can't catch me!" Bella said, laughing. Remy felt sad because he wanted to play with Bella, but he could not fly. One day, while Bella was teasing Remy, she flew too high and got stuck in a tree. Remy saw Bella needed help. He used his long, strong legs to jump and reach Bella. Remy saved Bella, and she was very thankful. From that day on, Bella never teased Remy again. They played together happily in the forest. The moral of the story is: be kind to your friends and help each other when in need.

One day, a big cat saw a small mouse. The cat wanted to catch the mouse. The cat was fast, and the mouse was scared. Near the mouse, there was a sharp saw. The mouse had a plan. The cat ran after the mouse. The mouse ran fast too. The mouse ran near the sharp saw. The cat did not see the saw. The cat was too busy looking at the mouse. The cat got too close to the saw. The cat felt the sharp saw on its fur. The cat stopped running. The cat was scared now. The mouse was safe. The cat learned to be more careful. The cat and the mouse became friends.
