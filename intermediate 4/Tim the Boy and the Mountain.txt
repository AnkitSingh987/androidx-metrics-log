Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Mountain

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a small house near a huge mountain. He loved to play outside and look at the big mountain every day. One sunny day, Tim decided to climb the huge mountain. He put on his shoes and started to walk. As he walked, he saw a big tree and a pretty flower. He clapped his hands because he was so happy. When Tim reached the top of the mountain, he saw his friend, a little bird. Tim and the bird clapped their hands and wings together. They were so happy to be on the huge mountain. Then, they played and sang songs until it was time to go home.

One day, a little squirrel went to the park. He had a big, bushy tail and liked to jump from tree to tree. The squirrel was very happy because the sun was shining and the sky was blue. The squirrel saw a boy with a toy car. The boy was playing and having fun. The squirrel thought the car was crazy and wanted to play too. So, the squirrel went to the boy and showed him how he could jump and run. The boy laughed and showed the squirrel how to play with the toy car. They played together all day long. The squirrel and the boy became good friends. They had a lot of fun with the crazy toy car in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a prince. The prince was very obedient. He always listened to his mom and dad. One day, his mom asked him to promise her something. The prince said, "I promise, mom." One day, the prince went for a walk. He saw a big frog. The frog said, "Hello, prince. I need your help. Please kiss me." The prince was scared. He did not want to kiss a frog. But he remembered his promise to be obedient. So, he kissed the frog. The frog turned into a beautiful princess! She said, "Thank you, prince. I was under a spell. Now, I am free." The prince and the princess became friends. They played together every day. The prince was happy he had kept his promise.

Once upon a time, there was a small pigeon. It hop-hop-hopped around the playground, looking for something to eat. Suddenly, the pigeon spotted a big bread crust. But it was hard! The pigeon tried and tried, but it just couldn't peck at it. Just then, a small child sprang up to the pigeon. The child said, "Do you want this? I can't peck at it either." The pigeon felt so happy and thanked the child. The child said, "You don't have to thank me. Just share with me!" The pigeon and the child shared the crust and the pigeon was so full. Sadness came the next day. The pigeon hop-hop-hopped back to the playground looking for the child, but the child was nowhere to be found. The pigeon was so sad. It thought it had made a friend. But it hadn't. The child had just been trying to trick it all along. The pigeon was very sad and lonely as it flew away.

One day, a girl named Mia found a hidden comb. The comb was very pretty. Mia wanted to use the comb on her hair. But her hair was messy and tangled. Mia tried to zip her coat, but her hair got stuck in the zipper. She pulled and pulled, but her hair would not come out. Mia felt sad and did not know what to do. Mia's mom saw her and came to help. She gently took the comb and brushed Mia's hair. The tangles went away, and Mia's hair became smooth. Mia was happy, and she hugged her mom. They zipped up her coat, and Mia could go play outside.

Once upon a time, there was a kind doctor named Sam. He liked to visit his patients and help them feel better. One day, he went to see a little girl named Lily. Lily was not feeling well and needed the doctor to make her healthy again. Doctor Sam said, "Hello Lily! I am here to visit you and make you healthy again. Let's check you and see what we can do." Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Doctor Sam. I want to be healthy and play with my friends." Doctor Sam checked Lily and gave her some medicine. He told her, "Take this medicine and rest. Soon, you will be healthy and can play outside." Lily took the medicine and said, "I will listen to you, Doctor Sam. I want to be a good patient." After a few days, Lily was feeling much better. She was healthy and could play with her friends again. Doctor Sam was happy to see his patient Lily feeling well. And they all lived happily ever after.
