Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Loyal Dog and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Max. Max had a favorite toy, a ball with a string. He loved to play with the string all day long. One sunny day, Max was playing with his string in the yard. A little bird came to Max and said, "Please don't disturb my nest in the tree." Max looked up and saw the nest. He said, "I won't disturb your nest, little bird." Max played with his string all day, but he was careful not to disturb the bird's nest. The little bird was happy and thanked Max for being so nice. They became good friends and played together every day. And Max always remembered to be loyal to his new friend and never disturb the nest.

One day, a smelly cat and a big dog were playing near a house. The house had a high roof. The cat and dog liked to play together. They were good friends. The cat said, "Let's play a game. Who can finish running around the house first?" The dog said, "Okay, let's play!" They started running around the house. The cat was fast, but the dog was faster. As they ran, they did not see the big hole near the house. The dog fell into the hole. The cat tried to help the dog, but the hole was too deep. The cat said, "I will go find help!" But the cat could not find anyone to help. The dog stayed in the hole. The cat felt sad. The game did not have a happy ending. The cat and dog learned to be careful when playing near the house.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Lily. She had a fragile, gold cup that she loved very much. Her mom told her, "Lily, be careful with the gold cup. It can break." One day, Lily's friend, Tim, came to play. They played with toys and had fun. Then, Lily wanted to show Tim her gold cup. She took it out and said, "Look, Tim! This is my gold cup. It is very pretty, but we cannot play with it." Tim looked at the gold cup and said, "Wow, it is so shiny!" They put the cup on a high shelf so it would be safe. After that, they played more with their toys. When it was time for Tim to go home, they both stood by the door and said, "Bye, see you later!" Lily was happy that her gold cup was safe and that she had a fun day with her friend. She knew that she needed to be careful with her fragile, gold cup. From that day on, she always kept it on the high shelf and only showed it to her friends when they came to visit.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to eat pasta. One day, she wanted to cook pasta all by herself. So, she asked her mom to help her. Her mom said, "Lily, you need to stay organized when you cook." Lily nodded and began to cook the pasta. She stayed organized by putting all the things she needed in one place. She put the pasta in a big pot with water. She waited for the water to get hot. When the pasta was done, Lily was very happy. She had stayed organized and cooked her favorite food all by herself. Her mom was proud of her. They both sat down and ate the yummy pasta together.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to eat yogurt. One day, her mom told her not to eat all the yogurt. "Save some for your brother," she said. This reminded Lily to share with others. But Lily was very sorry because she could not stop eating the yummy yogurt. She ate all of it. Her brother came home and saw that there was no yogurt left. He was sad and angry. Lily felt bad for not sharing. In the end, Lily and her brother were both unhappy. Lily learned that being greedy is not good. She promised to share next time. The moral of the story is to always share with others and not be greedy.
