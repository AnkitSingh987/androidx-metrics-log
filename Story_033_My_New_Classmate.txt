Title: My New Classmate
Category: Beginner

It is the beginning of the school year. John is eleven years old. He walks into his classroom. It is Room 12. 
He looks at the desks. The teacher, Mrs. Smith, put name cards on the desks. John looks for his name. He finds it near the back of the room. He sits down. 

There is an empty desk next to John. He wonders who will sit there. He hopes it is his friend, Peter. But Peter is sitting at the front of the room. 
Then, a boy walks in. He is tall and has red hair. John does not know him. He is a new boy. 
The boy walks down the aisle. He looks at the name cards. He stops at the empty desk next to John. His name card says 'William'. 
The boy sits down. He puts his bag on the floor. 

"Hello," John says. "My name is John."
The boy looks at him. "Hello, John. I am William."
"Are you new to this school?" John asks. 
"Yes," William answers. "I moved here from London last week. My house is near the park."
"I live near the park too," John says. "It is a nice park."

Mrs. Smith walks into the room. "Good morning, class," she says. "Welcome to a new year."
The class is quiet. They listen to the teacher. She talks about the rules. She talks about the subjects they will learn. 
John listens, but he also looks at his new classmate. William has a very nice pencil case. It is black with a picture of a silver car. 

"I like your pencil case," John whispers when Mrs. Smith writes on the board. 
William smiles. "Thank you. I like cars. I like fast cars."
"Me too," John says. "Do you watch the car races on television?"
"Yes, every Sunday with my father," William says. 

Mrs. Smith turns around. "John and William," she says. Her voice is not angry, but it is firm. "Please stop talking. You must listen."
"Sorry, Mrs. Smith," they say together. They look down at their desks. 

The morning passes. They have an English class and a History class. William is very quiet in class. He does not raise his hand. John thinks William is a little shy. 
At eleven o'clock, it is time for the long break. The children call it recess. 

John stands up. He takes an apple from his bag. 
William sits at his desk. He does not have a snack. He just looks at his hands. 
"Do you want to come outside?" John asks his new classmate. 
"I don't know anyone here," William says. "I have no friends to play with."
"You know me," John says. "You can play with me and my friends. We are going to play football on the field."

William looks up. His eyes are bright. "Football? I love football. I am a goalkeeper."
"That is perfect," John says. "Our team needs a good goalkeeper. Come on!"
They walk out of the classroom together. They walk to the big green field behind the school. 

John's friends are already there. Peter has a black and white football. 
"Hey, John!" Peter shouts. "Hurry up! We want to start the game."
John runs to Peter. William runs behind him. 
"Guys, this is William," John says to his friends. "He is new in our class. He sits next to me. And he is a goalkeeper."
"Nice to meet you, William," Peter says. "Get in the goal! We are playing against the boys from Room 14."

William runs to the goal. The goal is two blue coats on the grass. 
The game starts. It is a fast and exciting game. The boys from Room 14 are very good. They run fast and kick the ball hard. 
But William is a fantastic goalkeeper. He jumps high. He dives on the grass. He catches the ball many times. He stops three goals. 
"Great save, William!" John shouts. 

At the end of recess, the game is over. John's team wins. The score is two to zero. 
The boys are tired and sweaty. They walk back to the school building. 
"You are a great goalkeeper," Peter says to William. "You must play with us every day."
"Thank you," William says. He is smiling a very big smile. He is not shy anymore. 

John and William walk back into Room 12. They sit at their desks. 
"That was fun," William says. 
"Yes, it was," John agrees. 
Mrs. Smith starts the next lesson. It is Science. 
John looks at his new classmate. He is glad William sits next to him. They like cars, they live near the park, and they both love football. John thinks they are going to be very good friends.
