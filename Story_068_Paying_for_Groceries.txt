Title: Paying for Groceries
Category: Beginner

Emma and her mother are at the big supermarket. Their shopping cart is completely full. They have fruit, vegetables, cereal, milk, cheese, and a fresh loaf of bread. 
"We have everything we need," Emma's mother says. "Now, we must go to the checkout to pay."

They push the heavy cart to the front of the supermarket. There are many checkout lanes. Some lanes are very busy, and people are waiting in long lines. 
"Let's go to lane number four," her mother says. "The line is short there."
They push their cart behind a man with a small basket. They wait quietly. 

Emma looks at the checkout area. It is a very interesting place. There is a long, black rubber belt. It looks like a small road. The cashier sits behind the belt. 
The man in front of them puts his items on the black belt. He has a newspaper, a bottle of water, and an apple. The cashier scans his items. Beep, beep, beep. The man pays with a few coins, takes his things, and leaves. 

"Okay, Emma. It is our turn," her mother says. "We have to work together as a team. We must put all our food on the belt."
"I will help," Emma says. 

Emma takes the light things from the cart. She takes the box of chocolate cereal and puts it on the black belt. She takes the loaf of bread and puts it gently on the belt. She takes the carton of eggs. 
Her mother takes the heavy things. She takes the big bottles of milk. She takes the bag of potatoes. She takes the fruit and vegetables. 

Soon, the shopping cart is empty, and the black belt is covered with their food. 
The cashier is a young man with a red shirt. He smiles at Emma. 
"Hello," he says. "Did you find everything you needed today?"
"Yes, thank you," Emma's mother replies. 

The cashier starts his work. He picks up the box of cereal and passes it over the red laser light. 
Beep! 
The price appears on a small screen. 
He picks up the milk. Beep! 
He picks up the cheese. Beep! 
His hands move very fast. He slides all the food down to the end of the counter. 

"Emma, can you go to the end of the counter and help pack the bags?" her mother asks. 
"Yes," Emma says. She walks to the end. There are some strong, reusable cloth bags waiting for her. 
Her mother comes to help. 
"Remember," her mother says. "Heavy things at the bottom, light things at the top. We do not want to squash the bread."

Emma puts the potatoes and the milk at the bottom of the first bag. Then, she puts the apples and the bananas on top. 
Her mother puts the cereal and the cheese in the second bag. She puts the soft bread and the fragile eggs carefully at the very top. 
They work quickly, packing the bags while the cashier finishes scanning. 

"That will be sixty-five dollars and twenty cents, please," the cashier says. 
He looks at Emma's mother. 
"Are you paying with cash or a card?" he asks. 
"I will pay with a card, please," her mother says. 

She opens her purse and takes out a small plastic bank card. She pushes the card into a small black machine on the counter. 
The machine asks for her secret number. Her mother types four numbers on the keypad. She covers the keypad with her hand so no one can see her secret number. 
The machine says 'Processing'. Then, it makes a happy beep sound. 'Approved'. 

"Thank you," the cashier says. He pulls a long white receipt from the register. He hands it to Emma's mother. 
"This is your receipt," he says. "Have a wonderful weekend."
"Thank you. You too," her mother says. She puts her bank card and the receipt back into her purse. 

Emma picks up one of the cloth bags. It is quite heavy, but she is strong. Her mother picks up the other bag. 
They walk out of the supermarket and through the automatic glass doors. 
"That was a lot of food," Emma says, carrying her heavy bag to the car. 
"Yes, it was," her mother says. "But now the fridge will be full, and we will have nice food for the whole week."
Emma puts her bag in the back of the car. She is happy the shopping is finished. Paying for groceries is the last step, and now they can finally go home.
