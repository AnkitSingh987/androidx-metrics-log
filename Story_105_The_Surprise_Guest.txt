Title: The Surprise Guest
Category: Beginner

Saturday afternoon was finally here. The 'Mystery Mansion' escape room was a massive success. Mia and her five friends had a brilliant time. They worked together, solved difficult puzzles, and managed to unlock the final door with only three minutes left on the clock. They felt very smart and very tired. 

Now, they were all back at Mia's house. The dining room looked fantastic. The silver and dark blue balloons floated near the ceiling, and the 'Happy Birthday' banner hung proudly on the wall. The table was set with white plates, and on each plate sat a small detective magnifying glass. 
Her friends loved the magnifying glasses. They were all wearing them over one eye and pretending to investigate the pizza. 

"This is the best party ever, Mia," her friend Chloe said, taking a large bite of cheese pizza. "That escape room was so stressful but so much fun."
"I agree," Noah added. "I really thought we were going to be trapped in that room forever when we couldn't figure out the code for the padlock."
"But Maya figured it out!" Mia laughed, pointing at her friend. "Maya is a genius."

They were talking loudly, laughing, and drinking soda. Mia's parents were in the kitchen, giving the teenagers some space to enjoy themselves. 

Suddenly, the front doorbell rang. 
'Ding-dong!'
Mia frowned. She looked around the table. Chloe, Maya, Leo, Noah, and Zoe were all sitting there. All her invited friends were already at the party. 
"Are you expecting someone else?" Leo asked, putting his pizza down. 
"No," Mia said, confused. "I didn't invite anyone else. Maybe it is the postman delivering a package."

Mia's father walked out of the kitchen and went to the front door. He opened it, and Mia heard him say, "Well, hello there! What a wonderful surprise!"
Mia stood up from the table and walked into the hallway. 

Standing in the doorway was a tall young man with a large backpack over his shoulder. He had a big, warm smile on his face. 
Mia stopped completely still. She could not believe her eyes. 
"Daniel?" she gasped. 

It was her older brother, Daniel. Daniel was nineteen years old, and he was currently studying at a university in another city, four hours away by train. He had told Mia on the phone that he had too many exams to study for and could not come home for her birthday. 

"Happy fifteenth birthday, little sister!" Daniel laughed, dropping his heavy backpack on the floor and opening his arms. 
Mia ran across the hallway and hugged him tightly. She was so happy she almost cried. 
"What are you doing here?" she demanded, looking up at him. "You said you had to study for your exams!"

"I lied," Daniel grinned mischievously. "I finished my hardest exam yesterday afternoon. Mom and Dad knew I was coming. We planned it as a secret surprise. I caught the early train this morning."
Mia looked at her father, who was smiling broadly. "You kept a secret from me?" she asked him, pretending to be angry. 
"We thought you would appreciate the surprise," her father chuckled. 

"Come into the dining room," Mia said, grabbing Daniel's hand. "My friends are here, and we just finished the escape room."
Daniel followed her into the dining room. He said hello to all her friends. He was much older, but he was always very friendly and funny with Mia's group. 

"Well," Mia's mother said, walking in from the kitchen carrying the huge, two-layer chocolate cake. "Since the surprise guest has arrived, I think it is time for the main event."
The cake looked magnificent. There were fifteen small candles burning brightly on top. 

Everyone gathered around the table. They sang 'Happy Birthday' loudly, and Daniel sang the loudest of all. 
Mia closed her eyes. She thought about her wish. She wished for more days exactly like this one. She blew out all fifteen candles in one deep breath. Everyone clapped and cheered. 

"You baked this yourself, didn't you?" Daniel asked, looking at the thick chocolate frosting. 
"I did," Mia said proudly. "Mom only supervised."
"Well, cut me a huge slice," Daniel said, rubbing his hands together. "I haven't eaten anything since breakfast, and university food is terrible."

Mia cut the cake and handed out the slices. The chocolate was rich and moist, just as they had planned. She sat at the table surrounded by her best friends, eating delicious cake, and talking to her brother who had traveled a long way just for her. The escape room was a great adventure, but having her whole family together was the best birthday present she could ever ask for.
