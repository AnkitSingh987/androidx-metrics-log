Title: A Package Arrives
Category: Beginner

John loves to read. He reads every day. He reads in the morning before work. He reads in the evening after work. He reads on the bus. He reads in the park. 
His apartment has many books. There are books on shelves. There are books on the table. There are books near the bed. 

Last week, John ordered a new book on the computer. It is a very big book. It is a book about history. He is very excited to read it. 
The computer said the book will arrive on Tuesday. Today is Tuesday. 
John is at home. It is his day off. He is waiting for the book. 

He sits in his living room. He looks out the window. He looks at the street. He is watching for a delivery van. A delivery van brings packages. 
It is ten o'clock. No van. 
It is eleven o'clock. No van. 
John makes a cup of tea. He drinks his tea slowly. He tries to read an old book, but he cannot focus. He is thinking about the new book. 

At one o'clock in the afternoon, he hears a loud sound. 
Vrooom! 
A big white van stops in front of his building. The van has writing on the side. It says 'Fast Delivery'. 
John stands up. He is excited. "My book is here!" he says. 

A man gets out of the van. The man is wearing a blue uniform. He is carrying a square brown box. He walks to the front door of the building. 
John's apartment is on the first floor. He opens his apartment door. He walks into the hall. He waits. 

The delivery man comes up the stairs. He looks at the numbers on the doors. He stops at John's door. 
"Hello," the delivery man says. "Are you John Smith?"
"Yes, I am," John says. 
"I have a package for you," the man says. He holds out the brown box. 
The box is heavy. John can see his name on the box. He can see his address. 

"Please sign here," the delivery man says. He gives John an electronic machine and a plastic pen. 
John takes the pen. He writes his name on the screen of the machine. He signs for the package. 
"Thank you," John says. He takes the box. It is heavy. He likes heavy books. 
"You are welcome. Have a good day," the man says. He walks back down the stairs. 

John carries the box into his apartment. He closes the door. He puts the box on the kitchen table. 
The box is closed with brown tape. It is strong tape. 
John cannot open it with his hands. He needs something sharp. 
He goes to the kitchen drawer. He takes out a pair of scissors. The scissors are silver and sharp. 

He takes the scissors to the table. He carefully cuts the brown tape. Snipp, snipp, snipp. He cuts the tape along the top of the box. 
He puts the scissors down. He opens the top flaps of the box. 
Inside the box, there is a lot of crumpled paper. The paper is there to protect the book. 
John pulls out the crumpled paper. He throws it on the floor. He will put it in the bin later. 

Under the paper, he sees his prize. 
It is his new book. 
He lifts the book out of the box. It is very heavy. It has a beautiful red cover. There are gold letters on the cover. The title is 'A History of the World'. 
John smiles. The book smells like new paper and ink. He loves that smell. 

He opens the book. The pages are crisp and white. He looks at the first page. He reads the first sentence. 
He is very happy. The wait is over. The package is here. The book is perfect. 

He takes the empty brown box. He takes the crumpled paper from the floor. He puts the paper back in the box. He takes the box to the recycling bin outside. 
He washes his hands. 
Then he goes back to the living room. He sits on his favorite green chair. He turns on a reading lamp. The light shines on the red cover. 
He opens his new book. He starts to read chapter one. The world around him disappears. He is in the world of history now. 
A package in the mail is a wonderful thing. It brings joy right to your door.
