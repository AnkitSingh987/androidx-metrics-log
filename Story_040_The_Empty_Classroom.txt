Title: The Empty Classroom
Category: Beginner

The school day is over. It is four o'clock. All the students went home a long time ago. All the teachers went home too. 
The big school building is quiet. There is no shouting. There is no running. There is no laughing. 

Leo is walking back to the school. He is a young boy. He is walking fast. He looks worried. 
He arrived home at half past three. He wanted to do his homework. But when he opened his bag, he realized something. He forgot his math book. He needs his math book to do his homework. His mother said, "Go back to the school quickly. The door might still be open."

Leo reaches the school gates. They are open. He runs across the playground. The playground looks very big and empty without the children. 
He goes to the front door of the school. He pulls the handle. 
Click. 
The door opens. He is lucky. The cleaner is still in the building, so the door is not locked. 

Leo steps inside. The hallway is long and dark. Only one small light is on. It feels strange. During the day, the hallway is full of noise and people. Now, it is silent. Leo's shoes make a loud noise on the floor. Tap, tap, tap. The sound echoes in the empty hall. 

He walks to his classroom. It is Room 4. 
The door is closed. He turns the handle and pushes the door. It opens with a small squeak. 
Leo walks into the empty classroom. 

It looks very different. The afternoon sun is shining through the windows. The light hits the dust in the air. The dust looks like tiny golden stars. 
All the chairs are upside down on top of the desks. The cleaner put them there to sweep the floor. The floor is clean and shiny. 
The blackboard is completely clean. There is no white chalk on it. 
The room smells like cleaning soap and old paper. 

Leo stands at the door for a minute. He listens. He can only hear the sound of a clock ticking on the wall. Tick, tock, tick, tock. 
It is a little spooky, but it is also very peaceful. 

He remembers why he is here. He needs his math book. 
He walks to his desk. His desk is in the third row. He carefully lifts the upside-down chair. He puts it on the floor. 
He bends down and looks inside his desk drawer. 
It is dark inside the drawer. He puts his hand in. He feels a broken pencil. He feels a piece of paper. And then, he feels a thick book. 

He pulls it out. It is his red math book. 
"Yes," Leo whispers. He is very happy. He found it. 

He holds the red book tightly. He must put the chair back. He lifts the chair and puts it upside down on the desk again. He leaves the room exactly as he found it. 
He turns to leave. But he stops for one more moment. 

He looks at the teacher's big desk at the front. He looks at the colourful posters on the wall. He looks at the empty desks waiting for the children tomorrow. 
He likes the empty classroom. It is like a secret place. It is a place that is usually full of action, resting quietly. 
He takes a deep breath of the soapy air. 

Then, he hears a noise in the hallway. 
Clatter, clank! 
It is the cleaner with his bucket and mop. 
Leo jumps a little. The noise breaks the silence. 
It is time to go. He does not want the cleaner to find him here. 

He walks quickly out of Room 4. He closes the door quietly behind him. 
He runs down the dark hallway. He runs out the front door into the bright afternoon sun. He runs across the empty playground. 

He walks home with a fast step. He has his red math book. He can do his homework now. 
But he will always remember the feeling of the empty classroom. The golden dust, the ticking clock, and the quiet peace. It was a strange adventure at the end of a normal school day.
