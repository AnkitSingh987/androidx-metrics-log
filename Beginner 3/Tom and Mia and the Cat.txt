Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Mia and the Cat

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play together. One day, they want to play music. They have a big box of toys. They search the box for music things. "Look, I found a drum!" Tom says. He bangs the drum with his hands. It makes a loud noise. "I found a flute!" Mia says. She blows the flute with her mouth. It makes a high noise. They make music with the drum and the flute. They are happy. But then, they hear a silly noise. It is not from the drum or the flute. It is from the box. They stop playing and look at the box. "What is that?" Tom asks. "Let's see!" Mia says. They open the box. They see a cat. The cat is inside the box. The cat was making the silly noise. The cat meows at them. "Hello, cat!" Tom and Mia say. They laugh. They pet the cat. The cat purrs. They think the cat is funny. They make music with the cat. They use the drum, the flute, and the cat. They have fun. They are friends.

Once upon a time, there was a humble cat named Kitty. Kitty liked to watch videos on the big box. One day, Kitty noticed a new video about birds. Kitty loved birds. She wanted to watch the video all day. Kitty's friend, Doggy, came to visit. Doggy saw the video too. Doggy wanted to play outside, but Kitty wanted to watch the video. Doggy felt sad. Kitty did not notice that Doggy was sad. Doggy went outside alone. Kitty watched the video and did not play with Doggy. Later, Doggy went away and did not come back. Kitty was sad. She missed her friend. The video was not fun anymore.

One day, a cheerful girl named Lily wanted to take her stamps to the park. She loved her stamps very much. They were her favorite toy. She put them in her bag and went to the park with her mom. At the park, Lily met a boy named Tim. They played together and had fun. But then, Lily saw that Tim had her stamps. She asked him to give them back, but Tim said no. Lily was sad and did not know what to do. Lily's mom saw her sad face and asked what was wrong. Lily told her mom about the stamps. Her mom talked to Tim's mom, and Tim gave the stamps back to Lily. Lily was happy again, and they all played together. In the end, Lily and Tim became friends and shared their toys.

Once upon a time, there was a big, tough bear. He lived in a cave near the woods. He liked to wrap himself in a warm, soft blanket when he slept. The bear had many friends in the world, like birds, rabbits, and squirrels. One day, the bear found a small, weak bird in the woods. The bird was cold and scared. The bear wanted to help the bird, so he took the bird to his cave. He wrapped the bird in his warm, soft blanket. The bird felt safe and happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The weak bird started to grow bigger and stronger. The bird became a big, strong eagle! The eagle thanked the bear for helping him. The bear and the eagle became best friends. They learned that even the smallest act of kindness can change someone's world.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to wonder about many things. One day, she went outside to play with her ball. While she was playing, she met a new friend. His name was Tim. Tim was very generous and shared his toys with Sue. Sue and Tim played together all day. They laughed and had fun. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew their ball far away. They were sad and didn't know what to do. But then, a bird flew down from the sky with the ball in its beak. The bird dropped the ball right in front of Sue and Tim. They were so surprised and happy! They thanked the bird and played with their ball again. Sue and Tim were best friends forever.
