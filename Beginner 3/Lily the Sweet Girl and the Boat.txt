Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Sweet Girl and the Boat

Once upon a time, there was a sweet girl named Lily. She loved to learn new things. One day, she got a model toy from her mom. It was a model of a big boat. Lily was very happy and wanted to show it to her friend, Tom. Lily went to Tom's house to show him the model. "Look, Tom! I got a new toy! It's a model boat!" she said. Tom looked at the boat and thought it was very cool. He wanted to learn how to make one too. They played with the model boat all day. But then, a big wind came and blew the model boat away. Lily and Tom tried to catch it, but they couldn't. The boat fell into a big puddle and broke. Lily and Tom were very sad. They learned that sometimes, things can break and not be fixed.

Tom and Lily were playing in the garden. They saw a big tree with a hole in it. They wanted to see what was inside. "Let's go and look," Tom said. "OK, but be careful," Lily said. They walked to the tree and peeked into the hole. They saw a shiny metal box. It had a lock on it. "Wow, what is that?" Tom asked. "I don't know, maybe it's a treasure," Lily said. They tried to open the box, but it was too hard. They needed a key. "Maybe we can find the key somewhere," Tom said. "Maybe it's in the house," Lily said. They ran to the house and looked for the key. They searched in the kitchen, the living room, the bedroom, but they could not find it. They were about to give up when they saw a picture on the wall. It was a picture of their grandma and grandpa. They were holding a pie and smiling. "Look, it's grandma and grandpa," Lily said. "They look happy," Tom said. They looked closer at the picture. They saw something shiny in grandma's hand. It was a key. "Look, grandma has the key," Tom said. "Maybe she can help us," Lily said. They took the picture off the wall and ran to the phone. They called grandma and told her about the box in the tree. "Hello, Tom and Lily," grandma said. "Hello, grandma, we need your help," Tom said. "What is it, my dears?" grandma asked. "We found a box in the tree, but we can't open it. We need the key. You have the key, grandma, we saw it in the picture," Lily said. "Oh, that box. That's an ancient box. It belongs to your grandpa. He found it when he was a boy. He gave me the key as a gift. He said it was a secret," grandma said. "What's inside the box, grandma?" Tom asked. "I don't know, my dear. I never opened it. Your grandpa said it was a surprise. He said he would provide us with a clue when we were ready," grandma said. "Can we have the key, grandma? Can we open the box?" Lily asked. "Well, I suppose you can. But you have to promise me something. You have to share the surprise with me. And you have to bring me some pie. I love pie," grandma said. "OK, grandma, we promise. We will share the surprise with you. And we will bring you some pie. Can you come to our house?" Tom asked. "Of course, my dears. I'll be there soon. Wait for me," grandma said. Tom and Lily hung up the phone. They were excited. They wondered what was inside the ancient box. They hoped it was something good. They also hoped there was some pie left in the kitchen. They ran to check.

Once upon a time, there was a creative little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her toy truck. She would load it up with her blocks and drive it around her room. One day, she found a big box in the corner of her room. Sue got excited and decided to load her toy truck with the things inside the box. She found a funny picture that looked like bones. Her mom told her it was called an x-ray. Sue thought it was cool and put the x-ray on her truck. As Sue was driving her truck around, something unexpected happened. The x-ray started to glow! Sue was surprised and didn't know what to do. She called her mom and showed her the glowing x-ray. Her mom looked at it and laughed. She had put a glow-in-the-dark sticker on the x-ray as a surprise for Sue. They both laughed and played with the glowing x-ray and the toy truck together.
