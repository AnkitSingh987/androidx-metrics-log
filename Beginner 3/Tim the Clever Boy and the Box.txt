Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Clever Boy and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a clever boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy motorcycle. Tim would ride it all around his house and make "vroom vroom" sounds. One day, Tim wanted to see if his motorcycle could fit in a small box. He tried to push it in, but it was too big. Tim was sad and didn't know what to do. Then, Tim had an idea. He took off the wheels of the motorcycle and tried again. This time, it fit in the box! Tim was so happy and proud of himself. He learned that sometimes, you have to be clever to solve a problem.

One day, a little dog named Bella went for a walk with her friend, a big cat named Tom. They were very happy to play outside. Bella was an obedient dog and always listened to her mom. During their walk, they found two paths. One path was long and the other was short. Bella and Tom had to choose which way to go. Bella wanted to go on the short path, but Tom wanted to go on the long path. They thought about it for a moment. Then, Bella saw a toy near the short path. She wanted to play with it, so she chose the short path. Tom agreed and they both walked on the short path. They had lots of fun playing with the toy. On their way back, Bella hurt her leg a little, but Tom helped her walk home. They were good friends and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a mom and her daughter. They lived in a small house with a big yard. In the yard, there were many harmless bugs that the daughter liked to play with. One day, a big bird flew into the yard. The bird saw the bugs and wanted to eat them. The daughter did not want the bird to eat her bug friends. She had an idea to stop the bird. The daughter ran to her room and grabbed her big, red ball. She went outside and threw the ball high in the air. The bird saw the ball and flew after it. The daughter saved her bug friends and the bird found other food to eat. From that day on, the daughter, the bugs, and the bird all lived happily in the yard.

Once upon a time, there was a little skeleton named Skelly. Skelly lived in a small house in the woods. One day, Skelly was feeling frustrated. He wanted to play, but he had no friends to play with. Skelly went outside and saw a big green frog. The frog could spring high into the air. Skelly said, "Hi, frog! Can we play together?" The frog said, "Yes, Skelly! Let's play!" Skelly and the frog played all day. They had so much fun. Skelly was not frustrated anymore. The frog and Skelly became best friends. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little bear. His name was Fred and he loved to play. One day, Fred decided to help do the laundry. But as he grabbed a basket of clothes, he suddenly heard a loud rip! He looked around, alert. He had torn one of his mother's shirts! Fred was worried, he didn't know what to do. Should he tell his mom he had broken her shirt? He ran to his room and started to cry. But then his mom came up to Fred's room and hugged him. She said not to worry, she would be able to fix the shirt. She must have seen Fred was worried, so she asked him if he would like to help her fold the laundry. Fred was so happy he got to help with the laundry. He was very careful and was so proud of helping his mom. Fred had learned his lesson and was much more alert when he was helping with the laundry from now on. The End.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He was very eager to meet new friends. One day, Tom went to the park with his mom. He saw many kids playing and having fun. Tom saw a girl named Sue. She had a toy car with a zip on it. Tom wanted to meet Sue and play with the toy car. He walked up to her and said, "Hi, I am Tom. Can I play with your toy car?" Sue was happy to meet Tom. She said, "Hi Tom! My name is Sue. You can play with my toy car." They played with the toy car and had lots of fun. Tom and Sue became good friends. They played together every day, and they were always eager to meet at the park.
