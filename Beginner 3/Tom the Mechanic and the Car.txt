Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Mechanic and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a mechanic named Tom. He liked to fix cars. One day, he had to prepare a car for a big race. Tom was excited to help. As Tom worked on the car, he hurt his hand. "Ouch!" he said. He was sad because he could not fix the car anymore. He sat down and thought about what to do. Suddenly, a big, friendly dog came into the shop. The dog picked up Tom's tools with its mouth and started to fix the car! Tom was surprised and happy. The dog helped Tom finish the car for the big race. They became best friends and worked together to fix many more cars.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had a flea. The flea was wild and liked to jump. One day, Spot met a big dog named Max. Max was always cold and would shiver a lot. Spot and Max became friends. "Max, why do you shiver so much?" Spot asked. Max said, "I don't know, but I wish I could feel warm like you." One day, the wild flea jumped from Spot to Max. Max stopped shivering. He felt warm and happy. Spot saw this and knew the wild flea had helped Max. "Max, I think my flea friend helped you feel warm," Spot said. Max smiled and said, "Thank you, Spot. Now we can play without me shivering!" And they played happily ever after.

One day, a man wanted to get cheap hay for his animals. He went to a farm where they sold hay. He asked the farmer, "Do you have cheap hay?" The farmer said, "Yes, I have cheap hay. But it is not good for animals. They may get sick." The man did not listen and took the cheap hay for his animals. He gave the hay to his animals. They ate the hay, but they did not feel good. The man was sad. He should have listened to the farmer. Now, his animals were not happy, and the man had to find new hay to make them feel better.

One day, a compassionate cat named Tom met a little bird named Tim. They became friends and liked to play together. Tim liked to fly and Tom liked to chase him. They had a lot of fun. One day, Tim got stuck in a tree. He cried, "Help me, Tom! I can't get out!" Tom tried to climb the tree to save his friend. He thought of a plan. Tom found a cut branch on the ground and used it to help Tim. But then, a big dog came and scared them. The dog barked loudly and chased Tom and Tim. They ran and ran, but the dog was fast. They tried to hide, but the dog found them. Tom and Tim were very scared. They didn't know what to do. The dog kept barking and getting closer. They wished for help, but no one came. In the end, the dog caught Tim and Tom. They were very sad and scared. The fun day they had planned was over. The friends had to find a new way to be safe and happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with bricks. He had many bricks to build with. He liked to make tall towers with his bricks. They were his original towers. One sunny day, Tim was building a tower with his bricks. He wanted to make it very tall. He put brick after brick to make it grow. Tim was very happy with his tower. Then, Tim had an idea. He wanted to fill his tower with something fun. Tim went to the kitchen and found a big bowl. He filled the bowl with yummy jelly. He carried the bowl back to his tower. Tim started to fill his tower with the jelly. The tower was now full of jelly. Suddenly, the tower started to shake. The bricks could not hold the jelly. The tower fell down with a big crash. Tim was sad, but then he saw something unexpected. The jelly had made a fun slide on the floor. Tim smiled and played on the jelly slide. He had fun even though his tower was gone. Tim learned that sometimes, unexpected things can be fun too.
