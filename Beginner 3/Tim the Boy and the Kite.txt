Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Kite

Once upon a time, in a big park, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy car. He would make it zoom all around. One day, Tim saw a high tree with a big kite stuck in it. He wanted to get the kite down, but he was too small. A tall figure walked by and saw Tim looking sad. It was a kind man named Jack. Jack asked Tim what was wrong. Tim told Jack about the kite stuck in the high tree. Jack smiled and said he would help Tim get the kite down. Jack climbed the tree and got the kite down for Tim. Tim was very happy and thanked Jack. They played with the kite and the toy car together. The wind made the kite zoom high in the sky. Tim learned that it is good to ask for help and to help others when they need it. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Sam. She loved to wear her favorite dress, which was blue with a big pink bow. One day, Sam went for a walk in the woods with her mom. Suddenly, they heard a noise. "What was that?" Sam asked. "I'm not sure," her mom replied. "But stay close to me!" The noise got louder, until they saw a man walking towards them. He was wearing a terrifying mask and carrying a big weapon. "Oh no!" Sam's mom said. "Let's go!" She grabbed Sam and they ran away, but the man chased after them. Finally, they reached a big house. Sam's mom opened the door and they quickly ran inside. The man stopped outside and shouted, "You can't escape! I'll get you!" Sam and her mom were so scared. They hid behind the couch and waited for a long time. Finally, the noise stopped. Sam's mom peeked outside, but the man was gone. "We are safe now," Sam's mom said. She hugged Sam tightly. "Let's never go for a walk in the woods again." Sam nodded. She never wanted to experience something so terrible again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a new ball that she loved to play with. One day, she went outside to play with her ball in the sun. She saw a hedge near her house. Sue thought it was a great place to play. Sue kicked her new ball around, having lots of fun. But then, her ball went into the hedge. She was sad and did not know what to do. Sue asked her mom for help. Her mom said, "Wait here, I will get something to help you." Her mom came back with a long stick. Sue asked, "What is that for?" Her mom said, "Watch and see." Her mom used the stick to get the ball out of the hedge. Sue was so happy! She thanked her mom and went back to playing with her new ball.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a box. The box had a label on it. Tim could not read the label, so he asked his mom to help him. His mom looked at the label and said, "This is a box of toys, Tim!" Tim was very excited and opened the box. Inside, he found a toy bear. The bear looked sad and miserable. Tim wanted to make the bear happy, so he gave it a big hug. "Don't worry, bear," Tim said, "I will be your friend!" As Tim hugged the bear, something unexpected happened. The bear started to smile and talk! "Thank you, Tim," said the bear, "I was sad because I was all alone in that box. Now that you found me, I am happy!" Tim and the bear became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play by the sea. One day, she saw a big wave coming. She was scared, but her mom said, "Don't worry, it will vanish." The next day, Lily and her mom went to the sea again. Lily wore her comfortable shoes. She saw a little crab and said, "Hello, little crab! Have you seen the big wave?" The crab said, "Yes, but don't worry, it will vanish." Later, Lily saw the big wave again. She remembered what her mom and the crab said. She was not scared this time. She watched as the big wave came closer and closer. Then, just like her mom and the crab said, the big wave vanished. Lily smiled and played by the sea, happy and safe.
