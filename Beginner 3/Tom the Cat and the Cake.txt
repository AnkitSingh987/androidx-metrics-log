Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Cat and the Cake

One day, a cat named Tom wanted to play golf. Tom had never tried golf before, but he thought it would be fun. He managed to find a golf ball and a stick to hit it with. Tom was very excited to play. As Tom played golf, he saw a big tasty cake on a table nearby. He wanted to eat the cake, but he knew he had to finish his golf game first. Tom tried to focus on the golf, but the cake looked so tasty that he couldn't stop thinking about it. Tom decided to hit the golf ball as hard as he could, hoping to finish the game quickly so he could eat the cake. But the ball flew too far and hit the cake! The cake fell on the ground and got dirty. Tom was very sad because he couldn't eat the cake anymore, and his golf game was over too.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a man and his dog. The man had a long bottle of wine that he loved very much. He wanted to protect it from everyone. So, he told his dog, "Please protect my wine. Do not let anyone take it." One day, a big, bad wolf came to the house. The wolf saw the long bottle of wine and wanted to take it. The dog said, "No, you cannot have it. My friend told me to protect it." The wolf got angry and said, "I will take it anyway." The dog tried to protect the wine, but the big, bad wolf was too strong. The wolf took the long bottle of wine and ran away. The man came home and saw that his wine was gone. He was very sad. The dog said, "I am sorry. I tried to protect it, but the wolf was too strong." The man and his dog were sad because they could not protect the wine from the big, bad wolf.

Once upon a time, there was a big desk. The desk was rough and brown. It lived in a room with many toys. One day, a toy car wanted to drive on the desk. The car was small and red. It went vroom vroom all over the rough desk. The car had fun driving on the desk. It made friends with the other toys. They all played together on the rough desk. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim wanted to become a big boy and go to school. He knew that in the classroom, he could learn many things and play with his friends. He thought it would be perfect. One day, Tim went to school and saw his friend, Sam. They were both excited to be in the classroom. Tim said, "I want to be the best in class!" Sam said, "No, I want to be the best!" They both wanted to be the best, but they did not know how. Tim and Sam tried to be the best, but they did not help each other. They did not share their toys and they did not play together. They were not happy. The teacher was sad to see them fight. In the end, no one was the best, and Tim and Sam were not friends anymore. The classroom was not perfect for them.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a huge towel in his room. He thought it was very big and fun. He played with the towel, making it into a cape, a blanket, and a tent. He showed his mom the huge towel and said, "Look, Mom! I can do so many things with this big towel!" His mom smiled and said, "That's great, Tim! But be careful not to get it wet. It's a very special towel." Tim played with the towel all day. But then, he spilled some water on it. The huge towel started to shrink! It got smaller and smaller until it was just the size of a toy. Tim was sad and scared. He didn't want his mom to be mad at him. He went to his mom and showed her the tiny towel. "I'm sorry, Mom. I got it wet and it shrank." His mom laughed and said, "It's okay, Tim. I knew it would shrink. That's why it's special. Now you have a little towel to play with, too!" Tim was happy and played with his tiny towel. He learned that even when things don't go as planned, it can still be fun and exciting.
