Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Sue and the Magic

Tom and Sue were friends who liked to play in the garden. They had a big pot that they filled with water and flowers. They pretended it was a magic pot that could make wishes come true. One day, they decided to have a race around the garden. They agreed that the winner would get to make a wish with the magic pot. Tom was fast and strong, but Sue was graceful and smart. She knew a shortcut through the bushes that Tom did not see. "Ready, set, go!" they shouted and ran as fast as they could. Tom was ahead at first, but Sue quickly caught up with him. She slipped through the bushes and reached the pot before him. She was very happy and proud. "I win, I win!" she said. "Now I can make a wish with the magic pot. I wish for a big cake with lots of icing and sprinkles!" But as soon as she said that, the pot started to shake and bubble. The water and flowers spilled out and made a big mess. Sue was shocked and scared. She did not know what was happening. Tom came running and saw the pot. He was angry and sad. He had wanted to make a wish too. He thought Sue had cheated and ruined the pot. "You are a bad friend!" he said. "You took the shortcut and broke the pot. Now we can't play with it anymore. I don't want to play with you!" Sue felt sorry and ashamed. She realized she had been greedy and selfish. She had not thought about Tom's feelings. She had not been a good friend. "I'm sorry, Tom," she said. "I was wrong. I should have shared the pot with you. I should have been fair and kind. Can you forgive me?" Tom looked at her and saw that she was sincere. He remembered that they had been friends for a long time. He decided to forgive her and give her another chance. "It's okay, Sue," he said. "I'm sorry too. I should not have yelled at you. You are still my friend. Can we play together again?" Sue nodded and smiled. They hugged and made up. They decided to clean up the pot and the garden. They realized that the pot was not magic after all. It was just a pot. But their friendship was more precious than any wish. The moral of the story is: Be honest and generous with your friends. Don't let greed and pride ruin your friendship. Friendship is more valuable than anything.

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse named Tim. Tim lived in a small house near the park. He loved to play with his friends and eat cheese. One day, he found a big piece of cheese under a tree. The cheese was pale and looked very yummy. Tim was very happy and wanted to eat it all. As Tim was about to eat the cheese, a big cat came by. The cat saw Tim and the cheese. The cat wanted to eat the cheese too. The cat stared at Tim, and Tim stared back. They both wanted the cheese very much. Tim was scared but did not want to lose his cheese. Tim had an idea. He said to the cat, "Let's share the cheese." The cat agreed, and they both ate the cheese together. After that, Tim and the cat became good friends. They played together in the park and shared more cheese. And they both lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet little cat named Mimi. Mimi loved to play catch with her friends in the park. One sunny day, Mimi and her friends went to the park to play their favorite game. Mimi was very good at catch. She would run and jump to catch the ball. All her friends would clap and cheer for her. After playing for a long time, Mimi and her friends felt tired. They saw a big seat under a tree and decided to rest there. As they sat on the seat, Mimi saw a small bug. She wanted to catch it, but her friend said, "No, Mimi. We should be kind to all living things, even bugs." Mimi listened to her friend and let the bug go. They all learned that being kind is more important than catching bugs. And that was the moral value they learned that day.
