Category: Beginner
Title: The Wise Man and the Story

Once upon a time, there was an old wise man who wanted to tell a story. He invited his grandkids over and said, "I have a story to tell you." The grandkids were excited and asked, "What's the story about?" The wise old man smiled and said, "It's about a little bunny who went on an adventure." The grandkids were very curious and asked, "Can we hear it?" The wise man chuckled and said, "Yes, I'd love to invite you to listen to it." So they all gathered around and listened while the wise man told them the story. After the story was over, the grandkids were filled with happiness and thanked the wise old man for an amazing story. The wise old man smiled and said, "You're welcome! It was my pleasure to share this story with you."

Once upon a time, there was a big structure in the park. It was so big that all the kids loved to play on it. One day, a little boy named Tim and his friend, Sue, went to the park to play on the big structure. Tim said to Sue, "I dare you to climb to the top of the big structure!" Sue was scared, but she wanted to show Tim that she was brave. So, she started to climb the big structure. Tim watched her and cheered her on. As Sue climbed higher and higher, something unexpected happened. A big, friendly bird came and landed on the big structure. The bird said, "Hi, I am here to help you." Sue was so happy to have a new friend. The bird helped Sue climb to the top of the big structure. Tim and Sue laughed and played with their new friend, the big bird, all day long.

Lily and Tom were best friends. They liked to play in the park and share their toys. One day, they found a shiny coin on the ground. Lily picked it up and said, "Look, Tom, we are rich! We can buy anything we want!" Tom was happy too. He said, "Yes, we are rich! Let's go to the candy store and get some sweets!" But on their way to the candy store, they saw a man who was very sad. He was sitting on a bench and had no coat. He was shivering and hungry. Lily and Tom felt sorry for him. Lily said, "Tom, maybe we should give him our coin. He needs it more than we do." Tom said, "But we found it, Lily. It is ours. We can buy candy and have fun." Lily said, "But he has nothing, Tom. He is cold and hungry. We have coats and food and toys. We can share our coin with him. Please, Tom, let's do it. It is a good thing to do." Tom thought for a moment. He liked candy, but he also liked Lily. He said, "Okay, Lily, let's do it. But you have to promise me something." Lily said, "What is it, Tom?" Tom said, "You have to promise me that we will always be friends, no matter what." Lily said, "I promise, Tom. We will always be friends, no matter what." They smiled and hugged. Then they went to the man and gave him their coin. The man was very surprised and grateful. He said, "Thank you, children. You are very kind and generous. You have made my day. God bless you." Lily and Tom felt happy and proud. They had done a good thing. They had kept their promise. They had learned that being rich is not about having money, but about having a good heart.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big pond with ducks and fish. Lily and Ben want to swim with them. "Can we swim, Mom?" Lily asks. "No, dear. The water is filthy. It has dirt and trash. You will get sick," Mom says. "But we have our swim bags. We can put on our suits and goggles," Ben says. "Sorry, Ben. Your swim bags are not magic. They will not keep you clean. The pond is not for swimming. It is for looking," Mom says. Lily and Ben are sad. They want to have fun in the water. They see a shiny thing in the pond. It looks like a toy. "Look, Lily. What is that?" Ben says. "I don't know. Let's get it," Lily says. They run to the edge of the pond. They reach for the shiny thing. It is not a toy. It is a can. A filthy can. "Yuck. This is not fun. This is gross," Lily says. They drop the can. They go back to Mom. They do not want to swim anymore. They want to play on the swings. They are happy again.
