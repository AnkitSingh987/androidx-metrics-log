Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Gray Cat and the Window

Once upon a time, there was a gray cat named Tom. Tom liked to gaze at the birds in the sky. He would sit by the window and watch them fly. One day, Tom found a mask. It was a funny mask with big eyes and a smile. Tom put the mask on and looked at himself in the mirror. He laughed and thought it was fun. Tom wore the mask and went outside to play. The birds saw him and thought he was a new friend. They came down to say hi. Tom was happy to have new friends and they all played together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim wanted to be a successful cook. He loved to taste all the yummy food he made. One day, he decided to make a cake for his mom's birthday. Tim mixed all the things he needed for the cake. But he saw a round thing on the table. It was an ashtray. He thought it was a special bowl for cake mix. So, he put some of the mix into the ashtray. He didn't know it was not for food. When the cake was done, Tim tasted it. It was yummy! He was so happy. He gave the cake to his mom. She tasted it too, and said it was the best cake ever. Tim felt like a successful cook. But then, they found the ashtray mix. They laughed and knew next time to use a real bowl.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely giant named Bob. Bob was big and tall, but he was very nice. He had many friends in the forest. One day, Bob lost his favorite toy, a small red ball. Bob was sad and asked his friend, Cat, "Have you seen my red ball?" Cat shook her head and said, "No, I have not seen it, but I will help you look for it." So, Bob and Cat looked all around the forest for the red ball. As they looked, they asked their friends, Dog and Bird, if they had seen the red ball. Dog and Bird had not seen it, but they wanted to help too. Together, the four friends found Bob's red ball under a big tree. Bob was so happy and said, "Thank you, friends!" They all laughed and played with the red ball together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He loved to discover new things. Today, he wanted to find something useful. He looked under rocks, in the trees, and in the grass. Tim hoped to find something amazing. Then, Tim saw a big event happening in the park. There were many people and lots of fun games. He decided to join in the fun. He played games and ate yummy food. Tim was having a great time, but he still wanted to find something useful. Finally, it was time for the big surprise at the event. Everyone gathered around a big box. They opened it, and out came a cute dog! The dog ran to Tim and licked his face. Tim was so happy. He discovered that the dog was lost and needed a home. Tim knew that the dog would be useful, and they became best friends. The unexpected thing that happened was that Tim found a new friend at the event.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy frog named Fred. Fred lived by a big river. He loved to jump and play in the water all day. One day, Fred met a tiny bug. The bug was sad. "What's wrong?" Fred asked. The bug said, "I fell in the river and now I am wet!" Fred wanted to help. He said, "I can help you dry off. Let's go to the sun!" Fred used his long tongue to pinch the bug. He held the bug up to the sun. The sun was warm and bright. Soon, the bug was dry and happy. The bug said, "Thank you, Fred! You are a good friend." From that day on, Fred and the bug were best friends. They played by the river every day. And they were always there to help each other.
