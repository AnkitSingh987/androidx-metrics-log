Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Park

Tom and Lily were playing in the park with their toy cars. They liked to make roads and bridges with the sand and rocks. They also liked to pretend that the big hill was a volcano that could make fire and smoke. One day, they saw a new boy in the park. He had a big red truck that looked very strong and fast. He drove his truck over the sand and rocks, making a lot of noise and mess. He did not care about Tom and Lily's roads and bridges. He even drove his truck over their volcano, smashing it. Tom and Lily were very sad and angry. They wanted to make him stop, but they were also a little scared. He was bigger than them and his truck was bigger than their cars. They did not know what to do. Then, they had an idea. They remembered that their volcano had a hole in the top, where they put some baking soda and vinegar to make it fizz. They thought that maybe they could use it to scare the new boy away. They quickly rebuilt their volcano, making it bigger and stronger. They also hid some more baking soda and vinegar behind a rock. When the new boy came back with his truck, he saw the new volcano and laughed. He thought it was a silly toy that could not hurt him. He drove his truck towards it, ready to smash it again. But Tom and Lily were ready too. They waited until he was very close, then they threw the baking soda and vinegar into the hole. The volcano erupted, making a loud sound and a lot of foam. The foam sprayed all over the new boy and his truck, making them wet and sticky. The new boy was very surprised and scared. He thought that the volcano was real and that it was angry at him. He dropped his truck and ran away, crying. He did not come back to the park again. Tom and Lily were very happy and proud. They had used their volcano to scare the new boy away. They hugged each other and their cars. They decided that their volcano was very useful and fun. They played with it for a long time, making more fire and smoke. They also shared it with their other friends, who liked it too. They had a lot of fun in the park.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play at the park. They see a big slide. They want to go on the slide. "Let's go on the slide!" Anna says. "OK!" Ben says. They run to the slide. They take turns to go down. They have fun. But then, they see a boy. He is not a regular boy. He is a mean boy. He has a stick. He pokes other kids with the stick. He makes them cry. He sees Anna and Ben. He runs to them. He pokes Anna with the stick. "Ow!" Anna says. "That hurts!" "Stop it!" Ben says. "You are not nice!" The mean boy laughs. He pokes Ben with the stick. "Ow!" Ben says. "That hurts too!" Anna and Ben are sad. They do not like the mean boy. They want him to go away. They see a big girl. She is a regular girl. She is a nice girl. She has a ball. She sees the mean boy. She does not like him either. She throws the ball at him. The ball hits him on the head. He drops the stick. He says "Ow!" He cries. The nice girl runs to Anna and Ben. She gives them a hug. She says "Are you OK?" "Yes, thank you!" Anna and Ben say. They smile. They are happy. They have a new friend. They go back to the slide. They invite the nice girl to join them. They take turns to go down. They have more fun.

One day, Tim went with his mom to the mailbox. The mailbox was big and open. Tim saw a letter inside. The letter was for his mom. Tim wanted to help his mom and give her the letter. "Mom, I will get the letter for you!" said Tim. His mom smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim!" But, a strong wind came. The wind tried to take the letter away. Tim wanted to prevent the wind from taking the letter. He held the letter tight in his hands. "Good job, Tim!" said his mom. "You stopped the wind from taking our letter!" Tim was happy that he could help his mom. They went back home and read the letter together.
