Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Sun

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. It was a sunny day. Tim was happy. He saw a big tree and found an umbrella under it. The umbrella was red and blue. Tim liked the umbrella. Suddenly, the sky turned dark. It started to rain. Tim felt dizzy. He opened the umbrella and held it over his head. The umbrella kept him dry. Tim was not wet from the rain. The rain stopped and the sun came out again. Tim felt better. He was not dizzy anymore. He closed the umbrella and went back home. Tim showed his mom the umbrella. She smiled and said, "Good job, Tim! You stayed dry in the rain!"

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing in the kitchen. She saw a big pot on the stove. Lily wanted to help her mom cook, so she took a spoon and began to stir the soup. She was very gentle with the spoon, so she didn't spill anything. Lily's mom came into the kitchen and saw her stirring the soup. She said, "Thank you, Lily! You are a big help!" Lily smiled, happy to help her mom. She went to the faucet to wash her hands. When she turned the handle, water came out and made her hands clean. Just then, a little frog hopped into the kitchen. Lily saw the frog and gasped. The frog hopped over to the pot of soup and looked at Lily. "Ribbit!" said the frog. "Can I have some soup?" Lily was surprised but she said, "Yes, Mr. Frog. You can have some soup." She gave the frog a small bowl and they all had a nice meal together.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot liked to go for walks on the path near his house. One sunny day, Spot saw a big dog on the path. The big dog barked loudly at Spot, and it made him feel scared. Spot felt ashamed that he was scared of the big dog. He wanted to be brave, but he didn't know how. Spot's friend, a little bird named Tweet, saw that Spot was scared. Tweet flew down to comfort Spot and told him it was okay to feel scared sometimes. Together, Spot and Tweet walked down the path, and the big dog didn't seem so scary anymore. Spot felt better with his friend by his side. From that day on, Spot and Tweet walked on the path together, and Spot wasn't ashamed of being scared anymore.

Once upon a time, in a normal little town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to sit on the soft grass in his yard and play with his toys. One day, while he was sitting, he saw a new face. It was a girl his age. She was his new neighbor! The neighbor, named Sue, came over to Tim. She asked him, "Can I sit and play with you?" Tim said, "Yes!" They sat on the grass together and played with Tim's toys. They laughed and had a lot of fun. As the sun went down, it was time for Sue to go home. Tim and Sue said bye to each other. They knew they would play again the next day. From that day on, Tim and Sue were best friends. They always sat and played together, and their days were happy and full of fun.

Once upon a time, in a big park, a little girl named Mia went to play. Mia had a hoop, and she loved to roll it around. She would run after it and laugh. Mia's hoop was very pretty, and all her friends liked it too. One day, Mia saw a new boy in the park. His name was Tim. Tim did not have a hoop, but he had a big ball. Mia and Tim played together. They took turns with the ball and the hoop. Tim was envious because he liked Mia's hoop a lot. Then, they both discover a new game. They would roll the hoop and try to throw the ball inside it. Mia and Tim had so much fun playing together. They became best friends, and they always shared their toys.
