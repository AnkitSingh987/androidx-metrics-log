Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and the Mouse

Tom is a fat cat. He likes to sleep and eat. He does not like to play or run. He is lazy and grumpy. One day, Tom sees a mouse in the kitchen. The mouse is small and fast. It runs under the table and behind the fridge. Tom thinks the mouse looks tasty. He wants to bite it and eat it. Tom gets up and chases the mouse. He is slow and clumsy. He bumps into the chairs and the pots. He makes a lot of noise. The mouse is smart and sneaky. It dodges and hides. It makes fun of Tom. Tom gets angry and tired. He tries to catch the mouse with his paw. He misses and hits the knife. The knife is sharp and shiny. It makes a cut on Tom's paw. Tom feels pain and blood. He cries and runs away. The mouse is safe and happy. It laughs and cheers. It finds some cheese and nibbles it. It thinks Tom is a silly and fat cat. It does not want to bite him or eat him. It wants to play and run. It is lively and friendly.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Anna. She had a sister named Bella. Anna and Bella liked to play together. One day, they found a big, red ball. They both wanted to play with it, but there was only one ball. Anna said, "Let's split the ball, so we both can play." Bella felt envious because she wanted the ball all to herself. But she knew that sharing was good, so she agreed. They took a big knife and split the ball in half. Now, Anna and Bella each had half of the ball. They played with their halves and had lots of fun. They were happy because they shared and played together. From that day on, they always shared their toys and had fun playing together.

Once upon a time, there was a big, yellow sun. The sun was very happy and excited every day. It loved to shine bright and make everyone feel warm and happy. One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. Tim was a spoiled boy who always wanted more. He looked at the sun and said, "Sun, I want you to be bigger and brighter!" The sun felt sad but wanted to make Tim happy. So, the sun tried to be bigger and brighter. But the sun became too big and too bright. It was too hot for everyone. The trees, flowers, and animals all felt sad and tired. Tim realized he made a mistake. He said, "Sun, please go back to being your normal size and brightness." The sun went back to normal, and everyone was happy again. From that day on, Tim learned not to be spoiled and to be happy with what he had.

Once upon a time, there was a little seed named Sam. Sam wanted to grow big and strong. One day, Sam said, "I want to go on a journey to find the best place to grow!" Sam's friend, a bird named Lily, heard him and said, "I can help you find a good place to grow!" So, Lily picked up Sam with her beak and they went on a journey together. Lily found a comfortable spot for Sam and dropped him there. Sam was happy and started to grow. But then, something unexpected happened. Sam did not grow into a big tree like he thought. He grew into a beautiful flower! Sam and Lily were both surprised and happy with the twist. They learned that sometimes things don't go as planned, but they can still be wonderful.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play in the yard. The yard has grass, flowers, and a big tree. The tree has a swing and a birdhouse. One day, Anna and Ben go to the yard. They see a bird on the birdhouse. The bird is blue and yellow. It sings a pretty song. "Look, a bird!" Anna says. "It is so pretty!" "Yes, it is!" Ben says. "Do you want to swing or watch the bird?" Anna thinks. She likes to swing, but she also likes the bird. She chooses to watch the bird. She sits on the grass. The grass is soft and green. "I want to swing," Ben says. He runs to the tree. He climbs on the swing. He pushes with his feet. He goes high and low. He feels the wind on his face. Anna watches the bird. The bird watches Anna. They look at each other. Anna smiles. The bird sings. They are happy. Ben swings and swings. He sees Anna and the bird. He stops the swing. He jumps off. He runs to Anna. "Can I watch the bird with you?" he asks. "Sure," Anna says. "The bird is nice. It likes us." Ben sits next to Anna. They watch the bird together. The bird sings louder. They laugh. They are happy.
