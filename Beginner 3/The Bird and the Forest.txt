Category: Beginner
Title: The Bird and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a kind bird. The bird loved to scatter food for all the little animals. Every day, the bird would fly around and drop food on the ground for her friends. One day, the weather was harsh. The wind blew hard, and the rain fell down. The bird knew her friends needed food, so she put on her little hat and went out to scatter food for them. The little animals were happy to see the bird and the food. The bird and the little animals became best friends. Even when the weather was harsh, they played and ate together. They knew they could always count on each other. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim loved to play hockey. He was very fit and fast. One day, he had a plan to play hockey with his best friend, Sam. Tim said, "Sam, let's play hockey today!" Sam was happy and said, "Yes, Tim! I want to play too!" They went to the park to play. But when they got to the park, they saw a big dog playing with their hockey ball! Tim and Sam were scared at first, but the dog was friendly. They played hockey with the dog and had lots of fun. The dog was very good at hockey, and they all became friends. The day was even better than Tim's plan.

---

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a dove on a rough tree. The dove was sad. Tim asked, "Why are you sad, Dove?" The dove said, "I want to bring my friend a gift, but I can't fly far." Tim wanted to help, so he said, "I will bring the gift for you. What is the gift?" The dove gave Tim a pretty flower. Tim walked far to find the dove's friend. He saw many animals, but no doves. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took the flower away! Tim was sad, but he did not give up. He looked for a new gift. He found a shiny stone and brought it to the dove. The dove was happy, and so was Tim. They both learned that even when things don't go as planned, you can still find a way to make others happy.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. It was a hot day, so they brought a big hat for Lily to wear. At the park, Lily saw a small bug. She liked to observe the bug as it moved on the ground. She thought it was fun to watch. Lily's mom sat down on a bench and said, "Lily, come here. I have a story for you." Lily ran to her mom and sat down next to her. Her mom opened a book with big pictures. She began to read the story out loud to Lily. The story was about a big, red ball that went on an adventure. The ball met many friends, like a green frog and a yellow duck. They all played together and had fun. Lily liked the story very much. When the story was done, Lily said, "Thank you, Mom. I love the story." After the story, Lily went back to observe the bug. She thought about the big, red ball and its friends. She imagined herself playing with the ball, the green frog, and the yellow duck. It was a fun day at the park for Lily and her mom.

Lily and Max were playing with their toys in the living room. They had a lot of fun with their cars, dolls, and blocks. They made noises and stories with their toys. They laughed and smiled. But soon, they felt tired. Their eyes were heavy and their bodies were slow. Their mom came and said, "It's time for a nap, my loves. You played a lot and you need some rest." Lily and Max did not want to nap. They wanted to play more. They said, "No, mom, no nap. We are not sleepy. We want to play." Mom smiled and said, "I know, but napping is good for you. It helps you grow and learn. And when you wake up, you will feel alert and happy. You can play more then. Come on, let's go to your room. You can choose your favorite blanket and toy to nap with." Lily and Max followed mom to their room. They saw their beds with their yellow blankets. Yellow was their favorite color. It made them think of the sun and the flowers. They also saw their favorite toys on their pillows. Lily had a teddy bear and Max had a dinosaur. Mom tucked them in and gave them a kiss. She said, "Sweet dreams, my loves. I will be here when you wake up. I love you." Lily and Max hugged their toys and closed their eyes. They felt warm and cozy. They started to nap. They dreamed of playing with their toys in the living room. They were happy.
