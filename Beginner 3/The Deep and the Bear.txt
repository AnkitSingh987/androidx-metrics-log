Category: Beginner
Title: The Deep and the Bear

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a deep, dark cave. In this cave lived a nice, friendly bear. The bear had a big bag that he used to carry things he found in the forest. One sunny day, the bear went out to find some yummy food. He walked and walked until he saw a big apple tree. The bear picked many big, red apples and put them in his bag to carry back to his cave. When the friendly bear got back to his cave, he shared the big, red apples with his friends. They all ate the apples and had a fun time together. The bear was happy that he could carry the apples and share them with his friends in the big, dark cave.

Once upon a time, there was a quiet little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his red wagon. He would pull it up and down the street all day. One day, Tim's mom told him a secret. She said, "I have a surprise for you, but you must wait until tomorrow." The next day, Tim woke up very early. He was excited to find out what the surprise was. He went outside with his wagon and played while he waited for his mom to tell him. He saw his friend, Sue, and they played together. They knew something fun was going to happen soon. Finally, Tim's mom came outside and said, "It's time for your surprise!" She showed Tim a big box. Tim and Sue put the box in the wagon and pulled it together. When they opened it, they found a new toy car! Tim and Sue played with the car and the wagon all day. They were so happy that the secret surprise was worth the wait.

One day, a cat named Max saw a big cup. Max was very dry and wanted a drink. He looked for a way to get the water from the cup. Max saw a long stick and had an idea. Max used the stick to stir the water in the cup. He stirred and stirred, but he could not drink. Max's friend, a dog named Sam, saw Max and asked, "What are you doing, Max?" Max said, "I am dry and want to drink, but I can't." Sam had an idea. He told Max to tip the cup with the stick. Max did as Sam said, and the water came out. Max and Sam both had a drink. They were not dry anymore. Max and Sam were very happy and played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with his friends in the park. One day, Tom found a big chest in the park. He was very excited! Tom called his friends to come and see the chest. They all gathered around and tried to open it. They pushed and pulled, but the chest would not open. Then, Tom had an idea. He asked his friends to cover their eyes while he tried to open the chest. While Tom's friends had their eyes covered, a little bird flew down and opened the chest with its beak. Inside the chest, there were lots of toys and treats for all the animals to share. Tom's friends opened their eyes and saw the open chest. They were so surprised and happy! They all played with the toys and shared the treats. And they thanked the little bird for helping them open the chest.

Lily and Ben were playing with blocks in the living room. They wanted to build a big tower with all the blocks they had. They worked hard and had fun, but they also made a big mess on the floor. "Look, our tower is almost done!" Lily said. "We just need a few more blocks on top." "OK, let's finish it quickly," Ben said. "But we have to clean up after, OK? Mommy said we have to put the blocks back in the box when we are done playing. That's our promise." "Yes, yes, I know," Lily said. "But I want to see how high our tower can go. Come on, help me find more blocks." They searched for more blocks under the sofa, behind the chair, and in the corner. They found some and added them to the tower. It was very tall and wobbly. "Wow, it's amazing!" Lily said. "Let's take a picture of it." "OK, but hurry up," Ben said. "Mommy will be home soon and we have to clean up." Lily ran to get her camera and came back. She took a picture of the tower and smiled. She was very proud of their work. "OK, now let's clean up," Ben said. "We have to keep our promise." But Lily didn't want to clean up. She wanted to play more with the tower. She wanted to see if she could make it taller or wider or prettier. "No, I don't want to clean up," she said. "I want to play more. It's not fair. We worked so hard to build it and now we have to take it apart." Ben was anxious. He knew Mommy would be angry if they didn't clean up. He knew they had to keep their promise. He tried to convince Lily to help him. "Please, Lily, let's clean up," he said. "We can play with the blocks again tomorrow. But we have to do what Mommy said. She will be happy if we do. And we will be happy too. It's not nice to break a promise." Lily thought about it. She knew Ben was right. She knew Mommy would be happy if they cleaned up. She knew it was not nice to break a promise. She felt sorry for being stubborn. "OK, Ben, you are right," she said. "Let's clean up. I'm sorry for being mean. I don't want to make Mommy angry or sad. I want to keep our promise." They hugged and started to clean up. They put the blocks back in the box and put the box in the closet. They picked up the other toys and books and put them in their places. They made the living room neat and tidy. They finished just in time. Mommy came home and saw the living room. She was very happy and proud of them. "Wow, you did a great job, Lily and Ben!" she said. "You cleaned up everything. Thank you so much. You kept your promise. I'm so proud of you." She hugged them and kissed them. She gave them a cookie and a glass of milk. They smiled and thanked her. They were happy too. They learned that keeping a promise is important and rewarding. They learned that cleaning up is not so bad if they do it together. They learned that Mommy loves them no matter what.
