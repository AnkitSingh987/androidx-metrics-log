Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Crab

Once, there was a good boy named Tim. He had a pet crab. The crab was small and red. Tim loved his crab very much. They were best friends. One day, Tim and his crab went to the park. They played in the sand. The crab liked to dig holes. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. They had so much fun together. In the end, Tim and his crab went home. Tim knew he had a special friend. He was happy to own a crab. They were a good team. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big house. The house needed to look nice. So, a man came to do the work. He wanted to decorate the house. He was very good at his job. The man painted the walls with bright colors. He put up pretty pictures. He made the house look modern. Everyone who saw the house said, "Wow! It looks so nice now!" The people who lived in the house were very happy. They liked their modern house. They thanked the man for his work. They had a party to celebrate. Everyone had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big truck. The truck was worried. It had a job to do but did not know how to do it. One day, the truck had to move some toys to a new home. The truck was scared. It had to reverse to put the toys in. The truck did not know how to reverse. A small car saw the worried truck. The car wanted to help. The car showed the truck how to reverse. The truck was happy. The truck reversed and put the toys in. The truck and car were friends. They worked together and moved the toys to the new home. The truck was not worried anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty frog named Lily. She lived near a pond with her friends. They liked to play and swim all day long. One day, Lily saw an attractive grill near the pond. She thought it would be fun to have a cookout with her friends. So, she asked her friend, Sam the squirrel, if she could borrow the grill for the day. Sam said, "Yes, you can borrow it, but be careful!" Lily and her friends had a great time at the cookout. They cooked yummy food on the grill and shared it with everyone. After they finished eating, Lily thanked Sam for letting her borrow the grill. They all agreed it was the best day ever.

Ben and Mia were eager to go to the park. They put on their hats and shoes and ran to the door. But the door was locked. "Mom, open the door!" Ben shouted. "Wait a minute, kids. You need to eat your lunch first," Mom said from the kitchen. Ben and Mia did not want to eat their lunch. They wanted to play on the swings and slides. They were angry and sad. "Please, Mom, we are not hungry. We want to go now," Mia begged. Mom came to the door with two plates of sandwiches and fruits. She smiled and said, "You need to eat something, or you will be tired and hungry later. How about we make a deal? You eat your lunch, and I will give you a surprise." Ben and Mia were curious. They wondered what the surprise was. They decided to eat their lunch. They took the plates and sat on the couch. They ate their sandwiches and fruits. They were not so bad. When they finished, they gave the plates to Mom and asked, "What is the surprise, Mom?" Mom took out a big bag from behind the door. She opened it and showed them two kites. One was blue and one was pink. "Wow, kites!" Ben and Mia exclaimed. "Yes, kites. You can fly them at the park. Do you like them?" Mom asked. Ben and Mia nodded and hugged Mom. They said, "Thank you, Mom. You are the best. We love you." Mom smiled and said, "You are welcome, kids. I love you too. Now, let's go to the park and have some fun." She unlocked the door and they went outside. They were happy and eager to fly their kites.
