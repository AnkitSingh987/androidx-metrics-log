Category: Beginner
Title: The Ant and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a big gray rock, there lived a little ant. The little ant loved to listen to the wind as it blew through the trees. Every day, the ant would go outside and sit on the gray rock to listen. One day, while the ant was sitting and listening, it heard a small cry. The ant looked around and saw a baby bird on the ground. The baby bird had fallen from its nest and needed help. The little ant wanted to help the baby bird. The ant called its ant friends and together, they worked to lift the baby bird back into its nest. The baby bird was very happy and thanked the ants. From that day on, the little ant and the baby bird became friends. They would sit on the gray rock and listen to the wind together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to wear her mom's perfume. One day, Sue saw her mom's perfume on the table. She was very glad. Sue put on the perfume and went outside to play. Sue saw her friend, Tom, and said, "Hi Tom! Do you like my perfume?" Tom said, "Yes, Sue! It smells good." They played together and had a lot of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A big dog came running to Sue and Tom. The dog smelled the perfume and thought Sue was a flower. The dog tried to eat Sue! Tom yelled, "No, dog! Sue is not a flower!" The dog stopped and looked at Sue. The dog was sorry and ran away. Sue and Tom laughed and went back to play. They were glad the dog did not eat Sue.

Lila and Ben are twins. They like to play outside. One day, they see a big hill. It is brown and has smoke on top. "What is that?" Lila asks. "I don't know. Let's go and see," Ben says. They run to the hill. They see a hole. It is hot and red. They hear a loud noise. "Boom!" the hole says. Lila and Ben are scared. They run away. "That is a volcano," a voice says. They turn around. They see a man. He has a hat and a bag. "Who are you?" Ben asks. "I am a scientist. I study volcanoes. They are very interesting," the man says. "Why do they make noise?" Lila asks. "They make noise because they have fire and rocks inside. Sometimes they spit them out. That is called an eruption," the man says. Lila and Ben are curious. They want to know more. "Can we see your bag?" Lila asks. "Sure. I have some tools and books in my bag. Do you want to learn more about volcanoes?" the man asks. "Yes, please," Lila and Ben say. The man smiles. He opens his bag. He shows them his tools and books. He tells them more about volcanoes. Lila and Ben listen and ask questions. They learn a lot. They are happy. The end.

One sunny day, Tim and his mom went to the park. Tim saw a weird bug. It had big eyes and a long tail. Tim wanted to show his friend, Ben. So, Tim's mom said, "Let's use the camera and take a picture." Tim took the camera and clicked a picture of the weird bug. He was happy. Then, he saw Ben coming to the park. Tim ran to Ben and said, "Look, I have a picture of a weird bug!" Ben looked at the picture and said, "Wow, that is a weird bug. I have never seen one like that before." They both laughed and played in the park all day. Tim and Ben had a lot of fun with the weird bug picture.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to poke things. One day, he found a note on the ground. The note was from a polite girl named Sue. The note said, "Please do not poke the big red button." Tim thought about the note and the big red button. He wanted to poke it, but he also wanted to be polite like Sue. So, Tim decided to wait and think more about it. As Tim waited, he could not stop thinking about the big red button. He thought about how fun it would be to poke it. Finally, he could not wait any longer. Tim ran to the big red button and poked it. Suddenly, a loud noise came from the sky. The noise was so loud that it scared Tim and he fell down. Tim learned that he should have listened to the polite note from Sue.
