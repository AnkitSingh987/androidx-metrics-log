Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Apple

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a little boy named Tim and his mom. Tim was hungry and wanted a snack. He asked his mom, "Mom, can you recommend a snack for me?" His mom thought for a moment and said, "How about an apple? It's not harsh on your tummy and it's yummy!" Tim smiled and took the apple from his mom. He sat down and ate his snack happily. Outside, the sun was shining bright and it was a perfect day to play. Tim finished his apple and said, "Thank you, Mom! The apple was a great snack!" Then, Tim went outside to play with his friends, feeling full and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a huge dog named Max. Max liked to jog every day. One sunny day, Max went for a jog in the park. He saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad because she lost her toy. Max said, "I can help you find your toy!" Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Max!" They both went to look for the toy together. They looked under the trees, behind the bushes, and near the swings. Finally, they found the toy near the slide. Max provided the toy to Lily. She was very happy and gave Max a big hug. From that day on, Max and Lily became best friends and played together every day.

---

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to travel. He went to the airport with his mom. Tim was very happy because he thought airports were cool. He saw big planes and many people. Tim and his mom waited for their plane. While they were waiting, Tim saw a big bird. The bird said, "Hi, Tim! Do you want to fly with me?" Tim was surprised. He didn't know birds could talk. But he thought it would be fun to fly with the bird. So, Tim said, "Yes, I want to fly with you!" The bird took Tim on a cool adventure in the sky. They flew high and saw many things. But then, Tim realized he left his mom at the airport. Tim told the bird, "I need to go back to my mom!" The bird understood and took Tim back to the airport. Tim's mom was happy to see him. They got on the plane and had a great trip.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went for a walk. She saw a mysterious big box in the park. Lucy wondered what was inside the box. She asked her friend, Tom, "What do you think is in the box?" Tom said, "I don't know. Let's open it and see!" They opened the box and found many toys inside. They were so happy and played with the toys all day. Later, they saw a sad boy named Max. Max said, "I had an operation and now I can't play with my friends." Lucy and Tom felt bad for Max. They decided to give him some toys from the mysterious box. Max was very happy and said, "Thank you, Lucy and Tom!" The three friends played together and had lots of fun. They learned that sharing can make others happy and that being kind is important.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play with toys and draw pictures. One day, Tom has a new toy. It is a printer. He can print pictures from his computer. "Look, Mia, I can print a picture of a dog!" Tom says. He shows Mia the printer and the paper. Mia likes dogs. She wants to print a picture too. "Can I print a picture of a cat?" Mia asks. She likes cats. She has a cat at home. "Sure, Mia, you can print a picture of a cat," Tom says. He helps Mia find a picture of a cat on his computer. He presses a button. The printer makes a noise. But nothing comes out. The printer has no more paper. "Oh no, Tom, the printer has no more paper!" Mia says. She is sad. She wants to print a picture of a cat. "Don't be sad, Mia, we can find more paper," Tom says. He is sad too. He likes to print pictures. They look around the room. They see a window. The window has curtains. The curtains have paper on them. The paper has drawings on them. "Look, Tom, there is paper on the window!" Mia says. She is happy. She thinks they can use the paper on the window. "No, Mia, we can't use that paper," Tom says. He is not happy. He knows the paper on the window is not for printing. It is for decorating. "That paper is for the window, Mia. It is not for the printer. It has drawings on it. We made the drawings with Mom. Mom will be mad if we take the paper," Tom says. "Oh, Tom, you are right. That paper is for the window. It is not for the printer. It has drawings on it. We made the drawings with Mom. Mom will be mad if we take the paper," Mia says. She is sad again. She wants to print a picture of a cat. They sit on the floor. They are sad. They have no more paper for the printer. They look at the window. They see the paper with the drawings. They see the sun and the sky. They see a bird and a tree. "Look, Mia, there is a bird on the tree!" Tom says. He is not sad anymore. He likes birds. He points at the window. "Look, Tom, there is a cat on the roof!" Mia says. She is not sad anymore. She likes cats. She points at the window. They smile. They forget about the printer. They watch the bird and the cat. They are happy. They are friends.
