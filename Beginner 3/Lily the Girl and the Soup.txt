Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Soup

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to learn new things. One day, her mom said she would teach her how to cook. Lily was very excited. They went to the kitchen and stood by the stove. Lily's mom told her to be careful because the stove could be hot. Lily listened and was very careful. Together, they made a yummy soup. But, there was a spoiled cat named Max. Max wanted to eat the soup too. He jumped on the table and tried to eat the soup. Lily's mom said, "No, Max!" Max was sad, but he learned that the soup was not for him.

One day, a little dog named Max wanted to go to the opera. He liked to sing and dance. Max asked his friend, a big cat named Tom, to go with him. Tom said, "Yes, let's go to the opera!" At the opera, Max and Tom saw many people. They all sang and danced. Max and Tom had a lot of fun. Max urged Tom to dance fast with him. "Tom, dance fast with me!" said Max. Tom danced fast with Max. They danced and danced until they were tired. Then, they went home. Max and Tom were happy they went to the opera. They had a great time.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy car. One day, Tim saw a big tree. He wanted to see how tall it was. So, he took a ruler and started to measure it. But the ruler was only one inch long. Tim was very impatient. He asked his mom, "Mom, how can I measure the tree with this small ruler?" His mom said, "You can try to use the ruler many times and add the inches together." Tim thought it was a good idea. He started to measure the tree inch by inch. It took a long time, but he did not give up. In the end, Tim found out how tall the tree was. He was very happy. He learned that sometimes, you need to be patient to get what you want. And that is the story of Tim and the big tree.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to move and run. One day, he saw some wild animals in the park. There were rabbits, squirrels, and birds. They were all playing and having fun. Tim had an idea. He wanted to have a race with the wild animals. He asked the rabbit, the squirrel, and the bird if they wanted to race. They all said yes. They were very excited to race with Tim. The race started, and they all began to move very fast. The rabbit hopped, the squirrel ran, and the bird flew. Tim ran as fast as he could, but the wild animals were faster. In the end, the bird won the race. Tim was happy to play with his new friends, and they all had a fun day in the park.

One day, a bald man went to a mine. He had a big sack. The bald man wanted to fill the sack with rocks from the mine. He was very happy. The bald man went deep into the mine. He found many rocks. He put the rocks in the sack. The sack became very heavy. The bald man was tired, but he wanted more rocks. Then, something unexpected happened. A big, loud sound came from the mine. The bald man was scared. He tried to run with his heavy sack, but he fell. The rocks in the sack fell on him. The bald man could not get up. The mine was not a happy place for the bald man.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big ice-cream truck with many colors. Tim was very happy and wanted to eat ice-cream. His mom said, "Okay, Tim. Let's buy some ice-cream." As they walked to the truck, a small, harmless bug flew near Tim's face. Tim got scared and began to panic. He said, "Mommy, help me! The bug is going to get me!" His mom smiled and said, "Don't worry, Tim. The bug is harmless. It won't hurt you." Tim calmed down and they bought the ice-cream. They sat on a bench and ate their ice-cream together. Tim forgot about the bug and enjoyed his treat. He was happy that his mom was with him and they had a fun day at the park.
