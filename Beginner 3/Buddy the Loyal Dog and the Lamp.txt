Category: Beginner
Title: Buddy the Loyal Dog and the Lamp

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a loyal dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to play with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. They played all day long and were very happy. One day, Lily saw a pretty lamp in the corner of the room. She wanted to touch it, but it was too high for her. So, she started to crawl on the floor like a little bug to reach the lamp. Buddy saw her and wanted to help. Buddy pushed a small chair near the lamp. Lily climbed onto the chair and touched the pretty lamp. It made the room bright and shiny. Lily and Buddy were so happy. They played under the warm light of the lamp until it was time for bed. They were the best of friends, and they always helped each other.

Once upon a time, a little bird named Bobo lived in a small room. Bobo loved to fly and play all day. One day, he saw a big bird outside his room. The big bird looked serious. Bobo said, "Hi, big bird! Do you want to fly and play with me?" The big bird said, "No, I am too serious to play. I have important things to do." Bobo was sad, but he wanted to show the big bird that flying and playing was fun. So, he flew out of his room and tried to play with the big bird. But the big bird didn't want to play, and he flew away. Bobo tried to fly back to his room, but he couldn't find it. He was lost and very sad. The big bird had flown away, and now Bobo had no one to play with and no room to go back to.

Once upon a time there was a lady walking in the park. As she was walking, she slipped on the ground and fell. She was very worried and she started to cry. A small boy named Bobby saw her and ran to her. He asked the lady, "Are you okay?" The lady nodded her head yes, and then she said, "I'm okay, but I'm very worried. I can't seem to get up." Bobby held out his hand and said, "Do you need help? I can help you get up if you want." The lady smiled and said, "Yes, please. That would be wonderful." Bobby grabbed her hand and helped her stand up. After she was standing, the lady thanked Bobby. The lady was very relieved, and she was not worried anymore. She thanked Bobby again, and then she gave him a big hug before going on her way. Bobby smiled and waved goodbye as she walked away.

Tom and Sue were twins who liked to play in their room. They had many toys, but they always wanted more. One day, they saw a big candle in the living room. It was red and shiny and smelled good. They wanted to have it for their room. They waited until their mom and dad were busy. Then they sneaked into the living room and took the candle. They ran back to their room and put the candle on their table. They were very happy. They thought the candle was very pretty and special. But they did not know that the candle was cheap. It was made of bad wax and had a thin wick. It burned very fast and made a lot of smoke. Soon, the smoke filled their room and made them cough. They were scared and tried to blow out the candle, but it was too hot. They started to cry and call for help. Their mom and dad heard them and ran to their room. They saw the smoke and the candle and quickly put out the fire. They were angry and sad. They asked Tom and Sue why they took the candle. Tom and Sue said they were sorry. They said they wanted the candle because it was pretty and special. Their mom and dad hugged them and said they were glad they were safe. They said the candle was not pretty or special. It was cheap and dangerous. They said they should not take things that are not theirs. They said they should be happy with what they have. They said they love them and they are the most pretty and special things in the world. Tom and Sue learned their lesson. They said they were sorry again and thanked their mom and dad. They gave back the candle and said they did not want it anymore. They said they love their mom and dad and their toys. They said they will not take things that are not theirs again. They hugged their mom and dad and said they were happy.
