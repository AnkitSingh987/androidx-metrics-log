Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Cool Boy and the Spoon

Once upon a time, there was a cool boy named Tim. Tim had a spoon that he took with him everywhere. One day, he went to the park with his best friend, Sue. At the park, they saw a big tree. Tim said, "I want to climb the tree!" Sue said, "I will care for your spoon while you climb." Tim gave Sue the spoon and climbed the tree. As Tim was climbing, a bird took the spoon from Sue's hand and flew away. Tim saw this and was sad. But then, the bird came back with a shiny new spoon! Tim and Sue were very happy and played with their new spoons all day.

One day, a little girl named Sally was eating her cereal. She loved the sweet taste and the delicate crunch it made. Her mom came into the kitchen and said, "Sally, we need to change your cereal. It is not good for you." Sally was sad but said, "Okay, Mom. I will change my cereal." The next day, Sally tried a new cereal. It was not sweet and it was not delicate. She did not like it. That night, Sally heard a noise in the kitchen. She went to see what it was. She saw her old cereal box talking to the new cereal box. The old cereal box said, "We need to change and be better for Sally." The new cereal box agreed. The next morning, Sally tried her old cereal again, but now it was good for her and still tasted sweet and delicate. Sally was very happy and thanked the cereal boxes for changing.

Mommy and Daddy were getting ready to go out. Mommy put on some perfect makeup, and put Daddy in a nice shirt. They were so happy! Then Daddy went out to the car and started to drive. He drove in a big circle around the block with mommy in the passenger seat. They drove for a long time, until they got to the perfect place. It was a place with lots of pretty flowers and swings. Mommy and Daddy laughed and played together until it was time to go home. Before they left, they looked up at the sky, and it was so perfect. They got back in the car and drove away, and they knew they would have lots of fun again soon.

Lily and Tom were playing in the park. They liked to slide, swing and run. But they did not like Ben. Ben was mean. He liked to pinch other kids and make them cry. One day, Ben saw Lily and Tom having fun. He ran to them and pinched Lily hard on her arm. Lily screamed and Tom got angry. He pushed Ben away and said, "Stop it! You are bad!" Ben did not stop. He pinched Tom on his nose and laughed. Tom cried and Lily hugged him. They ran to the bench where their mom was sitting. They told her what Ben did. Mom was not happy. She said, "That is not nice. Pinching hurts. We should tell someone who can help." She looked around and saw a cop. A cop is a person who helps people and stops bad things. Mom waved to the cop and he came over. The cop asked, "What is wrong?" Mom said, "That boy over there pinched my kids and made them cry. He is very mean." The cop looked at Ben and frowned. He said, "That is not okay. I will talk to him and his mom." The cop walked to Ben and his mom. He told them what Ben did and why it was wrong. He said, "You should say sorry to Lily and Tom and be nice to them. Pinching is not a way to play. It is a way to hurt." Ben's mom was sorry too. She said, "I am sorry. I did not know he was doing that. He will apologize and learn to be nice." Ben and his mom came to Lily and Tom and their mom. Ben said, "I am sorry. I was mean. I will not pinch you again. Can we be friends?" Lily and Tom looked at him. They were still sad, but they were also kind. They said, "Okay. We can be friends. But you have to be nice." Ben nodded and smiled. He said, "I will be nice. Thank you for forgiving me." The cop smiled too. He said, "Good job, kids. You did the right thing. You told someone who can help and you gave Ben a chance to change. You are very brave and kind." He gave them a high five and a sticker. He said, "Have fun and be safe." He waved goodbye and left. Lily and Tom and Ben played together in the park. They slid, swung and ran. They did not pinch. They were nice. They were happy. Mom was happy too. She watched them and smiled. She was proud of them.
