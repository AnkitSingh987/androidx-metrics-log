Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben and the Car

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys in the living room. But sometimes they fight over the same toy. Mom tells them to share and be nice. One day, Anna and Ben want to play with the same car. Anna grabs the car and runs away. Ben chases her and tries to take it back. They push and pull and yell. "Stop it, you two!" Mom says. "Give me the car. You are not playing nicely. You need to punish." Anna and Ben look sad. They don't want to punish. They like to play. "What is punish, Mom?" Anna asks. "Punish is when you sit on a seat and think about what you did wrong. You can't play or talk or move until I say so. It is fair, because you both did something bad." Mom says. She points to two seats in the corner. Anna and Ben walk to the seats and sit down. They cross their arms and pout. They sit for a long time. They feel bored and sorry. They look at each other and whisper. "I'm sorry, Anna. I should have shared the car with you." Ben says. "I'm sorry, Ben. I should not have run away with the car." Anna says. They hug and say they are friends. Mom sees them and smiles. "Good job, Anna and Ben. You learned your lesson. You can play again, but remember to be nice and share." Mom says. She gives them the car and a kiss. Anna and Ben are happy. They play with the car and take turns. They don't fight anymore. They have fun.

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a monkey. The monkey had a serious face, but he liked to play. He had many friends in the forest. They would play all day and laugh together. One sunny day, the monkey and his friends went to the river. They enjoyed splashing water at each other. The monkey liked to jump from a branch into the water. His friends would clap and cheer for him. At the end of the day, the monkey and his friends were tired but happy. They went back to their homes in the forest. The serious monkey smiled as he remembered the fun day. He knew that tomorrow, they would play and enjoy even more.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to cook. One day, Spot found a big, red coat. The coat was filthy, but Spot didn't care. He put on the coat and went to the kitchen. In the kitchen, Spot saw his friend, Cat. Cat was cooking a big pot of soup. Spot wanted to help, so he put on his filthy coat and started to cook. They cooked and cooked, and soon the soup was ready. Spot and Cat called their friends to come and eat the soup. All their friends loved it. After they ate, Spot took off his filthy coat and washed it. When it was clean, he put it back on. Spot was very happy because he got to cook with his friends and wear his new coat.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had an orange ball that he loved to play with. One day, Tim's mom was cooking on the stove. She told Tim, "Please behave and don't play near the stove." Tim said, "Okay, Mom," and he went to play with his orange ball in the living room. But the ball bounced and rolled back into the kitchen, near the stove. Tim wanted his ball back, but he remembered what his mom said. He went to his mom and said, "Mom, my orange ball is near the stove. Can you please get it for me?" His mom was happy that Tim behaved and didn't go near the stove. She got the ball for him and said, "Good job, Tim! You listened and behaved well." From that day on, Tim always behaved and played far away from the stove. He and his orange ball had many fun days together, and Tim's mom was always proud of how well he listened.
