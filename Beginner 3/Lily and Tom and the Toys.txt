Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Tom and the Toys

One day, a girl named Lily and a boy named Tom went to the park. They both had toys to play with. Lily had a big red ball and Tom had a blue car. They liked to play with their toys, but they also liked to win. They were competitive. Lily and Tom decided to play a game. They wanted to see who could throw their toy the farthest. Lily threw her ball very far. Tom tried to throw his car, but it did not go as far. Lily said, "I win! My ball went farther than your car!" Tom felt sad. He wanted to win too. Then, Lily had an idea. She said, "Let's share our toys and play together. We can both have fun!" Tom liked the idea. They took turns playing with the ball and the car. They had a lot of fun. Later, they sat down and talked. Lily said, "I like sharing and playing with you, Tom." Tom smiled and said, "Me too, Lily. Sharing is fun!" From that day on, Lily and Tom always shared their toys and played together. They were still competitive, but they learned that sharing and playing together was more fun.

Lily likes to go to the park with her mom. She likes to see the trees, the flowers, the birds and the bugs. She thinks nature is beautiful. She likes to play on the swings, the slide and the sandbox. She likes to make friends with other kids. One day, Lily sees a shiny thing on the ground. She picks it up and looks at it. It is a ring. It has a red stone that sparkles in the sun. Lily thinks it is pretty. She puts it on her finger and shows it to her mom. "Look, mom, I found a ring!" Lily says. "Wow, that is a nice ring, Lily. Where did you find it?" mom asks. "I found it in the grass, over there." Lily points. Mom looks at the ring and smiles. She says, "Lily, you are very lucky. This ring is special. It is a magic ring. Do you know what it can do?" Lily shakes her head. She does not know. Mom says, "This ring can make a wish come true. If you hold it and say what you want, it will ring and grant your wish. But you can only use it once, so be careful what you wish for." Lily thinks for a moment. She wonders what she wants. She has many things she likes, but she also has some things she does not like. She does not like when it rains, when she is sick, when she has to go to bed early, or when she has no one to play with. She holds the ring and says, "I wish I could always be happy and have fun." The ring rings. It makes a soft and sweet sound. Lily feels a warm and fuzzy feeling in her heart. She smiles and hugs her mom. "Thank you, ring. Thank you, mom. I love you." Lily says. "I love you too, Lily. You are my happy and fun girl." mom says. They go back to playing in the park, enjoying the beautiful nature and the magic ring.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a lot of different toys. He liked to play with them all day. One day, it was very wet outside. Tim wanted to carry his toys outside and play in the rain. Tim's mom told him not to go outside. She said it was too wet, and his toys would get wet too. But Tim did not listen. He wanted to play outside with his different toys. So, he put on his boots and carried his toys outside. Tim played with his toys in the wet rain. He was having so much fun. But then, his toys got wet and stopped working. Tim was very sad. He should have listened to his mom. Now, all his different toys were broken, and he could not play with them anymore.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, dry towel that he loved to rub on his face. One day, Tim's mom said, "We will receive a special box today." Tim was very excited. He asked his mom, "What is in the box?" His mom smiled and said, "You will see when it comes." Later that day, the box came. Tim and his mom opened it together. Inside the box, there was a soft, little cat. Tim was so happy! He took his dry towel and gently gave the cat a rub. The cat purred and snuggled with Tim. From that day on, Tim and the cat were best friends. They played together and had lots of fun. And every night, Tim would use his dry towel to give the cat a rub before they went to bed.
