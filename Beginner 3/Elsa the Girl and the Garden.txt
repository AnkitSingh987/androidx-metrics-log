Category: Beginner
Title: Elsa the Girl and the Garden

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Elsa who was only three years old. She was so excited to go out and explore the outside world. One day, she decided to go out into the garden to find some new things. As she was walking around, she noticed a small spring popping out of the ground. She wasn't sure what it was, but it looked exciting. "What's that?" asked Elsa. "That is a spring," said her mother. Elsa was excited and wanted to get closer. When she did, she noticed there were some rough stones and rocks around the spring. She picked one up and put it in her pocket. Soon, Elsa's stomach started to rumble. Her mother said it was time for lunch, so she ran inside the house and saw that there was a delicious pizza on the table. She was so happy and immediately grabbed a slice. The pizza was so yummy and it was the perfect size for her to eat! Elsa was happy that she had such a fun day, with her rough rocks and pizza.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with his best friend, a little girl named Sue. One day, they were playing in the park when Sue's brush fell out of her bag. "Oh no, my brush!" Sue cried. Tom saw the brush on the ground and picked it up with his mouth. He gave it back to her. "Thank you, Tom!" Sue said with a big smile. The next day, Sue's mom gave her a new brush to replace the old one. Sue showed it to Tom. "Look, Tom! I have a new brush!" Tom liked the new brush, too. They played with it all day. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a careless panda named Po. He lived in a big forest. Po liked to play all day long. He didn't seem to care about anything else. One day, Po met a little bird. The bird said, "Po, why are you so careless? You should be careful in the forest!" Po just laughed and said, "I am a big panda. I don't need to be careful." As Po played, he didn't see a big hole in the ground. He fell into the hole and couldn't get out. The little bird saw Po and said, "I told you to be careful, Po." But it was too late. Po was stuck in the hole, and he was very sad.

Sam looked out of the window to see a dead tree. He wanted to measure it so he ran and got a bucket from the shed. He couldn't carry the bucket by himself so he called for help. His mom heard him, "Do you need help Sam?" "Yes, can you help me measure the tree?" Sam asked. Mom smiled and said, "Of course, let's go and get it done." They went outside and Sam filled the bucket with some stones and mud. Then he told Mom to hold one end while he held the other. Sam measured the tree and was very proud. Mom said, "You did a great job Sam. Now let's put the bucket away." Sam smiled and said, "Let's go!"

Once upon a time, in a small forest, there was a little plant. The little plant was very patient. It waited and waited to grow big and strong. One day, the little plant found a big root. The root was very good for the plant. It gave the plant lots of food to grow. One night, a small bug came to the plant. The bug said, "Hello, little plant! You are so patient. I have a gift for you." The bug touched the plant, and it began to glow. The plant was so happy. It glowed and glowed all night long. The other plants and bugs in the forest saw the glowing plant. They came to see the beautiful light. The glowing plant made many new friends. The patient little plant was very happy. It had grown big and strong, and now it had friends too. And they all lived happily ever after.
