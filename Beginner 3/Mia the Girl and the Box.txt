Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Box

One day, a little girl named Mia went outside to play. She saw a normal worm on the ground. Mia thought the worm was very interesting. She wanted to show her friend, Tom. "Tom, look at this worm I found!" Mia said. Tom came over to see the worm. He thought it was a normal worm too. They both looked at the worm and smiled. Mia and Tom decided to give the worm a new home. They got a box and put some dirt inside. They put the worm in the box and named it Wiggly. Mia and Tom were very happy with their new friend Wiggly, the normal worm.

Once upon a time, in a small pond, there lived a noisy mosquito named Momo. Momo loved to buzz around and play with his friends. One day, Momo met a little frog named Fifi. Fifi was sad because she lost her toy. Momo asked, "Why are you sad, Fifi?" Fifi said, "I lost my toy in the tall grass. I can't find it." Momo wanted to help. He promised Fifi, "I will find your toy for you." Fifi smiled and thanked Momo. Momo flew high and low, looking for the toy. He finally found it in the tall grass. He brought it back to Fifi, who was very happy. They became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big building. Many kids lived in the building. The kids loved to play and have fun. They liked to play games and be the best. One sunny day, the kids decided to play a game. They wanted to see who could spray water the farthest. They all got their water bottles and went outside. They were very excited and competitive. They took turns spraying water from their bottles. They laughed and cheered as they watched the water fly through the air. In the end, everyone had fun and they all agreed that they were all good at spraying water. They went back inside the building, happy and tired from their fun game.

Lila and Tom are friends. They like to play with toys and books. One day, Lila's mom says they have to move to a new house. Lila is sad and confused. She does not want to leave Tom and her old house. "Mom, why do we have to move?" Lila asks. "Because dad has a new job in another city. It is far away from here. We will have a bigger house and a nice garden. You will make new friends there, don't worry." Mom says. "But I like my old friends. I like Tom. He is my best friend. Can he come with us?" Lila says. "No, sweetie, he can't. He has his own family and house here. But you can still talk to him on the phone or send him letters. And maybe we can visit him sometimes." Mom says. Lila is not happy. She does not want to talk on the phone or send letters. She wants to play with Tom in person. She wants to hug him and laugh with him. She does not want to move. The next day, Lila and Tom meet at the park. They play on the swings and the slide. They have fun, but they are also sad. They know they will not see each other for a long time. "Tom, I will miss you. You are my best friend." Lila says. "I will miss you too, Lila. You are my best friend. I wish you could stay here." Tom says. They hug each other tightly. They feel warm and safe. They do not want to let go. "Promise me you will not forget me." Lila says. "Promise me you will not forget me." Tom says. They look into each other's eyes. They smile and nod. They hug again. They do not know when they will see each other again. But they know they will always be friends. They know they will always remember each other. They know they will always love each other.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. They run, jump, slide and swing. They have fun. One day, they see a big box in the park. It is brown and has a hole. They are curious. They want to see what is inside. "Let's go to the box!" Lily says. "OK!" Tom says. They run to the box. They look inside. They see a toy. It is a teddy bear. It is soft and brown. It has a red bow. It is hidden in the box. "Wow! A teddy bear!" Lily says. "Who put it here?" Tom says. They do not know. They think it is a surprise. They are happy. "Can I hold it?" Lily says. "Sure!" Tom says. He gives the teddy bear to Lily. She hugs it. She likes it. "Thank you, Tom!" Lily says. "You're welcome, Lily!" Tom says. They smile. They are friends. They share the teddy bear. They play with it in the park. They have fun.
