Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and the Plane

Ben likes to play with his plane. He makes it fly in the air and makes zoom sounds. He smiles when he sees his plane go fast and high. He thinks his plane is smart and cool. But one day, his sister Lily wants to play with his plane too. She takes it from his hands and runs away. Ben is angry and sad. He shouts at Lily and chases her. He wants his plane back. Lily runs to the garden and tries to fly the plane. But she does not know how to make it work. She throws it hard and it falls to the ground. It breaks into pieces. Lily is scared and sorry. She cries and says sorry to Ben. Ben sees his plane broken and feels very bad. He loves his plane and does not want to lose it. He cries and says it is not fair. He is mad at Lily and does not want to talk to her. But then, he sees Lily's face. She is also sad and sorry. She did not mean to break his plane. She just wanted to play with him. She says she will help him fix it. She hugs him and says she loves him. Ben feels a little better. He knows Lily is his sister and she is not bad. He hugs her back and says he loves her too. He says they can try to fix his plane together. He smiles and says they can also play with other toys. He thinks Lily is smart and kind.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little bird named Tim. Tim lived near an airport. He loved to watch the big planes go up and down. He wished he could fly as high as the planes. One sunny day, Tim saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she wanted to go on a plane, but she could not. Tim wanted to help Sue, so he decided to pray for her. He closed his eyes and prayed that Sue would get to go on a plane soon. The next day, Sue's mom told her they were going on a plane to visit grandma. Sue was very happy! She saw Tim and thanked him for his help. Tim was happy too, and they both watched the big plane take off into the sky.

One day, a little girl named Ruby wanted to visit her grandma, who lived in the woods. She put on her red coat and hat, and took a basket of cookies and milk for her grandma. Her mom said, "Be careful, Ruby. Don't talk to strangers, and don't stray from the path. There are wolves in the woods, and they are not nice." Ruby said, "OK, mom. I will behave and go straight to grandma's house." Ruby walked along the path, humming a song. She saw many flowers and birds, and wanted to pick some and play with them. But she remembered her mom's words, and kept walking. She did not see a big, bad wolf hiding behind a tree, watching her. The wolf licked his lips, and thought, "What a tasty snack! I will follow her and eat her and her grandma." The wolf ran ahead of Ruby, and reached grandma's house first. He knocked on the door, and pretended to be Ruby. He said, "Grandma, it's me, Ruby. I brought you some cookies and milk." Grandma was in bed, feeling sick. She said, "Come in, my dear. I'm so happy to see you." The wolf opened the door, and jumped on grandma. He ate her up, and put on her nightgown and cap. He got into her bed, and waited for Ruby. Soon, Ruby arrived at grandma's house. She knocked on the door, and said, "Grandma, it's me, Ruby. I brought you some cookies and milk." The wolf tried to sound like grandma, and said, "Come in, my dear. I'm so happy to see you." Ruby opened the door, and saw grandma in bed. But she noticed something was wrong. She said, "Grandma, what big ears you have!" The wolf said, "The better to hear you with, my dear." Ruby said, "Grandma, what big eyes you have!" The wolf said, "The better to see you with, my dear." Ruby said, "Grandma, what big teeth you have!" The wolf said, "The better to eat you with, my dear!" And he jumped out of the bed, and tried to catch Ruby. But Ruby was not scared. She ran to the door, and shouted, "Help! Help! There's a wolf in grandma's house!" A woodcutter, who was passing by, heard her cry. He ran to the house, and saw the wolf. He took his axe, and hit the wolf on the head. The wolf fell down, and did not move. The woodcutter cut open the wolf's belly, and found grandma inside. He took her out, and she was alive. She hugged Ruby, and thanked the woodcutter. Ruby gave her the cookies and milk, and they all had a nice tea. Ruby said, "I'm sorry, grandma. I should have listened to my mom, and not talked to strangers." Grandma said, "It's OK, my dear. You behaved very well, and you were brave. I love you, Ruby." Ruby said, "I love you too, grandma." And they lived happily ever after.
