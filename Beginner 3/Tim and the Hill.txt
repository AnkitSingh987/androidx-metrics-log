Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Hill

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a tray that he liked to use when he played. One day, Tim saw a big hill and thought it would be fun to use his tray to slide down the hill. Tim's mom saw him and said, "Tim, that hill is dangerous! You might fall and get hurt." But Tim did not listen. He put his tray on the ground and sat on it. Then, he pushed off and started to slide down the hill. As Tim went faster and faster, he realized his mom was right. The hill was dangerous! He felt scared and tried to stop, but he couldn't. Just then, his mom ran down the hill and caught him. Tim was safe, and he learned to listen to his mom when she said something was dangerous. From that day on, Tim only used his tray for safe games.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside in the rain. One day, while it was wet, he saw a big tree. He wanted to make a toy with the wood from the tree. Tim asked his dad, "Can we cut the tree, please?" His dad got an axe and they started to chop the tree. Chop, chop, chop! The tree fell down and they began to make a toy car. They worked fast and soon the toy car was ready. Tim put the car on the ground and gave it a push. The car went speed, speed, speed! Tim was so happy with his new toy. They played with the car all day, even in the wet rain.

One day, a small ant named Tim was walking in the park. He saw a big line of ants all marching together. Tim wanted to join them, so he ran up to the line of ants and said, "Can I march with you?" The leader ant looked at Tim and said, "Yes, you can march with us, but you have to be quick!" Tim was very happy and started to march with the other ants. They all marched up a hill, down a hill, and around a big tree. Tim was very quick and stayed with the line of ants the whole time. As they marched, they found a big pile of food. All the ants worked together to carry the food back to their home. Tim was proud to be part of the team and marched all the way home with his new friends. When they got home, the leader ant said, "Thank you, Tim, for being quick and helping us today." Tim smiled and knew that he had made new friends who liked to march just like him.

Once upon a time, there was a famous snake. He was very long and lived in a big forest. All the animals knew him and liked him a lot. One day, the famous snake went to a party. All his friends were there. They played games and ate yummy food. The famous snake was very happy. Soon, it was time for a big surprise. A big box arrived at the party. All the animals were excited. They opened the box and found lots of balloons inside. The famous snake and his friends had a great time. They played with the balloons all day and everyone was happy.

One sunny day, a pretty cat named Lucy went for a walk. She saw her friend, a big dog named Max. Max was trying to stretch up high to reach a piece of paper stuck in a tree. Lucy asked, "What are you doing, Max?" Max said, "I want to get that paper. It has a poem on it. I love poems!" Lucy wanted to help Max. She thought hard about how they could get the paper down. Lucy had an idea. She asked Max to stretch as high as he could. Then, Lucy climbed onto Max's back to reach the paper. She got the paper and they both sat down to read the poem together. They liked the poem and were happy they could read it. Lucy and Max smiled and continued their walk, now with a pretty poem to share with their friends.
