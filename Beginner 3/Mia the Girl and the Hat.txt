Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Hat

One day, a little girl named Mia was getting ready to play outside. She put on her hat, her shoes, and her sunglasses. But she forgot one important thing: her lotion! Her mom saw her and said, "Mia, don't forget your lotion!" Mia said, "Oops! I need to spray the lotion on my skin." She took the lotion and sprayed it on her arms and legs. Her mom helped her spray it on her face too. Now, Mia was ready to go outside and have fun. Mia played in the sun all day. She was happy that she had the lotion to protect her skin. When it was time to go inside, Mia said, "Thank you, Mom, for reminding me about the lotion!" Her mom smiled and said, "You're welcome, Mia. Now you can play outside and be safe."

Tom and Lily were playing with their toys in the living room. Tom had a big truck and Lily had a doll. Tom wanted to play with the doll, but Lily said no. "Please, Lily, let me play with the doll. It is so pretty and soft," Tom said. "No, Tom, it is my doll. You have your truck. Go play with that," Lily said. Tom did not listen to Lily. He tried to grab the doll from her hands. Lily held on tight and screamed. "Stop, Tom, stop! You are hurting me and the doll!" Lily said. But Tom did not stop. He pulled harder and harder. He wanted the doll so much. He did not care about Lily or the truck. He insisted on having the doll. Then, something bad happened. The doll's head came off. Tom and Lily both looked at the doll in shock. The doll was broken. Lily started to cry. "Look what you did, Tom! You broke my doll! You are a bad brother and a bad player!" Lily said. Tom felt sorry and scared. He did not mean to break the doll. He tried to fix it, but he could not. He wished he had played with his truck instead. He wished he had been more useful and kind to Lily. But it was too late. Lily was very angry and sad. She ran to their mom and told her what Tom did. Tom knew he was in big trouble. He wished he had not insisted on having the doll.

One day, a furry cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a restaurant and was very hungry. Tom walked into the restaurant and looked at the food. Tom did not know what to eat. He saw fish, chicken, and cake. He asked a man what was best. The man did not know. He just did a shrug. Tom picked fish, as he liked fish a lot. He ate the fish and it was very good. Tom was happy and full. He said thank you to the man and went back home.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play near the water. He liked to throw stones in it and watch the water splash. But his mom was always worried when he went near the water. One day, Tim asked his mom, "Mom, can I go play near the water?" His mom thought about it and said, "Yes, Tim, but you must be careful. I will allow you to play near the water, but do not go too close." Tim was so happy! He ran to the water and started to play. He threw stones and watched the water splash. As he played, a little duck came to him. The duck said, "Quack! I am worried about my baby ducks. They are lost. Can you help me find them?" Tim wanted to help the worried duck. He looked around and saw the baby ducks near the water. He carefully walked over and picked them up. He brought them back to the worried duck. The duck was so happy and said, "Thank you, Tim! You are a good friend." Tim went back home and told his mom about the worried duck and her baby ducks. His mom was proud of him for being careful near the water and for helping the duck. From then on, Tim and the duck became best friends and played near the water every day.
