Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Pillow

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big, soft pillow. He wanted to examine it. He looked at it very closely. The pillow was blue and white. It was very pretty. Tim took the pillow to his room. He put it on his bed. He was very happy. But then, he saw his old pillow on the floor. The old pillow looked sad and sorry. Tim felt bad. Tim picked up the old pillow. He put it next to the new pillow on his bed. Now, both pillows were happy. Tim was happy too. He hugged both pillows and went to sleep.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play outside in the snow. One day, he met a black cat. The cat was very quiet and did not make a sound. Tim said, "Hi, cat! Do you want to play with me?" The cat nodded its head and they started to play. They made a big snowman together. Tim put a hat on the snowman and the cat found a stick for its arm. Then, they played a game of freeze tag. Tim would run and the cat would try to catch him. When the cat touched Tim, he would freeze and not move. After playing for a while, they both got tired. They sat down under a tree and talked. Tim said, "I had so much fun today, cat. I hope we can play again soon." The black cat purred and snuggled up to Tim. They both enjoyed the quiet and peaceful moment together.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big lake in the park. Tim saw some people surf on the water. He thought it looked like fun. Tim asked his mom if he could try to surf. His mom said yes. She got a small surfboard for Tim. Tim tried to surf and fell in the water. He laughed and tried again. After playing in the water, Tim and his mom sat on the grass. They ate a delicious snack. They had a fun day at the park. Tim wanted to come back soon and surf again.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had a perfect skirt that she loved to wear. One day, she went to play outside with her friends. They played fun games and had a great time. The sun was shining, and the sky was blue. As they played, Lily's skirt started to increase. It got bigger and bigger! Lily didn't know what was happening. Her friends were surprised too. They stopped playing and looked at her skirt. They wondered why it was growing so big. Then, a little bird flew down from the sky. The bird told Lily and her friends that it was magic. The bird had made her skirt grow as a fun surprise. Lily and her friends laughed and thought it was a funny twist. They continued to play, and the bird made the skirt go back to its normal size. Everyone had a happy day.

Tom was a poor boy who lived in a small hut with his mother. They had no money and no food. One day, Tom went to the market to beg for some bread. He saw a big crowd of people and heard loud music. He wondered what was happening. He pushed his way through the crowd and noticed a big carriage with four horses. Inside the carriage was a man with a shiny crown and a red cloak. He was the king. The king looked out of the window and saw Tom. He smiled and waved at him. "Hello, little boy," the king said. "What is your name?" "Tom, your majesty," Tom said, bowing his head. "You look very hungry, Tom. Would you like to come with me to the palace? I have plenty of food and toys for you." Tom could not believe his ears. He nodded eagerly and ran to the carriage. The king helped him climb inside and gave him a hug. "Welcome to your new home, Tom. You are my son now," the king said. Tom felt very happy and grateful. He thanked the king and hugged him back. He had never met anyone so kind and generous. He looked out of the window and saw the crowd cheering for them. He waved back and smiled. He was no longer a poor boy. He was a prince.
