Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Chest

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a great big chest in his room. Tim loved to play with his toys from the chest every day. One day, Tim did not listen to his mom. He made a big mess in the living room. His mom said, "Tim, you must clean up now or I will punish you." Tim did not want to be punished, so he cleaned up the mess. After he cleaned up, Tim went to his room to play with his toys from the great big chest. He promised his mom that he would always listen and not make a mess again. From that day on, Tim was a good boy and never got punished again.

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse named Tim. Tim was not a happy mouse. He wanted to become a big and strong mouse, just like his friend Max. One day, Tim saw Max sharing his food with a small bird. Tim asked Max, "Why are you sharing your food?" Max said, "When we help others, we become happy too." Tim thought about it and decided to try it. Tim found a little bug who was hungry. He shared his food with the bug. The bug was very happy, and Tim felt happy too. From that day on, Tim always shared his food and helped others. The more he helped, the happier he became. And so, Tim the mouse learned that being kind made him the happiest mouse of all.

Sara was a very intelligent girl. She liked to learn new things and ask questions. She also liked to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, her mom asked her to wash some apples in the sink. Sara was happy to do it. She turned on the water and put the apples in the sink. But then, she saw something shiny in the drain. It was a ring. Sara wondered whose ring it was. She reached for it, but it was too far. She tried to turn off the water, but it was too hard. The water kept running and the sink started to fill up. Sara began to worry. What if the water spilled over and made a mess? What if the ring got lost? What if her mom got angry? She looked around for help. She saw her brother Tom playing with his toy cars on the floor. She called him. "Tom, Tom, come here! I need your help!" Tom heard her and came to the kitchen. He saw the water rising in the sink and the ring sparkling in the drain. He knew what to do. He was also very intelligent. He grabbed a stool and climbed on it. He reached for the faucet and turned it off. He used a spoon to scoop out the ring. He gave it to Sara. "Here, this is mom's ring. She lost it yesterday. She was very sad. She will be very happy when she sees it." Sara smiled and hugged him. "Thank you, Tom. You are very smart and very kind. You saved the day." They dried the sink and the apples. They put the ring on the table. They waited for their mom to come back. They knew she would be proud of them. They were very intelligent and very good.

Once upon a time, there was a little mole. The mole lived in a hole under the ground. The mole loved to dig and make his home smooth and nice. One day, the mole saw a big rock in his hole. The mole said, "I need help to move this rock." He asked his friend, the rabbit, to help him. The rabbit was strong and could hop very high. Together, the mole and the rabbit pushed the big rock out of the hole. The mole was happy because his home was smooth again. The mole thanked the rabbit for his help. They became best friends and played together every day.

Lily and Ben were friends who liked to play outside. One day, they found a big box of blocks in the garage. They decided to build something with the blocks. "What do you want to build?" Lily asked Ben. "I want to build a basketball hoop," Ben said. "I like to play basketball." "OK, let's build a basketball hoop," Lily said. "But we need a ball too." They looked around and saw a red ball in the yard. They ran to get it and brought it back to the garage. They started to build the hoop with the blocks. They worked hard and had fun. They made a tall tower with a hole on top. They put the ball on the hole and tried to push it through. "Yay, we did it!" Lily cheered. "Let's play basketball!" Ben said. They took turns throwing the ball into the hoop. They laughed and clapped when they scored. But then, they heard a loud noise. It was the wind. It was very cold and icy outside. The wind blew hard and knocked down their hoop. "Oh no, our hoop!" Lily cried. "Our blocks!" Ben said. They ran to pick up the blocks. But they were too late. The wind blew the blocks away. They could not find them. They felt sad and angry. They wanted to play more. They blamed each other. "It's your fault, you made the hoop too tall!" Lily said. "No, it's your fault, you threw the ball too hard!" Ben said. They stopped being friends. They went inside and did not talk to each other. The next day, they saw the blocks again. They were in the neighbor's yard. The neighbor was a nice man who liked to help. He had found the blocks and kept them safe. He saw Lily and Ben and waved to them. "Hello, kids, I have something for you," he said. "I found these blocks in the street. They look like yours." He gave them the blocks and smiled. "Thank you, sir," Lily and Ben said. They looked at the blocks and then at each other. They felt sorry for what they said. They remembered how much fun they had. They hugged and said sorry. "I'm sorry, Lily, I was mean to you," Ben said. "I'm sorry, Ben, I was mean to you too," Lily said. They became friends again. They thanked the neighbor and went back to the garage. They decided to build something else with the blocks. They learned that building things is fun, but building friendships is more important. They learned that the wind can break things, but words can hurt more. They learned to be kind and forgive.
