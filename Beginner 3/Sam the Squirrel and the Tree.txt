Category: Beginner
Title: Sam the Squirrel and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little squirrel named Sam. Sam was a very good squirrel, and he loved to find nuts. One day, he found a big nut under a tree. He was very happy. Sam saw his friend, a fierce bird named Bob. Bob said, "Sam, can I have the nut?" Sam did not recognize Bob's voice, so he said, "No, I found it first." But then, Sam looked at Bob and saw that he was very hungry. Sam thought about it and said, "Okay, Bob, we can share the nut." They both ate the nut and were very happy. The moral of the story is that it is good to share with our friends, even when we are fierce or do not recognize them at first.

Lily and Ben were playing outside in the snow. They made a big snowman with a hat and a scarf. They gave him a carrot nose and two rocks for eyes. They were very happy with their snowman. "Hello, Mr. Snowman!" Lily said. "Do you want to play with us?" "Yes, please!" Ben said, pretending to be the snowman. "But I am very cold. Can you give me a hug?" Lily and Ben ran to hug the snowman. They felt his cold and wet snow on their faces and hands. They laughed and squeezed him tight. Suddenly, they heard a loud voice behind them. It was their mom, calling them from the door. "Lily! Ben! Come inside now! It's time for lunch!" Lily and Ben let go of the snowman and turned around. They saw their mom holding a big tray with two bowls of soup and two slices of bread. The soup was steaming and smelled delicious. "OK, mom!" Lily said. "We're coming!" "Bye, Mr. Snowman!" Ben said. "We'll see you later!" They ran to the door, leaving the snowman alone in the yard. The snowman smiled and winked at them. He was glad they had played with him. He hoped they would come back soon. He did not mind the cold. He liked to freeze.

Lily and Ben were playing with a big puzzle. The puzzle had many pieces that made a picture of a mighty lion. Lily and Ben liked the lion, but they did not like to share the pieces. "I want this piece!" Lily said, grabbing a piece from Ben's hand. "No, it's mine!" Ben said, pulling it back. They both tugged and pulled, but the piece did not come out. It ripped in half, and they both fell down. "Oh no!" Lily said, looking at the broken piece. "We ruined the puzzle!" Ben started to cry. He did not want to play anymore. He wanted to go home. Lily felt sorry. She did not want to make Ben cry. She wanted to play with him. "Ben, I'm sorry," she said, getting up. "I was mean. Can we be friends?" Ben looked at her. He was still sad, but he nodded. "Friends," he said, wiping his tears. Lily smiled. She had an idea. "Look, we can still make the puzzle," she said, showing him the broken piece. "We can unite the two halves, like this." She put the two halves together, and they fit. She gave one half to Ben, and kept the other. "Let's find the other pieces, and unite them too," she said. "Then we can see the mighty lion." Ben liked the idea. He got up, and took Lily's hand. "Okay," he said. "Let's do it." They searched for the other pieces, and united them one by one. They worked together, and helped each other. Soon, they had all the pieces, and the puzzle was done. They looked at the mighty lion, and smiled. It was beautiful. They hugged each other, and cheered. "We did it!" they said. "We made the puzzle!" They were happy. They learned to share, and to unite. They were friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big crane. The crane was very serious. It liked to help people lift heavy things. It was very strong and tall. One day, a little cat wanted to climb. The cat saw the crane and thought it would be fun. So, the cat climbed up the crane. It was very high, but the cat was brave. The cat felt happy at the top. The serious crane saw the cat and wanted to help. The crane lowered its long arm, and the cat climbed down. The cat was safe and thanked the crane. They became good friends, and they played together every day.
