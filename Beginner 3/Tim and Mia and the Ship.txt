Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Mia and the Ship

Tim and Mia like to play pirate. They have a big box that is their ship. They have hats and swords and a flag with a skull. They pretend to sail on the sea and look for treasure. One day, they find a white map in the box. It has a red X on it. They think it is a treasure map. They follow the map to the backyard. They dig with their shovels and find a chest. They open the chest and see shiny things inside. They are very happy. They take the chest to their ship. They display the shiny things on the ship. They show them to their mom and dad. They say they are pirate gold and jewels. Mom and dad smile and clap. They say they are very good pirates. Tim and Mia are proud. They share the shiny things with their mom and dad. They have fun playing pirate.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy bird named Bob. Bob lived in a nest made of bricks on top of a big tree. Every day, Bob would soar high in the sky and play with his friends. One sunny day, while Bob was soaring high, he saw a big red ball coming towards him. It was so big and round, he thought it was a new friend. So, Bob flew closer to say hello. But the big red ball did not say anything. It just kept coming closer and closer. As the big red ball got very close, Bob realized it was not a friend, but a big balloon! The wind had blown it up into the sky. Bob was scared, but then he had an idea. He used his beak to pop the balloon. With a loud "POP!", the balloon was gone, and Bob was safe. He went back to his brick nest, feeling happy and brave. From that day on, Bob knew that sometimes, things are not what they seem at first.

Once there was a little girl named Will. She was 3 years old and loved to rock. She had a rocking chair in her room, and she would rock high and low. One day, Will's mom and dad took her to a magical place. It was a rocky hill with a very high peak. Will was fascinated. She said to her mom, "Mommy, can I rock here?" Her mom smiled and said, "Of course." Will began to rock on the hill. She pushed off the ground with her feet and rocked back and forth. She rocked so high that she felt like she was almost touching the sky. At the end of the day, Will was exhausted. But she smiled because she had so much fun. From then on, Will always remembered how much fun it was to rock high up on the hill.

Once upon a time, there was a hungry dog named Spot. Spot was walking down the street, looking for food. He saw a little girl named Amy holding a coin. "Hi Amy," said Spot. "I am very hungry. Can you help me?" Amy looked at Spot and said, "Yes, I can help you. I have a coin. Let's go to the store and buy some food." At the store, Amy gave the coin to the man behind the counter. The man looked at the coin and smiled. "This is a special coin," he said. "You can receive a big surprise." Amy and Spot were excited. They waited for the big surprise. Then, a big truck pulled up in front of the store. The back of the truck opened, and a huge pile of dog food came out! Spot was so happy. He had more food than he could ever eat. He shared his food with all the other hungry dogs in the town. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim had a clean net. He liked to take his net to the park. Tim wanted to catch a bug with his net. At the park, Tim saw a big, green bug. He tried to take the bug with his net. But the bug was too fast. Tim did not give up. He tried again and again. Finally, Tim was able to take the bug with his net. He was very happy. Tim showed the bug to his mom. She was proud of him. Then, Tim let the bug go back to its home.
