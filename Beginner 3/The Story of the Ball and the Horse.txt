Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Ball and the Horse

One day, a boy went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy a whip for their horse. The boy was excited, because he liked the horse a lot. The store was regular, with many things to buy. In the store, the boy found the whip. But, he saw something else too. It was a big, colorful ball. The boy wanted the ball more than the whip. He asked his mom if they could buy the ball instead. His mom said yes, and they bought the ball. When they got home, they played with the ball and the horse. The horse liked the ball too! They all had fun, and they did not need the whip after all.

Once upon a time, there was a little bubble named Bubbly. Bubbly loved to stay up high in the sky. He would float and play with his friends, the birds and the clouds. One day, Bubbly started to feel upset. He missed his family on the ground. So, he decided to go down and stay with them for a while. Bubbly's family was very happy to see him. Bubbly stayed with his family and played with them. They had lots of fun together. After some time, Bubbly felt happy again. He said goodbye to his family and went back up to the sky. Bubbly knew he could always come back if he felt upset. And he lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there were two furry twins. They loved to play and grab things. One day, they went to the park to play. At the park, they saw a big, red ball. The furry twins both wanted to play with it. They started to fight over the ball. One twin would grab the ball, then the other twin would grab it back. They fought and fought, not sharing the ball. A big dog saw the ball and wanted it too. The dog ran fast and grabbed the ball from the twins. The furry twins were sad and cried. They had lost their ball because they did not share.

One day, Tom and Tim went for a walk. They saw a big tree. An intelligent bird lived in the tree. The bird could talk. Tom and Tim were happy to meet the bird. The bird said, "I can help you find something fun." Tom and Tim wanted to go with the bird. They went to a big hill. They saw a big box on the hill. Tom and Tim opened the box. They thought there would be toys inside. But, surprise! There was a big cake in the box. The bird said, "I knew you liked cake!" They all laughed and ate the cake together.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tim. Tim liked to bake yummy treats. One day, he wanted to bake a big cake. To bake the cake, he needed wood to make a fire. So, he went to get lumber from the forest. In the forest, he met a small bird. The bird was sad because it had no food. Tim had a good heart. He gave the bird some of his cake mix. The bird was very happy. Tim took the lumber and went back home. Tim baked the big cake with the lumber. The bird came to his house with its friends. They all loved the cake. Tim was happy to share his cake with them. The moral of the story is that sharing with others makes everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot liked to play all day. He loved to run, jump, and swim in a small pond near his home. One sunny day, Spot went to the pond to swim. He splashed and played in the water. After a long swim, Spot felt tired. He found a soft, warm spot under a big tree and took a nap. When Spot woke up from his nap, he felt hungry. He ran home to find his food bowl filled with yummy treats. Spot ate his treats and smiled. He was happy and full. Then, he went back to the pond to play some more.
