Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Duck and the Pond

One day, a little duck wanted to become a big duck. He saw a big pond and thought, "If I swim in the big pond, I will become big like the other ducks." He was very excited. The little duck jumped into the pond and started to swim. He became very wet. The water was cold and made him shiver. The other ducks saw him and started to laugh. The little duck felt sad and began to cry. A wise old duck saw the little duck crying and swam over to him. The wise old duck said, "Don't cry, little duck. You will become big one day, but you need to be calm and patient." The little duck listened to the wise old duck and stopped crying. He swam calmly in the pond with the other ducks, and as the days went by, he slowly grew bigger and happier.

Once, there was a girl named Lucy. She had a very important notebook. Lucy loved her notebook and took it everywhere. One day, Lucy had a fight with her friend Tom. Tom was sad and said sorry to Lucy. But Lucy was still mad. Lucy wanted to make Tom feel bad. She took her important notebook and wrote a mean note to Tom. She gave it to him and he read it. Tom felt very sad and cried. Lucy felt good at first, but then she felt something bad inside her. Later, Lucy felt sorry for what she did. She wanted to say sorry to Tom, but he was not at school. Lucy asked her friends where Tom was, but they did not know. Lucy looked everywhere for Tom, but she could not find him. She wished she could take back the mean note she wrote in her important notebook. Lucy felt regret for hurting Tom. She never found Tom again and always felt bad about what she did.

Tom is a boy who likes to play with his toys. He has a lot of toys, but his favorite is a chubby owl. The owl is soft and brown and has big eyes. Tom likes to hug the owl and make it hoot. One day, Tom goes to the park with his mom and his owl. He sees a girl on the swing. She has a doll with a pink dress and long hair. Tom thinks the doll is pretty. He wants to play with the doll. He goes to the girl and says, "Can I see your doll?" The girl says, "No, this is my doll. Go away." Tom feels sad. He says, "But I want to see your doll. Please." The girl says, "No, no, no. You have your own toy. Leave me alone." Tom gets angry. He grabs the doll from the girl's hand. The girl screams and pulls the doll back. They tug and tug. The doll's dress rips. The girl cries. Tom cries. Mom hears the noise and runs to them. She sees the ripped doll and the crying children. She says, "What happened? Why are you fighting?" Tom says, "I wanted to see her doll. She wouldn't let me." The girl says, "He took my doll. He broke my doll." Mom says, "That's not nice. You can't take things that are not yours. You have to ask and share. And you can't break things that are not yours. You have to be careful and gentle. I'm sorry your doll is broken. Do you want to hug your owl?" Tom says, "Yes, I want to hug my owl." He hugs his chubby owl and feels a little better. Mom says, "And you, do you want to hug your doll?" The girl says, "Yes, I want to hug my doll." She hugs her doll and feels a little better. Mom says, "Do you want to say sorry to each other?" Tom and the girl look at each other. They say, "Sorry." They smile a little. Mom says, "Do you want to play together?" Tom and the girl nod. They go to the slide and take turns. They have fun. They learn to be friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was feeling miserable one day because he could not find his favorite puzzle. He looked under his bed, in his toy box, and even in the kitchen, but he could not find it anywhere. Tim's mommy saw that he was sad and decided to help him look for the puzzle. They looked all around the house together. Finally, they found the puzzle behind the couch. Tim was so happy that he jumped up and down with joy. Tim said "thank you" to his mommy for helping him find the puzzle. They sat down together and finished the puzzle. Tim was not miserable anymore, and he and his mommy had a great day playing with the puzzle.
