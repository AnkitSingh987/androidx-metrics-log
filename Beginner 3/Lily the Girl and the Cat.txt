Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a young girl named Lily. She had a pet cat named Tom. Lily and Tom loved to play all day. They would run and jump in the garden. They were very happy together. One day, Lily wanted to feed Tom. She looked for cat food in the house but could not find any. So, she asked her mom for help. Her mom showed her the way to the store. Lily and Tom went to the store together. At the store, they found the cat food. Lily was happy to feed Tom. They walked back home and played some more. Tom was happy and full. Lily and Tom had a great day.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Jim and Joe. They liked to play together and were very happy. One day, Jim and Joe were playing at the park. Then Joe saw something special - a very big tap. He wanted to play with it, but it was very large and he was very small. Jim had an idea. He said, "Let's shrink the tap, Joe." Joe was very surprised but he liked the idea and ran to get some wood for a fire. The fire started to grow and grow and eventually, the big tap got smaller and smaller. And then, Jim and Joe could play with the different, smaller tap. They had lots of fun and they learned a lesson: with courage and smarts, anything can be conquered.

Once upon a time, in a wild garden, there lived a little caterpillar named Catty. Catty loved to eat green leaves and play with her friends. One day, she met a big dog named Buddy. Buddy said, "Hi Catty, do you want to play with me?" Catty was scared at first, but then she said, "Yes, let's play together." They played and had fun all day long. Later, Catty saw a pretty flower and wanted to eat it. She asked Buddy to kneel down so she could climb on his back. Buddy kneeled down, and Catty climbed up. They both laughed and played some more. At the end of the day, Catty and Buddy were tired. They sat under a big tree and said, "We had so much fun today. Let's play together again soon." And they became best friends forever.

Anna and Ben love to play in the park. They like to run, swing, slide and climb. But their favorite thing is to feed the ducks. They always bring some bread and look for the ducks in the pond. One day, they see a new duck. It is small and yellow and has a big red bow around its neck. It looks like a toy duck, but it moves and quacks like a real duck. Anna and Ben are curious. They want to feed the new duck and make friends with it. "Hello, little duck," Anna says. "Do you want some bread?" She throws a piece of bread into the water. The new duck swims towards it and eats it. It quacks happily. "Wow, you are a good duck," Ben says. "What is your name?" He throws another piece of bread. The new duck swims to it and eats it. It quacks again. "I don't think it can talk," Anna says. "Maybe it has a tag with its name on it. Let's try to catch it and see." Anna and Ben run to the edge of the pond. They reach out their hands and try to grab the new duck. But the new duck is fast and smart. It swims away from them and quacks loudly. "Hey, stop that!" a voice says. "That's my duck. His name is Rudy. He's a birthday present from my grandma. She said he likes to swim and play, but he always comes back to me." Anna and Ben look up and see a boy standing on the other side of the pond. He has a big smile and a basket with more bread. He waves at them and whistles. The new duck hears him and swims to him. He picks up the duck and hugs it. "Sorry, we didn't know," Anna says. "He's a very nice duck. Can we play with him too?" "Sure, you can," the boy says. "But you have to be gentle and careful. He's not a toy, he's a life. And he doesn't like to be wet for too long. He likes to be dry and warm and cuddly." Anna and Ben nod and smile. They walk around the pond and join the boy and the duck. They share their bread and play together. They learn more about Rudy and his grandma. They make a new friend and have a lot of fun. They include the new duck in their game and he includes them in his life. They are all happy.
