Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Pastels

One day, a little girl named Lily found a huge box. Inside the box, there were many pastels. She liked the colors. They were pretty. She wanted to draw with them. Lily went to her friend Tom's house. She showed him the pastels. "Look, Tom! I found these," she said. Tom looked at the pastels and smiled. "Let's draw together!" he said. They took out paper and began to draw. As they drew, Lily held a blue pastel and Tom held a red one. They made fun pictures. They were happy. They drew all day with the huge pastels. It was a good day.

One day, a little dog named Spot was playing in the park. Spot was an independent dog who liked to play by himself. He loved to run, jump, and touch everything he could find. While playing, Spot saw a big ball. He ran to the ball and touched it with his nose. The ball started to roll. "Wow!" said Spot, "I want to play with this ball." He chased the ball and touched it again and again. Soon, a little girl named Lily came to the park. She saw Spot playing with the ball. "Hi, Spot!" she said. "Do you want to play with me?" Spot looked at Lily and wagged his tail. They played together, touching the ball and laughing. Spot was happy to have a new friend to play with.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside with his friends. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. One day, Tim and his friends wanted to make something new. They decided to make a wall using blocks. They were very alert and careful as they put each block on top of the other. The wall grew taller and taller. Finally, the wall was done. Tim and his friends were so happy. They played near the wall and had lots of fun. The wall made their playtime even better. And from that day on, Tim and his friends always remembered the fun they had making the wall together.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dad went to the lake. They took their red kayak. The water was calm and the sun was bright. They were very happy. Tim and his dad got in the kayak and started to paddle. They saw fish and birds. After a while, they felt tired. "Let's rest," said Tim's dad. They stopped paddling and let the kayak float on the calm water. As they rested, they heard a splash. A big, green frog jumped into the kayak! Tim and his dad were surprised. The frog said, "Hello! I can paddle, too!" Tim and his dad laughed. They let the frog help them paddle the kayak all the way back to the shore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play catch with his dog, Spot. They would run and laugh in the big yard near their house. There was a gate that kept them safe from the street. One sunny day, they were playing catch when the ball went over the gate. Spot was upset because he could not get the ball. Tim tried to open the gate but it was stuck. They did not know what to do. Just then, a bird flew down from the sky and picked up the ball with its beak. The bird flew over the gate and dropped the ball back into the yard. Tim and Spot were so happy and surprised! They thanked the bird and continued to play catch, making sure the ball stayed in the yard.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. He loved to play with his blocks. He had big blocks, small blocks, and many colors of blocks. Tim liked to build tall towers and small houses with his blocks. One day, Tim found a magic seed. His mom told him to plant the seed in the ground. Tim dug a hole, put the seed in, and covered it with dirt. He gave it water and waited to see what would happen. Every day, Tim looked at the spot where he planted the seed. Slowly, something began to grow. It grew taller and taller. Soon, it was a big tree with lots of branches. Tim was so happy. He played under the tree with his blocks and built even bigger towers and houses. The tree gave him shade and made his playtime even more fun.
