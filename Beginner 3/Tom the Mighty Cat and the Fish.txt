Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Mighty Cat and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a mighty big cat named Tom. Tom liked to observe the world around him. He would watch the birds fly and the fish swim in the pond. One day, Tom saw a little mouse named Jerry. Jerry was taking a turn running around the garden. Tom said, "Hi, Jerry! Do you want to play with me?" Jerry looked at Tom and said, "Okay, Tom. I will play with you." They played together all day long. They ran, jumped, and laughed. They had a lot of fun. But then, it started to get dark. The sky turned black, and the wind blew hard. Tom and Jerry got scared. They tried to find their way home, but they couldn't see. They got lost in the dark, cold night. They were very sad and afraid. And that is how their day ended, sad and lost in the dark.

Once upon a time, there was a big orange truck. The truck loved to use fuel to go on fun trips. One day, the truck went to the park to play with its friends. At the park, the truck saw a little blue car. The car looked sad. The truck asked, "Why are you sad?" The car said, "I cannot play, I have no fuel." The orange truck wanted to help. It had an idea! The truck went to get some fuel for the car. But when it came back, something unexpected happened! The little blue car was now a big green bus! The bus said, "Thank you for the fuel, now I can take kids to school!" The orange truck was happy to help, and they all played together at the park.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a big slide. It is very high. Lily wants to go on the slide. Ben is scared. "Come on, Ben. It is fun!" Lily says. She runs to the slide and climbs up the ladder. She sits on the top and smiles. She can see the whole park. "No, Lily. It is too high. I will fall!" Ben says. He stays on the ground and looks up. He feels sad. Lily slides down fast. She laughs and shouts. "Whee! That was great! You should try it, Ben. It is not scary. It is fun!" She runs to Ben and holds his hand. "Please, Ben. I will go with you. We can slide together. It will be fun. Trust me." Ben looks at Lily. He sees her smile. He likes her smile. He wants to have fun too. He nods and says. "Okay, Lily. I will try it. But you have to hold my hand." Lily and Ben climb up the ladder. They sit on the slide. They hold hands. They smile. They slide down fast. They laugh and shout. "Whee! That was great! We did it, Ben. We did it!" They hug each other. They are happy. They are brave. They are friends.

Once upon a time there was a bird that wanted to fly. So the bird opened its wings and flew up high. The bird was so happy to have the sky all to itself. The bird flew fast, higher and higher until it saw a cave. The bird decided to fly down and explore. As the bird flew at the entry of the cave, it noticed that the entrance was very tight. But the bird kept going and it flew through the tight opening. The bird saw that the cave was dark, but it decided to explore it. Suddenly, something small flew up the bird's face. It was a bat! The bat said "surprise!" and the bird almost flew away sadly but then the bat said: "Don't be scared, I just wanted to meet you!" The friendly bat then became the bird's friend. From then on, they would fly and explore the cave together. They flew around the walls, up and down, always having fun. They never wanted their cave adventures to end.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lisa was very eager to go to a fun party. Her mom told her that there will be a big surprise at the party that will excite her. Lisa could not wait to find out what it was. At the party, Lisa saw many kids wearing masks. She saw a big box in the middle of the room. Lisa thought the big surprise might be inside the box. She was very excited and couldn't wait to see what was inside. Finally, it was time to open the box. All the kids gathered around, and Lisa was the most eager of all. When they opened the box, they found many colorful masks inside! The big surprise was that they all could wear the masks and play together. Lisa was so excited and happy to be at the party with her friends.
