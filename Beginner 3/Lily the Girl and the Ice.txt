Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Ice

Once upon a time, in a land far away, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved ice. She liked to eat ice and play with ice. One day, Lily found a big piece of ice outside her house. She was very happy and started to play with it. Lily's friend, Tim, came to play with her. They both played with the ice, making it slide and spin. But as they played, the ice started to disappear. It became smaller and smaller. Lily and Tim were upset. "Why is the ice going away?" asked Lily. Tim said, "I don't know, but I'm upset too." They both felt sad that their ice was disappearing. But then, Lily's mom came outside and saw them. "Don't be upset," she said. "Ice disappears when it gets warm. Let's go inside and have some ice cream to cheer you up." Lily and Tim smiled and went inside to eat ice cream, feeling happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a little lion named Leo. He lived in a big forest with his family. Leo was a nervous lion. He was scared to talk to others and make new friends. One day, while Leo was playing near the trees, he saw a small bird. The bird said, "Hi, I'm Billy. Do you want to be friends?" Leo was nervous but wanted to try. He said, "Hello, I'm Leo. I would like to be friends." As they played together, Leo became less nervous. They had so much fun! At the end of the day, Billy said, "You are a good friend, Leo." Leo was happy and gave Billy a little kiss on the cheek. From that day on, Leo and Billy were best friends. They played together every day, and Leo was not nervous anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little frog named Tim. Tim lived with his parent in a small pond. One day, Tim's parent said, "Tim, be careful when you play near the big tree. There is a big snap thing there." Tim was a good frog, but he was also very curious. He wanted to see the big snap thing. So, he hopped to the big tree. He saw a big, bitter fruit hanging from the tree. Tim thought, "Maybe this is the snap thing." Just then, a big wind came and the bitter fruit fell on the ground. It broke open and made a loud "SNAP!" Tim jumped back in surprise. He realized that the bitter fruit was the snap thing his parent had warned him about. Tim hopped back to his parent and told them about the snap thing. His parent was happy that Tim was safe and that he had learned to be careful. From that day on, Tim played near the big tree, but he always remembered to stay away from the snap thing.

One day, a boy named Will went to the park. He saw a big, red ball. Will wanted to play with the ball, but it was not tight. So, he took a pump to fill the ball with air. Will started to pump air into the ball. A girl named Sue came over and said, "That's my ball!" Will said, "I want to play with it too." Sue was not happy. They both wanted the ball, but there was only one ball. Will and Sue decided to fill the ball together. They pumped and pumped, but they did not stop. The ball got very big and very tight. Then, the ball popped! Now, there was no ball to play with. Will and Sue were both sad.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a soft, yellow blanket that she loved very much. Sue would take her blanket everywhere she went. One day, Sue went to the park with her mom. She saw a big tree with a top that touched the sky. Sue wanted to see what was up there. She asked her mom if she could climb the tree. Her mom said yes, but to be careful. Sue started to climb the tree. She went up and up until she was very high. Sue peeked at the top of the tree and saw a big, pretty bird's nest. She smiled and climbed back down to tell her mom what she saw. Sue was so happy she got to peek at the top of the tree with her soft, yellow blanket.
