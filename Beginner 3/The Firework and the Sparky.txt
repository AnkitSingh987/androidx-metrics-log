Category: Beginner
Title: The Firework and the Sparky

Once upon a time, there was a firework. Its name was Sparky. Sparky was a patient firework. It waited and waited for its turn to light up the sky. One day, a big party happened. Many people came to see the fireworks. Sparky was very excited. It was finally time for Sparky to shine. But something bad happened. When it was Sparky's turn, it did not go up in the sky. Instead, it fell down and made a big mess. The people were not happy. Sparky was sad. It did not get to light up the sky.

One day, a tall boy named Tim went to the park with his scooter. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Tim was so happy to ride his scooter. At the park, Tim met a little girl named Sue. She saw his scooter and said, "Wow, that's a nice scooter!" Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Sue! Do you want to try it?" Sue was so excited and said, "Yes, please!" Sue rode the scooter and had a lot of fun. But then, she saw her mom coming. Her mom reminded her, "Sue, it's time to go home now." Sue gave the scooter back to Tim and said, "Thank you, Tim! I had so much fun!" Tim said, "You're welcome, Sue! See you again!" They waved goodbye and went home.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat named Tom. Tom lived in a small house with his friend, a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to drink milk from a glass every day. Tom liked to watch Sue drink her milk. One day, Sue's glass was near the edge of the table. Tom wanted to make sure the glass was safe, so he tried to move it. But, oh no! The glass fell off the table and broke into pieces. Sue was sad, and Tom felt bad. Tom wanted to make Sue happy again, so he had an idea. He found a new glass and filled it with milk. He gave it to Sue, and she smiled. Tom was happy, too. In the end, they both learned to be careful with the glass, so it would stay safe.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin lived in the sea with his family. One day, Fin saw a big, yummy cake floating in the water. He wanted to eat it, but he was not sure if he should. Fin swam to his friend, the crab, and asked, "Can I eat the cake?" The crab said, "No, it is not good for you. It is not from the sea." Fin was sad, but he knew his friend was right. He wanted to finish the cake, but he knew it was not good for him. Later, a big wave came and took the cake away. Fin and his friend were happy. They knew they had made the right choice. They swam together, playing in the sea, and found some yummy seaweed to eat instead.

Tim and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to slide, swing and run. But they also liked to look at the big bikes that some older kids had. The bikes had shiny wheels, loud horns and cool helmets. One day, a new kid came to the park with a bike. His bike was not shiny, but filthy. It had mud, rust and scratches all over it. His helmet was also filthy. It had a big crack, a missing strap and a sticker that said "Bad Boy". Tim and Lily stared at the new kid and his bike. They had never seen such a filthy bike and helmet before. They wondered why he did not clean them or get new ones. "Hey, what are you staring at?" the new kid shouted at them. He did not sound friendly. He sounded angry and mean. Tim and Lily were scared. They did not want to make the new kid mad. They did not know what to say. "We... we... we just like your bike and helmet," Tim lied. He hoped the new kid would believe him and leave them alone. The new kid snorted. He did not believe Tim. He thought Tim and Lily were making fun of him and his bike and helmet. "You like them? Then you can have them!" he said. He threw his helmet at Tim and his bike at Lily. Then he ran away. Tim and Lily dodged the helmet and the bike. They fell to the ground. They were not hurt, but they were shocked and confused. They looked at each other. They did not like the new kid and his bike and helmet. They liked their own toys and clothes better. They decided to ignore the filthy bike and helmet and go back to their fun games.
