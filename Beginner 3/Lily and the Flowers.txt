Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Flowers

One sunny day in spring, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a mysterious big tree. The tree had many pretty flowers. Lily wanted to choose some flowers to take home. She asked the tree, "Can I have some flowers, please?" The tree said, "Yes, you can choose some flowers, but only three." Lily said, "Thank you, tree!" She picked one red flower, one blue flower, and one yellow flower. Lily ran back home with her flowers. She showed them to her mom. Her mom was happy and said, "These flowers are so pretty! Let's put them in a vase." Lily and her mom put the flowers in a vase, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a little dog named Max. Max had a friend named Sam. Sam was a tiny bird who loved to fly high in the sky. Max and Sam liked to play together every day. One sunny day, Max and Sam wanted to join their friends at the park. But Max had a problem. His foot hurt, and he couldn't walk fast. Sam saw that Max was impatient to get to the park. So, Sam had an idea to help Max. Sam found a big stick and told Max to hold it in his mouth. Then, Sam held the other end of the stick with his feet. Sam flew and pulled Max to the park. Max was happy, and his foot felt better soon. They played with their friends all day long.

Sam and Mia are twins. They like to play with their toys and watch cartoons on the screen. Sometimes they fight over the remote. Mom says they have to share and be nice. One day, Sam finds a new toy in his room. It is a soft bear that makes noises when you squeeze it. Sam loves the bear and squeezes it hard. He hears it say "I love you" and "You are my friend". He smiles and hugs the bear. Mia sees the bear and wants to play with it too. She asks Sam to give her the bear. Sam says no, it is his bear. He squeezes it again and it says "You are the best". Mia gets angry and tries to grab the bear. Sam pulls it back and they start to tug. Mom hears the noise and comes to the room. She sees Sam and Mia fighting over the bear. She says "Stop! You are hurting the bear and each other. Why can't you share and take turns?" Sam and Mia look at Mom and feel sorry. They say "Sorry, Mom. Sorry, bear. Sorry, twin." Mom smiles and hugs them. She says "That's better. You are both healthy and smart. You can play with the bear together and make it happy. And you can also watch the screen together and learn new things. But remember to be kind and gentle. Can you do that?" Sam and Mia nod and say "Yes, Mom. We can do that." They give the bear to each other and squeeze it softly. The bear says "Thank you" and "You are both my friends". Sam and Mia smile and hug each other. They are happy.

Once there was a little girl named Lily. One day, she saw a shiny ball high up on a branch of a tree. She wanted to steal the ball because she wanted to play with it! Lily said, “Mummy, can I steal the ball from the tree? Please?” Her mummy said, “No, don’t steal, Lily. It’s not nice. Let’s find another way to get the ball.” So, Lily and her mummy climbed up the tree together and got the ball down. Lily was so happy! She took the ball and ran to play with it in the garden. This time, Lily saw a lily, a beautiful white flower, high up on a tall bush. She wanted to steal the lily to keep it in her room! Lily said, “Mummy, can I steal the lily? Please?” Mummy replied, “No, it’s not right to steal. We’ll find another way to get the lily down.” So, Lily and her mummy worked together to carefully pick the lily from the bush and put it in a special place in Lily’s room. Lily was so happy with her new flower!
