Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Bob. Bob had a long neck. He was a very scared dog. One day, Bob went for a walk in the park. In the park, Bob saw a big cat. The cat said, "Hello, Bob. Why are you so scared?" Bob did not know what to say. He was too scared to speak. Bob tried to run away from the cat. But the cat was too fast. It caught Bob's neck. The cat signed to Bob that he was in trouble. Bob was very sad and scared. The cat did not let Bob go, and Bob could not go home.

Nana was looking out the window with her grandson, Tom. He was only three. She pointed out at the ocean, telling him there was a special yacht available today. Tom asked what a yacht was. Nana explained that it was a big boat that was very special. She said if they left right now they could go and see it in person. Tom got so excited that he ran to get his shoes. Nana and Tom then left the house and walked to the beach. Nana showed Tom the yacht and pointed out how shiny and beautiful it was. He couldn't believe it! Then, Nana warned him that the yacht was only available for a limited time. Sure enough, after about an hour, the yacht disappeared. Tom was sad that it was gone. Nana told him that the yacht will come back, but until then, he would just have to remember how beautiful it looked today. Tom was filled with anticipation for when it would appear again.

Once upon a time, there was a sailor named Tom. Tom was a very independent man. He loved to sail on his boat all by himself. One day, Tom went out to sea with his boat. While he was sailing, he saw a big fish. The fish was stuck in a net. The fish could not swim. Tom wanted to help the fish. He used his strong arms to wrap the net around and around. Then, Tom pulled the net off the fish. The fish was free! The fish was very happy. It jumped high in the water to say thank you. Tom was happy too. He learned that even if you are independent, it is good to help others. And that is the moral value of the story.

Once there was a boy named Jack. He was three years old. He was very fond of books. He loved to read. One day Jack saw a piano. He wanted to read music from it. He asked his Mom, "Can I read the piano?" His Mom said, "Yes, but it has to be normal. No playing loud music at this hour." So Jack sat down at the piano and opened the book of music. He read the notes slowly and with care. He was careful to be normal. Jack played the notes he read from the book. After a while he managed to play a nice little song. His Mom was impressed. Jack was so proud of himself! He was happy that he could read music from the piano.

Once upon a time, there was a silly infant named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toy car all day. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, do not go near the big tree. It is not safe." But Tim did not listen. Tim went to the big tree with his toy car. He saw a big hole near the tree. Tim thought it would be fun to put his toy car in the hole. He said, "Car, you will go fast in the hole." And he put the car in the hole. Tim tried to get the car out of the hole, but it was too deep. He cried and cried. His mom heard him and came to help. She said, "I told you not to go near the big tree." Tim was very sad. He never saw his toy car again.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a girl named Sue playing with a toy car. Tim had a toy boat with him. He wanted to play with the car, so he asked Sue, "Can we trade toys?" Sue looked at Tim and replied, "Yes, let's trade!" They traded toys and played together. Tim was very alert while playing with the car. He made sure the car did not go too far away. Sue played with the boat near the water. She was also alert, so the boat did not get wet. After playing, Tim and Sue traded toys back. They were happy and had a good time. They said "bye" to each other and went home. Tim and Sue learned that sharing and trading toys can be fun.
