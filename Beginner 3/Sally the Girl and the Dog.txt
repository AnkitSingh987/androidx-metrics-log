Category: Beginner
Title: Sally the Girl and the Dog

One day, a little girl named Sally went to the store with her mom. They needed to buy yogurt for breakfast. "Yogurt is important for a strong body," said Sally's mom. Sally liked yogurt too. She wanted to be strong and support her friends when they needed help. At the store, Sally saw her friend Tim. Tim was sad. He wanted to buy yogurt, but he had no money. Sally had an idea. She went to her mom and asked if they could help Tim. Her mom smiled and said, "Of course, we can support our friends." Sally and her mom bought yogurt for Tim. Tim was very happy. But when they went outside, they saw a big dog eating all the yogurt! Sally, Tim, and Sally's mom were surprised. They did not know what to do. Then, Sally had another idea. She gave the dog a big hug, and the dog stopped eating the yogurt. The dog was just hungry and needed love. Now they all had yogurt and a new friend.

One day, a boy named Tim found a needle in his room. He did not like needles. Tim thought needles were disgusting. He wanted to show his mom the needle, so he raised it up high in his hand. Tim ran to his mom in the kitchen. "Mom, look what I found!" he said. His mom looked at the needle and was surprised. "Oh no, Tim! That's not good. We need to throw it away," she said. Tim's mom took the needle and put it in the trash. Just then, Tim's dad came home with a big smile. "I have a surprise!" he said. Tim and his mom went to see the surprise. It was a big, soft teddy bear! Tim hugged the bear and forgot all about the disgusting needle. They all laughed and played with the teddy bear, happy that the needle was gone.

One day, a small boy named Tim went to play outside. He liked to play in the dirt and discover new things. Today, he found something hard in the dirt. It was a nail. Tim was very happy to find the nail. Tim ran to his mom and said, "Look, Mom! I found a nail!" His mom was happy too, but she told Tim to be careful. "Nails can be sharp, so don't play with it too much," she said. The next day, Tim went to play again. It was difficult to find more nails, but he kept looking. Then, something unexpected happened. Tim found a big box buried in the dirt. He opened it and saw many toys inside. Tim was so happy and ran to tell his mom. They both knew it was a special day.

Once upon a time, there was a tutor named Tom. Tom was a very nice man, but he was poor. He lived in a small house with his dog, Max. Tom loved to teach kids how to read and write. One day, a girl named Lily came to Tom's house. She asked Tom to help her learn to read. Tom said yes and they started to learn together. Lily was very happy and she wanted to help Tom too. She had an idea. Lily went home and talked to her mom. Her mom helped her bring a big bag of food to Tom's house. When Tom saw the food, he was very happy. Now, Tom and Max had food to eat and Lily could learn to read. They all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a big, hairy dog named Max. Max loved to play all day. One day, Max found a round ball and a pot in the yard. Max rolled the ball with his nose. He rolled it fast and far. Then, he looked at the pot. Max had an idea. He put the ball in the pot and rolled the pot too. The pot went "clunk clunk clunk" as it rolled. Max's friend, a little girl named Lily, came outside. She saw Max rolling the pot. "Max, what are you doing?" she asked. Max barked and wagged his tail. "I see! You're playing with the pot and the ball!" said Lily. They both laughed and played together, rolling the pot and the ball until the sun went down.
