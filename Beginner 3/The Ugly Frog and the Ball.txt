Category: Beginner
Title: The Ugly Frog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was an ugly frog. The frog lived near a big pond. The frog liked to jump and play all day. He liked to make a lot of motion when he jumped. One day, the ugly frog saw a big ball near the pond. He wanted to play with the ball. He tried to push the ball, but he did not succeed. The ball was too big for the frog. Then, something unexpected happened. The big ball opened up, and a little girl came out. The girl was not a ball, but she was hiding. The ugly frog and the little girl became friends. They played and jumped together all day. The ugly frog was happy, and he did succeed in making a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a wise old cat named Whiskers. Whiskers loved to sit on the big chair near the window and watch the cars go by. One day, Whiskers saw a big, shiny automobile outside. It was red and went "beep beep!" Whiskers had an idea. He wanted to record the sound of the automobile so he could listen to it later. He found a small box that could record sounds. Whiskers sat by the window and waited for the automobile to go "beep beep" again. When the automobile made the sound, Whiskers pressed a button on the box to record it. He was very happy. Now, he could listen to the "beep beep" sound anytime he wanted. Whiskers was a wise cat who loved the big, shiny automobile and its fun sounds.

Once upon a time, there was a big red ball under a tree. A little boy, Tim, saw the ball and wanted to play with it. But there was a small box near the ball. The box had a lock on it. Tim knew he needed to unlock the box to play with the ball. Tim went to his mom and asked, "Mom, can you help me unlock the box?" His mom said, "Yes, but you must promise not to touch the spoiled food inside the box." Tim promised his mom he would not touch the spoiled food. Tim's mom unlocked the box, and Tim was happy to play with the big red ball. But he forgot his promise and opened the box. He saw the spoiled food and touched it. The spoiled food made Tim's hands dirty and smelly. Tim's mom was sad and said, "You broke your promise, and now you have to clean your hands and the box." Tim felt bad and learned he should always keep his promises.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with blocks. They make a big building with blocks. It has many windows and doors. They are happy. But then, a big dog comes. The dog is not mean, but he is curious. He sees the building with blocks. He wants to play too. He runs to the building and jumps on it. The building falls down. The blocks go everywhere. Lily and Ben are sad. The dog sees that Lily and Ben are sad. He feels sorry. He does not want to make them sad. He wants to say sorry. He does not know how to say sorry with words. He knows how to say sorry with his body. He lowers his head and his tail. He bows to Lily and Ben. He looks at them with his big eyes. Lily and Ben see that the dog is sorry. They are not angry anymore. They know that the dog did not mean to break their building. They know that the dog is honest. He is not a bad dog. He is a good dog. They smile at the dog. They pet the dog. They say, "It's okay. We can make another building. Do you want to help us?" The dog wags his tail. He is happy. He wants to help them. He helps them pick up the blocks. He helps them make a new building. He is careful not to break it. He is a good helper. Lily and Ben are happy. They have a new building and a new friend. They hug the dog. They say, "Thank you. You are a good dog. We like you." The dog licks their faces. He says, "Woof. Woof. I like you too." They all laugh. They all play. They are all happy. The end.
