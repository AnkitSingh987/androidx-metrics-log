Category: Beginner
Title: The Goat and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a chubby goat. The goat liked to play in the grass. The goat had many friends like the cat, the dog, and the duck. They all played together and had fun. One day, the goat found a big rock. He thought it would be fun to climb on the rock. The goat tried to climb, but he was too chubby. His friends, the cat, the dog, and the duck, tried to help him. They pushed and pulled, but the goat could not get on the rock. Then, something unexpected happened. The rock started to move! The goat and his friends were very surprised. They all stepped back and watched. The rock was not a rock at all! It was a big turtle! The turtle said, "Hello, I was sleeping." The goat and his friends laughed and played with the turtle. They all had a new friend and had more fun together.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a little flower. The little flower wanted to bloom and show its impressive colors. The sun shined and the rain fell, helping the flower grow. One day, a friendly girl named Anna came to the garden. She saw the little flower by her knee. She smiled and said, "Wow! You are so impressive!" The little flower felt happy and proud to bloom. Anna came to visit the little flower every day. They became good friends. The little flower bloomed even more, showing off its impressive colors. And they lived happily ever after.

Tom was a big dog with black fur. He liked to run and play in the park with his friend Sam, a small dog with brown fur. One day, they saw a new dog in the park. He was very big and had white fur. He looked mean and scary. Tom and Sam wanted to say hello to the new dog, but he growled at them. He said he was the boss of the park and no one could play there. He chased Tom and Sam away. Tom and Sam were sad and angry. They wanted to play in the park. The next day, Tom and Sam had a plan. They found a big stick and a rope. They tied the rope to the stick and hid it in the grass. They waited for the new dog to come. When they saw him, they ran to him and barked. The new dog was angry and chased them. Tom and Sam ran to the stick and the rope. They jumped over it, but the new dog did not. He stumbled on the rope and fell on the stick. He yelped and ran away. Tom and Sam were happy. They had tricked the new dog. They played in the park with their fur. They were fine.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She was playing at a park filled with trees and grass. She was running around and having a great time! Suddenly, Lily felt something under her foot. She looked down and saw a small stone. It was a fine stone, smooth and shiny. She picked it up and studied it carefully. "Do you like it?" a voice said behind her. Lily was so surprised that she gave a jump and her heart started to tremble. She turned around, and saw an old lady standing beside her. The old lady smiled and held out her hand. "It is a very fine stone," she said gently. "Would you like to keep it?" Lily nodded and smiled, then put the stone in her pocket. With a happy giggle, she ran off to play the rest of the afternoon. The old lady watched her fondly, then walked away into the sun.

Mark is a jolly boy who likes to play outside. He likes to see the birds, the flowers, the trees and the sky. He likes to smell the grass, the dirt and the rain. He likes to hear the wind, the water and the animals. He likes to touch the leaves, the rocks and the bugs. He likes to explore nature. One day, Mark finds a big stick in the park. He thinks it is a good tool for making marks. He drags the stick on the ground and makes lines, circles and squiggles. He marks his name, his age and his favorite things. He marks a sun, a cloud and a star. He marks a house, a car and a bike. He marks a cat, a dog and a fish. He is very proud of his marks. He sees a girl sitting on a bench. She is holding a book and a crayon. She is making marks too, but on paper. Mark wants to show her his marks. He runs to her and says, "Hi, I am Mark. Look at my marks. They are on the ground. Do you like them?" The girl looks up and smiles. She says, "Hi, I am Amy. Your marks are very nice. They are big and colorful. I like them. Look at my marks. They are in my book. Do you like them?" Mark looks at her book and nods. He says, "Your marks are very nice too. They are small and neat. I like them. Can we be friends?" Amy nods and says, "Yes, we can be friends. Do you want to make marks together?" Mark says, "Yes, I do. Can I use your crayon?" Amy says, "Yes, you can. Can I use your stick?" Mark says, "Yes, you can. Let's make marks together." They share their tools and make marks together. They make marks on the ground and in the book. They make marks of nature and of fun. They make marks of friendship and of joy. They are very jolly.
