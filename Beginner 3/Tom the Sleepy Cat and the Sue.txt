Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Sleepy Cat and the Sue

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy cat named Tom. Tom lived in a small house with his best friend, a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with Tom all day long. But one day, Tom was very, very sleepy. Sue wanted to help Tom feel better. She thought, "Maybe Tom is sleepy because his bed is dirty." Sue looked at Tom's bed and saw it was full of laundry. Sue knew she had to solve this problem to help Tom feel better. Sue took all the laundry and washed it. She made Tom's bed all clean and cozy. When Tom saw his clean bed, he was so happy. He jumped into his bed and had the best sleep ever. From that day on, Tom was never too sleepy to play with Sue again.

One day, a cat and a dog were in a park. The cat was glad and the dog was sad. The cat asked the dog, "Why are you sad?" The dog said, "I can't dance to the rhythm." The cat said, "I can teach you how to dance!" The dog nodded and they started to dance. The cat showed the dog how to move to the rhythm. The dog tried and tried but couldn't get it right. Just then, a bird flew by and saw them. The bird said, "I can help you dance, dog!" The dog nodded again, and the bird started to sing a new song. This time, the dog danced to the rhythm perfectly! The cat, the dog, and the bird were all glad and danced together in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a magnet who lived in a toy box. He had many friends like a ball, a doll, and a car. They liked to play together every day. But one day, they had a quarrel. The magnet wanted to play with the car, but the ball wanted to play with the car too. The magnet and the ball were not careful with their words. They said mean things to each other. This made the car and the doll very sad. They did not like it when their friends quarrel. One day, the car and the doll decided to help the magnet and the ball become friends again. They told the magnet and the ball to be careful with their words and be kind. The magnet and the ball listened and said sorry to each other. They all played together happily again. And from that day on, they were always careful not to quarrel and stayed the best of friends.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play in the cabin in the woods. The cabin is green and has a big door and a small window. Lily and Ben have many toys in the cabin. They have dolls, cars, balls, and books. One day, Lily and Ben want to dress up as pirates. They find some old clothes and hats in the cabin. They put on the clothes and hats and pretend to be pirates. They say "Arrr!" and "Ahoy!" and "Shiver me timbers!" They have a lot of fun. But then, they hear a loud noise outside. They look out the window and see a big bear. The bear is brown and furry and has sharp teeth and claws. The bear is hungry and smells the food in the cabin. The bear wants to eat the food and maybe the twins too. Lily and Ben are scared. They do not want to be eaten by the bear. They hide under the bed and whisper to each other. "What do we do?" Lily asks. "I don't know," Ben says. "Maybe we can scare the bear away," Lily says. "How?" Ben asks. "Maybe we can make a loud noise and wave our hats and pretend to be pirates," Lily says. "Maybe," Ben says. They decide to try. They crawl out from under the bed and grab their hats. They open the door and shout at the bear. "Arrr! Go away, bear! We are pirates and we have swords and cannons and we will fight you!" they say. The bear is surprised and confused. He does not like loud noises and he does not know what pirates are. He thinks the twins are strange and funny. He decides he does not want to eat them. He turns around and runs away. Lily and Ben are happy and relieved. They hug each other and laugh. "We did it! We scared the bear away!" they say. They go back to the cabin and eat their food. They play some more and then go home. They tell their mom and dad about their adventure. They are proud of being brave pirates. They love their green cabin in the woods.
