Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Notebook

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a special notebook that she loved. She drew pictures and wrote her name in it every day. One day, Lily could not find her notebook. She looked everywhere in her room, but it was not there. Lily started to feel very sad. She felt miserable because her notebook was lost. Lily's mom saw her crying and asked what was wrong. Lily told her mom about the lost notebook. Her mom helped her look for it, and they found it under Lily's bed. Lily felt very happy when she saw her notebook. She hugged her mom and thanked her for helping. From that day on, Lily always put her notebook in a safe place when she was done using it.

Sara was a curious girl who liked to explore the park. She saw many things that made her happy, like flowers, birds and bugs. But her favorite thing was to look for leaves that had different shapes and colors. She hoped to find a leaf that was very special, like a star or a heart. One day, she saw a big tree that had many leaves. She ran to the tree and looked up. She saw a leaf that was very big and shiny. It was yellow and red, like a fire. Sara wanted to have the leaf, but it was too high for her to reach. She tried to jump, but she was too small. "Can you help me, please?" she asked a boy who was playing nearby. He had a ball and a hat. He looked at Sara and the leaf. "Why do you want that leaf?" he asked. "Because it is very pretty and special. I hope to have it for my collection," Sara said. The boy shrugged. He threw his ball at the leaf, but he missed. He tried again, but the ball bounced back. He tried a third time, but the ball got stuck in the tree. "Oh no, my ball!" he cried. Sara felt sorry for the boy. She had an idea. She took off her hat and gave it to him. "Here, you can use my hat. It is big and soft. Maybe it can catch the leaf and your ball," she said. The boy smiled. He thanked Sara and took her hat. He threw it at the tree, and it worked. The hat caught the leaf and the ball, and they fell to the ground. "Wow, you are very smart and kind," the boy said. He gave Sara the leaf and the ball. "Here, you can have both. They are yours." Sara was very happy. She hugged the boy and thanked him. She had a new friend and a new leaf. She hoped they would play together again.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He loved to kick a ball high in the sky. One day, Tim went to school. He had an exam that day. Tim was a little scared. In the classroom, Tim sat next to his friend, Sam. Sam said, "Don't be scared, Tim. We can do the exam." Tim smiled at Sam and they both started the exam. After the exam, Tim and Sam went outside to play. They saw a big, high tree. Tim kicked the ball high and it got stuck in the tree. Tim and Sam laughed and tried to get the ball down. They were happy to be done with the exam and play together.

Once upon a time, there was a hard rock. On the rock, there was a small herb. Every day, a girl named Lily would come and look at the herb. She wanted to have it, but she did not know how to get it. One day, Lily met a boy named Tom. She asked him, "Can you help me get the herb?" Tom said, "Yes, I can help you." They went to the rock and tried to pull the herb out. But it was very hard to do. They tried and tried, but the herb would not come out. They pulled so hard that the herb broke. Lily and Tom were very sad. They wanted to have the herb, but now it was gone. The end.
