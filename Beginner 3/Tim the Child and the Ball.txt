Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Child and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little child named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toys in his open room. One day, Tim saw a big, red ball on a high shelf. He wanted to play with it, but he could not reach it. Tim called his friend, Sam. Sam was a tall boy. Tim said, "Sam, can you help me reach the big, red ball?" Sam smiled and said, "Yes, I can help you." So, Sam came over to Tim's house. Sam reached up high and got the big, red ball for Tim. Tim was so happy. They both played with the big, red ball in the open room all day long. Tim and Sam had a lot of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to go to the shop with his mom. One day, they went to a big shop to buy a new seat for their car. Tim was very excited. In the shop, Tim saw a seat that he liked a lot. It was blue and soft. But his mom said they needed a serious seat, not just a fun one. Tim was sad and didn't want to pick another seat. He wanted the blue one. Tim's mom talked to him and said they could get a serious seat that was also fun. They looked around and found a strong seat that was also blue. Tim was happy now. They bought the seat and went home. Tim loved the new seat, and his mom was happy that it was safe and serious.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy some food for dinner. Tim was very alert and looked around at all the things in the store. As they walked down an aisle, Tim saw a big crack on the floor. "Look, Mom! A big crack!" he said. His mom looked down and saw the crack too. "Be careful, Tim. Don't step on it," she warned. Just then, a little mouse came out of the crack. It was unexpected! The mouse looked at Tim and said, "Hello! I live in the crack. Can you help me find some cheese?" Tim and his mom were surprised, but they decided to help the little mouse find some cheese. In the end, they all became good friends.

Mama was at work when she heard a baby crying. She thought, "Who could that be?" Mama ran to the crib and saw Baby. He was very bossy; he didn't like to be left alone. Mama hugged Baby, and Baby stopped crying. Mama said, "Oh, Baby! Mama loves you. You can't be so bossy!" Baby smiled and said, "Mama, think with me?" Mama laughed and said, "OK, Baby. Let's think about our day." They thought about all the fun things they could do, and Baby laughed with joy. Mama put Baby in the crib and said, "Good night, sweet Baby. Mama will come see you in the morning." Baby smiled and said, "OK, Mama. Love you." Mama kissed Baby's forehead, said the one more time, "I love you," and left the room.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy was a very lucky girl. She loved to draw and make pretty patterns with her crayons. One day, Lucy wanted to improve her patterns, so she decided to go on a walk to look for new ideas. On her walk, Lucy saw a big tree with beautiful leaves. She thought the leaves would help her improve her patterns. So, she picked some leaves and went back home to draw. Lucy was excited to use the leaves to make her patterns even better. But, something unexpected happened. When Lucy started to draw with the leaves, they made her patterns look messy. Her pretty patterns did not improve, and they were not as nice as before. Lucy was sad that her lucky day turned out to be not so lucky after all.

Once upon a time, there was a big giant. He had an unusual smile. He lived in a big house. The giant was very nice. He liked to play with kids. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the giant's house. "Hello, giant," Tim said. "I like your smile. Can we play?" The giant smiled and said, "Yes, we can play. Let's play with the ball." Tim and the giant played with the ball. They had a lot of fun. They laughed and smiled. The giant's unusual smile made Tim feel happy. After they played, Tim said, "Thank you, giant. I had fun. I want to come back and play again." The giant smiled and said, "You can come back anytime, Tim. I like to play with you too." And from that day on, Tim and the giant became the best of friends.
