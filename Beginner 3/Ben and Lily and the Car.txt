Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Lily and the Car

Ben and Lily are playing with their toy cars in the garden. They make a road with sand and stones. They have fun racing their cars and making noises. "Look, Lily, my car is faster than yours!" Ben says. "No, it's not, Ben, my car is better than yours!" Lily says. They laugh and keep playing. Then they see a big bag of powder near the fence. It is white and soft. They wonder what it is. "Let's see what's in the bag, Ben!" Lily says. "OK, Lily, but be careful!" Ben says. They go to the bag and open it. They touch the powder. It feels nice and cool. They take some and throw it in the air. It makes a cloud of white dust. "Wow, this is fun, Ben!" Lily says. "Yeah, this is fun, Lily!" Ben says. They play with the powder for a while. They make shapes and letters with it. They sprinkle it on their cars and on each other. They giggle and sneeze. Then they hear their mom calling them. "Ben, Lily, time to finish playing and come inside!" Mom says. "OK, Mom, we're coming!" Ben and Lily say. They put their cars in their basket and close the bag of powder. They run to the door. They are covered in white dust. Mom sees them and gasps. "What have you been doing, Ben and Lily?" Mom asks. "We were playing with the powder, Mom!" Ben and Lily say. "What powder?" Mom asks. "The powder in the bag, Mom!" Ben and Lily say. Mom looks at the bag and sees the label. It says "Flour". "Oh, no, Ben and Lily, that's not powder, that's flour! That's for baking, not for playing!" Mom says. "Oops, sorry, Mom!" Ben and Lily say. Mom sighs and smiles. "It's OK, Ben and Lily, but you need to wash up now. You're very dirty and sticky!" Mom says. "OK, Mom, we'll wash up!" Ben and Lily say. They go to the bathroom and wash their faces and hands. They see themselves in the mirror and laugh. "We look like snowmen, Ben!" Lily says. "Yeah, we do, Lily!" Ben says. They hug each other and say. "That was fun, Ben!" "That was fun, Lily!"

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lucy went to the beach with her mom and dad. Lucy loved the soft sand under her feet. She liked to play near the water and watch the waves. Lucy's mom and dad sat on a big towel and watched her play. Lucy saw a big wave coming. She wanted to jump over it. Lucy ran to the wave and jumped high. But the wave was too big. Lucy fell into the water. She felt scared and began to cry. Lucy's mom and dad heard her cry and ran to help her. They pulled her out of the water. Lucy sighed and hugged her mom and dad. They were wet and sad. The beach was not fun anymore. They went home with a bad day at the beach.

Once upon a time there lived a boy called James. He loved playing but he was not good at drawing. One day he wanted to draw a picture but he didn't know how. He tried very hard but he could not do it. James wanted to ask someone to help him. He asked his friend Joe if he could have a pencil to draw a picture. Joe said that James did not need a pencil. He needed knowledge. James was confused. Joe explained that he did not need a pencil. Anyone can use a pencil. To draw nice pictures, he needed to learn how to draw. He needed to learn everything about the art of drawing. James asked his father and his mother. They both said the same thing as Joe. To draw nice pictures he needed to be learned, not to have a pencil. James understood that knowledge is a powerful thing. He kept trying until he became very good at drawing. He realized that ignorant people need pencils, but to do big things, people need knowledge.

Jimmy was busy. He had to do the laundry. His mommy said to put all the dirty clothes in the laundry basket. Jimmy felt confused. He didn't know what to do. Jimmy took a deep breath and grabbed the laundry basket. He started to pick up all the dirty clothes. Jimmy felt scared. He wished his mommy was there to help him. But he was brave and he kept going. Finally, Jimmy was done. All the dirty clothes were in the basket. He was very proud of himself. Jimmy felt happy. He had finished the laundry all on his own!
