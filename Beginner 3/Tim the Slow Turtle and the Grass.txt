Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Slow Turtle and the Grass

Once upon a time, there was a slow turtle named Tim. Tim had a friend named Bunny. They liked to play and eat together. One day, they went to a picnic. At the picnic, Tim saw a fork on the grass. "Look, Bunny, a fork!" Tim said. Bunny hopped over and looked at it. "Yes, a fork!" Bunny said. They used the fork to eat their yummy food. After they ate, Tim and Bunny played a game. Tim mentioned, "Let's play tag!" Bunny agreed, and they played tag until the sun went down. They had a great day together.

Once upon a time, there was a little blue boat. The boat lived in a harbor. The harbor was the boat's home. The boat liked to play with its friends, the fish and the birds. One day, the boat did something silly. It made a big splash with its oars. All the fish and birds laughed at the boat. The boat felt embarrass. It didn't like when its friends laughed at it. The boat decided to be kind and help its friends. It helped the fish find food and gave the birds a ride. The fish and birds were happy. They all learned that being kind is better than making fun of others. And the boat was no longer embarrass.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Jack. He was three years old. He was sad because he had just skinned his knee. He was very brave and cried only a little bit. His mommy came in and kissed his knee. Then she put a bandage on it. The bandage was bright and colorful. She told Jack it would help his knee get better. Next, she gave Jack a yummy treat. It was a cookie! He smiled and was happy again! He squealed with joy as he ate his yummy treat. At the end, Jack was all better again. He grabbed his toy cars and ran outside to play with his friends. Mommy said he didn't need the bandage anymore. She had taken good care of Jack!

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toy rod. One day, Tim saw a modern house. The house was big and pretty. Tim wanted to go inside and look. Tim walked to the door and knocked. A kind woman opened the door. "Hello," she said. "My name is Sue. What's your name?" Tim said, "I am Tim. I like your house. Can I come in?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, you can come in, Tim." Tim went into the house and saw many new things. He played with his rod and had fun. Tim tried to do a trick with his rod, but it was hard. He tried again and again. At last, Tim succeeded! Sue clapped her hands and said, "Good job, Tim!" Tim was happy and proud.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a bear named Ben. Ben was a big, brown bear who liked to play with his friends. One day, Ben saw a bird lying on the ground. He thought the bird was dead. "Are you okay?" Ben asked the bird. The bird opened its eyes and said, "I'm just tired. I need a little rest." Ben was happy the bird was not dead. Ben and the bird became good friends. They played together in the forest every day. Ben would think of fun games to play, and the bird would sing songs. They were very happy and had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time there was a jolly man called Jack. One day Jack was rolling down a hill when he spotted a beautiful white veil. He wondered what it was so he decided to pick it up. He went to his best friend Lily and asked, "What is this?" "It looks like a veil!" smiled Lily. Jack then decided to have some fun. He put on the veil and started to roll around the garden. The two of them laughed and Jack said, "This is more jolly than rolling down the hill!" After playing for a while, they decided to hang the veil on the tree and keep it safe until the next time they wanted to play with it.
