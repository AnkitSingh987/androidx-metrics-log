Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Sam and the Hill

Ben and Sam are brothers. They like to play with their toy jeep. The jeep is amazing. It can go fast and make noises. Ben and Sam pretend they are soldiers. They supply food and water to their friends. One day, they find a big hill in the park. They want to drive the jeep up the hill. But the hill is too steep. The jeep slides back down. Ben and Sam try again and again. They get angry and sad. "Let me drive!" Ben says. He grabs the jeep from Sam. "No, it's my turn!" Sam says. He pulls the jeep back. They start to fight. They push and shout. The jeep falls on the ground. It breaks into pieces. Ben and Sam stop fighting. They look at the jeep. They feel sorry. They hug each other. They say they are sorry. They pick up the pieces. They go home. They ask their mom to fix the jeep. Mom says she will try. She says they should share and be nice. Ben and Sam nod. They love their jeep. They love their mom. They love each other.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim found a dirty, glowing rock near a big tree. He picked it up and saw that it was very pretty. Tim wanted to show it to his friend, Sue. "Look, Sue! I found a rock that can glow!" Tim said. Sue's eyes grew big and she smiled. "Wow, Tim! That's so cool! Let's go show it to our friends!" So, they ran to their friends, holding the glowing rock. They found their friends near the open field, playing with a ball. When they showed the glowing rock to their friends, everyone was amazed. "Let's play a game with the rock," said one friend. They all agreed and started playing catch with the glowing rock. As they played, the sun began to set. The rock seemed to glow even brighter in the dark. Tim, Sue, and their friends played until they were tired and dirty from running around. When it was time to go home, Tim put the glowing rock in a special box. He knew it was a magical rock that brought them all together. From that day on, Tim, Sue, and their friends played with the glowing rock every day, making many happy memories.

Pecan was very excited. It was time to play in her garden. She put on her gardening gloves and started to work. Pecan planted some flowers and watered them, then she said, "Now these flowers will grow and be so beautiful!" Pecan stepped back and noticed something strange. It was too wet! She asked her mum, "Why is the ground so wet?" Her mum replied, "I don't think it is normal. We should go inside and see what is going on". Pecan and her mum went inside their house. They opened the door to the living room and found the entire area flooded with water. Pecan was very disappointed, nothing was normal anymore. Her dream garden was ruined and it was all because of the wetness.

One day, a big dog and a little cat went on a walk. They march, march, march down the road. They saw a big, open field. The dog said, "Let's play here!" The cat said, "Yes, let's run and jump!" As they played, they found a big, shiny sink in the middle of the field. "What is this?" asked the cat. The dog said, "I don't know. Let's look inside." They both look in the sink, but they could not see the bottom. Suddenly, a big frog jumped out of the sink! The dog and the cat were very surprised. The frog said, "Hello, I live in this sink. Do you want to be my friends?" The dog and the cat were happy to have a new friend. They all played together in the big, open field.

He pointed to an impressive red stove that had been placed outside. His mom smiled. "Yes, Jack," she said. "It looks good, doesn't it? It must be very heavy." Jack nodded and moved towards the stove. He reached out and held onto it with both hands. "See mommy? I'm holding it!" he said proudly. His mom smiled again and said, "Yes, that is very impressive, Jack. But you should be careful. That stove is probably very hot in the summer sun." Jack backed away from the stove, looking a bit worried. "It's alright Jack," his mom said. "Let's go inside and have a snack. I bet it won't be so hot in the house." Jack nodded and they went in. After they ate, Jack asked his mom if they could go back outside and look at the stove again.
