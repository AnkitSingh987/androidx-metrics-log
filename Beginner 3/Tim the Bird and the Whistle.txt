Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Bird and the Whistle

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim loved to whistle all day long. One day, Tim saw a big pie on the table in a house. He wanted to eat the pie, but he was worried. His mom told him to never go into houses. Tim could not stop thinking about the pie. He wanted to eat it so bad. So, he made a plan. He would whistle a nice song for the people in the house. Maybe they would share the pie with him. The next day, Tim went to the house and started to whistle his best song. The people inside heard him and smiled. They liked Tim's song so much that they decided to share the pie with him. Tim was so happy, and he enjoyed the pie with his new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to play outside with her friends. One day, Sue found a big rock in her yard. The rock was very big and round. Sue thought it was a good rock to sit on. Sue called her friend, Tom, to come see the rock. Tom came over and saw the rock. They played a game where they spun around the rock. They spun so much that they got dizzy. They laughed and laughed because they felt so silly. After they stopped spinning, Sue and Tom felt dizzy. They sat down on the rock to rest. Sue gave Tom a big hug, and they smiled at each other. They knew they had found a fun new place to play.

Mummy and Daddy were playing hide and seek with their little girl, who was 3 years old. Daddy said to her, "You stand here and count to ten, ok?". She nodded her head and said, "Ok!". With that, Daddy disappeared into a strange cupboard. Mummy and the little girl started counting together. "One, two, three, four, five...," they counted. When they reached ten, the little girl opened her eyes and saw something incredible - Daddy had disappeared into the cupboard! The little girl smiled and asked her Mummy, "Where is Daddy?". Mummy laughed and said, "I don't know, maybe he is still in the cupboard". The little girl opened the cupboard and found Daddy! She was amazed and shouted, "Daddy! You were in the cupboard all along!". Daddy said, "Yes, I was. It was a great hiding place!". The little girl thought it was incredible and gave Daddy a big hug.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big spin toy. Tim wanted to play on it. He ran to the spin toy and saw a girl named Sue. Sue was spinning so fast. Tim said, "Can I play too?" Sue was rude and said, "No, it's my spin toy!" Tim was sad but he had an idea. He saw a print on the ground. It was a map to find a new spin toy. Tim followed the print to find the new toy. Tim found the new spin toy, but it was broken. He tried to spin it, but it didn't work. Sue saw Tim and laughed. Then, the spin toy Sue was on broke too. Now, both spin toys were broken and no one could play.

One day, a big boy named Tim went to the park. The sun was bright and the sky was blue. Tim saw a box near a tree. The box had a pretty bow on it. Tim knew it was a gift. Tim wanted to lift the gift, but it was very heavy. He tried and tried, but he could not lift it alone. Tim asked his friend, Sam, to help him. Together, they could lift the gift. They were strong. Inside the gift was a rare toy. It was a big red ball that could bounce very high. Tim and Sam played with the rare red ball all day. They had a lot of fun. They knew the gift was special because they had to work together to lift it.

Once upon a time, there was a big panda who had a very special skill. He could perform amazing tricks! Everybody was so impressed with his performance. He delighted audience after audience with his show. One day he was putting on his act when a thief came along and tried to steal his things. The panda was so angry! He performed a very loud roar and chased the thief away. The thief was so scared; he ran away as fast as he could. But the panda was determined to get his stuff back. He leaped high into the air and performed a crazy spin that sent the thief into a spin. The thief dropped all of the panda's things and he was finally able to get them back. The thief had learned his lesson; never steal from a panda when they're doing their show!
