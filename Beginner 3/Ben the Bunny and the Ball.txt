Category: Beginner
Title: Ben the Bunny and the Ball

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a little bunny named Ben. Ben loved to hop and play with his friends all day long. One day, Ben and his friends found a big ball. They played pass with the ball, laughing and having fun. But then, Ben got sad. He forgot where he put his favorite toy, a small red car. He looked under the trees, near the river, and behind the rocks, but he could not find it. Ben's friends saw that he was sad, so they stopped playing with the ball and decided to help him find his toy. Together, they looked all around the forest. Finally, one of Ben's friends, a tiny squirrel, found the red car under a big leaf. Ben was so happy and thanked his friends for helping him. They all played pass with the ball and the red car, smiling and having a great time. And Ben never forgot how lucky he was to have such good friends.

Once, a little boy named Tim had a big orange ball. He loved to play with his ball every day. One day, he saw a big race in the park. Tim wanted to win the race and get a trophy. Tim ran very fast in the race. He passed many kids and said "thank you" when they cheered for him. Tim was so close to the finish line. He could see the shiny trophy waiting for him. But then, Tim tripped on a rock and fell. He did not win the race. He did not get the trophy. Tim was sad and his orange ball rolled away. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big red car. A man with a hat loved to drive the car. The man liked to drive fast and sometimes he was careless. One day, the man drove his car to the park. He wore his hat and he was happy. But he was careless and drove too fast. He hit a big rock and his hat flew off his head. The man was sad because he lost his hat. He looked for his hat and found it under a tree. He said sorry to his car and promised to be careful. From that day, the man with the hat drove his car slowly and carefully.

Once upon a time, there was a little red bird named Bob. Bob loved to sing all day long. One day, he found a stick on the ground. Bob was very happy because he could use the stick to make music by tapping it on the trees. Bob was so excited that he wanted to record his music so he could share it with all his friends. He asked his friend, the wise old owl, how to do this. The owl told him he needed to find a magic rock that could record his music. Bob was anxious because he didn't know where to find the magic rock. Bob looked everywhere for the magic rock. He searched high and low, near and far. Finally, he found the magic rock under a big tree. Bob tapped the stick on the tree and made music. The magic rock recorded his music, just like the wise old owl said it would. Now Bob could share his music with all his friends, and they danced and sang along to his happy tunes.

Lily and Ben are brother and sister. They like to play outside in the sun. They have a ball and a bike. They share and take turns. They are friendly. One day, they see a big dog. The dog is brown and furry. It has a long tail and sharp teeth. The dog barks and runs to them. Lily and Ben are scared. They want to go home. "Run, Lily, run!" Ben says. He pushes Lily on the bike. Lily pedals fast. Ben runs behind her. The dog chases them. Lily and Ben are almost home. They see their mom and dad. They wave and shout. "Help, help, a dog!" But then, Ben steps on a rock. He falls down. The dog catches him. The dog bites him. Ben screams. Lily cries. Mom and dad run to help. But it is too late. Ben is hurt. The dog is bad. The end.
