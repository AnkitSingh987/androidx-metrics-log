Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big jar. He liked to load his jar with cool rocks he found outside. One day, Tim saw a very cool rock. He wanted to put it in his jar, but his jar was full. Tim went to his friend, Sam. Sam had a jar too. Tim asked Sam if he could put his cool rock in Sam's jar. Sam said no. Tim was sad. He did not know what to do with his cool rock. Tim's mom saw him and said, "Share your cool rocks with others." Tim thought about it and decided to give some of his cool rocks to his friends. Now, he had room for his new cool rock in his jar. Tim and his friends were all happy. Tim learned that sharing is good.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy boy named Tim. Tim liked to look at everything around him. He wanted to know all about the world. One day, he saw a big, red ball in the park. Tim wanted to play with the ball, so he went to the park. In the park, Tim met a dog. The dog was big and had a loud bark. Tim wanted to trust the dog, so he tried to pet it. But the dog did not like Tim. It growled at him and scared him away. Tim was sad, but he still wanted to play with the big, red ball. Suddenly, the ball popped! It was not a ball at all, but a big, red balloon. Tim was very surprised and upset. He went home and told his mom about the nosy boy, the dog, and the big, red balloon.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree in the park. The tree had many green leaves. Kids loved to play near the tree. Birds liked to sing in the tree. The tree was very happy. One day, it became dark outside. The sun went to sleep. The tree felt cold and alone. A little girl named Lily saw the tree. She wanted to make the tree feel better. Lily walked up to the tree and gave it a big kiss. The tree felt warm and happy again. Lily said, "I love you, tree!" The tree smiled and said, "I love you too, Lily!" They were both happy, even when it was dark outside.

Once there was a thin jellyfish. He loved to swim in the sea, but it seemed like something was missing. He just didn't feel full. Suddenly, he noticed a fishing boat nearby and someone threw a line into the water. He was excited because he thought he was finally going to fill up. The jellyfish swam up to the boat and saw it was filled with lots of yummy things. He could hardly wait to eat. He wiggled around and around the boat, until someone grabbed his thin body. He felt scared, but when he looked up, he saw it was the fisherman. The fisherman filled his hungry tummy with lots of goodies from the boat. Then he carefully put the jellyfish back into the water. Now the jellyfish was full and happy. He swam away with a smile on his face.

One day, a cat named Sam and a dog named Max were playing in the park. They liked to perform tricks for each other. Sam could jump high, and Max could run fast. Sam found a tray in the park. He said, "Max, let's play a game with this tray." Max said, "Okay, what game can we play?" Sam said, "We can see who can make the loudest noise with the tray." Max went first. He put the tray on the ground and jumped on it. It made a loud noise. Sam said, "Good job, Max! That was loud." Then it was Sam's turn. He picked up the tray with his mouth and dropped it from high in the air. It made a very loud noise when it hit the ground. Max said, "Wow, Sam! That was even louder than mine!" They both laughed and continued to play with the tray, making loud noises and having fun together in the park.
