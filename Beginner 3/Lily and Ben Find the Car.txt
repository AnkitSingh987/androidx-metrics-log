Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben Find the Car

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with toys. Lily has a doll and Ben has a car. One day, Ben wants to play with the doll. He asks Lily to trade. "Can I have your doll? I will give you my car," Ben says. Lily likes her doll. She does not want to trade. She says no to Ben. "Please, Lily, just for a little while. I want to see how the doll works," Ben says. Lily still says no. She hugs her doll. She does not want to let go. Ben gets angry. He tries to take the doll from Lily. He pulls hard. Lily pulls back. She does not want to lose her doll. They pull and pull. The doll breaks. The head falls off. The arms fall off. The dress tears. Lily and Ben stop pulling. They look at the doll. They are both sad. They are both sorry. They wish they did not pull the doll. They wish they did not break the doll. They wish they were still friends. But it is too late. The doll is broken. The friendship is broken. They cry.

One day, a girl named Lily went to play outside. She wore her favorite blouse. It was red and had pretty flowers on it. She liked to look at the pretty flowers. While playing, Lily saw her friend, Tim. Tim looked at her blouse and said, "Lily, your blouse is dirty!" Lily looked down and saw that her blouse was covered in mud. It looked terrible! She was sad. Lily went home and showed her mom the dirty blouse. Her mom said, "Don't worry, Lily. We can clean it." They washed the blouse together, and it became clean and pretty again. Lily was happy, and she said, "Thank you, Mom!"

Lily was a little girl who loved to sing. She sang every day at home, at school, and with her friends. She had a big dream: to sing on the big stage, where many people could hear her. One day, she saw a poster on the wall. It said: "Sing for the Stars! A talent show for kids. Come and show your voice. The winner will sing with a famous singer!" Lily was very excited. She wanted to join the show and sing with a famous singer. She told her mom and dad about the show. They were happy for her and helped her practice. They also bought her a pretty dress and a shiny microphone. They said: "You are our star, Lily. We are proud of you." The day of the show came. Lily and her parents went to the big stage. There were many kids there, and many people in the seats. Lily felt nervous. She saw the famous singer, who was the judge. He smiled and waved at the kids. Lily was the last one to sing. She walked to the stage, holding her microphone. She looked at the people and the judge. She felt her legs tremble. She wanted to run away. She thought: "I can't do this. I'm too scared." Then she heard her mom and dad say: "We love you, Lily. You can do it. Just sing your song." She also heard her friends say: "Go, Lily. You are the best. We are with you." She felt their love and support. She took a deep breath and opened her mouth. She sang her song, loud and clear. She sang with all her heart. The people clapped and cheered. The judge smiled and said: "Wow, Lily. You have a beautiful voice. You are a star. You are the winner. Come and sing with me." He gave her a hug and a trophy. He took her hand and led her to the center of the stage. They sang a song together, while the people clapped and cheered. Lily was very happy. She had sung on the big stage, with a famous singer. She had made her dream come true. She thanked her mom and dad, her friends, and the judge. She said: "This is the best day of my life. I love singing. I love you all."

One day, little Jack and his mom went to the theater. Jack was excited to see the show, but he was very scared of the dark. "Look, Jack," said Mom, pointing to the big stage. "It's clear and bright up there. You can see the lights and the actors. There's nothing to be scared of. Jack looked at the stage and pushed away his fear. Taking a deep breath, he stepped inside the theater. Mom took Jack to their seats and whispered in his ear, "Now remember to be quiet, okay?" Jack nodded and concentrated on the show. He watched the actors with wide eyes, forgetting that he was scared. Then, when the show was over, Mom said to Jack, "Did you have fun?" He smiled and said, "Yes, I did!"
