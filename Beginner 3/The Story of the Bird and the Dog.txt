Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Bird and the Dog

One day, a big, generous dog found a baby bird. The baby bird was lost and could not fly. The dog said, "I will help you find your mom and dad." The dog and baby bird went on a long walk. They met a wise, old cat. The cat said, "I know where the baby bird's mom and dad are. But first, you must show me what you value most." The dog thought and thought. He loved his big, soft bed, but he knew the baby bird was more important. The dog took the cat to his home and showed him the baby bird. The cat smiled and said, "You passed my test. You value friendship more than things. I will show you where the baby bird's mom and dad are." The cat led them to a tall tree, and there were the baby bird's mom and dad. The baby bird was so happy to see them. The dog and cat had become friends, and they all lived happily together.

Once upon a time, there was a little jet. The jet was sad and gloomy because it could not fly high in the sky. It wanted to fly with the birds and see the world, but its mom and dad did not permit it. One day, the little jet met a big, strong bird. The bird saw the jet was gloomy and asked, "Why are you sad, little jet?" The jet told the bird that it was not allowed to fly high in the sky. The bird felt bad for the jet and wanted to help. The bird talked to the jet's mom and dad. It told them how much the little jet wanted to fly high in the sky. The mom and dad thought about it and decided to permit the jet to fly. The little jet was so happy! It thanked the bird and flew high in the sky, no longer gloomy.

Once upon a time, there was a dish named Dolly. Dolly was a weak dish because she was very old. She lived in a kitchen with other dishes, cups, and spoons. They were all friends and liked to play together. One hot day, an ice cream cone named Icy came to visit. "Hi, Icy!" said Dolly. "Hi, Dolly!" said Icy. "It's so hot today, I might melt!" Icy was scared because she didn't want to melt. Dolly wanted to help her friend. Dolly had an idea. She asked all her friends in the kitchen to help keep Icy cool. The cups and spoons made a big fan to blow cool air on Icy. Dolly let Icy sit on her, so she didn't have to touch the hot floor. They all worked together to help Icy stay cool and not melt. Icy was very happy and thanked all her friends.

Once upon a time, there was a cheap, little toy named Hook. Hook was a tiny car that had a hook on the back. Hook was very good at helping others. One day, Hook was playing with his friends in the toy box. They were having fun, but then they saw a big ball stuck under the bed. All the toys wanted to play with the ball, but they could not get it out. Hook had an idea to solve the problem. Hook drove under the bed and used his hook to pull the ball out. All the toys were so happy! They cheered for Hook and played with the ball all day. Hook felt proud that he could solve the problem and help his friends.

One day, a friendly caterpillar named Bob was in the garden. He saw a big green leaf and wanted to eat it. He hoped the leaf would taste good. Bob met another caterpillar named Sue. Sue said, "I hope we can be friends!" Bob said, "Yes, I want to be friends too!" They both ate the big green leaf together. They were very happy. Then, something unexpected happened. Bob and Sue started to feel strange. They were changing! They became beautiful butterflies. They learned that when you share and are friendly, good things happen. They flew away, happy and free.

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog named Max. Max was a very good dog. He liked to play with his ball and run in the park. Max had a friend named Tim. Tim was a little boy. Tim and Max were best friends. They played together every day. One day, Tim and Max went to the store. Tim's mom said they needed to buy some food. Tim and Max were happy to help. They looked for food to supply their home. They found apples, bread, and milk. Max was a good helper. But then, something unexpected happened. Max saw a big cake with the number zero on it. Max loved cake! He thought it was for him. Max jumped up and tried to eat the cake. But the cake was not for Max. The cake was for Tim's grandma. Tim and Max got in big trouble. Tim's mom was very sad. They had to buy a new cake. Max learned not to eat things that were not his.
