Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Woods

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a walk in the woods. She saw a witch who looked worried. The witch was trying to find her lost cat. Sue wanted to help the witch find her cat. Sue and the witch started to look for the cat. They looked under a big tree and behind a bush. Then, they heard a "meow" sound. "Did you hear that?" asked Sue. "Yes, let's run and find my cat," said the worried witch. They ran to the sound and found the cat. But, it was not the witch's cat! It was a tiny kitten. The kitten was lost too. The witch was so happy to find a new friend. She was not worried anymore. Sue, the witch, and the kitten became best friends and played together in the woods.

Once, there was a boy named Tim. He loved sauce. One day, he saw a big jar of sauce in the kitchen. He wanted the sauce, but it was too high for him to reach. So, he asked his big sister, Sue, to help him get the sauce. Sue said no, but Tim was persistent. Tim asked Sue again and again. Sue got mad and said, "No!" very loud. Tim was sad but still wanted the sauce. He had an idea. He found a chair and tried to get the sauce by himself. He climbed up the chair and reached for the sauce. But, Tim was not careful. The chair fell down and so did Tim. The jar of sauce broke on the floor. Tim cried, and Sue came to see what happened. She was not happy. They both had to clean up the mess. Tim did not get any sauce, and Sue was still mad at him.

Jane was glad to be in the park. She loves to count things. She saw a table and sat at it. She started counting the flowers around it. Her friend Anna saw her and came to join her. "What are you counting, Jane?" asked Anna. "The flowers around the table," Jane said happily, smiling at Anna. "Can I help you count?" Anna said. "Yes, that sounds great!" Jane said, and the two started counting together. They counted and laughed and finally they finished. "We counted all the flowers," Anna said. "Yes, and it was fun!" Jane said, feeling glad.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat named Tom. Tom was very hungry, so he wanted to find a meal. He looked around for something to eat. He saw a big, red apple on a tree. Tom thought the apple would be delicious. Tom had to hurry to get the apple before another animal took it. He ran fast and jumped high to reach the tree. When he got to the tree, he climbed up and grabbed the apple with his paw. Tom climbed down and took a big bite of the apple. It was very delicious! He ate the whole apple and felt happy. Tom had a full belly and went to take a nap under the tree.

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a little bird. The bird had many friends. They all lived happily in the tree. One day, a big, foolish cat came to the tree. The cat saw the bird and wanted to eat it. The cat said, "Bird, come here!" But the bird was smart and did not want to go near the cat. The cat got mad and demanded, "Bird, come down now!" The bird was scared but did not listen. The bird's friends came to help. They all chirped loudly and made a lot of noise. The noise scared the foolish cat away. The bird and its friends were happy again. They knew they were safe when they stayed together. And they lived happily ever after in their big tree.

It was time for naptime. Little Jack lay down on his bed, but he felt something rough underneath him. He reached under his pillow and found a small cardboard box. In the box was a tiny toy car. Jack was so excited and he just had to play with it. He lay on the bed and zoomed the car around. Suddenly his mom came in, but Jack had to lie so he wouldn't get in trouble. He quickly pretended to be fast asleep. But Mom saw the toy car and knew Jack had been lying. She said, "Naptime is for sleeping, not for playing with toys." Jack knew he was in trouble, but he was happy his mom had found the surprise box.
