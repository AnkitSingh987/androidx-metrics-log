Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Lily and the Duck

Ben and Lily were twins who liked to play together. They had many toys, but their favorite was a big rubber duck that could squirt water. They always took the duck to the bathtub when it was time to wash. One day, their mom said, "Ben and Lily, it's time for your bath. Come on, let's go to the bathroom." But Ben and Lily did not want to stop playing. They said, "Mom, can we play a little more? Please, please, please!" Mom said, "No, you have to take your bath now. It's getting late and you have to go to bed soon. Don't delay, or you will miss your bedtime story." She took their hands and led them to the bathroom. Ben and Lily were not happy. They did not like to wash. They liked to play. They thought of a plan to make mom go away. They said, "Mom, we are hungry. Can we have a snack before the bath?" Mom said, "No, you already had dinner. You can have a snack tomorrow. Now get in the bathtub and wash with the duck." She filled the bathtub with warm water and soap bubbles. She put the duck in the water and said, "Have fun, but don't splash too much." Ben and Lily got in the bathtub, but they did not wash. They played with the duck and splashed water everywhere. They laughed and giggled and made a big mess. They did not see that mom had left the bathroom and closed the door. Mom went to the kitchen to get a glass of water. She saw a bottle of hot sauce on the counter. She had a bad idea. She said, "Ben and Lily are being naughty. They don't want to wash. They want to play. I will teach them a lesson. I will make their bath spicy." She took the bottle of hot sauce and went back to the bathroom. She opened the door and said, "Ben and Lily, are you done with your bath?" But Ben and Lily did not hear her. They were too busy playing. Mom poured the hot sauce in the bathtub and said, "There, now your bath is spicy. Enjoy!" Ben and Lily felt a burning sensation on their skin. They screamed and cried. They said, "Mom, what did you do? It hurts, it hurts! Help us, help us!" But mom did not help them. She said, "That's what you get for delaying your bath. Now you have to stay in the bathtub until you wash off the hot sauce. And no bedtime story for you." Ben and Lily were very sad and very angry. They hated mom and they hated the duck. They wished they had listened to mom and taken their bath earlier. They learned a hard lesson. They never delayed their bath again.

Once upon a time, there was a wild monkey. He lived in a big tree in the forest. The monkey loved to hang from the tree branches and play all day long. One sunny day, the monkey found a new fruit on the tree. He had never seen it before. The fruit was red and shiny. The monkey was very curious about how it would taste. The monkey picked the fruit and took a big bite. It tasted so sweet and yummy! The monkey was very happy. From that day on, the wild monkey would always hang from the tree and eat the tasty fruit.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird. The little bird loved to fly high in the sky. One day, the little bird saw some toast on the ground. The toast looked very yummy. The little bird wanted to eat the toast. The little bird flew down to the ground. He looked at the toast and said, "Yummy toast! I want to eat you." The toast did not talk back because it was just toast. The little bird began to eat the toast. It was very happy. But then, a big cat came. The cat saw the little bird eating the toast. The cat said, "Little bird, I want to eat you." The little bird tried to fly away, but it was too late. The big cat ate the little bird. The end.
