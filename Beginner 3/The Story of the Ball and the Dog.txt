Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Ball and the Dog

One day, a big dog and a little cat were playing in the park. They liked to play together. The big dog was deaf, so he could not hear the little cat when she talked. The little cat would shake her tail to get his attention. They were happy friends. One minute, they were playing with a ball. The big dog would shake the ball in his mouth, and the little cat would laugh. But then, the ball went too far away. They could not find the ball. The little cat said, "Where is the ball?" The big dog did not hear her. They both looked sad. The little cat had an idea. She shook her tail to get the big dog's attention. She pointed to a big tree. The big dog looked up and saw the ball stuck in the tree. They worked together to get the ball down. The little cat climbed the tree, and the big dog caught the ball. They were happy friends again.

Once there was a boy named Jeff. He was only three years old and had a lot to learn. Every day he would explore and discover new things. One day, Jeff went to the beach. He noticed something graceful in the water. He was excited to find that it was a whale. He wanted to learn more about whales and was determined to make friends with it. However, Jeff soon realized that the whale was scared of him. He tried to get closer, but the whale splashed around and ran away. Jeff was so sad that he couldn't learn more about whales. He was determined not to give up. That night, Jeff couldn't sleep. He went to the beach in the morning and tried to get closer to the whale. This time, the whale stayed still and watched him. Jeff learned that whales don't like to be alone and that they like to be around other whales. He then knew that if he brought his whale friends, the whale would be happy and he could learn even more about them. So Jeff told all of his friends about the whales and they all went back to the beach with him. The whales were so happy to see them and Jeff was able to learn more about them. From that day on, Jeff and the whales were friends and explored the beach together. Jeff was so excited to have learned so much about whales!

Tom and Lily were at the park with their mom. They saw a big statue of a lion with water coming out of its mouth. It was a fountain. Tom and Lily liked the fountain. They wanted to play with the water. "Can we go near the fountain, mom?" Tom asked. "Sure, but be careful. Don't get too wet," mom said. Tom and Lily ran to the fountain. They saw the water spray from the lion's mouth. They felt the water splash on their faces. They laughed and had fun. "Look, Lily, I can spray water too!" Tom said. He put his mouth near the fountain and tried to spray water like the lion. But he only got water in his nose. He coughed and sneezed. "Are you okay, Tom?" Lily asked. "I'm okay, Lily. But that was not a good idea," Tom said. Mom came to check on them. She saw Tom's wet face and nose. She smiled and gave him a tissue. "You two are silly. The lion is a statue. It can spray water, but you can't. You are ordinary, but special," mom said. Tom and Lily hugged mom. They learned something new. They were ordinary, but special. They liked the fountain and the statue. They liked the park and mom. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog named Spot. Spot liked to play in the park with his friends. One day, he saw a new dog at the park. Spot wanted to introduce himself to the new dog. As Spot walked to the new dog, he saw a big trap on the ground. He wanted to help his new friend, so he tried to move the trap. But, the trap was too heavy, and it didn't move. Suddenly, the trap opened and caught Spot inside! The new dog was not a real dog, but a robot dog that set the trap. Spot was stuck in the trap, and his friends couldn't help him. The robot dog laughed and went away. Poor Spot was sad and trapped.
