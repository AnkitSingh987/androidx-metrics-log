Category: Beginner
Title: Elle the Elephant and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was an elephant named Elle. Elle was a very original elephant. She had a big, long nose and two big ears. She lived in a big, green forest with her family. One day, Elle saw a big tree with yummy fruit on it. She dared to reach the fruit with her long nose. She stretched her nose up, up, up, and got the fruit! Elle was very happy and proud of herself. Elle shared the yummy fruit with her family. They were very happy too. Elle learned that it was good to dare and try new things. From that day on, Elle and her family had many fun adventures in the big, green forest.

Once upon a time, there was a big orange cat named Max. Max loved to play and jump all day. Max had a friend, a small dog named Buddy. Buddy and Max played together every day. One day, Max and Buddy found a big box. Max said, "Let's open it!" They tried and tried, but they struggled to open the box. They pushed and pulled, but it was too hard. Max said, "We need help!" A big bird named Sam flew down and saw Max and Buddy. Sam said, "I can help you open the box!" With a flap of his wings, the box opened. Inside the box, there was a big soft pillow. Max, Buddy, and Sam were surprised! Max said, "This is perfect for a massage!" They all took turns giving each other massages on the soft pillow. They were so happy they found the big box and were now able to relax together.

Billy was out in the sunny park with his dad. He wanted to go sailing. "Dad, can we go sailing?" said Billy. "Well, we'd need a boat and a sail," said his dad. Then Billy saw something pale in the grass. It was a glove. "Look dad!" said Billy. "It's a glove!" "Let's use this as our sail," said his Dad. They tied the glove to a stick and put it in the water. It floated. "Let's go sail!" said his dad. So together, they both sailed on the pale glove across the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play outside. One day, Tim went to the park to play. He saw a big slide and wanted to go down it. Tim's mom gave him a sandwich with butter for a snack. He ate the sandwich, but some butter got on his hands. Tim did not know that his hands were slippery from the butter. He climbed up to the top of the slide. Tim was ready to go down the slide, but when he tried to hold on, his hands slipped! He slid down very fast and fell off the slide. Tim cried and his mom came to help him. They went home, and Tim did not play on the slide again.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends, Sam and John. Sam was big and John was small. One day, they decided to plan something fun. Sam suggested they should make lots of sugar. John asked, "What do you mean?" Sam said, "We can make it in my kitchen. My mom has lots of sugar! We can make cakes and cookies. It will be so fun!" John was excited. "That sounds great! Let's do it!" So the two friends went to the kitchen and started to plan. They mixed the sugar, shaped it into big cookies, and put it in the oven. After a few minutes, the big, sweet cookies were ready to eat! Sam and John had a great time making them, and they enjoyed them too.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a brown dog named Max. Max had a very special red ball he loved to play with. One day, Max was playing with his red ball when it rolled into a big tree. A little girl named Sue saw Max looking sad. "Why are you sad, Max?" she asked. Max barked and pointed to his red ball in the tree. Sue wanted to help Max get his ball back. She looked around and found a long stick. She used the stick to rub the ball out of the tree. But just as the ball fell, a big man came and took it away. "This is my ball now," he said. Max barked and Sue said, "No, it's Max's ball! Please give it back!" The man looked at Max's red ball and saw the word "license" on it. He realized it was not his ball after all. The man gave the ball back to Max and said, "I'm sorry, here is your ball." Max and Sue were very happy. They played with the red ball all day and became the best of friends. And from that day on, Max always kept his special red ball close to him, so it would never get lost again.
