Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was going to her friend's birthday party. Lily wanted to give her friend a present, so she went to her room to find something nice. Lily found a pretty toy in her room. She thought her friend would love it! She took a box and started to pack the present. She wrapped it with colorful paper and tied a bow on top. On the way to the party, Lily saw a big dog. She felt frightened because the dog was barking loudly. But then, the dog's owner came and took the dog away. Lily felt happy again. She went to the party and gave her friend the present. They all had a fun time!

Once upon a time, there was a little cat who lived in a small chamber. The cat liked to play and have fun. One day, the cat saw a big, dangerous dog outside. The dog was very big and scary. The cat was afraid of the dog, but it wanted to play outside. The cat thought it could be brave and go outside to play. It went outside and saw the big, dangerous dog. The dog saw the cat and started to chase it. The cat ran and ran, trying to get away from the dog. The dog was very fast and the cat was scared. In the end, the cat had to surrender to the big, dangerous dog. The dog caught the little cat and it was very sad. The cat should have stayed in its safe chamber. The cat learned that sometimes it is better to stay safe and not go near dangerous things.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to climb trees. One sunny day, she saw a big tree in her yard. It was the perfect tree to climb. Sue was enthusiastic and wanted to reach the top of the tree. Sue started to climb the tree. She used her hands and toes to hold on tight. As she climbed higher, she saw her friend Tom. Tom looked up and said, "Hi Sue! Can I climb with you?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, come on up!" Together, Sue and Tom climbed the big tree. They were happy and laughed as they climbed higher. At the top, they sat on a strong branch. They looked around and saw their whole town. Sue and Tom had a fun day climbing the tree with their hands and toes.

Sara and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big white bird on a tree. The bird has a red beak and a long tail. "Look, Sara, a white bird!" Tom says. "Wow, it is so pretty!" Sara says. They want to say hello to the bird. They look for something to give to the bird. Sara finds a bowl on the grass. The bowl is blue and has some seeds in it. "Maybe the bird likes seeds," Sara says. She picks up the bowl and walks to the tree. "Be careful, Sara," Tom says. "The bird might fly away." Sara nods. She holds the bowl up to the bird. The bird looks at Sara and the bowl. It makes a loud sound. "Tweet, tweet!" Sara smiles. She thinks the bird is happy. She tries to send the bowl to the bird. She throws the bowl gently to the branch where the bird is. But the bowl is too heavy. It falls down and breaks on the ground. The seeds spill everywhere. "Oh no!" Sara says. She feels sad. The bird flies away. It makes another sound. "Bye, bye!" Tom runs to Sara. He hugs her. "It's okay, Sara," he says. "The bird was not hungry. It just wanted to say hi." Sara hugs Tom back. "Thank you, Tom," she says. "You are a good friend." They look at the sky. They see the white bird flying high. They wave to the bird. "Bye, bye, white bird!" they say. "Come back soon!"

Once upon a time, there was an enormous vase. It was so big that all the kids in town could play inside it. One day, a little boy named Tim found the vase while playing outside. He was very excited and wanted to show all his friends. Tim invited all his friends to come and see the enormous vase. They all came and played inside it. They had so much fun! They played hide and seek, and they pretended the vase was a castle. Everyone was having a great time. But then, the vase started to wobble. Tim and his friends got scared. They didn't want the vase to break. So, they all held hands and stood around the vase. They said, "Please, don't fall!" The enormous vase heard them and stopped wobbling. It accepted their help, and they all played together happily ever after.
