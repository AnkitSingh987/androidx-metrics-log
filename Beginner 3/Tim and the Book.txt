Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Book

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to read. One day, he found a new book called a "novel" in his room. The novel was big and had many pages. Tim was very happy. Tim started to read the novel. But, his hands were dirty from playing outside. The pages of the novel became dirty too. Tim's mom saw the dirty pages and said, "Tim, you must release the novel and wash your hands first." Tim listened to his mom and released the novel. He went to wash his hands. When his hands were clean, he picked up the novel again. Tim read the novel and enjoyed the story. He was careful to keep the pages clean. And they all lived happily ever after.

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to play with toy cars and trucks. One day, they have a big fight. Tom says his car is faster than Sam's truck. Sam says his truck is stronger than Tom's car. They yell and push each other. Tom and Sam are mad. They do not want to be friends anymore. They say they are at war. They take their toys and go to different sides of the yard. They throw rocks and sticks at each other. They try to strike each other's toys and make them break. Mom sees Tom and Sam fighting. She is sad. She does not like war. She says war is bad. She says friends should be good to each other. She tells Tom and Sam to stop fighting and say sorry. She tells them to share their toys and play nicely. Tom and Sam listen to Mom. They feel sorry for fighting. They say sorry to each other. They hug and make up. They share their toys and play together. They are friends again. They say war is bad and peace is good. Mom is happy. She smiles and hugs them.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big smile. Tim was going to a meeting with his mom. He was not happy. Tim thought meetings were terrible. But his mom said there was a surprise for him. At the meeting, Tim saw a big box in the corner. He wondered what was inside. His mom smiled and said, "Wait until the end, Tim." He sat and waited, feeling a little excited now. When the meeting was over, Tim's mom opened the box. Inside was a big, bouncy ball! Tim was so happy. He did not think the meeting was terrible anymore. He smiled and played with the ball all day.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a drive with his mom. They drove to the store. Tim saw a big box of crayons. He wanted to color with them. His mom said yes, and they took the crayons home. At home, Tim started to color with his crayons. He saw a tasty cookie on the table. He wanted to eat it. But when he picked it up, it was not a cookie! It was a crayon that looked like a cookie. Tim was sad, but then he had an idea. He used the cookie crayon to color a picture of a big, tasty cookie. His mom saw the picture and smiled. She went to the kitchen and made real tasty cookies for Tim. They ate the cookies together and had a fun day.

One day, Daddy and his little girl went for a walk. They saw a big tree with smooth bark. Daddy touched the tree and said, "This tree is so smooth!" They kept walking and found some yummy fruit. The little girl asked, "Daddy, can we eat this fruit?" Daddy said, "Yes, this fruit is good to eat. The tree will yield us some tasty snack." So they picked the fruit and sat under the smooth tree. They ate the fruit and talked. Daddy and his little girl had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a popular cook named Tom. He loved to cook for all his friends. They would all sit in a row and eat the yummy food he made. One day, Tom's friend Sue came over. "Can you cook for us today?" she asked. Tom said, "Yes, I will cook for everyone!" So, Tom and Sue went to the store to buy food. They cooked a big meal together. All their friends came and sat in a row. They ate and laughed. Everyone loved the food Tom and Sue made. They were the best cooks in town!
