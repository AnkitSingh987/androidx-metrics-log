Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Useful Mule and the Hat

Once upon a time, there was a useful mule named Max. Max helped a boy named Tom carry things. They were friends and did many things together. One day, Tom and Max went to the market. They were very happy. At the market, Tom had to leave Max alone for a little while. Max waited and waited. Suddenly, a big wind came and blew Max's hat away. Max was sad and scared. He did not know what to do. Then, a kind dog saw Max and brought back his hat. Max was so happy and surprised! The dog said, "I can help you with your hat." Max and the dog became friends. When Tom came back, he was happy to see Max with his new friend. They all went home together, and they had fun every day.

Once upon a time, in a great big world, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his friends. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. One day, Tim and his friends found a big hill. They wanted to race to the top. So, they started to run up the hill. But, the grass was wet, and Tim's foot began to slip. He fell down and started to roll. His friends saw him and started to laugh. Tim felt sad, but he got up and tried again. This time, he was careful not to slip. He made it to the top of the hill and felt so happy. His friends cheered for him. They all played on the hill together, and it was a great day in their big world.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a nurse named Mary. She liked to balance things on her head. One day, she felt very miserable because she was sick. Mary wanted to help people, but she could not go to work. Mary stayed in her little house. She tried to balance things on her head, but she was too sad. Her friend, Tom the cat, came to visit her. Tom wanted to make her feel better. He tried to balance a ball on his head too. But Tom the cat was not very good at balancing. The ball fell off his head and broke a window. Mary became more miserable because of the broken window. Tom the cat felt bad and left. Mary stayed sad and sick in her house, and she could not balance things on her head anymore.

One day, a little fly was sad. It did not like being a fly. It would complain to its friend, the bee. "I don't like being a fly," said the fly. "I want to be a bee like you." The bee was glad to be a bee. It liked making honey and flying from flower to flower. The bee wanted to help its friend. "Let's go see the wise old owl," said the bee. "Maybe he can help you feel better." They went to see the owl. The wise old owl listened to the fly's complaints. The owl thought for a moment. Then, he said, "I will give you a wish. You can be a bee for one day." The fly was very happy. It turned into a bee and flew around with its friend. They made honey and visited flowers. But then, something unexpected happened. A big bird tried to catch the fly and the bee. They had to fly very fast to get away. At the end of the day, the fly turned back into a fly. It was glad to be a fly again. "Being a bee was fun," said the fly. "But I am happy to be a fly. I can fly fast and be safe." The bee agreed, and they both flew off, happy to be themselves.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved her mittens. She wore them all the time. One day, she went out to play. She saw her friend Tom. Tom was sad. He lost his mitten. Sue wanted to help Tom. She thought, "If I delay going home, I can find Tom's mitten." So, Sue and Tom looked for the mitten. They looked under a big tree. They looked by the swings. But they could not find the mitten. Then, something unexpected happened. A big dog came and took Sue's mitten! Sue was sorry she did not go home. Now, both Sue and Tom had no mittens. They were sad. The moral of the story is: sometimes, it is better to take care of your own things first.
