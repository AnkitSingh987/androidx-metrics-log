Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Tree

One day, a little girl named Mia and her dog, Ruff, went for a walk. It was a wet day, and they both wore coats. They saw a big twisty tree, and Mia said, "Ruff, let's play near the twisty tree!" Ruff barked happily, and they ran to the tree. As they played, a boy named Tom came by with a cup of cocoa. He saw Mia and Ruff and wanted to play too. But he was not careful and spilled his cocoa on Ruff's coat. Ruff was sad and cold. Mia said, "Tom, you made Ruff's coat wet with cocoa! Now he is cold." Tom felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, Mia. I didn't mean to make Ruff wet. I will help make it right." Tom took off his coat and put it on Ruff to keep him warm. Mia smiled and said, "Thank you, Tom. Now Ruff is warm again." They all played together under the twisty tree, and Ruff was happy. From that day on, Mia, Ruff, and Tom were good friends, and they always played carefully near the twisty tree.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to push his toy car fast all around the house. One day, while Tim was playing, he heard a tiny voice say, "Please, don't push me so fast!" Tim looked around and saw that the voice was coming from his toy car. He was very surprised. "I didn't know you could talk!" Tim said. The toy car replied, "Yes, I have a life too, and I get scared when you push me too fast." From that day on, Tim learned to play more gently with his toy car. They became the best of friends and had lots of fun together. And the toy car was very happy that Tim listened and didn't push him too fast anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, filthy toy box. He loved to play with all his toys, but sometimes, he couldn't resist making a mess. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, it's time to clean your toy box. We need to make it nice and neat." Tim didn't want to clean, but he knew he had to listen to his mom. Tim started to clean. He found a big frame in the toy box. He said, "Look, Mom! I can put my picture in this frame." Tim's mom smiled and said, "That's a great idea, Tim. Now, let's finish cleaning up." Tim and his mom cleaned the toy box together, and it was no longer filthy.

Once upon a time, in a mild land, there was a pretty flower. The flower was waiting to bloom. It was very happy and excited. One day, a little girl came to visit the flower. She saw the flower was not yet bloomed. She said, "Hello, flower! When will you bloom?" The flower smiled and said, "Soon, little girl. I will bloom very soon." The next day, the little girl came back to visit the flower. This time, the flower had bloomed! It was big and bright. The little girl was so happy. She said, "Wow, flower! You look so pretty now!" The flower smiled and said, "Thank you, little girl. Your visit made me happy, so I bloomed just for you."

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a good boy, but sometimes he would do things that he should not do. One day, Tim went upstairs to play in his room. He saw a big box on the floor. Tim felt a bit guilty, but he wanted to see what was inside. Tim opened the box and found many toys. He played with the toys for a long time. Tim was very happy. But then he heard his mom coming upstairs. Tim knew he should not have opened the box, so he tried to put the toys back quickly. When his mom came into the room, she saw the open box and the toys on the floor. She asked Tim if he opened the box. Tim felt guilty and said, "Yes, I did." His mom told him that he should always ask before doing things like that. Tim said he was sorry, and they put the toys away together. Tim learned a lesson about asking before doing things.
