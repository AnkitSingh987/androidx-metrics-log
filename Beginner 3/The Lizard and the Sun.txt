Title: The Lizard and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a little lizard. The lizard had a friend named Tim. They liked to play in the sun. The lizard was very good at hiding. One day, the lizard decided to play a game. He wanted to see if he could make himself vanish. The lizard found a big rock to hide behind. He was very quiet, and he did not move. Tim looked for the lizard. Tim looked under a tree, but the lizard was not there. Tim looked by the water, but the lizard was not there either. Tim was sad because he could not find his friend. Then, something unexpected happened. The lizard jumped out from behind the rock and said, "Surprise!" Tim was very happy to see his friend. The lizard was very proud of how well he could vanish. Tim said, "You are a very persistent lizard!" They both laughed and played in the sun all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Bow. Bow loved to play with his ball all day. One day, Bow saw something new in the yard. It was a small, round thing. Bow thought it was a ball, but it was a prune. Bow picked up the prune in his mouth and ran to his best friend, Cat. "Look, Cat," Bow said, "I found a new ball!" Cat looked at the prune and said, "That's not a ball, Bow. That's a prune. But we can still play with it." Bow and Cat played with the prune all day long. They had so much fun. But then, the prune broke into small pieces. Bow and Cat were sad. They did not have a ball or a prune to play with now. The next day, Bow and Cat went for a walk. They found a big, bouncy ball in the park. They were so happy! Bow and Cat played with the new ball all day. They knew that even if they lost the ball, they would always find something new to play with.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to take a nap every day. One day, before his nap, his mom told him an unusual story. The story was about a big, blue cat. The cat could talk and liked to eat ice cream. Tim went to his room and lay down for his nap. He closed his eyes and began to dream. In his dream, he met the big, blue cat from the story. The cat said, "Hi, Tim! Would you like some ice cream?" Tim said, "Yes, please!" They both ate ice cream together and had lots of fun. After a while, Tim woke up from his nap. He told his mom about the dream with the big, blue cat. His mom smiled and said, "That was an unusual dream, Tim. I'm glad you liked the story." Tim hugged his mom and asked her to tell him more unusual stories before his naps.

One day, a friendly dog named Spot was playing in the park. He was running and jumping with his friends. They were all having fun. Spot saw a shiny thing on the ground. He went to look at it. It was a small, white tooth. Spot picked up the tooth and showed it to his friend, a little girl named Sue. "Look, Sue! I found a tooth!" said Spot. Sue looked at the tooth and smiled. "Wow, Spot! That's a nice tooth. Let's find who lost it." They walked around the park, asking all their friends if they lost a tooth. But no one knew who it belonged to. They were about to give up when they saw a sad squirrel sitting by a tree. Sue asked, "Did you lose a tooth, Mr. Squirrel?" The squirrel looked up and said, "Yes, I did! I lost it while eating a nut." Sue gave the tooth to the squirrel, and he was very happy. Spot and Sue felt good for helping their new friend. They played together all day long, and the squirrel never lost his tooth again.
