Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Cat

One day, a boy named Tim found a light film. He wanted to show it to his friend, Sam. Tim called Sam to come over and watch the film. Sam was very excited to see it too. They sat down and started to watch the film together. While they were watching, they saw a big dog and a small cat in the film. The big dog was not nice to the small cat. The dog took the cat's food and ran away. The cat was very sad and hungry. Tim and Sam did not like what the dog did. In the end, the dog saw how sad the cat was and felt bad. The dog called the cat and gave back the food. The cat was happy again. Tim and Sam learned that it is important to be kind to others and share. They promised to always help their friends and be nice to everyone.

One day, a girl named Sue put on her pretty skirt. She liked to play in her yard. Sue saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. She was very alert and looked around for a good spot to start. Sue saw a low branch and thought it was perfect. As Sue climbed the tree, she saw a big bird. The bird had a long beak and bright colors. Sue thought the bird was very pretty. She wanted to get closer to the bird. The bird saw Sue and was alert too. It did not fly away. Sue got close to the bird and saw that it had a nest. In the nest, there were little baby birds. The big bird looked at Sue and opened its beak. It made a loud sound, like it was going to shoot something. Sue got scared and climbed down the tree. She ran back to her house, her skirt flying behind her. Sue learned that it was best to leave the birds alone. She knew they were just trying to protect their babies. From that day on, Sue watched the birds from a safe distance and let them live in peace.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a hidden boot. A tiny mouse named Mimi lived inside it. One day, Mimi heard a loud noise outside her boot. She peeked out and saw a big cat named Tom. Tom was sad and said, "I can't find my toy ball. Can you help me?" Mimi wanted to support her new friend. So, she came out of her boot and said, "I will help you find your toy ball, Tom." Together, they looked under the big tree and behind the small rock. Finally, they found the toy ball near the pond. Tom was happy and thanked Mimi. From that day on, Tom and Mimi were best friends who always supported each other. And they both lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Timmy loved to play with his toy phone. He liked to pretend that he was talking to his friends. One day, his mommy told him that she needed to use the real phone to call grandma. "Timmy, can you please settle down and play with your toy phone? I need to use the real phone," his mommy said. Timmy was sad because he wanted to use the real phone too. But he knew that mommy needed to call grandma, so he agreed. "Okay, mommy. I will play with my toy phone," he said. While Timmy played with his toy phone, he heard his mommy talking to grandma. They talked about how Timmy was a good boy and how much they loved him. Timmy felt happy to hear that. After mommy finished her call, she told Timmy that the real phone was now available for him to use. "Timmy, do you want to talk to grandma on the real phone?" she asked. Timmy's eyes lit up, and he said, "Yes, mommy! I want to talk to grandma!" And so, Timmy got to talk to his grandma on the real phone, and they had a lovely chat.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. It was a hot day, so they packed a big bag with food, toys, and a hat for Tim. They walked to the park and found a nice spot under a big tree. As Tim played with his toys, he met a new friend named Sue. They played and laughed together. "Let's play tag!" said Sue. Tim smiled and said, "OK!" They ran around the tree, but Tim bumped his toe on a rock. "Ouch!" he cried. Tim's mom heard him and came over. She looked at his toe and gave him a hug. "It's OK," she said. "Let's eat some food and rest for a bit." Tim and Sue sat down and enjoyed the food from the packed bag. They had a great day at the park, even with Tim's hurt toe.
