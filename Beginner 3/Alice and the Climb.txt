Category: Beginner
Title: Alice and the Climb

Alice was so excited to explore her new home. She saw a tall hall and wanted to climb it. But she was too small and couldn't reach. "Mummy," she said. "Can you help me climb the hall?" Mummy looked at Alice and smiled. "No," she said, "I'm sorry sweetheart, but it's too high. You can't climb it yet." Alice was sad, so Mummy said, "Don't worry, I have a fun surprise. Just wait and see." Alice sat and waited, curious to find out what her surprise would be. Then, Mummy brought in the cutest puppy Alice had ever seen! Alice gasped in delight at her surprise. With joy she twirled around the puppy and exclaimed "I will call him Climb!" Alice couldn't wait to run with Climb and play games in the hall. She quickly grabbed Climb and started to climb the hall, step-by-step. With excitement and determination she slowly made her way to the top. At last, Alice reached the top and smiled with pride. She had done it! With the help of her new puppy, she had conquered the hall.

Lily was a shy girl who liked to play with her dolls. She had a big dollhouse with many rooms and doors. She liked to lock the doors and pretend that her dolls had secrets. She had a key for each door and kept them in a small box. One day, her mom asked her to help with the laundry. She said, "Lily, can you bring me the iron from the closet? I need to iron your dad's shirt." Lily nodded and went to the closet. She saw the iron on a shelf, but it was too high for her to reach. She looked around and saw a chair. She dragged the chair to the closet and climbed on it. She reached for the iron, but it was heavy and hot. She dropped it and it fell on the floor with a loud clang. Lily was scared and jumped off the chair. She ran to her room and locked the door. She hid under her bed with her dolls. She heard her mom calling her name, but she did not answer. She was afraid that her mom would be angry with her for dropping the iron. Her mom knocked on the door and said, "Lily, are you okay? I heard a noise. Please open the door, honey. I'm not mad at you. I just want to make sure you're not hurt." Lily did not say anything. She hugged her dolls and hoped that her mom would go away. Her mom tried to open the door, but it was locked. She said, "Lily, where is the key? I need to open the door. Please, talk to me, sweetie. I love you." Lily felt sorry for her mom, but she was too shy to speak. She remembered that she had the key in her box. She crawled out from under the bed and opened the box. She found the key and unlocked the door. She opened the door and saw her mom's worried face. She said, "I'm sorry, mom. I dropped the iron. It was hot. I was scared." Her mom hugged her and said, "It's okay, Lily. I'm glad you're okay. The iron is not broken. It was just a little accident. You don't have to be shy. You can always tell me what happened. I'm here to help you." Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, mom. I love you too." They went to the kitchen and had some cookies and milk. Lily felt better and learned to be more careful with the iron.

Once upon a time, there was a huge duck. The duck loved to paint. Every day, the duck would paint pretty pictures with its wings. One day, the duck saw a big tree. The duck wanted to paint the tree. So, the duck got some paint and started to paint the tree. The tree looked very pretty with all the colors. Soon, all the other ducks came to see the huge duck's painting. They loved it! All the ducks started to paint with their wings too. They painted the whole park with pretty colors. And they all lived happily ever after.
