Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Pale Cat and the Tree

One day, a pale cat named Tim went for a walk. He wore a pretty tie around his neck. Tim liked to show off his tie to all his friends. While walking, he saw his friend, a dog named Max. "Hi Max!" said Tim. "Do you like my tie? It's new!" Max looked at the tie and said, "Yes, it's nice! But be careful, it might vanish if you don't watch it." Tim laughed and said, "A tie can't vanish, silly Max!" Later, Tim played near a tree. He did not see the tie fall off his neck. When he looked down, the tie was gone. It had vanished! Tim felt sad and went to find Max. "Max, you were right," said Tim. "My tie vanished. Can you help me find it?" Max smiled and said, "Of course, Tim! Let's look for it together." Tim and Max looked everywhere. Finally, they found the tie under the tree. Tim was happy, and he thanked Max for helping him. From that day on, Tim always made sure his tie was tied tight so it would not vanish again.

One day, a boy named Tim saw a brilliant automobile. It was big and red. He liked it a lot. He wanted to play with it. The automobile had four wheels and went very fast. Tim's friend, Sue, came to play with him. They both liked the brilliant automobile. They ran and shouted, "We love the automobile!" They were very happy. Tim and Sue played with the automobile all day. They made it go fast and slow. They had so much fun. When it was time to go home, they said bye to the automobile. They couldn't wait to play with it again.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to play outside. She saw a pretty bush with many colors. Mia thought it was very pretty. She wanted to make it even more pretty. So, Mia ran back into her house to find her paint. Mia found her paint and a big brush. She went back outside to the pretty bush. She started to paint the bush with her favorite colors: pink, blue, and yellow. The bush looked even more pretty now. Mia was so happy. Just then, her friend Tim came over. He saw the pretty painted bush and said, "Wow, Mia! That bush looks so pretty! Can I help you paint it?" Mia smiled and said, "Yes, Tim! Let's paint it together!" They both painted the pretty bush and had a fun day.

One day, in a big nation, a boy named Tom wanted to play. He went outside to find his original toy, a red ball. Tom loved to play with his red ball, and he took it everywhere he went. While playing, Tom saw a big dog. The dog was playing with a blue ball. Tom wanted to play with the dog, so he threw his red ball. The dog ran to get it, but something unexpected happened. The dog came back with a green ball instead of the red one! Tom was very surprised. He threw the green ball, and the dog ran to get it again. This time, the dog came back with the blue ball. Tom laughed and realized that the dog was playing a fun game with him. They played together all day long, and Tom and the dog became the best of friends.

Tom and Lily liked to play with blocks. They had many blocks of different colors and shapes. They could make houses, towers, bridges and more with their blocks. One day, they decided to make a temple with their blocks. A temple was a big and beautiful place where people went to pray and thank God. Tom and Lily had seen a picture of a temple in a book. They wanted to make their temple look like the one in the book. They started to fill the floor with blocks. They made a square shape with blocks. Then they made walls with blocks. They made the walls high and strong. They left a space for a door. They made a roof with blocks. They made the roof pointy and shiny. They added some blocks on top of the roof to make a cross. They looked at their temple and smiled. It was almost done. They only needed one more thing. They needed a bell. A bell was a round and metal thing that made a loud and nice sound. The temple in the book had a bell. Tom and Lily wanted their temple to have a bell too. They looked around for something to make a bell. They saw a tin can on the table. It was empty and shiny. It was perfect. They took the tin can and tied a string to it. They made a hole in the roof of their temple and put the string through it. They hung the tin can inside their temple. They pulled the string and made the tin can ring. They heard the sound of the bell and clapped. They had made a temple with their blocks. It was not exactly like the one in the book, but it was their original temple. They were very proud and happy. They went inside their temple and prayed and thanked God. They played with their temple until it was time for dinner.
