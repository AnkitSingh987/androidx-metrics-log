Category: Beginner
Title: Jake and the Car

Once upon a time there were three friends. One was called Jake, the other was Joe and the last one was Lucy. They all loved the same toy. It was a bright blue car and it was lots of fun to play with! The friends had an idea. They wanted to plan a race using their toy blue car. Jake was the boss so he decided what the rules would be. He was a little bit bossy but Joe and Lucy didn't mind. The race was on and it was lots of fun! The car zipped around the yard and Joe was in the lead. But then Lucy went even faster and won! Joe was so happy for her. At the end of the race, the happy friends hugged and laughed. They had such a fun day playing with their toy blue car.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play dress-up and make her own fashion. One day, she found a pretty necklace in her mom's room. She wanted to wear it and show her friends. Lily put the necklace on and went outside to play. She was so happy and proud of her new fashion. But while she was playing, she felt very hungry. She ran to the kitchen to find some food. She ate a big apple and felt better. As Lily was eating, the necklace got caught on a chair. When she stood up, it pulled and the necklace broke. Lily was so sad and scared that her mom would be mad. She told her mom what happened. Her mom hugged her and said, "It's okay, we can fix it together." They fixed the necklace, and Lily learned to be more careful with things that can break.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird with a pretty wing. The bird lived in a big tree. One day, the bird saw a yummy worm on the ground. The bird wanted to eat the worm, but it was scared. The bird started to tremble because it was scared of the big cat that lived near the tree. The bird was brave and flew down to get the yummy worm. The bird moved very fast with its wing. The big cat saw the bird and wanted to catch it. The bird was still trembling, but it did not stop. The bird got to the worm before the cat could catch it. The bird ate the yummy worm and flew back to the tree. The bird was happy and did not tremble anymore. The bird knew that it could be brave with its pretty wing.

Once upon a time, there was a little squirrel named Sam. Sam loved to play with his smooth acorn all day. One day, while playing with his acorn, Sam saw a pretty dress hanging on a tree. Sam called his friend, Lily the bird, and said, "Look at the pretty dress! Can you help me wear it?" Lily replied, "Yes, Sam! Let's dress you up!" So, Lily helped Sam put on the pretty dress. Sam felt very happy. Sam and Lily played together, running and laughing in the forest. Sam's acorn rolled away, but they found it under a big tree. Sam and Lily had a great day, and Sam loved wearing his pretty dress.

Sara and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to pretend they were a queen and a knight. Sara wore a crown made of flowers and Ben had a stick for a sword. "Queen Sara, I will protect you from the dragon!" Ben said, waving his stick. "Thank you, loyal knight Ben!" Sara said, smiling. "You are very brave and strong!" They heard a loud roar from behind the bushes. It was their dog, Rex, who wanted to join the game. He ran towards them, barking and wagging his tail. "Look out, it's the dragon!" Ben shouted. He ran to meet Rex and pretended to fight him. "Help, help, save me!" Sara cried. She ran to the other side of the garden and hid behind a tree. Ben and Rex chased after her, playing and laughing. They reached the tree and saw Sara peeking out. "Ha, we got you, queen!" Ben said. He and Rex gave Sara a big hug. "OK, you win, knight and dragon!" Sara said. She hugged them back. "But you have to share your part of the cake with me!" "Deal, queen!" Ben said. "You are very kind and fair!" They all went inside to have some cake and juice. They were happy and had fun. They were the best of friends.
