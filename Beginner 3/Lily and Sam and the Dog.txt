Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Sam and the Dog

Lily and Sam liked to go to the park with their mom. They liked to play on the swings, slide down the slide, and run on the grass. Sometimes, they also liked to buy ice cream from the vendor. The vendor was a nice man who had a big cart with many flavors of ice cream. One day, they went to the park and saw the vendor. They ran to him and asked for ice cream. But there was a problem. There was a big dog next to the cart. The dog was barking and growling. It did not let anyone get close to the cart. It wanted to eat all the ice cream. The vendor was scared of the dog. He tried to shoo it away, but it did not listen. He tried to call for help, but no one heard him. He did not know what to do. The dog was preventing him from selling ice cream. Lily and Sam were sad and impatient. They really wanted ice cream. They did not like the dog. They wished it would go away. They looked around and saw a stick. They had an idea. They picked up the stick and threw it far away. The dog saw the stick and ran after it. It forgot about the ice cream. The vendor was happy and relieved. He thanked Lily and Sam for their help. He gave them each a big scoop of ice cream. They chose their favorite flavors. They licked their ice cream and smiled. They were glad they could prevent the dog from ruining their fun. They enjoyed their ice cream and their day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a soft cat named Fluffy. Fluffy had a mop. He loved his mop so much. He would play with it every day. Fluffy would say, "I value my mop." One day, Fluffy saw a dog named Spot. Spot was big and liked to bark. Fluffy wanted to be friends with Spot. So, Fluffy went to Spot and said, "Do you want to play with my mop? I value my mop." Spot looked at Fluffy and said, "Yes, I want to play with your mop." They played with the mop together. They had so much fun. But then, Spot got too excited. He bit the mop and pulled it hard. The mop broke into two pieces. Fluffy was very sad. He said, "My mop is broken. I can't play with it anymore." From that day on, Fluffy and Spot were not friends. Fluffy missed his mop and never found a new one.

Lila and Tom are playing with their toy cars. They like to make them go fast and make noises. Lila has a red car and Tom has a blue car. "Look, Lila, I can turn my car like this!" Tom says. He moves his car in a circle on the floor. "That's cool, Tom, but I can turn my car like this!" Lila says. She lifts her car in the air and spins it around. They both laugh and keep playing. They pretend that their cars are flying in the sky. They see clouds and birds and planes. "Wow, look at that big land!" Tom says. He points to a picture on the wall. It shows a green field with flowers and trees. "Can we go there, Tom?" Lila asks. She likes the colors and the shapes. "Maybe, Lila, maybe. But we have to ask Mommy first." Tom says. He knows that they can't go anywhere without Mommy. "Okay, Tom, let's ask Mommy. Maybe she can take us to the big land." Lila says. She puts down her car and holds Tom's hand. They run to find Mommy and tell her about their game and their wish. They hope that Mommy will say yes. They want to see the big land for real.

Once upon a time, there was a vendor who sold toys. He had many original toys that all the kids loved. Every day, children would come to his shop to see what new toys he had. One day, the vendor wanted to surprise the children. He made a big, colorful toy that could sing and dance. The children saw the toy and laughed with joy. They had never seen a toy like this before. The children played with the new toy all day long. They thanked the vendor for the wonderful surprise. The vendor was happy to see the children's smiling faces. From that day on, the children always looked forward to the vendor's original and surprising toys.
