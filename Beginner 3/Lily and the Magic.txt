Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Magic

Lily liked to watch her favorite show on the screen. She liked the songs and the colors and the funny animals. She always watched it with her big brother Sam, who was nice to her and helped her with the remote. One day, Lily and Sam were watching the show when the screen went black. Lily was scared and sad. She asked Sam, "What happened? Where did the show go?" Sam looked at the remote and saw that the batteries were gone. He said, "Oh no, the batteries vanished. They ran out of power. We need new ones to make the screen work again." Lily did not understand what batteries were. She thought they were magic. She said, "Can we find them? Can we make them come back?" Sam shook his head. He said, "No, we can't. They are gone for good. We have to ask Mom or Dad for new ones. Maybe they have some in the kitchen." Lily and Sam went to the kitchen, where Mom was making lemonade. They asked her for batteries, but she said she did not have any. She said, "I'm sorry, kids. We ran out of batteries. We have to go to the store and buy some later. Do you want some lemonade instead?" Lily and Sam nodded. They were thirsty and hoped the lemonade would make them feel better. Mom poured them each a glass and gave them a straw. Lily took a sip and made a face. The lemonade was sour. She did not like it. She said, "Yuck! This is bad. This is not like the show. The show is sweet and fun." Sam took a sip and smiled. He liked the sour taste. He said, "I like it. It is good. It is like the show. The show is sour and funny." Lily and Sam looked at each other. They did not agree. They liked different things. But they still loved each other. They hugged and waited for Mom or Dad to bring them new batteries. They hoped the show would come back soon. They missed it.

Once upon a time, a cat named Tom lived in a small house. Tom loved to stretch on the soft grass every day. He liked to watch the birds and play with his friends. One day, Tom saw a new cat in the yard. Her name was Sally. Tom wanted to show Sally how he could stretch. He said, "Look at me, Sally! I can stretch very far!" But when Tom tried to stretch, he fell down. He felt embarrassed. Sally laughed and said, "It's okay, Tom! We all fall down sometimes." Just then, the grass started to move. It was not the wind. It was a big dog named Max! Max wanted to play with Tom and Sally. They all played together and had a lot of fun. Tom was not embarrassed anymore. He was happy to have new friends.

One day, a boy named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big jar under a tree. He thought, "What is in the jar?" Tom wanted to look inside. Tom opened the jar and there was a terrible smell. He saw something green and sticky. "Yuck!" said Tom. He did not like the terrible smell. He closed the jar very fast. Tom went home and told his mom about the jar. Mom said, "Do not touch the jar again!" Tom said, "Okay, Mom." Tom was happy to be away from the terrible jar.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog and a little cat. They were not friends. But one day, they decided to unite and be friends. They wanted to play together and have fun. They were grateful to have each other. One day, they found a bag of powder. They didn't know what it was. The big dog and the little cat decided to play with the powder. They threw it in the air and watched it fall like snow. They laughed and played with the powder all day. But then, the big dog and the little cat started to feel sick. They didn't know that the powder was bad for them. They tried to go home, but they were too sick. The big dog and the little cat were very sad. They wished they had not played with the powder.
