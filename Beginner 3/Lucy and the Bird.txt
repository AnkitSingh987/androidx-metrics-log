Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy and the Bird

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lucy had a small bag of oats. She loved oats because they were delicious. She wanted to share the oats with her friends. One day, Lucy went to the park to play with her friends. She was so excited to show them the oats. But, Lucy forget the bag of oats at home. She was sad and told her friends about it. While they were playing, a big bird flew by and dropped a big bag of oats in front of them. Lucy and her friends were so surprised and happy. They shared the delicious oats and had a fun day at the park.

One day, a little dog named Max went for a run. He saw a big shelf in a house. On the shelf, there were many good toys. Max wanted to play with the toys, so he went inside the house. Max met a little girl named Lily. She saw Max looking at the toys. Lily said, "Hi Max, do you want to play with my toys?" Max barked and wagged his tail. Lily took a toy from the shelf and gave it to Max. Max and Lily played with the good toys all day. They had so much fun. When it was time for Max to go home, he barked and wagged his tail to say thank you. Lily said, "You're welcome, Max. Come back soon to play!" Max ran home, happy after a fun day with his new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big car. It was very dirty. One day, a kind man decided to clean the car. He did not know that the car was very special. While he was cleaning the car, something unexpected happened. The car started to talk! The man was so surprised. The car told the man that it was magic. The man did not know this because he was ignorant about magic cars. The man and the car became good friends. They went on many fun rides together. The man was happy that he cleaned the car. Now, the man was not ignorant anymore, and they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little creative bird named Tim was flying in the sky. He saw a big tree with many branches. Tim thought it would be a good place to rest. So, he flew down and sat on a branch. While he was sitting there, he saw a big cat with a sharp mouth. The cat looked at Tim and said, "Hello, little bird. I am so hungry. Can you help me find some food?" Tim was scared, but he wanted to help the cat. Tim had an idea. He told the cat, "I know where to find food. Follow me." Tim flew high in the sky, and the cat had to jump from branch to branch to follow him. Suddenly, Tim flew very fast towards a bush with sharp thorns. The cat could not stop and fell into the bush. Tim flew back to the cat and said, "Now you have food. The bush has berries for you to eat." The cat was surprised but happy. He thanked Tim for his help. From that day on, the cat and the little creative bird became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, her mom was making a cake. Sue wanted to surprise her mom, so she took a pan and tried to cook on her own. Sue's mom saw her and said, "Sue, be careful! The pan is hot." Sue looked at the pan and touched it. "Ouch!" she cried. The pan was very hot. Her mom hugged her and said, "Always ask for help when you need it, my dear." From that day on, Sue learned to ask for help when she needed it. She knew that it was better to be safe than sorry. And her mom was always there to help her, making their time in the kitchen fun and full of love.

One day, a little fish named Fin was in the sea. He saw a big fish who was sad. The big fish said, "I can't swim very well." Fin wanted to help the big fish, so he had a brilliant idea. Fin said, "I can offer to teach you how to swim!" The big fish was happy and said, "Thank you, Fin!" They started to swim together. Fin showed the big fish how to move his tail and fins. As they swam, something unexpected happened. The big fish started to change colors! He became very bright and beautiful. Fin was surprised and said, "Wow! You are a rainbow fish!" The big fish smiled and said, "Thank you for helping me, Fin. Now I can swim and be happy!" They swam together, the best of friends, in the brilliant sea.
