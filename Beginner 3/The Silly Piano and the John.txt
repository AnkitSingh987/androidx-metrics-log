Category: Beginner
Title: The Silly Piano and the John

Once upon a time there was a silly piano. It belonged to two brothers, Tom and John. Tom loved to play the piano and it made him very happy. But John didn't like it. He thought it was too noisy. One day, Tom was playing the piano when John walked in. He shouted at Tom to stop playing the piano. In anger, John slapped the piano and made a big noise. Tom was very scared and he began to cry. He said to John, "Why did you do that?" John looked very angry and said, "Because I don't like the noise your piano makes. It's too silly!" Tom told John that he was sorry and he would never play the piano again if John didn't want him to. John felt bad for scaring Tom. He hugged him and said, "It's ok. You can play the piano if you want to." Tom smiled and hugged his brother back. From then on, both of them enjoyed playing the piano together.

Once upon a time, there was a charming cat named Luna. Luna loved to play with her toys and take long naps in the sun. One day, Luna found a veil in the garden. She didn't know what it was, so she decided to show it to her friend, the wise old owl. Luna went to the big tree where the owl lived. She climbed up and said, "Hello, Mr. Owl! Look what I found. What is it?" The owl looked at the veil and said, "Ah, Luna, this is a veil. People wear it on their head." Luna took the veil and put it on her head. She felt very pretty and happy. She went back to the garden and played with her toys. As the sun started to set, Luna began to yawn. She knew it was time for her nap. Luna went to her favorite spot, laid down with the veil on her head, and fell asleep, dreaming of all the fun she had that day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very sad because it had been dry for a whole week. He wanted to play in the rain, but the sun was out every day. One day, Tim met a big frog. The frog said, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim told the frog, "I want to play in the rain, but it's been dry all week." The frog thought for a moment and then said, "I have an idea! Let's play a game. If you can slap your hands together three times before I jump, I will make it rain for you." Tim was excited and said, "Okay, let's do it!" They got ready to play the game. Tim slapped his hands together once, twice, and then three times. The frog jumped high into the air, but it was too late. Tim had won the game. The frog smiled and said, "Well done, Tim! I will keep my promise." The frog closed its eyes and made a wish. Suddenly, dark clouds appeared, and it started to rain. Tim was so happy that he could finally play in the rain. He thanked the frog, and they played together in the rain all day long.

One day, a girl named Lucy was having lunch with her mum. Lucy's mum said, " Lucy, time to dress!" Lucy went to her room and put on her dress, which was pink and sparkly. On her plate, she noticed a pear. "Can I have a pear for lunch?" she asked her mum. Her mum said, "Yes, but it looks a bit poor." Lucy's mum had bought the pear from a poor farm. Even though it looked a bit old, it was still juicy and sweet. So Lucy and her mum enjoyed their lunch of pear and dress. Lucy was so happy with her meal that she clapped her hands together and smiled.

Once upon a time, there was a big red hat. The hat could talk. One day, the hat met a little dog. The dog was very happy to see the talking hat. The hat was serious. "Hello, little dog," said the hat. "I am a talking hat. Do you want to be friends?" The little dog barked and wagged its tail. "Yes, I want to be friends," said the dog. "I like talking to you, hat." They played all day. They ran and jumped. The hat and the dog talked and laughed. They were very happy together. And they were friends forever.
