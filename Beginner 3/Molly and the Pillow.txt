Category: Beginner
Title: Molly and the Pillow

Molly was a very creative three year old. She loved to paint and draw. One day her mum said to her, "Molly, why don't you paint a nice picture on your pillow?" Molly thought this was a great idea and sat down with her paints. She carefully chose the colors she wanted to use and started painting her pillow. When she finished, she was so excited and wanted to show her mum. She ran to the living room and saw her mum sitting on the sofa. She said, "Mum, look what I made!" Her mum smiled and said, "That looks very useful. Well done, Molly." Molly was so happy that her mum liked her pillow painting. She knew it was something useful and she was very proud of it.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big tree with green balls on it. Anna says, "What are those?" Ben says, "They are avocados. My mom uses them to make salad. They are yummy and good for you." Anna says, "Can we pick some?" Ben says, "I don't know. Maybe they belong to someone. Maybe they are dangerous." Anna says, "They don't look dangerous. They look soft and smooth. Let's try one." Ben says, "Okay, but be careful. We need a stick to reach them." They find a stick and poke the avocados. One falls down and rolls to them. Anna picks it up and tries to open it. She says, "It's hard. How do we eat it?" Ben says, "We need a knife to cut it. But we don't have a knife. And knives are dangerous too." Anna says, "Maybe we can smash it with a rock." Ben says, "That sounds fun. Let's do it." They find a rock and hit the avocado with it. It cracks and splits. They see the green flesh and the big seed inside. Anna says, "Wow, it's pretty. Can we eat it now?" Ben says, "I think so. But we have to share it. And we have to throw away the seed. It's too big to eat." They peel off the skin and take a bite of the avocado. They make a face. Anna says, "It's not yummy. It's bitter and mushy." Ben says, "You're right. It's not yummy. Maybe it's not ripe. Or maybe it needs salt and lemon." Anna says, "Or maybe it's not for us. Maybe it's for birds or squirrels." Ben says, "Maybe. Let's leave it here. Maybe they will like it." They drop the avocado and run away. They decide to play something else. They say, "Avocados are not fun. They are hard and bitter and dangerous. We don't like them." The end.

One day, a girl and her aunt went to the park. They played with a ball and ran around. The girl was very happy. The aunt was happy too. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. They were having a lot of fun. Suddenly, the girl saw a distant tree shake. "Look, aunt!" she said. "That tree is shaking!" The aunt looked at the tree and saw it shake too. They both wondered what could make the tree shake like that. Then, a big wind came and blew the tree's leaves. The girl and her aunt laughed. "It was just the wind!" said the aunt. The girl and her aunt played in the park some more and went home. The day was full of fun and surprises.

One sunny day, a little girl named Sue was playing outside. She saw her friend Tom with a big box. Sue wanted to join Tom and see what was inside the box. Tom was generous and said, "Sue, come and look at my box." Inside the box, there were many colorful rags. Sue and Tom decided to play with the rags. They tied them together and made a long, pretty line. They were having a lot of fun. Suddenly, the rags started to move! Sue and Tom were scared. But then they saw a little kitten playing with the rags. The kitten was making the rags move! They laughed and played with the kitten. Now, they had a new friend to join their fun.
