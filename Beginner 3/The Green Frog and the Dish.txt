Category: Beginner
Title: The Green Frog and the Dish

Once upon a time, there was a green frog. The frog lived in a pond. One day, the frog found a dish. The dish was big and round. The frog wanted to play with the dish. He jumped on the dish and the dish began to spin. The frog had so much fun spinning on the dish. He did not want to quit. After a while, the frog got tired. He decided to quit playing with the dish. The frog jumped off the dish and went back to his pond. The green frog was happy he found a fun dish to play with.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the forest with his mom. They wanted to attach a birdhouse to a tree. The forest was big and had many trees. Tim was very happy to help the birds find a new home. As they walked, they saw a sad, miserable bird. "Why are you so sad?" asked Tim. The bird said, "I lost my friend, and I can't find him." Tim wanted to help the bird find his friend. So, they all looked around the forest together. Just when they were about to give up, they found the bird's friend stuck in a hole. Tim and his mom helped the bird's friend get out of the hole. The birds were so happy they could be together again. They thanked Tim and his mom, and they all went to attach the birdhouse to a tree. The birds now had a new home, and Tim had made new friends in the forest.

One day, a small crab named Sam went for a walk on the beach. He wanted to find a new shell to relax in. His old shell was too small. He looked around, but he could not find any shells. Sam saw his friend, a seagull named Sue. Sue was very organized. She knew where all the shells were. Sam asked Sue, "Can you help me find a new shell?" Sue said, "Yes, I can help you!" They looked for shells together. Soon, they found a big, pretty shell. Sam was happy. He could relax in his new shell. Sam and Sue played on the beach all day.

One day, Tom and Ann went to play at the park. They had a big, red ball to play with. They liked to share the ball and play together. The park was very quiet and they were happy. Tom saw a spicy pepper on the ground. He picked it up and said, "Look, Ann! I found a spicy pepper!" Ann said, "Be careful, Tom. Spicy peppers can be hot!" Just then, a small, green bird flew down and took the spicy pepper from Tom's hand. The bird ate the pepper very fast. Tom and Ann were surprised! They didn't know birds liked spicy food. The bird started to dance and sing a fun song. Tom and Ann laughed and clapped their hands. They were not in a quiet park anymore. The bird became their new friend and they all played with the big, red ball together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to his classroom. He saw a new girl sitting in the room. She was very pretty. Tim thought she was attractive. He wanted to be her friend. Tim walked up to the girl and said, "Hi, I am Tim. What is your name?" The girl smiled and said, "My name is Lily. I am new here." Tim and Lily started to talk and became friends. Lily liked to draw beautiful pictures. Tim would watch her draw and admire her work. They had fun in the classroom together. Tim and Lily were happy to be friends.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a little mole. The mole liked to dig holes. He was very persistent. He dug holes every day. He had many friends in the garden, like the rabbit, the bird, and the ant. One day, the mole and his friends wanted to play a game. They had to choose a game they all could play. The rabbit wanted to race, but the mole was slow. The bird wanted to fly, but the mole could not fly. The ant wanted to carry things, but the mole was not very strong. The mole was sad. He wanted to play with his friends, but he could not do what they could do. Then, the mole had an idea. He asked his friends to play a digging game. They all agreed. The mole was very happy. He showed his friends how to dig holes. They all had fun and learned something new. The mole was proud of himself for being persistent and finding a game they all could play.
