Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Cheerful Boy and the Sue

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful boy named Tim. He loved to play outside with his friends. One day, Tim's mom told him they were going to a camp. Tim was very excited. At the camp, Tim met a girl named Sue. They played together all day. They ran, jumped, and laughed. Tim said to Sue, "I hope we can be friends forever!" Sue smiled and said, "Me too, Tim!" On the last day of camp, Tim and Sue had a race. They wanted to see who could run the fastest. They both ran as fast as they could. Tim started to increase his speed. He ran faster and faster. In the end, Tim won the race. Sue was happy for him. They hugged and promised to see each other next year at camp.

One day, a big dog and a small cat lived in a yard. The yard was dry and had no water. The big dog was happy, but the small cat was not. The small cat wanted to play with the big dog, but the big dog would always ignore her. One day, the small cat said, "Big dog, please play with me. I am so sad." The big dog looked at the small cat and said, "No, I will not play with you. You are too small and weak." The small cat was very sad, but she had an idea. The next day, the small cat found a big ball in the yard. She pushed the big ball to the big dog and said, "Big dog, let's play with this big ball together." The big dog was surprised and said, "Okay, small cat. I will play with you." They played with the big ball all day, and the big dog did not ignore the small cat anymore. The small cat was happy, and the big dog learned that it is nice to play with friends, even if they are small.

Once upon a time, there was a soldier. He was very strong and brave. He lived in a place where the sun was harsh and hot. The soldier had a big hat to help him feel cool. One day, the soldier lost his hat. He felt sad because the sun was too hot. He started to search for his hat. He looked under the big rocks and behind the green trees. As he searched, a little bird saw him. The bird wanted to help the soldier. The bird found the hat on a tree branch. The soldier was happy to have his hat back. The soldier and the bird became good friends, and they played together every day.

One day, a boy named Tim had a red wagon. He liked to pull the wagon all around his yard. The sun was big and bright in the sky. Tim saw a bird with a harsh voice. The bird said, "Help me, I hurt my wing!" Tim wanted to help the bird, so he put the bird in his red wagon. The bird was happy and said, "Thank you, Tim!" Tim and the bird went to find help. They saw a girl named Sue. Sue could help the bird. She fixed the bird's wing. The bird was so happy, it started to clap its wings. Tim and Sue clapped their hands too. They all laughed and played together.

One day, Sally was out for a walk in the park. She was skipping merrily when she spotted something charming in the grass. She ran to it as fast as she could, and found it was a lovely blanket. "Oh, how pretty!" Sally said. Sally picked up the blanket and hugged it. She was so happy that she decided to take it with her. She skipped back home and presented the blanket to her parents. "What a charming gift!" Sally's parents said. Sally was delighted. She put the blanket in her room and it quickly become her favorite thing in the world. Every night she would snuggle beneath it while she listened to her parents read her stories. And that's how Sally found her charming blanket in the park.

Once upon a time, in a small garden, there lived a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to wander and look at all the plants. One day, Mia saw a new plant. It was small and green. Mia went to her friend Tom and said, "Look! I found a new plant!" Tom was upset because he wanted to find a new plant too. So, they decided to take care of the plant together. But Mia and Tom did not know how to take care of the plant. They gave it too much water and not enough sun. The plant became very upset and soon, it died. Mia and Tom were sad that they could not save the plant. They promised to learn more about plants before trying to take care of another one.
