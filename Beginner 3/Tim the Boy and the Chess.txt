Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Chess

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy chess set. He liked to play chess with his friends. One day, he found his chess set was filthy. It was very dirty, and Tim did not like it. Tim asked his friend, Sam, "Did you make my chess set dirty?" Sam said, "No, I did not. I think I know who did it." Tim and Sam went to find who made Tim's chess set filthy. They found a big, scary cat. The cat liked to scare little kids. Tim and Sam were brave. They said to the cat, "You made my chess set dirty. Please do not do it again." The cat said, "I am sorry. I will not do it again." Tim and Sam were happy. They cleaned the chess set and played together. The scary cat did not scare them anymore. They all became friends and played chess together.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat named Tom. Tom was very hungry. He wanted to cook some eggs in a pan. He was happy to make his food. Tom put the eggs in the pan. But, he was not good at cooking. The eggs scatter all over the floor. Tom was frustrated. He did not know what to do. Then, a little mouse named Jerry came. Jerry saw the messy floor. He wanted to help Tom. Jerry and Tom became friends. They cleaned up the mess together. They both learned how to cook eggs. And, they were not hungry anymore.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a red wagon in his yard. He was very happy. Tim thought it would be fun to put his toys in the wagon and pull them around. He put his teddy bear, his ball, and his toy car in the wagon. As Tim pulled the wagon, he met his friend Sue. Sue looked at the wagon and said, "Can I play too?" Tim said, "Yes, but we need to repeat what I am doing." So, Sue found a box and put her doll and her toy dog in it. They pulled their toys together and had fun. Then, a thoughtful big boy named Jack came by. He saw Tim and Sue playing and wanted to join them. But Jack had a big toy truck and it was too big for the wagon and box. He had an idea. Jack put the wagon and box in his truck and said, "Now we can all play together!" Tim, Sue, and Jack laughed and played all day with their toys.

One day, a bald man went for a walk. He liked to walk in the quiet park. The park was full of trees and flowers. He liked the silence there. As the bald man walked, he saw a big box. He opened the box and found a hat. He put the hat on his head. The hat made him feel happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The hat jumped off his head and returned to the box. The bald man was sad. He wanted the hat back. But the hat stayed in the box. The man walked away, still bald, but he learned that some things are better left alone.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a cat named Tom and a dog named Max. They were regular friends who played together every day. One day, Tom saw a new cat in the yard. The new cat looked hungry. Tom said, "Hello, new cat. Welcome to our yard. Do you want to play with us?" The new cat smiled and said, "Yes, I would like that." After playing, they all felt hungry. Max said, "Let's eat some meat." They went inside the house and found a big plate of meat. They all ate together and became good friends. From that day on, they always played and ate together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a wise judge. The judge was very nice and everyone liked him. He had a rare big book that he used to help people. One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to borrow the rare big book. She asked the judge, "Can I borrow your big book, please?" The judge said, "Yes, you can borrow it, but please be careful." Lily took the rare big book home and read it. She learned many new things. When she was done, she brought it back to the judge. The judge was happy and said, "Thank you for taking good care of my big book." Lily smiled and felt proud.
