Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben and the Slide

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play with Spot, the dog. Spot is big and brown and has a black spot on his ear. Spot likes to run and jump and lick their faces. One day, Anna and Ben go to the park with Spot. They see a big slide and a swing and a sandbox. They want to try them all. But Spot wants to stay with them. He barks and pulls on his leash. "Spot, stop!" Anna says. "We want to play here. You can play with us later." But Spot does not listen. He breaks free from his leash and runs after them. He jumps on the slide and makes Anna fall. He pushes the swing and makes Ben dizzy. He digs in the sandbox and throws sand everywhere. "Spot, no!" Ben says. "You are being bad. You are ruining our fun. Go away!" Spot feels sad. He does not understand why his friends are angry. He just wants to play with them. He lowers his head and walks away. He finds a corner under a tree and lies down. He closes his eyes and whimpers. Anna and Ben finish playing. They look for Spot. They do not see him anywhere. They start to worry. "Where is Spot?" Anna asks. "Maybe he ran away. Maybe he is lost. Maybe he is hurt." "Maybe he is mad at us," Ben says. "Maybe we were too mean to him. Maybe he does not love us anymore." They feel sorry. They remember how much they love Spot. They remember how much fun they have with him. They decide to find him and say sorry. They search the park. They call his name. They ask other people. They do not give up. They finally see him under the tree. He is still lying down. He is still closing his eyes. He is still whimpering. "Spot!" Anna and Ben shout. "We found you! We are so happy! We are so sorry!" They run to him and hug him. They pet him and kiss him. They tell him they love him. Spot opens his eyes. He sees his friends. He feels happy. He wags his tail and licks their faces. He forgives them. They take him to the slide and the swing and the sandbox. They play with him. They share their toys. They have fun. They learn a lesson. They learn to be kind to Spot. They learn to include him in their games. They learn to be good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a humble little girl named Sally. She had a big red button that she loved. The button was shiny and made her smile. Sally took the button everywhere she went. One day, Sally went to her friend Tom's house. She wanted to bring her button to show Tom. Sally and Tom played with the button all day. They put it on their shirts and pretended it was a magic button. At the end of the day, Sally and Tom were tired. Sally put her button in her pocket and went home. She was so happy that she could bring her button to her friend's house. They had a fun day together, and the button made them both smile.

Lily liked to play with her dad's wallet. It had many cards and pictures and a zipper. She liked to open and close the zipper and look at the cards and pictures. Sometimes she put the cards and pictures in different places in the wallet. She thought it was fun. One day, her dad was going to the store. He needed his wallet to buy things. He looked for his wallet in his coat, but he could not find it. He looked for his wallet in his pants, but he could not find it. He looked for his wallet in his bag, but he could not find it. He was worried. He asked Lily if she had seen his wallet. Lily remembered that she had played with his wallet. She had put it in her toy car. She had pushed the car under the sofa. She had forgotten to give it back to her dad. She felt sorry. She ran to the sofa and pulled out the car. She opened the car and took out the wallet. She ran to her dad and gave him the wallet. Her dad was happy. He thanked Lily and hugged her. He looked at his wallet and saw that the cards and pictures were in different places. He smiled and said, "You have a very modern way of organizing my wallet. Maybe you can teach me how to reverse it." He and Lily laughed and went to the store.
