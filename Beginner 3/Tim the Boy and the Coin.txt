Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Coin

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a small, dark room. In his room, he found a shiny coin. Tim was very happy. He put the coin in his pocket and went outside to play. While playing, Tim met his friend, Sam. Tim wanted to show Sam his shiny coin. But when he looked in his pocket, the coin was gone. Tim felt sad and let out a big sigh. Sam wanted to help Tim find the coin, so they looked everywhere. As they searched, they saw a squirrel holding the shiny coin. They were surprised! Tim and Sam chased the squirrel, and it dropped the coin. Tim picked it up and smiled. From that day on, Tim and Sam were always careful with their things.

One day, a big, dull box sat in the yard. A little boy named Tim saw the box and went to see what was inside. He tried to open it, but it was close shut. Tim was sad and didn't know what to do. A small bean came out from the ground and spoke to Tim. "I can help you open the box," said the bean. Tim was happy and asked the bean to help him. The bean grew into a big, strong beanstalk and pushed the box open. When the box opened, there was a big, mean cat inside. The cat jumped out and scared Tim and the beanstalk. The cat laughed, "I am free now!" And it ran away to cause trouble. Tim and the beanstalk were sad. They had let the mean cat out, and now they couldn't fix it.

Once upon a time, there were two fish. One was called Jack and the other was called Jill. They loved to play and swim together in the pond. One day, Jack thought of a game. He said to Jill, "Let’s play hide and seek!". Jill was excited, so she said yes. Jack said, "I’ll hide first. You try to find me". He swam away and hid behind a rock. Jill looked around the pond and swam around, looking for Jack. She couldn't find him anywhere. She looked everywhere and began to worry. Suddenly, out popped Jack! He had been hiding behind the rock the whole time. Jill was surprised, but also happy. She said to Jack, "That was a really competitive hide and seek game". Jack smiled and said, "Yes, it was. Shall we play again?" Jill said, "Yes, let’s play again!" So they swam away and started to play their favorite game again.

One day, a little boy named Tim was eager to go outside and play. He put on his leather shoes to prevent getting his feet wet from the rain that had just stopped. Tim saw his friend, Sam, and they decided to play a game together. "Let's play catch!" said Tim. Sam nodded and they started throwing the ball back and forth. They were having a lot of fun, but they didn't notice that they were getting closer and closer to the street. Suddenly, the ball rolled into the street. Tim ran after it, forgetting to look both ways. A big car was coming fast, and it couldn't stop in time. Tim was hit by the car, and he got hurt very badly. Sam and Tim's mom came running to help Tim, but it was too late. Tim should have been more careful when playing near the street. From that day on, Sam always remembered to be careful and look both ways before crossing the street.

Lily and Tom were best friends. They liked to play in the garden and look for bugs and flowers. One day, they saw a big hedge at the end of the garden. They wondered what was behind it. "Let's go and see!" said Tom. "But Mom said we should not go near the hedge. It has thorns and it is not safe," said Lily. "Mom will not know. Come on, it will be fun!" said Tom. He took Lily's hand and they ran to the hedge. They pushed through the branches and felt the thorns prick their skin. They cried out in pain. "Ow, ow, ow! This hurts!" said Lily. "Shh, don't be loud. Mom will hear us," said Tom. They reached the other side of the hedge and saw a big road with many cars and trucks. The cars and trucks made a lot of noise and smoke. The air was harsh and dirty. "This is not fun. This is scary," said Lily. "I don't like it either. Let's go back," said Tom. They turned around and tried to find their way back through the hedge. But they got lost and confused. They could not see the garden or their house. They could only see the hedge and the road. They started to cry and call for Mom. But Mom could not hear them. She was inside the house, making lunch. They wished they had listened to Mom and stayed away from the hedge. They wished they had not broken their promise. They learned a hard lesson that day. They learned that promises are important and that Mom knows best. They learned that some things are not worth the risk and that some places are not meant for children. They learned to be careful and obedient.
