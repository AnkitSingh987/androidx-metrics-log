Category: Beginner
Title: The Great Ball of Tim and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that he loved to play with. But one day, he saw his friend, Sam, playing with a big, rough ball. Tim thought the rough ball looked fun and wanted to play with it too. Tim asked Sam if they could trade toys for a day. Sam said yes, they could trade. Tim gave Sam his toy car, and Sam gave Tim the rough ball. They both played with their new toys and had lots of fun. After a day, Tim started to miss his toy car. He realized he didn't like the rough ball as much as he thought. Tim didn't hate the rough ball, but he loved his toy car more. So, Tim and Sam traded their toys back. They were both happy to have their favorite toys again, and they continued to play together with their toys every day.

Once upon a time, in a beautiful land, there lived a little girl named Lily. Lily had a big, red ball that she loved to play with. One day, while she was playing, a mean boy named Jack came up to her. Jack said, "I will spoil your ball if you don't give it to me." Lily was scared of Jack's threat, so she gave him the ball. Jack laughed and ran away with the ball. But then, something unexpected happened. A big, friendly dog saw Jack and wanted to play. The dog jumped on Jack, and the ball flew out of his hands. The ball rolled back to Lily, and she picked it up. The dog stayed with Lily, and they played together all day. Jack never bothered Lily again, and she made a new friend in the big, friendly dog.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. Lily was very excite to play. She saw a clean, green pitch and said, "Mom, let's play here!" Her mom agreed and they started to play catch with a soft ball. "Throw the ball, Lily!" her mom said. Lily threw the ball high in the air. Her mom caught it and threw it back. They laughed and played for a long time. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. It was a happy day at the pitch. After playing, Lily and her mom sat down on a clean, soft blanket. They ate yummy sandwiches and sweet fruit. "I love playing with you, mom," Lily said, smiling. Her mom smiled back and said, "I love playing with you too, Lily." They had a fun day at the park, and Lily was very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy blocks. He would build a big stack with them. One day, Tim saw something new in his yard. It was smelly and green. It was a plant! "Mommy, what is that?" Tim asked. "That is a plant, Tim. It will grow big and tall," said Mommy. Tim was very excited. He wanted to watch the plant grow. Every day, Tim would go outside and look at the plant. He saw it grow a little bit more each day. The plant was still smelly, but Tim didn't mind. He was happy to see his new friend grow. And as the plant grew, so did Tim's love for it.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She liked to collect pretty rocks. One day, she went to the park to find more rocks for her collection. As she was looking, she found a shiny crown on the ground. It was hard and pretty. Lucy put the crown on her head and felt like a princess. Lucy wore the crown all day as she played in the park. When it was time to go home, she took off the crown and put it with her rocks. Now, Lucy had a new, special thing in her collection.

One day, a little yellow bird named Sunny lived in a big tree. Sunny loved to sing. Every day, he would try to make his song better. He wanted all his friends to hear his pretty song. One morning, Sunny met a new friend, a small bug named Buzz. Buzz said, "I want to sing like you, Sunny. Can you teach me?" Sunny smiled and said, "I will help you try to sing, Buzz." They practiced together, making their songs better and better. But one day, while they were singing, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew Buzz away! Sunny was so sad. He missed his friend. So, he sang a special song, hoping Buzz would hear him. The wind heard Sunny's song and brought Buzz back to him. They were so happy to see each other and kept singing together.
