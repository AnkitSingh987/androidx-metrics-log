Category: Beginner
Title: Jack the Boy and the Wheat

One day, a boy named Jack was walking in the park. He saw some wheat and decided to shake it. He shook it gently, but nothing happened. Jack went on walking. On the way, he saw a wheat farmer. The farmer was harvesting some wheat. He was shaking the wheat gently and the grains were coming off the stalk. Jack stopped and watched as the farmer shook the wheat. He was fascinated with the gentle movements of the farmer's hands. He wanted to try it out. So Jack asked the farmer if he could shake the wheat. The farmer said yes and gave Jack some wheat. Jack shook the wheat gently, and a shower of golden grains rained down. He laughed in delight. Jack was so glad he had seen the wheat farmer and asked if he could try shaking the wheat himself. From then on, Jack spent lots of time shaking wheat - and never got tired of it.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big lollipop. He was very happy. He wanted to measure the lollipop to see how big it was. Tim asked his mom for help. "Mom, can you help me measure my lollipop?" Tim said. Mom came and helped Tim measure the lollipop. They found out it was very big! Tim wanted to eat it all, but Mom said, "Tim, you should eat only a little bit. It is not healthy to eat too much candy." Just then, a little bird came and took the lollipop away. Tim was sad, but Mom said, "Don't worry, Tim. Now you can eat something healthy instead." Tim smiled and they went inside to eat some yummy fruits.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his ball. He was a brilliant player. One day, Tim went to the park to play. He saw a big dog. The dog was very big and very loud. Tim wanted to play, but the dog was in his way. Tim said, "Dog, please move. I want to play." The dog did not mind Tim. The dog just sat there. Tim was sad. He did not know what to do. Then, a girl named Sue came to the park. Sue saw Tim and the dog. Sue said, "Tim, you can ask the dog nicely. Say, 'Dog, may I play, please?'" Tim tried it. He said, "Dog, may I play, please?" The dog moved! Tim was happy. He played with his ball all day. The moral of the story is to always be polite and ask nicely.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a little girl named Mia. Mia was very adventurous. She loved to play with her best friend, a small cat named Tom. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. One day, Mia and Tom saw two big dogs having a fight. Mia said to Tom, "We must stop the fight!" So, they both ran to the dogs and Mia kneeled down. She said, "Please stop fighting, friends!" The dogs stopped and looked at Mia and Tom. Then, something unexpected happened. The dogs started to laugh! They said, "We were not fighting, we were playing a game. Thank you for caring, Mia and Tom." Mia and Tom were happy that the dogs were not fighting. They all became friends and played together every day.

One day, a big cat and a little dog went to play in the park. The sun was shining and the grass was thick and green. They were very happy to play together. The cat liked to jump and the dog liked to run. As they were playing, they heard a loud sound. "What was that?" asked the cat. The dog said, "I don't know. Let's go see!" They ran to the sound and saw a big box on the ground. They were very curious. The cat and the dog looked at the box and heard a small voice inside. "Help me, please!" said the voice. They opened the box and found a little bird. The bird was scared but happy to be free. The cat, the dog, and the bird all played together in the park. They had a lot of fun and became good friends.
