Category: Beginner
Title: The White Ball and the Bird

One day, a white ball went for a walk. It liked to roll around and see new things. As it rolled, it saw a big cliff near the sea. The ball was scared but wanted to get closer. As it got near the cliff, it met a little bird. The bird said, "Hi! I like your white color. Be careful near the cliff, it's very high!" The ball said, "Thank you, bird. I will be careful." The ball and the bird played together. They had fun and laughed a lot. But the ball remembered to stay away from the cliff. The white ball and the bird became good friends and had many more fun days together.

Lily and Ben were friends who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big tree with many red fruits. Lily wanted to pick some fruits, but Ben said no. "Those fruits are not good. They are sour and hard. Let's play on the swings instead," Ben said. But Lily did not listen to Ben. She ignored him and climbed the tree. She reached for a fruit and pulled it hard. But the fruit was not ripe. It was green and bitter. Lily made a face and threw the fruit away. "Yuck! This fruit is bad. Maybe this one is better," Lily said. She picked another fruit, but it was the same. She tried many fruits, but none of them were sweet or juicy. Ben watched Lily from the ground. He felt sorry for her. He knew that the fruits would be good in the summer, when they were red and soft. But now it was spring, and the weather was mild. The fruits needed more sun and rain to grow. "Lily, come down. You are wasting your time. The fruits are not ready yet. Let's play something else," Ben said. Lily looked at Ben. She realized that he was right. She had been stubborn and foolish. She climbed down the tree and apologized to Ben. "Ben, I'm sorry. I should have listened to you. You are a good friend. Can we still play together?" Lily said. Ben smiled and hugged Lily. He forgave her and said yes. They went to the swings and had fun. They learned that it is better to listen to your friends than to ignore them. And they learned to be patient and wait for the right time to enjoy the fruits.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy boy named Tim. He had many toys and a big house. One day, Tim saw a girl named Sue. She was sad because her toy was dirty. Tim wanted to help Sue. He took a cloth to wipe her toy clean. Sue was very happy. They played together with the clean toy. But then, Tim's zipper on his bag broke. All his toys fell out. Tim was sad. Sue helped Tim pick up the toys. They fixed the zipper together. Now, Tim and Sue were both happy and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly man named Mr. Brown. He was very old and liked to sit on a bench in the park. Every day, he would watch what was happening around him. He liked to observe the birds, the trees, and the people. One day, while Mr. Brown was at the park, he saw a big sign. The sign said that there was a new law. It said that people had to be nice to each other in the park. Mr. Brown thought this was a good law. He liked it when people were nice to each other. As Mr. Brown sat on his bench, he saw a little boy and a little girl playing. They were sharing their toys and being nice to each other. Mr. Brown was happy to see the new law working. He knew that being kind made the park a better place for everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a fat bear named Benny. He was very hungry and wanted something to eat. He found some chocolate and wanted to eat it. But it was too hard for him to break, so he picked it up and started to tear it. He kept tearing until it was in small pieces. Then he put the pieces in his mouth and started to eat. It tasted so yummy that Benny kept eating it until it was all gone. When he was finished, Benny was so full and happy. The end.
