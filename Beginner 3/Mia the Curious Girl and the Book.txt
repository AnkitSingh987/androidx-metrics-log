Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Curious Girl and the Book

Once upon a time, there was a curious little girl named Mia. She loved to learn new words. One day, Mia found a big book in her room. It was a dictionary. She was very happy. Mia started to look at the dictionary every day. She learned many new words. One day, she found a special word in the dictionary. The word was "be". She did not know what it meant. She was very curious. Mia asked her mom, "What does 'be' mean?" Her mom smiled and said, "It means to exist or to live." Mia thought about it and said, "Oh, I understand now!" But then, something unexpected happened. The dictionary started to talk! It said, "Hello, Mia! I am a magic dictionary. I can help you learn even more words." Mia was surprised but excited to have a new friend. They had lots of fun learning words together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toy car. One day, he saw a big mud puddle. He thought it would be fun to drive his car through it. As he played, the car got very dirty. Tim knew he had to clean it. So, he took it to the sink and washed it with water. The car looked clean again, and Tim was happy. But then, Tim's mom saw the mess he made in the sink. She was not happy. The truth was, Tim did not clean the sink after he washed his car. Tim's mom told him he could not play with his toy car for a whole day. Tim felt sad and learned that he should always clean up after himself.

Once upon a time, there was a big, wide tree. It was in a navy-blue forest. The tree had many friends like birds, squirrels, and bugs. One sunny day, a little bunny was playing near the tree. The bunny was running and jumping around. It was having a lot of fun. But then, the bunny did not see a big rock and it stumbled. The bunny fell down and felt sad. The wide tree saw the bunny and wanted to help. The tree asked its friends, the birds, to sing a happy song. The squirrels came and played with the bunny. The bugs made the bunny laugh. The bunny felt better and said thank you to the tree and its friends. They all played together in the navy-blue forest, and they were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She liked to play in the park. One day, she saw a pretty rock on the ground. It was a gem! Mia was very happy and picked it up. Mia showed the gem to her friend Tom. "Look!" she said. Tom was surprised. "Wow!" he replied. They played with the gem all day. But then, a fierce dog came to the park. The dog wanted the gem too! Mia and Tom were scared, but they had an idea. They threw the gem far away. The dog ran to get it. But, the gem was not a gem! It was a ball. The dog just wanted to play. Mia and Tom laughed and played with the dog.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to eat beans. One day, she found a big bean in her garden. She was very happy. Sue showed the bean to her mom. Her mom said, "Oh no, Sue! That is a terrible bean. Do not eat it!" Sue did not want to eat the terrible bean, but she wanted to see what would happen if she planted it. Sue dug a hole and put the bean inside. She twisted the dirt back on top of the bean. She watered it and waited. The next day, a big plant grew from the bean. Sue was so surprised! "Mom, look!" Sue said. "The terrible bean made a big plant!" Her mom looked at the plant and smiled. "Maybe it's not so terrible after all," she said. Sue and her mom laughed and played by the big plant all day.

Once upon a time, there was a humble tree. It lived in a big forest with many other trees. One day, a little bird came to the tree and said, "Hello, humble tree! I lost my ornament. Can you help me find it?" The humble tree wanted to help the little bird. So, they looked high and low for the ornament. They looked in the grass and on the ground. They looked in the sky and in the air. But they could not find the ornament anywhere. Finally, the humble tree and the little bird found the ornament. It was lying under a big rock. The little bird was so happy! The humble tree and the little bird became best friends. They played together every day, and they lived happily ever after.
