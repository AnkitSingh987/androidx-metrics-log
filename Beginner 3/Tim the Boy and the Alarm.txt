Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Alarm

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He wanted to find a new toy to play with. He saw many toys, but none of them were available for him to take home. Tim started to wonder where he could find the perfect toy. As Tim walked, he saw a big red alarm on a wall. He had never seen an alarm like this before. Tim thought it might be a toy, so he pushed the alarm button. Suddenly, loud noises came from the alarm, and many people came running. The people were not mad at Tim. They were happy! They told Tim that the alarm was a test to see if it worked, and he helped them find out that it did. They thanked Tim and gave him a toy as a reward. Tim was so happy that he found a new toy and helped the people with their alarm test.

One day, a dog named Max went to a lab with his friend, a cat named Lily. Max was a big dog, and Lily was a small cat. They liked to play together. The lab was a fun place where they could play with many toys. It was a wet day, so they were happy to be inside. In the lab, Max found a big, wet ball. He picked it up with his mouth and said, "Lily, do you want to play with this ball?" Lily said, "Yes! I like it when the ball is wet. It is more fun to play with." So, they played with the wet ball, running and jumping. As they played, Max accidentally bit Lily's tail. "Ouch!" cried Lily. "That hurt, Max!" Max felt bad and said, "I am sorry, Lily. I did not mean to bite your tail." Lily said, "It's okay, Max. I know you didn't mean it." They hugged and went back to playing with the wet ball, being more careful not to bite each other again.

Once upon a time, there was a little bridge over a small river. The bridge was always alert, watching the fish swim and the birds fly. One day, a big rain came and started to fill the river with water. A duck and her babies came to the river. They wanted to cross the bridge, but the water was too high. The duck said, "Oh no, my babies! We need to find a way to cross the bridge." A kind frog heard the duck and jumped to help. The frog said, "I have an idea! Let's all hold hands and hop across the bridge together." The duck and her babies held hands with the frog and hopped across the bridge. They all made it safely to the other side of the river. The duck said, "Thank you, frog, for helping us!" The frog smiled and said, "You're welcome. I'm happy to help my friends." The duck and her babies were so happy, and they all became good friends with the frog.

Once upon a time, there was a dirty car. The car was sad because it was dirty. The car needed oil to feel better. The car had a friend, a little boy named Tim. Tim wanted to help the dirty car. Tim went to his dad and asked for oil. His dad gave him the oil. Tim was happy. He wanted to take the oil to the car. Tim carried the oil to the dirty car. The car was excited to get the oil. But Tim was too small. He tried to put the oil in the car, but he spilled it. The car was still dirty and now it had no oil. The car was still sad. Tim was sad too. They could not fix the car.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a pretty rose. The rose was high up on a bush. Lily wanted to pick the rose, but she was too small. Lily saw a tall boy named Tom. She asked, "Can you help me get the rose?" Tom said, "Yes, I will join you." They walked to the rose bush together. Tom reached up high and picked the rose for Lily. But when Tom gave the rose to Lily, it turned into a beautiful butterfly! Lily and Tom were very surprised. They watched the butterfly fly away. It was a magical day for Lily and Tom.
