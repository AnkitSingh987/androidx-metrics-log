Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Tom and the Bird

Sara and Tom are twins. They like to play together. They have a big toy box with many toys. They have dolls, cars, blocks, books and more. One day, they find a weird toy in the toy box. It is a bird with a blue body and a red wing. But the wing is not on the bird. It is separate. The bird has a hole where the wing should be. "Look, Tom, a weird bird!" Sara says. She picks up the bird and the wing. "Let me see, Sara!" Tom says. He wants to play with the bird too. "No, it is mine!" Sara says. She holds the bird and the wing tight. "Give me the wing, Sara!" Tom says. He tries to grab the wing from Sara. "Stop, Tom!" Sara says. She pulls the wing away from Tom. They start to fight over the bird and the wing. They pull and push and shout. The bird and the wing fall to the floor. Suddenly, they hear a voice. It is Mom. "What are you doing, Sara and Tom?" Mom says. She sees the bird and the wing on the floor. She is not happy. "We found a weird bird, Mom!" Sara says. "But it has a separate wing, Mom!" Tom says. Mom picks up the bird and the wing. She looks at them. "This is not a weird bird, Sara and Tom," Mom says. "This is a puzzle. You have to put the wing in the hole to make the bird whole." She shows them how to fit the wing in the hole. The bird looks happy. "Wow, Mom, you are smart!" Sara says. "Can we play with the bird, Mom?" Tom says. "Yes, you can, Sara and Tom," Mom says. "But you have to share and be nice. No fighting, okay?" "Okay, Mom," Sara and Tom say. They hug Mom and thank her. They take the bird and the wing to their room. They play with the puzzle. They take turns to separate and join the wing. They make the bird fly and sing. They have fun together. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play outside. One day, she went to the park. At the park, Sue saw a fly. The fly was very fast. Sue tried to catch the fly, but it was too quick for her. Then, the fly did something that made Sue very surprise. The fly began to talk! The fly said, "I have a gift for you, Sue." The fly gave Sue a big, sour lemon. Sue smiled and said, "Thank you, Mr. Fly!" Sue took the lemon home and made lemonade. She shared the lemonade with her friends, and they all laughed and played together.

Once there was a little boy named Tim. He had a soft pillow that he loved very much. Every night, Tim would hug his pillow tight when he went to sleep. One day, Tim's friend Sam came to play. They played with toys and had lots of fun. Then, Sam saw Tim's pillow and said, "Can I play with your pillow?" Tim thought for a moment and said, "Okay, but be careful. It is my favorite pillow." Sam played with the pillow, but he was not careful. He spilled juice on it. Tim was very sad and told Sam, "I let you play with my pillow, and now it's dirty. I regret giving it to you." Sam felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, Tim. Let's clean it up." They worked together to clean the pillow. Tim's mom saw them and helped too. She said, "Next time, be more alert when you play with special things." From that day on, Tim and Sam were more careful when they played. They stayed friends and had many more happy playdates. And Tim's pillow stayed clean and soft, just the way he liked it.

Once upon a time, there was a small girl named Sally. She had an adventurous spirit and enjoyed exploring her world. One day, she stumbled upon a mysterious texture in her backyard. She crouched down to touch it. When she felt it, Sally trembled because the texture was very slimy and disgusting. She exclaimed, "Eww! What is this stuff?" Suddenly, a voice answered, "Hi there, Sally! I'm Timmy the Mushroom. I'm here in your backyard to make sure that no one eats me." Sally backed away in shock. "You can talk?" She asked, her voice trembling. "Yes," replied Timmy, "I'm a special kind of mushroom. I'm here to protect the other plants and animals in your backyard. You see, some things are really disgusting and bad for us, like trash or chemical sprays. I'm here to help protect your backyard." Sally smiled. She touched the mushroom gently and thanked him for his kindness. From that day on, Sally and Timmy were the best of friends. She visited him often and together, they kept her backyard safe and happy.
