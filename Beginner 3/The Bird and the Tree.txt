Category: Beginner
Title: The Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little bird who was very stubborn. This bird did not like to rest. It liked to fly all day long. The bird had a friend, a big palm tree. The palm tree liked to rest in the sun. One day, the palm tree said to the bird, "You should rest with me. It is good to rest sometimes." But the stubborn bird did not want to rest. It wanted to keep flying. After a while, the bird got very tired. It finally decided to rest on the palm tree. The palm tree was happy to have its friend rest with it. The bird learned that it is good to rest sometimes, and they both enjoyed the sun together.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a pretty lily. The lily was very obedient. It always listened to the sun and the rain. The sun would say, "Shine!" and the lily would open up wide. The rain would say, "Drink!" and the lily would drink up the water. One day, a tiny bug came to the lily. The bug said, "I am hungry. Can I eat you?" The lily did not want to be eaten. The lily did not want to rot. So, the lily said, "No, please do not eat me. I can help you find other food." The bug was sad, but it listened to the lily. The lily and the bug looked around the garden. They found a big leaf for the bug to eat. The bug was happy and said, "Thank you, lily!" The lily was happy too because it did not rot. From that day on, the lily and the bug were good friends. They played together in the garden. The lily stayed pretty and obedient, and the bug had food to eat. And they both lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy triangle. He would make fun sounds with it all day long. One day, Tim found a big, heavy rock in his yard. He thought it would be fun to play with too. Tim tried to lift the heavy rock, but it was too hard for him. He pushed and pulled, but the rock would not move. Tim felt sad and let out a loud scream. His mom heard him and came running to see what was wrong. Mom saw Tim trying to move the heavy rock and had an idea. She took the triangle and played a fun tune. The rock began to dance and move! Tim smiled and played with his triangle and the dancing rock all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a kind teach named Miss Mary. She loved to help little kids learn. One day, Miss Mary wanted to show them how to wash their hands. She knew that some kids might think washing hands was scary, but she wanted them to see it was not. Miss Mary took the kids to the sink. She showed them how to turn on the water and use soap. The kids watched her wash her hands. They saw that it was not scary at all. They wanted to try it too. One by one, the kids took turns washing their hands. They laughed and played with the bubbles from the soap. They were not scared anymore. They knew that washing hands was fun and good. Miss Mary was happy that she could help them learn.

Lily and Tom were twins who liked to play outside. One day, they saw a big puddle of mud in the garden. They wanted to jump in it and make splashes. "Let's go, Lily!" Tom said. "It will be fun!" But Lily was not sure. She saw their new shoes that Mom had bought for them. They were white and shiny and had ribbons. Lily liked them very much. "Tom, we can't go in the mud," Lily said. "We will spoil our new shoes. Mom will be sad and angry." Tom did not listen. He ran to the puddle and sprang in it. He laughed and shouted as he made big splashes. He did not care about his shoes. Lily watched him and felt bad. She wanted to join him, but she also wanted to keep her shoes clean. She did not know what to do. Then, Mom came out of the house. She saw Tom in the mud and Lily on the grass. She was not happy. "Tom, what have you done?" Mom said. "You have spoiled your new shoes. They are all dirty and wet. How can you be so careless?" Tom stopped laughing. He looked at his shoes and felt ashamed. He wished he had listened to Lily. "I'm sorry, Mom," Tom said. "I was silly. I wanted to have fun, but I did not think about my shoes." Mom sighed. She took Tom by the hand and brought him inside. She told him to take off his shoes and put them in the sink. She said he had to wash them and dry them. She also said he could not play outside for the rest of the day. Lily followed them. She felt sorry for Tom, but she also felt proud. She had made the right choice. She had not spoiled her new shoes. She had obeyed Mom. Mom smiled at Lily. She hugged her and praised her. "Lily, you are a good girl," Mom said. "You have been careful and respectful. You have not spoiled your new shoes. You have made me happy." Lily smiled back. She was happy too. She learned that it was better to be careful and respectful than to be careless and spoiled. She hoped Tom would learn too.
