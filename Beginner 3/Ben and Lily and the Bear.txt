Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Lily and the Bear

Ben and Lily liked to play in the big place with many trees and flowers. They liked to pretend they were animals and make noises. Ben liked to be a lion and roar loud. Lily liked to be a bird and sing sweet. One day, they saw a new thing in the place. It was big and brown and had four legs and a long tail. It looked like a big dog, but it was not a dog. It was a bear. The bear saw Ben and Lily and walked towards them. Ben and Lily were scared. They did not know what the bear wanted. They ran away, but the bear was faster. Ben and Lily climbed a tree and hoped the bear would go away. But the bear did not go away. It stood under the tree and looked at them. "Roar!" Ben said to the bear. He wanted to scare the bear and make it leave. But the bear did not get scared. It roared back louder and shook the tree. "Stop, Ben!" Lily said to Ben. "You are making the bear angry. We need to be quiet and still. Maybe the bear will think we are not here." Ben and Lily stopped roaring and singing. They hugged each other and waited. The bear sniffed the tree and the air. It did not see any food or danger. It got bored and walked away. Ben and Lily were happy. They saw the bear go far away. They climbed down the tree and ran back to their home. They told their mom and dad what happened. They said they would not play in the big place with the bear anymore. They said they would play in the small place with the toys. They said they would still be a lion and a bird, but they would not roar or sing too loud. They said they learned a lesson. They said they were brave.

Once upon a time, there was a little child. The child had a gray cat. They liked to play together every day. One day, the child wanted to get a ball for the cat. They went to the store with their mom. The child saw a big, red ball. They thought the cat would love it. When they got home, the child gave the gray cat the red ball. The cat was so happy! They played together all day long with their new toy. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big eraser. One day, he saw a distant hill. He wanted to go there. Tim walked to the hill. On the way, he met a girl named Sue. Sue said, "Hi Tim! Where are you going?" Tim said, "I am going to the distant hill. I want to tell a story there." Sue said, "Can I come too?" Tim said, "Yes, let's go together!" Tim and Sue went to the distant hill. They sat down and Tim took out his eraser. He told a story about a magic eraser that could make things disappear. Sue liked the story. They both laughed and played with the eraser until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a flexible boy who liked to play and bend his body. One day, Tim went to a fun park with his mom. At the park, Tim saw a game where you strike a ball with a stick. Tim wanted to play the game, so his mom gave him a ticket. Tim took the ticket and went to the game. He said to the man, "I want to play this game, here is my ticket." The man took the ticket and gave Tim a stick. Tim was excited to play the game. Tim used the stick to strike the ball. The ball went very far because Tim was so flexible. Everyone clapped and cheered for Tim. Tim was very happy that he played the game and had a great day at the fun park with his mom.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a big gray dog playing with a ball. The dog looked very happy and healthy. Lily wanted to play with the dog, so she went to the dog's owner. Lily asked the owner, "Can I play with your dog, please?" The owner smiled and said, "Yes, you can. His name is Max." Lily and Max played together for a long time. They ran, jumped, and laughed. Lily had so much fun with her new friend. When it was time to go home, Lily gave Max a big hug and said, "Bye, Max! I hope to see you again soon!"
