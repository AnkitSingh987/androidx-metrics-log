Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Motorcycle

One day, a boy named Tim saw a big motorcycle. It was red and shiny. Tim thought it was very pretty. He wanted to touch it, but he knew he should ask first. Tim found the owner, a nice man named Bob. "Can I touch your motorcycle?" asked Tim. Bob smiled and said, "Yes, but be careful." Tim walked to the motorcycle and gently pushed it. The motorcycle moved a little. Tim felt happy. He pushed it again, but this time he pushed too hard. The motorcycle fell down with a loud noise. Tim was scared. Bob heard the noise and came running. He saw the motorcycle on the ground. Tim said, "I'm sorry, I didn't mean to push it so hard." Bob looked at Tim and then at the motorcycle. He smiled a mild smile and said, "It's okay, Tim. We can fix it together." Tim felt better, and they both worked to put the motorcycle back up.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a wise cat named Tom. Tom loved to hide and listen to music. One day, a big dog named Max came to the house. Max did not like music at all. He wanted the house to be quiet. Tom and Max did not get along. They would fight about the music. Tom wanted to listen to it, but Max wanted it to stop. One day, Tom decided to hide the music from Max. He thought this would make Max happy. But Max was not happy. He was very sad because he missed Tom. They both missed each other. In the end, the house was quiet, but Tom and Max were not friends. They both learned that sometimes, it is better to share and be kind than to fight.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his bone. One day, he found a big, rare bone in his yard. He was very happy. Spot played with the rare bone all day. He ran, jumped, and rolled with it. It made him very dirty. His fur was full of mud and dirt. Spot's mom saw him and said, "Oh no, Spot! You need a wipe!" Spot's mom took a big, wet cloth and wiped his fur. She wiped and wiped until he was clean again. Spot felt so much better. He was happy to be clean and still had his rare bone to play with. And from that day on, Spot played with his bone but always made sure to stay clean.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Tom. Tom loved to bake yummy treats for his friends. One sunny day, Tom wanted to bake a big cake for his best friend, Sam the dog. Tom said, "Sam, let's bake a cake with our hands!" Sam wagged his tail and said, "Yes, let's do it!" They put on their aprons and started to mix the cake with their hands. They had so much fun playing with the cake mix. Suddenly, the cake mix started to grow bigger and bigger. It filled the whole room! Tom and Sam were scared. They didn't know what to do. Then, they remembered to ask for help. Their friend, Lily the bird, came and helped them clean up the mess. In the end, Tom and Sam learned that it's always good to ask for help when you need it. They were very happy and thankful for their friend, Lily. And they all enjoyed a small, yummy cake together.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree. In the tree, there was a small nest. In the nest, there were two birds. One bird was big, and the other bird was small. They were friends. One day, the big bird and the small bird saw a shiny watch on the ground. Both birds wanted the watch. They did not want to share. They started to fight. Then, the big bird and the small bird decided to talk. They did not want to fight anymore. They decided to share the shiny watch. They put the watch in their nest. Now, they were happy friends again.

Once upon a time, there was a bug. It grew and itched all over. The itchy bug was very unusual. It was very small, but it was causing lots of trouble. The bug needed to be solved. A big man came to help. He tried all sorts of things to get rid of it. He tried to swat it, but he missed. He tried to cover it up, but it kept coming back. He tried to catch it, but it was too fast. The man was getting very frustrated. He decided to do something very drastic. He decided to hit the walls and ceiling, hoping that the bug would go away. But the bug didn't go away. It made a big mess. The man was very sad. He had not been able to solve the itchy bug problem. And the bug was still there, causing trouble. It was very unusual. Unfortunately, this bad story had a bad ending.
