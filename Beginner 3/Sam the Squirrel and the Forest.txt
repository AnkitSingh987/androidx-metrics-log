Category: Beginner
Title: Sam the Squirrel and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a squirrel named Sam. Sam had a soft, brown tail and loved to find nuts to eat. One day, while Sam was looking for nuts, he met a bird named Betty. Betty was blue and had a pretty song. "Hi Sam," said Betty. "Do you trust me to help you find nuts?" Sam thought for a moment and said, "Yes, Betty, I trust you." Together, they looked for nuts all day. They found big nuts, small nuts, and round nuts. Sam was very happy and thanked Betty for her help. From that day on, Sam and Betty were the best of friends, and they always helped each other find food in the big green forest.

Once upon a time, in an ordinary little house, there lived a girl named Sally. Sally loved to visit her Aunt May. Aunt May was very nice and always had fun things to do. One day, Sally went to Aunt May's house. They played with dolls and made yummy cookies. As they were eating their cookies, they heard a loud noise. "What was that?" asked Sally. Aunt May said, "I don't know, let's go see." They went outside and saw a big ball stuck in a tree. Sally tried to get the ball down, but she was too small. Aunt May had an idea. She said, "Let's slap the tree and see if the ball falls down." They both slapped the tree and the ball fell down. Sally was so happy and they played with the ball all day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little dog named Spot. Spot had a tiny flea friend named Fifi. Fifi lived on Spot's back and they played together every day. One day, Spot saw a big tree with high branches. He wanted to climb the tree and see how high he could go. Spot asked Fifi, "Do you want to climb the tree with me?" Fifi answered, "Yes, let's go!" So, Spot and Fifi started to climb the tree together. As they climbed higher and higher, Spot and Fifi saw many new things. They saw birds, squirrels, and pretty leaves. When they reached the top, they looked around and saw their small house far away. Spot and Fifi were so happy they had climbed the high tree together. After a while, they climbed back down and went home to rest. They couldn't wait to have more fun adventures together.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play outside. He wanted to act like an astronaut. He put on a big hat and wide boots. He said, "I am going to a new planet!" Tim jumped and ran around. He saw a big tree and said, "This is my planet!" He climbed the tree and looked around. He saw a wide field with many flowers. Then, Tim heard a voice. "Hello, astronaut!" It was a small green bug. The bug said, "I live on this planet, too!" Tim was surprised. He did not know bugs could talk. They became friends and played together on their wide planet.

Once upon a time, in a big field, there was a lot of wheat. The wheat was very hairy. It liked to dance when the wind blew. The wheat was happy in the sun. One day, a small bug came to the wheat field. The bug was lost and wanted to find his way home. The bug asked the hairy wheat, "Can you help me find my home?" The wheat did not know where the bug's home was, so it just shrugged. The bug was sad, but it did not give up. It asked a big tree near the field. The big tree knew where the bug's home was. The tree told the bug, "Go past the wheat and over the hill. You will find your home there." The bug thanked the tree and went on its way. Soon, the bug found its home and was happy again.

One day, a little girl named Mia wanted to play a game. She looked for her toy box, but it was empty. Mia felt sad because she wanted to play with her toys. Mia's mom saw her and had an idea. "Let's go outside and play a new game," she said. They went outside and saw a big, green field. Mia's mom said, "Let's play a game called 'spring'." In the game, Mia and her mom would jump high like a spring. They would try to touch the sky. Mia and her mom jumped and laughed. They had so much fun playing the game of spring. Mia forgot all about her empty toy box and was happy.
