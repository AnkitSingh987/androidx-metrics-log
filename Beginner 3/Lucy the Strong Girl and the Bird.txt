Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Strong Girl and the Bird

One day, a strong girl named Lucy went to play outside. She saw her friend, Tom, in the park. They played with a ball and had fun. But then, they saw a big comb on the ground. They both wanted the comb to brush their hair. "I want the comb!" said Lucy. "No, I want it!" said Tom. They both tried to grab the comb at the same time. They pulled and pulled, but the comb was very strong. It did not break. Suddenly, a little bird came and took the comb away. Lucy and Tom stopped fighting and looked at the bird. They were surprised. The bird flew to a tree and put the comb in its nest. Lucy and Tom understood that the bird needed the comb more than they did. They smiled and went back to playing with their ball. They were happy to let the bird have the comb.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big zigzag slide. Tim was very happy because the slide was available. No one else was playing on it. Tim climbed up the stairs and sat at the top of the slide. He looked down and saw his friend, Sue, at the bottom. "Hi, Sue!" Tim shouted. "I'm going to slide down now!" Sue shouted back, "Okay, Tim! I'll wait for you at the bottom!" Tim slid down the zigzag slide very fast. He laughed as he went down. At the bottom, he landed with a thud. Sue clapped her hands and laughed too. They took turns sliding down the zigzag slide. They shouted and laughed together. They had a lot of fun at the park that day.

One day, a cat named Graceful went to the theater. She was very excited to see a show. Graceful loved to dance and wanted to be in a show too. At the theater, Graceful saw a big pot. The pot was full of yummy soup. Graceful wanted to help, so she started to stir the soup. But, she was not strong enough to stir the big pot. She tried and tried, but the soup would not stir. Then, a big dog named Max came in. Max was strong and he could stir the soup. Graceful and Max worked together to stir the soup. They stirred and stirred until the soup was perfect. In the end, Graceful and Max became friends and danced together in the show. Everyone loved their dance and they all had a great time at the theater.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a microphone in his toy box. He did not know what it was. He felt helpless. Tim showed the microphone to his mom. She smiled and showed him how to use it. Tim talked into the microphone and his voice became very loud. He liked it a lot. He played with the microphone all day. He sang songs and told stories. Tim was very happy. Then, something unexpected happened. The family cat, Fluffy, jumped up and grabbed the microphone from Tim. Fluffy started to meow into the microphone. Tim was surprised! The cat was singing too. Tim and Fluffy sang songs together and had lots of fun.

Lila and Sam were best friends. They liked to play in the woods near their homes. They were very creative and made up stories and games with sticks and stones and leaves. One day, they found a creek in the woods. It was a small creek, but it had clear water and shiny fish. Lila and Sam decided to build a bridge over the creek with some big sticks. They worked hard and had fun. "Look, we made a bridge!" Lila said. "Now we can cross the creek and explore the other side." "Let's go!" Sam said. They held hands and walked over the bridge. They felt proud and happy. But when they reached the other side, they heard a loud snap. They turned around and saw that their bridge had broken. The sticks had fallen into the water and floated away. "Oh no!" Lila said. "Our bridge is gone! How will we get back?" Sam felt scared. He did not like the other side of the creek. It was dark and strange. He did not see any other way to cross the water. He started to cry. "I want to go home!" he sobbed. Lila hugged him. She felt scared too, but she wanted to be brave for Sam. She looked around and saw a big log near the creek. It was long and thick and had a smooth top. She had an idea. She wiped her tears and said, "Don't cry, Sam. I have a plan. We can use this log as a boat. We can push it into the water and sit on it. Then we can paddle with our hands and go back to our side." Sam looked at the log. He was not sure if it would work, but he trusted Lila. He nodded and said, "OK, let's try." They worked together and pushed the log into the creek. They climbed on it and sat in the middle. They used their hands as paddles and moved the log slowly across the water. They were careful not to fall off or get wet. They saw their bridge sticks floating by and waved goodbye to them. They reached their side and jumped off the log. They were happy and relieved. They hugged each other and said, "We did it! We are back!" They ran back to their homes and told their parents what happened. Their parents were glad they were safe and praised them for being creative and brave. Lila and Sam smiled and said, "We are best friends. We can do anything together."
