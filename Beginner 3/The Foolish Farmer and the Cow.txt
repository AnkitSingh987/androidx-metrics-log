Category: Beginner
Title: The Foolish Farmer and the Cow

Once upon a time, there was a foolish farmer. He lived on a farm with his cow, his pig, and his dog. The farmer loved to play with fire, but he did not know that fire can burn things. One day, the farmer said to his cow, "I want to play with fire, but I don't want to burn the farm." The cow said, "Moo! That is not a good idea. Fire can be dangerous." But the foolish farmer did not listen. He played with fire near the barn. The fire grew big and started to burn the barn. The pig, the cow, and the dog ran away from the fire. They were scared. The farmer saw the fire and said, "Oh no! I was foolish. I should have listened to the cow." The farmer learned his lesson and never played with fire again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to play with his ball. One day, he went to the park with his mom. At the park, there was a rail near the pond. Tim saw a big frog near the rail. He was frightened. He said, "Mom, I am scared of the big frog." His mom said, "It's okay, Tim. The frog won't hurt you." Tim wanted to be brave. He took his ball and threw it at the frog. The frog jumped away. Tim was not frightened anymore. He played with his ball and had a fun day at the park with his mom.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely pigeon. The pigeon had a friend, a little boy. They liked to spend time together in the park. One day, they saw a big cat. The cat wanted to play too, but the pigeon was scared. The little boy said, "Don't worry, pigeon! I will keep you safe." The pigeon felt better and they all played together. They ran, jumped, and laughed. It was fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The cat got too close to the pigeon. The pigeon tried to fly away, but the cat caught it. The little boy was sad and cried. The lovely pigeon was gone, and they could not spend time together anymore. The little boy learned to be more careful with his friends.

Lily and Tom were playing in the garden. They had a big box of toys. They liked to make up stories with their toys. Lily had a doll and Tom had a car. "Let's play a game," Lily said. "We can pretend that my doll is a princess and your car is a dragon. The dragon wants to take the princess away, but the princess is clever and has a magic powder. She can use the powder to make the dragon sleep." "OK," Tom said. "But I want to be the dragon. Dragons are cool. They can fly and breathe fire." "Fine," Lily said. "But you have to let me use the powder. It's part of the game." They started to play. Tom made his car fly around the garden, making loud noises. Lily hid her doll behind a bush, holding a small bottle of powder. She waited for the right moment. "Roar!" Tom said. "I see you, princess. You can't hide from me. I'm going to take you to my cave." He moved his car closer to the bush. Lily opened the bottle and threw some powder at the car. "Shh!" Lily said. "You have to pretend that the powder makes you sleep. It's a magic powder." Tom pretended that his car fell to the ground, snoring. Lily smiled and picked up her doll. "Ha!" Lily said. "I told you I was clever. I saved myself from the dragon. Now I can go back to my castle." She ran to the other side of the garden, where she had a cardboard box that looked like a castle. Tom opened his eyes and saw her. "Hey!" Tom said. "That's not fair. You can't just leave me here. Wake me up!" Lily laughed and ran back to him. She sprinkled some more powder on his car. "OK, OK," Lily said. "You can wake up now. But you have to promise to be nice to me. And you have to include me in your games." "OK, OK," Tom said. "I promise. You are a clever princess. And I am a nice dragon. Can we be friends?" "Yes, we can be friends," Lily said. "And we can play more games together. But only if you share your toys with me." They hugged and smiled. They put their toys back in the box and went inside for a snack. They had a lot of fun playing in the garden.
