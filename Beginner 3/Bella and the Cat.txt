Category: Beginner
Title: Bella and the Cat

Bella was a big dog with brown fur and a long tail. She loved to play with her ball and run in the park. She also loved to help her family and protect them from danger. One day, Bella saw a small cat on the road. The cat was black and white and had green eyes. It looked adorable, but it was in trouble. A big car was coming fast and the cat did not see it. Bella knew she had to prevent the cat from getting hurt. Bella ran to the road and barked loud. The car stopped and the driver saw Bella and the cat. He got out and picked up the cat. He thanked Bella for saving the cat and gave her a treat. He said he would take the cat to his home and give it a name. Bella was happy and wagged her tail. She licked the cat's paw and said goodbye. The cat purred and rubbed its head on Bella's nose. They became friends and waved as the car drove away. Bella went back to her family and they hugged her. They said she was a brave and good dog. They gave her more treats and played with her ball. Bella felt proud and loved. She had a great day.

Lily and Tom were friends. They liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big pond with ducks and fish. Lily and Tom wanted to feed the ducks. They had some oats in a bag. "Let's give some oats to the ducks," Lily said. "They must be hungry." "OK," Tom said. "But be careful. The pond is deep and wet." Lily and Tom walked to the edge of the pond. They threw some oats to the ducks. The ducks came closer and quacked. Lily and Tom smiled. They liked the ducks. But then, they saw something else in the pond. It was big and hairy. It had sharp teeth and claws. It was a bear. The bear smelled the oats. It wanted to eat them. It saw Lily and Tom. It wanted to eat them too. The bear jumped out of the pond. It splashed water everywhere. It ran towards Lily and Tom. It roared loudly. "Run!" Tom shouted. "It's a bear!" Lily and Tom were scared. They tried to run away. But the bear was faster. It caught them. It bit them. It hurt them. Lily and Tom cried. They could not escape. The bear ate them. The ducks flew away. The pond was quiet. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to crawl and play with his toys. One day, Tim found a big, sour lemon under the table. He picked it up and thought it was a fun ball. Tim crawled to his mom and showed her the sour lemon. His mom told him that it was not a ball, but a lemon. She said, "Tim, the lemon is sour, not for play. If you want to have fun, share your toys with others." Tim listened to his mom and went to find his friends. He shared his toys and they all played together. They had a lot of fun, and Tim learned that sharing is better than playing alone. The moral of the story is to share with others and have fun together.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a very enthusiastic dog named Max. Max loved to walk and play with his friends. Every day, Max would wake up, eat his food, and go for a walk around the village. One sunny day, Max went for a walk with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. They walked through the village and saw many fun things. They saw a big tree, a pretty flower, and a fast car. Max and Lily were very happy to walk together. At the end of their walk, Max and Lily found a big park. They played with a ball and ran around. They were very tired but had a lot of fun. Max and Lily walked back to their homes in the village, excited for their next adventure together.
