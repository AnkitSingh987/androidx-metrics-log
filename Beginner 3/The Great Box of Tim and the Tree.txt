Category: Beginner
Title: The Great Box of Tim and the Tree

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Max, went to play outside. It was an icy cold day, but they did not care. They liked to play in the snow. They made a big snowman and had a fun snowball fight. Suddenly, they heard a loud sound. "What was that?" Tim asked Max. Max barked and ran towards the sound. Tim followed Max, and they found a small box on the ground. Tim opened the box and found a toy gun inside. "Wow, let's play with this!" Tim said. Tim and Max played with the toy gun. Tim would shoot it, and Max would chase the toy bullets. They were having so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. As Tim shot the toy gun, a real bullet came out instead of a toy one. The bullet hit a tree, and the tree fell down with a loud crash. Tim and Max were scared, and they ran home. They never played with the toy gun again.

Once upon a time, there was a huge, round ball. It was so big and it had lots of light. The ball was in the sky, and it was called the Sun. One day, a little car wanted to see the Sun up close. The car started to speed on a long road. It went fast and faster, trying to reach the Sun. The car was so small, but it was very brave. As the car went fast, it saw many things. It saw a big tree, a little dog, and a pretty flower. The car could not reach the Sun, but it had a fun day. The Sun saw the car and smiled. It was a happy day for everyone.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to go to the store and buy treats for herself. Today she was at the store buying tea. When Lucy was about to pay for the tea, she was surprised to see her friend, Jane, standing right next to her. "Hi Lucy!" said Jane. "What are you buying there?". Lucy proudly showed her the tea, "Look! I'm buying my own tea". "Wow! That's so selfish", said Jane. Lucy was a little sad and confused. "But why is it selfish?" she asked. "Well", Jane said, "It's not nice to buy things for yourself all the time. It's better to share things with others and make them happy too". Lucy felt wise beyond her years when she remembered Jane's words. She now knew what it meant to be kind and share with others. The moral of the story is that it's better to share and make others happy, than to be selfish and buy things only for yourself.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was anxious about going to a big party. Her mom said, "Don't worry, you'll have fun!" At the party, Lily saw a big table with yummy food. She took a napkin and put some food on it. Then, she saw her friend Tim. "Hi Tim!" said Lily. "Hi Lily!" said Tim. They ate and talked together. Soon, Lily saw a big dog run into the room. The dog was going to eat the food on the table! Lily warned the dog, "No, dog! Don't eat the food!" The dog listened and went away. Everyone at the party was happy. Lily was not anxious anymore.

Lily and Ben were twins who liked to go to the market with their mom. They saw many things to buy, like fruits, toys, and books. But their mom said they could only buy one thing each. Lily saw a sack of shiny stones. She liked how they sparkled in the sun. She asked her mom if she could buy the sack. Her mom said yes, because the stones were not too expensive and they were rare. Lily was happy and hugged the sack. Ben saw a sack of soft balls. He liked how they bounced and rolled. He asked his mom if he could buy the sack. Her mom said yes, because the balls were not too big and they were rare. Ben was happy and hugged the sack. Lily and Ben thanked their mom and went home with their sacks. They played with their stones and balls in the yard. They shared them with each other and their friends. They had fun with their rare sacks.
