Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Grumpy Bird and the Fly

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy bird named Bob. Bob did not like to fly. He liked to sit on the stove in the warm kitchen all day. He was not happy when he had to leave his stove. One day, a nice girl named Sue came to the kitchen. She saw Bob on the stove. Sue said, "Bob, you need to fly and play outside with the other birds." Bob did not want to fly. He was grumpy. But Sue was kind and helped Bob learn to fly. They went outside and played with the other birds. Bob was not grumpy anymore. He was happy to fly and play with his new friends. And after playing, Bob could still sit on his stove in the warm kitchen.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. It was a hot day, so they put on their hats and took some water with them. They walked down the street and saw a big sign. The sign had a picture of a park on it. Tim and his mom decided to go to the park. When they got to the park, Tim saw a big tree. He liked the tree and wanted to admire it. He sat under the tree and looked up at the leaves. The leaves were green and pretty. Tim's mom sat next to him and they both admired the tree. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise. They looked up and saw a big bird flying out of the tree. The bird had been hiding in the tree all along! Tim and his mom were surprised. They laughed and clapped their hands. The big bird flew away and they continued to admire the tree. The unexpected bird made their day at the park even more fun.

Once upon a time, in a big jungle, there lived a happy monkey and a friendly lion. They were best friends and liked to play together. The jungle was full of big trees, pretty flowers, and many animals. One day, the monkey and the lion found a big fruit tree. The monkey said, "I want to climb up and get the fruit. It is easy for me." The lion said, "No, I want to shake the tree and make the fruit fall. It is easy for me." They did not agree on what to do. After a little time, the monkey and the lion decided to try both ways. First, the monkey climbed up the tree and got some fruit. Then, the lion shook the tree and more fruit fell down. They both laughed and enjoyed the fruit together. They learned that it was okay to disagree and still be friends. And they lived happily in the big jungle.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a rich man named Tom. He had a big house and many toys. One day, Tom saw a trail in the woods. He thought it would be fun to walk on it. As Tom walked, he met a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she lost her ball. Tom said, "I will help you find your ball." They walked and talked, looking for the ball. Finally, they found the ball near a big tree. Sue was very happy. Tom saw a big hole in the trail. He put a big rock there to prevent people from falling. They both walked back home, happy and safe. The moral of the story is: Helping others can make you happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy bird. The bird had many beautiful feathers. One day, the bird left his nest to find food. While the bird was away, a little mouse found the nest. The mouse saw a pretty feather. The mouse took the feather to his home. The mouse was happy. When the bird came back, he saw the feather was gone. The bird was sad. Then, the bird saw the happy mouse with the feather. The bird and the mouse became friends. Now, they share the pretty feather and play together.

Tom and Lily liked to play soccer in the park. They had a ball and two cones for the goal. They took turns to kick the ball and try to score. One day, a big dog came running to the park. It saw the ball and wanted to play too. It ran after the ball and grabbed it with its teeth. Tom and Lily were scared of the dog. They did not know if it was friendly or not. "Hey, give us back our ball!" Tom shouted at the dog. But the dog did not listen. It ran away with the ball, wagging its tail. "Come on, let's chase it!" Lily said. She was alert and brave. She followed the dog, and Tom followed her. They ran across the park, dodging other people and trees. The dog was fast, but they did not give up. They saw the dog go into a house and shut the door behind it. "Oh no, now what do we do?" Tom asked. He was sad and tired. "Maybe we can knock on the door and ask for our ball," Lily suggested. She was hopeful and smart. They walked to the door and knocked gently. A lady opened the door and smiled at them. "Hello, kids. What can I do for you?" she asked. "Please, can we have our ball back? Your dog took it from us," Lily said politely. "Oh, I'm sorry. My dog loves to play with balls. He didn't mean to take yours. Come in, I'll get it for you," the lady said kindly. She invited them in and gave them their ball. She also gave them some cookies and juice. The dog was friendly and licked their faces. Tom and Lily thanked the lady and the dog. They were happy and grateful. They went back to the park and continued to play soccer. They had fun and scored many goals. They learned that sometimes, things can go wrong, but they can also go right. They also learned that dogs can be good friends. They had a good day.
