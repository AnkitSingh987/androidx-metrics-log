Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Flowers

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a green lizard outside. The lizard looked gloomy because it was lost. Tim wanted to help the lizard, so he decided to follow it. Tim and the lizard walked and walked. They saw trees, flowers, and birds. The lizard started to feel happy. Tim said, "Don't worry, lizard! We will find your home!" After a while, they found a big rock. The lizard jumped on it and smiled. "This is my home! Thank you, Tim!" said the lizard. Tim was happy he could help. They waved goodbye and Tim went back to his house.

One day, a little girl named Lily put on her pink dress. She wanted to help her mom make coffee. Lily's mom said, "Be careful, the coffee is hot." Lily took a step and slip! She fell on the floor. "Oh no!" she said. Her mom helped her up and asked, "Are you okay?" Lily nodded her head. Together, they made the coffee. Lily was happy to help her mom. They sat down and enjoyed their coffee. Lily felt proud and loved her pink dress.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a bald man named Tom. Tom had a dog named Max. Max loved to play with his bone. One day, Max lost his bone in the yard. Tom said, "Max, let's dress up and go find your bone." Max wagged his tail and they put on their coats. They went outside to look for the bone together. They looked and looked, but they could not find the bone. Max was sad. Then, a little bird saw them and said, "I saw your bone by the big tree!" Tom and Max went to the big tree and found the bone. Max was very happy and thanked the little bird. They all played together and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a small cat named Kitty. Kitty liked to play with her dish. One day, Kitty saw a big dog in her yard. The big dog wanted to play with Kitty's dish too. Kitty did not like this. Kitty said, "No, big dog! This is my dish. You can't play with it." The big dog did not listen. He tried to take the dish from Kitty. Kitty was sad and scared. Kitty tried to get her dish back. She ran fast and jumped at the dish. The big dog stumbled and let go of the dish. Kitty took her dish back and ran away. The big dog learned to not take things from others. Kitty was happy to have her dish back.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was a very spoiled girl who always wanted her way. One day, she met a person named Tom. Tom was a kind person who liked to help others. Lily said, "Tom, I want to play with your toys." Tom replied, "Okay, but we need to share and play nice." Lily didn't like to share, but she wanted to play with Tom's toys, so she agreed. They played with the toys for a while, but Lily started to get bossy. She took all the toys and locked them in a box. Tom was sad and said, "Lily, that's not nice. We need to share." Lily thought about it and realized that Tom was right. She unlocked the box and they shared the toys. They played nicely together and became good friends.

There was a dove. It was very comfortable in the tree. The dove liked to hang from the branch. Every day it would hang upside down and close its eyes. One day, a little girl walked by the tree. She was 3 years old and she saw the dove hanging. She was very excited. She put her hand out and tried to touch it. The dove didn't mind; it felt comfortable with the little girl. The girl smiled. She said hello to the dove and the dove tilted it's head. She touched it very gently and then started to climb the tree. Soon, the little girl and the dove were both hanging from the branch. The dove was happy and the little girl was smiling. They stayed there together until the sun went down. Then they both flew away, up into the sky.
