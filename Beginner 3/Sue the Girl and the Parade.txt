Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Parade

One day, a little girl named Sue was very excited. She was going to see a parade with her mom and dad. She had never been to a parade before. Sue felt proud to go with her family. As they walked to the parade, Sue saw her friend, Tim. "Hi, Tim!" Sue said. "Are you going to the parade too?" Tim nodded and smiled. "Yes, I am going with my mom and dad too!" They were both very happy. At the parade, Sue and Tim saw big floats and people in fun costumes. They heard loud music and saw people dancing. Sue had a question. She asked her mom, "Why are the people so happy?" Her mom said, "Because they are proud to be in the parade and share their joy with everyone." Sue and Tim clapped and cheered as they watched the parade together.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim had a glove. The glove was rough and old. Tim loved his glove very much. He played with it every day. One day, Tim's mom said they were going to celebrate his dad's birthday. Tim was very happy. He wanted to play catch with his dad at the party. So, he took his rough glove with him. At the party, Tim and his dad played catch. Tim threw the ball and his dad caught it with the rough glove. But then, the glove broke! Tim was sad and his dad said, "Oh no, my glove is broken!" They could not play catch anymore. The party was not fun after that.

One day, a small boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a big hole in the ground, which was a pit. Tim thought the pit was attractive because it was so big and round. He wanted to play near the pit. While Tim was playing, he saw his friend, Sue, walking towards him. Sue also thought the pit was attractive. They both stood near the pit and looked at it. They were very happy to play together. Suddenly, Tim's mom saw them playing near the pit. She was worried that they might fall in. So, she shouted, "Tim, Sue, come away from the pit!" Tim and Sue heard her shout and moved away from the pit. They went to play in the park instead, and they were still very happy.

One day, a black cat went for a walk. The cat saw a dry creek. The cat wanted to play near the creek. The cat jumped over the dry creek. It found a big stick. The cat played with the stick and had fun. At the end of the day, the black cat went home. It was happy it found the dry creek and the big stick. The cat slept well that night.

Anna and Ben were playing in the kitchen. They liked to pretend they were chefs and make soup with water and toys. Anna turned on the faucet and filled a big pot with water. She added some blocks, a spoon and a doll. "This is chicken soup," she said. "Do you want some, Ben?" Ben nodded and took a bowl. He poured some water from the pot into his bowl. But he poured too much and the water spilled on the floor. Anna saw the mess and got angry. "Ben, you are so silly!" she shouted. "You made the floor wet and dirty. Now Mom will be mad at us. I don't want to play with you anymore." Ben felt sad and sorry. He did not mean to spill the water. He wanted to make Anna happy. He looked at Anna and said, "I'm sorry, Anna. I didn't do it on purpose. Please don't be mad at me. Can we still be friends?" Anna looked at Ben and saw his sad face. She remembered that Ben was her best friend and that he always shared his toys with her. She also remembered that Mom always told her to forgive people who say sorry. She thought that Ben was smart and kind. She smiled and said, "It's okay, Ben. I forgive you. We can still be friends. Let's clean up the floor together and then we can have some real soup. Mom will be proud of us." Anna and Ben hugged and then got some towels. They wiped the floor and put the toys back in the box. They turned off the faucet and washed their hands. They went to the living room and found Mom. She had made some real chicken soup for them. She gave them each a bowl and a spoon. She said, "You are such good kids. I love you both. Enjoy your soup." Anna and Ben smiled and said, "We love you too, Mom. Thank you for the soup. It's delicious." They ate their soup and talked about their game. They were happy and glad that they forgave each other. They learned that friends sometimes make mistakes, but they can always say sorry and make up. They also learned that water is fun, but they have to be careful with the faucet. They decided to play in the kitchen again, but next time they would use less water and more toys.
