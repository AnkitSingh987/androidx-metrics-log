Category: Beginner
Title: The Vendor and the Toys

One day, a vendor went to the park to sell his toys. He had many colorful toys for kids to play with. The vendor was happy and smiled at everyone. He did not know much, but he was good at selling toys. A little girl named Lucy saw the vendor and wanted a toy. She went to the vendor and said, "Can I have a toy, please?" The vendor said, "Of course, pick any toy you like!" Lucy picked a pretty doll and was very happy. Just then, a big wind came and blew all the toys away. The vendor was sad and didn't know what to do. But Lucy had an idea. She said, "Let's leave and find the toys together!" The vendor and Lucy went on a fun adventure to find the toys. In the end, they found all the toys and the vendor was happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a clean jet. This jet was very special because it could lift things. It loved to help people and make them happy. One day, the jet saw a little girl with a big box. The box was too heavy for her to lift. The jet wanted to help, so it used its special lift power. The box went up, up, up in the air. The little girl was so happy and said, "Thank you, clean jet!" The clean jet flew away, looking for more people to help. It saw a boy with a broken toy car. The jet used its lift power to pick up the toy car. It took the car to a man who could fix it. The boy was so happy and said, "Thank you, clean jet!" The clean jet smiled and flew away, ready to help more people.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily liked to play in her room with her toys. She had a shelf where she put all her favorite toys. One day, she noticed that her favorite toy, a teddy bear, was missing from the shelf. Lily felt upset. Lily asked her mom, "Mom, where is my teddy bear? It's not on the shelf." Her mom said, "I don't know, Lily. Maybe he is hiding. Let's look for him together." So, they started to look for the teddy bear. They looked under the bed, behind the door, and in the toy box. Finally, they found the teddy bear in the closet. Lily was so happy! She hugged her teddy bear and put him back on the shelf. "I'm glad we found you," she said to her teddy bear. Now, Lily always makes sure her toys are on the shelf so they don't get lost again.

Tom and Lily are twins. They like to play with their toys. Tom has a big truck and Lily has a doll. They are very organized. They keep their toys in different boxes. One day, Tom wants to play with Lily's doll. He says, "Lily, can I have your doll? I want to trade. You can have my truck." Lily says, "No, Tom. I like my doll. She is pretty and soft. You can't have her. Go play with your truck." Tom gets angry. He grabs Lily's doll and runs away. Lily screams, "Tom, give me back my doll! That's not fair! You are mean!" Lily chases Tom around the house. They knock over the boxes and make a big mess. Their toys are everywhere. Mom hears the noise and comes to see what is wrong. She sees Tom and Lily fighting and the toys on the floor. She is very upset. She says, "Tom and Lily, stop it right now! What are you doing? You are not sharing. You are not organized. You are making a big mess. You are in trouble!" She takes away their toys and puts them in a closet. She says, "You can't play with your toys until you say sorry and clean up. Go to your rooms and think about what you did." Tom and Lily are sad and angry. They go to their rooms and cry. They don't have their toys. They don't have each other. They have a bad day.
