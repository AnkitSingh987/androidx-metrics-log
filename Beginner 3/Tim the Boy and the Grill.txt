Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Grill

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a light blue grill near the trees. Tim said, "Mom, can we cook on the grill?" His mom said, "Yes, I think we can cook on the grill. We need to go home and get food first." They went home and got food. They brought back hot dogs and buns. Tim and his mom put the hot dogs on the grill. They waited for the hot dogs to cook. Tim's friend, Sam, came to the park too. Sam asked, "Can I have a hot dog too?" Tim said, "Yes, you can have one too." They all ate hot dogs and had fun at the park. They liked the light blue grill and said they would come back to cook on it again.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He had a toy microscope. He loved to look at tiny things with it. One day, he found a dirty rock. Tim wanted to see the rock up close with his microscope. His big sister, Sue, was playing with her blocks. She saw Tim with his microscope and the dirty rock. Sue did not like dirty things. She wanted the dirty rock away from her blocks. Sue took the dirty rock from Tim. She threw it far away. Tim was sad. His microscope was now empty. He wanted to destroy Sue's blocks. But he knew that was not nice. So, Tim went outside to find another rock. He found a shiny rock and looked at it with his microscope. Tim was happy again, and Sue kept her blocks clean.

Once upon a time, there was a man with a big mustache. He lived in a safe, little house. He had a dog named Spot. One day, the man and Spot went for a walk. They saw a big tree with a red ball stuck in it. Spot wanted to play with the ball, so the man tried to help. He said, "I will pull it down for you, Spot." The man jumped and pulled on a branch. The red ball fell down. Spot was happy and wagged his tail. They played with the red ball all day long. At night, they went back to their safe, little house. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a strong girl named Amy. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Amy loved to play outside and help her mom and dad with things. One day, Amy saw a big box near her house. The box was closed. She wanted to see what was inside, so she asked her mom, "Can I open the box?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Amy was very strong, so she opened the box easily. Inside the box, there was a cute puppy. The puppy asked, "Can I play with you?" Amy was happy to answer, "Yes, let's play together!" They played all day and had lots of fun. From that day on, Amy and the puppy became best friends.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk in the busy park. There were many kids playing and having fun. Lily liked to watch them and sometimes join them too. While she was walking, she saw something on the ground. Lily bent down to pick it up. It was a mask! It was a very pretty mask with lots of colors. Lily wanted to find the person who lost the mask. Lily walked around the park, asking all the kids if the mask was theirs. But no one knew who it belonged to. Lily felt sad because she wanted to help. Then, she saw a little boy sitting by himself, looking sad too. Lily went to him and asked, "Is this your mask?" The little boy's face lit up with a big smile. "Yes, it is! Thank you for finding it!" he said. Lily felt very happy that she could help the little boy. They both put on their masks and played together for the rest of the day in the busy park.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Jack and Jill. One day, it was raining outside so they decided to stay in and play inside. Jack wanted to play with his toy car but Jill wanted to take a nap. Jack said to Jill, "Let's play with my car. We can go really fast outside when it stops raining." But Jill said, "No! I'm too sleepy. I want to take a nap." Jack was disappointed but he knew that he couldn't make Jill do what he wanted. So he said, "OK, but when you get up, I need you to help me with something. It'll take both of us to do it." Jill agreed and went off to take a nap. While she was sleeping, Jack prepared a surprise for her. He put a napkin on the floor and put a few of Jill's most favourite things on it. When Jill woke up, she was very surprised to see what Jack had done for her. She thanked him for the surprise and the two friends went off to play together outside.
