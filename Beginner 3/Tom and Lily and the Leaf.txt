Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Leaf

Tom and Lily were playing in the garden. They liked to dig in the dirt and look for bugs and worms. Sometimes they found shiny stones or pretty flowers. One day, they saw something moving under a big leaf. They were curious and wanted to see what it was. They lifted the leaf very slowly and carefully. They saw a long, thin animal with scales and no legs. It was a snake! It was orange and black and had a forked tongue. It hissed at them and showed its sharp teeth. Tom and Lily were scared and dropped the leaf. They ran back to the house and told their mom what they had seen. She said they had to be careful and not touch snakes, because some of them could hurt them. She gave them some cookies and milk and hugged them. She said they were brave and smart to run away from the snake. She said she would reveal a surprise for them later. They wondered what it could be. Maybe a new toy or a book or a game. They felt happy and safe with their mom.

Once upon a time, there was a grill. The grill was very reliable. It could do many things. It lived in a big yard with a family. One day, the family wanted to have a party. They asked the grill to help. The grill said, "Yes, I can do it!" The grill cooked food for everyone at the party. The food was very good. At the end of the day, the family was very happy. They thanked the grill for all the help. The grill was very proud to be a part of the family. And they all lived happily ever after.

Ben and Lily are friends. They like to play with toys and eat pizza. One day, they go to a pizza place with their moms. The pizza place is very loud. There are many people and cars and noises. Ben and Lily sit at a table and wait for their pizza. They see a big pizza with cheese and pepperoni. It smells good. They want to eat it. "Can we have some pizza, mom?" Ben asks. "Yes, but be careful. It is hot," his mom says. She cuts a slice for Ben and puts it on his plate. "Thank you, mom," Ben says. He blows on his pizza to cool it down. Lily looks at Ben's pizza. She likes cheese and pepperoni too. She wants a slice. "Can I have some pizza, mom?" Lily asks. "Of course, sweetie. Here you go," her mom says. She cuts a slice for Lily and passes it to her. "Thank you, mom," Lily says. She blows on her pizza to cool it down. Ben and Lily smile at each other. They are happy. They have pizza and they have friends. They take a bite of their pizza. It is yummy. They eat their pizza and talk and laugh. They have fun at the pizza place. They are not afraid of the loud noises. They are brave and hungry. They love pizza.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to cook. He asked his mom, "Can I cook with you?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim! Let's cook together." First, they went to the faucet to wash their hands. Tim turned the faucet on and water came out. They washed their hands with soap and water. Then, they went to the kitchen to start cooking. Tim and his mom were successful at cooking a yummy meal. They made a big pot of soup. Tim said, "I like cooking with you, Mom!" His mom was happy and said, "I like cooking with you too, Tim!" They ate the soup together and it was very good. Tim was proud that he helped cook the soup. From that day on, Tim loved to cook with his mom.

One day, Tim was playing outside. It was a cold day, but he was having fun. He saw a small bird on the ground. The bird looked cold and wet. Tim wanted to help the bird. "Hello, bird," Tim said. "You look cold. I will help you get dry." Tim took off his warm hat and wrapped it around the bird. He held the bird in his hands, rubbing it gently with his thumb to help it get dry. After a while, the bird was dry and warm. It looked at Tim and said, "Thank you, Tim. You are a good friend." The bird flew away, happy and warm. Tim felt happy too, knowing he had helped a new friend.
