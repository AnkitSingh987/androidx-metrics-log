Category: Beginner
Title: The Unique Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a unique dog. This dog had a big, red ball. One day, the dog wanted to play with the ball. So, the dog went to the park with the ball. At the park, the dog saw a big box. The dog was curious. The dog wanted to open the box. When the dog opened the box, a cat jumped out! The dog was very surprised. The cat wanted to play with the dog and the ball. The dog and the cat played together. They had a lot of fun. The dog and the cat became best friends. And they played at the park every day.

Once upon a time, in a dark, dark room, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was scared to sleep at night. He had a big pole by his bed to help him feel safe. But Tim was still scared. One night, Tim heard a noise. He thought it was a monster. Tim hid under his bed, holding his pole. He was very scared. The noise got louder and louder. Tim held his pole tighter and tighter. Then, a tiny mouse came into the room. The mouse was what made the noise. Tim saw the mouse and laughed. He was not scared anymore. Tim went back to bed with his pole and fell fast asleep.

Jimmy was feeling very cold. He put on lots of layers of clothes, but he still was not happy. Suddenly, Jimmy had a great idea. He would go to the barber and get a new hairstyle. Jimmy walked to the barber's shop. The barber was friendly. He said, "What can I do for you today?" Jimmy said, "I want to change my look." The barber said, "Ok, let's take a look at what we can do." The barber trimmed Jimmy's hair and combed it differently. When he was done, Jimmy smiled. He liked his new look and he was no longer cold. Jimmy thanked the barber and left. He was happy that he changed his look!

Once upon a time, there was a silly cat named Tom. Tom liked to play with his toy shield all day. He would run and jump around with the shield in his mouth. One day, Tom's shield got very dirty. His mom said, "Tom, you need to bathe your shield to make it clean!" So, Tom took his shield to the water and started to wash it. He splashed and scrubbed until the shield was shiny and clean. When Tom was done, he felt very happy. He showed his clean shield to his mom and she was proud of him. From that day on, Tom always made sure to keep his shield clean and shiny. And they lived happily ever after.

Once there was a small dog named Spot. Spot had a new toy. He loved to play with his new toy all day. One day, Spot felt a little shiver. He did not know what it was. He went to his mom and dad. Spot told his mom and dad about the shiver. They said, "Maybe it will happen again. Just wait and see." Spot went to play with his toy again. He still felt happy but also a little scared. The next day, Spot felt the shiver again. This time it was a big shiver. It was an earthquake! Spot's mom and dad came to him. They were all safe. Spot was happy he told his mom and dad about the shiver. They were ready for the earthquake.

Jenny and Jack were twins who shared a crib. They liked to play together, but sometimes they fought over toys and blankets. One day, their mom said, "You are too big for one crib. You need to separate and sleep in your own beds." She showed them two new beds in their room, one with pink sheets and one with blue sheets. Jenny and Jack did not like this idea. They wanted to stay in their crib. They cried and said, "No, no, no! We want our crib!" Their mom said, "Sorry, but you have to try. You will like your new beds, I promise. They are cozy and soft. And you can still see each other and talk to each other." She kissed them and tucked them in their new beds. But as soon as their mom left, Jenny and Jack got out of their beds and climbed back into their crib. They hugged each other and said, "We like our crib. It is ours. We don't want to separate." They fell asleep in their crib. The next morning, their mom came to wake them up. She was surprised to see them in their crib. She said, "Oh, you two are so persistent! You really love your crib, don't you?" She smiled and said, "Okay, okay. You can keep your crib for a little while longer. But you have to share it nicely. No fighting, okay?" Jenny and Jack nodded and said, "Okay, mom. We will share. We love you." They hugged their mom and thanked her for letting them keep their crib. They were happy.
