Category: Beginner
Title: Blue the Gentle Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a gentle bird named Blue. Blue lived in a tree near a big rock. One day, Blue wanted to find a new home. So, Blue flew around to find a place that would fit. Blue saw a hole in the rock. "Maybe I can fit in there," Blue said. Blue tried to go in the hole, but it was too small. Blue did not fit. Next, Blue saw a bush near the rock. Blue thought, "This bush looks nice and gentle." Blue tried to fit in the bush, but it was too big. Blue did not fit. Finally, Blue saw a small tree near the rock. Blue said, "This tree looks just right." Blue tried the tree and it was a perfect fit. Blue was happy and said, "I found my new home!" Blue lived in the small tree by the rock, happy and safe.

One day, a little girl named Sue was playing with her toy cat, Kitty. Sue loved Kitty very much. They played all day in the sun. Sue said, "Kitty, let's pray for a tasty treat today!" Kitty did not say anything, but Sue knew Kitty wanted a tasty treat too. Sue and Kitty went inside to take a shower. They were very dirty from playing outside. While they were in the shower, Sue's mom heard her pray for a tasty treat. She thought it was a great idea. So, she went to the kitchen to make something special for Sue and Kitty. After the shower, Sue and Kitty went to the kitchen. They found a big plate of cookies on the table. Sue was so happy and said, "Kitty, our prayer worked! We got our tasty treat!" But then, something unexpected happened. Kitty came to life and said, "Thank you, Sue, for praying for us! Now, we can enjoy these tasty treats together!" Sue and Kitty ate the cookies and played even more, now as best friends forever.

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They saw a big truck with many boxes. They wanted to see what was inside the boxes. They climbed on the truck and opened a box. They found a gun. It was black and shiny. Tom picked up the gun. It was heavy. He wanted to weigh it. "Look, Lily, I found a gun!" Tom said. "It is very heavy. How much do you think it weighs?" Lily was frightened. She knew guns were bad. They could hurt people. She wanted to go away. "Tom, put down the gun!" Lily said. "Guns are not toys. They are dangerous. We should not touch them. Let's go back to the swings." But Tom did not listen. He liked the gun. He wanted to play with it. He pointed it at a bird. He pulled the trigger. BANG! The gun made a loud noise. The bird flew away. Tom and Lily were scared. They dropped the gun. They ran away from the truck. A man came out of the truck. He heard the noise. He saw the gun on the ground. He was angry. He shouted at Tom and Lily. "Hey, you kids! What are you doing with my gun? Get away from my truck! I'm calling the police!" Tom and Lily did not stop. They ran to their mom. They told her what happened. She was worried. She hugged them. She said they were very naughty. She said they should never touch guns again. She said guns could hurt them and others. She said they were lucky nothing bad happened. Tom and Lily were sorry. They said they were sorry. They learned their lesson. They never played with guns again.

Once upon a time, there was a mysterious big cat in the woods. The big cat liked to play and run around. One day, the big cat saw a small mouse and wanted to catch it. The mouse was very scared and tried to escape. It ran and ran, but the big cat was fast too. The mouse saw a hole in a tree and ran inside. The big cat could not fit in the hole, so it sat outside and waited. Inside the tree, the mouse met a wise old owl. The mouse told the owl about the big cat and asked for help. The owl said, "Let's pray that the big cat goes away." So, the mouse and the owl prayed together. Outside, the big cat heard them and said, "I will go away if you give me some food." The mouse and the owl thought this was a good idea. They gave the big cat some food, and the big cat promised to leave them alone. But the big cat did not keep its promise. When the mouse came out of the hole, the big cat caught it. The owl was very sad and learned that the mysterious big cat could not be trusted. The mouse did not escape and the big cat went back into the woods, looking for more friends to trick.
