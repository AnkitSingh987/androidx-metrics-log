Category: Beginner
Title: The Hairy Dog and the Ball

One day, a hairy dog went for a walk. The dog liked to look for things. He saw a big tree and wanted to know what was behind it. He went around the tree and found a small ball. The dog was full of wonder. The dog played with the ball. He kicked it, and it rolled far away. He ran to get the ball, but it went into the woods. The dog was scared, but he wanted his ball back. He had to be brave and remain strong. The dog went into the woods and found the ball. He was so happy! He took the ball and ran back home. The dog showed his new toy to his family. They all played with the ball and had a fun day. The hairy dog was proud of his find and was no longer scared of the woods.

Once upon a time, there was a big parade in a small town. Many people came to watch and have fun. They saw floats, balloons, and bands playing music. Everyone was happy and excited. In the parade, there was an honest clown named Bobo. Bobo was very funny and made everyone laugh. He had a red nose and big shoes. But one day, something unexpected happened. A big balloon got loose and started to float away. Bobo saw the balloon and began to panic. He ran after the balloon, but it was too fast. He tried to jump and grab it, but he fell down. The people watching the parade were not laughing anymore. They were sad for Bobo. In the end, the balloon floated away, and Bobo was very sad. The parade was not fun anymore. The people went home, and Bobo learned that sometimes, things do not go as planned.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a phone on the floor. He picked it up and wanted to find the owner. Tim saw a girl named Sally. She looked sad and sorry. He thought maybe the phone was hers. Tim decided to follow Sally to ask if the phone was hers. But Sally did not see Tim and walked away fast. Tim tried to catch up but was too slow. He felt sad because he could not give the phone back. Finally, Tim saw Sally stop and sit on a bench. He walked up to her and showed her the phone. Sally smiled and said, "Yes, that is my phone! Thank you, Tim!" Tim was happy he could help Sally and they became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had long hair and needed a haircut. One day, his mom took him to the barber shop to get a haircut. When they got to the barber shop, Tim saw a big chair. He sat in the chair and the barber used scissors to cut his hair. Tim's hair went "zoom" as it fell to the ground. Tim liked the sound of "zoom" and started to laugh. After the haircut, Tim and his mom went to get ice cream. Tim got a yummy ice cream cone. He was very happy with his new haircut and his yummy ice cream. They went home and Tim showed his dad his new haircut. Everyone was happy.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing. The cat liked to tease the dog. The dog did not like it. They saw a prize on a high shelf. The prize was a big, red ball. They both wanted the ball. The cat said, "I can get the ball easy. You can't jump high." The dog was sad but did not give up. He tried to jump high to get the ball. The cat laughed at him. The dog had an idea. He pushed a chair near the shelf. He jumped on the chair and got the prize. The cat was surprised. The dog said, "I got the ball. Let's play together and not tease each other." They played with the ball and became good friends.

Tom and Lily were best friends. They liked to play outside in the fair weather. They had a big ball, a red bike and a yellow kite. They shared everything and had fun. One day, they went to the park with their moms. The sun was shining and the sky was blue. Tom and Lily ran to the swings and took turns. They laughed and smiled. "Look, Lily, I can swing very high!" Tom said. "Wow, Tom, you are so brave!" Lily said. They swung higher and higher, feeling the wind in their hair. Then they saw a big dog. The dog was brown and fluffy. He had a blue collar and a friendly face. He came to Tom and Lily and wagged his tail. "Hello, doggy, what is your name?" Lily asked. The dog barked and licked Lily's face. Lily giggled and petted the dog. "Can we play with him, mom?" Tom asked. "Sure, honey, he seems nice. But be gentle and ask his owner first," Tom's mom said. Tom and Lily looked around and saw a man sitting on a bench. He had a book and a hat. He smiled and waved at them. "Hi, kids, this is Max. He loves to play and make new friends. Do you want to play with him?" the man asked. "Yes, please, mister, he is so cute!" Tom and Lily said. "Okay, then, have fun. But don't go too far and be careful," the man said. Tom and Lily nodded and thanked the man. They took Max to the grass and played fetch with the ball. Max ran fast and brought the ball back. He barked and jumped and rolled on the ground. Tom and Lily laughed and hugged him. They had a great time with Max. They made a new friend and learned a new name. They were happy and smiled. The sun smiled too.
