Category: Beginner
Title: Amy the Girl and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to sort her toys. One day, Amy found a tough rock. The rock was big and hard. Amy had a feeling that the rock was not a good thing to have. Amy wanted to keep the rock, but her mom said no. This made Amy very sad. She did not want to let go of the tough rock. Amy and her mom were not happy with each other. They both wanted different things. In the end, Amy's mom took the rock away. Amy was very sad and cried a lot. The story ends with Amy still feeling sad, and the tough rock was gone.

Ben and Lily were playing in the park with their dog, Spot. They liked to run and chase and throw the ball for Spot. Spot was a smart dog. He could catch the ball and bring it back and do tricks. Ben and Lily taught him how to sit, roll over and bow. One day, the sky became dark and windy. Ben and Lily heard a loud noise. It was a hurricane. A hurricane is a big, scary storm that can blow things away. Ben and Lily were afraid. They grabbed Spot and ran to their mom, who was waiting in the car. "Mom, mom, there's a hurricane!" Ben shouted. "Don't worry, we're safe in the car. The hurricane will pass soon. We just have to wait," mom said. She turned on the radio and listened to the news. The news said the hurricane was very strong and people should stay inside. Ben and Lily hugged Spot and looked out the window. They saw trees and signs and trash cans flying in the air. They hoped their house and their toys were okay. After a while, the wind stopped and the sun came out. The hurricane was over. Mom drove them home. They saw some damage on the way, but their house was fine. They were happy and relieved. They got out of the car and thanked mom for keeping them safe. Then they saw their neighbor, Mr. Jones, who was cleaning his yard. He had a big smile on his face. "Hello, kids. You survived the hurricane. Good for you. And look at your dog. He's so smart. He can bow," he said. He bent down and said to Spot, "Bow, Spot, bow." Spot did as he was told and bowed to Mr. Jones. Ben and Lily laughed and clapped. They were proud of their smart dog. They gave him a hug and a treat. They were glad the hurricane was gone and they could play again.

Once upon a time, there was a big, tough bird named Bob. Bob loved to fly high in the sky. One day, he met a little bird named Tim. Tim wanted to fly high too, but he did not know how to do it. Bob said, "I can teach you how to fly high in the sky, Tim!" Tim was very happy and said, "Yes, please teach me, Bob!" So, they started to learn together. Bob showed Tim how to flap his wings and go up, up, up. After a few days, Tim was able to fly high in the sky, just like Bob. They both loved flying together and had lots of fun. Tim was very thankful to Bob for teaching him how to fly high. They became the best of friends and flew in the sky every day.

Once upon a time, there was a calm rat. The rat liked to play in the park. One day, the rat saw a big tree with tasty food. The rat wanted the food, so he tried to climb the tree. As the rat climbed, he met a big bird. The big bird said, "This is my tree. You must go away." The rat did not want to fight, so he said, "Please, can we share the food?" The big bird thought about it and said, "OK, we can share." Then, something unexpected happened. The tree started to shake and lots of food fell down. The rat and the big bird were happy. They both said "thank you" to the tree. The tree had yielded food for them to share. The rat and the big bird became good friends and played together every day.
