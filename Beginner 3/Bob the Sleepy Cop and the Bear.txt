Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Sleepy Cop and the Bear

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy cop named Bob. Bob liked to help people. One day, Bob was walking down the street when he saw a little girl crying. "Hi, little girl. Why are you sad?" asked Bob the cop. The little girl looked up at Bob and said, "I lost my toy bear. I can't find it." Bob wanted to help the little girl. He said, "Don't worry, I will help you find your toy bear." They walked together and looked for the toy bear. The little girl was not sad anymore. Soon, they found the toy bear under a big tree. The little girl was so happy. She said, "Thank you, Bob!" The little girl held her toy bear tight. Bob the sleepy cop smiled and said, "You're welcome. Have a good day!" Then, Bob walked away to take a nap.

One day, a chubby cat named Sam went for a walk. He liked to touch things with his soft paws. Sam touched the grass, the flowers, and the trees. He was very happy. Sam saw a nurse walking by. She was kind and smiled at him. The nurse was going to help people who were sick. Sam wanted to help too, so he followed the nurse. Together, Sam and the nurse helped people feel better. Sam would touch their hands with his soft paws, and they would smile. The nurse and Sam made a great team, and they became best friends.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a wet, shiny prize under a big tree. She was very happy and wanted to show her mom. Lily ran home with the prize in her hands. She saw her mom in the kitchen and showed her the prize. Lily's mom smiled and said, "This is a special prize! You can eat it!" Lily was excited to eat her prize. Her mom helped her open the prize and inside was a yummy, sweet treat. Lily took a big bite and her face lit up with joy. From that day on, Lily always looked for wet prizes under the big tree. She found many more and shared them with her friends. They all loved to eat the sweet treats and play together in the sun.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Timmy. Timmy lived in a big tree with his family. Every day, Timmy would wake up early in the morning and sing a happy song. One day, Timmy saw a spicy red pepper on the ground. He thought it looked yummy and wanted to eat it. But Timmy's mom said, "Be careful, Timmy. Spicy things can hurt your mouth." Timmy didn't listen and ate the spicy red pepper. His mouth felt hot and he didn't like it. Timmy tried to escape the hot feeling by drinking water. His mom helped him feel better. From that day on, Timmy learned to listen to his mom and be careful with new things. The moral of the story is to always listen to your parents because they know what's best for you.

Ben and Lily were playing in the park with their ball. They liked to kick the ball to each other and run after it. Sometimes they missed the ball and it rolled away. Then they had to chase it before it went too far. One time, the ball rolled near the road. Ben ran after it, but he did not look both ways. He did not see the big car coming fast. The car hit Ben and he fell down. He cried out loud and his leg hurt a lot. Lily saw what happened and ran to Ben. She was scared and sad. She tried to help Ben, but she did not know what to do. She shouted for help. "Help, help! Ben is hurt! He needs help!" A man heard Lily and came to see. He saw Ben on the ground and the car that hit him. He called the ambulance on his phone. He told Lily to stay with Ben and to be brave. He said the ambulance would come soon and take Ben to the hospital. Lily held Ben's hand and talked to him. She told him he would be okay and that she loved him. She tried to make him smile, but he only looked dull and tired. He said his leg hurt a lot and he wanted his mom and dad. Soon, they heard a loud noise. It was the ambulance. It had a red light and a siren. It stopped near Ben and Lily. Two people came out of the ambulance. They had white clothes and bags. They were the doctors. They raised Ben on a stretcher and put him in the ambulance. They told Lily to come with them. They said they would take Ben to the hospital and fix his leg. Lily got in the ambulance with Ben. She hugged him and told him not to be afraid. She hoped he would get better soon. She wished they could play with their ball again.
