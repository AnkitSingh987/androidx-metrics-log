Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl Finds the Sun

One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to paint. She took out her paint and brushes. Lily was very excited. She wanted to make a crazy picture. Lily started to paint. She painted a big red sun, a blue tree, and a green cat. Then, she had an idea. She saw some sauce on the table. Lily thought, "I can use the sauce to paint too!" Lily took the sauce and painted a big, brown house. Her mom came into the room and said, "Lily, what are you doing with the sauce?" Lily smiled and said, "I'm painting a crazy picture, Mommy!" Her mom laughed and said, "That's very creative, Lily. But next time, let's use only paint for our pictures, okay?" Lily nodded and they both laughed. They had fun making crazy pictures together.

Once upon a time, in a big park, there was a pretty fountain. Kids loved to play near the water. One day, a silly little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim was very happy to play near the fountain. While Tim was playing, he saw a big fish in the water. The fish jumped out and said, "Hi, Tim!" Tim was so surprised. He wanted to touch the fish. So, he gave the fish a little slap. The fish did not like it. The fish got mad and splashed water all over Tim. Tim got all wet and started to cry. His mom came running and took him away from the fountain. They went home, and Tim was sad. He knew it was not nice to slap the fish.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a little pen on the floor. She picked it up and thought it was very pretty. She showed it to her mom and said, "Look, Mom! I found a little pen!" Her mom smiled and said, "That's a nice pen, Mia. Let's put it in your room for now. Maybe someone will come looking for it later." The next day, Mia and her mom went to the park. While they were playing, a boy named Tom came up to them. Tom looked sad. He said, "I lost my little pen yesterday. Have you seen it?" Mia thought about the little pen in her room. She said, "Yes, I found a little pen! Let's go to my house and I will give it to you." They went to Mia's house and Mia passed the little pen to Tom. Tom was very happy and thanked Mia. From that day on, Mia and Tom became good friends, and they always shared their toys and things with each other.

Once upon a time, in a big, big forest, there lived a nice rabbit and a weird bird. The rabbit had long ears and the bird had a very long leg. They were best friends and they played together every day. One day, they found a ball in the forest. The rabbit and the bird wanted to play with the ball. The rabbit said, "Let's shoot the ball with our leg and see who can shoot it far." The weird bird agreed and they started to play. They took turns and had lots of fun. But then, the rabbit had an idea. He said, "Let's not shoot the ball too hard. If we shoot it too far, we might lose it in the forest." The weird bird listened to the rabbit and they played gently. They did not lose the ball and had fun all day. The moral of the story is to be careful and listen to your friends.

Once upon a time there were two best friends, named Jean and Joe. They did everything together and had lots of fun. One day, they decided to play a game. Jean thought of a special one: She would find a way to the top of a hill. She said Joe should go first and find the best path to the top. Joe agreed and started walking up the hill. But half way up, he got stuck. He shouted down to Jean but she couldn't help. Joe yelled and shouted, but it wouldn't move him. Suddenly, he had an idea. He shouted for Jean to come up and help him! Jean came quickly and the two of them tugged and pushed together until they reached the top. Together, they looked down and waved. But then Jean noticed something: Joe didn't look happy. She asked why, but Joe stayed quiet. Finally, Jean asked if Joe was mad because he had been stuck and she didn't help. Joe nodded and said that he had felt very rude. Jean said that was silly and hugged Joe. She said they were still best friends, no matter what happened. That made Joe feel better. They smiled and hugged, and decided to find another game to take part in.
