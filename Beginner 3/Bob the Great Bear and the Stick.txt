Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Great Bear and the Stick

Once upon a time, there was a great big bear named Bob. Bob loved to eat grapes. One day, he found a big grape tree with lots of grapes on it. He was very happy. Bob wanted to pick the grapes, but they were too high up. He tried to jump and reach, but he could not get them. So, Bob had an idea. He would fix a long stick to get the grapes. Bob found a long stick and fixed it with some vines. Now, he could reach the grapes! He picked lots and lots of grapes with his new stick. Bob was very proud of his great idea. Bob took the grapes home and shared them with his friends. They all loved the grapes and thanked Bob for sharing. From that day on, Bob always used his great stick to pick grapes from the tree. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big, grey elephant named Ella. Ella lived in a jungle with her friends. They liked to play in the mud and have fun all day. One day, Ella saw that she was filthy from playing in the mud. She wanted to be clean, so she went to the river. Ella used her long nose to spray water on herself. She washed the mud away and felt happy. Ella went back to her friends and showed them how clean she was. They all wanted to be clean too. So, they went to the river and cleaned themselves just like Ella. Now, all the elephants were clean and happy. They played in the jungle and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toy cars. One day, she saw a match on the floor. It was dead, so she picked it up to play with it. Lily gazed at the match and said, "I will make you my new car!" She put the match with her toy cars and they all played together. The toy cars liked the match, even though it was dead. Lily's mom came into the room and saw her playing. "What are you playing with, Lily?" her mom asked. Lily showed her the dead match and said, "It's my new car!" Her mom smiled and said, "That's very creative, Lily! But remember, don't play with matches when they're not dead."

Tom and Anna are playing in the park. They like to pretend they are spies and have a pistol each. They hide behind the trees and bushes and try to catch each other. But then they hear a loud noise. It is not a pistol noise. It is a big dog noise. They see a big dog running towards them. It has sharp teeth and angry eyes. They are scared. They drop their pistols and run to the slide. They climb up the ladder and slide down. They hope the big dog will not follow them. But the big dog is fast and jumps on the slide too. Tom and Anna scream. They think the big dog will bite them. But then they see something. The big dog has a collar and a tag. It says "Rex". And it has a cute smile and a wagging tail. It is not an angry dog. It is a friendly dog. The big dog licks Tom and Anna's faces. It wants to play with them. Tom and Anna laugh. They are not scared anymore. They think the big dog is adorable. They pet the big dog and give it their pistols. The big dog likes the pistols. It chews them and makes funny noises. Tom and Anna make a new friend. They call him Rex. They play with him until their mom comes to pick them up. They say goodbye to Rex and hope to see him again. They have a fun day in the park.

Once upon a time there was a tractor. He was a very strong tractor and worked in the fields. One day the tractor got very busy. He had to load tomatoes into the truck. The tomatoes were big and bright red. The tractor worked and worked. He filled the truck with the red tomatoes. When he was done, he said goodbye and drove away. The truck drove the tomatoes to the market. It was so exciting! The people in the market were so happy to get the fresh red tomatoes. The tractor was happy too. He was glad he could help load the truck with tomatoes so everyone in the market could have some.
