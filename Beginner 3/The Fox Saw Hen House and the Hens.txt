Category: Beginner
Title: The Fox Saw Hen House and the Hens

One day, a fox saw a big hen house. He was very hungry and wanted to eat some hens. He waited until it was dark and then he sneaked inside. He saw many hens sleeping on their nests. He licked his lips and grabbed one by the neck. "Help! Help!" the hen squawked. "A fox is here! He wants to eat me!" The other hens woke up and started to make a lot of noise. They flapped their wings and pecked at the fox. The fox was scared and tried to run away. But he was too late. The farmer heard the noise and came out with his gun. "Bang! Bang!" the farmer shot at the fox. He hit him in the chest and the head. The fox fell down and did not move. He was dead. The farmer picked up the fox by the tail and threw him in the trash. He checked on his hens and gave them some corn. He was happy that he saved his hens from the fox. The hens were happy too. They thanked the farmer and went back to sleep. They were safe and warm. They did not like the fox. He was bad and mean. He tried to eat them. He got what he deserved. He was dead.

One day, a girl named Sue was playing with her dog, Max. They were running in the park when they saw a big tree. Sue said, "Max, let's pause and rest." They sat down under the tree and Sue took out a sandwich. The sandwich had butter in it. Sue took a bite of her sandwich and made a face. "Yuck! This butter is spoiled!" she said. Max looked at Sue and barked. Sue didn't want to eat the bad sandwich, so she threw it away. They got up and started to play again. As they played, a bird flew down and picked up the sandwich Sue threw away. The bird flew up to the tree and shared the sandwich with other birds. It turned out that the birds loved the spoiled butter! They all chirped happily and thanked Sue and Max for the tasty treat. Sue and Max smiled, happy to have made new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little car. The car had a driver. The driver was a nice man. The man drove the car every day. They went to many places together. One day, the car stopped. It could not go. The driver felt helpless. He did not know what to do. He looked at the car and said, "Why can't you go?" The car did not say anything. It just sat there. A big truck came by. The truck saw the little car and the driver. The truck driver asked, "Do you need help?" The man said, "Yes, please." The truck driver shrugged and said, "I can help you." The truck pushed the little car. The little car started to go again. The driver was happy. The car and the driver went home.

Once upon a time, there was a weird little monkey named Momo. Momo loved to wrap things with his long tail. One day, Momo saw a big tree with a lot of big leaves. He had a fun idea! Momo said to his friend, Lulu the bird, "I want to teach you how to wrap things with leaves!" Lulu was excited and said, "Yes, please teach me, Momo!" So Momo and Lulu started to wrap things with the big leaves. They wrapped a big ball, a small toy, and even a little bug. Lulu was very happy to learn from Momo. They played and wrapped things all day. In the end, they were tired but had a lot of fun. Momo and Lulu became best friends and loved to teach each other new things.

One day, a polite little ball named Bouncy wanted to have a big success. Bouncy loved to bounce all day long. He bounced in the park, he bounced at home, and he even bounced in his sleep. Bouncy was very happy when he bounced. One sunny day, Bouncy met a new friend named Froggy. Froggy also loved to bounce. Bouncy asked, "Do you want to bounce with me?" Froggy said, "Yes, let's bounce together!" So, Bouncy and Froggy bounced all day long. They bounced high and low, and they even bounced on the soft grass. As they bounced, they saw a big tree. Bouncy and Froggy wanted to see who could bounce the highest. They took turns bouncing up and up. Bouncy bounced very high, but Froggy bounced even higher. Bouncy was happy for his friend's success. They both knew that bouncing together was the best fun of all.
