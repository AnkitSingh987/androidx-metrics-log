Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Car and the Tree

Once upon a time, in an ancient land, there was a big, red car. The car loved to zoom fast on the ground. The car had a friend, a little blue car. They liked to play and zoom together. One day, the big, red car said, "Let's have a race! We will see who can zoom the fastest!" The little blue car agreed, and they started the race. They zoomed and zoomed, and the little blue car was in front. But then, something unexpected happened. A big, green tree fell on the ground right in front of the little blue car! The little blue car stopped and said, "Oh no! What do we do?" The big, red car thought for a moment and said, "Let's push the tree together!" So, the big, red car and the little blue car pushed the tree off the ground. They were happy they could help each other. From that day on, they were the best of friends and loved to zoom and play together.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a tie that he loved very much. It was a dull tie, but he liked it. He wore the tie every day. One day, Tim saw a girl who had a pretty ribbon, and he wanted it. He thought that if he cut the ribbon, he could have it. So, Tim took some scissors and cut the ribbon. The girl was sad that her ribbon was cut. Tim felt bad too. He learned that it was not nice to take things from others. He gave the ribbon back to the girl and said sorry. From that day on, Tim always asked before taking something, and he was a kind boy.

One day, a girl named Mia went to the grocery store with her mom. They needed to buy food for dinner. Mia liked to help her mom pick out the food they needed. She felt like a big girl when she helped her mom. At the store, Mia saw a spicy pepper. She wanted to try it. But her mom said, "No, Mia. It is too spicy for you. It will make your mouth hurt." Mia was sad and did not understand why she could not try the spicy pepper. Later, when they got home, Mia's mom started to cook dinner. Mia still wanted to try the spicy pepper. So, she took a small part of it and put it in her mouth. Suddenly, her mouth felt very hot! Mia started to cry. Her mom heard her and came to help. Her mom gave her a glass of milk to make her mouth feel better. Mia learned that her mom was right. The spicy pepper was not good for her. From that day on, Mia knew to listen to her mom when she said something was too spicy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy called Joe. Joe was a great kid who loved to play outside. One day, Joe saw a glowing tire in his backyard. He couldn't believe it! Joe asked his mom if she saw the tire glow. She said she did not, so Joe decided to go closer and take a look. When he got closer to the tire he heard a voice coming from it. It said, "Hello little boy, I am a magical tire." Joe was amazed and asked, "What can you do?" The tire replied, "I can make you fly high in the sky"! Joe was so excited and said, "That would be great!". The tire said, "Turn me 3 times and I will make you fly". Joe turned the tire 3 times and before he knew it, he was soaring high in the sky with a big smile on his face. Joe had a great time flying in the sky and when he was ready, the tire brought him safely back to his backyard. Joe thanked the tire and told all of his friends about his magical adventure.

One day, a weird bird came to town. The bird was very big and had many colors. The people in the town did not know the bird. They thought it was strange. The bird said, "I want to be friends. Can you welcome me and show me around?" The people said, "Yes, we can give you a tour of our town." They took the bird to see the park, the school, and the big tree. The bird was happy. The people were happy too. They liked the weird bird. They all became friends and played together. The town was a fun place with their new friend, the weird bird.
