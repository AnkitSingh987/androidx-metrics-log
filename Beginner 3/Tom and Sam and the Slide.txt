Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Sam and the Slide

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to play at the park. They like to read books and go on the slide. One day, they go to the park with their moms. They see a big, gray cloud in the sky. They hear a loud boom. It is going to rain. Tom and Sam run to the slide. They want to go on it before it rains. But there is only one slide. Tom gets there first. He climbs up the ladder. Sam is behind him. He wants to go on the slide too. He pushes Tom. Tom falls off the ladder. He hits his head on the ground. He cries. Sam is sorry. He did not mean to hurt Tom. He tries to help him. But Tom's mom is angry. She yells at Sam. She says he is a bad boy. She takes Tom to the car. She says they are going home. Sam's mom is sad. She says they have to go home too. She says they can't play at the park anymore. Tom and Sam are not friends anymore. They are sad and mad. They don't read books or go on the slide together. They don't have fun. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He went to the store with his mom. Tom saw a big, round orange. He wanted to eat it, so his mom bought it for him. Tom took the orange home and put it on his table. He forgot about it for a few days. When he remembered, he saw that the orange had started to rot. It was not good to eat anymore. Tom was sad. His mom knew he was sad, so she brought him a fake orange. This orange would not rot. Tom played with the fake orange and was happy again. Tom learned that sometimes, fake things can be fun too.

Anna and Ben were twins who liked to play outside at night. They loved to look at the stars and the moon and make wishes. One night, they saw a big, bright moon that looked very fancy. It had a ring of light around it and different colors. "Wow, look at the moon!" Anna said. "It is so pretty and shiny. Do you think it is a special moon?" "I don't know," Ben said. "Maybe it is a magic moon. Maybe it can grant our wishes." They both closed their eyes and wished for something they wanted. Anna wished for a new doll and Ben wished for a new bike. Then they opened their eyes and waited for their wishes to come true. But nothing happened. The moon was still there, but their wishes did not come true. They felt sad and confused. "Why didn't the moon give us what we wanted?" Anna asked. "We were good and polite. We said please and thank you." Ben shrugged. He did not know either. He wondered if the moon was angry or mean. Just then, their mom came outside to call them in for bed. She saw them looking at the moon and smiled. "Did you enjoy the moon tonight?" she asked. "It is a very special moon. It is called a halo moon. It happens when there are ice crystals in the air that make the light bend and change colors." She explained to them what ice crystals were and how they made the moon look fancy. She also explained that the moon was not magic and could not grant wishes. She said that wishes were good, but they had to work hard and be patient to make them come true. Anna and Ben listened to their mom and learned something new. They realized that the moon was not mean or angry, but beautiful and amazing. They also realized that they had to be grateful for what they had and not expect everything to be easy. They thanked their mom for explaining the moon to them and gave her a hug. Then they went inside to get ready for bed. They still looked at the moon from their window and smiled. They still made wishes, but they also made plans. They knew that the moon was not magic, but it was still a wonder.
