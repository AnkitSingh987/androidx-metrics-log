Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Mule and the Farm

One day, a mule named Max lived on a farm. Max had a wide mouth and loved to speak. He spoke to all the animals on the farm. The cow, the pig, and the sheep all liked to listen to Max speak. But there was a problem. The farm dog, named Spot, did not like Max. Spot was mad that Max could speak so well. Spot wanted to be the one who spoke to all the animals. So, Spot thought of a plan to make Max stop speaking. Spot went to Max and said, "Max, if you speak too much, you will lose your voice!" Max was scared. He did not want to lose his voice. So, Max stopped speaking. The farm was very quiet. The cow, the pig, and the sheep were all sad. They missed Max's voice. Then, the wise old owl saw that everyone was sad. The owl knew that Spot had tricked Max. The owl told Max the truth. Max was happy to know that he could speak again. He went back to speaking with all the animals. Spot was sorry for his trick and promised to be nice to Max. From that day on, Max and Spot became good friends. And the farm was a happy place again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was a fearful boy who was scared of many things. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw other kids playing on the swings and slides, but he was too scared to join them. Tim's mom saw him standing there and gave him a gentle push. "Go on, Tim," she said. Tim took a deep breath and sighed. He walked slowly to the swings, his heart beating fast. He sat down on a swing and held the chains tight. With a little push from his mom, Tim started swinging. He felt the wind on his face and saw the other kids laughing and playing. Tim's fear started to go away. He smiled and laughed as he swung higher and higher. Tim was no longer a fearful boy, and he had a great time at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was very sleepy one day. She wanted to take a nap. Before she went to sleep, she wanted to pick her favorite toy to sleep with her. Mia had two toys she loved a lot. One toy was on the left side, and one toy was on the right side. Mia picked the toy on the left side. It was a soft, cute teddy bear. She hugged the teddy bear and lay down to sleep. Soon, she was dreaming of fun things like playing with her friends and eating ice cream. While Mia was sleeping, something unexpected happened. The teddy bear on the right side started to move! It was not just a toy, but a magical teddy bear! The magical teddy bear saw Mia and the other teddy bear sleeping. It wanted to be picked too. So, the magical teddy bear joined them and lay down on the other side of Mia. When Mia woke up, she was very surprised to see both teddy bears with her. From that day on, Mia knew she had two very special toys to sleep with her.

One day, a tall girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw her shadow on the ground. Lily thought it was fun to play with her shadow. She jumped, and her shadow jumped too. She danced, and her shadow danced with her. Lily saw a boy named Tom. He was playing with a red ball. "Hi Tom," said Lily. "Do you want to play with my shadow?" Tom looked at Lily's shadow and said, "Yes, let's play!" They played with the shadow and the red ball. They had a lot of fun. But then, something strange happened. When Lily tried to pull the red ball, her shadow pulled it too. The ball went up in the sky and did not come down. Lily and Tom were very surprised. They looked at each other and laughed. Now, they had a new game to play with the shadow and the red ball.
