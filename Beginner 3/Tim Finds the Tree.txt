Category: Beginner
Title: Tim Finds the Tree

One sunny day, Tim and his fat cat, Sam, went to the park. They loved to play there. Today, they wanted to play a new game. Tim said, "Let's play 'guess the animal' game, Sam!" Sam meowed and nodded. Tim looked around and saw a big tree. He said, "Guess, Sam! What animal lives in a tree?" Sam looked up at the tree and thought for a moment. Then, Sam meowed and pointed at a bird. "Yes, Sam! It's a bird!" Tim clapped and smiled. Next, they saw a small pond. Tim asked, "Guess, Sam! What animal lives in the water?" Sam looked at the pond and saw something swimming. He meowed and pointed at the fish. "Great job, Sam! It's a fish!" Tim hugged his fat cat. They had so much fun playing the 'guess the animal' game today.

One day, a tutor named Tim went to the park to play. He saw a big ball and wanted to throw it. Tim was very alert and saw his friend, Sam, near a tree. "Hey Sam, want to play?" Tim asked. Sam smiled and said, "Yes, I want to play too!" Tim threw the ball to Sam. Sam caught the ball and threw it back. They laughed and played with the ball for a long time. The sun was shining, and they were very happy. After a while, they got tired and sat under the tree. Tim and Sam talked about their day and ate snacks. They had a great time at the park, and Tim was happy he could throw the ball with his friend.

Mum and Dad took the children on a journey. They went on a train. The children were very excited. "Where are we going?" asked the youngest child. "You'll find out soon," said Dad. "Just be patient." When they arrived, they examined the area. The children saw lots of trees and flowers. It was harmless. They felt safe. "Look over there," said Mum. "It's a beautiful lake." The children quickly ran over to the lake and splashed in the water. They had a wonderful day and had many fun adventures. When it was time to leave the children were sad, but they looked forward to their next journey.

One day, a boy named Tom went to the park with his friend, Will. The sun was mild, and the sky was blue. They wanted to play and have fun. Tom had a ball. He said to Will, "Let's throw the ball!" Will was happy and said, "Yes, let's play!" They took turns throwing the ball to each other. As they played, a little dog came running to them. The dog wanted to play too. Tom threw the ball, and the dog ran to catch it. They all laughed and played together. It was a fun day at the park.

One day, a little dog named Max went for a walk. Max saw a big, dirty ball near a tree. He thought it would be fun to play with the ball. So, Max used his paw to push the ball. As Max played with the dirty ball, his paw got dirty too. He did not care because he was having so much fun. Max played with the ball all day long. When the sun went down, Max went home. His mom saw his dirty paw and cleaned it for him. Max was happy to be clean again. He went to sleep and dreamed of playing with the big, dirty ball again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia liked to look at the sky. She saw many clouds. She saw a big cloud that was very attractive. The cloud was white and fluffy like a big pillow. Mia started to wonder about the cloud. She wondered if she could touch it. She wondered if she could play with it. She wanted to jump on the cloud and have fun. One day, Mia told her friends about the attractive cloud. They all went outside to see the cloud. They all wondered about it too. They had a fun day playing under the cloud, and they were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a playful little girl named Lily. She loved to pretend and play all day. One day, Lily found a big box full of costumes in her room. She was so happy and excited. Lily put on a costume that looked like a big, fluffy cat. She pretended to be a cat and went around the house, meowing and playing with her toys. Her mom and dad laughed and played with her, too. After a while, Lily took off the cat costume and put on a costume that looked like a pretty princess. She pretended to be a princess and danced around the house. Her mom and dad clapped and cheered. They all had so much fun playing together.
