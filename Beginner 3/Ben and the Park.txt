Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and the Park

A little boy named Ben was walking in the park. He saw an old man shaking a wallet. Ben asked, "What is that?" The old man slowly replied, "This is a wallet. I'm shaking it to see if I can hear any money inside." Ben was surprised and excited. He held out his hands and smiled. "I want to try shaking it!" The old man smiled and handed Ben the wallet. Ben shook it carefully, and to his delight, he heard the sound of coins rattling inside. He grinned and danced around with the wallet. He shook it faster and faster. Soon the coins were falling out of the wallet and onto the ground. The old man laughed and said, "Slow down, Ben. Shaking it too fast won't get you any more money." Ben laughed and slowed down his shaking. He kept shaking the wallet until all the coins were out. Then, he thanked the old man and ran off with his pocket full of coins.

Once upon a time there was a rabbit who had an idea. He wanted to stay up all night and have lots of fun. But his mom told him that he needed to go to sleep. At first the rabbit was frustrated, but his mom said, "Sleep will make you feel better, and you'll have energy to do all the fun things you want." The rabbit listened to his mom and went to bed. The next morning the rabbit woke up full of energy and ready to have lots of fun. He quickly remembered his mom's advice, "Sleep will make you feel better!" and smiled. The moral of the story is that sometimes it's best to listen to your parents. Taking care of yourself by getting enough sleep can help you feel better and have more fun.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big tree with many leaves. Tim wanted to climb the tree, but his mom said, "No, it's too high." Tim saw a little girl named Sue playing with a ball. She threw the ball to Tim, and he caught it. They passed the ball back and forth, laughing and having fun. Tim's mom watched them play and smiled. After playing, Tim and Sue were hungry. Tim's mom gave them a healthy treat. "Here are some yummy apples," she said. Tim and Sue ate the apples and felt strong and happy. They thanked Tim's mom and continued to play in the park.

One day, a little dog named Max went for a walk. Max was a poor dog who had no home. He was very tired and found a big tree to sleep under. Max went to sleep and had a nice long nap. Max woke up and saw a big cat in front of him. The cat said, "Hello, I am Kitty. I live in this tree." Max was scared and started to panic. He did not know what to do. Max said, "Please don't hurt me. I am just a poor dog looking for a place to sleep." Kitty smiled and said, "Don't panic, Max. I won't hurt you. I am a nice cat. We can be friends and you can live with me in my tree." Max was happy and thanked Kitty. They both lived together in the tree and became best friends.

Tom and Lily are playing in the park. They like to run and jump and slide. Tom has new sunglasses. He thinks they make him look strong and cool. "Look at me, Lily!" Tom says. "I can run very fast with my sunglasses!" Lily smiles. She likes Tom's sunglasses too. She wants to try them on. "Can I wear your sunglasses, Tom?" Lily asks. "Please?" Tom thinks for a moment. He likes to share, but he also likes his sunglasses. He decides to let Lily wear them for a little while. "Okay, Lily. You can wear them. But be careful. They are very special." Lily says thank you. She puts on the sunglasses. They are big for her face, but she does not mind. She feels strong and cool too. "Let's go to the slide, Tom!" Lily says. "I can slide very fast with your sunglasses!" Tom and Lily run to the slide. They take turns to slide down. They laugh and have fun. But then, something bad happens. Lily slides too fast. She loses her balance. She falls off the slide. "Ouch!" Lily cries. "That hurts!" Tom runs to Lily. He sees that she is not hurt badly, but she has a scratch on her knee. He also sees that his sunglasses are broken. One of the lenses is cracked. He feels sad and angry. "Lily, look what you did!" Tom says. "You broke my sunglasses! They were very special!" Lily feels sorry. She did not mean to break Tom's sunglasses. She knows that he liked them a lot. She wants to make him feel better. "I'm sorry, Tom. I'm sorry I broke your sunglasses. I did not want to fall. It was an accident. Please don't be mad at me." Tom looks at Lily. He sees that she is sad and sorry too. He remembers that she is his friend. He does not want to be mad at her. He decides to forgive her. "It's okay, Lily. I'm not mad at you. I know it was an accident. You are my friend. Friends share and forgive." Tom and Lily hug. They feel better. They go to the bench. They put a band-aid on Lily's knee. They throw away the broken sunglasses. They decide to play something else. They are still strong and cool. They are happy.
