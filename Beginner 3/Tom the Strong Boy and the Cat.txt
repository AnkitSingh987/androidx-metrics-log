Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Strong Boy and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a strong boy named Tom. Tom liked to gain big nails. One day, Tom went to the store to buy a big nail. He was very happy when he found one. On his way home, Tom saw a cat stuck in a tree. Tom wanted to help the cat. He thought of using his big nail to make a ladder. Tom put the nail in the tree and climbed up. But, surprise! The cat was not a real cat. It was a toy cat. Tom laughed and took the toy cat down. He showed it to his friends and they all laughed too. Tom was happy he could help, even if it was just a toy cat.

---

One day, a little girl named Lucy went on a walk. She wanted to discover new things. She was very intelligent. Lucy had a notebook with her. She liked to draw in it. On her walk, Lucy saw a boy named Tom. Tom was sad. He said, "I lost my ball. Can you help me find it?" Lucy wanted to help. They looked for the ball together. Lucy and Tom found the ball near a big tree. Tom was happy. Lucy said, "I am glad I could help you!" They both played with the ball. Then, Lucy drew a picture of them in her notebook. They became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a nice white cat. The cat loved to decorate. One day, the cat found a big box. The box was a cooler. The cat was very happy. The cat wanted to decorate the cooler. The cat found some pretty stickers. The cat put the stickers all over the cooler. The cooler looked very pretty. The cat was very proud. The cat showed the cooler to all the friends. The friends liked the cooler too. The cat and friends had a fun party. They ate yummy food from the cooler. Everyone was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a large bear. The bear lived in a land with lots of ice. The bear liked to play on the ice all day. He would slide and jump on the ice. The bear had many friends who would play with him. One day, the sun began to rise high in the sky. The ice started to melt. The bear and his friends were sad. They did not want the ice to go away. They tried to find more ice to play on, but it was hard. The bear and his friends kept looking for ice. They walked and walked, but they could not find any. The sun was still high, and the ice kept melting. The bear and his friends were very sad. They could not play on the ice anymore.

One day, a little cat went for a walk. The cat was feeling poor because it was hungry. It walked and walked, looking for food. Soon, the cat saw a big dog. The dog wanted to play, but it did not know that the cat was scared. The dog ran to the cat, and the cat got more scared. The cat ran away, but the dog followed. They ran and ran until they found a big tree. The cat climbed the tree to be safe. The dog sat under the tree, waiting to play. But the cat was still scared. Soon, a kind girl found them and gave the cat some food. The cat was not poor anymore and felt happy.

Once upon a time, there was a new toy named Mr. Key. Mr. Key loved to unlock things. He lived in a small toy town with his friends. They all played together and had fun. One day, Mr. Key heard his friends talking. "We can't play outside," they said. "There is a war between the toy soldiers and the toy cars. It is not safe." Mr. Key wanted to help his friends, so he had an idea. Mr. Key went to the toy soldiers and the toy cars. He said, "Can we all be friends and play together?" The toy soldiers and the toy cars agreed. Mr. Key used his special power to unlock the gates of the town. The toys all played together and there was no more war. Everyone was happy and they all had fun.
