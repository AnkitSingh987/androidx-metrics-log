Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Tom

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a flute that she loved to play. One day, she saw a big boy named Tom. Tom was eating a disgusting snack. It was so yucky! Lily wanted to play her flute for Tom. But Tom did not want to listen. He said, "Go away, Lily!" Lily felt sad. She wanted Tom to like her flute music. But Tom just wanted to ignore her. Lily was brave. She played her flute even louder. Tom stopped eating his snack. He listened to Lily's music. Tom said, "I like your flute, Lily!" They became friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sally. She loved to play with her toy car. One day, she wanted to drive her car in a new, original way. So, she asked her mom, "Can I drive my car in the bath?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful not to get the car too wet." Sally was so happy and excited. She filled the bath with water and bubbles. She put her toy car in the bath and started to drive it around. As she was playing, she saw a bottle of shampoo. She thought it would be fun to put the shampoo on her car. But, when she squeezed the bottle, too much shampoo came out! The whole bath was full of bubbles. Sally and her mom laughed and laughed. They had never seen so many bubbles before.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to organize his toys. He would line them up, sort them by color, and make sure they were all smooth and clean. One day, he had a great idea. He wanted to have a race with his toys. He called his friend, Sue, and asked her, "Do you want to play a race game with me and my toys?" Sue said, "Yes! I will bring my toys too." So, Tim and Sue met in the park with their toys. They organized the toys into two teams. Tim's team was blue, and Sue's team was red. The race started, and the toys ran as fast as they could. It was a close race, but in the end, Sue's team won. Tim was sad, but Sue said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can play again tomorrow, and maybe your team will win." Tim smiled and said, "Yes, let's play again. It was fun!" So, they both went home, excited to play again the next day.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Tom. He had a glove that he loved very much. One day, he went to the park to play with his glove. But he saw a big boy playing in the same place. Tom felt scared and remained distant from the big boy. Tom wanted to play with his glove, but he was too scared to go near the big boy. He sat on a bench and watched the big boy play. Suddenly, a strong wind came and took Tom's glove away. It flew right into the big boy's hands. Tom was very sad and didn't know what to do. The big boy saw Tom's sad face and walked over to give the glove back. Tom was surprised and happy. The big boy said, "Let's play together!" They played all day and became good friends. The moral of the story is to not be scared of others and to share and play together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very brave boy. He loved to play outside, and he was not scared of anything. One day, Tim saw a big jar of cookies on the top shelf in the kitchen. He really wanted a cookie, but he was too small to reach the jar. Tim had a plan. He would wait until it was time to sleep, and then he would sneak out of his bed and get the cookies. When it was dark outside, and everyone was asleep, Tim got out of bed. He was very brave, even though it was dark and quiet. Tim climbed on a chair and reached for the cookie jar. He got the jar and opened it. Inside, there were many yummy cookies. Tim took one cookie and put the jar back. Then, he went back to his bed and ate the cookie. It was the best cookie he ever had. Tim was happy that he was brave and got his cookie. He went back to sleep with a big smile on his face.
