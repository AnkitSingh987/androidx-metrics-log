Category: Beginner
Title: The Strong Farmer and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a strong farmer. He had a big farm with many animals. Every day, he would wake up early to take care of his farm. One day, the farmer was very tired. He let out a big yawn. He saw his animals yawning too. They all felt sleepy. The farmer had an idea. He thought they all needed a break. So, the strong farmer and his animals took a nap under a big tree. They all felt happy and rested. When they woke up, they were ready to work and play again. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a successful cat named Kitty. Kitty was very good at helping people find things they lost. One day, a little girl named Sue lost her toy bunny. Sue asked Kitty, "Can you help me find my toy bunny?" Kitty liked to answer questions, so she said, "Yes, I will help you." They walked around the town, looking for the toy bunny. At last, they found the toy bunny under a big tree. Sue was very happy and said, "Thank you, Kitty!" Kitty felt successful because she helped Sue find her toy bunny. They walked back to the town, and everyone was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was very smelly. He liked to play in the mud and roll around. One day, Spot saw a quiet cat sitting by a tree. The cat was sad because it had a hurt paw. Spot wanted to help the cat. Spot had an idea. He went to find a special leaf that could heal. He remembered where he saw it last time. It was near the big rock. Spot ran to the big rock and found the special leaf. He was so happy and excited to help his new friend. Spot brought the leaf to the cat. The cat looked at the smelly dog and the leaf. The cat did not want to use the leaf because it was from the smelly dog. But then, something unexpected happened. The leaf began to glow! The cat decided to trust Spot and put the leaf on its hurt paw. The paw healed! The cat was happy and thanked Spot. From that day on, the smelly dog and the quiet cat became the best of friends.

Ben was a polite boy. He always said please and thank you. He liked to help his mom and dad. One day, he wanted to help his mom with the car. He saw her wallet on the table. He thought she might need it. He took the wallet and ran to the car. He opened the door and got in. He saw a key in the car. He thought it was for the car. He put the key in the hole and turned it. He heard a click. He had unlocked the car. He was happy. He wanted to drive the car. He pushed some buttons and moved some levers. But he did not know how to drive. The car started to move. It bumped into a tree. Ben was scared. He heard his mom shout. She ran to the car. She saw Ben and the wallet and the key. She was angry and worried. She took Ben out of the car. She hugged him and checked if he was hurt. She was glad he was okay. She told Ben that he should not take her wallet or her key. She said that he could not drive the car. She said that he should ask before he touched anything. She said that he was a polite boy, but he had made a big mistake. She said that he had to say sorry and help her fix the car. Ben felt bad. He did not mean to make his mom angry or hurt the car. He said sorry to his mom. He said he wanted to help her. He said he would not do it again. He asked how he could help her fix the car. His mom smiled. She said he could help her call the man who fixed cars. She said he could help her clean the car. She said she loved him. Ben hugged his mom. He said he loved her too. He said he would be more careful next time. He learned his lesson.
