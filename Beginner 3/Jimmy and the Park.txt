Title: Jimmy and the Park

Jimmy was a little boy. He liked to run everywhere. One day, he went to a big park. There was a big pile of cardboard. Jimmy thought it looked tough. He wanted to jump on it but his mom yelled, "Jimmy, no! Don't run there!" So, Jimmy stopped. He looked at the cardboard and wanted to touch it, but he was scared. He knew he had to stay away. Then his dad came over. He smiled and said, "Let's make a fort out of this tough cardboard! It will be so much fun!" Jimmy was excited and he shouted, "Yeah, let's build it!" He ran over and hugged his dad. Together, they started to build the fort. It was a tough job, but they worked hard together and had lots of fun. When it was finished, Jimmy felt proud of what he had done. He had built something tough out of cardboard!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a violin that she loved to play. She was very creative and could make up songs about anything. One day, Lily's mom said, "Lily, we are going on a trip. Please pack your things." So Lily went to her room and packed her clothes, her toys, and her violin. On the trip, Lily met a new friend named Sam. Sam asked, "What's that in your bag?" Lily said, "It's my violin! I love to play it." Sam was excited and asked, "Can you play a song for me?" So Lily played a fun song she made up just for Sam. They both laughed and danced to the music.

Once upon a time, there was a king who lived in a big castle. The king was very kind and loved to play with the children in his kingdom. One day, the king saw a light in the sky. It was very pretty, and he wanted to share it with everyone. The king called his friend, the bird, and said, "Look at the light in the sky! Can you help me prevent it from going away, so everyone can see it?" The bird nodded and flew high to try and keep the light from leaving. The bird tried very hard, but the light still went away. The king was sad, but he knew the bird did his best. The next day, the light came back, and the king was happy again. He learned that sometimes, we can't prevent things from happening, but it's okay because they might come back. The king and the bird continued to play and share the light with their friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a pink dress that she loved to wear. One day, her uncle came to visit. He was very tall and had a big smile. Lily was happy to see him. Lily's uncle brought a gift for her. It was a toy car. But Lily did not like it. She said, "I hate this car! I only like pink things!" Her uncle was sad and asked, "Why do you hate it? It can be fun to play with." Lily thought about what her uncle said. She decided to give the toy car a try. To her surprise, she had lots of fun playing with it. She learned that hating something just because it is not pink is not good. It is better to try new things and be kind to others.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a small pet cat. The cat was very cute and had a sharp claw. Sue liked the cat and wanted to take it home. She said, "Come with me, little cat. You can be my pet." The cat was happy and followed Sue to her house. Sue's mom saw the cat and said, "What a good pet you found, Sue!" Sue smiled and said, "Thank you, Mom. I will take care of my new pet." Sue and the cat played all day. They had fun and the cat was very good. At night, they both slept in Sue's bed. The cat was happy to have a new friend and Sue loved her new pet.
