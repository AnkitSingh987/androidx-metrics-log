Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Bear and the Bird

One day, a big bear and a little bunny were friends. They wanted to organize a party today. They wanted to make the party different and fun for all their friends. The big bear said, "Let's have a race!" The little bunny said, "No, let's play hide and seek!" They could not agree on what to do. They started to feel sad. Then, a tiny bird flew by and said, "Why not do both?" The big bear and the little bunny looked at each other and smiled. They had a race and played hide and seek. The party was different and fun for everyone.

There was a 3 year old called Lucy. She had a brilliant plan to give her friends a surprise. She wanted to give them all some ice-cream! But, when Lucy went to get the ice-cream, she saw that the shop was closed. She felt really sad and started to cry. Suddenly, Lucy heard someone's name being called. It was Jack, the ice-cream man! He said that he had some ice-cream with him and he was ready to give it to Lucy's friends. Lucy was thrilled! She thanked Jack and quickly ran off to give the ice-cream to her friends. Everyone was so happy when they got their ice-cream! Lucy's plan was a brilliant success! Everyone cheered and clapped for Lucy.

One day, a dog and a cat were friends. They liked to play together. But today, the dog was tired. He did not want to play. The cat wanted to play with the dog. The cat said, "Why are you tired, friend? Let's play!" The dog said, "I am tired because I did not sleep well. I cannot play now." The cat was sad. He wanted to play, but his friend was tired. The cat thought of an idea. He said, "I will help you sleep, friend. Then you will not be tired." The dog agreed. The cat sang a song to the dog. The dog slept well. When he woke up, he was not tired anymore. The dog and the cat played together again. They were happy friends.

Once upon a time, there was a fat queen. She lived in a big castle with her cat. The queen loved her cat very much. They played together all day. One day, the queen spilled her juice. The cat jumped on the table and tried to drink it. The queen said, "No, no, cat!" She took a cloth to wipe the juice. The cat watched her and learned. The next day, the cat spilled its milk. It remembered what the queen did. So, the cat took a cloth and wiped the milk. The queen saw this and laughed. She was happy that her cat learned from her. From that day on, they wiped spills together and lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to skip and play by the sea. Every day, she would go to the beach with her mom and dad. They would make sand castles, swim in the water, and have lots of fun. One day, while Lucy was skipping on the sand, she found a weird rock. It was shiny and had many colors. She showed it to her mom and dad. They were amazed by the rock and told her it was a special treasure. Lucy decided to keep the weird rock and put it in her pocket. She skipped all the way home, excited to show her friends her new treasure. From that day on, Lucy always remembered her special day at the sea and the fun she had with her family.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a red scarf. He loved his scarf very much. One day, Tim went to the park with his scarf. In the park, Tim saw a big tree. He wanted to play there. As he played, he heard a loud sound. "Who is there?" Tim asked. A small bird came out of the tree. The bird looked sad. "I am lost," said the bird. "Can you help me find my mom?" Tim wanted to help the bird. They looked for the bird's mom together. They found a sour apple on the ground. The bird did not like sour apples. They kept looking. Then, they saw a big shadow. The bird was scared. "Don't be scared," said Tim. "I am here with you." Tim and the bird found the bird's mom. She was so happy. She thanked Tim and gave him a hug. Tim and the bird became good friends. They played in the park every day. And Tim always wore his red scarf.
