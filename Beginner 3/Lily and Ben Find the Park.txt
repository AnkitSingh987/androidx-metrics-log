Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben Find the Park

Lily and Ben were best friends. They liked to play with toys, draw pictures and read books. One day, they went to the park with their moms. There, they saw a big fountain with many coins in the water. "Look, Ben, shiny money!" Lily said. "Can we have some?" "I don't know, Lily. Mom said we should not take things that are not ours," Ben said. "But they are just lying there. No one will miss them. And we can buy candy with them," Lily said. She reached out her hand and grabbed a penny from the fountain. "Hey, stop that!" a voice shouted. It was the park keeper. He was not friendly. He was angry. He walked towards Lily and Ben. Lily and Ben were scared. They dropped the penny and ran to their moms. The park keeper followed them and told their moms what they did. Lily and Ben felt bad. They knew they did something wrong. They said sorry to the park keeper and their moms. They also said sorry to the fountain and the coins. They learned their lesson. They regret taking the penny. They did not want to make anyone sad or mad. They wanted to be good and kind. They hugged each other and promised to be better friends.

Once upon a time, there was a great big planet. It was full of surprises and fun things to explore. One day, a little boy named Sam was on the planet. He was walking around, exploring with his big eyes. He saw lots of animals, plants, and colours. As he continued to explore, he heard a funny sound. It was coming from the tallest tree in front of him. He got closer and saw a little bird sitting on a branch. The bird couldn't stop shivering! Sam felt sorry for the little bird. He stepped closer and put his hand out for it to sit on. To his surprise, the bird flew onto his hand. Sam gave the bird a warm hug and it stopped shivering. The bird flew away but Sam was happy that he could help. He continued to explore the planet and found many other great things.

Lily and Tom were playing in the park with their dolls. They liked to pretend that their dolls were their babies and they were the mommies and daddies. They made a house out of sticks and leaves and put their dolls inside. "Look, our babies are so happy in their house!" Lily said. "Yes, they are. And we are happy too!" Tom said. They hugged each other and smiled. But then they heard a loud noise. It was a big dog. It was barking and running towards them. It looked angry and scary. "Help! Help!" Lily and Tom cried. They ran away from the dog, but they forgot their dolls. The dog saw the dolls and thought they were toys. It grabbed them with its teeth and tore them apart. It made a big mess. Lily and Tom hid behind a tree and watched. They felt very sad and scared. They wanted their dolls back. They wanted to comfort them and measure them and make sure they were okay. But they couldn't. The dog had ruined them. "Why did the dog do that?" Lily asked. "I don't know. It was a bad dog. A mean dog. A mysterious dog." Tom said. They wished they had never seen the dog. They wished they had never left their dolls. They wished they had a happy ending. But they didn't. They had a bad ending.

One day, a cat named Tom and a dog named Max went for a walk. They saw a wet bow on the ground. Tom said, "Look Max, a wet bow! Let's measure it." Tom and Max used their paws to measure the wet bow. They were having fun. Max said, "It is big, Tom!" Tom agreed, "Yes, it is!" Suddenly, the wet bow started to move! It was not a bow, but a snake! The snake said, "Hello, Tom and Max! I am Sam the Snake. I was hiding in the wet grass." Tom and Max were surprised, but they became friends with Sam. They all played together and had a great day.
