Category: Beginner
Title: A Tale of the Dog

One day, a fast dog and a slow turtle wanted to have a race. They both wanted to see who was the fastest. The dog was very sure he would win because he was so fast. The turtle was not so fast, but he was rare and special. They started the race on a sunny day. As they raced, the dog ran very fast, and the turtle walked slowly. The dog was so far ahead, he thought, "I can rest now and still win the race." So, the dog lay down under a big tree and fell asleep. The turtle kept walking slowly and passed the sleeping dog. The dog woke up and saw the turtle almost at the finish line. He ran as fast as he could, but the turtle won the race. The dog said, "You are a rare and special turtle. I should not have rested. I will race better next time." The turtle smiled and said, "Thank you, dog. Sometimes slow and steady wins the race."

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big hill with sand. Tim wanted to roll down the hill. He was a little fearful, but he knew it would be fun. Tim said to his friend Sam, "I want to roll down the hill. Will you roll with me?" Sam was also fearful, but he agreed. They both climbed up the hill and got ready to roll. As they started to roll, they laughed and had fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A big rain cloud came and it started to rain. The sand turned into mud, and Tim and Sam got very dirty. They were sad and had to go home.

One day, a boy named Tim had a broken toy. He was sad because he could not play with it. Tim's dad saw him and asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim said, "My toy is broken, and I can't play with it." Dad said, "Don't worry, I have a tool to fix it." He went to get the tool and came back. Tim watched as his dad fixed the toy. Tim was very happy and said, "Thank you, Dad!" But then, Tim's sister, Sue, came and took the toy from him. Tim was sad again. He said, "Sue, please give my toy back!" Sue nodded and gave the toy back to Tim. They both played with the toy together and had lots of fun. Tim was happy, and Sue was happy too.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim had a regular bike with pedals. He liked to ride his bike very fast. Tim saw his friend, Sam, at the park too. Sam had a new bike with a lock on it. Sam said, "Look, Tim! I have a lock on my bike. It keeps my bike safe." Tim wanted a lock like Sam's. He asked his mom if he could have one. His mom said, "Sure, but you must learn to unlock it first." Tim was excited to learn how to unlock a lock. The next day, Tim and Sam were at the park again. They saw a big dog running fast. The dog ran to Sam's bike and knocked it over. The lock broke! Sam was sad. Tim said, "Don't be sad, Sam. We can still have fun riding our bikes together." The moral of the story is that things can break, but friendships are more important.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Amy. She loved to play with her dolls. One day, Amy saw her big brother John polishing a board. She was so curious and wanted to join in. "Can I help you polish the board brother?" Amy asked. John was a bit jealous so he didn't let her join him. "Sorry Amy, it's not for you. This board is just for me," he said. Amy was a bit disappointed but she didn't give up. She was determined to shine the board like John. The next morning, Amy woke up early and collected some of her dolls' blankets. She got a rag and started to polish the board with the blankets. Before long, the board was shining. Amy was so proud of her work and ran to show her big brother, John. John was impressed and smiled at Amy. He was no longer jealous and was happy to see his little sister having fun. Together, they both polished the board until it shined.
