Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Bald Man and the Drawing

One day, a bald man named Tom went to the park. He saw a big board on the ground. Tom had an idea. He wanted to put the board on the wall. He thought it would be fun to draw on it. Tom asked a little girl named Sue, "Do you want to help me put the board on the wall?" Sue said, "Yes, I want to help!" So, Tom and Sue put the board on the wall together. When the board was on the wall, Tom and Sue took turns drawing. They drew a sun, a cat, and a big tree. They had so much fun drawing on the board. At the end of the day, they were very happy with their drawings.

Sara liked to play with her pet bird, Lila. She gave Lila seeds and water, and sang to her every day. Lila was happy in her cage, and chirped back to Sara. One day, Sara's mom brought home a new toy for Sara. It was a big, shiny car that made noises and lights. Sara was very excited, and wanted to play with it right away. She forgot about Lila, and left her cage in a corner of the room. Lila felt lonely and sad. She called out to Sara, but Sara did not hear her. She ignored Lila, and only played with her new toy. Lila tried to get out of her cage, but it was locked. She had no seeds or water, and the room was dark. Lila got weaker and weaker. She stopped chirping, and lay down on the bottom of her cage. She closed her eyes, and did not open them again. Sara did not notice that Lila was gone. She kept playing with her new toy, and never looked at her cage. She did not know that she had lost her friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a toy chain that she loved to play with every day. The chain was light and easy to carry around with her. She would make different shapes with it and show them to her friends. One sunny day, Sue was playing outside with her friend, Tom. "Look at my chain!" Sue said to Tom. "What can we do with it?" Tom thought for a moment and then decided, "Let's make a circle and play a game!" Sue liked the idea and they both started to play. They made a circle with the chain on the ground and took turns jumping in and out of it. They laughed and had so much fun. Sue's mom called them inside for a snack. Sue decided to share her snack with Tom. They sat and ate their snack, talking about the fun they had with the light chain. They couldn't wait to play with it again the next day.

One day, a little gray cat named Tim went to help his friend, Sam the dog, unpack. They were moving into a new home. Tim and Sam were very happy to be living together. They were good friends who liked to play and have fun. As they unpacked, Tim found a big box. He tried to open it, but it was too heavy. Sam came over to help Tim. They both pushed and pulled on the box. Suddenly, the box opened, and out came a big, gray bird! The bird said, "Hi, I am Bob. I was in the box because I was lost." Tim and Sam were very surprised. They did not know that a bird could talk. They asked Bob if he wanted to stay with them in their new home. Bob said, "Yes, I would love to stay with you." So, Tim, Sam, and Bob all lived together in their new home. They had lots of fun playing and talking. They were very happy to have found each other. And the big, gray bird was happy to have found new friends at the exit of the box.

One day, a red triangle was sleeping in the sun. It was very happy. The sun was warm on its face. The red triangle liked to sleep a lot. But then, a big wind came and woke the red triangle up. The red triangle was not happy. It wanted to go back to sleep. The wind was not nice. It was loud and it made the red triangle feel cold. The red triangle tried to find a way to stop the wind. It put a big rock in front of it. The wind could not blow the red triangle anymore. The red triangle was happy again. It could go back to sleep in the warm sun.
