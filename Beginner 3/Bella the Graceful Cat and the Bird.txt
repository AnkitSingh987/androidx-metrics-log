Category: Beginner
Title: Bella the Graceful Cat and the Bird

One day, a graceful cat named Bella was walking in the park. She saw a phone on the ground. Bella did not understand what it was. She looked at it and started to play with it. A little boy named Tim saw Bella playing with the phone. He said, "Hey! That's my phone!" Bella looked at him and meowed. Tim picked up the phone and said, "You are a nice cat. I will call my mom." But when Tim tried to call his mom, a bird came and took the phone away. Tim and Bella were very surprised. They did not expect that to happen. They looked at each other and started to laugh. Together, Tim and Bella chased the bird to get the phone back. The bird dropped the phone, and Tim caught it. Tim said, "Thank you, Bella! You are a good friend." Bella meowed happily, and they played in the park all day.

Once upon a time there was a little girl. She liked to play games with her friends. One day she decided to play a game. It was called "Zipper Win". The little girl asked her friends: "Who can win at Zipper Win?" Her friends all shook their heads, no one wanted to play. So the little girl said: "Let me try!". She placed her big finger on the zipper and said "Ready, set, go!" She then smiled and started to pull the zipper. At last, she got to the end. Everyone was so happy and cheered her name. The little girl felt so happy. She said: "It's such a peaceful feeling to win at Zipper Win". Everyone was glad that she won.

Once upon a time there was a plane. Inside the plane was a little boy. He said to his mommy, "I want to change it." His mommy replied, "Okay, why not?" The little boy started to work. He took out some tools and changed the plane. He put new pieces on the plane so that it would fit. When he was done, he stepped back and looked at the plane with a big smile on his face. He said, "I'm so happy that I changed it! It looks great now!" His mommy smiled too. She said, "You did a wonderful job. You're so fit!" The little boy was very proud of himself. He said, "This plane looks better than ever!" Then, he went off to play with his plane.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to stir things. She liked to stir her soup, her juice, and even her toys. One day, Sue found a big white bowl in the kitchen. She thought, "I can stir something in this!" Sue went to her mom and asked, "Mom, can we make something with this white bowl?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, we can make cookies!" Sue was very happy. She and her mom went to the kitchen to make cookies. Sue and her mom mixed the dough in the big white bowl. They stirred and stirred until the dough was ready. Then, they made fun shapes with the dough. Sue made a star, a heart, and a circle. After baking the cookies, they shared them with the family. Everyone loved the cookies and Sue was very proud of her shapes.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a light rain. The rain fell softly on the ground. A little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, were inside their house. They wanted to go out and play, but the rain made it hard. Tim looked out the window and saw a big flood. "Oh no!" he said. "The water is everywhere!" Spot barked at the water too. They were both sad because they could not play outside. Then, the sun came out and the rain stopped. The water started to go away. Tim and Spot went outside and saw their friends. Their friends said, "Come join us, Tim and Spot!" They all played together and had lots of fun in the light sun.

Once upon a time, there was a huge ice cream mountain. It was so big that all the kids could play on it. One sunny day, a little girl named Lily went to the ice cream mountain to play. Lily wore her favorite blouse. It was pink with white dots. She loved it very much. She climbed the ice cream mountain and had lots of fun with her friends. But then, the sun got hot and the ice cream mountain began to melt. Lily and her friends had to leave. They were a little sad, but they knew they could come back another day. And Lily's blouse got lots of ice cream on it, but she didn't mind. She knew her mom could clean it for her.
