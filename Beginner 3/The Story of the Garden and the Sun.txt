Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Garden and the Sun

One day, two little girls were playing in the garden. The sun was shining and birds were singing. "What are you doing?" asked one little girl. "I'm packing soil," the other little girl replied. She was packing the soil gently, with her little hands. "Why are you packing the soil?" the first little girl asked. "I'm making a bed for my toy bunny," the other little girl said, smiling. The little girls worked together to make sure the soil was packed into the perfect bed for the toy bunny. When they were done, they looked at the soil bed and smiled. It was perfect! The toy bunny loved it too. The little girls laughed and clapped their hands. Their bed for the toy bunny was done.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a kind doctor named Dr. Brown. Dr. Brown liked to help people and make them feel better. He was very patient when he talked to them. He always listened to their problems and worries. One day, a little girl named Lucy came to see Dr. Brown. She was scared because she had a big boo-boo on her knee. Dr. Brown looked at her knee and knew just what to do. He smiled and said, "Don't worry, Lucy. I recommend we put a band-aid on your boo-boo to keep it safe and clean." Dr. Brown carefully put the band-aid on Lucy's knee. She felt much better and wasn't scared anymore. She thanked Dr. Brown and gave him a big hug. From that day on, Lucy knew that Dr. Brown would always keep her safe and help her feel better.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little boy named Tim. He liked to know what everyone was doing all the time. One day, Tim went to school and met a new friend, Sue. Sue was a kind and happy pupil. They played together and had lots of fun. "Hi, Sue," said Tim. "Do you want to play with me?" "Yes, Tim," Sue replied. "Let's play together." One day, Tim started to worry. He thought that Sue was not happy. He saw her sitting alone and not smiling. Tim wanted to help Sue feel better. "Hi, Sue," said Tim. "Why are you sad?" "I lost my toy," Sue replied. Tim and Sue looked for the toy together. They found it under a big tree. Sue was happy again, and they played together all day. Tim learned that it is good to help friends when they worry.

Once there was a boy named Bob. Bob was three years old and loved to have fun. Today, he was out in the park and the sun was shining. As he was walking through the grass, Bob noticed a melon. He looked around and saw no one, so he grabbed it. He was so excited and ran off with it to show his friends. Once he got to the park bench he found his friends, but they gave him a rude look when he showed them the melon. "Where did you get that melon Bob?" asked one of his friends. "I grabbed it from the park," Bob said. His friends all laughed and started eating the melon. They were having so much fun! Bob smiled. He was glad he grabbed the melon and decided it was okay to be a little bit rude sometimes.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim wanted to go to the fun park. He asked his mom, "Can we go to the fun park today?" His mom said, "Yes, we can go if you help me first." Tim had to raise his toy box lid. He put all his toys inside. Then, he helped his mom clean the house. When they were done, his mom said, "Good job, Tim! Let's go to the fun park now." At the fun park, they saw a man selling tickets. Tim's mom bought two cheap tickets for them. They had a great time at the fun park. They played games, ate ice cream, and went on the rides. Tim was very happy, and they went home with big smiles on their faces.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a little bird named Bluey. Bluey was very frustrated. He wanted to organize his cage, but he did not know how. One day, Bluey met a wise old owl named Mr. Hoot. Bluey asked, "Mr. Hoot, can you help me organize my cage? It is messy, and I am frustrated." Mr. Hoot said, "Of course, Bluey! I will help you." Together, Bluey and Mr. Hoot started to clean the cage. They put all the toys in one corner and the food in another. Soon, the cage was nice and tidy. Bluey was very happy and thanked Mr. Hoot. From that day on, Bluey always kept his cage organized and was never frustrated again.
