Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Quick Cat and the Bear

Once upon a time, there was a quick cat named Tom. Tom liked to catch things. One day, Tom saw a busy bee. Tom wanted to catch the busy bee. Tom jumped and tried to catch the bee. The bee flew away. Tom tried again and again. The bee was very quick. Tom could not catch the bee. Then, something unexpected happened. The bee flew into a hole in a tree. Tom put his paw in the hole to catch the bee. But, instead of the bee, Tom caught a big, angry bear. The bear was not happy. Tom ran away fast. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little black dog named Spot. Spot was a good dog, but he was scared of big noises. One day, he went for a walk with his best friend, a little girl named Sue. As they walked, they saw a big hand on the ground. Spot was curious and wanted to see it. He went close to the hand and sniffed it. Suddenly, there was a loud noise that made Spot tremble. Sue saw that Spot was scared, so she ran to him and gave him a big hug. Sue held Spot's paw and they walked away from the big hand. They went back home and played together all day. Spot was happy to be with Sue and he didn't tremble anymore. They were the best of friends and had a great day.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to a party. It was his friend's birthday. There were many kids, and they played fun games. Tim saw a big cake on the table. The cake was perfect. It had pretty colors and lots of sweet stuff on it. Tim wanted to eat the cake right away, but he had to wait. The birthday girl, Lily, said, "Let's play a game first!" All the kids played a game where they had to clap their hands. They clapped fast, and they clapped slow. They had so much fun. Tim clapped very loud, and he was happy. Then, it was time to eat the cake. But when they looked at the table, the cake was gone! All the kids were sad. "Who took our cake?" asked Lily. Just then, they saw a little dog in the corner. The dog had cake all over its face. The dog had eaten the cake! Everyone laughed, and they had a fun time playing with the dog. In the end, they ate other yummy treats and had a great party.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to eat tasty food. One day, he saw a tasty cake on the table. But there was a razor near the cake. Tim knew that razors were not for kids. He wanted the cake, but he did not want to touch the razor. Tim had an idea. He called his mom to help him. His mom came and saw the razor. She was surprised. She did not know how the razor got there. She took the razor away and gave Tim the tasty cake. Tim was very happy. Tim said "thank you" to his mom. They enjoyed the tasty cake together. Then, they found out that Tim's dad put the razor there by mistake. They all laughed and had a good time. Tim's dad said "sorry" and "thank you" to Tim's mom for fixing the problem.

Lily and Ben liked to play in the park. They liked to swing, slide and run. One day, they saw some big equipment near the park. It was yellow and black and made loud noises. Lily and Ben wanted to see what it was. They ran to the equipment and climbed on it. It was very high and had many buttons and levers. Lily and Ben pushed and pulled them. The equipment started to move and shake. Lily and Ben were scared. They wanted to get off, but they did not know how. They shouted for help, but no one heard them. The equipment moved faster and faster. It went up and down and left and right. Lily and Ben held each other tight. They closed their eyes and hoped it would stop. But it did not stop. It crashed into a tree and broke. Lily and Ben fell to the ground and hurt themselves. They cried and cried. They wished they had not played with the equipment. It was too scary and dangerous. They learned a hard lesson. The end.
