Category: Beginner
Title: Flick the Lizard and the Forest

Once upon a time there was a tiny lizard named Flick. He lived in a big forest, and he loved to explore. One day he decided to explore even further and escape from the forest. He felt so brave and determined. Flick began crawling along tall grass and over fallen logs. He wanted to find his way out of the forest. Suddenly, he heard a loud noise coming from a nearby tree. Flick looked up and saw two lizards racing up the tree trunk! They were both so fast and competitive. Flick was so excited to have found other lizards to play with. He shouted to the lizards, “What are you doing? Let me join too!” The lizards stopped running and stared down at Flick. One of they said, “We’re trying to find a way out of the forest, can you help us?” Flick smiled up at the two lizards. He was so proud that they believed he could help them escape. He showed them the way out of the forest and the three lizards were able to find freedom beyond the trees.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw a big slide and he wanted to play on it. But the slide was wet from the rain. Tim felt sad. His mom said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will wipe the slide for you." She took a soft thing from her bag and wiped the slide until it was dry. Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Mom!" Tim played on the slide all day. He went up and down, up and down. He had so much fun. But soon, he felt tired. Tim told his mom, "I am tired, Mom. Can we go home now?" His mom said, "Yes, let's go home and rest." They went home and Tim took a long nap.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Sue. Sue liked to play with her toys. She had a cart that she loved very much. The cart was red and blue. Sue pushed the cart all around her house. One day, Sue met a boy named Tom. Tom was nice and wanted to play with Sue. Sue wanted to play with Tom too. They decided to play with Sue's cart. Sue said, "Let's share my cart and play together." Tom and Sue played with the cart all day. They put their toys in the cart and pushed it around. They laughed and had lots of fun. They learned that sharing made playing even more fun. And from that day on, Sue and Tom became best friends.

One day, a big rabbit and a small turtle wanted to see who was faster. They decided to have a race. The rabbit had a lot of speed, but the turtle was very slow. The rabbit was sure he would win. Before the race, the rabbit said to the turtle, "I will win because I am fast. You are slow, so I can even take a nap and still win." The turtle did not like the rabbit's joke. He said, "We will see who wins!" The race started, and the rabbit ran very fast. The turtle walked very slow. The rabbit was so far ahead, he thought he could take a nap. When he woke up, he saw the turtle near the finish line. The rabbit could not believe it! He ran as fast as he could, but the turtle won the race. The rabbit learned that being fast is not always enough.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful girl named Lily. She liked to ride her red bike. She rode it every day. Lily had a lot of fun riding her bike. One day, Lily saw mist near her home. The mist was soft and cool. Lily wanted to ride her bike in the mist. She put on her coat and went outside. Lily rode her bike in the mist. She felt happy and free. The mist tickled her face as she rode. After a while, the mist went away. Lily went back home with a big smile. She had a great day riding her bike in the mist.
