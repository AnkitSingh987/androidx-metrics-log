Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Frog and the Leaf

Once upon a time, there was a little frog named Tim. Tim lived in a pond with his best friend, Sam the fish. Tim loved to jump from one vine to another, while Sam would swim and watch him. One day, Sam felt jealous because he could not jump like Tim. "I wish I could jump too," said Sam. Tim thought for a moment and had an idea. "Let's find a big leaf, and I'll help you jump with me!" Sam was happy and they found a big leaf. Tim held Sam in the leaf and started to jump from vine to vine. Sam was excited and felt like he was jumping too. Suddenly, while they were jumping, a strong wind blew and took them high up in the sky. Tim and Sam were scared, but they held onto the leaf tightly. The wind carried them to a new pond where they met new friends. Tim and Sam had a great time with their new friends and learned that they could have fun together, even if they couldn't do the same things. And Sam was not jealous anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a big, old temple. It was very pretty. Many people liked to go there. Inside the temple, there were lots of games to play. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the temple with his mom and dad. Tim saw a fun game. The game was to throw a ball into a hole. Tim wanted to win a big prize. He tried very hard. He threw the ball one time, two times, three times. On the fourth time, the ball went into the hole! Tim was very happy. Tim won a big, soft teddy bear. He hugged the teddy bear and smiled. He showed his mom and dad his prize. They were so proud of him. Tim, his mom, and his dad went home. They had a great day at the old temple.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They were shopping for food. Tim saw a big steak and wanted it for dinner. He asked his mom, "Can we get the steak, please?" His mom said, "Yes, we can have it for dinner tonight." When they got home, Tim helped his mom cook the steak. They put it on a plate and sat down to eat. Tim took a seat at the table and was very happy. He felt successful because he helped his mom. As they ate the steak, Tim's mom said, "You did a great job helping me today." Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Mom. I like helping you." They both enjoyed their dinner and had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to eat cereal every morning. One day, Lily's mom gave her a big bowl of cereal. Lily was very happy. As Lily walked to the table, she did not see her toy on the floor. She stumbled on the toy and her cereal went everywhere. "Oh no!" cried Lily. Her mom came to help her clean up the mess. After cleaning up, Lily's mom said, "Let's put away the toy so it won't make you stumble again." Lily agreed and put the toy away. Then, she got a new bowl of cereal and ate it all up. She was very careful not to spill it this time.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a big dog named Max. Sue and Max liked to play in the park. They were always glad when they could go outside and have fun together. One sunny day, Sue and Max were playing in the park. They saw a big tree with a branch that was part way up. Sue wanted to get the branch, but she was too small. She asked Max, "Can you help me get the branch, Max?" Max barked and wagged his tail. Max jumped and tried to get the branch. He jumped and jumped, but he couldn't reach it. Then, a nice boy named Tom came to help. Tom was taller than Sue, so he could reach the branch. With a big smile, Tom said, "I can help you!" He pulled the branch down, and Sue was so glad. Sue, Max, and Tom played together in the park all day. They became good friends and had lots of fun.
