Category: Beginner
Title: The Red Wagon and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little red wagon. It had four big, wide wheels. The wagon loved to go on fun trips with its friend, a little boy named Tim. One sunny day, Tim and the wagon went to play in the park. They saw a big tree with many leaves. Tim wanted to see what was behind the tree. He said, "Let's peek!" So, they went close to the tree and peeked around it. They saw a cute, small dog playing with a ball. Tim and the wagon played with the dog all day. They had so much fun. When the sun went down, Tim and the wagon went back home. They were very happy and couldn't wait for their next adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a little spark. The spark lived in a rich forest with many trees and animals. Every day, the spark would play with his friends, the squirrel and the bird. One day, the squirrel said to the spark, "You are so small, but you can do big things. I encourage you to try something new!" The spark thought about it and decided to try to light up the sky. The spark jumped high into the sky and started to glow. The animals in the forest looked up and saw the spark shining like a star. They were all very surprised and happy. The squirrel and the bird cheered for the spark. The spark felt proud and happy that he could do something special for his friends. From that day on, the spark would light up the sky every night, and the animals in the forest would always remember the night their friend the spark became a star.

Tom is a boy who likes to run and play. He has a big dog named Spot who is his best friend. They like to go to the park and chase balls and sticks. One day, Tom and Spot are playing in the park when Spot sees a cat. Spot runs after the cat, but the cat is fast and jumps over a fence. Spot tries to jump too, but he gets stuck on the fence. He hurts his leg and cries. Tom hears Spot and runs to him. He sees that Spot has a cut on his leg and it is bleeding. He is scared and sad. He wants to help Spot. He looks around and sees a scarf on the ground. He picks it up and ties it around Spot's leg. He hopes it will stop the bleeding. He hugs Spot and tells him it will be okay. He calls his mom on his phone and tells her what happened. His mom says she will come right away and bring some medicine for Spot. She says Tom is a good boy and a strong boy for helping Spot. Tom waits with Spot until his mom comes. His mom gives Spot some medicine and puts a bandage on his leg. She says Spot will be fine, but he needs to rest for a few days. She thanks Tom for being brave and caring. She says they will go home and give Spot some treats and cuddles. Tom is happy that Spot is better. He tells Spot he loves him and he is his best friend. Spot licks Tom's face and wags his tail. They go home and snuggle on the couch. They are both strong and happy.

One day, a big dog named Max was very hungry. He saw a man with a steak on a plate. Max wanted the steak, so he gave the man a serious look. The man looked back at Max and said, "This is my steak, but I will give you some if you are a good dog." Max thought about it and decided to be good. He sat down and waved his tail. The man smiled and gave Max a piece of steak. Max was very happy and ate it quickly. But then, another dog named Sam came running and tried to take the steak from Max. Max growled and did not want to share. Sam looked sad and walked away. Max felt bad and remembered what the man had said. He called Sam back and shared the rest of the steak with him. They both ate and were happy. The man saw this and gave them more steak. Max learned that sharing is good and makes everyone happy.
