Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Thoughtful Girl and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful girl named Lily. She loved to dance and buy things for her friends. One day, she wanted to buy a big cake for her friend's birthday. She went to the shop to buy the cake. On the way to the shop, she saw a little boy who was sad. He had no friends to dance with. Lily had an idea. She asked the boy to come to her friend's birthday. The boy was very happy. At the party, everyone danced and had fun. But then, the cake was not there! Lily forgot to buy it! She was sad, but the little boy had a surprise. He had a big cake that he brought to the party! Everyone was happy, and they all danced and ate cake.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a kind man named Tom. Tom had a big box of gold. He liked to add more gold to his box every day. One day, Tom found a big rock of gold in the woods. Tom felt very happy but also uncomfortable. The gold rock was very heavy. He did not know how to take it home. He tried to carry it, but it was too big. So, he sat down and thought about what to do. A little girl named Lily saw Tom and asked, "Why are you sad?" Tom said, "I found this big gold rock, but I can't take it home. It's too heavy." Lily smiled and said, "I have an idea! We can roll the rock home together." Tom liked the idea. They rolled the big gold rock all the way to Tom's house. Tom added the gold rock to his box. He was happy and thanked Lily for her help. They became good friends and shared the gold with the whole village.

John was feeling nervous. He had to make a difficult decision. He decided to sit on the park bench where he could think in peace. As he sat there he started to come up with an escape plan. He thought of a way he could break free and do something he wanted to do. But just then he was interrupted by his mom calling him home. John reluctantly returned home, feeling nervous and still not sure how to escape. That night, an idea popped into his head. He quickly wrote it down and the next morning, he put his plan into action. John slipped away from his house and followed his plan. He felt a small sense of freedom. But then, to his surprise, he found his mom waiting for him. John was nervous, but as he looked at his mom he saw that her face was smiling. She said, "I knew you needed a break so I came to help you escape. We can have a day together and relax. Come. Let's go have fun!" John smiled. Sitting on the bench really helped him come up with a plan to escape!

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump and slide. They saw a big gray cloud in the sky. It looked like rain. "Let's go home, Lily," Tom said. "We don't want to get wet." "No, Tom, let's play some more," Lily said. "I like the rain. It makes puddles." Tom did not like the rain. He did not like puddles. He liked to be dry and warm. He saw his shadow on the ground. It was long and thin. "Look, Lily, our shadows are funny," Tom said. "They are like pictures of us." Lily looked at her shadow. It was short and round. She laughed. "They are funny, Tom," Lily said. "Let's make faces at them." Tom and Lily made faces at their shadows. They stuck out their tongues and crossed their eyes. They made their shadows do the same. The gray cloud got darker and bigger. It made a loud noise. It was thunder. Tom and Lily got scared. They did not like thunder. It was loud and scary. "Let's go home, Tom," Lily said. "I don't like the thunder. It makes me sad." "OK, Lily, let's go home," Tom said. "I don't like the thunder either. It makes me mad." Tom and Lily ran to their home. They held hands. They did not look at their shadows. They did not look at the gray cloud. They wanted to be safe and happy. They got to their home. They saw their mom and dad. They hugged them. "Are you OK, Tom and Lily?" their mom asked. "Did you have fun in the park?" "We had fun, mom," Tom said. "But we did not like the rain and the thunder. They were bad." "We did not like them, mom," Lily said. "They were mean." Their mom and dad smiled. They kissed them. "Don't worry, Tom and Lily," their dad said. "The rain and the thunder will go away soon. The sun will come back. And so will your shadows." Tom and Lily smiled. They liked the sun. They liked their shadows. They liked their mom and dad. They were glad they remained together.
