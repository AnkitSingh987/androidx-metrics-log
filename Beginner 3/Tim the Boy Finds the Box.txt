Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Box

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big, tall flag. The flag was red, blue, and white. Tim thought the flag was crazy because it moved in the wind. Tim wanted to touch the flag. He tried to jump, but he could not reach it. So, Tim had an idea. He found a big box and put it under the flag. He stood on the box and leaned to touch the flag. Just as Tim touched the flag, a big wind came. The wind blew the box away, and Tim fell down. The crazy flag wrapped around him, and he could not get out. Tim was sad and scared. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a poor little boy named Tim. He lived in a small house with his mom and dad. They did not have much, but they were happy. One day, Tim saw a big pile of waste near his house. He thought, "I can get something to clean this up!" So, he went to his mom and said, "Mom, can I get a broom to clean the waste?" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim, you can." Tim got the broom and started to clean up the waste. It was hard work, but he was happy to help. After some time, the waste was all gone. Tim's mom and dad were so proud of him. They all hugged and laughed, and their little house was clean and happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He lived in a small house with his mother. One day, his mother asked him to help her clean the house. Tom did not want to help. He said, "No, I refuse to help you, Mother." His mother was sad but she kept on cleaning. Tom went outside to play. As he played, the sky started to get dark. It was not light outside anymore. Tom was scared of the dark. He wanted to go back home. When Tom got home, he saw that his mother was very tired from cleaning all by herself. He felt bad for not helping her. His mother was too tired to make dinner. Tom and his mother had to go to bed hungry that night. Tom learned that he should always help his mother when she asks for help.

One day, a boy named Tim got a mysterious invitation. It was for a fun party. Tim was very happy. He told his mom, "I got an invitation for a party!" His mom said, "That's great, let's go!" At the party, there were many games. Tim saw a game called "Shoot the Ball". He wanted to play. He picked up a ball and tried to shoot it into a hole. But the ball did not go in. Tim was sad. He tried again and again, but the ball would not go in. Then, a girl named Sue came over. She said, "I know a secret way to shoot the ball." Tim was curious. Sue showed Tim how to hold the ball and throw it just right. Tim tried Sue's way and the ball went in! Tim was so happy. He said, "Thank you, Sue!" They played together and had lots of fun at the party.

Lily was sleeping in her bed. She had a big teddy bear that she hugged every night. She loved her teddy bear very much. It was soft and brown and had a red bow. One morning, Lily heard a noise. She opened her eyes and saw her brother Tom in her room. He had a roll of tape in his hand. He was smiling. "Tom, what are you doing?" Lily asked. "I have a surprise for you, Lily. Come and see!" Tom said. He took Lily's hand and led her to the living room. There, Lily saw a big cardboard box. It had holes and windows cut out of it. It looked like a house. "Look, Lily, I made you a house for your teddy bear. You can play with him inside. Do you like it?" Tom said. Lily was very happy. She hugged Tom and thanked him. She thought he was a very nice brother. She put her teddy bear in the box and went inside with him. The box was cozy and fun. It had tape on the walls and the roof to hold it together. Lily and Tom played with the teddy bear in the house for a long time. They pretended he was a king and they were his servants. They gave him food and water and told him stories. They laughed and had fun. Lily thought her teddy bear was adorable. She thought her brother was adorable too. She was glad he made her a house for her teddy bear. She felt very lucky. She decided to share her teddy bear with Tom sometimes. She knew he liked him too. They were a happy family.
