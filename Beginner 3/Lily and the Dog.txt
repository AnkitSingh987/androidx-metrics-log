Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Dog

Once upon a time, a girl named Lily wanted to pack her bag. She wanted to go to her grandma's house. In her bag, she put a fake toy, a red ball, and a small plant. On her way, she met a big dog. The dog looked at Lily's bag and saw the fake toy. The dog wanted to play with the toy. Lily was scared, but she gave the toy to the dog. The dog was happy and played with the toy. Then, the dog saw the small plant in Lily's bag. The dog sniffed the plant and did something unexpected. The dog started to dance! Lily laughed and clapped her hands. The dog and Lily became friends. They went to grandma's house together, and they all had fun.

Once upon a time, two children were playing in the park. The first child, Anna, saw a fan lying on the grass and said to her friend, "Let's organize this fan so someone can use it again!" Her friend agreed and together they placed the fan in a nearby tree. But as they were admiring their work, a grown-up came and told them, "That fan is broken. You can't make it work no matter how hard you try, so you should put it back where you found it." Anna was sad that her work was for nothing, but she obeyed the grown-up and the two children took the fan back where they had found it and put it on the ground. The moral of the story is: You can't fix something broken no matter how hard you try. Anna had to accept that her efforts were in vain and quite unsatisfying, but she had learned an important lesson - organizing something broken doesn't make it right.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play outside. They have a big rope. They can swing on the rope or jump over the rope. One day, they find a gray cat. The cat is sad. It has no home. Lily and Ben want to help the cat. They give it some milk and some fish. The cat is happy. It purrs and rubs against them. "Can we keep the cat?" Lily asks Ben. "I don't know," Ben says. "We have to ask our moms and dads." They take the cat to Lily's house. They show it to Lily's mom. Lily's mom smiles. She likes the cat. "You can keep the cat, but you have to take good care of it," she says. "You have to feed it, clean it, and love it." "Yay!" Lily and Ben say. They hug the cat. They name it Gray. They spend the rest of the day playing with Gray. They swing on the rope with Gray. They jump over the rope with Gray. They cuddle with Gray. They are very happy. Gray is happy too. He has a new home and new friends.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park to collect pretty rocks. She was very excited to start her new rock collection. As she walked, she found a big, shiny rock that she liked a lot. She put it in her bag and kept looking for more. "Hi Lucy!" said her friend, Tim. "What are you doing?" Lucy told him about her rock collection and showed him the shiny rock. Tim was impatient to help, so they both started to look for more rocks together. Suddenly, the shiny rock in Lucy's bag started to move! It wasn't a rock at all. It was a little turtle! Lucy and Tim laughed and decided to help the turtle find its way back to the pond. They had a fun day at the park and made a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with her ball. One day, she was playing with her ball near a big bin. The bin was deep and full of old things. Kitty's ball went into the bin. She was sad and started to panic. She wanted her ball back, but the bin was too deep for her to reach. Kitty needed help to get her ball out of the bin. Kitty's friend, a dog named Buddy, came to help her. Buddy was strong and tall. He looked into the deep bin and saw the ball. Buddy put his paw into the bin and got the ball out for Kitty. Kitty was so happy and thanked Buddy. They played with the ball together and had lots of fun.
