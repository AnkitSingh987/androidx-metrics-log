Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Dress

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a purple box in her room. She was very happy to see it. She opened the box and found a pretty dress inside. The dress was also purple. Lucy wanted to dance in her new dress. Lucy put on the dress and went to the living room. She saw her mom and said, "Mom, look at my new dress! Can I dance in it?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, Lucy. You can dance in your new dress." Lucy danced and danced in her purple dress. She was so happy. Then, she saw a letter on the table. It was for her. She opened the letter and it said, "I hope you like the purple dress. Let's dance together soon. Love, Grandma." Lucy knew that Grandma had mailed her the dress. She couldn't wait to dance with her grandma.

Lily and Sam are friends. They like to play with toys and watch videos. One day, Lily brings a new video to Sam's house. It is about a big dragon and a brave knight. "Can we watch this video?" Lily asks Sam. Sam looks at the video and feels fearful. He does not like dragons. They are scary and loud. He thinks the dragon will eat the knight. "I don't want to watch this video," Sam says. "It is too scary for me." Lily is surprised. She likes dragons. They are cool and strong. She thinks the knight will fight the dragon. "It is not scary, it is fun," Lily says. "The knight will win. He has a sword and a shield." Sam shakes his head. He does not want to see the sword and the shield. He thinks they will hurt the dragon. "No, no, no," Sam says. "The dragon will win. He has fire and wings." Lily and Sam argue. They both want to own the video. They both want to watch what they like. "Give me the video," Lily says. "No, it is mine," Sam says. They pull the video from each other. They are angry and sad. Then, Sam's mom comes in. She sees Lily and Sam fighting. She is not happy. "What are you doing?" she asks. "Why are you fighting over a video?" Lily and Sam stop pulling. They look at Sam's mom. They feel ashamed. "We want to watch different things," Lily says. "I like dragons, he likes knights," Sam says. Sam's mom smiles. She has an idea. "Why don't you watch the video together?" she says. "Maybe you can learn something new. Maybe you can see that dragons and knights are not so different. Maybe you can be friends with both." Lily and Sam think about it. They are curious. They want to learn something new. They want to be friends with both. "Okay," Lily says. "Okay," Sam says. They give each other the video. They hug and say sorry. They go to the couch and watch the video together. They are not fearful anymore. They are happy.

One day, a frog and a duck went to the pond. The frog was green, and the duck was white. They liked to sit and talk by the pond. The frog said, "I am tired. Let's sit and rest." The duck agreed, "Yes, I am tired too. Let's sit." As they sat by the pond, they saw a big fish jump out of the water. The fish was blue and shiny. The frog and the duck were surprised. They had never seen a fish like that before. The blue fish said, "Hello, friends! I am a magic fish. I can make one wish come true for each of you." The frog and the duck were very happy. The frog wished to be a big frog, and the duck wished to be a fast duck. The magic fish made their wishes come true. Now, the frog and the duck were not tired anymore. They played and had fun at the pond all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a hungry little boy named Tim. He loved to eat oranges. One day, he found a big, shiny orange under a tree. Tim was very happy. Before he could eat the orange, his mom told him to clean it. So, Tim went to the sink and washed the orange with water. The orange was now clean and ready to eat. Tim sat down and ate the tasty orange. It was sweet and juicy. Tim was not hungry anymore. He smiled and thanked the tree for the yummy orange.
