Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Ladder

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to go on the slide, the swings and the seesaw. But they did not like the big zip line. It was too high and too fast for them. One day, a new boy came to the park. His name was Max. He was very brave and he wanted to try the zip line. He climbed up the ladder and grabbed the handle. He looked at Tom and Lily and said, "Do you want to go with me? It's fun!" Tom and Lily shook their heads. They were frightened of the zip line. They did not want to go with Max. But Max did not listen. He urged them to come with him. He said, "Don't be scared. It's easy. Just hold on tight and let go. Come on, it's your turn!" Max pushed Tom towards the ladder. Tom did not want to go, but he did not want to look silly in front of Max. He climbed up the ladder and took the handle. He looked down and saw how far it was. He felt his heart beat fast. He wanted to go back, but Max was behind him. He said, "Ready, set, go!" Max let go of the handle and pushed Tom. Tom screamed as he flew through the air. He felt the wind in his face and the zip in his ears. He was very scared. He closed his eyes and hoped it would be over soon. Lily watched Tom on the zip line. She felt sorry for him. She did not want to go on the zip line. She did not care what Max said. She ran away from the ladder and hid behind a tree. She waited for Tom to come back. She hoped he was okay. She did not like Max. He was mean and bossy. She wished he would go away.

One day, a big zebra named Zara went for a walk. She saw a little bird and said, "Hi, bird! Want to go with me?" The little bird said, "Yes, let's go!" They walked and walked. Soon, they saw a big tree with a big hole. Zara said, "Let's look in the hole." The little bird flew up to look. But then, the little bird said, "I see something big inside!" Zara and the little bird were scared, but they wanted to know what was inside. Zara put her head in the hole and saw a big mirror. She laughed and said, "It's just me! I am the big thing in the hole!" The little bird laughed too, and they went home happy.

Mummy was teaching Toby how to make a sausage. She gave him a big bowl and showed him how to mix the ingredients together. First, Toby added the soft flour. Then, he added some herbs and spices. Finally, he added some sausage meat. Mummy said, 'Now mix it all together until it's soft.' Toby mixed and mixed and mixed until the contents of the bowl were soft. Mummy said, 'Well done Toby! Now, I'll show you how to make the sausages.' She showed Toby how to make a sausage using a special sausage-making tool. Toby watched intently as she worked. Once Mummy had finished, she said, 'Now it's your turn!' Toby carefully made his own sausage, just like Mummy had taught him. Mummy smiled and hugged Toby. 'Well done! You made the best sausage I've ever seen!' Toby was so proud of himself.

One day, a girl named Sue went for a walk. She saw a big orange ball in the sky. It was the sun. She liked the sun because it made her feel warm and happy. Sue looked around and saw a blue bird sitting on a tree. The blue bird sang a pretty song. As Sue walked, she found a big blue box. She was very curious. Sue opened the box and saw a little orange cat. The cat looked at her with big eyes. Sue was surprised and happy to find a new friend. Sue took the orange cat home. She played with the cat all day. But then, the cat started to change. The cat grew bigger and bigger. Sue looked at the cat and saw it was not a cat at all! It was a big orange tiger! Sue was scared, but the tiger was nice. They became best friends and had many fun days together.
