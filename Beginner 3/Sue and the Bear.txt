Category: Beginner
Title: Sue and the Bear

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a big room, which she called her chamber. Sue loved her chamber very much. It was full of toys and pretty things. Her chamber had a mild smell, like flowers from the garden. One day, Sue's mom asked her to bring her teddy bear to her chamber. Sue was happy to do it. She picked up her teddy bear and walked to her chamber. She put the teddy bear on her bed and gave it a big hug. Sue played with her teddy bear all day in her chamber. They had so much fun. When it was time for bed, Sue's mom came to the chamber and said goodnight. Sue hugged her teddy bear and went to sleep, dreaming of more fun days in her chamber.

One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to have a fancy lunch. She asked her mom, "Can we have a fancy lunch today, please?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, we can." Lily and her mom put on pretty dresses and set the table with nice plates. They made yummy sandwiches and cut them into fun shapes. They also had colorful fruits and a big glass of milk. When they sat down to eat, Lily's mom told her a funny story about a cat and a dog who had a fancy lunch too. Lily laughed and enjoyed her special lunch with her mom. They had a great time together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big black cat. The cat was playing with a fake toy mouse. Tim wanted to play with the cat too. He walked closer to the cat. "Hi, cat! Can I play with you?" Tim asked. The cat looked at Tim and said, "Yes, you can play with me and my fake mouse." Tim was happy. He ran to the cat, but he did not see a big rock in his way. Tim stumbled on the rock and fell down. The cat ran to Tim to see if he was okay. "Are you okay, Tim?" asked the cat. Tim said, "Yes, I am okay. I just stumbled on the rock." They both laughed and continued to play with the fake mouse. They had a fun day together.

Once upon a time there was a tree with a big leaf. It was a happy leaf—bright and green! But one day, the leaf got old and gray. So the tree found a new leaf and decided to replace it. The new leaf was full of life. It was a beautiful yellow and blew in the wind. The old gray leaf was sad. It wanted to stay and have fun with the tree. But it knew it was time to go. So the old leaf gracefully let go, and the new leaf took its place. The new leaf waved goodbye and the tree smiled with happiness.

One day, a quiet girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a boy named Tim. Tim had a big bow. He was trying to make the bow work, but he did not know how. Lily wanted to help him. Lily walked to Tim and said, "Do you need help with your bow?" Tim looked at her and gave a little shrug. He did not know what to do. Lily took the bow and showed him how to use it. Now, Tim could play with the bow. He smiled and said, "Thank you, Lily!" They played together in the park all day. They became good friends and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big family. They lived in a cozy house. In the house, there was a dishwasher. The dishwasher helped the family wash their plates and cups. One day, Mom said, "The dishwasher is ready. Can you complete putting the dishes in?" Dad smiled and said, "Yes, I can do it." He put the plates and cups in the dishwasher. The dishwasher made a soft sound while it washed the dishes. When the dishwasher was done, the family took out the clean plates and cups. They were happy because they could eat their food on clean plates. The dishwasher made their life easy and fun.
