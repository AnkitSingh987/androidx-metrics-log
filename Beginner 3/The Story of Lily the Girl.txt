Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of Lily the Girl

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and her cat, named Whiskers. Lily loved to play with Whiskers all day long. They would run, jump, and play hide and seek. One hot day, Lily and Whiskers were playing in the backyard. They saw a big, fierce dog next door. The dog was barking loudly. Lily was scared and Whiskers was scared too. They did not want the dog to see them. Lily had an idea. She whispered to Whiskers, "Let's go inside the house and play there." Whiskers liked the idea. They quietly went inside the house and played with their toys. The fierce dog did not see them. Lily and Whiskers were happy and safe. They played together all day long.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big dream. He wanted to cook the most incredible steak for his family. One day, Tim asked his mom, "Can I cook a steak for us?" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. Let's go to the store and get a steak for you to cook." At the store, Tim picked the perfect steak. He was so excited to cook it. When they got home, Tim's mom helped him cook the steak. It smelled so good! When the steak was done, Tim shared it with his family. They all took a bite and said, "Wow, this is incredible!" Tim was so happy that his dream came true. From that day on, Tim knew he could make his dreams come true if he tried hard and believed in himself. And his family would always remember the incredible steak that Tim cooked.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim was a tough bird who liked to fly high in the sky. One day, Tim flew by a big room with lots of kids. This was a classroom. Tim saw a girl named Sue. Sue was sad. Tim wanted to help Sue. He flew into the classroom and said, "Hi Sue, why are you sad?" Sue looked at Tim and said, "I lost my toy. I can't find it." Tim wanted to help Sue find her toy. He flew all around the classroom, looking high and low. Suddenly, Tim found the toy hiding in a big box! Sue was so happy, she hugged Tim. The kids in the classroom clapped and cheered. From that day on, Tim and Sue were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was excited to make a glove. He wanted to make the glove for his mom. She was always cold. Tim thought the glove would make her happy. But Tim had a problem. His dog, Max, wanted to play. Max took the glove away from Tim. Max ran and Tim chased him. Tim was sad because he could not make the glove for his mom. Tim had an idea. He gave Max a toy to play with. Max was happy with the toy. Tim got the glove back. He finished making the glove for his mom. Tim's mom was happy and warm. Tim felt good that he could help his mom.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy little town. In this town, there was a long line of ants. The ants were very busy and always working. One day, a big bug came to the town. The big bug was not nice. The big bug wanted the ants to surrender and stop working. The big bug said, "You ants must give up and not work anymore!" The ants did not want to surrender. They wanted to keep their town tidy and work together. So, the ants talked to the big bug. They said, "We will not give up. We like to work and keep our town tidy." The big bug saw how the ants worked together and how happy they were. The big bug decided to be nice and help the ants. They all worked together to keep the town tidy and the line of ants kept working happily ever after.
