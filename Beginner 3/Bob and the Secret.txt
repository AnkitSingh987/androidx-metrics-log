Category: Beginner
Title: Bob and the Secret

Once upon a time, a wealthy man named Bob wanted to learn a secret. He asked a bird, 'What should I do to know the secret?' But the bird only said, 'Find a sign at the top of the mountain.' Bob wanted to find the sign, so he went on a long journey to the top of the mountain. It was hard, but he kept going. Along the way, he urged his friends to join him, but none of them would come. When he reached the top of the mountain, Bob saw a big sign that said 'Risk it all!' Bob was so excited and he started to think of all the things he could do with a secret. He decided to take the risk and he jumped off the cliff. At first he thought he was falling forever, but then suddenly he felt something catch him. He looked down and saw a big eagle had caught him in its talons. Bob knew the secret he had been searching for was within his grasp. He urged the eagle to take him to the secret and it did without hesitation. Finally, Bob and the eagle arrived at the secret. It was an amazing land, full of riches and treasures beyond his wildest dreams. Bob was so happy that he had risked it all for the secret. With his newfound wealth, he could do anything he wanted and live a life of luxury.

Once upon a time, there was a restless little boy named Tim. He loved to attach things to other things. One day, Tim saw a big cupboard in the kitchen. He had a great idea! He found a lot of cups and plates. Tim began to attach them to the cupboard with sticky tape. He thought it looked very pretty. But then, he heard his mom coming. His mom saw the cupboard and was surprised. She told Tim that it was not the right way to use the cupboard. Tim felt sad. But his mom had an idea. She showed him how to put the cups and plates inside the cupboard. Tim learned how to use the cupboard the right way, and he was not restless anymore.

One day, a lively dog named Spot saw a boy playing with a baseball. The boy threw the ball high in the air. Spot wanted to play too, so he ran fast to catch the ball. Spot jumped and tried to dive for the ball, but he missed it. The boy saw Spot and said, "Hey, do you want to play with me?" Spot barked and wagged his tail. The boy threw the ball again, and this time, Spot caught it. They played and laughed together. As they played, a big bird flew down and took the baseball from Spot's mouth. The boy and Spot looked up in surprise. They didn't know what to do. Then, the bird dropped the ball right back to them. They laughed and kept playing. The bird had joined their game.

Lily and Tom were going to fly in an aeroplane with their mum and dad. They were very excited to see the big blue sky and the white clouds. They packed their bags and got in the car. But when they got to the airport, they saw a long line of people. They had to wait and wait and wait. Lily and Tom felt bored and hungry and sleepy. They wanted to go on the aeroplane now. "Mum, when can we go on the aeroplane?" Lily asked. "Soon, my dear, soon. We just have to check in our bags and show our tickets," Mum said. "But I'm tired of waiting. I want to fly now," Tom said. "I know, my love, I know. But we have to be patient and follow the rules," Dad said. Lily and Tom tried to be patient, but they couldn't. They started to cry and whine and kick their legs. They made a lot of noise and people looked at them. Mum and Dad felt embarrassed and tried to calm them down. "Shh, shh, it's okay. We'll be on the aeroplane soon. Look, there it is. It's big and shiny and has wings. Do you want to see it?" Mum said, pointing to the window. Lily and Tom stopped crying and looked at the window. They saw the aeroplane and their eyes grew wide. It was big and shiny and had wings. They wanted to see it up close. They wanted to go inside it. They felt curious and happy. "Wow, look at the aeroplane. It's so cool. Can we go on it now?" Lily asked. "Yes, yes, we can. Come on, let's go. The line is moving. Hold my hand and follow me," Dad said. Lily and Tom held their mum and dad's hands and followed them. They walked to the counter and gave their bags and tickets. They walked to the gate and showed their passports. They walked to the bridge and boarded the aeroplane. They found their seats and buckled their belts. They looked out the window and saw the sky and the clouds. They smiled and clapped their hands. "We're on the aeroplane. We're going to fly. Yay!" Lily and Tom said. Mum and Dad smiled and hugged them. They were glad they stopped weeping. They were glad they were on the aeroplane. They were ready to fly.
