Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Radio

One day, a boy named Tim found a big box. Inside the box, there was a radio. Tim was very happy. He took the radio and showed it to his mom. "Look, mom! I found a radio!" he said. His mom was calm and smiled. Tim played with the radio all day. He liked the music and the funny voices. But one day, while Tim was playing with the radio, he heard a strange voice. The voice said, "Hello, Tim! I am a talking radio!" Tim was very surprised. He did not know that radios could talk. The radio said, "I can do a trick. I can make you dance!" Tim did not believe the radio. But then, the radio played a song and Tim started to dance. Tim danced and danced. He could not stop. He danced in front of his friends and they laughed. Tim felt embarrass. He asked the radio, "Please, stop the music!" The radio stopped, and Tim stopped dancing. He learned that sometimes, surprises can be both fun and embarrassing.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved the color pink. One day, Sue's mom took her to the mall to buy a new toy. At the mall, Sue saw a big, pink ball. She wanted it so much. But, there was a problem. Another little boy named Tim saw the pink ball too. Tim and Sue both wanted the pink ball. They did not want to share. Sue was sad, and Tim was mad. Then, Sue's mom had an idea. She said they could both play with the pink ball at the mall. Sue and Tim took turns playing with the ball. They had lots of fun. In the end, Sue and Tim became friends and learned to share.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little wild mouse. The mouse liked to play and run all day. One day, the mouse saw a big cat outside the house. The cat looked at the mouse and wanted to catch it. The wild mouse was scared but dared to go out and play. The big cat saw the mouse and started to chase it. The mouse ran as fast as it could, but the cat was faster. They ran and ran, but the mouse could not get away from the cat. In the end, the wild mouse was too tired to run anymore. The big cat caught the little mouse. The mouse should not have dared to go out and play with the big cat. The wild mouse was not careful, and the story had a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a big bookcase. It was very serious. It had many colorful books on it. The bookcase lived in a room where kids came to read. One day, a little girl named Anna came to the room. She saw the serious bookcase and wanted to pick a book to read. She found a book about a funny cat. Anna liked the book and wanted to take it home. Anna asked the bookcase, "Can I take this book home?" The serious bookcase thought for a moment and then accepted. "Yes, you can take it home, but please bring it back when you are done," it said. Anna was very happy and promised to bring the book back. She went home to read her new book and had a great time.

One day, a big wolf came to a small house. In the house, there was a soft bed. The wolf was very tired and wanted to sleep on the soft bed. But a small boy lived in the house too. The small boy said, "You cannot sleep on my soft bed, wolf." The wolf was sad, but he had an idea. "Please, let me sleep on your soft bed. I will lead you to a big treasure if you let me sleep on your bed," said the wolf. The small boy agreed and let the wolf sleep on his soft bed. The next day, the wolf and the boy went to find the treasure. The wolf lead the boy to a big tree. But there was no treasure. The wolf laughed and ran away. The small boy was very sad and never saw the wolf again.
