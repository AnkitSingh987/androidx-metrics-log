Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog and the Sun

Once, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to run fast. He would run and play all day in the sun. He would try to catch his tail, but it was not easy. One day, Spot saw a big red ball. He wanted to play with it. He tried to push the ball with his nose, but it was too big. Spot did not give up. He tried again and again. Finally, Spot pushed the ball very fast. It rolled and rolled in the sun. Spot was so happy. He played with the big red ball all day. And at the end of the day, he caught his tail.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy monster. He was always grumpy and wanted to be alone. One day, the monster saw two kids playing. The monster was too shy to talk to them, but he still wanted to make some noise. So he started to tease them! He yelled out, "Boooo". The kids looked up in surprise. But then the monster had a different idea. He said,”Come here, I have something to show you!” The kids were very curious and went to the monster. He showed them the most beautiful sight. Its colours were so bright and amazing! The kids praised the monster and thanked him. The monster felt much less grumpy and was soon the best of friends with the kids. The End.

Once upon a time, in a small country, there lived a boy named Tim and a girl named Sara. They were best friends. They liked to play together and gather pretty rocks and flowers. One day, Tim found a big, shiny rock. He showed it to Sara and said, "Look what I found!" Sara felt jealous because she wanted a shiny rock too. So, she went to look for one. Sara looked and looked but could not find a shiny rock. She felt sad. Then, Tim saw her and asked, "Why are you sad?" Sara told him, "I want a shiny rock like yours." Tim thought for a moment and then said, "Let's share the shiny rock. We can both have it." Sara smiled and agreed. They played together with the shiny rock and were happy again. From that day on, they learned to share and not be jealous of each other.

Once upon a time, there was a gentle cat named Tim. Tim liked to nap all day. One day, while Tim was napping, he heard a noise. He opened his eyes and saw a little boy named Sam. Sam said, "Hi Tim! Let's play!" Tim was sleepy, but he wanted to be nice, so he played with Sam. They played with a ball and had lots of fun. But then, the ball went near a big tree. As they went closer, they saw the ball increase and become a big ball! Tim and Sam were very surprised. They played with the big ball and had even more fun. After playing, they both took a long nap together.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy cat named Max. Max did not like to play with the other cats. One day, Max went for a walk. He walked a long way from his home. On his way, Max met a little bird. The bird said, "Hi Max, do you want to play with me?" Max looked at the bird and said, "No, I am grumpy. I don't want to play. I want to be alone." The bird looked sad but said, "Okay, Max. I hope you feel better soon." Max kept walking and soon he got tired. He wanted to go back home but he was lost. He did not know the way home. Max started to cry. The little bird saw Max crying and came to help. The bird said, "Don't cry, Max. I can help you find your way home." Max looked at the bird and said, "Thank you, little bird." The bird showed Max the way back to his home. When they got there, Max was not grumpy anymore. He said to the bird, "You are a good friend. I am sorry I was grumpy before. Let's play together now." The bird was happy, and they played together all day long. From that day on, Max was never grumpy again.
