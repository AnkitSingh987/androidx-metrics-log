Category: Beginner
Title: Sam and the Bird

One day, a big, hairy dog named Sam went for a walk. He walked by a small stream. The water in the stream was cool and clear. Sam was thirsty, so he drank some water. He felt happy. Sam saw a bone near the stream. He picked it up with his mouth. He wanted to bury it, so no one would take it. He dug a hole with his paws and put the bone in it. Then, he covered the hole with dirt. Sam wagged his tail. A little bird saw Sam bury the bone. The bird said, "Why did you bury the bone, Sam?" Sam said, "I want to keep it safe. Now, no one can take it." The bird and Sam became friends. They played by the stream all day.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Joe and Sam. Joe was a kind-hearted giraffe, and Sam was a mischievous raccoon. One day, Joe and Sam decided to go for a walk near a pond. As they looked around, Sam noticed a small hole near the pond, and began to dig it. Joe tried to stop him, but Sam would not listen. After some time, the hole became bigger and deeper. Joe told Sam it was dangerous, but Sam just laughed and kept digging. Suddenly, Sam felt the ground shake and heard a loud rumble. He was scared, but it was too late. Water began to fill the hole, and the two friends were stuck in the miserable water. Joe tried to calm Sam down, but Sam was too embarrassed to listen. Then, two kind turtles came and helped the two friends out of the hole. They brought them to the pond, and helped them wash away the mud and dirt. Joe and Sam were so thankful and promised to be more careful next time.

One day, a jolly girl named Lily went to play outside. She wore her favorite dress with pretty flowers on it. Lily loved to twirl and dance in her dress. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. It was a perfect day for her to have fun. Lily saw her friend, Tom, who lived next door. Tom was playing with a big, red ball. "Hi, Tom!" said Lily. "Can I play with you?" Tom smiled and said, "Yes, let's play catch!" They laughed and played, tossing the ball back and forth. As they played, Lily felt her dress get caught on a branch. She tried to pull it free, but it wouldn't let go. "Oh no!" she cried. Tom came over to help her. He pulled the branch, and Lily's dress came free. But when he did, the ball dropped to the ground. They both laughed and picked up the ball. Then they went back to playing, happy and jolly once again.

Jenny loved to paint. She had many colors and brushes and papers. She liked to paint flowers and animals and stars. But her favorite thing to paint was hearts. She painted big hearts and small hearts, red hearts and blue hearts, happy hearts and sad hearts. She painted hearts for her mom and dad and brother and friends. One day, she wanted to paint a heart for her teacher, Miss Lee. She found a nice white paper and a bright pink color. She started to paint a big heart in the middle of the paper. She was very careful and happy. But then, she heard a loud noise. It was her brother, Tom. He was playing with his cars and trucks. He made them go zoom and crash and beep and honk. He was very noisy and messy. He did not care about Jenny's painting. "Tom, please be quiet!" Jenny said. "I am painting a heart for Miss Lee. It is very important." "I don't care about your heart," Tom said. "I care about my cars and trucks. They are more fun and cool. Your heart is boring and silly." He grabbed one of his trucks and threw it at Jenny's paper. It hit the heart and made a big hole. It also splashed pink paint all over Jenny's face and clothes. Jenny was very sad and angry. She started to cry. "Tom, you are mean and bad!" Jenny said. "You ruined my heart for Miss Lee. You are not my brother anymore." She ran to her mom and told her what Tom did. Mom was very sorry and mad. She scolded Tom and made him say sorry to Jenny. She also helped Jenny clean up and make a new heart for Miss Lee. She gave Jenny a hug and a kiss. "Jenny, I am proud of you," Mom said. "You are very sweet and kind. You have a big heart. Miss Lee will love your heart." Jenny smiled and thanked Mom. She finished her heart and put it in her backpack. She was ready to go to school and give it to Miss Lee. She hoped Miss Lee would like it. She also hoped Tom would be nicer to her. She still loved him, even if he was noisy and mean.
