Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Ant and the Garden

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there lived a tiny ant named Tim. Tim loved to be organized. Every day, he would try to make his home neat and tidy. All the other bugs in the garden were messy, but not Tim. One hot day, Tim saw a big tree with lots of shade. He thought it would be a great place to rest. He invited all his friends to join him under the tree. They played and laughed in the shade, away from the hot sun. When it was time to go home, Tim's friends saw how organized his home was. They liked it and wanted their homes to be organized too. They asked Tim to help them, and he did. Soon, all the bugs in the garden had neat and tidy homes. The moral of the story is that being organized can make everyone's life better and happier.

Once upon a time, there were two twins named Tim and Tom. They lived in a small house with their mom and dad. They loved to play with their toy cars and run around in the yard. One day, Tim and Tom found a small bird that was stuck in a net. They wanted to help the bird, so they tried to release it from the net. But they didn't know how to do it, and they were ignorant about how to help the bird. They didn't want to hurt the bird, so they called their mom to help them. Their mom came and showed them how to gently untangle the bird from the net. They carefully released the bird, and it flew away happily. Tim and Tom learned that it's important to ask for help when you don't know how to do something. They were happy that they could help the bird and learned a valuable lesson.

Once upon a time, there was a big, ugly fish. This fish was sad because no one wanted to be friends with him. He tried to unite with the other fish, but they swam away. One day, the ugly fish met a group of fish called the navy. The navy fish were strong and fast. They said to the ugly fish, "We will be your friends if you help us." The ugly fish was happy and said, "Yes, I will help you!" The navy fish wanted to take food from the little fish. They told the ugly fish to scare the little fish so they could take the food. The ugly fish did what the navy fish said. He scared the little fish and the navy fish took the food. The little fish were very sad and hungry. In the end, the ugly fish was still sad because he knew it was bad to take from others. The moral of the story is that it's not good to hurt others, even if it means making friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom had a toy spear. The spear was dull. He liked to play with it all day. One day, Tom went to the park. He played with his dull spear. He threw it high in the sky. But the spear did not come down. Tom was sad. A big bird had the spear. The bird gave it back to Tom. The spear was not dull anymore. It was shiny! Tom was happy. The bird was his new friend. They played together every day.

Sara and Ben like to collect shells on the beach. They go with Mom and Dad every summer. They have a big bucket to put the shells in. They look for different colors and shapes. One day, Sara finds a shiny pink shell. She is very happy. She shows it to Ben. "Look, Ben, look! I found a pretty shell!" she says. Ben looks at the shell. He likes it too. He wants to have it. He tries to take it from Sara. "No, Ben, no! It is mine!" Sara says. She holds the shell tight. Mom sees them. She comes over. "What is going on, Sara and Ben?" she asks. "Sara has a nice shell and I want it!" Ben says. "Sara found the shell and it is hers. You have to be patient, Ben. Maybe you will find another nice shell soon. Or you can ask Sara to share with you nicely," Mom says. Sara thinks. She likes her shell, but she also likes Ben. She decides to share. She gives the shell to Ben. "Here, Ben, you can have it. But you have to be careful. It is very special," she says. Ben smiles. He is happy. He thanks Sara. He puts the shell in the bucket. "Thank you, Sara. You are very kind. Maybe we can find more shells together," he says. Sara smiles too. She hugs Ben. "You are welcome, Ben. You are a good brother. Yes, let's find more shells together," she says. Mom is proud of them. She gives them a kiss. "You are both very good. I love you. Now let's go and have some ice cream," she says. Sara and Ben agree. They love ice cream. They take their bucket and follow Mom. They have a fun summer day.
