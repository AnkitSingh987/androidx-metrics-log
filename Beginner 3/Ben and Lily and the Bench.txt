Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Lily and the Bench

Ben and Lily were twins who liked to draw and paint. They were gifted with colors and shapes. They made many pictures of animals, flowers, cars and houses. They wanted to share their pictures with everyone. One day, they saw a big billboard near their home. It was white and empty. Ben had an idea. He said to Lily, "Let's go and put our pictures on the billboard. Then everyone can see them and be happy." Lily agreed. They took their pictures and some tape and walked to the billboard. They climbed on a bench and started to stick their pictures on the white space. They made a beautiful collage of their art. But then, a man came and shouted at them. He said, "Hey, you kids! What are you doing? This is my billboard. You can't put your pictures here. Get down and go away!" Ben and Lily were scared and sad. They did not mean to make the man angry. They thought they were doing something good. They got down from the bench and ran away. They left their pictures on the billboard. They ran back to their home and hugged their mom. She saw their tears and asked them what happened. They told her about the man and the billboard. She comforted them and said, "I'm sorry, my loves. The man was not nice. He did not understand your pictures. But I do. And I'm very proud of you. You have a lot of talent and kindness. Your pictures are wonderful. And you know what? I have a surprise for you." She took them to the living room and showed them a big frame. Inside the frame, there were all their pictures that they had made before. She had collected them and made a collage for them. She said, "This is your own billboard. You can hang it on the wall and look at it every day. And you can make more pictures and add them to it. This is your gift from me. Because I love you and I love your art." Ben and Lily smiled and hugged their mom. They thanked her and said, "We love you too, mom. And we love our billboard. It is the best gift ever." They hung their billboard on the wall and admired their pictures. They felt happy and proud. They forgot about the mean man and his billboard. They had their own billboard, and it was beautiful.

Once upon a time, there was a big bull named Bob. Bob was very confused. He did not know what to do. One day, he met a little girl named Sue. Sue wanted to help Bob. Sue said, "Let's polish your horns, Bob!" Bob liked the idea. They found a soft cloth and started to polish his horns. Bob was very happy. But then, a mean man came. He wanted to take Bob away. Sue tried to stop him, but she was too small. The mean man took Bob away. Sue was very sad. The end.

One day, a cat named Tom was feeling very thirsty. He went to the kitchen and saw a big mug on the table. The mug was full of milk, and Tom was very excited. He jumped up on the table, ready to drink the milk. Just then, a dog named Max walked into the kitchen. He saw Tom on the table and said, "Tom, I must warn you. That is not your milk. It is for our friend, the little mouse." Tom looked at Max and said, "But I am so thirsty! Can I have just a little bit?" Max thought for a moment and then said, "Okay, but only a little. We need to make sure our friend has enough to drink too." Tom was happy and drank a little bit of milk from the mug. He felt much better and was glad that Max had warned him. They both went to find their friend, the little mouse, to share the rest of the milk.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a big clock in her room. The clock had a light face with big numbers. Mia liked to look at the clock. One day, Mia was in her room and she saw the clock. She started to stare at the clock. She stared and stared. She watched the clock's hands move. The hands went around and around. Mia saw that it was time for lunch. She went to the kitchen. Her mom made her a yummy sandwich. After lunch, Mia went back to her room. She stared at the clock again. She liked the light face and big numbers. Mia was happy.
