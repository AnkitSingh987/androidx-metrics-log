Category: Beginner
Title: Mia and Ben and the Toys

Mia and Ben are twins. They like to play in their bedroom. They have many toys and books and games. But sometimes they fight over who gets what. Then they yell at each other. One day, they are playing with their blocks. Mia wants to build a big tower. Ben wants to make a long train. They do not have enough blocks for both. Mia takes some blocks from Ben's train. Ben gets mad and grabs some blocks from Mia's tower. Mia and Ben yell, "Stop! That's mine!" Mom hears them yell. She comes to their bedroom. She sees the blocks on the floor and the twins with angry faces. She says, "Mia and Ben, why are you yelling? You need to share your toys. You are twins. You should be reliable friends." Mia and Ben feel sorry. They say, "Sorry, Mom. Sorry, twin." They hug each other. They decide to work together. They use all the blocks to make a big castle. They are happy. Mom smiles and says, "That's better. I'm proud of you, Mia and Ben. You are reliable twins." She gives them a kiss and leaves them to play.

Once upon a time, there was a hunter named Tom. He was very independent and liked to go into the woods alone. One day, he saw big footprints on the ground. Tom thought, "I will follow these footprints and see what I find." As Tom followed the footprints, he met a little bird. The bird said, "I will help you find what you are looking for." Tom and the bird followed the footprints together. They saw a big shadow up ahead, and Tom thought it was a big animal. But when they got closer, they saw it was just a big tree! The footprints stopped at the tree. Tom was surprised and laughed. The little bird said, "Sometimes, things are not what they seem." Tom learned that it's good to have friends to help, even when you are independent. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a little town, there lived a clever cat named Tom. Tom was good at many things, and he liked to help his friends. One day, Tom saw his friend, a small bird named Tim, in trouble. "Help me, Tom!" cried Tim. "A big dog wants to catch me. What should I do?" Tom thought for a moment and then said, "I have an idea! We can prevent the dog from catching you. We can use my clever opinion to make a plan." Tom and Tim worked together to make a trap for the big dog. They put a big box near a tree and tied a long string to the box. When the dog came near, Tom pulled the string, and the box fell on the dog. "Thank you, Tom!" said Tim. "Your clever opinion saved me. You are a good friend." From that day on, Tom and Tim were best friends, and they always helped each other when they needed it.

Jill and Tom were twins who liked to play with their toys. They had many toys, but their favorite was a shiny ornament that hung on the tree. It was red and round and sparkled in the light. They liked to take it down and roll it on the floor. One day, their mom said, "I have to go to the store. Be good and don't touch the ornament. It is very old and can break easily." She gave them a kiss and left. Jill and Tom nodded, but they did not listen. As soon as mom was gone, they ran to the tree and took the ornament. They rolled it on the floor and laughed. They did not worry about breaking it. But then, something bad happened. The ornament hit a chair and cracked. Jill and Tom gasped. They saw a big crack on the ornament and a small piece fell off. They felt very sad and scared. They knew mom would be angry. They tried to fix the ornament. They used glue and tape and paper. But it did not work. The ornament looked worse. They did not know what to do. Then, Tom had an idea. He was very clever. He said, "I know! Let's put the ornament back on the tree. Maybe mom won't notice the crack. And we can hide the piece under the rug." Jill agreed. They put the ornament back on the tree and hid the piece under the rug. They hoped mom would not see it. But mom did see it. She came back from the store and saw the ornament. She saw the crack and the piece under the rug. She was very angry. She said, "Jill and Tom, what did you do? You broke the ornament. You did not listen to me. You lied to me. This ornament was very special. It was a gift from your grandma. She made it with love. How could you be so careless and dishonest?" Jill and Tom felt very sorry. They cried and said, "We are sorry, mom. We did not mean to break it. We just wanted to play with it. We were afraid to tell you. We tried to fix it. Please forgive us." Mom sighed. She said, "I love you, Jill and Tom. But you did a very bad thing. You disobeyed me and you broke something that was not yours. You should have respected the ornament and left it alone. You should have told me the truth and asked for help. You should have learned to be careful and honest. Now, you have to face the consequences. You have to give up your toys for a week. And you have to make a new ornament for grandma. And you have to say sorry to her." Jill and Tom nodded. They said, "We understand, mom. We are sorry. We will do what you say. We will be careful and honest from now on. We love you, mom." Mom hugged them. She said, "I love you, too. I hope you learned your lesson. And I hope you will make a beautiful ornament for grandma. She will be happy to see it." Jill and Tom smiled. They said, "We will, mom. We will make the best ornament ever. And we will never touch the old one again."
