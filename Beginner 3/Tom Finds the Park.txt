Category: Beginner
Title: Tom Finds the Park

One day, Tom went to the park to play. He took his spade with him. He wanted to dig in the sand. Tom was very organized. He liked to keep his toys neat. When he got to the park, he could not find his spade. He looked everywhere. He looked under the slide, by the swing, and behind the bench. But he could not find it. Then, he saw a girl named Sue. Sue had found his spade. She gave it back to Tom. They played together in the sand. They had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He loved to play in the mud and make a big, messy pile. His clothes would always get dirty, but he didn't care. One day, Tom was playing with his friend, Sue. They were making mud pies when Sue said, "I think we should stop. My knee hurts." Tom disagreed, "But it's fun! Let's keep playing." Sue didn't want to, but Tom didn't listen. Later, Tom's mom saw their messy clothes and said, "You two need to clean up." Tom and Sue were sad. They had to stop playing and wash their clothes. The moral of the story is to listen to your friends when they disagree, and to be careful when playing so you don't get hurt.

One day, a tall boy named Tim wanted to play with his toy cars. He had a big box of cars, but they did not match. He wanted to find a friend who had a car that matched his red car. Tim went to the park and saw a girl named Sue. She had a red car too! Tim said, "Hi! Can our cars be a match? Can we play together?" Sue said, "Yes! Let's play!" They played with their cars and had lots of fun. But then, a dog came and took Tim's red car. Tim was sad. Sue said, "Don't be sad. I have an idea!" Sue took out a big blue car from her bag. She said, "Now we both have blue cars! They match!" Tim was happy and they played together with their new matching cars.

One day, a little comet was flying in the sky. The comet felt frustrated because it could not play with the stars. It would cry every night, wishing to join the stars. One night, the comet saw a big star and said, "Why can I not play with you?" The big star replied, "You are too small, and you move too fast. We cannot catch you." The comet felt sad, but it did not give up. It tried to slow down and grow bigger. Then, something unexpected happened. The comet turned into a beautiful shooting star! All the stars were amazed and wanted to play with the comet now. The comet was happy and did not cry anymore. It had many new friends and played with the stars every night. The comet learned that sometimes, being different can be a good thing.

Lily liked to play hide and seek with her big brother Tom. She was very good at finding hiding places that he could not see. One day, she had a new idea. She saw a big cupboard in the kitchen that had a door that could open and close. She thought it would be a good place to hide. She opened the door and climbed inside. She saw many things in the cupboard, like pots and pans and spoons and forks. She also saw a big jar of cookies that Mom had made. She felt hungry and took one cookie. It was very yummy. She took another one and another one. She forgot about the game and ate many cookies. Tom looked for Lily everywhere. He could not find her. He checked under the bed, behind the sofa, in the closet, but she was not there. He started to worry. Where could she be? He went to the kitchen and saw the cupboard. He thought maybe she was hiding there. He opened the door and saw Lily with a big smile and crumbs on her face. "Lily, I found you!" he said. "What are you doing in there?" Lily giggled and said, "I was hiding and eating cookies. They are very good. Do you want some?" Tom saw the jar of cookies and saw that it was almost empty. He was not happy. He said, "Lily, you ate too many cookies. Mom will be angry. You are not supposed to eat them before dinner. That is not fair." Lily did not understand why Tom was angry. She thought he would like the cookies too. She said, "But they are harmless. They are just cookies. They make me happy. And you are my brother. I want to share with you." Tom looked at Lily and saw that she was not trying to be bad. She was just a little girl who liked cookies. He felt sorry for her. He said, "Okay, Lily. I am sorry for being angry. You are my sister. I love you. But we have to tell Mom the truth. Maybe she will not be mad if we say sorry." Lily nodded and said, "Okay, Tom. I love you too. Let's tell Mom. Maybe she will laugh and give us more cookies." They hugged and left the cupboard. They went to find Mom and tell her what they did. They hoped she would laugh and not be mad.
