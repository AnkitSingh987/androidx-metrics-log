Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Crane

One day, a boy named Tim saw an unusual crane. The crane was big and had many colors. Tim loved to watch the crane work. It would pick up things and put them in new places. Tim had a ball. He liked to throw the ball very high. One day, he threw the ball and it went too far. It got stuck on the crane. Tim was sad. He looked at the crane and said, "Please, can you give my ball back?" The crane did not say anything. Tim went home and slept. The next day, he went back to see the crane. He was surprised! The crane had many balls now. It had found more balls and put them all together. The crane heard Tim and gave all the balls back. Tim was so happy. He said, "Thank you, crane!" The crane was happy too. From that day, Tim and the crane became friends. They played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a cool toy car that he loved to play with all day. He also had a folder where he kept his drawings of the car. One sunny day, Tim was playing with his toy car near a big tree. Suddenly, he saw a bright light coming from the tree. The light was so hot that it started to burn the tree. Tim was scared and ran away to find help. When he came back with his mom, they saw that the tree was not burned at all. Instead, there was a shiny, new toy car just like his old one! It turns out that the bright light was a magic light that made a new toy car for Tim. Now, Tim had two cool toy cars to play with and he was very happy. He put his new toy car in the folder with his drawings and played with both cars every day.

One day, a little girl named Lily saw a rainbow in the sky. The rainbow was very pretty with many colors. Lily wanted to touch the rainbow, so she tried to climb up a tree. But she could not reach it. She felt frustrated. Lily's mom saw her and asked, "What are you trying to do, Lily?" Lily said, "I want to touch the rainbow, but I can't reach it." Her mom smiled and said, "The rainbow is very high up. We can't touch it, but we can enjoy looking at it." Then, Lily had an idea. She took her toy broom and tried to clean the sky to make the rainbow come down. But the rainbow stayed up high. Lily's mom laughed and said, "We can't clean the sky, but we can clean our room together." Lily agreed and they both cleaned the room. When they finished, Lily felt happy. She looked at the sky again and saw the rainbow was still there, shining brightly.

Once upon a time, there was a little seed. The little seed was in the ground. It wanted to rise and see the sun. One day, the seed started to grow. It grew a tiny root. The root went down into the ground. The seed was very happy. It knew it would rise soon. The seed grew more and more. It became a brilliant flower. The flower rose up and saw the sun. The flower was very happy. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big bull named Bob. Bob lived on a farm with many friends. He liked to play with his friends all day. They would run, jump, and have fun in the warm sun. One day, Bob and his friends saw a big plate of yummy food. It was for a party the farmer was having. Bob and his friends wanted to try some. They took a little bite and it was so yummy! They ate and ate until they were full. Then, the farmer came out and saw the food was all gone. He was not mad, he just laughed. He said, "Well, I guess the party is finished!" Bob and his friends were happy they got to eat the yummy food. They went back to play and had a great day.
