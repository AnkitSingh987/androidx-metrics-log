Category: Beginner
Title: The Boy Called Gavin and the Door

Once there was a little boy called Gavin. He was three years old and he loved to march. Everywhere he went he marched with his legs moving up and down, up and down in a very fun way. One day, Gavin went to visit his friend Susie. He marched all the way until he arrived at her house. When he got there, he knocked on the door and Susie opened it. "Hi Gavin!" She said. "Come in and see my new fan!" Gavin stepped inside, and there it was, a long and shiny fan. He was very excited and wanted to try it out. "Can I march with the fan?" He asked Susie. "No, sorry," Susie said. "You can't use the fan, it's too dangerous". Gavin was disappointed, but he understood why Susie wouldn't let him march with the fan. He had to find another way to march, even if it wasn't as fun as marching with a fan.

Once upon a time there was a bird that lived in the forest. It was so special because it could soar very high. Everyone in the forest admired it. One day, the bird decided to take a risk. It wanted to soar so high, it would reach the clouds. Everyone was amazed by the courage of the bird. Before the bird could take off, the bunny said: "Please be careful. You don't want your wings to burn in the sun." The bird nodded, thanked the bunny and took off. As the bird soared into the clouds, it felt so free that it let out a happy song. The sun was so warm on its wings and its body felt so light. It saw so many things that it had never seen before. The bird eventually flew down to the original soil of the forest. As it felt the soft soil beneath its feet, it was so grateful for the adventure it had just taken.

Tom and Sue were playing in the kitchen. They liked to pretend they were chefs and make yummy food. Tom was the bossy one. He told Sue what to do and how to do it. Sue did not like that. She wanted to have fun and try new things. "Give me the spoon, Sue. You are doing it wrong. You have to stir the soup like this," Tom said, snatching the spoon from Sue's hand. "But I want to stir the soup too, Tom. It is my turn. You are not nice," Sue said, feeling sad and angry. "No, it is not your turn. I am the chef and you are the helper. You have to listen to me. Now, go and wash the dishes in the sink," Tom said, pointing to the pile of dirty plates and cups. Sue did not want to wash the dishes. She wanted to stir the soup. She had an idea. She decided to surprise Tom. She went to the sink and turned on the tap. She filled a big cup with water. She sneaked behind Tom and poured the water over his head. "Surprise!" Sue shouted, laughing. Tom was not happy. He was wet and cold. He screamed and dropped the spoon. The soup spilled over the stove and the floor. It was very hot and sticky. Tom and Sue heard a loud noise. It was their mom. She came into the kitchen and saw the mess. She was very angry. "What have you done? This is not a game. This is dangerous. You could have burned yourselves or the house. You are both in big trouble. Go to your rooms right now. No more playing for today," Mom said, scolding Tom and Sue. Tom and Sue felt sorry and scared. They ran to their rooms and cried. They wished they had played nicely and not made a mess. They learned their lesson. They did not surprise each other again.

Lila and Tom are twins. They like to play in the park. One day, they wake up early. They want to see the sun rise. They put on their coats and hats. They sneak out of the house. They are very quiet. They do not want to wake up Mom and Dad. They walk to the park. They find a hill. They climb up the hill. They sit on the grass. They wait for the sun. They see the sky change color. It is pink and orange and yellow. They see the sun peek out from behind the trees. It is bright and warm. They smile at each other. "Look, Lila, the sun is rising!" Tom says. "It is a beautiful sight, Tom!" Lila says. They hug each other. They are happy. They watch the sun rise higher and higher. They see the birds fly and sing. They hear the cars and people start to move. The park is not quiet anymore. It is alive. They decide to go back home. They hope Mom and Dad are not angry. They run down the hill. They hold hands. They have a secret. They saw the sun rise. They will never forget.
