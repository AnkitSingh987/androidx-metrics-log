Category: Beginner
Title: The Wide Tree and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a wide tree. In the tree, there was a little bird. The little bird had a hurt wing. The bird needed a bandage. A kind girl saw the bird. She wanted to help. She put a bandage on the bird's wing. The bird felt better. The bird said "thank you" to the girl. The bird flew away. It was happy. One day, the bird came back. It returned to the wide tree. The girl and the bird played together. They were friends forever.

One day, a little dog named Spot was very impatient. He wanted to go for a walk with his boy, Tim. Spot barked and jumped, showing Tim his leash. "Okay, Spot, let's go for a walk," said Tim. He put the leash on Spot and they went outside. They walked and walked, and Spot was very happy. Tim saw his friend, Sally, and they started to talk. While Tim and Sally talked, Spot saw a big red ball in the park. He wanted to play with it. Spot pulled on his leash, but Tim was still talking. Spot pulled harder and harder until the leash broke! Spot ran to the ball and started to play. Tim saw Spot run away and got scared. He said bye to Sally and ran after Spot. When Tim found Spot, he was happy to see him safe. Tim fixed the leash and said, "Thank you, Spot, for not going too far. I will pay more attention next time." From that day on, Tim always made sure to watch Spot closely when they went for walks. And Spot learned to be more patient. They both had fun and were thankful for each other.

Lily and Tom were friends. They liked to play together. One day, they found a big box in the yard. They opened the box and saw many things inside. There were clothes, hats, shoes, and jewels. Lily and Tom wanted to try them on. "Let's play marry!" Lily said. She put on a white dress and a shiny ring. She gave Tom a black coat and a hat. "You are the groom and I am the bride," she said. Tom nodded. He liked to play with Lily. He put on the coat and the hat. He looked at Lily and smiled. "You are a pretty bride," he said. They walked to the door of the house. They wanted to show their parents their game. But the door was locked. They tried to turn the knob, but it did not move. They pushed and pulled, but it did not open. They banged and shouted, but no one heard them. They were scared. They did not know what to do. They sat on the floor and cried. They wished they did not play marry. They wished they stayed with their parents. They wished they were healthy and happy. But they were not. They were stuck in the box. And no one came to help them. The end.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a rare, pretty bird. The bird had a nest with her eggs. She wanted to protect her eggs from danger. So, she made a line with sticks around her nest. She thought the line would keep her eggs safe. One day, the bird went to find food. She left her nest for a short time. A nice squirrel saw the line and thought it was a fun game. The squirrel jumped over the line and played near the nest, not knowing the bird's plan. When the bird came back, she saw the squirrel near her nest. She was scared at first, but then she saw that the squirrel was just playing. The squirrel became her friend and helped her protect the eggs. The line didn't keep the eggs safe, but the squirrel did. The bird and the squirrel were happy friends, and they took care of the eggs together.

Will was playing in the park when he saw something had fallen from a nearby tree. It was a cushion. He wanted to know who it belonged to. He asked a woman who was sitting nearby. She said it was her cushion. Will asked her how she managed to get it in the tree. The woman said that she and her friend were playing hide and seek, and she hid the cushion in the tree. She apologised and said that she forgot she had put it there. Will offered to help the woman find her cushion. He asked her if he could look in the tree. She thanked him and said that was very honest of him. Will tried his best to reach it, but it was too high up. He tried to climb the tree but he wasn't strong enough. He looked around and saw a stick nearby. He used the stick to manage to get the cushion down. He handed it to the woman, who was very thankful. She told him he was a very kind and honest boy. Will smiled and he went back to playing.
