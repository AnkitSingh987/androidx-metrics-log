Category: Beginner
Title: Sue and the Garden

Once upon a time, in a busy little town, there was a girl named Sue. Sue loved to grow plants in her garden. Every day, she would water her plants and watch them grow big and strong. One day, Sue's mom asked her to help make a meal for the family. Sue had a great idea! She would use the vegetables from her garden to make a yummy salad. She picked some lettuce, tomatoes, and carrots. Sue's mom was very happy with her help. Sue and her mom made a big meal for the family. They all sat down to eat together. Everyone loved the salad Sue made from her garden. They were so proud of how well her plants had grown. From that day on, Sue always helped her mom make meals with the vegetables she grew in her garden.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly fish named Fin. Fin had a best friend, a little crab named Snap. They loved to play together in the sea. One day, they found a big hook stuck in the sand. Snap got too close to the hook and got caught! Fin was very scared. He wanted to help Snap, but he didn't know how. He tried to pull the hook, but it was too strong. Fin felt sad because he couldn't separate Snap from the hook. Then, a big, friendly whale named Bubbles came along. Bubbles saw Fin and Snap in trouble and wanted to help. With a big splash, Bubbles used her strong tail to break the hook. Snap was free! Fin, Snap, and Bubbles were so happy. They played together all day long, and the hook was gone. The sea was safe and fun again for the three friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big red ball. It loved to bounce all the time. One day, the ball went on a journey to find a new friend. It bounced and bounced, going very far away from home. On its journey, the ball met a fake toy dog. The toy dog could not talk or play, but the ball wanted to be friends. The ball tried to bounce with the toy dog, but the toy dog did not move. The ball was very sad. It wanted a real friend to bounce and play with. The ball tried to go back home, but it was lost. It bounced and bounced, but could not find its way. The ball was alone and missed its home.

Once upon a time, there was an adorable little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his favorite toy, a soft ball. One day, Spot's ball went into a dark room. Spot was scared, but he knew he had to get his ball back. Spot's mommy said, "Spot, remember to use a candle to see in the dark room." Spot took the candle and went into the dark room. He held the candle high and saw his ball. He was so happy! Spot said, "Thank you, mommy, for helping me." His mommy smiled and said, "You're welcome, Spot. I'm proud of you for being brave." Spot felt so happy and safe with his mommy, and they played with the ball together all day long.

Once upon a time there was a penguin. He was walking down a path when he saw a sour lemon. He wanted the lemon, but he couldn’t reach it! He tried to stretch, jump, and even flap his wings, but it was too high up. He turned to a passing bird and asked for help. The bird laughed and said, “I’ll help you, but only if you share the lemon with me.” The penguin nodded and the bird grabbed the lemon. The penguin thanked the bird and was about to take a bite of the lemon when the bird warned him, “It’s sour!” The penguin took a tiny nibble and said, “Oh, you’re right. It’s much too sour for me.” He thanked the bird again and the bird flew away. The penguin turned back home with a smile on his face.

Once upon a time, there was a clever boy named Tim. Tim had a big machine. The machine could spread paint on the wall. One day, Tim had a plan. He wanted to paint his room with the machine. Tim told his friend, Sam, "I will use my machine to paint my room. It will be fun!" Sam said, "Be careful, Tim. The machine is very big." But Tim did not listen. Tim started the machine and it began to spread paint. But the paint went everywhere! It was on the floor, the bed, and even the cat! Tim's mom came in and saw the mess. She was very mad. Tim was sad because he made a big mess and had to clean it all up.
