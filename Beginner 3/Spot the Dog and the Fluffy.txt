Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog and the Fluffy

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was very impatient. He wanted to play all day long. One day, Spot's best friend, a cat named Fluffy, got sick. Fluffy had to go to the doctor for an operation. Spot was sad and worried. Spot went to visit Fluffy after the operation. "How are you, Fluffy?" asked Spot. Fluffy said, "I am okay, but I need to rest." Spot wanted to play, but he knew he had to accept that Fluffy needed to rest. Spot sat by Fluffy's side all day. He was a good friend. Soon, Fluffy felt better and they could play again. Spot learned to be patient and accept things when they were not perfect. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a wild sea, there lived a little oyster. The oyster was sad because he was all alone. One day, a small fish swam by and saw the lonely oyster. "Hi, oyster!" said the fish. "Why are you sad?" "I am all alone," the oyster said. "I want a friend." The fish thought for a moment and then said, "Why don't you ask someone to be your friend?" The oyster thought about it and decided to ask the fish, "Will you be my friend?" The fish smiled and said, "Yes, I will be your friend!" So, the oyster and the fish became friends and played together every day. They learned that if you ask for help or friendship, you might just find it. And they lived happily ever after. The moral of the story is, don't be afraid to ask for help or make new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat and a little dog. They did not like each other. They would fight all the time. The big cat would hiss, and the little dog would bark. Their fight was loud and scary. One day, a new friend came to play. It was a cute baby bird. The baby bird was full of love. It wanted to be friends with the big cat and the little dog. The baby bird would sing a happy song to them. The big cat and the little dog stopped fighting. They did not want to scare the baby bird. The baby bird showed them a new game. They played together, and it was not disgusting. The big cat, the little dog, and the baby bird became good friends. They learned that love was better than fighting. And they all lived happily ever after.

Lily and Ben were playing with their toy cars on the floor. They liked to make them zoom and crash and honk. Lily had a red car and Ben had a blue car. They were having fun. Then, Lily saw a shiny jar on the table. It was full of white grains. She wondered what they were. She got up and went to the table. She opened the jar and took a pinch of the grains. She put them in her mouth. They were very salty. She made a face and spat them out. "Yuck! What is this?" she asked. Ben looked up and saw the jar. He knew what it was. It was salt. His mom used it to cook. He also got up and went to the table. "That's salt, Lily. You don't eat it like that. It's for food. It makes food taste good," he said. He took the jar from Lily and closed it. He put it back on the table. He was thoughtful. He did not want Lily to get in trouble. "Come on, let's play with our cars again," he said. He took Lily's hand and led her back to the floor. They picked up their cars and started to play again. They forgot about the salt. But they did not see that the jar was not on the table anymore. It had disappeared. Someone had taken it. It was their cat, Milo. He was curious. He liked shiny things. He had jumped on the table and knocked the jar off. He had carried it away to his hiding place. He was happy. He had a new toy. He did not care about the salt. He liked to play with the jar. He made it roll and spin and clatter. He was having fun.
