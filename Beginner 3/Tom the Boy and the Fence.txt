Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Fence

One day, a boy named Tom went for a walk. He liked to walk around his street. He saw his neighbor, Mr. Brown, in his yard. Mr. Brown had a big bag. Tom was curious and asked, "What's in the bag, Mr. Brown?" Mr. Brown said, "I have a useful tool in the bag. It can help us fix things." Tom was excited and wanted to help. They opened the bag and took out a big, red hammer. They started to fix a broken fence together. As they were fixing the fence, a big wind came. The wind blew the bag away! Tom and Mr. Brown ran after the bag. They caught it and found a small, green hammer inside. They didn't know the small hammer was there! They laughed and used both hammers to fix the fence faster. Tom and Mr. Brown were very happy.

Once there was a little girl. Her name was Katie. She was very excited, because that day she was finally old enough to go to the farm! She wanted to see all the animals and find a pony. At the farm, Katie saw chickens, cows, and even a donkey. But then she had a idea. She wanted to find a pony by herself. She ran around, searching near the barn, in the pastures and behind the trees. At last, she saw it in the corner of the barn. It was the cutest little pony. She was so excited! She ran up to it and hugged it. But then the farmer came over, looking angry. He said "That's my pony, you can't pet it without asking me first". Katie felt embarrassed and ashamed. The farmer said "Next time, make sure to ask first. Then you can pet my pony". Katie smiled, thanked the farmer and agreed to remember. She ran back to the barn to visit the pony again, as long as she asked first.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Abbie and she had a basket. She loved to carry it everywhere. One day she was walking and saw a hot sun in the sky. Abbie was so hot she thought she'd try to cool down by having a clap. So she clapped her hands together and then began to wave her basket around too. Suddenly a gust of wind blew the basket away! Abbie chased the basket out of the garden and into the street. She clapped and called for the basket, but the wind was too strong. She was so upset. But then, just as she was about to cry, she saw the basket resting gently on the ground. The wind had died down and the basket was back! Abbie quickly ran and clapped with joy. She was so happy to have her beloved basket back. The End.

Once upon a time, there was a big pot and a little pot. They lived in a kitchen with a nice cook. The cook liked to make food with them. One day, the cook made a spicy soup. The big pot was happy because the spicy soup made steam. The little pot was sad because it did not have any soup. The big pot said, "I am better because I have spicy soup and steam." The little pot did not like this. They began to quarrel. They did not want to be friends anymore. The cook saw the pots quarrel and felt sad. She did not like when her pots were not friends. So, she made more spicy soup and put some in the little pot. Now, both pots had spicy soup and steam. The big pot and little pot were happy again. They said sorry to each other and became best friends. They promised to share the spicy soup and steam from now on.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park with their mom. They liked to swing, slide and run on the grass. Mom had a big bag with snacks and drinks for them. She said they could have a snack after they played for a while. "Can we have a snack now, mom?" Lily asked. She was hungry and thirsty. "Not yet, sweetie. Play a little more and then we'll have a snack," mom said. Lily and Ben saw a big tree with red apples. They ran to the tree and tried to reach the apples. They were too high for them. "Let's climb the tree, Ben," Lily said. "Maybe we can get some apples." "OK, Lily. But be careful," Ben said. They climbed the tree, holding tight to the branches. They saw some apples that looked juicy and sweet. They reached for them, but they were too far. "Ben, I have an idea. You hold me and I'll get the apples," Lily said. "OK, Lily. But don't let go," Ben said. He held Lily by her waist and lifted her up. Lily stretched her arms and grabbed an apple. She was happy. "Look, Ben, I got one!" she said. But then, something bad happened. Ben lost his balance and let go of Lily. Lily fell from the tree and hit the ground. She cried out loud. She was hurt. Mom heard Lily's cry and ran to the tree. She saw Lily on the ground, bleeding and crying. She was scared. "Lily, Lily, are you OK? What happened?" mom asked. She picked up Lily and hugged her. She saw Ben on the tree, looking down. He was sorry. "Mom, I'm sorry. I dropped Lily. We wanted some apples," Ben said. Mom was angry and sad. She took Lily and Ben to the car. She drove to the hospital. She hoped Lily would be OK. She said to Ben, "You were very naughty, Ben. You should not have climbed the tree. You should have waited for the snack. Now Lily is hurt and we have to go to the hospital. You made a big mistake." Ben felt bad. He wished he had not climbed the tree. He wished he had held Lily tight. He wished he had listened to mom. He said to Lily, "I'm sorry, Lily. I love you." Lily did not say anything. She was in pain. She closed her eyes. She wished she had not climbed the tree. She wished she had not wanted the apples. She wished she had played with Ben. She said to mom, "I love you, mom."
