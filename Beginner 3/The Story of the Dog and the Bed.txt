Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Dog and the Bed

Once upon a time, there was a big, comfortable bed. It was so soft and nice to sleep on. One day, a surprise came to the bed. A small, cute dog named Spot had a job to do. He wanted to make the bed even more comfortable for his friends. Spot worked very hard on his job. He found soft pillows, warm blankets, and even a little toy for his friends to play with. He put them all on the big, comfortable bed. Spot was very happy with his work. When his friends came over, they saw the big, comfortable bed. They were so surprised and happy! They all jumped on the bed and played with the toy. They had so much fun. Spot felt proud of his job.

Once upon a time, there was a nice bunny named Bob. Bob lived in a small house in the woods. He loved to hop, play, and eat. One day, Bob felt hungry. He knew he needed to eat something. He thought of his favorite food, vegetables. He went to his garden to find some. In the garden, Bob found many vegetables. He saw green peas, orange carrots, and red tomatoes. He ate some of each. They tasted very good. Bob was happy and full. He played and hopped all day long.

---

Lily and Sam were playing with their toys in the living room. Lily had a big jet that could fly and make noises. Sam had a small car that could roll and beep. They liked to pretend that they were going on adventures. "Let's go to the moon!" Lily said, holding her jet in the air. "We can see the stars and the rocks!" "Okay!" Sam said, pushing his car on the floor. "But we need more fuel. Can we add some water to the car?" Lily nodded and ran to the kitchen. She found a cup and filled it with water from the tap. She came back and poured some water on Sam's car. "Oops!" Lily said, as the water spilled on the floor and the car. "Too much water!" Sam looked at his car and saw that it was wet and not beeping anymore. He felt sad and angry. "Lily, you broke my car!" Sam shouted. "Now it can't roll or beep! You are a bad friend!" Lily felt ashamed and sorry. She didn't mean to break Sam's car. She wanted to help him. "I'm sorry, Sam!" Lily said, with tears in her eyes. "I didn't want to break your car. I wanted to add fuel. Can we fix it?" Sam looked at Lily and saw that she was crying. He felt sorry too. He knew that Lily was not a bad friend. She made a mistake. "It's okay, Lily!" Sam said, hugging her. "Maybe we can fix it. Or we can play with something else. We are still friends!" Lily smiled and hugged Sam back. They decided to play with the jet together. They flew it to the moon and had fun. They forgot about the car and the water. They were happy.

Lily and Ben were playing with blocks. They liked to build towers and bridges and houses. They had many blocks of different colors and shapes. "Look, Lily, I made a bridge!" Ben said. He put two long blocks on the floor and put a flat block on top of them. "Now the cars can go over the water." "That's clever, Ben!" Lily said. She had a car block and drove it over the bridge. "Beep beep! Thank you for the bridge!" Lily wanted to make something clever too. She looked at the blocks and had an idea. She took four square blocks and put them together to make a bigger square. Then she took four triangle blocks and put them on the corners of the square. She made a roof! "Look, Ben, I made a house!" Lily said. She put a door block and a window block on the front of the house. "Now the people can live here." "That's clever, Lily!" Ben said. He had a people block and put it inside the house. "Hello, I live here!" Lily and Ben were happy. They had completed their bridge and their house. They played with their blocks for a long time. They were clever and creative.
