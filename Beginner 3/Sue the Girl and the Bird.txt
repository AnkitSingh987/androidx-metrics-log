Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Bird

One day, a little girl named Sue found a big brown box. She wanted to see what was inside, so she tried to lift it. But it was too heavy for her. She asked her friend, Tom, for help. "Tom, can you help me lift this box?" Sue said. Tom came over and together, they lifted the brown box. When they opened it, they saw many pretty pictures inside. They looked at all the pictures and talked about them. "I like this picture of the cat," said Tom. "I like the picture of the dog," said Sue. They laughed and had fun looking at the pictures. Then, something unexpected happened. As they were looking at the pictures, one of the pictures started to move! A little bird flew out of the picture and into the room. Sue and Tom were very surprised. They watched as the bird flew around the room and then went back into the picture. After that, Sue and Tom knew they had found a very special box of pictures.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a toy bear that she loved very much. The bear helped comfort her when she was sad. Lucy and her bear lived in a small house near a big hill. One day, Lucy and her bear went for a walk. They saw a distant hill with something shiny on it. They went closer to see what it was. To their surprise, they found a big pile of gold! They were very happy and decided to take some gold home. When they got back to their house, the gold turned into candy! Lucy and her bear couldn't believe it. They laughed and ate the candy together. They didn't need the gold, but they were happy to have a sweet treat.

Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to pretend they were a king and a queen. They had a big cardboard box that was their castle. They had a crown and a cape and a wand and a sword. One day, a big dog came into the garden. It was barking and running around. It saw the cardboard box and wanted to play with it. It jumped on it and bit it and tore it. Lily and Ben were scared. They ran out of the box and hid behind a tree. "Go away, dog!" Ben shouted. "This is our castle! You are not nice!" The dog did not listen. It kept barking and tearing the box. It saw Lily and Ben behind the tree and ran towards them. Lily and Ben hugged each other. "Ben, what do we do?" Lily asked. Ben picked up his sword. It was made of wood and had a star on it. He held it in front of him. "I will protect you, Lily. I am the king. You are the queen. We have to be careful. We have to be brave." Ben said. He waved his sword at the dog. The dog stopped and looked at him. It tilted its head and wagged its tail. It thought Ben was playing with him. It ran to Ben and licked his face. Ben dropped his sword and laughed. Lily laughed too. The dog was not mean. It was friendly. It wanted to play with Lily and Ben. Lily and Ben petted the dog and gave it a hug. They decided to make the dog their friend. They gave it a name. They called it Star. They played with Star in the garden. They forgot about the cardboard box. They were happy.

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a walk. She saw a small, honest dog with big eyes. The dog looked lost and sad. Sue wanted to help the dog find its home. Sue asked the dog, "What is your name?" The dog said, "My name is Max." Sue and Max walked and looked for Max's home. They saw a lady with a big hat. Sue asked the lady, "Do you know where Max lives?" The lady looked at Max and said, "Oh, yes! Max lives in the blue house down the street." Sue and Max walked to the blue house. Max licked Sue's lip to say thank you. Sue was happy that she could help Max find his home.
