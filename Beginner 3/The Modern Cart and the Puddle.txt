Category: Beginner
Title: The Modern Cart and the Puddle

Once upon a time, there was a modern cart. It was a big, pretty cart that could go very fast. The cart was red and shiny. The cart loved to go fast and show off to all the other carts. One day, it started to rain. The rain was cold and wet. The modern cart didn't like the rain. But it still wanted to go fast and show off. So the cart went out in the rain and started to go very fast. As the cart went faster and faster in the rain, it got more and more wet. The cart didn't see the big puddle in front of it. The cart went into the puddle and got stuck. The cart could not move. It was sad and wet. The other carts saw the modern cart stuck in the puddle. They knew it was not smart to go fast in the rain. The modern cart learned that showing off can lead to bad things. The moral of the story is to be careful and not show off when it is not safe.

Once upon a time there was a little boy named Jack. Jack was three years old and he loved playing in the garden. One day, Jack was playing in the garden when he saw something strange. It was a mask! Jack was very curious and he picked up the mask. He liked the way it looked and kept it. Soon after, Jack's mom saw him with the mask. She told him that it was not safe to keep a mask and that he should not keep it. Jack said he was sorry and put the mask down. Then he asked his mom what was special about masks. His mom replied, "A mask can remind us to keep our distance and be safe. Masks help us protect ourselves and stay healthy." Jack felt a bit sad he had to give up his mask, but he also knew that it was important to keep himself safe. So instead of keeping the mask, Jack had some tasty ice-cream as a special treat. This was a lesson to Jack that there are things more important than keeping something that looks good - like keeping yourself safe.

Ben and Mia liked to play in the garden. They had a big tub of water and some toys. They splashed and soaked each other with the water. They laughed and had fun. One day, they saw a small cat in the garden. It was black and white and had green eyes. It looked at them and meowed. Ben and Mia wanted to pet the cat. They got out of the tub and walked towards it. But the cat did not want to be petted. It was a wild cat. It liked to be independent and free. It did not like water or people. It hissed and ran away from Ben and Mia. "Come back, kitty!" Ben called. "We want to be your friends!" But the cat did not come back. It jumped over the fence and disappeared. Ben and Mia felt sad. They liked the cat. They wanted to play with it. They went back to the tub and soaked each other again. They tried to forget the cat. But they still hoped to see it again someday.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his favorite toy, a ball with a string. He would run and jump to catch the ball and string in his mouth. One day, the string on Spot's ball broke. Spot was sad because he could not play with his toy. His friend, a kind girl named Sue, saw that Spot was sad. She wanted to make Spot happy again. Sue found a new string and helped repair Spot's toy. She tied the new string to the ball. Spot was so happy to play with his ball and string again. He wagged his tail and gave Sue a big lick on her face. Spot and Sue played together all day, and they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, a little fish named Finny lived in a deep pond. One day, he saw a shiny thing at the bottom. He wanted to touch it. Finny tried to swim down, but it was very deep. He did not succeed at first. He tried and tried again, but he could not reach the shiny thing. Then, Finny asked his friend, the big fish, for help. They swam down together. This time, Finny succeeded and touched the shiny thing. Finny was very happy and thanked his friend. They swam back up and played together in the pond.
