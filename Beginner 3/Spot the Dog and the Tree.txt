Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot was a very available dog. He always played with the kids in the park. One day, Spot met a new friend named Tim. Tim was a little boy who loved to play with dogs. They had a lot of fun together. "Spot, let's play with the ball!" said Tim. They played with the ball for a long time. Then, they got tired and wanted to rest. They saw a big tree and decided to sit under it. But, they didn't know that there was a fire nearby. The fire started to burn the tree. Spot and Tim didn't see the fire. They were too busy talking and laughing. The fire got bigger and bigger. Finally, they saw the fire, but it was too late. The tree fell on them, and they were both very sad.

Sara was playing with her doll in the garden. She liked to pretend that her doll was a doctor and she was a patient. She wrapped a bandage around her doll's arm and said, "There, you are all better now. You are very strong, doll." But then, she heard a loud noise from the shed. She ran to see what it was. She saw her brother, Tom, holding a big wrench. He was trying to fix his bike, but he was not very good at it. He twisted the wrench too hard and it slipped from his hand. It hit his leg and made a big cut. "Ow! Ow! Ow!" Tom cried. He dropped the wrench and grabbed his leg. He saw blood coming out of the cut. He was scared and in pain. Sara saw what happened and ran to help him. She remembered her bandage and her doll. She took the bandage from her doll and wrapped it around Tom's leg. She said, "Don't worry, Tom. I will help you. You are very strong, Tom." Tom felt the bandage on his leg and saw Sara's face. He smiled a little and said, "Thank you, Sara. You are very brave and kind. You are a good doctor, Sara." Sara smiled back and said, "You are welcome, Tom. You are a good brother, Tom." They hugged each other and waited for their mom to come. They knew she would be proud of them. They learned how to twist, bandage, and be strong.

Once upon a time, there was a healthy boy named Tim. He liked to play with his toys all day. His mom always had to remind him to eat his food and to take a bath. Tim had a favorite toy, a big metal car. He loved it so much and took it with him everywhere. One day, Tim went to the park to play. He was having so much fun that he forgot about his metal car. When it was time to go home, he left the car at the park. That night, Tim felt very sad because he missed his car. The next day, Tim went back to the park to look for his car. But it was not there. Tim was very sad. Then, a little girl walked up to him with the metal car in her hand. She said, "I found this car yesterday. I knew someone must be missing it." Tim was so happy! He thanked the little girl and they became friends. The moral of the story is that it's important to take care of your things and to be kind to others. Sometimes, unexpected things happen, but with a kind heart and good friends, everything will be okay.

Once upon a time, in a modern house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars. One day, he saw a big box in the kitchen. Tim's mom said, "This box has food for us to eat. Can you help me push it?" Tim was excited to help. He pushed the box with all his might. "I'm strong!" he said. His mom smiled and said, "Yes, you are!" Together, they pushed the box into the kitchen. Tim's mom opened it and showed him all the yummy food inside. They had a fun time eating and talking about their day. Tim felt happy that he could help his mom with the big box of food.
