Category: Beginner
Title: The Unique Bird and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a unique bird. This bird lived in a tree near a lawn. The bird loved to play on the lawn all day. One day, a big dog came to the lawn. The dog liked to run and bark. The dog disturbed the bird while it was playing. The bird was sad. The bird had an idea. It sang a pretty song to the dog. The dog liked the song and stopped barking. They became friends and played on the lawn together. And they did not disturb each other anymore.

---

Lily and Ben were playing with their new kitten in the snow. They made a snowman and put a hat and a scarf on it. The kitten liked the snowman and rubbed its head on it. "Look, the kitten loves the snowman!" Lily said. "Yes, it does. Let's go inside and get some hot cocoa. We can come back later and play some more," Ben said. They left the kitten and the snowman in the yard and went inside. They drank their hot cocoa and ate some cookies. They forgot about the kitten and the snowman. The sun came out and shone on the snow. The snow started to melt. The snowman started to melt. The hat and the scarf started to slide off. The kitten was cold and wet. It tried to find a warm place. It climbed on top of the snowman's head. It curled up in the hat. The snowman melted more and more. The hat and the kitten fell to the ground. The kitten was scared and hurt. It meowed loudly. Lily and Ben heard the meow. They ran outside and saw the kitten and the snowman. "Oh no! The snowman melted! And the kitten!" Lily cried. "Quick, let's get the kitten inside. Maybe we can save it," Ben said. They picked up the kitten and ran inside. They wrapped it in a towel and put it near the fireplace. They tried to warm it up and make it feel better. But it was too late. The kitten was too cold and wet. It did not move or meow. It did not open its eyes. It did not breathe. The kitten was gone. Lily and Ben were very sad. They hugged each other and cried. They wished they had not left the kitten and the snowman alone. They wished they had been more careful and kind. They wished they could have their kitten back. But they could not. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big cabinet in a calm house. The cabinet was full of toys and books for kids. One day, a little boy named Tim found a pen and paper in the cabinet. He wanted to write something. Tim sat down and started to write on the paper. He wrote about a big cat and a little dog. The cat and the dog were friends. They played together in the sun. Tim was happy to write his story. But then, the wind blew through the window. It was a strong wind. The wind took the paper away from Tim. He tried to catch it, but he could not. The paper flew out the window and was gone. Tim was very sad. His story was lost, and he could not write it again.

Lily and Ben are cousins. They like to play together at their uncle's house. One day, they find a big ball in the garage. They want to play with it, but they do not ask their uncle first. They take the ball to the backyard and start to kick it. The ball is very bouncy and goes very high. Lily and Ben have fun, but they do not see where the ball goes. The ball hits a window and breaks it. Lily and Ben hear a loud noise and get scared. They run to the garage and hide the ball. They hope their uncle does not find out. But their uncle hears the noise too. He comes to the backyard and sees the broken window. He is angry and sad. He likes his window. He looks for Lily and Ben. He finds them in the garage. He asks them, "Did you break my window?" Lily and Ben are quiet. They do not want to tell the truth. They are afraid their uncle will be mad at them. But their uncle knows they did it. He says, "You should not lie to me. You should tell me what you did. You should say sorry. You should ask me before you play with my things. You should be careful and respectful." Lily and Ben feel bad. They know their uncle is right. They say, "We are sorry, uncle. We broke your window. We did not ask you. We were not careful and respectful. We will not do it again." Their uncle hugs them. He says, "Thank you for saying sorry. I forgive you. I love you. But you have to help me fix the window. And you have to play with your own toys. Or you have to ask me first. Do you understand?" Lily and Ben nod. They say, "We understand, uncle. We will help you fix the window. We will play with our own toys. Or we will ask you first. We love you too." They go to the backyard and help their uncle fix the window. They learn their lesson. They learn to be honest, to say sorry, and to respect other people's things.
