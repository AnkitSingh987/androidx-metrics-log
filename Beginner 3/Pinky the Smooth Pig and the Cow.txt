Category: Beginner
Title: Pinky the Smooth Pig and the Cow

Once upon a time, there was a smooth pig named Pinky. Pinky lived on a small farm with her friends. She loved to play in the mud and roll around all day. One day, Pinky saw her friend, the cow, making pretty prints in the mud with her hooves. Pinky said, "Wow, I want to make prints like that too!" The cow replied, "You can do it, Pinky! Just use your hooves and try." So Pinky tried to make prints in the mud, but her prints did not look as nice as the cow's prints. Pinky was sad. Then, the wise old owl saw Pinky and asked, "Why are you sad, Pinky?" Pinky told the owl about her prints. The owl said, "Don't worry, Pinky. We all have different prints. Your prints are special because they are yours." Pinky thought about it and said, "You are right, owl. I should be happy with my prints." From that day on, Pinky was happy with her prints and played in the mud with her friends. The moral of the story is to be happy with what makes you special.

Once, there was a little boy named Tom. He loved to watch birds. One day, he saw a small, fearful bird in his yard. The bird was lost and could not find its way home. Tom wanted to help the bird. He said, "Don't be scared, bird. I will keep you safe." The bird looked at Tom and felt better. Tom made a warm, cozy nest for the bird to stay in. Every day, Tom would watch over the bird and play with it. Soon, the bird was not fearful anymore. It was happy and safe with Tom. And they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a wealthy man. He had a big house near a creek. The man did not like to share his things with others. One day, a little boy came to the man's house. The boy was lost and hungry. The boy asked the wealthy man for help. But the man did not believe the boy. He thought the boy was trying to take his things. The man sent the boy away. The little boy walked by the creek and fell in. He could not swim. The boy called for help, but no one could hear him. The wealthy man did not know what happened to the boy. The boy did not have a happy ending.

One day, a little fly went out to play. The fly loved to fly high in the sky. It was very fun for the fly. But sometimes, it was difficult for the fly to find its way home. One day, the fly went very far away. It was so far that the fly got lost. The fly was sad and scared. It did not know how to go back home. The fly tried very hard to find its way, but it was too difficult. Then, something unexpected happened. The little fly met a big bird. The bird was kind and wanted to help the fly. The bird told the fly to follow it. The bird knew where the fly's home was. The fly was very happy and followed the bird. They flew together, and soon the fly was back home. The fly thanked the bird and they became good friends.

Mommy and Daddy put on their coats. They held the baby's hand and they went outside. They walked to the street. Mommy put the baby in a stroller. She looked both ways and saw a lot of traffic. Daddy smiled and said, "Let's cross the street." He held the baby's hand and started to go. The traffic was too much and the baby was scared. He was too young to cross the street by himself. Mommy held his hand and said, "It's ok, sweetheart. Follow Mommy and we will get across the street together. Walk slow and hold Mommy's hand. It's ok. I won't let go." The baby held onto her tight and the two of them crossed the street. They were safe when they were done. Mommy was so proud that the baby was so brave and strong.
