Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Cookie

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved cookies. One day, he saw a mighty big cookie jar on the kitchen table. He wanted to eat all the cookies inside. Tim had a plan. He would go to the cookie jar when his mom was not looking. When his mom went to the other room, Tim ran to the jar. He was so happy! But when he opened the jar, he found no cookies inside. Instead, there was a note. The note said, "You are a good boy, Tim. Go to your room for a surprise." Tim went to his room and found a big plate of cookies waiting for him. His mom knew he loved cookies and wanted to give him a special treat. Tim learned that good things come to those who wait.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to zip her coat. One day, she went to the park with her mom. The sun was shining and the sky was blue. At the park, Sue saw a thin cat. The cat was hungry. Sue wanted to help the cat. She looked around and found a piece of bread on the ground. Sue said, "Here, kitty! Eat this piece of bread." The thin cat ate the piece of bread. It was very happy. Sue and her mom played with the cat. They had a fun day at the park. Sue learned that it was nice to help others.

Once upon a time, there was a big red ball. The ball had a little spot on it. One day, the ball met a small blue ball. The blue ball said, "Hello! Can we play together?" The red ball said, "No! I am too big and you are too small. Go away!" The blue ball felt sad and went away. It met a little green ball. The green ball was very nice. They played together and had fun. The red ball saw them and felt a pinch in its heart. It wanted to play too, but it was too selfish to share with others. In the end, the red ball stayed alone with its spot. The blue and green balls played together and had lots of fun. The red ball wished it could join, but it was too late. It had learned that being selfish only brings a bad ending.

One day, a hairy dog named Spot went for a walk. Spot liked to be quiet when he walked. He saw a cat named Fluffy. Fluffy was not quiet. Fluffy was loud. Spot said, "Fluffy, can you be quiet? I want to record the sounds of the birds." Fluffy said, "No, I like to be loud! I like to chase the birds." Spot was sad. He wanted to enjoy the quiet and record the sounds. Then, Spot had an idea. He said, "Fluffy, if you are quiet, we can play a game after I record the sounds." Fluffy thought about it and said, "Okay, I will be quiet." Spot was happy. He recorded the sounds of the birds. After that, Spot and Fluffy played a fun game together. They both had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a little dancer named Lily. She loved to dance and twirl all day long. One day, she was going to perform in a big show. She was so happy and excited. Before the show, Lily had to prepare. She put on her pretty dress and shiny shoes. Her mom helped her with her hair. "You look so pretty," said her mom. Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Mom!" But when it was time to go on stage, Lily felt upset. She was scared to dance in front of so many people. Her friend, Tim, saw her looking sad. He said, "Don't worry, Lily. Just have fun and dance like you always do." Lily took a deep breath and went on stage. She danced her best and everyone clapped for her. Lily was so happy and proud of herself.

Once upon a time, in an enormous universe, there was a little star named Twinkle. Twinkle loved to travel and see new things. One day, Twinkle saw a big, dark cloud in the sky. "Hello, big cloud," said Twinkle. "I want to travel past you to see the other stars." "No," said the big cloud. "You can't go past me. I am too big and dark." Twinkle was sad. But then, she had an idea. She asked all her star friends to come and help her. They all came together and shined their light very bright. The big cloud saw all the bright light from Twinkle and her friends. The cloud started to go away. "Okay, you can go past me," said the big cloud. Twinkle and her friends were happy. They all traveled through the enormous universe and saw many new things. And they all lived happily ever after.
