Category: Beginner
Title: Sam and the Rock

Sam and his mom were walking down the black road. Sam asked his mom, "What is that noise?" His mom said, "It's the sound of the rock on the road." They walked a bit more and saw a big rock blocking the road. It was black and shiny. Sam was excited and asked, "Can I play with it?" His mom said yes, so Sam picked up the rock and started to rock it back and forth. He rocked it harder and harder, until it flew off the road and into a nearby pond. Sam said, "Whoa! I didn't mean to make it fly that far!" His mom laughed and said, "That's ok, sometimes things fly away when we rock them!" Sam smiled and said, "Let's go find another rock to rock!" So together, they walked further down the black road and looked for more rocks to rock.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max loved to play with his ball and run in the park. One day, Max saw a yummy treat on the table. He wanted to eat the treat, but it was too high for him to reach. Max had a friend, a little bird named Tim. Max yelled to Tim, "Help me get the treat!" Tim flew to the table and used his beak to push the treat down. The treat fell on the floor and Max was very happy. Max and Tim shared the treat and played with the ball in the park. They were good friends and loved to help each other. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to improve his drum sound. He loved to play the drums and make impressive beats. Tim practiced every day to get better and better. His friends liked to listen to him play. One sunny day, Tim was playing his drums when he heard a strange sound. It was coming from the woods near his house. Tim was curious, so he followed the sound. He walked and walked until he found a big, shiny rock. Tim tapped the rock with his drumsticks, and it made a beautiful sound. He played his drums along with the rock, and it sounded amazing. But then, the rock started to move! It wasn't a rock at all; it was a friendly turtle who also loved music. The turtle had been hiding in its shell. Tim and the turtle became best friends. They played music together every day, and their sound got better and better. Everyone thought their music was very impressive, and they were happy to share it with their friends.

Once upon a time, there was a hairy dog named Spot. Spot loved to play in the yard. One day, Spot saw a big rake. The rake was used to clean the yard. Spot thought it was a big toy. Spot's friend, Tim, saw the rake too. Tim wanted to help clean the yard. He encouraged Spot to help him. "Come on, Spot! Let's use the rake to clean up," said Tim. Spot wagged his tail and barked. They were excited to work together. Tim and Spot used the rake to clean the yard. They picked up leaves and sticks. They made a big pile. When they were done, they jumped in the pile and laughed. They had so much fun with the rake.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom wanted to go on a journey. He asked his mom and dad if he could go. They said yes, but it would be expensive. Tom was happy and said he would save his money. Tom saved his money for a long time. He worked hard and did not buy toys. One day, he had enough money for the journey. He told his mom and dad. They were proud of him. Tom went on the journey. He saw many new things and met new friends. They all laughed and played together. But one day, Tom lost all his money. He was sad and could not go home. Tom's journey had a bad ending.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. It was a cold, icy day. Tim wanted to play on the slide, but there was a long line. He did not like waiting, so he said, "I hate this line!" A girl named Sue was in front of Tim. She heard him and said, "I don't like lines too. Let's play a game while we wait." Tim and Sue played a fun game called "I Spy" to pass the time. As they played, something unexpected happened. The line went away very fast! Tim and Sue looked at each other and smiled. They learned that playing together made the time go by quickly. Now, they could enjoy the slide and had a new friend to play with.
