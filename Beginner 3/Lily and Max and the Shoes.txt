Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Max and the Shoes

Lily and Max were best friends. They liked to play together in the park. One day, they found a big map on a bench. The map had many colors and pictures of animals and places. "Wow, look at this map!" Lily said. "It shows us where the zoo is. Let's go see the lions and the monkeys!" Max nodded. He loved the zoo. He folded the map and put it in his pocket. They ran to the bus stop and waited for the bus. On the way, they saw a big fountain. It had water that went up and down. Lily and Max wanted to splash in the fountain. They got off the bus and ran to the fountain. They took off their shoes and socks and jumped in. They laughed and splashed each other. They felt the water on their faces and legs. They had so much fun. But then, they saw a boy watching them. He had a sad face and a dirty shirt. He did not have any shoes or socks. He looked envious of Lily and Max. He wanted to splash in the fountain too, but he was afraid. Lily and Max saw the boy. They felt sorry for him. They wanted to share their fun with him. They waved at him and smiled. "Hi, what's your name?" Lily asked. "Tom," the boy said shyly. "Do you want to splash with us?" Max asked. Tom nodded. He came closer to the fountain. Lily and Max helped him get in. They gave him some of their water toys. They splashed him gently and made him laugh. They became friends. Tom was happy. He thanked Lily and Max for being nice to him. He asked them where they were going. "We are going to the zoo," Lily said. "We have a map that shows us the way. Do you want to come with us?" Tom nodded. He wanted to see the zoo. He had never been there before. He was curious about the animals and places. Lily and Max were happy too. They liked Tom. They wanted to show him the zoo. They took him by the hand and ran to the bus stop. They got on the bus and showed him the map. They pointed at the animals and places they wanted to see. They went to the zoo and had a wonderful day. They saw the lions and the monkeys and many other animals. They learned new things and had fun. They shared their snacks and stories. They became best friends. They were glad they found the map and the fountain and each other.

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog named Max. Max loved to play with his friends. One day, Max had to go on a big trip with his family. He needed his passport to go on the trip. Max looked everywhere for his passport. He looked under his bed, in the toy box, and behind the door. But he could not find it. Max was sad because he thought he lost his passport. Then, Max's friend, a little girl named Lily, came over to play. She saw the passport behind the couch. Max was so happy! He thanked Lily and they played together. Max did not lose his passport, and he could go on the trip with his family.

Once there was a chubby little boy called Jack. He had a big sister named Lucy. One day he was very curious and asked his sister a question: "What is the biggest animal in the world?" Lucy giggled and said "I know the answer! It's the elephant." Jack was so amazed. He asked his sister lots of questions, like "What colour are elephants?" and "Where do they live?" Lucy knew the answers to them all and she smiled and told Jack all about the big animals. Jack thought how great it was to have a big sister who knew so much!

Once upon a time, there was a new ornament. It was a pretty star. The star lived on top of a big tree. The tree was in a nice house. One day, a small girl named Amy saw the star. She wanted to touch it. Amy took a step on a chair. Then, she took another step on the table. She was close to the tree. "Be careful, Amy," said her mom. "The tree might fall." Amy stopped and looked at her mom. She knew her mom was right. She took a step back and smiled. The pretty star was safe on the tree. And everyone was happy.
