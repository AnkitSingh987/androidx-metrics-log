Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Tree and the Woods

Once upon a time, there was a big, rough pine tree in the woods. It had many green leaves, and little animals liked to play near it. One sunny day, a small bunny named Ben was hopping around the tree. As Ben hopped, he saw a little girl named Sue taking a step near the pine tree. Sue saw Ben and said, "Hi, little bunny! Do you want to play with me?" Ben nodded his head and they started to play together. They played hide and seek, and tag around the rough pine tree. They laughed and had a lot of fun. When it was time for Sue to go home, she said, "Bye, Ben! Let's play again soon!" Ben waved goodbye, and they both knew they had found a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a fast car. The car liked to speed down the street. The car was red and shiny. A young boy named Tim loved the car. Tim was a youth who liked to play with cars. One day, Tim saw the car speed by his house. He wanted to see where the car was going. Tim ran after the car. The car went into a deep hole. Tim was scared, but he wanted to help the car. Tim went into the deep hole too. But, the hole was not a hole. It was a big, bouncy bed! Tim and the car jumped and played on the bed. They had so much fun. The car was not stuck, it was just playing. They were happy and played all day.

Once upon a time, there was a furry elephant named Elly. Elly had a big trunk and loved to play with her friends in the jungle. Every day, they would run, jump, and laugh together. One day, Elly and her friends decided to weigh themselves. They found a big scale and took turns standing on it. Elly went first. She used her trunk to hold on to a tree branch and stepped on the scale. "I weigh a lot!" said Elly. Her friends laughed and clapped their hands. Next, it was her friends' turn. They all weighed less than Elly, but they didn't mind. They knew that Elly was special because of her big trunk and furry body. At the end of the day, they all hugged and went home. They were happy to be friends, no matter how much they weighed.

One day, a man named Tom had to pack his bag. He was going to his office. He put in his lunch, a book, and a toy. Tom liked to play with toys when he had a break at work. He was excited to go to the office. At the office, Tom had a lot of work to do. He felt frustrated. He wanted to play with his toy, but he had to finish his work first. Tom worked hard to finish his work quickly, so he could play with his toy. Finally, Tom finished his work. He was happy. He took out his toy and played with it. Tom felt better after playing with his toy. He packed his toy back in his bag and went home. Tom had a good day at the office.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat and a small dog. They lived in a nice house. The cat liked to rest on one side of the house, and the dog liked to rest on the other side. They were not friends, but they were not mean to each other. One day, the sky was very clear and the sun was shining. The cat and the dog were both resting on their sides of the house. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise. They both jumped up and ran to see what it was. When they got to the noise, they saw a big box. The cat and the dog looked at each other, and then they looked at the box. They were scared, but they were also curious. They slowly walked to the box, and the cat jumped on top of it. The dog sniffed the box, and then he jumped on it too. The box opened, and out came a big, fun toy for them to play with. The cat and the dog looked at the toy, and then they looked at each other. They started to play with the toy together, and they became best friends. The unexpected toy made them happy and they learned to share and have fun together.
