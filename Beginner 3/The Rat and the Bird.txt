Category: Beginner
Title: The Rat and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little rat. The rat was worried. He could not find a place to sleep. He looked and looked, but all the places were too big or too small. One day, the rat saw a big tree. He thought it would be a good place to sleep. He climbed up and up. When he got to the top, he found a big hole. The rat was very happy. He went inside to sleep. But, when the rat went to sleep, something unexpected happened. A big bird came to the tree. The big bird was the real owner of the hole. The big bird was nice and did not get mad. Instead, the big bird helped the rat find a new place to sleep. The rat was not worried anymore. They became friends and lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there were two children, Susie and Tom. They were very excited, because today was their first day of school! They put on their backpacks and their most favourite clothes and headed off to school. When they entered the school, their teacher welcomed them warmly and showed them to their room. It was a very quiet room, with a lot of desks and chairs. Susie and Tom felt comfortable and excited at the same time. After a while, it was time for Susie and Tom to play. The teacher said they could play outside, but first they had to stay in their quiet room until the bell rang. Susie and Tom had so much fun staying quiet together. They pretended to be cats and dogs, snuck around like goblins and even had a tea party with their imaginary friends! When the bell finally rang, Susie and Tom raced outside to enjoy their first day at school!

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to sing. Every day, she would go to the park and sing songs with her friends. One day, while Mia was singing, she saw a filthy grape on the ground. "Yuck!" she said. "Who left this dirty grape here?" Mia's friend, Tim, came over and looked at the grape too. "I don't know," he said. "But we should throw it away." Mia and Tim picked up the filthy grape and threw it in the trash. Then, they went back to singing with their friends. They sang and sang until the sun went down. And when it was time to go home, they said goodbye to their friends and promised to meet again the next day to sing some more.

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat named Tom. Tom liked to play outside all day. He would run, jump, and climb trees. One day, Tom stayed out too long and it started to get dark. Tom began to worry. He did not like the dark. It was hard to see and he felt scared. He wanted to go home, but he did not know the way. Tom sat under a tree and cried. Just then, a kind bird saw Tom. The bird knew Tom was lost and wanted to help. The bird flew up high and looked for Tom's house. When the bird found it, he showed Tom the way home. Tom was happy and thanked the bird. They became friends and played together every day.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They have a red ball and a yellow kite. They run and laugh and have fun. One day, they see a big hill. They want to climb it and see the view. They hold hands and walk up the hill. It is hard and they are tired, but they keep going. When they reach the top, they smile. They see the park, the trees, the flowers, the birds, and the sky. They see their homes and their families. They see the sun and the clouds. They think the view is beautiful. "Wow, look at that!" Lily says. "It is so pretty!" Ben says. They sit on the grass and share a sandwich. They are happy and hungry. They thank each other for being kind and sharing. They fly their kite and watch it soar. They kick their ball and catch it. They sing and dance and hug. They love the view and they love each other. They are the best of friends.
