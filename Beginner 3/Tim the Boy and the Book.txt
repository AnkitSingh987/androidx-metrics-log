Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Book

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim had a big bookshelf in his room. He loved to read books every day. One day, he saw a book on the top shelf. He wondered how to get it. "Mom, can you help me get the book?" Tim asked. His mom was busy and said, "Not now, Tim. I am making dinner. You can try to get it yourself." Tim felt sad. He tried to reach the book but he was too small. He thought and thought. Then, he had an idea. He got his toy box and stood on it. He reached up and got the book. He felt so happy. Tim showed his mom the book. She smiled and said, "Good job, Tim! You solved the problem all by yourself." They sat down and read the book together. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lucy had a big box. She wanted to unpack the box in her room. Inside the box, she found an adorable toy bear. Lucy was very happy and wanted to play with the bear. Lucy took the bear to her room and said, "Bear, let's play together in my space." The bear nodded and they played all day. They had so much fun, laughing and playing in her room. But then, it was time for Lucy to go to bed. She told the bear, "Bear, I need to sleep now. You stay here." The bear looked sad and didn't want Lucy to go. In the night, the bear got up and made a big mess in Lucy's room. When Lucy woke up, she saw the mess and was very sad. The bear was not a good friend after all.

Once upon a time, there was a big party. Everyone was happy and having fun. There was a big table with lots of food and drinks. One of the drinks was a red juice called wine. Some people liked it, but others thought it was disgusting. A little girl named Lily was at the party with her mom and dad. She saw the red juice and wanted to try it. Her mom said, "No Lily, that is wine. It is not for kids." Lily was sad, but she listened to her mom. The party was almost at the end. Lily's mom and dad said it was time to go home. They said goodbye to their friends and left the party. Lily was happy that she had a fun time, even if she didn't get to try the red juice.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to watch a video. He asked his mom, "Can I watch a video, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but first you need to clean your room." Tim did not want to clean his room, but he really wanted to watch the video. So, he cleaned his room very fast. After Tim cleaned his room, he went to his mom and said, "I cleaned my room! Can I watch the video now?" His mom looked at his room and saw that it was not very clean. She said, "You need to do a better job." Tim was sad, but he went back to his room and cleaned it more. This time, Tim cleaned his room very smooth. He put all his toys away and made his bed. He went back to his mom and said, "I cleaned my room better! Can I watch the video now?" His mom looked at his room and saw that it was very clean. She said, "Good job, Tim! Now you can watch the video." Tim was very happy and he watched the video. He knew that if he did what his mom asked, he would get what he wanted.

Once upon a time, there was a polite dog named Max. Max loved to play with his basketball. One sunny day, Max went to the park to play with his friends. At the park, Max saw his friend, a cat named Kitty. Kitty was sitting on a bench. Max said, "Hi, Kitty! Do you want to play with my basketball?" Kitty replied, "Yes, Max! I would love to play!" Max and Kitty played with the basketball for a long time. They were very happy. After playing, they sat under a tree to rest. Max and Kitty were the best of friends, and they loved playing together.
