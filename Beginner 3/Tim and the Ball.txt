Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Ball

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his dad. They took a big bag with them. In the bag, there were golf things. Tim was very excited to play golf with his dad. At the park, they found a nice place to play. They put the golf things on the ground. Tim's dad showed him how to hit the ball. Tim tried to hit the ball, but it was hard. He felt frustrated. "I can't do it," Tim said. Just then, a magic cat appeared. The cat said, "Don't be sad, Tim. I will help you." The cat touched the golf stick with its paw. Tim tried again, and this time, he hit the ball very far. Tim was happy, and his dad was proud. They played golf all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was sad and miserable because he had no friends to play with. He wanted to find a way to make friends, so he decided to think of an idea. Tim thought and thought, and finally, he decided to make a toy weapon. He found some sticks and strings and made a toy sword. He was very proud of his toy weapon and believed it would help him make friends. The next day, Tim took his toy weapon to the park. He saw other kids playing and showed them his toy sword. They liked it and wanted to play with Tim. They all played together, pretending to be knights and having fun. Tim was not miserable anymore, and he made many friends with his toy weapon.

Once upon a time there was an adorable bear who lived in a little home by the woods. The bear was always very happy and liked to measure things and make sure they were just right. One day, the bear decided to measure some plants in the woods. While he was measuring, the bear suddenly felt himself shrink! He looked around and tried to find out what had happened, but he couldn't tell. All he knew was that he was shrinking. He kept on shrinking until he was so small that he scared away. Everything became blurry and the bear felt so alone. The bear eventually stopped shrinking, but his measure was nowhere to be found. He was so sad about it and could not find his way back home. The bear never got to return to his adorable home and was never seen again.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim had a dark pen. He loved to draw with it. One day, his friend, Sam, asked, "Can you bring your pen to my house? I want to draw too." Tim said, "Sure!" and they both went to Sam's house. But when they got there, they saw Tim's little sister, Sue, had taken the pen. She was drawing on the wall! The wall now had many dark marks. Tim and Sam were not happy. They told Sue, "You should not draw on the wall. That's not good!" Sue started to cry. She didn't know it was wrong. Tim and Sam helped Sue clean the wall. Then, they all drew together on paper with the dark pen. They had fun and learned to share.

Lily and Ben were playing on the beach. They liked to dig in the sand and find shells. Lily found a big shell that was shiny and pink. She was very happy. "Look, Ben, I found a pretty shell!" she said, holding it up. Ben looked at the shell and felt jealous. He wanted a pretty shell too. He only had a small shell that was brown and dull. "That's not a pretty shell, that's a silly shell!" he said, trying to tease Lily. "My shell is better than yours!" Lily felt sad and angry. She did not like Ben's words. She thought her shell was very pretty and not silly at all. "No, your shell is not better, your shell is boring!" she said, sticking out her tongue. "My shell is the best!" They started to argue and shout. They did not want to share or play nicely. They forgot to have fun. A big wave came and splashed them. They got wet and cold. They stopped arguing and looked at the water. They saw a crab crawling out of the wave. It had big claws and eyes. It looked hungry. The crab saw the shells in their hands. It wanted to eat them. It moved towards them, snapping its claws. "Uh-oh, look, a crab!" Lily said, feeling scared. "Let's run, it's hungry!" Ben said, feeling scared too. They dropped their shells and ran away. They did not want to be eaten by the crab. They ran to their mom and dad, who were sitting on a blanket. They hugged them and felt safe. They looked back at the beach. They saw the crab picking up their shells and taking them to its hole. They felt sorry for their shells. They wished they had not fought over them. They learned to be kind and not tease. They decided to play together and find more shells. They had fun again.
