Category: Beginner
Title: Laura and the Garden

There was a little girl called Laura. She was three years old and she loved blueberries. She went to the garden one day to pick blueberries. She looked around carefully, but she couldn't find any. As Laura turned around to go back home, she saw something high in the tree! It was a big, juicy blueberry! Laura asked her dad if he could help her to reach the blueberry. He frowned, but then he smiled and said, “Yes, I can help you.” He put his hands around her waist and lifted her up. She slowly stretched out her arm and grabbed the blueberry. It was delicious! Laura’s dad said, “It’s time to go now.” Laura smiled and replied, “Can I come back tomorrow to pick more blueberries?” Her dad smiled and said, “Of course, but this time, I will help you look for them.” Laura was so happy and she was looking forward to going back to the garden the next day.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy cat named Silver. Silver loved to play and jump around the house. One day, Silver saw a big book on the floor. Silver wanted to read the book. Silver sat down and opened the book. The book had many pictures of cats, dogs, and birds. Silver liked the pictures and tried to read the words. But, Silver was a clumsy cat, and the book fell on the floor. Silver's friend, a little girl named Lily, saw the book on the floor. Lily picked up the book and started to read it to Silver. Silver was happy to listen to Lily read the book. They both had a fun time reading and looking at the pictures together.

Lily and Ben were playing in the kitchen. They liked to pretend they were chefs and make food with the pots and pans. Lily saw the freezer and opened it. She saw a big tub of ice cream. She wanted some. "Ben, look! Ice cream!" she yelled. Ben ran over and saw the ice cream too. He wanted some as well. "Can we have some, please?" he asked. Lily nodded. She grabbed a spoon and scooped some ice cream. She put it in her mouth. It was very cold and bitter. She made a face and spat it out. "Yuck! This is not ice cream!" she cried. Ben looked at the tub. It said "Lemon Sorbet". He did not know what that meant. He thought it was ice cream too. He took a spoonful and tried it. He also felt the cold and bitter taste. He spit it out too. "Yuck! This is not ice cream!" he agreed. They threw the spoons in the sink and ran away from the freezer. They did not like lemon sorbet. They wanted real ice cream. They decided to play with something else. They hoped their mom would come home soon and give them some real ice cream. They liked sweet and creamy ice cream. Not cold and bitter ice cream.

Once upon a time, there was a big room with a floor. On the floor, there were many toys. The toys liked to play with each other when no one was around. They would jump and dance on the floor, having lots of fun. One day, a new toy came to the room. It was a small toy car. The car wanted to play with the other toys, but it did not know how to be careful. It would zoom around the floor very fast, making the other toys scared. The other toys told the car to be more careful. They showed the car how to play nicely and be safe on the floor. The car learned to be careful, and all the toys had fun playing together. They were happy and enjoyed their time on the floor.

Sara and Ben like to play with cars. They have many cars of different colors and sizes. They make a line of cars on the floor and pretend they are racing. One day, Sara has a new car. It is shiny and red and fast. Ben has an old car. It is dull and blue and slow. Sara says, "My car is better than your car. I will win the race." They start the race. Sara's car zooms ahead of Ben's car. Ben tries to catch up, but his car is too slow. Sara laughs and says, "I told you I will win. You have a dull car." Ben feels sad. He does not like to lose. He does not like Sara's laugh. He says, "It is not fair. You have a new car. I have an old car. Let me try your car." Sara says, "No, this is my car. You have your car. We can race again." They race again. Sara's car wins again. Ben feels sadder. He does not want to play with Sara anymore. He says, "You are not nice. You only care about winning. I do not like you." He picks up his car and goes to his room. Sara feels sorry. She does not want to make Ben sad. She does not want to lose her friend. She says, "Wait, Ben. I am sorry. You can try my car. I can try your car. We can share. We can have fun." She follows Ben to his room. She gives him her car and takes his car. They race again. This time, Ben's car wins. Ben smiles. Sara smiles. They hug and say, "We are friends. We do not care about winning. We care about playing."
