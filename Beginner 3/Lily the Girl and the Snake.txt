Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Snake

One day, a girl named Lily went for a walk. She was a very reliable friend. She always helped her friends when they needed it. Lily saw a snake on the path. The snake was big and long, and it was in her way. Lily was scared, but she knew she had to keep going. So, she took a deep breath and walked closer to the snake. The snake moved and made a hissing noise. Lily was brave and decided to slap the snake away. With a big slap, the snake moved away from the path. Lily was happy that she was able to get past the snake. She continued her walk and told her friends about the snake. They were happy that Lily was so brave and reliable.

Once upon a time, there was a little boat. The boat loved to sail on the mild water. One day, the boat met a duck. "Hi, duck!" said the boat. "Do you want to sail with me?" "Yes, boat!" said the duck. "I like the mild water too!" So, the boat and the duck sailed together. They saw a big tree by the water. Under the tree, they found a bag of oats. "Look, duck!" said the boat. "We found oats! Let's eat them." The duck and the boat ate the oats. They were very happy. They sailed together every day and became the best of friends.

One day, a little fish named Fin wanted to play with his friends. They all lived in a big, blue sea. Fin loved to swim fast and dive deep. His friends liked to watch him dive and splash in the water. They would clap and cheer for him. Fin and his friends found a soft, green plant. They thought it was a fun place to play. They swam around it and played hide and seek. Fin hid behind the soft plant, and his friends tried to find him. Near the soft plant, they saw a band of shiny fish. The shiny fish were swimming and playing music. Fin and his friends loved the music. They danced and played with the shiny fish band all day. They had so much fun and made many new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big gorilla named Gogo. He lived in a hidden cave in the forest. Gogo loved to play with his friends, the little birds and the squirrels. One sunny day, Gogo found a big, yellow ball in the forest. He was very happy and wanted to play with it. He picked up the ball and gave it a big squeeze. The ball made a funny sound, "Squeak!" Gogo laughed and squeezed the ball again. Gogo's friend, a little bird, flew down and asked, "What is that, Gogo?" Gogo smiled and said, "It's a fun ball! It makes a funny sound when I squeeze it. Do you want to try?" The little bird tried to squeeze the ball, but it was too big for her. So, Gogo helped her, and they both laughed at the funny sound. Together, Gogo and his friends played with the ball all day long. They took turns squeezing it and listening to the funny sound it made. When the sun went down, they said goodbye and went back to their hidden homes in the forest. Gogo kept the ball in his cave, and they played with it every day.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree. The tree had many fruits. The tree's job was to yield fruits for everyone. One day, a little boy came to the tree. He wanted to get the fruits. But the tree was very tall. The boy could not get the fruits. Then, a kind bird came. The bird helped the boy. The bird took the fruits from the tree and gave them to the boy. The boy was happy. The tree did its job and the bird was a good friend.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a big chocolate. It was different from other chocolates because it was very big. Tim was happy. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came to play. Sam saw the big chocolate and said, "Wow! Your chocolate is so big and different! Can I have some?" Tim said, "No, it's mine." Sam was sad. Tim went to clean his room. When he came back, the big chocolate was gone! Tim cried and cried. He should have shared his big chocolate with Sam. Now, no one can enjoy the chocolate.
