Category: Beginner
Title: Jimmy and Maria and the Ball

Jimmy and Maria were playing at the park. They had been playing for hours and were having so much fun. They ran and ran, shouted and laughed. Suddenly, Jimmy saw a big, red ball and he ran over to it. He started to kick it with his feet. He kicked it so hard that the ball went into the bushes. Maria went to look and found a big, silver battery. It was very comforting to hold. She showed it to Jimmy and he said, "Let's take it home!" So they did. They took it home and put it away in a safe place. From then on they played with the battery every day. They took it on walks and had lots of fun.

Joey was so proud of his new toy truck. He shared it with his Mom and Dad and they were very proud of him too. Joey’s Mom said, “Joey let's go for a drive and show your toy truck around the neighborhood.” Joey smiled and nodded his head. Joey put his toy truck in the car and he and his Mom went for a ride. As they drove, Joey looked out the window and imagined his truck driving all over the place. He kept nodding his head as he watched. When they got to the house, Joey grabbed his truck and ran to the front door. He was so excited to show off his new truck to his friends. But, when he walked in the house, his foot slipped on the shiny floor. His truck went flying across the room and smashed into pieces. Joey looked so sad and he was so embarrassed. His Mom was so disappointed, too. She put her arm around him and said, “We can get you another truck but we need to be more careful.” Joey nodded his head, but he was no longer proud. He slowly picked up the pieces of his broken truck and held them close.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a deaf dog named Spot. Spot could not hear anything, but he was very good at feeling things. He had a best friend named Tom, who was a little boy. One day, Tom wanted to play with Spot. He went to the kitchen and saw Spot near the tap. The tap was dripping water. Tom insisted that Spot should come and play with him outside. Spot felt the vibrations from Tom's feet and knew that Tom wanted to play. So, Spot and Tom went outside and played in the sun. They ran and jumped and had a great time. Even though Spot was deaf, he was still a very happy dog. Tom and Spot were best friends forever.

Lily was eager to play with her new doll. She had got it for her birthday and it was very pretty. It had long hair, a pink dress and shiny shoes. Lily wanted to show it to her friend Mia, who lived next door. She ran to the door and called, "Mama, can I go to Mia's house?" Mama was in the kitchen, making a cake. She said, "Yes, you can, but be careful on the stairs. And don't forget your coat, it's cold outside." Lily put on her coat and grabbed her doll. She ran down the stairs, but she was so excited that she missed a step and fell. She hurt her knee and dropped her doll. She started to cry. Mama heard her and came to help. She picked up Lily and her doll and hugged them. She said, "Oh, sweetie, are you okay? Let me see your knee. It's just a little scratch, don't worry. I have a band-aid for you. And your doll is fine, too. See, she's smiling at you." Lily stopped crying and smiled back at her doll. She said, "Thank you, Mama. You are very nice. Can I still go to Mia's house?" Mama said, "Of course you can. But be more careful on the stairs, okay? And have fun with Mia. Maybe you can include her in your doll game." Lily nodded and kissed Mama. She said, "I will, Mama. I love you." She put on her coat again and held her doll. She walked to Mia's house, being careful on the stairs. She knocked on the door and Mia opened it. She said, "Hi, Lily. Come in. What do you have there?" Lily said, "It's my new doll. She's very pretty. Do you want to play with her?" Mia said, "Yes, I do. She's very pretty. Come on, let's go to my room. I have more dolls there. We can have a tea party." Lily said, "Okay, let's go. I'm eager to play with you and your dolls." They went to Mia's room and had a lot of fun. They included all their dolls in their tea party and shared their cake and juice. They were very happy.
