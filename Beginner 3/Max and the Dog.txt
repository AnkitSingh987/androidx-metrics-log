Category: Beginner
Title: Max and the Dog

Sara has a dog named Max. Max is big and brown and likes to run and play. Sara loves Max very much and gives him hugs and kisses every day. One day, Sara and Max go to the park with Mom. They see many other dogs and people there. Max runs to a yellow ball and brings it back to Sara. Sara throws the ball again and again. Max is happy and wags his tail. But then, Max starts to scratch. He scratches his ears, his back, his legs. He stops playing and looks sad. Sara is worried and goes to Max. She sees a tiny black bug on Max's fur. It is a flea. The flea bites Max and makes him itch. "Mom, look!" Sara says. "Max has a flea! It hurts him!" Mom comes and sees the flea too. She picks it up and throws it away. She says, "Don't worry, Sara. We will give Max a bath and some medicine. He will be fine." Sara smiles and hugs Max. She says, "I'm sorry, Max. I love you. You are a good dog." Max licks Sara's face and wags his tail. He smiles too. He loves Sara. She is a good girl.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy cat named Tom. Tom was always grumpy because he did not have any friends. One day, Tom was walking on the ground when something magical happened. A big, red ball appeared in front of Tom. He was very surprised and happy. He played with the ball all day long, rolling it and chasing it. Tom was not grumpy anymore. Soon, other cats saw Tom playing with the big, red ball. They wanted to play too. Tom shared his ball with the other cats and they all played together. Tom was not grumpy anymore because he had made new friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the calm lake with his dad. They brought a small boat to play with. Tim was very excited to put the boat in the water. Tim's dad said, "Okay, Tim, let's put the boat in the water and see it float." They put the boat in the water and watched it go. Tim was so happy to see the boat floating on the calm water. After a while, it was time to go home. Tim's dad said, "Tim, can you help me pull the boat out of the water?" Tim grabbed the string tied to the boat and pulled it out of the water. He felt very proud that he could help his dad. On their way home, Tim told his dad, "I had so much fun playing with the boat today. Thank you, Dad!" His dad smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim. We can come back to the calm lake and play with the boat again soon." Tim couldn't wait for the next time they could go to the calm lake and play with the boat. He knew he could help his dad pull the boat out of the water again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a lot of furniture in his room. He had a bed, a chair, and a table. Tim loved to play with his toys on the furniture. One day, Tim was playing with his toys on the table. He was so happy. But then, Tim started to feel worried. He heard a noise coming from his bed. He thought, "What could it be?" Tim went to his bed to see what was making the noise. He saw a little bird under his bed. The bird was scared and worried too. Tim said, "Don't worry, little bird. I will help you." Tim opened his window and the bird flew away. Tim felt happy again, and he went back to playing with his toys. The next morning, Tim woke up and saw the bird outside his window. The bird was happy and not worried anymore. They both became friends and played together every day.

One day, a little girl named Lucy wanted to help her mom make a cake. Her mom had to explain how to use a teaspoon. Lucy was excited to learn. Lucy's mom said, "Be careful, the teaspoon is not too big. We need just a little bit of sugar." Lucy listened and tried her best. She put the sugar in the bowl. But it was a little bit harsh when she mixed it. Her mom smiled and said, "That's okay, we can fix it." They added more sugar and mixed it again. The cake was not harsh anymore. They were happy and enjoyed the cake together.
