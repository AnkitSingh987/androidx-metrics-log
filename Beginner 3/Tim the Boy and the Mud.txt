Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Mud

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the mud. One day, he got very dirty. Tim's mom saw him and said, "Oh no! You need a bath!" She rushed to fill the bathtub with warm water and bubbles. Tim got into the bathtub and splashed around. He washed away all the dirt and mud. When he was all clean, he was happy. Tim's mom was happy too. They both said, "No more playing in the mud!"

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the shore with her mom. They saw a big boat in the water. Amy felt dizzy as she looked at the boat going round and round. She held her mom's hand tight. "Mom, can I go peek in the boat?" Amy asked. Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful and don't go far." Amy went to the boat and peeked inside. She saw a big, round cake. She was so happy! She thought it was a gift just for her. She took the cake and ran back to her mom. But then, a mean man came running after her. He was very mad. He took the cake from Amy and said, "This is not for you!" Amy was sad, and her mom hugged her. They went home without the cake, and Amy learned not to take things that were not hers.

Anna loves fashion. She likes to wear different clothes and colors. She has a big closet with many dresses, skirts, shirts and pants. She also has many shoes, hats, scarves and bags. She likes to mix and match them and make new outfits. Today is a special day. Anna is going to a birthday party. She is eager to see her friends and have fun. She wants to look nice and show her fashion. She opens her closet and looks for something to wear. She sees a red dress with white dots. She likes it a lot. She takes it out and puts it on. She zips it up in the back. She looks in the mirror and smiles. She needs some accessories. She finds a white hat with a red ribbon. She puts it on her head. She looks for a bag. She sees a red one with white flowers. She likes it too. She takes it and puts some toys and candy inside. She is ready to go. She runs to the living room where her mom is waiting. She shows her mom her outfit. She says, "Look, mom, I am fashion!" Her mom smiles and hugs her. She says, "You are very fashion, Anna. You look beautiful. Are you ready to go to the party?" Anna nods and says, "Yes, mom, I am ready. Let's go!" They go to the car and drive to the party. Anna is happy and excited. She can't wait to see her friends and have fun. She is fashion and she knows it.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a big, red ball near a tree. Tim wanted to play with the ball, so he gave it a big push. The ball went very fast and hit a small, angry cat. The cat was not happy and ran away. Tim felt bad and wanted to help the cat. He saw the cat had a small cut on its leg. Tim had an idea. He ran to his house and got a bandage for the cat. He put the bandage on the cat's leg to make it feel better. The angry cat was not angry anymore. It was happy and started to purr. The cat looked at Tim and did something unexpected. The cat changed into a big, friendly dog! Tim was surprised but happy. Now, Tim and the dog could play together with the big, red ball.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue had a purse. The purse was messy. Sue wanted to get her purse clean. Sue took everything out of her purse. She found toys, a ball, and a book. Sue put all the things in a box. Now, her purse was empty. Sue washed her purse with soap and water. She scrubbed it until it was clean. Sue put her toys, ball, and book back in her purse. Now, her purse was not messy anymore. Sue was happy with her clean purse.
