Category: Beginner
Title: Timmy and the Garden

Once upon a time, in a small garden, a rabbit named Timmy found a tight hole. He saw a big carrot inside the hole. Timmy wanted the carrot, but the hole was too tight for him to get in. He was sad. Timmy's friend, a bird named Benny, saw him and asked, "Why are you sad, Timmy?" Timmy told Benny about the tight hole and the big carrot. Benny wanted to help Timmy solve the problem. Benny had an idea. He said, "I can help you, Timmy. I can go in the tight hole and push the carrot out." Benny went in the hole and pushed the carrot out. Timmy was happy. They both ate the carrot together and played in the garden.

Once there was a little boy who wanted a cup. He asked his Mom if he could have one, but Mom said no. The little boy kept asking, but Mom stayed firm. She said he was too young to have a cup. The little boy was very persistent. He asked day after day. After a while, Mom saw how determined her little boy was, so she decided to permit him to have a cup. The little boy was overjoyed! He hugged Mom and thanked her for being so kind. The next day, the little boy proudly took his cup with him wherever he went. He enjoyed drinking from it and showing it off to his friends. He was so happy to finally have a cup!

One day, a little girl named Lucy was very excited. She wanted to win a big race at school. She practiced every day to run fast. She would stand at the start line and run as fast as she could. On the day of the race, all the kids were ready to run. Lucy was sure she would win. But then, something unexpected happened. A small dog ran onto the race track. All the kids stopped running to look at the dog. Lucy did not want to stop. She wanted to win. But she saw the dog was scared. So, she stopped running and helped the dog. She took the dog off the track. All the kids cheered for Lucy. She did not win the race, but she felt happy because she helped the dog.

Mia and Joe were playing in their backyard. Joe saw a frame. He wanted to show Mia. He said, "Look Mia, a frame!" Mia was happy, but a bit scared. Mia asked Joe, "Can I raise it?" Joe said it was too heavy for them. But Joe had an idea. He said, "Let's get Dad to help us raise it." Mia was still a bit scared. She said, "It looks dangerous." Joe said, "Don't worry. Dad will help us. He'll keep it safe". Mia was happy again. So they went and asked Dad to help them. Dad was amused and agreed to help. He offeered his strong arms and said, "Let me raise it together with you." They smiled and all together, they raised the frame safely.

Once upon a time there was a soldier called John. One day, John decided to wander in the woods. As he walked, the woods grew darker and darker. He soon heard a noise. A weak voice said, "Who's out there?" John stopped and replied, "It's me, John." The voice replied, "I'm scared. Are you lost?" John shook his head. He said, "No, I'm just wandering. Do you need help?" The voice said, "Yes, I'm lost and weak. Can you help me get home?" John smiled and held out his hand. He said, "Come with me. I promise I'll get you home safe and sound." John and the voice made their way out of the woods. Finally, they reached the voice's home. He was so happy and hugged John tightly. John smiled and said, "You're safe now. It's time for me to wander again." The voice said, "Goodbye and thank you, soldier!

Tom and Sam were best friends. They liked to play football in the park. They had a cheap football that was old and flat. But they did not care. They had fun. One day, they saw a big sign in the shop. It said: "New footballs! Only five coins!" Tom and Sam wanted a new football. They ran to their homes to get their coins. They had to hurry. They did not want to miss the sale. They met at the shop. They had ten coins together. They could buy a new football. They were happy. They went inside. They saw many new footballs. They were shiny and round. They picked one. They gave their coins to the shopkeeper. "Thank you, boys," the shopkeeper said. "But be careful. This football is very bouncy. It can fly away if you kick it too hard." Tom and Sam did not listen. They were too excited. They ran to the park. They wanted to try their new football. They kicked it and passed it and scored goals. They had more fun than ever. But then, Sam kicked the football too hard. It flew over the fence. It landed on the road. A big car was coming. Tom and Sam watched in horror. The car ran over their new football. It popped. It was ruined. Tom and Sam were sad. They had lost their new football. They wished they had listened to the shopkeeper. They wished they had kept their cheap football. They learned a lesson. Sometimes, cheap is better than new.
