Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Clever Cat and the Hill

One day, a clever cat named Tom found a cooler. The cooler was big and blue. Tom wanted to open the cooler and see what was inside. But the cooler was very heavy. Tom tried to push the cooler, but it did not move. Tom thought of a plan to make the cooler move. He saw a hill and thought if he could increase the speed, the cooler would roll down the hill. Tom pushed the cooler up the hill. It was very hard work, but Tom did not give up. When the cooler was at the top of the hill, Tom let it go. The cooler rolled down the hill very fast. But at the bottom of the hill, there was a big rock. The cooler hit the rock and broke into pieces. Tom was sad because the cooler was gone and he never got to see what was inside.

Timmy was very sleepy. He wanted to sleep in his bed, but his mom said he had to go to school. He did not like school. He liked to play with his toys and watch cartoons. "Come on, Timmy, get up. You have to get ready for school," his mom said. "No, mom, I want to sleep. School is boring," Timmy said. "You can't sleep all day, Timmy. You have to learn new things and make friends at school. It's fun and good for you," his mom said. Timmy did not believe his mom. He closed his eyes and pretended to sleep. His mom sighed and left the room. Timmy smiled. He thought he was very clever. But soon, he heard a loud noise. It was his dad. He was making breakfast in the kitchen. He was making pancakes. Timmy loved pancakes. He smelled the sweet syrup and the butter. His mouth watered. He wanted to eat pancakes. He opened his eyes and got out of bed. He ran to the kitchen. He saw his dad and his sister, Lily. They were sitting at the table, eating pancakes. They looked happy. "Hi, Timmy. Do you want some pancakes?" his dad asked. "Yes, please, dad. I love pancakes," Timmy said. He sat down and took a plate. He put some pancakes on it and poured some syrup. He took a big bite. It was delicious. "Did you have a good sleep, Timmy?" his sister asked. "Yes, I did, Lily. I was very sleepy," Timmy said. "Are you ready for school, Timmy?" his dad asked. Timmy paused. He realized he had made a mistake. He had missed the bus. He had to walk to school. He did not like to walk. He liked to ride the bus. He looked at his dad. His dad looked angry. "Timmy, you were supposed to get up when mom told you. You can't skip school. You have to go now. And you have to walk. It's too late for the bus," his dad said. Timmy felt sad. He wished he had listened to his mom. He wished he had gone to school. He did not want to walk. He wanted to ride the bus. He wanted to eat pancakes. He wanted to sleep. He learned a lesson. He learned that sleeping too much was not good. He learned that school was important. He learned that he had to listen to his mom and dad. He said sorry to his dad. He said goodbye to his mom and sister. He took his backpack and walked to school. He hoped he would not be late. He hoped he would not miss anything fun. He hoped he would have pancakes again.

Once upon a time, there was a healthy little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his friends. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. One day, Tim saw a big, red ball. He wanted to touch it with his hands. So, he ran to the ball and touched it. The ball was soft and bouncy. Tim and his friends played with the ball all day. They had so much fun. When it was time to go home, Tim's mom told him to wash his hands. Tim knew it was important to have clean hands before eating. He didn't want to put dirty hands in his mouth. So, Tim washed his hands and ate a yummy dinner. And they all lived happily ever after.
