Category: Beginner
Title: The Tall Man and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a tall man. He loved to organize things in his big house. One day, he could not find his key. He looked and looked, but the key was not there. The tall man asked his cat, "Did you see my key?" The cat shook its head. He asked his dog, "Did you see my key?" The dog shook its head too. The tall man was sad. He needed the key to open a special box. The tall man thought and thought. He remembered he had not looked in his room yet. He went to his room and found the key under his bed. The tall man was happy. He organized his things and put the key in the special box. And they all lived happily ever after.

Josh and his dad went for a drive in their car. They drove to a big park. "Wow!", Josh said. "This park is so impressive!" His dad smiled. There were lots of people in the park. Some children were running around and playing. Josh watched them in awe. He wanted to join them! Josh's dad noticed a big field with trees and grass. "Come on, Josh. Let's play fetch!", his dad said. Josh ran to the field and his dad followed him. Josh found a big bone and picked it up. His dad said, "Wow! That is an impressive bone!" Josh threw the bone as far as he could. His dad ran to catch it. He threw it back to Josh. Josh and his dad played fetch all day. They were so happy. When the sun started to set, they both packed up and drove home.

Once upon a time, in a cheerful little town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim had a big red box with a lock on it. He wanted to open the box. One day, a talking bird flew into Tim's room. The bird knew a special language. The bird said, "Hi Tim! I know the language to open your box." Tim was very happy. He asked the bird, "Please tell me the language to open my box!" The bird said the magic words and the box opened. Inside the box, there were many colorful toys for Tim to play with. Tim and the bird played with the toys and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she wanted to marry a boy named Tom. But Tom lived far away, behind a big gate. Sue tried to open the gate, but it was hard to do. One day, Sue met a dog named Max. Max said, "I can help you open the gate!" Sue was happy. They went to the gate and Max dug a hole under it. Sue and Max crawled through the hole to find Tom. When they found Tom, Sue said, "Tom, will you marry me?" Tom looked at Max and said, "I like Max more. I want to marry Max!" Sue was sad, but then she saw a boy named Sam. Sam said, "I like you, Sue. Let's marry!" Sue smiled and said, "Yes!" So Sue and Sam got married, and they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a cat set off on a walk. The cat saw a big barrel. The cat was very curious and wanted to see what was inside the barrel. The cat jumped on the barrel and looked inside. There was a flexible snake in the barrel. The snake was very friendly and wanted to play with the cat. The cat and the flexible snake played all day near the barrel. They had lots of fun together. At the end of the day, they said goodbye and the cat went back home. The cat was very happy to have a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was very obedient and tried to do what his parents said. One day, his parents said he had to take a twig and go to the park. Tom nodded and obiediently walked to the park with the twig. When he arrived at the park, he saw lots of other boys and girls playing. Some were running around and some were playing games. He decided to join in with the games and forgot about the twig. But then it started to get dark, and Tom had to go home. But he couldn't find the twig! He looked and looked, but he couldn't find it anywhere. He knew his parents would be mad at him if he didn't have the twig, so he started to cry. Sadly, Tom never found the twig and had to tell his parents what happened. His parents were very angry with him, and Tom was very sad. He learned that he should never forget the things his parents tell him to do.
