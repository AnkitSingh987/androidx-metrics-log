Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Leaf

One day, a little girl named Lucy went for a walk in the park. She saw a big tree with lots of leaves. She picked up a red leaf from the ground and started to wonder where it came from. Lucy saw a squirrel sitting on a branch. She asked the squirrel, "Did this leaf come from your tree?" The squirrel looked at the leaf and said, "Yes, it did! I like to eat the delicious nuts that grow on this tree." Lucy smiled and thanked the squirrel. She put the leaf in her pocket and walked home. She showed her mom the red leaf and told her about the squirrel. They both agreed that it was a fun and special day in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a regular boy named Tim. Tim liked to cut paper with his safe scissors. One day, Tim saw a big tree with a lot of leaves. He thought, "I can cut leaves too!" Tim walked up to the tree and said, "Hi tree! Can I cut your leaves?" The tree nodded its big branches. Tim started to cut the leaves, and the tree was happy. Tim cut and cut until he saw something inside the tree. Tim found a lung! The lung was sad and needed help. Tim said, "Don't worry, lung. I will help you!" Tim knew just what to do. He ran to his house and got a big band-aid. Tim put the band-aid on the lung, and the lung felt much better. The tree and the lung said, "Thank you, Tim!" From that day on, Tim, the tree, and the lung were the best of friends.

One day, a big rhinoceros was walking in the forest. He was not very smart and did many stupid things. He liked to play with his friends, but sometimes he would break their toys. His friends wanted to prevent him from breaking more toys, so they thought of a plan. They decided to hide their toys when the rhinoceros came to play. This way, he could not find them and break them. When the rhinoceros came to play, he could not find any toys. His friends told him that they were hiding them so he would not break them. The rhinoceros felt sad and promised to be more careful. From that day on, he played nicely with his friends and did not break their toys.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing. The cat found a pink paper. The dog found a print box with ink. The cat said, "Let's make a pretty picture with the pink paper and ink." The dog said, "Yes, let's print a big heart on the pink paper." They put the pink paper on the ground. The dog opened the print box and got ink on his paw. He was ready to print the heart. But, when the dog put his paw on the paper, the ink made a big mess! The cat and the dog were sad. They did not know what to do with the messy pink paper. Just then, a bird flew down and looked at the paper. The bird said, "This is a beautiful picture! It looks like a pretty flower!" The cat and the dog were happy. They did not know they made a flower, but it was a nice surprise.

Once there was a girl called Sally. She was three years old and very curious. One day she was in her bedroom playing with her dolls. She saw something shiny, so she went over to take a closer look. It was a hook. Sally was so excited, she wanted to show it to someone. So, she grabbed the hook and ran out of her bedroom to find her mom. As she went down the stairs, she tripped on the stairs and dropped the hook. It broke into three pieces. "Oh no!" she said. Sally's mom heard the noise, so she came running. When she saw the broken hook, she looked very sad. "Sally, why did you do that? You were so stupid. Now I don't have my hook anymore," her mom said. Sally looked at her mom with tears in her eyes. She said sorry and gave her mom a hug. Her mom smiled and hugged her back. "It's ok, I know you didn't mean to break it. Let's go and get a new hook," she said. So, the mom and Sally went to the shop together and bought a new hook. Sally learned her lesson, not to be so silly and careless with things.
