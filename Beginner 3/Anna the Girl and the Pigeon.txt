Category: Beginner
Title: Anna the Girl and the Pigeon

One day, a little girl named Anna went to the park with her mom. She saw many pigeons on the grass and on the benches. She loved pigeons very much. She wanted to feed them some bread. "Mom, can I give some bread to the pigeons?" she asked. "Sure, honey, but be gentle and careful. Don't scare them or hurt them," her mom said. Anna took a piece of bread from her mom and broke it into small bits. She walked slowly to the pigeons and threw some bread on the ground. The pigeons came closer and pecked at the bread. Anna smiled and giggled. She threw more bread and watched the pigeons eat. One pigeon was different from the others. It was smaller and had a white spot on its head. Anna liked this pigeon the best. She wanted to touch it and hold it. She reached out her hand and tried to grab the pigeon. "Hey, don't do that!" the pigeon said. Anna was surprised. She had never heard a pigeon talk before. "Sorry, I didn't mean to scare you. I just wanted to be your friend. You are so cute and special," Anna said. "Thank you, but I don't like to be grabbed. I like to be free and fly. If you want to be my friend, you have to respect me and my space," the pigeon said. Anna nodded. She understood. She wanted the pigeon to be happy and free. "OK, I'm sorry. I won't grab you again. Can I still feed you some bread?" Anna asked. "Sure, you can. But only a little bit. Too much bread is not good for me. I need to eat seeds and fruits and vegetables too," the pigeon said. Anna gave the pigeon a little bit of bread. The pigeon ate it and thanked her. "You're welcome. What is your name?" Anna asked. "My name is Spot. What is yours?" the pigeon asked. "My name is Anna. I'm glad to meet you, Spot. You are a very smart and nice pigeon," Anna said. "Thank you, Anna. You are a very kind and sweet girl. I'm glad to meet you too. You are a very good friend," Spot said. Anna and Spot smiled at each other. They had made a new friend. They both felt happy and loved.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Lily had a fancy dress that she loved to wear. One day, Lily's mom told her they were going to a party. Lily was very excited and wanted to wear her fancy dress. Before the party, Lily went to find her fancy dress. She looked in her room, but she could not find it. Lily felt sad and asked her mom for help. Her mom said, "I remember seeing it in the living room." They both went to the living room and found the fancy dress under the couch. At the party, Lily was very happy in her fancy dress. She played with her friends and ate yummy food. While playing, Lily tripped and fell down. She felt pain in her knee. Her mom came to help her and put a band-aid on her knee. After that, Lily was careful when playing and had a great time at the party.

Tom and Lily are young. They like to play in the park. They have a ball and a kite. They run and laugh and have fun. One day, they see a big truck in the park. The truck is loud and smelly. It makes a lot of noise and smoke. The truck is blocking the path to the swings. Tom and Lily are sad. They want to swing. They go to the truck driver. He is sitting in the truck. He is talking on a phone. He looks angry and busy. "Excuse me, mister," Tom says. "Can you please move your truck? We want to play on the swings." The truck driver looks at Tom and Lily. He shakes his head. He says, "No, I can't. I have to wait here. There is too much traffic on the road. I have to deliver these boxes. Go away, kids. You are bothering me." Tom and Lily are confused. They do not understand what traffic is. They do not understand why the truck driver is so mean. They look at each other. They shrug. They do not know what to do. They decide to go to the slide instead. They hope the truck will go away soon. They still want to swing. They think the park is for playing, not for trucks.
