Category: Beginner
Title: Lila and the Door

Lila loved to play with her model plane. It was red and shiny and had a big wing. She got it from her grandpa, who was very old and knew a lot about planes. He told her stories of how he used to soar in the sky when he was young and brave. One day, Lila wanted to show her model plane to her friend Ben, who lived next door. She ran to his house and knocked on the door. Ben opened it and smiled. He liked Lila and her plane. "Hi, Lila! Do you want to play?" he asked. "Yes, Ben! Look, I have my model plane. Do you want to see it fly?" she said. "Wow, it's so cool! How do you make it fly?" he said. "Grandpa taught me. You have to throw it hard and up. Like this!" she said, and threw the plane in the air. The plane soared high and fast, making a loud noise. Lila and Ben watched it with wide eyes. They clapped and cheered. "Yay, Lila! You're so good at flying planes!" Ben said. "Thank you, Ben! You're so nice!" Lila said. They hugged and laughed. They played with the plane until it was time to go home. They said goodbye and promised to play again soon. Lila was happy. She loved her model plane and her friend Ben. And she loved her grandpa, who gave her the best gift ever.

Lila was a cheerful dancer. She loved to dance with her friends at the park. They would spin, jump, and clap to the music. Lila was good at rolling, too. She could roll on the grass, on the slide, and on the mat. One day, Lila saw a new girl at the park. She had a pink dress and a blue bow. She looked shy and sad. Lila wanted to be her friend. She ran to her and said, "Hi, I'm Lila. Do you want to dance with me?" The new girl shook her head. She said, "No, thank you. I can't dance. I have a bad leg. It hurts when I move." Lila felt sorry for her. She said, "I'm sorry your leg hurts. But you can still have fun. You can roll with me. Rolling is fun and easy. Come on, I'll show you." Lila took the new girl's hand and led her to the mat. She showed her how to roll on her back, on her side, and on her tummy. The new girl smiled and tried to roll, too. She found out that rolling was fun and easy. She laughed and said, "Thank you, Lila. You are a good friend. Rolling is fun. Can I dance with you, too?" Lila nodded and hugged her. She said, "Of course you can. You are a dancer, too. You can dance with your arms, your head, and your heart. Come on, let's dance with our friends." Lila and the new girl joined the other dancers at the park. They spun, jumped, and clapped to the music. They rolled, too, when they felt like it. They had a lot of fun. They became good friends. The moral of the story is: You can always find a way to have fun and make friends, no matter what your challenges are. You can be a cheerful dancer, too.

One sunny day, a smart crab named Sam was walking on the beach. He was very excited because he had found a big, pretty shell. Sam wanted to show his best friend, Tim the turtle. "Look, Tim!" said Sam, holding up the shell. "I found a big shell. Isn't it pretty?" Tim looked at the shell and smiled. "Yes, Sam, it is very pretty! You are so smart for finding it." Sam and Tim played with the shell all day. They took turns wearing it on their backs and pretending to be big turtles. They laughed and had lots of fun. The big shell made them very happy and excited.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Jane. She had a doll and was very happy. Every day, she would prepare the doll for a new adventure. Jane was always very careful in getting the doll ready. But one day, Jane was too busy to prepare the doll. She decided she could do it later. Unfortunately, she forgot all about the poor doll. Finally, it was time to play with the doll. Jane was so excited. She ran to get it, but it was gone! Jane had been so careless that somebody had taken the doll away. She was sad and frustrated. Her doll was nowhere to be found, no matter how hard she looked for it. This is a sad ending for Jane and her doll. No matter how ready Jane had been to take care of the doll, her carelessness had brought a bad result.
