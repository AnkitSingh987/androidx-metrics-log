Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Compassionate Boy and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a compassionate boy named Tom. Tom loved to help others. One day, he saw a cat stuck in a tree. "Don't worry, cat! I will rescue you!" Tom said. Tom found a big bag and put it under the tree. He climbed the tree and got the cat. "Jump into the bag, cat!" Tom said. The cat jumped into the bag and Tom climbed down. But, Tom forgot to close the bag. The cat ran away and got lost. Tom was sad because he could not find the cat. The ending was bad for Tom and the cat.

Once upon a time, there was a big, strong guard named Tom. Tom was very dependable. He always took care of the animals in the forest. One day, Tom saw a little bird who was sad. "Little bird, why are you sad?" asked Tom. The little bird said, "I want to pick some berries, but they are too high for me to reach." Tom wanted to help the little bird. He walked to the berry tree and picked some berries for the little bird. The little bird was very happy. From that day on, the little bird and Tom became best friends. They played together and helped each other. All the animals in the forest knew they could count on Tom, the dependable guard.

Once upon a time, there was a girl called Mandy. She was very fit and energetic, and she loved to keep busy and play sports. Mandy was walking near a lake one day, when she saw a scale on the ground. Intrigued, Mandy gave it a little kick to see if it would move. To her surprise, the scale started to move! Mandy picked up the scale and looked at it. It was covered in dirt, and Mandy thought it would look much better if it was clean. She knew just the person who could help - her grandpa. Mandy went to grandpa's house, eager to show him her find. "Grandpa, look what I found!" said Mandy, holding out the scale. Grandpa smiled. "Well isn't that nice! I think we can clean this up and make it look nice." Mandy and Grandpa went to Grandpa's shed and grabbed some cleaning supplies. Grandpa taught Mandy how to clean the scale, and soon the scale was sparkling and fit. Mandy was very proud of her work, and she thanked Grandpa for his help. And they all lived happily ever after!

Once upon a time, in a big farm, there lived an adorable turkey. The turkey had many friends, like the cow, the pig, and the duck. They all played together and had fun in the farm. One day, the turkey said to the cow, "Let's play a game! We can run and see who can gain the most speed." The cow agreed, and they started to run. The turkey ran fast, and the cow ran faster. They laughed and had fun. In the end, the turkey gained more speed and won the race. The cow said, "Good job, turkey! You are very fast!" The turkey felt happy and proud. They all went back to play with their other friends, and they had a great day.

Lila and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump and slide. They saw a big hill with grass and flowers. They wanted to climb it and see what was on the other side. They held hands and started to walk up the hill. They saw a zigzag path that went to the top. They followed the path and laughed and sang. They were having fun. When they reached the top, they saw a big lake. It was blue and shiny. They wanted to touch it and see if it was cold or warm. They ran to the edge of the lake and put their fingers in the water. "Ow!" Lila said. "The water is bitter! It hurts my fingers!" Ben tried to taste the water. He spat it out. "Yuck! The water is bitter! It tastes bad!" They looked at each other and made faces. They did not like the bitter water. They wanted to go back to the park. They turned around and saw a big cloud. It was gray and angry. It made a loud noise. It was a thunder cloud. It started to rain. They were scared. They wanted to go home. They ran to the zigzag path and started to go down the hill. But the rain was too fast. It made the path wet and slippery. They fell and slid and bumped. They got dirty and wet and hurt. They cried and called for help. They wished they never climbed the hill. Then they saw a bright light. It was a rainbow. It was red and orange and yellow and green and blue and purple. It was beautiful. It appeared in the sky and smiled at them. They stopped crying and looked at the rainbow. They felt happy and calm. They felt the rainbow was their friend. They heard a voice. It was their mom. She was looking for them. She saw them on the hill. She ran to them and hugged them. She said, "I'm so glad I found you! Are you okay? You're wet and dirty and hurt!" They said, "We're okay, mom. We saw a bitter lake and a thunder cloud and a rainbow. The rainbow was our friend. It appeared and made us happy." She said, "That's a very interesting story. I'm sorry you had a bad adventure. But I'm proud of you for being brave and strong. Come on, let's go home and get dry and clean and warm." They said, "Okay, mom. We love you. And we love the rainbow." They held hands and walked to the car. They looked at the sky and waved at the rainbow. The rainbow waved back and disappeared. They smiled and said, "Bye, rainbow. Thank you for being our friend."
