Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Lily loved to play with her toys and go on walks with her dog, Max. One day, Lily and Max went for a walk in the park. They saw a scary big tree. "Wow, that tree is so big," said Lily. Max barked at the tree. They walked around the tree and found a path on the left. They went down the path and saw something they didn't expect. There was a little boy named Tim who was weeping under a small tree. Max ran to Tim and licked his face. Tim stopped weeping and smiled at Max. Lily asked, "Why are you weeping?" Tim said, "I was scared of the big tree, but Max made me feel better." Lily, Max, and Tim became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, in a happy little town, there was a small cell. The cell lived inside a big tree. The cell had a friend, a tiny bug. The bug liked to tease the cell. They played together all day. One sunny day, the bug said, "I bet I can fly higher than you!" The cell laughed and said, "You can fly, but I can't. Let's play a game instead." They played hide and seek together. The bug was very good at hiding, but the cell was good at finding him. As they played, a strong wind came and blew the bug far away from the tree. The cell was sad and worried. But then, something unexpected happened. The wind blew the bug back to the tree, and he landed right next to the cell. They both laughed and were happy to be together again. From that day on, they never stopped playing and teasing each other.

One day, a colorful bird flew into a big tree. The bird was very happy to see the tree. It thought the tree was a good place to live. The tree had many green leaves and strong branches. The bird said, "Hello, tree! Can I live in you?" The tree said, "Yes, bird! You are welcome to live here." The bird was very happy and started to build a nest on a branch. A little girl saw the colorful bird in the tree. She said, "Hi, bird! I like your colors!" The bird said, "Thank you, little girl! I like your colorful dress too!" They both smiled and the bird continued to build its nest. The little girl thought the bird was very nice and they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom was a reliable friend. One day, Tom saw a bench near his house. He wanted to sit on the bench with his friend, Sam. But Sam was not there. Tom waited and waited. He was sad. He did not know what to do. Tom decided to look for Sam. He walked around and called out, "Sam, where are you?" But Sam did not come. Tom was very sad. He walked back to the bench and sat down. He cried because his friend Sam was not there. The bench was empty, and Tom was all alone. And that was the end of the story.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to play with his toys. He asked his mom, "Please, can I have my plastic car?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim, you can play with your plastic car." Tim looked everywhere for his plastic car, but he could not find it. He was sad. His mom said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can go to the store and find a new plastic car for you to play with." At the store, there were many plastic cars available. Tim was so happy! He picked a big, red plastic car. His mom said, "Good choice, Tim! Now you can play with your new car." Tim and his mom went home. Tim played with his new plastic car all day. He was very happy and said, "Thank you, Mom, for the new car!" His mom smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim. Have fun playing!"
