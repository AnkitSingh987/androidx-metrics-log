Category: Beginner
Title: Amy the Girl and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a box of colorful socks. One day, she wanted to match her socks. She felt a little anxious because she wanted to do it just right. Amy started to look at the socks. She found two socks that were the same. "I did it!" she said with a smile. She felt happy with the result. She kept matching more socks. After she matched all her socks, she showed her mom the result. Her mom was very proud of her. Amy was not anxious anymore. She knew she could match her socks all by herself.

Tim and Mia are friends. They like to play with trains. They have a big train set with many tracks and cars. They make the trains go fast and slow, and make noises like choo-choo and beep-beep. One day, they see a new train in the toy store. It is a steam train. It has a big black engine and a red caboose. It can make real steam and whistle. Tim and Mia want the steam train very much. They ask their moms to buy it for them. But their moms say no. They say the steam train is too expensive and too big. They say Tim and Mia have enough trains already. They say they can play with the steam train in the store, but they cannot take it home. Tim and Mia are sad and angry. They do not want to play with the old trains. They want the new steam train. They start to yell at their moms. They say they need the steam train. They say they will not be happy without it. They say they will not share it with anyone. Their moms are not happy either. They say Tim and Mia are being rude and selfish. They say they have to stop yelling and calm down. They say they have to be grateful for what they have and be friendly to others. They say they have to leave the store if they do not behave. Tim and Mia do not listen. They keep yelling and crying. They do not care about their moms or the other people in the store. They only care about the steam train. But then, something happens. The steam train starts to make a loud noise. It is not a whistle. It is a hiss. The steam train is broken. The steam comes out of the engine in a big cloud. It fills the air with smoke and heat. It makes everyone cough and sneeze. Tim and Mia are scared. They stop yelling and run to their moms. They hug them and say they are sorry. They say they do not want the steam train anymore. They say they want to go home and play with their old trains. Their moms are glad. They hug them back and say they forgive them. They say they are proud of them for being brave and honest. They say they love them very much. They say they can go home and play with their old trains, and maybe, one day, they can get a new train that is not broken.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big tree with tasty red apples. Tim's mom said, "Tim, do you know how to pick an apple?" Tim said, "No, but I can try!" Tim jumped and jumped, but he could not reach the apples. Just then, a friendly dog came running to Tim. The dog could jump very high. Tim asked the dog, "Can you help me get an apple?" The dog barked, "Yes, I can!" The dog jumped and got an apple for Tim. Tim was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The dog started to talk! The dog said, "I am not a real dog. I am a memory from a dream you had." Tim was very surprised. He did not know that his memory could talk. Tim, the dog, and his mom went home and had a tasty apple together.

Once upon a time, there was a big sack. It was a boring sack, with no colors or pictures on it. The sack just lay there, doing nothing all day. It was not fun, and nobody wanted to play with it. One day, a little boy named Tim found the sack. He thought it was sad that the sack was so boring. Tim wanted to make the sack more fun. He tried to draw on it, but the sack was too big for his little hands. Tim had an idea. He would ask his friends to help him make the sack more fun. They all tried, but nobody could make the sack less boring. They tried to play with it, but the sack just lay there, not moving or doing anything fun. Tim and his friends were sad. They wanted the sack to be fun, but it was just too boring. They tried one more time to make the sack fun, but it didn't work. In the end, the sack stayed boring, and nobody wanted to play with it. The boring sack lay there, all alone. It never became fun, and nobody ever played with it again. And that is the sad end of the story of the big, boring sack.
