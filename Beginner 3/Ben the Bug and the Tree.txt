Category: Beginner
Title: Ben the Bug and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little bug named Ben. Ben loved to crawl in the green grass every day. One day, while Ben was crawling, he saw his friend, a big bird named Bob. "Hi, Bob!" said little Ben. "Do you want to crawl with me?" Bob smiled and said, "I can't crawl, Ben. I can only fly. But I can watch you crawl." As they played, dark clouds came, and rain started to fall. Ben and Bob didn't like the rain. They needed to find a dry place to hide. They saw a big tree with a hole in it. They went inside the tree to stay dry. Inside the tree, they found a surprise! There was a big, shiny rock. Ben and Bob didn't know where it came from, but they knew it was special. They decided to keep the rock and show it to their friends when the rain stopped. They felt happy to have found something so special while hiding from the rain.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim was a busy boy who liked to play with his toys all day. He had a big toy box filled with lots of fun toys. Tim's favorite toy was a red ball that he could throw, kick, and bounce. One day, Tim went to play with his red ball, but it was not in the toy box. He looked and looked, but he could not find it. Tim was sad and did not know what to do. He thought maybe someone had taken it. Tim asked his mom if she knew who might steal his red ball. Tim's mom told him that his little sister, Sue, had been playing with the red ball. Tim went to Sue's room and saw her playing with the ball. He was happy to find it, but he knew he should not steal it back from her. Instead, Tim asked Sue if they could play together. Sue smiled and said yes. They played with the red ball all day and had lots of fun. From that day on, Tim and Sue always shared their toys and played together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was very adventurous. Lily loved to play outside with her friends. One day, Lily's mother said, "Lily, let's discuss where you can play today." Lily thought for a moment and said, "I want to play in the park with my friends, please." So, Lily and her mother went to the park. Lily played on the swings, the slide, and the seesaw. She laughed and had so much fun with her friends. After playing, Lily and her mother sat on a bench. They talked about the fun day they had. "I love playing at the park, Mommy!" said Lily. Her mother smiled and said, "I'm glad you had a great time, Lily. We can come back again soon." And so, Lily and her mother went home, happy and tired from their adventurous day at the park.

One day, a little sheep was playing in the field. The sheep was very happy. The sun was shining and the grass was green. A big bird came and saw the sheep. The bird wanted to tease the sheep. The bird said, "You are a silly sheep. Look at your wool, it's so messy!" The sheep felt embarrassed and sad. The sheep did not know what to say. Then, a kind mouse came to the sheep. The mouse said, "Don't listen to the bird. You are a nice sheep. Your wool is soft and warm." The sheep smiled and felt happy again. The sheep and the mouse played together in the field. The bird flew away, and the sheep was not embarrassed anymore.

Once there was a little bear called Tom. Tom always kept himself very clean and tidy. He had a soft, warm trunk made of brown fur. One day, Tom decided he wanted to show his trunk to the rest of the forest. He proudly walked to the clearing and bowed down to all the animals. He showed off his clean and shiny trunk and smiled. All the other animals were very impressed by Tom's trunk! They began to clap and cheer for him. Even the birds in the sky began singing a special song for Tom. The other animals couldn't believe how clean and well-made Tom's trunk was. They were all so happy to see his beautiful work!
