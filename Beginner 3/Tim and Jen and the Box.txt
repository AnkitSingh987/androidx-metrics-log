Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Jen and the Box

Tim and Jen are twins. They like to play with their toys and their dog. One day, they find a big box in the living room. They wonder what is inside. They think it is a surprise for them. They open the box and see a lot of feathers. They are soft and white and fluffy. They laugh and throw the feathers in the air. They make a big mess. They think it is fun. But the feathers are not for them. They are for their mom. She is making a dress for a party. She is very angry when she sees the feathers all over the floor. She yells at Tim and Jen. She says they are naughty and they have to clean up. She says they will not get any cake or ice cream. Tim and Jen are sad and sorry. They cry and say they did not know. They try to pick up the feathers, but there are too many. They wish they did not open the box. They wish they did not ruin the surprise. Their dad comes home. He is tall and strong. He sees the feathers and the dress. He sees the twins and the mom. He is not happy. He says they have to go to their room. He says they have to stay there until he says so. He says they have to think about what they did. Tim and Jen go to their room. They hug their dog. They feel bad. They do not like the surprise. They do not like the feathers. They do not like the tall dad. They do not like the story.

One day, Tim saw a helpless bug on a celery. The bug was sad because it could not fly. Tim wanted to help the bug. He thought, "How can I help the bug?" Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, there is a bug on a celery. It looks helpless. What can I do?" Mom smiled and said, "Let's observe the bug, Tim. Maybe we can find a way to help it." So, Tim and his mom went to the bug. They saw that the bug was not sad anymore. The bug had wings now! It could fly! The bug was happy. Tim and his mom were happy too. They did not need to help the bug anymore. The bug was not helpless. It flew away and said, "Thank you, Tim and Mom!"

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a gentle cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play with his friends and eat yummy food. One day, Fluffy saw a big restaurant. It smelled so good, he wanted to go inside. As Fluffy walked into the restaurant, he saw a big table with lots of food. He wanted to eat some, but he knew it was not right to steal. Fluffy's friend, a little bird named Chirpy, flew in and saw Fluffy looking at the food. "Fluffy, don't steal the food!" Chirpy said. "It's not nice to take what is not yours." Fluffy knew Chirpy was right, so they both left the restaurant and went to play in the park. In the park, Fluffy and Chirpy played with their friends and had a lot of fun. They were happy they did not steal the food from the restaurant. And at the end of the day, Fluffy's mom gave them some yummy treats to eat. From that day on, Fluffy and Chirpy always remembered to be gentle and kind to others. They knew that stealing was not good, and it was better to share and play with their friends. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a smelly dog went to the doctor. The dog felt sick. The doctor was kind and wanted to help the dog feel better. The doctor said, "Why do you feel sick, dog?" The dog said, "I ate something bad. Now, my tummy hurts." The doctor looked at the dog's tummy and thought about what to do. The doctor gave the dog some medicine. The dog drank the medicine and felt better. The dog said, "Thank you, doctor!" The doctor said, "You're welcome, dog. Don't eat bad things anymore." The dog went home and played happily.
