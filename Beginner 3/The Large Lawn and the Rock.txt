Category: Beginner
Title: The Large Lawn and the Rock

Once upon a time there was a large lawn. On the lawn was a little boy who loved playing there. One day the boy heard a big shout from across the lawn. He was very curious and wanted to see who was making the shout. He started walking slowly towards the shout. After a while he could see a big rock and someone standing beside it. It was a man, and he was looking hard at something on the ground. The boy shouted "Who are you?" The man looked up and smiled. He said "I'm looking for something I lost!" The boy wanted to help, so he started to yell very loudly, "Maybe I can help you find it!" The man smiled and said, "Thank you! Let's look together!" So they both searched the large lawn until the man finally shouted with joy, "I found it!" The little boy was very happy for the man and he waved goodbye as the man started walking away. The little boy couldn't contain his joy and shouted, "Good luck!" as the man went back across the large lawn.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had an important job to do - to keep spiders away from the garden. One day, when Lucy was walking through the garden, she heard a noise. She looked around and saw that someone was stealing her spider catcher! Lucy quickly ran up to him and said, “Stop! That’s my spider catcher! Give it back!” The thief looked at her and replied, “No way! This is mine now!” Lucy quickly thought of a plan. She said, “My spider catcher is very important to me. Please give it back!” The thief thought for a moment and then said, “Okay. Here, take it back.” He handed the spider catcher back to Lucy. Lucy thanked the thief and then ran back to her garden. From that day on, Lucy made sure to always keep her important spider catcher safe.

Mia and Ben are friends. They like to play on the farm. They see many animals on the farm. They see cows, pigs, sheep, horses and chickens. One day, they go to the barn. They see a big pile of hay. Hay is dry grass. Animals eat hay. Mia and Ben want to play with the hay. "Let's make a hay fort!" Mia says. "We can hide in the hay and pretend we are kings and queens." "OK!" Ben says. "That sounds fun!" They start to move the hay and make a fort. They make a door and a window and a roof. They go inside the fort and sit on the hay. "This is our castle!" Mia says. "We are the rulers of the farm!" They hear a noise outside. They look out the window. They see a horse. The horse is big and brown. The horse has a long mane and a long tail. The horse sees the hay fort. The horse walks to the hay fort. The horse smells the hay. The horse likes hay. Hay is delicious. The horse opens its mouth and takes a bite of the hay. "Hey!" Mia says. "That's our fort! Stop eating our fort!" The horse does not listen. The horse eats more hay. The hay fort starts to fall apart. "Go away!" Ben says. "You are ruining our fort!" The horse does not care. The horse eats and eats. The hay fort is gone. Mia and Ben are sad. They worked hard to make the fort. They see the farmer. The farmer is the owner of the farm. The farmer knows the horse. The farmer knows the horse is hungry. "Sorry, kids," the farmer says. "That's Daisy. She loves hay. She didn't know you were playing with it. She thought it was her lunch." Mia and Ben look at Daisy. Daisy looks at Mia and Ben. Daisy has hay on her mouth. Daisy looks happy. Mia and Ben are not angry anymore. They understand. Daisy is a nice horse. Daisy did not mean to be bad. "Can we pet Daisy?" Mia asks. "Sure," the farmer says. "She likes to be petted. She is very gentle." Mia and Ben pet Daisy. Daisy nuzzles Mia and Ben. Daisy is soft and warm. Mia and Ben smile. They like Daisy. They decide to make a new fort. They ask the farmer for some old blankets and pillows. They make a cozy fort in the corner of the barn. They invite Daisy to join them. Daisy lies down next to them. They share some carrots and apples with Daisy. Daisy thinks they are delicious. They have a picnic in the barn. They are happy. They are friends.
