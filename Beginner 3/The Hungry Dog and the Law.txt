Category: Beginner
Title: The Hungry Dog and the Law

Once upon a time there was a very hungry dog. He was walking around in search of something to eat. He came across a law that said he had to wait for his dinner. The dog did not want to believe the law. He said, "I'm very hungry, why do I have to wait?" The law said, "It's how things work, you have to wait until your dinner is ready". The dog sighed, "But I'm so hungry." The law replied, "You can believe it or not, but that's the way it is". The dog thought for a while, then said, "Ok, I'll wait, I believe the law". So the dog waited until his dinner was ready. He was happy and no longer hungry.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One day, Tim saw a big, open box near his house. He was very curious about what was inside the box. Tim went to the box and tried to open it. But it was too hard for him. He paused for a moment and thought of a plan. He decided to ask his big brother for help. Tim knew his big brother was very strong and could help him open the box. Tim and his big brother went to the box. With a big push, they opened it together. Inside the box, they found a lot of fun toys! They played with the toys all day long. Tim was so happy that he had asked his brother for help.

Once upon a time, there was a new tree. This tree was in the west part of a big garden. Many kids liked to play near the tree. The tree was very happy to have friends. One day, the sky turned dark and it rained a lot. The tree was sad because the kids could not come and play. The rain made the tree's roots wet and some even started to rot. Soon, the sun came out and the kids came back to play. The tree felt better and the rot went away. The tree and the kids were happy again in the west part of the garden, and they played together for many days.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to race with his friends. They would run fast in the park, laughing and having fun. One day, while they were racing, Tim saw a phone on the ground. It was big and shiny. He picked it up and showed it to his friends. Suddenly, the phone made a loud noise. Tim and his friends were scared. They didn't know what to do. Then, a nice lady came up to them. She said, "That's my phone! Thank you for finding it." Tim gave the phone back to the lady, and she was very happy. The nice lady had a surprise for Tim and his friends. She took them to a big race with lots of other kids. They all had so much fun racing together. Tim even won a prize for being the fastest. And that's how Tim and his friends had the best day ever.

Tim and Mia like to play in the garden. They have a big hose that can spray water. They spray water on the flowers, the grass and each other. They laugh and get wet. One day, they see a lemon tree in the corner of the garden. It has many yellow fruits. Tim and Mia want to try them. They pick a lemon and cut it in half. They squeeze the lemon and spray the juice in their mouths. "Yuck!" Tim says. "This is sour!" Mia makes a funny face. "I don't like it either!" They spit out the juice and run to the kitchen. They see their mom making a cake. She has a bowl of sugar and a bottle of milk. "Mom, can we have some sugar and milk?" Tim asks. "Sure, what for?" Mom says. "We want to make lemonade!" Mia says. Mom smiles. "That's a good idea. But you need some water too. Here, take this pitcher and fill it with water from the tap." Tim and Mia take the pitcher and go back to the garden. They add water, sugar and milk to the lemon juice. They stir it with a spoon. They taste it. "Mmm, this is good!" Tim says. Mia nods. "Much better than sour lemon!" They pour the lemonade into two cups. They give one to Mom and keep one for themselves. They drink and enjoy. Mom sips the lemonade. "This is impressive!" she says. "You made this by yourselves?" Tim and Mia nod proudly. "Yes, we did!" Mom hugs them. "You are very clever and creative. I'm proud of you!" Tim and Mia hug her back. "We love you, Mom!" They smile and finish their lemonade. They are happy.
