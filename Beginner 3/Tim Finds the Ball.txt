Category: Beginner
Title: Tim Finds the Ball

One day, Tim went to play with his red ball. He threw the ball high in the sky. Tim saw a quick bird fly by and take his ball. Tim was sad and wanted his ball back. Tim asked his friend, Sue, to help get his ball back. They both looked high and low, but they could not see the quick bird. They felt sad and did not know what to do. Then, they saw the quick bird in a tree. The tree was very high. Tim and Sue asked the bird to give the ball back. The bird was nice and dropped the ball. Tim and Sue were happy and played with the ball together.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Sara went to the beach. She loved the beach because it was so peaceful. Sara liked to read books and look for shells on the sand. One day, Sara found a big, pretty shell. She was so happy! She sat down to read her book with the shell next to her. The sun was warm, and the waves were soft. Sara felt very good. But then, something unexpected happened! The shell started to move! Sara was so surprised. The shell opened up and a little crab came out. The crab looked at Sara and smiled. Sara smiled back, and they became friends. They played together all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little bear named Bobby. Bobby loved honey. One day, Bobby saw a big honey pot near a big tree. He was very happy. Bobby said, "I want to go get the honey!" Bobby went to the tree and started to eat the honey. But he did not see the fierce bees that lived in the tree. The bees were mad that Bobby was eating their honey. They said, "That is our honey! We will not let you eat it all!" The bees came out of the tree and chased Bobby. Bobby tried to run away, but the bees were very fast. He cried, "I just wanted some honey! Please don't be mad!" But the bees did not stop. They stung Bobby, and it hurt a lot. Bobby learned that taking honey without asking is not nice. He was very sad and his face was all red from the bee stings. The bad ending made Bobby remember to always ask before taking something that is not his.

Once upon a time, there was a smart little bird named Tim. Tim loved to ride on the back of his big friend, Sam the dog. One sunny day, Tim and Sam went for a ride in the park. As they were riding, Tim saw an egg on the ground. "Oh no!" said Tim. "The egg needs help!" Tim and Sam wanted to find the egg's mom, so they looked all around the park. They asked other animals if they knew where the egg's mom was. Finally, they found a sad bird named Sue. "Is this your egg?" asked Tim. Sue was so happy to see her egg! "Yes, thank you!" she said. Tim and Sam felt very proud that they had helped Sue and the egg. They all became good friends and played together in the park every day.

One day, a boy named Tom went for a walk in the woods. He liked to play with his toy arrow. He had a lot of fun throwing it high in the sky and then running to catch it. The arrow was red and shiny, and Tom loved it very much. As Tom was playing, he saw a big tree. The tree was very tall and had many branches. Tom had a serious idea. He wanted to stand on one of the branches and throw his arrow from high up. Tom thought it would be even more fun to play with his arrow that way. Tom climbed the tree and stood on a strong branch. He threw his arrow into the sky, and it went very high. When the arrow came down, Tom caught it with a big smile on his face. He was very happy to play with his arrow in the tree. Tom had a great day in the woods with his toy arrow.
