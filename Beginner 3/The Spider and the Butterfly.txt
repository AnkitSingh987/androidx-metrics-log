Category: Beginner
Title: The Spider and the Butterfly

Once upon a time, there was a little spider. The spider felt good in his small web. He liked to play, eat, and sleep all day. He had a lot of fun with his spider friends. One day, the spider saw a big, pretty butterfly. He wanted to be friends with the butterfly. But the butterfly was scared of the spider. The spider felt sad. He did not know what to do. Then, the spider had a big idea. He made a beautiful web, just for the butterfly. The butterfly saw the web and liked it a lot. The butterfly was not scared anymore. They became good friends, and they played together every day. The spider was very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a toy car with a big button on it. The button made the car go very fast. Mia loved to play with her toy car and make it rush all around her room. One day, Mia found a light button in her room. When she pushed it, the room became very bright. She got an idea to have a race between her toy car and the light. She thought it would be fun to see who would win. Mia pushed the button on her toy car and it started to rush across the room. Then, she pushed the light button and the room filled with light. The toy car and the light raced around the room. Mia laughed and clapped her hands as she watched her toy car and the light race each other. In the end, the toy car won the race, but Mia loved playing with both her toy car and the light button.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy car. One day, Tim and his toy car went on a big adventure in the park. As they played, the toy car had a big fall into a hole. Tim was sad and wanted to rescue his toy car. He thought hard and came up with a plan to save it. Tim found a long stick and used it to reach the toy car. He pulled it out of the hole and was very happy. To celebrate, Tim and his mom had a yummy snack. They ate spicy chips and laughed together. Tim and his toy car were safe and ready for more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a barber named Tom. Tom was very good at cutting hair. He had a small shop in the town. Every day, many people came to his shop to get their hair cut. Tom was a very persistent barber, which means he never gave up and always tried his best. One day, a little boy named Tim came to Tom's shop. Tim was scared of getting his hair cut. He cried and didn't want to sit in the chair. Tom was patient and kind. He talked to Tim and made him feel better. Tim finally sat in the chair, and Tom cut his hair. Tim liked his new haircut and was happy. At the end of the day, Tom closed his shop. He made sure to lock the door to keep everything safe. He went home to rest, knowing he had done a good job helping people like Tim. Tom was a great barber, and everyone in the town loved him.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, Sue's mom said they would bake a potato for dinner. Sue was excited. Sue's mom put the potato in the oven. She told Sue to be careful because the oven was hot and could hurt her. Sue listened carefully to her mom's words. When the potato was baked, Sue's mom took it out of the oven. She let it cool down so it wouldn't hurt Sue. Then, they ate the yummy potato for dinner. Sue was happy she could help her mom and didn't get hurt.

Tom and Lily are playing in the park. They like to swing and slide and run. Tom has a new tie that his mom gave him. It is red and blue and shiny. He is very proud of his tie. "Look at my tie, Lily!" Tom says. "It is so pretty!" Lily looks at Tom's tie. She likes it too. She wants to touch it and feel it. She asks Tom, "Can I have your tie, please?" Tom shakes his head. "No, Lily. It is my tie. Mom gave it to me. You can't have it." Lily feels sad. She really wants Tom's tie. She thinks it is not fair. She tries to grab Tom's tie. Tom pulls away. They start to fight. They pull and tug and shout. "Stop it, stop it!" a loud voice says. It is the teacher. She is watching the children play. She sees Tom and Lily fighting. She is not happy. She walks over to them and separates them. "What is going on here?" the teacher asks. "Why are you fighting?" Tom and Lily point at each other. They both say, "He/she started it!" The teacher sighs. She looks at Tom's tie. She sees that it is torn and dirty. She feels sorry for Tom. She looks at Lily. She sees that she is crying and sorry. She feels sorry for Lily too. "Tom, Lily, fighting is not nice. You are friends. You should share and be kind. Tom, I know you like your tie, but it is just a thing. It is not more important than your friend. Lily, I know you want Tom's tie, but you should ask nicely and respect Tom's choice. It is not okay to take things without permission. Do you understand?" Tom and Lily nod. They feel ashamed and sorry. They say, "Yes, teacher. We understand." The teacher smiles. She says, "Good. Now, can you say sorry to each other and hug?" Tom and Lily look at each other. They say, "Sorry, Tom/Lily." They hug and make up. The teacher says, "That's better. Now, let's go and fix your tie, Tom. And then you can play together again. Okay?" Tom and Lily say, "Okay, teacher. Thank you." They follow the teacher to the bench. They fix Tom's tie. They go back to play. They are happy and friends again.
