Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Fat Cat and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with his toy, a big barrel. One day, Tom's mom said, "Tom, I permit you to play outside with your barrel." Tom was very happy. He went outside with his barrel and played all day. He rolled the barrel up and down the hill. He jumped over the barrel and hid behind it. Tom was having so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The barrel started to roll on its own! It went faster and faster, and Tom could not catch it. The barrel rolled all the way to a big tree and hit it. The tree shook, and lots of apples fell down. Tom was surprised, but now he had lots of yummy apples to eat. In the end, Tom was very happy with his barrel and the apples it helped him find.

Once upon a time, there was a nice little girl named Mia. Mia loved to clap her hands and dance. One day, she went to the park to play with her friends. At the park, Mia saw a group of kids, they made a network of friends. They were all playing together and having fun. Mia walked up to them and asked, "Can I play with you?" The kids said, "Yes, of course! Let's all play together!" Mia and her new friends played games, sang songs, and clapped their hands. They all had a great time. Mia was happy she made new friends in the network. From that day on, the park became a nicer place for everyone.

Tom is a boy who likes to dig in the dirt. He has a shovel and a bucket. He digs and digs and finds many things. He finds rocks and worms and bugs. He likes to look at them and put them in his bucket. One day, he digs and digs and finds something big and hard. He tries to pull it out, but it does not move. He digs some more and sees that it is a root. A root is a part of a plant that grows under the ground. He wonders what plant it is. He asks his mom, who is watching him from the porch. "Mom, what is this?" he says, pointing at the root. His mom comes closer and looks at the root. She smiles and says, "That is a root of an old tree, Tom. An old tree that used to grow here, before we moved in. It was very big and tall and had many leaves and branches. But it got sick and died, and they cut it down. But the root stayed in the ground." Tom is sad to hear that the old tree died. He likes trees. They are green and nice and give shade and fruits. He says, "Can we wake the old tree, mom? Can we make it grow again?" His mom shakes her head and says, "No, Tom, we can't wake the old tree. It is gone forever. But we can plant a new tree, if you want. A new tree that will grow and live and be your friend. Do you want to do that?" Tom nods and says, "Yes, mom, I want to plant a new tree. A new tree that will be happy and strong and beautiful." His mom hugs him and says, "That's a good idea, Tom. Let's go and get a seed and some water and some dirt. And we will plant a new tree together, right next to the old root. And we will watch it grow and take care of it. And maybe, one day, it will have roots of its own."

One day, a little girl named Lucy was playing in her kitchen with her toys. She had a teaspoon, a toy turtle, and a toy crocodile. She liked to pretend they could talk to each other. The teaspoon was jealous because it didn't have a friend like the turtle and the crocodile. The teaspoon said, "I want a friend too!" The turtle and the crocodile felt bad for the teaspoon. They wanted to find a way to make the teaspoon happy. They thought and thought, and then the turtle had an idea. The turtle said, "Let's find another spoon to be your friend!" So, they all went on a hunt for a new spoon friend. They looked in the kitchen drawers and found a little spoon. The teaspoon was very happy. The crocodile said, "Now you have a friend too!" They all played together and had a great time. The turtle and the crocodile would snap their mouths, and the teaspoons would scoop and stir. They all became the best of friends and played together every day.
