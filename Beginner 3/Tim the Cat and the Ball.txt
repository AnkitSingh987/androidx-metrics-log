Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a young cat named Tim. Tim loved to stretch in the warm sun. Every day, he would find a sunny spot and stretch his legs and tail. One day, Tim's mom gave him a task. She asked him to find a soft ball for his little sister. Tim was excited to help. He searched all over the house, looking under the bed and behind the couch. Finally, Tim found the soft ball in a box. He stretched his paw to reach it. He was so happy that he could complete the task. Tim brought the ball to his sister, and they played together all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a quiet little boy named Tim. Tim liked to work in his garden every day. He loved to see the flowers grow. One day, Tim saw a big rock in his garden. He wanted to move the rock, but he was too small. Tim asked his big sister, Sue, to help him move the rock. "Sue, can you help me? This rock is in the way," said Tim. Sue was strong and she wanted to help. She tried to move the rock, but it was too heavy. Sue felt pain in her arms. "Ouch! This rock is too heavy for me too," said Sue. The next day, Tim and Sue went to the garden again. They saw that the rock had moved all by itself! Under the rock, they found a small, pretty box. Inside the box, there was a note that said, "Thank you for working hard. Love, the garden fairies." Tim and Sue were very happy. They learned that when you work hard, even if it is tough, good things can happen.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to imagine things. One day, he imagined that he was in a world full of snow. The snow was soft, cold, and fun to play with. In the snow world, Tim met an elderly cat. The cat was old and wise. The cat told Tim that he could play with the snow all day long. Tim was very happy. He made a big snowball and threw it at the cat. The cat laughed and threw a snowball back at Tim. They played in the snow until it was time for Tim to go home. The elderly cat said goodbye to Tim and told him to always remember the fun they had in the snow. Tim promised he would never forget his snow world and the cat. And every time it snowed, Tim would imagine his friend, the elderly cat, and smile.

One day, a small boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a big tree and ran to it. Under the tree, he found a jar of jelly. Tim was very happy and said, "Mom, look! I found jelly!" His mom came over and looked at the jar. She saw that the jelly was very old and dead. She said, "Tim, this jelly is dead. We can't eat it." Tim was sad, but he understood. Just then, a little girl named Sue came up to them. She said, "That's my jelly! I lost it a long time ago." Tim gave the jelly to Sue, and they both became friends. They played in the park together and had lots of fun. Even though the jelly was dead, it helped Tim find a new friend.

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to go to the store with their mom. They liked to look at the toys and the books and the candy. But their favorite thing was to ride the elevator. The elevator was a big box that moved up and down and had buttons and lights and a ding sound. One day, they went to the store and saw a new toy. It was a big red truck with wheels and a horn and a door that opened and closed. Tom and Lily both wanted the truck, but their mom said they could only have one. Tom and Lily started to argue. "I saw it first!" Tom said. "No, I did!" Lily said. "Stop it, you two!" their mom said. "You have to share. You can take turns playing with the truck. Now, let's go to the elevator and go home." Tom and Lily followed their mom to the elevator. But they were still angry and jealous. They both wanted to push the button to make the elevator go. They both reached for the button, but Tom was faster. He pushed the button and smiled. "Ha! I pushed the button!" Tom said. Lily felt sad and mad. She wanted to push the button too. She pushed Tom and tried to push the button. But Tom pushed her back and pushed the button again. "Stop it, Tom!" Lily said. "Stop it, Lily!" Tom said. They pushed and shoved and yelled. Their mom tried to calm them down, but they did not listen. They made a lot of noise and trouble. The elevator door opened and a man came in. He looked at Tom and Lily and their mom. He shook his head and said, "What a noisy and rude pair of twins! You should be ashamed of yourselves!" Tom and Lily stopped fighting. They felt embarrassed and sorry. They looked at their mom and the man and the truck. They realized they had been bad and silly. They did not want to make their mom and the man unhappy. They did not want to fight over the truck. They wanted to be good and nice. They said, "We're sorry, mom. We're sorry, sir. We won't fight anymore. We will share the truck and the button and everything." Their mom smiled and hugged them. She said, "That's very good, Tom and Lily. I'm proud of you. You are good and nice twins." The man smiled and nodded. He said, "That's better. You are polite and kind twins." The elevator ding-ed and reached their floor. Tom and Lily got out with their mom and the truck. They held hands and said, "Thank you, sir. Have a nice day." The man said, "You're welcome, kids. You too." Tom and Lily went home with their mom and the truck. They played with the truck and took turns. They pushed the button and made the horn sound. They opened and closed the door. They had fun and laughed. They were happy and friendly. They were not jealous and angry anymore. They were good and nice twins.
