Category: Beginner
Title: The Boy Called Jack and the Flowers

Once upon a time there was a boy called Jack who loved to play outdoors. Every day he would race around, explore and pick flowers. Jack's mum always warned him to not to spoil his pants, as he would get in trouble! But Jack didn't listen, he would always run and jump in the mud. One day, Jack was walking in the park and he saw something that he wanted to try. His mum had said it was boring, but Jack wanted to take a risk. He made his way to the unusual machine that he had seen and jumped in the middle. It started to spin and his clothes got incredibly muddy! Jack's mum was furious when she saw his muddy clothes. She said, "Jack, why didn't you listen to me? You have spoiled your new pants!" But Jack wasn't embarrassed. He said, "Mum, that was really fun! We can come back tomorrow and do it again?" Mum said, "No. We won't be going back. That machine was too dangerous and boring. Now get changed and we will go home." And they did. Jack learned his lesson and always listened to his mum. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a wide blue lake. In the lake, there was a big fish. The fish liked to play in the water with his friends. One day, the big fish saw a small boat on the lake. He thought, "I want to play with the boat too!" The big fish swam up to the boat and said, "Hello, boat! Let's play together!" The boat did not say anything. The big fish was sad. He went back to his friends and told them about the boat. His friends said, "Maybe the boat needs some time to settle in the lake. Let's wait and see." The next day, the big fish and his friends went back to the boat. They saw something new. The boat had a little girl in it! The big fish was so happy. He said, "Hello, little girl! Let's play together!" The little girl smiled and said, "Yes, let's play!" The big fish and his friends played with the little girl and her boat all day. They splashed in the water and had lots of fun. The big fish learned that sometimes, things need time to settle before they can play. And the wide blue lake was a happy place for everyone.

Once upon a time, a big black whale lived in the deep blue sea. The black whale had many friends, but he was sad. He could not glow like the other fish in the sea. One day, the black whale met a wise old turtle. The turtle knew how to help the whale. He told the whale to eat a special kind of fish that glows. The black whale was very happy and went to find the glowing fish. The black whale ate the glowing fish and started to glow too. His friends were so happy to see him glow. But then, a big shark came and wanted to eat the glowing fish too. The black whale and his friends worked together to scare the shark away. The shark was scared and swam away. The black whale and his friends were safe and happy, and they all glowed together in the deep blue sea.

John was a very cheerful 3 year old. He was always happy and liked to whistle. One day, John's mom brought him a new towel. John got very excited and whistled with joy. John's mom laughed and said, "John, why don't you go outside and show your new towel to your friends?" John smiled and ran outside. He felt so happy to have a new towel, he whistled and hugged it tightly. John's friends saw the new towel and were so surprised. They all crowded around and touched it. John felt so proud. He cheerfully whistled again and showed off his new towel. John's friends were so jealous that they all started complaining about not having a new towel for themselves. John just laughed and kept on whistling!

One day, a green frog named Bob wanted to cook a big meal for all his friends. He was very excited to make food for them. He hopped to the pond to find some yummy bugs to cook. But there was a problem - the pond was full of thick fog, and Bob could not see the bugs. Bob was sad and worried that he could not cook for his friends. He thought hard about what to do. Then, he had a great idea. Bob asked his friend, the wind, to help him blow the fog away. The wind was happy to help. The wind blew hard, and the fog went away. Bob could see the bugs now. He cooked a big meal for his friends, and they all loved it. They had a great time eating and playing together. Bob was very happy, and he knew that his friends would always help him when he needed it.
