Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Max and the Toys

Lily and Max were playing with their toys in the living room. They had a lot of fun making noises and pretending to be animals. Lily was a cat and Max was a dog. They chased each other around the couch and the table. "Meow, meow, I'm a cat!" Lily said. "Woof, woof, I'm a dog!" Max said. They played until they were tired and hungry. They decided to look for a snack in the kitchen. They walked to the kitchen and saw a bowl of fruit on the counter. There were apples, bananas, oranges and grapes. "Wow, look at all the fruit!" Lily said. "Yum, yum, I want a banana!" Max said. They reached for the fruit, but it was too high for them. They tried to jump, but they could not touch it. They felt sad and frustrated. "How can we get the fruit?" Lily asked. "Maybe we can stretch?" Max suggested. They tried to stretch their arms and legs as much as they could, but it was not enough. They still could not reach the fruit. They felt even more sad and frustrated. "Stretching is hard!" Lily said. "Stretching is not working!" Max said. They were about to give up, when they heard a voice behind them. "Hello, kids, do you want a snack?" It was Mom, who had just come home from work. She saw Lily and Max trying to get the fruit. "Yes, please, Mom, we want a snack!" Lily and Max said. Mom smiled and picked up the fruit. She washed it and cut it into small pieces. She put the pieces on a plate and gave it to Lily and Max. "Here you go, kids, enjoy your snack!" Mom said. "Thank you, Mom, you are the best!" Lily and Max said. They took the plate and sat on the floor. They ate the fruit and felt happy and full. They liked the fruit, it was sweet and juicy. They shared the fruit and said what they liked. "I like the apple, it is crunchy!" Lily said. "I like the grape, it is round!" Max said. They finished their snack and thanked Mom again. They gave her a hug and a kiss. They felt grateful and loved. "Mom, you are always available for us!" Lily said. "Mom, you always help us!" Max said. Mom hugged and kissed them back. She felt proud and happy. "I love you, kids, you are always amazing!" Mom said. They smiled and went back to the living room. They continued to play with their toys, but this time they pretended to be fruit. They had a lot of fun making noises and stretching. They were a happy family.

Once upon a time, there was a woman. She lived in a small house. One day, she wanted to cook a meal. So, she went to the market to buy some food. At the market, she found some spicy peppers. The woman liked spicy food. She bought the peppers and went back to her house. She was happy and excited to start cooking. The woman started to cook her meal. She put the spicy peppers in the pot. The smell was very good. When the meal was ready, she sat down to eat. She loved her spicy meal and felt very happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim went on a walk with his mom. They saw a big hill with rocks on it. Tim wanted to climb the hill. His mom said, "Be careful, Tim!" As Tim climbed the hill, he found a dull rock. It was not shiny like the others. He thought this rock was special. He picked it up and put it in his pocket. Tim continued to climb the hill. At the top, he saw a big, shiny rock. He thought it was a special mineral. He wanted to take it home. But when he touched the rock, it moved! The big, shiny rock was not a rock at all. It was a big, shiny turtle! The turtle was nice and helped Tim climb back down the hill. Tim and his mom went home, and Tim kept the dull rock as a memory of his fun adventure.
