Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Dog and the Cat

One day, a little red hen found some wheat. She was very happy. She wanted to plant the wheat, so she asked her friends for help. "Will you help me plant the wheat?" she asked her friends. Her friends were a dog, a cat, and a duck. They were all confused. They didn't know what wheat was. The little red hen decided to hurry and plant the wheat by herself. The wheat grew tall and strong. The little red hen asked her friends again, "Will you help me cut the wheat?" But her friends still didn't know what wheat was. They just looked at her, confused. The little red hen was sad. She cut the wheat by herself. She wanted to make bread, but her friends didn't help her. The little red hen made the bread all alone. When the bread was done, it smelled very good. Her friends wanted to eat the bread, but the little red hen said, "No, you didn't help me. I will eat the bread by myself." The little red hen ate the bread, and her friends were sad. They wished they had helped her, but now it was too late.

One day, a boy named Tim and his friend Sue went to play. They had a cart. They liked to put things in the cart. They put toys, rocks, and leaves in it. They were very organized. They put the toys on one side, the rocks on another, and the leaves on the last side. They pushed the cart around the yard. Tim said, "Let's play a game! I will push the cart and you put things in it!" Sue liked the idea. They played the game. But then, they saw a big hill. Tim wanted to go down the hill with the cart. Sue was scared. "No, Tim! It's too big!" said Sue. Tim didn't listen. He pushed the cart down the hill. The cart went very fast. Sue yelled, "Stop, Tim! The cart will break!" Tim tried to stop, but the cart did not yield. It hit a tree. The cart broke. Tim was sad. He said, "I'm sorry, Sue. I should have listened to you." Sue said, "It's okay, Tim. We can fix the cart together." They worked together and fixed the cart. They learned to listen to each other and be more careful.

Once upon a time, there was a cheese. It was normal cheese, like what you might see in the store. The cheese spun around and around and around. It thought it was so much fun! Finally, it stopped and smiled. It loved spinning so much. The cheese wanted to share its joy, so it asked its friends to come join him. One by one, they all started spinning. They spun around and around until they were dizzy. Even the little ants that were watching joined in the spinning fun. The cheese laughed and clapped his hands. It loved to see everyone having so much fun. After a while, everyone slowed down and stopped spinning. They were all out of breath and worn out, but they had a great time. The Cheese knew it would always remember the fun they had spinning together.

Once upon a time, there was a family of ants. They lived in a big ant hill. The family had a mom, a dad, and many little ants. They loved to march in a line when they went to find food. One sunny day, the family went out to find food. They marched in a long line. The little ants were very impatient. They wanted to find food fast. They marched faster and faster. Finally, they found a big cookie. The family was very happy. The little ants were not impatient anymore. They marched back home, carrying the cookie. They had a big feast and lived happily ever after.

One day, a poor cat named Tom was very hungry. He went to the park to find some food. He saw a big salad on a bench. Tom did not like salad, but he could not resist because he was so hungry. Tom started to eat the salad. A little girl named Mary saw him and said, "Hey, cat, do you like salad?" Tom looked up and said, "No, but I am very hungry, so I eat it." Just then, a big wind came and blew the salad away. Tom was sad because he was still hungry. But then, Mary took out a yummy fish from her bag and gave it to Tom. Tom was so happy and ate the fish. Now, Tom and Mary are best friends.
