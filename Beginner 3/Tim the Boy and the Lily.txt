Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Lily

One day, a little boy named Tim found a razor in the bathroom. He did not know what it was, but it looked fun. Tim took the razor and went to show his big sister, Lily. "Look, Lily! I found this in the bathroom. What is it?" Tim asked. Lily saw the razor and knew it was not a toy. She wanted to make sure Tim was safe. "Tim, that is a razor. It is not for playing. It can hurt you. Give it to me, please," Lily said. Tim gave the razor to Lily. He felt a little sad because he thought it was a fun toy. Later, Lily and Tim were playing outside. They saw a weak, little bird on the ground. The bird tried to fly, but it could not. Tim and Lily laughed as they watched the bird hop around. They knew they needed to help the bird. They took the bird to their mom, who helped the bird get strong again. Soon, the bird was able to fly. Tim and Lily were happy that they could help the bird. They learned that it was better to play with safe things and help others.

Lily and Max are friends. They like to play with paper and crayons. They can print their names and draw pictures. One day, they find a pack of gum in the kitchen. They want to try it. "Can we have some gum, please?" Lily asks Mom. "Sorry, no. Gum is not for little kids. It can make a mess and hurt your teeth. You can have a cookie instead," Mom says. But Lily and Max are curious. They sneak the gum when Mom is not looking. They each take a piece and put it in their mouths. It is sweet and minty. They chew and blow bubbles. "This is fun!" Max says. "Yes, it is!" Lily agrees. They print their names on a paper with the gum. They think it looks perfect. They stick the paper on the fridge. They are proud of their work. But then, Mom sees the paper. She is not happy. She sees the gum on the paper and on the fridge. She sees the gum in their mouths and on their clothes. She sees the empty pack of gum on the floor. "What did you do?" Mom asks. "We printed our names with gum. See? It is perfect!" Lily and Max say. "No, it is not. It is a big mess. You should not take things without asking. You should not play with gum. It is not a toy. It is not good for you. You need to clean up and brush your teeth," Mom says. Lily and Max are sorry. They did not mean to make Mom angry. They did not know gum was bad. They spit out the gum and throw it away. They help Mom wipe the paper and the fridge. They go to the bathroom and brush their teeth. They say sorry to Mom. Mom hugs them. She is not angry anymore. She loves them. She knows they are still learning. "Next time, listen to me. I know what is best for you. And if you want to print something, use crayons. They are safer and prettier," Mom says. Lily and Max nod. They promise to do better. They hug Mom back. They still like to print, but not with gum.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to run and play. He liked to exercise in the park. One day, Max went to the store with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. They wanted to buy a fine ball to play with. At the store, Max and Lily saw many fine balls. They picked a big, red one. Max barked, "Woof! This one is great!" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, Max! Let's buy this one and play!" Max and Lily went to the park with their new ball. They had so much fun exercising and playing together. They played until the sun went down. Max and Lily loved their fine red ball and their fun day at the park.

Jimmy was walking around the park with his camera when he suddenly heard a noise. Looking around, he could see an empty box lying nearby. He walked closer and saw a small bird inside! The bird looked scared and Jimmy wanted to help. He said: "Don't worry, little bird. I will get you out." The bird seemed to understand and stayed still. Jimmy carefully opened the box and the bird was free. But then another problem seemed to appear. Now the bird wouldn't fly away! Jimmy tried everything he could to make the bird fly but nothing seemed to work. He was starting to feel frustrated. Just then, a kind old man walked by. He asked Jimmy why he was so worried. Jimmy told him his problem and the old man smiled. He pointed to a nearby tree and said: "Just place the bird on a branch and it will fly away." Jimmy quickly followed his advice. He gently placed the bird on a branch and amazingly, it started to fly away! Jimmy was so relieved and thanked the old man. He smiled, then turned and walked away, leaving Jimmy alone with his camera in the empty park.
