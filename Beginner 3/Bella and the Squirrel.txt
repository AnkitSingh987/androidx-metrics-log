Title: Bella and the Squirrel

Bella had been searching all day. She was looking for a nut. She walked through the quiet wood and searched, but could not find anything. Later, as the sun was setting, she saw a small opening in the base of a tree. She peeked inside and there, in the corner, she spotted a nut! "What luck!" she said, and bent down to collect it. But when Bella reached for the nut, a squirrel ran up to her and started scolding. "What are you doing?". the squirrel squeaked. "I just found this little nut and wanted to take it," Bella explained. The squirrel shook its head. "No, no," it said, "I was looking for this nut all day. It's mine, You can't take it." Bella sighed. She wanted to take the nut, but she knew it wasn't right. "I'm sorry," she said, "I didn't know it was yours". She stood up and started to leave. But then the squirrel stopped her. "Wait!" it said, "I have an idea. We can both have it. I'll crack the nut open and we can both eat." Bella nodded, smiling. "Yes, that sounds perfect!" she said. So the two of them worked together, carefully cracking the nut open and sharing the pieces. Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to read books. One day, he saw a big pitch near his house. He wanted to play there, but he had no friends available. Tim had an idea. He took his favorite book and went to the pitch. "Maybe I can find a friend here," he thought. He sat down and started to read. Soon, a girl named Sue came by. "What are you doing?" she asked. "I am reading," said Tim. "Do you want to play with me?" Sue said yes, and they played together. They had lots of fun, but then the sky turned dark. Tim and Sue knew they should go home. But they wanted to play more. So they stayed. Suddenly, it started to rain. Tim and Sue got very wet and cold. They wished they had gone home earlier.

Once upon a time, there was a big ocean. In the ocean, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin liked to swim and play with his friends. One day, Fin saw something shiny on the ocean floor. He swam down to see what it was. Fin found a pretty stone. It was not shiny enough, so he decided to polish it. He rubbed the stone on the sand to make it shiny. Fin was very happy with his shiny stone. He wanted to show his friends. But Fin was sometimes careless. While he swam to his friends, he dropped the stone. He looked and looked, but he could not find it. Fin was sad, but his friends helped him look for the stone. They found it together and all the friends were happy. They learned to be more careful with their things.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a bit shy and did not like to feel embarrass. He liked to play with his toys and have fun. Tim had a birthday coming up in a few days. He was excited because he would be one year older. Tim's mom and dad were planning a big party for him. They invited all his friends from school. Tim was a little worried because he did not want anything to go wrong. He did not want his friends to think his party was dull. On the day of the party, Tim's mom and dad had set up games and fun things to do. Tim's friends came and they all had a great time. Tim was happy because he did not feel embarrass and his friends did not think his party was dull. In the end, Tim had the best birthday ever and he could not wait for next year's party.

One day, a little red car named Zoom was very naughty. He liked to go fast like lightning. He had fun racing with his friends. They would laugh and play all day long. One sunny day, Zoom was racing with his friend, a little blue car. They went fast and faster. Zoom was winning the race. He was so happy that he did not see the big rock in front of him. Zoom hit the rock very hard. He flipped in the air and landed on his side. The little blue car stopped and looked at Zoom. Zoom was hurt and could not race anymore. All his friends were sad. Zoom learned that being naughty and going too fast was not good.
