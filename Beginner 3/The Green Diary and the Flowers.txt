Category: Beginner
Title: The Green Diary and the Flowers

Once upon a time, in a little house, there was a green diary. It was a very special diary. A little girl named Lily lived in the house. She loved to write in the diary every day. One day, Lily went to the park with her mom. She saw a big tree with lots of flowers. "Mom, can I pick some flowers?" asked Lily. "Of course, my dear," said her mom. So, Lily picked some pretty flowers and put them in her green diary. The next day, Lily's friend, Tim, came over to play. They played with toys and had fun. Then, Lily showed Tim her green diary. "Look, Tim! I picked flowers at the park," said Lily. Tim liked the flowers too. They both smiled and continued to play. And that night, Lily wrote about her fun day with Tim in her green diary.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy cat named Tom. Tom loved to eat cream. One day, Tom was very hungry, and he did not have any cream left. Tom was very sad. Tom went to his friend, a kind bird named Sue. Sue saw Tom was sad and asked, "Why are you sad, Tom?" Tom said, "I am hungry and I have no cream." Sue had an idea. She said, "Let's pray for cream!" Tom and Sue prayed together. They asked for cream to make Tom happy again. Then, they heard a noise. They looked and saw a big bowl of cream! Tom and Sue were so happy. Tom ate the cream and felt much better. He was not sleepy anymore. He played with Sue all day. They were the best of friends, and they knew that praying together had helped them.

Ben and Mia like to play in the park. They have a ball, a bike and a kite. They run and laugh and have fun. They see many things in the park: trees, flowers, birds, dogs and people. One day, they see something new in the park. It is a large, orange and black animal. It has stripes, a long tail and sharp teeth. It is a tiger! The tiger is in a cage, with a sign that says "Do not disturb". Ben and Mia are curious. They want to see the tiger up close. They go near the cage and look at the tiger. The tiger is sleeping. It makes a loud snore. Ben and Mia giggle. They think the tiger is funny. They want to make the tiger wake up. They throw the ball at the cage. The ball bounces off. The tiger does not move. They ride the bike around the cage. The bike makes a noise. The tiger does not move. They fly the kite over the cage. The kite flaps in the wind. The tiger does not move. Ben and Mia are bored. They think the tiger is lazy. They want to make the tiger roar. They have an idea. They find a stick on the ground. They poke the stick through the bars of the cage. They poke the tiger on the nose. The tiger wakes up. It is angry. It roars. It is a loud and scary roar. Ben and Mia are scared. They drop the stick and run away. They disturb the tiger. The tiger is mad. It shakes the cage. It tries to get out. It roars again and again. It is a big and noisy roar. The people in the park hear the roar. They are scared too. They run away. They call the zoo. The zoo comes and takes the tiger away. They say sorry to the park. They say the tiger escaped from the zoo. They say the tiger is not a toy. They say the tiger is not for fun. They say the tiger is a wild animal. They say do not disturb the tiger.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big red truck. He loved to play with his truck all day. It was his favorite toy. One day, Tim saw a little girl crying. She was sad because she lost her toy. Tim wanted to help her. He said, "Don't cry! I will share my truck with you." The little girl smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim!" Tim was gentle with his truck. He let the girl grab the truck and play with it. They both played with the truck together. They had so much fun. They became good friends and played with the truck every day. And they were both very happy.
