Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Fish

Once, there was a boy named Tim who lived near the sea. One normal day, Tim went to play by the water. He saw a big fish jump out of the sea. Tim was very surprised. Tim said, "Wow, that fish is so big! I want to talk to it." The big fish seemed to hear Tim and came closer. The fish said, "Hello, Tim! I am a talking fish." Tim was even more surprised. He asked, "Can you be my friend?" The big fish said, "Yes, but I have a secret. I am not a normal fish. I am a magic fish. I can make your wishes come true." Tim was so happy to have a new friend who could do magic. They played and talked by the sea every day. And that is how Tim's normal days became very special.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot liked to play with his ball in the park. One day, the ball rolled into a big hole. Spot was worried. He wanted to get his ball back. Spot saw a big girl named Lucy. He barked at her. "Woof, woof!" Lucy looked at the hole and wanted to help. She said, "Don't worry, Spot. I will rescue your ball." Lucy found a long stick. She put the stick in the hole and got the ball out. Spot was so happy. He wagged his tail and barked, "Woof, woof, thank you!" Lucy smiled and said, "You're welcome, Spot!" They played together in the park and became good friends.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big slide and wanted to play on it. Tim ran to the slide and climbed up the steps. When he got to the top, he sat down and slid all the way down. "Wee!" Tim shouted, "I love to slide!" After playing on the slide, Tim saw a puddle near the slide. There was a leak from a water pipe. Tim felt sad because he did not want the slide to get wet. He saw a girl named Sue playing nearby. "Sue, can you help me fix the leak?" Tim asked. Sue nodded, and they both tried to stop the water. As Tim and Sue worked together, a friendly dog came to the park. The dog saw the leak and started to dig a hole. To their surprise, the dog found a hidden treasure chest! Tim, Sue, and the dog were very happy. They had fixed the leak and found a treasure. Now they could play on the slide all day without any worries.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had a big, red ball. She loved to play with it. One day, she saw a big field with green grass. It was called a pitch. Lily thought it was an impressive place to play. Lily's mom said, "You can play on the pitch, but you must promise to be careful." Lily promised to be careful. She was so excited to play with her red ball on the big, green pitch. Lily played and played with her ball. She was having so much fun. But then, she kicked the ball too hard. It went far away and landed in a tree. Lily was sad because she had promised to be careful. But, a nice bird saw the ball and helped get it down. Lily was happy again and remembered to be careful, just like she promised.

Once upon a time, there was a strong man named Tom. Tom liked to work hard. He built big things with his hands. One day, Tom was working on a big tower. He wanted it to be very tall. While Tom was working, a little bird came by. The bird said, "Please do not disturb me. I am trying to sleep." Tom stopped his work and said, "Okay, I will be quiet." Tom waited for the bird to sleep. Then, he started to work again. He was very careful not to disturb the bird. Soon, the tower was finished. Tom was happy, and the bird was happy too. They both looked at the big tower and smiled.
