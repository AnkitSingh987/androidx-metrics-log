Category: Beginner
Title: Max and Izzy and the Park

Max and Izzy were best friends. They loved playing together. One day, they went to the park and saw a big open square. They ran to it, excited to explore. Max looked around and saw a really tall slide. He wanted to go on it. He started running but Izzy stopped him. She wanted to stay in the open square. Max was mad and he made a face. Izzy looked sad. They both looked down at the open square. It was so big and inviting. Then, Izzy had an idea. She suggested that they build a fort out of all the small squares. Max liked the idea and they both got to work. They worked together until the fort was built. When they were done, they both looked up at it. It was so tall and it looked like a real castle. Max and Izzy hugged each other, happy to be best friends.

Lily and Ben are playing in the garden. They like to dig in the dirt and look for bugs. Lily finds a brown worm and shows it to Ben. Ben says, "Ew, I don't like worms. They are slimy and wriggly." Lily says, "I like worms. They are soft and friendly. They help the plants grow. Do you want to touch it?" Ben shakes his head. He does not want to touch the worm. He says, "No, thank you. I want to find something else. Maybe a snail or a ladybug." Lily puts the worm back in the dirt. She says, "Okay, let's find something else. Maybe we can find a carrot. Mommy said there are carrots in the garden. They are orange and crunchy." Ben nods. He likes carrots. He says, "Yes, let's find a carrot. Maybe we can eat it for a snack." They dig some more in the dirt. They find some rocks, some leaves, and some seeds. But they do not find any carrots. They are sad. They want to find a carrot. Then, Lily sees something orange poking out of the dirt. She says, "Look, Ben, I found a carrot! It is big and bright. Can you help me pull it out?" Ben runs over to Lily. He says, "Wow, you found a carrot! It is big and bright. Let me help you pull it out." They both grab the carrot and pull hard. The carrot comes out of the dirt. It is long and fat. It has green leaves on top. They are happy. They found a carrot. They brush off the dirt from the carrot. They feel the carrot. It is smooth and hard. They smell the carrot. It is fresh and sweet. They bite the carrot. It is crunchy and juicy. They like the carrot. It is yummy. They share the carrot. They eat it all up. They smile at each other. They say, "Thank you, carrot. You are a good snack. We are full and happy."

One day, a shy cat named Tim went to a big park. In the park, there was a fun game. The game was to see who could jump the highest. Many kids and animals were there to play. Tim wanted to try, but he was too shy. He saw a small bird and said, "Hi, do you want to play with me?" The bird said, "Yes! Let's play together and win!" So, Tim and the bird played the game. They jumped and jumped. It was hard, but they did not give up. They jumped higher and higher. In the end, they won the game! Everyone was happy and clapped for them. After the game, there was a big party. At the party, there was a lot of food. Tim and the bird saw a big plate of toast. They both loved toast! But when they went to eat it, the toast started to talk! "Please don't eat us!" said the toast. Tim, the bird, and everyone at the party were very surprised. They did not eat the talking toast, but they all became friends and had a great time at the party.

Sara and Ben were playing with a big map of the world. They liked to point at different places and pretend they were there. Sara wanted to go to China, where her grandma lived. Ben wanted to go to Australia, where he saw kangaroos on TV. "Let's go to China first," Sara said. "We can see grandma and eat noodles." "No, let's go to Australia first," Ben said. "We can see kangaroos and play with boomerangs." They both pulled on the map, trying to make the other agree. But the map was old and thin, and it ripped in half. Sara and Ben both gasped and let go of the map. They looked at each other, scared and sad. "I'm sorry, Sara," Ben said. "I didn't mean to tear the map." "I'm sorry, Ben," Sara said. "I didn't mean to tear the map either." They hugged and said they were sorry again. Then they looked at the map pieces. They saw that each piece had a compass on it, showing the directions. "Hey, look," Sara said. "We can still use the map. We can follow the compass and find our way to China and Australia." "Really?" Ben said. "That's cool. How do we do that?" Sara and Ben decided to ask their dad, who was very smart and compassionate. He showed them how to use the compass and the map, and told them stories about the places they wanted to visit. He also taped the map back together, so they could play with it again. Sara and Ben were happy and thanked their dad. They learned that even when they have a problem, they can solve it together.
