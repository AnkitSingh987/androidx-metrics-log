Category: Beginner
Title: Billy and the Star

Billy was playing with his blocks when his mother came in. She was holding something shiny in her hands. "Look what I have!" she said with a smile. Billy saw the bright, red diamond. "Wow!" he said. His mother smiled. "Let's organize this diamond," she said. She took all of the blocks and put them in a neat pile. Then, she placed the diamond on top. Billy smiled. "It looks pretty!" he said. "It's like a star!" His mother laughed. She picked up the diamond and put it in her pocket. "Let's keep this for a special occasion", she said. Billy nodded. He was happy to help organize the diamond. He knew it was something very special.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a clumsy guard named Mark. He would always trip and fall while walking. One day, Mark saw a big ball. He wanted to play with it. But he was scared that he may fall again. Mark's friend, Tom, saw this and wanted to help. He told Mark, "Let's play together. I will hold your hand so you won't fall." Mark was happy and agreed to play. They both held hands and started to play with the big ball. As they played, Mark started to feel more confident. He didn't fall anymore. Mark and Tom had a lot of fun playing with the big ball. In the end, Mark learned that with a little help from his friends, he could do anything. And he was not a clumsy guard anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Mark. Mark had a toy phone. He liked to call his friends with it. One day, he went to the park with his mom. At the park, Mark met a big dog. The dog had a harsh bark. It scared Mark. Mark ran to his mom. Mark's mom said, "Don't be scared, Mark. The dog just wants to play." Mark was brave and went back to the dog. He used his toy phone to call the dog. The dog wagged its tail and played with Mark. They had a lot of fun. After playing, Mark and the dog were tired. They sat under a tree. Mark called his mom to come sit with them. They all enjoyed the day at the park. Mark and the dog became good friends. And Mark was not scared of the dog's harsh bark anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a cool cat named Kitty. Kitty lived in a big house with a nice family. The family loved to eat yummy food. Kitty liked to watch them eat and play. One day, after the family finished eating, they had to store the plates in the dishwasher. Kitty wanted to help, so she pushed the plates with her paw. The family laughed and said, "Good job, Kitty!" Kitty felt happy and proud. She liked helping her family. Every day, she would help them store the plates in the dishwasher. The family loved their cool cat and gave her a big hug. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a pink cat and a purple dog were playing in the park. They were best friends. The pink cat said, "Let's play close to the big tree." The purple dog agreed, "Yes, let's go!" As they played near the tree, they found a big box. The box was closed. They looked at each other and the pink cat said, "Let's open it!" The purple dog nodded, "Yes, let's see what's inside!" They opened the box and, to their surprise, they found a lot of colorful balls inside! They were so happy. They played with the balls all day, close to the big tree. The pink cat and the purple dog became even better friends, and they loved their new toys.

Mum and Dave went outside. They could see a big yard. Grandma was standing in the yard and she smiled when she saw them. Mum pointed to the swing in the corner. "Look Dave! You can go play on the swing," she said. Dave smiled and ran over to the swing. He sat down and pushed his feet, making the swing go high up into the air. He then pulled on the ropes and it made him go even higher. Grandma said, "Be careful Dave. That swing is flexible so it can wobble a bit if you push too hard." Dave nodded and kept swinging. He enjoyed the feeling of flying up and down. When Dave was tired he got off and ran to Mum and Grandma. He said, "That was so much fun. Can I go on again?" Mum and Grandma said yes and Dave ran back over to the swing with a big smile on his face.
