Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Sam Find the Dog

One day, a little boy named Tim had a friend named Sam. They liked to play and take toys to the park. They were very happy friends. One day, while playing, Sam said, "I will take this toy and hide it. You find it, Tim!" Tim thought it was a foolish game, but he played anyway. Sam went to hide the toy, and Tim closed his eyes. When Tim opened his eyes, he saw a big dog with the toy in its mouth! The dog was running away with the toy. Tim and Sam were very surprised. They ran after the dog and said, "Hey, give us our toy back!" The dog stopped and dropped the toy. The dog wanted to be their friend too. Now, Tim, Sam, and the big dog played together every day. They had lots of fun and were never foolish again.

Once upon a time, there was a big island. On the island, there was a small boy named Tim. Tim loved to paint. He painted trees, flowers, and the blue sky. It was easy for him to paint because he practiced a lot. One day, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue asked, "What are you doing?" Tim said, "I am painting the island." Sue looked at Tim's painting and said, "Wow! You are good at painting!" Tim smiled and said, "Thank you! I can show you how to paint too. It's easy!" Tim and Sue painted together all day. They painted the island with bright colors. They had so much fun together. From that day on, they were best friends. Whenever they could, they would paint the beautiful island together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. One gloomy day, Sue saw her dad yawn. He was very tired from work. Sue wanted to help her dad feel better, so she thought of a plan. Sue remembered her mom talking about a fun trip they took before she was born. She thought maybe a trip would make her dad happy again. Sue found a passport in her mom's room. She showed it to her dad and asked, "Can we go on a trip like you and mom did?" Her dad smiled and said, "Sue, I would love to go on a trip, but we can't right now. Work is very busy. But thank you for trying to help me." Sue felt sad, but she understood. She gave her dad a big hug, and that made him feel better. Sue learned that sometimes, just being there for someone can make them happy.

Tom was a lonely boy. He had no friends at school. He liked to draw pictures on paper with his crayons. He drew cars, animals, and houses. He wished he had someone to play with and share his pictures. One day, a new girl came to his class. Her name was Lily. She had long brown hair and big blue eyes. She smiled at Tom and said hello. Tom felt happy. He wanted to be her friend. At recess, Tom ran to Lily and asked her if she wanted to see his pictures. Lily nodded and followed him to his backpack. Tom showed her his paper with his drawings. Lily looked at them and said they were very nice. Tom felt proud. He asked her if she wanted to draw with him. Lily said yes and took a crayon. They sat on the grass and drew on paper together. They drew flowers, stars, and hearts. They laughed and talked. Tom felt less lonely. He thought Lily was his friend. But then, a big boy came and snatched Tom's paper. He tore it into pieces and threw it in the air. He said Tom's pictures were ugly and stupid. He pushed Tom to the ground and laughed. Lily saw this and ran away. She did not help Tom or say anything. She was scared of the big boy. Tom felt sad and hurt. He cried and picked up the pieces of his paper. He had no friend. He was lonely again.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom loved to eat limes. One day, Tom saw a big lime tree. He wanted to climb the tree to get a lime, but he was too fat. Tom said to himself, "I need to improve." Tom started to run and play every day. He wanted to become thin so he could climb the tree. He was getting better and better. One day, Tom met a small dog named Max. Max said, "I can help you improve, Tom. Let's play together!" Tom and Max played together every day. They ran, they jumped, and they had fun. Tom was getting thinner and thinner. Finally, one day, Tom could climb the lime tree. He got a big, green lime and shared it with his friend Max. They were both very happy. Tom learned that with a little help and hard work, he could improve and reach his goals.
