Title: Tim the Boy and the Passport

One day, a boy named Tim found a passport on the floor. It was not his. Tim wanted to find the person who lost it. He asked his mom for help. She said, "We can look for the person together." They walked around the town, showing the passport to people. No one knew whose it was. Tim felt sad but he did not give up. He wanted to do the right thing. Then, they saw a big fire. It was burning a house. Tim and his mom ran to help. They saw a man with a clear face standing near the fire. He was not happy. Tim showed the passport to the man. The man's face changed. He was happy now. The passport was his! The man was a hero who put out the fire. Tim felt good that he helped someone today.

One day, a little rabbit named Tim was very tired. He had played all day with his friends. When he came back home, he saw a tray of yummy cookies on the table. Tim's mommy had made them for him. Tim wanted to eat all the cookies, but his mommy said, "Tim, you can have one cookie now and save the rest for later." Tim was not happy. He wanted all the cookies now. When his mommy left the room, Tim took the tray and hid it under his bed. Later, Tim's friends came to visit. They were hungry too. Tim's mommy looked for the tray of cookies, but it had disappeared. She asked Tim, "Where are the cookies?" Tim felt bad for hiding the tray. He showed his mommy where the tray was. They all shared the cookies. In the end, Tim learned that it is good to share and not be greedy. Sharing makes everyone happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. It was a very hot day, and Tim felt the heat on his face. He saw his friend, Sue, playing with a big, red ball. Tim wanted to play with the ball too, so he asked Sue, "Can I play with your ball?" Sue said, "No, it is my ball!" Tim was sad, but he had a great idea. He ran to his house and got his own ball. It was blue and just as big as Sue's ball. They started to play together, but then, the wind blew both balls away. Tim and Sue were very sad. They looked and looked for the balls, but they could not find them. Then, a big dog came running with both balls in his mouth. The dog dropped the balls and ran away. Tim and Sue were so happy! They said, "Thank you, dog!" and went back to playing. The balls had returned, and they had a great day playing together.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Sue. She was three years old and very honest. One day Sue asked her mum for a new video. Her mum smiled and said, "I think you will fit a surprise". Sue went to bed happy but confused. The next morning she ran down the stairs and saw a big box on the table. She opened it and inside was a brand new video. Sue beamed and said, "Thank you, mummy". She hugged her mother and hugged the video hugged it in return. For weeks, Sue played with the video. She was very honest with it and she was sure it was the best present she ever had. Eventually, Sue realised she had almost outgrown it. She looked into the distance and said to no one in particular, "It won't fit much longer".

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a toy cat named Chess. Chess was a restless cat. He could blink his eyes and liked to run around the house. One day, Mia and Chess were playing in the living room. Mia saw that Chess was blinking a lot. She thought Chess wanted to play a game. Mia said, "Let's play hide and seek, Chess!" Mia started to count, and Chess ran off to hide. Mia looked and looked for Chess. She found him in a box. Mia was so happy to find Chess. They played hide and seek all day, and Chess didn't blink as much anymore.
