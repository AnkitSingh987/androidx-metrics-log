Category: Beginner
Title: The Prince and the Apple

Once upon a time, there was a prince who lived in a big castle. The prince liked to chew his food very well. He loved to eat all kinds of food, but his favorite was apples. One day, he found a big, red apple in the garden. The prince took a big bite of the apple and started to chew. But he didn't know that the apple was from a scary tree. The scary tree had put a spell on the apple. When the prince chewed the apple, he felt very strange. The prince fell asleep and never woke up again. The castle became a sad place, and everyone missed the prince. The scary tree had won, and the story had a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a big wolf who lived in the woods. The wolf was sorry because he had no friends. He was sad and lonely. One day, the wolf met a little bird. The bird said, "Hello, Mr. Wolf, why are you so sad?" The wolf said, "I am sorry, little bird, but I have no friends." The bird thought for a moment and said, "I can help you unite with other animals and make friends!" The wolf and the bird went to see the other animals. They met a squirrel, a rabbit, and a deer. They all became friends and played together. The wolf was no longer sorry, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who was very curious. She asked lots of questions and wanted to explore all the time. One day, she heard a big, loud shout. She was so curious - she wanted to know why someone was shouting. So she set off to find out. She followed the sound of the shout and soon she reached a big field. In the field she could see some children and they were all shouting and jumping up and down! They were all playing a game to see who could win. The little girl watched the children for a while and then decided to join in. She shouted with them and jumped up and down. She was so excited when she won - she just couldn't believe it! The little girl couldn't stop smiling. She was so happy that she had been brave enough to join in the game - it had been so much fun. And she was really proud of herself for winning, too. From that day on, the little girl and always remember to be curious when she heard a shout.

One day, a boy named Tim found a sack in his room. Inside the sack, there were many fake toys. He took out a fake star that could glow. Tim was very happy with his new toy. At night, when it was dark, Tim showed the glowing star to his friend, Sam. "Look, Sam!" Tim said, "My star can glow in the dark!" Sam was very excited and wanted to see the glowing star. Tim and Sam played with the glowing star all night. They pretended they were in space with the stars. The fake glowing star made them very happy. In the end, they put the star back in the sack and went to sleep, dreaming of more fun adventures.

Lily and Max were playing with paper and glue. They wanted to make a big purple bird that could fly. They cut out a big body, two wings, a tail and a head. They glued them together and added eyes and a beak. "Look, our bird is done!" Lily said. "It is so pretty and purple!" "Let's make it fly!" Max said. "We can throw it in the air and watch it soar!" They ran outside with their bird. They took turns throwing it and catching it. The bird flew high and low, left and right. It looked like a real bird. "Wow, it flies so well!" Lily said. "It is the best bird ever!" But then, a gust of wind came and blew the bird away. Lily and Max chased after it, but it was too fast. They saw it fly over the fence and into the neighbor's yard. "Oh no, our bird is gone!" Max said. "We worked so hard on it!" "Maybe we can get it back!" Lily said. "Let's ask the neighbor if we can go in his yard!" They knocked on the neighbor's door. A friendly man opened it. He saw the children and smiled. "Hello, what can I do for you?" he asked. "Hello, sir. We made a purple bird with paper and glue. It flew away and landed in your yard. Can we please have it back?" Lily asked. "Sure, you can have it back. It is a very nice bird. I saw it fly and land on my tree. Come, I'll show you where it is." he said. He led them to his backyard. There, on a branch, was their purple bird. It looked happy and safe. "Thank you, sir. You are very kind." Max said. "You're welcome, kids. You are very creative. Have fun with your bird, but be careful with the wind." he said. He helped them get their bird down. They thanked him again and ran back to their house. They decided to play with their bird inside, where it was warm and calm. They hugged their bird and each other. They were happy.
