Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Frog and the Grass

Once upon a time, there was a small frog named Tim. Tim loved to hop all day. One day, he saw a long cord in the grass. He thought it was a fun new toy to play with. As Tim hopped closer to the cord, he saw a mighty big bird named Bob. Bob was sad. "Why are you sad?" asked Tim. Bob said, "I can't find my cord to make my nest." Tim smiled and said, "I found your cord! Let's hop and get it together." So Tim and Bob hopped to the cord. But when they got there, they saw a twist. The cord was not a cord at all! It was a long, twisty snake! Tim and Bob were scared, but they knew they had to be brave. They hopped together and scared the snake away. Bob found a real cord, and they both became best friends. They hopped and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful cat. The cat lived in a big house with a little girl. The little girl loved to hug the cat. They were very happy. One day, the cat found a mitten on the floor. The cat played with the mitten. The little girl saw the cat and wanted the mitten too. The cat did not want to give the mitten to the little girl. They both wanted the mitten. The little girl thought for a moment. She had an idea. She went to her room and found another mitten. The little girl gave the new mitten to the cat. Now, they both had a mitten to play with. The cat was happy and the little girl was happy too. They played with their mittens all day. At the end of the day, the little girl hugged the beautiful cat. They were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty poppy. It wanted to grow big and strong. The pretty poppy lived in a garden with other flowers. They all wanted to grow too. They all lived next to a big tree. One day, the sun was shining bright. The pretty poppy saw a little girl come to the garden. The little girl looked sad. The pretty poppy wanted to help her. It thought, "If I grow big, I can make the little girl happy." The pretty poppy tried very hard to grow. It drank water and got lots of sun. Soon, it grew bigger and bigger. The other flowers saw the pretty poppy grow. They wanted to grow too. They all worked together to grow big and strong. The little girl came back to the garden. She saw the pretty poppy and the other flowers. They had all grown so big! The little girl smiled and was happy. She picked the pretty poppy and took it home. The pretty poppy and the other flowers learned a lesson. When they worked together, they could grow big and strong. They could make others happy too. And that is how the pretty poppy learned the value of working together.

Once upon a time, there was a little monkey. The monkey liked to play and jump all day. But one day, the sun was very harsh. It was too hot to play outside. The monkey felt sad. He wanted to play, but he didn't want to be in the harsh sun. So, he had an idea. He would sit under a big tree. The tree had many leaves, and it made a nice, cool shade. The monkey sat under the tree and played with his toys. He had so much fun. His friends came to sit with him too. They all played together under the tree, away from the harsh sun. And they lived happily ever after.

Lily and Tom were playing in the garden. They liked to dig and find things in the dirt. Lily found a bean. It was green and shiny. "Look, Tom, I found a bean!" Lily said. "Wow, can I see?" Tom asked. Lily gave him the bean. Tom held it in his hand. He wanted to see what was inside. He tried to break the bean, but it was too hard. "Ouch, this bean is hard. I can't break it," Tom said. "Let me try," Lily said. She took the bean back. She used her teeth to bite the bean. She heard a crack. She broke the bean. "Yay, I broke the bean!" Lily said. She opened the bean and saw two smaller beans inside. They were white and soft. "What are these?" Lily asked. "They are baby beans. They grow inside the big bean," Tom said. "Wow, they are so cute. Can we keep them?" Lily asked. "Sure, why not? But we have to be careful. They are weak. They need water and sun to grow big and strong," Tom said. Lily and Tom put the baby beans in a small pot. They filled it with dirt and water. They put it in a sunny spot. They hoped the baby beans would grow into big beans. They were happy with their new friends.
