Category: Beginner
Title: The Bear and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a bear that wanted to go for a swim. The bear stepped on the scales to see how much he weighed. "9," he heard the scales say. The bear thought that was funny and he jumped in the lake. The bear swam around and around. He was enjoying the cool water. Suddenly, the bear got stuck under a rock. He tried to push it but it was too heavy. So the bear called out for help. His friend the fox came and weighed the rock. The fox said, "It weighs 10!" The bear was amazed. He pushed and pushed, but he couldn't move it. His friend the rabbit came over. The rabbit said, "I can make this fun!" He put a balloon under the rock. When the balloon popped, the rock floated up! The bear was so happy. He thanked his friends and the three of them went for a swim. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a creative boy named Tim. He loved to sail his toy boat on the lake near his house. One day, he wanted to see where the lake went, so he got on his boat and sailed away. As Tim sailed, he saw a big factory by the lake. The factory made toy boats, just like the one Tim was sailing. He was so happy to see where his toy boat came from! He wanted to see more, so he went inside the factory. Inside the factory, Tim found a big room with lots of toy boats. But then, something unexpected happened! All the toy boats came to life and started to sail around the room. Tim was so surprised, but he was happy to have so many new friends to sail with. And so, Tim and the toy boats had a fun day together, sailing and playing in the factory.

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn puppy named Max. Max did not like to listen. He would always run away and not come back when his mom called him. One day, Max saw a big ball and wanted to play with it. Max's mom said, "Max, don't go too far away!" But Max did not listen. He just shrugged and ran away to play with the ball. Max's mom was very sad and worried. After playing for a long time, Max got tired and hungry. He wanted to go back to his mom, but he could not find her. Max was lost. He called out, "Mom, where are you?" But there was no answer. Max was all alone and he cried.

Anna and Ben were playing in the yard. They liked to swing on the big tree. They felt happy and free. But then they saw dark clouds in the sky. They heard loud noises and saw bright flashes. They felt scared and cold. "Mom, mom, there is a storm!" Anna cried. She ran to the house with Ben. Mom opened the door and hugged them. She said, "Don't worry, my loves. The storm will pass soon. Come inside and have some hot cocoa." She took them to the kitchen and gave them mugs of warm drink. She said, "The storm is powerful, but we are safe here. We can hang some pictures on the fridge to cheer us up. Do you have any drawings you want to show me?" Anna and Ben nodded. They ran to their room and brought back some colorful papers. They showed Mom their drawings of the sun, the flowers, and the birds. Mom smiled and said, "These are beautiful. Let's hang them on the fridge with some magnets." They hung their drawings on the fridge and admired them. They felt happy and warm again. They said, "Thank you, Mom. We love you." Mom said, "I love you too, my loves. You are my sunshine."

Once upon a time, there was a graceful cat named Herb. Herb liked to play with his friends in the park. One sunny day, Herb and his friend, a little bird, started a race. They wanted to see who was faster. Herb said, "Ready, set, go!" and they started to run. The little bird flew fast, and Herb ran on the ground. They were having so much fun. Suddenly, a big wind came and blew Herb up into a tree. Herb was scared, but the little bird said, "Don't worry, I will help you!" The little bird flew up to the tree and helped Herb climb down. They were both happy and safe. In the end, Herb and the little bird didn't care who won the race. They were just happy to be friends and help each other. They played in the park until the sun went down, and then they went home to rest.
