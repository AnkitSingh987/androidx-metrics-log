Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and the Toys

Sara was a pupil in a big school. She liked to learn new things and play with her friends. But she did not like to share her toys or her snacks. She always kept them in her bag and said, "Mine, mine, mine!" One day, her teacher said, "We are going to the store today. You can buy something nice for yourself or for someone else. But you have to bring your own money." Sara was happy. She had a lot of money in her piggy bank. She thought, "I will buy the biggest and best toy for myself. And I will not share it with anyone!" At the store, Sara saw many toys. She wanted them all. She picked up a big doll, a shiny car, a soft bear, and a noisy drum. She went to the counter and said, "I want these, please." The lady at the counter said, "That will be twenty dollars, please." Sara opened her bag and took out her money. But she only had ten dollars. She had not counted her money before. She said, "But I want them all!" The lady said, "I'm sorry, you don't have enough money. You have to choose one or put some back." Sara was angry and sad. She did not want to choose one or put some back. She wanted them all. She started to cry and scream. She threw the toys on the floor. She said, "No, no, no!" The other pupils and the teacher saw her. They were shocked and scared. They said, "Sara, what are you doing? That is not nice. That is not normal. You have to calm down and be polite." Sara realized that she was being bad. She felt ashamed and sorry. She said, "I'm sorry, I'm sorry, I'm sorry." She picked up the toys and put them back on the shelf. She said, "I don't want any toys. I just want to go home." The teacher said, "Sara, I'm glad you said sorry. But you have to learn to share and be happy with what you have. You can't have everything you want. That is not how life works. You have to be kind and generous to others. That is how you make friends and have fun." Sara nodded and said, "I understand, teacher. I will try to be better. I will try to share and be happy with what I have. I will try to be kind and generous to others. I will try to make friends and have fun." The teacher smiled and said, "That's good, Sara. I'm proud of you. Come on, let's go back to the school. Maybe you can find something nice to give to your friends." Sara smiled and said, "Okay, teacher. Thank you for teaching me." She held the teacher's hand and walked back to the school with the other pupils. She felt happier and wiser. She learned a valuable lesson that day.

Tom and Sue are twins. They like to play with their toys. But they do not like to share. They always fight over who gets the best toy. One day, Mom gives them a big box of cookies. She says, "You can have one cookie each, but you have to share the rest with your friends. Do not be greedy or wasteful." Tom and Sue nod, but they do not listen. They sneak into the kitchen and eat all the cookies. They do not leave any for their friends. Mom comes back and sees the empty box. She is very angry. She says, "Tom and Sue, you are very bad. You ate all the cookies and did not share. You wasted the food and hurt your friends. You have to say sorry and pray for forgiveness." Tom and Sue feel sorry. They say, "Mom, we are sorry. We were greedy and wasteful. We hurt our friends. We will pray for forgiveness. Please do not be mad at us." Mom hugs them and says, "I love you, but you have to learn to share and be kind. Next time, think of others before you take. Pray to God and ask him to help you be good."

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a bike with pedals. Tim wanted to ride the bike, but he did not know how. He asked his mom, "Can you teach me to ride the bike?" His mom said, "Yes, I can help you." She held the bike while Tim sat on it. Tim put his feet on the pedals and pushed. At first, it seemed hard. But Tim did not give up. He tried and tried, and soon he was riding the bike all by himself. Tim was very grateful to his mom for helping him. He said, "Thank you, Mom, for teaching me to ride the bike." His mom smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim. I'm happy to help you." They had a fun day at the park, and Tim was excited to ride his bike again.
