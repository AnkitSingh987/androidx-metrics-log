Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Toy

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy was worried because she could not remember where she put her favorite toy. She looked and looked, but she could not find it anywhere. One day, Lucy decided to enter her big sister's room. She thought maybe her toy was hiding in there. She looked under the bed, behind the chair, and even in the closet. But, her toy was not there. Lucy was sad and her memory was not helping her find her toy. Just then, Lucy's mom came into the room. She saw that Lucy was worried and asked her what was wrong. Lucy told her mom about her lost toy. Her mom smiled and said, "Lucy, I remember where your toy is!" She took Lucy's hand and led her to the living room. There, on the couch, was Lucy's favorite toy! Lucy was so happy and hugged her mom. From that day on, Lucy always remembered to put her toys away.

There was once a little boy named Tim. He had a really special rug he had found. It was an original one, just like none other. One day, Tim's mom asked him to set the rug in his room. He thought it would look perfect. So he said to her, "Yes, Mommy!" and ran as fast as he could to his room. When he reached his room, he spread out the rug in the middle of the room. He took a step back and saw how wonderful it looked. He jumped in and out of the rug, happy and proud. Suddenly, his mom appeared at the door. She smiled and said, "Wow, what a great job you did setting the rug, Tim!" He grinned and nodded his head with pride. The rug has remained in Tim's room ever since, and it invokes great memories for both Tim and his mom.

Tim and Kim were twins who liked to play by the river. They saw a big otter who was swimming and eating fish. They wanted to pet the otter and make him their friend. "Hello, otter!" Tim said. "Can we play with you?" The otter looked at them and shook his head. He did not want to play with the twins. He liked to be alone and free. "Please, otter!" Kim said. "We will be nice to you. We have a big cage at home where you can sleep and eat." The otter did not like the sound of that. He did not want to sleep and eat in a cage. He wanted to sleep and eat in the river. "No, no, no!" the otter said. "Leave me alone!" But the twins did not listen. They ran after the otter and tried to catch him. They had a big net and a big bag. They wanted to take the otter home. The otter was scared and angry. He tried to run away, but the twins were faster. They threw the net over the otter and put him in the bag. They laughed and cheered. "We got him, we got him!" they said. "We have a new pet!" They carried the bag to their home. They put the bag in the cage and locked it. They gave the otter some water and some bread. They thought the otter would be happy. But the otter was not happy. He was sad and troubled. He missed the river and the fish. He hated the cage and the bread. He wanted to go back to his home. He cried and cried. He scratched and bit. He tried to break the cage and the lock. But he could not. He was trapped. The twins did not care. They ignored the otter's cries and scratches. They thought the otter was just being silly. They went to play with their other toys. The otter gave up. He stopped crying and scratching. He lay down in the cage and closed his eyes. He wished he was dead. The moral of the story is: Do not take what is not yours. Do not force others to do what they do not want to do. Respect the lives and feelings of other creatures.

Once there was a girl named Lucy who wanted to be able to lift things. One day she saw wheat in the fields and decided to try to lift it. She went to the field and tried to lift the wheat but she couldn't. She kept trying and trying but she just couldn't lift it. It was too heavy for her. Every day she went back to the field to try to lift the wheat. Lucy was getting more and more frustrated. She just couldn't lift it. She was getting discouraged and it made her very sad. One day, Lucy was frustrated and so she started to cry. A big bird flew by and saw her crying. The bird saw the pretty wheat and decided to help. It flew towards the wheat and picked it up easily. The bird flew over to Lucy and gave it to her. Lucy was so happy, she thought she could finally lift it. She took the wheat, ran up a hill, and tried to lift it. But it was too heavy for her. She couldn't lift it and started crying again. The bird had only been trying to help but it didn't work. Lucy was sad and disappointed that she couldn't lift the wheat. The bird flew away sadly and Lucy had to accept that she just couldn't lift the wheat.
