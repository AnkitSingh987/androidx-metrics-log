Category: Beginner
Title: Zee the Zebra and the Lion

Once upon a time, there was a zebra named Zee. Zee lived in a big field with his friends. He was a tough zebra who liked to run and play all day. One day, Zee saw his friend, the lion, lie under a tree. The lion looked sad. Zee asked, "Why are you sad, lion?" The lion said, "I lost my toy, and I can't find it anywhere." Zee wanted to help his friend. They looked all around the field, but they could not find the toy. Then, something unexpected happened. A tiny bird flew down from the sky with the lion's toy in its beak. The bird said, "I found this toy up in the tree. Is it yours, lion?" The lion was so happy to have his toy back. He thanked the bird and Zee for their help. From that day on, the lion, the zebra, and the bird became best friends. They played together every day and always helped each other when they needed it.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a rude boy. He liked to play with his whip. One day, Tim saw a big box. He knew he would receive something special soon. The next day, Tim got a gift. It was the big box! He was very happy. He opened the box and found a toy horse. He loved the toy horse very much. But Tim remembered his whip. Tim played with his toy horse and his whip. He was not nice to the toy horse. He used the whip on the toy horse. This made the toy horse very sad. Then, one day, the toy horse had enough. It ran away from Tim. Tim was sad and cried. He learned that being rude is not good. He promised to be nice to all his toys from now on.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park. She saw kids playing with a ball. They were having fun and laughing. Lucy wanted to play too. She asked if she could join, and they said yes. They all enjoyed playing together. After playing, they saw a wet dog. The dog was running around and splashing in a big puddle. The kids laughed as the dog got them wet too. They all played with the dog and had a great time. At the end of the day, the kids had a race. They ran as fast as they could. Lucy won the race and got a trophy. She was very happy. All the kids clapped for her. They all went home with big smiles on their faces, excited to play again another day.

Once upon a time, a little girl called Sarah lives in a small town. She was three years old and loves to explore the town. Sarah was walking in the town one day and noticed a perfect little park. It had a small pond with ducks, a grassy area with nice smelling flowers, and even some swings. She was so excited! "We should go to the park!" she said to her mom. Her mom thought it sounded like a great idea and both of them went. They have been walking happily around the park and noticed lots of people playing and having fun. Suddenly, Sarah noticed a perfect picnic spot with a checkered tablecloth and a place to get yummy food. "We should have a picnic here mommy," she said. Her mom smiled and agreed to stay for a picnic lunch. It was the perfect day! They took the time to enjoy all the sights around the park and it was just the perfect day for a picnic.

One day, a little cat named Sam went into the woods. He was very hungry and wanted to eat. He saw a big, bad wolf. The wolf said, "I want to eat you, little cat!" Sam was scared, but he had an idea. He said, "Wait! I know where there is a lot of food. You can eat that and not me." The bad wolf thought about it and said, "Okay, show me where the food is." Sam took the wolf to a big tree. He said, "Climb this tree, and you will find a lot of food." The bad wolf tried to climb, but he was too big, and the tree was too small. While the wolf was busy, Sam made his escape. The bad wolf was angry, but he could not catch Sam. From that day on, the little cat was more careful when he went into the woods to eat. And the bad wolf learned that not all food was easy to catch.
