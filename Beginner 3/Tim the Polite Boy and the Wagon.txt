Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Polite Boy and the Wagon

Once upon a time, there was a polite boy named Tim. He liked to help everyone in his town. One day, Tim's mom asked him to get some wood for their home. Tim went to the forest with his little red wagon. He found a big pile of wood. He put the wood in his wagon and started to go back home. On his way, he saw a little bird who was cold. Tim felt bad for the bird, so he gave it some of the wood from his wagon. The bird was very happy and thanked Tim. Tim smiled and continued on his way home. When Tim got home, his mom was proud of him for being so polite and helpful. They used the wood to make their home warm and cozy. Tim knew that helping others was a good thing to do.

Once there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was three years old and he loved to run around and make noise. One day, Tom's parents decided to go shopping and left him home with his big sister, Wendy. "Be a good boy, Tom!" they said, as they left the house. Tom was so excited! He wanted to play with Wendy, and so he ran around making as much noise as he could. But Wendy didn't like it at all. "Tom, stop it!" she shouted. "I'm trying to make a cake in the oven and your noise is making it hard for me to concentrate". But Tom just kept running and shouting. "Tom, STOP!" said Wendy hopelessly. Suddenly, Tom stopped and ran over to his big sister. He gave her a big hug and said, "I'm sorry Wendy, I didn't mean to be noisy. I just wanted to play with you!" Wendy smiled and hugged Tom back. "That's OK Tom," said Wendy. "It's better to be nice than noisy!" Tom and Wendy finished baking the cake together, and it tasted delicious. From then on, Tom learned that being kind and considerate to other people is much better than being noisy and disruptive.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big jail. Inside the jail, there were many people who did not know how to be good. They were ignorant. They did not listen to their moms and dads, and they did not say "please" or "thank you." One day, a kind man came to the jail. He wanted to help the people in the jail learn how to be good. He told them nice stories about sharing and being kind to others. The people in the jail started to listen and learn. After the kind man talked to the people in the jail, they all felt tired. They needed to rest. So, they lay down on their beds and closed their eyes. As they rested, they dreamed of being good and making their moms and dads proud. And when they woke up, they tried their best to be good people.

One day, a humble cat named Tom went for a march. He walked and walked in the sun. He saw a little red bird in a tree. The bird sang a happy song. Tom said, "Hi bird! Can I march with you?" The bird said, "Yes, Tom! Let's march together." So, Tom and the bird marched and sang. They had lots of fun. As they marched, they saw a door. The door was open. Tom said, "Let's shut the door, bird." They pushed and pushed. The door shut with a loud noise. Tom and the bird smiled and marched back home.

Once upon a time, in a dark forest, there was a reindeer. The reindeer had big antlers and a red nose. He was very sad because he was lost and couldn't find his way back home. One day, the reindeer met a little girl. She was also lost in the dark forest. The little girl said, "Hi, reindeer! I am lost. Can you help me find my way out?" The reindeer nodded and said, "Yes, I can help you. Let's find our way out together." So, the little girl and the reindeer walked through the dark forest. They saw a big tree with a long vine. The reindeer said, "Let's pull the vine and see what happens." They both pulled the vine together. Suddenly, the dark forest turned bright and they could see their way out. They were very happy and thanked each other for their help.
