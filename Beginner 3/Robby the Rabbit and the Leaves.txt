Category: Beginner
Title: Robby the Rabbit and the Leaves

Once upon a time, in a deep, deep forest, there lived a little rabbit named Robby. Robby loved to chew on fresh green leaves all day long. His favorite season was spring because there were so many yummy leaves to eat. One day, while Robby was chewing on some leaves, he met a friendly squirrel named Sally. Sally liked to eat nuts, and she was very good at finding them. "Hi, Robby!" said Sally. "Do you want to play with me?" Robby stopped chewing his leaves and said, "Yes, I would love to play with you, Sally! What game should we play?" Sally thought for a moment and then said, "Let's have a race to see who can find the most food before the sun goes down!" Robby and Sally raced around the deep forest, looking for food. Robby found lots of leaves to chew, and Sally found many nuts to eat. They were both very happy. When the sun started to go down, they counted their food. Sally had found more nuts than Robby had found leaves. "Good job, Sally!" said Robby. "You are the winner!" They both laughed and played some more before going to their homes to sleep. And from that day on, Robby and Sally were the best of friends, always playing together in the deep, deep forest.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, were playing outside. They saw a big tree with many rich, red apples. Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's pour water on the tree and see if the apples fall down." Tim and Spot went to get a big bucket of water. They poured the water on the tree. But, instead of apples falling down, the tree started to shake. Tim and Spot were scared. A big bird came out of the tree. It said, "Thank you for pouring water on my tree. I was very thirsty." Tim and Spot were happy that they helped the big bird. The bird gave them some apples to eat, and they all became good friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, winter came. The snow was white and cold. A jolly snowman stood in a park. His name was Mr. Snowy. He had a big smile and wore a hat. He liked to play with the kids who came to the park. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the park. He saw Mr. Snowy and wanted to play. Tim said, "Hi, Mr. Snowy! Can I play with you?" Mr. Snowy nodded and smiled. Tim and Mr. Snowy played all day. They had so much fun. But then, dark clouds came. The wind began to blow. A big storm was coming. Tim was scared. He said, "Mr. Snowy, I have to go home now. The storm is coming." Mr. Snowy was sad, but he knew Tim had to leave. The storm struck the town. It was very strong. The wind blew hard and the snow fell fast. Mr. Snowy tried to stay up, but the wind was too strong. He fell down and broke into pieces. When the storm was over, Tim came back to the park. He saw Mr. Snowy broken on the ground. Tim was very sad. He cried and said, "Goodbye, Mr. Snowy. I will miss you." Then, he went home. The park was quiet and cold without Mr. Snowy.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear. He was very wealthy. He had a lot of honey. He liked to look at his honey every day. One day, a little bee came to the bear. The bee said, "Mr. Bear, can I have some honey?" The bear said, "No, this is my honey." The bee flew away, but he had a plan. The next day, the bear woke up and went to look at his honey. But the honey was gone! The little bee had taken it all. The bear was very sad. He should have shared his honey.

Tom liked to draw with ink. He had many colors of ink in his pens. He drew cats and dogs and cars and flowers on his paper. He was very good at drawing. One day, he wanted to show his mom his drawings. He ran to the kitchen with his paper and his pens. He saw his mom making a cake. He said, "Mom, look at my drawings! They are very nice!" His mom was busy with the cake. She said, "Not now, Tom. I have to finish the cake. Wait a minute, please. Be patient." She did not look at his drawings. Tom was sad. He did not want to wait. He wanted his mom to see his drawings now. He thought his mom did not care about his drawings. He felt angry. He decided to do something bad. He took his pens and started to draw on the wall. He drew big and messy lines with his ink. He drew on the wall near the cake. He said, "Now mom will see my drawings!" His mom heard him and turned around. She saw what he did. She was very mad. She said, "Tom, what have you done? You made a big mess on the wall! You ruined the cake! You are a naughty boy!" She took his pens and his paper and threw them in the trash. She said, "You will not draw with ink anymore. You will go to your room and stay there. You will not have any cake. You will not have any fun." Tom started to cry. He did not like his mom. He did not like his room. He did not like his drawings. He wished he had been patient. He wished he had not drawn on the wall. He wished he had not made his mom mad. He wished he had not done something bad. He clapped his hands on his face and sobbed. He was very unhappy.
