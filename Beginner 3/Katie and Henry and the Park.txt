Category: Beginner
Title: Katie and Henry and the Park

Once upon a time there were two friends, Katie and Henry. One day they were playing in the park when they saw a museum. “Let’s bounce over there!” shouted Henry. He liked to bounce everywhere. The friends started to bounce towards the museum but when they got there, they saw that it was locked. Katie said, “That’s not fair. I wanted to explore the museum.” Henry felt jealous. “You can still explore. You just have to do it in your imagination,” Henry said. So, the two friends used their imagination to explore the museum. They pretended to climb the stairs, to look at the art and read about the history. Katie and Henry had lots of fun. The friends learnt a valuable lesson: even if something doesn’t work out the way you expected, you can still make the most of it.

Once upon a time, there was a graceful zebra named Zoe. She lived in a big zoo with her family. Zoe loved to run and play with her friends. Her long legs made her very fast. One day, a man came to the zoo. He wanted to pay to see the animals. He saw Zoe running and playing with her friends. The man liked Zoe the best because she was so graceful. Zoe and the man became friends. He would pay to visit her at the zoo every week. They would play together and have fun. Zoe was very happy to have a friend who loved her graceful moves.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside in the sun. One day, it was very hot. Tim wanted to play in the shade under a big tree. The shade was dark and cool. Tim saw a big bug under the tree. The big bug said, "Don't come in my dark shade, or I will punish you!" Tim didn't listen and went into the shade anyway. The big bug got very angry. The big bug punished Tim by taking away all the shade. Tim was sad and hot. He went back home and told his mom. His mom said, "You should have listened to the big bug." Tim learned to listen better, but he was still hot and sad.

Tom and Mia liked to play in the park. They liked to run, swing, slide and climb. One day, they saw a big bird in the park. It had a long neck, long legs and black and white feathers. It was an ostrich. "Wow, look at that bird!" Tom said. "It is so big and funny!" "Can we go and say hello?" Mia asked. "OK, but be careful. It might be scared of us," Tom said. They walked slowly towards the ostrich. The ostrich saw them and tilted its head. It was a nosy ostrich. It wanted to know what they were doing. "Hello, bird. We are Tom and Mia. What is your name?" Mia said. The ostrich did not answer. It opened its beak and made a loud noise. It sounded like a honk. "Wow, you can honk! That is cool!" Tom said. The ostrich honked again. It flapped its wings and ran towards them. It was not scared of them. It was angry at them. "Uh-oh, it does not like us. Hurry, run!" Tom said. Tom and Mia turned around and ran away. The ostrich chased them. It was faster than them. "Help, help! The bird is after us!" Mia said. They ran to the gate of the park. They saw their mom waiting for them. She had a big bag of popcorn. "Mom, mom! There is a big bird in the park. It is an ostrich. It is nosy and angry. It honked at us and chased us. We are scared!" Tom and Mia said. Their mom looked at the ostrich. It stopped at the gate and looked at them. It smelled the popcorn. It was a hungry ostrich. It wanted to eat the popcorn. "Oh, dear. That is not a nice bird. It is a mean bird. It should not be in the park. It should be in the zoo. Come here, kids. Let's go home and have some popcorn. Maybe the bird will go away," their mom said. She took their hands and walked away. The ostrich watched them go. It honked again. It was still hungry. It saw another bag of popcorn on a bench. It ran to the bench and ate the popcorn. It was a happy ostrich. It liked popcorn.
