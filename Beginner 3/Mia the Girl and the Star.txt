Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Star

One day, a little girl named Mia wanted to decorate her room. She wanted it to look modern, like the new house next door. Mia asked her mom, "Can we make my room pretty and new?" Her mom said, "Yes, we can decorate your room together. But first, let's eat lunch. My stomach is hungry." Mia and her mom ate lunch. They ate yummy sandwiches and fruit. After lunch, Mia's stomach was full and happy. They went to the store to buy things for Mia's new room. At the store, they found pretty paint and fun stickers. Mia said, "I want blue paint and star stickers for my modern room!" They bought everything and went home to start decorating. Mia and her mom painted the walls blue and put up the star stickers. They laughed and had fun. When they finished, Mia's room looked modern and pretty. Mia hugged her mom and said, "I love my new room! Thank you, Mom!"

One day, a powerful big cat was walking in the woods. He saw a small hole in the ground. The hole went underground. The big cat was very curious. He wanted to see what was in the hole. So, he put his paw in the hole. Suddenly, a little voice said, "Hello, big cat!" The big cat was surprised. He did not know who was talking. He looked around, but he could not see anyone. The voice said again, "I am underground, big cat. I am a little mouse." The powerful big cat wanted to be friends with the little mouse. He said, "Can I come underground too?" The little mouse said, "No, big cat. You are too big to fit in my hole." The big cat was sad. He wanted to play with the little mouse. Then, the little mouse had an idea. He said, "Let's play a game, big cat. We can play hide and seek." The big cat was happy. He said, "Yes, that is a fun game! I will hide first." The big cat hid behind a big tree. The little mouse looked for the big cat. The little mouse found the big cat very fast. The big cat was surprised. He said, "You are very good at this game, little mouse!" The little mouse smiled. He said, "I may be small, but I am very fast and smart." The powerful big cat and the little mouse became good friends and played many games together.

Lily and Ben were friends who liked to play with blocks. They could build many things with blocks, like houses, cars, and towers. One day, they found a big pipe in the yard. It was shiny and round, and it made a funny sound when they tapped it. "Wow, look at this pipe!" Lily said. "It is rare. I have never seen a pipe like this before. Let's build something with it." "OK, what should we build?" Ben asked. "Let's build a rocket!" Lily said. "We can use the pipe as the body, and some blocks as the wings and the nose. Then we can pretend to fly to the moon." "That sounds fun!" Ben said. "But we need to ask Mom if we can use the pipe. Maybe it belongs to someone else." Lily and Ben ran to the house and asked Mom. Mom said they could use the pipe, but they had to be careful and not break it. She said the pipe was very old and special, and it came from her grandfather. He used to play music with it, like a flute. Lily and Ben thanked Mom and ran back to the yard. They started to build their rocket with the pipe and the blocks. They worked hard and had fun. They made the rocket look very cool and colorful. But then, a big dog came running to the yard. It was Rex, the neighbor's dog. Rex liked to chase and bite things. He saw the rocket and thought it was a toy. He ran towards it and barked loudly. "Rex, no!" Lily and Ben shouted. "That is our rocket! Go away!" But Rex did not listen. He jumped on the rocket and bit the pipe. He broke the pipe in half and made a loud noise. Lily and Ben were very sad and scared. They cried and hugged each other. Mom heard the noise and came to the yard. She saw the broken pipe and the dog. She was very angry and sad. She yelled at Rex and the neighbor. She took the pipe and the blocks and went to the house. She told Lily and Ben to come with her. "I'm sorry, Mom," Lily and Ben said. "We did not mean to break the pipe. We just wanted to build a rocket." "I know, sweeties," Mom said. "But the pipe was very rare and precious. It was a gift from my grandfather. He loved to play music with it. He taught me how to play it when I was little. It was his way of telling stories and making people happy. Now the pipe is gone, and I can never hear his music again." Lily and Ben felt very sorry for Mom. They hugged her and said they loved her. They said they would help her fix the pipe, or find a new one. They said they would learn how to play music with it, too. Mom smiled and kissed them. She said she loved them, too. She said they could try to fix the pipe, or find a new one. She said she would teach them how to play music with it, too. She said music was a way of telling stories and making people happy. She said maybe they could build a new rocket someday, too.
