Category: Beginner
Title: The Cat and Dog and the Surf

One day, a cat and a dog went to the beach. They wanted to play in the surf. The cat was scared, but the dog was brave. The dog said, "Come on, cat! Let's play in the surf!" The cat was not sure. The dog ran into the surf and got wet. He came back to the cat and said, "It's fun! Try it!" The cat did not want to try. She was scared of the water. The dog said, "If you don't try, you will be dead inside!" The cat did not want that. The cat took a deep breath and went into the surf. The water was cold, but it was fun. The cat and the dog played in the surf all day. They were happy. The cat was glad she tried something new. She would repeat this fun day again.

Once upon a time, there was a delicate little bird who lived in a tree. The bird loved to sing and play with its friends. One day, the bird noticed a big cross on the ground. The cross was made of sticks and stones. The bird was curious and wanted to know more about the cross. The bird asked its friends, the squirrel and the rabbit, about the cross. They said that the cross was a sign to be kind and share with others. The bird, the squirrel, and the rabbit decided to spread the message of the cross to all their friends in the forest. As they shared the message, all the animals in the forest began to be more kind and helpful to each other. They shared their food and played together happily. The delicate little bird, the squirrel, and the rabbit were proud of their work. The moral of the story is to always be kind and share with others.

Once there was a thin boy named Joe. Joe was so excited because it was his first day at a new club! But when he arrived at the club, he was surprised to see that nobody was talking to him. They all seemed to be ignoring him. Joe felt sad and confused. He went up to some of the other kids and tried to talk to them, but they just walked away and continued to ignore him. Joe felt so small and alone. Then, all of a sudden, a nice girl came over and said hello. Joe was so happy! He was so glad that she didn't ignore him like the other kids. Joe and the girl talked and played together all day. By the end of the day, Joe was happy again. He had made one special friend who didn't ignore him. He knew he would have lots of fun at the club.

One day, a lovely cat named Sam was walking in the park. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Sam jumped and climbed up the tree. He sat on a branch and looked at the sky. He loved to admire the clouds and the birds. As Sam sat in the tree, he saw a little girl named Lucy. Lucy looked at Sam and said, "Hi, cat! Can you come down and play with me?" Sam liked Lucy, so he climbed down the tree to play with her. They played and had fun, but soon Sam's stomach began to hurt. He didn't know why. Then, Lucy's mom came with a big cake. It was a surprise party for Sam! Lucy and her mom had planned it all along. Sam was so happy, he forgot about his stomach, and they all enjoyed the party together.

One day, a small cat named Lily went for a walk. The sun was up and the day was perfect. Lily saw her friend, a big dog named Max. Max was looking for a ball to play with. "Can you help me find my ball?" Max asked Lily. Lily nodded her head and said, "Yes, I can help you." They looked under a tree, near a big rock, and in a small hole. But they could not find the ball. Then Lily saw a small tip of the ball under a bush. She ran to the bush and pulled the ball out. Max was very happy. "Thank you, Lily!" he said. They played with the ball for the rest of the day.
