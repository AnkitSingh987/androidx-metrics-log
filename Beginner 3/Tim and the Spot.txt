Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Spot

Once upon a time, in a small house, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, lived with their mom. Every day, Tim's mom made a big meal for them to eat. Tim and Spot loved the food, and they always ate it all up. They were very happy. One day, Tim's mom had to leave for a while. She told Tim and Spot to be good and not to touch the meal on the table. The meal was hot and not harmless. Tim and Spot said they would listen, and they watched their mom leave the house. But Tim and Spot did not listen. They wanted the meal so much. They jumped on the table and started to eat. The meal was very hot, and they both got hurt. When Tim's mom came back, she saw them crying. They were sad, and they had learned their lesson.

One day, a big jet was flying in the sky. The jet was very bossy. It liked to tell all the little birds where to fly. The jet said, "Listen, birds! You must fly this way!" The birds did not like the bossy jet. A little bird named Tim did not want to listen to the jet. Tim said, "No, jet! I will fly my own way!" The bossy jet was not happy. The jet wanted all the birds to listen to it. Then, something unexpected happened. The bossy jet flew too fast and got stuck in a big tree. The jet said, "Help, help! I am stuck!" Tim and the other birds saw the jet in the tree. They all worked together to help the jet get out. The bossy jet learned to be nice, and they all became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a bossy cat named Tom. Tom liked to tell all the other animals what to do. One day, Tom saw a little fire in the woods. He was scared and ran to get help. Tom found a big dog named Max. He told Max about the fire. "Max, come help me put out the fire!" said Tom. Max did not like how bossy Tom was, but he wanted to help. So, Max and Tom went to the woods to put out the fire. When they got to the woods, the fire was gone. Instead, they saw a little girl with a birthday cake. She had blown out the candles on her cake. The fire was just the candles! Tom and Max were happy that there was no real fire. They learned not to be scared so fast and not to repeat stories without checking first. The little girl shared her cake with them, and they all became friends.

Once upon a time there was a bright tuna. It lived happily in the sea. One day, the bright tuna went for a swim. As it swam, the bright tuna blinked. Soon, a big, scary boat appeared. It moved closer and closer to the bright tuna. The bright tuna's heart raced with fear as it tried to swim away. But, sadly, the boat was too strong. It caught up with the bright tuna. Out of the boat came a big net. It trapped the bright tuna and the boat took it away. The bright tuna blinked sadly as the boat took it away. It knew that it would never swim freely in the sea again.

Once upon a time, there was a clean animal named Bobo. Bobo lived in a big forest. He had many friends who liked to play with him. One day, Bobo and his friends wanted to cross a river. It was very hard, but they tried to manage. They jumped on the stones and swam in the water. At last, Bobo and his friends managed to cross the river. They were happy and played more. They went home to their clean homes and slept well.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the dirt. One day, he was playing with his toy truck in the garden. He noticed that the truck was very dirty. Tim thought it would be fun to give the truck a taste. "Yuck!" Tim said, after tasting the dirty truck. "That's not good!" Tim's friend, Lily, came over and saw him making a funny face. "What's wrong, Tim?" she asked. "I tasted my dirty truck," Tim told Lily. "It was yucky!" Lily laughed and said, "Silly Tim, we don't eat dirt!" They both giggled and decided to wash the truck together. After it was clean, they played with the truck and had a fun day.
