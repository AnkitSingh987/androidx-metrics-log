Category: Beginner
Title: The Dog and Cat and the Ride

One day, a big dog and a little cat went to the fair. The dog was very happy and the cat was a bit scared. They saw many fun things at the fair like games, rides, and yummy food. The big dog said to the little cat, "Let's go on a ride!" The little cat was scared but didn't want to be alone. So, they went on a ride together. The ride went up and down, round and round. The big dog laughed, but the little cat closed her eyes tight. After the ride, the big dog wanted to play a game. The little cat said, "No, I want to go home." But the big dog didn't listen. He played the game and won a big prize. The little cat was sad and walked away. The big dog was so busy with his prize, he didn't see the little cat leave. When the fair was over, the big dog looked for the little cat, but she was gone. The big dog was sad and went home alone.

Once upon a time, there was a big tower in a small town. Many kids wanted to win a race to the top of the tower. One day, a boy named Tom and a girl named Sue decided to have a race. "Ready, set, go!" said Tom. They both ran very fast. Tom was in the lead, but he felt guilty for going too fast. So, he slowed down to let Sue catch up. They both laughed and continued to race up the tower. As they climbed, something unexpected happened. A big bird flew down and picked up Tom and Sue. The bird took them to the top of the tower before they could finish the race. They both laughed and said, "We both win!" The bird smiled and flew away. From that day on, Tom and Sue were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a big red ball. He liked to play catch with his friends. One day, Tim saw an old man. The old man had a blue ball. Tim wanted the blue ball. Tim asked the old man, "Can we trade balls?" The old man said, "Yes, we can trade." So, they traded balls. Tim was happy with his new blue ball. He went to play catch with his friends. But something unexpected happened. When Tim threw the blue ball, it did not come back. The blue ball flew up, up, up into the sky and did not come down. Tim was sad. He missed his red ball. The old man laughed and walked away with Tim's red ball.

Once upon a time, on a Sunday, there was a lonely frog. The frog liked to jump high and spring far. He had no friends and felt sad. One day, the frog saw a big red ball. He thought it was a new friend. He was very happy. The frog and the big red ball played all day. They had so much fun. But then, the wind blew the big red ball away. The frog was sad again. But, a little girl came and saw the frog. She said, "Hi, frog! Let's be friends!" The frog was happy again. He had a real friend now. They played together every Sunday.

One day, a little girl named Lucy wanted to help her mom. She saw the dishwasher and thought, "I know how to use that!" So, she opened the dishwasher and began to put the dirty dishes inside. As she was doing this, her little brother Tom came into the kitchen. He saw a tasty cookie on the table and wanted it. He said, "Lucy, can I have that cookie?" Lucy said, "No, Tom! That's the last cookie and I want it too!" Tom was sad, but he had an idea. He said, "If I help you with the dishwasher, can we share the cookie?" Lucy thought about it and said, "Okay, let's do it!" They finished putting the dishes in the dishwasher and then shared the tasty cookie. They were both happy and proud that they worked together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a big, messy room. It was full of toys and clothes. One day, her mom told her, "Amy, you need to clean your room." Amy did not want to clean her room. She wanted to play with her toys. That night, Amy had a dream. In her dream, she saw a big yellow bird. The bird said, "Amy, I can help you clean your room. But you must be kind to your mom." The next day, Amy woke up and saw her messy room. She remembered her dream and decided to clean her room. She picked up her toys and clothes. When her mom saw the clean room, she was so happy. Amy told her mom about the yellow bird in her dream. Her mom smiled and said, "Sometimes, dreams can teach us important things." From that day on, Amy always kept her room clean and was kind to her mom.
