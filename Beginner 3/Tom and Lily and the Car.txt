Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Car

Tom and Lily were playing with their toys in the garden. They had a lot of toys, but they liked the same ones. Tom had a red car and Lily had a blue doll. "Can I play with your car, Tom?" Lily asked. "No, it's mine. You have your doll," Tom said. "But I like your car. It's fast and shiny," Lily said. She waited until Tom was not looking and then she took his car. She ran away with it and hid behind a big tree. "Hey, that's not fair! You stole my car!" Tom shouted. He ran after her and tried to get his car back. Lily did not want to give it back. She held the car tight and said, "No, it's mine now. You can have my doll." Tom did not want her doll. He wanted his car. He was angry and sad. He pulled the car and Lily pulled the car. They both pulled hard and the car broke in half. "Oh no, look what you did!" Tom cried. "Oh no, I'm sorry!" Lily cried. They both felt bad. They looked at the broken car and the blue doll. They did not want to play with them anymore. They looked at each other and said, "I'm sorry. I should not have stolen your car. I should not have been mean to you." They hugged and said, "It's okay. We are still friends. We can share our toys." They went back to the garden and found some other toys to play with. They found a ball and a bucket. They had fun playing with them. They were happy and mild. They learned not to steal and to be nice to each other.

Once, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a delicate toy cat that she loved very much. Lucy took her toy cat everywhere she went. One day, Lucy and her toy cat went outside to play. While playing, Lucy saw a big rock. She wanted to gain the power to lift it. So, she tried to lift the rock with all her might. But, oh no! Lucy felt a pain in her arm. She was sad and her toy cat was sad too. Lucy's mom saw her and came to help. She gave Lucy a hug and told her that the pain would go away soon. Lucy felt better and learned that she should be careful when trying to lift heavy things. Lucy and her toy cat went back to playing, happy and safe.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy. He had a special friend, and her name was Daisy. Daisy had a wonderful ship. It was a charming ship, and it was shaped like a star. Daisy wanted to take her little friend for a ride in her ship. She asked the boy if he wanted to come, and he said yes! Daisy let the boy board the ship and they went sailing. The little boy had the best time. The ship was so much fun! They sailed around the bay, and they watched the birds in the sky. They even saw a few dolphins swimming. Finally, after a while, Daisy let the boy off the ship. He thanked Daisy for taking him on the adventure and he hugged her goodbye. Then he went home with a big smile on his face.

Jack had a lazy day. He lounged in his living room and took a nap. But then he heard the sound of marching outside his window. Jack took a look and saw a group of children marching in a line. It made Jack want to join in. So Jack pulled on his shoes and marched outside. He saw that the group was headed to the playground. Jack was so excited; he wanted to join in so badly. At the playground, Jack and the group sat on the edge of the playground and looked out. The view was so beautiful. They could see hills rolling in the distance and birds soaring through the sky. Jack felt so happy to be a part of this. A little while later, Jack got to experience marching another day. But this time, he was the one leading the group. They made their way to the same playground and took in the same view as before. Jack realized that he was lucky to have experienced this same view in two very different ways.
