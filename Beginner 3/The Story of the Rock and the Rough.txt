Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Rock and the Rough

One day, a group of friends were playing a game. They wanted to make a score by working together. They knew that if they unite, they could win the game. So, they held hands and played as a team. As they played, they met a big, rough rock. The rock said, "You cannot pass me. I am too big and too rough." The friends were scared, but they knew they could not give up. They had to think of a way to pass the big, rough rock. Then, something unexpected happened. The big, rough rock started to move! It was not a rock at all, but a big, friendly animal. The animal said, "I will help you if you promise to be kind." The friends promised to be kind, and the big, friendly animal helped them pass. They united, made a score, and won the game. They were very happy and thanked the big, friendly animal.

Once upon a time, a long train went on a trip. The train used steam to go fast. The train was very happy to take people to new places. One day, the train saw a big rock on the tracks. It wanted to prevent a crash. The train blew its horn very loud. The people heard the sound and came to help. The people worked together to move the big rock. The train said thank you and went on its way. The people waved goodbye. The long train kept going, using its steam to help people go places.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went for a walk. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. They saw a big hill with a zigzag path. Tim said, "Let's go up the hill, Spot!" And so, they started to walk up the zigzag path. As they walked, the sky turned dark and it began to rain. Tim and Spot got very wet. They saw a big tree and ran under it. Tim said, "We need to zip our coats to stay dry." So, Tim zipped up his coat and Spot zipped up his little dog coat. They waited for the rain to stop, but it didn't. Tim and Spot felt sad because they couldn't go up the hill. Just then, a friendly bird flew down and said, "Don't worry, the rain will stop soon." And just like that, the rain stopped, and the sun came out again. Tim and Spot were happy. They said, "Thank you, bird!" The bird smiled and flew away. Tim and Spot went back to the zigzag path and walked all the way up the hill. When they reached the top, they looked out at the beautiful view and knew it was worth the wait.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a good boy. He liked to help his mom in the kitchen. One day, Tim's mom asked him to get some ice from the freezer. The freezer was a big, cold box where they kept food. Tim opened the freezer door. He saw a terrible monster inside! The monster was big and scary. Tim was very brave. He said, "Please, Mr. Monster, yield me some ice for my mom." The monster looked at Tim and smiled. He was not a bad monster. He gave Tim some ice. Tim took the ice to his mom. He told her about the monster in the freezer. She laughed and said, "That's just our ice monster, Tim. He helps keep our food cold. He's not terrible at all." Tim smiled and knew that he had made a new friend.

One day, a little girl named Amy was eager to weigh her new toy. She wanted to know how heavy it was. She went to her mom and asked, "Mom, can I use the scale?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can. But first, write the weight in your journal. This will help you remember it later." Amy was excited. She put the toy on the scale and saw the number. She wrote it down in her journal. Later, she shared the number with her friends. They all learned how to weigh their toys too. In the end, Amy and her friends had fun weighing their toys and writing the numbers in their journals. They learned that it is good to share and help each other. The moral of the story is: sharing and helping others can bring happiness and make learning more fun.
