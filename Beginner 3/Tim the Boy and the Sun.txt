Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved dinner time because his mom made the best food. One day, Tim's mom said she had a big surprise for dinner, but he had to promise not to peek. Tim was so excited, he couldn't wait for dinner. He played with his toys and thought about what the surprise could be. The sun was shining in the sky, making everything light and bright. Tim promised his mom that he wouldn't look, but it was hard not to. Finally, it was time for dinner. Tim sat at the table, and his mom brought out a big plate. When she lifted the cover, Tim saw a huge pizza with all his favorite toppings! He was so happy and thanked his mom for the surprise. Tim kept his promise, and he got to enjoy the best dinner ever.

One day, a fat mole went out to play. He liked to bounce on the soft ground. He saw a big ball and wanted to bounce with it too. The fat mole tried to push the ball, but it was too big. He felt sad. A little girl saw the fat mole and asked, "Why are you sad, Mr. Mole?" The mole said, "I want to bounce with the big ball, but it's too big for me." The little girl smiled and said, "Don't worry, Mr. Mole, I can help you!" The little girl pushed the big ball and the fat mole bounced on it. They were having so much fun! Suddenly, the big ball popped! The fat mole and the little girl were surprised. But then, they saw many small balls come out of the big ball. The fat mole and the little girl laughed and played with the small balls. Now, the fat mole could bounce with the balls easily. They had a great day and the fat mole was happy. The little girl and the fat mole became best friends.

One day, a funny dog found a skull in the yard. The dog was very happy and wanted to show it to his friend, a big cat. The dog ran to the cat and said, "Look what I found! Let's play with it!" The cat looked at the skull and said, "That's not a toy, it's a skull! We should not play with it. We need to work together to put it back where it belongs." But the funny dog did not listen. He thought it was a fun toy and kept playing with it. The big cat tried to help, but the dog did not want to work with the cat. The funny dog played with the skull all day long. He threw it up in the air and caught it with his mouth. He rolled it on the ground and chased after it. The big cat watched, feeling sad that the dog did not want to work together. At the end of the day, the funny dog was tired from playing with the skull. He lay down to sleep, but he had a very bad dream. In his dream, the skull came to life and chased him. The dog woke up scared and wished he had listened to the big cat. He learned that some things are not meant to be played with and that it's better to work together with friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big bunny. The big bunny loved to hop. One day, while hopping, he found a napkin. The napkin was very pretty. The big bunny picked up the napkin to take it home. On his way home, the big bunny met a little mouse. The little mouse was sad. The big bunny asked, "Why are you sad?" The little mouse said, "I lost my napkin." The big bunny showed the napkin to the little mouse. The little mouse was very happy. The big bunny and the little mouse became friends. They hopped and played together. But then, something unexpected happened. The napkin started to grow! It grew and grew until it was big enough for the big bunny and the little mouse to sit on. The big bunny and the little mouse had a fun ride on the big napkin. They hopped and played all day long on the big napkin. And they were very happy.
