Category: Beginner
Title: The Tidy House and the Sue

Once upon a time, there was a tidy little house. In this house lived a happy family. There was a mom, a dad, and a little girl named Sue. They all loved each other very much. One day, Sue's mom said, "We are having a meeting with our friends tonight." Sue was excited. She wanted to help make the house tidy for the meeting. So, she asked her mom, "Can I help make the house tidy?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can help me." Sue and her mom worked together to make the house tidy. They put away toys, cleaned the table, and made the beds. When they were done, the house was very tidy. Sue's mom said, "Thank you for your help, Sue. The house looks great!" That night, the friends came over for the meeting. They all said, "Wow, your house is so tidy!" Sue felt proud that she had helped her mom. She knew that she could do big things, even though she was a little girl. After the meeting, Sue's mom gave her a big hug. She said, "Thank you for helping me today, Sue. You did a great job!" Sue smiled and hugged her mom back. She was happy that she could help her family and make her house tidy.

Once upon a time, in a small forest, a little bird named Tim was restless. He wanted to find a big, green leaf to make his nest. He flew from tree to tree, looking for the perfect leaf. One day, Tim met a squirrel named Sam. "Hi, Tim! Why are you so excited?" asked Sam. Tim told him, "I want to find a big, green leaf for my nest." Sam said, "I know where to find the best leaf! Follow me." Tim was happy and followed Sam. They found a big, green leaf on a tall tree. Tim was excited and said, "Thank you, Sam!" But when Tim tried to take the leaf, it fell to the ground. A big wind came and took the leaf away. Tim was sad, and he had to start looking for a new leaf again.

Once upon a time, there was a blue circle named Billy. Billy was very sad because he had a tear. He wanted to be a perfect circle, but the tear made him feel different. One day, Billy met a friend named Sally. Sally was a red square. Sally saw the tear and said, "Oh no, Billy! You have a tear! Let's try to fix it." They tried to fix the tear, but it just got bigger and bigger. In the end, Billy's tear grew so big that he split in half. Now, he was not a circle anymore. Both Billy and Sally were very sad. They wished they had left the tear alone.

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys and books. But sometimes they fight over them. Sara wants to play with the red car. Ben wants to play with the blue car. They both pull and tug on the cars. They make loud noises. Mom hears them and comes to see what is wrong. She sees Sara and Ben fighting over the cars. She is not happy. She says, "Sara and Ben, you need to share. You have many toys and books. You can take turns with the cars. Or you can play with something else. Fighting is not nice." Sara and Ben stop pulling and tugging. They look at Mom. They feel sorry. They say, "Sorry, Mom. We will share. We will not fight." Mom smiles. She says, "That's good. I'm glad you are sorry. You are good twins. You can hug and make up." Sara and Ben hug each other. They say, "Sorry, Sara. Sorry, Ben. We love you." They feel happy. They put the cars away. They choose another toy to play with. They play nicely together. Mom says, "You are very flexible. You can change your mind and your mood. You can be kind and gentle. I'm proud of you. You can have dessert after dinner. What do you want for dessert?" Sara and Ben say, "Ice cream!" Mom says, "OK, ice cream it is. But first, you have to eat your vegetables." Sara and Ben say, "OK, Mom. We will eat our vegetables. We like vegetables. And we like ice cream. And we like you." They hug Mom. Mom hugs them back. They are a happy family.
