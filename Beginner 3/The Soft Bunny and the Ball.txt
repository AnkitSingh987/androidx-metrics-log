Category: Beginner
Title: The Soft Bunny and the Ball

Once upon a time, in a big park, there was a soft bunny. The bunny liked to play on the ground. One day, the bunny was playing with his ball. He kicked it very hard and it went far away. The bunny felt regret because he could not find his ball. The bunny looked for the ball everywhere. He asked his friends, the bird and the squirrel, to help him. They looked high and low, but they could not find the ball. The bunny was very sad. Then, something unexpected happened. A big dog came with the ball in his mouth. The bunny was scared at first, but the dog was nice. He gave the ball back to the bunny. The bunny was so happy! The dog and the bunny became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a sister named Emma. Emma was deaf, but they loved to play together. One day, they went to the park to have fun. While they were playing, Lucy found a big stick. She wanted to show Emma how strong she was, so she tried to snap the stick in half. Emma watched and smiled, but Lucy could not break the stick. Lucy felt sad and thought she was not strong enough. A kind man saw Lucy and came to help. He showed Lucy how to snap the stick by using both hands. Lucy tried again, and this time, she broke the stick. Emma clapped her hands and laughed. Lucy learned that asking for help can make things easier. And they both played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a charming little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy missile. One day, he wanted to dress up his missile to make it look funny. As Tim put clothes on his missile, something unexpected happened. The missile started to talk! Tim was so surprised. The missile said, "Hi, Tim! I can help you with your games." Tim was happy to have a new friend. They played together all day long. But then, the missile said, "I have a secret to tell you. I am not really a missile. I am a magic wand!" Tim couldn't believe it. From that day on, Tim and his magic wand had many fun adventures. They played dress up, and Tim knew that his charming missile was truly special.

Tim and Sam were friends who liked to play in the park. They had a plan to make a fort with sticks and leaves. They found a big stick and a red flag to put on top of their fort. "Look, our fort is done!" Tim said. "We are the kings of the park!" Sam nodded and waved the flag. "Yes, we are! No one can take our fort!" But then they saw a big dog running towards them. The dog was filthy and had mud and dirt on its fur. It barked loudly and showed its teeth. "Help! A dog!" Tim cried. "Run, Sam, run!" They tried to run away, but the dog was faster. It jumped on their fort and knocked it down. It tore the flag with its mouth and shook it. It chased Tim and Sam and bit their legs. "Ow! Ow! Ow!" Tim and Sam screamed. "Go away, dog! Go away!" But the dog did not go away. It kept biting and barking. Tim and Sam were hurt and scared. They wished they had not made a fort in the park. They wished they had stayed home.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a big, weird cart. She loved to push it around and put her toys in it. One day, Sue went to the park with her mom and her cart. At the park, Sue met a boy named Tom. Tom saw Sue's cart and said, "That's a weird cart!" Sue smiled and said, "I like my weird cart. It's fun!" Tom asked if he could play with Sue and her cart. Sue said yes, and they played together all day. As the sun went down, Sue's mom called her. "Sue, it's time to go home. Remember to take your cart!" Sue said goodbye to Tom and pushed her weird cart home. She couldn't wait to play with Tom and her cart again soon.
