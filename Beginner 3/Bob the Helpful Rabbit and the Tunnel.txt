Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Helpful Rabbit and the Tunnel

Once upon a time, there was a helpful rabbit named Bob. Bob liked to help his friends. One day, he found a big tunnel. The tunnel was dark and long. Inside the tunnel, Bob saw two groups of animals. They were not happy. They wanted to separate and go to different places. But they did not know how to do it. Bob had an idea. He told the animals to make a line. Then, he helped them separate. The animals were happy and said thank you to Bob. Now, they could go to their own places. And they all lived happily ever after.

Lucy and her mom were walking in the countryside. It was a sunny day, they could see many cows in the field. Lucy was so excited, she started to talk to her mom: "Mom, look at the cows! They are so big!" Her Mom smiled and said: "Yes, Lucy, cows are big and generous too". Lucy then asked her mom: "What does generous mean?" Her Mom said: "It means that cows give us lots of things, like milk and other things. And cows can be so friendly too!". Lucy then said: "I wish I could pet one of the cows!", her mom laughed and said: "Maybe one day we can come back here to visit the cows!" Lucy was so excited that she started to run around and around the field. The cows watched her, they seemed so generous and kind. Suddenly one of the cows came to her and started to talk: "Hello Lucy, I am so happy to see you here today!" Lucy was so amazed to hear the cow speaking that she hugged it tightly. She thanked it for being so generous and kind. After that, Lucy and her mom said goodbye to the cows and went on their way. From then on, Lucy and her mom always remembered the generous cows in the countryside.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. There is a big bridge in the park. The bridge goes over a small river. One day, Lily and Tom go to the park. They see some ducks in the river. They want to feed the ducks. They have some bread. They break the bread into small pieces. They go to the bridge. They throw the bread to the ducks. The ducks are happy. They quack and swim to get the bread. But then, Lily and Tom see a big cloud. The cloud is dark and scary. They hear a loud boom. It is thunder. They see a flash. It is lightning. They are afraid. "Tom, I don't like the thunder and lightning. It is too loud and bright. I want to go home." Lily says. "Me too, Lily. But we have to cross the bridge. The bridge is too far from home. We have to be brave." Tom says. They hold hands. They walk on the bridge. They see the rain. The rain is wet and cold. They shiver. "Tom, I am wet and cold. I wish I had a coat." Lily says. "Me too, Lily. But I have an idea. We can use the bread. We can cover our heads with the bread. The bread will keep us dry and warm." Tom says. They take the bread. They put the bread on their heads. They look funny. They giggle. They cross the bridge. They run to their homes. They hug their moms. Their moms are proud of them. "Lily, Tom, you are very brave. You crossed the bridge in the storm. You used the bread to cover your heads. You are smart and creative." Their moms say. Lily and Tom smile. They are proud of themselves. They are still friends. They still like to play in the park. They still like to feed the ducks. But they don't like the thunder and lightning. They like the sun and the sky.

Once upon a time, there was a fierce dog named Max. Max lived in a small house with his best friend, Tim. They loved to play with a big tube in their yard. The tube was blue and very long. One day, Max said to Tim, "Let's work together and make the tube into a fun slide." Tim agreed and they started to work. They pushed and pulled the tube until it was like a slide. Max went first and slid down the tube. "Whee!" he shouted. Then it was Tim's turn. "Wow, this is fun!" Tim said as he slid down. They played with their new slide all day and had lots of fun. Max and Tim were very happy that they worked together to make something new and exciting.
