Category: Beginner
Title: The Ball of Tim the Boy

One day, a little boy named Tim found a yellow ball in his room. He loved the ball very much. He played with it every day. Tim would throw the ball, and it would bounce back to him. He was very happy. One sunny day, Tim took the yellow ball outside to play. He threw the ball high in the sky, but it did not come back. Tim looked up and saw the ball stuck in a big tree. He was sad and did not know how to get it back. A friendly bird saw Tim and asked, "Why are you sad?" Tim told the bird about his yellow ball stuck in the tree. The bird said, "I will help you save your ball!" The bird flew up and pushed the ball out of the tree. The ball fell down, and Tim caught it. He was very happy and thanked the bird. But when he looked at the ball, he saw that it was not yellow anymore. It was now green! The bird had helped him find a new, fun ball to play with. Tim and the bird played together, and they became best friends.

Once there were two little brothers. The oldest was named Sam and he was five years old. He had a little brother named Jake, who was just three. Jake loved playing with his toys, especially the funny ones. One day, Mommy went to the store to buy the boys some new toys. She asked Sam what he wanted. He said he hoped for a new train set. Then she asked Jake what he wanted. "I hope for a funny toy," said Jake. So, Mommy got them each a new toy. Sam got a shiny new train set and Jake got a silly yellow duck. Jake was so excited! He couldn't contain his excitement as he laughed and smiled. Mommy smiled too and said, "I'm so glad you like your new toys. They will keep you both busy and happy." And they did! The boys played and laughed together, enjoying their funny toys.

Once upon a time, there was an incredible bee. This bee was very nice and loved to play. One day, the bee flew near a big red ball. The bee thought, "I want to play with the ball!" A little boy saw the bee and thought, "I want to be friends with the bee!" So, the boy let the bee play with the ball. They had so much fun together. They laughed and played all day long. But then, something unexpected happened. The bee touched the ball and it changed colors! It turned from red to blue. The boy and the bee were very surprised. They found out that when the bee touched things, they changed colors. They had more fun changing colors of many things. And they became best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Will. Will loved to paint. He painted every day. He painted big and small things. He painted with many colors. One day, Will saw a little bird. The bird was very alert. It looked left and right. It looked up and down. The bird was pretty. Will wanted to paint the bird. Will got his paint and brush. He painted the alert bird on a big paper. He used blue, red, and yellow. The bird liked the painting. The bird and Will became friends. They painted together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to play outside with her toys. One day, she found a big box of dust. Sue thought it was fun to play with the dust. Sue had an idea. She decided to put the dust on her toys to make them look old. It was easy for her to do this. Her toys looked very different with the dust on them. Sue was happy with her idea. Later, Sue's mom saw the dusty toys. She told Sue that it was time to clean them. Sue and her mom put water on the toys to wash off the dust. After they were clean, Sue put her toys away. She learned that playing with dust can be fun, but it is also important to clean up after.
