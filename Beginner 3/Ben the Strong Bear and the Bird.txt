Category: Beginner
Title: Ben the Strong Bear and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a strong bear named Ben. He lived in a big forest with many trees. One day, he went for a walk to find some food. As Ben walked, he saw a big trap on the ground. He recognized it because it was shiny and had sharp teeth. He knew it was not safe. Ben said to himself, "I must stay away from that trap. It can hurt me." Then, a little bird flew by and saw Ben. The bird said, "Ben, you are strong! Can you help me? My friend is stuck in a trap like that one." Ben agreed to help. They found the trapped friend, and Ben used his strong paws to open the trap. The friend was free, and they all went home happy.

One day, a thin boy named Tim found a big rocket in his yard. He was very happy and wanted to ride the rocket. So, he put on his hat and climbed into the rocket. Tim said, "I am ready to go up, up, up!" As the rocket went up into the sky, Tim saw a big bird. The bird was lost and did not know how to go home. Tim wanted to help the big bird. He said, "Come with me, bird. I will help you find your home." The big bird was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tim!" The bird and Tim went up, up, up in the rocket. They looked for the bird's home. But, the rocket did not go up anymore. It started to go down, down, down! Tim and the big bird were scared. They did not want to fall. Then, the big bird had an idea. It used its big wings to help the rocket go up. The bird's wings made the rocket fly again! Tim and the bird were very happy. In the end, Tim and the big bird found the bird's home. Tim said, "Thank you for helping us fly, big bird!" The bird said, "Thank you for helping me find my home, Tim!" Then, Tim went back to his yard in the rocket. He was happy he had a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. One day, she went to the park. Lucy was so excited because it was a sunny and warm day! When she got to the park, Lucy saw a big pit in the ground. She asked her mom, "Mom, what's that?" Her mom replied, "That's a pit, Lucy." Then, Lucy asked her mom, "Please can I play in the pit?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful!" So, Lucy went over to the pit. She was careful not to get too close to the edge. It was very warm in the middle of the pit, and Lucy felt cosy in her sweater. She played in the pit for a while, imagining it was her very own big, warm blanket! At the end of the day, Lucy said to her mom, "Thank you for letting me play in the pit. It was lots of fun!" Lucy and her mom returned home with big smiles on their faces.

One day, a girl named Lily and a boy named Tom went on a picnic. They took a big red ball, yummy food, and a soft mat to sit on. They wanted to play a game with the ball. They thought it would be fun to play match. They would throw the ball to each other very fast. As they played match, Lily threw the ball to Tom fast. But something unexpected happened. The ball did not go to Tom. It went up, up, up into the sky! They both looked up and saw the ball fly away. They were very surprised. Then, a big bird flew down from the sky. The bird had their red ball! The bird gave the ball back to Lily and Tom. They were so happy. They thanked the bird and continued their picnic. They played match, but not too fast, so the ball would not fly away again.

Once upon a time, there was a funny goat. The goat liked to play and jump all day. He lived in a big farm with many other animals. One day, the funny goat wanted to find a new friend to play with. He walked around the farm and saw a small cat. The goat said, "Hi cat, do you want to play with me?" The cat said, "Yes, let's play together!" They played and had fun all day. The funny goat and the cat became best friends. They were very happy to find each other. And from that day on, they played together every day on the farm.
