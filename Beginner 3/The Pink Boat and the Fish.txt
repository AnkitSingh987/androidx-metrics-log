Category: Beginner
Title: The Pink Boat and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a pink boat. It liked to sail on the water. One sunny day, the boat met a big squash. The squash was floating in the water. "Hi, squash! Do you want to sail with me?" asked the pink boat. The squash was happy and said, "Yes, I would love to sail with you, pink boat!" So, the pink boat and the big squash sailed together. They saw fish and birds. They played in the water all day. They became best friends. And they sailed happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk in the park. She saw a small stone on the ground. She picked it up and said, "Hello, stone! You are my friend now. Please come with me." Lily and the stone went to play near the pond. They met a duck who looked guilty. The duck said, "I took bread from the boy over there. I am sorry." Lily said, "It's okay, duck. Just say please next time." The duck, Lily, and the stone played together all day. They had a lot of fun. When it was time to go home, Lily said, "Goodbye, duck. Goodbye, stone. Please come and play again soon."

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play in his garden. It rained earlier, so the garden was wet. Tim saw a big puddle and jumped in it. "Splash!" went the water. Tim's shoes and socks got wet too. Tim's mom saw him and said, "Tim, remove your wet shoes and socks before you come inside!" Tim listened to his mom and took off his wet shoes and socks. He put them near the door to dry. Tim went back to the garden to play some more. He saw a frog near the puddle. The frog said, "Ribbit! I like the wet garden." Tim smiled and played with the frog until it was time to go inside.

Once upon a time, there was a big, hairy dog named Max. Max lived in a small house with a little girl named Emma. Emma loved Max very much. They played together all day. One day, Max was playing in the yard when he heard a noise. He did not want to disturb Emma, so he went to look for the sound. Max saw a little bird in a tree. The bird said, "Help me, Max! My nest is broken." Max wanted to help the bird, so he used his big, hairy tail to fix the nest. The bird was very happy. She said, "Thank you, Max! You are very kind." The bird gave Max a reward: a big, shiny bone. Max took the bone to Emma. Emma was happy, too. She said, "Good job, Max! You are a good friend." And they played together all day, just like always.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a big, mysterious rock. The rock was very big and had a funny shape. Tim wanted to climb on the rock and balance on top. Tim tried to climb the rock, but it was too hard. He saw a long stick, like a saw, on the ground. Tim thought the saw could help him climb the mysterious rock. He put the saw next to the rock and started to climb. Tim climbed and climbed. He was almost at the top. But then, the saw moved and Tim fell down. Tim was sad and hurt. He did not balance on the mysterious rock. Tim went home with a bad ending to his day.

Once upon a time, there was a doctor who lived in a distant town. He had a big box with a secret inside. One day, he decided it was time to show everyone the secret. The doctor called all the kids from the town to come and see. They all gathered around the big box. The doctor said, "I will now reveal what is inside this box!" The kids were very excited. The doctor opened the box and out came many colorful balls. The kids laughed and clapped their hands. They played with the balls all day long. The doctor was happy to see the kids having so much fun. And that is the end of the story.
