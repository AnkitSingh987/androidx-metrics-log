Title: Jane the Girl and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. Jane was very polite. She smiled at everyone and always said please and thank you. One day, Jane wanted to buy a toy she saw at a shop. She didn't have enough money. So she went to her friend and asked if she could lend her some money. Her friend said yes, and was very polite. Jane thanked her and went to the shop to buy her toy. She also bought something for her friend to say thank you. And that's the story of how Jane got the money she needed and how her friend was very polite.

Once upon a time there lived a clumsy young girl. One day she took her special heart and held it tight in her hands. She wanted to be sure it wouldn't vanish away. Suddenly, an owl swooped down from the sky. The girl looked up in wonder. The owl said, "What is that you have in your hands, young one?". The girl clutched her heart tighter, not wanting to let go. She said, "This is my special heart. I don't want it to vanish away." The owl nodded its head in understanding, then said, "Your heart is very special. You must keep it safe. Follow me and I will show you a safe place where you can keep it." So hand in hand, the owl and the girl walked to the safe place and the girl put her heart down. She watched it carefully and smiled, knowing it wouldn't vanish away. The End.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot liked to run and play all day. Yesterday, he saw a big ball in the park. It was so big and round, and Spot wanted to play with it. He thought it would be a perfect fit for him. Spot ran to the ball and tried to push it with his nose. But the ball did not move. Spot was sad. He tried again and again, but the ball was too big for him. Spot did not give up. He wanted to show that he was strong and could play with the big ball. Just then, a small bird flew by and saw Spot. The bird felt bad for Spot and wanted to help. The bird told Spot, "You do not need to play with the big ball to show you are strong. You are strong in your own way." Spot listened to the bird and felt better. He went to find a ball that was the right fit for him. In the end, Spot learned that it is not always about being the biggest or strongest. Sometimes, it is about finding the right fit for you. And that is what makes you special.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly cat named Mr. Whiskers. He lived in a big house with a nice family. Mr. Whiskers liked to go on a tour of the house every day. One day, while on his tour, Mr. Whiskers saw a little mouse. The little mouse liked to tease Mr. Whiskers. The mouse would run and hide, and Mr. Whiskers would try to catch him. The little mouse and Mr. Whiskers played and teased each other all day. They had lots of fun. At the end of the day, Mr. Whiskers was tired but happy. He went to sleep, dreaming of his next tour and the little mouse.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very lazy boy. He did not like to do any work. He just wanted to play all day. One day, Tim found a big lollipop in his room. He was very happy. But his mom told him, "You cannot eat the lollipop now. You must finish your work first." Tim did not want to do his work. So, he told a lie to his mom. He said, "I already did my work, mom." His mom was not happy with Tim. She knew he was lying. She said, "If you do not tell the truth, you cannot have the lollipop." Tim felt bad for lying. He knew he had to do the right thing. So, Tim did his work and told his mom the truth. He said, "I am sorry, mom. I lied to you. I will not lie again." His mom was happy that Tim learned his lesson. She gave him the lollipop, and they both enjoyed a sweet treat together.
