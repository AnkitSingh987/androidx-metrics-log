Category: Beginner
Title: The Tree and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big tree. The tree had many red cherries. A little boy loved to eat cherries. He wanted to eat more and more. He wanted the cherries to increase. One day, a bird came to the tree. The bird said, "I can help you. I can make the cherries increase." The boy was very happy. He was proud of his big tree with more cherries. The bird sang a song. The cherries increased. But the boy was greedy. He wanted more and more cherries. The bird sang the song again and again. The cherries increased too much. The tree became too heavy. The tree fell down. The boy was sad. The cherries were gone. The boy learned a lesson. He should not be too greedy.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the shore with his mom. They had a red ball to play with. Tim was very happy. The sun was shining, and the water was cool. Tim threw the ball to his mom, and they laughed. Suddenly, the ball went into the water. Tim was upset. He did not want to lose his red ball. Tim's mom said, "Don't worry, Tim. We will get the ball back." They walked along the shore, looking for the ball. Finally, they saw the red ball near some rocks. Tim's mom picked it up and gave it to Tim. He was so happy! They went back to playing with the ball on the shore. Tim was not upset anymore. They had a great day at the shore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy. He wanted to go to the park to play. So he set off, walking down the street. As he went, he saw a gray flag sticking up from the park. He thought it looked like fun, so he started to run towards it. He was so excited! Soon he reached the park, and he saw the flag was even bigger than he had thought. He smiled and started climbing it. But then he saw something below the flag. It was a big hole in the ground! He stopped and stared, feeling a bit scared. He didn't know what to do. Suddenly, he had a good idea. He jumped off the flagpole and ran straight to his mom. He told her what he had seen, and his mom knew exactly what to do. She took him back to the park, and they planted some grass and flowers around the hole. When it was all finished, the little boy was so excited. He went back to the flagpole, and proudly raised the gray flag. Everyone was so happy – the little boy felt like a hero!

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing in his room. He was playing with his flexible toy snake. He loved his snake because it could bend and twist in many ways. Tim heard a noise and saw a ghost. He was scared and began to tremble. "Boo!" said the ghost. "I am a ghost, and I am here to scare you!" Tim was very scared, but he had an idea. He took his flexible toy snake and made it look like a real snake. He showed it to the ghost. "Look, I have a snake!" Tim said to the ghost. The ghost looked at the snake and started to tremble too. The ghost was scared of snakes! The ghost said, "Please, put your snake away! I do not want to scare you anymore. I am sorry." Tim put the snake away, and the ghost stopped scaring him. They became friends and played together. The ghost was not so scary after all.

Sam and Mia were bored. It was a gloomy day and they could not go outside to play. They looked around the house for something fun to do, but they found nothing. They only saw books, toys, and clothes that they had seen many times before. "Let's go to the kitchen and get a snack," Mia said. "Maybe we can find something yummy." "Okay," Sam agreed. "But we have to be quiet. Mom is working in her room and she said we should not bother her." They tiptoed to the kitchen and opened the cupboard. They saw a big jar of cookies on the top shelf. They smiled and reached for it. "Be careful," Sam warned. "Don't drop it." "I won't," Mia said. "I have it." But she did not have it. The jar slipped from her fingers and fell to the floor with a loud crash. The cookies scattered everywhere and the jar broke into pieces. "Oh no!" Sam and Mia cried. "Mom will be so mad!" They heard Mom's footsteps coming down the hall. They panicked and ran to the bathroom. They locked the door behind them and hoped Mom would not find them. "Sam? Mia? What was that noise?" Mom asked, knocking on the bathroom door. "Are you okay?" Sam and Mia did not answer. They hid behind the shower curtain and held their breath. "Sam? Mia? Open the door, please. I'm not angry. I just want to make sure you're safe." Sam and Mia looked at each other. They felt sorry for breaking the jar and making a mess. They also felt hungry for the cookies they did not get to eat. They decided to open the door and face Mom. They hoped she would forgive them and give them a hug. And maybe a snack.
