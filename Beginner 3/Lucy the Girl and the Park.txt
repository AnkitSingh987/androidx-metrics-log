Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Park

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park. She saw a boy sitting alone. The boy looked sad. Lucy felt lucky because she had a snack in her bag. She thought she could share her snack with the boy. Lucy walked to the boy and said, "Hi, I am Lucy. Do you want to share my snack? I have some yummy cookies!" The boy looked at Lucy and smiled. "Yes, please! I am very hungry. My name is Tom," said the boy. They sat and ate the cookies together. After they finished, Lucy and Tom started to play. They had so much fun! They played all day and became good friends. The moral of the story is that sharing can make you and others happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big, round pumpkin. It was near a tree. The pumpkin was restless. It wanted to move and see new things. One day, a little boy named Tim saw the pumpkin. He wanted to play with it. He tried to pick it up, but it was too big. So, he pushed it with his hands. The pumpkin started to roll. As the pumpkin rolled, it began to slip and slide. It went faster and faster. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. The pumpkin was happy to move and see new things. And they all had a fun day.

Once upon a time, in a deep, deep forest, there was a big tree. On one side of the tree, there was a little bell. The bell would ring when someone needed help. One day, a small bird was flying near the tree. The bird saw a big cat. The cat wanted to catch the bird. The bird was scared and flew to the side of the tree with the bell. The bird hit the bell to make it ring. The bell rang loud and clear. When the bell rang, a big, kind bear came to help. The bear saw the cat and roared. The cat got scared and ran away. The bird was happy and thanked the bear. From that day on, the bird and the bear became the best of friends.

One day, a thoughtful boy named Tim saw a journal on the floor. He picked it up and looked at it. It had many colors and drawings on it. Tim thought it was very pretty. Tim went to his friend, Sally, and showed her the journal. Sally said, "Wow, that's a nice journal! Let's write in it." Tim and Sally wrote their names in the journal and drew some pictures. They were very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took the journal away from them. Tim and Sally tried to catch it, but they couldn't. The wind blew the journal into a big puddle. The journal got all wet and the drawings were gone. Tim and Sally were very sad.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a penny. The penny was shiny and round. Tim was very happy. He put the penny in his pocket and zipped it up. He wanted to show his mom and dad his new treasure. Later, Tim saw a hairy dog in the park. The dog was big and friendly. Tim played with the dog and they had a lot of fun. But, when Tim checked his pocket, he saw that it was open. His penny was gone! Tim was sad and looked everywhere for his penny. Then, the hairy dog started to bark. Tim looked at the dog and saw something shiny in its mouth. The dog had found the penny! Tim was so happy and thanked the dog. The dog wagged its tail and barked. Tim and the dog were now best friends.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to swim in the sea with their dad. One day, they see a big boat. The boat takes them to a reef. A reef is a place where many fish and plants live under the water. Anna and Ben put on masks and fins. They jump into the water with their dad. They see many colors and shapes. They see yellow fish, blue fish, red fish. They see starfish, coral, seaweed. They are happy and excited. But then, Anna sees a big shark. The shark has sharp teeth and a long tail. Anna is scared. She swims to her dad and holds his hand. She says, "Dad, dad, there is a shark! I am scared!" Dad looks at the shark. He says, "Don't worry, Anna. The shark is not hungry. He is just curious. He will not hurt us. He is a friend of the reef. He helps to keep the reef healthy and clean. Let's wave to him and say hello." Anna and Ben wave to the shark. The shark waves back with his tail. He swims away. Anna and Ben are not scared anymore. They are brave and proud. They swim back to the boat. The people on the boat applaud them. They say, "Well done, Anna and Ben! You are great explorers of the reef!"
