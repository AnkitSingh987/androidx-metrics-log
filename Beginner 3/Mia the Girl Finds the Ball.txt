Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl Finds the Ball

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Mia. Mia had a red ball that she loved to play with. One sunny day, she went outside to play with her ball in the yard. As Mia was playing, her ball went into the tall tree. Mia was sad and did not know how to get her red ball back. She tried to jump, but the ball was too high. She tried to climb the tree, but she was too small. Then, Mia saw a big bird in the tree. She asked the bird, "Can you help me get my red ball back?" The bird opened its wings and flew to the ball. It picked up the red ball with its feet and dropped it into Mia's open palm. Mia was so happy and thanked the bird. From that day on, Mia and the bird became good friends, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a big bear. The bear was very patient. He liked to sit near the river and eat fish. One day, a little fox came to the river. The fox saw the bear's fish and wanted to eat it too. The little fox said, "Hi bear! Can I have some of your fish?" The patient bear said, "No, little fox. I caught these fish for myself. You can catch your own fish." The little fox was not happy. He wanted to steal the bear's fish. The next day, the little fox came back to steal the fish while the bear was sleeping. But the patient bear was not really asleep. He opened one eye and saw the little fox trying to take his fish. The bear said, "Little fox, stealing is not nice. You should ask for help if you need it." The little fox felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, bear. I should not try to steal your fish. Can you teach me how to catch fish?" The patient bear smiled and said, "Of course, little fox. I will help you." They spent the day together, and the bear taught the little fox how to catch fish. From that day on, the little fox and the patient bear became best friends. They always helped each other and shared their fish. The little fox learned that it is better to ask for help and not to steal. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Jack. He wanted to open the refrigerator but it was locked. He came to his mum and said, “Mum, I want to look in the refrigerator.” His mum said, “No, it is closed.” Jack was unhappy and he started to stare at the refrigerator. His mum said, “Do not look at the refrigerator, it is important.” Jack did not understand why the refrigerator was important and he went off to play with his toys. The next day, Jack and his family had a picnic. They put all the food in the refrigerator to keep it cool. Jack's mum opened the refrigerator and Jack was surprised to see all the food! He stared at the food and said, “Wow!” He finally understood why the refrigerator was so important.

Ben and Mia are friends. They like to play at the park. There is a big slide at the park. Ben and Mia are eager to go on the slide. They run to the slide and wait for their turn. "Can I go first?" Ben asks Mia. "Okay, you can go first," Mia says. "But then I go next." Ben nods and climbs up the ladder. He sits on the slide and slides down. He laughs and shouts, "Whee! This is fun!" Mia watches Ben and claps her hands. She is happy for Ben. She also wants to slide. She climbs up the ladder and sits on the slide. She slides down and smiles. She says, "Whee! This is fun, too!" Ben and Mia hug each other. They are good friends. They like to share and take turns. They are glad that their moms allow them to play at the park. They want to go on the slide again and again.
