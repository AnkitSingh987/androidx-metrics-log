Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Fish

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. They saw a lovely pond with lots of fish. The fish were all different colors. Lily liked the red fish the best. Lily pointed to the fish and said, "Look, mom! Fish!" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, those are fish. They live in the water." Lily clapped her hands and laughed. She liked watching the fish swim around. Lily and her mom sat by the pond for a long time. They watched the fish play and splash in the water. Lily was very happy. When it was time to go home, Lily waved goodbye to the fish. She couldn't wait to come back and see her new fish friends again.

Once upon a time, there were two twins named Tim and Tom. They were very clumsy. One day, they went to play in the park with their big red ball. While playing, the ball rolled far away into the woods. Tim and Tom went to find it. They saw the ball near a big tree. But when they got closer, they saw a big bear sleeping next to it. They began to panic. But then, the bear woke up and picked up the ball. It wasn't a real bear, but a man in a bear costume! He was just taking a nap in the woods. The man gave the ball back to Tim and Tom, and they all laughed. The twins were happy they got their ball back, and they made a new friend.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys. They have many vehicles: cars, trucks, buses, and trains. They make noises when they push them on the floor. Vroom, vroom, beep, beep, choo, choo! One day, they are playing with their vehicles in their room. They have a big race. They pretend their vehicles are very fast and strong. They cheer for their vehicles. Go, go, go! But Mom is in the next room. She is trying to sleep. She has a headache. She hears the loud noises from Lily and Ben's room. She is not happy. She gets up and goes to their room. "Shh, shh, shh!" she says. "You are too loud. I need to sleep. You need to sleep too. It is nap time." Lily and Ben look at Mom. They are sorry. They love Mom. They do not want to make her sad. "Sorry, Mom," they say. "We will be quiet. We will sleep too." They put their vehicles away. They hug Mom. They go to their beds. They close their eyes. They sleep. Mom smiles. She goes back to her room. She sleeps too.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big toy box full of fun toys. One day, she found a harmless little bug in her room. The bug was trying to fight a piece of thread that it thought was a bad thing. Lily watched the bug and the thread. She laughed as the bug tried to fight the thread. The thread did not move or fight back. It just lay there on the floor. The bug thought it was very strong and brave. After a while, Lily picked up the thread and put it away. The bug stopped fighting and looked at Lily. She smiled at the bug and let it go outside. The bug was happy to be free and flew away. Lily went back to her toys and played, knowing she had helped a new friend.

Once there was a lazy bear called Bright. Bright always liked to sleep a lot, and never wanted to pass his time doing anything else. All his animal friends saw how lazy he was, and were unhappy about it. One day, Bright's friends decided to teach him a lesson. They gave Bright the task to name all the animals in the forest. Bright knew he had to be clever, so he tried his best, but failed to name all the animals by sunset. His friends, who had been waiting for him, were so disappointed. Bright was embarrassed and felt ashamed. From then on, he made sure to use all his time wisely and not be lazy. He now realizes that he needs to work on something meaningful, or it will pass him by forever. He remembers that if you are too lazy, you can miss out on some great experiences!
