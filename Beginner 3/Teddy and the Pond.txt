Category: Beginner
Title: Teddy and the Pond

Teddy and his friends were playing in the park one day. As always, they were having lots of fun. “Let’s go see the pond!” Teddy said. His friends agreed, so they started walking. They soon spotted a gate. It had a big word that everyone could read – Exit. “Let’s go this way instead!” Teddy said, poking the exit sign. His friends followed, but after walking a few more steps, they reached an even bigger gate. “It says No Entry. What now?” Teddy said. His friends looked around, but there was no other way. Suddenly, they spotted an arrow pointing to a small door. It was written Available. “Maybe we can go through there!” Teddy said, pointing to the door. His friends eagerly followed, and sure enough, the door led them to the pond. They ran to the water’s edge and started playing. Teddy had known the exit sign would lead them to the pond – and he was right!

Timmy was a spoiled boy who always wanted more toys. He had a big room full of cars, trains, dolls, and books, but he was never happy. He always cried and whined when his mom and dad said no to him. One day, he saw a shiny red ball on the ceiling of his room. He wanted it very much, but he did not know how to get it. He tried to jump and reach it, but he was too short. He tried to climb on his bed, but he fell down. He tried to use a chair, but he stumbled and hurt his knee. He was very angry and frustrated. He started to scream and throw his toys around. He did not care if he broke them or made a mess. He just wanted the ball. His mom and dad heard the noise and came to his room. They saw the chaos and the ball on the ceiling. They were very upset and disappointed. They told Timmy that he was being naughty and selfish. They said that he had to clean up his room and apologize. They also said that he could not have the ball, because it was not a toy. It was a smoke detector that they had installed to keep him safe. Timmy did not understand. He thought his mom and dad were mean and unfair. He did not want to listen or obey. He kept crying and kicking. He did not learn his lesson. He was still a spoiled boy.

Tom and Jen are friends. They like to play with blocks. They make towers and houses and cars. They have fun. One day, they find a big box. It is empty and brown. They think it is a good toy. They want to fit in the box. They take turns to go inside. They pretend it is a spaceship or a cave or a castle. They laugh and shout. But the box is not dependable. It is old and weak. It has holes and tears. It can break easily. Tom and Jen do not know this. They play too hard. They jump and push and kick. The box rips and falls apart. Tom and Jen fall out. They hit the floor. They hurt their heads and knees and elbows. They cry and bleed. They are scared and sad. The blocks are scattered and broken. The view is messy and ugly. The fun is gone. The box is a bad toy. Tom and Jen are sorry. They wish they played with something else.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a silly boy who liked to play all day. One day, Tim was playing with his ball in the house. He knew he should not do this, but he did it anyway. Tim threw the ball very hard, and it hit the wall. The wall now had a big hole in it. Tim was scared and did not know what to do. He tried to fix the wall, but he could not. When Mom came home, she saw the hole in the wall. She was very sad and told Tim that he had spoiled the wall. Tim felt bad for what he did. Mom said that Tim could not play with his ball for a long time. Tim was sad and learned that being silly can sometimes lead to bad things.
