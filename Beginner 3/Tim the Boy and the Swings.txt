Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Swings

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy made of copper. He loved his toy very much. It was shiny and fun to play with. Tim would take his toy everywhere he went. He felt comfortable when he had his toy with him. One day, Tim and his mom went to the park. Tim was so excited that he started to rush. He wanted to play on the swings and the slide. But, as he was running, he dropped his copper toy on the ground. Tim didn't notice, and he kept running to the playground. When it was time to go home, Tim looked for his copper toy. He couldn't find it anywhere. He started to cry. His mom tried to help him look for the toy, but they couldn't find it. Tim was very sad. He learned that he should not rush and be more careful with his things.

Once upon a time, in a small house near the ocean, lived a little boy named Tim and his mom. Tim loved to play outside and listen to the mighty waves crash on the shore. One day, Tim asked his mom, "Mom, can you explain the ocean to me?" Mom smiled and said, "Well, Tim, the ocean is a big place full of water and many fish. It goes on and on, further than we can see." One sunny day, Tim went to play by the ocean. As he was playing, he saw something unexpected. A tiny fish was flopping on the sand. "Oh no!" Tim said. "You need to be in the water, little fish!" Tim picked up the fish and gently put it back in the ocean. The fish swam away, happy to be back in its home. Just then, Tim heard a voice. It was the fish! "Thank you, Tim!" said the fish. "You saved me! I have a secret to share with you." The fish told Tim that it was a magical fish and could grant him one wish. Tim thought for a moment and wished for a new friend to play with. The magical fish nodded and swam away. The next day, as Tim played near the ocean, he saw a new little boy. They became best friends, and Tim knew that his wish had come true. From that day on, Tim and his new friend spent many days playing by the mighty ocean, and Tim always remembered the magical fish that had brought them together.

Ben and Lily liked to play in the garden. They had a boat that they made from a big box. They painted it blue and red and put a flag on it. They pretended it was a pirate ship and they sailed to find treasure. One day, they saw a big bush with pink flowers. They had never seen it before. It was near the fence, where they could not go. They wondered what was behind the fence. Maybe there was more treasure there. They wanted to see the flowers up close. They thought they were very pretty. They decided to take their boat to the bush. They pushed it across the grass. It was difficult. The boat was heavy and the grass was long. They had to work hard. They did not give up. They were brave pirates. They reached the bush. They were happy. They smelled the flowers. They were sweet. They picked some and put them in their boat. They were going to take them home and show their mom. But then they heard a loud bark. It came from behind the fence. It was a big dog. It was angry. It did not like the pirates. It did not like the flowers. It jumped over the fence and ran towards them. It wanted to bite them. Ben and Lily were scared. They dropped the flowers and ran away. They left their boat behind. They did not look back. They ran to their mom. She hugged them and made them feel safe. They learned a lesson. They should not go near the fence. They should not take flowers that did not belong to them. They should be careful of dogs. They were sorry for their boat. They hoped the dog would not break it. They hoped the flowers would bloom again.
