Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Sam

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his friend, Sam. They wanted to buy a toy to play with. Tim's mom told them to be obedient and come back home soon. Tim and Sam said, "Yes, Mom" and went to the store. At the store, they saw a big, red ball. They both wanted it. Tim said, "We should share the ball." But Sam wanted the ball all for himself. Sam took the ball and ran away. Tim was sad and went back home without the ball. When Tim got home, his mom saw that he was sad. She asked him what happened. Tim told her about Sam and the ball. Tim's mom smiled and said, "You were obedient and shared. That is good." She then gave Tim a big, red ball just like the one at the store. Tim was happy and learned that being obedient and sharing is always the best choice.

Mary remembered the day she got a new skirt. It was a little one, with pretty flowers all over it. She wore it to school that day and her friends said it was beautiful. At recess, Mary played with her friends and her skirt spun around when she twirled. She wore the skirt almost every day from then on. One day, Mary's mummy asked her why she was wearing the same skirt all the time. Mary replied, "I want to remember the day I got the skirt". Mummy smiled. She said, "Well, you can remember the day in other ways too. You can look at pictures and tell stories about it. That way, you won't wear your skirt too much". Mary nodded. She was so happy she got to remember the day she got her skirt. She was sure she'd never forget it.

One day, a dog named True went for a walk. True was a regular dog with four legs and a tail. He liked to look at things in the park. He saw a big tree and thought, "I want to look at the top of the tree." True tried to look up, but he could not see the top. The next day, True went to the park again. He saw a tall ladder by the big tree. "Maybe I can use the ladder to look at the top of the tree," True thought. He climbed up the ladder, step by step. True was so happy that he could see the top of the tree. But when True looked down, he saw something he did not expect. There was a big, soft bed at the bottom of the tree. True was not a regular dog anymore - he was a dog who could climb ladders and jump into soft beds! True jumped down from the ladder into the bed and had a big, happy smile.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a fierce lion. He liked to swing on the trees and play with his friends. One day, he saw a big frog near the pond. The frog looked sad. The lion asked the frog, "Why are you sad?" The frog said, "I can't swim in the pond. There is poison in the water. I don't want to get sick." The lion wanted to help his new friend. The lion and the frog worked together to clean the pond. They took out the poison and made the water safe again. The frog was so happy, he jumped into the water and swam. The lion and the frog became best friends and played together every day.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw a long rail near the playground. He wanted to walk on the rail, but he was a bit scared. Tim's mom said, "Don't worry, I will hold your hand." As Tim and his mom walked on the rail, a big bird came and started to tease them. The bird flew around them and made loud noises. Tim felt troubled and scared. He said, "Mom, I don't like the bird. It is not nice." Then, something unexpected happened. The bird flew down and picked up a pretty, shiny stone from the ground. It gave the stone to Tim as a gift. The bird said, "I'm sorry for teasing you. I just wanted to play. Will you be my friend?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, I will be your friend." From that day on, Tim, his mom, and the bird had fun at the park together.
