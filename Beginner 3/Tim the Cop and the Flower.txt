Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Cop and the Flower

Once upon a time, there was a friendly cop named Tim. He liked to help people in the town. Tim had a big hat and a shiny badge. He walked around the town with a big smile on his face. One day, a little girl named Lily was playing in the park. She saw a pretty flower and wanted to pick it. But she was too small to reach it. Lily saw Tim the friendly cop and asked for help. "I want the pretty flower, please," she said. Tim the friendly cop smiled and picked the flower for Lily. She was so happy and said, "Thank you, Tim!" They both laughed and played in the park. From that day on, Lily and Tim were the best of friends.

Once upon a time there was a little boy called Tim. He was three years old and full of energy. One day Tim went to the park to play with his friends. They ran and played tag. They laughed and had lots of fun. Just before it was time for Tim to go home, he spotted an old man sitting on a bench. He looked very sad and Tim wanted to help. He ran over and poked the man’s arm gently to get his attention. The man smiled at Tim. He said that he had been thinking about a special memory which made him feel very upset. He had forgotten all the details and felt so sad. Tim was determined to make the old man feel better. He put his hand into his pocket and pulled out a small toy. He gave it to the man and said, “This will help you remember your special memory.” The man was so thankful that he gave Tim a big hug. He said the toy brought back the memory and he was no longer upset. He thanked Tim for being so kind and helpful, then wished him on his way. Tim was so happy that he had made the old man feel better. He waved goodbye, then ran off to find his friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a black cat named Luna. One day, Mia and Luna went for a wander in the woods near their house. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. As they wandered, they found a small, dark cave. Mia said to Luna, "Let's go in and see what's inside!" They went in the cave and it was very dark. Mia remembered she had a candle in her bag. She lit the candle so they could see. The cave was full of pretty rocks and shiny things. Mia and Luna looked around and found a big, shiny rock. Mia said, "This is the best rock ever! Let's take it home!" They took the rock home and showed it to Mia's mom. Mia's mom said, "Wow, what a special rock! This was a great adventure!" And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a fierce dog named Max. He loved to play with a sack. One day, Max saw a toilet in the yard. It was new and shiny. Max was curious about it. Max played with the sack near the toilet. He tried to put the sack in the toilet. But something unexpected happened. The toilet started to shake and make a loud noise. Max was scared and ran away. But then, the toilet turned into a big, friendly dog. The dog said, "I am not a toilet, I am a dog! I was stuck in the toilet, but your sack helped me get out. Thank you, Max!" Max and the new dog became best friends and played with the sack together.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a big basketball on the grass. Tom wants to play with it. He runs to the basketball and picks it up. "Look, Lily, I have a basketball!" he says. "Do you want to play with me?" Lily is fearful. She sees a big boy near the basketball. He looks angry. He has a red shirt and a black hat. He is bigger than Tom and Lily. "No, Tom, put it down!" she says. "That is not our basketball. That is his basketball. He will be mad at us." Tom is not fearful. He likes to dare. He thinks the big boy is not scary. He thinks he is funny. He throws the basketball in the air and catches it. "Ha, ha, ha!" he laughs. "He can't do anything. He is too slow. Come on, Lily, don't be fearful. Let's play!" The big boy sees Tom and Lily. He is mad. He runs to them and shouts. "Hey, you, give me back my basketball!" he says. "That is mine. You are bad. Go away!" Tom and Lily are scared. They drop the basketball and run away. They hide behind a tree. They hear the big boy kick the basketball and walk away. "Tom, that was not nice," Lily says. "You should not take things that are not yours. You should not dare the big boy. He could hurt us." Tom is sorry. He knows Lily is right. He hugs Lily and says. "I'm sorry, Lily. You are a good friend. I will not do that again. I will be nice. Let's go home."
