Category: Beginner
Title: The New Car and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a new car. The car was very fast and everyone wanted to see it. One day, a boy named Tim saw the car and wanted to drive it. He asked his mom, "Can I please drive the new car?" His mom said, "No, Tim. It is not safe for you to drive the car. You are too little." But Tim did not listen. When no one was looking, he got in the car and started to drive. He drove very fast and had a lot of fun. But soon, he saw a big tree in front of him. Tim did not know what to do. He tried to reverse the car, but it was too late. The car hit the tree and broke. Tim was very sad and scared. His mom and dad came running and saw the broken car. They were very upset with Tim. They told him that he should have listened and stayed safe. Now, the new car was gone, and Tim learned a big lesson.

Once upon a time, there was a little leaf. The leaf was green and lived on a big tree. The leaf was very happy on the tree. One day, the leaf saw a new leaf next to it. The leaf said, "welcome!" to the new leaf. They became best friends. One day, the sky turned dark and the wind blew hard. The little leaf was frightened. It held on tight to the tree with its friend. The wind blew and blew, but the leaves did not let go. The next day, the sun came out and the sky was blue again. The wind had stopped. The little leaf and its friend were still on the tree. They had been brave together. Now, they knew they could face anything as long as they had each other.

John was a silly boy. He loved to play on his swing. He liked to push himself so high that he felt like he could soar with the birds. On this day, John pushed himself so high that something bad happened. He fell off of the swing and hurt his arm. He started to cry. Mama ran to his side and hugged him. She said, "It's okay, John. I forgive you. Let's go get you a bandage for your arm." John stopped crying. He looked up at Mama and said, "You forgive me? Do you still love me?" Mama smiled and said, "Yes, I will always love you no matter what." Then she kissed John's forehead and hugged him tight. John felt so much better. He knew he could always trust Mama to forgive him no matter what. He gave her one more hug and ran off to the swing to play some more.

Once upon a time, there was a famous chicken. The chicken lived on a farm. The farm was very big. The chicken had many friends on the farm. The chicken liked to play with the other animals. One day, the chicken was talking to the cow. The chicken said, "I want to own a farm too." The cow said, "You can do it, chicken!" The chicken worked very hard. One day, something unexpected happened. The chicken found a magic seed. The chicken planted the seed. The seed grew into a big tree. The tree had many farms on it. The chicken was very happy. The chicken picked a farm from the tree. The chicken owned the farm. All the animals came to live on the chicken's farm. They all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little gray cat set out on a road. The cat wanted to find a friend to play with. The sun was shining, and the cat was happy. As the cat walked, it saw a big tree. Up in the tree, there was a bird. The cat thought the bird could be a fun friend. The cat jumped up and tried to reach the bird, but it was too high. Then, something unexpected happened. The bird flew down and landed on the cat's back. The cat was surprised, but happy. The bird and the cat became best friends and played together all day on the road.
