Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Cat and the Bird

One day, a chubby cat named Tom was on a porch. He liked to spin around and play with his ball. The sun was shining, and Tom was very happy. Suddenly, a big wind came and took Tom's ball away. Tom was very sad. He wanted to find his ball. He looked everywhere, but he could not find it. Then, Tom saw a little bird. The bird had his ball! The bird was spinning the ball on its beak. Tom was very surprised. The bird gave Tom his ball back, and they played together. Now, Tom and the bird are best friends.

Once upon a time, there was an enormous fish named Fin. Fin had big scales that were very pretty. He lived in a pond with many friends. One day, Fin saw a new fish named Lily. Lily was sad because her scales were not as big and pretty as Fin's. Fin wanted to help Lily, so he had an idea. Fin said, "Let's spread the pretty scales from me to you." They touched fins and the pretty scales spread to Lily. Now, both Fin and Lily had big, pretty scales. They were very happy and swam together in the pond.

One day, a cheerful girl named Lily went to the park. She loved to skip by the pond. As she skipped, she saw a small boat. The boat was very pretty. Lily met a boy named Tim. Tim was sad. "Why are you sad?" asked Lily. "I lost my boat," said Tim. Lily had an idea. She took Tim to the pond. "Is this your boat?" she asked. Tim was so happy. "Yes, that's my boat! Thank you, Lily!" Lily and Tim played with the boat. They had lots of fun. Then, a big wind came. The boat flew up in the sky! Lily and Tim were surprised. They laughed and watched the boat fly away. They learned that it's good to share and help others.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk in the park. He saw a big stick on the ground. He wanted to play with the stick, but his mom said, "Don't touch it! It might hurt." Tim was sad, but he listened to his mom. They walked away from the stick. Suddenly, a little dog came running up to them. The dog saw the stick and picked it up in his mouth. The dog did not get hurt. Tim was surprised. He thought the stick would hurt the dog, but it did not. The dog brought the stick to Tim and wanted to play. Tim looked at his mom, and she said it was okay. They played with the dog and the stick all day, and no one got hurt.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max loved to run and play all day long. One day, Max saw an enormous red ball in the park. He wanted to play with the ball, but he had to stop because he saw a bow on it. Max thought, "I wonder who this ball belongs to?" In the park, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad because she lost her big red ball. She looked everywhere for it, but she could not find it. Lily's mom said, "Don't worry, we will find your ball soon." Lily felt a little better, but she still wanted her ball back. Max decided to bring the big red ball back to Lily. He picked up the ball with his teeth and carried it to her. When Lily saw Max with her ball, she was so happy! She gave Max a big hug and said, "Thank you, Max!" The bow on the ball was a clue that it belonged to someone special. Max and Lily became best friends and played with the big red ball every day. And they always stopped to take a break and share a bow, just like the one that brought them together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Jack. He was very curious, and he loved to explore. One day, Jack went looking for something new to explore. He searched high and low, until he could go no further. Suddenly, Jack found a sail! It was a cute little sail that was blue with yellow stripes. Jack was so excited to find the sail, he wanted to find out where it had come from. He grabbed the sail and ran with it. Jack raced up the hill with the sail and looked around. He could see the sea in the distance, and he knew that was where the sail must have come from. Jack was determined to find out more about the sail, so he started walking towards the sea. When Jack arrived at the sea, he saw a beautiful boat with its sail already up! He realised that the cute sail he had found must have belonged to the boat. He was so delighted that he had discovered the sail's home. From then on, Jack would take his sail and go sailing on the sea every day. He had so much fun with his new sail that he was sure he'd never forget it!
