Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Tom and the Car

Lily and Tom were going to the park with Mom. They put on their hats and shoes and got in the car. But the car did not start. Mom said, "Oh no, the car is broken. We have to delay our trip to the park. I have to call Dad for help." Lily and Tom were sad. They wanted to play on the swings and slides. They did not want to wait in the car. They looked out the window and saw a big dog. The dog was barking and running around. Lily was scared of the dog. She said, "Mom, I don't like the dog. He is loud and mean. Can we go away?" Mom said, "Don't worry, Lily. The dog is just playing. He can't hurt us in the car. You are brave, Lily. You can look at something else. Look, there is a light in the sky. Do you see it?" Lily and Tom looked up and saw a bright light. It was a rainbow. It had many colors. It was beautiful. Lily and Tom smiled. They said, "Wow, Mom, look at the rainbow. It is so pretty. What is it?" Mom said, "A rainbow is a light that you see when the sun and the rain meet. It is a sign of hope and joy. It means that good things will happen. Maybe our car will be fixed soon and we can go to the park. Or maybe we can find another way to have fun. Do you want to sing a song with me?" Lily and Tom nodded. They liked to sing with Mom. They sang a song about the rainbow and the park. They felt happy and brave. They forgot about the dog and the car. They enjoyed the light and the music. They waited for Dad to come and help them. They hoped to see the rainbow again.

Anna and Ben like to draw with crayons. They have many colors and papers. They draw animals, houses, flowers and cars. Sometimes they show their drawings to Mom and Dad. One day, Anna wants to draw a new image. She thinks of a big bird with many colors. She has seen it in a book. She tries to draw the bird, but it is hard. She does not know how to make the wings, the beak and the eyes. She gets worried. She thinks her drawing is bad. She shows her drawing to Ben. Ben looks at it and smiles. He says, "Wow, Anna, your bird is very pretty. I like the colors. Can you tell me about your bird?" Anna feels happy. She tells Ben about the bird. She says, "This bird is called a parrot. It can talk and fly. It lives in the forest. It likes to eat fruits and nuts." Ben says, "That is very cool. I want to draw a parrot too. Can you help me?" Anna says, "Yes, of course. I can show you how to draw the wings, the beak and the eyes. We can draw together." They get more crayons and papers. They draw more parrots. They have fun. They are not worried anymore. They are proud of their images.

One day, a chubby cat named Kitty went to the kitchen. She saw a cabinet. Kitty wanted to see what was inside. She jumped up and opened the cabinet with her paw. Inside, she found many yummy treats. Kitty was very happy. Kitty's friend, Doggy, came into the kitchen. He saw Kitty with the treats. "Can I have some treats too?" Doggy asked. Kitty said, "Yes, you can have some." They both ate the treats and were very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The cabinet door began to shut all by itself! Kitty and Doggy were scared. They jumped back and watched the cabinet. It shut with a loud noise. They looked at each other, surprised. They did not know that the wind from the open window had shut the cabinet. They decided to play outside and forget about the scary cabinet.

Ben is a boy who likes to play in the park. He likes to use the slide, the swing and the seesaw. He also likes to look at the birds, the flowers and the bugs. One day, he sees a big, hairy dog on a bench. The dog is brown and black and has a long tail. Ben thinks the dog is cute and wants to pet it. He walks slowly to the bench and says, "Hello, doggy. Can I be your friend?" The dog wags its tail and licks Ben's face. Ben giggles and rubs the dog's ears. He says, "You are soft and warm. What is your name?" The dog barks and Ben thinks it means, "Woof". Ben and Woof play together for a while. They run, jump and roll on the grass. They share a snack of crackers and cheese. They are happy and have fun. Then, Ben hears his mom calling him. He says, "Bye, Woof. I have to go home now. I hope I see you again." He gives the dog a hug and a kiss. The dog barks and wags its tail. He says, "Bye, Ben. You are a good friend. I hope you come back soon." They wave to each other and smile. Ben and Woof are the best of friends.
