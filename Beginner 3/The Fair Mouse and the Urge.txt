Category: Beginner
Title: The Fair Mouse and the Urge

Once upon a time, there was a fair little mouse. She was very sweet and kind. One day, she got a very special urge. She wanted to try something new. The little mouse decided to follow her urge and set off on an adventure. Everywhere she went, she saw interesting things. She went high and low, far and wide, looking everywhere. Finally, she arrived at the beach. The beach was filled with children playing in the sand and the waves. The little mouse was delighted. She heard a child calling her name. She turned around, and a little girl was smiling at her. The little girl said, "Hi, mouse! I have an urge to give you a hug. Will you let me?" The little mouse grinned and shook her head “Yes!” The little girl gave the little mouse the most special hug--a fair one! From that day on, the little mouse remembered that special moment.

Anna and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to pretend they were soldiers and fight with sticks. Anna was the leader and Ben was her helper. They had a big post made of cardboard where they kept their supplies. One day, a big boy came to the park. He saw Anna and Ben and wanted to play with them. But he did not ask nicely. He said, "Give me your post and your sticks, or I will hit you." He was very mean and loud. Anna and Ben were fearful. They did not want to fight with the big boy. He was much bigger and stronger than them. They tried to run away, but he chased them. He caught Ben and pulled his stick. Ben cried and said, "Let me go, let me go!" Anna saw Ben in trouble. She wanted to help him, but she was also scared. She did not know what to do. She thought maybe if she gave the big boy what he wanted, he would leave them alone. She said, "Please, don't hurt us. We surrender. You can have our post and our sticks. Just let us go." The big boy laughed. He said, "Too late. I don't want your post and your sticks. I want to have fun. And you are my fun." He pushed Ben to the ground and grabbed Anna's hair. He dragged them to his bike and tied them to it. He said, "Now, you are my prisoners. I will take you to my house and make you do whatever I want. And no one will find you. Ha ha ha!" Anna and Ben screamed and cried. They wished they had not surrendered. They wished someone would come and save them. But no one heard them. The big boy rode away with them. They did not know what would happen to them. They were very sad and afraid. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a fierce dog named Spot. Spot lived near a bridge. The bridge was big and long. Spot liked to sit near the bridge and watch the water. One day, a little girl named Lucy came to the bridge. She saw Spot and was scared. She thought Spot did not belong there. Lucy wanted to cross the bridge, but she was too scared of the fierce dog. Then, something unexpected happened. Spot saw a ball near the bridge. He picked up the ball and brought it to Lucy. Lucy was not scared anymore. She saw that Spot just wanted to play. They played with the ball near the bridge all day. Lucy and Spot became best friends, and now they both belonged near the bridge.

One day, a clumsy cat named Tom saw a big box with a label on it. The label had a picture of a fish. Tom loved fish. He wanted to steal the fish from the box. He thought, "I will take the fish when no one is looking." Tom waited and waited. Then, he saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was playing with her ball. Tom thought, "Now is my chance to steal the fish!" He slowly moved towards the box. But he was so clumsy, he knocked over a cup. Sue heard the noise and looked at Tom. Sue said, "Tom, what are you doing?" Tom felt bad for trying to steal the fish. He said, "I'm sorry, Sue. I wanted the fish in the box." Sue smiled and said, "It's okay, Tom. We can share the fish." Tom was happy, and they both enjoyed the fish together.
