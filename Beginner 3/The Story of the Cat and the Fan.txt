Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Cat and the Fan

The lazy cat was lying in the sunshine. He had nothing to do, so he simply opened his eyes and blinked. Suddenly, he heard a noise. He looked around and saw a fan spinning around in the corner. He was curious and wanted to find out what it was. He carefully crawled closer and then he saw it. It was a fan! He had never seen one before. He thought it was amazing. He got so excited that he wanted to try it. So he ordered the fan on. But nothing happened. He tried again. Still nothing. He got very frustrated and tried one more time, and finally it worked. The lazy cat felt a gentle breeze on his fur. He was so happy and content that he fell asleep for the rest of the afternoon.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max lived in a perfect, big house with a nice family. Max loved his life with his family. One day, Max saw a big, shiny ball in the yard. He wanted to play with it. Max ran up to the ball, but it was not a ball. It was a big, round bee nest! The bees came out and buzzed around Max. Max was scared and ran away. He said, "I regret touching the bees' house!" Max ran back to his family, who helped him feel better. They told Max, "It's okay to make mistakes. Just learn from them and be more careful next time." Max learned that sometimes things are not what they seem, and it is important to be careful. And that's how Max became an even better dog, living his perfect life with his family.

Tom likes to read novels. Novels are big books with many words and pictures. Tom likes to go to the library and find new novels to read. He likes to sit on a soft chair and rub his eyes when he reads. One day, Tom went to the library with his mom. He saw a novel that he wanted to read. It had a picture of a dragon on the cover. Tom picked up the novel and went to the chair. He was happy. But then, a boy came and took the novel from Tom. The boy was rude. He said, "This is my novel. You can't have it. Go away." He pushed Tom and ran away with the novel. Tom was sad and angry. He wanted to read the novel. He wanted to see the dragon. He said to his mom, "Mom, the boy took my novel. He was rude. He pushed me. I want my novel back." His mom hugged him and said, "I'm sorry, Tom. That was not nice of the boy. He should not take things that are not his. He should not push you. He should say sorry. Let's go and find him and tell him that." Tom and his mom went and looked for the boy. They found him in another corner of the library. He was reading the novel. He did not look happy. He looked bored. Tom's mom said to the boy, "Excuse me, but that novel belongs to Tom. He found it first. He wanted to read it. You took it from him. You were rude. You pushed him. You should give it back to him and say sorry." The boy looked at Tom's mom and then at Tom. He saw that Tom was sad and angry. He felt bad. He said, "I'm sorry. I was rude. I pushed you. I didn't mean to. I just wanted to read the novel. But it is not very good. It is too hard for me. I don't understand the words. You can have it back." He gave the novel back to Tom. Tom took it and smiled. He said, "Thank you. I'm sorry too. I was mad at you. But I'm not anymore. Do you want to read the novel with me? I can help you with the words. Maybe we can be friends." The boy smiled too. He said, "Yes, I would like that. I'm sorry for being rude. I'm glad you are not mad. Maybe we can be friends." Tom and the boy went to the chair and sat together. They opened the novel and started to read. They saw the dragon and many other things. They learned new words and had fun. They were happy. They were friends.
