Category: Beginner
Title: Fluffy the Dog and the Duck

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Fluffy. Fluffy had a big nose and was very lonely. He had no friends to play with. One day, Fluffy saw a little duck near the pond. The duck was taking a bath in the water. Fluffy thought it looked like fun. Fluffy went to the pond and asked the duck, "Can I bathe with you?" The duck said, "Yes, come join me!" So, Fluffy jumped in the water and they both splashed and played together. Fluffy was so happy to have a friend. After they were done playing, Fluffy and the duck went to find more friends. They found a cat and a rabbit. They all played together and had lots of fun. Fluffy was not lonely anymore. The moral of the story is that if you are kind and ask others to play, you can make new friends and not be lonely.

Tom and Anna are playing in the park. They like to pretend they are heroes and save the world from bad guys. Tom has a red cape and Anna has a blue mask. They run and jump and hide behind the trees and slides. But then, a big boy comes and takes Tom's cape. He says it is his now and he laughs. Tom is very sad and angry. He wants his cape back. He says to the big boy, "Give me my cape! You are not a hero, you are a bad guy!" The big boy does not listen. He says, "No, this is my cape. You are a baby. Go away!" He runs away with the cape. Anna sees what happens. She wants to help Tom. She has an idea. She says to Tom, "Don't worry, Tom. I have a plan. Follow me." Anna and Tom sneak behind the big boy. They wait until he is not looking. Then, Anna grabs the cape from his back. She says, "Ha! We got you! We are the heroes, you are the bad guy!" The big boy is surprised and angry. He turns around and sees Anna and Tom. He says, "Hey, give me that cape! That is not fair!" But Anna and Tom do not listen. They run away with the cape. They say, "No, this is our cape. You are a bully. Go away!" They find a hidden spot in the park. They put on the cape and the mask. They hug and smile. They say, "We did it! We are the best heroes ever!"

Once upon a time, there was an old oyster. The old oyster lived in the sea. The old oyster wanted to make friends. One day, a little fish swam by. The old oyster said, "Hello, little fish! I want to be your friend." The little fish said, "Yes, I want to be your friend too!" The old oyster and little fish played and talked. They were happy. They were good friends. The old oyster did not feel lonely anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a unique bird named Bluey. Bluey lived in a big tree near a small pond. Bluey had many friends, like Missy the cat and Buzzy the bee. One day, a storm came to their home. The wind blew hard and the rain fell down. Bluey, Missy, and Buzzy were scared. They knew they needed to find a safe place to hide. Bluey said, "We need to go to the big cave!" So, they all went to the big cave. It was dry and safe inside. They waited for the storm to stop. When the storm was over, they went back to their homes. The sun was shining again. Bluey, Missy, and Buzzy were happy they helped each other. From that day on, they knew they could always count on each other when they needed help. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play near the water. One day, while he was playing, he saw a troubled duck. The duck was stuck in the mud and could not get out. Tim wanted to help the duck, so he yelled to his mom, "Mom, come help me! The duck is stuck!" His mom came running to see what was wrong. She tried to help the duck too, but she could not reach it. Then, a big wind came and blew the duck out of the mud. The duck was free! Tim and his mom were so happy. They learned that sometimes, when you are in trouble, help can come from unexpected places.
