Category: Beginner
Title: Sue and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She lived in a small house with her mom. One day, a new neighbor moved in next door. Sue was very excited to meet her new friend. Sue went over to the new neighbor's house and knocked on the door. The door opened and there stood a big, friendly dog. "Woof! Woof!" said the dog. Sue was surprised, but she loved dogs, so she was very happy. "Hi dog, I am Sue. Do you want to play?" Sue asked. The dog wagged its tail and barked, "Woof! Woof!" Sue and the dog played all day. They ran, jumped, and laughed together. Sue loved her new neighbor, the dog. When it was time to go home, Sue went back to her house and told her mom all about her new friend. From that day on, Sue and the dog were best friends and had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a kite. His kite was very old, but he loved it. Tim liked to play with his kite outside. One day, Tim's kite flew away. The wind took it far, far away. Tim was very sad. He wanted to find his kite. So, Tim started to search for his kite. He looked in the park and behind the trees. Finally, Tim found his kite. It was stuck in a big tree. Tim was very happy. He got his kite back and went home. Tim played with his ancient kite every day, and they had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Julia. She was three years old and loved to create. One day, she closed her eyes and began to imagine cop. He was a tall and strong police officer. When Julia opened her eyes she went outside to find a big angry cop. He was standing right in front of her. Julia was scared and began to cry. The cop said kindly, “Why are you so scared, little one? I’m not angry with you, I’m just here to help.” Julia smiled and told the cop that she wanted to find a way to help create a better world. The cop smiled and said “That’s a great idea. It’s up to us to make the world better. It’s easy to start with small things, like taking care of our environment or helping people in need." Julia was very happy. She thanked the cop for his advice and said goodbye. She went back home and created a beautiful world with her imagination.

Sara and Ben are playing in the sun. They have a big bucket of water and some cups. They pour water on each other and laugh. They are having fun. But then Sara feels something on her skin. It is hot and red. It hurts. She cries. Ben sees her and stops playing. He asks her what is wrong. "I have a boo-boo. The sun is bad. It made my skin hurt." Sara says. Ben feels sorry for her. He remembers what his mom told him. He runs to the house and gets a bottle of lotion. He comes back to Sara and says. "I have something for you. It is lotion. It is good. It makes your skin feel better. My mom gave it to me when I had a boo-boo from the sun. She said it helps." He opens the bottle and squeezes some lotion on his hand. He rubs it gently on Sara's skin. Sara feels the lotion. It is cool and soft. It does not hurt. She smiles. "Thank you, Ben. You are a good friend. You provide me with lotion. You make me feel better." Sara says. Ben smiles too. He is happy to help. He says. "You are welcome, Sara. You are a good friend too. I am sorry the sun was bad to you. Next time we play in the sun, we will put lotion on before. Then we will not have boo-boos. OK?" Sara nods. She agrees. They hug. They are friends. They are happy.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a fancy lion. He had a big mane and loved to roar very loud. All the animals in the forest would stop and listen when they heard him. One day, the fancy lion was very hungry. His stomach was growling like a big storm. He walked around the forest, looking for food to eat. He saw a little rabbit and thought, "That would be a good snack." But when the fancy lion roared, the little rabbit got scared and ran away. The lion was sad and his stomach still growled. Then, he saw a big pile of fruit under a tree. He ate all the fruit and his stomach felt better. The fancy lion learned that he could find food without scaring others with his loud roar.
