Category: Beginner
Title: Kitty the Nosy Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to question everyone she met. One day, she saw a big hook hanging from a tree. Kitty saw a bird on the tree and asked, "Bird, what is that hook for?" The bird replied, "I don't know, Kitty. But be careful, it looks dangerous." Kitty didn't listen and jumped to touch the hook. It got stuck on her tail. Kitty cried, "Help me, bird! I'm stuck!" The bird tried to help, but Kitty's tail was too tight on the hook. No one could help Kitty. She stayed stuck on the hook and was very sad. From that day on, she learned not to be so nosy and to listen to her friends.

Once upon a time, in a big park, there were many kids playing. One of the games they played was called "part the number". In this game, the kids would split into two groups. The game was very popular. One day, a new kid came to play. He wanted to join the game, so he asked the other kids if he could play too. They said yes, but they told him to be careful. They said that sometimes, when they part the number, something bad happens. The new kid did not listen. He thought they were just trying to scare him. So, they started to play the game. They began to part the number, and the new kid was having fun. But then, something bad happened. As they were parting the number, a big, mean dog came running into the park. The dog was very angry and started chasing the kids. The kids ran away as fast as they could, but the new kid was not fast enough. The mean dog caught up to the new kid and bit him. The other kids felt sad for the new kid, but they knew that they had warned him. From that day on, they never played "part the number" again.

Once upon a time, there was a long line of ants. They went on a big march. They wanted to find food. They were very hungry. They walked and walked, looking for something to eat. One day, they found a toy car. It was red and shiny. They thought it would be fun to drive. So, they all climbed in and started to drive the car. They drove fast and slow, having a lot of fun. As they drove, they found a big cake. The ants were so happy! They stopped driving and ate the cake. Their hungry bellies were full. They all went back home, feeling happy and full. The end.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing near their shelter. The sun was shining and the sky was clear. They were very happy. The cat found a big sack. She said to the dog, "Look at this sack! Let's play with it." The dog agreed and they started to play. They jumped on the sack and rolled it around. But then, they started to fight. The cat wanted to sit on the sack, and the dog wanted to push the sack. The cat said, "No, I want to sit!" The dog said, "No, I want to push!" They were very sad and stopped playing. They sat near the shelter and thought about how to fix their fight. The cat had an idea. She said, "Let's take turns! I will sit on the sack first, and then you can push it." The dog agreed, and they played happily with the sack. They learned that sharing and taking turns made their playtime more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a crazy mule named Max. Max lived on a big farm with other animals. He liked to run, jump, and play all day long. One sunny day, Max saw a big red ball in the field. He wanted to play with it, so he ran to grab it. But when he got close, the ball started to move! Max was very surprised. He thought, "Balls don't move by themselves!" Max kept trying to grab the ball, but it kept moving away. Then, a little girl came out from behind a tree. She was holding a long string. The string was tied to the ball! The little girl laughed and said, "Got you, Max!" Max realized the girl was playing a trick on him. They both laughed and played with the ball together. And from that day on, Max and the girl became best friends.
