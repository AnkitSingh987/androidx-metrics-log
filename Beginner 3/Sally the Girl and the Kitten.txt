Category: Beginner
Title: Sally the Girl and the Kitten

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. She had a band of friends who liked to play together. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. One day, Sally found a little lost kitten. It was very scared and hungry. Sally said, "Don't worry, little kitten. I will take care of you." She gave the kitten some food and a soft bed to sleep in. Sally's friends came over to see the kitten. They all loved it very much. One of her friends said, "Let's give the kitten a kiss to make it feel better." They all took turns giving the little kitten a gentle kiss on its head. The kitten felt loved and happy, and soon it was not scared anymore. Sally and her band of friends played with the kitten every day, and they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a mask. He thought it was fun to wear the mask and play. He showed it to his friend, Sue. Sue was upset because Tim scared her with the mask. Tim did not want Sue to be sad. He wanted to make her feel better. Tim said, "I promise I won't scare you with the mask again." Sue was still upset, but she wanted to be friends with Tim. She asked Tim if they could play a game together. Tim said yes, and they started to play. As they played, Sue started to feel better. She was not upset anymore. Tim kept his promise and did not use the mask to scare Sue again. They played all day, and they were both very happy. They knew they would always be good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a happy boy named Tim. Tim loved to read books. Every day, he would sit on a stand and read his favorite book. One day, Tim found a new book at the park. He was very happy. He sat on the stand and started to read the new book. The book was about a cat and a dog who were friends. The cat and the dog played together all day. They were very happy. Tim liked to read about the cat and the dog. He read the book until the sun went down. Then, he took the book home with him. At home, Tim read the book to his little sister. She liked the story too. They both smiled and were very happy. Tim knew that he would always love to read books.

Tommy was a chubby boy who liked to watch TV. He watched TV every day after school and ate many snacks. He did not like to play outside or share his toys with his sister. One day, his mom said, "Tommy, you have watched enough TV. Turn it off and go play outside. It is a sunny day and you need some fresh air." Tommy did not want to turn off the TV. He wanted to watch his favorite show. He said, "No, mom, I want to watch TV. Please, mom, let me watch TV." His mom said, "No, Tommy, you have to turn off the TV. You have watched too much TV. It is not good for you. Go play outside or read a book or draw a picture." Tommy got very angry. He did not want to do anything else. He wanted to watch TV. He started to scream. He screamed very loud. He screamed, "I want to watch TV! I want to watch TV! I want to watch TV!" His mom got very upset. She did not like Tommy's scream. She said, "Tommy, stop screaming. You are being very rude and naughty. If you do not stop screaming, you will go to your room and have no TV for a week." Tommy did not stop screaming. He screamed louder and louder. He screamed, "No, mom, no! I want to watch TV! I want to watch TV! I want to watch TV!" His mom had enough. She said, "That's it, Tommy. You are going to your room. You have no TV for a week. Maybe then you will learn to behave and listen." She took Tommy by the hand and dragged him to his room. Tommy cried and screamed all the way. He did not like his mom. He did not like his room. He did not like anything. He only liked to watch TV.
