Category: Beginner
Title: The Ant Was Walking and the Cake

One day, a little ant was walking in the park. The ant saw a big, fake cake. The ant was sad and started to complain. "Why is this cake not real? I want to eat it!" The ant cried. Just then, a big bird came and heard the ant. The bird said, "Don't complain, little ant. I can help you find a real cake to eat." So, the bird and the ant went to look for a real cake together. They looked and looked, but they couldn't find any cake. Then, something unexpected happened. The bird and the ant found a big, real cake in the park! They were so happy. The bird and the ant ate the cake together and became good friends. And the ant never complained again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a fancy dress that she loved to wear. One day, Amy went to the park to play with her friends. At the park, Amy saw a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy called a magnet. Amy said, "Hi Tim! Can I play with your magnet?" Tim said, "Yes, you can, Amy. Welcome to play with me!" Amy and Tim played with the magnet. They picked up little things like paper clips and coins. They had lots of fun. Then, it was time to go home. Amy said, "Bye Tim, see you tomorrow!" Tim said, "Bye Amy, see you!" And they both went home, happy after a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, in a deep forest, there lived a little girl named Mia. She had a pet bird named Lulu. Mia loved to feed Lulu every day. They played together and had lots of fun. One day, Mia got lost in the deep forest. She was scared and called out for Lulu. Lulu heard Mia's voice and flew to her. Lulu had strong lungs and could fly very high. Mia was so happy to see her friend. Lulu helped Mia find her way home. They walked through the deep forest, hand in wing. When they got home, Mia gave Lulu a big hug and fed her favorite seeds. They were safe and happy together again.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a cool dog playing with a ball. He thought the dog was very funny. Tim wanted to play with the dog too. So, he asked his mom, "Can I play with the dog?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Tim started to run with the dog. They ran and ran and had so much fun. But then, Tim tripped and fell. He hurt his leg and it was very painful. His mom saw him fall and quickly came to help him. She hugged him and said, "Don't worry, we will go to the hospital." At the hospital, the kind doctor looked at Tim's leg. He put a bandage on it and gave Tim a sticker. Tim felt much better. His mom said, "Now you can play again, but remember to be careful when you run." Tim smiled and promised he would be more careful next time.

Once upon a time, there was a new fountain in the park. It was very pretty. People liked to go there and watch the water go up and down. One sunny day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw the fountain and wanted to play near it. He ran to the fountain, but he did not see the wet ground. His foot started to slip, and he fell down. Tim's mom came to help him up. Tim's mom told him to be careful near the fountain. She said, "Watch out for the wet ground, so you don't slip again." Tim listened to his mom and played carefully. He had a fun day at the park with the new fountain.

One day, a charming cat named Kitty saw a new toy. It was a laser. Kitty did not recognize the laser. She looked at it and said, "What is this?" Kitty's friend, a dog named Buddy, came over. Buddy said, "That is a laser! It is fun to play with. I will show you." Buddy turned on the laser. A little red dot appeared on the floor. Kitty and Buddy chased the red dot. They had lots of fun. They did not know that the red dot was from the laser. They played all day long. At the end of the day, they were happy and tired.
