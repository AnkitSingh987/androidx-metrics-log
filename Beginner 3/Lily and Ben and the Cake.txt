Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Cake

Lily and Ben were playing in the kitchen. They liked to pretend they were chefs and make yummy food. Lily wore a pink apron and Ben wore a blue one. They had a big bowl, a spoon, some flour, some eggs and some milk. "Let's make a cake!" Lily said. "I love cake!" "Me too!" Ben said. "But we need some sugar and some chocolate. Where are they?" Lily looked around. She saw a jar of sugar and a bar of chocolate on the top shelf. They were too high for her and Ben to reach. "Maybe we can climb on the chair and get them," she said. "OK, let's try," Ben said. They dragged a chair to the shelf and Lily climbed on it. She stretched her chubby arms and tried to grab the jar and the bar. But they were still too far. "Ben, help me!" she said. Ben climbed on the chair too and pushed Lily up. Lily felt a little scared, but she wanted the cake. She reached again and touched the jar and the bar. But then she heard a loud noise. "ROAR!" It was their dog, Rex. He saw them on the chair and thought they were playing. He wanted to join them. He jumped on the chair and barked. Lily and Ben screamed. The chair wobbled and they fell down. The jar and the bar fell too and broke on the floor. The sugar and the chocolate spilled everywhere. Rex licked his lips and wagged his tail. He thought it was a treat. Lily and Ben cried. They were hurt and messy. They heard their mom coming. "What happened here?" she asked. She saw the mess and the dog and the kids. She was angry and worried. "Sorry, mom," Lily and Ben said. "We wanted to make a cake." Mom sighed. She hugged them and kissed their boo-boos. She took off their aprons and cleaned them up. She scolded Rex and put him outside. "Next time, ask me for help," she said. "And don't climb on the chair. It's dangerous." Lily and Ben nodded. They felt sorry and silly. "Can we still have cake?" they asked. Mom smiled. She loved them very much. "Maybe later," she said. "But first, let's clean up this mess."

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play outside. One day, it started to rain. Tim was sad because he could not play outside. He looked out the window and saw that his toy car was broken. He wanted to fix it. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, my toy car is broken. Can you help me fix it?" His mom said, "I am sorry, Tim. I cannot fix it right now. We will try later." Tim was sad but he waited for the rain to stop. The rain did not stop all day. Tim's mom could not fix his broken toy car. Tim was very sad. He went to bed that night with a broken toy car and no time to play outside. The day had a bad ending for Tim.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a new toy pony. She loved her pony very much. She played with her pony every day. One sunny day, Mia took her pony to the park. There, she met a boy named Tom. Tom had a toy dog. Mia and Tom played together with their toys. They had so much fun. Mia said to Tom, "Let's have our pony and dog marry!" Tom agreed and they had a toy wedding. The pony and dog were now friends forever. Mia and Tom were happy and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a metal ship. It was the bravest ship in the sea. It was always ready for a long rest. The captain of the ship was a brave sailor. One day, the captain decided to explore a new island. He decided that before he went, he needed to have a spicy meal. He asked the cook to make him something special. The cook came back with a spicy stew. The captain was so delighted that he slurped it up in one gulp! He felt the heat inside his belly. "Ah," he said with a chuckle, "this will be perfect for a long sail to the new island!" The captain and the ship's crew set off the next morning and sailed until they came to the island. It was full of spicy flavours, trees, and beaches. The ship was happy to have a long rest there. But the captain knew more adventures were ahead, and that the metal ship was ready to explore them.
