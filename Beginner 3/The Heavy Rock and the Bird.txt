Category: Beginner
Title: The Heavy Rock and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a heavy rock. Under the rock was a little root. The root wanted to grow big and strong, but the heavy rock was in the way. One day, a bird came and sat on the rock. The bird liked to tease the root. The bird said, "You can't grow big and strong with me on this rock." The root felt sad but did not give up. The root tried very hard to push the heavy rock. It took many days, but the root became strong. One day, the root pushed the heavy rock away. The bird saw this and stopped teasing the root. The root grew big and strong, and they all became friends.

Tom and Anna were friends. They liked to play together and share their toys. One day, they found a big box of pastry in the kitchen. They were very hungry and wanted to eat some. "Let's pretend we are getting married and have a pastry party," said Tom. "OK, that sounds fun," said Anna. "But we need some rings and a cake." They looked around and saw some shiny paper and a big muffin. They cut out some paper rings and put them on their fingers. Then they put the muffin on a plate and decorated it with some candy. "Now we are ready," said Tom. "Do you want to marry me, Anna?" "Yes, I do, Tom," said Anna. "You are my best friend." They hugged and kissed and said, "I do, I do, I do." Then they ate the muffin and some pastry. They were very happy and thought it was a great day. But they did not know that Mom and Dad were watching them from the door. They smiled and winked at each other. They had a secret plan. They wanted to surprise Tom and Anna with a real pastry party for their birthday. They had invited all their friends and family and bought a big cake and lots of pastry. They waited until Tom and Anna finished their pretend wedding and then said, "Surprise! Happy birthday, Tom and Anna! We have a real pastry party for you!" Tom and Anna were very surprised and excited. They ran to Mom and Dad and hugged them. Then they saw all the people and the cake and the pastry. They thanked Mom and Dad and said, "This is the best day ever!" They shared their cake and pastry with everyone and had a lot of fun. They still wore their paper rings and said, "We are married, we are married, we are married." Everyone laughed and clapped and wished them a happy birthday.

Lily and Ben were playing with their toys in the living room. Lily had a big doll that she loved very much. She took care of her doll every day. She brushed her hair, changed her clothes, and gave her hugs. Ben had a small car that he liked to zoom around. He made loud noises with his mouth as he drove his car on the floor. "Look at my doll, Ben. She is so pretty and expensive. She has a dress with flowers and a hat with a ribbon. Do you like her?" Lily asked. Ben looked at the doll and shrugged. "She is okay, I guess. But she is not as fun as my car. My car is fast and shiny. It can go anywhere. Do you want to play with it?" Ben offered. Lily shook her head. "No, thank you. I don't like cars. They are noisy and dirty. I only like dolls. They are nice and soft. You don't know how to play with dolls. You are a bad judge of toys." Lily said. Ben felt hurt by Lily's words. He did not want to be a bad judge of toys. He wanted to be a good friend. He thought for a moment and then said, "Lily, I'm sorry. I didn't mean to make you sad. I like your doll, too. She is pretty and expensive. But I also like my car. It is fast and shiny. We can play with both of them. We can share and care. What do you say?" Lily smiled and nodded. She realized that Ben was not a bad judge of toys. He was a good friend. She said, "Okay, Ben. I'm sorry, too. I like your car, too. It is fast and shiny. We can play with both of them. We can share and care. Let's do that." They hugged and then played with their toys together. They had fun and learned to respect each other's choices. They were happy and cared for each other.
