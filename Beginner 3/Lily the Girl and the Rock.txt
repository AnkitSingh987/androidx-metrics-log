Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Rock

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing in her room. She saw a pretty pattern on the floor. It looked like a big circle with many colors. Lily wanted to touch it, so she kneeled down on the floor. As Lily kneeled, she felt a hurt on her knee. She looked and saw a small rock. "Ouch!" she said. She picked up the rock and showed it to her mom. "Mom, this rock hurt my knee," Lily told her mom. Her mom looked at the rock and said, "I'm sorry, Lily. Let me help you." She took the rock away and put a soft bandage on Lily's knee. "All better now," her mom said with a smile. Lily smiled too and went back to play with the pretty pattern on the floor.

Once upon a time, there was a big building. In the building, there was a nice man. The nice man liked to provide toys for kids to play with. One day, a little girl came into the building to play with the toys. The little girl said, "Thank you for the toys! I want to play with them." The nice man smiled and said, "You're welcome. Be careful not to get hurt while you play." But the little girl did not listen. She played too fast and too hard. She fell down and got hurt. The nice man saw her and was sad. He tried to help her, but she was still hurt. The little girl went home feeling sad, and the nice man felt bad too.

Lily and Tom were playing hide and seek in the park. Lily was the seeker and Tom was the hider. Tom ran fast to find a good spot to hide. He saw a big tree with a hole in it. He thought it was a perfect place to hide. He crawled inside the hole and waited for Lily to find him. Lily counted to ten and then shouted, "Ready or not, here I come!" She looked around the park and tried to find Tom. She saw the swings, the slide, the sandbox, but no Tom. She wondered where he was. She walked towards the big tree and heard a noise. It sounded like a growl. Tom heard the noise too. He felt scared. He wished he had not hidden in the hole. He wanted to get out, but he could not. Something was blocking the hole. It was furry and had sharp teeth. It was a bear! The bear had smelled Tom and wanted to eat him. Tom screamed for help. He put his thumb in his mouth and cried. Lily heard Tom's scream. She ran to the tree and saw the bear. She was terrified. She wanted to help Tom, but she did not know how. She looked for a stick, a rock, anything to scare the bear away. But it was too late. The bear had dragged Tom out of the hole and was biting him. Lily saw blood and bones. She screamed too. She ran away from the tree and the bear and the park. She never played hide and seek again.

Sara and Tom are twins, but they are very unique. Sara likes to rock on her chair and sing songs. Tom likes to play with his cars and make noises. They have different toys and clothes, but they love each other very much. One day, Sara and Tom decide to share their toys and play together. Sara gives Tom her doll and Tom gives Sara his truck. They pretend that the doll and the truck are friends and go on adventures. They have a lot of fun and laugh a lot. But then, Sara sees that Tom is rocking on her chair with the doll. She feels angry and says, "Stop! That is my chair and my doll! You are not rocking right!" Tom feels sad and says, "But I like to rock and the doll likes it too! You are not playing right with my truck!" They start to argue and pull the toys from each other. They forget that they are twins and unique. Mom hears the noise and comes to see what is wrong. She sees Sara and Tom fighting and says, "What is going on here? Why are you not sharing and playing nicely?" Sara and Tom look at Mom and feel ashamed. They remember that they love each other and that they can play with different toys and still have fun. They say, "We are sorry, Mom. We were not being good twins. We are unique, but we can still share and play together." Mom smiles and says, "That's right, Sara and Tom. You are unique, but you are also twins. You can rock and play with any toy you want, as long as you respect each other and have a good time." Sara and Tom hug Mom and say, "We love you, Mom. And we love each other too." They go back to their toys and play happily. They rock and sing and make noises. They are unique, but they are also twins. They have a good time.
