Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Bunny and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Bob. Bob liked to play all day and sleep all night. One day, Bob felt helpless because he lost his favorite toy. He looked all around, but he could not find it. Bob was very sad. Then, Bob met a friendly bird named Sue. Sue saw that Bob was sad and asked, "Why are you sad, Bob?" Bob said, "I lost my toy and feel helpless. I cannot find it anywhere." Sue wanted to help Bob because she knew the toy had value to him. Sue and Bob looked for the toy together. They looked under rocks, in the grass, and up in the trees. Finally, they found the toy near a big tree. Bob was so happy! He thanked Sue for helping him. That night, Bob slept happily with his toy, knowing that he had a good friend in Sue.

Once upon a time, there was a big camp in the woods. Many kids came to play and have fun. One day, they all went to gather sticks and leaves. They wanted to build a big fort. Everyone was happy and excited. While they were gathering, one little girl felt anxious. She thought she saw something move behind a tree. She told her friends, but they did not see it. They kept gathering and building their fort. Suddenly, the thing behind the tree came out. It was a big, friendly dog! The kids were so surprised. They all laughed and played with the dog. The dog helped them gather more sticks and leaves. In the end, they built the best fort ever, and the dog stayed to play with them at the camp.

Once upon a time, there was a shy little dog named Max. Max had a pretty collar that he wore all the time. He loved to gaze at the big park near his home, but he was too scared to play with the other dogs. One day, Max saw a big dog with a shiny collar. The big dog said, "Hi, I am Sam. Do you want to play with me?" Max was scared, but he wanted to make a new friend. So, Max said, "Okay, let's play." As they played, Max's collar fell off. He was sad and scared. But Sam said, "Don't worry, Max. I will help you find your collar." They looked and looked, and then they found Max's collar under a big tree. Max was so happy! He thanked Sam for helping him. From that day on, Max was not shy anymore, and he and Sam played together all the time.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a pair of red pants that he loved very much. They were useful pants because they had big pockets to hold his toys. One day, Tim's mom warned him not to play in the mud with his red pants. But Tim did not listen. He went outside and jumped in the mud. His red pants got very dirty. Tim's mom saw his dirty pants and was sad. She told Tim to take off the pants and she washed them. When they were clean, Tim said sorry and promised to listen next time. From that day on, Tim always listened to his mom's warning and kept his red pants clean.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim had a rare, red spade. He loved to play in the sand with his spade. One day, he saw a little bird. The bird was sad because it lost its nest. Tim said, "Don't worry, little bird. I will protect you with my spade." He used the spade to dig a hole in the sand. He put the bird in the hole and covered it with leaves. Then, a big dog came. The dog wanted to play with the spade. But the dog stepped on the leaves and the bird flew away. Tim was sad, but the bird came back with its friends. They all played together with the rare, red spade.

One day, a red car and a blue car were on the road. The red car could drive very fast. The blue car was envious. The blue car wanted to drive fast too. They saw a big patch of mud on the road. The red car said, "I can drive fast through the mud!" The blue car said, "I want to try too!" So, the red car went first. It drove very fast through the mud. The blue car watched. Then, it was the blue car's turn. The blue car tried to drive fast, but it got stuck in the mud. The red car came back to help. They both pushed and pushed. Finally, the blue car was out of the mud. The red car and the blue car were happy. They were friends. They drove together on the road, and the blue car was not envious anymore.
