Category: Beginner
Title: The Plant and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little plant. It lived in a big pot in a sunny room. The little plant loved the sun and grew big and strong. One day, the plant saw a toy car. The car could move, and it looked easy and fun. The plant wanted to move, too. It tried to move, but it could not. The plant was sad. A little girl saw the sad plant. She wanted to help. She picked up the plant and put it on the toy car. Now the plant could move, too! The plant was happy, and the little girl was happy. They played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He liked to play outside in the sun. One day, Tom was playing with his ball near a drain. The ball rolled into the drain, and Tom was sad. Tom saw a big dog near the drain. The dog's name was Max. Tom and Max meet and become friends. Max wanted to help Tom get his ball back. Max was not afraid of the drain. Max put his head into the drain and took the ball out with his mouth. Tom was so happy and gave Max a big hug. Tom was not ashamed to ask for help, and he learned that friends can help each other. They played together all day, and Tom and Max were best friends forever.

One day, a boy and his mom went to a hotel. The boy was a little scared. He had never been to a hotel before. His mom said, "Don't be scared. We will have fun!" At the hotel, they saw a big dog. The boy was scared of the dog. The mom said, "Don't worry, the dog is nice." The dog came closer and the boy saw it was a toy dog. He was not scared anymore. They played with the toy dog and had a lot of fun. It was time to leave the hotel. The boy said, "Bye, hotel! Bye, toy dog!" They went home, happy and not scared anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a big, slow turtle named Tom. He liked to walk and talk with his friends in the forest. One day, he met a little bird named Billy. "Hi, Billy!" said Tom. "Can you help me with a task?" "What task?" asked Billy. "I need to find my way home," Tom said. "I am lost and it is getting late." Billy thought for a moment and then said, "I can help you, but you must give me a worm to eat." Tom agreed and they both went to find a worm. They found a big, juicy worm under a rock. Tom picked it up and gave it to Billy. "Yum!" said Billy. "Now I will help you find your way home." Billy flew high in the sky and looked around. He saw Tom's house and led him there. Tom was very happy and thanked Billy for his help. From that day on, they became best friends and always helped each other with their tasks.

Once upon a time there was a girl named Abby. One day, Abby wanted to go on an adventure, so she left her house and went looking for fun. She came across a crazy man. He said to her, "What's your name?" Abby was scared but answered, "My name is Abby." The man said, "Come with me and I will show you a good time." Abby was too scared to say no and followed him. The man brought Abby to an old, dark, scary place. He said, "We are here!" The man's face changed. His eyes were now crazy and it scared Abby. She wanted to run away, but it was too late. The man grabbed hold of Abby and laughed. The man said, "You're never going to escape!" Abby cried and screamed for help but nobody came. And so, Abby sadly had to stay with the crazy man, forever.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to sell his basketball. It was a big, round, and bouncy ball. He thought it would be fun to sell it to his friend, Sam. So, Tim went to Sam's house with his basketball. When Tim got to Sam's house, he showed Sam the basketball. He said, "I want to sell this ball to you." Sam looked at the basketball and thought it was a little bit stupid. But, Sam wanted to be a good friend to Tim, so he said, "Okay, I will buy your basketball." Tim was very happy that Sam bought his basketball. They played with the ball together for a long time. Even though Sam thought the basketball was a little bit stupid, he had a lot of fun playing with it. And, Tim was happy that he could sell his basketball to his friend. They both had a great day.
