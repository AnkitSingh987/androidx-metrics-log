Category: Beginner
Title: The Yacht and the Boat

Once upon a time, there was a big yacht. The yacht was in the water, far away. It was distant. The yacht wanted to go on a trip to a new place. A small boat saw the yacht and wanted to help. The small boat went to the yacht and said, "I can support you on your trip. We can go together." The yacht was happy and said, "Thank you, little boat. Let's go on a trip together!" So, the small boat and the big yacht went on their trip. They saw many things in the water. They had fun together. The small boat supported the big yacht, and they became good friends.

One day, a little bird wanted to fly high in the sky. The bird was very tired, but it tried hard to fly. It wanted to see the big world around it. The bird flapped its wings and went up, up, up. As the bird flew, it saw a small needle on the ground. The needle was sharp and could hurt the bird. The bird was scared and did not want to get hurt. It tried to fly away from the needle, but it was so tired. The bird asked for help from its friends. A big, strong bird came and picked up the needle. It took the needle far away, so it could not hurt the little bird. The little bird was happy and safe. It thanked its friend and went to sleep, feeling less tired.

One day, a cute little girl named Mia went to the park. She saw a small beetle on the ground. Mia gazed at the beetle and smiled. The beetle had pretty colors and tiny legs. Mia liked the beetle a lot. Mia said to the beetle, "Hi, little beetle! You are very cute. Do you want to be my friend?" The beetle did not talk, but it moved its tiny legs. Mia thought the beetle said yes. Mia and the beetle played in the park. They went on the slide and the swings. Mia was very happy with her new friend. The beetle was happy too. They had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, there was a little car who felt troubled. It was spring, and the car wanted to drive, but it did not know how. The little car felt sad. One day, the little car met a big car. The big car said, "Why are you sad, little car?" The little car replied, "I want to drive, but I do not know how." The big car smiled and said, "I can help you, little car." So, the big car and the little car drove together. They drove all through the spring. The little car was not troubled anymore. The little car was happy.

One day, a girl named Lily went for a walk in the park. She saw a big tree and under it, she found an unusual brush. The brush was very pretty and had many colors on it. Lily picked it up and said, "Wow! I like this brush!" Lily walked with the brush in her hand. She met her friend, Tom. Tom saw the brush and said, "That is a cool brush, Lily! Can I see it?" Lily gave the brush to Tom. When Tom touched the brush, it started to make a loud noise. Both Lily and Tom were scared. They did not know that the brush could scream. Tom dropped the brush, and it stopped screaming. Lily and Tom looked at each other and laughed. They knew they found a very special brush. They played with the screaming brush all day and had lots of fun. The unusual brush became their favorite toy.

Once upon a time, there was a three-year-old girl named Kate. One day, Kate was playing with her toys in the living room when her mom came in and said, “I have a surprise for you, Kate!” Kate was very excited and quickly jumped up to get it. “Can I open it now?” she asked. “No, sweetie. I need to remove it from the box before you can open it,” replied her mom. Kate watched her mom carefully as she removed the surprise. When the surprise was revealed, Kate was very disappointed. It was only a bad toy. “No, I don't like it!” she said. “Okay, let's take it back then,” said her mom. Kate was relieved, and soon they were on their way to find a better surprise.
