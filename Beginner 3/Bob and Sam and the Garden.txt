Category: Beginner
Title: Bob and Sam and the Garden

Once upon a time, there were two friends called Bob and Sam. Both of them were jolly and loved playing outside. One day, they were playing in their garden with a big gear they had found. They were pushing and pulling it around the garden. Suddenly, Bob wanted to move the gear faster. He asked Sam, "Can you help me move it?" Sam smiled and said, "Yes! I can help you." The two friends started pushing the gear together. Together, they could move it faster than ever and they laughed as they went. They kept on pushing and pulling the gear around and around the garden. Soon, they had made enough laps around the garden that the gear was going very fast. They were so jolly and had so much fun. At the end of the day, they were both so tired that they didn't want to move anymore. But, the next day, the friends were ready to play with their gear again.

Lily was a daughter who liked to play outside. She had a ball, a bike and a dog. She liked to throw the ball, ride the bike and run with the dog. But sometimes she played too close to the road. The road had many cars and trucks that went very fast. They could hurt Lily if she was not careful. One day, Lily's mom was worried. She saw Lily playing near the road with her ball. She called to her, "Lily, come here! The road is not safe. You have to stay away from it." But Lily did not listen. She wanted to play with her ball. She threw it high in the air and ran after it. The ball bounced and rolled to the road. Lily's mom was very worried. She ran to Lily and grabbed her hand. She pulled her back from the road. She said, "Lily, you have to listen to me. I love you and I want to prevent you from getting hurt. The road is dangerous. You have to play in the yard, not near the road. Do you understand?" Lily looked at her mom. She saw that her mom was scared and sad. She felt sorry for making her mom worry. She said, "I'm sorry, mom. I understand. I love you too. I will play in the yard, not near the road. Can I still play with my ball, my bike and my dog?" Lily's mom smiled and hugged her. She said, "Yes, you can still play with your ball, your bike and your dog. But you have to be careful and stay in the yard. And you have to listen to me when I call you. OK?" Lily nodded and said, "OK, mom. Thank you for preventing me from getting hurt. You are the best mom ever." She kissed her mom and ran to the yard. She played with her ball, her bike and her dog. She had fun and was safe. Lily's mom watched her and was happy. She was not worried anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a cool cat named Tom. Tom liked to play in the yard with his best friend, a little girl named Sue. They loved to play hide and seek and run around in the grass. One day, while they were playing, Tom lost his favorite toy mouse. He looked and looked but could not find it. Sue saw Tom was sad and decided to help him look for the toy mouse. Together, they searched high and low. At last, they found the toy mouse under a big tree. Tom was so happy and gave Sue a big hug. They played in the yard until the sun went down, laughing and having fun. And from that day on, they made sure not to lose any more toys.

The fox was walking through the forest. Suddenly, he saw a green light in the trees above. He stopped and looked. He knew he had to be careful. His mom had always warned him to never go near green lights. But this one was so bright and pretty. The fox wanted to get closer to the light. He took a few steps towards it. He quietly tiptoed forward. But then he heard a loud screech! A large owl was perched high in a branch above the light. Its big eyes were looking right at the fox. The owl screeched again and flapped its wings. The fox was scared and ran away. He remembered what his mom had said and thought it was wise to stay away from green lights. He was glad the owl had warned him.
