Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Mom

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the beach with her mom. She loved to play in the sand and look for shells. The sun was light and warm on her skin. She found a big shell and showed it to her mom. "Look, Mom! I found a big shell!" she said. Her mom smiled and said, "Wow, that's a beautiful shell, Sue!" As Sue played with her shell, she saw a tiny crab walk out of it. She was very surprised! "Mom, look! There is a crab in my shell!" Sue said. Her mom looked at the crab and said, "Oh my, what a surprise! The crab was hiding in the shell." Sue and her mom decided to put the shell back on the sand so the crab could go back to its home. They said goodbye to the crab and watched it walk back into the shell. Sue was happy that she found a new friend at the beach, and she loved her day with her mom.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Tim. Tim went to the museum with his mom. He saw big animals and pretty rocks. Tim was very excited. In the museum, Tim saw a weak old chair. He wanted to sit on it. But his mom said, "No, Tim. Don't sit on it." Tim did not listen. He sat on the chair and it broke. Tim was very sad. Tim said, "I'm sorry, mom." His mom hugged him and said, "I forgive you, Tim. We all make mistakes. Next time, listen to me." Tim promised to listen to his mom. They left the museum and went home.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk near his home. He saw a wild bird with pretty colors. The bird was sad and all alone. Tim wanted to help the bird, so he said, "Come join me at my home, little bird. Don't be sad." The wild bird looked at Tim and said, "Thank you, Tim. I would like to join you at your home." So, Tim and the bird walked back to his home together. They were both very happy. When they got to Tim's home, his mom saw the wild bird. She said, "What a pretty bird! You can both play together." Tim and the bird played all day long. They became best friends and the bird was not sad anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a nervous boy. He did not like to play outside. He was scared of the ground. Tim thought the ground would hurt him. One day, Tim went outside with his mom. They saw a big, red ball. Tim wanted to play with the ball but was scared. His mom said, "Do not be nervous. The ground will not hurt you." Tim kicked the ball and it went very far. He was happy. Then, something unexpected happened. The ground started to shake. Tim was scared, but his mom was not. The shaking stopped and a big, friendly dog came out. The dog had been digging a hole. Tim was not nervous anymore. He played with the dog and the big, red ball. Tim learned the ground was not scary after all.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Bobo. Bobo loved to eat carrots. One day, he found a big, orange carrot in the garden. It was a new, unknown carrot that he had never seen before. He was very happy! Bobo wanted to eat the carrot, but it was very sharp. He tried to bite it, but it hurt his teeth. He didn't know what to do. So, he asked his friend, the bird, for help. The bird had a good idea. The bird told Bobo to use a rock to make the carrot not sharp. Bobo did what the bird said. He hit the carrot with the rock until it was not sharp anymore. Then, he could eat the carrot. Bobo was happy, and he shared the carrot with his friend, the bird. They both enjoyed the yummy carrot together.

One day, a friendly fish named Bob lived in a big sea. Bob loved to dive deep down into the water to find fun things. He had many friends who liked to play with him. One day, while Bob was diving, he found a big box. It was a computer! Bob did not know what it was, but he thought it was fun to play with. He showed his friends, and they all liked it too. Together, Bob and his friends learned how to use the computer. They played games and had a lot of fun. The computer became their new favorite toy. And they all lived happily in the sea.
