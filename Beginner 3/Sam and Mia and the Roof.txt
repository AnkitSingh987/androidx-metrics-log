Category: Beginner
Title: Sam and Mia and the Roof

Sam and Mia were playing with their dad's tools. They wanted to repair their old dollhouse that was broken. They found a hammer and some nails in the garage. "Look, I can fix the roof with this nail!" Sam said, holding the hammer. "Be careful, Sam, you might hurt yourself!" Mia warned. But Sam did not listen. He hit the nail hard with the hammer. But he missed the nail and hit his finger instead. He cried out in pain and dropped the hammer. "Ow, ow, ow! It hurts so much!" Sam sobbed. Mia ran to get their mom. She saw what happened and was very angry. "What were you doing with dad's tools? You know you are not allowed to touch them!" she scolded. She took Sam to the bathroom and put some ice on his finger. She also checked the dollhouse and saw that it was more broken than before. "Now you have made it worse! You can't repair it with nails, you need glue and paint. And you have ruined dad's hammer too!" she said. She took away the tools and the dollhouse and put them in a closet. She told Sam and Mia that they were not allowed to play with them anymore. Sam and Mia felt very sorry and sad. They wished they had listened to Mia and left the tools alone. They learned that they should not play with things that are not theirs and that they should be careful with sharp and heavy things. They hoped that one day they could repair their dollhouse and make it look original again. But they knew it would take a long time and a lot of work.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk in the park. He saw a spark on the ground. The spark was very bright and pretty. He picked it up and said, "Thank you, spark, for making the park look nice." Tim took the spark home with him. He put it in a jar so he could look at it all the time. But the spark did not like being in the jar. It felt sad and the jar was a harsh home for the spark. One night, the spark jumped out of the jar and went back to the park. Tim woke up and saw that the spark was gone. He was sad, but knew the spark was happier in the park. So, Tim went to the park and said, "Thank you, spark, for showing me that you need to be free." And the spark shined even brighter, making the whole park glow.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a big, comfortable shoe in her room. She was very happy and wanted to show it to her friend, Tom. Lily ran to Tom's house and knocked on the door. "Tom, look at my big shoe!" she said. Tom looked at the shoe and clapped his hands. "Wow, that's a big shoe!" he said. Lily and Tom decided to play with the shoe. They took turns wearing the big shoe and pretending to be giants. They laughed and clapped their hands as they played. Suddenly, they heard a noise coming from the shoe. "What's that?" asked Tom. Lily didn't know, so they decided to look inside the shoe. When they looked inside the shoe, they found a little mouse. The mouse was wearing a tiny hat and had a small bag. "Hello!" said the mouse. "I'm Max, the traveling mouse. I live in this big shoe. Thank you for finding my home!" Lily and Tom were surprised but happy to meet Max. They clapped their hands and invited him to play with them. From that day on, Lily, Tom, and Max became the best of friends and had many more fun adventures together.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends called Tom and Sarah. Tom and Sarah were always playing together and planning fun things to do. One day, Tom said to Sarah, "Let's plan something special!" Sarah was so excited that she smiled. "What should we plan, Tom?" Tom thought for a while and then his eyes lit up. "Let's plan a surprise for your mom!" he said. Sarah said, "That's a big plan - what will we do?" Tom said, "Let's pick some big roses and make a bunch for her." So the two friends went outside and they picked some big, beautiful roses. They made a big bunch and took it back to Sarah's house. When Sarah's mom saw the big roses, she was so happy she cried. "These roses are so beautiful - thank you so much!" she said. Tom and Sarah smiled at each other. They were so glad that their special plan had worked.
