Category: Beginner
Title: The Girl Called Lucy and the Mommy

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Lucy. She was only 3 years old. Lucy was very curious and always asked questions. One day, Lucy asked her mommy, "Can I have a treat, please?" Her mommy said, "Yes, sweetheart. What would you like?" Lucy beamed with happiness. "I want candy!" Mommy smiled and said, "Let's go for a walk and hope we find some candy." So, Lucy and Mommy went for a walk. Soon, they came across a candy shop. "Oh, look!" exclaimed Lucy. "Can we get some candy here?" Mommy said, "Yes, sweetheart. Let us hope that we find a nice treat." Lucy was so excited that they went into the store. Sure enough, they found lots of different kinds of candy. Lucy couldn't believe her luck. She chose her favorite candy, gummy bears. Lucy was delighted. "Yay! Gummy bears! I love them," she said. Mommy smiled. "I'm so glad you found a treat that you enjoy, sweetheart." Lucy hugged her mommy and they went home. She couldn't wait to eat her gummy bears.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing with his toy printer. He loved to print pictures of animals and trees. As he was printing, he saw a big tornado outside his window. Tim was very upset and ran to his mom. "Mom, there is a tornado outside!" Tim said. His mom looked out the window and saw the tornado too. She hugged Tim and said, "Don't worry, we will be safe inside our home." The tornado went away after some time. Tim and his mom were happy it was gone. Tim went back to printing his pictures, and his mom gave him a big hug. They were glad the tornado was gone and they were safe.

Once upon a time, there was a big lake. Near the lake, there was a small house. In the house, there was a family. The family had a mom, a dad, a boy, and a girl. They liked to play near the lake. One day, the boy found a box. The box was locked. The boy and the girl wanted to open the box. They asked their mom and dad for help. Their mom and dad could not open the box. They tried very hard, but the lock was very strong. The family saw a big tree near the lake. The tree was dead. The dad thought the dead tree could help them open the box. They pushed the dead tree on the box. The lock broke. Inside the box, they found a lot of toys. The boy and the girl were very happy. They played with the toys near the lake all day.

Sara was sick and had to go to the hospital. She did not like the hospital. It was big and loud and smelled bad. She missed her mom and dad and her toys. A nurse came to her bed and smiled. She had a white hat and a blue dress. She said, "Hello, Sara. I'm here to help you feel better. Do you want to play a game with me?" Sara nodded. She liked games. The nurse gave Sara a fake stethoscope. It was a toy that looked like the one the nurse had. She said, "This is a stethoscope. It lets you hear your heart and your lungs. Do you want to try it?" Sara nodded again. She put the stethoscope in her ears and the nurse put the other end on her chest. Sara heard a thump-thump-thump. It was her heart. She smiled. She liked the sound. The nurse said, "Your heart is strong and healthy. That's good. Now let's listen to your lungs. Take a deep breath and blow it out." Sara did as the nurse said. She heard a whoosh-whoosh-whoosh. It was her lungs. She giggled. She liked the sound. The nurse said, "Your lungs are clear and happy. That's good too. You're doing great, Sara. You're a brave girl. Do you want to listen to my heart and lungs?" Sara nodded for the third time. She was curious. She moved the stethoscope to the nurse's chest and ears. She heard a thump-thump-thump and a whoosh-whoosh-whoosh. They sounded like hers, but a little different. She gazed at the nurse's face. The nurse smiled back. She said, "You have a good ear, Sara. You're a smart girl. Do you know what else you are?" Sara shook her head. She did not know. The nurse said, "You're a sweet girl. And I'm glad I met you. You made my day better. Thank you for playing with me." She gave Sara a hug and a sticker. Sara hugged her back and said, "Thank you, nurse. You made my day better too. You're a nice nurse. And a good friend."
