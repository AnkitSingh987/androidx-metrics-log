Category: Beginner
Title: Mama and the Park

"Mama, look!" shouted Molly. She had found something under the bed. Mama smiled. "What do you have there?" she asked. Molly held out a soft, brown furry toy. "I found it under my bed!" Mama's smile widened. "That looks like my old teddy bear," she said. Molly hugged the teddy really tight. "I finish now," she declared. Mama chuckled. "Yes, you sure do. Let's take him to the park and finish playing together there." Molly grinned and grabbed Mama's hand. "Yes! Let's go!" So, with the teddy in tow, Mama and Molly set off for the park. They finished playing together on the swings and the slides. Soon the sun began to set and it was time to go home. Molly hugged her teddy tight and snuggled in to Mama's arm. "Thank you for finding my teddy," she said. Mama smiled. "Of course. Let's finish by giving it one last cuddle." Molly squealed with delight as she hugged her teddy tight. That was the best finish ever!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to draw and make prints with his hands. One day, he made a very nice print and put it on the wall. A little bird saw the print and wanted it. The bird flew into the house and tried to steal the print. Tim saw the bird and was sad. He didn't want the bird to take his print. Just when Tim thought the bird would steal his print, the bird stopped. The bird was very tired. Tim saw the tired bird and had an idea. He gave the bird some food and water. The bird was happy and didn't want to steal the print anymore. Tim and the bird became friends and made more prints together.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a graceful deer named Will. Will loved to run and play with his friends. They would play hide and seek, and race each other to the big tree. One sunny day, Will and his friends were playing near the river. They saw a little bird stuck in a bush. The bird was scared and shouted, "Help, help! I can't get out!" Will and his friends wanted to help the little bird. "Let's help the bird," said Will. His friends nodded and they all worked together. They used their noses to gently push the branches away. The bird was free and very happy. "Thank you, Will and friends!" said the bird. They all smiled and played together for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, there was a silly little dog named Max. Max loved to play with his red ball. One day, Max saw a big truck. The truck was there to bring oil to Max's house. Max thought the truck was very big and loud. Max wanted to play with the truck, but he knew it was not a toy. Max had a stupid idea. He thought if he put oil on his red ball, it would be more fun to play with. So, Max took the oil and put it on his ball. When Max tried to play with his oily ball, it was very slippery. Max could not catch the ball because it would slip out of his mouth. Max learned that putting oil on his ball was a stupid idea. From that day on, Max played with his red ball without any oil.

One day, a big oak tree stood in the woods. It was a scary tree because it was very tall and had long arms. A little boy named Tim was playing near the tree. He liked to run and jump around the tree. As Tim was playing, a small cat came out from behind the oak tree. The cat was scared and started to rush away. Tim saw the cat and wanted to help it. He ran after the cat to make sure it was okay. Tim caught up with the cat and picked it up. The cat was not scared anymore. Then, something unexpected happened. The big, scary oak tree started to laugh! It was not a scary tree after all. It was a magic tree that could talk! The tree thanked Tim for being kind to the little cat. From that day on, Tim, the cat, and the magic oak tree became best friends.
