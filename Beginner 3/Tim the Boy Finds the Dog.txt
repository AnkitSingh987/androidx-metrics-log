Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Dog

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing in his room. He heard a weird noise outside. Tim was scared but he wanted to find out what it was. He went to the window and looked out. He saw a big dog and a little cat. The dog and the cat were yelling at each other. The dog said, "You are too small to play with me!" The cat said, "I am not too small! I can play too!" Tim wanted to help them be friends. Tim went outside and said, "Let's all play together!" The dog and the cat stopped yelling. They played with Tim all day. They had fun and became friends. The weird noise was gone, and Tim was happy.

One day, a boy named Tim went to his friend's house. His friend, Sam, had a secret. Sam told Tim, "I have a secret. I will test you to see if you can find it." Tim was excited and said, "I will try my best!" Tim looked everywhere in Sam's room. He looked under the bed, in the closet, and even behind the curtains. He could not find the secret. Then, he saw a big, comfortable chair. He thought maybe the secret was hiding there. Tim looked under the chair and found a box. He opened the box and saw a toy robot inside! Tim was so happy to find the secret. He showed the toy robot to Sam and said, "I found your secret! It is a toy robot!" But Sam laughed and said, "That is not the secret. The real secret is that the toy robot can talk!" Tim was surprised and they played with the talking robot all day.

One day, a zebra named Zara wanted to go play. She saw her friend, a bird named Bob. Zara asked, "Bob, do you want to go play with me?" Bob said, "Yes, let's go!" As they played, Zara got very dirty. She had mud all over her. Bob said, "Zara, you are dirty! Let's go to the water to get clean." Zara agreed, and they walked to the water. When they got to the water, they saw something big and green. It was a frog! The frog said, "I can help you get clean, Zara." The frog jumped into the water and splashed Zara with water. Zara was now clean and not dirty anymore. Zara, Bob, and the frog all played together and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a little clock. The clock lived in a big room with many toys. The clock loved to tell the time for all his friends. Every day, the clock would say, "Tick, tock, it's time to play!" One day, the clock saw a new toy in the room. It was a small blue car. The clock wanted to be friends with the car. The clock said, "Hi, I am clock. I tell time. Let's be friends!" The car smiled and said, "Sure, I am car. Let's play together!" They played all day and had lots of fun. The clock showed the car when it was time to eat and when it was time to take a nap. After a long day of playing, the clock started to feel tired. The clock said, "Car, I am tired. I need to rest." The car understood and said, "Okay, clock. You rest now. Tomorrow, we can play again." The clock was happy and went to sleep. The next day, the clock and the car played together again, and they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to watch cartoons on his TV. One day, his mom showed him a big book. She said, "This is an ancient book with many stories." Tim was curious. He asked, "Can we read a story from the book?" His mom smiled and said, "Of course!" They sat down on the soft seat together and opened the book. As they read the story, Tim imagined the characters as cartoons. They were funny and made him laugh. He loved the ancient stories and couldn't wait to read more with his mom.
