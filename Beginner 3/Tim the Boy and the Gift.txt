Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Gift

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom and dad. Tim loved to play in the sand and look for shells. He also liked to see the crabs that lived near the water. While playing, Tim saw a big, angry crab. The crab looked like it wanted to pinch Tim. Tim was scared, but he wanted to help the crab. He thought that maybe the crab was angry because it was lost. Tim called his mom and dad to come see the crab. They were surprised to see that the crab was not angry at all. It was just holding a shiny shell in its claws. The crab gave the shell to Tim as a gift. Tim was so happy, and the crab went back to its home in the water. From that day on, Tim and the crab were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim was only three years old. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. They wanted to find a big tree to sit under. Tim loved the park because it was fun to play in. As they walked, Tim saw a small bug. "Mom, look! A bug!" he said. His mom looked down and smiled. "Yes, Tim, that's a bug. Let's keep looking for the big tree." Just then, they found the big tree and sat down. Tim was happy. While they were sitting, something unexpected happened. A big bird came and took Tim's hat! Tim was surprised. "Mom, the bird took my hat!" he said. His mom laughed and said, "Don't worry, Tim, we will find your hat." They looked up and saw the bird put the hat in its nest. Tim and his mom went home and got a long stick. They came back to the park and got the hat from the nest. Tim was happy to have his hat back, and they played in the park all day.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went for a walk. They saw a big hill. Tim said, "Let's go up the hill, Spot!" Spot barked, and they started to climb. On the hill, they found a deep hole. Tim looked in and said, "Wow, that's deep!" He saw a toy truck next to the hole. Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's load the truck with dirt and fill the hole!" Tim and Spot worked hard. They loaded the truck with dirt and dumped it in the hole. They did this many times. The hole got smaller and smaller. Soon, the hole was gone! Tim and Spot were happy. They had filled the deep hole on the hill. They went back down the hill and went home. They were tired but proud of their work.

The sun was rising above the hills and the birds were singing in the trees. Susan was in the backyard doing her favourite thing: watching the crickets. She stretched out on the grass and waited quietly. Suddenly, she saw something hopping across the grass towards her. It was a tiny black cricket. She watched it closely, her eyes wide. The cricket looked at the little girl and it seemed scared. Susan was feeling compassionate. She carefully held out her hand and spoke calmly. "Don't be scared," she said. "I won't harm you." The cricket gazed into Susan's eyes and then crawled into her palm. Susan smiled and said, "We can be friends?" The cricket chirped happily and together, they watched the sun rise over the hills.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk in the park. He was very happy because he had a new toy car to play with. Tim played with his car near the trees and the flowers. But suddenly, he could not find his toy car. He started to feel terrible. Tim began to search for his toy car. He looked under the trees, behind the flowers, and even near the big rock. Tim could not find his toy car anywhere. He felt very sad and started to cry. A little girl named Sara saw Tim crying and asked, "Why are you crying?" Tim said, "I lost my toy car and I feel terrible." Sara decided to help Tim in his search. They looked everywhere and finally found the toy car near the pond. Tim was so happy and said, "Thank you, Sara!" They became good friends and played together in the park every day.
