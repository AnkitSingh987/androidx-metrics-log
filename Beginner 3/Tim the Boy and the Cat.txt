Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Cat

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a quiet cat sitting on a wall. Tim said, "Hi, cat! Do you want to play with me?" The cat just nodded its head. Tim and the cat played together. They played catch with a ball. But then the cat saw a big dog. The dog wanted to join their game. The cat did not like the dog. The cat hissed, "Go away, dog! You are too big and loud!" The dog was sad. He wanted to play too. Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's all march together like soldiers!" The cat and the dog agreed. They all marched up and down the street. They had fun and became friends. The cat and the dog learned to play together nicely.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a modest boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toys, especially his toy rods. He had many rods in different colors like red, blue, and green. One day, Tim wanted to see if his rods could fit into a small box. He tried to put the red rod in first. "Will it fit?" he asked his little sister, Sue. "I don't know, try it!" Sue replied. Tim pushed the red rod into the box, and it fit just right. Next, Tim tried the blue rod. It was a little bigger than the red one. He pushed and pushed, but it didn't fit. "Oh no, it's too big!" Tim said. Sue laughed and said, "Try the green one!" Tim picked up the green rod and gently placed it in the box. It fit perfectly, just like the red one. Tim and Sue smiled and clapped their hands. They were happy that the rods fit in the box and continued to play together all day.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little orange fox. The fox liked to play in the trees and the grass. One day, the fox saw a big march of ants. The ants were going to the fox's home. The fox did not want the ants in his home. He tried to stop the march. The fox jumped and ran but the ants did not stop. The ants seemed very strong. The fox had an idea. He found a big stick and put it in front of the ants. The ants climbed on the stick and went away. The fox was happy and his home was safe.

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing in her room. She was bored because all her toys were old and not fun anymore. Lily wanted to find something new to play with. She heard her mom talking about a pretty flame in the kitchen. Lily thought that maybe the flame could be a fun toy. Lily went to the kitchen to see the flame. She saw it on the stove and it was very pretty. Lily wanted to play with the flame, but she remembered her mom saying that it could be hot and not safe. Lily thought about it and decided not to touch the flame. Later, Lily's mom came into her room with a surprise. It was a new toy for Lily to play with! Lily was so happy that she forgot about the flame. She played with her new toy all day and was not bored anymore. She was glad she listened to her mom and stayed safe.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to play with her toys. She had a toy snake that was very flexible. Sue could bend the snake any way she wanted. One day, Sue found a label. The label had a picture of a snake on it. Sue thought it would be fun to put the label on her flexible snake toy. So, she stuck the label on the snake. Sue then took her snake with the label to show her mom. Her mom was lying on the couch. Sue said, "Look, Mom! My snake has a label!" Her mom smiled and said, "That's great, Sue!" They both laughed and played with the flexible snake together.
