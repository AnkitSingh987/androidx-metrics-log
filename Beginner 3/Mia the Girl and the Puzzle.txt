Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Puzzle

One day, a little girl named Mia went to play outside. She loved to skip and sing. Mia saw a hidden box under a big tree. "What is this?" she asked herself. She opened the box and found a puzzle inside. Mia was very happy and started to play with the puzzle. A little boy named Tom saw Mia playing with the puzzle. He walked up to her and asked, "Can I play too?" Mia smiled and said, "Yes, let's play together!" They both started to put the puzzle pieces together. As they put the last piece of the puzzle, something unexpected happened. The puzzle started to glow and it turned into a magic key! Mia and Tom were very surprised. They decided to look for a hidden door to use the key. And so, their new adventure began.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved balloons. One day, she got a big, red balloon from her mom. She was very happy and played with it all day. Mia took her balloon outside to play. But, the wind blew her balloon away. It went up, up, up in the sky. Mia was worried. She wanted her balloon back. She tried to reach for it, but it was too high. A tall boy saw Mia and her balloon. He wanted to help. He reached up and got the balloon for Mia. She was so happy! Mia said thank you to the boy. The boy was happy too. The moral of the story is: helping others can make you happy.

Judy was a good girl. She loved playing with her dolls and painting. But one day, her mom gave her a very special present - a mask! Judy looked at the mask and said, "Wow, it is so pretty!" She put it on and could feel the warmth of the sun on her face. Mom said, "Now look at the sun, Judy." Judy did as she was told. The mask began to melt and change colors. Judy was so excited. She said, "Mom, that is so cool!" Mom said, "The mask melted because of the sun. It's a good thing you were wearing it! Next time you go out in the sun, make sure you wear it." Judy said, "I will, Mom. Thank you for the mask. I love it!"

One day, a mole was digging in the ground. He liked to make tunnels and find food. He was a wise mole, and he knew how to stay away from danger. He had a soft fur coat and a pink nose. But as he was digging, he felt something squeeze his tail. It hurt a lot. He turned around and saw a big snake. The snake had sharp teeth and a long tongue. The snake wanted to eat the mole. The mole was scared, but he was also brave. He used his claws to scratch the snake's face. He used his teeth to bite the snake's tail. He used his voice to make a loud noise. He hoped that someone would help him. Soon, he heard another noise. It was a hawk. The hawk had big wings and sharp eyes. The hawk saw the snake and the mole. The hawk wanted to eat both of them. The snake saw the hawk too. The snake let go of the mole's tail. The snake tried to hide in the grass. But the hawk was faster. The hawk swooped down and grabbed the snake with its talons. The hawk flew away with the snake. The mole was free. He was happy and relieved. He thanked the hawk for saving him. The hawk nodded and said, "You're welcome. You are a wise and brave mole. I respect you." The hawk flew away. The mole went back to his tunnel. He was glad to be safe. He was proud of himself. He learned a lesson. He learned that sometimes, even a small mole can fight a big snake. And sometimes, even a big hawk can be a friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a regular boy who liked to play with his toys. One day, Tim found a magic rock in his yard. The rock was very special. It could blink like a little light. Tim took the rock with him everywhere he went. The rock blinked all the time. Tim showed the rock to his friends. They all liked the rock too. They played with the rock for a whole week. After the week was over, Tim put the rock back in the yard. He knew that the magic rock should be shared with other kids. Tim felt happy that he had a fun week with the blinking rock.
