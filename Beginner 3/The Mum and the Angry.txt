Title: The Mum and the Angry

Once upon a time there was a mum, who was very angry. The mum was angry because her 3 year old child had not listened to her. She had asked him to be quiet and he had not done as he was told. The mum said, "I asked you to be quiet! That's not ok!" The 3 year old replied, "I don't like it when you are angry, mummy". The mum knew her child understood her. She took a deep breath and said, "Let's pray. That will help us put things in order". The 3 year old prayed, "Dear God, please help us be quiet and put things in order". After they had prayed, the mum hugged her child and they both smiled. The mum said, "It's ok now, we can both talk quietly together". The 3 year old was relieved and happy that his mummy was not angry anymore. They went off together and enjoyed a fun time filled with laughter and smiles.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a cool pencil that he loved to draw with. One day, he went to the park with his mom and took his pencil with him. At the park, Tim saw a girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she did not have a pencil to draw with. Tim thought for a moment and then nodded his head. He decided to share his cool pencil with Sue. They drew pretty pictures together and became good friends. The next day, Tim could not find his cool pencil. He was sad. But then, Sue came to his house with a big surprise. She had found two cool pencils at the store and bought them for both of them. Tim was so happy! The moral of the story is that when you share with others, good things will happen to you.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a tough kid who liked to play outside. One day, he found a big maze near his house. The maze was made of tall green bushes. Tim wanted to go in and find his way out. As Tim walked through the maze, he saw many paths. Some paths were short, and some were long. He had to choose which way to go. Tim hoped he would pick the right path to get out of the maze. He didn't want to be lost all day. Tim walked and walked. He turned left and right many times. It was not easy, but he didn't give up. At last, he saw the way out! Tim was so happy. He had made it through the tough maze. Now, he could go home and tell his friends about his big adventure.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She wore her warm purple mittens. She liked her mittens very much. They were soft and kept her hands warm. On her walk, she saw a big tree. Lily wanted to climb the tree. She went up and up. Suddenly, one of her mittens fell off her hand. "Oh no!" she said. A squirrel saw the mitten fall. He picked it up and said, "Is this your mitten?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, thank you!" The squirrel gave her the mitten back, and Lily went home happy.

One day, Tim and his mom went to the park. Tim had his camera with him. He wanted to take pictures of the pretty trees and flowers. Mom said, "Taking pictures is easy, Tim. I can help you if you need it." While they were walking, Tim saw a big, red ball. A little girl was trying to get it out of a bush. She looked sad. Tim said, "I can help you get your ball!" He put down his camera and helped the girl get her ball. She smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim!" Just then, a funny squirrel picked up Tim's camera and ran away! Tim and the little girl chased the squirrel. They thought they would never see the camera again. But the squirrel stopped, took a picture of them, and then put the camera back on the ground. Tim and the girl laughed. They did not know that squirrels could take pictures too!
