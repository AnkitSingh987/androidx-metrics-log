Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Sam and the Dog

Tim and Sam are best friends. They like to play with rockets. They have many rockets of different colors and sizes. They pretend that the rockets can fly to the moon and the stars. One day, they decide to have a race with their rockets. They go to the park and find a big hill. They put their rockets on the top of the hill and count to three. Then they let go of their rockets and watch them roll down the hill. "Go, go, go!" Tim shouts. His rocket is red and fast. He hopes it will win the race. "Come on, come on, come on!" Sam cheers. His rocket is blue and shiny. He thinks it is the best rocket ever. The rockets zoom down the hill, faster and faster. They are very close to each other. Tim and Sam run after them, excited and happy. They want to see who will reach the bottom first. But then, something happens. A big dog runs across the path of the rockets. It barks and chases them. It thinks they are toys. It wants to catch them and play with them. "Oh no!" Tim and Sam cry. They are scared for their rockets. They love their rockets. They don't want the dog to hurt them. They reach the bottom of the hill and see the dog. It has caught Tim's rocket in its mouth. It is shaking it and biting it. Tim's rocket is broken and torn. "Hey, stop that!" Tim yells. He runs to the dog and tries to get his rocket back. He is angry and sad. Sam sees what is happening. He feels sorry for Tim. He knows how much Tim likes his rocket. He also sees his own rocket. It is safe and whole. It has won the race. But Sam does not care about the race. He cares about his friend. He is a loyal friend. He picks up his rocket and runs to the dog. He throws his rocket to the dog and says, "Here, take this one instead. Leave Tim's rocket alone." The dog hears Sam and sees his rocket. It drops Tim's rocket and runs to Sam's rocket. It grabs it and runs away, happy and proud. Tim and Sam look at each other. They hug and smile. They are not mad or sad anymore. They are glad and grateful. They have each other. They are best friends. They say, "Thank you for being my friend. You are the best." The end.

Once upon a time, there was a kind dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his friends in the park. One day, he found a tube on the ground. It was big and round. Spot thought it would be fun to play with the tube. Spot decided to act like a clown to make his friends laugh. He put his head in the tube and started to dance around. His friends laughed and clapped their hands. They thought Spot was very funny! Soon, all the animals in the park came to watch Spot's show. They all laughed and had a great time. Spot was happy that he could make everyone smile with his kind act and the tube. And from that day on, Spot and his friends always played with the tube in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a silly cat named Tom. Tom loved to write, but he had not written anything yet. One day, Tom decided it was time to write a story. He found a pen and some paper, and sat down to think about what to write. Tom's friend, a little bird named Sue, flew by and saw Tom sitting with his pen and paper. "What are you doing, Tom?" Sue asked. "I want to write a story, but I don't know what to write yet," Tom said with a sigh. Sue thought for a moment and said, "Why don't you write a story about us and our fun day at the park?" Tom liked Sue's idea, so he began to write. He wrote about how they played on the swings, slid down the slide, and had a yummy picnic. Tom and Sue laughed as they remembered their fun day together. When Tom finished writing, he read his story out loud to Sue. They both loved the story and decided to share it with all their friends. From that day on, Tom and Sue wrote many more stories together. They had lots of fun, and their friends loved to hear the silly stories they wrote. And Tom, the silly cat who loved to write, was happy that he finally wrote something.
