Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Fat Cat and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Bob. Bob lived in a small house with a girl named Sue. Sue loved Bob very much. They played together every day. One day, Bob saw a police car outside their house. The police officer came to the door and talked to Sue. "We need your help," he said. "Can Bob improve our town by catching mice?" Sue thought it was a good idea. Bob went with the police officer to catch mice. He worked very hard and improved the town. Everyone was happy. Sue was proud of her fat cat, Bob. They were the best of friends.

Once, there was a fox that liked to move around the forest. He always hopped along on his two back legs. Where ever he went there were vines swinging and blowing in the wind. One day he was feeling especially brave, so he decided to hop across one of the vines. For a while he felt secure and confident as he slowly moved across the Vine. But then he felt the vine start to move beneath his feet! He looked up to see a large eagle flying around in circles, getting closer and closer. The fox knew that he couldn't stay on the vine for much longer, so he quickly hopped on to the ground. The vine had been reliable for the fox up until that point, but it wasn't enough to help the fox that day. The fox was safe for now, but he never wanted to rely on a vine again.

One day, a smart cat and a dog were walking in the park. They saw a stand with many toys. The cat said, "I want a toy!" The dog said, "Me too!" The cat and the dog went to the stand. But the man at the stand frowned. He said, "You have no money. You can't have a toy." The cat and the dog were sad. They walked away from the stand. The cat said, "I want a toy so bad!" The dog said, "Me too!" But they had no money, so they could not get a toy. They went home sad.

Ben likes to help his dad fix things. His dad is a plumber. He works with pipes and water. Sometimes the pipes are made of copper. Copper is a shiny brown metal. One day, Ben and his dad go to a house where the water is not working. The lady who lives there is sad. She says there is a leak in the copper pipe under the sink. Ben and his dad look at the pipe. It has a hole in it. Water is dripping out. Ben's dad says they can fix it. He takes out his tools. He has a wrench, a cutter, a solder and a torch. He tells Ben what he is going to do. He says it is not easy, but he knows how. He asks Ben to hold the torch for him. Ben feels proud. He holds the torch very carefully. He watches his dad cut the old pipe and put a new piece in. He sees his dad solder the joints with the torch. He hears his dad say "good job, Ben". He smiles. The lady who lives there is happy. She turns on the faucet and sees the water running. She thanks Ben and his dad. She gives them cookies and milk. Ben and his dad say goodbye. They go back to their truck. Ben says he likes to fix things with his dad. His dad says he likes to fix things with Ben. They hug.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Mia. Mia liked to play dress-up. One day, she found a costume. It was a big, pretty costume. Mia wanted to wear it. Mia put on the costume and went outside. She saw a boy named Tom. Tom was very calm. Mia said, "Hi Tom! Do you like my costume?" Tom said, "Yes, Mia! Your costume is nice." Mia and Tom played together. They saw a sign. The sign said, "Party here!" Mia and Tom went to the party. They had fun in their costumes. They were happy and played all day.
