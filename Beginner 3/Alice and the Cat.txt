Category: Beginner
Title: Alice and the Cat

Alice had a small pet. Her pet's name was Cat. Cat liked to sit in the sun and purr. One day Alice wanted to study for school. Alice asked Cat,"Will you sit quietly while I study?" Cat shook her head and said, "No. I want to play!" Cat jumped up and down with excitement. Alice said, "But I have to study. It is important." Cat was not listening. She kept jumping and shaking her head. Alice tried to be firm. She pointed to the corner and said, "Cat, sit here and be quiet now." Cat slowly sat down and looked away. She was sad because Alice was not playing with her. Alice smiled and said, "I promise I will play with you later. Now I have to study." Cat sighed and watched Alice as she opened her books and started to study.

One day, a modern cat and a dog were playing in the park. The cat had a big sack. The dog looked at the sack and asked, "What is in your sack, cat?" The cat said, "I have my toys in the sack. Do you want to play with them?" The dog was very happy and said, "Yes, I want to play with your toys." They played with the toys all day. The sun went down, and it was time to go home. The dog said, "Cat, I had a lot of fun today. In my opinion, you are a very good friend." The cat smiled and said, "Thank you, dog. I think you are a good friend too." They put the toys back in the sack and went to their homes. The cat and the dog were very happy because they had a fun day and made a new friend.

One day, a youth named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big tree with tasty fruit. Tim wanted to eat the fruit, but it was too high to reach. His mom said, "We will come back tomorrow with a long stick to get the fruit." The next day, Tim and his mom came back to the park with a long stick. They used the stick to get the fruit from the tree. Tim was very happy. He said, "Thank you, mom! I can't wait to eat the tasty fruit." Tim and his mom went home and washed the fruit. They squeezed the fruit to get juice. They drank the juice and it was very tasty. Tim was so happy that he and his mom got the fruit from the tree. They laughed and enjoyed the tasty juice together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a colorful toy box. She loved her toy box very much. One day, she found a glass jar inside the toy box. The glass jar was new and shiny. Mia was very happy. Mia wanted to reveal what was inside the glass jar. She tried to open it, but it was too tight. Mia asked her big brother, Tom, for help. Tom was happy to help his sister. He opened the glass jar for her. Inside the glass jar, there were many colorful stones. Mia and Tom played with the stones. They made nice pictures with them. Then, they put the stones back in the jar. They learned that sharing and helping each other made them happy. And that was the moral of the story: share and help others to be happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Bob. Bob loved to hop and play all day. One day, he hopped to the park to meet his friend, Tina the turtle. When Bob met Tina, he saw that she was upset. "What's wrong, Tina?" asked Bob. Tina said, "I lost my juice. I am sad." Bob wanted to help his friend, so they began to look for the juice together. They hopped and crawled around the park, looking near the trees and under the slides. Finally, they found the juice by the swings. Tina was so happy! "Thank you, Bob!" she said. They both drank the juice and played together until the sun went down.

One day, a red caterpillar was walking in the garden. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. The red caterpillar was very slow, but he did not give up. He smiled and said, "I can do it!" A little boy saw the red caterpillar trying to climb the tree. The boy frowned and said, "You are too small and slow to climb the tree." The red caterpillar felt sad and stopped trying to climb the tree. The next day, a strong wind blew the tree down. The red caterpillar was happy he did not climb the tree, but he was still sad that he did not try. The moral of the story is, "Don't let others stop you from trying new things."
