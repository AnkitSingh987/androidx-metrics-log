Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Ball

Lily was very tired. She had played a lot with her toys and her dog. She wanted to take a nap, but she was not sleepy yet. She yawned and stretched her arms and legs. She looked around the room and saw a big chair. The chair was soft and cozy. It had a pillow and a blanket on it. Lily thought the chair was perfect for napping. She walked to the chair and climbed on it. She snuggled with the pillow and pulled the blanket over her. She closed her eyes and tried to sleep. But she heard a noise. It was her dog, Spot. Spot was barking and wagging his tail. He wanted to play with Lily. He jumped on the chair and licked Lily's face. "Spot, no!" Lily said. "I want to sleep. Go away!" But Spot did not go away. He barked louder and nudged Lily with his nose. He wanted Lily to throw his ball for him. He dropped the ball on the chair and looked at Lily with his big eyes. "Please, Lily, please!" Spot said. "Play with me! I'm bored!" Lily opened her eyes and saw Spot's ball. She felt sorry for Spot. He was her best friend. She decided to play with him for a little while. She picked up the ball and threw it across the room. Spot ran after it as fast as he could. He caught the ball and brought it back to Lily. He dropped it on the chair again and waited for Lily to throw it. Lily and Spot played like this for a long time. They had fun and laughed. Lily forgot that she was tired. She was happy with Spot. She threw the ball one more time, but this time she threw it very slow. Spot ran after it very slow too. He caught the ball and came back to the chair very slow. He jumped on the chair and lay down next to Lily. He gave her a lick and a wag. "Thank you, Lily, thank you!" Spot said. "You are the best!" Lily hugged Spot and smiled. She was glad that she played with him. She felt sleepy now. She put the ball on the floor and cuddled with Spot. She closed her eyes and fell asleep. Spot fell asleep too. They both dreamed of playing and napping on the big chair. They were happy.

Mummy wanted her daughter to eat her lunch, but she kept insisting on playing. Mummy said, "please eat your lunch" but the little girl still wanted to play. Lastly, Mummy had to insist she should stay at the table and eat her lunch. The little girl was unhappy, but she sat down and started to eat. Suddenly, she heard a loud noise. It was a cricket running around on the floor. She was so excited and wanted to catch it. Mummy said that if she finished eating, she could go and catch the cricket. The little girl gobbled her food and ran for the cricket. But it was too fast. The little girl got so angry that she shouted and knocked over her lunch. Mummy was very angry and told her to clean the mess up. The little girl was so ashamed that she started to cry and didn't want to clean the mess. But Mummy kept insisting, until the little girl had to do as she was told. The little girl was still very sad and she never got to catch the cricket.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park with her mom. Mia saw a big bush with little round things on it. They were blueberries! Mia's mom said, "We can pick some and take them home." Mia was so happy. She knew something brilliant was going to happen. Mia and her mom picked lots of blueberries. They put them in a big bowl. Mia's mom said, "We will make something yummy with these." Mia thought about what they could make. She remembered her grandma made something with blueberries once. Mia felt a hug in her heart thinking about her grandma. The next day, Mia's mom made a surprise for her. It was a blueberry pie! Mia was so excited. She knew the brilliant thing would be yummy. Mia took a bite and it was the best pie ever. She gave her mom a big hug and said, "Thank you, Mommy! This is just like Grandma's pie!"
