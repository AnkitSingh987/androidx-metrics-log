Category: Beginner
Title: The Hero and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a hero. The hero was very strong and loved to help people. One day, it was very hot outside. The hero saw a big pool. The pool had no water. The hero wanted to help fill the pool. The hero found a big hose. He put the hose in the pool and turned it on. The water started to fill the pool. People came to watch the hero. They were happy and clapped their hands. The hero smiled and waved at them. But then, something unexpected happened. A big fish jumped out of the hose and into the pool. Everyone was surprised! The hero laughed and said, "Now the pool has water and a new friend!" The people laughed too. They all jumped in the pool and had a fun day with the hero and the big fish.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy cat named Tom. Tom liked to hurry and run all around the house. One day, he saw a toy gun on the floor. Tom went to his friend, a bird named Polly. "Look, Polly! A toy gun!" said Tom. Polly looked at the toy gun and said, "Let's play! But we must hurry, it's almost time for dinner." Tom and Polly played with the toy gun. They took turns, and both had fun. They laughed and played until it was time for dinner. Then, they hurried to eat their food.

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with her friends. One day, Kitty found a ticket on the ground. The ticket was for a fun party. Kitty wanted to go to the party with her friends. But Kitty's friends did not have tickets. So, Kitty had to separate from her friends to go to the party alone. At the party, Kitty did not have fun. She missed her friends and felt sad. Kitty wished she had stayed with her friends instead of going to the party alone. The end.

Tom and Lily were playing in the yard. They liked to dig and make mud pies. They saw a big saw near the shed. It was shiny and had many teeth. "Look, a saw!" Tom said. "Let's play with it!" "No, Tom, that's scary!" Lily said. "Dad said we can't touch it. It can hurt us!" Tom did not listen. He picked up the saw and ran to the tree. He wanted to cut the tree like Dad. He rubbed the saw on the tree. It made a loud noise. "Stop, Tom, stop!" Lily shouted. "You are making the tree sad!" But Tom did not stop. He rubbed the saw harder and harder. He did not see the bee nest on the tree. The bees were angry. They came out of the nest and buzzed around Tom. "Ow, ow, ow!" Tom cried. "The bees are stinging me!" He dropped the saw and ran to Lily. Lily hugged him and helped him go inside. She got some ice and put it on his stings. "I'm sorry, Lily," Tom said. "You were right. The saw was scary. I should have listened to you and Dad." "It's okay, Tom," Lily said. "I'm glad you are not hurt too much. Next time, let's play with something else. Something fun and safe."

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a walk. She saw a big mountain far away. She wanted to go to the top of the mountain. So, she started to walk and walk. As she walked, she met a small, delicate bird. The bird said, "Hi Sue, can I go with you?" Sue said, "Yes, let's go to the mountain together!" So, they walked and walked together. When they got to the top of the mountain, they saw a big box. They opened the box and found many pretty rocks. Sue said, "Let's add these rocks to our bags and take them home!" The bird said, "Yes, let's do that!" But when they added the rocks to their bags, the rocks turned into candy! Sue and the bird were very happy and ate the candy together.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to build a tower. He was very happy when he started. He picked up blocks and put them on top of each other. The tower got taller and taller. Tim's friend, Sue, came over and saw the tower. She said, "Wow, Tim! You made a big tower! Can I help you?" Tim was happy to have help, so he said, "Yes, Sue! Let's make it even taller!" They both moved more blocks and put them on the tower. But then, Tim and Sue started to move too fast. The tower fell down with a big crash! They were sad, but they learned that they should not rush when they build. They started again, moving slowly and carefully. They built a new tower together, and they were very happy. Moral: Slow and steady wins the race.
