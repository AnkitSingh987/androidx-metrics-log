Title: Amy the Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Amy who was very generous. Everywhere she went she shared her toys and brought a smile to others. One day, Amy went to the shop. She saw a beautiful shelf and decided she needed it, so she asked her mom if she could buy it. But her mom replied, "That shelf looks very nice, but we don't need it. We already have a shelf of our own." Amy thought about it for a moment and then said excitedly, "I know! I can give the shopkeeper our old shelf and trade it in for the new one!" Mom agreed that this was a generous thing to do. So they brought their old shelf to the shop, and in return the shopkeeper gave Amy the new shelf she was dreaming of! Amy learned that day that being generous is not only a nice thing to do, but it can also bring unexpected rewards.

One day, a little bird was scared to fly. It saw all its friends up in the sky, but it just could not do it. It sat on a branch and watched everyone else have fun. Then, a big bird came by and said, "Why are you not flying with us? It's so much fun!" The little bird said, "I am scared. What if I fall?" The big bird smiled and said, "Don't worry, I will help you. Just flap your wings and I will be right by your side." So the little bird tried to fly. It flapped its wings and, to its surprise, it was flying! The big bird stayed close, just like it promised. They flew together, high up in the sky. The little bird was so happy. As they flew, they saw a dinner on a table in a house. The big bird said, "Let's go eat that dinner!" But, the little bird said, "No, that is not our dinner. We should find our own food." Just then, a big wind came and blew the big bird far away. The little bird was scared, but it kept flying. It found a worm for dinner and ate it. The little bird was proud that it could fly and find its own dinner.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big slide and wanted to play on it. Tim ran to the slide, and his dog, Max, followed him. Max had soft, brown fur and liked to play with Tim. As Tim got ready to slide, he saw a cat at the top. The cat was scared and did not know how to get down. Tim wanted to help the cat, so he pointed at Max and told him to stay. Max sat down and watched as Tim climbed up the slide. When Tim got to the top, the cat jumped onto his head! Tim was surprised, but he held onto the cat and slid down the slide with it. Max barked happily at the bottom. The cat was not scared anymore, and it became friends with Tim and Max. They all played together in the park until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a silly cat named Tom. He loved to play and jump all day. Tom had a big dream. He wanted to be the best jumper in the world. One sunny day, Tom saw a big tree. He wanted to jump to the top. He took a deep breath and jumped very high. But he did not reach the top. Instead, he got stuck in the tree. Tom was scared. He started to scream for help. His friend, a little bird, heard him and came to help. The bird told Tom not to worry. Together, they found a way to get down. Tom was happy to be safe and learned that it's okay to ask for help.

Once upon a time, there was a gray bird. The bird liked to fly high in the sky. One day, the bird saw a big tree. The tree had many leaves, but it looked sad. The bird asked the tree, "Why are you sad?" The tree said, "I want to become a big, strong tree, but I am too small and gray." The bird wanted to help the tree, so it flew away to find help. The bird found a magic seed. The bird took the seed to the tree. The tree ate the seed. Something unexpected happened! The tree became big and strong, but it also turned into ash. The bird was sad, but it knew the tree was happy now.
