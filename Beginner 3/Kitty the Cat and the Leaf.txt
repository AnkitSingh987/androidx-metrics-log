Category: Beginner
Title: Kitty the Cat and the Leaf

One day, a small cat named Kitty was very fearful. She did not like her style. She wanted to look like her friend, Doggy. Doggy had big ears and a long tail. Kitty had small ears and a short tail. Kitty said, "I want to look like you, Doggy!" Doggy said, "Don't be fearful, Kitty. You have your own style. You are nice just the way you are!" But Kitty was still sad. So, they went to take a nap under the big tree. They liked to nap there because it was cool and quiet. While they were napping, a bird came and put a leaf on Kitty's head. It had a funny shape. When Kitty and Doggy woke up, they saw the leaf. They both laughed. Kitty said, "Look, Doggy! I have a new style!" Doggy said, "Yes, you do! And it looks fun!" Kitty was not fearful anymore. She was happy with her new style.

Once there was a fish who lived in a deep blue ocean. He loved swimming around with his friends. One day he had a great idea to catch his dinner with a net. He swam to a temple and asked the fish there if he could borrow a net. They refused because it was not theirs to give. The fish was sad but he had an even better idea! He found a big leaf at the bottom of the ocean and decided to use it to catch his dinner. He waved the leaf around and the fish jumped right into it. He was so happy! With his dinner safely in the leaf, the fish made his happy journey home.

Anna liked to sew with her mom. She had a small needle and some thread. Mom had a big needle and a lot of thread. They made pretty things with cloth and buttons. One day, Anna saw a bright red button on the table. She wanted to sew it on her doll's dress. She took the small needle and some thread. She did not ask mom. She thought she knew how to sew. She put the thread through the hole in the needle. She tried to tie a knot, but it was hard. She pulled the thread too much. The needle slipped from her fingers. It fell on the floor. Anna did not see the needle. She stepped on it. It was very sharp. It hurt her foot. She cried out loud. Mom heard her and came running. She saw the needle and the blood. She picked up Anna and hugged her. She said, "Oh, Anna, you should be careful with needles. They are sharp and can hurt you. Next time, ask me before you sew. I will help you and teach you." Mom took Anna to the bathroom. She cleaned her wound and put a band-aid on it. She gave her a kiss and a cookie. Anna said she was sorry. She said she loved mom. Mom said she loved Anna too. She said, "Let's go back to the table and finish our sewing. I have a bright red button for your doll's dress. We can sew it together." Anna smiled and nodded. She held mom's hand and walked to the table. She learned her lesson. She was careful with needles. She had fun sewing with mom.

Once upon a time, there was a warm little house. In the house lived a boy named Tim and his mom. They liked their house because it was cozy and nice. One day, Tim found a sharp thing with a handle in the kitchen. He asked his mom, "What is this?" She said, "It is a knife. Be careful, Tim. It is sharp and can hurt you. Always ask me for help when you need to use it." Tim listened to his mom and put the sharp thing with a handle back. From that day on, he always asked for help when he needed to use the knife. Tim and his mom lived happily in their warm little house.

Once upon a time, there was a new panda named Pandy. Pandy was very cute and lived in the big forest. One day, Pandy wanted to find a friend to play with. As Pandy walked, he stumbled on a big rock. "Ouch!" he said. Then, he saw a little bird sitting on a tree branch. "Hi, bird! Do you want to play with me?" Pandy asked. The bird nodded and they started to play together. While they were playing, they heard a loud noise. It was a big, scary bear! The bear wanted to play too, but Pandy and the bird were scared. Then, something unexpected happened. The bear started to dance and sing! Pandy and the bird laughed and joined the bear. They all became good friends and played together every day.
