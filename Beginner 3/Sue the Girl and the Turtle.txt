Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Turtle

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to go for walks in the park. One sunny day, she went to the park with her mom. In the park, Sue saw a new thing. It was a big, green, and patient turtle. The turtle was sitting on a rock near the pond. Sue went to meet the turtle. She said "Hi" to the turtle and the turtle said "Hi" back. Sue and the turtle became friends. They played and had fun together at the park. Sue was very happy to meet the patient turtle. They had a great day, and Sue couldn't wait to come back to the park to play with her new friend again.

Sara likes to draw. She draws with crayons and paper. She draws flowers and stars and hearts. She draws a letter for her mom. The letter says "I love you, Mom". Sara thinks the letter is beautiful. Sara wants to give the letter to her mom. She runs to the kitchen. She sees her mom cooking. She says "Mom, look, I made a letter for you". She holds the letter in her hand. But mom is busy. She is making soup. She has a big pot on the stove. She says "Not now, Sara, I'm busy. Go play somewhere else". She does not look at the letter. She does not mind Sara. Sara feels sad. She thinks mom does not love her letter. She thinks mom does not love her. She drops the letter on the floor. She runs to her room. She cries. The letter is on the floor. The soup is boiling. The pot is too full. The soup spills over. The soup is hot. The soup burns the letter. The letter is gone. The letter is not beautiful anymore.

Tom and Lily were playing in the yard. They liked to dig holes and find bugs and rocks. They had a lot of fun with their stuff. They had shovels, buckets, cars, dolls and balls. But one day, they saw a big pile of smelly stuff near the fence. It was brown and wet and had flies on it. It was poop from a dog. Tom and Lily did not like the smelly stuff. They wanted to play far away from it. But then, they saw a cat coming from the other side of the fence. The cat was black and white and had a bell on its collar. The cat saw the smelly stuff and wanted to play with it. The cat jumped over the fence and ran towards the smelly stuff. Tom and Lily did not want the cat to play with the smelly stuff. They wanted to prevent the cat from getting dirty and sick. They ran after the cat and shouted, "No, cat, no! That is not good stuff! That is bad stuff! Go away, cat, go away!" The cat heard Tom and Lily and stopped. The cat looked at them and then at the smelly stuff. The cat did not understand why they were so angry. The cat thought the smelly stuff was fun and tasty. The cat did not care if it was dirty and sick. But then, the cat heard another sound. It was a loud bark from a big dog. The dog was the owner of the smelly stuff. The dog was angry that the cat was near its smelly stuff. The dog wanted to chase the cat and bite it. The cat saw the dog and got scared. The cat did not want to play with the dog. The cat wanted to run away. The cat turned around and ran back to the fence. The cat jumped over the fence and ran away. Tom and Lily saw the cat and the dog. They were glad that the cat was safe. They were also glad that the dog was gone. They did not like the dog or the smelly stuff. They went back to their holes and bugs and rocks. They had a lot of fun with their stuff. They forgot about the smelly stuff.

Once upon a time, there was a gentle bunny named Ben. Ben loved to hop and play with his friends in the forest. One day, Ben found a big pile of salt near a tree. He was very excited and called his friend, a bird named Tim. "Tim, come and see what I found!" said Ben. Tim flew down and saw the salt. "Wow, that's a lot of salt!" said Tim. They both decided to share the salt with their friends in the forest. Before they shared the salt, Ben made a promise to Tim. "Tim, I promise to always share with you and our friends," said Ben. Tim was very happy and promised the same thing to Ben. Together, they shared the salt with all their friends in the forest. Everyone was happy and grateful. Ben and Tim kept their promise to each other and became the best of friends. They played and shared many things together, and they all lived happily ever after.
