Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Graceful Cat and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a graceful cat named Lily. Lily loved to cook. She cooked yummy food for her friends. One day, she wanted to make a new dish. She asked her friend, Doggy. Lily said, "Doggy, what should I cook today?" Doggy thought for a moment and said, "Let's cook a big cake!" Lily and Doggy started to cook the cake. They mixed and stirred. They baked the cake in the oven. When the cake was ready, it smelled like perfume. They were so happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind blew through the window. It blew the cake away! Lily and Doggy were sad. They looked at each other and said, "Let's cook another cake!" So, Lily and Doggy cooked another cake. This time, they closed the window. They enjoyed the yummy cake together. They were very happy.

Once upon a time, in a small valley, there were two friends. They were Tom and Sam. They liked to play games. They were very competitive. They always wanted to win. One day, they played a game. The game was to include picking up rocks. They needed to pick up many rocks. Tom and Sam wanted to see who could pick up the most rocks. Tom picked up five rocks. Sam picked up six rocks. Sam was very happy. He won the game. Tom was a little sad, but he knew they would play again. They were still good friends in the small valley.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a big house with a pink room upstairs. Mia loved her pink room very much. One day, she heard a noise coming from upstairs. Mia felt scared but wanted to know what the noise was. Mia went upstairs to her pink room. She saw a little bird by the window. The bird said, "Help! I am stuck!" Mia felt sad for the bird. She wanted to help it go back outside. Mia thought of a plan to help the bird. She opened the window wide and said, "Fly out, little bird!" The bird flew out of the window and said, "Thank you, Mia!" Mia felt happy that she helped the bird. She knew that being brave and kind was important. From that day on, Mia was never scared of noises coming from upstairs.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to slide, swing and run. But their favorite thing was to make mud pies. They used a bucket, a spoon and some water. They mixed the dirt and the water until it was sticky and brown. Then they shaped it into pies and put them on a big rock. "Look at our mud pies!" Lily said. "They are so yummy!" "Let's pretend we are chefs and sell them to people!" Ben said. He made a sign with a stick and some leaves. It said: "Mud pies for sale. One leaf each." They waited for someone to come and buy their mud pies. They saw a lady with a dog, a boy with a bike and a girl with a doll. But no one wanted their mud pies. "No, thank you," the lady said. "My dog does not like mud." "Yuck!" the boy said. "Mud is dirty and gross." "Ew!" the girl said. "Mud will ruin my doll's dress." Lily and Ben felt sad. They worked hard to make their mud pies. Why did everyone refuse them? Then they saw a man with a big smile. He was wearing a hat, a coat and a tie. He looked very important. "Hello, little chefs!" he said. "What are you selling?" "Mud pies!" Lily and Ben said. "They are very yummy!" The man looked at their mud pies. He smiled even more. "Wow, they look delicious!" he said. "Can I have one, please?" Lily and Ben were surprised. They gave him a mud pie. He gave them a leaf. "Thank you!" he said. He took a big bite of the mud pie. He made a funny face. He spit out the mud. "Blech!" he said. "This is not yummy at all! This is mud!" Lily and Ben laughed. They knew it was mud. They were just pretending. The man laughed too. He was not angry. He was playing along. "You got me, little chefs!" he said. "You are very clever and creative. I like your mud pies. They are fun!" He gave them a hug and a wink. He left with a muddy mouth. Lily and Ben were happy. They found someone who liked their mud pies. They decided to make more and have a mud pie party. They invited the lady, the boy and the girl. They all had fun with the mud. They did not care if they were dirty. They were happy.
