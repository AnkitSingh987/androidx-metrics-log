Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and the Ball

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a little boy named Tom and his deaf dog, Max. They were best friends and played together every day. Tom loved Max very much. One day, Tom's mom said, "Tom, you will receive a surprise today!" Tom was happy and excited. He wondered what the surprise could be. He asked Max, "What do you think the surprise is?" Max wagged his tail and barked. Later, Tom's dad came home with a big box. Tom opened it and found a new ball inside. He shouted, "Wow! A new ball for Max and me!" Max barked and jumped around, happy to play with the new ball. They played together all day long and had a lot of fun. Tom knew that even though Max was deaf, they could still have a great time together.

Once upon a time, there was a car with a thin tire. The car was sad because it could not go fast like the other cars. It wanted to be fast and strong too. One day, the car met a big truck. The truck saw the thin tire and wanted to help. The truck had a strong rope. It said, "I will tie the rope to your tire, and you can follow me. We will go fast together." The car was happy. The truck tied the rope to the thin tire, and they went fast together. The car felt strong and fast like the other cars. The car and the truck became good friends and had lots of fun.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a big truck. The truck was red and shiny. Tim loved trucks and wanted to play with it. But the truck was too far away. Tim tried to reach the truck, but he was too small. He was sad. Tim's friend, Sue, saw him and asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim said, "I want to play with the truck, but I can't reach it." Sue had an idea. She said, "Let's use a stick to reach the truck!" They found a long stick and used it to reach the truck. But when they touched the truck, something unexpected happened. The truck was not a real truck! It was made of disgusting, slimy goo. The goo got all over the stick and their hands. Tim and Sue were very sad. They thought they had found a fun truck to play with, but it was just a yucky mess. They went home with dirty hands and no truck to play with. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little bee named Buzz. Buzz had a big eye that he used to see all the pretty flowers. One day, Buzz felt dizzy as he flew around the garden. Buzz didn't like feeling dizzy, so he went to his friend, a wise old owl. The owl told Buzz that he might suffer if he didn't rest. Buzz didn't want to suffer, so he listened to the owl. Buzz took a long nap in his cozy beehive. When he woke up, he didn't feel dizzy anymore. Buzz was happy that he listened to the wise old owl, and now he could fly around the garden without any worry.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to play at the park. One day, she went to the park and saw a new swing. She was very happy. Sue wanted to display her new swing to her friend, Tim. She said, "Tim, come see my new swing!" Tim came to the park and saw the swing. He said, "Wow, that is a cool swing!" They played on the swing all day. After playing, Sue and Tim were hungry. Sue's mom gave them a delicious snack. They ate the snack and said, "Thank you, Mom!" Then, they went back to play on the swing until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Dare. Dare loved to play and have fun. One day, he saw a big, beautiful house. It had a big welcome sign on the door. Dare was envious of the house because it was so pretty. Dare walked up to the door and knocked. A kind lady opened the door and said, "Hello, little dog. Welcome to my house! Would you like to come in and play?" Dare wagged his tail and went inside. Inside the house, Dare met a little girl named Lily. Lily and Dare played all day long. They played with toys, ran around, and had lots of fun. At the end of the day, Dare and Lily were very happy. Dare was not envious of the house anymore, because he made a new friend.
