Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Dog

Once upon a time, in an ordinary little town, there lived a girl named Mia. Mia loved to play outside, run, and jump. One day, she went for a walk with her mom and dad. They walked and walked, taking many steps. As they walked, Mia saw a big box under a tree. Mia was very excited to see what was inside. Her dad said, "Mia, you can open the box." Mia took a step closer to the box and opened it. Inside, Mia received a big surprise. It was a toy dog that could walk and talk! Mia had never seen a toy like this before. She played with the toy dog all day long. In the end, it was not just an ordinary day, but a very special day for Mia.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a broken toy in her room. She was sad because it was her favorite toy. She went to her mom and said, "Mom, my toy is broken. Can you fix it?" Her mom looked at the toy and said, "I am sorry, Lily. I cannot fix your toy. But I can tell you a poem to make you feel better." Lily smiled and said, "Okay, mom. Tell me the poem." Her mom said the poem, "Once there was a toy, its name was Joy. It danced and played, and made a happy noise. When it was broken, it felt sad. But then it was fixed, and it was glad." Lily liked the poem a lot. She felt better and said, "Thank you, mom. That poem made me happy." Lily went to her dad and said, "Dad, can you fix my broken toy?" Her dad said, "I will try, Lily." He took the toy and fixed it with tape. Lily was so happy to have her toy back. She played with it all day and remembered the poem her mom told her.

One day, a small girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a small cactus on the ground. She thought it was cute and wanted to take it home. So, she picked it up and started to walk back to her house. As she was walking, she met her friend, Tim. "Look!" she said, "I found a small cactus!" Tim was excited to see it, but as he tried to touch it, he dropped it. They both looked down and saw the cactus start to wiggle. Suddenly, the cactus grew big and tall! It was not a small cactus anymore. Lily and Tim were very surprised. They never thought that a small cactus could grow so big. They both learned that even small things can have big surprises.

Once upon a time, there was a great big cat. The cat had a toy that it loved very much. The toy was a small ball. The cat played with the ball all day. The cat knew that the ball belonged to it. One day, the cat was playing with the ball. It kicked the ball with its foot. The ball went far away. The cat looked for the ball but could not find it. The cat was very sad. Then, a small dog came to the cat. The dog had the ball in its mouth. The cat was happy to see the ball. But the dog said, "This ball is mine. It belongs to me." The cat was surprised. The cat and the dog played with the ball together. They became great friends.

Once upon a time, in a busy little town, there lived a king. The king wore a big shiny crown on his head. He was a good king, but he was not very strong. One day, a mean man came to the town. He wanted to be the new king. He told the good king to give him the crown. The good king did not want to fight, so he decided to surrender. He took off his crown and gave it to the mean man. The mean man became the new king. The town was not happy anymore. The people missed the good king. They wished the good king could come back, but he could not. The crown was gone, and the town was sad.
