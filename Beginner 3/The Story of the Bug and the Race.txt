Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Bug and the Race

Once upon a time, there were two bugs in a race. One was a little tiny bug, and one was a big bug. The little bug was very fast, but the big bug was slow. They both started to race, and the little bug ran quickly, but the big bug could not keep up. The big bug kept running, but it was not fast enough. The little bug kept running, but the big bug was still too slow. The little bug was getting ahead and was almost at the end of the race. But then something happened that changed the race. The big bug felt a harsh wind behind it, pushing it forwards. This wind gave the big bug extra speed, and before long the big bug was running just as fast as the little bug. The two bugs raced to the end, and in the end the big bug won! The little bug was surprised, but it was happy for the big bug. After that, the two bugs were best friends forever.

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a walk. She saw a lovely bird in a tree. The bird could talk! Sue was so happy to meet a talking bird. They talked and played all day. One day, the bird said, "Sue, I need your help! My friend is in a cage with a lock. Can you help me get him out?" Sue wanted to help her friend, so they went to find the cage. When they found the cage, Sue tried to open the lock, but it was too hard. Then, a big dog came by and saw them. The dog said, "I can help you open the lock!" The dog used his strong teeth to break the lock, and the bird's friend was free. Sue, the bird, and the dog were all happy and played together. They were friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a loud mule named Max. Max lived on a farm with his friend, a little boy named Tim. They liked to play together every day. One day, Tim saw a big red ball stuck in a tree. He wanted to play with it, but he could not reach it. Tim asked Max, "Can you help me get the ball?" Max nodded and tried to help Tim. Max stood on his back legs and tried to reach the ball with his long nose. He tried and tried, but he could not reach it. Then, Max had an idea. He told Tim to climb on his back. Tim climbed up and reached for the ball. With Max's help, Tim got the ball and they played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved playing with her toys in her room. One day, she found a bar of soap in the bathroom. She thought it would be fun to play with the soap in her room too. Mia's mom told her not to make a mess or disturb her baby brother, who was sleeping. Mia played with the soap in her room, but she was not careful. The soap made her toys and floor very slippery. She knew she should have listened to her mom. Mia felt ashamed that she did not listen. She was also worried that she might disturb her baby brother with all the noise she was making. Just then, Mia's mom came into the room and saw the slippery mess. She was not happy, but she helped Mia clean up. They were very quiet, so they did not disturb the baby. Mia learned her lesson and promised never to play with soap in her room again. From that day on, Mia always listened to her mom and never felt ashamed again.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a piece of paper on the floor. The paper was fine and clean. Tim wanted to draw on the paper. So, he took his crayons and started to draw a big sun, a house, and some trees. Tim's mom saw what he was doing and said, "What a fine drawing, Tim! You are good at it." Tim was happy to hear this. He said, "Thank you, mom! I like to draw." Later, Tim's friend, Sam, came to play. Tim showed Sam his drawing. Sam liked it too. They both laughed and decided to draw together. They had a fun day drawing on the fine paper, and they were very happy.
