Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Math

Lily liked math. She liked to count and add and take away. She had a math book with pictures and numbers. She liked to do the math problems in the book. One day, Lily went to school. She was happy to see her teacher and her friends. Her teacher said they would do math today. Lily smiled. She took out her math book and opened it. But then, Lily saw something that made her scared. The math problems in the book were different. They had big numbers and strange signs. They looked hard and confusing. Lily did not know how to do them. She looked at her teacher. Her teacher said, "Don't worry, Lily. These are new math problems. They are fun and easy. I will show you how to do them. Just follow me." Lily looked at her teacher. She wanted to trust her. But she was still scared. She did not want to do the new math problems. She wanted to do the old math problems. She wanted to close her book and hide it. She whispered to her friend, Ben. "Ben, I don't like the new math problems. They are scary. Can we do the old math problems instead?" Ben looked at Lily. He saw that she was scared. He felt sorry for her. He said, "Lily, I know you like the old math problems. But the new math problems are not scary. They are just different. They are fun and easy, too. You can do them. I will help you. Just follow me." Lily looked at Ben. He was her friend. He liked math, too. He was not scared. He was brave. He opened his book and started to do the new math problems. Lily took a deep breath. She decided to try. She opened her book and followed Ben. She looked at the new math problems. She listened to her teacher. She counted and added and took away. She found the answers. She was surprised. The new math problems were not scary. They were fun and easy. She liked them. She smiled. She thanked Ben. She thanked her teacher. She was proud of herself. She liked math.

Once upon a time, there was a curious little dog named Max. Max loved to play and explore the world around him. One day, Max found a tap in the park. He looked at it and wondered what it could do. Max decided to perform a trick with the tap. He jumped and turned the handle with his paw. Water came out of the tap, and Max was so happy! He drank the water and played in the puddles. Soon, other animals came to see Max's trick. They all took turns drinking and playing in the water. The animals learned that working together and sharing can make everyone happy. Max was proud of his new trick, and he made many new friends. The moral of the story is to be kind and share with others, just like Max and the tap.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little mouse. This mouse loved to build things. One day, he wanted to build a big tower with his toy blocks. As the mouse started to build, his friend, a silly cat, came to play. The cat wanted to build too, but he was not very good at it. He would knock down the blocks when he tried to help. The mouse got mad at the cat and they began to fight. After some time, the mouse and the cat stopped fighting. They knew they needed to work together to build the tower. They started to build again, this time helping each other. The mouse showed the cat how to stack the blocks and the cat learned quickly. Soon, they built the biggest tower they had ever seen. The moral of the story is that working together is better than fighting.

One day, a boy named Tim found a lucky rock. He showed it to his friend, Sam. Tim said, "Look at my rock. It is in my palm. I am lucky now." Sam wanted to be lucky too. He asked Tim, "Can you tell me where to find a lucky rock?" Tim said, "I found it by the big tree. You can look there." Sam went to the big tree. He found a rock, but it was not lucky. He was sad. Then, a bird came and dropped a lucky rock in Sam's palm. Sam was happy and said, "Thank you, bird!" Now, both Tim and Sam were lucky.
