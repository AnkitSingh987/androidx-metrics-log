Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl Finds the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys and have fun. One day, Lily was playing with her toy car. She wanted to show it to her daddy. Lily ran to her daddy and said, "Look, daddy! My car is perfect!" Her daddy smiled and said, "Yes, it is perfect, just like you!" Lily was very happy. She loved her daddy very much. Later, Lily saw a big dog outside. She stared at the dog through the window. The dog stared back at her. Lily was not scared. She knew her daddy was close by. She knew that with her daddy, everything was perfect.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy shield. He loved to play with his shield all day. One day, Tim's shield became smelly. He did not know why. Tim went to his mom and requested, "Mom, can you help me clean my smelly shield?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim. Let's clean it together." They cleaned the shield with soap and water. The shield was not smelly anymore. The next day, Tim found out the shield was smelly again. But this time, it was not his shield. It was a real shield! The real shield was smelly because it was old. Tim was happy to have a real shield. He played with it all day and forgot about his toy shield.

One day, a little girl was marching outside. She was wearing a heavy cape so it was hard for her to move. She was tired but she kept marching until the sun started to set. When the sun went down, the girl looked for a place to rest. She found a soft tree and lay down underneath it. She closed her eyes and let out a deep sigh. But then, she heard something strange. It was a voice saying, "Liar! Liar!" She opened her eyes and saw a robin looking at her with bright eyes. The robin was trying to tell her something. The little girl smiled and said, "I'm not lying. I'm just taking a rest because I was marching a lot and my cape was too heavy". The robin hopped closer and nodded its head. Then, it flew away as the little girl curled up and fell asleep. From then on, whenever the girl felt too tired from marching, she would remember the robin's words and find a place to rest instead of lying down.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a black bag. She opened the bag and saw a lot of money inside. Sue was very happy and wanted to tell her mom. Sue ran to her mom and said, "Mom, look what I found! There is money in this black bag!" Her mom looked at the bag and said, "Sue, we need to find who this money belongs to. It is not ours." They went to their friend, Mr. Brown, who knew a lot of people. Sue asked, "Mr. Brown, do you know whose money this is?" Mr. Brown thought for a moment and said, "Yes, I think I do. This money belongs to Mrs. Green. She lost it yesterday." Sue and her mom went to Mrs. Green's house and gave her the black bag with the money. Mrs. Green was so happy and said, "Thank you, Sue! You are very kind." Sue felt good because she did the right thing. And that is what happened to the black bag with the money.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the garden to pick flowers. She saw a big, smooth pot. She wanted to put the flowers in the pot. But her friend Tim was there too. Tim wanted to use the pot for his toys. Lily said, "I want to use the pot for my flowers!" Tim said, "No, I want to use the pot for my toys!" They both looked at the pot and did not know what to do. Then, Lily had an idea. She said, "Why don't we share the pot? You can put your toys on one side, and I can put my flowers on the other side." Tim liked the idea. They both put their things in the pot and were happy. They learned that sharing can make everyone happy.
