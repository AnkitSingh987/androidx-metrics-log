Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Drawing

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play on the swing. One day, he went to the park with his mom. He saw a big blackboard near the swing. Tim was very happy. Tim's mom said, "Tim, you can draw on the blackboard while you wait for your turn on the swing." Tim was stubborn and didn't want to draw. He said, "No, Mom. I just want to swing." After a while, it was Tim's turn to swing. He swung very high and felt like he was flying. He heard other kids talking about the blackboard. They said it was fun to draw on it. Tim thought about it and decided to try. He got off the swing and went to the blackboard. Tim picked up a piece of chalk and started to draw. He drew a big sun, a house, and a swing. Tim was very proud of his drawing. He said, "Mom, look! I drew the swing and our house!" Tim's mom smiled and said, "That's a beautiful drawing, Tim! I'm glad you tried something new." From that day on, Tim loved to draw on the blackboard and play on the swing.

Once there was a boy named Jake who had a very special statue. He found it in a special place that no one else seemed to know about. He would take it out every day and set it in the garden so everyone could see it. One day, Jake was out playing in the garden when it started to rain. He was so worried that his statue would be ruined. Then, a mild wind blew the statue over to a bush, which protected it from the rain. When Jake realised that the bush had protected his statue he was very happy. He knew that he should take care of the things he cherished, and that he should also take care of the things others cherished, like the bush. Afterwards, Jake always remembered to look after his statue, as well as all the other things in his garden that made him happy. From then on, he was careful to make sure that everything was kept safe and cared for, just like his beloved statue.

Sara loves to zip her coat. She likes the sound it makes and how it keeps her warm. She zips it every morning before she goes to school. She also likes to zip her backpack. She puts her books and her lunch and her toy fly inside. Her toy fly is her favorite. It is green and shiny and has big wings. She pretends it can fly and buzz around. One day, Sara forgets to zip her backpack. She does not notice when she gets on the bus. She sits next to her friend Ben and they talk and laugh. She does not see her toy fly fall out of her backpack and land on the floor. She does not hear it buzz and flap its wings. The bus driver sees the toy fly. He thinks it is a real fly. He does not like flies. He tries to swat it with his hand. He misses and hits the horn. The horn makes a loud noise. The children scream and the bus stops. The bus driver looks for the fly. He sees it on the dashboard. He grabs it and throws it out the window. Sara sees her toy fly fly away. She is sad. She cries and tells Ben what happened. Ben hugs her and says he is sorry. He says he has a toy spider he can share with her. He says it is black and fuzzy and has eight legs. He says it can crawl and spin webs. Sara smiles and says thank you. She says she likes spiders. She says they are pretty. She says maybe her toy fly will find a new friend. She says maybe it will zip around the sky and be happy. She says maybe she will see it again someday. She zips her backpack and holds Ben's hand. They get off the bus and go to school.

Once upon a time, there was a big stadium. Many people came to watch a fun game. They all sat and waited for the game to start. A man named Tom was there too. Tom was deaf, but he loved to watch games. He liked to see the players run and play. Tom brought his little dog with him. The dog was very smart and could do tricks. During the game, the dog ran onto the field. The players stopped and laughed. The dog did a trick and the crowd clapped. Then, the dog did the trick again. It liked to repeat the trick. Everyone was happy and surprised. The dog became a star and made many friends at the stadium. Tom and his dog went home with big smiles.
