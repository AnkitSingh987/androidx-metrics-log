Category: Beginner
Title: The Round Ball and the Box

Once upon a time, in an ordinary town, there was a round ball. The round ball was sad because it did not have any friends. One day, the round ball saw a box. The box was packing its things to move away. The round ball said, "Hi box, why are you packing?" The box replied, "I am moving because I have no friends here." The round ball said, "I have no friends too. Can I come with you?" The box thought for a moment and then said, "Yes, you can come with me. Let's pack your things too." So, the round ball and the box packed their things and started their journey to find new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big box of toys. She loved to play with them all day. One day, she got a new toy. It was a soft, round pill. Her mommy told her not to touch it. But Lily wanted to touch it. "Mommy, can I touch the pill?" asked Lily. Her mommy said, "No, Lily. It is not a toy. It can make you sick." Lily was sad, but she listened to her mommy. One day, Lily was playing with her toys. She saw her spoiled cat, Tom, playing with the pill. "Oh no, Tom! Don't touch the pill!" Lily cried. She ran to her mommy and told her about Tom and the pill. Mommy was proud of Lily for telling her. They took the pill away from Tom and kept it safe. Lily learned that it is important to listen to her mommy. She also learned to keep things safe from her spoiled cat. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Fluffy. Fluffy liked to travel around his house and look for new places to play. One day, Fluffy found a big drawer in the kitchen. Fluffy jumped into the drawer and saw many fun things. There were spoons, forks, and cups. Fluffy played with the spoons and made a lot of noise. He had so much fun in the drawer. After a while, Fluffy got tired from playing. He jumped out of the drawer and went back to his bed. Fluffy took a long nap and dreamed about his next adventure. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty bird. The bird liked to sing happy songs. The bird lived near a rail. Every day, the bird would fly and play near the rail. One day, a big storm came. The sky was dark and loud. The pretty bird was scared. The bird tried to hide near the rail. The bird hoped the storm would go away soon. The storm did not go away. The wind blew hard. The rain fell fast. The pretty bird tried to hold on to the rail. But the wind was too strong. The wind struck the bird, and the bird fell. The pretty bird did not get up again. The storm won.

Once upon a time, in a distant land, there was a big zoo. The zoo had many animals like lions, monkeys, and birds. Every day, people came to see the animals and hear their sounds. One day, a little girl named Mia went to the zoo with her mom. They saw the lions roar, the monkeys jump, and the birds fly. Mia was very happy to see all the animals. When the sun went down, it was time for the animals to sleep. Mia and her mom said bye to the animals and went home. At home, Mia told her dad all about the zoo. Then, Mia went to sleep, dreaming about the distant zoo and all her new animal friends.

Once there was a small flea named Tim. Tim lived on a big dog. The big dog's name was Max. Tim and Max were good friends. One day, Max had a problem. His toy was stuck in a tree. Max said, "Tim, can you help me get my toy?" Tim thought for a moment. He had an idea. "I will twist the tree branch, and the toy will fall," said Tim. Tim jumped to the tree and twisted the branch. The toy fell down. Max was very happy. "Thank you, Tim! You are a useful flea." Tim smiled and jumped back on Max. They played with the toy all day.
