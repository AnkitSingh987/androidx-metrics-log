Category: Beginner
Title: It and the Mama

It was a rainy day and mama was in the living room with her three year old daughter. They were watching a movie together. Mama loved it and kept saying how expensive it was. The daughter looked up at Mama and said, "It's not expensive Mama. I recognize the movie. We have it at home too." Mama smiled down at her daughter and said, "Oh that's right. But we didn't buy it. We got it from a friend. That's why it's not expensive." The daughter nodded her head and said, "Oh yeah, I recognize it now. Can we watch it at home too? Please?" Mama smiled and said, "Of course! It's going to be so much fun." So the daughter and mama went home and put on the movie. It was just the same, but for the daughter, watching it with mama was extra special.

Once upon a time, a yellow bird named Bobby lived in a big tree. Bobby loved to fly high in the sky. One day, Bobby met a pilot named Tom. Tom had a big, shiny plane. Tom and Bobby became good friends. One day, Tom said, "Bobby, let's fly together in my big plane!" Bobby was happy and said, "Yes, let's go!" They flew high in the sky, but then dark clouds came. The wind was very strong, and the big plane shook. Bobby was scared. Tom tried to fly the plane down, but he could not. The big plane fell from the sky and hit the ground. Tom and Bobby were very sad. They had to bury the big, shiny plane. Bobby and Tom were not able to fly together again.

Once upon a time, there was a big sea. In the sea, there lived a fish named Bob. Bob liked to play with his friend, Sam the crab. One day, they found a fake treasure box on the sea floor. "Look, Sam! I found a fake treasure box!" said Bob. They wanted to cover it with sand to hide it from the other sea animals. They thought it would be a fun game to play. So, they started to push sand on top of the fake treasure box. As they were playing, a big, mean shark came by. The shark saw the fake treasure box and wanted it for himself. "That's mine now!" said the shark. Bob and Sam were very scared. They tried to swim away, but the shark was too fast. The shark took the fake treasure box and swam away. Bob and Sam were very sad. They had lost their fun game and the fake treasure box. From that day on, they knew they had to be more careful when playing in the big sea.

Once upon a time, there was a clever cat named Tom. Tom lived in a small house with a big closet. One day, Tom wanted to go to the closet to find his toy. As Tom went to the closet, he saw his friend, a little bird named Sue. Sue said, "Tom, what are you doing?" Tom said, "I want to go to the closet to find my toy." Tom and Sue went to the closet together. They looked and looked, and finally, they found the toy. Tom was happy, and Sue was happy too. They played with the toy all day long, and they had lots of fun.

One day, a boy and his mom went to a big hotel. The boy was very happy. The mom was a little troubled. She did not want to forget anything. They went to their room and put their things down. The boy saw a big red button on the wall. He asked his mom, "What does this button do?" The mom said, "I don't know, but don't touch it." The boy wanted to touch it, but he did not. Later, the boy went to the bathroom. He saw the same red button. He could not help it. He pushed the button. The whole room started to shake. The boy was scared. He ran to his mom and said, "I pushed the button!" The mom was not mad. She laughed and said, "It's okay. This hotel is special. When you push the button, the room changes. Look!" The boy looked around and saw that the room was now a big playroom. He was so happy. They played all day and the mom was not troubled anymore.
