Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big box of soft bricks. She liked to play with them every day. One day, she decided to set her bricks on the floor and build a big tower. As she was building, her little brother, Tim, came into the room. He saw the soft brick tower and wanted to help. "Can I play too?" Tim asked. Lily smiled and said, "Yes, let's build the tower together!" They set more soft bricks on top of each other. The tower grew taller and taller. Suddenly, their dog, Spot, ran into the room. He bumped into the tower and the soft bricks fell down. Lily and Tim laughed and started to build again. They had fun playing with their soft bricks all day long.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went for a walk. They wanted to play outside before it got dark. Tim was very fit and liked to run fast. Spot liked to run fast too. They ran up and down the hills, laughing and barking. As they played, the sky got dark and grey. Tim saw a big flash of lightning. He was scared and said, "Spot, we need to finish playing and go home now!" Spot barked and wagged his tail. They started to run back home. On their way, they saw a little girl crying. Tim stopped and asked, "Why are you crying?" The girl said, "I'm scared of the lightning and I can't find my way home." Tim smiled and said, "Don't worry, my dog Spot and I will help you get home." They all walked together, and soon they found the girl's house. Her mom was so happy to see her and thanked Tim and Spot. They all went inside and had a big party to celebrate the girl's safe return.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim was in his room. Tim felt frustrated because he could not find his toy car. He was sad and began to scream. His mommy heard him and came to his room. Mommy looked at Tim's shoulder and saw a small toy car. She said, "Tim, look! Your toy car is on your shoulder!" Tim was surprised and happy. He took the toy car from his shoulder and stopped screaming. Just then, their cat, Fluffy, jumped into the room. Fluffy knocked over a cup of water, and the water went all over the floor. Tim and his mommy laughed at the funny twist. They cleaned up the water and played with the toy car together.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a rare, big watch. It was very special to him. He wore his watch every day. One day, Tim went to school. He wanted to study with his friends. He sat down at his desk and looked at his watch. It was time to learn. Tim's friend, Lily, saw the watch. She said, "Wow, Tim! That watch is so cool!" Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Lily. I like it too!" Together, Tim and Lily studied and played at school. They had a fun day. Tim's rare watch made them both very happy.

Lily liked to play with tubes. She had many tubes of different colors and sizes. She liked to make things with them, like towers, bridges, and snakes. She was very original and creative. One day, she found a big tube in the garage. It was gray and long and had a hole at each end. She wondered what she could do with it. She decided to make a tunnel for her dolls. She put the tube on the floor and put some pillows around it. She got her dolls and put them inside the tube. She pretended they were going on an adventure. She wanted to join them, but the tube was too small for her. She tried to squeeze in, but she got stuck. She felt scared and cried for help. Her mom heard her and came to the garage. She saw Lily in the tube and smiled. She pulled her out gently and hugged her. She said, "Lily, you are very original and brave, but you have to be careful. This tube is not for you. It is for the car. It is called a hose. It helps the car breathe. If you step on it, you can break it and the car will be sad. Do you understand?" Lily nodded and said, "I'm sorry, mom. I didn't know. I just wanted to play. Can I still play with my other tubes?" Her mom said, "Of course, you can. But only with the tubes that are safe and fun. And don't forget to clean up when you are done. OK?" Lily said, "OK, mom. Thank you. I love you." Her mom said, "I love you too, Lily. Now, go and have fun with your tubes. And be original and careful."
