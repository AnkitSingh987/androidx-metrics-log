Category: Beginner
Title: Jen and Ben and the Banana

Jen and Ben were best friends. They liked to play in the park every day. They liked to slide, run, and swing. But today, Jen was sad. She had a sour face. "What is wrong, Jen?" Ben asked. He wanted to make her happy. "I don't have a snack," Jen said. She pointed to her tummy. "I am hungry." Ben looked in his backpack. He had a sandwich, a banana, and a cookie. He smiled. He had an idea. "Here, Jen. You can have some of my food," Ben said. He gave her half of his sandwich and his banana. "I don't like bananas. They are sour." Jen took the food and smiled. She was not hungry anymore. She thanked Ben. He was a good friend. "Can we swing now?" Jen asked. She liked to swing high and feel the wind. "Sure, Jen. Let's swing," Ben said. He liked to swing too. He helped Jen get on the swing. Then he got on his swing. They swung together. They laughed and sang. Jen and Ben were happy. They had food, swings, and each other. They did not sigh. They did not have sour faces. They had fun.

Once there was a dad who wanted to buy a big metal box. He thought it would help him to be organized. When he got home, his daughter asked him, "What did you buy, Dad?" He was very proud as he said, "I bought a metal box, honey. It will help me to stay organized!" The little girl was very curious and asked, "Can I see it, Dad?" Dad smiled, opened the box and said, "Look! It is organized and it will help me to put things in the right place." The daughter was very happy to see her dad so organized and she said, "Thank you, Daddy. Now you won't have any trouble staying neat and tidy!"

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim lived in a big tree with his family. They were a happy group. Tim liked to fly and play with his friends all day. One day, Tim tried to pass a ball to his friend, Sam, but it did not go well. The ball fell in a puddle and got all wet. Tim was upset because he wanted to play with the ball. Sam was upset too, but they knew they had to find a way to fix it. Tim and Sam worked together to dry the ball under the sun. They took turns holding the ball up high, so the sun could reach it. After a while, the ball was dry and they could play again. Tim and Sam were not upset anymore. They played together and had a lot of fun with their group of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in his backyard. The backyard was big and had lots of fun things. There was a swing, a slide, and a sandbox. Tim liked to turn on the swing and go round and round. One sunny day, Tim found a big, spoiled apple under the tree in the backyard. The apple was yucky and smelled bad. Tim thought it was funny, so he picked it up and threw it far away. He watched as it flew through the air and landed with a plop. Tim's mom saw him playing with the spoiled apple and said, "No, Tim! Don't touch the yucky apple. It's not good to play with." Tim listened to his mom and went back to playing on the swing. He turned and turned, laughing and having fun in his backyard.

Sam and Lily were twins who liked to watch cartoons on TV. They had a big TV in their living room, but it needed a cable to work. The cable was old and sometimes it broke. When that happened, the TV made a loud noise and showed funny colors. One day, Sam and Lily were watching their favorite cartoon when the cable broke again. The TV made a loud noise and showed funny colors. Sam and Lily were sad and scared. They wanted to watch their cartoon, but they did not know how to fix the cable. They ran to their mom, who was in the kitchen. They told her what happened and asked her to help them. Mom said she was busy making dinner and could not help them right now. She said they had to wait until dad came home from work. She said dad was good at fixing things and he would fix the cable. Sam and Lily did not want to wait. They wanted to watch their cartoon now. They went back to the living room and looked at the TV. They saw the cable hanging from the wall. They had an idea. They decided to pray to God and ask him to fix the cable. They knelt down on the floor and closed their eyes. They said, "Dear God, please fix our cable. We want to watch our cartoon. We love you. Amen." Then they opened their eyes and looked at the TV. The TV was still making a loud noise and showing funny colors. They were disappointed. They thought God did not hear them or did not care. They were about to cry when they heard the door open. It was dad. He came home from work. He saw Sam and Lily on the floor and the TV making a loud noise and showing funny colors. He asked them what was wrong. They told him what happened and how they prayed to God and how nothing changed. Dad smiled and hugged them. He said, "I'm sorry, kids. I know you want to watch your cartoon, but sometimes things break and we have to fix them. God hears your prayers, but he does not always do what we want. He does what is best for us. He gave us brains and hands and tools to fix things ourselves. He also gave us a reliable cable that I bought yesterday. I was going to surprise you, but I guess now is a good time to install it. Come on, let's fix the TV together." Sam and Lily were happy. They helped dad install the new cable. The TV stopped making a loud noise and showing funny colors. It showed their cartoon. Sam and Lily thanked dad and thanked God. They learned that God loves them and helps them in different ways. They also learned that dad is good at fixing things and he is reliable. They watched their cartoon and enjoyed it. They had a good day.
