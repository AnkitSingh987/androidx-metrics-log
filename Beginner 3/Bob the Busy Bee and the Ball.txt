Title: Bob the Busy Bee and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a busy bee named Bob. Bob loved to clean his steel home. Every day, he would wipe the steel walls and floors. One day, while Bob was wiping, he found a big red ball. He said to his friend, "Look, I found a big red ball! I think something fun will happen soon!" His friend smiled and said, "Let's play with it later." The next day, Bob and his friend went outside to play with the big red ball. They had so much fun! They laughed and played all day long. And from that day on, Bob knew that wiping his steel home could lead to fun surprises.

Tom and Anna like to play with bubbles. They have a big bottle of bubble soap and a wand. They go to the park and blow bubbles in the air. They laugh and clap when they see the bubbles fly. "Look, Anna, that bubble is very big!" Tom says. He points to a bubble that is bigger than his head. "Wow, it is big!" Anna says. She runs after the bubble and tries to catch it. But the bubble is too thin and pops when she touches it. Anna makes a sad face. "Don't be sad, Anna. We can make more bubbles," Tom says. He gives her the wand and the bottle. "You blow, and I will catch." Anna smiles and nods. She dips the wand in the soap and blows hard. Many bubbles come out of the wand. Tom jumps and runs and tries to catch them. He gets some bubbles in his hands, but they pop too. He gets some bubbles on his nose, but they pop too. He gets some bubbles on his hair, but they pop too. "Pop, pop, pop!" Tom and Anna say together. They giggle and hug. They like to play with bubbles. They are happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big, orange ball. He loved his new ball and played with it all day. He would throw it, kick it, and even tease his dog, Spot, with it. Spot would bark and chase the ball, but Tim was always faster. "Spot, you can't catch my orange ball!" Tim laughed as he ran around the yard. Spot barked happily and kept trying to catch the ball. After playing for a while, Tim needed to use the toilet. He left the orange ball outside and went inside the house. When he came back, he saw that Spot finally got the ball! Spot was so proud and wagged his tail. "Good job, Spot!" Tim said, smiling. From that day on, Tim and Spot played with the orange ball together, and they became the best of friends.

One day, a small fox was in the woods. He was looking for food. The sky was getting dark, and he was very hungry. He saw a big tree with a hole in it. The small fox thought, "Maybe there is food in there." The small fox went to the hole and said, "Hello, is there any food in here?" A little bird came out and said, "I have some food. I can share with you." The small fox said, "Thank you, little bird." The little bird gave the small fox some food. The small fox ate the food, but he was still hungry. The small fox looked at the little bird and thought, "The bird is small, but I am hungry." The small fox was not nice. He ate the little bird too. The small fox was not hungry anymore, but he felt bad. He should not have eaten the little bird. The small fox was sad and alone in the dark woods.
