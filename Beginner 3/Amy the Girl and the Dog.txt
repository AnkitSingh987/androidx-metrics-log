Category: Beginner
Title: Amy the Girl and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a map. The map was hard to read. She wanted to find a big surprise. She asked her mom to help her read the map. Her mom said yes. They walked and walked. The map showed them where to go. They saw a big tree. The map said the surprise was near the tree. They looked around. They could not find the surprise. Amy felt sad. Just then, a little dog came running to them. The dog had the surprise in its mouth! It was a big, shiny ball. The dog wanted to play. Amy laughed and played with the dog. The surprise was not what they thought, but it was even better.

Once upon a time, there was a big purple room. In this room, a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, loved to play. They played with toys and had fun all day. One day, Tim had to work with his dad. He told Spot, "You stay in the purple room. I will be back soon." Spot barked and wagged his tail. Tim left the room and went to work with his dad. While Tim was gone, Spot heard a noise. A little mouse came in the purple room. The mouse said, "I like this room. Can I stay and play?" Spot barked and wagged his tail. The mouse and Spot played together. When Tim came back, he saw the mouse and was surprised. "Wow, a new friend!" Tim said. Now, Tim, Spot, and the little mouse played together in the big purple room every day.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends, Max and Mia. One day, they decided to visit the forest together and had lots of fun. One day, however, an important conversation united them – they had seen strange creatures in the forest, and it was important to save it. Max and Mia wanted to unite their friends to help them protect the forest. Mia said, “Let’s unite our friends to help us save the forest. We could ask them to pick up the trash and spread awareness about how important the forest is.” Max agreed, and they decided to go get their friends the next day. They united their friends, who agreed to help them, and told them what they could do to protect the forest. The next day, everyone set out to help protect the forest. They cleaned up the trash, spread awareness and showed others the importance of saving the forest. With everyone's help, the forest was finally safe. Max and Mia were happy and felt like they had done something important. They had united their friends to save the forest!

One day, a little owl was in a big tree. The owl had a broken wing and could not fly. It was sad and alone. The sun was going down and the sky was turning dark. A small boy saw the owl and said, "Hi owl, why do you seem sad?" The owl told the boy about its broken wing. The boy wanted to help the owl. He thought of a plan. The boy found a big leaf and tied it to the owl's broken wing. The owl tried to fly, but the leaf turned into a magic leaf! The owl could now fly very high and very fast. The owl and the boy became best friends and played together every day.

Tom and Mia were playing in the garden. They liked to run and jump and laugh. They saw a big green thing with spikes. It was a cactus. "Look, Mia, a cactus!" Tom said. "Can we touch it?" "No, Tom, it is sharp. It can hurt you." Mia said. "Let's go and find something else to play with." They ran to the sandbox and made a castle with sand and shells. They were having fun, but they were also getting tired. The sun was hot and the sand was heavy. "Tom, I'm sleepy. Can we go inside?" Mia said. She yawned and rubbed her eyes. "Me too, Mia. Let's go and find Mommy." Tom said. He yawned and stretched his arms. They left the sandbox and the cactus and walked to the house. They saw Mommy sitting on the porch with a glass of water. "Hi, Mommy. We were playing in the garden. We saw a cactus." Tom said. "Hi, sweeties. You look tired. Did you have fun?" Mommy said. "Yes, Mommy. But we are sleepy now. Can we have a nap?" Mia said. "Of course, my loves. Come here and give me a hug. Then we can go to your room and read a story." Mommy said. Tom and Mia hugged Mommy and followed her to their room. They snuggled in their beds and listened to Mommy's voice. They closed their eyes and dreamed of the garden and the cactus.
