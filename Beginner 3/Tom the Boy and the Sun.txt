Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Sun

One day, a boy named Tom saw a weird toy in his room. It was a plane. It had big eyes and a funny smile. Tom liked the plane and wanted to play with it. Tom took the plane outside to show his friends. It was a hot day, and the sun was shining. They all played with the plane, flying it in the air and making it go fast. They had a lot of fun. But then, the plane started to feel soft. The sun was so hot that the plane began to melt! Tom and his friends were sad. They took the plane inside and put it in a cool place. The next day, the plane was not melted anymore. Tom and his friends learned to be careful with their toys on hot days.

Once upon a time, there was a clever dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball. One day, Spot found a big red button with a print of a paw on it. Spot was curious and pressed the button with his paw. Suddenly, many colorful balloons were released into the sky. Spot was very happy and excited to see the balloons. Spot jumped and tried to catch the balloons, but they were too high. He asked his friends, the birds, to help him. The birds flew up and brought the balloons down for Spot. They all played together and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a big umbrella. One hot day, Tim went for a walk. The sun was very hot. Tim felt hot too. He thought, "I should take my umbrella to stay cool." Tim met his friend, Sue. Sue was hot too. She said, "Tim, can I share your umbrella? I am very hot." Tim said, "No, my umbrella is for me only." Sue felt sad and walked away. Later, Tim saw Sue with a new friend. They were playing and laughing under a big tree. Tim felt alone and sad. He regretted not sharing his umbrella with Sue. Now, Sue had a new friend, and Tim was all alone.

Lily liked books very much. She liked to look at the pictures and hear the stories. She had many books in her room, but her favorite book was about a princess and a dragon. She always wanted to read that book before bed. One night, Lily asked her mom if she could read the princess book. Her mom said no. She said Lily had to read a different book. She gave Lily a book about a smelly skunk. Lily did not like the skunk book. She thought it was boring and stinky. "Mom, please, can I read the princess book?" Lily begged. "No, Lily, you have to read the skunk book. You read the princess book too many times. You need to learn new things. And you have to be quiet. Your dad is sleeping." Her mom said. Lily was sad and angry. She did not want to learn new things. She wanted to read the princess book. She did not care if her dad was sleeping. She threw the skunk book on the floor and screamed. "NO! NO! NO! I WANT THE PRINCESS BOOK! I HATE THE SKUNK BOOK! I HATE YOU!" Her mom was shocked and mad. She grabbed Lily and took her to the corner. She said Lily was very naughty and rude. She said Lily had to stay in the corner until she said sorry. She took away all of Lily's books and put them in a closet. She said Lily could not read any books for a week. Lily cried and cried. She felt sorry, but she did not say it. She wanted her books back, but she did not get them. She stayed in the corner all night, alone and unhappy. She wished she had read the skunk book. She wished she had been nice to her mom. She wished she had the princess book.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a delicate cap that she loved to wear. One day, she went for a walk in the park with her mom. Sue wore her cap and felt very happy. While they walked, a strong wind blew Sue's cap away. The cap got stuck in a tree. Sue was sad and didn't know how to fix this. Her mom tried to get the cap, but it was too high. Sue started to cry. Just then, a friendly bird flew down from the tree. The bird had Sue's cap in its beak. It flew to Sue and gave her the cap back. Sue was so happy and said thank you to the bird. The bird smiled and flew away. Sue and her mom went home, and Sue wore her delicate cap all the way.
