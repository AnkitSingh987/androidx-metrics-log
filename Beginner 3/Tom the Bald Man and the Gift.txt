Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Bald Man and the Gift

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. Tom loved to play with his big screen. One day, he saw a tiny bug on the screen. He wanted to pinch the bug and make it go away. Tom was about to pinch the bug, but then something unexpected happened. The bug grew big and turned into a funny-looking man. The man said, "Hi, I am Bug-Man! Thank you for not pinching me. I will give you a fun gift." Bug-Man gave Tom a magic pinch. When Tom pinched the screen, it changed into a big playground. Tom was very happy and played all day. And they became good friends forever.

One day, a boy named Tim saw a red kayak by the river. He wanted to play with it. Tim asked his mom, "Can I play with the kayak?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Tim got into the kayak and started to paddle. A duck saw him and wanted to follow. The duck swam behind the kayak, and they went on a fun adventure. The sun was shining, and the water was calm. But then, something unexpected happened. The duck was not a real duck! It was a tough robot duck! The robot duck said, "I am here to help you paddle faster." Tim was surprised but happy. Together, they went on more adventures in the red kayak.

Tom and Lily were playing in the yard. They liked to run and jump and laugh. They saw a big box in the garage. They wanted to see what was inside. They ran to the garage and opened the box. They saw four small balls of fur. They were kittens! They had soft fur and tiny noses and ears. They made cute noises. "Wow, look at them!" Tom said. "They are so cute!" "Can we play with them?" Lily asked. They reached for the kittens. But the kittens were scared. They did not know Tom and Lily. They sprang out of the box and ran away. "Hey, come back!" Tom and Lily shouted. They chased the kittens around the garage. They tried to catch them, but the kittens were fast and clever. The kittens hid under a car, behind a bike, and on a shelf. Tom and Lily could not find them. They were sad. Then they heard a voice. It was Mom. She came to the garage and saw Tom and Lily. "What are you doing here?" she asked. "We found kittens in the box!" Tom said. "Kittens?" Mom said. "Oh, those are Mrs. Lee's kittens. She asked me to keep them here for a while. She will come and get them later." "Can we play with them?" Lily asked. "Sure, but you have to be gentle and quiet," Mom said. "They are very shy. Maybe they will come out if you give them some food and water." Mom brought some food and water for the kittens. She put them on the floor. Tom and Lily sat down and waited. They did not make any noise. Soon, the kittens smelled the food and water. They were hungry and thirsty. They came out of their hiding places. They saw Tom and Lily. They saw that they were not bad. They saw that they were nice. The kittens walked to the food and water. They ate and drank. They felt better. They looked at Tom and Lily. They saw that they were cute. The kittens walked to Tom and Lily. They rubbed their heads on their legs. They purred. They wanted to play. Tom and Lily smiled. They petted the kittens. They played with them. They had fun. They liked the kittens. The kittens liked them. They were friends.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy cat. The cat lived in a small cell. The cat wanted to get out and play. One day, the cat saw a big ball. The cat wanted to get the ball. The cat tried to push the cell door, but it was too hard. The cat got very sad. It could not get the ball. The cat stayed in the cell, and the ball rolled away. The cat was still grumpy and sad.
