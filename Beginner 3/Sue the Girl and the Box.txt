Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Box

One day, a little girl named Sue found a big cardboard box. She thought it would be fun to play with it. She went inside the box and shut the door. It was dark and hard to see. Sue's friend, Tom, came over to play. He saw the big cardboard box and thought it was fun too. He knocked on the box and said, "Sue, are you in there?" Sue said, "Yes, I am. Come in and play with me!" Tom opened the door and went inside the box. But it was not Sue inside! It was a big, friendly dog! The dog wagged its tail and licked Tom's face. Tom was surprised and happy. Sue came out from behind a tree and laughed. "I found a new friend for us to play with!" she said. And they all played together, having lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She wanted to invite her friends to her birthday party. She was very excited because she loved her friends and wanted to play with them. Lucy's mom bought her an expensive gift for her birthday. It was a big, shiny toy car. Lucy loved her gift and couldn't wait to show it to her friends. She knew they would have so much fun playing with it together. At the party, Lucy and her friends played with the toy car and had a great time. They also ate yummy cake and sang songs. Everyone was so happy, and they all felt a happy spirit at the party. Lucy's birthday was the best day ever.

There was a bossy sheep called Shaun. Shaun was always bossing the other animals around. One day, Shaun saw a tasty steak next to a tree. The steak was too big for Shaun to carry. The other animals were jealous of Shaun. They all wanted the steak for themselves. Shaun sneezed and said, "Stay away from the steak or I'll tell the farmer!" But the other animals thought he was too bossy to control them. They worked together to drag the steak away from the tree. Shaun was very angry and he tried to get the steak back. He sneezed and he grunted but the other animals just laughed at him. Finally, the other animals gave Shaun a small piece of the steak. Shaun was satisfied that he got something and stopped being bossy. From then on, the other animals were always nice to Shaun whenever there was steak around.

Once upon a time, there was a hairy chicken. The chicken lived on a farm. The chicken had many friends. They liked to play and spend time together. One day, the chicken and friends played hide and seek. The chicken found a big box to hide in. The box was very dark. The chicken waited for friends to find him. But, a big wind came and blew the box away! The chicken was scared. The box opened and the chicken saw he was in a new place. The chicken met new friends there. They were also hairy! The chicken was happy and spent time with new friends. The old friends found the chicken and they all played together.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play in the farm. They run and jump and laugh. They see many animals and plants. One day, they find a big field of wheat. The wheat is yellow and soft. It moves with the wind. Lily and Tom want to touch it. "Can we go in?" Lily asks Tom. "I don't know," Tom says. "Maybe the farmer will be angry." Lily and Tom stare at the wheat. They think it looks like gold. They want to make a path in it. "Let's go in," Lily says. "Just a little bit. It will be fun." "OK," Tom says. "But be careful. Don't make too much noise." Lily and Tom go in the wheat. They hold hands. They feel the wheat tickle their faces and arms. They giggle and whisper. They make a circle in the wheat. They sit down and look at the sky. They see a big bird fly over them. It has black and white feathers. It makes a loud sound. "Caw, caw!" the bird says. Lily and Tom are scared. They think the bird will tell the farmer. They jump up and run out of the wheat. They see the farmer near the fence. He has a hat and a shovel. He looks at them. Lily and Tom are sorry. They think the farmer will be mad. They stop and say to him: "Sorry, sir. We just wanted to see the wheat. It is very pretty. We didn't hurt it." The farmer smiles. He is not mad. He is glad. "It's OK, kids. I'm glad you like the wheat. It is very pretty. You can look at it, but don't go in it. It is for the bread and the cakes. Do you like bread and cakes?" "Yes, sir. We like bread and cakes. Thank you, sir," Lily and Tom say. The farmer nods. He gives them a slice of bread and a piece of cake. They are warm and sweet. Lily and Tom are happy. They thank the farmer again. They wave goodbye. They go back to their homes. They tell their moms and dads about the wheat and the bird and the farmer. They eat their bread and cake. They are full and sleepy. They have a good nap. They dream of the wheat.
