Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and the Bird

Tom was a loyal dog. He loved his family very much. He liked to play with them, sleep with them, and protect them from bad things. He was always happy when they came home. One day, Tom saw a big bird in the sky. It had a long part on its head and big wings. Tom was curious. He wanted to see the bird better. He ran after it, barking and jumping. He did not see where he was going. The bird flew over a fence. Tom followed it. He jumped over the fence too. But he did not know that the fence was part of a farm. There were many animals on the farm. Some of them did not like dogs. A cow saw Tom and got angry. It mooed loudly and ran at him. Tom was scared. He tried to run away, but the cow was faster. It hit Tom with its head and knocked him down. Tom felt pain. He cried for help. The bird heard Tom's cry. It felt sorry for him. It flew down and landed on Tom's back. It used its wings to cover Tom and protect him from the cow. It pecked at the cow's nose and made it go away. Tom was surprised. He did not know that the bird was kind. The bird helped Tom get up. It looked at him with its bright eyes. It made a soft sound. Tom understood. The bird wanted to be his friend. Tom wagged his tail. He licked the bird's part. He thanked the bird for saving him. The bird and Tom became friends. They played together on the farm. They shared their food and stories. They were loyal to each other. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a tight hat. This hat was so tight that it made whoever wore it feel a little bit sad. One day, a bunny found the hat and put it on his head. He did not like how tight it felt, but he thought it looked nice. The bunny hopped along and found some celery. He loved celery, so he started to eat it. But the tight hat made it hard for him to eat. He tried and tried, but he just couldn't eat the celery. The bunny felt very sad. Then, a big mean bear came along. The bear wanted the hat and the celery. The bunny did not want to give up his hat or his celery, but the bear was too strong. The bunny had to surrender both the hat and the celery to the bear. The bear put on the tight hat and ate the celery. The bunny was left with nothing and felt very sad.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the beach with her mom. Sue loved the beach because the sand was so soft. She liked to play in the sand and build sand castles. Sue's mom told her, "I recommend you wear a hat and put on sunblock, so you don't get sunburn." At the beach, Sue met a boy named Tim. They played together and built a big sand castle. Tim said, "I wish we could live in this sand castle." Sue agreed, but then she remembered what her mom said. "My mom recommends we wear hats and sunblock, so we don't get sunburn," Sue told Tim. They both put on their hats and sunblock. After they played for a long time, the sun started to set. It was time to go home. Sue and Tim said goodbye and promised to play together again. They knew that they would always remember their fun day at the beach with the soft sand and the big sand castle they built together. And they knew they would always wear hats and sunblock, just like Sue's mom recommended.

Once upon a time, there was a bossy cat named Tom. Tom liked to tell everyone what to do. One day, Tom had to deliver a big box to his friend, Sam the dog. On his way, Tom carried the box on his neck. It was very heavy, but he didn't want help. As he walked, he saw a big hill. Tom thought, "I can do this. I am strong." But, as he went up the hill, something unexpected happened. The box opened and out jumped a little mouse! Tom was so scared, he ran away fast. Sam the dog saw Tom and laughed. From that day, Tom was not so bossy anymore.
