Category: Beginner
Title: The Garden and the Leaves

Once upon a time, there was a big garden. In the garden, there were many plants. One plant was a cauliflower. The cauliflower was confused. One day, a boy came to the garden. He saw the cauliflower and said, "Why are you confused, cauliflower?" The cauliflower said, "I want to raise my leaves, but I don't know how." The boy thought for a moment. He had an idea. He said, "Cauliflower, I will help you raise your leaves." The boy started to sing a happy song. The cauliflower liked the song so much that it raised its leaves high. But then, something unexpected happened. The cauliflower started to grow bigger and bigger. The boy was surprised. He said, "Wow, I didn't know my song could do that!" The big cauliflower smiled. It was not confused anymore. The boy and the cauliflower became good friends. They played and laughed in the garden every day.

Once upon a time, there was a polite boy named Tim. Tim loved to match his toys. He had a big box with many toys. He liked to find two toys that were the same. One day, Tim was playing with his toys when he felt a little shake. It was an earthquake! Tim was scared, but the earthquake stopped soon. He looked at his toys and saw that they were all mixed up. Tim started to match his toys again. But this time, something unexpected happened. When he matched two toy cars, they started to move by themselves! Tim was surprised, but he liked it. He matched more toys, and they all came to life. Tim was happy and played with his new magic toys all day.

Tom and Lily were on a big boat with their mom and dad. They liked to look at the water and the fish and the birds. One day, the boat stopped at a port. The port had many other boats and people and shops. "Can we go see the port, mom?" Tom asked. "Yes, we can go for a little while. But we have to be back before the boat leaves," mom said. Tom and Lily were happy. They put on their hats and shoes and followed mom and dad to the port. They saw many things. They saw a big fish that someone caught. They saw a man who played music with a guitar. They saw a lady who sold ice cream. "I want ice cream, mom," Lily said. "Me too, me too," Tom said. Mom looked at her watch. "Okay, we have time for one ice cream each. But then we have to go back to the boat. It will leave soon." Mom bought two ice creams, one for Tom and one for Lily. They licked their ice creams and smiled. But then, Tom saw something else. He saw a toy store. The toy store had many toys. He saw a car, a ball, a doll, a train. He wanted them all. "Mom, can I have a toy?" Tom asked. "No, Tom, you can't. You already have an ice cream. And we have to go back to the boat. It will leave very soon," mom said. But Tom did not listen. He was selfish. He wanted a toy. He ran to the toy store. He grabbed a car. He did not pay. "Tom, come back!" mom shouted. "Tom, that's bad!" Lily said. But Tom did not hear. He ran with the car. A man saw Tom. The man worked at the toy store. He was angry. He ran after Tom. "Hey, you, stop! That's my car!" the man shouted. Tom was scared. He dropped the car. He ran back to mom and dad and Lily. But it was too late. The boat had left. They missed the boat. Mom and dad were sad. Lily was sad. Tom was sad. He wished he had not been selfish. He wished he had listened to mom. He wished he had not taken the car. He felt bad. He said sorry to mom and dad and Lily. He said sorry to the man. The man was kind. He forgave Tom. He gave him back the car. But they still had to wait for another boat. They had to wait a long time. Tom learned a lesson. He learned to not be selfish. He learned to listen to mom. He learned to not take things that are not his. He hugged mom and dad and Lily. He hugged the car. He hoped they would see the water and the fish and the birds again. He hoped they would have fun on the next boat. He hoped they would not miss the port.
