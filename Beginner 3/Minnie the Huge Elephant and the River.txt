Category: Beginner
Title: Minnie the Huge Elephant and the River

Once upon a time, there was a huge elephant named Minnie. She liked to nap in the sun and take baths in the water. One day, Minnie wanted to take a bath, but the water was too wet. Not only that, some of the water was muddy and it made Minnie very mad. She yelled at the river, “Why are you so wet?” But of course the river didn’t answer. Minnie's friend, a small frog named Lily, saw what Minnie was doing and said, “Why don’t we try to make the water dry?” Minnie thought that was a good idea, so she and Lily worked together. Lily hopped around and tried to drink the water. She was able to drink some of it, but soon she yawned and said she was too tired. Minnie smiled and said, “It’s okay Lily. Sometimes it takes more than one person to make a change.” Lily and Minnie worked together until the water was no longer wet and muddy. Minnie was so happy that she thanked Lily for helping. The moral of the story is that you don't have to do something all by yourself. Working together with friends can make a big difference!

Once upon a time, there was a mighty bug named Bob. Bob loved to crawl all day. He crawled on the grass, the dirt, and the rocks. One day, while crawling, Bob met a new friend. This friend was a tiny ant named Amy. "Hi, I'm Amy!" said the ant. "I like to crawl too! Can we crawl together?" Bob was happy to have a new friend. So, they crawled and played all day long. As they crawled, they found a big gas can. "Oh no!" said Bob. "We must be careful. Gas is not safe for us bugs." Amy agreed. They crawled away from the gas can and played in the sun. They were very happy and had lots of fun.

Lily and Tom were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump and laugh. They saw a lot of flowers in the grass. They were yellow and white and pink and purple. Lily liked the yellow ones best. She said they were called daisies. "Look, Tom, I found a huge daisy!" Lily said. She held up a flower that was bigger than her hand. "It is so pretty and sunny!" Tom ran over to see. He liked the huge daisy too. He wanted to smell it. He leaned in and sniffed. But then he sneezed. He was allergic to daisies. He rubbed his nose and eyes. "Ah-choo! Ah-choo! Ah-choo!" Tom said. He felt itchy and sore. Lily saw that Tom was not happy. She felt sorry for him. She wanted to help him. She threw away the huge daisy. She ran to the bench where their mom was sitting. She called her loudly. "Mom, mom, come quick! Tom is sick! He sneezed from the daisy!" Lily said. Mom heard Lily's call. She got up and ran to Tom. She saw that he was red and swollen. She gave him a hug and a tissue. She took out some medicine from her bag. She gave it to Tom to make him feel better. "Tom, you are allergic to daisies. You have to stay away from them. They make you sneeze and itch. I'm sorry, honey. You will be okay soon." Mom said. Tom nodded and sniffled. He thanked mom for the medicine. He thanked Lily for calling mom. He said he was sorry for ruining their play. Lily said it was not his fault. She said they could play something else. She said she loved him. They hugged and smiled. They went back to the park. They played with the ball and the swing and the slide. They had fun. They did not pick any more daisies. They were happy.

Tim and Lily were playing in the garden. They liked to pretend they were animals. Tim was a lion and Lily was a rabbit. Tim chased Lily around the trees and the flowers. He tried to catch her with his paws. "Roar!" Tim said. "I am a big and strong lion. You are a small and weak rabbit. I will eat you for lunch!" Lily was not afraid of Tim. She was fast and smart. She ran and hid behind a bush. She waited for Tim to come closer. Then she jumped out and ran to the other side of the garden. "Ha ha!" Lily said. "You are a silly and slow lion. You cannot catch me. I am a clever and brave rabbit. I will escape from you!" Tim was angry and frustrated. He wanted to catch Lily and win the game. He ran after her again. He did not see the fence at the end of the garden. He bumped into it and fell down. He hurt his nose and his knee. He started to cry. Lily heard Tim's cry. She stopped running and turned around. She saw Tim lying on the ground. She felt sorry for him. She ran back to him and helped him up. "Are you okay, Tim?" Lily asked. "I am sorry you got hurt. It was just a game. You are a good lion and a good friend." Tim sniffed and wiped his tears. He looked at Lily. She smiled at him. He smiled back. "I am okay, Lily," Tim said. "Thank you for helping me. It was just a game. You are a good rabbit and a good friend." They hugged and made up. They decided to play a different game. They looked at the sky and saw some clouds. They tried to guess what they looked like. They saw a distant cloud that looked like a dragon. They both roared and laughed. They had fun.
