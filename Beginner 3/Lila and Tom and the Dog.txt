Category: Beginner
Title: Lila and Tom and the Dog

Lila and Tom are playing in the park. They like to draw with chalk on the ground. They make a big line of different colors. They are happy. But then a big dog comes. The dog is hungry. He sees the chalk and thinks it is food. He runs to the line and tries to eat it. Lila and Tom are scared. They do not want the dog to eat their line. They shout, "No, dog, no! Go away!" But the dog does not listen. He barks and bites the chalk. He makes a mess of the line. Then Lila has an idea. She picks up a stick and waves it at the dog. She says, "Stop, dog, stop! This is our line. We protect it. You cannot have it." The dog sees the stick and thinks it is a toy. He forgets the chalk and chases the stick. Lila throws the stick far away. The dog runs after it. Lila and Tom are safe. They hug each other. They are brave. They say, "We did it. We protected our line. We are a good team." They smile and go back to their line. They fix it and make it more beautiful. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a pale blue cube. It lived in a big box with other toys. One day, the cube met a small ball. "Hi, I'm Cube," said the pale blue cube. "Do you want to play with me?" "Hi, Cube! I'm Ball," said the small ball. "Yes, let's play together!" Cube and Ball played together all day. They played hide and seek, and they rolled around the room. They had lots of fun. But then, something strange happened. Cube started to disappear! "Ball, help! I'm disappearing!" cried Cube. Ball was scared but wanted to help Cube. So, Ball rolled as fast as it could to find help. It found a big teddy bear and told it about Cube. "Don't worry," said the big teddy bear. "I know how to make Cube come back." Teddy Bear, Ball, and Cube all held hands. They closed their eyes and wished very hard. Just like magic, Cube stopped disappearing! From that day on, Cube, Ball, and Teddy Bear were the best of friends. They played together every day and had lots of fun. And Cube never disappeared again.

One day, a little girl named Amy put on her red coat. She wanted to wander outside and play. Her mom said, "Be careful, and don't go too far!" Amy went outside and saw a cat. The cat was spoiled and liked to be petted. So, Amy petted the cat, and they played together. The cat followed Amy as she wandered around. Amy and the cat wandered far from her house. They found a big tree and sat under it. Amy said to the cat, "We should go home now. My mom will be worried." The cat meowed, and they walked back home together. Amy's mom was happy to see her. She said, "I'm glad you're back, but don't wander so far next time." Amy hugged her mom and said, "I won't, Mommy. I promise."

Once upon a time, there was a big field. It was a very peaceful place. Many animals lived there. They liked to play in the field. Every day, they would run and jump and make fun games. One day, while they were playing, they heard a loud noise. It was a big truck. The truck stopped and a man came out. He started to make a big hole in the field. The animals were scared and did not know what to do. The man saw the animals and stopped. He said, "I am sorry, I did not know you lived here." The man decided to help the animals. He made a beautiful park in the field instead of a hole. The animals were very happy and they all played together in the new park. The field was peaceful again.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to ride his bike. He put on his helmet and went outside. Tim saw a weird plate on the ground. It was round and shiny. He picked it up and put it in his pocket. Tim started to ride his bike very fast. Suddenly, the plate in his pocket started to glow. Tim felt his bike go up, up, up into the sky. He was flying! Tim was scared at first, but then he started to like it. As Tim flew higher, he saw a big bird. The bird looked at the plate in Tim's pocket and said, "That's my plate!" Tim gave the plate back to the bird. The bird said, "Thank you!" and flew away. Tim's bike went down, down, down, and he landed on the ground. He was happy to be back, and he had a fun story to tell his friends.
