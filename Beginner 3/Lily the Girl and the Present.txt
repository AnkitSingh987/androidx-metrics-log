Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Present

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a kind girl named Lily. She loved to help others and make them happy. One day, while walking in the park, she saw a boy named Timmy crying. Lily went to Timmy and asked, "Why are you sad?" Timmy said, "It's my birthday, but I lost my present." Lily wanted to help Timmy find his present. So, they both looked around the park for it. They looked under trees and behind bushes. Finally, they found the present near a big rock. Timmy was so happy! He said, "Thank you, Lily! You are very kind." They both smiled and played in the park. From that day, they became best friends and always helped each other.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy dog named Spot. Spot loved to nap all day long in his cozy sweater. His sweater was red and warm, and it made him feel happy. One day, Spot went outside to play in the rain. He splashed in puddles and ran around the yard. But his sweater got very wet and dirty. Spot's mom told him to take off his sweater and let it dry. Spot left his wet sweater outside for many days. The sun made the sweater warm and dry, but something bad happened. The sweater began to rot. It got holes and smelled bad. Spot was sad because he loved his sweater. Spot's mom saw how sad he was and decided to help. She sewed the holes and washed the sweater. The rot went away, and the sweater was like new again. Spot was so happy to wear his cozy sweater and take a long, sleepy nap.

Once upon a time, there was a little spider. The spider liked to hang on a big tree. The tree had many rich, green leaves that made the spider very happy. One day, a big wind came and blew the rich leaves away. The spider was sad and scared. He did not know what to do without his leaves. The spider thought, "I must find a way to get the leaves back." The spider tried to hang on other things, like a house and a car, but it was not the same. Then, the spider saw a little girl. She was picking up leaves and putting them in a bag. The spider asked the girl for help. The girl said, "If you help me pick up the leaves, we can put them back on the tree together." So, the spider and the girl worked together to put the rich leaves back on the tree. The moral of the story is that when we work together, we can solve problems and make things better.

One day, a girl named Sue went for a jog. Sue saw a grumpy cat by a tree. The cat had a purse in its mouth. Sue said, "Mr. Cat, that is not your purse!" The cat looked at Sue and said, "You are right. I found it by the tree. I wanted to give it back, but I didn't know how." Sue thought for a moment and said, "Let's find the owner together!" As they walked, they saw a lady looking sad. Sue asked, "Is this your purse?" The lady was happy and said, "Yes, thank you!" She gave Sue and the cat a big hug. The grumpy cat smiled, and they all became good friends.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big iron. He did not know what it was for. He showed it to his friend, Sam. "Look what I found!" said Tim. "What do you think it does?" Sam looked at the iron and said, "I don't know. Let's play with it!" They played with the iron and made it go up and down. It was tight and hard to move. Suddenly, the iron made a loud noise. Tim and Sam were scared. "What was that?" asked Sam. Tim did not know. They decided to return the iron where they found it. When they put it back, a door opened in the ground. Tim and Sam looked at each other and smiled. They found a secret place!

One day, a boy named Tim found a cable in his toy box. It was a gloomy day, and he was bored. He thought the cable could be fun to play with. So, he took the cable and started to play with it. Tim saw his friend, Sam, outside his window. He wanted to show Sam the cable. Tim went outside and said, "Look, Sam! I found this cable. Let's play with it!" Sam looked at the cable and said, "I don't know, Tim. It looks old and dirty. I don't want to play with it." Tim felt sad but he did not complain. He just wanted to have fun on this gloomy day. As they played, they found a big hole in the ground. Tim had an idea. He tied the cable to a tree and said, "Let's use the cable to go down the hole!" Sam was scared but he did not want to complain. So, they both held the cable and went down the hole. But the cable was old and broke. Tim and Sam fell down and got hurt. They both cried and wished they had listened to Sam's feelings about the cable. The moral of the story is to listen to your friends' feelings and not to play with old and dirty things.
