Category: Beginner
Title: The Dinosaur and the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a big dinosaur. The dinosaur was very enthusiastic. He liked to play with his friends. One day, the dinosaur met a little boy. The little boy was not scared of the dinosaur. The little boy said, "I dare you to race me!" The dinosaur was happy and said, "Okay, let's race!" They started to run very fast. The dinosaur was winning the race, but then something unexpected happened. A big hole appeared in front of the dinosaur. The dinosaur did not see the hole and fell in. The little boy was sad because the dinosaur could not get out. The dinosaur was stuck in the hole, and they could not play anymore. The end.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw a big tire lying on the grass. He thought it would be fun to play with it. He asked his mom, "Can I take the tire and play, please?" His mom said, "Yes, you can, but be careful." Tim started to roll the tire around the park. He saw a deaf dog named Max. Max was a good dog, but he could not hear anything. Tim wanted to be friends with Max. He said, "Hi Max, do you want to play with me and the tire?" Max wagged his tail and barked, but he could not hear what Tim said. Tim knew that Max was deaf, so he showed Max the tire. Max understood and started to play with Tim and the tire. They rolled the tire together and had a lot of fun. After playing for a long time, Tim and Max were very tired. They sat down under a big tree to rest. Tim's mom came over with some juice and cookies. She said, "You both did a great job playing together!" Tim and Max smiled, happy to have a new friend.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a little old man. The old man said, "I can cook gold. Do you want some?" Tim was very happy. He wanted gold. The old man and Tim went to the little boy's house. The old man started to cook. Tim was very excited. He could not wait to see the gold. He said, "When will the gold be ready?" The old man laughed and said, "Very soon, my little friend." But then, something unexpected happened. The old man's pot broke and a big mess was all over the floor. There was no gold. The old man said, "I am sorry, little boy. I cannot cook gold after all." Tim was very sad. He thought he was going to have gold, but now he had nothing. The old man left, and Tim was left with a big mess to clean up.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a little boy named Tom. He was a happy boy who loved to watch the sunrise every morning. Tom had a friend named Sam. They liked to play together, but sometimes Sam would be rude. One day, Tom and Sam were playing near the river. Sam said, "I want to be the boss of our game." Tom didn't like it when Sam was rude and bossy. He said, "We should take turns and behave nicely to each other." Sam thought about it and agreed to behave better. They played all day and had lots of fun. As the sun went down, they watched the beautiful sunrise together. From that day on, Sam was never rude again. The moral of the story is to always behave nicely to your friends and take turns, so everyone can have fun and enjoy the sunrise together.

Tom and Jen are friends. They like to play with toys and make things. One day, they go to the lab with Jen's dad. Jen's dad is a scientist. He makes new things in the lab. "Can we see your things, dad?" Jen asks. "Sure, but be careful. Some things are very perfect. They can break easy," Jen's dad says. He shows them a glass ball. It has many colors inside. It looks like a rainbow. "Wow, it is so pretty!" Tom says. He wants to touch it. "Wait, Tom. Don't touch it. It is perfect. It can break," Jen says. But Tom does not listen. He reaches for the ball. He bumps into a table. The ball falls to the floor. It breaks into many pieces. The colors are gone. "Oh no! What have you done, Tom?" Jen's dad says. He is very sad and angry. "I'm sorry, dad. I'm sorry, Jen. I did not mean to break it. I just wanted to see it," Tom says. He is very sorry and scared. Jen's dad sighs. He picks up the pieces. He says, "It's okay, Tom. I know you did not mean to break it. But you have to be more careful. Perfect things are not for playing. They are for looking and learning." Jen hugs Tom. She says, "It's okay, Tom. I'm still your friend. Maybe we can make a new perfect thing together. But first, we have to clean up this mess." Tom nods. He says, "Okay, Jen. I'm sorry. I will help you clean up. And I will be more careful next time." They help Jen's dad clean up the lab. They learn a lesson. They are still friends. They still like to play with toys and make things. But they do not touch perfect things anymore.
