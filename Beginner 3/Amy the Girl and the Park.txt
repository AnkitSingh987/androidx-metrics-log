Category: Beginner
Title: Amy the Girl and the Park

One day, a little girl named Amy went on a picnic with her family. They went to a big park with lots of green grass and trees. Amy saw her mom put a big, red blanket on the clean ground. She noticed a big basket with yummy food inside. Amy's dad opened the basket and took out sandwiches, fruits, and cookies. They all sat down on the red blanket and started to eat their picnic food. Amy loved the sweet, juicy apples and the yummy sandwiches. She noticed a little ant walking on the ground near the food. After they finished eating, Amy and her family played fun games in the park. They laughed and ran around in the sun. When it was time to go home, they put all the food back in the basket and folded the red blanket. They left the park clean and said goodbye to the little ant.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a soft, warm blanket that he loved very much. Tim took his blanket everywhere he went. One day, while playing outside, Tim's blanket got dirty and spoiled. Tim's mom saw the dirty blanket and said, "Oh no, Tim! Your blanket is spoiled. We need to wash it so it's clean again." Tim was sad but he knew his mom was right. He gave her the blanket to wash. While the blanket was washing, Tim waited and played with his toys. After a while, his mom came back with the clean blanket. "Here you go, Tim! Your blanket is all clean now," she said. Tim was so happy to have his clean blanket back. He hugged it tight and said, "Thank you, Mom!" From that day on, Tim made sure to keep his blanket clean and safe.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a lively cat named Fluffy. Fluffy liked to jump and play all day long. She had a soft cushion that she loved to sleep on when she was tired. One sunny day, Fluffy smelled something burn. She looked around and saw a little fire near her cushion. Fluffy was scared and didn't know what to do. She ran to find her friend, the big dog named Buddy. Buddy came to help Fluffy. He saw the fire and knew just what to do. He used his big paw to put dirt on the fire. The fire went away and Fluffy's cushion was safe. Fluffy was so happy that Buddy saved her cushion. They played together all day and became best friends.

Tom and Tim are twins. They look the same, but they are not the same. Tom likes to use his toys and share them with his friends. Tim likes to use his toys and keep them for himself. One day, Tom and Tim go to the park with their mom. They see a big slide and a swing. Tom runs to the slide and waits for his turn. Tim runs to the swing and pushes a girl off. "Hey, that's not nice!" the girl cries. "You can't use the swing like that!" "Yes, I can!" Tim says. "It's my swing! Go away!" Tom hears the noise and comes to see what is wrong. He sees Tim being mean to the girl. He feels sad and angry. "Tim, stop it!" Tom says. "You can't be so ignorant! You have to share and be kind!" "No, I don't!" Tim says. "You are ignorant! You always let others use your toys!" Tom and Tim start to argue. They yell and pull each other's hair. Their mom hears them and comes to stop them. "Boys, boys, what are you doing?" she says. "Why are you fighting?" "Tim is ignorant!" Tom says. "He doesn't share and he hurts people!" "Tom is ignorant!" Tim says. "He doesn't care about his toys and he lets people take them!" Their mom shakes her head. She is disappointed and tired. "Boys, you are both ignorant!" she says. "You don't understand what it means to use something. To use something is to enjoy it and respect it. To use something is to take care of it and let others have fun with it. To use something is to be grateful and generous. You are twins, but you don't act like brothers. You need to learn how to use your toys and your hearts." She takes them by the hand and leads them away from the park. Tom and Tim look at each other. They feel sorry and ashamed. They want to use their toys and their hearts better. They want to be brothers.
