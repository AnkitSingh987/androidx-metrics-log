Category: Beginner
Title: The Red Car and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a big red car. The car could drive very fast. A man named Tom loved to drive the car. He would drive wild and fast. Tom felt happy when he drove wild. One day, Tom got sick. He had to take a pill to feel better. But Tom did not like the pill. It was big and hard to eat. Tom did not want to take the pill. He just wanted to drive his car. Tom drove the car without taking the pill. He felt bad, but he still drove wild. The car went very fast. Then, the car hit a tree. The car was broken, and Tom was hurt. If Tom took the pill, he would not get hurt. The moral of the story is: always take care of yourself before doing fun things.

One day, a little bird named Tim was in his nest. He was a lucky bird because he had a big, open sky to fly in. Tim loved to soar high up in the sky and play with his friends. One sunny day, Tim and his friends were playing a game. They were soaring up and down, and having so much fun. Tim felt so happy in the open sky, with the warm sun on his wings. As the day went on, Tim and his friends got tired. They flew back to their nests to rest. Tim felt very lucky to have such a great day of soaring in the open sky with his friends.

Tim and Jen are twins who like to play with their toys. They have many toys, but their favorite is a big truck with a wire. They can pull the wire and make the truck move and beep. One day, Mom tells them it is time for a nap. Tim and Jen do not want to nap. They want to play with their truck. They say, "No, Mom, we are not sleepy. We want to play." Mom says, "You need to nap, Tim and Jen. Napping is good for you. It makes you grow and learn. If you nap, you can play more later." Tim and Jen do not listen to Mom. They sneak out of their room and go to the living room. They find their truck and start to play. They pull the wire and make the truck move and beep. They have fun, but they are also very tired. Soon, they fall asleep on the floor, holding the wire. The truck stops moving and beeping. Mom comes to the living room and sees them. She smiles and shakes her head. She picks them up and carries them to their bed. She tucks them in and kisses them. She says, "You should have napped, Tim and Jen. Napping is good for you. It makes you alert and happy. Maybe next time, you will listen to Mom." The moral of the story is: Napping is good for you and you should listen to your parents.

Once upon a time, there was a compassionate girl named Lucy. She loved to feed the ducks in the park. Every day, she would take some bread and give it to the ducks. They loved Lucy and would follow her around. One day, there was a big parade in the town. People were happy and excited. Lucy wanted the ducks to see the parade too. So, she took some bread and went to the park. She called the ducks and they came to her. Lucy started to walk to the parade with the ducks following her. When they reached the parade, people smiled and clapped. The ducks were happy to see the parade too. Lucy felt proud and happy to share the parade with her duck friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Lily. She had a pet squirrel named Chip. Chip loved to eat peanuts. One day, Lily found a big bag of peanuts in the kitchen. She wanted to give some to Chip. Lily took the bag and started to shake it. The peanuts made a fun sound as they moved around in the bag. Chip heard the sound and came running. He was very excited to see the peanuts. Lily opened the bag and gave some peanuts to Chip. Chip took the peanuts and started to eat them. They were very tasty. He was so happy that he did a little dance. Lily laughed and clapped her hands. She loved watching Chip enjoy the peanuts. They both had a fun day together.
