Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and the Zebra

Anna likes to use her zebra toy a lot. She plays with it every day. She hugs it, talks to it, and makes it run. She thinks her zebra is very dependable. It is always there for her. One day, Anna goes to the park with her mom and dad. She brings her zebra with her. She wants to show it to her friends. She is very happy. She runs and jumps and swings with her zebra. But then, a big boy comes to the park. He sees Anna's zebra. He wants it. He runs to Anna and grabs her zebra. He pulls it hard. Anna cries. She tries to get her zebra back. But the big boy is stronger. He runs away with her zebra. Anna is very sad. She screams and cries. Her mom and dad come to her. They hug her and comfort her. They tell her they will buy her a new zebra. But Anna does not want a new zebra. She wants her old zebra. She loves her old zebra. She thinks her old zebra is very dependable. But now, her old zebra is gone. She feels very alone.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to find her mom. Her mom was in the kitchen. "Mom, I have a loud cough," said Amy. Her mom looked worried and said, "We need to find your medicine." Amy and her mom looked in the bathroom, but they could not find the medicine. They looked in the bedroom, but it was not there either. They looked in the living room, and there it was! The medicine was on the table. Amy's mom gave her the medicine and her cough got better. The next day, Amy's dad had a loud cough too. Amy knew what to do. She went to the living room to find the medicine. But it was gone! Amy's little brother was holding the bottle. He had a big smile and said, "I found it for dad!" Everyone laughed and dad got his medicine too.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to play in the park. He would run, jump, and steal balls from other dogs. Max was very fast, and he always had a big smile on his face. One day, Max saw a delicious cake on a table. He wanted to taste it, but he knew it was not his. Max decided to steal the cake and share it with his friends. He ran very fast, grabbed the cake, and took it to his friends. Max and his friends ate the delicious cake and had a great time. They played games and tried to score points. In the end, Max learned that it's important to share with friends, but it's not good to steal. From that day on, Max only took things that belonged to him.

One day, a tidy cat named Tim and a dog named Dan were playing in the park. They liked to play with a big red ball. Tim would kick the ball, and Dan would catch it. They were very good friends. "Did you hear that?" asked Tim. Dan listened and heard a soft sound. "Not yet," said Dan. They kept playing, but the sound got louder and louder. They stopped playing and looked around. They saw a big box moving on its own! Tim and Dan walked up to the box. They were a little scared, but they wanted to see what was inside. They opened the box, and out jumped a bunny! The bunny said, "Surprise! I am your new friend, Ben the Bunny!" Tim and Dan were so happy. They all played with the big red ball together and had a great day.

Lily and Ben were playing in the attic. They liked to look at the old things that their grandma kept there. They found a box full of shiny glass animals. Lily picked up a blue bird and held it in her hand. "Look, Ben, this is so pretty!" she said. Ben nodded. He saw a red fish and reached for it. But he was not careful and knocked over a green frog. The frog fell on the floor and broke into many pieces. "Oh no!" Ben cried. "I broke it! Grandma will be mad!" Lily put down the blue bird and ran to Ben. She hugged him and said, "It's okay, Ben. It was an accident. Grandma will not be mad. She loves you." Ben sniffled. He looked at the broken frog and felt sad. He saw some dust on the floor and blew on it. The dust flew in the air and made him sneeze. "Achoo!" he said. Lily giggled. She saw the dust sparkle in the sunlight. She waved her hand and made more dust fly. "Look, Ben, it's like magic!" she said. Ben smiled. He waved his hand too and made more dust fly. He forgot about the broken frog and felt happy. He and Lily played with the dust and pretended they were fairies. They did not hear their grandma come up the stairs. She saw them playing and smiled. She did not mind the broken frog. She was glad they were having fun. She joined them and waved her hand too. "Hello, my little fairies!" she said. "Do you want some cookies?"
