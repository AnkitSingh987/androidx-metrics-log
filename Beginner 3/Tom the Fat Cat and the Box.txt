Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Fat Cat and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom liked to have fun. He played with his friends every day. One day, Tom saw a big box. He thought it would be fun to poke it. Tom poked the box and out jumped a little mouse. The mouse said, "Hi! I am Sam. Do you want to play with me?" Tom was happy to find a new friend. They decided to play hide and seek. Tom and Sam had a lot of fun together. They played all day long. When the sun went down, Tom and Sam said goodbye. They couldn't wait to play together again the next day.

Ben and Mia like to play with toys. They have many toys, but their favorite is a new jet. The jet is big and red and can fly fast. They take turns to make the jet zoom in the air and make noises. One day, they want to play with the jet together, but they do not agree on how to do it. Ben wants to fly the jet high and low, and Mia wants to fly the jet in circles and loops. They start to argue and pull the jet. "Stop!" says Mom. "You need to share the jet and be nice. Why don't you discuss how to play with it?" Ben and Mia look at each other. They do not know what discuss means. Mom explains that discuss means to talk and listen and find a way to agree. Ben and Mia try to discuss. Ben says he likes to fly the high and low because it is fun. Mia says she likes to fly in circles and loops because it is pretty. They listen to each other and think. They decide to take turns to fly the jet the way they like. They also try to fly the jet together and make new shapes in the air. They say sorry for arguing and hug. Mom smiles and says they did a good job. Ben and Mia are happy and play with the jet for a long time. They like their new jet and their new word.

One day, a little fish named Fin wanted to count how many times he could swim around his pond. He asked his friend, a big frog named Fred, to watch him. "Fred, can you watch me swim and count for me?" Fin asked. Fred said, "Yes, I will help you count, Fin." Fin started to swim. He swam around the pond one time, then two times, then three times. He was very successful at swimming fast. Fred counted out loud so Fin could hear him. But then, a big bird saw Fin swimming. The bird was hungry and wanted to eat Fin. As Fin swam around the pond for the fourth time, the bird swooped down and caught Fin in its beak. Fred was sad. He had counted four times, but Fin was gone. The big bird had taken his friend away. The day had a bad ending for both Fin and Fred.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to read. Every day, he would read books with big, wide pictures. He liked to look at the pictures and read the words. One sunny day, Tim went outside to play. He saw a mailbox near his house. The mailbox was blue and had a flag on it. Tim was excited. He thought, "Maybe there is a letter for me inside!" Tim opened the wide mailbox and found a letter with his name on it. He was so happy! He ran back into the house to read it. The letter was from his friend, Sam. Sam invited Tim to come and play at his house. Tim smiled and went to play with Sam. They had a fun day reading books and playing together.

Once upon a time, there was a funny dog named Bobo. Bobo liked to travel and play with his friends. One day, Bobo went on a long walk to find a gift for his best friend, Kitty. Bobo walked and walked until he found a big, red ball. He thought, "This is a good gift for Kitty!" So, he picked up the ball and started to travel back home. On the way, he met his friend, Birdy. Birdy saw the gift and said, "That is a funny gift for Kitty!" Bobo and Birdy laughed and played with the ball. They had so much fun. When Bobo gave the gift to Kitty, she was very happy. They all played with the big, red ball together. Bobo, Kitty, and Birdy had a great day, and they knew that they would always be the best of friends.
