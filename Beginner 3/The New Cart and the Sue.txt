Category: Beginner
Title: The New Cart and the Sue

Once upon a time, there was a new cart. It was red and shiny. The cart liked to help people carry things. It was very strong and happy. One day, a girl named Sue came to the store. Sue needed to buy food. She saw the new cart and wanted to use it. Sue put apples, bananas, and milk in the cart. She pushed the cart to the front of the store. At the front of the store, Sue told the man what she wanted to buy. She gave him her order. The man put the food in bags for Sue. Sue thanked the man and the cart. The new cart was very happy to help Sue. It knew it was a good cart. Sue pushed the cart to her car and put the bags inside. Sue said goodbye to the cart and went home. The cart was ready to help more people.

Once upon a time, there was a little red brick. This brick was not a regular brick like the others. It could talk and travel! One day, the brick met a little girl named Emma. The brick said, "Hi, I am a talking brick. I can travel too! Can I be your friend?" Emma was very happy and said, "Yes, I would love to be your friend!" Together, they went on many travels. They saw big trees, pretty flowers, and little bugs. They played in the park and had fun. Emma and the talking brick were the best of friends. And they lived happily ever after.

Sam and Mia were playing in the backyard. They found a can of spray and a lighter. They did not know what they were, but they looked fun. "Let's spray this on the grass and make a flame!" Sam said. "OK, but be careful!" Mia said. Sam shook the can and pressed the button. A mist came out of the can. He moved the lighter near the mist and clicked it. A big flame burst out of the mist and made a loud noise. "Wow, look at that!" Sam said. But the flame was too big and too hot. It spread to the grass and the flowers. It reached the fence and the tree. It made more noise and more smoke. "Help, help!" Mia screamed. She ran to the house and called for Mom and Dad. Sam dropped the can and the lighter and followed her. He was scared and sorry. Mom and Dad came out of the house and saw the fire. They were angry and worried. They called the firemen and tried to put out the fire with the hose. The firemen came with a big truck and a loud siren. They wore helmets and coats. They used a big hose and a lot of water. They put out the fire and checked the damage. They talked to Mom and Dad and Sam and Mia. They told them that the spray and the lighter were very dangerous and should not be played with. They told them that they were lucky that no one was hurt, but that they had to pay for the damage. Mom and Dad were very sad and very mad. They hugged Sam and Mia and told them that they loved them, but that they had to learn a lesson. They took away their toys and their TV and their snacks. They made them do chores and say sorry. Sam and Mia felt very bad and very ashamed. They wished they had never played with the spray and the lighter. They wished they had listened to Mom and Dad. They learned that fire was not a game, but a flame that could hurt and destroy.

One day, a girl named Sue went for a jog. She liked to run in the park because it was attractive. The park had pretty flowers and big trees. Sue was happy when she ran. As Sue ran, she saw a cat. The cat was also running. Sue thought it was funny. She wanted to pass the cat. But the cat was fast. Sue tried hard to pass the cat, but she could not. Then, something unexpected happened. The cat stopped running and started to fly! Sue was very surprised. She did not know cats could fly. The cat smiled at Sue and flew away. Sue knew she would never pass the flying cat, but she was happy to have seen something so amazing.
