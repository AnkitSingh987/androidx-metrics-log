Category: Beginner
Title: Ruby the Red Bird and the Fox

Once upon a time, in a wide forest, there lived a little red bird named Ruby. She had many friends, but she did not like Mr. Fox. She would say, "I hate Mr. Fox!" One day, Mr. Fox heard her. Mr. Fox said, "Why do you hate me, Ruby? I just want to be friends." Ruby did not trust Mr. Fox. She flew away fast. Mr. Fox felt sad. The next day, Ruby was flying and could not find air. She needed oxygen to breathe. Mr. Fox saw Ruby and wanted to help. But Ruby still did not trust Mr. Fox. She did not let him help her. In the end, Ruby could not get the oxygen she needed and fell to the ground.

Once upon a time, there was a town with a subway. The subway was a place where people went to ride fast trains. One day, two friends, Tom and Tim, wanted to make the subway better. They both had good ideas, but they were competitive. They wanted to see who could design the best plan for the subway. Tom thought the subway should be more colorful. He designed a plan with bright colors, fun pictures, and big signs. Tim thought the subway should be more safe. He designed a plan with more lights, wider doors, and better seats. The friends showed their plans to the people in the town, and they all liked both ideas. The people in the town decided that they would use both plans. They painted the subway with Tom's colors and pictures, and they added Tim's lights, doors, and seats. The subway was now better, and the friends were happy. They learned that working together was more fun than competing. The town and the subway were now the best they could be, all because of Tom and Tim's teamwork.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to pretend they were brave hunters. They had sticks for arrows and a big box for a bow. They looked for animals to hunt. "Look, there is a rabbit!" Lily said, pointing to a brown furry thing. "Let's get it!" They ran after the rabbit, but it was too fast. It ran into a hole under a tree. Lily and Ben stopped and looked at the hole. "Maybe we can get it out," Ben said. "You choose an arrow and I will shoot it in the hole." Lily picked a stick from the ground. She gave it to Ben. Ben put it on the box and pulled it back. He aimed at the hole and let go. The stick flew into the hole. They waited to see if the rabbit would come out. But instead, they heard a loud roar. A scary bear came out of the hole. It was angry and hungry. It saw Lily and Ben and growled. "Run!" Lily shouted. "It's a bear!" Lily and Ben dropped the box and the sticks. They ran away as fast as they could. The bear chased them for a while, but then it gave up. It went back to the hole. Lily and Ben were safe, but they were scared. They hugged each other and cried. "Sorry, Lily," Ben said. "I chose the wrong arrow." "It's okay, Ben," Lily said. "We are still brave hunters. But next time, let's choose a different hole."

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Lucky. Lucky liked to run and play in the park. One day, he saw a big sign near the park. The sign had a picture of a dog and some words. Lucky did not know what the words said, but he did not worry. He just wanted to play. Lucky ran and played with his ball. He was very happy. But then, he saw a big bird near the sign. The bird looked at Lucky and made a loud sound. Lucky started to worry. He did not know if the bird was nice or not. Lucky's friend, a little girl named Amy, came to the park. She saw Lucky and the bird. Amy could read the sign. The sign said, "Do not worry, the bird is nice." Amy told Lucky, and he felt happy again. Lucky and Amy played with the bird, and they all had a fun day at the park.
