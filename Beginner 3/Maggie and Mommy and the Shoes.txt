Category: Beginner
Title: Maggie and Mommy and the Shoes

Maggie and Mommy had a very exciting day. Mommy had just gotten home from the store and had a surprise for Maggie. As soon as she saw the bag, Maggie knew something was hidden inside. She couldn't wait to unpack it! Maggie couldn't hold back her excitement. She pulled out the contents of the bag and spread it all over the floor. Out came a brand new pair of shoes! Maggie excitedly slipped them onto her feet and squealed. Mommy showed Maggie how to tie her laces. Maggie had a cute little smile on her face. She proudly said, "I did it all by myself Mommy!" Mommy smiled and said, "I'm so proud of you! Now give me a compassionate toe hug to say thank you!" Maggie giggled and hugged her Mommy's toe. Mommy took Maggie outside for a little walk in her new shoes. She felt like a special princess! They both enjoyed the afternoon breeze and Maggie felt so happy with her special shoes.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was an elderly man. He had a big hat and loved to walk. One day, he went for a walk in the park. He saw a ball on the ground. He wanted to play with it. The elderly man tried to grab the ball, but it rolled away. He chased the ball and tried to grab it again. This time, he got it. Then, he saw a cop walking in the park. The cop smiled and waved at the elderly man. The cop walked closer to the elderly man. The cop said, "I saw you grab the ball. I want to play too!" The elderly man was surprised. He did not think a cop would want to play. They played with the ball together and had lots of fun. The cop and the elderly man became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a plan to make a big cake. She wanted to share it with her friends. Sue thought it would be easy to make the cake. Sue got all the things she needed and started to make the cake. But she forgot to look at the recipe. When she was done, there was a big mess in the kitchen. There was no cake left to share with her friends. Sue felt sad, but she learned a lesson. She understood that having a plan is good, but it is also important to follow the steps. Next time, Sue will look at the recipe and make the cake the right way. Then, she can share it with her friends, and they will all be happy.

Lily and Tom were friends. They liked to play outside in the sun. They had a lot of fun. One day, they found a big ball. It was red and shiny. They wanted to play with it. But they did not want to share. Lily said, "It is my ball. I saw it first." Tom said, "No, it is my ball. I like it more." They started to fight. They pulled the ball from each other. They said mean words. They did not act like friends. The ball was not happy. It did not like to be fought over. It wanted to be played with nicely. It had a magic power. It decided to teach Lily and Tom a lesson. It used its power to make the sun go away. It became very cold and dark. Lily and Tom were scared. They stopped fighting. They looked for the sun. But they could not find it. The ball had hidden it behind a big cloud. It said, "You are not nice. You do not deserve the sun. You only care about yourself. You do not care about your friend. You have to learn to share and be kind. Then maybe the sun will come back." Lily and Tom felt sorry. They realized they were wrong. They said sorry to each other. They said sorry to the ball. They said they would share and be kind. They asked the ball to bring back the sun. But the ball did not listen. It said, "Too late. You had your chance. Now you have to live in the cold and dark. Maybe you will remember this next time. Goodbye." The ball flew away. Lily and Tom cried. They hugged each other. They wished they had not fought. They wished they had been good friends. They wished they had the sun. But it was too late. The sun was gone. They were cold and sad. The end. Moral: Share and be kind to your friends, or you might lose the sun.
