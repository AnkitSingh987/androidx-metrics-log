Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Lily and the Boat

Tim and Lily are playing in the park. They see a big pond with many ducks. They want to feed the ducks some bread. They break the bread into small pieces and throw them into the water. "Look, Lily, the ducks are eating the bread!" Tim says. "Yes, they are, Tim. They are happy ducks," Lily says. They see a small boat with a sail on the pond. It is red and white and has a flag. It looks fancy. They want to play with the boat. "Can we touch the boat, Lily?" Tim asks. "I don't know, Tim. Maybe it belongs to someone," Lily says. They go closer to the pond and reach for the boat. But a big duck swims to the boat and bites the sail. It makes a loud quack. "Ouch! That duck is mean!" Tim says. "Let's go away, Tim. The duck is angry," Lily says. They run away from the pond and find another game to play. They leave the boat and the duck alone. They hope the boat is not broken. They hope the duck is not hungry. They are still friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was an ugly toilet. It was sad because no one liked to use it. The ugly toilet had a big dream. It wanted to be friends with everyone in the house. One day, a little girl came to the bathroom. She saw the ugly toilet and was scared. The ugly toilet wanted to show the girl it was nice. So, it did a little bow. The girl smiled and they became friends. From that day on, the ugly toilet was not sad anymore. It bowed to everyone who came to the bathroom. All the people in the house liked the ugly toilet now. They were all friends and lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a soft maze. It was filled with interesting twists and turns. Every day, two friends would part the soft walls of the maze. The friends couldn't see the end, but they still kept exploring. One day, the walls of the maze moved and the friends got scared. But it was ok! The walls didn't stay still and the friends kept on playing. They had fun going around the corners and sliding down the bumpy walls. Even though they never found the end of the maze, they still had lots of fun. The friends have been playing together in their special soft maze ever since. Yay!

One day, a little sheep went for a walk. The sheep was not big, but it felt mighty inside. It walked and walked, looking at all the things around it. The sun was hot, and the sky was blue. The sheep was happy. Then, the sheep saw a big hill. It wanted to go up the hill and see what was there. The sheep walked and walked, going up the hill. At the top, it saw a big tree. The sheep was tired, so it sat under the tree to rest. While the sheep sat there, a little boy came by. The boy saw the sheep and pointed at it. The sheep looked at the boy and said, "Hello!" The boy was very surprised. He did not know that sheep could talk. The sheep and the boy became friends and played together under the tree. They had a fun day, and the sheep felt even more mighty than before.

Once upon a time, there was a king. The king was very big and heavy. He lived in a big castle with his queen. They were both very kind and loved to play with the kids in their kingdom. One day, the king wanted to do something fun. He went to the park with the queen. They saw a little boy playing with a ball. The boy said, "Hi, king! Do you want to play with me?" The king smiled and said, "Yes, I would love to play with you!" They played together all day. The heavy king was very happy. The queen was happy too. They laughed and had lots of fun. At the end of the day, the king said, "Thank you for playing with me, little boy." The boy smiled and said, "You're welcome, king! I had fun too!" And they all went back to the big castle to rest.
