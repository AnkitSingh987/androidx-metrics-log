Category: Beginner
Title: Sally the Girl and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a kind girl named Sally. Sally was very compassionate. She liked to help everyone. She had a pretty dress that she loved to wear. One day, Sally went to play with her friends. While playing, she saw a little bird. The bird was sad because it had dropped its food. Sally wanted to help the bird. Sally picked up the food and gave it back to the bird. The bird was very happy. Sally's dress got dirty, but she didn't care. She was happy to help her new friend. And they played together all day.

Ben and Lily are friends. They like to play in the park. They have a big bag with toys and snacks. They run and slide and swing and laugh. "Look, Ben, a big hill!" Lily says. "Let's go up and see what is there." "OK, Lily, let's go!" Ben says. They hold hands and climb the hill. It is hard work, but they are happy. When they reach the top, they see a big lake. It is blue and shiny. They see ducks and fish and frogs. They hear birds and crickets and wind. "Wow, Lily, this is so pretty!" Ben says. "Can we jump in the lake?" "No, Ben, we can't!" Lily says. "The lake is too deep and cold. We might get wet and sick. And we don't have swimsuits." "Then, can we jump on the hill?" Ben says. "It looks soft and fun." "OK, Ben, let's jump!" Lily says. They put down their big bag and start to jump. They jump high and low, fast and slow, forward and backward. They feel the grass and the dirt and the air. They giggle and shout and sing. They jump until they are tired and hungry. Then they sit down and open their big bag. They eat their snacks and drink their juice. They share their toys and stories. They hug and smile and thank each other. They are happy. They are friends. They like to jump.

Once upon a time, there was a flexible cat named Tim. Tim liked to play with his friends in the sun. One day, while they were playing, they saw a big tornado coming. They all ran away to hide, but Tim was not scared. He wanted to join the tornado and see where it would take him. As the tornado got closer, Tim jumped into it. He went round and round, up and down. He was having so much fun! Then, something unexpected happened. The tornado stopped, and Tim found himself in a new place. Tim looked around and saw a big party with many other animals. They were all happy and dancing. Tim was so surprised! The tornado had taken him to a fun party. He joined the animals and danced all day long. And from that day on, Tim was never scared of tornadoes again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a careless boy who always made a mess. One day, Tim found a magic lamp in his room. He rubbed the lamp, and a genie came out. "Hello, Tim!" said the genie. "I am here to give you one wish. What do you want?" Tim thought for a moment. He knew he was careless, so he wanted to be more careful. Tim said, "I wish to be a careful boy, please." The genie smiled and said, "Your wish is my order!" With a wave of his hand, the genie made Tim a careful boy. From that day on, Tim was no longer careless. He cleaned up his messes and took good care of his things. Tim's mommy and daddy were very happy, and Tim felt good about his wish.

One day, a big dog named Spot and a little cat named Kit went for a walk. They saw a long strip of grass. They wanted to play on it. Spot and Kit were very good friends. Spot said, "I demand we find a useful toy to play with on the grass!" Kit agreed. They looked around and found a red ball. They took turns rolling the ball to each other. But then, something unexpected happened. A bird swooped down and took the red ball away! Spot and Kit were sad. They did not know what to do. Then, the bird came back and dropped the ball on the strip of grass. The bird wanted to play too! Now, Spot, Kit, and the bird played together and had lots of fun.
