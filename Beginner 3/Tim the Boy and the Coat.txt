Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Coat

One day, a boy named Tim went to play outside. It was an icy cold day, but Tim put on his warm coat and hat. He saw his friends gather near the big tree. They were all talking and laughing. "Hi, Tim!" said his friend, Sam. "We found a big eraser under the tree. We don't know where it came from. Let's play with it!" Tim and his friends started to draw on the ground with sticks. Then, they used the big eraser to rub out their drawings. They laughed and played for a long time. But then, something unexpected happened. The big eraser started to grow bigger and bigger! Tim and his friends were so surprised. They did not know what to do. The eraser became so big that it could rub out the whole tree! The friends looked at each other and decided to hide the big eraser so it could not grow any more. They learned that sometimes, it's better to leave things alone.

Once upon a time, there was a big turkey named Tom. Tom lived on a farm with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. They loved to play and have fun together. One day, they found a fake toy turkey in the barn. "Look, Tom! I found a toy that looks just like you!" said Tim, showing the fake turkey to his friend. Tom looked at the toy and felt a little sad. He thought that Tim might like the toy more than him. The next day, Tom saw Tim playing with the fake turkey. He felt even sadder. But then, something unexpected happened. The toy turkey broke! Tim started to cry. Tom wanted to support his friend, so he walked over to Tim and gave him a big hug. "Thank you, Tom," said Tim, smiling through his tears. "I know you will always be there to support me, and I will support you too." From that day on, Tom and Tim knew that they would always be there for each other, no matter what. And they learned that real friends are better than any fake toy.

Ben and Mia are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a big fan near the pond. The fan makes the water move and the air cool. "Look, a fan!" Mia says. "Let's go near it." "OK, but be careful. The fan is loud and fast," Ben says. They run to the fan and feel the wind on their faces. They laugh and shout. They see some ducks in the pond. The ducks swim away from the fan. "Hey, ducks, come here!" Mia calls. "We want to feed you." Ben has an idea. He jokes with Mia. He says, "Mia, do you know why the ducks are scared of the fan?" "No, why?" Mia asks. "Because the fan is a big duck-eater!" Ben says. He makes a scary face and a quack sound. Mia screams and runs away from the fan. She thinks Ben is telling the truth. She is afraid of the fan. Ben laughs and follows her. He says, "Mia, Mia, wait! I was joking. The fan is not a duck-eater. It is just a fan. I'm sorry. Don't be mad at me." Mia stops and looks at Ben. She sees that he is smiling. She realizes that he was joking. She is not afraid of the fan anymore. She is not mad at Ben. She says, "Ben, you are silly. You scared me. But you are also funny. I forgive you." They hug and go back to the fan. They play and joke with each other. They have fun. They are happy.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Alex. She was three years old, and she was playing with her dolls in the garden. Suddenly, Alex saw a needle lying on the grass. She didn't know that it was very sharp and dangerous. Her mother saw her and yelled, "No, Alex! Don't touch it! Come here!" But Alex kept playing with the needle, and she started to poke it into her arm. Her mother tried to encourage her to stop, but Alex wouldn't listen. She was too helpless to know what she was doing was wrong. Eventually, Alex's mother took the needle away, but it was too late. The needle had pierced her arm and Alex started to cry. She was very sad, and her mother was very worried. She took Alex to the hospital to get help. In the end, Alex was ok, but the needle had caused her some pain. It was very important for Alex to remember that needles are dangerous and should never be played with.
