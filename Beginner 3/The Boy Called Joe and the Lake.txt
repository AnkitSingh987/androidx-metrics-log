Category: Beginner
Title: The Boy Called Joe and the Lake

Once upon a time, there was a little boy called Joe. He was three years old and he loved to go on adventures. He wanted to go on the kayak. His mom said, “Joe, if you are careful, you can bring the kayak.” So Joe said, “Yay!” and went to get the kayak. He put it in the lake and jumped inside. Joe was paddling along when suddenly he heard a noise. It was a big, scary noise. Joe felt frightened. He said to his mom, “Mom, I am scared!” His mom said, “Don’t worry Joe, I’m right here. I will bring you back safely.” Joe felt better and kept paddling. Soon, he and his mom were back on land. Joe said, “That was so much fun. I can’t wait to do it again!”

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. It was wet from the rain, but he didn't care. He put on his boots and went to the park. At the park, he saw a small, glowing bug. The bug was lost and needed help to find its way home. "Hi, bug," said Tim. "I can help you go home." "Thank you," said the glowing bug. "I am lost and can't see where to go." Tim and the bug started to walk. They saw a big tree and a little pond. The bug's home was near the pond. They walked and walked until they found the bug's home. The bug was so happy. "Thank you, Tim," said the bug. "You are a good friend." Tim smiled and waved goodbye to the bug. He went back to play in the wet park, happy to have helped his new friend.

Sara and Ben were friends. They liked to play in the park. They liked to find acorns and make them into hats and necklaces. One day, Sara found a big acorn. It was shiny and brown. She was very happy. She wanted to make it into a hat for herself. But Ben saw the acorn too. He wanted to make it into a necklace for his mom. He grabbed the acorn from Sara's hand. He ran away. Sara was sad and angry. She chased Ben. She shouted, "Give me back my acorn! It is mine!" Ben was scared and confused. He did not want to give up the acorn. He did not want to make Sara cry. He did not know what to do. He stopped running. He turned around. He saw Sara's face. She looked very sad. He felt sorry. He said, "I'm sorry, Sara. I was wrong. I should not have taken your acorn. Here, you can have it back." Sara was surprised. She took the acorn. She smiled. She said, "Thank you, Ben. I forgive you. You are a good friend." Ben smiled too. He said, "You are welcome, Sara. You are a good friend too. Can we share the acorn? Maybe we can make a hat and a necklace together." Sara nodded. She said, "Yes, we can share the acorn. That is a good idea. Let's go and find some more acorns. Maybe we can make more hats and necklaces for us and our moms." They held hands. They ran to the park. They looked for more acorns. They were happy. They were friends.

Frank had a big, bushy mustache. He was walking in the park one day when he saw a little girl. She was looking down at the ground and frowning. Frank wanted to help so he politely asked the little girl why she was so sad. She said that her pet rabbit had run away. Frank smiled underneath his mustache and said he would help her look for it. He walked around the park with her, looking for the missing rabbit. But they couldn't find it anywhere. The little girl frowned even harder and started crying. Suddenly, Frank had an idea. He ran off and soon returned with a brown and white rabbit! The little girl was so happy she gave Frank a big hug. Frank smiled warmly and said his friend had found the rabbit running around in his yard. Everyone was relieved, and the little girl thanked Frank for being so polite and helpful. They all said goodbye and were happy they had found the missing rabbit.
