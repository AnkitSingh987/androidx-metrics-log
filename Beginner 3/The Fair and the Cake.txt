Category: Beginner
Title: The Fair and the Cake

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a big fair. Kids and grown-ups came to play games and eat yummy food. At the fair, there was a contest to decorate a big cake. The winner would get a prize. A little girl named Lily wanted to join the contest. She was very excited. Lily took some colorful icing and started to decorate the cake. She made pretty flowers and a happy sun. Her friend Tom came over and said, "Wow, your cake looks really nice!" When the contest was over, the people looked at all the cakes. They saw the result of the kids' hard work. Lily's cake was the most beautiful. She won the prize and shared it with her friend Tom. They had lots of fun at the fair together.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a team of animals. They were very good friends. There was a big bear, a little bunny, and a tiny bird. They played and ate together every day. One day, they found a big apple tree with many red apples. The big bear said, "Let's eat these apples!" The little bunny and the tiny bird agreed. They all started to eat the apples. They were very happy and full. As they were eating, a big squirrel came by. The squirrel said, "Can I eat with your team?" The big bear, little bunny, and tiny bird said, "Yes, come eat with us!" So, the big squirrel joined them, and they all ate apples together. They became an even bigger team of friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. The park was very busy. There were kids running and playing everywhere. Tim saw a big seat near the sandbox. He ran to the seat and sat down. In the sandbox, Tim saw a girl named Lily. She was playing with a toy shovel. Tim asked, "Can I play with you?" Lily said, "Yes, let's bury something!" Tim found a small toy car and they started to bury it in the sand. They dug a hole and put the car inside. Then they covered it with sand. After they buried the car, they sat on the seat and watched the other kids play. Tim and Lily had a fun day at the park.

Ben liked to lift things. He lifted his toys, his books, his pillow, even his cat. He wanted to see how strong he was. He liked to show his mom and dad how he could lift. They smiled and clapped for him. One day, Ben saw a big box in the garage. It was brown and had a picture of a bike on it. Ben wanted to lift the box. He thought it would be fun. He walked to the box and put his hands on it. He tried to lift it, but it was too heavy. He pushed and pulled, but it did not move. He felt sad and angry. He heard his dad coming. He wanted to hide. He did not want his dad to see him fail. He ran behind a pile of old clothes. He hoped his dad would not find him. His dad saw the box. He smiled and walked to it. He lifted it with ease. He looked around and saw Ben's shoes sticking out from behind the clothes. He knew Ben was there. He had a plan. He put the box down and pretended to struggle. He made noises like he was tired and hurt. He said, "Oh, this box is so heavy! I can't lift it by myself! I need help!" He looked at the clothes and winked. Ben heard his dad. He felt sorry for him. He wanted to help. He came out from behind the clothes. He saw his dad's wink. He understood. He smiled and ran to the box. He put his hands on it and lifted with his dad. Together, they lifted the box. Ben felt happy and proud. His dad said, "Wow, Ben, you are so strong! You helped me lift the box! Thank you!" He hugged Ben and kissed him. He said, "Do you want to see what's inside the box?" He pointed to the picture of the bike. Ben nodded and said, "Yes, please!" He was excited. He wondered if it was a bike for him. He hoped it was. He liked bikes. He wanted to ride one. His dad opened the box and took out a shiny red bike. It had two wheels, a seat, a handle, and a bell. It was just the right size for Ben. It was a normal bike, but to Ben, it was the best bike ever. His dad said, "This is your bike, Ben. I got it for you. Happy birthday!" He gave Ben the bike and helped him get on it. He said, "Are you ready to learn how to ride?" Ben said, "Yes, dad! Thank you, dad! I love you, dad!" He rang the bell and smiled. He was ready to ride. He was ready for anything. He was strong. He was happy. He was Ben.
