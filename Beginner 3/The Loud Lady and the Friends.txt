Category: Beginner
Title: The Loud Lady and the Friends

Once upon a time, there was a loud lady. She liked to play games with her friends. One day, she played a game to win a big toy. The loud lady and her friends played the game. They ran and jumped. The loud lady was very fast. She wanted to win the toy so much. At the end of the game, the loud lady did not win. She was sad and cried. Her friends tried to help her, but she was still sad. The loud lady went home without the big toy.

Once upon a time, there was a red crib. It was in a room with toys and a big bed. A baby and a cat lived in the house. The baby slept in the crib, and the cat slept on the bed. One day, the baby and the cat went to play outside. They saw a big red ball. They played with the ball and had fun. They laughed and rolled the ball around the yard. Then, the ball went into the house. The baby and the cat went back inside to find the ball. They looked everywhere, but they could not find it. They were sad because they wanted to play more. Then, the baby looked at the crib. In the red crib, they found the big red ball! They were so happy and surprised. The baby and the cat played with the ball all day long. They had lots of fun and never lost the ball again.

One day, a wise old owl sat high up in a tree. He saw a boy playing with a ball. The boy would throw the ball up and catch it. The owl watched the boy play and thought it was fun. Then, the boy saw the owl in the tree. He thought it would be fun to throw the ball at the owl. So, he threw the ball with his left hand, but he missed. The owl was scared and flew away. The boy tried to catch the ball, but it went far away. He looked and looked, but he could not find it. The boy was sad because he lost his ball. He knew he should not have thrown the ball at the wise old owl.

Once upon a time, there was an adorable little girl named Lucy. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Lucy loved to help her parents with little chores around the house. One day, Lucy saw her mom struggling to carry a big box. She went over to her mom and said, "Mom, can I help you?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, Lucy, thank you for offering to help." Together, they carried the big box into the house. Later, Lucy's dad was fixing a broken chair. Lucy went to her dad and asked, "Dad, can I help you too?" Her dad was happy and said, "Of course, Lucy! You can hold the tools for me." They fixed the chair together, and it was as good as new. At the end of the day, Lucy's mom and dad thanked her for being so helpful. They were proud of their little girl. And Lucy learned that helping others can make everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a green frog named Fred. Fred had a friend, a big green tree. The tree had many green leaves. Fred loved to play with the leaves. One day, Fred saw a print on the ground. It was a big foot print. Fred did not like the print. He said, "I hate this print. It is not nice." Fred was sad because he did not know who made the print. Fred asked his friend, the big green tree, "Do you know who made this print?" The tree shook its leaves and said, "I do not know." Fred and the tree looked around. They saw a big green dinosaur. The dinosaur said, "I made the print. I am sorry, Fred. I did not mean to make you sad." Fred, the tree, and the dinosaur became friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play with her friends, but she had one big wish. She wanted to climb the tallest tree in the forest. But her mom and dad did not permit her to do that because it was too high and not safe. One sunny day, Lily saw a small bird on the ground. The bird looked ready to fly, but it was too small. Lily wanted to help the bird. She asked the bird, "Do you want to go up in the tree?" The bird chirped and nodded its head. Lily thought of a plan. She would ask her mom and dad if she could climb the tree to help the bird. She went to her mom and dad and said, "I want to help the bird. Can I please climb the tree?" Her mom and dad thought about it and finally said, "Yes, you can, but be very careful." Lily was very happy. She carefully climbed the tree with the bird in her hand. When she reached the top, she put the bird in its nest. The bird chirped happily and thanked Lily. From that day on, Lily and the bird became best friends. And Lily's big wish came true. She was so proud of herself for helping her new friend.
