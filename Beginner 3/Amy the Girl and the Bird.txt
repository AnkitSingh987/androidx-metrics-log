Category: Beginner
Title: Amy the Girl and the Bird

One day, a little girl named Amy went for a walk. She saw a big tree with interesting shade. The sun was hot, so she sat under the tree. Amy felt nice and cool in the shade. A little bird came and sat on the tree. The bird said, "Hello, Amy! Do you like my tree?" Amy said, "Yes, I like your tree. The shade is very interesting." The bird and Amy played under the tree. They played hide and seek. When it was time to go home, Amy said, "Bye, bird! I will shut my eyes and count to ten. You can fly away and hide." The bird flew away and Amy went home, happy to have a new friend.

Anna and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to hide and seek. Anna counted to ten and then looked for Ben. She saw his shoes under the slide. She ran to the slide and shouted, "I found you!" Ben came out from under the slide. He smiled and said, "You are good at finding me. Now it is my turn to count. You go and hide." Anna nodded and ran away. She looked for a good place to hide. She saw a big bush with red flowers. She crawled under the bush and stayed very quiet. She hoped Ben would not find her. Ben counted to ten and then opened his eyes. He looked for Anna. He checked behind the tree, under the bench, and in the sandbox. He could not see her. He scratched his head and said, "Where is Anna? She is good at hiding." He walked around the garden and looked for clues. He saw a sausage on the grass. He wondered who left it there. He picked it up and smelled it. It smelled yummy. He was hungry. He decided to eat it. He took a bite and then heard a scream. He looked up and saw Anna. She was under the bush. She was angry and sad. She said, "That was my sausage! I hid it there for later. You ate my sausage!" Ben felt sorry and said, "I am sorry, Anna. I did not know it was yours. I was hungry and I thought it was for anyone. Please do not be mad at me." Anna looked at Ben. He looked sad too. She did not want to be mad at him. He was her friend. She said, "It is okay, Ben. I forgive you. But next time, please ask before you eat something. And do not look for sausages when you play hide and seek. Look for me." Ben nodded and said, "I am sorry, Anna. I will not do it again. And I will look for you. You are more fun than sausages." Anna smiled and said, "Thank you, Ben. You are a good friend. Come on, let us play some more. Maybe we can find some more sausages." Ben laughed and said, "Okay, Anna. Let us play some more. And welcome to my garden. I am glad you are here." They hugged and ran to the slide. They played and had fun. They forgot about the sausage. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a huge dog named Buddy. Buddy did not like to wear his vest. In fact, he would say, "I hate my vest!" His owner, a little boy named Tim, wanted Buddy to wear the vest so he could be safe when they played outside. One day, Tim said to Buddy, "Please wear your vest. I want you to be safe." Buddy looked at the vest and then looked at Tim. He knew that Tim loved him, so he decided to try the vest on. When Buddy put on the vest, he realized it was not so bad. Buddy and Tim played outside all day. They ran and jumped and had lots of fun. Buddy was safe in his vest, and Tim was happy. From that day on, Buddy did not hate his vest anymore. He knew it was important to wear it, so he could play with Tim and be safe.

Once upon a time, there was a little duck who loved to splash in the water. The duck had a friend, a small frog. They played together near a pond every day. One day, they found an unusual thing near the pond. It was a shiny chain. The duck and the frog did not know what it was, but they thought it was pretty. They took the chain and put it in the water to see if it could splash too. The chain did not splash like the duck and the frog. But when they moved the chain in the water, it made a fun sound. The duck and the frog laughed and played with the chain all day long. They were very happy to have found a new friend.
