Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy and the Park

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lucy went to the park. She saw a big ice cream truck. Lucy wanted to taste the sweet ice cream. She asked her mom, "Can I have some ice cream, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, but first say 'please' and 'thank you' so you are not rude." Lucy whispered, "Please" and "thank you" to the ice cream man. He smiled and gave her a big ice cream cone. Lucy tasted the ice cream. It was so yummy! She shared it with her mom. They both laughed and had a great time at the park. And Lucy remembered to always say "please" and "thank you" so she wouldn't be rude.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play outside. One day, they go to the river. The river is big and fast. Anna and Ben see some ducks on the water. They want to feed the ducks. Anna has some bread in her bag. She breaks the bread into small pieces. She throws the pieces to the ducks. The ducks come closer and eat the bread. Anna is happy. She likes the ducks. Ben has no bread. He is sad. He wants to feed the ducks too. He looks around. He sees some rocks on the ground. He picks up a rock. He throws the rock to the ducks. The rock makes a splash. The ducks get scared. They fly away. Anna is angry. She says to Ben, "That was silly. You scared the ducks. Rocks are not food. Ducks do not like rocks. You should not throw rocks at the ducks." Ben is sorry. He says to Anna, "I am sorry. I did not know. I wanted to feed the ducks too. I thought they would like the rocks. I was silly. I will not throw rocks at the ducks again." Anna and Ben hug. They are friends again. They learn a lesson. They learn that ducks like bread, not rocks. They learn to be kind to the ducks. They learn to share. They go home. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play outside all day. One day, while Spot was playing in the yard, he saw dark clouds in the sky. He knew a storm was coming. Spot started to rush back to his house. He wanted to be safe and dry before the rain started. As he ran, the wind blew and the leaves danced around him. Spot was very fast and reached his house just in time. Inside the house, Spot watched the storm through the window. The rain fell hard, and the wind howled loudly. But Spot was safe and warm with his family. When the storm ended, Spot went back outside to play in the puddles. He was a very happy dog.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the busy park with her mom. The park was full of kids playing and laughing. Lily saw a big slide and ran to it. She climbed up and slid down with a big smile on her face. As the sun went down, the park started to get dark. Lily's mom said, "Lily, it's time to go home now." Lily was sad but knew it was time to leave. They walked home, holding hands and smiling. At home, Lily had her dinner and got ready for bed. She brushed her teeth and put on her pajamas. Her mom tucked her in and said, "Good night, Lily. I love you." Lily smiled and said, "I love you too, Mommy." Then, she closed her eyes and went to sleep.

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to collect stamps. They had a big book where they stuck their stamps with glue. They got stamps from their dad, who worked at the post office, and from their grandma, who traveled a lot. One day, Lily got a new stamp from grandma. It was very pretty and shiny, with a picture of a butterfly. Lily was very happy and showed it to Tom. Tom was envious. He wanted the butterfly stamp for himself. He thought it was not fair that Lily got it and he did not. When Lily was not looking, Tom took the butterfly stamp from her book. He tried to peel it off, but it was stuck too hard. He pulled and pulled, until he tore the stamp in half. He felt bad, but he hid the pieces under his bed. Lily noticed that her stamp was missing. She looked everywhere, but she could not find it. She asked Tom if he had seen it, but he lied and said no. Lily was very sad and cried. She loved her stamp and wanted it back. Their dad came home and saw Lily crying. He asked what was wrong, and Lily told him about her stamp. He looked at Tom and saw that he was guilty. He asked Tom to tell the truth, and Tom confessed that he had taken and torn the stamp. He said he was sorry and gave the pieces to Lily. Lily was still sad, but she forgave Tom. She said they could try to fix the stamp with tape. Their dad was proud of Lily for being kind and of Tom for being honest. He said that stamps are nice, but sharing and caring are more important. He gave them both a hug and a new stamp each. Tom and Lily learned their lesson and never fought over stamps again.
