Category: Beginner
Title: Kitty the Nosy Cat and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to watch Mom cook in the kitchen. One day, Mom put on her apron to make a big cake. Kitty sat on the floor and watched closely. "Kitty, do you want to help me?" asked Mom. Kitty meowed and nodded her head. Mom gave Kitty a small bowl to fill with flour. Kitty was very happy to help. Kitty filled the bowl with flour and gave it to Mom. Mom smiled and said, "Thank you, Kitty!" They made the cake together and it smelled so good. When the cake was done, they shared it with the whole family. Kitty felt proud and loved being a helpful, nosy cat.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the garden. He saw a big, colorful squash. It was very pretty. Tim wanted to pick it up, but it was too heavy. He started to struggle. A talking bird saw Tim and said, "Why do you struggle, Tim?" Tim said, "I want the big, colorful squash. But it is too heavy for me." The bird said, "I can help you. Let's try together." Tim and the bird tried to lift the squash. They still struggled, but they did not give up. Then, something unexpected happened. The squash began to float in the air! Tim and the bird were very happy. They took the squash home and shared it with their friends.

Mandy wanted something to eat for a snack. Her mom said, "Why don't you try this original mint?" Mandy looked at the mint. She had never seen one before. She smelled it and said, "It smells delicious!" Mandy's mom said, "Why don't you try it? I'm sure you'll like it!" Mandy was a bit scared. She said, "Is it safe to eat?" Her mom said, "Of course it is, I wouldn't give it to you if it wasn't." So Mandy closed her eyes and took a bite. The mint was sweet, and it had a unique taste. She smiled and said, "I really like it!" Mandy ate the whole mint and said, "Can I have another one please?" Her mom said, "Why not! I'm sure you'll enjoy it as much as you did the first one!"

One day, a polite cat named Kitty went for a walk. She was very excite to see her friend, Doggy. They liked to play together. Kitty saw Doggy and said, "Hi, Doggy! Let's play!" Doggy wagged his tail and said, "Yes! Let's play!" They ran and jumped in the park. They were having so much fun. Then, Kitty stepped on a rock and hurt her toe. She cried, "Ouch! My toe!" Doggy stopped playing and went to Kitty. He said, "I will help you." Doggy was a good friend. He helped Kitty walk home. They were happy to be together, even when Kitty's toe hurt.

Once there was a boy named Bob who was popular amongst the children. He had bright green eyes and a contagious laugh. One day while Bob was playing outside, he suddenly started to cry. All the other kids wondered why he was crying. One of the children, Ann, walked up to Bob, patted him on the shoulder, and asked: "Bob, why are you crying?". Bob wiped the tears from his eyes and said "I'm crying because I'm not popular enough". He looked up at all the other kids, and he thought he was the only one not included in their games. Ann smiled and said: "That's okay Bob, green is the colour of hope". The next day Bob noticed that all the other kids had put green tape around their wrists. He soon found out that green was the popular colour among his friends, and he too could be included in their games. Bob looked up and smiled, feeling better than ever. Now, he had finally been accepted among his peers.

Anna and Ben were hungry. They wanted to eat soup for lunch. But they had a problem. They only had one pot and one spoon. How could they share? "I want to stir the soup!" Anna said. She grabbed the spoon and put it in the pot. "No, I want to stir the soup!" Ben said. He pulled the spoon from Anna's hand and stirred the soup. "Stop it!" Anna said. She tried to get the spoon back. "Let go!" Ben said. He held the spoon tight. They both pulled and pulled. The spoon flew out of their hands. The pot tipped over. The soup spilled on the floor. "Oh no!" Anna and Ben said. They looked at the mess. They felt sad and sorry. Mom came in. She saw the soup on the floor. She saw Anna and Ben with tears in their eyes. "What happened?" Mom asked. "We wanted to stir the soup, but we fought over the spoon and the pot," Anna said. "We were not humble. We were selfish," Ben said. Mom hugged them. She said, "I'm glad you realized your mistake. Stirring the soup is not as important as sharing and caring. Next time, you can take turns or use two spoons. Now, let's clean up and make some more soup. I love you both." Anna and Ben said, "We love you too, Mom. We're sorry. We'll be humble and kind." They helped Mom clean up and make more soup. They ate their soup happily. They thanked Mom for the soup. They learned their lesson.
