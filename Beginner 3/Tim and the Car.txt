Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Car

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They wanted to buy a toy for Tim's friend's birthday. When they got to the store, Tim saw a big, red ball. He wanted to hold it. He asked his mom, "Can I hold the ball, please?" His mom said, "Yes, you can hold it, but be careful. We need to find a toy for your friend first." Tim held the ball and looked at the toys. He saw a small car and a soft bear. He asked his mom, "How much is the car and the bear?" His mom looked at the price tag. She said, "The car is not too much, but the bear is a little harsh." Tim thought about it. He wanted to give his friend a nice gift. He said, "I want to get the car for my friend. He will like it." His mom smiled and said, "That's a good choice, Tim. Let's buy the car and then you can play with your friend at his birthday party." Tim was happy. He put the red ball back and they bought the car for his friend. Tim couldn't wait to give it to him at the party.

Anna and Ben were bored. They had no toys to play with and it was raining outside. They looked at the television, but it was broken. Mama said she would fix it later, but she was busy in the kitchen. "I have an idea," Ben said. "Let's play twist." He showed Anna how to twist his body and make funny shapes. Anna giggled and tried to copy him. They twisted and turned on the floor, making each other laugh. They twisted their arms, their legs, their necks, and their faces. They twisted so much that they got dizzy and fell down. But then, they heard a loud snap. They looked up and saw that the television cord had come loose from the wall. It was thin and frayed, and it had twisted around Ben's foot when he was playing. "Uh-oh," Ben said. "Mama is going to be mad." They heard Mama coming from the kitchen. She saw the television cord and the broken plug. She saw Ben and Anna on the floor, looking scared. "What did you do?" she asked, her voice stern. "We were playing twist, Mama," Anna said. "We didn't mean to break the television." Mama sighed. She was not happy, but she was not angry either. She knew that Ben and Anna were just trying to have fun. She hugged them and said, "It's okay. I can fix the television later. But next time, be careful with the cords. They are not toys. They can be dangerous." She unplugged the television and put the cord away. She told Ben and Anna to go to their room and find something else to play with. She hoped they would not twist anything else.

John and his mom went to the park. John saw a big pool. He thought it was very impressive. John wanted to go in the pool, so his mom said yes. He put on his swimsuit and went to the edge of the pool. John dropped his sandals on the grass and smiled. He was so happy, he laughed and jumped into the pool. He splashed around and had lots of fun. His mom was impressed with how well he could swim. John said he wanted to stay in the pool for ever. His mom told him he had to get out eventually, so he said goodbye to the pool and went home.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She was three years old and loved to do lots of different things. One of her favorite activities was to use crayons to draw pictures. One day, the little girl was playing with her crayons. She was coloring on a clean piece of paper. Suddenly, the little girl dropped some of her crayons on the floor. The little girl started to cry. She looked down at the crayons and realized that they were no longer clean. She asked her mom, “What do I do now?” Her mom took a deep breath and said, “There’s nothing you can do. The crayons are no longer clean. We’ll have to throw them out.” The little girl was heartbroken. She had loved her crayons so much, but now she had to throw them away. Sadly, she said goodbye to her crayons and watched them disappear.
