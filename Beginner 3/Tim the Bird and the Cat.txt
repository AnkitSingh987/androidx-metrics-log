Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Bird and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. He had an ugly neck, but he did not care. Tim loved to sing and play with his friends in the big tree. One day, Tim saw a cat. The cat wanted to play with him, but Tim was scared. He did not want to play with the cat because it might hurt his neck. So, Tim called his friends for help. His friends came and scared the cat away. Tim was so happy! He thanked his friends and they all played together in the big tree. From that day on, Tim knew that even with his ugly neck, he had the best friends in the world.

Once, there was a boy named Mark. Mark was sad because he thought his days were boring. He wanted to have fun and smile, but he did not know how. One day, Mark went to the park. He saw a dog with a big, happy smile. The dog was running and playing with a ball. Mark thought that looked like fun. He went to play with the dog and soon, they were both smiling. Now, Mark and the dog were friends. They played together every day. Mark's days were not boring anymore. They were full of fun and smiles. Mark learned that a friend can make any day better.

One day, a girl named Sally went on a safari with her mom and dad. They wanted to see animals that were not normal. Sally was very excited. Her mom and dad were happy too. On the safari, they saw a lion. The lion said, "Hi, I am Leo. Let me introduce you to my friends." Leo took them to see his friends. They met a big elephant, a tall giraffe, and a fast cheetah. Sally and her parents talked to the animals and had fun. But then, a big storm came. It was very loud and scary. The wind blew and the rain fell. The animals ran away to hide. Sally and her parents tried to run too, but they got lost. They were wet and cold. The safari was not fun anymore.

Tom and Lily were playing with their toy cars on the floor. They liked to make them go fast and make loud noises. Sometimes they crashed into each other and laughed. "Look, Lily, I can make my car reverse!" Tom said, pushing his car backwards. "Beep beep!" "Wow, Tom, that's cool!" Lily said, trying to do the same with her car. "Beep beep!" They heard a knock on the door. It was their mom, holding a big box. "Hello, kids, I have a surprise for you!" she said, smiling. "Do you want to see what's inside?" "Yes, yes, yes!" Tom and Lily said, jumping up and running to the door. Mom opened the box and lifted something out. It was a pig! A real pig, with pink skin and a curly tail. It was small and cute and made a snorting sound. "A pig!" Tom and Lily shouted, clapping their hands. "Can we pet it, mom?" "Of course, this is your new pet, kids. His name is Pippy, and he's very friendly. You can feed him, play with him, and take him for walks. But you have to be gentle and kind to him, okay?" "Okay, mom, we will!" Tom and Lily said, hugging the pig. Pippy licked their faces and wagged his tail. They took Pippy to their room and showed him their toys. Pippy liked the toy cars, too. He tried to push them with his nose and make noises. He also liked to roll on the floor and dig in the carpet. "Look, mom, Pippy can reverse, too!" Tom said, laughing. "Beep beep!" "He's so smart, Tom!" Lily said, patting the pig. "And he's so deep, mom!" "Deep?" mom asked, confused. "Yes, mom, deep. He has a deep voice, and a deep belly, and a deep love for us!" Lily said, cuddling the pig. Mom smiled and kissed them all. "You're right, Lily, he's very deep. And you're very lucky to have him. I hope you'll have a lot of fun with Pippy, kids. He's a special pig."
