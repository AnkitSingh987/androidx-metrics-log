Title: The Sleepy Crocodile and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy crocodile. He lived near a pond. He liked to sleep all day long. The other animals did not like him. They were scared of him. One day, the animals wanted to play. They wanted to spring up and down. They wanted to have fun. But the sleepy crocodile was in the way. They did not want to wake him up. They were too scared. The animals tried to be quiet. They tried to play without making noise. But they were not careful. They woke up the sleepy crocodile. The crocodile was angry. He ate the animals. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small park, there was a noisy tree. It was a big tree with many birds. The birds sang all day long. The tree was happy because it had many friends. One sunny day, a little boy named Tim came to the park. He heard the noisy tree and walked over. He saw the birds singing and said, "Hello, birds! You sing so well!" The birds stopped singing for a moment and said, "Thank you, Tim! We love to sing. Do you want to sing with us?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, I want to sing, too!" So, Tim and the birds sang together under the noisy tree. They had so much fun. After a while, Tim had to go home. He said, "Thank you, birds, and thank you, tree, for the fun day. I will come back to sing with you again." The birds and the tree said, "You're welcome, Tim! We will be here waiting for you." And from that day on, Tim would always come back to the park to sing with his new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a polite cat named Tom. Tom loved to eat tuna. One day, he saw a big can of tuna on the table. He wanted to eat it, but he knew it was not his. Tom saw his friend, a little mouse named Tim. Tom asked Tim, "Can you help me delay eating the tuna? I want to be a good cat." Tim said, "Yes, I can help you. Let's play a game. It will make you forget the tuna for now." So, Tom and Tim played games together. They had lots of fun. Tom forgot about the tuna for a while. Later, Tom's owner saw how good he was and gave him some tuna to eat. Tom was happy, and he thanked Tim for helping him be a good cat.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to play in the forest near his house. One day, Tim met a talking bird in the forest. The bird was very colorful and could say many words. "Hello, Tim!" said the bird. "Do you want to play a game with me? I can pick things up with my beak. Let's see who can pick up the most sticks!" Tim thought it was a fun idea. So, they started to play the game. Tim picked up sticks with his hands, and the bird picked up sticks with its beak. They laughed and had a great time. After a while, Tim saw a big rock and tried to pick it up. He couldn't do it because the rock was too heavy. The bird laughed and said, "Silly Tim, that's a stupid rock to pick! Let's go back to picking sticks!" And they continued to play and have fun in the forest.
