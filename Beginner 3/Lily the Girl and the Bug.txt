Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Bug

One day, a little girl named Lily went to school. She was a pupil in Miss Brown's class. Lily liked her teacher and friends. They played games, sang songs, and learned new things every day. One morning, Lily saw her friend Timmy eating a big, disgusting bug. "Yuck!" said Lily. "Why are you eating that?" Timmy smiled and said, "It's not real! It's just a toy bug!" Lily laughed, and they played with the toy bug together. After playing, Lily's hands were dirty. She went to the sink and used a towel to wipe her hands clean. Then she went back to play with Timmy and the toy bug. They had a fun day at school.

---

Lily and Ben are friends. They go to the same school. They like to play and learn together. One day, their teacher tells them they will have a quiz on Friday. The quiz is about animals and their sounds. Lily and Ben want to do well on the quiz. They decide to study together. They look at pictures of animals and say their names and sounds. They also play a game where one of them makes an animal sound and the other one guesses the animal. "Meow, meow," Lily says. "Cat!" Ben says. "Good job, Ben. Your turn," Lily says. "Oink, oink," Ben says. "Pig!" Lily says. They have fun and learn a lot. They improve their animal knowledge. On Friday, they take the quiz. They are not scared. They remember what they studied. They answer all the questions correctly. Their teacher is proud of them. She gives them stickers and stars. Lily and Ben are happy. They hug each other. They feel rich. They have good grades and good friends. They say thank you to their teacher and to each other. They look forward to the next quiz.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk in the cool woods. He saw a big tree and heard a noise. Tim looked up and saw a small bird. The bird was blue and yellow. Tim was happy to see the bird. "Hello, bird!" Tim said. "Why do you make that noise?" Tim asked the bird a question. The bird looked at Tim and said, "I make noise to wake my friends. We like to sing together." Tim thought that was a good idea. He wanted to sing with the bird and his friends too. So, Tim started to sing. The bird's friends woke up and joined them. They all sang and had fun in the cool woods.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a fierce boy with big muscles. He liked to wipe things clean. One day, Tim went to the park to play. At the park, Tim saw a big mess. It was all over the slide, the swings, and the sandbox. Tim wanted to help, so he started to wipe the mess away. He used his muscles to clean up the whole park. But then, something unexpected happened. The mess came back! It was not a normal mess. It was a magic mess! Tim had to think of a new way to clean up the park. He wished very hard, and the magic mess went away. The park was clean again, and Tim was happy.

---

One day, a girl named Amy went for a walk in the woods. She liked to walk because it was easy and fun. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Amy felt happy. As she walked, she heard a scream. "Help me, please!" said a little bird. Amy looked up and saw the bird stuck in a tree. The bird was scared. "Don't worry, I'll help you," said Amy. Amy found a long stick made of wood. She used the stick to help the bird get out of the tree. But, surprise! The bird could talk! "Thank you, Amy," said the bird. They became best friends and played in the woods every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little bug named Tim. Tim was a tiny bug with a big, round belly. He lived in a beautiful garden with lots of flowers and trees. One night, Tim saw his belly start to glow. It was a soft, warm light. He was very frightened and didn't know what to do. He ran to his friend, the wise old owl, for help. The owl told Tim that his glow was a special gift. Tim decided to use his glow to help others. At night, he would light up the dark paths for his friends, so they could see where they were going. Soon, Tim wasn't frightened anymore. He was very happy that he could help his friends with his special round, glowing belly.
