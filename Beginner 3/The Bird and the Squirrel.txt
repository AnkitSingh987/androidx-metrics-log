Category: Beginner
Title: The Bird and the Squirrel

Once upon a time, there was a little bird. The bird had a cozy nest in a tree. The nest was hidden in the tree, so it was hard to see. The bird liked the hidden nest because it was safe and warm. One day, the bird saw a sad squirrel. The squirrel was cold and tired. The bird wanted to help the squirrel. The bird said, "Come to my nest. It is hidden and warm. I will comfort you." The squirrel climbed the tree and found the hidden nest. The bird and the squirrel sat in the nest together. The bird comforted the squirrel. They became good friends. From that day on, they shared the warm nest and were always happy.

One day, a big bear named Bob and a little rabbit named Lily went on a walk in the woods. They marched side by side, singing a happy song. The sun was shining, the birds were singing, and the flowers were dancing in the breeze. As they walked, they found a puzzle on the ground. It was a picture of a lemon. Bob and Lily decided to put the puzzle together. They worked hard, and soon the puzzle was complete. "Wow, that was fun!" said Lily. Bob agreed and said, "Yes, it was! But do you know what a lemon is, Lily?" Lily shook her head. Bob explained, "A lemon is a fruit that is very sour. When you taste it, your face goes like this!" Bob made a funny face, scrunching up his nose and eyes. Lily laughed and tried to make the same face. They both giggled and marched back home, excited to tell their friends about their fun day and the sour lemon puzzle.

Once upon a time, in a small classroom, there was a gray cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play with paper. One day, Fluffy found a pair of scissors. Fluffy wanted to cut the paper into fun shapes. Fluffy's friend, a little girl named Sally, came into the classroom. She saw Fluffy with the scissors and said, "Fluffy, be careful! Scissors can cut you if you're not careful." Fluffy listened to Sally and put the scissors down. Together, Sally and Fluffy cut the paper into many shapes. They made a big mess in the classroom, but they had so much fun. In the end, they cleaned up the mess and put the scissors away. Fluffy learned to be careful when using scissors and had a great day with Sally in the classroom.

One day, a bird named Bob was flying in the sky. He saw a house with a modern roof. Bob thought it was a good place to build his nest. He flew down to take a closer look. "Hello, house!" said Bob. "Can I build my nest on your roof?" The house said, "Yes, Bob, you can build your nest here." So, Bob decided to build his nest on the modern roof. Bob worked hard to make his nest. He knew something big was coming. One day, it started to rain. The rain was heavy, but Bob's nest on the modern roof was safe and dry. The house kept Bob and his nest safe. Bob was happy he decided to build his nest there.

Tom and Lily were playing in the garden. They liked to swing on the big tree. They took turns to push each other. They laughed and had fun. But then, Tom saw a big bottle of juice on the table. He was thirsty. He wanted to drink some juice. He said to Lily, "I want juice. Can you get it for me?" Lily said, "OK. I will get it for you. But you have to wait here. Don't go anywhere." She ran to the table and grabbed the bottle of juice. But when she came back, she saw something scary. Tom was not on the swing. He was hanging from a branch. He had tried to reach the juice, but he had slipped and fallen. He was holding the branch with his hands, but he could not get up. He was scared and cried, "Help! Help!" Lily was worried. She did not know what to do. She shouted, "Tom! Tom! Hold on! I will get help!" She put the bottle of juice on the ground and ran to the house. She found their mom and told her what happened. Mom was worried too. She ran to the garden with Lily. She saw Tom hanging from the branch. She said, "Tom! Don't worry! I will get you down!" She got a ladder and climbed up to the branch. She reached for Tom and pulled him up. She hugged him and said, "You are safe now. You are brave. But you should not do that again. It is dangerous." Tom was happy to see his mom. He said, "I'm sorry, mom. I was thirsty. I wanted juice. But I fell. I was scared." Mom said, "It's OK, Tom. I'm glad you are OK. But next time, ask me for juice. Don't try to get it yourself. And always stay on the swing. It is safer." Tom said, "OK, mom. I will. Thank you, mom. I love you." Mom said, "I love you too, Tom. And you too, Lily. You are a good sister. You helped Tom. You are smart." Lily said, "Thank you, mom. I love you too. And I love Tom. He is a good brother. He is fun." Mom said, "You are both good children. Now, let's go inside and have some juice. And some cookies. How does that sound?" Tom and Lily said, "Yes, please! That sounds good!" They all went inside and had some juice and cookies. They were happy and not worried anymore. They learned a lesson and had a good day.
