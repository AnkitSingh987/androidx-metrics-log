Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Hill

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to pretend he was a brave hero. One day, he saw a big, bright firework in the sky. It was so pretty, but Tim knew fireworks could be dangerous. Tim ran to his mommy and said, "Mommy, I saw a firework! It was big and bright, but I know it's dangerous. What should I do?" Mommy smiled and said, "It's okay, Tim. Fireworks are only dangerous if we touch them or play with them. We can watch them from far away and be safe." So Tim and his mommy sat on a hill and watched the fireworks together. They saw many colors and shapes in the sky. Tim pretended he was a hero, saving the world from the dangerous fireworks. He felt happy and safe with his mommy by his side.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a note on the floor. He did not know what the note said. Tim was stubborn and did not want to ask for help. He tried to read the note by himself but could not. Tim saw his friend, Sally. He asked her a question, "Can you read this note for me?" Sally said, "No, I can't. Let's ask our teacher, Miss Kate." Tim and Sally went to Miss Kate and showed her the note. Miss Kate read the note out loud. It said, "Be kind and help others." Tim learned that it is good to ask for help and help others. From that day, Tim was not stubborn anymore and always asked questions when he needed help.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play in the mud. One day, Spot saw a big bowl of sauce on the table. He wanted to taste the sauce, but it was too high for him to reach. Spot had an idea. He found a chair and pushed it to the table. He climbed on the chair and then onto the table. Spot tasted the sauce and it was so yummy! But, he got sauce all over his face. Just then, Spot's owner, a girl named Lily, came into the room. She saw the mess and knew Spot had been on the table. Lily was not happy, but she couldn't stay mad at cute Spot. She took a cloth and helped wipe the sauce off Spot's face. From that day on, Spot knew he shouldn't climb on the table and Lily always put the sauce away.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big red apple. He was very proud of his apple. He wanted to show it to his friend, Sam. So, he put the apple in his bag and went to find Sam. Tim found Sam playing in the park. Tim said, "Sam, look at my big red apple!" Sam looked at Tim's apple and said, "Wow! That's a nice apple! Can I hold it?" Tim said, "No, I don't want to lose it." They played in the park all day. When it was time to go home, Tim looked in his bag for his apple. But the apple was not there. Tim was very sad. He had lost his big red apple. Sam felt bad too. They both went home with sad faces, wishing they could find the lost apple.

Once upon a time, there was a poor little bird. The bird lived in a cage. The cage was small and not fun. The bird was sad. One day, the bird saw a glow. The glow was big and pretty. The bird wanted to see the glow up close. But the bird was in the cage. The bird could not fly to the glow. The bird was sad and mad. The bird tried to open the cage. The bird pushed and pushed. The cage opened! The bird was happy. The bird flew to the glow. The glow was warm and nice. The bird was free and happy. The bird and the glow were friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was a very lucky girl. Lucy had a magic teaspoon. One day, she found out that her teaspoon could freeze things. Lucy went to her friend Tim's house. She said, "Look, Tim! My teaspoon can freeze things. Let's try it on some water." They found a cup of water and Lucy put her teaspoon in it. The water turned to ice! Tim was amazed. He said, "Wow, Lucy! You are so lucky to have a magic teaspoon!" They had fun freezing things all day. They even froze some juice to make ice pops. Lucy and Tim were very happy with their magic teaspoon.
