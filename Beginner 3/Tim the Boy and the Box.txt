Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, heavy box. Inside the box, there were many invitations. Tim wanted to give the invitations to his friends for his birthday party. Tim went to his friend, Sue. "Sue, watch me open the heavy box," he said. Sue watched as Tim opened the box and gave her an invitation. Sue was happy and said, "Thank you, Tim! I will come to your party." Tim gave invitations to all his friends. They all came to his birthday party. They played games, ate cake, and had fun. At the end of the party, Tim's mom said, "Good job, Tim! You shared your invitations and made your friends happy." The moral of the story is: Sharing makes everyone happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a tiny ant. The ant was walking on the ground. Tim thought ants were boring. He wanted to pinch the ant. "Please don't pinch me," said the ant. "I have a family and friends. They will miss me." Tim thought about it. He did not want the ant to be sad. So, he did not pinch the ant. But he was still bored. Then, the ant had an idea. "Let's play a game," said the ant. Tim and the ant played a fun game. They were both happy, and Tim did not think ants were boring anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat named Tom. Tom liked to play in the house. One day, Tom found a sink in the kitchen. He had never seen a sink before. He wanted to see what it was. Tom jumped into the sink. He was very curious. Suddenly, water came out of the sink. Tom was wet and cold. He was very surprised. He did not know that water could come out of the sink. Tom jumped out of the sink. He shook the water off his fur. He was not cold anymore. Tom learned something new that day. He knew that sinks could surprise him. Tom was a happy cat, and he continued to play in the house.

One day, a boy named Tom went to the store with his mom. They took a big cart to pack all the things they needed to buy. Tom's mom told him, "Tom, stay close to me and help me pack the cart." Tom saw a fine toy on the shelf and wanted to play with it. He forgot what his mom said and went to get the toy. While playing, he got lost in the store. Tom cried and called out, "Mom, where are you?" A store helper found Tom and tried to help him find his mom. They looked and looked, but they could not find her. Tom was very sad and scared. The store helper took Tom to the lost and found area, but his mom was not there. In the end, Tom had to wait at the store until his mom found him. He cried and wished he had listened to his mom. The moral of the story is to always listen to your parents and stay close to them when you are in a big place.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they find a key on the grass. It is shiny and big. "Wow, look at this key!" Tom says. "I wonder what it opens." "Maybe it opens a treasure chest!" Lily says. "Or a magic door!" They run around the park, looking for something to unlock with the key. They see a slide, a swing, a bench, a fountain, but nothing fits the key. They are about to give up, when they see a small house in the corner of the park. It has a red door and a yellow roof. It looks nice and cozy. "Let's try the key here!" Tom says. He runs to the door and puts the key in the lock. It turns with a click. He opens the door and looks inside. He sees a table, a chair, a book, and a toy. He smiles and nods to Lily. "Come on, let's go in!" he says. "This is our secret house!" Lily follows him and nods too. She likes the house. It is warm and bright. They go in and close the door behind them. They sit on the chair and read the book. They play with the toy. They have fun. They are happy. They have found a nice place to play with their key.
