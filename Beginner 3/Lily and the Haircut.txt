Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Haircut

Lily wanted a haircut. Her hair was long and messy. She did not like to brush it. It hurt her head. She asked her mom to take her to the salon. "Are you sure you want a haircut, Lily?" her mom asked. "You have such beautiful hair." "Yes, mom, I want a haircut. I want to look like Anna. She has short and soft hair. She is my best friend." "Okay, Lily, we can go to the salon. But you have to be brave and sit still. The scissors are sharp and they can cut you if you move too much." Lily nodded. She was not afraid of the scissors. She was excited to get a haircut. At the salon, Lily met a nice lady. Her name was Sue. She was the hairdresser. She smiled at Lily and gave her a cape to wear. "Hello, Lily. I'm Sue. I'm going to give you a haircut. What kind of haircut do you want?" she asked. "I want to look like Anna. She has short and soft hair. She is my best friend." Lily said. Sue looked at Lily's hair. She picked up a comb and a pair of scissors. "I recommend that we cut your hair to your shoulders. That way, it will be easier to brush and style. And it will still be soft and pretty. Do you like that idea?" she asked. Lily thought for a moment. She liked her long hair, but she also wanted to look like Anna. She trusted Sue. She was a nice lady. "Yes, I like that idea. Thank you, Sue." Lily said. Sue smiled and started to cut Lily's hair. She was gentle and careful. She talked to Lily and made her laugh. Lily did not feel any pain. She felt happy. When Sue was done, she showed Lily her haircut in the mirror. Lily gasped. She looked like Anna. She had short and soft hair. She loved it. "Wow, Sue, you are amazing. I love my haircut. Thank you so much." Lily said. "You're very welcome, Lily. You look beautiful. I'm glad you like it." Sue said. Lily hugged Sue and thanked her again. Then she ran to her mom and showed her her haircut. Her mom smiled and hugged her. "You look wonderful, Lily. I'm so proud of you. You were very brave and polite. And you have such soft hair." her mom said. Lily smiled and hugged her mom. She was happy. She had a haircut. She looked like Anna. She was her best friend.

One day, a boy named Tim found a long box under his bed. He opened it and saw a game of chess inside. He did not know how to play, so he asked his Dad to teach him. Dad said, "Let's play chess, Tim! I will show you how. But first, let's set up the board." They put the pieces on the board and started to play. Tim tried to move his piece, but it was hard. He pushed too much and it made a snap sound. Tim was sad and asked, "Did I break it, Dad?" Dad looked and said, "No, Tim. It's okay. Let's keep playing." They played for a long time and Tim learned how to move the pieces. He liked the game of chess and played with Dad every day.

Once upon a time, there was a small, humble boy. He was walking through a field one day when he noticed something glinting in a patch of clover. He knelt down and saw that it was a tiny jewel! He reached out to take it, but then he frowned. He remembered what his mother had told him - he should not take things that did not belong to him. The boy got up and continued walking. A few minutes later, he spotted an old wooden box, tucked away in some tall grass. It was too heavy for him to carry, so with a little grunt and a lot of effort, he opened the box. Inside, the jewels glistened and gleamed - more jewels than he had ever seen! The boy collected them, and carefully closed the lid. As he picked up the box, he remembered his mother's words. He thought of the little jewel he had wanted to take - and his humble heart was glad he had made the right choice. The boy took the box home and told his mother what he had found. He gave her the jewels and smiled, for he had found reward for his honest behavior. His mother smiled in return and said, "My son, I am so proud of you. Honesty really is the best policy!"
