Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Surf

One day, a boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. They saw a man on a surf. The man was not ordinary. He could lean on the surf and do fun tricks. Tim wanted to try too. Tim said, "Mom, can I try the surf?" Mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Tim got on the surf and tried to lean like the man. But he fell in the water. Tim was sad. Then, a big fish came and said, "I can help you lean on the surf." The fish pushed Tim up and helped him lean. Tim was happy and said, "Thank you, big fish!" Now, Tim could surf like the man and had a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max liked to walk in the park every day. He liked to play with his friends, a small cat and a tiny bird. They liked to play and run in the grass. They had so much fun together. One day, while they were playing, they saw a high tree. The tiny bird said, "I can fly high up in the tree!" The small cat said, "I can climb high up in the tree!" Max the big dog said, "I can only walk, but I will watch you from the ground." The tiny bird flew high up in the tree. The small cat climbed high up in the tree. Max the big dog watched them from the ground, wagging his tail. They played all day long. When the sun went down, they all walked home together, happy and tired from their fun day of play.

Once upon a time, there was a big, rude bird. The bird lived high up in the sky. The bird liked to tease and laugh at the animals down below. One day, the bird took a small key. The key could unlock a special box. Inside the box was yummy food for all the animals. The bird flew away with the key and hid it in the sky. All the animals were sad. They wanted the key to unlock the box. A brave little mouse had an idea. The mouse climbed a tall tree to reach the sky. The mouse found the key and brought it back down. The animals unlocked the box and shared the yummy food. The rude bird saw this and felt sorry. The bird promised to be kind and never tease the animals again.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a small bird named Tim. Tim was a happy bird who loved to fly and sing. The forest was a safe place for Tim and his friends. One day, while Tim was flying, he saw his friend, Tom the squirrel, crying. Tim flew down to Tom and asked, "Why are you sad, Tom?" Tom said, "I can't find my way home. I'm lost!" Tim wanted to help his friend, so he said, "Don't worry, Tom. I will help you find your way home." They started to look for Tom's home together. As they walked, a big storm came. Rain started to fall and thunder began to strike. Tim and Tom were scared, but they knew they had to keep going. They held hands and walked through the forest. Finally, they saw Tom's home. They were so happy! They ran inside to be safe and dry. Tim said, "We did it, Tom! We found your home!" Tom hugged Tim and said, "Thank you, Tim. You are a great friend." From that day on, Tim and Tom were the best of friends. They played and laughed together in the safe forest, and they always helped each other when they needed it. And they lived happily ever after.

Cindy was very happy. She had been looking for something special, and today she had found it. It was an impressive screen. She carried the screen carefully back home. When she got home, she showed everyone the screen. They were all very impressed. Cindy felt very proud. Cindy set up the screen so she could play her favorite TV show. Everyone gathered to watch it with her. They watched cartoons and had lots of fun. Cindy and her family enjoyed the screen a lot. They were very impressed with the picture it made. It was much better than watching on a smaller screen. Cindy was so glad to have found her special screen. She was the happiest she had been in a long time.
