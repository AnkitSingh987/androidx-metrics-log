Category: Beginner
Title: The Race and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a big race. Many kids wanted to win the prize. Tim was a boy who liked to win a lot. He was competitive. Tim said, "I will take the prize." The day of the race came. All the kids lined up to run. Tim was sure he would win. He looked at the other kids and thought they were not fast. The race started, and Tim ran very fast. But then, something unexpected happened. A small dog joined the race. The dog was very fast too. It ran next to Tim. Tim did not think a dog could win the race. But the dog crossed the finish line first. The dog took the prize! Tim learned that anyone, even a dog, can win if they try hard.

One day, a big bear and a small bunny went to the park to play. The sun was shining and the sky was very blue. They saw a big tree with a swing. The bear and the bunny wanted to play on the swing. The bear said, "I am big, so I will weigh the swing down. You are small, so you can sit on top." The bunny thought this was a fair idea, so they tried it. The bear sat on the swing and the bunny sat on the bear. They had fun swinging back and forth. Then, a little bird came and said, "I want to play too!" The bear and the bunny looked at the bird and said, "You can sit on the swing with us!" But when the bird sat on the swing, something unexpected happened. The swing went up, up, up into the sky! The bear, the bunny, and the bird were surprised. They laughed and laughed as they flew high above the park. The three friends had a great time playing together in the sky.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big screen in his room. He liked to watch his favorite show on the screen. One day, Tim's screen was empty. There was no show to watch. Tim was sad. Tim's mom saw that he was sad. She said, "Let's go to the park and play." Tim liked the park. He played with his friends and had fun. After playing, Tim was tired. He wanted to rest. Tim and his mom went home. When they got home, the screen was not empty anymore. Tim's favorite show was back on the screen. Tim was happy. He watched his show and then went to rest.

One day, a kind boy named Tim went outside to play. He saw dark clouds in the sky. Tim was scared of lightning, but he wanted to play. He decided to be brave and play anyway. While he was playing, he found a big box. Tim was curious and wanted to see what was inside. He opened the box to reveal a magic umbrella. The umbrella could protect him from the lightning. Tim was very happy. As the storm came, Tim used the magic umbrella. The lightning could not hurt him. But then, something unexpected happened. The umbrella started to glow and it took Tim up into the sky. He was safe from the lightning and he could see the whole world. Tim learned that being brave can lead to amazing adventures.

Lily liked to mix things in her big bowl. She mixed water and flour and salt and oil. She made a sticky dough. She wanted to make a cake for her pet cat, Mimi. But Mimi did not like the dough. She sniffed it and hissed. She scratched the bowl and spilled the dough on the floor. Lily was sad and angry. She shouted at Mimi. "Bad cat! You ruined my cake! Go away!" Mimi ran away and hid under the bed. She was scared and hurt. She did not understand why Lily was so harsh. Lily's mom came and saw the mess. She was not happy. She asked Lily what happened. Lily told her about the cake and the cat. Mom sighed and hugged Lily. "I know you love Mimi, but you can't make a cake for her with flour and oil. Cats don't eat that. They need meat and fish and milk. And you can't yell at Mimi when she doesn't like something. She is your pet and your friend. You have to be kind and gentle with her." Lily felt sorry. She said sorry to mom and to Mimi. She helped mom clean the floor. Then she went to find Mimi. She found her under the bed. She coaxed her out with a piece of tuna. She stroked her fur and said sorry again. "Mimi, I'm sorry. I love you. You are my best pet. Will you forgive me?" Mimi purred and rubbed her head against Lily's hand. She licked the tuna and looked at Lily with her green eyes. She forgave her. Lily smiled and hugged Mimi. They were friends again.
