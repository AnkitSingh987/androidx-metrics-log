Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Boat

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big scale in his house. He saw his mom put things on the scale to see how heavy they were. Tim thought the scale was boring, so he went to find something more fun to play with. In his room, Tim found a big piece of paper. He decided to fold the paper to make a fun toy. He folded and folded, and soon, he had a paper boat. Tim was very happy with his boat and wanted to show his mom. Tim took his paper boat to the kitchen where his mom was using the scale. He put the boat on the scale, and it was very light. His mom smiled and said, "Good job, Tim! You made a fun toy from a boring scale." Tim felt proud and played with his paper boat all day.

Once upon a time, a little dog named Max lived in a small house. Max liked to play in the yard, but he didn't like the dry grass. It made him sneeze. One day, he saw a big hole in the fence. He thought it was an escape to a new place. Max went through the hole and found a pond with water. He was so happy! He jumped in the water and splashed around. He didn't want to disturb anyone, so he played quietly. The water was cool and wet, not like the dry grass at home. After a while, Max heard his mom calling him. He knew it was time to go back. He went through the hole in the fence and ran to his mom. She was happy to see him but worried. Max promised not to escape again. From then on, they played together in the yard, and Max didn't mind the dry grass so much.

Once upon a time, there was a little bug who loved to crawl. He was very enthusiastic about his motion. One day, he met a big bug who also liked to crawl. "Hi, big bug!" said the little bug. "I like to crawl! Do you want to crawl with me?" "Sure, little bug!" said the big bug. "Let's crawl together." So, the little bug and the big bug crawled together. They crawled fast and slow, up and down. They had lots of fun. But then, they saw a big puddle. The little bug was scared. "Big bug, I don't want to crawl through the puddle. It looks deep and dark." But the big bug said, "Don't worry, little bug. I will crawl first, and you can follow me." The big bug crawled into the puddle. He was having fun. But when the little bug tried to crawl in, the puddle was too deep for him. He got stuck and could not get out. The big bug tried to help, but it was too late. The moral of the story is: always be careful when following others, because what is easy for them might not be easy for you.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. One day, Tim's mom gave him a tray with his lunch on it. Tim looked at the tray and said, "No, I don't want this." His mom was worried. She didn't know why Tim refused his lunch. Tim's mom asked, "Why don't you want to eat your lunch?" Tim said, "There's a bug on my tray!" His mom looked at the tray and saw a little bug. She was surprised. She didn't expect to see a bug on the tray. Tim's mom took the bug outside and let it go. She gave Tim a new tray with his lunch on it. Tim was happy to eat his lunch now. The bug was happy to be outside. And Tim's mom was not worried anymore. They all had a good day.

Once upon a time, in a small house with a red roof, there lived a funny cat named Tom. Tom loved to play and joke with his best friend, a colorful bird named Sally. They had lots of fun together every day. One sunny day, Tom and Sally were playing on the roof. They laughed and joked as they ran around. The sun made the roof warm and the sky was very blue. Tom and Sally were very happy. As they played, a big wind came and blew Sally's colorful feathers all around. Tom and Sally laughed even more as they tried to catch the feathers. They kept joking and playing until the sun went down. Then, they went inside the house, ready for another fun day tomorrow.
