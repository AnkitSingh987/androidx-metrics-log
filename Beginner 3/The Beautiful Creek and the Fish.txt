Category: Beginner
Title: The Beautiful Creek and the Fish

Once, there was a beautiful creek. It had many pretty fish. The fish liked to swim and play in the water. They had a lot of fun together. One day, a big fish came to the creek. It wanted to have all the water and the pretty rocks. The big fish was not nice. The pretty fish were sad and did not want to play. The pretty fish had a plan. They all swam together and pushed the big fish away. The big fish went to another creek. The pretty fish were happy again. They swam and played in their beautiful creek.

Once upon a time, in a big blue sea, there lived a little red crab named Carl. Carl had many friends in the sea, like fish, turtles, and even a big, friendly shark. They all liked to play together and have fun. One sunny day, Carl and his friends decided to march on the beach. They all lined up, one by one, and started to march. "Left, right, left, right," said Carl as they marched together. They were having so much fun. As they marched, they saw something new on the beach. It was green and slimy. "Ew, that's disgusting!" said Carl's fish friend, Fin. "What is it?" asked Carl. "I don't know," said Fin, "but let's stay away from it." So, Carl and his friends marched away from the disgusting green thing and continued to have fun on the beach.

Once upon a time, there was a little pink cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to climb trees and play with her friends in the forest. One day, she saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. As Kitty started to climb the tree, she met a squirrel named Sam. Sam was holding a stick, which he used as a weapon to protect his nuts. "Hi, Kitty! Do you want to play with me?" asked Sam. Kitty smiled and said, "Yes, Sam! Let's play together!" So, Kitty and Sam played all day long, climbing trees and having fun with their friends. They were very happy and became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a small, smart rabbit named Remy. Remy had a big friend named Ellie the elephant. They liked to play together. One day, Remy had a fun idea. He wanted to scare Ellie for fun. Remy went to find Ellie near the big white tree. He hid behind the tree and waited for Ellie to come. When Ellie came close, Remy jumped out and said, "Boo!" Ellie got very scared and her big foot stepped on Remy by accident. "Oh no! Remy, I am so sorry!" cried Ellie. But it was too late. Remy was hurt and could not play anymore. Ellie was very sad and missed her friend. The other animals in the forest learned to not scare their friends for fun. The moral of the story is to be careful when playing and not to scare your friends, because it can lead to bad things happening.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and run in the park with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. One day, they found a paper on the ground. Spot picked it up with his mouth and brought it to Tim. Tim looked at the paper and said, "Let's examine this paper, Spot!" They both looked at the paper, but they did not know what it said. Tim decided to take the paper home and show it to his mom. On the way home, it started to rain very hard. The paper got all wet and Tim could not read it anymore. Tim and Spot were sad. They never found out what the paper said. The end.

Lila was sad. She did not have any friends at the new school. She missed her old school and her old friends. She sat on the bench and looked at the other kids playing. She wished she could join them, but she was too shy. Then she saw a boy with curly hair and a big smile. He came to her and said, "Hi, I'm Sam. Do you want to play with me and my friends?" Lila was surprised. She did not know what to say. She felt her face turn red. She put her thumb in her mouth. It was a habit she had when she was nervous. Sam saw her thumb and said, "That's okay. I used to suck my thumb too. It makes you feel warm and safe, right?" Lila nodded. She felt a little less scared. She took her thumb out of her mouth and said, "Yes, it does. But my mom says I should stop. She says it's not good for my teeth." Sam shrugged. "My mom said the same thing. But I stopped when I was ready. You will too. Don't worry about it. Everyone has something they do to feel better. Some kids have blankets, some have toys, some have songs. What matters is that you are happy and kind." Lila smiled. She liked Sam. He was nice and funny. He did not make fun of her thumb. He accepted her as she was. She said, "Thank you, Sam. You are very kind. I would like to play with you and your friends." Sam grinned. He took her hand and said, "Great! Come on, let's go. We are playing tag. You can be on my team." Lila felt happy. She had made a new friend. She ran with Sam to join the other kids. She felt warm and safe. She did not need her thumb anymore. She had Sam.
