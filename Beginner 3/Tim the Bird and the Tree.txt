Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim lived in a big tree. One day, the sky was gray and Tim felt miserable. He did not want to fly or sing. A strong wind came and blew the tree. The tree caught fire. Tim saw the fire and was scared. The fire made lots of ash. Tim wanted to protect his home, the tree. Tim called his friends, the ants. The ants came with water. They worked together to put out the fire. The tree was safe. Tim was happy and not miserable anymore. He thanked the ants for helping him protect his tree.

Lily and Ben like to play with blocks. They make big structures with different shapes and colors. Sometimes they make houses, sometimes they make towers, sometimes they make bridges. They are proud of their structures and show them to Mom and Dad. One day, they hear a loud noise outside. They look out the window and see a big truck. The truck has a big arm that can grab things and break them. The truck is coming to their street. Lily and Ben are scared. They think the truck will break their structures. They run to their blocks and grab as many as they can. They decide to march to the backyard and hide their blocks there. They hold hands and march like soldiers. They say, "We are strong, we are brave, we will save our blocks!" They reach the backyard and find a good spot to put their blocks. They start to build a new structure, but they are not happy. They miss their old structures. They wonder if the truck will break them. They hear the truck stop in front of their house. They hear Mom and Dad talk to the truck driver. They hear the truck driver say, "I'm sorry, I have the wrong address. I'm looking for number 12, not number 10. I'm here to demolish an old shed, not a nice house. I'll go now, bye!" Lily and Ben are relieved. They run back to the front yard and see their structures are still there. They hug their structures and say, "We love you, we are sorry, we are glad you are safe!" They see Mom and Dad smiling at them. Mom and Dad say, "You are very brave and smart, but you don't have to worry. The truck was not here to break your structures. It was a mistake. Your structures are safe and beautiful. We are proud of you!" Lily and Ben are happy. They say, "Thank you, Mom and Dad! We are sorry we ran away. We were scared. Can we have a snack?" Mom and Dad say, "Of course, you can have a snack. How about some cookies and milk?" Lily and Ben say, "Yes, please! We love cookies and milk!" They go inside and have a snack. They share their cookies and milk with their structures. They say, "You are our friends, you are sweet, you are not sour!"

Once upon a time, there was a little infant named Tim. Tim was a very lucky baby. He had a big toy box full of fun toys to play with every day. One day, Tim saw a new toy in his toy box. It was a big, red ball. Tim wanted to play with the ball, but he did not know how. So, he asked his mom for help. "Mom, how do I play with the ball?" Tim said. His mom smiled and said, "You can try to roll the ball, Tim." Tim tried to roll the ball, and he was very good at it. He felt happy and lucky to have such a fun new toy to play with.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that he loved very much. It was a red car, but it was very dirty. Tim played with his car all day and night. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, asked if he could lend the car. Tim thought about it and said yes. Sam was very happy and promised to give it back the next day. Tim knew that his car had value, and he trusted his friend. The next day, Sam came back with the car. But the car was even more dirty and broken. Tim was very sad because his car was not the same. Sam said he was sorry, but Tim's car was not fixed. From that day on, Tim learned to be more careful with his toys and who he lent them to.
