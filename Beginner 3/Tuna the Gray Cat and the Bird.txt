Category: Beginner
Title: Tuna the Gray Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a gray cat named Tuna. Tuna lived in a small house with a boy named Tim. Tuna loved to play with Tim and hold his toys with her paws. One day, Tuna saw a bird outside the window. She wanted to catch it, but she could not open the door. Tuna went to Tim and said, "Tim, can you help me open the door? I want to play with the bird." Tim looked at Tuna and said, "Okay, Tuna. But you must be nice to the bird." Tuna nodded her head, and Tim opened the door. Tuna ran outside and tried to hold the bird gently with her paws. The bird flew away, but Tuna was happy she got to play outside. At the end of the day, Tuna and Tim went back inside the house. They were both tired from playing. Tuna curled up on Tim's lap, and they both took a nap. They had a fun day together.

One day, a little girl named Lily found an adorable kitty. The kitty was small and had soft fur. Lily loved the kitty very much. She wanted to show the kitty to her friend, Tom. "Tom, look at my kitty! Isn't she cute?" Lily asked. Tom looked at the kitty and smiled. "Yes, she is very cute! I know a game we can play with her." Lily and Tom played with the kitty, and they were happy. They gave the kitty a tray with food and water. The kitty ate and drank, and then she did something unexpected. The kitty jumped on the tray and started to dance! Lily and Tom were surprised. They laughed and clapped their hands. The dancing kitty made them very happy. And from that day on, they knew that their adorable kitty was special.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tom. Tom liked to play with his toy car. One day, Tom saw something on the ground. It was big and green. Tom thought it was disgusting. He did not want to touch it. But he knew it would be important later. Tom went to his mom and told her about the big, green thing. His mom said not to touch it. She said it could be bad. Tom listened to his mom. He went back to play with his toy car. But he kept looking at the big, green thing. He knew something would happen. The next day, Tom saw a big bug near the green thing. The bug was eating it. The bug got bigger and bigger. Tom was scared. He told his mom about the bug. His mom came and saw the bug. She knew what to do. She got a big net and caught the bug. Tom was happy. He knew the green thing was important.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big egg. Tim liked to kick his egg. He would kick it all day long. His friends liked to watch him kick the egg. They thought it was fun. One day, a new kid named Sam came to play. Sam saw Tim and his friends with the egg. "Can I play too?" Sam asked. Tim said, "Yes, you can kick the egg too!" Sam kicked the egg, and it went very far. Tim and his friends clapped for Sam. They were all happy. Now, Tim and Sam were both popular. They played with the egg every day. All the kids liked to watch them kick the egg. They had lots of fun together. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a hairy dog named Bob. Bob loved to play with his toy rocket. One sunny day, Bob and his friend, a little boy named Tim, went to the park to play. Tim set the rocket on the ground and said, "Let's see how high it can go!" Bob wagged his tail and barked, "Yes, let's go!" Tim pushed a button and whoosh, the rocket went up, up, up in the sky! Bob and Tim laughed and clapped their hands. The rocket came back down and landed near Bob and Tim. They played with the rocket all day long. When the sun went down, they went home, tired but happy. The hairy dog and the little boy had the best day ever.
