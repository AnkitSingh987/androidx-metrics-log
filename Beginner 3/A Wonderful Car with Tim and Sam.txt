Category: Beginner
Title: A Wonderful Car with Tim and Sam

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that was his favorite. It was a different color than all the other cars. He played with it every day. One day, Tim's friend Sam came over to play. Sam saw the different toy car and wanted to play with it too. Tim didn't want to share, but his mom said, "Tim, you need to share your toys. Don't spoil the fun for Sam." So Tim and Sam played with the toy car together. They took turns making the car go fast and making car noises. "Vroom, vroom!" said Tim. "Beep, beep!" said Sam. They laughed and had lots of fun. As they played, Tim and Sam learned new words from each other. They were happy to share and play together. In the end, they both had a great time and became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was scared of the dark. One night, Lily heard a noise in her room. She hid under her bed covers and waited. A small voice said, "Hi, I am a friendly monster. Don't be scared." Lily peeked out of her covers and saw a tiny, blue monster. His name was Max. Max was patient and kind. He wanted to help Lily not be scared anymore. Max said, "Let's be friends." Lily agreed. Max suggested, "Let's count to ten together." They counted together, and Lily felt better. Now, Lily was not scared of the dark anymore because she had a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big, tough ant. The ant liked to carry things. One day, the ant found a shiny coin. The ant wanted to carry the coin to his home. So, the ant started to carry the coin. As the ant carried the coin, he met a tiny bug. The tiny bug also liked to carry things. The tiny bug wanted to help the tough ant carry the shiny coin. So, the ant and the tiny bug carried the coin together. As they carried the coin, they saw a big hill. The ant and the tiny bug thought they could not carry the coin up the big hill. But then, a kind bird saw them. The kind bird helped the ant and the tiny bug carry the coin up the big hill. They all became good friends and the ant, the tiny bug, and the kind bird carried the shiny coin to the tough ant's home together.

Once upon a time there was a little twig. He was so small and felt like he would never grow. One day the twig was walking along and he noticed the sun. The sun was so red and warm. The twig said to the sun, “I wish I could be big and strong like you.” The sun smiled and said, “Don’t worry twig, one day you will grow. Just keep believing and stay in the sunshine.” The twig was so happy, he thanked the sun and kept walking. He found a patch of soil and decided to stay there. He eagerly soaked up the sun’s warmth and waited. Little by little the twig began to grow. His roots stretched down deep and his branches reached up high. He was so excited to become big and strong just like the sun. Finally, the twig was a beautiful, red tree. He waved his branches in the wind and smiled, for he had done what the sun said he could. The twig was so proud of himself and was happy to have grown.

One day, a wise old cat named Tom saw a big bin. He was hungry and wanted to find food. He jumped on the bin and looked inside. There was a lot of food in the bin. Tom was happy. Tom's friend, a little mouse named Jerry, saw him. Jerry said, "Tom, we must hurry! A dog is coming!" Tom heard the dog's loud bark and knew Jerry was right. They had to be fast. Tom and Jerry ran away from the bin. The dog chased them, but they were too quick. They hid behind a tree and waited. The dog could not find them. Tom and Jerry were safe. They laughed and went back to the bin to eat the food.
