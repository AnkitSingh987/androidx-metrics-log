Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Tree

One day, a little girl named Lily went on a journey. She walked with her mom and dad. They were proud of her because she was a big girl now. They walked and walked, and the sun was shining on them. Then, they saw a big tree. Lily's dad said, "Look, Lily! There is a surprise behind the tree." Lily ran to the tree and saw a big box. She was so happy and excited. Lily opened the box to reveal a pretty doll. She said, "Thank you, mom and dad!" They all smiled and hugged each other. The journey was fun, and Lily loved her new doll.

Once upon a time, there was a duck. The duck liked to whistle. Every day, the duck would whistle a happy song. One day, the duck met a friend. The friend was a reliable dog. They liked to play together. The duck would whistle, and the dog would wag its tail. The duck and the dog were very happy. They played every day. They were the best of friends. The duck's whistle and the dog's wagging tail made everyone smile.

One day, a little fish was swimming in the sea. The fish was very happy and liked to relax in the water. The fish had many friends who liked to play with him. One day, while the fish was relaxing, a big wave came and pushed the fish to the shore. The fish felt helpless on the sand. He could not swim back to the sea. The fish was very sad and scared. Just then, a kind little girl saw the helpless fish on the shore. She wanted to help the fish. The girl picked up the fish and put him back in the water. The fish was so happy, he jumped and splashed. The girl and the fish became best friends. They played together every day, and the fish was never helpless again.

Once upon a time, there was a little pig named Pinky. Pinky lived on a farm with his friends. They liked to play in the mud and eat yummy food. One day, Pinky saw some dirty hay on the ground. He thought it would be fun to roll in it. Pinky rolled and rolled in the dirty hay. He was having so much fun! His friends saw him and wanted to play too. They all rolled in the dirty hay together. They laughed and played until they were very tired. Then, Pinky realized he was very dirty from the hay. He looked at his friends and they were dirty too. They all decided to go to the pond to wash off. They splashed in the water until they were clean again. Pinky and his friends were happy they had a fun day playing in the dirty hay.

Tom and Mia are playing with their toys in the living room. Tom has a big truck and Mia has a doll. Tom likes to make loud noises with his truck and pretend it is crashing into things. Mia likes to dress up her doll and talk to it. "Vroom, vroom, crash!" Tom says, as he drives his truck into Mia's doll. "Your doll is in trouble, Mia! She needs help!" "Hey, stop it, Tom!" Mia says, as she picks up her doll. "You are hurting my doll! She is not a toy for your truck!" Tom laughs and pokes Mia's doll with his finger. "Poke, poke, poke! Your doll is silly, Mia! She does not feel anything!" Mia gets angry and pokes Tom back. "Poke, poke, poke! You are silly, Tom! You are mean to my doll!" Tom and Mia start to poke each other harder and harder. They poke each other's faces, arms, legs, and bellies. They poke so hard that they start to cry. "Ow, ow, ow! Stop poking me, Mia!" Tom says, as he rubs his eyes. "Ow, ow, ow! Stop poking me, Tom!" Mia says, as she holds her stomach. Mom hears their cries and runs into the living room. She sees Tom and Mia poking each other and crying. She is worried and angry. "What is going on here?" Mom says, as she picks up Tom and Mia. "Why are you poking each other? This is not a game! This is an emergency!" Tom and Mia stop poking and look at Mom. They are scared and sorry. "We are sorry, Mom," Tom and Mia say, as they hug Mom. "We were playing with our toys and we got mad. We did not mean to poke each other so hard." Mom hugs Tom and Mia back. She is glad they are sorry, but she is still upset. "You should not poke each other, ever," Mom says, as she checks their faces, arms, legs, and bellies. "Poking can hurt and make you cry. Poking can also make you sick or bleed. Poking is not fun. Poking is bad." Tom and Mia nod and say, "We know, Mom. We will not poke each other again. We will be nice to each other and to our toys." Mom smiles and says, "That's good, Tom and Mia. I love you both. Now, let's go to the kitchen and have some juice and cookies. But first, wash your hands. You don't want to poke your mouth with dirty fingers." Tom and Mia say, "Okay, Mom. We love you too." They run to the bathroom and wash their hands. Then they go to the kitchen and have some juice and cookies. They eat and drink fast, because they are hungry and thirsty. They also talk and laugh fast, because they are happy and sorry. They do not poke each other anymore. They are good friends.
