Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Grandma

One day, a little girl named Lily went to her grandma's house. She saw a big curtain in the living room. Lily thought the curtain was pretty. She wanted to play with it. So, she ran behind the curtain and hid. Her grandma came into the room and said, "Where is Lily?" Lily giggled and jumped out from behind the curtain. She said, "Here I am, Grandma!" Her grandma smiled and said, "I found you! Let's go make a snack together." They went to the kitchen and made a spicy snack. Lily tasted it and said, "Yum! This is spicy, but I like it!" Her grandma smiled and said, "I'm glad you like it, Lily." They both laughed and ate their spicy snack together.

Once upon a time, in a big yellow house, there lived a little girl named Mia. She had a cute cat named Fluffy. They loved to play together all day. Mia and Fluffy were best friends. One sunny day, Mia and Fluffy were playing in the garden. Fluffy saw a butterfly and started to chase it. Fluffy ran too fast and got a small cut on her paw. Mia saw this and began to panic. She didn't know what to do. Just then, a kind bird flew down from a tree. The bird saw Fluffy's cut and knew how to help. The bird quickly flew away and returned with a small leaf. To Mia's surprise, the leaf had magic powers! The bird gently put the leaf on Fluffy's cut, and it healed right away. Mia and Fluffy were so happy, and they thanked the kind bird. From that day on, they all became great friends and had many more fun adventures together.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the mall with his mom. Tim saw a big mess on the floor. He said, "Mom, look! We need to help make it tidy!" Tim and his mom started to clean up the mess. A lady who worked at the mall saw them and said, "Thank you for helping! You are very kind." Tim and his mom felt happy. Just then, a big robot came out from behind a store. The robot said, "I made that mess. I am sorry. I wanted to help, but I made a mess instead." Tim, his mom, and the lady all laughed. They helped the robot learn how to clean up and be tidy. From that day on, the robot helped keep the mall clean and everyone was happy.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a box. The box was ugly and old. Sue opened the box and saw something soft. It was velvet! The velvet was red and pretty. Sue had an idea. She wanted to show her friend Tom. Sue went to Tom's house. "Look, Tom!" she said, "I found velvet!" Tom was not happy. "I found velvet too," he said, "but mine is ugly and green." Sue and Tom did not know what to do. They both wanted pretty velvet. That night, Sue had a dream. In the dream, she saw a big tree. The tree had red and green leaves. When Sue woke up, she knew what to do. She ran to Tom's house. "Tom, I have an idea!" she said. Sue and Tom put the red and green velvet on the tree. It looked beautiful! The tree was not ugly anymore. The red and green velvet made the tree look like a big, pretty flower. Sue and Tom were happy. They learned that together, they could make something beautiful.

One day, a big rock sat on the floor. It was a rude rock. It did not like to play with the other rocks. It would say, "Go away! I don't want to play!" One small rock wanted to be friends with the big rock. The small rock said, "Please, let's play together! I don't want to be alone." The big rock said, "No! I don't want to play with you!" But then, something strange happened. The big rock started to roll away. It rolled and rolled, and it could not stop! The small rock saw the big rock rolling away and felt sad. It wanted to help the big rock. The small rock rolled after the big rock and bumped into it. The big rock stopped rolling. The big rock looked at the small rock and said, "Thank you for helping me. I'm sorry for being rude. Let's be friends and play together." From that day on, the big rock and the small rock played together and were very happy. They were the best of friends, and the big rock was never rude again.
