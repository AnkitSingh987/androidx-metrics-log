Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Slide

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big slide and wanted to play on it. Tim was a bit pale because he was not feeling well, but he still wanted to have fun. Tim went up to the slide and demanded, "Let me play on the slide!" His mom said, "Okay, but be careful." Tim climbed up the steps and sat down at the top of the slide. He slid down the slide very fast and landed on the ground with a big smile. Tim played on the slide all day long, and he felt better. His mom was happy to see him having fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim all day long in the big blue sea. One day, he swam very fast and went far away from his home. Fin started to feel worried. He missed his family and friends. As he swam, he took big breaths with his lungs so he could swim faster to find his way back home. After swimming for a long time, Fin saw his family and friends. They were so happy to see him. Fin was not worried anymore. He learned to stay close to his home while swimming and always be with his family and friends. And they all swam happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to play with his toys. One day, he wanted to find new toys to play with. His mom told him to go to the attic and choose a toy from there. Tim went up the stairs very slow. He was a little scared, but he wanted to find a new toy. When he got to the attic, he saw many toys. There were cars, dolls, and balls. Tim looked at all the toys and then he chose a big red ball. He took the big red ball and went back down the stairs. He was very happy with his new toy. Tim played with the big red ball all day. He would throw it and kick it. And that is how Tim found a new toy to play with in the attic.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play with their dog, Spot. Spot is a loyal dog. He always follows them and protects them from danger. One day, Anna and Ben want to go to the park. They ask their mom if they can take Spot with them. Mom says yes, but they have to brush Spot first. Spot has a lot of fur and it gets dirty and tangled. Anna and Ben grab the brush and go to Spot. They start to brush his fur, but Spot does not like it. He wiggles and barks and tries to run away. Anna and Ben complain. "Spot, stop moving! You are making it hard for us to brush you!" Anna says. "Spot, be quiet! You are hurting our ears with your barking!" Ben says. Spot does not listen. He thinks brushing is boring and painful. He wants to go to the park and have fun. Mom hears the noise and comes to see what is going on. She sees Anna and Ben struggling with Spot. She smiles and says, "I know Spot does not like brushing, but it is good for him. It makes his fur shiny and healthy. It also shows him that you love him and care for him. He is a loyal dog, and he deserves your kindness and patience." Anna and Ben think about what mom says. They realize that mom is right. They love Spot and they want him to be happy and well. They decide to try again. They speak to Spot in a gentle and calm voice. They praise him and pet him when he stays still. They brush his fur slowly and carefully. Spot feels the difference. He relaxes and stops wiggling and barking. He even licks Anna and Ben's faces to show his gratitude. Soon, Spot's fur is smooth and shiny. Anna and Ben are proud of their work. They hug Spot and say, "Good boy, Spot! You are a loyal dog, and we are loyal to you. We are sorry for complaining. We are ready to go to the park now. Do you want to come with us?" Spot wags his tail and barks happily. He does want to go to the park with them. He follows them to the door and waits for mom to put on his leash. He knows that Anna and Ben are his best friends, and he will always be loyal to them. The moral of the story is: Be kind and patient with your loyal friends, even when they do something you don't like. They will appreciate your love and care, and they will be loyal to you in return.
