Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Bird

One day, a boy named Tom found a coin on the ground. He picked it up and put it in his pocket. He was happy to find the coin. Tom saw a bird in a tree. The bird could whistle a nice song. Tom tried to whistle like the bird, but he could not. He asked the bird to teach him how to whistle. The bird was rude and did not want to help Tom. Tom was sad, but he did not give up. He kept trying to whistle. After a long time, he learned how to whistle like the bird. Tom was happy and proud of himself.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play dress up. They have many hats, coats, shoes and scarves. They can be pirates, astronauts, doctors or anything they want. One day, they decide to dress up as lions. They find some yellow coats and brown boots. They make some ears and tails with paper and tape. They put them on and roar. "Look at us, we are fierce lions!" Anna says. "Let's go and scare Mommy!" Ben says. They sneak out of their room and go to the kitchen. Mommy is making lunch. She does not see them. "Roar!" Anna and Ben shout. Mommy turns around and sees them. She is very shocked. She drops the spoon and jumps. "Ah!" Mommy screams. Anna and Ben laugh. They run to Mommy and hug her. "Sorry, Mommy, we are just playing dress up!" Anna says. "We are not real lions, we are your cubs!" Ben says. Mommy smiles and kisses them. "You are very cute lions, but also very naughty!" Mommy says. She tickles them and they giggle. "Come on, let's have some lunch. You must be hungry after all that roaring!" Mommy says. They take off their lion costumes and sit at the table. They eat some soup and bread. They are happy. They love to play dress up.

Once upon a time, there was a humble cat. The cat had long hair. The cat liked to seat on a big chair. One day, the cat went to find the chair, but it was not there. The cat was sad and looked for the chair. The cat looked in the house. The cat looked in the yard. The cat did not find the chair. Then, the cat saw a little mouse. The mouse had the chair! The cat was surprised. The mouse said, "I took the chair to clean it for you." The cat and the mouse became friends. They shared the chair. They played and had fun. The cat was happy to have a new friend. The humble cat with long hair learned that sharing is good. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Amy. She was a very attractive girl with a big smile. Amy loved to eat pasta, and she ate it every day. One sunny day, Amy was eating her pasta in the park. She was very happy. But then, a big bird came and took her pasta away. Amy was sad and wanted her pasta back. Amy ran after the bird and asked for her pasta back. The bird saw how sad Amy was and gave her pasta back. Amy was happy again. She went back to the park and finished her pasta. And they all lived happily ever after.

Sam was a very flexible boy. One day, he wanted to paint something special. So he went to his room and searched for his paint box. He found it and brought it to the kitchen table. Sam planned to paint all day. But then he remembered it was almost the end of the week. He couldn't paint all day because he had work to do. He was sad, but he stayed flexible. So Sam decided to paint for only a few hours. He was careful and painted slowly. Then he put his paint box away until the next week. With his paint box put away, Sam was happy and excited for the next week.

One sunny day, a little boy named Tom found a pair of sunglasses. They were ancient sunglasses. They were big and had many colors. Tom liked the sunglasses and put them on. He felt like a hero. Tom saw a cat stuck in a tree. The cat was sad and scared. Tom wanted to help the cat. He said, "Don't worry, cat! I will rescue you!" Tom's mom came and helped him climb the tree. They saved the cat together. The cat was happy to be saved. Tom's mom was proud of him. She said, "Good job, Tom! You are a hero with your ancient sunglasses!" Tom smiled and knew he could rescue anyone with his special sunglasses.
