Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Brown

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to run very fast. One day, Tim went outside to play. He saw his neighbor, Mr. Brown. Mr. Brown was big and tall. Tim wanted to race with him. Tim said, "Mr. Brown, let's race!" Mr. Brown laughed and said, "Okay, Tim!" They started to race. Tim ran fast, but Mr. Brown was faster. Tim was sad because he lost the race. Then, something funny happened. A little dog came and raced with them. The dog ran very fast! The dog won the race! Tim and Mr. Brown laughed. They were happy because the dog was so fast. Now, they all play together every day.

Once upon a time, a dog named Spot lived in a small house. Spot loved to search for things. One day, his friend, a cat named Whiskers, came to visit. Whiskers had a big problem. She lost her toy in the big park. Spot wanted to help Whiskers find her toy, but he knew it would be difficult. Spot and Whiskers went to the big park to search for the toy. They looked under trees, behind rocks, and inside bushes. As they searched, they found a bottle with a picture of grapes on it. Spot thought it was juice, but Whiskers knew it was wine. They left the wine and continued their search. Finally, after a long day of searching, Spot found Whiskers' toy under a big tree. Whiskers was so happy to have her toy back. They went back to Spot's house and played with the toy all day. Even though it was difficult, Spot and Whiskers worked together to solve the problem. And they knew not to touch the wine they found in the park.

Sara and Tom are playing in the park. They like to run, jump and slide. But today, the sun is very hot. They feel the heat on their skin and their hair. They are thirsty and tired. "Let's go to the shade," Sara says. "I want some water." "OK," Tom says. They walk to a big tree. Under the tree, they see a man. He is sitting on a bench. He is not moving. He is very pale. His eyes are open, but he does not blink. He stares at nothing. "Who is he?" Sara asks. She is curious. She wants to talk to him. "I don't know," Tom says. He is scared. He does not want to go near him. "Hello, mister," Sara says. She waves her hand. The man does not answer. He does not look at her. He stares at nothing. "Let's go, Sara," Tom says. He pulls her hand. "He is not nice. He is sick." Sara and Tom run away from the man. They find their mom. She has a bottle of water and some cookies. They drink and eat. They feel better. "Mom, who was that man?" Sara asks. She points to the tree. "I don't know, honey," Mom says. She looks at the man. He is still sitting on the bench. He is still pale. He still stares at nothing. "Maybe he needs some help. Maybe we should call someone." Mom takes out her phone. She calls someone. She tells them about the man. She hopes he will be OK. She hugs Sara and Tom. She tells them she loves them. They love her too. They are happy. They are safe.

Once there was a little girl who had a very special costume. It was a modern, shiny dress with frills and sparkles. She loved it very much and wanted to wear it every day. One morning she put on the costume, but her mommy said "No, not today. Today you have to leave it at home." The little girl didn't want to, but her mommy was very firm about it. The little girl wanted to stay in her costume so she started to cry. But her mommy said "I'm sorry, you can't. It's too modern for today. You must leave it at home." The little girl was sad, but she listened to her mommy and left the costume. She was still a bit disappointed, but was excited because she would get to wear her costume another day!
