Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Sam and the Hill

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Tom and Sam. Tom was brave, but Sam was shy. One day, Tom said, "Let's go to the hill!" But Sam said, "I'm too shy." Tom said, "Don't worry, it will be fun!" So, they went to the hill together. When they got there, something exciting happened. From the top of the hill, they saw a beautiful view of the ocean. They looked around in awe then suddenly, something even more exciting happened. There was a big butterfly that floated around them. Tom said, "That's so cool!" But Sam was still too shy to move. Tom said, "Come on, Sam. Let's catch it!" So, they both chased the butterfly together until it flew away. Sam was no longer shy and said, "That was fun!" They laughed and said, "Let's do it again tomorrow!"

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there lived a naughty bird named Tim. Tim liked to tease his friends. He would fly around and take their food. Tim's friends did not like it when he teased them. One day, Tim's friends had to do some work. They needed to build a new home in the tree. Tim did not want to work. He just wanted to tease his friends. So, he flew around and made fun of them while they worked. But then, Tim's friends had an idea. They all worked together and made a big net. When Tim tried to tease them again, they caught him in the net. Tim was very sorry for being naughty. He promised to be nice and help his friends work. From that day on, Tim and his friends lived happily together in their new home.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing with his toy drum. He loved to play the drum and make rhythms. He would tap, tap, tap all day long. His mom and dad would dance to his music, and they were all very happy. One day, Tim was careless while playing with his drum. He hit the drum too hard, and tear a hole in it. Tim was sad, and he didn't know what to do. His mom and dad saw the hole and tried to think of a way to fix it. Then, something unexpected happened. Tim's grandma came to visit. She saw the hole in the drum and had an idea. She took out her sewing kit and sewed the hole back together. Tim was so happy! He started to play the drum again, and the rhythm was even better than before. The whole family danced and laughed, and they all had a great time.

One day, a large squash grew in the garden. It was so big and round. A little girl named Mia saw the squash and wanted to open it. Mia tried to open the large squash, but it was too hard. She asked her friend Tom for help. Tom said, "Let's try to open it together!" They both tried to open the squash, but it still did not open. A wise old owl saw them trying to open the squash. The owl said, "You need a tool to open the large squash." Mia and Tom found a tool and used it to open the squash. Inside, they found lots of seeds. They were happy and shared the seeds with their friends.

Once upon a time, there was a pale bird named Ben. Ben lived in a big tree with his friends. They all played together and had fun. But Ben was very sad because he could not fly like his friends. He would watch them fly high in the sky and wish he could join them. One day, Ben's friend, Tom the squirrel, said, "Ben, why don't you try to fly? You can do it if you believe in yourself!" Ben was scared, but he wanted to fly so much. So, he decided to try. He stood on a branch, spread his wings, and jumped. At first, he fell, but then he started to fly! Ben was so happy that he could fly with his friends. As they flew around, they saw a sign that said, "No flying past this point. It's the law." Ben's friends wanted to break the law and fly past the sign. But Ben said, "No, we must follow the law. It is there for a reason." His friends listened and they all flew back home. That night, there was a big storm. The wind was very strong past the sign. If they had broken the law and flown past the sign, they would have been in danger. Ben and his friends were safe because they followed the law. The moral of the story is to always try your best and to follow the rules, because they are there to keep you safe.
