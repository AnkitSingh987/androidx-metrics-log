Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Bunny and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Bob. Bob loved to eat radish from the big garden. One day, he saw a very big radish. It was the biggest radish he had ever seen. He wanted to eat it, but it belonged to a mean old farmer. The poor bunny tried to escape with the radish. He pulled and pulled, but the radish was too big. The mean old farmer saw the bunny trying to take his radish. He was very angry and started to chase the bunny. Bob tried to run away, but he was too slow. The mean old farmer caught the poor bunny. He took the radish away from Bob and sent him away. Bob was very sad and hungry. He never got to eat the big radish.

One day, a healthy bird named Bob saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad. She looked at the sky and saw a big cloud. The cloud was in the way of the sun. Sue wanted to play outside, but the cloud made it too dark. Bob wanted to help Sue. He flew up to the cloud and said, "Go away, cloud! Sue wants to play!" The cloud did not move. Bob thought and thought. He had an idea. He waved his wings very fast. The wind from his wings pushed the cloud away. Sue saw the sun come out from behind the cloud. She was so happy! She looked up and saw Bob. Sue waved to Bob and said, "Thank you, Bob! Now I can play outside!" Bob waved back and flew away. Sue played outside all day under the bright sky.

Once upon a time, a big red bus named Bobby loved to travel. Bobby had many friends who would ride with him. They would go to fun places like the park and the store. One day, Bobby had to take a patient to the doctor. The patient was a little girl named Sue. Sue was not feeling well. She had a tight feeling in her chest. Bobby knew he had to help Sue. Bobby tried to travel fast, but there was a big truck in the way. The truck was very slow. Bobby and Sue were worried. They needed to get to the doctor soon. Bobby honked his horn and the truck moved out of the way. Bobby traveled fast to the doctor. Sue was happy and thanked Bobby. Soon, Sue felt better and they all went to the park to play.

One day, Tim went to the park with his dad. Tim saw a big tree with a rough bark. He wanted to reach the high branch. Tim's dad said, "Be careful, Tim." Tim tried to reach the high branch, but he couldn't. He saw a wallet on the ground. It was his dad's wallet! Tim picked it up and gave it to his dad. His dad was very happy. But then, a big bird came down from the tree. The bird took the wallet from Tim's dad! The bird flew up high with the wallet. Tim and his dad were so surprised. They laughed and clapped. The bird dropped the wallet back to them. Tim's dad said, "Thank you, bird!"

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was very excited to go to school for the first time. He put on his new clothes and shoes, and he was ready to go. When Tim got to school, he saw a big door. He was confused and did not know how to open it. Then, a nice teacher came up to him and said, "Hello, Tim! Just touch the door handle and pull. The door will open for you." Tim did what the teacher said, and the door opened! Inside the school, Tim met many new friends. They played games and learned new things. Tim was happy and not confused anymore. He loved going to school and could not wait to come back every day.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Tom. Tom had a big house and many toys. One day, Tom saw a small stream near his house. He wanted to play with his toy boats in the stream. Tom called his friend Sam and said, "Sam, hurry! Come to my house. We can play with my toy boats in the stream." Sam ran to Tom's house as fast as he could. They both played with the toy boats, and they were very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A big rain came, and the stream became a big river. The toy boats went far away, and Tom and Sam could not find them. They were very sad. The rain took all their toy boats, and they could not play anymore.
