Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Pasta

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue loved pasta. She thought pasta was very yummy. She would eat pasta every day. She liked to share her pasta with her friends. Sue was always happy when she ate pasta. One day, Sue's mom told her to mind her little brother, Tim. Sue didn't want to mind Tim. She wanted to eat her yummy pasta. Sue thought it would be okay to leave Tim alone for a little bit. She went to the kitchen to make her pasta. While Sue was in the kitchen, Tim found her yummy pasta. Tim started to eat all of Sue's pasta. When Sue came back, she saw Tim eating her pasta. Sue was very sad. She should have minded Tim like her mom told her to. Now, Sue had no yummy pasta to eat.

Once upon a time, in a big pond, there was a shy frog named Lily. Lily liked to sit on a raft all day. She loved to watch the sun shine on the water. One day, while Lily was on her raft, she saw a big fish. The big fish was sad. Lily wanted to help the sad fish. She asked the fish, "Why are you sad?" The big fish said, "I lost my friend. Can you help me find him?" Lily wanted to help the big fish find his friend. They looked all around the pond. They could not find the friend. Then, Lily saw something shine in the water. It was the big fish's friend! The friend was not a fish, but a shiny rock. The big fish was so happy to see his friend again. Lily felt happy too. From that day on, Lily, the big fish, and the shiny rock were the best of friends.

Once there was a boy named Tom. Tom had a big toy that could shrink. He loved to play with it all day. One day, Tom saw a crack in the wall. He wanted to see what was inside. Tom said to his toy, "Please shrink me so I can go in the crack." The toy made Tom very small. Now, Tom could go into the crack. Inside the crack, he found a room full of toys. Tom was very happy. Tom saw a sign that said, "Toys available for everyone." He took some toys and said, "Thank you, room!" Then, Tom asked his toy to make him big again. He went out of the crack and showed his new toys to his friends. They all played together and had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a busy farm with a big red stable. In the stable, there were many animals like cows, pigs, and horses. They all liked to play together and perform tricks for the farmer. One day, the horse named Bob said to the cow named Sally, "Let's perform a fun trick for the farmer!" Sally agreed and said, "Yes, let's do it!" They planned their trick and practiced it all day long. When the farmer came to the busy stable, Bob and Sally were ready. Bob jumped high in the air while Sally mooed a happy song. The farmer laughed and clapped his hands. All the animals in the stable cheered for Bob and Sally. They all had a great day on the busy farm.

Once upon a time, in a small town in the West, there was a brave dog named Max. Max loved to tie things with his strong paws. He would tie his toys, his food bowl, and even his owner's shoes. Everyone in the town loved Max because he was so helpful. One sunny day, Max was playing in the park. He saw a big red ball stuck high up in a tree. Max wanted to help, so he grabbed a long rope and started to tie it around the tree. He pulled and pulled, but the ball would not come down. Just then, a tiny bird flew down from the sky. The bird saw Max and wanted to help too. So, the bird flew up to the big red ball and gave it a gentle push. To Max's surprise, the ball fell down! Max was so happy that he wagged his tail and thanked the tiny bird. They became best friends and played together all day long. From that day on, Max learned that sometimes even the smallest friends can be the bravest.
