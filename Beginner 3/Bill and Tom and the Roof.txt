Category: Beginner
Title: Bill and Tom and the Roof

Bill and Tom were the best of friends. They liked to do everything together. One day, they took a walk and found a huge bounce house. The bounce house was big and bright and it stood quite high. They were both so excited! Bill asked Tom, "Do you want to get in and bounce?" Tom laughed and answered, "Of course! This looks like so much fun!" The two of them ran inside the bounce house and jumped around for what felt like forever. They jumped higher and higher and laughed whenever they touched the roof. It felt like the best event ever! When the event was over, Bill and Tom hugged each other goodbye. They were both so excited to have experienced something so fun together. They promised to come back soon to bounce together again.

Once upon a time, there was a big red hat. A little boy named Tim had the hat. He loved his hat very much. It was hard to find, but Tim did not mind. He wore his hat every day. One day, Tim went to play with his friends. They played a game of hide and seek. Tim hid behind a big tree. His hat was so big, it was hard to hide. Tim did not mind. He liked his hat. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took Tim's hat away. It flew high into the sky. Tim was sad, but he did not mind. He knew his hat would come back. And it did! A nice bird brought the hat back to Tim. The bird wanted to be friends. Tim was happy. He had his hat and a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to swing on the swings at the park. One day, she saw a fence around the swings. The fence was tough and tall. Lily was sad because she could not swing. Lily had an idea. She asked her big brother, Tom, to help her climb the fence. Tom said, "No, it's not safe. We should ask Mom for help." But Lily did not want to wait. So, she tried to climb the fence by herself. She slipped and fell, and it hurt a lot. Mom heard Lily cry and came running. She saw the fence and knew what happened. Mom hugged Lily and said, "You should have waited for help. Now you know that it is important to be patient and ask for help when you need it." Lily learned her lesson and always asked for help when she needed it.

One day, a little girl named Sue was playing in her room. She heard a noise and thought it was a ghost. Sue was scared, but she wanted to be brave. She went to the dark room where the noise came from. The wind outside was harsh and made the tree hit the window. Sue saw it was just the tree and not a ghost. She felt better and not scared anymore. Sue went back to her room and kept playing. She was happy to know there was no ghost. She knew she was brave and could face anything.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a weird bug on a leaf. He wanted to show his mom, so he raised the leaf very carefully. Tim walked back to his house with the weird bug on the leaf. As he walked, Tim found a small pebble on the ground. He liked the pebble because it was shiny. He picked it up and put it in his pocket. Now, he had a weird bug and a shiny pebble to show his mom. When Tim got home, he showed his mom the weird bug on the leaf and the shiny pebble from his pocket. His mom smiled and said, "Wow, Tim! You found some very special things today!" Tim felt happy and put the pebble in a special box in his room.

One day, a little girl named Sue wanted to help her daddy make a cake. Daddy said, "Okay, you can stir the mix." Sue was so happy to help. She took a big spoon and began to stir the cake mix in the bowl. As Sue was stirring, her brother Tom came into the kitchen. Tom said, "Sue, you are stirring the mix all wrong! It will make the cake ugly!" Sue was sad and didn't want to make an ugly cake. Daddy saw Sue was sad and said, "Don't worry, Sue. Keep stirring the mix. It's okay if the cake is not perfect. We will still love it." Sue smiled and kept stirring. In the end, the cake was not ugly, but very yummy!
