Category: Beginner
Title: The Red Button and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a big red button. This button was very selfish. It did not like to share or play with the other buttons. One day, the sun was shining very bright and hot. The big red button started to feel warm. It did not know that the sun can burn if it gets too hot. The other buttons tried to warn the big red button, but it did not listen. Then, something unexpected happened. A cloud came and covered the sun. The big red button was not burned after all. It was so happy and thankful to the cloud. From that day on, the big red button learned to be nice and share with the other buttons.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the lake. He saw a big boat. Tim wanted to go on the boat. He asked the boat man, "Can I go on the boat?" The honest boat man said, "You need to sign your name first." Tim did not know how to sign. The boat man helped Tim sign his name. Tim was happy. They went on the boat together. While on the boat, they saw a big fish. The fish jumped out of the water. It was a magic fish! The fish said, "I will give you a wish, but you must be honest." Tim wished for a new toy. The fish gave Tim the toy. Tim learned that being honest is good.

One day, Bear and Fox were walking in the icy woods. Bear was big and strong, but he was also very nice. Fox was small and fast, but he was not always nice. They saw a big piece of cake on the ground. Bear said, "Let's share the cake." Fox said, "No, I want it all." Bear was sad, but he did not want to fight with Fox. So, he let Fox have the whole piece of cake. Later, they saw a big hill. Bear said, "I bet there's something fun on the other side of the hill." Fox said, "I'm too full to walk up the hill. I will remain here." Bear went up the hill and found a big playground with lots of fun things to play on. He played on the swings, the slide, and the seesaw. Fox heard Bear laughing and having fun, and he wished he had shared the cake with Bear. Then, he could have gone up the hill too. But now, Fox was too full and could only remain where he was, feeling sad.

Once upon a time, there was a dry tree. In the tree, there was an owl. The owl was nice. The owl could fly high in the sky. The owl liked to help his friends. One day, the owl saw his friend, a small bird. The bird was sad. The bird was thirsty. There was no water. The owl wanted to help the bird. The owl had an idea. The owl flew away. The owl found water. The owl took the water in his beak. The owl came back. The owl gave the water to the bird. The owl supplied water to the bird. The bird was happy. The bird said thank you to the owl. The owl was happy to help.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom liked to walk with his dog, Spot. They walked every day near their house. One day, they saw a big window. The window was on a new house. Tom and Spot wanted to see what was inside. When Tom and Spot looked in the window, they saw a cat. The cat looked uncomfortable. It wanted to go outside. Tom opened the window to help the cat. But, something unexpected happened. The cat jumped out of the window and turned into a big, friendly bear. Tom and Spot were surprised. They did not know that the cat was really a bear. The bear was happy to be outside. He thanked Tom and Spot for helping him. Now, Tom, Spot, and the bear walk together every day. They became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a brave little dog named Spot. Spot lived in a small house with his family. They loved him very much. One day, there was a big thunder outside. Spot was scared, but he wanted to be brave for his family. Spot's mom said, "Spot, can you provide help for your little sister? She is scared too." Spot nodded and went to his sister. He sat with her and held her paw. They hugged each other, feeling safe and warm. As the thunder went away, Spot's sister said, "Thank you, Spot. You are so brave." Spot smiled and felt proud. From that day on, Spot knew that he could be brave, even when things were scary. And his family knew they could always count on him.
