Category: Beginner
Title: The Ugly Car and the Story

Once upon a time, there was an ugly car. It was sad because no one wanted to ride with it. The car had a driver named Tom. Tom loved the ugly car and wanted to help it. One day, Tom had an idea. He decided to raise the car up high on big wheels. He thought it would make the car happy. The ugly car was excited and said, "Thank you, Tom! I feel special now!" Soon, other cars and people saw the ugly car up high. They thought it was cool and wanted to ride with it too. The ugly car became very popular and had many friends. The moral of the story is that it's not how you look, but how you treat others that makes you special.

One day, a bald man named Bob went for a walk. He saw a big lawn with pretty flowers. Bob liked the lawn and wanted to play on it. Bob saw a dog on the lawn. The dog was very happy. Bob walked up to the dog and said, "Hi, dog! Can I play with you?" The dog wagged its tail and said, "Yes, let's play!" Bob and the dog played on the lawn. They ran and jumped. Bob started to feel hot. He took off his hat and rubbed his bald head. The dog saw this and rubbed its head too. They laughed and played until the sun went down.

One day, a big, healthy dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a small, pretty bird. The bird had a ticket in its beak. Max wanted to know where the bird was going. Max said, "Hello, bird! Where are you going with that ticket?" The bird yawned and said, "I am going to a fun party. Do you want to come with me?" Max was very happy and said, "Yes, I want to go!" Max and the bird went to the party together. They danced, ate yummy food, and played fun games. They were very tired at the end of the day. Max and the bird yawned and went home. They had a great time at the party.

One day, a little girl named Mia saw a big fireplace. She liked to sit by the warm fire. Mia's cat, Tom, liked to sit there too. They would nod their heads and smile at each other. It was a nice place to be. One day, they heard a loud sound from the fireplace. Mia and Tom were scared. They did not know what it was. They looked at each other and nodded. They knew they had to find out what made the loud sound. Mia and Tom went close to the fireplace. They saw a big bird. The bird was not scary. It was just lost. The bird had come down the chimney by mistake. Mia and Tom helped the bird go back outside. They were not scared anymore. They were happy to make a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly cat named Kitty. She loved to spend her days playing with her best friend, a little girl named Lucy. They would play with toys and run around in the garden. One day, a big dog came into the garden. Kitty was scared and ran away. Lucy was sad and cried, "Kitty, come back!" But Kitty was too afraid of the big dog. The big dog wanted to play, but Kitty thought the dog was mean. Lucy had an idea. She talked to the big dog and said, "Please be nice to Kitty. She is my friend." The big dog listened and wagged his tail. Kitty saw this and came out from hiding. The big dog and Kitty became friends too. The moral of the story is to not judge someone by their looks, but by their actions.

Once upon a time, there was a shy cat named Kitty. Kitty had a friend named Doggy. They liked to play together in the park. One day, Kitty told Doggy a secret. She said, "I want to marry Mr. Cat next door. He is nice and funny." Doggy was happy for Kitty. He said, "That's great! Let's go tell Mr. Cat." They walked to Mr. Cat's house and knocked on the door. Mr. Cat came out and Kitty told him, "I want to marry you." Mr. Cat smiled and said, "I want to marry you too, Kitty." Kitty and Mr. Cat got married and had a baby. The baby was a cute little kitten. They named the kitten Baby. Kitty, Mr. Cat, and Baby lived happily together with their friend Doggy. They played in the park every day and had lots of fun.
