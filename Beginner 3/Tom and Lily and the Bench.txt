Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Bench

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a bench near the pond. The bench is red and shiny. "Let's sit on the bench and watch the ducks," Tom says. "OK, that sounds fun," Lily says. They run to the bench and sit down. They see many ducks in the pond. Some ducks are white, some ducks are brown, some ducks are black. The ducks swim and quack. "Look, that duck has a green head," Tom says. "He is pretty," Lily says. They throw some bread to the ducks. The ducks come closer and eat the bread. They are happy and make more noise. "Quack, quack, quack," the ducks say. "Hello, ducks," Tom and Lily say. They laugh and clap their hands. They like the ducks. Then they hear a voice. "Tom, Lily, it's time to go home," the voice says. They look up and see their mom. She is standing near the gate. She is holding a bag. "Mom, we are playing with the ducks," Tom says. "We can come back another day," Mom says. "I have a surprise for you in the bag." "What is it?" Lily asks. "It's something sweet and yummy," Mom says. Tom and Lily are curious. They get up from the bench and run to their mom. They hug her and say thank you. They open the bag and see two big cookies. The cookies have chocolate chips and sprinkles. "Wow, cookies!" Tom and Lily say. They take a bite and smile. The cookies are sweet and delicious. They share some with the ducks. The ducks like the cookies too. They wave goodbye to the ducks and walk home with their mom. They are happy and full. They had a good day in the park.

One day, a girl named Lily went to the beach with her mom and dad. They had fun playing in the sand and making big sand castles. The sun was warm and mild, and the water was cool and blue. Lily liked to hear the sound of the waves as they crashed on the shore. She wanted to go closer and play in the water, so her mom held her hand as they walked to the edge of the waves. As they stood there, a small wave came up and splashed Lily's feet. She giggled and jumped back. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wave came up and splashed Lily's whole body! She was very wet, but she started to laugh. Her mom and dad laughed too. They all played in the water together, and even though the big wave was a surprise, they had a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little comet named Starry. Starry lived up in the sky with his family. They all loved to fly around and make pretty lights in the night for everyone to see. One day, Starry was playing with his friend, Moon. They were having lots of fun. But Starry made a mistake and bumped into Moon. Moon got a little hurt, but he was patient. He knew Starry didn't mean it. Starry said, "I'm sorry, Moon." Moon smiled and said, "I forgive you, Starry." After that, Starry and Moon kept playing and having fun. They knew that sometimes accidents happen, and it's important to be patient and forgive each other. They were the best of friends, and they loved to light up the sky together.

One day, a little boy named Tim was very excited. He was going to ride in a big jeep with his dad. They drove the jeep to the park to play. Tim loved the park because he could run and play with his friends. At the park, Tim saw a small dog. The dog was cute and happy. Tim wanted to pet the dog, so he asked the dog's owner, "Can I pet your dog?" The owner said, "Yes, but be gentle." Tim was careful and pet the dog softly. Suddenly, the dog gave Tim a little bite. Tim was surprised and a little sad. The dog's owner said, "I'm sorry, the dog has never done that before." Tim's dad came over and hugged Tim to make him feel better. They decided to go home and have some ice cream. Even though something unexpected happened, Tim still had a fun day at the park with his dad and the jeep.
