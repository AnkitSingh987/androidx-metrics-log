Category: Beginner
Title: The Ugly Cable and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was an ugly cable. It was not happy because no one liked it. It wanted to move far away and find a new home where it could be happy. One day, the ugly cable saw a big tree. It thought the tree would be a good home. So, it moved to the tree and lived there. The ugly cable was happy in its new home. After some time, a big storm came. The tree fell down, but the ugly cable was strong and held the tree up. The ugly cable saved the tree, and everyone in the forest liked it. The ugly cable was happy, and it never wanted to move again.

One day, a boy named Tim had a tray. He liked his tray a lot. He used it to carry his toys. Tim had a big dog named Max. Max liked to protect Tim and his tray. One day, Tim saw a distant hill. He said, "Max, let's go to the hill!" Max barked and wagged his tail. They walked and walked until they got to the hill. It was a fun walk. On the hill, Tim saw a girl named Sue. Sue had a tray too! Tim said, "Hi, Sue! I have a tray. You have a tray. Let's play!" Sue said, "Yes! Let's play!" But then, a big wind came and blew the trays away. Tim and Sue were sad. Max ran fast and caught the trays. He brought them back to Tim and Sue. They were happy and played together with their trays. Max protected them and their trays all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a very important spoon. This spoon was special because it was shiny and made him happy when he ate with it. One day, Tim's friend, Sue, came over to play. Sue saw the spoon and wanted to play with it too. But Tim didn't want to share his important spoon. He decided to ignore Sue and keep the spoon to himself. Sue was sad, but then she found another spoon in the kitchen. She showed it to Tim, and they both played with their spoons together. They learned that sharing can be fun, and it's not good to ignore your friends.

One day, a thoughtful boy named Tim wanted to wear a jet costume. He asked his mom, "Can I wear the jet costume today?" His mom said, "Yes, you can wear it, but be careful." Tim put on the jet costume and went outside to play. He ran around making jet sounds, like "Zoom! Zoom!" His friends saw him and wanted to play too. They all wore different costumes and played together. Then, something unexpected happened. A real jet flew down from the sky! The jet pilot said, "Hello, kids! I saw you playing and thought it was fun. Can I join you?" The kids were so happy and surprised. They all played together, and it was the best day ever.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a pink bunny. The pink bunny loved to play with his friends. One hot day, the pink bunny and his friends were playing near a big tree. Suddenly, the pink bunny saw a big, hot sun in the sky. The pink bunny said, "Wow! The sun is very hot today!" His friend, a little bird, agreed, "Yes, it is very hot!" The pink bunny and his friends decided to find some shade to cool down. They looked around and saw a big, green tree. They all ran to the tree and sat under it. The pink bunny shouted, "Yay! We found shade!" His friends shouted too, "Yay! We are not hot anymore!" They all played under the tree until the hot sun went down. Then, the pink bunny and his friends went back to their homes, happy and cool. They couldn't wait to play together again the next day.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim loved to dance to the rhythm of the music. He would dance all day and all night. His mom told him to behave, but Tim didn't listen. One day, Tim went to the park to dance. He danced and danced, not behaving like his mom told him. He didn't see the big hole in the ground. Tim was not being careful. Tim fell into the hole and got hurt. He cried and cried. His mom came to help him, but she was sad. Tim learned that he should have behaved and listened to his mom. The end.
