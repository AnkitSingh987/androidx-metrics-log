Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Sam

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. It was a cold day, and the ground was icy. Tim saw his friend, Sam, who was holding a big red ball. "Hi Sam! Can I play with your ball?" Tim asked. Sam said, "Okay, but first you must polish it. It is dirty." Tim rubbed the ball until it was clean and shiny. They played with the ball, but then something unexpected happened. The ball got very hot! It was giving off heat. Tim and Sam were scared, but they had an idea. They rolled the hot ball on the icy ground. The ice started to melt, and the ball cooled down. Tim and Sam were happy, and they played with the ball all day.

One day, a purple cat named Mimi went for a walk. Mimi saw a big puddle of mud. She thought it would be fun to jump in it. So, she jumped into the mud and got all dirty. Mimi's friend, a small dog named Max, saw her in the mud. Max said, "Mimi, remember, we should not play in the mud. It is not clean." Mimi did not listen and kept playing in the mud. Soon, it was time to go home. Mimi's mom saw her all dirty and said, "Mimi, you should not play in the mud. Now, you have to take a bath." Mimi did not like baths, so she was sad. She took a bath and got clean. The next day, Mimi remembered what Max and her mom said. She did not play in the mud. Instead, she played with Max in the grass. They had lots of fun and stayed clean. The moral of the story is to listen to your friends and family. They know what is best for you.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was very excited because it was her birthday. She could not wait to see her friends and get her birthday present. When Sue's friends came to her party, they all brought her a present. Sue's mom had a huge present for her too. Sue wanted to open the huge present first, but her mom explained that she had to wait. After playing games and eating cake, Sue finally got to open her presents. She opened the huge present from her mom. Inside, there was a big, soft teddy bear. Sue loved her new teddy bear and hugged it tight. It was the best birthday ever.

Once upon a time there was a little bee named Buzz. He lived in a big hive with all of his friends. Every day Buzz woke up and got dressed. He liked to be comfortable and wore a coat with lots of pockets. Buzz often flew around the garden looking for flowers. He buzzed around and around in search of the sweetest nectar. One day, Buzz found a very special flower. It was so beautiful that he wanted to put on his best clothes. He found a colorful shirt and a hat with stripes. He felt so comfortable and looked so nice that all the other bees in the hive clapped and cheered. Everybody knew that Buzz was the best dressed bee around!

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a big slide. They want to go on the slide. But there is a mean boy on the slide. He does not let anyone go on the slide. He says it is his slide. He pushes and kicks anyone who tries to go on the slide. Lily and Ben are sad. They want to have fun on the slide. They have a plan. They see a bell near the slide. The bell is cool. It makes a loud sound when you hit it. Lily and Ben run to the bell. They hit the bell with their hands. The bell makes a loud sound. The mean boy hears the sound. He thinks it is something scary. He gets off the slide and runs away. Lily and Ben are happy. They run to the slide. They go on the slide. They have fun on the slide. But the mean boy is not gone. He is hiding behind a tree. He is angry. He wants to get back at Lily and Ben. He waits for them to go on the slide again. Lily and Ben go on the slide again. They do not see the mean boy. The mean boy runs to the slide. He grabs the slide. He pulls the slide. The slide falls down. Lily and Ben are on the slide. They fall down with the slide. They hit the ground. They get hurt. They cry. The mean boy laughs. He runs away. He is happy. He thinks he is smart. Lily and Ben are sad. They are hurt. They are not happy. They wish they could prevent the mean boy from being mean. They wish they could play on the slide. They wish they had a good ending.
