Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Tiger

Lily was hungry. She wanted to eat cereal for breakfast. She went to the kitchen and saw a big box of cereal on the table. It had a picture of a smiling tiger on it. Lily thought the tiger was attractive. She liked his orange and black stripes and his big teeth. She opened the box and poured some cereal into a bowl. She looked for milk in the fridge, but there was none. She asked her mom, "Mom, where is the milk?" Mom came to the kitchen and said, "Sorry, Lily, we ran out of milk. You can have some juice instead." Lily did not like juice with her cereal. She thought it would make it wet and soggy. She wanted her cereal to be dry and crunchy. She said, "No, thank you, Mom. I want milk." Mom said, "I'm sorry, Lily, but we don't have any milk right now. How about some water?" Lily did not like water with her cereal either. She thought it would make it bland and boring. She said, "No, thank you, Mom. I want milk." Mom said, "Lily, you have to eat something. You can't have milk today. Maybe we can go to the store later and buy some. But for now, you have to choose something else." Lily looked at her bowl of dry cereal. She looked at the attractive tiger on the box. She thought for a moment. Then she said, "OK, Mom. I will eat my cereal. But can I have some honey on it?" Mom smiled and said, "Sure, Lily. That's a good idea. Honey will make your cereal sweet and sticky. Here you go." She gave Lily a spoon and a jar of honey. Lily drizzled some honey on her cereal and stirred it. She took a bite and smiled. It was delicious. She said, "Thank you, Mom. This is yummy. And thank you, tiger, for the cereal." Mom hugged Lily and said, "You're welcome, Lily. I'm glad you like it. And I'm sure the tiger is happy too."

Tom and Sam are young brothers. They share a room and a bed. But they do not like to share their toys. They always fight over who gets to play with what. One day, Mom says, "Enough! You two need to separate your toys. You can each have half of the room and half of the bed. But you cannot touch the other's toys. Do you understand?" Tom and Sam nod. They do not want to make Mom angry. They start to separate their toys. Tom takes his cars and trains and puts them on his side of the room. Sam takes his blocks and animals and puts them on his side of the room. They draw a line on the floor with a crayon to show where their sides are. But soon, they get bored. They look at the other's toys and wish they could play with them. They miss each other. They do not like to be alone. "Sam?" Tom whispers. "Are you still awake?" "Yes, Tom," Sam whispers back. "Do you want to play?" "Yes, Sam," Tom says. "But we cannot cross the line." "Who cares about the line?" Sam says. "We can move it. Or we can erase it. We are brothers. We can share." Tom smiles. He agrees. He gets out of his bed and goes to Sam's bed. They hug and giggle. They play with their toys together. They forget about the line. Mom hears them and smiles. She is glad they are happy. She does not mind the mess. She loves her young boys.

One day, a cute cat named Lily went for a walk. She saw a big scale in the park. Lily was very curious about the scale. She wanted to know how much she weighed. A little boy named Tim saw Lily and said, "Hi, Lily! Let's play!" But Lily wanted to use the scale, so she ignored Tim. She jumped on the scale and waited. Suddenly, the scale started to move! It was not a scale, but a secret door! Lily fell into a room full of toys and treats. Tim saw this and said, "Wow! Look at all the fun things!" They both played and shared the treats, and Lily learned that it's more fun to play with friends than to ignore them.
