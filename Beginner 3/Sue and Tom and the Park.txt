Category: Beginner
Title: Sue and Tom and the Park

Sue and Tom were best friends. They loved to go on walks together and play in the park, but today Sue was feeling sad. Tom asked her why and she said, "My old doll got broken and I don't know how to repair it." Tom smiled and said, "Let me help you, I'm sure we can fix it!" So they found some glue and started to work on Sue's doll. As they were wiping off some extra glue, a little boy came up to them. He asked what they were doing, and Sue showed him her old doll. The boy laughed and said, "That's silly, no one would ever fix a broken toy!" Tom got angry, but Sue just smiled and said, "People are only selfish when they don't want to help someone. Be kind and you will never have to be selfish." The little boy nodded and said, "You're right, I'm sorry for being so rude." So Tom and Sue continued to wipe off the glue, and soon Sue's doll was repaired. Tom and Sue hugged, and the little boy even gave the doll a hug, too.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the station with his mom. The station was big and boring. Tim did not like the station. He wanted to go home and play with his toys. But his mom said they had to wait for the train. While they waited, Tim saw a toy gun on the ground. He picked it up and started to play. He thought it was fun to shoot the toy gun. His mom told him to stop, but Tim did not listen. Then, something unexpected happened. A big dog came running at Tim. Tim got scared and tried to shoot the dog with the toy gun, but it did not work. The dog bit Tim's leg, and it hurt a lot. Tim cried, and his mom took him home. They did not go on the train that day. Tim learned that playing with things he found on the ground was not always a good idea.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play near the creek. One day, he saw a big frog. Tim gazed at the frog with big eyes. He wanted to be friends with the frog. The next day, Tim went back to the creek. He brought a bitter apple for the frog. He thought the frog would like it. But the frog did not like the bitter apple. The frog was sad and jumped away. Tim felt bad. He wanted to make the frog happy. The next day, he brought a sweet treat for the frog. When he went to the creek, the frog was there. Tim gave the sweet treat to the frog. The frog was happy, and they became friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a hero named Tom. Tom was strong and brave, but he had one difficult job. He had to stop a big, bad dog that liked to bite people. The town was scared, and they needed Tom's help. One day, Tom met a girl named Sue. Sue said, "Tom, I am scared of the big, bad dog. Can you help us?" Tom smiled and said, "Do not worry, Sue. I will stop the dog from biting people." Tom had a plan. He found a big, strong net and waited for the dog. When the dog came, Tom threw the net on it. The dog could not bite anymore. The town was safe, and everyone was happy. Tom was the hero of the town, and no one was scared of the big, bad dog again.

Once there was a boy called Jeremy. He was three years old. He had an alarm in his room. One day he played with his toy car and his friend. His friend loved to tease him. His friend always drove the car faster than Jeremy. This made Jeremy very competitive. Jeremy wanted to improve and be better than his friend. So he came up with an idea; he decided to set the alarm on his bedroom wall. The alarm would help Jeremy to wake up early so he could practice with his toy car. This would make him the fastest and most competitive driver of the two. The next day Jeremy woke up with the alarm. He wanted to practice, so he raced his friend. He won and became the fastest driver! The moral of the story is that practice makes perfect and if you want to be competitive, you need to set the alarm and put in the hard work!
