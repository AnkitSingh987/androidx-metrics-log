Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Rock

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a slow walker. Every day, he would walk to the park to play with his friends. But he always got there late because he walked so slow. One day, as Tim was walking to the park, he saw a big rock in the way. He tried to move it, but it was too heavy. Tim paused and thought about what to do. He decided to ask his friends for help. They all came and helped Tim move the rock out of the way. After they moved the rock, they all went to play at the park. Tim was happy that he asked for help. The friends learned that it is good to help each other when there is a problem. And Tim learned that it is okay to be slow, as long as you have friends to help you along the way.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had many plants in her garden. One day, she saw a big plant that was ready to sell. Lily wanted to sell the plant to buy a new toy. Lily took the plant to her friend, Tom. "Do you want to buy my plant?" she asked. Tom said, "No, I have no money." Lily was sad but she did not give up. She went to her other friend, Sue. "Do you want to buy my plant?" she asked again. Sue said, "Yes, I will buy it!" Lily was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A small bird flew down and sat on the plant. The bird was hurt and needed help. Lily and Sue looked at the bird and knew they had to help it. They forgot about selling the plant and took care of the bird instead. They learned that helping others is more important than getting what they want. And they all became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was a restless dog who liked to run and play all day. He lived in a big house with his friend, Sam. Sam worked in a lab. One day, Max missed Sam a lot. Sam was gone for a very long time. Max wanted to find Sam, so he left the house and went to the lab. Max did not know that the lab was a dangerous place for a little dog. When Max got to the lab, he saw many big things he did not understand. He ran around and knocked over some of the big things. The lab became a big mess. Max got scared and wanted to go home. But he could not find the way out. Sam came back to the lab and saw the big mess. He was very sad and angry. Max was also very sad. Sam could not fix the mess in the lab. Max and Sam were not friends anymore. Max had to go away and live in a new home. And they never saw each other again.

Once upon a time, there was a little bug who loved to crawl. The bug had a friend named Fish. Fish lived in a big lake. The lake was near the bug's home. They liked to play together every day. One sunny day, the sky was very bright. The bug went to visit Fish at the lake. The water was clear and blue. Fish swam up to the top to say hello. The bug and Fish played all day long. As the day went on, the sun started to set. The sky turned orange and pink. The bug and Fish said goodbye to each other. The bug crawled back to its home, and Fish swam deeper into the lake. They knew they would play together again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a crab. The crab was not happy. He would always complain. He would say, "I am too small. I want to be big and strong." The crab thought that if he was big and strong, he would be dangerous. One day, the crab met a big fish. The fish was sad too. The fish said, "I am too big. I want to be small like you." The crab and the fish talked and talked. They both wanted to be different. Then, something unexpected happened. A magic fish came by and heard them talk. The magic fish said, "I can help you both. I will make you change." The crab became big and the fish became small. But they were not happy. They learned that being different was not better. They asked the magic fish to change them back. The magic fish did, and they were happy again.
