Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Vine

One day, a boy named Tim saw a vine. The vine was long and green. Tim thought it would be fun to swing on the vine like a monkey. He asked his mom, "Can I swing on the vine, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful. The vine might be hurt." Tim started to swing on the vine. He was having so much fun! But then, something unexpected happened. A big bird came and sat on the vine. The bird said, "Hello! This is my vine. You can swing on it too!" Tim was surprised. He did not know that birds could talk. The bird and Tim became friends. They played on the vine all day. And the vine did not get hurt.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a wander. He was glad to be outside. The sun was shining, the birds were singing, and Tim was having fun. Tim found a big box on the ground. Inside the box, there was a film. Tim did not know what the film was about, but he wanted to find out. He took the film and went back home. Tim put the film in the TV and started to watch. But the film was not good. It was very scary. Tim was sad and scared. He wished he had never found the film. The end.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Hilda and Jack. It was a special day - Hilda and Jack had decided to go for a picnic in the woods. When they reached the woods, Hilda spotted a big log. She wanted to sit on it and admire the view. But Jack warned her it was dangerous. He said, “No, Hilda, don’t sit on it. It looks like it might roll down the hill!” Hilda wouldn’t listen. She wanted to sit on the log so badly. So Jack had an idea. He said, “Let’s tie a rope around the log so it won’t move.” So the two friends used the rope to tie the log firmly to the ground. Hilda was very pleased and they both sat on the log to enjoy the view. The moral of this story is that we always need to be careful and listen to our friends. They may not always know the best, but if we work together, we can make even the most dangerous situations safe.

Once there was a girl named Lily. She had a cat named Cut. Lily and Cut liked to play in the park. One day, they found an empty box. Lily said, "Let's raise the box and see what's inside!" Lily and Cut raised the box together. Inside the box, they found a big red ball. "Wow!" said Lily. "Let's play with the ball!" Cut meowed and jumped up and down. Lily and Cut played with the ball all day. They had a lot of fun. When it was time to go home, they put the ball back in the empty box. Lily said, "We can come back tomorrow and play again!" Cut meowed happily, and they went home.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sally. Sally loved to dress up in pretty clothes. One day, she put on a fancy dress that was very uncomfortable. She said, "Mom, I don't like this dress. It's too tight." Her mom said, "Sally, you don't have to wear that dress. You can wear something else. You should wear something that fits your style." Sally changed her clothes and wore her favorite dress. It was pink and had flowers on it. She felt happy and comfortable in her dress. Sally went to a party where she met a boy named Tim. They played together and had lots of fun. One day, Tim said, "Sally, I like your style. When we grow up, I want to marry you." Sally smiled and said, "Yes, Tim, I will marry you when we grow up." And so, Sally and Tim stayed friends and played together every day. They both knew that when they grew up, they would get married and be happy together.

Tim and Mia liked to play in the park. They liked to swing, slide and run. One day, they saw a big spider on a web. The spider was black and shiny. It had eight legs and many eyes. "Look, a spider!" Tim said. "It is scary and ugly." "No, it is not," Mia said. "It is modern and clever. It can make a web and fly with the wind." "Fly? How can it fly?" Tim asked. "Watch," Mia said. She took a stick and touched the web gently. The spider felt the stick and ran away. It jumped off the web and spun a thin thread behind it. The thread caught the wind and lifted the spider up. The spider flew away like a kite. "Wow, it can fly!" Tim said. "It is modern and clever. But it is still scary and ugly." Mia laughed. She liked the spider. She thought it was beautiful and brave. She wanted to fly with the wind too. She picked up a leaf and threw it in the air. The leaf flew away like the spider. "Look, I can fly too!" Mia said. Tim smiled. He picked up another leaf and threw it in the air. The leaf flew away like Mia's. "Me too!" Tim said. They laughed and played with the leaves. They pretended they were spiders flying with the wind. They had fun in the park. They forgot about the swing, the slide and the run. They only wanted to fly.
