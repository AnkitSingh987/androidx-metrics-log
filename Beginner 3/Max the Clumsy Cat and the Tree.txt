Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Clumsy Cat and the Tree

One day, a clumsy cat named Max went for a walk. Max saw a bottle hanging from a tree. He wanted to play with it. Max jumped up and tried to grab the bottle, but he missed and fell down. Max saw a dog named Sam walking by. Max asked Sam, "Can you help me get the bottle?" Sam said, "Yes, I will help you." Sam jumped up and grabbed the bottle with his mouth. But when Sam pulled the bottle down, it opened and water came out. Max and Sam got all wet! They laughed and said, "That was fun!" They played with the wet bottle and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to fold paper. One day, he went to the park with his mom. Tim saw a big tree with a lot of picks on the ground. He was very happy and wanted to pick them up. Tim started to pick up the picks and fold them. He made a big pile of folded picks. Tim was very grateful for the fun day at the park. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all the folded picks away. Tim was sad, but then he saw a little girl named Sue. She was playing with the folded picks that the wind blew to her. Tim and Sue became friends and played with the folded picks together. They were both grateful for the wind that brought them together.

Once upon a time, there was a big, silly cat named Bob. Bob loved to play with his friends. One day, Bob's mom told him that he could have a party. Bob was very happy. Bob's mom said, "We will provide food and games for the party. You need to give an invitation to your friends." So, Bob went to his friends and said, "You are invited to my party! It will be fun!" At the party, Bob and his friends played many games. They ate yummy food that Bob's mom provided. Bob's friends had a lot of fun. They laughed at Bob's silly games and tricks. In the end, they all went home happy and tired.

Tom and Mia liked to play with their blocks. They built towers and houses and bridges with the blocks. They had many colors and shapes of blocks. They were very busy and happy. But then a big storm came. The sky was dark and loud. They heard thunder and saw lightning. They were scared and ran to their mom. She hugged them and said it was okay. The storm was very strong. It made the wind blow hard and the rain fall fast. The wind and the rain destroyed their blocks. They knocked them down and washed them away. Tom and Mia were sad and angry. They cried and shouted. Mom saw their blocks were gone. She felt sorry for them. She said they could make new blocks with paper and glue. She helped them cut and stick the paper. They made new towers and houses and bridges with the paper blocks. They had many colors and shapes of paper blocks. They were very busy and happy again.

Lily liked to play with her toys in her room. She had a big clock on the wall that made a loud sound every hour. She knew that when the clock said six, it was time for dinner. She was an obedient girl and always listened to her mom. One day, she was playing with her teddy bear and her doll when she heard a strange noise. She looked at the clock and saw that it was not six yet. She wondered what the noise was. She walked to the door and opened it. She saw a big rabbit with a coat and a hat. The rabbit was holding a watch and looking very worried. "Hello, little girl," the rabbit said. "Do you know what time it is?" Lily was surprised and scared. She had never seen a talking rabbit before. She shook her head and said, "No, I don't know." The rabbit sighed and said, "Oh dear, oh dear, I'm late, I'm late, for a very important date. I have to go to the wonderland, but I can't find the hole. Can you help me?" Lily was curious and wanted to see the wonderland. She nodded and said, "Yes, I can help you. Where is the hole?" The rabbit smiled and said, "Thank you, thank you, you are very kind. The hole is in the garden, behind the big tree. Come with me, but hurry, hurry, we don't have much time." Lily grabbed her teddy bear and followed the rabbit. They ran to the garden and found the hole. The rabbit jumped in and said, "Come on, come on, follow me. It's a wonderful place, you'll see." Lily was about to jump in too, when she heard her mom's voice. "Lily, Lily, where are you? It's time for dinner." Lily stopped and looked at the hole. She wanted to go to the wonderland, but she also wanted to obey her mom. She was torn between the two choices. She looked at the rabbit, who was waiting for her. He said, "Please, please, don't be late. The wonderland will vanish soon. Come with me, it's fun, it's fun." Lily looked at the clock and saw that it was almost six. She made up her mind and said, "I'm sorry, Mr. Rabbit, but I have to go. My mom is calling me. Maybe another day, I'll see the wonderland." The rabbit frowned and said, "Oh no, oh no, you'll miss it, you'll miss it. It's now or never, little girl. Please, please, change your mind." Lily shook her head and said, "No, I can't. I have to be obedient. Goodbye, Mr. Rabbit." She turned around and ran back to the house. She closed the door and went to the kitchen. She saw her mom and said, "Hi, mom, I'm here. I'm ready for dinner." Her mom smiled and said, "That's good, Lily. You're a good girl. What were you doing in the garden?" Lily said, "I was playing with a rabbit. He wanted me to go to the wonderland, but I said no." Her mom looked puzzled and said, "A rabbit? What rabbit? I didn't see any rabbit." Lily said, "He was a big rabbit with a coat and a hat. He had a watch and he was late. He jumped into a hole and he said the wonderland would vanish soon." Her mom laughed and said, "Oh, Lily, you have such a big imagination. That sounds like a story from a book. There's no such thing as a talking rabbit or a wonderland or a hole that can vanish. You must have dreamed it." Lily said, "No, mom, I didn't dream it. It was real. I saw it. I almost went with him." Her mom said, "Well, I'm glad you didn't. You're a smart and obedient girl. You know that you have to listen to me and not to strangers. Now, let's have dinner. Maybe later, we can read a book together. How about Alice in Wonderland?" Lily said, "Yes, mom, I like that book. Maybe I'll see the rabbit again. Maybe he'll come back for me." Her mom said, "Maybe he will, Lily. Maybe he will. But remember, it's only a story. A story that can vanish."
