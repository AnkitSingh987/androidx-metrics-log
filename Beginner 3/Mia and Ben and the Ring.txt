Category: Beginner
Title: Mia and Ben and the Ring

Mia and Ben are friends. They go to the same school. They like to play and learn together. Today, they have lunch in the cafeteria. Mia has a sandwich and a banana. Ben has a pizza and an apple. "Can I have some of your pizza?" Mia asks Ben. "Sure, you can have a slice. Can I have some of your sandwich?" Ben says. "Okay, we can trade. I like pizza too," Mia says. They swap their food and start to eat. But then, Mia feels something in her mouth. It is hard and shiny. She spits it out on her napkin. It is a ring. "Ben, what is this?" Mia says, holding the ring. Ben looks at the ring and turns red. He feels embarrassed. "Oh, no. That is my mom's ring. I was playing with it this morning and I forgot to take it off. I must have dropped it in the pizza box. I'm sorry, Mia," Ben says. Mia is surprised. She does not know what to say. "Are you mad at me?" Ben asks. Mia shakes her head. She is not mad. She is a good friend. "No, I'm not mad. It was an accident. But you should keep your mom's ring safe. She might be worried if she can't find it," Mia says. Ben nods. He is glad that Mia is not mad. He is a good friend too. "Thank you, Mia. You are right. I will keep it safe. I will give it back to my mom when I go home. And I will share my apple with you," Ben says. Mia smiles. She likes apples. She likes Ben too. They finish their lunch and go to play. They are happy. They are friends.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Sarah. She was playing in the woods one day, when she heard a funny noise coming from behind her. Sarah turned around, and there was a big wolf! The wolf opened its mouth and said, “I want to eat you!” Sarah was so scared, and she shouted, “No!” But then she remembered the advice her mum had given her. She took a deep breath and shouted back, “No! I won’t let you eat me! I order you to go away!” The wolf was surprised. He hadn’t expected this. He stepped away, and Sarah felt glad that she had been brave. The wolf stepped away, and Sarah was happy. But then the wolf said, “I’ll be back soon” and he ran away into the woods. Sarah was relieved, but she was still a bit scared. She ran home quickly and told her mum what had happened. Her mum was glad to hear that Sarah had been so brave and she gave her a big hug. “Well done, Sarah” she said. “You were very brave!”

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a room with many toys. But his room was not organized. Toys were everywhere on the floor. One day, Tim's mom came into his room. She saw the mess and said, "Tim, you should organize your room. It will make it easier to find your toys and play." Tim did not want to clean, but he listened to his mom. Tim spent the day putting his toys away. He put the cars in one box and the dolls in another. At the end of the day, his room was organized. Tim felt happy because he could find his toys easily. Now, Tim always keeps his room organized. He learned that listening to his mom was a good thing to do.

Once upon a time, there was a fancy duck. This duck liked to wear a big hat and a nice bow. All the other ducks looked at the fancy duck and wanted to be like it. One day, the fancy duck saw a sign near the pond. The sign said, "Be kind to others." The fancy duck thought about the sign and wanted to be kind to the other ducks. The fancy duck started to share its hat and bow with the other ducks. They all took turns being fancy. Soon, all the ducks were happy and kind to each other. They learned that being kind and sharing made them all feel fancy and special. The moral of the story is to always be kind and share with others.
