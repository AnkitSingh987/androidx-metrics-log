Category: Beginner
Title: The Gray Dog and the Ball

One day, a gray dog went to play outside. The dog loved to roll on the grass. He was a very obedient dog and always listened to his owner. The owner would throw a ball, and the dog would run and roll on the grass to get it. One sunny day, the owner threw the ball very far. The gray dog ran and ran to get it. As he rolled on the grass to catch the ball, he saw something very strange. There was a big hole in the ground where the ball had landed. The gray dog went closer to look at the hole. He was very surprised to see a big, round rock inside the hole. It was not his ball! The dog tried to roll the rock out of the hole. It was not easy, but he tried very hard. Finally, the gray dog rolled the rock out of the hole. His owner came to see what had happened. The owner was very happy and proud of the dog. They found out that the rock was very special and valuable. From that day on, the gray dog became a hero in his town. He was still an obedient dog who loved to roll on the grass. But now, everyone knew him as the dog who found the special rock.

Anna and Ben were playing in the kitchen. They wanted to make a cake for their mom. They had a big bowl, some flour, some eggs, some milk and some sugar. They also had a spoon to stir. "Let's stir the flour and the eggs first," said Anna. She cracked an egg and poured it into the bowl. Ben did the same. Then they added some flour and started to stir. "Stir, stir, stir," they sang. "We are making a cake, a cake, a cake." They stirred until the mixture was smooth and yellow. Then they added some milk and some sugar and stirred some more. "Stir, stir, stir," they sang. "We are making a cake, a cake, a cake." They were having so much fun that they did not hear the door open. A mysterious man came into the kitchen. He had a long coat, a hat and a beard. He looked at the children and smiled. "Hello, little ones," he said. "What are you doing?" Anna and Ben stopped stirring and looked at him. They did not know who he was. They felt scared. "Who are you?" asked Anna. "I am your uncle Jack," said the man. "I came to visit your mom. She is my sister." Anna and Ben looked at each other. They did not remember having an uncle Jack. They wondered if he was telling the truth. "Where is mom?" asked Ben. "She is in the living room," said the man. "She will be happy to see you. Why don't you come with me?" Anna and Ben did not want to go with him. They wanted to stay with their cake. They did not trust him. He was too mysterious. "No, thank you," said Anna. "We are busy. We are making a cake, a cake, a cake." The man frowned. He did not like their answer. He reached for the bowl. "Let me see your cake," he said. "Maybe I can help you." He grabbed the bowl and lifted it up. But he did not hold it well. The bowl slipped from his hands and fell to the floor. The cake mixture splashed everywhere. It covered the man's coat, hat and beard. It also covered Anna and Ben's shirts. "Oops," said the man. Anna and Ben gasped. They looked at the mess. They looked at the man. They looked at each other. Then they started to laugh. "Ha, ha, ha," they laughed. "You are funny, uncle Jack. You are funny, funny, funny." The man did not laugh. He was angry. He was not their uncle Jack. He was a thief. He wanted to steal their mom's jewels. But his plan was ruined. He ran out of the kitchen. Anna and Ben did not care. They were happy. They had made a cake, a cake, a cake. They hugged each other and smiled. They did not need a spoon to stir. They had each other.
