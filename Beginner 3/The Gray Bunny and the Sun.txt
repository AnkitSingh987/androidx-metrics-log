Category: Beginner
Title: The Gray Bunny and the Sun

One day, a gray bunny went to the sea. He wanted to find a friend. He saw a nice oyster in the water. The oyster was big and shiny. The gray bunny wanted to play with the oyster. The bunny asked the oyster, "Can I borrow a shiny thing from you?" The oyster said, "Yes, you can borrow one." The gray bunny was very happy. He took the shiny thing and showed it to his friends. The gray bunny and the oyster played together every day. They had lots of fun. The gray bunny was very happy to have a new friend. They shared many shiny things and played in the sun.

One day, a little dog named Max wanted to travel. He put on his hat and went outside. It was a hot day. Max saw his friend, the cat named Sam. Sam had a hurt leg. "Sam, why is your leg hurt?" asked Max. Sam said, "I fell from a tree. It hurts a lot." Max wanted to help Sam. They needed to find a doctor. Max and Sam walked and walked. They met a big dog who did not want them to pass. "You can't go this way," said the big dog. Max and Sam were scared but they needed to find the doctor. Max said, "Please, let us pass. My friend Sam has a hurt leg." The big dog saw Sam's leg and felt bad. He let them pass. Max and Sam found the doctor. The doctor helped Sam's leg. They both said thank you and went home. Max and Sam were happy. They played together all day.

Tommy was walking in the park with his mother when he spotted a duck. He pointed and said, "Look! A duck!" His mother smiled, took out a ball and said, "Let's play a game." The game was to wrap the ball around the duck. Tommy laughed and said, "This seems impossible!" His mother answered, "Nothing is impossible for a flexible person like you." The more Tommy tried, the more frustrated he became. He was about to give up, when his mother said, "Remember, you have to be flexible in more than one way". That was just the advice Tommy needed, so he squatted down and slowly wrapped his arms around the duck. Suddenly, the duck flew away, safe and sound. Tommy laughed with joy, and his mother hugged him tightly, saying, "You wrapped that duck in a very clever way. You are so flexible!"

One day, a little girl named Amy found a big toy called a microscope. She was very excited to play with it. The microscope was enormous, much bigger than her other toys. Amy knew that it could help her see tiny things up close. Amy went to her garden and found a small bug. She put the bug on the microscope and looked through it. The bug looked very big and she could see its legs and wings. Amy was very happy to see the tiny bug so clearly. Amy wanted to show her mommy the bug. She was so excited that she gave the microscope a tight squeeze. The bug was safe and Amy's mommy was happy to see her daughter's new toy. They both had fun looking at the tiny bug through the enormous microscope.

Lily was a little girl who loved to play with her blue ball. She would bounce it, roll it, and throw it in the air. She would take it with her everywhere she went. She thought her blue ball was the best toy in the world. One day, she went to the park with her mom and dad. She saw some other kids playing with different toys. She saw a red bike, a yellow kite, and a green frog. They looked fun, but not as fun as her blue ball. She ran to join them and said, "Hi, can I play with you?" The other kids looked at her and said, "Sure, you can play with us. But you have to share your blue ball. We want to try it too." Lily felt scared. She did not want to share her blue ball. She thought it was hers and only hers. She said, "No, you can't have my blue ball. It belongs to me. Go away!" The other kids felt sad and angry. They said, "That's not nice. You are being selfish. If you don't share, we don't want to play with you. Go away!" Lily felt lonely and confused. She did not understand why the other kids did not like her. She hugged her blue ball and ran back to her mom and dad. She said, "Mom, dad, why don't they want to play with me? I have a blue ball. It's the best toy in the world." Her mom and dad smiled and hugged her. They said, "Lily, we love you and your blue ball. But sometimes, it's good to share with others. Sharing makes friends. Friends make you happy. You can still keep your blue ball, but you can also let others play with it. Then, you can play with their toys too. It's more fun that way. Do you want to try?" Lily thought for a moment. She felt a little sleepy. She said, "Maybe. But not now. I'm tired. Can we go home?" Her mom and dad said, "Okay, we can go home. But tomorrow, we can come back to the park. And maybe, you can make some new friends. How does that sound?" Lily nodded and smiled. She said, "Okay. That sounds good. I love you, mom and dad. And I love my blue ball. But maybe, I can share it too." She closed her eyes and fell asleep in her mom's arms. She dreamed of playing with her blue ball and her new friends. She felt happy.
