Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Cat and the Sun

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl and her pet cat. The girl had a shiny crystal that she loved. The crystal was her favorite toy. She would play with it every day. One day, the sun was very harsh. It was too hot to play outside. So, the little girl and her cat decided to take a nap. They lay down in the cool shade of their house. The girl held her crystal close to her as they both fell asleep. While they napped, the crystal started to glow. It made the room fill with nice colors. The girl and her cat had happy dreams. When they woke up, the sun was not harsh anymore. They went outside to play, and the girl knew her special crystal had helped them have a good nap.

A mom and her 3-year-old daughter Lucy were having a good time in the park. Lucy was running around, smiling and enjoying the nature. Suddenly, she heard a loud and scary noise. She stopped in her tracks and looked around, not knowing what was going on. Mom saw that Lucy was scared and walked up to her. Mom said, “What’s wrong, Lucy? Is something scaring you?” Lucy pointed to a bush nearby. “It was a loud and scary noise from that bush. I don’t like it!” she said. Mom smiled and said, “That’s okay. Let’s try and solve this.” So Mom and Lucy slowly went up to the bush. Lucy was scared but bravely followed her mom. Together, they looked inside the bush and saw a little bird flying away. Mom said, “See, Lucy. It was only a little bird. Now we solved the mystery!” Lucy smiled and said, “Yay! I’m not scared anymore. Nature is so amazing!”

One day, a little boy named Tim found a smooth stone. He liked how it felt in his hand. He took the stone to his desk and put it down. Tim went to sleep. When Tim woke up, he saw something new on his desk. It was a little green plant. It appeared right next to the smooth stone. Tim was very happy. He liked the plant a lot. Every day, Tim took care of the plant. He gave it water and sun. The plant grew big and strong. Tim and the plant were very happy together. And the smooth stone stayed on the desk, always with them.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toy car. One day, Tim saw an expensive toy car in the store. He had an urge to buy it. But his mom said no, it was too much money. Tim was sad, but he tried to remember his mom's words. He knew she was right. But the urge to have the expensive car was strong. So, Tim took some money from his mom's purse when she was not looking. He went to the store and bought the expensive car. Tim played with it all day. But then, his mom found out. She was very mad and sad. Tim felt bad for what he did. He knew he should have listened to his mom. Now, Tim had to give the car back and he was in trouble. The end.

Lily liked to feed her plants. She had a big pot with a green plant and a small pot with a red flower. She gave them water every day and talked to them. She wanted them to grow big and pretty. One day, she saw a bag of soil in the garden. She thought it was food for her plants. She took some soil and put it in her pots. She hoped her plants would like it and grow faster. But the next day, her plants looked sad. The green plant had brown leaves and the red flower had no petals. Lily was sad too. She did not know what she did wrong. She asked her mom for help. Her mom looked at the pots and saw the soil. She explained to Lily that soil was not food for plants. It was where plants lived and got some things they needed. But too much soil could hurt them. She said Lily was impatient and wanted her plants to grow too quickly. She helped Lily take out some of the soil and put it back in the bag. She told Lily to be gentle and patient with her plants. She said they would grow if she gave them water, sun and love. Lily said sorry to her plants and hugged them. She learned to feed them the right way.
