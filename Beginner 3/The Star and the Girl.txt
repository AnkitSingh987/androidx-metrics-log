Category: Beginner
Title: The Star and the Girl

Once upon a time, there was a friendly star. The star lived up high in the sky. Every night, the star would sign to all the kids on Earth. One night, a little girl saw the star. She waved and the star signed back. They became very good friends. The little girl would talk to the star every night. One day, the friendly star had to go away. The star signed goodbye to the little girl. The little girl was sad, but she knew her friend would come back. And when the star came back, they were happy again.

One day, Tim went to the kitchen to eat breakfast. He wanted to eat cereal. He took a big bowl and poured the cereal in it. Then, he added milk. Tim took a spoon and began to stir the cereal. It looked delicious. Just then, Tim's friend, Sam, came over. Sam looked at the cereal and said, "Yum! That looks delicious. Can I have some too?" Tim smiled and said, "Sure! Let's share." They both took spoons and began to eat the cereal. As they were eating, something unexpected happened. A tiny, funny-looking creature jumped out of the cereal box! It was a cereal-loving elf! The elf said, "Hello, friends! I love cereal too. Can I join you?" Tim and Sam laughed and said, "Of course! Let's all eat cereal together!" And they all enjoyed the delicious cereal, laughing and talking with their new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was a very generous girl. She loved to share her toys and treats with her friends. One day, Lucy found a shiny coin on the ground. She was very excited and wanted to find a way to share her new coin with her friends. Lucy had an idea. She went to the store with her mom and used her coin to buy a big bag of candy. Lucy was excited to share the candy with her friends. She put the candy in her bag and went to the park to find her friends. At the park, Lucy saw her friends playing on the swings. She ran up to them and pulled the candy out of her bag. "Look what I have!" she said. Her friends were so happy to see the candy. They all sat down and enjoyed the sweet treats together. Lucy felt happy that she could be generous with her coin and make her friends smile.

Lily was bored. She did not want to play with her toys. She did not want to watch TV. She did not want to read a book. She wanted to do something fun. She heard a noise in the kitchen. She ran to see what it was. She saw her mom making a cake. She saw eggs, flour, sugar, and milk. She wanted to help. "Can I help, mom?" she asked. "Sure, Lily. You can pour the milk in the bowl," her mom said. Lily took the milk and poured it in the bowl. She felt happy. She liked to help her mom. She liked to make a cake. She heard a beep. It was the oven. The cake was ready. "Can I see the cake, mom?" she asked. "Of course, Lily. But be careful, it's hot," her mom said. She saw the cake. It was big and brown and smelled good. She wanted to eat it. "Can I have a piece, mom?" she asked. "Yes, Lily. But first, let it cool down a bit," her mom said. Lily waited. She was not bored anymore. She was excited. She made a cake with her mom. She was going to eat a piece. She was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tom. Tom was a good and reliable friend. He liked to play with his friends in the park. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. Tom and his friends were full of youth and happiness. One sunny day, Tom found a big, red apple under a tree. He picked it up and showed it to his friends. They all wanted to taste it. So, Tom decided to share the apple with them. He took a big bite and started to chew it. The apple was sweet and juicy. Tom's friends took turns to chew the apple too. They all loved it. After they finished the apple, they played some more. They were happy and thankful to have a reliable friend like Tom. And from that day on, they always shared their treats with each other.
