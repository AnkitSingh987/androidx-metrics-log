Category: Beginner
Title: The Clumsy Bear and the Woods

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy bear. He was walking in the woods when he saw something strange - a TV! He had never seen one before and was curious to know more. He went up to the TV, but was startled when he heard a voice come from it. "What are you doing here?" the voice asked. The bear was so afraid he froze in place. Finally he said, "I just wanted to see what this was?" The voice then replied, "This is mine, and you can't have it. Now go away." The bear didn't want to leave empty handed, so he said, "If I can't have the TV, then can I at least bury it?" The voice thought for a moment and replied, "That's a good idea. You can have the TV if you bury it deep in the ground." So the bear dug a hole, put the TV in and covered it up. He was so clumsy he had to try a few times before he got it all buried. When he finished, he felt proud that he had buried the TV so deep. The end.

Tom is a famous cat. He likes to sing and dance on TV. Many people watch him and clap. He is happy and proud. One day, Tom has to go to a big show. He wears a nice hat and a shiny bow. He gets in a car and drives to the stage. He is ready to have fun. But when he gets there, he sees something bad. On his plate, there is broccoli. Tom hates broccoli. He thinks it is yucky and green. He does not want to eat it. He tries to hide it under his napkin. But the broccoli is sneaky. It pokes out of the napkin and tickles Tom's nose. Tom feels a big sneeze coming. He tries to stop it, but he can't. He sneezes very loud and hard. The sneeze makes his hat fly off his head. It also makes his bow come undone. His fur gets messy and his eyes get watery. He looks funny and silly. The people who watch him laugh. They think he is joking. They clap and cheer. Tom is confused and embarrassed. He does not like broccoli. He does not like sneezing. He does not like laughing. He runs off the stage and hides in his car. He wants to go home and cuddle with his toy mouse. He does not want to be famous anymore. He just wants to be a normal cat.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play and talk. They talk about many things. They talk about toys, animals, colors and stories. They have fun. One day, they see a big bus. The bus has many pictures on it. The pictures are of famous people. They sing and dance and make movies. Lily and Tom want to see them. They want to talk to them. They think they are cool. They run to the bus. They do not look around. They do not see the car. The car is fast. The car does not stop. The car hits Lily and Tom. They fall down. They do not get up. They do not talk anymore. The bus stops. The famous people come out. They see Lily and Tom. They are sad. They cry. They call for help. But it is too late. Lily and Tom are gone. The end.

Once upon a time, there was an adorable little dog named Spot. He loved to run, jump, and play all day. Spot had a favorite toy, a pair of pants that he liked to carry around in his mouth. One sunny day, Spot was playing in the yard with his pants toy. He was running very fast and having so much fun. But then, he did not see a puddle of water on the ground. Spot ran right into the puddle and started to slip and slide. Spot slid across the yard, still holding onto his pants toy. He was scared at first, but then he started to laugh. Slipping and sliding in the puddle was so much fun! Spot played in the puddle all day, and when he was done, he curled up with his pants toy and took a nap.
