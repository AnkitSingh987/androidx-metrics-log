Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Window

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play in his backyard. One day, it began to rain. Tim was sad because he could not play outside. He looked out the window and saw his toy car in the rain. Tim's mom saw him and said, "Tim, don't worry. We can play inside until the rain stops." Tim felt a little better, but he was still sad about his toy car. As they played inside, Tim's dog, Max, was alert. He saw the toy car in the backyard. Max ran outside and brought the toy car back inside. Tim was so happy! He hugged Max and said, "Thank you, Max!" Then they all played together until the rain stopped.

Bob was a young boy. He was three years old and he loved animals. One day, he decided to go to the farm with his mommy. At the farm, there were many animals! Bob was so excited. He wanted to pick one special animal to bring home. He looked at the horses, the chickens and the cows. He finally decided on a cute little goat with white fur. His mommy said it was time to leave, but Bob didn't want to go yet. He was embarrassed because he couldn't choose between the animals. He wanted to take them all! Bob's mommy wanted to make him feel better. She brought him some hay to feed the goat. Bob was so happy! He knew that he had made the right choice. The goat said goodbye to all the other animals, and Bob and his mommy went home. Bob felt proud that he chose the goat. He knew that it would be a very special friend!

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big sign in the park. The sign has many colors and pictures. It says: "Come and dance with us!" Lily and Ben are curious. They want to see what the sign means. They follow the sign to a big tent. Inside the tent, they hear music and see people dancing. They see people wearing funny hats and costumes. They see people spinning and jumping and clapping. "Wow, this looks fun!" Lily says. "Can we dance too?" "Yes, of course!" a lady with a big smile says. She gives Lily and Ben each a hat and a ribbon. "You can join us anytime. We are having a dance party. Everyone is welcome!" Lily and Ben are happy. They put on their hats and ribbons. They follow the lady to the dance floor. They copy the moves of the other dancers. They laugh and have fun. They dance with the music and the people. They dance with each other. They dance until they are tired. They thank the lady and the other dancers. They say goodbye to the sign. They go back to the park. They are still smiling. "That was the best dance ever!" Ben says. "I agree!" Lily says. "Let's do it again sometime!" They hug and go home. They dream of dancing.

Sam and Lily were friends. They liked to play with dolls and cars. One day, they went to the park with their moms. They saw a big slide and a swing. They wanted to get on them. "Let's play on the slide first," Sam said. "OK," Lily said. They ran to the slide and waited for their turn. Sam went first. He climbed up the ladder and slid down. He laughed and said, "That was fun!" Lily followed him. She climbed up the ladder and slid down. But she slid too fast and fell off the slide. She hurt her knee and cried. "Ow, ow, ow! That was terrible!" Sam ran to her and helped her up. He said, "I'm sorry, Lily. Are you OK?" Lily nodded. She said, "It's OK, Sam. It's just a scratch. Let's play on the swing instead." They went to the swing and took turns pushing each other. They had fun and forgot about the slide. They played until their moms called them. They said goodbye and hugged each other. They were still friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big oak tree in a forest. Many animals lived in the oak tree. One day, a brave squirrel named Sam wanted to help his friends. Sam saw a big leaf stuck in the tree. He wanted to remove it so his friends could play. Sam climbed up the tree and pulled the leaf with all his might. The leaf came off, and all the animals were happy. Sam felt proud of what he did. All his friends cheered for him. They played together under the oak tree all day long. The brave squirrel had made their home better.
