Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Max and the Ball

One day, a funny cat named Tom and a creative dog named Max were playing in the park. They were best friends. Tom and Max loved to play with a big, red ball. They also loved to make music together. Tom played the drums, and Max played the piano. As they played, a little girl named Lily came to the park. She heard their music and started to dance. "Wow, I like your music!" Lily said with a big smile. Tom and Max were happy that Lily liked their music. They played even louder. Suddenly, a big wind came and took the red ball away. Tom, Max, and Lily were sad. But then, Lily had an idea. "Let's make a new game with our music!" she said. They all laughed and started to dance and sing. The wind brought the ball back, and they all played together. They had the best day ever.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing outside. He saw a frightened cat stuck in a tree. Tim wanted to help the cat, so he tried to climb the tree. As Tim climbed higher, his pants got stuck on a branch. "Oh no!" he cried. "My pants are stuck!" The frightened cat looked at Tim and said, "Meow, meow!" They both needed help. Just then, a big bird flew by and saw them. The bird was not scared at all. It flew up to the branch and helped Tim get his pants free. Then, the bird helped the frightened cat down from the tree. Tim and the cat were so happy. They both said, "Thank you, bird!" The bird smiled and flew away.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a cat sitting by a tree. The cat looked sad. Lily thought it was difficult for the cat to be happy. "Hi cat," said Lily. "Why are you sad?" The cat looked at Lily and said, "I lost my toy. It is hard for me to find it." Lily wanted to help the cat. She started to observe and look around for the toy. Lily saw something under a bush. She went closer and saw the toy! She picked it up and gave it to the cat. The cat was very happy. "Thank you, Lily!" said the cat. "You are a good friend." And from that day on, Lily and the cat were best friends.

Lily liked to play with her dolls. She had many dolls, but her favorite was Anna. Anna had long hair and a pretty dress. Lily liked to brush Anna's hair and make her look nice. One day, Lily's mom came home with a new doll. She gave it to Lily and said, "This is Elsa. She is Anna's sister. She is a princess too. Do you want to introduce her to Anna?" Lily was happy to have a new doll. She said, "Yes, mom. Thank you for Elsa. She is very pretty." She took Elsa and went to her room. She put Elsa next to Anna on her bed. She said, "Hello, Anna. This is Elsa. She is your sister. She is a princess too. Can you be friends?" Anna smiled and said, "Hello, Elsa. I am happy to meet you. You are very pretty too. Yes, we can be friends." She gave Elsa a hug. Elsa hugged her back and said, "Thank you, Anna. You are very kind. I am happy to be your sister and friend." Lily watched them and smiled. She said, "You are both very nice. I love you both. Do you want to play with me?" Anna and Elsa said, "Yes, Lily. We want to play with you. You are our best friend." Lily said, "Let's play massage. I will massage your hair and make you feel good. You can massage each other too. It is fun and relaxing." She took a comb and started to massage Anna's hair. Anna closed her eyes and said, "That feels good, Lily. Thank you." Elsa watched and said, "Can I try too?" Lily said, "Yes, Elsa. You can massage Anna's hair too. Be gentle and graceful. Like this." She showed Elsa how to comb Anna's hair softly and smoothly. Elsa nodded and said, "OK, Lily. I will try." She took the comb and started to massage Anna's hair too. Anna said, "You are doing well, Elsa. That feels good too. Thank you." Lily, Anna, and Elsa played massage for a long time. They massaged each other's hair and said nice things to each other. They felt happy and calm. They were a happy family of dolls and friends.
