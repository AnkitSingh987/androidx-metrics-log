Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Sue and the Pond

Tom and Sue are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big fly. It is smooth and shiny. It has wings and legs and eyes. "Look, a fly!" Tom says. "I want to catch it." "No, don't catch it," Sue says. "It is not nice. The fly will be sad." But Tom does not listen. He runs after the fly. He tries to grab it with his hand. The fly is fast. It flies away. Tom is angry. He wants the fly. He runs faster and faster. He does not see the pond. He falls into the water. Splash! Sue runs to help him. She sits on the grass. She holds out her hand. "Tom, are you okay?" she asks. Tom is wet and cold. He is sorry. He takes Sue's hand. He gets out of the pond. "I am okay," he says. "But I am sorry. You were right. I should not catch the fly. The fly is free. It is happy." They hug. They are friends. They see the fly again. It flies over their heads. It makes a buzz sound. "Bye, fly," they say. "Have a good day." They smile. They go home. They are happy too.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a very hungry boy named Tim. Tim was always looking for food to eat. One day, Tim saw a big sack in his yard. He was very excited and ran to the sack. When Tim opened the sack, he saw a lot of yummy food inside. He was so happy! He wanted to share the food with his friend, Sam. So, Tim called out, "Sam, come here! Look at this sight!" Sam came running and saw the sack full of food. "Wow!" Sam said, "Let's eat!" Tim and Sam sat down and ate the food together. They were not hungry anymore. They were very happy and played all day long.

Once upon a time there was a cat named Trevor. Trevor was a big fluffy cat with a long tail. He was always so curious and loved to explore. One day, Trevor was minding his own business walking around town. He smelled something that was really sour and was too curious not to find out what it was. So Trevor followed the smell deep into the forest. At the edge of the forest, he found an old abandoned cabin. He could smell the sour smell was coming from inside. Trevor slowly opened the door and saw a large pot of a sour stew. He looked around and still no one was there. He decided to take a small taste of the stew with his tail. It was sour and he didn't like it at all. He closed the door and quickly ran away back home. From then on, Trevor made sure to mind his own business and stay away from the cabin!

Once upon a time, there was a bear named Bob. Bob liked to wear a big red hat. One day, Bob went to the lumber store to buy some wood. He saw many expensive things there. He wanted to buy them all, but he had only a little money. Bob thought hard about what to do. He decided to buy only what he needed. He bought the wood and went home. At home, Bob made a nice chair with the lumber. He felt happy that he did not spend all his money on expensive things. Bob learned that it is good to be wise with money. He understood that buying only what you need is important. Bob's big red hat reminded him of this lesson every day. And that is how Bob the bear became a wise and happy bear.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a tiny bug stuck in a sour lemon. The bug was sad and wanted to get out. Lily wanted to help the bug, so she used her finger to rescue it. "Thank you for helping me," said the bug. "You are very kind." Lily smiled and said, "You're welcome, little bug. I am happy to help you." The bug was free and happy now. It flew away and found a sweet flower to live in. Lily felt good for helping the bug and went to tell her mom about her new friend.
