Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toys. One day, he wanted to play with his toy car. But he could not find it. He looked and looked but it was not there. Tim was sad. Then, Tim's mommy came into the room. She saw that Tim was sad. She asked him what was wrong. Tim told her he could not find his toy car. Mommy said, "Let's look together." They looked in all the places where toys were available. As they looked, Tim noticed a big box. He had a memory of putting toys in the box. Tim and his mommy opened the box. Inside, they found the toy car! Tim was so happy. He thanked his mommy for helping him. From that day on, Tim remembered to put his toys away in the box. That way, he could always find them when he wanted to play. Tim and his mommy played with the toy car and had lots of fun together.

Once, there was a little girl who found a jewel. She brought it to her mommy, who was very serious and said, "This is a very special jewel. It needs to stay safe with us." The little girl was sad, but her mommy gave her a hug to comfort her. The next day, the little girl was playing in her garden when a stranger came by. He asked her if she had found a special jewel. When the little girl said yes, the stranger became very serious and he grabbed the jewel and ran away! The little girl was very upset, but her mommy was there to comfort her. She told her that she was brave for telling the truth and gave her a special hug. Although the little girl was comforted by her mommy, she still felt sad. The jewel was gone forever and the little girl was reminded not to trust strangers.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little bunny named Ben. Ben was very bored. He had no fun games to play. One day, Ben saw a big horn on the ground. He picked it up and started to chew on it. It was fun to chew the horn. Ben's friend, a bird named Tim, saw him chewing the horn. Tim told Ben that the horn was magic. If you chew it, something fun will happen. Ben did not believe Tim. He thought it was a boring horn. But Ben kept chewing the horn. Suddenly, a big slide appeared in the forest. Ben and Tim were so happy. They played on the slide all day long. The boring horn was not boring after all. It was a magic horn that made their day fun.

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play in the park. They had a big slide, a swing, and a sandbox. They also had a dog named Spot who followed them everywhere. One day, they saw a sign that said "No dogs allowed". They were sad because they wanted to play with Spot. They asked their mom if they could take Spot to the park. Their mom said no, the sign did not permit it. She said Spot had to stay at home. Tom and Lily did not want to leave Spot alone. They had an idea. They said they would go to the store and buy a treat for Spot. Their mom said yes, they could do that. She gave them some money and told them to be careful. Tom and Lily took Spot with them to the store. They bought a bone for Spot and hid it in their backpack. Then they went to the park. They pretended they did not have Spot with them. They put Spot in the sandbox and covered him with sand. They said he was a sand castle. They played on the slide and the swing. They had fun. But then a man came and saw Spot in the sandbox. He was angry. He said dogs were not allowed in the park. He said he would call the animal control. He said Spot was a bad dog. Tom and Lily were scared. They did not want to lose Spot. They said Spot was a good dog. They said they loved Spot. They said they bought a treat for Spot. They showed the man the bone in their backpack. The man looked at the bone. He looked at Spot. He looked at Tom and Lily. He saw they were sorry. He saw they were happy. He changed his mind. He said he would not call the animal control. He said he would permit Spot to stay in the park. He said he liked dogs too. He said he had a dog at home. Tom and Lily were happy. They thanked the man. They gave Spot the bone. They played with Spot in the park. They had a great day. They learned a lesson. They learned to follow the rules. They learned to be honest. They learned to be kind.
