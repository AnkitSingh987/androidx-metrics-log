Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben and the Magic

Anna and Ben like to play with their jeep. It is big and red and has four wheels. They can push it and make it go fast. They can also sit inside and pretend to drive. One day, they find a magic wand in the park. It is shiny and has a star on the end. Anna and Ben think it is a toy. They wave it and say funny words. But the wand is not a toy. It is a real magic wand. And it does strange things. When Anna waves it at the jeep, the jeep shrinks. It becomes very small. Smaller than a bug. Anna and Ben are shocked. They can't play with the jeep anymore. They try to make the jeep big again. They wave the wand and say sorry. But nothing happens. The jeep stays small. Anna and Ben are sad. They don't know what to do. Then they see a friendly ant. The ant is helpful. It sees the small jeep and likes it. It picks it up and carries it to its home. It shows it to its friends. The ants are happy. They have a new toy. Anna and Ben follow the ant. They see the jeep in the ant home. They ask the ant to give it back. The ant is kind. It gives the jeep back to Anna and Ben. They thank the ant and say goodbye. They wave the wand again and hope for the best. This time, the jeep grows. It becomes big again. Bigger than before. Anna and Ben are happy. They hug the jeep and the wand. They decide to keep the wand as a secret. They don't want to shrink anything else. They go home and play with the jeep. They have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to learn about history. He wanted to introduce his friends to history too. One day, Tim took his friends to a big museum. In the museum, they saw old things from long ago. Tim's friends were very happy. They liked history too. Then, they met a green frog. The frog was envious of Tim and his friends. The frog wanted to learn about history as well. Tim and his friends introduced the frog to history. Now, they all learned together.

Once upon a time, there was a little frog named Bob. He lived in a pond with his family. Bob was a weak frog, but he had a big heart. One day, it started to rain. All the other frogs ran to hide under the big leaves. But Bob saw a small bug who was stuck in the rain. He felt sad for the bug and wanted to help. Bob hopped over to the bug and gave it a kiss. Suddenly, the bug turned into a beautiful butterfly! The butterfly thanked Bob for his kindness and flew away. The other frogs saw this and learned that even if you are weak, you can do great things if you have a big heart.

One day, a girl named Mia went outside to play. She saw a pretty rainbow in the sky. Mia loved the colors and wanted to show her friend, Tom. Tom was deaf, so he could not hear Mia. But he could see the rainbow when she pointed to it. Tom was happy to see the rainbow too. They both smiled and clapped their hands. Mia and Tom wanted to keep the rainbow. They asked the sky to give them the rainbow. The sky was nice and let them receive the rainbow. Mia and Tom played with the rainbow all day. They were very happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim was eager to play outside. He put on his warm coat, hat, and gloves. The cold air made him shiver, but he was excited. He saw the ground was white with snow. He tried to freeze the snow with his breath, but it was already frozen! Tim saw a big tree with a hole in it. He walked closer and found a small, wet book inside the hole. "Who put this book here?" he asked. Tim wanted to read the book, but the pages were stuck together. He felt sad. Just then, a squirrel came down from the tree. "That's my book!" the squirrel said. "I hid it there to keep it safe." Tim gave the book back to the squirrel. The squirrel was happy and said, "Thank you, Tim!" They both smiled and played in the snow until it was time for Tim to go home.
