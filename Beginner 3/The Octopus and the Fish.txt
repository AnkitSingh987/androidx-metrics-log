Category: Beginner
Title: The Octopus and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a big octopus. He lived in the sea. One day, he met a small fish. The fish was sad. "I am lost," said the fish. "Can you help me find my mom?" The octopus wanted to help the fish. They swam together. They saw a big, dead tree in the water. The octopus said, "Let's look here." The fish was scared. "I don't know if I can trust you," said the fish. The octopus said, "Trust me, I will help you." They looked around the dead tree. They found the fish's mom! The fish was very happy. The octopus and the fish became good friends. The moral of the story is to trust others when they want to help.

One day, a big bear named Ben went to the forest. He met a little bird with a sad face. The bird was sitting on a tree branch. Ben asked the bird, "Why are you sad?" The bird said, "I want to weigh myself, but I don't know how." Ben thought for a moment and had an idea. He found a big rock and put it on one side of a seesaw. Then he asked the bird to sit on the other side. As the bird sat on the seesaw, a spirit came out from the tree. The spirit was happy and said, "Thank you for helping my friend, the bird. As a gift, I will make you both fly!" The spirit waved its hand, and Ben and the bird started to fly high in the sky. They were so happy and laughed as they flew together. From that day on, the big bear and the little bird became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a penny. He loved his penny very much. He would play with it and repeat, "My penny is the best!" One day, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue also had a penny. Sue said, "My penny is better than your penny!" Tim did not like that. They both became competitive. They wanted to see whose penny was the best. Tim and Sue had a race. They rolled their pennies down a hill. Tim's penny went very fast. Sue's penny went fast too. They both watched their pennies roll down the hill, hoping to win. At the bottom of the hill, the pennies stopped. Tim's penny was first, and Sue's penny was second. Tim was happy. He said, "My penny is the best!" Sue was sad, but she said, "Let's race again another day." From that day on, Tim and Sue became friends. They played with their pennies together. They learned that it was more fun to play together than to be competitive. And they always remembered to share and be kind.

Once upon a time, there was a shy boy named Tim. Tim loved to play chess with his friends. He would play chess every day after school. One day, Tim saw a little girl sitting alone. She looked sad. Tim wanted to help her, so he hurried to her side. "Hi, I'm Tim. Do you want to play chess with me?" he asked. The little girl smiled and said, "Yes, I would love to play chess." They played chess together and became good friends. From that day on, Tim and the little girl played chess every day, and they were never shy again.

Lily was a big sister. She had a baby brother named Tom. Tom was very small and liked to sleep a lot. Lily wanted to play with Tom, but Mom said he was too little. One day, Lily saw Tom move his arms and legs in his crib. He made a funny noise. Lily ran to him and said, "Hi, Tom! Do you want to play with me?" She picked up a soft toy and showed it to him. Tom looked at the toy and smiled. He reached out his hand and grabbed it. He put it in his mouth and made another funny noise. Lily laughed and said, "That's not how you play with it, silly! You have to shake it and squeeze it. Like this!" She took the toy from him and demonstrated. Tom watched her and tried to copy her. He shook the toy and squeezed it. He giggled and dropped it. Lily picked it up and gave it back to him. She said, "You are very good at playing, Tom! You are a smart baby!" Mom came in and saw them. She smiled and said, "What a generous sister you are, Lily! You are sharing your toy with Tom. He loves you very much." She hugged them both and said, "You are my sweet children. I love you very much too."
