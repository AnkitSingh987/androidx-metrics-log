Category: Beginner
Title: The King and the Castle

Once upon a time, in a big castle, there was a king. The king was very foolish. He did not like to listen to others. People did not like the king because he always made bad choices. One day, a little girl came to the castle. She said, "King, why do you hate listening to others? It is not good." The king was surprised. No one had ever talked to him like that before. The little girl told the king that if he listened to others, he would make better choices. The king thought about it and decided to try. He started to listen to the people in his kingdom. They were happy and did not think he was foolish anymore. The king learned that listening was important and he became a better king.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat. The cat was very hungry. It wanted to find some food to eat. It looked for food all day. It found a big piece of meat. The cat was very happy. The cat wanted to eat the meat. But the meat was not clean. The cat did not know that. It was too hungry to think. So, the cat started to feed on the meat. It ate and ate until it was full. After the cat ate the meat, it did not feel good. Its tummy hurt a lot. The cat was sad because it ate the dirty meat. The cat was sick and had to stay in bed. It wished it had found clean food to eat.

One day, a fat cat named Max went to the park. He saw a bean on the ground. Max thought the bean looked good to eat. He picked it up and started to walk home. On the way, he met his friend, a dog named Sam. "Hi Max," said Sam. "What do you have there?" Max showed Sam the bean. "I found a bean. I want to eat it, but I miss my mom. She makes the best beans." Sam had an idea. "Let's go to your house and ask your mom to cook the bean!" When they got to Max's house, his mom was surprised. "Max, this is not a bean, it's a magic bean!" She told them to plant the bean in the yard. Max and Sam were excited. They planted the bean and watered it. The next day, the bean had grown into a big beanstalk. Max and Sam climbed it all the way to the sky. At the top, they found a giant's house with lots of food. Max and Sam ate and played all day. They had so much fun, they almost forgot about the bean. But they knew they had to go home before it got dark. Max and Sam climbed back down the beanstalk and went home. They were happy and full from their adventure. They knew they would never forget the magic bean and the fun they had with the giant.

One day, a cat named Tom found a weird tray. The tray was big and had many colors. Tom wanted to act like a big cat, so he thought he could carry the tray. He picked it up with his mouth and walked around. While Tom was walking, he saw his friend, a dog named Max. Max saw the tray and wanted to help Tom. Max held the other side of the tray with his mouth. Together, they carried the tray to a big tree. When they put the tray down, something unexpected happened. The tray turned into a big, fun slide! Tom and Max were very happy. They took turns sliding down the slide and playing together. The weird tray was a magic tray that made their day full of fun.

Once upon a time there was a little boy named Tom. Tom wanted something yummy for his snack. He spotted his mom's garden. In the garden there were carrots. The carrots had long pointed roots. Tom was very excited. Tom said to himself, “Yummy, I will pick some carrots.” He carefully dug out the carrots and put them in his basket. He spread their roots on the ground. Tom's mom saw him from the window. She opened the window and said, “Tom! What are you doing?” Tom said, “I want something yummy for my snack. So I picked some carrots and spread their roots on the ground." His mom said, “That is a great idea! Come in and wash the carrots. Then we can make something yummy for you.” Tom smiled and ran back to the house. He washed the carrots and his mom cooked them into something yummy. Tom was very happy and enjoyed his snack.
