Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Cat

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play with dough. They have red dough, blue dough, yellow dough and green dough. They can make shapes and animals with dough. One day, Lily and Ben are playing with dough in the kitchen. Lily makes a dough cat. She gives it ears, eyes, nose and tail. She is happy with her cat. She shows it to Ben. "Look, Ben, I made a cat. Do you like it?" Lily asks. Ben is busy making a dough car. He gives it wheels, windows, doors and lights. He is happy with his car. He ignores Lily. "Ben, look at my cat. It is cute. Do you like it?" Lily asks again. Ben still ignores Lily. He pretends he does not hear her. He makes his car go vroom vroom on the table. Lily feels sad. She thinks Ben does not like her cat. She thinks Ben does not want to play with her. She picks up her cat and goes to the living room. She finds a quiet spot on the sofa. She hugs her cat and says, "I like you, cat. You are my friend." Mama sees Lily and Ben. She sees Lily is sad and Ben is ignoring her. She goes to the kitchen and talks to Ben. "Ben, why are you ignoring Lily? She made a cat with dough. She wants to show you. She wants to play with you. Don't you like her cat?" Mama asks. Ben looks at Mama. He feels sorry. He says, "I like her cat. It is cute. But I like my car more. It is cool. I want to play with my car." Mama says, "I understand. You can play with your car. But you can also play with Lily. She is your sister. She loves you. You can share your dough and make more things together. It will be more fun." Ben thinks. He says, "You are right, Mama. I love Lily too. I will share my dough and play with her. I will say sorry to her." Ben takes his car and goes to the living room. He sees Lily on the sofa. He says, "Lily, I am sorry. I was ignoring you. I like your cat. It is cute. Do you want to see my car? It is cool. We can play with dough together. We can make more cats and cars and other things. Do you want to?" Lily looks at Ben. She smiles. She says, "Yes, Ben, I want to. I am sorry too. I was sad. I like your car. It is cool. We can play with dough together. We can make more things. We can be friends." Lily and Ben hug. They take their dough and go back to the kitchen. They share their dough and make more shapes and animals. They have fun. Mama is happy. She says, "That's my good twins. I am proud of you."

---

Lily and Ben were playing pirates in the garden. They had a big box that was their ship, and a map that showed them where to find treasure. They wore hats and eye patches and said "Arrr!" a lot. "Look, Ben, the map says the treasure is under the big tree!" Lily said, pointing to a spot on the paper. "Let's go, Lily, maybe we will find gold and jewels!" Ben said, grabbing a shovel. They ran to the tree and started to dig a hole. They dug and dug, but they did not find any treasure. They only found dirt and rocks and worms. They were very sad and tired. "Maybe the map is wrong, Lily. Maybe there is no treasure here," Ben said, dropping the shovel. "Or maybe someone else took it already, Ben. Maybe a bad pirate or a sneaky squirrel," Lily said, sitting on the ground. They felt very dizzy from digging so much. They felt like they were on a boat that was rocking on the waves. They closed their eyes and rested. When they opened their eyes, they saw something shiny in the hole. It was a necklace with a big red heart. It sparkled in the sun. "Wow, Lily, look! We did find treasure after all!" Ben said, picking up the necklace. "It's so pretty, Ben! Maybe it was a gift from a good pirate or a friendly fairy," Lily said, admiring the necklace. They felt very happy and proud. They put the necklace around Lily's neck and hugged each other. "We are the best pirates ever, Lily. We found the most beautiful treasure in the world," Ben said. "Yes, we are, Ben. And you are my best friend in the world," Lily said. They smiled and went back to their ship, ready for more adventures.
