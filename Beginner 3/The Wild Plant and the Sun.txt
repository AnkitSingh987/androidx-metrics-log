Category: Beginner
Title: The Wild Plant and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a wild plant. It grew in a big garden. The wild plant was green and tall. It had many friends like the sun, the rain, and the wind. The wild plant loved to dance with the wind. One day, a little bug came to the wild plant. The bug was small and liked to pinch. The wild plant did not like the pinch. It made the wild plant feel sad. The wild plant wanted the bug to go away. The sun, the rain, and the wind saw the wild plant was sad. They wanted to help their friend. The sun shone bright, the rain fell soft, and the wind blew strong. The little bug did not like this. The bug stopped pinching and went away. The wild plant was happy again. The wild plant and its friends lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to a fun place with his mom. They saw many kids playing and having fun. Tim was so excited that he became impatient. He wanted to play with the other kids right away. Tim's mom said, "Wait, Tim! I have to talk to the other moms first." But Tim didn't want to wait. He saw a girl with a pretty red ball. He walked up to her and said, "Hi, can I play with you?" The girl smiled and said, "Sure! My name is Lily. Let's play catch!" As they played, a big wind came and took the ball far away. Tim and Lily were sad. They didn't know what to do. Then, a nice man saw them and waved. He had their red ball! Tim and Lily were so happy. The nice man said, "I found your ball! Be careful next time." Tim and Lily said, "Thank you!" They played together for the rest of the day, and Tim learned that sometimes, good things can happen when you least expect it.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a big yellow ball in the sky. The yellow ball was the sun. Lily was happy to see the sun because it made her feel warm. As she walked, Lily saw a march. Many people were in the march. They were all wearing yellow clothes. Lily thought it was fun to watch them march. She clapped her hands and danced along. Then, something unexpected happened. The yellow ball in the sky started to move. It came down and joined the march! The sun was not a ball at all, but a big yellow balloon. Lily laughed and clapped her hands. She was happy to see the yellow balloon in the march.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Zara. She loved to do everything herself, especially when it came to getting dressed in the morning. One day, Zara's mom said to her, "Zara, it's time to get ready. Please put on your dress." Zara happily picked out her dress from her closet and put it on. She couldn't wait to show her mom what she was wearing, so she ran to her mom's room. When she opened the door, Zara said, "Mom, look at me! I'm so cool!" Her mom smiled and said, "You look beautiful! Now let's put the buttons together." "Buttons?", thought Zara. She had never done buttons before, but luckily her mom was there to help. Together, they worked to prepare the buttons, and soon they were all fastened together. Zara felt very proud of herself. She had been so cool and helped to prepare the buttons! She knew she could do it all on her own next time.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a note on the floor. The note had a twist. It said, "I took the cookie." Tim felt guilty because he did not take the cookie. Tim showed the note to his friend, Sue. Sue looked at the note and said, "I did not take the cookie either. Let's find who did." They went to their friend, Max. Max had crumbs on his face. Tim asked, "Max, did you take the cookie?" Max looked guilty and said, "Yes, I took the cookie. I am sorry." Tim and Sue forgave Max. They all played together and had fun. They learned that it is better to tell the truth and not feel guilty.
