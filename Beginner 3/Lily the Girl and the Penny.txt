Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Penny

Once upon a time, there was a small girl named Lily. Lily had a penny that she loved very much. One day, she lost her penny and started to worry. Lily asked her friend, Tom, "Did you see my penny?" Tom said, "No, I did not see your penny." Lily was very sad and kept looking for her penny. Lily looked and looked, but she could not find her penny. She started to cry. The penny was gone forever. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue had an amazing mask. She loved her mask so much. The mask was her favorite thing to wear. She wore it all the time. One day, Sue went to the park to play. She played with her friends and had lots of fun. But then something sad happened. Sue lost her mask. She looked and looked, but she could not find it. Sue was very sad. Then, a nice dog came to the park. The dog had something in its mouth. It was Sue's amazing mask! The dog had found it for her. Sue was so happy. She thanked the dog and put on her mask. From that day on, Sue and the dog were the best of friends.

Ben and Sam are friends. They like to play with their toy trains. They have a big network of tracks and bridges. They make the trains go fast and slow, and stop and go. One day, Ben has an idea. He says to Sam, "Let's feed the trains. They must be hungry." Sam nods and smiles. He likes Ben's idea. They go to the kitchen and find some bread and cheese. They break them into small pieces and put them on the tracks. They run back to the living room and watch the trains. The trains pick up the bread and cheese with their magnets. They make happy noises. Ben and Sam clap and laugh. They think the trains like their food. But then, Mom comes in. She sees the mess on the tracks. She is not happy. She says to Ben and Sam, "What are you doing? You can't feed the trains. They are not regular animals. They don't need food. They need batteries. And you are wasting good food. That's not nice." Ben and Sam feel sorry. They did not know. They say to Mom, "We are sorry, Mom. We just wanted to make the trains happy. We won't do it again." Mom sighs and smiles. She says to Ben and Sam, "It's okay. I know you meant well. But next time, ask me before you feed anything. Now, help me clean up this mess. And then we can have some bread and cheese for ourselves." Ben and Sam nod and help Mom. They still love their trains, but they know they don't need food. They need love and care.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived an orange fox. The fox had a long, fluffy tail. He liked to play with his friends, the rabbit and the squirrel. One day, the fox wanted to surprise his friends. He hid behind a big tree and waited for them to come. The rabbit and the squirrel walked by, talking to each other. "I like to jump high," said the rabbit. "I like to climb trees," said the squirrel. The orange fox jumped out from behind the tree and shouted, "Surprise!" The rabbit and the squirrel laughed and clapped their hands. They all played together in the forest, having lots of fun. The fox was happy that he had made his friends smile with his big surprise.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Max. Max loved to dig in the yard. One day, Max found a big bone. He was very happy. Max wanted to show his friend, a cat named Lily. Max said, "Look, I found a big bone!" Lily said, "You are brave to dig so deep." Max said, "Thank you, I will behave and share the bone with you." But then, something unexpected happened. The bone started to wiggle and move! Max and Lily were surprised. The bone was not just a bone, it was a dinosaur toy! Max and Lily played with the dinosaur toy and had lots of fun.
