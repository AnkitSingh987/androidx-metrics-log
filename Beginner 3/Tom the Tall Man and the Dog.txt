Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Tall Man and the Dog

One day, a tall man named Tom went for a walk. He liked to listen to the birds and look at the flowers. The sun was shining, and Tom was happy. Suddenly, Tom heard a loud sound. It was an ambulance! The ambulance stopped near him. Tom was confused. He did not know why the ambulance was there. Then, the ambulance door opened, and out came a big, friendly dog. The dog jumped and licked Tom's face. It turned out the dog was the ambulance driver's pet. They were just stopping to say hello. Tom laughed and played with the dog. It was a fun and unexpected day for Tom.

Once upon a time, in a peaceful little town, there lived a kind man named Tom. Tom liked to take walks in the park and say "hi" to his friends. One sunny day, Tom saw his friend Sue. Sue was holding a bottle of wine. "Hi Tom!" said Sue. "Do you want to take this wine to the park and share it with our friends?" Tom smiled and said, "Yes, Sue! That sounds like fun!" So, Tom and Sue went to the park with the wine. They found their friends, and they all sat down on the soft, green grass. Tom opened the wine and poured it into cups. They all drank the wine and laughed. The sun was warm, the birds were singing, and the friends had a great time. The peaceful day in the park was perfect for sharing wine with friends.

Once upon a time, there was a huge bear. He lived in a big forest. The huge bear liked to seat on a big rock near the trees. One day, the huge bear saw a little girl. She was lost and sad. The little girl saw the huge bear and wanted a hug. She thought the bear was nice. The huge bear saw the girl and wanted to give her a hug too. They came close to each other. But the huge bear was too big and strong. When he tried to give the little girl a hug, he hurt her. The little girl cried and the huge bear was sad. They could not be friends because the huge bear was too big for the little girl.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly cat named Tom. Tom loved to play and run in the park. One day, he found a message in a bottle. The message said, "Please come to the big tree." Tom went to the big tree, curious about the message. He saw many of his friends there. They were all smiling and happy. They said, "Please, Tom, come play with us!" Tom felt very excited and joined them in their games. After playing for a while, Tom's friends gave him a big surprise. They had a party under the big tree! The message was an invitation to Tom's own surprise party. Everyone had a great time, and Tom felt very loved by his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big tent in the woods. In the tent, there lived a fierce lion and a little mouse. They were good friends and played together every day. One day, the lion was very sad. The little mouse asked, "Why are you sad, my friend?" The lion said, "I want a kiss from my mom, but she is far away." The mouse thought for a moment and said, "I have an idea! Let's go find your mom." So, the lion and the mouse went on a long journey to find the lion's mom. They walked and walked until they found her. When the lion saw his mom, he was so happy. His mom gave him a big kiss and the lion felt better. The lion and the mouse went back to their tent in the woods. They learned that sometimes, when you are sad, all you need is a little help from a friend. And that's the story of the fierce lion and the little mouse who lived in a tent.

Once upon a time there was a dove who didn't have a place to belong. He was worried because he had nowhere to go. So he started to look around for a place to fit in. He looked high and he looked low, but all he found were birds that didn't seem to want him. One day he saw a kind lady who was feeding birds in her garden. She smiled kindly when she saw the dove. He timidly flew closer. She smiled even more and gave him some food. The dove felt happy for the first time in a long time. He belonged! He was so relieved that he found a place to call home. He stayed with her, and she cared for him every day. And they all lived happily ever after. The end.
