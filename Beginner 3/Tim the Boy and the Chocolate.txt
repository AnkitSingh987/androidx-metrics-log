Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Chocolate

One day, a small boy named Tim found a chocolate bar in the kitchen. He wanted to eat it, but his mom said he could not. Tim was sad, but he had an idea. When his mom went to the store, Tim took the chocolate. He removed the wrapper and took a big bite. It was so yummy. He ate and ate until it was all gone. Then, he heard the door open. His mom came back and saw the empty wrapper. "Tim, did you eat the chocolate?" she asked. Tim said, "Yes, I did." His mom was not happy. She told Tim that he should not take things without asking. She said there would be no more chocolate for a long time. Tim was very sad, but he knew he did something wrong.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had an amazing toy. It was a marble. He loved to play with it all day. He would twist and turn it in his hands. It was his favorite toy. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He took his marble with him. He played with it as he walked. He twisted it in his hands and watched it shine in the sun. Tim was very happy. But then, something bad happened. Tim dropped his marble. It rolled away very fast. He tried to catch it, but he could not. The marble rolled into the street. A big truck came and crushed the marble. Tim was very sad. His amazing marble was gone forever.

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play with their toys. One day, they found a big toy in their dad's room. It was a microscope. It had a long tube, a round glass and a light. They wanted to see what it could do. They took the microscope to their playroom and put it on a table. They looked for something small to watch with it. They found a bug on the floor. It had six legs, two wings and a black body. They put the bug on a slide and put the slide under the glass. "Wow, look at the bug!" Tom said. He turned the knob and made the bug look bigger. He could see its eyes, its mouth and its hairs. He was very happy. "Let me see, let me see!" Lily said. She pushed Tom away and looked into the tube. She saw the bug too. She was very curious. But the bug was not happy. It was scared and angry. It wanted to get away from the light and the glass. It moved and wiggled and tried to escape. It made a loud buzz. "Ah, the bug is angry!" Lily said. She was worried. She did not want the bug to hurt them. She moved back and bumped into the table. The microscope shook and fell to the floor. It broke into pieces. "Oh no, the microscope is broken!" Tom said. He was worried too. He did not want their dad to be angry. He looked at the broken toy and the bug. He felt very sorry. They heard their dad coming up the stairs. They did not know what to do. They hid under the bed and hoped their dad would not see the mess. They wished they had not played with the microscope. They learned a lesson.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot lived in a small house with his friend, a girl named Lily. One day, they went outside to play. The wind was strong and noisy. Spot and Lily felt cold and started to shiver. They saw a big tree with lots of leaves. Spot said, "Let's play under the tree. The wind won't be so noisy there." Lily agreed, and they went to the tree. Under the tree, they did not feel the wind as much. They stopped shivering and started to play. They played all day under the tree. They ran, jumped, and laughed. The wind was still noisy, but they did not care. They were having fun together. When the sun went down, they went back to their small house. They were happy and warm.
