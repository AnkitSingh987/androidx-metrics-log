Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Ball

One day, a little boy named Tom found a dry paper on the ground. He picked it up and thought about what he could use it for. He saw his friend Sam playing with a ball and had an idea. Tom went to Sam and said, "Let's use this paper to make a hat for the sun. Then we can play ball and not feel hot." Sam liked the idea. They sat down and made a hat out of the dry paper. They put it on and played with the ball under the sun. As they played, the hat broke because it was too dry. Tom and Sam were sad. But then, they remembered that they could use their hands to make shade for their eyes. They learned that they could find other ways to solve a problem when one way does not work.

Once upon a time, there was a big, angry dog named Max. Max liked to bark a lot. One day, Max was playing in the park when he saw a small, round thing on the ground. As Max got close to the small, round thing, he heard a tiny sound. He put his ear close to the ground and listened. The tiny sound was a little bug crying. Max was surprised because he had never heard a bug cry before. Max decided to help the little bug. He gently picked it up with his mouth and carried it to a safe place. The little bug was so happy that it stopped crying and started to sing. Max was not angry anymore. He and the little bug became friends, and they played together every day.

One day, a unique fish named Fin met a big shark named Chomp. Fin liked to joke and make friends. Chomp was always sad because other fish were scared of him. Fin wanted to help Chomp feel better. Fin said to Chomp, "Why don't we play a joke on the other fish? They will laugh and not be scared of you." Chomp agreed and they planned a funny joke. They were going to pretend Chomp was stuck in a big net. When the other fish saw Chomp stuck, they came to help. Fin jumped out and said, "Joke! Chomp is not really stuck!" The fish laughed and Chomp smiled. But then, something unexpected happened. A real big net came and caught all the fish! Chomp used his strong teeth to cut the net and save everyone. The fish were happy and not scared of Chomp anymore. They all became friends and played together.

Once upon a time, there was a little flea named Fifi. Fifi lived on a big, heavy dog named Daisy. Daisy liked to run and play, but Fifi would fall off when Daisy ran too fast. One day, Daisy met another dog named Max. They became friends and played together. Fifi tried to hold on, but she fell off Daisy and landed on Max. "Oh no!" said Fifi. "I fell off Daisy. I need to find my way back!" Fifi jumped and hopped on Max, trying to get back to Daisy. Max felt Fifi on his back and said, "Hey, what are you doing?" Fifi told Max that she fell off Daisy and needed help to get back. Max helped Fifi by getting close to Daisy, so Fifi could jump back onto her friend. Fifi was so happy to be back with Daisy. She thanked Max for helping her. From that day on, Daisy, Max, and Fifi played together and became the best of friends. And Fifi learned how to hold on tight, so she wouldn't fall off again.

Lila and Tom were playing in the park. They saw a big hill with many stones. Lila wanted to climb the hill, but Tom was scared. "Come on, Tom, let's go up the hill. It will be fun!" Lila said. "I don't know, Lila. The hill is too high and the stones are too hard. What if we fall?" Tom said. Lila saw a friendly dog near the hill. The dog was wagging its tail and barking. Lila had an idea. "Look, Tom, the dog wants to play with us. Maybe he can help us climb the hill. He can show us the way and we can follow him. He is not afraid of the stones. He is brave and strong." Lila said. Tom looked at the dog and smiled. The dog seemed nice and happy. Tom liked dogs. "Okay, Lila, let's try. But we have to be careful and hold hands. And we have to say thank you to the dog." Tom said. Lila and Tom ran to the dog and petted him. The dog licked their faces and wagged his tail more. He was glad to have new friends. He led them to the hill and started to climb. Lila and Tom followed him, holding hands and encouraging each other. They stepped on the stones and reached the top. They felt proud and excited. They saw the whole park and the sky. They hugged the dog and thanked him. "Wow, Lila, this is amazing. We did it! We climbed the hill!" Tom said. "Yes, Tom, we did. And we had fun. And we made a new friend. The dog is the best. He helped us and encouraged us. He is a good dog." Lila said. The dog barked and wagged his tail. He was happy too. He liked Lila and Tom. They were good kids. They played together on the hill and had a wonderful time.
