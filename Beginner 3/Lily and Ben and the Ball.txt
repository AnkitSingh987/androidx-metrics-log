Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Ball

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They have a pink ball. They like to kick the ball and race to get it. One day, they see a big dog in the park. The dog is black and white. The dog is heavy and fast. The dog sees the pink ball and runs to it. He grabs the ball with his mouth and runs away. "Hey, that's our ball!" Lily shouts. She and Ben race after the dog. They want their ball back. The dog is too fast for them. He runs to the other side of the park. He drops the ball and starts to dig a hole. He wants to hide the ball in the hole. Lily and Ben catch up with the dog. They see the ball in the dirt. They try to get the ball, but the dog growls at them. He does not want to share the ball. "Please, give us our ball," Ben says. He is scared of the dog. He does not want to make the dog angry. The dog looks at Ben and Lily. He sees that they are sad. He feels sorry for them. He decides to be nice. He picks up the ball and brings it to them. He wags his tail and licks their faces. "Thank you, dog," Lily says. She and Ben smile. They are happy to have their ball back. They invite the dog to play with them. They kick the ball and race to get it. The dog joins them. He is happy to have new friends. They have fun in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird who loved to sing. The bird was blue and very happy. Every day, the bird would sing for all the other animals in the forest. The animals loved to hear the bird sing. One day, the blue bird wanted to be successful. It wanted to be the best singer in the whole forest. So, the bird practiced singing louder and louder. It sang so loud that all the animals could hear it from far away. But the bird sang too loud. The animals did not like the loud singing. They were sad and did not want to listen to the bird anymore. The blue bird was not successful. It was sad because it could not sing for the animals anymore. The blue bird learned that sometimes, trying too hard can make things worse.

Once upon a time there was an orange motion. It had four parts that always moved around and around. One day the parts got so excited they started to move up and down too! All the other colors, shapes and sizes in the sky watched in wonder as the orange motion made its way across the sky. The motion was so much fun that the other colors, shapes and sizes started to join the motion too. Soon the orange motion was so big and so beautiful that it lit up the sky all night long! Everyone was so happy and had so much fun! Finally the orange motion stopped and the colors, shapes and sizes all went back to their homes in the sky. They all knew it was time for a good night's sleep.

One day, a big dog and a little cat had a race. They wanted to see who was the fastest. The dog was very big and the cat was very little, but they both wanted to win. They started the race near a big tree. The dog ran very fast, and the cat ran very fast too. Soon, they saw a big wet pot near the road. The dog was so big that he did not see the wet pot. The dog ran into the wet pot and got stuck! The little cat saw the big dog in the wet pot and stopped running. The cat helped the big dog get out of the wet pot. The dog said, "Thank you, little cat!" They both smiled and walked back to the big tree together. They did not finish the race, but they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful dog named Spot. He loved to play and run in his big yard. One day, Spot found a big barrel. It was round and brown. Spot thought it would be fun to push the barrel around. Spot worked hard to push the barrel. He used his strong legs and nose. The barrel rolled and rolled. Spot was so happy to see the barrel move. He barked and wagged his tail. But then, the barrel rolled too fast. Spot could not stop it. The barrel rolled away and hit a tree. The tree fell down and made a loud sound. Spot was sad and scared. He wished he never found the barrel.
