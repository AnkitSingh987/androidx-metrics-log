Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Dog

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park. She had a big basket in her hands. Sue wanted to fill the basket with pretty flowers. She was very happy. As she picked flowers, a nosy dog came by. The dog wanted to play with Sue. It jumped and run around her. Sue laughed and played with the dog. They both had fun. Then, something unexpected happened. The nosy dog took Sue's basket and run away. Sue was sad. She followed the dog to a big tree. The dog put the basket down. Sue looked inside and saw lots of yummy treats! The dog wanted to share a surprise with Sue. They both ate the treats and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very intelligent. He loved to learn and use new things. One day, he found a cut on his finger. He did not know what to do. His mom saw the cut and said, "Oh no, Tim! You have a cut on your finger. Let's use a band-aid to make it better." Tim watched as his mom put the band-aid on his finger. He felt happy because the cut was not hurting anymore. The next day, Tim saw his friend, Sue, crying. Sue had a cut on her knee. Tim remembered what his mom did for him. He said, "Sue, don't cry. I can help you. Let's use a band-aid to make your cut better." Sue smiled, and Tim put a band-aid on her knee. They both felt happy and played together all day.

Once upon a time there was a poor little girl called Lucy. She lived with her family in a small house. One day, their neighbour Mrs Hughes had a surprise for Lucy and her family. Mrs Hughes had made a quiz! Lucy was very excited. She loved to do quizzes. She put on her hat and coat and ran outside to Mrs Hughes' garden. She found Mrs Hughes sitting by her table. On the table was the special quiz. Mrs Hughes smiled at Lucy and handed her the quiz. Lucy read the questions and was so surprised by how clever Mrs Hughes' quiz was! She sat and thought about the answers. After a few minutes Lucy had finished the quiz. Mrs Hughes was very happy to see how well Lucy had done. She gave Lucy a special prize and gave her a hug. Lucy ran back to her house feeling very proud. She couldn't believe she had done so well on Mrs Hughes' quiz, what a surprise!

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot was a very popular dog in his town. All the kids loved to play with him. Spot had a special trick that made him popular. He could count with his heel! One sunny day, Spot was playing with his friends. A little boy named Tim asked, "Spot, can you count to five with your heel?" Spot barked happily and started to count. He hit his heel on the ground one, two, three, four times. But when he tried to hit it for the fifth time, something unexpected happened. Instead of hitting the ground, Spot's heel hit a hidden button in the grass. The ground opened up, and a big slide appeared. All the kids were surprised and excited. They took turns sliding down the slide, laughing and having fun. Spot had found a secret slide, and now he was even more popular!

Tim and Sue were friends. They liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big hill with mist on top. They wanted to go up and see what was there. "Let's go, Tim!" Sue said. "Maybe we can find a castle or a dragon!" "OK, Sue!" Tim said. "But we have to be careful. The mist is wet and cold." They ran up the hill, holding hands. They felt the mist on their faces and hair. They laughed and shouted. They did not see the hole in the ground. Sue fell into the hole. She screamed. Tim tried to help her, but he could not reach her. He was scared. "Tim, help me!" Sue cried. "It's dark and sour here!" "I'm sorry, Sue!" Tim said. "I don't know what to do!" He looked around. He saw a big stick. He thought he could use it to pull Sue out. He ran to get it. But when he came back, he saw a big snake. The snake was in the hole with Sue. It was hissing and coiling. It was ready to bite Sue. "Tim, help me!" Sue cried again. "The snake is going to eat me!" Tim was very scared. He wanted to help Sue, but he was afraid of the snake. He dropped the stick and ran away. He ran down the hill, through the mist, to the park. He did not look back. He did not hear Sue's screams. He ran home and hid in his room. He cried and cried. He felt bad. He wished he had helped Sue. He wished he had been brave. But it was too late. Sue was gone. The snake had eaten her. Tim had lost his friend. He knew Sue would never forgive him. He knew he would never forgive himself. He knew he would never be happy again.
