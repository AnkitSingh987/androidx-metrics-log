Category: Beginner
Title: Timmy and the Dog

Timmy liked to play with his toy monkey. He named him Bobo and took him everywhere. He hugged Bobo when he was sad, he shared Bobo with his friends, and he slept with Bobo at night. One day, Timmy's mom said they had to go to the store. She told Timmy to stay in the car with Bobo while she got some things. Timmy did not like to stay in the car. He wanted to go with his mom. He felt anxious and scared. He looked at Bobo and said, "Don't worry, Bobo. Mom will be back soon. We can play here until she comes." He tried to make Bobo smile, but Bobo did not smile. He was just a toy. Timmy saw a big dog outside the car. The dog saw Bobo and barked. He wanted to play with Bobo too. He jumped on the car and scratched the window. Timmy was afraid. He held Bobo tight and cried. He wished his mom would come back. He wished the dog would go away. He wished Bobo could talk to him. He felt very alone. Then he heard his mom's voice. She came back with a bag of things. She saw the dog and shooed him away. She saw Timmy and Bobo and opened the door. She hugged Timmy and said, "I'm sorry, Timmy. I didn't know the dog was here. Are you okay?" Timmy nodded and showed her Bobo. He said, "Bobo was anxious too. He didn't like the dog. He wanted you to come back." Mom smiled and said, "I'm sorry, Bobo. I'm glad you stayed with Timmy. You are a good monkey. You are a good friend." Timmy smiled too. He felt better. He said, "Thank you, Mom. Thank you, Bobo. I love you both." They went home and played with Bobo. Timmy was happy. He was not anxious anymore. He had his mom and his monkey. He had everything he needed.

Billy was a brave little boy. He wanted to be successful in what he did. One day, his mom showed him a big blue cord. She said it was time to crawl across it. He was a bit scared but he wanted to be successful. So he took a deep breath and started to crawl. He was very jiggly and slow, but he kept going. He was focused on making it across. After a long time, he made it! He was successful. His mom was so proud of him. He felt so happy. He had done it. He had crossed the big blue cord!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a big test at school the next day. Amy's mom told her to mind her time and study for the test. But Amy did not want to study. She wanted to play with her toys. Amy's mom went to the store and left Amy alone. She told Amy, "Mind your time and study for the test." While her mom was away, Amy played with her toys instead of studying. She forgot to mind her mom's words. When the test day came, Amy felt sad. She did not study and did not know the answers. She learned that she should have minded her mom's words. From that day on, Amy was never spoiled again. She always minded her mom and did her best in school.

Once upon a time there were two friends: Joe and Sam. Joe was lying on the floor. He was tired and all he wanted to do was have a nap. Sam, who was always looking for fun, said, “Let’s play a game!” Joe reluctantly agreed and Sam said, “Let’s play circle!” So Joe and Sam rolled a big circle on the ground. Joe said, “Let’s see who can get to the other side first!” Sam smiled and said, “I bet I can do it fast!” So Joe and Sam lay down and started to roll fast across the floor. Joe rolled quickly and he was the first one to get to the other side. Sam laughed and congratulated Joe. They both had lots of fun rolling around in circles, playing and laughing together. In the end, Joe and Sam both agreed that it was a great game!
