Category: Beginner
Title: Bobo the Bird and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Bobo. Bobo lived in a small cage. He was restless because he wanted to fly and play with his friends outside. Every day, he would admire the big blue sky and dream of being free. One day, Bobo's owner left the cage door open. Bobo saw this and thought, "This is my chance to fly and play outside!" He flew out of the cage and into the big blue sky. He was so happy to finally be free. Bobo played with his friends all day long. But as the sun began to set, he started to miss his cage. It was warm and safe. So, Bobo flew back home to his cage. His owner saw him and was happy to have him back. From that day on, Bobo was no longer restless. He knew he could play outside, but he also loved his cozy cage.

Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They had a big tub of water and some toys. Lily had a yellow crown that she liked to wear. Ben had a blue boat that he liked to sail. "Look at my crown!" Lily said. "It is shiny and pretty. I am a queen!" "Look at my boat!" Ben said. "It is fast and strong. I am a captain!" They took turns to put their toys in the water and make them splash. They had fun and laughed. But then, Ben had an idea. He grabbed Lily's crown and threw it in the water. It sank to the bottom. "Hey!" Lily cried. "That is not nice! Give me back my crown!" Ben laughed. He reached for the crown, but it was too deep. He could not get it. "Ha ha!" Lily said. "Now you can't have it either!" Ben felt bad. He did not mean to make Lily sad. He tried to say sorry, but Lily was angry. She splashed water at him. He splashed back. They got wet and cold. The water was rough and dirty. It soaked their clothes and hair. It made their skin itch. They did not like it. They stopped splashing and looked at each other. They were both sorry. They hugged and said sorry. They got out of the water and dried themselves. They looked for the crown, but they could not find it. It was gone. They were sad, but they decided to share the boat. They played with it on the grass. They pretended to sail to a new land. They found a new crown made of flowers. They took turns to wear it. They were happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big, soft bed that she loved to sleep in at night. One day, Lily was playing in her room when she heard her mom call her for dinner. Lily was so excited that she jumped off her bed and ran to the door. But when she tried to open the door, it was closed. Lily felt embarrassed because she forgot to close the door before she started playing. She pushed the door open and ran to the kitchen. After dinner, Lily went back to her room and remembered to close the door. She climbed into her cozy bed and fell asleep. And from that day on, Lily always remembered to close the door before playing or going to bed.

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a walk with her mom. They noticed a big, old cactus in the park. Sue asked her mom, "What is that, Mom?" Her mom said, "That is a cactus, Sue. It is very old and has been here for many years." Sue saw a small bird trying to land on the cactus. But the cactus had sharp points, and the bird got hurt. Sue felt sad and wanted to help the bird. She asked her mom, "Can we help the bird, Mom?" Her mom nodded and said, "Yes, let's find a safe place for the bird to rest." They carefully picked up the bird and placed it in a soft, warm spot under a tree. The bird felt better and flew away, happy and safe. Sue and her mom felt good for helping the bird and continued their walk, hand in hand.
