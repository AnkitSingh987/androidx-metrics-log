Category: Beginner
Title: The Red Car and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a red car. The red car had a big friend, the blue car. They liked to play and race together. One day, they saw a small bug on the road. The bug was dead. The red car and the blue car felt sad. The red car wanted to help the bug. But the blue car wanted to destroy the bug. The red car said, "No, we should not destroy things. It is not nice." The blue car did not listen. The blue car went fast and tried to destroy the bug. The blue car went too fast and hit a big rock. The blue car was now broken too. The red car felt very sad. The blue car learned that it is not good to destroy things. The moral of the story is to be kind and not destroy things or you may get hurt.

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. He liked to dig in the dirt and find things. Tim saw a big tree with many roots. He wanted to see what he could do with the roots. He started to dig near a big root. The root was very long and went to a distant part of the yard. Tim followed the root and found a small hole. He thought it was a secret place. Tim put some of his toys in the hole near the root. He was happy to have a secret spot. He played with his toys there all day. When it was time to go home, Tim said bye to the root and his toys. He knew he would come back to play again soon.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a tall man with a jug. The jug is big and blue. It has water in it. "Hello, tall man. What is your jug for?" Lily asks. "Hello, little girl. My jug is for drinking. I jog in the park every day. Jogging makes me thirsty. Do you want some water?" the tall man says. "Yes, please. Thank you, tall man. Can we jog with you?" Ben says. "Sure, you can. But be careful. Jogging is fast and hard. You have to wear good shoes and breathe well. Are you ready?" the tall man says. Lily and Ben nod. They put on their shoes and hold hands. The tall man smiles and holds his jug. They start to jog in the park. They jog past the trees, the flowers, the swings and the slide. They jog and jog and jog. Lily and Ben have fun. They feel the wind on their faces and the sun on their backs. They laugh and talk with the tall man. They drink some water from his jug. They are happy. Jogging is fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big barrel in a small town. The barrel was full of chewy candy. The town kids loved the candy and would chew it all day. But one day, an angry dog came to the town. The dog was always mad and would growl at the kids. One day, a kind girl named Lucy talked to the angry dog. She said, "Why are you always angry, dog?" The dog said, "I am angry because I can't chew candy like the kids." Lucy felt sad for the dog and wanted to help. Lucy had an idea. She took some candy from the barrel and gave it to the dog. The dog chewed the candy and stopped being angry. The dog became friends with the kids and they all chewed candy together. The town was happy again. The moral of the story is that sometimes, a little kindness can change someone's life.

One day, a big crocodile and a small bird were friends. The bird was dependable and always helped the crocodile. They liked to play near the water and talk to each other. One day, the crocodile said, "Let's play a game. You stand on my back and we swim in the water." The bird was scared and said, "No, I refuse. I cannot swim and I am scared of the water." The crocodile felt sad but wanted to show the bird that the water was fun. So, the crocodile jumped into the water and started to play. The bird saw the crocodile having fun and slowly went near the water. The bird slipped and fell into the water. The crocodile quickly swam to the bird and saved it. The bird was happy and said, "Thank you, crocodile. You are a good friend." From that day, the bird was not scared of the water anymore.
