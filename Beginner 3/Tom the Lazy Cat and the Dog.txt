Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Lazy Cat and the Dog

One day, a lazy cat named Tom saw a big curtain. He wanted to play with it. So, he asked his friend, a little dog named Max, to help him. Max was not lazy, but he was very small. Tom said, "Max, please help me play with the curtain." Max said, "Okay, but we have to be careful." They started to play with the curtain, but Tom was not careful. He pulled the curtain too hard. The curtain fell down on top of them. Tom and Max were very scared. They tried to get out, but the curtain was too heavy. They were stuck under the curtain. The bad ending was that they had to stay under the curtain until someone found them.

One day, a big red bus went on a tour. It had high seats for kids to see all the fun things. A little girl named Amy was on the bus with her mom. Amy was very happy to be on the tour. As the bus moved, Amy saw a big tree with a cat on it. The cat meowed loud. Amy said, "Mom, look! A cat in the high tree!" The bus stopped so they could help the cat. Everyone on the bus tried to think of a way to get the cat down. Then, a little boy named Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's shut our eyes and count to three. Maybe the cat will come down by itself." So, everyone shut their eyes and counted, "One, two, three!" When they opened their eyes, the cat was gone! But guess what? The cat was now on the bus! It had jumped through an open window. Everyone laughed and clapped. The tour went on, and now they had a new friend, the cat.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a man with a big hose. The man was spraying water on the grass to help it grow. Tim liked the water and wanted to play with the hose. Tim asked the man, "Can I spray the water too?" The man said, "Sure, but be careful, it's very strong!" Tim took the hose and started to spray the water. He was having so much fun! Suddenly, Tim saw his mom's cup of coffee on the bench. He thought it would be funny to spray the coffee with water. But when he did, the coffee turned disgusting! His mom was shocked and said, "Oh no, my coffee!" Tim felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, mom." They both laughed, and Tim's mom bought a new cup of coffee.

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play with their toys. One day, they found a big box of napkins in the kitchen. They decided to use them to make different things. They tore some napkins and made snowflakes. They folded some napkins and made hats. They rolled some napkins and made snakes. They had a lot of fun, but they also made a big mess. The napkins were everywhere on the floor, the table, and the chairs. Their mom came in and saw the mess. She was not happy. She told them to clean up and put the napkins back in the box. Tom and Lily did not want to clean up. They wanted to keep playing. They said they were sorry, but they did not mean it. They tried to hide some napkins under the sofa and behind the curtains. They hoped their mom would not see them. But their mom was not fooled. She saw the napkins they hid. She was very angry. She told them to go to their room and take a nap. She said they could not play with their toys until they learned to change their behavior. Tom and Lily were sad and mad. They did not like to take a nap. They did not like to change their behavior. They thought their mom was mean. They went to their room and lay on their beds. They did not sleep. They just cried and complained. But then, they heard a knock on the door. It was their grandma. She was elderly and kind. She had a big smile and a big hug for them. She said she came to visit and to bring them a surprise. She had a bag of cookies and a book of stories. She said she would read to them and share the cookies with them. Tom and Lily were happy and glad. They loved their grandma. They loved cookies and stories. They forgot about the napkins and the mess. They forgot about their mom and the nap. They jumped off their beds and ran to their grandma. They said they were sorry and they meant it. They said they would change their behavior and they meant it. They hugged their grandma and thanked her for the surprise. Their mom heard them and smiled. She was not angry anymore. She was proud of them. She saw that they learned to change their behavior. She saw that they learned to say sorry and mean it. She saw that they learned to love and respect their grandma. She saw that they learned a lot from a box of napkins.
