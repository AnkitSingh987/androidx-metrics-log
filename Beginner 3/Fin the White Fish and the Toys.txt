Category: Beginner
Title: Fin the White Fish and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a white fish named Fin. One day, Fin wanted to visit his friend, an octopus named Ollie. So, Fin swam through the blue sea to find Ollie's home. When Fin found Ollie's home, he saw Ollie playing with his toys. Ollie was very happy to see Fin. They laughed and played together all day long. They played with a red ball and a yellow boat. As the sun went down, it was time for Fin to go home. Fin said goodbye to Ollie and swam back to his home. Fin was so happy that he visited his friend Ollie, the octopus. They had lots of fun and promised to play together again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a mechanic named Tom. He had a regular job fixing cars. One day, he was fixing a car when he heard a snap sound. He looked around and saw a big green frog. Tom was surprised because frogs don't come to his work. The frog looked at Tom and said, "Hi, I can help you fix cars!" Tom did not know that frogs can talk and help fix cars. He thought it was a dream. The frog showed Tom how to fix the car fast. Tom and the frog became best friends. They fixed many cars together. Everyone was happy, and Tom's job was more fun with his new friend, the frog.

Lily and Ben were best friends. They liked to play with their toys, read books, and sing songs. Sometimes they had sleepovers at Lily's house, where they slept in a big bed with Lily's mom and dad. One night, Lily and Ben were very sleepy after playing all day. They put on their pajamas and brushed their teeth. Then they climbed into the big bed and snuggled with Lily's mom and dad. "Good night, sweeties," Lily's mom said. "We love you very much." "Good night, mommy. Good night, daddy. I love you too," Lily said. "Good night, Lily. Good night, mom. Good night, dad. I love you too," Ben said. Lily's mom and dad kissed them on their cheeks and turned off the light. Lily and Ben closed their eyes and listened to the soft sound of their breathing. Lily felt very happy and warm. She liked having Ben as her friend. She wanted to show him how much she cared. She opened her eyes and looked at Ben. He was sleeping peacefully, with a smile on his face. She leaned over and kissed him gently on his neck. Ben woke up and felt something tickle his neck. He opened his eyes and saw Lily's face close to his. He felt surprised and shy. He didn't know what to do. He looked at Lily and saw her smile. "Hi, Ben," Lily whispered. "I kissed you because you are my best friend." Ben smiled back. He felt happy and warm too. He liked having Lily as his friend. He wanted to show her how much he cared. He leaned over and kissed her gently on her cheek. "Hi, Lily," Ben whispered. "I kissed you because you are my best friend too." Lily and Ben hugged each other and giggled. Then they closed their eyes and fell asleep, holding hands. Lily's mom and dad heard them and smiled. They were glad that their daughter had such a good friend. They kissed each other and fell asleep too, feeling proud and grateful.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and run around all day. One day, he found a big ball in the park. Spot was very happy and wanted to show his friend, Max, the cat. Spot ran to Max's home and said, "Max, look at my big ball! Let's play together!" Max looked at the ball and said, "Wow, Spot! That's a great ball. But I am busy now. I am going to the lab to see my friend, Doc, the frog." Spot felt sad, but then he had an idea. "Max, why don't we split the ball and take half each? Then we can both play with it later." Max liked the idea, and they split the ball into two pieces. After Max went to the lab, Spot played with his half of the ball in the park. He was very happy and couldn't wait to play with Max later. And when Max came back from the lab, they played together with their halves of the ball, and they had a lot of fun.
