Category: Beginner
Title: The Poor Cat and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a poor little cat. The cat was very sad. One day, the cat found an envelope on the ground. The cat was curious. Inside the envelope, there was a note. The note said, "Bow to the big tree and you will get a surprise." The cat was excited and went to the big tree. The cat bowed to the big tree. But, the surprise was not good. A big dog came out and chased the cat away. The cat was scared and ran far away. The cat did not find a happy surprise.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play in their big yard. They have a small house with a red roof. They love their house. One day, their mom and dad tell them something. They say they have to move to a new house. Anna and Ben are sad. They do not want to leave their house and their yard. "Why do we have to move?" Anna asks. "Because dad has a new job in a different town," mom says. "We will find a nice house there, too." "Will it have a red roof?" Ben asks. "Maybe not," dad says. "But it will have a roof to keep us warm and dry." Anna and Ben are not sure. They pack their toys and clothes in boxes. They say goodbye to their house and their yard. They get in the car with mom and dad. They drive for a long time. They see many different things on the road. They see mountains and rivers and farms and towns. They ask mom and dad many questions. They finally arrive at their new house. It is bigger than their old house. It has a blue roof. It has a small yard with a swing and a slide. Anna and Ben get out of the car. They look at their new house. They feel curious and nervous. "Welcome to our new home," mom says. "Let's go inside and see your rooms." Anna and Ben follow mom and dad. They see their boxes and their toys and their clothes. They see their beds and their books and their pictures. They see their new rooms. They smile at each other. They hug mom and dad. "Thank you for our new home," Anna says. "It is different, but it is nice," Ben says. Mom and dad smile, too. They are happy. They are a family. They can change houses, but they cannot change their love.

Once upon a time, a little bird lived in a big tree. The bird felt helpless because it could not find food. One day, the bird saw a twig on the ground. The twig reminded the bird of something. The bird remembered that its mom used twigs to make a nest. The bird thought, "If I use the twig, I can make a nest too." So, the bird picked up the twig and started to build a nest in the tree. Soon, other birds saw the little bird's nest and wanted to help. They all worked together to find more twigs and make more nests. The little bird was not helpless anymore. The moral of the story is that when we work together, we can do big things.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. As he tried to climb, he saw a little girl named Sue who was also trying to climb the tree. They both wanted to reach the top, but the tree was too big for them. Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's push the tree to make it smaller." Sue agreed, and they both started to push the tree. They pushed and pushed, but the tree did not move. They felt sorry for the tree and for themselves. Then, a wise old man walked by and saw them. He said, "You cannot push the tree to make it smaller. But if you work together, you can help each other climb the tree." Tim and Sue listened to the wise old man. They helped each other, and they were able to climb the tree together. They felt happy and proud of their teamwork.
