Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Stone

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was an honest boy who loved to gaze at the big blue ocean. He would sit on the warm sand and watch the waves come and go. One day, Tim found a small, shiny stone on the beach. He picked it up and thought it was very special. He put the stone in his pocket and took it home with him. He had a feeling that this stone would bring him a big surprise one day. A few days later, Tim went back to the ocean with his shiny stone. He sat on the sand and gazed at the water. Suddenly, a big fish jumped out of the ocean. The fish saw Tim's shiny stone and wanted it. Tim, being an honest boy, decided to give the fish his stone. In return, the fish gave Tim a beautiful shell. The shell made a soft sound when Tim held it to his ear, like the sound of the ocean. Tim knew that the shiny stone brought him a big surprise, just like he thought it would.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom had a toy sword that he loved very much. He played with his sword all day long. One day, Tom went to the park with his sword. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. When Tom climbed the tree, he saw a little bird in a nest. The little bird looked helpless. It could not fly. Tom wanted to help the bird, but he did not know how. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew Tom's sword away. Tom shouted, "Oh no, my sword!" But the wind was so strong, it also blew the little bird out of the tree. The bird started to fly. The wind had helped the bird learn to fly! Tom was happy he could help the bird, even though he lost his sword. In the end, Tom and the bird became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the snow. One day, it was very icy outside, but Tim wanted to play anyway. So, he put on his coat, his hat, and his boots. Tim's mom helped him tie his shoes, so they would not come off while he played. Tim went outside and saw a big pile of junk. He thought it would be fun to build a fort out of the junk. Tim found some big boxes and put them together. He used a rope to tie the boxes to a tree, so they would not fall down. As Tim played in his junk fort, he started to feel cold from the icy air. He decided it was time to go inside and warm up. Tim untied his shoes and took off his coat, hat, and boots. He sat by the warm fire and drank some hot cocoa. Tim was happy he played outside, but he was even happier to be warm and cozy inside.

One day, there was a little girl named Jill. She was three years old. Jill had a teddy bear, and she liked to play with it every day. One day, it was hot outside. Jill wanted to find a place to cool off. She found a nice shady tree and sat in the shade. It was nice and cool there. She was feeling a bit lonely, so Jill hugged her teddy bear. She was happy to have her bear to cuddle and comfort her. Then, her mother came. She had a big bowl of sweet strawberries. Jill was so happy! She ate a few and shared some with her bear. They were delicious and comforted her tummy. Jill thanked her mother for the sweet strawberries and the comforting shade. The End.

Once upon a time there was a large bow. It was so large it was like nothing anyone had ever seen. One day, a little girl called Jean saw the bow and asked her father what it was. "That bow is for playing," said her father. "What do we do?" Jean asked. "We shoot arrows with it," her father said. "Do you want to try it?" Jean smiled and said, "Yes, I do!" So they grabbed the arrows and went to the large bow. Jean drew the bow back and shot an arrow. "Good job!" said her father. Jean smiled and said "I want to do it again!" So they kept playing for hours, and Jean was happy to have learned something new!
