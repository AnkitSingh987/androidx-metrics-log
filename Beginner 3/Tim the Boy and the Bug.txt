Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Bug

One day, a boy named Tim went to the east with his mom. They went to a big shop to buy a toy. Tim was happy and excited. In the shop, he saw a harmless bug. The bug was green and small. Tim said to his mom, "Look, mom! A harmless bug!" His mom looked at the bug and said, "Yes, Tim. It is harmless. Let's buy your toy now." Tim picked a toy car and went to pay. As they were going to pay, the harmless bug jumped on the toy car. Tim didn't see it. They paid for the toy car and went home. At home, Tim played with his new car. The bug was still on it. Then, the harmless bug started to grow big. It was not a harmless bug after all. It was a big, bad bug. The big, bad bug ate Tim's toy car. Tim was sad and cried. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a brilliant sun. It shined very bright in the sky. Under the sun was a big desert. In the desert, there was a special place with water and trees. This place was called an oasis. One day, a thirsty bird saw the oasis. The bird wanted to drink the water. But the bird saw a sign. The sign said, "Do not drink the water." The bird did not accept what the sign said. The bird was very thirsty. The bird drank the water from the oasis. The sun saw the bird drink the water. The sun got very angry. The sun made the desert very hot. The bird was too hot and could not fly away. The bird was very sad. The end.

Once upon a time there was a boy. His name was Mark. Mark had a purple ball. He was very careful with his ball. Every day he played with it outside with his friends. One day, Mark was feeling tired. He knew it was time to relax. So he put on his purple blanket and laid down on the grass. He closed his eyes and took deep breaths. He felt so relaxed. Mark’s friends noticed he was taking a break. They asked if he wanted to play, but Mark said no. He wanted to stay and relax. He said he would be careful, but he just needed some quiet time for himself. The friends understood and left Mark alone. They knew how important it is to take time to relax. Mark stayed in the grass, breathing and resting until he felt ready to join them again.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the doctor with his mom. Tim was scared. He did not like the doctor. When they got to the doctor's office, Tim tried to peek behind the door. He saw a big chair and some toys. "Tim, come in and sit down," said the doctor. Tim looked guilty. He knew he should not be scared. The doctor was nice. But he still felt scared. Tim sat in the big chair and his mom sat next to him. The doctor looked at Tim's ear. Tim did not want the doctor to touch his ear. He moved away. "No, no!" he said. The doctor looked sad. "I just want to help you feel better," he said. Tim's mom held his hand. Tim took a deep breath and let the doctor look at his ear. It was not so bad after all.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big tree with a hidden door. Tim was very curious. He opened the door and found a big room full of candy. Tim was so happy, he wanted to eat all the candy. In the room, Tim met a little girl named Lily. She was eating candy too. They became friends and decided to share the candy. They nodded at each other and started to eat the candy together. Tim and Lily were having so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The candy began to talk! The candy said, "Please don't eat us! We are magical candy and we can grant you wishes!" Tim and Lily were surprised. They stopped eating the candy and wished for more fun toys and games. The magical candy made their wishes come true. Tim and Lily played with their new toys and became best friends.
