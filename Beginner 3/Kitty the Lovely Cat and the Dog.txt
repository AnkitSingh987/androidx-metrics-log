Category: Beginner
Title: Kitty the Lovely Cat and the Dog

One day, a lovely cat named Kitty got an invitation to a fun party. She was happy and wanted to go. The invitation had pretty colors and a big "Come to my party" written on it. Kitty put on her best dress and went to the party. At the party, Kitty met a nice dog named Doggy. They played with a big ball and ate yummy cake. Doggy said, "I like your dress, Kitty!" Kitty smiled and said, "Thank you, Doggy! I like your hat!" While they were playing, a big bug flew near Kitty. She did not like bugs. So, she used her paw to slap the bug away. Doggy and Kitty laughed and went back to playing. They had a great time at the party.

One day, a little girl was feeling very sad. A big, high tree was blocking out the sun and it made her feel even worse. Suddenly, her mom came and comforted her. She hugged the girl and whispered gentle words in her ear. The girl felt much better. Then Mom took out a lovely set of clothes. She said they were a special surprise for the girl. They were bright, colourful and so very high-quality. The girl was so excited. Mom helped the girl put her new clothes on and they both admired how beautiful she looked. The girl smiled and felt happy again. The mom and the girl hugged one more time and then went on their way.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a big box full of pins. Sue loved to play with her pins and make pretty things. She was very happy. One day, Sue wanted to make a rich looking dress for her doll. She managed to find some nice cloth and her pins. She put the pins in the cloth to hold it together. Sue was very careful because the pins were sharp. Sue showed her mom the rich dress she made with the pins. Her mom was very proud of her. They put the dress on the doll and it looked beautiful. Sue and her mom played with the doll all day. They had so much fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved to chew. She had a mild stick that she would chew all the time. "What does it taste like?" her friends asked her. "I can't explain it," the little girl replied. "It's like sparks in my mouth!" The friends looked around and saw a spark flying from the sky. The spark quickly made its way into the little girl's mouth and she started to chew it with a huge smile on her face. "It's delicious!" the little girl exclaimed. "It tastes so mild and sweet! You should try it!" The friends decided to try it, and they were all delighted with the flavor of the spark. From then on, they all enjoyed chewing the mild spark together.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a very unusual boy. He loved to pack his bag with many things. One day, Tim found a big, pink gum on the ground. He thought it was very special. He said to his friend, "Look at my gum! Isn't it big and pink?" His friend smiled and said, "Yes, it's very big and pink. But don't eat it, it's dirty!" Tim put the gum in his bag with his other things. Later, Tim and his friend went to the park. They played with their toys and had fun. Tim opened his bag to get a toy. But the gum was gone! He looked everywhere, but he couldn't find it. Suddenly, they saw a big, pink bird flying in the sky. The bird had the gum in its beak! The bird flew away with the gum, and Tim and his friend laughed. They knew that even unusual things can bring fun and surprise.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big, noisy truck drive by. Tim liked the truck and waved to the driver. The driver waved back with a big smile. Tim's mom said, "Good job, Tim!" In the park, Tim saw a small cat. It was stuck in a tree. Tim wanted to help the cat. He asked his mom, "Can I help the cat, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Tim took a deep breath and started to climb the tree. As Tim got closer to the cat, he saw it was not a cat. It was a big, soft toy! Tim was surprised. He grabbed the toy with his finger and climbed down. He showed the toy to his mom. She laughed and said, "What a funny twist! Now you have a new friend to play with." Tim smiled and waved to his new toy friend.
