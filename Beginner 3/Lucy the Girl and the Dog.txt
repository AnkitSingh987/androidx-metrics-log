Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Dog

One day, a little girl named Lucy went out to play in her yard. She saw a deep hole in the lawn. "Mom, look!" she said. "I see a hole!" Her mom came out and said, "Be careful, Lucy. Remember, we don't know what's in there." The next day, Lucy and her friend Tom were playing near the hole. They found a big stick and put it in the hole. They heard a sound. "What was that?" Tom asked. Lucy said, "I don't know. I will ask my mom." Lucy remembered what her mom said and told Tom to be careful. They went to tell Lucy's mom about the sound. Lucy's mom came out and looked at the hole. She saw a little lost dog. It was stuck in the deep hole. Lucy's mom helped the dog out of the hole. Lucy and Tom were happy. They played with the little dog all day long.

---

One day, a big band came to town. The band had many people who played music. They had drums, guitars, and other fun things to make sounds. Everyone was so happy to see the band. They all thought the band was very impressive. In the band, there was a funny dog who could speak. The dog would say "hello" and "goodbye" to the people. The kids loved the speaking dog. They would laugh and clap when the dog talked. At the end of the day, the band played one last song. The speaking dog said "goodbye" to everyone. The people all clapped and cheered for the impressive band. The kids were very happy and couldn't wait for the band to come back again.

Anna and Ben were twins. They liked to play together, but sometimes they fought. Anna was bossy. She always wanted to tell Ben what to do. Ben did not like that. One day, their mom said, "I have a surprise for you. We are going to make a cake for dad. It is his birthday tomorrow. You can help me mix the batter and decorate the cake. It will be a present for him." Anna and Ben were happy. They loved cake. They ran to the kitchen and put on their aprons. Mom gave them a big bowl and a wooden spoon. She poured some flour, sugar, eggs, and milk into the bowl. "Now, you can take turns to mix the batter. Be careful not to spill it," she said. Anna grabbed the spoon and started to mix. She said, "I will mix first. I am older by two minutes. You can mix later." Ben was angry. He wanted to mix too. He said, "No, that is not fair. You are always bossy. Let me mix now." He tried to take the spoon from Anna, but she held it tight. They pulled and pushed and shouted. Mom heard the noise and came back to the kitchen. She saw the twins fighting over the spoon. She saw the batter flying out of the bowl and landing on the floor, the table, the walls, and their faces. She was not happy. She said, "Stop it, you two. You have made a big mess. This is not how you make a cake. This is not how you show love to dad. You have to work together and share. Go to your room and think about what you have done. I will clean up and finish the cake by myself." Anna and Ben felt sorry. They knew they had been bad. They said, "We are sorry, mom. We are sorry, dad. We wanted to make a nice cake for you. We love you." They hugged mom and went to their room. They decided to be nice to each other and help mom more. They hoped dad would like the cake and the present they made for him. They drew a picture of their family and wrote, "Happy birthday, dad. We love you." They put it in an envelope and stuck a sticker on it. They waited for dad to come home and surprise him.

Lily and Ben are hungry. They want to eat pasta for lunch. Mom says she will make pasta for them, but they have to wait. Mom goes to the kitchen and puts water in a big pot. She turns on the fire and waits for the water to boil. Lily and Ben are bored. They look for something to play with. They see a shiny key on the table. They wonder what it is for. They pick up the key and run around the house. They try to put the key in different holes. They find a hole in the door of Mom's bedroom. They put the key in the hole and turn it. They hear a click. They have locked the door! Mom hears the click and goes to check. She sees that her bedroom door is locked. She asks Lily and Ben what they have done. They say they were playing with the key. Mom says they should not play with the key. The key is for the car, not for the door. Mom says they have to give her the key so she can unlock the door. Lily and Ben are sorry. They did not know they were doing something bad. They give Mom the key and say they are sorry. Mom unlocks the door and hugs them. She says it is okay, they made a mistake. She says they should be careful with things they do not know. She says she loves them and they are nice kids. Mom takes Lily and Ben to the kitchen. She says the pasta is ready. She puts some pasta on their plates and adds some cheese and sauce. She says they can eat now. Lily and Ben are happy. They say thank you to Mom and start to eat. They say the pasta is yummy. They are glad they have pasta for lunch.
