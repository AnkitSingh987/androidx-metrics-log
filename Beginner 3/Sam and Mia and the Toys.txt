Category: Beginner
Title: Sam and Mia and the Toys

Sam and Mia were playing with their toys in the living room. Sam had a big truck that was heavy and loud. Mia had a doll that was soft and pretty. She liked to comb her doll's hair with a purple brush. "Can I try your truck?" Mia asked Sam. "No, it's mine!" Sam said. He did not want to share his truck. "Please, just for a little bit," Mia said. She reached for the truck, but Sam pulled it away. "Ow!" Mia cried. She felt something sharp on her finger. She looked and saw a drop of blood. She had cut herself on the truck's metal edge. "Mia, are you okay?" Sam asked. He saw Mia's finger and felt sorry. He did not mean to hurt her. "I'm sorry, Sam. I just wanted to play with your truck," Mia said. She started to cry. "Here, you can have my truck," Sam said. He gave Mia his truck and took her doll. He wrapped his doll's hair around Mia's finger. "This will make it better. The doll's hair is soft and purple, like your brush." "Thank you, Sam. You are a good brother," Mia said. She smiled and hugged Sam. They played with each other's toys and forgot about the cut. They were happy.

One day, a fast cat named Speedy had an interesting message to give to his friend, a dog named Buddy. Speedy ran very fast to Buddy's house. He wanted to tell Buddy the message right away. When Speedy got to Buddy's house, he said, "Buddy, I have a message for you! We can play at the big park today." Buddy was very happy to hear this. He wagged his tail and said, "Thank you for the message, Speedy! Let's go to the park now." Speedy and Buddy went to the park and played all day. They ran, jumped, and had lots of fun. At the end of the day, they were very tired but happy. They knew they would remember this interesting day for a long time.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to find things in her big backyard. One day, she went outside to play and find new things. Lily saw a small, weak bird on the ground. "Why are you so weak, little bird?" she asked. The bird said, "I need to find a magic spell to make me strong again." Lily wanted to help the weak bird. She looked all around the backyard and found a shiny rock. The rock had a spell on it that said, "Say my name and you will be strong." Lily said the spell and the weak bird became strong again. The bird was so happy and thanked Lily for her help. From that day on, Lily and the bird were the best of friends.

Once there was a hungry pigeon. The pigeon lived in a big park. In the park, there were many trees and flowers. The pigeon liked to eat seeds. One day, the pigeon met a little girl. The girl had a bag of seeds. She said, "Hello, hungry pigeon! I want to give you some seeds. But first, we need to separate the big seeds from the little seeds." The pigeon and the girl worked together to separate the seeds. They put the big seeds in one pile and the little seeds in another pile. The pigeon was very happy. After they separated the seeds, the girl gave the little seeds to the pigeon. She said, "Here you go, hungry pigeon. Eat the little seeds and be happy." The pigeon ate the seeds and was not hungry anymore. The pigeon and the girl became good friends. They played in the park every day.

Once, there was a sweet little girl named Lily. She loved to play in the water. One day, Lily saw a big boat. It was called a kayak. She wanted to have one too. So, she decided to build a small kayak. She asked her dad to help her. Lily and her dad worked hard to build the kayak. They used wood and paint. They made it look pretty. They painted it blue, like the water. Lily was very excited. She could not wait to try her new kayak. The next day, Lily and her dad went to the lake. They put the kayak in the water. Lily got in and started to paddle. She was so happy. She had built her own kayak and now she could play in the water. The sun shone brightly and the water was cool. It was a perfect day for Lily and her kayak.
