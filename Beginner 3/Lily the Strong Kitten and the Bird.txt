Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Strong Kitten and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a strong kitten named Lily. She lived in a little house with her mom. One day, it started to rain outside. Lily looked out the window and saw the rain falling down. Lily's mom said, "Stay inside, Lily. It is wet and cold outside." But Lily wanted to play outside. She put on her rain boots and went out to play in the rain. She jumped in the puddles and had lots of fun. While Lily was playing, she met a little bird. The bird was wet and cold. Lily said, "Come with me, little bird. We can go inside my house and get warm." The bird was happy and followed Lily. They went inside, and Lily's mom dried them off. They all had a warm drink and played together. The strong kitten and the little bird became best friends.

Ben and Mia are twins. They like to play in their room. But they do not like to tidy up their toys. They leave them on the floor, on the bed, on the table. Mom and Dad are not happy. One day, Mom and Dad go out. They leave Ben and Mia with Grandma. Grandma says, "You can play, but you have to tidy up your toys when you are done. Or you will have no dessert." Ben and Mia nod, but they do not listen. They play with their cars, their dolls, their blocks. They have fun. They make a lot of noise. They do not tidy up their toys. They forget what Grandma said. Grandma is in the kitchen. She makes a cake. She wants to call Mom and Dad. She goes to the living room. She looks for her phone. She does not see it. She walks to the twins' room. She opens the door. She sees a mess. Toys are everywhere. She sees her phone on the floor. She wants to pick it up. But she does not see a car. She steps on the car. She slips. She falls. She hurts her back. She cries. Ben and Mia hear Grandma cry. They run to her. They see her on the floor. They see her phone. They see the car. They feel bad. They say, "We are sorry, Grandma. We did not tidy up our toys. We were naughty. Are you okay?" Grandma says, "I am not okay. I hurt my back. I need help. I need to call Mom and Dad. Can you give me my phone?" Ben and Mia give her the phone. She calls Mom and Dad. She tells them what happened. She says, "Please come home. I need to go to the doctor." Mom and Dad come home. They see Grandma on the floor. They see the mess. They are angry. They say, "Ben and Mia, what did you do? You did not tidy up your toys. You made Grandma slip. You made her hurt. You were very bad. You have to say sorry to Grandma. And you have to tidy up your toys. And you have no dessert." Ben and Mia say sorry to Grandma. They hug her. They say, "We love you, Grandma. We will not do it again. We will tidy up our toys. We will be good." Grandma says, "I love you, too. But you have to learn. You have to be careful. You have to be tidy. Or you will have trouble." Ben and Mia learn their lesson. They tidy up their toys. They help Grandma. They are good. They are happy.

Billy was at home packing a suitcase. He was going on a long trip and he wanted to be sure to have everything he needed. He had packed lots of clothes and put them neatly in the suitcase. He put in some food and some toys, too. Suddenly, Billy felt something strange in his left eye. He put his hand to it and felt a sharp pain. He cried out and shouted for his mother. She came running in and saw the problem right away. Billy had something stuck in his eye. His mother got some tweezers and carefully tried to take it out. She was calm and gentle, but no matter how hard she tried, nothing worked. In the end they had to go to the hospital. The doctors were able to get the object out, but not before it had caused some serious damage. Billy's eye was red and swollen. He was going to have to wear an eye patch for a few weeks until it healed. Billy was sad. His trip would have to wait until his eye was better. He had been excited about his journey and now he had to remain at home instead.
