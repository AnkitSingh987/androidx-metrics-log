Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and the Bird

Anna liked to fold paper. She could make hats, boats, and birds with her paper. She liked to show her paper things to her mom and dad. They always smiled and said, "Good job, Anna!" One morning, Anna was folding a paper bird at the table. She wanted to surprise her mom with it. She heard her mom in the kitchen, making breakfast. Anna smelled something yummy. She hoped it was cereal. She loved cereal with milk and bananas. She finished her paper bird and put it on her plate. She got up and went to the kitchen. She saw her mom holding a big pan. It had eggs and bacon and toast in it. Anna did not like eggs and bacon and toast. She wanted cereal. "Mom, can I have cereal?" Anna asked. "Sorry, sweetie, we don't have any cereal today. We ran out yesterday. But look, I made you a nice breakfast. You should try it. It's good for you." Mom said. Anna felt scared. She did not want to try the breakfast. She did not like how it looked and smelled. She wanted her paper bird and her cereal. She ran back to the table and grabbed her paper bird. She held it close to her chest. "Anna, come on, don't be scared. It's just food. You might like it if you taste it. Please, come and eat with me." Mom said. Anna shook her head. She did not want to eat. She wanted to fold more paper. She wanted to make a paper bowl and fill it with paper cereal. She thought that would be better than the breakfast mom made. She started to fold another paper. She did not hear mom sigh and put the pan down. She did not see mom sit next to her and hug her. She did not feel mom kiss her hair and say, "I love you, Anna. You are my paper bird."

Sam likes to collect things. He has a big box of things he finds. He has rocks, shells, buttons, feathers and more. He likes to look at his things and sort them by color or shape. One day, Sam sees a shiny thing on the floor. It is a coin. He picks it up and puts it in his pocket. He wants to add it to his box of things. He runs upstairs to his room. He opens his box and takes out his things. He makes a space for his coin. But then he hears a knock on the door. It is his grandpa. He is bald and has a big smile. He comes to visit Sam and play with him. Sam loves his grandpa. He puts his things back in his box and closes it. He runs downstairs to hug his grandpa. "Hello, Sam," his grandpa says. "What have you been doing?" "I was collecting things," Sam says. "I have a big box of things upstairs. Do you want to see?" "Sure, I would love to see your things," his grandpa says. "But first, can I show you something?" "What is it?" Sam asks. His grandpa takes out a coin from his pocket. It is shiny and has a picture of a bald man on it. He gives it to Sam. "This is a special coin," his grandpa says. "It is very old and rare. It has the picture of a bald king on it. He was a great leader and a brave warrior. I want you to have it. It is a gift from me to you." Sam looks at the coin. He is amazed. He has never seen a coin like this. He feels happy and proud. He hugs his grandpa and says, "Thank you, grandpa. I love you. And I love your coin. It is the best thing I ever collected."

Once upon a time, in a quiet little town, there lived a girl named Lily. She loved to play with her friends and guess what animals they were pretending to be. One day, while playing in the park, Lily met a new friend named Tim. "Guess what I am!" Tim said. He made a big roar and stomped around. Lily thought for a moment and then said, "You are a big, strong lion!" Tim smiled and nodded. They played together all day, pretending to be different animals. As the sun began to set, Lily's mom called her to go home. Lily said goodbye to Tim and told him, "I had the best day of my life playing with you!" Tim agreed and they promised to meet again the next day to play and guess more animals. And so, Lily and Tim became the best of friends, making their quiet little town a happier place to live.
