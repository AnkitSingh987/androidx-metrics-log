Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Sam and the Ball

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a big ball. They want to play with the ball. "Can I strike the ball?" Tom asks Sam. "Yes, you can. But be careful. The ball is very fast," Sam says. Tom runs to the ball. He strikes the ball with his foot. The ball flies in the air. It lands on a spot on the grass. The spot is cold and wet. "Oops! The ball is in the mud!" Tom says. Sam runs to the ball. He picks up the ball. The ball is dirty and cold. He tries to wipe the ball with his shirt. His shirt gets dirty and cold too. "Uh-oh! We need to clean the ball and the shirt," Sam says. Tom and Sam look for a tap. They find a tap near the slide. They wash the ball and the shirt with the water. The water is cold and makes them shiver. "Brrr! The water is cold!" Tom says. "Let's go home and get warm," Sam says. Tom and Sam take the ball and the shirt. They say goodbye to the park. They walk home with their teeth chattering. They are cold but happy. They had fun playing with the ball.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He had a big, round football. Tim loved to play with his football every day. One day, he saw his friend, Sam, looking sad. Tim asked, "Why are you sad, Sam?" Sam looked serious and said, "I lost my toy car. My mom wants me to find it." Tim had an idea. "Let's play with my football. Maybe we will find your car while we play." So, Tim and Sam started to play. They kicked the football and ran around. They were having lots of fun. Then, they saw something shiny under a bush. It was Sam's toy car! They were so happy. Tim said, "Now, we can deliver the toy car back to your mom." Sam smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim. You are a good friend." They walked back to Sam's house, and his mom was happy too. From that day on, Tim and Sam played football together every day.

Once upon a time, in a beautiful garden, there lived a big rock. Near the rock, there was a tall apple tree. The tree had many red apples hanging from its branches. One day, a little girl named Lily came to the garden. She saw the apple tree and said, "Wow! These apples look so yummy!" She wanted to eat one, but they were too high for her to reach. So, she asked the rock, "Can you help me get an apple, please?" The rock wanted to help Lily, so it began to rock back and forth, trying to push the tree. The tree shook, and an apple fell down. Lily picked up the apple and took a big bite. "Thank you, rock!" she said with a smile. They became good friends and played together in the beautiful garden every day.

Once upon a time there was an old witch who wanted to make a special potion. She found all the ingredients she needed except for one herb. No matter how hard she looked, she just couldn't find the herb. She got frustrated. At last, she thought of a plan. She decided to ask her little neighbor, who was three year old. She knocked on the door and the little girl opened it. With a friendly smile, the witch said: "Hello young one. I am looking for an herb. Do you know where I can find it?" The little girl thought for a moment, then she pointed to her garden and said: "I'm pretty sure it's in there. I can show you if you want?" The witch smiled, thanked her and they went together to the garden. Sure enough, the herb remained there, in the little garden! The witch thanked the little girl again and said: "Thank you so much! I'm so happy I have everything that I need now." And from then on, the old witch and her little neighbor remained good friends.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to look at the television. He was very happy because his mom said he could watch his favorite show. Tim was a fit boy who liked to run and play outside, but he also liked to watch his show on the television. While Tim was watching, something unexpected happened. The television stopped working! Tim was sad and didn't know what to do. He went to tell his mom about the television. Mom knew what to do. She told Tim to look behind the television. Tim found a big red button. He pushed the button and the television started working again! Tim was so happy and watched his show. He learned that sometimes the answer to a problem is right behind it.
