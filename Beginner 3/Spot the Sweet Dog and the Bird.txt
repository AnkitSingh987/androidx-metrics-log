Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Sweet Dog and the Bird

One day, a sweet dog named Spot went to play at the park. Spot loved to run and jump in the sun. He had sunglasses to keep his eyes safe. He wore them every day. At the park, Spot saw a big tree. He wanted to see what was at the top. Spot climbed up the tree. But as he climbed, his sunglasses fell off! Spot was sad. He did not want to lose his sunglasses. Just then, a bird flew by and saw the sunglasses. The bird picked them up and flew to Spot. The bird reminded Spot to be careful with his sunglasses. Spot was happy and thanked the bird. They became friends and played together in the park. And Spot always remembered to be careful with his sunglasses.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely little duck named Ducky. Ducky loved to splash in the water all day long. One day, Ducky saw a big mountain far away. He thought it would be fun to go there and see what it was like. Ducky asked his friend, Bunny, "Do you want to go to the mountain with me?" Bunny said, "Yes! Let's go!" So, Ducky and Bunny hopped and waddled all the way to the mountain. It was a long walk, but they were excited to see what was on the other side. When Ducky and Bunny reached the top of the mountain, they saw a big, beautiful lake. They were so happy! Ducky said, "This is the best place to splash!" Ducky and Bunny played in the water, splashing each other and having lots of fun. They had a great day at the lovely mountain lake.

One day a little girl was playing with her toys in her backyard. She was having a lot of fun! Suddenly, she noticed a top spinning on the ground and she wanted to play with it too. She ran to get it but her big brother was already reaching for it. The little girl said, "That's my top. Please give it to me!" Her brother replied, "No way! I'm going to store it in my room and you won't be able to get it." The little girl was upset and cried out, "Nooooo! Please don't take my top!" But her brother was too strong and he grabbed the top and ran off. The little girl was determined to get her top back. She ran inside and looked in her brother's room. She found the top in the closet, but it was tucked away in the corner. She tried to reach it, but it was too high. She pushed and pulled, but the door was too tight. Just when the little girl was about to give up, her mom walked in and saw what was happening. She opened the closet and reached in to get the top. The little girl was so relieved and gave her mom a big hug. She was so happy to have her top back.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. It was a cold day, but Tim was excited to play in the surf. He put on his warm coat and his boots. As they walked, Tim saw a big, pretty shell. He picked it up and showed it to his mom. "Look, Mom! I found a shell!" Tim said with a big smile. As Tim played near the surf, he saw a big wave coming. He was scared, so he ran as fast as he could. But Tim did not see the rock in the sand. He fell down and his coat got a tear. "Oh no, my coat!" Tim cried. Just then, a friendly crab came out from under the rock. The crab had a special gift. It could fix things! The crab saw the tear in Tim's coat and wanted to help. The crab waved its claw, and the tear was gone! "Thank you, crab!" Tim said, happy again. From that day on, Tim and the crab were the best of friends. They played in the cold surf together and had lots of fun. And Tim always remembered to watch out for rocks in the sand.
