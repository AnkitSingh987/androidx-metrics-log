Category: Beginner
Title: Spot and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. One day, Spot decided to wander away from his home to find a new friend. He walked and walked, hoping to meet someone nice. As Spot walked, he saw a big tree. He thought, "Maybe someone is hiding behind the tree." Spot took a look behind the tree, and there he found a little cat named Fluffy. Fluffy was shy, but Spot was very friendly. They started to play together and became good friends. Spot and Fluffy played all day. They ran, jumped, and laughed together. They were very happy and felt successful in their search for a new friend. When the sun started to set, they walked back to Spot's home, hand in hand, knowing they would always be there for each other.

Lily and Lola are twins. They look the same and like the same things. They have a big toy box with many toys. They like to play with their toys every day. One day, Lily and Lola want to play with the same toy. It is a pink doll with a blue dress. They both reach for the doll and pull it. They start to fight. "Give me the doll! It is mine!" Lily says. "No, it is mine! I saw it first!" Lola says. They pull harder and harder. The doll makes a loud rip sound. The doll's dress tears and the stuffing comes out. Lily and Lola stop and look at the doll. They feel sad. They hear their mom coming. She smells something smelly. She sees the doll on the floor and the twins with tears in their eyes. "What happened here?" Mom asks. Lily and Lola look at each other. They do not want to tell mom that they fought. They do not want to get in trouble. They remain quiet. They hug each other and say sorry. They pick up the doll and the stuffing. They ask mom to help them fix the doll. Mom smiles and hugs them. She says, "I am glad you are sorry and you love each other. Fighting is not nice. Sharing is nice. Let's fix the doll together and then we can play with other toys." Lily and Lola nod and smile. They are happy again. They love their mom and their twin. They do not like to fight. They like to share.

Once upon a time, there was a brave boy named Tom. One day, Tom went to the park with his mom. In the park, Tom saw a little infant. The infant was crying. Tom went to the infant and said, "Why are you sad?" The infant said, "I lost my toy." Tom wanted to help the infant. He looked all around the park. Then, he found the toy under a big tree. Tom picked up the toy and held it in his hands. He went back to the infant and said, "Here is your toy." The infant stopped crying and said, "Thank you, Tom." But then, something unexpected happened. The infant turned into a big, friendly bear! The bear said, "I am a magic bear. You were so kind and brave to help me. Now, we can play together in the park." Tom and the bear played all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a clean train. The train loved to help people. One day, the train met a little boy named Tim. Tim was sad because his toy car was stuck in a tree. The train wanted to help Tim. "Choo-choo! I can help you, Tim!" said the train. "Climb on my back and I will take you to the tree." Tim climbed onto the train and they went to the tree. The train helped Tim reach the toy car. Just then, a big wind came. The wind wanted to blow the toy car away. The train wanted to prevent the wind from taking the car. "No, wind! You cannot take Tim's toy car!" said the train. The wind stopped and the toy car was safe. Tim was happy and thanked the train. They became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red ball. It was perfect and shiny. One day, the ball saw a pretty jewel. The jewel was stuck in ice. The ball wanted to help the jewel. The ball asked a friend, a small, warm rock. "Can you help me get the jewel?" The rock said, "Yes, I can help. But we must be careful." The ball and the rock tried to free the jewel. They pushed and pushed, but the ice started to freeze more. The rock warned, "We must stop now, or the jewel will be stuck forever." But the ball did not listen. It pushed harder and harder. The ice froze more and more. The jewel was stuck forever. The ball was sad, and the rock was sad too. They learned that sometimes, you must listen to your friends.
