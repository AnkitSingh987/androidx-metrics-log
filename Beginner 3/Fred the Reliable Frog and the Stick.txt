Category: Beginner
Title: Fred the Reliable Frog and the Stick

Once upon a time, there was a reliable frog named Fred. Fred lived in a pond with his friends. They all liked to play and swim together. The pond was their home, and it was a fun place to be. One day, Fred found a sharp stick near the pond. He showed it to his friends, and they wondered what it was for. They decided to play a game with the stick. They took turns jumping over the stick without touching it. As they played, a big bird came to the pond. The bird wanted to eat the frogs, but Fred had an idea. He used the sharp stick to scare the bird away. The bird flew away, and the frogs were safe. Fred and his friends were happy that the sharp stick helped them. They learned that sometimes, unexpected things can be helpful. They put the stick near the pond, just in case the bird came back. And from that day on, they knew they could rely on each other and the sharp stick to keep them safe.

Lily was playing with her dolls in her room. She liked to dress them up and comb their hair. She had a small box with buttons, ribbons and needles. She used them to make clothes and accessories for her dolls. One day, she saw a shiny needle in the box. She wanted to use it to make a necklace for her doll. She reached out her hand and tried to grab the needle. But the needle was sharp and it poked her finger. Lily felt a sting and a drop of red came out of her finger. She cried out loud. "Mama, mama, it hurts!" she shouted. Mama heard her cry and ran to her room. She saw Lily holding her finger and the needle on the floor. She quickly picked up Lily and hugged her. "Oh, Lily, what happened?" she asked. "I wanted to grab the needle, but it hurt me," Lily sobbed. Mama took a tissue and wrapped it around Lily's finger. She kissed her cheek and said, "I'm sorry, Lily. Needles are not for playing. They are for sewing. They can hurt you if you grab them. You have to be careful with them." Lily nodded and sniffled. She felt better with Mama's hug and kiss. She said, "I'm sorry, Mama. I won't grab needles again. Can I have a band-aid?" Mama smiled and said, "Of course, my sweetie. Let me get you a band-aid. And then we can play with your dolls together. How about that?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, please. I love you, Mama." "I love you too, Lily," Mama said.

One day, a boy named Tim and a girl named Sue went to play. It was a cold day, but they were healthy and happy. They wanted to have a race to see who could run the fastest. "Ready, set, go!" said Tim, and they started to run. They ran and ran, trying to win the race. Sue was in front, but then something unexpected happened. A big dog jumped out and started to run with them. The dog wanted to play too! Tim and Sue laughed and let the dog join the race. They all ran together, having fun in the cold. "Wow, the dog is fast!" said Sue. "But we are all having fun, so we all win!" In the end, Tim, Sue, and the big dog were all winners. They played together until it was time to go home, happy and healthy.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly lady named Sue. Sue loved to help people. One day, she saw a man named Tom. Tom was sad because he lost his wine. The wine was very special to him. Sue wanted to help Tom. She said, "Tom, let's find your wine!" They looked high and low. They looked in the park, near the trees, and by the pond. Finally, they found the wine behind a big rock. Tom was so happy! He said, "Sue, thank you for helping me find my wine!" Sue smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tom! I'm glad I could help." Tom and Sue became good friends and they always helped each other when they needed it.
