Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Crab and the Sam

One day, a little crab named Tim found a big shell. He thought his old shell was very boring. So, he wanted to hide in the new shell. He went inside and felt very happy. But, something strange happened. The new shell started to move! Tim was very scared. He did not know that the shell was not a shell. It was a big snail named Sam! Sam was not mad. He said, "Hi, Tim! I am not a shell. I am a snail. Do you want to be friends?" Tim was happy. He said yes. Now, Tim and Sam play together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a farmer. He had a big purple hat. He liked to work in the sun. One day, the farmer fell down. His leg hurt. He could not work. He was sad. A kind bird saw the farmer. The bird wanted to help. The bird sang a song. The song made the farmer's leg heal. The farmer was happy. He said, "Thank you, bird!" The farmer and the bird were friends.

One day, a dog named Spot and a cat named Fluffy were playing in the yard. Spot had a red collar and Fluffy had a blue collar. They liked to run and jump together. Today, they found a big, round ball. Spot and Fluffy both wanted to play with the ball. Spot said, "I want the ball!" Fluffy said, "No, I want the ball!" They both felt frustrated because they both wanted to play with the ball. Then, Spot had an idea. He said, "Let's share the ball!" Fluffy thought it was a good idea. So, Spot and Fluffy took turns playing with the ball. They rolled it, kicked it, and even jumped on it. They had so much fun sharing the ball together. At the end of the day, they were both happy and tired. Spot and Fluffy learned that sharing can be fun.

Tom and Lily are playing in the garden. They like to dig and water the plants. They see a big red strawberry on a green bush. They want to eat it. "Can we have the strawberry, Mom?" Tom asks. "Yes, you can share it. But wash it first, please," Mom says. Tom and Lily run to the tap. They wash the strawberry and break it in half. They each take a bite. It is sweet and juicy. They hear a loud noise. It sounds like a growl. They look around. They see an unknown animal. It is big and furry. It has sharp teeth and claws. It is coming towards them. "Mom, help!" Lily screams. Mom hears them. She runs to the garden. She sees the animal. It is a bear. It is hungry and angry. It wants the strawberry. Mom grabs a stick. She waves it at the bear. She shouts at it. "Go away, bear! Leave my children alone!" The bear is scared of Mom. It runs away. It goes back to the woods. Mom hugs Tom and Lily. They are safe and happy. They finish the strawberry. They thank Mom for saving them. "Mom, you are brave and strong," Tom says. "Mom, you are kind and smart," Lily says. Mom smiles. She loves Tom and Lily. They are her sweet strawberries.

Once upon a time, there was a red car named Zoomy. Zoomy was a cheap car, but he was very happy. He loved to drive around with his friend, a little boy named Tim. One sunny day, Tim and Zoomy went to the park. They saw a big tree with a lot of shade. Tim said, "Zoomy, let's relax under the tree." Zoomy agreed, and they parked under the tree. While they relaxed, Tim and Zoomy saw a blue car. The blue car was sad because he had no friends. Tim said, "Hi, blue car! Come relax with us!" The blue car was happy and joined them. They all became good friends and had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, in a gentle town, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim had a lemon tree that he loved very much. He took care of the tree every day. He was very proud to own his lemon tree. One sunny day, Tim went to pick a lemon from his tree. He saw the biggest, most yellow lemon he had ever seen. Tim was so happy! He wanted to show the big lemon to his mom. But when he tried to pick it, something unexpected happened. The big lemon began to wiggle and giggle! Tim was shocked. It turned out that the lemon was not a lemon at all. It was a friendly bug dressed like a lemon! The bug said, "Hi, I'm Lemon Bug. I just wanted to play with you." Tim laughed and they became good friends. From that day on, Tim and Lemon Bug played together under the lemon tree, and they lived happily ever after.
