Category: Beginner
Title: Buddy the Gifted Dog and the Slide

One day, a gifted dog named Buddy went to the park. He liked to slide down the big slide. Buddy saw his friend, a cat named Mittens, sitting near the slide. "Hi Mittens!" said Buddy. "Do you want to slide with me?" Mittens looked at the slide and said, "Yes, let's slide together!" But, when they tried to slide, it was not fun. The slide was too slow. Buddy had an idea. He found some oil and put it on the slide. The oil made the slide very fast. Buddy and Mittens slid down the slide again. This time, it was so much fun! They laughed and played on the slide all day. They were happy to have each other as friends.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tower in a big field. The tower was black and very old. Many kids did not care about the tower, but a little girl named Amy did. Amy loved to look at the tower and play near it every day. One sunny day, Amy saw a small bird near the tower. The bird looked sad and could not fly. Amy said, "Don't worry, little bird. I will care for you." She took the bird home and fed it. The bird started to feel better and became Amy's friend. After some days, the bird could fly again. The bird said, "Thank you, Amy, for caring for me." The bird flew up to the top of the black tower and found a shiny gold coin. The bird gave the coin to Amy as a thank you gift. Amy learned that when you care for others, good things can happen.

One day, a little bug was sad. He was only an inch long and he wanted to zoom fast like the big bugs. His friend, the ant, saw him and asked, "Why are you sad, little bug?" "I want to zoom like the big bugs, but I am too small," said the little bug. The ant thought for a moment and said, "Let's race to the big tree. Maybe you can zoom faster than me!" As they started to race, a big gust of wind came. It picked up the little bug and made him zoom very fast. He flew past the ant and reached the big tree first. The little bug was so happy he could zoom fast! The ant smiled and said, "See, little bug? You can zoom fast too!" The little bug was not sad anymore. He learned that even small bugs can do big things. From that day on, the little bug and the ant played and raced together, always having fun.

One day, a fat bunny named Bob hopped in the garden. He saw a big carrot. Bob wanted to eat it. He said, "Yum! I will eat this carrot and be full!" Bob tried to pull the carrot out of the ground. It was hard to do. He pulled and pulled, but the carrot did not move. Bob was sad. He said, "I need help to get this carrot out." A bird named Sue saw Bob. Sue was Bob's friend. Sue said, "I will help you, Bob!" Sue and Bob pulled and pulled. The carrot came out! But, it was not a carrot. It was a big, orange key! Bob and Sue were very surprised. They looked around and saw a little door. They used the key to open it. Inside, they found a room full of carrots! Bob and Sue were so happy. They ate carrots and got very full.

Once upon a time, there was a clever ant named Andy. Andy loved to dance. One day, he found a big hill of salt. He thought it would be fun to dance on the salt hill. Andy told his ant friends about the salt hill. They all wanted to dance on it too. They danced and danced, having so much fun. But they did not know that the salt hill was not just salt. Then, something unexpected happened. The salt hill started to move! It was not a hill at all. It was a big, friendly snail named Sally. Sally had been sleeping, but the ants' dancing woke her up. Sally was not mad. She liked the ants and their dancing. So, they all danced together on Sally's back. Andy and his friends learned that sometimes, things are not what they seem. And they all lived happily ever after, dancing and playing with their new friend Sally.
