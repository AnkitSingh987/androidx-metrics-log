Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Cheerful Boy and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful little boy named Tim. He liked to play with his toys. Tim had a toy shelf in his room where he kept all his toys. One day, Tim saw a new toy on the shelf. He wanted to get it, but he was too small. He tried and tried, but he could not reach it. Tim was sad. Then, a friendly bird came in through the window. The bird saw Tim and wanted to help. The bird flew up and got the toy for Tim. Tim was so happy! But when the bird gave the toy to Tim, it turned into a big balloon. It was not a toy at all! Tim and the bird laughed and played with the balloon. They had a fun day together.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Lizzie who loved to go for bike rides. She had a big, red bike with one pedal on each side. One day, Lizzie put on her helmet and said to her mom "Can I go for a ride?" Her mom said "Your bike is too big for you, it won't fit". Lizzie pouted and said "Please?" Her mom thought about it for a moment and then she smiled and said "I know what we can do". She took Lizzie to the bike shop and they got her a new, smaller bike with two pedals. It was just the right size for Lizzie, it fit perfectly! Lizzie was so happy. She quickly hopped on her new bike and rode off down the street, having an adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a big hall. In the hall, there were many silly animals. The animals liked to play and have fun. One day, a small bird named Tim came to the hall. Tim saw the silly animals and said, "Hi, I want to play too!" The animals were happy to have a new friend. They played all day long. The next day, it was time for the animals to go home. Tim, the small bird, decided to lead the way. He said, "Follow me, I will show you the way home." The silly animals followed Tim down the hall and out the door. They all went home and had a good night's sleep.

Once upon a time, there was a big festival in a small town. All the people were very happy. They played games, ate yummy food, and danced. In the town, there was a little girl named Lily. She saw her friends wearing pretty dresses at the festival. Lily felt envious because her dress was old. She wanted to change her dress and look pretty like her friends. Lily went to her mom and asked for a new dress. Her mom hugged her and said, "You are beautiful just the way you are." Lily felt happy and went back to the festival. She played games, ate yummy food, and danced with her friends.

Tom and Anna were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump and slide. But Tom was not careful. He ran too fast and fell down. He hurt his knee and it was bleeding. "Ow, ow, ow!" Tom cried. "It hurts!" Anna ran to him and looked at his knee. She saw a lot of red. She was scared. "Don't cry, Tom. I will help you," Anna said. She took off her scarf and wrapped it around his knee. "This is a bandage. It will stop the red." Tom stopped crying a little. He looked at the bandage. It was pink and had flowers on it. "Thank you, Anna. You are a good friend," Tom said. They walked back to their mommies. They were sitting on a bench and talking. They saw Tom and Anna and their faces changed. "What happened, Tom? What is that on your knee?" Tom's mommy asked. "I fell down and hurt my knee. Anna gave me a bandage," Tom said. Anna's mommy looked at the bandage. She smelled something bad. She frowned. "Anna, where did you get that bandage?" Anna's mommy asked. "It was my scarf. I used it to help Tom," Anna said. Anna's mommy gasped. She pulled the bandage off Tom's knee. She saw a lot of green and yellow. She was angry. "Anna, this is not a bandage. This is a smelly scarf. You used it to wipe your nose when you had a cold. It is full of germs. You made Tom's knee worse. Now he has an infection. He needs to go to the doctor right away," Anna's mommy said. Tom and Anna looked at each other. They were shocked and sad. They did not know they did something bad. They wanted to help each other. But they made a big mistake. They started to cry. Their mommies took them to the car and drove to the doctor. They did not play in the park anymore. They had a bad ending.
