Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Dog and the Kids

One day, a little dog named Max went for a wander. He walked down the street and saw a big building. The building was very tall and had many windows. Max was curious about what was inside the building, so he walked closer to take a look. As Max got closer, he saw some kids playing with a ball. The ball was hard and it bounced high in the air. Max wanted to play too, so he ran up to the kids and wagged his tail. The kids saw Max and laughed. They thought Max was very cute and wanted to play with him. The kids threw the hard ball and Max chased it. He caught the ball in his mouth and brought it back to the kids. They all played together for a long time. Max was very happy that he wandered to the big building and made new friends.

Once upon a time there was a rich man. He was very kind and loved to lend things to others. One day he had a magazine in his hands. He wanted to lend it to someone, so he went outside and looked around. He saw a little 3-year-old girl who was playing in the garden. He went up to her and asked if she would like to borrow the magazine. The girl smiled a big smile and said, “Yes please!” She thanked the rich man for lending it to her. The rich man was very happy to see the girl so happy. They both said goodbye and the girl went off with her magazine. She spent the day reading the stories and looking at the pictures. She was so happy to have something to do. And the rich man was happy that he was able to lend the magazine to her.

Tim and Sue are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a new fence in the park. The fence is ugly. It is brown and rusty and has sharp wires. "Who put this ugly fence here?" Tim says. "I don't like it. It makes the park look bad." "Me neither," Sue says. "Maybe we can break it. Then we can play on the other side." Tim and Sue look around. They do not see anyone. They run to the fence. They try to pull and kick the fence. But the fence is strong. It does not break. "Ow!" Tim says. He hurts his hand on the wire. He sees blood. He cries. "Tim, are you okay?" Sue says. She is scared. She wants to help Tim. But then they hear a loud bark. They turn around. They see a big dog. The dog is angry. It is behind the fence. It wants to bite them. "Help!" Tim and Sue say. They run away from the fence. But the dog is faster. It jumps over the fence. It chases them. Tim and Sue scream. They try to find a place to hide. But the dog is too quick. It catches them. It bites them. It hurts them. Tim and Sue are sorry. They wish they did not touch the ugly fence. They wish they listened to their moms. They wish they stayed home.

Once upon a time, there was an unusual man. He liked to open many things. He opened doors, boxes, and bags. People in the town did not like him much. One day, the unusual man found a big box. He wanted to open it. The box was in the park. The unusual man thought it was a fun game. He did not know what was inside. When the man opened the box, many big bugs came out. The bugs were not nice. They scared the people in the town. The unusual man was sad. He did not want to open things anymore. The town had a bad day because of the big bugs.

One day, a big cat named Tom went to get a haircut. He was very excited because he loved getting his hair cut. Tom liked to look nice and he took care of his hair every day. When Tom got to the haircut place, he saw a little mouse named Jerry. Jerry was scared because he had never had a haircut before. Tom wanted to help Jerry, so he told him not to worry. They both sat down and waited for their turn. When it was time for their haircuts, Tom and Jerry sat in the big chairs. But something unexpected happened! The person who cut their hair made a little mistake. Instead of cutting Tom's hair, they cut Jerry's hair to look like Tom's, and they cut Tom's hair to look like Jerry's! Tom and Jerry looked at each other and laughed. They didn't care that their haircuts were mixed up because they had made a new friend.
