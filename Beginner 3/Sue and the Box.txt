Category: Beginner
Title: Sue and the Box

One day, a little girl named Sue found a box. The box had pretty jewelry inside. Sue thought the jewelry was very attractive. She wanted to wear the jewelry and become a princess. Sue put on the jewelry and went to her friend, Tom. "Look, I am a princess now!" she said. Tom looked at Sue and smiled. "You look very pretty, Princess Sue!" he said. Together, Sue and Tom played in the garden. They pretended to be in a big castle. They had a fun day, and Sue felt like a real princess with her attractive jewelry.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big dog. The dog was rare and had a little tip on his tail. One day, he saw a small cat. The big dog did not like the small cat. He wanted the cat to go away. The big dog went to the small cat and said, "Go away!" The small cat did not want to go away. The big dog was very mad. He demand the small cat to leave now. The small cat was sad but did not leave. Then, the big dog got very angry. He ran at the small cat. The small cat ran away fast. The big dog chased the small cat. But the big dog did not see the big tree. He hit the big tree and fell down. The big dog was hurt and the small cat was gone. The big dog was sad and alone.

Once upon a time, in a small wood, there was a little red bird named Tim. Tim loved to fly around and play with his friends. One day, Tim saw a big tree with a hole in it. He thought it would be a great place to set his nest. Tim worked hard to build his nest. He used dry leaves and small sticks. While he was working, it started to rain. The rain made the wood and leaves very wet. Tim was sad because he could not finish his nest. The next day, the sun came out and dried the wood and leaves. Tim's friend, a squirrel named Sam, came to help him. Together, they finished the nest. Tim was happy and thanked Sam for his help. The moral of the story is that when things get hard, friends can help make it better. And sometimes, the unexpected rain can lead to new friendships.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Sarah. She lived in a big, dirty city. Every day she would roll her way to the playground to play with her friends. One day it was raining outside and Sarah couldn't go to the playground. She was so bored she decided to roll around her house. Big mistake! She rolled right into a big pile of mud! Sarah's clothes quickly became dirty, and she was so sad. Sarah's mom was upset too. She told Sarah that she couldn't play outside anymore until she cleaned up. Sarah knew that it was dirty work, but she had to do it so she rolled up her sleeves and began to clean. After a few hours of hard work, Sarah finally cleaned up the mud. But when she looked up, she saw that her life was also dirty now. All her friends had left without her and she was all alone. Sarah had a bad day.

Lily and Tom were playing in the kitchen. They liked to pretend they were chefs and make food with pots and pans. They also liked to wash the dishes in the sink. But they had to be careful not to make a mess or spill water on the floor. One day, they were having fun with the sink. They turned on the tap and filled the sink with water. They put some dishes and spoons in the water and pretended to wash them. They also made bubbles with the soap and blew them in the air. "Look, Lily, I can make a big bubble!" Tom said. He took a big spoon and dipped it in the soap. He lifted it up and blew hard. A huge bubble came out of the spoon and floated in the air. "Wow, Tom, that's a powerful bubble!" Lily said. She clapped her hands and tried to catch the bubble. But the bubble was too fast and too high. It flew towards the window and popped on the glass. "Oh no, the bubble is gone!" Tom said. He was sad. He wanted to make another bubble. He turned to the sink and reached for the soap. But he did not see that the water was overflowing from the sink. He slipped on the wet floor and fell down. "Ouch, that hurts!" Tom said. He started to cry. He had bumped his head and his knee. He felt pain and he suffered. Lily heard Tom cry and ran to him. She saw that he was hurt and that the floor was wet. She felt sorry for him and she wanted to help him. "Tom, are you okay?" Lily said. She hugged him and kissed his head and his knee. She tried to make him feel better. "I'm sorry, Lily, I made a mess," Tom said. He sniffed and wiped his tears. He looked at the sink and the floor. He felt bad and he was sorry. "It's okay, Tom, it was an accident," Lily said. She smiled and held his hand. She looked at the window and saw that the sun was shining. She had an idea. "Let's go outside and play with the hose. We can make more bubbles and water the flowers. And we can dry up and forget the pain," Lily said. "Okay, Lily, that sounds like fun," Tom said. He smiled and nodded. He got up and followed Lily. They went outside and played with the hose. They made more bubbles and watered the flowers. They also laughed and forgot the pain. They were happy and they were friends.
