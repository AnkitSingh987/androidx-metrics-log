Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Mouse and the Farm

Once upon a time, on a great big farm, there was a little mouse named Tim. Tim loved to play in the hay. One day, Tim saw a big cow named Molly. Molly liked to tease Tim. She would say, "You're so small, Tim!" One day, Tim saw a big pile of hay. He had an idea. Tim called Molly to come over. "Hey, Molly! Look at this big pile of hay!" Molly came over and saw the hay. She laughed and said, "You can't climb that, Tim!" Tim smiled and started to climb the hay. It was hard, but he made it to the top. Molly was surprised. "Wow, Tim! You are great!" From that day on, Molly never teased Tim again. They became good friends and played in the hay together.

Sara and Ben are friends who like to play on the farm. They like to see the cows, the pigs, the chickens and the horses. One day, they decide to play hide and seek in the barn. Sara counts to ten and then looks for Ben. She looks behind the hay, under the wagon, and in the stalls. She cannot find Ben anywhere. She hears a giggle from above. She looks up and sees Ben standing on a beam. He is holding a rope. "Found you!" Sara says. "How did you get up there?" "I climbed the ladder," Ben says. "It was fun. Watch this!" He swings on the rope and jumps down. He lands on a pile of straw. "Wow, that was cool!" Sara says. "Can I try?" "Sure, but be careful," Ben says. He helps Sara climb the ladder. She stands on the beam and grabs the rope. She feels excited and scared. "Ready?" Ben asks. "Ready!" Sara says. She swings on the rope and tries to jump like Ben. But she jumps too soon and misses the straw. She falls on the hard floor. She hurts her knee and her elbow. She starts to cry. Ben runs to her and hugs her. "Are you okay?" he asks. "No, I'm not okay," Sara says. "I'm hurt and I'm embarrassed. Everyone saw me fall." "No, they didn't," Ben says. "No one is here. Only me and you. And I don't think you're embarrassed. I think you're brave. You tried something new and hard. That's what friends do." Sara stops crying and smiles. "Really?" she asks. "Really," Ben says. "You're my best friend. And I'm sorry you got hurt. Do you want to go see the nurse?" Sara nods. "Yes, please," she says. "But can we play again later?" "Of course," Ben says. "But next time, let's play something else. Maybe tag or hopscotch. Or we can feed the animals." "Okay," Sara says. "That sounds good. I like the farm. And I like you." They hold hands and walk out of the barn. They are still friends.

Once upon a time there was a very sleepy cat. The cat was always so tired and did not know what to do. Then the cat made a promise that he would change. The cat started waking up early each day, and spending more time outside. He said hello to all of his animal friends and he did not stay up late. Soon, the cat felt like a new cat. The cat loved his new life and asked all of his friends if he could play all day. His friends said yes, and so the cat was happy again. He was not so sleepy anymore and he could play with his friends all day. He was no longer a sleepy cat. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a mole named Momo. Momo was a rare and special mole because he could turn into any color he wanted. He lived in a big garden with lots of flowers and trees. One day, Momo saw a big bird in the sky. He knew the big bird liked to eat moles. So, Momo had an idea. He turned into the same color as the grass. The big bird flew by, but he could not see Momo because he was hiding so well. Momo was happy that his rare gift helped him stay safe. From that day on, Momo knew that he could turn into any color he wanted to hide from danger. And he lived happily ever after in his big garden home.
