Title: The Yellow Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a big yellow cat. The cat lived in a house with a long hall. The cat liked to play in the hall. One day, the cat found a toy. The toy was a small ball. The cat wanted to know how much the ball weighed. So, the cat put the ball on a scale. The scale said the ball weighed one pound. The cat was happy to know how much the ball weighed. The cat played with the ball in the long hall. The cat and the ball were friends. The yellow cat and the ball lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a pink mill. The mill was a happy place where people made yummy bread. The town's people loved the pink mill. One sunny day, a group of kids decided to march to the pink mill. They walked in a line, singing a song. "We go to the mill, we go to the mill, to see the pink and make some bread," they sang. When they got to the mill, they met a kind lady. She said, "Hello, kids! Welcome to the pink mill. Would you like to see how we make bread?" The kids said, "Yes, please!" They all went inside and watched the bread being made. They had a fun day at the pink mill.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom loved to play outside with his friends. One day, while playing, Tom fell down and felt a pain in his leg. He was sad and didn't want to play anymore. Tom's mom saw him and told him that it was rare for him to quit playing. She said, "Tom, when you feel pain, it's okay to take a break, but don't quit playing forever. It's important to be brave and try again." The next day, Tom went outside and played with his friends. He was careful and didn't fall down. Tom learned that it's okay to feel pain, but he should not quit doing what he loves. The moral of the story is to never give up and always try again.

One day, John was feeling impatient. He wanted something to excite him. He looked around and saw his cupboard. He thought, "Maybe there's something interesting inside". John excitedly opened the cupboard and peered inside. To his disappointment, he discovered that nothing was inside. John groaned and said, "Where's the fun? I'm bored!" Suddenly, John heard a voice coming from the back of the cupboard. He was scared and asked, "Who are you?" The voice said, "I am the cupboard monster. I live in cupboards and I'm very hungry. I need something to eat!" John was frozen in fear. He couldn't move. The cupboard monster ate John, leaving no trace of him behind. John's parents called out for him, but John never returned. The cupboard monster had gotten its meal and was satisfied.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to paint. He found his paint and brush. He thought it would be easy. He wanted to paint a march of ants. Tim started to paint on the wall. Tim's mom saw him painting on the wall. She said, "Tim, no! Paint on paper, not the wall!" Tim felt sad. He wanted to make his mom happy. He said, "I will paint on paper, mom." Tim tried to paint the march of ants on paper. But it was not easy. The ants did not look like ants. Tim's mom saw the painting. She said, "It is okay, Tim. You can try again." But Tim did not want to try again. He was sad. The painting was not good and the wall was still dirty.

Once upon a time, there was a man who wanted to sell rocks from his mine. He had a big mine with many rocks. The man was very strong and healthy because he worked hard every day. He liked to help people and give them nice rocks. One day, a little boy came to the mine. The boy wanted to buy a rock for his mom. The man showed the boy many rocks from his mine. The boy picked a pretty rock that was shiny and smooth. The man was happy to sell the rock to the boy. The boy took the rock home to his mom. She loved the rock and put it in their garden. The boy, his mom, and the man all became friends. They played together and had fun. The man kept selling rocks from his mine and stayed healthy and strong.
