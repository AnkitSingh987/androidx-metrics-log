Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Fish and the Bad

One day, a small fish named Tim saw a bad jellyfish. The bad jellyfish wanted to play a game. Tim was scared but wanted to win. "Let's race," said the bad jellyfish. "If you win, I will go away. If I win, I will stay." Tim thought for a moment and said, "Okay, let's race!" They started to race. Tim swam fast, and the bad jellyfish swam slow. Tim won the race. "I won!" said Tim. The bad jellyfish was sad but kept his promise. He went away, and Tim was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little penny. The penny lived in a wild forest. The forest was full of big trees, pretty flowers, and many animals. One day, the penny wanted to make new friends. It started to listen very carefully. It heard a bird singing a sweet song. The penny went to the bird and said, "Hello, bird! Can we be friends?" The bird was happy and said, "Yes, let's be friends!" Together, the penny and the bird went on many fun adventures in the wild forest. They played games, sang songs, and listened to the sounds of the forest. And they were the best of friends forever.

One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to make cocoa. She asked her mom, "Can I make cocoa?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful. The cocoa can is heavy." Lily tried to lift the heavy cocoa can. It was too heavy for her. She dropped it on the floor. The cocoa spilled everywhere. Lily felt sad and cried. Her mom came and said, "It's okay. Let's clean it up together." After they cleaned up, Lily's mom helped her make the cocoa. Lily stirred it with a big spoon. They both drank the cocoa and smiled. Lily learned that it's okay to ask for help when something is too hard.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. They saw the big blue sea with big waves. The sea seemed rough today. Tim wanted to surf on the waves, but his mom said, "No, Tim. It is not safe." Tim was sad, but he understood. He played with his beach ball and built a sand castle instead. While playing, he saw a small crab. The crab looked scared because it was stuck in the sand. Tim wanted to help the crab. Tim called his mom and said, "Mom, look! The crab needs help." His mom came and helped Tim free the crab. They put the crab back in the sea. Tim felt happy that he could help the crab. The rough sea could not stop Tim from having a fun day at the beach.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to climb trees. One day, he saw a big tree in the park. He wanted to climb it. Tim started to climb the tree. It was a little dark, but he kept going. He climbed higher and higher. Tim was very happy. Then, Tim slipped and hurt his lip. He was sad and scared. His mom came and helped him down. She hugged him and made him feel better. Tim learned to be more careful when climbing trees.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree. In the tree, there was a big nest. A little bird lived in the nest. The little bird liked to write stuff on the leaves. One day, a big bird came to the tree. The big bird said, "This is my tree now. You must go." The little bird was sad, but it did not want to fight. So, the little bird left the tree to find a new home. The little bird looked for a new tree, but all the trees were full. The little bird was very tired and sad. It missed its old tree and the stuff it used to write. The little bird could not find a new home, and it felt very bad.

One day, a furry bee named Bob was flying in the sky. He saw a little girl named Suzy. Suzy was sad because her toy was broken. Bob wanted to help her, so he said, "Suzy, I can repair your toy." Suzy looked at Bob and smiled. She gave her toy to the bee. Bob used his little legs to fix the toy. He worked hard and soon, the toy was like new. Suzy was so happy and said, "Thank you, Bob. You are a good bee." But then, something unexpected happened. The toy started to talk! It said, "I am a magic toy, and I want to be your friend too, Bob." The furry bee and the little girl were very surprised. They all became great friends and played together every day.
