Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play with her toys. One day, she found a toy she did not know. It had a handle and was very pretty. Lucy asked her mom, "Does this toy belong to me?" Her mom said, "No, it belongs to your big sister, but you can play with it." Lucy was very happy and played with the toy all day. Later, Lucy found out that the toy with the handle was not a toy. It was a tool to make tasty food! Her big sister showed her how to use it. They made tasty treats for everyone. Lucy was surprised, but she loved the tasty food they made together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lisa. She wanted to have a fancy tea party with her friends. So, she decided to invite them all to her house. The day of the party was sunny and the weather was nice. Lisa and her friends sat in her garden, and they had tea and cookies. They wore pretty dresses and big hats. They talked and laughed together. One friend said, "Thank you for inviting us, Lisa. This is a fun tea party!" Lisa smiled and said, "I'm happy you all came. The weather is perfect for our fancy tea party!" They played games and had a great time together.

Once upon a time, there was a curious little dog. He liked to wander and look at all the things around him. One day, he saw a big, red ball in a cut near his home. He wanted to play with it. The little dog went to the cut and tried to get the ball. He pushed it with his nose, but it was stuck. He tried to use his paws, but it was still stuck. The little dog did not give up. He wagged his tail and kept trying. Finally, the ball came out of the cut. The little dog was so happy! He played with the ball all day long. He would throw it, chase it, and catch it. The curious little dog had so much fun with his new toy. And from that day on, he never stopped wandering and finding new things to play with.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to take a cool bath. He went to the bathtub and turned on the water. As the water filled the tub, he felt it with his hand to make sure it was just right. Tim's big sister, Sue, came into the bathroom. "I want to help you," she said. "Let's measure the water to make sure it's not too much." Tim was not happy. He wanted to take his bath alone. But Sue was bigger, so he let her help. Sue and Tim used a small cup to measure the water. They counted together, "One, two, three, four..." After ten cups, Sue said, "That's enough water for your bath, Tim." Tim was happy that his sister helped him. Now, he could enjoy his cool bath.

Lily loved to dress up as a queen. She had a big hat with a feather, a shiny dress with many colors, and a necklace with beads. She felt very rich and pretty when she wore them. One day, she asked her mom if she could dress up as a queen and go to the park. Her mom said yes, but she had to be careful with her clothes. Lily said she would, and put on her queen outfit. She looked at herself in the mirror and smiled. At the park, she saw her friend Ben. He was playing with a ball and a truck. He saw Lily and said, "Hi, Lily. What are you wearing?" Lily said, "I am a queen. I have a big hat, a shiny dress, and a necklace. Do you like them?" Ben said, "They are nice, but they are not for playing. You can't run or jump or slide with them. You will get dirty or tear them. Why don't you play with me?" Lily said, "But I want to be a queen. I feel rich and pretty. I don't want to play with a ball or a truck. They are boring." Ben said, "They are not boring. They are fun. You can make noises and pretend to drive or kick. You can have more friends if you play with them. You can be a queen later, at home." Lily thought for a moment. She liked Ben, and she liked to have fun. She also liked to be a queen, but she did not want to be alone. She said, "OK, Ben. I will play with you. But can I keep my hat?" Ben said, "Sure, you can keep your hat. But you have to take off your dress and your necklace. They are too fancy for the park. You can wear them again when we go back." Lily agreed, and took off her dress and her necklace. She put them in her mom's bag, and kept her hat. She ran to Ben, and they played with the ball and the truck. They made noises and pretended to drive or kick. They had a lot of fun, and made more friends. Lily was still a queen, but she was also a friend. She was happy.
