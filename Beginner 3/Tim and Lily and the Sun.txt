Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Lily and the Sun

Tim and Lily are friends. They like to draw with pastels. Pastels are soft and colorful. They make pretty pictures on paper. One day, they go to the park with their moms. They bring their pastels and paper. They find a big table under a tree. They start to draw. "Look, I draw a flower," Lily says. She shows Tim her picture. It has a yellow center and pink petals. "Wow, that's nice," Tim says. He shows Lily his picture. It has a blue sky and a green grass. They draw some more. They are happy. Then, a bald man comes to the table. He is angry. He says, "Hey, kids, this is my table. Go away. You are making a mess with your pastels." Tim and Lily are scared. They don't know what to do. They look at their moms. Their moms come to the table. They are not scared. They are calm. They say, "Hello, sir. We are sorry if we disturbed you. We didn't know this was your table. The kids are just drawing with their pastels. They are not making a mess. They are being creative." The bald man looks at the moms. He looks at the kids. He looks at the pastels. He sees the pretty pictures. He feels a little sorry. He says, "Well, I guess you are right. The kids are being creative. And the pastels are not so bad. They are soft and colorful. They make pretty pictures." He smiles a little. He says, "Maybe I was too harsh. Maybe I can share the table with you. Maybe I can even try to draw with your pastels. Do you have a spare paper?" Tim and Lily are surprised. They are happy. They say, "Yes, we have a spare paper. You can draw with us. We can share our pastels." They give the bald man a paper and some pastels. He sits down with them. He starts to draw. He draws a sun. It has a yellow center and orange rays. He shows Tim and Lily his picture. He says, "Look, I draw a sun. It is bright and warm. It makes me happy." Tim and Lily smile. They say, "Wow, that's nice. We like your sun. It makes us happy too." They draw some more. They are friends. They solve the problem. They have fun.

One day, a red car and a blue car were in a race. They wanted to see who was the fastest. The red car was big and strong, but the blue car was small and cheap. They both thought they could win. "Ready, set, go!" said the red car. The cars raced down the street as fast as they could. The red car was in front, but the blue car was not far behind. They raced past houses and trees, and they saw a folder on the ground. The folder was open and papers were flying everywhere. "Let's stop and help," said the blue car. The red car agreed. They stopped racing and picked up the folder and the papers. They put the papers back in the folder and gave it to a little girl who was looking for it. The girl was happy and thanked the cars. The red car and the blue car smiled and decided that helping was more fun than racing.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy cat named Max. Max lived in a small house with his friend, a little girl named Amy. Max loved to play with Amy's toys, but he never helped her clean up the mess. One day, Max found a new toy. It was a long tape. Max thought the tape was a lot of fun to play with. Amy saw Max playing with the tape and said, "Max, we need to clean up our toys. Will you help me?" Max looked at Amy and then at the tape. He didn't want to stop playing, but he knew Amy was right. So Max decided to help Amy clean up their toys. Together, Max and Amy picked up all the toys and put them away. They even used the tape to fix a broken toy. Max felt happy that he helped Amy clean up. From that day on, Max was not a grumpy cat anymore. He learned that helping others can make you feel good inside. And that was the moral of the story: helping others can make you happy.
