Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to dance. Every day, Sue would put on her tight red shoes and dance around her room. She was very happy when she danced. One day, Sue found a note under her bed. The note said, "Come to the big tree in the park." Sue was excited and ran to the park. She saw the big tree and many kids there. All the kids were dancing around the big tree. Sue put on her tight red shoes and joined them. They all danced and laughed together. Sue made new friends and they danced until the sun went down. Sue had the best day ever.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful girl named Lily. She had big blue eyes that sparkled like the sky. Lily loved to play with her toys, but she did not like to share them with her little brother, Tim. One day, Tim saw a toy he really liked. It was a shiny red car. He asked Lily if he could play with it. Lily said, "No, this is mine!" Tim was sad and cried. Their mom heard the noise and came to see what was wrong. Mom said, "Lily, you need to share your toys with Tim. Sharing is good." Lily thought about it and decided to let Tim play with the car. Tim's eyes lit up and he smiled. They played together and had lots of fun. From that day on, Lily learned to share her toys and they were both very happy.

Tim and Mia lived near the big water. They liked to play on the sand and look at the fish. One day, the sky became dark and the wind became loud. Tim and Mia's mom said, "There is a hurricane coming. We have to go inside and stay safe." Tim and Mia were scared of the hurricane. They heard it roar and shake the house. They saw the rain and the waves. They hugged their mom and their teddy bears. Mom said, "Don't worry, we are together. The hurricane will go away soon." Tim and Mia felt a little better. They squeezed their mom and their teddy bears. They sang songs and read books. They made a fort with pillows and blankets. They crawled inside and felt comfortable. They said, "We are brave. We are strong. We love each other." Mom said, "Yes, you are. And I love you too."

Once upon a time, there was a famous girl named Lily. She was famous for her big, pretty veil. Everyone in the town loved her veil. One day, a boy named Tom wanted to deliver a gift to Lily. Tom went to Lily's house and said, "Lily, I have a gift for you!" Lily looked at the gift and said, "Thank you, Tom!" But when she opened the gift, her pretty veil was gone! She was sad and asked, "Where is my veil?" Tom was surprised and said, "I don't know! Let's look for it together." They looked everywhere in the town. They asked everyone if they saw the veil. But no one knew where it was. At last, they found the veil in a tree. The wind had blown it away! Lily was happy to have her veil back. She said, "Thank you, Tom, for helping me find my veil!" And they were friends forever.

One day, a fox went for a walk. The fox saw a big rock and thought, "This looks like a good place to sit." So, the fox sat on the rock. The sun was warm, and the fox felt happy. After a while, a bird came by and said, "Hi, fox! Why do you sit here?" The fox replied, "I sit here because it is warm and I like it." The bird thought for a moment and said, "You are a tough fox to sit on this hard rock." The fox smiled and said, "Yes, I am a tough fox. But I like to sit here because it is warm and makes me happy." The bird nodded and flew away. The fox stayed on the rock, enjoying the warm sun, feeling proud to be a tough fox.
