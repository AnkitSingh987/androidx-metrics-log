Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Bird

Tom and Lily were friends. They liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big bird in a cage. The bird looked sad and scared. "Let's help the bird," Tom said. "Maybe he wants to fly away." Lily nodded. They went to the cage and opened the door. The bird flapped his wings and flew out. He was happy and healthy. But the bird was not nice. He bit Tom's hand hard. Tom cried out in pain. He let go of the door. The bird flew away. Lily ran to Tom and hugged him. She saw blood on his hand. She was scared and sorry. "Tom, are you okay?" she asked. Tom shook his head. He felt sick and hurt. He wanted his mom. They heard a loud voice. It was the man who owned the bird. He was angry and mean. "Hey, you kids! What did you do to my bird? He was worth a lot of money! You will pay for this!" He ran to them and grabbed them by their arms. He dragged them to his car. He locked them inside. Tom and Lily were trapped. They cried and screamed. They wished they never saw the bird. They wished they never opened the cage. They wished they could go home.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a sad dog sitting by a cable. The dog looked at Tim and said, "I am sad because I can't pass this cable." Tim wanted to help the sad dog. He thought for a moment and said, "I will help you pass the cable, and then you won't be sad anymore." Tim looked around and found a big stick. He used the stick to lift the cable, and the dog was able to pass under it. The dog was very happy and wagged his tail. "Thank you, Tim," said the dog. "Now I am not sad anymore." Tim and the dog played together all day. They became best friends and were never sad again.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a gifted hen named Henny. Henny was not like other hens. She could lay big, colorful eggs. One day, Henny laid a very special egg. It was shiny and sparkly. "Wow, Henny!" said her friend, Bunny. "Your egg is so pretty! Can I see it?" Henny nodded and showed her friend the special egg. They both looked at it and smiled. But then, it started to get very cold. The wind blew and the air felt like ice. Henny and Bunny started to freeze. They knew they had to keep the special egg warm. So, they put the egg between them and hugged it tight. As they hugged the egg, it began to glow. The glow made them feel warm and cozy. Henny and Bunny were so happy. They knew that their love and care had saved the special egg. And from that day on, they never felt cold again.

One day, Tim went to play outside. He put on his shoes and tried to tie them, but he did not know how. Tim asked his mom for help. His mom tied his shoes and told him to be careful. Tim went to the park and saw a big flood. The water was smelly. He wanted to play in the water, but he knew it was not safe. Tim saw a frog near the water. The frog jumped into the flood. Tim wanted to save the frog. He went close to the water, but he fell in. The water was cold and smelly. Tim was scared and called for help. His mom heard him and came running. She pulled Tim out of the water. They both went home. Tim was sad he could not save the frog. The frog stayed in the smelly flood, and Tim learned to be more careful.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to carry his toy mouse around the house. One day, Fluffy saw a big box in the living room. "What is in the box?" Fluffy thought. Fluffy's friend, a little girl named Lily, came into the room. She saw Fluffy looking at the box. "Do you want to see what's inside?" she asked. Fluffy meowed happily. Lily opened the box, and there was a new toy mouse inside! Fluffy was so happy. He played with the new toy mouse and carried it everywhere. At the end of the day, Fluffy put his old toy mouse and the new toy mouse in the tidy box. Lily smiled and said, "Now we have a special place for your toys, Fluffy!" And they lived happily ever after.
