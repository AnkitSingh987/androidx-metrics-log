Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Sam and the Book

Tim and Sam are brothers. They like to play with their toys, but they do not like to share. One day, they find a big book in the attic. The book has many pictures and words. It is a quiz book about ancient things. Tim wants to look at the book. He likes to learn new things. He opens the book and sees a picture of a pyramid. He reads the question: "What is the name of this ancient building?" He thinks hard. Sam wants to look at the book too. He likes to see the pictures. He tries to grab the book from Tim. He says: "Give me the book! It is my turn!" Tim does not want to give the book. He says: "No, it is not your turn! I found the book first! Go away!" Sam does not go away. He pulls the book harder. Tim pulls the book harder too. They both shout and cry. Mom hears the noise. She comes to the attic. She sees Tim and Sam fighting over the book. She is angry. She says: "Stop it, both of you! You are being very naughty! You need to share and take turns!" She takes the book from them. She says: "This book is too old and fragile for you. You might break it or tear it. It is an ancient book. It belongs to Grandpa. He gave it to me when I was little. He loves this book very much." She shuts the book and puts it on a high shelf. She says: "You can look at the book later, but only if you promise to be careful and respectful. And only if you promise to play nicely with each other. Do you understand?" Tim and Sam nod. They say: "Yes, Mom. We are sorry. We understand." Mom hugs them and says: "That's good. I love you both. Now, why don't you go and play with your other toys? I have a surprise for you. I made some cookies for you. They are in the kitchen. Do you want some?" Tim and Sam smile. They say: "Yes, Mom. We want some. Thank you, Mom." They run to the kitchen. They forget about the book. They eat the cookies and play with their other toys. They share and take turns. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a small, friendly bunny. Every morning the bunny went out to the meadow to look for treats. One day, the bunny found a big, hard flower. The bunny quickly ran up and chewed the flower. But the flower was too hard for the bunny, and it hurt the bunny's teeth. The bunny was very sad and started crying. Then, a wise owl flew down and said to the bunny, "Be careful what you chew, little bunny. Not everything is good or safe to eat". The bunny thanked the wise owl, and from that day on paid more attention to what it was crawling on and chewing. The bunny never chewed on a hard flower again, and was always wary of what was safe to chew. The bunny learned an important lesson: be careful what you chew!

One day, a boy named Tom went to the navy with his dad. The navy had big ships and noisy boats. Tom liked to watch them go by. Tom saw a noisy boat and said, "Dad, I need to go on that boat!" His dad smiled and said, "Okay, let's go." They got on the noisy boat and started to ride on the water. As they rode, Tom saw a little fish jump out of the water. He laughed and said, "Look, dad! The fish is having fun too!" They had a great day at the navy, and Tom couldn't wait to go back again.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom liked to write. He wrote on paper, on the wall, and even on the ground. One day, he saw a big network of lines on the wall. It looked like a spider web. Tom wanted to write on it, but he didn't know how. Tom's friend, Sue, saw the big network too. Sue was good at writing. She took a pen and started to write on the network. Tom felt jealous. He wanted to write on the network too, but he didn't know how to do it like Sue. Tom asked Sue to teach him how to write on the network. Sue said yes. They practiced writing on the network together. Soon, Tom was good at it too. They were both happy, and they wrote on the network all day. They were not jealous of each other anymore. They were best friends who loved to write together.
