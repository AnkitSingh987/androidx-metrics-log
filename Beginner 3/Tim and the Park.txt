Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Park

Once upon a time, there were two twins named Tim and Tom. They were popular in their town. Everyone liked them because they were nice and always helped others. One day, Tim and Tom were playing in the park when a little girl named Lily came up to them. She was sad because she lost her toy. "Please, can you help me find my toy?" Lily requested. The twins said, "Yes, we will help you." So, Tim, Tom, and Lily looked all around the park. They looked under the trees and behind the swings. Finally, they found the toy near the slide. Lily was so happy! She thanked the twins and they all played together for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, in a cool farm, there lived a pig. The pig was pink and small. He had many friends on the farm. One day, the pig met a duck. The duck said, "Hello, pig! Let's play!" The pig and the duck played all day. They ran in the grass and jumped in the mud. The pig was happy. He said, "Duck, you are a good friend!" One day, the pig saw a big dog. The dog was lost. The pig said, "Dog, do you need help?" The dog said, "Yes, please. I need to find my way home." So, the pig and the duck lead the dog to his home. They walked and walked until they found the dog's house. The dog was happy to be home. He said, "Thank you, pig and duck!" The pig and the duck were happy to help. They went back to the cool farm and played some more. They were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a humble bunny named Bob. Bob liked to hop around and observe things. One day, he saw a big, white cauliflower in a garden. "Wow!" said Bob to his friend, Cat. "Look at that big cauliflower!" Cat looked at the cauliflower and agreed, "Yes, it is very big!" Bob and Cat wanted to eat the cauliflower, but first, they asked the kind farmer. The farmer said, "You can have the cauliflower if you help me with my work." So, Bob and Cat helped the farmer and got the cauliflower. Bob and Cat were very happy. They shared the cauliflower with their friends and had a big party. Everyone had fun and enjoyed the tasty cauliflower. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a big, sleepy dog named Bo found a big barrel in his yard. He did not know where it came from. He went to his friend, the little bird named Tim. "Tim, did you send this barrel?" Bo asked. "No, Bo, I did not send it," Tim said. "Let us go and ask Cat if she sent it." So, Bo and Tim went to find Cat. Cat was sitting by a tree. "Cat, did you send the big barrel?" Bo asked. Cat shook her head. "No, I did not send it," Cat said. They all went back to the barrel and looked at it, feeling confused. Then, they heard a small voice from inside the barrel. "Help, help!" it said. Bo, Tim, and Cat all looked at each other. Bo used his big paw to open the barrel. Out came a little bunny named Sue. "Thank you for helping me!" Sue said. "I was stuck in the barrel and could not get out." Bo, Tim, and Cat were happy they could help Sue. They all became good friends and played together every day.

One day, a girl named Sue put on her thick apron. She wanted to walk to her friend Tom's house. She liked her apron because it was big and had many colors. Sue walked down the street, feeling happy. On her way, she saw a cat. The cat said, "Hi Sue! I like your apron. Can I walk with you?" Sue said, "Yes, you can walk with me. Let's go to Tom's house together." So, Sue and the cat walked side by side. When they got to Tom's house, Tom came out to play. He saw Sue's apron and the cat. Tom said, "Wow, I like your apron, Sue! And hello, cat!" They all played and had fun. It was a good day for Sue, Tom, and the cat.
