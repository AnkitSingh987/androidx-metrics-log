Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Mom

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing with his toys. He had a lot of fun with his cars and dolls. Suddenly, he felt a pinch on his finger. "Ouch!" he said, looking at his finger. He saw a tiny bug that had pinched him. Tim was troubled and didn't know what to do. Tim ran to his mom and said, "Mom, a bug pinched me! It hurts!" His mom looked at his finger and saw a little red spot. "Oh no, this is an emergency!" she said. Tim's mom was worried, but she knew what to do. She took Tim to the kitchen and put some ice on his finger. The cold ice made the pain go away. Tim felt better and said, "Thank you, Mom!" His mom smiled and said, "You're welcome. Be careful with bugs next time." Tim nodded and went back to play with his toys, happy that his mom had helped him in his emergency.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a little book called a novel. She loved to read her novel every day. One day, she saw a little crab on the beach. The crab was very cute, but Sue knew it could pinch. Sue said to her mom, "I want to play with the crab, but I don't want it to pinch me." Her mom said, "Be careful, and it won't pinch you." Sue was happy and went to the crab. She remembered her mom's words and was very careful. Sue played with the crab all day. She even read her novel to the crab. The crab liked the story too. At the end of the day, Sue said goodbye to the crab. She was so happy that she played with the crab and it didn't pinch her.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She wanted to have a party for her friends. Lily had a big idea. She wanted to make a hidden treasure game for the party. Lily asked her mom, "Can we print clues for the hidden treasure game?" Her mom said, "Yes, let's do it!" They printed the clues with fun pictures and easy words. On the day of the party, Lily's friends came over. They were all excited to play the hidden treasure game. They followed the clues and found the hidden treasure. It was a big box of fun toys and treats. All the kids laughed and had a great time at the party.

Sara and Lily were playing in the garden. They liked to water the flowers with the hose. They also liked to spray each other and laugh. "Look, Lily, I can make a rainbow!" Sara said, holding the hose up to the sun. "Wow, that's pretty!" Lily said, admiring the colors. But then, Sara turned the hose on Lily and soaked her. Lily gasped and got wet. "Hey, that's not fair!" Lily said, trying to dodge the water. "You soaked my blouse!" Sara giggled and said, "Sorry, Lily, I was just having fun. You can soak me back if you want." Lily smiled and grabbed the hose from Sara. She sprayed Sara and soaked her too. Sara squealed and got wet. "Ha, ha, now we're even!" Lily said, feeling happy. They both laughed and hugged. They didn't mind being wet. They liked to play in the water. But then, they heard their mom calling them. "Girls, time to come inside and have lunch!" Mom said, looking at them. "Oh, no, we're in trouble!" Sara said, looking at her wet clothes. "Quick, let's go inside and change!" Lily said, running to the house. They ran inside, but they were too slow. Mom saw them and shook her head. "Girls, what have you done? You're soaked!" Mom said, sounding cross. "Sorry, Mom, we were playing with the hose and we soaked each other," Sara said, feeling sorry. "It was fun, Mom, we made a rainbow and we laughed," Lily said, feeling sorry too. Mom sighed and said, "I'm glad you had fun, but you can't play with the hose like that. You'll catch a cold and ruin your clothes. Next time, ask me first, okay?" "Okay, Mom, we're sorry," Sara and Lily said, nodding. Mom smiled and said, "It's okay, I love you. Now, go and change into dry clothes and then come and have lunch. And no more soaking, okay?" "Okay, Mom, we love you too," Sara and Lily said, hugging her. They went and changed into dry clothes and then came and had lunch. They ate their sandwiches and drank their juice. They were still happy, even though they were wet. They liked to play in the garden. They liked to water the flowers. They liked to spray each other. They liked to make rainbows. They liked to laugh. They were best friends.
