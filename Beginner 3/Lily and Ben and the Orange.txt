Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Orange

Lily and Ben were playing in the park with their mom. They had a big bottle of soap and a wand to make bubbles. Lily loved to make bubbles and watch them fly in the air. She liked how they shone with different colors in the sun. "Look, Ben, look!" she said, pointing to a big bubble that she had made. "It's so pretty!" Ben looked at the bubble and smiled. He wanted to touch it and see what it felt like. He reached out his hand and tried to catch the bubble. But as soon as he touched it, the bubble popped and disappeared. "Aw, no!" he said, feeling sad. "The bubble is gone!" Lily saw that Ben was sad and wanted to cheer him up. She had an idea. She dipped the wand in the soap and blew a new bubble. But this time, she added something to the soap. She had a small bottle of orange juice that she had brought for her snack. She poured some of the juice into the soap and mixed it well. "Watch this, Ben, watch this!" she said, blowing a new bubble. "It's a special bubble!" Ben looked at the new bubble and gasped. It was orange, just like the juice. He had never seen an orange bubble before. He gazed at it with wonder and curiosity. He wanted to touch it, but he was afraid it would pop again. "Go on, Ben, touch it!" Lily said, encouraging him. "It won't pop, I promise!" Ben trusted his sister and reached out his hand again. He touched the orange bubble gently and felt it bounce on his fingers. It didn't pop. It was soft and squishy and wet. He giggled and squeezed the bubble. It made a funny sound. "Wow, Lily, this is amazing!" he said, laughing. "How did you do that?" Lily smiled and hugged her brother. She was happy that he was happy. She told him her secret and showed him the bottle of juice. They decided to make more orange bubbles and have fun with them. They blew them, caught them, threw them, and popped them. They made their mom laugh too. They had a great time in the park with their orange bubbles.

One day, a young boy named Tom was playing with his toy fireman. He loved to pretend that he was a fireman too, just like his dad. Tom's dad was a real fireman who helped put out fires and save people. Tom was playing when he heard a loud noise. "Help! Help!" someone yelled. Tom looked and saw a little girl stuck in a tree. "I will save you!" Tom said. He put on his toy fireman hat and climbed the tree. The little girl was scared, but Tom remembered what his dad told him: "Be brave, and help others." Tom reached the girl and said, "I am a fireman, just like my dad. I will get you down." The girl smiled and said, "Thank you, fireman Tom!" They climbed down together, and the girl was safe. Tom's dad saw what happened and was very proud of his brave son.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play outside. One day, they see dark clouds in the sky. They hear a loud noise. It is thunder. "Let's go inside!" Ben says. "It will rain soon." Anna nods. She picks up her toys. She sees a shiny thing on the grass. It is a bracelet. It has many colors. "Look, Ben!" Anna says. "I found a bracelet. It is so pretty. Can I keep it?" Ben looks at the bracelet. He smiles. He likes the colors too. "Sure, Anna. You can keep it. It is a gift from the sky. Maybe someone lost it and the wind brought it here." Anna puts the bracelet on her wrist. She feels happy. She hugs Ben. "Thank you, Ben. You are a friendly friend. I will share the bracelet with you. We can take turns to wear it." Ben hugs Anna back. He feels happy too. They run inside the house. They hear the rain start to fall. They watch the raindrops from the window. They hold hands. They are glad they have each other.
