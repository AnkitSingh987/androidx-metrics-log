Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Tom and the Rabbit

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play in the garden. They see a little rabbit in the grass. The rabbit is white and soft. It has long ears and a pink nose. "Look, a rabbit!" Lily says. "Can we keep it?" Tom shakes his head. "No, we can't. It is not our rabbit. It belongs to someone else. Maybe it is lost." Lily feels sad. She wants to keep the rabbit. She thinks it is cute and funny. She has an idea. She runs to the kitchen and gets a carrot. She breaks it in half and gives one piece to Tom. "Let's give the rabbit a carrot. Maybe it is hungry. Maybe it will like us," she says. Tom nods. He likes the idea. He follows Lily to the garden. They kneel down and hold out the carrot pieces. The rabbit sees them and hops closer. It sniffs the carrot and nibbles it. It makes a happy sound. "Hello, rabbit. Do you like the carrot?" Lily asks. The rabbit looks at Lily and Tom with its big eyes. It wiggles its nose and ears. It seems to say thank you. Lily and Tom smile. They feel happy too. They pet the rabbit gently. They play with it for a while. They forget about keeping it. They just enjoy being with it.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear named Ben. He lived in a distant forest, far away from people. Ben loved to eat, and his favorite thing to cook his food on was a grill. One sunny day, Ben was cooking his food on the grill. He smelled the yummy food and his tummy growled. As he was cooking, he heard a noise and decided to pause. He looked around and saw a little bunny hopping towards him. The bunny said, "Hi, I'm Bree! Can I have some food too?" Ben was surprised because he thought bunnies only ate plants. But Bree told him she liked to try all kinds of food. So Ben shared his grilled food with Bree, and they became best friends. From that day on, they cooked on the grill together and tried many new foods. The forest was never the same again.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They had a fun time playing on the swings and slides. Tim felt warm in the sun as he played. Then, a magic man appeared. The magic man had a big whip in his hand. He showed Tim and his mom how he could make the whip go snap, snap, snap. They were amazed. Tim and his mom said thank you to the magic man. They went home and told dad about the magic man and his whip. They all felt happy and warm inside.

Abby and her brother, Tom, played in their garden. Abby found a stick. She was excited because she wanted to write something with it. Tom smiled and said, "Maybe you can use it with the chalk." Abby looked at him, then returned her gaze to the stick. She grabbed the chalk and drew it on the stone path. She was happy to make something with it. Abby went to show her Mom. She smiled and said, "That looks cool!" Abby looked proud. Tom was helpful and grabbed a stick to draw some shapes around Abby's drawing. Suddenly, Abby noticed the stick rising in the air. She gasped, but then realized it was wind and the stick was just dancing in the sky. On Abby's last day of playing she looked up to the sky. She could see the stick still dancing. She was happy and said, "I'm glad Tom was helpful. Chalk can do special things!"

One day, a boy named Tim found a smooth rock. He liked it a lot. He showed it to his friend, Sue. "Look at my smooth rock!" Tim said. Sue liked it too. They played with the rock all day. The next day, Tim and Sue saw something strange. The smooth rock was bigger! They were happy. "The rock is growing!" Sue said. They played with the rock again. Each day, the rock would increase in size. One day, the rock was very big. Tim and Sue were playing near the rock. Suddenly, the rock started to move! It was not a rock at all, but a big turtle! The turtle looked at Tim and Sue. "Thank you for playing with me," the turtle said. Tim and Sue were surprised, but they were happy to have a new friend. They played with the turtle every day from that point on.
