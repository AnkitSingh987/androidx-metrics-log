Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Bird and the Dish

One day, a little bird named Tim was flying in the sky. He saw a light dish on the ground. Tim wanted to eat from the dish, but a big cat named Max was near it. Tim was scared of Max, but he wanted the dish very much. Tim had an idea. He flew up high and began to whistle. The whistle was loud and pretty. Max looked up and saw Tim. Max liked the whistle sound and wanted to hear more. So, Max walked away from the dish to follow Tim. Tim whistled and flew far away from the dish. Max followed Tim, still listening to the pretty whistle. When they were very far, Tim stopped whistling and hid in a tree. Max looked around, but he could not find Tim. So, Max walked back to find the dish. While Max was away, Tim quickly flew back to the dish. He ate all the food in it. When Max came back, the dish was empty. Tim was very happy that he got the dish and Max did not.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a happy dog who loved to play. One day, Spot saw a tie on the floor. The tie was red and pretty. Spot wanted to play with the tie, so he picked it up with his mouth. Spot's owner, a little girl named Sue, was getting ready for a party. Sue saw Spot with the tie and laughed. She thought it was funny that Spot wanted to wear a tie too. Sue put the tie on Spot and said, "Now you're ready for the party too, Spot!" While Sue and her family were at the party, Spot saw a butterfly outside. He wanted to chase it and play. Spot was able to escape through the open door and ran after the butterfly. He had lots of fun playing outside. When Sue came home, she found Spot and the butterfly in the yard. She was happy that Spot had fun too. They all went inside and had a big hug.

Once there lived a little rabbit. He was very clever. He wanted to know what rain was. So one day he asked his mother: "Mom, what is rain?" His mother smiled and said: "Rain is wet drops coming from the sky." The little rabbit was very interested and asked again: "But why does it rain?" His mother replied: "It rains because the clouds are full of water and the water has to come down somehow." The little rabbit was so amazed. He smiled and said: "That's so clever!" The little rabbit was so excited that he couldn't wait for it to rain. He wanted to see and feel the raindrops. Finally it rained. The little rabbit looked up and saw the wet drops falling from the sky. He giggled in delight and said "That's so cool!" He felt the raindrops on his fur and he was so happy. He learned that rain is so cool and that it can bring happiness.

Once upon a time, there was a smart girl named Lily. She liked to learn and care for her toys. One day, she found a book about a big volcano. Lily wanted to know more about it. Lily asked her mom to read the book to her. Her mom read about the big volcano that could make fire and rocks. Lily was so happy to learn about the volcano. She told her mom that she wanted to be smart like the volcano. Lily's mom said, "You are smart, Lily. You care about learning new things. That's what makes you smart." Lily smiled and hugged her mom. She knew that she was smart, just like the big volcano in the book.

Once upon a time, there was a big swan. The swan lived in a pond. The pond was in a big park. One day, a little boy came to the park. He saw the swan in the pond. He was so happy. He pointed at the swan and said, "Look, Mom! A swan!" The swan saw the boy too. The swan swam to the boy. The boy and the swan played near the pond. They had a lot of fun. Soon, it was time for the boy to go home. He waved goodbye to the swan. The swan swam to the distant part of the pond. The boy knew he would come back to see his swan friend again.
