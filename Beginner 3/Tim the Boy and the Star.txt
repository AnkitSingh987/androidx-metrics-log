Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Star

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very anxious because he was going to the airport for the first time. He held his mom's hand tight as they walked to the big building. Inside the airport, Tim saw a man cutting paper to make pretty shapes. He wanted to try too. Tim's mom said, "Be careful, Tim. Don't cut your finger." Tim listened and cut the paper slowly. He made a beautiful star. On the way home, Tim showed his star to his friends. They all wanted to learn how to cut shapes too. Tim taught them how to do it safely. They all had fun and no one got hurt. The moral of the story is to always be careful and listen to good advice.

Once upon a time, there was a modern car. It had four big tires. The car loved to go fast. One day, the car went to a fun show where all the cars came to play. At the show, there was a game. The cars had to clap their doors. The car with the loudest clap would win a prize. The modern car wanted to win the prize, so it tried very hard to clap its doors loud. The modern car clapped its doors as loud as it could. The other cars clapped too. But the modern car's clap was the loudest. It won the prize! The car was so happy, it went home and told all its friends. They all clapped their doors to celebrate.

Once upon a time, in a distant land, there was a big march. Many people came to walk and play. They had fun and laughed a lot. They all believed in being kind and sharing. One day, a small boy named Tim joined the march. He saw a girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she lost her toy. Tim said, "Don't worry, Sue. I will help you find your toy." They looked and looked. They asked everyone in the march if they saw the toy. A tall man said, "Yes, I saw your toy near the big tree." Tim and Sue went to the big tree and found the toy. Sue was very happy. She said, "Thank you, Tim! I believe in you!" They became good friends and played together in the march. Everyone was happy and had a great time.

"Mommy, can I get the stack?" asked Jack. "Yes, but be careful," replied Mommy. So Jack slowly walked over to the stack and carefully picked it up. He was so excited to have it. Jack ran back to his mommy so he could show her. But the stack was too heavy, and started to fall out of Jack's hands. He tried his best to hold onto it, but it was too late. The stack crashed to the ground and broke into pieces. "Oh no!" cried Mommy. She was very worried. Jack started to cry. He felt so sad. He knew it was his fault that the stack was ruined. "It's alright. We can get a new stack," said Mommy, trying to reassure Jack. But Jack knew that it would not be the same. Nothing would ever be the same again.

Once upon a time there was a mighty toy. It liked to zip around the room and play. Everywhere it went it was the fastest toy. No other toy could ever catch it. The other toys watched with envy as the mighty toy dashed around them. They wished they could be that fast. One day, they decided to challenge the mighty toy to a race. They all lined up, and the race began! The toy zoomed faster than ever before. It shot past all of the other toys and crossed the finish line first. Everyone cheered for the mighty toy! The toy bowed to its competitors and said, "I am the fastest toy around. I zipped past you all and won the race. Now I can zip around the room and play some more". And so, the mighty toy zipped around all day, happy and fast!

Lila and Tom are friends. They like to play outside. One day, they see a big road with many cars. They want to cross the road, but they know it is not safe. They look for a place where they can cross. They see a bridge over the road. They walk to the bridge. The bridge is high and long. They are a little scared, but they hold hands. On the bridge, they see many things. They see birds and trees and flowers. They see a sign that says "pick me". They see a basket with many fruits. They are hungry and curious. They go to the basket. They pick some fruits. They taste them. They are sweet and juicy. They are happy. They share the fruits with each other. They hear a loud noise. They look down. They see a big truck on the road. The truck hits a hole in the road. The hole is deep and wide. The truck stops. The driver gets out. He is angry and sad. Lila and Tom feel sorry for the driver. They wave to him. They throw some fruits to him. The driver sees them. He smiles. He says "thank you". He picks up the fruits. He eats them. He feels better. Lila and Tom are glad. They finish their fruits. They cross the bridge. They go back to their homes. They hug each other. They say "bye bye". They are good friends.
