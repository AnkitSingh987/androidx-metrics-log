Category: Beginner
Title: The Prince and the Frog

Once upon a time, there was a prince who lived in a big castle. The prince loved to play outside. One day, he found a wet, little frog near the pond. The frog was all alone and could not jump. The prince wanted to help the frog. He had an idea. He took a small leaf and attach it to the frog's foot. Now, the frog could use the leaf like a shoe and jump again. But then, something unexpected happened. When the frog jumped, it turned into a beautiful butterfly. The prince was very happy to see the butterfly. They became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Tim. He liked to play with his toy car. One day, Tim saw a switch on the wall. He was scared to touch it. But he wanted to know what it did. So, he asked his big sister, Lily. Lily did not know, so she just shrugged. Later that day, Tim and Lily were playing in their room. They heard a loud noise outside. Both of them were scared. They saw a big truck outside. The truck had a big switch on the side. Tim and Lily were curious about what the switch did. Tim and Lily went outside. They were brave and decided to try the switch on the truck. When they pulled the switch, something unexpected happened. The big truck turned into a big fun playhouse! Tim and Lily were so happy. They played in the playhouse all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play golf. He had a small golf set with a red ball and a blue club. Tim would play golf every day in his yard. One day, Tim saw a naughty cat. The cat was running fast and grabbed Tim's red golf ball. Tim was sad to see his ball taken away. He wanted to get his ball back from the naughty cat. Tim chased the cat and saw it hiding under a big tree. He asked the cat nicely to give his golf ball back. The naughty cat felt sorry and gave Tim his red ball back. Tim was happy to see his golf ball again. They both played golf together and became good friends.

Tom and Lily were playing a game of cards. They liked to win and they were very competitive. They did not like to lose or share. "Your turn, Lily," Tom said. He had a good hand and he hoped to finish the game soon. Lily looked at her cards and frowned. She did not have any good cards. She wanted to cheat and look at Tom's cards, but he was watching her. She had an idea. "Tom, can I ask you a question?" she said. "Sure, what is it?" Tom said. He was curious. Lily pointed to the clock on the wall. "What is that?" she asked. Tom looked at the clock. "That is a clock. It tells you the time. See, the big hand is on the twelve and the little hand is on the three. That means it is three o'clock." Lily nodded. She did not really care about the clock. She just wanted to distract Tom. While he was looking at the clock, she quickly peeked at his cards. She saw that he had a lot of red cards. She smiled and put her cards down. "I have a lot of black cards. I win!" she said. Tom looked at her cards and then at his cards. He realized that she had cheated. He was angry and sad. He did not like cheaters. He did not want to play with Lily anymore. "That is not fair! You cheated! You looked at my cards!" he shouted. Lily did not care. She was happy that she had won. She did not care about Tom's feelings. She did not want to be his friend anymore. "I did not cheat! You are just a sore loser! You are a bad friend!" she shouted back. They started to fight and yell. They did not listen to each other. They did not say sorry. They did not make up. They did not have fun. They had a bad ending.
