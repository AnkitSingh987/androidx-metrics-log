Category: Beginner
Title: Jane the Girl and the Ball

One day, a little girl named Jane found a lamp in her room. The lamp was dirty, so Jane decided to polish it. She took a soft cloth and rubbed the lamp to make it shiny and clean. As she polished the lamp, a harmless, friendly genie came out. The genie was big and blue. He said, "Thank you for cleaning my home! I will give you one wish." Jane thought for a moment and said, "I wish for a new toy to play with." The genie nodded and with a wave of his hand, a big, red ball appeared. Jane was very happy and thanked the genie. From that day on, Jane took good care of the lamp and played with her new ball. She learned that helping others can bring happiness and good things.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a brother named Sam. They liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big, fair wheel. They wanted to ride it. Tim and Sam went on the fair wheel. They went up, up, up! But then, the wheel stopped. Tim started to panic. He said, "Sam, I am scared!" Sam looked at Tim and said, "Don't worry, brother. It will move again soon." And it did! The fair wheel started to move again. Tim and Sam went down, down, down. They were happy and not scared anymore. When they got off the wheel, they gave each other a big hug. They went home and told their mom about their fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a big purple bird. The bird loved to fly and sing. One day, it saw a cherry tree. The tree was full of red cherries. The bird was very happy. The purple bird flew to the cherry tree. It picked a cherry with its beak. The cherry was sweet and juicy. The bird ate more cherries and sang a happy song. A little girl heard the bird's song. She walked to the cherry tree and saw the purple bird. The girl and the bird became friends. They ate cherries and sang songs together. They were happy and played every day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside when the sun was shining. One day, he saw his big sister, Jane, studying for an exam. She looked worried. Tim wanted to help Jane with her exam. He thought that if the sun could shine, maybe he could shine too. So, he went outside to play and found a shiny rock. He brought the shiny rock to Jane and said, "This is for you. It will help you shine in your exam." Jane smiled and thanked Tim for the shiny rock. On the day of the exam, Jane took the shiny rock with her. She felt happy and did her best. When the exam was over, Jane had done very well. She knew that Tim's shiny rock had helped her. The moral of the story is that even the smallest act of kindness can make a big difference.

Once there was a little girl who wanted a pear. She asked her mom for one, but mom said she didn't have any. The little girl was sad and started to cry. Then, her big brother said he could lend her a pear. He went to the tree and pulled one off for her. But when he gave it to her it was dead. The little girl got angry, but her brother said he could get her another one. This time it was fresh and juicy and the little girl was so happy. She thanked her brother for lending her a pear.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to watch movies on the big TV. Today, they want to watch a funny movie with animals. But first, they need to make popcorn. Anna goes to the kitchen and gets a big bag of popcorn. She puts it in the microwave and pushes the button. Ben waits in the living room and looks at the movie box. He sees a picture of a lion, a zebra, a giraffe and a penguin. He thinks they look funny. The microwave makes a loud noise. Pop, pop, pop! Anna hears the popcorn popping. She smiles and opens the door. She takes out the hot bag and puts it in a big bowl. She adds some salt and butter. She smells the popcorn. It smells good. She brings the bowl to the living room and puts it on the table. Ben sees the popcorn and claps his hands. He says, "Yay, popcorn! Thank you, Anna!" He takes a handful and puts it in his mouth. He crunches and munches. He says, "Mmm, yummy!" Anna sits next to him and takes some popcorn too. She says, "You're welcome, Ben! Are you ready to watch the movie?" Ben nods and says, "Yes, I'm ready! Let's watch the movie!" They turn on the TV and press play. They see the animals on the screen. They laugh and giggle. They enjoy the movie and the popcorn. They are happy.
