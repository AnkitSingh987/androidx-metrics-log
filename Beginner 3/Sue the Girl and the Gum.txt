Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Gum

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the store with her mom. Sue saw a rare gum that she wanted to try. It was pink and looked very yummy. Sue asked her mom, "Can I have the gum, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can have it." Sue was very happy and said, "Thank you, mom!" She opened the gum and started to chew it. It tasted so good, and she liked it a lot. She shared the gum with her friend, Tom, who also liked it very much. Tom said, "This is the best gum ever!" Sue and Tom played together and chewed their rare gum. They were very happy and thankful for the yummy treat. They promised to share more fun things with each other in the future.

Sam was a gifted boy. He could draw, sing and run very fast. He liked to learn new things and make new friends. But he was also shy and scared of big changes. One day, his mom and dad told him they had a surprise for him. They said he was going to camp for a week. Sam felt nervous. He had never been to camp before. He did not know what it was like. He did not want to leave his home and his toys. He asked his mom and dad if he could stay with them. His mom and dad hugged him and said they understood. They said camp was a fun place where he could play, explore and meet other gifted kids like him. They said they were proud of him and they would miss him, but they would also be happy for him. They said they had a special gift for him to take to camp. They gave him a backpack with his name on it and a drawing book and crayons inside. Sam looked at the backpack and the drawing book and crayons. He liked them very much. He thanked his mom and dad and smiled. He felt a little less nervous. He thought maybe camp could be fun after all. He decided to accept the surprise and try something new. The next day, his mom and dad drove him to camp. They helped him find his cabin and his counselor. They introduced him to some of the other kids. They said goodbye and told him to have a great time. Sam waved and hugged them. He felt a little sad, but also excited. He put his backpack on his bed and opened his drawing book. He saw a note from his mom and dad. It said: "We love you, Sam. You are a gifted boy. You can do anything you set your mind to. Have fun at camp and make lots of memories. We can't wait to see you and hear all about it. Love, Mom and Dad." Sam felt a warm feeling in his heart. He closed his drawing book and looked around. He saw the other kids playing, laughing and talking. He saw the trees, the lake and the sky. He felt a spark of curiosity and adventure. He thought maybe camp could be the best thing that ever happened to him. He decided to accept the challenge and make the most of it.

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn little boy named Tim. He loved to paint. One day, he found a big gray rock in his yard. Tim thought it would be fun to paint the rock. Tim tried to paint the rock, but the paint would not stick. He tried again and again, but the paint still would not stay on the gray rock. Tim was sad, but he did not give up. Tim asked his mom for help. She gave him a new kind of paint. Tim painted the gray rock one more time. This time, the paint stayed on the rock! Tim was very happy. He learned that if you keep trying, you can do anything.

One day, a little bird was cold. He wanted to find a warm place to stay. He flew around and saw a restaurant. The bird thought, "This looks like a nice place to get warm!" The bird flew to the door and tried to whistle. He wanted to let the people inside know he was there. But the door was closed, so the people did not hear him. The bird was sad, but he did not give up. He knew there must be another way to get inside. The bird flew around and saw a window. He whistled again, and this time a little girl saw him. She opened the window and let the bird in. The bird was so happy to be warm inside the restaurant. The little girl and the bird became friends, and they played together all day long.
