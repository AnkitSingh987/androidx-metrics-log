Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a nice cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with his ball. One day, Tom saw a big oven in the kitchen. He was very curious about it. Tom's friend, the dog named Max, came over. Max said, "Tom, don't go near the oven! It can be hot!" But Tom didn't listen. He wanted to see the oven up close. When Tom got close, the oven's door opened by itself. Tom was scared, but he was brave, so he went inside. Inside the oven, Tom found a magic button. It said "reverse". Tom pushed the button, and something unexpected happened! The oven turned into a big toy house. Tom and Max played in the toy house all day long. They were so happy and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the sunshine. One day, he saw something unusual in his yard. It was a big, shiny rock. Tim was very excited. Tim ran to his mom and said, "Mom, come see this big rock I found!" His mom came outside and knelt down beside him to look at the rock. "Wow, Tim, that is unusual!" she said. "I have never seen a rock like this before." Tim and his mom decided to take the rock inside to show his dad. They all sat around the rock, talking about how special it was. Tim felt very happy that he found something so unusual to share with his family. They all smiled and enjoyed the sunshine together.

Tim had a dog named Spot. Spot was a good dog. He liked to play and run and bark. Tim loved Spot very much. He gave him food and water and toys and hugs. One day, Tim and Spot went to the park. It was a hot day. The sun was very bright and warm. Tim and Spot saw a big pond. The pond had water and ducks and frogs. Tim wanted to swim in the pond. He said to Spot, "Come on, Spot, let's go in the water. It will be fun and cool." But Spot did not want to go in the water. He was afraid of the water. He did not like to swim. He said to Tim, "No, Tim, I don't want to go in the water. It is scary and wet. I want to stay here and watch the ducks and frogs." Tim did not listen to Spot. He said, "Don't be silly, Spot. The water is not scary or wet. It is nice and cool. You will like it. Come on, Spot, follow me." And he ran to the pond and jumped in. Spot did not follow Tim. He stayed on the land and watched Tim swim in the pond. He barked and wagged his tail. He wanted Tim to come back and play with him. But Tim did not come back. He stayed in the pond for a long time. He swam and splashed and laughed. He did not see the sign that said, "Danger: Do not swim in the pond. The water is very hot. It can melt you." The water was very hot. It was not nice and cool. It was from a factory that made things with fire. The water was so hot that it melted Tim. Tim melted in the pond. He became water too. He could not swim or splash or laugh anymore. He could not hear Spot or see Spot or hug Spot anymore. Spot saw Tim melt in the pond. He was very sad and scared. He cried and howled and ran away. He did not want to stay in the park anymore. He wanted to go home and be safe and warm. But Spot could not go home. He did not know the way. He was lost and alone. He wandered in the streets and looked for Tim. He did not find Tim. He only found cars and people and noise. He was hungry and thirsty and tired. He was a poor dog. The moral of the story is: Listen to your friends. They know what is good for you. Do not do things that are dangerous. They can hurt you. And always look before you leap. You never know what is in the water.
