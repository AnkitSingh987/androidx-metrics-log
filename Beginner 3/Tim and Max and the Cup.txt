Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Max and the Cup

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim and his friend, a dog named Max, wanted to reach for the win in a big race. They practiced running every day. They were very fast. One day, they saw a big, shiny cup. It was the prize for the race. But it was up high. Tim and Max tried to reach it, but they could not. A tall man said, "You can only have this cup if you win the race." The race day came. Tim and Max ran very fast. But there was a twist. A cat named Sam was also in the race. Sam was very fast too. Sam had a fake leg, but it did not slow him down. Tim, Max, and Sam ran and ran. At the end, Tim, Max, and Sam all reached the finish line at the same time. It was a big surprise! They all won the race together. The tall man gave them the big, shiny cup. They were all very happy.

Sara was a shy girl who liked to play with her dolls. She had a favorite doll named Lily, who had long yellow hair and a pink dress. Sara always took good care of Lily and brushed her hair every day. One day, Sara went to the park with her mom and Lily. She saw some other kids playing on the swings and slides, but she was too shy to join them. She sat on a bench and hugged Lily close to her. "Hello, what's your name?" a voice asked. Sara looked up and saw a boy with curly brown hair and a big smile. He was holding a toy car in his hand. "I'm Sara," she said softly. "I'm Ben. Do you want to play with me?" he asked. Sara nodded and smiled. She liked Ben. He seemed nice and friendly. They went to the sandbox and made some castles and roads. Ben let Sara use his car and Sara let Ben hold Lily. They had fun together. But then, something bad happened. A big dog came running to the sandbox and barked loudly. Sara and Ben got scared and ran away. Sara dropped Lily on the sand and Ben dropped his car. The dog saw the toys and thought they were fun. He grabbed Lily with his teeth and shook her hard. He tore her dress and pulled her hair. He also bit Ben's car and broke a wheel. Sara and Ben saw what the dog was doing and cried. They wanted their toys back. They loved their toys. "Mommy, help!" Sara shouted. "Mommy, help!" Ben shouted. Their moms heard them and came running. They saw the dog and the toys and got angry. They yelled at the dog and scared him away. They picked up the toys and gave them to Sara and Ben. "I'm sorry, Sara. Your doll is broken," Sara's mom said. "I'm sorry, Ben. Your car is broken," Ben's mom said. Sara and Ben looked at their toys and felt sad. They hugged their toys and each other. "Can you fix them, mommy?" Sara asked. "Can you fix them, mommy?" Ben asked. Their moms nodded and smiled. "Yes, we can. We can restore them. We can sew the dress and the hair. We can glue the wheel and the car. They will be good as new," they said. "Really?" Sara asked. "Really?" Ben asked. "Yes, really. But you have to be patient. It will take some time. Why don't you play with each other while we work?" they said. "Okay," Sara said. "Okay," Ben said. They wiped their tears and smiled. They went to the swings and slides and played together. They were not shy anymore. They were friends. The end.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends, Tom and Sarah. They loved to play games and explore together. One day, Sarah said to Tom, "Let's join shapes!" Tom was so excited. He quickly grabbed the shapes and started making some pictures. They both laughed as they joined the shapes together. Suddenly, the sky turned dark and gloomy. Tom rushed to Sarah and said, "we've got to go home! It looks like a storm is coming!" But Sarah didn't want to leave. She wanted to join more shapes and have more fun. Tom looked at her and said, "Let's make a promise. Tomorrow when it's sunny, we can join shapes again as much as we like." Sarah smiled and agreed. They packed up the shapes and ran back home. The next day, the sun was shining bright and Sarah and Tom joined shapes all day until the sun set. They had such a wonderful time together.
