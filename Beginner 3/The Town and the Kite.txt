Category: Beginner
Title: The Town and the Kite

Once upon a time, there was a small town. In the town, there were many nice people. There was also a police who helped everyone. The police had a big car with lights. The police liked to fix things. He was good at repair. One day, the police saw a harsh wind break a little girl's toy. The toy was a small kite. The little girl was very sad. The police wanted to help her. He thought he could repair her toy. The police tried to repair the kite. But, the wind was too strong. Suddenly, the wind took the kite high up in the sky. The little girl and the police looked up. They saw the kite change into a big, beautiful bird. It was a magic kite! The little girl and the police were very happy. They clapped and laughed. The magic bird flew away, and they never forgot that day.

Ben and Mia were playing with their blocks. They liked to build tall towers and pretend they were in a big city. Mia made a building with a red roof and a yellow door. Ben made a building with a blue roof and a green door. "Look at my building, Ben. It is so pretty," Mia said. "Look at my building, Mia. It is so strong," Ben said. They were having fun, but they did not see that their dog, Rex, was getting bored. Rex wanted to play too, but he did not know how to play with blocks. He was a careless dog. He ran around the room and knocked over the blocks with his tail. "Oh no, Rex! You ruined our buildings!" Mia cried. "Bad dog, Rex! Go away!" Ben shouted. Rex felt sad. He did not mean to be bad. He just wanted to have fun. He ran out of the room and hid under the bed. Mia and Ben started to pick up the blocks and build again. They were still angry at Rex. They did not notice that the window was open and a strong wind was blowing. The wind blew a paper bag into the room. The paper bag flew around and landed on the lamp. The lamp was hot and the paper bag caught fire. "Fire! Fire!" Mia screamed. "Help! Help!" Ben yelled. They were scared. They did not know what to do. They ran to the door, but it was locked. They forgot to unlock it when they came in. They were trapped in the room with the fire. Rex heard their screams. He came out from under the bed and saw the smoke and the flames. He was a brave dog. He ran to the door and scratched it with his paws. He barked loudly to get someone's attention. Mom and Dad heard Rex's barks. They ran to the room and saw the fire. They quickly unlocked the door and opened it. They grabbed Mia and Ben and carried them out of the room. They called the firemen to put out the fire. "Are you okay, Mia and Ben?" Mom asked. "Yes, Mom. We are okay," Mia and Ben said. They hugged Mom and Dad. They were glad they were safe. They looked at Rex. He was wagging his tail and licking their faces. He was a good dog. He rescued them from the fire. "Thank you, Rex. You are a hero," Mia and Ben said. They hugged Rex and gave him a treat. They were sorry they were mean to him. They learned to be more careful and to share their toys with him. They loved Rex very much.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Timmy. Timmy was playing with his toy car in the yard. He loved his toy car very much. One day, Timmy saw a big dog. The big dog took Timmy's toy car and ran away. Timmy was very sad. Timmy went to his daddy and said, "Daddy, the big dog took my toy car. Can you help me get it back?" Daddy said, "Don't worry, Timmy. I will return your toy car." So, Daddy and Timmy went to find the big dog. They looked and looked, but they could not find the big dog. It was getting dark, and they had to go home. Timmy was sad because he did not get his toy car back. Daddy said, "I'm sorry, Timmy. We will try again tomorrow." But the next day, they still could not find the big dog. Timmy never saw his little toy car again, and he was very sad.
