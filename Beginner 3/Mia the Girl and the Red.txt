Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Red

One day, a little girl named Mia found a red ball. She liked the red ball a lot. Mia played with the red ball all day. She threw it up and down, and she rolled it on the ground. Mia was very happy with her red ball. Mia's friend, Tom, came to play with her. Tom saw the red ball and said, "Wow, Mia! That's a big red ball! Can I play with it too?" Mia said, "Yes, Tom! Let's play together!" They played with the red ball, and they had lots of fun. But then, the red ball got stuck in a tree! Mia and Tom were sad. They couldn't reach the ball. Mia said, "Let's pray that the ball comes down." They closed their eyes and prayed. Suddenly, a bird flew by and hit the ball with its wing. The red ball fell down, and it landed on Mia's waist! Mia and Tom were so surprised and happy. They laughed and played with the red ball again.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park. She saw a pretty butterfly with big, bright wings. The butterfly looked troubled. It was stuck in a bush and could not fly away. Mia wanted to help the butterfly. Mia reached out her hand to the bush. She was careful not to hurt the butterfly. She used her fingers to snap the little branch that held the butterfly. The butterfly was free now. The butterfly flew around Mia, happy to be free. Mia felt happy too. They played together in the park until the sun went down. Mia and the butterfly became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a clever dog named Spot. Spot loved to play in the park every day. One day, Spot saw an eraser on the ground and he wanted to play with it. He put his foot on the eraser so he could pick it up but then it rolled away. Spot chased after it and finally he settled on it in the grass. Then, a little girl came over to Spot and said "Hello, Spot. Can I have the eraser?" Spot wagged his tail and then he settled it down in front of the girl. She smiled and said "Thank you, Spot! You're so clever!" Spot wanted some more fun, so he rolled around and chased his tail for a few minutes. The girl and Spot enjoyed playing together for the rest of the afternoon. Every time the eraser rolled away, Spot was able to settle it down in front of her. Finally when it was time to go home, the girl put the eraser in her pocket and said goodbye to Spot. He watched her walk away, happy and proud that he had been so clever.

One sunny day, a little boy named Tim went out to play. He saw his shadow on the ground and thought it was a new friend. "Hi, shadow! Want to play with me?" he asked. The shadow didn't talk, but it stayed with Tim everywhere he went. Tim liked his new friend because it was very persistent. Tim and his shadow played all day. They ran, jumped, and had lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The sky became dark, and the shadow was gone. Tim was sad and didn't know where his shadow went. "Where did you go, shadow? Please come back!" he cried. The next day, the sun came out again, and Tim's shadow was back. Tim was so happy to see his new friend again. He learned that the shadow would always be with him when the sun was shining. The moral of the story is to keep looking for the good things even when they seem to be gone.

Once upon a time, there was a blue bird named Bob. Bob lived in a big tree. One day, Bob felt sad and had a frown on his face. Bob's friend, Cat, saw Bob's frown. Cat wanted to help Bob feel better. Cat thought and thought. Then, Cat had an idea. Cat would give Bob a massage. Cat gave Bob a gentle massage. Bob's frown went away. Bob felt happy again. Bob and Cat played together all day. They were the best of friends.
