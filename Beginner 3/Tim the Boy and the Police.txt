Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Police

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim was a wild boy who liked to shout and play all day. He had many friends, but sometimes he would shout too much and scare them away. One day, while Tim was shouting and running around, he saw a police car. The police were there to help people and keep the town safe. Tim thought it would be fun to shout at the police, so he did. The police did not like this and told Tim to be quiet. Tim felt sad that the police were upset with him. He decided to be a good boy and not shout anymore. He learned that it is important to be respectful and not scare people with loud noises. From that day on, Tim was a kind and quiet boy, and everyone in the town loved him.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to learn about history. One day, her mom mentioned a big castle that was very, very old. Lucy wanted to visit the castle. She asked her mom, "Can we go see the castle? I want to learn more about history!" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, we can go. It will be a safe trip." They went to the castle and Lucy learned a lot about history. She felt happy and safe. Lucy and her mom had a great day at the castle. They went home and told everyone about their fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a little player named Tim. Tim loved to cook. Every day, he cooked with his mom in their small kitchen. One day, Tim wanted to cook a big meal for his friends. He asked his mom, "Can I cook a big meal for my friends, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful. Some things in the kitchen are fragile." Tim started to cook. He cooked a big pot of soup, some bread, and a yummy cake. He was very careful with the fragile things in the kitchen. He did not break anything. When his friends came over, they all sat at the table. They ate the soup, the bread, and the cake. Everyone was happy and full. Tim was proud of the meal he cooked. His friends said, "Thank you, Tim. You are the best cook!"

Tom likes to write. He has a big book with many pages. He writes stories about animals and cars and stars. He uses a blue pen and draws pictures too. One day, Tom goes to his dad's office. His dad works on a big computer. He types and talks on the phone. Tom wants to write on the computer too. He asks his dad, "Can I write on your computer, please?" His dad smiles and says, "Sure, Tom. But be careful. Don't touch anything else. And don't write too much. We have to go home soon." Tom sits on his dad's chair and looks at the keyboard. He sees many letters and numbers. He tries to write his name. He presses T and O and M. He sees his name on the screen. He is happy. He writes more words. He writes cat and dog and sun. He writes I love you dad. His dad finishes his work and looks at Tom's writing. He is proud of Tom. He says, "You are a good writer, Tom. You wrote many words. And you wrote I love you dad. That is very sweet. I love you too, Tom." He hugs Tom and gives him a kiss. He says, "Let's go home now. Mom made a tasty cake for us. Do you want some cake, Tom?" Tom nods and says, "Yes, please. I like cake. Cake is tasty. Can I write on your computer again, dad?" His dad laughs and says, "Maybe another day, Tom. But now, let's go and eat some cake."

Once upon a time, there was a little, hairy dog named Max. Max loved to collect sticks in the park. One day, Max saw a big, red ball with a handle. He wanted to play with it. Max said to his friend, the cat, "Look at the red ball with the handle! Let's play with it!" The cat replied, "Yes, Max! It will be fun!" So Max and the cat played with the big, red ball all day long. At the end of the day, Max and the cat were tired but happy. Max said, "Today was a good day. I collected sticks and played with the red ball. I like the handle on the ball. It was easy to hold." The cat agreed, "Yes, Max. We had a lot of fun. Let's play again tomorrow!" And Max and the cat went home to rest, excited for another day of play.
