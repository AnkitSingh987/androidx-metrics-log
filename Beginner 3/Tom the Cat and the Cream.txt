Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Cat and the Cream

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Tom. Tom loved to eat cream. One day, Tom saw a big bowl of cream on the table. It was so easy for him to jump up and take a sip. But he heard his mom say, "Tom, do not eat that cream. It is not for you." Tom wanted the cream so much, but he listened to his mom. He played with his toys and forgot about the cream. Later, his mom went out to buy some food. Tom thought it was his chance to eat the cream. He jumped on the table and began to eat the cream. It was so yummy! As Tom ate the cream, he started to become very big. He was scared and did not know what to do. His mom came home and saw him. She said, "Tom, I told you not to eat the cream. It was magic cream to help you become a big cat when you are older." Tom was sad, but his mom gave him a hug. She said, "It is okay. We will find a way to make you small again." And they did. Tom learned to listen to his mom and not eat things that were not for him.

Once upon a time, there was a little red train named Toby. Toby had a strong engine. He loved to go fast on the tracks. One day, Toby saw his friend, a big blue train named Billy. "Hi Billy!" said Toby. "Why do you look sad?" Billy shrugged. "My engine is not working, and I have no one to help me," he said. Toby wanted to help his friend. He thought and thought. Then, he had an idea. "I will find someone to fix your engine!" Toby said. Toby went to look for help. He found a kind man named Tom. Tom was good at fixing things. Toby told Tom about Billy's engine. Tom agreed to help. They went to Billy with a big bag of tools. Tom fixed Billy's engine. Billy was very happy. They all went to the train station to celebrate. Toby and Billy raced down the tracks, their engines roaring. The station was empty, but they didn't care. They were happy to be together again.

Once upon a time, there was a little mosquito. His name was Dull. Dull was not like other mosquitoes. He was not good at flying and he did not like to bite people. One day, Dull met a big bug. The big bug said, "Hi, my name is Big Bug. Do you want to be friends?" Dull was happy and said, "Yes, I want to be friends with you!" They played together all day. But one day, something bad happened. Dull and Big Bug were playing near the water. Dull fell into the water and could not fly out. Big Bug tried to help, but he was too big and could not reach Dull. In the end, Dull could not get out of the water and he stayed there forever. Big Bug was very sad and missed his friend Dull.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily lived in a small house with her mom and dad. One day, Lily saw a lot of dust on the floor. She wanted to help her mom and clean it up. Lily's mom said, "No, Lily, it's too hard for you. Let me do it." But Lily did not agree. She wanted to show that she could do it too. Lily found a broom and started to sweep the dust. As she swept, she found a small, sour candy under the dust. Lily thought it was a treat for doing a good job. She picked it up and showed it to her mom. Her mom said, "Lily, that candy is old and dirty. You should not eat it." But Lily disagreed and ate the candy anyway. After eating the candy, Lily's tummy began to hurt. She started to cry and her mom knew what happened. Her mom said, "Lily, you should have listened to me. Now you don't feel good." Lily felt sad and wished she had agreed with her mom. The moral of the story is to always listen to your parents because they know what is best for you.
