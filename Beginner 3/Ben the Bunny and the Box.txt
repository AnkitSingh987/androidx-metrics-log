Category: Beginner
Title: Ben the Bunny and the Box

Once upon a time, in a humble little village, there lived a kind bunny named Ben. Ben had many friends who liked to play with him. One day, a new friend arrived in the village. His name was Max, and he was a tiny mouse. Ben and Max became good friends. They played hide and seek, and they laughed a lot. One sunny day, Ben and Max went to the park to play. They saw a big box and decided to play inside it. Max said, "This is fun, let's hide here!" While they were playing, the box closed, and they couldn't get out. Ben and Max tried to find a way to escape. They pushed and pushed, but the box wouldn't open. Then, they heard their friend, Tom the turtle, walking by. Ben called out, "Help, Tom! We are stuck in the box!" Tom heard them and slowly walked over to the box. He used his strong shell to push the box open. Ben and Max were free at last! They thanked Tom for his help and gave him a big hug. From that day on, the three friends played together and had lots of fun in their humble village.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful dog named Bow. Bow loved to play with his board. One day, he took his board to the park to play with his friend, Cat. Bow said to Cat, "Look at my board! We can play together!" Cat looked at the board and was very happy. They took turns sliding the board down the hill and laughing. After playing, Bow and Cat sat under a big tree. They were tired but had so much fun. Bow looked at his friend and said, "I am glad we played with my board today." Cat smiled and said, "Me too, Bow. Let's play again tomorrow!" Bow wagged his tail, and they both went home.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his dad. Tim saw a big, rough rock that looked like a horn. He wanted to ride on it. Tim said, "Dad, can I ride the horn rock?" His dad said, "Yes, but be careful, Tim." Tim climbed up the rock and started to ride it like a horse. He was having so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The horn rock started to move! The rock was not a rock at all. It was a big, friendly animal. The animal said, "Hello, Tim. My name is Rocky. I like it when you ride on my back." Tim was very surprised. He said, "Hi, Rocky. I am Tim. Can we be friends?" Rocky said, "Yes, we can be friends." So Tim and Rocky played together in the park all day.

Once upon a time there was a boy called Jack. Jack was three years old and he loved to polish things. One day, Jack wanted to polish a magazine. He grabbed it and started rubbing it with a cloth. His mom, who was watching him, was happy. She said: "That's so kind of you, Jack. You're a very helpful boy". Jack smiled and kept polishing the magazine. When he was done, the magazine looked shiny and new. He looked up to his mom and said: "Look, mom. I polished the magazine." His mom said: "That's wonderful. Let's take it out and show it to everyone." Jack smiled and said: "Yay! Let's go!" So Jack and his mom went out with the nicely polished magazine. Everyone was amazed at how good it looked. Jack was so proud and happy. Jack's mom said to him with a smile: "You did a great job, Jack. You are so kind to help out like that." Jack smiled and said: "It was nothing. I like polishing things."

One day, a little red bird was looking for dinner. He saw a big tree with red berries. He flew to the tree and started eating the berries. The red bird was very happy. A big cat saw the red bird eating the berries. The cat wanted to eat the red bird for dinner. The cat jumped up the tree and said, "Red bird, red bird, come down and play with me." The red bird looked at the cat and said, "No, thank you. I am eating my dinner." The cat did not like that the red bird did not want to play. The cat went down the tree and thought of a plan. The cat found a red berry on the ground and started to repeat, "Yummy red berries, yummy red berries." The red bird heard the cat and wanted more berries. The red bird flew down to the ground to get more berries. The big cat was waiting and caught the red bird. The red bird could not fly away. The cat ate the red bird for dinner. The red bird should have stayed in the tree.
