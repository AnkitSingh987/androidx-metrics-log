Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and the Tree

Once upon a time, there were twin cats named Tom and Tim. They lived in a small house with their mom. They liked to play, but sometimes they felt bored. One day, Tom and Tim were very bored. They decided to wander outside to find something fun to do. Their mom told them not to go far, but they didn't listen. They wandered far away from their house, looking for adventure. As they wandered, they saw a big tree. They thought it would be fun to climb. But when they got to the top, they couldn't get down. They were scared and cried for help. Their mom heard them and came running, but she couldn't reach them either. In the end, a kind man had to help them down. The twins learned that wandering too far can lead to a bad ending.

One day, a boy named Tom found a big, plastic toy in his room. It was an original toy, not like the others. It was red and had four round wheels. Tom wanted to test the toy, so he took it outside. Tom met his friend Sue. "Look at my new toy," Tom said. "It is big and red. I want to test it." Sue was happy to help. They pushed the toy on the ground and watched it go fast. The toy was fun to play with. After they played, Tom and Sue sat under a tree. "I like your toy," Sue said. "It is very original." Tom smiled and said, "Yes, it is. Let's play with it again tomorrow." And they did, having fun with the big, red, plastic toy every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big yard. The yard was very organized. In the yard, there were many toys and games. The children loved to play in the yard. One day, a little girl named Lily asked her mom if she could play in the yard. Her mom said, "Yes, but don't go too far away." Lily was happy and went to play. She played with her ball and her dolls. She had lots of fun. But then, Lily saw a big tree. She wanted to climb the tree. She called out, "Mom, can I climb the tree?" But her mom did not answer. Lily thought it was okay to climb. She went up, up, up the tree. But then, she fell down. She was hurt and cried. The fun day in the yard had a bad ending.

Once upon a time there was a pirate called Bill. Bill was very brave, but he was also a bit rough at times. He loved sailing the seas and looking for adventure. One day as he was sailing, he heard a loud call. "Help me!" it yelled. Bill quickly looked around to see where the call was coming from. He soon spotted a small stage floating on the waves. It was filled with sea creatures, who were stranded and scared. Bill quickly steered his boat to the stage and lifted the creatures on to his deck. He then looked at them with his kind eyes and said "My friends, everyone should help each other and never be too rough. That's how to be a good pirate". The sea creatures thanked Bill and they all remained friends forever. From that day on, Bill was known as a kind and brave pirate by the seas.

One day, a little bird found a weird egg in her nest. She did not recognize it. "Whose egg is this?" she asked her friends. They did not know. A big bird came and said, "That is my egg!" The little bird was scared. She did not want to give the weird egg to the big bird. The big bird was angry and they started to fight. Then, the weird egg started to shake. It cracked open and a baby bird came out. The baby bird was not a little bird or a big bird. It was a different bird! The little bird and the big bird stopped fighting. They were happy to see the new baby bird and they all became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot liked to walk a lot. He had an original collar that his owner, Sam, gave him. The collar was blue and had a little bell on it. One day, Spot and Sam went for a walk in the park. They played with a ball and ran in the grass. Spot liked to hear the bell on his collar jingle when he ran. They were having lots of fun. But then, Spot saw a squirrel and wanted to chase it. He ran very fast and his collar fell off. Spot was sad because he lost his original collar. Sam looked for it, but they could not find it. They went home with a bad ending to their day.
