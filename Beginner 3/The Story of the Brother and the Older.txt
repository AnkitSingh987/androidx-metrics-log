Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Brother and the Older

Once upon a time, there were two brothers. The older brother was selfish and always took what he wanted. One day, he saw his brother's radio on the table and wanted to play with it. He asked his brother if he could have it, but his brother said no. He was so angry that he picked up the radio and ran away. The younger brother was sad and started to cry. The older brother went to his room and turned on the radio. He started to sing and dance. But then, the radio stopped working and the older brother got angry. He screamed and yelled and said he hated the radio. The younger brother was still sad in his room. He heard his brother screaming and hit the walls. He was scared and felt very lonely. He decided to go and talk to his brother and make peace. When the younger brother arrived in the older brother's room, he asked what was wrong. The older brother said that he was too selfish and shouldn't have taken the radio away from his brother. He realised he had made a huge mistake. The older brother felt so bad that he packed his things and left the house. The younger brother was left alone and was so sad. He sat in his room and thought his brother would return, but he never did. Sadly, the two brothers were never reunited again. The younger brother was left alone and learned to be less selfish.

Once upon a time, there was a farmer. He had a big farm with many animals. One day, he saw a delicate bird in his barn. The bird was sad and could not fly. The farmer said, "Little bird, why are you sad?" The bird replied, "I have a thorn in my wing. It hurts so much, and I can't fly." The kind farmer wanted to help the bird. He said, "Let me remove the thorn for you." The farmer took out the thorn very carefully. The bird was happy and could fly again. The bird said, "Thank you, farmer!" The farmer smiled and waved goodbye as the bird flew away. From that day on, the bird always came back to visit the farmer and sing a happy song.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big hole in the ground. They want to know what is inside. "Let's go and look," says Tom. "OK, but be careful," says Lily. They go near the hole and peer inside. It is very dark and deep. They hear a strange sound. It sounds like a growl. "What is that?" asks Lily. "I don't know," says Tom. "Maybe it is a monster." They are scared. They want to run away. But then they hear another sound. It sounds like a voice. "Help, help!" the voice says. "I am stuck in here. Please get me out." Lily and Tom are surprised. They look at each other. "Who are you?" they shout. "I am Ben," the voice says. "I was looking for butter. I like butter. But I fell into the hole. Now I can't get out." Lily and Tom feel sorry for Ben. They want to help him. "Where is the butter?" they ask. "It is in my backpack," Ben says. "It is near the hole. Can you see it?" Lily and Tom look around. They see a backpack. It is blue and has a picture of a star. They go and get it. "Is this yours?" they ask. "Yes, yes, thank you," Ben says. "Can you throw it down to me? I am hungry." Lily and Tom throw the backpack into the hole. They hear a thud. "Did you catch it?" they ask. "Yes, I did," Ben says. "Thank you, thank you. You are very kind. Now can you find a rope or a ladder? I need to get out of here." Lily and Tom nod. They look for a rope or a ladder. They see a rope. It is hanging from a tree. They pull it down. They tie it to a branch. They throw the other end into the hole. "Can you reach it?" they ask. "Yes, I can," Ben says. "Thank you, thank you. You are very smart. Now I can climb out of here." Lily and Tom watch as Ben climbs out of the hole. He is a boy. He is about their age. He has brown hair and green eyes. He is holding his backpack. He smiles at them. "Hello, I am Ben," he says. "You are Lily and Tom, right?" "Yes, we are," they say. "Nice to meet you," Ben says. "You are very brave and nice. You saved me. Can I be your friend?" Lily and Tom smile back. They like Ben. He is funny and friendly. They nod. "Of course you can," they say. "Thank you, thank you," Ben says. "You are the best. Can I share my butter with you? I have enough for all of us." Lily and Tom agree. They like butter too. They sit down on the grass. They open the backpack. They take out some bread and butter. They eat and talk. They laugh and have fun. They are happy. They are friends.
