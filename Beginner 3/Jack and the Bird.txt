Category: Beginner
Title: Jack and the Bird

One day Jack, who was three years old, was playing in his backyard. He noticed a flock of birds in the sky and they were so adorable! They all had different colors, but Jack's favorite was the blue one. He yelled, "Look up! The blue bird is so cute!" His mom looked up and said, "Yes, that is one beautiful bird." Jack then asked his mom to include him in the fun. "Mommy, can I join the birds in the sky?" His mom smiled and said, "Of course, honey. Just imagine that you have wings, and you can fly up with the birds." So, Jack imagined that he had wings, and he flew up into the sky with the blue bird. He loved flying with the birds and he was so happy. Jack and the blue bird stayed in the sky for a long time, and then flew back down. Jack waved goodbye to the bird and thanked his mom for including him in the fun.

One day, a boy named Tim had a new jacket. He was very proud of it. The jacket was red and warm. He wanted to show it to his friend, Sue. So, he put it on and went to her house. When he got to Sue's house, he called, "Sue, come see my new jacket!" Sue came out and said, "Wow, Tim! That's a nice jacket!" They played outside and had lots of fun. As they played, a small dog came running up to them. The dog jumped and bit Tim's jacket. Tim was sad and Sue was surprised. But then, the dog pulled out a small toy from the hole it made. Tim and Sue laughed and played with the dog and the toy. Now, Tim's jacket had a story to tell.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and run all day. One day, Spot saw a big hill. He wanted to go up the hill, but it looked tough. Spot was not scared. He wanted to try. Spot started to run up the hill. He tried one way, but it was too steep. He tried another way, but it was too rocky. Spot did not give up. He knew there must be a way to get up the hill. Finally, Spot found a way that was not too steep or too rocky. He tried very hard and ran all the way up the hill. When Spot got to the top, he was very happy. He had tried and found a way, even though it was tough. Spot played on the hill all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big, furry dog named Toby. Toby loved to play with his ball all day. One day, while playing, Toby lost his ball in the tall grass. Toby looked and looked for his ball. He felt sad and regret for not watching where it went. As he searched, he found a big hole. Toby thought his ball might be in the hole, so he put his body inside to look. But it was not his ball in the hole! It was a small, scared cat named Luna. Toby helped Luna out of the hole. Luna was so happy, she gave Toby a new ball. Now Toby and Luna are best friends who play together every day.

One day, a little puppy went for a walk. He saw a big tree and wanted to play near it. He saw a long string on the ground. The puppy thought it would be fun to play with the string. The puppy picked up the string in his mouth and began to run around the tree. As he ran, the string began to tie around the tree very tight. The puppy didn't notice and kept running. Soon, the string was so tight that the puppy couldn't move anymore. He started to cry. A little boy heard the puppy's cry and came to see what was wrong. "Don't cry, puppy! I will help you," said the boy. The boy carefully untied the tight string from the tree and the puppy was free. The puppy wagged his tail and licked the boy's face to say thank you. From that day on, the boy and the puppy were the best of friends, and the puppy never played with strings again.
