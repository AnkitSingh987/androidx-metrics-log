Title: Sue the Helpful Girl and the Blanket

One day, a helpful girl named Sue went to her friend Tom's house. Tom was not feeling well. Sue wanted to help Tom feel better. She put a thermometer in Tom's mouth to check if he was hot. The thermometer showed that Tom was hot. Sue knew that Tom needed to rest. Sue thought of a plan to help Tom. She went to her house and got a big, soft blanket. Sue put the blanket on Tom to keep him warm. She also got some cold water for Tom to drink. Tom started to feel a little better. After a while, Tom felt much better. He thanked Sue for being so helpful. Sue was happy that she could help her friend. The moral of the story is to always help your friends when they need you.

One day, Peter was walking in the grass and he saw something funny. He saw a knot. It was big and round. Peter was very excited and tried to pick it up with his hands, but it was too slippery. He tried to pick it up with his foot, but he was too clumsy and he dropped it. Suddenly he heard a voice. His mom said, "Peter, what are you doing?". Peter said, "I'm trying to pick up that knot". His mom smiled and said, "Let me help you". She walked over and showed Peter how to pick up the knot with both hands. Peter was so happy that he droped the knot in the middle of the grass and ran off to play.

Once upon a time, there was a big hedge. A small cat and a big dog lived on each side of the hedge. They both liked to step and play, but they did not fit through the hedge to play together. The cat was sad and the dog was sad too. One day, the cat found a hole in the hedge. The hole was small, but the cat could fit. The cat was so happy and thought, "Now I can play with the dog!" The dog saw the cat and they both started to step and play. But then, the big dog tried to fit through the hole too. The dog got stuck in the hedge. The cat tried to help, but the dog was too big. The dog was sad and the cat was sad too. The dog could not get out and stayed stuck in the hedge.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree with many yummy peaches. The tree had the best peaches available for everyone. The peaches were big and sweet. Everyone loved to eat them. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the tree. He wanted to get a peach. But, he saw a little bird sleeping near the peaches. Tim did not want to disturb the bird. He waited for the bird to wake up. After a while, the bird woke up and flew away. Tim picked a peach and shared it with his friends. They all enjoyed the yummy peach and were happy. They promised not to disturb the sleeping birds again.

One day, a big rabbit named Bob wanted to test a new food. He heard about a thing called "salad" from his friends. He thought it would make him strong and fast. So, he went to find some salad to eat. He was very happy to try it. Bob found some salad under a big tree. But there was an angry bird named Tim in the tree. Tim did not want Bob to eat the salad. The bird said, "This is my salad! You can't have it!" Bob was sad, but he really wanted to try the salad. So, he thought of a plan. Bob told Tim, "I will race you to the other side of the forest. If I win, I can eat the salad. If you win, you can keep the salad." Tim agreed to the race. They ran and ran, but Bob was much faster than Tim. Just as Bob was about to win, he saw another big salad on the ground. He stopped to eat it, and Tim won the race. Bob was happy because he got to try the salad, and Tim was happy because he got to keep his salad. In the end, both of them were happy and became good friends.
