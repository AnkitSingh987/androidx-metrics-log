Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Pond

Lily loved to feed the ducks at the pond. She would bring bread and toss it to them. The ducks would quack and swim to get the bread. Lily liked to watch them and hear them. One day, a man came to the pond. He wore a black robe and a white wig. He was a judge. He did not like the ducks. He said they were noisy and dirty. He said Lily should not feed them. He said it was bad for them and the pond. Lily was scared of the judge. He was big and loud. He looked angry. He took the bread from Lily and threw it in the trash. He told Lily to go away and never come back. He said he would call the police if she did. Lily ran away, crying. She felt ashamed. She thought she did something wrong. She thought the judge hated her. She thought the ducks hated her too. She did not want to feed them anymore. But the ducks did not hate Lily. They missed her. They liked her bread and her smile. They did not care about the judge. They thought he was mean and silly. They wished Lily would come back and feed them again. They quacked and waited for her.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a charming mill. The mill was near a big hill. The mill made flour from wheat. People from the town would come to the mill to get flour to make bread. One day, a kind man named Tom went to the mill. Tom wanted to order flour for his bakery. He met the miller, who was a nice lady. Tom said, "Hello! I want to order flour for my bakery. Can you help me?" The miller said, "Yes, I can help you. I will make the flour for you." Tom was very happy. He waited while the miller made the flour. When the flour was ready, Tom took it to his bakery. He made yummy bread with the flour from the charming mill. Everyone in the town loved Tom's bread. And they all loved the charming mill, too.

Once upon a time, there was a small, tidy house. In this house lived a little girl named Lily and her mom. Lily loved to eat cereal for breakfast every day. Her mom would always make sure the kitchen was tidy before they sat down to eat. One day, Lily wanted to help her mom in the kitchen. She saw the cereal box on the table and decided to squeeze it. The cereal box made a funny noise and Lily laughed. Her mom smiled and said, "Thank you for helping, Lily. But we don't need to squeeze the cereal box. Let's pour some cereal into a bowl instead." Lily listened to her mom and poured the cereal into a bowl. She was careful not to make a mess. After they finished eating, Lily helped her mom make the kitchen tidy again. They put away the cereal and washed their bowls. Lily felt happy and proud to help her mom keep their house tidy.

One day, a boy named Tim and a girl named Sue went to the park to play a game. They saw a big box with a long rope. The box was empty. They thought it would be fun to play a game of pull with the rope. Tim said, "Let's play a game. You pull one end of the rope and I will pull the other end. The one who pulls the most wins!" Sue agreed and they started to play the game. They pulled and pulled, but the rope did not move. Suddenly, a big dog came and started to pull the rope too. Tim and Sue were surprised. The dog was very strong and they could not win the game. The dog said, "I am sorry, I just wanted to play too. Can we all play the game together?" Tim and Sue smiled and said, "Yes, let's all play together!" They played the game with the dog and had lots of fun. In the end, they all became good friends.

One day, a charming little girl named Sue saw a fly. The fly was on her window. Sue wanted to help the fly go outside. She opened the window and said, "Go, fly! You can go out now." The fly did not go out. Instead, it started to talk! "Thank you for trying to help me," said the fly. Sue was very surprised. She did not know flies could talk. The fly told Sue that it was a magic fly. It could do something special for her. Sue thought for a moment. She asked the magic fly to make her toys come to life. The fly agreed and with a buzz, all her toys started to move! Sue was so happy. She played with her toys all day long. The magic fly stayed with Sue and they became the best of friends.
