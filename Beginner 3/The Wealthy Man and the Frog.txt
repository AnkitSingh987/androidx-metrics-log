Category: Beginner
Title: The Wealthy Man and the Frog

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man who had a big lawn. He loved his lawn very much. One day, he saw that his lawn was not green anymore. It was turning yellow. He felt sad. He asked his friend, "What can I do to make my lawn green again?" His friend said, "You need to give it water. It is suffering because it is thirsty." The wealthy man started to give water to his lawn every day. But one day, when he was giving water to his lawn, something unexpected happened. A big, green frog jumped out of the grass! The frog said, "Thank you for giving water to my home. I will help you make your lawn green again." The wealthy man was very surprised to see the talking frog. But he was also happy because his lawn would be green again. And from that day, the wealthy man, the frog, and the lawn all lived happily ever after.

Lila and Ben were friends. They liked to play with ropes and make knots. They learned how to make different knots from a book. They made loops, twists, and ties. They had fun with ropes and knots. One day, they found a big rope in the park. It was long and thick. They wanted to play with it. They took it to a tree and tied it to a branch. They made a swing with the rope. They took turns to swing on the rope. They laughed and shouted. But then, a boy came to the park. He was rude. He saw the rope and wanted it. He did not ask Lila and Ben. He just ran to the rope and pulled it. He broke the knot that Lila and Ben made. He took the rope and ran away. Lila and Ben were sad and angry. They tried to chase the boy, but he was too fast. They lost the rope and the swing. They could not play with ropes and knots anymore. They cried and went home. They were not friends with the rude boy. He was mean. He made them unhappy. The end.

One day, a colorful bug named Zip went for a walk. Zip liked to look at pretty things. He saw a big drain and went to look at it. The drain was very big and dark. "Hello!" said Zip to the drain. "You are very big and dark. I am Zip, a colorful bug." But the drain did not say anything. Zip was sad. He wanted to make a new friend. Then, something unexpected happened. A big, wet raindrop fell from the sky and hit Zip. He fell into the drain! But inside the drain, he found a lot of colorful bugs just like him. They all said, "Hi, Zip! We are your new friends!" Zip was happy and not sad anymore.

Tim and Lily were twins. They liked to play with their toys and watch cartoons. But they did not like to share. They always fought over who got the best toy or the biggest piece of cake. One day, their mom came home with a big package. She said it was a surprise for them. Tim and Lily were excited. They hoped it was something fun, like a new game or a doll. Mom opened the package and took out a weird thing. It was brown and furry and had four legs and a tail. It made a loud noise. "Woof, woof!" it said. Tim and Lily looked at it and screamed. They hated dogs. They were scared of them. They thought they were mean and dirty and smelly. "Mom, why did you get a dog?" Tim asked. "We don't want a dog!" "Yeah, mom, take it back!" Lily said. "We want a different surprise!" Mom smiled and hugged the dog. She said it was a gift from their aunt. She said the dog was very nice and friendly and needed a home. She said they could name it and play with it and love it. Tim and Lily frowned. They did not want to name it or play with it or love it. They wanted mom to get rid of it. They did not like the surprise at all. They thought it was the worst surprise ever.
