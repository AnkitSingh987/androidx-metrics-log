Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Drawing

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a white card with a big heart on it. Lily wanted to give the card to her mom for her birthday. But she needed help to make it pretty. Lily asked her big brother Tom to help her. Tom was good at drawing. He drew a nice flower on the card. Lily was happy, but she wanted more colors on the card. They had a small fight because Tom did not want to add more colors. Then, Lily's dad came and saw them fighting. He wanted to help too. Dad found some colorful pens and gave them to Lily and Tom. They both said sorry and started to color the card together. In the end, the white card was very pretty and full of colors. Mom was so happy when she got the card. Lily and Tom learned that helping each other is the best way to make things nice.

One morning, a big cat and a little dog were playing in the park. The sun was shining, and the grass was dry. They were having lots of fun running and jumping together. "Let's remove our shoes and run in the grass," said the big cat. The little dog agreed, and they both took off their shoes. They ran and played some more, laughing and having a great time. Soon, it was time to go home. The big cat and the little dog looked for their shoes, but they were gone! They searched the park, but they could not find them. They knew they should have kept their shoes on or put them somewhere safe. The big cat and the little dog sadly walked home with no shoes on their feet. Their feet hurt from stepping on rocks and sticks. They learned a lesson that day: always take care of your things and don't leave them behind.

Lily was a cat with a long tail. She liked to dance with her tail. She would swing it from side to side, and curl it up and down. She felt happy when she danced with her tail. One day, she saw a bird on a branch. The bird had a long tail too. It was red and yellow and blue. It was very pretty. Lily wanted to dance with the bird. She said, "Hello, bird. You have a long tail like me. Do you want to dance with me?" The bird looked at Lily and said, "No, thank you, cat. I do not want to dance with you. You are a cat and I am a bird. You might hurt me with your claws and teeth. I do not trust you." Lily felt sad. She said, "But I do not want to hurt you. I just want to dance with you. Your tail is so pretty. Please, bird, please dance with me." The bird said, "No, no, no. Go away, cat. Leave me alone. I do not want to dance with you. I want to fly away." And the bird flew away to another tree. Lily felt very sad. She went back to her home. She curled up on her bed. She did not want to dance with her tail anymore. She wished she had a friend who would dance with her.

Once upon a time, there was a big race. Many animals came to run. There was a fast dog, a slow turtle, and a happy rabbit. The sun was hot that day, so it was very warm. Before the race, a wise old owl warned the animals. "It is hot today. Be careful when you run," said the wise old owl. The animals listened to the owl and nodded their heads. The race started, and the animals ran fast. The dog was very fast, but he remembered the owl's warning. He slowed down a little, so he didn't get too hot. The turtle and the rabbit ran too, but they also listened to the owl. In the end, the dog won the race. All the animals were happy because they listened to the owl's warning. They didn't get too hot, and they had a great day at the race.
