Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Sam and the Hat

Ben and Sam are friends. They like to play outside in the snow. They wear warm coats and hats and gloves. They make a big snowman with a carrot nose and a scarf. "Look at our snowman!" Ben says. "He is very big and nice." "Yes, he is!" Sam says. "But he needs something more. He needs a hat!" Sam runs to his house and comes back with a hat. It is his dad's hat. It is brown and has a string to tie under the chin. "Here, let's put this hat on the snowman!" Sam says. He climbs on Ben's shoulders and puts the hat on the snowman's head. He ties the string under the snowman's chin. "There, now he is perfect!" Sam says. But then they hear a voice. It is Sam's dad. He is angry. "Sam, where is my hat?" he shouts. "I need it to go to work!" Sam and Ben look at each other. They are scared. Sam's dad is deaf. He cannot hear them say sorry. "Uh-oh," Ben says. "We have to give the hat back." They run to the snowman and try to take the hat off. But the string is too tight. They pull and pull, but the hat does not come off. "Help!" Sam says. "We can't get the hat off!" Sam's dad comes closer. He sees the snowman with his hat. He is very mad. "Sam, what have you done?" he says. "You took my hat and put it on a snowman? How could you?" He reaches for the hat and yanks it off. The snowman's head falls off too. The snowman is broken. Sam and Ben are sad. They did not mean to break the snowman. They did not mean to make Sam's dad mad. They look at Sam's dad. They hope he will not be too mad. They say in a small voice, "We are sorry, dad. We just wanted to make the snowman happy."

Once upon a time, there were two little bunnies. They were very excited to go on an adventure to the lab. On their way, they started to tremble as they were so scared. All of a sudden, they saw a mean wolf. He was scaring them and laughing at them. The bunnies started to feel ashamed. They stayed still and quiet, hoping the wolf wouldn't see them. After a few moments, the wolf went away. The bunnies were so happy and continued to the lab. Finally, they arrived at the lab safely. They both gave a big sigh of relief and smiled to each other. The bunnies explored the lab and had lots of fun. It was the best adventure ever!

Once upon a time, there was a little bird who loved to sing. Every day, the bird would sit on a tree and sing happy songs. The bird had colorful skin that made it look very pretty. Everyone in the forest liked the bird's songs. One day, a crazy cat heard the bird singing. The cat wanted to catch the bird and make it stop singing. The cat tried to climb the tree, but it was too hard. So, the cat thought of a plan. The cat pretended to be nice and asked the bird to come down. The bird believed the cat and came down from the tree. The crazy cat caught the bird and took it away. The bird could not sing anymore. The forest was sad and quiet without the bird's songs. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big, soft armchair in the middle of the room. It was a very interesting armchair because it could talk. One day, a little girl named Lily came into the room and saw the armchair. She was very surprised that it could talk. The armchair said, "Hello, Lily! My name is Mr. Chair. I can help you learn new things." Lily was very happy and sat down on Mr. Chair. They started to learn about colors, animals, and shapes together. Lily liked learning with Mr. Chair because he made it fun. Every day, Lily and Mr. Chair would learn something new. They became good friends and had many interesting adventures together. And they all lived happily ever after.
