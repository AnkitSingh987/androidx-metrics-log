Category: Beginner
Title: The Lively Bird and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a lively bird who loved to soar in the sky. One day, the bird saw a big pile of sand near the sea. It looked like a fun place to play. The bird flew down and met a crab who lived in the sand. "Hi, crab! Can I play with you on the sand?" asked the bird. The crab said, "Yes, but be careful not to go too close to the water." The bird was having so much fun playing with the crab and didn't listen. It went too close to the water, and a big wave came and took the bird away. The crab tried to help, but it was too late. The bird was gone, and the crab was very sad. The moral of the story is always to listen to your friends when they give you good advice.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree. In the tree, there was a cable. The cable was very long. It was hanging on a branch. A little boy named Tim saw it. He thought it was disgusting. One day, Tim and his friend, Sam, wanted to play. They went to the tree. They thought the cable was a snake. They were scared. They did not want to play near the tree. But then, a bird came. The bird sat on the cable. It was not a snake! It was just a cable. Tim and Sam laughed. They were not scared anymore. They played near the tree all day.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went for a walk with her mom. They saw a big rainbow in the sky. Lucy felt happy and clapped her hands. "Mom, look! A rainbow!" she said. Her mom smiled and nodded. "Yes, Lucy, it's beautiful," she replied. As they walked, the sun was very hot. Lucy started to feel uncomfortable. She told her mom, "I'm hot and tired, Mom." Her mom nodded and said, "Let's find some shade and rest for a bit." They found a big tree and sat under it. The cool shade made Lucy feel better. After resting, Lucy and her mom continued their walk. They saw many pretty flowers and birds. Lucy even found a small rock that she liked. When they got home, Lucy showed her dad the rock and told him about the rainbow. Everyone was happy, and they all had a good day.

Once upon a time, there was a huge king. He had a big crown on his head. He loved to match his clothes with his crown. Every day, he wore something new and shiny. One day, a little bird came to the king's window. The bird saw the king's crown and wanted to play with it. The bird took the crown and flew away. The king was sad and did not know what to do. The next day, the bird came back with the crown. The king was very happy! But when he looked at the crown, he saw that it was not his. It was a crown made of leaves and flowers. The king liked the new crown more than his old one. He wore the new crown and matched his clothes with it. The king and the bird became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a small pine tree. It was hot outside, and the little pine tree was thirsty. The little pine tree had a friend, a kind squirrel. The squirrel liked to help the pine tree when it was hot. One day, the squirrel said to the pine tree, "I will find a way to help you when it is hot." The pine tree was happy to hear that. The squirrel went away to look for something that could help the pine tree. The squirrel found a bottle that could spray water. The squirrel brought the bottle to the pine tree and said, "I found this for you!" The squirrel showed the pine tree how to use the bottle. The pine tree was very happy and thanked the squirrel. From that day on, when it was hot, the pine tree could spray water on itself to feel better. The squirrel and the pine tree were happy friends forever.
