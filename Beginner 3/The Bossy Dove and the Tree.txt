Category: Beginner
Title: The Bossy Dove and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a bossy dove. The bossy dove liked to tell all the other birds what to do. The other birds did not like the bossy dove very much. One day, a big wind came and started to shake the tree. The bossy dove was scared that the wind would destroy their tree. The other birds were scared too. They all wanted to save their tree, but they did not know how. Then, the bossy dove had an idea. The dove told all the other birds to hold on to the tree with their strong wings. The birds worked together and stopped the wind from destroying their tree. In the end, the bossy dove learned that it is better to work together with friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was going on a trip with his mom and dad. They had to pack their luggage. Tim's mom told him to wrap his toy car in a smooth cloth. This would keep the toy safe in the luggage. Tim and his family went to the big car. They put their luggage in the back. Tim was excited for the trip. He knew his toy car was safe in the luggage because it was wrapped in the smooth cloth. At the end of the trip, Tim and his family got to their new house. They opened their luggage. Tim found his toy car wrapped in the smooth cloth. It was safe, just like his mom said. Tim was happy to play with his toy car in his new home.

One day, a cat and a dog went for a walk. They saw a car with a tight rope on it. The cat said, "Why is that rope so tight?" The dog said, "I don't know. Let's go see." They went to the car and saw a license on it. The cat said, "What is a license?" The dog said, "It is a paper that lets you drive." They looked at each other and smiled. They wanted to drive the car. The cat and dog got in the car and started to drive. They were having fun. But then, the tight rope came to an end. The car stopped. They looked around and saw that they were in a big park with lots of other cats and dogs. They were all playing and having fun. The cat and dog decided to stay and play in the park. And that was the end of their adventure.

One day, a dog and a cat were playing near an empty box. The dog said, "I have a joke! Why did the cat sit in the empty box?" The cat looked at the dog and asked, "Why?" The dog laughed and said, "Because it was a purr-fect fit!" The cat did not laugh. He refused to sit in the empty box. The dog felt sad that the cat did not like his joke. He wanted to make the cat happy again. The next day, the dog found a ball. He brought it to the cat and said, "Let's play with this ball!" The cat looked at the ball and then at the empty box. He smiled and said, "I have an idea!" The cat jumped into the empty box and batted the ball back and forth with the dog. They played for hours, and both the dog and the cat were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a large dog named Max. Max loved to play with his friends in the park. One sunny day, Max went to the park and saw a big bowl of salad. Max had never seen a salad before, so he went closer to take a look. As Max sniffed the salad, he suddenly felt a tickle in his nose. Max tried to hold it in, but he couldn't. Max let out a big sneeze! The salad went flying into the air and landed all over the park. Max's friends laughed and helped him clean up the salad. They all had fun playing together and Max learned that he should be careful when sniffing new things. From that day on, Max always remembered the time he sneezed the salad all over the park.
