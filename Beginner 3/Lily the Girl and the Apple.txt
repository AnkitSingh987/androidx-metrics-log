Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Apple

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily was a bit naughty. She liked to play and run all day. One day, Lily was very hungry. She said to her mom, "I want lunch, please." Mom made a yummy lunch for Lily. Lily sat down to eat. But then, a little bird flew into the house. The bird wanted Lily's lunch too! Lily looked at the bird and said, "No, this is my lunch!" The bird was sad, but it had an idea. It flew away and came back with a big, red apple. The bird gave the apple to Lily. Lily was surprised! She shared her lunch with the bird. They ate together and became friends. From that day on, Lily was not naughty anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little crab named Tim. Tim lived in a peaceful shell near the sea. He loved to play in the sand and look for new shells. One day, Tim met a new friend named Sue. Sue was a small fish who lived in the sea. They talked and played together. Sue said, "Let's plan a fun day at the beach!" Tim and Sue planned their day. They played in the sand, swam in the sea, and found pretty shells. They laughed and had a great time. As the sun went down, Tim and Sue said goodbye. They promised to play together again soon. Tim went back to his peaceful shell, happy to have a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a mom and her daughter. The daughter was very enthusiastic. She liked to play, jump, and run all day. One day, they went to the park. The daughter saw a big tree. She said, "Mom, I want to climb the tree!" Mom said, "Hmm, let me think." She thought for a moment and then said, "Okay, but be careful." The daughter climbed the tree, and she was very happy. She looked at the sky and saw a bird. The daughter said, "Hi, bird!" The bird said, "Hi!" Then, the daughter climbed down and told her mom about the bird. They both smiled and went home.

Once upon a time, in a big red house, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his red ball. He would throw it up in the air and catch it all day long. One day, Tim saw his friend, Sam, playing with a blue ball. Tim thought that the blue ball looked fun too. He wanted to play with Sam and the blue ball. The next day, Tim went to Sam's house to play. They played with the blue ball and had lots of fun. But Tim missed his red ball. He knew that he should tell Sam the truth about his feelings. So he told Sam, "I like your blue ball, but I miss my red ball. Can we play with both balls?" Sam smiled and said, "Of course, we can play with both balls!" So they played with both the red and blue balls, and they had even more fun. Tim was happy that he told Sam the truth. From that day on, they always played with both balls and had the best time together.

Once upon a time, there was a famous bear. The bear was very hungry, so he went looking for something to eat. He saw a pair of scissors. He tried to eat them but they did not taste very good. Then he found a tasty apple and ate it. He was happy after he ate the apple. He decided he would always remember to eat tasty foods and never try to eat scissors again. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big fish named Fin. Fin was a very graceful fish. He loved to swim in the deep blue sea. One day, Fin saw something round and shiny on the sea floor. He wanted to see what it was. Fin took a big breath and started to dive. He went deeper and deeper into the sea. As he got closer, he saw that the round thing was a big pearl. It was so pretty! Fin was very happy to find such a beautiful thing. Fin took the round pearl back to his friends. They all loved it too. They played with the pearl all day long. They would take turns diving to catch it. They had so much fun, and Fin was the best at diving.
