Category: Beginner
Title: Speedy the Red Car and the Hill

Once upon a time, there was a little red car named Speedy. Speedy loved to go fast. He liked to race with his friends. They would all drive very quick. One day, Speedy saw a perfect hill. He wanted to speed down the hill. His friends watched as he went up the hill. They were excited to see him go fast. Speedy started to go down the hill. He went faster and faster. His friends cheered as he zoomed by. He felt so happy. Speedy won the race and had a great day with his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a stubborn boy who did not like to nap. One sunny day, Tim went to the park with his mom. At the park, Tim saw a big tree with a shiny arrow stuck in it. "Mom, look at the arrow!" Tim said. "Can I have it?" His mom said, "No, Tim. We don't know who it belongs to." Tim was sad but kept playing. Suddenly, a big bird flew down and took the arrow from the tree. Tim and his mom were so surprised! The bird flew away with the arrow, and Tim and his mom laughed. Tim was not sad anymore. He played until he was tired and then took a nap under the big tree.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play with sand. They go to the park with their moms. The park has a big sandbox. Tom and Mia want to make a castle. They use buckets and shovels and cups. They fill them with sand and dump them on the ground. They make a big pile of sand. Then they use their fingers and sticks to make shapes. They make walls and towers and doors. They make a flag with a leaf. They are happy. They have a cute castle. "Look at our castle!" Tom says. "It is the best castle ever!" "Yes, it is!" Mia says. "We are the king and the queen of the castle!" They pretend to live in the castle. They have fun. They talk and laugh and sing. But then a big boy comes. He is mean. He kicks their castle. He breaks their walls and towers and doors. He takes their flag. He laughs. He runs away. Tom and Mia are sad. They cry. Their castle is gone. Their moms come. They hug them. They say sorry. "It's okay," Tom says. "We can make another castle." "Yes, we can," Mia says. "But we need to find a new place." They look around. They see a corner of the sandbox. It is empty. It has more sand. "Let's go there," Tom says. "We can make a bigger and better castle." "Okay," Mia says. "But we need to be careful. We don't want the mean boy to come again." They take their buckets and shovels and cups. They go to the corner. They start to make a new castle. They are friends. They are happy. They part the sand.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to play outside in the sun. One day, at noon, she wanted to play with her friend, Tom. Tom had a toy car that was perfect. It was red and shiny, and Amy wanted to play with it too. When Amy asked Tom if she could play with his car, he said no. Amy was sad and angry. She took the car from Tom and broke it. Tom cried and Amy felt bad. She knew she did something wrong. Amy said sorry to Tom and gave him a hug. Tom forgave Amy and they became friends again. They learned that saying sorry and forgiving each other are important. The moral of the story is to always forgive and be kind to your friends.

Tom and Sam were best friends. They liked to play with cars, balls and books. They also liked to share their snacks and toys. But one day, they had a big fight. Tom wanted to play with Sam's new calendar, but Sam said no. He said it was his and he did not want to share. Tom got very angry. He said Sam was a bad friend and he did not want to play with him anymore. He grabbed the calendar and ran away. Sam ran after him and tried to get it back. They both pulled and pushed and shouted. The calendar tore in half and fell to the ground. They both stopped and looked at the broken calendar. They felt very sad. They realized they had been very silly and mean. They said sorry to each other and hugged. They decided to fix the calendar with some tape and glue. They also decided to send a card to their teacher, who had given them the calendar as a gift. They wrote: "Thank you for the calendar. We are sorry we broke it. We are best friends again. We love you." The teacher was very happy when she received the card. She said they were very kind and brave to admit their mistake and make up. She said they had learned a mighty lesson: that friendship is more important than things. She gave them a big smile and a new calendar to share. Tom and Sam smiled back and thanked her. They promised to take good care of the calendar and their friendship. They played happily ever after.
