Category: Beginner
Title: Millie the Girl and the Garden

One day, a little girl named Millie was in her garden. She was excited because she was looking for worms. She spotted an ugly worm crawling along the ground. She bent down and tried to put it in her bucket. But the worm wriggled away. Millie was frustrated and started to cry. Suddenly, her dad came to help. He said, "Don't worry Millie, I can help you catch that worm." Millie wiped away her tears and smiled. She watched as her dad slowly put his hand near the worm. Then the worm crawled onto his hand. Millie was so happy that she cheered and clapped. Her dad smiled and said, "Let's put it in your bucket now." So Millie and her dad put the worm in the bucket. Millie was so proud of herself for catching the ugly worm. From then on, Millie always asked for her dad's help when she wanted to put worms into her bucket.

Once upon a time, there was a famous big bird. The bird lived in a tree near a pretty lake. Every morning, the bird would sing a song when the sunrise came. All the animals loved to hear the bird sing. One morning, a little rabbit came to the tree. The rabbit said, "Bird, your song is so nice. Can you teach me how to sing like you?" The bird was happy to help. They spent the day singing together. The next day, the rabbit and the bird sang a new song at sunrise. All the animals came to listen. They clapped and said, "You are both so good at singing! Thank you for the beautiful song!" The rabbit and the bird became best friends and sang together every morning.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cart. One day, he pushed the cart outside to play in the sun. Tim saw a big box on the ground. It was heavy. Tim thought, "What is in the box?" He put the box in his cart and pushed it home. Tim's mom saw him with the box. She said, "What did you find, Tim?" Tim said, "I don't know, Mom. Can we peek inside?" They opened the box together and found a big, soft teddy bear. Tim was very happy and hugged the teddy bear tight.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. They saw the big water and the sand. Tim liked to play in the sand and watch the water. He saw the water wave and it made him happy. He saw a man with a big beard and thought he had a lot of wisdom. Tim went to the man and said, "Hi, can you tell me something I don't know?" The man with the big beard smiled and said, "Sure, little boy. Do you know that you should not go near bad animals?" Tim shook his head. He didn't know that. Suddenly, they saw a big crab on the sand. It looked bad. Tim was scared, but the man with the big beard said, "Don't worry, I will help you." The man with the big beard picked up a stick and poked the crab. But then, something unexpected happened. The crab started to dance! It wasn't a bad crab after all. It just wanted to play. Tim and the man with the big beard laughed and played with the crab. They all had a fun day at the beach.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat and a little mouse. The big cat wanted to catch the little mouse. The little mouse was scared and wanted to protect himself. The little mouse thought of a plan. He made a trap for the big cat. He found a box and a stick. He tied the stick to the box with a tight string. When the big cat went under the box, the little mouse would pull the string and the box would fall on the big cat. The big cat saw the trap and was very smart. He did not go under the box. Instead, he waited for the little mouse to come out. The little mouse was scared and stayed in his hole. The big cat was hungry and angry. One day, the little mouse tried to run away. But the big cat was too fast. The big cat caught the little mouse. The little mouse could not protect himself anymore. The big cat ate the little mouse, and the story has a bad ending.
