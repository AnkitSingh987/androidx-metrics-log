Category: Beginner
Title: The Building and the Family

Once upon a time, there was a big building. A nice family lived in the building. The family was very honest. They always told the truth. One day, the family wanted to settle in a new place. They talked to their friends. The mom said, "We want to find a new home." The friends said, "We will help you." They all looked for a new place to live. They found a nice house. The family was happy. They settled in the new house. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim found a clear belt. He put it on and went to play with his friends. They all liked his new belt and wanted to see it. Tim felt happy and proud of his clear belt. Tim and his friends went to the park. They saw a funny man who liked to joke. The funny man made them laugh with his jokes. He also had a clear belt like Tim's. They all thought it was so funny that they both had the same belt. The funny man and Tim became friends. They joked and played together in the park. Tim's friends also liked the funny man. They all had a good day with lots of laughter and fun. And Tim's clear belt was the start of a new friendship.

Once upon a time, there were two brave adventurers named Lucy and Jack. They were exploring a dark, dangerous cave. Suddenly, Jack saw a bone lying on the ground. "Look Lucy! I found a bone. What should we do?" Lucy thought for a moment and then smiled. "Hold onto it! It could come in handy." Jack picked up the bone but it glowed with a bright light. He felt scared but they kept exploring. Finally, they reached the end of the cave. Lucy looked around and then saw a giant monster. Its stomping shook the cave and Jack gulped. But then Lucy started to display the bone. Suddenly, the monster stopped moving. Its eyes widened, and it stepped aside. Jack could hardly believe it. He looked at Lucy and said, "Thanks for having the brilliant idea of displaying the bone! I thought it was dangerous but it saved us!" Lucy smiled and said, "I knew it would work! Let's get out of this cave!" So, with courage and bravery, Lucy and Jack safely made it out of the dangerous cave.

One day, a small squirrel named Tim found a big nut. This nut was impressive. Tim was very happy. He wanted to share the nut with his friend, Ben the bird. Tim thought, "I will offer the nut to Ben." The next day, Tim went to see Ben. He showed Ben the nut. Ben was happy too. But Ben said, "I cannot eat this nut. I know who can eat it. Let's go see Sam the rabbit." Tim and Ben went to find Sam. When they found Sam, Tim offered the nut to him. Sam was very happy. He had a big tool to open the nut. Inside the nut, there were more nuts for everyone to eat. Tim, Ben, and Sam all shared the nuts and had a fun time.

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They saw a big tree with a tall trunk. They wanted to climb the tree and see the birds. "Come on, Lily, let's go up!" Tom said. "OK, Tom, but be careful!" Lily said. They climbed the tree and reached a branch. They saw a nest with three eggs. They were blue and speckled. "Wow, look at the eggs!" Tom said. "They are so pretty!" "Yes, they are!" Lily said. "But we should not touch them. They belong to the birds." Suddenly, they heard a loud noise. It was a big truck. It came to the park and stopped near the tree. A man got out of the truck. He had a saw and a rope. "What is he doing?" Tom asked. "I don't know, but it looks bad!" Lily said. The man started to cut the tree with the saw. The tree shook and the branch cracked. "Help! Help!" Tom and Lily shouted. The man did not hear them. He kept cutting the tree. The branch broke and Tom and Lily fell. But they did not hit the ground. They landed on something soft and furry. It was a bear. The bear had seen the man and the truck. He had run to the park to rescue the tree and the birds. He had caught Tom and Lily with his back. "Thank you, bear!" Tom and Lily said. The bear nodded and growled. He carried them to a safe place. He put them down and licked their faces. "You are welcome, kids!" the bear said. "But you should not climb trees that are in danger. It is not safe." "We are sorry, bear!" Tom and Lily said. "We did not know. We just wanted to see the birds." The bear smiled and hugged them. He showed them the way back to their parents. He waved goodbye and went back to the forest. Tom and Lily ran to their parents and told them what happened. Their parents were scared and happy. They thanked the bear and the park ranger who had stopped the man and the truck. They learned that the tree and the birds were protected and that the man was a bad guy who wanted to cut them down. They were glad that the bear had rescued them and the tree. They promised to never climb trees that were in danger again. They also promised to be friends with the bear and the birds. They went home and drew pictures of their adventure. They had a lot of fun.
