Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Cat and the Owl

One day, a chubby cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a small chair and decided to seat on it. While he was sitting, a wise old owl came to talk to him. "Hello, Tom!" said the owl. "Do you know that I have wisdom? I can teach you how to be smart, too!" Tom wanted to learn, so he listened to the wise old owl. They talked and talked. Tom felt like he was getting smarter. But then, something unexpected happened! A big wind came and blew the chair away! Tom was scared, but the wise old owl said, "Don't worry, I will help you!" The owl used his wisdom to find a new chair for Tom. They found a big, soft chair that was perfect for Tom. They both sat on the chair and talked some more. Tom was very happy to have a wise friend like the owl.

Once upon a time there was a nice girl. She was pretty and liked to have fun. One day she went to a friend's house. She asked if she could borrow a snack! Her friend said yes and gave her a yummy apple. It was so shiny and red. She thanked her friend and went home with the apple. It was so pretty that it made her smile. She took a bite and it tasted so good! She enjoyed her snack very much. The end.

Once there was a pretty little girl called Clara. She was so excited, because today she was going to complete her first puzzle. Clara sat down with the jigsaw and looked at all of the pieces. She smiled and started putting them together. After a while she realised one piece was missing, it had a little leak in it. Clara ran to her mum and showed her the leaky piece. Her mum got some glue and they were able to fix it. Clara was so happy, she went back and finished the puzzle. When she had complete it she felt very proud. She showed it to her mum and they both smiled.

Anna and Ben are playing with a big box. They pretend it is a house. They cut out windows and a door. They draw flowers and trees on the box. They have fun. But then Ben sees a shiny pin on the floor. He picks it up and says, "Look, Anna, a pin! I can use it to make a hole in the box. Then we can spy on the cat." Anna says, "No, Ben, that is a silly idea. The pin is sharp. It can hurt you or the cat. Put it back where you found it. Let's play something else." But Ben does not listen. He runs to the box with the pin. He tries to make a hole in the window. But the pin is too small. He pushes harder and harder. He slips and pokes his finger. He cries, "Ow, ow, ow!" Anna hears him and follows him. She sees the pin and the blood. She says, "Oh, Ben, you are hurt. I told you the pin was silly. Come, I will help you. We will wash your finger and put a band-aid on it. Then we can play again. But no more pins, okay?" Ben nods and says, "Okay, Anna, I am sorry. The pin was silly. You are a good friend. Thank you for helping me." He hugs Anna and smiles. They go to the bathroom and clean his finger. Then they go back to the box and play. They forget about the pin and the cat. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play with her best friend, Tommy. They liked to play with bubbles. They would blow the bubbles and watch them fly. One day, Lucy and Tommy had a terrible problem. Their bubble bottle was empty! They did not know what to do. They sat down and began to discuss how to make more bubbles. Lucy said, "I think we need soap and water." Tommy agreed. They found some soap and water, mixed it together, and made more bubbles. They were so happy and played with their bubbles all day long.
