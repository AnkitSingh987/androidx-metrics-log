Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Butterfly

Once there was a girl named Sue. Sue was out in the yard when she saw a white butterfly fluttering in the breeze. She whispered softly to the butterfly, wanting to make it her friend. Sue watched in awe as the butterfly flew from flower to flower. Suddenly, an unexpected thing happened. Two more white butterflies appeared! Sue thought it was strange to see the three butterflies together. They were different in size and shape, but they were both still white. Sue smiled and pointed out the differences to her mom. The butterflies seemed to know she was talking about them and they flew near her and began to whisper back. Sue was delighted! She had made friends with three different white butterflies.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to eat pears. One day, he found a big, green pear in his yard. Tim was very happy and wanted to eat it. But, Tim's mom said, "Wait! That pear may be dangerous. It fell from the tree and might be spoiled." Tim didn't want to listen, but he put the pear down and went to play. Later, Tim saw a little bird fly near the pear. The bird tapped the pear and, to Tim's surprise, out came a tiny, friendly bug! The bug said, "Thank you for not eating the pear. I was hiding inside to stay safe." Tim was glad he didn't eat the pear, and he made a new friend.

One day, a curious cat named Tom went outside to play in the yard. He saw a big bowl with something inside. Tom went closer to see what it was. Tom saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was trying to stir the stuff in the bowl. But the spoon was too big for her. "I can't stir it," she said. "It's too hard." Tom wanted to help Sue. He jumped on the table and used his tail to stir the stuff in the bowl. Sue was happy. She said, "Thank you, Tom!" They both laughed and played in the yard all day.

One day, a little bird named Tim was flying around. He saw a big log on the ground. Tim felt lucky because he was looking for a new home. He thought the log would be a perfect place to live. Tim went inside the log and saw a tiny bug. The bug said, "Hi, I am Ben. I live here too. Do you want to be friends?" Tim said, "Yes, I would love to be your friend, Ben!" They both felt lucky to have found each other. Every day, Tim would go out and find food to feed Ben. They would share the food and talk about their day. They were very happy to live in the log together. Tim and Ben felt lucky to have a safe home and a good friend.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a mysterious frame. This frame had a picture of a cat. The cat was big and had a long tail. The cat lived in a tree near the house. One day, the cat got stuck in the tree. It cried for help. A little boy named Tim heard the cat. He wanted to rescue the cat. Tim thought of a plan to save the cat. Tim got a long stick. He used the stick to help the cat. The cat grabbed the stick with its paws. Tim pulled the stick and the cat came down from the tree. The cat was happy and safe. Tim felt proud that he could rescue the cat. They became best friends and played together every day.

One day, a little boy named Tim had a big bag. Inside the bag, he had many toys. He liked to play with his toys all day. Tim was never sleepy because he loved to play so much. One sunny day, Tim took his bag to the park. He saw a girl named Sue. Sue had a bag too. They both wanted to have fun, so they decided to race. They put their bags at the end of the park and got ready to run. Tim and Sue ran as fast as they could. They were both very fast, but Tim was a little bit faster. He reached his bag first and won the race. After the race, they were both very sleepy. Tim and Sue sat down, opened their bags, and played with their toys until it was time to go home.
