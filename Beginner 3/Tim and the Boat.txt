Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Boat

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went to a restaurant with his mom and dad. Tim was a bit fearful because it was his first time going to a restaurant. He held his mom's hand tight as they walked in. At the restaurant, they sat at a big table. Tim saw a big paper on the table. His mom said, "Tim, you can fold the paper to make a boat." Tim was happy and started to fold the paper. But it was hard, and he couldn't fold it right. Tim's dad saw him getting sad and came to help. Together, they folded the paper and made a nice boat. Tim was not fearful anymore. He played with the boat and had a fun time at the restaurant with his mom and dad.

Once upon a time, in a small house, a little girl named Lily lived with her mom and dad. It was winter, and the snow was falling softly outside. Lily was glad because she loved to play in the snow. One day, Lily went outside to play. She saw her friend Tim from next door. "Hi, Tim!" said Lily. "Do you want to play a game?" Tim said, "Yes! Let's see who can number the most snowballs." Lily and Tim started to make snowballs, counting as they went. But then, a big dog came and took some of their snowballs away. Lily and Tim were sad. "Hey, that's not fair!" said Lily. Tim agreed, "We need to get our snowballs back!" They thought of a plan to get their snowballs back from the big dog. Lily and Tim made a big pile of snowballs and put them in the middle of the yard. When the big dog came back, they started to throw the snowballs at the dog. The big dog was surprised and ran away, leaving their snowballs behind. Lily and Tim were glad they got their snowballs back. They continued their game, counting the snowballs they made. They played all day in the snow, having lots of fun together. The end.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys in their room. But they do not like to share. Sometimes they fight over the same toy and make a lot of noise. One day, Mom gives them a treat. She says, "This is a cake. It is very yummy. You can have it after lunch. But you have to be good and share your toys. If you fight, I will lock the cake in the cupboard and you will not get any. Do you understand?" Lily and Ben nod. They say, "Yes, Mom. We will be good and share." Mom smiles and puts the cake on the table. She says, "I will be back soon. Do not touch the cake until I say so." She leaves the room. Lily and Ben look at the cake. It is big and round and has pink frosting and sprinkles. It looks very yummy. They want to have some now. They forget what Mom said. Lily says, "I want a piece of cake. It is my treat." Ben says, "No, it is mine. I saw it first." They start to argue. They pull the cake from each other. The cake breaks into two pieces. Some frosting and sprinkles fall on the floor. Mom hears the noise. She comes back to the room. She sees the mess. She is very angry. She says, "What have you done? You did not listen to me. You did not share. You ruined the cake. Now you will not get any treat. I will lock the cake in the cupboard and throw it away later. Go to your room and think about what you did." Lily and Ben feel very sorry. They cry. They say, "We are sorry, Mom. We were bad. We want some cake. Please do not lock it away." But Mom does not listen. She takes the cake and puts it in the cupboard. She locks it with a key. She says, "Maybe next time you will be good and share. Now go to your room and stay there until I call you." Lily and Ben go to their room. They are very sad. They hug each other. They say, "We are sorry. We love you. We will share next time." They hope Mom will forgive them and give them some cake later. They do not know that the cupboard is very thin and has a hole. A mouse smells the cake and sneaks in. The mouse eats the cake and leaves nothing behind. The mouse is very happy. Lily and Ben are not.
