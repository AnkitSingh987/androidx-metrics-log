Category: Beginner
Title: Maria and the Mom

One day, Maria had a great plan. She wanted to show off her new fashion! She went to her closet and pulled out a beautiful dress. It was pink with yellow polka dots. She was so proud of it. When Maria's mom saw her, she said, "Oh my! You look so pretty! What fashion did you plan?" Maria smiled and said, "I have a new dress and I was so proud when I put it on! I'm going to show everyone today!" Her mom gave a big smile and said, "You certainly do look very proud. You look like a beautiful princess!" "Thank you, Mom!" said Maria. She hugged her mom and then went off to show everyone her new fashion.

One day, a young boy named Tim found a phone on the floor. He picked it up and showed it to his mom. "Mom, look what I found!" he said. His mom looked at the phone and said, "Let's see if we can find the owner." Tim and his mom went outside to look for the owner of the phone. They asked everyone they saw if they lost a phone. But no one said it was theirs. Tim was sad because he wanted to help. Just then, a little bird flew down and landed on the phone. The bird started to talk! "Thank you for finding my phone!" said the bird. Tim and his mom were very surprised. They didn't know birds could talk! The bird took the phone and said, "Now I can shut it and keep it safe." Tim and his mom were happy they could help the bird, and they went home with a big smile.

Once upon a time there lived a little girl who was very busy. She loved to try new things, but there was always something she was too scared to do. Today she was going to try something different. As she was walking, she saw an old lady with a basket of pies. She smiled and said to the little girl, "Would you like a pie?" The little girl nervously replied, "I don't know. I'm not sure if I should try." The old lady then said, "It's ok, my little one, sometimes it's good to take risks. Try the pie and see if you like it." So the little girl did. She took a bite and she was surprised at how yummy the pie was! She finished the entire pie and thanked the old lady. She waved goodbye and felt much braver. From that day forward, the little girl was always trying new things. She realized that it was ok to take risks, and that things can be delicious if she just gave it a try!

Once upon a time, there was a pretty rose in a garden. The rose was red and smelled very nice. Many bugs loved the rose and would gather around it all day. One day, a new flower grew in the garden. The new flower was very big and had many colors. The bugs started to go to the new flower. They liked how it looked and wanted to be near it. The rose felt sad and jealous. It missed the bugs and wanted them to come back. The rose thought of a plan. It decided to grow bigger and have more colors too. The rose tried very hard, and soon it was big and colorful. The bugs saw the rose and thought it was very pretty. They started to gather around the rose again. The rose was happy, and the new flower was happy too. They both had bugs to share, and they became friends. The garden was a happy place for all.

One day, a group of kids wanted to play a big game. They decided to gather at the big stadium. The stadium was a fun place where they could run, jump, and play with their friends. One of the kids, named Tim, was worried. He wasn't sure if he could play well. He told his friend, Sue, "I am worried. What if I can't play well?" Sue smiled and said, "Don't worry, Tim! We are here to have fun and help each other." In the stadium, the kids played and laughed. Tim tried his best and his friends cheered him on. Soon, Tim was not worried anymore. He was having fun with his friends at the big stadium.
