Category: Beginner
Title: The Ancient Tree and the Hat

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was an ancient tree. It was very big and old. Near the tree, there was a beautiful lawn. Many kids liked to play on the lawn. One sunny day, a little boy named Tim went to play on the lawn. He saw a tiny ant. The ant wore a little hat. Tim said, "Hello, ant! I like your hat." The ant said, "Thank you, Tim! I wear this hat to keep the sun away." Tim and the ant played on the lawn all day. They had lots of fun. When it was time to go home, Tim said, "Goodbye, ant! I will come back to play with you soon." The ant said, "Goodbye, Tim! I will miss you." The next day, Tim wore a hat just like the ant's. He learned it was good to wear a hat in the sun. The moral of the story is to learn from our friends and take care of ourselves.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to ride his bike with pedals. He would ride it every day in the park. He had so much fun going fast and seeing all the trees and flowers. One day, Tim felt terrible. He was very tired and didn't want to play with his bike. His mom said, "You need to sleep, Tim. It will make you feel better." So, Tim went to his bed and closed his eyes. He dreamed about riding his bike with pedals in the park. When Tim woke up, he didn't feel terrible anymore. He was happy and full of energy. He ran to his mom and said, "Thank you for telling me to sleep, Mom. I feel much better now!" Tim went outside and rode his bike with pedals all day long, having lots of fun in the park. And from that day on, Tim always remembered to sleep when he felt terrible, so he could be ready to ride his bike again.

Ben and Mia are playing with their toy cars on the floor. They like to roll them fast and make loud noises. Ben has a red car and Mia has a blue car. They pretend they are racing on a big road. "Look, my car is faster than yours!" Ben says to Mia. "No, my car is faster than yours!" Mia says to Ben. They roll their cars faster and faster, until they bump into each other. The cars fall down and make a loud crash. Ben and Mia laugh. "Oops, sorry!" Ben says. "It's okay!" Mia says. They pick up their cars and hug each other. They are good friends. Then they hear their mom calling them from the kitchen. "Ben, Mia, it's time for food!" Mom says. Ben and Mia run to the kitchen. They are hungry. They see a big plate of sandwiches on the table. Mom has made them with cheese and ham and lettuce. They look yummy. "Wow, thank you, Mom!" Ben says. "Thank you, Mom!" Mia says. They sit down and eat their sandwiches. They are delicious. Mom smiles and watches them. "I'm proud of you, Ben and Mia," Mom says. "You are playing nicely and sharing your toys. And you are eating your food without making a mess. You are good kids." Ben and Mia smile and hug Mom. They are happy. They love Mom. And they love their food. And they love their cars. They are having a good day.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a fat bunny. The fat bunny liked to play and hop around all day. One sunny day, the fat bunny was hopping near a big tree with many roots. While hopping, the fat bunny saw a hole near a root. The fat bunny wanted to peek inside the hole. So, the fat bunny went close and put his head in the hole to peek. He saw something shiny and pretty inside the hole. The fat bunny pulled the shiny thing out of the hole. It was a magic carrot! The fat bunny ate the magic carrot and something unexpected happened. The fat bunny became very small, like a baby bunny. The small bunny laughed and played, happy with his new size. Now, he could explore many more places in the big green forest.
