Category: Beginner
Title: The Orange Shrimp and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a little orange shrimp. The shrimp lived in the sea with his family. They were all very happy. One day, the little shrimp saw a big fish. The big fish looked hungry. The shrimp was scared. He swam to his mom and said, "Mom, I saw a big fish. Can you help me?" His mom listened to his request. She said, "Don't worry, little shrimp. I will talk to the big fish." So, the mom swam to the big fish. The mom said to the big fish, "Please don't eat my child. We are a happy family." The big fish listened and said, "Okay, I will not eat your child. I will find other food." The little shrimp was very happy. He thanked his mom and gave her a big hug. They swam together, safe and sound.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim and a little girl named Sue went for a walk in the park. They saw a big tree with a light, shiny ball stuck high up in the branches. Tim wanted to climb the tree and get the ball. Sue said, "No, it's not safe!" Tim and Sue started to disagree. Tim said, "I can do it!" Sue said, "It's too high!" They were both getting upset. Then, a friendly squirrel came by and heard their argument. The squirrel said, "Let me help you!" The squirrel climbed up the tree and got the light, shiny ball for Tim and Sue. They were so happy! They thanked the squirrel and played with the ball together. From that day on, Tim and Sue learned that it's better to work together and stay safe.

One day, a powerful big cat named Leo went to a hotel. He wanted to find a place to sleep. When he got there, he saw a small mouse named Tim. "Hello, can I stay here?" Leo asked Tim. "Yes, but you must start by saying please," said Tim. So, Leo said "please" and went inside the hotel. In the hotel, Leo was very loud and broke many things. The other animals did not like it. They told Tim to make Leo leave. Tim went to talk to Leo. "Leo, you must go. You are too loud and big," said Tim. Leo was sad but he did not want to leave. He tried to be quiet, but he was still too powerful. In the end, the hotel fell down because of Leo. All the animals had to find a new place to stay. Leo was very sorry, but it was too late.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Jill. She was very excited because it was her first day of school. She walked into the class and saw all the kids. She was a bit scared, but excited at the same time. Suddenly, the teacher entered the classroom. "Good morning everyone," he said. He had a big smile on his face, except for when he looked at Jill. He had a grumpy expression. The teacher asked everyone to pick a math book from the shelf. Jill picked the smallest book she could find. All the other kids were looking at her with puzzled eyes. The teacher started speaking again. "Jill, why did you pick that book?" he asked in a grumpy voice. "I wanted to find the easiest book," Jill shyly replied. The teacher smiled and said, "That is a great choice, Jill. Math is important, but it doesn't have to be too difficult." Jill smiled and everyone cheered. She was no longer scared and was excited to learn new math skills.

Once upon a time, there was a good girl named Sue. Sue had a pretty scarf. One day, she went to the park to play. At the park, Sue met a boy named Tim. Tim said, "I like your scarf. Can I have it?" Sue did not want to yield her scarf. She said, "No, it is mine." Tim got mad and took the scarf from Sue. He ran away fast. Sue cried and went home. The scarf was gone and Sue was sad. The end.

Once upon a time there was a little girl. She was only three years old and wanted to have some fun. So she went to the park. When she got there she saw a big brown bear. The girl was scared so she ran away. The bear was very funny and said, "Where are you going? Come sit with me!" The little girl said, "What do you have for me?" The bear said, "I have a muffin for you!" The little girl was so excited. She said, "Thank you! Let's share the muffin together." They sat in the park and ate the muffin. It was so much fun! The little girl went home with a smile on her face.
