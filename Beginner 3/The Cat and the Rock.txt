Category: Beginner
Title: The Cat and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a big cat. The cat was very hungry. He saw a big steak. The steak was very tasty. The cat wanted to eat the steak. The cat tried to fit the steak in his mouth. But the steak was too big. The cat tried to cut the steak. He found a useful rock. The rock was sharp. The cat cut the steak with the rock. But the cat was not careful. He cut his paw. The cat was sad. He could not eat the steak. The cat went away hungry.

One day, a little helicopter was flying in the sky. The helicopter felt very important. It was a bright and sunny day, and the helicopter could see many things below. As the helicopter flew over a park, it saw a little boy waving at it. The boy looked very happy. The helicopter wanted to say hello, so it flew lower and made a loud noise. The boy laughed and waved even more. The helicopter said to the boy, "Hello! I am an important helicopter. I like to fly and help people. Do you like to wave at helicopters?" The boy smiled and said, "Yes! I love helicopters. They are big and strong. I want to fly like you one day." The helicopter and the boy became good friends and waved to each other every time they saw each other.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet little car named Timmy. Timmy loved to drive fast on the open road. One day, he met a big truck named Tom. "Hi, Tom!" said Timmy. "Do you like to drive fast too?" "No, Timmy," said Tom. "I like to drive slow and steady." Timmy wanted to show Tom how fun it was to drive fast. But first, he needed oil. So, he went to the oil shop. At the oil shop, Timmy got his oil. He was so happy! Now he could drive fast again. Timmy and Tom drove together. Timmy drove fast, and Tom drove slow. They had so much fun! And they became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a clean beach. On this beach, there was a little jellyfish. The jellyfish was blue and soft. It liked to play in the water near the sand. One day, the sun was very hot. The jellyfish did not want to burn. So, it swam to the cool water to stay safe. The jellyfish saw a big fish and they played together. They had a lot of fun in the water. At the end of the day, the sun was not hot anymore. The jellyfish went back to the clean beach. It was happy because it did not burn. The jellyfish and the big fish said goodbye. The jellyfish went to sleep on the clean sand, dreaming of more fun days.

One day, a cat and a dog decide to have a race. The prize is a big bowl of food. The cat thinks she is fast and the dog thinks he is faster. They both want to win the food. The cat says, "I will win because I am fast." The dog says, "No, I will win because I am faster." They argue, but they do not know who is right. They are both a little ignorant. They start the race. The cat runs fast and the dog runs faster. They both try their best. In the end, the dog wins the race. The cat is sad, but the dog shares the food with her. They both learn that it is more fun to share than to argue.

Ben hates the classroom. He hates the chairs, the books, the teacher. He hates everything. He wants to go home and play with his toys. One day, he sees a big, hairy spider on the wall. He is scared. He screams and runs to the door. But the door is locked. He can't get out. "Ben, what's wrong?" the teacher asks. She comes to him and tries to calm him down. "It's just a spider. It won't hurt you." Ben points at the spider. "It's big and hairy and scary!" he cries. "I don't like it!" The teacher smiles. She picks up the spider gently and puts it in a jar. "Look, Ben, it's not so bad. It's just a little animal. It has eight legs and eyes and a mouth. It likes to make webs and catch flies. Do you want to see?" Ben shakes his head. He doesn't want to see. He hates spiders. He hates the classroom. He hates everything. He wants to go home and play with his toys.
