Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Fox and the Garden

Once there was a little girl who was very lucky. She loved to play near a big bin in her garden. Every day, she would look inside and find new things to make her happy. One day, she heard a funny ringing noise coming from the bin. It sounded sweet, like the chimes of a music box. She peered inside, but she wasn't ready for what she saw. There was a hungry fox! Its tail was ringing one of the bin's metal bars. She screamed, and the fox ran away. She was no longer lucky. She stopped going to the bin, for fear of what else she might find. Someday, she might be lucky again, but for now, she was afraid of the bin.

One day, in a town, there was a band. They played fun songs for all. The band had a cat. The cat owned a hat. The cat was busy with the hat. One day, the band played a big show. Many kids came to dance and sing. The cat was busy with the hat. But the hat fell off the cat's head. The cat was sad. Then, something unexpected happened. The hat started to dance! All the kids laughed and clapped. The cat was happy. Now, the cat and the hat danced together at every show. The band was more fun than ever.

Once upon a time, there lived a queen who was very proud. She liked to sort things, and would often ask her children to help her. One day, her youngest child, a three-year-old, noticed the queen sorting some coins. Showing off, he said, “I can help, too!” The queen smiled and said, “Then come and sort with me.” She showed him how to hold two coins in each hand, and count how many were in each pile. The three-year-old watched closely, and with a bit of help, was soon sorting the coins like a pro. The queen, so proud of her little helper, smiled and said, “Well done! You’ve sorted just like a queen!” The three-year-old beamed brightly. He had helped the queen, and she liked it! From then on, he helped her sort anything he could find.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her toys and draw on paper. One day, she felt nervous because she could not find her favorite paper to draw on. Sue looked all around her room but could not find it. She felt sad and went to her mom. Her mom said, "Let's look together." They looked all around the house but still could not find the paper. Then, Sue's mom had an idea. "Let's look in the toy box," she said. They opened the toy box and found the paper! But there was a twist. The paper was not empty. Sue's little brother had drawn a big heart on it. Sue was not sad anymore. She smiled and hugged her brother. From that day on, they always drew together and shut the toy box when they were done.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy drum that he loved to play. He played music on his drum all day long. One day, Tim's friend Sam came over to play. They wanted to celebrate because they were happy. Tim said, "Let's play music on my drum!" Sam was excited and said, "Yes, let's play!" But when they started to play, they heard a loud crack. The drum was broken! Tim was very sad and started to cry. They tried to fix the drum but it was still broken. Tim and Sam could not celebrate anymore. They were sad and went to sit under a tree. Then, a big wind came and blew the broken drum away. Now, Tim and Sam could not play music or celebrate. They went home with a bad ending to their day.

One day, Tom went with his mom to find a new chair. They went to a big store with many chairs. Tom saw a big chair that he liked. It was very soft and nice to sit on. But his mom said it was too expensive. Tom was sad, but he kept looking. He saw a small chair that was not expensive. He tried sitting on it and it was just right for him. He showed it to his mom and she liked it too. They bought the chair and took it home. Now, Tom has a chair that is just right for him. He likes to sit on it and read his books. He is happy that they were able to find a chair that was not too expensive.
