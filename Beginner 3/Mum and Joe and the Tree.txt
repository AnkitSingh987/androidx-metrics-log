Category: Beginner
Title: Mum and Joe and the Tree

Mum and Joe ran to the park. Joe was a chubby boy and he loved playing with friends. He stopped to look at all the different pictures on the walls. When they arrived at the park, Joe saw all his friends playing. He saw something else too - a great big tree with a big hole in it! He asked Mum what it was for. Mum told Joe it was for hiding in. She said that when friends arrived at the park, they could play hide and seek in the tree. Joe thought this was so exciting and ran to join them. When the game had finished, Joe and his friends ran to hide in the tree. There were so many exciting places to explore in the tree. Soon they found a special picture hidden away in one of the branches. Joe was so happy! He had seen this picture before - it was the same one he had seen on the walls. He thanked his friends and ran to tell his mum how his day had gone.

Once upon a time, there was a big, round pumpkin. It was very happy living in the garden. One day, a little boy named Mark came to the garden. He saw the pumpkin and thought it was very pretty. He wanted to take it home with him. Mark said to the pumpkin, "You are so pretty! I want to take you home with me. But I don't want to be selfish. Can I take you home?" The pumpkin thought for a moment and said, "Yes, you can take me home. But you must promise to be kind and share me with your friends." So Mark took the pumpkin home with him. He showed it to all of his friends and they all loved it. They played with the pumpkin and had lots of fun. Because Mark was not selfish, he and his friends were all very happy. And the pumpkin was happy too, because it had made many new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a pale little cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to wear her pretty scarf. One day, Kitty went for a walk in the park. In the park, she met a big dog named Doggy. Doggy saw Kitty's scarf and wanted to play with it. "Can I play with your scarf?" asked Doggy. Kitty said, "No, it's my favorite. I don't want it to suffer." Doggy felt sad but he understood. They played in the park all day. Kitty and Doggy became good friends. Kitty let Doggy wear her scarf for a little while. They laughed and had fun. From that day on, Kitty and Doggy played together every day, and they always took good care of the pretty scarf.

Once upon a time, there was a humble cat. The cat liked to play with its friends. One day, it saw a big, red ball. The cat wanted to play with the ball. The cat tried to get the ball, but it would not yield. The cat pushed and pushed. Suddenly, the ball began to roll away. The cat chased the ball as it rolled down the street. As the cat chased the ball, a police car came. The police car wanted to stop the cat. But, the cat did not see the police car. The cat kept chasing the ball. Then, the ball popped. The cat was sad, and the police took the cat away.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the lab. The lab has many toys and tools. They can make things and learn things in the lab. One day, they find a big box in the lab. The box has a picture of a robot. Lily and Ben want to make the robot. They open the box and see many parts. They look at the instructions. The instructions have words and pictures. "Let's make the robot!" Lily says. "It looks fun!" "OK!" Ben says. "I will help you!" They start to make the robot. They put the parts together. They use a screwdriver and a wrench. They follow the instructions. They work hard and have fun. After a while, they finish the robot. The robot is big and shiny. It has two arms, two legs, a head and a body. It has eyes, a mouth and a nose. It has buttons and lights. "Wow!" Lily says. "We did it! We made the robot!" "Yay!" Ben says. "It is awesome! Let's turn it on!" They find the switch on the robot's back. They turn it on. The robot makes a sound. It says, "Hello, I am Robo. I am your friend. Thank you for making me." Lily and Ben are happy. They hug the robot. They play with the robot. The robot is fit. It can walk, talk and dance. It can do many things. It is smart and kind. Lily and Ben love the robot. They love the lab. They love to make things and learn things. They are good friends. They are happy.
