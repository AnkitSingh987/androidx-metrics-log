Category: Beginner
Title: The Bird and the Whistle

Once upon a time, there was a little bird. The bird had a whistle. The bird loved to whistle all day long. The whistle was very pretty. Everyone liked to hear the bird whistle. One day, the bird lost its whistle. The bird was very sad. The bird looked everywhere for its whistle. The bird could not find its whistle. The bird felt very poor without its whistle. The bird started to cry. A nice boy heard the bird cry. The boy wanted to help the bird. The boy looked for the whistle. The boy found the whistle. The bird was happy again. The bird and the boy played together. They were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom loved to eat. Every day, he would sneak into the kitchen and open the refrigerator. He would eat all the food he could find. Tom's owner, a little girl named Sue, would always get sad when she saw the empty refrigerator. One day, Tom saw a big cake in the refrigerator. He wanted to eat it all by himself. But the cake was on a high shelf. Tom tried to rise up on his back legs to reach the cake, but he was too fat. He tried and tried, but he just could not get the cake. Sue saw Tom trying to get the cake. She knew that if Tom ate the cake, there would be no food left for her. So, Sue decided to help Tom lose some weight. They went for walks together and played fun games. Soon, Tom was not so fat anymore. He could rise up and reach the cake, but he decided not to eat it. Tom and Sue shared the cake together and were both very happy.

One day, a lonely cat named Tom went for a walk at night. The moon was big and bright. Tom walked down the street, looking for a friend to play with. He saw a dog named Max sitting by a tree. "Hi, Max!" said Tom. "Do you want to play with me?" Max looked at Tom and said, "I don't play with cats. I only play with dogs." Tom felt sad and walked away. As he walked, he saw something shiny on the ground. It was a magic stone! Tom picked it up and wished for a friend to play with. Suddenly, a friendly fox named Foxy appeared. Foxy said, "Hi, Tom! I want to play with you!" Tom was happy and they played all night long. The next day, Max saw them playing and felt lonely. He learned that it's nice to have friends who are different.

Once upon a time, in a land far away, there was a big hill. The hill was very smooth. On top of the hill, there was a little red ball. One day, the wind blew the little red ball. The ball started to go down the smooth hill. It went faster and faster. The little red ball liked to go fast. At the bottom of the hill, there was a pond. The little red ball went into the pond with a big splash. The fish in the pond said hello to the ball. The little red ball liked the land and the new friends it made.

Once upon a time, there was a small girl named Mia. Mia had a toy with a horn. She loved to play with her horn all day. One day, she went to the park to play with her friends. At the park, Mia saw a boy named Tim. Tim did not have a toy to play with. Mia felt sad for Tim, so she decided to share her horn with him. They played together and had lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A dog came running to them and took the horn away. Mia and Tim were surprised and sad. They thought the horn was gone forever. But the dog came back with two more horns! Now, Mia, Tim, and the dog all had a horn to play with. They all shared and played together, and they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little gray bunny named Bob. Bob loved to mix things. One day, he found a green mint leaf in the grass. He hopped to his friend, the big brown bear named Ben. "Look, Ben! I found a mint leaf! Let's mix it with something," said Bob. Ben thought for a moment. "Let's mix it with honey!" So, they mixed the mint leaf with honey. They tasted it, and it was so yummy. They shared it with their friends, and everyone loved it. From that day on, Bob and Ben became the best mint-honey makers in the forest.
