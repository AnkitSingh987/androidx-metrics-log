Category: Beginner
Title: The Great Park of Tim and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a wide smile that made everyone happy. Tim liked to play with his toys all day. His parent would watch him and laugh. One day, Tim's parent said they would go to the park. Tim was very excited. He put on his shoes and hat. But then, the phone rang. It was a call that would delay their trip to the park. Tim was sad, but he knew he had to wait. While they waited, Tim and his parent played a game together. They had fun and laughed. When the phone call was over, they went to the park. Tim's wide smile was back, and they had a great time playing.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very obedient boy. He always did what his mom and dad told him to do. One day, Tim's mom told him to listen carefully. She said, "Tim, I lost a coin in the park. Can you help me find it?" Tim went to the park to look for the coin. He looked under the trees, near the swings, and by the slide. He listened carefully to the sounds around him. Tim heard a bird singing, a dog barking, and children playing. Finally, Tim found the coin near a big rock. He picked it up and ran back to his mom. She was so happy and proud of her obedient boy. Tim listened well and helped his mom find the coin. They went home and had a big hug. Tim felt very happy that he could help his mom.

Once upon a time, in a humble little house, there lived a boy named Tim and his pet dog, Max. They were best friends and loved to play outside. One day, it was very cold, so Tim put on his warm coat. As they went outside, they saw a big tree with many apples. Tim wanted to get an apple for his snack. He tried to reach up high, but he was too little. Tim had an idea. He said, "Max, can you help me get an apple?" Max wagged his tail and jumped up to the tree. He used his teeth to bite the apple gently. The apple fell down, and Tim caught it with his hands. They were both very happy. Tim and Max went back to their humble house. Tim shared the apple with Max as a thank you for helping him. They both enjoyed their snack and continued to play outside in their warm coats.

Lily liked to sort her toys. She had a group of dolls, a group of cars, a group of blocks and a group of animals. She put each toy in the right group and made them neat. One day, she had a new toy. It was a teddy bear. She did not know where to put it. It was not a doll, not a car, not a block and not an animal. She was confused. She asked her mom. "Mom, where does the teddy bear go?" She showed her mom the toy. Her mom smiled. "The teddy bear can go in any group you want, Lily. It is a special toy. It can be your friend." Lily thought for a moment. She was shy and did not have many friends. She liked the teddy bear. It had soft fur and a cute nose. She decided to keep it with her. She hugged the teddy bear. "You are my friend, teddy bear. You can go with me everywhere." She said. The teddy bear did not say anything, but it seemed to smile. Lily was happy. She had a new friend. She did not need to sort it. She loved it.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely farm. On this farm, there was a girl named Amy. Amy liked to plant beans. One day, Amy went to the garden with her beans. She was ready to plant them. As she walked, she did not see a hole in the ground. She tripped and dropped all the beans into the hole. "Oh no," said Amy, "my beans are gone." Amy went back home feeling sad. The next day, she went to the garden to see the hole. To her surprise, there was a bean plant growing out of the hole. It was the tallest bean plant she had ever seen. Amy was so happy that her beans had grown into a big plant. And that is how Amy's lovely farm got a magical bean plant.
