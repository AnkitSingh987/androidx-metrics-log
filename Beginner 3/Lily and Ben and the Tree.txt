Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Tree

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They saw a big tree with a long trunk. Lily said, "Let's pretend the trunk is a rocket. We can shoot to the moon." Ben nodded and ran to the trunk. He climbed on it and held on tight. Lily followed him and sat behind him. She said, "Ready, set, go!" She made a loud noise with her mouth. "Boom! We are shooting to the moon!" Ben laughed and said, "Look, there are stars and planets!" They pretended to see many things in the sky. But they did not see the big dog that came to the park. The dog was angry and hungry. He saw the children on the tree and barked. He ran to the trunk and bit it hard. The trunk cracked and broke. Lily and Ben fell down with a thud. They cried and hurt. The dog ran away. A man and a woman heard the noise and came to help. They were Lily and Ben's parents. They saw the broken trunk and the children on the ground. They were scared and sad. They hugged Lily and Ben and took them to the doctor. The doctor said they had bruises and cuts, but they would be okay. He gave them bandages and ice cream. Lily and Ben learned a lesson. They should not play on a trunk that can break. They should be careful and watch out for dogs. They said sorry to their parents and the tree. They hoped the tree would grow a new trunk. They did not want to shoot to the moon anymore.

Lily and Ben were playing in the snow. They made a big snowman with a hat and a scarf. They gave him a carrot nose and two rocks for eyes. They were very happy. "Look, Mommy! We made a snowman!" Lily shouted. Mommy came out of the house. She smiled and hugged them. "You did a great job, my darlings. Do you want to come inside and have some hot chocolate?" "Yes, please!" Ben said. They ran to the door, but Lily stopped. She looked at the snowman. "Wait, Mommy. Can we give the snowman a present?" Mommy nodded. "Sure, honey. What do you want to give him?" Lily thought for a moment. She saw a red ribbon on the porch. She picked it up and ran back to the snowman. She tied the ribbon around his neck. "There, now he has a present. He will like it." She kissed the snowman on the cheek. "Bye, snowman. We will come back and play with you later." She joined Mommy and Ben and went inside the house. The snowman stood still in the yard. He felt the ribbon on his neck. He felt the carrot on his nose. He felt the rocks on his face. He felt the cold in his body. He felt nothing else. He was frozen. He was dead. He did not know what a present was. He did not know what a kiss was. He did not know what play was. He did not know what happy was. He did not know anything. He was just a snowman.

One day, a little bird wanted to fly high in the sky. He saw a brilliant red kite up in the air. The bird thought it would be fun to play with the kite. So, he flapped his wings and flew up to the kite. "Hi, kite! Let's play together," the bird said. The kite did not talk, but it danced in the sky. The bird was happy to have a friend to play with. They danced and played in the sky, and it was a beautiful sight. After a while, the bird got tired. He said, "Bye, kite! I need to go home now." The bird flew back to his nest and told his family about his fun day with the brilliant red kite. They all wished they could have seen the sight too.

Once upon a time, in a small forest, there lived a little bird named Billy. Billy was very organized. He liked to keep his nest clean and tidy. One day, Billy found a twig on the ground. He thought it would be perfect for his nest. Billy took the twig and flew back to his nest. When he got there, he saw that his nest was messy. He decided to fold his leaves and twigs to make his nest neat again. As he was folding, his friend, Sally the squirrel, came by. "Hi, Billy!" said Sally. "What are you doing?" "I found a new twig and I'm folding my nest to make it organized," Billy replied. "That's a great idea!" said Sally. "Can I help you?" "Yes, please!" said Billy. So, Billy and Sally worked together to fold the leaves and twigs in the nest. When they were done, the nest was very organized and looked beautiful. They were both very happy and proud of their work.
