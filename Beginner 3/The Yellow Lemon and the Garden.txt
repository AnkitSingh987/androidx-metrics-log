Category: Beginner
Title: The Yellow Lemon and the Garden

The little girl was walking in the garden when she saw a big yellow lemon. It looked so juicy and interesting! She wanted to touch it. She reached out her hand but then her mother warned her. She said "No, hot!" The little girl's hand was very close to the lemon, but she stopped. She knew that when her mother warned her, it was serious. She had learned that it was important to listen to her mother's warnings. The little girl stepped away from the lemon and stared at it. It looked so tempting, but she knew it was too hot for her to touch. She watched it for a little while, until her mother said it was time to go inside. The little girl was sad. She didn't get to touch the lemon, but she was glad she had listened to her mother's warning. Her mother always knew best!

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park to play. She brought her toy car and her toy dog with her. Sue loved to play with her toy car and make it go fast. She also liked to play with her toy dog and pretend like it could talk. While Sue was playing, she saw a boy named Tim. Tim had a jar of jam with him. Sue went over to Tim and asked, "Can I play with your jam?" Tim said, "Yes, but be careful. The jar is tough to open." Sue tried to open the jar, but it was very tough. She tried and tried, but she couldn't open it. Sue and Tim decided to play together. They played with Sue's toy car and toy dog. They also played with Tim's jam, even though they couldn't open the jar. They had a lot of fun playing together at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a messy caterpillar. He lived in a big tree. He liked to eat leaves all day. His name was Sam. One day, Sam found a box. It was a mail box. Sam wanted to send a letter to his friend, the butterfly. He wrote, "I miss you. Come visit me." Sam put the letter in the mail box. He was very happy. Soon, his butterfly friend came to see him. They played and laughed all day. Sam was not messy anymore.

Sam was out on the golf course with his dad. They had just set out on Sam's first golfing trip together. Sam was interested in the activity and asked his dad; "What is golf?". Dad said, "Golf is when we use a special stick to hit a white ball into a hole. Look at the green grass, that is the golf course". Sam was excited. He wanted to try it. He saw the white ball in the hole and wanted to get it. Dad held out a special golf club, it had a sharp edge. "This is called a golf club, I'm going to cut the ball out of the hole for you," Dad said. Sam watched in fascination. Dad lined up the golf club and made a swift slice - he had cut the white ball out. Dad then handed the ball to Sam and said, "This is yours, now you try". Sam was overwhelmed - so much had happened! It was his turn to try and he couldn't wait to see where the white ball would go. He lined up the golf club and made the same swift slice - he had cut the ball out perfectly. Sam smiled in delight - he had made the cut! With this success, he knew he had the potential to become a great golfer. It would only be a matter of time.

Once upon a time, in the evening, there was a playful cat. The cat loved to play outside. The cat had a big yard with lots of fun things to do. One day, the playful cat saw a ball. The cat ran to the ball very fast. The cat wanted to play with the ball. But, the ground was wet from the rain. The playful cat did not see the wet ground. The cat tried to stop but it could not. The cat began to slip on the wet ground. The cat slipped and fell on the grass. The playful cat was okay. The cat got up and shook the water off. The cat learned to be more careful when playing on wet ground. The cat played with the ball and had lots of fun.
