Title: Lily and Tom and the Park

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play music. They have a band. Lily plays the drum. Tom plays the guitar. They sing songs. One day, they go to the park. They see a man. He is bald. He has no hair on his head. He has a big hat. He plays the saxophone. He is very good. "Hello, bald man," Lily says. "Can we play with you?" The bald man smiles. He says, "Sure, little ones. What is your band's name?" Lily and Tom think. They say, "We are the Rock Stars!" The bald man laughs. He says, "That is a great name. I am Jazz. Do you want to learn some jazz music?" Lily and Tom nod. They say, "Yes, please!" Jazz teaches them some jazz notes. They play together. They make a new sound. It is fun. They are happy. People in the park hear them. They clap and cheer. They like the music. They like the band. They like Lily, Tom and Jazz. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little bug. The bug was weak and it liked to crawl. It had a small bag that it carried on its back. The bug wanted to find food to eat. One day, the bug saw a big apple. It was so happy! The bug crawled to the apple with its bag on its back. The apple was on the ground and it was very big. The bug crawled up the apple and started to eat. But then, a big bird saw the bug. The bird was hungry too. It flew down to the apple and saw the weak bug. The bug tried to crawl away, but it was too slow. The bird caught the bug and ate it. The bug's bag was left on the ground, all alone.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom lived in a small house near a subway. Every day, Tom would go out to play with his friends. One day, it rained a lot and Tom got all wet. He did not like being wet and wanted to be dry. Tom had an idea. He went into the subway to get dry. Inside the subway, he met a little mouse named Jerry. Jerry was also wet and wanted to get dry. Tom and Jerry decided to help each other get dry by sharing a big towel. As they got dry, Tom and Jerry became friends. They played together in the subway and had lots of fun. Soon, the rain stopped, and they went outside to play with their other friends. All the animals saw Tom and Jerry playing together and they learned that it is good to help each other. The moral of the story is: Helping others can make new friends and bring happiness to everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She loved to take walks in the park with her dog, Max. One sunny day, Mia and Max went for a walk together. Mia was always very careful when she walked with Max, holding his leash tightly. As they walked, they saw a big tree. Mia said, "Max, let's turn around the tree!" They both turned and walked around the tree, having lots of fun. Max wagged his tail, and Mia laughed happily. After their walk, they met Mia's friend, Tom. Tom said, "Hi Mia, did you have a fun walk?" Mia smiled and replied, "Yes, we did! We were very careful and turned around a big tree. It was so much fun!" Tom laughed and said, "That sounds great! Maybe next time, we can all walk together!"

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They were looking for food to make a yummy meal. Tim saw a big, brown avocado. He picked it up and showed it to his mom. "Mom, can we get this avocado?" Tim asked. His mom looked at the avocado and smiled. "Yes, we can get it," she answered. They took the brown avocado home and made a tasty snack. Tim was happy to share the snack with his mom. They both loved the avocado and had a great time eating it together.
