Category: Beginner
Title: Abigail and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little girl and her name was Abigail. She had a special tray that she kept all her toys in. One day Abigail wanted to play with her toys so she opened the tray. But to her surprise, all the toys were gone, the tray was empty! Abigail was sad so she ran to tell her mommy. Mommy listened carefully and then came up with an idea. Together the two of them went to the store and found a color toy car. They brought it home and put it in the tray. Abigail was so excited to play, she grabbed the car and started to zip around the room. She had so much fun with her new toy car!

Once upon a time, there was a humble cat. The cat liked to lean on a big tree. The tree was in a pretty park. One day, the cat found a book under the tree. The book was a novel. The cat could not read, but it liked to look at the pictures. The cat showed the novel to a little girl in the park. The girl could read. She read the novel to the cat. They both liked the story. They became good friends and read the novel together every day under the big tree.

Bella is a dog. She likes to run and play with her owner, Sam. Sam is a boy. He loves Bella very much. He gives her food and water and toys. One day, Sam and Bella go to the park. They have fun. They chase a ball and a stick. They meet other dogs and boys. They get very dirty and sweaty. Sam says, "Bella, we need to bathe. You are very dirty and smelly. I am too. Come on, let's go home." Bella nods. She likes to bathe. She likes the water and the soap. She likes to be clean and fresh. Sam and Bella go home. They go to the bathroom. Sam fills the tub with water. He checks the water. It is not too cold and not too hot. It is just right. He puts some soap in the water. He says, "Bella, get in the tub." Bella jumps in the tub. She splashes and wags her tail. Sam gets in the tub too. He rubs Bella with a sponge. He makes bubbles and foam. He says, "Bella, you are a good dog. You are my best friend." Bella licks Sam's face. She says, "Sam, you are a good boy. You are my best friend too." Sam and Bella bathe. They rinse and dry. They smell good and feel good. They are happy. They hug and cuddle. They say, "We love each other." They are a good owner and a good dog.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Amy. Amy had a soft skirt that she loved very much. She wore the skirt every day. One day, she saw a cat stuck in a tree. Amy wanted to save the cat, but she didn't know how. Amy thought about how she could save the cat. She looked at her soft skirt and had an idea. She took off her skirt and tied it to the tree. Then, she climbed up the tree using her skirt like a rope. When Amy reached the cat, she held it tight and climbed back down using her skirt. The cat was happy to be saved. Amy was happy too, because her soft skirt helped her save the cat. From that day on, Amy and the cat were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a mild and sunny land, there was a king. The king had a shiny crown on his head. The crown made him feel very special. One day, he was walking in the forest and saw a small bird. The bird said, "Listen to me, king. I have something to tell you." The king stopped and listened to the bird. The bird told the king that there was a big party in the forest that night. All the animals were going to be there. The king went to the party with the bird. He saw many animals and they all wore crowns too. They danced and played all night. The king was very happy to be with his new friends. In the end, the king said, "Thank you, bird, for telling me about the party. I had a lot of fun!"
