Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Medal

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was scared of the dark. One day, his big sister, Sarah, gave him a fake medal. She said, "This medal will keep you safe. It will scare the dark away." At night, Tim wore the medal to bed. He felt brave and not scared anymore. The dark did not scare him now. He said, "Thank you, Sarah. I am brave with my medal." One night, Tim heard a noise. He was scared, but he had his medal. He went to see what made the noise. It was a cat! The cat was playing with the medal. Tim laughed and said, "Silly cat, you can play with my medal too. We are not scared of the dark now."

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max liked to play with his board. One day, he played too hard and hurt his paw. Max felt sad and slow because he could not play anymore. Max's friend, Anna the cat, saw him feeling sad. She knew what to do to help him. She told Max to rest and be slow, so his paw would heal. Max listened to Anna and stayed slow and quiet. After a few days, Max's paw began to heal. He felt happy and fast again. Max and Anna played with the board together. They both learned that sometimes it is good to be slow and let things heal.

Lily liked to help her mom clean the house. She had a small spray bottle with water and a cloth. She followed her mom to the bathroom. Mom sprayed the sink and the mirror with a big bottle. Lily sprayed the floor and the toilet with her small bottle. She wiped them with her cloth. "Good job, Lily!" Mom said. "You are very thoughtful. You help me a lot." Lily smiled. She liked to make Mom happy. She saw a spider on the wall. She did not like spiders. She sprayed the spider with her water. The spider fell down. Lily screamed. Mom came to see what was wrong. She saw the spider on the floor. She picked it up with a tissue and threw it in the trash. "Don't be scared, Lily. It's just a spider. It can't hurt you." Mom said. She hugged Lily and kissed her cheek. Lily felt better. She was brave. She sprayed the wall again. She wiped it with her cloth. She was thoughtful. She helped Mom clean the bathroom.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a band. The band was very organized. They played nice songs for everyone. People loved to listen to them. The band had a big drum, a small guitar, and a shiny trumpet. One day, a new man came to town. He did not like the band. He would complain every time they played. He would say, "Stop! I don't like this sound!" The band was sad. They did not know what to do. They wanted everyone to be happy. Then, something unexpected happened. The man who complained found a little cat. The cat was lost and scared. The band played a soft, sweet song for the cat. The cat stopped being scared and went to the man. The man was so happy. He said, "Thank you, band. I like this song." From that day on, the man and the band became friends, and they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a cat and a dog had a quarrel. They were very mad at each other. The cat said, "You are too loud!" The dog said, "You are too lazy!" Soon, all the animals had a meeting. They wanted to stop the quarrel. The animals were envious of the cat and dog's friendship. They liked how they played together. The animals said, "We want to be friends like you!" At the meeting, the cat and the dog saw a big cake. They both wanted the cake. They forgot their quarrel and ran to the cake. But, the cake was a trick! It was a big, soft pillow. The cat and the dog fell on the pillow and laughed. They were friends again.
