Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Boy

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Sue. One day, Sue and her mom went for a walk in the snow. Sue was very cold, so she started to run and jump around trying to warm up. Suddenly, Sue saw a boy she didn't recognize. He was standing still and had a troubled look on his face. Her mom went over to him and asked him why he was so troubled. The boy replied, "I'm so cold, I'm ready to quit!" Sue was so surprised to hear this that she ran over to the boy and put her small hands on his hands. She said, "We don't quit when we're faced with hard times, we get help and be brave!" The boy looked into Sue's eyes and nodded. He thanked her and they were both very warm from then on. The moral of the story is that no matter what you face, never quit and always ask for help when you need it.

Once upon a time, there was a big building. It was a fun place where kids could play. One day, a boy named Tim went there with his mom. Tim saw a funny man in the building. The man had a spicy red hat. Tim asked the man, "Why is your hat spicy?" The man just gave a little shrug and smiled. Tim and the man played all day. They laughed and had fun. When it was time to go, Tim waved bye to his new friend. He couldn't wait to come back to the building again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Tara. She was only three years old. Tara was playing in her garden when she found a special net. She thought it would be fun to play with it. But then, her mom came out and ordered her to put it away. Tara was embarrassed because she wasn't supposed to play with it. She quickly put it away and ran away to hide in her room. Her mom found her there and asked why she was so embarrassed. Tara told her mom that she had been playing with the net and mom scolded her. But then mom smiled and said that it was alright. She explained that sometimes it was good to play with new things. Tara was happy and happy to hear that her mom wasn't mad.

Once upon a time, there was a warm little house. In this house lived a boy named Mark. Mark had a pocket on his shirt. He loved his pocket very much. One day, Mark's friend, Tim, came to play. They played with their toys and had lots of fun. Mark showed Tim his pocket. "Look, Tim, I have a pocket on my shirt!" said Mark. Tim smiled and said, "Wow! That's a nice pocket!" Later, they went outside to play in the sun. Mark put a small toy in his pocket. They ran and laughed together. Mark's pocket kept his toy safe and warm. At the end of the day, Tim went home and Mark went back to his warm little house. Mark was happy he had a pocket and a good friend like Tim.

Once upon a time, there was a noisy farm. On the farm, there was a big red barn. Inside the barn, there were many animals. There was a cow, a pig, a horse, and a little yellow chick. One day, the little chick found a piece of corn. The chick wanted to attach the corn to her nest. So, she asked her friend, the pig, "Can you help me attach this corn to my nest?" The pig said, "Yes, I will help you." They worked together to attach the corn to the chick's nest. The pig held the corn, and the chick used her beak to attach it. When they were done, the chick was very happy. The pig said, "Now your nest is ready for a surprise!" Later, the other animals came to see the chick's nest. They saw the corn attached to the nest and knew it was a special day. They all sang "Happy Birthday" to the chick, and she was so happy. The corn on her nest was a fun surprise for her birthday.
