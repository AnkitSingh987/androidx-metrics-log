Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Hill

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play outside. One day, they saw a big hill near their house. They wanted to climb it and see what was on the other side. "Let's go, Lily!" Tom said. "Maybe we can find something fun." "OK, Tom!" Lily said. "But we have to be careful. The hill is very high and steep." They put on their shoes and hats and ran to the hill. They started to climb, holding hands and helping each other. They saw many rocks, flowers, and bugs on the way. They also heard some birds singing and some cows mooing. They reached the top of the hill and looked around. They saw a beautiful view of the fields, the trees, and the sky. They also saw a big lake with some ducks and a boat. "Wow, Tom! Look at the view!" Lily said. "It is so pretty and shiny!" "Yes, Lily! Look at the lake!" Tom said. "It is so blue and calm!" They sat on the grass and watched the view. They felt happy and proud. They had climbed the hill by themselves. They decided to gather some flowers and rocks to take home. They picked some yellow, pink, and purple flowers and some smooth, shiny, and colorful rocks. They put them in their pockets and in their hats. They were ready to go back when they heard a loud noise. It sounded like a horn. They looked at the lake and saw a big boat coming closer. It had a flag and some people on it. "Tom, what is that?" Lily asked. "Is it a pirate boat?" "I don't know, Lily," Tom said. "I have never seen a boat like that before." They felt confused and scared. They did not know who the people were or what they wanted. They did not know if they were friendly or mean. They grabbed each other's hands and ran down the hill. They wanted to go back to their house and tell their mom and dad what they had seen. They hoped the boat would not follow them or hurt them. They ran as fast as they could, holding their flowers and rocks. They did not look back at the lake or the boat. They only looked at their house and their parents, who were waiting for them with a hug and a smile.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a gentle giant named Tom. Tom liked to help people with their work. He was very strong and could do many things. One day, Tom met a little boy named Tim. Tim was sad because his toy house was destroyed. Tom wanted to help Tim and said, "Don't worry, I will fix your toy house." Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Tom!" Tom worked hard to fix Tim's toy house. He was very careful not to destroy anything else. Soon, the toy house was as good as new. Tim was so happy and said, "You are the best, Tom!" From that day on, Tom and Tim became the best of friends, and they played and worked together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a girl. She liked to play games with her friends. She was very competitive. She always wanted to win. One day, the girl and her friends played a race game. They ran very fast. The girl was in front, but then something unexpected happened. A little dog joined the race. The dog was fast too. The girl did not quit. She ran faster and faster. But the little dog was still in front. In the end, the dog won the race. The girl was not sad. She laughed and played with the dog. From that day, the girl and the dog became best friends.

One day, a little girl named Mia was playing in her room. She saw a big cupboard near her bed. Mia was curious and wanted to see what was inside. She opened the cupboard door and saw a lot of toys and clothes. But then, something magical happened. A fair fairy appeared from the back of the cupboard. "Hello, Mia!" said the fairy. "I am your new friend. My name is Lily. I came from the land of fairies to play with you." Mia was very happy. She played with Lily all day long. They played with the toys and tried on the clothes from the cupboard. They laughed and had so much fun. Mia was glad that she found a new friend in her cupboard. At the end of the day, Lily said, "Mia, I have to go back to the land of fairies now. But I will come back to play with you again soon." Mia was sad, but she knew that she would see Lily again. She said goodbye to her new friend and closed the cupboard door. Mia was excited for the next time Lily would appear to play with her.
