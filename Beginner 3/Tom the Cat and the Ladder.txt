Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Cat and the Ladder

One day, a chubby cat named Tom saw a pretty vase. The vase was on a high shelf. Tom wanted to play with the vase, but he could not reach it. He thought and thought about how to get the vase. Tom saw his friend, a bird named Sue. Tom asked Sue, "Can you help me get the pretty vase from the high shelf?" Sue said, "I will try to help you, Tom." Sue flew up and tried to get the vase, but it was too heavy for her. Tom had an idea. He said, "Sue, let's guess how we can get the vase down." Sue said, "Okay, let's guess." Tom guessed that if he jumped on the shelf, he could get the vase. Sue guessed that if they found a long stick, they could push the vase down. They tried both guesses, but none worked. Then, they saw a tall ladder. They climbed up the ladder together and got the vase down. Tom and Sue were happy because they worked together and solved the problem.

Tom and Mia are at the park with Mom. They see a big fountain with water that goes up and down. The water is shiny and makes a nice sound. "Look, Mom, look!" Tom says. "Can we go near the fountain?" "Sure, but be careful not to get wet," Mom says. She holds their hands and walks with them to the fountain. Tom and Mia feel the water spray on their faces. It is cool and refreshing. They laugh and try to catch the drops. "Wow, this is fun!" Mia says. "The fountain is cool!" "I like the fountain too," Tom says. "Can we make a wish?" "A wish? What is that?" Mia asks. Tom explains that a wish is something you want to happen. He says that if you throw a coin in the fountain and close your eyes, your wish might come true. "Really? Let's try it!" Mia says. She finds a coin in her pocket and gives it to Tom. Tom and Mia each hold the coin and close their eyes. They think of something they want. Then they toss the coin in the fountain and open their eyes. "What did you wish for?" Tom asks Mia. "I wished for a new doll," Mia says. "What about you?" "I wished for a big ice cream," Tom says. Mom smiles and hugs them. She says that wishes are nice, but sometimes they don't come true. She says that they should be happy with what they have and enjoy the moment. Tom and Mia agree. They say that they are happy with the fountain and with Mom. They hug her back and thank her for taking them to the park. They watch the fountain for a while and then go to play on the swings.

One day, a big, honest dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a square box on the ground. Max was curious and wanted to know what was inside the box. So, he went closer and sniffed the box. Max's friend, a small cat named Lily, came by and saw Max with the box. Lily asked, "What is that, Max?" Max said, "I don't know, but I want to open it." Lily thought for a moment and then suggested, "Maybe we can push it to open it." Together, they pushed the square box, and something unexpected happened. The box opened, and out came a lot of colorful balloons. The balloons flew up into the sky, and Max and Lily were very surprised. They laughed and played with the balloons, having a great time.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy cat named Tom. Tom lived in a big house with his best friend, a little girl named Sue. One day, Tom and Sue were playing outside. They saw a big dog who liked to tease them. The dog said, "You can't catch me!" and ran away. Tom and Sue wanted to show the dog that they were not scared. So, they ran after the dog to catch him. As they ran, they saw a police car coming down the street. The police car stopped, and a nice police officer got out. He said, "I saw the dog teasing you. I will help you catch him." Tom, Sue, and the police officer worked together to catch the dog. When they caught the dog, they found out that he was just lonely and wanted to play. The dog said, "I'm sorry for teasing you. I just wanted to be friends." Tom, Sue, and the police officer all became friends with the dog, and they played together every day. And they all lived happily ever after.
