Category: Beginner
Title: Lila and Ben and the Dog

Lila and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big dog. The dog is brown and has a long tail. The dog looks at them and barks. Lila is scared of the dog. She thinks the dog is mean. She wants to run away. But Ben is not scared. He thinks the dog is nice. He wants to pet the dog. He says to Lila, "Don't be afraid. The dog is friendly. Let's go and say hello." Lila does not want to go. But she wants to be polite. She does not want to make Ben sad. She says to Ben, "OK, I will go with you. But we have to be careful. The dog might bite." They walk slowly to the dog. The dog sees them and barks louder. Lila and Ben stop. They smile at the dog. They say, "Hi, dog. We are Lila and Ben. We want to be your friends." The dog does not like Lila and Ben. The dog does not want to be their friend. The dog is angry. The dog jumps at them and bites them. Lila and Ben scream. They are hurt. They cry. A man hears them and runs to help. He is the dog's owner. He says, "I'm sorry. My dog is not polite. He does not like strangers. You should not have come near him. You should have stayed away." Lila and Ben are sad and mad. They say, "You are not polite either. You should have kept your dog on a leash. You should have told us to stay away. You are a bad owner. You and your dog are mean." The man feels bad. He says, "I'm sorry. I will take you to the doctor. I will pay for your medicine. I will teach my dog to be polite. Please forgive me." But Lila and Ben do not forgive him. They do not want to see him or his dog again. They go to the doctor. They get medicine. They go home. They do not play in the park anymore. They are afraid of dogs. They are not happy. The end.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to school. She was a pupil in a big, blue room. Lucy had a yummy snack in her bag. It was a big, red apple. At snack time, Lucy looked for her yummy apple, but it was gone. It disappeared! She was sad and asked her friend, Tom, "Did you see my yummy apple?" Tom said, "No, I did not see it. But let's look for it together." They looked everywhere. They looked under the table, in their bags, and behind the door. Then, Lucy saw her apple on the teacher's desk. She was so happy! She said, "Thank you, Tom, for helping me find my yummy apple!" They both smiled and ate their snacks together.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to speak to his friends. He would speak to the birds, the cats, and even the bugs. Max was a very happy dog. One day, Max's best friend, a little bird named Lucy, flew away. Max was sad and did not know where she went. He looked left, he looked right, but he could not find her. He called out to her, but she did not come back. Max felt angry because he missed Lucy so much. He did not want to speak to anyone. But then, one sunny day, Lucy came back. She had been away to find food for her babies. Max was so happy to see her, and they played all day. From that day on, Max and Lucy were always together, and Max was never angry again.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to play with his favorite toy, a big bone. One day, he saw an impressive, shiny new bone in the yard. He wanted to grab it and show it to his best friend, a little cat named Kitty. "Kitty, look at my new bone!" Buddy said to his friend. Kitty was very happy for Buddy. "Wow, Buddy! That's a nice bone!" Kitty said. They both played with the bone and had lots of fun. Later, Buddy and Kitty took a nap under a big tree. Buddy was so happy to have his new bone and his best friend Kitty. They had a great day playing together and sharing the impressive bone.
