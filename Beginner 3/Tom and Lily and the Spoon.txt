Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Spoon

Tom and Lily were having lunch with Mom. They had yummy soup and bread. Mom gave them spoons to eat the soup. But Tom wanted to use a fork. He said, "Fork, fork, where is fork?" Mom said, "Tom, you don't need a fork for soup. A fork has sharp points. It can hurt your mouth. Use a spoon, please." But Tom did not listen. He got up from his chair and started to search for a fork. He looked under the table, in the drawers, in the cupboards. He could not find a fork. He was sad and angry. Lily saw Tom and laughed. She said, "Tom, you are silly. You can't eat soup with a fork. A fork is for salad or pasta or cake. A spoon is for soup or yogurt or ice cream. Here, have my spoon. I will share with you." Tom looked at Lily and smiled. He said, "Thank you, Lily. You are nice. I will use your spoon. And I will give you my bread. We can share too." Mom saw Tom and Lily and smiled too. She said, "That's very kind of you, Tom and Lily. You are good siblings. Now, finish your lunch and then we can have some cookies for dessert."

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy hammer all day. One day, Tim's mom told him, "Tim, you need to put away your hammer and eat your lunch." Tim did not want to stop playing. He said, "No, I want to play more!" He kept hitting things with his hammer. But then, he hit his finger by mistake. "Ouch!" he cried. Tim's finger hurt a lot. His mom came over and said, "Oh no, you suffer because you did not listen." Tim's mom gave him a hug and helped him feel better. She said, "Next time, you should listen when I tell you to stop playing." Tim learned his lesson and promised to be less stubborn. From that day on, he always listened to his mom and took breaks when she asked him to. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, Mamma and Baby were playing in the garden. It was a hot day, so Baby was feeling tired. Mamma said “Let’s go inside and have a nap.” They went inside and Mamma drew the curtains. Baby looked up and said “What’s that curtain?” Mamma said “It is to stop the sun from shining in and to make it dark.” Mamma lay down on the bed and said “Come and lie down with me and we can both have a nap.” Baby lay down and said “I won’t forget the curtain.” Mamma smiled and said “Well, don’t forget to close your eyes!” They both closed their eyes and soon Baby was fast asleep. Mamma was so tired, she forgot to close the curtains. The sun shone into the room, but Baby was still sleeping. Mamma smiled and closed the curtains. They both slept until it was time for dinner.

Once upon a time, in a big red barn, there lived a little chick named Chirpy. Chirpy had many friends, like the cow, the pig, and the horse. They all played together every day. One sunny day, Chirpy found a sharp rock near the barn. Chirpy showed it to the cow. "Look, Cow! I found a sharp rock," Chirpy said. Cow looked at the rock and said, "Be careful, Chirpy. It might hurt you." The next day, the ground was very dry. Chirpy and his friends were thirsty. Chirpy had an idea. "Let's use the sharp rock to dig a hole, and maybe we'll find water," Chirpy said. All the friends agreed and started to dig. After a while, they found water in the hole. They all drank the water and felt happy. "Thank you, Chirpy, for finding the sharp rock and helping us get water on this dry day," said Pig. Chirpy and his friends played and drank water all day long, and they lived happily in the big red barn.

Ben and Lily liked to play with their toys. They had many toys, but their favorite was a big truck. The truck had four wheels, a horn, and a trailer. They liked to fill the trailer with blocks, dolls, and cars. One day, they were playing with the truck in the living room. They drove the truck around the sofa, the table, and the rug. They made loud noises with their mouths. Vroom, vroom, beep, beep! But then, something bad happened. The truck hit a chair and the trailer broke. One of the wheels came off and the trailer fell apart. Ben and Lily were sad. They loved their truck. How could they fix it? They looked around for something to help them. They saw a roll of tape on the shelf. Tape was sticky and strong. Maybe they could use tape to save their truck. They asked their mom if they could have the tape. Their mom said yes, but be careful. Ben and Lily took the tape and went back to the truck. They worked hard to put the wheel and the trailer back together. They used a lot of tape. They wrapped tape around the wheel, the trailer, and the truck. They were persistent. They did not give up. Finally, they finished. They looked at their truck. It was not the same as before. It had tape all over it. But it was still their truck. And it still worked. They tried to drive it around the living room. It moved slowly and wobbled a bit. But it did not break again. Ben and Lily were happy. They hugged their truck. They thanked the tape. They had saved their truck. They continued to play with it. They made loud noises with their mouths. Vroom, vroom, beep, beep!
