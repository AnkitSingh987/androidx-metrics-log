Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Rock and the Train

Once upon a time, there was a big, ugly rock. This rock was near a train track. Every day, the train would go by and let out steam. The ugly rock did not like the steam. It made the rock feel wet and cold. One day, a little boy named Tim came to play near the train track. He saw the ugly rock and wanted to kick it. He thought it would be fun. But when he tried to kick the rock, it did not move. Tim was sad and went home. The next day, Tim came back to the train track. He saw the train coming and thought of a plan. He thought if he kicked the rock when the train was close, the steam would help move the rock. But when Tim kicked the rock, it still did not move. The train went by very fast and the steam hit Tim. He fell down and got hurt. Tim learned that it was not a good idea to play near the train track.

One day, a boy named Tom went to the store with his mom. He saw gum on the shelf and he really wanted it. Tom asked his mom, "Can I have the gum, please?" His mom said yes because she was very generous. Tom was so happy and said, "Thank you, mom!" When they got home, Tom opened the gum and started to chew it. He shared the gum with his sister, Jane. They both liked the gum a lot. Suddenly, the gum made a big pop sound in their mouths. They looked at each other and started to laugh. They did not know the gum could do that. Tom and Jane played with the gum all day. They found out that the gum could make different sounds. They had so much fun with the gum. In the end, the gum taught them that even small things can be full of surprises.

Sara loved to play in the garden. She liked to smell the flowers, chase the butterflies and dig in the dirt. She had a lot of fun with her toys and her dog, Spot. One day, Sara saw a big spider in the garden. It was black and hairy and had eight legs. Sara was very scared of spiders. She screamed and ran away. She dropped her doll and her shovel. She wanted her mom to help her. Mom heard Sara scream and came out of the house. She saw the spider and the toys. She was not scared of spiders. She picked up the doll and the shovel and gave them to Sara. She said, "Don't worry, Sara. The spider is not going to hurt you. It is just looking for food and a home. It is more afraid of you than you are of it." Sara looked at the spider and saw that it was not so big and scary. It was just a little creature. She felt sorry for it. She said, "I'm sorry, spider. I didn't mean to scream. You can stay in the garden if you want. But please don't come too close to me." The spider nodded and crawled away. Sara hugged her mom and her toys. She felt better. She said, "Thank you, mom. You are very brave and kind. Can we play in the garden some more?" Mom smiled and said, "Of course, Sara. The garden is a beautiful place to play. But remember to be gentle and respectful to all the living things in it. They are part of nature and deserve our care." Sara nodded and said, "I will, mom. I love the garden and I love you." They played in the garden until it was time for lunch. They had a happy and peaceful day.

One day, a little boy named Tim wondered what was in the big freezer. He asked his mom, "Mom, what's in the freezer?" His mom was very compassionate and said, "It's a surprise, Tim. You'll see soon." Tim really wanted to know what was in the freezer. He waited until his mom was not looking and opened the freezer. Inside, he saw a big ice cream cake. Tim was so happy and decided to eat some. As he ate the ice cream cake, Tim heard his mom coming. He quickly tried to put the cake back in the freezer. But, it was too late. His mom saw the mess and was very sad. Tim felt bad for not waiting and now the surprise was ruined.
