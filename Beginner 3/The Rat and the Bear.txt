Category: Beginner
Title: The Rat and the Bear

Once upon a time there was a small rat. This rat was very stubborn and always wanted to do things his own way. One day, he was exploring in the forest looking for food. Suddenly he saw a huge bear. He was so scared that he began to tremble. "P-p-please don't hurt me," stammered the rat. The bear just looked at him and chuckled. "You are very brave for a tiny rat," he said. The rat was surprised. He stopped trembling and looked at the bear. "I'm not brave. I'm just stubborn," he said. The bear smiled. "Well, that stubbornness may be the thing that saves you. Now go on your way, brave little rat." The rat thanked the bear and scurried off. From that day on, he always remembered the bear's kind words. He never forgot how his stubbornness made him brave.

Sara and Tom are friends. They like to play together. Today, they go to the park with their moms. They see a big slide, a swing, and a sandbox. They are eager to have fun. "Can we play on the slide, Mom?" Sara asks. "Sure, honey. But be careful and take turns," Mom says. Sara and Tom run to the slide. They climb up the ladder and slide down. They laugh and scream. They do it again and again. "Look, a peanut!" Tom says. He sees a peanut on the ground. He picks it up and shows it to Sara. "Wow, a peanut! Can I have it?" Sara says. "No, it's mine. I found it," Tom says. "But I want it. It looks yummy," Sara says. They start to fight over the peanut. They pull and tug. They shout and cry. "Hey, hey, stop that!" Mom says. She hears the noise and comes over. She sees the peanut in their hands. "What's going on here? Why are you fighting over a peanut?" Mom says. "Sara wants my peanut. But I found it first," Tom says. "No, I want it. It's not fair," Sara says. Mom sighs. She takes the peanut from them. She breaks it in half. She gives one half to Sara and one half to Tom. "Here, now you both have a peanut. Can you share and be nice?" Mom says. Sara and Tom look at their peanut halves. They feel sorry. They say sorry to each other. "Sorry, Tom. I was mean," Sara says. "Sorry, Sara. I was greedy," Tom says. They hug and smile. They eat their peanuts. They are happy again. "Can we play on the swing now?" Sara asks. "Sure, let's go," Tom says. They run to the swing. They push each other. They fly high and low. They have fun. They are friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had an amazing toy, a big red ball. Lucy loved to play with her ball all day long. She would bounce it, throw it, and roll it around her yard. One day, while Lucy was playing with her ball, it rolled near a big rock. There was a crack in the rock. Lucy tried to get her ball back, but it was stuck in the crack. She was very sad and didn't know what to do. Lucy's big brother, Max, saw her crying. He wanted to help his sister, so he went to save the ball. Max pushed and pulled until the ball was free from the crack. Lucy was so happy! She hugged Max and thanked him for saving her amazing ball. They played together for the rest of the day, and Lucy was careful not to let her ball get stuck again.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with her ball and run around in the sun. One day, Kitty saw a cookie on the table. She wanted to eat it, but her mom said no. Kitty was sad, but she insisted on having the cookie. She asked her mom again and again. Her mom said, "Kitty, you can have the cookie after dinner." Kitty waited and played with her ball until dinner time. Finally, it was time for dinner. Kitty ate her food quickly and her mom gave her the cookie. She was so happy! The cookie was yummy and sweet. Kitty knew that waiting was a good thing.
