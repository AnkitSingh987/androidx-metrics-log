Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Squirrel

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk in the woods. He saw a big oak tree with a mysterious hole in it. Tim thought, "I wonder what's inside the hole." Tim saw a squirrel near the oak tree. He asked the squirrel, "What's in the hole?" The squirrel said, "I don't know, but I think it could be something special. Come back tomorrow, and you will see." The next day, Tim went back to the mysterious oak tree. He looked inside the hole and saw a shiny, pretty rock. Tim was so happy! He knew the squirrel was right. The special rock made Tim's day very fun.

Once upon a time, there was a famous road. Many people walked on it. One day, a big rock fell on the road. Everyone was scared. A little bird saw the rock. The bird wanted to help. The bird tried to push the rock but it was too heavy. The bird asked a strong horse to support her. The horse said yes. Together, the bird and the horse pushed the rock. But the rock was not a rock! It was a big egg. The egg opened and a baby dragon came out. The baby dragon became friends with the bird and the horse. They all played on the famous road.

One day, a clever cat named Tom was in his bedroom. He liked to play with his toys and run very fast. Tom had a best friend named Jerry, a little mouse. They would play together all day. One sunny day, Tom and Jerry were playing in the garden. Tom said, "Let's see who is faster!" They decided to race to the big tree. Tom was very fast, but Jerry was also quick. They both ran with all their might. As they sped towards the tree, something unexpected happened. A big bird swooped down and tried to catch Jerry! Tom saw this and quickly changed his direction. He ran faster than ever and jumped high in the air. He saved Jerry just in time. The bird flew away, and Tom and Jerry were safe. From that day on, they knew they would always help each other, no matter what.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy. His name was Tim. Tim had a dog. The dog's name was Spot. Tim and Spot liked to play outside. They played in the sun and in the rain. They were very good friends. One day, it was dark outside. Tim and Spot were not scared. They wanted to play. Tim put on his coat. Spot put on his leash. Tim lead Spot outside. They walked and walked. They saw the moon and stars in the dark sky. Then, they saw a big tree. Tim and Spot ran to the tree. They played hide and seek. Tim hid behind the tree. Spot found him. They laughed and played. Soon, it was time to go home. Tim lead Spot back to their warm house. They had a fun time playing in the dark.

Once upon a time, there was a big, wide tree. It lived in a pretty park with lots of grass and flowers. The tree loved to twist and turn its branches. It liked to exercise and stretch its leaves up to the sky. One sunny day, a little girl named Mia came to play near the tree. She saw the tree twisting its branches and thought it was fun. Mia started to twist and turn her body too. She was doing her own exercise, just like the tree. After a while, Mia and the tree became good friends. Every day, they would twist and exercise together. They had lots of fun, and the tree's branches grew even wider. Mia and the tree were happy, and they lived happily ever after.

Tom and Anna are twins. They like to play together. One day, they find a big box of clothes in the attic. They want to dress up and pretend. "Look, Anna, I found a white coat and a hat. I can be a doctor!" Tom says. "Wow, Tom, you look good. And I found a dress and a crown. I can be a princess!" Anna says. They put on the clothes and have fun. They pretend to be in a hospital and a castle. They laugh and run around. But then, Anna trips on the dress and falls down the stairs. She hits her head and cries. Tom runs after her and sees blood. He is scared. "Anna, Anna, are you okay?" Tom says. But Anna does not answer. She is still and quiet. Tom does not know what to do. He screams for help. Mom and Dad hear the scream and come fast. They see Anna and Tom. They are shocked and sad. They call 911 and take Anna to the hospital. Tom goes with them. He holds Anna's hand and hopes she will wake up. But Anna does not wake up. She is hurt very bad. The doctors try to help her, but they cannot. Anna dies. Tom is very sad. He wishes he did not play with the clothes. He wishes he could say sorry to Anna. He wishes he could hug her and tell her he loves her. But he cannot. He cries and cries. He misses Anna very much.
