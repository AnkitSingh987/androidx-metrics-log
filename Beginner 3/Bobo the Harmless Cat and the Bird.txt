Category: Beginner
Title: Bobo the Harmless Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a harmless cat named Bobo. Bobo liked to play with ink. One day, Bobo found a big bottle of ink in the house. He was very happy and started to play with it. Bobo's friend, a dog named Kiki, came to visit. Kiki saw Bobo playing with the ink and said, "Bobo, can I play with the ink too?" Bobo replied, "Yes, Kiki, we can play together!" So, they both played with the ink and had lots of fun. After a while, they saw a little bird who had hurt its wing. The bird needed help. Bobo and Kiki decided to support the bird. They carefully picked up the bird and took it to a safe place. They stayed with the bird until it felt better. The bird thanked Bobo and Kiki for their support and flew away happily. Bobo and Kiki felt very proud of themselves for helping their new friend.

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to play with toys. They have a big jet. The jet is red and blue. It can fly in the air. One day, they take the jet to the park. They find a hill. They run up the hill with the jet. They pretend they are pilots. "Ready, Sam?" Tom says. "Ready, Tom!" Sam says. They throw the jet in the air. The jet flies fast and high. They watch the jet with big eyes. They clap their hands. "Wow, look at our jet!" Tom says. "It is so cool!" Sam says. The jet makes a loop. It comes back to them. They catch the jet. They hug the jet. They are happy. They are playful boys. They have a playful jet. They have fun in the park.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to a museum with his mom. The museum was very big and full of old things. Tim liked to look at all the old things with his mom. They saw big animals that lived a long time ago and funny looking cars. In one room, Tim saw a big, old chair. He wanted to sit in it but his mom said, "No, Tim. We can't touch the old things in the museum." But Tim did not listen. When his mom was not looking, he sat in the chair. Suddenly, the chair started to talk! "Hello, Tim," it said. "I am a magic chair! I can comfort people who sit on me." Tim was very surprised. He said, "Wow! Can you comfort my mom too?" The chair said, "Yes, I can." Tim called his mom over to the chair. She was very surprised too. They both sat in the chair and felt very happy and comforted. From that day on, Tim and his mom always talked about their special day at the museum.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toys in his room. He had a big box of toys and a tight little space where he liked to sit and play. One day, Tim found a tray in the kitchen. He thought it would be fun to use the tray to carry his toys to his tight little space. So, Tim kneeled down and put his toys on the tray. He was very happy with his idea. But when Tim picked up the tray, something unexpected happened. The tray started to talk! "Hello, Tim!" said the tray. Tim was very surprised. The tray became his new best friend, and they played together every day.

One Sunday, Tom and Tim went to play. They were very organized. They had a big box of toys. In the box, there were cars, balls, and dolls. They liked to play together. But today, they had a little fight. Tom wanted to play with a blue car, and Tim wanted the same car too. They both tried to grab the car. Tom poked Tim with his finger. Tim felt sad and cried. He didn't like being poked. Tom saw Tim cry and felt bad. He said sorry to Tim and gave him the blue car. Tim stopped crying and smiled. Now, they both played happily. Tom found another car and they raced together. They had fun on that Sunday. They learned to share and be good friends.
