Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Car

Lily and Ben were playing with their toy cars in the living room. They made loud noises and pretended to race each other. Lily had a red car and Ben had a blue car. They liked their cars very much. But then, Ben's car stopped moving. He tried to push it, but it did not go. He looked at Lily and saw that her car was still running. He felt sad and angry. "Give me your car!" he said to Lily. "My car is broken. I want your car!" Lily shook her head. "No, Ben. This is my car. You have your own car. Maybe you need a new battery." Ben did not know what a battery was. He did not care. He wanted Lily's car. He grabbed it from her hands and ran away. Lily cried and chased him. "Ben, give it back! That's not nice!" she shouted. Mom heard the noise and came to see what was wrong. She saw Ben holding Lily's car and Lily crying. She asked them what happened. "Ben took my car!" Lily said. "He said his car is broken. But he did not ask. He just took it!" Mom looked at Ben's car. She saw that it was smelly and had black smoke coming out of it. She knew that the battery was bad. She explained to Ben that he could not use Lily's car. He had to give it back and get a new battery for his car. "But I want this car!" Ben said. He did not want to give it back. He liked the red car. He insisted that it was his car now. Mom was not happy. She told Ben that he was being selfish and rude. He had to say sorry to Lily and share his toys. She took the red car from him and gave it to Lily. She hugged Lily and said she was proud of her for being kind and patient. Ben felt ashamed. He realized that he was wrong. He said sorry to Lily and asked her if he could play with her car sometimes. Lily smiled and said yes. She said she would help him find a new battery for his car. Mom smiled and said she was proud of both of them. She said they could play with their cars together, but they had to be careful and respectful. She said she loved them very much. The end.

Once there was a big lake. In the lake lived a little girl. One day, the girl wanted to have an adventure, so she made a raft out of branches. The raft was heavy, so it was hard to push it into the lake. But the girl was determined and finally managed to get it in the water. Next, she paddled out into the lake with her oar. The wind was blowing, and the water was churning. Suddenly, she saw a big wave coming towards her. The wave was way bigger than the raft, and it felt like it was about to capsize it. The girl felt scared, so she looked around for something to protect her. She saw a big tree branch sticking out of the lake and quickly grabbed hold of it. The branch was strong and held the raft steady. The wave passed and the girl was safe. The little girl was very happy. She had made a raft and protected herself, all on her own.

One day, a little frog named Tim saw a big box in his yard. The box was brown and had a pretty ribbon on it. Tim was very curious and wanted to know what was inside the box. Tim used his strong legs to jump on the box. But he did not know that it had rained last night, and the box was wet. When he jumped, the box became soft and broke. Tim fell inside the box and found many colorful balls. Tim was so happy to find the balls. He called his friends to come and play with him. They all jumped in and out of the wet box, playing with the balls all day long. They had a lot of fun together.
