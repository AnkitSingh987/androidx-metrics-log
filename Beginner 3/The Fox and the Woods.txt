Category: Beginner
Title: The Fox and the Woods

Once upon a time, there was an elderly fox. The fox was old and slow. He lived in a small house in the woods. The fox was sad because he could not find food easily. One day, a little girl saw the fox. She wanted to care for him. The girl brought him food every day. The fox was happy because he was not hungry anymore. The girl and the fox became good friends. But one day, the girl did not come. The fox was sad and hungry. He waited and waited, but the girl never came back. The fox was all alone again. He tried to find food, but he was too old and slow. In the end, the elderly fox was too weak and he passed away.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue lived on a farm with her mom, dad, and her dog, Spot. They grew wheat on their farm, and it was very tall and pretty. Sue loved to play in the wheat field with Spot every day. One day, Sue saw her friend Tim from the next farm over. Tim looked at the wheat and said, "I dare you to race me to the other side!" Sue thought it would be fun, so she agreed. "Okay, let's go!" she said. Tim was envious of Sue's wheat field, because his farm did not have one. Sue and Tim started to run through the wheat. They were laughing and having fun. But then, something unexpected happened. As they ran, they didn't see the hole in the ground. Sue and Tim both fell into the hole, and they couldn't get out. They called for help, but no one could hear them. Sue's mom and dad started to worry when they couldn't find Sue. They looked and looked, but they couldn't find her. Spot, the dog, knew something was wrong. He ran to the wheat field and found the hole where Sue and Tim were. Spot barked and barked until Sue's mom and dad came to see what was wrong. When they found Sue and Tim, they were very sad. The hole was too deep, and they couldn't get them out. Sue and Tim had to stay in the hole forever. The wheat field was not a fun place to play anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a bossy girl. She didn't like it when people answered her. One day, she went to a meeting. She was very bossy and asked lots of questions. Everyone was getting tired of her. They thought it was mean to be bossy. The bossy girl was not happy. She got very mad and started yelling because nobody would answer her questions. Everyone was scared and started to leave the meeting. The bossy girl was all alone. She learned that being bossy isn't nice and it doesn't always get her what she wants. The moral of the story is that you should be kind, not bossy, or else you will be alone.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy had a toy bear. The bear had an ugly patch on its tummy. Lucy loved her bear very much. One day, Lucy's friend, Tom, came to play. Tom saw the bear and said, "Why does your bear have an ugly patch?" Lucy said, "My bear is special. The patch makes it one of a kind." Tom thought about it and said, "You're right, Lucy. The patch makes your bear special. I want a toy with a patch too!" Lucy and Tom played with the bear and had a lot of fun. From that day on, they both loved the bear with the ugly patch.

Once upon a time, a unique bird lived near a temple. The bird had many colors and could sing pretty songs. One day, a little girl named Sue came to the temple to play. Sue saw the bird and said, "Bird, come play with me!" The bird came down and sang a song for Sue. They played all day near the temple. Sue loved the bird and its songs. But one day, a big wind came and blew the bird away. Sue was very sad. She went to the temple every day to wait for the bird. But the bird did not come back. Sue missed her unique bird friend and their happy days at the temple.
