Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Rope

Here is one possible story, but there are many other ways to write it. Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They saw a big rope hanging from a tree. They wanted to swing on it and have fun. Lily ran to the rope first and grabbed it. She said, "This is my rope. You can't have it." Ben was angry. He wanted to swing on the rope too. He said, "No, it's not your rope. It's for everyone. Let me have a turn." He tried to pull the rope from Lily, but she held on tight. They both pulled and pulled, but neither of them would let go. They started to yell and scream at each other. They did not hear the loud snap. The rope broke and they both fell to the ground. They hit their heads and their arms and their legs. They cried and cried. They were hurt and sad. A lady came and saw them. She said, "What happened? Why were you fighting over the rope?" She helped them get up and took them to a bench. She said, "You should not be greedy and mean. You should share and be nice. The rope was not yours. It was for everyone. Now look what you did. You broke it and you hurt yourselves. You are very naughty." Lily and Ben felt sorry. They said, "We are sorry. We were wrong. We should have shared and been nice." They hugged each other and said, "We are sorry. We are friends." But the lady was still angry. She said, "You are too late. You can't play on the rope anymore. You have to go home now. And you have to tell your parents what you did. They will be very angry too." Lily and Ben were scared. They did not want to go home. They did not want to tell their parents. They wished they had not fought over the rope. They wished they had been good. They squeezed each other's hands and walked away. They had a bad day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was three years old and loved to play outside. One day, he asked his mom if he could play outside. His mom said yes, but only if he was careful. Tim had a lot of fun playing. He wanted to stay outside longer and this made his mom worried. She asked him to come inside, but he just gave her a big wink. This made his mom very angry. She asked him why he wasn't listening. Tim just gave her another wink and kept playing. His mom warned him again, and this time he listened. Tim stopped playing and went inside with a worried face. He knew he had made his mom angry and he was sorry.

One day, a little girl named Olive was feeling sad. She let out a big sigh. Olive was sad because she lost her lucky toy. It was a small, soft bunny. She loved her bunny very much. She looked for her bunny all day, but she could not find it. Olive's mom saw her looking sad. She asked, "Why are you sad, Olive?" Olive told her mom about her lost lucky bunny. Her mom wanted to help, so they looked all over the house together. They looked in the toy box, under the bed, and even in the kitchen. But they still could not find the bunny. Just when Olive was about to give up, her little brother came into the room. He had a big smile on his face and was holding something behind his back. He said, "Look, Olive! I found your lucky bunny!" Olive was so happy and surprised! She hugged her brother and thanked him. Olive, her mom, and her brother all laughed and played together with the lucky bunny.

One day, a funny cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. As he climbed, he heard a little bird say, "Hi, funny cat! Do you want to play with me?" Tom said, "Yes, I want to play. Let's be friends!" The bird was happy to accept Tom as a friend. They played games and had fun in the tree. But then, Tom slipped and had a big fall. The bird flew down to help him. Tom was not hurt, but he was scared. The bird said, "Don't worry, Tom. I am here to help you." Tom was happy to have a new friend who cared for him. They played more games on the ground and had a great day together. And from that day on, the funny cat and the little bird were best friends.
