Category: Beginner
Title: The Great Car of Tim and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that he loved very much. But one day, the toy car was broken. Tim was sad because he could not play with his favorite toy. Tim's mom saw him crying and had an urge to help. She told Tim that it was important to value the things we have, even if they are broken. So, she gave him some glue and paper to fix the car. Tim was happy and excited to make his toy car work again. Tim and his mom worked together to fix the broken toy car. They used the glue and paper to put the pieces back together. After they were done, the car looked like new. Tim learned to value his toys and take care of them. He also learned that with a little help, even broken things can be fixed.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play outside. One day, he found a big berry bush. The bush had many red berries on it. Tim was very happy. Tim saw his friend, Sue, walking by. She was sad. She had no berries to eat. Tim wanted to make her feel better. He thought it would be nice to share his berries with her. So, he picked some berries from the bush and gave them to Sue. Now, Sue was happy too! The next day, Tim and Sue went to play outside again. They found a tough tree to climb. They climbed up the tree and saw more berry bushes. They picked more berries and shared them with their friends. Everyone was happy because they all had berries to eat. Sharing made Tim and Sue feel very good.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a watch. It was a loud watch. It made a tick-tock sound. Tim liked the watch very much. He put it on his wrist and went to show his mom. "Mom, look at my watch!" Tim said. His mom looked at the watch and said, "Wow, that's a loud watch, Tim!" She tried to explain how the watch worked, but Tim was too excited to listen. Tim went to the park to show his friends his loud watch. As he was playing, he heard a loud sound. He looked down at his watch, but it was not the watch making the sound. It was a big dog barking! The dog wanted to play with Tim and his friends. They all had fun playing together. Tim forgot about his watch for a while. When it was time to go home, Tim looked at his watch and saw that it was very late. He said goodbye to his friends and the dog, and ran home as fast as he could.

Once, there was a True flower. It grew and the days were wide and sunny. One morning, the flower began to bloom. Its petals were soft, pink and round. First, one petal opened and the flower looked surprised. Then, two more petals opened wide and the flower smiled. Soon, the flower was beaming and everyone who saw it was happy too. The flower kept blooming all day and night, until it was a beautiful bright flower with all its petals open. It was a True bloom that lit up the sky.

Once upon a time, on a farm, there lived a little duck. The little duck liked to snap at bugs. One day, the little duck saw a big bug. The big bug was on a helpless baby bird. The baby bird was sad and scared. The little duck wanted to help the baby bird. He tried to snap at the big bug, but the big bug was too fast. The little duck felt bad. He wanted to help the baby bird, but he did not know how. Then, the little duck had an idea. He called his farm friends. The cow, the pig, and the sheep came to help. They all worked together to scare the big bug away. The baby bird was happy, and the little duck was proud. They all became good friends on the farm.
