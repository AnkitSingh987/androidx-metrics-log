Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Fast Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a fast cat named Tim. Tim liked to lean on a big tree near his home. One day, Tim saw a small bird on the tree. The bird was sad because it could not fly. Tim wanted to help the bird. He had an idea. Tim found a big leaf and put it on the ground. He told the bird to jump on the leaf. The bird did as Tim said. Tim then took a big breath with his lung and blew on the leaf. The leaf with the bird on it flew up into the sky. The bird was so happy that it could fly. But then, something unexpected happened. The bird started to fly on its own! It turned out the bird could always fly, but it just needed a little help to learn. Tim and the bird became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a big oak tree. It was a mysterious tree. It had big branches that spread wide. Many animals lived in the tree. They were all friends. One day, a little squirrel named Sam was playing near the oak tree. Sam saw a big nut. He wanted to share it with his friends. So, Sam climbed the oak tree and called his friends. "Look what I found!" said Sam. "Let's share it!" All the animals came to see the big nut. They all sat around it and spread it open. Inside, they found a sweet surprise. It was a big, yummy cake! They all laughed and enjoyed the cake together. The mysterious oak tree was a happy place for all the animals.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to play dress-up. One day, she found a veil in her mom's room. The veil was long and white. Mia put it on and looked in the mirror. She thought it was pretty. Mia wanted to show her friend, Tom. She went outside and saw Tom playing. "Look at my veil!" Mia said to Tom. Tom looked at Mia and said, "It's nice, but it could be scary too." Mia didn't believe him. She thought the veil was just pretty. Later, Mia wore the veil while playing hide and seek. She hid behind a tree. When Tom found her, he yelled, "Boo!" Mia was so scared! She didn't know the veil could be scary. Tom laughed and said, "I told you!" Mia laughed too. They played together with the veil, sometimes pretty and sometimes scary.

One day, a boy named Tim had a messy room. He liked to play with his toys, but did not like to put them away. In his room, there was a big red helmet. Tim loved to wear the helmet when he played outside. One morning, Tim wanted to feed his pet fish. He went to find the fish food, but it was lost in his messy room. He looked and looked but could not find it. Tim was sad because he wanted his fish to be happy. Then, something unexpected happened. Tim's big red helmet started to shake and move. Tim was very surprised. He picked up the helmet and found the fish food under it! Tim was so happy. He fed his fish and promised to keep his room clean from now on.

Anna and Ben were playing in the kitchen. They liked to pretend they were chefs and make yummy food. They used pots and pans and spoons and bowls. But they did not use the knife. The knife was sharp and dangerous. Mommy told them to never touch the knife. But one day, Anna saw the knife on the counter. It was shiny and big. She wanted to see it better. She climbed on a chair and reached for the knife. But the chair was wobbly and Anna lost her balance. She stumbled and fell. The knife fell too. It landed near Ben's foot. Ben saw the knife and was frightened. He screamed and ran away. He did not want the knife to hurt him. He bumped into Mommy, who was coming to the kitchen. Mommy saw the knife and Anna on the floor. She was scared and angry. Mommy picked up Anna and checked if she was hurt. She was not hurt, but she was crying. Mommy hugged her and said, "Anna, you were very naughty. You could have hurt yourself or Ben with the knife. You must never touch the knife. Do you understand?" Anna nodded and said, "I'm sorry, Mommy. I was curious. I won't touch the knife again." Mommy kissed her and said, "I love you, Anna. But you have to be careful and listen to me. The knife is not a toy. It is for grown-ups only." Mommy put the knife away and took Anna and Ben to the living room. She gave them some cookies and milk and read them a story. They felt better and said, "We love you, Mommy. We will be good and not touch the knife." Mommy smiled and said, "I love you too, my little chefs."
