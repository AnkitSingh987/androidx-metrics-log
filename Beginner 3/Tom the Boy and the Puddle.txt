Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Puddle

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. One day, Tom went outside to play. He saw a wet puddle on the ground. He jumped in the puddle and laughed. Tom found a pretty stamp on the ground. It had a picture of a big, red ball on it. He picked up the stamp and put it in his pocket. Tom saw his friend, Sue, and wanted to share his new stamp with her. Tom showed Sue the stamp, and she liked it too. They decided to share the stamp and take turns using it. Tom and Sue played with the stamp all day, and they were very happy. Sharing made them the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a fast cat named Velvet. Velvet liked to run and play all day. One day, Velvet saw a big dog. The dog was mean and wanted to catch Velvet. Velvet said, "You can't catch me!" and ran away fast. The big dog tried to catch Velvet, but he was too slow. The big dog was sad and said, "I miss having a friend to play with." A small bird saw the big dog and said, "I can be your friend!" The big dog was happy to have a new friend. They played together, and Velvet saw them. Velvet felt bad for running away and said, "I'm sorry, big dog. I didn't know you just wanted a friend." The big dog, the small bird, and Velvet all became friends. They learned that it is important to be kind and not judge others too quickly. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a toy on a shelf. The toy was a little car. The car was very jealous. It saw other toys play with kids. But no one would come and play with the car. One day, the car saw a big ball roll by. The car wanted to play too. It tried to come off the shelf. But the car fell down. The car felt sad and hurt. In the end, the car was still on the floor. No one came to help the car. The car was more jealous than ever. It wished someone would come and play with it. But no one did. And the car stayed sad.

Once there were two friends, Tom and Jane, who lived in a modern city. One day, Tom and Jane decided to go outside and explore the nearby forest. The forest was so big, with lots of tall trees. They walked around and soon came across a big rock. Tom wanted to climb it, so the two friends worked together and carefully climbed up the rock. But when Tom and Jane got to the top, they saw something terrible. The rock was connected to a very big machine. The machine was rumbling and making loud sounds. They could even feel the vibrations from the rock. Tom and Jane were scared and immediately climbed down. Tom and Jane quickly ran as fast as they could, away from the forest. They both felt so sad and scared. Unfortunately, the modern machine was in the forest to cut down all the trees. All the trees in the forest were gone by the end of the day. Tom and Jane never wanted to go back to the forest again. The rock that had looked like so much fun turned out to have a bad ending.

One day, a girl named Sue went to the park. She saw a colorful pet bird in a tree. The bird was red, blue, and green. Sue said, "Hi bird! You are pretty." The bird said, "Hi Sue! I like your hat." Sue was happy the bird could talk. They talked and played. The bird told Sue, "I can sing a special song. It will make you happy when you are sad." One day, Sue was sad because she lost her toy. She remembered the bird's special song. Sue went to the park and found the bird. She asked the bird to sing the special song. The bird sang the song and Sue felt happy again. Sue and the bird became best friends. They played in the park every day and the bird always sang the special song when Sue was sad.
