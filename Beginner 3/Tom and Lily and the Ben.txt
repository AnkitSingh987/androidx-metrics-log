Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Ben

Tom and Lily liked to play with blocks. They had many blocks of different colors and shapes. They could make towers, bridges, houses, and anything they wanted. They were very organized. They put each block in a box that matched its color and shape. One day, they decided to make a big building with all their blocks. They worked hard and used all their blocks. They made a building that was taller than them. They were very proud of their building. They wanted to show it to their mom and dad. But then, they heard a loud noise. It was their baby brother, Ben. He had crawled into their room. He saw the building and wanted to touch it. He reached out his hand and pushed the building. The building fell down with a crash. All the blocks were scattered on the floor. Tom and Lily were very sad and angry. They shouted at Ben. "No, Ben! Bad, Ben! You broke our building! Go away!" Ben started to cry. He did not understand why Tom and Lily were mad at him. He just wanted to play with them. He crawled away to his mom. Mom heard the noise and came to see what happened. She saw the mess and the tears. She hugged Ben and comforted him. She said, "It's okay, Ben. You did not mean to break the building. You are too little to know. Tom and Lily, I know you are upset, but you should not yell at Ben. He is your brother. You should share and be nice to him." Tom and Lily felt sorry for Ben. They realized they were too harsh with him. They said, "We're sorry, Ben. We love you. You can play with us. But please be careful with our blocks. They are fragile. We can help you learn how to build with them." They gave Ben a kiss and a hug. They picked up their blocks and put them back in their boxes. They decided to make a new building together, with Ben's help. They were happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted bird named Blue. Blue was very good at flying high and fast. One day, Blue met a new friend, a small bug named Buzz. Blue and Buzz liked to play together. They would fly around and have fun all day. One day, they decided to discuss where they should go next. They talked and talked. Blue said, "Let's go east!" Buzz agreed and said, "Yes, let's go east!" So, Blue and Buzz went east. They saw many fun things and had a great time. They were very happy to have each other as friends.

Once upon a time there was a house with a window. It was old and broken, but the people in the house liked it. One day, a man came to the house. He looked at the window and said "I can restore this window!" The people in the house were happy! The man worked very hard to restore the window. He fixed the broken pieces and made it look nice. The window was no longer dangerous. The people in the house were very happy. They had a safe window that looked good too. It was the best window ever! The end.

One day, a bald man named Tom went to the park. He had a big bag with him. Inside the bag were yummy snacks. Tom sat on the grass and opened his bag to eat. A little girl named Lily saw Tom and walked to him. She asked, "Can I touch your bald head?" Tom laughed and said, "Yes, you can." Lily touched his head and giggled. Then, Tom gave her a snack from his bag. Suddenly, a big wind came and blew the bag away. Tom and Lily were sad. But then, a nice dog ran to them. The dog had Tom's bag in his mouth! Tom and Lily were happy again. They shared the snacks with the dog and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a cute bunny. The bunny liked to spring high in the air. One day, the bunny found a guitar. The bunny wanted to play the guitar, but it was too big for him. He was sad. The bunny asked his friend, the bird, for help. The bird had a great idea. The bird would hold the guitar up high, and the bunny would spring up to play it. They tried and tried, but the bunny still could not play the guitar. Just when they were about to give up, a big wind came. The wind blew the guitar away. The bunny and the bird were sad, but then they saw the guitar land in a tree. The tree's branches played the guitar, making a beautiful sound. The bunny and the bird were so happy, they danced and sang along with the music.
