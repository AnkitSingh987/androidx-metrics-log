Category: Beginner
Title: The Tree of Tim the Boy

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his dog, Jazz. They liked to play in the park. Tim would poke Jazz, and Jazz would run around and bark. They had a lot of fun together. One day, while they were playing, they saw a big, dead tree. Tim said, "Look, Jazz! That tree is dead. Let's go see it." They walked closer to the dead tree, and Tim poked it. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise. Out of the tree came a big bird, and it said, "Why did you poke my tree?" Tim was very surprised. He didn't know that birds could talk! Tim said, "I'm sorry, Mr. Bird. I didn't know this was your home." The bird forgave Tim, and they all played together in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely parrot. The parrot lived in a big tree in the park. He had many friends who played with him every day. One day, the parrot had a job to do. He had to deliver a letter to his friend, the cat. The cat lived in a house near the park. The parrot took the letter in his beak and flew to the cat's house. When the parrot found the cat, he said, "Here is a letter for you, my friend!" The cat was happy to get the letter. She said, "Thank you, parrot!" They both smiled and played together for the rest of the day. The parrot was happy he could help his friend.

One day, a little cat and a little dog were playing in the yard. The cat saw a big vine hanging from a tree. The cat said, "I want to climb the vine." The dog said, "No, I want to play with the vine." The cat and the dog started to disagree. They both wanted to play with the vine but didn't want to share. The cat tried to climb the vine, but the dog pulled it away. The cat got mad and the dog got mad too. Just then, a big bird flew down and sat on the vine. The bird was very alert and saw the cat and the dog fighting. The bird said, "Why don't you both play with the vine together?" The cat and the dog thought about it and agreed. They both played with the vine and had lots of fun. They learned that sharing is better than fighting.

Once upon a time, there was a restless little dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play all day long. One day, Spot saw a big piece of beef on the ground. Spot's eyes grew wide, and he ran to the beef. Spot started to chew on the beef. It was so yummy! Spot's friend, a little cat named Whiskers, came over. Whiskers looked at Spot and said, "Can I have some beef too?" Spot shared the beef with Whiskers. They both chewed on it happily. After they finished eating, they were not restless anymore. Spot and Whiskers played together until it was time to go home. They were very happy and could not wait to play again the next day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a dog named Max. Max was a happy and playful dog. He loved to run, jump, and play with his friends. One day, Max found a big bone in his yard. He was very happy to find the bone because it was his favorite thing to play with. Max decided to make a game with the bone. He would hide the bone and then try to find it again. Max played the game all day long. He hid the bone under a tree, behind a rock, and even inside a bush. Each time, Max would find the bone and start the game again. One day, while Max was playing with the bone, a rude cat named Tom came to his yard. Tom saw the bone and wanted it for himself. He tried to take the bone from Max, but Max did not let him. Max told Tom that it was not nice to take things from others. Tom felt bad and said sorry to Max. They decided to share the bone and play together. From that day on, Max and Tom became good friends and played with the bone every day.
