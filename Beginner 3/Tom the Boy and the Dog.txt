Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom loved to ride his bike. One day, he went on a ride in the park. The sun was up, and the birds were singing. Tom was very happy. As Tom rode his bike, he saw a big hole in the soil. The hole was deep. Tom was curious. He wanted to see what was in the hole. So, he got off his bike and looked inside. To his surprise, Tom found a little dog in the hole! The dog was scared and could not get out. Tom helped the dog by lifting it out of the hole. The dog was happy and wagged its tail. Tom and the dog became best friends, and they rode home together on Tom's bike.

Once upon a time, there was an uncle who liked to stretch every morning. He would stand up tall, stretch his arms wide and reach for the sky. He felt so good when he stretched his body! One day, his nephew wanted to have a turn too. So the uncle showed him how to stretch. He said, "Stand tall and reach your arms wide." The little nephew copied his uncle and stretched too. He was so proud of himself and smiled ear to ear! After they were done stretching, they hugged and the uncle said, "You're very good at stretching!" The little nephew was so happy that he could stretch just like his uncle. From then on, every morning the uncle and nephew stretched together. And every morning, they stretched their arms wide and smiled.

One day, a boy named Tim found a magnet. Tim was very proud of his magnet. He loved to rub it on things to see if they would stick. He played with it all day long. One day, Tim's mom asked him to go to the store to get some milk. Tim took his magnet with him. He rubbed it on everything he saw. He was having so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. Tim rubbed his magnet on a big metal box. The magnet got stuck! Tim tried to pull it off, but it was too strong. Just then, a nice man came and helped Tim get his magnet back. The man was a magician! He showed Tim how to make the magnet stick and unstick with a magic word. Now, Tim was not only proud of his magnet, but also of his new magic trick.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a fair boy with a big smile. He loved to play with his red ball in the park. One sunny day, Tim went to the park with his mom. At the park, Tim saw a big tree. Under the tree, there was a bowl. The bowl was full of yummy fruit. Tim wanted to eat the fruit, but he had to ask his mom first. He ran to his mom and asked if he could eat the fruit. His mom said yes. Tim was happy. He took the bowl and sat under the tree. He ate the fruit and played with his red ball. When it was time to go home, Tim didn't want to leave the bowl. So, his mom said they could take it home. They turned around and walked back to their house with the bowl. Tim had a fun day at the park.

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a walk. She saw a big orange ball in the sky. It was very pretty. Sue wanted to show her friend, Tim, the orange ball. She ran to his house and said, "Tim, come see the big orange ball!" Tim and Sue went to look at the orange ball. But when they got there, the ball was gone. Instead, they saw a shiny trophy! It was big and gold. They were very surprised. "Wow, a trophy! Let's take it home," said Tim. They picked up the trophy and started to walk. But then, the orange ball came back. It was a big balloon! The trophy was inside the balloon all along. Sue and Tim laughed and played with the orange balloon and the shiny trophy all day.
