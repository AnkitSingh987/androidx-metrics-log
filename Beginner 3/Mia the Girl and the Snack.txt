Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Snack

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was very hungry and wanted a snack. She looked in her bag, but her snack was not there. She sighed, feeling sad. Her friend, Tom, saw her and asked, "Why are you sad, Mia?" Mia said, "I can't find my snack." Tom felt bad for Mia and wanted to help her. They looked for the snack together. After looking everywhere, they found Mia's snack under a tree. Mia was so happy and thanked Tom. They both sat under the tree and shared the snack. Mia was not frustrated anymore and they both had a fun day.

There once was a boy named Tom. Tom had a ball made of leather. It was dull and old, but he loved it so much. Every day, Tom would play with the ball in the park. He would throw it, kick it, and then return it to his bag when he was done playing. One day, Tom was playing with his ball in the park. He kicked it very hard and it went far away. Tom looked and looked, but he could not find the ball. He was very sad and went home without it. That night, Tom could not sleep because he missed his ball so much. The next day, Tom went back to the park to look for his ball. He saw a big dog playing with a shiny, new leather ball. The dog saw Tom and came to him. The dog dropped the ball at Tom's feet. Tom was happy to play with the new ball, but he still missed his old, dull ball. Then, the dog's owner came and said, "I found your old ball yesterday, and I bought a new one for my dog. But my dog likes your old ball better, so let's return them." Tom and the dog's owner swapped the balls. Now, Tom had his old ball back, and the dog had a new ball to play with. They all played together in the park, and Tom was very happy.

Once upon a time, in a little town, there was a persistent nurse named Jane. She loved to help people feel better. One day, Jane saw a little girl named Sue, who was sad because her cat was stuck in a tree. Jane said, "Don't worry, Sue. I'll help you get your cat down." Jane tried to spring up and reach the cat, but she couldn't jump high enough. She tried again and again, but still couldn't reach the cat. Sue said, "Thank you for trying, Jane." A big, strong man saw Jane and Sue and came to help. He was able to spring up and grab the cat from the tree. Sue was so happy to have her cat back, and she thanked Jane and the man. The man said, "You're welcome! It's good to help others." The moral of the story is that if you are persistent and kind, you can make a difference in the lives of others.

One day, a little girl named Daisy went for a walk. She wore a thick coat because it was cold. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Daisy saw a big tree and wanted to play near it. She zipped up her coat and ran to the tree. Under the tree, Daisy found a small, friendly cat. The cat said, "Meow, do you want to play with me?" Daisy was very happy and said, "Yes, let's play hide and seek!" The cat went to hide, and Daisy started to count. Daisy counted to ten and began to look for the cat. She looked behind the tree, under a bush, and in a hole. But she could not find the cat anywhere. Then, something unexpected happened. Daisy saw a big, colorful bird come down from the sky. The bird said, "I saw the cat go up the tree!" Daisy looked up and saw the cat stuck in the tree. She helped the cat get down, and they both laughed and played together for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, there was a polite cat named Tom. He lived in a big house with his friend, a little pupil named Tim. They loved to play together in the garden. One sunny day, Tom saw a big, red ball in the garden. He wanted to play with it, but the ball started to roll away. Tom tried to catch it, but it was too fast. It rolled into a small hole in the fence. Tom wanted to escape and go after the ball, but the hole was too small for him. Tim saw Tom trying to escape through the hole. He wanted to help his friend. So, Tim opened the gate and let Tom out. Together, they chased the red ball and had lots of fun. And from that day on, they always played together and helped each other.
