Category: Beginner
Title: The Round Ball and the Magic

Once upon a time, there was a round ball. It was not an ordinary ball. It was a magic ball. The magic ball liked to play with kids. It would roll and bounce all day long. The kids were very happy with the magic ball. One day, a little girl named Lily found the round magic ball. She was so excited to play with it. She took it to the park. Lily and the magic ball played together. They rolled and bounced all around. Lily was very happy. After playing, Lily got tired. She wanted to sit down. So, she kneeled on the grass next to the round magic ball. They looked at the sky and saw the sun setting. It was a beautiful day. Lily and the magic ball were very happy. They knew they would have more fun days together.

Once upon a time, there was a big blueberry. It lived in a small house in the woods. The blueberry had a friend, a loud bird. They liked to play together every day. One sunny day, the loud bird said, "Let's play a fun game! I will rub my wings on the ground, and you roll down the hill." The blueberry liked the idea and said, "Okay, let's play!" The loud bird rubbed its wings on the ground, making a funny sound. The blueberry rolled down the hill, laughing and having fun. They played all day long until the sun went down. The blueberry and the loud bird were very happy and tired. They went back to their small house, ready for a good night's sleep.

Once upon a time, there was a little black engine. This engine loved to slide down the big hill. Every day, the engine would go up and down the hill, having lots of fun. One day, the black engine met a new friend. The new friend was a big red engine. The red engine wanted to slide down the hill too. So, the black engine showed the red engine how to slide. The black engine and the red engine had lots of fun sliding down the hill together. They played all day and became best friends. From that day on, they always slid down the hill together, laughing and having fun.

Tom and Mia like to play in the park. They have a bow and some arrows. The arrows are made of sticks and feathers. They pretend to be hunters and look for animals. One day, they see a big bird in a tree. It has yellow and black feathers and a long tail. Tom says, "Look, Mia, a bird! Let's catch it with our arrows!" Mia nods and says, "OK, Tom, you shoot first. I will shoot next." Tom pulls the bow and aims at the bird. He lets go of the arrow. The arrow flies in the air. But it does not hit the bird. It hits a branch and falls down. The bird sees the arrow and flies away. Tom says, "Oh no, I missed!" Mia says, "Don't worry, Tom, I will try." She pulls the bow and aims at the bird. She lets go of the arrow. The arrow flies in the air. It hits the bird in the wing. The bird cries and falls down. Mia says, "Yay, I got it!" Tom and Mia run to the bird. They see that it is hurt and bleeding. They feel sorry for the bird. They say, "We are sorry, bird, we did not mean to hurt you. We were just playing." The bird looks at them with sad eyes. It says, "Please help me. I need water. My wing is dry and sore." Tom and Mia say, "OK, bird, we will help you. We will get some water and a bandage. We will make you feel better." They go to the fountain and fill a cup with water. They find a cloth and wrap it around the bird's wing. They give the bird some water and some seeds. The bird drinks and eats. It says, "Thank you, Tom and Mia. You are kind and good. I forgive you for shooting me." Tom and Mia say, "You are welcome, bird. We are sorry for shooting you. We will not do it again. We will be your friends." The bird says, "I will be your friend too. But please, do not play with arrows. They are dangerous and can hurt. Play with something else, like a ball or a kite." Tom and Mia say, "OK, bird, we will. We will play with a ball or a kite. We will have fun and be safe." They hug the bird and say goodbye. They take their bow and arrows and put them away. They get a ball and a kite and go back to the park. They play and laugh and have fun. They are happy and the bird is happy too.
