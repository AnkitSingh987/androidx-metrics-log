Category: Beginner
Title: The Girl Called Jane and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Jane. Every Saturday morning, Jane would go to the park with her mom. One Saturday morning, Jane and her mom arrived at the park. "Let's have some fun!" Jane's mom said. "Can we do something unusual?" asked Jane. "Sure," said her mom. "What did you have in mind?" "How about a tea party at noon?" said Jane. "That's a great idea," said her mom. So that's what they did. They included some chairs, a table and some cakes for the party. At noon, Jane and her mom joined in the tea party. It was so unusual, but it was also lots of fun. Jane and her mom had the best Saturday afternoon and were happy that they included something unusual.

Rudy and Anna were the best of friends. One day, they decided to go on a fun holiday. They gathered their things and drove to the beach. They were very excited. The beach was cool and they had a lot of fun. They gathered shells and sand dollars, built sandcastles and played in the waves. Rudy couldn't believe how much fun he was having. At the end of the day, they gathered their things and drove home. Anna said it was the best holiday ever. Rudy agreed. He was so happy and told Anna they will have to do it again soon!

Tommy liked to look at the birds in the birdcage. He liked their colors and their songs. He wanted to play with them, but his mom said no. She said the birds were happy in their home and did not like to be touched. One day, Tommy saw a bird on the window sill. It was not in the birdcage. It was a blue bird with a yellow beak. Tommy was curious. He opened the window and reached for the bird. The bird was scared. It flew away. "Tommy, what are you doing?" his mom asked. She saw the open window and the empty birdcage. She was angry. "I wanted to play with the bird, mom," Tommy said. He was confused. "Why did it fly away?" "Tommy, that was not our bird. That was a wild bird. It does not live in a cage. It lives in the sky. You scared it away. And you let our birds out too. They might be lost now. How could you do that?" his mom said. Tommy felt bad. He did not mean to hurt the birds. He blinked his eyes. He saw some feathers on the floor. He hoped the birds would come back. He said sorry to his mom. He said sorry to the birds. He learned a lesson. He would not touch the birds again. He would only look at them and listen to them. He would be a good boy.

One day, a big angry dog set a ball on the ground. He said to the small happy dog, "Do not touch my ball! I will be back soon." The small happy dog looked at the ball and wanted to play with it. The small happy dog thought, "I will just play with the ball for a little bit. The big angry dog will not know." So, the small happy dog took the ball and played with it. He had a lot of fun. Soon, the big angry dog came back. He saw that his ball was not on the ground. The big angry dog said, "Who took my ball?" The small happy dog felt bad and gave the ball back. The big angry dog saw that the small happy dog just wanted to play. So, they played with the ball together and became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play outside. One day, it was very cold. Tim put on his warm coat and went out to play. Tim found a big stick. He wanted to test how it could make sounds. He hit the stick on the ground. It made a loud sound. He hit the stick on a tree. It made a different sound. Tim was very happy. Tim's mom called him back inside the house. She had made hot soup to help him feel warm. Tim told his mom about the stick and the sounds. They both laughed and enjoyed their soup.
