Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Man and the Car

Once upon a time, in an ordinary little town, there lived a kind man named Tim. Tim had a special razor. This razor could reverse anything it touched. Tim loved to help people with his special razor. One day, Tim met a sad boy named Sam. Sam had a broken toy car. "Can you help me fix my toy car?" asked Sam. Tim smiled and said, "Of course, I can help you with my special razor!" Tim took his razor and gently touched the broken car. In a blink, the car was fixed! Sam was so happy. He thanked Tim for helping him. From that day on, Tim and Sam became good friends, and Tim continued to help others with his special razor.

Once upon a time, there was a humble musician. He lived in a small house with his cat, Tom. The musician liked to play songs on his guitar every day. One day, the musician went to the park to play his guitar. Many people stopped to listen to his music. They clapped and danced to the songs. The musician was happy to see everyone enjoying his music. But then, a big dog came running into the park. The dog started to yell, "Woof! Woof!" The people stopped dancing and looked at the dog. The humble musician played a soft song to make the dog feel better. The dog stopped yelling and wagged its tail. Everyone went back to dancing and clapping. The musician and Tom the cat went home with big smiles on their faces.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One day, Tim went on a journey with his toy truck. He walked and walked until he found a big pile of sand. Tim said, "I want to bury my truck in the sand!" So, he dug a hole with his hands and put the truck in the hole. Then, he covered the truck with the warm sand. Tim was happy. Soon, it was time for Tim to go home. He dug up his truck and said, "Bye, sand! I will come back soon!" Tim walked back home with his truck, excited to tell his mom about his fun journey.

Once upon a time, there was a little boat. The boat was empty and sad. It wanted to play with the kids on the beach. One day, a boy named Tim saw the boat. He said, "Boat, do you want to play with me?" The boat was happy and said, "Yes, let's play!" Tim and the boat played near the water. They had fun. But then, Tim found a sharp shell. He wanted to cut something. He tried to cut the boat. The boat was hurt and said, "No, Tim! That hurts!" But Tim did not stop. He cut the boat more and more. In the end, the boat was broken. It could not play with the kids anymore. The boat was sad and Tim was sad too.

Lily and Ben were playing with sand. They wanted to make a big castle. They had a bucket, a shovel and some shells. They worked hard and had fun. "Look, our castle is almost done!" Lily said. "It is so pretty and strong!" "Yes, it is!" Ben agreed. "But we need a flag. What can we use for a flag?" Lily looked around. She saw a red scarf on a bench. It belonged to a lady who was reading a book. Lily ran to the bench and took the scarf. "Hey, that's mine!" the lady shouted. She was angry. She got up and chased Lily. Lily was scared. She ran back to Ben with the scarf. The lady followed her. "Give me back my scarf, you crazy girl!" the lady said. She reached for the scarf. But Lily did not want to give it back. She wanted to use it for a flag. She held it tight and ran around the castle. "Stop, stop!" Ben said. He did not like the lady. He did not like the shouting. He wanted to relax and play with the castle. He had an idea. He took some sand and threw it at the lady. The sand got in her eyes and mouth. She coughed and blinked. "Ow, ow, ow!" she said. She dropped the book and rubbed her eyes. Lily and Ben saw their chance. They put the scarf on the castle as a flag. They smiled and hugged. "We did it!" they said. "We have the best castle ever!" They relaxed and admired their work. They did not care about the lady or the book. They were happy.
