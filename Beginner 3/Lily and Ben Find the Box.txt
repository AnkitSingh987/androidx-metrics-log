Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben Find the Box

Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They saw a caterpillar on a leaf. It was green and fuzzy. "Look, a caterpillar!" Lily said. "It is so cute!" Ben nodded. He wanted to touch it. He reached out his hand. "Wait, Ben!" Lily said. "Don't touch it. It might be scared." Ben stopped. He looked at Lily. "Why?" he asked. "Because it is unique," Lily said. "It is not like us. It has a different body and a different life. It can act in different ways. Maybe it likes to be alone. Maybe it doesn't like to be touched." Ben thought about it. He wanted to be kind to the caterpillar. He wanted to learn more about it. He asked Lily, "How can we be friends with it?" Lily smiled. She had an idea. She said, "We can watch it. We can talk to it. We can make it a home. We can give it some food. We can be gentle and quiet." Ben liked the idea. He said, "Okay, let's do that." They found a small box. They put some leaves and grass in it. They carefully lifted the caterpillar and placed it in the box. They gave it a name. They called it Fuzzy. They watched Fuzzy. They talked to Fuzzy. They made Fuzzy a home. They gave Fuzzy some food. They were gentle and quiet. Fuzzy seemed happy. It moved around the box. It ate the leaves. It listened to Lily and Ben. It acted like a friend.

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. It was a gloomy day, but he did not care. He wanted to have fun. He put on his coat and boots, and went to the park. At the park, Tim saw a big tree. He sat under the tree and looked up at the sky. He saw the sun trying to come out. He mentioned to his friend, "Look, the sun is coming!" His friend smiled and they played under the tree. As they played, something unexpected happened. The sun came out and the gloomy day turned into a sunny day. Tim and his friend were so happy. They played all day in the sun, laughing and having fun. The day that started gloomy ended up being the best day ever.

One day, a little girl named Lucy got a new toy. It was a pretty figure. She was very happy. Lucy wanted to show her new toy to her friend Tim. So, she began to prepare for a play date. Lucy and Tim started to play with the new figure. They had lots of fun. They played in the garden and made the figure run and jump. They were having a great time. But then, something unexpected happened. A big bird came and took the new figure away. Lucy and Tim were very sad. They tried to get the figure back, but the bird flew away with it. The play date ended badly, and they went home without the new figure.

One day, a sad little girl named Lily wanted to get the moon. She looked up at the sky and saw how big and bright it was. She thought if she had the moon, she would not be sad anymore. So, she asked her friend, Tom, for help. Tom said, "I have an idea. We can use a big stick to reach the moon." They found a big stick and tried to get the moon. But the stick was not long enough. They were sad, but they did not give up. The next day, they saw a big hill. Tom said, "Let's go up the hill. Maybe we can get the moon from there." They climbed the hill, and when they got to the top, they were closer to the moon. They jumped and reached, but they still could not get the moon. Even though they did not get the moon, they looked at the beautiful view and smiled. From that day on, Lily and Tom were not sad anymore, because they had each other as friends.

Lila and Tom love to play at the park. They like to swing, climb and run. But their favorite thing is to slide. They slide down the big slide, the small slide and the twisty slide. They laugh and shout as they go fast. One day, they see a new slide at the park. It is very high and long. It has a tunnel and a curve. Lila and Tom are curious and excited. They want to try the new slide. They climb up the stairs and wait for their turn. When it is Lila's turn, she sits on the slide and holds on tight. She slides down the tunnel and feels the wind on her face. She slides around the curve and sees a charming view. She sees the trees, the flowers and the birds. She sees the sun, the sky and the clouds. She smiles and says, "Wow!" When it is Tom's turn, he sits on the slide and pushes off hard. He slides down the tunnel and feels the thrill in his tummy. He slides around the curve and sees a charming view. He sees the lake, the bridge and the boats. He sees the ducks, the fish and the frogs. He cheers and says, "Cool!" Lila and Tom slide down the new slide again and again. They have so much fun. They think the new slide is the best slide ever. They are happy and grateful. They say, "Thank you, park!"
