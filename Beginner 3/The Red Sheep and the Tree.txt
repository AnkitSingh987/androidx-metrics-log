Category: Beginner
Title: The Red Sheep and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a red sheep. The red sheep lived on a farm with other sheep. The red sheep was not happy because it was different from the other sheep. One day, the red sheep saw a big tree. The red sheep wanted to lean on the tree to feel better. The tree was big and tall, and the red sheep thought it would be nice to rest there. As the red sheep leaned on the tree, the tree started to fall. The red sheep tried to run away, but it was too late. The tree fell on the red sheep, and the red sheep was hurt. The other sheep saw what happened and felt sad for the red sheep. The red sheep wished it had not leaned on the tree.

Lily and Ben were brother and sister. They liked to play in the garden, but sometimes they would quarrel. One day, they found a big hole in the ground. They wondered what was inside. "Let's go and see!" said Ben. "No, it's too dark and scary!" said Lily. "Are you a baby?" said Ben. "I'm not afraid. I'll go first and you follow me." Ben jumped into the hole and Lily followed him. They went down and down until they reached the underground. It was very dark and cold. They could not see anything. "Ben, where are you?" said Lily. "I'm here, Lily. Don't worry. I have a flashlight." said Ben. He turned on the flashlight and pointed it around. They saw many strange things in the underground. There were bones, spiders, worms and rats. Lily was very scared. "Ben, I don't like it here. Let's go back!" said Lily. "OK, OK. Let's go back." said Ben. They tried to find the way back, but they could not. They had walked too far and got lost. They walked and walked, but they only found more dark and scary things. "Ben, I'm tired and hungry. I want to go home!" said Lily. "Me too, Lily. Me too." said Ben. They started to cry. They wished they had not quarreled and had not gone into the hole. They wished they had listened to their mom and dad. They wished they had stayed in the garden. But it was too late. They were stuck in the underground. It was terrible.

Once there was a little tree growing all alone in the big, wide field. It was the only tree, so it was lonely. Its branches were covered in knots and it always wondered why. One day it saw an old man walking past and it waved at him. The old man waved back and came over to the tree. He told the tree that the knots were his way of giving it hugs every time he passed by. The little tree smiled and grew bigger and bigger. And every time the old man passed by, the knots would become a little bit more. The tree was no longer lonely and each new knot reminded the tree of the old man’s love. Eventually, the tree grew so big and strong that it provided shade for other animals. Together, the tree and the old man had grown something beautiful!

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play with toys and books. Today, they have a quiz at school. The quiz is about animals and colors. Tom is good at the quiz. He knows the names of many animals and colors. He can say them loud and clear. Lily is not so good at the quiz. She does not understand some words. She feels sad and scared. "Tom, can you help me?" Lily whispers to Tom. "I do not know this word. What is it?" Tom looks at Lily's paper. He sees the word "elephant". He knows what it is. It is a big animal with a long nose and big ears. He likes elephants. They are gray and smart. "Shh, Lily, you cannot talk. It is a quiz. You have to do it by yourself." Tom says. He does not want to be nosy. He wants to be a good friend. But he also wants to follow the rules. "But I do not understand. Please, Tom, please." Lily begs. She is afraid to get a bad mark. She wants to be a good student. The teacher hears Lily and Tom. She walks to their desks. She is not happy. She does not like when children talk during a quiz. She thinks they are cheating. "Lily and Tom, what are you doing? Why are you talking?" The teacher asks. She looks at their papers. She sees the word "elephant" on Lily's paper. She sees that Tom has the same word on his paper. "Tom, did you tell Lily the answer?" The teacher asks. She sounds angry. Tom feels nervous. He does not want to lie. He does not want to get in trouble. He does not know what to say. Lily feels sorry. She knows she did something wrong. She does not want to make Tom sad. She does not want to make the teacher angry. She decides to tell the truth. "Teacher, I am sorry. I did not understand the word. I asked Tom to help me. He did not tell me the answer. He told me to be quiet. He is a good friend. He is not nosy. He is smart." Lily says. The teacher listens to Lily. She sees that Lily is honest. She sees that Tom is loyal. She sees that they are both sorry. She decides to be kind. "Thank you, Lily, for telling the truth. I appreciate your honesty. But you should not ask for help during a quiz. You should try to do it by yourself. You can learn from your mistakes. You can ask me if you do not understand something. I am here to help you." The teacher says. She turns to Tom. She smiles. "Thank you, Tom, for being a good friend. I appreciate your loyalty. But you should not talk during a quiz. You should focus on your own work. You can help Lily after the quiz. You can teach her what you know. You are smart and helpful." The teacher says. She gives them both a new paper. She tells them to do the quiz again. She tells them to be quiet and careful. Tom and Lily nod. They say sorry again. They do the quiz again. They do not talk. They do their best. They are happy. They are friends.
