Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Modest Girl and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a modest little girl named Lily. She loved to draw with her crayons. One day, she found a big box of crayons under her bed. She was so happy! Lily wanted to share the crayons with her friend, Tom. She called out, "Tom, come here! I have a big box of crayons. Let's draw together!" Tom came running and saw the crayons. He was excited too. They sat down and started drawing with the crayons. They drew trees, flowers, and animals. They had so much fun. After they were done, they showed their drawings to their moms. Their moms were very proud. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim had a kite. He wanted to use it outside. Tim was an honest boy. He always told the truth. He asked his mom if he could go outside and play with his kite. His mom said yes, but only if he stayed close to home. Tim went outside and started to use his kite. The wind was strong, and the kite went up high in the sky. Tim was happy. He loved to see his kite fly. But then, something unexpected happened. A big bird came and took his kite away! Tim was sad, but he knew he had to be honest and tell his mom what happened. He went inside and told her about the bird. His mom was surprised but not mad. She said they could get a new kite. Tim was happy again, but he still missed his old kite. The next day, Tim and his mom went to the park with a new kite. They started to use the kite, and it went up high in the sky. Then, the big bird came back! But this time, the bird had Tim's old kite in its beak. The bird dropped the old kite down to Tim and flew away. Tim was very happy to have his old kite back. He learned that being honest was good, and sometimes, unexpected things can happen.

One day, a shy boy named Tim went to the gym with his mom. Tim had never been to a gym before. He looked around and saw many kids playing and having fun. Tim felt a little scared to join them. Tim's mom said, "Go play with the other kids. It will be fun." Tim wondered if he could make friends with the other kids. He took a deep breath and went to play with them. At first, Tim was very shy. But after a while, he started to have fun with his new friends. They played catch and jumped on the big mats. Tim was so happy that he didn't want to leave the gym. He couldn't wait to come back and play again.

One day, a jolly girl named Lily went for a walk. She liked to step on the pretty leaves that fell on the ground. As she walked, she heard a nice sound. It was a violin! Lily wanted to know where the sound came from. Lily found a little cat playing the violin. The cat was very good at it. Lily said, "Wow, you can play the violin! Can you teach me?" The cat nodded and gave Lily the violin. Lily tried to play the violin, but it made a loud, bad sound. The cat jumped, and the violin broke! Lily and the cat were sad. They did not know what to do. Then, a kind bird flew down and said, "Don't worry! I can fix your violin." The bird fixed the violin with its beak, and it was like new. Lily and the cat were so happy. They thanked the bird and played music together, making the day jolly again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy that could glow. He loved his toy very much. One day, Tim saw a cup on the table. The cup was full of something. Tim wanted to see what was inside the cup. Tim went to the cup and looked inside. It was full of something green and disgusting. He did not like it. But Tim had an idea. He put his glowing toy in the cup to make the green stuff glow too. He thought it would make the green stuff look nice. But when Tim put his toy in the cup, the green stuff got on his toy. His toy stopped glowing. Tim was very sad. The green stuff had broken his toy. Now, Tim had no toy that could glow. He learned that putting his toy in disgusting things was not a good idea.
