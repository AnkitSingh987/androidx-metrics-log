Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Porch

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play on his porch with his toy cars. One sunny day, Tim was playing with his cars when he saw a big, heavy box on the porch. He did not know what was inside. Tim ran to his mom and said, "Mom, there is a heavy box on the porch! What is in it?" His mom smiled and said, "Let's open it and see." They went to the porch and opened the box. Inside the box, there were many plates and cups. Tim's mom said, "These are to serve food on when we have friends over." Just then, a small puppy jumped out of the box! Tim and his mom were very surprised. The puppy had been hiding under the plates and cups. Tim laughed and said, "We have a new friend to serve food to!" They all played on the porch, and Tim was very happy to have a new puppy friend.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a fast rabbit. The rabbit loved to run and play all day. He had many friends, but his best friend was a big bear. The bear was not fast, but he was very strong. One day, the rabbit found a sharp spear in the forest. He thought it was fun to play with. He showed it to the bear, but the bear said, "No, rabbit, we must stay away from the spear. It is not safe." But the rabbit did not listen. He kept playing with the spear. One day, while he was running fast, he tripped and fell on the spear. The rabbit was hurt, and he could not run anymore. The bear found his friend and tried to help him. But it was too late. The rabbit had not listened to the bear, and now he could not run and play anymore. The rabbit and the bear were very sad, and they knew they should have stayed away from the spear.

One day, a big dog named Max went to play near a stream. He saw a huge rock and thought it would be fun to jump on it. Max wanted to perform a trick for his friends, so he got ready to jump. But when Max jumped, he slipped on the rock and fell into the stream. His friends gasped as they saw him fall. Max was scared and didn't know what to do. He started to swim, but the water was moving fast. Then, something unexpected happened. A small fish swam up to Max and said, "Don't be scared, I can help you!" The fish showed Max how to swim with the stream and get back to the bank. Max was so happy and thanked the fish. He had made a new friend and learned that even the smallest creatures can be very helpful.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy cat named Tom. Tom loved to eat fish. Every day, he would go to the pond and try to catch a fish. But Tom was very sleepy, so he would often fall asleep while trying to catch the fish. One day, Tom met a wise old owl named Mr. Hoot. Mr. Hoot knew a lot about history. He told Tom stories about the past. Tom liked to listen to Mr. Hoot's stories. He learned that cats have been trying to catch fish for a very long time. Tom wanted to catch a fish like the cats in history. So he tried to be less sleepy. He went to bed early and woke up early. The next day, Tom went to the pond again. This time, he was not sleepy. He caught a big fish and ate it happily. Tom was proud to be like the cats in history.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had a long tail. He liked to wag his tail when he was happy. One day, Spot saw a big, red ball. He thought it was a toy and started to play with it. Spot did not know that the big, red ball was not a toy. It was a very important thing that belonged to a big man. The big man saw Spot playing with the big, red ball and got very angry. The big man thought Spot was a stupid dog. The big man decided to punish Spot for playing with the big, red ball. He took Spot's long tail and tied it to a tree. Spot was very sad and scared. He could not wag his tail anymore. The big man left Spot tied to the tree, and Spot stayed there, unable to play or wag his tail ever again.
