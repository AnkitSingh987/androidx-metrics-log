Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Box

Once there was a girl named Lily. She liked to be quiet and play alone. One day, Lily found a pretty box in her room. She opened the box and saw a beautiful bracelet inside. The bracelet was shiny and had many colors. Lily wanted to fill the bracelet with special things. She found small stones, flowers, and leaves from the garden. She put them all in the bracelet. The bracelet looked even more beautiful now. Lily wore her bracelet all day. She felt happy and proud. When her mom saw the bracelet, she smiled. Lily showed her friends the bracelet too. They all loved it and wanted to make one too. Lily was happy she could share her quiet fun with her friends.

Lila and Ben were playing with blocks. They liked to build towers and houses and bridges. Lila had a lot of blocks, but Ben only had a few. He wanted more blocks to make his tower bigger. "Can I have some of your blocks, Lila?" Ben asked. "No, they are mine. You have enough," Lila said. "But your blocks are better. They are steel. They are shiny and strong. My blocks are wood. They are dull and weak," Ben said. He was envious of Lila's blocks. "That's not true. Your blocks are good too. They are colorful and smooth. And they fit together well. See, I can make a house with your blocks," Lila said. She took some of Ben's blocks and made a house. Ben looked at the house. It was nice. He smiled. "Can I help you make the house, Lila?" Ben asked. "Sure, you can. Here, take some of my blocks. They are steel. They are shiny and strong. We can make the roof with them," Lila said. She gave some of her blocks to Ben. Ben took the blocks. They were heavy and cold. He tried to fit them on the house, but they were too big and hard. They did not fit well. "These blocks are not good for the house, Lila. They are too big and hard. They do not fit well. They make the house fall down," Ben said. He was sad. "I'm sorry, Ben. You are right. These blocks are not good for the house. They are good for other things, like bridges or cars. But your blocks are good for the house. They are colorful and smooth. And they fit together well. Let's use your blocks for the house, and my blocks for something else," Lila said. She was sorry. "Okay, Lila. That sounds good. Let's use your blocks for something else. Like a bridge or a car. And let's use my blocks for the house. They are colorful and smooth. And they fit together well. We can make a nice house," Ben said. He was happy. Lila and Ben used their blocks to make a house, a bridge, and a car. They played with their blocks for a long time. They were not envious of each other's blocks. They were friends.

Once upon a time, there was a humble cat named Tom. Tom liked to organize his toys every day. He had a big box where he put all his toys in. One day, Tom found a note on the floor. The note said, "I took your toys. Love, Dog." Tom was sad and wanted his toys back. He went to find Dog to ask for his toys. Tom found Dog and said, "Dog, please give me my toys back." Dog felt sorry and said, "Okay, Tom. I will give you your toys back." Tom and Dog became friends and played with the toys together. They both learned to share and organize their toys every day.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They saw a cactus. The cactus was very harsh. Tim's mom said, "Don't touch it, Tim. It can hurt you." Tim did not listen. He wanted to pinch the cactus. When he did, he felt a big pain. "Ouch!" Tim cried. His mom was right. The cactus was not nice to touch. Then, something unexpected happened. A small bird flew down and landed on the cactus. The bird did not get hurt. Tim was amazed. He learned that some things are not good for him to touch, but they can be okay for others.
