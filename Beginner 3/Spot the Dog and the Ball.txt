Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was an obedient dog. He always did what his owner, Tim, told him to do. They played a lot and had fun together. One day, Tim and Spot went to the park. They had a good start to their day. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Tim threw a ball for Spot to catch. Spot ran very fast to get the ball. While running, Spot saw a big rock. He did not see it soon enough and he stumbled on the rock. Spot fell down, but he was okay. He got up and kept running. Spot got the ball and brought it back to Tim. They played all day and had a great time.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to help her mom in the kitchen. She was a very gentle girl who loved to be helpful. One day, Sue's mom asked her to wash the plates. Sue was so happy to help. She filled a big bowl with warm, soapy water. Then, she took a soft cloth and started to wash the plates. Sue washed one plate, then two, then three. She was very gentle with the plates, so they did not break. When she finished, all the plates were clean and shiny. Sue's mom was so proud of her. They both smiled and hugged. Sue felt happy that she could help her mom and be a gentle helper.

Once upon a time, a polite girl named Lily lived in a small house. She loved to cook yummy food for her friends. One day, Lily found a passport on the ground. Lily asked her friend Tom, "Do you know whose passport this is?" Tom said, "No, but we can ask our friends if they lost their passport." So, Lily and Tom went to their friends' houses to ask if the passport belonged to them. They knocked on each door and asked politely, "Is this your passport?" But no one said yes. Finally, they found their friend Sue. Sue said, "Yes, that is my passport! Thank you for finding it." Lily and Tom were happy to help Sue. To say thank you, Sue came to Lily's house to cook a big meal for everyone. They all ate the yummy food and had lots of fun.

One day, Tom and Sue went to the park. They wanted to play near the big post. The post was very tall and had a bell on top. Tom and Sue liked to listen to the bell when it rang. Tom said, "I want to ring the bell!" But it was difficult for him to reach. Sue tried to help Tom, but she was too small too. They were sad because they couldn't ring the bell. Just then, a big, friendly dog came running to them. The dog jumped up and hit the bell with its paw. The bell rang loud and clear. Tom and Sue were so happy! They laughed and said, "Thank you, dog!" The dog wagged its tail and ran away. Now, they could listen to the bell whenever they wanted.

One day, a soft crab was playing near the water. The crab liked to play with the tide. When the tide came in, the crab would ride the water. When the tide went out, the crab would run on the sand. A big fish saw the crab and said, "You should not play with the tide. It can be dangerous. If you don't listen, I will punish you." The crab did not want to be punished. So, the crab stopped playing with the tide. The crab found a safe place to play on the sand. The big fish was happy and did not punish the crab. They both played together and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a friendly cat named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toy mouse. One day, he saw a big box in the living room. Tim was very curious. Tim went to the box and gave it a little shake. Inside, he heard something move. "Who's there?" Tim asked. "I am a new friend," said a voice from inside the box. Tim was excited to meet his new friend. Tim opened the box and saw a cute dog named Sam. They played together all day. They did not want to waste any time. Tim and Sam became the best of friends and lived happily ever after.
