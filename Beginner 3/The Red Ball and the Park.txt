Category: Beginner
Title: The Red Ball and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a red ball. The red ball had a big tooth. The red ball liked to play with kids. One day, a boy named Tim saw the red ball. Tim said, "Hi red ball! Can I play with you?" The red ball was happy and said, "Yes, Tim! Let's play!" Tim and the red ball played in the park. Tim would kick the red ball, and the red ball would bounce back to him. They played all day, and Tim's friends came to play too. At the end of the day, the red ball was tired. Tim said, "Thank you, red ball, for playing with me. Let's play again soon!" The red ball smiled with its big tooth and said, "I had fun, Tim! See you soon!" And they both went home, excited to play together again.

Once upon a time there was a boy named Jack. Jack lived with his Mom and Dad, who loved him very much. One day Jack’s Mom and Dad asked him to clean his room. Jack was feeling lazy, so he did not do it. “Jack, you must do your chores,” said Mom. “But it’s boring,” said Jack. Mom and Dad said that if Jack cleaned his room, he would get a trophy. Jack thought that sounded fun. So he listened to Mom and Dad, and began to clean. When Jack was done, he looked around his room. He had done a great job! But when Jack asked Mom and Dad for his trophy, they said there was no trophy. They said he did not deserve one for being so lazy. Jack felt very sad. He was disappointed that he did not get the trophy he wanted. He learned a lesson that day, to always do his chores even if he is feeling lazy.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a smelly cushion. The cushion was sad because no one wanted to sit on it. One day, a little girl named Lily came to the house. She saw the smelly cushion and felt sad for it. Lily said, "Why are you so smelly, cushion?" The cushion started to weep. "I don't know," it cried. "I just want to be soft and nice like the other cushions." Lily wanted to help the cushion. She took it outside and gave it a good wash. She scrubbed and scrubbed until the smelly cushion was clean and soft. The cushion was very happy and said, "Thank you, Lily!" From that day on, the cushion was no longer smelly. It was soft and nice like the other cushions. Lily and the cushion became the best of friends, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a thick book full of stories. A little boy named Tim loved to read the stories in the book. One day, Tim found a new story in the book. It was about a big, kind bear and a little, happy bird. The bear and the bird were friends. They liked to play and talk with each other. One day, the bear said, "I am tired. Let's settle under this tree and rest." The bird agreed and said, "Yes, let's rest here. We can share a story to make us feel good." So, the bear and the bird sat under the tree. The bird told a funny story about a silly frog who tried to jump to the moon. The bear laughed and laughed. The bird and the bear felt happy and loved their time together under the tree. They knew that sharing stories made their friendship strong and special.

Anna and Ben were playing in the park. They saw a big sign that said "Zoo". They wanted to see the animals. "Can we go to the zoo, Mom?" Anna asked. "Please, Mom, please!" Ben said. Mom smiled and nodded. "Okay, we can go to the zoo. But we have to follow the rules. No running, no shouting, no feeding the animals. And we have to stay together. Do you understand?" Anna and Ben said yes. They were very happy. They followed Mom to the zoo. They saw many animals. They saw lions, monkeys, elephants, and giraffes. They liked them all. But they liked one animal the most. It was a rhinoceros. It had a big horn on its nose and thick skin. It looked strong and brave. "Wow, look at the rhinoceros!" Anna said. "It's so cool!" Ben said. They went closer to the fence. They wanted to see it better. They saw a sign that said "Do not touch the rhinoceros. It may hurt you." But Ben did not read the sign. He was too curious. He reached out his hand to touch the rhinoceros. He thought it would be easy. "Ben, no!" Anna shouted. But it was too late. The rhinoceros saw Ben's hand. It did not like it. It moved its head and hit Ben's hand with its horn. Ben felt a sharp pain. He cried out loud. "Ow, ow, ow!" Ben said. Mom heard Ben's cry. She ran to him. She saw his hand. It was bleeding. She was scared and angry. "Ben, what did you do?" Mom said. "Didn't you see the sign? Didn't you hear the rules? You could have been hurt badly. You were very naughty." She took Ben to the nurse. The nurse cleaned his wound and put a bandage on it. She gave him a lollipop to make him feel better. "Next time, be more careful and listen to your mom," the nurse said. Ben nodded. He was sorry. He learned his lesson. He did not touch the rhinoceros again. He did not request anything else. He just held Mom's hand and said sorry. Mom hugged him and said it was okay. She was glad he was not hurt worse. Anna was glad too. She did not want her brother to be hurt. She also did not want to miss the other animals. She asked Mom if they could see the rest of the zoo. Mom smiled and said yes. They went to see the other animals. They had fun. But they never forgot the rhinoceros.
