Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Boat

One day, a little girl named Mia was playing with her toy boat in a small river. The sun was shining, and it was a good day. Mia loved to watch her boat float and make waves. Suddenly, the sky turned dark, and it started to rain. The rain made a flood in the river. Mia's toy boat was going too fast, and she could not catch it. She was sad and started to cry. A friendly frog saw Mia and asked, "Why are you crying?" Mia told the frog about her lost toy boat. The frog said, "I can help you get your boat back." With a snap of his fingers, the frog jumped high and caught the boat. He gave the boat back to Mia, and she was very happy. They both played together until the sun came out again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to play outside. One day, it was rainy. Amy's mom said, "Amy, take your umbrella." Amy took her amazing umbrella with her. Amy went outside and saw a cat. The cat was wet. Amy said, "Come, cat. Sit under my umbrella." The cat sat under the umbrella with Amy. They both stayed dry. Amy and the cat became friends. They played under the umbrella all day. The rain stopped, but they still had fun. The cat said, "Thank you, Amy." And they lived happily ever after.

Lily liked to read books. She had many books on her bookshelf. She liked to look at the pictures and the words. Sometimes she read to her teddy bear. One day, she saw a big book on the top of the bookshelf. She wanted to read it. She tried to reach it, but she was too small. She tried to climb on a chair, but it was too wobbly. She tried to jump, but she could not jump high enough. She asked her mom for help. "Mom, can you get me that book?" she said. "Which book, Lily?" her mom said. "That one, on the top. It looks fun," Lily said. Her mom looked at the bookshelf. She smiled. She knew what book it was. She had put it there on purpose. It was a surprise for Lily. "Okay, Lily. I will get you that book. But first, you have to do something for me," her mom said. "What, mom?" Lily said. "You have to close your eyes and count to ten," her mom said. Lily was curious. She did what her mom said. She closed her eyes and counted. "One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten!" She opened her eyes. Her mom was holding the book. She gave it to Lily. Lily was happy. She hugged her mom and thanked her. She looked at the book. It was a silly book. It had funny pictures and jokes. It was called "The Book with No Pictures". Lily laughed. She loved it. She read it to her teddy bear. They had a lot of fun. Lily was glad she tried to get the book. It was the best book ever.

Once upon a time, there was a patient mouse named Max. Max loved to watch the world around him. One day he heard a funny sound coming from the garden. He cautiously ventured closer and squinted his eyes to try and make out what was making the sound! Max finally saw a beautiful bird perched in a tree singing a sweet song. Max sat in amazement and watched the bird for a long time. When the bird began to fly away, Max said out loud, "Thank you for your song". The bird stopped in the sky and hovered, turning around to face Max. It said in a soft voice, "You are so patient and kind. The world needs more mouse like you." Max said, "I'm just trying to learn. Will you come back and teach me more?" The bird smiled and said, "I will come back as often as I can. Be patient and you will learn". Max gazed in wonder as the bird flew away. He was excited to see his teacher soon!

One day, Tom and his mom went to the mall. Tom saw a toy he liked. It was a flexible snake. He asked his mom, "Can I have it, please?" His mom said, "No, we have to buy food." Tom was sad. He wanted the snake. He saw a game at the mall. The game had many things to match. If Tom could match them all, he would win the snake. Tom said, "I want to play!" Tom played the game. He tried to match the things. It was hard. But Tom did not give up. He kept trying. Finally, he matched all the things. He won the flexible snake! Tom was very happy. His mom was proud of him. They went home with the snake and the food. Tom played with his new toy all day.
