Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Basket

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She was very pale and loved to play outside. One sunny day, Mia and her mom decided to have a picnic at the park. Mia's mom said, "Mia, do you dare to help me pack the picnic basket?" Mia smiled and said, "Yes, Mommy! I dare!" They packed yummy sandwiches, fruits, and juice in the basket. At the park, Mia and her mom spread a big blanket on the grass. They sat down and enjoyed their picnic. They talked, laughed, and shared their food. After the picnic, they played together until the sun went down. And they went home happy and tired, looking forward to their next picnic adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted dog named Spot. Spot could see very well. He liked to help his friends find things they lost. One day, he saw a shelter in the woods. Inside the shelter, he found a cat named Fluffy. Spot and Fluffy became good friends. They played together every day. One day, when they were playing near the shelter, they saw a big box. It was a gift for Spot and Fluffy. They were very happy and excited. But when they opened the box, they found another little dog inside. It was a surprise! The little dog was lost and needed a home. Spot and Fluffy decided to help the little dog find his way back. They all became best friends and lived happily in the shelter together.

One day, a cat named Tom was very tired. He wanted to take a nap. He looked for a nice space to sleep. He found a big box. Tom thought, "This is a good space for a nap!" Tom's friend, a dog named Max, saw Tom go into the box. Max was an obedient dog. He wanted to watch the box and make sure Tom was safe. Max said, "I will stay here and watch my friend." While Tom was napping, a bird came and picked up the box. The bird flew away with the box! Max saw this and thought, "Oh no! I must save Tom!" Max ran and jumped. He caught the bird's leg. The bird let go of the box. Tom woke up and saw Max. He said, "Thank you, Max! You saved me!" Max was happy that he could help his friend. They went home together, and Tom took a nap in a safe space.

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing in the park. She saw her friend, Tim, on the other side. Lily wanted to give Tim a big hug, so she began to rush to him. As Lily ran, she saw a healthy, big dog near Tim. The dog was wagging its tail and looked friendly. Lily thought the dog wanted a hug too. "Can I hug your dog?" she asked Tim. "Yes, you can!" Tim said. But as Lily went to hug the dog, it jumped up and took a toy from her hand. Lily was surprised, but she laughed. The dog just wanted to play. So, Lily and Tim played with the dog and had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big pit. The pit was very interesting. Every day, children would come to see it. They liked to throw stones in the pit and listen to the sounds. One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to go to the pit. He asked his mom, "Can I go to the pit?" His mom said, "No, it's not safe." But Tim insisted, "Please, mom. I want to see the interesting pit." Finally, his mom said, "Okay, but be careful." Tim went to the pit and saw his friends. They threw stones in the pit and had fun. Then, they went home to tell their moms and dads about the interesting pit.

One day, a noisy bird named Tim was flying in the sky. He saw a big, dry tree. Tim thought it was a good place to rest. He sat on a branch and saw a stamp on the ground. "Hi, Mr. Stamp!" said Tim. "Why are you here on the ground?" The stamp said, "I fell from a girl's hand. She was playing with me, but I am lost now." Suddenly, a strong wind came and blew the stamp up into the air. The stamp landed on Tim's wing. The wind was so strong that it pushed Tim and the stamp into the air. They flew higher and higher. Tim was scared, but then he saw the girl who lost the stamp. She was looking for it. Tim flew down to her with the stamp on his wing. The girl was very happy to see her stamp. She said, "Thank you, Tim! You are a good bird!"
