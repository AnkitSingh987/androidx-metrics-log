Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a friend named Tom. Tom was a very reliable friend. They liked to play together. One day, Tim and Tom went to the creek near their house. They saw fish in the water. They played with the fish and splashed water. They had a lot of fun. The next morning, Tim was excited to wake up and play with Tom again. He ran to the creek and found Tom there. They played all day and had a great time. And they were always the best of friends.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went for a walk. She saw a square on the ground. She picked it up and said, "I can imagine this square can talk." "Hello, Lucy!" said the square. "I am a magic square. I can make delicious food for you." Lucy was very happy. Lucy asked the square, "Can you make me a big, delicious cake?" The square said, "Yes, but you have to close your eyes and count to three." Lucy closed her eyes and counted, "One, two, three!" When she opened her eyes, she saw a big, delicious cake in front of her. But the cake had legs and started to run away! Lucy and the square laughed and chased the cake together.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a white mitten in her room. She put it on her hand and went outside to play. The sun was shining very bright, and the sky was blue. Lily saw her friend, Tom, who was playing with his red ball. "Look, Tom!" said Lily, showing her white mitten. "I found it in my room. It's so soft and warm." Tom smiled and said, "That's a nice mitten, Lily. But it's too hot today to wear it. Maybe you can wear it when it's cold." Lily and Tom played with the red ball all day. The sun started to go down, and it was not as hot anymore. Lily felt a little cold, so she put on her white mitten. She was happy that she had it with her.

One day, a nosy cat saw a big pear on a tree. The cat wanted to eat the pear. She tried to jump and get it, but she could not. The cat was sad. A kind dog saw the sad cat and asked, "Why are you sad, cat?" The cat said, "I want the pear, but I can't get it." The dog had an idea. "I will help you get the pear. You can rub my back, and I will feel good. Then, I will get the pear for you." The cat rubbed the dog's back, and the dog felt good. The dog jumped high and got the pear for the cat. But when the dog gave the pear to the cat, it was not a pear. It was a ball! They laughed and played with the ball. The nosy cat and the kind dog became good friends. They learned that helping each other is better than being nosy or sad. And they played with the ball every day.

One day, a busy bee named Ben went to find some sugar. He flew to a big tree where his friend, a tiny ant named Abby, lived. Ben asked Abby, "Can you show me where to find some sugar?" Abby said, "Yes, follow me!" They walked and walked until they saw a big, red apple on the ground. Abby said, "Here is some sugar!" Ben was happy and said, "Thank you, Abby!" But then, something unexpected happened. A big bird came down and took the apple away! Ben and Abby were sad. They looked at each other and said, "Let's find more sugar together!" And they went on a new adventure to find more sweet treats.

One day, a red bird named Ruby saw a rainbow in the sky. She wanted to fly up and touch it. But Ruby was scared. She had never flown so high before. Ruby's friend, a blue bird named Bobby, saw her looking at the rainbow. He said, "You can do it, Ruby! I will encourage you!" Ruby felt better with her friend's help. Together, they flew up to the rainbow. It was hard, and Ruby was tired. But she did not give up. Bobby cheered her on, saying, "You're doing great, Ruby!" At last, they reached the rainbow and touched it. Ruby was so happy and proud.
