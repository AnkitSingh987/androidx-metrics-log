Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Jen and the Leg

Tom and Jen are friends. They like to play with toys and books. One day, they find a big machine in the basement. It has buttons and a screen. Jen says, "Let's print something!" Tom says, "OK, but be careful. Mom and Dad said not to touch the machine." They look at the screen and see many pictures. They see a picture of a leg. Jen says, "I want to print a leg. It looks funny." Tom says, "No, Jen. That's a bad idea. The leg is not a toy. It is for people who need it." Jen says, "I don't care. I want to print it." She pushes the button. The machine makes a loud noise and a leg comes out. It is not a real leg. It is made of metal and plastic. It has wires and screws. Jen is happy. She says, "Look, Tom. I printed a leg. It is cool." Tom is scared. He says, "Jen, you are in trouble. Mom and Dad will be angry. Put the leg back in the machine." Jen does not listen. She picks up the leg and runs with it. She says, "I want to play with the leg. It is mine." Tom follows her. He says, "Jen, stop. The leg is not a toy. It is dangerous." Jen does not stop. She runs up the stairs and into the living room. Mom and Dad are there. They see Jen with the leg. They are shocked. They say, "Jen, what are you doing? Where did you get that leg?" Jen says, "I printed it. It is my toy." Mom and Dad are angry. They say, "Jen, that is not a toy. That is a bad thing. You should not have touched the machine. You are in big trouble." They take the leg from Jen and send her to her room. Jen is sad. She cries. She says, "I'm sorry. I just wanted to play." Tom is sad too. He says, "I'm sorry too. I tried to stop her." Mom and Dad are sad too. They say, "We're sorry too. We should have locked the machine. We hope no one finds out what we did." They look at the leg. It has a label. It says, "Property of the military. Top secret. Do not print."

One day, a big, impatient dog named Max found a tool in the park. He wanted to use it to split his big toy ball. Max was so excited that he ran to his friend, the small cat named Lily. "Look, Lily, I found a tool to split my big ball!" Max said. Lily looked at the tool and then at Max. She was not sure if it was a good idea. But Max was very impatient and wanted to try it right away. Max used the tool on his big ball, but something unexpected happened. Instead of splitting the ball, it made a loud sound and the ball jumped high into the sky. Max and Lily looked up and saw the ball fly away. Max felt sad because he lost his ball. Lily told Max, "Next time, we should think before we try something new." The moral value of the story is to think before you act, especially when you are impatient.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play and eat. One day, Fluffy ate too much food. His tummy got big and he felt heavy. Fluffy's weight was more than before. Fluffy went to the park to play with his friend, Bunny. Bunny saw Fluffy's big tummy and said, "Oh, Fluffy! You look so big!" Fluffy felt embarrassed. He did not like to be called big. Fluffy and Bunny played in the park. They ran and jumped. Fluffy's big tummy made it hard to play. Bunny said, "Let's eat less and play more, so we can have fun!" Fluffy agreed, and they played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, a polite girl named Lily found a camera. She loved to take pictures of everything. One day, she took the camera to the park to take pictures of her friends. At the park, Lily met her friend Tom. "Let's split this cookie," said Tom. So, they broke the cookie in half and shared it. After that, they played and laughed together. Lily took pictures of Tom and her other friends with her camera. They had so much fun. At the end of the day, they said goodbye and went home. Lily looked at the pictures on her camera and smiled. She loved her day at the park with her friends.
