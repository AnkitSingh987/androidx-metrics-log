Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Door

Lily likes to play dress-up with her mom's clothes and accessories. She has a big box full of hats, scarves, necklaces, and masks. She thinks masks are fun because they can make her look like someone else. She has a mask with feathers, a mask with glitter, and a mask with a long nose. One day, she finds a new mask in the box. It is very unique. It has a shiny key on the forehead, and a lock on the mouth. Lily wonders what it means. She puts it on and looks in the mirror. She feels like a secret agent. She decides to go on a mission with her mask. She sneaks out of her room and walks down the hall. She sees her dad working on his laptop in his office. She thinks he might have some clues for her mission. She tiptoes to his door and tries to open it. But it is locked. She looks at her mask and sees the key. She thinks maybe the key can open the door. She takes off her mask and puts the key in the lock. She turns it and hears a click. She opens the door and goes inside. She sees her dad's laptop on his desk. She thinks it might have some secrets. She tries to turn it on, but it asks for a password. She does not know the password. She looks around and sees a paper with some numbers and letters on it. She thinks it might be the password. She types it in and presses enter. She sees a lot of words and pictures on the screen. She does not understand them. She thinks they might be codes. She tries to find some clues. She hears a noise behind her. She turns around and sees her dad. He looks surprised and angry. He sees her mask and his key. He sees his laptop and his paper. He says, "Lily, what are you doing? You are not supposed to be here. You are not supposed to touch my things. You are not supposed to wear that mask. That mask is very special. It belongs to your grandma. She gave it to me before she died. It is a family heirloom. It has a story behind it. The key and the lock are symbols of love and trust. You cannot just play with it. You have to respect it. You have to give it back to me." Lily feels scared and sad. She did not know the mask was so important. She did not mean to upset her dad. She did not mean to break the rules. She says, "I'm sorry, dad. I was just playing. I thought the mask was fun. I thought it was unique. I wanted to go on a mission. I wanted to find some clues. I did not know the mask was grandma's. I did not know the key and the lock were special. I did not know the laptop and the paper were yours. I did not know the password. I did not know the codes. I did not know anything. Please don't be mad at me. Please forgive me." Her dad sighs and hugs her. He says, "I'm sorry, too, Lily. I was too harsh. I know you were just curious. I know you like to explore. I know you have a big imagination. I love that about you. But you have to be careful. You have to ask permission. You have to listen to me. You have to learn some things. The mask is not a toy. It is a treasure. It has a history. It has a meaning. I will tell you about it someday. But for now, you have to give it back to me. And you have to promise me you won't touch it again. And you won't touch my laptop or my paper either. They are not for you. They are for my work. They are confidential. You have to respect them. You have to promise me." Lily nods and says, "I promise, dad. I will give you back the mask. I will not touch it again. I will not touch your laptop or your paper either. I will respect them. I will respect you. I love you, dad." Her dad smiles and says, "I love you, too, Lily. You are my unique and wonderful daughter. Now, let's go back to your room and find something else to play with. How about some puzzles or books or dolls? They are more suitable for you. They are more fun for you. They are more safe for you." Lily agrees and follows her dad. She takes off the mask and hands it to him. She feels a little sad to say goodbye to it.
