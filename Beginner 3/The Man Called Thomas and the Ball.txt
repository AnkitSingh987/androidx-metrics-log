Category: Beginner
Title: The Man Called Thomas and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a man called Thomas. He had a cane and he always used it to get around the town. One day, Thomas went to the park and he saw a little boy there. The boy was crying and he was very scared. "What's wrong?" Thomas asked. "My ball is stuck in the tree!" the little boy yelled. Thomas was very kind and he used his cane to get the ball out of the tree. When the little boy got his ball back, he was so happy. He yelled out with joy, “Thank you, Thomas!” Thomas smiled and said, “You're welcome, my friend.” The little boy was no longer fearful.

Once upon a time, there was a big car. It was a very comfortable car. It had soft seats and big windows. The car liked to drive fast and slow. It also liked to make a "beep beep" sound. One day, the car needed gas. It went to the gas place. The car was happy when it got gas. Now, it could drive more. The car saw a big tree. It wanted to go near the tree. But, it needed to reverse. So, the car went back, back, back. It stopped near the tree. The car was happy. It could now see the big tree up close.

Ben and Lily were having breakfast with Mom and Dad. They liked to eat oat with milk and honey. Oat was good for them, Mom said. It made them strong and smart. But today, the oat was smelly. Ben and Lily did not want to eat it. They made faces and pushed their bowls away. Mom and Dad smelled the oat and made faces too. "Yuck, what is wrong with the oat?" Mom asked. Dad looked at the box of oat. He saw a hole in it. He opened the box and saw something brown and furry inside. It was a mouse. The mouse had eaten some of the oat and made it smelly. Dad explained to Ben and Lily what he found. He said the mouse was bad and had spoiled their breakfast. He said they had to throw away the oat and the box and get a new one. Ben and Lily were curious about the mouse. They wanted to see it. But Mom and Dad said no. They said the mouse was dirty and could make them sick. They said they had to wash their hands and get ready for school. Ben and Lily were sad and disappointed. They liked oat, but not smelly oat. They hoped the mouse would not come back and ruin their food again. They wished they had something else to eat for breakfast.

One day, a little girl named Amy found an ugly lock. It was big and old. She wanted to know what it could open. She showed it to her friend, Tom. "Look at this ugly lock I found," Amy said. "Let's try to find what it opens." Tom and Amy went around their little town. They tried to open many things with the ugly lock. They tried doors and boxes, but nothing worked. They didn't want to give up. They wanted to keep looking. Finally, they found a small, old door behind a big tree. They tried the ugly lock, and it opened the door! Inside, they found a room full of toys and games. They played and had fun all day. They decided to keep the ugly lock to remember their adventure.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a helpful boy named Tim. He loved to play with his drum. Every day, he would sit on the floor and make music with his drum. He had lots of fun. One day, Tim's mom asked him to help her. She needed to clean the house. Tim was a helpful boy, so he put down his drum and helped his mom. They cleaned the house together, and it looked very nice. When they were done, Tim's mom said, "Thank you for being so helpful, Tim!" She was very happy. Tim felt good for helping his mom. Then, he went back to sit and play with his drum. He played a happy song, and they both danced and laughed.
