Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben and the Garden

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the garden. One day, they find a big potato in the dirt. They are very happy. "Look, Anna, a big potato!" Ben says. "Wow, Ben, it is big! Can we eat it?" Anna asks. "Yes, we can. But we have to wash it first. Come on, let's go to the tap." Ben says. They run to the tap and wash the potato. Then they go to the kitchen and ask Ben's mom to cook it for them. "Mom, mom, we found a big potato in the garden. Can you cook it for us?" Ben says. "Sure, Ben. That's a nice potato. Do you want to share it with Anna?" Ben's mom says. "Yes, mom. Anna is my friend. We want to eat it together." Ben says. "Okay, then. I will cut it in half and boil it for you. It will be ready soon." Ben's mom says. She puts the potato in a pot of water and turns on the stove. Anna and Ben wait patiently. They smell the potato cooking. It smells good. "Mom, is the potato ready?" Ben asks. "Yes, Ben. It is ready. Here you go. Be careful, it is hot." Ben's mom says. She gives them each a plate with half a potato on it. They thank her and sit at the table. They put some butter and salt on their potatoes. They take a bite. It is delicious. "Mmm, this is a good potato. Thank you, Ben, for sharing it with me." Anna says. "You're welcome, Anna. Thank you, too. This is a fun day." Ben says. They smile at each other and finish their potatoes. They are full and happy. They hug each other and say goodbye. They go back to the garden and play some more. The end.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a spark in a store. Tim was worried because he did not know what the spark was. He wanted to take the spark home and show his mom. Tim took the spark home and put it in his room. The spark began to grow bigger and bigger. Tim was worried, but he did not tell his mom. The spark became very big and very hot. The hot spark soon made Tim's room very hot too. Tim's mom smelled the hot and came into his room. She saw the big spark and was very scared. She tried to put out the spark, but it was too late. The spark burned their house down. Tim and his mom were very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his blocks. He would stack them high and then knock them down with a big smile on his face. One day, it was very cold and bitter outside. Tim's mom said, "Stay inside and play with your blocks, Tim." So Tim played with his blocks all day long. He made a big tower and even a little house for his toy dog. When it was time for bed, Tim did not want to stop playing. But his mom said, "You must cover your blocks and go to sleep now." Tim covered his blocks with a soft blanket and went to bed. He dreamed of more fun days playing with his blocks.

Sam was out for a walk in the park with his mom. The sun was high and hot in the sky. As they walked, Sam looked around at all the beautiful trees and the birds that flew above. Suddenly, he stopped and pointed eagerly "Look, mommy!" he said, pointing at a hot air balloon in the distance. His mom smiled and said "That's nice, Sam. Let's go see it up close." They walked up to the hot air balloon, and Sam peered inside. He saw a pair of travellers sitting in the basket, looking out at the scenery. Sam smiled and waved, but the travellers just shrugged and continued to look around. Sam smiled in confusion, then turned to his mom. "Why did they shrug?" he asked His mom put her arm around his shoulder and said "Maybe they were just thinking of a happy memory they had while they were in the hot air balloon." Sam smiled and nodded, and promised himself to never forget this special moment.
