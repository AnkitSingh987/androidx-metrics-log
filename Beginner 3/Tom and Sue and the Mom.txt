Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Sue and the Mom

Tom and Sue are hungry. They want to eat a meal. They ask Mom to make them something yummy. Mom says, "I will supply you with a meal, but you have to help me first. Can you set the table for me?" Tom and Sue are eager to help. They get the plates, the cups, the forks and the spoons. They put them on the table. They also get the napkins and the salt and the pepper. Mom says, "Thank you, Tom and Sue. You are very good helpers. Now, let's see what we have for our meal. We have some bread, some cheese, some ham, some lettuce and some tomatoes. We can make sandwiches. Do you like sandwiches?" Tom and Sue nod. They like sandwiches. They help Mom to cut the bread and the cheese and the ham. They also wash the lettuce and the tomatoes. They put everything on the bread. They make big and tasty sandwiches. Mom says, "Our meal is ready. Let's eat. Bon appetit!" Tom and Sue say, "Thank you, Mom. You are the best. We love you and we love our meal." They eat their sandwiches with big bites. They are happy and full.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a lot of clothes. She loved to wear them and play with them. One day, Sue found a big bag with a zip. She thought, "I can put all my clothes in this bag and take them everywhere I go!" So, she put all her clothes in the bag and zipped it up. Sue took her bag of clothes outside. She showed her friends her clothes and they all played dress-up. They had so much fun! After they were done playing, Sue zipped up the bag and took it home. She was so happy she could share her clothes with her friends.

Anna and Ben liked to play at the beach. They made sand castles and looked for shells. They also liked to splash in the water, but not too far from the shore. Their mom told them to be careful of the big waves. One day, they saw a big wave coming. It was bigger than any wave they had ever seen. It looked scary and angry. Anna and Ben ran to their mom, who was sitting on a towel. She saw the wave too and told them to hold her hand. The wave crashed on the beach. It was very loud and wet. It knocked over their sand castles and took some of their shells. It also pushed a small boat onto the sand. The boat had a hole in it and was leaking water. Anna and Ben saw a man in the boat. He looked hurt and sad. Anna and Ben wanted to help the man. They asked their mom if they could. She said yes, but they had to be careful. She gave them a blanket and a bottle of water. They ran to the boat and climbed inside. They wrapped the blanket around the man and gave him some water. They asked him his name and where he came from. The man said his name was Sam and he came from a faraway island. He said he was fishing when the big wave hit his boat. He said he was lucky to be alive and thanked Anna and Ben for helping him. He said they were very brave and kind. Anna and Ben smiled. They felt happy and proud. They had helped a stranger in need. They had also made a new friend. They asked Sam if he wanted to play with them. He said yes, but first he had to fix his boat. He said he had some tools and some wood in his boat. He asked Anna and Ben if they wanted to help him. They said yes, but they had to ask their mom first. They ran to their mom and told her about Sam and his boat. She said they could help him, but they had to be serious and careful. She said she would watch them from the shore. Anna and Ben ran back to the boat. They helped Sam fix his boat. They used hammers and nails and glue. They also painted the boat with bright colors. They made it look new and happy. They also gave Sam some of their shells to decorate his boat. They had fun working together. When the boat was fixed, Sam thanked Anna and Ben again. He said he had to go back to his island, but he would never forget them. He said they were his heroes. He gave them each a hug and a kiss. He also gave them a gift. It was a necklace made of shells and beads. He said it was a lucky charm. He said it would protect them from the big waves. Anna and Ben put on their necklaces. They felt lucky and loved. They waved goodbye to Sam as he sailed away. They watched him until he disappeared in the horizon. They hoped he would be safe and happy. They also hoped they would see him again someday. They went back to their mom and told her everything. She hugged them and said they were amazing. She said she was proud of them. She said they had learned a valuable lesson. She said they had learned to help others in need. She said that was the best way to make friends and have fun. She said that was the best way to live.
