Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and the Cup

Anna was sick. She had a big cough and a hot head. She did not want to play or eat. She only wanted to lie in her bed and hug her teddy bear. Mama was worried. She called the nurse. The nurse came to Anna's house with a big sack. The sack had many things inside. The nurse took out a thing that looked like a spoon and put it in Anna's mouth. She said, "This is a thermometer. It tells me how hot you are." Anna opened her mouth and let the nurse see the thermometer. The nurse looked at it and said, "You have a fever. You need some medicine." She took out a bottle and a cup from her sack. She poured some medicine into the cup and gave it to Anna. She said, "This will make you feel better. Drink it all up." Anna did not like the medicine. It was bitter and yucky. She made a face and said, "Blech! I don't want it!" She pushed the cup away. The nurse smiled and said, "I know it's not tasty, but it will help you. How about this? If you drink the medicine, I will show you something amazing from my sack. Something that will make you happy." Anna was curious. She wanted to see what the nurse had. She said, "Okay. I will drink it. But only a little bit." The nurse said, "No, you have to drink it all. It's not much. Come on, you can do it." She held the cup to Anna's lips and helped her sip the medicine. Anna made a face again, but she drank it all. The nurse said, "Good girl! You are very brave. Now, are you ready to see what I have for you?" She reached into her sack and pulled out something soft and fluffy. It was a bunny. A white bunny with pink ears and a pink nose. It had a bow around its neck. The nurse said, "This is for you. A special gift. You can keep it and cuddle it. It will be your friend." Anna's eyes lit up. She loved the bunny. She reached out and took it from the nurse. She hugged it and said, "Thank you! Thank you! It's so cute! I love it!" The nurse said, "You're welcome. I'm glad you like it. I hope you get well soon. I have to go now, but I will come back tomorrow to check on you. Be good and rest well." Anna said, "Bye bye, nurse. You are very nice. And you have an amazing sack." She smiled and waved as the nurse left. She snuggled with her bunny and her teddy bear and felt a little better. She closed her eyes and fell asleep.

Once upon a time, there was a smart bunny who lived in a small yard. The bunny loved to take baths. Every day, he would take a long bath to keep himself clean and fresh. One day, the bunny saw a big mess of mud outside his yard. He wanted to play in the mud, but he knew if he did, he would get messy and dirty. So instead, he kept playing outside without getting into the mud. The smart and clean bunny taught us an important lesson: even if what we want to do is fun, we should sometimes think about the consequences and see if it is really worth it. So, if something looks like it might be a lot of fun, but it could make us too messy, don't do it!

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play in their room. They have many toys and books. But today they want to do something new. They want to make puppets with socks. They find some old socks in their drawer. They also find some buttons, yarn and glue. They are careful not to make a mess. They put the socks on their hands. They glue the buttons for eyes and the yarn for hair. They make funny faces and voices with their puppets. "Hello, I am Bob the sock puppet. What is your name?" Ben says with a deep voice. "Hi, I am Mia the sock puppet. I like to sing and dance. Do you want to be my friend?" Lily says with a high voice. "Yes, I want to be your friend. You are very nice. Do you want to play with me?" Ben says. "Sure, I want to play with you. What do you want to play?" Lily says. They decide to play hide and seek with their puppets. They take turns hiding and finding each other. They have a lot of fun. They forget about their other toys and books. They only want to play with their sock puppets. They are happy.
