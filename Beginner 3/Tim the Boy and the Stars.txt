Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Stars

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to look at the stars and dream about going to space. One day, he got a big idea. He wanted to touch a planet! So, Tim built a spaceship with the help of his dad. They used boxes and paint to make it look real. Tim put on his astronaut suit and got ready to fly. He waved goodbye to his dad and said, "I'll be back soon!" Up in space, Tim saw a big, angry-looking planet. It had a frown on its face and was red all over. Tim wanted to make the planet happy. He landed his spaceship and walked over to the planet. "Why are you angry?" Tim asked. The planet said, "I'm lonely. No one ever comes to visit me." Tim smiled and said, "Well, now you have a friend! I'll come visit you again soon." The planet's frown turned into a big smile. Tim touched the planet's surface and felt happy that he made a new friend. He got back in his spaceship and flew back home to tell his dad all about his adventure.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim had a plan. He wanted to jump in a big puddle outside his house. But it was difficult because his mom said he can't get his shoes wet. One day, Tim saw a big rain cloud in the sky. He knew it would rain soon. He was very excited because he knew the rain would make a big puddle for him to jump in. But he remembered his mom said he can't get his shoes wet. The rain came and made a big puddle just like Tim thought. Tim had a plan to jump in the puddle and not get his shoes wet. He put on his rain boots and went outside. He jumped in the big puddle and had so much fun. His shoes did not get wet, and his mom was happy. Tim's plan worked, and he could jump in puddles whenever it rained.

Once upon a time, in a big, green field, there lived a silly cat and a happy dog. They liked to play and run all day long. But they never played together. One sunny day, the cat and the dog saw a big, red ball in the middle of the field. They both wanted the ball, so they ran very fast to get it. When they reached the ball, they touched it at the same time. "This ball is mine!" said the cat. "No, it's mine!" said the dog. Just then, a little bird flew down and said, "Why don't you unite and play together?" The cat and the dog thought about it, and they decided to be friends. They played with the big, red ball together and had lots of fun. From that day on, the silly cat and the happy dog always played together, and they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Law. Law was enormous and loved to play outside. One day, the sun was hiding, and it was very cold. Law went outside to play and started to shiver because of the cold. He wanted to find a warm place to stop shivering. Law saw a small house and thought it would be warm inside. Law went inside the house and felt warm. He stopped shivering and was happy. Law played inside the house all day and made new friends. They all had fun together and stayed warm.

Sara and Ben liked to play outside. They liked to run and jump and make mud pies. One day, they saw a big bird nest in a tree. They wanted to see the eggs and the baby birds. They found a long twig on the ground. They tried to poke the nest with the twig. But the twig was too short. They looked for a longer twig. They found one near the fence. They ran back to the tree. They lifted the long twig and pushed it to the nest. They were very patient. They wanted to see the eggs and the baby birds. But the nest was too high. They could not reach it. Suddenly, they heard a loud squawk. It was the mother bird. She was angry. She saw Sara and Ben with the twig. She thought they wanted to hurt her eggs and her baby birds. She flew down and pecked at Sara and Ben. She pecked their heads and their arms and their legs. Sara and Ben screamed and dropped the twig. They ran away from the tree. But it was too late. The twig had hit the nest. The nest had fallen from the tree. The eggs and the baby birds had broken on the ground. They were dead. Sara and Ben felt very sad and very sorry. They had done a bad thing. They wished they had not played with the twig. They wished they had left the nest alone.
