Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Bunny and the Carrot

One day, a bunny named Bob saw a big carrot. He was very happy. He thought, "I want to eat that carrot." But, another bunny named Tim saw the carrot too. Tim was envious. He said, "I want the carrot!" Bob and Tim ran to the carrot. They both wanted to get it first. They did not want to share. They both touched the carrot at the same time. They looked at each other and said, "This is not good. We need to find a way to share." Bob had an idea. He said, "Let's reverse and go back. Then we can take turns to eat the carrot." Tim agreed. They both went back and took turns eating the carrot. They were both happy and full. They learned that sharing is better than fighting.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his stuff. He had a ball, a bone, and a soft toy. One day, Spot's toy got stuck in a tree. Spot felt helpless. He did not know how to get his toy back. Spot's friend, Kitty, saw him looking sad. She asked, "Why are you sad, Spot?" Spot told her about his toy in the tree. Kitty wanted to help, so she climbed the tree. She tried to reach the toy, but it was too far. Kitty had an idea. She told Spot to rub his back on the tree. Spot did as Kitty said, and the tree shook. The toy fell from the tree, and Spot was so happy. He thanked Kitty for her help. Now, Spot and Kitty play with the stuff together, and they are the best of friends.

One day, a girl named Sue was drawing a picture. It was a picture of her cat, Tom. The picture was dull because she only had a gray pencil. Sue wanted to make her picture more fun. Sue asked her mom, "Can you supply more colors?" Her mom said, "Yes, let's go to the store." At the store, they found a big box of colors. Sue was very happy. At home, Sue used the new colors on her picture. Suddenly, the cat in the picture started to move! It jumped out of the paper and became a real cat. Now, Sue had two cats, Tom and her new cat, Art. They all played together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time there was a charming princess named Daisy. She loved to fly in her spare time. One day while Daisy was flying, she saw a magical herb. The herb had enchanting colors like a rainbow and it smelled wonderful. Daisy wanted to buy the herb and she tried to find the owner. When she arrived in town, she found an old man with a cart of herbs. "Hello there," Daisy said. "Would you sell me that herb?" The old man replied, "No, it is not for sale. But I could give it to you as a gift. It is the most magical thing I have seen. I hope you enjoy it." Daisy was so happy and thanked the old man for his kind gift. She then flew away with the herb in her hands feeling very grateful. From then on, Daisy never forgot the old man who gave her the charming herb.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with blocks and cars. One day, they go to the park with their moms. There are many other kids at the park. They have swings, slides and seesaws. Lily and Ben want to go on the slide. But there is a long line of kids waiting for their turn. Lily and Ben have to wait too. They are not happy. They want to slide now. "Look at them," Lily says to Ben. "They are having fun. Why can't we go?" Ben nods. He is envious of the kids on the slide. He wishes he could be on the slide too. "Be patient," Lily's mom says. "You will get your turn soon. The line is moving fast." Lily and Ben try to be patient. They count the kids in front of them. They watch the birds and the clouds. They talk to each other. Finally, it is their turn. They climb up the ladder and sit on the slide. They hold hands and smile. They slide down fast and laugh. They feel the wind and the sun. They are happy. They want to go again. But they see another long line of kids waiting. They decide to try something else. They see a seesaw. There is no line for the seesaw. They run to the seesaw and hop on. They go up and down. They have more fun. They are not envious anymore. They are glad. They have a good day at the park.
