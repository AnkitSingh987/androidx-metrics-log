Category: Beginner
Title: The Dark Cave and the Bear

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a dark cave. A little bear lived in the cave. One day, the bear went outside to play. He played with his ball and had a lot of fun. After playing, the bear went back to his cave. But when he arrived, he saw that his ball was not there. The bear was upset. He looked around and tried to find his ball. The bear looked and looked, and then he found his ball near a tree. He was so happy! The bear took his ball and went back to his cave. From that day on, the bear always kept his ball safe in his cave.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who thought it would be nice to have a party. She noticed that all her friends had a special place to have a party and she wanted one too. So she asked her mommy if they could have a party and mommy said yes. The little girl was so excited she started to plan right away. She wanted to make it a nice party. She got lots of pretty decorations, treats, games and balloons. When it was time for the party to start, the girl noticed that all her friends were at the front door. Everyone was wearing nice party clothes and they had big smiles on their faces. Everyone had a great time at the party. They played games, ate treats and laughed a lot. When it was time to go home, the little girl was sad. But she noticed one thing, the smiles on everyone's faces. It made her happy to know that she had thrown a nice party for her friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big hippo who loved to cook. One day, he decided to make a spicy soup for all his friends. He was very happy and excited. The hippo put a big pot on the fire and added some water. He found some spicy things, like hot peppers and ginger. He put them in the pot too. The soup started to smell very good, and the hippo danced around the pot. Soon, all his friends came to try the spicy soup. They took big sips and smiled. The hippo was so glad that everyone liked his soup. They all had a fun day eating and laughing together. The end.

Once upon a time, there was an old man. He had an arm that was not happy. The old man's arm would suffer sometimes. The old man wanted to make his arm feel better. One day, the old man went to a park. He saw a little girl playing with a ball. The old man asked the girl if he could play with her. The little girl said yes, and they played together. As they played, the old man's arm started to feel better. The old man was very happy. The little girl was happy too. They played until the sun went down. The old man's arm did not suffer anymore, and they became good friends.

Ben and Mia were playing with paper. They liked to fold it and make shapes. Ben made a boat and Mia made a hat. They were happy. But then, Max came. Max was a big boy who liked to tease. He saw Ben and Mia's paper shapes and laughed. He said they were silly and ugly. He grabbed Ben's boat and Mia's hat and threw them in the pit. The pit was a hole in the ground where people put trash. It was smelly and dirty. Ben and Mia were sad and angry. They ran to the pit and tried to get their paper shapes back. But the pit was too deep and they could not reach them. "Ha ha, look at them!" Max said. "They are trying to get their trash back. They are so dumb!" Ben and Mia did not like Max. They wanted to make him stop. They looked around and saw a big pile of mud. They had an idea. They picked up some mud and threw it at Max. The mud hit Max in the face and made him dirty and smelly. "Yuck, what are you doing?" Max said. He wiped the mud off his face and ran away. He was crying and scared. Ben and Mia smiled. They felt better. They did not care about their paper shapes anymore. They had fun with the mud. They made mud balls and mud pies and mud castles. They were happy.
