Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Pasta

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to play with his pasta. He thought it would be fun to attach pasta to his toy cars. So, Tim took some glue and attached the pasta to the cars. He was very happy with his new pasta cars. Tim's sister, Sue, saw what he was doing and said, "Tim, what are you doing with your pasta?" Tim was embarrassed because he thought Sue would laugh at him. But instead, Sue said, "That's a fun idea! Can I play with you?" So, Tim and Sue played with the pasta cars together. They had lots of fun attaching more pasta to the cars and racing them around the room. They were both very happy and didn't feel embarrassed anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He loved to rush and run all day. One day, Tim saw a big tree with a big box on top. The box had the word "reward" on it. Tim wanted the reward all for himself. He was a selfish boy. Tim tried to climb the tree to get the reward. But the tree was very tall. He felt sad. Then, a little bird came and said, "I can help you get the reward, but you have to share it with me." Tim thought about it and said, "Okay, I will share the reward with you." The little bird flew up and brought the box down. Tim and the bird opened the box together. They were excited to see what was inside. But, surprise! The box was empty. Tim and the bird looked at each other and laughed. They learned that being selfish is not good. The real reward was their new friendship.

Once upon a time, there was a big box of crayons. The crayons were all different colors. They liked to play together and draw pretty pictures. One day, a new crayon came to the box. This crayon was a sour green color. The other crayons didn't know him, so they were a little scared. The sour green crayon wanted to play with the other crayons, but they were too scared to play with him. They thought he might be mean because he was a sour color. But the sour green crayon was not mean. He was very nice and just wanted to be friends. One day, the crayons decided to give the sour green crayon a chance. They let him play with them and draw pictures. They found out that the sour green crayon was not scary at all. He was a lot of fun to play with. The crayons all became good friends and drew pretty pictures together. They were not scared of the sour green crayon anymore.

One day, a boy named Tom went to the beach with his mom. The sky was gloomy and the sand was wet. Tom wanted to play in the sand, but his mom did not allow him because it was too wet. Tom was sad, but then he saw a big crab. The crab was playing in the wet sand. Tom thought, "If the crab can play in the sand, so can I!" So, Tom started to play with the crab in the wet sand. Suddenly, the sun came out and the sky was not gloomy anymore. The sand became dry and warm. Tom's mom saw him playing with the crab and smiled. She said, "Now you can play in the sand, Tom!" Tom was happy, and he played in the sand with the crab all day.

Anna loved waffles. She ate them every morning with honey and butter. She liked to make them with her mom in the kitchen. They had a special machine that made the waffles round and crispy. One day, Anna's mom said they had a new neighbor. His name was Ben and he was three years old, like Anna. He had just moved in with his dad next door. Anna's mom said they should go and say hello and introduce themselves. Anna was shy. She did not know Ben. She wondered if he liked waffles too. She decided to bring him one of her waffles as a gift. She wrapped it in a napkin and put it in a basket. She and her mom walked to Ben's house and knocked on the door. Ben's dad opened it and smiled. He said hello and invited them in. He said Ben was in his room, playing with his toys. Anna followed her mom to Ben's room. She saw a boy with brown hair and blue eyes. He had a lot of cars and trucks on the floor. He looked up and saw Anna. He said, "Who are you?" Anna felt nervous. She held out her basket and said, "I'm Anna. I live next door. I brought you a waffle. Do you like waffles?" Ben's eyes lit up. He said, "I love waffles! Thank you, Anna. That's very nice of you. Do you want to play with me?" Anna smiled. She said, "Yes, I do. Can I see your cars and trucks?" Ben nodded. He said, "Sure. Come on, I'll show you. They are very cool." Anna and Ben sat on the floor and played with the cars and trucks. They shared the waffle and talked about their favorite things. They became friends. Anna's mom and Ben's dad watched them and smiled. They were happy that their children got along so well. They decided to make more waffles and have a picnic in the backyard. The moral of the story is: Sharing and being kind can help you make new friends.
