Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Boat and the Fish

Once upon a time, a little white boat had a big sail. The boat liked to float on the water. The boat loved to feel the wind blow its sail and make it go fast. One day, the boat saw a big fish in the water. The fish liked to peek at the boat. The boat and the fish became friends. They played together in the water every day. The boat and the fish had lots of fun. They raced each other and laughed. The little white boat with the big sail and the big fish were happy friends forever.

---

One day, a small bird named Tim saw a mysterious thing in the sky. He did not know what it was. He flew to his friend, the big bear named Ben, to ask him. Tim pointed to the sky and said, "Ben, what is that?" Ben looked at the sky and said, "I do not know, Tim. Let's find out together." They walked and walked until they found a nest on the ground. Inside the nest, there were small, shiny rocks. The rocks were from the mysterious thing in the sky. Tim and Ben decided to take the rocks back to their homes. But then, the rocks started to shake and jump. They were not rocks, but baby birds! The baby birds had come from the mysterious thing in the sky. Tim and Ben learned that it is important to help others and not to take things that are not theirs.

---

Once upon a time, there was a pink train. The train went fast on the tracks. It made a lot of steam. The steam went up into the sky. The train loved to go fast. One day, the train saw a big red stop sign. The train had to stop. It stopped very fast. The steam stopped too. The train was sad. It did not like to stop. But then, something unexpected happened. The pink train saw a parade! There were people dancing and playing music. The train was happy again. It joined the parade and made steam in time with the music. The train did not mind stopping now.

Lily and Tom are playing in the park. They like to run and jump and slide. They see a big hill with flowers and grass. They want to go up the hill and see what is on the other side. They hold hands and start to climb the hill. They see many bugs and birds and rocks. They stop to look at them and say their names. They are having fun. When they reach the top of the hill, they see a pond with ducks and fish. They are very happy. They kneel down and watch the water. They see their faces in the water. They laugh and make funny faces. Tom remembers that he has something in his pocket. He takes it out and shows it to Lily. It is a shiny coin. He says he found it on the way to the park. He says he wants to give it to Lily. He says she is his best friend. Lily is very glad. She hugs Tom and says thank you. She says he is her best friend too. She puts the coin in her pocket. She says they can buy ice cream with it later. They smile and stand up. They hold hands and run down the hill. They are very happy.

One day, a little deer was walking in the forest. The deer saw a distant hill and felt very excited. The deer wanted to go to the hill and play. The deer met a rabbit on the way. The rabbit said, "Hello, deer! Where are you going?" The deer replied, "I am going to the distant hill to play. Do you want to come with me?" The rabbit was excited too and said, "Yes, let's go together!" So, the deer and the rabbit went to the distant hill. They played and had a lot of fun. They were very happy to be friends and play together.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a pretty flower and wanted to enjoy it. But a mean boy named Tom came and spoiled the flower. Lily was sad and cried. Lily's friend, Sam, saw her crying and asked, "Why are you sad, Lily?" Lily told him about the spoiled flower. Sam said, "Don't worry, I have a plan." The next day, Lily, Sam, and Tom went to the park. Sam showed them a big flower that was even more pretty. Tom wanted to spoil it too, but Sam said, "Wait! If you don't spoil this flower, I will show you something fun." Tom agreed. Sam took them to a secret place with many pretty flowers. They all enjoyed the flowers and had fun. Tom learned not to spoil flowers anymore, and they all became good friends.
