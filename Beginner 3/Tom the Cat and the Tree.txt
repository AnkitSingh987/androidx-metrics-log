Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Cat and the Tree

One day, a cat named Tom was very hot. He wanted to find a cool place to rest. Today was a very hot day, and Tom was tired. Tom saw a big tree with a lot of shade. He thought it would be a nice place to rest. But, there was a big dog named Max near the tree. Max did not like cats and wanted to chase Tom away. Tom wanted to be smart, so he tried to slip past Max without being seen. But, Tom stepped on a stick and it made a loud noise. Max heard the noise and saw Tom. Max started to chase Tom. Tom ran very fast, and Max could not catch him. Tom found another tree to rest under. It was not as big, but it was still cool. Tom was happy he got away from Max, and he could rest now.

One sunny day, a little boy named Tim found a big chair in his room. Tim liked the chair because it was reliable, and he could sit on it without falling. He decided to play a game with his friend, Sam. "Sam, let's play hide and seek!" Tim said. Sam agreed, and they started the game. Tim hid behind the big chair, and Sam started to count. While Sam was counting, Tim tried to peek to see if Sam was still counting or if he was coming to find him. But when Tim peeked, he saw a big cat sitting on the chair! The cat was looking right at him. "Oh no!" Tim said. "The cat found me!" Sam heard Tim and came running. The cat jumped off the chair and ran away. Sam laughed, and they decided to play another game. They were happy because they had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to watch cartoons on TV. One day, he saw a weird cartoon. It was about a magic door that let people enter other worlds. Tim wanted to enter the cartoon world too. So, Tim drew a door on his wall with his crayons. He hoped the door would be magic like the one in the weird cartoon. Tim tried to enter the door, but he could not. He felt sad and went to sleep. That night, while Tim was sleeping, the door on the wall became real. It opened and a big monster from the cartoon entered Tim's room. The monster was not nice. It took all of Tim's toys and went back through the door. Tim woke up and saw that his toys were gone. He cried and wished he never tried to enter the weird cartoon world.

Once upon a time, in a peaceful town, there was a big wall. The wall was very dirty. People did not like the dirty wall. They wanted to make it clean and pretty. One day, a kind girl named Lily had an idea. She told her friends, "Let's wash the wall!" They all agreed and got their buckets and soap. They were very excited to clean the wall. The next day, Lily and her friends started to wash the wall. They scrubbed and scrubbed until the wall was clean. When they were done, the wall looked so beautiful. The whole town was happy, and they all thanked Lily and her friends for their hard work.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a little girl named Lily. Lily loved the summer. In the summer, the sun was warm and the days were long. Lily liked to play outside with her friends. One day, Lily decided to collect pretty things she found outside. She looked for rocks, leaves, and flowers. She found a fragile butterfly with colorful wings. Lily was very careful with the butterfly because it was so delicate. Lily showed her friends the fragile butterfly and the other things she collected. They all thought it was beautiful. They decided to collect more pretty things together. They had a fun time in the summer sun, making a big collection of pretty things. And they all lived happily ever after.

Tom is a cop. He likes to help people and catch bad guys. He wears a blue suit and a hat with a star. He has a car with lights and a siren. He thinks his job is fun. One day, Tom goes to a school. He wants to teach the kids about being a cop. He brings his car and his hat. He shows them how to turn on the lights and the siren. He lets them try on his hat. He tells them how to be safe and follow the rules. Some kids think Tom is cool. They ask him many questions. They want to be cops when they grow up. They say thank you to Tom. They give him hugs and high fives. But one kid thinks Tom is boring. His name is Ben. He does not listen to Tom. He does not ask any questions. He does not try on the hat. He does not say thank you. He yawns and rolls his eyes. Tom sees Ben. He feels sad. He wants Ben to like him. He wants Ben to learn. He goes to Ben and says, "Hi, Ben. Do you like being a cop?" Ben shakes his head. He says, "No, I don't. Being a cop is boring. I want to be a pirate. Pirates are fun. They have swords and treasure and ships. They do whatever they want. They are not boring like cops." Tom smiles. He says, "I see. Pirates are fun, but they are also bad. They steal and fight and break the rules. They make people sad and scared. They are not nice like cops. Cops are good. They help and protect and follow the rules. They make people happy and safe. They are not boring like pirates." Ben thinks for a moment. He says, "Really? I didn't know that. Maybe being a cop is not so boring. Maybe being a pirate is not so fun. Maybe I can learn more about being a cop. Can I try on your hat?" Tom nods. He says, "Sure, Ben. You can try on my hat. You can also learn more about being a cop. You can ask me anything you want. I will be happy to answer. Maybe you will like being a cop after all." Ben smiles. He says, "Thank you, Tom. You are a nice cop. You are not boring. You are fun. Maybe I will be a cop when I grow up. Or maybe I will be something else. But I will always be good and follow the rules. And I will always learn new things." Tom hugs Ben. He says, "You are welcome, Ben. You are a smart kid. You are not boring. You are fun. You can be anything you want when you grow up. But I hope you will always be good and follow the rules. And I hope you will always learn new things."
