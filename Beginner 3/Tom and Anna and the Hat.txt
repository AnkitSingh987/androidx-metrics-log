Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Anna and the Hat

Tom and Anna were playing in the snow. They made a big snowman with a hat, a scarf and a carrot nose. They wanted to give him eyes and a mouth, but they had no stones. They looked around and saw a big pile of coal near the house. "Let's use coal for his eyes and mouth!" Tom said. "OK!" Anna agreed. They ran to the pile of coal and picked up two pieces each. They ran back to the snowman and pressed the coal into the snow. The snowman looked funny with his black eyes and mouth. But then they heard a loud scream from the house. It was their mom. "Tom! Anna! What have you done? That coal was for the fire! Now we have no coal to keep us warm!" she shouted. Tom and Anna felt sad. They did not know that the coal was for the fire. They only wanted to make the snowman happy. "Sorry, mom!" they said. They ran to the snowman and took out the coal. They gave it back to their mom. "Please don't be mad, mom. We love you!" they said. Their mom sighed. She was not mad, but she was worried. She hugged them and said: "I love you too, but you have to be careful. Coal is not for playing. It is for burning. We need it to stay warm in the winter. Do you understand?" Tom and Anna nodded. They understood. They said sorry again and helped their mom carry the coal to the fire. They felt warm and cozy. They looked at the snowman and smiled. He still had his hat, his scarf and his carrot nose. He looked happy too.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They saw a big shadow on the ground. Tim asked, "Mom, what is that?" His mom said, "That is your shadow, Tim. It follows you when the sun is out." Tim was a bit embarrass. He didn't like his shadow following him. He tried to run away from it, but the shadow was always there. Tim's mom laughed and said, "Don't worry, Tim. Your shadow won't hurt you." As they walked, they saw a disgusting bug on the ground. Tim made a face and said, "Ew, that's yucky!" His mom told him, "It's okay, Tim. Just leave the bug alone and it won't bother you." Tim learned that day that some things, like shadows and bugs, might seem scary or yucky, but they won't hurt him if he leaves them alone. He was happy to have his mom there to help him understand the world.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a boy named Tim. One day, Tim went to the dark forest to play. He was not scared because he was a brave boy. In the forest, he met a big bear. The bear had a question for Tim. "Will you return my lost ball?" asked the bear. Tim said, "Yes, I will help you find your ball." So, Tim and the bear looked for the ball together. They found the ball under a big tree. The bear was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tim!" Tim was happy too because he helped a friend. They both returned to their homes. The moral of the story is to always help others when they need it.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went outside to play. There was a lot of snow on the ground. It was very cold, but Lucy did not care. She put on her warm coat, hat, and mittens. She wanted to make a big snowman with her friend, Tom. Lucy and Tom started to roll big snowballs. They made one big snowball for the body and one smaller snowball for the head. They put the snowballs together to make the snowman. They used sticks for the arms, a carrot for the nose, and rocks for the eyes and mouth. As they were making the snowman, Lucy found an unusual hat. It was old and had a tear on the side. Lucy said, "Let's put this hat on our snowman." Tom agreed, "Yes, it will make our snowman look funny!" They put the hat on the snowman's head and laughed. Soon, it was time for Lucy and Tom to go inside. They said goodbye to their snowman and went home. They were happy they had a fun day playing in the snow with their unusual snowman friend.
