Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Clumsy Boy and the Cloud

One day, a clumsy boy named Tim went outside to play. He saw a big cloud in the sky. The cloud looked like a funny face. Tim laughed and said, "Look at that silly cloud!" Tim's friend, Sue, came to play with him. They ran around and played tag. Tim was very clumsy and fell down a lot. Sue said, "Be careful, Tim!" Tim felt embarrass but he kept playing. As they played, the cloud moved and changed shapes. Tim and Sue saw that it now looked like a big, fluffy cat. They both laughed and said, "Wow, that cloud is so funny!" They played under the funny cloud all day, and Tim didn't feel embarrass anymore.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a gym. The gym was not like other gyms. It was unusual. It was made of ice! Kids loved to play there. They could slide, skate, and have fun. One sunny day, the sun was very hot. The gym started to melt. The kids were sad. They did not want their ice gym to go away. They had an idea. They would use big umbrellas to stop the sun. The kids opened their big umbrellas and put them over the gym. The sun could not melt the gym now. The kids were happy. They could play in their unusual ice gym every day. And they all lived happily ever after.

Tom and Lily were going to the park with Mom. They put on their coats and hats, because it was cold outside. Mom said they could play on the swings and the slide, but they had to stay close to her. On the way to the park, they saw a big dog. The dog was brown and furry, and had a long tail. It was barking at a cat on a fence. Tom liked dogs, but Lily was scared of them. "Mom, can we pet the dog?" Tom asked. "No, Tom, we don't know the dog. It might not be friendly. We should leave it alone," Mom said. "But why is it barking?" Tom questioned. "It is barking because it sees the cat. Maybe it wants to chase the cat, or maybe it is telling the cat to go away. Dogs and cats don't always get along," Mom explained. Lily held Mom's hand and was quiet. She did not like the noise. She hoped the dog would stop barking soon. They walked past the dog and the cat, and reached the park. There, they saw other children playing. Tom and Lily ran to the swings and the slide, and had fun. They forgot about the dog and the cat. Mom watched them and smiled. She was happy they were having a good time.

One day, a boy named Tom went to the woods. He wanted to make a berry pie. He saw red berries, blue berries, and even purple berries. He picked a lot of them and put them in a big bag. Tom met a girl named Sue in the woods. Sue said, "I can help you make the pie." Tom was happy. They went to Sue's house to make the pie. They mixed the berries with sugar and put it on the dry dough. While the pie was in the oven, they heard a loud noise. A big bird flew into the room! The bird said, "I smell the berry pie. Can I have some?" Tom and Sue were surprised but said yes. They all sat down and ate the pie together. It was the best pie they ever had.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily liked to play outside. One day, she found a big buckle on the ground. It was shiny and pretty. Lily wanted to show it to her mom. Lily ran to her mom and said, "Look, mom! I found a buckle!" Her mom smiled and said, "That's a rich buckle, Lily! Let's settle down and think of something fun to do with it." They sat on the floor and thought. Lily had an idea. She said, "Let's make a necklace with the buckle!" So, Lily and her mom found some string and put the buckle on it. Now, Lily had a pretty, rich necklace to wear. They were both happy and settled down to play with the new necklace.
