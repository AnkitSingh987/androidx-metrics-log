Category: Beginner
Title: Billy and the Door

Billy was a happy three year old. He liked to play with his friends and they loved him. One day they were all playing a game. Billy was doing his best to win but he was feeling helpless. His friends laughed at him and he grew sad. Billy wanted to leave but his friends convinced him to stay. Feeling helpless, he stayed and kept playing. His friends started teasing him again and he felt so angry. He shouted at them and ran away. His friends followed him and he grew more scared. He ran away as fast as he could until he reached his home. He locked the door, but his friends had come too quickly. They kept knocking and shouting at the door. Finally, his parents opened the door. His friends stood there, their faces angry. Billy's parents were cross with him and sent him to his room. Billy was left feeling helpless and scared in his room, watching the game outside from his window.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Ann. Ann had a pretty scarf. She loved to wear it all the time. Ann was a healthy girl. She liked to play outside with her friends. One day, Ann had to choose. She could play with her friends or go to the store with Mom. Ann chose to play with her friends. She put on her scarf and went outside. They played and had fun. But then, it started to rain. Ann's pretty scarf got all wet. She was sad. The rain made her scarf dirty and not pretty anymore. Ann wished she had gone to the store with Mom instead.

One day, a big, bossy cat and a little, fast dog had a race. They wanted to see who was the fastest. The cat said, "I am big and strong, I will win!" The dog said, "I am small and fast, I will win!" They started the race. The cat and the dog ran very fast. They ran past trees, rocks, and a big lake. Everyone watched them race. They cheered for both the cat and the dog. Then, something unexpected happened. A tiny, slow turtle joined the race! The cat and the dog laughed. They said, "You are too slow, you cannot win!" But the turtle did not care. He raced with all his might. The cat and the dog were so busy laughing, they did not see the finish line. The turtle won the race! Everyone clapped and cheered for the turtle. The cat and the dog learned that it is not nice to be bossy and laugh at others.

One day, a little boy named Tim had a fish. The fish was red and small. Tim liked his fish very much. He took care of the fish every day. The fish was ready to play with Tim. Tim wanted to show his fish to his friend, Sam. Sam lived far away. Tim had an idea. He would mail the fish to Sam. He put the fish in a box with water. He gave the box to the mailman. The mailman took the box to Sam. Sam opened the box and saw the fish. He was very happy. Sam called Tim and said, "Thank you for the fish!" Tim was happy that his fish made Sam smile. They both loved the little red fish.

Once upon a time, in a big town, there was a human named Tom. Tom was a boring man. He did the same thing every day. He would wake up, eat, work, and sleep. He never tried new things or had fun. One day, Tom saw a big, red fruit on a tree. He had never seen this fruit before. He thought, "Maybe I will taste it." So, Tom picked the fruit and took a bite. It was sweet and yummy. He liked it a lot. After that, Tom started to try new things. He tasted new foods and played new games. He was not boring anymore. Tom was happy and had many friends. The end.

One day, a big bird saw the moon. The moon was very big and bright. The bird wanted to fly to the moon. So, the bird flew up, up, up into the sky. As the bird flew, it saw a spicy pepper. The bird thought the pepper would fill its tummy. So, the bird ate the pepper. "Oh no!" the bird said. "This is too spicy for me!" The bird flew back down and drank some water. The bird felt better. Then, a twist happened. The moon came down to the bird! The bird and the moon became good friends. They played and had fun together.
