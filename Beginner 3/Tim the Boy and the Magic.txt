Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Magic

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was an ordinary boy. He had a toy car that he liked to play with. The car needed a battery to go. One day, the battery was lost, and the car could not go. Tim was very sad. Tim's mom saw him crying and asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim said, "My car can't go. The battery is lost." Tim's mom said, "Let's find the battery and make the car go again." They looked everywhere for the battery. They looked under the bed, in the toy box, and even in the kitchen. They could not find the battery. Tim was about to cry again when something unexpected happened. The car started to go by itself! Tim and his mom were very surprised. They didn't know how the car could go without the battery. Then, they saw a little fairy sitting on the car. The fairy said, "I saw you were sad, so I used my magic to heal your car. Now it can go without the battery!" Tim and his mom thanked the fairy. Tim was very happy to play with his car again. And from that day on, the ordinary boy and his magic car had many fun adventures together.

Once, there was a boy named Tim. He had a wide kite that he loved to play with. One day, he took his kite to the park. The sky was blue, and the sun was bright. Tim was very happy. While Tim was playing with his kite, a big wind came. The wind took the kite up, up, up into the sky. Tim was sad because he could not get his kite back. He looked up and saw a tall tree near him. The kite was stuck in the tree! Just then, a friendly squirrel saw Tim's sad face. The squirrel climbed the tall tree to save the kite. Tim was so happy when the squirrel brought his kite back. He thanked the squirrel and gave it a big hug. The squirrel had saved the day, and Tim and the squirrel became best friends.

Tom and Lily were playing with their toys in the living room. They had a lot of fun making noises and pretending to be animals. Tom had a big truck and Lily had a doll. They shared their toys and laughed a lot. But then Tom saw something shiny on the floor. It was a needle that Mom had used to sew a button on Dad's shirt. Tom did not know what it was, but he wanted to touch it. He reached for the needle and said, "Look, Lily, what is this?" Lily saw the needle and felt scared. She knew it was sharp and could hurt Tom. She remembered that Mom had told them to never touch needles. She shouted, "No, Tom, don't touch it! It is a needle! It can make you bleed!" Tom did not listen to Lily. He grabbed the needle and felt a sting in his finger. He dropped the needle and cried, "Ow, ow, ow! It hurts! It hurts!" He looked at his finger and saw a drop of blood. He felt wet and scared. Lily ran to Tom and hugged him. She said, "I'm sorry, Tom. I tried to stop you. Let me help you." She took a tissue from the table and wrapped it around Tom's finger. She said, "It will be okay, Tom. Mom will make it better. Come, let's go to Mom." They left their toys and ran to Mom, who was in the kitchen. Mom saw Tom's finger and said, "Oh, no, Tom, what happened?" She took the tissue off and saw the needle mark. She said, "Did you touch a needle, Tom? That was very dangerous. You could have gotten sick or hurt yourself more. You should always listen to Lily. She is a good sister. She cares for you." Tom felt sorry and ashamed. He said, "I'm sorry, Mom. I didn't know. It was shiny and I wanted to see it. I won't touch needles again. I love you, Mom. I love you, Lily." Mom hugged Tom and Lily and said, "I love you, too, Tom. I love you, too, Lily. You are both very brave and sweet. But you have to be careful and follow the rules. Needles are not toys. They are for grown-ups only. Now let me clean your finger and put a band-aid on it. Then we can have some cookies and milk and read a story. How does that sound?" Tom and Lily smiled and said, "Yes, Mom. That sounds good. Thank you, Mom." They followed Mom to the bathroom and let her take care of Tom's finger. Then they had some cookies and milk and read a story. They felt happy and safe. They learned their lesson and never touched needles again. The end.
