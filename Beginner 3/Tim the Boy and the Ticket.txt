Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Ticket

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very flexible. He could bend and twist like a noodle. One day, Tim went to the fair with his mom and dad. They were all very excited. At the fair, Tim's dad bought a ticket for a fun ride. Tim held the ticket tightly in his hand. But as they walked to the ride, Tim saw a funny clown. He laughed and laughed at the clown. Then, he drop the ticket by accident. The ticket flew away with the wind. Tim was sad, but he didn't give up. He used his flexible body to look for the ticket. He bent down low and looked under a table. He twisted around a pole. Finally, he found the ticket stuck in a bush. Tim was so happy, and he went on the fun ride with his mom and dad. They all had a great day at the fair.

Lily and Ben are playing with a big box. They want to make a house with it. They cut a door and some windows with scissors. They draw flowers and stars with crayons. They are happy with their house. But then, Tom comes. Tom is older and bigger than Lily and Ben. He is not nice. He says, "This is my box now. Go away!" He pushes Lily and Ben out of the house. He laughs and goes inside. Lily and Ben are sad and angry. They want their house back. They think of a plan. They say, "Tom, we have a game for you. Do you want to play?" Tom says, "What game?" He is curious. Lily and Ben say, "It is a twist game. You have to twist your body like a snake. If you can twist more than us, you win. But if you twist less, you lose. And if you lose, you have to give us the box back. Do you agree?" Tom says, "Yes, I agree. I can twist better than you. I will win." They start the game. Lily and Ben twist their bodies a little bit. They pretend to be tired. They say, "Wow, this is hard. We can't twist anymore." Tom says, "Ha, ha, you are weak. I can twist more. Watch me." He twists his body a lot. He twists his arms and legs and neck and back. He twists so much that he gets stuck in the box. He can't move. He says, "Help, help. I can't get out. Let me go." Lily and Ben smile. They say, "You lose, Tom. You twist too much. You are not wise. You are silly. We win. This is our box. Bye, bye." They take the box and leave. Tom cries and shouts. He is sorry. He learns a lesson. He should not be mean to others. He should share and be kind.

One day, a little ant was walking in the grass. He saw a big house. Inside the house, a girl was cooking. The ant wanted to see what she was doing. He walked closer to the house and climbed up the wall. The girl saw the ant and said, "Hello, little ant! I am cooking. Do you want to watch?" The ant nodded his head and watched the girl cook. She was making a yummy cake. The ant thought it was fun to watch her cook. After the girl finished cooking, she gave the ant a small piece of cake. The ant thought it was tough to eat, but he liked it. The girl and the ant became good friends. They talked and laughed, and the ant helped the girl cook every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big zoo. In the zoo, there were many animals. There was a tall giraffe, a big elephant, and a fast monkey. All the animals were happy in the zoo. One day, a new animal came to the zoo. It was a pretty peacock. The peacock could bow and show its colorful tail. The other animals liked to watch the peacock bow. They thought it was fun. The tall giraffe was envious. It wanted to bow like the pretty peacock. So, the giraffe tried to bow, but it was not easy. The big elephant and the fast monkey helped the giraffe. They all practiced bowing together. Soon, the tall giraffe could bow too. All the animals in the zoo were happy and played together.
