Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog and the Puddle

One day, a little dog named Spot went for a walk. He saw a big puddle on the ground. Spot thought, "That looks like a fun place to play!" He ran around the puddle, feeling very happy. Spot saw his friend, a cat named Fluffy. Fluffy looked at the puddle too. "Wow, that's a big puddle!" said Fluffy. "Let's jump in it and make a crazy splash!" Spot agreed, and they both got ready to jump. Spot and Fluffy jumped into the puddle with a big splash! They laughed and played in the water. They were having so much fun. Then, the sun came out and dried up the puddle. They looked at each other and smiled. They knew they would always remember their crazy puddle adventure.

One day, a small boy named Tim found a piece of paper. He wanted to draw a big, icy snowman on it. So, he took his crayons and set them on the table. He started to draw a big, round snowman with a hat and a scarf. As Tim was drawing, his friend, Anna, came to play. "What are you doing?" she asked. "I am drawing an icy snowman," Tim said. "Can I help?" Anna asked. "Yes, you can draw the snowman's face," Tim said with a smile. Together, they drew the snowman's happy face, with a big smile and two round eyes. When they were done, they set the paper on the wall. They both looked at their icy snowman and felt very proud. Tim and Anna played together all day, talking about the fun things they would do if the snowman came to life.

Tom was a shy boy who liked to watch films. He had many films at home, but he wanted to see a new one at the big house with many seats. His mom said they could go on Saturday, and Tom was very happy. On Saturday, Tom and his mom went to the big house. They bought some popcorn and juice, and found their seats. The film was about a dog who had many adventures. Tom liked the dog very much, and laughed at his tricks. But then, Tom needed to go to the bathroom. He told his mom, and she said he could go by himself. She gave him a ticket and pointed to the door. Tom was a little scared, but he wanted to be brave. He took the ticket and walked to the door. But the big house was very crowded and noisy. Tom saw many people and many doors. He forgot which door was the bathroom, and which door was the film. He started to wander around, looking for his mom. He felt lost and sad. "Mommy, where are you?" he cried. Then, he heard a friendly voice. "Hey, little buddy, are you lost?" It was a man who worked at the big house. He wore a red shirt and a name tag. He smiled at Tom and took his hand. "Don't worry, I'll help you find your mom. What's your name?" "Tom," Tom said, wiping his tears. "Nice to meet you, Tom. I'm Dave. Do you have a ticket for the film?" Tom nodded and showed him the ticket. "Ah, you're watching the dog film. That's a good one. Come on, let's go back to your seat. Your mom must be worried about you." Dave led Tom to the right door and opened it. Tom saw the big screen and the dog. He also saw his mom, who was looking around anxiously. She saw Tom and Dave and ran to hug him. "Tom, I'm so glad you're okay. I was so scared. Where did you go?" Tom told her what happened. He said he was sorry for wandering away. He thanked Dave for helping him. "It's okay, Tom. I'm just happy you're safe. And you're very brave for going to the bathroom by yourself. Thank you, Dave, for finding him." "You're welcome, ma'am. It's my job to help people. Enjoy the film, Tom. And don't wander away again, okay?" "Okay," Tom said. He smiled and hugged his mom. He was happy to be with her. He watched the rest of the film and enjoyed it very much. He learned that wandering can be scary, but also that there are nice people who can help. He decided he liked the big house, but he liked his mom more.
