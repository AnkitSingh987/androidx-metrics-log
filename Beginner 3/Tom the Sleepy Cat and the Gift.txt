Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Sleepy Cat and the Gift

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy cat named Tom. Tom liked to sleep all day and play all night. One day, Tom's friend, a little girl named Sue, wanted to send him a gift. Sue knew that Tom loved to play on a stage. So, she thought of sending him a toy stage. Sue worked hard to make the stage. She used a big box, some paint, and a lot of love. When she was done, the stage was very pretty. Sue was excited to send it to Tom. She put the stage in a big bag, and carried it to Tom's house. When Tom saw the stage, he was so happy! He jumped on the stage and played all day long. Tom was so thankful for Sue's gift. That night, Tom was very sleepy. He curled up on the stage and slept all night. And from that day on, Tom and Sue had even more fun playing together.

Tim and Mia liked to play with their toys in the garden. They had many toys, but their favorite was a big red car that could make noises and move by itself. They took turns to drive the car on the grass and the dirt, and sometimes they pretended to be in a race. One day, they saw a big rock in the garden. It was round and shiny and had a crack on one side. Tim and Mia wondered what was inside the rock. Maybe it was a treasure, or a dinosaur bone, or a magic stone. They decided to try to open the crack and see. They used their hands, their feet, and their toys to hit the rock, but it was very hard and did not break. They tried to push the rock, but it was very heavy and did not move. They tried to pull the rock, but it was very stuck and did not budge. They were about to give up, when Mia had an idea. "Let's use the car!" she said. "The car is strong and fast and can make the rock open!" Tim agreed. He took the car and put it on the ground. He pressed a button and the car started to make noises and move by itself. He aimed the car at the rock and said, "Ready, set, go!" The car zoomed towards the rock. Tim and Mia watched with excitement. They hoped to see the rock split and display what was hidden inside. But they did not see that. Instead, they heard a loud bang and saw the car bounce back and fly in the air. The car landed on the grass and stopped making noises and moving. The rock was still whole and unopened. Tim and Mia ran to the car. They saw that the car had a big dent and a crack on the front. The car was broken and could not be fixed. Tim and Mia felt very sad and sorry. They wished they had not used the car to hit the rock. They wished they had left the rock alone. They hugged each other and said, "We are sorry, car. We are sorry, rock. We are sorry, Mom and Dad. We learned our lesson. We will not do that again."

Once upon a time, there was a bird named Bob. Bob had many smooth feathers. He liked to fly and play with his friends. One day, he found a big feather on the ground. It was very pretty. Bob wanted to talk to his friends about the big feather. He flew to find them. He saw his friend, Cat, and said, "Look at my big feather!" Cat liked the feather too. They were both very happy. Bob, Cat, and their friends played with the big feather all day. They took turns holding it and feeling how smooth it was. They talked about how fun it was to find something so special. They all had a great day together.

Once upon a time, there was an enormous tree in the woods. Many animals lived near the tree. One day, a little squirrel and a big bear decided to go on a trip. They wanted to find a camp. "Let's go to camp!" said the squirrel. "Yes, let's go!" said the bear. They walked and walked, looking for the camp. On their way, they saw many pretty flowers, tall trees, and small bugs. As they walked, a big fly came and landed on the bear's nose. The bear did not like it. He tried to slap the fly away, but it was too fast. The squirrel laughed and said, "You are so big, but you can't catch a tiny fly!" Finally, they found the camp. They played games, sang songs, and ate yummy food. The squirrel and the bear had a great time. They were very happy they had found the camp.
