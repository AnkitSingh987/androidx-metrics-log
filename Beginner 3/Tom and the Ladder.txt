Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and the Ladder

One day, a cat named Tom saw a big tree. He wanted to see what was on top. So, he started to climb the tree. He climbed and climbed until he got very high. Then, he looked down and got scared. "Help! I can't get down!" Tom cried. A dog named Max heard Tom. Max wanted to help. He saw a ladder near the tree. Max grabbed the ladder and put it against the tree. "Tom, use this ladder to come down!" Max said in a serious voice. Tom looked at the ladder and felt brave. He climbed down the ladder, step by step. When Tom got to the ground, he was so happy. "Thank you, Max!" Tom said. Max and Tom became best friends and played together every day.

Lisa and Tom were friends. They liked to play with dolls and cars. One day, they found a large box in the garage. It had a lock on it. "Let's open it!" Lisa said. "Maybe there are toys inside." Tom nodded. He looked for a key, but he could not find one. He tried to break the lock, but it was too hard. "Maybe we can ask Mom or Dad," Lisa said. "They know how to open locks." Tom shook his head. "No, they will be angry. They said this box is for their marriage. They said we can't touch it." Lisa frowned. "What is marriage?" she asked. Tom shrugged. "I don't know. Maybe it's a game they play." Lisa smiled. "Maybe we can play it too. We can pretend we are married." Tom nodded. "Okay. How do we play?" Lisa thought for a moment. "We can wear Mom's and Dad's clothes. And we can say nice things to each other. And we can hug and kiss." Tom made a face. "Yuck. I don't want to kiss you. You are a girl." Lisa giggled. "Fine. We can just hug. Come on, let's go to their room." They ran to their parents' room and put on their clothes. Lisa wore a dress and a hat. Tom wore a suit and a tie. They looked very funny. "Hello, dear," Lisa said. "You look very handsome." "Hello, sweetie," Tom said. "You look very pretty." They hugged each other and laughed. They felt very happy. They did not hear the garage door open. They did not hear their parents come in. They did not hear them walk to their room. They only heard them gasp when they saw them. "What are you doing?" Mom asked. "We are playing marriage," Lisa said. "With our clothes?" Dad asked. "And our box?" Mom asked. Lisa and Tom looked at each other. They knew they were in trouble. They wished they had not touched the lock.

Once upon a time, there was a green frog named Spring. Spring loved to sing. One day, he wanted to sing at the big show called Opera. All his friends said he was too small. But Spring knew he had a big voice. Spring went to the Opera. He saw big animals singing. He was scared but wanted to try. He jumped onto the stage and started to sing. The big animals stopped and stared at him. They did not know a small frog could sing so well. Then, something unexpected happened. The big animals started to dance. They asked Spring to join them. Spring was so happy. He knew he could do it. Now, Spring was a big star at the Opera, and everyone loved his big voice.

One day, a little girl named Lucy was playing in her room. She saw a weird toy that looked like a telephone. Lucy picked it up and started to play with it. She pretended to call her friends and talk to them on the telephone. Suddenly, the telephone started to talk back to her! Lucy was very surprised. The telephone said it was lost and needed help to find its way back home. Lucy decided to help the telephone and started to search for its home. As they searched, they found a hidden door in Lucy's room. They opened the door and saw a room full of talking toys! It turns out the telephone was not weird at all. It was a magic telephone that could talk to all the toys in the secret room. Lucy was so happy to find the telephone's home and now she had many new friends to play with.
