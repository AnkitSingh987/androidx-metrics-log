Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Park

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park with her mom. Mia wore her favorite clothes, a blue dress with red flowers. In the park, they saw a big tree with tasty apples. Mia said, "Mom, can we eat the apples?" Mom looked at the tree and said, "Yes, Mia, but we need to ask the park man first." They found the park man and asked him. The park man said, "Sure, you can have some apples. I suggest you pick the red ones, they are the most tasty." Mia and her mom picked some apples and took them home. At home, Mia and her mom made a tasty apple pie. They ate the pie together and it was very good. Mia was happy she could share the apples with her mom.

Once there was a little girl named Jennie. She loved to cook delicious food with her mom. Every day, Jennie used the stove and the oven together to make warm and yummy meals. One day, Jennie was at the store with her mom and found something unknown. It was a strange food that neither of them had ever seen before. “What is this?” asked Jennie. Her mom smiled and said, “Let’s find out. We can bring it home and try it.” Jennie eagerly agreed and they brought the unknown food home. When they got there, Jennie and her mom cooked the food together. When they were done cooking, the smell of the strange food filled the kitchen. Jennie and her mom both tasted it and found it was a delicious new treat. They had made something wonderful by cooking the unknown food.

One day, a poor cat named Tom went for a walk. He wanted to find a new toy to play with. He looked and looked, but he could not find anything. Tom was sad. Then, Tom saw an ashtray on the ground. He thought it was a fun toy. He played with the ashtray all day long. It made him very happy. At night, Tom went home with his new toy. His mom saw the ashtray and said, "Tom, that is not a toy. It is for grown-ups." She took the ashtray away. Tom was sad again. His mom gave him a book to read. Tom read the book and felt happy again.

Anna liked to watch the doves in the park. They were white and soft and made cooing sounds. She wanted to touch one, but they always flew away when she came near. One day, she saw a dove that was hurt. It had a broken wing and could not fly. It was hiding under a bench, looking gloomy and scared. Anna felt sorry for the dove. She wanted to help it. She walked slowly and quietly to the bench. She reached out her hand and said, "Don't be afraid, dove. I won't hurt you. I want to be your friend." The dove looked at Anna with its dark eyes. It did not move or make a sound. Anna gently stroked its feathers and felt its warm body. She smiled and said, "You are so pretty, dove. What is your name?" The dove did not answer, but it seemed to relax a little. It let Anna pick it up and hold it in her arms. Anna felt happy and proud. She had made a new friend. She ran to her mom, who was sitting on a bench nearby. She showed her the dove and said, "Look, mom, I found a dove. It is hurt, but I will take care of it. Can we keep it, please?" Her mom looked surprised and worried. She said, "Oh, Anna, that is very kind of you, but we can't keep a wild dove. It belongs to the sky, not to our home. It needs a vet, not a pet. We have to take it to the animal shelter, where they can help it heal and fly again." Anna felt sad and angry. She did not want to give up her dove. She hugged it tighter and said, "No, mom, it is my dove. I love it. It loves me. It wants to stay with me. Please, mom, please!" Her mom sighed and said, "Anna, I know you love the dove, but you have to think of what is best for it. It is not happy in a cage or a house. It needs to be free and with its own kind. Trust me, it will be happier in the shelter than with us. You can visit it there, and maybe one day, you can see it fly again." Anna looked at the dove in her arms. It looked back at her with its dark eyes. It made a soft cooing sound. Anna felt a tear roll down her cheek. She knew her mom was right, but it was hard to let go. She kissed the dove and said, "Goodbye, dove. I will miss you. I hope you get better soon. Thank you for being my friend." She handed the dove to her mom, who wrapped it in a scarf. They walked to the car, where they had a box to put the dove in. Anna looked at the sky, where she saw other doves flying and settling on the trees. She hoped her dove would join them someday. She felt gloomy, but also brave. She had done the right thing.
