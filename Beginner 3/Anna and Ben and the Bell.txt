Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben and the Bell

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play outside. One day, they see a new bicycle in front of a big house. The bicycle is shiny and red. It has a bell and a basket. "Wow, look at that bicycle!" Anna says. "It is so pretty!" "Yes, it is," Ben says. "But it is also expensive. The big house belongs to Mr. Lee. He is very rich. He bought the bicycle for his son, Sam." Anna and Ben walk closer to the bicycle. They want to touch it and ride it. But they are afraid of Mr. Lee. He is not very nice. He does not like children. "Hello, who are you?" A voice says behind them. They turn around and see Sam. He is the same age as Anna and Ben. He is wearing a helmet and gloves. "We are Anna and Ben," Anna says. "We live in the small house over there. We like your bicycle. It is very nice." "Thank you," Sam says. "Do you want to try it? I can share it with you. I have no friends to play with. Mr. Lee is always busy. He does not have time for me." Anna and Ben are surprised. They think Sam is kind. They smile and nod. Sam helps them to get on the bicycle. He shows them how to ring the bell and use the brakes. They take turns to ride the bicycle. They have a lot of fun. They forget about Mr. Lee and the big house. They only see the shiny and red bicycle. They make a new friend. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy who wanted a berry. He asked his mom to go and find one for him. His mom told him to go outside and find one himself. The little boy was a bit anxious, but he decided to go and search for the berry. He explored the garden and the trees around the house, but he could not find one. The little boy started to behave badly. He threw sand and rocks, shouted and screamed. His mom was so angry. Soon, the little boy heard a voice. It was the voice of a spirit. He asked the spirit if he could help him find a berry. The spirit told him he could, but the little boy had to promise to never behave badly again. The little boy said that he would, and the spirit told him to go to the far corner of the garden. There, he found a big red berry. He was so happy! Sadly, when the little boy was about to pick the berry, the spirit appeared again. He told the boy that he had broken his promise, and that he had to return the berry. The little boy sadly put the berry back and went home. He was very sad, and learned a valuable lesson about being a good boy.

Once upon a time, there was a smart cat named Tom. Tom loved to play all day long. He liked to chase birds, climb trees, and find new friends. One day, Tom found a small piece of gum on the ground. He did not know what it was, so he decided to ask his friends. First, Tom asked his friend, the dog. The dog said, "I don't know, Tom. I think it's not good for us. Let's leave it alone." Then, Tom asked the bird. The bird said, "I don't know either, Tom. But it looks yucky. We should not touch it." Tom was curious, but he listened to his friends. That night, Tom went to sleep feeling happy. He knew that he made a good choice by listening to his friends. The next day, Tom's owner told him that gum was not good for animals. Tom was glad he did not eat it. The moral of the story is to listen to our friends when they give us good advice, and always be careful with things we do not know.

One day, a bright sun was in the sky. A boy named Tim saw a big tree. He wanted to climb it. So, he started to go up. When he was high, he saw a cord. It was long and strong. He had an idea. Tim called his friend, Sam. "Sam, come see this cord!" Sam came running. "Wow, it's so long!" Sam said. They decided to play with the cord. They tied it to the tree and leaned on it. It was fun, like a swing! But then, something unexpected happened. The cord broke! Tim and Sam fell down. They were scared but not hurt. They learned that it's not good to play with things they don't know. From that day, they were more careful and played safe games.
