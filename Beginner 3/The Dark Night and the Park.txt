Category: Beginner
Title: The Dark Night and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a dark night. A little boy named Tim wanted to play on the swing. He went to the park with his mom. The park was very dark, but Tim was brave. While Tim was swinging, he saw a bright light in the sky. It was a comet! The comet was big and shiny. Tim was amazed and said, "Mom, look! A comet!" But mom was scared of the dark and did not want to look. Tim wanted his mom to see the comet too. He said, "Please, mom, don't be scared. I am here with you." Mom took a deep breath and looked at the sky. She saw the comet and smiled. They both enjoyed the beautiful comet together.

Once upon a time, there was a little comet who loved to fly fast. He was very competitive and always wanted to be the best. He liked to race with his friends, the stars, to see who could go the farthest in the sky. One day, the comet felt very tired, but he did not want to stop. He thought that if he took a rest, he would lose the race. So, he kept flying and flying, even though he was very sleepy. Soon, the comet learned that taking a rest is important. When he finally took a break, he felt much better and could fly faster. The moral of the story is that it is important to rest when you are tired, even if you want to win.

Once there was a dog named Spot. Spot was an independent dog who liked to go on walks by himself. One day, when Spot went for a walk, he saw a big box. Spot looked at the box and thought, "I wonder what's inside." Just then, a little girl named Sue came running up to Spot. Sue was the owner of the box. She said, "Hi, Spot! Do you want to see what's in my box?" Spot barked and wagged his tail. Sue opened the box, and inside was a magic hat. Sue put the hat on Spot's head. Sue said, "Watch this, Spot!" She waved her hands and said, "Disappear!" Spot looked around and saw that the box had disappeared. Spot and Sue laughed and played with the magic hat all day. They made many things disappear and had a lot of fun.

One day, a beautiful yacht was on the water. The yacht had big sails and was very fast. The sun was shining, and the sky was blue. On the yacht, there was a boy named Tim and his dad. They were having fun sailing together. Tim saw a toy gun on the yacht. He picked it up and said, "Dad, can I shoot the toy gun?" His dad looked at him and said, "Tim, we should not shoot at things for fun. It can be dangerous and scare others." Tim thought about it and agreed with his dad. They put the toy gun away and continued to sail. They saw birds, fish, and other boats. They waved at the people on the other boats and everyone was happy. At the end of the day, Tim learned that it's better to be kind and not scare others. He knew that they could still have fun without shooting the toy gun. Tim and his dad sailed back home, and they had a great day on their beautiful yacht.

One day, a child named Tim wanted to play outside. He put on his thick coat and went to the park. At the park, he saw a big hill. Tim wanted to climb the hill, but it was too steep. He tried to walk up, but he fell down. Tim was sad. Then, Tim had an idea. He decided to crawl up the hill. He got on his hands and knees and started to crawl. It was hard work, but he did not give up. Tim crawled and crawled, and he was getting closer to the top. Finally, Tim reached the top of the hill. He was so happy! He looked down and saw his thick coat had helped him crawl up the hill. Tim felt proud that he did not give up, and he had fun playing at the park all day.
