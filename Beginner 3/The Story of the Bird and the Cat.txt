Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Bird and the Cat

One day, a big, impressive bird met a small bird. The big bird wanted to be friends with the small bird. The big bird said, "I can offer you a big tree to live in for one year." The small bird was happy and said yes. The small bird moved into the big tree. They lived together and played together. The big bird and the small bird had a lot of fun. But one day, they had a fight. The small bird wanted to play a new game, but the big bird did not want to play. The small bird was sad and went to find a new friend. The small bird met a big cat. The big cat was nice and wanted to play the new game. The small bird was happy and played with the big cat. But the big cat was not a good friend. The big cat ate the small bird. The big bird was sad and missed the small bird. The big bird learned that sometimes, friends can fight, but they should still be friends.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to visit her Aunt Jane. Aunt Jane had a pretty, delicate flower in her hair. Lily loved her Aunt Jane very much. She was always happy to see her. Aunt Jane said, "Lily, let's play catch!" They went outside and started to play. Lily threw the ball to Aunt Jane, but she missed it. The ball rolled away. They both laughed and ran to get the ball. After playing, they went back inside. Aunt Jane made Lily a yummy snack. They talked and laughed together. Lily said, "I miss you when I don't see you, Aunt Jane." Aunt Jane smiled and said, "I miss you too, Lily." They hugged and promised to see each other again soon.

Ben and Mia liked to draw with crayons on paper. They had many colors and shapes to make pictures. Sometimes they made mistakes and wanted to change their drawings. They used an eraser to rub out the crayon marks. The eraser was pink and soft. One day, they found a new eraser in their box of crayons. It was black and hard. Ben picked it up and tried to use it on his paper. He rubbed it on his drawing of a sun. But the eraser did not work. It made the paper dark and dirty. Ben was sad and angry. "Stop, stop, stop!" Mia shouted. "That is not an eraser, that is a coal! It makes things black, not white. You are ruining your picture!" Ben looked at his paper and saw that Mia was right. His sun was gone and there was a big black spot instead. He felt sorry and ashamed. He threw the coal on the floor and said sorry to Mia. "It's okay, Ben," Mia said. "We can make a new picture together. We can use the pink eraser to make the paper white again. And we can use the yellow crayon to make a new sun. And we can use the blue crayon to make the sky. And we can use the green crayon to make the grass. And we can use the red crayon to make the flowers. And we can use the brown crayon to make the tree. And we can use the white crayon to make the clouds. And we can use the orange crayon to make the bird. And we can use the purple crayon to make the butterfly. And we can use the pink crayon to make the heart. And we can use the black crayon to write our names. And we can use the silver crayon to make the stars. And we can use the gold crayon to make the moon." Ben smiled and nodded. He liked Mia's idea. They took their crayons and their eraser and started to make a new picture. They had fun and they were happy. They made the best picture ever.

Once upon a time, in a humble little forest, there lived a tiny bug named Tim. Tim was very small, but he had a big heart. He loved to play with his friends, the ants and the bees. One day, Tim found a big log. He wanted to squeeze through a hole in the log to see what was inside. He pushed and pushed, but he could not fit. Tim felt sad, but he did not give up. Just then, a friendly bird came by and saw Tim. The bird had an idea. She used her beak to make the hole bigger. Tim tried again, and this time, he could squeeze through! Inside the log, he found a shiny treasure! Tim and the bird shared the treasure with all their friends, and they all lived happily ever after.
