Category: Beginner
Title: The Grumpy King and the Crown

Once upon a time, there lived a grumpy king. The king wore a golden crown. Every morning, he'd wear the crown to show his greatness. One morning, the king woke up and couldn't find his crown. He called for his servants. "My crown! Where is it?" the king said, looking grumpy. "We don't know, your majesty," one of the servants said. The king looked everywhere. He looked in the castle, he looked in the garden, he even looked in the pond. But his crown was nowhere to be found. Then, the king thought of a magical creature that lived in the forest. The king hurried to the forest and asked the magical creature. "Have you seen my crown?" the king said. The magical creature smiled and said, "Yes, a mischievous fox stole it and disappeared". The king was very sad, but the magical creature gave him a beautiful new crown. The king was thrilled and thanked the magical creature. He put on the new crown and went back to the castle. From then on, he never let the crown disappear!

One day, a boy named Tim went to a shop. He wanted to sell his toy. The shop had many things, like a big cell with a lock. Tim felt lucky because he thought he would get a lot of money for his toy. A man at the shop said, "I want to buy your toy. But first, can you help me open this cell?" Tim said, "Yes, I can help you." They both tried to open the cell. They pulled and pushed, but it was hard. Just then, a little dog ran into the shop. The dog jumped and hit the cell. The cell opened! Inside the cell, there were more toys! Tim and the man were very happy. The man said, "You are a lucky boy, Tim. You can keep your toy and take one from the cell too!" Tim smiled and thanked the man and the little dog.

Once upon a time, in a small swamp, there lived a frog. The frog liked to jump and play all day. One day, the frog saw a glow in the dark water. It was pretty and bright. The frog wanted to see what it was. The frog jumped into the water and swam to the glow. As the frog got closer, it saw that the glow came from a deaf fish. The fish could not hear the frog coming. The frog thought it was fun to play with the deaf fish, so they played together all day. As the sun went down, the glow from the deaf fish got brighter. The frog and the fish were having so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A big bird saw the glow and thought it was food. The bird swooped down and grabbed the deaf fish. The frog tried to help, but it was too late. The bird flew away with the fish, and the frog was left all alone in the dark swamp.

Once upon a time, in a big, big park, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom liked to play and sleep all day. One day, Tom saw a tent in the park. He thought it was a fun place to play. Tom went inside the tent and saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was playing with her toys. Tom said, "Meow, meow," and Sue smiled at him. They played together with the toys. After a while, Tom got hungry. He looked at Sue and said, "Meow, meow," to demand food. Sue understood and gave Tom a big bowl of yummy cat food. Tom ate it all up and said, "Meow, meow," to say thank you. They played some more and then took a nap in the tent. They became best friends and had lots of fun together.

One day, a naughty little cat named Tom found a long string. Tom wanted to play with the string, so he tried to tie it around his tummy. But Tom did not know how to tie a knot. He tried and tried, but he could not do it. Tom's friend, a big dog named Max, saw Tom trying to tie the string. Max was good at tying knots, so he came to help Tom. Max said, "I will help you tie the string around your tummy, Tom." Tom was happy and let Max help him. With Max's help, Tom tied the string around his stomach. Tom and Max played together, running and jumping with the string tied around Tom's tummy. They had a lot of fun and laughed all day. Tom was not naughty anymore, and he and Max became best friends.
