Category: Beginner
Title: Jane the Girl and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a very brave girl named Jane. One day, she was searching for something special. She wanted to find some black paint so she could color her favourite picture. Jane searched high and low, all around her house and outside in the garden. But she couldn't find the black paint anywhere. She looked through the cupboards and drawers, but all the colours were too bright. Suddenly, Jane had a great idea. She took some dark blue paint and mixed it with black paper. After a few minutes, she had made her very own black paint. Jane excitedly ran back to her favourite picture and started to paint. She was so proud of her new black paint. With all the fun colours, she had made the perfect painting!

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a big triangle on the ground. It was made of sticks. Lily wanted to play with the triangle, so she tried to pick it up. But it was too heavy! She felt helpless. Then, a friendly dog named Max came to the park. He saw Lily and asked, "Why are you sad?" Lily said, "I can't pick up this triangle. It's too heavy." Max wanted to help Lily, so he had an idea. He told her to lean on the triangle and push it. Lily leaned on the triangle and pushed with all her might. To her surprise, the triangle started to roll! It rolled and rolled until it stopped at the other side of the park. Lily and Max laughed and played with the rolling triangle. They never knew that triangles could be so much fun!

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They went down the noisy street where cars went beep-beep and people talked loud. Tim liked the noisy street because it was fun and full of life. As they walked, Tim saw a dog with a big smile. The dog wagged its tail and came to say hi. Tim's mom said, "Tim, the dog wants to show you something!" The dog picked up a ball in its mouth and dropped it at Tim's feet. Tim laughed and said, "You want to play?" Tim threw the ball and the dog ran after it. They played for a while, and Tim was very happy. His mom watched and smiled as they played. After some time, the dog's owner came and said, "It's time to go home, buddy." The dog gave Tim one last wag of its tail and went away. Tim waved goodbye and continued walking with his mom, feeling happy about the new friend he made on the noisy street.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big toy chest in his room. Inside the chest, there were lots of fun toys. Tim loved to play with his toys every day. One day, Tim found a weird toy in his chest. It was a small, green ball. Tim wanted to play with it, but his mom said "no". She told him it was not safe. But Tim did not listen. He refused to put the ball back in the chest. Tim played with the weird ball all day. But then, the ball broke. Green goo came out of the ball. It got all over Tim's toys and his room. Tim was very sad. His room was a big mess and some of his toys were ruined.

One day, a sweet frog named Lily lived near a swamp. She liked to jump and play with her friends. They loved to swing on the vines over the swamp water. One sunny day, Lily saw a new friend by the swamp. It was a little bird named Max. Max was scared to swing on the vines. "I want to try, but I am too scared," said Max. Lily had an idea. She held Max's hand and said, "We can swing together! I will help you." Max smiled and they swung on the vine. They went back and forth, over the swamp. Max was so happy and said, "Thank you, Lily! That was fun!" Now, Lily and Max swing on the vines every day. They became best friends and had lots of fun at the swamp.
