Category: Beginner
Title: Jack the Loyal Dog and the Elevator

Once upon a time there was a loyal dog named Jack. Jack liked to ride in the elevator with his owner. Every time they got in, Jack would seat himself in the corner, like a good boy. One day, Jack was so excited to get in the elevator again. But this time, something was different. As they entered the lift, Jack's ears perked up. He noticed something strange - the elevator was going up and down by itself! Jack got up and looked around. There were no people in the elevator, yet it was still moving. It was seem like a magical adventure! Eventually, the elevator stopped at it's destination and out stepped a kind old man. The man smiled at Jack and his owner. Jack was so happy and loyal to the old man for making the elevator ride so magical. He knew he would never forget it!

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a persistent boy who never gave up. One day, Tim saw a cat stuck in a tree. The cat was sad and wanted to come down. Tim wanted to help the cat. He tried to use his hand to reach the cat, but the cat was too high. Tim did not give up. He found a long stick and used it to help the cat. The cat was happy and jumped down from the tree. The people in the town saw what Tim did. They were happy and clapped for him. Tim felt good that he did not give up. The moral of the story is to always try your best and never give up.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to join a club. He saw his big sister, Sue, with her friends. They had a club. Tim asked Sue if he could join, but Sue said, "No, you are too little." Tim was sad, but he did not give up. He told Sue, "I can be careful and help the club." Sue thought about it and said, "Okay, but first, you must show us that you can be careful." The next day, Tim saw a big, red ball near the road. He remembered that he needed to be careful, so he looked both ways before getting the ball. Sue and her friends saw him and clapped. They said, "Good job, Tim!" Sue told Tim, "You can join our club now." Tim was so happy. He had shown that he could be careful, and now he was part of the club.

Tom and Sam were friends who liked to play with cars and trucks. They had many toys, but they always wanted more. One day, they saw a big box of gear at the park. Gear was things like wheels, nuts, bolts, and tools. They thought gear was fun to make cars and trucks with. They ran to the box and started to grab gear. They did not care who the gear belonged to. They did not ask or say please. They just grabbed and grabbed. They filled their bags with gear and ran away. But the gear was not theirs. It was Mr. Lee's gear. Mr. Lee was a bald man who worked at the park. He used the gear to fix the swings, the slides, and the benches. He was very kind and helpful. He saw Tom and Sam take his gear and he was very sad. He followed them to their houses and knocked on their doors. He told their moms and dads what they did. Tom and Sam felt very bad. They knew they did a wrong thing. They said sorry to Mr. Lee and gave back his gear. They also said sorry to their moms and dads and promised to be good. Mr. Lee was happy to get his gear back. He thanked Tom and Sam for being honest and sorry. He also gave them a gift. He gave them each a small car made of gear. He said he made them himself and he wanted them to have fun. He also said he would teach them how to use gear if they wanted to learn. Tom and Sam were very happy and grateful. They thanked Mr. Lee and hugged him. They learned a lesson. They learned that gear was not for grabbing, but for sharing and caring. They also learned that Mr. Lee was a good friend and a good teacher. They played with their cars and trucks and gear and had a lot of fun.
