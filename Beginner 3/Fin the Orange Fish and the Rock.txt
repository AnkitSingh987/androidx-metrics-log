Category: Beginner
Title: Fin the Orange Fish and the Rock

One day, a little orange fish named Fin was swimming in the sea. He saw a big net in the water. He wanted to swim away, but he could not resist going closer to see what was inside. Fin swam up to the net and said, "Hello, is anyone in there?" A small fish named Bubbles replied, "Help! I am stuck in this net, and I can't get out!" Fin thought for a moment and said, "I have an idea! I will find a way to help you." Fin swam away and found a sharp rock. He remembered that sharp things can cut through things. He took the rock and swam back to the net. He used the rock to cut the net, and Bubbles was free! "Thank you, Fin!" Bubbles said. "You saved me!" Fin smiled and said, "I am happy to help a friend." From that day on, Fin and Bubbles swam together and had many fun adventures in the sea.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a curious little boy named Tim. Tim liked to look at everything and ask questions. One day, he saw a big table in the kitchen. He wondered what it was for. Tim's mom was cooking food. She put the food on the table. Tim looked at the table and saw many different things to eat. But he did not like some of the food. He began to complain. "I don't want this," said Tim, pointing at the green stuff. His mom smiled and said, "Just try it, Tim." Tim tried the green stuff and found out that it was good. He stopped complaining and ate all of his food. From that day on, Tim was not scared to try new things. He learned that sometimes, even if something looks strange, it can be good. And the curious little boy and his mom lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a hungry alligator named Al. He lived in a big pond. Al had a friend, a little duck named Dee. They would play together every day. One day, Al and Dee were playing by the pond. Al said, "I am so hungry, Dee. I want to eat." Dee thought for a moment and then said, "Let's go find some food, Al!" As they were looking for food, they saw a big cake on a table. Al and Dee were so excited! They ran to the cake and started to eat it. But, oh no! The cake was very messy. Al got cake all over his face. Dee saw the mess and said, "Al, let me help you!" She took a napkin and began to wipe Al's face. As she was wiping, suddenly, Al's face turned into a big smiley face! The cake was magic! Al was not a hungry alligator anymore. He was a happy alligator! Al and Dee laughed and played for the rest of the day.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to study. He sat at his small table with his book. He liked to learn new things. Tim's mom was in the kitchen. She was cooking a big steak for dinner. Tim could smell the yummy food. Suddenly, a loud noise came from outside. Tim was scared. He did not know what the noise was. He looked out the window and saw a big dog. The dog was barking at a cat. The cat was scared too. Tim's mom heard the noise and came outside. She saw the dog and the scared cat. She shooed the dog away and saved the cat. Tim felt safe again. They all went inside and had the yummy steak for dinner. Tim was happy that his mom was brave and helped the scared cat.

Once there was a boy named Tim. Tim had new pants. The pants were smooth. Tim liked his smooth pants very much. He wore them every day. One day, Tim found a small bug. The bug was lost. Tim wanted to help the bug. He took a string and attach it to the bug. Tim attach the other end of the string to his smooth pants. Tim walked with the bug. The bug followed him. Tim and the bug were friends. They played and laughed. But then, something unexpected happened. The bug started to grow. It grew and grew. It was not a small bug anymore. It was a big bug. The big bug pulled Tim with the string. Tim was scared. But then, the big bug stopped. It looked at Tim and smiled. Tim knew the big bug was still his friend. They played and laughed together with the smooth pants.
