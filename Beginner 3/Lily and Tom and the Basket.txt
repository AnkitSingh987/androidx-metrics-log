Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Tom and the Basket

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play on the farm. They see many animals on the farm. They see cows, pigs, chickens and sheep. One day, they go to the sheep pen. They want to pet the sheep. The sheep have thick wool. The wool is soft and warm. Lily and Tom touch the wool gently. "Look, this sheep is small and cute!" Lily says. She points to a lamb. The lamb is white and fluffy. It bleats softly. "Can I lift it?" Tom asks. He wants to hug the lamb. "I don't know. Maybe it is too heavy for you." Lily says. She is not sure. Tom tries to lift the lamb. He puts his arms around it. He pulls hard. But the lamb is too heavy for him. He cannot lift it. He falls down. The lamb runs away. "Ouch!" Tom says. He rubs his head. He is sad. "Are you okay?" Lily asks. She helps him up. She is worried. "I'm okay. But I wanted to lift the lamb." Tom says. He pouts. "Maybe you can lift something else. Something lighter." Lily says. She looks around. She sees a basket. The basket has eggs. The eggs are from the chickens. "Look, Tom. You can lift this basket. It has eggs. Eggs are light." Lily says. She smiles. "Really? Can I try?" Tom asks. He is curious. "Sure. But be careful. Don't drop the eggs." Lily says. She warns him. Tom goes to the basket. He puts his hands on it. He lifts it. It is not too heavy for him. He can lift it. He is happy. "Yay! I can lift the basket!" Tom says. He shows Lily. "Good job, Tom! You are strong!" Lily says. She claps. They are both happy. They play with the basket and the eggs. They are careful. They do not drop the eggs. They have fun on the farm.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a colorful house. In this house, there lived a nice man named Tim. Tim had a job. His job was to repair things that were broken. One day, a little girl named Sue came to Tim's house. She had a broken toy. Sue said, "Tim, can you please repair my toy? It is my favorite." Tim smiled and said, "Of course, Sue. I will do my best to fix it for you." Tim worked very hard to repair Sue's toy. After a while, he fixed it! Sue was so happy. She said, "Thank you, Tim!" Tim was happy too. He loved doing his job and helping others. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had a pretty dress that she loved very much. One day, Lily wanted to wear her dress to play outside. She put on the dress and went out to the yard. As she played, Lily saw a deep hole in the ground. She was curious and wanted to see what was inside. Lily went closer and closer to the hole. Suddenly, she fell in! The dress got dirty and torn. Lily felt sad and regret for not being careful. But then, something unexpected happened. A kind bird flew down to help Lily. The bird picked up Lily with its strong wings and lifted her out of the hole. Lily was happy to be safe, but her dress was still dirty. The bird showed Lily a secret place with beautiful flowers. Lily picked some flowers and made a new dress out of them. Now, she had a special dress and a new friend.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the beach with her mom. They saw many shells and pretty things. Mia found a pale oyster. She said, "Mom, look at this oyster! It is so pretty!" Mia's mom said, "Yes, it is very pretty. But be careful, it might have a pearl inside." Mia was very happy and she wanted to find the pearl. She tried to open the oyster but it was hard. A big ball came rolling to Mia. She had an idea. She kicked the ball at the oyster. The oyster opened and something shiny was inside. Mia was so excited. She thought she found a pearl. But it was not a pearl. It was a small, shiny key. Mia and her mom were very surprised. They looked around and saw a little treasure box. Mia used the key to open the box. Inside, there were many pretty pearls. Mia and her mom were so happy. They had a fun day at the beach and found a big surprise.
