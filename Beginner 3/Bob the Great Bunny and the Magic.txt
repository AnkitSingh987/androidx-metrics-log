Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Great Bunny and the Magic

Once upon a time, there was a great bunny named Bob. Bob loved to eat carrots. One day, he found a big carrot in the garden. He was so happy! Bob tried to pull the carrot out of the ground, but it was stuck. He pulled and pulled, but it would not move. Then, his friend, the bird, came by and said, "Why don't you reverse and push the carrot out?" Bob thought it was a great idea. Bob reversed and pushed the carrot with all his might. Suddenly, the carrot popped out of the ground! But, it wasn't just a carrot, it was a magic carrot! The magic carrot could talk and it said, "Thank you for helping me. Now, I can grant you one wish." Bob wished for more carrots, and the magic carrot made it happen. From that day on, Bob and the magic carrot were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big farm. On this farm, there lived a farmer and his dog, Spot. The farmer grew corn. Spot loved to help the farmer with the corn. One day, the farmer said, "Spot, we need to deliver the corn to our friend, Tom." Spot wagged his tail and barked, "Woof!" Together, they put the corn in a big truck and drove to Tom's house. When they got to Tom's house, Spot was very sorry. He saw that some of the corn had fallen out of the truck. He looked at the farmer and said, "Woof, woof." The farmer smiled and said, "It's okay, Spot. We still have enough corn to give to Tom." They delivered the corn, and Tom was happy. Spot and the farmer went back to the farm, ready to grow more corn.

Lily was sick. She had a big fever and a sore throat. She did not want to eat or play. She only wanted to sleep. Her mom was worried. She called the doctor. The doctor came to Lily's house. He wore a white coat and a mask. He looked at Lily's throat and ears. He listened to her chest. He said, "Lily has a bad infection. She needs to go to the hospital. She will receive medicine there. A nurse will take care of her." Lily did not like the hospital. It was big and noisy and scary. She did not know anyone there. She missed her mom and dad and her toys. She cried a lot. A nurse came to her bed. She wore a blue dress and a hat. She smiled and said, "Hello, Lily. I'm your nurse. My name is Anna. I'm here to help you. You will receive medicine soon. It will make you feel better." Lily did not trust the nurse. She thought the nurse was mean. She did not want to receive medicine. She did not want to stay in the hospital. She wanted to go home. She said, "No, no, no! I don't want medicine! I don't want to stay here! I want to go home! I want my mom and dad!" The nurse did not listen to Lily. She said, "Lily, you have to receive medicine. It's good for you. It will make you well. You can't go home until you are well. You have to be brave and strong. This is a modern hospital. They have the best doctors and machines. They will take care of you." The nurse gave Lily a shot. It hurt a lot. Lily screamed and kicked and cried. She hated the nurse. She hated the hospital. She hated the medicine. She wished she was never sick. She wished she was never born. She did not feel better. She felt worse. She did not get well. She died.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely raven. The raven had a lot of friends. They all liked to play together. One day, the raven and his friends were playing in the big tree. While they were playing, a big wind came. The wind blew the raven's toy far away. The raven was very sad. He did not know how to get his toy back. His friends tried to help, but they could not find the toy. Then, a little mouse came. The mouse said, "I can help you find your toy." The raven and his friends did not think a little mouse could help. But they let the mouse try. The mouse ran fast and found the toy. The raven was so happy! The raven said, "Thank you, little mouse!" All the friends were very surprised. They did not know a little mouse could be so helpful. From that day on, the raven and the mouse were the best of friends. They all played together and had lots of fun.
