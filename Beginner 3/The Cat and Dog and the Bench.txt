Category: Beginner
Title: The Cat and Dog and the Bench

One day, a cat and a dog went to play at the park. They saw a bench and decided to sit on it. The bench was not soft, it was harsh. The cat did not like the harsh bench, so she jumped off. The dog saw a ball near the bench. He picked up the ball with his mouth and began to play. The cat wanted to play too, so she chased the dog. They ran around the park, having lots of fun. As they played, the sun began to turn down. The sky changed from bright to dark. The cat and the dog were tired from playing. They went back to the harsh bench and lay down. They were happy, even though the bench was not soft. They had a great day at the park.

Tom and Sue were friends who liked to play on the farm. They liked to run, jump, and chase the animals. One day, they saw a big cow with brown spots and horns. They wanted to touch it and see if it was soft. "Let's go near the cow," Tom said. "It looks rare and nice." "No, Tom, we can't," Sue said. "The farmer said the cow is angry and can hurt us. He said we should lock the gate and stay away." But Tom did not listen to Sue. He was curious and brave. He opened the gate and ran to the cow. Sue followed him, afraid and worried. The cow saw Tom and Sue and did not like them. It mooed loudly and shook its head. It ran towards them, fast and hard. Tom and Sue were scared and tried to run back to the gate. But they were too late. The cow caught them and pushed them with its horns. Tom and Sue fell down and cried. They were hurt and bleeding. The farmer heard the noise and came to see what happened. He saw Tom and Sue on the ground and the cow standing over them. He was angry and sad. He chased the cow away and helped Tom and Sue. He took them to the house and called their parents. Tom and Sue learned a lesson that day. They learned to listen to the farmer and not to go near the angry cow. They learned that rare things are not always good and that curiosity can be dangerous. They learned to lock the gate and stay safe.

Once upon a time, there was a little mole. The mole lived in a small hole in the ground. Every day, the mole would come out to play. One day, the mole found a toy. The toy was broken. The mole wanted to fix the toy. So, the mole began to examine the toy. The mole looked at the toy very closely. With a little help from a friend, the mole fixed the toy. They were so happy. They played with the toy all day long. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who lived in a small village. She loved to play outside and go on adventures. One day, when she was out, it started to rain. She was all alone, and she was very scared. The little girl started to cry and said, “I am so sorry! I want to go home.” Suddenly, an old man came walking by. He said, “My child, don’t be sorry. The rain is not so bad. You can go home when it stops raining.” The little girl smiled and said, “Thank you!” The old man said, “You’re welcome. It is important to remember that even if something seems hard, you can find a way.” The girl took this advice to heart, and the old man walked away. Soon the rain stopped, and she was able to go home. The moral of this story is that with persistence and courage, you can overcome anything.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable dog named Spot. Spot had a shield that he loved to play with. One day, he went to the park with his shield. At the park, Spot saw a little bird who was sad. The bird needed help to find her mom. Spot wanted to help, but he did not want to delay his play time with his shield. He thought for a moment and decided to help the bird. Spot and the bird looked all around the park. They found the bird's mom, and the bird was happy. Spot felt good for helping, even if it meant a delay in playing with his shield. In the end, Spot learned that being dependable and helping friends is more important than playing alone.
