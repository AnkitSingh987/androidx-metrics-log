Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Dog

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They saw a big dog with a red ball. The dog ran and jumped and barked. Lily and Ben liked the dog. They wanted to play with him. "Hello, dog!" Lily said. "Can we play with you and your ball?" The dog wagged his tail. He dropped the ball and ran away. Lily and Ben followed him. They ran and laughed and shouted. They followed the dog to a house. The house had a fence and a gate. The dog went inside the gate. Lily and Ben stopped at the fence. They could not go inside. "Where are you going, dog?" Ben asked. "Can we come with you?" The dog barked again. He came back to the fence. He had something in his mouth. It was a bone. A big, tasty bone. The dog looked at Lily and Ben. He wanted to share his bone. "Thank you, dog!" Lily said. "You are very nice. Can we have some of your bone?" The dog nodded. He put the bone on the ground. He let Lily and Ben have a bite. The bone was tasty. Lily and Ben smiled. They liked the bone. They liked the dog. They made a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim liked to climb trees. One day, he saw a big tree in his yard. He thought, "I want to climb that tree!" Tim started to climb the tree. He went up, up, up! But then, he saw something shiny. It was a tooth! "How did a tooth get in the tree?" Tim thought. Tim kept the tooth in his pocket. He climbed down the tree. He showed the tooth to his mom. She smiled and said, "That's your dad's tooth! He lost it when he was young, just like you. He climbed this tree too!" Tim was happy to find his dad's tooth and felt closer to him.

Sara and Ben are twins who go to school. They wear the same uniform every day: a blue shirt and a red skirt or pants. They like to learn new things and play with their friends. One day, their teacher tells them that they will have a special lunch from another country. She says it is very spicy and some children may not like it. She recommends that they try a little bit first and drink a lot of water. Sara is curious and wants to try the spicy food. She thinks it will be fun and tasty. Ben is scared and does not want to try it. He thinks it will be hot and hurt his mouth. He wants to eat his sandwich and apple instead. At lunchtime, Sara gets a plate of the spicy food and sits next to Ben. She takes a small bite and makes a funny face. "Wow, this is spicy!" she says. "But I like it. It has a lot of flavor. Do you want to try some, Ben?" Ben shakes his head and holds his sandwich. "No, thank you. I don't like spicy food. I like my sandwich and apple. They are good and safe." Sara shrugs and eats more of the spicy food. She drinks a lot of water and feels happy. Ben eats his sandwich and apple and feels bored. He watches Sara and the other children who try the spicy food. They look like they are having fun and learning something new. The next day, the teacher asks the children what they thought of the spicy food. Sara raises her hand and says, "I liked it. It was different and exciting. I learned that there are many kinds of food in the world and I want to try more." Ben lowers his head and says nothing. He feels sad and ashamed. He wishes he had tried the spicy food too. He learned that there are many kinds of food in the world and he missed a chance to try something new. The moral of the story is: It is good to be curious and try new things. You may discover something you like and learn something new. It is not good to be scared and avoid new things. You may miss something you like and learn nothing new.
