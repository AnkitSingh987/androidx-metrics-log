Category: Beginner
Title: The Huge Wolf and the Rabbit

Once upon a time, there was a huge wolf who lived in the forest. All the other animals respected and valued him. One day, a little rabbit was hopping in the woods and spotted the wolf. He was very scared and wondered what he should do. The rabbit timidly said to the wolf, "Please don't hurt me. I value you and I don't want to get hurt." The wolf looked at the rabbit and said, "You do not need to be scared. I value you too. I will never hurt you." The little rabbit felt safe and thanked the wolf. He continued hopping through the woods, thankful for the huge wolf's protection and kindness.

One day, a boy named Tim went to a big, modern train station with his mom. He saw a train with steam coming out of it. Tim was very excited and wanted to ride on the train. "Mom, can we ride the train with the steam?" asked Tim. His mom smiled and said, "Yes, we can put our bags on the train and ride it together." When they got on the train, Tim looked out the window and saw a big, green dinosaur. The dinosaur was friendly and waved at Tim. Tim was surprised and waved back. "Mom, look! A dinosaur!" Tim pointed at the window. His mom looked and laughed, "That's not a real dinosaur, Tim. It's just a statue at the train station." Tim felt happy to see the dinosaur, even if it was just a statue. He enjoyed the train ride with his mom and couldn't wait to tell his friends about the dinosaur he saw.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a smooth, shiny magnet. He loved to play with his magnet all day. One day, he took his magnet to the park to show his friends. At the park, Tim met his friend, Sue. "Look at my smooth magnet!" Tim said. Sue looked at the magnet and said, "Wow! It's so smooth and shiny!" They played with the magnet together. They put the magnet near things and watched it pull them close. They laughed and had fun. After a while, Tim and Sue saw a big dog. They wanted to see if the magnet would work on the dog's collar. They put the magnet close to the dog. The dog did not like it. It barked and chased Tim and Sue. They ran and ran, but the dog caught up to them. It took the smooth magnet from Tim and ran away. Tim and Sue were sad. They did not applaud. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Sue. Sue had a big smile that made everyone happy. She liked to play with her friends in the park. One day, it was getting dark outside. Sue's friend, Tim, lost his ball. He was sad and asked Sue for help. Sue wanted to lend a hand to her friend. Sue and Tim looked for the ball together. They found it under a tree. Tim was happy and thanked Sue for her help. They went back home, smiling and holding hands. And from that day on, they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a bright sunny forest, there was a little bunny with soft fur. This bunny loved to play with his friends. One day, he found a big red ball in the grass. He knew it was not his, but he insisted on playing with it. The bunny and his friends played with the red ball all day long. They rolled it, kicked it, and threw it high in the sky. They had so much fun, but they did not know who the ball belonged to. The bunny with soft fur thought that maybe they should find the owner, but his friends did not want to stop playing. As the sun started to go down, a little girl came into the forest. She saw the bunny with soft fur and his friends playing with her big red ball. She was happy to see them having fun, but she was sad because she missed her ball. The bunny with soft fur saw the little girl and knew it was her ball. He hopped over to her and gave her the big red ball. The little girl was so happy, and she thanked the bunny with soft fur. From that day on, they all played together and became the best of friends.
