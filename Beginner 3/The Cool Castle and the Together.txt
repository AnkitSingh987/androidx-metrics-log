Category: Beginner
Title: The Cool Castle and the Together

Once upon a time, there was a cool castle. In the castle, there lived a king, a queen, and a little prince. They were a happy family. They played together, ate together, and had fun together. One day, the little prince wanted to build a big sand castle. The king wanted to help him. But they disagreed on how to make it. The prince wanted a tall castle, and the king wanted a wide castle. They were not happy. Then, the queen came and saw them disagree. She had an idea. She said, "Why not make a tall and wide castle?" The king and the prince liked the idea. They worked together and built the best sand castle ever. They were happy again and played together in their cool castle all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a gray cat named Lily. Lily liked to clean her fur. One day, Lily went for a walk in the park. She saw a big tree and climbed to the top. In the tree, Lily met a bird. The bird said, "Hi! I like your clean fur!" Lily said, "Thank you! I like your nest!" The bird and Lily became friends. One day, while Lily was in the tree, she found a hat. The hat was magic! When Lily put the hat on, she could fly like a bird. Lily and the bird flew in the sky, happy and free.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had thick, long hair. Every day, she would use her comb to brush her hair. She liked to make her hair look nice. One day, Sue went outside to play. She saw a big red car. The car went "zoom" down the street. Sue liked the sound the car made. She ran after the car, laughing and shouting "zoom, zoom!" Sue played outside all day long. When it was time to go inside, her hair was messy. She took her comb and brushed her thick hair. Sue was happy after a fun day of playing and hearing the car go "zoom."

One day, a cat named Max had a plan. He wanted to get some milk from the big cow in the farm. Max was a deaf cat, so he could not hear very well. He asked his friend, a dog named Sam, to help him. Max said, "Sam, I have a plan. Let's go to the big cow and get some milk. Can you help me?" Sam was happy to help Max. They both walked to the big cow. When they reached the cow, Sam barked loud to get the cow's attention. Max could not hear the bark, but he saw the cow look at them. They asked the cow for some milk. The cow was kind and gave them milk to drink. Max and Sam were very happy and thanked the cow. They both enjoyed the milk and went back home.

Once upon a time, there was a little goat. The goat lived in a big farm with many animals. The goat had many friends, like the cow, the pig, and the sheep. They played together every day. One day, the goat heard a loud noise. It made the goat feel frightened. The goat ran to the cow and said, "I am scared. What was that noise?" The cow said, "Do not worry, little goat. It is just the farmer starting his truck." The goat felt better and said, "Thank you, cow. I am not scared now." The goat went back to playing with its friends, happy and safe. From that day on, the goat knew that the noise was just the truck, and it never felt frightened again.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man. He loved to count and number things. One day, he went to see a band. The band played fun songs. The bald man danced and clapped his hands. While he was dancing, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew off his hat. Under his hat, there was a surprise! The bald man had a big, colorful tattoo on his head. The band saw the tattoo and stopped playing. They looked at the bald man and smiled. They asked him to join their band. The bald man was very happy. He said yes and started to play music with the band. And they all played fun songs together.
