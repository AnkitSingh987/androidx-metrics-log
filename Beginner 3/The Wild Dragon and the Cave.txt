Category: Beginner
Title: The Wild Dragon and the Cave

Once upon a time, there was a wild dragon. This dragon was big and strong. The dragon lived in a cave near a small village. The people in the village were scared of the dragon. One day, a little girl named Lily saw the dragon. She was not scared. She wanted to be friends with the dragon. So, she brought some food to the dragon's cave. The dragon was very hungry and started to eat the food. It ate and ate until it was full. The wild dragon and Lily became good friends. They played together every day. The people in the village were not scared anymore. They knew the dragon was a good friend. And they all lived happily ever after.

Max was so excited! He was about to play a game of kick with his best friend, Katie. He grabbed a large ball, hopped up and down, and shouted, “Yippee! Let’s go!” Katie laughed and said, “Ok, I’ll hold the wheat. You kick!” Max smiled and ran over to the field. He took a deep breath anda big kick. The ball flew really fast and high! Max cheered, “Wow, I kicked it really far!” Katie said, “Now I’ll kick.” She ran up to the ball, took a deep breath and gave it a large kick. The ball flew even higher and further than Max’s! Together they kicked the ball back and forth, up and down, until the sun went down. What a fun day!

Once upon a time, there was a dancer named Lily. She loved to dance all day long. Lily was very good at dancing. One day, Lily's friend, Tim, wanted to dance too. But Tim did not have any dance shoes. So, Lily decided to lend him her extra pair of dance shoes. Tim was very happy and said thank you to Lily. Together, they danced and danced. They had so much fun. But then, they tasted a bitter lemon. They made a funny face and laughed. Lily and Tim kept dancing and had a great day.

One day, a new little dog came to the park. He was very happy to see other dogs playing. He had a big urge to join them and make new friends. So, he ran to them with a big smile. The other dogs were playing a fun quiz game. The new little dog wanted to play too. They asked him easy questions like, "What color is the sky?" and "What sound does a cat make?" The new little dog knew all the answers and felt very smart. The new little dog and his new friends played the quiz game all day. They laughed and had so much fun. The new little dog was very happy that he found friends who liked to play the same games as him. They all became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a gray fountain in a park. The fountain had water coming out of it. Kids loved to play in the water. They would splash and laugh. One day, the sun was very bright and hot. The water in the fountain started to dry up. The kids were sad because they could not play in the water anymore. A kind man saw the kids and wanted to help. He went to his home and brought a big hose. He filled the gray fountain with water again. The kids were happy and played in the water all day.

Tim and Mia were playing in the park with their ball. They liked to kick the ball to each other and run after it. Sometimes the ball went too far and they had to chase it. One time, the ball rolled under the fence and into the street. Tim and Mia ran to the fence and looked for the ball. They saw it on the other side, near a big car. Tim wanted to get the ball back. He said to Mia, "I can go under the fence and get the ball. You stay here and watch." Mia said, "No, Tim, don't go. The street is dangerous. There are many cars and they go fast. You can get hurt. We can ask a grown-up to help us." But Tim did not listen to Mia. He said, "I am not afraid. I am fast. I can escape the cars. You are a baby. You are scared." He crawled under the fence and ran to the ball. Mia was worried. She shouted, "Tim, come back. Please, come back." She saw a bright light coming from the car. It was the sun reflecting on the window. She blinked her eyes and rubbed them. She could not see Tim or the ball. She heard a loud honk and a screech. She heard Tim scream. She felt a cold sweat on her forehead. She cried, "Tim, Tim, are you okay?" She saw a man get out of the car. He ran to Tim and picked him up. He said, "Don't worry, kid, you are okay. You just have a scratch on your knee. But you were very lucky. You could have been hit by the car. Why did you run into the street? Where is your mom or dad?" Tim said, "I wanted to get my ball. It was under the fence. I thought I could escape the cars. I am sorry. I was wrong. I should have listened to Mia. She told me not to go. She was right. The street is dangerous." The man said, "Yes, she was right. And you should be sorry. You were very foolish and reckless. You should always listen to your friends when they care about you. And you should never play near the street. It is not a place for kids. It is a place for cars. Do you understand?" Tim said, "Yes, I understand. I am sorry, Mia. I am sorry, mister. I will never do that again. I will always listen to Mia. She is my friend. She is not a baby. She is smart." Mia said, "It's okay, Tim. I am glad you are okay. I am your friend. I love you. But don't ever scare me like that again. Promise?" Tim said, "I promise. I love you too. Can we have our ball back, please?" The man said, "Sure, here you go. But be careful. And have fun. And remember, the street is not a place for kids. It is a place for cars." The end. Moral value: The story teaches the children to listen to their friends when they care about them, to avoid playing near the street, and to be careful of the dangers of cars.
