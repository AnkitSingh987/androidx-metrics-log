Category: Beginner
Title: Rosie the Rabbit and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little rabbit named Rosie who loved exploring. One day, Rosie discovered a treasure chest filled with lots of interesting things. Rosie was so excited and decided to examine everything inside the chest. At the bottom of the chest, Rosie found a small orange ball. She picked up the ball and looked all around it. Suddenly, Rosie heard a sound coming from the ball. “Let me out!” called a tiny voice from inside the orange ball. Rosie was shocked. She carefully opened the ball and a little orange frog hopped out. “How did you get in there?” Rosie asked the frog. The frog explained he had been taking a swim in the nearby drain and the strong water pushed him into the orange ball. Rosie and the frog felt sad and agreed to never play near a drain again. They learned an important lesson: playing near a drain is dangerous.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little cat. The cat liked to play in a wide park. The cat did not know that there was a big dog that lived near the park. One day, the cat went to the wide park to play. The big dog saw the cat and wanted to play too. But the cat was scared of the big dog. The big dog did not know that the cat was scared. The big dog ran to the cat to play. The cat got very scared and ran away fast. The big dog did not know what to do. The cat ran and ran until it got lost. Now, the cat could not find its way home. The cat was sad and the big dog was sad too.

Ben and his dad went to the barber. Ben did not like the barber. The barber had a big white beard and a loud voice. He made Ben sit on a high chair and put a cloth around his neck. "Hello, little man. I'm going to cut your hair. Don't be scared, okay?" the barber said. Ben was scared. He did not want the barber to cut his hair. He liked his hair long and curly. He did not know the barber. He wanted his dad. "Dad, I don't want to. I want to go home. Please, dad," Ben cried. His dad smiled and hugged him. "It's okay, Ben. The barber is a nice man. He knows how to make your hair look good. It won't hurt, I promise. You'll be done soon. Do you want a sticker when you're done?" Ben sniffed and nodded. He liked stickers. He liked the ones with cars and animals. He hoped the barber had those. The barber smiled too. He took a pair of scissors and a comb. He started to cut Ben's hair gently. He talked to Ben about his favorite things. He asked him what he liked to do, what he liked to eat, what he liked to watch. Ben started to relax. He answered the barber's questions. He told him about his toys, his friends, his mom. He found out that the barber liked some of the same things. He also learned that the barber had a dog, a bike, and a grandson. Ben realized that the barber was not so bad. He was kind and funny. He knew a lot of things. He made Ben's hair look neat and tidy. "Wow, you look great, Ben. You're a handsome boy. Here, pick a sticker. You did a good job," the barber said. Ben smiled and looked at the stickers. He saw one with a car and one with an animal. He liked both. He could not decide. "Can I have two, please?" he asked. The barber laughed and nodded. He gave Ben two stickers. He also gave him a lollipop. "Sure, you can have two. You're a good customer. Come back anytime. And enjoy your lollipop," he said. Ben thanked the barber. He stuck the stickers on his shirt. He unwrapped the lollipop and put it in his mouth. He was happy. He held his dad's hand and walked out of the barber shop. He waved to the barber. "Bye, barber. See you soon," he said. The barber waved back. He was happy too. "Bye, Ben. See you soon," he said.
