Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Slide

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a big slide with a funny shape. The shape was like a big snake. Lily wanted to try the slide. Lily climbed up the slide and sat at the top. She looked down and said, "This slide is so high!" Her friend, Tim, was at the bottom of the slide. He said, "Don't worry, Lily! It's not tough. Just slip down the slide and you'll be okay!" Lily took a deep breath and let herself slip down the slide. She went fast and it was fun! When she reached the bottom, she laughed and said, "That was so much fun! Let's do it again!" Tim and Lily played on the slide all day, slipping down the funny shape and having a great time.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They saw a big, expensive house with a tall gate. Tim wanted to see what was inside the gate, but it was closed. He asked his mom, "Can we open the gate and look inside?" His mom said, "No, Tim. That is someone else's house. We can't just go in." Tim was sad, but he understood. They continued their walk and found a small dog on the street. The dog was hungry and looked sad. Tim wanted to help the dog, so he asked his mom, "Can we give the dog some food?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim. That's a kind thing to do." They went home and got some food for the dog. They came back and gave the food to the dog. The dog was very happy and wagged its tail. Tim felt good about helping the dog. He thought that even if he couldn't go inside the big gate, he could still do nice things for others.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved going to school. One day, Tim had to carry a big, flexible toy snake to school. He wanted to show it to his friends. But the toy snake was very long, and it was hard for Tim to hold it. On his way to school, Tim met a big dog. The dog saw the flexible toy snake and thought it was real. The dog barked and tried to take the snake away from Tim. Tim was scared and did not know what to do. Then, a kind girl named Lily saw Tim and the dog. She knew the dog, and she told it to go away. The dog listened to Lily and left. Tim was happy and thanked Lily. They both went to school together, carrying the big, flexible toy snake. Tim and Lily became good friends, and they played with the toy snake at school with their friends.

One day, Joe and his mom went to the park. They saw something that made Joe’s belly feel funny and his eyes get bigger. It was an enormous cabinet. “What’s that?" asked Joe. "It's a cabinet," said his mom. "But it's so big," said Joe, his eyes widening in amazement. "Yes, it is," said his mom. Then something even better happened. Joe and his mom saw something big and yellow fly around the cabinet. “Mom, what is it?" asked Joe. "It's a bee, Joe," said his mom. Joe saw the bee fly and he felt so happy. “Can I fly too, Mom?" said Joe. His mom smiled and took Joe's hand. Together, they ran and ran until they both felt like they were flying.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a sleepy cat named Tom. Tom loved to eat. His favorite food was dough. One day, Tom found a big ball of dough in the kitchen. He was so happy! While Tom was eating the dough, he heard a loud noise. It was a chair that fell and broke. Tom was scared. He wanted to repair the chair before his owner found out. But Tom was just a cat, and he didn't know how to fix things. Then, something unexpected happened. A little mouse named Jerry saw Tom and wanted to help. Tom was surprised because cats and mice are not usually friends. But Jerry knew how to repair the chair. Together, they fixed the chair, and Tom shared his dough with Jerry. From that day on, Tom and Jerry became best friends.
