Category: Beginner
Title: John and the Dad

John and his dad were in the jeep on a sunny day. John was excited to be going out and seeing the world. “I want to be the driver,” said John to his dad. “Not today, buddy,” said his dad, “you’re a bit too young.” John didn’t like that answer and pouted. Eventually they stopped at a place with trees and rocks. “Let’s shoot the gun,” said his dad. John was very excited and rushed to the gun. His dad showed him how to use it and gave him a target. John was bossy and lined up the target with the gun and took aim. John pulled the trigger and the gun shot a bullet towards the target. John cheered when he saw he hit the bullseye. His dad was proud of him and hugged him tightly.

Once upon a time, there was a little red wagon. The wagon was very tidy. It was in a small town. In the town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with the wagon. He would call his friends to play with him. One day, Tim and his friends were playing with the wagon. They were having fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took the wagon away. Tim and his friends were sad. They looked for the wagon, but they could not find it. After a while, a girl named Sue found the wagon. She saw that it was tidy and knew it was not hers. Sue called Tim and his friends. She gave the wagon back to them. They were all happy. They learned that it is good to be tidy and to help others.

Once upon a time there was a huge player. He was very strong and brave. One day, he decided to manage something very difficult. He wanted to climb to the top of a huge hill. He ran and ran and eventually reached the bottom of the hill. He was tired but he was determined to continue. He started climbing, but it was tough. He almost gave up, but then he heard a voice. "Come on!" said the voice. "I believe in you. You can do it!" The player looked up and saw a magical creature. It had wings and it was flying around the hill. The player was encouraged. He managed to reach the top of the hill. He was so happy and proud of himself. He thanked the magical creature for believing in him. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a weird toy, a lime. He loved to play with it all day. One day, Tim lost his lime at the park. He was very sad. Tim went to the park to look for his lime. He saw his friend Sue. Sue saw Tim was sad. "Why are you sad, Tim?" she asked. Tim said, "I lost my lime at the park." Sue wanted to help Tim find his lime. Tim and Sue looked everywhere for the lime. They looked under the slide, by the swings, and in the sandbox. Finally, they found the lime by a big tree. Tim was so happy to have his lime return. He said, "Thank you, Sue!" They played with the lime together and had a fun day at the park.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk in the woods. He saw a big tree with a stick hanging from a branch. Tim was a bit frightened, but he wanted the stick to play with. "Can I have the stick, please?" Tim asked the tree. The tree did not say anything. So, Tim tried to jump and grab the stick. But he could not reach it. He tried again and again, but the stick was too high. A big bird saw Tim and said, "I can help you get the stick." The bird flew up and tried to get the stick for Tim. But the stick was stuck very tight. The bird tried very hard, but the stick would not come down. The bird got very tired and said, "I am sorry, Tim. I cannot get the stick for you." Tim was sad and frightened. He did not get the stick, and he walked home with tears in his eyes. The stick stayed hanging in the tree, and Tim never got to play with it.
