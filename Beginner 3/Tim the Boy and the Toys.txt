Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to eat peanuts. One day, he found a big peanut under his bed. Tim was very happy. That night, Tim had a dream. In the dream, the peanut became a big, rude peanut man. The peanut man was not nice. He took all of Tim's toys away. Tim was very sad. When Tim woke up, he saw that his toys were gone. The big peanut was gone too. Tim cried and cried. He never found his toys or the big peanut again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to gaze at the big, bright sun in the sky. One day, he saw a big, green frog near the pond. The frog said, "Ribbit! I can show you a new taste." Tim was curious, so he followed the frog to a big tree. The frog found a fruit and said, "Here, try this. It's a special taste." Tim took a bite of the fruit, but it was disgusting! He made a funny face and said, "Yuck! This is not good." Just then, the frog started to laugh. It was not a frog, but a tricky fairy in disguise! The fairy said, "I'm sorry, Tim. I wanted to play a joke on you." She waved her wand and turned the disgusting fruit into a sweet, tasty treat. Tim smiled and they both enjoyed the new taste together.

Tom and Lily like to play with sand. They have a big bucket and a small shovel. They make a castle with sand. They put shells and stones on the castle. They are happy. But then a big boy comes. He is ugly and mean. He kicks the castle with his foot. He laughs and says, "Your castle is bad. I can make a better castle." Tom and Lily are sad and angry. They stand up and shout, "No, you can't! Go away! You are bad!" The big boy is surprised and scared. He sees Tom and Lily's mom and dad. They are coming to the sand. They look angry too. He runs away. Tom and Lily's mom and dad hug them. They say, "We are sorry. That boy was rude. Your castle was beautiful. Do you want to make another one?" Tom and Lily nod and smile. They say, "Yes, please. We like to make castles. They are fun." They take their bucket and shovel and start again. They are happy.

One day, a little girl named Mia went outside to play. The sky was full of big, gray clouds. Mia saw that the rain was coming soon. She knew she had to find a place to stay dry. Mia had an easy idea. She would go to the big tree near her house. Mia ran to the big tree and sat down. Soon, the rain started to fall. It was a soft rain at first, but then it became a big, loud rain. Mia was happy that she was under the tree. The tree's big leaves helped keep her dry. As the rain came down, Mia saw a little bird. The bird was wet and cold. Mia wanted to help the bird. She had another easy idea. Mia took off her coat and put it close to the bird. The bird went under the coat and stayed dry. Mia smiled and felt happy. She and the bird were safe from the rain under the big tree.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a big, interesting toy. One day, Lucy fell and hurt her arm. Her mom took her to the hospital. At the hospital, the nice doctor looked at her arm. He gave her a fun bandage with pretty colors. Lucy was happy and felt better. She wanted to go home and play with her interesting toy. When they got home, Lucy saw her toy and gave it a big kiss. She was so happy to be home and play with her toy again. From that day on, Lucy always remembered to be careful when playing, so she wouldn't have to go back to the hospital.
