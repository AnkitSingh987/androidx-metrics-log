Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Mill and the Tim

Once upon a time, there was a big, ugly mill. It was old and not pretty. One day, a boy named Tim went to the mill. He saw the mill lean to one side. Tim was scared. "Mom, look at the mill!" Tim said. "It is not straight." Mom looked at the mill and said, "Don't worry, Tim. It is just old." Just then, they heard a loud sound. The mill was not leaning anymore. It was straight and not ugly. A big, nice man came out of the mill. "Hi, I am Mr. Mill," he said. "I fixed the mill." Tim and his mom were happy. They thanked Mr. Mill for making the mill pretty. The mill was not scary anymore. And they all became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a favorite cloth. The cloth was red and soft. He took the cloth everywhere he went. One day, Tim went to play at the park. He took his cloth with him. At the park, Tim saw a big tree. He wanted to climb it. He put his cloth on a branch and started to climb. Tim heard a snap. The branch with the cloth broke and fell. Tim was sad. A distant voice said, "Don't be sad, Tim!" It was a talking bird. The bird picked up the cloth and gave it back to Tim. Tim was happy and thanked the bird. They became friends and played at the park every day.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a big, black dog named Max. They loved to play together outside in the sun. One day, it was very hot, but Tim and Max still wanted to play. Tim and Max went to the park. They saw a big tree with lots of shade. Tim said, "Let's play under the tree, Max!" Max was happy and wagged his tail. They played catch with a red ball. Max would run and catch the ball, then bring it back to Tim. After a while, they were both tired. Tim said, "Max, let's rest." Tim kneeled down on the grass with Max. They sat under the tree, feeling the cool breeze. They were happy to be together, even on a hot day.

Once upon a time, there was a bright sun. It loved to paint the sky with pretty colors. One day, the sun saw a girl named Lily. She was doing yoga under a big tree. The sun said, "Hi Lily! I like your yoga. Can I paint you a picture?" Lily said, "Yes, please! I love pretty colors." The sun painted a beautiful picture of Lily doing yoga. Lily was so happy. She showed her friends the picture. They all loved it too. They all wanted to do yoga under the big tree. The moral of the story is that doing nice things for others can make everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Will. He loved to walk and play outside. One day, he went for a walk in the big park. The sun was shining, and Will was very happy. As he walked, Will saw a big, round rock. He didn't know it, but this rock was not just a rock. It was a magic rock! Will was ignorant about the rock's magic powers. He touched the rock, and something unexpected happened. Suddenly, Will could talk to animals! A little bird flew down and said, "Hello, Will!" Will was so surprised. He talked to the bird and made many new animal friends. From that day on, Will's walks in the park were even more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy car. His toy car was made of metal. One day, Tim met a new friend named Sam. "Hi, I'm Sam," said the new friend. "What are you playing with?" "I have a metal car," said Tim. "Do you want to play with me?" Sam said, "Yes, I want to play too!" As they played with the metal car, Tim saw a spicy pepper on the ground. He picked it up and said, "Look, Sam! I found a spicy pepper!" Sam looked at the pepper and said, "Be careful, Tim! Don't pinch the spicy pepper. It can make your fingers hurt." Tim listened to Sam and put the spicy pepper down. They went back to playing with the metal car and had a fun day together.
