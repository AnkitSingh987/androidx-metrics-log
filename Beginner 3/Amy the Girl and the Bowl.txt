Category: Beginner
Title: Amy the Girl and the Bowl

One day, a little girl named Amy wanted to make a snack. She went to the kitchen and saw a big bowl. Amy found some wet fruit in the fridge. She put the fruit in the bowl. Then, she started to stir the fruit with a spoon. Amy's little brother, Tim, came into the kitchen. He saw Amy stirring the fruit. "What are you doing?" Tim asked. "I am making a snack with wet fruit," Amy said. "Can I help?" Tim asked. "Yes, you can," Amy said with a smile. Together, they stirred the fruit in the big bowl. When the snack was ready, they put it on two plates. Amy and Tim sat down at the table and ate the wet fruit snack. They both liked it a lot. They were happy to share the yummy snack they made together.

Sara and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they find a big pink gum on the ground. Sara picks it up and says, "Look, Ben, gum! Can we chew it?" Ben shakes his head and says, "No, Sara, no. That gum is old and dirty. It can make us sick. We should throw it away." But Sara does not listen. She puts the gum in her mouth and chews it. It tastes bad and sticky. She makes a face and says, "Yuck, Ben, you are right. This gum is no good. I want to spit it out." She tries to spit out the gum, but it is stuck to her teeth and tongue. She pulls and pulls, but the gum does not come out. She starts to cry and says, "Help, Ben, help. The gum is hurting me. I can't talk or eat or smile." Ben feels sorry for Sara. He runs to the water fountain and fills a cup with water. He brings it to Sara and says, "Here, Sara, here. Drink some water. Maybe it will help you get rid of the gum." Sara drinks the water and tries to spit out the gum again. This time, some of the gum comes out, but not all of it. She still has some gum on her teeth and tongue. She looks pale and sad. She says, "Thank you, Ben, thank you. But I still have gum in my mouth. What can I do?" Ben thinks for a moment and says, "I have an idea, Sara, I have an idea. Maybe if I hug you, the gum will go away. Hugs make everything better. Do you want a hug?" Sara nods and says, "Yes, Ben, yes. I want a hug. Please hug me." Ben hugs Sara and says, "Don't worry, Sara, don't worry. I am your friend. I will help you. We will find a way to get rid of the gum." Sara hugs Ben back and says, "You are a good friend, Ben, a good friend. I am sorry I did not listen to you. I will never chew old gum again." They hug each other and smile. The gum does not go away, but they feel a little better. They go to find a grown-up who can help them. They hold hands and walk together. They are happy to have each other.

Once upon a time there was a little fox who went on an adventure. He was so excited and he wanted to explore the world. He was feeling so brave! One day the fox came across a peach tree. The fox was hungry and he wanted to eat the peaches from the tree. He grabbed a peach and started to take a bite. Just then, he heard a voice. It said, "Welcome! Please don't hurt me". The fox was so surprised and looked around to see who was speaking. He couldn't see anyone, just the peach tree. The fox realized that it was the peach tree speaking! The fox apologized to the peach tree and said he would never hurt it again. He respectfully put the peach back. He thanked the Peach Tree for its kindness and promised to never cause any harm. The little fox learned an important lesson that day. He understood that all living creatures are equal and should be respected. Respect and kindness will bring you much more than hurt and harm!
