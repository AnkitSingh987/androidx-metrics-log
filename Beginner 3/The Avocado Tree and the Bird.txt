Category: Beginner
Title: The Avocado Tree and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big avocado tree. In the tree, there was a generous avocado. It liked to give food to its friends. One day, the generous avocado saw a hungry bird. The bird was sad because it did not have any food. The avocado wanted to help the bird. It decided to part itself and give some of its yummy insides to the bird. The bird was very happy to eat the avocado. It sang a happy song and thanked the generous avocado. From that day on, the bird and the avocado were the best of friends, and they shared many happy days together. And the avocado tree grew more avocados to help other hungry friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was adventurous and loved to spin around in the park. One day, Lily saw a butterfly and decided to follow it. As she chased the butterfly, she met a little boy named Tim. Tim said, "Hi! I like to spin too! Can I spin with you?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, let's spin together!" So, Lily and Tim spun around and around, laughing and having fun. But then, Lily felt a pain in her foot. She stopped spinning and said, "Ouch! My foot hurts." Tim looked at her foot and saw a small rock. He carefully took it out and said, "There, all better!" Lily thanked Tim, and they continued to spin and play, having a great day in the park.

Tim and Sam are friends. They like to play with blocks. They make a big stack of blocks. The stack is taller than Tim and Sam. "Look at our stack!" Tim says. "It is so big and strong!" "Yes, it is!" Sam says. "We are good at building!" But then, a big dog comes. The dog is friendly, but he does not know about the stack. He runs to Tim and Sam. He wants to play with them. "Hi, dog!" Tim says. "You are cute and fluffy!" But the dog jumps on the stack. He tears the stack down. The blocks fall everywhere. "Oh no!" Sam says. "Our stack is gone!" Tim and Sam are sad. They worked hard on the stack. They look at the dog. The dog looks sorry. He wags his tail. "I'm sorry, dog," Tim says. "But you broke our stack. That was not nice." The dog licks Tim's face. He wants to say sorry. He wants to be friends. "Maybe we can build another stack," Sam says. "But this time, we can let the dog help us." Tim and Sam smile. They are not mad at the dog. They like the dog. They pick up the blocks. They start to build again. The dog helps them. He brings them blocks. He does not tear the stack down. They make a new stack. The stack is bigger and stronger than before. Tim, Sam, and the dog are happy. They are friends. They play together.

Once there was a little girl named Lily. She was only three years old and loved to play in her garden. One day, Lily was outside playing when her mom called her inside. Her mom told her that they were going to the city. Lily was excited! When they arrived in the city, Lily was amazed. She had never seen so many people before. Everywhere she looked people were walking around, talking and laughing. But suddenly the city was quiet. People had stopped talking and started to run away. Lily watched as her mom threw her arms up in the air and yelled, "It's a zombie!" Lily didn't know what a zombie was, so she asked her mom. Her mom told her it was a dead person that never sleeps. Lily was scared and started to cry. Her mom hugged her tight and said, "it's ok, we're going to be ok. We're going to skip out of here before the zombie gets us." And with that, Lily and her mom ran away and never looked back.

Once upon a time, there was a little white dog. The dog had a long tail. He liked to play with his tail. One day, he saw a big cat. The cat had a short tail. The white dog wanted to play with the cat's tail. The dog went to the cat and said, "Let's play!" The cat did not want to play. The dog did not listen. He tried to play with the cat's tail. The cat got mad. The cat said, "You will pay if you play with my tail!" The white dog did not listen. He played with the cat's tail again. The cat got very mad. The cat chased the white dog. The dog ran and ran. He was scared. But the cat was fast. The cat caught the white dog. The cat said, "I told you, you would pay!" The white dog was sad. He knew he should have listened. The cat did not want to be friends. The white dog learned that he should not play with other's tails without asking.
