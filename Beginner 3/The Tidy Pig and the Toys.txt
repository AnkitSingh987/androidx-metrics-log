Category: Beginner
Title: The Tidy Pig and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a tidy pig. The pig lived in a small, clean house. The pig liked to keep everything neat and nice. One day, the pig saw a big box outside. The pig was curious and wanted to see what was inside the box. As the pig opened the box, a lot of toys jumped out. They were all messy and dirty. The pig did not like messy things. The pig said, "I will let you stay in my house, but you must be tidy like me." The toys did not want to be tidy, but they wanted a place to stay. So, the toys tried to be tidy. They cleaned themselves and tried to be neat. The pig was happy and let them stay. But one day, when the pig was away, the toys played and made a big mess. When the pig came back, it saw the mess and was sad. The toys saw the sad pig and felt bad. They all worked together to clean up the mess. The pig was happy again, and they all lived neatly ever after.

Ben and Lily were playing in the bathroom. They saw a shiny thing on the sink. It was a razor. Ben wanted to touch it. "Look, Lily, a shiny thing!" Ben said. "No, Ben, don't touch it! It can hurt you!" Lily said. But Ben did not listen. He reached for the razor. He felt a sharp pain in his finger. He cried out loud. "Ow, ow, ow! It bit me!" Ben said. Lily ran to him. She saw blood on his finger. She was scared. She slapped the razor away. It fell on the floor. "Bad shiny thing! Go away!" Lily said. She hugged Ben. She tried to make him feel better. "I'm sorry, Ben. Are you okay?" Lily said. Ben nodded. He stopped crying. He looked at his finger. It had a small cut. "It's okay, Lily. It's just a little cut. It will heal soon." Ben said. Lily smiled. She was glad Ben was okay. She took him to the kitchen. She gave him a band-aid and a cookie. "Here, Ben. This will help your finger. And this is a tasty cookie. You can have it." Lily said. Ben smiled. He put the band-aid on his finger. He ate the cookie. It was tasty. He thanked Lily. "Thank you, Lily. You are a good sister. I love you." Ben said. "I love you too, Ben. You are a good brother. But next time, listen to me, okay?" Lily said. "Okay, Lily. I will listen to you. Next time, we will play with something else. Something that doesn't bite." Ben said. They hugged each other. They were happy. They learned a lesson. They never played with the razor again.

Once upon a time, there was a thin girl named Lily. She had a small garden where she planted a bulb. She wanted to protect the bulb so it would grow big and strong. One day, a naughty rabbit came to the garden. The rabbit saw the bulb and wanted to eat it. Lily saw the rabbit and said, "No, Mr. Rabbit! You cannot eat my bulb. I must protect it." The rabbit was sad but understood. Lily and the rabbit became friends. They worked together to protect the bulb and other plants in the garden. The bulb grew into a big, beautiful flower, and they all lived happily ever after.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play together at the park. One day, they see a big red bus. The bus is full of children. They are going on a visit. "Wow, look at the bus!" Lily says. "Where are they going?" "I don't know," Ben says. "Maybe to the zoo, or the museum, or the farm." Lily and Ben admire the bus. They wish they could go on a visit too. They are envious of the children on the bus. "Hey, do you want to play with us?" a girl on the bus asks. She opens the window and smiles at Lily and Ben. "Really?" Lily says. "Can we?" "Sure, why not?" the girl says. "We have some extra seats. Come on, hurry up!" Lily and Ben are happy. They run to the bus and climb on board. They thank the girl and find a seat. They wave goodbye to the park and look forward to their visit. "Where are we going?" Lily asks. "You'll see," the girl says. "It's a surprise. But I think you'll like it. It's very fun and exciting." Lily and Ben are curious. They wonder what the surprise is. They hold hands and smile. They are not envious anymore. They are glad they met the girl on the bus.
