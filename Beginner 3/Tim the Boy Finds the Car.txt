Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Car

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car. It was old and expensive. One day, Tim went to the park to play. He took his toy car with him. Tim played with the car on the grass. He was very happy. Then, Tim saw a big dog. The dog was playing with a ball. Tim wanted to play with the dog too. So, he put his toy car down and played with the dog. They ran and jumped. They had lots of fun. But, Tim forgot his toy car at the park. He went home with the dog. At home, Tim was sad because he lost his car. The next day, Tim went back to the park. He found his toy car! But it was not old and expensive anymore. It was shiny and new! A kind fairy saw Tim's car and made it new for him. Tim was very happy and played with his new car and the dog every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a big box of clothes. Mia loved to play dress-up. One day, she found a dress in the box that she had never seen before. She thought, "This dress must be from an unknown place." Mia put on the unknown dress and walked outside. She saw her friend Tom. Tom said, "Wow, Mia! That dress is so pretty!" Mia smiled and said, "Thank you, Tom! I found it in my clothes box." Mia and Tom played in the park all day. When it was time to go home, Mia looked at her dress. It was dirty. Mia sighed and said, "I need to wash my dress now." Tom said, "I will help you, Mia." They washed the dress together and it became clean again. Mia and Tom were happy and they played dress-up another day.

Once upon a time, there was a pale cat named Tim. Tim liked to bathe in the sun every day. One day, Tim met a new friend, a little dog named Sam. Sam asked Tim, "Do you want to play with me?" "Yes, I do!" said Tim, and they played together every day. They liked to play in the sun, and they were very happy. But one Tuesday, something unexpected happened. The sun was gone, and it was raining. Tim and Sam did not know what to do. They looked at each other and had an idea. They decided to bathe in the rain instead of the sun. They played and splashed in the puddles, and they were happy again. From that day on, Tim and Sam knew that they could have fun no matter what the weather was like.

Once upon a time, there was a big, mysterious bookcase. It was in a small house. A little girl named Sue lived there. Sue loved to read books from the bookcase. One day, she saw a new book on the bookcase. It had a picture of a big tree on it. Sue took the book and sat down. She started to read the book. The book was about a tree that could talk. The tree told Sue, "Hello, I have a secret." Sue asked, "What is the secret?" The tree said, "My leaves rot and turn into magic dust." Sue wanted to see the tree. She went outside and looked for it. She found the tree in her yard. The tree was real! Sue was happy. She played with the tree and the magic dust. The mysterious bookcase had brought the tree to life.

One day, a boy named Tom went for a trip to the park. He saw an enormous tree with many big leaves. Tom wanted to climb the tree and see how high he could go. He started to climb up, up, up. As Tom climbed, he saw a little bird in the tree. The bird said, "Be careful, Tom! Don't fall!" Tom smiled and said, "I will be safe, little bird." But as he climbed higher, he felt the tree shake. Suddenly, the enormous tree began to fall! Tom was scared, but the little bird had an idea. The bird called all its friends, and they flew together to catch Tom. They gently put him down on the ground. Tom was happy and thanked the birds for saving him. From that day on, Tom and the little bird were best friends.
