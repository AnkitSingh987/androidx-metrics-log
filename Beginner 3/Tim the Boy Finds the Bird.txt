Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Bird

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to travel. He told his mom, "I want to go far away." His mom said, "Okay, but be back for dinner." Tim was an intelligent boy, so he knew he could not go too far. Tim walked and walked until he found a big tree. He saw a little bird in the tree. The bird said, "I can help you travel." Tim was very happy. The bird took Tim up into the sky. They flew and flew. But then, something unexpected happened! The bird took Tim to a land full of candy. Tim said, "Wow! I did not know this place was here!" They played in the candy land until it was time for dinner. The bird took Tim back home, and they had a big dinner together.

One day, a boy found an envelope under his bed. He was very happy. He wanted to know what was inside. He opened the envelope to reveal a big surprise. Inside, there were many colorful stickers. The boy loved stickers. He smiled and clapped his hands. The boy went to his friend's house. He showed his friend the envelope with the stickers. His friend was excited too. They both played with the stickers, putting them on their toys and books. The boy was generous and shared his stickers with his friend. They both had lots of fun. At the end of the day, the boy and his friend were very tired. They had played with the stickers all day. The boy thanked his friend for playing with him. They hugged and said goodbye. The boy went home with his envelope, feeling very happy. He couldn't wait to find more surprises under his bed.

One day, a beautiful bird flew into the kitchen. It saw a pie on a table. The bird wanted to eat the pie. It flew down and tried to take a bite. But the pie was too big for the bird. The bird tried to break the pie into smaller pieces. It used its beak to peck at the pie. Just then, a little girl named Lily walked into the kitchen. She saw the bird and the pie. Lily said, "Bird, why are you breaking the pie?" The bird replied, "I want to eat it, but it is too big for me." Lily smiled and said, "Let me help you." She broke the pie into small pieces and shared it with the bird. They both enjoyed the pie together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. It was a wet day, so he put on his coat. He liked his coat because it was big and warm. Tim saw a puddle and jumped in it. His coat got wet, but he was having fun. Tim saw his friend, Sue. She was also wearing a coat. "Hi, Sue!" Tim said. "Do you want to play?" Sue looked at the puddle and smiled. "Yes, I want to play!" she answered. They both jumped in the puddle and laughed. Their coats were wet, but they didn't care. After playing, they went back home. Their mom saw their wet coats and said, "You two had fun, didn't you?" Tim and Sue both answered, "Yes, we did!" They took off their coats and hung them up to dry. They couldn't wait to play in the puddles again.

Once upon a time, there was a fast dog named Max. Max loved to run and play all day. One day, Max was in the park with his friends. They were all having fun, making lots of noise. Max's mom called out to them, "Max, please order your friends to be quiet." Max barked at his friends and they all stopped making noise. They sat down and listened to Max's mom. Max's mom said, "Thank you for being quiet. Now you can all have a treat." Max and his friends were happy. They ate their treats and played nicely for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the grass. One day, he saw an incredible bug on the grass. The bug was colorful and had big wings. Tim said to the bug, "Hi, bug! Do you want to play with me?" The bug nodded its head and they played together all day. They ran in the grass, played hide and seek, and had fun. As the sun began to set, the bug had to return to its family. Tim was sad, but he said, "Bye, bug! Come back soon!" The bug flew away, promising to return. Tim knew he had made a new friend and couldn't wait to play with the incredible bug again.
