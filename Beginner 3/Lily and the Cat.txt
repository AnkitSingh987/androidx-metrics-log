Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Cat

One day, it started to rain. A kind girl named Lily saw a cat outside. The cat was wet and cold. Lily wanted to help the cat, so she went to her room and got a big, soft sheet. Lily went back outside with the sheet. She put the sheet over the cat to keep it dry and warm. The cat was very happy and started to purr. Lily and the cat sat under the sheet together, watching the rain fall. When the rain stopped, Lily took the sheet off the cat. The cat was dry and warm now. The cat gave Lily a big, happy meow. Lily knew she had made a new friend. She waved goodbye to the cat as it walked away, and she went back inside her house.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a wish to rescue a cat. One day, he went for a walk and saw a cat stuck in a tree. The cat was wet and wanted to be dry. Tim said, "Don't worry, cat! I will rescue you!" He found a long stick and used it to help the cat come down from the tree. The cat was so happy that it purred and rubbed against Tim's leg. Tim took the cat home and gave it a warm, dry towel. They became best friends, and Tim's wish came true. They played together every day, and the cat was always grateful for Tim's help.

Lily liked to polish her horse. She had a soft cloth and a shiny bottle. She rubbed the cloth on the horse's fur and made it smooth and bright. She also brushed the horse's mane and tail and gave it a carrot. One day, Lily wanted to polish her horse, but it was difficult. The horse was in the barn and it did not want to come out. It made a loud noise and kicked the door. Lily was scared and ran to her mom. "Mom, mom, the horse is angry!" Lily cried. "I want to polish it, but it won't let me!" Mom hugged Lily and said, "Don't worry, sweetie. The horse is not angry, it is just cold. It likes to stay in the barn when it is cold. Why don't we wait until it is warmer and then we can polish it together?" Lily nodded and said, "Okay, mom. Can I give it a carrot now?" Mom smiled and said, "Sure, you can. But be careful and gentle. The horse will like that." Lily and mom went to the barn and Lily gave the horse a carrot. The horse ate it and nuzzled Lily's hand. Lily felt happy and said, "I love you, horse. You are so soft and shiny. I will polish you later, when it is not cold."

Once there was a white bear. He was very happy and loved to play in the meadow. One day, he heard a strange sound. He turned and saw a waffle in the grass. The bear was excited and said “Welcome” to the waffle. The waffle said hello back. The bear and the waffle became very good friends. They spent all day playing together in the meadow and laughing. They enjoyed doing many things such as running on the grass and collecting flowers. At the end of the day, the bear said goodbye to the waffle. The bear gave the waffle a big hug and said “Thank you for being my friend.” The waffle smiled and said “You are welcome. I had a lot of fun too.” The bear waved goodbye and the waffle left the meadow.

Anna loved music. She liked to sing and dance and play with her toy piano. She wanted to be a musician when she grew up. She was very excited when her mom told her they were going to see a real musician at the park. They walked to the park and saw a man with a guitar. He was sitting on a bench and playing songs. Anna liked his songs. They were happy and fun. She clapped her hands and smiled. She wanted to talk to him. She went to the bench and said, "Hello, I like your music. You are a good musician. Can I try your guitar?" The man smiled and said, "Hello, thank you. You are very kind. Sure, you can try my guitar. But be careful, it is very special to me." He gave her the guitar and showed her how to hold it. He said, "This is how you make music. You rub the strings with your fingers. Like this." He rubbed the strings and made a sound. Anna tried to rub the strings too. She rubbed them softly and then harder. She made different sounds. She liked the sounds. She said, "This is fun. I like your guitar. Can I keep it?" The man laughed and said, "No, you can't keep it. It is mine. But you can play with it for a little while. Maybe you can be a musician too someday. You have a good ear for music." Anna was happy. She played with the guitar and sang along with the man. They made music together. They had a good time. Anna's mom watched them and smiled. She was proud of her daughter. She thought she had a gift for music.
