Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and the Car

Ben and his dad were going to the park in their car. Ben liked the car. It was red and shiny and made a loud noise. But on the way, the car stopped. Dad looked at the car and said, "Oh no, the car is broken. We need a mechanic." A mechanic is a person who can fix cars. Dad called a mechanic on his phone and asked him to come. Ben and his dad waited for the mechanic. It was a hot day and the sun was bright. Ben felt hot and thirsty. He had a popsicle in his hand. It was red and sweet and cold. But the popsicle started to melt. It dripped on Ben's hand and his shirt. Ben said, "Dad, my popsicle is melting. Can I have another one?" Dad said, "I'm sorry, Ben, but we don't have any more popsicles. We have to wait for the mechanic. He will be here soon. He is very busy. He has to fix many cars. Maybe he will have some popsicles in his truck. You can ask him when he comes." Ben nodded. He licked his popsicle as fast as he could. He hoped the mechanic would come soon and have some popsicles. He also hoped the mechanic would fix the car so they could go to the park. He wanted to play on the swings and the slide. He liked the park. It was green and fun and had many birds and flowers. He looked at the sky and saw some clouds. They were white and fluffy and looked like animals. He saw a cloud that looked like a dog. He pointed at it and said, "Look, Dad, a dog cloud!" Dad smiled and said, "Yes, Ben, a dog cloud. That's very nice. Maybe the mechanic will come soon and we can go see some real dogs at the park. Would you like that?" Ben said, "Yes, Dad, I would like that. I like dogs. They are furry and friendly and make a happy noise. Woof, woof!" Dad said, "Woof, woof, Ben. You are a good boy. I love you." Ben said, "I love you too, Dad. And I love the car. And the popsicle. And the mechanic. And the park. And the dog cloud." They hugged and waited for the mechanic.

One sunny day, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. They went to a big tree with lots of harmless bugs. Lily liked to watch the bugs play in the grass. "Mom, can I have a strawberry?" asked Lily. Her mom gave her a big, red strawberry. Lily sat down and ate her strawberry. She was very happy. Suddenly, a little bird flew down and took Lily's strawberry away! Lily was sad, but then she saw the bird share the strawberry with its baby birds. Lily smiled and said, "It's okay, Mom. The baby birds need the strawberry more than I do."

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big door with a knob. Tim wanted to see what was behind the door. He put his hand on the knob and gave it a shake. The door opened. Behind the door, Tim found a big, harsh dog. The dog was loud and scary. Tim was scared, but he knew he had to be brave. He took a deep breath and said, "Hello, dog." The dog stopped barking and looked at Tim. Tim reached out his hand and gave the dog a gentle shake. The dog was not harsh anymore. It was now a happy and friendly dog. Tim and the dog became best friends and played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to touch things. One day, he saw a big red button on a wall. He felt confused because he did not know what the button did. Tim's friend, Sam, was with him. Sam said, "Don't touch the button, Tim." But Tim did not listen. He touched the button with his shoulder. The floor started to shake, and they both fell down. The floor opened, and they fell into a big hole. They could not get out. They were very sad. The moral of the story is to listen to your friends and not touch things you do not know about.
