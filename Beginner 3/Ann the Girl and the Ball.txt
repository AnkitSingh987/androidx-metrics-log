Category: Beginner
Title: Ann the Girl and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Ann. Ann loved to make dough with her mom. One day, Ann wanted to make a big, heavy ball of dough all by herself. She mixed and mixed, and the dough got bigger and bigger. Ann saw a sign that said, "Do not make a heavy dough ball." But Ann did not listen. She kept mixing and mixing. The dough ball was so big and heavy that it was hard for her to hold. Ann's mom saw her and helped her with the heavy dough ball. Together, they made it smaller and easier to hold. Ann learned that it is important to follow signs and listen to what they say.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park with her mom. Sue brought her favorite toy, a small red ball. It was a cold day, so Sue wore a warm coat and a hat. At the park, Sue played with her ball and had lots of fun. "Mom, look at me!" Sue said as she threw the ball high in the air. Her mom smiled and clapped her hands. Sue was so happy to play with her toy. But then, Sue saw a big, shiny slide. She wanted to go down the slide, so she ran to it and climbed up. Sue had so much fun on the slide that she forgot about her toy ball. When it was time to go home, Sue and her mom left the park. At home, Sue felt sad. She could not find her toy ball. "Mom, where is my ball?" Sue asked. Her mom thought for a moment and said, "Oh no, we forgot it at the park!" The next day, Sue and her mom went back to the park to look for the ball. When they got there, they saw a little boy playing with the red ball. Sue felt surprised and a little upset. But the boy saw Sue and said, "Is this your ball? I found it here. Let's play together!" Sue smiled and played with the boy. They shared the toy ball and had lots of fun. From that day on, Sue and the boy became good friends, and they always remembered to share their toys.

Once upon a time, there were two friends named Jill and John. They were playing together in the park. Jill found a board and grabbed it. She was so excited because it was the perfect size for a game that she wanted to play. John was a bit jealous and said, "Can I grab it too?" Jill said, "No, John. This board is for me." She was starting to feel embarrassed because she didn't want to make John sad. John felt a bit sad, but he understood. He said, "That's okay. We can play together." Jill smiled. She said, "Yes, let's play together! We can both grab this board and have lots of fun!" They grabbed the board and ran around the park together. They had a great time and weren't embarrassed anymore.

Once upon a time there was a boy. He was walking in the woods and saw something on the ground. It looked like a long skinny thing. He was scared so he shouted for help. Suddenly, a snake appeared! It was cold and slimy. The boy was so scared that he was frozen in fear. "Don't worry," said the snake. "I won't hurt you. I'm just looking for someone." The boy was still scared but he asked the snake, "Who are you looking for?" The snake replied, "I'm looking for my friend. The last time I saw him, he was wearing a blue hat." The boy shouted, "I don't see anyone!" The snake said, "Can you show me around? Maybe I'll find my friend." The boy, although he was still scared, agreed to help. He and the snake looked around for a while but the snake's friend was nowhere to be found. Eventually, the snake thanked the boy for his help and vanished into the woods. The boy was relieved and incredibly happy that the snake had gone. He ran all the way home to safety.

Once upon a time, in a warm season, a little bunny named Ben lived in a big forest. Ben loved to spring and hop all day long. He was an independent bunny, and he liked to play by himself. One day, while Ben was hopping, he saw a big, shiny rock. He thought it was a new friend to play with. So, he hopped closer to it. But, when he touched the rock, it started to move! Ben was very surprised. The rock was not a rock at all! It was a big, friendly turtle named Tim. Tim said he had been sleeping and Ben woke him up. Now, Ben had a new friend to play with. They both hopped and played all day long, and they were very happy. And from that day on, Ben and Tim were the best of friends.
