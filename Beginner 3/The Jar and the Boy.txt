Category: Beginner
Title: The Jar and the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a big jar. The jar was dark and heavy. It was in a room with a small bed. The bed was for rest. One day, a little boy found the jar. He was very tired. He wanted to rest on the bed. But first, he wanted to open the jar. He thought there was something fun inside. The little boy opened the jar. But it was not fun. Out came many bugs. The bugs were not nice. They did not let the little boy rest. The boy was sad and the room was not good anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys. One day, Tim saw a sign outside. The sign had a picture of a big ice cream cone. Tim wanted to eat the ice cream. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, can I have ice cream?" His mom said, "Yes, but only if you are good. Do not spoil your dinner." Tim was happy and played with his toys. But, he could not stop thinking about the ice cream. Later, Tim's sister, Sue, came home. Sue had a bitter face. She was mad because she did not get ice cream. Tim and Sue started to fight. Their mom heard them and said, "No ice cream for anyone who fights." Tim and Sue stopped fighting and said sorry to each other. They both got ice cream and were happy.

Tom likes to jog with his dad. They jog in the park, where there are many trees and flowers. Tom likes to see the birds and the squirrels. He also likes to count the signs that tell them where to go. One day, they jog past a big sign that says "Toy Store". Tom sees many toys in the window. He sees cars, dolls, balls, and puzzles. He wants to go inside and play with them. He asks his dad if they can stop and look. His dad says no, they have to keep jogging. He says the toys are expensive, and they have enough toys at home. He says they are jogging to be healthy and strong. He says they can play with their own toys later. Tom feels sad. He really likes the toys in the window. He thinks they are more fun than his toys at home. He thinks his dad is mean. He stops jogging and sits on the ground. He cries and says he wants a toy. His dad stops and kneels down. He hugs Tom and says he is sorry. He says he loves him and he is not mean. He says he understands that he likes the toys, but they cannot buy them. He says they can jog to the library instead, and borrow some books. He says books are also fun, and they are not expensive. He says they can read them together and learn new things. Tom wipes his tears and nods. He likes books too. He likes to look at the pictures and hear the stories. He thinks his dad is smart and kind. He gets up and holds his dad's hand. They jog to the library, where there are many books and signs. Tom feels happy again.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a purple whistle. He was very happy. He ran to his friend, Sue, to show her the whistle. "Look, Sue! I found a purple whistle!" Tim said. Sue liked the whistle too. They played with the whistle together. As they played, a big bird came down from the sky. The bird heard the whistle and wanted to go with Tim and Sue. The bird said, "Can I go with you and play with the whistle?" Tim and Sue were surprised but said yes. They all played together. But then, something unexpected happened. The bird blew the whistle very hard, and it made a loud sound. Tim and Sue were scared. The bird said, "I'm sorry, I didn't mean to scare you." Tim and Sue forgave the bird, and they all continued to play together.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to play hockey with his friends. He was very excited. He put on his shoes and went outside. The sun was shining bright and it was a great day for playing. Tim saw his friend, Sam, near the park. Sam looked angry. Tim asked, "Why are you angry, Sam?" Sam said, "I think I lost my hockey stick. I can't play without it." Tim wanted to help Sam find his hockey stick so they could play together. They looked everywhere for the hockey stick. They looked under the slide, behind the trees, and near the swings. Finally, they found the hockey stick near a big rock. Sam was happy and not angry anymore. They played hockey together and had a lot of fun.
