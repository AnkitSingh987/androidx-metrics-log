Category: Beginner
Title: Danny and the Park

Danny and his mom were in the park. It was a sunny day and Danny wanted to have fun. His mom showed him a flea that was jumping on a leaf. Danny was amazed. He watched it jump from one leaf to another. "That's amazing, mom!" said Danny. "Yes, isn't it?" said his mom. She then showed Danny how to catch the flea. She said it was easy. Danny tried to catch it but he couldn't. He tried again and again, but he just couldn't get it. His mom smiled, then she showed him how to do it. She showed him how to be patient and how to be gentle and soon Danny was able to catch the flea. He was so happy! "I did it, mom!" shouted Danny. "Congratulations," said his mom, "you did it! Now let's let the flea go and find some more fun things to do!" And then they went off to have more fun in the park.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to find hidden things. One day, she found a big book with a lot of numbers in it. She wanted to own the book, so she took it home. Amy showed the book to her mom and dad. They didn't know where the book came from, but they let Amy keep it. Amy was happy and started to learn the numbers. She counted one, two, three, and so on. One day, when Amy was counting the numbers, something unexpected happened. The numbers jumped out of the book and started to run around the house. The numbers made a big mess and Amy's mom and dad were not happy. They told Amy to put the numbers back in the book, but she didn't know how. In the end, Amy had to say goodbye to the book and the numbers. She was sad, but she learned that some hidden things are better left alone.

Once upon a time, there was a peaceful beach. On this beach, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to play with shells. One day, she found a big, shiny shell. Amy picked up the shell and said, "Wow! This shell is so shiny. I want to show it to my friend, Tom." So, Amy walked to Tom's house. Tom was playing in his yard. Amy showed the shiny shell to Tom. Tom said, "That shell is very pretty, Amy. Let's go to the beach and find more shells!" They both went to the beach and found many shiny shells. They played in the sun until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a restless lion named Leo. He loved to run and play all day long. Leo had many friends, like birds, rabbits, and monkeys. They all played together and had fun. One day, while playing, Leo found a pepper plant. He didn't know what it was, so he asked his friend, the wise owl. "What is this?" Leo asked. The owl said, "That is a pepper plant. Be careful, it is very hot!" Leo was curious and wanted to try it. Leo took a bite of the pepper and suddenly, he let out a loud roar! It was so hot that he could not stop roaring. All his friends heard the roar and came to see what was wrong. The wise owl told them, "Leo didn't listen and tried the hot pepper." All his friends helped him by bringing water to cool his mouth. After that day, Leo learned to listen to his wise friends and not to try things without asking first. The moral of the story is to always listen to the advice of those who care for you.

One day, a frog and a dog were playing. The frog was fit and could jump very high. The dog was big and strong. They were good friends. The frog said to the dog, "I can jump higher than you!" The dog did not accept this. He said, "No, I can jump higher!" They had a big fight. They jumped and jumped to see who was the best. At last, they were very tired. They sat down and said sorry to each other. The frog said, "I am sorry, dog. We can both jump high." The dog said, "Yes, frog. We are both good at jumping." They were friends again and played together.
