Category: Beginner
Title: The Red Ball and the Grass

Once upon a time, there was a little red ball. It liked to roll around in the deep grass. One day, the ball got lost in the deep grass. It wanted to find its way home. The ball saw a small cord. The ball decided to follow the cord. The cord led the ball to a big tree. The ball decided to peek behind the tree. When the ball peeked, it found a big box. Inside the box, the ball found all its friends. They were so happy to see the ball. The ball and its friends played in the deep grass all day.

Once upon a time, there was a deaf boy called Tom. Tom had a rug, but he couldn't fit it in his bedroom. He asked his mum and dad why it would not fit and they said: "The rug is too big." Tom was sad. He wanted to make his rug fit, so he smiled at his mum and dad and said: "I can make it fit." Together, Tom and his parents worked hard to make the rug fit into Tom's bedroom. They cut it in two pieces, so it could fit nicely into the corner. Once the rug was in place, Tom and his parents felt proud. Tom's mum said: "It's important to never give up. If we work together, we can make everything fit!" Tom and his parents smiled and hugged each other. From then on, Tom never gave up and always found a way to fit the pieces of his life together. The moral of the story is: You can do anything if you never give up.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the mall with his mom. Tim liked the mall because it was big and had many good toys. He wanted to find a good toy to play with his friends. While they were at the mall, Tim's mom told him to be good and stay close to her. Tim listened and held his mom's hand. They walked around and saw many good things. Tim saw a big ball and a small car. He liked them both. At the end of the day, Tim's mom let him pick one good toy. Tim was very happy and told his mom that he wanted the small car. They bought the car and went home. Tim played with his new car and had a lot of fun with his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his blocks. Every day, he would look at his blocks and build big towers. One sunny day, Tim was playing with his blocks outside. He was building a very big tower. Suddenly, a little bird flew down and sat on top of the tower. Tim was very surprised! He did not know that birds liked to play with blocks too. Tim and the little bird became good friends. They played with the blocks together and built the biggest tower ever. But then, something unexpected happened. The little bird flew up and pushed the tower with its beak. The tower fell down and all the blocks went everywhere. Tim was sad, but then he saw the little bird was laughing. Tim realized it was just a fun game for the bird. So, Tim laughed too and they played together all day long.

One day, a boy named Tim found a toy gun in his room. He felt confused because he did not know where it came from. He picked it up and looked at it. It was a big, red gun with a long handle. Tim went to his mom and asked, "Mom, where did this gun come from?" His mom looked at the gun and said, "Oh, that is your old toy gun. You used to play with it when you were younger." Tim decided to play with the gun outside. He pretended to destroy big rocks and trees with it. He made loud noises like "Bang! Bang!" as he played. He had a lot of fun with his toy gun. As the sun began to set, Tim knew it was time to go inside. He took his toy gun with him and showed it to his little sister. She was excited to see it too. They both played with the gun and had a great time. Tim was happy that he found the toy gun and could share the fun with his sister.
