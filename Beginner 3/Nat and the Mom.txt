Category: Beginner
Title: Nat and the Mom

Nat was playing outside when he saw a shirt lying on the ground. He picked it up and he saw that it was really pretty. He thought it would be fun to wear it so he put it on. When his mom saw him, she became very mad. Her face was harsh and she shouted at him. She said he must never do that again and that he had to take it off. Nat got scared and quickly took it off, but his mom was still angry. She said she was going to keep the shirt and it would stay in her closet forever. Nat was sad and felt bad for not listening. He never saw the shirt again and his mom never let him play outside again. He was sad that his mom was so harsh and that his day had ended so badly.

One day, a little crab was playing near the sea. He was very glad because it was sunny and warm. The little crab loved to play in the sand and listen to the waves. As the little crab played, he heard a sad sound. He looked around and saw a small fish stuck in a net. The fish was scared and wanted to go back to the sea. The little crab wanted to help the fish, so he used his claws to open the net. The fish was free and very happy. The fish said "thank you" to the little crab. They both played together in the sea and became good friends. The little crab was glad he could help his new friend. The moral of the story is to always help others when they are in need. Be kind and make new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a bright sun in the sky. It shined on a big field with many potatoes. The potatoes were happy and grew big and round. One day, a little bug came to the field. It saw the big potatoes and wanted to eat them. The bug pinched a potato with its tiny feet. The potato felt a little hurt, but it was okay. The bright sun kept shining, and the potatoes grew even bigger. The little bug pinched other potatoes too, but it could not eat them all. In the end, the potatoes stayed big and round, and the little bug found other things to eat. The potatoes and the bug lived happily in the field under the bright sun.

Molly and her mom were going to the post office. "What are we going to the post office for, Mommy?" asked Molly. "We're going to mail a package to Grandma," answered her Mom. When they got to the post office, Molly saw something really tasty. "Mommy, what are those?" asked Molly. "Those are shrimp," her mom said. "They look so tasty!" Molly said as she got closer. Molly's mom laughed and said, "They do look tasty, but we can't eat them here. We have to mail them to Grandma!" Molly was amazed that you could actually mail food. She helped her mom put them in a package to mail them to Grandma. Molly and her mom waved goodbye to the shrimp as they put them in the mail. "Bye bye tasty shrimp! Grandma is going to love them!" Molly said.

Anna and Ben are twins. They live in a big city with many cars and people. They like to play in the park with their ball and their dog, Spot. One day, they see a big truck with a hose and a brush. It is a truck that washes other cars. "Look, Ben, a wash truck!" Anna says. "Can we go and see?" "OK, but be careful," Ben says. "The hose is very strong. It can hurt you." They run to the truck and watch how it sprays water and soap on a dirty car. The car becomes shiny and clean. Anna and Ben clap their hands and laugh. Spot barks and wags his tail. "Can you wash our dog, too?" Anna asks the man who drives the truck. The man smiles and says, "Sure, why not? He looks like he needs a bath. Come here, Spot." He lowers the hose and the brush and calls Spot. Spot runs to the man, thinking he will get a treat. But when the man turns on the hose, Spot gets a big shock. The water is cold and hard. It hits Spot on his head and his back. Spot yelps and runs away. He is wet and scared. "Spot, come back!" Anna and Ben shout. They chase after Spot, but he is too fast. He runs across the street, dodging cars and people. He is looking for a place to hide. Anna and Ben follow him, but they are not careful. They do not look where they are going. They do not see the red light or the big bus. The bus honks and brakes, but it is too late. It hits Anna and Ben with a loud crash. They fall to the ground. They are hurt and bleeding. The man with the wash truck sees what happens. He drops the hose and the brush and runs to help. He calls for an ambulance and a police. He is sorry and sad. He wishes he had not washed Spot. Spot hears the sirens and the noise. He comes out of his hiding place. He sees Anna and Ben lying on the ground. He sees the wash truck and the man. He feels angry and afraid. He barks and growls. He bites the man on his leg. The man screams and kicks Spot. Spot runs away again. He is hurt and lonely. The end.
