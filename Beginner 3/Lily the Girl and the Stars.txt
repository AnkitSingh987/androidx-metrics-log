Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Stars

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a doll that she loved very much. They played together every day. Lily was very happy when she played with her doll. One day, Lily heard about a big show where people would clap and cheer. Lily had an idea. She wanted to be in the big show with her doll. She practiced a dance with her doll. They danced and danced. Lily was excited about the big show. She knew people would clap and be happy. The day of the big show came. Lily and her doll danced on the stage. They danced their best dance. When they finished, everyone clapped and cheered. Lily and her doll were so happy. They knew their hard work had paid off. And from that day on, Lily and her doll were the stars of the show.

---

Once upon a time, there was a kind nurse named Mary. She worked in a big hospital and took care of many sick people. Mary loved to help others and make them feel better. One day, a little girl named Sue came to the hospital. Sue was very sick and needed help. Mary saw that Sue was scared and didn't know what to do. Mary knew that Sue was ignorant, but she wanted to help her. So, Mary told Sue, "Don't worry, I will take care of you." Mary wanted to make Sue feel better. She knew that Sue loved to draw, so she asked her friend to lend her some crayons and paper. Mary gave the crayons and paper to Sue and said, "Here, you can draw while I take care of you." Sue was very happy and started to draw. As the days went by, Sue started to feel better. She and Mary became good friends. Sue learned many things from Mary and was not ignorant anymore. In the end, Sue went home all better, and she was very thankful to Mary for helping her.

---

One day, a boy named Tim got a scooter. He was very happy. He rode the scooter all day. Tim was an honest boy. He always told the truth. One day, Tim saw a girl crying. The girl said, "I lost my toy." Tim wanted to help. He thought his scooter could provide help. He rode his scooter to look for the toy. He looked everywhere. Then, Tim saw a dog. The dog had the toy in its mouth. Tim was scared, but he wanted to help the girl. He said, "Please, dog, give the toy back." The dog looked at Tim and gave the toy back. The girl was very happy. Tim felt good because he was honest and helpful.

---

Lily and Ben are friends. They go to the same school. They like to play and learn together. Today, they have an exam. The exam is about animals. Lily and Ben love animals. They know many things about them. Before the exam, they discuss. They talk about what they remember. They help each other. They say the names of the animals, and what they eat, and where they live. The exam is fast. Lily and Ben finish quickly. They are happy. They think they did well. They smile at each other. After the exam, they play. They pretend to be animals. They run, and jump, and roar, and squeak. They have fun. They are good friends.

---

Lily was restless. She did not want to sleep. She wanted to play with her toys and watch TV. But Mom said it was bedtime and she had to go to her room. "No, Mom, no!" Lily cried. "I want to stay up!" Mom sighed. She was tired. She had a long day at work. She wanted to relax and eat some pizza. She had ordered it from the phone and it smelled good. "Lily, you need to sleep. You have school tomorrow. And you can have some pizza for breakfast if you are good. Now, please, go to your room and be quiet." Lily pouted. She did not like school. She did not like her teacher. She did not like her classmates. They were mean to her. They called her names and took her crayons. She suffered a lot at school. She went to her room, but she did not sleep. She turned on her lamp and looked at her books. She wished she had a friend. Someone who would play with her and share her pizza. Someone who would make her happy. She heard a knock on the door. It was Dad. He had come home late from work. He smiled and opened the door. "Hi, Lily. How are you?" he asked. Lily ran to him and hugged him. She loved Dad. He was nice to her. He always listened to her and made her laugh. "Dad, I'm restless. I can't sleep. I don't like school. I don't have any friends. I'm sad," she said. Dad hugged her back. He felt sorry for her. He knew she had a hard time at school. He wanted to help her. "I'm sorry, Lily. I know school is hard. But you are a smart and brave girl. You can make friends. You just have to be kind and friendly. And you can talk to me or Mom anytime you feel sad. We love you very much." He kissed her forehead and tucked her in. He turned off the lamp and left the door open. "Good night, Lily. Sleep well. And don't worry. Tomorrow will be a better day. And you can have some pizza for breakfast. How does that sound?" Lily smiled. She felt a little better. She liked pizza. She liked Dad. She hoped he was right. She closed her eyes and tried to sleep. She dreamed of pizza and friends.
