Category: Beginner
Title: Copper the Great Dog and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a great big dog named Copper. Copper liked to play in the park with his friends. He would run, jump, and roll in the grass. One day, Copper saw a little bird in a tree. The bird was singing a pretty song. Copper thought it would be fun to play with the bird, so he barked, "Hey bird, do you want to play with me?" The little bird stopped singing and said, "Please don't disturb me, Copper. I am trying to sing my song." Copper felt sad that he had disturbed the bird. He said, "I'm sorry, little bird. I didn't mean to disturb you. I just wanted to play." The little bird smiled and said, "That's okay, Copper. I will sing my song, and then we can play together." Copper sat down and listened to the bird's song. After the song, they played together and had a great time.

Once upon a time, there was a little crab. The crab had a big, ugly shell. The crab lived near the sea. The crab loved to play in the sand and water. One day, the crab saw a big rock. The crab wanted to peek behind the rock. He walked slowly to the rock and peeked. Behind the rock, he found a new friend. It was a small fish. The crab and the fish played together. They had lots of fun in the water. The crab was happy that he peeked behind the rock. The ugly shell did not matter, because he had a new friend. They were friends forever.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a little deer. The deer had a horn on his head. He was very eager to play with his friends. Every day, he would run and jump with them. One day, the little deer and his friends were playing a game. The game was called "don't quit." They had to keep running and not stop. The deer with the horn was very good at this game. He ran and ran, and he did not quit. But then, the little deer heard a loud noise. It was a big, scary animal. The deer and his friends were scared. They all quit the game and ran away. The little deer with the horn was very fast. He ran to his mom, and she hugged him tight. They were safe, and they went home to rest.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went for a walk. They found a stream with water that was not moving. Tim said, "Look, Spot! The stream is dead." A big, wise tree heard Tim and said, "The stream is not dead. It is just sleepy. If you sing a song, the stream will wake up." Tim thought it was a fun idea, so he sang a song. Spot barked and wagged his tail. As Tim sang, the stream started to move. It was not dead after all! The tree was right. Tim and Spot laughed and played by the stream. They had a great day. The tree nodded and smiled, happy to see them have so much fun.

Once upon a time, there was a curious little dog. He loved to play and run around the park. One day, he saw a patch of soft grass. The little dog wanted to test the grass. He ran and jumped on the patch. It felt nice and soft under his paws. He rolled around and had so much fun. His friends, a cat and a bird, came to see what he was doing. They were curious too. They all played on the patch together, laughing and having a great time. And they all became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big dinosaur. He was very bossy. He liked to tell all the other animals what to do. They did not like it, but they were scared of the big dinosaur. One day, a little bird flew to the dinosaur. The bird was not scared. The bird said, "I will give you a gift if you stop being bossy." The dinosaur thought about it and said, "Okay, I will try." The little bird gave the dinosaur a pretty flower. The dinosaur liked the flower. He started to be nice to the other animals. They all became friends and played together. The big dinosaur was not bossy anymore, and everyone was happy.
