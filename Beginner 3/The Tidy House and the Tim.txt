Category: Beginner
Title: The Tidy House and the Tim

Once upon a time, there was a tidy little house. In the house lived a boy named Tim and his mom. One day, Tim's mom made a sandwich for lunch. She told Tim, "Here is your sandwich. Be careful not to drop it." Tim took the sandwich and went to play outside. He saw his friend Sam. Sam had a hurt knee. Tim wanted to help Sam heal. He remembered his mom saying that food can make you feel better. So, he gave Sam his sandwich. Sam ate the sandwich and felt better. His knee started to heal. Sam said, "Thank you, Tim! You helped me feel better with your sandwich." Tim was happy that he could help his friend. And from that day on, they always shared their sandwiches with each other.

Once upon a time, there was a new fountain in the park. The fountain was big and pretty. It had water that went up and down. Many kids came to see it every day. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the park with his mom. He saw the fountain and wanted to play near it. He saw a big rock next to the fountain. Tim decided to sit on the rock and watch the water. But the rock was not safe. It was wet and slippery. When Tim sat on the rock, he fell into the fountain. He got all wet and cold. Tim's mom was sad, and they went home. The new fountain was not fun for Tim anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom had a toy car. The car had a long cord. Tom loved to play with the car every day. One day, Tom's friend, Sam, came to play. Sam saw Tom's toy car and said, "Wow, that's a cool car! Can I play with it too?" Tom said, "Yes, but be careful. The cord is fragile. We don't want to break it." Tom and Sam played with the car all day. They were very happy. Then, Tom's mom came in with a box. She said, "Tom, you received a gift from your grandma!" Tom and Sam opened the box together. Inside was a new toy car with a strong cord. They played with both cars and had lots of fun.

Anna and Ben were twins who liked to play pretend. One day, they decided to play restaurant. Anna put on a suit and said, "I am the boss. I will take the orders and cook the food." Ben put on an apron and said, "I am the helper. I will set the table and serve the food." They went to the kitchen and found some bread, cheese, ham, and fruit. Anna said, "This is what we will cook. We will make sandwiches and fruit salad. They are delicious." Ben said, "OK, let's cook." They used a knife to cut the bread and cheese, and a bowl to mix the fruit. They put the sandwiches and fruit salad on plates and carried them to the living room. They pretended that their teddy bears and dolls were the customers. Anna said, "Hello, welcome to our restaurant. What would you like to eat?" She took their orders and gave them to Ben. Ben said, "Coming right up." He brought the plates to the table and said, "Enjoy your meal." The teddy bears and dolls ate the sandwiches and fruit salad. They said, "Mmm, this is delicious. You are very good cooks." Anna and Ben smiled and said, "Thank you. We are happy you like our food." But then, they heard a loud noise. It was their mom, who came home from work. She saw the mess in the kitchen and the living room. She said, "What is going on here? Who made this mess?" Anna and Ben looked at each other and said, "We did. We were playing restaurant. We cooked sandwiches and fruit salad. They were delicious." Their mom said, "I see. Well, I'm glad you had fun, but you also have to clean up after yourselves. Cooking is fun, but it also comes with responsibility. You have to wash the dishes, wipe the table, and put away the food. And you have to be careful with the knife and the suit. They are not toys. They can be dangerous or dirty." Anna and Ben said, "We're sorry, mom. We didn't mean to make a mess. We will clean up right away. And we will be careful with the knife and the suit. We learned our lesson." They hugged their mom and said, "We love you, mom. You are the best cook in the world." Their mom said, "I love you too, Anna and Ben. You are the best kids in the world. And you are very creative and imaginative. Maybe someday, you will have your own restaurant. But for now, let's clean up and then we can have some cookies and milk." The end.
