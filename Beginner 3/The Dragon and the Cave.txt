Category: Beginner
Title: The Dragon and the Cave

Once upon a time, there was a big dragon. The dragon lived in a deep cave. The dragon was not mean, but sometimes he did bad things. One day, the dragon took a toy from a little boy. The little boy was very sad. He wanted his toy back. The dragon saw the boy was sad and felt bad too. The dragon wanted to say sorry to the boy. The dragon went back to the boy and gave him his toy. The dragon said, "I am sorry. I should not have taken your toy." The little boy was happy to have his toy back. The boy forgave the dragon. They became good friends and played together every day.

Tom and Lily were best friends. They liked to play in the park every day. They liked to slide, swing, and run. They were very fast and happy. One day, they saw a big dog in the park. The dog was barking and running after a ball. Tom and Lily were scared of the dog. They ran away from the dog and hid behind a tree. "Tom, I am worried. What if the dog finds us and bites us?" Lily said. "Don't worry, Lily. We are safe here. The dog is not bad. He just wants to play with his ball. He will not hurt us." Tom said. "How do you know that, Tom?" Lily asked. "Because my dad told me. He has a lot of wisdom. He knows a lot of things. He said dogs are our friends, not our enemies. We just have to be nice to them and they will be nice to us." Tom said. "Really? Are you sure?" Lily said. "Yes, I am sure. Come on, let's go and say hello to the dog. Maybe he will let us play with his ball too." Tom said. Tom and Lily came out from behind the tree. They walked slowly and gently towards the dog. The dog saw them and wagged his tail. He dropped his ball and licked their faces. Tom and Lily laughed and petted the dog. They played with him and his ball. They were not scared anymore. They were very fast and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to play with his ball and run in the park. One day, Buddy saw a big cat with a big piece of meat. Buddy wanted the meat, so he went to talk to the cat. Buddy said, "Hi, cat! Can you show me the meat?" The cat looked at Buddy and said, "I will show you the meat, but you must help me first. I need you to find my toy mouse. It is under the big tree." Buddy ran to the big tree and found the toy mouse. He brought it back to the cat. The cat showed Buddy the meat and said, "Thank you, Buddy! Now you can have the meat." Buddy was very happy. But as Buddy was eating the meat, he started to feel sick. He had eaten too much, too fast. Buddy lay down on the ground, not feeling well at all. The cat came over and said, "Oh, Buddy, you should not eat so much so fast." The moral of the story is to not be greedy and to eat slowly.

Once upon a time there was a girl who loved to go to the beach. She always brought her net with her. One day when she went to the beach, she saw lots of little fish swimming. She was very patient and decided to wait and see if she could catch some of them. She waited and waited until suddenly, she scooped one up in her net! The little fish was so surprised, but the girl was very gentle and soon put the fish back into the sea. The girl brought her net back home to use again. But she was always on the lookout for more fish, and was always patient for when she could use her net again.

Once there was a boy named Tim. He was three years old. He wanted to make something special. So he decided to make a rocket! He went to the store to buy some fake parts and tools. He put the parts together and made a rocket. When he was done, he wanted to test it. He went outside to the backyard and put it on top of a tall hill. He stepped back and pushed the button. "Vrooom!" it said. The rocket soared up into the sky and made a loud noise. When it came back down, Tim was happy. He had made a rocket! He showed it to his mom and dad and they were very proud of him. From that day on, Tim was always making things with his fake parts. He never made anything as special as his rocket, but he was happy with each one!
