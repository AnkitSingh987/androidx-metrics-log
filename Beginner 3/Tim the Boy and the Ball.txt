Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big rubber ball. He loved to play with it every day. One day, Tim's ball got a terrible hole. Tim was very sad. Tim's mom saw him crying. She asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim showed her the hole in his rubber ball. His mom said, "Don't worry, I will print a new ball for you." Tim's mom went to the big printer. She printed a new rubber ball for Tim. When it was done, she gave it to Tim. He was so happy! Tim said, "Thank you, Mom!" Tim played with his new rubber ball all day. He never had a terrible hole in his ball again. Tim and his mom were very happy. They lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a baby named Tim. Tim was very filthy from playing in the mud all day. His mom saw him and said, "Oh no, Tim! You are so dirty!" "Mom, I had fun playing outside," Tim said with a big smile. His mom reminded him, "It's time for your bath, Tim. You need to be clean." Tim went to the bathroom with his mom. She filled the tub with warm water and bubbles. Tim splashed and played with his toys. Soon, he was clean and not filthy anymore. After the bath, Tim put on his pajamas and went to bed. His mom kissed him goodnight and said, "Now you are a clean baby, Tim." Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Mom. I love you." And they both lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a very interesting cat. This cat loved to welcome new friends. One day, a little bird flew into the village. The cat saw the bird and said, "Hello, little bird! Welcome to our village. Do you want to be my friend?" The little bird said, "Yes, I would love to be your friend!" They played and had fun all day. But then, something unexpected happened. The little bird started to change. It grew bigger and bigger until it was a huge bird! The cat was surprised but still wanted to be friends with the big bird. The big bird said, "Thank you for still being my friend even though I am so big now." They continued to play and have fun together, and the whole village loved their interesting new friend.

Lily loved birds. She liked to watch them fly and sing in the sky. She wanted to hug a bird and be its friend. One day, she saw a large bird in the park. It was white and had a long neck. Lily ran to the bird and tried to hug it. "Hello, bird! You are so pretty! Can I hug you?" Lily asked. But the bird did not like Lily. It was a swan and it was angry. It hissed and bit Lily's arm. Lily cried and let go of the bird. She ran back to her mom, who was sitting on a bench. "Mom, mom, the bird hurt me!" Lily sobbed. "Oh, Lily, I'm sorry. Are you okay?" Mom said. She looked at Lily's arm and saw a red mark. She kissed it and hugged Lily. "Lily, you should not hug birds that you don't know. They might be scared or mean. They are not like your toys. They are wild animals. You have to be careful and respectful of them. Do you understand?" Mom said. Lily nodded. She felt sorry for the bird and for herself. She learned that not all birds are friendly and that she should not hug them without asking. She hugged her mom and said, "I love you, mom. Thank you for hugging me." "I love you too, Lily. You are my sweet girl. And you know what? There are other ways to show your love for birds. You can watch them, feed them, draw them, or sing to them. But you don't have to hug them. Okay?" Mom said. "Okay, mom. I will do that. I still love birds, but I will not hug them anymore. Unless they hug me first." Lily said with a smile.
