Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Purple

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. Tim saw a big, purple toy car. He wanted it so much. He asked his mom, "Can I have the purple car, please?" Mom said, "Yes, but you need to pay for it." Tim looked at the price tag. It had a number on it. The number was five. Tim had five coins in his pocket. He went to the store lady and gave her the five coins. The lady said, "Thank you!" and handed Tim the purple car. Tim was very happy. But when Tim and his mom went outside, they saw something unexpected. There were many purple cars, just like Tim's, all over the street. Tim looked at his mom and said, "Wow, everyone has a purple car like mine!" Mom laughed and said, "Yes, it looks like purple cars are very popular today!" Tim felt happy that he had a special purple car, just like everyone else.

One day, a girl named Sue wanted to ride her bike. She rode down the street and saw a big billboard. The billboard had a picture of a cat. Sue loved cats. Sue saw her friend Tom. "Look at the billboard!" she said. Tom smiled. "It is a nice cat," he said. They both liked the cat on the billboard. The sun was mild and warm. Sue and Tom rode their bikes under the big billboard. They had a fun day riding and looking at the cat. They went home happy.

One day, a big bus came to town. It was a tour bus. Many people got on the bus to see new places. They were all very happy and excited. In one house, there was a little girl named Lily. She saw the big tour bus and wanted to go too. She asked her mom if she could go on the tour. Her mom said yes, and they got on the bus together. Lily was very proud to be on the big bus. On the bus, Lily saw a funny clown. The clown made her laugh. But then, the clown made a loud noise. Lily was scared and she screamed. Her mom hugged her and told her it was okay. The clown said sorry and gave her a big balloon. Lily smiled and enjoyed the rest of the tour.

Once upon a time, there was a blue ball. The blue ball loved to roll around on the floor. It had many friends on the floor. There were red blocks, yellow ducks, and green frogs. One day, the toys on the floor wanted to play together. They wanted to unite and have fun. The blue ball was very happy. It rolled to the red blocks, yellow ducks, and green frogs. They all played together on the floor. After a while, it was time to clean up. The blue ball, red blocks, yellow ducks, and green frogs went back to their spots. They were happy they could unite and have fun on the floor. They knew they would play together again soon.

---

Lily and Ben were playing with their toys in the living room. They liked to pretend they were in a shop and sell things to each other. Lily had a doll, a teddy bear, a ball and a book. Ben had a car, a train, a puzzle and a hat. "Lily, can I buy your doll?" Ben asked. "Sure, Ben. The price is two cookies," Lily said. "Two cookies? That's too much. How about one cookie and a hug?" Ben said. Lily thought for a moment. She liked cookies and hugs, but she also liked her doll. She had a secret. She wanted to reveal it to Ben, but she was not sure if he would like it. "OK, Ben. You can have my doll for one cookie and a hug. But first, I have to tell you something," Lily said. "What is it, Lily?" Ben said. Lily looked around. She wanted to be quiet, so no one else would hear. She leaned closer to Ben and whispered in his ear. "My doll is magic. She can talk and sing and dance. But only when we are alone. Do you want to see?" Lily said. Ben's eyes widened. He loved magic. He nodded eagerly. "OK, but you have to promise not to tell anyone. And you have to be very nice to her. She is my best friend," Lily said. "I promise, Lily. I will be very nice to her. And I won't tell anyone. Cross my heart," Ben said. Lily smiled. She gave Ben her doll and took his cookie and hug. Then they went to the corner of the room, where they had a little tent. They crawled inside and closed the flap. Lily whispered a magic word and the doll came to life. She said hello to Ben and sang a song. Ben was amazed. He hugged the doll and thanked Lily for sharing her secret. They played happily for a long time, until their mom called them for dinner. They put the doll back to sleep and left the tent. They agreed to play again tomorrow, and to keep their secret safe. They were the best of friends.
