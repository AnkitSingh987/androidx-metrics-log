Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Pin

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was very tidy and liked to keep her room clean. One day, she found a small pin on the floor. She picked it up and thought about where to put it. Lucy went to her mom and said, "Mom, I found this pin on the floor. Where should I put it?" Her mom smiled and said, "Lucy, do you remember the small box in your room? You can put the pin in the box." Lucy went back to her room and put the pin in the small box. She felt happy that she had found a place for the pin. Now her room was tidy again. Lucy's mom was proud of her for being such a helpful and tidy girl.

Once upon a time, there was an ancient train station. Many people would hurry to catch their trains. One day, a little boy named Tim and his mom went to the station. Tim saw a big train and said, "Mom, look! The train is here. Let's hurry!" They ran to the train and got on. The train made a loud sound and started to move. As the train went on, Tim looked out the window. He saw trees, houses, and animals. Tim and his mom had a fun time on the train. They were happy they hurried to the ancient station.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a grumpy cat by a tree. The cat had a wallet in its mouth. Tim wanted to meet the cat and get the wallet. Tim walked to the cat and said, "Hi, cat! Can I have the wallet?" The cat hissed and ran away. Tim was sad but he knew he had to get the wallet back. He thought of a plan. Tim got a toy mouse and went back to the tree. He put the toy mouse on the ground and hid. The grumpy cat came back and saw the toy mouse. It dropped the wallet to play with the toy. Tim got the wallet and was very happy.

One day, a tall boy named Tim was playing in the park. He liked to run and jump. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Tim was very happy. Tim's mom said, "Tim, don't go too high. It's not safe!" But Tim did not listen. He climbed higher and higher. Then, he slipped and fell. Tim was hurt and cried loudly. An ambulance came to help Tim. The people in the ambulance said, "We will take you to the doctor." Tim's mom was sad. She had to leave him with the ambulance people. Tim was scared and alone.

Once upon a time, there was a big firework show. It was a perfect night. The sky was dark, and the stars were bright. Many people came to watch the pretty lights. The first firework went up into the sky. It split into lots of colors. Everyone clapped and cheered. They were so happy to see the show. But then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew by and picked up a firework. The bird thought it was food. The firework split in the bird's beak, and the bird became a firework too. It made the show even more perfect. Everyone laughed and clapped. It was the best firework show ever.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to climb trees. One day, he saw a big tree with a shiny copper coin stuck in it. Tim thought it would be fun to climb the tree and get the coin. Tim started to climb the tree. He was very good at it. As he climbed higher, he saw a terrible-looking bird in the tree. The bird looked at Tim and squawked loudly. Tim was scared, but he wanted the copper coin. He kept climbing. When Tim got to the top, the terrible bird flew away. Tim was happy and grabbed the shiny copper coin. He climbed down the tree and showed the coin to his friends. They all cheered for Tim. Tim learned that even if something seems scary, if you try your best, you can do it.
