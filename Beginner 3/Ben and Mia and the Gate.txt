Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Mia and the Gate

Ben and Mia like to play in the park. They see many things there. They see birds and dogs and flowers. They also see a big gate. The gate has a sign. The sign says "Do not enter. Wild animals inside." Ben and Mia are curious. They want to see the wild animals. They look at the gate. The gate has a hole. The hole is thin. Ben and Mia think they can fit through the hole. They crawl through the hole. They see a lot of grass and trees. They hear a loud roar. They see a big lion. The lion is angry. The lion sees Ben and Mia. The lion runs towards them. Ben and Mia are scared. They scream for help. They run back to the hole. They try to crawl through the hole. The hole is too thin. They are stuck. They hear a voice. The voice says "Who are you? What are you doing here?" The voice is the zookeeper. The zookeeper sees Ben and Mia. The zookeeper is angry too. The zookeeper asks them many questions. Ben and Mia do not answer. They are too scared. They cry and say sorry. The zookeeper pulls them out of the hole. The zookeeper takes them to the gate. The zookeeper tells them to never come back. The zookeeper says "This is not a place for children. This is a place for wild animals. Wild animals are dangerous. You are lucky the lion did not catch you." Ben and Mia nod. They say sorry again. They run to their mom. Their mom is worried. She hugs them and asks them what happened. Ben and Mia tell her everything. Their mom is angry too. She says "You should not go where you are not allowed. You should not be so curious. You should listen to the signs. You could have been hurt." Ben and Mia nod again. They say sorry again. They promise to never do it again. They learn their lesson. They go home. They do not play in the park anymore. They play in their room. They play with their toys. They do not play with wild animals.

Once upon a time, there was a big, open door. A little boy named Tom saw the door and wanted to go in. He walked up to the door and saw a spike on the floor. Tom was curious about the spike, so he picked it up. Tom played with the spike near the open door. He liked how it felt in his hands. Then, Tom saw a big red button on the wall. He thought it would be fun to push the button. Tom did not know that the button would close the door. Tom pushed the button with the spike. The door started to close very fast. Tom tried to run away, but he was not quick enough. The door closed on Tom, and he fell down. Tom was sad, and he cried. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was an ancient tree. This tree was very big and old. Many kids thought the tree was scary. They did not like to play near it. One day, a little boy lost his tooth. He looked everywhere for it, but he could not find it. He was very sad. His friends told him that they saw his tooth near the ancient tree. The boy was scared, but he wanted his tooth back. The little boy went to the ancient tree. He was scared, but he took a deep breath and looked for his tooth. And there it was! The boy was so happy that he forgot to be scared. From that day on, the kids were not scared of the ancient tree anymore. They even started to play near it, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Mark. Mark loved to teach kids how to draw. One day, a little girl named Lucy came to Mark's class. She wanted to learn how to draw a cat. Mark said, "Lucy, I will teach you how to draw a cat. First, draw a big circle for the head. Then, draw two small triangles for the ears. Next, draw a small circle for the body. Finally, draw a long line for the tail." Lucy listened to Mark and drew a cat. She was so happy! She showed her cat drawing to her mom. Her mom said, "Wow, Lucy! You did a great job! Thank you, Mark, for teaching my little girl how to draw a cat." From that day on, Lucy and Mark became good friends. They always had fun drawing together. And Lucy learned many new things from her bald friend, Mark.
