Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben and the Bench

Anna and Ben were friends who liked to play together. One day, they went to the park with their moms. They saw a big machine that made popcorn. The popcorn smelled good and looked yummy. "Can we have some popcorn, please?" Anna and Ben asked their moms. "Sure, you can have some popcorn, but you have to share it," their moms said. They gave them some money and let them buy a big bag of popcorn. Anna and Ben were happy. They took the popcorn and sat on a bench. They started to eat the popcorn, but they did not share it. They each wanted to have more popcorn than the other. "Give me some popcorn, Anna!" Ben said. "No, it's mine! You have enough!" Anna said. They began to fight over the popcorn. They pulled and pushed the bag. They did not see the dog that was watching them. The dog was hungry and liked popcorn too. He saw his chance and jumped on the bench. He grabbed the bag of popcorn and ran away. Anna and Ben were shocked. They looked at the empty bench and then at each other. They felt sad and sorry. They had lost their popcorn and their fun. They realized they had been ignorant. They had not listened to their moms. They had not shared their popcorn. They had not been good friends. They ran to their moms and hugged them. They said they were sorry and asked for forgiveness. Their moms forgave them and hugged them back. They told them that sharing is caring and that friends should be kind and generous. Anna and Ben learned their lesson. They promised to share and be good friends. They hoped to find the dog and give him some popcorn too. They smiled and played together again.

One day, a dog named Max went for a walk. Max liked to walk in the park. He saw trees, flowers, and other dogs. Max was happy when he walked. On this day, Max saw a nail on the ground. He was not sure what it was. Max picked up the nail with his mouth. He wanted to show his friend, a cat named Luna. Max walked to Luna's house. He was tired from his long walk. Luna saw Max with the nail and was scared. She thought it was a snake! Luna jumped high in the air. Max dropped the nail and laughed. It was not a snake, just a nail! Luna and Max laughed together. They were happy to have a fun day.

One day, a little girl named Sue went with her mom to a small office. It was a modest office, not too big or too fancy. Inside, Sue saw a man sitting at a desk. He had a big smile on his face. "Hi Sue," he said. "I have a surprise for you!" Sue was very excited about the surprise. She looked around the office and saw many things. She saw a big chair, a small chair, a red ball, and a blue box. Sue thought the surprise was in the blue box. She asked the man, "Is the surprise in the blue box?" The man laughed and said, "No, Sue. The surprise is not in the blue box. But you will see it soon." Sue waited patiently, looking around the office for more clues. She saw a door at the back of the office. Sue thought the surprise might be behind the door. Then, the man opened the door, and Sue saw the surprise. It was not what she expected at all! There was a big, beautiful dog with a bow on its collar. Sue was so happy! She never thought she would get a new friend at the office. She thanked the man and hugged her new dog. Sue knew this was the best surprise ever.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They saw a big, round ball in the park. "Mom, can I play with the ball?" Tim asked. His mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Tim started to play with the ball, when a fierce dog came running. The dog wanted to play too! Tim was scared, but he watched the dog. The dog just wanted to play, so Tim said, "Okay, dog, you can play with me." They played with the round ball, and the dog was not fierce anymore. Tim's mom watched them play and she was happy. Then, the dog's owner came and said, "Thank you for playing with my dog, his name is Max." Tim smiled and said, "You're welcome, Max is my new friend." And they all played together in the park.
