Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a light blue dress that she loved to wear. Mia liked to play with her best friend, Tom the cat. They played in the sun and had lots of fun. One day, Mia and Tom were hungry. They went to the kitchen to find some food. Mia saw a big bowl of apples on the table. She said, "Tom, let's eat these apples! They look yummy!" Tom meowed happily and agreed. Mia took an apple from the bowl and gave one to Tom. They both took a big bite and enjoyed the sweet taste. They were very happy to have a tasty snack. After they finished eating, they went outside to play some more.

Once there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a modern toy robot. The robot had a number on it. The number was five. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. Sam saw the robot and said, "I want to play with the robot too!" But Tim did not want to share. He ignored Sam and kept playing with the robot. Later, Tim's robot stopped working. The number five was missing. Tim was sad. Sam said, "I found the number five! Let's put it back and play together." Tim learned to share, and they played with the robot together.

Lila and Ben went to the park with Mom. They saw a big pond with many ducks and swans. Lila liked the swans. They had long necks and white feathers. She wanted to feed them some bread. She took a piece of bread from Mom and walked to the pond. She saw a swan near the edge. She threw the bread to the swan. But the swan did not move. It did not eat the bread. It did not look at Lila. It did not make any sound. Lila was sad. She thought the swan was sick or hurt. She asked Mom, "Why does the swan not move, Mom?" Mom looked at the swan. She saw something shiny on its neck. She said, "Oh, Lila, that is not a real swan. That is a metal swan. Someone put it there as a decoration. It cannot move or eat or talk. It is just a fake swan." Lila was surprised. She looked at the metal swan again. She saw that it was dirty and rusty. She thought it was disgusting. She said, "Yuck! I do not like the metal swan. It is not nice. It is not fun. It is not a friend. I like the real swans better. They are pretty and soft and alive. Can we feed the real swans, Mom?" Mom smiled and nodded. She said, "Yes, Lila, we can feed the real swans. They are hungry and happy to see us. Come on, Ben, let's go find some real swans." Lila and Ben followed Mom to another part of the pond. They saw some real swans swimming and quacking. They threw some bread to the real swans. The real swans moved and ate and looked at Lila and Ben. They made some sounds. Lila and Ben laughed and clapped. They had fun with the real swans. They forgot about the disgusting metal swan.

One Sunday, a powerful wind blew into town. It was so strong that it could zip through the trees and make them dance. A little girl named Amy was playing outside with her toy car when the wind came. "Wow!" said Amy, "The wind is so fast and strong today!" The wind heard Amy and thought it would be fun to play a game. As Amy pushed her toy car, the wind zipped by and took the car with it. Amy was shocked and started to chase after her toy car. As she ran, she saw the wind carrying her toy car up into the sky. Amy was sad and yelled, "Please give me back my toy car!" The wind felt bad for taking her toy car and decided to help Amy. It zipped around and found a big, red balloon. The wind tied the toy car to the balloon and gently floated it back down to Amy. She was so happy to have her toy car back and thanked the wind for the fun adventure.
