Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Cake

One day, a little girl named Sue was very excited. She wanted to cook with her mom. Sue loved to cook and learn new things. Her mom said, "Today, we will cook a big cake for dad's birthday!" While they were cooking, Sue's mom told her how much value it is to help others. Sue listened and nodded her head. She wanted to help her mom and make her dad happy with the cake. As they finished the cake, Sue had an idea. She said, "Mom, let's share the cake with our friends too!" Her mom agreed and they went outside to share the cake. But, surprise! Their friends were already there with a big cake for Sue's dad. They all laughed and had a great time. Sue learned that helping others and sharing brings happiness to everyone.

Max was a small boy. He was only 3 years old. He was always looking for new exciting things to do. One day his mommy said, "Max, do you want to fold a tank?" Max said, "Yes! What's a tank?" Mommy replied, "It's an open square made of paper. You can fold it together to make a 3D figure. Would you like to try?" Max said, "Yes! Please show me how to do it." Mommy took out a piece of paper and showed him how to fold it. Max followed her instructions and soon made his very own tank. Max said, "Wow! I did it!" Mommy laughed and said, "Yes, you did great!" Max played with his tank all day long. He loved it and was so proud that he had made it all by himself!

Once upon a time, there was a hairy dog named Spot. Spot had a big hat that he loved to wear every day. One day, Spot met a little cat named Fluffy. Fluffy did not like Spot's hat. "Spot, I hate your hat," Fluffy said. Spot was sad, but he still wore his hat. The next day, Spot and Fluffy played outside. Suddenly, it started to rain. Spot's hat kept him dry, but Fluffy was all wet. "Spot, can I wear your hat too?" Fluffy asked. Spot said yes, and they both wore the hat. Now Fluffy liked the hat, and they were both happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little red bug. The bug liked to crawl all around the big green tree. Every day, the bug would crawl up and down, and side to side. The bug was very happy. One day, the bug met a new friend. The friend was a tiny fish. The fish told the bug about a thing called oxygen. The fish said, "I need water to breathe. Water has oxygen for me." The bug said, "I breathe air. Air has oxygen for me." The red bug and the tiny fish became best friends. They played together every day. The bug would crawl on the tree, and the fish would swim in the water. They both loved the oxygen that helped them breathe and play together. And they lived happily ever after.

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to play with toys and watch cartoons on the screen. But they have only one screen and many toys. Sometimes they fight over the screen and the toys. One day, Mom says, "You have to share the screen and the toys. Sharing is good. You can take turns and have fun together." Tom and Sam nod. They want to be good and make Mom happy. They try to share the screen and the toys. But it is hard. Tom wants to watch a show about cars. Sam wants to watch a show about animals. They argue and push the buttons. The screen changes colors and makes noises. Mom hears them and comes to see. "What are you doing?" Mom asks. "You are not sharing. You are making the screen dull. It is not fun to watch a dull screen. It is better to watch a bright screen with nice pictures and sounds." Tom and Sam feel sorry. They say, "We are sorry, Mom. We want to share. But we like different shows. How can we share the screen?" Mom smiles. She says, "You can share the screen by choosing a show that you both like. Or you can watch one show for a while, and then switch to another show. Or you can watch no show and play with the toys instead. There are many ways to share the screen and the toys. You just have to be kind and fair and listen to each other." Tom and Sam think. They say, "Thank you, Mom. You are right. Sharing is good. We will try to be kind and fair and listen to each other." They hug Mom and then hug each other. They decide to watch a show about cars and animals. They also play with the toys and share them. They have fun and make the screen bright. Mom is happy and proud of them.
