Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to walk to school every day. He liked to look at the big trees and the pretty flowers on the way. Tim was a happy boy. One day, Tim saw a big blackboard in his classroom. It was very fancy. Tim's teacher said, "We will draw on the blackboard today." Tim was excited to draw with his friends. But when they started to draw, something unexpected happened. The blackboard came to life! It could talk and walk! The blackboard said, "Hello, I am Mr. Chalky!" Tim and his friends were surprised, but they liked Mr. Chalky. They all had fun drawing and learning together. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was an intelligent crocodile named Coco. Coco lived in a big river. She loved to decorate her home with pretty things she found in the water. One day, Coco found a shiny, round thing. She did not know what it was, but she thought it would look nice in her home. Coco put the shiny, round thing in her home. She showed it to her friends, and they all liked it too. They said, "Coco, you are so good at decorating!" Coco was very happy. But she still did not know what the shiny, round thing was. One day, a big storm came. The water in the river went up and up. Coco's home was in danger. She had to think fast. She remembered the shiny, round thing. Coco put the shiny, round thing in the water, and it floated. Coco and her friends climbed on it. The shiny, round thing saved them from the storm. Coco and her friends were safe and happy. They learned that the shiny, round thing was not just for decorating, but it could help them too.

Once upon a time, there was a big hippo. He lived in a river. One day, he saw a small bird. The bird was sad. The bird said, "I want to test my wings, but the river is dangerous." The hippo said, "I can help you. I will carry you on my back. Then you can test your wings." The bird was happy. They went to the river. The bird sat on the hippo's back. The bird tried to fly. But a big fish jumped out of the water. The fish was dangerous! The bird was scared. The fish bit the hippo. The hippo was hurt. The bird could not fly. They both were sad. The river was too dangerous for them.

Lily and Tom are twins. They like to play with toys. One day, they go to the toy shop with Mom. They see many toys. They want to buy them all. "Mom, can I buy this doll?" Lily asks. She holds a pink doll with a dress. "Mom, can I buy this car?" Tom asks. He holds a red car with wheels. Mom smiles. "You can buy one toy each. But you have to share with each other. That is the truth." Lily and Tom think. They want to buy their own toys. They do not want to share. They are upset. "Mom, I do not want to share. This is my doll. Tom can buy his own doll." Lily says. "Mom, I do not want to share. This is my car. Lily can buy her own car." Tom says. Mom frowns. "That is not nice. Sharing is good. Sharing is fun. If you do not share, you cannot buy any toys. That is the truth." Lily and Tom look at each other. They do not want to lose their toys. They do not want to make Mom sad. They decide to share. "Mom, we will share. Lily can play with my car. I can play with her doll." Tom says. "Mom, we will share. Tom can play with my doll. I can play with his car." Lily says. Mom smiles. "That is very good. You are kind and smart. You can buy your toys. That is the truth." Lily and Tom hug Mom. They are happy. They buy their toys. They share them. They have fun. That is the truth.
