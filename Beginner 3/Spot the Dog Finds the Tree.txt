Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog Finds the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was very upset because he had no friends to play with. He would sit under a big tree and watch the birds fly by, wishing he could join them in their fun. One sunny day, Spot saw a small cat named Fluffy. Fluffy was looking for a friend too. Spot ran up to Fluffy and said, "Hi! I need a friend. Do you want to play with me?" Fluffy smiled and said, "Yes! I need a friend too. Let's play together!" Spot and Fluffy played all day long. They chased each other, played with a big ball, and took a nap under the big tree. Spot was not upset anymore. He had found a friend in Fluffy. From that day on, Spot and Fluffy were the best of friends and they played together every day.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He wanted to play with his ball. The park had a wild look, with big trees and tall grass. Tim felt happy to be there. In the park, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue asked, "Can I play with you?" Tim said, "Yes!" They played catch with the ball. They felt very happy. As they played, a big wind came. The wind took the ball high up in the sky. Tim and Sue looked up and saw a big bird. The bird caught the ball in its beak. Tim and Sue felt surprised. Then, the bird flew down and gave the ball back to them. They thanked the bird and kept playing, feeling very happy again.

Ben and Jen are twins. They look the same and like the same things. They always play together and share their toys. But today, they have a problem. They both want to play with the same red car. "I saw it first!" Ben says. "No, I did!" Jen says. They pull the car from each other. They pull so hard that the car splits in two. The wheels come off and the roof falls off. Ben and Jen are sad and angry. They have broken their favorite toy. Mom hears them cry and comes to see what is wrong. She sees the broken car and the twins. She is not happy. "Ben and Jen, what have you done?" she asks. "We wanted to play with the car, but we both wanted it," Ben says. "So we pulled it and it split," Jen says. Mom shakes her head. She picks up the pieces of the car and puts them on the table. "Ben and Jen, you need to learn to share and take turns. You can't have everything you want. Sometimes you have to let your brother or sister have something. That's what being twins means. You are the same, but you are also different. You have different feelings and different needs. You have to respect that and be kind to each other," she says. She gives them a hug and a kiss. She tells them to sit on the couch and think about what they did. She says she will try to fix the car later. Ben and Jen sit on the couch. They feel sorry for breaking the car and for making Mom unhappy. They look at each other and say sorry. "I'm sorry, Ben. I should have let you play with the car first," Jen says. "I'm sorry, Jen. I should have given you a turn with the car," Ben says. They smile and hug. They decide to play with something else. They find a big book with pictures and words. They take turns to read and look at the book. They learn new things and have fun. They are happy and Mom is happy. They are twins, but they are also different. And that's okay.

Once upon a time, in a land full of sun, there lived a little girl named Ava. Ava loved to play outside when the sun was up. She liked to share her toys with her friends, but sometimes she did not have enough toys available for everyone. One day, Ava and her friends were playing near a big tree. They saw a box under the tree. Ava opened the box and found many toys inside. They were very happy and started to play with the new toys. They did not know what would happen next. As they played, a small bird came and told them that the toys belonged to another child who lived nearby. Ava and her friends felt sad for taking the toys. They decided to give the toys back and say sorry. The other child was very happy and thanked Ava and her friends. They all learned that it is important to ask before taking something that is not yours.
