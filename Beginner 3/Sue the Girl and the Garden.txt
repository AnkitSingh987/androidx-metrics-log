Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, Sue's mom asked her to serve food to her friends. Sue was very happy to help. Sue went to the sink to wash her hands. The sink was full of water and bubbles. Sue washed her hands and then dried them with a towel. She was ready to serve the food. Sue carried the food to her mom's friends. They were sitting at a distant table in the garden. Sue served the food to them with a big smile. Her mom's friends said "thank you" and Sue was very proud.

One day, a cat named Tom set out to play in the garden. The sun was up, and the sky was blue. Tom was happy to be outside. He saw many pretty flowers, and he liked to jump on the soft grass. In the garden, Tom found a smelly old shoe. He sniffed it and made a funny face. "Yuck!" he thought. But then, he saw a little mouse run out of the shoe. Tom wanted to play with the mouse, so he chased it around the garden. They ran and played all day, under the trees and in the sun. The mouse was fast, but Tom was faster. At the end of the day, Tom and the mouse were tired. They sat down in the garden, under a big tree, and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big brother and a little brother. They liked to play together. The big brother always supported the little brother when they played games. They were both very healthy and strong. One day, they went to the park to play. They saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. The big brother helped the little brother climb up the tree. They were having so much fun. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and the big brother fell from the tree. But the little brother, who was now strong and healthy too, caught him. The little brother supported the big brother, and they both laughed. They were happy to help each other.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a big, ugly bug. He did not recognize it. The bug was on his dinner plate. Tim was scared and did not want to eat his dinner. "Mom, there is an ugly bug on my dinner!" Tim cried. His mom looked at the plate and said, "Oh no, that is not good. Let's take it outside." When they went outside, the ugly bug started to talk! "Thank you for not eating me," said the bug. Tim and his mom were very surprised. They did not know that bugs could talk. The bug told them that he was lost and needed help. Tim and his mom decided to help the bug find his family. They all became friends and had a big dinner together. The ugly bug was not so scary after all.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was a thoughtful girl who liked to play with her toys. One day, she found a cup in her room. The cup was red and shiny. Mia picked up the cup and said, "Hello, cup! Do you want to play with me?" The cup did not say anything, but Mia pretended it did. She took the cup with her and went to the garden. In the garden, Mia saw a big tree. She thought it would be fun to play hide and seek with the cup. Mia hid the cup behind the tree and said, "I will count to ten, and then I will find you, cup!" Mia closed her eyes and counted. When she was done, she went to the tree and peeked. There was the red cup! Mia laughed and said, "I found you, cup! Let's play again!" And so, Mia and the cup played hide and seek all day long. They had lots of fun together, and Mia was always happy to have a new friend to play with.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big, scary tree. Tim was scared, but he wanted to be brave. He walked up to the tree and said, "Hello, scary tree! I am not scared of you!" The scary tree did not say anything. But, the wind blew and the tree's big branches moved. It looked like the tree was waving at Tim. Tim smiled and said, "I will be your friend, scary tree." Tim found a long tie on the ground. He picked it up and tied it around the tree. "Now, we are friends forever!" he said. The wind blew again, and the tree's branches shut like it was giving Tim a hug. Tim knew that he and the scary tree would be friends for a long time.
