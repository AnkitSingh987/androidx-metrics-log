Category: Beginner
Title: The Queen and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a queen. She lived in a big castle. The queen was sometimes bitter. She did not like to share her toys with the other kids in the land. One day, the queen met a kind girl. The girl asked the queen to mind her manners. The queen thought about it and said, "Okay, I will try to be nice and share." The queen started to share her toys with the other kids. They all played together and had fun. The queen was not bitter anymore. She was now a happy and kind queen who minded her manners. And they all lived happily ever after.

---

One day, a little girl named Anna was playing with her toys. She had a small box and many attractive toys that she liked a lot. She wanted to see if her toys could fit in the box. So, she started to try putting them inside one by one. Anna tried to measure the box to see if her big toy bear would fit. But, as she was measuring, something unexpected happened! The box started to grow bigger and bigger. Anna was very surprised and happy. Now, she could fit all her toys in the box easily. But then, the box stopped growing. Anna tried to put her big toy bear in the box, but it was still too small! The box had only grown big enough for her smaller toys. Anna laughed and thought it was a funny twist. She put her smaller toys in the box and played with her big toy bear outside the box.

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Lucy. Lucy lived in a small town. The town was having a big festival. Lucy wanted to go to the festival and have fun. At the festival, Lucy saw many animals. There was a dog, a bird, and a mouse. Lucy wanted to scare the mouse. She thought it would be fun. She jumped at the mouse and said, "Boo!" The mouse was very scared and ran away. Lucy felt bad for scaring the mouse. She knew it was not nice to scare others. Lucy found the mouse and said, "I am sorry." The mouse forgave Lucy, and they became friends. They played together at the festival and had a great time. The moral of the story is to be kind to others and not to scare them.

Tim and Sam are friends. They like to play in the park. They have a red ball and a blue tie. They take turns to throw the ball and catch it with the tie. One day, a big dog comes to the park. It sees the ball and the tie. It wants to play too. It runs to Tim and Sam and barks. Tim and Sam are scared. They drop the ball and the tie and run away. "Help! Help!" they shout. "The dog is chasing us!" A man hears them. He is the dog's owner. He calls the dog and holds its collar. He says, "I'm sorry, boys. My dog is friendly. He just wanted to play with you. He likes balls and ties." Tim and Sam stop running. They look at the dog. The dog wags its tail and licks its nose. He looks happy. "Can we play with your dog?" Tim asks. "Sure, if you want to. His name is Spot. He is very excited to meet you," the man says. Tim and Sam smile. They go back to the ball and the tie. They throw the ball and Spot runs after it. He catches it with his mouth and brings it back. He drops it and picks up the tie. He shakes it and tosses it in the air. He catches it again and runs to Tim and Sam. They laugh and pet Spot. They play with him until it is time to go home. They say goodbye to the man and Spot. They are happy. They have a new friend.

One day, a big dog named Max found a spade in the yard. He thought it was a toy, so he picked it up with his mouth. Max was very happy to play with the spade. He ran around the yard with it, wagging his tail. Max saw a small hill in the yard. He decided to act like a person and dig with the spade. He put the spade in the dirt and pushed with his nose. Soon, he made a small hole in the ground. Max was very proud of himself. Max's friend, a little cat named Lily, came to see what he was doing. She saw the hole and the spade. Lily wanted to help Max. They worked together, digging with the spade. They made the hole bigger and bigger. At the end of the day, Max and Lily were tired but happy. They had so much fun with the big spade.
