Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Bird

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big tree with a funny looking bird on it. The bird had a long beak and fierce eyes. Tim pointed at the bird and said, "Look, Mom! What is that?" His mom looked up and said, "That's a special bird called a toucan. It has a big beak to eat fruit." Tim wanted to see the bird up close, so he walked closer to the tree. The bird looked at Tim and opened its big beak. Suddenly, the bird jumped from the tree and landed on Tim's arm! Tim was scared, but then he saw that the bird was holding a small palm leaf in its beak. The bird dropped the leaf into Tim's hand and flew back to the tree. Tim looked at the palm leaf and saw that there was a message on it. It said, "Thank you for being my friend." Tim smiled and waved at the bird. The bird waved back with its wing and flew away. Tim knew that he had made a new friend that day.

Lisa liked to draw with her pen. She had a red pen that made nice pictures. She drew flowers and stars and hearts on her paper. She was happy with her pen. Tommy wanted to draw too. He did not have a pen. He saw Lisa's red pen and he liked it. He went to Lisa and said, "Can I have your pen?" Lisa looked at Tommy and said, "No, this is my pen. I am drawing with it. You can't have it." Tommy felt sad and angry. He wanted to draw with the red pen. He said, "That's not fair. You have to share. Sharing is good." Lisa said, "But I like my pen. It is my favorite pen. I don't want to share it." Tommy tried to grab the pen from Lisa. Lisa held on to the pen. They both pulled and tugged. The pen broke in half. They both cried. Miss Jane heard them cry. She came to see what was wrong. She saw the broken pen and the sad faces. She said, "What happened here?" Lisa and Tommy told her what happened. They both said they were sorry. Miss Jane said, "It's okay to be sorry, but it's better to be kind. Sharing is a kind thing to do. When you share, you make friends. Friends are more fun than pens. Do you want to be friends?" Lisa and Tommy nodded. They said, "Yes, we want to be friends." Miss Jane smiled. She said, "That's good. I have more pens for you. You can each have a pen and draw together. You can also share your pens with other kids. They will like you and you will be popular. Popular means many kids like you. Do you want to be popular?" Lisa and Tommy nodded. They said, "Yes, we want to be popular." Miss Jane gave them each a pen. Lisa got a blue pen and Tommy got a green pen. They said, "Thank you, Miss Jane." They went to a table and started to draw. They shared their pens and their paper. They drew nice pictures. They smiled and laughed. They made friends with other kids. They were happy and popular.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was a very lucky girl. One day, Lily found a big gift under her bed. She was so happy! She wanted to open the gift right away. But, her mommy said, "Lily, you must wait until your birthday." Lily wanted to open the gift so badly, but she knew she had to listen to her mommy. Days went by, and Lily was very good. She didn't try to open the gift early. She knew that if she was patient, her mommy would allow her to open it on her birthday. Finally, Lily's birthday came! She was so excited. Her mommy allowed her to open the gift. Inside was a beautiful doll! Lily was so happy that she had waited. She played with her new doll all day. The moral of the story is to always be patient and listen to your mommy.
