Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Tall Man and the Toys

One day, a tall man named Tom went for a walk. He wore his cool sunglasses. The sun was very bright, and the sunglasses helped his eyes feel good. Tom saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad. "What is wrong?" Tom asked. Sue said, "I lost my toy." Tom wanted to comfort Sue. "Don't worry, we will find your toy," he said. They looked for the toy together. Suddenly, Tom found something unexpected. It was a big box of new toys! Tom and Sue were so happy. They played with the new toys and forgot about the lost toy.

Once upon a time, there was a king who lived in a big castle. The king was very stubborn. He wanted everyone to do what he said. One day, the king wanted to see who could run the fastest in his kingdom. He told all the people to come to the castle and run. The people were scared, but they came to the castle. The king said, "Ready, set, go!" and everyone started to run. They ran and ran, but the king was not happy. He wanted them to run faster. A little boy named Tom was watching the race. He said, "I can run faster than everyone else." The king laughed and said, "Okay, you try." Tom ran as fast as he could, and he won the race. The king was very surprised. He learned that even a little boy could be faster than the others. From that day on, the king was not as stubborn and listened more to the people in his kingdom.

Once upon a time, there was a big, fair dog named Max. Max loved to sit in the sun and eat his favorite treat, waffles. One day, Max found a big waffle on the ground. He was so happy! Max wanted to share the waffle with his friends. So he carefully picked it up and walked to find his friends. He found his friend, a small cat named Lucy. Max showed Lucy the waffle and asked if she wanted to share it with him. Lucy was very happy to see the waffle. She said, "Yes, I would love to share the waffle with you, Max!" So Max and Lucy sat down together and ate the big waffle. They were both very happy and full after eating the waffle. And from that day on, Max and Lucy were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily loved to dance. She would dance all day and all night. One day, her mom said, "Lily, we are going to visit your grandma today!" When they got to grandma's house, Lily was so excited to show her dance moves. She started to dance in the living room, but then she tripped and fell. Lily felt embarrassed and her face turned red. Grandma saw Lily and said, "Don't be embarrassed, my dear. I have a surprise for you!" Grandma went to her room and came back with a big box. Inside the box was a beautiful dress for dancing. Lily put on the dress and started to dance again. This time, she didn't trip and everyone clapped for her. Lily was so happy and she danced with grandma all day long.

One day, a boy named Tom went to the park. He saw a big tree with fruit on it. The fruit was red and looked yummy. Tom wanted to eat the fruit, but it was too high for him to reach. Tom saw a girl named Sue with a spray bottle. Sue was using the bottle to spray water on the flowers. Tom asked Sue, "Can you help me get the fruit?" Sue said, "I can try to spray the fruit down." They tried to spray the fruit, but it did not fall. A calm dog walked by and saw them. The dog barked and jumped up to get the fruit. The dog got the fruit and gave it to Tom and Sue. Tom and Sue said, "Thank you, dog!" They shared the fruit and played with the dog at the park. They were all happy and had a fun day.

One day, a small boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. It was a cold day, and Tim began to shiver. He saw a big flag near the park. The flag was red and blue. Tim liked the flag very much. Tim's mom saw him shiver and said, "Are you cold, Tim?" Tim nodded and said, "Yes, Mom, I am cold." His mom hugged him tight to keep him warm. Tim felt grateful for his mom's hug. He stopped shivering and smiled. Then, Tim's mom had an idea. She took off her big scarf and wrapped it around Tim. Tim felt warm and cozy now. He said, "Thank you, Mom!" Tim and his mom played near the flag until the sun went down. They were happy and warm, and Tim was grateful for his mom's love.
