Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Red Cat and the Bird

One day, a red cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big tree with a small bird on a branch. The bird had a red hat. Tom thought the bird looked funny. Tom climbed the tree and said, "Hello, bird. I like your red hat." The bird said, "Thank you, Tom. I have a secret to tell you. Come closer so I can whisper in your ear." Tom moved closer to the bird. The bird whispered, "There is a big box under the tree. It has a surprise for you." Tom got excited and climbed down the tree. He found the big box and opened it. Inside, there was a red hat just like the bird's! Tom put it on and they both laughed. Now they both had red hats and looked funny together.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to play a game. He wanted to match the colors of his toys. He had a red car, a blue ball, and a yellow duck. Tim tried to find a toy to match each color. Tim's big sister, Sue, came into the room. She saw Tim playing with his toys. Sue wanted to join, but she wanted to play with her ball. She asked Tim for a pass. Tim was not happy. He did not want to stop his game. Sue was sad, but she had an idea. She told Tim that they could play together. They could match the colors and pass the ball at the same time. Tim liked the idea, and they started to play. They were successful, and both Tim and Sue were happy. They learned that playing together was more fun than playing alone.

One day, Tom and Sue went to play a game. They wanted to win a big prize. The prize was a holiday to a fun place with an orange sun. Tom and Sue played the game. They tried very hard. "I want to win!" said Tom. "Me too!" said Sue. They played and played until the game was over. At the end, Tom and Sue won the game! They were very happy. "We won! We won!" they said. They got the holiday with the orange sun. They had a great time and made new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a peaceful monkey named Bobo. Bobo loved to bounce on his bed all day. He was very happy. One day, while Bobo was bouncing, he heard a strange noise. He stopped bouncing and listened. The noise came from outside. Bobo was curious, so he went out to see. When he got outside, he saw a big, bouncy ball. It was bouncing all by itself! Bobo was so surprised. He started to bounce with the ball, and they became best friends. They bounced together every day, and Bobo was even happier than before.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to play outside in the sun. One day, he saw a small beetle on the ground. Tim was very careful when he looked at the beetle. He did not want to hurt it. Tim wanted to show the beetle to his mom. So, he tried to grab the beetle with his hand. But the beetle was very fast. Tim tried again and again, but he could not catch the beetle. Finally, Tim got very mad. He stomped on the ground near the beetle. The beetle was scared and tried to run away. But Tim stomped too hard and the beetle was hurt. Tim felt bad, but it was too late. The beetle could not play anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was a fearful girl. She was scared of many things. One day, Sue's mom told her it was time to take a bath. Sue did not like the bathtub. She was scared of the water. Sue's mom said, "Don't be scared, Sue. I will help you. We can spend time together in the bathroom. You will see that the bathtub is fun." Sue and her mom went to the bathroom. Sue's mom filled the bathtub with warm water. She put bubbles and toys in the water. Sue saw the fun bathtub and smiled. She got in the water with her mom's help. Sue played with her toys and splashed the water. She was not scared anymore. Sue's mom was right. The bathtub was fun. Sue was happy she spent time with her mom in the bathtub. From that day on, Sue was not fearful of the bathtub anymore.
