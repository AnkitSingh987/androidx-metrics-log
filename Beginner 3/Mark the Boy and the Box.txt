Category: Beginner
Title: Mark the Boy and the Box

One day, a boy named Mark wanted to be successful. He was a good boy who liked to help others. Mark thought that if he could pray every day, he would become successful. Every day, Mark prayed and helped his friends. One day, he found a big box. Mark was excited and thought that maybe his prayers were answered. He opened the box, but it was empty. Mark was sad, but he kept praying and helping others. One day, Mark's friend told him that he was successful because he was kind and helpful. Mark was happy and realized that he was already successful by being a good friend. The empty box showed Mark that success was not something he could find, but something he could be by helping others.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a purple cat named Lily. She was the only purple cat in the town. Lily was young and loved to play with her friends. One sunny day, Lily met a new friend named Tom, who was a brown dog. Tom was also young, just like Lily. They played together all day long. They ran, jumped, and laughed together. They were very happy. Then, Lily saw a big, pretty ball. She asked Tom, "Can I borrow your ball to play?" Tom said, "Yes, you can, Lily. Let's play together with my ball." They played with the ball and had lots of fun. They played until the sun went down. At the end of the day, Lily gave the ball back to Tom. They said goodbye to each other and went to their homes. They knew they would meet again and play together soon. And that's how Lily and Tom became best friends.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play in their room at night. They have a big box of toys. They have dolls, cars, books, and animals. They also have a fake sword and a fake crown. One night, they pretend to be a king and a queen. Ben wears the crown and Lily holds the sword. They sit on their bed and act like they rule the land. They make up stories and talk to each other. "King Ben, I see a dragon outside the window!" Lily says. She points to the dark sky. "Queen Lily, do not be afraid. I will save you from the dragon!" Ben says. He jumps off the bed and grabs a pillow. He throws the pillow at the window and makes a loud noise. "Roar! I am the dragon! I will eat you, king and queen!" a voice says. It is their dad. He is hiding behind the curtain. He likes to play with them and make them laugh. Lily and Ben scream and giggle. They run to the window and pull the curtain. They see their dad smiling and holding a fake tail. "Dad, you are so silly! You are not a dragon, you are a fake dragon!" Lily says. "You are right, my princess. I am a fake dragon. But you are a brave queen. And you, Ben, are a strong king. You saved your sister from me!" their dad says. He hugs them and kisses them. He tells them it is time to sleep. He turns off the light and tucks them in. He says good night and leaves the room. Lily and Ben smile and close their eyes. They dream of being a king and a queen. They dream of having a real sword and a real crown. They dream of saving each other from dragons and monsters. They are happy. They love to play. They love their dad. They love each other.

Once upon a time, there was a dancer named Lily. She loved to dance, but she was a terrible dancer. One day, Lily went to the park to dance. She saw a girl named Sue. "Hi Sue!" said Lily. "Do you want to dance with me?" Sue looked at Lily and said, "I don't know how to dance. Can you teach me?" Lily smiled and started to teach Sue. They danced and laughed all day. But Lily was still a terrible dancer. Sue had an idea. She said, "Lily, why don't you wear my shoes? They might help you dance better." Lily put on Sue's shoes and started to dance. She was still a terrible dancer, but she didn't care. She was having so much fun with her friend. They danced and laughed until the sun went down. The moral of the story is that it's not about being the best, it's about having fun and enjoying time with friends.
