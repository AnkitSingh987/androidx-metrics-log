Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Mom

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy some food. Tim saw a cheap toy car that he wanted. "Mom, can I have this car?" he asked. His mom looked at the price and said, "Yes, it seems cheap enough. You can have it." Tim was very happy and gave his mom a big hug. When they got home, Tim played with his new car. He noticed that the car had a little button. When he pushed it, the car gave a wink with its lights. Tim laughed and showed his mom. They both thought it was a fun toy.

One day, a queen was in her big room. She wanted to fold her clean clothes. She had a lot of clothes to fold. She felt tired, but she had to do it. The queen called her friend, the cat. "Cat, can you help me fold my clean clothes?" she asked. The cat said, "Yes, queen. I can help you." They started to fold clothes together. As they were folding, they found a small box in the clothes. The queen opened the box, and a little bird flew out. The bird said, "Thank you for freeing me! As a thank you, I will fold all your clothes for you!" The queen and the cat were very happy. They watched as the bird folded all the clothes very fast.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to dress up in her mom's clothes. One day, she found a magic star in her room. She made a wish to the star. She wished to be healthy and strong. Amy's mom saw her dress up and knew she was trying to be like her. She thought of a great idea. She told Amy that they would play a game. The game would help her be healthy and strong, just like her wish. Every day, they played the game. They would run, jump, and eat good food. Amy loved the game. She felt healthy and strong. One day, her mom gave her a special dress. It was for being healthy and strong. Amy was so happy. Her wish came true.

Once upon a time, there was a little engine. The engine was scared because it had never gone far from home. One day, the engine decided to be brave and go on a big adventure. The little engine went up and down hills, through the woods, and across a river. The engine saw many new things like big trees, pretty flowers, and little animals. The engine was not scared anymore. At the end of the day, the little engine went back home. It was happy it had decided to go on the adventure. The engine was not scared anymore and could not wait to go on another adventure soon.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was a lucky girl who had a big toy stove. She loved to play with her stove every day. One day, Lucy decided to pray for a real stove, just like her mom's. The next day, Lucy woke up and saw a real stove in her room! She was so happy and surprised. She wanted to cook something yummy. But Lucy was too small to use the real stove all by herself. Lucy's mom saw the stove and knew it was not safe for Lucy. She took the stove away and gave Lucy her toy stove back. Lucy was sad, but she knew her mom was right. She decided to pray for a big toy stove instead. The next day, Lucy got an even bigger toy stove! She was so lucky and happy to play with her new toy.

One day, a little cat was walking in the park. The cat saw a big, hard ball on the ground. The cat wanted to play with the ball. So, the cat jumped and hit the ball with its head. But the ball was too hard and it hurt the cat's head. The cat started to weep because it was in pain. A kind girl saw the cat weeping and went to the cat. She asked, "Why are you weeping, little cat?" The cat said, "I hit the hard ball with my head and now it hurts." The kind girl picked up the cat and hugged it. She said, "Don't weep, little cat. I will help you feel better." The girl took the cat home and gave it some food and a soft bed. The cat stopped weeping and started to feel better. The cat and the girl became best friends and played with soft toys instead of hard balls.
