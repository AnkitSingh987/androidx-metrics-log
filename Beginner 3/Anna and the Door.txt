Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and the Door

Anna liked to play dress-up with her mom's clothes. She found a pretty blouse in the closet and put it on. It was too big for her, but she did not care. She felt like a princess. She wanted to show her mom how pretty she looked. She ran to the kitchen, where her mom was making lunch. She did not see the door was close. She bumped into it with a loud bang. She fell down and hurt her nose. She started to cry. Her mom heard the noise and came to see what happened. She saw Anna on the floor, with blood on her face and the blouse. She was scared and worried. She picked up Anna and hugged her. She said, "Oh, my poor baby, what did you do?" Anna sobbed and said, "I wanted to be pretty like you, mom. I'm sorry I ruined your blouse." Her mom kissed her and said, "You are always pretty, Anna. And the blouse is not important. I can wash it or buy a new one. But you are my precious girl, and I love you very much. Come on, let's clean your nose and put a band-aid on it. Then we can have some cookies and milk, okay?" Anna nodded and smiled. She felt better. She loved her mom too. She learned to be more careful with doors and clothes. She still liked to play dress-up, but she asked her mom first. Her mom sometimes let her wear her old blouses, and they had fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very adventurous. He loved to play outside and explore new things. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. The sky was dark, but Tim didn't care. He wanted to play on the swings and slide. While Tim was playing, he saw a big hill. He wanted to climb it. His mom said it was okay, so he started to climb. When he got to the top, he found a big cup. He picked it up and saw that it was full of water. Tim decided to pour the water out of the cup. As he poured the water, something unexpected happened. The water turned into a rainbow! The dark sky was now full of bright colors. Tim was so happy and surprised. He ran down the hill to tell his mom. They both looked at the rainbow and smiled. From that day on, Tim always remembered his adventurous day at the park when he found the magic cup.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big cushion on the grass. It is red and soft. "Look, a cushion!" Lily says. "Let's go and sit on it." They jog to the cushion and sit down. They feel happy and cozy. But then, a man comes. He looks angry. He says, "Hey, that's my cushion! Give it back to me!" Lily and Ben are scared. They don't know what to do. "Sorry, mister," Ben says. "We didn't know it was yours. We just wanted to sit on it." The man looks at them. He sees that they are honest and sorry. He says, "It's okay, kids. I forgive you. But please don't take things that are not yours. That's not nice." Lily and Ben nod. They say, "Thank you, mister. We won't do it again." They get up and give the cushion back to the man. They say goodbye and jog away. They learned a lesson that day. They learned to be honest and respectful.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to laugh and play. Her favorite thing in the world was red jam. She ate it every day. One sunny day, Amy went for a walk in the park. She saw a big tree with red leaves. The tree was very pretty. She walked closer and saw something strange. There was a jar of red jam on the ground near the tree. Amy was so happy and picked it up. But when she opened the jar, she heard a funny sound. It was not jam, but a tiny red bird inside! The bird started to laugh. Amy was surprised, but she laughed too. They played together all day and became best friends. And from that day on, Amy loved red birds just as much as red jam.
