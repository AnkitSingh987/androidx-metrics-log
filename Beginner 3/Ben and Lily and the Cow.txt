Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Lily and the Cow

Ben and Lily were playing in the farm with their toys. They liked to pretend they were farmers and take care of the animals. Ben had a toy cow and Lily had a toy horse. "Look at my cow, Lily. She is very big and strong. She can give us milk and cheese," Ben said. "My horse is stronger, Ben. She can run very fast and jump over fences. She can also carry us on her back," Lily said. They started to argue about which toy was better. They raised their voices and made angry faces. "Stop, stop, stop!" their mom said. She came to see what was wrong. "Why are you fighting, Ben and Lily? You both have nice toys. You should share and play nicely." "But mom, my cow is better than her horse. She is stronger and more useful," Ben said. "No, mom, my horse is better than his cow. She is faster and more fun," Lily said. Mom smiled and hugged them both. "I will explain something to you, Ben and Lily. Your toys are different, but they are both good. A cow and a horse are both animals that help farmers. A cow gives us milk and cheese, but a horse helps us plow the fields and transport things. They have different skills and abilities, but they work together. Do you understand?" Ben and Lily nodded. They felt sorry for fighting. They said sorry to each other and hugged. "Can we play together, Ben?" Lily asked. "Sure, Lily. Let's pretend we are farmers and take care of our cow and horse," Ben said. They smiled and ran to their toys. Mom watched them and smiled too. She was happy they learned to appreciate their differences and get along.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a little red rose. The rose was sad because it did not fit in with the other flowers. They were all big and tall, but the rose was small. One day, the rose met a sour bug. The bug said, "Hi, little rose. Why are you sad?" The rose said, "I am sad because I don't fit in with the other flowers. They are all big and tall, but I am small." The bug said, "Don't be sad. I can help you." The sour bug had a magic trick. It could make things grow bigger. The bug said, "Close your eyes, little rose." The rose closed its eyes. The bug did its magic trick, and the rose grew and grew. When the rose opened its eyes, it was big and tall, just like the other flowers. The rose was so happy, and the sour bug was happy too. They both fit in the garden now, and they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man who loved to play with his big stack of toy planes. He had a huge room filled with all kinds of toys. One day, he found a very special toy plane. It was bright red and shiny. The wealthy man took the toy plane outside to play. He threw it into the air, and it began to soar high up in the sky. It went up and up, higher than the trees. The man was so happy to see his toy plane fly. As the toy plane soared, it made a new friend, a little bird. The bird and the toy plane played together in the sky. They flew in circles and raced each other. At the end of the day, the toy plane came back down, and the wealthy man caught it. He was so happy that his toy plane had a fun day soaring with its new friend.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a hurt bird. The bird had a bad wing. Tim wanted to help the bird. He picked it up with his thumb and other fingers. He took the bird to his mom. "Mom, can we help this bird?" asked Tim. His mom looked at the bird and said, "Yes, we can help. But we need to be careful not to spoil the bird." Tim and his mom took care of the bird. They gave it food and water. They made a little bed for the bird to rest. The bird started to feel better. But one day, the bird did not want to fly. It liked being spoiled by Tim and his mom. It did not want to leave its little bed. Tim and his mom knew they had to teach the bird to fly again. They took the bird outside and let it try to fly. At first, the bird was scared. But Tim and his mom helped the bird feel brave. Soon, the bird was flying high in the sky. Tim and his mom were happy they could help the bird.
