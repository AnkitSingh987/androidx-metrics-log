Category: Beginner
Title: Poppy the Lively Girl and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a lively little girl named Poppy. Poppy was always happy and loved to play with her friends. One day, Poppy went to the park to play with her ball. Poppy was playing when she saw a small bird that looked sad. "Why are you sad?" Poppy asked the bird. The bird said, "I worry about my friend. He is lost and I can't find him." Poppy wanted to help the bird. She looked all around the park, but she couldn't find the lost friend. Then, something unexpected happened. Poppy saw her ball had rolled into a bush. She went to get the ball and found the bird's friend hiding there! Poppy called the bird over, "Look! I found your friend!" The bird was so happy and thanked Poppy. They all played together in the park until the sun went down. From that day on, Poppy, the bird, and the bird's friend were the best of friends, and they never had to worry again.

Anna was sick. She had a bad cough and a hot head. She did not want to go to school. She wanted to stay in bed and watch cartoons. But Mom said, "You have to see the nurse. She will help you feel better. Come on, let's go." Anna did not like the nurse. The nurse was weird. She wore a white coat and a mask. She had a big needle and a cold stethoscope. She made Anna sit on a chair and asked her many questions. "How are you feeling, Anna?" the nurse said. "I feel bad," Anna said. "I want to go home." The nurse smiled. "I know, sweetie. But I have to tell you something. You have a virus. It is making you cough and have a fever. You need to take this medicine and drink lots of water. It will make the virus go away." Anna did not like the medicine. It was bitter and sticky. She made a face and spat it out. "Yuck!" she said. "I don't want it." The nurse frowned. "You have to take it, Anna. It is good for you. If you don't, you will get worse. Do you want that?" Anna shook her head. She did not want to get worse. She wanted to feel better. She wanted to play with her friends and eat ice cream. She looked at the nurse. The nurse was not so weird. She was nice. She wanted to help Anna. Anna nodded. She opened her mouth and took the medicine. She swallowed it and drank some water. "Good girl," the nurse said. "You are very brave. Now you can go home and rest. You will be fine soon." Anna smiled. She felt a little better. She hugged the nurse and said, "Thank you." The nurse hugged her back and said, "You're welcome."

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Tom. Tom had a big house and many toys. One day, on a Tuesday, Tom's friend, Sam, came to play at his house. While they were playing, they both wanted the same toy car. Tom yelled, "No, I want it!" Sam yelled back, "But I want it too!" They both got very sad and didn't know what to do. Then, Tom's mom came into the room and saw them fighting. She said, "Why don't you both play with a toy car each?" Tom and Sam looked at each other and smiled. They both picked a toy car and played happily together.

One day, a funny dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a package on the ground. Max was curious about the package. He sniffed it and barked, "What is this?" A little girl named Lily heard Max and came over. She looked at the package and said, "This is a package, Max. Maybe there is a toy inside!" Max wagged his tail and barked happily. Lily and Max lay down on the grass and opened the package together. Inside, they found a big, bouncy ball. Max and Lily played with the ball all day long, laughing and having fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog. His name was Spike. Spike had a hard ball he loved to play with. One day, Spike saw a big cat. The cat was playing with a soft ball. Spike wanted to play with the cat. Spike said to the cat, "Hi, my name is Spike. Do you want to play with my hard ball?" The cat looked at Spike and said, "No, I like my soft ball. But we can play together with both balls." They played and played. Then, something unexpected happened. The hard ball and the soft ball bumped into each other. They stuck together! Now, the hard ball and the soft ball were one big ball. Spike and the cat looked at the big ball. They were surprised, but they liked the big ball. They played with the big ball all day long. Spike and the cat became best friends. And they always played with their big ball together.
