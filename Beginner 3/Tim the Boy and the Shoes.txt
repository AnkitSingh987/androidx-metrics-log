Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Shoes

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big box of shoes. He liked to play with the shoes. One day, Tim decided to scatter the shoes all around his room. It was fun to see the shoes everywhere. Tim's mom saw the shoes all over the room. She asked Tim to put the shoes back in the box. Tim tried to pick up a hard shoe, but it was too heavy for him. His mom helped him put the hard shoe back in the box. Together, Tim and his mom picked up all the shoes. They put the shoes back in the box. Tim learned that it was fun to scatter the shoes, but it was also important to clean up after playing. Tim was happy, and his mom was proud of him.

One day, a little ant went for a walk. The ant saw a big, interesting rock. The ant wanted to climb the rock, but it was very high. The ant was scared, but it wanted to see what was on the other side. The ant met a friendly bug. The bug said, "I can help you climb the rock." The ant was happy and said, "Yes, please." But the bug wanted to play a game first. The ant did not want to play. The ant said, "No, I want to climb the rock now." The bug did not listen and kept asking to play. The ant got upset and insisted, "No, I want to climb the rock!" The bug finally understood and helped the ant climb the interesting rock. When they reached the top, the ant and the bug saw a beautiful garden. They played together in the garden and became good friends. The ant was happy it insisted on climbing the rock, and the bug learned to listen to its friends.

Tim and Mia like to play on the beach. They make sand castles and look for shells. But today, the sky is dark and the wind is strong. They hear a loud sound. "Boom!" Tim says. "What is that?" "It is rain," Mia says. "Rain is water that falls from the sky. It makes the sand wet and the sea big." "Big sea?" Tim asks. "How big?" "Very big," Mia says. "The tide is high. The tide is when the sea goes up and down. When the tide is high, the sea comes close to the sand. When the tide is low, the sea goes far from the sand." "Can we play in the sea?" Tim asks. "No, we can't," Mia says. "The sea is too big and too fast. It can push us and hurt us. We have to be careful. We have to stay away from the sea." "OK," Tim says. "Let's go back to the house. We can play inside. We can read books and draw pictures. Maybe the rain will stop soon." "OK," Mia says. "That sounds fun. Let's go." They hold hands and run to the house. They are wet and cold, but they are happy. They like to play on the beach, but they also like to play inside. They are careful and smart. They know how to be safe.

Once there was a stubborn little dog. His name was Spot. He lived with a family, who fed him delicious treats and gave him lots of cuddles. Spot had big dreams, he wanted to explore the world! He didn't know how to make this happen, but it filled his heart with hope to even dream about it. One day Spot’s family gave him a calendar. He started to look through, imagining all the different places it could take him. He wanted to see them all so badly! Spot stubbornly decided that if he wanted to make his dreams come true then he would have to work for it. He begged his family to take him new places, and didn't give up until his dreams were realised. The moral of Spot's story is if you are stubborn and determined, you can make your dreams come true!

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse named Tim. Tim was an adventurous mouse. He liked to explore and find new things. One day, Tim went out to play in the big yard. While playing, Tim saw a big trap. He was curious and wanted to see what it was. Tim went closer and closer to the trap. He touched the trap with his little paw, and it started to close. Tim jumped back quickly and said, "Oh no! That was scary!" Tim ran back to his mommy and told her about the trap. His mommy said, "Tim, you must be careful when you play. Some things can be dangerous." Tim understood and promised to be more careful. From that day on, Tim still had fun exploring, but he always remembered to be safe.
