Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Dog

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. It was a mild day, so Tim wore his favorite sweater. At the park, they saw a little dog. The dog was cute and had a red ball. Tim's mom said, "Tim, kneel down, and the dog will come to you." Tim knelt down, and the dog ran to him. The dog dropped the red ball near Tim's feet. Tim picked up the ball and threw it. The dog ran after the ball and brought it back to Tim. Tim and the dog played for a long time. When it was time to go home, Tim said, "Bye, dog! I hope I see you again!" The dog wagged its tail and barked. Tim and his mom walked home, and Tim couldn't wait to come back to the park to play with his new friend.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Joe and Sam. Joe was the bigger of the two, because he was always eating tomato sandwiches! Sam was much smaller and never seemed to weigh much. One day, Joe and Sam went to the beach together. When they got there, Sam was very excited. He wanted to go for a swim, but Joe said he should weigh himself first to make sure he was safe. So Sam stepped onto the scales, and he was so small that it didn't even move! Joe laughed and said it was probably better for him to stay on the sand. But Sam was very frightened- he didn't want to miss out on all the fun. Joe saw how upset Sam was and came up with a plan. He put a few tomatoes on the scales and it finally moved! Sam was relieved and they both went for a lovely swim together.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big net on the ground. Tim thought it would be fun to try to catch a ball with the net. He asked his friend, Sam, to play with him. "Hey Sam, want to try to catch the ball with this net?" Tim asked. Sam said, "Yes, that sounds fun!" Tim and Sam played with the net and the ball. They tried very hard to catch the ball, but it was tough. They were not very good at it. They kept trying and trying, but the ball always fell out of the net. They did not give up. Just when they thought they would never catch the ball, a big wind came. It blew the ball high up into the air. Tim and Sam watched as the ball flew up and up. Then, something unexpected happened. A bird grabbed the ball in its beak and flew away! Tim and Sam looked at each other and laughed. They did not catch the ball, but they had a fun time trying.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a pair of pants that he loved very much. They were red and had big pockets. Every day, Tim would put on his pants and walk to the park to play. One day, Tim met a bossy squirrel at the park. The squirrel said, "Hey, kid! You have to walk around me to play here." Tim was not happy, but he did what the squirrel said. The squirrel then took Tim's pants and ran up a tree. Tim was very sad and asked the squirrel, "Please, can I have my pants back?" The squirrel thought for a moment and said, "Okay, but you must promise to be my friend and play with me." Tim agreed, and the squirrel gave his pants back. From that day on, Tim and the bossy squirrel became best friends. They walked and played together every day at the park. Tim learned that sometimes, even bossy friends can be fun to play with.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a fair. The fair had a fun track for kids to race on. Tim and Sam were two boys who wanted to race on the track. Tim said, "I want to race you, Sam!" Sam agreed and they both got ready to race. They both wanted to win the race and have fun. As they raced, Tim started to run very fast. Sam tried to catch up, but he could not. Sam got mad and pushed Tim. Tim fell down and got hurt. The race was not fun anymore. Sam said, "I'm sorry, Tim." But Tim was still sad and hurt. The fair was not fun for them anymore. They both went home with a bad ending to their day.
