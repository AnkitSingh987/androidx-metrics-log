Category: Beginner
Title: Bobo the New Toy and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a new toy named Bobo. Bobo had one arm. He wanted to find his other arm. He looked everywhere, but he could not find it. Bobo felt sad. One day, a friend named Lulu came to help Bobo. They looked for the arm together. They looked under the bed and behind the door. Then, they saw a bird outside the window. The bird had Bobo's arm! Lulu had an idea. She asked all the friends to unite and help Bobo. They made a big ladder with their bodies. Bobo climbed up the ladder and got his arm back from the bird. The bird was sorry and became friends with Bobo and Lulu. Bobo was happy to have his arm and new friends.

Once upon a time there was a perfect body. It was so perfect that everyone admired it. One day, something bit the body. It hurt so much! The body didn't know what had bitten it. It tried to figure it out, but it couldn't. The pain kept increasing. In the end, the body ended up very sick and it had to go to the doctor. The doctor told the body that it had been bitten by a spider. The bite was so bad that it ruined the perfect body.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful girl named Lily. She loved to play in her big garden every day. The garden had pretty flowers, green grass, and tall trees. One sunny day, Lily saw a little duck in her garden. The duck was lost and sad. Lily wanted to help the duck find its way home. She held the duck's hand and said, "Don't worry, I will lead you home." Lily and the duck walked through the garden. They passed the pretty flowers, green grass, and tall trees. Soon, they found the duck's family near a pond. The duck was so happy to see its family again. Lily felt happy too. She waved goodbye to the duck and went back to her cheerful garden.

Tom and Mia liked to play with cards. They had many cards with pictures of animals, fruits, and colors. They would take turns to show a card and say what it was. If they said it right, they would keep the card. If they said it wrong, they would give the card to the other. One day, Tom showed a card with a picture of a snake. He said, "This is a snake. It is long and flexible. It can bend and twist." Mia nodded. She knew what a snake was. She had seen one in a book. Then, Mia showed a card with a picture of a banana. She said, "This is a banana. It is yellow and sweet. You can peel and eat it." Tom shook his head. He did not know what a banana was. He had never seen one before. He said, "No, this is not a banana. This is a moon. It is yellow and round. You can see it at night." Mia laughed. She said, "No, silly. This is not a moon. This is a banana. You are wrong. Give me the card." Tom did not like to be wrong. He did not like to give the card. He was angry. He said, "No, you are wrong. This is a moon. I am right. I keep the card." He slapped the card out of Mia's hand. Mia was shocked. She did not like to be slapped. She was sad. She said, "Ow, that hurt. Why did you do that? That was not nice." Tom felt bad. He saw that Mia was sad. He did not want to make her sad. He said, "I'm sorry, Mia. I was wrong. This is a banana. You are right. You can have the card." He gave the card to Mia. Mia smiled. She said, "Thank you, Tom. I forgive you. You are nice." They hugged and continued to play with cards. They learned new words and had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a quiet vendor. He sold red apples. The vendor liked to help people. He was very nice. One day, a little girl came to the vendor. She was sad. She had no money to buy an apple. The quiet vendor saw her sad face. He wanted to help the little girl. The vendor offered the girl a big red apple. The girl smiled and said thank you. She ate the apple and was happy. The quiet vendor was happy too. They both had a good day.
