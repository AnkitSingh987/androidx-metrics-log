Category: Beginner
Title: The Generous Sun Shone and the Tree

One day, a generous sun shone in the sky. It made everything warm and happy. The sun saw a big tree and said, "Hello, Tree! I will give you lots of warm light so you can grow big and strong." The tree was happy and said, "Thank you, Sun! I will grow big and strong with your help." The sun smiled and shone its light on the tree every day. One day, a little bird came to the tree. The bird was tired and wanted to rest. The tree said, "Little Bird, come sit on my branch. I will keep you safe." The bird was happy and sat on the branch. The bird started to sing a beautiful song. The song was so beautiful that the leaves on the tree started to dance. The leaves danced so much that they started to slap each other. They laughed and played all day. The sun, the tree, and the bird were all happy together.

Once upon a time, there was a small car. The car was dull and old. It lived in a big garage with its family. The garage was a warm and safe place for them. One day, the car went for a ride. It was a long ride, and the car got tired. It wanted to go back to the garage and settle down for the night. The car found its way back to the garage. It settled down next to its family. They were all happy to be together again. They slept well in the garage, and the car had sweet dreams.

One day, a boy named Tom got a new bicycle. He was very happy. He rode the bicycle up and down the street. His friend, Sue, saw him and wanted to ride too. Tom and Sue rode their bicycles together. They laughed and joked as they raced each other. They had a lot of fun. But then they saw a big, angry dog. The angry dog barked and ran after them. Tom and Sue were scared, but they rode their bicycles very fast. The dog could not catch them. They got away and went back home. Tom and Sue were safe and happy. They told their moms and dads about the angry dog. They still had fun riding their bicycles and joking together.

Once there was a little boy who loved to write. He had a special bowl to hold his pencils and some paper. One day, he had a tough day at school. He was so tired, he couldn't write a single word. He thought he could try tomorrow. Suddenly, his special bowl fell and broke into tiny pieces. He felt so sad and couldn't write anymore. He had lost his special bowl, and it was too tough to mend. He was so upset that he never wrote again.

Once upon a time, there was a big billboard. It was dark outside. The billboard had a picture of a cat on it. A little boy named Tim saw the billboard. He asked his mom, "Why is the cat so big?" His mom did not know. She just gave a little shrug. Tim liked the cat on the billboard. He smiled and waved at it. The dark night was not so scary anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty dog named Max. Max was big and strong. He liked to help his friends. One day, Max saw a lot of waste on the ground. Max did not like waste. He wanted to make the world clean and pretty. Max started to pick up the waste. He put it all in a big bag. Max's friends saw him and wanted to help, too. They all picked up waste together. Soon, the ground was clean and pretty again. Max and his friends were happy. They knew that they had done a good thing. The waste was gone and their home was clean. In the end, Max and his friends had a big party to celebrate. They played and had fun, knowing they had helped their world.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big rock that he loved to play with. One day, Tim's rock was gone. He was sad and started a search to find his rock. Tim looked under his bed and asked his cat, "Did you see my rock?" The cat said, "No, I did not see your rock." Tim felt frightened because he thought he would not find his favorite rock. Then, he looked in the yard and saw his dog playing with the rock. Tim was so happy! He said, "Thank you, dog, for finding my rock!" Tim took his rock and played with it all day long.
