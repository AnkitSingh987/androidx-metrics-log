Category: Beginner
Title: The Fairy and the Forest

Once upon a time there lived a fairy who lived in a deep forest. One day, the worried fairy walked through the trees and came across two 3 year old children who were arguing. The first child said "That toy is mine!" The second child said "It's not, it's mine!" The fairy fluttered her wings and flew down between them. "What is all this disagreeing about?" she asked. The first child said "She said the toy is hers, but I said it's mine!" The fairy smiled and said "It looks like the best thing to do is share. Why don't you both use it together?" The children thought about it and smiled too. They agreed to share the toy and they were no longer worried. The fairy smiled and fluttered away.

One day, a little girl named Mia wanted to bake a cake. She put on her apron and went to the kitchen. Mia asked her mom, "Can you help me bake the cake, please?" Her mom said, "Of course, let's make a yummy cake together!" Mia and her mom used a modern oven to bake the cake. The oven had big buttons and was easy to use. Mia was very excited to see the cake when it was done. She said, "I can't wait to eat the cake!" After the cake was baked, they took it out of the oven. Mia saw a magnet on the fridge. The magnet was shaped like a star. She had an idea! She asked her mom, "Can we put the star magnet on the cake?" Her mom laughed and said, "No, sweetie! Magnets are not for eating. Let's use a real star-shaped candy instead." Mia smiled and put the candy on the cake. They both enjoyed the cake and had a fun day baking together.

Lily and Ben like to play in the sand. They create castles and roads and bridges with their buckets and shovels. One day, they see a big pit in the sand. It is very deep and dark. "Who made this pit?" Lily asks. "I don't know. Maybe a dog or a big bird," Ben says. "Let's go inside and see," Lily says. She is curious and brave. "No, don't go. It is bad. It is dangerous. You can fall and hurt yourself," Ben says. He is scared and careful. But Lily does not listen. She runs to the pit and jumps in. She screams. She cannot see anything. She cannot get out. She is stuck. "Help! Help!" Lily cries. Ben hears her. He runs to the pit. He sees her. He is worried. "Lily, are you okay? Hold on, I will help you," Ben says. He grabs a rope from his bag. He throws it to Lily. She catches it. He pulls her up. She is dirty and scared. She hugs him. "Thank you, Ben. You are a good friend. I am sorry I did not listen to you. The pit was bad. It was not fun," Lily says. Ben smiles. He is happy. He hugs her back. "It's okay, Lily. I am glad you are safe. Next time, let's create something else. Something good. Something fun," Ben says. They walk away from the pit. They find a nice spot in the sand. They create a big cake with candles and flowers. They share it and laugh. They are happy. They are friends.

Once there was a sweet and friendly girl named Sally. Sally had a plan. She wanted to organize a fun day at the lake. Sally gathered all of her family and friends and they took a kayak to the lake. They had so much fun! They raced each other in their kayaks and splashed around in the lake. They ate some yummy snacks and drank sweet fruit punch. Everyone had a great time! When the day was over, Sally had organized the best day ever!

Once there was a famous little girl called Sam. One day, Sam wanted to pay the sky. She asked her mum: "Mum, can I pay the sky?" Her mum shook her head and said: "No, you can't pay the sky, Sam!". Sam wasn't going to give up. She thought of a plan. She asked her mum again: "Mum, can I pay with a painting?". Her mum replied: "That's a great idea, Sam!" So, Sam took her paintbrush and paper and began to paint the most beautiful picture of the sky, with the sun shining down on it. She proudly showed her mum her masterpiece. Her mum was very pleased and said: "Sam, that's wonderful! You have paid the sky with your beautiful painting".
