Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Turtle

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. Lily loved to camp with her mom and dad. They would go to a big park with trees and a lake. Lily enjoyed playing with a ball and swimming in the lake. One day, Lily saw a slow turtle near the camp. The turtle was green and had a hard shell. Lily liked the turtle and named it Tom. She would give Tom leaves to eat and watch him walk very slow. At night, Lily, her mom, and her dad would sit by the campfire. They would eat yummy food and sing fun songs. Lily enjoyed the camp and her new friend, Tom the turtle. She hoped to come back to the camp again soon.

---

Sara and Tom are playing in the kitchen. They like to pretend they are cooking and eating. Sara has a red dish and Tom has a blue dish. They fill their dishes with toy food and sit at the table. "Listen, Tom," Sara says. "I made you a yummy soup. It has carrots and peas and noodles. Do you want to try it?" Tom nods and takes a spoon. He pretends to taste the soup and makes a funny face. "Blech!" he says. "This soup is yucky. It has too much salt. I don't like it." Sara feels sad. She worked hard to make the soup. She thinks Tom is being rude. She says, "Well, I like my soup. It is tasty and warm. You are missing out. If you don't want it, I will eat it." She picks up her red dish and moves to another chair. She starts to eat her soup with a smile. Tom watches her and feels sorry. He thinks maybe he was too mean. He says, "Sara, I'm sorry. I was just joking. Your soup looks good. Can I have some?" Sara looks at him and thinks for a moment. She decides to forgive him. She says, "Okay, Tom. You can have some. But you have to say please and thank you. And you have to listen to me when I talk to you. Deal?" Tom agrees and says, "Deal. Please can I have some soup, Sara? Thank you for sharing with me. I will listen to you better." Sara smiles and gives him some soup. They eat and talk and have fun. They are happy to be friends.

Once upon a time there was a small girl who loved to smile. Whenever she saw something nice or received a present, she would give the biggest smile. One day, the girl felt an urge to go on an adventure. She was so excited and filled with happiness that she couldn't help but smile. So she set off to explore the world. Along the way, she came across a beautiful lake. When she saw it, she was so amazed and overwhelmed that she couldn't help but smile even bigger. She decided to take a dip in the lake, so she took off her clothes and jumped in. Once she was in the lake, she noticed a lot of people seemed to be staring at her. She was embarrassed and quickly swam back to shore. To her surprise, she saw that the people were smiling. They were smiling because they were touched by her bravery and courage. Not only did the little girl go on an adventure, but she also discovered that smiles can be shared and appreciated by all. She felt full of joy, which made her smile even bigger and brighter.

Tim and Lily were playing in the park. They saw a big tower made of blocks. They wanted to climb it and see how high they could go. "Let's go to the tower!" Tim said. "OK, but be careful!" Lily said. They ran to the tower and started to climb. They were having fun, but they did not see the dark clouds in the sky. Soon, it started to rain. The rain was heavy and cold. It soaked their clothes and hair. The blocks became wet and slippery. "Oh no, the rain is bad!" Lily said. "We have to go down!" Tim said. But as they tried to go down, the tower started to fall. The blocks were cheap and weak. They could not hold the weight of the rain and the children. The tower crashed to the ground with a loud noise. Tim and Lily fell with it. They were not hurt, but they were very wet and dirty. They got up and looked at the mess. The tower was gone. The blocks were broken and scattered. They felt sad and angry. "Who made this tower? It was cheap and bad!" Tim said. "I don't know, but they should make a better one!" Lily said. They ran to their mom, who was waiting for them with an umbrella. She saw them and smiled. "Did you have fun, my darlings?" she asked. They shook their heads and told her what happened. She hugged them and said. "I'm sorry, that was not a good tower. But don't worry, we can make a new one at home. We have good blocks and glue. And we can dry your clothes and hair. Come on, let's go home!" They nodded and followed her. They still felt sad, but they also felt hopeful. They wanted to make a new tower, a better one. A tower that would not fall or soak. A tower that they could be proud of.
