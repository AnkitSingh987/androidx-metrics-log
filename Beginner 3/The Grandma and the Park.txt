Category: Beginner
Title: The Grandma and the Park

Once there was a grandma who was very wealthy. She was really proud of her wealth. Grandma wanted to give her grandchild something special. So she gave him some gum. The little boy was really excited, he ran quickly to the park to play with his friends and show them his gum. They were all so excited to have some gum, but the little boy refused to share with them. "It's all mine!" he said. His friends weren't happy so they started to argue. "You have to share!" one of they said. But the little boy shook his head. The friends kept arguing and the little boy was becoming more and more angry. Suddenly he shouted, "No one can solve this! It's all mine!" But then his grandma appeared. "I'm sorry, sweetheart, you must share with your friends," she said. But it was too late. The little boy had spoiled the fun and the friends had gone home. Now the little boy was alone with his grandma's gum. He had wanted to show off his wealth, but all he had now was an empty bag of gum.

One day, a big dog and a little cat went for a walk. They saw a clean, green hill. The dog said, "Let's go up the hill!" The cat said, "Yes, let's go!" So they went up the hill. At the top of the hill, they saw a big tree. The dog saw a stick and said, "I want to play with the stick!" The dog tried to snap the stick with his teeth, but it was too big. The cat said, "Let me help you!" They both tried to snap the stick, but it was still too big. Then, something unexpected happened. The stick moved! It was not a stick at all! It was a long, green snake! The snake said, "Why did you try to snap me?" The dog and the cat were very sorry. They said, "We thought you were a stick. We are sorry." The snake forgave them, and they all became friends. They played on the clean, green hill together, and had a great day.

One day, a lonely napkin sat on the table. The napkin was sad because no one used it. It wanted a friend to play with. It looked around and saw a spoon, a fork, and a plate. They all looked happy. The napkin said, "Hi, can I play with you?" The spoon, fork, and plate looked at the napkin and said, "Yes, you can play with us!" The napkin was so happy to have friends. They played all day and had lots of fun. When the day was about to end, the napkin said, "Thank you for playing with me. I'm not lonely anymore." The spoon, fork, and plate said, "We are happy to be your friends." And they all smiled and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a panda. The panda was weak. He did not play with his friends. He sat under a big tree. He felt sad. One day, a little bird saw the panda. The bird was worried. She wanted to help the panda. She flew down and sat next to him. She asked, "Why are you sad?" The panda said, "I am weak. I can't play with my friends." The bird thought hard. She said, "We will find a way to make you strong." The bird and the panda went to find food. They ate a lot. The panda started to feel better. He was not weak anymore. He was happy. The panda and the bird became good friends. They played together every day. The panda was not sad anymore. He was strong and happy. And the bird was happy too, because she helped her friend. They lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a regular boy named Tom. Tom had a black toy car that he loved very much. Every day, he would play with his black car and make it go fast. One day, Tom saw a big red wagon in the park. He wanted to put his black car in the wagon and pull it. Tom put his black car in the red wagon and started to pull it with a big smile on his face. As Tom pulled the red wagon, his black car went for a fun ride. He felt happy and proud. Tom played in the park all day with his black car and the red wagon. At the end of the day, Tom went home with his black car, ready to have more fun tomorrow.
