Category: Beginner
Title: The Cat and the Tree

One day, a little cat went for a walk. The cat saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. The cat climbed up and up, but then it got hurt. The cat was sad and cried for help. A kind girl heard the cat cry. She looked up and saw the hurt cat in the tree. The girl wanted to help the cat. She called her dad to come and see. The dad looked at the cat and nodded. He knew how to help the hurt cat. The dad got a long stick. He reached up with the stick and helped the cat come down. The cat was so happy to be safe. The girl and her dad took the hurt cat home. They took care of the cat until it was all better. The cat was happy and loved its new family.

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys in their room. But Sara is tidy and Ben is messy. Sara puts her toys in the chest when she is done. Ben leaves his toys on the floor. One day, Sara is angry at Ben. She says, "Ben, you are messy. You make our room dirty. You need to put your toys in the chest like me." Ben says, "No, I don't. I like my toys on the floor. I can find them easily. You are bossy. You need to let me play how I want." They start to argue. They shout and push each other. They knock over the chest. The toys spill out. They make a big mess. Mom hears the noise. She comes to their room. She sees the mess. She is not happy. She says, "Sara and Ben, what are you doing? Why are you fighting? Why are you making a mess? You need to clean up your room and say sorry to each other." Sara and Ben feel bad. They know they were wrong. They say sorry to Mom and to each other. They hug and make up. They help each other to clean up their room. They put their toys in the chest. They support each other to be tidy. Mom is proud of them. She says, "Good job, Sara and Ben. You are good twins. You are good helpers. You are good friends." She gives them a kiss and a hug. They smile and say, "We love you, Mom."

Once upon a time, there was a kind boy named Sam who was very curious about the world. One day, Sam decided to explore the forest. As Sam was walking through the forest, a big cloud appeared in the sky. It brought lots of heat to the forest. Sam was so amazed by the beauty of the forest. Suddenly, a wonderful animal came out from the bushes. Sam was so excited to see the animal and he wanted to know more about it. He discovered that the animal was a fox, and it was so kind to Sam. They played together, and the fox gave Sam a big hug. It was so amazing! Sam thought that what he experienced was more valuable than any of the shiny treasures he could have found in the forest. He realized that the best things in life can appear when you least expect them. The heat of the forest disappeared, and the fox went back to its home in the woods, but Sam was grateful for the amazing gift.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to joke and play with his friends. One day, Tim and his friends wanted to test who could jump the highest. They all took turns jumping. Some friends jumped high, but Tim was weak and could not jump as high as the others. Tim felt sad because he wanted to jump high too. But then, Tim had an idea. He told a funny joke and made all his friends laugh. They all forgot about the test and played together happily. Tim was happy too, because even though he was weak, he could still make his friends laugh.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a good friend, a marble. The marble was very pretty and shiny. Tim and the marble liked to play games together. They would roll and bounce all day long. One day, Tim and the marble went to the park. They were having so much fun. But then, the marble started to roll away. It was trying to escape! Tim ran after the marble as fast as he could. He did not want to lose his good friend. Tim chased the marble, but it was too fast. The marble rolled down a hill and into a big hole. Tim was very sad. He could not get his good friend, the marble, out of the hole. And so, Tim and the marble could not play together anymore.
