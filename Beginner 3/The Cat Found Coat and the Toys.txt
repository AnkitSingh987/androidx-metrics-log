Category: Beginner
Title: The Cat Found Coat and the Toys

One day, a nice cat found a coat. The coat was big and red. The cat put on the coat and felt very warm. The nice cat liked the coat a lot. It made her feel happy. The nice cat saw a sack. The sack was near a tree. The cat went to the sack and looked inside. In the sack, there were many toys. The cat liked the toys and played with them. The nice cat took the sack and the coat. She went to her friends, the dog and the bird. They all played with the toys from the sack. They were very happy. The nice cat shared her coat with her friends, and they all felt warm and cozy.

Once upon a time, there was a big, soft couch in a small house. A little boy named Timmy loved to play on the couch. One day, Timmy saw a small box under the couch. He thought, "What is in the box?" The next day, Timmy played on the couch again. He saw the box and remembered it. Timmy picked up the box and started to shake it. He heard a sound inside the box. He was very excited to find out what was in the box. Timmy opened the box and found a small toy car inside. He was very happy to have a new toy. He played with the toy car on the soft couch all day long. The box under the couch was a fun surprise for Timmy.

Once upon a time, there was a fish named Sam. He was a very bright fish and always wanted to go on adventurous trips. One day, Sam suggested going to the deep dark depths of the sea. His friends were hesitant, but they decided to go with him. Along the way, they noticed something unusual. The water began to get colder and darker until they could no longer see. That's when they heard a loud growl. Sam then realized it was the Big Scary Monster that lurked at the bottom of the ocean. Sam's friends were scared and told him to turn around. "No," said Sam, "I need to see this." His friends suggested that he go back, but Sam ignored them and kept swimming forward. But it was too late. The Big Scary Monster snatched Sam in its jaws and disappeared into the darkness. Sam's friends were so sad and cried for a long time. It was a bright idea, but it ended in a bad way.

Tom and Lily were playing with their toys in the living room. Tom had a big pirate ship with many pirates and cannons. Lily had a small doll with a pink dress and a hat. Tom liked his pirate ship very much, but he also wanted to play with Lily's doll. "Can I have your doll for a while?" Tom asked Lily. "No, this is my doll. You have your pirate ship," Lily said. "But I want to try your doll. It is so pretty and soft," Tom said. He reached out his hand and tried to grab the doll from Lily. Lily held on to her doll and pulled it back. They both started to tug and shout. "Let go of my doll! It is mine!" Lily cried. "Give me your doll! I want it!" Tom yelled. Their mom heard the noise and came to the living room. She saw Tom and Lily fighting over the doll. She was not happy. "Stop it, both of you! Why are you fighting over a toy?" she asked. "Tom wants to take my doll. He is jealous of my doll," Lily said. "I just want to play with her doll. She is selfish with her doll," Tom said. Mom shook her head and took the doll and the pirate ship from them. She put them on a high shelf. "Neither of you can play with these toys until you learn to share and be kind to each other. Fighting and grabbing are not nice. You should respect each other's things and feelings. How would you like it if someone took your toy without asking?" she said. Tom and Lily felt ashamed and sorry. They realized they had been mean and rude to each other. They looked at each other and said: "I'm sorry, Lily. I should not have tried to grab your doll. It is your doll and you love it." "I'm sorry, Tom. I should not have been selfish with my doll. You can play with it if you ask nicely." They hugged and made up. Mom smiled and gave them back their toys. She was proud of them. "Good job, Tom and Lily. You have learned a valuable lesson. Sharing and being kind are better than fighting and being jealous. Now you can play together and have fun," she said. Tom and Lily thanked mom and played with their toys. They took turns with the doll and the pirate ship. They had more fun when they shared and were kind. They were happy.
