Category: Beginner
Title: The Large Chair and the Grass

Once upon a time, there was a large chair in a big room. The chair was very big and very blue. It was the favorite chair of a little girl named Lily. One day, Lily wanted to create something special. She found some paper and colors. Lily started to draw a picture of her large chair. She used blue for the chair and green for the grass around it. When Lily finished her picture, she was very happy. She showed her picture to her mom and dad. They were very proud of Lily and her beautiful drawing. They put the picture on the wall, and everyone could see Lily's large chair.

One day, a fox named Red saw a big race in the forest. Red wanted to win the race. He thought he was very fast. But there was a problem. Red's tummy was empty. He needed to find food to have the energy to run fast. Red looked for food. He saw a big apple tree. He tried to jump and get the apples, but they were too high. Red was sad. He wanted to win the race, but he was still hungry. Then, Red saw a little girl with a basket of food. The girl saw that Red was hungry and gave him some food. Red ate the food and felt strong. He ran fast in the race and won. Red was so happy that he won the race and made a new friend.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a fat cat named Tom. Tom loved to cook for his friends. One day, he decided to cook a big meal for all of them. Tom went to the kitchen and saw a lot of dust on the table. "Oh no!" he said. He cleaned the dust and started to cook. He made yummy soup, tasty bread, and sweet cake. He was very happy with his cooking. Soon, his friends came to the house. They saw the food and said, "Wow, Tom! You are a great cook!" They all ate the food and had a fun time. At the end of the day, they thanked Tom for the delicious meal and went back to their homes. Tom felt proud and happy that he could make his friends smile.

Once upon a time, there was a cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with yarn. One day, he found a ball of fine yarn. It was red, like a big apple. Tom was very happy. Tom took the yarn and started to twist it. He twisted it around and around. The yarn made a fun game for Tom. He jumped and rolled with the yarn. It made him laugh. After a while, Tom was tired. He lay down next to the twisted yarn. He felt good and sleepy. Tom took a long nap, dreaming of more fun days with his fine yarn. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim went for a walk with his mom. They liked to step on the ground and hear the crunch of leaves. Tim felt happy when he walked with his mom. One day, Tim stepped on something sticky. "Ew!" he said. It was gum. Tim felt uncomfortable with gum on his shoe. His mom helped him take the gum off. They put the gum in the trash. After that, Tim and his mom kept walking. They stepped on more leaves and laughed. Tim was happy again. They went home and told dad about the gum. They all laughed and had a fun day.

One day, a small boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy food for dinner. Tim liked to help his mom. He wanted to make it easy for her. At the store, Tim's mom asked him to find a jar of olives. Tim looked and looked, but he could not find the olives. He started to struggle. A nice man saw Tim and asked, "Do you need help?" Tim said, "Yes, I need to find olives for my mom." The man took Tim to a secret room in the store. Inside, there were many jars of olives. The man said, "These are magic olives. They can talk!" Tim was surprised. He took a jar of magic olives and said, "Thank you!" He went back to his mom and they went home. They had a fun dinner with their new talking olive friends.
