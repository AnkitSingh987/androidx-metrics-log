Category: Beginner
Title: The Bright Sun and the Puddle

Once upon a time, there was a bright sun in the sky. It was a warm day. A little girl named Mia was playing outside with her pen and paper. Mia liked to draw pictures with her pen. She drew the sun, the trees, and her house. She was very happy. Mia saw a big puddle from the rain last night. She had an idea. She took her pen and put it in the puddle. The pen started to soak up the water. It was fun to watch the pen get wet. Mia thought that maybe the pen could make new colors now. Mia took her wet pen and started to draw on her paper. The pen made bright colors on the paper. The colors mixed together and made a beautiful picture. Mia was very proud of her art. She showed her mom and dad, and they loved it too. They hung it on the wall for everyone to see.

Tim and Kim are friends who like to play at the pond. They see many ducks swimming and quacking. Tim and Kim want to feed the ducks some bread. They each have a big piece of bread in their hands. "Look, Kim, that duck is so big and pretty!" Tim says, pointing at a white duck with a red face. "I want to give him my bread. He is the best duck." "No, Tim, that duck is not the best duck. He is a mean duck. He always bites the other ducks and takes their bread. He is not humble. He is greedy." Kim says, shaking her head. "I like that duck over there. He is small and brown, but he is kind and shares his bread. He is humble. He is the best duck." Tim and Kim start to argue. They both think their duck is the best duck. They do not want to share their bread with the other duck. They raise their voices and frown at each other. The ducks hear Tim and Kim arguing. They stop swimming and quacking. They look at Tim and Kim with sad eyes. They do not like to see their friends fight. They wish Tim and Kim would be humble and share their bread with all the ducks. One duck, the small and brown one, swims up to Tim and Kim. He quacks softly and nudges their hands with his beak. He wants them to stop arguing and be friends again. He shows them that he does not care which duck gets the bread. He just wants them to be happy. Tim and Kim look at the small and brown duck. They feel ashamed. They realize they were being mean and greedy, like the white duck with the red face. They say sorry to each other and to the duck. They tear their bread into small pieces and throw them into the pond. They watch the ducks eat and swim and quack. They smile and hug each other. They are humble and happy.

Once upon a time, in a big blue sea, there was a purple jellyfish. The jellyfish was sad. It did not have any friends. All the other fish would swim away when they saw the jellyfish. They were scared of it. One day, a little fish came to the jellyfish. The little fish was not scared. It said, "Why are you sad?" The jellyfish said, "I have no friends. Everyone is scared of me." The little fish did not like to see the jellyfish sad. It wanted to help. The little fish went to the other fish and said, "Do not be scared of the jellyfish. It is nice and just wants to be friends." Some fish did not want to listen. They would complain and swim away. But some fish listened to the little fish. They went to the jellyfish and became friends. The purple jellyfish was not sad anymore. It had friends and they all played happily in the big blue sea.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends called Alex and Rosa. They were in the same class at school and were always playing together. One day, in the playground, Alex wanted to play a game with Rosa. Rosa did not want to, but Alex wanted to fight. Rosa said, "No, Alex! We don't fight - we're friends!" But Alex insisted, saying "Come on! Let's fight - I'm ready!" Rosa shook her head and said, "No Alex, we are in the same class and we are best friends - let's go play something else instead". Alex smiled and said, "OK! You're right, we really should be friends." The two friends hugged and went off to play a different game. Alex and Rosa have stayed best friends ever since.
