Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Lily and the Ball

Ben and Lily were friends who liked to play together. They had a club in the park, where they kept their toys and books. One day, they went to the club after school, but they could not find their favorite ball. It was red and shiny, and they loved to kick it and throw it. "Where is our ball?" Ben asked. "I don't know. Maybe someone took it," Lily said. They were very sad and angry. They decided to search for their ball in the park. They looked behind the trees, under the benches, and in the trash cans. They asked the other kids, but no one had seen their ball. They were about to give up, when they saw a boy sitting on a swing. He was holding their ball in his hands. He was smiling and bouncing it. "Hey, that's our ball!" Ben shouted. "Give it back!" Lily said. The boy looked at them and shook his head. "No, it's mine now. I found it in the grass. Finders keepers!" Ben and Lily felt hurt and angry. They wanted to grab their ball from the boy, but they knew that was wrong. They thought of a better way to solve the problem. They went to the boy and said, "We are sorry, but that ball is ours. We left it in our club, but someone must have moved it. We searched for it everywhere. It is very special to us. We can share it with you, if you want. Do you want to play with us?" The boy looked at them and felt ashamed. He realized he had been selfish and mean. He said, "I'm sorry, too. I should have asked you before I took your ball. It is very nice and shiny. I wanted to play with it, but I didn't have any friends. Can I join your club?" Ben and Lily smiled and nodded. They said, "Sure, you can join our club. We can be friends and play together. Sharing is more fun than keeping." They gave the boy a hug and took him to their club. They played with their ball and their other toys. They had a lot of fun and learned a valuable lesson. They learned that it is wrong to take things that belong to others, and that it is better to share and be kind.

Once upon a time, there was a kid named Tim. Tim liked to play with his friends outside. The sky was clear and the sun was bright. They would run, jump, and enjoy their time together. One day, Tim saw a new kid in the park. The new kid was sad because he had no friends. Tim thought about it and decided to ask the new kid to play with them. The new kid was very happy and they all played together. As they played, Tim learned that when you are kind and share with others, everyone can enjoy and be happy. The moral of the story is to always be kind and make new friends.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the beach with her mom. They played in the sand and Lily liked to wave at the boats in the water. Her mom said, "Wave to the nice boat, Lily!" and Lily waved with a big smile on her face. That night, Lily had a bad dream, a nightmare. In her dream, she saw a big, dead fish on the beach. It did not move or make any noise. Lily was scared and woke up crying. Her mom came into her room and hugged her tight. "It's okay, Lily. It was just a bad dream," her mom said. The next day, they went back to the beach and Lily was happy again. She waved at the boats and played in the sand with her mom. And that night, Lily had a good dream about the beach and the boats.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a little girl named Emma. Emma loved to use her hands to make things. One day, she decided to make cookies with her mom. They used a big bowl and put in all the things they needed. Emma was so happy to make cookies. Emma and her mom mixed and mixed the cookie dough. They made round cookie shapes and put them on a tray. Then, they put the tray in the hot oven. Emma could feel the hot air when the oven door opened. She was so excited for the cookies to be done. After a little while, the cookies were ready. Emma and her mom took them out of the hot oven. They smelled so good! Emma could not wait to eat a cookie. But first, they had to cool down. When the cookies were not too hot, Emma took a big bite. The cookie was so yummy! Emma and her mom were very happy. They loved making cookies together.
