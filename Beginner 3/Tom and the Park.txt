Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and the Park

One day, a boy named Tom went to the park. He wanted to see something fun. He walked and walked until he saw his friend, Sam. Sam was eating a spicy snack. Tom saw Sam's red face and laughed. Sam said, "Do you want to hear a joke?" Tom nodded and smiled. Sam told the joke, and they both laughed. They thought the joke was very funny. Tom and Sam played and laughed all day long. As the sun went down, Tom and Sam said goodbye. They were happy they got to see each other and share a joke. Tom went home and told his mom the spicy joke. She laughed too. Tom felt good knowing he made his mom smile.

Once there was a little duck named Ducky. Ducky was a very happy duck and loved to swim in the pond. One day, Ducky decided to rise up and get a better sight of the pond. He was excited to see more of the pond. But the pond was very big and high, so Ducky did not know how he could rise up so high. Then suddenly, he saw a beautiful blue balloon in the sky. The balloon was expensive to buy, but Ducky knew he had to get it so he can rise up and see more of the pond. So Ducky swam to the pond bank and used all his strength to buy the balloon. After he was done, he grabbed the balloon and jumped into the air. Ducky started to rise up, higher and higher until he could see the entire pond! He was so excited to see the sight from the sky. From up there, the pond looked amazing and Ducky was very happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim had an orange wallet. He loved his wallet very much. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He played with his friends and had lots of fun. But when it was time to go home, Tim could not find his orange wallet. He was very sad. Tim and his mom looked all around the park. They asked people if they had seen the wallet. A little girl named Sue said, "I found a wallet! Is it yours?" Tim looked at the wallet. It was not orange, it was blue. "No, that wallet does not belong to me," Tim said. "But thank you for trying to help." Finally, Tim's friend, Ben, came running up to him. Ben said, "I found your orange wallet, Tim! It was under the slide." Tim was so happy! "Thank you, Ben!" Tim said. "You are a good friend." Tim's mom was glad too. They all went home, and Tim made sure to keep his orange wallet safe.

Once upon a time, in a mild and nice place, there was a little girl named Jane. Jane liked to go on walks and examine things. One day, Jane saw a big tree with a little cat in it. "Meow!" said the cat. Jane looked up and saw the cat. "Oh no!" said Jane. "I need to help this cat!" Jane tried to climb the tree, but it was too hard. She felt sad. Just then, a boy named Tom came by. He saw Jane and the cat. "I can help!" said Tom. He climbed the tree and got the cat. "Thank you, Tom!" said Jane. They both smiled and walked home with the cat. The cat was happy, and so were Jane and Tom. They had made a new friend.

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. He was enthusiastic about exploring his big backyard. As he walked, he observed a tiny fly buzzing around. The fly was very fast and went from one flower to another. Tim said, "Hello, little fly! What are you doing?" The fly stopped and looked at Tim. It said, "I am finding yummy things to eat on these flowers. Do you want to watch me?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, I would like that!" So, the fly showed Tim how it ate the sweet stuff on the flowers. They spent the whole day together, playing and talking. Tim was happy to have a new friend in the fly.
