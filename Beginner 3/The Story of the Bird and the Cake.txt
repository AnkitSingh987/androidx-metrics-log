Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Bird and the Cake

One day, a colorful kangaroo wanted to bake a cake. She put on her apron and got all the things she needed. She mixed the flour, eggs, and milk in a big bowl. The kangaroo was very happy and excited to bake her cake. As the colorful kangaroo was baking her cake, she heard a strange noise. She looked around and saw a little bird. The bird was sad because it was hungry. The kangaroo wanted to help the bird, so she gave it some cake mix. The bird was happy and ate the cake mix. But something unexpected happened! The bird grew very big and turned into a giant bird! The colorful kangaroo was surprised and a little scared. But the giant bird was very friendly and thanked the kangaroo for the cake mix. The giant bird and the colorful kangaroo became good friends and they baked many more cakes together.

Tim and Mia are friends. They like to play with toys and books. One day, they find a big board in the yard. The board is brown and hard. It has some nails and paint on it. "Look, a board!" Tim says. "We can make something with it." "What can we make?" Mia asks. Tim thinks. He has an idea. "We can make a house for my dog, Rex. He likes to sleep outside." Mia nods. She likes Rex. He is a big dog with black fur. He is very friendly, but he is deaf. He cannot hear anything. Tim and Mia carry the board to a corner of the yard. They find some more boards and a hammer. They start to make the house. They hit the nails with the hammer. They paint the boards with different colors. They have fun. Rex watches them. He is curious. He smells the board. He likes the smell. He bites the board. He chews the board. He makes a hole in the board. Tim and Mia hear a noise. They turn around. They see Rex chewing the board. "Rex, no!" Tim shouts. "That is our house!" Mia runs to Rex. She tries to take the board from him. Rex growls. He does not want to give the board. He chews harder. "Rex, stop!" Mia says. "You are ruining our house!" Rex does not listen. He is deaf. He cannot hear them. He only hears his own chewing. He likes to chew. Tim and Mia are sad. They look at their house. It has many holes. It is not a house anymore. It is a chewed board. They look at Rex. He is happy. He wags his tail. He thinks he is playing. Tim and Mia sigh. They love Rex, but they wish he would not chew their board. They decide to make another house. They look for a new board. They hope Rex will not chew it.

Once upon a time, there was a weak crab. The crab lived near the sea. He liked to count the waves. One day, he met a little fish. "Hi, crab!" said the fish. "Why are you so weak?" The crab said, "I don't know. I just am." The fish wanted to help the crab. The fish had an idea. "Let's find some food to make you strong!" They went to look for food. They found a big, yummy shell. The crab ate the shell and felt much better. But then, something unexpected happened. The weak crab became a big, strong crab! He was so happy. The fish said, "Now you can count more waves and have fun!" The crab thanked the fish for helping him. They became best friends and played near the sea every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big, beautiful palace. In the palace, there lived a poor little girl named Lily. She was sad because she had no friends to play with. One day, she went outside to find a friend. While walking, Lily saw a big, colorful bird. She asked the bird, "Do you want to be my friend?" The bird did not answer. Lily felt sad and walked away. As she walked, she saw a small, funny dog. She asked the dog, "Do you want to be my friend?" The dog did not answer. Lily was very sad and started to cry. Suddenly, the bird and the dog came to Lily. They both said, "We want to be your friends!" Lily was very happy and surprised. She did not know that the bird and the dog could talk. They all played together and had lots of fun. From that day on, Lily, the bird, and the dog were the best of friends in the palace.
