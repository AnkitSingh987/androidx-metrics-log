Category: Beginner
Title: Mr the Jolly Man and the Park

Once upon a time, there lived a jolly man named Mr. Happy. Mr. Happy had a very special suit. This suit was magic. When he wore the suit, he made everyone around him feel happy too. One day, Mr. Happy went to the park. He saw a sad little girl named Lily. She was crying because she lost her toy. Mr. Happy walked over to her and said, "Don't cry, Lily. I will help you find your toy." Lily looked at Mr. Happy's suit and started to smile. Together, they searched the park. They asked others if they had seen the toy. Finally, they found the toy under a big tree. Lily was so happy that she hugged Mr. Happy. She said, "Thank you, Mr. Happy! You made me feel better." Mr. Happy smiled and said, "Always remember to help others when they are sad." The moral of the story is that helping others can make everyone feel happy.

One day, a small frog named Bob liked to hop all day long. He had a big goal. His goal was to hop to the wild woods. His mom said, "Be careful, Bob!" Bob hopped and hopped until he got to the wild woods. He saw a big, wild cat. The cat said, "Why are you here, little frog?" Bob said, "I want to hop in the wild woods!" The wild cat and Bob became good friends. They played and hopped all day long. But then, something unexpected happened. The wild cat could hop too! They both hopped and laughed together, and Bob was very happy. He made a new friend and reached his goal.

Once upon a time, there was a funny little dog named Spot. Spot had a big box with a lock. He wanted to unlock the box to see what was inside. One day, he found a shiny coin under his bed. He thought the coin might help him unlock the box. Spot put the coin in the lock and turned it. The lock clicked and the box opened! Inside the box, there were many toys and treats for Spot. He was so happy to have unlocked the box. Spot shared his toys and treats with his friends. The next day, Spot and his friends played with the toys and ate the treats. They all had a great time together. Spot learned that sharing with friends is more fun than keeping things for himself. And that was the day, Spot and his friends became best friends forever.

One sunny day, a boy named Tim went outside to clean the yard. He had a rake to help him. He felt calm and happy while he worked. The leaves were all over the ground, but Tim knew he could clean them up. As Tim was raking, he found a big box under the leaves. He was surprised! Tim opened the box and found a toy inside. It was a toy rake! He was so happy and couldn't believe it. Tim played with the toy rake all day. He forgot about cleaning the yard. When his mom came outside, she saw the leaves were still all over the ground. But she saw Tim's happy face and let him keep the toy rake. They both cleaned the yard together, and it was the best day ever.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a top. The top could spin very fast. Tim had a big urge to play with it. So, he took the top outside to a big, flat rock. Tim gave the top a tough push. It started to spin and spin. It went so fast that it made a little wind. The wind blew the leaves on the ground. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. The top spun for a long time. But then, it started to slow down. Tim felt sad, but he knew he could spin it again. He picked up the top and got ready to play some more.

Once upon a time, there was a big red ball. The big red ball liked to lay in the sun all day. One day, the big red ball saw some luggage near the door. The luggage was blue and had wheels. The big red ball was curious. It rolled over to the luggage and said, "Hello, I am a big red ball. Do you want to play?" The luggage said, "Yes, I would like to play, but I am waiting for my friend, the little girl." The big red ball and the luggage played all day. They rolled and laughed in the sun. The little girl came outside and saw them playing. She was so happy to see her luggage and the big red ball having fun. She joined them and they all played together, laughing and rolling until the sun went down.
