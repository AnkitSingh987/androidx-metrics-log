Category: Beginner
Title: The Bird and the Flower

Once upon a time, there was a little bird. The bird had a nice nest in a big tree. One day, the bird saw a big flower start to bloom. The flower was very pretty. A big wind came and the little bird was frightened. The wind was so strong that it shook the tree. The nest started to fall out of the tree. The little bird did not want to lose its home. The little bird flew fast to its nest. It held the nest tight with its wings. The wind stopped and the nest was safe. The little bird was happy again. It looked at the pretty flower and sang a happy song.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat apples. He had a big apple tree in his yard. The apples on the tree were red and mild. Tim would pick apples from the tree every day. One day, Tim saw a small fire in his yard. The fire was near the apple tree. Tim was scared. He did not want the fire to burn the tree. He wanted to save the apples. Tim ran to the house and got a big cup of water. He threw the water on the fire. The fire went out. The apple tree was safe. Tim was happy. He picked an apple and ate it with a big smile.

Once upon a time, there was a brave little girl named Lily. She had a big dream to fly like a bird in the sky. Every night, she would pray for her dream to come true. One sunny day, Lily met a talking bird named Blue. Blue said, "Hello, Lily! I heard you want to fly like me. I can help you!" Lily was very happy and said, "Yes, please teach me how to fly!" Blue taught Lily to be brave and believe in her dream. They played and practiced flying together every day. Soon, Lily was able to jump very high and felt like she was flying. Lily and Blue became best friends, and they were both very happy.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a brown dog named Spot. Spot loved to crawl under the sofa to find his toys. One day, he found a small ball under the sofa. He was very happy. Spot went to his friend, a little bird named Tweet. Tweet lived in a tree near the house. Spot said, "Tweet, look at my new ball! Do you like it?" Tweet looked at the ball and said, "Yes, Spot! I like your ball. It is very nice." Spot and Tweet played with the ball all day. They had a lot of fun. They asked their other friends what they think about the ball. All their friends liked the ball too. Spot and Tweet were very happy that everyone had the same opinion about the ball. They played together until the sun went down and it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a fireman named Tom. Tom liked to ride his big red truck. He was very good at his job. He helped people every day. One day, Tom went to a park. He saw a hidden toy under a bush. It was a small, blue ball. Tom picked it up and looked around. He wanted to find the child who lost it. Tom saw a little girl crying. She was looking for her ball. Tom walked to her and gave her the ball. The little girl smiled and said, "Thank you, fireman!" Tom felt very happy to help. He waved goodbye and rode away in his big red truck.

Once upon a time, there was a busy vendor. He sold many things like toys, balls, and hats. Every day, many people came to buy things from him. The vendor was always happy to see them. One day, a little bug came to the vendor's shop. The bug had big eyes that would blink fast. The bug saw a toy it liked, but it had no money. The vendor saw the bug and felt sad for it. The vendor thought for a moment. Then, he picked up the toy and gave it to the bug. The bug blinked its big eyes in surprise and was very happy. It thanked the vendor and flew away to play with its new toy. From that day on, the bug and the vendor became good friends.
