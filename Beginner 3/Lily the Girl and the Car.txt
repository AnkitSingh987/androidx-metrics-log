Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Car

One day, a little girl named Lily found a comb on the floor. She picked it up and looked at it. It was a pretty comb with a picture of a cat on it. She thought it was very nice. Lily saw her friend Tom playing with a toy car. She went to him and said, "Tom, look what I found! It's a comb with a cat on it." Tom looked at the comb and said, "That's a stupid comb. My toy car is better." Lily felt sad and walked away. Later, Tom felt bad for what he said. He went to Lily and said, "I'm sorry, Lily. The comb is not stupid. It's nice." Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Tom. Let's play together." They played with the comb and the toy car, and they had lots of fun. At the end of the day, they were close friends again.

One day, a gentle cat named Fluffy went for a walk. She saw many shapes in the park. There was a big round ball and a tall, thin tree. Fluffy loved to look at all the shapes. As Fluffy walked, she met a small dog named Spot. Spot was sad because he lost his toy bone. "I hope I can find my toy," said Spot. "Will you help me look?" "Of course!" said Fluffy. They looked under the round ball and behind the tall, thin tree. Then, they found the toy bone near a small, square box. Spot was so happy. "Thank you for helping me," said Spot. Fluffy and Spot became good friends. They played with the toy bone and had fun with all the shapes in the park.

One day, a good dog saw a jet in the sky. The jet was big and loud. The dog wanted to play with the jet. So, the dog ran and jumped high to catch it. But the jet was too fast. The dog's friend, a cat, saw the dog trying to catch the jet. The cat said, "You can't catch the jet. It's too high and fast. Let's play with our ball instead." The dog wanted to prevent the jet from flying away. But the dog knew the cat was right. So, they played with their ball and had lots of fun. The jet flew away, but the dog and cat were happy playing together.

Once upon a time, there was a rat. The rat liked to eat yummy food. One day, the rat saw a big cake. The rat wanted to eat the cake, but it was on a high table. The rat tried to climb the table, but it was too hard. A cat saw the rat and said, "I can help you get the cake." The rat shrugged and said, "Okay, let's get the cake." The cat helped the rat climb the table. The rat was happy and ate the yummy cake. But then, the cat ate the rat. The rat should not have trusted the cat. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a lively little dog named Zigzag. He loved to run and play in the park every day. One day, Zigzag saw a big ball stuck in a tree. He wanted to get the ball down so he could play with it. Zigzag tried to jump and reach the ball, but he was too small. He started to struggle to find a way to get the ball down. Zigzag thought and thought, and then he had a great idea. He would ask his friend, Bird, to help him. "Hi, Bird! Can you help me get the ball down from the tree?" Zigzag asked. Bird was happy to help his friend. Bird flew up to the tree and pushed the ball with his beak. The ball fell down, and Zigzag caught it with a big smile. "Thank you, Bird!" Zigzag said. Now, Zigzag and Bird could play with the ball together. They had lots of fun and played all day long. Zigzag learned that friends can help each other when they struggle. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there were two best friends, Sunny and Tony. They loved to explore together and one day, they ventured to a tall cliff. It was dull and plain. They looked up and saw something strange. It was a shining object stuck in the wall. They were very curious and wanted to explore. Tony bravely stepped forward and pulled the object out of the wall. Sunny and Tony gasped in amazement as the object revealed a secret. It was a sparkly, golden key. The friends were so excited that they decided to search for a door to fit the key. They searched around the cliff, but they couldn't find anything. So they climbed up the cliff and could see a large, plain door. They both tried the key and it opened the door! Inside, they discovered a hidden room full of wonderful treasures! They found bright, colourful jewels and coins. Sunny and Tony celebrated their discovery and vowed to come back and explore the cliff again.
