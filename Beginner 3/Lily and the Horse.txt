Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Horse

Lily loved to dance. She danced in the morning, she danced in the afternoon, she danced at night. She danced with her dolls, she danced with her dog, she danced with her mom and dad. She danced when she was happy, she danced when she was sad, she danced when she was bored. One day, Lily went to the farm with her mom. She saw many animals there. She saw cows, pigs, chickens, horses, and sheep. She wanted to dance with them too. She ran to the sheep and said, "Hello, sheep! Do you want to dance with me?" The sheep looked at Lily and said, "Baa, baa, no, no. We do not dance. We eat grass and wool. Go away, little girl." Lily felt sad. She did not understand why the sheep did not want to dance. She thought they were rude. She walked away and looked for another animal to dance with. She saw a big, black, fierce dog. He had sharp teeth and a loud bark. He was guarding the farm. He saw Lily and said, "Woof, woof, go, go. This is my farm. You are not welcome here. Go away, little girl." Lily felt scared. She did not like the dog. He was mean. She ran away and looked for her mom. She found her mom near the horses. She hugged her mom and said, "Mom, I want to dance, but the sheep and the dog do not want to dance with me. They are rude and mean. Why do they not like me?" Her mom smiled and said, "Lily, not everyone likes to dance. Some animals have different jobs and hobbies. The sheep make wool for us, and the dog protects the farm. They are not rude and mean, they are just busy and serious. But I know someone who likes to dance as much as you do. Look, there is a horse who wants to dance with you." Lily looked and saw a brown, friendly, gentle horse. He had a long mane and a soft nose. He nickered and nodded at Lily. He said, "Neigh, neigh, yes, yes. I want to dance with you. You are a nice little girl. Come, let us dance together." Lily felt happy. She liked the horse. He was kind. She ran to the horse and said, "Hello, horse! Thank you for wanting to dance with me. You are a nice horse. Let us dance together." The horse and Lily danced. They danced in the sun, they danced in the shade, they danced in the grass. They danced with joy, they danced with fun, they danced with love. They danced and danced and danced. Lily and the horse were happy. They were friends. They loved to dance.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Zelda. She was playing in the park with her friends when she saw a big box of paint. "What's in there?" Zelda asked curiously. "Oh, it's just a box of paint," her friend said. Excitedly, Zelda opened the box and grabbed some paint. She waved to her friends and ran off to find something to paint. She found a big tree and got to work. Suddenly, a man came up to her. He was holding a bottle of wine. He said to Zelda, "Don't be scared. I just want to watch you paint." Zelda was a bit fearful, but she couldn't help but admire the man's kind eyes. She smiled and nodded, and went on painting. She painted until the sun started to set. When she was done, the man applauded her painting. He said, "That's beautiful! Here, I brought this for you." He gave her the bottle of wine. Zelda thanked the man and ran off to show her painting to her friends. They cheered for her painting, and then it was time for Zelda to go home. She said goodbye to her friends and happily held the bottle of wine all the way home.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a bossy cat named Tom. Tom liked to talk a lot. He talked to all the animals in the town. One day, Tom saw a big flood coming to the town. Tom ran to his friend, a dog named Sam. "Sam, a big flood is coming! We have to tell everyone!" Tom said. Sam was scared but he wanted to help. So, Tom and Sam went to talk to all the animals. As they talked to the animals, something unexpected happened. The flood was not water, but it was a flood of toys! The toys filled the town, and all the animals were not happy. They had no place to play. Tom and Sam tried to fix it, but they could not. In the end, the town was full of toys and the animals were sad.
