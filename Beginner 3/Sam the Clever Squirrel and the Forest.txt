Category: Beginner
Title: Sam the Clever Squirrel and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there was a clever little squirrel named Sam. Sam loved to play with his friends, the birds and the rabbits. They would run and jump all day long. One day, Sam saw a big log in the middle of the forest. He frowned because he didn't know what it was. Sam was very curious, so he asked his friend, the wise old owl, what the log was for. The wise old owl told Sam that the log was a special place for all the animals to play. Sam was very happy to hear this. He told all his friends about the log, and they all played on it together. They had lots of fun and laughed all day long.

Lila and Ben liked to play on the beach. They liked to make sand castles and dig holes. One day, they saw a big jellyfish on the sand. It was pink and shiny and had long tentacles. "Look, a jellyfish!" Lila said. "Can we touch it?" "No, it can sting you!" Ben said. "We should bury it so it can't hurt anyone." They got their shovels and started to pile sand on the jellyfish. They made a big mound and patted it down. They felt proud of their work. "Let's go get some ice cream," Ben said. "We did a good thing." They ran to the ice cream truck and got their cones. They licked them happily and forgot about the jellyfish. But the jellyfish was not dead. It was only sleeping. It felt the sand on its body and woke up. It was angry and scared. It wanted to go back to the water. It used its tentacles to push the sand away. It crawled slowly to the shore. On the way, it saw a little girl playing with a bucket and a spade. She had curly hair and a red dress. She was Lila. She did not see the jellyfish. She was busy making a sand cake. The jellyfish reached her and wrapped its tentacles around her leg. It stung her hard. Lila screamed and dropped her bucket. She felt a lot of pain. She was very anxious. Ben heard her scream and ran to her. He saw the jellyfish on her leg and tried to pull it off. But it was too late. The jellyfish had poisoned Lila. She stopped screaming and fell to the ground. She did not move. Ben cried and hugged her. He wished he had not buried the jellyfish. He wished he had left it alone. He wished he could make Lila wake up. But he could not. Lila was gone.

Tim and Mia liked to play with their toy cars and trucks. They made roads and bridges with blocks and cardboard. They pretended to drive and honk and crash. Sometimes they made loud noises and said, "Boom! Boom! Destroy!" One day, they saw a big cloud in the sky. It was dark and scary. It made a loud noise and flashed. Tim and Mia were afraid. They ran to their mom and said, "Mom, mom, what is that?" Mom hugged them and said, "Don't worry, my loves. That is a storm. It is a big cloud that makes rain and thunder and lightning. It looks scary, but it is harmless. It can't hurt you or your toys. It will go away soon." Tim and Mia felt better. They asked, "Can we watch the storm, mom?" Mom said, "Yes, you can. But stay inside and be safe. You can watch from the window." Tim and Mia went to the window and watched the storm. They saw the rain and the thunder and the lightning. They said, "Wow, wow, look at that!" They saw the storm destroy the sky. But they were not afraid. They knew it was harmless. They knew mom was with them. They knew the storm would go away. And they knew they could play with their toy cars and trucks again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to sit in her garden and watch the flowers grow. One day, she found a big bulb in the dirt. Sue was very excited. She thought, "I will plant this bulb and see what comes up." Sue dug a hole and put the bulb in the ground. She watered it every day. Sue was very tired, but she did not stop. She wanted to see the flower grow. Days went by, and the bulb started to grow. It grew taller and taller. One day, something unexpected happened. The bulb did not grow into a flower. Instead, it grew into a big tree with lots of leaves. Sue was surprised, but she was happy too. She now had a big tree to sit under and rest when she was tired. And that's how Sue got her very own special tree.
