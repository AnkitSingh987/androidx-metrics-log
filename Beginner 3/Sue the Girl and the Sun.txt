Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a shiny marble. The marble was very special to her. It was blue and green, and it would shine when the sun hit it. Sue loved her marble very much. One day, Sue went to play outside. It was a cold day, but she wore a warm coat. She took her marble with her. She liked to see it shine in the sun. Sue played with her marble all day, rolling it on the ground and watching it shine. When it was time to go home, Sue was very tired. She forgot her marble outside. The next day, she went back to find it. The marble was cold from the night, but it still shone bright in the sun. Sue was happy to have her special marble back and promised to always keep it safe.

One day, a boy named Tim saw a big, red ball. It was so amazing! He wanted to play with it. Tim asked his friend, Sam, to come to the event. They both wanted to play with the big, red ball. Sam said, "Let's strike the ball and see how far it goes!" Tim agreed. They both took turns striking the ball. It went up and down, left and right. They laughed and had fun. But then, the ball went too far. It went into the street. Tim and Sam ran after it. They did not look for cars. A car came and hit the ball. The ball popped. Tim and Sam were sad. They learned to be careful when playing near the street.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a toy that could glow. She loved her glowing toy. Mia's toy had a switch to make it glow. Every night, she would turn on the switch to see her toy glow before going to sleep. One night, Mia turned on the switch, but her toy did not glow. "Oh no!" she said. Mia felt poor without her glowing toy. She was sad and did not know what to do. She tried to turn the switch on and off many times, but nothing happened. Just as Mia was about to cry, her toy started to glow all by itself! Mia was so happy and surprised. It turns out, her toy had a secret switch that made it glow even brighter. Mia and her glowing toy had the best night ever, and she never felt poor again.

Once upon a time, there lived a witch. She was old and had a tall pointy hat. She lived in a far away village, and all the people there were very obedient to her. Every day, the witch would take a little rock and throw it into the sky. Then she would make a magical wish and the rock would fly off into the night. One day the witch saw a little child playing in the field with a rock. She went over to him and asked him to give her the rock. The little child was very obedient, so he happily gave her the rock. The witch thanked the child, and then she threw the rock into the sky. As it flew off into the night, she wished for three very special things. The next morning, the little obedient child came back to the field and he was so very surprised to find that all three of the witch's wishes had come true! He clapped his hands and was so happy for the witch. From that day on, the witch came to find the little child at the same field each day to ask for a rock to make her wishes. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Spot. Spot lived on a farm with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. They loved to play in the wheat field together. One day, as they were playing, they heard a strange noise. "Spot, what is that?" asked Lily. Spot didn't know, so they decided to find out. They walked through the wheat field until they found a big, round rock. The noise was coming from behind the rock. When they looked behind the rock, they found a baby bird that couldn't fly. The bird was laughing and playing with the wheat. Spot and Lily were surprised to see the bird, but they were happy to have a new friend. "Hi bird, do you want to play with us?" Lily asked. The bird nodded and they all played together in the wheat field. From that day on, Spot, Lily, and the bird were the best of friends, and they always had fun together.
