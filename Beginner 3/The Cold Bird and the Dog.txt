Category: Beginner
Title: The Cold Bird and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a cold little bird. The bird lived in a small jail. The bird was sad because it was all alone. One sunny day, a big, friendly dog walked by the jail. The dog saw the cold little bird and felt sad too. The dog wanted to help the bird. So, the dog invited the bird to come out and play. The bird was so happy to be free and to have a new friend. The dog and the bird played all day long. They were very happy. From that day on, the bird was never cold or alone again.

Once upon a time, there was a gray boat. The boat lived in a big blue sea. The boat liked to help all the sea friends. One day, the boat saw a little fish who was lost. The boat wanted to help the fish find its family. The boat and the little fish went on a long trip. They saw many sea friends on their way. The boat asked the sea friends if they knew where the little fish's family was. The sea friends said they did not know. The boat and the little fish did not give up. Finally, they found the little fish's family. The little fish was so happy. The little fish said "thank you" to the gray boat. The gray boat was very happy to help. From that day on, the gray boat and the little fish were best friends.

Jane was very tired. She wanted to take a nap. She asked her mom, "Will you take me to the cliff to sleep?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes of course!" When they arrived at the cliff, Jane looked out at the beautiful view. It was so fair. Jane was so excited. She hugged her mom and said, "Thank you for taking me here!" They lay down together on the grass. The sun was setting in the sky. Jane started to feel sleepy. Her mom said, "We should go to sleep now. The night is coming." So, Jane and her mom closed their eyes and fell asleep at the cliff. Jane dreamed of the beautiful fair view from the cliff and of running through the grass. When Jane's mom woke up, she carried Jane back home feeling happy.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Bow. Bow lived in a big lab. He was a very happy dog, but he did not know much. Bow was ignorant. One day, Bow met a cat. The cat said, "Hi, Bow! Do you want to play with me?" Bow wagged his tail and said, "Yes, let's play!" Bow and the cat played together all day long. Bow learned many new things from the cat. Bow was no longer ignorant. They became best friends and lived happily in the big lab.

One day, a mighty hurricane came to a small town. It rained a lot. The wind blew strong. The people in the town were scared. A little boy named Tim saw the rain and wind. He said to his mom, "Mom, I am scared of the hurricane." His mom hugged him and said, "It's okay, Tim. We are safe in our house." After the hurricane was gone, the sun came out. The town was wet, but everyone was happy the storm was over. Tim and his mom went outside to play in the puddles. They had a fun day after the mighty hurricane.

Once upon a time, in a wide green park, there was a little bird who loved to sing. Every day, the bird would sit on a big tree and sing happy songs. All the kids in the park would stop and listen to the bird's beautiful songs. One sunny day, while the bird was singing, it saw a small skull on the ground. The skull was from a little animal that lived in the park long ago. The bird felt sad for the little animal, but it wanted to make the skull feel happy too. So, the bird picked up the skull with its beak and put it on a branch in the tree. Then, the bird sang a special song just for the skull. The song was so sweet and lovely that it made the skull feel happy and loved. From that day on, the bird and the skull became the best of friends, and they shared many happy days together in the wide green park.
