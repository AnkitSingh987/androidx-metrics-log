Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and the Garden

Once upon a time, a boy named Tom and his mom lived in a cabin. They had a big garden with tasty fruits. Tom loved to eat these fruits. One day, Tom saw a man near the cabin. The man looked sad. Tom asked, "Why are you sad?" The man said, "I have no food." Tom felt bad for the man and gave him some tasty fruits. The next day, Tom and his mom found that all their fruits were gone. The man had taken all of them. Tom and his mom were very sad because they had no more tasty fruits to eat. The moral of the story is: be careful who you trust.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to her mom. "Mom, can you make me a coat?" she asked. Her mom said, "Yes, but we need to buy some cloth first." Sue was very happy and could not wait to have her new coat. The next day, Sue and her mom went to the store to buy cloth. They found a nice, soft, and warm cloth. Sue's mom said, "This will make a nice coat for you. But first, you have to make your room tidy." Sue went back home and cleaned her room. She put away her toys and made her bed. Her room was very tidy now. The next day, Sue's mom made the coat for Sue. Sue put on her new coat and said, "Thank you, Mom! I love my new coat." Her mom was happy to see Sue in her new coat. She said, "You look great, Sue! I am glad you made your room tidy. Now, you can wear your new coat and play outside with your friends." Sue went outside and had lots of fun with her friends, all warm and cozy in her new coat.

Once upon a time, there was a big, fast rabbit named Bob. Bob loved to eat carrots. One day, he found an enormous carrot in the woods. It was the biggest carrot he had ever seen. He wanted to take it home to eat, but it was too heavy for him to move. Bob had a friend named Tim, who was a slow turtle. Bob asked Tim to help him move the big carrot. Tim wanted to help, but he was very slow. Bob tried to speed up Tim, but Tim could not go fast. Bob was sad that he could not take the enormous carrot home. Then, he had an idea. He asked other animals in the woods to help them. Many animals came to help Bob and Tim. Together, they moved the big carrot to Bob's home. At last, Bob could eat the big carrot. He shared it with Tim and all the animals who helped them. They all enjoyed the enormous carrot and were happy. From that day on, Bob learned that working together with friends could solve problems.

One day, a small cat named Tom found an amazing pan. He wanted to cook some food in it. He went to his friend, a dog named Max. Tom said, "Max, I found an amazing pan! Let's cook some food together." Max was excited and said, "Yes! Let's do it!" They went to the kitchen and found some vegetables. Tom started to cut the vegetables into small pieces. But Max wanted to cut the vegetables too. They both wanted to cut and cook, but there was only one knife. Tom and Max started to argue. "I want to cut the vegetables!" Tom said. "No, I want to cut them!" Max replied. They were both sad and angry. Then, a wise bird named Lucy came to help. She said, "Why don't you both cut the vegetables together? You can take turns." Tom and Max thought that was a good idea. They took turns cutting the vegetables and cooking in the amazing pan. In the end, Tom and Max made a delicious meal. They learned that sharing and working together is better than fighting. They were happy and enjoyed their meal with their friend Lucy.

Once upon a time, in a little town, there lived a boy named Tim. He had a big red ball that he loved to play with. One day, the ball rolled away to the west. Tim ran after it, but it went very far. As he ran, Tim saw a bird sitting on a branch. "Hello, bird," he said. "Did you see my ball?" The bird looked at him and replied, "Yes, I saw it roll that way." The bird pointed with his wing. "Thank you," said Tim and he kept running to the west. After a while, Tim found his ball near a little tree. He was so happy! He picked up the ball and ran back home. When he passed the bird again, he waved and said, "Thank you for helping me, bird!" The bird replied, "You're welcome, Tim. Have fun playing!" And Tim did have lots of fun playing with his big red ball.
