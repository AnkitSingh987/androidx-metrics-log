Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Stars

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very enthusiastic about stars. He loved to look up at the night sky and see the stars twinkle. One day, he had an idea. He wanted to catch a star. Tim asked his friend, Sally, if she wanted to help him catch a star. Sally said, "Yes! Let's do it together!" So, they went outside in the night with a big net to catch a star. They tried and tried, but they couldn't catch one. The stars were too high up in the sky. Just when they were about to give up, a friendly bird flew by. The bird saw Tim and Sally and asked, "Why are you sad?" Tim told the bird they wanted to catch a star, but they couldn't reach them. The bird had an idea. "Hold on to my feet, and I'll fly you up to the stars!" Tim and Sally held on tight, and the bird flew them up to the sky. They were so close to the stars! But then, something unexpected happened. The stars began to talk! They said, "Please don't catch us! We need to stay in the sky to light up the night for everyone." Tim and Sally were surprised, but they understood. They let go of the bird's feet and floated back down to the ground. They didn't catch a star, but they made new friends and had a magical adventure they would never forget.

Once upon a time there was a lazy bear. He loved to sleep all day. One day he was so lazy he decided to take a nap on a rail. He made himself a nice little bed and rubbed it so it was extra comfy. Then he curled up and napped all day. But the thing was, he forgot to hop off the rail. Soon a big noisy train came rolling around the corner. The bear felt the rail shaking and heard the noise. He opened his eyes, but he was too lazy to move. He just kept rubbing the rail and went back to sleep. The train came closer and the bear rubbed the rail harder. Then with one big jump, the bear hopped off the rail just in time. He felt lucky and relieved. After that, the lazy bear made sure to always check that everything was safe before he napped!

One day, a big truck was driving down the road. It was very hard for the truck to go fast because it was so big. The truck driver was sad because he wanted to go fast like the little cars. The truck driver saw a little boy with a toy truck. The little boy said, "Why are you sad?" The truck driver said, "My truck is too big and slow." The little boy said, "I have an idea! Let's trade trucks!" The truck driver laughed and said, "Okay!" They traded trucks and the truck driver was happy. But then, the little boy's toy truck started to grow bigger and bigger! The truck driver was surprised and said, "Oh no! This is too big!" The little boy said, "I tricked you! My toy truck can grow big when I want it to." The truck driver had to lie down because he was so shocked. The little boy laughed and gave the truck driver his big truck back. They both learned that it's better to be happy with what they have.

One day, a boy named Tom found a wet toy motorcycle. He picked it up and wiped it with his hands. Tom loved to play with his new toy. Tom's mom saw him play with the wet toy motorcycle. She said, "Tom, we need to cut the wet grass outside." Tom wanted to help his mom, so he took his toy motorcycle outside. Tom and his mom cut the wet grass together. Tom pushed his toy motorcycle through the wet grass, pretending it was helping too. When they were done, they went inside and Tom's mom dried off his wet toy motorcycle. Tom was happy to have a new toy and help his mom.

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to play with cars. They have many cars of different colors and sizes. They drive them on the floor, on the table, and on the sofa. One day, they see a big truck outside. The truck is full of wheat. Wheat is what bread is made of. Tom and Sam think the truck is funny. They want to drive it. They sneak out of the house and run to the truck. They climb inside and find the keys. They turn the keys and the truck starts. It makes a loud noise. Tom and Sam laugh. They drive the truck on the road. They do not know how to drive well. They hit other cars and trees and signs. They make a big mess. People are angry and scared. They shout and honk and call the police. The police come and stop the truck. They take Tom and Sam out. They are not happy. They tell Tom and Sam that they are in big trouble. They call their parents and take them to the station. Tom and Sam are sad and sorry. They cry and say they are sorry. They wish they did not drive the truck. They wish they played with their cars at home.
