Category: Beginner
Title: Max and the Ball

Once there was a little dog named Max. Max liked to play with his ball in the open yard. One day, Max was playing with his ball when he heard a loud alarm. The noise scared Max, and he ran away to hide. Max's friend, a big cat named Sam, saw Max run away. Sam went to Max and asked, "Why are you scared?" Max said, "The alarm was loud, and it scared me." Sam told Max, "It's okay, the alarm is gone now. Let's go play with your ball again." Max and Sam went back to the open yard to play with the ball. Max was happy that Sam was there to help him when he was scared. Max said, "Thank you, Sam, for helping me. I forgive the alarm for scaring me." Sam smiled and said, "You're welcome, Max. We are good friends, and friends help each other." They played with the ball until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom lived in a small house with a big kitchen. One day, Tom saw a mouse named Jerry in the kitchen. Tom wanted to catch Jerry, but he was too fat to run fast. Tom said, "I need to be thin to catch Jerry." So, Tom started to eat less food and run more. Jerry saw Tom trying to be thin and thought, "I need to be ready for Tom." One day, Tom was thin enough to run fast. He saw Jerry in the kitchen and chased him. They ran and ran, but Jerry was smart. He led Tom to a big, sticky trap. Tom got stuck in the trap, and Jerry was safe. Tom and Jerry learned that being smart is more important than being fat or thin. They became friends and helped each other in the kitchen. The house was happy, and they lived together in peace.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a bald man named Tom. Tom had a big purple ball that he loved to play with. He would bounce the ball up and down, and throw it high in the sky. All the kids in the town liked to watch Tom play with his purple ball. One day, a little girl named Sue came up to Tom and said, "Can I play with your purple ball too?" Tom thought for a moment and then said, "Yes, you can play with my purple ball, but only if you promise to be careful with it." Sue promised to be careful and they played together all day long. As they played, more kids came to join them. Tom shared his purple ball with everyone and they all had a great time. At the end of the day, Tom realized that sharing his purple ball made him much happier than just playing by himself. The moral of the story is that sharing can bring happiness to everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy fork. He played with it all day. It was not a heavy fork. It was light and easy to hold. One day, Tim went for a walk with his fork. He saw a big rock. Tim wanted to move the rock. So, he tried to use his fork to lift the heavy rock. But the fork was too small. Tim had to pause and think. Then, Tim saw his friend, Sam. Sam had a big, strong fork. Together, they used their forks to move the heavy rock. They were happy they could do it together. And they played with their forks all day long.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went for a walk. She saw a big building. Lucy wanted to go inside, but the door was shut. She felt sad and wanted to open it. Lucy saw a kind man near the building. She asked him to help her. The man was patient and tried to open the door. But it was still shut. Lucy and the man waited and tried again. After some time, the door opened. Lucy was so happy! She hugged the kind man and said thank you. They both went inside the big building and had lots of fun.
