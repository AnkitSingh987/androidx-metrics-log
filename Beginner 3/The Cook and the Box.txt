Category: Beginner
Title: The Cook and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a big cook. He loved to cook yummy food. One day, he wanted to cook something new. He looked around his kitchen for ideas. He saw a hidden box on a high shelf. He opened the box and found a gingerbread man. The gingerbread man had a big smile and a yummy body. The cook was so happy. The cook showed the gingerbread man to his friends. They all loved the gingerbread man. They ate the yummy gingerbread man and said, "Thank you, cook!" The cook was very happy to make his friends smile.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Buddy. He loved to play with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. They would run and jump in the park every day. One sunny day, Tim said, "Buddy, let's play a new game. I will shake my hand, and you will give me your paw." Buddy wagged his tail and listened to Tim. Tim shook his hand, and Buddy gave him his paw. They both laughed and clapped. From then on, Tim and Buddy played the shake hand game every day. They were very happy and always had fun together. And that is how Buddy the loyal dog and Tim the little boy became best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a colorful fish named Fin. Fin lived in the big ocean with his family. They liked to play and swim all day long. One day, a big net came to catch them. Fin's family was scared. But Fin was brave. He wanted to help his family escape from the net. Fin swam very fast around the net. He found a small hole and told his family to swim out. They all escaped and swam back to their home in the ocean. They were happy and safe, thanks to Fin the colorful fish.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big dog. The dog is brown and has a long tail. The dog runs fast and barks loud. Anna is scared of the dog. She says to Ben, "I do not like the dog. The dog is big and loud. The dog can hurt us. Let us go away." Ben is not scared of the dog. He says to Anna, "The dog is nice. The dog wants to play. The dog can be our friend. Let us talk to the dog." Anna and Ben go to the dog. Ben says to the dog, "Hello, dog. What is your name? Do you want to play with us?" The dog wags his tail. The dog says to Ben, "Woof, woof. My name is Max. I want to play with you. You are nice." Anna sees that the dog is nice. She is not scared of the dog anymore. She says to Ben, "You are right. The dog is nice. The dog can be our friend. I want to play with the dog too." Anna and Ben play with the dog. They throw a ball and the dog catches it. They run and the dog runs with them. They laugh and the dog barks. They have fun. Anna and Ben are not worry. They are happy. The dog is happy too. They are friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird who lived high up in a tree. The bird had many friends who lived with him in the tree. They would play and sing all day long. But one day, two of his friends had a fight. This made the little bird very sad. The little bird tried to stop the fight, but his friends would not listen. So, the bird started to cry. His tears fell down from the high tree, and the other animals heard him cry. They all came to see what was wrong. When the animals saw the little bird cry, they all wanted to help. They talked to the bird's friends and told them to stop fighting. The friends listened to the animals and said sorry to each other. The little bird stopped crying and was happy again. All the animals played together and there were no more fights in the high tree.
