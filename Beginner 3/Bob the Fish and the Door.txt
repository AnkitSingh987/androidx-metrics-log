Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Fish and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a big fish named Bob. Bob liked to dive deep in the water. One day, Bob saw a great big closet at the bottom of the sea. He was very curious about what was inside. Bob swam closer to the closet and opened the door. Inside, he found many beautiful clothes. Bob put on a pretty dress and felt very happy. He swam around, showing off his great new look to all his fish friends. But then, something unexpected happened. A small fish named Tim saw the dress on Bob and wanted to wear it too. Instead of fighting, Bob shared the dress with Tim. They both looked great and had fun together. The other fish saw this and learned that sharing is good. From that day on, all the fish in the sea learned to share and be kind to each other.

Once upon a time, there was a dancer named Lily. She loved to dance in her tight dress. One day, she went to the park to dance. At the park, Lily met a boy named Tim. Tim said, "Can I dance with you?" Lily said, "Yes, let's dance together!" They started to dance and had lots of fun. But then, Lily tripped and fell. She felt regret because she did not tie her shoe. Tim helped her up and said, "Don't worry, we can still dance!" They danced some more and had a great day.

One day, a girl and a boy went to a temple. The temple was big and pretty. They walked and walked until they arrived at the temple. The sun was hot, but the grass was soft. At the temple, they saw a cat. The cat was soft and nice. The girl said, "I like this cat." The boy said, "Me too." They played with the cat for a long time. They were very happy. But then, it started to rain. The rain was very heavy. The girl, the boy, and the cat got wet. They were sad. The cat ran away, and they could not find it. The girl and the boy went home, feeling sad that they lost their new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy top that he loved to play with. One day, his friend Sam came over to play. Sam was sometimes foolish and liked to make trouble. Tim and Sam played with the top, but Sam wanted to play with it in a silly way. He suggested they throw the top in the air and try to catch it. Tim knew this was not a good idea, but he didn't want to make Sam upset. They threw the top in the air, and it fell down, breaking into pieces. Tim was sad and mad at Sam for his foolish idea. Sam felt bad too. They both learned that it's important to play with toys the right way.

Once upon a time, a girl named Sue and her cat, Max, lived in a small house. Sue loved to cook, and she had a big pot. One day, Sue wanted to make soup for her friends. She asked Max, "Will you help me cook?" Max just looked at her and did not answer. Sue put the pot on the stove and started to cook. She added some fine vegetables to the pot. The soup smelled good. Max looked at the pot with big eyes. He wanted to taste the soup, but Sue told him, "No, Max. It is too hot for you." Later, Sue went outside to play with her friends. She forgot to turn off the stove. Max jumped on the stove to see the pot. He tried to taste the soup, but the pot was too hot. The pot fell off the stove and the soup spilled everywhere. Max was scared and ran away. When Sue came back, she saw the mess in the kitchen. She was sad because her soup was gone and Max was nowhere to be found. Sue learned a lesson to always turn off the stove when she leaves the house.

One day, a big ugly bird saw a small bird with a big sign. The sign said, "Free juice for all birds!" The ugly bird flew to the small bird and said, "Can I have some juice, please?" The small bird smiled and said, "Of course! I will pour you some juice now." The small bird poured juice into a cup and gave it to the ugly bird. The ugly bird drank the juice and felt very happy. Other birds saw the sign and came to get juice too. They all talked and laughed together. The ugly bird made many new friends that day. The moral of the story is that being kind and sharing can help you make friends, no matter how you look.
