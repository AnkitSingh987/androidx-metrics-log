Category: Beginner
Title: Lila and the Rock

Lila was a unique girl. She liked to rise early and watch the sun. She liked to draw and sing and dance. She did not like to play with dolls or cars. She liked to play with rocks and sticks and leaves. One day, she found a big rock that looked like a man. It had a round head, two eyes, a nose, a mouth, and two arms. She decided to make it her friend. She gave it a name: Rocko. "Hello, Rocko," she said. "Do you want to play with me?" Rocko did not answer. He was a rock. He could not talk. Lila did not mind. She talked for both of them. She told him stories and jokes and secrets. She showed him her drawings and songs and dances. She made him a hat and a scarf and a necklace. They had fun together. Lila was happy. She had a unique friend.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a blue car. The car had a driver. The driver was very kind. He liked to help people. One day, he saw some kids on the road. They were trying to gather their lost toys. The driver stopped his blue car. He got out and helped the kids gather their toys. But, a big red truck came fast. The truck did not stop. It hit the blue car. The driver and the kids were sad. A nice man in a green car saw what happened. He stopped and helped the driver. They all learned that it is important to be careful on the road. The moral of the story is to always watch out for others and help when you can.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy food for dinner. Tim saw a big piece of beef and asked his mom, "Can we have beef for dinner?" His mom said, "Yes, we can try to cook beef tonight." When they got home, Tim's mom put the beef in the oven. Tim was excited to eat it. But then, their dog, Spot, smelled the beef cooking. Spot felt guilty because he wanted to eat the beef too. He knew it was not for him, but he couldn't help it. Suddenly, the oven door opened by itself! Spot was surprised, but he quickly grabbed the beef and ran away. Tim and his mom heard the noise and saw Spot with the beef. They laughed and said, "Oh, Spot! You are a silly dog!" They decided to share the beef with Spot because they loved him very much.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a house with her mommy, daddy, and her dog, Spot. Lily was worried because she did not know how to go upstairs by herself. She wanted to learn, but she was scared. One day, Lily's mommy said, "Lily, it's time to learn how to go upstairs. I will help you." Lily held her mommy's hand and they started to go up the stairs together. Spot, the dog, followed them. Step by step, Lily felt less worried. When they reached the top, Lily was so happy. She said, "Mommy, I did it! I went upstairs!" Her mommy smiled and said, "I knew you could do it, Lily. You are a big girl now." From that day on, Lily was not worried about going upstairs anymore. She had learned how to do it with her mommy's help.

One day, a little girl named Lisa went to her mom and made a request. She asked, "Mom, can I please have some jam on my bread?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, you can have jam on your bread." Lisa was so happy. She loved jam a lot. But when she went to the kitchen, she saw that her big brother Tom had taken all the jam. Lisa was sad and said, "Tom, please give me some jam too." Tom looked at the almost empty jar and said, "I'm sorry, Lisa. There is no more jam left." Lisa was very upset. She wanted the jam so much. Then, she had an impressive idea. She said, "Let's ask Mom to make more jam together!" Tom agreed, and they both went to their mom. Their mom thought it was a great idea. They all worked together to make more jam. In the end, Lisa and Tom shared the new jam on their bread. They were both very happy and learned that sharing is important.
