Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Ball

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park. She saw a big plastic box on the ground. Lucy was very curious and wanted to see what was inside the box. So, she went to the box and was very careful not to trip on anything. Lucy tried to open the box, but it was hard for her. She thought and thought about how to open it. Then, she had an idea! Lucy decided to use her strong arms to pull the box open. She pulled and pulled with all her might. Finally, the plastic box opened! Inside, Lucy found a big, bouncy ball. She was so happy and started to play with it. She bounced the ball high in the air and caught it again. Lucy played with the ball all day long at the park. She was very careful with the ball and the plastic box, and she had a lot of fun!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big ball that he loved to kick. One day, he went to the park with his ball. The sun was shining, and it was a brilliant day. At the park, Tim saw a girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she lost her toy. Tim wanted to help Sue feel better. He asked, "Do you want to play with my ball?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, please!" Tim and Sue kicked the ball and had lots of fun. They felt love in their hearts as they played together. The ball brought them joy and made them good friends. From that day on, Tim and Sue always played together at the park. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was scared of the big, scary house next door. The house had a tall chimney. Tim thought that a scary monster lived there. He was too scared to go near the house. One sunny day, Tim was playing with his ball in his yard. He kicked the ball too hard, and it went flying into the scary house's yard. Tim knew he had to get his ball back. He took a deep breath and walked to the scary house. As he got closer, he heard a shout coming from the chimney. Tim was so scared, but he wanted his ball back. He looked up and saw a bird stuck in the chimney. The bird was shouting for help. Tim was brave and found a long stick. He used the stick to help the bird get out of the chimney. The bird was so happy, and Tim was not scared anymore. The scary house was not scary after all. It was just a house with a tall chimney, and Tim had saved the bird. From that day on, Tim was not scared of the big, scary house next door.

Once upon a time, there was a strong boy named Tom. Tom had a sad face. He wanted to restore his toy car. The toy car was broken. Tom went to his friend, Sam. Sam said, "I can help you fix your toy car." They tried to restore the toy car, but it was not easy. Tom's face was still sad. In the end, the toy car was not fixed. Tom's face was very sad. Tom and Sam learned that not everything can be fixed.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a rag in her room. She did not know what to do with it. So, she asked her mom, "What can I do with this rag?" Her mom said, "You can clean your toys with it. They will be very happy." Sue started cleaning her toys. Her toy bear, Mr. Fluffy, was very dirty. Sue cleaned him with the rag, and he became very clean. Mr. Fluffy said, "Thank you, Sue!" Sue felt happy. The other toys saw how clean Mr. Fluffy was. They felt envious. They wanted to be clean too. Sue saw this and cleaned all her toys with the rag. The toys said, "Thank you, Sue!" Sue felt very happy that she could help her toys.
