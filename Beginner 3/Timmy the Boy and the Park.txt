Category: Beginner
Title: Timmy the Boy and the Park

Once there was a boy named Timmy who loved playing outside with his friends. One day, Timmy went to the park and noticed how wide the traffic was. He asked his dad, "Why is the traffic so wide?" "It's because we need to stay safe," his dad said. Timmy couldn't wait to find a way to help the traffic, so he set to work. He made a game using two sticks, some rocks and a pile of leaves. He asked his friends to join in. The game was simple. Everyone had to take turns balancing on one of the sticks and jump over the traffic. Everyone laughed, and it was so much fun! When it was time to go home, Timmy's dad said, "What a great way to keep safe! You set a wonderful example for everyone." Timmy smiled with pride as he watched the traffic flow freely.

Once upon a time, a little dog named Spot was playing outside. Yesterday, he saw a big red ball near a tree. Spot thought it would be fun to play with the ball, but he was not sure if it was his ball. Spot had a friend named Kitty. He asked Kitty if she knew whose ball it was. Kitty did not know, but she had an idea. She said, "Why don't we go ask our friend Bird if he knows?" Spot liked that idea, so they went to find Bird. Bird was sitting in the tree with the ball. Spot and Kitty asked Bird if he knew whose ball it was. Bird said, "I saw a foolish dog playing with it yesterday, but I don't know whose it is." Spot and Kitty decided to keep the ball and play together. They had lots of fun and were happy they had asked their friend Bird for help.

One day, a thoughtful boy named Tim went to play with his friend, Sam. They wanted to play a game with a ball. They tried to hit the ball and make a score. Tim hit the ball and Sam tried to catch it. "Let's see who can make the best score!" said Tim. Sam agreed and they started to play. But soon, they started to fight over who was better. Tim said, "I am better!" Sam said, "No, I am better!" As they fought, they did not see the ball go into the street. A big car came and hit the ball. The ball broke into two parts. Tim and Sam were very sad. They learned that fighting was not good and they should have played nicely together.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Max. Max was a troubled dog. He did not like the chain on his neck. Max wanted to run and play with his friends, but the chain was too tight. One day, Max saw a big tree. He thought, "If I pull the chain, maybe it will yield." Max pulled and pulled, but the chain did not yield. Max was sad. He sat down and cried. A little girl named Lily saw Max. She felt bad for Max. Lily went to Max and hugged him. She said, "Don't cry, Max. I will help you." Lily found a key and unlocked the chain. Max was free! He was so happy. Max and Lily played all day. They became best friends.

Tom and Lily were playing by the river. They liked to throw stones and watch the water splash. Sometimes they saw fish or ducks or frogs. They also liked to cross the bridge that went over the river. It was a big bridge made of wood and metal. It had holes and cracks and rust. But Tom and Lily did not care. They thought it was fun and exciting. One day, as they were crossing the bridge, they heard a loud roar. They stopped and looked around. They saw a big, ugly monster coming out of the water. It had green scales, yellow teeth, and red eyes. It was angry and hungry. It wanted to eat Tom and Lily. "Run!" Tom shouted to Lily. They turned around and ran back to the other side of the bridge. The monster followed them, roaring and snapping. It was faster than them. It reached the bridge and started to break it with its claws and teeth. Tom and Lily were scared. They did not know what to do. They saw a hole in the bridge. It was big enough for them to fit in. They decided to hide in the hole and hope the monster would not find them. They crawled into the hole and stayed very quiet. They peeked out of the hole and saw the monster still breaking the bridge. It was making a lot of noise and dust. It did not see Tom and Lily. They hoped it would go away soon. "Shh, don't make any sound," Tom whispered to Lily. "Maybe it will get bored and leave." "I hope so," Lily whispered back. "I don't like the monster. It is very ugly and mean." They hugged each other and waited for the monster to go away. They wished they had never crossed the bridge. They wished they were safe at home. They wished the monster would never come back.
