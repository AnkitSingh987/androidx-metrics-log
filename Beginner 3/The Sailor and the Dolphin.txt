Category: Beginner
Title: The Sailor and the Dolphin

One day, a sailor went to the sea with his boat. He liked to catch fish and see new places. He was a regular sailor who worked hard and loved his family. But one day, he saw a big storm coming. The sky was dark and the wind was loud. The sailor was scared and wanted to go back home. He tried to turn his boat around, but the waves were too strong. He felt his boat shake and break. "Help! Help!" he shouted. "Is anyone there?" He heard a voice from the water. It was a dolphin. The dolphin was friendly and smart. He swam to the sailor and said, "Don't worry, sailor. I will help you. Grab my fin and hold on tight. I will take you to the shore." The sailor was surprised and grateful. He did what the dolphin said and held on to his fin. The dolphin was fast and brave. He swam through the storm and avoided the rocks. He brought the sailor to a safe place where he could see his home. "Thank you, dolphin. You saved my life. How can I repay you?" the sailor said. The dolphin smiled and said, "You don't have to repay me, sailor. I'm happy to help. But I have one request. Please stay away from the storm next time. It is dangerous and you could get hurt. Listen to the weather and be careful. That is the best way to thank me." The sailor nodded and said, "You are right, dolphin. I was foolish and greedy. I wanted to catch more fish and see more places. But I forgot about the most important thing: my family. They are waiting for me and they love me. I will stay away from the storm and be a good sailor. Thank you for teaching me a lesson." The dolphin waved his fin and said, "You're welcome, sailor. I'm glad you learned your lesson. Now go and hug your family. They will be happy to see you. And remember, I'm always here if you need me. Goodbye, sailor." The sailor waved back and said, "Goodbye, dolphin. You are a good friend. I will never forget you." The sailor walked to his home and hugged his family. He told them what happened and how the dolphin helped him. They were amazed and proud of him. He was a regular sailor who learned a valuable lesson: to stay away from the storm and to stay close to his family.

One sunny Sunday, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a funny dog playing with a ball. The dog was jumping and running around. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. "Mom, can I play with the funny dog?" Tim asked. His mom said, "Yes, but be nice to the dog and don't take his ball." Tim went to the dog and tried to play with him. The dog did not want to yield his ball. He held the ball tight in his mouth. Tim said, "Please, dog, let me play with the ball too." The dog looked at Tim and then dropped the ball. Tim and the funny dog played together all Sunday. They had so much fun. Tim's mom smiled and watched them play.

Once upon a time, there was a bossy crab named Carl. He lived in a big blue sea with his friends. Carl liked to play with his plastic toy boat. One day, while Carl was playing with his boat, he saw a little fish named Fin. Fin looked sad. Carl swam over and asked, "Why are you sad, Fin?" Fin said, "I lost my toy ball, and I can't find it." Carl wanted to help Fin. So, they looked for the ball together. Carl used his pinchers to move rocks and look under them. Finally, Carl found the ball and pinched it with his claw. "I found it!" he said. Fin was so happy, and they played together with the plastic boat and the ball. Carl learned that it was more fun to be kind and helpful than to be bossy. And they became the best of friends.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to her attic. She wanted to find her favorite toy. The attic was high up in her house. Mia saw many old things there. She saw a big box and opened it. Inside the box, Mia found a small toy cat. The toy cat was clear and shiny. It was her favorite toy. Mia was very happy. She said, "I found you, toy cat!" The toy cat said, "Thank you, Mia!" Mia went downstairs with her toy cat. She showed it to her mom. Her mom said, "What a pretty toy!" Mia sighed. She was tired but happy. She played with her toy cat all day long.
