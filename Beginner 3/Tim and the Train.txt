Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Train

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim had a toy car. He loved his toy car so much. He played with it every day. But one day, Tim saw his friend, Sam, playing with a toy train. Tim started to wonder if he would like a toy train too. Tim thought about a trade with Sam. Tim asked Sam, "Can we trade my car for your train?" Sam said, "Yes, let's trade!" So, they traded their toys. Tim was happy with his new train, and Sam was happy with his new car. But then, Tim played too hard with his train and it broke. Tim felt sad and hurt. He missed his old toy car. Sam saw that Tim was sad, so he said, "Let's trade back!" And they did. Tim and Sam learned that sometimes it's better to keep what you have and be happy.

One day, a cat named Sam went for a walk. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. As he went up, he saw a pretty view. From the tree, he could see his house, the park, and the pond. Sam was happy. Then, Sam heard a small voice. A tiny bird was hurt and couldn't fly. "Help me, please," said the bird. Sam wanted to help, so he thought of a plan. Sam told the bird to hold on to his tail. He started to climb down the tree. But as he climbed, his tail got stuck! Sam rubbed his tail to make it feel better. Then, he tried again. This time, he got down safely. The bird thanked Sam and they became friends.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a mailbox. It was a big, red mailbox. Tim did not know whose mailbox it was. It was unknown to him. It was a hot day. The sun was shining. Tim was thirsty. He saw a puddle. He wanted to soak his feet in the puddle. He put his feet in the puddle and said, "Ah, this feels nice!" Suddenly, the mailbox started to talk! It said, "Hi Tim, I am a magic mailbox. I can give you a surprise!" Tim was so happy. He opened the mailbox and found a big, cold drink. Tim was not thirsty anymore. He thanked the magic mailbox and went home with a big smile on his face.

Once upon a time, there was a colorful witch. She lived in a little house in the woods. She loved to chew on candy all day long. One sunny day, a little girl named Lily walked by the witch's house. She saw the colorful witch chewing on her candy. "Hi, witch! Can I have some candy too?" Lily asked. The colorful witch smiled and said, "Of course, little girl! Here, take this candy and chew it. It will make you happy!" Lily took the candy and started to chew it. They both laughed and chewed on their candy together. From that day on, they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a pale boy named Tim. He loved to play outside and run around with his friends. One hot day, Tim and his friends went to the park to play. While playing, Tim saw a sign near a big tree. The sign said, "Drink water here." Tim was very thirsty, so he went to the tree and found a small tap. He drank some water and felt much better. Then he called his friends to drink water too. After everyone drank water, they all felt happy and strong. They played all day without getting tired. Tim learned that it was important to drink water and take care of his body. The moral of the story is to always listen to signs and take care of ourselves.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a big shadow on the wall. He was scared. He thought it was a monster. Tim leaned on the wall and said, "Please, Mr. Shadow, don't hurt me." The shadow did not move. Tim's big sister, Sue, came into the room. She saw Tim and the shadow. Sue said, "Don't be scared, Tim. It's just a shadow. Look, it's my hand making the shadow on the wall." Sue showed Tim how to make shadows with her hands. They made a dog, a bird, and a funny face. Tim was not scared anymore. He was very happy. He said, "Thank you, Sue. Shadows are not serious. They are fun!"
