Category: Beginner
Title: Lila and Ben and the Park

Lila and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to run and slide and swing. They saw a big hill with a lot of rocks. They wanted to climb it and see what was on the other side. "Let's go, Lila!" Ben said. "Maybe we can find something cool!" "OK, Ben!" Lila said. "But be careful. The rocks look sharp." They ran to the hill and started to climb. They used their hands and feet to find good places to hold on. They went higher and higher. They could see the park and the trees and the sky. They felt happy and brave. But then, Ben slipped. He lost his grip and fell. He hit a sharp rock on the way down. He cried out in pain. Lila saw him and was scared. She tried to go down to help him, but she was too high. She could not find a safe way. "Ben, Ben, are you OK?" Lila shouted. "No, Lila, it hurts!" Ben shouted back. "I'm bleeding! Help me, Lila!" Lila did not know what to do. She looked around for someone to help them, but no one was there. They were too far from the park. They were alone on the hill. Lila felt sad and sorry. She wished they had not climbed the hill. She wished they had stayed in the park. She wished they had listened to their mom, who had told them not to go too far. But it was too late. They had made a bad choice. And now they were in trouble.

Once upon a time, there was a little chicken named Chip. Chip lived on a farm with his family. Chip's mom was very organized. She liked to keep their home clean and neat. One day, Chip's mom asked him to help her unpack a big box. Chip was excited to help. He said, "Okay, Mom! I will help you unpack the box!" Together, they opened the box and took out all the things inside. They found new toys, clothes, and food for the family. Chip and his mom put everything in the right place. They were happy to see their home looking nice and organized. After they finished unpacking, Chip's mom said, "Thank you, Chip! You are a big help!" Chip smiled and felt proud. He knew that helping his mom made her happy. And they both enjoyed their clean and organized home.

Once upon a time, in a big castle, there lived a kind queen. She loved to display her beautiful toys for all the children in her land. One day, a small bird flew into the castle. The bird was helpless and could not fly. The queen saw the bird and said, "Oh, little bird, you need help. I will take care of you." The bird was happy and thanked the queen. Days went by, and the queen took good care of the bird. Soon, the bird was strong and could fly again. The bird said, "Thank you, queen, for helping me." The queen smiled and was happy to help. From that day on, the bird and the queen were the best of friends, and they played with the beautiful toys together.

Once upon a time, there was a modest cat named Mimi. Mimi loved to play with her soft toy, a little mouse. One day, Mimi was playing with her toy mouse and she gave it a big squeeze. The toy mouse made a funny sound that made Mimi laugh. Mimi's friend, a wise old owl named Ollie, saw her playing and asked, "What are you doing, Mimi?" Mimi said, "I am playing with my toy mouse. When I squeeze it, it makes a funny sound." Ollie nodded and said, "That's because you are squeezing the air out of its lung." Just then, a real mouse ran by, and Mimi chased it. But the mouse was too fast for Mimi. She was sad and tired. Ollie said, "Mimi, you don't need to catch the real mouse. Your toy mouse is just as fun and it won't run away." Mimi learned that it's better to be happy with what she has instead of always wanting more. She played with her toy mouse and gave it a big squeeze, enjoying the funny sound it made. From that day on, Mimi was happy and content with her modest toy mouse.
