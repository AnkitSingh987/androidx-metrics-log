Category: Beginner
Title: The Hidden Nation and the Mia

Once upon a time, there was a hidden nation. The nation was full of nice people. They liked to play and laugh all day. They did not want anyone to know where they were. So, they stayed hidden. One day, a little girl named Mia found the hidden nation. She saw the people playing and laughing. Mia said, "Hi, I am Mia!" The people were surprised but happy to meet her. One of the people said, "Hi Mia, we are the hidden nation. We like to have fun and play. Do you want to play with us?" Mia played with the people all day. They had a lot of fun together. When it was time for Mia to go home, she said, "I had fun with you all. I will keep your nation hidden." The people smiled and said, "Thank you, Mia. You can come back and play with us anytime!" So, Mia went home, and the hidden nation remained hidden.

Once upon a time two friends, Sam and Tom, were visiting a swamp. They were so excited! Tom saw a frog and quickly ran over to it. Sam warned him and said, "Be careful Tom! That swamp isn't safe." But Tom was too excited and wouldn't listen. He promised Sam he would be ok and hopped into the swamp. Sam was very worried. He waited for what felt like hours for Tom to come out. But he didn't. Then Sam remembered that his promise to be ok had been broken. He did not ever come out of the swamp and forever disappeared. Sam was so sad and never forgot his friend. He always felt worried that something bad had happened, and he learned from his mistake never to trust a promise when something dangerous is involved.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had an ordinary basket. She liked to do many things with her basket. One day, she went to the park to play with her basket. At the park, Sue met a boy named Tom. Tom asked, "What do you do with your basket?" Sue said, "I put things in it and carry them around." Tom wanted to play with Sue and her basket. They played together, putting things in the basket and taking them out. But then, a mean dog came to the park. The dog saw the basket and wanted it. The dog ran to Sue and Tom, and took the basket away. Sue and Tom were sad. They tried to get the basket back, but the dog was too fast. The mean dog ran away with the basket, and Sue and Tom never saw it again.

Tom and Anna are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a new boy. He is sitting on a bench. He has a big book. "Who is he?" Tom asks Anna. "I don't know. Let's go and say hello," Anna says. They walk to the bench. The boy looks up. He has brown eyes and curly hair. "Hi, we are Tom and Anna. What is your name?" Anna says. The boy is quiet. He does not say anything. He holds his book. Tom and Anna wait. They want to hear his name. They have a question. "Can we play with you?" Tom asks. The boy is still quiet. He shakes his head. He does not want to play. Tom and Anna are sad. They do not understand. They like to play with new friends. "Why not?" Anna asks. The boy is quiet for a long time. Then he whispers. "I can't talk. I have no voice." Tom and Anna are surprised. They feel sorry for the boy. "That's okay. You can still play with us. We can use signs and pictures. We can be quiet too," Anna says. The boy smiles. He nods. He opens his book. He shows them a picture of a dog. Tom and Anna smile too. They point to the dog. They make a barking sound. The boy laughs. He makes a barking sound too. They have fun. They play with the book. They meet a new friend. They are quiet, but they are happy.
