Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Story

One day, a boy named Tim found a letter on the ground. The letter was fine and pretty. It had a big red stamp on it. Tim wanted to know what was inside the letter, but he could not read yet. Tim went to his friend, Sue. He said, "Sue, can you read this letter? I found it on the ground." Sue looked at the letter and smiled. She said, "I can read, but I will not tell you what it says. I will tease you instead." Tim felt sad because he wanted to know what was in the letter. Sue saw Tim's sad face and felt bad for teasing him. She said, "I am sorry, Tim. I will read the letter for you now." Sue read the letter out loud. It was a happy story about a cat and a dog who were friends. Tim and Sue laughed and felt fine. They were happy to share the story from the letter.

Once upon a time, there was a small raft. The raft was restless. It wanted to go on a big trip. The raft had a friend, a little bird. The bird liked to whisper to the raft. One day, the bird whispered to the raft, "Let's go on a trip!" The raft was very happy. They went on the trip together. The bird sat on the raft, and they floated on the water. They saw fish and plants in the water. It was fun. But then, they saw a big rock. The raft was scared. The bird whispered, "Don't worry, I'll help you." The bird flew fast and pushed the big rock away. The raft was safe. They were happy and went on more trips together. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a strong farmer named Tom. He had a big farm with many animals and plants. One day, he planted some corn in his field. A little boy named Tim saw Tom working in the field. "What are you doing, Tom?" asked Tim. "I am planting corn," Tom said. "I insist you watch and learn." Tim watched as Tom put the corn seeds in the ground and covered them with dirt. Days went by, and the corn grew tall and strong. Tim and Tom would visit the field and talk about the corn. "Look how strong the corn is!" said Tim. "Yes, it is strong because we took good care of it," Tom replied. One day, it was time to pick the corn. Tom and Tim picked the corn together and put it in a big basket. They were very happy with their strong corn. They shared it with their friends and family in the village, and everyone enjoyed the yummy corn.

One day, a little boy named Tim was very hungry. He went to his mom and said, "Mom, I want a snack!" His mom gave him a cookie and said, "Here you go, Tim. But don't forget, we will use the microscope later today." Tim was excited to use the microscope. He ate his cookie and played with his toys. He knew something fun was going to happen soon. Later, Tim's mom called him over to the table. She had the microscope all set up. Tim looked through it, but instead of seeing tiny things, he saw a tiny world with tiny people! They were dancing and playing. "Wow!" Tim said, "I didn't know the microscope could show me this!" His mom smiled and said, "Sometimes, unexpected things can be the most fun."

One day, a little boy named Tim went to a new place with his mom. They were going to a fun park. Tim was very excited to see what was there. He wanted to play on the slides, swings, and see the big toy house. But when they got to the park, the big toy house was not there. Tim was sad and asked his mom, "Where is the big toy house?" His mom said, "Let's look for it, maybe it's hiding." They looked and looked, but still could not find the big toy house. Tim was very tired from looking and wanted to go home. Just when they were about to leave, Tim's mom saw a big box. She said, "Let's open the box and see what's inside." Tim opened the box and it revealed the big toy house! It was just hiding all along. Tim was so happy and played with the big toy house until it was time to go home.
