Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Huge Horse and the Amy

Once upon a time, there was a huge horse named Bob. Bob liked to play with his friends in the big field. They ran, jumped, and laughed all day long. One day, a little girl named Amy came to the field. She saw Bob and said, "Wow, you are so huge! Can you shrink so I can ride you?" Bob thought for a moment and said, "I don't know, but I can try!" Bob closed his eyes and tried very hard to shrink. And guess what? He did! Bob was now small enough for Amy to ride. They played together in the field, and Bob felt happy that he could help his new friend. At the end of the day, Amy said, "Thank you, Bob, for shrinking and playing with me." Bob smiled and said, "You're welcome, Amy. I had fun too!" And from that day on, Bob and Amy were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a brilliant top. It loved to spin all day long. The top had a friend, a small ball. The ball liked to bounce and play. One day, the top said to the ball, "Let's play together!" The ball was happy and said, "Yes! Let's spin and bounce all day!" So, they played together. The top spun fast and the ball bounced high. As they played, a little girl saw them. She laughed and clapped her hands. The top and the ball were happy to make her smile. They played together every day, and they all became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a helpful girl named Sally. Sally liked to play with her toy truck. One sunny day, Sally went to the park with her truck. At the park, Sally saw a big square sandbox. She thought it was a great place to play with her truck. Sally drove her truck to the sandbox and said, "Let's load some sand in the truck!" Sally scooped the sand into her truck. She filled it up and drove it around the sandbox. Then, she met a boy named Tim. Tim was playing with a toy digger. Sally asked, "Do you want to play with me?" Tim said, "Yes, I want to help load sand too!" Sally and Tim played together, loading sand into their toys. They had fun and were very helpful to each other. They became good friends and played in the square sandbox every day.

One day, a girl named Sue went for a jog. She liked to jog in the park. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Sue jogged past the trees and the flowers. She felt happy and free. After her jog, Sue went home. She walked into the kitchen. Her mom was there. Her mom was making a yummy cake. The cake was big and rich. Sue could smell the sweet cake in the air. Sue and her mom ate the rich cake together. They laughed and talked. They had a fun day in the kitchen. Sue was happy she went for a jog and got to eat cake too.

Once upon a time, there was a peaceful cat named Sam. Sam liked to wear a red hat. One day, Sam went on a walk and found a big subway. Sam went into the subway. He saw many people. They all wore different hats. Sam liked the hats. He felt happy and peaceful. Sam rode the subway all day. He saw new places and met new friends. At the end of the day, Sam went home. He wore his red hat and felt very peaceful.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very excited to try going to school for the first time. He put on his new shoes and his new shirt. At school, Tim met a girl named Sue. They played together. "The sun is hot today!" said Sue. Tim and Sue tried to make a big sand castle. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the sand away. Tim and Sue were sad. But they did not give up. They tried again and made an even bigger sand castle. They were so happy and had a fun day at school.
