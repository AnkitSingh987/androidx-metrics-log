Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Ben and the Spoon

Sara and Ben were playing in the kitchen. They liked to pretend they were chefs and make food with pots and pans. Sara had a big spoon and Ben had a small fork. They were having fun. But then Sara saw a shiny knife on the table. She wanted to play with it. She reached for it and said, "I want the knife. It is pretty." Ben saw what Sara was doing and said, "No, Sara, no! The knife is sharp. It can hurt you. Give it to me." Sara did not listen to Ben. She said, "No, it is mine. Go away." She held the knife tight and waved it around. Ben was scared. He ran to the door and shouted, "Mommy, mommy, help! Sara has a knife!" Mommy heard Ben and came to the kitchen. She saw Sara with the knife and was angry. She said, "Sara, what are you doing? Drop the knife right now. You are in big trouble." Sara dropped the knife and started to cry. She said, "I'm sorry, mommy. I just wanted to play." Mommy picked up the knife and put it away. She said, "Sara, you know you are not allowed to touch knives. They are dangerous. You could have hurt yourself or Ben. You have to be careful in the kitchen." She hugged Sara and Ben and said, "I love you, but you have to listen to me and Ben. He was trying to help you. Next time, play with something safe, okay?" Sara and Ben nodded and said, "Okay, mommy. We're sorry." Mommy smiled and said, "It's okay. I forgive you. But no more knives, okay?" Sara and Ben said, "Okay, mommy. No more knives."

Once upon a time, in a big red house, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy named Mr. Brain. Mr. Brain was very smart and could talk. Tim and Mr. Brain were best friends. One day, Tim lost Mr. Brain. He looked everywhere but could not find him. Tim was very sad and began to weep. He called out, "Mr. Brain, where are you?" There was no answer. As Tim cried, his big sister, Sue, came into the room. She saw Tim crying and asked, "Why do you weep, Tim?" Tim told her about Mr. Brain and how he was lost. Sue said, "Don't worry, Tim. We will find Mr. Brain." Sue and Tim looked all around the big red house. At last, they found Mr. Brain under Tim's bed. Tim was so happy! He hugged Mr. Brain and said, "I missed you, Mr. Brain!" Mr. Brain replied, "I missed you too, Tim!" From that day on, Tim and Mr. Brain played together every day in the big red house. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy dog named Spot. Spot was very good at making his friends laugh. Today, he wanted to play with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. "Let's play today, Lily!" said Spot. Lily smiled and said, "Okay, Spot! Let's play catch!" They went to the park to play. Spot was excited but also a little scared because he was clumsy. As they played, Spot tried to catch the ball, but he was so clumsy that he fell down. Lily laughed and said, "It's okay, Spot! You make me laugh!" Spot felt better and tried again. This time, Spot caught the ball and brought it back to Lily. They both laughed and played until the sun went down. Spot was very happy that he could make Lily laugh even though he was clumsy.

One day, a big, noisy dog named Max went to a food shop. He was very hungry. He wanted to order a big bone to eat. Max stood in line and waited for his turn. When it was Max's turn, he tried to order his bone. But the shop lady could not understand him. Max barked louder and louder. The shop lady got scared and gave Max a big yet. Max was confused. He did not want a yet, he wanted a bone! Max took the yet outside and put it on the ground. Suddenly, the yet opened and a small, quiet cat came out. The cat said, "Thank you for letting me out! I will help you get your bone." The cat went into the shop and got Max his bone. Max and the cat became best friends and shared the big bone.
