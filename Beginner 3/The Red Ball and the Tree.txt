Category: Beginner
Title: The Red Ball and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a big park, there was a little red ball. The ball loved to bounce all day long. It would bounce on the grass, on the sand, and even on the soft flowers. The ball was very happy when it bounced. One day, a small boy found the ball. He wanted to play with it. The boy had a long finger. He poked the ball with his finger to make it bounce. The ball went up and down, up and down. The boy and the ball had a lot of fun together. But then, the boy did a stupid thing. He threw the ball very high in a tree. The ball got stuck in the tree and could not bounce anymore. The boy was very sad. He wanted his friend, the ball, to be happy again. So, the boy climbed the tree and got the ball back. The ball and the boy were happy again, and they bounced all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a parrot named Polly. Polly liked to get things organized. She lived in a big tree with her friends. One day, Polly had an idea. She said to her friends, "Let's get our tree organized!" Her friends agreed and started to help. They put their toys in one place and their food in another. As they worked, a squirrel came by. He saw how organized the tree was and said, "Wow! Your tree looks great!" Polly and her friends were happy and proud of their hard work. From that day on, Polly and her friends kept their tree organized and clean. They played together and had lots of fun in their tidy home.

Once there was a little bug named Bob. Bob was a fly. He liked to fly in the sun. The sun would shine on him and make him feel warm and happy. One day, Bob met a new friend named Lily. Lily was a firefly. She could shine too, but only in the dark. One night, Bob said to Lily, "Let's play in the dark!" Lily said, "Okay, I will shine for us." So they went out to play when the sun was gone. It was very dark, but Lily's light helped them see. They played and laughed together. Then, something unexpected happened. Bob saw that he could shine too! He was so happy. He said, "Look, Lily! I can shine like you!" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, you can! We can both shine in the dark." They learned that even in the dark, they could find light and happiness together. The moral of the story is that friends can help each other find light in dark times.

Once upon a time, a girl named Mia had a toy boat. She liked to play with the boat in the water. One day, she put her boat in a big puddle near a drain. The boat floated and Mia was happy. Mia saw her friend Tom come by. Tom liked the boat too. Mia decided to lend her boat to Tom. Tom was very happy and said thank you. They played with the boat together, and it was a normal day. But then, something unexpected happened. A big frog jumped out of the drain and landed on the boat! Mia and Tom were very surprised. The frog looked at them and said, "Ribbit!" Then, the frog jumped off the boat and went back into the drain. Mia and Tom laughed and played with the boat some more. They would always remember the day the frog came to play.

One day, a boy named Tim went to visit his friend, Sam. They liked to play with toy cars and run in the park. Tim walked slow to Sam's house because he was excited to see him. When Tim got to Sam's house, Sam said, "Let's play a game! We have to rub our hands on the floor and see who can make the biggest sound." They both rubbed their hands on the floor, but they couldn't make a big sound. They tried again and again, but the sound was still small. Then, Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's rub our feet on the floor instead!" They rubbed their feet on the floor and made a big sound! They were so happy and laughed. They didn't know that rubbing their feet could make such a fun noise. Tim and Sam played the new game all day and had a great visit together.
