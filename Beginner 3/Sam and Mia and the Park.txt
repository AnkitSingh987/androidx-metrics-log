Category: Beginner
Title: Sam and Mia and the Park

Sam and Mia were excited to go to the park. They liked to play on the swings, the slide and the seesaw. They also liked to watch the birds, the dogs and the flowers. But when they got to the park, it was very hot. The sun was shining and there was no wind. Sam and Mia felt sweaty and tired. They wished they had a fan to cool them down. "Can we go home, Mom?" Sam asked. "I don't like the heat." "Me too," Mia said. "The park is not fun today." Mom looked at them and smiled. She had a surprise for them. She took out a big fan from her bag. It was a colorful fan with pictures of animals on it. She turned it on and held it in front of Sam and Mia. "Look, I have a fan for you!" Mom said. "Now you can enjoy the park and stay cool." Sam and Mia were happy. They felt the breeze from the fan on their faces. They thanked Mom and gave her a hug. They decided to quit being grumpy and have fun. They ran to the swings, the slide and the seesaw. They also watched the birds, the dogs and the flowers. They had a great time at the park with their fan.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside in the dirt. One sunny day, Spot was playing in the yard with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. Tim said, "Spot, let's bury our waste in the ground!" Spot wagged his tail and barked happily. They dug a big hole and put the waste inside. Then, they covered the hole with dirt. From that day on, Tim and Spot always buried their waste in the yard. They were very happy and had a clean and fun place to play. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big red mountain. On top of the mountain, there was a door. The door was always locked. No one knew what was behind the door. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the mountain. He wanted to see what was behind the door. Tim asked his friend, Sue, to go with him. Sue said, "Yes, let's go to the mountain and find the key to the door!" Tim and Sue walked up the mountain. They looked for the key. They found a small box under a rock. Inside the box, there was a key. Tim said, "Sue, we found the key! Let's open the door!" They went to the door and unlocked it. When the door opened, they saw a beautiful garden with lots of pretty flowers. Tim and Sue played in the garden all day. They were very happy they unlocked the door on the red mountain.

Once upon a time, there was a little human named Tom. Tom was an obedient boy who always did what his mom and dad told him to do. One day, Tom's mom told him not to leave the house. She said, "Stay inside, Tom. It is not safe to go out today." But Tom saw his friends playing outside. They were having so much fun. He wanted to play with them, too. So, Tom decided to leave the house for just a little while. He thought, "I will be back before mom knows I'm gone." Tom went outside and played with his friends. They laughed and ran around. Suddenly, it started to rain very hard. Tom and his friends ran to find shelter, but they all got wet. The rain did not stop, and Tom could not go home. Tom's mom found out that he had left the house. She was very sad and worried. Tom was cold and wet, and he wished he had listened to his mom. The rain never stopped, and Tom could not go home. He learned that being obedient was very important.

Once upon a time, in a deep, dark cave, there lived a big bear. The bear loved to sleep all day and play all night. One day, the bear decided to start a new game. The bear wanted to find a friend to play with. He went out of the cave, and he saw a little rabbit. The bear asked the rabbit, "Will you play with me?" The rabbit was scared but said, "Yes, I will play with you." They played and played until it was dark. The bear wanted to keep playing, but the rabbit was tired. The rabbit tried to go home, but he was lost. The bear did not help the rabbit, and the rabbit could not find his way home. The rabbit was very sad and scared, and he stayed lost in the woods.
