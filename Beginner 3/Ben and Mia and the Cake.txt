Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Mia and the Cake

Ben and Mia are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big cake on a table. It looks tasty. "Wow, look at that cake!" Ben says. "Can we have some?" "I don't know," Mia says. "Maybe it is for someone's birthday." They go closer to the table. They see a sign that says: "Happy Birthday, Lily!" "Who is Lily?" Ben asks. "I don't know," Mia says. "Maybe she is a girl who plays here." They look around. They do not see any girl or anyone else. They see the cake. It has pink frosting and sprinkles. "It looks so tasty," Ben says. "I want to try it." "Me too," Mia says. "But we should not take it. It is not ours." They think for a moment. Then they have an idea. "Maybe we can just have a little bit," Ben says. "We can use our toes." "Our toes?" Mia asks. "Yes, our toes," Ben says. "We can take off our shoes and socks. We can dip our toes in the cake. No one will notice." Mia thinks it is a funny idea. She agrees. They take off their shoes and socks. They sneak to the table. They dip their toes in the cake. It feels soft and sticky. They lick their toes. It tastes sweet and yummy. They giggle and smile. They dip their toes again. They lick their toes again. They do not see the woman who comes to the table. She is Lily's mom. She is angry. "Hey, what are you doing?" she yells. "That is my daughter's cake! You are ruining it with your toes!" Ben and Mia are scared. They drop their toes. They put on their shoes and socks. They run away. They do not talk to each other. They feel bad. They know they did something wrong. They wish they did not touch the cake. They wish they said sorry to Lily's mom. They wish they had a tasty cake of their own.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to watch his favorite cartoon on TV. Every day, he would sit and stay in front of the TV, ready to watch his cartoon. One day, Tim's friend Lily came over to play. "Tim, do you want to play outside?" asked Lily. Tim thought for a moment and said, "No, I want to stay and watch my cartoon. It is almost ready to start." Lily felt sad, but then she had an idea. "Why don't we play while we wait for your cartoon?" she suggested. Tim agreed, and they played together until the cartoon was ready. They had so much fun that they decided to play together every day before watching the cartoon. And from that day on, Tim learned that it was more fun to play with friends than to just stay and watch TV.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Kitty. Kitty did not like to bathe. She thought it was scary. One day, her mom said it was time for a bath. Kitty was not happy, but she knew she had to listen to her mom. While Kitty was in the bath, she saw something in the water. It was a toy. Kitty played with the toy and started to have fun. She forgot that she was scared of the bath. The bath was not so scary anymore. The result was that Kitty loved to bathe now. She would play with her toy every time she took a bath. One day, when she was playing in the bath, the toy started to talk! The toy said, "Thank you for playing with me, Kitty!" The unexpected twist was that the toy was a magic toy. Kitty and the magic toy became best friends, and they lived happily ever after.

Once, there was a girl. She was walking to the shop. The girl felt angry. Suddenly, a man came out of the shop. He slapped the girl hard. He was very angry. The girl was very scared. She started crying. She shouted, "why did you do that?" The man said, "You are very naughty. You must be punished". The girl was very upset. She said, "Please don't hit me. I won't do it again". But the man did not listen. He slapped her again. He laughed and ran away. The girl felt very sad. She had been hurt and she couldn't do anything. She started walking home, crying. She knew she would never go to the shop again.
