Category: Beginner
Title: The Yellow Duck and the Bridge

Once upon a time, there was a yellow duck. The duck liked to play near a bridge. One day, the duck met a frog under the bridge. The frog said, "Hi, duck! I can order bugs for us to eat." The duck was happy and said, "Yes, please! I like bugs too!" So, the frog ordered many bugs for them to eat. They ate and played together by the bridge. They were very good friends. One day, a big wind came and pushed the bridge. The bridge moved and made a loud noise. The duck and the frog were scared. But then, they saw that the wind had moved the bridge to a new place with more bugs to eat. The duck and the frog were very happy and said, "Thank you, wind!" They had more fun and more bugs to eat in their new place.

Lily and Ben were twins who liked to play outside. One morning, they saw a lot of mist in the garden. They wondered what it was and where it came from. "Let's go and see!" Lily said. She put on her boots and coat and ran to the door. "Wait for me!" Ben said. He was feeling lazy and did not want to get up. He stayed in bed and watched Lily through the window. Lily had fun in the mist. She pretended she was a fairy and made shapes with her hands. She saw a bird and a flower and a star. She laughed and sang and danced. Ben felt bored in bed. He wished he had gone with Lily. He tried to see what she was doing, but the mist was too thick. He felt lonely and sad. After a while, the mist went away and the sun came out. Lily came back to the house. She was happy and warm. "Ben, Ben, come and see what I found!" she said. She showed him a shiny stone and a feather and a leaf. "They were in the mist. They are so pretty and special. Do you want to share them with me?" Ben looked at the things Lily had. He felt sorry and ashamed. He had missed the fun and the magic of the mist. He had been too lazy to join her. "I'm sorry, Lily. I was silly and lazy. I should have gone with you. Can you forgive me?" he said. Lily smiled and hugged him. She was kind and generous. She gave him half of the things she had. "Of course I forgive you, Ben. You are my brother and I love you. Next time, let's go together and explore the mist. Maybe we will find more surprises!" she said. Ben nodded and thanked her. He learned his lesson. He decided to be more active and curious. He did not want to miss any more adventures with Lily. The moral of the story is: Don't be lazy and miss out on the wonders of nature. Be brave and curious and share your discoveries with others.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom loved to play with his toy dog, Sam. One day, Tom and Sam went outside to play in the yard. They found a big bowl. Tom had an idea. He wanted to bury the bowl in the dirt. Tom and Sam dug a hole in the yard. They put the big bowl in the hole and covered it with dirt. They had so much fun playing and burying the bowl. Tom knew that one day, they would dig it up again. A few days later, Tom's mom made a big cake. But, oh no! The cake was spoiled. Tom's mom needed a new bowl to make another cake. Tom remembered the bowl they buried in the yard. Tom and Sam went outside, dug up the bowl, and gave it to his mom. She was so happy that Tom had found the bowl. They all enjoyed the new cake together.
