Category: Beginner
Title: The Mouse and the Box

Once there was a small mouse that lived in a big forest. It was wild and free and trusted in everyone. One day it heard a strange noise. It went to investigate and found a metal box hidden in a bush. The mouse was curious, so he went up to the box. He sniffed and smelt something sweet. He wanted to open the box and see what was inside. Just then he heard a voice. "Don't open it!" yelled the voice. The mouse was scared and ran away. But he kept thinking about the box and eventually trusted that voice was wrong. He went back and opened the box. Inside was a raging wild fire. In a moment the fire spread and the mouse was soon surrounded by flames. He was never seen again.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a mighty lion named Leo. Leo was very strong and all the animals were scared of him. One day, as Leo was walking, he saw a big trap. It was a trap for animals, and Leo was curious about it. As Leo got closer to the trap, a little mouse named Mimi saw him. Mimi was very small, but she was also very brave. She went up to Leo and said, "Leo, don't touch the trap! It's dangerous!" Leo looked at Mimi and said, "Thank you, Mimi. I won't touch it." But Leo's tail accidentally hit the trap, and it closed around his paw. Leo was scared and cried out, "Help me, Mimi!" Mimi knew what to do. She used her tiny teeth to pull the trap open. It took a long time, but finally, the trap let go of Leo's paw. Leo looked at Mimi and said, "Thank you, Mimi. You saved me!" Mimi smiled and said, "You're welcome, Leo. We all need help sometimes." From that day on, Leo and Mimi were the best of friends, and they always looked out for each other in the big forest.

One day, a cat named Tom went for a walk. Tom liked to look at the view from the top of a hill. He was a flexible cat and could climb up high. On his way, he met a dog named Max. "Hi, Max!" said Tom. "Do you want to come with me to see the view from the hill?" Max wagged his tail and said, "Yes, let's go!" As they climbed the hill, Tom felt something on his back. He asked Max to rub it for him. Max used his paw to rub Tom's back. Suddenly, a big bird flew down and landed on Tom's back. It was the thing that Tom felt! The bird said, "Thank you for the rub, Tom and Max! Now, I will show you the best view ever!" The bird took them on a ride through the sky, and they saw the most beautiful view. Tom and Max were so happy and couldn't believe their eyes. They thanked the bird and knew they had a new friend.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play in the park with their mom. Today, they see a big truck in the park. The truck has a long hose and a loud noise. The truck is watering the flowers and the grass. "Wow, look at the truck!" Lily says. "It has a lot of water!" "Can we watch the truck, mom?" Ben asks. "Please, please, please?" "Okay, you can watch the truck for a minute," mom says. "But then we have to go home. It is almost lunch time." Lily and Ben run to the fence and watch the truck. They see the water come out of the hose. The water is thick and strong. It makes the flowers and the grass wet and shiny. "Look, the water is like a snake!" Lily says. "It moves and twists!" "Look, the water is like a rainbow!" Ben says. "It has many colors!" They watch the water and the truck. They are happy and excited. They forget about the time. "Time's up, kids!" mom says. "We have to go home now. Say bye to the truck!" Lily and Ben wave to the truck. The truck honks back. They smile and run to mom. They hold her hands and walk home. They are hungry and ready for lunch. They had fun watching the truck.
