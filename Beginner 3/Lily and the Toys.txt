Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Toys

Lily liked to play with her dolls. She had many dolls, but her favorite was Anna. Anna had long hair and a pretty dress. Lily liked to brush Anna's hair and make her talk. One day, Lily's mom said they had to go see a lawyer. Lily did not know what a lawyer was. She asked her mom, "What is a lawyer?" Her mom said, "A lawyer is a person who helps us with some papers. We have to sign the papers so we can move to a new house." Lily did not want to move to a new house. She liked her old house. She liked her room and her toys and her friends. She said, "I don't want to move. I want to stay here." Her mom said, "I know, sweetie, but we have to move. The new house is bigger and nicer. You will have a new room and new toys and new friends. You will like it there." Lily was not sure. She felt sad and scared. She hugged Anna and said, "Will you come with me, Anna?" Anna nodded. She said, "Of course I will, Lily. I am your doll. I will always be with you. Wherever you go, I go too." Lily smiled. She felt a little better. She said, "Thank you, Anna. You are my best friend." Anna nodded. She said, "You are welcome, Lily. You are my best friend too."

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing outside. He saw a big pole in the park. He thought it would be fun to kick the pole. So, he ran up to it and kicked it very hard. A gentle bird was sitting on the pole. When Tim kicked the pole, the bird got scared and flew away. Tim felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, bird! I didn't see you there." The bird did not come back. The next day, Tim went to the park again. He saw the pole and remembered the bird. He said to himself, "I will not kick the pole today. I will be gentle." As he walked past the pole, he heard a soft sound. It was the bird! The bird came back with a friend. They both sang a happy song for Tim.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Dare. Dare was not like other dogs. He had a very big nose. His nose was so big that he could smell things from very far away. Dare liked to use his big nose to help his friends. One day, Dare and his friends were playing in the park. They were playing a game of hide and seek. Dare's friend, Kitty, was hiding, but no one could find her. The other dogs thought the game was getting boring. Dare knew he could help. He used his big nose to smell where Kitty was hiding. Dare sniffed and sniffed, and soon he found Kitty. She was hiding behind a big tree. All the other dogs were so happy that Dare found Kitty. They all cheered and clapped for Dare. From that day on, Dare and his big nose were never boring again. And Dare's friends always wanted him to play with them, because he made every game more fun.

Lily and Tom were going to the beach with Mom and Dad. They were very happy. They wanted to play in the sand and swim in the water. They had to pack their bags first. They put in their swimsuits, towels, hats and sunscreen. They also packed a board to ride on the waves. They got in the car and drove to the beach. It was a long drive. They sang songs and played games to pass the time. They saw the sea and the sky and the palm trees. They were almost there. But then they saw something terrible. There was a big sign that said "Beach Closed". There was a lot of trash and dirt on the sand. There was a bad smell in the air. There were no people or animals. The water was brown and dirty. The board was useless. Lily and Tom were very sad. They wanted to go to the beach. They had packed their bags for nothing. They asked Mom and Dad why the beach was closed. Mom and Dad said it was because some people did not care about the earth. They threw their trash and waste in the water and on the land. They made the beach sick and dirty. Lily and Tom felt angry and sorry. They wished they could help the beach. They wished they could make it clean and beautiful again. They wished they could play in the sand and swim in the water. They wished they could use their board. They hoped that one day, the beach would be open again.
