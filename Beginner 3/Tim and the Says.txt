Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Says

Tim likes to surf. He has a big board that is blue and white. He goes to the beach with his dad and his sister. They have fun in the water. One day, the water is rough. The waves are big and fast. Tim's dad says it is not safe to surf today. He says they can play in the sand or swim near the shore. Tim's sister says she wants to make a sandcastle. She takes her bucket and shovel and runs to the sand. Tim is not happy. He wants to surf. He thinks he is brave and strong. He decides to surf anyway. He takes his board and goes to the water. He does not listen to his dad. Tim tries to surf on a big wave. But the wave is too strong. It pushes Tim and his board down. Tim feels scared. He cannot breathe. He cannot see. He wants his dad. Tim's dad sees Tim in trouble. He runs to the water. He swims to Tim and grabs him. He pulls him and his board out of the water. He hugs Tim and says he is glad he is okay. He says Tim was very naughty. He says Tim must never surf when the water is rough. He says Tim must always listen to his dad. Tim is sorry. He says he is sorry to his dad. He says he was silly. He says he will not surf when the water is rough. He says he will always listen to his dad. He says he loves his dad. Tim's dad says he loves Tim too. He says they can go and play with Tim's sister. He says they can make a big sandcastle together. He says they can surf another day when the water is calm. Tim smiles. He says he likes that idea. He says he likes to surf. But he likes to be safe too.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a big idea in her mind. She wanted to make a cake, but she was worried. She had never made a cake before. One day, Sue's friend Tom came to play. Sue told Tom about her idea. Tom said, "I can help you make the cake!" They went to the kitchen and started to make the cake. Sue was still worried, but she tried to be brave. As they made the cake, Sue and Tom had a lot of fun. They laughed and played, and soon the cake was ready. When they took the cake out of the oven, Sue was so happy. She was not worried anymore. Sue and Tom ate the cake together, and it was the best cake they ever had.

Once there was a girl called Lucy. She was only three years old. She was playing in her garden when something unusual caught her eye. She saw a gray closet in the corner of her garden. She walked over to the closet and started to whistle. Mommy noticed the sound and came out to see the source. She said, "Lucy, what are you doing by the closet?" Lucy replied, "Mommy, I'm whistling to the closet. Do you think it likes music?" Mommy smiled and said, "I'm sure the closet loves to hear you whistle, sweetheart." Lucy was happy and continued to whistle to the closet. She never knew what was inside the closet but it was her favorite spot to play. She knew that the gray closet was her secret friend.

Once upon a time, there was a funny cat named Tom. Tom loved to play and have fun with his friend, a little bird named Tim. They lived in a big park with lots of trees and flowers. One day, Tom and Tim decided to play a new game. They wanted to balance on a long stick. Tom went first. He put one paw on the stick, then the other. He tried very hard, but he fell off. "Oh no!" said Tom. "I can't do it!" Next, it was Tim's turn. He flew onto the stick and balanced with his tiny bird feet. He looked at Tom and said, "You can do it too! Just try again!" Tom tried again and again. Finally, he was able to balance on the stick. They both laughed and clapped their paws and wings. From that day on, Tom and Tim played the balance game every day. They had so much fun together, and they became the best of friends. And they lived happily ever after in their big park.
