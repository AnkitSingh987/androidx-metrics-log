Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Ben and the Tree

Tom and Ben are brothers. They like to play cops and robbers. Tom is the cop and Ben is the robber. They have toy guns and hats. One day, they are playing in the yard. Tom says, "Stop, robber! I will catch you!" Ben runs away and hides behind a tree. Tom follows him and says, "I see you, robber! Come out with your hands up!" Ben says, "No, cop! You can't catch me! I have a gun!" He points his toy gun at Tom and makes a loud noise. "Bang, bang, bang!" Tom pretends to fall down and says, "Oh, no! You shot me, robber! You are very bad!" He lies on the grass and closes his eyes. Ben feels sorry for Tom. He runs to him and says, "I'm sorry, cop! I didn't mean to hurt you! Are you okay?" He touches Tom's face and says, "Please, open your eyes!" Tom opens his eyes and smiles. He says, "I'm okay, robber! You didn't really shoot me! It was just a game!" He hugs Ben and says, "You are a good brother!" Ben hugs Tom and says, "You are a good brother, too! And a good cop! You are very obedient and brave!" They laugh and play again. They are happy.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park. She wanted to enjoy her day and play with her friends. When she got there, she saw a boy with an unusual toy. It was big and round, and it looked fun. Mia wanted to play with it too. Mia asked the boy, "Can I play with your toy?" The boy said, "No, it's mine!" Mia was sad, but she still wanted to play. She thought of a plan to make the boy share the toy. Mia found a big stick and started to play with it. She acted like it was the most fun toy ever. Soon, the boy with the unusual toy saw Mia and said, "Can I play with your stick?" Mia said, "Only if we can share your toy too." The boy agreed, and they both enjoyed playing with the unusual toy and the stick together.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty butterfly. It had many colors on its wings. It liked to fly in the sun. One day, the butterfly saw a big tree. It wanted to rest on the tree. But the tree started to tremble. The butterfly was scared. The tree said, "Don't be scared, pretty butterfly! I tremble because I am happy to see your colors. Come and rest on me." So the butterfly and the tree became friends. They were happy together.

The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Froggy wanted to play outside. He grabbed a big bowl and went to the pond. He could see the fish swimming and jumping in the pond. He was happy. Froggy put the bowl in the water and watched it float. Then, he made a splash and the water rippled around the bowl. The fish were friendly and came close to Froggy and the bowl. Froggy scooped his hands in the water and the fish came closer. Then he splashed the water again and they swam away! The bowl also moved away and made a loud sound when it hit the bank. Froggy was really happy and jumped up and down. He splashed even more and laughed. He was having so much fun! The fish kept coming back, but they were a bit more careful. They only came close when Froggy stopped splashing. He watched them swirl around the bowl and it was so beautiful. He had the most fun day ever!

Once upon a time, there was a little frog named Fifi. Fifi loved to play in the wet pond near her home. One day, while she was playing, she saw a big fish named Bob. Bob was sad because he couldn't stay in the pond anymore. The pond was too small for him. Fifi wanted to help Bob. She asked, "Why can't you stay in the pond?" Bob said, "My mouth is too big, and I need a bigger home." Fifi thought of a plan to help Bob find a new home. Fifi and Bob went on a journey to find a bigger pond. They hopped and swam until they found the perfect pond. It was big and wet, just right for Bob. Bob was so happy and said, "Thank you, Fifi!" Fifi smiled and said, "You're welcome, Bob!" Now, Bob could stay in his new home, and they played together every day.
