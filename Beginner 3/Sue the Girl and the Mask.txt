Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Mask

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play. She was very adventurous. One day, Sue found a mask. It was a funny mask. She wanted to play with it. Sue put on the mask and went outside. She saw her friend Tom. They played together. They ran, they jumped, and they laughed. They were having so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. While they were playing, Sue's mask fell off. And guess what? Tom was wearing a mask too! They both laughed and laughed. They didn't know they both had masks. They kept playing together, now without masks, and had an amazing day.

Once upon a time, there was a quiet town. In this town, people did not speak much. They liked to be alone. But they had a big board in the middle of the town. On this board, people wrote what they wanted to say. One day, a little girl named Amy went to the board. She wanted to write something too. She wrote, "I like to speak!" Then, she went back to her house. The next day, everyone went to the board to see what was new. They saw what Amy wrote. They all looked at each other and started to speak. They found out that they liked to speak too! From that day on, the quiet town was not quiet anymore. People spoke and became friends. All because of Amy and the board.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful girl named Sue. She loved to cook with her mom. They cooked yummy things like cake and pie. One day, Sue and her mom went to a wide park to have a picnic. At the park, they saw a big tree. They put their food under the tree. They played games and had fun. But when they came back, they saw a surprise. Their food was gone! Sue and her mom were sad. Then, they saw a squirrel with a big, wide smile. The squirrel had their food! The squirrel was very cheerful. It shared the food with Sue and her mom. They all ate together and had a fun time at the park.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a red top. He loved to spin the top and watch it go round and round. He played with the top all day, every day. One time, Tim saw a cute cat. The cat was sleeping on a soft bed. Tim wanted to play with the cat, so he used his top to poke the cat. The cat woke up and looked at Tim with big, sad eyes. Tim felt guilty for poking the cat. He said sorry to the cat and gave it a nice pat. The cat forgave Tim, and they played with the top together. The cat would chase the top as it spun around, and they both had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there lived a clever fox. He lived in a forest filled with lots of stuff. One day, he noticed a big tree. Curious, he decided to explore it. He carefully climbed up, but he soon got tired. Suddenly, he slipped and fell! He landed on a branch and hung on for dear life. Just then, he heard a voice. "Are you okay?" It was an owl. The fox said he was okay and thanked the owl for checking on him. The owl smiled. The owl offered the fox some help. She knew there was something special about this tree that the fox didn't know. She said if he trusted her, she could help him get to the top. On the top, there was a special surprise. The fox was so excited! He accepted the help and carefully trusted the owl as she carried him further up. Sure enough, at the top was a huge nest filled with delicious things to eat! The clever fox was so happy and thanked the owl for her help.

There once was a little cat. The cat liked to play with a tap. One day, the cat saw a dog near the tap. The dog was big and the cat was scared. The cat said, "Do you know how to play with the tap?" The dog said, "No, I don't know. Can you show me?" The cat felt better because the dog was nice. The cat and the dog played with the tap. They had fun and became friends. They played with the tap every day. The little cat was not scared anymore.
