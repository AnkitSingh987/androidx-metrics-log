Category: Beginner
Title: The Peaceful House and the Woods

Once upon a time, there was a peaceful little house in the woods. In this house, there lived a kind man named Tom. Tom loved to stir big pots of soup for all his animal friends. One day, Tom saw a big tree with a lot of fruit. He picked the fruit to make a yummy soup for his friends. He went back to his house and started to stir the soup in a big pot. Tom was very happy. Soon, all the animals came to Tom's house. They could smell the yummy soup. Tom shared the soup with all his friends. They ate and played together. At the end of the day, they all said "thank you" to Tom and went back to their homes.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big, clear balloon. He wanted to show his friend, Sue. Tim tied a long string to the balloon and went to find Sue. "Sue, look at my big balloon!" Tim said. Sue looked up and saw the clear balloon. "Wow, it's so big and pretty!" Sue said. They both held the string and played with the balloon. Suddenly, a strong wind came and pulled the balloon away from them. The string slipped from their hands. They tried to catch the string, but it was too late. The wind made the balloon go up, up, up. Tim and Sue were sad. Just then, a bird flew by and saw the balloon. The bird grabbed the string with its beak and flew down to Tim and Sue. The bird released the string, and the balloon was back in their hands. Tim and Sue were so happy and thanked the bird. They played with the balloon all day long, and the bird joined them too.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside in the snow. One day, he found a big, heavy pipe in his yard. He did not know what it was for, but he thought it was fun to play with. Tim's mom saw him playing with the pipe and said, "Tim, be careful with that heavy pipe. It might make you shiver if you play with it too long in the snow." Tim did not listen and kept playing with the pipe. After a while, Tim started to shiver from the cold. He remembered what his mom said about the heavy pipe. Tim put the pipe down and ran inside to get warm. He learned that his mom was right and he should listen to her more often.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a cheap toy in the shop. It was a small comet toy. He asked his mom, "Can I have the comet toy, please?" His mom said, "Not today, but I promise we will come back and get it later." A few days later, Tim and his mom went back to the shop. But the comet toy was gone. Tim was sad. His mom said, "I promise we will find another comet toy for you." They went to many shops, but they could not find a comet toy. Tim's mom said, "Let's go home and try again tomorrow." Tim agreed, but he was still sad. That night, Tim looked out his window and saw a real comet in the sky. It was big and bright. He ran to his mom and said, "Look, Mom! A comet!" His mom looked out the window and smiled. She said, "See, I promised you a comet, and there it is!" Tim was happy. He knew his mom always kept her promises. They watched the real comet together, and Tim forgot all about the cheap comet toy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. Sally had a toy fan that she loved very much. One day, Sally was playing with her fan outside in the sun. She was so happy. But then, a big wind came and blew her fan away. Sally was very sad and didn't know what to do. She asked her mommy and daddy to help her find her fan. They all looked for the fan, but they could not find it. Sally's dog, Spot, saw how sad she was. Spot wanted to help. He sniffed and sniffed until he found the fan stuck in a tree. Spot barked and jumped to rescue the fan. He gave the fan back to Sally. She was so happy and hugged her dog. Now, Sally was sleepy and took a nap with her fan and Spot by her side. They all lived happily ever after.
