Category: Beginner
Title: Ziggy the Clever Zebra and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a clever zebra named Ziggy. Ziggy lived in a big forest with many friends. They played all day long and had lots of fun. Ziggy was very good at minding his friends when they needed help. One day, Ziggy and his friends were playing near a big river. They liked to run and jump, but they knew not to go too close to the water. Ziggy was minding his friends, making sure they were safe. Suddenly, a big wind came and blew one of Ziggy's friends, a little bird, into the water. The little bird was scared and could not fly. All the friends were worried, but Ziggy had a clever plan. He quickly found a long stick and put it into the water for the bird to hold on to. The little bird held the stick and Ziggy pulled him out of the water. Ziggy's friends were very happy and thanked him for being so clever and minding them. They learned that it is important to help each other and be careful near the water. From that day on, they always played safely and knew that Ziggy would be there to mind them.

Once upon a time, there was a clean cat named Tom. Tom liked to order things on the phone. One day, Tom ordered a violin to play with. When the violin came, Tom tried to play it. But it was hard for a cat to play the violin. Tom got sad, but he did not give up. He tried and tried, but the violin did not make nice sounds. One day, Tom was playing the violin outside. A big dog heard the bad sounds and came running. The dog did not like the sounds and took the violin away from Tom. Tom was very sad and never got his violin back.

One day, Tom went to the farm. He saw a lot of corn. The sun was hot. Tom was happy. He wanted to eat some corn. Tom's friend, Sue, came to the farm. "I want some corn too," Sue said. Tom and Sue picked the corn. They took it to their house. At the house, they saw that the corn was bad. "Oh no! We can't eat this," Tom said. Sue was sad. "What can we do?" she asked. Tom had an idea. "We can replace the bad corn with good corn," he said. Sue smiled. They went back to the farm and picked good corn. Tom and Sue ate the good corn. It was very yummy. They were happy. The hot sun made the corn taste even better.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to draw with her white crayon. One day, Mia's white crayon broke. She was sad and did not know what to do. Mia's mom saw her crying and asked, "Why are you sad, Mia?" Mia showed her broken white crayon to her mom. Her mom said, "Don't worry, we can replace it with a new one." Mia smiled and they went to the store to buy a new white crayon. At the store, Mia's mom told her, "Be careful when you pick the crayon, so it doesn't break again." Mia was careful and picked a new white crayon. She was happy and went home to draw more pictures. Mia learned that it's important to be careful with her things, and if something breaks, she can always ask for help to replace it.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He liked to collect things. One day, he found a bottle. It was a big, pretty bottle. Tim was very happy. Tim was sometimes a bit selfish. He did not want to share the bottle with his friends. So, he hid the bottle in his room. He looked at it every day and smiled. One day, Tim's mom found the bottle. She thought it was trash. She threw it away. Tim was sad, but he learned to share. From then on, he shared his toys and had more fun with his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim loved to organize his toys. He had toy cars, toy trains, and toy animals. Tim had a big box to put his toys in when he was done playing. One day, Tim was playing with his toys and he felt something funny on his foot. He looked down and saw that his toe was stuck in a toy car! Tim tried to get his toe out, but it was really stuck. Tim's mom saw him and came to help. She gently pulled the toy car off Tim's toe. Tim was so happy and said, "Thank you, Mom!" After that, Tim made sure to always organize his toys so his toe wouldn't get stuck again.
