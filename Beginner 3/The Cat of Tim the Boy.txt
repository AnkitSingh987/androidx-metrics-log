Category: Beginner
Title: The Cat of Tim the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to watch films. His favorite film reminded him of a big, strong hero. Tim wanted to be like the hero, but he felt weak. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a small cat stuck in a tree. Tim was scared, but he wanted to help the cat. He thought of the hero from the film and felt brave. Tim started to climb the tree. He felt weak, but he kept going. When he reached the cat, something unexpected happened. The cat jumped onto Tim's back and started to purr. Tim couldn't believe it! He was a hero, just like in the film. The cat helped Tim feel strong, and they climbed down together. From that day on, Tim knew that even if he felt weak, he could still be a hero.

Once upon a time, there was a little train named Timmy. Timmy was nervous because he had a big job to do. He had to take a lot of food to the town for a big party. On his way, Timmy met a friendly cook named Sally. Sally said, "Hi, Timmy! What are you doing?" Timmy said, "I'm taking food to the town for a big party. But I'm nervous. Can you help me?" Sally said, "Yes, I can help you cook the food for the party!" Timmy and Sally cooked the food together. They made yummy treats for everyone. When they got to the town, all the people were happy to see the food. Timmy wasn't nervous anymore. He was happy that he and Sally could help make the party fun.

Lily liked jazz. She liked the sounds of the drums, the piano, the saxophone and the trumpet. She liked to dance and clap her hands when she heard jazz. She had a little toy saxophone that she played with at home. One day, she went to the park with her mom and dad. There, she saw a band playing jazz on a stage. There were many people watching and enjoying the music. Lily wanted to join them. She asked her mom, "Can I go and play with the band?" Her mom smiled and said, "No, sweetie, you can't. The band is for grown-ups. They have practiced a lot to play jazz. You can watch and listen, but you can't join them." Lily felt sad. She wanted to play jazz with the band. She looked at her little toy saxophone and wished it was real. She wondered if the band would like her if she had a real saxophone. Then, she heard a voice behind her. It was a boy, about her age, holding a little toy trumpet. He said, "Hi, I'm Max. I like jazz, too. Do you want to play with me?" Lily turned around and saw his friendly face. She smiled and said, "Yes, I do. I'm Lily. I have a saxophone. Do you want to join me?" Max nodded and said, "Yes, I do. Let's play jazz together." They sat on a bench and pretended to play their toy instruments. They made sounds with their mouths and moved their fingers. They had fun and felt happy. They made their own little jazz band.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park. She saw a big tree with an unusual shape. It had a long neck that looked like a giraffe. Mia wanted to see what was on top of the tree, so she started to climb. As Mia climbed higher and higher, she began to feel scared. She wanted to see what was at the top, but she was too scared to go all the way up. So, she decided to just peek from where she was. When she peeked, she saw a big, shiny star on top of the tree. Mia was so happy to see the star, but she was still scared to climb all the way up. Suddenly, the tree neck started to bend down. The tree neck brought the star down to Mia. She took the star and hugged it. The tree neck was not scary at all, it just wanted to help Mia. The tree and Mia became good friends, and they played together in the park every day.
