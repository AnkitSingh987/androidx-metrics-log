Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Lily and the Waffle

Ben and Lily were having breakfast with their mom and dad. They liked to eat waffles with syrup and butter. Mom made the waffles in a special machine that made them hot and crispy. "Can I have more syrup, please?" Lily asked. "Sure, honey. Here you go," Mom said, and poured some syrup on Lily's waffle. Ben wanted more syrup too, but he did not ask. He tried to reach the bottle on the table. He leaned over his plate and stretched his arm. But he leaned too far and fell off his chair. He landed on the floor with a thud. "Ouch!" Ben cried. "Ben, are you okay?" Dad asked, and ran to help him. "I'm okay, I'm okay," Ben said, rubbing his head. He looked up and saw something hairy on the floor. It was their dog, Spot, who had heard the noise and came to see what happened. "Hi, Spot," Ben said, and hugged the dog. Spot licked Ben's face and wagged his tail. "Look, Mom, Spot likes me," Ben said, and smiled. Mom and Dad smiled too. They were glad that Ben was not hurt. They helped him get back on his chair and gave him a new waffle. "Be careful next time, Ben," Mom said. "And use your words if you want more syrup." "Okay, Mom," Ben said. "I'm sorry I fell." "That's okay, Ben. We love you," Dad said. "I love you too," Ben said. "And I love waffles. And Spot." They all laughed and ate their waffles. Spot got a piece too. It was a good breakfast.

One day, a boy named Tom went to the zoo with his mom and dad. He liked to see all the animals, but his favorite was the lion. He wanted to meet the lion and say hello. His mom and dad told him to be careful and stay close to them. They walked to the lion's cage and saw a big, yellow lion with a long, brown mane. He was lying on a rock and looked very sleepy. Tom thought he was cute and friendly. He wanted to get closer to the lion and touch his fur. He saw a small hole in the fence and crawled through it. His mom and dad did not see him. Tom was very happy and excited. He ran to the lion and said, "Hi, lion! I am Tom. You are very pretty. Can I be your friend?" The lion opened his eyes and saw Tom. He was very surprised and angry. He did not like strangers in his home. He roared very loud and showed his sharp teeth. He wanted to scare Tom away. Tom was very scared and sorry. He realized he made a mistake. He did not want to bother the lion. He wanted to go back to his mom and dad. He turned around and ran to the fence. But he was too clumsy and tripped over a stick. He fell down and hurt his knee. He cried very loud and said, "Help, help! The lion is mad at me!" His mom and dad heard him and saw him. They were very worried and scared. They ran to the fence and called for help. A zookeeper came and opened the gate. He took Tom out of the cage and gave him a hug. He said, "You are very lucky, Tom. The lion could have hurt you. You should never go near the animals without permission. They are wild and dangerous. You have to respect them and keep a safe distance." Tom nodded and said, "I am sorry, zookeeper. I am sorry, lion. I was curious and silly. I will not do it again. I learned my lesson." He hugged his mom and dad and said, "I love you. Thank you for saving me." They hugged him back and said, "We love you too, Tom. We are glad you are okay. But please, listen to us next time. We only want you to be safe and happy." They left the zoo and went home. Tom never forgot his adventure with the lion. He always remembered to be careful and respectful around animals.

Once upon a time, there was a proud cat. The cat was white and soft. She liked to play with her ball. One day, she saw a big box. The box was open, so she went inside. Inside the box, the cat found a banana. The cat picked up the banana and said, "What is this?" She did not know what to do with it. The cat tried to play with the banana, but it was not fun. Then, the cat had an idea. She used her claws to split the banana. The banana was now in two parts. The cat was happy and said, "Now, I can share with my friend!" She took the two parts of the banana and went to find her friend, the dog. They both enjoyed the banana together.
