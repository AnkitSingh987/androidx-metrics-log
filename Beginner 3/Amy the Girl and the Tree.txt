Category: Beginner
Title: Amy the Girl and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to explore and discover new things. One day, she went for a walk with her mom. They saw cars and buses in the traffic. Amy asked her mom, "What is that?" Her mom said, "That is traffic, Amy. It's when many cars and buses are on the road." Amy and her mom kept walking. They saw a big tree with yummy apples. Amy's eyes got big and she said, "Mom, can we eat the apples?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, Amy, we can eat the apples. They are yummy and good for you." They picked some apples and sat under the tree. Amy took a big bite and said, "Wow, this is so yummy!" They laughed and enjoyed the yummy apples together. After they finished eating, they went back home, happy to have discovered new things during their walk.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable dog named Spot. Spot was a good dog who always helped his friends. One day, his friend, a bird named Chirpy, got stuck in a jail. The jail was made of sticks and Chirpy could not fly away. Spot said, "Don't worry, Chirpy! I will help you!" Spot tried to break the sticks, but they were too strong. He thought of a way to improve his strength. Spot saw a big rock and pushed it with all his might. The rock rolled and hit the sticks, breaking the jail. Chirpy was free! He said, "Thank you, Spot! You are a true friend!" Spot felt happy that he could help his friend. From that day on, Spot and Chirpy knew that they could always depend on each other. The moral of the story is to never give up and to always be a dependable friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She was worried because she lost her favorite gem. It was red and shiny, and she loved it very much. Mia looked and looked, but she could not find it anywhere. Mia's friend, Tim, saw that she was sad. He asked her what was wrong. Mia told him about her lost gem. Tim wanted to help, so they both looked for the gem together. They looked under the bed, in the toy box, and even in the cookie jar. Finally, Mia saw something shiny in the grass outside. She ran to it and picked it up. It was her gem! Mia sighed with happiness. She was so glad that she found her gem and that her friend Tim helped her. From that day on, Mia and Tim were the best of friends.

One day, a blue cat named Max went for a walk. He saw a mint plant in a garden. Max thought it smelled good. He wanted to eat the mint, but the garden was close to a big house. Max was scared that someone might see him. Max met a dog named Sam. Max asked, "Can you help me get the mint?" Sam said, "Yes, I will help you." They went to the garden together. Max was happy to have a friend. But when they got to the mint, Sam ate it all! Max was sad and angry. He said, "You were not a good friend." Then, Max walked away. He never talked to Sam again.

Lily was a little bird who loved to fly. She had delicate wings that were blue and yellow. She liked to soar high in the sky and sing with her friends. One day, she saw a big cloud that was dark and scary. It made loud noises and flashed bright lights. Lily was afraid of the cloud. She wanted to fly away, but she did not know where to go. She saw a big tree that had many leaves. She thought it would be a good place to hide. She flew towards the tree, but she did not see a big cat that was waiting there. The cat saw Lily and jumped to catch her. Lily was very scared. She tried to fly faster, but the cat was too quick. The cat grabbed Lily's wing with its paw and pulled her down. Lily felt a sharp pain in her wing. She cried out loud. But then, she heard another noise. It was her friends, the other birds. They heard Lily's cry and came to help her. They flew around the cat and pecked it with their beaks. They made loud noises and scared the cat away. Lily was happy to see her friends. They helped her get out of the cat's paw and flew with her to a safe place. They looked at her wing and saw that it was hurt. They found some soft leaves and wrapped them around her wing. They told her that her wing would heal soon. Lily thanked her friends for saving her. She was glad that they were brave and kind. She learned that the cloud was not so scary, but the cat was. She also learned that she should always be careful and watch out for danger. She hugged her friends and smiled. She knew that they would always be there for her.
