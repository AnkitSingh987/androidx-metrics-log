Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Dog and the Turtle

Once upon a time, a fast dog and a slow turtle wanted to have a race. The dog said, "I will win because I am very fast!" The turtle said, "We will see who wins." The race started, and the dog ran very fast. The turtle walked slow but did not stop. The dog was so far ahead that he thought he could take a nap. He lay down under a tree and fell asleep. While the dog slept, the turtle kept walking. The turtle passed the sleeping dog and went ahead. When the dog woke up, he saw the turtle near the finish line. The dog ran as fast as he could, but it was too late. The turtle won the race. The dog felt sad and said, "I was stupid to take a nap. I regret not running the whole time." The turtle smiled and said, "Slow and steady wins the race."

Tom and Lily were hungry. They wanted to eat some jam on bread. They saw a jar of jam on the shelf. It was red and shiny. It looked perfect. "Can you open the jar, Tom?" Lily asked. She was too small to reach the shelf. Tom nodded. He was big and strong. He climbed on a chair and took the jar. He tried to open it. He twisted and pulled. But the jar was too tight. He could not open it. "Let me try, Tom," Lily said. She climbed on the chair too. She took the jar from Tom. She tried to open it. She twisted and pulled. But the jar was too tight. She could not open it either. They looked at each other. They felt sad. They wanted to eat some jam on bread. But they could not open the jar. Then they heard a voice. It was Mom. She came into the kitchen. She saw Tom and Lily on the chair. She saw the jar of jam on the table. "What are you doing, kids?" Mom asked. She smiled. "We want to eat some jam on bread, Mom," Tom said. "But we can't open the jar. It's too tight." "Can you help us, Mom?" Lily said. "Please?" Mom nodded. She took the jar from Lily. She put it on the counter. She tapped it with a spoon. She twisted it with her hand. She opened it. "Here you go, kids," Mom said. She gave the jar to Tom and Lily. "Now you can eat some jam on bread. But be careful. Don't make a mess." Tom and Lily smiled. They thanked Mom. They took some bread and a knife. They spread some jam on the bread. They ate it. It was sweet and yummy. It was perfect.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy old cat named Tom. He liked to collect things. One day, he found a shiny ornament in the park. He wanted to take it home. Tom showed the ornament to his friend, a happy dog named Max. Max said, "Wow! That is pretty. Can I see?" Tom said, "No, this is mine. I found it." Max was sad, but he had an idea. He said, "Let's find more ornaments together!" Tom agreed, and they went to the park. They found more shiny ornaments and collected them together. Tom was not grumpy anymore. They both were happy and had a lot of fun.

Mum was keen to take the eager little girl to the park. She told the three year old that she could pick any flower she wanted, and soon the girl ran off with a smile. As the girl found a beautiful poppy, its petals were as delicate as snow. She smiled, happy as could be, and lifted the poppy to her nose. That's when she saw someone else also admiring the flower. He was small too, not much bigger than her. The boy asked if he could pick the flower too, but the girl shook her head, ready to cry. Mum then appeared. She smiled at them both and said “There are lots of poppies, why don't you both pick one?” The girl was relieved, and the two children smiled. They chose the two prettiest poppies and eagerly ran away to show Mum their beautiful flowers.
