Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Hairy Cat and the Fish

One day, a hairy cat named Tom went to a pond. He saw a lot of fish in the water. Tom was very hungry and he wanted to catch a fish to eat. Tom saw a big fish near the edge of the pond. He got ready to rush and catch the fish. He moved very fast, but the fish was faster. The fish swam away and Tom fell into the water. Tom was wet and sad, but he did not give up. He tried again and again to catch a fish. At last, he caught a small fish. Tom was very happy and went home to eat his fish.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly dog named Max. Max lived in a small house with a big yard. The yard had a gate that Max liked to sit by every day. One day, Max saw a little girl named Lily walk by the gate. She looked sad. Max wanted to help, so he reminded her to smile by wagging his tail and barking happily. Lily smiled and felt better. From that day on, Max and Lily became best friends. Every day, Lily would come to the gate to play with Max. They had lots of fun together and always remembered to smile.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play in the park. He saw a big rock and looked under it. He found a small bag. Inside the bag, there was gold! Tim was very happy. He said, "Wow! I found gold! This is a good day!" Tim took the bag of gold to his friend, Sam. Sam wanted the gold too. They both held the bag and did not want to let go. Tim said, "This is my gold. I found it." Sam said, "No, I want the gold. Please give it to me." They did not know what to do. Then, a wise old man came to them. He said, "You both can have some gold. Just yield and share it." Tim and Sam looked at each other. They knew the old man was right. They both said, "Okay, we will share the gold." They became good friends again and played happily in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a rocket. The rocket was very big and shiny. One day, the rocket wanted to go up into the sky. It wanted to see the stars and the moon. But a rude bird did not want the rocket to fly. The bird was scared that the rocket would make too much noise. The bird had an idea. It took a pair of scissors and tried to cut the rocket. The bird thought that if it cut the rocket, the rocket could not fly. But the bird was not very smart. The rocket was too big and strong for the bird to cut. Then, something unexpected happened. The rocket started to talk. It told the bird that it would not make too much noise. The bird was surprised and happy. So, the bird and the rocket became friends. They went up into the sky together and saw the stars and the moon. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He lived in a small house with his mom and dad. Tim loved to play with his ball. One day, he went outside to throw his ball in the yard. Tim saw a little bird sitting in a tree. The bird was calm and quiet. Tim said, "Hi, bird! Do you want to play with me?" The bird looked at Tim and said, "Yes, I would like to play with you." Tim threw the ball to the bird, and the bird caught it with its beak. They played catch until it was time for Tim to go inside for his wake. Tim said goodbye to the bird and went inside. They both had a fun day playing together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to climb. One day, he went to the park with his mom. At the park, he saw a big orange stand. The stand had many yummy things to eat. "Mom, can I have an orange?" Tim asked. His mom smiled and said, "Yes, you can have one." Tim was happy. He took the orange and started to climb a tree. He climbed higher and higher. When he got to the top, he sat on a branch and ate his orange. It was sweet and juicy. After he finished eating, Tim climbed down the tree. His mom was waiting for him. "Did you like your orange?" she asked. Tim nodded and said, "Yes, it was very yummy!" Then they went home, and Tim told his dad all about his adventure at the park.
