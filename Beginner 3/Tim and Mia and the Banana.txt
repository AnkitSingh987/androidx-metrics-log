Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Mia and the Banana

Tim and Mia like to play with their toy boat. They pretend to sail on the sea and have adventures. One day, they find a big island with many trees and animals. They want to explore it. "Look, Mia, a gorilla!" Tim says, pointing to a big, hairy creature. The gorilla is eating bananas and making loud noises. "Wow, Tim, he is big and strong. Do you think he is friendly?" Mia asks, feeling a little scared. "Let's find out. Maybe he wants to play with us." Tim says, holding Mia's hand. They walk slowly towards the gorilla. The gorilla sees them and stops eating. He looks at them with curiosity and tilts his head. He grunts and waves his arms. "Hello, gorilla. We are Tim and Mia. We come from the boat. Do you want to be our friend?" Tim says, smiling. The gorilla smiles back and nods. He picks up a banana and offers it to them. "Thank you, gorilla. You are very nice. Can we play with you?" Mia says, taking the banana. The gorilla nods again and claps his hands. He leads them to a big swing made of vines. He shows them how to swing and jump. Tim and Mia laugh and have fun. They play with the gorilla until the sun sets. Then they say goodbye and thank him for the banana and the swing. They promise to come back and visit him again. They sail back to their home, happy and tired. They tell their mom and dad about their new friend, the big gorilla.

Jen and Sam were friends. They liked to play with dolls and cars. One day, Jen had an idea. She said to Sam, "Let's go to the park. We can slide and swing and run." Sam said, "Yes, that sounds fun. But we need to ask mom first. She may not permit us to go." Jen said, "No, we don't need to ask. Mom is busy. She will not see us. Come on, let's go." Sam was scared, but he followed Jen. They took their dolls and cars and ran to the park. They had fun for a while. They slid and swung and ran. They laughed and talked. But then, a big dog came. The dog was not soft. The dog was angry. The dog barked and growled. The dog chased Jen and Sam. They dropped their dolls and cars and ran away. They cried and screamed. Mom heard them. She ran to the park. She saw the dog. She saw the dolls and cars. She was very mad. She grabbed Jen and Sam and took them home. She said, "You are very naughty. You did not ask me to go to the park. You did not listen to me. You could have been hurt by the dog. You lost your dolls and cars. You are in big trouble. Go to your room. No more play for you today." Jen and Sam were very sad. They wished they had not gone to the park. They wished they had asked mom first. They wished they had a better idea.

John and Jenny were walking to the park. It was a sunny day and they had lots of fun. Suddenly, they saw a cop. John and Jenny paused, looking at the cop. He smiled and said, "Hello, what are you guys up to?" John replied, "We're going to the park. We love playing there." The cop laughed, "That sounds like fun. Do you have anything to eat? I'm hungry." John thought for a minute and then said, "Oh yes, I have some spicy chips." The cop smiled, "That sounds great! I'll have some, please." John handed the cop the chips and he thanked them, before walking away. John and Jenny watched as the cop walked away. They smiled and continued on to the park, feeling proud of themselves.

Once upon a time, there was a little purple bird named Polly. Polly loved to fly around and play with her friends. One day, she saw a big screen in a house. The screen was showing pictures of birds just like her. Polly went to her friend, a kind cat named Tom. "Tom, I saw a screen with birds like me," said Polly. Tom wanted to help his friend, so he said, "Let's go see the screen together!" When they got to the house, they saw a little girl crying. She was sad because she missed her friends. Polly and Tom wanted to comfort her. They sang a happy song and danced. The little girl stopped crying and smiled. From that day on, they all became good friends and played together every day.
