Category: Beginner
Title: Sam the Healthy Cat and the Bird

One day, a healthy cat named Sam went for a walk. It was a sunny day in March. Sam liked to walk and balance on the fence. Sam saw a bird on the fence too. The bird was blue and small. "Hi, bird," said Sam. "Do you want to balance with me?" The bird looked at Sam and said, "Yes, let's balance together." Sam and the bird started to balance on the fence. They were having fun. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the bird off the fence. But Sam caught the bird with his paw. "Thank you, Sam," said the bird. "You saved me!" Sam was happy he could help. They became best friends and balanced on the fence every day in March.

Once there was a boy named Jake. Jake wanted to have fun and go fast. So he decided to ride his bike near the landscape. He began to ride so fast the wind was blowing his hair. He was so happy and excited he let out a loud yell. But he was going too fast! He was going so fast he couldn't see the pothole in the road. His bike tripped in the pothole, and Jake flew over the handlebars. He landed with a thud and his bike went flying. Jake laid there, feeling dizzy and scared. He looked up at the landscape and cried for help. But no help came. He tried to get up, but he hurt too much. Jake was never able to go back to his home. He was stuck in the landscape forever and ever.

Once upon a time, there was a big, heavy gorilla named Gogo. Gogo lived in a nice, green forest with his friends. They liked to play and have fun all day. One day, Gogo wanted to get a big, red ball to play with. He asked his friends to help him find one. They looked high and low, but they could not find a big, red ball. Then, Gogo saw the big, red ball up in a tall tree. He tried to get it, but it was too high. His friends helped him by making a big pile of soft leaves. Gogo jumped on the leaves and got the big, red ball. They were all happy and played with the ball all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had long, pretty hair. Lily loved to play with her hair all day. But sometimes, she was selfish and did not want to share her hair toys with her friends. One day, Lily met a tiny mouse. The mouse was very shy and would only whisper. The mouse whispered to Lily, "Can I please play with your hair toys too?" Lily thought about it and decided to share her toys with the mouse. Lily and the mouse had so much fun playing together. They laughed and whispered secrets to each other. Lily learned that sharing her hair toys was not so bad. She was happy to have a new friend and not be selfish anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little dog named Spot. Spot loved to chew on things. One day, Spot found a big rock in the yard. He thought it would be fun to chew on the rock. Spot's friend, a cat named Fluffy, saw him with the rock. Fluffy said, "Spot, don't chew on that rock! It's not good for your teeth." But Spot didn't listen. He was too nosy and wanted to know how the rock tasted. Spot chewed and chewed on the rock. But then, he felt a pain in his mouth. He had broken his tooth! Spot was sad and his mouth hurt. He wished he had listened to Fluffy. From that day on, Spot learned not to chew on rocks and to listen to his friends.

One day, a little fish named Fin wanted to swim. He swam to a camp where kids play. He saw the kids and felt envious. He wanted to play with them too. Fin said, "Hi kids, can I play with you?" The kids looked at Fin and said, "Yes, but we don't know how to swim." Fin smiled and said, "I can teach you!" Fin taught the kids how to swim. They were having lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. Fin started to grow legs! He became a little boy. Now, Fin could play with the kids at the camp all day. They were all very happy. And the kids never forgot how their new friend taught them how to swim.
