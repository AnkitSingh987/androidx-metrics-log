Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Drawer

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a drawer. It was a big drawer. Tim wanted to open it, but his mom said it was dangerous. Tim was sad. He wanted to see what was inside. So, he went to his friend, Sam. He asked, "Can you help me open the drawer?" Sam said, "Yes, but we need to be careful." They went back to Tim's house. They opened the drawer. Inside, they found a big, red ball. They were very happy. They played with the ball. But then, the ball hit a lamp. The lamp fell and broke. Tim's mom came in and saw the broken lamp. She was very angry. She said, "I told you not to open the drawer. Now you have made a big mess." Tim and Sam were sad. They knew they should have listened to Tim's mom.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Lily. She was very hungry after playing all day. Her mom made a yummy dessert for her to eat. Lily was so happy and excited. Lily wanted to eat the dessert very fast. She ran to the table, but she did not look where she was going. She did not see the toy on the floor. Lily stumbled on the toy and fell down. Her dessert fell on the floor too. Lily was sad and her mom was worried. Her mom gave her a hug and told her to be careful next time. Lily learned that she should not run when she is hungry and excited. She should walk slowly and look where she is going. This way, she can enjoy her dessert and be safe.

Sara likes to eat oat for breakfast. She puts oat and milk in a bowl and puts it in the microwave. She waits for the beep sound. But today, the microwave is very slow. Sara is impatient. She wants to eat her oat now. She taps her foot and looks at the clock. She counts to ten. She counts to twenty. She counts to thirty. The microwave is still not done. Sara is very impatient. She opens the door and takes out her bowl. The oat is still cold and hard. Sara is unhappy. She puts the bowl back in the microwave and closes the door. She pushes the buttons. She hopes the microwave will end soon. She waits for the beep sound. She hears a ding. She opens the door and takes out her bowl. The oat is hot and soft. Sara is happy. She takes a spoon and eats her oat. She likes the sweet and creamy taste. She smiles and says, "Yum, yum, yum!" She finishes her oat and puts the bowl in the sink. She is full and ready for the day. She says, "Bye, bye, oat!" and runs to her room. She looks for her toys. She wants to play.

One day, a healthy person named Sam went for a walk. Sam liked to walk in the park. The park was big and had many trees. The sun was shining and birds were singing. While walking, Sam saw a small house. The door was open. Sam said, "Hello? Can I enter?" A soft voice from inside said, "Yes, please come in." Sam entered the house and saw a little cat. The cat was cute and fluffy. Sam and the cat became friends. They played together in the house. They laughed and had fun. Then, it was time for Sam to go home. Sam said, "Goodbye, little cat. I will come back to play again." The cat said, "Goodbye, Sam. I will wait for you." And they both smiled, knowing they would see each other again.

Anna and Ben like to play with puzzles. They have many puzzles with different pictures. They can solve the puzzles by finding the right pieces and putting them together. One day, they find a new puzzle in the toy box. It has a picture of a big lion. They want to solve the puzzle and see the lion. They take out the pieces and start to look for the ones that match. But some of the pieces are broken. They have cracks and holes. Anna and Ben are sad. They cannot solve the puzzle with broken pieces. "Maybe we can fix them with glue," Ben says. He remembers that Mom has glue in the drawer. He runs to get it. He comes back with a bottle of glue. He tries to put some glue on the broken pieces. But the glue is weak. It does not stick well. The pieces fall apart again. "This glue is no good," Ben says. He throws the bottle away. Anna has an idea. She says, "Maybe we can use tape instead of glue. Tape is strong. It can hold the pieces together." She finds some tape in the box. She cuts some pieces of tape and puts them on the broken pieces. The tape sticks well. The pieces do not fall apart. Anna and Ben are happy. They can solve the puzzle with tape. They finish the puzzle and see the lion. The lion looks good, even with some tape on it. They show the puzzle to Mom. Mom smiles and says, "You did a great job. You solved the puzzle and fixed the pieces. You are very smart and creative."
