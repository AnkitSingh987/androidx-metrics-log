Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Story

Lily was a little girl who liked to play with her toys. She had a doll, a bear, a ball and a car. She gave them all names. The doll was Anna, the bear was Ben, the ball was Coco and the car was Dan. She liked to make them talk and have fun. One day, Lily was playing with her toys in her room. She pretended that Anna and Ben were having a tea party, and Coco and Dan were racing around the floor. She made them say hello and bye and thank you and please. She was having a lot of fun. But then, she heard her mom call her from the kitchen. "Lily, it's time for lunch!" her mom said. Lily put her toys down and ran to the kitchen. She was hungry. She saw her mom had made her a sandwich and some soup. She said thank you and sat down to eat. She took a bite of her sandwich. It was good. She liked cheese and ham. She took a sip of her soup. It was hot. She blew on it to cool it down. She took another sip. It was spicy. She made a face and said, "Mom, this soup is spicy!" Her mom smiled and said, "I know, honey. I put some pepper in it. Do you like it?" Lily shook her head and said, "No, mom. I don't like spicy. It makes my tongue hurt." Her mom said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I thought you might like to try something new. Here, have some water and some fruit. They will make your tongue feel better." Lily drank some water and ate some fruit. They did make her tongue feel better. She finished her lunch and said, "Mom, can I go back to play with my toys?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can. But first, you need to take a nap. You look tired. Naps are good for you. They make you grow and dream." Lily nodded and said, "OK, mom. I will take a nap. But can I take my toys with me?" Her mom said, "Sure, you can. But only one. Which one do you want?" Lily thought for a moment and said, "I want Ben. He is soft and cuddly. He will keep me warm and safe." Her mom said, "OK, then. Take Ben and go to your bed. I will tuck you in and read you a story." Lily took Ben and went to her bed. Her mom tucked her in and read her a story. It was about a princess and a dragon. Lily liked the story. She hugged Ben and closed her eyes. She fell asleep and had a dream. She dreamed that she and Ben were flying on the dragon. They saw Anna, Coco and Dan waving at them from the ground. They waved back and smiled. They were happy.

Once upon a time there were two brothers, Joe and Dave, who loved to learn new things. Joe was always eager to teach Dave something new so they often went to the street together to learn new things. One day, Joe was teaching Dave how to ride a bike. Dave was having a hard time but Joe kept encouraging him. Suddenly, a new neighborhood friend, Jake, came by. Jake was envious of what Joe was teaching Dave so he asked if he could learn too. Joe quickly said yes and he started teaching Jake too. Dave was a bit jealous but Joe reassured him that there's plenty of things to teach. After some practice, both Dave and Jake were able to ride their bikes on their own. The brothers felt very proud of themselves and their new friend. From that day on, they went to the street together to teach and learn new things.

Once upon a time, there was a little reindeer. He was a troubled reindeer because he could not fly like his friends. One day, he met a nice bird who wanted to help him. The bird said, "We can shoot up into the sky together!" The reindeer was happy and wanted to try. So, the bird held on to the reindeer and they tried to fly. They jumped and ran, but the reindeer could not fly. The reindeer was sad, but the bird had an idea. "Let's try again, but this time, close your eyes and believe you can fly!" The reindeer closed his eyes, and as they tried to shoot up into the sky, he started to fly! The reindeer was not troubled anymore, and he and the bird became best friends. They flew together all the time, and they were very happy.
