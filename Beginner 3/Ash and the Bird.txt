Category: Beginner
Title: Ash and the Bird

Once upon a time in a big forest, there was a friendly, reliable tree named Ash. Ash was friends with all the animals in the forest. One sunny day, a little bird came to Ash and said, "Ash, can you please help me? I lost my nest." Ash, being a good friend, wanted to help the little bird. So, Ash bowed its branches down low, so the little bird could look for its nest. They looked and looked, but they could not find the nest. Finally, Ash had an idea. "Little bird, why don't you build a new nest on one of my branches? I will keep you safe and warm." The little bird was happy with Ash's idea and built a new nest on Ash's branch. From that day on, the little bird and Ash became the best of friends, and they lived happily ever after.

One day, a helpful dog named Spot went for a walk. He liked to observe things around him. Spot saw a big gray thing in the sky. It was a tornado! The tornado was very big and loud. Spot ran to his friends, the birds. He told them about the tornado. The birds were scared. They wanted to fly away from the tornado. Spot wanted to help them. Spot and the birds went to a safe place. They hid under a big tree. The tornado went away. Spot and the birds were happy. They thanked Spot for being so helpful.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to bake with her mom. They would bake cookies, cakes, and pies. Lucy always felt warm and happy when they baked together. One day, Lucy's mom said, "Let's bake a big cake!" Lucy was so excited. They put flour, sugar, and eggs into a bowl. They mixed it all up. Then, they put the cake in the oven to bake. While they waited, they talked and laughed. When the cake was done, they took it out of the oven. It was warm and smelled so good. Lucy's mom said, "Wow, this cake has a lot of weight!" They both laughed. They shared the cake with their friends and family. Everyone loved it and they all had a great day.

One day, a big, hairy dog named Spot went for a walk. Spot liked to play and run. He saw a ball and wanted to play with it. The ball was next to a plant. The plant was poison, but Spot did not know. Spot played with the ball and touched the poison plant. His paw started to hurt. Spot did not like the hurt. He wanted the hurt to stop. Spot went to find help. Spot found his friend, a little girl named Sue. Sue saw Spot's hurt paw and knew what to do. She washed the poison off his paw and gave him a hug. Spot's hurt started to stop. Spot and Sue were happy and played together all day.

One day, a big ant named Tom found a rake in the garden. He wanted to take it to his ant friends. He knew they could use it to help clean their home. So, Tom started to march with the rake. As he marched, Tom felt very alert. He looked around to make sure no birds or big feet would step on him. He saw a big rock, a tall tree, and a pretty flower. The ants' home was not far away now. When Tom got to the ants' home, he showed them the rake. They were happy and said, "Thank you, Tom!" They used the rake to clean their home and make it look nice. Tom felt proud and marched back to the garden with a big smile.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly lady who had a big prize. The prize was a big, red ball. She loved her prize very much. One day, she went to the park to play with her prize. She was very happy. In the park, there was a mean boy. He saw the elderly lady with her prize. He wanted the big, red ball for himself. The elderly lady did not want to give her prize to the mean boy. The mean boy got very mad. The mean boy tried to think of a way to get the prize. He thought and thought. Then, he had an idea. He ran up to the elderly lady and took her prize away. The elderly lady was very sad. The mean boy was happy, but nobody liked him anymore.
