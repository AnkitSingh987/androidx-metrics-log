Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Tom Find the Toys

Lily and Tom were twins who liked to play with their toys. One day, they found a big armchair in the living room. It was soft and cozy and had a lot of pillows. Lily and Tom wanted to sit on the armchair and pretend it was a castle. But there was a problem. The armchair only had room for one person. Lily and Tom both tried to climb on the armchair, but they pushed and pulled each other. They started to fight and shout. "Go away, Tom! This is my armchair!" Lily said. "No, it's mine! You go away, Lily!" Tom said. They fought so hard that they fell off the armchair and bumped their heads. They both started to weep and cry. Mom heard the noise and came to the living room. She saw Lily and Tom on the floor, weeping and holding their heads. She also saw the armchair with the pillows. "What happened here?" Mom asked. "Lily and Tom wanted the armchair, but it was too small. They fought and fell and hurt themselves," Mom said. Mom hugged Lily and Tom and kissed their heads. She said, "I'm sorry you got hurt, my loves. But you know, fighting is not nice. You have to share and take turns. That's fair and kind." Lily and Tom stopped weeping and looked at Mom. They felt sorry for fighting. They said, "We're sorry, Mom. We love you. We love each other. We want to share and take turns." Mom smiled and said, "That's good, my loves. I love you too. Now, why don't you play with the armchair together? You can take turns sitting on it, or you can both sit on the floor and use the pillows to make a fort." Lily and Tom liked Mom's idea. They said, "Thank you, Mom. That sounds fun." They hugged Mom and then went to play with the armchair and the pillows. They were happy and fair and kind.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had an amazing dog named Spot. They loved to play together every day. One day, Amy saw her big sister study. She asked, "What are you doing?" Her sister said, "I am studying for school." Amy wanted to study too, but she was too young for school. Amy had an idea. She put her books on Spot's shoulder and they studied together. They had so much fun learning new things. And that's how Amy and Spot became the best study buddies ever.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a wise old man. He had a big treasure box. Many people in the town wanted the treasure, but no one could get it. The wise man hid it very well. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the wise man's house. He asked the wise man if he could see the treasure. The wise man said, "Yes, but you must not lose it." Tim promised he would not lose the treasure. Tim took the treasure box to show his friends. They all looked at the treasure and played with it. But then, they forgot to put it back in the box. The wind blew, and the treasure was lost. Tim and his friends searched and searched, but they could not find the treasure. The wise man was very sad when he found out the treasure was lost. Tim felt bad and said sorry, but the treasure was still gone. The town people were also sad because they lost the wise man's treasure forever. From that day on, Tim learned to be more careful with things that were not his. But the treasure was never found again, and the town was not as happy as before.

Once there was a boy named Tim. He wanted to send a gift to his friend, Sam. Tim went to the store to find a gift. He saw a cheap suit. He thought that Sam would like the suit. Tim said, "I will buy this cheap suit for Sam." He bought the suit and sent it to Sam's house. When Sam got the gift, he was very happy. He called Tim and said, "Thank you, Tim! I love my new suit." Tim was happy that Sam liked the gift. They both learned that gifts do not have to be expensive to make someone happy. The important thing is to be kind and think of others.
