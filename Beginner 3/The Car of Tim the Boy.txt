Category: Beginner
Title: The Car of Tim the Boy

One day, a boy named Tim saw a broken toy car on the floor. It was empty inside. He wanted to repair it and make it work again. Tim asked his friend, Sam, to help him. "Sam, can you help me fix this toy car?" Tim asked. Sam said, "Yes, I can help you!" They worked together to repair the toy car. They put all the parts back in place and the toy car looked good as new. But then, something unexpected happened. A big dog came running and took the toy car away in its mouth. Tim and Sam tried to get the toy car back, but the dog ran away with it. They couldn't see the dog anymore. Tim and Sam felt sad because they couldn't play with their fixed toy car.

Once upon a time, in a tidy room, there was a box of colorful marbles. They were shiny and pretty. A little boy named Tim loved to play with them every day. One day, Tim saw a big marble that he wanted to grab. It was blue and very nice. He reached out his hand and grabbed the big marble. He was so happy to have it in his hand. Tim played with the big marble and the other marbles all day. He made them roll and jump. When it was time to sleep, Tim put all the marbles back in the box. He made sure the room was tidy again. Tim went to bed with a big smile, excited to play with his marbles again the next day.

John went to the park on Wednesday. He felt happy to find so many things he liked. He saw lots of birds, trees, swings and slides. He was so excited. John kept playing until it got dark. Suddenly, he felt so scared and alone. He started to cry and yelled, "Mommy! Where are you?" John heard someone calling him. It was his mommy! He ran to her and hugged her tightly. John's mommy said, "It's terrible to be alone in the dark. I'm glad you waited here and didn't wander off. Let's go home now." John nodded and smiled. He was glad that he found his mommy again.

Once, there was a little girl named Sue. She was three years old and liked yellow things. One day, Sue and her mom were playing in the park when they saw a loud balloon. The balloon was yellow and Sue wanted to take it home. "Can we take this balloon home?" she asked her mom. But her mom refused. "No darling, that balloon doesn't belong to us," she said. Sue was very sad and started to cry. She asked her mom again and again, but her mom kept refusing. Finally, Sue's mom said, "Let's get you a new yellow balloon that belongs to you. That will make you happy!" Sue was delighted and smiled. She and her mom bought a new yellow balloon and went home. Sue hugged the balloon tightly and said goodbye to the loud yellow one at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. He loved to imagine and play all day. One day, Tim found a big iron in his mom's room. He did not know what it was, but he thought it looked cool. Tim showed the iron to his friend, Sam. "Look what I found!" he said. Sam looked at the iron and said, "I don't know what it is, but let's play with it!" They played with the iron all day, pretending it was a magic wand. They did not know what would happen next. The next day, Tim's mom was looking for her iron. She asked Tim, "Have you seen my iron?" Tim showed her where he and Sam had left it. When she picked it up, it was covered in glitter from their playtime. Tim's mom laughed and said, "Well, I guess it really is a magic wand now!" Tim and Sam smiled, happy that their fun game had made his mom laugh.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a big hat. One day, he was careless and lost his hat. Tim was sad. Tim went to his friend, Sue. "Sue, help me find my hat," said Tim. Sue said, "Okay, let's look for your hat." They looked and looked, but could not find the hat. Then, Sue had an idea. "Let's attach a string to your hat, so you won't lose it again." They found the hat under a tree. They attached a string to the hat. Tim was happy and said, "Thank you, Sue!" Now, Tim will not be careless with his hat again.
