Category: Beginner
Title: The Blackboard and the Helpless

Once upon a time, in a small school, there was a big blackboard. The blackboard was very sad and felt helpless. It wanted to be with the other blackboards, but it was stuck on the wall. One day, a kind teacher saw the sad blackboard. She wanted to help the blackboard. She thought hard and had a good idea. She asked her strong friend to come and help her pull the blackboard off the wall. The strong friend came and pulled the blackboard with all his might. With a big tug, the blackboard was free! The blackboard was so happy and thankful. Now, it could be with the other blackboards and not feel helpless anymore. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play with her mom's jewelry. One day, Lily found a dull necklace in her mom's jewelry box. She thought it was not pretty, so she decided to make it shiny and bright. Lily hurried to her friend, Tim, who lived next door. "Tim, can you help me make this necklace pretty?" she asked. Tim looked at the dull necklace and said, "Yes, let's hurry and find some shiny things to add to it!" They found some pretty beads and colorful strings. They worked together to make the dull necklace bright and shiny. When they were done, Lily said, "Thank you, Tim! The necklace is so pretty now!" They both smiled and hurried back to show Lily's mom the beautiful necklace they made together.

One day, a little bird named Tim was looking for food. He flew near a big bush. Tim was very intelligent. He knew the bush had yummy berries. As he was eating, he heard someone complain. "Ouch! My foot hurts!" said a tiny bug, named Sam. Tim saw Sam near the bush. Sam was sad because he was hurt. "Hi, Sam! I can help you," said Tim. Tim picked up Sam and put him on a soft leaf. Sam felt better and thanked Tim. They became good friends and played near the bush every day.

Ben and his dad were going to the office. Dad worked at the office. He helped people with their papers and computers. Ben liked to visit the office. He could see dad's friends and play with some toys. "Are you ready, Ben?" dad asked. He put on his coat and hat. "Yes, dad!" Ben said. He grabbed his backpack and his teddy bear. He followed dad to the car. They drove to the office. Dad parked the car and took Ben's hand. They walked to the big door. Dad pushed a button and the door opened. They went inside. "Hello, Ben!" a lady said. She was dad's boss. She smiled and gave Ben a sticker. It had a star on it. "You are a very dependable boy. You always come with your dad when he returns to the office." "Thank you!" Ben said. He put the sticker on his backpack. He liked the lady. She was nice and gave him stickers. Dad took Ben to his desk. He showed him his papers and his computer. He let Ben type some letters on the keyboard. Ben had fun. He made some words and some pictures. "Look, dad!" Ben said. He pointed to the screen. He had typed "I love dad". Dad hugged Ben. He was proud of his son. He said, "I love you too, Ben. You are the best thing that ever happened to me." They spent some time at the office. Then they said goodbye to the lady and dad's friends. They went back to the car. They drove home. Ben was happy. He had a good day with his dad. He knew his dad was dependable. He always returned to the office and to him.

One day, a gray cat went for a walk. The cat saw a big loop on the ground. The loop was red and blue. The cat played with the loop and had lots of fun. A little girl saw the cat play. She wanted to play too. She asked the cat, "Can you give me the loop?" The gray cat was nice and gave the loop to the little girl. The little girl and the gray cat played with the loop. They had so much fun together. They became best friends and played with the loop every day.
