Category: Beginner
Title: The Bug and the Ant

Once upon a time, there was a little bug who loved to crawl. He had many friends in the garden, but his best friend was a tiny ant. They played together every day. One sunny morning, the bug and the ant found a big piece of toast on the ground. They were very hungry and wanted to share the toast. The bug said, "Let's break it and eat it together." The ant agreed, and they started to nibble on the toast. But then, the bug accidentally stepped on the ant's foot. The ant cried out in pain. The bug felt very sorry and said, "I am sorry, my friend. I did not mean to hurt you." The ant smiled and said, "It's okay. I know you didn't mean it." They hugged each other and continued to eat the toast, happy to be friends again.

Once upon a time, in a cool, green park, there lived a cat named Stretch. Stretch was a very happy cat. He loved to stretch his long legs and chase after birds and bugs. One day, Stretch met a new friend named Dream. Dream was a small, blue bird. "Hi, Stretch!" said Dream. "Want to play?" Stretch was very happy to have a new friend. They played all day and had lots of fun. But one day, something unexpected happened. Stretch and Dream were playing, and Stretch almost caught Dream! Dream was scared. Stretch felt bad. He said, "I'm sorry, Dream. I didn't mean to scare you." From that day on, Stretch learned to be more careful when playing with his friends. He didn't want to scare them away. The moral of the story is to always think about how your actions can affect others.

John was walking down the street when he noticed a worried little girl. He asked her if something was wrong. She said, "Yes! I let go of my mattress, and it's gone!" John was very worried now. He asked her where the mattress had gone and she said, "I don't know. I just let it go, and now it's gone". John was so worried he wanted to help her look for it. So, they began to search the streets for the lost mattress. Sadly, after hours of searching, they still couldn't find it. The little girl was very sad and started to cry. John didn't know what to do. He wanted to help her but the mattress was gone and there was nothing he could do.

Ben and Mia were twins who loved to go to the zoo with their mom. They liked to see all the different animals and learn their names. But sometimes they did not behave very well. They would run away from their mom, touch the animals when they were not allowed, and make a lot of noise. One day, they went to see the whale show. The whale was very big and could do amazing tricks. It could jump out of the water, splash with its tail, and make loud sounds. Ben and Mia were very excited and wanted to get closer to the whale. They did not listen to their mom when she told them to stay with her. They ran to the edge of the pool and leaned over the railing. "Look, Mia, the whale is so fancy!" Ben said. "It has a shiny skin and a funny nose. I want to touch it!" "Me too, Ben!" Mia said. "Maybe it will let us ride on its back!" They reached out their hands to try to touch the whale. But they did not see the sign that said "Do not touch the whale. It may bite." The whale saw their hands and thought they were food. It opened its mouth and snapped at them. "Help, mom!" Ben and Mia screamed. They were very scared and pulled back their hands. They almost fell into the water. Their mom heard their screams and ran to them. She grabbed them and hugged them. She was very angry and worried. "Ben and Mia, what were you doing?" she asked. "You could have been hurt by the whale. You did not behave at all. You did not follow the rules. You did not listen to me. You were very naughty." Ben and Mia felt very sorry. They realized they had made a big mistake. They apologized to their mom and to the whale. They promised to behave better next time. The whale heard their apology and forgave them. It gave them a friendly wink and a splash. It hoped they had learned their lesson. The end.
