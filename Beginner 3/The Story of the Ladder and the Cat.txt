Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Ladder and the Cat

One day, a big cat and a little dog were friends. They liked to spend time together. The big cat had a fancy hat. The little dog wanted a fancy hat too. The big cat said, "I know where to find a fancy hat. It is up high. We need a ladder." They found a ladder and put it against a tall tree. The big cat climbed up the ladder first. The little dog was scared, but he wanted the fancy hat. The little dog climbed up the ladder too. They both found fancy hats. But the little dog fell off the ladder. He got hurt. The big cat said, "I am sorry. We should not have climbed the ladder." The moral of the story is: Do not do things that are too hard or scary for you, even if you want something fancy.

Ben and Mia are playing with blocks. They like to build towers and houses and cars. Mia has a screw that she found in the garage. She thinks it is shiny and cool. She puts it on top of her tower. Ben sees the screw and wants it. He thinks it is his. He says, "Give me that screw, Mia. It is mine. I saw it first." Mia says, "No, Ben. It is mine. I found it. It is on my tower." They start to argue. Ben tries to grab the screw, but Mia holds it tight. Ben gets angry and pushes Mia. Mia falls down and cries. The tower falls down too and the blocks scatter. The screw rolls away. Mom hears the noise and comes to see what is wrong. She sees Ben and Mia on the floor. She sees the blocks and the screw. She is not happy. She says, "What happened here? Why are you pushing and crying? Why do you have a screw?" Ben and Mia feel sorry. They say, "We are sorry, Mom. We were fighting over the screw. We both wanted it. We forgot to share and be nice." Mom says, "The screw is not a toy. It is dangerous. You could hurt yourself or break something. You should not play with it. You should play with your blocks. They are safe and fun. And you should share and be nice to each other. That is how you make friends and have a good time." Mom takes the screw and puts it away. She helps Ben and Mia pick up the blocks. She gives them a hug and a kiss. She says, "I love you, Ben and Mia. You are my lively and lovely children. Now, let's play together and have some fun." Ben and Mia smile and nod. They say, "We love you, Mom. We are sorry. We will play together and have some fun." They hug and kiss Mom. They start to build a new tower with their blocks. They share and be nice. They are happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a cheerful boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy sack. He would fill it with his favorite toys and take it everywhere he went. One sunny day, Tim went to the park with his toy sack. He saw a figure standing under a tree. It was his friend, Lily. "Hi, Lily!" Tim said. "Do you want to play with me and my toy sack?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, Tim! I would love to play with you and your toy sack." So, Tim and Lily sat down on the soft grass and took out the toys from the sack. They played with their toys, laughed, and had a lot of fun. After a while, it was time for Tim and Lily to go home. They put the toys back in the sack and said goodbye to each other. Tim felt happy that he had shared his toy sack with his friend. He knew that playing with friends was the best part of his day.

One day, a dog named Spot went to the veterinarian. The veterinarian was a nice man who helped animals feel better. Spot was deaf, but he was a very happy dog. Spot's owner, a little girl named Lucy, loved him very much. At the veterinarian, Spot saw a big cat. The cat said, "Hello, I am Fluffy. Do you remember me?" But Spot could not hear Fluffy because he was deaf. Fluffy looked sad, but then had an idea. Fluffy wrote "Hi, I'm Fluffy" on a piece of paper and showed it to Spot. Spot wagged his tail and was happy to meet Fluffy. While they were waiting, the veterinarian came out with a big box. He opened the box, and a bunch of balloons flew out! It was a surprise party for Spot and Fluffy. The veterinarian wanted to make them feel special. Spot and Fluffy played with the balloons and had a great time. Lucy was so happy to see Spot having fun, and she knew the veterinarian was a very kind man.
