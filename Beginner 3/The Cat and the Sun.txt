Category: Beginner
Title: The Cat and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a big cat. The cat loved to lie in the sun. The sun made the cat feel hot and happy. The cat felt like it was a luxury to lie in the sun every day. One day, the cat went to lie in the sun, but there was no sun. The cat was sad. It looked up at the sky and saw a big cloud. The cloud was hiding the sun. The cat did not feel hot or happy anymore. Just then, a strong wind blew the cloud away. The sun came out, and the cat felt hot and happy again! But, the wind also blew a big red ball to the cat. The cat had never seen a ball before. The cat played with the ball all day. Now, the cat had a new luxury: lying in the sun and playing with its new red ball.

One day, a big, loud horn lived in a small house. The horn loved to read books. He read books every day. He had a friend named Bird. Bird liked to sing songs. One morning, Bird came to visit the horn. "Let's play!" said Bird. "I want to read my book first," said the horn. Bird waited for the horn to finish reading. While the horn read, Bird saw a big tree outside. The tree looked like a fun place to play. When the horn finished reading, Bird said, "I found a big tree outside! Let's play there!" The horn and Bird went to the big tree. They played and had fun all day. The horn was happy that he read his book and also had fun with his friend Bird.

Once upon a time, there was a gloomy day. A little boy named Tim wanted to play outside. He put on his coat and boots. He picked up his favorite bag. In the bag, there were many fun things to play with. Tim went to the park. He saw his friend, Sam. They decided to play catch with a ball. Tim reached into his bag and took out the ball. He threw the ball to Sam. Sam caught the ball and threw it back to Tim. They laughed and had lots of fun. As they played, the gloomy day became sunny. The sun came out and made everything bright. Tim and Sam played all day long. They were very happy. When it was time to go home, they put the ball back in the bag. They said goodbye and went home.

Sara was playing with her dolls when she heard a noise. She looked up, and saw the most adorable little bird flitting around in the sky. She tried to reach up and catch it, but it was too high. Sara sighed. Suddenly, an even cuter creature appeared. It was a tiny purple alien and it had a twinkle in its eye. "Hi, I'm Mie. What's your name?" The alien asked. "I'm Sara," she said. Mie smiled and Sara couldn't help but smile back. The alien said "I come from space, but I'm all alone here. Could you be my friend?" Sara was so happy! She said "Yes! Of course I'll be your friend!" She hugged Mie and then planted a soft kiss on the alien's cheek. Mie giggled and said "This is the best thing I've ever seen!" Sara and Mie became the best of friends and played together all the time. They would go on adventures and explore new and exciting places. Sara would always give Mie cute little kisses, and Mie would tell stories about his home in space. It was the perfect friendship and they were both so happy.

Once there was a big steel pot. The pot lived in a gloomy kitchen. The pot was sad because no one talked to it. The pot wanted to have friends. One day, a little spoon came into the kitchen. The spoon saw the sad pot and said, "Hi, I am Spoon. Why are you sad, Pot?" The pot was happy that someone talked to it. The pot said, "I am sad because I have no friends." Spoon and Pot became good friends. They talked and played all day. The kitchen was not gloomy anymore. The pot was not sad anymore. They were happy to have each other as friends.
