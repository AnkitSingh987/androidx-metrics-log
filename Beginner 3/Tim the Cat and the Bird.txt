Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Cat and the Bird

One day, a little cat named Tim went for a long walk. He walked and walked until he found a big tree. Tim saw a bird on a branch and wanted to play with it. Tim tried to resist, but he could not. He climbed the tree to reach the bird. "Hello, bird!" Tim said. "Do you want to play with me?" The bird looked at Tim and said, "I am too busy. I need to find oil for my nest. Can you help me find some oil?" Tim and the bird looked around. They saw a small can of oil under the tree. "There it is!" Tim said. They took the oil back to the bird's nest. The bird was very happy and thanked Tim for his help. "Now we can play together," said the bird. Tim and the bird played all day long. They became best friends and promised to help each other when they needed it. And they lived happily in their tree, always ready for a new adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big hose in his yard. He liked to sit on the grass and play with the hose. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. They were very happy. "Let's play with the hose!" said Tim. "Okay!" said Sam. They played with the hose, making water go everywhere. They laughed and had a brilliant time. But they did not know that something unexpected was about to happen. While they were playing, a big bird flew by and saw the water. The bird wanted to play too. It swooped down and picked up the hose. Tim and Sam were very surprised. The bird sprayed water all over them. They laughed even harder. The bird played with them, and they all had a great time.

Molly was a 3-year-old girl who lived in a big house. One day she asked her mom for a novel. Her mom said no and Molly frowned. She wanted a novel so she went to ask her dad. “Dad, can I have a novel?” Molly asked. “No,” he said. “That's too distant for a girl your age.” Molly frowned again. She wasn't happy with her parents' answer. However, she heard something coming from the living room. It was the sound of laughter. Molly went to the living room and saw her mom and dad laughing together. Molly smiled and ran to her mom and dad. She hugged them and they hugged her back. Molly knew that even though her parents weren't getting her the novel, she still had the best parents in the world.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly fish named Fin. Fin lived in a small pond with other fish. They all liked to play together. One day, the water in the pond got very cold. Fin and his friends started to shiver. They swam close to each other to keep warm. Fin had an idea. He told his friends to swim fast in a circle. They all swam fast and the water got warmer. They stopped shivering and played happily again.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the farm with his mom. They saw many big pumpkins. Tim wanted to pick the biggest pumpkin to take home. His mom said, "We can write your name on the pumpkin, Tim!" On the farm, there was a naughty goat. The goat liked to eat everything. Tim saw the goat and said, "You better not eat my pumpkin, naughty goat!" The goat just looked at Tim and went away. But Tim knew the goat would come back. Later, Tim found the biggest pumpkin and wrote his name on it. He remembered the naughty goat and thought of a plan. He put the pumpkin on a high table, so the goat could not reach it. When it was time to go, Tim and his mom took the big pumpkin home. They were happy that the naughty goat did not eat their pumpkin.

Tom and Lily were playing in the garden. They liked to dig in the dirt and look for bugs. Tom had a big shovel and Lily had a small one. "Look, Lily, I found a worm!" Tom said, holding up a long, slimy thing. "It is brown and wiggly. Do you want to touch it?" Lily shook her head. She did not like worms. They made her feel icky. She wanted to find something else, like a ladybug or a butterfly. "No, thank you, Tom. I do not want to touch the worm. You can keep it," she said, digging in another spot. Tom shrugged. He liked worms. They were fun to watch and play with. He put the worm in his pocket and dug some more. Suddenly, he noticed something shiny in the dirt. He dug it out and saw that it was a ring. It had a big, red stone on it. It sparkled in the sun. "Wow, Lily, look what I found! It is a ring! It is pretty and shiny. Do you want to see it?" Tom said, running to Lily. Lily looked up and saw the ring. She liked shiny things. She wanted to see the ring. She reached out her hand. "Yes, please, Tom. I want to see the ring. It is pretty and shiny. Can I try it on?" she said, smiling. Tom nodded. He gave the ring to Lily. She put it on her finger. It was too big for her, but she did not care. She felt happy and proud. "Look, Tom, I have a ring! It is pretty and shiny. I look like a princess!" she said, waving her hand. Tom smiled. He was happy for Lily. He did not mind giving her the ring. He still had his worm. "You do look like a princess, Lily. You are brave and kind. I am glad you are my friend," he said, hugging her. Lily hugged him back. She was glad he was her friend too. He was funny and generous. She gave him the ring back. "Thank you, Tom. You are a good friend. You can have the ring back. I do not need it. I have you," she said, giving him the ring. Tom took the ring. He did not need it either. He had Lily. He put the ring in his pocket, next to his worm. They smiled at each other. They went back to digging in the dirt. They had fun. They were happy.
