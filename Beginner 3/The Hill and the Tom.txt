Category: Beginner
Title: The Hill and the Tom

Once upon a time, there was a big hill. On top of the hill was a small house. In the house lived a fit man named Tom. One day, Tom met a girl named Sue. Sue said, "You look fit. Do you value being strong?" Tom said, "Yes, I do. It helps me go up and down the hill." One day, Tom and Sue went to the hill to play. They ran up and down, but Tom was too fast for Sue. Sue got sad and fell down. Tom did not value being a good friend. He did not help Sue get up. Sue was hurt and cried. Tom did not have a friend to play with anymore.

There was a baby chick who was very frightened. He was living in a barn and the barn was very dark. One day, the baby chick heard a strange noise outside the barn. He was scared and he wanted to run away. The baby chick said to his mother, "Mommy, I'm scared. What was that noise?" His mother said, "Don't be scared, little one. Just listen and you will find out." As the baby chick listened, the strange noises got louder and closer. He heard footsteps and some loud cries. Suddenly, the doors of the barn opened and four ducks ran in. They were scared, too. The baby chick was so surprised. He looked at his mommy and saw that she was not scared. She understood what was going on. The baby chick learned that his mommy was right; he should have listened. He realised that he was safe and the ducks were only scared of the storm outside.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a humble man named Tim. He had a big van. Tim liked to help people with his van. One day, Tim saw a little girl crying. She said, "My toy is broken. Can you help me?" Tim wanted to help the girl. He thought he could restore her toy. Tim took the toy to his van. He fixed the toy with his tools. The toy was like new again. The little girl was very happy. She said, "Thank you, Tim!" From that day on, Tim helped many people in the town with his van. Everyone loved the humble man with the big van. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She wore her favorite heavy shoes. Lily liked to lead her dog, Spot, on their walks. They both loved to explore together. As they walked, they saw a big cat. Spot wanted to chase the cat. The cat said, "No, Spot! Don't chase me!" Spot did not listen, and the cat ran away. Lily's heavy shoes made it hard to run after Spot and the cat. Lily called out, "Spot, come back!" Spot stopped and came back to Lily. She said, "We need to be nice to the cat. No more chasing." Spot looked sad, but he knew Lily was right. They walked home, and Lily promised to play with Spot in the yard.

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk with her mom. They went to the park to play. It was a hot day, so they brought water to drink. Mia loved to play on the swings and slide. While playing, Mia saw a big ice cream truck. She looked at her mom and said, "Mom, can we get ice cream? I am hot and want something cold." Her mom said, "Yes, let's go get some ice cream." They walked to the ice cream truck and Mia realized she had to wait in line. There were other kids who wanted ice cream too. Mia waited and waited. Finally, it was her turn. Mia got a big ice cream cone and her mom got one too. They sat on a bench and enjoyed their cold treat on the hot day.

A small girl was very shy. She had an eraser and she wanted to draw with it. She went to a park and it was empty. The girl was very scared - she had never been to a park alone before. But it was quiet, so she felt brave and decided to stay. She sat down and started to rub the eraser against the grass. She rubbed back and forth until it was smooth and smooth. Suddenly, she felt a little less shy. She looked around to see if anyone was watching her, but no one was there. She was very happy - she had made something all by herself! She kept rubbing and rubbing until it was time to go home, and she left with a smile on her face.
