Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Furry Cat and the Bird

One day, a furry cat named Tom went out to play. He saw a little bird on a tree. Tom wanted to tease the bird, so he said, "Hey bird, I can jump higher than you!" The bird looked at Tom and said, "No, you can't." Tom jumped high to show the bird he was right. But when he jumped, he hit his head on a big skull that was hiding in the tree. The skull fell down and Tom was scared. The bird laughed and said, "I told you, you can't jump higher than me!" Tom felt sad and walked away. But then, something unexpected happened. The skull started to talk! It said, "Thank you for making me fall down. I was stuck in that tree for a long time." Tom was surprised and said, "You're welcome!" They became friends and Tom learned not to tease others.

One day, a strong cop named Tom walked down the street. He saw a little girl named Lily. She was sad and crying. Tom wanted to help her feel better. "Hi Lily, why are you sad?" asked Tom. "I lost my toy," Lily said. Tom wanted to comfort her. "Don't worry, I will help you find your toy," he said. They looked around, but the toy was not there. Then, something unexpected happened. A big dog came running with the toy in his mouth. The dog gave the toy to Lily. She was so happy! Tom and Lily thanked the dog. Now, Lily was not sad anymore, and they all became good friends.

Molly was playing in her garden. She was so happy. Suddenly a big gust of wind blew by and grabbed her wrap. The wind was so strong, it lifted the wrap up into the sky. Molly wanted her wrap back. She ran after it, but it flew much faster than she could. Molly asked her mom for help. Her mom suggested that Molly call her cat, Tiddles. Tiddles was good at catching things. Molly called Tiddles and he came running. He jumped up, grabbed the wrap and brought it back safely to Molly. Molly was so happy to have her wrap back. She hugged the wrap and hugged her cat, too. She thanked him for being a clever cat and getting her wrap back.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the gym with his mom. He liked to step and jump on the soft mats. Tim saw his friend, Ben, at the gym too. They were happy to play together. "Let's play a game," said Ben. "We can step on the mats and not touch the floor." Tim liked the game. They stepped and jumped, having fun. Then, Tim found a sour candy on the floor. He picked it up and showed it to Ben. "Yuck, that candy is sour!" said Ben. Tim wanted to taste it too. But when he put it in his mouth, something unexpected happened. The sour candy made Tim jump very high! He was not scared, he was excited. "Wow, look at me!" Tim shouted as he jumped high in the air. Ben and his mom were amazed. The sour candy had given Tim the power to jump higher than ever before. They all laughed and clapped, and Tim knew he had found a new fun way to play at the gym.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars. One sunny day, Tim went to the park with his mom to play with his friends. At the park, Tim saw a helpless little bird on the ground. It couldn't fly. Tim's friend, Amy, came over and said, "Oh no, the bird can't fly! What can we do to help?" Tim thought for a moment and said, "We can delay our play and help the bird!" So, Tim and Amy carefully picked up the little bird and showed it to their moms. Their moms called a nice person who knew how to help birds. Soon, the little bird was flying again, and Tim and Amy went back to their play, happy that they helped the bird.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim did not like to take a bath in the bathtub. One day, his mom said, "Tim, it is time for your bath." Tim looked at his mom and said, "No, I refuse!" Tim's mom was not happy. She said, "If you do not take a bath, a fierce monster will come and get you!" Tim was scared, but he still did not want to take a bath. So, he went to his room and hid under his bed. Later that night, Tim heard a noise. He looked out from under his bed and saw a big, fierce monster! The monster said, "I heard you do not like baths. I do not like them either." Tim was surprised. He and the monster became friends, and they played together. From that day on, Tim and the monster took baths together, and they were not scared anymore.
