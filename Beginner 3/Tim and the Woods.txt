Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Woods

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was sad because his mom and dad said they were going to a camp. Tim thought camp was boring. He did not want to go. At the camp, Tim met a wise old owl. The owl could see that Tim was not happy. The owl wanted to help, so he tried to explain to Tim that camp can be fun. Tim did not believe the owl, but he decided to listen. The owl took Tim on a walk in the woods. They saw a magical tree with golden leaves. Tim had never seen anything like it! He was so surprised! Tim started to have fun and he realized that camp was not boring after all. The moral of the story is to always give new things a chance.

Lily and Ben were playing with their dolls. They liked to pretend that they were delivering mail to their friends. Lily had a pink bag with letters and stickers. Ben had a blue bag with cards and stamps. "Let's go to Sam's house first," Lily said. "He likes stickers." "OK," Ben said. "But be careful. The road is busy." They put on their helmets and got on their bikes. They rode along the sidewalk, looking for Sam's mailbox. Lily saw it first. It was red and had a star on it. "Here it is," she said. "I'll deliver the mail." She got off her bike and ran to the mailbox. She opened the pink bag and took out a letter with a sticker. She put it in the mailbox and closed it. "Good job, Lily," Ben said. "Now let's go to Anna's house. She likes cards." They got on their bikes again and rode to the next street. Anna's mailbox was yellow and had a flower on it. Ben saw it first. "Here it is," he said. "I'll deliver the mail." He got off his bike and ran to the mailbox. He opened the blue bag and took out a card with a stamp. He put it in the mailbox and closed it. "Good job, Ben," Lily said. "Now let's go to Max's house. He likes letters." They got on their bikes again and rode to the last street. Max's mailbox was green and had a dinosaur on it. Lily and Ben saw it at the same time. "Here it is," they said together. "We'll deliver the mail." They got off their bikes and ran to the mailbox. They opened their bags and took out a letter each. They put them in the mailbox and closed it. "Good job, Lily and Ben," they said to each other. "We delivered all the mail." They were very happy and proud. They decided to go back to their house and have a snack. They got on their bikes and started to ride. But they did not see the big truck coming. The truck was very fast and loud. It did not stop for the red light. It hit Lily and Ben and their bikes. They flew in the air and landed on the ground. They did not get up. They did not move. They did not breathe. The truck driver did not see them. He did not stop. He did not care. He was very clumsy and mean. Lily and Ben were gone. Their bags were torn. Their mail was scattered. Their dolls were broken. Their helmets were cracked. Their bikes were smashed. Their parents were sad. Their friends were sad. Everyone was sad. The end.

Once upon a time, in a land far away, there was a tall tower. In this tower, there lived a kind king and a sweet queen. They had a baby girl named Lily. Everyone in the land loved Lily. One day, a big dragon came to the tower. The dragon was not nice. It wanted to spray fire on the tower. The king and queen were scared. They did not want their tower to burn. They needed help. A brave knight came to save the day. He had a big water hose. The knight sprayed water on the dragon. The dragon felt sorry and stopped spraying fire. The tower was safe. The king, queen, and Lily were happy. They thanked the knight and they all lived happily ever after.
