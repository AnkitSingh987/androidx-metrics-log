Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Strong Girl and the Shoes

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a strong girl named Sue. Sue liked to play outside with her friends. One day, while they were playing, they saw a witch. The witch was not nice, and she took Sue's friend, Tim. Sue wanted to save Tim from the witch. She put on her strong shoes and went to find the witch's house. The house was big and dark. Sue was a little scared, but she wanted to help her friend. Sue went inside the house and found Tim. The witch was sleeping, so Sue was very quiet. She took Tim's hand, and they left the house. Sue and Tim ran back to their friends. They were happy that Sue was strong and saved Tim from the witch.

One day, a furry dog named Max went to school. He was a new pupil in the class. All the kids loved Max because he was so soft and nice. Max was very happy to make new friends. In class, the teacher said, "Today, we will choose a helper for the week." All the kids wanted to be the helper. Max also wanted to help. The teacher looked at Max and smiled. "Max, you can be our helper this week," she said. The kids clapped, and Max wagged his tail. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind blew the door open, and a funny-looking bird flew into the class. The bird was also furry! The kids laughed and cheered. The bird said, "I want to be a pupil too!" The teacher smiled and said, "Of course, you can join our class." Now, Max and the bird were both new pupils, and they became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a smart dog named Max. Max loved to play with his ball in the park. One day, Max saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad because her ball was stuck in a tree. Max heard Lily's voice and decided to help her. He said, "Don't worry, I will get your ball!" Max rushed to the tree and jumped high. He caught the ball with his mouth and brought it back to Lily. Lily was very happy and said, "Thank you, Max! You are so smart!" Max wagged his tail and felt proud. From that day on, Max and Lily became best friends and played together every day.

One day, there was a wide hotel. A family saw it and wanted to park there. The father drove the car and parked close to the hotel. But when he tried to get out, he saw that the car wouldn't move! Something was stuck and it was very stuck. The father got help and they looked closely. It was a tree trunk that was stuck to their car! The father was so sad and the family had to leave without staying in the hotel.

One day, a strong boy named Tim went to play outside. He liked to twist his toy snake and make it dance. His friend, Sue, came to play with him. They had fun twisting the toy snake and laughing. Sue said, "I had a bad dream last night. It was a nightmare!" Tim said, "I'm sorry, Sue. I had a bad dream too. But we are safe now, and we can play together." As they played, a real snake came out of the grass. Tim and Sue were scared, but Tim was strong. He picked up the snake and twisted it gently. The snake started to dance and wasn't scary anymore. Tim and Sue laughed and played with the snake, and they weren't scared of nightmares anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog named Max. Max loved to play with his ball. One day, Max's ball broke. He felt sad because he could not play with his ball anymore. Max wanted to fix his ball. Max went to his friend, a little girl named Lily. Lily was good at fixing things. Max showed Lily his broken ball. Lily saw that Max was sad. She wanted to help him. Lily got some tape and fixed Max's ball. It was as good as new! Max was very happy. He wagged his tail and jumped around. Max gave Lily a big hug to say thank you. Now, Max and Lily played with the ball together. They had a lot of fun. From that day on, Max and Lily were the best of friends.
