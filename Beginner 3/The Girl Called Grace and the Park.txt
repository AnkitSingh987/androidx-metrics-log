Category: Beginner
Title: The Girl Called Grace and the Park

Once upon a time, there lived a girl called Grace. Grace was only three years old, but she was very brave. One day, she was talking to her mama when she heard a strange word: "Ashamed". She asked her mama what "ashamed" meant, but her mama just smiled. She said that later, she might introduce a new word to Grace and then she'd know what ashamed meant. Grace was a bit confused, but she was also excited. She wanted to know what the new word was! Later that day, Grace and her mama went for a walk in the park. Grace was playing around when her mama suddenly said, "Grace, do you remember that word I mentioned before?". Grace nodded. Her mama said, "Ashamed means when you did something wrong and you feel embarrassed because of it. That's when you can say you're ashamed". Grace was all ears. She nodded, understanding what her mama had said. Now she knew what it meant to be ashamed, and she wouldn't forget that feeling or that word. She also knew that she shouldn't do anything she'd be ashamed of.

One day, a girl named Lily found a big box. The box was unknown to her. Inside the box, Lily saw paint and a brush. She wanted to paint something fun. She thought about what to paint and got a fun idea. Lily asked her mom, "Can I paint my hair?" Her mom said, "No, it will be too hard to wash out." Lily was sad, but she still wanted to paint. She thought more about what to paint. Later, Lily found her old doll with messy hair. She remembered her idea from before. Lily painted the doll's hair with the colors from the unknown box. The doll looked so pretty and fun. Her mom saw it and said, "What a great idea, Lily!" Lily was happy that she could paint hair after all.

One day, a happy cat named Tom went to the kitchen. He was enthusiastic to find some yummy food. In the kitchen, he saw a big, red apple on the table. Tom jumped up to get it. Just then, a funny bird named Sally flew in through the window. Sally said, "Hi Tom! What are you doing?" Tom said, "I want to eat this apple!" Sally laughed and said, "That's not an apple, silly! That's a ball!" Tom and Sally played with the ball in the kitchen. They laughed and had so much fun. They didn't know that the ball was full of candy. When the ball bounced high, it broke open and candy fell out. Tom and Sally were very surprised and happy. They ate the candy and played all day.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to play with her brother, Tom. Tom had brown hair and big brown eyes. They both liked to play in the park near their home. Mia and Tom loved to run, jump, and have fun together. In the park, they saw a brown dog. The dog was very friendly and wagged its tail. Mia and Tom looked at the dog and smiled. They wanted to play with the dog, so they ran after it. The dog was happy and ran with them. After playing with the dog, Mia and Tom looked for their mom. They found her sitting on a bench. Mom was happy to see them having fun. She gave them a big hug and they all walked back home together.

Lila and Ben are friends. They like to play in the snow. They make a big igloo with snow blocks. They are very happy. But then, a new kid comes. His name is Max. He has a red coat and a blue hat. He sees the igloo and says, "I want to play in the igloo. It is mine now." Lila and Ben are sad and angry. They say, "No, it is not yours. We made it. You can play with us, but you have to share." Max is bossy. He says, "No, I don't want to share. I want the whole igloo. Go away. Or I will break it." Lila and Ben are scared. They don't want to lose their igloo. They don't know what to do. Then, they see Sam. Sam is a big kid. He is nice and strong. He likes to help. He sees what is happening and says, "Hey, Max, why are you being mean? Lila and Ben made the igloo. You can't take it from them. That is not fair." Max is surprised. He says, "Who are you? You can't tell me what to do. I can do what I want." Sam says, "No, you can't. You have to be nice and polite. If you want to play in the igloo, you have to ask Lila and Ben. Maybe they will lend it to you for a while. But you have to give it back when they want it. That is how you make friends." Max thinks for a moment. He says, "Okay, fine. I will ask them. Can I play in the igloo, please?" Lila and Ben smile. They say, "Sure, you can play in the igloo. But you have to share and be nice. And you have to give it back when we say so." Max says, "Okay, I will. Thank you for lending me the igloo." They all play in the igloo. They have fun. They make snowballs and snowmen. They are friends. They are happy.
