Category: Beginner
Title: Lila and Ben and the Pebble

Lila and Ben were playing on the beach. They liked to wave at the boats and the birds. They also liked to find pebbles and make patterns in the sand. Lila found a very pretty pebble. It was shiny and smooth and had many colors. She showed it to Ben and said, "Look, I found a pretty pebble!" Ben liked the pebble too. He wanted to have it for himself. He said, "Give me the pebble, Lila. It is mine now." He tried to grab it from Lila's hand. Lila did not want to give it to him. She said, "No, Ben. It is my pebble. I found it first." She held it tight and ran away from Ben. Ben was angry. He chased Lila and shouted, "Give me the pebble, Lila. Give me the pebble!" Lila was scared. She ran faster and faster. She did not see the big wave coming. The wave was very big and very loud. It splashed over Lila and Ben and made them wet and cold. The pebble slipped from Lila's hand and fell into the water. Lila and Ben could not see it anymore. Lila and Ben were sad. They lost the pretty pebble. They also lost their fun. They looked at each other and felt sorry. They said, "I'm sorry, Lila. I'm sorry, Ben." They hugged and made up. They decided to play together and share their things. They found another pebble and waved at the boats and the birds. They were happy again.

One day, a little girl named Mia wore her favorite blouse. It was a pretty blouse with flowers on it. Mia liked to play outside with her friends. They played with a ball and had lots of fun. Suddenly, the sky became dull and it started to rain. Mia and her friends ran inside to stay dry. Mia's blouse got wet and dirty from the rain and mud. She was sad and didn't know what to do. Mia's mom saw her sad face and said, "Don't worry, Mia. We can wipe your blouse clean." Mia's mom took a cloth and wiped the dirt off the blouse. The blouse was clean again, and Mia was happy. She thanked her mom and went back to play with her friends when the rain stopped.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a pet bird named Tweetie. Tweetie lived in a birdcage. Sue loved Tweetie very much. One day, Sue's mom said, "Sue, it's time to clean Tweetie's birdcage. It's dirty." Sue did not like to clean, but she knew it was important for Tweetie. Sue used a soft cloth, not a harsh one, to make sure she did not hurt Tweetie's home. Sue talked to Tweetie while she cleaned. "Don't worry, Tweetie. I'll make your birdcage nice and clean." When Sue was done, Tweetie's birdcage was shiny and clean. Tweetie was happy, and Sue felt good for helping her friend.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a toy gun. He wanted to play with it. He knew he could shoot it and have fun. But, he also knew that it could cause trouble if he did not play with it the right way. Tim went outside to play. He saw a big tree with wide branches. He thought it would be fun to shoot at the tree. So, he aimed and shot the toy gun. The toy made a loud noise, but it did not hurt the tree. Tim was happy. After a while, Tim's mom came outside. She saw him with the toy gun and knew he could cause trouble. She told him to be careful and not to shoot at people or animals. Tim promised to be careful and only shoot at the tree. They both smiled and Tim continued to play safely.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a dirty boy because he did not like to wash. He had a very funny style of dressing because he did not care about how he looked. Tim's mom always told him to clean up, but he did not listen. One day, Tim went to a party. All the other kids looked nice and clean, but Tim was very dirty. The kids did not want to play with him because he looked so messy. Tim felt sad and alone. He thought about what his mom told him. Tim decided to change his ways. He went home and took a bath. He put on clean clothes and combed his hair. The next day, Tim received a gift from his mom for being clean. Tim learned that being clean and having a nice style is important. He made new friends and was much happier. The moral of the story is to always listen to your mom and to take care of yourself.
