Category: Beginner
Title: Ben the Dog and the Stick

One day, a little dog named Ben went to the beach. He saw the big surf and wanted to play in it. Ben barked, "Woof! Let's go play in the surf!" Ben's friend, a tiny crab named Sam, was scared. Sam said, "I don't know, Ben. The surf looks hard to play in." Ben smiled and said, "Don't worry, Sam. I will help you." They went into the surf and started to play. They had so much fun! Then, Ben saw a big stick. He tried to pick it up, but it was too hard. He tried again and again, but he could not do it. Sam saw Ben and wanted to help. He went to the stick and used his claw to snap it. Now, the stick was small enough for Ben to pick up. Ben and Sam played with the stick in the surf. They had a great day at the beach.

Once upon a time, there was a big room with a cheap carpet. In the room, there lived a funny dog named Bobo. Bobo liked to play on the cheap carpet all day long. One day, Bobo met a cat named Mimi. Mimi said, "I want to play on the carpet too!" Bobo was happy to have a new friend to play with. They played together and had lots of fun. They liked to bow to each other as they played. But then, one day, the cheap carpet started to tear. Bobo and Mimi were sad. They could not play on the carpet anymore. The carpet was too old and weak. Bobo and Mimi had to say goodbye to their favorite carpet. They were very sad and missed playing on it together.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue liked fashion a lot. She had many pretty clothes. One day, she felt restless. She wanted to get new clothes. So she went to the store with her mom. At the store, Sue saw a big box. The box had a pretty dress. Sue was very happy. She wanted to get the dress. But her mom said, "No, we cannot buy it today." Sue was sad. She went to bed that night. She had a dream. In her dream, she got the pretty dress. But when she put it on, it turned into a frog! Sue woke up and laughed. She was not sad anymore. She knew that sometimes, things are not what they seem.

One sunny day, a little bird named Tim was flying high in the sky. He saw a hidden nest in a big tree. Inside the nest, he found a small egg. Tim was very happy and wanted to tell his friend, Sam the squirrel, about his find. Tim flew down to Sam and said, "Sam, I found a hidden egg in the sky! Let's laugh and be happy!" Sam smiled and they both laughed together. They decided to take care of the egg and wait for it to hatch. Days went by, and one day the egg started to crack. A tiny bird came out of the egg. Tim and Sam were very happy to see the new baby bird. They took care of the baby bird and taught it how to fly and find food. The baby bird grew up to be a big, strong bird, and it was all thanks to Tim and Sam's love and care. The moral of the story is that when we find something hidden and special, we should share it with our friends and take care of it together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy in a hospital. He was feeling very sick and his parents were worried about him. So, they took him to the doctor. The doctor told the little boy that he was going to give him an x-ray. The little boy looked scared. He asked, "What is x-ray?" The doctor smiled and said, "It's nothing to be worried about. An x-ray is a special picture that shows inside your body. We will be able to tell if anything is wrong." The little boy was still scared, but he gathered his courage and entered the x-ray room. The process wasn't too difficult, although he had to stay very still. After a few minutes, the x-ray was done, and the little boy was relieved. The doctor examined the x-ray and said, "There is nothing wrong with you. You are going to be all right." The little boy was relieved, and his parents were glad to hear the good news. The little boy smiled and said, "That wasn't so difficult, was it?"
