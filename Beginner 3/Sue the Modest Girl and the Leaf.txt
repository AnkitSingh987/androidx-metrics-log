Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Modest Girl and the Leaf

One day, a modest little girl named Sue went for a walk. She saw a big tree with many leaves. Sue picked up a leaf and tried to fold it. The leaf broke into two parts. She found another leaf and tried again. This time, she folded the leaf without breaking it. Sue walked to her friend, Tim, and showed him the folded leaf. "Look, Tim!" she said. "I made a tiny pocket with the leaf!" Tim looked at the leaf and smiled. "Wow, Sue! Good job!" he said. Tim wanted to try too, so he picked up a leaf and folded it just like Sue. Together, Sue and Tim made many leaf pockets. They put little stones and flowers inside them. They had a fun day playing with their new leaf pockets. They showed their friends how to fold leaves and make pockets too. Everyone was happy and had a great time.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty lion. He was the king of the forest. All the animals liked him. One day, the lion found a big frame. It was a pretty frame. He thought, "I want to wear this frame like a necklace." He asked his friend, the monkey, for help. "Monkey, can you help me wear this frame?" The monkey said, "Yes, Mr. Lion! I will help you." So, the monkey put the frame on the lion like a necklace. The lion walked around the forest. All the animals saw him. They said, "Wow, Mr. Lion! You look so nice with the frame!" The lion was happy. He thanked the monkey for helping him. They were good friends, and they played together in the forest.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog with three legs. One day, he met a new friend, a cat with an unknown leg. The cat was sad because he didn't know how many legs he had. The little dog wanted to help the cat. The little dog said, "Let's add your legs and my legs, and we can share them." The cat agreed, and they started to add their legs. The dog had three legs, and the cat had one leg that they could see. They added the legs and found out that they had four legs together. The cat was happy to know that he had a leg, and the dog was happy to help his new friend. They played and shared their legs, and they became best friends. The moral of the story is that we should help others when they need it, and we can all be friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. Jane and her mom decided to go out for a visit. On the visit, they went to a big store. Jane saw a green vase that she really wanted. She asked her mom, "Mommy, can I have it please?" Her mom said, "Well, why don't you look around and see if there is something else?" Jane looked around, but she kept coming back to the same green vase. She finally asked again and her mom said, "Ok, let's get it!" Jane was so excited to have her own vase. When they walked back to their car, Jane held it really tight. When they got home, they put the green vase in the livingroom. Jane said, "This is a great visit!"

Once upon a time, there was a big house with a fireplace. It was a cool day, so Mom made a warm fire. Tim, the little boy, liked to sit by the fireplace. He felt happy and warm there. One day, Tim's friend, Sue, came to play. They played with toys near the fireplace. Then, they saw a little cat. The cat was too close to the fire! Tim said, "Oh no! We must care for the cat. It's not safe there!" Tim and Sue moved the cat away from the fire. They thought the cat was safe now. But then, the cat jumped and knocked over a vase! Water came out of the vase and put out the fire. Tim and Sue were surprised. Mom came in and saw the mess. She was not happy. But then, she saw the cat. She smiled and said, "It's okay. You cared for the cat. That's important. We can clean this up." Tim, Sue, and Mom cleaned up together. They all felt good because they cared for the little cat.
