Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Tom and the Box

Lily and Tom were playing in the garden. They liked to dig and find things in the dirt. Sometimes they found worms, sometimes they found stones, and sometimes they found old toys. One day, they found a big wooden box. It was brown and had a knob on the lid. They wondered what was inside. They pulled the knob and opened the box. "Wow!" Lily said. "Look at all these things!" The box was full of old clothes, hats, shoes, and books. They looked very old and dirty. Some of them had holes and stains. Some of them smelled bad. "Let's try them on!" Tom said. He picked up a jolly red coat and put it on. It was too big for him and dragged on the ground. He also put on a black hat with a feather. He looked very funny. Lily laughed. She picked up a blue dress and a white scarf. She put them on and twirled around. She also looked very funny. They pretended to be different people. They talked in silly voices and made up stories. They had a lot of fun. But then, they heard a loud voice. "Hey, you two! What are you doing with my things?" It was Mr. Green, the old man who lived next door. He was very angry. He came over and looked at the box. "These are my things! They are very precious to me! They belonged to my family! How dare you touch them and ruin them!" He shouted. Lily and Tom were scared. They did not know the things were his. They did not mean to ruin them. They just wanted to play. "We're sorry, Mr. Green. We didn't know. We thought they were trash. They look very old and rot." Lily said. "Rot? Rot? How can you say that? These are not trash! These are treasures! They have history and memories! You don't understand anything!" Mr. Green said. He took the things from them and put them back in the box. He slammed the lid and locked the knob. He carried the box away and glared at them. "Stay away from my things! And don't come near my garden again!" He said. Lily and Tom ran back to their house. They felt very bad. They did not want to make Mr. Green angry. They did not want to hurt his feelings. They learned a lesson. They learned that some things are not what they seem. And they learned to be more careful and respectful.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy cat named Tom. Tom had a tutor, a wise old owl named Mr. Hoot. Mr. Hoot would teach Tom many things, like how to jump and how to catch a ball. Tom liked to learn from his tutor. One day, Tom was feeling very sleepy. Mr. Hoot saw this and said, "Tom, you should take a nap." Tom thought this was a good idea. So, he found a nice, soft bed and curled up to sleep. He started to dream about playing with his friends. But then, something unexpected happened. Tom woke up and found himself in a new place. He was not in his soft bed anymore. Instead, he was in a big, green field with lots of flowers. Tom was confused. "How did I get here?" he wondered. Then, he saw Mr. Hoot flying above him. "Mr. Hoot!" Tom called. "What happened?" Mr. Hoot smiled and said, "Tom, I used my magic to bring you to this beautiful field. I thought it would be a nice surprise for you when you woke up from your nap." Tom looked around and saw all the pretty flowers and the big, blue sky. He felt happy and excited. "Thank you, Mr. Hoot!" Tom said. And they played in the field together all day long.

Once upon a time, there was an old toy bunny. This bunny had a stitch loose. The little boy who owned the bunny loved it very much. One day, the boy said to his mommy, "Mommy, can you fix bunny's stitch, please?" The mommy said, "I will try, but I need to find my sewing kit first." The boy and his mommy looked all around the house. They looked in the kitchen, the bedroom, and the living room. They could not find the sewing kit. The boy felt sad, but he knew they would find it. Later, while playing in the yard, the boy found the sewing kit under a big tree. He remembered that he had played there with his mommy before. He ran to his mommy and said, "Look, I found it!" The mommy smiled and fixed the bunny's stitch. The boy and his old toy bunny were very happy. They knew they would always find what they needed if they kept looking.
