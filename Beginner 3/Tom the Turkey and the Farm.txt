Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Turkey and the Farm

Once upon a time there was a turkey named Tom. Tom was a very fit turkey. Every day Tom would run around the farm and flap his wings. One day, Tom decided to explore the world outside the farm. He started walking and eventually entered a dense forest. There were all sorts of new plants and animals in this new environment that Tom had never seen before. He continued to explore until he came across a huge lake. Tom followed the shore and soon arrived at a clearing with several people surrounding a campfire. Tom stopped to watch and listen. One of the people asked, "Tom, did you come to join us?" Tom was startled and couldn't answer. He was too scared and didn't know what to say. He just stared. The people all smiled and started talking to him. They asked him questions, "Do you like turkey? Are you fit? Do you think you can join us?" Tom nodded and smiled. He knew he wanted to stay with these people. So Tom hung around the campfire and made friends with everyone. Every night, they would roast a fit turkey and enjoy it around the campfire. Tom was happy that he had explored beyond the farm and made some new friends.

Ben and Mia were at the lake with their mom and dad. They saw many boats on the water. Some were big and some were small. Some were fast and some were slow. Ben and Mia wanted to go on a boat. "Can we go on a boat, please?" they asked their mom and dad. "Maybe later," their mom said. "First, we have to eat our lunch." Their mom gave them sandwiches and juice. They were sweet and yummy. Ben and Mia ate them quickly. They were still hungry. "Can we have some cookies, please?" they asked their mom. "OK, but only one each," their mom said. She gave them each a cookie. They were sweet and crunchy. Ben and Mia ate them happily. They were still hungry. "Can we have some more cookies, please?" they asked their mom. "No, you had enough," their mom said. "You don't want to spoil your dinner." Ben and Mia were sad. They wanted more cookies. They looked at the boats again. They wanted to go on a boat. "I have an idea," Ben said to Mia. "Why don't we suggest to mom and dad that we go on a boat now? Maybe they will say yes." "That's a good idea," Mia said. "Let's do that." They went to their mom and dad. They smiled and said, "Mom, dad, can we suggest something?" "What is it?" their mom and dad asked. "Can we go on a boat now?" Ben and Mia said together. "Please, please, please?" Their mom and dad looked at each other. They smiled and said, "OK, why not? You ate your lunch and you were good. Let's go on a boat." Ben and Mia were happy. They cheered and hugged their mom and dad. They went to the boat rental and chose a small and slow boat. They put on life jackets and got on the boat. Their dad steered the boat and their mom took pictures. Ben and Mia waved at the other boats and enjoyed the breeze and the sun. They had a lot of fun on the boat. They were glad they suggested it.

One day, it started to rain. Little Joe and his mom were outside and they got very wet. Joe was very sad. “Mom, why does it rain?” asked Joe sadly. “The rain is here so that we can have a drink from it,” she said. Joe jumped up with excitement. “Can we make our own drink from the rain?” “Yes, we can!” said his mom. So, they ran to get a black tank and put it outside. Then they waited. Soon, the tank began to fill up with rain. It didn't take long. “Let's drink the rain!” said Joe. His mom smiled. She filled two cups with the water and they took a sip. It was cold and delicious! Joe smiled. Rain was not so bad after all!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a favorite film that she loved to watch. She would watch it every day after playing with her toys. One day, she could not find her film. She looked and looked, but it was not there. Amy felt helpless and sad. Amy's mom saw her looking for her film. She told Amy to think back to where she had it last. Amy remembered that she had it in the living room. She went to the living room and looked around. Then, she saw a corner of the film sticking out from under the couch. Amy realized that her film was hiding there. Amy was so happy that she found her film. She put it in the player and watched it again. Her mom smiled and was glad that Amy found her film. From that day on, Amy always remembered to put her film back in its case when she was done watching it.
