Category: Beginner
Title: Mia and the Ball

Once, a little girl named Mia wanted to try a new sport. She saw her big sister play with a big, soft ball. Mia thought it looked fun. So, she asked her mom if she could play too. The next day, Mia went to the park with her mom and sister. They started to play the sport with the soft ball. Mia tried very hard to throw and catch the ball like her sister. But it was not easy for her. Mia kept trying, but she could not do it. She felt sad and cried. Her mom said it was time to go home. Mia did not get to play the sport well. She went to bed with a sad face, wishing she could play like her sister.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a basket. She loved her basket very much. She took it everywhere with her. One sunny day, Lily went to the park with her basket. In the park, she met her friend, Tim. They liked to play together. Lily showed Tim her basket and said, "I love my basket. It is the best." Tim looked at the basket and said, "No, I disagree. My toy car is the best." Lily and Tim talked about their favorite toys. They could not agree on which toy was the best. In the end, Lily and Tim decided to play together with the basket and the toy car. They had a lot of fun. They learned that it was okay to disagree and still be friends.

One day, a cat named Tom went for a walk. The sky was gray, and the air was cold. Tom felt a shiver, but he wanted to play outside. He saw his friend, a dog named Max. Max was alert and looking at the sky. "Max, do you think it will rain?" asked Tom. "I don't know, but we should be ready," said Max. They played and played, but then they saw big dark clouds. The wind blew, and Tom shivered again. They knew rain was coming. They ran back to Tom's house just in time. The rain started to fall, and they were safe and warm inside. They looked out the window and saw the rain. Tom and Max were happy they listened to each other and went inside before the rain came.

Once upon a time, there was a nice skeleton. The skeleton lived in a big, open house. The house was very pretty and had many colors. One day, a little girl came to the open house. She saw the skeleton and was not scared. The skeleton was happy and wanted to be friends. So, the skeleton offered the little girl a big, red ball to play with. They played together all day and had lots of fun. The little girl and the skeleton became best friends. They always played in the open house and shared many happy times.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot liked to play with his stuff, a smooth ball. One sunny day, Spot went outside to play with his ball. He was very happy and ran around fast. Suddenly, Spot felt a tickle in his nose. He tried to stop it, but he couldn't. Spot let out a big sneeze! The sneeze was so strong that it made his smooth ball fly far away. Spot looked around and could not see his ball. Spot was sad and went to look for his ball. He walked and walked until he found a big tree. Under the tree, Spot saw a little cat playing with his smooth ball. The cat was named Whiskers. Whiskers saw Spot and gave the ball back. Spot was happy, and they both played together. From that day on, Spot and Whiskers became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a friendly car. The car loved to play with the kids. One day, the car met a little boy named Tim. Tim said, "Hi, car! Do you want to play with me?" The friendly car beeped and nodded. They played all day long. They raced and had fun. As they played, Tim saw a tiny bug. He wanted to pinch it. But the friendly car shook its head. "No, Tim. We should be nice to bugs too." Tim smiled and let the bug go. They kept playing, and the bug joined them. They all had a great day.
