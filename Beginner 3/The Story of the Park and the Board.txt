Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Park and the Board

Once upon a time, there was a big, red board. The board lived in a park with many trees and flowers. Every day, many kids came to play near the board. The board liked to watch them have fun. One day, a little girl named Mia came to the park. She saw the big, red board and said, "I know! I will draw a picture on the board." She took out her chalk and began to draw. The board was very happy. As Mia drew, a boy named Tim came to the park. He saw Mia and the big, red board. Tim said, "Wow, that's a big board! Can I draw too?" Mia smiled and said, "Yes, let's draw together!" So Mia and Tim drew on the board all day, and the board was the happiest it had ever been.

Once upon a time, there was an old cat. The cat lived in a small house with a girl named Lily. Lily loved her old cat very much. They played together every day. One day, Lily and the cat went for a walk in the park. They saw a big tree. The cat saw a bird in the tree and tried to catch it. The cat jumped and tried to snap at the bird, but the bird flew away. Lily and the cat went back home. They were a little sad, but they still had fun. The old cat was tired from the walk and the snap. Lily gave the cat a big hug, and they both went to sleep.

Once upon a time, there was a little duck who decided to go on an adventure. The duck paddled away, but then he found himself tired and hungry. He stopped at the edge of a lake and sighed. Suddenly, a voice startled him - it was an old frog! The frog said, "Why are you so foolish? Why don't you quit paddling and take a rest? If you don't, you'll get more tired and hungry!" The duck thought about it for a moment and then decided to quit. He stopped paddling and finally took a break. After a few minutes, he felt so much better and continued on his adventure, never again feeling so foolish as he did before.

One day, a deaf boy named Tim went to the park. He liked to play with his ball. He saw another boy named Sam. Sam was not deaf. Sam saw Tim's ball and wanted to play too. Tim tried to sign to Sam, "Do you want to play with me?" Sam did not understand. Sam tried to talk to Tim, but Tim could not hear. They both wanted to play, but they did not know how to talk to each other. Tim got sad and hit his head on the tree. He started to cry. Sam did not know what to do. They both went home without playing. The day was bad.

One day, a little red cat named Tuna went for a walk. Tuna liked to see the big green trees and the blue sky. The sun was very bright and warm. Tuna saw a big dog. The dog said, "Hi, Tuna! Do you want to play?" Tuna was scared at first, but then said, "Yes, let's play!" They played and laughed together. After playing, Tuna and the dog were tired. They sat under a tree and looked at the sky. Tuna said, "We are friends now." The dog smiled and said, "Yes, we are." They were happy and went home together.

Once upon a time there was a rabbit who lived alone. He was so lonely! He saw some other bunnies hopping around and he wanted to join them, but they were too far away. One day, the rabbit had an idea. He took some long strips of grass and laid them on the ground. He hopped from strip to strip until he was close to the other bunnies. Then he waved his arms and said "hello!" The bunnies said hi back and soon the rabbit was playing with them. He had so much fun. Now the rabbit never felt lonely. He had made a bridge of grass strips and bridged the gap between him and his friends.
