Category: Beginner
Title: Fin the Fish and the Play

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. He lived in the sea. He was an enthusiastic fish who loved to play with his friends. One day, a big fish came to their sea. The big fish wanted to be the boss of all the little fish. Fin did not like this. He did not want the big fish to be the boss. He wanted all the fish to be friends and play together. Fin talked to the big fish. He asked the big fish to be nice and play with them. The big fish said no. He wanted to be the boss. Fin did not accept this. He talked to his friends. They all worked together to show the big fish how fun it is to play and be friends. The big fish saw how happy they were. He changed his mind and became their friend. They all played happily in the sea.

Once upon a time, there was a small town with many vehicles. One day, a big truck got stuck in the mud. The truck driver was very serious. He did not know what to do. All the people in the town came to see the truck. A little boy named Tim saw the truck and had an idea. He said, "Let's lift the truck out of the mud!" The people looked at Tim and laughed. They did not think a little boy could help the big truck. But Tim was serious. He asked his friends to help him. They all tried to lift the truck, and it started to move! The truck driver was happy. He said, "Thank you, Tim, for your help!" The people in the town learned that even a little boy can make a big difference. The moral of the story is that everyone can help, no matter how small they are.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. The sun was shining and it was a beautiful day. Tim played on the swings and slide, and he felt very safe because his mom was watching him. While playing, Tim saw a shiny coin on the ground. He picked it up and showed it to his mom. "Look, Mom! I found a shiny coin!" he said. His mom told him, "That's great, Tim! But remember, we must always be careful when we find things. We don't know if it's clean or not." Later, Tim needed to use the toilet, so his mom took him to the park's toilet. Tim remembered what his mom said about being careful with things he found. He washed his hands with soap and water after using the toilet, just like his mom taught him. In the end, Tim learned that it's essential to be careful with the things he finds and always keep clean. He felt happy and safe knowing that his mom was there to guide him. The moral of the story is that we should always be cautious and listen to our parents because they want to keep us safe and healthy.

Lily liked to write. She had a big book with many pages. She wrote her name, her friends' names, and some words she learned. She also drew pictures of her family, her toys, and her dog. One day, Lily wanted to write a story. She thought hard and made up a story about a princess and a dragon. She wrote the story in her book and drew the princess and the dragon. She was very proud of her story. She showed her story to her brother Max. Max was older and he liked to read. But he was also bossy. He looked at Lily's story and said, "This is not a good story. The princess is too small and the dragon is too big. And you spelled some words wrong. You should write like me." Lily felt sad and angry. She said, "This is my story. I can write how I want. You are not the boss of me. Go away!" She grabbed her book and hugged it to her waist. She ran to her room and closed the door. She did not want to talk to Max. She wanted to write more stories. She liked to write.
