Category: Beginner
Title: Fluffy the Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a cat named Fluffy. Fluffy had soft fur. She loved to stretch in the sun. Fluffy was a spoiled cat. She lived in a big house with a nice family. One day, Fluffy went outside. She saw a little bird. The bird said, "Hi, Fluffy! Do you want to play?" Fluffy stretched her legs and said, "Yes, let's play!" They played all day long. At the end of the day, Fluffy went back home. Her family gave her a big hug. They said, "We love you, Fluffy!" Fluffy felt happy and loved. She knew she was a lucky cat.

Lily and Sam were best friends. They liked to play with dolls and cars and books. They were popular in their class. Everyone wanted to be their friend. One day, they went to the park with their moms. They saw a big sign that said: Fireworks Show Tonight! Lily and Sam were very excited. They loved fireworks. They were loud and bright and pretty. They asked their moms if they could stay and watch the fireworks. Their moms said yes, but they had to be good and stay close. Lily and Sam nodded and smiled. They played on the swings and the slide and the sandbox until it was dark. Then they heard a loud boom. The fireworks show had started. They ran to their moms and held their hands. They looked up at the sky and saw all the colors and shapes. "Wow!" Lily said. "Look at that one! It's like a star!" "Cool!" Sam said. "And that one! It's like a flower!" They clapped and cheered and oohed and aahed. They were having so much fun. But then, something bad happened. A firework went wrong. It flew too low and hit a tree. The tree caught fire. The fire was big and scary. It made a lot of smoke and noise. Lily and Sam were very scared. They started to cry. They hugged their moms and buried their faces in their chests. "Mommy, I'm scared!" Lily said. "Me too!" Sam said. Their moms tried to calm them down. They told them it was okay. They said the firefighters were coming to put out the fire. They said they were safe and loved. They waited until the fire was out. Then they left the park. They said goodbye to each other and hugged. "I'm sorry the fireworks show was bad," Lily said. "Me too," Sam said. "But I'm glad we're still friends." "Me too," Lily said. "You're my best friend." They smiled and waved. They went home with their moms. They hoped the next fireworks show would be better.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. She had a sister named Lily. They loved to play together in their big yard. One day, Jane and Lily found a dead bug on the ground. Jane said, "Look, Lily, a dead bug!" Lily looked at the bug and said, "It's so big!" They decided to measure the bug to see how big it was. They went inside and asked their mom for a ruler. Their mom gave them a ruler and they went back outside. They put the ruler next to the dead bug and saw it was two inches long. Jane and Lily were very happy to find and measure the big dead bug. They went inside and told their mom all about it.

Once upon a time, there was a little pupil named Tim. Tim was very sad and felt miserable. He didn't know how to make friends at school. He sat alone at lunch and played by himself during break time. One day, a new girl named Sue came to school. Sue was also sad and didn't know anyone. Tim saw Sue and thought that maybe they could be friends. So, Tim walked up to Sue and asked, "Do you want to be my friend?" Sue looked up and smiled. She said, "Yes, I would like that!" From that day on, Tim and Sue played together and were very happy. They were no longer miserable and lonely. And they both learned that all they needed to do was ask to make a new friend.
