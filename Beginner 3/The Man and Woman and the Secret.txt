Category: Beginner
Title: The Man and Woman and the Secret

Once upon a time there was a man and a woman who wanted to marry each other. The man was very shy and he never wanted anyone to know about his secret. Every day, he would look into the mirror and whisper, “I will protect you.” One day, the woman said to him, “I love you and want us to be married.” The man was surprised and felt scared, he was too shy to say anything. He just sat there, looking down at the ground. Finally, the woman spoke again: “Please marry me, I don’t want to keep this a secret any longer.” The man looked into her eyes and said, “Yes, I will marry you. I will keep your secret safe.” The couple talked and shared their secrets. They were so happy and their love for one another grew every day. They married soon after and have been together ever since!

Once upon a time there lived a little girl and her name was Kate. She was happy and loved to play outdoors. But one night, she heard a strange noise coming from her bedroom. Kate didn't understand what it was and felt scared. So she went to her mum and asked her what the noise was. Her mum smiled and said it was just a scary monster. She told Kate he was fierce, but friendly too. But Kate couldn't understand why a monster would be friendly. She was scared. Mum then took Kate to her bedroom and showed her a big teddy bear. She said he was the friendly monster. Kate smiled and it didn't seem so scary anymore. She hugged the teddy and went to sleep. The next day, Kate sat down with her mum and drew a picture of the monster. She showed it to her mum and said it was fierce and brave - like her. Mum smiled and said she was proud of her brave daughter.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim found a pipe in his yard. He picked it up and thought it was crazy. He wanted to show his mom, so he ran inside the house. As he ran, Tim stumbled on a toy. He fell down but did not cry. He got up and brushed off his pants. He held the pipe tight in his hand and kept going. Tim showed his mom the crazy pipe. She smiled and said it was special. They put the pipe on a shelf to keep it safe. Tim was happy and felt like a big boy.

Once upon a time, there was a famous loop. This loop was in a big park where many kids came to play. One day, two friends, Tom and Sue, went to the park to play with the loop. Tom said, "Let's unite and play with the loop together." Sue agreed, and they started to play. They threw the loop high in the air and caught it. They had a lot of fun. But then, a big wind came and took the loop away. Tom and Sue tried to catch it, but they could not. The famous loop was gone, and they were sad. They went home with no loop to play with.

Sam is a young boy who likes to play with his toys. He has a big train set with many tracks and cars. He can make the train go fast or slow by using a switch. The switch has two colors: red and green. Red means stop and green means go. One day, Sam is playing with his train set in his room. He is having fun making the train go around the tracks. He uses the switch to make the train stop and go. He likes to watch the train move and make noises. But then, his sister Lily comes in. Lily is younger than Sam and she does not know how to play with the train set. She sees the switch and wants to use it too. She grabs the switch from Sam and pushes it back and forth. She makes the train go very fast and very slow. She does not care about the red and green colors. Sam is angry. He wants his switch back. He says, "Lily, stop! That is my switch! You are making the train go crazy!" He tries to take the switch from Lily, but she holds it tight. She says, "No, Sam! I want to play too! The switch is fun!" They start to fight over the switch. They pull and tug and shout. They do not hear their mom coming. She opens the door and sees them fighting. She is not happy. She says, "Sam, Lily, what are you doing? Why are you fighting over the switch? You need to share and play nicely!" She takes the switch from them and puts it on the table. She says, "No more train for now. You need to calm down and say sorry to each other. Then you can take turns using the switch and playing with the train. Do you understand?" Sam and Lily nod. They feel bad for fighting. They say sorry to each other and hug. They say sorry to their mom too. She smiles and hugs them. She says, "That's better. I love you both. Now, let's go have a snack and then you can play with the train again. But remember, use the switch carefully and share with your sister."
