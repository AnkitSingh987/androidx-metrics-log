Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a dog named Max. They liked to play outside. One day, Tim's mom made a big cake. She put cream on it. She said, "Tim, the cake is ready for the party. Do not touch it." Tim and Max went outside to play. They played with a ball. Tim threw the ball and Max ran to get it. But Max did not see the cake on the table. Max jumped up and drop the cake on the ground. The cream was all over the floor. Tim's mom came outside and saw the mess. She was sad. Tim said, "I am sorry, Mom. I did not watch Max." Tim's mom said, "The party is over. We have no cake now." Tim learned that he should always listen to his mom and watch his dog.

Once upon a time, there was a big building. In this building, there were many friends who played together every day. One of these friends was a loyal dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his friends and always helped them when they needed it. One day, Spot and his friends were playing near the building. They found a big box. "What's in the box?" asked one friend. "I don't know," said another friend. "Let's open it!" So, they tried to open the box, but it would not yield. Suddenly, the box started to shake and move. The friends were scared, but loyal Spot did not run away. He stayed with his friends to protect them. The box opened, and out came a big, friendly bear! The bear said, "Hello, friends! I was stuck in the box, but you helped me get out. Thank you!" Spot and his friends were happy that the bear was nice. They all played together and had lots of fun. From that day on, the bear joined their group, and they all became the best of friends.

Lila and Sam were playing on the beach. They liked to dig in the sand and look for shells and rocks. Sometimes they saw crabs and fish in the water. "Look, Lila, I found a big shell!" Sam said, holding up a shiny pink shell. "It is so pretty and smooth. I value it a lot. Do you want to see?" "Yes, please!" Lila said, running over to Sam. She took the shell and looked at it closely. "Wow, it is incredible! Can I keep it?" Sam thought for a moment. He liked the shell too, but he liked Lila more. He wanted to make her happy. He nodded and smiled. "Sure, you can keep it. It is a gift for you." "Thank you, Sam! You are very kind. I value you a lot too." Lila said, hugging Sam. She put the shell in her bucket and looked around for more treasures. Suddenly, they saw something moving in the water. It was big and purple and had many arms. It was an octopus! It came out of the water and crawled towards them. "Wow, look at that!" Sam said, pointing at the octopus. "It is incredible too. Do you think it wants to play with us?" Lila was not sure. She felt a little scared of the octopus. It looked strange and slimy. She held Sam's hand and backed away. "Hello, little ones," the octopus said, in a friendly voice. "I am Ollie, and I live in the sea. I saw your shell and I liked it. Can I see it?" Lila and Sam were surprised. They did not know that octopuses could talk. They looked at each other and then at Ollie. "Um, hello, Ollie," Lila said, shyly. "This is my shell. Sam gave it to me. It is very pretty and smooth. I value it a lot." Ollie nodded and smiled. "I can see that. It is a very nice shell. I value shells too. I collect them and make them into art. Do you want to see?" Lila and Sam were curious. They nodded and followed Ollie to the water. There, they saw a pile of shells of different colors and shapes. Ollie had arranged them into patterns and pictures. "Wow, Ollie, you are very talented!" Sam said, admiring Ollie's art. "These are incredible! How do you do that?" Ollie laughed and said, "Thank you, Sam. I use my arms and my suction cups to move the shells and stick them together. It is fun and relaxing. Do you want to try?" Lila and Sam agreed. They picked some shells and started to make their own art. Ollie helped them and gave them tips. They had a lot of fun and learned a lot. They became friends with Ollie and played with him until the sun set. They gave him some shells as a gift and he gave them some of his art. They hugged and said goodbye. "Thank you, Ollie, for showing us your art and playing with us. You are very nice. We value you a lot." Lila said, smiling. "Thank you, Lila and Sam, for sharing your shell and your time with me. You are very sweet. I value you a lot too." Ollie said, waving. Lila and Sam went back to their parents, who were waiting for them. They showed them their shells and their art and told them about Ollie. Their parents were amazed and proud. They said they had an incredible day. Lila and Sam agreed. They said they had an incredible day too.
