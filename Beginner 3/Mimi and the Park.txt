Category: Beginner
Title: Mimi and the Park

One evening, a little cat named Mimi was walking slow in the park. Mimi saw a big dog named Max. Max looked at Mimi and said, "Hi! Do you want to play with me?" Mimi was scared of Max because he was so big. But Max smiled and said, "Don't be scared. You can trust me. I am a nice dog." Mimi thought about it and said, "Okay, let's play." Max and Mimi played in the park until the sun went down. They had so much fun together. Mimi was happy she trusted Max, and they became good friends. From that evening on, they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her friends. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. Sue was very competitive. She always wanted to win. One day, Sue and her friends were playing a game of tag. Sue was it, and she ran after her friend, Tom. Tom was fast, but Sue was faster. Just as Sue was about to tag Tom, she heard a voice say, "Wait!" Sue paused and looked around. A big, friendly dog stood there, wagging its tail. "Can I play too?" the dog asked. Sue and her friends were very surprised. They had never seen a talking dog before! But they decided to let the dog join their game. The dog was very good at tag, and soon, everyone was laughing and having fun. Sue learned that it was more fun to play together than to always try to win.

Sara was lonely. She had no friends to play with. She liked to make dolls from old socks and buttons. She used a needle and thread to sew them. She filled them with cotton and gave them names. One day, she found a big sock with a hole in it. She thought it would make a good doll. She took her needle and thread and started to sew. But the needle was sharp and it hurt her finger. She cried out loud. "Ow! Ow! Ow!" she said. Her mom heard her and came to see what was wrong. She saw the blood on Sara's finger and the needle on the floor. "Oh, Sara, what happened?" she asked. "I was making a doll and the needle poked me," Sara said. Her mom hugged her and took her to the bathroom. She washed her finger and put a band-aid on it. "There, there, it's okay. The needle won't hurt you anymore," she said. "Thank you, mom. I'm sorry I was loud," Sara said. "It's okay, honey. I know you were just trying to have fun. But you have to be careful with needles. They are not toys. They are tools for grown-ups," her mom said. "I understand, mom. Can I still make my doll?" Sara asked. "Of course you can. But let me help you. I'll show you how to use the needle safely. And maybe we can find some other things to fill your doll with. How about some beans or rice?" her mom said. "Okay, mom. That sounds good. Can we make a doll for you too?" Sara said. "Sure, why not? I would love a doll from you. Then we can both have a friend to play with," her mom said. Sara smiled. She was not lonely anymore. She had her mom and her dolls. She was happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play ball with his friends. One sunny day, Tim and his friends went to the pitch to play. While playing, Tim saw a worried little girl named Lily sitting alone near the pitch. She was sad because she had no one to play with. Tim went to Lily and said, "Hi! Do you want to play ball with us?" Lily's face lit up, and she said, "Yes, please!" Tim offered his hand to help Lily up, and they went to play with the other kids. Everyone had lots of fun, and Lily was not worried anymore. Tim felt happy that he had made a new friend. The moral of the story is: always be kind and offer to help others. You can make new friends and make others happy.
