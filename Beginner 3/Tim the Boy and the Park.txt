Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play ball. One day, Tim saw a big goal in the park. He wanted to kick the ball into the goal. But the goal was very far away. Tim felt uncomfortable because he was scared he could not do it. Tim saw a big fruit near the goal. The fruit was old and starting to rot. Tim had an idea. He thought, "If I kick the ball and hit the fruit, it will show me that I can reach the goal too." Tim kicked the ball as hard as he could. The ball flew through the air and hit the fruit! The fruit broke into pieces because it was old and rotten. Tim was so happy! He knew he could reach the goal now. Tim tried to kick the ball into the goal again. This time, he was not scared. He was not uncomfortable anymore. The ball went into the goal! Tim was so proud of himself. He learned that if he tried hard, he could do anything. And he never felt uncomfortable again.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a lucky beetle on the ground. The beetle was sad because it could not find its way home. Tim wanted to help the beetle. Tim and the beetle walked and walked. They saw a big tree and a small rock. The beetle was still lost. Tim thought hard and had an idea. He picked up the beetle and put it on the big tree. The beetle was very happy. The lucky beetle found its home in the big tree. Tim felt good because he helped the beetle. The beetle thanked Tim and they became friends. The moral of the story is to always help others when they need it.

Once upon a time, there was a curious little bird named Blue. Blue loved to sing all day long. He had a happy spirit and always wanted to make new friends. One day, while he was singing, he saw a big cat sitting near him. The cat looked at Blue with big eyes. Blue thought, "Maybe the cat wants to be my friend!" So, he sang a sweet song to the cat. The cat moved closer to Blue, but it did not look like the cat wanted to be friends. Blue felt scared and flew away to a high tree branch. The cat tried to climb the tree, but it was too high for the cat. Blue's friend, the wise old owl, saw what happened. Owl told Blue, "Be careful, little bird. Not everyone wants to be your friend. Some animals might want to hurt you." Blue thanked the owl for his help. From that day on, Blue was more careful when making new friends. But he never stopped singing and spreading his happy spirit.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was an honest boy. One day, Tim went to play in the park. He saw a long rope on the ground. Tim thought it was fun to play with the rope. Tim picked up the rope and started to play. He played jump rope and tied the rope to the tree. Tim was having so much fun. But he did not see a big rock in front of him. Tim was running with the rope and did not see the rock. He stumbled on the rock and fell down. Tim was sad and hurt. But he remembered the fun he had with the rope. Tim got up and went home. He knew he would be more careful next time he played with the rope.

One day, a little boy named Tim had a brilliant idea. He wanted to have a crocodile as a friend. Tim went to the park and saw a big crocodile near the pond. He said, "Hello, Mr. Crocodile! Do you want to be my friend?" The crocodile looked at Tim and said, "Yes, I would like to be your friend. But I am not a crocodile. I am a big green log that looks like a crocodile." Tim was very surprised. He did not know that the big green log could talk. He asked, "How can you talk, Mr. Green Log?" The green log said, "I am a magic log. I can talk and be your friend, too." Tim was very happy to have a magic log as his friend. They played at the park all day and had lots of fun together.
