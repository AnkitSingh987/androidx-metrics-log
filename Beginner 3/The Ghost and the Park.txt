Title: The Ghost and the Park

One day, Gabby was walking in the park when she saw a ghost. She was so scared that she pulled her mom's hand. "Mommy, what was that?" Gabby asked. Her mom said, "It's just a ghost. Don't worry, it won't hurt you." But the ghost was not happy. It was dry and wanted to play. So it decided to follow Gabby and her mom. Gabby pulled her mom's hand even harder as the ghost followed them. They were scared and started to run. The ghost was too fast, so it caught up with them. It waved its arms in the air and made a scary noise. Gabby was so shocked and scared that she cried. Her mom tried to make it go away, but it just wouldn't leave. Until finally the ghost decided to leave. But it was too late, Gabby was very upset. The bad ending was that Gabby was so scared of the ghost that she wouldn't go to the park again.

One day, a red cat named Kitty went outside to play. She saw Mom hanging laundry on the line. Kitty liked to jump and play with the clothes. "Kitty, don't play with the laundry," said Mom. But Kitty did not listen. She saw a big red shirt and wanted to jump on it. Kitty jumped high in the sky. She grabbed the red shirt, but then she started to fall. The laundry line broke, and all the clothes fell down too. Kitty was scared and closed her eyes. When she opened her eyes, she saw that she was safe. The big red shirt had saved her from the fall. Mom was happy that Kitty was okay, but she said, "Next time, listen when I tell you not to play with the laundry." Kitty learned her lesson and never played with the laundry again.

Once upon a time, there was a colorful bird. The bird loved to sing and dance. The bird had many friends in the big tree. The bird was happy. One day, the bird met a nice cat. The cat wanted to marry the bird. The bird said "yes". All the friends in the big tree came to the wedding. They had fun. At the wedding, they had coffee. The cat drank the coffee. The cat felt not good. The cat went to sleep and did not wake up. The bird was sad. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little bear called Sam. Sam was always so sleepy. He was so sleepy he would often yawn. One day, Sam's Mum bought him a new leather hat. It was shiny and black and Sam smiled when he put it on. Sam went outside to go play but the leather hat was too fake. It was too stiff and heavy and it made Sam yawn. He couldn't play with comfort. But then his Dad said he had a surprise. He took Sam to the store and they bought him a new hat. It was made of soft fur. When Sam put it on, he felt warm and comfy. He smiled and did not yawn. After that, Sam played in the sunshine for hours with his new hat. He never wore the fake leather one again.

Once upon a time, there was an old cat named Tom. Tom liked to sit in the sun all day. He was very happy when he sat in the sun. One day, Tom found a piece of gum on the ground. He thought it was a toy. Tom played with the gum and had lots of fun. He pushed it with his paw and chased it around. But then, the gum got stuck on Tom's paw. He tried to shake it off, but it would not come off. Tom's friend, a little girl named Sue, saw him and helped him. She took the gum off his paw. Tom was very happy and went back to sit in the sun.

Opal dreamed of being a doctor some day. She was three years old, but she was determined to make her dream come true. One day, Opal found a rag on the ground and picked it up. She said to herself, "This is a perfect thing to use when I'm a doctor someday." As Opal imagined herself healing people in the future, she smiled. That night, Opal's mom found her playing with the rag. She asked Opal why she was so happy. Opal replied, "Mommy, I'm happy because I'm going to be a doctor and use this rag to heal people." Her mom chuckled and said, "That's wonderful, Opal! You should practice healing now, with your rag." Opal nodded and ran off to play with her rag. She pretended to give shots and bandage people up. She was so excited to help that she felt a little frustrated when she couldn't do it for real. But with her determination and her love for helping others, Opal knew that her dream of being a doctor would come true one day.
