Category: Beginner
Title: Mum and Dad and the Sandwich

Mum and Dad have a special treat for their three year old -- a sandwich. The little one is so excited! They put the sandwich on a plate, and when the three year old sees it, her eyes get big. “Oh, wow!” She gasps. “Is it for me?” Mum and Dad laugh and say yes. The three year old takes a bite and smiles with happiness. The little one’s two older brothers watch. They are jealous. They want a sandwich too! Mum and Dad tell them that everyone will have a sandwich soon. The two older brothers look relieved and happy. Mum and Dad hand them sandwiches and everyone eats together. The three year old is glad that her brothers can have a sandwich, too. She smiles at them. It is a happy day with tasty sandwiches!

Once upon a time, there was a broken drawer in a little boy's room. The boy's name was Tim. Tim was sad because he could not put his toys in the broken drawer. He wanted to fix it. Tim asked his dad for help. They went to the room and looked at the broken drawer. Tim's dad said, "We can fix it together!" They got some tools and started to work on the drawer. After a while, they fixed the broken drawer. Tim was very happy. He put all his toys back in the drawer. Now, Tim and his dad could play together in a clean room. They had a fun day fixing the drawer and playing with toys.

Ben and Lily liked to play in the snow. They made a big snowman with a hat and a scarf. They gave him a carrot nose and two stones for eyes. They were very happy with their snowman. "Look, he is smiling!" Lily said. "Yes, he is. He likes the snow too," Ben said. They decided to have a snowball fight. They rolled some snow into balls and threw them at each other. They laughed and dodged and ran. They had a lot of fun. But then, they saw a big dog running towards them. The dog was barking and jumping. He wanted to play too. He ran to the snowman and bit his nose. He pulled and pulled until he released the carrot from the snowman's face. "Hey, stop that!" Ben shouted. "That is our snowman's nose!" Lily was scared of the dog. She hid behind Ben and cried. "Go away, dog! You are not nice!" The dog did not listen. He wagged his tail and ran to Ben and Lily. He licked their faces and tried to play with them. He did not mean to be bad. He just wanted to have fun too. But Ben and Lily did not like the dog. They wanted him to leave them alone. They looked for help. They saw a man walking on the street. He was the dog's owner. He saw what his dog had done. "Rex, come here!" he called. "Bad dog! You should not ruin the children's snowman!" He ran to the dog and grabbed his collar. He apologized to Ben and Lily. "I'm sorry, kids. He is a fine dog, but he does not know how to behave. He likes snow too much. I will buy you a new carrot for your snowman." Ben and Lily were still sad and angry. But they accepted the man's apology. They hoped he would teach his dog to be more careful. They went back to their snowman and fixed his face. They decided to finish their snowball fight. They still liked to play in the snow.

Once upon a time, there was a little blue owl named Ollie. Ollie lived in a big tree with his family. He loved to fly around and play with his friends during the day. One day, Ollie saw a big storm coming. He knew that he and his friends would need a safe place to stay. Ollie told his friends about the storm. They all got scared. Ollie said, "Don't worry, my tree is big and strong. We can all stay there until the storm is over." His friends felt better and they all went to Ollie's tree. They played games and had fun while they waited for the storm. When the storm came, it was very loud and scary. Ollie's friends started to cry. Ollie knew he had to comfort them. He gave them big hugs and said, "It's okay, we are safe in my tree." The storm went away and the sun came out. Ollie's friends were happy and thanked him for being so brave and kind. They knew they could always count on Ollie to help them.
