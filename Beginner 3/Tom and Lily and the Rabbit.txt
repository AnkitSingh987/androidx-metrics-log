Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Rabbit

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play outside. They loved nature and all the animals and plants they could see. One day, they found a furry rabbit in their backyard. The rabbit was white and soft and had long ears. "Hello, rabbit!" Tom said. "Do you want to be our friend?" The rabbit did not answer. It was scared of the twins. It tried to run away, but Lily caught it in her arms. "Don't be afraid, rabbit!" Lily said. "We won't hurt you. We want to play with you and give you food." Tom and Lily took the rabbit to their playhouse. They had a toy kitchen there, where they liked to pretend to cook. They gave the rabbit some carrots and lettuce from their toy fridge. "Look, rabbit, we can cook for you!" Tom said. He put a carrot on a toy stove and pretended to turn it on. "This will make the carrot warm and yummy!" The rabbit did not like the toy stove. It smelled like plastic and made a loud noise. It thought the twins wanted to cook it, not the carrot. It was very afraid. It bit Tom's finger and jumped out of his arms. It ran out of the playhouse and back to the backyard. "Ow, rabbit, that hurt!" Tom cried. He held his finger and looked sad. Lily ran after the rabbit. She wanted to say sorry and make it happy. But the rabbit was too fast. It found a hole in the fence and escaped. "Bye, rabbit!" Lily shouted. "We hope you find a nice home in nature!" Tom and Lily went back to their playhouse. They felt sorry for the rabbit. They decided to not catch any more animals. They would only play with their toy animals and cook for them. They hoped the rabbit would forgive them and maybe come back to visit. They still wanted to be its friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear. The bear wanted to weigh himself. He went to the store to find a test to know how heavy he was. The store had many tests, but he picked the one that was unknown to him. He thought it would be fun to try something new. The bear took the test home and put it on the floor. He stood on the test, waiting to see how much he weighed. But something unexpected happened. The test began to shake and make a loud noise. The bear was scared and jumped off the test. The noise was so loud that it woke up a big, angry monster from its sleep. The monster came to the bear's house, very mad. The bear tried to hide, but the monster found him. The bear was very sad because the monster took all of his honey, and he could not weigh himself that day.

Tom and Lily were playing with clay in the garden. They liked to make different shapes and animals with the clay. Tom made a big snake and Lily made a small bird. "Look at my snake!" Tom said. "It is long and green and scary!" "Look at my bird!" Lily said. "It is cute and yellow and happy!" They put their clay creations on the grass and admired them. Then they heard a loud noise. It was a big truck coming down the road. The truck was carrying a lot of clay. "Wow!" Tom and Lily said. "That truck has so much clay! It is incredible!" They ran to the fence and watched the truck pass by. They saw the clay moving and changing in the back of the truck. It looked like magic. "Maybe the clay can turn into anything!" Tom said. "Maybe we can get some clay from the truck and make more things!" Lily said. They waited for the truck to stop at the red light. Then they climbed over the fence and ran to the truck. They reached for the clay, but they did not know that the clay was hot and sticky. It burned their hands and stuck to their fingers. "Ow!" Tom and Lily said. "The clay is hot! It hurts!" They dropped the clay and ran back to the fence. They cried and rubbed their hands. They wished they had not touched the clay. "Let's go back to our clay!" Tom said. "Yes, let's go back to our clay!" Lily said. They ran back to the grass and picked up their clay snake and bird. They hugged them and felt better. They learned that their clay was the best clay. It was not hot or sticky. It was soft and cool and fun. They played with their clay until it was time to go inside. They were happy and proud of their clay creations. They thought they were incredible.
