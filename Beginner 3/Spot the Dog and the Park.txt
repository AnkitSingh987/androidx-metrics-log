Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a calm dog who loved to play with his friends. One day, Spot went to the park to play with his best friend, a cat named Fluffy. While they were playing, another dog came up to them. This dog was not calm. He wanted to fight with Spot and Fluffy. Spot did not like this. He said to the dog, "We don't want to fight. We just want to play together." The other dog did not listen. He tried to fight with Spot and Fluffy again. But Spot and Fluffy refused to fight. They walked away from the mean dog and found a new place to play. They were happy that they did not fight and enjoyed the rest of the day playing together.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play with dough. They have many colors of dough. They can make animals, flowers, and cars with dough. One day, Lily makes a big pink pig with dough. She puts two black eyes, a curly tail, and a funny nose on it. She is very happy with her pig. She shows it to Ben. "Look, Ben, I made a pig with dough. Do you like it?" Lily asks. Ben looks at the pig. He thinks it is very nice. But he is also envious. He wants to make a pig too. But he does not have any pink dough. He only has blue, green, and yellow dough. "I like your pig, Lily. But I want to make a pig too. Can I have some of your pink dough?" Ben asks. Lily thinks for a moment. She likes her pink dough. She does not want to share it. But she also loves her brother. She does not want him to be sad. "Okay, Ben, you can have some of my pink dough. But only a little bit. And you have to share your other colors with me too. Deal?" Lily says. Ben nods. He is happy. He takes a little bit of pink dough from Lily. He gives her some of his blue, green, and yellow dough. They both start to make their own pigs with dough. They have fun with dough. They make many pigs. They make big pigs, small pigs, fat pigs, and thin pigs. They make pigs of different colors. They show their pigs to each other. They laugh and hug. They are not envious anymore. They are happy. They love dough. They love each other.

One day, a big red car went down the road. The car stopped near a farm. A man got out of the car. He saw a farmer with a lot of hay. The man asked the farmer, "Can I have some hay, please?" The farmer was very patient. He said, "Sure, you can have some hay. But first, you must help me with something." The man agreed to help the farmer. The farmer told the man to reverse his car near the hay. The man did as the farmer said. As the man was reversing his car, something unexpected happened. A big group of ducks came out from behind the hay! The ducks quacked and walked around the car. The farmer laughed and said, "You found my lost ducks! Thank you!" The man was happy he could help, and the farmer gave him some hay.

Once upon a time, there was a reliable jug. It was tall and it was filled with a liquid. Each day, a child would touch the jug, and it felt cold to the touch. Every day, the child tried to tip the jug. But it was too heavy and the child was not strong enough. Then one day, the child used a chair to help. He pushed it up against the jug and managed to tip it over. But a big surprise came out of the jug! Water went everywhere, and the child was happy. He had been trying to prevent it from tipping over, but he was too late. From that day on, the child remembered the reliable jug. He knew to make sure it was never tipped as it would make a huge mess. The child was very careful when he was near it to prevent it from happening again.
