Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Castle

Lily and Ben were playing with blocks in the playroom. They liked to build towers and bridges and castles. Lily wanted to make a big castle with a moat and a drawbridge. She asked Ben to help her. "Ben, can you give me that blue block? I need it for the water." But Ben did not answer. He was busy making his own tower. He wanted to make it very tall and strong. He ignored Lily and kept stacking blocks. "Ben, please! I need that block!" Lily said again. She was getting angry. She tried to reach for the block, but Ben moved it away. "No, it's mine! Go away!" Ben shouted. He pushed Lily with his hand. Lily fell down and bumped her head. She started to cry. The teacher heard the noise and came to the playroom. She saw Lily on the floor and Ben with his tower. She was not happy. "Ben, what did you do? Why did you push Lily?" she asked. Ben did not want to answer. He felt embarrassed. He knew he was wrong. He looked at his tower and saw that it was wobbly. He tried to fix it, but it was too late. The tower fell down and the blocks scattered everywhere. Lily stopped crying and looked at Ben. She saw that he was sad. She felt sorry for him. She got up and walked to him. "Ben, I'm sorry. I didn't mean to take your block. Do you want to help me make a castle?" she asked. Ben looked at Lily and nodded. He smiled a little. He said he was sorry too. He took her hand and they went to get some blocks. They decided to share and work together. They made a beautiful castle with a moat and a drawbridge. They were happy and proud. The teacher saw them and smiled. She praised them for being kind and friendly. She gave them each a sticker and a hug.

Once upon a time, there was a purple ball. The ball was very hard. One day, the ball met a small, soft ball. The purple ball said, "Hello, I want to introduce myself. I am a hard purple ball." The soft ball smiled and said, "Nice to meet you, I am a soft little ball. Let's play together!" They started to play, but the hard purple ball was too strong. It hit the soft ball very hard, and the soft ball started to cry. The hard purple ball didn't know what to do. It tried to say sorry, but the soft ball was too sad. In the end, the soft ball went away, and the hard purple ball was left all alone. The hard purple ball felt bad, but it was too late.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin lived in a big pond with his family. One day, he saw an unknown fish with a shiny scale. Fin wanted to make a new friend, but he was scared. Fin asked his mom if he could go play with the new fish. His mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Fin swam up to the new fish and said, "Hi, I am Fin. Do you want to play?" The new fish said, "Yes, I am Sparkle. Let's play!" While they were playing, a big bird came to the pond. The bird wanted to eat the fish. Fin and Sparkle were scared. They knew they had to serve and help each other. Fin and Sparkle swam very fast to hide under some rocks. The bird could not find them and flew away. Fin and Sparkle were happy they helped each other. They became best friends and played together every day. The pond was a happy place for all the fish.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Chewy. Chewy loved to chew on everything. He lived in a small house with his friend, Shadow the cat. Shadow was very organized. He liked to keep his toys in a neat line. One sunny day, Chewy saw a big, red ball on the ground. He wanted to chew it, but Shadow said, "No, Chewy! That is not a toy." Chewy did not listen. He took the ball and started to chew on it. Shadow shook his head and walked away. Later, Chewy and Shadow went for a walk. They saw a big tree with lots of leaves. Chewy wanted to chew on the leaves, but Shadow said, "No, Chewy! That is not a toy." Chewy did not listen. He took a leaf and started to chew on it. Then, the wind blew the leaves away, and Chewy saw a hidden toy under the tree. It was a new toy for Chewy to chew on. Shadow smiled and said, "See, Chewy? Good things come when you listen to your friend."
