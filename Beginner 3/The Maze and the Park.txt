Category: Beginner
Title: The Maze and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a big maze in a fun park. Many kids liked to play in the maze. It was a sunny day, and the maze was covered with pretty flowers. Two friends, Tom and Sue, wanted to play in the maze. They were very competitive and liked to win. Tom said, "I bet I can get through the maze faster than you!" Sue smiled and said, "We will see, Tom!" They started running into the maze. They turned left and right, trying to find the way out. The maze was tricky, but they kept going. In the end, Sue found the way out first. She was very happy and said, "I won, Tom!" Tom was a little sad, but he knew that they both had fun. They laughed and played in the park some more. The sun was setting, and they knew it was time to go home. They left the maze, excited for the next fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a large tree. The tree had many napkins on it. The napkins were very pretty. One day, a little bird came to the tree. The bird was looking for a place to make a home. The bird saw the napkins and thought they would be perfect. The little bird started to build a nest with the napkins. The bird found sticks and leaves too. The bird used the napkins to support the sticks and leaves. The nest was getting bigger and bigger. The bird was very happy. One day, a big wind came. The wind blew the napkins and sticks all around. The little bird was scared. But the large tree was strong. The tree did not let the wind take the nest away. The tree helped support the bird's home. The bird was safe and happy.

One day, a big dog and a little cat met in the park. The dog had a red ball, and the cat had a small toy mouse. They both looked at each other's toys and felt a bit guilty for wanting them. "Can we trade toys?" asked the dog, as he nodded his head. The cat nodded back and said, "Yes, we can trade." So, the dog gave his red ball to the cat, and the cat gave her toy mouse to the dog. They played happily with their new toys and became good friends. They learned that sharing and trading can be fun.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to sell his toys. He put them in a box and went outside. He put a sign that said "Toys for Sale". Tim waited for a long time, but no one came to buy his toys. A girl named Lily walked by and saw Tim's toys. "Wow, you have modern toys!" she said. Tim smiled and said, "Yes, I want to sell them. Do you want to buy one?" Lily looked at the toys and said, "I have zero money, but I can trade you one of my toys for one of yours." Tim thought it was a good idea. So, they traded toys and both were happy. They played with their new toys all day. From that day on, Tim and Lily became good friends and always shared their toys with each other.

Once upon a time, there was a big, heavy box. A little boy named Tom wanted to open it. He tried to lift it, but it was too heavy. Tom was sad and did not know what to do. One day, a nice dog came to play with Tom. The dog was hungry, so Tom gave him some meat. The dog was very happy. Tom had an idea. He asked the dog to help him with the heavy box. Tom and the dog worked together. Tom kneeled down and pushed the box from one side, and the dog pulled it from the other side. They tried very hard, and finally, the heavy box moved. Tom was so happy, and the dog wagged his tail. Inside the box, they found many toys and treats. Tom and the dog played all day and shared the treats. They became the best of friends, and they knew they could do anything when they worked together.
