Category: Beginner
Title: The Barrel and the Sue

Once upon a time, there was a big barrel. It was an expensive barrel. It was not like the other barrels. It had a hole in it. A little girl named Sue saw the barrel and wanted to fix it. Sue asked her mom for help. Her mom said they needed a special tool to fix the expensive barrel. They went to the store to buy the special tool. Sue was so happy, she couldn't wait to fix the barrel. When they got home, Sue and her mom started to fix the barrel. But then, something unexpected happened! When they fixed the barrel, a lot of colorful balls came out of the hole! The expensive barrel was full of fun balls to play with. Sue was so surprised and happy. She now had a fixed barrel and lots of balls to play with.

One day, a boy named Tim and a girl named Sue were playing in the park. They had many toys with them. Tim had a big red ball and Sue had a small blue car. They were having a lot of fun. Tim said, "Sue, let's trade toys for a little while." Sue thought Tim was generous and agreed. So Tim played with the small blue car and Sue played with the big red ball. They were both very happy. Suddenly, a dog came running towards them. The dog took the big red ball in its mouth and ran away! Tim and Sue were very sad. But then, the dog came back with two balls! One was the big red ball and the other was a new yellow ball. The dog wanted to play too. Tim and Sue were so surprised and happy. They all played together and had a great time at the park.

One day, a boy named Tom and a girl named Sue went for a walk. They saw a huge hill and wanted to go up. They started to march up the hill. They marched and marched until they were very tired. But they did not stop. At the top of the hill, they found a big hole. "What is this?" asked Sue. "I think it's a space to play," said Tom. They played in the space for a long time. They had lots of fun. They did not know that something was about to happen. Suddenly, a big wind came and blew them away. They landed in a new place. "Where are we?" asked Sue. "I don't know," said Tom. They looked around and saw a big sign. The sign said, "Welcome to the land of fun!" They were very happy. They played all day and made many new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy cat named Tom. Tom was always making messes. One day, he decided to help his mom in the kitchen. Mom was making a big dish for dinner. Tom asked, "Mom, can I help you?" Mom said, "Yes, Tom, you can help me. Be careful and don't make a mess." Tom tried his best to be careful. He held the dish with both paws. But Tom was still clumsy. He dropped the dish on the floor. "Oh no!" said Tom. Mom was sad, but she still loved Tom. "It's okay, Tom. We all make mistakes. Let's clean up and try again." They cleaned up the mess and made a new dish together. Tom learned that even if he was clumsy, he could still help his mom. And they had a yummy dinner together.

Once upon a time, there was a big universe. In this universe, there was a small planet. On this planet, there lived a kind boy named Tim and a girl named Lily. They were best friends and loved to play together. One day, Tim and Lily found a magic wand. Tim said, "Let's change something with this wand!" Lily agreed, "Yes, let's change the color of the sky!" They waved the wand and the sky turned from blue to green. They were very happy. But then, an angry bird saw the green sky and did not like it. The bird said, "Change it back! I want the blue sky!" Tim and Lily felt bad and used the magic wand to change the sky back to blue. The bird was happy again, and so were Tim and Lily. They learned that it's important to think about how changes might make others feel.
