Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Box and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a big, dull steel box. It sat in the park every day. No one knew what was inside. The kids would play around it, but no grown-up cared to open it. One day, a small boy named Tim went to the park to play. He saw the dull steel box and thought, "I wonder what's inside?" Tim went to the box and tried to open it. To his surprise, the box opened easily. Inside the box, Tim found a big, friendly dog. The dog jumped out and started to play with Tim. They had so much fun together. All the other kids came to play with the dog too. From that day on, the park was never dull again. Everyone cared about the dog and the big steel box was just a memory.

Once there was a little girl. She wanted something new, so she asked her mom to buy her a chest. Mom took her to the store and they found a very nice chest. The chest was very big and shiny, and it was strong too! Mom had to use her strong hands to lift it. The girl was thrilled and couldn't wait to get it home. When they got home, the girl took the new chest up to her room and put her special things inside. She loved her new chest and was so happy to have it. The girl was always so excited to open and close the chest. She would take it everywhere with her, so she could keep her special things safe. The strong chest was just perfect for the little girl!

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to eat peaches. One day, she saw a big peach tree in her yard. It was difficult for her to reach the peaches because they were very high up. Mia asked her mom, "Mom, can you help me get the peaches?" Her mom said, "Yes, Mia. I will shake the tree, and the peaches will fall down." So, Mia's mom shook the tree, and many peaches fell to the ground. Mia was very happy. She picked up the peaches and put them in a big bowl. She shared the peaches with her friends, and they all enjoyed eating them together. The peaches were sweet and juicy, and everyone loved them.

One day, a girl named Lucy went to the museum with her mom. The museum was big and cold. Lucy wore a warm coat. She saw many things in the museum, like old toys and pretty rocks. In one room, Lucy saw a big red button. It said "Do not push." Lucy wanted to push the button. She looked around and saw no one. So, she pushed the button. Suddenly, the museum changed. The cold museum became warm and the old toys started to play. The pretty rocks turned into candy. Lucy and her mom were surprised. They had fun in the new museum.

Once upon a time, in a calm town, there was a street. On this street, there lived a nice man named Tim. Tim had a job to deliver boxes to people's homes. Every day, he would carry the boxes and give them to the people who lived on the street. One sunny day, Tim had a big box to deliver. He walked up to the house and knocked on the door. But, no one was there. Tim was sad because he could not give the box to the person. He decided to try again later. Later, Tim went back to the house with the big box. This time, the door opened, and a little dog came out! The dog took the box from Tim with a big smile. Tim was very surprised! The dog was the one who wanted the box all along. Tim was happy he could deliver the box, and the dog was happy to get it. They both had a great day on the calm street.

Tom and Sam are brothers. They like to play with their toy cars and trucks. They have many gears that make the cars and trucks move and sound. Tom has a big red truck with a loud horn. Sam has a small blue car with a fast engine. One day, Tom wants to play with Sam's car. He thinks it is more fun than his truck. He asks Sam to share, but Sam says no. He likes his car and does not want to give it to Tom. Tom gets angry and tries to grab the car from Sam. Sam holds on tight and does not let go. Tom and Sam start to fight over the car. They pull and push and shout. Tom pinches Sam's arm hard. Sam cries out and pinches Tom's ear. Tom screams and pinches Sam's nose. Sam kicks and pinches Tom's leg. They hurt each other and make a lot of noise. Mom hears the noise and comes to see what is wrong. She sees Tom and Sam pinching and kicking each other. She is not happy. She takes the car and the truck away from them. She tells them to stop fighting and to say sorry. She says they cannot play with the gears until they learn to share and be nice. Tom and Sam feel bad. They are sore and sad. They have no car and no truck. They have no gears to play with. They are helpless. They look at each other and say sorry. They hug and make up. They ask Mom to give them back their toys. Mom smiles and says yes. She says they can play with the gears together, but they have to take turns and be gentle. Tom and Sam agree. They thank Mom and go to play with the gears. They have fun and do not fight anymore.
