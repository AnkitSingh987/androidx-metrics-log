Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Spoon

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big, icy hill. Tim wanted to slide down the hill. He looked around and found a spoon. Tim thought the spoon would help him slide down the icy hill. Tim sat on the spoon and started to slide down. But something unexpected happened. The spoon began to grow! Tim was scared and began to frown. He didn't know what to do. The spoon became bigger and bigger as it slid down the hill. When Tim reached the bottom of the hill, the spoon stopped growing. Tim looked at the big spoon and smiled. He was not scared anymore. He realized that he now had a giant spoon to play with. Tim and his friends had lots of fun playing with the big spoon all day long.

One day, a boy and a girl went to the beach. They wanted to have fun and play. The boy said, "We must behave and be nice to each other." The girl agreed and they started to build a sand castle. As they built their sand castle, they tried to make it original. They added shells and sticks to make it pretty. The boy and girl were very happy with their castle. They said to each other, "Wow! Our castle is the best!" But then, something unexpected happened. A big wave came and crashed into their sand castle. The boy and girl were sad. Their castle was gone. They did not have a good day at the beach after all.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to run and play with his friends. One day, there was a big race at school, and Tim wanted to win a trophy. Before the race, Tim's mom said, "Remember to be quiet and listen to your teacher. Keep trying your best, and you might win the trophy." Tim nodded and went to the race. During the race, Tim tried his best and kept running. He saw his friend, Sam, fall down. Tim stopped and helped Sam stand up. They both kept running together. In the end, Tim and Sam did not win the race, but they were happy because they helped each other. The teacher saw Tim help Sam and gave them both a special trophy for being good friends. Tim and Sam learned that being kind and helping others is more important than winning.

Sara and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. The park has many trees. Some trees are green and tall. They are pine trees. Sara and Tom like to smell the pine trees. They smell good. One day, Sara and Tom go to the park. They see a big pine tree. It has many cones. The cones are brown and hard. Sara and Tom want to touch the cones. They are curious. They climb the pine tree. They are careful. They do not fall. They reach the cones. They pick some cones. They feel the cones. They are rough and prickly. They throw the cones. They make a game. They are playful. They laugh and have fun. But then, they hear a noise. It is a squirrel. The squirrel lives in the pine tree. The squirrel is angry. The cones are his food. He does not like Sara and Tom. He scolds them. He makes a loud sound. He shows his teeth. Sara and Tom are scared. They do not want to make the squirrel mad. They say sorry to the squirrel. They drop the cones. They climb down the pine tree. They run away. They go to another place. They find a swing. They play on the swing. They are happy. They forget the squirrel. They are still friends.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree in a big park. The tree was very happy because it had many friends. The birds, the squirrels, and the kids all loved the tall tree. One sunny day, a family came to the park for a picnic. They put a big blanket under the tall tree. They had yummy food like apples, sandwiches, and juice. The tall tree liked to watch them eat and play. A little girl asked her mom, "Why is the tree so tall?" Her mom tried to explain. She said, "The tree grows tall to reach the sun. The sun helps the tree grow big and strong." The little girl smiled and hugged the tall tree. The tall tree felt very loved.
