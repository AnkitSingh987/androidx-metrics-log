Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Compassionate Mouse and the Park

One day, a compassionate mouse named Tim went out to play. He loved to scatter little bits of cheese for his friends. He would share his cheese with everyone in the park. As Tim played, he met a new friend named Sam. Sam was a little squirrel who also loved cheese. Tim and Sam played together all day, laughing and scattering cheese for their friends. At the end of the day, Tim and Sam were very happy. They had shared their cheese with everyone and made many friends. They knew they would always be compassionate and help others.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a dark river. Many kids liked to play near the river, but they were always careful. One sunny day, a little girl named Lily and her friends went to play by the river. While they were playing, Lily saw a big fish in the water. She wanted to poke the fish to see what would happen. Her friends told her not to do it, but Lily didn't listen. She poked the fish, and something unexpected happened. The fish started to talk! It thanked Lily for poking it, because it had an itch it couldn't reach. The fish told Lily and her friends that if they help others, good things will happen to them too. From that day on, Lily and her friends learned the importance of helping others and being kind.

Once there was a little girl named Anna. Anna loved the ocean and she often dreamed of going on an adventure. One day, her dream came true and her parents took her to a port. When Anna saw the port, she was amazed. The boats in the harbor were so bright and colorful that she couldn't help but smile. She ran around looking at all the different boats, but then it started to get chilly. Anna started to shiver from the cold, so her parents wrapped her up in a blanket and found a warm spot for her. She watched the boats for the rest of the day and dreamt of going on one someday. Anna had a wonderful day at the port and she never forgot the amazing sights and sounds she had seen. She can't wait to go back soon and explore the port again.

Tom is a farmer. He has a big farm with many animals. He likes to feed them and play with them. He has a dog, a cat, a cow, a pig, a horse and a hen. One day, Tom wants to ride his horse. He puts on his hat and his boots. He goes to the barn and finds his horse. His horse is brown and strong. His name is Max. "Hello, Max," Tom says. "Are you ready for a ride?" Max neighs and nods. He likes to run and jump with Tom. Tom leans on a stool and puts a saddle on Max. He buckles the straps and checks the reins. He leans on Max's back and swings his leg over. He sits on the saddle and holds the reins. "Let's go, Max," Tom says. "We will have fun today." Max trots out of the barn and into the field. He sees the other animals and greets them. The dog barks, the cat meows, the cow moos, the pig oinks and the hen clucks. They are happy to see Tom and Max. Tom and Max ride around the field. They go fast and slow. They turn left and right. They jump over fences and logs. They feel the wind and the sun. They are having fun. Tom and Max are good friends. They love each other and the farm. They are happy.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Sue lived in a small house. She had a dog named Spot. One day, Sue and Spot went for a walk in the park. They saw a big tree with sweet red apples. Sue said, "Spot, let's pick some apples!" As they picked apples, a little boy walked up to them. He had a frown on his face. Sue asked, "What's your name?" The boy said, "My name is Tim." Sue asked, "Why do you frown, Tim?" Tim said, "I lost my toy car." Sue and Spot wanted to help Tim. They looked all around the park for the toy car. Then, Spot started to bark at the big tree. Sue looked up and saw Tim's toy car in the tree! They all laughed. Sue said, "How did it get there?" Tim said, "I don't know." They picked the car from the tree and Tim's frown turned into a big smile. From that day, Sue, Spot, and Tim became best friends.
