Category: Beginner
Title: Mama and the Door

"Mama, mama!" shouted Bob. He was closing the door to his house on Wednesday. "What is it, my little one?" replied Mama. Bob said, "The room is full of hairy spiders! I can't stand it any more!" Mama laughed and said, "Spiders are not so bad, Bob. Let's try to find a way to get rid of them together." Bob said, "Okay mama! Can we close the window?" Mama agreed and they closed the window. But then Bob shouted, "Look! More spiders coming from the wall!" Mama said firmly, "Okay, let's close the door so the spiders can't get in. We can figure this out together." Bob and Mama closed the door. Mama said, "Now we need to figure out a way to get rid of the spiders forever. How can we do that?" Bob thought and then said, "We need to find a hairy spider catcher!" Mama smiled and said, "I think I know someone who can help us. Just wait here while I go make a call." Bob waited patiently while Mama made the phone call. When she came back, she said, "The hairy spider catcher will come tomorrow Wednesday and get rid of all the spiders!" Bob was very happy and said, "Thank you mama!" Mama hugged Bob and said,"No problem, my little one.

Tim and Sam are brothers. They like to play with blocks. They make houses and cars and towers with blocks. They have many blocks of different colors and shapes. One day, they want to make a big house with a roof. They use big blocks for the walls and small blocks for the windows and doors. They look for blocks to make the roof. But they only have thin blocks left. They are not good for the roof. The roof will fall down. "Tim, what can we do?" Sam asks. "We need more blocks for the roof." Tim thinks hard. He sees a big piece of paper on the table. He has an idea. He runs to the table and gets the paper. He brings it to Sam. "Look, Sam, we can use this paper for the roof. It is big and flat. We can attach it to the blocks with tape. Then we will have a roof." Sam likes the idea. He helps Tim to cut the paper and fold it. They make a triangle shape with the paper. They use tape to attach the paper to the blocks. They put the paper on top of the walls. They have a roof. "Wow, Tim, you are smart. Our house is done. It has a roof. It looks nice." Sam says. "Thank you, Sam. You are good at making walls and windows. Our house is strong. It will not fall down." Tim says. They are happy. They play with their house. They pretend to live in it. They have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little ghost. The ghost was eager to make friends. He wanted to improve his talking, so more kids would like him. One day, the ghost saw a little girl. She was playing with her toys. The ghost said, "Hi, can I play too?" The little girl looked scared. She did not like the ghost. The ghost tried to improve his talking. He said, "Please, I want to be your friend." The little girl was still scared. She ran away from the ghost. The ghost was sad. He could not make friends. Even though he tried to improve, no one wanted to play with him. The ghost stayed alone, and the story had a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a little pink car. This car was very weird. It could only go reverse. The car had a lot of fun going backward all the time. One day, the little pink car met a big blue car. The big blue car could go forward and backward. The big blue car showed the little pink car how to go forward. The little pink car was very happy. Now, the little pink car can go forward and reverse. The little pink car and the big blue car became best friends. They had lots of fun playing together. And they lived happily ever after.
