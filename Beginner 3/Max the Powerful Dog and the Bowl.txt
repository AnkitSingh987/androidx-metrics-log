Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Powerful Dog and the Bowl

Once upon a time, there was a powerful dog named Max. Max loved to play and run. One day, Max went to a gym to play with his friends. At the gym, Max saw a big bowl. In the bowl, there were many colors. Max wanted to mix the colors. He put his paw in the bowl and mixed the colors. The colors became a beautiful rainbow. Max showed his friends the rainbow. They all thought it was amazing. They played and jumped in the rainbow. Max felt very happy and proud. He knew that mixing colors was a powerful thing to do. And they all had a fun day at the gym.

Once upon a time, there was a green frog named Fred. Fred lived in a small pond. The pond had soft mud that made Fred feel comfortable. Fred had many friends who lived in the pond too. One day, Fred and his friends found a box with a lock. They did not know what was inside. They tried to open it, but they could not. Fred's friend, Sam the fish, said, "We should ask for help to open the lock." So, they went to ask Mr. Owl, who was very smart. Mr. Owl told them, "To open the lock, you must be kind to each other and work together." Fred, Sam, and their friends listened to Mr. Owl's words. They all tried to open the lock together, and it worked! Inside the box were toys for everyone to share. Fred and his friends learned that being kind and working together can help solve problems. They played with their new toys and had lots of fun.

Tom and Lily were friends who liked to play on the shore. They liked to run on the sand, splash in the water, and look for shells and stones. One day, they decided to have a race to see who was faster. "Ready, set, go!" Tom shouted, and they both ran as fast as they could along the shore. Lily was very fast, but Tom was faster. He reached the big rock first and turned around to see where Lily was. "I win, I win!" he yelled, jumping up and down. "I am the fastest!" Lily was not happy. She felt sad and angry. She did not like to lose. She stopped running and sat down on the sand. She did not want to play with Tom anymore. "Hey, Lily, don't be mad," Tom said, running back to her. "It was just a game. You are fast too. And you are my friend. I don't want to make you sad." He gave her a big hug and a smile. He also gave her a pretty shell he had found on the way. Lily felt better. She smiled back and hugged him too. She also gave him a shiny stone she had found. "Thank you, Tom," she said. "You are a good friend. And you are very fast. But maybe next time I will win!" They laughed and got up. They decided to play another game. They had fun on the shore. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She loved to go to the park and play on the swings. One sunny day, Mia went to the park with her mommy to swing. As she swung higher and higher, Mia looked up at the sky. She saw a tiny star shining bright. Mia was very happy and wanted to swing all the way up to the star. But after swinging for a long time, Mia started to feel tired. Her mommy saw she was tired and helped her off the swing. They walked home, holding hands. That night, Mia looked out her window and saw the same little star. She smiled and went to sleep, dreaming of swinging up to the star again.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big, shiny marble. It was so pretty! Tim wanted to show it to his friend, Sam. He ran to Sam's house and knocked on the door. Sam opened the door and saw the marble. "Wow, what a nice marble!" Sam said. "Can I have it?" Sam asked. Tim thought for a moment. He didn't want to be rude, so he said, "Okay, I will give you the marble. But you have to promise to be nice and share it with me." Sam smiled and said, "I promise!" Tim and Sam played with the marble all day. They rolled it on the ground and threw it in the air. They had so much fun! Tim was happy he gave the marble to Sam, and they both learned that sharing is much better than being rude.
