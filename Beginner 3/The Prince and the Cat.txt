Category: Beginner
Title: The Prince and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a kind prince. He lived in a big castle. The prince liked to help people. He was very nice. One day, the prince wanted to serve food to his friends. He asked his friends to come to the castle. They were happy and said yes. They all sat at a big table. When the prince served the food, a little cat jumped on the table. The cat ate the food very fast. The prince and his friends laughed. They did not know the cat could eat so much. The cat was a surprise. They all had fun and played with the cat. The kind prince and his friends had a great day.

---

Tim and Lily were playing with their toys in the living room. They liked to pretend they were in a big castle, and they had a lot of fun. But then, their mom called them to come to the kitchen. She said they had to eat their lunch. Tim and Lily did not want to stop playing. They said, "No, mom, we are not hungry. We want to play more." Their mom was not happy. She said, "You have to eat, or you will be sick. Come now, or I will be very angry." Tim and Lily did not listen. They kept playing with their toys. They did not hear their mom coming to the living room. She was very angry. She entered the room and saw the mess they made. She said, "What are you doing? You are very naughty. You did not obey me. You made a big mess. You have to clean it up right now." Tim and Lily were scared. They said, "Sorry, mom, we were just playing. Please don't be angry. We will clean it up." But their mom did not listen. She said, "No, you will not clean it up. You will go to your room and stay there. You will not play with your toys anymore. You will not have any dessert. You will not watch TV. You will not have any fun. You are punished." Tim and Lily were very sad. They cried and begged their mom to forgive them. But their mom did not forgive them. She took them to their room and locked the door. She said, "You will stay here until I say so. You will think about what you did. You will be sorry. I am the judge, and this is my rule." Tim and Lily were very sad. They hugged each other and cried. They wished they had listened to their mom. They wished they had eaten their lunch. They wished they had not made a mess. They wished they could play again. But they could not. They were punished. They were very sad. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a normal boy named Tim. Tim liked to build things with his toy blocks. He would build tall towers, big houses, and long roads. One day, Tim wanted to build a cross with his blocks. He put one block down, and then another block on top. He put more blocks on the sides. Tim was very happy with his cross. Tim showed his cross to his mom and dad. They were very proud of him. They put the cross on the shelf for everyone to see. Tim felt happy and went to play with his blocks again.

Once upon a time, there was a little red house. In the house lived a girl named Amy and her dog, Spot. Amy and Spot loved to play and have fun together. One day, Amy saw a big box on the table. She stared at the box, wondering what was inside. Spot stared at the box too, wagging his tail. They were both very curious. Amy opened the box and found lots of chocolate inside. She was so happy! Amy shared the chocolate with her family, but not with Spot, because chocolate is not good for dogs. Instead, she gave Spot a yummy dog treat. They both enjoyed their treats and continued to play happily in their little red house.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big park. In the park, there was a sign. The sign said, "Keep the toys separate." But the kids did not like this rule. They wanted to play with all the toys together. One day, a little boy named Tim and a little girl named Sue went to the park. They saw the sign and looked at each other. Tim said, "I don't like this rule. Do you?" Sue shook her head and said, "No, I don't. Let's play with all the toys together!" So, Tim and Sue started to play with all the toys together. They had so much fun. But then, the park guard came. He was not happy. He said, "You must follow the sign. Keep the toys separate!" Tim and Sue were sad. But then, a nice lady came to the park. She saw the sign and the sad kids. She said, "I have an idea! Let's make new signs that say, 'All toys available to play together!'" The kids smiled and helped the nice lady make new signs. From that day on, all the kids in the park could play with all the toys together. They were very happy. And they always remembered to share and play nicely with each other.
