Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Ship

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a big ship. The ship was very gloomy. It was dark and had a sad face. Tim wanted to make the ship happy. Tim had an idea. He said to the ship, "Let's play a fun game. It will excite you!" The ship looked at Tim and said, "Okay, let's try." They played a game of hide and seek. Tim hid and the ship looked for him. The ship found Tim and they both laughed. The ship was not gloomy anymore. It was happy and excited to play with Tim. They played all day and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She liked to go to the market with her mom. At the market, they would find many yummy things to eat like apples, bananas, and cookies. One day, Lily saw a cute, little cat at the market. The cat was very patient and waited for Lily to come close. Lily wanted to take the cat home, but she had to ask her mom first. She ran to her mom and asked if they could keep the cat. Mom said yes, and they took the cat home. Lily and the cat became best friends. They would play all day, and at night, the cat would sleep next to Lily's bed. Lily and the cat were very happy together, and they always remembered the day they found each other at the market.

Once upon a time there was a farmer named Tom. Tom lived on a big farm and everyday he laid in his big field. One day, Tom saw something odd laying in his field. It was round and brown, like a barrel. He went out to see what it was and it was a barrel filled with something gross and disgusting. Tom was so disgusted that he wanted to get it out of his field. He grabbed a big stick and pushed the barrel out of the field. After that, Tom never saw it again. The End.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They had a lot of fun playing on the swings and slide. After playing, Tim felt very hot and sweaty. His mom said, "Let's go home and get you dry." When they got home, Tim's mom opened a drawer to find a dry shirt for him. But the drawer was empty! Tim's mom looked at him and asked, "Did you take all the shirts out of the drawer?" Tim looked at the floor and felt guilty. He said, "Yes, I did. I'm sorry, Mom." Tim's mom helped him pick up all the shirts and put them back in the drawer. She told him, "It's okay to make mistakes, but we need to fix them." Tim promised to keep his shirts in the drawer and not make a mess again. After they were done, Tim put on a dry shirt and gave his mom a big hug.

One day, a lively boy named Tim went to the train station with his mom. They were going to visit grandma. Tim was very excited to see the big train. He had never been on a train before. While waiting, Tim saw a big box. He asked his mom, "Can I open the box?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Tim opened the box and revealed a big, colorful ball. He was so happy and started to play with it. A man came and said, "Thank you for finding my ball." Tim gave the ball back to the man. The man was a train driver! He let Tim and his mom ride on the train for free. They had a great time visiting grandma. The moral of the story is to always be kind and help others.

Jill and Ben wanted to make a necklace for Mom. They had some beads and a needle, but they needed thread. They asked Dad if they could go to the shop and buy some thread. "OK, but be careful and don't talk to strangers," Dad said. He gave them some money and a bag. "And don't buy anything else, just thread." Jill and Ben walked to the shop. They saw many things that they liked: toys, books, candy. But they remembered what Dad said and only looked for thread. They found a big spool of red thread. It was shiny and soft. "Let's buy this one," Jill said. "Mom likes red." They took the thread to the counter and paid the lady. She gave them a receipt and a smile. "Have a nice day," she said. Jill and Ben put the thread in the bag and started to walk back home. They were happy and excited to make the necklace. But on the way, they saw a man with a cart. He was selling ice cream. It looked yummy and cold. "Can we have some ice cream, please?" Ben asked Jill. "We have some money left." Jill looked at the money and the ice cream. She wanted some too. She forgot what Dad said and nodded. "OK, just one scoop each." They went to the man and asked for ice cream. He smiled and took their money. He gave them two cones with big scoops of vanilla ice cream. "Enjoy," he said. Jill and Ben licked their ice cream. It was yummy and cold. They forgot about the thread and the necklace. They forgot about Dad and Mom. They just enjoyed their ice cream. But then, a big dog came running. It saw the ice cream and wanted some too. It barked and jumped on Jill and Ben. It knocked them down and took their cones. It ate the ice cream and ran away. Jill and Ben cried. They were hurt and dirty. They had no ice cream and no money. They looked for the bag with the thread, but it was gone. The dog had taken it too. They had nothing to make the necklace for Mom. They had nothing to show Dad. They had been bad and foolish. They wished they had listened to Dad and not bought anything else, just thread.
