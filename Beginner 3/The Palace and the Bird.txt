Category: Beginner
Title: The Palace and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big palace. In the palace, there was a king named Tom. Tom was very sad. He had no friends to play with. One day, he went for a walk in the garden. In the garden, he saw a little bird. The bird was also sad. Tom asked the bird, "What is your name?" The bird said, "My name is Sue. I am sad because I have no friends too." Tom smiled and said, "Let's be friends, Sue!" Sue was very happy. They played together every day. The palace was not sad anymore. Tom and Sue were best friends and they lived happily ever after.

One day, a small fish and a big fish were swimming in a wide pond. The small fish was sad because he was not as big as the other fish. The big fish saw the small fish and swam up to him. "Hi, small fish," said the big fish. "Why are you sad?" The small fish told the big fish that he wanted to be big too. The big fish thought for a moment and then mentioned, "I have a magic scale that can make you big like me. But you must promise to be kind and help others." The small fish promised to be kind and help others. The big fish gave the small fish the magic scale. The small fish touched the scale and became big like the big fish. One day, the big fish got stuck in a net. He could not get out. The small fish, now big, saw the big fish in trouble and remembered his promise. He swam up to the net and used his strong body to break it. The big fish was free. The two big fish swam together, and the small fish learned that being big is good, but being kind and helpful is even better.

Once upon a time, there was a lively cat named Tom. Tom liked to play with his toy bow. He would run and jump with the bow in his mouth. Tom was very happy. One day, Tom could not find his bow. He started to think. "Did I leave it by the tree?" Tom went to look. But it was not there. Tom felt sad. Tom kept thinking. "Maybe it is in the house." He went inside and looked around. And there it was! Tom found his bow under his bed. He was so happy, he danced and played with his bow all day long.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tom. Tom had a mysterious toy called a microscope. He loved to look at things with it. It made small things look big. One day, Tom's friend, Sam, came to play. Tom showed Sam his microscope. Sam said, "Wow! Can I play with it too?" Tom thought about it and said, "Okay, I will allow you to play with it, but be careful." But Sam was not careful. He dropped the microscope and it broke. Tom was very sad. Sam said, "I am sorry, Tom." Tom said, "It's okay, but next time, be more careful with other people's things." The bad ending was that Tom's microscope was broken, and he couldn't play with it anymore. The moral value of the story is to always be careful with other people's things, so we don't break them.

Once upon a time, there was a smelly sailor named Tom. Tom lived near the sea, and he loved to sail his boat. One day, Tom saw that his boat was very dirty. He knew he needed to clean it. Tom got a big bucket of water and a cloth. He started to clean his boat. He scrubbed and scrubbed until it was shiny and clean. The smelly sailor was very happy with his clean boat. The next day, Tom went sailing on his clean boat. He felt so proud and happy. As he sailed, the wind blew away the smelly smell. Now, Tom was a clean sailor with a clean boat, and he lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a red cap that he loved very much. He wore his cap every day, even when it was hot or when it rained. Tim was a stubborn boy who did not like to take off his cap. One day, Tim's mom asked him to try a new cap. She said, "Tim, your red cap is old. Let's try this blue cap." But Tim did not want to try the new cap. He only wanted his red cap. Tim's mom was sad, but she did not make him try the new cap. The next day, Tim went to play with his friends. They saw his red cap and said, "Tim, your cap is old. Why don't you try a new one?" Tim thought about what his friends said. He went home and told his mom, "Mom, I will try the new cap." Tim tried the blue cap and liked it. He was not stubborn anymore, and he learned that trying new things can be fun.
