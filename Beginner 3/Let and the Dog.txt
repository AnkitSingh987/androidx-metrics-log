Category: Beginner
Title: Let and the Dog

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the yard. The cat said to the dog, "Let's unite and find a dish of smooth food to eat." The dog agreed, and they went to look for food together. They looked high and low, but they could not find any smooth food. They were about to give up when they saw a big dish of smooth food. They were very happy and started to eat it. But then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew by and said, "That dish is not for you. It is for the sick animals who need smooth food to feel better." The cat and the dog felt bad and decided to give the dish back. They learned that it is good to help others and not just think about themselves.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a good boy. He liked to help his mom and dad. One day, Tim's dad asked him to deliver a magazine to the neighbor's house. Tim took the magazine and went to the neighbor's house. He walked and walked. But, something unexpected happened. Tim saw a big dog in the yard. The dog barked and barked. Tim was scared. Then, the dog stopped barking. Tim saw that the dog just wanted to play. He played with the dog and had fun. In the end, Tim delivered the magazine and made a new friend. The big dog was not scary, but a good friend.

Lily and Ben were twins who liked to play together. One day, they found a big box of crayons in their room. They wanted to draw pictures with the crayons, but they did not know how to use them. "Look, this crayon is sharp!" Lily said, holding a red crayon. "I can make a line with it." "Me too!" Ben said, grabbing a blue crayon. "I can make a circle with it." They started to draw on a sheet of paper, but soon they ran out of space. They looked around and saw the wall. It was white and empty. "Let's draw on the wall!" Lily said. "It is like a big paper." "OK!" Ben said. "It will be fun." They ran to the wall and began to draw with their crayons. They made shapes, animals, flowers, and stars. They did not hear their mom coming up the stairs. "What are you doing?" Mom asked, seeing the wall covered with crayon marks. She was angry and sad. "You have ruined the wall! You are not supposed to draw on the wall. You are supposed to draw on paper. You have to clean this up right now!" Lily and Ben felt sorry. They did not mean to make mom angry and sad. They did not know that drawing on the wall was bad. They thought it was fun. "We are sorry, mom," they said. "We did not know. We will clean it up." Mom gave them a cloth and a bucket of water. They tried to wipe off the crayon marks, but they were hard to remove. They had to scrub and scrub. It took a long time and a lot of work. When they finished, the wall was white again, but they were tired and bored. They wished they had not drawn on the wall. They wished they had listened to mom. Mom saw that they had cleaned the wall. She was still angry, but she was also proud. She knew that they had learned their lesson. "I hope you understand why you should not draw on the wall," she said. "Drawing is good, but you have to respect the things that belong to others. You have to use the right tools and the right places. Do you understand?" "Yes, mom," they said. "We understand. We are sorry. We will not do it again." Mom smiled and hugged them. She was glad that they were sorry and that they understood. She wanted to reward them for their good behavior. "I have a treat for you," she said. "Do you want some cookies?" "Yes, please!" they said. "We love cookies!" Mom gave them each a cookie. They were soft and sweet and yummy. They thanked mom and ate their cookies. They were happy and grateful. They learned that drawing on the wall was bad, but drawing on paper was good. They learned that respecting others was important, but being sorry and understanding was also important. They learned that mom loved them, but also wanted them to be good. They learned that being good could bring them treats, but also happiness.
