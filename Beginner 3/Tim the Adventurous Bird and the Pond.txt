Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Adventurous Bird and the Pond

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived an adventurous little bird named Tim. Tim loved to fly high and explore new places. One sunny day, Tim found a big palm tree near a small pond. He was very happy to see the tree and the pond. Tim met a wise old frog named Fred near the pond. Fred said, "Be careful, Tim. Don't soak in the pond for too long or something bad might happen." Tim nodded but he wanted to have fun, so he didn't listen to Fred. Tim played in the pond and soaked his wings for a long time. He was having so much fun. But then, a big fish came and bit Tim's wing. Tim cried and flew back to the palm tree. His wing was hurt and he couldn't fly high anymore. Tim was very sad. The moral of the story is to always listen to wise advice from others.

Once upon a time, there was a man and his wife. They lived in a small house near the woods. The man was troubled because he did not have much food for his wife and him. One day, the man went into the woods to find food. While he was gone, his wife tried to cook a meal. She put a pot on the fire, but the fire began to burn too big and too hot. The wife was scared and did not know what to do. The fire got bigger and bigger. The house started to burn too. The man came back and saw the fire. He tried to help, but it was too late. The house burned down, and the man and his wife were very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big bookshelf in his room. The bookshelf was very old and filthy. It was dirty because Tim never cleaned it. Tim loved to read books from his bookshelf every day. One day, Tim's mom told him to clean his bookshelf. She gave him a cloth to wipe the dust away. Tim did not want to clean it, but he knew he had to. So, he started wiping the bookshelf. As he cleaned, he found a book that he had never seen before. Tim gave the new book to his mom. She smiled and said, "Good job, Tim! You found a special book because you cleaned your bookshelf." Tim was happy. He learned that it was good to keep things clean. From that day on, Tim always kept his bookshelf clean and tidy.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw his friend, Sue, playing with a big bag of popcorn. Tim wanted to play with Sue and the popcorn. Sue said, "Let's play popcorn party!" They played and threw the popcorn in the air. They laughed and had fun. The popcorn fell on the ground and got dirty. They didn't care, they just kept playing. Then, a big bird came and saw the dirty popcorn. The bird started to eat the popcorn. Tim and Sue were surprised. They stopped playing and watched the bird. The bird said, "Thank you for the popcorn!" Tim and Sue smiled and went to play a new game.

Once upon a time, there was a farm. On this farm, there were many animals. There was a big cow, a little pig, and a funny chicken. One day, the chicken said to the pig, "I dare you to jump over the fence!" The pig looked at the fence and said, "I don't know. It looks too high." The chicken laughed and said, "Don't be scared. It's fun!" The pig thought about it and said, "Okay, I dare you to jump first." The chicken jumped over the fence and landed on the other side. The pig watched and thought it looked fun. So, the pig jumped over the fence, too. On the other side of the fence, they found a big, shiny, expensive toy. The pig and the chicken played with the toy all day. They had a lot of fun together. When the sun went down, they went back to the farm and told the cow about their adventure. The cow smiled and said, "I'm glad you had fun. The farm is a great place to live and play!"
