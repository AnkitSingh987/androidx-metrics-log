Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Cake

One day, a boy named Tim found a mysterious rag in his room. He did not know where it came from. Tim showed the rag to his mom. She said, "That's a magic rag. If you write on it, something bad will happen." Tim did not believe his mom. He thought, "What if I write something good?" So, he took a pen and wrote on the rag, "I want a big, yummy cake." Suddenly, a big cake appeared on the table. Tim was very happy. Tim's friend, Sam, came to play. Tim told Sam about the magic rag. But Sam did not listen to Tim's warning. Sam wrote, "I want a big, scary monster." A big, scary monster appeared and chased Tim and Sam. They were very scared and ran away. The monster ate the cake and destroyed their toys. Tim and Sam learned that they should always listen to their mom's advice.

Once upon a time, there was a smart little girl. She was always exploring. One day, the girl was walking through the forest and she came across something shiny. It was a big lump of gold! She was so excited, she wanted to take it back with her. But then, a big, mean bear appeared. He said it was his gold and he wouldn't let her take it. He was so scared, she started to cry. The bear then told her that if she could prove she was smart, he would let her keep the gold. He tested her with all kinds of clever questions, and she answered them all. At last, the bear was convinced she was smart. He let her keep the gold and they both went away to their respective homes. The girl was so happy she could sleep soundly that night.

The year was hot, and summer was long. Anna was three. She was a brave and strong girl. Anna’s brother, Thomas, was very selfish. He never shared his toys and always grabbed the best ones for himself. Anna was sad but she didn’t know what to do. One day, Anna decided to stand up for herself. She said to Thomas, “It’s not fair for you to take all the best toys. I want some too.” Thomas was surprised. He had never seen Anna be brave before. Thomas was no longer selfish. He shared all his toys with Anna, and even offered to play together. From then on, Anna was proud of herself. She knew she had the courage to stand up for what was right.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a humble dog named Spot. Spot was very kind and always helped his friends. One day, his friend, a little bird named Tweet, had a task. Tweet needed to invite all her friends to her birthday party. Spot wanted to help Tweet with her task. He went to each of their friends' homes. He invited the cat, the frog, and the rabbit. They were all very happy to be invited to Tweet's party. On the day of the party, all the friends came. They played games, ate cake, and gave Tweet presents. Tweet was very happy that her humble friend, Spot, had helped her with her task. They all had a great time at the party, and they thanked Spot for being such a good friend.

Tim and Kim are twins. They like to play in the park. But today is a bad day. It is raining and cold. They cannot go to the park. "Mom, can we go to the park?" Tim asks. "No, sweetie, it is too wet and too cold. You will get sick," Mom says. "But we want to play on the swings and the slide," Kim says. "I know, honey, but you can play inside. I have some books and toys for you," Mom says. Tim and Kim are sad. They do not want to play inside. They want to go to the park. "Maybe the rain will stop soon," Tim says. "Maybe," Kim says. They look out the window. They see a big, gray cloud. They do not believe the rain will stop soon. They sigh and go to their room. They see a big box. It has a sticker that says "For Tim and Kim". "What is this?" Tim asks. "I don't know. Let's open it," Kim says. They open the box. They see a big tent, two sleeping bags, a flashlight, and some snacks. "Wow! This is awesome!" Tim says. "Look! Mom gave us a camping trip inside!" Kim says. They smile and hug Mom. They thank her for the surprise. They set up the tent in their room. They crawl inside the sleeping bags. They turn on the flashlight. They eat the snacks. They tell stories and jokes. They have fun. They forget about the rain. They do not care about the park. They have a good day. They believe Mom is the best.
