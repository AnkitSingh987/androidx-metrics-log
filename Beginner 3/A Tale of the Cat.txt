Category: Beginner
Title: A Tale of the Cat

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the yard. The cat was very hungry. The dog said, "I know where to find some meat. Let's go to the store!" The cat was very happy and they both went to the store. At the store, the dog took some meat and gave it to the cat. The cat said, "Thank you, dog!" They both ate the meat and felt guilty because they did not pay for it. Just then, a kind store worker saw them and said, "It's okay, cat and dog. I will pay for your meat." The cat and dog were surprised and happy. They thanked the store worker and went back to their yard to play.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big tree. The tree was not real. It was a fake tree! Tim was very surprised. He touched the tree and it felt funny. A bird came and sat on the tree. The bird said, "Hi, I am Bob. I live in this fake tree." Tim asked, "Why do you live in a fake tree?" Bob said, "I like it because it is safe and warm." Just then, the fake tree started to move! It was a robot tree! The tree walked and danced. Tim and Bob were happy. They clapped and applauded the robot tree. The robot tree became their new friend.

Once upon a time there was a weird elephant that lived in the jungle. The elephant had a funny way of talking that made everyone laugh. Every day he would call out to everyone passing by. One day, a monkey came by and they started talking. The elephant said, “Hi, monkey. How are you today?” The monkey was not happy. He said, “Why are you always so weird, elephant? Why can't you just be normal?” The elephant was sad, so he just hung his head down and did not say anything. Suddenly, all the animals of the jungle came together, and they talked to the elephant. They said, “You are special, elephant. Your weirdness is what makes you special.” The elephant felt better, and from then on, he kept calling to everyone who passed by. He was loved by all the animals of the jungle, and he would always have a friend. And they'd all have lots of fun.

Tom and Sam are brothers. They like to play in the yard. One day, they find a big hammer in the shed. Tom wants to use the hammer to make a house. Sam wants to use the hammer to hit a ball. "Give me the hammer, Tom!" Sam says. "No, it is mine!" Tom says. They pull the hammer from each other. They are angry. They do not share. Suddenly, they hear a loud noise. It is a dog. A big, black dog. The dog runs to them. It barks and shows its teeth. It wants to bite them. Tom and Sam are scared. They drop the hammer and run to the house. They scream for Mom. Mom comes out. She sees the dog. She is not scared. She knows the dog. It is Max, the neighbor's dog. He is friendly. He just wants to play. "Max, go home!" Mom says. Max wags his tail and runs away. He is not angry. He just wants to play. Mom hugs Tom and Sam. She is glad they are safe. She is not mad. She just wants them to share. "Are you okay, boys?" Mom says. "Yes, Mom," Tom and Sam say. They are sorry. They are sad. They hug each other. "Can we share the hammer, Sam?" Tom says. "Yes, Tom. We can share the hammer," Sam says. They smile. They are happy. They go back to the yard. They use the hammer to make a house and hit a ball. They play together. They are brothers.

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing in her yard. She saw a big giant standing near her house. The giant looked very sad. Lily was a little bit worry, but she wanted to help the giant. Lily went up to the giant and asked, "Why are you sad, Mr. Giant?" The giant said, "I lost my way home and now I can't find my family." Lily thought for a moment and said, "Don't worry, Mr. Giant. I will help you find your family." Lily and the giant walked and walked. They looked everywhere. Finally, they found the giant's family. The giant was so happy to see them. He said, "Thank you, Lily. You are a very nice girl." Lily smiled and said, "You're welcome, Mr. Giant. Now everything is fine. Have fun with your family!"
