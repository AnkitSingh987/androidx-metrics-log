Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Kitten

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a tray. She was very careful with her tray. She put her toys on the tray and carried them around the house. One day, Mia found a small, soft kitten. The kitten was tired and wanted to sleep. Mia thought the kitten could sleep on her tray. So, she put the kitten on the tray and carried it to her room. Mia made a cozy bed for the kitten on her tray. She was very careful not to wake the kitten. The kitten slept all day on the tray. When the kitten woke up, it was happy and played with Mia. They became best friends and had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a playful fish named Fin. He loved to dive deep in the water and play with his friends. One day, Fin saw a shiny stone at the bottom of the pond. He wanted to show it to his friends. Fin tried to dive down to get the stone, but he was scared. His friends saw him and wanted to help. They all started a search to find the stone for Fin. They looked under rocks and in the plants, but they could not find it. Finally, Fin was brave and decided to dive down himself. He swam deeper and deeper, and at last, he found the shiny stone. He was so happy! His friends cheered for him and they all played together with the stone. From that day on, Fin was never scared to dive deep again.

One day, a cat and a dog were in the kitchen. The cat saw some butter on the table. The dog saw a tight jar. The cat said, "I want the butter!" The dog said, "I want the jar open!" The cat tried to get the butter, but it was too high. The dog tried to open the jar, but it was too tight. They both felt sad. Then, they heard someone whistle. A little bird flew in through the window. The bird saw the cat and the dog. The bird said, "Why are you sad?" The cat said, "I can't get the butter." The dog said, "I can't open the jar." The bird had an idea. The bird flew up and pushed the butter off the table. The cat was happy! Then, the bird helped the dog open the tight jar. The dog was happy too! They all laughed and played together.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a cane in his grandpa's room. He picked it up and began to play with it. Tim liked to joke with his friends, so he thought of a funny way to use the cane. Tim went to his friend, Sarah, and pretended the cane was a magic wand. He said, "I can make you laugh with my magic cane!" He waved it and told a joke. Sarah laughed so hard, she fell on the ground. Tim felt happy that his joke made her laugh. But then, Tim's grandpa saw him playing with the cane. Grandpa was not happy. He said, "Tim, that cane is not a toy. I need it to walk." Tim felt ashamed for not asking before using it. He gave the cane back to his grandpa and said sorry. Tim learned to always ask before using other people's things.

Lily liked to play with her drum. She got it for her birthday from her grandma. She liked to make loud noises with it and sing along. She felt happy when she played with her drum. One day, she took her drum to the park with her mom. She wanted to show it to her friends. She saw Ben and Anna on the swings. She ran to them with her drum. She said hello and banged on her drum. But Ben and Anna did not say hello back. They looked at Lily with angry faces. They did not like the loud noise. They said, "Stop it, Lily! You are hurting our ears! Go away!" Lily felt sad and confused. She did not understand why her friends were mean to her. She liked her drum. Why did they not like it too? She looked at her mom for help. Her mom came to her and gave her a hug. She said, "Lily, I know you love your drum, but not everyone likes the same things. Sometimes, loud noises can make people feel uncomfortable. They can make their ears hurt or their heads ache. Maybe you can play with your drum somewhere else, where it does not bother anyone. Or maybe you can play with something else with your friends, like a ball or a doll. Would you like that?" Lily nodded. She recognized that her mom was right. She did not want to make her friends feel bad. She wanted to play with them and have fun. She said sorry to Ben and Anna and put her drum away. She asked them if they wanted to play with her ball. They smiled and said yes. They all ran to the grass and played together. Lily felt happy again. She still liked her drum, but she also liked her friends. She learned to be kind and respectful. She was a good girl.
