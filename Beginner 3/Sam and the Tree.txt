Category: Beginner
Title: Sam and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a little squirrel named Sam. Sam loved to find nuts and hide them in his small house. One day, Sam found a huge nut under a tall tree. He was very happy and wanted to take it home. Sam tried to push the big nut with his tiny paws, but it was too heavy. He thought about what to do. Then, he remembered his friend, Tina the snake. Tina was very flexible and could easily move things. So, Sam went to find Tina and asked for her help. Tina came to the tree and saw the big nut. She wrapped herself around the nut and started to push it with her strong body. With Sam and Tina working together, the nut began to move. They pushed it all the way to Sam's house. In the end, Sam was thankful to have a flexible friend like Tina to help him. They shared the nut and had a great feast. From that day on, Sam and Tina became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a big green field, there was a little red strawberry. The strawberry lived with her dependable friend, the sun. The sun was always there to help the strawberry grow big and sweet. One day, a big bird saw the strawberry and wanted to eat it. The bird flew down and said, "I will eat you, little strawberry!" The strawberry was scared and knew she had to escape. The dependable sun saw what was happening and hid behind a cloud. The field became dark, and the bird got scared. "Where did the sun go?" cried the bird. The sun came back out and said, "Leave my friend alone, or I will hide again!" The bird flew away, and the strawberry was safe. From that day on, the strawberry and the sun were the best of friends. They knew they could always count on each other to help when there was trouble. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a dog named Max went for a walk. Max saw a cat. The cat had a treat. Max wanted the treat. He said, "Can I have the treat, please?" The cat said, "You can have the treat if you can fit in this box." Max tried to fit in the box. He put his front legs in first. Then, he tried to put his back legs in too. But Max was too big. He did not fit in the box. Max felt sad. He said, "I can't fit in the box. I can't have the treat." The cat saw that Max was sad. The cat said, "I will give you the treat if you try to catch your tail." Max tried to catch his tail. He ran around and around. Max was so funny. The cat laughed and gave Max the treat. Max was happy. They became friends and played together.

Once upon a time, in a big room, there was a big scale. It could weigh anything. One day, a small boy named Tim came to play in the room. He saw the scale and wanted to weigh his toy car. He put the toy car on the scale and it showed the car was not heavy. Tim's big sister, Sue, had an exam that day. She was studying hard in her room. She was reading a big book and writing in her notebook. Tim wanted to help Sue with her exam. He thought if he could weigh her book, it would make her happy. Tim took Sue's book and put it on the scale. The scale showed the book was heavy. He ran to Sue and told her, "Your book is heavy, Sue! I weighed it for you!" Sue smiled and hugged Tim. She said, "Thank you, Tim. You made me happy." They both played with the toy car and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim had a clean room with many toys. His favorite toy was a small hammer. He loved to play with it all day. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came to play. They played with the hammer, but Sam wanted to play with other toys too. Tim did not want to share. The boys started to argue. "I want to play with the hammer!" said Sam. "No, it's mine!" said Tim. Tim's mom heard the noise and came into the room. She saw the boys fighting and said, "Boys, you need to learn to share. If you share, your fun will increase." Tim thought about it and decided to share his hammer with Sam. They played together and had a great time. From that day on, Tim learned to share and his friendships grew stronger.
