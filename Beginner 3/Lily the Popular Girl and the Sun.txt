Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Popular Girl and the Sun

One day, a popular girl named Lily went to the beach with her friends. They played in the water and had lots of fun. The sun was shining, and the sky was blue. They were very happy. Lily saw a jellyfish in the water. She wanted to touch it. Her friend Tom said, "No, don't touch the jellyfish! It can hurt you." But Lily didn't listen. She wanted to show her friends she was brave. Lily reached out to touch the jellyfish. It stung her hand, and she cried. Her friends helped her and took her to her mom. Her mom put something on her hand to make it feel better. Lily learned not to touch jellyfish, and she was still popular with her friends.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Mia wanted to make a great cake. She put all the things she needed in a big bowl. Mia mixed and mixed until it was ready. She was so happy and could not wait to eat the cake. Mia put the cake in the hot oven to cook. She went to play with her toys while she waited. But, Mia forgot about the cake! The cake stayed in the oven for a very long time. When Mia remembered the cake, she ran to the oven. She opened the oven door and saw the cake had started to melt! It was not a great cake anymore. Mia was sad, but then she had an idea. She took the melted cake and put it in a cup. She called it a "melted cake cup" and shared it with her friends. They all loved it and said it was the best thing they ever tasted. In the end, Mia was happy that her mistake made something new and yummy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play in the mud with her dog, Max. They would get very dirty, but they had so much fun. One day, after playing, Sue's mom said it was time to get clean. Sue and Max went to the bathroom. Sue's mom gave her some soap to spread on her hands. Max barked because he wanted some soap too. Sue spread the soap on Max's fur and they both got clean. Max was so happy, he wagged his tail. Later, Sue and Max went to the park. They saw a boy who was being rude. He would not share the toys with the other kids. Sue and Max went over to the boy and shared their toys with him. The boy learned it was better to be nice and share. Sue, Max, and the boy played together and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He liked to play with his toys. One day, he found a rare toy. It was a big, red car. Tim was very happy. Tim wanted to number his toys. He started with his rare car. He put a "1" on it. Then, he numbered his other toys. He used glue to stick the numbers on them. The glue made the numbers stay on the toys. Tim played with his toys all day. He liked his rare car the most. The glue on the numbers dried, and they did not fall off. Tim had lots of fun with his numbered toys. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big elephant named Ellie. Ellie had a long trunk and she loved to play with her friends. She wanted to be independent and do things by herself. One day, Ellie saw a tree and thought, "I can push that tree with my trunk!" So she walked up to the tree and pushed it very hard. The tree didn't move. Her friends saw her and said, "Good job, Ellie! You are so strong!" They all clapped their hands to applaud Ellie. But then, something unexpected happened. The tree started to fall down! Ellie and her friends were scared. They tried to run away, but the tree fell on Ellie's trunk. Ellie was hurt and her friends were sad. They all learned that being independent is good, but sometimes it's better to ask for help.
