Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Ben and the Flower

Sara and Ben are playing in the garden. They like to run and jump and laugh. They are healthy and happy. They see a big yellow flower. Sara wants to smell it. She springs to the flower and bends down. "Hello, flower," she says. "You are pretty and sunny. Do you have a name?" The flower does not answer. Sara is sad. She wants to talk to the flower. Ben comes to Sara. He has a telephone in his hand. It is a toy telephone. He gives it to Sara. "Here, Sara," he says. "You can use this telephone to talk to the flower. Maybe the flower will hear you." Sara smiles. She takes the telephone and puts it to her ear. She pretends to dial a number. "Hello, flower," she says again. "This is Sara. I like you very much. Do you like me?" She waits for a reply. She hears a buzzing sound. It is a bee. The bee is on the flower. The bee is angry. It thinks Sara is trying to take the flower. "Buzz off, Sara," the bee says. "This is my flower. Go away or I will sting you." Sara is scared. She drops the telephone and runs away. Ben runs after her. They go to their mother. She hugs them and kisses them. "Don't worry, Sara," she says. "The bee was just protecting the flower. The flower is not a real friend. It cannot talk to you. But you have a real friend. Ben is your real friend. He gave you the telephone. He is kind and nice. You should say thank you to him." Sara nods. She turns to Ben. She gives him a hug. "Thank you, Ben," she says. "You are my best friend. I love you." Ben smiles. He hugs her back. "I love you too, Sara," he says. "You are my best friend. Let's play some more." They go back to the garden. They find another game to play. They are happy. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big, great cat named Tom. Tom had a big sack. He liked to put fun things in his sack. One day, he found a small, red ball. He put the ball in his sack and smiled. Tom went to the park to play with his friend, Sam the dog. Sam saw the big sack and asked, "What's in your sack, Tom?" Tom smiled and said, "A fun ball to play with!" Sam was happy and wagged his tail. Tom and Sam played with the ball all day. They threw it, rolled it, and had a lot of fun. When it was time to go home, Tom put the ball back in his sack. They both smiled and said, "Today was a great day!"

Once upon a time, there was a lonely sunflower. It was sad because it had no friends. It stood in a big field all by itself. The sunflower wanted to bloom and be happy. One day, a little boy came to the field. He saw the lonely sunflower and felt sad for it. He thought of a way to make the sunflower happy. The little boy went away and came back with a bag of seeds. He planted the seeds all around the lonely sunflower. The sunflower felt happy and hopeful. As days went by, the seeds grew into beautiful flowers. The lonely sunflower was not lonely anymore. It bloomed with joy. The little boy came back to see the sunflower. He was happy to see it blooming and surrounded by friends. The sunflower and the boy became friends too. The moral of the story is that a little help and care can make someone's life beautiful and happy.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play with their scooters in the park. They have fun and go fast. One day, they see a big road near the park. The road has many cars and trucks. The cars and trucks are loud and fast. Tom and Mia think the road is dangerous. "Let's go back to the park," Mia says. "The road is not for scooters." But Tom is curious. He wants to see the road closer. He does not listen to Mia. He rides his scooter to the road. "Tom, stop!" Mia shouts. "The road is dangerous!" But Tom does not stop. He goes on the road. A car is coming. The car is very fast. The car honks. Tom is scared. He tries to turn his scooter, but he falls. Mia runs to Tom. She grabs his hand and pulls him off the road. The car passes by. It is very close. Tom and Mia are safe. "Tom, you are silly!" Mia says. "You could have been hurt! The road is dangerous!" "I'm sorry, Mia," Tom says. "You are right. The road is not for scooters. You are a good friend. You saved me." Tom and Mia hug. They go back to the park. They play with their scooters. They have fun and go fast. But they stay away from the road. The road is dangerous.
