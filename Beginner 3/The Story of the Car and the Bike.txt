Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Car and the Bike

Once upon a time, there was a little car with a big tire. The car was very filthy because it liked to play in the mud. One day, the car met a new friend, a small bike. "Hi, I am a bike. I have a small tire," said the bike. "I like to play in the mud too, but I can't manage to get as filthy as you." The little car laughed and said, "I can show you how to get really filthy, just follow me!" So, the car and the bike went to play in the mud together. While they were playing, something unexpected happened. The sky became very dark, and it started to rain. The rain washed away all the mud from the car and the bike. They were both clean again! The car and the bike were surprised, but they were happy to be clean. From that day on, they played in the mud and the rain together, always managing to have fun.

Lily and Tom are friends. They live on a farm with many animals. They like to play and help on the farm. One day, they see a big cake on the table. It smells good and looks yummy. They want to eat some cake. "Can we have some cake, please?" they ask Mom. "Not now, my dears. The cake is for later. We have guests coming soon. You can have some cake after dinner. Now go and play outside," Mom says. Lily and Tom go outside. But they still think about the cake. They are hungry and curious. They see a clever fox near the farm. The fox is hungry too. He sees the cake on the table. He wants to eat some cake. "Hello, little ones. Do you want to play a game with me?" the fox says. "What game?" Lily and Tom ask. "It's a fun game. I will tell you a secret. If you guess the secret, you can have some cake. If you don't guess the secret, I can have some cake. Do you want to play?" the fox says. Lily and Tom nod. They like games. They want to know the secret. They want to eat some cake. "OK, here is the secret. It is something that is big and round and sweet and has many colors. What is it?" the fox says. Lily and Tom think hard. They look around. They see the sun, the sky, the flowers, the fruits. But none of them is big and round and sweet and has many colors. They don't know the answer. They shake their heads. "Ha ha, you don't know the secret. The secret is the cake. The cake is big and round and sweet and has many colors. Now I can have some cake. Bye bye, little ones," the fox says. He runs to the table. He jumps on the table. He bites the cake. But Mom sees the fox. She is angry. She shouts at the fox. She chases the fox away. "Go away, you naughty fox. You can't eat our cake. That cake is for our guests. And for Lily and Tom. They are good children. They waited for the cake. They deserve some cake. Come here, my dears. You can have some cake now," Mom says. Lily and Tom are happy. They run to Mom. They hug Mom. They thank Mom. They eat some cake. The cake is delicious. They share some cake with the animals. The animals are happy too. They learn a lesson. They don't trust the clever fox. They listen to Mom. They enjoy the farm.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. He was very hungry. In the store, he saw a big cake. Tim asked his mom, "Can I have the cake, please?" His mom looked at the price and said, "No, it is too much money." Tim was sad, but he saw a small cake. He asked his mom, "Can I have this cake?" His mom looked at the price and said, "Yes, you can have it." Tim was very happy. When they got home, Tim's mom went to the kitchen. She opened the cake box and screamed, "Oh no!" Tim ran to see what happened. Inside the box, there was a toy car, not a cake! Tim was sad, but then he started to play with the toy car and forgot about being hungry.
