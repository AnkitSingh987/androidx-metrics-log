Category: Beginner
Title: The Tall Tree and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree. It was very big and strong. A little bird lived in the tree. The bird liked to play with the other birds. They liked to fly and sing songs. One day, the bird found a net. The net was on the ground. The bird had an idea. It wanted to replace its old nest with the net. The bird thought the net would be better. The bird and its friends worked together. They picked up the net and put it in the tree. The bird was happy. The net was a good new home. The bird and its friends played and sang in the tall tree.

One day, a little boy named Tim was scared to go to bed. He did not want to have a bad dream. His mom said, "Don't worry, I have a special trick for you." Mom took a small cup and pretended to pour a dream into it. She said, "Here is a happy dream. I will pour it into your head." She touched the cup to Tim's head and made a funny sound. That night, Tim went to sleep and had a very happy dream. He dreamed he was playing with big, talking toys. They were his friends. The toys told Tim, "We are here to make you happy, and you will never be scared again." Tim smiled and played with his new friends all night long.

One day, a dog and a cat wanted to play. They were eager to have fun. They thought of a race to see who was faster. The dog said to the cat, "I urge you to race me." The cat said, "Yes, let's race!" They went to a big field with lots of grass. They started the race. The dog ran fast, and the cat ran fast too. They both wanted to win. The dog and the cat ran and ran. In the end, the dog won the race. The cat was a little sad, but they both had fun. They played more games and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a rich man who loved to watch movies. He had a big room with a huge screen to watch his favorite movies. He was very happy watching movies all day long. One day, the rich man fell down and hurt his leg. He was sad because he could not walk to his movie room. His leg needed time to heal. He stayed in bed and wished for his leg to get better quickly. As the days passed, the rich man's leg began to heal. He was very happy when he could walk again. He went to his movie room and watched his favorite movies with a big smile on his face. The rich man learned to be careful and not to fall down again. He enjoyed his movies and lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to gather some pretty flowers for her mom. She went to the garden and saw many flowers. She picked some red ones, some yellow ones, and some blue ones. She put them all in a big open basket. Lily's mom was looking at a big calendar on the wall. She said, "Tomorrow is a special day. It's your dad's birthday!" Lily heard this and had an idea. She wanted to give the flowers to her dad for his birthday too. The next day, Lily woke up early. She took the open basket with the flowers she gathered and went to her dad. "Happy birthday, Dad!" she said. Her dad smiled and hugged her. "Thank you, Lily! These flowers are so beautiful, just like you!"

Once upon a time, there was a big, ancient bathtub. It was so old and special. The bathtub lived in a house with a little girl named Amy. One day, Amy went to take a bath. She looked at the ancient bathtub and said, "I guess it's time for my bath." She filled the bathtub with warm water and lots of bubbles. Amy loved to play with her toys in the bathtub. As Amy played in the ancient bathtub, she had lots of fun. She made her toys swim and jump in the water. Amy liked her old bathtub very much. After playing for a long time, she got out and said, "Thank you, ancient bathtub, for a fun bath." The ancient bathtub was happy to help Amy have a good time.
