Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Tough Boy and the Hill

One day, a tough boy named Tim had a toy jeep. He loved his jeep so much. It was his favorite toy. Tim would play with his jeep every day. He would tell his friends, "I own a jeep, and it's the best toy ever!" One day, Tim went to the park with his jeep. He met a girl named Sue. Sue saw Tim's jeep and said, "Wow! That's a cool jeep! Can I play with it?" Tim said, "Yes, but be careful. My jeep is very special to me." As they played, Tim saw a big hill. He told Sue, "I bet my jeep can go up that big hill." Sue said, "I don't know, it looks too big." But Tim was sure his jeep could do it. They pushed the jeep up the hill together. At the top of the hill, Tim said, "Now, let's see if my jeep can go down the hill fast!" Sue said, "Okay, let's try!" They let the jeep go, and it went down the hill very fast. It was so fun to watch. At the bottom of the hill, the jeep was still in one piece. Tim and Sue were happy. Tim said, "See, I told you my jeep was tough!" Sue said, "Yes, it is! Thank you for letting me play with your jeep." They played with the jeep until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a little train named Timmy. Timmy was a very available train. He always had time to help his friends. One day, Timmy was going down the tracks when he saw a little bird named Betty. Betty was sad because she lost her nest. "Please help me find my nest," cried Betty. Timmy wanted to help, so he let Betty hop on his back. They went down the tracks together, looking for the lost nest. Soon, they found the nest on a tree near the tracks. "Thank you, Timmy," said Betty. "You are a very good friend." Timmy was happy to help and went back to being an available train. From that day on, Timmy and Betty were the best of friends.

One day, a girl named Lucy found a map. The map showed her where to find a big tree with yummy fruits. Lucy wanted to get the fruits, so she took the map and went on her way. On her way, she met a boy named Tom. Tom asked, "What do you have there, Lucy?" Lucy said, "I have a map to find a big tree with yummy fruits. Do you want to come with me?" Tom said, "Yes! Let's go!" So, they both walked together, following the map. At last, they found the big tree. The tree had many fruits. Lucy and Tom picked the fruits. The tree gave, or yielded, a lot of fruits for them. They were very grateful and happy. They shared the fruits with their friends and everyone enjoyed them.

One day, a little fish named Tim swam fast in the sea. He liked to play with his friends. But today, Tim was alone. He started to worry. What if a big shark comes? He swam faster and faster. Then, Tim saw a big shadow. He thought it was a shark! Tim was very scared. He swam as fast as he could to hide. But the shadow followed him. Tim did not know what to do. But then, something unexpected happened. The big shadow came close to Tim. It was not a shark! It was a big, friendly whale. The whale said, "Don't worry, little fish. I will help you find your friends." Tim was so happy. He swam with the whale, and soon they found his friends. They all played together and had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a pale candle. It lived in a small house with a kind family. The family loved the candle very much. They used it to light up their home when it was dark outside. One day, the candle became very small. It did not give much light anymore. The family needed to replace the candle with a new one. They went to the store to find a new candle that was just as pale as the old one. They found a new pale candle at the store. The family was very happy. They brought the new candle home and put it where the old one used to be. Now, their home was bright and happy again. The family and the new pale candle lived happily ever after.
