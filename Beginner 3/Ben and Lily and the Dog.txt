Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Lily and the Dog

Ben and Lily are playing in the park. They have a bucket and a shovel. They want to make a big sandcastle. "Look, Ben, I found a shell!" Lily says. She puts the shell in the bucket. "Wow, that's pretty!" Ben says. He digs some more sand with the shovel. "We need more sand for our castle." They fill the bucket with sand and carry it to their spot. They dump the sand and pat it with their hands. They make a tower and a wall. "Let's make a moat!" Lily says. She runs to the fountain and fills the bucket with water. "Be careful, Lily, don't spill it!" Ben says. He follows her with his shovel. They are almost at their castle when they see a big dog. The dog is black and furry. It has a red collar. It looks at them with bright eyes. It barks. "Hello, doggy!" Lily says. She is not afraid of dogs. She likes them. "Arf, arf!" The dog says. It runs to them. It wants to play. "Uh-oh, Lily, watch out!" Ben says. He is alert. He sees the dog is very fast. It might knock them over. He drops his shovel and grabs Lily's hand. They move to the side. The dog speeds past them. It does not see the bucket. It hits the bucket with its nose. Splash! The water goes everywhere. The dog is wet. The bucket is empty. The sandcastle is safe. "Oops, sorry, doggy!" Lily says. She giggles. She thinks it is funny. "Wow, that was close!" Ben says. He breathes hard. He is glad they are okay. The dog shakes its head. It sneezes. It wags its tail. It licks Lily's face. "Ha, ha, ha!" Lily laughs. She pets the dog. She likes it. "Good doggy!" Ben says. He smiles. He likes it too. They hear a whistle. They look up. They see a man. The man is the dog's owner. He calls the dog. "Come here, Rex!" The man says. He waves his hand. "Bye, Rex!" Lily says. She waves back. "Bye, doggy!" Ben says. He picks up his shovel. The dog runs to the man. The man puts a leash on the dog. He says sorry to Ben and Lily. He says they have a nice sandcastle. He walks away with the dog. Ben and Lily go back to their sandcastle. They fill the bucket again. They make the moat. They are happy. They have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Toby. Toby was very tired because he played all day with his friends. He liked to run, jump, and bark with them. Toby knew it was time to go to sleep, but he didn't want to. He wanted to play more. Toby's mom gave him a small pill to help him sleep. She told him, "This pill will make you feel better and not so tired." Toby didn't like taking pills, but he trusted his mom. He took the pill and drank some water. Soon, Toby felt his eyes get heavy. He went to his own bed and lay down. As he closed his eyes, he thought about all the fun he had with his friends. Toby knew he needed to rest so he could play again tomorrow. And with that, Toby fell asleep, dreaming of more fun days to come.

Once upon a time there were two friends, John and Anne. They were wonderfully happy together. One day, John suggested that they should play a special game, so Anne was very excited. John said to Anne, "Let's read a novel!" Anne looked at John with wide eyes, as she didn't know what a novel was. John explained, "It's a type of book with lots of interesting stories. You'll love it!" Anne nodded eagerly and asked, "Where do we get one?" John suggested that they go to the library. Anne and John put on their shoes and went to the library. There were lots of novels to choose from, so they chose one that looked the most fun. They took the novel home, and soon they felt like they were in a different world! They felt so happy and they had a wonderful time reading together. Anne and John were happy they had made this exciting suggestion!
