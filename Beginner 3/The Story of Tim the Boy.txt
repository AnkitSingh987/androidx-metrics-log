Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of Tim the Boy

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim had a book to read and a snack to eat. He sat on a bench and started to read his book. Tim was very happy. A big, dead tree was near the bench. A squirrel lived in the tree. The squirrel saw Tim's snack and wanted some. The squirrel jumped down and said, "Can I have some snack, please?" Tim said, "No, this is my snack." The squirrel was sad and went back to the dead tree. It was very windy that day. The wind made the tree fall down. The tree fell on the bench where Tim was sitting. Tim's mom ran to help him but it was too late. The moral of the story is to always share with others, because you never know what can happen.

One day, a nosy cat named Tom found a big bathrobe. He thought it was a fun thing to play with. Tom liked to mind his own fun, but he was always looking for new things to do. Tom put on the bathrobe and it was very big on him. He walked around the house, feeling very happy. He showed his friend, a dog named Max. Max laughed and thought it was funny too. Tom and Max played in the bathrobe all day. They took turns wearing it and had a lot of fun. At the end of the day, they were tired but happy. They learned that sharing and trying new things can be fun.

Susie went shopping with her mommy. She was so excited. Susie saw a lovely wink in the store window. She asked her mommy to buy it for her. But mommy said no. "That Wink is too mighty for you, Susie," she said. Susie started to cry. She really wanted that Wink. But her mommy said no. Susie asked, "Please mommy, why can't I have it? It's so lovely." Her mommy replied, "The Wink is too mighty for you. It will take too much care." But Susie didn't give up. She wanted that Wink more than anything. "Please can I buy it?" she begged. Finally, her mommy agreed. She bought the Wink for Susie. She smiled as she held the wink in her arms. Susie was so happy. She ran around the store with it, showing it to everyone. Now she had her Wink, and it was the mightiest Wink ever.

Lily and Max are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a big green ball. They both want the ball. "I saw it first!" Lily says. She runs to the ball and touches it. "No, I saw it first!" Max says. He runs to the ball and tries to take it from Lily. They fight over the ball. They pull and push and shout. They are not happy. A lady sees them. She is the ball's owner. She walks to them and says, "Stop! This is my ball. You can't fight over it. That is not nice." Lily and Max stop fighting. They feel sorry. They say, "We are sorry, lady. We just wanted to play with the ball." The lady smiles. She says, "It's okay. You can play with the ball, but you have to share. Can you do that?" Lily and Max nod. They say, "Yes, we can share. Thank you, lady." The lady gives them the ball. They take turns to touch it and throw it and catch it. They play nicely. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a fireman named Tom. Tom was a very tidy fireman. He liked to keep his fire truck clean and his fire station neat. One day, Tom saw a little cat stuck in a tree. He knew he had to help the cat. Tom got ready to save the cat. He put on his fireman hat and climbed the big ladder. As he reached out to grab the cat, it suddenly disappeared! Tom was very confused. He looked around, but the cat was gone. Later that day, Tom found the cat playing in the fire station. It turned out that the cat was a magic cat! The magic cat had disappeared from the tree and went to the fire station to play. Tom and the magic cat became best friends, and they kept the fire station tidy together.
