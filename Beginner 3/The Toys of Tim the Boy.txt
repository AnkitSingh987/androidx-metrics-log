Category: Beginner
Title: The Toys of Tim the Boy

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim was very enthusiastic to play with his toys. He had a big bag full of cars, balls, and blocks. Tim's mom told him to play near the safe, so he could be close to her. Tim started to sort his toys. He put the cars in one pile, the balls in another pile, and the blocks in a third pile. While he was sorting, a small dog came up to him. The dog was friendly and wanted to play with Tim's toys too. Tim and the dog played together with the toys. They had so much fun that they didn't notice the piles of toys got mixed up again. When it was time to go home, Tim's mom came over and saw the toys all mixed up. She was surprised but happy to see Tim had made a new friend. They all laughed and went home together.

Once upon a time there was a brilliant computer. It was the smartest computer in the whole world and everybody wanted it. One day, a young boy came and demanded to have it. The computer was so impressed that someone so young wanted it. It decided to give the boy a chance to prove he was smart enough to own it. The boy answered all the tricky questions the computer asked. He answered them in a brilliant way. The computer was amazed and knew he was the perfect person to own it. So, the computer agreed to go home with the boy. From that day, the boy and the computer were the best of friends. They shared lots of stories and played games together. They had so much fun! The end!

Once upon a time, there was a little mole. The mole lived in a hole under a big tree. One day, the mole saw something yellow on the ground. It was a yellow leaf. The mole picked it up and showed it to his friend, the bird. The bird said, "That is a pretty leaf. I think something fun will happen with it." The mole smiled and kept the yellow leaf. Later, the mole felt cold. He started to tremble. He remembered the yellow leaf and wrapped it around himself like a blanket. The mole felt warm and stopped trembling. The bird saw the mole and said, "I knew the yellow leaf would help you. Now you are warm and happy." The mole thanked the bird and they played together, with the yellow leaf keeping the mole warm.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly little girl named Amy. Amy loved to play with her shapes. She had a box full of different shapes like circles, squares, and triangles. One day, Amy's friend, Tim, came over to play. "Can I play with your shapes?" Tim asked. "Yes, you can," said Amy with a smile. They both played with the shapes and had lots of fun. While playing, Tim found a new shape that didn't belong to Amy. It was a star. "Where did this star come from?" Tim asked. Amy didn't know, but they decided to find the owner of the star. They went to their friend, Lily's house and asked, "Does this star belong to you?" Lily said, "No, but it's very pretty!" Amy and Tim kept asking their friends, but no one knew who the star belonged to. Finally, they found a little boy named Max, who was sad because he lost his star. "Is this your star?" Amy asked. Max's face lit up, and he said, "Yes, it is! Thank you for finding it!" Amy and Tim felt happy for helping Max find his star. They learned that it's good to help others and return things that don't belong to them. And from that day on, they always tried to help their friends whenever they could.

Tom and Jen are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they find a big box. They open the box. Inside, there are many toys. They are happy. But some toys are old and bad. They have holes and dirt. They smell bad. They are rot toys. Tom and Jen do not like rot toys. They throw them away. One toy is not rot. It is a bear. It is soft and brown. It has a red bow. It has a name. The name is Ted. Tom and Jen like Ted. They hug him and take him home. It is dark now. Tom and Jen are in bed. They have Ted with them. They are not afraid of the dark. They have a friend. They say good night to Ted. They close their eyes. They sleep.
