Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Garden and the Bug

Once upon a time, there was a big, ugly bug. The bug lived in a garden. The garden had many plants. One day, the bug saw a red radish. The bug liked the radish. The bug wanted the radish. So, the bug tried to stir it. The bug tried hard, but the radish was too big. The bug was sad. The bug wanted help. A tiny ant saw the bug. The ant wanted to help. The ant and the bug stirred the radish together. They got the radish out. The bug and the ant were happy. They shared the red radish and became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Tim. Tim was a scared cat. He did not like to go outside. He liked to stay in the house with his friend, a girl named Sue. One day, Sue was playing with a cord. Tim saw the cord and wanted to play too. They played and played, having so much fun. Tim was not scared when he played with Sue and the cord. After they played, Tim was not so scared anymore. He learned that it was okay to stay with Sue and play. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there were two best friends, True and Wall. True was a white rabbit who loved to fold everything. Wall was a green frog who was jealous of True for being able to fold anything so easily. One day, Wall said to True, "Show me how you can fold that piece of paper so fast". True answered, "It's easy. You just have to focus and practice." Wall thought it was impossible and said, "No way! It's too hard". True said, "Maybe if you practice, you'll be able to do it too", but Wall refused to listen. He just stayed jealous of True for being able to fold things so easily. True felt sad and said, "I'm sorry you're so jealous, but you can do it if you try hard enough." Wall nodded and said, "You're right. I'll try". So True showed Wall how to fold the paper, and with time and practice, Wall was finally able to fold the paper as easily as True. They both celebrated and were happy to have found a way to be the best of friends.

Anna and Ben were playing with their toys in the living room. Anna had a pink doll that she loved very much. Ben had a blue car that he liked to zoom around. They were having fun until Ben saw Anna's doll and wanted to play with it. "Give me your doll!" Ben said, grabbing the doll from Anna's hands. "No, it's mine!" Anna said, pulling the doll back. "You have your car!" "But I want the doll too!" Ben said, tugging harder. "It's pink and pretty!" Anna and Ben started to fight over the doll. They shouted and cried and made a lot of noise. Their mom heard them and came to see what was wrong. "What are you doing?" Mom asked, looking at the mess. "Why are you fighting?" "Ben took my doll!" Anna said, pointing at Ben. "Anna won't share her doll!" Ben said, pointing at Anna. Mom sighed and picked up the doll and the car. She held them in her hands and looked at Anna and Ben. "Listen, you two," Mom said. "You have to learn to share and play nicely. Fighting is not good. It makes you and others unhappy. How would you feel if someone took your toy without asking?" Anna and Ben thought for a moment. They felt sorry for being mean to each other. "I would feel sad," Anna said. "I would feel angry," Ben said. "Exactly," Mom said. "So, how can you solve this problem?" Anna and Ben looked at each other. They had an idea. "Maybe we can take turns," Anna said. "Or we can play together," Ben said. "That sounds good," Mom said. "Why don't you try that?" Anna and Ben nodded. They smiled and hugged each other. They said sorry and thanked Mom. Mom gave them back their toys and watched them play. Anna and Ben decided to settle their fight. They shared the doll and the car. They made up stories and had fun. They learned that sharing was better than fighting. They also learned that pink was not a stupid color. It was a nice color that they both liked.
