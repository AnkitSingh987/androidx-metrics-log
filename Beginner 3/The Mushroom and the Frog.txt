Category: Beginner
Title: The Mushroom and the Frog

Once upon a time, there was a big mushroom. The mushroom was very sad. It was difficult for the mushroom to find a friend. One day, a nice frog came to the mushroom. The frog said, "Hi, mushroom! Will you marry me?" The mushroom was happy. "Yes, I will marry you!" said the mushroom. But, the sky turned dark. A big rain came. The rain was too much for the mushroom. The mushroom went away. The frog was sad. The end.

One day, a little fish named Bob was swimming in the dark sea. He saw a jellyfish and said, "Hi, jellyfish! Do you want to play with me?" The jellyfish said, "Yes, let's play together." As they played, they saw a big rock. Bob had an idea. "Let's play hide and seek! I will hide first, and you find me." The jellyfish agreed and started to count. Bob swam fast and hid behind the big rock. He saw a hole in the rock and went inside to hide. While the jellyfish was looking for Bob, it started to rain. The rain made the sea water soak the rock. Suddenly, the big rock moved! It was not a rock, but a big turtle! The turtle said, "Hello, little fish! I am Tom, the turtle. I was sleeping, but the rain woke me up." Bob was surprised, but he made a new friend. The jellyfish found Bob and they all played together. They had a fun day in the dark sea, even when it rained.

One day, a little mole went out to play. He was a tired mole, but he wanted to have fun. He saw a big red truck. The truck had many things to load. The mole said to the truck, "Can I help you load the things?" The truck said, "Yes, little mole. You can help me." The mole was happy to help. They started to load the things together. After some time, the mole and the truck were done. The tired mole said, "Thank you, truck, for letting me help you." The truck said, "Thank you, little mole, for helping me." They both smiled and the tired mole went back to his home to rest.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a modern cat named Max. Max was a very clean cat. Every day, Max would wash his face and ears with his paws. One sunny day, Max went outside to play. He saw a big puddle and jumped in it. Max had so much fun playing in the water. But when he was done, his ears were all wet. Max knew he needed to dry his ears. He went back to his house and used a soft towel to dry them. Max felt much better with clean and dry ears. Max's friend, a little dog named Sam, saw Max drying his ears. Sam also had wet ears from playing in the puddle. Max showed Sam how to dry his ears too. Sam felt much better and thanked Max for helping him. The moral of the story is to always help your friends and keep yourself clean.

Once there was a curious little girl called Alice. One day, Alice heard something strange. She stopped and listened. “What was that?” she wondered. Alice looked around and saw a pill. It was ice blue and quietly spinning in the air. Alice was very curious. She wanted to know what it was. She looked around and saw a wise old owl. “Excuse me Mr. Owl, can you tell me what this pill is?” asked Alice. The owl nodded his head slowly. “Listen close, young one. This is a magic pill. It will answer any question you have,” said the owl. Alice thanked the owl and held the pill. She closed her eyes and asked a question. “What should I do today?” she said. The pill replied in a soft voice, “Go for a walk and explore the world around you!” Alice smiled, thanked the pill and the owl and went outside to explore. She was excited to see what was out there.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Bob. Bob lived in a small hole near a big field of grass. He loved to hop and play all day long. One sunny day, Bob met a new friend, a tiny bird named Tim. Tim was sad because he needed to find some grass to make his nest, but he was too small to carry it. "I need help," said Tim. "The grass is tough and I can't carry it to my tree." Bob wanted to help his new friend. He hopped over to the grass and picked up some with his teeth. "I can help you," said Bob. Together, they carried the tough grass to Tim's tree. They worked hard all day and soon the nest was finished. Tim was so happy that Bob helped him. They played and laughed together in the grass. From that day on, Bob and Tim were the best of friends, and they always helped each other when they needed it.
