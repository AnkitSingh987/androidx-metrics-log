Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Orange

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had an orange ball that he loved to play with. One day, he went to the park with his mom to play with his ball. At the park, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue had a shiny coin that she found on the ground. Tim and Sue became friends and played together. They took turns throwing the orange ball and showing each other their special things. "Look at my orange ball!" said Tim. "It's so much fun to play with!" Sue smiled and said, "That's cool! Look at my shiny coin. I found it on the ground." Tim and Sue played until the sun went down, and they went home with their mom and dad. They were happy to have made a new friend.

One day, a girl named Sue found some paint. She wanted to paint her hand. So, she put her hand in the paint. Her hand was all blue now. She was very happy. Then, her mom came in the room. She saw Sue's blue hand. "Sue, what did you do?" her mom asked. Sue felt guilty. She knew she should not have played with the paint. Sue's mom was not mad. She had an idea. "Let's put your blue hand on a paper," she said. Sue did it, and it made a pretty handprint. They both laughed and had fun making more handprints.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a tall tree. The tree was very old and strong. All the animals loved the tree because it gave them shade and a place to play. One day, a big wind came and tried to destroy the tree. The wind blew and blew, but the tree did not fall. It was difficult for the wind to destroy the tree because it was so strong. A little bird who lived in the tree said, "Don't destroy our tree! We love our tree and it's our home!" The wind listened to the bird and stopped blowing. The tree was safe, and all the animals were happy. They played under the tree and thanked the little bird for saving their home.

Anna and Ben were playing in the park with their mom. They liked to slide, swing and run. They also had a bottle of juice to share when they were thirsty. "Can I have some juice, please?" Anna asked Ben, who was holding the bottle. "OK, but be careful. Don't spill it," Ben said, and gave the bottle to Anna. Anna took a sip of juice and then put the bottle on the ground. She saw a butterfly and ran after it. She forgot about the bottle. Ben saw a dog and wanted to pet it. He ran after the dog and also forgot about the bottle. A careless boy was riding his bike in the park. He did not look where he was going. He bumped into the bottle and knocked it over. The juice spilled on the grass. "Hey, that's our juice!" Ben shouted, when he saw what happened. He ran back to the bottle and picked it up. It was empty. "Sorry, I didn't see it," the boy said, and rode away. Anna came back from chasing the butterfly. She saw Ben holding the empty bottle and looking sad. "Where is the juice?" she asked. "A boy on a bike spilled it. He was careless," Ben said. "Oh no, that's not fair. I'm thirsty," Anna said. "Me too," Ben said. They looked at their mom, who was sitting on a bench. She saw their faces and smiled. "Don't worry, I have another bottle in my bag. Come here, and I'll give you some," she said. Anna and Ben ran to their mom and hugged her. She gave them the bottle and they drank some juice. They thanked her and felt better. They learned to be more careful with their things and to watch out for careless people. They also learned that their mom always had a backup plan. They continued to play and have fun in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a nice dog named Buddy. Buddy liked to play outside in the yard. One day, he saw a big gate. Buddy wanted to go through the gate and play on the other side. But, there was a problem. The gate was closed. Buddy did not know how to open it. He tried to push it and he tried to jump over it. But, he could not do it. Buddy was sad. Then, a friendly cat came along. The cat saw Buddy and wanted to help. The cat showed Buddy how to accept help from others. Together, they opened the gate. Buddy was so happy. Now, he could play on the other side of the gate with his new friend, the cat.
