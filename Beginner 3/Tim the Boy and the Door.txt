Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Door

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing outside. He loved to imagine he was driving a large car. He would sit on the ground and make car noises as he moved his hands like he was steering a car. Tim had a friend named Sam who lived next door. Sam liked to imagine he was driving a large car too. They would play together, racing their pretend cars up and down the street. They laughed and had a lot of fun. But one day, Tim and Sam were playing near the real road. They were imagining they were driving their large cars very fast. They did not see the real car coming. The car could not stop in time. Tim and Sam got hurt. It was a bad ending to their fun day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a new shop. This shop had lots of fruit. A big sign said, "Yummy Fruit Shop." Tom, a little boy, saw the sign and wanted to go in. He asked his mom if they could go inside. His mom said, "Yes, we can look for fruit." In the shop, Tom and his mom saw many fruits. They saw red apples, round oranges, and long bananas. Tom wanted to get a big, green fruit. He thought it was a new kind of apple. His mom said, "Okay, we can try it." At home, Tom and his mom cut the green fruit. It looked different inside. It was not an apple! It was a big, green orange. Tom and his mom looked at each other and laughed. They tried the green orange and it was very yummy. They were happy to find a new fruit.

Tom and Anna are friends. They like to play with toys and books. One day, they find a big file in the closet. The file has many papers with words and pictures. "What is this?" Tom asks. "I don't know. Maybe it is Mom's or Dad's. Let's look inside," Anna says. They open the file and see many papers. Some papers have words and numbers. Some papers have drawings and colors. Some papers have stickers and stamps. "Wow, this is fun! Look, I found a paper with a cat. And this one has a star. And this one has a smiley face," Anna says. "Me too! I found a paper with a dog. And this one has a moon. And this one has a heart," Tom says. They take out the papers and make a big pile on the floor. They sort them by shapes and colors. They stick them on the wall and the door. They make a collage and a banner. They are very happy. But then, they hear a noise. It is Mom and Dad. They are coming home. "Oh no! We have to hide the file. Mom and Dad will be angry. They will think we made a mess," Tom says. "Where can we hide it? There is no time. They are here," Anna says. They panic. They try to put the papers back in the file. But they are too many. They fall out of the file. They scatter on the floor. They are everywhere. Mom and Dad open the door. They see the papers. They see Tom and Anna. They are surprised. "What is this? What have you done?" Mom asks. Tom and Anna are scared. They are sorry. They did not mean to make trouble. They just wanted to have fun. "We are sorry, Mom. We are sorry, Dad. We found a file in the closet. We looked inside. We liked the papers. We played with them. We did not know they were important. Please don't be mad," Tom says. Mom and Dad look at the papers. They look at Tom and Anna. They are not angry. They are amused. They understand. "It's okay, Tom. It's okay, Anna. We are not mad. We are surprised. You are very creative. You made a beautiful art. But this file is not for playing. It is for work. It has important papers. We need them. Next time, please ask us before you touch our things. And please clean up after you play," Dad says. Tom and Anna nod. They are relieved. They are happy. They hug Mom and Dad. They say sorry again. They help to pick up the papers. They put them back in the file. They put the file in the closet. They learn a lesson. They have a surprise.
