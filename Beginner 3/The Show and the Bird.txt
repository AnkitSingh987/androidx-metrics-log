Category: Beginner
Title: The Show and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big show in town. Many people came to see it. There was a big stage where all the fun things happened. Everyone was happy and excited. One day, a man entered the stage to do a trick. He had a big box. He said, "I will make something dead come to life!" The people were scared, but they wanted to see the trick. The man opened the box and pulled out a dead bird. The people gasped. The man waved his hands and said magic words. Suddenly, the dead bird came to life! It flew around the stage and the people clapped. But then, the bird turned into a big, beautiful butterfly! It was not a dead bird at all! The people were surprised and happy. They knew it was a fun trick and not scary at all. The big show was a big hit and everyone went home smiling.

One day, a big whale was in the sea. The whale liked to swim very fast. He would speed through the water and make big waves. The whale was loud when he played, and all the fish could hear him. A little fish saw the whale and wanted to play too. The little fish tried to speed like the whale, but he was too small. The whale saw the little fish and said, "You can't play with me. You are too small." The little fish was sad, but then he had an idea. He asked all his small fish friends to join him. They all swam together and made a big fish. The big fish could speed like the whale and was loud too. The whale saw the big fish and wanted to play with them. The big fish and the whale played together and had fun. They learned that even small fish can be big and fast when they work together. The whale was happy to have new friends, and they all played in the sea every day.

One day, a little girl named Sue was walking in her garden. She saw a rough tree with some prunes on it. Sue wanted to pick the prunes, but she was too small to reach them. Sue saw a big bird, and she asked, "Can you help me get the prunes, please?" The big bird said, "Yes, I can help you!" The bird flew up and picked the prunes for Sue. Sue was happy and did not worry anymore. She took the prunes to her mom. They made yummy food together. Sue and her mom had a great day.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play outside. He had a big box of chalk. Tim loved to draw with chalk on the ground. His hands were always messy after playing with chalk. Tim drew a big sun and a pretty flower. He saw a bug on the ground. Tim wanted to pick it up, but when he tried, the bug pinched him. "Ouch!" said Tim. He was sad and sat down. Then, something unexpected happened. The bug took a piece of chalk and started to draw. It drew a big heart and a smiley face. Tim was so surprised! He laughed and clapped his hands. The bug and Tim became friends, and they drew messy pictures together all day.

Anna and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to run, slide and swing. They saw a big hill with many trees. They wanted to see what was on the top. "Let's go up the hill!" Anna said. "OK, but be careful. It might be steep." Ben said. They climbed the hill, holding hands. They saw many flowers, bugs and birds. They were happy and curious. They reached the top and looked around. "Wow, look at that!" Anna said, pointing at something weird. "What is it?" Ben asked. They saw a big metal thing with a round window and a long tail. It looked like a rocket, but it was old and rusty. It had some letters on it, but they could not read them. "Maybe it is a toy from a long time ago." Anna said. "Maybe it is a spaceship from another place." Ben said. They wanted to discover more. They went closer to the metal thing. They saw a door with a handle. They pulled the handle and opened the door. They saw a dark and dusty inside. They saw some buttons, wires and screens. "Wow, this is cool!" Anna said. "Let's go in and explore!" Ben said. They went in and closed the door behind them. They did not know that the metal thing was not a toy or a spaceship. It was a bomb from a war that never happened. They did not know that they had just activated it. They did not know that they had only ten seconds left.
