Category: Beginner
Title: The Ranch and the Horse

Once upon a time, there was a ranch. On this ranch, there were many animals. There was a big horse, a small cat, and a helpful dog. They were friends and liked to play together. One day, the horse felt helpless. He was very dirty and needed a wash. The horse said, "I need help. Can you wash me, please?" The helpful dog and the small cat wanted to help their friend. The dog and the cat worked together to wash the big horse. They used water and soap to make him clean. The horse was very happy. He said, "Thank you, friends. You are the best!" They all played together on the clean ranch, and they were all happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a noisy dog named Ben. Ben loved to play with his ball. One day, Ben saw a big tear in his ball, and he was sad. Ben went to his friend, Cat, and said, "Cat, can you help me fix my ball? There is a big tear in it." Cat thought for a turn and said, "Yes, Ben, I can help you. Let's go to my house." At Cat's house, they found a special tape to fix the tear in Ben's ball. As they fixed it, they heard a loud noise outside. They went to see what it was, and they found a big truck with a lot of new balls. The truck driver said, "I have too many balls, and I want to give some away. Would you like one?" Ben and Cat were very happy, and they said, "Yes, please!" They took the new balls and played together all day. Ben learned that even when things look bad, good things can happen if you ask for help and have good friends. The moral of the story is: always help your friends, and good things will come to you.

Lila and Tom are friends. They like to play with toys. Today they have a new toy. It is a penguin. The penguin is black and white. It has a beak and flippers. It is soft and cute. Lila and Tom take turns to hug the penguin. They make noises like a penguin. They pretend the penguin can swim and slide on the ice. They have fun with the penguin. But then Tom spills his juice on the penguin. The penguin is wet and sticky. It is not clean anymore. Lila is sad. She does not want to play with the penguin. She says to Tom: "Lila: You are bad. You made the penguin dirty. I do not like you." Tom is sorry. He does not want to make Lila sad. He says to Lila: "Tom: I am sorry. I did not mean to spill the juice. I like you. I like the penguin. Can we clean the penguin?" Lila thinks for a moment. She is still sad, but she likes Tom. She likes the penguin. She says to Tom: "Lila: OK. We can clean the penguin. But you have to be careful. You have to be gentle. The penguin is not a real penguin. It is a toy." Tom nods. He is happy. He says to Lila: "Tom: OK. I will be careful. I will be gentle. The penguin is a toy. But it is a nice toy. It is our toy." Lila and Tom go to the sink. They wash the penguin with water and soap. They dry the penguin with a towel. The penguin is clean again. It is soft and cute. Lila and Tom hug the penguin. They make noises like a penguin. They are friends again. They are happy.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a shy cat named Fluffy. Fluffy had soft fur and loved to hide from people. One day, Fluffy saw a bottle of shampoo on the floor. She sniffed it and thought it smelled nice. Fluffy's friend, a dog named Spot, came into the room. Spot saw the shampoo and said, "I don't like shampoo. It makes me wet and cold." Fluffy looked at Spot and said, "I disagree. Shampoo makes my fur soft and clean." Spot thought for a moment and then said, "Okay, let's try it together." Fluffy and Spot took turns using the shampoo. They splashed and played in the water. After they were done, they both had soft, clean fur. They laughed and agreed that shampoo was not so bad after all.
