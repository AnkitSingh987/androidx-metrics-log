Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Sleepy Cat and the Mouse

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy cat named Tom. Tom lived in a house with a big freezer. One day, Tom wanted to perform a dance for his friend, a small mouse named Jerry. Tom said to Jerry, "I will perform a dance for you. Please watch me." Jerry was happy and sat down to watch Tom dance. Tom danced and danced, and Jerry clapped his hands. After the dance, Tom felt very tired. He wanted to take a nap. He saw the freezer and thought it would be a good place to sleep. Tom opened the freezer and went inside. Jerry saw Tom and said, "No, Tom! The freezer is too cold! Come out!" Tom heard Jerry and came out of the freezer. They both laughed and went to find a warm place to sleep.

Once upon a time, there was a smelly cat named Tom. Tom lived in a small house with a girl named Lily. Lily loved to read her novel every day. Tom wanted to go outside and play, but Lily was always busy reading. One day, Tom saw a big hole in the wall. He thought, "I can escape through the hole and play outside!" So, he squeezed through the hole and went outside. Tom played all day, running and jumping in the grass. As the sun began to set, Tom started to feel lonely. He missed Lily and her novel. He went back to the hole and called out, "Lily, I'm sorry I left. Can I come back inside?" Lily heard Tom and said, "Of course, Tom! I missed you too." Tom went back inside, and they both snuggled up and read the novel together.

Once upon a time, in a busy little town, there lived a happy cat named Tom. Tom loved to play all day and spoil his toys. He liked to chase the light that came in through the window. Tom would jump and dance as he tried to catch the light. He was always full of joy. One sunny day, Tom saw a new light on the wall. This light was different. It was small and round. Tom thought it was a new toy to spoil. He jumped and tried to catch it, but the light moved away. Tom was surprised but excited to play with this new light. As Tom chased the light, he found out that it was not a toy, but a little bug. The bug was busy flying around the room, and Tom was happy to have a new friend. They played together all day, and Tom didn't spoil the bug. In the end, Tom learned that not all things were meant to be spoiled, and he had a new friend to play with.

Once upon a time, there was a fox. The fox liked to play. He had many friends. They would run and jump all day. The fox enjoyed playing with his friends. One day, the fox met a new friend. The new friend was a bird. The bird said, "Hi, fox! I need help. Can you help me find my nest? It is important." The fox said, "Yes, I will help you." The fox and the bird looked for the nest. They found it in a tree. The bird was happy. The fox was happy too. They enjoyed helping each other. Now, the fox and the bird are best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse named Max. Max lived in a small house with his mom and dad. Max loved to play and run all day long. One day, Max saw a big piece of cheese in the kitchen. He was hungry and wanted to eat the cheese. As Max got close to the cheese, he saw a trap next to it. Max was scared, but he was also very smart. He paused and thought of a plan to get the cheese without getting caught in the trap. Max was ready to try his plan. Max found a long stick and used it to push the cheese away from the trap. He was very careful and slow. When he got the cheese, he ran back to his mom and dad. Max was so happy he could eat the cheese without getting caught in the trap. His mom and dad were proud of him for being so smart and brave.
