Category: Beginner
Title: Sam and Tom and the Flower

Sam and Tom were friends who liked to play with toy guns. They pretended to be soldiers and spies and had fun shooting at each other. One day, they found a big building with a lot of statues and paintings. It was a temple where people prayed and learned about God. "Wow, this is cool!" Sam said. "Let's go inside and explore!" "OK, but be careful," Tom said. "We don't want to break anything or make noise." They went inside the temple and saw many people sitting quietly and listening to a man who wore a long robe. He was talking about love and peace and kindness. Sam and Tom did not understand much, but they felt a warm and friendly feeling in the temple. They also saw a big statue of a man with a smile and a halo. He was holding a flower and a book. He looked very nice and gentle. "Who is that?" Sam asked. "I don't know," Tom said. "Maybe he is God or a saint." Sam had an idea. He took out his toy gun and aimed at the statue. "Bang! Bang! I shoot you!" he shouted. Tom was shocked. He grabbed Sam's arm and pulled him away. "Stop it, Sam! That's not nice! You can't shoot at God or a saint! That's very bad and rude!" Sam did not understand. He thought it was just a game. He did not mean to hurt anyone. "But it's just a toy gun," he said. "It doesn't do anything." Tom shook his head. "It doesn't matter. It's still wrong. You have to respect the temple and the people and the statues. They are here to teach us good things, not to play with. You have to say sorry and ask for forgiveness." Sam felt sorry. He realized he made a mistake. He followed Tom to the man in the long robe and bowed his head. "Excuse me, sir," he said. "I'm sorry I shot at the statue. I didn't know it was God or a saint. I was just playing. Please forgive me." The man in the long robe smiled and touched Sam's head. "It's OK, my child," he said. "I forgive you. You are young and curious. But you have to learn to be more careful and respectful. The temple is a sacred place where we honor God and his friends. The statue is a symbol of his love and wisdom. You can look at it and learn from it, but you can't shoot at it. That's not friendly or polite. Do you understand?" Sam nodded. "Yes, sir. I understand. Thank you for teaching me." The man in the long robe hugged Sam and Tom. "You're welcome, my children. You are always welcome here. God loves you and wants you to be happy and good. Remember to be friendly and kind to everyone and everything. That's the best way to play and live." Sam and Tom smiled and thanked the man in the long robe. They left the temple feeling happy and good. They learned a valuable lesson that day. They decided to play with their toy guns in a different way. They pretended to be heroes and helpers and had fun shooting at bad guys and monsters. They never shot at God or his friends again. They respected the temple and the people and the statues. They were friendly and kind to everyone and everything.

Once upon a time, there was a messy room. In this room, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toys. One day, Tim's mom came into the room. She saw the messy room and said, "Tim, you need to polish your room." Tim did not want to polish his room. He wanted to play outside. The wind was blowing, and it was a nice day. So Tim went outside to play instead of polishing his room. He played with his kite in the wind. It went up, up, up in the sky. While Tim was playing, his mom went into his room. She saw that he did not polish his room like she asked. She was not happy. When Tim came back inside, his mom said, "Tim, you did not polish your room. Now you cannot play outside tomorrow." Tim was sad, but he knew he should have polished his room.
