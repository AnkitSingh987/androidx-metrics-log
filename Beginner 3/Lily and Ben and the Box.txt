Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Box

Lily and Ben were best friends. They liked to play together in the park. One day, they found a big box under a tree. They opened the box and saw many clothes inside. "Look, Lily, a blouse!" Ben said. He took out a pink blouse with flowers on it. "Do you want to try it on?" "Yes, please!" Lily said. She put on the blouse over her dress. It was too big for her, but she liked it. "How do I look?" "You look pretty, Lily!" Ben said. He smiled and took out a hat. He put it on his head. "And I look handsome!" They laughed and pretended to be grown-ups. They walked around the park and talked. They saw a bench with some flowers on it. They sat on the bench and held hands. "Let's get married, Lily!" Ben said. He gave her a flower. "OK, Ben!" Lily said. She gave him a kiss. They were very happy. They did not see the man who was looking for his box. He was angry and frightened. He ran towards them and shouted. "Hey, you kids! That's my box! Give me back my clothes!" Lily and Ben were scared. They jumped off the bench and ran away. They left the blouse and the hat behind. They did not want to get married anymore. They just wanted to go home.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim wore his favorite shirt. It was red and very comfortable. At the park, they saw a duck by the pond. The duck was hungry. Tim's mom said, "Tim, would you like to feed the duck?" Tim nodded and smiled. His mom gave him some bread to give to the duck. Tim walked to the duck and threw the bread in the water. The duck was happy and ate the bread. After feeding the duck, Tim played on the swings and slides. He had so much fun in the park with his mom. When it was time to go home, Tim and his mom waved goodbye to the duck. The duck quacked back, saying goodbye too. Tim felt happy and couldn't wait to come back to the park again.

One day, a small cat named Kitty was very hungry. She went outside to look for some food. Kitty saw a big tree with lots of birds. She thought, "I can catch a bird to eat!" Kitty found a stick on the ground. She said, "This is my weapon to get the bird." She held the stick in her mouth and started to climb the tree. The birds saw Kitty coming and they were scared. As Kitty got closer to the birds, they all flew away. But one little bird was too slow. Kitty caught the bird with her weapon. The bird said, "Please don't eat me, I can help you find food." Kitty let the bird go and they became friends. The bird showed Kitty where to find lots of food and they were both happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his blocks. He would build big towers and small houses with them. Tim's mom always told him to stay quiet when he played, so he would not wake up his baby sister. One sunny day, Tim and his friend, Sam, played with the blocks outside. They built a big tower, but they did not stay quiet. They laughed and shouted as they played. Tim's mom called out to them, "Stay quiet, or the tower will fall!" But Tim and Sam did not listen. They kept being loud and having fun. Suddenly, a big wind came and knocked the tower down. The blocks went everywhere, and the boys were sad. The moral of the story is: always listen to your mom and stay quiet when she tells you to. If Tim and Sam had stayed quiet, their tower might not have fallen down.

Once upon a time there were two friends: Bill and Sarah. Bill had a towel and Sarah new it was very special. "What's so crazy about this towel?" asked Sarah pointing to Bill's towel. "Oh, it's special because if I wish for something and hold it tight, it might come true!" said Bill excitedly. Sarah looked amazed. "Really? Will it work for me too?" "I hope so," said Bill. So Sarah grabbed the towel, closed her eyes and wished really hard for a new ball. When she opened her eyes, there was a brand new ball on the floor! "Wow!" exclaimed Sarah. She hugged Bill. "You have a crazy towel!" Bill smiled. He was happy that Sarah's wish had come true. They hugged and laughed in excitement. They both hoped for many more magical wishes!
