Category: Beginner
Title: Leo the Rich Lion and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a rich lion named Leo. He had so many toys and a big cave to nap in. All the animals liked him, but they were afraid of him too. One day, Leo was taking a nap when a little bird flew into his cave. The bird had a hurt lung and could not fly well. "Help me, please," the bird said. Leo opened his eyes and saw the bird. He felt sorry for the bird and wanted to help. Leo took care of the bird and shared his toys with him. Slowly, the bird's lung got better, and he could fly again. The bird said, "Thank you, Leo. You are not only rich in toys but also rich in kindness." Leo felt happy and proud. From that day on, Leo and the bird became best friends. The other animals saw how kind Leo was and they were not afraid of him anymore. They all played together and had fun. The moral of the story is: Being rich in kindness is more important than having many things. When you are kind, you will have many friends who love and care for you.

Ben and Mia are in school. They like to learn new things and play with their friends. But today, they have a terrible teacher. She is very mean and angry. She does not smile or help them. She only shouts and writes on the blackboard. "Look at this word!" she says. "It is 'cat'. Can you say 'cat'?" The children say 'cat' in a small voice. They are scared of the teacher. "Wrong!" she says. "You are not saying it right. You are not smart. You are bad. Say it again!" The children say 'cat' again. They want to cry. Ben and Mia wonder why the teacher is so mean. They wonder if she is sad or sick or lonely. They wonder if they can do something to make her happy. They look at each other. They have an idea. They raise their hands. They wait for the teacher to see them. "Yes?" she says. "What do you want?" Ben and Mia stand up. They walk to the blackboard. They pick up a chalk. They write something on the blackboard. They turn around. They smile at the teacher. The teacher looks at the blackboard. She sees what they wrote. It says: "We love you." The teacher is surprised. She does not know what to say. She feels something in her heart. She looks at Ben and Mia. She sees their smiles. She smiles back. She says: "Thank you."

One day, a big dragon was sad. He let out a big sigh. His cave was empty. He had no friends to play with. The dragon felt very lonely. One day, a small boy came to the cave. He saw the sad dragon. He asked, "Why are you sad, Mr. Dragon?" The dragon said, "I am sad because my cave is empty and I have no friends to play with." The small boy had an idea. He said, "I can be your friend, Mr. Dragon!" The dragon was very happy. They played all day. The dragon was not sad anymore. His cave was not empty, and he had a new friend.

One day, a big ship was on the water. The ship had many fun things on it. A boy named Tim and a girl named Sue were playing on the ship. They saw a crazy bird flying around. The bird was making funny sounds and dancing in the air. "Look at that crazy bird!" said Tim to Sue. They both laughed and clapped their hands. The bird saw them and came closer. It landed on the ship near the kids. "Hello, crazy bird!" said Sue. The bird looked at them and made a funny face. It had something in its beak. The bird offered them a pretty shell. Tim and Sue were happy and said, "Thank you, crazy bird!" They played with the crazy bird all day on the big ship. When the sun went down, they waved goodbye to the bird as it flew away. Tim and Sue knew they would remember their fun day with the crazy bird on the big ship forever.
