Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Bench

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved pink ice-cream. One sunny day, she went to the park with her mom. Lucy saw an ice-cream truck and asked her mom, "Can I have pink ice-cream, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, but you need to share with your friend, Tim." Lucy and Tim sat on a bench with their pink ice-cream. Tim had trouble holding his ice-cream. He struggled to keep it from falling. Lucy saw Tim struggle and decided to help him. She held his ice-cream for him so it would not fall. Tim said, "Thank you, Lucy." They both enjoyed their pink ice-cream together. Lucy learned that helping her friends was a good thing to do. And from that day, Lucy and Tim became best friends who always helped each other.

Once upon a time, there was a big notebook. A boy named Tim loved to paint in it. He painted all day long. He painted the sun, the moon, and the stars. Tim was very happy when he painted. One day, Tim saw a big, dark cloud in the sky. He thought it would be fun to paint the cloud in his notebook. So he painted the cloud with dark colors. The cloud looked scary, but Tim liked it. The next day, the big, dark cloud came back. It was bigger and darker than before. Tim was scared. He tried to paint it away, but it did not work. The big, dark cloud covered the whole sky. It rained and rained, and Tim's notebook got all wet. His beautiful paintings were gone. Tim was very sad, and he could not paint anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Ann. Ann was very independent. She liked to do things by herself. One day, Ann wanted to help her mom with the laundry. Ann's mom said, "You can help me load the washing machine." So, Ann picked up a big towel and put it in the machine. She was very happy to help. When the laundry was done, Ann's mom thanked her for being a good helper. Ann felt proud. The moral of the story is that even little kids can help and be independent if they try.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim had a secret. He found a hidden door in his room. The door was on the ceiling. Tim wanted to know what was behind the door, but he was too small to reach it. Every night, Tim would listen. He would hear strange sounds coming from the hidden door. One day, he asked his big sister, Sue, for help. Sue was tall and could reach the door. Sue opened the door and they saw a big, colorful bird. The bird was sad. It wanted to go home. Tim and Sue talked to the bird. They asked it where its home was. The bird said it was far away. The bird was scared. Tim and Sue wanted to help the bird. They made a plan. The next day, Tim and Sue took the bird to the park. They let the bird go. The bird was happy. It flew high in the sky. The bird went home. Tim and Sue felt good. They helped the bird find its home. And from that day on, Tim and Sue were the best of friends.

The little girl wanted to go on an adventure. She had her favourite stick in her hand and headed out. But as she walked, the stick fell onto the ground. "Oh no!" she cried. "What's wrong," asked her dad who had come out to follow her. "I've lost my stick!" She said sadly. Her dad put his hand on his hip. He thought for a moment then asked, "Where did you last have it?" The little girl pointed to a spot on the ground. Her dad bent down and said, "Oh! Here it is, right here". He picked up the stick and gave it back to the little girl. She looked with relief and said, "Oh good! Now I can go back to my normal adventure." She smiled and hugged her dad. Then off she went on her special journey.
