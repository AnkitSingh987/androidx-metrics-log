Category: Beginner
Title: Penn and Anna and the Toys

Once upon a time there were two friends, Penn and Anna. They loved to share things with each other, like their favourite toys, snacks, and stories. One day, when they were playing, Anna had an idea. She said, “Let’s play a game. I’ll use my magic spell to make you say something grumpy.” Penn thought it sounded like fun, so he agreed. Anna started chanting and waving her hands in front of Penn. Nothing happened at first, but after a few moments, Penn was suddenly grumpy. She had done it! Anna was so excited that she cheered. Penn was curious, so he asked Anna to teach him the spell. Anna happily agreed, and they took turns sharing the spell with each other. Whenever one of them would get grumpy, the other would wave their hands and chant the spell, and it always worked. The two friends were so excited about the spell that they decided to share it with the world. They ended up creating a website where people could learn how to use the spell. It was one of their favorite things to do together and it brought them so much joy. They felt really proud of themselves for sharing their special spell with the world.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had many mittens in her room. One day, it was very cold outside. Lily had to choose which mitten to wear. Lily asked her friend Tom, "Which mitten should I choose?" Tom said, "Choose the red one. It is warm and nice." But a rude boy named Max laughed and said, "No, choose the blue one. It is better." Lily did not like it when Max was rude. She said, "I will choose the red mitten. Thank you, Tom." Lily and Tom went outside to play in the snow. They had a fun day, and Lily's red mitten kept her warm.

Tim was a little boy who liked to play with his toys. He had a big bear that was gray and soft. He hugged his bear every night and told him stories. He called his bear Gray. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom and Gray. He saw some other kids playing with a ball. He wanted to join them, but he was shy. He asked his mom if he could take Gray with him. "Sure, honey, but be careful. Don't lose Gray, he is your best friend," his mom said. Tim nodded and ran to the kids with Gray. He asked them if he could play, too. They said yes, but they laughed at his bear. "Look at that silly bear! He is so old and gray. He has no eyes and no nose. He is ugly," one of the kids said. The other kids agreed and started to tease Tim and Gray. They took Gray from Tim and threw him around. They pulled his ears and his tail. They made fun of him. Tim felt very sad and angry. He tried to get Gray back, but the kids were bigger and faster. He shouted at them to stop, but they did not listen. He started to weep. His mom heard him and came to see what was wrong. She saw the kids being mean to Tim and Gray. She scolded them and took Gray from them. She hugged Tim and Gray and comforted them. "I'm sorry, honey. Those kids were very rude and mean. They don't know how to be nice and share. They don't know how to appreciate a good friend like Gray. Gray is a very special bear. He has been with you since you were a baby. He loves you and you love him. That's what matters. Don't let anyone make you feel bad about him," she said. Tim nodded and wiped his tears. He hugged his mom and Gray and thanked them. He learned that he should not care about what others say or do. He learned that he should be proud of his bear and his friendship. He learned that he should be kind and respectful to others, even if they are not. He learned that Gray was more than a toy. He was a friend.
