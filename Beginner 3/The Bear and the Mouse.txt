Category: Beginner
Title: The Bear and the Mouse

Once upon a time there was a big bear who was very excited. He went to find some juice. Then he saw a little mouse also looking for juice. The big bear asked the little mouse, “Do you have any juice for me?” The little mouse shook his head, “No, I have no juice for you.” The big bear got angry. He lay down on the ground and started to growl. The little mouse was scared and started to run away. The big bear grabbed the mouse and said, “If you give me the juice, I won’t hurt you.” The little mouse shook his head and said, “I don’t have any juice.” The big bear was so angry that he hurt the little mouse and ate him. The end.

It was a gloomy day when Tommy and his father set out for the barn. Tommy loved to go to the barn and his father enjoyed taking him. When they arrived, Tommy could see lots of young animals. Nestled in the far corner of the barn were two baby rabbits. Tommy was full of joy and asked his father if he could take them home. His father said they weren't ready yet and that they would need to increase, or grow bigger, before they could go home. The sun was disappearing and the day was coming to an end. As father and boy made their way home, Tommy couldn't help thinking of the baby rabbits and how much he wanted to take them home. A few weeks later, his father called him outside to show him that the rabbits had increased, or grown bigger. Tommy, with joy in his heart, ran to the barn to get the rabbits. Once home, Tommy took care of the rabbits and they brought joy and happiness to the family on all the gloomy days.

Anna liked to play with her dolls in her room. She had many dolls with different dresses and hair. She made them talk and have tea parties. She was happy and peaceful. But one day, she saw a big dust ball under her bed. She was curious and wanted to touch it. She reached under the bed and grabbed the dust ball. It was soft and fluffy. She liked it and wanted to keep it. She put the dust ball in her doll house and gave it a name. She called it Fluffy. She pretended Fluffy was a pet for her dolls. She played with Fluffy and her dolls for a long time. But then, her mom came to her room. She saw the dust ball in the doll house and was angry. She said, "Anna, what is this? This is not a toy. This is dust. It is dirty and makes you sneeze. You have to throw it away." Anna was sad and scared. She said, "But mom, this is Fluffy. He is my friend. He is not dirty. He is soft and fluffy. Please let me keep him." Her mom said, "No, Anna. You cannot keep Fluffy. He is not a friend. He is dust. You have to learn to keep your room clean. If you don't, I will punish you. I will take away your dolls for a week." Anna cried and begged. She did not want to lose her dolls or Fluffy. She loved them. But her mom did not listen. She took Fluffy and threw him in the trash. She also took Anna's dolls and put them in a box. She said, "You can have your dolls back when you learn to clean your room. Now go to bed." Anna went to bed, but she could not sleep. She missed Fluffy and her dolls. She was not happy or peaceful. She was sad and lonely. She wished she could have Fluffy back. She wished her mom would not punish her. She wished she could play with her dolls again.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had a cold, wet nose. One day, Spot found a ball in the park. He wanted to play with it. He tried to balance the ball on his nose. It was hard, but he did it! Spot's friend, a big cat named Fluffy, saw him. "Wow, Spot! You balance the ball so well!" Fluffy said. Spot smiled and wagged his tail. He was happy that his friend liked his trick. But then, Spot felt an itch on his ear. He tried to scratch it, but the ball fell off his nose. Spot and Fluffy laughed. They played with the ball all day, and Spot's itch went away. They had so much fun together.
