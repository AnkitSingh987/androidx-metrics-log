Category: Beginner
Title: The Proud Cat and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a proud cat. She had soft fur. The cat liked to play with a dog. The dog was big and strong. They were best friends. One day, the cat and the dog found a big ball. The cat said, "I want to play with the ball." The dog said, "No, I want to play with the ball." They disagree. Then, they decided to take turns. The cat played with the ball first. Then, the dog played with the ball. They were happy and proud to share.

---

One day, a girl named Lily wanted to paint a big picture. She had many colors to use. She was very happy. Lily picked up her brush and started to paint on a big paper. She painted a sun, a house, and some trees. It was a pretty picture. While Lily was painting, her little brother, Tim, came into the room. He saw Lily's picture and wanted to help. But Tim was not very good at painting. He took a brush and started to paint on Lily's picture. But, oh no! Tim made a wreck on the picture. Lily was sad and Tim felt bad. Lily had an idea. She thought they could fix the wreck and make the picture fit. Lily gave Tim a new brush and showed him how to paint. They painted together and fixed the wreck. Now, the picture was fit and pretty again. Lily and Tim were happy and proud of their work.

One day, a yellow cat and a brown dog were playing in the sun. They liked to play together, but they were also competitive. They wanted to see who was the best at everything. The yellow cat said, "I bet I can shrink smaller than you!" The brown dog laughed and said, "No way, I can shrink the smallest!" So they both tried to shrink as small as they could. The yellow cat became very small, but the brown dog was even smaller. Just then, a big bird saw them and wanted to play, too. The big bird asked, "Who is the smallest now?" The yellow cat and the brown dog looked at each other and smiled. They didn't care who was the smallest anymore. They just wanted to play with their new friend, the big bird. So they all played together and had lots of fun in the sun.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went to a place called a library. A library is a big room with many books. Tim loved to look at the books and find interesting ones to read. One day, Tim found a very interesting book about a sad cat. The cat was weeping because it lost its toy. Tim felt sad for the cat and wanted to help it. In the end, the cat found its toy and stopped weeping. Tim was happy for the cat and learned that sometimes things can be sad, but they can also get better. Tim loved the library and the interesting books he found there.

Lila and Tom went to the beach with Mom and Dad. They liked to play in the sand and look for shells. Lila found a big red crab under a rock. She was very happy and showed it to Tom. "Can I hold it?" Tom asked. "Okay, but be careful. It can pinch you," Lila said. Tom took the crab and held it in his hand. He felt its hard shell and its legs. He wanted to see its eyes and mouth. He lifted the crab closer to his face. "Ow!" Tom cried. The crab pinched his nose. He dropped the crab and ran to Mom. Lila ran after him. Mom hugged Tom and looked at his nose. It was red and sore. She took a wet cloth and washed his nose. "Are you okay, Tom?" Mom asked. "It hurts, Mom. The crab was mean," Tom said. "I'm sorry, Tom. The crab was scared. It thought you were going to hurt it. That's why it pinched you. It was trying to protect itself," Mom said. Tom looked at the crab. It was still on the sand, near the rock. It looked small and scared. Tom felt bad. He did not want to hurt the crab. He was ashamed. "I'm sorry, crab. I did not mean to scare you. I just wanted to see you," Tom said. He went back to the crab and picked it up gently. He carried it to the water and let it go. The crab swam away. Lila smiled at Tom. She was proud of him. "That was very nice of you, Tom. You are a good brother," Lila said. Tom smiled back. He felt better. "Thank you, Lila. You are a good sister. Can we play some more?" Tom asked. "Sure, Tom. Let's go find some more shells," Lila said. They held hands and ran to the shore. Mom and Dad watched them and smiled. They were happy.
