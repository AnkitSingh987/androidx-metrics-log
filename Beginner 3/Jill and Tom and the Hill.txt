Category: Beginner
Title: Jill and Tom and the Hill

Jill and Tom were best friends. They liked to play in the park with their bikes. One day, they saw a big hill. Tom wanted to go down the hill very fast. He said to Jill, "Let's zip down the hill. It will be fun!" Jill was scared. She did not like to go fast. She said to Tom, "No, thank you. I like to go slow. The hill is too steep for me." Tom smiled. He gave Jill a wink. He said, "Don't worry. I have a plan. I will go first and wait for you at the bottom. You can go after me. I will catch you if you fall." Jill trusted Tom. She nodded. She watched Tom zip down the hill. He looked happy and brave. She heard him shout, "Whee!" Jill took a deep breath. She pushed her bike to the top of the hill. She looked down. She felt nervous. She remembered Tom's plan. She said to herself, "I can do this. Tom is waiting for me." She let go of the brakes. She felt the wind in her hair. She felt the bike go faster and faster. She closed her eyes. She screamed, "Aaaah!" She opened her eyes. She saw Tom at the bottom. He had his arms open. He had a gentle smile. He said, "You did it, Jill! You are awesome!" Jill felt a rush of joy. She reached Tom. He hugged her. He said, "I'm so proud of you, Jill. You are my best friend." Jill hugged him back. She said, "Thank you, Tom. You are my best friend, too."

One day, a strong dog named Max wanted to go to the garage. He liked to play there. Max asked his friend, a little cat named Lily, "Do you want to go to the garage with me?" Lily said, "Yes, I want to go too!" So, Max and Lily went to the garage together. In the garage, they found a big ball. They played with the ball and had a lot of fun. After playing, Max and Lily saw a small box. They opened the box and found many toys. Max said, "We should share these toys with our friends." Lily agreed, "Yes, sharing is good!" Max and Lily took the toys and shared them with their friends. All the animals had fun playing with the new toys. They learned that sharing makes everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a barber named Tom. Tom loved to cut hair. He had a small shop in the town. Every day, many people came to his shop to get their hair cut. One day, a little boy came to Tom's shop. The boy was scared. He had never had a haircut before. Tom wanted to help the boy. He talked to him and showed him the tools he would use. The boy started to feel better. Tom began to cut the boy's hair. He was very careful and gentle. Soon, the haircut was complete. The boy looked in the mirror and smiled. He was not scared anymore. Tom was happy that he could help the boy. They both knew that the boy would come back to Tom's shop for his next haircut.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to watch television. He would sit in front of the screen and bark with joy. One day, Max's friend, a cat named Lucy, came over to play. Lucy said, "Max, let's play outside!" Max didn't want to stop watching television, but he knew playing with Lucy would be fun. So, Max turned off the television and hurried to the door. Outside, Max and Lucy played in the sun. They ran and jumped and had a great time. After a while, Max said, "I'm tired, let's go back inside." Lucy agreed, and they both hurried back into the house. Max turned on the television again, and they both sat down to watch. They laughed and barked and meowed at the funny things on the screen. Max was happy that he had played with Lucy and now they could enjoy the television together.

One day, a hunter went into the woods. He had a soft hat on his head. He wanted to find food for his family. As he walked, he saw a big tree with a rope. He thought, "I can tie the rope to a tree and catch an animal." The hunter tied the rope and waited. Soon, a small rabbit came by. The rabbit saw the rope and said, "Hello, Mr. Hunter. What are you doing with that rope?" The hunter said, "I want to catch food for my family." The rabbit felt sad for the hunter's family. The rabbit had an idea. "I can help you, Mr. Hunter. I know where the big animals are." The hunter was surprised. He did not know rabbits could talk. The rabbit led the hunter to a place with many big animals. The hunter got food for his family, and they all became friends with the rabbit.
