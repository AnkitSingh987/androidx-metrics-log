Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue wanted to succeed in making a big, pretty tower with her toy blocks. She was a big fan of towers and loved to build them every day. One day, Sue saw an expensive toy tower in a shop. She asked her mom to buy it for her. Her mom said, "No, it is too costly. You have enough toys at home." Sue was very sad and angry. Sue tried to take the toy without her mom knowing. But she was caught by the shopkeeper. Sue's mom was very upset with her. Sue learned that it is not good to take things without asking.

Once upon a time, in a swamp, there lived a frog named Bob. Bob was an unusual frog because he could hear very well. One day, Bob met a duck named Quack. "Hi, I am Bob. I hear well," said Bob. "Hi, I am Quack. I swim fast," said Quack. They became good friends and played in the swamp every day. One day, they heard a loud noise. "What is that?" asked Bob. "I don't know," said Quack. They were scared, but they went to see what it was. They found a big, angry monster. The monster saw them and chased them. Bob and Quack tried to run away, but the monster caught them. The monster took Bob and Quack to his home. They were very sad and scared. They never saw their swamp again. The end.

One day, a boy named Tim had a big closet. He liked to play in his closet. Tim found a mild, soft blanket in the closet. He put the blanket on the floor and sat on it. Tim's friend, Sue, came to play with him. Sue said, "What do you have in the closet, Tim?" Tim said, "I have a soft blanket. We can sit on it and play with my toys." Sue was happy and sat on the blanket with Tim. While they were playing, they heard a funny sound from the closet. They looked and saw a small, friendly dog. The dog had been hiding in the closet! Tim and Sue were surprised and happy. They played with the dog and had a fun day.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park with her mom. They saw a big tree with tasty pears. Mia wanted to pick a pear, but they were too high. Her mom said, "We will find a way to get the pears, just wait and see." At the park, Mia saw her friend, Tom. Tom was tall and could reach high things. Mia had an idea. She asked Tom, "Can you help me pick a tasty pear from the tree?" Tom said, "Yes, I can help you." Tom reached up high and picked a tasty pear for Mia. Mia and her mom were very happy. They thanked Tom for his help. Mia shared the tasty pear with her mom and Tom. They all enjoyed the pear and had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time there was a shy little rabbit called Alice. One day Alice was taking a nap in her treehouse when she heard the sound of a crack in the nearby forest. She got up and hopped closer to investigate. When she arrived, Alice saw a little squirrel who had been trying to crack open a nut. Alice said "Hello! May I help you? The squirrel was so surprised he couldn't speak at first. He nodded his head and said "Thank you, I can't quite crack this nut open." Alice stepped in and smiled. She quickly took the nut and cracked it open for the squirrel. The squirrel thanked Alice and said, "You're so kind. I knew I should have taken a nap in this forest instead of trying to crack my nut open all alone." Alice smiled, feeling pleased. She and the squirrel continued to be friends and often went for naps together in the forest.

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse named Tim. Tim lived in a small, icy hole in the woods. He loved to play in the snow and make new friends. One day, while Tim was playing outside, he met a big bear named Ben. Ben looked sad. Tim asked, "Why are you sad, Ben?" Ben said, "I am hungry, and I don't have any food." Tim wanted to help his new friend. He went back to his icy home and got some cheese to serve to Ben. He carried the cheese on his back and walked to Ben's cave. When Tim got to Ben's cave, he said, "Here is some cheese for you, Ben." Ben was so happy! He smiled and thanked Tim for serving him food. They both sat down and ate the cheese together. From that day on, Tim and Ben were best friends.
