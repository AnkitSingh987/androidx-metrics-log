Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Bear

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. He loved to hold his toy bear. The toy bear was his best friend. They played together all day. One day, Tim and his toy bear went to the park. They saw a big tree with delicious red apples. Tim wanted to eat the apples. He tried to reach them, but he was too small. Then, a kind girl came and helped Tim. She picked the delicious apples for him. Tim and his toy bear were very happy. They shared the apples with the girl and played together. They all became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted dog named Max. Max could sit, jump, and play with his ball. He loved to sit on the soft rug and watch his family. One day, Max's family told him they were going on a holiday. Max was excited to go with them. On their holiday, Max and his family went to a big park. Max saw other dogs and wanted to play. Max's family let him go and he ran fast to meet the other dogs. They all played and had fun. Max was so happy. But then, something unexpected happened. Max saw a big cat sitting on a tree. Max thought the cat was just another friend to play with. He ran to the tree and barked, but the cat jumped down and started to chase Max! Max was scared and ran back to his family. The big cat stopped and went away. Max learned that not all animals want to play, but he was safe and happy with his family.

Once, there was a dependable dog named Spot. Spot was a very good dog. Spot had a job. His job was to bring the tray to his family when they needed it. One day, Spot's family had a big meal. They needed the tray to put all the food on. Spot saw that his family needed the tray, so he went to get it. Spot picked up the tray with his mouth and carried it to his family. They were so happy! They said, "Thank you, Spot! You are such a dependable dog!" Spot wagged his tail and was happy to help.

One day, a boy named Tom found a big box of paint. He was very happy. He wanted to mark the walls with the paint. Tom's mom saw him and said, "No, Tom! We do not mark the walls. Let's go outside and mark on paper." Tom and his mom went outside with the paint. Tom's dog, Max, was alert and wanted to play too. Max ran around them, wagging his tail. Tom dipped his hands in the paint and started to mark on the paper. The paint was red, blue, and green. Tom's mom said, "Good job, Tom! Your marks look nice." Tom smiled and gave Max a pat on the head. Max barked happily. They had a fun day marking with paint and playing with Max.

Once there was a smart dog named Max. Max liked to help people. One day, Max saw a little girl named Lucy. Lucy was sad. She had a big bag of waste that she couldn't carry. Max went to Lucy and said, "I can help you, Lucy. I can provide help with the waste." Lucy smiled and said, "Thank you, Max. You are a smart dog." Max took the waste and put it in the right place. Lucy was happy. She gave Max a big hug. They became good friends. From that day, Max and Lucy always helped each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside. One day, she found a long maze in her garden. Lily was very excited. Lily walked into the maze. She turned left and right, but she could not find the way out. She saw a little bird and asked, "Can you help me find the way out?" The bird said, "Yes, I can help you!" The bird flew up high and looked at the maze. It saw the way out and told Lily, "Go left, then right, and you will find the way out." Lily followed the bird's words and found the way out of the long maze. Lily was very happy and said, "Thank you, little bird!" The bird smiled and flew away. Lily went back home to tell her mom about her adventure in the long maze.
