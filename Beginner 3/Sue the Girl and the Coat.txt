Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Coat

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a coat that she loved very much. But one day, her coat became broken. Sue was sad because her coat could not protect her from the cold wind anymore. One sunny day, Sue saw a big tree. She decided to hide behind the tree to protect her from the cold wind. She played with the leaves and forgot about her broken coat. Suddenly, she found a little bird with a broken wing. Sue knew she had to help the little bird. She took off her broken coat and wrapped the little bird in it. The bird became warm and safe. To Sue's surprise, the broken coat began to fix itself! The coat was fixed and the little bird was happy. Sue learned that sometimes, when we help others, we help ourselves too. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big, modern drum. It was red and shiny. Tim was very happy. He wanted to play the drum and make loud sounds. So, he took the drum and went to the park. At the park, Tim saw his friends. They all wanted to play with the drum too. They took turns hitting the drum. The drum went "boom, boom, boom!" The friends laughed and danced to the drum sounds. After they played with the drum, the sun started to go down. Tim and his friends were tired. They said bye and went to their homes. Tim took his modern drum home too. He knew he would play with it again tomorrow.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max loved to play with his friends. One day, Max saw a new little cat in the park. Max wanted to welcome the cat and be friends. Max ran to the cat and said, "Hi, I am Max. Let's play!" The cat looked at Max and said, "Okay, let's play!" Max and the cat played all day. They ran, jumped, and had a lot of fun. Max felt very happy to have a new friend. But then, something unexpected happened. Max saw the cat do a big jump and land on a tree branch. Max was in shock. He thought, "How can a little cat jump so high?" The cat looked at Max and smiled. "Max, you are a foolish dog," the cat said. "I am not just a little cat. I am a magic cat!" The cat jumped down from the tree and touched Max with its paw. Suddenly, Max could jump very high too. Max and the magic cat played together even more, having lots of fun with their new jumping powers. And they became best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy cat. This cat was black. The cat liked to play with a ball. One day, the cat saw a big red ball. The cat wanted to play with the big red ball. The cat went to the big red ball and tried to pull it. The ball did not move. The cat pulled harder. A dog came by and saw the cat. The dog said, "Do not pull that ball. It is not a good ball." The cat did not listen to the dog. The cat pulled the ball very hard. The ball made a loud sound. The ball was not a ball. It was a big red balloon. The balloon popped. The sleepy black cat was scared and ran away. The cat was sad that the big red ball was gone.

Once upon a time, there was a little seed. The seed wanted to grow big and strong. It was very enthusiastic to become a tall tree. One sunny day, the seed heard a noise. It was rain! The seed was happy because rain helps seeds to grow. The rain made the ground wet, and the seed started to grow. Slowly, the seed grew into a small plant. It made more noise as it grew. The plant was excited and enthusiastic to grow more. It wanted to be the tallest tree in the world. And with the help of the sun, rain, and its enthusiasm, the plant kept growing and growing.
