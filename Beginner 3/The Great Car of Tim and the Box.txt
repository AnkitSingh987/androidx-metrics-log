Category: Beginner
Title: The Great Car of Tim and the Box

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went to the park. They played with a ball and had fun. Tim was hot, so he sat on a bench with his towel. Spot ran around and found an empty box. Spot barked at the box, and Tim went to see what was inside. In the box, there was a broken toy car. Tim wanted to repair the toy car, so he took it home with the empty box and the towel. At home, Tim fixed the toy car with glue and paint. He was happy and proud of his work. He showed the car to Spot, and they played with it all day. The next day, when Tim woke up, he found that the toy car was not a toy anymore! It had turned into a real, big car in the night. Tim and Spot were very surprised. They went for a ride in their new car and had lots of fun. The empty box and the towel had brought them a magical adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to help her mom cook. One day, Sue and her mom decided to cook rice for dinner. They put the rice in a big pot and waited for it to cook. While the rice was cooking, Sue saw a small bug in the kitchen. She was frightened! Sue called her mom to come see the bug. Her mom told her not to be scared, because it was just a friendly bug that would not hurt her. Sue felt better and went back to cooking the rice with her mom. When the rice was done, they all sat down to eat. Sue was happy that she helped cook the rice and was not frightened of the bug anymore. They all enjoyed their dinner and Sue couldn't wait to help her mom cook again.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy yacht. He loved his toy yacht very much. It was small, blue, and harmless. Every day, Tim played with his toy yacht in his room. One sunny day, Tim took his toy yacht to the park. He put it in the pond to watch it float. But then, something unexpected happened. A big frog jumped on Tim's toy yacht. Tim was scared, but the frog was harmless. The big frog looked at Tim and said, "You own this yacht?" Tim nodded. The frog smiled and said, "I like your yacht, too. Can we be friends?" Tim smiled back and said, "Yes, we can be friends." From that day on, Tim and the big frog played together with the toy yacht at the park. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. He loved to play outside in the sun. One day, he saw a big ball on the side of his house. It was hidden behind a bush. Max wanted to play with the ball, but he knew he should resist. Max tried to be a good dog and not touch the ball. But it was so hard for him to resist. He thought about the ball all day. When the sun went down, Max could not resist any longer. He ran to the side of the house and got the ball. Max played with the ball all night. It made him very happy. The next day, he showed the ball to his friend, a little cat named Luna. They played with the ball together and had lots of fun. From that day on, the ball was not hidden anymore, and Max and Luna played with it every day.

Sandra was a little girl. She was three years old. She had a special skirt. It was pink and purple. It was her favorite one. One day, she wanted to wear it. She put it on and was so happy. But then she was scared. She found out that there was a giant spider on it! The spider was big and black. Sandra was frightened. She didn't know what to do. Then she knew. She had to choose. She thought hard. Then she decided to take off her skirt. The spider stayed on it! Sandra was very happy. She was not scared anymore. She chose to take off her skirt and the spider stayed there. Sandra was so proud of herself.
