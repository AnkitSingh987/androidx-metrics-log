Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Pencil

One day, a little girl named Mia found a sharp pencil on the floor. She liked how it looked, so she picked it up and put it in her pocket. She wanted to draw something brilliant with it later. But when she got home, she forgot about the pencil and ran to play with her toys. She jumped on her bed and rolled around, laughing and having fun. She did not notice the sharp pencil poking out of her pocket. Suddenly, she felt a pain in her face. She touched it and saw blood on her hand. She had scratched her face with the pencil. She cried out loud and ran to her mom. Her mom saw the wound and the pencil and hugged her. She cleaned the cut and put a band-aid on it. She told Mia that she should be careful with sharp things, and that she should not put them in her pocket. She said that sharp things can hurt her and others, and that she should only use them with adult help. Mia felt sorry and learned her lesson. She said sorry to her mom and thanked her for taking care of her. She gave her mom a kiss and a hug. She decided to put the pencil in a safe place and only use it when she wanted to draw something brilliant with her mom. She also decided to be more careful with her toys and her face. She knew that her face was precious and that she should protect it. She smiled and felt happy again.

One day, a little dog named Tim was playing with his best friend, a cat named Sue. They were in the bathroom, taking turns to shake the water from their fur after a fun bath. Tim liked to shake very fast, while Sue liked to shake slowly. "Look at me, Sue!" said Tim, shaking fast. "I can shake faster than you!" Sue looked at Tim and felt a little jealous. She wanted to shake as fast as Tim, but she was a cat and cats shake slowly. Sue tried to shake faster, but she couldn't. Tim saw that Sue was jealous and wanted to help. "It's okay, Sue," said Tim. "We are different, but we can still be friends and have fun together." Sue smiled and nodded. She knew that Tim was right. They didn't have to be the same to be friends. So, they continued to play in the bathroom, shaking their fur and laughing together. And from that day on, Sue was never jealous of Tim's fast shaking again.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly cat named Tom. Tom loved to play and have fun. One sunny day, Tom went to the kitchen to find some food. He saw a big dish on the table. The dish was filled with yummy treats. Tom called out to his friend, the dog named Max. "Max, come here! Look at this big dish with yummy treats!" Max came running and wagged his tail. He was very happy to see the treats too. Together, Tom and Max decided to remove the dish from the table. They worked as a team and carefully took the dish down. Then, they enjoyed the yummy treats and had a fun day playing. They were both very jolly and full from the treats.

Lily and Sam love to play in the snow. They make snowmen, snowballs and snow angels. One day, they see a big animal in the woods. It has brown fur, long legs and big horns. It is a reindeer. "Wow, look at that!" Sam says. "It is so big and strong!" "Can we pet it?" Lily asks. "It looks so soft and nice!" They walk slowly towards the reindeer. The reindeer sees them and stops. It does not run away. It looks at them with kind eyes. "Hello, reindeer," Lily says. "We are Lily and Sam. We want to be your friends." The reindeer nods its head. It lets them touch its fur and horns. It is warm and gentle. Lily and Sam are happy. They play with the reindeer for a while. They feed it some carrots and apples. They make a snowman with horns like the reindeer. They have fun. But then, they hear a loud noise. It is a hunter. He has a gun. He wants to shoot the reindeer. "Run, reindeer, run!" Lily and Sam shout. "He is bad! He will hurt you!" The reindeer hears them and runs. It is fast and smart. It hides behind some trees. The hunter does not see it. He walks away. Lily and Sam are scared. They hug each other. They hope the reindeer is safe. They see the reindeer again. It comes out of the woods. It is not hurt. It is brave. It thanks them for saving it. It gives them a lick on the cheek. It is humble. Lily and Sam smile. They say goodbye to the reindeer. They promise to come back and visit. They walk home. They tell their mom and dad about the reindeer. They say it is their friend. They say it is the best reindeer in the world.
