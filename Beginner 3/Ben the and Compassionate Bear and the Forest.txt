Category: Beginner
Title: Ben the and Compassionate Bear and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a kind and compassionate bear named Ben. He lived in a big forest. Ben loved to help his friends when they were sad or scared. One day, Ben heard his friend, a little bird named Tim, cry. "Why are you sad, Tim?" asked Ben. Tim said, "I lost my bathrobe and I feel cold. I am scared I will not find it." Ben wanted to help his friend, so they went to look for the bathrobe together. They looked and looked, but they could not find the bathrobe. Then, they saw a big tree with a hole. Inside the hole, they found Tim's bathrobe! Tim was very happy and not scared anymore. Ben and Tim went back to their homes, feeling warm and safe. They knew they would always help each other when they were scared.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl and her name was Sarah. She loved to take her daddy on walks. One day, daddy said it was time to take a walk. So daddy and Sarah put on their warm coats and went outside into the cold, snowy forest. Sarah was excited and she skipped along holding onto daddy’s hand. Then she looked up at her daddy’s face and said, “Do you like this walk, Daddy?” Daddy smiled down at her and said, “Yes, I love it! The snow is so bright and the air is so warm!” Sarah smiled back and said, “Me too! Let’s take lots more walks together Daddy, okay?” Daddy agreed and they finished their walk, holding hands and feeling very warm.

One day, a little dog named Max was playing in the park. He saw a big horn on the ground. Max was very excited and wanted to show it to his friend, Lily the cat. Max ran to Lily's house and said, "Lily, look at my big horn! Let's play with it!" Lily was calm and said, "Okay, Max. Let's play a game with the horn." While they were playing, the horn made a loud noise. Max and Lily started to panic. They did not know what to do. They were scared of the loud noise. Max's mom came outside and saw them panic. She said, "Don't worry, Max and Lily. The horn is just making noise. It's okay." Max and Lily felt better and went back to playing with the horn. They had a fun day in the park.

Once upon a time, in a small garden, there was a little grape. The grape lived on a big vine with all its grape friends. They liked to play and laugh all day. One day, a scared little bug came to the grape. The bug was lost and didn't know how to get home. The grape wanted to help the bug. The grape told the bug, "Don't be scared. We will find your home." The grape and the bug went to look for the bug's home. They walked and walked. They saw a big tree and a small pond. The grape and the bug met a wise old owl. The owl said, "Blink your eyes three times, and you will see the way home." The bug blinked three times, and saw a path to its home. The grape and the bug walked on the path and found the bug's home. The bug was happy and thanked the grape. The grape went back to its vine and told its friends the story. All the grapes learned that it is good to help others when they are scared or lost. They promised to always be kind and helpful to others. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was an enthusiastic kid who loved to wander and explore. One day, he found a big ball that looked like a planet. It was so big and round, he wanted to play with it. As Tim played with the planet, he met a girl named Sue. Sue said, "Hey, that's my planet! Give it back!" Tim didn't want to give it back, so they both started to argue. They both wanted the planet, but they didn't know how to share. Then, Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's play together with the planet!" Sue thought it was a good idea, so they both played with the planet together. They rolled it, threw it, and had so much fun. In the end, Tim and Sue became good friends and learned how to share.
