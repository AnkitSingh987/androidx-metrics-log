Category: Beginner
Title: The Family of Bears and the Cave

Once upon a time, there was a family of bears. There was a big bear, a middle bear, and a small bear. They lived in a soft, warm cave in the woods. One day, the bears went for a walk in the woods. While they were gone, a girl named Lily came to their cave. She saw the soft beds and wanted to try them. She jumped on the big bed and the middle bed, but she did not destroy them. Then she tried the small bed, and it was just right. Lily fell asleep on the soft, small bed. Soon, the family of bears came back to their cave. They saw their beds and knew someone had been there. The big bear and the middle bear were happy their beds were not destroyed. They found Lily in the small bed and woke her up. Lily was sorry for breaking the small bed. She helped fix the bed, and they all became friends. The bears and Lily played together in the woods, and they all lived happily ever after.

Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They saw a big box with a lock on it. They wanted to know what was inside. "Let's unlock it!" Ben said. He found a key under a flower pot. He put the key in the lock and turned it. The box opened. "Wow!" Lily said. She saw many things in the box. There were books, toys, clothes, and food. She picked up a cookie. It had chocolate chips and nuts on it. It looked yummy. "Can I have one?" Ben asked. He reached for a cookie too. "Wait!" Lily said. "We should ask Mom first. Maybe these are not for us." But Ben did not listen. He bit the cookie. He felt a strange texture in his mouth. It was hard and bitter. He spat it out. "Yuck!" Ben said. "This is not a cookie. This is soap!" Lily looked at the cookie. She saw a label on it. It said "Soap". She felt sorry for Ben. "Are you okay?" Lily asked. Ben shook his head. He felt sick. His mouth was full of bubbles. He ran to the house to find Mom. Mom was angry when she saw Ben. She saw the open box and the soap. She knew what happened. "Who unlocked the box?" Mom asked. Lily was scared. She did not want to get in trouble. She pointed at Ben. "Ben did it!" Lily said. Mom was very angry. She scolded Ben and Lily. She said they were naughty and careless. She said they could have hurt themselves. She said they had to go to their rooms. She said they could not have any real cookies for a week. Ben and Lily felt sad. They wished they had not unlocked the box. They wished they had listened to Lily. They wished they had asked Mom first. They learned a hard lesson.

Once upon a time, in a modest little house, there lived a girl named Lucy. Lucy had a magic toy x-ray. The x-ray could see through things and make them disappear. One day, Lucy's mom asked her to clean her room. Lucy didn't want to clean, so she used her magic x-ray. She pointed it at her toys and said, "Disappear!" Suddenly, all her toys were gone, and her room was clean. Lucy's mom came into the room and said, "Wow, Lucy! You cleaned your room so fast!" Lucy smiled and said, "Yes, Mom. I used my magic x-ray to make my toys disappear." Her mom laughed and said, "That's a good trick, Lucy. But remember, you must always use your magic for good things." Lucy promised to always use her magic x-ray to help people and make them happy.

Once upon a time, in a small forest, there lived a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to pick blueberries. One sunny day, Lily went to the forest to pick some blueberries. She found a big, dry tree full of blueberries. Lily was so happy! But then, a big, mean bird came. The bird wanted to eat all the blueberries. Lily did not want the bird to eat all the blueberries. She wanted to save some for her friends. So, Lily thought of a plan. Lily ran to her house and got a big, shiny pot. She came back to the tree and started to make loud noises with the pot. The big, mean bird got scared and flew away. Lily saved the blueberries! She picked them and shared them with her friends. They all had a big, fun blueberry party.
