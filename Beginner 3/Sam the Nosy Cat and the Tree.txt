Category: Beginner
Title: Sam the Nosy Cat and the Tree

One day, a nosy cat named Sam went for a walk. He saw a big light in the sky. It was so bright! Sam wanted to know what the light was. He walked and walked until he found a big tree. The light was at the top of the tree. Sam said, "I will choose to climb the tree and see the light." He climbed up the tree very fast. When he got to the top, he saw a big bird sitting next to the light. The bird said, "Hi, I am Ben. This light is my friend. We play together!" Sam was surprised. He did not know that birds could have light friends. Ben said, "You can play with us too!" Sam, Ben, and the light played together in the tree. They had so much fun, and Sam was happy he chose to climb the tree.

Anna and Ben are playing in the park. They see a big box with a hole in it. They are curious. They want to see what is inside the box. "Maybe there is a toy or a candy in the box," Anna says. "Let's go and look," Ben says. They run to the box and peek inside. They see nothing. It is dark and empty. Suddenly, they hear a loud noise. The box closes on them. They are trapped. They cannot get out. They are scared. "Help! Help!" they shout. A man comes out from behind a tree. He is holding a net and a bag. He has a mean smile. He is the one who made the trap. He wants to catch children and send them away. "Ha ha ha! I got you, little ones! You fell for my hidden trap! Now you are mine! I will send you to a faraway place where you will work for me!" he says. He walks to the box and tries to open it. But he cannot. The box is locked. He does not have the key. Anna and Ben see a shiny thing on the floor. It is the key. The man dropped it when he ran to the box. They quickly grab the key and unlock the box. They jump out and run away. They see a policeman nearby. They run to him and tell him what happened. The policeman runs to the box and catches the man. He puts him in handcuffs and takes him away. Anna and Ben are safe. They hug each other and thank the policeman. They go back to their parents and tell them the story. Their parents are happy and proud of them. They give them a big hug and a kiss. They go home and have a nice dinner. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a smart dog named Max. Max loved to walk in the park every day. One day, while Max was walking, he saw a hose near a big tree. Max was very curious about the hose. Max went to the hose and picked it up with his mouth. Suddenly, the hose started to wiggle like a snake! Max was so surprised! The hose was not a hose at all, it was a long, friendly snake named Sam. Max and Sam became best friends. They played together in the park every day. Max learned that even when things look scary, they can turn out to be nice and fun. And that is how Max found his new best friend, Sam the snake.

Once upon a time, there was a bird named Bobo. Bobo loved to fly high in the sky. One day, he found a big building. It was a library. The library was a peaceful place where people read books. Inside the library, Bobo met a little girl named Lily. Lily was looking at a book with pictures of birds. Bobo flew down to say hello. "Hi, I am Bobo. I can fly!" he said. Lily smiled and said, "Hi, Bobo! I love birds that fly." Bobo and Lily became good friends. They spent the day looking at books and talking about flying. The library was a happy place for them to be.

One day, a smart dog named Max went for a walk. He liked to discover new things. On his walk, he saw something shiny. It was a big, pretty jewel! Max was very happy. Max picked up the jewel and ran to his friend, a cat named Lily. "Look what I found!" he said. Lily looked at the jewel and said, "Wow, that's so pretty! But, we should give it back. It might be someone's." Max didn't want to give it back. He wanted to keep the jewel. So, he ran away from Lily with the jewel. He didn't know that the jewel belonged to a big, mean dog. The big, mean dog saw Max with his jewel. He was very mad. He chased Max and took the jewel back. Max was sad and scared. He wished he had listened to Lily.
