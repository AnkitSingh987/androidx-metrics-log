Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Polite Boy and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a polite boy named Tim. He liked to help others and always said "please" and "thank you." One day, Tim saw a cat stuck in a tree. He wanted to help the cat, so he decided to climb the tree. As Tim climbed the tree, a man walked by and said, "Be careful, boy! That tree is not safe!" But Tim did not listen. He wanted to help the cat and thought he knew best. Tim reached the cat, but the tree branch broke. They both fell down. Tim got hurt and the cat ran away. The man shook his head and said, "You should have listened, Tim. Sometimes it's better to ask for help." Tim learned that being polite is good, but listening to others is important too.

One day, a woman went for a slow walk. She liked to think about happy things. She saw a big, red ball in the grass. The woman thought it was a toy for her to play with. She kicked the ball, but it did not move. The woman was very surprised. She tried to push the ball, but it was stuck. She thought, "Why won't the ball move?" Then, the ball opened up! Inside was a small, funny dog. The dog said, "Hello, I was hiding in my ball house!" The woman laughed, and they played together. She did not think a dog could live in a ball, but it was a fun surprise.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a mighty dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball. One day, he saw a cross on the ground. He did not know what it meant, but he thought it was a fun place to play. Spot's friend, a little girl named Lily, came over to play with him. She saw the cross too. "Spot, we should not play here," she said. But Spot insisted, "No, it's fun! Please play with me." So, Lily agreed and they played together near the cross. While they were playing, a big wind came and blew the cross away. Under the cross, they found a hidden box! They opened the box and inside were lots of toys and treats. Spot and Lily were very happy. They learned that sometimes, unexpected things can bring joy and fun.

Once upon a time, there was a witch. She lived in a small house in the woods. She had a big pot. The witch liked to fill the pot with yummy soup. One day, a little cat came to the witch's house. The cat was hungry. The witch saw the cat and wanted to help. She was a very persistent witch. She did not give up. She filled a bowl with soup for the cat. The cat ate the soup. It was very happy. The cat and the witch became good friends. They filled their days with fun and play. The persistent witch and the cat lived happily ever after.

Sara and Tom were playing on the beach. They liked to dig in the sand and make castles. Sometimes they found shells and stones that were pretty and shiny. "Look, Sara, I found a big shell!" Tom said, holding up a pink and white shell. "It has a hole in it. I can blow into it and make a sound." "Wow, Tom, that's a nice shell!" Sara said. "Can I try it?" "OK, but be careful. Don't break it," Tom said, handing her the shell. Sara put the shell to her mouth and blew hard. She heard a loud sound, like a horn. She smiled and blew again. "Hey, what's that noise?" a voice said from the water. Sara and Tom looked up and saw a fish with a long nose and a stripe on its side. It was swimming near the shore. "Hello, fish!" Sara said. "We are making sounds with this shell." "Hello, kids!" the fish said. "That's a conch shell. It comes from the reef. Do you know what a reef is?" "No, what is it?" Tom asked. "A reef is a place where many fish and plants and animals live. It is under the water. It is very colorful and beautiful. Some parts are hard and some parts are soft," the fish said. "Can we see it?" Sara asked. "Sure, if you have goggles and fins. But you have to ask your parents first. They have to allow you to go into the water. It can be deep and dangerous," the fish said. "OK, we will ask them. Thank you, fish!" Tom said. "You're welcome, kids! Have fun!" the fish said, and swam away. Sara and Tom ran to their parents, who were sitting on a blanket. They showed them the shell and told them about the fish and the reef. Their parents agreed to let them go into the water, but only with them. They put on their goggles and fins and followed the fish to the reef. They saw many things that made them happy and curious. They had a wonderful time.
