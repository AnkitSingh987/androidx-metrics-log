Category: Beginner
Title: The Spark and the Bear

Once upon a time, there was a little spark. The spark was very patient. It lived in a big forest with many trees and animals. One day, the spark met a big bear. The big bear was not happy. "Go away, little spark!" said the big bear. "I don't want you here!" The spark was sad but it was patient. It did not want to make the big bear more angry. So, the spark went away to find a new friend. The spark met a kind bird. The bird saw the spark was sad. "Why are you sad, little spark?" asked the bird. The spark told the bird about the big bear. The bird had an idea. "Let's make the big bear happy!" said the bird. The bird and the spark worked together. They made a big show with music and dance. The big bear saw the show and started to smile. The big bear began to clap and applaud. The big bear was happy now. The moral of the story is to be patient and kind, even when others are not nice. Sometimes, a little bit of love can make a big change.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big open field with many flowers. Tim liked to run and play in the open field. He saw a big tree and wanted to sit under it. Tim's mom had a big leather bag. She put the bag under the tree and they both sat down. Tim looked at the flowers and said, "Mom, the flowers are so pretty!" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, they are, Tim." As they sat, a small bug came by. Tim did not want to disturb the bug, so he watched it walk by. The bug went to a flower and flew away. Tim laughed and said, "Bye, bug!" Then, they played in the open field until it was time to go home.

One day, a bird named Tim wanted to soar high in the sky. He was very excited. Tim was a flexible bird and could fly very well. He had a big exam today to see how high he could fly. All his bird friends were there to watch him. Tim started to fly up, up, up. He was doing great, and all his friends were happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and pushed Tim far away. His friends could not see him anymore. Tim was scared, but he did not give up. He used his flexible wings to fly back to his friends. When he got back, they all cheered. Tim had passed his exam by flying so high, and he also learned how to fly in the wind. Tim was very proud and his friends were too.

Once upon a time, in a big jungle, there lived a furry bear. The bear had many friends. One day, the bear met a little bird. The bird was sad. The bear asked, "Why are you sad, little bird?" The bird said, "I can't fly very high. I want to see more of the jungle." The bear had an idea. He said, "Climb on my back, and I will take you to see the jungle." The bird was happy, and they went on an adventure. As they walked, the bear requested, "Please sing a song, little bird." The bird sang a beautiful song. They saw many things in the jungle. Then, they saw a big hill. The bear and the bird climbed the hill. At the top, they saw something unexpected: a big, shiny lake. The bird was so happy to see the lake. The bear said, "You can fly down to the lake and play." The bird thanked the bear and flew down to the lake. The bear and the bird became best friends. They learned that helping each other made them both happy. And that was the moral of the story: when you help others, you become happy too.

One day, a fat cat named Tom arrived at the park. He saw kids playing with a baseball. Tom wanted to play too, but he was shy. He sat under a tree and watched them play. A little girl named Sue saw Tom and walked up to him. "Hi, cat! Do you want to play with us?" she asked. Tom looked at Sue and nodded his head. Sue took Tom's paw and led him to the group. Tom and the kids played baseball all day. They had a lot of fun. Tom was happy he had made new friends. The kids learned that it is good to be kind and include everyone, even if they are different. And Tom learned that it is okay to ask for help when you are shy.
