Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Mouse and the Cat

One day, a big cat and a little mouse were playing in the garden. The big cat said, "I can run very fast. Can you?" The little mouse said, "No, but I can make a knot very fast." The big cat laughed and said, "Show me how you can make a knot." The little mouse took a piece of string and made a knot very fast. The big cat was surprised and said, "Wow! That's a good knot!" Suddenly, the big cat felt a sneeze coming. He tried to stop it, but it was too strong. "Achoo!" he sneezed. The sneeze was so big that it made a dangerous tree fall down near them. The tree was about to hit the little mouse. The big cat was scared, but he remembered the little mouse's knot. He quickly used the string with the knot to pull the little mouse away from the dangerous tree. The little mouse was safe, and they both felt happy. From that day on, the big cat and the little mouse became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a lively little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his red ball. One day, while playing, his ball rolled under a big rock. Spot wanted to get his ball back, but he could not reach it. Spot began to struggle. He tried to push the rock, but it was too heavy. He tried to dig under the rock, but the ground was too hard. Spot did not know what to do. He was very sad. Then, Spot saw a cap on the ground. He had an idea! He put the cap on his head and felt very strong. With all his might, he pushed the rock again. This time, the rock moved! Spot got his red ball back and was very happy. From that day on, Spot always wore his cap when he played, feeling like the strongest dog in the world.

One day, a black cat named Tom went to a human house. He wanted to borrow a toy. Tom knocked on the door. A little girl named Lucy opened the door. She was happy to see Tom. "Hi, Tom! What do you want?" Lucy asked. Tom said, "Can I borrow a toy?" Lucy smiled and said, "Yes, you can borrow my ball." Tom was very happy and played with the ball all day. But then, something unexpected happened. The ball changed into a big, black bird! Tom was very surprised. The bird said, "Thank you, Tom and Lucy! I was a bird stuck in a ball. Now I can fly again!" Tom and Lucy were happy to help the bird. They waved goodbye as the bird flew away.

One day, Tim went to the park to play. He saw a girl with a mask. Tim wanted to meet her. He walked up to her and said, "Hi, I'm Tim. What's your name?" The girl said, "Hi Tim, I'm Jane. I like your toy car." Tim felt happy to meet Jane. They played with the toy car and had a lot of fun. Later, Tim wanted to play on the swings, but they were all taken. Tim felt frustrated. Jane saw Tim and said, "Let's wait for our turn." They waited and soon, they got to play on the swings together. Tim and Jane had a great day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a big man who liked to create things. One day, he made a valley. The valley was very big and had many trees and flowers. The big man was happy with the valley he made. But the valley was cold. The people who lived near the valley did not like the cold. They wanted the valley to be warm and sunny. So, they asked the big man to help them. The big man thought and thought. Then, he had a good idea. He made a big, warm sun and put it in the sky. The sun made the valley warm and not cold anymore. The people were happy and played in the valley all day long. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to ride her bike. Every day, she would put on her helmet and ride around the park. Her helmet was very important because it kept her head safe. One day, Amy saw a big pile of mud. She thought it would be fun to ride through it. So, she did! The mud was disgusting, but Amy didn't care. She laughed as it splashed all over her and her bike. After playing in the mud, Amy went home. Her mom saw her and said, "Oh my! You need a bath!" Amy's bath time included lots of bubbles and her favorite toys. After her bath, Amy put on her clean clothes and went to bed. She had so much fun that day and couldn't wait to ride her bike again tomorrow.
