Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Nosy Dog and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little dog named Spot. Spot liked to dig in the dirt and create holes in the ground. He would dig and dig all day long. One day, Spot met a friendly bird named Chirpy. Chirpy saw Spot digging a hole and asked, "Why do you create holes, Spot?" Spot looked up from his hole and said, "I like to find things in the ground. It's fun!" Chirpy thought it sounded fun too. So, Chirpy helped Spot dig more holes. Together, they found a shiny rock and a big worm. They were very happy and played together all day. And from that day on, nosy little Spot and Chirpy were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big room. In the middle of the room was a tree. The tree was plain and plain and plain, and the room looked sad. "I know!" said a little girl. "Let's decorate the tree!" So the little girl and her friends got to work. They found some pretty stars and some quick stripes and some sweet hearts. They worked quickly and soon the tree was a work of art. It sparkled with stars, blazed with stripes and shone with hearts. The little girl and her friends stepped back to admire their work. They had turned a plain tree into something beautiful. "Isn't it sweet?" the little girl said, smiling. All of her friends agreed. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a big pile of clay near his house. Tim thought it would be fun to make something with the clay. So, he knelt down and started to play with it. He made a small house, a tree, and a cat. Tim was very happy with his clay creations. Tim's friend, Sue, came over and saw what he was doing. She said, "Wow, Tim! That looks fun! Can I play with the clay too?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, Sue! Let's make more things together!" So, Sue knelt down next to Tim, and they both played with the clay. They made a car, a dog, and a big sun. After a while, it was time to go inside. Tim and Sue looked at their hands and saw that they were very dirty from the clay. They went inside and washed their hands with soap and water. Now their hands were clean again. They both had a great time playing with the clay and making new things. They couldn't wait to play with the clay again soon.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a toy pistol in his toy box. He thought it was brilliant! The toy pistol was red and blue, and it made a fun noise when he pushed the button. Tim loved playing with his new toy. Tim had a pet cat named Fluffy. Fluffy was hungry, so Tim decided to feed her. He put some cat food in her bowl and said, "Here you go, Fluffy! Eat your food!" Fluffy purred and started to eat her food. While Fluffy was eating, Tim played with his toy pistol. He pretended to be a superhero, saving the day with his brilliant toy. Fluffy finished eating and joined Tim in playing. They had a great day together, and Tim was happy he could feed Fluffy and play with his new toy pistol.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim liked to polish his shoes. He had a big sack with all his shoes. Tim was very flexible and could bend down to polish his shoes easily. One day, Tim met a new friend named Sue. Sue saw Tim polishing his shoes. She said, "Tim, can you help me polish my shoes too?" Tim said, "Yes, I can help you, Sue." So, Tim and Sue polished their shoes together. As they were polishing their shoes, a big wind came. It blew the sack away with all their shoes inside. Tim and Sue tried to catch the sack, but they couldn't. They were very sad. They lost all their shoes, and they had to walk home with no shoes on their feet.
