Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Driver and the Car

One day, a driver named Tom had a big car. He liked to drive fast. But today, he wanted to be obedient and go slow. He knew it was important to be safe. Tom saw his friend, Sam, on the road. Sam said, "Tom, let's race!" Tom thought about it, but he wanted to be obedient. "No, Sam. I want to be safe and slow today," Tom said. Sam was sad, but he understood. As they drove, they saw a big rock on the road. Sam was going too fast, and he could not stop. His car hit the rock, and it broke. Tom was going slow, so he stopped in time. Sam said, "You were right, Tom. Being obedient and slow is better." Tom helped Sam fix his car, and they both learned to be safe drivers.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her friends and was always eager to learn new things. One day, while playing in the park, she found a shiny crystal. It was so pretty that she wanted to show it to her best friend, Tom. Lily ran to Tom's house and knocked on the door. When Tom opened it, Lily said, "Look, Tom! I found a crystal! Isn't it pretty?" Tom's eyes grew big, and he said, "Wow, it's so shiny! Let's send it to the sky and see if it can touch the clouds!" They both smiled and started to throw the crystal up in the air, trying to make it reach the clouds. They played with the crystal all day long, and it made them very happy. When the sun began to set, Lily and Tom said goodbye and promised to play with the crystal again the next day. They knew their new crystal would bring them many more fun days together.

Tim and Jen are friends who like to play outside. They have a big yard with green grass and a swing. They also have a dog named Spot who is very dependable. He always comes when they call him and he likes to play fetch. One day, Tim and Jen decide to make a picnic on the grass. They bring some bread, cheese, and apples from the kitchen and a blanket to sit on. They spread the blanket on the grass and put the food on it. Then they call Spot to join them. "Spot, come here, boy!" Tim says. "We have a picnic for you!" Spot runs to them and wags his tail. He sniffs the food and licks his lips. He looks at Tim and Jen with his big brown eyes. "Can I have some, please?" he seems to ask. "Of course, you can, Spot!" Jen says. "You are our friend and you provide us with fun and love. You can have some cheese and a bite of apple. But not the bread, it is not good for you." Tim and Jen share their food with Spot and enjoy the picnic. They talk and laugh and pet Spot. They are happy and grateful for their dependable dog and the green grass. They think it is a perfect day.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He had a spade that he loved to play with. One day, Tim went outside to dig in the dirt. He dug and dug until his spade was filthy. Tim wanted to clean his spade. He saw a big puddle near his house. He thought the water would make his spade clean again. So, he ran to the puddle and put his spade in the water. But when Tim tried to turn the spade in the water, it got stuck. He pulled and pulled, but the spade would not come out. Tim was sad. His spade was stuck in the muddy water, and he could not play with it anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a warm, little house. In the house, there was a boy named Tim and a girl named Sue. They liked to play all day. One day, Tim and Sue found a cobweb in the corner of their room. They didn't like it. Tim said, "Let's clean it!" Sue agreed. They urged their mom to help them clean the cobweb. Mom got a broom and started to clean the cobweb. But something unexpected happened! A little, friendly spider came out of the cobweb. The spider said, "Please don't clean my home!" Tim, Sue, and their mom were surprised. They talked and decided to let the spider stay. They all became good friends and played together every day.
