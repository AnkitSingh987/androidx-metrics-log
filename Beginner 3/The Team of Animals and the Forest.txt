Category: Beginner
Title: The Team of Animals and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a team of little animals. They lived in a big forest. The team had a tiny, fragile bird named Lily, a small, fast rabbit named Tim, and a big, strong bear named Sam. One sunny day, the team went out to gather food for a picnic. Lily flew high in the sky to find berries. Tim hopped around to find carrots. Sam walked around to find honey. When they all came back, they shared their food. The team had a fun picnic. They laughed and played together. They were the best of friends. And they all lived happily ever after.

---

One day, a thin cat named Tom went for a jog in the park. He liked to run and play with his friends. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. Tom was very happy. While Tom was jogging, he saw a big dog. The dog was not nice. The dog said, "I will catch you, Tom!" Tom was scared and started to run faster. He did not want the dog to catch him. Tom ran and ran, and then he saw a tree. He had an idea! He yelled, "Help me, birds!" The birds heard Tom and came to help. They flew down and picked him up. The dog was surprised and could not catch Tom. The birds saved Tom, and they all became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a cat with soft fur. The cat lived in a big house with a rich man. The rich man was not nice. He never shared his toys with other kids. The cat was sad. One day, the cat met a dog. The dog was very kind. The dog mentioned that sharing is good. The cat wanted to learn how to share. The cat went back to the rich man and told him about the dog. The rich man listened and learned to share. The cat and the rich man were happy. They played with other kids and shared their toys. The cat's soft fur was now full of joy. The moral of the story is that sharing makes everyone happy.

Sara and Ben are going to visit their grandma. They have to pack their bags with clothes and toys. Sara likes to pack her doll and her book. Ben likes to pack his car and his ball. "Are you ready?" Mom asks them. "We have to go soon." "Yes, Mom!" Sara and Ben say. They zip their bags and carry them to the car. On the way to grandma's house, they see many things. They see cows and horses and birds. They see trees and flowers and hills. They see a big hedge with many leaves and branches. "Look, Mom!" Ben says. "There is something in the hedge!" Mom slows down the car and looks. She sees a small animal with brown fur and a long nose. It is a hedgehog. It is hiding in the hedge. "It is a hedgehog, Ben," Mom says. "It lives in the hedge. It is wild. It does not like people. We have to be quiet and let it be." "Wow, it is so cute!" Sara says. "Can we take it home?" "No, Sara," Mom says. "We cannot take it home. It belongs in the hedge. It is happy there. We have to respect the wild animals and their homes." "OK, Mom," Sara says. "But can we say hello to it?" "Sure, Sara," Mom says. "But be gentle and quiet." Sara and Ben roll down the window and wave to the hedgehog. They say hello in a soft voice. The hedgehog looks at them and snorts. Then it curls up into a ball and hides its nose. "Bye, hedgehog!" Sara and Ben say. "Have a nice day!" Mom drives away and smiles. She is proud of her children. They are kind and curious. They love to see new things. They are ready to have fun with grandma.

One day, a little mouse named Tim went out to observe. He loved to watch things around him. The sun was shining, but it was a dull day. As Tim walked, he saw a big piece of cheese. "Wow! I love cheese," he thought. Tim wanted to eat the cheese, but it was too big for him to carry. He needed help. Tim saw his friend, Sam the squirrel. "Sam, can you help me with the cheese?" Tim asked. "Sure, Tim. Let's take it home!" Sam said. They both picked up the cheese and took it to Tim's house. At home, Tim and Sam shared the cheese. They were very happy. It was not a dull day anymore. They learned that by helping each other, they could do big things.
