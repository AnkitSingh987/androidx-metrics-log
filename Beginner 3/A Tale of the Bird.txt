Category: Beginner
Title: A Tale of the Bird

One day, a little yellow bird was flying in the sky. The bird saw a big tree and sat on a branch. The bird was very happy. The yellow bird saw a small boy under the tree. The boy looked up and saw the bird. The boy said, "Hello, yellow bird! Can you hear me?" The yellow bird put its ear close to the boy and answered, "Yes, I can hear you!" The boy and the yellow bird talked and played all day. They were very happy. When the sun went down, the boy went home. The yellow bird flew away to find a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big red truck. The truck was very troubled. It had a big job to do, but it was stuck in the mud. One day, a nice car came by. The car saw the troubled truck and wanted to help. The car said, "I will marry you if you help me get out of the mud." The truck was happy and said yes. Together, they worked hard to get the truck out of the mud. The car pushed and the truck pulled. Soon, the truck was free. They got married and lived happily ever after. The truck was not troubled anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She loved to wave her hands to say hello to her friends. One sunny day, Mia went to the park with her mommy. At the park, Mia saw an elderly lady sitting on a bench. The lady had kind eyes and a warm smile. Mia walked up to her and waved her hand to say hello. The elderly lady waved back and smiled even bigger. Mia and the elderly lady became friends. They played together, laughed, and waved to each other every time they saw each other at the park. And they both lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a humble little town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to go for walks on the trail near his house. One sunny day, Tim put on his hat and went for a walk on the trail. As Tim walked, he saw a big rock. The rock had a nice seat on top. Tim said, "Hello, rock! Can I sit on you?" The rock did not talk, but Tim knew it was okay to sit. So, Tim sat on the rock and looked at the pretty trees and flowers. Tim's friend, Sue, came to the trail too. She saw Tim on the rock and said, "Hi, Tim! Can I sit with you?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, Sue! Come sit with me." Sue sat next to Tim, and they both enjoyed the beautiful day on the trail together.

Once upon a time there was a little girl. She was playing when she saw something on the screen. It looked scary and made her feel helpless. She screamed and ran away. The little girl didn't know what to do. It was so scary! She ran to her mom for help. Mom was very kind and hugged her tight. "Don't worry," Mom said. Mom turned off the screen and the scary thing was gone. The little girl felt better and went back to play. Now, she feels safe when she is playing. She learned that her mom can help her when she is scared.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Timmy. Timmy wanted to fly, but he did not know how. He felt ashamed because all his friends could fly. One day, Timmy's mom said she would teach him to fly. Timmy's mom took him to a big tree. She said, "Timmy, watch me fly. Then you can try." Timmy watched his mom fly from the tree. He felt scared, but he wanted to fly too. He thought of how happy he would be when he could fly with his friends. Timmy tried to fly. At first, he fell. But he did not give up. He tried again and again. Each time, he got better. Finally, Timmy could fly! He was so happy. Now, Timmy was not ashamed anymore. He could fly with his friends and have fun. And it was all because his mom taught him how to fly.
