Category: Beginner
Title: Bobo the Colorful Bird and the Pillow

Once upon a time, there was a colorful bird named Bobo. Bobo loved to fly around and play with his friends. One day, Bobo found a pillow on the ground. It was soft and fluffy. He thought it would be fun to play with the pillow. Bobo tried to pick up the pillow with his beak, but it was too heavy. He tried to weigh it with his wings, but he could not lift it. Bobo was sad. He wanted to play with the pillow, but he could not do it alone. Bobo asked his friends for help. They all came and helped him lift the pillow. Together, they played with the pillow and had lots of fun. The moral of the story is that when we work together, we can do things that we cannot do alone.

One day, a popular bird named Timmy wanted to play with his friends. He saw them near a hedge, a wall made of plants. They were all taking turns to dive into the hedge and come out with a big smile. "Can I play too?" asked Timmy. His friends said, "Yes! All you have to do is dive into the hedge and find a nice stick." So, Timmy took a deep breath and dove into the hedge. He found a nice stick and came out with a big smile, just like his friends. Later, a small bird named Benny wanted to join the fun. Benny was shy and had no friends. Timmy saw Benny and said, "Come play with us, Benny!" Benny was happy and joined them in diving into the hedge. Soon, they all became good friends. The birds learned that playing together and being kind to everyone made them all happy. The moral of the story is to be nice to others and include everyone in your fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a red case that he loved very much. Every day, Tim would put his toys in the case and carry it with him wherever he went. One day, Tim went to the park with his case. He opened it to play with his toys, but the case was empty! Tim was sad and didn't know what to do. He looked around the park to find his toys, but they were not there. Then, Tim's big sister, Sue, came to help him. She said, "Let's prevent this from happening again." Sue helped Tim find his toys and put them back in the case. From that day on, Tim always made sure his case was full of toys before he left the house.

Once upon a time there was a lady who wanted to go for a long walk. She put on her coat and headed outside. She started to walk and when she couldn't walk any more, she stopped to take a rest. As she sat there, she saw a child playing in the park. The lady smiled and said to the child, "Would you like to spend the day with me?" The child looked up at her and smiled. "Yes, I would love to!" So the lady and the child spent the day walking, talking and playing games. They played for so long that the sun started to go down. The lady said to the child, "It looks like it's time for me to go home now. I had such a lovely long day with you!" The child said goodbye and waved as the lady walked away.

One day, a big van came to the park. It had many colors and made happy music. Kids were very excited and impatient. They ran to the van with big smiles on their faces. Inside the van, there were many sweet treats. The kids could taste ice cream, cake, and candy. They laughed and played as they ate their treats. After they ate, the kids said "thank you" to the van man. They waved goodbye as the van left the park. The kids went home with happy bellies, ready to tell their friends about the fun day.

Tom and Anna are playing in the park. They like to slide, swing and run. They see a big house with a fence. They are curious. They want to see what is inside. They find a small hole in the fence. They crawl through it. They see a door with a lock. They see a key on the ground. Tom picks up the key. He puts it in the lock. He turns it. The door opens. "Let's go in!" Tom says. He holds Anna's hand. They walk into the house. It is dark and dusty. They see old furniture and toys. They hear a noise. It sounds like a dog. "Who is there?" a voice says. It is a man. He sounds angry. He comes out of a room. He sees Tom and Anna. He is holding a stick. "What are you doing here?" he yells. "This is my house! Go away!" Tom and Anna are scared. They run to the door. They want to get out. But the door is closed. The key is still in the lock. The man is blocking the way. "Please, let us go!" Anna says. She is crying. "We are sorry. We just wanted to see." The man does not listen. He raises his stick. He is going to hit them. Tom and Anna are helpless. They hug each other. They close their eyes. They hope someone will help them. They remain very still.
