Category: Beginner
Title: The Lot of Corn and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a big field, there was a lot of corn. In the middle of the corn, there was an ancient tree. It was very old and wise. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the corn field. He wanted to relax under the old tree. As Tim sat under the tree, he heard a voice. "Hello, little boy," said the voice. Tim looked around and saw a big, friendly bird on a branch. "Hi," said Tim. "I'm here to relax." The bird and Tim became friends. They talked and played all day. Then, something unexpected happened. The ancient tree began to shake. The corn around them started to dance and sing! Tim and the bird were very surprised. The tree told Tim and the bird that it was a magical tree. It could make the corn come to life when it was happy. Tim, the bird, and the dancing corn had a big party under the ancient tree. They laughed and played until the sun went down. And from that day on, Tim would visit the corn field to relax and have fun with his new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little infant named Tim. Tim had a big sister named Sue. Sue and Tim liked to play together every day. They played with their toys, ran in the park, and laughed a lot. One day, Sue went to her friend's house to play. Tim stayed home with mom. Tim missed Sue a lot. He felt sad and did not want to play with his toys alone. Mom saw that Tim was sad and gave him a sour apple to eat. Tim made a funny face when he tasted the sour apple. Soon, Sue came back home. Tim was so happy to see her. They played together again and Tim forgot about the sour apple. Sue and Tim knew that they were the best of friends and they never wanted to miss each other again.

Once upon a time, there was a normal cat named Tom. Tom was black and loved to play. One day, Tom went outside to find a new friend. Tom saw a bird and offered to play. The bird said yes, and they played a fun game. They ran, jumped, and laughed. Tom was very happy to have a new friend. But then, something unexpected happened. The bird changed into a big, black dog! Tom was surprised but still wanted to play. The dog smiled and said, "I was just playing a trick on you. I wanted to see if you would still be my friend." Tom laughed and said, "Of course, I will be your friend!" And they played happily ever after.

One day, a little bee was flying in the sky. The bee was looking for flowers to make sweet honey. The bee saw a big, pretty flower and went to it. As the bee got close to the flower, it saw a dead bug on the ground. The bee felt sad for the bug. The bee knew it was important to be careful and stay away from danger. Just then, a girl saw the bee and started to clap her hands. She liked the bee and wanted to be friends. The bee flew to her hand, and they became friends. The girl and the bee played together all day, and they were both very happy.

Lily wanted to play with the sand in the park. She liked the texture of the sand. It was soft and smooth and she could make shapes with it. She asked her mom if she could play with the sand. "Mom, can I play with the sand?" Lily said. "Yes, you can, but only for a little while. We have to go home soon," Mom said. Lily was happy. She ran to the sand and started to make a castle. She used a bucket and a shovel and some sticks. She was very proud of her castle. But then, a big boy came and kicked her castle. He broke it and made a mess. Lily was very sad and angry. She cried and shouted at the boy. "Hey, why did you do that? That was my castle! You are very mean!" Lily said. The boy laughed and said, "I don't care. It's just sand. You can't tell me what to do. I can do whatever I want." Lily felt very hurt. She wanted to hit the boy, but she knew that was wrong. She looked for her mom. She saw her mom coming towards her. She ran to her mom and hugged her. "Mom, that boy broke my castle. He was very rude. He didn't permit me to play with the sand," Lily said. Mom hugged Lily and said, "I'm sorry, Lily. That was not nice of him. He should have asked you first. He should have respected your work. You did a great job with your castle. I'm proud of you." Mom took Lily to another spot in the park. She helped her make a new castle. She gave her a cookie and a juice. Lily felt better. She thanked her mom and smiled. She learned that some people are mean, but some people are kind. She learned that she should always ask before she touches someone else's things. She learned that she should always be polite and gentle. She learned that her mom loves her very much.
