Category: Beginner
Title: The Boy Called Tom and the Pot

Once upon a time, there was a boy called Tom. Tom was very small, only three years old. One day, Tom was looking for something special to play with. He came across a shiny pot. It was sooo fine! Tom smiled and wanted to pick it up. He kneeled down to pick it up, but it was too big and too heavy. He was really sad. Then, suddenly, something magical happened. Tom waved his hands and the pot became small and light. Tom was so happy! He smiled and kneeled to pick it up. Tom waved his hands again and the pot became big and heavy. He carried the heavy pot outside and laughed as he played. It was a fun day for Tom.

Once upon a time, in a big zoo, there was a zebra. The zebra was very organized. Every day, the zebra made sure his home was nice and clean. One day, the zoo had a big show. The zebra wanted to display his nice home to everyone. He worked very hard to make everything look perfect. At the show, all the people came to see the zebra's home. They were very happy to see how organized it was. The zebra felt proud and everyone clapped for him. And they all lived happily ever after.

---

One day, a cat and a dog had a quarrel. The cat was restless because the dog took her toy. The dog wanted to taste the toy, but the cat said, "No, that is mine!" The next day, the cat saw a big box. She thought, "I will hide my toy in the box. The dog will not find it there." The dog watched the cat put the toy in the box. He had a plan. Later, the dog went to the box and took the toy out. The cat saw the dog and said, "Give it back!" The dog said, "I will give it back if you share." The cat agreed, and they played together. From that day, they did not quarrel anymore, and they were happy.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to play. He looked for his toys but they were not available. Tim felt sad. Then, he saw some dough on the table. Tim's eyes lit up and he started to play with the dough. Tim made a little friend with the dough. He named his dough friend, Dee. Tim and Dee had a lot of fun. They would talk and play all day. Tim asked Dee, "Do you want to go to the park?" Dee replied, "Yes, let's go!" So, Tim and Dee went to the park. They played on the swings and slides. They laughed and had a good time. Soon, it was time to go home. Tim and Dee were happy they had a fun day at the park. They knew they would have more fun days together.

Once upon a time, there was a rat named Tom. Tom was a reliable rat, always helping his friends. One day, Tom and his friend, a cat named Max, were playing with a ball. "Be careful, Max," said Tom. "Don't break the ball." Max nodded and they played together. They had so much fun. Suddenly, the ball went too high and hit a big tree. The tree shook and a big egg fell down. "Oh no!" cried Tom and Max. They looked at the egg, not knowing what to do. A bird flew down and said, "Thank you, Tom and Max! You found my lost egg!" The bird was happy, and Tom and Max were happy too. They didn't break the ball, and they helped their new friend.

One day, a little bird named Bob was very nosy. He liked to look at everything. He looked at the sky, the trees, and the flowers. He looked at the other birds and animals too. Bob found a nest in a tree. He looked at the nest and saw three eggs. He was very nosy, so he looked closer. The eggs were blue and pretty. Bob wanted to see what was inside the eggs. A big bird came back to the nest. She saw nosy Bob looking at her eggs. The big bird said, "Go away, Bob! These are my eggs." Bob was sad, but he knew he should not be nosy. He flew away and looked at other things, like the clouds and the sun.
