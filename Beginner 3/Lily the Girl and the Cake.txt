Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, her mom asked her to unpack the food from the big bags. Lily was very happy to help. Lily put the cold things in the freezer. The freezer was very tight, but she made the food fit. She put the other food on the table. When she was done, she saw a big box. It was a surprise for her. Lily opened the box and saw a big cake. It had her name on it. She was so happy! But then, the cake started to move. Lily was scared. The cake opened up, and out came her best friend, Kim! It was a fun trick. Lily laughed and hugged Kim. They all had a great party with the cake.

One day, a gray cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a shiny medal on the ground. He wanted to show his friend, Sam the dog. Tom went to Sam's house and said, "Look, Sam! I found a medal!" Sam looked at the medal and smiled. He was happy for his friend. They played with the medal for a while. Then, Tom said, "We should remain friends forever!" Sam agreed and they played some more. The gray cat and the dog had a fun day with the shiny medal.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy who loved to call on his friends. One day he called on his best friend to play. His friend said, "Let's go to the store to get some shrimp!" The little boy was so excited! He said, "Yes! I'm ready!" So they went to the store and got some shrimp. But when they went home the shrimp had gone bad. The little boy said to his friend, "Oh no! Now we can't eat the shrimp!" His friend replied, "We should have left it in the store until it was ready!" But it was too late and they had to throw away the shrimp. The little boy had a very bad day.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big green tree. Under the tree, there was a box. The box was hot. Tim was very curious. Tim opened the hot box. Inside, he found a piece of paper with the number "3" on it. Tim looked around and saw another box. This box had the number "2" on it. He opened the box and found a toy car. Tim was so happy! Just then, a little girl named Sue came up to Tim. She said, "Hey! That's my hot box! And my toy car!" Tim was surprised. He said, "I'm sorry, I didn't know." Sue smiled and said, "It's okay. Let's play together!" Tim and Sue played with the toy car under the big green tree and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big, loud dog named Buddy. Buddy had soft fur that everyone loved to touch. Buddy liked to play with his friends, a small cat named Kitty and a tiny bird named Tweet. One day, Buddy, Kitty, and Tweet wanted to go play in the park. But, there was a problem. Buddy's mom said they had to wait. She needed to delay their trip to the park because she had to finish her work first. Buddy, Kitty, and Tweet were sad, but they knew they had to listen. While they waited, Buddy, Kitty, and Tweet played inside the house. They played hide and seek, and Buddy's loud bark made them laugh. Finally, Buddy's mom finished her work and took them to the park. They played all day, and Buddy's soft fur was perfect for snuggling when they got tired.

Once there was a boy named Paul. He was only three years old and loved to whistle. All day long he would whistle and sing and the birds in the trees would chirp along. One day Paul found something very special. It was a unique little chain. It glinted and sparkled in the sun. Paul was so excited, he started to whistle louder and louder. He twirled and danced, the chain clinking and sparkling as he moved. Paul quickly made the chain his own, wearing it around his neck proudly. From then on, he whistled even more, enjoying the musical, twinkling sound the chain made with each step.
