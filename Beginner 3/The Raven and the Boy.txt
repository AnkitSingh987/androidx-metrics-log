Category: Beginner
Title: The Raven and the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a big raven. The raven was very reliable. He had many friends because he always helped them. One day, the raven met a little boy. The boy was sad. The raven asked the boy, "Why are you sad?" The boy said, "I lost my toy. Can you help me find it?" The raven agreed to help. They looked everywhere for the toy. The raven had an idea. He would raise his wings and fly up high to see the toy from the sky. As the raven flew up high, he saw a big surprise. The toy was not lost. The boy's mom had it! She was hiding it for a fun game. The raven told the boy, and they both laughed. The boy was happy again, and the raven felt good for helping his new friend.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a firework in the park. He picked it up and wanted to touch it. Tim's friend, Sue, saw him and said, "No, don't touch the firework! It can hurt you!" Tim felt upset. He didn't want to get hurt, but he wanted to see the firework go up in the sky. He asked Sue, "What can we do with the firework?" Sue thought for a moment and said, "Let's ask a grown-up for help. They know what to do with fireworks." Tim agreed and they found a grown-up to help them. The grown-up helped them set up the firework safely. That night, Tim, Sue, and the grown-up watched the firework go up into the sky. It made big, bright colors and they all smiled. Tim was happy he didn't touch the firework and that they could still see it light up the sky.

Once upon a time there was a very busy dad. He loved to mail letters. And every day he would go to work and mail lots of letters. One day, a curious three year old asked her dad why he mails so many letters. She watched on curiously as he worked away. Her dad replied with a tender smile: "I mail letters because I want to make lots of people happy. That makes me feel really good!" The little girl smiled and said, "Then I will mail lots of letters too!" Her dad chuckled and said, "You can help me and mail some letters with me. That will make me feel even better!" The little girl nodded and got busy helping her dad mail out all of the letters. Together they made lots of people happy by sharing love through mail. The end.

One day, a little otter wanted to sell his toys. He was very impatient, so he went to the market. There, he met a big bear who was also selling things. "Hello, big bear. I want to sell my toys," said the otter. "Okay, little otter. Put your toys here with mine," said the big bear. So the otter put his toys with the bear's things. Many people came and bought things. The otter was happy because he sold all his toys. At the end of the day, the otter thanked the bear and they became good friends.

One day, a grumpy cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big horn on the ground. Tom was curious and picked it up. He thought it was a fun toy to play with. Tom met his friend, a dog named Max. "Look at my new toy," Tom said to Max. Max looked at the horn and said, "That's not a toy, it's a horn!" Tom didn't care and started to play with it. As Tom played with the horn, he accidentally pressed it and it made a loud noise. "Yell!" Tom said, surprised by the sound. Max laughed and said, "I told you it's a horn!" Just then, a big group of birds flew out of a nearby tree. They were scared by the loud noise. Tom and Max were surprised, but they laughed and played with the horn together.

Once upon a time, there was a happy little mouse named Tim. Tim lived in a small house near a big tree. He liked to play with his friends in the sun. One day, Tim saw a big piece of cheese on the ground. He wanted to eat it, but he was scared. He thought it might be a trap. His friend, a wise old owl, told him to trust his heart. Tim listened to his heart and did not eat the cheese. He went back to his small house and played with his friends. They were all very happy, and Tim knew he had made the right choice.
