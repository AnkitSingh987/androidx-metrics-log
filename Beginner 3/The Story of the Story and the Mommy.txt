Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Story and the Mommy

Once upon a time, there was a family of 3. They had a mommy, a daddy and a little boy. One day, the little boy said, "Mommy, I want something fancy!" Mommy smiled and said, "Let's go find something fancy." The family went out to look for something fancy and they stumbled upon a store filled with glittering gold. The little boy's eyes widened. Daddy said, "We're not here to buy this gold. Let's go find something else." The family left, but the little boy couldn't stop thinking about the gold. So the mommy told him a story. "A long time ago, there were two best friends. One day the first friend found a sack full of gold. He wanted to keep all the gold to himself, so he didn't share it with his best friend. But his friend prevented him from doing it because he knew that wouldn't be fair. The little boy nodded as he listened to the story. Mommy said, "See, it pays to be fair and share with others. That's why it's important to be generous and kind to others. That way, everyone can be happy!" The moral of the story is to always be kind and generous to others. Sharing is the best way to prevent greed.

One day, there was a small boy named Tim. Tim had a desk in his room. He was glad when he saw it. He liked to draw on his desk. He had a big box with many colors. Tim's mom told him not to draw on the desk. But Tim did not listen. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came to play. Sam saw the desk with drawings. He said, "Wow, Tim! You have a fun desk!" Tim was happy that Sam liked his desk. They played and drew on the desk together. Later, Tim's mom came into the room. She saw the drawings on the desk. Tim's mom was not glad. She said, "Tim, I told you not to draw on the desk." Tim was sad. He knew he should listen to his mom. He said, "I am sorry, Mom. I will clean it." Tim and Sam cleaned the desk. They recognized it was better to listen to Tim's mom. They were glad to have a clean desk again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He lived in a comfortable house with his mom, dad, and his dog, Max. Tim loved to play with Max in their big yard. One day, Tim's mom told him that if he cleaned his room, he would get a reward. Tim wanted the reward, so he tried his best to clean his room. But he didn't put away his toys and left a mess. Tim's mom saw the mess and had to punish him. She said, "No playing with Max until your room is clean." Tim felt sad but knew he had to clean his room. He picked up his toys and put them away. When his room was finally clean, his mom gave him the reward - a big, tasty ice cream cone. Tim was happy and learned that doing a good job brings rewards, and not doing it can bring punishment. He enjoyed his ice cream with Max in their comfortable yard.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a candle in her room. She thought it was pretty and wanted to show her friend, Tom. Lily went to Tom's house and said, "Look at my pretty candle!" Tom looked at the candle and said, "I am sorry, but I don't think it is pretty." Lily was sad, but she still valued her candle. They played with other toys and had fun. Later, Lily's mom came to pick her up. When they got home, Lily's mom saw the candle and said, "Oh, this is a magic candle! When you light it, something special happens." Lily was so surprised and happy. She knew her candle was special and she valued it even more.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty train. The train had a big, strong engine. It could go very fast. The train loved to go on long trips. One day, the train needed more coal to keep going. The train's driver put a lot of coal into the engine. The train was very happy. It could go on its trip now. As the train went on its trip, it tasted the coal. The coal made the train feel strong. The train went faster and faster. The train was so happy to be mighty and fast. And they all had a great trip.
