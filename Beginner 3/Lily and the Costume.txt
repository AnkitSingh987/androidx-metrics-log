Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Costume

Lily wanted to sleep in her costume. She liked her costume very much. It was a gray cat costume with a tail and ears. She wore it for Halloween and had fun. But Mom said no. She said Lily had to take off her costume and put on her pajamas. She said costumes were for playing, not for sleeping. Lily was sad and angry. She did not want to take off her costume. She wanted to be a cat all night. She said to Mom, "Meow! Meow! I am a cat, not a girl!" Mom smiled and hugged Lily. She said, "You are a very cute cat, but you are also my girl. And my girl needs to sleep well and be warm and cozy. You can wear your costume again tomorrow, but now it is bedtime." Lily pouted and whimpered. She did not like Mom's words. She wanted to sleep in her costume. But she was also tired and sleepy. She knew Mom loved her and wanted her to be happy. She sighed and nodded. She took off her costume and put on her pajamas. Mom tucked her in and kissed her goodnight. She said, "I love you, Lily. Sweet dreams, my cat-girl." Lily closed her eyes and snuggled with her stuffed cat. She still felt like a cat, but she also felt like a girl. She said to Mom, "I love you, Mom. Meow, meow." She fell asleep and dreamed of cats.

One day, a blue car and a red car wanted to race. They were very excited. The blue car said, "I am very fast! I will win!" The red car said, "No, I am faster! I will win!" The race started. The blue car went very fast. The red car went very fast too. They both wanted to win the race. The cars went up and down hills, and around turns. The blue car and red car were having fun racing each other. At the end of the race, they watched a video of their race. They saw how fast they went and how much fun they had. The blue car said, "We both did a great job!" The red car agreed, "Yes, we did. Let's race again soon!"

One day, a boy named Tim went to the doctor with his mom. The doctor said, "Tim, we need to take an x-ray to see your bones." Tim was a little scared but his mom said it would be mild and not hurt. As they waited for the x-ray, Tim could hear a dog barking. He asked his mom, "What is that sound?" His mom said, "It is a dog, Tim. Dogs come to the doctor too." When it was time for the x-ray, Tim went into the room. He was ready for the mild x-ray. But, when the doctor turned on the machine, they saw the dog from before! The dog had run into the room and jumped on the x-ray table. Tim and his mom laughed, and the doctor took the x-ray of the dog first. Then, it was Tim's turn, and the x-ray was very mild, just like his mom said.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw green grass and some harmless bugs. Tim liked to watch the bugs crawl on the grass. He believed they were his friends. A big girl named Sue came to the park. She said, "I don't like bugs. They are yucky!" Tim said, "But they are harmless. They don't hurt anyone." Sue did not believe Tim. She wanted to step on the bugs. Suddenly, the bugs started to dance on the grass. Sue was very surprised. She never saw bugs dance before. Tim said, "See? They are not yucky. They are fun!" Sue started to believe Tim. They both sat on the grass and watched the bugs dance together.

One day a little girl called Lucy went to the park with her mom. It was a lovely day and Lucy skipped around and played. Suddenly she heard a loud noise like thunder coming from near the swings. Lucy ran towards the sound and was surprised to see a baby rabbit stirring up a shower of leaves. The rabbit was frightened and tried to run away, but the poor animal couldn't move! Lucy decided to help the animal and gently scooped it up in her arm. She said "Don't worry little one, you're safe now!". The rabbit seemed to relax a little and Lucy carried it home. At home, Lucy showed mom the rabbit. Mom was so happy that Lucy had been so kind and helped the animal. Mom said, "We will take care of the rabbit, it's so lovely that you wanted to help". The little rabbit was named 'Fuzzy' and from then on was part of the family. Lucy and Fuzzy became the best of friends and learned to love each other deeply. The end.
