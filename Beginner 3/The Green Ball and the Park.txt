Category: Beginner
Title: The Green Ball and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a big green ball. It was a modern ball because it could roll all by itself. The green ball had many friends who liked to play with it. One day, the green ball went to the park. It rolled and rolled down the hill. All the kids saw the green ball and wanted to play with it. They took turns rolling the ball and laughing as it went up and down the hill. At the end of the day, the green ball was tired from all the rolling. It said goodbye to its friends and rolled back home. The green ball was very happy it had such a fun day at the park.

One warm day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a gum on the ground. He picked it up and put it in his pocket. Tim liked to find things when he walked. Today, he found a gum. As Tim walked, he saw a big tree. He wanted to climb it. Tim put his foot on a branch and started to climb. But as he climbed, the tree began to spring! Tim was surprised. The tree was not a tree. It was a big, friendly giant! The giant said, "Hi, Tim! I am a Gum Giant. I lost my gum. Can you help me find it?" Tim smiled and gave the gum from his pocket to the giant. The giant was so happy. Tim and the Gum Giant became best friends and played together all day.

One day, a girl named Lily found a big blue key. She did not know what it could unlock, but she wanted to find out. So, she walked and walked, looking for something that the key could open. As Lily walked, she saw a big blue boat with a sail. She thought, "Maybe the key can unlock this boat!" She tried the key, and the door opened! Lily went inside the boat and found a big blue map. The map showed a secret island with a big X on it. Lily wanted to go there. Lily sailed the boat to the island. When she got there, she saw a big blue box with a lock. She used the key to unlock the box. Inside, she found a big blue bird! The bird said, "Thank you for unlocking my box! I am a magic bird, and I can grant you one wish!" Lily thought for a moment and said, "I wish for a new friend to play with." The magic bird smiled and turned into a little girl. Lily and her new friend played all day long on the secret island.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a toy cat named Mr. Whiskers. Lucy loved Mr. Whiskers very much. They played together every day. One day, Lucy was playing with Mr. Whiskers near a big tree. Lucy was stubborn and wanted to climb the tree with Mr. Whiskers. She held Mr. Whiskers with one arm and started to climb. It was hard to climb with only one arm, but Lucy did not want to let go of her toy. Lucy's big brother, Sam, saw her climbing the tree. He wanted to prevent Lucy from falling. Sam ran to the tree and said, "Lucy, stop! It's not safe!" Lucy listened to Sam and came down from the tree. Sam gave Lucy a big hug and they played together with Mr. Whiskers on the ground.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily was a good girl who liked to help her mom and dad. One day, Lily saw a phone on the table. She wanted to be helpful, so she picked up the phone. Lily went to her mom and said, "Look, Mom! I found a phone!" Her mom was not happy. She said, "Lily, that is not your phone. You should not touch things that are not yours." Lily felt sad because she wanted to gain her mom's love by being helpful. She went to her room and cried. Lily learned that touching things that are not hers can make people upset. From that day on, she promised to be more careful.
