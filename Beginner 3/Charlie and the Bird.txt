Category: Beginner
Title: Charlie and the Bird

Charlie was a little cat who loved to explore his world. One day he came across a lime tree, which had lots of juicy limes. He wanted to eat the limes but he couldn’t reach them. He was very sad and he didn’t know what to do. Then one day, he saw a bird in the tree. The bird was feeding on the limes and was having a great time. Charlie asked the bird to help him and after some negotiation, the bird agreed. She took an original lime from the tree and gave it to Charlie. Charlie was so happy and thanked the bird. He learnt a valuable lesson; that when you can’t do something yourself, it's always good to ask for help.

Once upon a time, two friends, John and Sam, were playing together. John had a big rocket, and Sam wanted to try it out. "Can I have a go on your rocket John?" Sam asked. But John didn't want to share his big rocket, so he disagreed. Crestfallen, Sam asked, "Please can I just have one turn?" John thought for a moment, and then he said "Okay, but just one turn." So Sam took the rocket and flew it high into the sky. He flew it so high and so fast that soon he had gone too far. John was scared that he had lost his friend so he tried to run after him. But John was too slow. Just when he was about to give up hope, John heard a loud boom: the rocket had come back to earth and landed safely next to him. John had a big smile on his face. He was so happy that his friend was safe. He called out to Sam, "Let's never disagree again!" And they were friends forever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a clean puzzle on the floor. He wanted to play with it. He started to imagine what the picture on the puzzle would look like when it was done. He thought it would be a big, happy sun. Tim's friend, Sue, came over to play. She looked at the puzzle and said, "I think it's a big, green tree!" Tim and Sue started to put the puzzle together. They were excited to see who was right. As they put the last piece in, they saw that the picture was not a sun or a tree. It was a big, funny clown! Tim and Sue laughed and laughed. They did not expect that! They had fun playing with the puzzle all day.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the park. They saw a big bin near the trees. The cat said, "Let's go see what's inside the bin!" The dog agreed and they both went to the bin. As they got closer, a magic bird appeared! The bird was clear like glass. The cat and dog were very surprised. The bird said, "Hello, I am a magic bird. I can make one wish come true for you. What do you want?" The cat and dog thought for a moment. Then the cat said, "We want to be best friends forever!" The magic bird smiled and with a wave of its wing, made their wish come true. From that day on, the cat and dog were always together, and they never forgot the clear magic bird who made their wish come true.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to play with her toy stack. She would take the rings and put them on the stick, one by one. The stack had many colors, like red, blue, and yellow. One day, Amy got a new toy. It was a modern phone that could ring. She was so happy. She played with the phone and made it ring all day. She would pick it up and say "Hello!" and then laugh. Amy still loved her stack, too. She thought of a fun game. She would make the phone ring, then run to her stack and put a ring on it. She played this game all day long. Amy was very happy with her toys and her new game.
