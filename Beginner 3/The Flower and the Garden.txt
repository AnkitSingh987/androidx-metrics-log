Category: Beginner
Title: The Flower and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a big flower. It wanted to bloom and be pretty. One day, a little bee came by. The bee said, "Hello, big flower! When will you bloom?" The big flower said, "I will bloom soon! I want to be the prettiest flower in the garden." Then one day, a big wind came. It was a tornado! The tornado was very strong. It blew all the flowers away. But the big flower held on tight to the ground. The next day, the sun came out. The big flower finally bloomed. It was the prettiest flower in the garden. The little bee came back and said, "Wow, big flower! You are so pretty now!" And they were both very happy.

Lily and Ben were playing in the attic. They liked to find old things and pretend they were new. Lily found a box of makeup. She opened it and saw many colors and brushes. "Look, Ben, I found makeup! Let's play dress up!" Lily said. Ben looked at the makeup and made a face. He did not like makeup. He liked cars and robots. He saw another box next to the makeup. He opened it and saw a mask. It was a scary mask. It had big teeth and red eyes. It looked like a monster. "Look, Lily, I found a mask! Let's play scare!" Ben said. Lily looked at the mask and screamed. She did not like scary things. She liked flowers and dolls. She dropped the makeup and ran away. "Wait, Lily, it's just a mask! It's not real!" Ben said. He put on the mask and followed Lily. He wanted to make her laugh. He did not know the mask was old and rotten. It smelled bad and had bugs in it. He felt something crawl on his face. He screamed and took off the mask. "Yuck, yuck, yuck! This mask is rotten! It has bugs in it!" Ben said. He threw the mask away and ran after Lily. He wanted to hug her and say sorry. He did not want to play scare anymore. He wanted to play dress up with her. He hoped she would not be mad at him. He hoped she would still be his friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to see her uncle. Her uncle lived in a big house with a big garden. Lily and her uncle liked to play in the garden. One day, Lily and her uncle found a fruit tree. The fruit on the tree was red and tasty. They picked the fruit and ate it. Lily said, "Yum! This is very tasty!" Her uncle smiled and agreed. After they ate the fruit, Lily and her uncle played more in the garden. They saw butterflies and birds. They had a fun day together. Lily was very happy to see her uncle and play in the garden.

One day, a clumsy girl named Sue went to play. She wore her favorite skirt. She met her friend, Tom. They wanted to do something fun. Tom said, "Let's do a race!" Sue was excited. But she was worried about her skirt. It was long and might make her fall. They started to race, and Sue ran fast. Sue's skirt got in the way. She fell down. Tom stopped and helped her up. Sue said, "I knew my skirt would make me fall!" They laughed and decided to play a different game. Sue learned to pick a better skirt for racing next time.

One day, there was a bunny called Bob. Bob was hopping around in the garden and he was having lots of fun. He saw a tree with lots of apples on it and he hopped closer to have a look. Suddenly, Bob realised he couldn't reach the apples! So, he spotted a cool frog nearby and he hopping towards it. The frog introduced himself as Fred. Bob asked Fred for help. Bob said "Fred, I need help. Can you please help me?" Fred replied "Sure, I can help you. Just give me your arm!" So Bob did and Fred held it with his cool, slimy frog hands. With Bob's help, Fred was able to grab the apples off the tree for Bob. Bob was so happy and he thanked Fred lots. He gave Fred a great big hug and hopped away happily into the sunshine.
