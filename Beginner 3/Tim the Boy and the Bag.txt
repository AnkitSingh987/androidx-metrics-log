Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Bag

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big, empty bag. He wanted to fill it with fun things to play with. Tim walked around his house, looking for toys to put in his bag. Tim's mom saw him and said, "I suggest you look in your room, Tim. There are many toys there." Tim went to his room and found his toy cars, blocks, and stuffed animals. He put them all in his big, empty bag. The bag was not empty anymore! Tim carried his bag to the park. He met his friend, Sue. They played with the toys from the bag all day. They had so much fun! When it was time to go home, Tim and Sue put the toys back in the bag. Tim's mom was happy to see him having fun with his friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a baby tiger in his yard. The tiger was quiet and nice. Tim wanted to keep the tiger, but he did not know if his mom would let him. "Mom, can I keep the tiger?" Tim asked. His mom was scared at first, but she saw that the tiger was quiet and did not make any noise. She said, "Yes, you can keep the tiger, but you must keep it quiet so it does not scare the other kids." Tim and the baby tiger played all day. They had so much fun, but the baby tiger started to make noise. The other kids heard the noise and got scared. They did not want to play with Tim and the baby tiger anymore. Tim was sad, but he knew he had to keep the tiger quiet. He gave the baby tiger a big hug and told it to be quiet. The baby tiger listened to Tim and stayed quiet. The other kids saw that the tiger was quiet and not scary, so they started to play with Tim and the baby tiger again. Tim was happy that he could keep his quiet tiger and play with his friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tom. Tom had a fancy flag that he loved very much. The flag was red, blue, and yellow. He would play with it every day. One day, Tom could not find his flag. He searched in his room, under his bed, and in his toy box. He asked his mom, "Mom, have you seen my fancy flag?" His mom said, "No, Tom, I have not seen it. Let's search together." Tom and his mom searched all around the house. They looked in the kitchen, the living room, and even the bathroom. Finally, they found the flag in the backyard. Tom was so happy! He said, "Thank you, Mom, for helping me search for my fancy flag." They went inside and hung the flag in Tom's room, so he would always know where it was.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy dog named Max. Max loved to play with his cone toy. One day, Max was playing with his cone when it suddenly broke. Max was very sad. Max went to his friend, a little girl named Lily. "My cone broke," said Max. Lily saw how sad Max was and wanted to help him. "Don't worry, Max. We can fix your cone," said Lily. Lily found some tape and helped Max fix his cone. Max was so happy to play with his cone again. He wasn't grumpy anymore. Max and Lily played together with the cone, and they both had lots of fun. From that day on, Max and Lily were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a smooth rock. The rock lived in a big park. The rock was very happy. Every day, the sun would shine on the rock. The rock would get warm and feel good. One day, the sun did not come out at noon. The rock felt cold and sad. The rock started to complain. "I want the sun to come back," said the rock. "I don't like being cold." A little bird heard the rock complain. The bird flew down and sat on the rock. "Don't be sad," said the bird. "The sun will come back soon. We can be friends until then." The rock smiled and felt a little better. They talked and laughed until the sun came back.
