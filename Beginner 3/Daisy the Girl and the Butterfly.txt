Category: Beginner
Title: Daisy the Girl and the Butterfly

One day, a little girl named Daisy had a dream. In her dream, she saw a charming butterfly. The butterfly was very pretty, with big wings of many colors. Daisy loved the butterfly and wanted to play with it. Daisy asked the butterfly, "Can we be friends?" The butterfly said, "Yes, but first, you must be kind to all the bugs you meet." Daisy agreed, and they played all day. They flew in the sky, and they danced in the sun. Daisy was so happy with her new friend. When Daisy woke up, she remembered her dream. She went outside and saw many bugs in her garden. She was kind to all of them, just like the butterfly told her. Soon, many charming butterflies came to play with Daisy. She learned that being kind to others brings happiness and friends.

Once upon a time, there was a small cat named Tiny. Tiny lived in a big house with his best friend, a dog named Max. They played together all day long. One day, Tiny found a file on the floor. Tiny did not know what it was. He showed it to Max. Max said, "I think it is a toy!" But Tiny disagreed. He thought it was something else. They took the file to their friend, a wise old owl. The owl said, "This is a file. It is used to make things smooth." Tiny and Max were happy to learn something new. They played with the file and had fun all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Mandy. She was very excited to go to the store with her mom that morning. As they were walking, they heard somebody rush by them. It was a crazy dog! The dog was running so fast that Mandy couldn't even see him. Mandy's mom called out to the dog, and it stopped. She asked the dog what his name was, but the dog just wagged his tail and kept running. Mandy was amazed that she had seen the dog, but she was sad that she didn't find out his name. Then Mandy had an idea. She ran after the dog, trying to catch up with him. She ran and ran, but she just couldn't catch him. After a while she was so tired that she had to stop! Mandy wanted to know the dog's name so badly, but she was unable to find out. Mandy and her mom continued on to the store, but Mandy still wondered what the crazy dog's name was. She still wishes she could've known, but it seems like it will remain a mystery forever!

One day, a charming little girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a big tree and sat under it. She saw a small bird in the tree. The bird looked sad. Lily asked, "Why are you sad, bird?" The bird said, "I need to send a letter to my friend, but I can't fly. My wing is hurt." Lily wanted to help the bird. She took the letter and said, "I will send it for you!" Lily walked and walked to find the bird's friend. She met a big dog on the way. The dog was hungry and wanted to eat the letter. But, Lily said, "No! This letter is for the bird's friend." The big dog felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I didn't know." Then, the dog helped Lily find the bird's friend. They sent the letter together. The bird's friend was very happy. The moral of the story is to help others, even when it's hard. And, always be kind to everyone you meet.

Mia and Leo were twins who liked to play together. One day, they found a big box in the attic. They opened it and saw many old things inside. They saw a doll, a hat, a book, and a blanket. Mia picked up the doll and said, "Look, Leo, a baby!" Leo looked at the doll and said, "That's not a baby, that's a doll. Babies are alive and cry and eat and sleep. Dolls are not alive and do not cry or eat or sleep." Mia said, "But this doll looks like a baby. It has eyes and a nose and a mouth and hair. Maybe it was a baby once and became a doll." Leo said, "That's silly, Mia. How can a baby become a doll? That's impossible." Mia said, "Maybe it was a magic baby. Maybe someone cast a spell on it and made it a doll. Maybe it was a mysterious baby." Leo said, "A magic baby? A mysterious baby? You have a big imagination, Mia. But I don't believe you. Let me see the doll." Mia gave the doll to Leo and he held it in his arms. He looked at it closely and saw that it was very old and dirty. He said, "This doll is not magic or mysterious. It's just old and dirty. It needs a bath and some new clothes." Mia said, "Maybe you're right, Leo. Maybe it's just a doll. But I still like it. Can we keep it?" Leo said, "OK, Mia. We can keep it. But we have to ask Mom and Dad first. And we have to clean it and find a name for it." Mia said, "OK, Leo. Let's go ask Mom and Dad. And let's call it Lily. Lily the doll." Leo said, "OK, Mia. Lily the doll. I like that name. Come on, let's go."
