Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Fish and the Boy

Once there was a little boy who was very excited. He wanted to go for a swim in the ocean, but his mom kept insisting that he stay on the reef. The reef was close to the shore, so the boy could still see the ocean. He looked out at the blue water, full of fish and colourful coral. He was so glad that he could still be close to the sea. The little boy kept asking his mom if he could go in the water, but she kept insisting that he stay on the reef. She wanted him to be safe. She said that if he stayed out of the deep water he wouldn't get too wet. But the boy wasn't convinced; he wanted to experience the sea. Finally, his mom agreed that he could go for a swim, but only if he stayed close to the reef. She said that if he needed her help he just had to call out. The boy agreed and was so glad that he could go for a swim. He had a wonderful time splashing around and playing in the shallow water. He even managed to get a little wet, much to his mum's surprise. He had a wonderful day and when it was time to go back to shore, he was very glad to have spent his day in the ocean.

Once upon a time, there was a poor chicken. She wanted to go to the big barn but she didn't know where it was. She asked her friend, a cow, if he could help her. The cow said, "No problem! Just follow me. I can see the barn, it isn't far." The chicken excitedly hopped after the cow and soon, they could see the big barn. Inside the barn, there were many chickens. All of them were very happy and clucking excitedly. The cow smiled at the chicken and said, "You see? We made it here!" The chicken was so happy to be with her new friends! From that day on, she was never poor again.

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a walk. She saw a wild bird sitting on a twig. The bird looked sad. Sue said, "Why are you sad, bird?" The bird said, "I lost my friend. He flew away and I can't see him." Sue wanted to help the bird. They looked for the bird's friend together. They looked high and low, but they could not see him. Then, something unexpected happened. Sue tripped on a twig and fell. But when she looked up, she saw the bird's friend! He was hiding in a bush. The wild bird was so happy to see his friend again. Sue felt good for helping the bird. They all played together and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Will. He liked to examine things. He was a reliable friend. One day, Will went to the park with his friends. They played with a ball. They had lots of fun. But then, the ball rolled into the woods. Will and his friends went to find the ball. They looked and looked. They found a big, shiny rock. It was not the ball, but it was fun to look at. Then, the rock started to move! It was not a rock at all! It was a big turtle! The turtle had the ball! Will and his friends were so surprised. They laughed and played with the turtle. They had a great day.

One day, it started to rain. A little girl named Mia was inside her house. She looked out the window and saw the rain coming down. She wanted to play outside, but she had to wait until the rain stopped. Mia's mom was making a special treat in the kitchen. It was a yummy cream. Mia asked if she could try some. Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful, it's spicy!" Mia took a little taste and her mouth felt hot. She drank some water to cool down. When the rain stopped, Mia went outside to play. She jumped in the puddles and had lots of fun. After playing, she went back inside and her mom gave her some more cream, but this time it was not spicy. Mia smiled and enjoyed her special treat.
