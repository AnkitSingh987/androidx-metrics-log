Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Grass

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a shy boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his red flag. He would wave it up and down, and run with it in the wind. He was very happy when he played with his flag. One sunny day, Tim went to the park to play with his flag. He saw a big tree and decided to lay under it. He lay on the soft green grass, and the warm sun shone on his face. He felt very relaxed and soon fell asleep. While Tim was sleeping, a little girl named Sue came to the park. She saw Tim's red flag and thought it was pretty. Sue picked up the flag and started to play with it. When Tim woke up, he saw Sue with his flag. He was a bit shy, but he walked up to her and asked if they could play together. Sue smiled and said yes. They became good friends and played with the red flag together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a peaceful house with her mommy and daddy. Lily loved flowers. Her favorite flower was the white one that grew in her garden. One sunny day, Lily went outside to look at the flowers. She saw a little bee flying around. The bee said, "Hello, Lily! Can you help me find more flowers?" Lily thought for a moment and then said, "I suggest we look near the big tree." Lily and the bee walked to the big tree. There, they found many beautiful flowers. The bee was so happy, and Lily was happy too. From that day on, Lily and the bee became best friends, and they always played together in the peaceful garden.

Tom and Anna were playing in the snow. They made a big snowman with a hat, a scarf and a carrot nose. They gave him two buttons for eyes and a smile. They were very happy with their snowman. But then, a big boy came and kicked their snowman. He broke his hat, his scarf and his nose. He laughed and ran away. Tom and Anna were very sad and angry. They wanted to stop him. "Hey, you!" Tom shouted. "Why did you do that? That was our snowman!" The big boy turned around and saw Tom and Anna. He was not scared of them. He was bigger and stronger. He had a mean face and a big glove. "So what?" he shouted back. "It's just snow. I can do what I want. You are too small and weak to stop me." He threw a snowball at Tom and hit him in the face. Tom fell down and cried. Anna ran to help him. She was brave and humble. She did not like the big boy. "Leave us alone!" she shouted. "You are not nice. You are mean and rude. You should say sorry and help us fix our snowman." The big boy laughed again. He did not care. He threw another snowball at Anna. But this time, he missed. The snowball flew over Anna's head and hit a tree. The tree shook and dropped a lot of snow on the big boy. He was covered in snow. He could not see or move. He was cold and scared. Tom and Anna looked at him. They felt sorry for him. They were not mean like him. They were kind and good. They decided to help him. They went to him and took off the snow. They helped him stand up and gave him his glove. They asked him if he was okay. He was surprised and ashamed. He did not expect them to help him. He realized he was wrong. "I'm sorry," he said. "I was mean and rude. I should not have kicked your snowman. You are nice and good. Can you forgive me?" Tom and Anna smiled. They forgave him. They asked him if he wanted to play with them. He said yes. He wanted to be their friend. He helped them fix their snowman. They gave him a hug. They played together in the snow. They were happy.
