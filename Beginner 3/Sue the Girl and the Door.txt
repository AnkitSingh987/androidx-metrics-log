Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was very independent. She liked to do things all by herself. One day, Sue found a key on the ground. She was so excited! Sue wanted to find out what the key could open. She tried the key on many doors, but it did not work. Sue was sad, but she did not give up. She kept looking for the right door. Finally, Sue found a small door in her room. The key fit! Inside, she found a pretty dress. But the dress had a small tear. Sue knew just what to do. She got her mom's sewing kit and fixed the tear all by herself. Sue was so happy and proud of her independent self!

One day, a big dog named Max went for a walk on a trail. He liked to run and play in the sun. Max saw a small cat named Tom sitting on a large rock. Tom was sad because he could not find his way home. Max said, "Don't be sad, Tom. I can help you find your home." Tom smiled and they both started to walk. They walked and walked, and then they saw a large tree. Max and Tom heard a funny sound coming from the tree. They looked up and saw a big bird laughing. The big bird said, "You two look lost! Can I help you find your way?" Max and Tom looked at each other and laughed too. They did not know that the big bird was Tom's friend, and he knew where Tom's home was. The big bird led Max and Tom to Tom's home, and they all laughed and played together.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely bird. The bird liked to fly and soar high in the sky. The bird saw many things while flying. One day, the bird saw a pretty pattern on the ground. The pattern was made of rocks. The bird wanted to make a new friend. So, the bird thought, "I will make a friend with this pattern!" The bird flew down to the ground to play with the pattern. But, something unexpected happened. When the bird got close to the pattern, it saw that the rocks were not just rocks. They were little birds! The little birds were making the pretty pattern. The lonely bird was happy because now it had many friends to soar with in the sky.

Once, there was a little rocket. It wanted to become big and fly high in the sky. Other rockets were big and fast. The little rocket was envious of them. One day, the little rocket met a wise bird. The bird told the little rocket about a magic button. The bird said, "Press the button and you will become big and fast." The little rocket was very happy. The next day, the little rocket found the magic button. It pressed the button and became big and fast. The little rocket was ready to fly high. But, the twist was that the magic button made all the other rockets small and slow. Now, the little rocket was the biggest and fastest of them all.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to tease his sister, Sue. One day, they went to play outside in the west part of their yard. Sue found it difficult to keep up with Tim because he was very fast. "Wait for me, Tim!" Sue said. But Tim didn't listen and kept running. He wanted to tease Sue more by hiding behind a big tree. He thought he could jump out and scare her when she walked by. As Tim hid behind the tree, he saw a big dog. The dog was not happy to see Tim in his yard. The dog barked loudly and started to chase Tim. Tim tried to run away, but the dog was faster. "Help me, Sue!" Tim yelled. But Sue was too far away to hear him. The dog caught up with Tim and bit his leg. Tim cried and wished he had not teased Sue. Now, he knew it was not nice to tease others.
