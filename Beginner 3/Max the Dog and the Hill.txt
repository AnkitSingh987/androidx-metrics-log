Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Dog and the Hill

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max lived in a big city with lots of traffic. He loved to run and play in the park every day. One day, Max saw a big, difficult hill. He wanted to climb it, but he was scared. Max's friend, the sun, told him, "Do not worry, Max! I will be here to help you." The next day, Max went to the big, difficult hill. He started to climb it. It was hard, but Max did not give up. As he climbed, the sun went behind a cloud. Max began to shiver. He was cold and scared. He thought about giving up, but then he remembered what the sun had said. Max kept climbing the big, difficult hill. He shivered and shook, but he did not give up. Finally, Max reached the top of the hill. The sun came out from behind the cloud and warmed Max up. Max was so happy! He knew that with a little help from his friend, he could do anything. And from that day on, Max was never afraid to climb big, difficult hills.

One day, a boy named Tom had a big, heavy bag. He needed to take it to work with him. But the bag was so heavy, he could not carry it. Tom felt sad and did not know what to do. Tom's friend, Sue, saw him and wanted to help. She had an idea! Sue found a long zip and put it on the bag. She said, "Let's zip the bag and make it smaller. Then it will not be so heavy." They zipped the bag and it became smaller. Tom could now carry it to work. He was very happy and thankful to Sue. They went to work together and had a fun day. The heavy bag was not a problem anymore.

One day, a dog named Spot found a perfect bone. Spot was very happy. He wanted to own the bone and keep it forever. He picked up the bone in his mouth and ran to show his friend, Max. Spot said, "Look, Max! I found a perfect bone! I want to own it and keep it forever!" Max looked at the bone and smiled. He said, "That is a nice bone, Spot. But I have a surprise for you!" Max took Spot to a big tree. Under the tree, there were many bones. Spot was very surprised. He didn't know there were so many bones in the world! Max said, "You can own all these bones, Spot! They are all perfect, just like yours!" Spot was so happy. He and Max played with the bones all day. They shared the bones and had lots of fun. And from that day on, Spot and Max were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly ghost named Boo. Boo loved to have fun and play with his friends. One day, Boo saw a little girl named Lily who was sad. Boo went to Lily and said, "Hi, I am Boo. Why are you sad?" Lily looked at Boo and said, "I don't have any friends to play with." Boo smiled and said, "I will be your friend! Let's play together." Boo and Lily played all day and had lots of fun. They laughed and shared their toys. Soon, other kids saw how much fun Boo and Lily were having. They wanted to play too. In the end, Lily had many friends to play with, and Boo was very happy. The moral of the story is: when you are kind and share, you will have many friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a brave dog named Max. Max lived on a street with many trees and flowers. He loved to play with his friends in the park. One sunny day, Max saw a big ball. He wanted to balance the ball on his nose. He tried and tried, but the ball kept falling down. Max's friend, a little bird named Lily, flew by and saw him trying. "Max, you are so brave to try!" said Lily. "I can help you balance the ball." So, Lily held the ball on Max's nose with her tiny wings. Together, they balanced the ball and played happily in the park. All the other animals on the street saw Max and Lily playing. They clapped and cheered for the brave dog and his little friend. From that day on, Max and Lily became the best of friends and played together every day.
