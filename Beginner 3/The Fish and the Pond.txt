Category: Beginner
Title: The Fish and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a fish who lived in a small pond. The fish was very dirty and felt bad about it. The fish was not happy because the other fish did not want to play with it. The dirty fish felt embarrass. One day, a big rain came and washed the fish out of the pond. The fish was scared and did not know what to do. The rain washed the fish to a new pond. The new pond was very big and had many fish. In the big pond, the fish found out that the water was very clean. The clean water washed the filthy fish and made it shiny and pretty. The other fish in the new pond liked the shiny fish and wanted to play with it. The fish was no longer embarrass and lived happily with its new friends.

Once upon a time, in a quiet little town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy bricks. He would build tall towers and small houses with them. One day, as he was playing, he heard a tiny voice. "Hello, Tim!" the voice said. Tim looked around and saw a little mouse. The mouse asked, "Can you promise to be my friend?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, I promise to be your friend." Together, Tim and the mouse played with the bricks. They built a big house for the mouse to live in. They laughed and had so much fun. From that day on, Tim and the mouse were the best of friends, and they always kept their promise to be there for each other.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there was a little cat named Tom. Tom was a mighty cat who loved to search for things. He liked to search for his toys, his food, and his parent. One day, Tom's parent told him to search for a big red ball. Tom was very excited to find the ball. He looked under the trees, behind the rocks, and inside the bushes. But he could not find the ball. Tom was sad. Then, something unexpected happened. Tom saw a big red bird in the sky. The bird had the big red ball in its beak! Tom was very surprised. The bird saw Tom and dropped the ball. Tom was happy and took the ball back to his parent. They played with the ball all day long. And from that day on, Tom knew that sometimes, you can find things in the most surprising places.

Lily and Ben were playing hide and seek in the park. Lily was the seeker and Ben was the hider. Lily counted to ten and then opened her eyes. She looked around for Ben, but she could not see him. Where did he go? She walked around the trees and the swings, but Ben was not there. She called his name, but he did not answer. She started to feel worried. Did he vanish? She hoped he was not lost or hurt. She saw a big slide and decided to check there. Maybe Ben was hiding behind it. She ran to the slide and looked behind it. She saw a pair of eyes staring at her. They were huge and green. Lily screamed. "Boo!" Ben said, jumping out from behind the slide. He was wearing a mask that looked like a monster. He laughed and said, "I scared you, Lily! I did not vanish, I was hiding here!" Lily was not happy. She felt angry and scared. She took off the mask from Ben's face and threw it on the ground. She said, "That was not funny, Ben! That was mean! You made me worry! You have huge eyes, but you are a bad pupil!" Ben felt sorry. He saw that Lily was crying. He said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I did not mean to make you cry. I just wanted to play a joke. You are a good seeker and a good friend. Can you forgive me?" Lily looked at Ben. He looked sad and sorry. She said, "OK, Ben. I forgive you. But don't do that again. And don't wear that mask. It is ugly and scary." Ben nodded and hugged Lily. He said, "Thank you, Lily. You are a good friend too. And you are brave and smart. Can we play again?" Lily smiled and said, "OK, Ben. We can play again. But this time, you are the seeker and I am the hider. And no masks!" They ran to the slide and started a new game. They had fun and forgot about the mask. They did not vanish, they were happy.
