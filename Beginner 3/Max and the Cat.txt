Category: Beginner
Title: Max and the Cat

One day, a dog named Max saw a cat stuck in a tree. The cat was scared and cried for help. Max wanted to rescue the cat, so he called his friend, a bird named Polly. Max was impatient and wanted to save the cat fast. Max said, "Polly, please help me rescue the cat in the tree!" Polly saw the cat and wanted to help too. Polly had an idea. She saw a pattern of branches that they could use to get the cat down. Polly told Max, "I see a pattern of branches. I will go up and push the cat down to you. Get ready to catch the cat." Max was happy and got ready to catch the cat. Polly went up the tree and pushed the cat. But, the cat did not want to be saved. The cat got mad and jumped on Max. Max and the cat fell down and got hurt. They did not become friends, and Max learned not to rescue cats who do not want help.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely cat. The cat loved to read books. One day, the cat found a big book in the park. The cat was very happy and took the book home. At home, the cat spread the book on the floor. The cat's friend, a little dog, came over to see the book. The dog said, "Wow! What a big book! Can we read it together?" The cat smiled and said, "Yes, let's read it together." So, the cat and the dog sat down and read the book. They laughed and had a lot of fun. When they finished reading, the cat said, "That was a lovely book! I am happy we read it together." The dog agreed and they both went to sleep, dreaming of more books to read.

Sara and Tom are friends. They like to play with their toys. Sara has a doll with long hair. Tom has a bear with soft fur. They share their toys and have fun. One day, Sara and Tom go to the park. They see a big dog. The dog has black and white fur. It looks friendly. Sara and Tom want to pet the dog. They ask the dog's owner if they can. The owner says yes, but he mentions something. He says the dog is great, but he is very strong. He says the dog likes to play, but he can pull hard. He says the dog is not mean, but he can bark loud. He says the dog is not a toy, but a living thing. Sara and Tom listen to the owner. They are careful with the dog. They pet him gently. They do not pull his fur. They do not make him angry. They do not scare him. The dog likes Sara and Tom. He wags his tail. He licks their faces. He plays with them. He does not bark. He does not pull. He is a good dog. Sara and Tom are happy. They thank the owner. They say goodbye to the dog. They go back to their toys. They have a new friend. They have a great day.

Once, there was a bossy girl named Sam. She loved giving orders to people and everything had to be done the way she wanted it. One day, she asked her mom if she could go to the park. Her mom thought about it and said that she would permit Sam to go. Sam was very excited and she quickly put on her shoes and grabbed her pillow. At the park, Sam had a lot of fun. She played with her friends on the swings and slides and even put her pillow down on the ground so she could take a nap. Everyone loved to play with Sam because she was so bossy and always made sure that everyone followed the rules. When it was time to go home, Sam gathered her pillow and waved goodbye to her friends. She was so excited to come back to the park again the next day!

Jim and his mom were walking in the park. He looked around and saw a lot of interesting things. Everything was so exciting! He wanted to explore and see everything. Suddenly, Jim saw a funny shape in the distance. He told his mom, “Mommy what’s that funny shape?” His mom smiled and said, “That’s a purse, Jim.” The purse was flexible and had many pockets. Jim was so amazed by this new discovery that he began to joke around. He ran towards the purse and kept spinning it around. His mom was giggling at his jokes. Jim was enjoying his time with his mom. He was having so much fun that he even forgot where he was. His mom said, “We better go now, Jim.” So Jim said goodbye to the flexible purse, and with a big smile, ran off to explore the park with his mom.
