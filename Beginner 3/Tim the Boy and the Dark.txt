Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Dark

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim was scared of the dark. Every night, he had a bad dream called a nightmare. In his nightmare, he felt helpless and alone. One day, Tim's friend, Anna, came to play. Tim told Anna about his nightmares. Anna said, "Let's point at the dark and say, 'Go away, bad dreams!' Maybe that will help." They pointed at the dark corners and said, "Go away, bad dreams!" Tim felt a little better. That night, Tim went to bed. He remembered what Anna said and pointed at the dark. He said, "Go away, bad dreams!" But the nightmare came back, and Tim felt helpless again. The pointing didn't work, and Tim was still scared of the dark.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a loyal dog named Spot. Lily and Spot were best friends. They played together every day. One day, Lily's mom made cookies. Lily loved cookies. She wanted to share them with Spot. Lily said, "Spot, do you want a cookie?" Spot barked happily. He wanted a cookie too. Lily decided to give Spot a cookie. They sat down and ate their cookies together. They were very happy. Lily and Spot played more after they ate. They were the best of friends.

---

One day, a lively cat named Max went to the grocery store. Max wanted to buy some food for his friends. Max saw a big, red apple and put it in his bag. He also found a yellow banana and some green grapes. Max was happy with his food. As Max walked home, he saw his friend, the dog named Sam. Sam looked sad. Max asked, "Why are you sad, Sam?" Sam said, "I lost my toy ball." Max wanted to help Sam find the toy ball. Max called his other friends, the bird and the frog. They all looked for Sam's toy ball. After some time, they found the toy ball under a big tree. Sam was very happy and said, "Thank you, Max and friends!" They all went to Max's house and shared the food from the grocery store. They had a fun day together.

One day, a jolly bird named Polly found a big egg. She was very happy. Polly wanted to show her friend, Tim the cat. Tim saw the egg and said, "I want the egg too!" Polly did not want to give the egg to Tim. They both wanted the egg so much. They started to fight over it. "No, it's mine!" said Polly. "No, I want it!" said Tim. In the end, the egg fell and broke. Both Polly and Tim were sad. They both started to weep. They learned that fighting was not good. They promised to be better friends and not fight over things anymore.

One day, in a big nation, there was a gifted dog named Spot. Spot could do a fun thing. He could number all the toys he had. He had many toys, and he loved to play with them. Spot had a friend named Tim, who was a little boy. They liked to play together. One day, Tim said to Spot, "I have a new toy for you. Can you number it with your other toys?" Spot was very happy and started to number his toys. He counted one, two, three, and four. But when he got to five, he saw something odd. The new toy was not a toy at all! It was a little cat. The cat said, "Hello, I am not a toy. I am a cat. My name is Whiskers." Spot and Tim were very surprised. They did not know that the new toy was a cat. They all became friends and played together every day. And Spot learned that sometimes, the best toys are not toys at all, but new friends.

One day, a charming little boy named Tim went to play with his friend, Sue. They both loved to draw and play with toys. Tim brought his big box of crayons to Sue's house. Sue saw the crayons and said, "Wow! So many colors! Can we share them?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, we can share my crayons. Let's draw a big, pretty picture together!" They both started to draw and had lots of fun. They drew a big sun, a blue sky, and a green tree. They were very happy with their picture. After they finished drawing, Sue's mom came into the room. She said, "You both did a great job! Now, it's time for a treat." Sue's mom went to the freezer and took out two cold, yummy ice cream bars. She gave one to Tim and one to Sue. They both said, "Thank you!" and enjoyed their ice cream together.
