Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Bowl

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a little boy named Tim. He loved to help his mom with tasks. One day, his mom asked him to help her in the kitchen. "Tim, let us make a delicious cake together," she said. Tim was very excited. He and his mom mixed the cake stuff in a big bowl. They put the cake stuff in the oven and waited. While they waited, Tim's mom said, "Thank you for helping me, Tim. You did a great task." When the cake was done, they took it out of the oven. It smelled so good. Tim and his mom cut a big piece for each of them. They ate the delicious cake and were very happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw a big tree with dry leaves. He picked up a leaf and held it in his palm. Tim looked at the leaf and said, "Imagine, Mom! This leaf was once green and wet. Now it's dry and brown." Mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. The leaf changed because of the sun and the wind. Now it's time for new leaves." Tim put the dry leaf on the ground and went to play with his friends. They played under the big tree all day, imagining they were pirates and the tree was their ship. As the sun went down, Tim and his friends said goodbye. Tim looked at the tree and smiled, knowing that one day, new leaves would grow and the tree would be green again.

Tom and his wife Sue went to the park with their dog Rex. Rex was big and loud, but harmless. He liked to play with other dogs and people. Tom and Sue liked to watch him run and jump. At the park, they saw a little girl with a ball. She was alone and looked sad. Rex saw the ball and wanted to play. He ran to the girl and barked. The girl was scared and cried. "Rex, no!" Tom shouted. He ran after Rex and tried to catch him. Sue followed him. They wanted to help the girl and calm Rex. "Sorry, sorry, we are sorry!" Tom said to the girl. He took the ball from Rex and gave it back to her. "Rex is harmless, he just wanted to play. He is a good dog, but sometimes he is too excited." Sue smiled at the girl and said, "Are you OK? What is your name? Where are your mom and dad?" The girl sniffed and said, "I'm Lily. I'm OK. My mom and dad are over there." She pointed to a bench where a man and a woman were sitting. They looked busy with their phones. Tom and Sue felt sorry for Lily. They asked her if she wanted to play with them and Rex. Lily nodded and smiled. She liked Rex now. He was soft and warm. She threw the ball and Rex fetched it. They had fun. Tom and Sue looked at each other and smiled. They were happy to make a new friend. They hoped Lily's mom and dad would pay more attention to her. They thought she was a sweet and brave girl. They decided to stay at the park for a while and enjoy the sun.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They had a yummy picnic with sandwiches and juice. Tim saw a tap near the big tree. He wanted to wash his hands after eating. Tim went to the tap and turned it on. But, the water came out very fast and splashed all over him. Tim's mom saw this and said, "Oh no, Tim! You are all wet!" Tim felt embarrass, but his mom just laughed and hugged him. Just then, a big, friendly dog came running to them. The dog saw Tim all wet and thought it was a game. The dog shook its fur and splashed water on Tim and his mom. They all laughed and played together, and the day turned out to be more fun than they ever thought.

Once upon a time there lived a princess who loved visiting her Kingdom's modern castle. Every day, the princess would take a stroll around the castle walls. One day, however, the princess was worried about something. She was scared of the dark shadows growing across the castle walls. "What is that?" She asked her companion, a small rabbit. The rabbit replied, "I don't know, let's go and ask the King." So off they went to the King's throne room. "Your Majesty," the princess said. "I am worried; there are shadows growing on the castle walls". The King stroked his beard and said, "Trust me, Princess; there's nothing to worry about. The shadows are just a modern way of decorating the castle." The princess smiled. The King was right - there was nothing to worry about after all. And so the Princess and the rabbit went back onto their daily strolls around the castle, happy and content.
