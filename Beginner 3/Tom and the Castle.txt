Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and the Castle

One sunny day, Tom and his mom went to the beach. Tom loved the beach. He liked to play in the sand and splash in the water. Today, Tom wanted to build a big sandcastle. He was very patient as he filled his bucket with sand and flipped it over to make the first tower. "Mom, look at my castle!" Tom said, showing his mom the sandcastle. "It's so big and strong!" A little crab came to visit Tom's sandcastle. The crab saw a tasty snack and decided to twist its way into the sandcastle. Tom watched the crab and laughed as it twisted and turned. "Hello, crab!" Tom said. "Do you like my castle?" The crab seemed to nod and then scurried away. Tom and his mom spent the whole day at the beach, playing in the sand and splashing in the water. They had so much fun, and Tom was happy that he had been patient to build his big sandcastle.

Lily loved her dog Spot. Spot was a big dog with black and white fur. He liked to run and play with Lily in the park. One day, they were playing fetch with a ball. Lily threw the ball far and Spot ran after it. But he did not see the puddle of water on the grass. He slipped and fell. He yelped and did not get up. Lily ran to Spot and saw that he was hurt. His leg was bleeding and he looked sad. Lily was scared and cried. She did not know what to do. She hugged Spot and said, "Don't worry, Spot. I will help you. You are my best friend." A man saw Lily and Spot. He was a veterinarian. He helped animals that were sick or hurt. He had a white coat and a bag. He came to Lily and Spot and said, "Hello, I'm Dr. Lee. I'm a veterinarian. I can help your dog. What happened?" Lily told him what happened. She said, "Spot slipped and fell. His leg is bleeding. He is in pain. Can you make him better?" Dr. Lee nodded and said, "Yes, I can. Don't worry, Lily. Spot is a brave dog. I will take him to my car and drive him to my clinic. I will clean his wound and give him some medicine. He will be fine. Do you want to come with me?" Lily nodded and said, "Yes, please. I want to be with Spot. Thank you, Dr. Lee. You are incredible." Dr. Lee smiled and said, "You're welcome, Lily. You are incredible too. You love your dog very much. Come on, let's go." He lifted Spot gently and put him in his car. Lily followed him and sat next to Spot. She stroked his fur and said, "It's okay, Spot. Dr. Lee will make you better. I love you, Spot." Spot licked her face and wagged his tail. He was glad that Lily was with him. He knew that she loved him too. He trusted Dr. Lee. He hoped that he would be better soon. He closed his eyes and rested.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Zoey. She was three years old and loved playing in the garden. One day, while she was playing, she heard a loud sound coming from the nearby woods. She was curious and wanted to find out what it was. “What’s that noise?”, she asked her dad. Her dad looked up at her, smiling. “It’s an urge,” he said. “An urge to explore the woods and discover what’s hidden there.” Zoey was excited. She put on her shoes and raced into the woods. As she explored, she discovered many different things that were interesting. But the further she went, the more difficult it became. Suddenly, she came across a beautiful lake, shimmering in the sunlight. She was awe struck! The lake revealed something that she had never seen before - a magical waterfall! The waterfall sparkled with light and made a soft, bubbling noise. Zoey was so happy that she decided to come back again soon. She had been on an amazing journey and wanted to experience it again.

Ben likes to solve puzzles. He has many puzzles with animals, cars, and shapes. He can solve them very fast. He likes to show his mom and dad how he solves puzzles. On Tuesday, Ben gets a new puzzle from his grandma. It is a big puzzle with a map of the world. Ben is very happy. He wants to solve the new puzzle right away. He opens the box and dumps the pieces on the floor. But the new puzzle is very hard. Ben does not know where to start. He tries to find the edges, but they all look the same. He tries to match the colors, but they are too many. He tries to look at the picture on the box, but it is too small. Ben feels sad. He thinks he cannot solve the new puzzle. Then, his mom comes to help him. She says, "Don't give up, Ben. You can solve the new puzzle. You just need some help. Let's do it together." She sits down with him and helps him sort the pieces. She shows him how to find the corners and the sides. She helps him match the continents and the oceans. She tells him the names of the places on the map. She praises him when he finds the right piece. Soon, Ben and his mom solve the new puzzle. They see the whole world on the floor. Ben is very proud. He says, "Thank you, mom. You helped me solve the new puzzle. It is very fun. I learned a lot. I love you." His mom hugs him and says, "You're welcome, Ben. You did a great job. I love you too." They smile and take a picture of the new puzzle. Ben thinks it is the best Tuesday ever.
