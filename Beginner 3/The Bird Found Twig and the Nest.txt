Category: Beginner
Title: The Bird Found Twig and the Nest

One day, a little bird found a twig. The bird had a dream to build a big nest. So, the bird picked up the twig and flew away. The bird felt happy and excited. The bird met another bird and said, "Look at my twig! I will build a big nest." The other bird looked at the twig and laughed. "That twig is too small," said the other bird. The little bird felt ashamed. The little bird tried to find a bigger twig, but could not. The bird felt sad and gave up on the dream. The bird went home and cried. The big nest was never built.

Once upon a time, there was a little frog named Timmy. He loved to jump and play all day long. One day, Timmy's mom told him, "If you jump over the big rock, you will get a reward." Timmy was anxious. He had never jumped over such a big rock before. He was scared, but he wanted the reward. So, he took a deep breath and said, "I can do it!" With all his might, Timmy jumped as high as he could. Up, up, up he went, and over the big rock he flew. When he landed on the other side, he was so happy! His mom clapped and said, "Good job, Timmy! Here is your reward." She gave him a big, juicy bug to eat. Timmy smiled and knew that he could do anything if he tried.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was a quiet dog who liked to play with his ball. Every day, Max would wait for his best friend, a little girl named Lily, to come home from school. One day, when Lily came home, she saw that Max had a new toy. It was a big, red ball. Lily wanted to play with Max and the new ball, but Max refused to share. He just wanted to play with it by himself. Lily felt sad and decided to wait until Max was ready to share. After a while, Max saw how sad Lily was and decided to share his new ball with her. They played together and had lots of fun. From that day on, Max never refused to share his toys with Lily, and they were always happy to play together.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big box under a tree. They run to the box and open it. Inside, they find many shiny things. They are jewels! Lily and Ben are very happy. They look at the jewels and touch them. They are red, blue, green, yellow and purple. They are smooth and sparkly. Lily and Ben put some jewels on their necks and fingers. They pretend to be kings and queens. But then, they hear a loud voice. It is the owner of the box. He is angry. He says, "Hey, you two! Give me back my jewels! They are not yours!" He runs to the box and grabs the jewels from Lily and Ben. He scolds them and tells them to go away. Lily and Ben are scared and sad. They run away from the box and the angry man. They cry and hug each other. They say, "We are sorry. We just wanted to play. We did not know the jewels were his." They go to their mom, who is sitting on a bench. She sees them crying and asks them what happened. They tell her the story. She hugs them and says, "It's okay. You made a mistake, but you learned from it. You should not take things that are not yours. You should ask first. And you should say sorry if you hurt someone." She gives them a sweet kiss and a sweet cookie. She says, "You are my jewels. You are more precious than anything in the world. I love you." Lily and Ben smile and say, "We love you too, mom." They eat the cookie and feel better. They are still twins. They still like to play in the park. But they do not touch the box anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little kitten named Fluffy. Fluffy was always impatient. He wanted to play with his ball all day long. One day, Fluffy saw a big tree. He thought, "I want to climb that tree." Fluffy started to climb the tree. He had to stretch his legs to reach the high branches. Fluffy was so happy to be up in the tree. He saw a bird and wanted to play with it. But the bird flew away. Fluffy was sad and wanted to go down. Fluffy was scared to climb down. He called for his mom. His mom came and told him to stretch his legs again. Fluffy did what his mom said. He climbed down and was safe. Fluffy learned to be patient and not to climb trees without his mom.
