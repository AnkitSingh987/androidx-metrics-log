Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Tree

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big tree with lots of fruit. Tim wanted to eat the fruit, so he tried to get one. The tree did not yield, so Tim tried again. This time, the tree gave him the fruit. Tim was very happy. Tim looked at the fruit. It was green and round. He touched the fruit and felt its texture. It was smooth and a little bit bumpy. Tim thought it would taste very good. He took a big bite of the fruit. But the taste was not good at all. It was disgusting! Tim made a face and threw the fruit away. Just then, a little girl named Sue came to the park. She saw the fruit on the ground and picked it up. Sue knew that this fruit was not for eating. It was a toy! Tim and Sue laughed and played with the toy fruit all day long.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They wanted to buy some jelly. Tim liked regular jelly the best. They found the jelly aisle and saw many different kinds. There were big jars and small jars. Tim picked a regular jar of jelly. At home, Tim's mom opened the jar of jelly. She tried to separate the jelly from the jar, but it was stuck. "Oh no!" said Mom, "The jelly won't come out!" Tim looked sad. He wanted to eat his jelly. Then, something unexpected happened. The jar of jelly started to shake. It shook and shook until the jelly jumped out of the jar! The jelly landed on a plate. Tim and his mom laughed. They were happy that the jelly was out of the jar. Now, they could enjoy their regular jelly.

Once upon a time, there was a big bull named Bob. Bob was very fast. He lived on a farm with many other animals. One day, a man came to the farm. He wanted to steal something. Bob saw the man and knew he was not nice. He watched the man walk around the farm. The man tried to take a little pig. Bob did not want the man to steal the pig. Bob had a plan. Bob ran very fast and hit the man with his big body. The man fell down and the pig was safe. The farmer saw what happened and thanked Bob. From that day on, Bob was the hero of the farm.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw a girl named Sue crying. He asked, "Why are you sad?" Sue said, "I lost my toy. Can you help me find it?" Tim and Sue looked all around the park. They looked under the slide, behind the swings, and even in the sandbox. Then, Tim had an idea. He looked in his pocket and found a clean tissue. He gave it to Sue to wipe her tears. Together, they found Sue's toy under a big tree. Sue was very happy and said, "Thank you for helping me, Tim!" From that day on, Tim and Sue became best friends. The moral of the story is to always help others when they need it.

One day, a little girl named Lucy wanted to eat her cereal. She looked in the kitchen, but she could not find it. So, she started to search for her cereal. She looked in the living room and even under her bed, but it was not there. Lucy felt very silly. Lucy asked her mom, "Mom, where is my cereal?" Her mom smiled and said, "Lucy, the cereal is in the kitchen. Look in the big box on the top shelf." Lucy went back to the kitchen and looked up. She saw the big box on the top shelf, but she was too small to reach it. Lucy's mom saw her trying to reach the box and helped her get it down. They both laughed and thought it was silly that they had to search for the cereal. They sat down at the table and enjoyed a big bowl of cereal together.
