Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Max and the Chicken

Lily and Max were having dinner with Mom and Dad. They liked dinner time because they could talk and eat yummy food. Mom made chicken and rice and peas. Lily and Max liked chicken and rice, but they did not like peas. They thought peas were green and yucky. "Please pass me the dish with the chicken, Max," Lily said. She wanted more chicken because it was soft and tasty. "Here you go, Lily," Max said. He passed her the dish with the chicken. He did not want more chicken. He wanted more rice because it was white and fluffy. "Thank you, Max," Lily said. She took some chicken from the dish and put it on her plate. She tried to hide the peas under the rice. She hoped Mom and Dad would not notice. "Please pass me the dish with the rice, Lily," Max said. He wanted more rice because he was still hungry. "Here you go, Max," Lily said. She passed him the dish with the rice. She did not want more rice. She wanted to finish her chicken and leave the table. "Thank you, Lily," Max said. He took some rice from the dish and put it on his plate. He saw the peas under the rice. He did not mind the peas. He thought peas were green and fun. "Are you enjoying your dinner, kids?" Mom asked. She smiled and looked at their plates. "Yes, Mom," Lily and Max said. They did not want to hurt Mom's feelings. They knew Mom worked hard to make dinner for them. "That's good," Dad said. He was proud of his kids. He knew dinner was tough for them sometimes. He also did not like peas when he was little. He winked at Lily and Max and said, "Maybe we can have ice cream for dessert if you finish your plates."

Once upon a time, there was a little triangle. The triangle was very delicate. It lived in a big box with other shapes. They all played together and had fun. One day, the little triangle wanted to play a new game. It asked its friends, "Let's play a game. When I say 'stop', we all stop and see who can stay still the longest." The other shapes liked the idea and agreed to play. They started the game, and the little triangle shouted, "Stop!" All the shapes stopped and stayed still. Suddenly, the box started to shake. The shapes did not know what was happening. The shaking stopped, and the box opened. It was a little girl who wanted to play with them. The shapes were so happy to have a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy pistol. Tim was stubborn and always wanted to play with his pistol, even at bedtime. One day, Tim's mom told him a story about a magic tree. The tree would give a gift to those who were kind and listened well. Tim thought about the tree and how he wanted a gift too. But he didn't know where the tree was. The next day, Tim and his mom went for a walk. They found the magic tree! Tim remembered the story and decided to be kind and listen to his mom. He put his toy pistol away and held his mom's hand. The tree saw this and gave Tim a gift. Tim was so happy and knew that being kind and listening was important.

Tom and Mia liked to play near the pond. They saw many fish and frogs and bugs in the water. Sometimes they caught some and put them in a jar. They wanted to see them better. One day, they saw a new thing in the pond. It was small and pink and had many legs. Tom said it was a shrimp. Mia said it was cute. They caught it and put it in their jar. They were happy. They took the jar home and showed it to their mom. Mom said they should not take things from the pond. She said the shrimp might be sick or dead. She said they should let it go. Tom and Mia did not listen. They wanted to keep the shrimp. They put the jar in their room and went to eat. They forgot about the shrimp. They did not mention it to anyone. They did not give it any water or food. They did not check on it. The next day, they remembered the shrimp. They ran to their room and looked at the jar. The shrimp was not moving. It was not pink. It was gray and smelly. It was dead. Tom and Mia felt sad and sorry. They cried. They wished they had listened to their mom. They learned a hard lesson.
