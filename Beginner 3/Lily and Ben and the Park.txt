Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Park

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to swing, slide and run. They saw a big tree with a hole in it. Lily wanted to look inside. "Maybe there is a treasure in there," she said. "Or maybe there is a monster," Ben said. They went closer to the tree. Lily peeked inside the hole. She saw something shiny. She reached her hand to grab it. "Wait, Lily, don't touch it!" Ben shouted. But it was too late. Lily pulled out a can. It was old and rusty. It had a message on it. It said: "Do not open. Disgusting inside." Lily was curious. She opened the can. A bad smell came out. It was like rotten eggs and fish. Lily and Ben coughed and gagged. "Yuck, what is this?" Lily asked. "I don't know, but it's disgusting," Ben said. They threw the can away. They ran to the fountain. They washed their hands and faces. They felt better. "Let's never mention this again," Lily said. "Agreed," Ben said. They went back to playing. They forgot about the can and the message. They had fun in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He liked to play outside with his dog, Max. One sunny day, Tim and Max went to the park. They saw a big tree and decided to play near it. While playing, Tim found a cool rock on the ground. He picked it up and said, "Max, look at this cool rock I found!" Max wagged his tail and barked. Tim started to examine the rock. He noticed it had a strange rub on it. He showed it to Max, who sniffed it. Suddenly, the rock started to shake and glow! Tim and Max were surprised. The rock turned into a magic key! They used the key to open a secret door in the tree. Inside, they found a room full of toys and treats. Tim and Max had a great time playing and eating together. They promised to keep the secret room a secret, and they lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tom found a lucky cap. The cap was red and had a big star on it. Tom loved his new cap and wore it every day. Tom's friend, Sam, saw the lucky cap and wanted it too. Sam said, "Give me your cap!" Tom did not want to give his cap away. He told Sam, "No, it is my lucky cap." Sam was not happy. He demand the cap again and again. But Tom did not give it to him. Tom and Sam were not friends for some time. Then, one day, Sam found a lucky cap too. It was blue and had a big star on it. Sam was very happy. Tom and Sam both had lucky caps now. They played together and were friends again.

Once upon a time, there was a wild dog named Spot. Spot lived in the woods and liked to play all day. He had a big, long tie that he wore around his neck. One day, Spot went to a party with his friends. At the party, they played games and had fun. Spot wanted to help, so he decided to serve snacks to his friends. He put the snacks on a big plate and carried it with his mouth. As Spot walked around the party, his tie got caught on a tree. He tried to pull free, but the tie was stuck. His friends saw him and came to help. They worked together and got the tie off the tree. Spot was happy and continued to serve snacks to his friends. They all had a great time at the party.

Once upon a time, there was a big house. In the house, there was a big room. In the big room, there was a big cobweb. The cobweb was very old and dusty. One day, a little boy found a match. He wanted to play with the match. He did not know that playing with matches was not safe. He struck the match and it made a small fire. The little boy saw the big cobweb and wanted to make it go away. He put the fire near the cobweb. The cobweb caught fire and the fire grew bigger and bigger. Soon, the big room was on fire. The little boy was scared and ran away. The big house burned down, and everyone was sad.
