Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Car

One day, a little girl named Mia was playing with her toy car. She liked to make it go fast. Mia wanted to start a race with her friend Tom. Tom said, "Okay, let's race!" Mia and Tom lined up their toy cars. They were ready to start. Mia said, "On your mark, get set, go!" But Tom's car didn't move. Tom looked sad. Mia stopped her car and asked, "What's wrong, Tom?" Tom said, "My car is broken. I can't race." Mia thought for a moment. She had an idea. "We can fix it! Let's use my thumb to push it." Mia was an honest friend. She wanted to help Tom. They worked together to fix Tom's car. Mia used her thumb to push the car, and it started to move. Tom was happy. They raced again, and both cars went fast. Mia and Tom had fun playing together. They were honest friends who helped each other.

One summer day, a nosy little girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a glow in a bush. "What is that?" she asked herself. She went closer to see. She found a tiny bug, glowing like a little light. "Hello, little bug," said Lily. "Why do you glow?" The bug looked at her and said, "I glow to help my friends find me in the dark." Lily thought that was nice. She asked, "Can I be your friend too?" The bug smiled and said, "Of course! Friends are always good to have." So, Lily and the glowing bug played together all summer long.

One day, a little fish with orange skin swam in the water. The fish had a shiny scale that was very special. The fish liked to play with his friends in the sea. The fish met a crab who wanted to play. The crab said, "Let's play a game with your shiny scale!" The fish agreed, and they played a fun game together. They laughed and had a great time. But then, the fish got his scale stuck in some seaweed. The crab helped the fish by using his claw to cut the seaweed. The fish was happy and said, "Thank you, crab, for helping me!" The crab and the fish became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very tired. He had been playing all day with his toys. He wanted to take a nap, but he could not sleep. His room was too hot. Tim looked at the window and thought, "Maybe if I open the window, it will be cooler and I can sleep." Tim went to the window and tried to push it open. But it was stuck. He pushed and pushed, but the window did not move. Tim was very sad. He sat down on his bed and said, "I can't open the window. I am too tired to push it." Just then, his friend, a little bird, flew into his room through the small hole in the window. The bird saw Tim was sad and asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim told the bird about the window. The bird had an idea. "Let's both push the window together," said the bird. Tim and the bird went to the window and pushed together. To their surprise, the window opened very quickly! They didn't know that the window was a magic window that only opens when friends help each other. Tim was so happy, and his room was now cool. He thanked the bird and they both took a nice nap together.

Sara likes to go to the gym with her mom. The gym has many fun things to play with. Sara can jump on the trampoline, swing on the bars, and roll on the mats. She also makes new friends at the gym. One day, Sara meets a boy named Ben at the gym. Ben is very nice and shares his toys with Sara. They play together and have a lot of fun. Sara says to Ben, "You are my friend. You are dependable." Ben smiles and says to Sara, "You are my friend too. You are dependable." They hug and continue to play. Sara's mom watches them and feels happy. She says to herself, "Sara is growing up. She is learning to be kind and dependable. I am proud of her." She goes to the gym teacher and says, "Thank you for making the gym a safe and fun place for the kids." The gym teacher says, "You are welcome. I enjoy seeing them learn and have fun."
