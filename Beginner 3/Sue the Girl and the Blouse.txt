Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Blouse

One day, a girl named Sue found a pretty blouse in her mom's closet. It was very comfortable. She wanted to wear it, but her mom said, "No, Sue. That is my blouse." Sue was sad, but she had an idea. She asked her mom if they could part the blouse in two, so they could share. Her mom thought about it and said, "Okay, Sue. We can try." They took the blouse and cut it into two parts. Sue and her mom each had one part of the blouse. Sue was happy, but her mom was not. The blouse did not look nice anymore. Sue saw her mom was sad and said, "I'm sorry, Mom. I just wanted to share with you." Her mom hugged Sue and said, "It's okay, Sue. I know you wanted to share. We can still be happy with our parts of the blouse." They both smiled and wore their parts of the blouse, feeling comfortable and happy together.

One day, a big leopard was walking in the forest. He saw a little boy playing with a ball. The little boy would throw the ball up and catch it again. The leopard thought it looked fun, so he went up to the boy and said, "Can I play too?" The boy was not scared of the leopard. He said, "Yes, you can play with me. But you must be nice." So they played together, taking turns to throw the ball. The leopard was very persistent, he did not want to stop playing. As they played, a big wind came and blew the ball away. The boy and the leopard ran after it, but the ball went into a deep hole. The boy was sad, but the leopard said, "Don't worry, I will get it for you." The leopard jumped into the hole, but when he came out, he was not a leopard anymore. He was a little boy, just like the first boy! They were both very surprised, but they laughed and kept playing together. Now they were best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a truck named Tim. Tim had a very important job. He had to deliver fuel to all the cars in the town. One day, Tim woke up and felt worried. He had a big job to do, but he just couldn't remember where to go. Tim asked his friend, the car, "Do you know where I need to go to deliver the fuel?" The car said, "I think you need to go to the big store with the red roof." Tim thanked the car and went to the big store with the red roof. When Tim got to the store, he started to deliver the fuel. But something was wrong. The store was not the right place. The cars in the town did not get their fuel. They could not go anywhere. Tim felt very sad. He knew he should have asked for help sooner. Now, the town was stuck without fuel, and it was all because of Tim's mistake.

One day, a charming elephant named Elly went for a walk. She saw a big tree with a trunk. Elly thought it was a good place to play. "Hello, tree!" said Elly. "Can I play with you?" The tree did not answer. Elly was sad. She walked away, but she did not give up. The next day, Elly came back to the tree. She had an idea. She used her long trunk to tickle the tree's leaves. The tree started to laugh! "Hello, Elly!" said the tree. "I like your tickles. Let's play together!" Elly was very happy. They played all day and became good friends.

Tim and Sam were friends who liked to play with cars. They had many toy cars of different colors and sizes. They liked to race them on the floor and make loud noises. One day, Tim saw a big red car in Sam's garage. He wanted to play with it very much. He asked Sam, "Can I play with your big red car?" Sam said, "No, you can't. That is my dad's car. He does not permit anyone to touch it. It is very special and expensive. You can play with my toy cars instead." But Tim did not listen. He was not honest. He waited until Sam went inside the house to get a drink. Then he sneaked into the garage and opened the door of the big red car. He climbed inside and pretended to drive. He pushed some buttons and turned some knobs. He did not know what they did. Suddenly, the big red car started to move. It rolled out of the garage and onto the street. Tim was scared. He did not know how to stop it. He saw a big truck coming towards him. He screamed for help. But it was too late. The big red car crashed into the big truck. There was a loud bang and a lot of smoke. The big red car was smashed and broken. Tim was hurt and crying. Sam and his dad ran out of the house and saw the mess. They were very angry and sad. Tim was in big trouble. He had to go to the hospital and to the police. He had to say sorry to Sam and his dad. He had to pay for the big red car. He lost his friend and his toy cars. He wished he had been honest and listened to Sam. He learned a hard lesson.
