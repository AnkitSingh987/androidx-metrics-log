Category: Beginner
Title: The Stubborn Grill and the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn grill. It did not want to cooperate with anyone, no matter how hard they tried. One day, a little boy came to the grill with a smile on his face. He had a plan. He said, "Hello, Grill. I want to be your friend." But the grill didn't trust the boy and just stayed where he was. The boy was persistent, though. He talked and talked until the grill became curious and came closer. The boy wanted to cook some food over the grill so he asked the grill if he could help. The grill thought about it for awhile and then decided to help the boy. They started a campfire and the delicious food came out perfectly grilled. The boy was so happy! The boy and the grill became friends that day and the stubborn grill never backed away from helping the little boy out again.

One day, a big storm came to the village. The sky was dark and loud. The wind was strong and cold. The people were scared and ran to their houses. But one boy, Tom, was not scared. He liked storms. He wanted to see the lightning. He ran out of his house and climbed a hill. He saw the bright flashes in the sky. He heard the big booms. He felt the rain on his face. He was happy. "Tom, come back!" his mother shouted. "It is not safe! The lightning can strike you!" But Tom did not listen. He wanted to see more. He ran to a big tree. He touched the tree. He felt the bark. He felt something else. He felt a shock. He saw a flash. He heard a boom. He fell down. The lightning had struck the tree. And Tom. He was hurt. He was not happy. He was sorry. His mother ran to him. She saw him on the ground. She saw the tree on fire. She was scared and sad. She hugged him. She cried. "Tom, Tom, are you okay?" she asked. Tom opened his eyes. He saw his mother. He saw the smoke. He felt the pain. He was sorry. "Mom, I am sorry," he said. "I was bad. I did not listen. I wanted to see the storm. But it was not fun. It was rare. And dangerous." His mother kissed him. She was glad he was alive. She was not angry. She was sorry too. "Tom, I am sorry too," she said. "I love you. You are not bad. You are curious. But you have to be careful. The storm is rare. And dangerous. You have to stay with me. And be safe." She carried him to their house. She put him in his bed. She gave him some water. She sang him a song. He felt better. He felt sleepy. He closed his eyes. He dreamed of the storm. But he did not like it anymore. He liked his mother. And his house. And his bed. He was safe. He was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a robot. The robot was big and strong, but he was not dangerous. He wanted to help people and make friends. One day, he met a little girl named Lily. Lily was scared of the robot at first, but they soon became friends. The robot and Lily liked to play hide and seek. One day, while they were playing, they saw a dangerous snake. The snake wanted to bite Lily. The robot said, "Lily, hide behind me! I will keep you safe." Lily hid behind the robot, and the robot scared the snake away. After that day, Lily and the robot were even better friends. They always helped each other and kept each other safe. The moral of the story is to be kind and help your friends, even if they are different from you.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was going on a trip with her mom and dad. They had to unpack their bags when they got to the big red house. Sue found her favorite sweater in the bag. "Mom, look! I found my sweater!" she said with a big smile. Her mom said, "Good job, Sue! Now it will be easy to stay warm when it is cold outside." Sue wore her sweater when they went for a walk. It was easy for her to run and play in the big red house's yard. Sue and her mom and dad had a great time on their trip.
