Category: Beginner
Title: Jane the Girl and the Dog

One day, a little girl named Jane found a big tube in the park. She was very curious about it. Jane wanted to touch the tube. She asked her friend Tom, "Can I touch the tube? It looks so fun!" Tom said, "Yes, but be careful!" Jane touched the tube and it felt uncomfortable. It was sticky and wet. Jane didn't like it. She wiped her hand on her dress. Suddenly, the tube started to shake! Jane and Tom were scared. They didn't know what was happening. Then, a big, friendly dog jumped out of the tube! The dog was playing a trick on them. Jane and Tom laughed and played with the dog. They had a fun day in the park.

Once upon a time, in an ordinary little house, there was a girl named Amy. Amy was a good girl who always did what she was told. One day, her mom told her to remember to clean her room. If she did a good job, she would get a reward. Amy was excited about the reward, so she started to clean her room. She put her toys away and made her bed. But then, she found a big box under her bed. Inside the box, she saw a beautiful dress and shiny shoes. Amy wanted to wear them, but she remembered what her mom said. After cleaning her room, Amy showed her mom. Her mom was very happy and gave her the reward. The reward was the beautiful dress and shiny shoes from the box! Amy was so happy and wore them to a special party. She knew that remembering to do what she was told always led to good things.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. The sun was very bright, and it felt a little harsh on Tim's skin. He saw a big tree with lots of shade, so he ran over to it and sat down. He looked up at the sky and saw a pretty bird flying by. Tim saw a big box under the tree. The box was open. He looked inside and found a toy car. He picked it up and showed it to his mom. "Look, Mom! I found a car!" said Tim. His mom smiled and said, "That's a nice car, Tim. Let's play with it." Tim and his mom played with the toy car under the tree. They had lots of fun. The sun was not harsh under the tree, and they were happy. When it was time to go home, Tim put the car back in the open box. He hoped someone else would find it and have fun too.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One day, he saw a hidden sign near his house. The sign was behind a big tree. The sign had a picture of a pipe on it. Tim was very curious about the sign. Tim decided to follow the sign. He walked and walked until he found a big pipe. The pipe was in the ground. Tim thought it was a secret. He wanted to see what was inside the pipe. So, he crawled into the pipe. Inside the pipe, Tim found a small room. The room had many toys and games. Tim was very happy. He played with the toys and games all day. When it was time to go home, Tim crawled out of the pipe. He knew that he would come back to the hidden pipe room to play again.

Once upon a time, in a bright and sunny land, there lived a kind man named Tom. Tom had a staff that he used to gather fruits from trees. He loved to share these fruits with his friends. One day, Tom met a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she could not reach the fruits on the trees. Tom said, "Don't worry, Sue. I will use my staff to gather fruits for you." Sue smiled and said, "Thank you, Tom!" Tom used his staff to gather the bright, yummy fruits from the trees. He gave them to Sue, and they both ate the fruits together. Sue was happy, and they became good friends. From that day on, Tom and Sue would gather fruits and share them with all their friends in the sunny land.
