Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was fearful of climbing trees. One day, Tim saw other kids climbing a big tree in the park. They were having so much fun, and he wanted to join them. Tim walked up to the tree and looked up. He was scared, but he wanted to try. So, he put his foot on the lowest branch and started to climb. His stomach felt funny, but he kept going. When Tim got to the top, he saw his friend, Sam. Sam said, "Good job, Tim! I knew you could do it!" Tim smiled and felt happy. He was not fearful anymore. They both sat on the branch and looked at the sky, enjoying their time up in the tree.

Lila and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to swing on the swings and slide on the slide. They saw a big green thing with spikes. It was a cactus. "What is that?" Lila asked. "It is a cactus. It is a plant that lives in the desert. It has spikes to protect itself from animals," Ben said. "Can we touch it?" Lila wondered. "No, it will hurt you. The spikes are sharp and can poke you," Ben warned. But Lila was curious. She wanted to see how the cactus felt. She reached out her hand and touched the cactus. Ouch! It poked her finger. She cried out in pain. "Ow, ow, ow! It hurts! It hurts!" Lila shouted. Ben ran to her and took her hand. He saw a little spike stuck in her finger. He pulled it out gently and blew on it. "It's okay, Lila. It will get better soon. Don't touch the cactus again. It is not a friendly plant," Ben said. Lila nodded. She was confused. Why did the cactus have spikes? Why did it hurt her? She did not like the cactus. She liked the swings and the slide. She hugged Ben and said sorry. "I'm sorry, Ben. I was silly. I should have listened to you. Thank you for helping me," Lila said. Ben smiled and hugged her back. He was a good friend. He said it was okay. He said they could play some more. "Let's go swing on the swings and slide on the slide. They are fun and safe. They won't hurt you," Ben said. Lila agreed. She was happy. She forgot about the cactus. She and Ben ran to the swings and the slide. They had a lot of fun. They learned a lesson. They never touched the cactus again.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily and her dog, Buddy, went for a walk. They liked to play in the park. It was cold outside, so they wore warm clothes. Lily had a red coat, and Buddy had a blue one. In the park, they found a broken toy. "Oh no," said Lily, "someone's toy is broken." Buddy barked, and he started to dig a hole. "Good idea, Buddy! Let's bury the broken toy," said Lily. They put the toy in the hole and covered it with dirt. After that, they played more games and had a lot of fun. When it was time to go home, they said goodbye to the park. "We'll come back soon," said Lily. Buddy wagged his tail, and they went home, happy and warm.

Once upon a time, there was a big palace. In the palace lived a king and a queen. They were nice and loved their people. But one day, a harsh man came. The man wanted to take the palace for himself. The king and queen were sad. The king had a magic car. The car could reverse and go back in time. The king had an idea. He would use the car to stop the harsh man. So the king and queen got in the car and went back in time. They went back to the day the harsh man came. The king and queen talked to the man. They told him to be nice and not take the palace. The harsh man listened and left. The people were happy again. The king and queen used the magic car to go back to their time. They lived happily ever after in the big palace.
