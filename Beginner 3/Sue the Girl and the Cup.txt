Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Cup

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to clap her hands and play with her toys. One day, Sue found a new toy. It was a cup! Sue was very happy with her new cup. She filled the cup with water from the clear pond in her backyard. The water was so clear that she could see the fish swimming in it. Sue clapped her hands and laughed as she watched the fish. Sue took her cup and went to show her mom. She clapped her hands again and said, "Look, Mom, a cup with clear water and fish!" Her mom smiled and clapped her hands too. They both laughed and played with the cup all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily was adorable with big, blue eyes and long, curly hair. One day, Lily found a big mirror in her house. She loved looking at herself in the mirror and making funny faces. One hot day, Lily was playing outside with her friend, Sam. "It's so hot, I feel like I'm going to melt!" said Sam. Lily had an idea. She ran inside the house and brought the big mirror outside. She set the mirror up so that it would give them some shade. Sam asked, "What are you doing?" Lily smiled and said, "I'm using the mirror to keep us cool so we don't melt!" They both laughed and played under the shade of the mirror all day long. The sun was very hot, but Lily and Sam stayed cool and had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to collect junk from her yard. She found many things like old toys, broken cups, and torn clothes. Sue had a big box where she put all her junk. One day, Sue found a very uncomfortable chair in her yard. It was old and had many holes in it. Sue thought it was special, so she put it in her box with the other junk. Sue's friends came over to play. They saw her box of junk and thought it was fun. They all started to collect more junk together. They found old shoes, a broken bike, and a torn hat. They put all the junk in the box and had a great time playing with it.

Once upon a time, there was a busy cook named Tom. He liked to cook food for people. He had a friend named Sue, who was a model. Sue was tall and pretty. They both lived in a small town. One day, Tom asked Sue, "Do you want to cook with me?" Sue said, "Yes, that sounds fun!" They went to Tom's kitchen and started to cook. They made a big cake, some soup, and a pie. They were having a great time. But then, something unexpected happened. A little mouse ran into the kitchen! The mouse saw the food and wanted to eat it. Tom and Sue were surprised. They did not know what to do. Tom said, "We need to catch the mouse!" Sue agreed. They worked together and caught the mouse. They took it outside and let it go. Tom and Sue laughed and went back to cooking. They had a fun day and the mouse did not eat their food.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a new herb in her garden. Sue liked her garden a lot. She liked to watch the herbs grow. Sue wanted to show her herb to her friends. One day, Sue's friends came to play. They saw the new herb and thought it was a toy. They pulled the herb out of the ground. The herb was hurt. Sue started to weep. She was very sad. Sue tried to put the herb back in the ground. But the herb did not grow anymore. Sue wept every day for her herb. The herb was gone, and Sue was very sad. The end.

Once upon a time there was a lazy boy. He was very tired of playing all day so he had an idea. He decided to perform a show! He went to the mall and bought a new costume for the show. The show day arrived and he was excited! He put on the costume and got ready to start his show. But when he looked up, he saw nobody there! Everyone was too lazy to come to the show. The boy was so sad and he realized that sometimes it doesn't work out the way one plans. He learned that it was best to not be so lazy. Then he would have had a better show. The end.
