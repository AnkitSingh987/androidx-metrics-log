Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Bear

Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to dig in the dirt and look for bugs. They found a big worm and put it in a jar. "Look, Lily, it is moving!" Ben said. "Yes, it is. It is funny and slimy," Lily said. They wanted to show their worm to Mom. They ran to the house with the jar. But the door was closed and it was dark inside. "Mom, where are you?" Ben called. No one answered. They felt scared. "Maybe she is sleeping," Lily said. "Or maybe she is hiding," Ben said. They decided to look for Mom. They opened the door and went inside. They saw a shadow in the living room. They thought it was Mom. "Mom, we found a worm!" they shouted. But it was not Mom. It was a big bear. It growled and showed its teeth. "Aaaah!" Lily and Ben screamed. They dropped the jar and ran away. The worm fell out and crawled away. The bear followed them. It wanted to eat them. They ran to the kitchen and saw Mom. She was making a cake. "Mom, help! There is a bear!" they cried. Mom turned around and saw the bear. She was surprised and angry. "Who let this bear in?" she asked. She grabbed a broom and hit the bear. The bear was scared and ran away. Mom hugged Lily and Ben. She was glad they were safe. "Are you okay, my loves?" she asked. "Yes, Mom, we are okay. But we are dirty," they said. They looked at their hands and clothes. They were covered in dirt and worm slime. Mom smiled and took them to the bathroom. She filled the tub with warm water and soap. "Let's wipe off the dirt and the slime," she said. She gave them a sponge and a towel. They wiped their fingers and their faces and their bodies. They felt clean and happy. They thanked Mom for saving them from the bear. "You're welcome, my loves. But next time, please don't bring any worms or bears into the house," she said. They laughed and said sorry. They learned their lesson. They never played with worms or bears again.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They had a big bag of grapes to eat. Tim loved grapes. They were his favorite snack. At the park, Tim saw a broken toy on the ground. He looked at it and felt sad for the toy. "Mom, look!" Tim said, pointing at the broken toy. "Can we fix it?" His mom looked at the toy and thought for a moment. "I think we can try," she said with a smile. Tim and his mom took the broken toy home. They worked together to fix it. After they fixed the toy, Tim shared his grapes with his mom. They both felt happy that they could fix the broken toy and enjoy their grapes together.

Once upon a time, in a peaceful wood, there lived a kind bunny named Bob. Bob loved to eat berries. One day, he found a big bush with red and blue berries. He thought, "I will mix these berries to make a yummy treat!" As Bob began to mix the berries, a tiny bird flew down and sat on the bush. The bird looked sad and hungry. Bob thought, "I have so many berries, I can share with the bird." So, he gave some mixed berries to the bird. The bird ate the berries and smiled. The bird was so happy, it started to sing a beautiful song. More and more animals came to hear the song. They all shared the mixed berries and became friends. The wood became a happy place. The moral of the story is: when you share, you bring happiness to others and make new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, smelly toe. He did not like it, and he did not want to show it to his friends. Tim felt sad because he thought no one would like him because of his smelly toe. One day, Tim met a kind old lady. She told him to believe in himself and not to worry about his smelly toe. She said, "If you are a good friend and help others, they will like you for who you are." Tim thought about what the old lady said and decided to try to be a good friend. Tim started to help his friends and be kind to them. He shared his toys and played nice games. Soon, his friends did not care about his smelly toe. They liked him because he was a good friend. Tim learned that if you believe in yourself and be kind to others, they will like you for who you are. And that was the most important thing.
