Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Anna

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with toys and books. One day, Lily has a new toy. It is a baby doll. Lily loves her baby doll. She wants to teach her baby doll how to drink from a bottle. Lily says to Ben, "Look, Ben, this is my baby doll. Her name is Anna. She is very good. Do you want to help me teach her how to drink from a bottle?" Ben says, "Yes, Lily, I want to help you. How do we teach her?" Lily says, "We need a bottle and some water. I have a bottle here. You can fill it with water from the tap. Then we can give it to Anna and show her how to drink." Ben says, "OK, Lily, I will fill the bottle with water. You hold Anna and make her sit up." Ben goes to the tap and fills the bottle with water. He comes back to Lily and gives her the bottle. Lily holds Anna and makes her sit up. She puts the bottle to Anna's mouth and says, "Look, Anna, this is a bottle. It has water inside. Water is good for you. You can drink it like this. See, Lily and Ben drink water from the bottle. You can do it too. Try it, Anna, try it." Lily and Ben drink some water from the bottle and then give it to Anna. They watch Anna and wait for her to drink. Anna does not drink. She just looks at the bottle and makes a funny face. Lily and Ben are confused. They wonder why Anna does not drink. They try to make her drink again and again. They say, "Come on, Anna, drink, drink, drink. It is easy. It is fun. It is good for you. Drink, drink, drink." But Anna still does not drink. She just shakes her head and says, "No, no, no. I don't want to drink. I don't like water. I don't like bottle. I want milk. I want mommy. Mommy, mommy, mommy." Lily and Ben are surprised. They did not know that Anna can talk. They did not know that Anna wants milk and mommy. They feel sorry for Anna. They say, "Oh, Anna, we are sorry. We did not know that you want milk and mommy. We were trying to teach you how to drink from a bottle. We thought you would like it. We are sorry. We are your friends. We want you to be happy." Anna looks at Lily and Ben. She sees that they are sorry and that they are her friends. She smiles and says, "It's OK, Lily and Ben. I know you are my friends. I know you want me to be happy. Thank you for trying to teach me. But I don't need a bottle. I have a mommy. She gives me milk. I love milk. I love mommy. Mommy, mommy, mommy." Lily and Ben smile too. They hug Anna and say, "We love you, Anna. We are happy that you have a mommy. We are happy that you love milk. We are happy that you can talk. You are a good baby doll. You are a good friend." The end.

One day, a lively cat named Tom went for a walk. He loved to look at all the things around him. The sun was bright and the birds were singing. Tom saw a big tree and had an urge to climb it. He looked up and saw a pretty bird sitting on a branch. Tom wanted to be friends with the bird. So, he started to climb the tree. The bird saw Tom and flew down to say hello. They played together and had a lot of fun. They looked at the sky and the clouds. Tom and the bird became best friends, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a wise wizard named Harry. He had a magical shield that could protect him from any bad thing. One day, Harry was walking in the woods and found a beautiful cave. He decided to go inside and see what was inside. As soon as he entered the cave, a deep voice spoke up. It said, "I will lead you to safety if you give me your shield". Harry thought it was strange but he decided to follow the voice. He handed over his shield and followed the voice. Soon, the voice led him to a deep, dark abyss. Harry quickly realized he was tricked. The voice cackled and said, "Now you will never get out of this cave!" Harry was so sad and scared. He was never seen again. The End.
