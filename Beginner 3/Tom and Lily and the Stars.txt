Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Stars

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play and learn together. One day, they have math class with Miss Lee. Miss Lee is a wise teacher. She knows how to make math fun and easy. Miss Lee gives Tom and Lily some cards with numbers and shapes. She says, "Can you match the cards that have the same number or shape? If you do, you will get a sticker." Tom and Lily smile. They like stickers. They start to look at the cards. Tom finds a card with four circles. He looks for another card with four circles. He sees one on Lily's side. He says, "Lily, can I have that card? It has four circles like mine." Lily says, "Sure, Tom. Here you go." She gives him the card. Tom says, "Thank you, Lily. You are a good friend." He puts the cards together and shows them to Miss Lee. Miss Lee smiles and gives him a sticker. Lily finds a card with three stars. She looks for another card with three stars. She sees one on Tom's side. She says, "Tom, can I have that card? It has three stars like mine." Tom says, "Sure, Lily. Here you go." He gives her the card. Lily says, "Thank you, Tom. You are a good friend." She puts the cards together and shows them to Miss Lee. Miss Lee smiles and gives her a sticker. Tom and Lily are happy. They have matched all the cards and got many stickers. They say, "We love math. Math is fun." Miss Lee says, "You are very smart and kind. I am proud of you." She hugs them and gives them more stickers. Tom and Lily smile. They hug Miss Lee and say, "We love you, Miss Lee. You are a wise teacher."

Once upon a time, in a big green park, there lived a happy dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his friends, the birds, and the squirrels. Spot was a good dog, but he had a problem. He was jealous of his friend, the big tree. One sunny day, Spot saw the big tree and decided to tell the tree his opinion. He walked up to the tree and said, "I am jealous of you, big tree. You are so tall and strong, and I am just a small dog." The big tree listened to Spot and smiled. Then, something unexpected happened. A little squirrel came running down the tree and gave Spot a big slap on his nose. Spot was surprised by the squirrel's slap. The squirrel said, "Don't be jealous, Spot. You are a good dog, and we all love you just the way you are." Spot smiled and thanked the squirrel. He learned that he didn't need to be jealous of the big tree because he was special in his own way. And they all played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom had a toy pistol that he loved to play with. One day, he saw a big dog in his yard. Tom thought the dog looked dangerous, so he wanted to protect his family. Tom ran to the dog and said, "Go away, dog! You are dangerous!" He pointed his toy pistol at the dog to scare it away. The dog just looked at Tom and wagged its tail. Tom's mom came outside and saw Tom with his toy pistol. She said, "Tom, don't worry. The dog is not dangerous. Let's cover the pistol and put it away." Tom listened to his mom and covered the toy pistol with a cloth. The dog became Tom's new friend, and they played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with yarn. One day, he found a big ball of fine yarn. Tom was very happy. He played with the yarn all day long. The yarn was so soft and nice. But, one day, Tom played too hard. The yarn broke into two parts. Tom was sad. He wanted to repair the yarn. So, he asked his friend, a dog named Max, to help him. Max was good at fixing things. Max and Tom worked together to repair the yarn. When they fixed the yarn, something unexpected happened. The yarn turned into a magic yarn. The magic yarn could talk! The yarn thanked Tom and Max for fixing it. It said it would grant them one wish. Tom and Max were surprised but happy. They wished to be best friends forever. The magic yarn made their wish come true. Tom and Max were very happy and played together every day.
