Category: Beginner
Title: The Table and the Window

Once upon a time, there was a big table. On the table, there were two plates. One plate was red and the other plate was blue. The red plate and the blue plate did not like each other. They would always quarrel. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the table. He heard the plates quarreling. Tim said, "Why are you fighting? You should be friends!" The plates thought it was difficult to be friends, but they listened to Tim. Just then, a strong wind blew through the window. It made the plates fly off the table! The red plate and the blue plate held onto each other. They were scared. The wind stopped, and the plates were safe. The plates learned that being friends was better than quarreling. They thanked Tim for helping them. From that day on, the red plate and the blue plate were best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a drum that he loved to play. He would grab his drum and make loud sounds with it. His friends liked to listen to him play. One day, Tim went to the park with his drum. He saw his friend Sally. "Hi, Sally!" said Tim. "Do you want to hear me play my drum?" Sally said, "Yes, please!" So Tim grabbed his drum and played a fun beat. Sally clapped her hands and danced to the music. Tim was very successful at playing his drum. His friends loved to hear him play. They would all gather around and dance to the beat. Tim was happy that he could make his friends happy with his drum. And they all lived happily ever after.

Anna and Ben were at the bakery with their mom. They saw many pastries in the window. They looked yummy and pretty. Anna liked the ones with pink frosting and sprinkles. Ben liked the ones with chocolate and nuts. "Can we have a pastry, mom?" Anna asked. "Sure, you can each choose one," mom said. Anna and Ben ran to the counter and pointed at the pastries they wanted. The baker smiled and put them in a paper bag. "Thank you, baker!" Anna and Ben said. "You're welcome, kids. Enjoy your pastries!" the baker said. Mom paid the baker and they went outside. Anna and Ben opened the bag and took out their pastries. They took a big bite. "Mmm, this tastes so good!" Anna said. "Me too, this tastes yummy!" Ben said. They ate their pastries and licked their fingers. They were happy and full. "Did you like your pastries, kids?" mom asked. "Yes, mom, we loved them!" Anna and Ben said. "Good, I'm glad. Now let's go home and wash your faces. You have frosting and chocolate all over them!" mom said. Anna and Ben laughed and followed mom. They had a fun day at the bakery.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a robot. The robot was great and big. Tim was very happy. He played with the robot all day. They played catch, and they played tag. Tim and the robot were best friends. One day, Tim and the robot went to the park. "Let's play catch," said Tim. They played catch and had lots of fun. Then, Tim threw the ball very high. The robot tried to catch it, but he couldn't. The robot drop the ball. "Oh no!" said the robot. "I'm sorry, Tim." But Tim was not sad. He had an idea. "Let's try again, but this time, I'll help you." Tim stood on the robot's hand, and the robot lifted him up. Together, they caught the ball. They laughed and played even more. From that day on, Tim and the robot always helped each other. They learned that when they worked together, they could do great things. And they were always the best of friends.

One day, a dog named Max found a hose in the yard. Max liked to play with the hose. He would bite and pull it. Max's friend, a cat named Luna, saw him play. "Max, don't play with the hose too much. It may break," Luna said. But Max did not listen. He kept playing with the hose. One hot day, Max's family needed the hose to give water to the plants. But the hose was broken. Max's family was sad. Max knew he broke the hose, so he felt bad. He wanted to make it right. Max went to Luna and said, "I should have listened to you. I will quit playing with the hose. I will help my family now." Max and Luna worked together to fix the hose. They made it clear to Max's family that they were sorry. The moral of the story is to listen to your friends and be careful with the things you play with.
