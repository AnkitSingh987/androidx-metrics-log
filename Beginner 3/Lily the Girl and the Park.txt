Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Park

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. Lily loved to play on the swings and slide. Her mom said, "Lily, stay close to me while we are at the park." Lily nodded and held her mom's hand. While they were at the park, Lily saw a little dog. The dog had a pretty ribbon tied around its waist. Lily asked her mom, "Can I pet the dog?" Her mom looked around and saw the dog's owner. She said, "Yes, but be gentle." Lily walked up to the dog and gently petted it. The dog wagged its tail and seemed to like Lily. Just then, the dog's owner came over and said, "Hi! My dog's name is Daisy. She loves kids and I see she likes you too." Lily smiled and said, "I like Daisy too!" After they played with Daisy, Lily and her mom went home. Lily was happy she got to meet a new friend at the park.

Tom and Mia liked to play in the big open park. They ran and jumped and laughed. They saw many wild things, like birds and bugs and flowers. They liked to look at them and learn their names. One day, they found a strange toy in the grass. It was a big red button with a picture of a star. Tom pushed the button and said, "What does it do?" Mia said, "I don't know. Maybe it makes a wish." But the button did not make a wish. It made a zap. A bright light flashed and Tom and Mia felt funny. They looked at each other and saw that they were very small. They had shrunk! They were scared and shouted, "Help! Help!" But no one heard them. They were too tiny. They saw the wild things get bigger and bigger. The birds and bugs and flowers looked like giants. They had to run and hide from them. They saw the button in the grass, but it was far away. They had to get to it and push it again. Maybe it would make them big again. They ran and dodged and jumped. They hoped no one would step on them or eat them. They reached the button and pushed it hard. They hoped it would work. They saw another flash and felt another zap. They closed their eyes and wished. When they opened their eyes, they saw that they were big again. They were happy and hugged each other. They said, "That was scary! Let's never play with that button again!" They threw the button in the trash and ran back to their mom. They told her what happened, but she did not believe them. She said, "That's a silly story. You have big imaginations." Tom and Mia smiled and nodded. They knew it was true, but they did not mind. They liked to have big imaginations. They liked to play and learn and have fun. They liked to be big and small and wild and open. They liked to be Tom and Mia.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly dog named Buddy. Buddy lived in a small house with a big yard. In the yard, there was a soft mattress. Buddy loved to lean on the mattress and take naps under the warm sun. One day, a little bird named Chirpy flew into the yard. Chirpy saw Buddy leaning on the mattress and asked, "Can I sit with you?" Buddy wagged his tail and said, "Yes, come sit with me!" Chirpy flew down and sat next to Buddy on the mattress. Buddy and Chirpy became good friends. They played together in the yard and shared the soft mattress. They laughed and talked every day. Buddy was happy to have a friendly bird to lean on the mattress with him, and Chirpy was happy to have a big, friendly dog to play with.

One day, Sam was very excited. He went to the park with his dad. They saw a big machine. The machine was for feeding ducks. Sam wanted to feed the ducks too. Sam said, "Dad, can we feed the ducks?" Dad smiled and said, "Yes, Sam. Let's get some food from the machine." They put coins in the machine and got food for the ducks. They walked to the pond and saw many ducks. Sam threw the food to the ducks. The ducks were happy and ate the food. Sam was excited and laughed. He and his dad had a fun day at the park.
