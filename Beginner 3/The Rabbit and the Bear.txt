Category: Beginner
Title: The Rabbit and the Bear

Once upon a time, there was a brave rabbit. He was so brave that he decided to go on an adventure. The rabbit wore a special leather jacket and he was ready to go. So he started off on his adventure. He hopped and hopped, until suddenly, he felt a chill. He started to shiver, so he looked for a place to keep warm. That's when he found a cave. The rabbit was happy to find a place to stay, but when he got inside, he heard a noise. He saw a big, scary bear. The bear was very angry and didn't want the rabbit there. He yelled and the rabbit was so scared that he started to shake. The bear grabbed the rabbit's leather jacket and began to pull. The brave rabbit was so frightened that he couldn't move. The bear pulled and pulled, until the rabbit felt a sharp pain. He was so scared that he just wanted to run away. Alas, the bear was too strong, and the poor rabbit couldn't escape. He shivered as the bear hurt him. In the end, the brave rabbit lost the adventure and sadly, he never went back home.

Tom and Lily are twins. They like to play with their toys and watch cartoons. But they do not like to go for a walk with Mom and Dad. They think it is boring and tiring. They hate to walk. One day, Mom and Dad say, "We are going for a walk in the park. Put on your shoes and coats. It will be fun." Tom and Lily groan and whine. They do not want to go. They want to stay home and play. Mom and Dad take them to the park anyway. They walk on the path and see many things. They see birds and squirrels and flowers and trees. They see a pond with ducks and a playground with swings and slides. They see other kids and dogs and bikes. "Look, Tom, look, Lily, how interesting!" Mom and Dad say. "Do you see the big red bird? Do you hear the quack of the ducks? Do you smell the fresh grass? Do you want to try the swing or the slide?" Tom and Lily stop groaning and whining. They start to look and listen and smell and touch. They see the big red bird and hear the quack of the ducks. They smell the fresh grass and touch the soft petals. They want to try the swing and the slide. They smile and laugh and run. They do not hate to walk anymore. They like to walk in the park. They think it is fun and exciting. They say, "Thank you, Mom and Dad, for taking us to the park. We love you and we love the park." Mom and Dad hug them and say, "We love you too, and we are glad you enjoyed the walk." They all go home happy.

Once upon a time, there was a happy little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his toys, especially his ball. One day, Spot saw a wire on the ground. He didn't know what it was, but he wanted to play with it. Spot tried to pick up the wire with his mouth, but it was hard to do. He tried and tried, but he couldn't pick it up. Spot felt sad. He didn't want to give up, but he didn't know what to do. Then, Spot's friend, a big dog named Max, came over to help. Max saw Spot trying to pick up the wire and knew what to do. Max said, "Spot, sometimes we need to surrender when things are too hard." Spot listened to Max and let go of the wire. Max then picked up the wire with his big mouth and gave it to Spot. Spot was happy again because he learned that it's okay to ask for help when things are hard.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily was a noisy girl who loved to play in the water. One day, she went to the park with her mom. At the park, Lily saw a big puddle. She jumped in it and splashed water all around. "Lily, come here!" her mom called. "It's time to go home and bathe." When they got home, Lily's mom filled the tub with warm water. Lily jumped in and splashed around, making lots of noise. "Lily, please be quiet," her mom said. "Your brother is sleeping." Lily tried her best to be quiet, but it was hard. She loved to make noise while she played in the water. After her bath, she put on her pajamas and said, "Mom, I promise to be quiet next time I bathe." Her mom smiled and hugged her. "Thank you, Lily. Now let's go to bed." And they all lived happily ever after.
