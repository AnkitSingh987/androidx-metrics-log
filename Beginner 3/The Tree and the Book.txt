Category: Beginner
Title: The Tree and the Book

Once upon a time, in a warm, sunny land, there was a big tree. Under the tree, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, liked to play. They were very happy. One day, Tim found a big, old book under the tree. "What is this?" he asked Spot. "It looks like history!" Spot barked, wagging his tail. Tim opened the book and saw many pictures of people and places from long ago. Tim and Spot sat under the tree and looked at the pictures together. Just then, Tim's sister, Sue, came by. "What are you doing?" she asked. "We found a history book," said Tim. "But don't spoil it!" Sue laughed and said, "I won't spoil it. Let's look at the book together." So, Tim, Sue, and Spot sat under the warm tree, looking at the history book and having a fun time.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Molly. She was very persistent. She wanted to touch the wall in her house but she was too small. Her mum said no. Molly frowned and wouldn't take no for an answer. Every day she asked her mum to let her touch the wall. Finally one day, Molly's mum said yes. Molly was so excited and she ran towards the wall. But when she went to touch it, she got a shock. It was very hot. She got scared and jumped back. Molly's mum smiled and said, "See, when I said no, it was to help keep you safe. It's important to listen to grown ups when they tell you something for your own safety." Molly nodded. She understood. She had been very persistent, but it was ok that this time she had to agree with her mum. From then on, Molly always listened to her mum's instructions and was careful to look out for her own safety.

Once upon a time, there was a little pine tree. The pine tree was not very big, but it was happy. It had many friends in the forest. The pine tree wanted to be the most successful tree in the forest. One day, a small bird came to the pine tree. The bird was sad. The pine tree wanted to help the bird. It used its soft branches to comfort the bird. The bird felt better and started to sing. The other trees in the forest heard the bird's song. They saw how the pine tree helped the bird. They thought the pine tree was very successful. The little pine tree was so happy to have made a friend and be the best tree it could be.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to play all day long. He would run, jump, and fetch sticks. One day, Max saw a big cat. Max wanted to play with the cat. Max said, "Please, cat, play with me." But the cat was not nice. The cat said, "No, you are foolish. I will not play with you." Max felt sad, but he still wanted to play. Max tried and tried to make the cat play. He would run around the cat and bark. The cat got mad. The cat jumped on Max and scratched him. Max cried and ran away. Max learned not to play with mean cats.

Once upon a time, there was a big red barn. In the barn, there was a little mouse. The little mouse liked to squeeze into small holes. The mouse was very good at it. It made him feel successful. One day, the little mouse saw a big hole. He wanted to squeeze into it. He thought it would be fun. So, he went to the big hole and tried to squeeze in. But, he got stuck. Then, something unexpected happened. A big cat came to the barn. The cat saw the little mouse stuck in the hole. The cat was very hungry. The little mouse could not run away. The cat ate the little mouse. The end.

One day, a little bug went for a crawl. The bug saw a big hedge. The hedge was very light green. The bug wanted to see what was on the other side of the hedge. The bug met a tiny ant. The ant said, "I know a hole in the hedge. I can show you." The bug and the ant crawled to the hole. The bug was excited to see what was on the other side. When they got to the other side, the bug saw a big, pretty garden. There were many flowers and trees. The bug was so happy. The bug said, "Thank you, ant, for showing me this beautiful place!"
