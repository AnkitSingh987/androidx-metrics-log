Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Bird

One day, a little girl named Mia and her dog, Buddy, went for a walk. The sun was shining, and the sky was clear. They walked and walked, and it was very hot. Mia started to feel the heat, so she said, "Buddy, let's find some shade." They found a big tree and sat under it. Mia twisted her hair into a bun to stay cool. Suddenly, they heard a noise. "What's that?" asked Mia. Buddy barked and ran towards the noise. Mia followed Buddy and saw a little bird stuck in a bush. The bird was twisting and turning, trying to get free. "Oh no!" said Mia. "We need to help the bird!" Mia and Buddy worked together to free the bird from the bush. "Thank you for helping me," said the bird. Mia and Buddy were happy they could help their new friend. The bird flew away, and Mia and Buddy continued their walk, feeling proud of their good deed.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a jolly bear named Bobo. Bobo loved to eat all kinds of food. One day, he met a new friend, a little bird named Lulu. Bobo wanted to introduce Lulu to his favorite taste. Bobo took Lulu to a big tree with sweet red apples. They picked some apples and ate them together. Lulu loved the taste of the apples. She was very happy and said, "Thank you, Bobo!" Next, Bobo and Lulu went to a big bush with yummy blueberries. They picked the blueberries and ate them too. Lulu liked the taste of the blueberries even more than the apples. They both laughed and played together, and they became best friends. From that day on, they shared many more tasty treats and had lots of fun in the big green forest.

One day, a brilliant girl named Lucy wanted to cook. She put on her apron and went to the kitchen. Lucy loved to cook because it made her happy. Lucy started to cook a yummy soup. She put in carrots, peas, and corn. While she cooked, Lucy danced and sang. But, oh no! Lucy stepped on her toe. She said, "Ouch! My toe hurts." Lucy's mommy came into the kitchen. She saw Lucy's hurt toe and gave her a hug. Mommy helped Lucy finish the soup. They ate the soup together and it was very yummy. Lucy's toe felt better, and she was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom liked to go to the shop with his mom. One day, they went to buy a new toy for Tom. He was very happy. In the shop, Tom saw a little cat. The cat was on a high shelf. It was scared and could not get down. Tom wanted to save the cat, but he was too little. He told his mom about the cat. Mom got a big man who worked at the shop to help. The big man took the cat down and gave it to Tom. Tom saved the cat and felt very happy. The cat was happy too, and Tom got his new toy. They all went home with big smiles on their faces.

Lily and Ben liked to play with water. They had a spray bottle and a club. The club was a big stick that they found in the yard. They pretended it was a sword or a wand. One day, they saw an enormous bug on the wall. It had many legs and eyes and it was black and shiny. Lily and Ben were scared of the bug. They wanted it to go away. Lily had an idea. She said, "Let's spray the bug with water. Maybe it will fly away." Ben agreed. He gave Lily the spray bottle and he held the club. They went close to the bug. Lily sprayed the bug with water. The bug did not like it. It made a loud noise and moved its legs. Ben waved the club at the bug. He said, "Go away, bug! You are not welcome here!" The bug was angry. It jumped off the wall and flew towards Lily and Ben. They screamed and ran away. The bug chased them around the room. They dropped the spray bottle and the club. They hid under the bed. They waited for a long time. They hoped the bug would leave. They heard a knock on the door. It was their mom. She said, "What are you doing under the bed? Why are you wet? And what is that on the floor?" Lily and Ben came out from under the bed. They pointed at the bug. They said, "Mom, there is an enormous bug in our room! We tried to spray it with water and hit it with a club, but it attacked us!" Their mom looked at the bug. She smiled. She said, "That is not a bug. That is a toy. It is a remote-controlled helicopter. Your dad bought it for you as a surprise. He must have left it on the wall. It is not scary. It is fun. Do you want to try it?" Lily and Ben looked at each other. They felt silly. They said, "Sorry, mom. We did not know. Can we play with it?" Their mom said, "Of course. But first, let's clean up the mess. And next time, ask me before you spray anything with water or hit it with a club." Lily and Ben said, "OK, mom. We love you." They hugged their mom. They picked up the spray bottle and the club. They looked at the toy helicopter. They decided to give it a name. They called it Buzz. They were not scared of Buzz anymore. They wanted to play with Buzz. They said, "Thank you, dad. This is a great surprise."
