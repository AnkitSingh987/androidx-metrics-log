Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Dog and the Ball

One day, a little dog named Max wanted to play with his ball. He tried to find it, but he could not see it anywhere. Max felt bad because he loved his ball so much. Max looked under his bed, but the ball was not there yet. He looked behind the door, but it was not there either. Max tried to think where he last saw his ball, but he just could not remember. Finally, Max went outside to the yard. He saw his ball under a big tree! Max was so happy that he found his ball. He tried to get it out and played with it all day long. Max was not bad anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to chew on things. One day, he found a hot red ball in the park. He was so happy to find a new toy to chew. Spot went to school with his friend, Sam. Sam was a smart boy. He had a quiz at school that day. Spot wanted to help Sam with his quiz. So, Spot took the hot red ball with him to school. At school, Sam was busy with his quiz. Spot saw Sam's quiz and thought it was a toy to chew. Spot chewed Sam's quiz into little pieces. When the teacher saw the chewed quiz, she was not happy. Sam did not pass his quiz, and Spot felt bad for chewing it. They both went home sad.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to climb a small hill. The hill was in the west, near his house. Tim asked his mom, "Can I climb the hill, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful. The hill is very fragile." Tim went outside and started to climb the hill. He saw a big tree and a small bird. The bird was singing a happy song. Tim smiled and kept climbing. When Tim reached the top of the hill, he saw the sun setting in the west. The sky was orange and pink. Tim felt happy and proud. He climbed back down and told his mom all about his adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to draw. One day, he drew a big picture of a cat. The cat had a big smile. Tim was very happy with his picture. He put it on the wall. Later, Tim's mom said it was time to remove the picture. Tim was sad, but he knew it was difficult to keep the picture on the wall. So, he took it off the wall and put it in a box. The next day, Tim opened the box to look at his picture. But, the cat in the picture was not smiling anymore! The cat had a big frown. Tim was very surprised. He showed his mom, and she was surprised too. They put the picture back on the wall, and the cat started smiling again.

One day, a little girl named Sue wanted to create something fun. She looked around her room and saw her toy box. Inside the box, she found some play dough. Sue thought, "I can make something with this!" Sue started to make a little sun with the play dough. She showed her mom and said, "Look, Mom! I made a sun. It is hot!" Her mom smiled and said, "That's great, Sue! But be careful, the sun can be very hot." The next day, Sue went outside to play. She saw the real sun in the sky and remembered what her mom said. Sue looked for a shady spot to play, but there were no available spots. She thought about her play dough sun and decided to create her own shade. Sue went back inside and found a big umbrella. She opened it up and took it outside. Sue put the umbrella in the grass and sat under it. Now she had her own shady spot to play in! Her mom came outside and saw Sue under the umbrella. She said, "Good job, Sue! You found a way to stay cool in the hot sun." Sue smiled and knew she had created something very helpful.
