Title: The Hoop and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a big hoop. It was a very pretty hoop. The hoop was red and blue. It was in a park where kids liked to play. One day, a boy named Tim saw the hoop. He wanted to display the hoop for everyone. He put it on a tree. All his friends looked at the hoop and said, "Wow!" Tim felt rich because he made his friends happy. They all played with the hoop. They laughed and had fun. The hoop made their day great.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly bunny who wanted to explore the world. He hopped around and eventually came across a tunnel. He was very curious and excited. He hopped inside the tunnel and kept hopping until he couldn't see the sun anymore. As he kept hopping, he started to get a little scared. Then, out of the darkness he heard a voice. It was an old mole offering a friendly welcome. The mole said he had been living in the tunnel for a long time and the bunny was welcome to join him. The bunny hesitated at first, but then the mole made him a jolly offer. He said the bunny could stay with him and would never be lonely or scared. The bunny jumped with joy and accepted the offer. The bunny and the mole lived happily ever after in the dark tunnel.
