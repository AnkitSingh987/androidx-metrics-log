Category: Beginner
Title: The Girl Called Lucy and the Church

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Lucy. She was only three, but very curious. One day, Lucy wanted to go to the church. She asked her mum, “Can I go to the church?” Her mum replied, “Yes, but you must balance when you walk. Balance means not to fall over. Can you do that?” Lucy nodded. “Yes, I can balance,” she said. So Lucy went for a walk to the church. But as she arrived, she noticed something strange. She smelled a smelly smell outside the church. “Mum, it smells smelly outside the church,” she shouted. “Ah, yes it does,” her mum said. “Let’s go back home. I think we should find another church to visit.” So they went back home. They found another church and it didn’t smell smelly. They had a nice day as they balanced to the church and back.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park with her mom. It was a sunny day and they were both very happy. They saw a big clock near the park. Lucy didn't know what the clock was for, so she asked her mom to explain. Her mom said, "Lucy, this clock tells us the time. When the big hand and the little hand both point to the number 12, it is noon. Noon is when the sun is high in the sky and it's time for lunch." Lucy was proud that she learned something new. She told her mom, "I can't wait to tell my friends about noon and the big clock!" They had a fun day at the park and went home for lunch when it was noon.

Lily and Ben were playing in the backyard. They liked to jump on the big trampoline. They bounced up and down and laughed. "Look at me, Lily!" Ben said. "I can spring very high!" He sprang up and did a flip in the air. Lily clapped and cheered. "Wow, Ben, you are good!" she said. "Can I try?" "Sure, Lily, it's your turn!" Ben said. He moved to the side and let Lily spring on the trampoline. But then, a rude boy came into the backyard. He was bigger than Lily and Ben. He had a mean face and a loud voice. "Hey, you two, get off my trampoline!" he yelled. "This is my backyard, not yours!" Lily and Ben were scared. They did not know the rude boy. They did not know why he was in their backyard. "But this is our backyard!" Lily said. "We live here! You don't!" The rude boy did not listen. He pushed Lily and Ben off the trampoline. He sprang on it by himself. He laughed and shouted. Lily and Ben ran to the house. They told their mom what happened. Their mom was angry. She went outside and talked to the rude boy. She told him to go away and never come back. She said he was not welcome in their backyard. Lily and Ben hugged their mom. They thanked her for helping them. They went back to the trampoline. They sprang and bounced and had fun. They forgot about the rude boy. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a soft, fluffy bunny named Boo. They were best friends and played together every day. One sunny day, Tim and Boo went to the park to play. Boo hopped and Tim ran. They laughed and had a lot of fun. Suddenly, Tim tripped and fell. "Ouch! My leg hurts," Tim cried. Boo hopped over to Tim and said, "Don't worry, Tim. I'll help you." Boo found a big, soft leaf and put it on Tim's leg. It felt better right away. Tim got up and said, "Thank you, Boo. You're a good friend." Boo smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim. Friends help each other." From that day on, Tim and Boo always helped each other when they needed it. They learned that being kind and helping friends is very important. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a gray cat named Tom. Tom was a good cat who liked to help his friends. One day, his friend, a little girl named Sue, needed help. Sue had a notebook that she needed to deliver to her friend, Tim. Tom wanted to help Sue, but there was a big dog in the way. The dog was very mean and would not let Tom pass. Tom was scared, but he knew he had to help his friend. So, he thought of a plan. Tom found a stick and threw it far away. The dog ran after the stick, and Tom quickly went past the dog. Tom was able to deliver the notebook to Tim. Both Sue and Tim were happy and thanked Tom for his help. The moral of the story is that even when we are scared, we can still find a way to help our friends.
