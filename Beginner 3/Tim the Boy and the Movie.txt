Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Movie

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to watch a movie. He asked his mom, "Can I watch a movie, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but first you need to clean your room." Tim thought it was foolish to clean his room before watching a movie. He wanted to watch the movie now. So, he tried to watch the movie without cleaning his room. But the movie would not play. Tim was sad. He went to his mom and told her the movie would not play. His mom said, "Did you clean your room?" Tim said, "No, I did not." His mom told him, "You need to clean your room first, then the movie will play." Tim cleaned his room and then the movie played. Tim was happy and learned that he should listen to his mom.

Once upon a time, there was an intelligent cat named Tom. Tom liked to play with his ball of yarn. One day, Tom saw a shiny needle in the yarn. He wanted to play with it, but he knew it could hurt him. Tom had an idea. He would ask his friend, the big tree, for help. Tom went to the tree and leaned on it. He told the tree about the needle in his yarn. The tree listened and thought about how to help Tom. The tree had a plan. It shook its branches and dropped a leaf on the needle. The leaf covered the needle, so Tom could play with his yarn safely. Tom was very happy and thanked the tree. From that day on, they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little caterpillar named Cally. Cally loved to crawl on the big green leaves in the garden. She had many friends, but her best friend was a butterfly named Bella. Bella had beautiful wings with lots of colors. One day, Cally saw a zipper on a bag in the garden. She crawled over to the zipper and tried to open it. Inside the bag, she found a pretty dress with tiny wings. Cally was so happy, she put on the dress and showed it to Bella. Bella said, "Wow, Cally! You look so pretty!" Cally felt a little jealous of Bella's big wings, but she was happy to have her tiny wings. They played together in the garden, flying and crawling, and they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a mom, a dad, and a little girl. One day, the little girl saw a game. She asked her dad "Can I play the game?" Dad said "Yes, of course!" and he smiled. The little girl was so glad. She started to dance around the game. Mom said, “Honey, why are you so happy?" The little girl said, “I am playing a game and dancing! I am so glad!” Dad laughed and said “That looks fun. Let’s all dance together!” Mama, Daddy, and the little girl all started to dance around the game. They laughed and smiled as they moved their feet. They were all so glad to be together and the little girl was so happy to have a game to play. The little family danced and danced until sunset, and then they stopped and hugged each other. They all said, “We are so glad we got to dance and play together!”

Once upon a time, a little bird lived in a colorful nest. She loved to fly and sing all day. She also liked to admire the pretty things around her. One day, she saw a post with a big, round top. It was very tall and strong. The little bird thought it was a good place to sit and sing. So, she flew to the post and sat on it. But the post was not a good place to sit. It was a trap! The little bird got stuck and could not fly away. She was very sad and scared. The colorful nest was now empty, and the little bird was gone forever.

Once upon a time, there was a small child named Tim. Tim was sad because he lost his toy. He walked around the house, looking for it. His mom saw him and asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim said, "I can't find my toy, Mom. It is lost and I am sad." His mom wanted to help. She said, "Let me remind you where you played with it last. Was it in your room?" Tim shook his head. "No, Mom. I played with it in the yard." So, they went to the yard to look for the toy. They looked under the bushes and behind the trees, but they could not find it. Tim was very sad. In the end, they never found Tim's toy. He was so sad that he cried. His mom hugged him and said, "I am sorry, Tim. Sometimes we lose things and can't find them again. But we can get a new toy for you." Tim nodded, but he still missed his old toy very much.
