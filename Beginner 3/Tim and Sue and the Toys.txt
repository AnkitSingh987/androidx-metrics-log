Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Sue and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a messy room. In the room, there was a boy named Tim and a girl named Sue. They loved to play with their toys, but they did not like to clean up. One day, their mom told them to separate their toys. Tim had a violin, and Sue had a doll. They did not want to share their toys. They began to fight, and the room became messier. Mom came into the room and saw the mess. She was not happy. She told Tim and Sue that they needed to learn to share and clean up. Tim and Sue listened to their mom and cleaned the room. They learned that sharing and cleaning made everyone happy.

Mama was glad to take her daughter to the park. She wanted the little girl to play with other children and have fun. While they were playing and running around, there was a young boy who wouldn't follow the rules. He raced around the playground, bumping into other children. Mama had enough and called the boy over. "Please quit running around so quickly," Mama said. She smiled at him, but the young boy stuck out his tongue and ran away. Mama looked back at her daughter and gave her a hug. Mama was glad the daughter wasn't being naughty like the young boy. Mama knew that if her daughter acted like this, she would have to deal with it. Mama laughed and continued to supervise her daughter, who was having the time of her life.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a girl named Amy. Amy loved to design things. One day, she found a weird rope in her room. The rope was red, blue, and yellow. Amy thought it was fun and wanted to play with it. Amy went outside to find her friend, Tim. She showed him the weird rope. Tim said, "Wow, that's a cool rope! What can we do with it?" Amy thought for a moment and said, "Let's design a game with the rope!" Amy and Tim used the rope to make a jump rope game. They took turns jumping over the rope while the other one swung it. They laughed and had so much fun. The weird rope made their day special. At the end of the day, Amy and Tim were tired but happy. They put the weird rope away and went home. They could not wait to design more games with the rope in the future.

One day, a brown cat saw a pie on a table. The pie was big and yummy. The cat wanted to eat the pie. The cat jumped on the table and said, "Yum! I will eat this pie." A little dog saw the cat and the pie. The dog said, "No, cat! That pie is not for you!" The cat replied, "But I want it. It smells so good." The dog said, "We must wait for mom to give us the pie." The cat and the dog waited for mom. They were very hungry. Then, a big bird came in the house. The bird saw the pie too. The bird said, "This pie is for me!" The cat and the dog were mad. They did not want to share the pie. The bird took the pie and flew away. The cat and the dog were sad. They did not get to eat the pie. But then, mom came in with a new pie. She said, "Here is a pie for you, cat and dog!" The cat and the dog were happy. They got to eat the new pie.

Once upon a time, there was a big ball. The big ball loved to bounce. One day, the big ball met a trumpet. The trumpet was very loud. The big ball and the trumpet became friends. The big ball and the trumpet played together every day. They had so much fun. The big ball would bounce high in the sky, and the trumpet would play a happy song. They made everyone around them smile and laugh. One day, they saw a big hill. The big ball and the trumpet decided to climb the hill. The big ball bounced up the hill, and the trumpet played a song to cheer them on. When they reached the top of the hill, they looked down and saw their friends. The big ball and the trumpet were so happy. They knew they would always be best friends.
