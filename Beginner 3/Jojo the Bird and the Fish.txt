Category: Beginner
Title: Jojo the Bird and the Fish

One day, a funny bird named Jojo lived by the harbor. The harbor was a place with lots of water and boats. Jojo loved to joke with his friends. He had a brilliant idea for a joke today. Jojo went to his friend, the big fish, and said, "Hey, let's play a joke on the little fish!" The big fish agreed and they both laughed. Jojo told the big fish to pretend to chase the little fish, but not to catch them. The big fish thought it was a funny joke. They started the joke and the big fish chased the little fish. The little fish were scared and swam away fast. Jojo and the big fish laughed and laughed. But then, the little fish swam too far away and got lost. They could not find their way back home. Jojo and the big fish felt bad for the little fish. They tried to find them, but they could not. The joke was not funny anymore. The little fish were lost and scared, and Jojo and the big fish were sad. The joke had a bad ending.

Once upon a time, in a jolly little town, there lived a cat named Tim. Tim loved to stretch his legs every morning when he woke up. One day, as he was stretching, he saw a tiny bird sitting on a branch. "Hi, bird!" said Tim. "Do you want to play with me?" "Okay!" said the bird. "Let's play a game. I will fly, and you try to catch me." Tim and the bird played together for a whole year. They became the best of friends. Every day, they would stretch, fly, and play in the jolly little town. One day, Tim and the bird met a dog named Max. Max was sad because he had no friends. "Max, do you want to be our friend?" asked Tim. "Yes, I would love that!" said Max. So, Tim, the bird, and Max became friends and played together every day. They stretched, ran, and had fun in the jolly little town. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a quick cat and a slow dog were playing near a big pond. They liked to play together even though they were very different. The cat was always fast, and the dog was always slow. The quick cat saw a broken toy boat in the pond. The cat said, "Look, dog! Let's get the broken boat and fix it." The slow dog agreed, and they both went to the edge of the pond. The cat jumped in and splashed water on the dog. The dog laughed and said, "You got me all wet!" The quick cat got the broken boat and brought it back to the dog. They both worked together to fix the boat. When it was fixed, they put it back in the pond and watched it float away. They were very happy and continued to play together all day long.

Jill and her mom walked to the museum. It was pale and pretty and they were excited. "What do you think we'll see today, Jill?" asked mom. "I want to reach a dinosaur!" Jill said. When they arrived at the museum, they started looking around. Then, Jill saw a big dinosaur way above her head. She stretched her hands up and tried to reach the dinosaur. But she couldn't reach it. Mom saw Jill's sad face and said, "Come on, let's keep looking." But no matter where they looked, Jill couldn't reach anything. She started to cry. Mom tried to cheer her up. "Let's try something else," she said. But Jill was too sad. Nothing seemed to help. They had to leave the museum without Jill reaching anything.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man called Bob. Bob was a very happy man and always had a big smile on his face. One day he was sitting in the park when a little girl with braided pigtails came up to him. The little girl was very curious and asked Bob a question. "Why do you always smile?" she said. Bob smiled and said, "Because it makes me and other people happy." The little girl looked at Bob's bald head and giggled. She said, "You're so bald, but you are so happy." "Yes, I am," Bob said. He patted the little girl on the head, and they both sat in the park, smiling together.
