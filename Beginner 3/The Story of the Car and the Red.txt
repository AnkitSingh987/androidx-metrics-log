Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Car and the Red

One day, a big, red car was driving down the road. The car was very selfish and did not like to share the road with others. A small, blue car was driving behind the red car. The blue car wanted to pass, but the red car would not let it. "Please let me pass," said the blue car. "I have an emergency!" But the red car did not care. It just kept driving and did not let the blue car pass. Finally, the red car had to turn left. The blue car quickly turned right and went to help with the emergency. The red car felt bad for being so selfish. From that day on, the red car learned to share the road and be kind to others.

One day, a little girl named Lisa saw a pink bird outside. She watched the bird fly in the sky. She observed its motion as it moved up and down. Lisa wanted to talk to the bird, so she called out, "Hello, bird!" The pink bird heard Lisa and flew down to her. "Hello, Lisa!" the bird said. "I like how you fly," Lisa told the bird. The bird was happy and flapped its wings. Lisa and the pink bird played together all day. They ran and jumped and laughed. They had so much fun. When it was time for the bird to go, Lisa waved goodbye. She knew she would always remember her new pink bird friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim felt uncomfortable one day. His mom gave him a pill to help him feel better. Tim did not like the pill. It was big and hard to swallow. Tim's mom had an idea. She said, "Let's change how we take the pill." She put the pill in a spoon with some yummy jam. Tim tried it and the pill went down easy. He started to feel better. Tim learned that sometimes we need to change how we do things to make them easier. And when we are uncomfortable, it's good to ask for help.

Once upon a time, in a nice little house, there lived a cat named Tom and a dog named Max. They were good friends and liked to play together. They would run in the yard, chase each other, and have lots of fun. One day, the sky became dark and cloudy. Tom and Max heard a loud noise. It was thunder! They were scared and ran into the house to hide. The thunder was so loud, it sounded like a big drum. As they hid under the bed, the thunder began to strike. Tom and Max held each other tight. After a while, the thunder stopped and the sky became clear again. Tom and Max went outside to play, happy that the thunder was gone. They knew they were safe and had each other to keep them brave.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the doctor with his mom. The doctor wanted to take a look inside Tim's body. So, he used a special thing called an x-ray. The x-ray would reveal what was inside Tim's body. Tim was a little scared, but his mom said it would be okay. The doctor took the x-ray and showed it to Tim and his mom. They saw something very high up in Tim's body. It was a toy car! Tim had put the toy car in his nose and it got stuck. Everyone was surprised. They didn't know how the car got so high up. The doctor said they needed to get the toy car out of Tim's nose. He used a long tool to reach the car. Tim was brave and didn't cry. Finally, the doctor got the toy car out. Tim was happy and his mom was relieved. Tim promised he would never put a toy in his nose again.

Once upon a time, there was a funny cat named Tom. Tom had a big job to do. He had to deliver a green ball to his friend, the little dog named Max. Tom started to walk to Max's house. On his way, he saw a big tree. He wanted to climb the tree for fun. He put the green ball down and went up the tree. When Tom was in the tree, something unexpected happened. A bird took the green ball and flew away. Tom was sad and worried. He didn't know what to do. But then, the bird came back with the green ball. The bird was Max's friend too! The bird wanted to help Tom deliver the green ball to Max. They all went to Max's house together and had a fun day playing with the green ball.
