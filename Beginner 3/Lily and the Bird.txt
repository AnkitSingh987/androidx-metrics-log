Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Bird

One day, a big, wide bird was flying in the sky. The bird saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad. She had no food to eat. The wide bird wanted to care for Lily. The bird flew down and said, "Lily, do not be sad. I will find you some food." The bird flew away and came back with some meat. Lily was happy and said, "Thank you, wide bird!" But then, something unexpected happened. The meat started to talk! It said, "I am not just meat. I am a magic meat! If you care for me, I will give you food every day." Lily and the wide bird were very surprised, but they decided to care for the magic meat. And they all lived happily with lots of food.

One day, a boy and a girl went to play. They liked to go to a big tree near their home. The boy said, "Look! I found gold!" He showed the girl a shiny rock. They were very happy. The girl had fair hair, and the gold looked nice in her hand. But then, a big dog came. The dog wanted the gold, too. The boy and girl were scared. The dog barked and jumped. They did not want to give the gold to the dog. They thought, "What can we do?" The boy had an idea. He picked up a stick and threw it far away. The dog saw the stick and ran to get it. The dog liked the stick more than the gold. The boy and girl were surprised. They laughed and played with the dog. They all had fun, and they did not care about the gold anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a big red ball. The ball lay in the east part of the yard. It was very glad to be there. Every day, the ball saw a small girl named Lily. Lily liked to play with the ball. They had a lot of fun together. One day, the ball saw a big tree. The tree had a long branch. The ball thought, "I want to be on that branch." But the ball knew it was not easy. The ball was sad. The next day, Lily came to play. She saw the ball was sad. Lily had an idea. She tried to throw the ball up to the branch. It took a few tries, but she did it! The ball was so glad to be on the branch. It could see the whole yard from up there. Lily and the ball were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to dress up in fun clothes. One day, he wanted to play with his best friend, Sam. He put on his favorite costume and went to Sam's house. At Sam's house, they played a game of pass. They threw a ball back and forth. But then, Tim's costume got in the way. The ball hit him and he fell down. Tim felt upset and started to cry. Sam said, "Don't be upset, Tim. Let's try again." They played pass again, but this time Tim took off his costume. They had lots of fun and Tim learned that it's okay to not wear a costume all the time. The moral value of the story is that sometimes it's better to be yourself and not hide behind a costume.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a big, fair dog named Buddy. They loved to play together in the sun. One day, Lucy's mom said they were going to the beach. Lucy was very happy. She ran to her room to pack her things. She put her swimsuit, her towel, and her toys in a bag. Buddy wagged his tail and watched. At the beach, Lucy and Buddy played in the sand. They built a big sandcastle. Lucy used her towel to dry Buddy off when he got wet. They had a great day at the beach.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his dad. Tim had a kite, and he wanted to see it rise high in the sky. His dad was very dependable, always helping him when he needed it. As they played, Tim said, "Dad, can you help me make my kite rise?" His dad smiled and said, "Of course, Tim! Let's try together." They ran across the grass, and the kite began to rise. Suddenly, a big gust of wind came, and the kite went even higher! But then, something unexpected happened. A bird flew by and grabbed the kite with its beak! Tim and his dad looked at each other, surprised. "Wow!" said Tim. "The bird took my kite!" His dad laughed and said, "Yes, but don't worry. We can get another kite and try again." And so, Tim and his dependable dad went to the store, bought a new kite, and had even more fun at the park. They knew that even when unexpected things happen, they could always count on each other.
