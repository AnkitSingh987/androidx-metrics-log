Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Box

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat popcorn. One day, he found a big, brilliant box full of popcorn. He was so happy! He wanted to eat it all. Tim took the box to the kitchen. He wanted to make the popcorn fast. But he did not know how. He tried to break the box open with his hands, but it was too hard. He thought and thought. Then, he had an idea. Tim put the box in the oven. He thought the oven would make the popcorn. But, something unexpected happened. The popcorn did not come out. Instead, the box broke and lots of little toys came out. Tim was surprised, but he was happy. Now, he had new toys to play with and he could still eat popcorn another day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a wealthy lady. She had a big sack full of toys and a needle to fix them when they broke. One day, the lady went to the park with her sack. She saw many kids playing there. They were happy, but they had no toys. The wealthy lady felt sad for them. She opened her sack and gave each kid a toy. The kids were very happy. They played with their new toys all day. One toy broke, but the lady used her needle to fix it. The kids said thank you to the wealthy lady and played with their toys every day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a careless boy. He always forgot to lock the door when he left his house. One day, Tim went out to play with his friends. He wore his favorite shirt. It was red and blue. While he was playing, a friend asked, "Did you lock your door today, Tim?" Tim thought for a moment and said, "Oops! I forgot again." His friend shook his head and said, "You need to be careful, Tim. Something bad might happen." When Tim came home, he saw that his house was a mess. His toys were all over the floor, and his favorite shirt was gone. Tim was very sad. He knew that if he had locked the door, this would not have happened. From that day on, Tim learned his lesson and always locked the door.

Lily and Ben were playing with their marbles on the floor. They liked to roll them and see who could get them closer to the wall. Lily had a shiny blue marble that was her favorite. Ben had a red marble that was very rough. "Can I see your marble?" Ben asked Lily. "OK, but be careful. It is very pretty and smooth," Lily said. She gave her blue marble to Ben. Ben looked at the blue marble and felt how smooth it was. He wanted to keep it for himself. He put it in his pocket and gave Lily his red marble instead. "Here, you can have mine. It is very rough and fast," he said. Lily took the red marble and felt how rough it was. She did not like it at all. She looked at Ben and saw a bulge in his pocket. "Hey, that is not fair! You took my blue marble and gave me your rough one. Give it back!" she said. "No, I like your blue marble. It is mine now. You can keep the rough one," Ben said. Lily was very angry and sad. She started to cry. She wanted her blue marble back. Mom heard Lily crying and came to see what was wrong. She saw Lily holding the red marble and Ben holding the blue marble in his hand. "What is going on here?" Mom asked. "Ben took my blue marble and gave me his rough one. He is mean!" Lily said. "Ben, is that true? Did you take Lily's marble?" Mom asked. Ben looked down and nodded. He felt bad for making Lily cry. "Ben, that is not nice. You should not take things that are not yours. You should give Lily her marble back and say sorry," Mom said. Ben gave the blue marble back to Lily and said sorry. Lily smiled and said thank you. She gave Ben a hug. Mom was proud of them for making up. She gave them both a kiss. "Why don't you play together and share your marbles? You can have more fun that way," Mom said. Lily and Ben agreed. They played with their marbles and took turns rolling them. They learned to be kind and fair. They had a lot of fun.
