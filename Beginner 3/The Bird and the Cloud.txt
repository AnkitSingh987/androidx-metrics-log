Category: Beginner
Title: The Bird and the Cloud

Once there was a bird who loved the sky. He was always so happy, flapping his wings and chirping his songs. But one day, the bird noticed something strange in the sky. A dark cloud was growing larger and larger. The bird was scared, but he kept flying and singing. The dark cloud kept getting bigger and bigger, and it soon blocked out the sun. The bird had to surrender and go back home. The following day, the bird saw that the dark cloud was gone and the sky was once again full of sunshine. He was happy and flew away to explore. But this time, he spotted something different. The bird saw a good friend of his in the sky. She had been in the dark cloud, but now she was happy and free. The bird and his friend flew together throughout the day, enjoying the sunshine and singing their favorite songs. The bird was so glad that he had surrendered to the dark clouds. Now, they could fly as far as they wanted in the bright and beautiful sky.

Sam and Lily are playing in the park. They like to swing, slide and run. They are having fun. But then Sam notices a big dog. The dog is barking and running towards them. Sam is scared. He runs to his mom. "Mom, mom, there is a big dog!" Sam cries. Lily does not run. She likes dogs. She thinks the dog wants to play. She stays and smiles at the dog. She says, "Hi, doggy. Do you want to be my friend?" But the dog is not friendly. The dog is angry. The dog jumps on Lily and bites her arm. Lily screams. She is hurt. She drops her teddy bear. Sam's mom hears Lily scream. She runs to help her. She pushes the dog away. She picks up Lily and hugs her. She says, "It's okay, Lily. I'm here. You are brave. But you need to be careful. Some dogs are not nice. They can hurt you." Lily cries. She says, "I'm sorry, mom. I just wanted to play. But the dog was mean. He hurt me and my teddy bear." Sam's mom says, "Don't be sorry, Lily. It's not your fault. The dog was bad. He should not bite. Come, let's go to the nurse. She will make you feel better. And we will fix your teddy bear too." Sam comes to Lily. He says, "I'm sorry, Lily. I was scared. I should have stayed with you. Are you okay?" Lily says, "I'm okay, Sam. Thank you for coming. You are my friend." Sam says, "You are my friend too, Lily. I'm sorry the dog hurt you. I hope you feel better soon." Sam and Lily hug. They are friends. They go to the nurse with Sam's mom. The nurse is nice. She cleans Lily's arm and puts a band-aid on it. She gives Lily a sticker and a lollipop. She says, "You are a brave girl, Lily. You will be fine. But remember, not all dogs are friendly. You should always ask before you pet them. And if a dog barks or growls, you should stay away." Lily says, "Okay, nurse. I will remember. Thank you for helping me." Sam says, "Thank you, nurse. You are kind." The nurse says, "You're welcome, Sam and Lily. You are good kids. Now go and play. But be careful and have fun." Sam and Lily smile. They take their teddy bear and lollipop. They go back to the park. They play some more. They are happy. They are friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little goat named Billy. Billy had a big horn on his head. One day, he went to play with his friends. Billy saw his friend, the sad duck named Ducky. Ducky was crying by the pond. Billy asked, "Ducky, why are you sad?" Ducky said, "I lost my toy in the pond." Billy wanted to help Ducky. Billy tried to use his horn to get the toy from the pond. He pushed his horn into the water. But, the toy went deeper into the pond. Billy tried again and again, but he could not get the toy. Ducky cried more. Billy felt sad too. They could not get the toy back. In the end, Ducky and Billy went home with sad faces.
