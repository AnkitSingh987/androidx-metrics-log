Category: Beginner
Title: Fin the Creative Fish and the Chew

Once upon a time, there was a creative little fish named Fin. Fin loved to chew on things he found in the water. One day, he saw something shiny and swam closer to see what it was. Fin saw a hook and thought it was a toy. He wanted to chew on it. Just then, his friend Bubbles the fish swam by and said, "Fin, don't chew on that! It's not a toy, it's a hook! It can hurt you!" Fin listened to Bubbles and swam away from the hook. Later, Fin and Bubbles found a real toy to play with. They had lots of fun together, and Fin was happy he didn't chew on the hook. He was glad he listened to his friend Bubbles, who helped keep him safe.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet little girl named Lucy. She loved to learn and play with her friends in the classroom. One day, her teacher, Miss Rose, decided to teach the kids how to share toys. As the kids played, Lucy noticed that her friend, Tom, did not have a toy. She felt bad and wanted to help. Lucy walked up to Tom and gave him one of her toys to play with. Tom was so happy and said "thank you" to Lucy. The next day, Lucy forgot her toy at home. She was sad and did not know what to do. To her surprise, Tom walked up to her and shared his toy with her. Lucy was so happy and they played together all day. The kids learned that sharing is good and it makes everyone happy.

Lily loved to help her mom cook in the kitchen. She liked to stir the soup, cut the bread, and taste the cheese. But she did not like onions. They made her eyes water and her nose sting. She always said, "No onions, please!" One day, her mom was making a special dish for dad. She said, "Lily, can you help me peel these onions?" Lily did not want to, but she wanted to be a good helper. She said, "Okay, mom, I will try." She took an onion and a knife and started to peel it. But soon, she felt the tears in her eyes and the pain in her nose. She dropped the onion and the knife and ran to the sink. She splashed water on her face and rubbed her eyes. Mom saw her and felt sorry. She said, "Oh, Lily, I'm sorry. You don't have to peel the onions. You can do something else." She hugged Lily and kissed her cheek. Lily felt better. She said, "Thank you, mom. I love you. Can I do something else?" Mom said, "Yes, of course. How about you set the table? You can use the pretty plates and the napkins. You are so graceful with them." Lily smiled. She liked to set the table. She said, "Okay, mom. I will do that." She dried her face and went to the cupboard. She took the plates and the napkins and walked to the table. She put them in the right places. She was careful and graceful. She did not drop anything. Dad came home and saw the table. He said, "Wow, what a beautiful table! Who did this?" Mom said, "Lily did. She helped me cook, too. She is a great helper." Dad said, "She is. She is a wonderful daughter." He hugged Lily and kissed her forehead. Lily felt happy. She said, "Thank you, dad. I love you. Can we eat now?" Dad said, "Yes, we can. Let's eat this delicious dish that mom made. It has onions, but they are sweet and soft. You might like them." Lily said, "Maybe I will try them. Just a little bit." She took a bite and chewed. She did not cry or sneeze. She said, "They are not bad. They are good." Mom and dad smiled. They said, "We are glad you like them. We are proud of you. You are brave and kind." They kissed Lily again and ate their dinner. They were a happy family.

Little Milly was walking in the park, looking for feathers. She found a little white feather, and she picked it up. She said to herself, "I wonder where this feather came from." She kept walking until she saw her friend Maggie. Milly ran over to her and showed her the feather. She asked Maggie, "Do you know where this feather came from?" Maggie studied the feather and said, "I think it came from a bird." Milly looked up at all the trees and said, "I wonder what kind of bird it was?" She got down on her knees and looked around, but she didn't spot any birds. Maggie said, "Why don't you climb up the tree and see if you can find out what kind of bird it was?" Milly smiled and said, "Yes! That's a great idea!" She started to climb the tree, and soon she spotted a little yellow bird with bright blue wings. It was singing a happy song. Milly smiled and said, "I found it!" She waved goodbye to Maggie and said, "Thank you for helping me figure out where my feather came from!
