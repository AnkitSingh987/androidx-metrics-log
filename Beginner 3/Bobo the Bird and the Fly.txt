Category: Beginner
Title: Bobo the Bird and the Fly

Once upon a time, there was a big bird named Bobo. Bobo loved to fly high in the sky. He was very fit and strong. Every day, he would prepare to fly by eating lots of yummy worms and stretching his wings. One day, as Bobo was flying in the sky, he saw a little bird named Lulu. Lulu was not fit like Bobo. She was sad because she could not fly high. Bobo wanted to help Lulu, so he showed her how to prepare to fly. First, they ate yummy worms together. Then, they stretched their wings. Soon, Lulu became fit and strong like Bobo. They both flew high in the sky, happy and free. And they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a girl named Sue. She loved to decorate her room with pretty things. One sunny day, she found a big box in her room. Sue was very happy and excited. Inside the box, Sue found a big hat. She put the hat on her head and looked in the mirror. "Wow, I look so pretty!" she said. Sue wore the hat outside to show her friends. It was a mild day, just right for playing. But when Sue went outside, a strong wind came and blew her hat away. Sue was sad and cried. Her friend Tom came and said, "Don't cry, Sue. We can make a new hat and decorate it together!" So, they made a new hat and learned that sharing and helping each other is more important than having pretty things.

Once upon a time, there was a big brown dog named Max. Max lived in a small house with his family. He loved to play and eat yummy food. One day, Max saw a big refrigerator in the kitchen. He knew there was food inside. Max wanted to eat the food, but he knew it was wrong to steal. Max was very hungry, so he decided to try and take some food anyway. Max tried to open the refrigerator, but it was too high for him. His family saw him trying to steal the food and said, "No, Max! It's not nice to steal." Max felt sad and knew he did something wrong. His family gave him a big hug and shared some food with him. Max learned that it was better to ask for help than to steal.

Tom was a mighty boy. He could run fast, jump high and lift heavy things. He liked to play with his friends in the park. They had a lot of fun together. One day, a new boy came to the park. His name was Sam. He was shy and quiet. He did not have any friends. He watched Tom and his friends play, but he did not join them. Tom saw Sam and felt sorry for him. He went to him and said, "Hi, I am Tom. What is your name?" "Sam," the new boy said. "Do you want to play with us?" Tom asked. Sam nodded. He wanted to play, but he was afraid. He did not know if he would belong. Tom smiled and took Sam's hand. He said, "Come on, Sam. You can play with us. You belong with us. We are friends." Sam felt happy. He followed Tom and met his friends. They welcomed him and shared their toys. They played tag, catch and hide and seek. Sam had a lot of fun. He felt like he belonged. Tom was a mighty boy. He was also a kind boy. He made a new friend and helped him belong. Sam was grateful. He was also a mighty boy. He made a new friend and learned to belong.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a toy box full of toys. One day, she found a dirty tape in her toy box. Mia did not know where it came from. She started to wonder who put it there. Mia went to her big brother, Tom. She asked, "Tom, did you put this dirty tape in my toy box?" Tom looked at the tape and said, "No, Mia. I did not put it there. I wonder who did." Mia and Tom went to their mom. They showed her the dirty tape. Mom said, "Ah, that tape is from when we fixed your toy car. Let's clean it and put it away." They cleaned the tape and put it in the right place. Mia and Tom were happy. They played with their toys and forgot about the dirty tape.
