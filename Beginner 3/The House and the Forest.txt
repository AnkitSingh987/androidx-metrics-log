Category: Beginner
Title: The House and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a small house. In the house lived a little boy named Tim and his mom. One day, Tim's mom told him, "Tim, do not go near the wall at the end of the forest. It is not safe." But Tim was ignorant and did not listen to his mom. He wanted to see the wall. So, he went to the forest to find the wall. He saw many animals on his way, but he did not stop. He wanted to see the wall. When Tim got to the wall, he saw a big door. He wanted to open it, but he felt scared. He tried to resist, but he could not. He opened the door and went inside. Suddenly, the door closed behind him, and it was very dark. Tim was very scared and sad. He should have listened to his mom. Now, he was lost and could not go back home.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was playing in his room when he saw a mysterious box. He wanted to know what was inside, so he opened it. Inside the box was a big switch. Tim thought, "What does this switch do?" Tim wanted to find out, so he turned the switch on. Suddenly, his toys started to move and dance! Tim was so happy. He thought, "This switch is magic!" Tim played with his dancing toys all day long. At the end of the day, Tim was tired. He thought, "It's time for bed." So, he turned the switch off, and the toys stopped dancing. Tim put the mysterious box under his bed and went to sleep. He knew he would have more fun with the magic switch tomorrow.

Once upon a time there was a friendly lady named Lucy. Everywhere she went she carried a newspaper, and everyone she met thought she was charming. One day Lucy was walking through the park and she stopped to sit on a bench. There was a little boy named Jack sitting on the bench too. Jack looked at Lucy and blinked. "Why do you have a newspaper, Lucy?" he asked. Lucy smiled. "I like to read the newspaper to find out what's happening in the world," she replied. Jack smiled too. "That sounds fun," he said. Lucy nodded. "It is," she said. She patted Jack's arm. "Come on, let's go play," she said. So Jack and Lucy got up and ran around the park together and laughed and giggled. It was very charming. The end!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to jump and play at the park. One sunny day, he went to the park with his mom. At the park, Tim met a new friend named Sam. Sam had a sour face. Tim asked, "Why are you sad?" Sam said, "I can't jump high like you." Tim smiled and said, "I can help you jump high." Tim and Sam practiced jumping together. Soon, Sam could jump high too. They both laughed and played together. They had so much fun at the park. The moral of the story is: when we help our friends, we can all have more fun.

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing in her room. She had many toys on the floor. She liked to play with her dolls and cars. But today, she was upset because her room was very messy. Her mom came into the room and said, "Lily, you need to clean up your toys. The floor is covered with them!" Lily looked around and saw that her mom was right. The floor was covered with toys. Lily started to pick up her toys and put them away. As she cleaned up, she began to feel better. Soon, the floor was clean and Lily was not upset anymore. She said, "Mom, I cleaned up my toys. Now I can play again!" Her mom smiled and said, "Good job, Lily. Now you can have fun in your clean room!"

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Max, went to play hide and seek. Tim was a stubborn boy who always wanted to win. He told Max, "You hide, and I will find you." Max ran to hide behind a big tree. Tim started to count, "One, two, three..." When he finished, he looked for Max. He saw Max's tail behind the tree. "I found you, Max!" Tim said with a big smile. Max came out from behind the tree with a shock in his eyes. Tim wanted to play again, but Max was tired. Max said, "No more, Tim. I am too tired." But Tim was stubborn and didn't listen. He told Max to hide again. Max went to hide, but this time he went too far. Tim looked and looked, but he could not find Max. He was sad and scared. He never found Max again.
