Category: Beginner
Title: The Great Cat of Tim and the Magic

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a tiny bug on the ground. The bug was only an inch long. Tim bent down to look at the bug. The bug was in the dark shade of a big tree. "Hi, bug!" Tim said. The bug looked up at Tim and spoke, "Hello, Tim!" Tim was very surprised. He did not know that bugs could speak. "I can talk because I am a magic bug," said the bug. "I can give you one wish. What do you want?" Tim thought for a moment. He wanted to be able to speak to all the animals. "I want to talk to all animals," Tim said. The magic bug nodded and touched Tim's nose with its tiny leg. "Now you can speak to animals," said the bug. Tim went home and found his cat. He said, "Hi, cat!" The cat looked at Tim and said, "Hi, Tim!" Tim was so happy that he could talk to animals. He spent the whole day speaking to his cat and all the other animals he could find.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they meet a new boy. His name is Max. He has a big bike and a loud voice. "Hi, I am Max. Do you want to play with me?" he asks. Lily and Tom nod. They think Max is cool. They follow him to the swings. "Look at me, I can swing very high!" Max says. He pushes the swing hard. He laughs. Lily and Tom try to swing too. But Max does not wait for them. He jumps off the swing and runs to the slide. "Come on, slowpokes!" he shouts. Lily and Tom feel sad. They do not like Max. He is bad. He does not share. He does not care. They go to the slide. But Max is there. He blocks the way. "Only I can go on the slide. You are too small and silly," he says. Lily and Tom are angry. They want to go on the slide. They do not like Max. He is mean. He is a judge. They say, "No, you are not the boss. You are not nice. You are not our friend." They push Max away. They go on the slide. They have fun. Max is alone. He cries. He wants to play. He wants to have friends. But Lily and Tom do not want to play with him. They do not want to meet him. They do not like him. He is bad. He is a judge.

Once there was a little boy named Peter. He always liked to drink something special, but he never knew what. Every day his mom gave him a glass of orange juice. One day Peter asked his mom, "Can I have something different to drink?" His mom replied, "How about some warm salt water?" Peter wasn't sure about the idea, but he decided to try it. He was surprised about the taste. Even though it was a little salty, it was delicious. From that day on, Peter drank little sips of warm salt water every day. One day, Peter grew up and became a doctor. He still drank warm salt water once a day, and he even recommended it to other people for certain health benefits. He was glad he had made that little decision to try something different.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with shapes. He had a big box full of shapes like circles, squares, and triangles. One day, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue saw Tim playing with his shapes and asked, "Can I play with your shapes too?" Tim thought it was only fair to share, so he said, "Yes, you can play with my shapes." Together, they grabbed the shapes and started to build a big tower. They used the circles for the base, the squares for the middle, and the triangles for the top. They laughed and had fun building the tower. Suddenly, the tower fell down and the shapes went everywhere. Tim and Sue looked at each other and giggled. They both grabbed the shapes again and started to build another tower. From that day on, Tim and Sue became best friends. They always played with the shapes and shared their toys. It was a happy time for both of them.
