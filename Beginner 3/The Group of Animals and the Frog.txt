Category: Beginner
Title: The Group of Animals and the Frog

Once upon a time, there was a group of animals that lived in the forest. There was a big bear, a little rabbit, and a funny bird. They were all good friends and played together every day. One day, they found something new in the forest. It was big, green, and slimy. "What is that?" asked the little rabbit. "I don't know," said the big bear. "It looks disgusting!" said the funny bird. They all looked at it and wondered what it could be. Just then, the big, green, slimy thing started to move. The animals were scared! But then, a small, friendly frog jumped out of the slimy thing. "Hi!" said the frog. "I was just playing in the mud. It's fun!" The animals were happy it was just a frog, and they all laughed. They asked the frog to join their group, and they all played together in the forest from that day on.

Lily and Ben are playing with their toys in the living room. Lily has a doll with a dress and a hat. Ben has a car with wheels and a horn. They are having fun, but they want different things. "I want to play with your doll," Ben says. He tries to grab the doll from Lily's hands. "No, this is my doll. You have your car," Lily says. She holds the doll tight and pulls it away from Ben. "But your doll can speak. She says hello and bye-bye. My car can only beep," Ben says. He is sad and angry. "So what? My doll can speak, but she has a stitch on her arm. She got hurt and I had to fix her. Your car is fine, it has no stitch," Lily says. She shows Ben the stitch on the doll's arm. It is a small, red line. Ben looks at the stitch. He feels sorry for the doll. He thinks of a way to make Lily happy. "I have an idea. Why don't we trade toys for a little while? You can play with my car and I can play with your doll. Then we can give them back," Ben says. He smiles and holds out his car. Lily thinks about it. She likes the car. It has wide wheels and a shiny horn. She nods and gives Ben her doll. "Okay, we can trade. But be careful with my doll. Don't make her speak too much. She needs to rest her stitch," Lily says. She takes the car and makes it zoom on the floor. "Okay, I will be careful. I will make her speak only a little. She can say hello and bye-bye to my car," Ben says. He hugs the doll and makes her speak softly. Lily and Ben are happy. They play with their toys and share them. They are good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty butterfly named Bob. Bob was a very colorful butterfly. He had many friends in the forest. One day, he met a little girl named Lily. Lily was very sad and worried. "Why are you sad?" asked Bob the butterfly. "I lost my way home," Lily said, her eyes full of tears. "Don't worry, I can help you find your way home," said Bob. So, they went together to find Lily's home. As they walked, they saw a big tree with a door in it. They opened the door and found a room full of candy! Lily was so surprised! "I didn't know this was in the forest," she said. Bob and Lily ate some candy and were very happy. Then, they continued their search for Lily's home. Finally, they found Lily's home. Her mom and dad were so happy to see her! They thanked Bob the mighty butterfly for helping Lily. From that day on, Lily and Bob became best friends, and they never worried when they were together.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful little girl named Lucy. She loved to play with her ball outside. One day, she saw a sad boy sitting on the grass. His name was Tom. He was holding his stomach and looked like he was in pain. Lucy walked up to Tom and asked, "Why are you sad? Does your stomach hurt?" Tom nodded his head and said, "Yes, my stomach hurts because I am hungry." Lucy felt bad for Tom and wanted to help him. Lucy had an idea. She went to her house and got some food. She came back and gave the food to Tom. Tom ate the food and felt much better. Then, Lucy said, "Let's play with my ball!" Tom smiled and agreed. They took turns to throw the ball to each other and had a lot of fun. From that day on, Lucy and Tom became best friends.
