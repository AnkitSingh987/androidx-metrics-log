Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Yellow Dog and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a yellow dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside. One day, he found a video. He did not know what it was, so he decided to bury it in the ground. The next day, Spot went to the same place to dig up the video. But it was not there! He looked around and saw a big bird holding the video in its beak. The bird flew away with the video. Spot was sad, but then he saw a little girl crying. She said that she lost her video. Spot knew what to do. He ran after the big bird and barked loudly. The bird got scared and dropped the video. Spot picked it up and gave it to the little girl. She was very happy, and they became best friends.

Lila and Ben liked to go to the park with their mom. They liked to play on the swings, slide down the slide, and run on the grass. Sometimes they saw a vendor who sold ice cream and balloons. The vendor was gentle and kind. He always smiled and gave them a treat. One day, when they were at the park, they saw a big dog. The dog was not gentle. It barked and growled and chased the birds. Lila and Ben were scared of the dog. They ran to their mom and hugged her. Their mom told them not to worry. She said she would protect them from the dog. The dog saw the vendor and ran towards him. The vendor was holding a bunch of balloons. The dog wanted to pop the balloons. It jumped and bit the balloons. The balloons made loud noises. The vendor was scared and dropped the ice cream. The ice cream fell on the ground. Lila and Ben felt sorry for the vendor. They wanted to help him. They asked their mom if they could give him some money. Their mom said yes. They took some coins from their mom's purse and ran to the vendor. They said, "We are sorry about the dog. Here is some money for the ice cream and the balloons. You are gentle and kind. Thank you for the treats." The vendor was happy and surprised. He thanked Lila and Ben for their generosity. He gave them each a new balloon and a new ice cream. He said, "You are gentle and kind, too. You made my day better. Thank you for the money. You are my heroes." Lila and Ben smiled and hugged the vendor. They felt proud and happy. They went back to their mom and showed her their balloons and ice cream. Their mom hugged them and said, "You are very brave and nice. You protected the vendor from the dog. You are my heroes, too."

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big sister named Sue. Tim was very proud of his big sister. Sue was good at many things, and Tim liked to watch her play. One day, Sue was playing with her friends. Tim wanted to play too, but Sue said, "No, Tim. You are too little." Tim felt sad and went to sit under a tree. He sat there for a minute, thinking about how he hated being left out. Then, Tim had an idea. He went to his room and found his favorite toy car. He brought it outside and showed it to Sue and her friends. They all thought it was cool and let Tim join their game. Tim was so happy, and Sue was proud of her little brother.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a small mirror that she loved very much. She would look into the mirror and talk to her reflection. One day, Sue said to her reflection, "I wish I could grow big and tall like my big sister." Her mom heard her and said, "Don't worry, Sue. One day, you will grow big and tall too. You just need to wait and see." Sue felt glad and hoped that her mom was right. Days went by, and Sue would look in the mirror every day to see if she had grown. One day, she noticed that her reflection looked a bit taller. She ran to her mom and said, "Look, Mom! I am growing!" Her mom smiled and said, "I told you that you would grow, Sue. Just be patient and eat your food, and you will grow even more." Sue was very happy and continued to watch herself grow in the mirror. She knew her mom was right, and she was glad that she was growing big and tall like her sister.
