Category: Beginner
Title: The New Cat Came to Town and the Tree

One day, a new cat came to town. His name was Tom. He liked to rest under the big tree. The big tree was his favorite place. One day, while Tom was resting, he saw smoke coming from a small house. "Oh no!" said Tom. He ran to the house to see what was wrong. The smoke was coming from a big pot. A kind lady was cooking soup. The lady saw Tom and said, "Hello, Tom! I made this soup for you!" Tom was very surprised. He thought the house was on fire, but it was just the lady cooking soup for him. Tom and the lady became very good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and her dog, Spot. Amy liked to help her mom cook in the kitchen. One day, they were making a great, big cake for her dad's birthday. Amy's mom asked her to borrow some salt from their friend, Mr. Brown. So, Amy put on her shoes, and with Spot by her side, they went to Mr. Brown's house. When they got there, Mr. Brown was happy to see them. He gave Amy the salt she needed and told her to have fun making the cake. Amy and Spot went back home with the salt. They put it in the cake mix, and the great cake was ready to bake. When the cake was done, they all ate it together. Everyone loved the cake, and Amy was proud of her help. They all had a great time at the party.

Once upon a time, there was a humble dog named Mark. Mark loved to take naps under the big tree in his yard. Every day, after playing with his ball, he would lay down and sleep. One day, while Mark was taking his nap, a little bird came to sit on the tree. The bird saw Mark sleeping and said, "Hi, Mark! Do you like to sleep?" Mark woke up and answered, "Yes, I love my naps. They make me feel good." From that day on, Mark and the little bird became friends. They played together and took naps under the big tree. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a pale cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big puddle from a flood. He wanted to jump in it. But, he was scared he would shrink if he got wet. Tom saw his friend, a dog named Max. Max said, "I want to jump in the puddle too, but I am scared I will shrink." Tom and Max thought for a moment. They had an idea. They found a big stick and poked the puddle. The water started to shrink! They poked it more and more until the puddle was very small. Now, they were not scared to jump in it. Tom and Max jumped in the tiny puddle. They did not shrink! They laughed and played in the small puddle. They were happy they could have fun without shrinking.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a pretty butterfly. Lily wanted to catch the butterfly. So, she ran after it. The butterfly flew away from her. Lily felt angry. She called out to the butterfly, "Please come back, I just want to play!" The butterfly stopped and looked at Lily. It said, "Okay, but you must be gentle." Lily promised to be gentle. They played together in the park. They had so much fun. Lily and the butterfly became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named True. True had a big red ball. He loved to play with his ball every day. One day, True wanted to play with his ball, but he had to wait. His mom said, "True, you must delay your playtime because we have to go to the store." True was sad, but he knew he had to listen to his mom. They went to the store and bought some food. True saw a big, high shelf with lots of toys on it. He wanted to look at the toys, but his mom said, "We must hurry, True. We can look at the toys next time." After they left the store, True and his mom went back home. True was so happy because now he could play with his big red ball. He threw the ball high in the air and caught it. He had so much fun playing with his ball. True learned that sometimes you have to delay the things you want to do, but in the end, it is worth the wait.
