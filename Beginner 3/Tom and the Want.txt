Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and the Want

Tom was a kid who did not like to bathe. He thought it was boring and cold and he hated soap. He liked to play in the mud and dirt and make messes. He did not care if he smelled bad or looked dirty. One day, his mom said, "Tom, it is time to bathe. You are very dirty and you need to be clean. Come on, let's go to the bathroom." But Tom said, "No, mom, I don't want to bathe. I want to play more. Bathing is no fun." He ran away and hid under his bed. His mom looked for him and found him. She said, "Tom, you cannot hide from me. You have to bathe. It is good for you. It will make you feel fresh and happy. And you will look nice and smell good. Don't you want that?" But Tom said, "No, mom, I don't care. I like being dirty. It is fun. Bathing is not fun. Leave me alone." His mom was sad and angry. She said, "Tom, you are being very naughty and stubborn. You are making me upset. You have to listen to me. I am your mom and I know what is best for you. If you don't bathe, you will have no dessert and no stories tonight. And you will have no friends. They will not want to play with you if you are dirty and smelly. Is that what you want?" Tom thought about it. He liked dessert and stories. He liked his friends. He did not want to make his mom upset. He did not want to be alone. He felt ashamed. He said, "Sorry, mom. I don't want to be naughty and stubborn. I don't want to be dirty and smelly. I want to be clean and nice. I want to have dessert and stories. I want to have friends. I want to make you happy. I will bathe. Please help me." His mom smiled and hugged him. She said, "That's a good boy, Tom. I am proud of you. I love you. Come on, let's go to the bathroom. I will help you bathe. It will be fun. You will see." She took him by the hand and led him to the bathroom. Tom followed her and got ready to bathe. He felt better. He learned his lesson. He decided to bathe more often. He did not want to be ashamed again.

Once upon a time, there was a graceful bird named Bella. Bella liked to fly high in the sky. She had many friends in the forest. One day, Bella and her friends went on a journey. They wanted to find a big tree to play in. They flew and flew until they found the perfect tree. The tree was very tall and had many branches. Bella and her friends had so much fun in the tree. They played all day long. They learned the value of being together and having fun. At the end of the day, they were all very happy and tired. They knew that their journey was a great adventure.

One day, a big red ball went bounce, bounce, bounce down the street. It was a very modern ball and all the kids loved it. The ball was so happy to be outside and play. Suddenly, the ball got stuck in a tree. "Oh no!" said the ball. "This is an emergency! I need help!" A kind little girl named Lily heard the ball and wanted to help. "Don't worry, ball," said Lily. "I will get you down!" Lily tried to jump and reach the ball, but she was too small. Then, she had an idea. She found a long stick and used it to push the ball out of the tree. The ball was free and went bounce, bounce, bounce again. "Thank you, Lily!" said the ball. "You saved me!" Lily smiled and said, "You're welcome, ball. Let's play together!" And they played and bounced all day long.

Once upon a time, there was an old man with a powerful magic. Every day, the old man would spread his magic dust over the ground. One day, the old man asked a 3 year old child, "Do you want to feel my powerful magic too?" The child was excited and nodded eagerly. So, the old man carefully poured a tiny bit of the magical dust over the child's skin and spread it out evenly. The child felt something strange, like their body was filled with tons of energy! They jumped up and down and laughed and smiled. The old man laughed and said, "There, now you can feel the powerful magic too!" The child was so happy they hugged the old man and thanked him. They could feel the magic all day long and it felt wonderful.
