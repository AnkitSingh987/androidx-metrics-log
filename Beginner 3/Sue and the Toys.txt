Category: Beginner
Title: Sue and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her toys. She had a big toy box with lots of fun things inside. One day, Sue's mom told her to be patient, because they were going to have a special dinner later. Sue was excited and couldn't wait. Sue played with her toys all day. She played with her dolls, her cars, and her blocks. She built a big tower with her blocks and pretended that her dolls were having a fancy dinner at the top. Sue's mom watched her play and smiled. "You're being very patient, Sue," her mom said. "Keep playing, and soon it will be time for our special dinner." Finally, it was time for the special dinner. Sue's mom called her to the table. When Sue saw the table, she couldn't believe her eyes. It looked just like the fancy dinner she had made for her dolls. There were plates, cups, and even a little cake. Sue was so happy, and she knew that being patient and playing with her toys had made the time go by faster. She enjoyed the special dinner with her mom, and they talked about all the fun they had playing that day.

Lily and Max were playing in the garden. They liked to dig and water the plants. Lily saw a big squash that was green and yellow. She wanted to pick it and show it to Max. "Look, Max, a squash!" she said. "Can we pick it?" Max shook his head. "No, Lily, we can't. Mom said we have to wait until it is ripe. If we pick it now, it will be bad and we can't eat it." Lily felt frustrated. She wanted to pick the squash. It looked so nice and round. She tried to pull it, but it was too hard. She pulled harder, but it did not move. "Stop, Lily, stop!" Max said. "You will hurt the squash and the plant. Mom will be angry." Lily did not listen. She pulled harder and harder. She was angry and sad. She wanted the squash. Suddenly, she heard a snap. The squash came off the plant, but it was not whole. It was broken in half. Lily saw the inside of the squash. It was white and soft. It did not look good. Lily started to cry. She dropped the squash and ran to Max. She hugged him and said, "I'm sorry, Max, I'm sorry. I broke the squash. Mom will be mad. What can we do?" Max felt sorry for Lily. He hugged her back and said, "It's okay, Lily, it's okay. Don't cry. We can tell Mom what happened. Maybe she can fix it. Or maybe she can make something else with it. Don't worry, Lily, don't worry. We can make it better."

One day, a boy named Tom found a broken toy in the park. He wanted to fix it, so he took it home. His mom saw the toy and nodded, saying he could try to fix it. Tom tried to fix the broken toy near the sink in the kitchen. While he was fixing it, his cat, Max, jumped on the table. Max wanted to play with the toy, but Tom said "no." Max was sad and angry. Max did not listen to Tom and jumped on the toy. It fell into the sink with a big splash. The toy was now wet and more broken. Tom was very sad, and Max knew he did a bad thing. They could not fix the toy, and it stayed broken forever.

Once upon a time, in a big brown house, there lived a little girl named Lucy. She loved to wander around her big backyard, looking for bugs and pretty flowers. She had a best friend, a happy dog named Max. Max was brown and very fluffy. They loved to play together all day long. One day, while they were wandering in the yard, Lucy and Max saw two birds having a fight. The birds were loud and jumped around, trying to win the fight. Lucy and Max didn't like to see the birds fighting, so they clapped their hands and barked to make the birds stop. The birds stopped fighting and flew away. Lucy and Max were happy that the fight was over. They continued to wander around the yard, playing and laughing. From that day on, Lucy and Max promised to help any friends who were fighting, so everyone could be happy and play together.
