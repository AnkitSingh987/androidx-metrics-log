Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog and the Box

One day, a big dog named Spot wanted to run. He liked to run every week. Spot had thick, soft fur that his friend, Sam the cat, liked to pet. Spot said to Sam, "Let's run to the big tree and back!" Sam agreed and they started to run. They ran fast, but then they saw a big box on the ground. They stopped and looked at the box. Sam said, "What is in the box?" They opened the box and found a big cake inside! They were so happy and surprised. They shared the cake with their friends and had a fun day. The end.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a big, red balloon tied to a bench. Tim loved balloons, so he asked his mom if he could have it. "Yes, you can have it," said his mom. Tim felt very happy and held the balloon tight. While they were walking, a strong wind blew the balloon out of Tim's hand. The balloon went up, up, up into the sky. Tim started to cry. He was sad because he lost his balloon. His mom tried to be helpful and said, "Don't cry, Tim. We will find another balloon." Just then, a nice girl named Sue walked up to Tim. She saw him crying and wanted to help. Sue said, "I have a blue balloon. You can have it if you want." Tim stopped crying and smiled. He said, "Thank you, Sue. You are very helpful." Tim took the blue balloon and played with it all day long.

Once upon a time there was a girl called Sarah. She was three years old and very thirsty. “I want a drink,” Sarah said. Her mum gave her a big glass of squash. It was great! Sarah took a big gulp and enjoyed it. She gulped and gulped until the glass was empty. “Oh no!” said Sarah. “It all gone! I want some more.” But Sarah’s mum said no more squash for her. Sarah was very sad. “Mummy, please!” she said. But Sarah’s mum just said no. Sarah was very thirsty but she had to go to bed without her squash. It was not a great ending for Sarah. But she had to obey her mum’s instructions. Now Sarah knows that she should not ask for too much squash!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very good at counting. He could count to ten all by himself. One day, Tim wanted to show his friends his impressive counting skills. So, he decided to count all the stars in the sky at night. That night, Tim went outside and started counting the stars. He was so happy to show his friends how good he was at counting. But as he was counting, he got very sleepy. Tim fell asleep and had a bad dream, a nightmare. In the nightmare, all the stars were gone, and he couldn't count them anymore. When Tim woke up, he was scared. But then, he looked up at the sky and saw all the stars were still there. He was so happy it was just a bad dream. Tim went back to counting the stars, but this time, something unexpected happened. As he counted, the stars began to move and form a big smiling face in the sky. The stars twinkled and shone even brighter. Tim knew that everything was okay now and his impressive counting skills had made the stars happy too.

One day, a regular cat named Tom found a guitar. He wanted to play it, but he did not know how. He had an idea. He went to find his friends to unite and learn together. Tom went to his friend, a dog named Max. "Max, I found a guitar. Let's unite and learn how to play it," said Tom. Max was happy and said, "Yes, let's do it." Tom and Max went to their friend, a bird named Lily. "Lily, we found a guitar. We want to unite and learn how to play it. Will you join us?" asked Tom. Lily said, "I would love to!" The three friends united and learned how to play the guitar together. They played songs and had lots of fun. They were happy they united and learned something new.
