Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Anna and the Garden

Tom and Anna like to play with their toy sail. They have a big tub of water in the garden, and they put their sail in it. They pretend they are pirates, and they look for treasure and fight sharks. One day, they see a real sail on the lake near their house. It is white and blue, and it moves fast with the wind. Tom and Anna are very enthusiastic. They want to go on the real sail and have more fun. They run to the lake, and they see a man on the sail. He is wearing a hat and a jacket. He looks busy and angry. Tom and Anna wave at him and shout, "Hello, can we play with your sail?" The man does not wave back. He does not like children. He yells, "Go away, this is my sail! You are not allowed here!" Tom and Anna are scared and sad. They do not understand why the man is so mean. They turn around and run back to their garden. They hug their toy sail and cry. They do not want to play with the real sail anymore. They only want to play with their toy sail, where they can be happy and free.

Ben and Lily went to the lake with their dad. They wanted to catch some fish for dinner. Dad gave them each a rod and a bucket. He showed them how to put a worm on the hook and throw the line in the water. "Be patient and quiet," he said. "The fish will come if they are hungry." Ben and Lily waited and waited. They saw some ducks and frogs, but no fish. They started to feel bored and hungry. "Can we go home now?" Lily asked. "Not yet," Dad said. "We have to catch some fish first." Just then, Ben felt a tug on his rod. He pulled hard and lifted a big fish out of the water. It was shiny and silver, with red spots. "Wow, look at my fish!" Ben shouted. "It's so big and pretty!" Lily clapped her hands. She was happy for Ben, but also a little jealous. She wanted to catch a fish too. "Can I measure your fish?" she asked. "Sure," Ben said. He put his fish in the bucket and took out a ruler. He laid the fish next to the ruler and looked at the numbers. "My fish is 12 inches long!" he said proudly. "That's very good," Dad said. "You caught a trout. They are very yummy." Lily looked at her rod. She hoped she would catch a fish soon. She threw her line in the water again and waited. Suddenly, she felt a tug too. She pulled hard and lifted a fish out of the water. It was brown and green, with a long mouth and sharp teeth. "Wow, look at my fish!" Lily shouted. "It's so scary and cool!" Ben looked at Lily's fish. He was surprised and a little scared. He didn't know what kind of fish it was. "Can I measure your fish?" he asked. "Sure," Lily said. She put her fish in the bucket and took out the ruler. She laid the fish next to the ruler and looked at the numbers. "My fish is 15 inches long!" she said proudly. "That's very good," Dad said. "You caught a pike. They are also very yummy." Ben and Lily smiled at each other. They were both happy. They had caught two fish for dinner. They hugged their dad and thanked him for taking them to the lake. "Let's go home and cook our fish," Dad said. "I bet they will taste delicious." Ben and Lily agreed. They packed their rods and buckets and walked back to the car. They couldn't wait to eat their yummy fish.

Lisa was a wealthy girl who had many toys and clothes. She liked to play with her dolls and dress them up in pretty dresses. One day, she saw a new toy in the store. It was a scale that could tell how much things weighed. Lisa wanted the scale very much, but her mom said no. "Mom, please, can I have the scale?" Lisa asked. "I want to weigh my dolls and see how heavy they are." "No, Lisa, you have enough toys already. You don't need the scale. It is too expensive and not very fun," her mom said. Lisa was sad and angry. She did not listen to her mom. She waited until her mom was busy and then she took the scale from the shelf. She hid it under her coat and ran out of the store. She did not pay for the scale. She stole it. Lisa ran all the way home with the scale. She was happy and excited. She went to her room and put the scale on the floor. She took out her dolls and started to weigh them. She put one doll on the scale and saw the number. She put another doll on the scale and saw another number. She did this for all her dolls. Lisa thought the scale was very fun. She did not think about what she did wrong. She did not think about how her mom would feel. She did not think about how the store would feel. She only thought about herself and her new toy. But then, something happened. The scale started to make a loud noise. It beeped and flashed. It said, "You are a thief. You are a thief. You stole me from the store. You are a thief." Lisa was scared and shocked. She tried to turn off the scale, but it did not stop. She tried to hide the scale, but it did not stop. She tried to throw the scale away, but it did not stop. The scale kept making noise and telling everyone that Lisa was a thief. Lisa cried and cried. She wished she never took the scale. She wished she listened to her mom. She wished she was not a thief. She learned a hard lesson. She learned that stealing is bad and wrong. She learned that having many toys does not make you happy. She learned that being wealthy does not mean you can do whatever you want. She learned that being good and honest is more important than anything.
