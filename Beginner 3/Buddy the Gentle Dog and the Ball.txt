Category: Beginner
Title: Buddy the Gentle Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a gentle dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to play with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. They played in the park every day. One day, Buddy and Lily were playing when they saw a big ball. Buddy said, "Lily, remember when we played with a big ball like this before?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, Buddy! That was so much fun!" They played with the big ball all day and had a great time. When it was time to go home, Buddy and Lily were tired but happy. They knew they would always remember their fun day with the big ball.

Tom and Lily were friends who liked to play with toys. One day, Tom got a new toy plane from his dad. It was big and shiny and made a loud noise when he pushed a button. Tom was very happy and wanted to show Lily his plane. He ran to Lily's house and knocked on the door. Lily opened it and saw Tom holding his plane. "Wow, what a nice plane!" she said. "Can I see it?" Tom nodded and gave Lily his plane. Lily looked at it and touched the wings and the wheels. She liked it too. She wanted to try to make it fly. She asked Tom, "Can I send it in the air?" Tom said, "Sure, but be careful. It is not a real plane. It is a toy plane. It can break if you throw it too hard or too high." Lily said, "OK, I will be careful." She took the plane outside and looked for a good spot to send it. She saw a big tree with thick branches. She thought, "Maybe I can send it over the tree. That would be fun." She ran and threw the plane as hard as she could. The plane flew in the air and made a loud noise. Lily was happy and clapped her hands. But then she saw something bad. The plane did not go over the tree. It hit a thick branch and fell down. It broke into pieces. Lily was sad and scared. She ran to pick up the pieces and cried. Tom heard the noise and came outside. He saw Lily holding the broken plane and crying. He was angry and sad too. He said, "Lily, why did you do that? I told you to be careful. You broke my new plane. It was a gift from my dad. I don't want to be your friend anymore." Lily said, "I'm sorry, Tom. I didn't mean to break it. I just wanted to have fun. Please don't be mad at me. Please be my friend." Tom looked at Lily and saw that she was really sorry. He felt sorry too. He said, "It's OK, Lily. I'm sorry I yelled at you. I know you didn't mean to break it. Maybe we can fix it together. Or maybe we can play with something else. You are still my friend." Lily smiled and hugged Tom. She said, "Thank you, Tom. You are a good friend. I'm sorry I was not careful. Next time, I will listen to you. And I will share my toys with you too." Tom and Lily learned a lesson that day. They learned to be careful with their toys and to respect each other's things. They also learned to forgive and to share. They were happy to have each other as friends.

Once upon a time, in a regular forest, there lived a little bird. The bird loved to fly and sing. One day, while flying, the bird saw a small twig on the ground. The bird picked up the twig and showed it to his friend, the squirrel. "Look what I found!" said the bird. "What can we do with this twig?" The squirrel thought for a moment and said, "Let's use it to help others. We can become helpers in the forest!" So, the bird and the squirrel used the twig to help their friends. They used it to reach high places, to scratch itchy spots, and to play fun games. All the animals in the forest loved the bird and the squirrel. The moral of the story is that even a small thing, like a twig, can be used to help others and make them happy. When we become helpers, we make the world a better place.
