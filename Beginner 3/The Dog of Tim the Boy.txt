Category: Beginner
Title: The Dog of Tim the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to write. He would write all day with his big pencil. He would write about his dog, his cat, and his house. Tim was very happy when he wrote. One day, Tim went to a dance. He saw his friends at the dance. They danced and danced. They jumped and clapped. Tim was so happy. He wanted to write about the dance. But then, Tim saw a dead bug. It was a little dead bug on the floor. Tim felt sad for the bug. He picked it up and took it outside. Tim put the bug under a tree. Then, he went back to the dance and danced with his friends. Tim was happy again.

Once upon a time, in a quiet little town, there lived a girl named Sue. She loved to prepare yummy treats for her friends. One day, she made a big cake for them to share. Sue saw a cat with a wink in its eye. The cat was quiet and sneaky. It wanted to eat the cake too. Sue went to get her friends, but she didn't see the cat jump on the table. When Sue came back with her friends, they saw the cat eating the cake. They were sad because the cake was gone. The cat just gave a wink and ran away. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big mountain. On the mountain, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to laugh and play with his friends. One day, Tim found a tree with sour fruit. He picked some fruit and had an idea. Tim called his friend, Sue, and said, "Sue, let's play a game. We will give this sour fruit to our friends and see their funny faces when they eat it." Sue laughed and said, "That sounds fun, let's do it!" So, they gave the sour fruit to their friends and laughed when they saw their friends' funny faces. But soon, their friends did not want to play with Tim and Sue. They were sad and did not understand why. Tim's mom saw them and asked, "Why are you sad?" Tim said, "Our friends don't want to play with us." Tim's mom said, "Maybe it's because you laughed at them when they ate the sour fruit. It's not nice to laugh at others. You should say sorry." Tim and Sue said sorry to their friends, and they all played together again. They learned that it is important to be kind and not laugh at others.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim lived in a dry tree in a big forest. One day, Tim heard a loud noise. It was a hurricane coming to the forest! Tim was scared. Tim's friend, a kind frog named Sam, saw Tim sigh. Sam said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can find a safe place before the hurricane comes." Tim and Sam went to a big cave to hide. The cave was safe and dry. The hurricane came and went. Tim and Sam were safe in the cave. When they went back to the forest, they saw that the dry tree was gone. Tim was sad, but Sam said, "It's okay, Tim. We can find a new home." They found a new, strong tree for Tim to live in. The moral of the story is: always help your friends in need.

One day, a generous boy named Tim went to his cupboard. He wanted to find something to eat. He looked and looked, but he could not find anything. He was very hungry. Then, something unexpected happened. A small mouse jumped out of the cupboard! The mouse was very hungry too. Tim wanted to help the mouse, so he gave it some of his food. The mouse was very happy. Tim and the mouse became good friends. They played and ate together every day. But one day, when they finished eating, the mouse turned into a big, friendly dog! Tim was surprised, but he was happy to have a new friend. They played and ate together, and they were always generous with each other.
