Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Dog and the Tree

One day, a big dog named Max saw a cat in a tree. The cat was scared and could not get down. Max wanted to help the cat. He tried to reach the cat with his paw, but he was not tall enough. Max was sad. Max saw a girl named Lily. He barked to get her help. Lily said, "What's wrong, Max?" Max looked at the tree and Lily saw the scared cat. She said, "Oh no! We need to help the cat!" Lily and Max tried to find a way to help the cat. They saw smoke from a fire far away. They ran to the fire and found a deaf man who could not hear the fire. Max barked and Lily waved her arms to get his attention. The deaf man saw them and the fire. He was scared, but they all got away from the fire. The deaf man was a tall man. He went with Lily and Max to the tree with the cat. The tall man reached up and saved the cat. The cat was happy, and so were Max, Lily, and the deaf man. They all became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a pink bird. The bird loved to eat limes. Every day, the bird would fly to a big lime tree. The tree would supply many limes for the bird to eat. One day, the bird met a new friend. The friend was a little green frog. The frog also loved to eat limes. The pink bird and the green frog would share the limes from the big lime tree. They had so much fun together. They played games and ate limes all day long. The pink bird and the green frog were very happy. They knew that the big lime tree would always supply them with tasty limes to eat.

One day, a bright sun shined in the sky. A girl named Lily went outside to play. She saw a big mop by the door. Lily had an idea to measure the mop. Lily asked her mom, "Can I measure the mop?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can." Lily took a small ruler and started to measure the mop. She found out it was very long. Lily decided to use the mop to clean. She cleaned the floor and made it shiny. Her mom was happy and said, "Good job, Lily!" Lily felt proud and put the mop away. She went back outside to play under the bright sun.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a girl named Lily. She had a favorite mug. It was a big, red mug. She loved to drink milk in her mug. One day, Lily's mug was empty. She was sad. She asked her mom, "Can you order more milk for my mug, please?" Her mom said, "Of course, Lily!" So, her mom ordered more milk for Lily's mug. The next day, Lily saw her big, red mug full of milk. She was so happy! She drank her milk and smiled. From that day on, Lily's mom always made sure her mug was never empty again.

Once upon a time, there was a prince named Tim. Tim was not like other princes. He did not have many things. He did not have a big castle or fancy clothes. Some people thought he was cheap. One day, Tim saw a cat stuck in a tree. The cat was scared and crying. Tim wanted to help the cat. So, he climbed the tree to rescue it. He held the cat and climbed down. The cat was happy and safe now. People saw what Tim did. They saw that even though he did not have many things, he was kind and brave. They learned that being cheap does not mean you are not a good person. The moral of the story is that it is more important to be kind and helpful than to have many things.

Once upon a time, there was an otter named Sam. Sam was very dependable. He was always on time, always helped his friends, and never let anyone down. One day, Sam was at the lake with his friends. They were all playing and having such a good time! Suddenly, they heard a loud noise. Everyone was scared! But Sam had an idea. He quickly grabbed a stick and used it to reach down into the water. He grabbed a big, slippery fish and threw it on the shore. His friends were so thankful! Sam was everyone's best friend. He was dependable, brave, and always there for them. All his friends cheered for Sam and said his name with a big smile. The end.
