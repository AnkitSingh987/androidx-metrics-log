Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Bird

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw many kids playing and having fun. Tim saw a big slide and wanted to go on it. He said to his mom, "Can I go on the big slide, please?" His mom said, "Yes, you can, but be careful." As Tim played, he saw a rare bird with bright colors. The bird was playing with a ball. Tim ran to his mom and said, "Mom, come see the rare bird!" His mom came and saw the bird too. They both smiled and watched the bird play. After a while, it was time to go home. Tim said goodbye to the park and the rare bird. He told his mom, "I had fun at the park today. I want to come back and see the rare bird again." His mom smiled and said, "We will come back soon, Tim."

One day, a messy dog named Spot was playing outside. He saw his friend, a little girl named Sue. Sue had a big bag that was full of something. Spot was very curious about what was in Sue's bag. "Hi Sue, what do you have in your bag?" asked Spot. Sue said, "I have oats in my bag. I want to include them in our snack today. Let's go to the kitchen and make something yummy!" In the kitchen, Sue and Spot made a big mess. Oats were everywhere! They had a lot of fun making their snack. Spot had a feeling that something was going to happen. He wagged his tail and looked at the door. Just then, Sue's mom came in and saw the messy kitchen. She was not happy. "Oh no, what happened here?" she asked. Sue and Spot looked at each other and giggled. They knew they had to clean up the mess. After cleaning up, Sue, Spot, and Sue's mom enjoyed the yummy oat snack together. Spot was happy he got to be included in making the snack, even if it was a little messy.

Once there was a big, heavy rock in the woods. A small, green vine grew on the rock. The vine wanted to have a friend. One day, a little bird came and sat on the rock. "Hi, bird!" said the vine. "Do you want to be my friend?" "Yes, vine! Let's be friends," said the bird. The bird and the vine played all day. When it was time for the bird to go home, it started to rain. The bird was scared. The vine saw the bird was scared and said, "Don't be scared, bird. I will help you." The vine moved close to the bird and covered the bird with its leaves. The bird was now safe from the rain. The bird said, "Thank you, vine! You are a good friend." The vine and the bird were happy to be friends. They played together in the woods every day.

But then it started to rain, so they took shelter in a nearby tree. Jack looked above and saw the jet flying in the sky. He got an idea and said, "Mommy, let's climb the tree and touch the jet!" His mommy smiled and said, "No, Jack. That is too dangerous. Let's just watch it." Jack did not understand. He said, "Why can't we touch it?" His mommy said, "The sky is too high, we cannot reach the jet. But we can still enjoy watching it." Jack was disappointed, but he listened to his mommy. Jack and his mommy enjoyed watching the jet flying in the sky. Then Jack remembered what his mommy told him. He said, "Sometimes it's better to watch than to touch. That way, we don't get hurt, like a sour lemon." Jack's mommy smiled and said, "That is a very wise lesson. Sometimes, it is better to be satisfied with what you can get and enjoy it instead of being too greedy."
