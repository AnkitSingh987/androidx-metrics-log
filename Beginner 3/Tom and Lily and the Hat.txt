Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Hat

Tom and Lily were playing in the park with their mom. They liked to run, slide, swing and climb. They saw a man in a suit walking fast. He had a big bag and a hat. "Who is he?" Lily asked. "I don't know. Maybe he is going to work," Tom said. The man in the suit looked around. He saw a big tree. He ran to the tree and hid behind it. He opened his bag and took out something shiny. "What is he doing?" Lily asked. "I don't know. Maybe he is looking for something," Tom said. The man in the suit pressed a button on the shiny thing. He smiled and said, "Bye bye, world!" Then he disappeared. "Where did he go?" Lily asked. "I don't know. Maybe he went to another place," Tom said. They ran to the tree and looked behind it. They saw the bag and the hat, but not the man. "Wow, he is gone!" Lily said. "Maybe he is a magician," Tom said. They picked up the bag and the hat. They looked inside the bag. They saw more shiny things. "Can we play with them?" Lily asked. "No, we can't. They are not ours. Maybe they are dangerous," Tom said. They put the bag and the hat back. They ran to their mom and told her what they saw. Their mom was surprised. She said, "That is very strange. Maybe he was a spy or a thief. We should call the police." She took out her phone and called the police. She told them about the man in the suit who disappeared. The police came and took the bag and the hat. They thanked Tom and Lily for being good and brave. They said they would try to find the man in the suit. Tom and Lily wondered where the man in the suit went. They hoped he was not bad. They hoped he would come back. They wanted to see him disappear again.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. One day, Lily wanted to help her mom cook dinner. She had a plan to make a yummy meal for her family. Lily went to the kitchen and saw a big onion on the table. She picked it up and said, "Mom, can I help cut the onion?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, Lily, but be careful with the knife." Lily carefully cut the onion into small pieces. Her eyes felt funny, but she was happy to help. When the dinner was ready, Lily, her mom, and her dad sat down to eat. They all loved the meal, and Lily's mom said, "Thank you for your help, sweet girl. Your plan made dinner so tasty!"

Once upon a time, in a small garden, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a new plant that she loved very much. She wanted to let the plant grow big and strong. One day, the sun came out to say, "Hello, Lily! How is your new plant today?" Lily smiled and said, "Hello, sun! My plant is small, but I will let it grow big with your help." The sun shined bright and warm on the new plant. Lily gave the plant water and love. The plant grew and grew. It became very happy and strong. Lily and the sun became good friends. They talked and laughed every day. They let the new plant grow bigger and bigger. And they all lived happily ever after.

The sun was shining brightly outside, and the birds were singing. It was a perfect day for a picnic! Little Jack's mom said, "Jack, let's have a pretend picnic!" Jack smiled and said, "Can I pack it?" Jack's mom nodded yes. Jack grabbed a blanket and some pretend food. He was so smart to think of it all! He helped his mom spread the blanket on the grass and put the food out. Jack and his mom pretended to eat the fake food and laughed a lot. They were both so happy! When it was time to go, Jack said, "I love pretend picnics. Can we do it again soon?"
