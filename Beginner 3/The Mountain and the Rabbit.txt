Category: Beginner
Title: The Mountain and the Rabbit

Once upon a time, there was a big mountain. The mountain was very peaceful. Many animals lived on the mountain. They all liked to play together. One day, a little bird told his friend, the rabbit, "I will bring you a gift from the top of the mountain." The rabbit was very happy. He said, "Thank you, bird. I can't wait!" The bird flew to the top of the mountain. He found a pretty flower. He brought the flower to the rabbit. The rabbit loved the gift. They played together all day long. The mountain was peaceful and happy.

One day, a smart cat named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big jail with a dog inside. Tim felt sad for the dog and wanted to help. He thought of a plan. Tim went up to the jail and said, "Hi dog, I am Tim. I will help you get out." The dog looked at Tim and blinked. Tim blinked back. They knew they needed to work together. Tim found a big stick and pushed it through the jail bars. The dog grabbed the stick with his teeth and pulled. The door opened! They were both very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The dog said, "Thank you, Tim. I am a magic dog!" He blinked and turned into a beautiful bird. The bird flew away, and Tim knew he had made a special friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy called Jack. He was three years old, and loved exploring the world. One day, Jack went to the park. He saw a big, green pitch and wanted to explore it. He ran up to the pitch, but slipped and fell. It was very wet, and Jack started to cry. A kind old man saw Jack and said, "Don't worry Jack, I know how to make it better!" Jack stopped crying and looked up. The old man smiled and said, "All you need to do is make it dry." He pointed to a bundle of dry grass. Jack gathered the dry grass and started rubbing it on the wet pitch. But no matter what he did, the pitch wouldn't dry. Jack tried harder and harder, but the pitch just stayed wet. Exhausted, Jack started to cry again. The old man said sadly, "I'm sorry Jack, it looks like the pitch won't ever be dry again." Jack sat on the pitch and felt sad. He would never be able to explore the green pitch again.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play in the kitchen. They pretend to cook and eat. They have many toys in the kitchen. Some toys are old and some toys are new. One day, Mom comes to the kitchen. She sees a big mess. She sees toys on the floor and on the table. She sees food on the plates and on the chairs. She is angry. She says, "Who made this mess? You have to clean it up!" Lily and Ben look at each other. They do not want to clean. They want to play. Lily says, "It was not me. It was Ben. He took the new toys and made the mess." Ben says, "No, it was not me. It was Lily. She took the expensive toys and made the mess." Mom does not believe them. She says, "You are both lying. You both made the mess. You both have to clean it up. And you both have to say sorry. The new toys and the expensive toys are not yours. They are mine. You have to ask before you use them." Lily and Ben feel bad. They know they did wrong. They reply, "Sorry, Mom. We will clean up. We will not use your toys without asking." They start to pick up the toys and the food. They put them in the right places. Mom helps them. She says, "Thank you, Lily and Ben. I love you. But next time, please be careful and respectful in the kitchen." Lily and Ben say, "We love you too, Mom. We will be careful and respectful next time." They hug Mom and finish cleaning. They learn their lesson.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a funny cat named Bobo. Bobo loved to play with his pastel ball. He would throw it and then run to catch it. He would repeat this game all day long. One day, while playing, Bobo saw a big tree. He thought it would be fun to climb the tree and look around. So, he threw his pastel ball up into the tree and started to climb. As he climbed higher and higher, the wind began to blow. Bobo didn't know that the wind could be very strong. The wind blew harder and harder, and Bobo got scared. He tried to hold onto the tree, but he fell down. Poor Bobo got hurt, and he couldn't play with his pastel ball anymore.
