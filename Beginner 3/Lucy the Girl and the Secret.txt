Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Secret

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to play with her friends. They wanted to act in a play. Lucy was a princess, and her friends were incredible animals. They all had fun acting together. But then, something unexpected happened. A real incredible animal came to play with them. It was a big, friendly dog! The dog wanted to be part of the play too. Lucy and her friends were happy to have a new friend. The dog acted with them and showed them a secret. The secret was the truth about the play. The play was magic! When they acted, they became real princesses and incredible animals. Lucy and her friends were excited about this twist. They played and acted all day, enjoying their new magic world.

Once upon a time, in a land far away, there was a rare, big cat with a key. This cat could unlock any door with its key. The cat had a friend, a small dog with big ears. They loved to play together. One day, the cat said to the dog, "I want to unlock the door to the big house on the hill. Let's go inside and play!" The dog, with his big ears, listened and said, "Okay, let's go!" They went to the big house and the cat used its key to unlock the door. They went inside and played. But then, they heard a loud noise. It was the owner of the house, a mean man who did not like animals. The man saw the cat and the dog and yelled, "Get out of my house!" The cat and the dog were scared and tried to run away. But the man caught the cat and took its key. The cat and the dog could not play together anymore. They were very sad. The end.

One day, a big ostrich was walking in the forest. The ostrich was bald and felt sad. He wanted to have nice hair like his friends. The bald ostrich met a kind bird. The bird said, "Why are you sad, ostrich?" The ostrich said, "I am bald and want nice hair like you." The bird wanted to help the ostrich. The bird had a plan. The bird told the ostrich to go to the tree with many leaves. The bird said, "Put the leaves on your head. You will look nice." The ostrich did what the bird told him. The ostrich was happy now. They both played and had fun in the forest.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Jaya. She was just three years old and very curious about the world around her. Jaya was playing in the park one day when the sun started to go down. She watched the orange and pink sunset in awe. In that moment, Jaya wanted to write a story about it. She ran home to get her pencil and paper. But when Jaya tried to write the story, she couldn't. All she wrote was scribbles on the page. Jaya felt very frustrated. Just then, her mom came over to her and said, "Why don't you use bigger words? You need to use longer words to write a story". Jaya thought about what her mom said. Then she smiled, grabbed her pencil, and began writing her story. The end.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play games. Today they play a game with flags. They have red flags and blue flags. They hide the flags in the park. Tom says, "I will find your flags, Lily. You can't hide them from me." Lily says, "We will see, Tom. You have to answer my questions first." Lily asks Tom, "What is the color of the sky?" Tom says, "Blue." Lily says, "Good. You can look for a blue flag." Tom runs and looks for a blue flag. He finds one behind a tree. He is happy. Lily asks Tom, "What is the color of the sun?" Tom says, "Yellow." Lily says, "Good. You can look for a yellow flag." Tom runs and looks for a yellow flag. He can't find one. He is sad. Lily says, "I tricked you, Tom. There is no yellow flag. I only have red flags and blue flags. You lose the game." Tom says, "No fair, Lily. You are mean." Lily says, "I am not mean. I am smart. And lucky. I have the best flags." She shows Tom her flags. She has a red flag with a star and a blue flag with a heart. Tom says, "Wow, Lily. Your flags are pretty. Can I have one?" Lily says, "OK, Tom. You can have one. But only if you say sorry." Tom says, "Sorry, Lily. You are a good friend." Lily says, "Thank you, Tom. You are a good friend too." They hug and share the flags. They are happy.
