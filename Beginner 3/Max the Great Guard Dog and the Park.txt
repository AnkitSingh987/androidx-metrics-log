Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Great Guard Dog and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a great guard dog named Max. Max was very big and strong. He loved to play with his friends in the park. They would run and jump and have lots of fun. One day, Max and his friends decided to have a race to see who was the fastest. They all lined up and got ready to run. When the race started, Max ran as fast as he could. His legs moved like the wind, and he felt very happy. Max ran faster and faster, and soon he was in the lead. He could see the finish line ahead of him. With one last big jump, Max crossed the finish line and won the race! All of his friends cheered and clapped for him. Max was the great guard dog who won the race, and he was very proud.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom had a toy box that he loved very much. One day, Tom was very upset because he could not open his toy box. He tried and tried, but it just would not open. Tom's mom saw him and asked, "Why are you upset, Tom?" Tom said, "I hate that I can't open my toy box!" Tom's mom had an idea. She said, "Let's get your dad to help us." Tom's dad came and tried to open the toy box. Suddenly, the toy box opened! But instead of toys, there were lots of yummy treats inside. Tom was so surprised and happy. He said, "I don't hate it anymore! I love my toy box even more now!" Tom, his mom, and his dad all laughed and enjoyed the yummy treats together. The toy box was not just for toys anymore, but for happy surprises too. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toys and have fun all day. One day, Tim saw his mom cooking on the big stove in the kitchen. He wanted to enjoy cooking too, but his mom said, "No, Tim. The stove is hot and you are too little." Tim was sad but he had a naughty idea. When his mom went to the other room, he went to the stove to try and cook. But as he got close, he saw a little mouse run by. The mouse was so fast, it made Tim jump back and he did not touch the stove. Just then, his mom came back and saw the mouse too. She was not happy, but she was glad Tim did not touch the stove. She thanked the little mouse for keeping Tim safe. From that day on, Tim learned to listen to his mom and not be naughty. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a lazy cat named Tom wanted to go on a trip. He had a big bag to pack all his things. Tom put his toys, food, and a small knife in the bag. The knife was for cutting his food. Tom walked and walked until he found a nice spot under a tree. He took out his toys and played all day. When he got hungry, he used the small knife to cut his food. He ate and felt happy. As the sun went down, Tom felt tired. He put his toys and the small knife back in the bag. Tom walked back home, ready to sleep in his warm bed. He had a fun day on his trip.

Once there was a big dragon. The dragon was green and had a long tail. The dragon lived in a dark cave. The dragon was very sad because he had no friends. He was also very hungry. One day, a small boy named Tim saw the dragon. Tim said, "Hello, dragon! Why are you so sad?" The dragon told Tim that he was hungry and had no friends. Tim felt bad for the dragon. Tim had an idea. He said, "I will help you, dragon! I will rescue you from this dark cave and find you some food." The dragon was happy. Tim and the dragon left the cave and went to find food. They found a big pile of food, but it was disgusting. The dragon did not want to eat it. Tim said, "Don't worry, dragon. We will find better food for you." They kept looking and found some yummy food for the dragon. The dragon was very happy. He said, "Thank you, Tim! You rescued me and found me yummy food. Now we can be friends!" Tim and the dragon were friends forever.
