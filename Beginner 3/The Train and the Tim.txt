Category: Beginner
Title: The Train and the Tim

Once upon a time, there was a little train. The train was very obedient. It always did what it was told to do. It went on the tracks and never went off. The train liked to help people. One day, a small boy named Tim came to see the train. Tim liked the train very much. He wanted to touch the train. Tim's mom said it was okay. So, Tim touched the train and felt happy. The train was happy too. It liked to make friends. So, the train and Tim became good friends. They played together every day. The train always listened to Tim and stayed obedient. Everyone loved the little train, and they all lived happily ever after.

Tom and Lily wanted to help their dad fix the fence. They saw a big hammer and some nails in the garage. They took them and went to the backyard. "Look, dad, we can help you!" Tom said, holding the hammer. "Be careful, kids, that hammer is heavy and sharp. You might hurt yourself or make a messy hole in the fence," dad said. But Tom and Lily did not listen. They tried to hit the nails with the hammer, but they missed a lot. They made loud noises and dents in the wood. They also dropped the nails on the grass. "Stop, stop, stop!" dad said. "You are not helping, you are making a mess. Give me the hammer and the nails, please." Tom and Lily felt sad. They wanted to help, but they did not know how. They gave the hammer and the nails to dad. "I'm sorry, dad, we wanted to help you," Lily said. "I know, kids, and I appreciate it. But you are too small to use the hammer and the nails. You can help me in other ways, like picking up the nails from the grass, or painting the fence when I'm done," dad said. Tom and Lily nodded. They spent the rest of the afternoon helping dad in the backyard. They had fun and learned how to fix the fence. Dad was proud of them and gave them a hug.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big hill. On top of the hill, there was a house. In the house, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was not happy. His toys were all dull and old. One day, Tim saw a new toy in a shop. It was a red ball. Tim knew he needed the ball to have fun. But the shop was far away, and Tim was too little to go alone. So, he asked his mom for help. Tim and his mom walked down the hill to the shop. They bought the red ball. Tim was so happy! They went back up the hill to their house. Now, Tim could play with his new ball and have lots of fun. And he was not sad anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a shy boy named Tim. Tim was too scared to talk to other kids. One day, he saw a big red ambulance. The ambulance had a loud siren and bright lights. Tim thought the ambulance was cool. Tim saw a girl named Sue looking at the ambulance too. He wanted to talk to her, but he was shy. Tim took a deep breath and said, "Hi, do you like the ambulance?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, I do! I want to be a helper when I grow up." Tim was happy he talked to Sue. From that day, Tim and Sue became good friends. They played together and learned about ambulances. They both wanted to help people when they grew up. The moral of the story is, don't be shy to talk to others. You might make a new friend and learn something new.

One day, a boy named Tim went to buy a toy ship. He was very happy. The man who sold the ship said, "Be careful, this ship can be dangerous." Tim did not listen. Tim took the ship to the park. He played with his friends. They all liked the ship. The ship moved fast on the water. The sky was dark. Tim did not see the danger. A big wind came. The ship went far away. Tim was scared. He cried, "Help! My ship is dangerous!" But it was too late. The ship was gone. Tim was sad. He should have listened to the man.
