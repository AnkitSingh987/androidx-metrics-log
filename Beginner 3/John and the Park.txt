Category: Beginner
Title: John and the Park

John and his mom were walking in the park. They were having a nice visit. As they were walking, John spotted something shiny in the grass. He stopped to pick it up. But his mom said ''No!'' John was worried and asked, ''Why not?'' His mom said, ''That could be dangerous. You could get hurt.'' John didn’t want to get hurt but he still wanted the shiny thing. He asked, ''Please?'' But his mom shook her head and said, ''No!'' John was angry and he struck his mom. His mom was so shocked that she took a few steps back. She asked in a stern voice, ''Why did you do that?'' John was scared now and he said, ''I don’t know.'' His mom still looked worried so she put her arm around John and said, ''It's ok. I'm not mad. But we must always be careful of what we pick up.'' John nodded and said, ''Okay.'' And they continued their visit through the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a favorite sock, but it was very old and poor. One day, Tim went to the park to play with his friends. He wore his favorite sock, even though it had a big hole in it. At the park, Tim saw a big red ball. He wanted to play with it, so he ran to it and gave it a big push. The ball rolled far away, and Tim ran after it. He did not see the big rock in his way. When Tim stepped on the rock, his foot hurt a lot. His poor sock did not help him. Tim started to cry, and his friends came to see what was wrong. They saw the hole in Tim's sock and the big rock. They knew that his poor sock could not protect his foot. Tim's foot hurt so much that he could not play with his friends. He had to go home and rest. His mom saw the poor sock and the big rock, and she knew what had happened. She felt sad for Tim, but she knew it was time to say goodbye to the poor sock. Tim had to learn that sometimes, even our favorite things can't help us when we need them most.

One day, a silly taxi was driving in the town. The taxi liked to scatter things all around. It would scatter leaves, toys, and even food. The taxi thought it was fun to make a mess. One day, the taxi saw a little boy named Tim. Tim was playing with his toy cars. The taxi stopped and said, "Hi, Tim! Can I play with you?" Tim was happy to have a new friend and said, "Yes, let's play together!" So, they played and had lots of fun. But the silly taxi started to scatter Tim's toy cars all around. Tim got sad and said, "Please don't scatter my toys, taxi." But the taxi didn't listen and kept scattering more things. Soon, Tim's mom came out and saw the mess. She was very angry and told the taxi to go away. The taxi felt sad and left, but it was too late. Tim and his mom were upset, and the taxi lost a new friend because of its silly actions.

One day, a little bird sat on a branch. The branch was on a big, bald tree. The bird liked to stare at the sky and sing songs. One day, while the bird was staring at the sky, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the branch. The bird fell off the branch and onto the ground. But the bird was not hurt! The bird looked up and saw that the wind had blown a big pile of leaves under the tree. The leaves were soft and they saved the bird from getting hurt. The bird was happy and went back to the branch to sing more songs.

Once there was a modern tire. It was pink and shiny, and it rolled around all day long. Everyone was happy with the tire! One day, a bull came along and saw the tire. He became very angry and started to disturb the tire. He screamed and kicked and sent it flying. Everyone was sad and scared. The kids who owned the tire were very brave. They tried to stand up to the bull and protect their tire. "Stop!" they shouted. "Stop disturbing our tire!" The bull looked angry, but he stopped. Then he said, "You are very brave. From now on, I won't disturb your tire." The kids were relieved and thanked the bull for understanding. Everyone learnt a lesson that day -- that it's important to stand up for ourselves and not let anyone disturb us!
