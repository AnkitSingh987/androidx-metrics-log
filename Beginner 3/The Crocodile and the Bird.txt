Category: Beginner
Title: The Crocodile and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big crocodile who lived in a river. He had many friends, but he was very impatient. He always wanted to play right away and did not like to wait for his friends. One day, the crocodile saw a little bird and wanted to play with it. The bird was scared because the crocodile was so big and impatient. The crocodile said, "I love to play, please play with me." The little bird was still scared and flew away. The crocodile was sad and asked his friends for help. They told him to be patient and kind. The next day, the crocodile tried to be patient and kind. He saw the little bird again and asked gently, "Can we play together?" This time, the little bird said yes, and they became good friends. The crocodile learned that being patient and kind can help make new friends.

Lily and Anna are best friends. They like to play together in the park. Today, they pretend to be princesses. They wear their lovely dresses and put flowers in their hair. "Look at me, Lily. I am Princess Anna of the Sun. I have golden hair and a golden crown," Anna says. "You are very pretty, Anna. I am Princess Lily of the Moon. I have silver hair and a silver tiara," Lily says. They run and dance in the grass. They sing songs and laugh. They see a big tree with a swing. They take turns to swing high and low. "Wow, this is fun. We are flying princesses," Anna says. "Yes, we are. We can fly to any kingdom we want," Lily says. They pretend to fly to different places. They see mountains and rivers and castles and animals. They have a lot of fun. They are happy princesses.

Anna and Ben like to play with boats. They have many boats of different colors and sizes. They go to the pond and sail their boats on the water. They have fun watching their boats go fast or slow, turn or stop. One day, they find a new boat in the toy store. It is big and red and shiny. It has a sail and a flag and a wheel. Anna and Ben want to buy it, but it is too expensive. They ask their mom if they can have it, but she says no. She says they have enough boats already. Anna and Ben are sad. They go back to the pond and sail their old boats. But they still think about the new boat. They wish they could have it. Then they see a boy with the new boat. He is sailing it on the pond. He looks happy. Anna and Ben go to him and say hello. They ask him if they can play with his boat. The boy says yes. He says his name is Sam and he got the boat for his birthday. He says he likes to share his toys. Anna and Ben are happy. They take turns sailing the new boat with Sam. They have fun together. They make a new friend. They learn that the new boat is not too big for the pond. It fits just right. They also learn that their old boats are still good. They can play with them and with the new boat. They have more boats and more fun.

One day, a big purple cat went for a walk. The cat saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. The cat was very happy to be outside. While the cat was walking, it noticed a little bird. The bird was singing a pretty song. The cat and the bird became friends. They played together all day. At the end of the day, the cat and the bird met a kind judge. The judge liked the cat and the bird. The judge said, "You are good friends." The cat and the bird were very happy. They went home and played more.

Tim and Sam were best friends. They liked to play with toy cars and trucks. They had many cars and trucks of different colors and sizes. Tim had a big red truck that could make loud noises and move fast. Sam had a small blue car that could reverse and turn easily. Tim thought his truck was better than Sam's car. He said to Sam, "My truck is more popular than your car. Everyone likes my truck. Your car is too small and boring." Sam felt sad. He liked his car very much. He said to Tim, "My car is not boring. It can reverse and go in different directions. Your truck can only go forward and backward. And it uses a lot of fuel. My car uses less fuel and is good for the air." Tim did not listen to Sam. He said, "I don't care about fuel or air. I only care about speed and noise. My truck is the best. Watch me go!" He pushed his truck hard and made it zoom across the floor. He did not see the table leg in front of him. He crashed his truck into the table leg and broke it. He heard a loud snap and saw his truck's wheel fall off. He cried, "Oh no! My truck is broken! What will I do?" Sam saw what happened. He felt sorry for Tim. He said, "Don't cry, Tim. I can help you. I have some glue and tape. We can fix your truck together. Maybe we can also make it reverse like my car. Then you can have more fun with it." Tim looked at Sam. He realized he was wrong. He said, "Thank you, Sam. You are a good friend. I'm sorry I was mean to you. Your car is not boring. It is cool and smart. Can we share our cars and trucks and play together?" Sam smiled. He said, "Of course we can. We are best friends. We can learn from each other and have more fun. Sharing is caring." They hugged and went to fix Tim's truck. They learned that being popular is not important. Being kind and helpful is more important. They also learned that using less fuel is good for the air and the earth. They played happily with their cars and trucks and had a great time.
