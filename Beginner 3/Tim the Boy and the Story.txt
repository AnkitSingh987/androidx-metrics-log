Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a big boy named Tim. He loved to play baseball. Tim had many friends who liked to play with him. One day, Tim and his friends were playing baseball in the park. Tim hit the ball very hard. It went far, far away. It did not stop. His friends ran to get it. But then, a little girl named Sue found the ball first. She wanted to play, too. Tim said, "No, you are too little." Sue felt very sad and walked away. Tim's friends saw this and told Tim to stop being mean. They said, "Let's all play together." Tim thought about it and said sorry to Sue. They all played baseball and had fun. The moral of the story is to be kind to everyone, no matter how big or little they are.

Tom and Jen are friends. They like to play games. Today they play a game with rice. They have two bowls of rice. They take turns to move some rice from one bowl to another. The one who has more rice in their bowl at the end wins. Tom is deaf. He cannot hear Jen. He uses his eyes and his hands to talk to her. Jen can hear, but she also knows how to talk with her eyes and her hands. They are good at talking to each other. Tom and Jen play the game. They move some rice. They count the rice. They smile and laugh. They have fun. Jen has more rice in her bowl. She wins. She shows Tom. She says, "I win!" Tom is not sad. He is happy for Jen. He claps his hands. He says, "You win! Good job!" He gives Jen a hug. He says, "You are my friend. I like you." Jen hugs Tom back. She says, "You are my friend too. I like you. You are good at the game. Next time, you can win." They share the rice. They eat some rice. They are full and happy. They play another game. They are friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim had to wake up early for class. He did not like to wake up early because it was still dark outside. Tim felt terrible when he had to get out of bed, but he knew he had to go to class. Tim walked to his class with his mom. When they got there, the teacher said, "Hello, Tim! Today we will learn about colors and shapes." Tim liked colors and shapes, so he felt a little better about being in class. At the end of the day, Tim was no longer feeling terrible. He had so much fun in class. He learned about red, blue, and yellow. He also learned about circles, squares, and triangles. Tim couldn't wait to wake up for class the next day!

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Lisa. One day Lisa was outside playing with her friends, when suddenly she saw a strange man walking down the street. Lisa was scared and ran away. She ran as fast as she could until she reached her house. Once she was inside, Lisa hugged her mommy. "Mommy, I was so scared," she said. Her mom hugged her and said, "What's wrong, sweetheart?" "I saw a man and he was carrying a newspaper. He scared me," said Lisa. "Don't worry, sweetheart," said her mom. "Newspapers are nothing to be scared of. They are just pieces of paper with words on them. People buy them to get information about what's going on in the world." "Oh," said Lisa. "At least now you know that newspapers are nothing to be scared of. You can do this!" said her mom, giving her a big hug. Lisa felt much better. She went back outside to play with her friends. Since then she never felt scared of newspapers again!

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim found a new slide at the park. He was very happy and excited to play on it. He ran to the slide and wanted to be the first one to try it out. He could not wait to start playing. Tim climbed up the steps to the slide. He sat down at the top and got ready to slide down. He felt a little scared, but he was brave and knew he could do it. With a big smile, he pushed himself and started to slide. As Tim slid down, something unexpected happened. The slide broke in half! Tim fell on the ground and began to cry. The new slide was not strong enough for him to play on. Now, no one could play on the slide anymore. It was a sad day for Tim and his friends at the park.
