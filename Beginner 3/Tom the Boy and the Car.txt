Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom loved to play with his equipment. He had a big red ball, a blue car, and an incredible green dinosaur toy. One day, Tom's friend, Lily, came over to play. They played with the equipment, but they both wanted to play with the green dinosaur toy. Tom said, "I want to play with it!" Lily said, "No, I want to play with it!" They were very upset. Tom's mom saw them and had an idea. She said, "Why don't you both raise the green dinosaur toy together?" Tom and Lily thought that was a great idea. They raised the dinosaur toy high up in the air, and they both smiled. They learned that playing together was more fun than fighting. And they played happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a small rug in his room. He liked the rug very much. It was soft and had many colors. Tim put the rug on the floor and sat on it. He felt happy. Tim had a friend named Sam. Sam came to play with Tim. Sam saw the rug and felt jealous. He wanted a rug like Tim's. Tim saw Sam was sad and had an idea. They both sat on the rug and started to pray. As they prayed, something unexpected happened. The rug began to grow bigger and bigger. Now, there was enough room for both Tim and Sam to sit on the rug. They were both happy and played together all day.

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys in the garden. One day, they see a big truck with many sheep in it. The truck stops near their house. "Look, Sara, sheep!" Ben says. "They have soft wool. Can we touch them?" Sara nods. They run to the truck and ask the man who drives it. The man smiles and says, "Sure, you can touch them. But be gentle. They are going to the farm to get a haircut." Sara and Ben touch the wool. It is warm and fluffy. They like it. They see a small sheep that is not in the truck. It is behind a bush. It looks scared. "Hello, little sheep," Sara says. "Why are you not in the truck?" The sheep bleats. It does not want to go to the farm. It likes its wool. "Can we keep it, please?" Ben asks the man. The man shakes his head. "No, you can't. It belongs to the farmer. He needs the wool to make clothes and blankets. Come on, little sheep, get in the truck." The sheep runs away. It does not want to lose its wool. Sara and Ben chase it. They catch it and hug it. "Please, let us keep it," Sara says. "We will take good care of it. We will not cut its wool." The man sighs. He sees that Sara and Ben love the sheep. He thinks for a while. Then he says, "OK, you can keep it. But you have to ask your mom and dad first. And you have to carry it to your house. It is heavy." Sara and Ben are happy. They thank the man. They ask their mom and dad. They say yes. They help Sara and Ben carry the sheep to their house. They give it a name. They call it Wooly. Wooly is happy too. It has a new home and new friends. It does not have to go to the farm. It keeps its wool. It is clear that Sara and Ben and Wooly love each other. They play together in the garden every day.

Mum and Dad were in the kitchen cleaning up after dinner. The little boy was excited as he used his toy broom to help. After putting away the dishes, Mum closed the cupboard and sighed. "It's so quiet", she said. The little boy smiled. "Yes, complete silence!" he said. Mum suddenly looked sad. "Oh sweetheart, why don't you go and play outside now?" she asked. The little boy stopped smiling and hung his head. He looked ashamed. "But why, Mum?" Mum gently put her hand on his shoulder. "I just think it would make you happy, sweetheart. Plus, Dad and I need some time to ourselves." The little boy nodded and ran outside. He shut the door behind him and forced a big smile. He was a little bit ashamed, but glad he still had a chance to play outside. The silence in the kitchen felt even bigger without him, but Mum and Dad were happy, knowing the little boy was happy too.
