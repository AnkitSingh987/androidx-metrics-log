Category: Beginner
Title: The King and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a king who lived in a big castle. The king was not like other kings. He was a normal king who liked to play with his friends. He had a big smile and always said "please" when he asked for things. One day, the king went to the park to play with his friends. They played catch with a big, red ball. The king was very good at catch, and everyone was happy. But the ball went too far and got stuck in a tree. The king looked at the tree and said, "Please, can someone help me get the ball?" A tall man with a long stick came to help the king. He used the stick to get the ball out of the tree. The king said, "Thank you!" and gave the man a big hug. Then they all went back to playing catch, and the king said "please" every time he threw the ball. At the end of the day, the king and his friends went back to the castle. They had a big party with cake and ice cream. The king was very happy that he had such good friends. They all said "please" and "thank you" and had a great time together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a serious face but a kind heart. Mia loved to help her mom. One day, Mia's mom asked her to find a thread. Mia wanted to gain her mom's love, so she said yes. Mia looked for the thread in her room. She saw her toy box and looked inside. There was a small, red thread hiding under her teddy bear. Mia was happy to find the thread. She picked it up and ran to her mom. Mia gave the thread to her mom. Her mom was very happy and gave Mia a big hug. Mia's serious face turned into a big smile. She had gained her mom's love by helping her. They spent the rest of the day sewing and laughing together.

One day, a lovely bird named Blue went to a big cliff. Blue liked to write on the cliff with her wings. She wrote nice words like "sun," "sky," and "fun." Blue had a friend named Red. Red was a small bug who lived on the cliff. One day, Red said, "Blue, can you write my name on the cliff?" Blue said, "Yes, Red, I can do that." So Blue wrote Red's name on the cliff. But when Blue wrote Red's name, something unexpected happened. The cliff started to shake and make loud sounds. Blue and Red were scared. They did not know what was happening. Then, the cliff stopped shaking. They saw that the cliff had turned into a big slide. Blue and Red were happy. They played on the slide all day and had a lot of fun. The cliff was now their new fun place to play.

Once upon a time, there was a small town. In this town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a lucky boy. He liked to play outside with his friends. One day, Tim received a big, red ball as a gift. The next day, dark clouds came, and the wind began to blow. The town saw a tornado coming. Everyone was scared. Tim and his friends ran home to be safe. The tornado was getting closer and closer. But, the tornado did something unexpected. Instead of hurting the town, it picked up Tim's big, red ball and carried it away. When the tornado was gone, the town was safe. Tim was sad about his ball, but happy that everyone was okay. And that's how Tim's big, red ball saved the town from the tornado.

One day, a little boy named Tom and a little girl named Sue went to the park. They saw a big tree with amazing green leaves. Under the tree, they found a small box. Tom opened the box and found a lot of mints inside. He was very happy. Tom said to Sue, "Let's share these mints!" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, that's a good idea!" They both took some mints and began to eat them. The mints were very tasty, and they both liked them a lot. As they ate the mints, they played in the park. They ran, jumped, and had a lot of fun. The sun was shining, and the sky was blue. Tom and Sue were happy they shared the mints and had an amazing day together.
