Category: Beginner
Title: The Mystery of the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little house with a red roof. In the house, there was a big empty room. A funny cat lived in the house. The cat loved to joke and play all day long. He had many friends who came to visit him in the empty room. One sunny day, the cat was on the roof. He saw a big bird flying in the sky. The cat had an idea. He wanted to joke with the bird. So, he called the bird to come down and play with him. The bird was happy to play with the cat. But, something unexpected happened. When the bird came down to play, it was not a big bird. It was a little bird with a big hat. The cat was very surprised. The little bird laughed and said, "I was joking with you too!" They both laughed and played together in the empty room. The cat and the little bird became best friends, and they loved to joke with each other every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big palace. In the palace, there was a creative king. The king liked to draw and paint. He made the palace very pretty with his art. Everyone who saw it said, "Wow!" One day, a little girl came to the palace. She saw the king painting. The girl said, "I like your art. Can I paint too?" The king said, "Yes, you can." The girl and the king painted together. They had lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The girl painted a big sun in the sky. The sun came to life and started to shine bright! The king was so happy. He said, "Thank you, little girl. Your painting made the sun shine!" They all lived happily ever after, painting and enjoying the sunshine.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Joe and Mike, who lived in the city. Joe was very smart but Mike was very stupid. One day they decided they wanted to buy something that they had always wanted. "I think we should buy a bike," said Joe. "No, we should buy candy," argued Mike. Joe was very frustrated by Mike's suggestion, he thought it was a stupid idea. "Mike, that's a stupid idea! We should buy a bike, it will be much more fun," said Joe. Mike wouldn't listen to Joe and he continued to argue for the candy. Finally, Joe had enough and he decided to go and buy the bike himself. Joe went and bought the bike and Mike stayed home, frustrated that he was not allowed to get his candy. Joe was very happy with his new bike and as he rode around the city with it, Mike was at home thinking about how stupid his idea was.

Tim and Pam are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big dog. The dog has a bone in its mouth. It is meat. Tim and Pam are hungry. They want the meat. "Let's take the meat from the dog," Tim says. "It looks tasty." "No, Tim, that is bad," Pam says. "The dog will be angry. The meat is his." But Tim does not listen. He runs to the dog. He tries to grab the meat. The dog growls. The dog bites Tim. Tim screams. Pam runs to help him. "Tim, Tim, are you okay?" Pam asks. She sees blood on Tim's hand. She is scared. The dog runs away with the meat. Tim cries. Pam cries. They go to find their moms. Their moms are worried. They take Tim to the doctor. The doctor gives Tim a bandage and a shot. He says Tim will improve, but he has to be careful. Tim is sorry. He learns his lesson. He says sorry to Pam and the dog. He never tries to take meat from a dog again. He knows it is bad. He knows it is not tasty. He knows it is dangerous.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They saw a big tree in the park. Tim asked his mom, "What is a tree?" His mom tried to explain, "A tree is a big plant that grows from the ground. It has leaves and sometimes fruits." Tim saw a man sitting under the tree. The man could not hear. He was deaf. Tim asked his mom, "Why can't he hear?" His mom said, "Some people are born deaf, and some lose their hearing later. But they can still talk and understand with their hands." Tim wanted to be friends with the deaf man. So, he waved and smiled at him. The man smiled back and waved. Tim was happy to make a new friend under the big tree.
