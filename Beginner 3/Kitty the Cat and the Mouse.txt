Category: Beginner
Title: Kitty the Cat and the Mouse

One day, a kind cat named Kitty was walking in a big room. In the room, there was a lot of furniture. Kitty saw a chair, a table, and a bed. She wanted to find a friend to play with. Kitty saw a little mouse named Mousy hiding behind the furniture. Kitty said, "Hi, Mousy! Do you want to play with me?" Mousy was scared but said, "Okay, Kitty. Let's play hide and seek." They started to play. Kitty closed her eyes and counted. Mousy ran to hide behind the furniture. When Kitty opened her eyes, she looked for Mousy. She found Mousy and they both laughed. But then, something bad happened. While they were playing, they knocked over a big lamp. The lamp broke and made a loud noise. Mommy and Daddy came into the room and saw the mess. Mommy and Daddy were not happy. They said, "Kitty and Mousy, you need to be more careful when playing. Now, we have to clean up the mess and buy a new lamp." Kitty and Mousy felt sad because they knew they did something wrong.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They have a rope. They use the rope to play jump. Lily and Ben take turns to jump over the rope. They count how many times they can jump. "Your turn, Ben!" Lily says. She holds the rope with her hands. She swings the rope over the ground. Ben runs to the rope. He jumps over it. He jumps again and again. He is very good at jump. He keeps jumping. He does not stop. "Wow, Ben, you are cool!" Lily says. She claps her hands. She is happy for Ben. But Ben is not happy. He is hot and tired. He wants to stop. He wants to drink some water. He wants to rest. "Stop, Lily, stop!" Ben says. He tries to catch the rope with his feet. He falls to the ground. He is sad. Lily stops the rope. She runs to Ben. She helps him get up. "I'm sorry, Ben. Are you okay?" Lily says. She is sorry for Ben. "I'm okay, Lily. But I'm thirsty. Can we go to the fountain?" Ben says. He is thirsty for water. "Okay, Ben. Let's go to the fountain. We can drink some water. We can cool down. Then we can play again." Lily says. She is nice to Ben. Lily and Ben hold the rope. They walk to the fountain. They drink some water. They feel better. They smile at each other. They are still friends. They still like to play.

Once upon a time, there was a mommy who wanted to teach her little girl something new. She opened the big, cold freezer and told her daughter to put her hand inside. The little girl was scared, but the mommy said not to worry. She said that she would teach her something amazing. The little girl did as her mommy said and stuck her hand inside the freezer. It was cold and slimy. After a few seconds, the mommy said to take her hand out. When she did, the mommy put a big, hot bowl in her hand. The little girl was very happy and couldn't believe it! Her mommy had taught her to take something cold and make it hot. She couldn't wait to show daddy and all of her friends. The little girl felt so grown up that day, thanks to her mommy's special teaching. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot was an attractive dog with a big, fluffy tail. He loved to play outside and run around in the park. One day, Spot's owner put a leash on him. The leash was red and blue. They went to the park to complete a fun game of fetch. Spot was very excited to play with his owner. At the park, Spot ran and jumped to catch the ball. He was so happy when he caught the ball. After they completed the game, they went home. Spot was tired but had a great day at the park.

One day, a girl named Sue went for a walk. She saw a clear pond with many stones on the ground. Sue liked the stones and wanted to play with them. She picked up a stone and showed it to her friend, Tim. "Look, Tim! I found a stone!" Sue said. Tim looked at the stone and smiled. "Let's play with the stones!" he said. They took turns throwing the stones into the pond. The stones made small splashes in the water. A frog near the pond saw them playing. The frog jumped and said, "Ribbit! Can I play too?" Sue and Tim smiled and said, "Yes, let's all play together!" They played with the frog, throwing stones and laughing. They had a fun day by the clear pond.
