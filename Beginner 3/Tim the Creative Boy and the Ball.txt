Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Creative Boy and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a creative little boy named Tim. He loved to draw and paint all day long. One day, Tim's mom told him not to go near the big cross on the road. She mentioned that it was not safe for him. Tim was playing outside with his ball. The ball went near the big cross on the road. Tim remembered what his mom had mentioned about the cross. But he was creative and thought of a plan to get the ball back without going near the cross. He got a long stick and tried to get the ball back. But his plan did not work. The stick was not long enough to reach the ball. Tim got sad and decided to go near the cross to get the ball. He thought it would be quick and easy. As Tim went close to the cross, a big car came fast down the road. Tim did not see the car and the car did not see Tim. The car hit Tim and he fell down. Tim's mom heard the noise and ran outside. She saw Tim on the ground and started to cry. Tim's mom called for help, but it was too late. Tim did not listen to his mom and went near the cross. Because of this, the story has a bad ending. Always listen to your mom and dad when they mention something important.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. One day, Tim's mom made a big pizza for dinner. Tim loved pizza very much. He was so happy. Tim's mom said, "Tim, you can only eat a little pizza. If you eat too much, you will feel bad." But Tim did not listen. He ate and ate until the pizza was all gone. Tim felt very full and not good at all. He knew he should have listened to his mom. He felt regret. Later, Tim saw a dead bug on the floor. He felt sad for the bug. Tim's mom told him, "If you eat too much and don't share, you may end up like the bug." Tim learned that it is important to share and not be greedy. From that day on, Tim always shared his food with others and felt much better.

Lily and Ben were playing with their toys in the living room. Lily had a doll with a pink dress and a button on her chest. Ben had a car with four wheels and a button on the roof. They liked to press the buttons and make sounds. "Look, my doll can sing!" Lily said, pressing the button on her chest. The doll made a pretty sound. "Wow, that's nice!" Ben said. "But my car can honk!" He pressed the button on the roof. The car made a loud sound. They took turns pressing the buttons and listening to the sounds. They were having fun. But then, Lily saw a shiny thing on the floor. It was a coin. She wanted to pick it up. She let go of her doll and reached for the coin. "Oh no!" Ben said. "Your doll!" Lily's doll fell from the couch and hit the floor. The button on her chest broke off. The doll stopped singing. Lily was sad. She cried. She loved her doll. "I'm sorry, Lily," Ben said. "It was an accident. Don't cry." He picked up the doll and the button. He tried to put the button back on the chest. But it didn't work. "It's okay, Ben," Lily said, wiping her tears. "You tried. Thank you." She hugged her doll. She was grateful for her brother. "Maybe we can fix it later," Ben said. "Or maybe we can find another button." He hugged his sister. He was grateful for her too. They put the coin in their piggy bank. They decided to share their toys. They pressed the button on the car and the button on the doll. They made sounds together. They were happy.

Sam was a 3 year old who lived in a big house. Every day, he would go to the bathroom and wonder what he could find there. He had been to the bathroom many times and it was always the same. It was dull. One day, when Sam went to the bathroom, he was in for a surprise. He heard a little voice on the other side of the door asking "Who's there?". Sam was very curious, so he opened the door. He saw a tiny fairy standing in the bathroom. She had wings and a wand. Sam was amazed and he asked the fairy "What are you doing here?" The fairy said, "I'm here because I heard you wondering about the bathroom and I wanted to show you a surprise." Sam's eyes got very wide. He asked, "What kind of a surprise?" The fairy said, "Close your eyes and when you open them, you'll see something magical." Sam closed his eyes and when he opened them, the dull bathroom was now full of bright colors and fun things. Sam thought it was wonderful and he thanked the fairy for her surprise. Then, the fairy waved her wand and vanished. Sam smiled and ran to tell his parents about the magical surprise he found in the bathroom.
