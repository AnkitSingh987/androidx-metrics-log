Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Zoom

One day, a little boy named Tim found a newspaper on his doorstep. He picked it up and saw a picture of a fast car. Tim loved cars and wanted to go zoom like the car in the picture. Tim asked his mom, "Can we go zoom in our car, please?" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, we can go zoom today." Tim was very happy and jumped up and down. They got in the car and drove to a clear, open space. Tim's mom said, "Ready, set, go!" and the car went zoom, just like the car in the newspaper. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. They had a fun day going zoom together.

One day, a little boy named Tim had a big, thick book. He loved to stand on it to reach the high shelf. On the shelf, there was a tray. Tim wanted the tray to play with his cars. So, Tim stood on the thick book to get the tray. But when he touched the tray, it moved! The tray was not a tray. It was a big, sleepy cat. The cat opened its eyes and looked at Tim. Tim was so surprised! He thought the cat was a tray. The cat jumped down and played with Tim and his cars. Now, Tim had a new friend. They played together every day, and Tim never used the cat as a tray again.

One day, a boy named Tim went to a pond with his mom. The pond had ducks and fish in it. Tim liked to watch them swim and play. The pond was near a big tree with rough bark. Tim liked to touch the tree and feel the rough bark on his hands. Before they went to the pond, Tim's mom helped him tie his shoes. Tim was learning how to tie his shoes, but it was hard. He tried again and again. His mom was very patient and showed him how to make loops and pull them tight. At the pond, Tim saw a little duck with a ribbon stuck on its foot. The duck was trying to swim, but the ribbon was tied too tight. Tim wanted to help the duck. He carefully reached down and untied the ribbon. The duck was free and swam away. Tim was happy he could help the duck by using what his mom taught him about tying.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park with her mom. They saw a big tree with many interesting ornaments hanging on it. Lucy wanted to look at them closely, so she sat on the grass under the tree. As she sat there, she saw a tiny door on the tree. She opened it and found a small, friendly squirrel inside. The squirrel told Lucy that he was the one who made the ornaments. He asked her if she wanted to learn how to make them too. Lucy was very happy and said yes. They spent the whole day making ornaments together. When it was time to go home, the squirrel gave her a special ornament to remember their fun day. From that day on, Lucy and the squirrel became best friends and they made many more interesting ornaments for the tree in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a modest little hill. On the hill, there lived a tiny bunny named Ben. Ben loved to stretch his legs and run up and down the hill. One day, Ben met a big turtle named Tim. Tim was slow and had a hard time going up the hill. "Hi, Tim," said Ben. "Why are you so slow?" "I am a turtle," said Tim. "I cannot stretch my legs like you." Ben wanted to help Tim. They thought of a plan. They would have a race to the top of the hill. Ben knew he was fast, but he wanted to make it fun for Tim, too. The race began, and Ben ran very fast. But then, he stopped and waited for Tim. They reached the top of the hill together. Tim was so happy that he could make it to the top. From that day on, Ben and Tim were the best of friends. They learned that it was more fun to help each other than to just win a race.
