Category: Beginner
Title: The Poor Turkey Was Walking and the Bird

One day, a poor turkey was walking in the rain. He was very wet and cold. He saw a big tree and went under it to get dry. Under the tree, he met a little bird. "Hello, little bird," said the turkey. "I am so wet. Can you help me get dry?" The little bird said, "I can try. Let's shake our feathers and jump up and down." The turkey and the little bird shook their feathers and jumped up and down. But the turkey was still wet. "Oh no," said the little bird. "I have an idea. Let's ask the sun to come out and dry you." The turkey and the little bird called to the sun, "Sun, sun, please come out and dry our friend, the poor turkey." The sun heard their call and came out from behind the clouds. The warm sun dried the turkey's feathers, and he was not wet anymore. The turkey and the little bird were so happy, and they played under the tree all day.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there was a little yellow frog. The frog loved to jump all day long. He would jump from one big rock to another, playing and having fun. One day, the little yellow frog met a new friend. It was a mild, sunny day when he found a small brown rabbit. The rabbit also loved to jump just like the frog. They decided to play together, jumping and hopping around the forest. As they played, they found a big, beautiful lake. The water was blue and clear. The little yellow frog and the small brown rabbit jumped into the water, splashing and laughing. They had so much fun, they became best friends. From that day on, they played and jumped together in the big green forest, and they lived happily ever after.

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play in their room. They have many toys and books and pillows. Sara likes to make a fort with the pillows. Ben likes to throw the pillows at Sara. "Stop it, Ben!" Sara says. "You mess up my fort!" "I want to play with you, Sara!" Ben says. "Pillows are fun!" Sara is angry. She does not want to play with Ben. She wants to play alone. She pushes Ben away. Ben falls on the floor. He cries. Mom hears Ben cry. She comes to the room. She sees the pillows everywhere. She sees Sara and Ben. "What is going on here?" Mom says. "Why are you fighting?" "Sara is mean!" Ben says. "She does not let me play!" "Ben is bad!" Sara says. "He throws pillows at me!" Mom is sad. She loves Sara and Ben. She wants them to get along. She picks up Ben and hugs him. She tells Sara to come too. She hugs Sara. "I know you both like to play, but you have to share and be nice," Mom says. "Pillows are not for throwing. They are for sleeping and cuddling. And you have to keep them clean. How about we put them back on the bed and read a story together?" Sara and Ben nod. They say sorry to each other. They help Mom put the pillows back. They choose a story. They sit on the bed with Mom. They listen to the story. They hug the pillows. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Bob. Bob was unusual because he had big, colorful wings. One day, Bob went for a fly near a cliff. As Bob flew, he saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad because her ball had rolled near the edge of the cliff. Sue said, "Help me, please! My ball is too close to the edge." Bob wanted to help Sue. He flew down and used his big wings to grab the ball. He picked it up and took it back to Sue. Sue was so happy! She said, "Thank you, Bob!" From that day on, Bob and Sue were the best of friends. They played together every day and had lots of fun. And everyone loved Bob's big, unusual wings.
