Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. He lived in a small house with a big bookcase. Max loved to chew on things. One day, he found a smelly sock under the bed. Max said, "Yum! I love to chew on smelly things!" He chewed and chewed on the smelly sock. He was very happy. Then, Max saw the big bookcase. He thought, "Maybe there are more smelly things to chew on!" He looked and looked, but he could not find anything smelly to chew. Max was sad. He went to his friend, Cat. Max asked Cat, "Do you know where I can find more smelly things to chew on?" Cat said, "No, but let's look together!" They looked all around the house and found a smelly shoe. Max and Cat were happy. They chewed on the smelly shoe together and had a fun day.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play with toys and books. One day, Anna has a new toy. It is a big bear. She loves her bear very much. She does not want to share it with anyone. Ben sees Anna's bear. He thinks it is nice. He asks Anna if he can play with it. Anna says no. She hugs her bear and runs away. Ben feels sad. He wants to be Anna's friend, but he also wants to see the bear. Anna hides behind a tree with her bear. She thinks Ben will not find her. She wraps her arms around her bear and smiles. She thinks she is happy. But then she hears a loud noise. It is a dog. The dog is big and black. It barks and runs towards Anna. Anna is fearful. She does not like dogs. She screams and drops her bear. Ben hears Anna's scream. He runs to help her. He sees the dog and the bear. He is brave. He picks up the bear and throws it to the dog. The dog stops barking and chases the bear. Ben grabs Anna's hand and pulls her away. They run to a safe place. Anna is thankful. She hugs Ben and says sorry. She says she was wrong. She says she will share her bear with him. Ben smiles and says it is okay. He says he is glad she is safe. He says he is her friend. They hug and play with the bear. They are happy. They share.

Once upon a time, there was a little yellow bird named Sunny. Sunny loved to eat oranges. Every day, he would fly to the orange tree in the park and eat one. One day, a mean boy saw Sunny eating an orange. The boy wanted the orange for himself. He thought of a plan to punish Sunny. He threw a rock at the little yellow bird to scare him away. But Sunny was very smart. He flew up high and dropped the orange on the mean boy's head. The boy felt sorry for trying to punish Sunny. He learned that it was not nice to take things from others. From that day on, the boy and Sunny became friends and shared the oranges from the tree.

Once upon a time, there was a king. The king was very kind. He had many friends. One day, the king got a letter in the mail. It was from his friend, the queen. The letter said, "I am sorry. I can't come to your party." The king was sad. He wanted the queen to come to his party. He thought, "I will write a letter back." The king wrote a letter to the queen. He said, "It's okay. I am sorry you can't come. We will have a party another day." He put the letter in the mail. The queen got the letter. She was happy the king was not mad. They had a party another day. The king and queen had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a cute dog named Spot. Spot was very happy because he had his very own TV. He loved to watch his favorite show about dogs playing and having fun. One day, Spot invited his friend, a cat named Fluffy, to watch TV with him. Fluffy thought the TV was very fun too. They laughed and played while they watched the dogs on the screen. After the show was over, Spot and Fluffy went outside to play together. They had so much fun that they forgot all about the TV. And from that day on, they knew that playing with friends was even better than watching TV.
