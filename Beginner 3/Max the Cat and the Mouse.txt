Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Cat and the Mouse

Once upon a time, in a regular house, there was a cat named Max. Max liked to serve food to his friends. One day, Max invited his friends over for a big meal. They were all very happy. While Max was serving the food, he saw a little mouse. The mouse wanted to escape from Max. Max stopped serving the food and started to chase the mouse. All the friends watched Max and the mouse run around the house. Then, something unexpected happened. The mouse jumped on Max's back, and Max stopped chasing him. The mouse whispered something in Max's ear. Max smiled and they became friends. Max and the mouse served the food together, and everyone had a great time.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little mouse. The mouse loved to play and explore the unknown places in the house. One day, he found a big, round, and shiny thing in the kitchen. He wanted to touch it but tried to resist. The mouse saw a cat and asked, "What is that shiny thing? Can I touch it?" The cat replied, "That is a hot pot. You should not touch it. It will hurt you." The mouse did not listen to the cat and still wanted to touch the shiny thing. The mouse could not resist his curiosity and touched the hot pot. He felt a lot of pain and cried out loud. The cat came running and said, "I told you not to touch it." The mouse learned that he should listen to others and not touch unknown things. But his paw hurt a lot, and he was sad.

Ben and Lily liked to watch films on the big TV. They had many films to choose from, but their favorite was the one with the powerful lion. The lion was the king of the animals and he had a loud roar. One day, Ben and Lily asked their mom if they could watch the lion film. Their mom said yes, but they had to set the table for dinner first. Ben and Lily agreed and ran to the kitchen. They took the plates, the forks, the spoons and the napkins and put them on the table. They did a good job and their mom was happy. Then, they went to the living room and turned on the TV. They found the lion film and pressed play. They sat on the couch and watched the lion and his friends. They laughed when the lion played with the monkey and the bird. They gasped when the lion fought with the bad lion. They cheered when the lion won and became the king again. They liked the lion film very much. They thought the lion was very powerful and brave. They wished they could be like him. When the film was over, they clapped and hugged each other. "Did you like the film?" their mom asked them. "Yes, we did!" Ben and Lily said. "The lion was awesome!" "I'm glad you enjoyed it," their mom said. "Now, let's go and have dinner. Maybe we can watch another film tomorrow." Ben and Lily nodded and followed their mom to the kitchen. They were hungry and happy. They loved their mom and they loved the lion film.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big, red ball. He felt lucky to find it. He wanted to play with it all day. He kicked it, he threw it, and he rolled it. As he played, he saw a girl named Sue. She was sad. "Hi Sue, why are you sad?" asked Tim. Sue said, "I lost my pretty, blue ball." Tim wanted to help Sue find her ball. They looked for it together. They looked under a tree and behind a bush. But they could not find the blue ball. Then, something unexpected happened. The big, red ball started to tear. Tim was sad. But as the ball tore, it showed a lot of power. The power turned the red ball into a shiny, new blue ball. Tim was happy and said, "Look Sue, it's your blue ball!" Sue was happy too. They played with the blue ball together and became good friends.
