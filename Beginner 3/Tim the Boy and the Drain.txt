Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Drain

One day, a little boy named Tim went to pack his bag for a trip. He put his toys, clothes, and a big red ball in the bag. He was very happy to go on the trip with his family. While walking, Tim saw an unusual thing. It was a big drain with water going down it. He looked at the drain and thought it was fun. He threw his big red ball into the drain, but then he felt sad because the ball was gone. Tim went back to his family, and they saw he was sad. They asked him what happened. He told them about the drain and the ball. Tim's family had a surprise for him. They had another big red ball! Tim was very happy again, and they all had a great trip.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big crane. The crane was very interesting. It could pick up heavy things and move them. The crane was very helpful to the people in the town. One day, a little boy named Tim saw the crane. He was very excited. He went to the crane and said, "Hello, crane! Can you help me? I request you to move my big toy car to my house." The crane was happy to help. It picked up the toy car and moved it to Tim's house. When Tim's friends saw the interesting crane helping him, they all wanted the crane's help too. They all said, "Crane, we also request your help!" The crane helped all the kids in the town. The kids were very happy, and the crane felt good about helping them.

Once upon a time, there was a big billboard near a small town. It was very light blue, and it had a picture of a smiling sun on it. Every day, people would walk by the billboard and smile back at the sun. One day, a little girl named Lily was walking by the billboard. She saw the sun and started to cry. She was sad because she lost her toy. The sun on the billboard wanted to help Lily, but it couldn't move. A nice dog saw Lily crying and came to her. The dog found her toy and gave it back to her. Lily stopped crying and hugged the dog. She looked at the billboard again and smiled. The sun on the billboard also seemed to smile even bigger, happy that Lily was not sad anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a small girl who loved to walk around in nature. On one of her walks, she came across a bean in the grass. She was so excited, she shouted out loud, "Look what I found!" Her mother, who was walking behind her, answered her, "That's a bad bean, let's leave it alone." The little girl didn't understand and picked up the bean in her hands. "Why is it bad mommy?" she asked. Her mother smiled and replied, "Because it won't grow into something special, like your other beans at home." The little girl smiled and said, "Ok, I'll leave it here then!" She put the bean back in the grass and they continued to walk together.

One day, a boy named Tim received a kayak for his birthday. He was very happy. Tim wanted to take his new kayak to the lake. He asked his mom, "Can we go to the lake today?" His mom said, "Yes, let's go!" At the lake, Tim saw many healthy ducks. They were swimming and playing in the water. Tim put his kayak in the water and started to paddle. He wanted to see the ducks up close. As Tim paddled his kayak, a duck came near him. Tim said, "Hello, duck! Do you like my new kayak?" The duck quacked and swam away. Tim laughed and paddled back to his mom. They had a fun day at the lake with the kayak and the healthy ducks.

Sam liked to play with his toy cars. He had many cars of different colors and sizes. He liked to make them race and crash on the floor. He did not like to share his cars with anyone. One day, his mom said they had to go to the store. She said he could bring one car with him. Sam looked at his cars and picked the red one. It was his favorite car. It was shiny and fast and had a loud horn. He did not want to leave his other cars alone. At the store, Sam saw a big bin of cheap cars. They were not as nice as his car. They were dull and slow and had no horns. But they were many and they were cheap. Sam wanted to have more cars. He thought he could buy some with his mom's money. He asked his mom if he could have some cars. She said no. She said he had enough cars at home. She said he should be happy with his red car. She said they had to hurry because a storm was coming. Sam was angry. He did not listen to his mom. He waited until she was not looking and grabbed some cars from the bin. He stuffed them in his pockets and ran to the door. He did not see his red car fall out of his hand. He got outside and saw the dark clouds. He heard the thunder. He felt the rain. He ran to the car where his mom was waiting. He got in and slammed the door. He smiled and looked at his pockets. But then he saw his mom's face. She was not smiling. She was angry. She saw the cars in his pockets. She saw the red car on the ground. She knew what he had done. She said he was a bad boy. She said he had to take the cars back. She said he had to say sorry. She said he had to give up his red car. She said he had to learn his lesson. Sam cried. He did not want to take the cars back. He did not want to say sorry. He did not want to give up his red car. He liked his red car. He wanted his red car. He looked out the window and saw his red car in the rain. It was wet and dirty and sad. He wished he had not taken the cheap cars. He wished he had listened to his mom. He wished he had been happy with his red car. He regretted what he had done.
