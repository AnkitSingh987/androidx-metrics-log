Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Cat and the Dog

One day, a cat and a dog were walking in the park. The cat saw a big pile of work. The dog looked at the cat and said, "I think we should do this work together." The cat agreed and they started to do the work. As they were working, they found something disgusting. The cat said, "Yuck! What is this?" The dog said, "I don't know, but it is disgusting!" They decided to keep working and not think about the disgusting thing. Then, something unexpected happened. A bird flew down and took the disgusting thing away. The cat and the dog were very happy. They finished their work and went home, feeling good about their day.

Once upon a time, there was a child named Tim. Tim had a toy car that he loved to play with every day. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. "Can you lend me your toy car?" Sam asked. Tim said, "Yes, but please be careful. I don't want it to get hurt." Sam played with the toy car, but he was not careful. The toy car crashed into a wall and got hurt. Tim was very sad, and he cried. Sam said, "I'm sorry, Tim. I didn't mean to hurt your car." Just then, Tim's mom came in with a big surprise. She had bought Tim a new toy car! Tim was happy again, and he decided to share his new car with Sam. They played together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and baby brother. Lily had a comfortable bed in her room. It was soft and warm. She loved to sleep in it every night. One day, Lily's mom and dad put up a pretty light in her room. It was shiny and bright. They hang the light up high on the wall, so Lily could see it when she was in her bed. Lily was very happy and excited to have the light in her room. That night, as Lily slept in her comfortable bed, something unexpected happened. The pretty light on the wall started to shake and wiggle. All of a sudden, the light fell off the wall and broke into many pieces. Lily woke up and saw the broken light. She was very sad and cried. Her pretty light was gone, and the room was dark again.

One day, a little ant went for a walk. The ant saw a big tree. The tree was very tall. The ant wanted to grow tall like the tree. The ant said, "I want to grow big too!" A silly bird heard the ant. The bird laughed and said, "You are too small to grow big like the tree. You are just an ant!" The ant felt sad but did not give up. The next day, the ant found a magic seed. The ant ate the seed and something unexpected happened. The ant started to grow and grow! The ant became as tall as the tree. The silly bird saw the big ant and was very surprised. The bird said, "Wow, you really did grow big like the tree!" The ant smiled and was happy. The ant and the bird became friends and played together every day.

Lily likes math. She likes to count and add and take away. She has a math book with many pages. She can do the math problems in the book. She feels happy when she does math. One day, Lily goes to the park with her mom and dad. She brings her math book with her. She wants to show her math book to her friend Ben. Ben likes math too. They can do math together. But when Lily gets to the park, she cannot find her math book. She looks in her bag, but it is not there. She looks on the ground, but it is not there. She feels sad. She thinks she lost her math book. She tells her mom and dad. They help her look for her math book. They ask other people in the park. They look in the trash cans. They look under the benches. But they cannot find her math book. Lily cries. She says she is sorry. She says she loves her math book. She says her math book is fragile. She says she does not want to lose her math book. Her mom and dad hug her. They say it is okay. They say they are sorry too. They say they will buy her a new math book. They say they love her. They say she is smart and good at math. Lily feels a little better. She says thank you. She says she loves them too. She says she will be careful with her new math book. She says she will not lose it again.
