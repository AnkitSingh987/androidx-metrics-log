Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys all day. One day, a big package came to his house. Inside the package were many toys. Tim was very happy. Tim's friend, Sam, came to play. Sam saw the new toys and wanted to play with them too. But Tim was selfish and said, "No, these are my toys!" Sam was sad and asked Tim again, "Can I please play with your toys?" Tim did not reply. Sam went home feeling sad. Tim's mom saw this and spoke to Tim. "You should share your toys with your friends," she said. Tim thought about it and felt sorry for Sam. He took some toys and went to Sam's house. At Sam's house, Tim said, "I am sorry for being selfish. Let's play with my new toys together." Sam was happy, and they played all day. From that day on, Tim always shared his toys and learned that sharing is caring.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had very long hair. It was time for a haircut. He wanted to look healthy and nice. Tim's mom took him to the haircut place. Tim was a little scared. He did not know what to expect. He asked his mom, "Will it hurt?" His mom said, "No, it will be fun!" The haircut lady was very nice. She cut Tim's hair and made him look healthy. Tim was happy with his haircut. He asked his mom, "Can we come back again?" His mom said, "Yes, we will come back when you need another haircut."

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big, hairy dog. The dog is friendly. He wags his tail and licks their faces. "Hello, dog!" Lily says. "What is your name?" The dog barks. He does not have a name. He does not have a house. He sleeps under a tree. "Can we take you home?" Ben asks. "We have a house. You can sleep on our bed." The dog barks again. He likes the idea. He follows Lily and Ben to their house. Their mom is surprised. "Who is this?" she asks. "This is our new friend," Lily says. "He is a big, hairy dog. He does not have a name or a house. Can he stay with us?" Mom smiles. She likes animals. She says yes. She gives the dog some food and water. The dog is happy. He sits on the bed with Lily and Ben. They hug him and give him a name. "His name is Max," Ben says. "Max is a good name," Lily says. Max wags his tail. He is glad. He has a name, a house, and two friends. He falls asleep with Lily and Ben. He dreams of the park.

Once upon a time, there was a healthy puppy called Flo. Flo loved playing in the garden on sunny days, especially when the mist came out. One day, Flo was in the garden and saw a butterfly. She barked excitedly and chased it. The butterfly flew up into the mist and Flo couldn't find it. "Flo!" called her owner from the doorstep. "Where are you?" Flo didn't want to leave, but she ran back to the house and barked up at the window. Her owner said, "It's time for dinner, Flo. Come inside and have something healthy to eat." Flo followed her owner inside and had a nice, healthy dinner. After dinner, Flo went back out into the garden to continue looking for the butterfly. But the butterfly was gone and the mist had gone too. Flo saw some other animals and played with them instead. She had a wonderful time and was glad she was so healthy.

Once there was a creative boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toys and make up fun games. One day, Tim saw a big gate with a guard standing near it. The guard was very tall and strong. Tim wanted to go through the gate and see what was on the other side. He thought of a plan to twist the guard's arm. Tim ran up to the guard, smiled, and said, "Let's play a game!" The guard looked at Tim and nodded his head. They played a game of twisting each other's arms. Tim was very creative and made the guard laugh. The guard liked Tim's game so much that he let Tim go through the gate. On the other side, Tim found a beautiful park with lots of flowers and trees. He played there all day and had lots of fun.
