Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Pale Kitten and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a pale kitten named Lily. Lily loved to play with her ball. One day, her ball broke. Lily was sad. Lily went to her friend, the dog named Max. Max was good at fixing things. Lily asked Max, "Can you repair my ball, please?" Max said, "Yes, I can help you." Max and Lily worked together to repair the ball. They used tape and glue. Soon, the ball was fixed. Lily was happy again. She said, "Thank you, Max!" They played together with the ball all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a person named Lily. Lily loved the color pink. She had pink clothes, a pink room, and even a pink cat. One day, Lily went to the store with her mom. She saw a big pink cake. The cake looked very yummy. Lily asked her mom if they could buy the cake. Her mom said yes. They took the cake home and Lily got to taste it. The cake was sweet and delicious. Lily was so happy to have her pink cake. She shared it with her friends and family. Everyone loved the pink cake too.

Lila was a shy girl who liked to play by the sea. She had a big red bucket and a small blue shovel. She liked to dig in the sand and look for shells and stones. One day, she saw something shiny in the sand. She dug and dug until she found a big metal thing. It was an anchor. Lila wondered who left it there and what it was for. She wanted to take it home, but it was too heavy for her. She heard a voice behind her. "Hi, I'm Max. What are you doing?" It was a boy who was playing with a ball. He had curly brown hair and a friendly smile. Lila was shy and did not know what to say. She hid behind the anchor and hoped he would go away. But Max was curious and came closer. He saw the anchor and said, "Wow, that's cool. Where did you find it?" Lila peeked from behind the anchor and said, "In the sand. It's mine." Max said, "Can I see it? Maybe we can play with it together." Lila was scared and said, "No, go away. It's mine." Max said, "Don't be shy. I won't take it from you. I just want to be your friend." Lila looked at Max and saw that he was nice. She felt a little less shy and said, "Okay, you can see it. But be careful. It's heavy." Max nodded and said, "Thank you. You're very kind." Lila and Max touched the anchor and talked about it. They wondered who left it there and what it was for. They made up stories and laughed. They played with the ball and the bucket and the shovel. They had fun. Lila and Max met by the sea. They found an anchor and became friends. They were not shy anymore.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a polite cat. The cat had soft, warm fur. She loved to play with her friends, the birds, and the rabbits. One day, a big, mean dog came into the forest. The dog wanted to chase the cat and her friends. The cat was scared. She did not want the dog to hurt her friends. So, she thought of a plan. The cat went to a quiet place and began to pray. She asked for help to make the dog go away. As she prayed, a kind fairy heard her. The fairy came to the forest and saw the scared cat. The fairy waved her wand and turned the mean dog into a nice dog. The dog stopped chasing the cat and her friends. The cat was so happy. She thanked the fairy and the dog became their new friend. From that day on, the cat, the dog, and all their friends played happily in the big green forest. They were all safe and had lots of fun together. And the polite cat with the soft, warm fur always remembered to pray when she needed help.
