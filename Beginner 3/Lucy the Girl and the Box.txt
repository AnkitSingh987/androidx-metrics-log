Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Box

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to a big building with her mom. They needed to go up to the top floor. They saw a big box with doors. It was an elevator. Lucy and her mom went inside. The doors closed and they started to go up. Inside the elevator, it was dark. Lucy was scared. She held her mom's hand tight. Her mom said, "Don't worry, Lucy. We will be there soon." Lucy felt better. She knew her mom would keep her safe. When they got to the top floor, the doors opened. Lucy saw a big room with lots of colors. There were people mixing colors in big bowls. Lucy's mom said, "We are here to mix colors for your new room." Lucy was happy. She and her mom mixed colors together and had lots of fun.

One day, a little cat named Sam was playing outside. It was a hot day, so Sam was very thirsty. He saw a big red cup on the table and wanted to drink from it. Sam jumped on the table and tried to drink from the cup. But, he knocked the cup over and water went everywhere! Sam was scared and ran away. He heard someone say, "Oh no, an emergency!" Mom came and saw the mess. She said, "Sam, we need to shut the door so you don't make more messes." Sam felt sad but knew it was best. He helped Mom clean up and then they shut the door.

Once upon a time, there was a wise old owl who lived in a big tree. One day, a small red car came to the tree. The car was sad because it needed fuel to go fast. The wise owl thought for a moment and said, "I know where you can find fuel, but you must promise to return and help me when I need you." The car agreed and the wise owl told the car where to find the fuel. The car went fast to the fuel place and filled up. The car was happy and said, "Thank you, wise owl! I will return when you need me." One day, a big storm came and the tree was about to fall. The wise owl was scared and called for the car. The car heard the owl and raced back to the tree. The car pushed the tree back up with all its strength. The tree stood tall again, and the owl was safe. The car and the owl became best friends and lived happily ever after. The moral of the story is to always keep your promises and help those who help you. And sometimes, even the smallest friends can make the biggest difference.

One day, a blue jeep went for a ride. The sun was up, and the sky was blue. The jeep liked to drive on the dirt road. It went fast, and it went slow. The jeep loved to play in the mud. The blue jeep saw a big tree. Under the tree, there was a cat. The cat was thirsty. The cat said, "I want to drink some water, can you help me?" The jeep wanted to help the cat. The blue jeep went to find water. It found a lake with clear water. The jeep took some water and went back to the cat. The cat drank the water and said, "Thank you, blue jeep, for helping me." The blue jeep was happy to help. It went back to the dirt road to play. The cat and the jeep became good friends. They played together every day under the big tree.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play with a big red ball. The ball can bounce very high. Tom and Lily take turns to throw the ball and catch it. One day, they decide to play a new game. They want to see who can bounce the ball more times. They count the number of bounces out loud. Tom goes first. He throws the ball hard and says, "One, two, three, four, five!" He catches the ball and smiles. He thinks he is good at bouncing. Lily goes next. She throws the ball softly and says, "One, two, three, four, five, six, seven!" She catches the ball and giggles. She thinks she is better at bouncing. Tom and Lily look at each other. They both want to win. They both want to bounce the ball more times. They throw the ball again and again. They say bigger and bigger numbers. But then, something happens. The ball bounces too high and hits a tree. The ball falls down and rolls away. Tom and Lily run after the ball. They forget about the game. They just want to get the ball back. They find the ball near a fence. They are happy. They hug the ball and each other. They say, "We are sorry, ball. We love you, ball. You are the best ball ever." They go back to their yard. They decide to play a different game. They decide to share the ball and have fun. They think they are both good at bouncing. They think they are both good friends.
