Category: Beginner
Title: Jack and Rose and the Park

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Jack and Rose. They both loved to play in the park every day. One day, Jack and Rose were playing hide and seek in the park. Suddenly, Jack looked up through the trees and saw something exciting. It was a victory flag that was blowing in the wind! Jack couldn't help himself. He raced to the flag and he tried to steal it for himself. But when he tried, it ripped in half. It was broken. Rose was very angry with Jack. She said, "Why did you do that? That was not the way to win a victory!". Jack felt so bad. He said, "I'm sorry, I just wanted to steal the victory." Rose said, "There are other ways to win a victory. You can play games together and have fair turns. That's the best way to have a victory. Let's do that together instead." Jack smiled and said, "Okay, let's play together." And so Jack and Rose played together and had lots of fun. They both won the victory by playing fair.

One day, a gray cat named Tim saw a cart. The cart had big, round wheels. Tim wanted to play with the cart. He blinked his eyes and ran to the cart. A little boy named Sam was with the cart. Sam said, "Hi, cat! Do you want to play with my cart?" Tim blinked and said, "Yes, I want to play with your cart!" So, Tim and Sam played with the cart. They pushed the cart up and down the street. But then, the cart hit a big rock. The cart broke into pieces. Tim and Sam were sad. They could not play with the cart anymore. The gray cat blinked and walked away.

One day, a light blue bird and a small red bug met on the ground. They both wanted the same yummy cookie that was on the ground. The bird said, "I want the cookie!" The bug said, "No, I want the cookie!" They began to fight over the cookie. The bird tried to pick up the cookie with its beak, but the bug held on tight with its tiny legs. They pushed and pulled, but neither could get the cookie. A big, wise tree saw the fight and said, "Stop fighting! You can both share the cookie." The bird and the bug stopped fighting and looked at the tree. They both said, "Okay, we will share the cookie." So, they broke the cookie in half and each had a piece. They were both happy and became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a modern house in the woods. In this house, a little girl named Anna lived with her mom and dad. They loved to take a nap after playing outside. One day, they found a big pile of lumber near their house. They were very happy because they could use the lumber to make new things. Anna's dad decided to build a small playhouse for Anna with the lumber. He worked hard and finished the playhouse in a few days. Anna was so excited to play in her new playhouse. She took her toys inside and played all day long. When she got tired, she took a nap in her playhouse. While Anna was napping, something unexpected happened. A little squirrel came into the playhouse to take a nap too. When Anna woke up from her nap, she saw the squirrel and was very surprised. The squirrel was scared at first, but then they became friends. Anna named the squirrel Nutty. From that day on, Anna and Nutty played together and took naps in the playhouse. They had lots of fun and lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a walk. She liked to skip in the sun. It was a light and happy day. Sue saw her friend Tom. Tom had a big red ball. "Hi Tom," Sue said. "Do you want to play with me?" "Yes, Sue!" Tom said. They played with the ball and had fun. Then, they saw ice on the ground. It was cold and wet. They knew they needed to be careful. Sue and Tom played near the ice. They did not step on it. They knew it was slippery. Sue threw the ball high in the air. The ball fell on the ice. "Oh no!" Tom said. "How will we get the ball?" Sue had a good idea. She found a long stick. She used the stick to get the ball off the ice. Sue and Tom were happy. They played with the ball and skipped away from the ice.
