Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Butterfly and the Tree

One day, a little butterfly was flying in the peaceful sky. The butterfly saw a big tree and wanted to rest on it. She flew to the tree and sat on a green leaf. A small boy saw the butterfly on the tree. He wanted to catch it and said, "Butterfly, don't move. I want to catch you!" The butterfly started to panic and flew away very fast. The boy ran after the butterfly, but he tripped on a rock and fell down. The butterfly saw the boy fall and felt bad. She flew back to the boy and said, "Don't be sad. I will stay with you." The boy smiled and they became good friends.

Lily likes jazz. Jazz is music with drums and horns and piano. Lily has a small jazz CD. She plays it every day on her CD player. One day, Lily wants to show her jazz CD to her friend Max. Max comes to her house and they go to her room. Lily takes out her jazz CD and says, "Look, Max, this is my jazz CD. It has good music. Do you want to hear it?" Max nods and says, "Yes, please. I like music too." Lily puts the jazz CD in the CD player and presses play. The music starts and they dance and clap. But then, Max sees a toy car on the floor. He likes cars. He runs to the car and picks it up. He forgets about the jazz CD. He throws the car in the air and catches it. He does it again and again. But one time, he throws the car too high. It hits the CD player and makes a loud noise. The music stops. The CD player is broken. The jazz CD is broken too. It has a big crack. Lily sees what happened and cries. She loves her jazz CD. She says, "Max, you broke my jazz CD! You broke my CD player! That was not nice. That was bad. I am sad." Max feels sorry. He did not mean to break the jazz CD and the CD player. He says, "I am sorry, Lily. I was playing with the car. I did not see the CD player. I did not want to break your jazz CD and your CD player. Can I fix them?" Lily shakes her head and says, "No, you cannot fix them. They are broken. You have to go home now. I do not want to play with you anymore." Max leaves and says, "Bye, Lily. I am sorry. I hope you can get a new jazz CD and a new CD player." Lily stays in her room and cries. She misses her jazz CD and her CD player. She hopes her mom can buy her a new one. She likes jazz. Jazz is music with drums and horns and piano.

Tom likes to write. He writes with a pen on paper. He writes his name, his mom's name, his dad's name. He writes big and small letters. He writes words he knows, like cat, dog, sun, moon. One day, Tom goes to school. He has a new teacher. The teacher gives him a paper with words on it. She says, "Write these words on your paper. Then I will check them." Tom looks at the words. He does not know some of them. He does not know how to spell them. He feels worried. He thinks, "What if I write them wrong? What if the teacher gets angry? What if I get a bad mark?" Tom tries to write the words. He writes some of them right. He writes some of them wrong. He writes some of them with missing letters. He writes some of them with extra letters. He writes some of them backwards. He writes some of them upside down. He writes some of them with funny shapes. He writes some of them with dots and lines. He writes some of them with colors and stickers. He writes some of them with smileys and hearts. The teacher comes to check his paper. She looks at his words. She smiles. She says, "Tom, you have a very creative way of writing. You have made some mistakes, but that is okay. Everyone makes mistakes when they learn. The important thing is that you try. You can learn from your mistakes and improve. You can also have fun with writing. Writing is not only about spelling. Writing is also about expressing yourself. You have a lot of ideas and feelings in your writing. I like that. You are a good writer, Tom." Tom feels happy. He says, "Thank you, teacher. I like to write. I want to learn more words. I want to write better. I also want to write fun. Can I write more words with colors and stickers and smileys and hearts?" The teacher says, "Yes, you can, Tom. You can write whatever you want. You can write your own stories. You can write your own books. You can write your own world. You can write your own magic." The moral of the story is: Writing is a skill that can be learned and improved, but it is also a way of expressing yourself and having fun. You should not be afraid of making mistakes, but rather learn from them and try again. You should also be creative and original with your writing, and use your imagination and emotions. Writing can be a wonderful and magical activity.
