Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, ugly knee. It was red and had a big bump. Tim did not like his ugly knee. He thought no one would want to play with him. One day, Tim went to the park. He saw some kids playing with a ball. Tim wanted to play too, but he was scared. He thought they would not like his ugly knee. But, Tim was brave and asked if he could play. The kids looked at his knee and said, "Sure, you can play with us!" As they played, Tim's knee did not seem to bother the other kids. They had fun and played all day. Tim was happy and learned that it is not how you look that matters, but how you treat others. The moral of the story is to not judge others by how they look, but by how they act.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big tree. Tim saw a lollipop on the ground. He was very happy. Tim picked up the lollipop and said, "Look, Mom! I found a lollipop!" His mom said, "That's nice, Tim. But it's not fit to eat. It's dirty." Tim was sad, but he knew his mom was right. He put the lollipop in the trash. Suddenly, a little squirrel came out of the tree. The squirrel pointed at the lollipop in the trash. Tim and his mom were surprised. The squirrel took the lollipop out of the trash and started to clean it. Then, the squirrel gave the clean lollipop back to Tim. Tim looked at his mom and she smiled. "Okay, Tim. You can eat the lollipop now." Tim was very happy and thanked the squirrel. The squirrel just waved and ran back up the tree. Tim, his mom, and the squirrel all had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly girl named Lily. She loved to play outside in the sun. One day, she went to the park with her mom. At the park, Lily saw a big circle on the ground. It was made with colorful chalk. She wanted to play a game with the circle. Lily asked her mom if she could pick some flowers to put on the circle. Lily and her mom picked pretty flowers together. They put the flowers on the circle and made a beautiful pattern. They had so much fun playing with the circle and flowers. They went home with big smiles on their faces.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to bed. In his dream, he saw a big chocolate cake. The cake had a big, silly face on it. The face made Tim laugh. He wanted to eat the cake, but the cake started to run away. Tim chased the silly chocolate cake. They ran through a big, green park. The cake was very fast, but Tim was faster. He caught the cake and took a big bite. It was the best cake he ever ate. When Tim woke up, he was very happy. He told his mom about the silly chocolate cake. His mom laughed and said, "That was a fun dream!" She made Tim a real chocolate cake with a silly face, just like in his dream. Tim and his mom ate the cake and laughed together.

One day, a small dog named Spot had an urge to play. He went to his friend, a big dog named Buddy. Spot said, "Let's play with the ball!" Buddy agreed and they went to the park. At the park, they saw a big truck. The truck was loading many toys. Spot and Buddy wanted to play with the toys, but the man in the truck said, "No, you can't play with these toys. They are for other kids." Spot and Buddy were sad. They went back to their ball and started to play. As they played, they saw the truck drive away. Spot said, "I wish we could play with those toys." Buddy agreed, but they knew they had to share. In the end, Spot and Buddy had fun playing with their ball. They learned that they can't always have what they want, but they can still have a good time with what they have.
