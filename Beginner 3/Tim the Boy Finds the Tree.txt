Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Tree

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Rex, were playing in the park. They were tired of playing the same games every day. Tim said, "Rex, let's do something new. I don't want to play boring games anymore." Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's have a race! We will run from this tree to that tree. The one who gets there first will win. Are you ready, Rex?" Rex barked happily, and they got ready to start the race. Tim counted, "One, two, three, go!" They both started to run as fast as they could. Tim and Rex were laughing and barking. They ran and ran until they reached the tree. Tim won the race, but it was very close. Rex was only a little bit behind him. After the race, they were both very happy. Tim said, "That was so much fun, Rex! We should do more races like this. It's much better than our old boring games." Rex barked in agreement, and they continued to play new and exciting games in the park. From that day on, Tim and Rex always tried new games and had lots of fun together. They learned that trying new things can have great results, and they were never bored again.

Once upon a time, there was a busy town. In this town, there was a gas shop. The gas shop was very important. People came to the gas shop to get gas for their cars. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the gas shop with his dad. Tim's dad needed gas for their car. Tim saw a big machine at the gas shop. Tim asked his dad, "What is that big machine?" His dad said, "That machine is where we get the gas." Tim's dad recommended that Tim should not touch the machine. But Tim was curious. When his dad was not looking, Tim touched the big machine. Suddenly, the machine started to shake! Tim was scared. Then, something unexpected happened. The machine turned into a big, friendly robot! The robot said, "Hello, Tim! I am here to help you and your dad." Tim and his dad were very surprised but happy. From that day on, the robot helped everyone in the busy town get gas for their cars.

Once upon a time, there was a brave dog named Max. Max lived in a small house with his family. Max loved to play with his ball and his family. He felt that he belonged to them. One day, Max smelled something bad. He saw a big fire in the house! Max barked and barked. "Woof, woof!" he said, trying to tell his family about the fire. His family heard him and saw the fire too. They were scared. Max's family tried to put out the fire, but it was too big. They had to leave the house. Max was brave, and he tried to help. But the fire was too strong. In the end, the house burned down, and Max's family was very sad.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big, scary skull in his room. He didn't know where it came from. He was so scared that he ran to his mom. "Mom, there is a scary skull in my room!" he said. His mom went to see the skull. When they got to the room, the skull was gone. Tim's mom looked around and saw a big box. She opened the box and found a big hat. The hat was so big that it didn't fit on Tim's head. They laughed and put the hat back in the box. Then, Tim's dad came in and said, "I got you a funny hat for the party, but it looks like it's too big!" Tim and his mom looked at each other and laughed. The scary skull was just the big hat all along!

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with toys and books. But on Tuesday, they have a big fight. Lily wants to play with the red car, but Ben has it. Ben does not want to share. "Give me the car!" Lily says. "No, it is mine!" Ben says. They pull and push. The car falls on the floor. It breaks. Lily and Ben are sad. They look at the car. They look at each other. "I am sorry, Lily," Ben says. "I am sorry, Ben," Lily says. They hug. They are friends again. They find another toy to play with. They are happy. They learn to share. They say sorry when they make a mistake. They have fun on Tuesday.
