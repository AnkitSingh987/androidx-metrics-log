Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Dog

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They brought bread to feed the ducks. Tim was very happy and comfortable in his warm coat. The ducks were happy too, swimming and quacking. "Mom, can I give the ducks the bread now?" Tim asked. His mom said, "Yes, but first, let's count the ducks in order." They counted one, two, three, four, five ducks in the pond. Tim started to feed the ducks the bread. As Tim was feeding the ducks, something unexpected happened. A big, friendly dog came running and barking. The dog wanted to eat the bread too! Tim was surprised, but he laughed and shared the bread with the dog. The ducks, the dog, and Tim all had a fun day at the park.

One day, a curious cat named Tom saw a big lawn. He wanted to play on the lawn. So, he ran to the lawn and started to play. While playing, Tom saw a big ball. He wanted to play with the ball. He went to the ball and started to rub it with his paw. "This is fun!" said Tom. Suddenly, the ball started to move. It was not a ball, but a big turtle! The turtle said, "Hello, I am Tim the Turtle. Do you want to play with me?" Tom was surprised but happy. They played together on the lawn and became good friends.

Ben and Lily were playing in the park with their dog, Spot. They liked to observe the birds and the squirrels that lived in the trees. Sometimes they would pretend to be hunters and chase them, but they never hurt them. One day, they saw a big bird with a long neck and a red head. It was a rare bird that they had never seen before. They wanted to get closer and observe it better. "Look, Lily, a rare bird!" Ben said. "Let's go and see it!" "OK, Ben, but be quiet and careful. We don't want to scare it away," Lily said. They walked slowly and quietly towards the bird, with Spot following them. The bird was eating some seeds on the ground. It did not notice them at first. But then, Spot barked loudly. He wanted to play with the bird. The bird was startled and flew away quickly. Ben and Lily were sad. They had lost their chance to observe the rare bird. "Spot, why did you bark?" Ben asked. "You scared the bird away!" "Sorry, Ben, sorry, Lily," Spot said. "I just wanted to say hello to the bird. I didn't know it was rare." Ben and Lily forgave Spot. They knew he was a good dog. They hoped to see the rare bird again someday. They went back to playing in the park, observing the other animals.

Once upon a time, there was a charming little girl. She had curly hair and wore a pink dress. One day, the little girl decided to visit the park. As soon as she arrived, she noticed a pigeon perched on the edge of a fountain. She smiled and took a few steps towards the bird, but just as she did, she slipped. The pigeon flew away to the other side of the park. The girl was sad. She quietly said, "Oh, pigeon! Come back!" Suddenly, the pigeon turned around and flew back to the girl. It perched on her shoulder and gave her a friendly nuzzle. The girl smiled, happy that the pigeon had listened to her. The pigeon stayed with the little girl the entire day. As the sun started to set, it flew off with its wings outstretched in a farewell. The girl's day had been made special by the charming little pigeon.

Ben and Mia liked to play outside. They had bikes and scooters and balls. They always wore helmets to be safe. Their helmets were dependable. They did not break or fall off. One day, they saw a big box in the yard. It was from their dad. He said it was a surprise. They wanted to open it. They asked their mom for scissors. She said no. Scissors were sharp. They could cut themselves. They had to wait for dad. Ben and Mia did not want to wait. They looked for something else to cut the box. They found a knife in the kitchen. They thought it was like scissors. They took it outside. They did not tell mom. They tried to cut the box with the knife. It was hard. They pushed and pulled. The knife slipped. It cut Ben's helmet. Ben was scared. He felt something wet on his head. He cried. Mia heard him cry. She dropped the knife. She ran to him. She saw the cut on his helmet. She saw the blood on his head. She was sorry. She hugged him. She called mom. Mom came outside. She saw the knife and the box and the helmet and the blood. She was angry and worried. She took Ben inside. She cleaned his wound. She put a bandage on it. She said he was lucky. His helmet was dependable. It saved him from a big cut. She said he was silly. He should not play with knives. He should listen to mom. Ben said he was sorry. He said he wanted to see the surprise. He said he loved his helmet. He said he loved mom. Mom said she loved him too. She said she forgave him. She said he could see the surprise later. She said it was a tent. They could camp in the yard. Ben smiled. He liked tents. He said thank you to mom. He said thank you to his helmet. He said he would be more careful. He said he would wait for dad. He said he wanted to camp with Mia. Mia said she wanted to camp with him too. She said she was sorry too. She said she loved him and his helmet. They hugged. They waited for dad. They learned a lesson.
