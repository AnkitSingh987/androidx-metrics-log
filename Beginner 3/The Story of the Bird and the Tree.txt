Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Bird and the Tree

One day, a little bird wanted to soar high in the sky. It saw a distant tree and thought it would be fun to fly there. The bird was so excited to go on this adventure. On the way to the distant tree, the little bird saw a closet. The bird was curious and went inside the closet to look around. Inside the closet, there were many things to see and play with. The bird was having a lot of fun. But then, the door of the closet closed, and the little bird was stuck inside. It tried to soar and fly, but there was not enough space. The bird was sad and could not go to the distant tree anymore. The little bird stayed in the closet, and its adventure had a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to listen to his mom's stories. One day, his mom told him a story about a magic gem. The gem was very special and could make people happy. One day, Tim went to the forest to find the magic gem. He met a big bird in the forest. The bird said, "I know where the gem is, but there is a bad wolf guarding it." Tim was not scared. He wanted to help everyone be happy. Tim and the bird went to the place where the gem was. They saw the bad wolf sleeping. Tim said to the bird, "Let's be quiet and take the gem." They took the gem and went back to their village. Everyone was happy, and they all thanked Tim and the bird for their help.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived an old man. He had a jar. The jar was very big. The old man liked his jar a lot. One day, a little boy came to the old man's house. He saw the jar and wanted to play with it. The little boy said, "Can I throw the jar, please?" The old man said, "No, the jar is too big to throw. You might break it." The little boy did not listen to the old man. He picked up the jar and threw it. The jar broke into many pieces. The old man was very sad. The little boy was sad too. And they could not fix the jar.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a big boot. He loved his boot very much. He played with it all day. One day, Tim's boot got very dirty. He felt ashamed. He wanted his boot to be clean again. So, he went to his mom and asked her to help him wash the boot. Tim and his mom washed the boot together. They scrubbed and scrubbed. Soon, the boot was clean again. Tim was very happy. He thanked his mom and promised to keep his boot clean. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Mia was playing in her room. She saw a big curtain hanging by the window. She wanted to touch it. Mia reached up high and touched the curtain. It felt soft and nice. Mia's mom came into the room and said, "Mia, do you want to help me make a spicy soup for dinner?" Mia nodded her head and followed her mom to the kitchen. They put some spicy things into the pot and stirred it together. While they were cooking, Mia heard a noise coming from her room. She went back to see what it was. When she opened the door, she saw that the curtain had turned into a big, friendly bear! The bear said, "Hello, Mia! I am your new friend. I came alive when you touched the curtain." Mia was so happy to have a new friend and they played together all day.

One day, a cute little cat saw a box. The cat wanted to play with the box. The cat said, "Please, can I play with the box?" The cat's friend, a dog, heard the cat's request. The dog said, "Yes, you can play with the box. I will help you." The cat and the dog played with the box. They had a lot of fun. They were very happy. And they became best friends.
