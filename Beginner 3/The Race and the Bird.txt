Category: Beginner
Title: The Race and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big race. Many animals wanted to win. The prize was a big, dry present. The rabbit, the cat, the dog, and the bird all ran very fast. They all wanted the present. The rabbit was fast, but the dog was faster. The cat was fast too, but the bird was the fastest. In the end, the bird won the race. All the animals clapped for the bird. The bird opened the big, dry present. Inside was a big, yummy cake for everyone to share. They all ate the cake and had a fun time.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful garden. In the garden, there were many pretty flowers and a big tree. Under the tree, there was a door to the underground. One sunny day, a little girl named Mia was playing near the tree. She saw the door and wanted to see what was underground. Mia requested her mom to open the door. Her mom said, "Okay, let's see what is there." Mia and her mom went underground. They saw many colorful stones and a small, friendly bug. They said "hello" to the bug and played with the stones. Mia and her mom had a fun time in the underground. Then, they went back up to the beautiful garden and played some more.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One day, Tim put on his red glove and went to the park. He saw a big hill and wanted to climb it. He thought it would be hard, but he wanted to try. As Tim climbed the hill, he stumbled on a small rock. He fell down, but he got up and tried again. He knew he had to be careful so he wouldn't stumble again. He looked at his red glove and felt brave. Finally, Tim made it to the top of the hill. He was so happy! He looked down and saw the small rock that made him stumble. He knew that he had to be careful when he climbed down. Tim took a deep breath, put on his red glove, and carefully climbed down the hill. He didn't stumble this time, and he felt proud.

One day, a big boat was near the shore. The boat had a big anchor. The anchor was very heavy. The boat's captain was a nice man. He thought the boat was boring. He wanted to have fun on the shore. "Come, let's go to the shore," said the captain to his friend. His friend was a little boy. The boy was very happy. He liked to play on the shore. They went to the shore together. On the shore, they saw many things. They saw sand, water, and birds. The captain and the boy played in the sand. They built a sand castle. The birds came and watched them play. The captain and the boy had a lot of fun. They were not bored anymore.

Tim and Lily were playing in the park with their ball. They liked to kick the ball to each other and catch it. Sometimes they missed the ball and it rolled away. Then they ran after it and laughed. One day, a big dog came to the park. It was black and hairy and had a loud bark. It saw the ball and ran after it too. It caught the ball in its mouth and ran away. Tim and Lily were scared and sad. They wanted their ball back. "Hey, give us our ball!" Tim shouted at the dog. The dog stopped and looked at Tim. It growled and showed its teeth. Tim and Lily were more scared. They did not know what to do. A man came to the park. He was wearing a hat and a coat. He looked mysterious. He saw the dog and the children. He walked to them and said: "Hello, I am the judge. I can help you with your problem. What is the matter?" Tim and Lily told the judge about the dog and the ball. They said they were worried and angry. The judge nodded and said: "I see. Well, the dog is not bad. He just likes to play. He does not know that the ball is yours. Maybe we can make a deal. If you give the dog a treat, he will give you the ball. Do you have a treat?" Tim and Lily looked in their pockets. They found a cookie and a cracker. They gave them to the judge. The judge gave them to the dog. The dog sniffed the treats and wagged its tail. He dropped the ball and ate the treats. Tim and Lily picked up the ball and smiled. "Thank you, judge!" they said. "You're welcome, children. Now you can play with your ball again. And maybe you can make friends with the dog. He is not so scary. He is just lonely. What do you say?" Tim and Lily looked at the dog. The dog looked at them. He licked his lips and barked. He seemed friendly. Tim and Lily decided to give him a chance. They threw the ball to the dog and he ran after it. Then he brought it back to them. They played together and had fun. The judge watched them and smiled. He was happy that he could help. He put on his hat and coat and left the park. He was mysterious. He had a secret. He was not really a judge. He was a magician. He liked to help people and animals with his magic. He had many more adventures to go.
