Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Ball

Tom and Lily were playing in the park with their ball. They saw a big hill and wanted to climb it. They left their ball on the grass and started to walk up the hill. But the hill was not a hill. It was a giant! The giant was sleeping and did not hear them. Tom and Lily reached his nose and thought it was a rock. They tried to push it, but it was too heavy. The giant sneezed and woke up. He opened his eyes and saw Tom and Lily. He was surprised and curious. "Who are you?" he asked in a loud voice. "Why are you on my nose?" Tom and Lily were scared and wanted to run away. But they were too small and the giant was too big. They could not find their ball or the way back. "We are Tom and Lily," they said in a small voice. "We are sorry. We thought you were a hill. We were playing with our ball and we wanted to climb you." The giant smiled and was not angry. He was friendly and liked children. He picked them up gently and put them on his hand. "I am Ben," he said. "I am not a hill. I am a giant. I like to play with balls too. Where is your ball?" Tom and Lily pointed to the grass where they left their ball. But the ball was too far and too small for them to see. The giant saw their ball and reached for it. He gave it to Tom and Lily. They were happy and thanked him. "Can we play with you?" they asked. "Of course," he said. "But first, I have a magic trick for you. I can shrink and grow as I please. Watch this." The giant said a magic word and shrank to the size of Tom and Lily. They were amazed and clapped their hands. The giant said another magic word and grew back to his normal size. He did this several times and made them laugh. Tom and Lily had fun playing with the giant and his magic trick. They learned that he was not scary, but kind and gentle. They also learned that size does not matter, but friendship does. They became good friends with the giant and visited him often. They always brought their ball and shared it with him.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Jane. Jane always liked to wear a green blouse. Every morning when she woke up, she would take her green blouse out of her dresser and put it on. She loved her blouse very much and wore it every day. One day, Jane was in a rush. She had to go to school, but she wanted to stay in bed a bit longer. She quickly grabbed her blouse and tried to put it on, but the buttons were too difficult for her. She started to get frustrated and her mom had to help her. Her mom taught her something important. She said, "Jane, when you're in a rush make sure you take time to do your buttons. Don't forget to always close them so your blouse won't fall off." Jane looked at her mom and nodded. She learned an important lesson that day. Taking the time to do your buttons, even when you're in a rush, is important. Jane smiled, hugged her mom, and went off to school with her green blouse properly closed.

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing in her room. She had many toys, but she did not like to share them. Her mom said she was selfish. Lily did not care. She liked her toys all to herself. While playing, Lily saw a pretty lady in her room. The lady was an angel. The angel said, "Lily, you must learn to share. It is not nice to be selfish." Lily did not recognize the angel, but she wanted to be a good girl. The next day, Lily went to the park with her toys. She saw a sad boy with no toys. Lily thought about the angel and decided to share her toys with the boy. The boy was so happy, and they played together all day. In the end, Lily was happy she shared and was not selfish anymore.
