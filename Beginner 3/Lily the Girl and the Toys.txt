Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily liked to go to the store with her mom. The store had many fun things to look at. One day, Lily and her mom went to the store. They had to wait in a long line. Lily did not like to wait, but she was a good girl and stood quietly. She looked at all the open boxes of toys and wished she could play with them. Finally, it was their turn to pay. Lily's mom bought her a new toy. Lily was so happy. They left the store and went home. Lily played with her new toy all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a gray bunny. The bunny lived in a small house in the woods. The bunny loved to play outside with his friends, the birds and the squirrels. One day, the bunny found some money on the ground. He was very happy. He wanted to buy some carrots with the money. So, he put the money in his pocket and started to rush to the store. As the gray bunny rushed to the store, he met his friends. They all went to the store together. They bought carrots, nuts, and seeds. The bunny and his friends had a big party with their new treats. They were very happy and played all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play games. One day, he found a modern game in a big box. The box was colorful and had many buttons. Tim was so happy and wanted to play the game right away. Tim called his friend, Sam. "Sam, come play this fun game with me!" Sam came over to Tim's house. They sat down and started to play the modern game. They were having so much fun, laughing and smiling. But then, Tim's little sister, Sue, came into the room. Sue wanted to play the game too, but she did not know how. She pressed all the buttons and made the game stop working. Tim was sad and said, "Sue, you spoil the game!" Sam was sad too, but they decided to teach Sue how to play the game. Soon, they were all playing the game together, having lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a new, soft blanket. She loved her blanket very much. She took it everywhere she went. It was her favorite thing. One day, Lily went to the park with her mommy and daddy. She brought her blanket with her. She played with her blanket in the sun. She made a little house with it. She was very happy. At the end of the day, it was time to go home. Lily was very tired. She held her blanket close and gave it a big kiss. She said, "Thank you, blanket, for being my friend." Then, Lily fell asleep in her mommy's arms, holding her blanket tight.

Anna and Ben were very happy. They went to a big park with their mom and dad. There was a big white castle in the park. It was not a real castle, but a castle made of air. Anna and Ben could bounce on it and have fun. Anna and Ben ran to the castle. They took off their shoes and climbed on it. They bounced high and low. They laughed and shouted. They had a lot of fun. But they did not see the other kids who wanted to bounce too. They did not share the castle. They bounced and bounced and bounced. The other kids were sad. They waited and waited and waited. They wanted to bounce too. But Anna and Ben did not let them. They said, "This is our castle. Go away!" They were not nice. They were not kind. Mom and dad saw what was happening. They were not happy. They called Anna and Ben. They said, "Anna and Ben, come here. You have to share the castle. The castle is for everyone. You have to be nice and kind. You have to take turns. That is the right thing to do." Anna and Ben felt bad. They knew they were wrong. They said sorry to the other kids. They said, "We are sorry. We were not nice. We were not kind. You can bounce too. Let's share the castle. Let's have fun together." The other kids smiled. They said, "Thank you. You are nice. You are kind. Let's bounce together. Let's have fun together." They put on their shoes and climbed on the castle. They bounced high and low. They laughed and shouted. They had a lot of fun. Anna and Ben had more fun too. They made new friends. They learned to share. They were happy.
