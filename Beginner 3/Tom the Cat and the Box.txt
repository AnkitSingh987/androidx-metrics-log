Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Cat and the Box

One day, a cat named Tom saw a ball of yarn. The yarn was red, blue, and yellow. Tom thought the yarn was crazy. He wanted to play with it. Tom said, "Yarn, you are so crazy, but I like you." Tom started to play with the yarn. He rolled it and chased it. He was having so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The yarn began to move by itself! Tom was surprised. The yarn rolled away from Tom. It rolled into a big box. Tom looked inside the box and saw a little girl. The girl was playing with a toy that made the yarn move. Tom said, "You made the yarn move! Let's play together." The girl and Tom played with the yarn and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a purple bird. The bird liked to fly and sing. One day, the bird found a long measure. The measure was very pretty and shiny. The bird had an idea. It wanted to attach the measure to its nest. The bird thought it would make the nest look nice. So, the bird picked up the measure with its beak and flew back to the nest. When the bird got to the nest, it tried to attach the measure. It was not easy, but the bird did not give up. After a while, the bird attached the measure to the nest. The nest looked very nice with the shiny measure. The purple bird was happy and sang a happy song.

One day, a smart dog named Max saw pasta on the table. He wanted to eat it. Max stared at the pasta, thinking of a way to get it. Max asked his friend, a cat named Lily, "How can I get the pasta?" Lily said, "Jump up and grab it!" Max tried to jump but he was too small. Just then, a bird flew in through the window. The bird picked up the pasta and flew away. Max and Lily stared at the bird, surprised. They did not get to eat the pasta, but they laughed and played together.

Sara was eager to see her aunt. Her aunt lived far away and only came to visit once a year. Sara liked her aunt because she was kind and funny and always brought her a gift. Today was the day that her aunt would arrive. Sara woke up early and ran to the window. She saw the sun rise in the sky and make everything bright and warm. She hoped her aunt would come soon. She heard a knock on the door and ran to open it. There she saw her aunt, smiling and holding a big bag. Sara hugged her aunt and said, "Hello, aunt! I missed you so much!" "Hello, Sara! I missed you too!" her aunt said. "I have something for you in my bag. Do you want to see it?" "Yes, please!" Sara said. She was curious and excited. She followed her aunt to the living room and watched her open the bag. Inside, there was a beautiful doll with long hair and a pink dress. Sara gasped and clapped her hands. "Wow! She is so pretty! Thank you, aunt!" Sara said. She took the doll and hugged it. She felt happy and loved. "You're welcome, Sara. I'm glad you like it. She is your new friend. You can play with her and dress her and comb her hair. What will you name her?" her aunt asked. Sara thought for a moment and then said, "I will name her Rose. Like the flower and like the sun when it rises." Her aunt smiled and said, "That's a lovely name, Sara. Rose is lucky to have you as her owner. And I'm lucky to have you as my niece. I love you, Sara." "I love you too, aunt," Sara said. She kissed her aunt and then ran to her room to play with Rose. She was eager to show her new friend to her mom and dad and to have fun with her aunt. She knew it was going to be a wonderful day.

Once upon a time, there was a cook named Bob. Bob had a big box. He did not know what was in the box. It was unknown to him. One day, a friend came to visit Bob. "Hi, Bob! What's in the box?" asked the friend. Bob said, "I don't know. It is unknown to me." Bob and his friend had an idea. They took the box to a doctor. The doctor used an x-ray to see inside the box. The x-ray showed a picture of a cat! Now, Bob knew what was in the box. Bob opened the box and the cat came out. The cat was happy to be free. Bob and his friend were happy too. They all played together and had a fun day. The end.
