Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Ball

One day, a little girl named Lily found a magic ball in her toy drawer. The ball was small and hairy. Lily was very happy to find it. She picked up the ball and said, "Hello, hairy ball. What can you do?" The ball started to talk! It said, "I can make things shrink. All you have to do is say 'shrink' and point at what you want to make small." Lily was very excited. She pointed at her big chair and said, "Shrink!" The chair became very small. Lily laughed and clapped her hands. She had fun playing with the magic ball all day.

Once upon a time, there was a big, thick tree in a small forest. The tree had many strong branches that reached up to the sky. Many little birds lived in the tree and they liked to sing. One sunny day, a little boy named Tim came to the forest. He saw the thick tree and wanted to climb it. Tim put his hands on the tree trunk and started to climb. As he went up, he saw a little bird on a branch. The bird had a pointy beak and was very pretty. Tim reached the point where the bird was sitting. He started to talk to the bird. They became friends and played together in the tree. After a while, it was time for Tim to go home. He said goodbye to his new friend and climbed down. Tim was happy and couldn't wait to come back and play with the bird again.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a fancy chair in her room. It was a special chair. It was big and had pretty colors. Lily wanted to sit on the chair, but it was dirty. So, she decided to wash the chair. Lily took a cloth and some water to clean the chair. She washed it until it was nice and shiny. Her mom came into the room and saw the chair. "Wow, Lily, what a pretty chair!" her mom said. "Did you wash it all by yourself?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, Mom, I did!" Now, Lily could sit on the fancy chair. She loved to read books and play with her toys on it. She was very proud of herself for washing the chair. And every time her friends came over, they all wanted to sit on the fancy chair too.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. Tom had a big red ball. He loved to play with his ball, but he had a problem. His ball had a small hole in it, so it was not fun to play with anymore. Tom went to his friend, Sue, who was good at fixing things. "Can you help me fix my ball?" Tom asked. Sue looked at the hole and said, "I can fix it, but I need a thread." Tom and Sue went to the store to buy a thread. At the store, they found a big box of threads. Tom wanted the blue thread, but Sue insisted on the green one. "No, the green one is better," she said. Tom and Sue argued for a while, but finally, Tom agreed to use the green thread. Sue fixed the hole in Tom's ball with the green thread, and they played together happily. Tom learned that sometimes it is good to listen to friends, even if you do not agree at first.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom loved to play in the rain. One day, it was wet outside, and Tom put on his boots and raincoat. He ran outside to play in the puddles. As Tom was playing, he saw a cat. The cat was wet too. Tom said, "Hi, cat! Do you like the rain?" The cat did not answer. Tom guessed that the cat did not like to be wet. So, he took off his raincoat and put it around the cat's waist. Now the cat was dry and happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The cat started to talk! The cat said, "Thank you, Tom, for making me dry. I am a magic cat, and now I will grant you one wish." Tom was so surprised! He thought for a moment and then wished for a big, warm hug. The magic cat hugged Tom, and they both felt happy and loved.
