Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben Find the Dog

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play together. They have a job. Their job is to feed the ducks at the pond. They have a bag of bread for the ducks. One day, they go to the pond. But they see a big dog. The dog is barking. The dog is running. The dog wants the bread. Anna and Ben are scared. They are uncomfortable. Anna says, "Let's go away. The dog is bad. The dog will take our bread. The dog will hurt us." Ben says, "No, let's stay. The dog is not bad. The dog is hungry. The dog needs bread. We can share our bread. The ducks have enough bread." Anna and Ben look at each other. They do not agree. They do not know what to do. A man comes. The man is the dog's owner. The man says, "I'm sorry. My dog is friendly. He likes bread. He likes ducks. He likes children. He will not hurt you. Can he have some bread?" Anna and Ben nod. They give some bread to the man. The man gives some bread to the dog. The dog stops barking. The dog wags his tail. The dog licks the man. The dog licks Anna and Ben. Anna and Ben smile. They are not scared. They are not uncomfortable. They are happy. They have a new friend. They share their bread with the dog and the ducks. They finish their job. They go home.

Once upon a time, a girl named Sue found a robot. The robot was big and smooth. It was in the park, near the slide. Sue was so happy to see the robot. She wanted to play with it all day. One day, Sue had to leave the robot at home. She went to the store with her mom. When she came back, she saw the robot was gone. Sue was very sad. She looked for the robot everywhere. Then, something unexpected happened. Sue found the robot in her room! The robot was wearing a hat and a big smile. It had learned to play hide and seek. Sue laughed and played with the robot all day long. They had so much fun together, and Sue was happy her robot friend could play games too.

Lily loved to read books about animals. She wanted to be a veterinarian when she grew up. She liked to learn how to help sick and hurt animals. One day, she found a book about a tough dog named Max. Max was a police dog who helped catch bad people. He was brave and smart and loyal. Lily read the book with her mom. "Wow, Max is a hero!" Lily said. "He saved his partner from a robber. He got a medal and a treat." "Yes, he is," Mom said. "But he also got hurt. He had a bullet in his leg. He needed a veterinarian to fix him." "Did the veterinarian help him?" Lily asked. "Of course," Mom said. "The veterinarian was very kind and gentle. She gave Max medicine and stitches. She made him feel better. She was a hero too." Lily smiled. She wanted to be like the veterinarian. She wanted to help animals like Max. She hugged the book and said, "Maybe I can be a hero too."

Ben and Lily were friends who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw some big kids having a race on their bikes. Ben and Lily wanted to join the race, but they did not have bikes. They only had scooters. "Can we race with you?" Ben asked the big kids. "No, you are too small and slow. Scooters are for babies. Go away!" one of the big kids said. He was mean and rude. Ben and Lily felt sad and angry. They did not like the big kids. They wanted to show them that they were not babies. They had an idea. "Let's observe them and see where they go. Maybe we can find a shortcut and beat them to the finish line," Lily whispered to Ben. "OK, let's do that. But we have to be careful and quiet. And we have to be obedient and listen to the signs and lights," Ben whispered back. They followed the big kids from a distance, and saw that they went around a long and busy road. Ben and Lily knew a better way. They went through a small and quiet path that led to the park. They scooted as fast as they could, and reached the finish line before the big kids. "Wow, we did it! We won the race!" Ben and Lily cheered. The big kids were shocked and confused. They did not see Ben and Lily on the road. They did not know how they got there so fast. "How did you do that? That's not fair! You cheated!" one of the big kids said. "We did not cheat. We observed you and found a shortcut. We were smart and brave. And we were obedient and safe. You were mean and rude. And you were slow and silly," Lily said. Ben and Lily smiled and high-fived each other. They were proud and happy. They did not care what the big kids said. They knew they were not babies. They were racers.
