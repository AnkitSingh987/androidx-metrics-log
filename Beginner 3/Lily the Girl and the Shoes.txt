Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Shoes

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had many shoes. One day, she could not find her favorite shoe. Lily was troubled. Lily decided to gather all her shoes in one place. She looked under her bed, behind the door, and in her toy box. She found many shoes, but not her favorite one. Finally, Lily saw her favorite shoe in the garden. She was so happy! She put on her favorite shoe and went to play with her friends. And they all had a fun day together.

---

Tom and Mia are playing in the garden. They have a toy gun and a bottle of juice. They pretend to be spies and hide behind the bushes. "Look, there is the bad man!" Tom says, pointing at a bird on the fence. "He wants to steal our juice. We have to shoot him!" Mia nods and aims the toy gun at the bird. She pulls the trigger and makes a loud noise. "Bang! Bang! Bang!" The bird flies away, scared. "Yay, we did it!" Tom cheers. "We saved our juice. It is very good for us. It makes us healthy and strong." He opens the bottle and pours some juice into two cups. He gives one to Mia and keeps one for himself. They drink and smile. "This is not juice, this is wine!" Mia says, making a funny face. "It is sour and bitter. I don't like it." Tom makes a funny face too. He spits out the juice and throws the cup away. "Yuck, you are right. This is wine. It is bad for us. It makes us sick and weak." He looks at the bottle and sees a label. It says "Wine. Do not drink. For grown-ups only." "Oops, we made a mistake. This is not our juice. This is Mommy's wine. She likes to drink it sometimes. We should not touch it." He puts the bottle back where he found it and hopes Mommy will not notice. He and Mia run away and look for something else to play with. They decide to never drink wine again.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Lily. She had a favorite shirt that was blue and had a big heart on it. She loved to wear it every day because it made her feel happy. One day, Lily went to play with her friend, Sam. They wanted to play a game that would include both of them. They thought and thought, and then Sam said, "Let's play dress-up!" Lily liked that idea, so they went to find more clothes to wear. They found funny hats, big shoes, and colorful dresses. Lily put on her favorite shirt and a silly hat. Sam put on a long dress and big shoes. Then they laughed and danced together, having lots of fun in their dress-up game.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park with her mom. Mia saw a big pond with ducks and frogs. Mia wanted to swim in the pond, but her mom said, "No, Mia. It's not safe to swim here." Mia was sad but she played with her ball near the pond. Then, a wild duck came out of the water. The duck was friendly and wanted to play with Mia. They played with the ball together and had lots of fun. Mia liked the duck a lot. Later, it was time to go home. Mia wanted to say goodbye to her new friend. She gave the wild duck a big hug. The duck quacked happily. Mia's mom said, "We can come back and play with the duck again another day." Mia smiled and knew she would see her friend again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a big farm. On the farm, there was a lot of corn. The corn was tall and yellow. Many animals lived on the farm, like cows, pigs, and chickens. One day, a small bird came to the farm. The bird saw the corn and wanted to eat it. The bird asked the cow, "Does this corn belong to you?" The cow said, "No, it does not belong to me. Ask the pig." The bird went to the pig and asked, "Does this corn belong to you?" The pig said, "No, it does not belong to me. Ask the chicken." The bird was patient and went to the chicken. The bird asked, "Does this corn belong to you?" The chicken said, "Yes, it belongs to all of us. You can have some too." The bird was happy and ate some corn. The bird became friends with the cow, the pig, and the chicken. They all shared the corn and lived happily on the farm.
