Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Drum

One day, a boy named Tim had a wide drum. He loved to hit the drum and make loud sounds. He played the drum all day, every day. Tim's mom and dad liked the drum too, but sometimes they wanted it to be quiet. One day, Tim found a big sack in his room. He did not know what was inside. He thought, "Maybe I can put my drum in the sack to make it quiet." So, he put the drum in the sack and closed it. Then, something unexpected happened. When Tim hit the sack, it made a new sound, like a big bell. Tim was so happy. He played the sack all day and made the new sound. His mom and dad liked the new sound too. They all had fun with the sack and the drum.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big, ugly tree. This tree was not like the other trees. It had no leaves or flowers. It was all alone. One day, a stranger came to the town. He saw the ugly tree and felt sad. He thought, "I want to make this tree happy." So, the stranger took a small, shiny stone and put it on the tree. The next day, the sun came out and it was very bright. The sun made the stone shine on the ugly tree. The people in the town saw the shine and came to see the tree. They all smiled and said, "Wow, this tree is not ugly anymore. It is special because of the shiny stone!" From that day on, the ugly tree was not alone. The people in the town loved the tree and the stranger who made it shine. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely bird named Bob. Bob lived in a small jail near the woods. He was sad because he had no friends. One day, a little girl named Sue came to the jail. She saw Bob and said, "Hi, bird! I am Sue. Do you want to be my friend?" Bob nodded his head, "Yes, I want to be your friend." Sue opened the jail door and let Bob out. They played together and had lots of fun. Now, Bob was not lonely anymore. He had a new friend, Sue, and they were happy together.

Once there was a car who was in need of help. He was feeling very sick and was not able to move. So the car's owner went to an honest mechanic to help. The mechanic examined the car very carefully, looking at all the parts. He saw that the car needed some new parts and he fixed them. The car was so happy to be feeling better. He thanked the mechanic for his help and asked him how much he owed. The mechanic said his work was free, as long as the car kept taking good care of himself. The car was thrilled and thanked the mechanic again. From then on, the car takes very good care of himself and visits the honest mechanic every now and then. The mechanic always gives him good advice so he can keep running smoothly. The car really appreciates the honest mechanic for his help.

Anna and Ben are playing in the park. They like to swing, slide and climb on the equipment. Anna has a pink hat that she loves very much. She thinks it makes her look pretty. "Look at my hat, Ben!" Anna says. "It is pink and soft and has a flower on it. Do you like it?" Ben does not like Anna's hat. He thinks it is silly and girly. He wants to play with his truck and his ball. He does not care about hats. "Your hat is dumb, Anna!" Ben says. "It is not for playing. It is for babies. Take it off!" Anna feels sad and angry. She likes her hat. She does not want to take it off. She thinks Ben is mean and rude. "No, Ben!" Anna says. "My hat is not dumb. It is nice and fun. You are dumb. You are not my friend!" Anna is so mad that she slaps Ben on the face. Ben is shocked and hurt. He feels a sting on his cheek. He starts to cry. "Ow, Anna!" Ben says. "Why did you slap me? That hurts!" A grown-up sees what happened. She comes over to Anna and Ben. She is not happy. She scolds them both. "Anna, Ben, what are you doing?" she says. "You should not slap each other. That is not nice. That is not how you play. You should say sorry and be kind." Anna and Ben feel sorry. They know they did wrong. They look at each other. They say sorry. "Sorry, Anna," Ben says. "I should not have said your hat is dumb. It is your hat. You can wear it if you want." "Sorry, Ben," Anna says. "I should not have slapped you. That was mean. It does not matter if you like my hat or not. You are still my friend." Anna and Ben hug each other. They feel better. They decide to play together. They share their toys and their equipment. They have fun. They forget about the hat and the slap. They are happy.
