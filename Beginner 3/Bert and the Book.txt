Category: Beginner
Title: Bert and the Book

Bert was a jolly three year old boy. He loved to explore and ask questions. One day, he asked his mom if he could go explore the forest. His mom said, "Yes, but you must stay close. You must permit me to come with you or Auntie Jane." Bert said he would and set off for the forest. When he arrived, he saw all sorts of interesting things. He heard birds chirping, saw beautiful flowers, and even stumbled upon a tiny house! Curious, he peeped inside. Inside he found a spell book with mysterious letters on it. He opened the book and saw three words: "Cheerful, jolly, and fun!" Bert thought it was so fun to read the words. He read them over and over until he knew them by heart. Suddenly, the spell book started to glow. Bert wanted to know what would happen next. He read the words aloud this time, and a huge bubble filled with colorful floating objects appeared. Bert was so excited! He bounced up and down in joy and shouted, "Thank you so much!" The spell book opened one more time and let out a jolly boisterous laugh. Bert knew he could explore in the forest thanks to this magical spell. He thanked the book and left the forest with a smile on his face.

One day, a girl named Mia went to the park. She saw a big loop on the ground. Mia wanted to play with the loop. She picked it up and started to play. Mia liked to make the loop go round and round. It was fun. Mia saw a boy named Tom. Tom had a spicy snack. Mia wanted to try it. Tom said, "Be careful, it's spicy!" Mia took a bite and her mouth felt hot. She did not like the spicy snack. Mia ran to get a drink of water. As Mia was drinking water, she saw the loop move. It was not just a loop, it was a snake! The snake was not mean, it just wanted to play. Mia and Tom were scared at first, but then they laughed. They all played together and had a great day at the park.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little mouse named Max. Max had a rough day, and he was very sad. He wanted to find his way home, but he was lost. As Max walked, he saw a big trap. He was scared, but he knew he must be brave. Max thought, "I can't quit. I must keep going." So Max jumped over the trap and kept walking. After a while, Max found his way home. He was so happy to see his family. They all hugged and played together. Max learned that even when things are rough, he should never quit. And Max never saw the big trap again.

One day, in a big classroom, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very proud of his new toy. It was a small red car. He loved to play with it and show it to his friends. During play time, Tim's friend, Sam, came over to see the red car. Sam said, "Wow, Tim! Your car is so cool!" Tim was very happy to hear this. But then, something unexpected happened. A little dog ran into the classroom. The little dog saw Tim's red car and wanted to play too. The dog ran up and bit the car. Tim and Sam were very surprised. They didn't know what to do. Tim said, "Sam, let's show the dog how to play nice with the car." They taught the dog to play without biting. Soon, they all played together and had a lot of fun. Tim was proud that he could share his car and make a new friend.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a jog. He jogged down the street and saw his friend, Sue. Sue was going on a tour with her mom. They were going to see big, dark caves. Tim thought that sounded fun. "Can I go on the tour with you?" Tim asked Sue. Sue smiled and said, "Yes, you can come with us!" So Tim, Sue, and Sue's mom went to the dark caves. They walked slowly and carefully. They saw bats and pretty rocks. Tim and Sue held hands so they wouldn't get lost. They liked the tour a lot. When they finished the tour, they went back home. Tim said, "Thank you for letting me come with you!" Sue said, "You're welcome! We had fun together." Tim and Sue were happy and tired from their big day.
