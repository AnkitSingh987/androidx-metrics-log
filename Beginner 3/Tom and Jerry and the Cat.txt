Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Jerry and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a cat named Tom and a dog named Jerry. They lived in a big house with their best friend, a little girl named Lily. Tom and Jerry did not trust each other at first. They would fight and chase each other all day long. Lily did not like this, so she tried to help them become friends. One day, Lily had a big party at her house. It was for a marriage of her aunt and uncle. There were lots of people, yummy food, and fun games. Lily asked Tom and Jerry to help her at the party. They had to work together to carry a big, heavy tray of spicy food. It was hard, but they did it! After the party, Tom and Jerry started to trust each other more. They stopped fighting and became good friends. They played games and shared toys with each other. Lily was very happy to see them getting along. From that day on, Tom, Jerry, and Lily were the best of friends and had lots of fun adventures together.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a gray board. It was in a big box. He wanted to open the box to see the board. He asked his mom, "Can I open the box, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Tim opened the box and took out the gray board. He put it on the floor. The board had a picture of a cat on it. Tim liked the cat. He played with the board all day. He put his toys on it and moved them around. But, the sky outside started to get dark. It was going to rain. Tim did not know that the board did not like water. When the rain came, it fell on the gray board. The board got wet and the cat picture went away. Tim was very sad. He wished he had put the board away before the rain came.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Mia had a big, fierce dog named Max. Max loved to eat, and Mia loved to feed him. One day, Mia found some junk in her yard. She thought Max might like to eat it. Mia said, "Max, do you want to eat this junk?" Max wagged his tail and barked, "Woof!" Mia gave the junk to Max, but he did not like it. He spat it out and made a funny face. Mia's mommy came outside and saw the junk. She said, "Mia, we don't feed junk to Max. It's not good for him." Mia felt sad and said, "I'm sorry, mommy." From that day on, Mia only fed Max good food, and they lived happily together.

Mikey was walking down the street and stopped to take a rest. He saw a stranger coming a long and waved to them. The stranger didn't wave back. Mikey felt hurt. He decided to keep walking, but became more and more confused. He soon saw the stranger again, but the stranger didn't stop. Mikey shouted out, "Hello! Why don't you STOP?" The stranger stopped and said, "I'm very sorry. I didn't mean to make you feel so sad. I should have waved back." Mikey smiled and said, "That's ok. I don't regret shouting out to you." The stranger smiled and waved goodbye as they walked away. Mikey waved back and continued his journey, not feeling hurt anymore.

One day, a little boy named Tim was in his bed. He was sad and started to cry. His mom came into his room and asked, "Why are you crying, Tim?" Tim said, "I don't want to be bald like Dad. I want hair!" His mom tried to make him feel better, but Tim was still sad. The next day, Tim met a new friend at the park. His friend was also bald. They played together and had fun. Tim saw that being bald was not so bad. He stopped crying and was happy again.

Tom and Lily were playing in the yard when they saw a big brown truck. The truck had a sign that said "mail". They ran to the fence and waved at the driver. "Hello, mail man!" Tom shouted. "Do you have a letter for us?" The driver smiled and stopped the truck. He got out and opened the back door. He took out a big envelope and handed it to Tom. "This is for you, Tom. It's from your grandma. She lives far away, but she loves you very much." Tom was very happy. He thanked the driver and ran back to Lily. He opened the envelope and found a card with a picture of a cake and some stickers. "Look, Lily! Grandma sent me a card for my birthday! And she gave me stickers too! Do you want some?" Lily nodded and smiled. She liked stickers. Tom shared his stickers with Lily and they stuck them on their shirts and faces. They were having fun when they heard a loud boom. They looked up and saw dark clouds and bright flashes of lightning. They were scared. "Let's go inside, Tom. It's going to rain and thunder. I don't like lightning. It's too noisy and scary." Tom agreed. He grabbed his card and envelope and followed Lily to the house. They ran inside and closed the door. They hugged their mom and showed her the card and stickers. "Wow, what a nice surprise from grandma!" Mom said. "And what lovely stickers you have! You are very lucky, Tom. Happy birthday!" Tom and Lily felt safe and happy. They loved their mom and grandma. They didn't mind the lightning anymore. They had mail.
