Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the House

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a polite boy who liked to help his mom. One day, his mom asked him to decorate the house for a party. Tim was happy to help. He put up pretty things all around the house. He used a lot of touch to make it look nice. He wanted his mom to be happy. But, Tim made a big mess. He put too much touch on the walls. The walls were sticky and dirty. The house looked bad. Tim's mom was sad, and the party was not fun.

Once upon a time, there was a fierce crab. The crab lived in the sea. It had big claws and would kick the sand. The crab liked to play with the fish. One day, the crab met a big fish. The fish was not nice. The fish wanted to eat the crab. The crab was scared. The crab kicked the sand at the fish. The fish did not like it. But then, something unexpected happened. The fish started to laugh. The fish said, "I was only joking! I won't eat you!" The crab was happy. The fierce crab and the big fish became friends. They played together in the sea every day.

Anna was a little girl who liked to play with her toys. She had a doll, a bear, a car and a cane. The cane was her grandpa's, but he let her use it sometimes. She liked to pretend she was a doctor or a teacher with the cane. One day, Anna was playing with her cane in the living room. She saw her grandpa sitting on the couch, reading a book. She wanted to show him her cane. She walked over to him and said, "Look, grandpa, I have your cane!" Grandpa looked up and smiled. He said, "Yes, you do, Anna. Do you like it?" Anna nodded. She said, "Yes, I do. It is fun. I can make noises with it." She tapped the cane on the floor and made a clack-clack sound. Grandpa laughed. He said, "That's right, you can. But be careful, don't hit anything or anyone with it. It might hurt." Anna said, "OK, grandpa, I won't. Can I give you a hug?" Grandpa said, "Of course, you can, Anna. I love your hugs." Anna put the cane down and climbed on the couch. She wrapped her arms around grandpa's neck and squeezed him tight. She said, "I love you, grandpa." Grandpa said, "I love you, too, Anna. You are my sweet girl." Anna felt happy and warm. She kissed grandpa's cheek and said, "You are my best grandpa." Grandpa said, "And you are my best Anna." They hugged and smiled. They were very happy. But then, they heard a loud noise. It was the cane. It had fallen off the couch and hit a vase. The vase broke and shattered on the floor. Anna and grandpa looked at the mess. They were confused. They did not know what to do. They said, "Uh-oh."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Mia. She had just turned three and wanted a new clock for her bedroom. Mia went to the store with her mommy. “Look, Mia,” said her mommy, “Here's a nice, cheap clock for you.” Mia liked the clock and wanted to buy it. But she didn’t have any money. “Mommy, can I gain the money to buy it?” asked Mia. Her mommy smiled and answered, “Yes, of course. You can save your pocket money and buy it yourself.” Mia was excited and started to save. She kept putting her pocket money in a box. After some months, Mia had enough money to buy the clock. Mia was very happy and ran back to the store to buy her lovely, cheap clock!

Once upon a time, in a far away land, there was a creative little monkey named Momo. Momo loved to hang from the trees and play all day. One day, Momo saw a big, red ball in the sky. Momo called out to his friend, Toto the bird, "Toto, look up! Big red ball in sky! What is it?" Toto looked up and said, "Momo, that is a big red balloon! Let's go get it!" Momo and Toto tried to get the big red balloon. Momo jumped high to grab it, but he missed. He fell down and landed on the ground. Toto tried to fly up and hold the balloon, but it was too big for him. Toto got tired and fell down too. In the end, Momo and Toto could not get the big red balloon. They were both sad and hurt. They learned that some things are just too big and high for them to reach.
