Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Apple and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who liked to explore. One day, she came across an old tree with apples growing on it. She wanted to reach for one of the apples, but it was too high. She thought to herself, "I wonder if I can get to the apple?" She jumped as high as she could, but she couldn't reach it. She jumped again and again, but still couldn't get it. Suddenly, the tree began to shake and the apple fell to the ground! The little girl smiled and ran over to it. She was so excited to eat the yummy apple! But when she picked it up, she saw something beneath it. It was a dangerous snake! The little girl was scared and took a step back, but the snake didn't move. It just sat there, not doing anything. The little girl slowly moved closer to it and held out her hand. Miraculously, the snake yielded and let her pick it up. She was amazed! In her hands, the snake was only a few inches long, but she knew how dangerous it could be. The little girl put the snake back and ran away, happy that she found something to explore.

Once upon a time, there lived a 3 year old girl who had a very interesting sheet. She loved her sheet and could spend hours playing with it. One day, she was having a lot of fun with it when suddenly she heard a noise. The noise disturbed her and made her feel scared. She looked around, trying to figure out what caused it. "Who's there?" she asked. A loud laugh answered her from behind the sheet. It was her 4 year old brother! He was playing a prank on her. "It's just me, silly," he said. The 3 year old started laughing, relieved that her brother was behind the noise. "Don't disturb me when I'm playing with my sheet," she said, laughing. Her brother decided to never scare her like that again. The sister and brother kept playing together and had a lot of fun with the interesting sheet!

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to watch a film. He asked his mom, "Can I watch a film?" His mom said, "Yes, you can watch a regular film for kids." Tim started to watch the film. He saw a big dog on the screen. The dog could move very fast. Tim liked the dog and wanted to play with it. He wished the dog could come out of the film. Just then, something unexpected happened. The dog jumped out of the screen and into Tim's room! Tim was very surprised. He said, "Wow! You can move out of the film!" The dog and Tim played together. They had a lot of fun. After a while, the dog went back into the film. Tim was happy he got to play with the dog from the film.

Once upon a time, there was a happy little girl named Sue. She loved to play outside with her big red ball. One day, she saw a small boot in the mud. Sue wanted to find the owner of the boot. She asked her friends, the birds and the bugs, if they knew who the boot belonged to. The birds and the bugs did not know. Sue was sad and started to weep. Just then, a small frog hopped by and saw Sue crying. The frog said, "That's my boot!" Sue gave the boot to the frog, and they both were happy. They played with the big red ball all day long.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a big, red ball. She loved to play with it. Mia would throw the ball and it would bounce back to her. She would repeat this over and over again. One day, while playing, she saw a note on the ground. The note had a harsh word on it. Mia did not like the harsh word. Mia showed the note to her mom. Her mom said, "We should find out who wrote this note." They went to the park and asked the other kids if they knew anything about the note. One boy named Tim said, "I think I saw someone write that note." Mia and her mom asked Tim to show them who wrote the note. Tim pointed at a big kid named Jack. Mia's mom talked to Jack and asked him why he wrote the harsh word. Jack looked sad and said, "I'm sorry. I was mad because I lost my toy car." Mia and her mom told Jack that it is not nice to use harsh words. Jack said he would not do it again. Then, Mia had an idea. She asked Jack, "Do you want to play with my big, red ball?" Jack smiled and said, "Yes, I would like that." Mia, Jack, and Tim played with the ball together. They all became good friends and forgot about the harsh word on the note.
