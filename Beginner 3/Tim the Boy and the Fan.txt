Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Fan

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big fan in his room. The fan kept him cool when it was hot. One day, Tim felt very uncomfortable because it was very hot. He wanted to turn on the fan but he couldn't reach the button. So, Tim asked his sister, Sue, for help. "Sue, can you please help me turn on the fan? I feel very hot," said Tim. Sue was tall and could reach the button. She pointed at the fan and said, "Sure, Tim! I can help you." Sue turned on the fan and the room became cool. Tim felt better and thanked Sue. The moral of the story is that it is good to ask for help when you need it. It is also nice to help others when they ask.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy. He was very weak and always forgot things. One day, his mom took him to the lab. She told him that he would find something special there. He was scared at first, but he knew his mom was there and so he was brave. When they arrived at the lab, he saw lots of strange things. The boy was unsure, but he remembered what his mom had said. He looked around, and then he saw it - a big box full of shiny stars! The little boy was so excited. He picked out a huge red star and hugged it tight. His mom smiled and said he could remember this moment forever. He was so happy, he kept the star with him always. The boy never forgot that day in the lab. He found something special and he was stronger after that. The little boy always remembered that big, red star.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park. She had a basket with her. In the basket, there were toys and snacks. Sue was very happy. At the park, Sue saw her friend Tom. Tom was playing with a toy. Tom asked, "Sue, can I see what's in your basket?" Sue said, "Yes, you can look." Tom saw the snacks and smiled. They both sat down and shared the snacks. After eating, they played with a twisty toy. They twisted it this way and that way. Sue said, "I like my basket. It is reliable. It holds my toys and snacks." Tom agreed, "Yes, your basket is very good." They played until it was time to go home. Sue and Tom were happy they had a great day at the park.

A little girl and her daddy were walking in the park. The little girl looked around and saw a mud puddle. Daddy saw it too. "Let's go over and do some pulling!" he said. The little girl was excited. She ran over to the mud puddle and looked down. "Mud!" she said. Daddy came to stand beside her. He bent over and picked up a little stick. He then bent down and put the stick into the mud. "I'll pull. You put the mud on the stick," he said. The little girl smiled. She scooped up some of the mud and put it on the stick. Daddy then pulled, and the mud came off the stick. The little girl and her daddy played together in the mud for a long time. They had lots of fun!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was a fair girl with a big heart. One day, she found a magic plate in her room. The plate was small, but it was very special. Mia showed the magic plate to her friend, Tom. She said, "Look, Tom! I found a magic plate!" Tom was very excited. He asked, "What can it do?" Mia smiled and said, "Watch!" She put her toy car on the plate, and it began to shrink. The car became very, very small. Tom and Mia laughed and clapped their hands. Mia and Tom played with the magic plate all day. They made their toys shrink and then grow big again. They had so much fun together. At the end of the day, they put the magic plate away. They knew they would play with it again soon.
