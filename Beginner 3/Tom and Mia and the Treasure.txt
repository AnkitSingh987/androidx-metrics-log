Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Mia and the Treasure

One day, Tom and Mia were playing with their colorful toys. They had a big ship, a small boat, some dolls and some animals. They pretended to be pirates and sailed on the water. "Ahoy, matey!" Tom said. "I am the captain of this ship. You are my crew. We are looking for treasure!" "Aye, aye, captain!" Mia said. "I have a map. It shows us where the treasure is. It is on an island with a palm tree and a star." They moved their ship and boat closer to the island. They saw a shiny box under the palm tree. They jumped out of their toys and ran to the box. "Wow, look at this!" Tom said. "It is full of gold and jewels and candy. This is the best treasure ever!" "Yay, we found it!" Mia said. "We are the best pirates ever!" But then, they heard a loud roar. It was a big, scary dragon. It had red scales, yellow eyes and sharp teeth. It flew over the island and saw the treasure. "Mine, mine, mine!" the dragon said. "I will take this treasure. It is mine!" The dragon swooped down and grabbed the box with its claws. It flew away with the treasure. "Hey, stop!" Tom said. "That is our treasure. You can't take it. That is not fair!" "Help, help!" Mia said. "The dragon took our treasure. What can we do?" Tom and Mia were very sad and scared. They did not know how to get their treasure back. They hugged each other and cried. They wished they had a brave hero to save them.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw a big tree with red apples. He wanted to pick one for his mom. He tried to reach the apples with his arm, but it was too short. Tim was sad. A tall, generous man named Mr. Brown saw Tim. He asked, "Do you need help, little boy?" Tim said, "Yes, please. I want to pick an apple for my mom." Mr. Brown smiled and used his long arm to pick an apple for Tim. Tim was very happy and said, "Thank you, Mr. Brown!" He gave the apple to his mom. She was proud of Tim for being kind and thankful. They all became good friends and played in the park together.

Once upon a time there was a colorful bird who liked to fly. His favorite thing to do was soar through the sky and enjoy the sun. One day, he was happily flying when he saw something he had never seen before. Something new and bright. He wanted to get closer, to touch it. So he flew towards it, getting closer and closer. But when he was close enough, he noticed it was too big for him to touch. He tried and tried but he just couldn't. It seemed like it wanted to stay away from him. He was sad, but he had an idea. He started to flutter around the big thing, trying to see if he could get close. But every time he would get close, it would move away. Finally though, he was able to get close enough to touch it. When he did, he was so excited! It was a beautiful, colorful butterfly! He knew it was worth the effort, and he enjoyed the feeling of touching it.

Once upon a time, there was a big purple cat named Tom. Tom was very proud of his purple fur. He loved to show it off to all his friends. One day, he saw a big tree and thought it would be fun to climb it. As Tom climbed the tree, he saw a little bird. The bird said, "Be careful, Tom! You might regret going too high!" But Tom did not listen. He wanted to be on top of the tree so everyone could see his purple fur. Soon, Tom reached the top of the tree. He felt very proud. But then, he looked down and saw how high he was. He felt scared. Tom tried to climb down, but he was stuck. He now regretted not listening to the little bird. His friends heard his cries and came to help. They got a long rope and helped Tom get back down. Tom was happy to be safe and promised to listen to his friends next time.
