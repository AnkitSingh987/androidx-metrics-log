Category: Beginner
Title: Fin the Smart Fish and the Lily

Once upon a time, there was a smart fish named Fin. Fin loved to splash in the water near the shore. One sunny day, while Fin was splashing, he saw a girl named Lily. Lily was playing in the sand near the water. "Hi, Lily!" said Fin, as he splashed water on her. "Do you want to play with me?" Lily looked at Fin and smiled. "Yes, I'd love to play with you, Fin!" she said. So, they played and splashed in the water together, having lots of fun. As they played, a big wave came and carried Lily far away from the shore. Fin was scared, but he knew he had to help Lily. He splashed and swam as fast as he could, and with a big splash, he pushed Lily back to the shore. Lily was safe, and they both laughed. From that day on, they were the best of friends.

Jim and Jessie were in their backyard playing together. Jim had a symbol in his hand. “Look, Jessie, what’s this?” Jim asked. Jessie looked and answered, “That’s a symbol!” Jim smiled and said, “Let’s go find more symbols!” So they both got up and went to the park. As they were walking, they looked for symbols along the way. They found a bright blue symbol on a tree trunk. Jim touched it gently and said, “Wow, it’s so pretty!” Jessie smiled and said, “Let’s go find more.” So they kept looking and soon they found more symbols. Jim and Jessie were so happy! When it was time to go home, they thanked each other for a fun day. They decided to always remember to find gentle symbols whenever they go out to play.

One day, a curious little boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They saw many cars on the street. Tim liked the cars and wanted to find a car that matched his toy car. His toy car was red and small. Tim saw a big red car and said, "Mom, that car looks like my toy car!" But his mom said, "No, Tim. That car is big, and your toy car is small." Tim was sad but kept looking for a car that matched his toy car. Finally, Tim saw a small red car parked near a store. He was very happy and said, "Mom, look! That car matches my toy car!" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. That car is small and red, just like your toy car." Tim felt proud that he found a car that matched his toy car, and they happily walked back home.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a girl named Mia. Mia had a big orange ball that she loved to play with. Every day, she would take her ball to the park and play with her friends. One day, Mia saw a boy named Sam at the park. Sam had a toy car that he liked to play with. Mia thought that the car looked fun and wanted to play with it too. So, Mia asked Sam if they could trade toys for a little while. Sam agreed, and they traded the orange ball for the toy car. Mia and Sam played together and had lots of fun. They decided to include more friends in their playtime. Soon, all the kids at the park were trading toys and playing together. They learned that sharing and trading made playtime even more fun. And Mia was happy that her orange ball helped everyone have a great time.

John and his mom were walking in the park. John looked around at all the sights and he saw a big yellow bird that was walking quickly. He couldn't help but smile at the bird. John's mom saw the bird too. "That looks like a duck doesn't it John?", she asked. John thought it looked like a duck but it was a bit different. He thought the duck had a yellow beak and a yellow belly. John asked his mom, "What kind of bird is it?" His mom thought for a moment and then she said, "It's a Seagull. They have long wings and big lungs and their big wings help them fly long distances." John thought that sounded cool. John wanted to see the Seagull fly and soon enough it took off and soared into the sky above him. He felt happy and amazed as he watched it fly away. John thought that Seagulls were really helpful.
