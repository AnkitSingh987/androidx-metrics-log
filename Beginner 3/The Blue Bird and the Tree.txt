Category: Beginner
Title: The Blue Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a blue bird. The blue bird lived in a big tree. The tree had rough bark. The blue bird liked to fly and play with its friends. One day, the blue bird saw a little girl. The little girl was sad. She lost her toy. The blue bird wanted to help the girl. So, the blue bird flew around to find the toy. The blue bird found the toy under a bush. It picked up the toy with its beak. The blue bird flew back to the little girl. The girl was so happy to see her toy. She gave the blue bird a big kiss. The blue bird and the girl became good friends. They played together every day.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a big yacht on the water. He wanted to go on the yacht, but he did not know how to swim. He asked his mom, "Can I go on the yacht?" His mom said, "No, Tim, it is too big for you. You need to learn to swim first." Tim felt sad, but he had an idea. He thought he could make a tiny boat out of paper. He made the boat and put it on the water. But, the paper boat was too small and it sank. Tim felt foolish for thinking a paper boat could take him to the yacht. Just then, a friendly duck swam by. The duck saw Tim's sad face and asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim told the duck about the yacht and the paper boat. The duck said, "I can help you. Hop on my back and I will take you to the yacht." Tim was so happy and climbed on the duck's back. The duck swam to the yacht and Tim got to see it up close. He thanked the duck and they became good friends. Tim learned that sometimes, help can come from unexpected places.

Timmy was walking with his mommy in the forest. He saw a cobweb in a tree. He asked his mommy, 'What is that?' She smiled and said, 'That is a cobweb.' Timmy said, 'Can I borrow it?' His mommy smiled and said, 'Yes, but be careful. It is weak and can easily break.' Timmy carefully reached out and he borrowed the cobweb. He held it in his hands and said, 'Thank you, Mommy.' He was amazed at how light and silky the cobweb was between his fingers. His mommy said, 'Be gentle with it. We have to give it back.' So Timmy took the cobweb home, and he kept it safe in a jar. Later, they went back to the forest, and they returned the cobweb back to its home in the tree. Timmy was so happy to have been able to borrow the cobweb, but he was also glad to give it back.

Once upon a time, there was a sailor. He had a big boat. The boat was expensive. The sailor loved his boat very much. He liked to sail on the water. One day, the sailor felt a sneeze coming. He tried to stop it, but he could not. "Achoo!" he sneezed. The sneeze was so big that it made his hat fly off his head. The hat fell into the water. The sailor was sad. He wanted his hat back. He tried to get the hat, but the water was too deep. So, he sailed back home without his hat. He told his friends about the sneeze and the hat. They all laughed and had fun. The sailor learned that it was okay to lose things, as long as he had his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely pilot named Tom. Tom loved to fly his big plane in the sky. The plane was red and shiny. Tom was very good at flying. One day, Tom needed to count how many people were on his plane. He started with one, two, three, and went all the way up to ten. There were ten people on the plane, all ready to go on a fun trip. Tom flew the plane up, up, up into the sky. The people on the plane were so happy to be flying with Tom. They all looked out the window and saw the pretty clouds. When they landed, everyone clapped for the lovely pilot. Tom smiled, happy to have helped his new friends.
