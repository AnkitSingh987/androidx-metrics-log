Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Cheerful Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful cat named Tom. Tom liked to play and run all day. One day, Tom saw a big red ball. He wanted to play with it. Tom jumped on the ball and gave it a rub. But the ball rolled away very fast. Tom ran after it, but he could not catch it. He felt sad and began to regret touching the ball. In the end, Tom learned that it is good to think before you do something. This made him a wiser and happier cat. And that is the story of the cheerful cat and the big red ball.

Anna likes to eat oat for breakfast. She likes to put pink berries on her oat. They make her oat sweet and pretty. But today, she sees something else on her oat. It is a big, green bug. Anna does not like bugs. She does not want to eat the bug. "Mommy, mommy, there is a bug on my oat!" Anna cries. She points to the bug. Mommy comes to see. She sees the bug too. She makes a face. "Yuck, that is a nasty bug. How did it get on your oat?" Mommy asks. She takes a spoon and tries to remove the bug. But the bug is fast. It jumps off the spoon and onto the table. It runs away. "Bye-bye, bug!" Anna says. She laughs. She is not scared of the bug anymore. She thinks it is funny. Mommy laughs too. She is glad Anna is happy. "Let's make you a new oat, okay? With more pink berries and no bugs," Mommy says. She throws away the old oat and gets a new bowl. She fills it with oat and pink berries. She gives it to Anna. "Thank you, Mommy. I love you and I love oat," Anna says. She smiles and eats her oat. She thinks it is the best oat ever.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a red ball that she loved to play with. One day, she went outside to play with her ball near the fence. Sue saw her friend Tom on the other side of the fence. Tom looked sad. Sue asked, "Why are you sad, Tom?" Tom said, "I lost my ball and I want to play." Sue wanted to help, so she said, "I can lend you my ball, Tom!" Tom was very happy and said, "Thank you, Sue!" They played with the ball, but the fence was uncomfortable to sit on. So, they took turns throwing the ball over the fence to each other. After a while, they got tired and sat down on the grass. They talked and laughed until it was time to go home. Sue and Tom were happy that they could play together even with the fence between them. And Sue learned that lending her ball to Tom made both of them happy.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw his friend, Sam, playing with a toy car. Tim had a toy car too, but it was hurt. Tim wanted to play with Sam's toy car. Tim said, "Sam, can we trade toy cars? Mine is hurt and yours is not." Sam thought about it and said, "Okay, Tim. We can trade, but you must mark my toy car so I know it is mine." Tim was happy. He marked Sam's toy car with a red dot. They traded toy cars and played together. But then, something unexpected happened. The hurt toy car started to move! The red dot Tim had put on the car fixed it! Tim and Sam were surprised and happy. They learned that sharing and helping each other can make things better. From that day on, they always shared and played together. And they knew that a little mark could make a big difference.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went for a walk. She wanted to find something fun to play with. As she walked, she saw a newspaper on the ground. Lucy picked it up and thought it would be fun to play with. Lucy walked to the park with her newspaper. She saw a big tree and decided to sit under it. She opened the newspaper and started to read. Suddenly, a loud noise came from behind her. It was a big dog barking! Lucy was scared at first, but the dog was friendly. The dog wanted to play with the newspaper too. Lucy threw the newspaper and the dog ran to get it. They played together for a long time. Lucy and the dog became good friends and they had a lot of fun with the newspaper.
