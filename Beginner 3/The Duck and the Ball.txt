Category: Beginner
Title: The Duck and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a duck. The duck lived near a big pond. The duck liked to play with its friends all day. One day, the duck was playing with his friend, a frog. They were playing with a ball. The duck kicked the ball too hard. The ball went into the pond and could not be found. The duck felt sorry. The duck and the frog looked for the ball but they could not find it. They were sad. Then, a big fish came and gave them the ball back. They were very happy. The duck said sorry to the frog. They played until the day end.

One sunny day, a boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. They saw the big blue sea and lots of waves. Tim wore a fancy hat to keep the sun off his face. Tim and his mom played near the water. They jumped over the waves as they came to the shore. Tim was having so much fun. He loved the beach and the waves. Later, it was time to go home. Tim and his mom shut the car doors and waved goodbye to the beach. They couldn't wait to come back and play with the waves again.

Once upon a time, there was a kind and compassionate bear named Ben. Ben lived in a big forest with many friends. Every morning, Ben would wake up early to help his friends with their tasks. One day, Ben saw a little bird named Billy who was sad. "Why are you sad, Billy?" Ben asked. Billy said, "I can't find my way home." Ben wanted to help Billy, so he thought of a plan. "I will make signs to show you the way," said Ben. Ben made signs with arrows pointing to Billy's home. Billy followed the signs and found his way back. Billy was very happy and said, "Thank you, Ben!" From that day on, Ben and Billy were best friends. The moral of the story is to always be compassionate and help others when they need it.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Ben. Ben loved to wear his red vest. One day, Ben felt worried. He could not find his vest anywhere. He looked all around his house, but it was not there. Ben went to his friend, Cat. He said, "Cat, have you seen my vest? I am worried." Cat said, "No, but let's look for it together." They looked all over the yard, but they could not find the vest. Ben was very sad. Then, a big wind came and blew the vest right to Ben's feet! Ben was so happy. He said, "Thank you, Cat, for helping me look for my vest. I will never quit being your friend." Cat smiled and said, "You're welcome, Ben. Friends never quit helping each other." And they both learned that when you are worried, it's good to have a friend who will not quit helping you.

One day, a pretty bird named Bella lived in a big tree. Bella loved to whistle. She would whistle all day long. Her friends liked to listen to her whistle. One day, Bella found a board near her tree. She had an idea. She put the board on the tree to make a stage. Bella wanted to whistle on the stage for her friends. She was very excited. Bella whistled on the stage, but no one came to listen. She felt sad. Then, a small bug came to Bella. The bug told her that her friends were helping a sick friend. Bella learned that helping others is more important than showing off. She joined her friends to help and they all whistled together after.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy dog named Max. He had a big heel that always hurt. Max lived in a small house with his friend, a little girl named Lily. One day, Max said to Lily, "My heel hurts so much. Please help me." Lily looked at Max's heel and saw that it was red and sore. She knew just what to do. She went to the kitchen and got some ice. She put the ice on Max's heel, and he started to feel better. As they sat together, Max began to demand a toy. He barked, "I want to play now!" Lily smiled and picked up a ball. She threw the ball, and Max ran after it, his heel feeling much better. They played all day, and Max was not grumpy anymore.
