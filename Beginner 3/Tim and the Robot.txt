Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Robot

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim found a big piece of metal in his yard. Tim thought it was very important. He wanted to cut it into a small shape, so he could keep it with him always. Tim asked his dad, "Dad, can you help me cut this metal? It is very important to me." His dad smiled and said, "Of course, Tim. Let's go to the garage and find some tools." In the garage, they found a tool that could cut metal. But as they started to cut, something unexpected happened. A tiny, friendly robot appeared from inside the metal! The robot said, "Thank you for setting me free! I was stuck in that metal for so long!" Tim and his dad were very surprised, but happy they could help. From that day on, Tim and the robot became best friends, and they had many fun adventures together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a big, scary dog named Max. Max was very quick and loved to run. One day, Sue and Max went to play outside in the yard. While they were playing, Sue saw a door that was open. She wanted to lock the door to keep her house safe. But Max saw a cat and ran after it very quick. Sue was scared Max would get lost, so she ran after him too. Sue caught Max and they went back to the house. When they got there, they saw that the door was now locked. Sue was confused, but then she saw her mom. Her mom had locked the door for her. The moral of the story is that sometimes, when we need help, it comes from the people who care about us the most.

Tim and Sam were brothers. They liked to play in the garden. One day, they found a big orange on the ground. It was round and shiny and smelled good. "Look, Sam, an orange!" Tim said. "Let's eat it!" "No, Tim, we have to ask Mom first," Sam said. "She told us not to eat things from the garden. They might be bad for us." Tim did not listen to Sam. He wanted to eat the orange. He took a bite and made a face. The orange was sour and bitter. It hurt his mouth. "Yuck, this is a bad orange!" Tim said. He threw the orange away. He lied to Sam and said, "I did not eat it. I just smelled it." Sam did not believe Tim. He saw the bite mark on the orange. He saw Tim's face. He knew Tim was lying. "Tim, you are lying!" Sam said. "You ate the orange. You are a bad brother. Mom will be angry with you." Tim felt sorry. He knew he did wrong. He wished he listened to Sam. He wished he did not eat the orange. He wished he did not lie. He hugged Sam and said, "I'm sorry, Sam. You are right. I ate the orange. I lied to you. I am a bad brother. Please forgive me." Sam was gentle. He hugged Tim back and said, "It's okay, Tim. I forgive you. You are not a bad brother. You just made a mistake. Next time, listen to Mom and me. We love you." Tim and Sam went back to the house. They told Mom what happened. Mom was not angry. She was glad they told the truth. She gave them a good orange to share. It was sweet and juicy and made them happy.

Once upon a time, there was a pale cat named Sam. Sam liked to nap all day under a big tree. One day, Sam heard a loud noise. It was a horn! Sam woke up from his nap and looked around. Sam saw a big truck with a horn. The truck was stuck in the mud. The man in the truck was sad. He needed help to get out. Sam thought of a plan. Sam ran to find his friend, the strong horse. The horse came and pulled the truck out of the mud with a rope. The man was happy and honked the horn again. Sam and the horse were heroes! They went back to the big tree, and Sam took another nap.
