Category: Beginner
Title: The Butterfly and the Garden

Once upon a time there was a pretty butterfly. She was very graceful and often flew around the garden. One day, the butterfly found a beautiful shoe, bright and shiny in the grass. She liked it very much so she decided to lie on top of it. Every morning the butterfly would come back and lie on the shoe until the sun would set. Soon, other animals in the garden noticed the butterfly's graceful movements as she lay and rested on the shoe. The other animals thought that the butterfly looked so pretty and wished they could look as graceful as her. Soon, the whole garden would gather to watch the butterfly lie on the shoe as the sun went down. The whole garden was in awe at how graceful the butterfly was. The end.

Anna loved to read books. She read books every day at home, at school, and at the library. The library was a big building with many books. Anna could borrow books from the library and take them home. She liked to read books about animals, fairy tales, and adventures. One day, Anna went to the library with her mom. She saw a new book on the shelf. It had a shiny cover and a picture of a castle. Anna wanted to read the book. She asked her mom, "Can I borrow this book, please?" Her mom looked at the book and said, "No, Anna, you can't. This book is not for borrowing. It is for looking only. It is a very old and special book. It belongs to a wealthy man who gave it to the library. He has many books like this in his big house. He is very kind to share them with us, but we have to be careful with them." Anna felt sad. She wanted to read the book. She wondered what the story was about. She wondered what the wealthy man's house looked like. She wondered why he had so many books and why he did not read them. She said to her mom, "Mom, why does the wealthy man have so many books if he does not read them? Does he not like books?" Her mom smiled and said, "I don't know, Anna. Maybe he likes to collect books. Maybe he likes to look at them. Maybe he reads them sometimes, but not all the time. Everyone has different ways of enjoying books. Some people like to read them, some people like to write them, some people like to share them, and some people like to keep them. What do you like to do with books, Anna?" Anna thought for a moment and said, "I like to read them, mom. I like to read them and learn new things and imagine new worlds. Books make me happy." Her mom hugged her and said, "Me too, Anna. Me too. Books are wonderful. Come on, let's find another book for you to borrow. Maybe we can find one about castles."

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a big peach tree. The tree stood tall and strong. It had many peaches on it. One day, a little girl named Mia walked by the tree. She saw the peaches and wanted to eat one. Mia asked her mom, "Can I have a peach, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can pick one." Mia stood on her tiptoes and reached for a big peach. She held it in her hands and smiled. Mia and her mom went home. It was a very hot day. Mia's mom had an idea. She said, "Let's make an icy treat with the peach!" They made an icy peach treat together. Mia took a bite and said, "Yummy! This is the best treat ever!"

Ben and Lily were walking with Mom to the park. They saw a big billboard with a picture of a dog and a bowl of food. The billboard said, "Feed your dog good food. He will love you more." "Mom, can we get a dog?" Ben asked. "Sorry, Ben, we can't. We live in a small apartment and dogs need a lot of space and care," Mom said. "But I want a dog. I will feed him good food and play with him and love him," Ben said. "Maybe when you are older and we have a bigger house, we can get a dog. But for now, we can enjoy the park and the birds and the flowers," Mom said. They reached the park and Mom found a bench to sit on. Ben and Lily ran to the swings and the slide. They had fun and laughed a lot. Then they saw a man with a big bag. He was walking to a trash can. He opened the bag and dumped a lot of food in the trash can. It was bread and cheese and fruit and cookies. "Wow, look at all that food!" Lily said. "Maybe he doesn't like it. Maybe he is not hungry," Ben said. "Or maybe he is mean and wasteful. He should give that food to someone who needs it. Like a dog," Lily said. Ben had an idea. He ran to the trash can and grabbed some of the food. He put it in his pockets and ran back to Lily. "Let's go find a dog and feed him this food. He will be happy and love us," Ben said. "OK, but we have to be quick. Mom will be looking for us soon," Lily said. They ran away from the park and looked for a dog. They saw a big dog near a fence. He was black and brown and had a collar. He looked angry and hungry. "Look, there is a dog. Let's feed him," Ben said. He took out some of the food from his pocket and threw it to the dog. The dog sniffed the food and ate it. He wagged his tail and barked. "See, he likes it. He is a good dog," Ben said. He took out more food and threw it to the dog. The dog ate it and barked louder. "Let's go closer and pet him. He will be our friend," Lily said. They walked closer to the fence and reached out their hands to the dog. The dog jumped up and bit their hands. He was not a good dog. He was a bad dog. He was a mean dog. Ben and Lily screamed and cried. They dropped the food and ran away. The dog chased them and barked louder. Mom heard their screams and saw them running. She ran after them and saw the dog. She was scared and angry. She grabbed Ben and Lily and hugged them. She saw their hands bleeding and called for help. "Ben, Lily, what were you doing? Why did you leave the park? Why did you feed that dog?" Mom asked. "We saw a billboard. It said feed your dog good food. He will love you more. We wanted a dog. We wanted him to love us. We found food in the trash can. We fed him. But he was not a good dog. He was a bad dog. He bit us. We are sorry, Mom," Ben and Lily said. Mom shook her head and sighed. She was sad and disappointed. "You should not have left the park. You should not have taken food from the trash can. You should not have fed a strange dog. You could have been hurt worse. You could have been sick. You could have been lost. You were not careful. You were not smart. You were not good," Mom said. She took them to the hospital and the doctor cleaned their wounds and gave them medicine. They had to stay in the hospital for a long time. They missed the park and the swings and the slide. They did not want a dog anymore. They learned their lesson. They were sorry. They were sad. They were bad.
