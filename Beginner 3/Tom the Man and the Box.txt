Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Man and the Box

One day, a big man named Tom went to the store. He needed to buy lumber to build a safe place for his pet dog, Max. Tom put the lumber in his big truck and drove home. When Tom got home, he saw a letter on the floor. It was from his friend, Sam. Tom read the letter. Sam wrote, "I will come to your house today. I want to see Max." Tom was happy and said, "Max, Sam is coming to see you!" Tom started to build the safe place for Max. He used the lumber and some nails. But then, something unexpected happened. Max found a big box in the yard. Max barked and barked at the box. Tom said, "What is it, Max?" He opened the box, and inside was a big, soft bed for Max! Sam had mailed the bed as a surprise. Now, Max had a new safe place to sleep, and Tom was very happy.

Lily and Ben are playing in the living room. They have a big carpet that is smooth and soft. They like to roll on the carpet and pretend they are animals. "Look, I'm a snake!" Lily says, as she slides on the carpet. She makes a hissing sound and wiggles her body. "I'm a lion!" Ben says, as he jumps on the carpet. He makes a roaring sound and shakes his mane. They have fun rolling and making noises. They laugh and giggle. But then, Mom comes in. She is not happy. She sees the carpet is messy and dirty. "Who made this mess?" Mom asks, with a frown. "You have to clean up the carpet. It is not a toy." Lily and Ben stop playing. They feel sorry. They did not mean to make Mom angry. "We're sorry, Mom," they say together. "We were just playing." Mom sighs. She knows they are good kids. She smiles and hugs them. "It's okay, my loves," she says. "But next time, please be careful with the carpet. It is not cheap. And it is not smooth when it is dirty." Lily and Ben nod. They help Mom clean up the carpet. They use a brush and a cloth. They make the carpet smooth and clean again. They learn their lesson. They still play on the carpet, but they are more gentle. They do not roll too hard or make too much dirt. They want to keep the carpet nice and smooth. And they want to make Mom happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was very enthusiastic and loved to play with her toys. One day, Lucy saw a pretty mug on the table. The mug was big and had a picture of a cat on it. Lucy liked cats, so she wanted to play with the mug. Lucy picked up the mug and started to play with it. But she was not careful and the mug fell on the floor. The mug broke into many pieces. Lucy was sad and scared because she knew she had done something wrong. She knew her mommy would not be happy. When her mommy saw the broken mug, she was not happy. She told Lucy that she had to punish her for breaking the mug. Lucy had to clean up the mess and say sorry. Lucy learned to be more careful with things that can break. From that day on, she only played with her toys and never touched mugs again.

One day, there was Jack and he said to his Mom, "I want a cone!" His Mom was nice and said, "Okay, we'll get one!" Jack was so excited. He jumped around and shouted for joy. So they went to the store and bought a nice cone. It was a cone with sprinkles on top and Jack said, "It looks so yummy!" Jack was so happy that he couldn't wait to tell his friends. He said, "Mom, can I tell my friends that I got a nice cone?" Mom said, "Yes, go ahead and tell them!" The next day, Jack's friends came to his house and they were so excited to see him with his cone. Jack was so proud as he showed them his nice cone with the sprinkles on top. Everyone was so happy and they laughed and talked about how delicious the cone looked. Jack smiled and said, "Mom, you were so nice to get me this cone!"
