Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Bowl

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Mia. She loved to play outside with her friends. One day, it was very hot outside. Mia and her friends wanted to find a way to cool down. Mia had an idea. She told her friends, "Let's make a big ice block and freeze it!" Her friends thought it was a great idea. They found a big bowl and filled it with water. Mia's mom helped them put the bowl in the freezer. The next day, the water turned into a big ice block. It was very cold and perfect for the hot day. Mia and her friends took turns sliding on the ice block. They had so much fun and felt much cooler. They learned that even on a hot day, they can still have fun with something as simple as ice. They also learned that working together is important because it helps them find new ways to play and have fun.

Once upon a time there was a little bunny called Jack. He was meant to be happy, but he was very worried. One day, he was hopping around in the forest and he heard a beautiful song. He was curious, so he jumped and jumped until he found the source of the song. He saw a big, friendly looking bird singing in the tree. Jack was shy, so he just watched from a distance. The bird saw him and asked, "Hello there, why are you so worried?" Jack replied, "I have been trying to find the beautiful song I heard". The bird smiled. "I am the one singing the song. Would you like to join me?" Jack smiled, too. He jumped up into the tree and sang along with the bird. From that day on, Jack wasn't so worried anymore. He knew that if he ever heard that song again, he would know where to find it.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big toy box with many toys. One day, Tim found a clear box with a lock on it. It was a trap! Tim did not know what to do. Tim went to his friend, Sue. Sue saw the trap and wanted to help. They thought and thought, and then they had an idea. They could share their toys to make the trap open. So, Tim and Sue went to get their toys. Tim and Sue put their toys in the clear trap. The trap opened, and out came a big, shiny ball. Tim and Sue were so happy! They played with the ball together and shared it with all their friends. The end.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He observed a girl named Sue playing with a ball. Tim wanted to play too, but he was too shy to ask. He saw that Sue had gum in her hand. Tim loved gum. Tim thought of a plan to get the gum. He walked up to Sue and took her gum without asking. That was very rude. Sue got upset and started to cry. Tim felt bad for making Sue cry. Tim said sorry to Sue and gave her gum back. Sue stopped crying and they both played with the ball. They became friends and shared the gum. Tim learned it was not nice to be rude.

One day, a foolish monkey named Bobo lived in a big tree. He loved to hang from the branches and play all day. He had a big stomach because he ate a lot of bananas. One day, while Bobo was hanging from a branch, he saw a little bird named Lulu. Lulu looked sad. Bobo asked, "Why are you sad, Lulu?" Lulu said, "I can't find my mom. I need help." Bobo wanted to help Lulu, so they went to look for her mom. They looked and looked, but they could not find Lulu's mom. Bobo was very tired and hungry. He saw a big, red fruit on a tree. Bobo thought it was a big, red banana. He was so foolish! He ate the fruit, but it was not a banana. It was a hot pepper! Bobo's stomach hurt very bad. He fell from the tree and got hurt. Lulu was still sad because she did not find her mom. Bobo and Lulu had a bad day.
