Category: Beginner
Title: Jill and Tom and the Dog

Jill and Tom were playing in the park. They liked to dig in the sand and look for shiny things. One day, they found a big gem. It was red and sparkly. They were very happy. "Wow, look at this gem!" Jill said. "It is so pretty!" "Let me see, let me see!" Tom said. He grabbed the gem from Jill's hand. He wanted to keep it for himself. "Hey, give it back!" Jill said. She tried to get the gem from Tom. They started to fight. A big dog saw them fighting. He ran over to them. He thought the gem was a ball. He snatched the gem from Tom's hand. He ran away with it. "Hey, come back!" Tom shouted. He chased the dog. Jill chased Tom. They ran across the street. They did not look for cars. A car was coming fast. It hit Tom and Jill. They fell down. They did not get up. The dog was lucky. He got away with the gem. He played with it in his yard. He did not know that Tom and Jill were hurt. He did not care. He was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was a happy girl who liked to play with her toys. One day, Lily found a big red telephone in the living room. She did not know what it was, but she thought it looked fun. Lily was a little careless and did not ask her mom about the telephone. Lily liked to look at the big red telephone. She would touch the buttons and listen to the sounds it made. One day, while she was playing with the telephone, she heard a voice. It said, "Hello, is anyone there?" Lily was surprised and scared. She did not know what to do. Lily ran to her mom and told her about the voice on the telephone. Her mom smiled and said, "That is how we talk to people who are far away, like grandma and grandpa." Lily learned that the telephone was not just a toy, but a way to talk to her loved ones. She was more careful with the telephone from then on, and enjoyed talking to her family.

Once upon a time there was a smooth scale. A fish swam up to the scale and wanted to join in. He was so happy when it happened! The scale felt so smooth when he climbed up and it was very exciting. The scale started to move up and down quickly. The fish was so surprised, he wasn't expecting it. He stayed on the scale, trying not to fall off. The scale went up and down so fast, it was like the fish was flying in the sky. After awhile, the scale started to slow down and the fish was so happy. He had so much fun joining the smooth scale. The fish thanked the scale and swam off happily. The scale smiled and waved goodbye to the fish. They had a wonderful day together.

Once upon a time, there was a school. In the school, there was a big apple. The apple was very tough. All the kids in the school wanted to eat the apple. But they could not bite it. One day, a little bug came to the school. The bug saw the tough apple. The bug was very hungry. It wanted to eat the apple too. The bug went to the apple and started to eat it. The apple began to rot. The kids in the school saw the apple rot. They were sad. They could not eat the apple now. But then, something unexpected happened. The rot made the apple soft. The kids could now eat the soft apple. They were very happy. The little bug helped them after all.

Once upon a time, there was a big forest. In the forest, there lived a tiny bug named Tim. Tim always wanted to be big and strong, but he was very small. One day, he met a magic frog who said, "I can make you big, but first, you must be ready to help others." Tim was excited and said, "Yes, I am ready!" So, the magic frog used his powers to make Tim grow. Tim was now big and strong. He went around the forest, helping his friends. He helped a bird make a nest, and he helped a squirrel find nuts. But one day, Tim saw a spider stuck in a hole. The hole was too small for Tim to help. Tim thought, "I need to shrink to help the spider." He went back to the magic frog and said, "Please make me small again so I can help my friend." The magic frog smiled and said, "You are truly ready to help others." He used his powers to make Tim shrink back to his tiny size. Tim went to the hole and saved the spider. From that day on, Tim was happy to be small because he knew that being big or small didn't matter as long as he was ready to help his friends.
