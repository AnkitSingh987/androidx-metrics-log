Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Blocks

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with blocks and cars. One day, they want to make a big tower with the blocks. They take turns to put one block on top of another. They hold the blocks carefully so they do not fall. The tower gets taller and taller. Lily and Ben are happy. They want to see how high they can make it. They look for more blocks. They find a big red block. Lily says, "Let me put it on top. It will look nice." Ben says, "No, let me do it. It is my turn." They both want the big red block. They start to pull the block from each other. They do not share. They do not listen. They do not hold the block well. The block slips from their hands. It hits the tower. The tower falls down. All the blocks make a loud noise. Lily and Ben are sad. They look at the mess. They look at each other. They feel sorry. They say, "I am sorry. I should have shared. I should have listened. Can we be friends again?" They hug each other. They say, "Yes, we can be friends again. Let's make a new tower. Together." They pick up the blocks. They hold them gently. They smile. They start to build again. They learn a result. Sharing and listening are good. Fighting and pulling are bad. They are happy. The end.

Little Joe was playing in his backyard as he heard a loud crash. He ran towards the sound and saw a ladder, laying on the ground. It had a pale colour and looked very old. "Oh no, the ladder broke, I have to find a way to fix it," Little Joe said. He picked up the ladder and started to look around the yard for something that could help him. He searched high and low, but he couldn't find anything. Suddenly, Little Joe heard footsteps coming from the house. He was scared, so he hid behind some bushes. As he peered out, he saw his dad coming towards him, carrying a toolkit! "Hi Joe, I heard the ladder break and thought you could use some help," Dad said. Relieved, Little Joe smiled and followed his dad. They soon fixed the ladder and Joe was very proud of himself. "See Joe, sometimes you need a little help to get things done," Dad said. Little Joe nodded and smiled. He learned an important lesson that day.

One day, a cat and a dog sat on the floor. They were friends. They liked to discuss things. They talked about the sun, the rain, and their favorite toys. "Dog, do you like my new ball?" asked the cat in a polite way. "Yes, Cat. I like it a lot!" said the dog. They played with the ball and had fun. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took the ball away. The cat and the dog were sad. But they knew they still had each other. They sat on the floor and talked about their day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very adventurous. He loved to play outside with his toys. One day, he found a saw in his dad's big box. Tim thought it was a toy, but it was not. Tim took the saw to the backyard. He saw a big tree and wanted to play with it. He tried to pour water on the tree, but nothing happened. Then, he had an idea. He used the saw to cut a small branch. Suddenly, the tree started to shake. The tree was not just a tree, it was a magic tree! It could talk and walk. The tree said, "Thank you, Tim, for setting me free!" Tim was surprised and happy. From that day on, Tim and the magic tree became best friends. They went on many more adventurous trips together.

One day, a playful dog named Max went to the park with his friend, a little girl named Lily. They liked to play and run in the park together. The sun was shining, and the grass was green. Max was very happy. At the park, Max saw a big cut on the ground. He barked at it and said to Lily, "Look, a big cut! Be careful!" Lily looked at the cut and thanked Max for warning her. They played around the cut, making sure not to get too close. Later, they saw a boy with a ball. The boy threw the ball, and Max ran to get it. He brought it back to the boy, who said, "Thank you, Max!" Max wagged his tail and barked happily. Lily and Max played with the boy and his ball, and they had a great time at the park. They were glad they stayed away from the big cut, and they went home safe and happy.
