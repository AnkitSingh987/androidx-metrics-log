Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Toys

One day, a little girl named Mia wanted to gather all her toys. She put them in a big box. She was very quiet as she did this. Mia did not want her baby brother to wake up from his nap. Mia's mom came into the room with a candle. "Look Mia, I have a candle," said her mom. "We can use it for your tea party with your toys." Mia was very happy. She loved tea parties. Mia and her mom had a quiet tea party with the toys. They used the candle to make it feel special. Mia's baby brother slept through the whole thing. They all had a fun and quiet time together.

Once there was a girl who wanted to win something more than anything. She was very determined. One day she decided to measure her progress with a ruler. But to her surprise, she hadn't made any progress at all. The girl felt very angry and she let out a loud cry. But she thought to herself that she would try her best and not give up. So she did just that. She worked hard and studied a lot. In the end, she won a competition! Everyone was so surprised and proud of her. They cheered as she celebrated her victory. It was a great day for her and she was very proud of herself. She had achieved her goal and had won!

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to run and play with his friends. One day, they decided to have a race to see who was the fastest. The race was set to start when the sun was light and bright in the sky. Tim was so excited that he could not sleep. When the day came, he woke up early and put on his shoes that fit just right. He went outside and met his friends at the starting line. "Are you ready to race?" asked Tim's friend, Sam. "Yes, I am!" said Tim with a big smile. They all lined up and waited for the signal. When they heard the word "Go!" they all started running as fast as they could. Tim and Sam were very close, but Tim ran a little faster and won the race. All the friends cheered and clapped for Tim. They were all happy and had a great day. From that day on, they loved having races and playing together under the light sun.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to visit her grandma. Grandma lived in a house with a huge garden. Amy loved to play in the garden with her dog, Spot. In the garden, there was a big table under a tree. Grandma would put snacks on the table for Amy and Spot to eat. One morning, Grandma told Amy, "I have a surprise for you on the table in the garden." Amy was excited and ran to the table with Spot. But when they got there, the table was empty. Amy was sad and Spot barked. They both looked around, trying to find the surprise. Just then, they heard a noise in the bushes. Amy and Spot went to look and found a little kitten. The kitten was the surprise! Amy was so happy and said, "Thank you, Grandma!" They all played together in the garden and had snacks on the big table under the tree.

Lily and Ben were friends. They liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big apple tree. The apples were red and shiny. Lily and Ben wanted to eat them. "Let's get some apples," Ben said. "They look yummy." "But the tree is very high," Lily said. "It is difficult to reach the apples." Ben had an idea. He found a big stick. He said, "I can use this stick to knock down the apples. You catch them in your hands." Lily agreed. She stood under the tree. Ben lifted the stick and hit the tree. An apple fell down. Lily caught it. She was happy. "Good job, Ben!" she said. "I love apples. Do you want to share?" "Yes, please," Ben said. He hit the tree again. Another apple fell down. But this time, it did not fall to Lily. It fell on Ben's head. It was hard and heavy. Ben felt a lot of pain. He dropped the stick and cried. "Ow, ow, ow!" he shouted. "The apple hurt me!" Lily ran to Ben. She saw blood on his head. She was scared. She did not know what to do. She dropped the apple and cried too. "Help, help, help!" she screamed. "Ben is hurt!" But no one heard them. They were alone in the park. The apple tree was silent. The apples were still red and shiny. But Lily and Ben did not want them anymore. They wanted to go home. They wanted their moms and dads. They were very sad. The end.
