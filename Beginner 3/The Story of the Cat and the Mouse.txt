Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Cat and the Mouse

One day, a big cat and a little mouse were walking in the park. They saw a tall tower. The cat said, "Let's go to the tower!" The mouse said, "Yes, let's go!" As they got closer, they heard loud sounds. The cat covered his ears and said, "I can't hear well. The sounds are too loud!" The mouse said, "I am deaf, so I can't hear the sounds. But I can feel them." They saw that the loud sounds were coming from a big clock. The clock would strike and make a loud noise. The cat and the mouse climbed the tower to see the clock. When they reached the top, they saw a big bell. The cat said, "This bell makes the loud sounds. When the clock strikes, it hits the bell." The mouse said, "I can feel it when the bell moves." The cat and the mouse had fun at the tower. They went home and told their friends about the big clock and the loud bell.

Lily loved to dream. Every night, she closed her eyes and saw wonderful things. She saw fairies and unicorns and rainbows. She saw her friends and her toys and her mommy and daddy. She was happy in her dreams. But one night, she had a bad dream. She saw a big, scary monster. It had sharp teeth and claws and horns. It roared and chased her. She ran and ran, but it was faster. She cried and screamed, but no one heard her. She was miserable in her dream. She woke up and saw that it was dark. She was alone in her bed. She wanted to hug her teddy bear, but it was not there. She looked around and saw that the monster had taken it. It was holding it in its mouth, tearing it apart. It saw her and smiled wickedly. "Hello, Lily," it said. "Did you miss me? I came to visit you again. I like your dreams. They are tasty. And I like your teddy bear. He is soft. But not as soft as you. Do you want to play with me?" Lily was terrified. She tried to hide under the covers, but it was too late. The monster jumped on her bed and grabbed her. She felt its teeth and claws digging into her skin. She felt its breath on her face. She felt its hunger and hate. She felt nothing else. The end.

One day, a boy named Tim saw a big plane. He thought the plane looked boring. Tim wanted to make the plane look fun. He had an idea. He wanted to decorate the plane. Tim asked his friend, Sue. "Do you want to help me make the plane look crazy?" Sue said, "Yes, let's do it!" They got paint and brushes. They painted the plane with many colors. People saw the crazy plane and smiled. Tim and Sue were happy. They made the plane look fun for everyone. The end.

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a bottle of lotion. She felt lucky because her hands were very dry. She decided to show her mom the lotion. "Look, Mom! I found lotion for my hands," Lucy said. Her mom smiled and said, "That's great, Lucy! Let's see if it works." Lucy put the lotion on her hands and started to rub it in. Suddenly, something unexpected happened. The lotion made her hands turn different colors! Lucy's hands were now red, blue, and green. She was surprised and said, "Mom, look at my hands!" Her mom looked at Lucy's hands and laughed. "Oh, Lucy," she said, "that's not just lotion, it's magic lotion!" They both laughed and enjoyed the colorful surprise. From that day on, Lucy felt even luckier when she found the magic lotion.

Once upon a time, there was a mule named Max. Max was very frightened of the dark. One night, Max heard a noise outside. He was so scared that he called out, "Who's there?" A small voice answered, "It's me, Sue the squirrel. I'm lost and need help." Max didn't want to be scared anymore, so he said, "Okay, Sue. Let's be brave together." Max and Sue walked in the dark, trying to find Sue's home. Suddenly, a big shadow appeared. Both Max and Sue were ready to surrender to the scary shadow. But then, the shadow spoke, "Don't be scared, friends. It's me, Luna the owl." Luna helped Sue find her way home, and Max learned that the dark wasn't so scary when he had friends by his side. From that night on, Max was never frightened of the dark again.
