Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Cat

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park. She saw a thin cat sitting on the grass. Lucy wanted to give the cat a taste of her snack. She had a yummy cookie in her hand. She walked up to the cat and said, "Do you want a taste of my cookie?" The cat looked at her and nodded its head. Lucy broke a piece of the cookie and gave it to the cat. The cat ate the cookie and smiled. But then, something unexpected happened! The cat started to grow bigger and bigger. It was not a cat at all! It was a big lion wearing a cat costume! Lucy was surprised, but the lion was friendly. It licked Lucy's hand and said, "Thank you for the cookie, Lucy." From that day on, Lucy and the lion were best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big red barn. In the barn, there lived a cow, a pig, and a horse. They were all very good friends. One day, they saw a big ball near the barn door. The cow said, "Let's play with the ball!" The horse was very alert. He remembered that the farmer had told them not to play near the door. But the pig and the cow did not listen. They started to push the ball back and forth. The horse watched them and thought, "I hope they don't push the ball too hard." As the cow and the pig played, they pushed the ball harder and harder. Suddenly, the ball hit the barn door and it opened wide. The wind blew and the door closed with a loud bang. The horse said, "I knew this would happen! We must be careful next time." The cow and the pig learned their lesson. They promised to listen to their friend, the horse, and not play near the door again. From that day on, they played safely in the barn and had lots of fun together.

One day, a boy named Tom went to the shore with his mom. He saw a big race with many kids running. Tom wanted to join the race too. He was competitive and wanted to win. His mom said, "You can do it, Tom!" During the race, Tom saw a girl fall down. She started to cry. Tom stopped running and thought, "Should I help her or keep running to win?" He decided to help the girl. He held her hand and helped her stand up. The girl said, "Thank you, Tom!" In the end, Tom did not win the race. But everyone clapped for him because he was kind and helpful. Tom learned that helping others is more important than winning. The girl became his friend, and they played together at the shore. Tom was happy because he did the right thing.

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog. The brown dog had a sock. He liked to play with the sock. One day, he saw a cat. The cat was sad. The brown dog wanted to help the cat. He thought the cat would like the sock. So, he went to the cat and said, "Do you want to play with my sock?" The cat looked at the sock and said, "No, thank you." The brown dog felt sad. He wanted to make the cat happy. He tried to offer the sock again. But the cat still said, "No, thank you." The dog did not know what to do. The brown dog went away with his sock. He felt very sad. The cat did not want to play with him. The dog played with the sock alone. The cat stayed sad and the dog felt bad. The end.

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to study different things. Tom liked to study bugs and Lily liked to study stars. One day, they decided to make a project with string and paper. Tom cut out paper shapes of bugs and glued them to pieces of string. He hung them from a stick and made a bug mobile. Lily cut out paper shapes of stars and glued them to pieces of string. She hung them from a hoop and made a star mobile. They showed their projects to their mom and dad. "Look what we made!" they said. Mom and dad smiled and clapped. "Wow, those are very impressive!" they said. "You both studied hard and made beautiful things with string and paper." Tom and Lily felt happy and proud. They hugged their mom and dad and thanked them. Then they hung their mobiles in their room and watched them spin and sparkle. They liked to study different things, but they also liked to share their projects with each other.
