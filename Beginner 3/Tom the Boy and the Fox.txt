Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Fox

One day, a boy named Tom went to school. At school, there was a nice teach named Mr. Fox. Mr. Fox was very honest. He liked to help all the kids learn new things. One day, Mr. Fox told the kids a story. In the story, there was a big, strong lion. The lion was very kind. He said to a little mouse, "Do you need help?" The little mouse said, "Yes, please." The lion helped the mouse, and they became good friends. Tom liked the story a lot. He asked Mr. Fox, "Can I be like the lion?" Mr. Fox said, "Yes, you can. Just be honest and kind to others." Tom was happy. He learned that being honest and kind was good.

Once upon a time there were two sisters named Jane and Lina. Jane was very fancy. She wore a pretty dress and had a sparkly bow in her hair. Lina had a very special heart. She loved to tell stories about it. One day Jane thought it would be fun to hear Lina's stories so she asked her to tell one. Lina told a sweet story about her heart. It was filled with happiness and love! Jane listened carefully and smiled at how fancy Lina's stories were. At the end of the story, Jane and Lina hugged. They both knew that their hearts were connected by love. Jane hugged Lina a little extra tight, and they continued to tell stories for the rest of the day.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a toy. It was a perfect toy. It had a string that you could attach to things. Sue wanted to show her mom. She went to look for her mom in the house. She looked in the kitchen, but her mom was not there. Sue went to the bathroom and saw her mom. "Mom, look at my perfect toy!" she said. "It has a string to attach to things." Her mom smiled and said, "That is a nice toy, Sue. What do you want to attach it to?" Sue thought for a moment. She wanted to attach it to something fun. She looked around the bathroom and saw the towel rack. "I will attach it to the towel rack, Mom!" Sue said. She attached the toy to the towel rack and played with it. Sue and her mom had a fun day playing with the perfect toy.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was feeling very bored. He did not know what to do. He saw his mom polish the window. Tim wanted to help his mom. Tim's mom said, "You can polish the window too, but be careful." Tim was happy to help. He started to polish the window. But, he did not see a big ball coming his way. The ball hit the window and it broke. Tim was scared. He thought his mom would be mad. But, his mom was not mad. She said, "It's okay. We can fix the window together." They fixed the window and it was not boring anymore. Tim learned that even when things go wrong, it's okay if you work together.

Once upon a time, there was a little crab who lived by the sea. The crab had a brilliant red shell that shone in the sun. He loved to play with his friends, the fish and the birds. One day, the crab saw a big bird with a pretty feather. "Please, can I have your feather?" asked the crab. The bird said, "Yes, you can have it. But first, you must help me find my lost friend, the fish." The crab agreed and they went to look for the fish. They looked in the sea, under the rocks, and near the shore. Finally, they found the fish trapped in a net. The crab used his strong claws to break the net and set the fish free. The fish and the bird thanked the crab for his help. The bird gave the crab the pretty feather, and they all played together by the sea. They were very happy and became the best of friends.
