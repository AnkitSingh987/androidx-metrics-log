Category: Beginner
Title: Bobo the Hairy Bear and the River

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a hairy bear named Bobo. Bobo loved to play in the mud all day long. One day, his mom told him it was time for a task. Bobo did not like tasks, but he listened to his mom. The task was to bathe in the river to get clean. Bobo did not want to do it, but he knew his mom would be happy if he did. So, he went to the river and started to bathe. As he played in the water, he began to have fun. Soon, Bobo was all clean and his mom was very happy. She gave him a big hug and a tasty treat. Bobo learned that even tasks can be fun if you give them a try. And from that day on, he always looked forward to his baths.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys. One day, he found a tray in the kitchen. He thought it would be fun to use the tray for his toys. Tim's mom saw him with the tray and asked, "Tim, what are you doing with the tray?" Tim felt ashamed because he knew he should not take things without asking. He said, "I'm sorry, Mom. I wanted to use it for my toys." His mom smiled and said, "It's okay, Tim. Next time, just question me first. You can use the tray for your toys today." Tim was happy and played with his toys on the tray all day long. He learned to always ask before taking things.

One day, a big dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a very big puddle. Max thought the puddle was impressive. He wanted to play in the puddle. Max jumped into the puddle. He splashed and splashed. He was having so much fun. Max saw his friend, a little cat named Lily. He asked Lily to guess what he was doing. Lily looked at Max and the big puddle. She said, "I guess you are playing in the puddle, Max!" Max smiled and nodded. Lily and Max played in the puddle all day. They were very happy.

Once upon a time, in a hot land, there lived a persistent little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside in the heat. One day, he saw a big red ball bouncing in the sun. Spot ran to the ball and said, "Hi, Ball! Do you want to play with me?" The ball did not talk. But it kept bouncing. Spot did not quit. He kept trying to play with the ball. Spot chased the ball all day in the heat. He was very tired, but he did not quit. At last, the sun went down, and the ball stopped bouncing. Spot lay down next to the ball and said, "Good night, Ball. We will play again tomorrow."

Timmy was a 3 year old boy. He loved adventure. One day he decided to go search for something. He decided to search for a cooler. He ran off, but he got tired so he decided to take a break and he asked his mom "Mommy, what is a cooler?". "A cooler is like a box," she said. "It's good for keeping food and drinks cold." Timmy thought it sounded great and he set off to find a cooler. He looked in all kinds of places. He looked in the store, and found some nice coolers but they were too expensive. Finally, he went to a garage sale and there, he found a cheap cooler. He was so excited he ran home to show his mommy. "Look, Mommy!" he said, holding up the cheap cooler. "I found it!" Mommy was so proud of him, and she hugged him tight. "You did a great job, Timmy! Let's fill it with food and drinks and go spend a fun day in the park!"

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a red bucket that he loved to play with. One day, Tim saw a filthy rag on the ground. He wanted to clean his bucket, so he picked up the rag and started to fold it. As Tim was folding the rag, he heard a noise. He looked up and saw a big dog. The dog wanted to play with Tim's bucket too. Tim was scared and didn't want to share his bucket with the dog. He tried to hide the bucket behind his back, but the dog saw it. The dog barked and jumped, trying to get the bucket. Tim closed his eyes and held the bucket tight. When he opened his eyes, the dog was gone! Instead, there was a small, friendly cat. The cat helped Tim clean his bucket with the folded rag. Tim was happy, and he played with the cat and his clean bucket all day long.
