Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a pet cat named Fluffy. Fluffy was a thin cat with soft fur. Sue loved to play with Fluffy every day. One day, Sue and Fluffy went outside to play in the sun. They walked and observed the pretty flowers and the big trees. Fluffy liked to chase the butterflies and Sue liked to watch. They were both very happy. After a while, they got tired and went back home. Sue gave Fluffy some food to eat and they both took a nap. They had a fun day playing and observing the world outside. Sue and Fluffy were the best of friends.

One day, a boy and his dog went to play at the park. They saw a big pit. The boy said to his dog, "Let's play in the pit!" The dog wagged its tail and barked, "Yes, let's play!" They jumped into the pit and found many cheap toys. The boy said, "Wow, look at these cheap toys! We can play with them!" The dog picked up a toy with its mouth and they played together. They had so much fun in the pit. Then, something unexpected happened. The pit started to fill with water! The boy and the dog tried to get out, but they could not. The boy said, "Help! We need help!" But no one was there to help them. The water got higher and higher, and the boy and the dog could not get out of the pit. The cheap toys floated away, and the boy and the dog were sad and scared.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree. It had many pale green leaves. One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to decorate the tree. Lily found a pretty red bow. She said to her friend Tim, "Look at this bow! Let's put it on the tree." Tim was happy and said, "Yes, let's decorate the tree with the bow!" They put the bow on the tree. The tree looked very nice. Lily and Tim were so happy. They danced and sang under the tree. The tree with the pretty red bow made everyone smile.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the garden. She saw a big squash. Sue wanted to take the squash home. She tried to pick it up, but it was too heavy. Sue sat down and started to cry. Then, she heard a voice. "Why are you crying?" it asked. Sue looked around and saw the squash was talking! "I want to take you home, but you are too heavy," Sue said. The squash smiled. "I have an idea! I will become small and light, but you need to promise me something." Sue was excited. "What do I need to promise?" she asked. The squash said, "You must promise not to be foolish and always be kind to others." Sue promised, and the squash became small and light. Sue took it home and they lived happily, always remembering to be kind and not foolish.

Once upon a time, there was a little flea. The flea lived in a big, heavy blanket. The blanket was on a bed in a small room. One day, the flea met a tiny bug. The bug said, "I am cold. Can you help me?" The flea wanted to help, but the heavy blanket was too big for the flea to move. The flea tried to cover the bug with the blanket, but it was too hard. The flea asked a big ant to help. The ant was very strong. The ant helped the flea move the heavy blanket. Together, they covered the bug with the blanket. The bug was warm and happy. The flea, the bug, and the ant became good friends.

Sara and Ben liked to help Mom in the kitchen. They wanted to make a cake for Dad's birthday. Mom gave them a big bowl and some eggs, flour, sugar, and butter. She told them to mix everything well. "Can I crack the eggs?" Sara asked. "OK, but be careful. Don't let the shells go in the bowl," Mom said. Sara took an egg and tapped it on the edge of the bowl. But she tapped too hard and the egg broke in her hand. The yolk and the white ran down her fingers. She felt sticky and yucky. "Oops, sorry," Sara said. "It's OK, we have more eggs. Here, use this one," Mom said. She gave Sara a paper towel to wipe her hand. Ben watched Sara and wanted to crack an egg too. He grabbed an egg from the carton and smashed it on the table. The egg splattered everywhere. Some of it landed on Mom's face and hair. Mom gasped and looked angry. "Ben, what did you do?" Mom yelled. "I wanted to crack an egg too," Ben said. "That's not how you crack an egg. You made a big mess. Go to your room right now," Mom said. Ben felt sad and scared. He ran to his room and slammed the door. He heard Mom sigh and clean up the kitchen. He heard her put the cake in the oven and set the timer. He wished he could say sorry and help her. But he didn't get a chance. The oven was old and broken. It got too hot and started a fire. The smoke alarm went off and Mom screamed. She grabbed Sara and ran out of the house. She called the firemen and waited for them to come. Ben heard the noise and smelled the smoke. He opened his door and saw the flames. He was trapped in his room. He cried and shouted for Mom. But she didn't hear him. He was alone and afraid. The firemen came and put out the fire. They searched the house and found Ben. But it was too late. He was gone. Mom and Sara were very sad. They lost their home and their Ben. They wished they could have their cake and their happy birthday. But they couldn't. They had nothing.
