Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Lion

Lily and Ben were going to see a show with their mom and dad. They were very excited to see the animals and the clowns on the stage. They had to sit in big chairs and wait for the show to start. Lily wanted to see everything. She leaned forward and tried to look over the people in front of her. She saw a big elephant and a funny clown. She smiled and clapped her hands. Ben wanted to see everything too. But he was too small to see over the people. He leaned to the left and to the right, but he could not see anything. He felt sad and angry. He wanted to sit on his mom's lap, but she said no. She said he had to sit in his own chair and be quiet. Ben was stubborn. He did not listen to his mom. He started to cry and kick his chair. He made a lot of noise. The people around him looked at him and said shh. His mom and dad were embarrassed. They tried to calm him down, but he did not stop. Lily saw her brother crying. She felt sorry for him. She leaned over and whispered in his ear. "Ben, don't cry. I will tell you what I see. Look, there is a lion and a lady. The lady is putting her head in the lion's mouth. Wow, that is brave. And there is a man and a bike. The man is riding the bike on a thin wire. That is amazing. And there is a monkey and a hat. The monkey is taking the hat from the people and putting it on his head. That is funny. Do you see them, Ben?" Ben stopped crying and listened to his sister. He tried to see what she saw. He saw the lion and the lady, the man and the bike, and the monkey and the hat. He smiled and laughed. He said thank you to his sister. He leaned back and enjoyed the show. His mom and dad were happy. They hugged him and kissed him. They said he was a good boy. They said they loved him and Lily very much. They all had a good time at the show.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree. The tree had a big letter "A" on it. The letter "A" was very special. One day, a little girl named Amy came to the tree. She saw the big letter "A" and said, "Wow! That's a big letter! I want to make it pretty." So, Amy decided to decorate the letter "A". Amy found some colorful flowers and leaves. She put them on the big letter "A". The letter "A" looked very pretty now. Amy was happy. Then, a little boy named Tom came to the tree. He saw the decorated letter "A" and said, "Wow! That's a pretty letter! Did you do that, Amy?" Amy smiled and said, "Yes, I did! Do you like it?" Tom said, "Yes, I do! It's very pretty!" Amy and Tom played under the big tree with the decorated letter "A" all day. They had so much fun and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, Sue's mom was very tired. She asked Sue to help her make a cake. Sue said, "Yes, Mom! I can help you." Sue's mom told her to measure some sugar with a teaspoon. Sue did as her mom said, but she was very excited. She didn't know that something fun was about to happen. As Sue was measuring the sugar, she accidentally spilled some on the floor. Her mom saw it and smiled. "Don't worry, Sue," she said. "We can clean it up later. Let's finish making the cake first." They finished making the cake and put it in the oven to bake. Sue's mom then said, "Now, let's clean up the sugar. But first, let's play a game!" Sue was so happy. They played a game where they had to find all the sugar on the floor. After they cleaned up and the cake was done, Sue and her mom enjoyed a big slice together. Sue felt proud that she could help her tired mom, and they both had a fun day in the kitchen.
