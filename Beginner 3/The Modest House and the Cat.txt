Category: Beginner
Title: The Modest House and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a modest house. In the house lived a cat and a dog. The cat and dog liked to stay in the house and play together. One day, the cat found a screen. The screen was big and fun to look at. The cat and dog sat in front of the screen and watched it all day. When it was time to sleep, the cat and dog were happy. They had a fun day with the screen in their modest house. They knew they would stay friends and have more fun days together.

One day, a boy named Tim was ready to unpack his toys. It was Tuesday, and he was very excited. He had a big box full of new toys from his birthday party. He opened the box and saw a new car, a ball, and a teddy bear. He was so happy! Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. "Wow, you have new toys!" said Sam. "Can I play with them too?" Tim thought about it for a moment and then said, "Yes, we can share my toys and play together." They played with the car, the ball, and the teddy bear all day long. They had so much fun! At the end of the day, Tim's mom said, "I'm proud of you for sharing your toys with Sam. It's good to share with friends." Tim felt happy that he shared his toys and made his friend happy too. He learned that sharing is caring, and it's more fun to play with friends when everyone is happy.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play pretend. Today, they pretend to be dentists and patients. Anna puts on a white coat and a mask. Ben sits on a chair and opens his mouth. "Hello, Ben. I am Dr. Anna. I will check your teeth. Do you brush them every day?" Anna asks. "Yes, I do. But I have a sore tooth. It hurts when I eat," Ben says. Anna looks at Ben's teeth. She sees a black spot on one of them. She takes a toy drill and makes a buzzing sound. "Don't worry, Ben. I will fix your tooth. It will not hurt. You are very brave," Anna says. She pretends to drill Ben's tooth and fill it with play-doh. She gives him a sticker and a hug. "There, all done. Your tooth is good now. You can eat anything you want. But remember to brush your teeth twice a day," Anna says. "Thank you, Dr. Anna. You are very compassionate. You are the best dentist ever," Ben says. They smile and high-five. They put away their toys and go to have a snack. They are happy. They are good friends. They like to play pretend.

One day, a rich man named Tom had a big hole in his favorite red shirt. He was very sad. At night, he asked his friend, Sam, to help him repair the shirt. Sam said, "I can help you fix your shirt, Tom. Let's use a blue patch to make it look nice again." Tom was happy and they started to repair the shirt. As they were fixing it, a big wind blew into the room. The wind picked up the blue patch and carried it away. "Oh no!" said Tom. "Now we can't fix my shirt." But then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew in through the window with the blue patch in its beak. The bird dropped the patch into Tom's hand. Tom and Sam were very surprised and happy. They thanked the little bird and finished repairing the shirt. The shirt looked even better than before, and Tom and Sam became better friends.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a tiny, adorable fairy. She had a magic wand. Every day, she would fly around and help her friends. Today, she met a sad bird. The bird could not fly. The fairy wanted to help the bird. She had an idea! She said, "Let me try something." The fairy waved her magic wand. She made a big, soft pillow. She put the pillow under the bird. The bird was not sure what the pillow was for. The fairy smiled and said, "You will see!" The next day, the bird tried to fly again. This time, when the bird fell, it landed on the soft pillow. The bird was not hurt! The bird was happy and said, "Thank you, fairy!" The adorable fairy smiled and flew away, ready to help more friends.
