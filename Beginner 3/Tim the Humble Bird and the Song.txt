Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Humble Bird and the Song

Once upon a time, there was a humble bird named Tim. Tim lived in a big tree near a small house. One day, Tim was sitting on a branch when he saw a little girl playing on the floor inside the house. The little girl looked sad. Tim wanted to make the girl happy, so he started to whistle a happy song. The girl heard Tim's song and looked out the window. "Who is singing?" she asked. "I am Tim, the bird," Tim said. "I want to make you happy with my song." The girl smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim. I like your song!" And from that day on, Tim and the girl became good friends.

One day, John saw a deer. He wanted to show everyone how brave he was, so he decided to solve the deer. He started to run towards the deer, but the deer was too fast. He chased the deer for a long time, but he couldn't catch it. John was getting angry. All of a sudden, the deer stopped. It almost looked like it was waiting for John. So John stayed really still and finally the deer came close. John was very happy and he showed everyone the deer he had solved.

---

One day, a noisy cat named Tom found a big muffin on the ground. He was very happy because he loved to eat muffins. Tom picked up the muffin and started to eat it. It was very yummy. Tom's friend, a dog named Max, saw him eating the muffin. Max asked, "Can I have some too?" Tom said, "No, this is my muffin!" Max was sad but he waited for his turn. Suddenly, a big bird came and took the muffin away from Tom. Tom and Max were both very surprised. They looked at each other and decided to work together to get the muffin back. They chased the bird and in the end, they got the muffin back. Tom and Max shared the muffin and became best friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the lake with his mom. They had a new ball to play with. Tim was very happy. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. At the lake, Tim saw a big duck. The duck walked up to Tim and said, "Give me the new ball!" The duck was demanding the ball. Tim was scared, but his mom told him it was okay. She said, "Let's give the duck the ball and see what happens." Tim gave the ball to the duck. The duck took the ball and jumped into the lake. Tim and his mom were surprised. They thought the duck would play with the ball. But then, the duck came back with a big splash. The duck had many little baby ducks with it. The baby ducks wanted to play too. Tim and his mom laughed and played with the ducks. They all had a great day at the lake.

One day, a little girl named Mia went out to play in the snow. She saw an igloo and wanted to go inside. As she walked closer, she began to tremble because it was so cold. Inside the igloo, Mia met a friendly penguin named Pippin. Pippin was very organized and had all his toys in a neat row. Mia said, "Hi, I like your igloo!" Pippin replied, "Thank you! Do you want to play with my toys?" Mia and Pippin played with the toys and had a great time. They laughed and talked, forgetting all about the cold. Soon, Mia's mom called her for dinner. Mia said goodbye to Pippin and promised to visit again. She went home feeling warm and happy from her new friendship.

Anna liked to imagine that she had a mouse friend. She named him Max and gave him a tiny hat and a cheese snack. She pretended that Max lived in her dollhouse and played with her toys. One day, Anna's mom took her to the park. Anna brought Max with her in her pocket. She wanted to show him the swings and the slide. She thought he would have fun with her. But when Anna reached the park, she saw a big cat. The cat had yellow eyes and sharp teeth. It looked hungry and mean. Anna was scared. She hugged Max and ran to her mom. "Mom, there is a cat! It wants to eat Max!" Anna cried. Her mom looked at the cat and smiled. "Don't worry, Anna. That cat is not real. It is a statue. See, it does not move or make a sound. It is just for decoration." Anna looked again and saw that her mom was right. The cat was not real. It was made of stone and painted. Anna felt silly and relieved. She laughed and kissed Max. "Sorry, Max. I thought the cat was real. You are safe with me. You are my charming mouse friend." Anna said. She put Max back in her pocket and went to play on the swings and the slide. She imagined that Max enjoyed the park too. She was happy.
