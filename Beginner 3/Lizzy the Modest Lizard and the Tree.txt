Category: Beginner
Title: Lizzy the Modest Lizard and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a modest lizard named Lizzy. Lizzy was small and green. She lived in a nice, warm place with her family. They liked to play and eat bugs together. One day, Lizzy wanted to go to the big tree. She asked her friend, Bunny, to go with her. "Bunny, let's go to the big tree," said Lizzy. "Okay, but I need to find my toy first," said Bunny. This made a delay because they had to look for the toy. They looked and looked, and finally, they found the toy under a bush. Lizzy and Bunny were happy. They went to the big tree and played all day. They had a lot of fun and went home when the sun went down.

---

One day, a little girl named Sally had a big box. She wanted to create something fun. She thought and thought. Then, she had an idea! She would make a fun play area with her toys. "Come play with me, Tim!" Sally said to her brother. Tim came to play. They played and played, but they made the area very dirty. They did not care, they were having fun. But, when it was time for bed, they forgot to clean up. That night, Sally had a bad dream. It was a nightmare! In her dream, the dirty play area came to life. The toys were big and scary. They chased her and Tim. Sally woke up scared and ran to Tim's room. "Tim, I had a nightmare! Can I sleep with you?" Sally asked. Tim said, "Yes, come in." They slept together and felt safe. The next day, they cleaned up the play area. They did not want any more nightmares. They learned to clean up after playing.

---

One day, a black lion went to the park. He was big and strong. He wanted to play with the other animals, but they were scared of him. The black lion felt sad because he could not make friends. The black lion saw a little girl with a big red ball. He wanted to get the ball and play with her. The little girl was not scared of the black lion. They played with the ball together and had fun. The black lion was happy to have a friend. But then, a big wind came and took the red ball away. The black lion and the little girl tried to get the ball, but they could not. The ball was gone. The black lion and the little girl were sad. They could not play anymore, and the black lion went back to being alone.

Anna and Ben were playing in the garden. They had a big box of toys and they shared them with each other. They were generous friends. "Look, I have a saw!" Ben said, holding a plastic tool. "I can cut this branch." "Be careful, Ben!" Anna said. "That saw is not real. It is only for pretend." Ben nodded. He pretended to cut the branch with the saw. He made a buzzing sound with his mouth. Anna laughed. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise. It was a truck. It was coming to their house. They paused and looked at each other. "What is that?" Anna asked. "Maybe it is a surprise!" Ben said. They ran to the gate and waited. The truck stopped and a man got out. He had a big smile on his face. "Hello, Anna and Ben!" he said. "I have something for you. Do you want to see?" "Yes, please!" Anna and Ben said. The man opened the back of the truck. Inside, there was a huge teddy bear. It was bigger than Anna and Ben. It had a red bow around its neck. "This is for you!" the man said. "Your grandma sent it to you. She loves you very much." Anna and Ben gasped. They were so happy. They thanked the man and hugged the teddy bear. "Wow, this is the best surprise ever!" Anna said. "Thank you, grandma!" Ben said. They dragged the teddy bear to the garden. They played with it and the other toys. They were generous friends. They shared everything with the teddy bear. They had a lot of fun.
