Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Sun

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside in the sun. One day, it was very hot. Lily felt the heat on her face. Lily saw a rare, big, red bird in her yard. She had never seen a bird like this before. "Hi, bird!" Lily said. The bird looked at her and said, "Hi, Lily! I am a rare bird from far away. The heat is too much for me." Lily wanted to help the bird. "Do you want to come inside my house? It is not hot in there," she said. The bird was happy and said, "Yes, please!" So, Lily and the bird went inside and played together all day. They became best friends, and the bird did not feel hot anymore.

Once upon a time, in a land far to the east, there was a big tree. The tree had many available fruits. One day, a little boy named Tim and his friend, a small bird named Bill, went to the tree. "Let's eat the fruits!" said Tim. Bill nodded and they both started to eat. They tried to resist eating all the fruits, but they were so yummy. They laughed and played under the tree, having a great time. Soon, it was time to go home. Tim and Bill said goodbye to the tree and walked back to their homes. They promised to come back and visit the tree again soon. And so, they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Lily went on a walk with her mom. They walked in the park and saw many pretty flowers. Lily was a stubborn girl and wanted to find something new. As they walked, Lily heard a sound. "Mom, what is that?" she asked. Her mom said, "It's a bird singing a song, Lily." Lily listened to the song and loved it. She wanted to discover where the bird was. They looked around and found the bird in a tree. "Hello, bird!" said Lily. "I like your song!" The bird sang more for Lily and her mom. They danced and laughed together. From that day on, Lily loved to discover new things in the park.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a big cloud in the sky. The cloud looked like a big, soft pillow. Lily was glad because she loved to watch the clouds. Lily saw a little boy named Tim looking at the cloud too. She walked up to him and said, "Hi! Do you like the big cloud too?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, I do! It looks so soft and fluffy!" Lily and Tim sat on the grass and watched the cloud together. They were very glad to have a new friend to share the fun with. As they sat, they saw more clouds and talked about what they looked like. They had a happy day at the park with the clouds.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was scared of the dark. Every night, he thought he saw a ghost in his room. The ghost looked big and bad. Tim would panic and hide under his blanket. One night, Tim's mom came into his room. She saw Tim hiding under his blanket. "What's wrong, Tim?" she asked. Tim said, "There is a bad ghost in my room, and I panic when I see it." Tim's mom looked around the room. She did not see a ghost. She saw a big shadow on the wall. It was just Tim's teddy bear. Tim's mom moved the teddy bear, and the shadow went away. "See, Tim, there is no ghost. It was just a shadow," she said. Tim felt better. He knew there was no bad ghost in his room. He hugged his teddy bear and went to sleep. From that night on, Tim did not panic when he saw shadows. He knew they were not ghosts.

Once upon a time there was a young girl called Lily. One day, while Lily was playing in her bedroom, she saw a new cushion lying on the floor. She was so excited she ran over and tried to catch it. "Catch, catch!" she shouted. Just then, her mum came in and said, "What do you have there, Lily?" "It's a new cushion," said Lily, with a big grin on her face. Her mum smiled and said, "What a lovely surprise. Let's put it on your bed so you can use it at night when you go to sleep." Lily was so happy that she hugged the new cushion and thanked her mum. Then, she curled up with it on her bed and snuggled up for a cozy sleep.
