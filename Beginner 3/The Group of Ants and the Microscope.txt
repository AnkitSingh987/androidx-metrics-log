Category: Beginner
Title: The Group of Ants and the Microscope

One day, a group of ants went on a march. They were very eager to find food for their family. They walked in a long line, one behind the other. As they marched, they found a big microscope. They did not know what it was. The ants were curious and wanted to see what they could find. They climbed up the microscope and looked through it. They saw pretty colors and shapes. The ants were happy and excited. They told their friends about the big microscope and marched back home with big smiles on their faces.

Anna likes to play with pebbles. She has many pebbles in her box. She has big pebbles and small pebbles. She has red pebbles and blue pebbles. She has shiny pebbles and dull pebbles. One day, Anna goes to the park with her mom. She sees a pond with ducks. She wants to feed the ducks. She asks her mom for some bread. Her mom says no. She says bread is not good for ducks. She says Anna can look at the ducks, but not touch them. Anna is sad. She wants to make the ducks happy. She thinks maybe the ducks like pebbles. She takes out a shiny pebble from her box. She throws it in the pond. The ducks swim away. They are scared. Anna does not understand. She thinks maybe the ducks like dull pebbles. She takes out a dull pebble from her box. She throws it in the pond. The ducks quack loudly. They are angry. Anna does not understand. Her mom sees what Anna is doing. She is not happy. She tells Anna to stop throwing pebbles. She says pebbles are not food for ducks. She says pebbles can hurt the ducks. She says Anna should be polite to the ducks. She says Anna should say sorry to the ducks. Anna feels bad. She does not want to hurt the ducks. She does not want to be rude to the ducks. She wants to be polite to the ducks. She says sorry to the ducks. She puts her pebbles back in her box. She waves to the ducks. The ducks calm down. They swim back to Anna. They nod to Anna. They forgive Anna. Anna smiles. She is happy. She learns a lesson. She says thank you to her mom. She says thank you to the ducks. She says goodbye to the ducks. She goes home with her mom. She plays with her pebbles. She is careful with her pebbles. She is polite to everyone.

One day, a clever cat named Tom wanted to travel. He put on his hat and said, "Bye, bed! I'm going to see the world!" Tom walked and walked until he met a dog named Max. "Hi, Max!" said Tom. "I'm traveling to see the world. Want to come with me?" Max wagged his tail and said, "Yes, Tom! Let's go!" So, Tom and Max walked and walked together. But then, something unexpected happened. They found a big, red ball! They played with the ball and had so much fun. They forgot about traveling. Tom and Max played all day and then went back to Tom's bed. They were happy to be friends and play together.

Sara and Tom were playing in the garden. They liked to run and jump and chase each other. They had a lot of fun, but they also felt very hot and tired. "Let's go inside and relax," Sara said. "We can drink some water and read a book." "OK," Tom agreed. "But can we also play with the knob?" The knob was a toy that Sara and Tom found in the garage. It was round and shiny and made a funny noise when they turned it. They did not know what it was for, but they liked to pretend it was a magic wand or a spaceship. They went inside and drank some water. Then they sat on the sofa and picked a book. It was a lovely book with pictures of animals and colors and shapes. Sara and Tom took turns to read the words and point at the pictures. "Look, this is a cat," Sara said. "It has fur and whiskers and a tail." "And this is a ball," Tom said. "It is red and round and bouncy." They read and played with the book and the knob. They felt happy and relaxed. They did not notice that their mom was watching them with a smile. She was glad that they had a good time and learned new things. She wondered where they got the knob from, but decided not to ask. She thought it was a lovely toy for them.
