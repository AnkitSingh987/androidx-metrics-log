Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl Finds the Cat

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a soft, small cat. The cat was sitting under a big tree. Lily wanted to play with the cat. She walked up to the cat and said, "Hello, cat! Do you want to play with me?" The cat looked at Lily and said, "Yes, I want to play. But I have a question. Can you find my toy? It is a small, red ball." Lily nodded her head and started to look for the red ball. Lily looked near the swings, but the ball was not there. She looked by the slide, but it was not there either. Finally, she found the red ball next to the sandbox. Lily picked it up and ran back to the cat. "Here is your red ball!" Lily said. The cat was very happy. They played together all day long. When it was time to go home, Lily said goodbye to the cat. The cat said, "Thank you for finding my ball and playing with me." Lily smiled and went home, excited to tell her mom about her new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a horse named Bob. Bob was a clear, pretty horse. He had many friends, like a dog, a cat, and a bird. They all lived on a big farm. One day, Bob and his friends played a game. They ran and jumped and had fun. But the dog was very fast, and Bob could not win. Bob felt sad and wanted to quit. Bob's friends saw that he was sad. They told him not to quit. They said, "Bob, you are a strong horse. You can do it!" Bob tried again, and this time he won. Bob and his friends were happy and played all day.

Once there was a boy named Tom. He had a toy model car. The car was red and fast. Tom liked to play with it every day. It was a very reliable toy because it never broke. One day, Tom went to the park with his model car. He saw a hill and thought it would be fun to raise the car up the hill. So, he pushed the car up and up until it reached the top of the hill. Then, Tom let the car go down the hill. It went very fast! Tom was happy to see his reliable model car race down the hill. He played with it all day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a daisy. This daisy was very strong. It lived in a big garden with many other flowers. The daisy liked to play with its friends. One day, the daisy decided to design a pretty picture. It asked its friends, the sun and the rain, to help. The sun shined bright and the rain made the garden wet. The daisy was very happy. In the end, the daisy made a beautiful picture with the help of its friends. Everyone in the garden loved it. The strong daisy felt proud of its design. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a smart cat named Tom. Tom had a fan. The fan could spin very fast. One day, Tom went for a walk with his fan. On his walk, Tom met a dog named Max. Max saw the fan and said, "Wow, what is that?" Tom replied, "This is my fan. It helps me stay cool when it's hot." Max thought the fan seemed fun and wanted to try it too. Tom let Max use the fan. Max was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tom! You are a good friend." Tom and Max played with the fan all day. They had lots of fun and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there were two friends called Tom and Lucy. They lived in a village and were always helping each other out. One day, a big storm arrived in their village. Tom and Lucy were worried and they both looked out the window to watch it. Lucy said, "This storm looks scary. What should we do?" Tom replied, "Let's stay here and be brave! We'll stay together and it will be okay." Tom was very compassionate and stayed with Lucy to make sure she was safe. They held hands and watched as the storm roared outside. Finally, the storm passed and they could relax. Tom turned to Lucy and smiled. "You were very brave today," he said. Lucy smiled back and replied, "So were you, Tom. We make a great team!" And from that day on, Tom and Lucy were the best of friends.
