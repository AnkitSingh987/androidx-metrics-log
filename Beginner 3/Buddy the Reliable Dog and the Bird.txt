Category: Beginner
Title: Buddy the Reliable Dog and the Bird

Once upon a time in a small forest, there was a reliable dog named Buddy. Buddy had four big paws that he used to help his friends. Every day, Buddy would listen and hear when his friends needed help. One day, Buddy heard a small bird cry. The bird was stuck in a tree. Buddy used his strong paws to shake the tree. The bird was free and happy. Buddy's friends knew they could always count on him. The moral of the story is to be like Buddy, be reliable and help your friends when they need you. If you listen and hear when others need help, you can use your own paws, or hands, to make a difference.

Tom and Mia were friends who liked to play together. One day, they had a plan to make a cake for Mia's mom. They found a big bowl, some flour, some eggs, some sugar, and some butter in the kitchen. They put everything in the bowl and mixed it with a spoon. "Let's put the bowl in the oven," Tom said. "Then the cake will be ready soon." "But we don't know how to use the oven," Mia said. "It is hot and dangerous. We should ask mom first." Tom did not listen to Mia. He wanted to surprise Mia's mom with the cake. He opened the oven and tried to put the bowl inside. But the bowl was too heavy and too hot for Tom. He dropped the bowl on the floor. The cake mix splashed everywhere. Tom and Mia were covered in flour, eggs, sugar, and butter. Mia's mom heard the noise and came to the kitchen. She saw the mess and the broken bowl. She was not happy. She scolded Tom and Mia for making a mess and playing with the oven. She told them to clean up and go to their rooms. Tom and Mia felt embarrassed. They said sorry to Mia's mom. They realized they should have listened to Mia and asked for help. They learned that it is not good to do things without permission or knowledge. They decided to make a card for Mia's mom instead of a cake. They hoped she would forgive them and like their card.

One day, a boy named Tom went to the park with his mom. He saw a big dog lying on a bench, holding a newspaper in his mouth. Tom loved dogs and wanted to pet the dog, but his mom said, "No, Tom, you have to mind the dog. He might not like it if you touch him or his newspaper. He might bite you or bark at you. You have to ask the owner first." Tom looked around and saw a man sitting on another bench, reading a book. He thought the man was the dog's owner, so he ran to him and said, "Hello, sir, can I pet your dog? He has a newspaper in his mouth. Is he reading it?" The man looked up and smiled. He said, "Sure, you can pet my dog. His name is Rex. He is very gentle and friendly. He likes to carry the newspaper in his mouth, but he doesn't read it. He just likes the smell and the sound of it. He is a very smart dog." Tom thanked the man and went to the bench where Rex was. He said, "Hello, Rex, you are a very smart dog. Can I pet you?" Rex wagged his tail and licked Tom's face. He let Tom pet him and play with him. He even gave Tom the newspaper to hold for a while. Tom was very happy and said, "You are a very nice dog, Rex. I like you a lot. Can I be your friend?" Rex barked and nodded. He liked Tom too. He was a very polite and kind boy. Tom's mom watched them and smiled. She was glad that Tom minded the dog and asked the owner first. She knew that some dogs might not be gentle or friendly, and that some owners might not like strangers to touch their dogs or their newspapers. She was proud of Tom for being respectful and careful. She said, "Tom, you did a very good thing. You made a new friend and learned something new. You are a very good boy." Tom hugged his mom and said, "Thank you, mom. I love you. And I love Rex too. He is a very good dog." Rex barked and wagged his tail. He loved Tom and his mom too. He was a very happy dog.
