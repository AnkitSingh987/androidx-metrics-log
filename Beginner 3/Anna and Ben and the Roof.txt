Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben and the Roof

Anna and Ben like to play in the park. They find a big pile of sticks and stones. They want to build a house with them. "Let's build a house here," Anna says. "We can make a door and a window and a roof." "OK," Ben says. "I will make the door and you make the window. We can use this patch of grass for the roof." They start to build their house. They use the sticks and stones to make the walls. They use the grass to make the roof. They are happy and proud of their work. But then a big dog comes. The dog is hungry and angry. He sees the house and thinks it is a toy. He runs to the house and starts to bite and scratch it. "Hey, stop it!" Anna shouts. "That is our house! Go away!" But the dog does not listen. He breaks the door and the window and the roof. He makes a big mess of the house. He ruins everything. Anna and Ben are sad and scared. They cry and run away. They leave their house behind. The dog is happy. He has a new toy. He plays with the sticks and stones and grass. He does not care that he destroyed an ancient house. He does not know what ancient means. He only knows how to bite and scratch.

One day, a little blue bird named Sky was flying to find her friend, Cat. Sky saw Cat near a big tree. "Hi Cat!" said Sky. "I want to play with you today!" Cat was happy and wanted to play too. They played a game where they would slap their hands together. But when Sky tried to slap Cat's hand, she missed and started to fall. Cat was scared and did not want Sky to get hurt. So, Cat thought of a plan. Cat quickly ran to a soft pile of leaves under the tree. "Fall here, Sky!" Cat yelled. Sky fell into the leaves and was safe. They both laughed at what happened. From that day on, they were more careful when they played together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lisa. Lisa wanted to play but she was confused. She couldn't find her toys. But then, her mommy said to her, "Let's unpack this stack of boxes." So that's what Lisa did. She opened the cardboard boxes and unpacked each one. She was so excited when she found lots of toys in the boxes. She put the toys in a stack on the floor and started playing with them. Lisa was so happy! She thanked her mommy for helping her to unpack the stack and not be feeling confused anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lucy. She wanted to marry a polite boy named Tom. They loved to visit their friends and play games. One day, they went to visit their friend, a big cat named Kitty. Kitty was very good at hiding. They played hide and seek, and Lucy and Tom looked for Kitty. They looked under the bed, and behind the door, but they could not find Kitty. Then, something unexpected happened. They heard a loud noise from the closet. They opened the door, and there was Kitty! But Kitty was not alone. She was with a nice dog named Max. Kitty said, "Surprise! I am going to marry Max!" Lucy and Tom were happy for Kitty and Max, and they all played games together.

Lila and Ben are twins. They like to play in the park with their mom and dad. One day, they see a big hill in the park. They want to climb the hill and see the view. "Can we go up the hill, mom?" Lila asks. "Sure, but be careful. The hill is steep and you might fall," mom says. Lila and Ben hold hands and start to climb the hill. They go slowly and help each other. They see flowers, rocks, and bugs on the way. They are happy and excited. They reach the top of the hill and look around. They see the park, the trees, the pond, and the sky. They see people, dogs, and birds. They see their mom and dad waving at them. "Wow, the view is so pretty!" Lila says. "Yes, it is. And we can see everything!" Ben says. They feel proud and happy. They have increased their view by climbing the hill. They sit on the grass and share a snack. They are generous and give some to a squirrel. The squirrel is happy and thanks them. They wave to their mom and dad and say, "We love you!" They love the hill, the view, and each other.
