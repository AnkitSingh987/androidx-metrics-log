Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the New

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. He had a big head with lots of ideas. Tim liked to insist on playing with his toys all day. He did not like to share with his friends. One day, a new kid came to town. His name was unknown to Tim. Tim saw the new kid playing with a toy that he had never seen before. Tim wanted to play with it too. Tim walked up to the new kid and said, "Let's play together." The new kid smiled and they played with the toy. Tim and the new kid became good friends. They shared their toys and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a small town. In this town, there was a big, strong fireman named Tom. Tom was a reliable helper. Everyone liked Tom because he was always there to help when they needed him. One day, a big fire started in a house. Thick smoke came out of the windows. The people in the town were scared. They knew they needed Tom's help. They ran to Tom and said, "Tom, please help us! The fire will destroy our house!" Tom quickly put on his fire hat and ran to the house with his big hose. Tom sprayed water on the fire and the smoke started to go away. The people watched and hoped that Tom could save their house. Finally, the fire was gone and the house was safe. The people in the town cheered for Tom. They knew they could always count on him to help them. And from that day on, Tom was known as the most reliable fireman in the town.

One day, a kind boy named Tim wanted to climb up to the attic. He asked his mom, "Can I go up to the attic?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Tim was excited to see what was up there. Tim climbed the stairs to the attic. It was dark and dusty. He saw an old box. He opened the box and found a big, funny hat. He put the hat on his head and laughed. Just then, the hat started to talk! It said, "Hi, I am a magic hat!" Tim was very surprised. The hat told Tim that it could make wishes come true. Tim was happy to have a new magic friend.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a helpful girl named Sue. Sue loved to help her friends and family. One day, she saw her friend, Tim, with a big bag on his head. Tim was trying to resist the bag from falling off. Sue went to Tim and said, "Tim, why do you have a bag on your head?" Tim replied, "I am trying to keep my head dry from the rain, but it is hard to resist the wind." Sue thought for a moment and had an idea. She ran to her house and brought back an umbrella. She gave the umbrella to Tim and said, "Here, use this to keep your head dry. It will help you resist the wind and rain." Tim smiled and thanked Sue for being so helpful. They both walked home together under the umbrella, happy to have each other as friends. From that day on, Tim never had to put a bag on his head again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved her doll named Lily. Sue and Lily played together every day. They were the best of friends. One day, Sue had an idea. "Let's print a picture of you, Lily!" she said. Sue put Lily on the table and got her hard crayons. She put a paper on top of Lily and started to color. Sue was very careful not to break the crayons. When Sue finished, she lifted the paper and saw a beautiful picture of Lily. "Look, Lily! Now we have a pretty picture of you!" Sue said. Lily looked at the picture and smiled. Sue and Lily were very happy. They hung the picture on the wall and played together all day.

One day, a little fish named Fin wanted to swim very fast. He had a task to find a cheap toy for his friend's birthday. Fin asked his mom, "Mom, can I go swim and find the toy?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful and don't swim too far." Fin swam and swam. He saw many toys, but they were not cheap. He felt sad. Then, he found a small, cheap toy. It was perfect for his friend. Fin swam back home and gave the toy to his friend. His friend was very happy. They learned that you don't need a big, fancy toy to make someone happy. A small, cheap toy can bring joy too.
