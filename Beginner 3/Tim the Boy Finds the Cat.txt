Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Cat

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a big ice-cream truck and wanted to have some ice-cream. His mom said yes, and they walked to the truck. Tim felt very happy. As Tim got his ice-cream, he saw a cat sitting on a bench. The cat looked uncomfortable. Tim wanted to help the cat, so he walked closer. When he got near, the cat jumped up and started to shout. Tim was very surprised. Then, something unexpected happened. The cat turned into a big, friendly dog. The dog wagged its tail and licked Tim's face. Tim laughed and shared his ice-cream with the dog. They became best friends and played together in the park all day.

One day, a fat cat went for a walk. The cat saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. The cat went up the tree and saw a bird. The bird said, "Why are you here, fat cat? You make me tremble!" The cat just looked at the bird and said, "I want to play." The bird did not want to play with the fat cat. The bird tried to fly away, but the cat grabbed the bird's zip. The bird was scared and said, "Please let me go!" The cat did not let the bird go. The bird tried to fly, but it could not. The cat was happy to have a new friend, but the bird was sad. In the end, the bird could not fly away, and the cat kept the bird with it forever.

It was a sunny day and Roxy woke up with a big smile on her face. She looked around her room and saw it was tidy. She always made sure her room was clean and tidy before she went to bed. Roxy jumped out of bed and ran out of the door. Her mum, Irene, was waiting in the kitchen. Roxy sat down in her chair, held her mum's hand and said, "Good morning!" Irene said, "Good morning, Roxy. Who can show me something special they brought with them today?" Roxy held up a small razor and said, "Look mum, I found this razor". Irene smiled and said, "That's a nice razor, Roxy. We'll have to be careful with it." Roxy nodded her head and Irene and Roxy enjoyed the rest of their breakfast. With the razor in her hand, Roxy ran out of the house to the garden to play with all the flowers and happy animals.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very hungry. His mom said, "Let's go to the market." Tim was happy. At the market, Tim saw many fruits. He saw red apples, yellow bananas, and green grapes. He wanted to eat them all. Tim's mom said, "You can choose one fruit." Tim picked the green grapes. When they got home, Tim and his mom played a game. They would throw the green grapes up in the air and try to catch them with their mouths. Tim laughed and laughed. He was not hungry anymore. And they all lived happily ever after.

Sam and Lily liked to play with the rope in the park. They took turns to swing on the rope and jump off. Sam was good at swinging high and landing on his feet. Lily was good at making funny faces and noises when she swung. One day, a big boy came to the park. He saw the rope and wanted to play with it. He pushed Sam and Lily away and grabbed the rope. He said, "This is my rope now. You can't have it. Go away." Sam and Lily were sad and angry. They wanted their rope back. They waited for the big boy to swing and jump. When he did, they ran to the rope and pulled it hard. The big boy lost his grip and dropped to the ground. He cried out in pain. Sam and Lily said, "That's what you get for being harsh and mean. This is our rope. You can't take it. Go away." The big boy got up and ran away. He was scared and sorry. Sam and Lily hugged each other and smiled. They swung on the rope and jumped off. They had fun and shared. They were happy and kind.
