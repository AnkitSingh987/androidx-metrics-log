Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and the Toys

One day, Tom and his mom were in the kitchen. Tom's mom let out a big sigh because the kitchen was very messy. She wanted the kitchen to be tidy and clean. Tom saw his mom was sad and wanted to help her. Tom said, "Mom, I will help you make the kitchen tidy." His mom was happy and said, "Thank you, Tom." They started to clean the kitchen together. Tom's mom used a knife to cut some fruit for a snack while Tom put away the toys. After they finished, the kitchen was tidy and clean. Tom's mom was so happy and gave Tom a big hug. They sat down together and enjoyed their snack. Tom felt proud that he helped his mom and made her happy.

Once upon a time, there was an honest cat named Sam. Sam lived in a small cell made of wood. He liked to play with his sack all day long. One day, Sam went to the park with his sack. He saw a big tree and thought it would be fun to put the sack on a branch. But when he tried to get the sack back, it was stuck! Sam was very sad. Then, a friendly bird came and asked Sam why he was sad. Sam told the bird about his sack. To Sam's surprise, the bird was able to get the sack down from the tree. Sam was so happy! From that day on, Sam and the bird became best friends and played with the sack together.

Lily and Tom were playing in the park. They saw a dove on a tree. The dove was white and pretty. Lily wanted to touch the dove. She ran to the tree and tried to climb it. "Stop, Lily!" Tom shouted. "You will scare the dove. It will fly away." But Lily did not listen. She wanted to touch the dove. She reached for the dove with her hand. The dove saw Lily and was afraid. It flapped its wings and flew away. Lily was sad. She wanted to touch the dove. She climbed down the tree and cried. Tom felt sorry for Lily. He went to her and hugged her. "I'm sorry, Lily. I know you liked the dove. But the dove was scared of you. You have to be gentle with animals. They are not toys." Lily looked at Tom. She wiped her tears. She understood what Tom said. "I'm sorry, Tom. You are right. I was not gentle with the dove. I was mean. I will be gentle next time." Tom smiled. He was happy that Lily learned her lesson. He saw another dove on another tree. He pointed to it. "Look, Lily. There is another dove. Maybe it is the dove's friend. Do you want to see it?" Lily nodded. She wanted to see the dove. But she did not run to the tree. She walked slowly and quietly. She did not try to touch the dove. She just looked at it. The dove looked at her too. It did not fly away. It cooed softly. Lily was happy. She smiled at the dove. The dove smiled back. Lily felt grateful. She thanked the dove for staying. "Thank you, dove. You are very nice. I'm sorry I scared your friend. I hope you are happy." The dove cooed again. It was happy too. It thanked Lily for being gentle. "Thank you, Lily. You are very kind. I'm sorry I flew away. I hope you are happy too." Lily and Tom were happy. They watched the dove for a while. Then they waved goodbye and went to play some more. They were good friends with each other and with the dove.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little girl named Lily. She loved to visit her grandma's garden. In the garden, there were many things to see and touch. Lily liked to look at the pretty flowers and the big green leaves. One day, Lily saw a big red tomato on a plant. She wanted to pick it and show it to her grandma. But there was a problem. A small, angry bird lived in the garden too. The bird did not like anyone to touch the plants. Lily had an idea. She found a big stick and tried to get the tomato with it. The bird saw her and got very mad. It flew around Lily, trying to stop her. But Lily was fast. She got the tomato and ran to her grandma. The bird was not happy, but Lily was very proud. She had the big red tomato, and her grandma was happy too.
