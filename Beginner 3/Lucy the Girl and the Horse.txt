Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Horse

One day, a brave girl named Lucy went to the park. She saw a big statue of a man on a horse. Lucy wanted to climb the statue, but her friend Tom said, "No, it's too high!" Lucy didn't listen. She started to climb the statue. When she was at the top, she grabbed the horse's nose. Suddenly, the statue moved! The horse came to life and started to walk. Lucy was scared but held on tight. The horse walked around the park with Lucy on its back. Tom yelled, "Wow, you are so brave!" The horse stopped, and Lucy got down. She hugged the horse and said, "Thank you for the fun ride!" The statue turned back to stone, and Lucy and Tom went home to tell everyone their amazing story.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely girl named Lily. She loved to hold her soft, pretty blanket. The blanket had a fun pattern with many colors. Lily took her blanket everywhere she went. One day, Lily and her mom went to the park. Lily held her blanket while she played with her friends. They played on the swings, slide, and in the sand. Lily's blanket made her feel happy and safe. As the sun began to set, Lily and her mom went home. Lily held her lovely blanket with the fun pattern close to her. That night, Lily slept with her blanket and had sweet dreams.

Once upon a time, there was a small, harmless cat named Fluffy. Fluffy had a big tooth. She liked to play with her friends in the park. One day, Fluffy saw a big ball. She wanted to play with it. Fluffy went close to the ball and it seemed very fun. As Fluffy played with the ball, it started to move on its own. Fluffy was scared. The ball seemed to have a secret. Fluffy didn't know what was happening. She ran to her friends for help. Her friends came to see the ball. They found out the ball was not just a ball. It was a big, round bug! The bug was not bad. It just wanted to play too. Fluffy and her friends were happy. They all played together with the big, round bug. The day was fun and full of surprises.

One day, a little girl got a surprise in the mail. It was a very special present. The girl was so excited and couldn’t wait to open it! She held it close and gently cleaned the gift before unwrapping it. Inside the box, the girl found a small, gentle puppy. She was over the moon! She petted him, and the puppy licked her face. She could tell that he was going to be a great friend. The girl looked around and spotted a big bucket. She decided to fill it with water to give her new puppy a bath. He was very scared at first, but the girl was so gentle and comforting. Soon, the puppy was happily swimming around and playing with all the bubbles in the water. The small girl and her pup are now best friends. She loves giving him baths and playing with him every day. All because of the special surprise gift she received in the mail!

Tom and Mia love to play soccer in the park. They have a big ball and a small goal. They take turns to kick the ball and try to score. They have a lot of fun. One day, a big dog comes to the park. It sees the ball and runs after it. It grabs the ball with its teeth and shakes it hard. The ball pops and makes a loud noise. The dog drops the ball and barks. Tom and Mia panic. They are scared of the dog and the noise. They run to their mom, who is sitting on a bench. They hug her and cry. "Mom, mom, the dog broke our ball!" Tom says. "Mom, mom, the dog is scary!" Mia says. Mom hugs them and tries to calm them down. She looks at the dog and sees that it is wearing a collar. She sees a man running towards the dog. He is the dog's owner. "Hey, I'm sorry, my dog likes to chase balls. He didn't mean to pop yours. Are you OK?" the man says. He picks up the ball and the dog. He comes to the bench and gives the ball to Mom. He smiles and says sorry again. Mom looks at the ball and sees that it is very flat. She sees that it has a picture of a beautiful flower on it. She likes the flower. She smiles and says thank you. She gives the ball to Tom and Mia. She tells them that the dog is not bad, he is just playful. She tells them that they can still play with the ball, even if it is flat. She tells them that they can pretend that the ball is a frisbee or a hat or a pizza. Tom and Mia stop crying. They look at the ball and the dog. They see that the dog is wagging his tail and licking his owner. They see that the ball is still colorful and has a beautiful flower on it. They smile and say thank you. They take the ball and go back to the goal. They try to throw the ball like a frisbee and catch it. They have fun again. The dog watches them and wags his tail. He likes them. He wants to play too.
