Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Ben and the Tree

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play in the park with their mom. One day, they see a big tree with red fruits. Mom says they are apples and they can pick some. Sara and Ben run to the tree and reach for the apples. But they are too high. They jump and stretch, but they cannot touch them. They feel helpless. "Mom, help us!" they cry. Mom smiles and nods. She picks up a long stick and shakes the tree. Some apples fall to the ground. Sara and Ben clap and cheer. They pick up the apples and thank mom. They bite into the apples and make a face. The apples are not sweet. They are sour and hard. They spit them out. "Mom, these are not good apples!" they complain. Mom laughs and says, "These are not apples, they are crab apples. They are not for eating. They are for making gum." "Gum?" Sara and Ben ask. "Yes, gum. You can boil them and add sugar and make a sticky candy. Do you want to try?" mom asks. Sara and Ben nod. They like candy. They follow mom to the kitchen and help her make gum. They stir and pour and cut and wrap. They make many pieces of gum. They taste the gum and smile. The gum is sweet and soft. They chew and blow bubbles. They have fun. They share the gum with their friends and tell them the story of the crab apples. They are not helpless anymore. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy loved to pick flowers in the park. One day, she saw a nosy squirrel running around the trees. Lucy wanted to play with the squirrel, so she picked a flower and tried to give it to the squirrel. But the nosy squirrel was scared and ran away. Lucy felt sad. Later, Lucy saw the squirrel again but this time, it had a nut. Lucy had an idea! She picked up a nut too and gave it to the squirrel. The squirrel was happy and took the nut from Lucy. They became friends and Lucy scored a new friend in the park.

One day, a little boy named Tim went with his mom to a big place called university. His mom had to talk to a teacher there. Tim saw a big bell in the middle of the grass. Tim asked his mom, "Can I ring the bell?" His mom said, "No, Tim. That bell is only for special times." Tim was a dependable boy, so he waited for his mom. But then, he saw a cat. The cat ran up to the bell and started to play with the rope. The cat pulled the rope and the bell went "ring, ring, ring!" Everyone looked at the bell and then at Tim. They thought Tim rang the bell. Tim said, "It was not me! The cat did it!" But no one saw the cat. The cat had run away. Tim's mom came back and said, "I believe you, Tim. You are a dependable boy." Tim felt happy that his mom believed him. And they went home together, laughing about the sneaky cat.

One day, a girl named Lily found a heavy box. The box had a message on it. The message said, "Find the match to open." Lily wanted to see what was inside the box. She started to look for the match. Lily looked in her room, under her bed, and in the kitchen. She could not find the match. She asked her mom for help. Her mom said, "Look in the yard." Lily went outside and looked in the yard. She found the match under a big tree. Lily was very happy. She took the match to the heavy box and opened it. Inside the box was a big, scary monster. The monster jumped out and scared Lily. She cried and ran away. The monster stayed in her house, and Lily was too scared to go back inside.

Once upon a time, there was a cat named Jazz. Jazz was a very anxious cat. He always worried about everything. One day, Jazz met a dog named Spot. Spot was a happy dog and loved to play. "Hi Jazz," said Spot. "Do you want to play with me?" Jazz was scared, but he wanted to try. "Okay, I will play with you," said Jazz. Spot was very happy and wanted to show Jazz how to play catch. Spot showed Jazz how to catch a ball. Jazz was not good at first, but he tried and tried. Soon, Jazz got better at catching the ball. Spot and Jazz played catch all day. They had so much fun. At the end of the day, Jazz was not anxious anymore. He had learned how to play catch and made a new friend. Jazz and Spot played together every day, and Jazz was a happy cat.
