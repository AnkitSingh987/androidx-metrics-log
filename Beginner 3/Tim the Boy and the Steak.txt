Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Steak

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy named Mr. Steak. Mr. Steak was a fragile toy, so Tim was very careful when he played with it. One day, Tim had a question. He asked his mom, "Can I take Mr. Steak to the park?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful. Mr. Steak is fragile and might break." So Tim took Mr. Steak to the park to play. They had lots of fun, but then something unexpected happened! A big dog saw Mr. Steak and thought it was a real steak. The dog took Mr. Steak and ran away! Tim was very sad and didn't know what to do. He asked his mom for help. Together, they found the dog and got Mr. Steak back. Tim was so happy, and from that day on, he made sure to keep Mr. Steak safe at home.

One day, a little cricket wanted to play. He was a healthy cricket who loved to jump and balance on things. He liked to make friends and have fun. The cricket found a big rock to balance on. He jumped up and down, having a great time. Then, something unexpected happened. A big bird came down and picked up the rock with the cricket on it! The bird took the rock and the cricket high up into the sky. The cricket was scared, but he held on tight. The bird dropped the rock into a soft, green field. The cricket looked around and saw many new friends. He was happy that the bird had brought him to a new place. The cricket learned that sometimes unexpected things can be good.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to build with his blue toy blocks. One day, Tim wanted to build a very tall tower with his blocks. He wanted it to be the biggest tower ever. As Tim started to build, he noticed that some of his blocks were thin. He thought it would be okay to use the thin blocks in his tower. Tim's tower grew taller and taller. He was very happy with his tower. But then, something unexpected happened. The thin blocks could not hold the tower up any longer. The tower fell down with a big crash! Tim was sad, but he learned that it is important to use strong blocks when building. The next time, Tim built his tower with strong blocks, and it stood tall and proud.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a fake toy lion. He liked to play with it a lot. The lion was big and had a long point on its tail. One day, Tim wanted to scare his friend, Sue. He took his fake lion and went to Sue's house. Tim hid behind a tree and waited for Sue to come out. When Sue came out, Tim jumped out with his fake lion and said, "Roar!" Sue was scared at first, but then she saw it was just a toy. They both laughed and played with the lion together. And they had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a shy man. He had a big mustache. The man did not like to speak. He lived in a small house with his cat. One day, the shy man went to the park. He saw a big tree. The tree was very tall. The man wanted to climb the tree. But he was scared. The man tried to climb the tree. He fell down. The man hurt his mustache. He could not speak. The cat was sad. The man did not feel good. The end.

Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to pretend they were brave hunters. They had sticks that they called spears. They threw them at the bushes and the flowers. "Look, I got a big lion!" Lily shouted, pointing at a yellow flower. "I got a bigger one!" Ben said, throwing his spear at a red bush. They were having fun, but they did not see that they were disturbing someone. Behind the fence, there was a grumpy old man. He did not like noise. He did not like children. He did not like spears. "Hey, you!" he yelled, poking his head over the fence. "Stop throwing those things at my plants! You are ruining them!" Lily and Ben were scared. They did not know the old man. They did not know he had plants. They thought they were just playing. "Sorry, sir," Lily said, picking up her spear. "We did not mean to disturb you." "Sorry, sir," Ben said, picking up his spear too. "We did not know they were your plants." The old man was still grumpy. He did not accept their apology. He did not like their spears. "Go away!" he said, waving his hand. "And take your spears with you! They are dangerous! You could hurt someone!" Lily and Ben ran away. They did not want to play in the garden anymore. They did not want to see the old man again. They did not like his plants. They did not like his grumpiness. They went to the park instead. They found a new place to play. They found new things to hunt. They had fun again. They forgot about the old man. They forgot about the spears. They forgot about the disturbance.
