Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Bird and the Wind

One day, a small bird named Tim was in his nest. The wind blew hard. Tim was not fit and strong. He tried to fly, but the wind pushed him back. Tim was sad and asked his mom for help. His mom said, "You must answer my question to become fit and strong. What do you need to do?" Tim thought and thought. He knew the answer. "I need to eat good food and practice flying," said Tim. His mom smiled and said, "Yes, that is right." Tim ate good food and practiced flying every day. Soon, he became fit and strong. The wind blew hard again, but this time, Tim could fly. He was so happy that he could fly with the wind. Tim had won the fight with the wind.

Once upon a time, there was a spoiled boy. His mommy and daddy loved him very much and gave him whatever he wanted. One day, the boy heard about marriage and he wanted to marry his mommy and daddy. So he found a special book that described how to do it. But before he could find out the steps, he heard his mommy coming and he quickly put the book away. The next day, the family was at the store shopping for groceries. The boy saw the book and ran off with it! His mommy and daddy started to chase him, but the boy was too fast. He ran and ran until he could escape. The boy smiled and never told his mommy and daddy about the book. He kept it a secret because he knew that marriage was not a game!

Lily wanted to help Mommy make cookies. She liked to mix the flour and the sugar and the eggs. She liked to make shapes with the dough and put them on the tray. She liked to smell the cookies when they came out of the stove. But today, Mommy was grumpy. She had a lot of work to do and she did not have time to play with Lily. She told Lily to go to her room and read a book. Lily did not want to read a book. She wanted to make cookies. She felt sad and angry. She decided to sneak into the kitchen and make cookies by herself. She found the flour and the sugar and the eggs. She poured them into a big bowl and stirred them with a spoon. She did not measure anything. She did not care. She just wanted to make cookies. She put some dough on the tray and put it in the stove. She did not know how long to wait. She did not set the timer. She did not care. She just wanted to make cookies. Soon, she smelled something bad. She opened the stove and saw smoke. The cookies were burned. They were black and hard and ugly. She started to cry. Mommy heard the noise and came into the kitchen. She saw the mess and the smoke and the burned cookies. She was very angry. She yelled at Lily. "Lily, what have you done? You could have started a fire! You could have hurt yourself! You know you are not allowed to touch the stove! Go to your room right now!" Lily looked at Mommy. Mommy looked at Lily. They both frowned. They both felt sorry. They both wanted to hug. But they did not. They just walked away.

One day, a boy went to play in the park. He saw a big tree with a door. The door was not a regular door. It was a magic door. The boy opened the door and went inside. Inside the tree, he found a giant. The giant said, "Hello, little boy. Do you want to play with me?" The boy was not scared. He said, "Yes, I want to play with you." They played with big toys and had fun. The giant was very nice. Then, the giant said, "I have to leave now. But before I go, I want to give you a gift." The giant gave the boy a small seed. The boy took the seed home and planted it. The seed grew into a big tree with a regular door. The boy learned that even giants can be kind and that we should not be scared of those who are different from us.
