Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Gray Cat and the Bird

One day, a gray cat named Tom saw a big tree. He wanted to climb the tree. Tom started to climb up, up, up. He saw a bird in the tree. The bird said, "Hi Tom! What are you doing?" Tom said, "I want to play. Can you play with me?" The bird said, "I can play, but I have to delay. I need to find food for my babies first." Tom said, "Okay, I will wait." While Tom waited, he saw a wink from a hole in the tree. He looked closer and saw a squirrel. The squirrel said, "I can play with you while the bird is away!" So Tom and the squirrel played together, and they had lots of fun. When the bird came back, they all played together. The bird's delay made Tom find a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that he loved to play with. One day, while playing with his toy car, it got stuck under the big chair. Tim tried to get it out, but it was too far away. Then, a tiny bug appeared near Tim. The bug saw Tim was sad and wanted to help. So, the bug went under the chair and gave the car a little rub. The car moved a bit, but it was still stuck. The bug tried again and again, but the car wouldn't come out. Tim knew the bug was trying hard, but he still felt bad. Then, he had an idea. He got a long stick and used it to reach the car. With the help of the bug and the stick, they finally got the car out. Tim was happy and thanked the bug. They became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a tiny, delicate star in the sky. The star was so small that it was hard to see. One day, a big, mean cloud came and wanted to destroy the star. The cloud said, "I am going to destroy you, little star!" The star was very sad and scared. It did not want to be destroyed. The star needed help to save itself from the mean cloud. A kind wind saw the star and the cloud. The wind wanted to help the star. So, the wind blew the mean cloud far away. The delicate star was safe and happy. The star said, "Thank you, kind wind!" And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a pink bird named Lily lived near a small volcano. Lily loved to splash in the water. She had many friends who liked to play with her. They would all splash together, laughing and having fun. One day, while they were playing, a new bird came by. He was blue and shy. "Hi, I'm Tim," said the blue bird. "Can I play with you?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, come splash with us!" Tim was so happy to make new friends. As they played, the small volcano started to shake. The birds got scared and flew away. But Lily and Tim stayed to help each other. They learned that being brave and kind is important. And from that day on, they all played together, splashing and having fun near the small volcano.

Once upon a time there was a boy named Jack. He loved to explore and play around in the park. One day he was playing alone when he heard a voice calling out his name. Jack looked around and saw a little dog. The dog barked, “Come and meet me!” Jack was excited to meet the little dog and started running towards him. As he was running, he stumbled and stubbed his toe on a rock. Jack was in lots of pain. He cried and the little dog quickly ran over and licked his face. Jack asked, “Why did you help me?” The little dog answered, “That’s what friends do. They help each other out when one is in need.” Jack understood that regular helping and kindness to each other was the right thing to do. He thanked the little dog for teaching him the lesson. From then on Jack shared his kindness with friends and family. He made sure he always did the right thing and enjoyed the company of others.
