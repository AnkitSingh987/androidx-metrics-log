Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Tom Find the Rabbit

Lily and Tom were friends. They liked to play in the forest near their home. They saw many animals and plants in the forest. They had fun. One day, they found a big hole in the ground. They were curious. They wanted to see what was inside. They looked at each other and smiled. They were excited. They went closer to the hole and peered inside. It was dark and deep. They heard a noise. It sounded like a cry. They were scared. They saw a small furry animal in the hole. It was a rabbit. It was trapped. It could not get out. It was sad. Lily and Tom felt sorry for the rabbit. They wanted to help. They looked around and found a long stick. They lowered the stick into the hole. They hoped the rabbit would climb on it. The rabbit saw the stick. It was happy. It jumped on the stick and held on tight. Lily and Tom pulled the stick up. They lifted the rabbit out of the hole. They were glad. The rabbit thanked Lily and Tom. It was grateful. It licked their faces and hopped away. Lily and Tom waved goodbye. They were proud. They decided to go back home. They had a new story to tell. They were friends. They were happy.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with toys and books. One day, they are invited to a party at Tom's house. Tom has many toys and books. He also has a big cake and balloons. At the party, Lily and Ben see a light toy. It is a star that changes colors. They both want to play with it. They run to the toy and grab it. They pull and pull. They do not share. "Let go! It is mine!" Lily says. "No, it is mine!" Ben says. They are angry and sad. They do not have fun. Tom sees them and comes to them. He has a smile on his face. He says, "Why are you fighting? This is a party. We can all play with the toy. We can share." He takes the toy and shows them how it works. He says, "Look, it has a button. If you press it, it changes colors. It is fun. Do you want to try?" Lily and Ben nod. They say, "Yes, please." Tom gives the toy to Lily. He says, "You can play with it first. Then you can give it to Ben. Then he can give it to me. We can take turns. That is sharing." Lily presses the button. The star turns red, then blue, then green. She says, "Wow, it is so light and pretty. Thank you, Tom." She gives the toy to Ben. He presses the button. The star turns yellow, then pink, then purple. He says, "Wow, it is so light and cool. Thank you, Tom." He gives the toy to Tom. He presses the button. The star turns white, then rainbow, then glitter. He says, "Wow, it is so light and amazing. Thank you, Lily and Ben." They all laugh and clap. They are happy and friendly. They have fun. They learn that sharing is good. Sharing makes everyone happy. Sharing is fun.

Once upon a time there was a small baby who was very difficult to look after. He wanted to be spoiled everyday! One day, his Mommy and Daddy took him to a very fine park. They took him to the edge of a very large pit. The baby looked down, eager to go and roll in the grass and play, but he was very scared. Mommy said, “Don't worry, we will go down the pit together. It's going to be fun!” The baby was so excited and ran ahead. He felt so brave when he reached the bottom of the pit, surrounded by all the tall grass and flowers. He laughed and splashed in the muddy puddles until they could not spoil him anymore. His Mommy and Daddy smiled, happy that their baby was having so much fun. They all had a very fine day in the park!

Once upon a time, there was a brown squirrel named Sam. Sam loved peanuts. He would find peanuts and eat them all day. Sam had many friends in the forest. One day, Sam found a big peanut. It was the biggest peanut he had ever seen. Sam's friend, Tim the rabbit, saw the big peanut. Tim asked Sam, "Can you lend me the peanut? I am very hungry." Sam thought about it but said, "No, I want this peanut for myself." Tim was sad, but he went away to find food. Later, Sam tried to eat the big peanut. But it was too big for Sam to open. He needed help. Sam went to his friends, but they all said no because Sam did not lend the peanut to Tim. In the end, Sam could not eat the big peanut and stayed hungry. The moral of the story is: if you do not help others, they will not help you.
