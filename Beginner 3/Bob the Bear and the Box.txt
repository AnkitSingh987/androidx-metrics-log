Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Bear and the Box

One day, a big bear named Bob found an enormous box. He wanted to pack his things in it. He put his toys, his hat, and his big ball inside. But he could not find his favorite napkin. He looked everywhere. Bob asked his friend, a small bird named Tim, "Did you see my napkin?" Tim said, "Yes, it is in the tree. I saw it when I was flying." Bob was happy. He climbed the tree and got his napkin. He put it in the box with his other things. Bob and Tim played all day. They had so much fun. When it was time to go home, Bob said, "Thank you, Tim, for helping me find my napkin." Tim said, "You're welcome, Bob. Let's play again tomorrow!" They both smiled and said goodbye.

Once upon a time, there was a big, scary lion. He lived in a dark forest. The lion was always hungry and had a big stomach. He wanted to find food to eat. One day, a little bird came to the lion. The bird saw that the lion was sad. The bird wanted to help the lion. The bird said, "I can provide food for you, Mr. Lion. Follow me." The lion followed the bird. They found a big tree with lots of fruit. The lion ate the fruit and his stomach was full. He was not scary anymore. The lion and the bird became good friends. They lived happily in the forest.

Once upon a time, there was a lively boy called Joe. One day, Joe was playing with his truck, when the handle came off in his hands. He looked up at his mum and said, “Mum, the handle slipped.” Mum smiled and said, “That's okay Joe. Can you find another handle?” Joe ran off, looking in the cupboards and all around the house. He eventually found a new handle in the garden. He ran up to his mum and excitedly said, “I found a handle mum!” His mum smiled and said, “Good job Joe. Let's get it fixed on your truck.” Joe and his mum worked together to get the handle firmly attached onto the truck. Joe was so pleased that the truck was back to being what it should be - lively!

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a chubby cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play with her toys and chase her tail. She also liked to drink milk from her favorite mug. One day, Fluffy saw a big shadow on the wall. She thought it was a monster and it scared her. Fluffy hid under the bed and waited for the monster to go away. She was very scared and held her mug close to her. After a while, Fluffy peeked out from under the bed. She saw that the monster was just her toy mouse. The sun was shining on the toy, making a big shadow on the wall. Fluffy laughed and was not scared anymore. She drank her milk from the mug and played with her toy mouse all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play in his backyard with his dog, Spot. They would run and jump and have lots of fun. One day, Tim had a big, stupid idea. He thought, "I want to fly like a bird!" So, he found a big box and tried to make it into a plane. He put wings on it and even drew a picture of a bird on the side. Tim and Spot tried to fly in the backyard. They ran as fast as they could, but the box did not fly. They fell and laughed. Tim said, "I think we can't fly, but we can still have fun!" And they played in the backyard all day long.

One day, a sharp cat saw a big mug. The mug was very pretty. The sharp cat wanted the mug. She was envious of anyone who had the mug. The cat thought of a plan to get the mug. The sharp cat went to the dog. The dog had the big mug. The cat said, "Dog, I want the mug." The dog said, "No, it is my mug." The cat was sad but she did not give up. The sharp cat thought of a new plan. She went to the dog again. She said, "Dog, let's share the big mug." The dog thought about it and said, "Okay, we can share the mug." Now, the sharp cat was happy. They both shared the big mug and were friends.
