Category: Beginner
Title: Lisa the Lady and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little lady named Lisa. She lived in a small house near a big tree. Lisa loved to play outside with her friends, the birds and the squirrels. One hot day, Lisa went outside to play. She saw a thirsty bird sitting on a branch. The bird said, "I am so thirsty, can you help me find water to drink?" Lisa replied, "Yes, I can help you!" Lisa and the bird walked to a small pond. The bird drank the water and said, "Thank you, Lisa! You are so kind." Lisa felt happy that she could help her friend. They played together all day long and had lots of fun.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play outside in the sun. One day, they see a big hill near their house. They want to climb the hill and see the view. They ask their mom if they can go. Mom says yes, but they have to be careful and stay together. She gives them a bottle of water and a hat each. Lily and Ben are happy. They say thank you and run to the hill. The hill is steep and green. They see flowers and bugs and birds. They hold hands and help each other. They are tired, but they keep going. They want to reach the top. They make it to the top. They are very proud. They sit on a rock and drink some water. They look around. They see their house, the road, the trees, the sky. The view is bright and beautiful. They smile and hug. They hear their mom calling them. It is time to go home. They wave and say bye to the view. They slide and roll down the hill. They are dirty and happy. They run to their mom and tell her what happened. Mom is glad. She hugs them and takes them home.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a popular toy, a big marble. Tim loved to play with his marble every day. He believed it was magic. One day, Tim was playing with his marble outside. Suddenly, it rolled away and went into a hole. Tim was sad and didn't know what to do. He believed his magic marble was lost forever. But then, something unexpected happened. A tiny mouse came out of the hole with the marble. The mouse gave the marble back to Tim. Tim was so happy and thanked the mouse. From that day on, Tim and the mouse became best friends.

Max was designing the perfect pizza. He had chosen all the right ingredients, but needed the right shape. So far, he hadn't been able to figure out what to do. It was like an unknown puzzle he had to solve. Max got really frustrated and he started to feel like he was never going to get it right. He wanted it to be perfect! Max decided to just take a break and come back to it later. He ate some food and went outside to play. Suddenly, he had an idea! He raced back inside and got to work. He used the ingredients to make a giant spiral. Max was so proud of his design. For once, his pizza was the best he had ever made. Max had conquered the unknown and won!

Once upon a time, there was a pretty lady named Sue. She had a big spear. Sue liked to mind her own things. One day, while she was walking, she saw a mean dog. The dog wanted to take her spear. Sue did not like this. She wanted to keep her spear, but the dog was very big. Sue was scared and did not know what to do. Then, a nice man named Tom came to help Sue. He talked to the dog and made it go away. Sue was happy and thanked Tom. They became good friends and Sue kept her spear.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a lazy boy named Tim. He did not like to work or play. He just liked to sleep and eat all day. Tim had a big rock that he liked to sit on and watch the other kids play. One day, Tim saw a long spear near the rock. He thought it would be fun to play with the spear. He picked it up and started to swing it around. The other kids saw Tim and thought it looked fun too. They all wanted to play with the spear. But Tim did not know how to use a spear. He swung it too hard and too fast. He hit a tree and the spear broke. The broken spear fell on the ground and Tim tripped on it. He fell on the rock and hurt himself. The other kids were sad because they could not play with the spear anymore, and Tim was sad because he was hurt.
