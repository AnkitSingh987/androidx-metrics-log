Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Garden

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to help Mom in the garden. Mom grows many herbs in the garden. Herbs are plants that smell good and make food tasty. Mom says herbs are healthy for us. One day, Lily and Ben see a big basket of herbs on the table. They want to play with them. They take some herbs and run outside. They pretend the herbs are toys. They throw them in the air, make them into hats, and rub them on their faces. Mom sees them and says, "Stop! What are you doing with my herbs? You are spoiling them! They are not toys, they are food. I need them for dinner. You are wasting them and making a mess." Lily and Ben feel sorry. They did not know they were spoiling the herbs. They say, "We are sorry, Mom. We just wanted to have fun. We did not mean to spoil the herbs. Can we help you clean them?" Mom smiles and says, "It's okay, I know you did not mean to spoil them. But you have to be careful with herbs. They are delicate and precious. They need to be washed and dried and stored properly. You can help me clean them and put them in jars. Then we can use them for dinner and enjoy their flavor and health benefits." Lily and Ben nod and say, "Yes, Mom. We will help you. We want to learn more about herbs. We want to eat healthy food with you." They hug Mom and go to the kitchen. They wash the herbs, dry them, and put them in jars. They label the jars with the names of the herbs. They are proud of their work. They can't wait to taste the herbs in dinner.

Anna and Ben are playing in the living room. They see a big white magazine on the table. It has many pictures of animals and flowers. "Look, Ben, a magazine!" Anna says. "Let's look at it." "Okay, Anna," Ben says. "But we have to be careful. It is Mommy's magazine. She likes to read it." They sit on the floor and open the magazine. They see a picture of a white rabbit. It has long ears and a fluffy tail. "Wow, a rabbit!" Anna says. "It is so cute. I want to pet it." "Me too," Ben says. "But we can't. It is only a picture. Mommy says we can't touch the pictures. They can tear." They turn the page and see a picture of a white cake. It has pink frosting and candles on it. "Yum, a cake!" Ben says. "It looks delicious. I want to eat it." "Me too," Anna says. "But we can't. It is only a picture. Mommy says we can't eat the pictures. They are not food." They hear Mommy coming from the kitchen. She is holding a tray with cups and plates. "Hi, kids," Mommy says. "I have a surprise for you. I made some cookies and milk. Do you want some?" "Yes, please, Mommy!" Anna and Ben say. They close the magazine and put it back on the table. They run to Mommy and hug her. "Thank you, Mommy!" they say. "You are the best!" Mommy smiles and kisses them. She puts the tray on the floor and sits with them. "You are welcome, kids," Mommy says. "I love you. Now, let's enjoy our snack. I prepared it for you."

One day, a little infant was playing outside. He was a fearful child and did not like to go far from his home. He saw a big bag with a zip on it. The infant was curious and wanted to see what was inside. The infant went to the bag and tried to open the zip. He heard a voice say, "Don't open the bag!" The infant looked around and saw a big cat. The cat looked scared too. "It is not safe," said the cat. But the infant did not listen. He opened the zip and out came a big, scary monster. The monster chased the infant and the cat. They tried to run away, but the monster was too fast. The monster caught them, and they were never seen again. The infant should have listened to the cat.
