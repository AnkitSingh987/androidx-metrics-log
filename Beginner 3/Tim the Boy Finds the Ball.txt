Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a kind boy named Tim. He had a cute puppy named Spot. They liked to play together all day. One day, they went to the park to play with a ball. Tim said, "Spot, I will put the ball here, and you go get it!" Tim threw the ball far away. Spot ran fast to get the ball. But when Spot got to the ball, he saw another puppy there. The other puppy said, "Hi, I am Luna. Can I play with you?" Spot was happy and said, "Yes, let's play together!" They played with the ball and had lots of fun. When it was time to go home, Tim called Spot. Spot came back with Luna. Tim was surprised to see another puppy. He said, "Who is this?" Spot said, "This is Luna, my new friend. Can she come home with us?" Tim thought for a moment and said, "Yes, Luna can come home with us. We will be one big, happy family!" And so, Tim, Spot, and Luna went home together, and they all lived happily ever after.

Tom and Anna are friends. They like to play with blocks and cars and dolls. They have a lot of toys in their room. But sometimes they make a big mess. Their mom is not happy when she sees the mess. She says they have to clean up and be organized. Tom and Anna do not like to clean up. They think it is boring. They want to keep playing. They have an idea. They take a big tie from their dad's closet. They use the tie to unite all their toys. They make a long line of toys with the tie. They think it is funny. They show their mom their line of toys. They say they are organized. They say they used the tie to unite their toys. Their mom looks at them. She does not know whether to laugh or to be angry. She sees that they tried to be creative. She also sees that they still made a mess. She tells them that they have to put their toys in the right places. She says that the tie is not for toys. She says that the tie is for dad. She helps them to untie their toys and to sort them. She shows them how to be organized. She says that they can play with the tie later, but only with dad. Tom and Anna say sorry to their mom. They say they love her. They hug her. They are happy.

One day, Mom was in the kitchen. She was using the oven to make a cake. She tried to explain to her little boy, Tim, that the oven was very hot. Tim was curious and wanted to see the cake. Mom told Tim to be careful and stay away from the oven. She said it was hot and loud. Tim didn't understand why the oven was loud, but he listened to Mom. He played with his toys and waited for the cake to be done. When the cake was ready, Mom opened the oven. It made a loud noise! Tim was surprised. He remembered what Mom said about the oven being loud. Now he knew what she meant. Tim was happy he listened to Mom and stayed away from the hot, loud oven. They enjoyed the yummy cake together.

One day, a happy dog named Spot went for a walk. He saw a tie on the ground. Spot picked up the tie and put it on. He felt very fancy. Spot walked and clapped his paws. He wanted to show his friends his new tie. He met his friend, Cat. Cat saw the tie and clapped her paws too. They were having so much fun. Then, something unexpected happened. The tie started to move! It was not a tie, but a long snake! Spot and Cat jumped back. But the snake was nice and just wanted to play. They all clapped and played together.

One day, a small dog named Tim went on a trip. He had to pack his bag with toys and food. He was a little scared because it was his first time going on a trip. But he knew his family would be with him, so he felt better. On the way, Tim saw a nail on the ground. He was curious and went to look at it. The nail was shiny and sharp. Tim's mom told him to be careful not to step on it. She picked it up and put it in a safe place. At the end of the trip, Tim was not scared anymore. He had fun playing with his toys and eating his food. He even showed his friends the nail he found. Tim learned that going on trips can be fun and exciting, even if you are a little scared at first.
