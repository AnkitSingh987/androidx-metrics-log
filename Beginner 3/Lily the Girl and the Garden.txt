Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Garden

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the garden. She saw a pretty rose. The rose was red and smelled nice. Lily wanted to pick the rose, but she was not ready. She knew it had pointy parts that could hurt her. Lily asked her mom, "Can I pick the rose?" Her mom said, "Wait, let me help you." But Lily did not want to wait. She tried to pick the rose by herself. She felt a sharp pain in her hand. Lily started to panic. Her mom came over and said, "Don't worry, Lily. I will help you." She took Lily's hand and removed the pointy part. Lily felt better. She learned to wait and be ready before doing something new.

Mia and her dad went to visit the park. It was a sunny day. Mia saw some orange birds flying up in the sky. When they got to the park, Mia saw a big clock tower. She thought it was so cool! Dad said it was over one hundred years old. At the park, they played at the playground and Mia saw lots of orange flowers. She gave one to her dad as a special gift. Before they headed home, they stopped to visit the clock tower again. Mia enjoyed watching the hands move around on the clock. They had a great day at the park and Mia was very happy. She couldn't wait to visit again!

Once there was a small seed. Every day it would grow. Soon it was a big and strong tree. The tree was very happy and it made a lot of friends. Everyday it would smile at them and say hello. One day, a lazy bird came to the tree. The bird had a blue mug in his beak. He asked the tree if he could rest and the tree said yes. The bird put his mug in the branches of the tree and took a nap. The sun shone on the tree and the bird slept until the evening. When the bird woke up, he thanked the tree and said goodbye. The tree was happy it had helped the lazy bird.

Once upon a time, in a small classroom, there was a light. This light was very bright and made everyone happy. The children would come to the classroom every day to learn and play. One day, the teacher had an idea. She wanted the children to play a game called "match." She showed the children cards with pictures on them. The children had to find the cards that had the same pictures. They were very excited to play this game. The children started to play "match" in the light classroom. They looked at the cards and tried to find the ones that were the same. They were so happy when they found a match. The children played the game all day and had so much fun. The light in the classroom made their day even brighter.

Once upon a time, there was a clean little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her toys and draw pretty pictures. She had a big heart and liked to share her toys with her friends. One day, Sue's mom asked her to help add more toys to her toy box. Sue was very happy to help. She picked up her toys and put them in the box. Her mom was proud of her for being such a good helper. After Sue finished cleaning, her mom gave her a new toy to add to her toy box. It was a pretty doll with a big smile. Sue's heart was happy because she had a new toy to share with her friends. They all played together and had a fun day.

One day, a little brown dog went for a walk. He saw a big strip of grass and wanted to play on it. But there was a big fence around the grass. The dog did not know how to get in. The dog saw a little girl and said, "Look, can you help me get in? I want to play on the grass." The little girl looked at the fence and thought for a moment. She said, "I will help you, little brown dog." The little girl and the brown dog tried to open the gate, but it was locked. They were sad because they could not play on the grass. Then, they saw a big rock next to the fence. The little girl said, "Let's move the rock and make a hole under the fence." They pushed the rock and made a hole under the fence. The little brown dog was happy. He said, "Thank you, little girl. Now we can play on the grass!" They played on the grass all day and became best friends.
