Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Bear and the Rabbit

Once upon a time, in a small gloomy forest, there lived a big bear and a small rabbit. They were best friends. The big bear loved to play with his toy arrow, while the small rabbit liked to hop around. One day, the big bear and the small rabbit were playing near their home. The big bear said, "I want to show you how far my arrow can go." The small rabbit was excited and said, "Okay, let's see!" The big bear pulled back the arrow and let it go. It flew high up in the sky and then came back down. But, oh no! The arrow hit their home and destroyed it. The big bear and the small rabbit were sad. They knew they had to fix their home. So, they worked together, using sticks and leaves to make it strong again. In the end, their home was even better than before. The big bear and the small rabbit were happy and continued to play together, but they made sure to be more careful with the arrow.

Once upon a time, Tom went to the park. It was very busy in the park. He found a big, shiny statue there. He was so excited that he wanted to hold it. But the statue was too heavy for him. So he asked his mum for help. His mum held the statue for him, so he could look at it closely. He was so happy! The statue was so beautiful. It looked like a princess, with long golden hair. Tom wanted to take it home, but his mum said it was too big and heavy to carry. So instead of taking the statue home, Tom looked at it for a long time until he had to go home. He said goodbye to the statue and waved. Tom was so proud of himself. He had helped mum hold the statue and he had seen the beautiful princess statue!

Once upon a time, there was a silly swan. The swan liked to play in the water all day. It would jump and splash with its friends. They had so much fun together. One day, the silly swan saw its reflection in the water. It stopped playing and started to stare at the reflection. The swan thought it was another swan. It wanted to be friends with the new swan. The silly swan tried to talk to the reflection. But the reflection did not talk back. The swan's friends laughed and said, "That's not a new swan, that's you!" The silly swan felt a little silly, but it was okay. The swan kept playing with its friends and had a great day.

Daniel was a little boy who loved trucks. He liked to play with them and look at them. One day, his mom came to him and said, "Daniel, do you want to take a ride in the truck?" Daniel was so excited and said, "Yes, let's go!" But when they went to the truck, Daniel saw that it was very pale in color. He did not like it at all. He refused to go in the truck. He shook his head and said, "No, no, no!" Daniel's mom tried to convince him to go, but he still refused. Finally, his mom said, "Okay, if you don't like that truck, we'll just get a different one!" Daniel smiled and happily said, "Yes, let's go!"

Once upon a time, there was a little red car. The car needed fuel to go fast. One day, the car saw a big truck. The truck had a lot of fuel. The car wanted the truck's fuel. The car did not understand how to be careful. It went very fast to get the fuel. The truck saw the car coming. The truck tried to stop, but it was too late. The car crashed into the truck. Both the car and the truck were very sad. The car did not get the fuel it wanted. The car learned it should be more careful next time. But now, the car was broken and could not go fast anymore.

Mark is a new friend. He likes to play with a ball. He has a big smile. He has a dog too. The dog is funny. One day, Mark and his friends go to the park. They want to throw the ball. They take turns. The dog runs and catches the ball. The dog brings the ball back. They all laugh. Mark throws the ball very far. The dog runs fast. The dog jumps and catches the ball. Everyone claps. They play until the sun goes down. They have a fun day at the park.
