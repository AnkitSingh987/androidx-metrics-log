Category: Beginner
Title: The Popular Machine and the Tim

Once upon a time, there was a popular machine. It was a big, fun toy that everyone liked. The machine made people happy when they played with it. One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to play with the machine too. Tim waited his turn to play with the machine. When it was his turn, he was very excited. But instead of playing with it nicely, Tim decided to kick the machine. He kicked it very hard, and the machine started to make a loud noise. The machine broke because Tim kicked it. Everyone was sad because they couldn't play with the popular machine anymore. Tim felt bad for what he did. The machine was never fixed, and the people didn't get to play with it again.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy old cat named Mr. Whiskers. He lived in a small house with a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to play with Mr. Whiskers, but Mr. Whiskers did not like to play. He just wanted to sleep all day. One day, Sue saw a big red truck drive by her house. The truck had a loud siren and people waved from it. Sue asked her mom, "What is that?" Her mom said, "That is an emergency truck. They help people who need it." Sue wanted to help too. She went to Mr. Whiskers and said, "Let's play emergency!" Mr. Whiskers was grumpy, but he played with Sue. They pretended to drive a big red truck and waved to everyone. In the end, even Mr. Whiskers had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a friend named Sue. They liked to play together. One day, they found a box. The box had a pretty card inside. Tim asked Sue, "Can I see the card?" Sue said, "Yes, you can." Tim took the card and looked at it. It had a picture of a mild cat on it. The cat was soft and nice. Tim and Sue liked the cat. Tim and Sue wanted to show the card to their mom. They went to their mom and said, "Look at this card we found." Their mom looked at the card and smiled. She said, "What a nice card with a mild cat!" Tim and Sue were happy that their mom liked the card too. They played with the card all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to build structures with his toy blocks. One day, Tim made a very slow structure. It was a tall tower that took a long time to build. Tim's mom came into the room and saw the tower. She said, "Wow, Tim! That is a big structure. But it looks like it might fall. Can you change it to make it stronger?" Tim thought for a moment and then said, "Yes, Mom! I can change it." So, Tim took some blocks from the top and put them at the bottom. The tower was now shorter but stronger. Tim was happy with his new structure. Later, Tim's dad came into the room and saw the new structure. He said, "Great job, Tim! You changed the tower to make it better. It's not slow anymore." Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Dad! I like my new structure too." And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat jam on his bread. One day, Tim saw a big jar of jam in the kitchen. It was on a high shelf. Tim wanted the jam so badly. Tim found a chair to stand on. He reached up to get the jam. As he did, he saw a big spoon next to the jar. The spoon was filthy, but Tim did not care. He just wanted the jam. Tim took the jar and the spoon down. Tim opened the jar and used the filthy spoon to stir the jam. He put some jam on his bread. As he took a bite, he felt something strange. Tim looked at his bread and saw dirt from the spoon. He learned that he should always use a clean spoon to stir his jam.
