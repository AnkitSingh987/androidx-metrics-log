Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Hat

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to print pictures on his printer. One day, Tim wanted to print a picture of a cold snowman. He thought it would be fun to show his friends. Tim's printer was sometimes stupid. It didn't always do what he wanted it to do. Today, the printer was being very stupid. It printed a picture of a cold snowman, but the snowman had a big nose and a small hat. Tim didn't like it. Tim tried again to print the right picture of the cold snowman. This time, the printer was not stupid. It printed the perfect picture of a cold snowman with a small nose and a big hat. Tim was happy and showed the picture to his friends. They all liked it and had fun playing with the picture of the cold snowman.

One day, a little penguin named Pip lived in a small igloo. He had a best friend named Tilly the seal. They liked to play and have fun together. One day, Pip had an idea to play a game. He wanted to scare Tilly and see her jump. Pip went to Tilly's home and hid behind a big ice block. He was very alert and waited for his friend. Tilly came back from swimming and did not see Pip. When she got close, Pip jumped out and yelled, "Boo!" Tilly was so scared, she jumped high in the air. Tilly laughed and said, "Pip, you scared me! Let's play this game again." They took turns trying to scare each other and had lots of fun. At the end of the day, they were both happy and tired. They went back to their homes and slept well, excited to play more games the next day.

Lily hated ink. She hated how it made her hands dirty and how it spilled on her books. She hated how it smelled and how it stained her clothes. She only liked to draw with crayons and markers. One day, her teacher gave her a pen and a paper. She said, "Lily, today we are going to practice writing our names. You can use this pen and this paper. It is fun and easy." Lily did not want to use the pen. She wanted to use her crayons and markers. She said, "No, thank you, teacher. I do not like ink. I like crayons and markers. Can I use them instead?" The teacher said, "No, Lily, you have to use the pen. It is good for you to learn how to write with ink. You can use your crayons and markers later. Come on, try it. It is not so bad." Lily was impatient. She did not want to wait. She did not want to try. She grabbed the pen and the paper. She scribbled on the paper. She pressed the pen hard. She made a big hole in the paper. She made a big mess on the table. She made a big noise with her mouth. She said, "I hate ink! I hate ink! I hate ink!" The teacher was sad. She said, "Lily, why did you do that? You ruined your paper and your pen. You made a big mess and a big noise. You were not nice and polite. You have to say sorry and clean up." Lily felt bad. She knew she was wrong. She said, "I am sorry, teacher. I was angry and rude. I will clean up and try again. Maybe ink is not so bad. Maybe I can learn to like it."

Once upon a time, there was a small school. In the school, there was a class of kids. These kids were very intelligent. They loved to learn new things every day. One day, their teacher said, "Today, we will play a game. You must remain in your seat and think of a fun animal. Then, we will share our animals with the class." The kids were excited to play the game. One by one, the kids shared their animals. A girl named Lily said, "I thought of a cat. Cats are soft and nice." A boy named Tim said, "I thought of a dog. Dogs are fun to play with." All the kids shared their animals and they had a great day in their class.
