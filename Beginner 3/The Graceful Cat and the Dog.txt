Category: Beginner
Title: The Graceful Cat and the Dog

One day, a graceful cat went to a festival. At the festival, there were many fun games and yummy food. The cat played with a ball and ate a big fish. While the cat was having fun, a big dog came to the festival too. The dog saw the cat and wanted to play. The cat was scared of the dog, but the dog just wanted to be friends. The dog tried to play with the cat by giving a little slap on the cat's back. The cat was surprised and jumped high in the air. But then, the cat saw that the dog was just playing and not trying to hurt her. So, the cat and the dog became friends and played together at the festival.

Julie was a little girl who lived in a small house by herself. She was always worried because she was so small. One day, Julie decided to take a walk outside to clear her head. She saw a big lovely tail waving at her from a nearby bush. Julie moved closer to see what it was, but she was startled. "Oh no!" she said. It was a big lion! The lion saw her too, but instead of growling, it started to go backwards! Julie was even more worried, but then she realised the lion just wanted to play with her. She smiled and said, "Do you want to play tag?" The lion nodded its head and the two of them set off in reverse directions. After some time, Julie finally won the game. She and the lion went back to the bush and said goodbye. The experience made Julie even more adventurous, and she knew her worries were gone.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spike. Spike was a warm and happy dog. He loved to run and play with his friends in the park. One sunny day, Spike got very dirty. He had mud all over his fur. Spike's mom said, "Spike, you need a wash!" So, she took him to the bathroom to give him a bath. Spike's mom filled the tub with warm water. Spike got in and splashed around. His mom used soap to wash him clean. After the bath, Spike was so happy to be clean again. He wagged his tail and gave his mom a big hug.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends, Tom and Sarah. Tom was three years old and Sarah was five. Tom had a loose tooth that had been bothering him all week. Tom told Sarah, "My tooth is wobbly. I'm scared to release it!" Sarah smiled and said, "Don't worry Tom. I can help you. Let's be alert, I'll count to three and when I say three, you can easily release your tooth." Tom nodded, feeling a little bit better. On the count of three, he closed his eyes and wiggled his tooth gently. With a huge pop, the tooth released from Tom's mouth. Tom clapped his hands, so happy he had released his tooth all by himself. Sarah smiled and hugged her best friend, and they celebrated with a big hug.

Tom and Lily were hungry. They wanted to eat bread and butter. They saw a big jar of butter on the table. But Mom said, "No, that is not for you. That is for the cake. Wait for lunch." Tom and Lily did not listen. They waited for Mom to go to the bathroom. Then they ran to the table. They took the jar of butter and a knife. They spread butter on their bread. They ate and ate. The butter was good. But soon they felt sick. Their tummies hurt. They had eaten too much butter. They saw Mom coming back. They were scared. They hid the jar of butter under the tablecloth. They hoped Mom would not see. Mom saw the bread crumbs on the table. She saw the knife with butter. She lifted the tablecloth. She saw the jar of butter. It was almost empty. She was angry. "Tom and Lily, what did you do?" she asked. "Did you eat the butter for the cake?" Tom and Lily looked at Mom. They looked at each other. They felt bad. They said, "Yes, Mom, we did. We are sorry. We regret it. We wanted to eat bread and butter. The butter was good. But now we feel sick. And we spoiled the cake." Mom sighed. She said, "Tom and Lily, you were naughty. You did not obey me. You wasted the butter. You ruined the cake. You made me sad. And you made yourselves sick. You should not have eaten the butter. You should have waited for lunch." Tom and Lily nodded. They said, "We know, Mom. We are sorry. We regret it. We will not do it again. Can you forgive us?" Mom hugged them. She said, "I love you, Tom and Lily. I forgive you. But you have to learn from your mistake. Next time, listen to me. And eat only what is good for you." Tom and Lily hugged Mom. They said, "We love you, Mom. We will listen to you. And we will eat only what is good for us."
