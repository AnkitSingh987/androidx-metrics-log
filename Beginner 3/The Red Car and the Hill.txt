Category: Beginner
Title: The Red Car and the Hill

Once upon a time, there was a red car. The red car loved to go fast. One day, the red car saw a big hill. The red car wanted to go up the hill very fast. The red car tried to go up the hill, but it was too steep. The red car had to reverse back down the hill. The red car felt sad. It wanted to find a way to go up the hill. The red car saw a fashion show. The fashion show had pretty clothes. The red car had an idea. It put on a pretty dress and tried to go up the hill again. But the dress did not help. The red car still could not go up the hill. The red car felt very sad and cried.

The sun shone outside and Mary had lots of energy to play. Mommy was busy in the kitchen, so Mary decided to look for something else to do. She saw Grandma sitting in the armchair and reading the newspaper. Mary waddled over and asked, "Grandma, what you doin?" Grandma smiled and said, "I'm reading the newspaper. It tells me lots of things about the world." She showed Mary the pretty pictures and words. Mary asked, "Do you like reading the newspaper, Grandma?" "Yes, I do," Grandma said. "It's important to read and learn—it takes patience and I'm very patient." Grandma showed Mary how to carefully turn the pages and read each one. Mary was very proud of how patient she was while reading the newspaper with Grandma. When they were done, they put it back in the stack of newspapers and went outside to play in the sun.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to bed. She had a very soft pillow that she loved. She was happy when she slept on her pillow. That night, she had a dream that she would wake up to a big surprise. In the morning, Lily did wake up. She looked around for the big surprise. She saw her pillow, but it was not on her bed. Her pillow was on the floor! She felt sad because she did not know how it got there. Then, she saw her little brother, Tim, come into the room. He had a big smile on his face. He said, "I moved your pillow to the floor so you could find a new toy under it!" Lily looked under her pillow and found a pretty doll. She was so happy! The surprise was even better than she dreamed.

Once upon a time, there was a fierce cat named Tom. Tom loved to cook. He would put on his little glove and cook yummy food for his friends. One day, Tom invited his friends to his house for a big meal. He wore his glove and cooked a big pot of soup. His friends, a dog and a bird, were very excited to eat Tom's soup. When the soup was ready, Tom and his friends sat down to eat. They loved the soup and had a very fun time together. They thanked Tom for the yummy soup and his fierce cooking skills.

Once upon a time, there was a funny dog named Spot. Spot was white with big black spots. He loved to play and make everyone laugh. One day, Spot found a big red ball. He thought it would be fun to fill it with air. So, he blew and blew until the ball was big and round. Spot was so happy with his big red ball. Spot took the ball to the park to play. He jumped and ran with the ball. All the kids at the park laughed and played with Spot and his ball. They had so much fun together. The day was filled with joy and laughter. And that's how funny, white Spot made everyone's day a little brighter.

Tom and Anna are twins. They like to play in their room. They have many toys and books. But their favorite thing is their big bed. They can jump on it, hide under it, and pretend it is a ship or a castle. One day, they play too hard on the bed. They hear a loud rip. They look and see a big hole in the sheet. They are scared. They know Mom will be angry. They try to hide the hole with a pillow. But it does not work. They hear Mom coming. They think fast. They grab some tape and scissors. They try to repair the sheet. They cut some tape and stick it on the hole. They hope Mom will not see it. Mom comes in. She smiles and says, "Hello, my sweeties. What are you doing?" She sees the tape on the sheet. She frowns and says, "What is this? Did you tear the sheet?" Tom and Anna look at each other. They feel sorry. They say, "Yes, Mom. We are sorry. We played too hard. We tried to repair it. But it was hard. We did not know how." Mom sighs and says, "I appreciate that you tried to fix it. But tape is not good for sheets. It will not last. And it will hurt the fabric. Next time, please be more careful with your things. And if you break something, tell me. I can help you repair it. Or we can buy a new one." Tom and Anna nod and say, "OK, Mom. We are sorry. We love you." They hug Mom and say, "Can we still play on the bed?" Mom laughs and says, "Yes, you can. But gently. And no more jumping. OK?" Tom and Anna say, "OK, Mom. Thank you." They kiss Mom and go back to their bed. They play softly and happily. They are glad Mom is not angry. They are glad they have a big bed. And they are glad they have each other.
