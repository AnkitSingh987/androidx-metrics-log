Category: Beginner
Title: The Teacher and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a teacher. She had many pupils in her class. Every day, they did regular things like play, learn, and eat. One day, the teacher wanted to measure how tall her pupils were. She used a long stick to see how high they could reach. One by one, they all stood by the wall. Then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew into the room! The bird was lost and scared. The pupils stopped and looked at the bird. They wanted to help it. The teacher and her pupils worked together to help the bird find its way out. They opened the window, and the bird flew away. The pupils were happy they could help. That day, they learned that sometimes, unexpected things can happen, but they can still have fun and help others.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim in the big blue sea. One day, Fin met a big fish named Bubbles. "Hi, Bubbles!" said Fin. "Do you want to swim with me?" Bubbles said, "Yes, Fin! Let's swim together." Fin and Bubbles swam and swam. They played near the rocks and the sand. But then, they swam too far away from their home. Fin's ear started to hurt. He was scared. "Ouch, my ear hurts!" cried Fin. "I want to go home." But Bubbles didn't know the way back. They were lost. Fin and Bubbles kept swimming, but they never found their way home. They were sad and scared forever.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing near a big tree. They liked to play and speak to each other. The cat was always polite to the dog. They were best friends. "Let's play hide and seek!" said the cat. The dog agreed and started to count. The cat ran and hid under the tree. The dog looked for the cat, but he could not find her. "Where are you?" the dog asked. Just then, a big bird came down from the tree. "I saw your friend!" said the bird. "She is under the tree!" The dog was surprised. He did not know that the bird could speak. They all laughed and played together. Now, they were three best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max had a red leash. Max liked to go for walks with his friend, a boy named Tim. They had a lot of fun together. One day, Max saw a big cat. The cat had serious eyes. Max started to bark and tried to chase the cat. Tim said, "No, Max! Stay!" Max stopped and blinked his eyes. He knew he had to listen to Tim. Max and Tim walked away from the cat. They played in the park and had a good time. Max was happy to be with his friend. Tim said, "Good boy, Max!" Max wagged his tail and knew he had been a good listener.

Timmy was excited. He was with his sister, playing outside in the park. His sister made up a game, and they both laughed until their sides hurt. Suddenly, his mum arrived. She was angry and wanted them to go home. Timmy was scared that his mum would punish him, but his sister smiled. Mum said, "Why were you playing outside so late?" Timmy's sister quickly replied, "I'm sorry mum, it was my fault!" Mum was still mad and said, "I expect both of you home right now!" Timmy's sister smiled and said, "Yes mum, we're already on our way home." Timmy was relieved that his sister had taken the blame. They both ran home and, as expected, mum punished them. But, Timmy wasn't so scared because he knew his sister had his back. She was always like that - looking out for him, and that made him feel really excited.

One day, a big bird and a small bird were friends. They always played together. The big bird was strong, and the small bird was smart. They were a great team. One time, they found a big fruit. The big bird said, "Let's separate the fruit and share it." The small bird used its brain to think of how to do it. The small bird was not helpless. It had a plan. "I will fly up high and drop the fruit. It will break into pieces, and we can both eat," said the small bird. The big bird agreed. The small bird flew up high and dropped the fruit. It broke into pieces, just like the small bird said. They both ate and were happy. They learned that even if you are small, you can still help. The moral of the story is: don't think you are helpless, use your brain and work together.
