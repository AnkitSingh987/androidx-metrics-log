Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Honest Fireman and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was an honest fireman named Tom. He was very nice and liked to help people. One day, Tom saw a cat stuck in a tree. He said, "Don't worry, I will help you!" Tom got the cat down from the tree. The cat was happy and said, "Thank you, Tom!" Tom liked to cook for his friends. One day, he invited his friends over to his house. He said, "I will cook for you today!" Tom cooked yummy food for his friends. They all ate and said, "Tom, this is very good!" Later, Tom saw a fire at a house. He put on his fireman hat and said, "I will put out the fire!" Tom and his friends worked together to put out the fire. The people in the house were happy and safe. They said, "Thank you, honest fireman Tom!" And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a farm. On the farm, there lived a fast horse named Max. Max loved to run all day and play with his friends. One day, Max saw a little boy named Tim. Tim was sad because he could not find a seat to watch the big race. Max had an idea. He said, "Tim, you can seat on my back and watch the race from there!" Tim was happy and climbed on Max's back. Together, they watched the race and cheered for their friends. After the race, Tim said, "Thank you, Max. You are a good friend." The moral of the story is to always help others when you can.

Once upon a time, there was a big, fat clown. He had a red nose and big shoes. The clown liked to make people laugh. One day, the fat clown went to a party. Kids were playing and eating cake. Then, the clown appeared. He said, "Hello, I am here to make you laugh!" The kids laughed and clapped. The fat clown made funny faces and danced around. Everyone was happy. The clown made the party fun. And they all had a great time.

Sara and Tom are friends. They like to play games at school. One day, they have a race. Sara runs very fast. She wins the race. She gets a medal. It is shiny and red. She is happy. Tom is not happy. He runs slow. He does not win the race. He does not get a medal. He is sad. He is jealous of Sara. He wants a medal too. He says to Sara, "Give me your medal. It is mine." Sara says, "No, Tom. It is not yours. It is mine. I won the race. You did not. You are not nice. You are mean." Tom is angry. He tries to take the medal from Sara. He pulls and pulls. Sara holds and holds. They fight and fight. The medal breaks. It falls to the ground. It is not shiny and red anymore. It is broken and dull. Sara and Tom stop fighting. They look at the medal. They feel bad. They weep. They say, "We are sorry. We are silly. We are not good friends." They hug and make up. They share the medal. They are happy. They are good friends.

Tommy liked to play with his toy cars on the big rug in the living room. The rug was soft and hairy and had many colors. Tommy had a lot of cars, but his favorite was a red one that looked like a fire truck. He always made it go fast and make loud noises. One day, Tommy's mom said they had to go to the store and buy some things. She told Tommy to put his cars away in his room and get ready. Tommy did not want to stop playing, but he did what his mom said. He put all his cars in a box, except for the red one. He hid it under the rug, thinking he would play with it later. At the store, Tommy saw a lot of things he liked. He saw books, candy, balloons, and more cars. He asked his mom if he could have them, but she said no. She said they only had money for what they needed. Tommy was sad, but he remembered his red car under the rug. He thought it would make him happy when they got home. But when they got home, Tommy had a big surprise. He ran to the living room and lifted the rug, but his red car was not there. Instead, he saw a big hole in the rug and some chewed up pieces of plastic. He looked around and saw his dog, Spot, wagging his tail and licking his lips. Spot had found the red car and thought it was a bone. He had eaten it up and made a mess of the rug. Tommy was very angry and sad. He cried and yelled at Spot. He said Spot was a bad dog and did not belong in the house. He said he hated him and wanted him to go away. Spot looked sad and confused. He did not understand why Tommy was so mad. He thought he was sharing his toy with him. Tommy's mom heard the noise and came to see what was wrong. She saw the hole in the rug and the broken car. She understood what had happened and felt sorry for both Tommy and Spot. She hugged Tommy and told him she was sorry he lost his favorite car. She said she knew he loved it very much, but it was just a thing. She said things can be replaced, but friends cannot. She said Spot was his friend and did not mean to hurt him. She said Spot belonged in the family and loved him too. Tommy stopped crying and looked at Spot. He saw his big eyes and his wagging tail. He remembered how Spot played with him and cuddled with him and made him laugh. He realized he did not hate Spot. He loved him too. He said he was sorry for yelling at him and gave him a hug. He said Spot was a good dog and belonged with him. Spot licked his face and wagged his tail harder. Tommy's mom smiled and said she was proud of him. She said she had a surprise for him. She went to the car and came back with a new toy. It was a bigger and shinier red car that looked like a fire truck. It had lights and sirens and a ladder. She said she saw it at the store and thought he would like it. She said it was a gift for being a good boy and a good friend. Tommy was very happy and thanked his mom. He said he loved her and the new car. He said he wanted to play with it on the rug with Spot. He said he hoped Spot would not eat it. He said he would share it with him, but only a little bit. Tommy's mom laughed and said that was fair. She said they would get a new rug soon, but for now, they could enjoy the old one. She said it was just a thing, but they were a family. She said they belonged together.
