Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Grape

One day, a boy named Tim saw a big grape. It was in a store. The grape was expensive. Tim wanted the grape, but he did not have money. He was sad. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, can I have the expensive grape, please?" His mom looked at the grape and said, "No, Tim. It is too expensive. We can buy other grapes." Tim was not happy. He wanted the big grape. He thought of a plan. Tim asked his mom, "Can I do jobs to get the grape?" His mom said, "Yes, you can try." Tim did many jobs. He cleaned the house and helped his mom cook. At the end of the day, Tim had enough money for the grape. Tim was happy. He bought the grape and shared it with his mom.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing outside. The sky was dark, and there was lightning. Tim was not scared, but he did admire the bright lights in the sky. He thought the lightning was like a big show just for him. Tim saw a little frog near a puddle. The frog said, "Ribbit, ribbit! The lightning is pretty, but the water is disgusting." Tim looked at the water and saw that it was dirty and full of mud. He agreed with the frog and said, "Yes, the water is not nice to look at." Tim and the frog became friends. They played together, and the frog showed Tim how to jump high like a frog. They both admired the lightning in the sky and stayed away from the disgusting water. And from that day on, Tim and the frog had lots of fun playing together.

Once upon a time, there was a busy dinosaur named Dina. She had many friends in the forest. Every day, she would go out and play with her friends. But sometimes, Dina would forget to feed her little pet bird, Tweet. One day, Dina went to play with her friends and forgot to feed Tweet again. Tweet was very sad and hungry. He flew away to find food on his own. Dina came back home and saw that Tweet was gone. She felt very sad and missed her little pet bird. Dina looked for Tweet everywhere. She asked her friends to help her find him. After a long time, they found Tweet in a big tree. He was eating some seeds. Dina was so happy to see him! She hugged Tweet and said sorry for not feeding him. From that day on, Dina always remembered to feed Tweet before going out to play. She learned that it is important to take care of our friends and pets.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird. The bird loved to sing songs. One day, the bird was worried. It could not find its friend. The bird flew and looked for its friend. It sang a song to call its friend. The friend did not come. The bird was very sad. Then, a big noise came. It was a loud "shoot". The bird was scared. It saw its friend on the ground. The friend did not move. The bird cried and the song was gone.

One day, a little girl named Sally wanted to ride her bike. It was a perfect, sunny day. She put on her helmet and went outside. Sally was very happy as she rode her bike up and down the street. As she was riding, she saw a little dog. The little dog was lost and hungry. Sally wanted to help the dog, so she took him home with her. She gave the dog some dinner, and they became good friends. But then, something unexpected happened. The little dog started to grow bigger and bigger! The dog wasn't a dog at all! It was a big, friendly bear. Sally was surprised, but she didn't mind. She and the bear played together all day and had lots of fun.

Lily and Max are best friends. They like to play with their cart in the park. They fill the cart with toys, books and snacks. They take turns to push and pull the cart. One day, they see a big dog in the park. The dog is brown and furry. It has a collar with a name tag. The name tag says "Spot". Spot looks friendly, but Lily and Max are scared of dogs. They stop and hold each other. "Hello, kids," says a voice. "Don't be afraid. This is Spot. He is my dog. He is very loyal and gentle. He likes to play with children." Lily and Max look up and see a man. He is smiling and holding a leash. He walks towards them with Spot. "Can we pet him?" asks Lily. "Of course," says the man. "He loves to be petted. Here, let me show you how to do it." The man kneels down and strokes Spot's head. Spot wags his tail and licks the man's face. "See? He is very happy," says the man. "Do you want to try?" Lily and Max nod. They let go of each other and approach Spot slowly. They touch his fur and ears. Spot sniffs them and gives them a lick. "He is so soft and warm," says Lily. "He is so nice and friendly," says Max. They smile and giggle. They forget their fear and play with Spot. They throw a ball and Spot fetches it. They lean on him and hug him. They share their snacks with him. They have a lot of fun with Spot and the man. They thank them for being so kind. They say goodbye and go back to their cart. They push and pull the cart together. They are happy and proud. They have made a new friend. They have learned to be brave. They have their loyal cart and each other.
