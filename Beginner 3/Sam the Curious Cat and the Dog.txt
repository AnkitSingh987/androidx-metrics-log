Category: Beginner
Title: Sam the Curious Cat and the Dog

One day, a curious cat named Sam went outside to play. He saw a big dog in the yard. The dog wanted to fight with Sam. Sam was scared but he said, "No, I don't want to fight." Sam ran away very fast. But the dog chased him. They ran and ran, until Sam climbed a tall tree. The dog could not climb the tree, so he went away. Sam's leg was hurt from the chase. His mom took him to the hospital. At the hospital, the doctor fixed Sam's leg. Sam was happy that he did not fight, and he learned to stay away from big dogs.

Sara and Max are playing in the living room. Sara has a pink crayon and Max has a blue one. They like to draw on paper, but sometimes they get bored. "Let's draw on the couch!" Sara says. "It is white and boring. We can make it pretty." Max nods. He thinks it is a good idea. They climb on the couch and start to mark it with their crayons. They draw flowers, stars, and faces. They laugh and have fun. But then Mom comes in. She sees the couch and gasps. She is very angry. "What did you do?" she asks. "You marked the couch! That is bad. You can't draw on the couch. It is not paper. It is not yours. You have to be careful with things." Sara and Max feel sorry. They did not know Mom would be mad. They thought they were making art. "We are sorry, Mom," they say. "We wanted to make the couch pretty. We like pink and blue." Mom sighs. She is still angry, but she loves Sara and Max. She knows they are little and they make mistakes. "I know you like to draw, but you have to draw on the right things," she says. "The couch is not for drawing. It is for sitting. You have to respect other people's things. And you have to clean up your mess. Here, take these wipes and help me erase the marks." Sara and Max take the wipes and help Mom. They rub the couch until the marks are gone. It takes a long time and it is hard work. They learn their lesson. They hug Mom and say they are sorry again. Mom hugs them back and says she forgives them. She tells them to go get some paper and draw something nice for her. They smile and run to get their crayons. They still like to draw, but they will never draw on the couch again.

Once upon a time, there was a big blue truck. The truck's job was to deliver things to people. The truck liked to go fast and was always impatient. One day, the blue truck had to deliver a big box to a little girl named Sue. Sue was very excited and couldn't wait for the box to come. She stood by the window, watching for the blue truck. The impatient blue truck zoomed down the street, going very fast. It stopped in front of Sue's house and delivered the big box. Sue was so happy to get her box. The blue truck drove away, ready to deliver more things to more people.

One day, a friendly dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a small bird on the ground. The bird was sad. Max asked, "Why are you sad, little bird?" The bird said, "I am very thirsty. I need water, but I cannot find any." Max wanted to help the bird. He thought of an idea. He said, "I can lend you my bowl. Let's go find some water." They walked and walked, and finally, they found a puddle of water. Max put his bowl on the ground, and the bird drank the water from it. The bird was very happy and said, "Thank you, Max, for lending me your bowl." From that day on, Max and the bird were best friends. They always helped each other when they needed it.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to design a special picture. He found some incredible powder that could make his picture very pretty. He was very happy and started to work on his picture. As Tim was using the powder, a talking cat came into his room. The cat said, "Hi, Tim! Can I help you with your picture?" Tim was surprised but said, "Yes, you can help me. Let's make this picture together." They worked and worked on the picture. When they finished, the incredible powder started to sparkle. Suddenly, the picture came to life and they could play with the things in the picture! Tim and the cat had a lot of fun and became good friends.
