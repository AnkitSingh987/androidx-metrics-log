Category: Beginner
Title: The Princess and the Apple

Once upon a time, there was a princess who lived in a big castle. She loved to play with her toys and eat yummy food. One day, she found a big, red apple in the kitchen. But the apple was not good to eat. It was old and starting to rot. The princess did not know this. The princess took the apple to her room and played with it. She pretended to eat the apple, but she did not really take a bite. Her little brother saw her and thought the apple looked tasty. He wanted to eat it too. The princess felt guilty because she knew the apple was not good. She decided to stop playing with the apple and tell her brother the truth. She said, "This apple is not good to eat. It is old and will make us sick." Her brother was sad, but he understood. They threw the apple away and found a new, fresh apple to share. They learned it is important to be honest and to not play with things that can make them sick.

Once upon a time, there was a pink bug. The bug liked to march all day. It marched up and down the big hill. One day, the pink bug saw a big box. The bug wanted to march to the box. As it marched closer, the box opened. Out came a new friend, a blue bug! The pink bug and blue bug were happy. They marched together up and down the hill. Now the pink bug was not alone, and they played all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little car. The car was soft and liked to go fast. One day, the car went to a big hill. The car was scared, but it wanted to go down the hill. The car said, "I am scared, but I want to try." So, the car went down the hill very fast. It went faster and faster. The car was scared but it did not stop. At the bottom of the hill, there was a big rock. The car did not see the rock. The car hit the rock and broke. The car could not go fast again. The car was very sad.

Tommy was a silly little boy who loved to spend his time in the park. One day, his dad told him he could take his new toy truck to the park. Tommy was so excited and ran out to the park as soon as he could. As he was playing in the park he saw another little boy with a truck. Tommy thought, "That's my truck! He must have taken it from me!" He asked the other boy, "Did you take my truck?" But the boy shook his head and said, "No". Then Tommy said, "Where did you get your truck?" The other boy turned around and pointed at Tommy's Dad. Tommy was so disappointed. His Dad had gone to the park and bought the boy a truck. He was too silly to remember that he had already bought one for Tommy! All of his fun in the park had been ruined. Tommy spent the rest of the day feeling very sad and confused.

One day, a big, impressive bird was flying in the sky. The bird saw a rail near a lake. The bird wanted to sit on the rail and look at the water. As the bird sat on the rail, a little boy came by. The boy looked at the bird and said, "Wow! You are so big and impressive!" The bird was happy and flapped its wings. Suddenly, the bird started to fall off the rail. The little boy shouted, "Oh no! Be careful!" The bird flapped its wings harder and stopped falling. It flew back up to the rail. The bird and the boy were both happy and they became good friends.

One day, a big, honest bear and a small, honest rabbit were walking in the woods. They saw a mushroom. The mushroom was red and pretty. They wanted to take it home. But they did not know if it was good to eat or not. The bear and the rabbit sat down to discuss. They talked about the mushroom. The bear said, "I think the mushroom is good." The rabbit said, "I think the mushroom is not good." They could not agree. Just then, a wise, old owl came. The owl knew about mushrooms. The owl said, "This mushroom is not good to eat. It can make you sick." The bear and the rabbit thanked the owl for being honest. They left the mushroom and went home.
