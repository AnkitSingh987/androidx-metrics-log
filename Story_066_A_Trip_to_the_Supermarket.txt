Title: A Trip to the Supermarket
Category: Beginner

It is Saturday morning. The house is very quiet, but the kitchen is busy. 
"Emma!" her mother calls from the kitchen. "Are you ready to go?"
Emma runs down the stairs. She is wearing her blue jeans and a yellow sweater. 
"Yes, Mother, I am ready," she says. "Are we going to the park?"
"No, not today," her mother says. "Today, we must go to the supermarket. The fridge is completely empty, and we need to buy food for the week."

Emma likes going to the supermarket. It is a very big store. It has bright lights, and there are many interesting things to look at. 
They get into the car and drive to the edge of town. The supermarket has a very large parking lot. It is full of cars because many people do their shopping on Saturday. 
Her mother parks the car, and they walk towards the big glass doors. 

"Okay, Emma," her mother says. "You can be the driver today. Go and get a shopping cart."
Emma smiles. She loves pushing the cart. She walks to the line of metal shopping carts. She pulls one out. It is big and heavy, but she pushes it carefully towards her mother. 
The glass doors open automatically with a 'whoosh' sound. They walk inside. 

The supermarket is huge. It has many long aisles. Each aisle has tall shelves full of different products. 
"Where do we start?" Emma asks. 
"We always start in the fresh food section," her mother says. "We need fruit and vegetables first."

Emma pushes the cart to the right. The fresh food section is very colorful. There are red apples, yellow bananas, green grapes, and orange carrots. 
"Can we get some strawberries?" Emma asks. She points to some small plastic boxes filled with bright red berries. 
"Yes, we can," her mother answers. "Strawberries are very good for you. Please put two boxes in the cart."
Emma carefully puts the boxes into the metal cart. 

Next, her mother picks up a bunch of bananas. She looks at them closely to make sure they are not brown. She puts them in the cart. Then, she chooses a bag of potatoes and some green broccoli. 
"Okay, we have enough fruit and vegetables," her mother says. "Now, we need to go to aisle four for the breakfast food."

Emma pushes the cart down the main walkway. They walk past the bakery. It smells like warm, fresh bread. They walk past the meat counter. 
They turn into aisle four. This aisle is full of cereal, oats, and jam. 
"We need cereal," her mother says. "What kind of cereal do you want this week?"
Emma looks at the tall shelves. There are so many boxes. There are boxes with cartoon animals on them. There are boxes with pictures of honey and nuts. 
"I want the chocolate cereal, please," Emma says. 
Her mother laughs. "Of course you do. Okay, you can reach it. It is on the bottom shelf."

Emma puts the box of chocolate cereal in the cart. The cart is starting to look full. 
"Now we need to go to the dairy section at the back of the store," her mother says. "It is always very cold there."
They walk to the back. There are long, open refrigerators. Emma can feel the cold air on her arms. 
Her mother takes two large bottles of milk and puts them in the cart. She takes a block of yellow cheese and a carton of eggs. 

"Is that everything?" Emma asks. She is starting to feel a little tired from pushing the heavy cart. 
"Let me check," her mother says. She looks into the cart. "We have fruit, vegetables, cereal, milk, cheese, and eggs. Oh, we forgot the bread! We must go back to the bakery."

They walk back to the bakery section. Her mother chooses a fresh, brown loaf of bread. She puts it gently on top of the other food so it does not get squashed. 
"Okay, now we are finished," her mother says. "We have all the food we need for the week. Let's go to the front and pay."

Emma pushes the cart towards the cash registers. There are many people waiting in line. 
"You are a very good cart driver, Emma," her mother says. "Thank you for helping me today."
"You are welcome," Emma says happily. She likes helping her mother, and she is looking forward to eating the chocolate cereal for breakfast tomorrow. A trip to the supermarket is hard work, but it is also a lot of fun.
