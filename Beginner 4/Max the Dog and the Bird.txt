Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Dog and the Bird

One day, a small dog named Max was walking in the park. He saw a big cat named Tom. Tom was sad. Max asked, "Why are you sad, Tom?" Tom said, "I don't understand why my back hurts." Max thought for a moment and said, "I can give you a massage. It will make you feel better." Max started to give Tom a deep massage. Tom started to feel better. Suddenly, something unexpected happened. A bird flew down from a tree and landed on Tom's back. The bird said, "I was sleeping on your back. That's why it hurt." Tom and Max laughed. They were happy to find the reason for Tom's pain. Now, they could all play together in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little red car named Zoomy. Zoomy loved to go fast. He was always ready to race with his friends. One day, Zoomy saw his friend, Bluey the bike, at the park. They decided to have a race. "Ready, set, go!" said Zoomy. Zoomy and Bluey started to move fast. They went up, down, and around the park. Their motion was so quick, everyone watched them race. Zoomy was in front, but Bluey was not far behind. As they neared the end of the race, Zoomy and Bluey were both very tired. They slowed down and stopped. "That was fun!" said Zoomy. "Yes, let's race again soon!" said Bluey. They both smiled, happy to have raced and played together.

Once upon a time, there were two friends named Jack and Sally. They always liked to play together. One sunny day, they were playing hide and seek. Jack was counting while Sally hid. When Jack finished counting he called out, "Ready or not, here I come!" Sally didn't answer. Jack started looking in all sorts of places. But he couldn't find her. Finally, he walked into Sally's room. He was surprised! She was wearing lots of bright jewelry and smiling at him. "Did you surprise me?" asked Jack. "Yes," said Sally, "I wanted to surprise you with my pretty jewelry. Do you like it?" "I do!" said Jack, "It's so sparkly and bright!" Sally smiled, and said, "I'm glad you like it. Now let's go outside and keep playing!" They ran outside, and kept playing for the rest of the day.

Billy was so excited. He was in the store, looking at all the cute cards. He loved looking at them, picking out the ones he liked best. Suddenly, Billy saw one that he just had to have. It was so cute and it had his name on it. He loved it and knew he had to have it. He asked his mom if he could have the card and she said yes. So he reached up and grabbed it off the shelf. He hugged it tight and was so happy. When he got home, he showed it to everyone. He told everyone just how much he loved it. He said it was his favorite card.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom liked to throw balls at the park. One day, Tom saw a big book on the table. The book was about math. Tom thought math was hard, but he wanted to learn. Tom asked his mom, "Can you help me with this math book?" His mom said, "Be careful, Tom. The book is fragile. We must not throw it like a ball." Tom listened and tried to learn math with his mom. But Tom was not good at math. He got mad and forgot what his mom said. He threw the fragile math book across the room. The book broke into many pieces. Tom's mom was sad, and Tom did not learn math that day.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily loved to read books. One day, she found a big, new book on the shelf. She was so happy to see it. She wanted to read the book, but it was too high for her to reach. Lily asked her big brother, Tom, for help. "Tom, can you help me reach the new book?" she asked. Tom was nice and tall. He reached up and took the book down for Lily. "Here you go, Lily!" he said with a smile. Lily sat down and started to read the new book. She turned each page with joy. She loved the bright pictures and the fun words. Lily and Tom spent the whole day reading the new book together. They were very happy and had lots of fun.
