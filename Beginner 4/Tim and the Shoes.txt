Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Shoes

One day, a boy named Tim was very sleepy. His mom said, "Tim, it is time for bed." Tim did not want to go to bed. He wanted dessert. His mom said, "You can have dessert after you learn to tie your shoes." Tim's big sister, Jane, wanted to help. She said, "I will teach you, Tim." Jane showed him how to tie his shoes. Tim tried, but it was hard. They kept trying, and Tim got better. Soon, Tim could tie his shoes all by himself. Tim was so happy, he ran to his mom. "Mom, I did it! I can tie my shoes!" His mom was proud of him. She said, "Great job, Tim! Now you can have dessert." But Tim was so sleepy from learning, he said, "I will have dessert tomorrow. I want to sleep now." And Tim went to bed with a big smile on his face.

Once upon a time, there was a big, slow turtle named Tim. Tim had a purple key that could unlock a magic door. He always carried the key with him, but he did not know where the door was. One day, Tim went for a walk in the forest. He saw a big tree with a door on it. Tim thought, "Maybe this is the magic door!" He was very excited. He walked slow to the door and took out his purple key. Tim put the purple key in the door and turned it. The door unlocked! Inside, there was a room full of toys and yummy food. Tim was so happy that he found the magic door. He played with the toys and ate the food all day. From that day on, Tim's life was full of fun and joy.

Once upon a time, there was a big, strong muscle named Max. Max lived in a dirty, filthy room. He didn't like his room, but he didn't know how to clean it. One day, Max saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was very good at cleaning. Max had a question for her. "Lily, can you help me clean my room?" he asked. Lily smiled and said, "Yes, Max! I can help you." Together, they cleaned Max's room. They picked up toys, washed the floor, and made the bed. Soon, the room was clean and shiny. Max was very happy. "Thank you, Lily!" he said. And from that day on, Max kept his room clean and they became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little house by the sea. A girl named Mia lived in the house. She loved to wave at the boats in the water. Every day, Mia would run to the sea and wave at the boats. One day, while Mia was waving, she saw something rare. A big, shiny fish jumped out of the water. The fish could talk! "Hello, Mia!" said the fish. Mia was very surprised. She had never seen a fish talk before. The fish told Mia that it was lost and needed help. Mia had an idea. She waved at a boat to come close to the shore. The boat came, and Mia asked the people on the boat to help the fish. They all worked together to help the fish find its way home. From that day on, Mia and the fish became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. Tom liked to sleep all day and did not want to play. One day, Tom found a puzzle on the floor. He did not understand how to play with it, but he tried anyway. As Tom played with the puzzle, he started to feel less lazy. He began to have fun and wanted to play more. Tom put the puzzle pieces together and made a picture of a big fish. But then, something unexpected happened. The fish in the puzzle came to life! It jumped out of the puzzle and started to swim in the air. Tom was so surprised that he was not lazy anymore. He played with the fish all day and they became good friends. And from that day on, Tom was never lazy again.

Once there was a bee who was very colorful. He was a happy bee and liked to fly around and play. One day, he was eating a flower when he heard a strange noise. It was a loud buzzing sound and it made him scared. The bee looked up and saw a bigger bee coming near him. He didn't want to fight and decided to fly away. But, the bigger bee started chasing him. He was very scared and tried to fly farther away. The little bee thought he could escape, but the big bee was too fast for him. Soon the big bee caught up and bit the little bee. It hurt very much and the little bee suffered a lot from the bite. But then, something good happened! A colorful butterfly came and scared the big bee away. The little bee was so relieved! He was glad to be safe and thanked the butterfly. From then on, the little bee was very careful when he flew around looking for flowers.
