Category: Beginner
Title: The True and the Stick

Once upon a time there was a True. It was very different to all the others. It had four legs, a tail and a long furry coat. It wanted to get around, but it wasn't sure how. Then one day it had a plan. It managed to find a stick and held it in its mouth. It used the stick to help itself move and suddenly it could jump and play. It moved faster and faster, until one day it could hop all over. It had managed it! The True was very happy. It could travel and explore a lot more than normal. Everywhere it went it saw different things; flowers and trees, stones and rivers. It almost felt like it could fly. And so the True carried on, happily travelling around its world, managing with its stick.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. He liked to see the big, high trees and the pretty flowers. One day, Tim saw a little bird on a high tree. The bird was singing a happy song. Tim wanted to see the bird up close. He tried to climb the high tree, but it was his first time. He was scared, but he tried his best. Tim climbed and climbed, and soon he was very high up in the tree. He could see the bird now, and it was so pretty. Tim spent a lot of time with the bird. They sang songs and played games. Then, it was time for Tim to go home. He climbed down from the high tree and said goodbye to his new friend. Tim knew he would see the bird again, and he couldn't wait for the next time they would play together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was scared of lightning. Every time it rained hard, he would hide under his bed. He did not like the loud noise and bright light. One night, a talking cat came to visit Tim. The cat could speak! "Don't be scared of lightning," the cat said. "It's just light and sound in the sky. It can't hurt you if you are inside your house." Tim listened to the talking cat. The next time it rained hard and there was lightning, he remembered what the cat said. He did not hide under his bed. Instead, he watched the lightning from his window. Tim was not scared anymore.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing with his toy car. He saw a big bug and wanted to scare it away. Tim said, "Shoo, bug! Go away!" The bug looked confused and didn't move. Tim felt sad because the bug did not listen to him. Tim's mom saw him and asked, "What's wrong, Tim?" Tim said, "I want to scare the bug, but it won't go away." His mom smiled and said, "Maybe the bug is scared too. Let's try to be kind and help it find its way home." Tim and his mom found a small stick and gently moved the bug onto it. They put the bug in the garden, where it could be happy. Tim felt good for helping the bug. His mom said, "Remember, it's always better to be kind than to scare others." Moral: Be kind to everyone, even the smallest creatures.

Once upon a time, there was a smooth rock. The rock was from the west. It had a friend called Tom. Tom was a boy. The rock and Tom liked to play. One day, Tom said to the rock, "Rock, will you accept my gift?" The rock said, "Yes, Tom." Tom gave the rock a hat. The rock was happy. They played in the sun. But then, something unexpected happened. The rock started to grow. It grew big and tall. It became a mountain! Tom was surprised. Now, Tom and the rock could play new games. They were happy.

One day, a small bird was in a cage. It was sad and did not like the cage. A big, unknown animal came to the bird. The animal was nice and wanted to help the bird. The animal said, "Do not be sad, little bird. I will help you get out of the cage." The bird was happy and said, "Thank you, big friend." The animal started to stir the cage with its big paws. Suddenly, the cage door opened! The bird was free! But, the big animal was not an unknown animal. It was a cat! The bird was scared. But, the cat said, "I am a nice cat. I will not eat you. Let's be friends." The bird was happy again and they became friends.
