Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Turkey

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a big turkey walking around. The turkey looked frightened. Tim wanted to help the turkey feel better. Tim picked up a ball and said, "Hi, Mr. Turkey! Do you want to play with me?" The turkey looked at Tim and nodded. Tim threw the ball to the turkey. The turkey tried to catch the ball with its beak. They played together for a while, and the turkey was not frightened anymore. They became good friends. Tim's mom watched them play and smiled. "Good job, Tim," she said. "You made a new friend today."

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a slow turtle named Tim. Tim loved to walk around and look at things. One day, he saw a big figure in the sky. It was a bird! Tim wanted to be friends with the bird. Tim had an idea. He would record the bird with his toy camera. He thought the bird would like to see the video and be his friend. Tim was very excited. But when the bird saw the video, it got angry. The bird did not want to be Tim's friend. The bird flew away, and Tim was left all alone. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty boy named Tom. He loved to play outside with his friends. One day, they saw dark clouds in the sky. They heard a loud sound like thunder. They all ran home to be safe. Tom went inside his house and saw his mom. She was in the kitchen. She was cutting an apple for him to eat. Tom was happy. He liked apples a lot. He ate the apple and felt strong like a mighty hero. Later, the thunder stopped and the sun came out. Tom and his friends went outside to play again. They felt brave and mighty. They knew they could face anything, even the loud thunder. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a pretty bird was in a tree. The bird liked to sing. The bird made a pretty noise. People liked to hear the bird sing. One day, a cat came to the tree. The cat saw the pretty bird. The cat wanted to catch the bird. The bird saw the cat and was scared. The bird did not sing. Then, the bird had an idea. The bird made a sign. The sign said, "I am not a bird. I am a cat." The cat saw the sign and was confused. The cat thought the bird was a cat too. The cat left the tree. The pretty bird was happy. The bird sang again. People liked to hear the pretty bird sing. The bird and the sign were safe in the tree.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big landscape with lots of trees, flowers, and animals. Tim loved playing outside and looking at all the colors. As Tim walked, he saw a big, angry bee. The bee was flying around, making loud noises. Tim was scared, but he wanted to help the bee. He said, "Why are you so angry, bee?" The bee said, "I am angry because I can't find my home. I am lost." Tim thought for a moment and said, "I can help you find your home, bee. Let's look together." As they searched, the angry bee saw a big flower. The bee flew close to it, and the flower slapped the bee with its petal! The bee was surprised but not hurt. Tim laughed and said, "Silly flower, don't slap our friend!" Finally, they found the bee's home. The bee was happy and said, "Thank you, Tim, for helping me." Tim smiled and said, "You're welcome, bee. Now you're not angry anymore." And they played together in the beautiful landscape until it was time for Tim to go home.

One day, a dog named Max found a collar. The collar was very pretty. Max wanted to wear the collar, but he did not know how to put it on. He asked his friend, a cat named Lily, to help him. "Can you help me put on this collar?" Max asked Lily. Lily was a calm cat and she said, "Yes, I can help you." Lily helped Max put on the collar. But when Max tried to run, the collar was too tight. Max did not like it. He wanted to destroy the collar. He asked Lily, "Can you help me take off the collar? I want to destroy it." Lily said, "No, Max. We should not destroy it. Let's make it bigger so it fits you better." They made the collar bigger and Max was happy. Now, Max and Lily played together with the collar on Max.
