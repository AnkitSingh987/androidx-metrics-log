Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Flowers

Lily liked to print with her crayons. She had many colors and papers. She printed flowers and stars and hearts. She gave some to her mom and dad. They smiled and said thank you. One day, Lily saw a big truck outside. It had many logs of lumber. They were brown and long and heavy. Lily wondered what they were for. She asked her mom. Her mom said they were for building a new house next door. Lily wanted to see the new house. She asked her mom if she could go. Her mom said yes, but be careful. The lumber was not for playing. It was for working. Lily said okay and put on her shoes. She ran to the new house. She saw many people and tools. They were cutting and hammering and measuring. They were making the lumber into walls and floors and roofs. Lily thought it was amazing. She saw a pile of lumber that was not used yet. It had a sign that said "Do not touch". Lily did not read the sign. She only saw the lumber. She thought it would be fun to print on it. She took her crayons and ran to the pile. She started to print on the lumber. She printed flowers and stars and hearts. She printed her name and her mom's name and her dad's name. She was happy and proud. But then, she heard a loud voice. It was the builder. He was angry. He saw Lily printing on the lumber. He said "Stop! What are you doing? You are ruining the lumber! This is not for printing. This is for building!" Lily was scared and sorry. She dropped her crayons and ran away. She cried and said "I'm sorry, I'm sorry, I didn't know". She ran to her mom and hugged her. Her mom said "It's okay, Lily, it's okay. You made a mistake. But you have to be careful. The lumber is not for printing. It is for working. You have to respect other people's things." Lily said "I'm sorry, mom, I'm sorry. I won't do it again. I just wanted to print something nice." Her mom said "I know, Lily, I know. You like to print. But you have to print on paper, not on lumber. Paper is for printing. Lumber is for building." Lily said "Okay, mom, okay. I understand. Can I still print on paper?" Her mom said "Of course, Lily, of course. You can print on paper as much as you want. But only on paper, okay?" Lily said "Okay, mom, okay. I love you." Her mom said "I love you too, Lily. Come on, let's go home and print some more."

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Ann. She was a good girl and helped her mom a lot. One day, Ann's mom asked her to polish her shoes. They were very filthy from playing in the rain. Ann said yes and started to clean the shoes. While Ann was cleaning the shoes, she found a shiny coin inside one of them. It was very pretty and bright. Ann was very happy and showed the coin to her mom. Her mom said that she could keep the coin if she promised to always help others. The next day, Ann saw a boy who was sad. He had lost his toy in the rain. Remembering her promise, Ann gave the boy her shiny coin. The boy was very happy and thanked Ann. From that day on, Ann always helped others and shared her things. And everyone in the town loved her.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big red statue. The statue was of a man with a hat. Tim asked his mom, "Can the statue talk?" His mom just smiled and shook her head. They walked around the park and played. Tim ran back to the red statue and said, "Hello, Mr. Statue!" To his surprise, the statue nodded and said, "Hello, Tim!" Tim was very happy. Tim ran to his mom and told her about the talking statue. At first, she did not believe him. But when they went back to the statue, it nodded and said, "Hello!" Tim's mom was so surprised. They all laughed and played together in the park.
