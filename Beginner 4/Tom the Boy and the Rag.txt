Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Rag

Once, there was a boy named Tom. He had a rag. It was a normal rag, but Tom loved it. He took it with him everywhere he went. One day, he went to a show with his mom and dad. At the show, people sang and danced. Tom was happy. Tom saw a girl on the stage. She was sad. She lost her rag. Tom thought, "I can help her." He gave her his normal rag. The girl said, "Thank you, Tom!" She was happy now. They danced together on the stage. The people at the show saw Tom and the girl. They liked their dance. They all clapped their hands. They said, "Good job, Tom!" Tom was very happy. He did not need his rag anymore. He had new friends and they all danced together.

Once upon a time there was a little tool. It was made out of wood and it was very quiet. It was all alone in the dark box, but it didn't mind. It liked being quiet. One day the tool was taken out of the box and it felt excited. It didn't know what was going to happen. Then a little boy came over and picked it up. He looked at it and smiled. He said, "This is a great tool, I'm going to make something special with it!" The little tool smiled and waited. The boy worked hard for a long time. He used the little tool to make something amazing. When he was done, he held it up and said, "Look, I made this!" The tool was so excited, it couldn't believe that it had helped make something so special. The little boy gave the tool a big hug, and thanked it for being so quiet. From that day forward, the little tool knew that it was loved and special.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a tall, colorful pole. The pole was very special. It was in the middle of the town square. All the people loved the colorful pole. One day, a big wind came. The wind was so strong, it made the colorful pole fall down. The people in the town were sad. They did not know what to do. The pole was too big for them to lift. A strong man from another town heard about the fallen pole. He came to the small town to help. With all his strength, he lifted the colorful pole. The people were so happy. They thanked the strong man. The colorful pole stood tall in the town square again.

Once upon a time, there was a gray bunny named Bob. Bob loved to hop around the green grass and enjoy the warm sun. One day, he found a yummy pastry on the ground. It was a sweet treat that he had never seen before. He wanted to eat it all by himself. Bob saw his friend, a little bird named Tim, flying nearby. Tim saw the pastry too and said, "Can I have some, please?" Bob thought for a moment and then said, "Yes, we can share the pastry." So, they both enjoyed the tasty treat together. After they finished eating, Bob and Tim played and laughed all day. They were very happy because they shared the pastry. The moral of the story is that sharing with friends makes everything more fun and enjoyable.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a blanket that he loved very much. One day, the blanket got a broken part. Tim was very sad. Tim's mom saw that he was sad. She said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can fix your blanket." Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Mom!" They took the blanket to Tim's grandma, who could sew very well. It was a hot day, and Tim saw an ice cream truck. He asked his mom if he could have some ice cream. His mom said yes, and Tim got a big ice cream cone. But the ice cream started to melt in the sun. Tim tried to eat it fast, but some of it dripped on his blanket. Tim's grandma saw the ice cream on the blanket. She said, "It's okay, Tim. I will wash the blanket and fix the broken part." Tim was happy. He knew his blanket would be as good as new. And from that day on, Tim was more careful with his ice cream and his blanket.
