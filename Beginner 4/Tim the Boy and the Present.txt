Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Present

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very happy because it was his birthday. He knew that he would get a present today. Tim went to his friend, Sara, to tell her about his birthday. Sara wanted to give Tim a present too. But she did not have any presents available. So, Sara made a sign that said "Happy Birthday, Tim!" and gave it to him. Tim loved the sign and said thank you to Sara. They played together all day. But then, it started to rain very hard. Tim's sign got all wet and the words went away. Tim was sad because his special sign was gone.

---

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a big motor car. It was red and shiny. Tim wanted to touch it, but his mom said, "No, Tim. That's not our car." Tim felt sad and ashamed. He didn't want to make his mom upset. So, he went to play with his ball. He threw the ball high in the air and tried to catch it. But the ball went too far and struck the big motor car. Tim was scared. Tim's mom came over and said, "It's okay, Tim. We will say sorry to the car's owner." They found the owner and said sorry. The owner was nice and said, "It's okay. Just be careful next time." Tim felt better and promised to be more careful.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a bell. The bell could ring. Lily liked the sound it made. It was amazing. She took the bell with her everywhere she went. She wanted to show her friends. Lily showed the bell to her friend Tom. Tom liked the bell too. He wanted to ring it. Lily let Tom ring the bell. They both laughed and gave each other a big hug. They played with the bell all day. But one day, Lily lost the bell. She looked everywhere for it. She asked Tom to help her find it. They could not find the bell. Lily was very sad. Tom gave her a hug, but she still missed her amazing bell.

One day, a girl named Lily wanted to decorate her cooler. She loved to draw and make things pretty. She got her colors and started to make the cooler look nice. The sun was shining, but it was not too hot. It was a good day to be outside. Lily's friend, Tim, came by to see what she was doing. He saw her cooler and thought it looked great. He wanted to help her decorate it. They both drew pictures on the cooler and had fun together. But then, the weather changed. The wind became harsh and cold. The sky turned gray. Lily and Tim were surprised. They did not know what to do. They picked up the cooler and ran inside. The cooler was not just pretty now, but it also kept their food warm in the cold weather. They were happy that they had decorated the cooler together.

One day, a big red van was driving down the road. A little boy named Tim saw the van and wanted to join the ride. He asked his mom, "Can I join the van, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but only if it is safe." So, they went to the van and met the driver. The driver was a very nice man. He said, "Come in, it is safe. I will take you to a fun place." They got in the van and started to drive. But then, something unexpected happened. The van turned into a big, shiny car! Tim and his mom were so surprised. The nice man said, "This is a magic car. It can take you anywhere you want to go!" Tim and his mom had a great day riding in the magic car. They knew they were safe and they had lots of fun. And they were very happy they decided to join the van that day.

Once upon a time, there was a little yellow bug. The bug liked to climb. One day, the bug saw a big tree. The bug wanted to climb the tree. The bug started to climb. It went up, up, up. The bug climbed an inch, then another inch. The bug was very happy. It liked to be high in the tree. The bug met a bird in the tree. The bird said, "Hi, bug! Why do you climb?" The bug said, "I like to see the world from high up." The bird and the bug became friends. They played in the tree every day.
