Category: Beginner
Title: The Puppy and the Bird

Once upon a time there lived a cute little puppy. He had bright eyes and four furry paws. Every day he would pull his leash and go for a walk. One day while walking in the park, the puppy spotted a small bird. He was so excited he started to pull his leash to chase the bird. Suddenly, his leash broke apart! The puppy started to worry. He sat by the broken leash and looked around for help. Just then, a kind-hearted man saw the puppy and said, "Don't worry little pup, I'll make some repairs!" The man took out a needle and some thread and started to mend the broken leash. Once the leash was mended, the man gave it a tug and said to the puppy, "Go on now and enjoy your playtime!" The puppy happily pawed the man's hand in thanks and ran off to find his feathered friend.

Once upon a time, in a busy little town, there was a girl named Sue. Sue loved to play outside. One day, she saw a pretty spring near her house. She would go there to gaze at the water and the fish. One day, a big boy named Tom came to the spring. He was not nice. He made the fish go away. Sue was sad. She wanted the fish to come back. Sue had a plan. She went to her big brother, Jim. Jim was strong. Sue told Jim about Tom. Jim went to the spring with Sue. He talked to Tom. Tom said he was sorry. He would not make the fish go away again. Sue was happy. She could gaze at the pretty spring and the fish again.

One day, a thoughtful girl named Mia noticed that her fridge was open. She went to her mom and said, "Mom, the fridge is open!" Her mom looked at the fridge and said, "Oh no! Let's see what is inside." They looked in the fridge and saw that all the food was gone. Mia and her mom were very sad. They did not know what to do. But then, they heard a noise coming from the living room. They went to see what it was. In the living room, they found Mia's little brother, Max, eating all the food from the fridge. He looked up and said, "I was hungry!" Mia and her mom laughed. They were happy to find the food and they all ate together. The open fridge was a surprise, but it made them all smile.

One day, a little boy named Mark went to the park with his mother. The sun was shining and the sky was blue. They had a fine time playing together. They played on the swings, the slide, and the seesaw. Mark was very happy. After a while, Mark's mother said, "It's time to go home now, Mark." But Mark didn't want to leave. He wanted to stay and play some more. So, he ran away from his mother and hid behind a big tree. Mark's mother looked for him, but she couldn't find him. She called out, "Mark, where are you?" But Mark didn't answer. He stayed very quiet. His mother started to cry. She was very sad and scared. Mark saw his mother crying and felt bad. He came out from behind the tree and said, "I'm sorry, mother. I didn't mean to make you cry." His mother hugged him tight, but she was still very sad. From that day on, Mark's mother was always scared when they went to the park. She didn't want to lose Mark again. They still went to the park sometimes, but it was never as fun as before.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys and make shapes with them. One day, she decided to prepare a surprise for her mom. Lily took her toy blocks and started to build a big heart shape. She worked very hard to make it look nice. When she was done, she called her mom to come and see her surprise. Her mom was so happy to see the generous heart shape that Lily made. She gave Lily a big hug and a kiss. They both smiled and played with the blocks together. And they lived happily ever after.
