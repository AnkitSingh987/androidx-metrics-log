Category: Beginner
Title: Emily the Girl and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a cute little girl named Emily. She was always excited and happy. One day, she was playing in the park when she saw something strange in the sandbox. She ran up to it and saw a big box. She asked her dad, "What is it?" Her dad replied, "Oh, it's a box of toys. Don't you want to open it?" Emily nodded yes and quickly set to work opening it. Inside the box was all sorts of crazy things - a fluffy bunny, a bouncing ball, and lots of colourful blocks. Emily smiled and said, "Wow! I can't believe it!" Her dad smiled too. He said, "Can you believe it, Emily? Let's play together and make something of all these crazy things!" And they did. Emily and her dad had a great time in the park, building and creating all sorts of things with the box of toys. What a fun day!

Once there was a very large tree in a meadow. It was so enormous, it seemed like it belonged in a storybook! One day, a little girl named Daisy walked through the meadow and saw the tree. She was in awe of the tree's beauty and size. She walked up to it, and lovingly hugged it. "You are so big and strong", said Daisy to the tree. Suddenly, the tree began to move, and an enormous bud started to bloom. Daisy was amazed! She watched as the bud grew bigger and bigger. It looked like it was glowing. Daisy could see colors of pink and yellow in the middle of the flower. It was so beautiful. "Hello, my little one", said the tree in a soft whisper. "Thank you for showing me kindness. The gift of love deserves the gift of beauty. Please, take this flower to remember me by." Daisy thanked the mighty tree for its wisdom, and carefully picked the flower. She looked up at the tree and smiled. From that day on, Daisy visited the meadow as often as she could, and she always brought a flower to show her gratitude.

Tom and Anna are friends. They like to play with round things. They have a ball, a hoop, and a wheel. They roll them, throw them, and spin them. They have fun. One day, they find a new round thing. It is shiny and has numbers on it. It is a watch. Tom likes it. He wants to have it. He takes it from Anna. "Give it back!" Anna says. "It is not yours!" "I want it!" Tom says. "It is pretty!" Anna is sad. She cries. The watch is important to her. It is her dad's watch. He gave it to her before he went away. She misses him. A lady sees them. She is Anna's mom. She comes to them. "What is wrong?" she asks. "Tom took my watch!" Anna says. "Tom, why did you do that?" the lady asks. "I like it!" Tom says. The lady is kind. She explains to Tom that the watch is not a toy. It is a special thing for Anna. It helps her remember her dad. She asks Tom to mind Anna's feelings. She asks him to give the watch back. Tom thinks. He is sorry. He does not want to hurt Anna. He gives the watch back. "Sorry, Anna," he says. "Thank you, Tom," Anna says. The lady smiles. She hugs them both. She tells them they are good friends. She tells them they can play with other round things. She gives them a cookie each. They are happy. They share the cookie. They play with the ball, the hoop, and the wheel. They have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He lived in a house with his mom, dad, and his dog, Max. They lived in a beautiful landscape with trees, flowers, and a big blue sky. One day, Tim was playing with Max outside. They were having fun, but Tim was in a hurry. He wanted to play with all his toys before dinner. So, he ran too fast and fell down. Tim was sad and his knee hurt. Max licked Tim's face to make him feel better. Tim's mom came outside and saw him crying. She hugged him and said, "When we hurry too much, we can get hurt. It's important to slow down and be careful." Tim learned that it's better to take his time and enjoy the fun with Max. And from that day on, Tim played more carefully and was a happier boy.
