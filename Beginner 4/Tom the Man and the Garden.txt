Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Man and the Garden

One day, a funny man named Tom liked to joke a lot. He made everyone laugh. Tom had a friend named Sam. Sam was a cook. Sam used a knife to cut food. One day, Sam was sad. He lost his knife. Tom wanted to help Sam. They looked for the knife together. They looked in the kitchen, the bedroom, and the garden. At last, they found the knife under the table. Sam was happy again. Tom made a joke about the knife. They both laughed. They were good friends and had a fun day.

One day, a cat and a dog were walking together. They saw a big, wet automobile on the road. The cat said, "I want to go inside the car." The dog said, "No, it is wet. We will get wet too." They walked away from the wet automobile and went to the park. In the park, they saw a big tree. The cat said, "Let's climb the tree!" The dog said, "No, I can't climb trees. Let's play on the ground." So they played on the ground. Later, they went back to the wet automobile. The cat and the dog talked about it. The cat said, "I still want to go inside the car." The dog said, "Okay, let's try it." They went inside the wet automobile and had lots of fun. The dog was happy that they tried something new.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy dog named Max. Max did not like to run or play. He just liked to sleep all day. One day, Max found a coin on the ground. The coin was shiny and pretty. Max decided to take the coin and find a friend to show it to. Max walked to the park and saw a cat named Luna. Max showed Luna the coin and asked her to accept it as a gift. Luna liked the coin very much and said, "Thank you, Max!" Max and Luna became good friends. Max and Luna played together every day. They were not lazy anymore. They ran, jumped, and had lots of fun. Max was happy that he found the coin and made a new friend. The end.

One day, a baby went to the park with his mom. They saw a big tree with tasty red apples. The baby wanted to eat one, so he asked, "Mom, can I have an apple, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but we must search for a low branch to pick one." The baby and his mom searched and searched but could not find a low branch. They saw a big dog nearby. The baby asked the dog, "Can you help us get an apple?" The dog barked, "Woof, woof!" and jumped up to get an apple. The dog got the tasty apple and gave it to the baby. The baby was happy and took a big bite. But the apple had a worm inside! The baby cried, and the mom felt sad. They went home, and the baby did not get to eat the tasty apple.

Once upon a time, there was a big magnet. The magnet lived in a wild place with lots of trees and grass. The magnet had many friends, like the ball, the stick, and the rock. One day, the magnet and its friends wanted to play a game. The stick said, "Let's settle down and play hide and seek!" All the friends agreed, and the magnet started to count. "One, two, three," the magnet counted, as the stick, the ball, and the rock ran to hide. The magnet looked for its friends in the wild place. First, it found the ball behind a tree. "I found you!" said the magnet. Then, it found the stick in the grass. "I found you too!" said the magnet. The magnet looked and looked, but it could not find the rock. The magnet called out, "Rock, where are you?" The rock came out from under a bush, and they all laughed. The friends played many games that day in the wild place. They had lots of fun together with the magnet, the ball, the stick, and the rock. When the sun went down, they settled down to rest, happy to have spent the day playing with each other.
