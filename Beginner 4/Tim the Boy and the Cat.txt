Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy weapon. It was a very expensive toy. He loved to play with it every day. One day, Tim met a talking cat. The cat said, "Hi, Tim! I like your weapon. Can we play together?" Tim was happy to talk with the cat and play together. They played with the weapon all day. Tim and the talking cat became best friends. They had lots of fun every day. And they lived happily ever after.

---

One day, a little boy named Tim found a puzzle. The puzzle had many pieces. Tim wanted to take the pieces and put them together. He thought it would be fun. Tim started to take each piece and tried to find where it went. It was not easy, but Tim did not give up. He kept trying and trying. Soon, he saw a picture in the puzzle. It was a picture of a happy dog. Tim was very happy when he put the last piece in the puzzle. He was successful! The puzzle was now all done. Tim showed his mom and dad. They were so proud of him. Tim felt good because he did not give up and finished the puzzle.

Jill and Ben were best friends. They liked to play in the park, share their toys and tell each other secrets. One day, they found a big box under a tree. They opened it and saw many shiny things inside. They were very happy and decided to take some of them home. But when they got to Jill's house, they had a problem. They had too many things to share. Jill wanted the necklace with the red stone, and Ben wanted the ring with the blue stone. They both said they saw them first. They started to fight and yell. They said mean things to each other. They forgot they were friends. Jill's mom heard the noise and came to see what was wrong. She saw the box and the shiny things. She looked very angry and scared. She said they had to give them back right away. She said they were not toys, but stolen jewels. She said the police were looking for them. She said they had to tell the truth or they would be in big trouble. Jill and Ben felt very bad. They realized they had made a big mistake. They said they were sorry and hugged each other. They said they didn't want to part or fight anymore. They said they wanted to be friends again. They followed Jill's mom to the park and gave back the jewels. They hoped the police would forgive them. They learned a bitter lesson that day. They learned that some things are not worth losing a friend over. They learned that telling the truth is always better than lying. They learned that playing with stolen jewels is not fun, but dangerous. They learned to be more careful and kind. They never forgot their friendship or their adventure.

One day, Tom went to play with his friend, Tim. They found a small teaspoon in the kitchen. Tom said, "Let's use this to dig in the yard." Tim shrugged and said, "Okay, let's go!" They went outside and started to dig with the teaspoon. They found a little box. Tim said, "What's inside?" Tom opened the box and saw a tiny, spoiled cake. "Ew!" said Tom, "This cake is bad." Tim and Tom laughed and went inside to tell Mom about their find. Mom said, "Good job, boys! Now, let's put the teaspoon back and wash our hands." They put the teaspoon away, washed their hands, and went to play again.

Once upon a time, there was a big hill. The hill was very tall and dark. On top of the hill, there was a little house. In the house, lived a boy and his mom. One day, the boy wanted to wear his new red hat. He put it on his head and went outside to play. He climbed up the dark hill, and the wind blew his hat away. The boy was sad, but he did not give up. He looked for the hat on the dark hill. At last, he found it near a big tree. The boy was happy and ran back home to show his mom. They both smiled and hugged.
