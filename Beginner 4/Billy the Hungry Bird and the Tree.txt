Category: Beginner
Title: Billy the Hungry Bird and the Tree

One day, a hungry bird named Billy went to visit his friend, Sally the squirrel. Billy loved to sing, and he knew Sally liked it too. As he flew to her tree, he sang a happy song. When Billy got to Sally's tree, he saw that she was sad. "Why are you sad, Sally?" asked Billy. Sally said, "I am hungry and I have no food." Billy thought about how he could help his friend. Billy remembered seeing a big tree with lots of nuts nearby. He told Sally, "I know where we can find food! Follow me!" They went to the big tree and found lots of nuts for Sally to eat. After they ate, Billy sang a happy song, and Sally felt much better. They played together all day, and from that day on, they always knew where to find food when they were hungry.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He loved to play on the slide. As he went up and down, he saw a big stick on the ground. He picked it up and said, "Look, Mom! I found a missile!" His mom looked and said, "Oh no, Tim! That's not a missile. It's just a stick. Be careful with it." Tim felt a little sad, but he still played with the stick. A poor dog named Max was also at the park. Max saw Tim's stick and wanted to play with it too. Max ran towards Tim and barked, "Woof! Woof! Can I play with your missile?" Tim was scared at first, but then he smiled and said, "Yes, let's play together!" As they played, Tim threw the stick and Max ran to catch it. But then, something unexpected happened. The stick hit a big rock and broke in half! Tim and Max were sad, but then they saw that the rock was really a hidden treasure chest! They opened it and found lots of fun toys to play with. Tim and Max became best friends and played at the park every day.

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat. The cat liked to watch TV. One day, the furry cat saw a big number on the TV. The number was "3". The cat was happy to see the number. The furry cat wanted to find the number "3" outside. So, the cat went out to look for it. The cat looked up and down, left and right. But the cat could not find the number "3". The furry cat was sad. It could not find the number "3". The cat walked back home. But when the cat got home, the TV was gone. The cat was very sad and could not watch the TV anymore. The end.

One day, a zebra had an original idea. It wanted to break a record. It started running faster and faster until it finally smashed the record. Everyone was so excited. The zebra celebrated by eating a big bunch of its favorite treats. It was so happy that it even sang a song. Everyone clapped and cheered. The zebra had made its original dream come true!

One day, a dog and a cat saw a hose in the yard. The dog said, "I know how to use the hose!" The cat said, "No, I know how to use the hose better!" They both wanted to show they were great at using the hose. The dog went first. He put the hose in his mouth and turned it on. Water went everywhere! The cat laughed and said, "You don't know how to use the hose!" The dog felt sad. Next, it was the cat's turn. She tried to hold the hose in her paws, but it was too hard. The water went everywhere again! The dog laughed and said, "You don't know how to use the hose either!" The cat felt sad too. They both tried to be great, but they did not know how to use the hose well. In the end, the yard was all wet, and the dog and cat were not happy.

One day, a warm sun shone on a big tree. A small cat lived in the tree. The cat liked to fit in a little box. The box was her home. A bird came to the tree. The bird had news. "A new friend is coming," said the bird. The cat was happy. She liked new friends. The new friend was a dog. The dog was nice. They all played under the warm sun. The cat, the bird, and the dog were happy. They were the best friends.
