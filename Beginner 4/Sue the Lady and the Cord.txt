Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Lady and the Cord

Once upon a time, there was an elderly lady named Sue. She had a long cord in her house. Sue liked to pretend with the cord. One day, Sue took the cord and pretended it was a big snake. She made the cord snake wiggle and dance. Sue had lots of fun with her pretend snake. The next day, Sue used the cord to pretend it was a jump rope. She played and jumped with the cord all day. Sue loved to pretend with her cord and have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that he loved to play with. It was his favorite toy, and he took it everywhere he went. One day, Tim was playing with his toy car when he saw a big rock. He thought it would be fun to drive his car up the rock. But as Tim pushed his car up the rock, the handle on the car broke. Tim felt very sad and thought it was a stupid idea to play with his car on the rock. He didn't know what to do. He was worried that his toy car was broken forever. Tim went to his mom and showed her the broken handle. His mom gave him a hug and said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can fix it together." They found some glue and fixed the handle back on the car. Tim was happy again and learned not to play with his toy car on big rocks.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom liked to play in his kitchen with his toy sink. One day, while playing, he saw a small bug near the sink. The bug was blinking its eyes. Tom felt a little scared but also curious. Tom said, "Hi, bug! Why do you blink your eyes near my sink?" The bug answered, "I feel comfortable here, and I like to watch you play." Tom felt happy that the bug liked to watch him. They became good friends. Every day, Tom and the bug played together by the sink. They had fun and felt comfortable with each other. Tom learned not to be scared of bugs, and the bug learned that humans can be kind. They were the best of friends, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, a little dog named Spot hurt his paw. He had a small cut and needed a bandage. Spot's mom, a kind dog named Daisy, put a bandage on his paw to help him feel better. Spot was very impatient. He wanted to run and play with his friends. But Daisy told him to wait. She said the bandage needed to stay on until his paw was all better. After a few days, it was time to replace the bandage. Daisy took off the old one and put on a new, clean bandage. Spot was so happy because his paw felt better. Now, he could run and play with his friends again. And they all lived happily ever after.

Anna liked to wander on the beach with her mom and dad. She liked to see the water, the sand, and the shells. Sometimes she saw birds and crabs too. She liked to run and jump and play. One day, she saw something new on the beach. It was big and round and gray. It had a nose, two eyes, and whiskers. It also had flippers, but no legs. It was a seal. Anna was curious and wanted to see it closer. She ran to the seal and said hello. The seal looked at her and made a noise. It sounded like "arf arf". Anna smiled and said "arf arf" back. She touched the seal's fur. It was wet and soft. The seal liked Anna and wagged its tail. Anna's mom and dad came to see the seal too. They said it was a friendly seal and it must have come from the sea. They said Anna was lucky to meet it. They took a picture of Anna and the seal. Anna gave the seal a hug and a kiss. She said goodbye and thank you. The seal said "arf arf" and waved its flipper. Anna waved back. She found a purple shell on the sand and gave it to the seal as a gift. The seal took it and nodded. Anna was happy. She had a new friend.
