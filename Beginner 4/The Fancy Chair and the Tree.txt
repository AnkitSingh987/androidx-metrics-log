Category: Beginner
Title: The Fancy Chair and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a fancy chair. It had a big, soft seat and pretty colors. The chair lived in a big house with a happy family. Every day, the family would sit on the chair and laugh, play, and talk. One day, the family went away. They left the door close, but the wind blew it open. The fancy chair felt lonely and sad. It wanted to find the family and be happy again. So, the chair went out the door to look for them. The chair walked and walked until it saw a big tree. The tree said, "I can help you find your family." The chair was so happy! But then, the tree said, "First, you must help me. My friend, the bird, lost her home. She needs a new place to sit." The chair agreed to help the bird. The bird sat on the fancy chair and made a nest. The chair was not lonely anymore. The chair, the tree, and the bird were all happy together. The fancy chair realized it didn't need to find the family; it had found new friends instead.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty chair that could talk. It had big legs and a soft seat. The chair lived in a house with a little boy named Tim. One sunny day, Tim and the chair decided to race. They went outside and stood at the start line. Tim said, "Ready, set, go!" And off they went! Tim ran fast, but the chair was faster. The chair's big legs made it go really quick. As they raced, Tim's friend Sam came outside and saw them. Sam laughed and said, "Wow, a chair that can race! That's so fun!" Tim and the chair finished their race, and they all played together. The mighty chair had a great day with its new friends.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a boy named Tom. Tom had a special gift. He could understand the language of animals. This was very unusual, but Tom loved it. One sunny day, Tom went to the park to play. He saw a bird on a tree. The bird was singing a pretty song. Tom said, "Hello, bird! I like your song." The bird was surprised and replied, "Hello, Tom! You can understand my language!" Tom and the bird became friends. They played in the park every day. One day, the bird pointed at a shiny thing on the ground. "Look, Tom! It's a shiny stone!" Tom picked it up and showed it to his mom. She was very happy and said, "This is a special stone, Tom. Keep it safe." Tom and the bird continued to play and talk every day. They had many fun adventures together. And that is how Tom learned that his unusual gift of understanding the language of animals could bring happiness and friendship.

Lily and Tom were playing in the park. They liked to swing, slide and run. But they did not like the big dog that lived next to the park. The dog was mean and loud. It always barked and chased them. One day, the dog got out of its fence. It ran towards Lily and Tom. They were scared and ran away. But the dog was faster. It caught up with Tom and bit his leg. Tom cried out in pain. Lily saw what happened. She was brave. She picked up a stick and hit the dog. The dog let go of Tom and ran away. Lily helped Tom get up. She hugged him and said, "It's okay, Tom. You are brave too. Let's go home and tell mom." Mom was at home. She saw Tom's leg. It was bleeding and had teeth marks. She was angry and worried. She cleaned and bandaged Tom's leg. She gave him a kiss and said, "You are very brave, Tom. I'm proud of you. But you should not play near the dog. It is dangerous." She called the dog's owner. She told him what happened. She said, "You should punish your dog. It hurt my son. It is not nice. You should keep your dog in your fence. Or I will call the police." The dog's owner was sorry. He said, "I'm sorry, mom. I did not know my dog got out. I will punish him. I will give him no treats and no toys. I will keep him in my fence. I hope your son is okay." Mom said, "Thank you, sir. I hope your dog learns to be nice. Goodbye." Mom hung up the phone. She smiled at Lily and Tom. She said, "You are both brave and good. I love you. How about we have some ice cream?" Lily and Tom smiled too. They said, "Yes, please!" They forgot about the dog. They were happy.
