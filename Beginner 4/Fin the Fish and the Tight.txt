Category: Beginner
Title: Fin the Fish and the Tight

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. He lived in a big sea with his family. Fin loved to swim around and play with his friends. One day, Fin found a tight hole in the sea. He was curious and wanted to see what was inside. As he swam into the tight hole, he started to suffer. The water inside was full of salt, and it made his eyes hurt. Just when Fin thought he couldn't take it anymore, something unexpected happened. The water around him changed and became less salty. Fin felt better and swam out of the tight hole. He learned that sometimes, things can change for the better when you least expect it.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Jack and Jill. They loved to do everything together, like playing and dancing. One day, Jack and Jill were so excited to dance together. They held hands and started to twirl around. They were making so many funny shapes that they even made their mom smile. Suddenly, they heard a ringing sound. Jack knew what it was and ran to the telephone in the corner of the room. He picked up the receiver and said hello. At the other end was an old man. He said, "The party is dead. Nobody wants to dance." Jack and Jill looked at each other and said in unison, "Let's dance!" And that's what they did. The old man laughed and told them to keep dancing and enjoying life. So they did, and they danced until they were too tired to keep going.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went to play in the park. They saw a big, fake bird near the trees. The bird was not real, but Tim and Spot did not know that. Tim was brave and went to touch the bird. Spot barked and wagged his tail. Tim touched the bird and it fell into a puddle of water. The bird became wet. Tim and Spot laughed. The wet bird looked funny. They picked up the bird and showed it to their friends. "Look at this wet bird!" said Tim. His friends laughed too. Suddenly, the wet bird let out a loud scream. Tim, Spot, and their friends were scared. They did not know that the bird could scream. They ran away from the bird and hid behind a tree. They peeked out and saw the bird was still there. They laughed again, knowing the bird was just a fake, and continued to play in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who was three years old. She was playing in her garden when she found a club. She was so excited that she ran to her mom and showed it to her. Mom said, "That looks like a good club. What are you going to do with it?" The little girl said, "I'm going to bury it in the dirt and make it disappear!" Mom smiled and said, "You can do whatever you want but be careful not to get too dirty." So the little girl took her club and started digging a hole. She buried her club and then patted the dirt over it. When she was finished, she stood up and said, "Now I know where it is! I can come back anytime and find it!" Mom said, "That's a great idea, it's a good way to remember where you buried it." The little girl smiled and said, "Yes, I think burying my club was a good idea!" The little girl took her club and went off to find other adventures. She never forgot where she buried her club.

Sam liked to ride his motorcycle. It was red and shiny and made a loud noise. He felt big and strong when he rode it around the park. He liked to show off to his friends and make them say wow. One day, he saw a new girl in the park. She had long hair and a pink dress. She was playing with a ball. Sam wanted to impress her. He rode his motorcycle closer to her and said, "Hi, I'm Sam. Do you like my motorcycle?" The girl looked at him and smiled. She said, "Hi, I'm Lily. Your motorcycle is nice, but can you do this?" She threw the ball high in the air and caught it with one hand. She looked playful and happy. Sam tried to do the same. He threw his ball high in the air and reached for it with one hand. But he forgot to hold his motorcycle with the other hand. His motorcycle fell to the ground and made a loud crash. The ball bounced away and hit a tree. Sam felt a pain in his arm and a tear in his eye. He felt embarrassed. He wanted to hide. He looked at Lily and saw her laughing. He thought she was laughing at him. He felt angry and sad. He said, "You are mean. You made me fall. I don't like you." Lily stopped laughing and said, "I'm sorry. I didn't mean to make you fall. I was just having fun. Are you okay? Do you need a hug?" Sam looked at her and saw her eyes were kind. He felt a warm feeling in his chest. He said, "I'm okay. It was an accident. I'm sorry I yelled at you. Do you want to be my friend?" Lily nodded and said, "Yes, I want to be your friend. Can I ride your motorcycle with you?" Sam smiled and said, "Sure, you can ride with me. But you have to hold on tight. It's fast and fun." They helped each other pick up the motorcycle and the ball. They got on the motorcycle and rode around the park. They laughed and talked and played. They forgot about the fall and the embarrassment. They were happy and playful. They were friends.
