Category: Beginner
Title: Mark the Boy and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Mark. Mark was a weak boy who did not like to eat. One day, he met a big, friendly dog. The dog was hungry and wanted some food. "Hello, dog," said Mark. "Why are you hungry?" "I need to eat," said the dog. "I am weak because I did not eat today." Mark wanted to help the dog. He went to his mom and asked for some food. His mom gave him a big bowl of food for the dog. Mark took the food to the dog and said, "Here, dog. Eat this food. It will make you strong." The dog ate the food and felt much better. The dog was not weak anymore. Mark and the dog became good friends. They played together every day. Mark learned that eating is important to be strong and healthy.

Once upon a time, there was a big, deep hole in the ground. In the hole, there was a little door with a round knob. A tiny mouse named Tim lived behind the door. One day, Tim came out to play. He saw a big cat named Tom. Tom liked to tease Tim. Tom would say, "I am going to get you, little mouse!" Tim would run back to the hole and hide behind the door. One day, Tim had an idea. He put a big, deep box near the hole. When Tom came to tease Tim, Tim ran to the box. Tom followed Tim, but fell into the deep box. Tim laughed and said, "Now you can't tease me, big cat!" Tim ran back to the hole and closed the door with the round knob.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to drink juice. One day, she was very sad. Her juice cup was empty. Sue asked her mom to fill the cup with juice. Her mom filled the cup with yummy apple juice. Sue's face lit up with a big smile. She drank the juice and felt happy again. Sue shared her juice with her friends, and they all had fun. Sue was not sad anymore. They all played together and laughed.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside. He liked to run and jump in the warm sun. One day, Spot saw a big, cold box in the yard. He did not know what it was. Spot walked up to the cold box and touched it. He imagined it was a big ice cube. Suddenly, the cold box opened. Spot jumped back, scared. Out came a little cat, shivering from the cold. Spot had never seen a cat before. Spot and the cat looked at each other. They did not know if they should be friends or not. But then, Spot had an idea. He showed the cat how to play in the warm sun. The cat liked it, and they became best friends. And that's how Spot and the cat found fun in something unexpected.

Once upon a time, there was a big, thick tree. It was home to many birds. One day, a big, loud missile came to the tree. The missile wanted to improve its aim. It wanted to hit the tree. The birds were scared. The birds did not want the missile to hit their home. They wanted to stop the missile. They had an idea. They could use their teamwork to improve the missile's heart. So, they sang a beautiful song to the missile. The missile heard the birds' song. It felt happy and did not want to hit the tree anymore. The missile's heart improved. It did not want to hurt the birds' home. The birds and the missile became friends. They lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a graceful bee named Blinky. Blinky lived in a big hive with all her bee friends. Blinky could blink her eyes very fast. She was the only bee in the hive who could do that. One day, Blinky met a new friend named Buzz. Buzz was amazed by Blinky's blinking eyes. "Wow, you can blink so fast!" said Buzz. Blinky smiled and said, "Yes, I can! It's fun to blink my eyes." Blinky and Buzz played together every day. They flew around the hive and blinked their eyes at each other. All the other bees loved to watch them play. Blinky and Buzz were the best of friends, and they all lived happily ever after.
