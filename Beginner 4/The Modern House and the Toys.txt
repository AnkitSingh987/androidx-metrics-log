Category: Beginner
Title: The Modern House and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a modern house. In the house, there was a girl named Lily. Lily liked to spend her days playing with her pastel toys. One day, Lily's friend, Max, came to visit. Max saw the pastel toys and said, "I like your toys, Lily! Can I play with them too?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, Max! Let's play together!" Lily and Max spent the whole day playing with the pastel toys. They built a big tower and a small house. They laughed and had a lot of fun. When it was time for Max to go home, they both said, "We had a great day! Let's play again soon!"

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a filthy street. On this street lived a little boy named Tim. Tim had a friend named Sam. They liked to play together on the street. One day, Tim and Sam found a box. Tim said, "Let's number the rocks we find and put them in the box!" So they started to look for rocks and count them. They found one, two, three, four, five rocks. As they were playing, a big mean dog came down the street. The dog saw Tim and Sam and started to bark loudly. Tim and Sam got scared and ran away. They left the box with the numbered rocks on the filthy street. The big mean dog knocked over the box and the rocks fell out. Tim and Sam were very sad that they lost their rocks and couldn't play with them anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little classroom. In this classroom, there were many kids who loved to learn. One day, their teacher, Miss Tina, had a fun idea. She wanted to teach the kids how to add. Miss Tina was very persistent. She knew that if she kept trying, the kids would learn to add. She drew two apples on the board and then added one more apple. "What do we have now?" she asked the kids. One little boy, named Timmy, raised his hand. "Three apples!" he said with a big smile. Miss Tina was so happy that her students were learning to add. The next day, Miss Tina brought some toy cars to the classroom. She wanted the kids to practice adding with the cars. She gave Timmy two cars and asked him to add one more car. Timmy counted the cars and said, "Now I have three cars!" Miss Tina and the kids in the classroom were so proud of their new skill. They knew that with Miss Tina's persistent help, they could learn anything. And they all lived happily ever after, adding and learning together.

One day, a little girl named Mia wanted to cross the street. She saw a lot of cars and traffic. She knew she had to wait for the cars to stop. Mia looked left and right, then left again. Mia's mom said, "I will allow you to cross, but only when it is safe." Mia nodded and kept looking. They waited until the cars stopped and the light turned green. Mia held her mom's hand as they crossed the street together. After crossing the street, Mia and her mom went to the store. They bought a big, sour lemon. Mia tasted it and made a funny face. She said, "Mom, this is so sour!" They both laughed and went home to make lemonade.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lucy. Lucy loved her pants. They were red and modern. She wore them every day. One day, Lucy went to play with her friend, Tom. Tom saw her pants and said, "Wow, I love your pants! They look so modern!" Lucy smiled and said, "Thank you! I love them too!" Together, Lucy and Tom played all day. They ran, jumped, and laughed. Lucy's pants made her feel happy and special. And that is the story of Lucy and her modern pants.

Once upon a time, there was a little brown shoe. The shoe liked to travel far and wide. It had a friend named Tim. Tim and the shoe would go on adventures together. One day, the shoe said to Tim, "Let's travel to the big hill!" Tim was excited and agreed. They walked and walked until they reached the hill. The shoe and Tim were so happy. As they climbed the hill, they saw many fun things. They saw a big tree, a pretty flower, and a small pond. When they reached the top of the hill, they looked around and saw their home far away. They laughed and said, "What a great adventure!" Then, they went back home, ready for their next trip.
