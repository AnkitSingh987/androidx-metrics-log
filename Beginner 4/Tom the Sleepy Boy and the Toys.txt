Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Sleepy Boy and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy boy named Tom. Tom was a youth who loved to store his toys in a big box. One day, Tom felt very tired, so he went to bed early. That night, something unexpected happened. While Tom was sleeping, his toys came to life! They played with each other and made a big mess. When Tom woke up, he was very surprised to see his toys all over the place. Tom learned that it is important to put things away and keep his room clean. He did not want his toys to make a mess again. So, he put them back in the big box and promised to always store them when he was done playing. And from that day on, Tom's room was always tidy.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw his friend, Sue, and wanted to invite her to play. They liked to play with ice. Tim and Sue were very happy to see each other. They found a big ice block in the park. They wanted to slide on the ice very fast. Tim went first and had fun. Then, it was Sue's turn to slide. She was scared but she tried. When Sue slid on the ice, something unexpected happened. The ice broke and there was a big hole. Sue didn't fall in the hole, but they found a big toy inside. They were so surprised and happy. They played with the toy all day.

One day, a big fish named Fin swam in a pond. He liked to splash and play with his friends. They all had fun in the water. The sun was warm, and the water was cool. It was a happy day. Then, Fin saw a yummy bug near a hook. He wanted to eat the bug. Fin's friend, Bubbles, said, "No, Fin! Don't eat the bug! It's near a hook!" But Fin did not listen. He swam fast to get the yummy bug. As Fin ate the bug, the hook caught him. He cried, "Help, help! The hook got me!" His friends swam fast to help him. They pulled and pulled. At last, the hook came out. Fin was free. He hugged his friends and said, "Thank you for helping me. I will listen next time."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to clap her hands and dance. One day, she found a big cup of cocoa in the kitchen. Sue was very happy because she loved cocoa. Sue took a sip of the cocoa, but it tasted terrible! She did not like it at all. Sue was sad and did not want to dance or clap anymore. She wanted to find out why the cocoa tasted so bad. Sue asked her mom for help. Her mom tasted the cocoa and found out that she had put too much salt in it. They made a new cup of cocoa together, and it tasted very good. Sue was happy again, and she clapped her hands and danced with joy.

One day, a girl named Sue found a pretty scarf. It was red and soft. Sue loved her new scarf and wore it every day. Her friend, Tim, saw the scarf and felt envious. He wanted a scarf like that too. One day, Tim said to Sue, "I like your scarf. Where did you find it?" Sue told him that she found it in the park. Tim went to the park to look for a scarf. He looked and looked, but he could not find one. Then, something unexpected happened. Tim saw a little bird shivering in the cold. He felt sad for the bird. He took off his coat and wrapped it around the bird to keep it warm. The bird seemed happy and flew away. The next day, the bird came back with a beautiful scarf for Tim. It was blue and soft. Now, Sue and Tim both had lovely scarfs and were very happy.

Once upon a time, in a bright sunny land, there lived a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play outside and make new friends. One day, she saw a big, strong wind called Hurricane. Hurricane was sad because he didn't have any friends. Everyone was scared of him. Lily walked up to Hurricane and said, "Hi, I am Lily. Do you want to be my friend?" Hurricane looked surprised and said, "Really? You're not scared of me?" Lily smiled and said, "No, I think you're cool. We can be friends." Together, they played and had fun. Hurricane learned to bow and say sorry when he made a mess with his strong winds. Lily taught him how to be gentle and kind. Soon, everyone in the land saw that Hurricane was not so scary after all. They all became friends and played together. The moral of the story is not to judge someone by their looks or what they can do. Everyone can learn to be kind and gentle if given a chance.
