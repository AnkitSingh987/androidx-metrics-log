Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Ben and the Hill

Sara and Ben are friends. They like to play with their scooters. One day, they go to the park with their moms. The park has a big path for scooters. "Can I lead the way?" Sara asks Ben. "Okay, you can lead," Ben says. He is cheerful. He likes to follow Sara. Sara and Ben ride their scooters fast. They go past trees and flowers and birds. They say hello to other kids. They have fun. But then, Sara sees a big hill. She is scared. She does not want to go down the hill. "Ben, I don't want to go down the hill," Sara says. She stops her scooter. Ben stops his scooter too. He sees that Sara is scared. He is kind. "Don't worry, Sara. I will help you. We can go down the hill slowly. I will hold your hand," Ben says. Sara smiles. She is happy. She trusts Ben. "Thank you, Ben. You are a good friend," Sara says. They hold hands and go down the hill slowly. They are careful. They are brave. They reach the bottom of the hill. They are proud. They hug each other. They ride their scooters back to their moms. They tell them about their adventure. Their moms are proud too. They give them hugs and kisses. Sara and Ben are friends. They like to play with their scooters. They are cheerful.

Lily liked to help her mom with the dishes. She would put the dirty plates and cups in the dishwasher and push the buttons. She felt very tidy and proud. One day, mom was busy on the phone and did not see Lily go to the kitchen. Lily wanted to help again, but she did not know that the dishwasher was already full and running. She opened the door and saw the water and soap bubbles. She thought they looked fun and pretty. She took out some plates and cups and put them on the floor. She poured some water and soap on them and started to scrub them with a sponge. She made a big mess and did not hear mom calling her name. Mom came to the kitchen and saw the water and soap everywhere. She saw Lily playing with the dishes and making more bubbles. She was very angry and scared. She shouted at Lily and took her away from the kitchen. "Lily, what have you done? You made a big mess and wasted the water and soap. You could have broken the dishes or hurt yourself. You are not helping, you are making trouble. Don't you ever repeat this again!" Lily started to cry and felt very sad and sorry. She did not mean to make mom angry or make a mess. She just wanted to help and have fun. She wished she had listened to mom and left the dishwasher alone. She learned a hard lesson that day.

One day, a dirty dog was playing in the park. The dog loved to dig and get dirty. A cop saw the dog and walked up to the dog's owner. The cop said, "Please keep your dog clean. We do not permit dirty dogs in the park." The owner felt sad and took the dog home. They gave the dog a bath and made him clean. The next day, they went back to the park. The cop saw them and smiled. "Good job," he said. "Now your dog can play here." Just then, a little girl lost her toy in the sand. The dirty dog saw her cry and wanted to help. He started to dig and found the toy. The girl was happy and hugged the dirty dog. The cop and the owner saw this and learned that sometimes it is okay to be dirty if it helps others.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park with her mom. They saw a small duck near the pond. Sue asked her mom, "Can we feed the duck?" Her mom said, "Yes, we can feed the duck, but we need to find food for it." Sue and her mom looked around and found a big tree with lots of normal-sized pins under it. Sue's mom said, "These pins are too big for the duck to eat. We need to find something else." Sue looked sad, but she kept looking. Then, something unexpected happened. A talking squirrel came down from the tree and said, "I heard you need food for the duck. I can help you!" The squirrel ran up the tree and came back with a small bag of seeds. Sue and her mom were very surprised, but they were happy. They thanked the squirrel and went to feed the duck. The duck was very happy too, and they all had a fun day at the park.
