Category: Beginner
Title: The Humble Oak Tree and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a small forest, there was a humble oak tree. The oak tree had many friends, like the birds, squirrels, and rabbits. They all loved to play around the tree and listen to its stories. One day, a little bird wanted to make her nest in the oak tree. She asked the tree if she could polish its branches to make them nice and clean for her new home. The oak tree was happy to help and let the bird polish its branches. While the bird was polishing the branches, she found a hidden door in the tree. The bird told everyone about the door, and they all gathered around to see what was inside. When they opened the door, they found a room full of shiny treasures! The animals were surprised and happy. The humble oak tree shared the treasures with all of its friends, and they all learned that when you help others, good things can happen. And from that day on, the forest was a happier place for everyone.

Sara and Tom like to play with their toys. They have many toys in their cabinet. They have cars, dolls, blocks, and puzzles. Sara likes puzzles. She likes to match the pieces and make pictures. Tom likes cars. He likes to zoom them on the floor and make noises. One day, Sara and Tom want to play together. Sara takes out a puzzle and asks Tom, "Do you want to help me match the pieces?" Tom says, "No, thank you. I want to play with my cars." Sara says, "OK, you play with your cars and I play with my puzzle." Sara and Tom play for a while. Sara matches the pieces and makes a picture of a farm. She sees cows, pigs, chickens, and a tractor. She is happy. Tom zooms his cars and makes noises. He pretends he is a driver. He is happy too. But then, Tom gets bored. He wants to try something new. He sees Sara's puzzle and thinks, "Maybe I can match the pieces too." He goes to Sara and says, "Can I play with your puzzle?" Sara says, "Sure, you can help me. There are still some pieces left." Sara and Tom start to match the pieces together. They find the pieces that fit and make the picture bigger. They see a barn, a fence, a dog, and a farmer. They are both happy. They smile and say, "We did it! We matched the pieces and made a picture of a farm. It looks fine!" They high-five and hug. They put the puzzle back in the cabinet and take out another one. They say, "Let's play again!"

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to slide, swing and run. They saw a shiny thing on the ground. It was a coin. Lily wanted to pick it up. "Look, Ben, a coin! Can I have it?" Lily asked. Ben shook his head. "No, Lily, it is not yours. Someone else dropped it. Maybe they need it. We should leave it here." Lily did not listen. She was careless. She ran to the coin and picked it up. She put it in her pocket. She smiled. "Ha, ha, I have a coin. I can buy candy. You don't have anything," Lily said. Ben was sad. He did not like Lily's words. He did not want to play with her anymore. He walked away. Lily felt lonely. She looked at the coin. It was not fun. It did not make her happy. She wished she had Ben back. She was sorry. "Ben, Ben, wait! I am sorry. I was wrong. You can have the coin. I don't want it. I want to play with you," Lily called. Ben heard Lily. He turned around. He saw her face. She looked sad. He felt sorry too. "OK, Lily, I forgive you. But we should not keep the coin. We should give it back. Maybe we can find the person who lost it. Or we can give it to someone who needs it more. That would be nice," Ben said. Lily nodded. She took the coin out of her pocket. She gave it to Ben. They held hands. They looked for the person who lost the coin. They were happy. They were friends.
