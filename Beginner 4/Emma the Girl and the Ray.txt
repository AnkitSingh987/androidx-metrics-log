Category: Beginner
Title: Emma the Girl and the Ray

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Emma. Emma was three years old and always very curious. One day while she was playing, she noticed a pale x-ray in her house. "Mommy, what's that?" asked Emma. Her Mother smiled at her and said, "That's an x-ray. It can tell us what's inside your body." "What does it mean, Mommy?" "Well, it can help us if we need to replace something," her Mom explained. Emma became very interested and looked closer. The x-ray was very mysterious and strange. At the end of the day, Emma had a much better understanding of what an x-ray was and how it could help her if she needed to replace something inside her body.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Tim. Tim liked to play and run around the house. One day, Tim saw a long cable on the floor. He wanted to play with it, so he ran towards it. As Tim played with the cable, he did not see that it was tangled. Tim tried to jump over it but he could not. Tim started to stumble and he fell on the floor. He was sad and did not want to play anymore. Tim's friend, a little bird named Sue, saw what happened. Sue flew to Tim and said, "Don't be sad, Tim. We all stumble sometimes. Let's play together and be more careful." Tim smiled and they played together. They had lots of fun and were more careful with the cable.

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play in the garden. One day, they saw a big hole in the fence. They wanted to know what was on the other side. They crawled through the hole and found a big pile of trash. They saw cans, bottles, papers, and other filthy things. They thought it was fun to dig and explore. They found a shiny thing that looked like a toy. Tom picked it up and pressed a button. He felt a shock in his hand. He dropped the shiny thing and cried. Lily ran to him and hugged him. She was scared too. They heard a loud bark. A big dog came out of the trash. It had dirty fur and sharp teeth. It looked angry. It ran towards Tom and Lily. They screamed and crawled back to the hole. They made it to the other side and ran to their house. They told their mom what happened. She was very worried. She checked Tom's hand and put a bandage on it. She said they should never go to the trash pile again. She said it was dangerous and dirty. She gave them a hug and a cookie. Tom and Lily learned their lesson. They decided to play in the garden with their toys and their cat. They were happy and safe.

One day, a nice man wanted to sell a big lion. This lion was not like other lions. It was harmless. It would not hurt anyone. The nice man took the lion to the park to show everyone. In the park, a little boy saw the lion and was scared. The nice man said, "Don't be scared. This lion is harmless. It will not hurt you." The little boy looked at the lion and saw it was friendly. He went closer and touched the lion's soft fur. The little boy and the lion became good friends. They played in the park all day. People saw how harmless the lion was and wanted to buy it. But the nice man said, "This lion has found a new friend. It will stay with the little boy." The little boy and the harmless lion were very happy together.

One day, a gentle cat named Lily was walking in the house. She saw a shiny ornament hanging on a tree. Lily thought it was a toy, so she jumped and tried to chew it. "Stop, Lily!" said Mom. "That ornament is not a toy. It is for the tree." Lily was sad and walked away. She wanted to find a toy to play with. Then, she saw a ball on the floor. She started to play with the ball. But, the ball was not a ball. It was a bug! The bug was scared and said, "Please, don't chew me! I am a friendly bug." Lily was surprised. She did not know bugs could talk. She said, "I am sorry, bug. I will not chew you." From that day, Lily and the bug became best friends. They played together every day and had lots of fun.
