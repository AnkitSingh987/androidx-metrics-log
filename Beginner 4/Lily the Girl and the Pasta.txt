Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Pasta

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small, modest house with her mom, dad, and her cat, Whiskers. Lily loved to eat pasta for dinner. One day, her mom made her favorite pasta for dinner. At dinner time, Lily's mom called her to the table. "Lily, come eat your pasta!" she said. Lily ran to the table and saw the yummy pasta. She started to eat it very fast, making a big mess on her face. Her mom and dad laughed, and Lily felt a little embarrass. Lily's dad said, "Lily, slow down when you eat. You don't want to make a mess." Lily nodded and started to eat her pasta slower. She didn't want to feel embarrass again. Whiskers jumped on the table and licked some pasta off Lily's face. They all laughed and enjoyed their dinner together.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin lived in a big pond with his family. He had a lot of friends and they liked to play together. One day, Fin saw a shiny rod near the pond. He swam closer to see what it was. As Fin got near the rod, he felt something pull him out of the water. He was scared and didn't know what to do. He tried to escape but it was hard. His friends saw him and they were envious because they thought he was going on a big adventure. Fin tried very hard to escape and soon, he was able to break free. He fell back into the pond and his friends cheered. They were happy to have Fin back with them. From that day on, they all stayed away from the shiny rod and played together safely in the pond.

Once upon a time, there was a slow puppy named Zip. Zip loved to play with his ball. One day, Zip saw a big tree with a hole in it. He thought, "I will hide my ball there for later." The next day, Zip went to play with his friends. He saw a fast bunny, a big bear, and a tiny bird. They all wanted to play with Zip's ball, but Zip said, "No, it is my ball. I hid it in the tree." The friends were sad, but they played other games and had fun. Later, Zip went to the tree to get his ball. But it was gone! He was very sad. He looked and looked, but he could not find it. His friends came to help him. They found the ball in a bush near the tree. Zip learned that sharing is good, and it is more fun to play with friends. From that day on, Zip shared his ball with everyone, and they all had a great time playing together.

One day, a big bus arrived in a small town. The bus had a huge sign on it. The sign had a picture of a big, red ball. A man got off the bus and said, "Come see the big, red ball!" All the kids in the town went to see the big, red ball. They were so excited! But when they got there, the big, red ball was not huge. It was small. The kids were sad. A little girl said, "We can still play with the small ball!" So all the kids played with the small ball and had fun. The man from the bus saw them and said, "You are right. Big or small, we can have fun with all balls." The kids learned that it is not the size of the toy that makes it fun. It is how you play with it that matters. And they all played happily together with the small, red ball.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a sleepy cat named Tom. Tom was a very lazy cat who loved to nap all day. One day, while Tom was sleeping under a big tree, he heard a loud noise. He woke up and saw a small bird on the ground. The bird was crying because it could not fly. Tom wanted to help the bird, so they went on a journey to discover what was wrong. They walked and walked until they found a big triangle. The triangle was stuck in the bird's wing. The bird told Tom that the triangle was making it hard for him to fly. Tom used his sharp claws to gently take out the triangle from the bird's wing. The bird was so happy that he could fly again. Tom felt proud and happy that he could help his new friend. From that day on, Tom and the bird became best friends, and Tom was not so sleepy anymore.
