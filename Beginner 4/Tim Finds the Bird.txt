Category: Beginner
Title: Tim Finds the Bird

One sunny day, a cricket named Tim wanted to soar. He tried and tried, but he could not fly. He saw a big bird named Bob. Tim asked, "Bob, can you help me soar?" Bob said, "Yes, I can help you." He put Tim on his back and they soared up high. Tim was happy. They flew over the perfect green park. Tim saw his friends down below. They were amazed and cheered for him. When they landed, Tim thanked Bob. He learned that it was good to ask for help. Tim also learned that friends can help you do things you can't do alone. And that was the perfect day for Tim and his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a wise cat. The cat had many friends. The cat liked to help them. But one day, the cat started to complain. The cat said, "I am tired." The cat's friends were sad. They wanted to help the wise cat. They thought of a plan. They wanted to surprise the cat. They made a big, soft bed for the cat to rest on. The wise cat saw the bed. The cat was happy and jumped on it. But then, something unexpected happened. The bed started to move! It was not a bed, but a big turtle. The turtle said, "I heard you were tired. Let me take you for a ride." The wise cat and the turtle became best friends.

Bunny and Mouse were friends who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big file on a bench. It had many papers with words and pictures inside. "Look, Bunny, what is that?" Mouse asked. "I don't know, Mouse. Maybe it is a book. Can we look at it?" Bunny said. They hopped on the bench and opened the file. They saw many papers with words and pictures. Some were about animals, some were about plants, some were about numbers. "Wow, this is so interesting, Bunny. Look, this is a picture of a lion. It is big and yellow and has a mane. And this is a picture of a flower. It is small and red and has petals. And this is a picture of a four. It is a number that means two and two," Mouse said. Bunny nodded and looked at the papers. He saw a picture of a kangaroo. It was big and brown and had a pouch. And it could hop very high and far. "Bunny, look, this is a picture of a kangaroo. It is like you, but bigger and better. It can hop higher and farther than you. Aren't you envious?" Mouse said. Bunny felt a sting in his heart. He liked to hop, but he could not hop as high and far as a kangaroo. He felt envious of the kangaroo. He wished he could be like the kangaroo. "No, Mouse, I am not envious. I am happy to be a bunny. I can hop fast and find carrots. And I have you as a friend. That is enough for me," Bunny said. Mouse smiled and hugged Bunny. He was sorry for making Bunny feel bad. He liked Bunny as he was. He did not want him to be envious of anyone. "You are right, Bunny. You are a good bunny and a good friend. I am happy to be a mouse. I can squeak and find cheese. And I have you as a friend. That is enough for me, too," Mouse said. They closed the file and hopped off the bench. They decided to play in the grass and look for carrots and cheese. They were happy and not envious of anyone. They were friends who liked to play in the park.

One day, a boy named Tom was very hungry. He wanted to eat pizza. So, his mom said, "Let's make pizza!" Tom and his mom started to make the pizza. They put cheese, tomato, and other yummy things on it. When it was ready, they put the pizza in the oven. Tom was so excited! Suddenly, a big dog came into the house. Tom was scared. The dog was rushing around and making a big mess. Tom said, "Go away, dog!" The dog stopped and looked at Tom. The dog was scared too. Tom's mom came in and saw the dog. She said, "Don't be scared, Tom. The dog just wants to play." Tom started to play with the dog, and they had a lot of fun. When the pizza was ready, Tom, his mom, and the dog ate the pizza together. They were all happy and full. Tom was not scared of the dog anymore. They became good friends.
