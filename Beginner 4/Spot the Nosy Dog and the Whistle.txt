Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Nosy Dog and the Whistle

Once upon a time, there was a nosy dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his red ball and his blue whistle. Every day, Spot would run and jump in the big green park. One sunny day, Spot met a new friend named Sam. Sam was a big, fluffy cat with a shiny bell on his collar. Spot wanted to play with Sam, so he took his whistle and blew it loud. Sam heard the whistle and came running to play with Spot. Spot and Sam played together all day long. They would run, jump, and even lead each other around the park. They had so much fun that they became best friends. From that day on, Spot and Sam played together every day, and they always used the whistle to call each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her ball. One day, Sue saw a big hill. She wanted to roll her ball down the hill. But she was scared. She had never gone up a big hill like that yet. Sue started to climb the hill. She began to struggle. The hill was very steep, and Sue felt dizzy. She stopped for a moment and took a deep breath. Sue knew she had to be brave. She thought about how much fun it would be to roll her ball down the hill. Finally, Sue made it to the top of the hill. She was so happy! She took her ball and rolled it down the hill. The ball went so fast! Sue laughed and clapped her hands. She had done it! Sue felt brave and proud. She knew that even though the hill was big and scary, she could do it. And she did!

One day, a boy named Tim saw rain outside. He was sad because he could not play. He looked at the rain and said, "Rain, rain, go away! I want to play outside today." In the kitchen, the fridge heard Tim. The fridge was persistent and wanted to help Tim. The fridge opened its door and took out a big umbrella. The fridge said, "Here, Tim! Take this umbrella and you can play outside." Tim was surprised! He did not know the fridge could talk. He took the umbrella and went outside. The rain stopped and the sun came out. Tim was so happy! He played outside and said, "Thank you, fridge! You are my friend." From that day on, Tim and the fridge were best friends. They played together and had lots of fun. The fridge was always there to help Tim, even when it rained.

Once upon a time, there was a weak shrimp who lived in the sea. The weak shrimp had a big wish. He wanted to be strong and fast like the other sea friends. One day, he met a wise old fish who told him about a magic thing he could use to be strong. The weak shrimp was so happy and went to find the magic thing. He looked and looked, and finally found it. It was a big, green leaf. The wise old fish said, "Use this leaf, and you will be strong and fast." The weak shrimp was so excited and used the leaf as a cape. With the cape on, the weak shrimp swam fast and felt strong. But then, something unexpected happened. The other sea friends saw the shrimp with the cape and they all wanted one too. They all used leaves as capes and swam even faster than the shrimp. The weak shrimp was sad again, but then he learned that it was not the cape that made him strong, but his heart and his friends.

One day, a little girl named Lily was troubled. She lost her favorite toy, a small strip of cloth. She called it her "blanky". It was very special to her. She could not sleep without it. So, she decided to search for it. Lily looked in her room. She asked her teddy bear, "Teddy, have you seen my blanky?" Teddy did not answer, but Lily knew he would help if he could. She looked under her bed and in her toy box, but she could not find it. Next, Lily went to the living room. She asked her dog, Spot, "Spot, have you seen my blanky?" Spot wagged his tail and barked. He knew how much Lily loved her blanky. They searched together, looking behind the sofa and under the table. But, they still could not find it. Finally, Lily went to the kitchen. She asked her mom, "Mom, have you seen my blanky?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, Lily. I found it on the floor and washed it for you. It's clean and dry now." Lily's face lit up, and she hugged her mom. Now that she had her blanky back, she was not troubled anymore.
