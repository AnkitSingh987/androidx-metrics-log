Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Bald Woman and the Hat

Once upon a time, there was a bald woman named Sue. Sue was sad because she had no hair. One day, Sue saw a big hat store. She thought, "Maybe I can find a hat to wear on my head." Sue went into the hat store and tried on many hats. She found a big, red hat that she liked. Sue put the hat on her head and was very happy. But when she went to leave the store, she saw a little girl crying. The girl said, "I lost my red hat. It was my favorite." Sue looked at the red hat on her head and knew what she had to do. She took off the hat and gave it to the little girl. The girl stopped crying and smiled. Sue felt happy, even without the hat. In the end, Sue and the little girl became friends. They played together every day. And soon, Sue's hair started to grow back. The little girl's mom, who was a hairdresser, helped Sue take care of her new hair. Sue was no longer sad, and she never forgot the day she met her best friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to draw and color. One day, she drew a symbol on a paper. The symbol was a big, red heart. She wanted to give it to her best friend, Tom. Sue went to Tom's house and gave him the heart. Tom was happy to receive it. He said, "Thank you, Sue!" Sue felt good because Tom liked her heart. But then, a big wind came and blew the heart away. Sue felt ashamed. She said, "I'm sorry, Tom." Tom smiled and said, "It's okay, Sue. We can make a new one together!" So they did, and they were both happy.

One day, a little boy named Tom found a bike with only one pedal. He thought it was a fun toy, so he took it home. Tom's friend, Sam, saw the bike and wanted to play with it too. But Tom was selfish and did not want to share the bike with Sam. Sam said, "Please, let me try the bike too." Tom said, "No, this is my bike. I found it and I will play with it." Sam was sad and walked away. Tom tried to ride the bike, but it was very hard with only one pedal. Then, something unexpected happened. A girl named Lily came and said, "That is my bike! I lost it when the pedal broke." Tom felt bad and gave the bike back to Lily. He said, "I am sorry, I should not have been selfish." Tom and Sam helped Lily fix the bike, and they all played together. They learned that sharing and helping friends is more fun than being selfish.

Once upon a time, there was a funny train. This train was not like the other trains. It was very colorful and always happy. The funny train loved to make the kids laugh. One day, the funny train saw a big scale. The scale was used to weigh things. The train thought it would be fun to see how much it weighed. So, it went on the scale and weighed itself. The funny train was very heavy! After weighing itself, the funny train felt sad. It thought that being heavy was not good. But then, the kids came and played with the funny train. They all laughed and had a great time. The funny train realized that it was not bad to be heavy. The kids still loved it, and it could still make them laugh. The moral of the story is that it is okay to be different. We should not feel bad about ourselves, but instead, be happy and have fun.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They saw a big tree with a print on it. The print looked like a big hand. Tim felt dizzy looking at the print. He said, "Mom, I don't feel good." His mom looked at him and said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will comfort you." She hugged him tight and soon, Tim felt better. They kept walking and saw a big rock. They sat on the rock and rested. As they sat, a funny thing happened. The rock started to move! It was not a rock, it was a big turtle! The turtle said, "Hello, I am Tina the turtle." Tim and his mom were surprised. They laughed and talked to Tina. They had a fun day with their new friend.
