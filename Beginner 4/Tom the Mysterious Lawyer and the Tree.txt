Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Mysterious Lawyer and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a mysterious lawyer named Tom. He liked to plan fun things for his friends. One day, he planned a big party. He invited all his friends to come and play. At the party, Tom's friend, Sue, lost her toy. Sue was very sad. She said, "Tom, help me find my toy." Tom and Sue looked everywhere for the toy, but they could not find it. They were worried and didn't know what to do. Then, Tom had an idea. He said, "Let's ask everyone at the party if they saw your toy." They asked all their friends, and finally, their friend Bob said, "I saw the toy by the tree." Tom and Sue went to the tree and found the toy. Sue was happy again, and they all continued to enjoy the party.

Lily liked to observe things. She liked to look at the sky, the trees, the birds, and the bugs. She liked to ask questions and learn new things. But sometimes, she was stubborn. She did not like to do what her mom and dad told her. One day, Lily and her family went to the park. There was a big lake with ducks and geese. Lily wanted to feed them bread. Her mom said, "No, Lily. Bread is not good for them. It can make them sick. We have seeds for them. You can give them seeds." But Lily did not listen. She was stubborn. She took a piece of bread from her sandwich and threw it to the ducks. The ducks came closer and closer. They wanted more bread. Lily threw another piece. Then, she saw something big and white. It was a goose. It had a long neck and a big beak. It looked angry. The goose saw the bread. It wanted the bread. It ran to Lily and tried to bite her. Lily was scared. She screamed and ran away. Her mom and dad came to help her. They chased the goose away. They hugged Lily and said, "Are you okay, Lily? That was a bad goose. You should not feed them bread. You should listen to us. We know what is best for you and the animals." Lily was sorry. She said, "I'm sorry, mom and dad. I was stubborn. I wanted to feed the ducks. I did not know the goose was mean. I will not feed them bread again. I will give them seeds. I will observe them from far away. I love you, mom and dad." Her mom and dad said, "We love you too, Lily. We are glad you are okay. You are a smart and curious girl. But sometimes, you need to follow the rules. They are there to keep you and the animals safe and happy. Come on, let's go and play on the land. There is a slide and a swing and a sandbox. You can observe more things there." Lily smiled and nodded. She held her mom and dad's hands and walked to the land. She saw more birds, bugs, flowers, and rocks. She asked more questions and learned more things. She was not stubborn anymore. She was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was troubled because she lost her favorite toy, a small teddy bear. She looked everywhere for it, but she could not find it. One day, Mia's mom mentioned that she had seen the teddy bear. Mia was so happy to hear this. Her mom said the teddy bear was in a wrap, like a blanket, and was in the living room. Mia ran to the living room and found her teddy bear wrapped in a soft, warm blanket. She was so happy to have her toy back. She hugged her teddy bear and played with it all day. From that day on, Mia and her teddy bear were never apart, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to eat muffins. One day, she found a very big muffin. She said, "Wow! This is a mighty muffin!" Amy wanted to share the mighty muffin with her friend, Tom. Tom came over and saw the muffin. He said, "I can't believe how big it is!" Amy and Tom ate the mighty muffin together. They were very happy. The end.
