Category: Beginner
Title: The Worm and the Apple

Once upon a time, there was a little worm. The worm lived in a big apple. The apple was on a tree. The worm was very happy in the apple. One day, the apple fell off the tree. The worm was scared. The apple started to rot. The worm did not like the rot. The worm thought, "I need a new home." The worm looked around. He saw a nice pear. The worm went to the pear. The pear was not spoiled. The worm was happy in the new home. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and her dog, Max. Every day, Lily and Max played in the yard. They liked to run, jump, and have fun. One day, Lily's mom said, "Lily, we are going to make jelly today!" Lily was very happy. She liked to help her mom. They took some regular fruit, like apples and grapes. They put the fruit in a big bowl. Then, Lily and her mom began to mix the fruit. They mixed and mixed until it was all squishy. Lily and her mom put the squishy fruit in a pot to cook. They waited and waited. Soon, the fruit turned into yummy jelly. Lily, her mom, and Max all tried the jelly on their bread. It was so good! They were happy and full. From that day on, Lily loved to help her mom make jelly.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Mia went to the park. She saw a weird bug. It had wings and was very pretty. Mia thought it was a fairy. Mia said "hello" to the fairy. The fairy did not talk back. Mia was sad. She requested, "Please talk to me, fairy." Then, something unexpected happened. The bug changed into a cat! The cat said, "I am not a fairy. I am a magic cat." Mia was very surprised. She played with the cat all day. They had lots of fun. And from that day, Mia and the magic cat were best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a brave girl named Sarah. She was three years old and loved to run. One day, Sarah wanted to go to the grocery store by herself. Her mom agreed and off she went! Sarah was very brave as she ran to the store. She opened the door and saw all the delicious food inside. She was so excited that she started to run around looking at everything! The store was very big and it took Sarah a long time to find everything she wanted. Finally, she held her bags full of groceries in her arms and ran back home. When she arrived, her mom was so happy and proud of her. She told Sarah she was the bravest three year old she'd ever seen! Sarah smiled and ran to show her mom all the yummy things she'd bought.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play by the river with his toy raft. One sunny day, he took his raft and went to the river to have fun. As Tim played, a naughty duck came by and took his raft. Tim was very sad and asked the duck to give it back. The duck just laughed and swam away with the raft. Tim waved to his mom for help. His mom came and saw the naughty duck with the raft. She waved her hand at the duck and said, "Give Tim's raft back!" The duck listened and gave the raft back to Tim. Tim was happy and thanked his mom. They both went home, and Tim played with his raft all day long.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the beach with her mom. Lucy loved the beach because she could play in the sand, swim in the water, and watch the birds fly. She saw a bird with a crazy dance and it made her laugh. "Look, mom! That bird is so funny!" she said. Lucy's mom looked at the bird and smiled. "Yes, it is!" she agreed. They watched the bird dance and wave its wings. Lucy thought it was the best thing she had ever seen. After playing in the sand, Lucy and her mom got hungry. They went to a small food stand near the beach. "Can we have some sauce with our food?" Lucy asked. Her mom said, "Sure, why not?" They put sauce on their food and enjoyed their meal while they watched the funny bird dance and wave its wings.
