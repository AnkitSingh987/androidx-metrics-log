Category: Beginner
Title: Luna the Black Cat and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a little black cat named Luna. Luna liked to play with her toy gear. One day, Luna went outside to play with her gear in the sun. While playing, Luna saw a big dog named Max. Max wanted to play with Luna's gear too. Luna was scared, so she yelled, "No, this is my gear!" But Max did not listen and took the gear. Luna was sad and yelled, "Give it back, please!" Max heard Luna and felt bad. He gave the gear back to Luna and said, "I'm sorry, let's play together." Luna and Max played with the gear, and they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to play near the train station. One day, Tim saw a clear, shiny rock on the ground. He picked it up and showed it to his friend, Sam. "Look, Sam! I found a pretty, clear rock!" Tim said. "Do you think it has any value?" Sam looked at the rock and said, "I don't know, Tim. But it is very nice." Tim and Sam wanted to find out if the rock had value. They went to a man who knew about rocks. The man said, "This is a very valuable rock. Be careful with it." Tim and Sam were very happy. But on their way back to the station, they saw a big dog. The dog wanted the rock too. The dog chased Tim and Sam, but they did not want to give the rock away. The dog was too fast for them, and it took the rock away. Tim and Sam were very sad. They had lost the valuable rock, and they never saw it again.

Once upon a time, there was a pink pig named Poppy. Poppy lived on a farm with lots of hay. She liked to play in the hay with her friends. One day, Poppy saw a hole in the fence. She thought it would be fun to escape and see what was outside the farm. So, she squeezed through the hole and went on an adventure. Poppy saw many new things outside the farm. She saw a big tree, a pretty flower, and a little bug. But soon, Poppy missed her friends and the hay. She went back to the hole in the fence and squeezed through to go home. Poppy was happy to be back with her friends, playing in the hay.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a man with a wild hat. Tim thought the hat was funny. "Mom, look at that hat!" said Tim. His mom laughed too. They walked and played in the park. Tim and his mom saw a big tree. Under the tree, there was a big bucket of water. Tim wanted to play with the water. His mom said, "Okay, but don't pour too much water." Tim was happy and started to pour little water on the ground. Suddenly, a big wind came and blew the wild hat off the man's head. The hat flew through the air and landed on Tim's shirt. Tim was surprised! "Oh no, my shirt is wet from the hat!" he said. His mom laughed and said, "That was unexpected, but it's okay. We can dry your shirt at home." They all laughed and went home with a funny story to tell.

Once upon a time, there was a pale blue car named Billy. Billy loved to drive fast and go on adventures. One day, Billy decided to go west, where the sun sets. He wanted to see what was there. As Billy drove west, he met a big red truck named Tommy. Tommy was going the same way. They decided to go together. Billy said, "Let's go west and see what we can find!" Tommy agreed and they drove along, happy and excited. After a while, Billy and Tommy came to a big hill. They had to go down the hill, but it was very steep. Billy was scared. Tommy told him, "Don't worry, just reverse down the hill slowly." So, Billy reversed down the hill, with Tommy right behind him. They made it down safely and continued their adventure to the west.
