Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Dog and the Box

One day, a dog named Tim went to a big hotel with his family. They were all very happy. Tim was a little worried because he had never been to a hotel before. He did not know what to expect. In the hotel, Tim met a cat named Sally. Sally said, "Hi, I live here. Are you worried?" Tim said, "Yes, I am worried. This is my first time in a hotel." Sally said, "Don't worry, it is fun here. Let's play together!" As they played, Tim and Sally went into a room they had never seen before. Inside, they found a big box. They opened the box and realized it was full of toys! They were so happy and played with the toys all day. Tim was not worried anymore. He loved the hotel and his new friend Sally.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play by the river. He loved to watch the fish swim. One day, Tim saw a crazy fish. This fish was not like the other fish. It was big and had many colors. Tim said, "Hello, crazy fish!" The fish said, "Hello, Tim." Tim was so surprised. He did not know fish could talk. The fish asked Tim, "Can you help me? I am lost. I need to find my friends." Tim said, "I can help you." Tim and the fish looked for the fish's friends. They looked and looked. They could not find them. Then, the fish said, "I have an idea. Let's repeat my fish song. My friends will hear it and come to us." Tim said, "Okay, let's do it." They sang the fish song together. "Fish, fish, fish, come to me. Fish, fish, fish, swim with me." They repeated the song many times. Suddenly, all the fish's friends came swimming to them. The fish was so happy. Tim and the fish became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a thin pigeon. It had soft grey feathers and was so small that it could fit in the palm of your hand. It loved perching on the windowsill and singing sweet songs all day long. One morning, it flew out the window and decided to explore the world. It flew high in the sky, admiring the clouds and the sun. Suddenly, it came face to face with a hungry cat. Its sharp claws and fierce eyes scared the pigeon away. The poor pigeon flew as fast as it could. But the cat was too fast. Before long, it had the pigeon in its clutches. The cat loved the pigeon and gobbled it up in an instant. The thin pigeon was no more.

Lily wanted to play with her new doll, but Mom said no. Mom said the doll was a gift for her cousin Mia, who had a birthday soon. Mom said she had to wrap the doll and put it in a box. Lily was sad and angry. She liked the doll very much. She wanted to keep it for herself. Lily was stubborn. She waited until Mom was busy in the kitchen. Then she sneaked into the living room and took the doll from the table. She ran to her room and shut the door. She hugged the doll and said, "You are mine. I will not give you away." Mom heard the door slam and went to check on Lily. She knocked on the door and said, "Lily, what are you doing? Open the door, please." Lily did not answer. She hid the doll under her pillow and pretended to sleep. Mom tried to open the door, but it was locked. She said, "Lily, this is not nice. You have to give the doll back. It is not yours. It is Mia's. She will be very happy to get it. Don't you want to make Mia happy?" Lily felt a little sorry for Mia, but she still wanted the doll. She said, "No, Mom. I want the doll. It is pretty and soft and smells good. I will not give it back." Mom said, "Lily, that is selfish and rude. You have to share and be kind. You have many other toys to play with. Mia does not have many toys. She lives far away and we don't see her often. She will be very sad if she does not get the doll. Please, Lily, open the door and give the doll back. Promise me you will do that." Lily thought about what Mom said. She thought about Mia, who was her cousin and her friend. She thought about how she would feel if someone took her favorite toy. She felt bad for being stubborn and mean. She said, "Okay, Mom. I promise. I will give the doll back. I am sorry." She unlocked the door and opened it. She gave the doll to Mom and said, "Here, Mom. This is for Mia. I hope she likes it." Mom smiled and hugged Lily. She said, "Thank you, Lily. That is very sweet and generous of you. I am proud of you. Mia will love the doll and she will love you too. Come on, let's go wrap the doll together and write a card for Mia." Lily nodded and followed Mom. She felt happy and good. She learned to keep her promise and to share.
