Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Fish and the Sam

One day, a little fish named Tim was swimming in the big blue sea. Tim was a very happy fish. He liked to play with his friends and look for pretty shells. One day, Tim received a big surprise. He saw a terrible shark named Sam. Sam was big and had sharp teeth. Tim was scared, but Sam said, "Don't be scared, I just want to be friends." So, Tim and the terrible shark Sam became friends. They played together and had lots of fun. All the other fish saw that Sam was not so terrible after all. They all became friends and played in the big blue sea.

Once there was an ant and a grasshopper. The ant worked hard all summer long gathering food while the grasshopper played. When the weather got cold, the ant stored all its food on a big white sheet by its doorway. The grasshopper had no food, so it came to the ant's door and asked for some. The ant looked at the grasshopper and said it was too late for it to work now and it should have collected food when it could. The grasshopper said it was sorry for not working harder, so the ant shared some of its great food from the sheet. The grasshopper was grateful and thankful for the ant's kindness. The story of the ant and the grasshopper teaches us that it's important to value hard work all the time, not just when we feel like it, so that we are prepared for whatever comes our way.

One day, Tim and his mom went to the park. They sat on a dry bench under a big tree. Tim asked his mom, "Can you read me a story?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim! Let's read a story about a laser." As they read the story, a funny thing happened. The laser in the book started to shine very bright. Tim and his mom were very surprised. They looked at each other and said, "Wow! How did that happen?" Then, a nice man came up to them. He said, "I can tell you why the laser is shining. I am a magic man, and I made the book special for you, Tim." Tim and his mom smiled and thanked the magic man. They had a fun day at the park reading the special laser book.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to eat toast every day. One day, she found a rare, shiny rock in her backyard. She was very happy and showed it to her mom. Her mom said, "Wow, that's a rare rock, Lily! Let's put it on the table next to your toast." Lily smiled and did what her mom said. She put the rare rock next to her toast. The next day, Lily's friend, Tom, came to play. Lily said, "Look, Tom! I found a rare rock!" Tom said, "That's cool! Can I see it?" Lily showed him the rock and they both smiled. Then, they ate toast together and played all day.

One day, a happy spider named Sam lived in a big tree. Sam liked to play with his friends, the bugs. They played tag and hide-and-seek all day long. Sam was very good at hiding because he was small. One day, Sam's friend, a bug named Ben, said, "I can't find a good hiding spot. Sam, can you suggest a place for me to hide?" Sam thought for a moment and said, "You can hide in the tall grass. No one will find you there!" Ben went to hide in the tall grass, but something unexpected happened. A big bird saw Ben and tried to catch him! Sam quickly spun a strong web and caught Ben, pulling him to safety. The bird flew away, and Ben was very thankful to Sam for saving him. From that day on, Sam and Ben were the best of friends. They played together every day and always looked out for each other. And Sam always had a new hiding spot to suggest to Ben, so they could keep having fun and stay safe.

One day, a boy and a girl went to the beach. They saw the sand and the water. The boy said, "Let's lay on the sand and play!" The girl said, "Yes, I like the sand. It's fun!" They found a cheap toy in the sand. It was a small boat. The boy said, "Let's put the boat in the water!" The girl said, "Okay, but be careful. Don't go too far." The boy put the boat in the water. It floated away. He went after it. The girl was scared. She said, "Come back! The water is deep!" But the boy didn't listen. The boy went too far. He could not swim. The girl cried. The boy was gone. The cheap boat floated away. The girl was sad and went home.
