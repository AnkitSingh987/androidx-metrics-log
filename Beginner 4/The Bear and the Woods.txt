Category: Beginner
Title: The Bear and the Woods

One day, a little bear went for a walk in the woods. He liked to roll on the soft grass and play with his friends. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. It was a nice day for fun. While the bear was rolling, he met a big, rude bear. The rude bear did not want to play with the little bear. He said, "Go away! I don't want to play with you." The little bear felt sad and walked away. The little bear kept rolling and playing in the woods. Soon, he found a kind, friendly bear who wanted to play with him. They rolled and played together all day long. The little bear was happy to have a new friend who was not rude.

Once upon a time, there was a hairy dog named Max. Max liked to play and run all day. One day, Max saw a faucet in the park. Max was very thirsty, so he dared to go near the faucet to get some water. But, there was a big cat named Tom who was guarding the faucet. Tom was not nice and did not want Max to drink water from the faucet. Max and Tom started to fight, because Max was very thirsty and Tom did not want to share the water. In the end, Max was too tired to fight and could not drink the water. Max went home very sad and thirsty. Tom was still guarding the faucet, not letting anyone drink the water.

Sam was hungry. He wanted a snack. He went to the kitchen and opened the refrigerator. He saw a lot of food inside. He saw cheese, eggs, milk, bread, and fruit. He also saw a brown bag. He wondered what was in the bag. He took out the bag and opened it. He smelled something bad. He looked inside the bag. He saw a green and fuzzy sandwich. It was disgusting. He felt sick. He wanted to throw the bag away. But he heard a snap. He looked at the bag again. He saw a hole in the bag. He saw some green and fuzzy stuff coming out of the hole. He saw some green and fuzzy stuff on his hand. He screamed. Mom heard Sam scream. She ran to the kitchen. She saw Sam holding the bag. She saw the green and fuzzy stuff. She knew what it was. It was Dad's sandwich. Dad forgot to eat his sandwich last week. It was very old and rotten. Mom took the bag from Sam. She threw it in the trash. She washed Sam's hand. She gave him a hug. She said, "I'm sorry, Sam. That was a disgusting sandwich. You can have some cheese or fruit instead. Do you want some cheese or fruit?" Sam nodded. He wanted some cheese and fruit. He was still hungry. He forgot about the disgusting sandwich. He smiled at Mom. He said, "Thank you, Mom. I love cheese and fruit. And I love you." Mom smiled back. She said, "I love you too, Sam. You are a brave boy. Next time, don't open any brown bags in the refrigerator. They might be disgusting." Sam agreed. He learned his lesson. He never opened any brown bags in the refrigerator again.

Once upon a time, there was a farm. On this farm, there were many animals. They were normal animals, like cows, pigs, and chickens. One day, a man came to the farm. He wanted to design a new house for the animals. The man talked to the cow. "Cow, I want to design a new house for you. What do you want it to look like?" The cow said, "I want a big house with lots of grass to eat." The man listened and started to design the new house. But the man did not know how to design a house very well. The house he made was not safe. One day, the house fell down. The cow was very sad. The other animals were sad too. The farm was not a happy place anymore. The moral of the story is: always ask for help if you do not know how to do something.
