Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Car

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing in her room. She had a toy car that could zoom very fast. Lily loved to make the car zoom all around her room. She would laugh and clap her hands as she watched it go. Lily's mom called from the living room, "Lily, come here!" Lily picked up her toy car and ran to her mom. Her mom was sitting on the big, soft couch. "I want to show you something," her mom said with a smile. Lily climbed up on the couch and her mom started to spin her around. "Whee!" Lily shouted as she went round and round. When her mom stopped spinning her, Lily felt dizzy. She giggled and said, "Mommy, that was fun! Let's do it again!"

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to cook. One day, it snowed a lot. Sue had an idea. She wanted to cook something with snow. Sue went outside and saw her friend Tom. "Let's cook snow!" Sue said. Tom was selfish and didn't want to share. "No, I want to play alone," Tom said. Sue was sad, but she still wanted to cook with snow. Sue went home and made snow soup. It was cold and yummy. Then, something unexpected happened. The snow soup turned into ice cream! Sue was so happy. She went to Tom and shared her ice cream. Tom was sorry for being selfish and they became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a blue bird named Bobby. Bobby loved to fly around and play with his friends. One day, Bobby's friend, Sarah the squirrel, told him about a place called the library. The library had many books with lots of fun pictures and stories. Bobby wanted to go to the library, but he was scared. He had never been inside before. Sarah saw that Bobby was scared, so she decided to encourage him. She said, "Don't worry, Bobby! I will go with you. It will be fun, and we can look at books together." Bobby felt better with Sarah's help. They went to the library and had a great time looking at books and learning new things. Bobby was so happy that he had a friend like Sarah to encourage him to try new things. From that day on, Bobby loved going to the library and wasn't scared anymore.

One day, a little girl named Sally went for a walk. She saw a big dog. The dog looked fearful. Sally felt scared but she wanted to help the dog. Sally said, "Hi dog, why are you scared?" The dog said, "I am lost. I need to go east, but I don't know where east is." Sally thought for a moment. She knew where east was. Sally said, "I can help you find east." She pointed to the right. "East is that way!" The dog was happy. They walked together to the east. Soon, the dog found his home. Sally and the dog were not scared anymore.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim had a dull toy car. He wanted to make it shiny and nice. Tim asked his mom for help. His mom said, "We can polish it to make it shiny!" Tim and his mom got a soft cloth and began to polish the toy car. They rubbed and rubbed, but the car still looked dull. Tim was sad. He wanted his car to be the best. One day, Tim found a card on the floor. It was a magic card! The card said, "Rub me on your toy car, and it will be shiny." Tim was very happy. He rubbed the card on his car, and it became shiny and new. Now, Tim's toy car was the best. He played with it all day and showed it to his friends. They all loved the shiny car. And Tim was happy because his car was not dull anymore.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a wander in the park. He saw a big, attractive tree with a switch on it. Tim was curious and wanted to know what the switch did. Tim walked up to the tree and said, "Hello, pretty tree! What does this switch do?" The tree didn't say anything, but Tim decided to try the switch anyway. He reached up high and flipped the switch. Suddenly, the tree started to talk! "Thank you, Tim!" said the tree. "I was stuck and couldn't talk. You helped me!" Tim was very surprised and happy to make a new friend. They played in the park all day and had lots of fun.
