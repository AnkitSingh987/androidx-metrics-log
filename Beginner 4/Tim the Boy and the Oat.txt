Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Oat

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat oats for breakfast. Every morning, his mom would make a big bowl of oat for him. It was his most favorite food in the whole wide world. One day, Tim saw an ignorant cat in his yard. The cat didn't know anything about oats. Tim wanted to help the cat learn about his favorite food. So, he decided to remove a bowl of oat from his kitchen and share it with the cat. Tim brought the bowl of oat outside and showed it to the ignorant cat. The cat sniffed the oat and tasted it. The cat loved the oat too! From that day on, Tim and the cat became best friends. They would eat oats together every morning and play all day long.

One day, a curious little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a big tree and wanted to find what was under it. He took his spade and started to dig. As he dug, he found a small box. Tim was very excited. He ran to his mom to tell her what he found. "Mom, I found a box under the tree!" he said. Mom came to see the box. She helped Tim open it. Inside, they found pretty rocks and a note. The note said, "You are a great finder!" Tim and his mom smiled and put the box back for someone else to find.

---

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to watch his favorite show on TV. He was very excited and ran to the living room. As he ran, he did not see the toy car on the floor. Tim stumbled on the toy and fell down. "Ouch!" he cried, feeling foolish for not looking where he was going. His mom heard him and came to help him. "What happened, Tim?" she asked. Tim said, "I stumbled on the toy car and fell down. I wanted to watch TV." His mom looked at him and said, "Next time, be careful and watch where you step." Tim's mom helped him up and turned on the TV for him. Tim watched his favorite show and felt happy. He learned to be more careful when he walked around the house. He did not want to stumble and feel foolish again.

One day, a friendly dog named Max was walking in the woods. He saw a big stick on the ground. Max picked up the stick with his mouth. He was happy to find a new toy. Max called the stick his "staff." As Max walked, he saw a big cat. The cat was scared and started to tremble. Max wanted to help the cat, so he said, "Don't be scared, I am a friendly dog. Let's play with my staff!" The cat stopped trembling and said, "Okay, let's play!" They played with the staff, throwing it and running after it. Then, something unexpected happened. The staff broke in half! Max and the cat were sad. But then, the cat had an idea. "Let's put the two pieces together and make a new toy!" They did that and made a new toy. Max and the cat were happy and played all day.

Once upon a time, there was a girl. She had a special dress. It was pink and sparkly. The girl went out on a walk with her dress. It was peaceful outside, just her and her dress. She passed through the trees and breathed in the warm air. As she walked, she twirled around with her dress. Then, the girl came to a waterfall. The waterfall was loud, but the girl felt peaceful. She kept walking and soon, the waterfall was far behind her. Then, she arrived at a big, green field. The grass was long and the sky was blue. The dress twirled around the girl in the gentle breeze. The girl stayed in the field until the sun began to set. Then, she passed back through the trees and went home. That night, she put away her special dress and fell asleep. The next day, the girl went out again with her dress. It was peaceful and beautiful, just like the day before.
