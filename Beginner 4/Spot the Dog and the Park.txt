Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved his owner, a kind girl named Lily. They played together every day. Spot was a fragile dog, so Lily was always gentle with him. One sunny day, Spot and Lily went to the park. Spot saw a big ball and wanted to play with it. He barked, "Woof! Woof!" Lily said, "Okay, Spot! Let's play!" They played with the ball and had lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The ball popped! Spot and Lily were very surprised. They looked around and saw a little girl with a pin. She said, "I'm sorry. I didn't mean to pop your ball." Lily said, "It's okay. We can get a new one." Spot, Lily, and the little girl became friends and played together every day.

Tim was a little boy who liked to play in the garden. He had a toy car that he drove on the grass and the dirt. He liked to make roads and bridges with sticks and stones. One day, he decided to bury his car under a big pile of leaves. He thought it would be fun to dig it out later. But when he came back to the garden, he could not find his car. He looked under the leaves, but it was not there. He looked under the flowers, but it was not there. He looked under the bushes, but it was not there. He felt sad and lonely. He wanted his car back. He ran to his mom, who was a nurse. She was in the kitchen, making soup. She saw Tim crying and asked him what was wrong. "Mom, I can't find my car. I buried it in the garden, but now it's gone. Someone took it. I want my car back." Mom hugged Tim and said, "Don't worry, sweetie. Maybe the wind blew the leaves away and your car is still there. Let's go and look for it together." Mom and Tim went back to the garden. They searched for the car, but they could not find it. Then they heard a bark. It was Tim's dog, Spot. Spot was wagging his tail and digging in the dirt. He had something in his mouth. It was Tim's car. "Spot, you found my car. You're a good dog. Thank you, Spot." Tim said. He took his car from Spot and hugged him. He was happy again. Mom smiled and said, "See, you're not lonely. You have me and Spot. We love you. Now let's go and have some soup. It's getting cold." Tim nodded and said, "Okay, Mom. I love you and Spot too. Can I play with my car after soup?" Mom said, "Of course you can. But don't bury it again. Or Spot might find it again." Tim laughed and said, "Okay, Mom. I won't bury it again. I'll keep it safe."

One day, a big cat named Max went to visit his friend, a little dog named Sam. They liked to play and run in the park. Max was very happy to see Sam, but Sam looked serious. He said, "I have a big sneeze, Max. I don't want you to get sick too." Max and Sam played far apart so they would not sneeze on each other. They had fun, but Max missed playing close to Sam. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew Sam's sneeze away. Sam felt better and stopped sneezing. Max and Sam were happy that the sneeze was gone. They played close together again and had more fun than ever. They learned that sometimes, unexpected things can make everything better.

Lily and Max were twins who liked to play together. They had many toys, but they also liked to see what their mom and dad had in their rooms. Sometimes, they were very nosy and opened drawers and closets that they should not. One day, they saw a big box in their mom's room. It had a sticker that said "order". They wondered what was inside. They tried to lift the box, but it was too heavy. They decided to open it and see. They pulled and ripped the tape and cardboard. They found many things inside: books, clothes, shoes, and a shiny necklace. They liked the necklace the most. They put it on and pretended to be kings and queens. But they did not hear their mom coming up the stairs. She had just come back from the store and wanted to settle her order in her room. She was very surprised and angry when she saw the mess and the twins with her necklace. She scolded them and told them to put everything back in the box. She said they should not be nosy and touch things that were not theirs. She said they had to go to their room and think about what they did. Lily and Max felt sorry and sad. They said they were sorry and helped their mom clean up. They went to their room and hugged each other. They decided to play with their own toys and not be nosy again.
