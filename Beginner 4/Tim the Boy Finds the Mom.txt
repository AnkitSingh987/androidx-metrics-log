Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Mom

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with his mom and dad. One day, Tim's mom asked him to help her spread jam on bread for lunch. Tim was very happy to help his mom. After they made the sandwiches, Tim went to wash his hands. He turned the faucet, but no water came out. Tim was confused. He tried again, but still no water. He called his mom and she tried too, but the faucet was not working. They looked outside and saw that the water pipe was broken. Tim's dad came home and saw the broken pipe. He told Tim and his mom that they needed to be more careful with the rough things around the house. Tim and his mom promised to be more careful. They all worked together to fix the pipe and soon the water was back. They were happy and learned that being careful with things can keep them from breaking.

Ben and Mia like to play at the park. They run and slide and swing and laugh. Sometimes they pretend to be spies or superheroes or pirates. They use sticks and stones and leaves as weapons. One day, they see a new boy at the park. He has a big red bike and a shiny helmet. He looks different from them. He does not say hello or smile. He rides his bike very fast and makes loud noises. Ben and Mia are curious. They want to play with him. They go to him and ask him his name. He does not answer. He pushes them away and says, "Go away! This is my park! You can't play here!" Ben and Mia are sad and angry. They do not like the new boy. He is mean and rude. They want to make him go away. They pick up their weapons and get ready to fight. But then they hear a voice. It is the new boy's mom. She comes to the park and sees them. She says, "Stop! What are you doing? Why are you fighting?" She goes to the new boy and hugs him. She says, "I'm sorry, honey. I know you are scared. This is a new place and you don't know anyone. But these are nice kids. They want to be your friends. You don't have to be alone." She goes to Ben and Mia and smiles. She says, "I'm sorry, too. This is my son, Sam. He is different from you. He has autism. He has trouble talking and understanding. He likes to ride his bike and make noises. It helps him feel calm. He doesn't mean to be mean or rude. He just needs some time and patience." Ben and Mia look at Sam. They feel sorry for him. They understand that he is different, but not bad. They put down their weapons and smile. They say, "It's okay, Sam. We are sorry, too. We want to be your friends. Can we play with you?" Sam looks at them. He feels happy. He nods and says, "Yes. You can play with me." They all play together at the park. They run and slide and swing and laugh. Sometimes they pretend to be spies or superheroes or pirates. They use sticks and stones and leaves as weapons. But only for fun.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a magic finger. The magic finger was very useful. It could do many things. Tim was so happy to have it. Tim went to play with his friend, Sam. "Look at my magic finger," Tim said to Sam. "It can shoot water!" Tim showed Sam how the magic finger could shoot water. They had a lot of fun playing with the water. Then, they saw a cat stuck in a tree. The cat was sad and scared. Tim said, "I will use my magic finger to help the cat." He pointed his magic finger at the tree and shot water to make the tree wet. The cat slid down the tree and was safe. Sam and the cat were very happy. They all played together and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a little horse named Happy. Happy lived in a stable with his friends. The stable was warm and cozy. Happy was always alert and loved to play with his friends. One day, Happy and his friends decided to make a new toy. They found some paper and a pair of scissors. Happy said, "Let's cut the paper and make a fun toy!" His friends nodded and they all started to cut the paper into different shapes. As they were cutting the paper, a little bird flew into the stable. The bird saw the horses cutting the paper and asked, "Can I play too?" Happy and his friends smiled and said, "Of course, you can!" So, the little bird joined them, and they all had a great time making toys together.
