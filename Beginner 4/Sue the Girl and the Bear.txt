Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Bear

One day, a little girl named Sue went to sleep. She had a dream about a big, rough bear. The bear was nice, but his fur was not soft. Sue and the bear played all day in her dream. In the morning, Sue did wake up. She saw her toy bear next to her. The toy bear gave her a little wink. Sue smiled and hugged her toy bear. She knew it was her friend from the dream. Sue and the toy bear played all day. They had fun and laughed. At night, Sue went to sleep again. She hoped to see her big, rough bear friend in her dreams.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to touch everything she saw. One day, Sue went to the market with her mom. At the market, Sue saw many things she wanted to touch. In the market, Sue saw a box of prunes. Sue did not know what prunes were, but she wanted to touch them. She reached out her hand and touched the prunes. They were soft and a little bit sticky. Sue's mom saw her touch the prunes and said, "Sue, please do not touch the prunes." Sue's mom then saw a cheap toy. It was a small ball that bounced very high. Sue's mom said, "Sue, you can touch this toy." Sue touched the cheap toy and it made her happy. She liked the way it felt in her hand. Sue's mom bought the toy for her and they went home. Sue played with the cheap toy all day and forgot about the prunes.

One day, a bird named Tim was sitting on a high wire. Tim was a very happy bird. He liked to talk to his friends. Tim saw his friend, Sam the squirrel, and said, "Hi Sam!" Sam looked up at Tim and said, "Hi Tim! Be careful on that wire. It is very high." Tim laughed and said, "I am not scared. I can fly!" Just then, a big wind came and blew Tim off the wire. Tim was embarrassed. He did not want his friend to see him fall. But Sam was a good friend. He came over to Tim and said, "It's okay, Tim. We all fall sometimes." Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Sam." And from that day on, Tim was more careful on the high wire. He knew that even the best birds can fall sometimes.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a little girl named Mia. Mia had a big brain. She was very smart. One day, she met a talking bird named Bobo. Bobo could not find his way home. He was sad. Mia wanted to help Bobo. She said, "Do not be sad, Bobo. I will take you home." Bobo was happy. They walked and walked. Mia used her big brain to find the way. On the way, they found a bitter fruit. Bobo said, "Mia, do not eat that fruit. It is bitter and not good." Mia listened to Bobo. They walked some more and found Bobo's home. Bobo was so happy. Mia and Bobo became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a poor cat named Kitty. Kitty lived in a small house with her friend, a little mouse named Mimi. They were very happy. They played games and shared food. Kitty and Mimi loved to learn about history. They liked to hear stories about long ago. One day, Kitty and Mimi found a big book. The book was about history. They wanted to look at the book. But, the book was on a high shelf. Kitty and Mimi were too small to reach it. They felt sad. Then, a kind dog named Buddy saw them. Buddy wanted to help. He was big and strong. Buddy allowed Kitty to climb on his back. Kitty reached the high shelf and got the book. Kitty, Mimi, and Buddy sat down. They read the book together and learned about history. They were very happy.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Amy. Amy had a special book called a passport. She was very glad when she looked at her passport because it had many pretty stamps from different places. One day, Amy saw a boy named Tim who was sad. He didn't have a passport and couldn't go to new places like Amy. So, Amy had an idea. She took Tim's hand and said, "Let's pull together and make you a passport!" They worked together, using paper and colors. After some time, Tim had his own passport. He was very glad, and so was Amy. They both learned that when you pull together and help others, you can make them happy too. And that made Amy and Tim the best of friends.
