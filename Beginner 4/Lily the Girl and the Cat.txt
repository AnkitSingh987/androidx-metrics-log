Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to skip and play outside. One day, she went on a walk with her mom and dad. On their walk, they saw a big tank. It was old and not scary. Lily's dad said it was from a long time ago. Lily thought it was fun to look at and touch. As they walked away from the tank, Lily saw a cat. The cat was not scary, but it was big and fluffy. Lily and the cat played and skipped together. Then they all went home, happy and tired.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Jack and Jill. They wanted to gather shells by the sea. But they could not agree on the way to get there. Jack said they should take a bus. But Jill said they should walk. They argued for a long time, but neither wanted to change their mind. They were both being very stubborn. At last, Jill said "Let's both be brave, and walk to the beach!" So, Jack and Jill gathered their things and started walking. They crossed a bridge and hopped over a river. They saw lots of trees and fields on the way. When they finally reached the sea, they were so happy. They played in the sand and gathered shells for hours. They even managed to find a very special shell to share. Jack and Jill had a wonderful day at the sea. And even though they had disagreed on the way, they were still the best of friends.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump and slide. They saw a big pole with a sign on it. The sign had a picture of a bird and some words. "What does it say?" Lily asked. Ben tried to read the words. He knew some letters, but not all. He sounded out the words slowly. "It says... whistle... here... to... see... a... parrot," he said. "A parrot?" Lily said. "What is a parrot?" Ben shrugged. He did not know what a parrot was. He thought it might be a kind of bird. "Let's whistle and see," he said. They both tried to whistle, but they could not. They only made funny noises with their mouths. They tried again and again, but nothing happened. They were about to give up, when they heard a loud squawk from behind the pole. They looked and saw a colorful bird with a big beak and a long tail. It was hidden in a cage behind the pole. "Hello, hello!" the bird said. Lily and Ben gasped. The bird could talk! "Hello, bird!" they said. The bird smiled and whistled back. "Are you a parrot?" Lily asked. "Yes, I am a parrot," the bird said. "My name is Polly. Do you want to be my friends?" Lily and Ben nodded. They liked Polly. They asked her many questions and she answered them. She told them about her feathers, her food, and her tricks. They had a lot of fun with Polly. They forgot about the sign and the pole. They only cared about their new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a magic toy. This toy could talk, but it was very rude. Tim did not like rude toys, so he wanted to teach the toy to be nice. One day, Tim told the toy, "You must be nice or I will not play with you." The toy did not want to be alone, so it tried to be nice. But it was hard for the toy to change. Tim did not give up. He wanted to help the toy succeed. After many days, the magic toy learned to be nice. It said "please" and "thank you" and played well with others. Tim was happy that he helped the toy succeed. They became the best of friends and had lots of fun together.

Tom and Sam are friends who like to play with cars. They have many cars of different colors and sizes. They like to make them go fast and make noises. One day, Tom brings a new car to Sam's house. It is a big red car with a shiny engine. Tom says, "Look at my new car! It is very fast and loud. Do you want to play with it?" Sam says, "Yes, I do! Your car is very nice. Can I see the engine?" Tom says, "Sure, you can. The engine is what makes the car go. It has many parts that work together. See, this is the battery, this is the fan, this is the belt, and this is the spark plug." Sam examines the engine carefully. He touches the parts and asks Tom questions. He says, "Wow, your engine is very cool. How do you know all these things?" Tom says, "My dad taught me. He is a mechanic. He fixes cars when they are broken. He says engines are like puzzles. You have to put the right pieces in the right places." Sam says, "That sounds fun. I want to learn more about engines. Maybe my dad can teach me too. He likes cars as well." Tom says, "That would be great. Then we can both be mechanics when we grow up. We can fix any car and make it go fast and loud." Sam says, "Yes, we can. We can have our own garage and help people with their cars. That would be awesome." Tom and Sam smile and hug. They are happy to have each other as friends. They play with the big red car and their other cars. They pretend they are mechanics and drivers. They have a lot of fun. They think engines are normal and amazing.
