Category: Beginner
Title: The Hippo and the Ball

Once upon a time, in a big, big river, there lived a big hippo. The hippo liked to play with his friends, the fish, the birds, and the frogs. They had a lot of fun together. One day, the hippo saw something interesting. It was a big, round ball. The hippo did not know what it was. He asked his friends, "What is this?" The fish said, "I don't know." The bird said, "I don't know." The frog said, "I don't know." Then, the hippo had an idea. He said, "I will ask my mom." His mom was very smart. She knew a lot of things. The hippo asked, "Mom, what is this?" His mom said, "This is a ball. It is fun to play with. I recommend you try it." The hippo was happy. He played with the ball and had a lot of fun. His friends liked the ball too. They all played together and had a great day.

Anna and Ben were twins. They liked to play with their toys and eat snacks. One day, their mom said they would have a visit from their aunt and uncle. Anna and Ben were happy. They liked their aunt and uncle. They were nice and funny. Mom told them to clean their room and put away their toys. Anna and Ben did not want to do that. They wanted to keep playing. They said no to mom. Mom was not happy. She said they had to do it or they would not get any snacks. Anna and Ben still said no. They ran away to the backyard. Mom was busy in the kitchen. She did not see them go. She thought they were in their room. She made a cake and some sandwiches for the visit. She put them on the table. She went to get some drinks from the fridge. Anna and Ben were hungry. They smelled the cake and the sandwiches. They sneaked into the kitchen. They saw the food on the table. They thought mom would not mind if they had a little bite. They climbed on the chairs and reached for the cake. They took a big piece and ate it. It was yummy. They wanted more. They took another piece and another. They did not see the crumbs and the frosting on the table and on their faces. Mom came back from the fridge. She saw Anna and Ben eating the cake. She saw the mess on the table. She was angry. She yelled at them. She said they were naughty and rude. She said they ruined the cake and the visit. She said they had to go to their room and stay there. Anna and Ben were scared. They heard mom yell. They saw her angry face. They felt bad. They did not mean to make mom angry. They did not mean to ruin the cake and the visit. They panicked. They dropped the cake and ran to their room. They cried and hugged each other. They said they were sorry. They wished mom would forgive them. They wished their aunt and uncle would still come. They wished they had listened to mom and cleaned their room and put away their toys.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to eat pizza. He told his mom, "I want pizza!" His mom said, "Okay, we will make pizza." Tim was very happy. While making the pizza, Tim was careless. He put too many things on the pizza. His mom did not mention it. She put the pizza in the oven. They waited for it to cook. When the pizza was done, they took it out of the oven. The pizza looked different. It was not flat anymore. It was big and round like a ball! Tim and his mom laughed. They ate the pizza ball and it was yummy.

Once upon a time, there was a colorful cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with her ink pen. One day, she was drawing on the wall with her ink pen. The wall was now very colorful too. Kitty's friend, Doggy, came to visit. He saw the colorful wall and said, "Oh no! We need to clean this!" Kitty did not want to clean the wall. She liked the colors. But Doggy said, "We must clean it or Mommy will be mad." Kitty and Doggy worked together to clean the colorful wall. They used soap and water. They scrubbed and scrubbed. The wall was clean again. They were happy they did not get in trouble. From then on, Kitty only drew on paper with her ink pen.
