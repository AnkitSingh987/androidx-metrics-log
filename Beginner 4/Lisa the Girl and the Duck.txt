Category: Beginner
Title: Lisa the Girl and the Duck

One day, a little girl named Lisa found a bright toy in her room. It was a small, yellow duck. She was so happy, she showed it to her mom. Her mom said, "Why don't you play with the duck in the bathtub?" Lisa liked the idea. So, she filled the bathtub with water and put the bright duck in it. The duck floated on the water. Lisa laughed and splashed water with the duck. After playing for a while, Lisa felt hungry. She asked her mom, "Can I have a snack, please?" Her mom suggested, "How about some apple slices and cheese?" Lisa agreed and said, "Yummy! Thank you, Mom!" She ate her snack and thought about how much fun she had with her bright duck in the bathtub.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside in the dirt. One day, he got very dirty. His fur was full of mud and leaves. Spot knew he needed a bath, but he didn't like baths. He had an urge to stay dirty and keep playing. As Spot played, the sun went down and the air got cold. Spot started to shiver because he was wet and cold from the mud. He knew he needed to go inside and get warm. But, he was worried his mom would be mad because he was so dirty. Spot went inside and showed his mom how dirty he was. She was not mad. She knew he needed help to get clean and warm. She gave Spot a warm bath and dried him off. Spot felt so much better. He was warm and clean, and he didn't shiver anymore. Spot learned that it's okay to get dirty, but getting clean and warm is important too.

Once upon a time, there was a little lion named Leo. Leo lived in the jungle with his family. He loved to play with his friends, but he was too shy to roar. One day, Leo's mom asked him to go to the store to get an order of food for dinner. Leo was scared, but he wanted to help his mom. So, he walked through the jungle to the store. On his way, Leo met his friend, the monkey. The monkey said, "Hi, Leo! Why don't you roar to say hello?" Leo felt embarrassed because he couldn't roar. He looked down and said, "I'm too shy to roar." The monkey smiled and said, "Don't worry, Leo. I'll help you learn how to roar." They practiced together, and soon Leo was able to roar very loud. He was so happy! Leo went to the store, got the order of food, and roared a big thank you to the storekeeper. He walked back home, proud of his new roar. From that day on, Leo was never too shy to roar again.

Once upon a time, there was a big, wild cat. This cat was very graceful. It liked to run and jump in the forest. The cat had a lot of fun every day. One day, the wild cat met a little bird. The bird was sad. It could not fly. The cat wanted to help the bird. They had an idea. The cat would teach the bird how to jump. The wild cat and the little bird practiced jumping together. They had fun and became good friends. One day, while they were jumping, the bird jumped so high that it started to fly. The wild cat was so happy for the bird. But then, something unexpected happened. The wild cat jumped so high that it started to fly too! Now, both the wild cat and the little bird could fly together. They had even more fun in the sky, and they were the best of friends forever.

One day, a girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a big hill and wanted to zoom down it. So, she got on her bike and zoomed down the hill very fast. As she zoomed, she saw a purse on the ground. She stopped her bike to pick it up. Lily opened the purse and found a delicious apple inside. She took a big bite and said, "Yum! This is the best apple I have ever had!" Just then, a lady came running up to Lily. She looked very sad and said, "Oh no! I lost my purse with my apple inside. Have you seen it?" Lily felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, I found your purse and ate your apple. I didn't know it was yours." The lady was not mad, but she was surprised. She said, "That's okay, but you must have magic powers now. That was a magic apple that makes you very strong!" Lily was so excited about her new powers. She used her strength to help people in the park. She helped a boy get his kite out of a tree and carried a heavy bag for an old man. From that day on, Lily and the lady became best friends, and they shared many more magical adventures together.
