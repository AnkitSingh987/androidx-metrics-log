Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Dog

One day, a young boy named Tim went outside to play. He saw his dad by the grill, cooking food. Tim asked, "Dad, what will happen if I touch the grill?" His dad said, "No, Tim! It's hot. You'll get hurt." Tim played with his ball but it went near the grill. He was scared to get it. A big dog came and took the ball in its mouth. Tim thought the dog would run away with his ball. But the dog did something unexpected. The big dog gave the ball back to Tim. Tim said, "Thank you, dog!" The dog was nice and wanted to play. Tim and the dog played all day. Tim learned not to touch the grill and made a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named April. April was so excited, because she heard that all the other bunnies in the forest were planning a march. April asked her mom what it was, and her mom said, "It's like a parade for bunnies. Everyone is marching together through the forest." April was so eager to join, but she was also a bit fearful. She asked her mom if she could go, and her mom said, "I promise that if you're careful and stick to the path, you can join the march." April's eyes lit up. She scampered off to join the other bunnies and have a lot of fun. She stopped to look around at all the other bunnies and noticed that even though some of them were a bit scared, they all felt excited about the march. It wasn't long before all the bunnies were happily marching towards the end, with April proudly leading the way!

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his toy ball. One day, he found a different ball in the park. This ball was big and yellow. Spot thought it seemed fun to play with. Spot took the big yellow ball home. He tried to play with it, but it was too big for his mouth. Spot was sad. Then, he had an idea. He found a piece of rope and tied it to the big yellow ball. Now, Spot could play with the big yellow ball using the rope. He was very happy. Spot learned that even if something is different, it can still be fun to play with. And from that day on, Spot and the big yellow ball were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a modern car that could talk. The car loved to travel far and wide. One day, the car met a little boy named Tim. Tim was hungry and wanted some toast. "Hello, car! Can you help me find some toast?" asked Tim. "Of course, Tim! Let's travel together and find you some toast," said the car. They traveled to a small house where they found a kind lady. The lady made Tim some warm, yummy toast. Tim and the car became good friends and traveled together, helping others along the way. And they all lived happily ever after.

Lily and Tom were playing with their toys in the living room. Lily had a doll with a delicate dress and a zip on the back. Tom had a car with big wheels and a loud horn. They were having fun, but they also wanted to try each other's toys. "Can I play with your car, Tom?" Lily asked. "OK, but be careful. It is not a toy for girls," Tom said. "Can I play with your doll, Lily?" Tom asked. "OK, but be gentle. She is not a toy for boys," Lily said. They swapped their toys and started to play. Lily drove the car around the sofa and pressed the horn. Tom held the doll and looked at her dress. He saw the zip and pulled it down. The dress fell off the doll and Tom saw her underwear. "Oops!" Tom said. Lily heard Tom and looked at him. She saw her doll without her dress and got angry. "Tom, what did you do? You ruined my doll!" Lily shouted. She ran to Tom and grabbed her doll. She tried to put the dress back on, but it was hard. The zip was stuck and the fabric was torn. "Sorry, Lily. I was just curious. I didn't mean to break it," Tom said. He felt bad and wanted to make Lily happy. He looked around and saw a blanket on the floor. He picked it up and wrapped it around the doll. He gave it to Lily and said: "Here, Lily. I made a new dress for your doll. It is warm and cozy. Do you like it?" Lily looked at her doll with the blanket dress. She smiled and hugged Tom. "Thank you, Tom. You are a good friend. I like it," Lily said. They hugged each other and played with their toys again. They learned to share and to be careful. They also learned to say sorry and to forgive. They were happy.
