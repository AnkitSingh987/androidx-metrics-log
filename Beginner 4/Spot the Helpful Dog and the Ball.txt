Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Helpful Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a helpful dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his rubber ball. He would run, jump, and even tear through the grass to catch it. One day, Spot saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad because her rubber doll was broken. Spot wanted to help, so he said, "Don't be sad, I can help you fix your doll!" Spot used his teeth to hold the doll's broken arm. He was very careful not to tear it more. Then, Lily found some tape and wrapped it around the arm. Together, they fixed the rubber doll. Lily was so happy, she hugged Spot and said, "Thank you for being so helpful, Spot!" From that day on, Spot and Lily were the best of friends. They played with the rubber ball and the rubber doll, and always had lots of fun.

One day, a little bird named Tim was sitting on a twig. He was very fearful because he saw a big cat near him. The cat looked at Tim and said, "Don't be scared, little bird. I want to be your friend." Tim was surprised and asked, "Really? You don't want to eat me?" The cat shook her head and said, "No, I want to share my food with you." So, the cat and Tim shared a big meal of yummy seeds and bugs. They were very happy. The other birds saw them and were scared too. But Tim told them, "This cat is nice. She shared her food with me." Soon, all the birds and the cat became friends. They learned that sharing is good and being kind can make new friends. And they all lived happily together.

Lily and Tom were playing near the sea. They liked to look at the big waves and the birds. They saw a big cliff with many rocks and plants. Lily wanted to climb the cliff and see what was on top. "Come on, Tom, let's go up there!" Lily said. Tom was scared. He did not like high places. He remembered when he fell from a tree and hurt his leg. "No, Lily, it is too high and dangerous. We might fall and get hurt. Mommy told us to stay away from the cliff. She will be angry if we go there," Tom said. Lily did not listen. She was curious and brave. She ran towards the cliff and started to climb. Tom followed her. He did not want to be alone. They climbed and climbed. They saw many things on the cliff. They saw flowers and bugs and shells. They saw a nest with baby birds. They saw a big hole with a rabbit. They were having fun. But they did not see the edge of the cliff. They did not see how close they were to the end. They did not see the big wave that was coming. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise. It was the wave. It hit the cliff and splashed water everywhere. Lily and Tom were wet and scared. They slipped and almost fell. They grabbed each other and held on. They cried for help. They wished they had listened to Tom. They wished they had stayed away from the cliff. Mommy heard their cries. She was busy making a sandcastle with their baby brother. She looked up and saw them on the cliff. She was shocked and worried. She ran to them and helped them down. She hugged them and checked them for injuries. She was glad they were safe. But she was also angry and sad. She scolded them for being naughty and careless. She reminded them of the rules and the dangers. She told them they could have died. Lily and Tom felt sorry and ashamed. They apologized to Mommy and promised to never do it again. They learned their lesson. They realized that the cliff was not a place to play. They realized that Mommy knew best. They realized that they loved Mommy and each other.

Once upon a time, in a big, old cave, there lived a kind bear named Ben. Ben had many friends in the forest. One day, Ben saw a small bird with a hurt wing. The bird was sad and could not fly. "Hello, little bird," said Ben. "Why are you sad?" "My wing hurts," said the bird. "I cannot fly to my nest." Ben wanted to help the bird. He had an idea. "I will offer you my cave to rest," said Ben. "You can stay here until your wing is better." The bird was happy and stayed in the cave with Ben. They became very good friends. Soon, the bird's wing was better, and it could fly again. The bird thanked Ben for his help and they played together in the forest every day.
