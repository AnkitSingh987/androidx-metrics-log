Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Wagon

Lily and Ben were playing with their wagon in the backyard. They liked to fill it with toys and pretend they were going on a trip. But today, they had a problem. There was only one wagon, and they both wanted to pull it. "I want to pull the wagon!" Lily said. "No, I want to pull the wagon!" Ben said. They both grabbed the handle and tried to pull it their way. The wagon did not move. It only made a loud noise. "Stop it!" their mom said from the window. "You are making too much noise. Share the wagon or I will take it away." Lily and Ben did not want to lose their wagon. They looked at each other and had an idea. They decided to whisper instead of yell. "Let's take turns," Lily whispered. "OK, you go first," Ben whispered. They nodded and smiled. Lily pulled the wagon for a while, then she let Ben pull it. They whispered to each other about their trip and the toys they had. They had fun and did not make any more noise. Their mom was happy. She saw them playing nicely and sharing the wagon. She came out and gave them a hug and a cookie. "Good job, Lily and Ben," she said. "You are very good at whispering and sharing. I am proud of you." Lily and Ben thanked their mom and ate their cookie. They were happy too. They learned that whispering and sharing were better than yelling and fighting. They continued to play with their wagon until it was time to go inside.

Ben and Lily were playing with their dog, Max, in the park. They threw a ball and Max ran to catch it. He was very fast and happy. Ben and Lily laughed and clapped. But then, a big man came and shouted at them. He said they were crazy and they had to leave. He said Max was bad and he would hurt his heel. He pointed at his shoe and showed them a hole. He said Max had bitten his shoe when he ran past him. Ben and Lily were scared and sad. They said they were sorry and they did not know. They said Max was good and he did not mean to bite. They said they would go home and not come back. They took Max and ran away. Max looked at them with his big eyes and wagged his tail. He did not understand why the man was angry. He just wanted to play and have fun. Ben and Lily regretted playing in the park. They wished they had gone somewhere else. They wished the man had been nice and not mean. They wished Max had not bitten his shoe. They hugged Max and told him they loved him. Max licked their faces and made them smile. He did not regret anything. He was happy with his friends.

One day, a clumsy dog named Max found a shiny copper coin in the park. He picked it up in his mouth and started to run. Max was so excited that he didn't look where he was going. He bumped into a big tree and the copper coin fell out of his mouth. A little bird saw Max and the copper coin. The bird flew down and said, "Hey, Max! That's my copper coin. Can I have it back, please?" Max didn't want to give the coin back, but he knew it was the right thing to do. Max looked at the bird and said, "Okay, I surrender. You can have your copper coin back." The bird was so happy and thanked Max. They became friends and played together in the park every day. And Max learned that it was good to be kind and share with others.

One day, a kind boy named Tom went to the airport with his mom. They were going to see his dad who was coming back from a trip. Tom was very happy and excited. He could not wait to see his dad. At the airport, Tom saw big planes. He saw people going here and there. He held his mom's hand tight. They waited for Tom's dad to come. Then, something unexpected happened. A big dog ran up to Tom and licked his face. Tom was surprised and laughed. The dog's owner came and said sorry. The owner was Tom's dad! Tom's dad had brought the dog as a surprise for Tom. They all went home together, happy and full of love.
