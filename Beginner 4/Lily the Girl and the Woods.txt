Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Woods

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big, purple ball. Lily loved to play with her purple ball every day. She would throw it up in the air and catch it with a big smile on her face. One day, Lily was playing with her ball near the woods. She threw it very high, but she missed it when it came down. The ball rolled into the woods, and Lily went to find it. As she looked for her ball, she heard a soft voice say, "Hello, do you need help?" It was a tiny bunny. Lily said, "Yes, I miss my purple ball. Can you help me find it?" The bunny nodded and they started to search together. The woods were very quiet, and Lily liked the silence. It made her feel calm and happy. After a while, they found the purple ball near a big tree. Lily was so happy! She hugged the bunny and said, "Thank you for helping me!" The bunny smiled and said, "You're welcome, Lily. Let's play together!" So, Lily and the bunny played with the purple ball, and they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She was three years old and very happy. She loved to play and smile with her friends. One day, Mia's best friend gave her a very special card. It had a big heart and lots of bright colors on it. Mia was so excited and smiled from ear to ear. Mia was very healthy and every morning, she would go for a walk. She would take the card with her and imagine all the fun things she could do. Whenever Mia showed the card to her friends, they would all smile. Mia was so happy to have it and she loved to show it off. Mia never got tired of her card. It always made her smile, no matter how she was feeling. She cherished it forever.

Once upon a time, in a big gray house, there lived a little boy named Tim and his white cat, Snowy. They loved to play together all day long. Tim would throw a ball, and Snowy would chase it and bring it back. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, you can't play inside today. I need to clean the house. You can play in the yard, but don't go too far." Tim was sad, but he knew he had to listen to his mom. So, he took Snowy outside to play in the yard. As they played, a big gray dog came up to them. He wanted to play too. Tim looked at the dog and said, "Hello, big gray dog! You can play with us, but you have to be nice to Snowy." The big gray dog wagged his tail, and they all played together. After a while, Tim's mom called him inside. "Tim, it's time to come in now. Thank you for playing outside like I asked." Tim said goodbye to the big gray dog and went inside with Snowy. From that day on, Tim, Snowy, and the big gray dog were the best of friends. They played together every day, and Tim's mom always allowed them to have fun in the yard.

One day, a big dragon was in the forest. The dragon was bald and had no friends. He was sad and wanted to play. A little bird saw the dragon and flew to him. The bird said, "Why are you sad, Mr. Dragon?" The dragon said, "I am bald and have no friends. No one wants to play with me." The bird felt bad for the dragon. "I will be your friend," said the bird. "Let's play together!" The dragon was very happy. They played all day and had fun. When it was time for the bird to leave, the dragon said, "Thank you for being my friend." From that day on, the bald dragon and the little bird were best friends. They played together in the forest every day and never felt alone again.

Lily and Tom were friends who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big tree with many red apples. They wanted to pick some apples to eat, but the tree was too high. "Let's find a stick to knock down the apples," Tom said. "OK, but be careful," Lily said. They looked around and found a long stick. Tom held the stick and tried to hit the apples, but he missed. He tried again and again, but he could not reach the apples. He got angry and threw the stick on the ground. "This is terrible! I hate this tree and this stick and these apples!" he shouted. Lily was scared by Tom's words. She did not like how he was acting. She said, "Tom, you should not be so mad. Maybe we can find another way to get the apples. Or we can play something else. It is not nice to say bad things about the tree and the stick and the apples. They did not do anything wrong to you." Tom did not listen to Lily. He said, "You are silly, Lily. You don't know anything. I don't want to play with you anymore. I want to pick the apples by myself. Go away!" Lily felt sad and hurt by Tom's words. She did not want to lose her friend, but she did not want to stay with him either. She said, "OK, Tom. I will go away. But I hope you will be sorry for what you said. You are not a good friend to me." She left the park and went home. Tom stayed in the park and tried to pick the apples by himself. He climbed on a rock and jumped to grab an apple, but he fell and hurt his knee. He cried and wished Lily was there to help him. He realized he was wrong and he missed Lily. He wanted to say sorry to her and be friends again. But it was too late. Lily did not come back to the park. She found a new friend who was kind and gentle. They played together and had fun. They shared their toys and their snacks. They even picked some flowers and made a necklace for each other. They said, "We are friends forever. We will never say bad things to each other. We will always help each other." Tom saw them from afar and felt lonely and sad. He wished he had not been so mean to Lily. He wished he had listened to her and played with her. He wished he had picked some flowers and made a necklace for her. He wished he had said, "You are my best friend. I want to marry you when we grow up." But he did not say those words. He did not pick those flowers. He did not make that necklace. He did not marry Lily. He lost his friend and his chance to be happy. The moral of the story is: Be nice to your friends and do not say bad things to them. You may regret it later and lose them forever.
