Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Grass

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a long strip of grass that was perfect for playing on. Tim wanted to play with his toy car, but his mom said, "Wait, Tim. We have to put on sunscreen first so we don't get burned." "But I want to play now!" Tim said. His mom told him, "We must delay playing for a few minutes to stay safe in the sun." As they put on sunscreen, a little girl named Sue came to the park. She had a toy car too! Tim and Sue played together and had lots of fun. They raced their cars up and down the strip of grass. At the end of the day, Tim was happy that he listened to his mom. He learned that sometimes it's good to delay things to stay safe. And because he waited, he made a new friend!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had an enormous room with many toys. Her favorite toy was a pretty lamp. She loved to turn it on and off. The lamp made her room bright and happy. One day, Lily's friend Tim came to play. They played with blocks and dolls. They laughed and had fun. Then, it was time for Tim to go home. They said goodbye and Lily went to turn on her lamp. But she could not find it. She forgot where she put it. Lily was sad and asked her mom, "Mom, have you seen my lamp?" Her mom said, "Yes, I put it on the table." Lily found her lamp and was happy again. She promised to never forget where she put her lamp. And from that day, Lily always remembered where her lovely lamp was.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a large snake. The snake was very long and very wide. He had many friends in the forest, like the small rabbit, the quick bird, and the happy frog. One day, the large snake could not remember where his home was. He looked and looked, but he could not find it. The snake was very sad. He asked his friends, the small rabbit, the quick bird, and the happy frog to help him find his home. They all looked together. The small rabbit hopped, the quick bird flew, and the happy frog jumped. At last, they found the large snake's home. The snake was very happy and said, "Thank you!" to his friends. They all played together and had lots of fun. And the large snake never forgot to remember where his home was again.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom had a toy boat with a big sail. He loved to play with his boat in the water. One sunny day, Tom went to the park with his mom. He saw a dry pond and said, "Mom, can I put my boat in the water?" Tom's mom looked at the pond and said, "There is no water, Tom. The pond is dry." Just then, a big rain cloud came and rain started to fall. The dry pond filled with water. Tom was so happy! He put his boat in the water and watched it sail away. But then, something unexpected happened! A duck came and took Tom's boat. "Hey, that's my boat!" Tom shouted. The duck looked at Tom and said, "I'm sorry, I thought it was my own boat." The duck gave the boat back to Tom. They both laughed and played with their boats together. Tom made a new friend that day, and they had lots of fun sailing their boats in the pond.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved going to the grocery with his mom. One day, Max and his mom went to the grocery to buy some food. Max was very happy. While they were shopping, Max saw a big cat. He wanted to play with the cat, but the cat did not want to play. Max was sad. He tried to play with the cat again, but the cat got angry. Max did not know what to do. Then, something unexpected happened. Max started to suffer. He felt ashamed for making the cat angry. Max ran away and hid behind a big box. The cat saw Max suffering and felt bad. The cat went to Max and said, "I am sorry for being angry. Let's be friends." Max was very happy, and they played together. From that day on, Max and the cat were best friends.
