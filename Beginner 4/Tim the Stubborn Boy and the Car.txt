Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Stubborn Boy and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn little boy named Tim. Tim loved to fight with his toys. He had a toy bear and a toy car, and they would fight all day long. Tim wished he could get a medal for winning the fights. One sunny day, Tim and his toys went to the park. They played and fought on the green grass. Suddenly, a big wind came and took away Tim's toy car. Tim was sad and didn't know what to do. He didn't want to fight anymore. Just then, a friendly bird flew down with the toy car in its beak. The bird said, "I saw you fight with your toys, and I wanted to play too!" Tim was so happy to have his toy car back. He learned that it was more fun to play and share than to fight. And guess what? The bird gave Tim a shiny medal for being a good friend.

Once upon a time, there was a lively flea named Fifi. Fifi liked to go jump and play all day. She lived in a big green tree with her family. One sunny day, Fifi met a new friend named Tom. Tom was a tiny ant. Fifi said, "Hi, Tom! Let's go play!" Tom said, "Yes, Fifi! Let's go!" Fifi and Tom had fun playing in the sun. They jumped and laughed together. Fifi was very happy to have a new friend. They played until the sun went down and it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Bob. Bob loved to gather food for his family. One day, he went out to find some yummy things to eat. Bob hopped and hopped until he saw a big, green broccoli. He felt a little anxious because he had never seen such a big broccoli before. But he knew his family would love it, so he tried to carry it home. It was hard work, but Bob did not give up. He finally got the big broccoli home to his family. They were so happy and proud of him. They all ate the yummy broccoli together and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a little chair. The chair was worried. It did not know if it was strong enough to hold people up. One day, a big chair saw the little chair looking sad. "Little chair, why are you worried?" asked the big chair. "I don't know if I can hold people up," said the little chair. The big chair wanted to help. They started to discuss how to be strong. The big chair said, "You can do it! Just try your best." The little chair felt better. It tried to be strong. Soon, it was not worried anymore. The little chair could hold people up, just like the big chair. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a nice little mouse. She lived in a nice little house in the forest. Every morning she would sort her food into piles and eat it for breakfast. She was a very tidy mouse. One day she heard a noise coming from outside her door. She was curious so she opened it to see what it was. "Hi mouse!" said a voice from outside. It was a friendly little fox. "Do you want to play?" The mouse smiled. "Yes, that would be nice!" she said. So the fox and the mouse played together all day. They did all sorts of nice things, like sorting the leaves into piles and making fortresses out of twigs. They had so much fun! At the end of the day, the fox said goodbye and thanked the mouse for such a nice day. The little mouse smiled and waved goodbye, watching the fox disappear into the forest. The mouse returned to her house and got ready for bed. She was tired from all the sorting and playing, but she had had such a nice day. She smiled to herself as she drifted off to sleep.

One day, a colorful bird flew to a child's window. The child saw the bird and was very happy. The bird sang a pretty song and the child clapped her hands. The child wanted to touch the bird, so she tried to stretch her arm out the window. But her arm was too short. The child felt sad and said, "Bird, please come closer so I can touch you." The bird looked at the child and said, "I want to help, but I am scared. What if you hold me too tight?" The child thought for a moment and said, "I promise to be gentle and kind." The bird trusted the child and flew closer. The child touched the bird softly and they both smiled.
