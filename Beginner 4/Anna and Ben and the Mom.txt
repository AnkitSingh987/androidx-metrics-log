Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben and the Mom

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play dress-up with their mom's clothes. One day, they find a big suit in the closet. It is blue and has many buttons. "Let's try it on!" Anna says. She puts on the jacket and the pants. They are too big for her. She looks funny. Ben laughs. "You look like a clown!" he says. Anna feels sad. She likes the suit. She thinks it is pretty. She does not want to take it off. "No, I don't!" she says. "I look like a grown-up. I like the suit. It is mine!" Ben shakes his head. "No, it is not. It is mom's. You have to share. Let me try it on too!" Anna is stubborn. She does not want to share. She holds the suit tight. She says "No, no, no!" They start to pull the suit. They make a loud noise. Mom hears them. She comes to the closet. She sees them fighting over the suit. "What are you doing?" she asks. "Why are you making such a mess?" Anna and Ben stop. They look at mom. They feel guilty. "We are sorry, mom," they say. Mom smiles. She is not angry. She understands. She likes to play dress-up too. She takes the suit from them. She puts it on the bed. "Listen, my loves," she says. "The suit is too big for both of you. It is not fun to wear. Why don't you try something else? I have some hats and scarves and dresses and shoes. You can mix and match them. You can make your own outfits. You can be anything you want. How does that sound?" Anna and Ben think. They like mom's idea. They nod. "Okay, mom," they say. Mom gives them a hug. She helps them find some clothes. They have fun. They try different things. They show mom their looks. Mom claps. She says they are beautiful and handsome and creative. Anna and Ben are happy. They forget about the suit. They thank mom. They say they love her. Mom says she loves them too. She is proud of them. She is glad they learned to share. She thinks they are the best twins ever.

Once upon a time, there was a long caterpillar named Carl. Carl loved to match things. One day, he saw a box with a label on it. The label had a picture of a red apple. Carl thought, "I want to find a red apple to match the label!" So, he went on a long journey to find the perfect red apple. As Carl crawled, he met a bird named Betty. Betty asked, "What are you doing, Carl?" Carl said, "I am trying to find a red apple to match the label on the box!" Betty said, "I know where to find red apples! Follow me!" Carl and Betty went to a big tree with lots of red apples. Carl was so happy! He found a red apple that matched the label on the box. Carl said, "Thank you, Betty!" They both smiled and played together under the big tree with the red apples.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Emma. She loved to play with her rag. Every day, she would use it to cover her face and make funny faces at herself in the mirror. She also liked to rock back and forth on the sill of her bedroom window. One day, her mom gave her a very special rag: it was delicate and so soft. Emma liked the new rag so much that she slept with it at night and brought it with her everywhere. Each day, she rocked with her rag in her arms. She spent hours watching the world outside her window, and feeling the gentle rocking motion help her relax. She felt so happy and peaceful as she rocked. Whenever she grew tired of rocking, Emma would go off exploring, her delicate rag trailing behind her like a happy flag. Wherever she went, she took her rag with her, for she had discovered a very special secret: no matter what, it always made her feel safe.

Once upon a time there were two little children, James and Sarah. James was very brave and Sarah was quite frightened. One day, they decided to explore the nearby forest. As they were walking they noticed a big log in the centre of the clearing. James thought it would be nice to sit on it and take a break. But when he got closer to the log, he heard a voice coming from it! He jumped back in fright. Sarah was so scared, she started to cry. James stepped forward bravely and asked the log, "Who are you?". The log spoke back saying, "I am a magic log! I can grant wishes." James and Sarah were delighted and knew exactly what to do. They asked the log for a picnic lunch and some ice cream! The log granted their wish and soon enough the children were enjoying their treats. The log said goodbye and disappeared back in to the forest. From then on James and Sarah were not so frightened of the forest. They knew the magic log was looking out for them. The end.
