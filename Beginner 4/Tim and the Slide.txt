Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Slide

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a big slide and wanted to go on it. But first, his mom said, "Tim, sit on the bench and let me put on your buckle." Tim did as his mom said and sat down. His mom put the buckle on his shoe. Then, Tim went to the slide. He saw a girl named Sue at the top. Sue was scared to go down the slide. Tim said, "Don't be scared, Sue. It's fun!" Sue felt better and went down the slide. But when Sue got to the bottom, she fell and started to cry. Tim felt embarrassed because he told her it was fun. Tim's mom came over and said, "Tim, you should have been more careful." Tim was sad that he made Sue cry and that his mom was mad at him.

Once upon a time there was a little bird. He was called Flyer. One day Flyer decided to go for a fly. He flew up high into the sky. His wings made a strong beat and he flew higher and higher. He eventually flew high enough to spot another bird up in the sky. He flew close to it and introduced himself. "Hi, I'm Flyer. What's your name?" The other bird eyed him fiercely. "I'm Chase," it said. It had huge wings, much bigger than Flyer's, and it seemed very serious. Chase did not like Flyer at all. He started flapping his big wings and tried to scare Flyer away. Flyer was scared and he tried to turn around and fly away but Chase was too fast for him. He started to chase Flyer through the sky. Flyer was scared and desperately flapped his little wings. Then, suddenly, something amazing happened. Flyer gained speed and managed to get away from Chase. He was so relieved and he was proud of his powerful little wings. From that day on, Flyer was no longer scared of big birds with fierce wings. He loved to fly higher and higher in the sky.

Lily and Ben were twins. They liked to play with toys. One day, they went to the toy store with their mom. They saw many toys that they wanted. "Look, mom, a big doll house!" Lily said. "Can I have it, please?" "Look, mom, a fast car!" Ben said. "Can I have it, please?" Mom shook her head. "I'm sorry, my loves, but those toys are too expensive. We can only buy one small toy each." Lily and Ben felt sad. They wished they had more money to buy the toys they liked. They walked around the store and looked for small toys. They saw a shelf with books. They liked books. They picked up a book each. The books had colorful pictures and stories. "Look, mom, a book about princesses!" Lily said. "Look, mom, a book about dinosaurs!" Ben said. Mom smiled. "Those are good choices, my loves. Books are not expensive, but they are very valuable. They can teach you many things and make you happy." Lily and Ben felt happy. They hugged their mom and thanked her. They paid for the books and went home. They read the books together and had fun. They forgot about the expensive toys. They realized that books were better than toys. They made a wish that they would always have books to read.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy car that he loved to play with. Yesterday, he took his car to the park to race with his friends. Tim was very competitive and wanted to win. At the park, Tim met his friend Sam. Sam also had a toy car. They decided to have a race. Tim said, "Let's put our cars here and race to that tree!" Sam agreed, and they started the race. They both ran as fast as they could, pushing their toy cars. In the end, Tim won the race. Sam was sad, but Tim shared a lesson with him. "Being competitive is fun, but it's more important to have fun with friends and be kind." Sam smiled, and they played together for the rest of the day.
