Category: Beginner
Title: The Cat and the Tree

One day, a small cat went for a walk. She saw a big tree with lots of shade. The small cat was hot, so she went under the tree to cool down. Under the tree, she met a big dog. The dog looked at the cat and said, "Hi, I am Dog. Do you want to play with me?" The small cat was scared at first, but she wanted to make a new friend. So, she said, "Hi, I am Cat. I want to play with you." They played and had fun under the tree. They ran and jumped in the shade. They were very happy. Then, something unexpected happened! The big tree started to speak! The tree said, "Hello, I am Tree. I like when friends play under me. Can I play with you too?" The small cat and the big dog were surprised, but they said, "Yes, Tree! Let's play together!" And they all played and had fun in the shade.

Once upon a time, there was a rough, old telephone. It lived in a small house with a boy named Tim and his mom. The telephone was always there to support them when they needed to talk to their friends and family. One day, Tim's mom asked him to use the telephone to call his grandma. Tim picked up the telephone and started to dial. But something unexpected happened! Instead of hearing his grandma's voice, he heard a cat meowing. Tim was very surprised. He told his mom, and she tried to call grandma too. But, she also heard the cat meowing. They were both very confused. They tried to call other friends, but every time, all they could hear was the cat meowing. Finally, Tim and his mom decided to look for the cat. They followed the meowing sound and found a little cat stuck in a tree near their house. They helped the cat get down and gave it some food. The cat was very happy and thanked Tim and his mom. From that day on, the rough, old telephone worked just fine. Tim and his mom never forgot the day when the telephone helped them find their new friend, the cat. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big jar. The jar was in a small house. In the house lived a boy named Tom and his mom. One day, Tom's mom said, "Tom, I want you to change the jar. It's easy. Just take out the old things and put in new things." Tom said, "Okay, mom." Tom changed the jar. He took out the old things and put in new things. It was easy. Tom's mom was happy. They both smiled, and Tom said, "I like changing the jar. It's fun and easy!"

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim got a new belt for his birthday. It was a nice belt with many colors. He was very happy. Tim wanted to wear his new belt to show his friends. But, he did not know how to make it fit. He tried and tried, but the belt was too big. Tim asked his mom for help. She showed him how to make the belt fit just right. Tim was very happy. He wore his new belt to play with his friends. They all liked his new belt, and Tim felt proud.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a big tree with a sign that said "Free cocoa". Lily loved cocoa, so she ran to the tree. But when she got there, she felt dizzy. She did not know why. Lily called out to her mom, "Mom, I feel dizzy!" Her mom came running to help her. "Oh no, Lily! You must sit down." Lily sat on the grass and her mom gave her some water. The dizziness went away after a while. Lily's mom looked at the cocoa sign and said, "We should not take cocoa from the tree. It might not be good for you." So, they went home and made their own cocoa. Lily felt better and learned not to take things from trees.
