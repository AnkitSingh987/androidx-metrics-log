Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Missile

A boy named Tim had an interesting toy. It was a missile. Tim wanted to create a fun game with it. One day, Tim's friend, Sue, came over. Tim said, "Let's create a game with my interesting missile!" Sue said, "Yes, let's do it!" They played with the missile and had a lot of fun. They made up a game where they would throw the missile and see who could catch it. They laughed and played until it was time for Sue to go home. Tim and Sue were very happy with their new game.

Once upon a time, there was a shy little dog named Tim. He was scared to play with the other dogs in the park. One day, he saw a new dog named Max. Max was friendly and wanted to play with Tim. Tim was shy and didn't know what to do. Max said, "Hey Tim, do you want to play with me?" Tim decided to be brave and said, "Yes, I want to play." They played together and had a lot of fun. They ran, jumped, and chased each other in the park. Tim was happy he decided to play with Max. After playing, they were both very dirty. Tim's mom brought a big towel to clean them up. She said, "You two had fun, but now it's time to get clean!" Tim and Max let Tim's mom use the towel to clean their fur. They were both happy and clean, ready to play again another day.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the grocery with his mom. It was a hot day, so Tim wore his favorite hat to stay cool. He saw a cat outside the grocery and wanted to care for it. "Mom, can we take the cat home?" Tim asked. His mom said, "Not now, but we can give it some food later." Inside the grocery, Tim and his mom bought food and a toy for the cat. Tim was very happy. They also bought ice cream for themselves because it was a hot day. Tim thought about how nice it would be to care for the cat and play with it. After they left the grocery, Tim and his mom saw the cat again. They gave it food and the toy. The cat was very happy and played with the toy. Tim's mom said, "You did a good job caring for the cat, Tim." Tim smiled and knew that he had helped the cat on this hot day.

Once upon a time, there was an enormous dog named Max. Max was a very big, but friendly dog. One day, Max saw a little church with an open door. He decided to go inside. In the church, Max saw a little girl named Sue. Sue saw how big Max was and got scared. She wanted to run away, but Max just wanted to be friends. Max tried to show he was nice by kneeling down on the floor. Sue saw Max kneel and thought maybe he was a nice dog. She started to walk closer to Max. As they got closer, Sue saw how kind Max's eyes were. She reached out her hand and patted Max's head. Max was so happy that Sue was not scared anymore. They became best friends and played together all day long. From that day on, the enormous dog and the little girl were always together, and the church became their favorite place to meet.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big sack. He liked to put toys in the sack and share them with his friends. One day, Tim met a girl named Sally. Sally was envious of Tim's sack of toys. She wanted to have her own sack too. Tim saw that Sally was sad and said, "Don't be sad, Sally. You can play with my toys too!" Sally smiled and accepted Tim's offer. Tim and Sally played with the toys together and had lots of fun. Soon, Sally learned that sharing is good. She was no longer envious of Tim's sack. The moral of the story is to share with others and be kind.
