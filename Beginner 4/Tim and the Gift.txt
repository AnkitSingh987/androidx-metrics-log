Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Gift

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim had a big wish. He wanted to give his friend, Sue, a special gift for her birthday. Tim was a little embarrassed because he did not have any money to buy a gift. One sunny day, Tim found a pretty rock near the park. He thought it would be a nice gift to give Sue. He put the rock in a small box and wrapped it with a colorful paper. He was excited to give his friend her birthday gift. At Sue's birthday party, Tim gave her the wrapped box with the pretty rock inside. Sue opened the gift and smiled. She loved her gift and thanked Tim. They played and laughed together, and Tim was happy that he could make his friend's birthday special.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful cat named Tom. Tom loved to relax in the sun. One day, he saw a big bin near his house. He wanted to see what was inside. Tom jumped on the bin and looked in. He saw a little mouse named Jerry. Jerry was scared and wanted to get out. Tom wanted to help Jerry, so he said, "Don't be scared, I will help you." Tom used his paw to help Jerry out of the bin. Jerry was very happy and thanked Tom. They became good friends and played together every day. And when they were tired, they would relax in the sun together.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little pond. Many animals lived near the pond. They liked to play and make friends there. The pond was very generous. It gave water to all the animals. They were happy and loved the pond. One day, a small bird came to the pond. He was thirsty and wanted to drink water. But he was scared of the big animals. The pond saw the small bird and wanted to help. So, the pond asked the big animals to be kind and let the small bird drink water too. The big animals agreed and moved away to make space for the small bird. The small bird was happy and thanked the pond. All the animals learned to share and be generous like the pond. They played and lived happily together. The pond taught them that being generous and kind makes everyone happy.

There was a creative little boy named Bob. One day he was playing in the park with his friends. He saw a bug on his leg and he got scared. Bob started to yell and yell. His friends were surprised to hear him make so much noise. They asked him what was wrong and Bob showed them the bug on his leg. His friends laughed and told him it was just a small bug, it couldn't hurt him. Bob was still scared, but he was also brave. After a few minutes he was able to stop yelling and he just watched the bug carefully. Finally, the bug flew away and Bob could relax and go back to playing.

Once upon a time, there was a little pupil named Tim. He was a very frightened boy. He was scared of many things like big dogs, loud noises, and the dark. One day, he went to a new school. At lunchtime, Tim sat with a new friend named Sam. Sam said, "I order pizza for lunch. What do you order?" Tim said, "I order a sandwich." They talked and laughed while they ate their lunch. But then, a big dog came into the school yard. Tim was very frightened. He ran away fast, but the dog chased him. Tim's new friend Sam tried to help, but he couldn't catch up. The dog caught Tim and bit his leg. Tim cried and was very sad. The dog went away, but Tim was still scared and hurt.

Tom and Lily are playing in the park. They like to run and jump and slide. They see a big tree with many leaves. They want to climb the tree. "Can we climb the tree?" Lily asks Tom. "Yes, we can. But we need to be careful. The tree is very high." Tom says. They hold hands and walk to the tree. They find a branch that is low and strong. They pull themselves up and sit on the branch. They look around and see many things. They see birds and flowers and cars and people. "Wow, this is fun!" Lily says. "Yes, it is. We can see everything from here." Tom says. They smile and blink their eyes. They feel the sun and the wind on their faces. They are happy. But then they hear a loud noise. It is a dog. It is big and black and has a red jacket. It is barking and running to the tree. It wants to play with Tom and Lily. "Look, a dog!" Lily says. "Uh-oh, a dog!" Tom says. They are scared. They do not like the dog. The dog is too loud and too fast. They want to get down from the tree. "Help, help!" Lily shouts. "Go away, go away!" Tom shouts. But the dog does not go away. It jumps and tries to reach the branch. It wags its tail and opens its mouth. It thinks Tom and Lily are friends. "Please, someone, help us!" Lily cries. "Please, someone, stop the dog!" Tom cries. They hug each other and close their eyes. They hope the dog will leave them alone.
