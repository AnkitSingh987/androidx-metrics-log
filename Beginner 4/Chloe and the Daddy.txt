Category: Beginner
Title: Chloe and the Daddy

Once there was a daddy and a little girl called Chloe. They went out for lunch at a restaurant. Chloe was very excited and looked at the menu. "What would you like to eat, Chloe?" asked Daddy. Chloe was a bit nervous but she pointed to the yummy pizza. Daddy smiled. "Okay, let's order the pizza," Daddy said. Chloe was so happy that she clapped her hands. The waiter came and brought the food. Daddy asked Chloe to eat but she was too nervous. "It's okay, sweetheart. You can try it," said Daddy. So Chloe accepted Daddy's suggestion and tried the pizza. She loved it and was very proud of herself. She gave Daddy a big hug and they finished their lunch together.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had four legs and a wet nose. He liked to play in the mud and run around with his friends. One day, Spot saw a big puddle. It seemed like a fun place to play. He jumped in and splashed around. He got his legs all wet and muddy. Spot was very happy. After playing in the puddle, Spot went home. His mom saw his wet legs and said, "Spot, you need a bath!" She washed his legs and dried him off. Spot was clean again, but he couldn't wait to play in the puddle another day.

Once upon a time, there was an old man. He was poor, so he did not have much. He had a house with just one chair and one table. One day, a youth walked to his door. The man invited him in and offered him the chair. The youth thanked him and said, "Mind if I rest here for a while?" The man said, "I do not mind at all. Welcome." The youth sat in the chair and told the man why he was so poor. He said he was trying to save money for his three-year-old son. The old man smiled. He reached into his pocket and gave the youth some coins. He said, "This isn't much, but it may help your poor boy." The youth thanked him again. As he left, the old man called out, "I hope everything works out."

One day, Joe and his father were walking along the shore. Joe saw a beautiful yacht floating in the water and asked his father if he could throw a rock at it. His father said, "No, Joe, that would be wrong." But Joe said, "Please can I throw one rock? That would be fun!" His father said, "No, Joe. That's not a good idea." Joe said, "Boring!" Suddenly, a man in the yacht shouted, "Hey! Who's throwing rocks at my yacht!" Joe and his father were scared and began to run away. Joe wanted to throw more rocks but his father said, "No, Joe. That's not a good idea." The man on the yacht shouted, "Stop! I won't call the police if you promise to stop!" Joe and his father agreed. Joe was sad but his father said, "Joe, it's not nice to throw rocks at other people's property. It's better to find something else to do, like playing with a ball." Joe agreed and smiled. He had learned a valuable lesson.

Once upon a time, there was a small red cherry. It lived on a big tree with many other cherries. One day, something big was going to happen. The small cherry was excited but did not know what it was. The small cherry saw a big bird fly near the tree. The bird looked at the cherries and smiled. The small cherry thought, "Maybe the big thing will happen soon!" It felt happy and waited for the big thing to happen. The next day, the big bird came back. It picked the small cherry from the tree and flew away. The small cherry was scared at first, but then it saw a little girl. The big bird dropped the small cherry into the girl's hands. She smiled and ate the cherry. The small cherry knew that this was the big thing that was going to happen. It was happy to make the little girl smile.
