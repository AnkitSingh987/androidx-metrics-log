Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Bowl

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to build a big salad for lunch. He took a big bowl and put in green leaves, red tomatoes, and yellow corn. Tim loved making salads because they were yummy and fun to make. While Tim was making his salad, he found a smelly sock in the bowl. "Ew!" said Tim. He did not know how the sock got in there. The sock was so smelly that Tim did not want to eat the salad anymore. He was sad and hungry. Just then, Tim's mom came into the room and saw the smelly sock. She laughed and said, "Oh, Tim! That's your baby sister's sock. She must have put it in there when we were not looking." Tim's mom took out the sock and helped Tim build a new salad. They all laughed and enjoyed the yummy salad together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a very attractive cat. The cat had a question. She went to ask her friend, the dog, for help. The dog was big and strong. "Dog, I have a question," said the cat. "Why do birds fly away when I try to play with them?" The dog thought for a moment and said, "Maybe they think you want to hurt them." The cat did not like the answer, so she went to the forest to find the birds. She saw a pretty bird in a tree and asked, "Bird, I have a question. Why do you fly away when I try to play with you?" The bird looked scared and flew away without answering the cat's question. The cat felt sad and decided to chase the bird. She climbed the tree and jumped from branch to branch. But the cat fell and hurt herself. The cat learned that it is not good to chase what does not want to be chased.

Once there was a boy called David. He was three years old and he loved to explore. One day, he decided to take a walk in the park. As he walked, he noticed a gate. He wondered what lay beyond, so he tried to unlock it. But it was so expensive that he could not afford to open it. So he asked his Dad to help him. He said, "Can you help me unlock the gate so I can explore what lies behind it?" Dad replied, "Yes, let's see if we can open it". So Dad and David approached the gate. Dad tried various keys until one of them worked. He said to David, "Ah, here's the key that unlocked the gate!" So, as David stepped through the gate, he exclaimed, "Wow, it's so exciting!" Dad and David then explored the park. They discussed the things they saw and the adventures they had. As the day came to an end, they locked the gate again and went home. David couldn't wait to go back and explore the other side of the expensive gate.

Lily was sad. She wanted to play with her doll, but her doll was broken. Her doll's arm was off and her hair was messy. Lily tried to fix her doll, but she could not. She sighed and hugged her doll. Mama heard Lily sigh. She came to see what was wrong. She saw Lily and her doll. She smiled and said, "Don't worry, Lily. I can help you fix your doll. Come with me." She took Lily and her doll to the sewing room. Mama found some thread and a needle. She sewed the doll's arm back on. She combed the doll's hair and tied a ribbon on it. She gave the doll back to Lily. Lily was happy. She touched the doll's arm and hair. They were soft. She thanked Mama and kissed her. Lily and her doll went back to her room. They played tea party and dress up. They had fun. Lily was not sad anymore. She sighed and smiled. She loved her doll and her mama.

One day, a girl named Lucy went to her aunt's house. Her aunt was deaf, so she couldn't hear very well. Lucy loved her aunt and liked to help her. Lucy saw her aunt's cat, Fluffy, stuck in a tree. She wanted to help Fluffy get down. Lucy asked her friend, Tom, "Can you lend me a hand?" Tom nodded and said, "Yes, I can help." Tom and Lucy tried to get Fluffy down from the tree. But, surprise! Fluffy jumped down all by herself. Lucy, Tom, and her aunt laughed and clapped. They were happy that Fluffy was safe.
