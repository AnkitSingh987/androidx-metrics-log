Category: Beginner
Title: The Lonely Tree and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a lonely tree. Near the tree, there was a journal. The journal was sad because no one read it. One day, a little girl named Lily found the journal. She picked it up and said, "Hi, I'm Lily. What's your name?" The journal replied, "I don't have a name. I'm just a lonely journal." Lily said, "Don't be sad. I'll read you and we can be friends." The journal was happy to have a friend. Lily would read the journal and turn its pages every day. They laughed and played together. One day, Lily saw a boy crying. She asked him, "Why are you sad?" The boy said, "I lost my toy and I have no friends." Lily thought for a moment and said, "You can play with us!" The boy, Lily, and the journal played together. They were all happy. The moral of the story is that being kind and sharing can turn loneliness into happiness.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a lot of mist in the air. Tim asked his mom, "What is that?" His mom said, "It's mist, Tim. It's like water in the air." Tim saw a big, expensive toy car on the ground. He wanted to play with it, but it was not his. Another boy, named Sam, said it was his car. Sam did not want to share. Tim was sad, but then he had an idea. He said, "Let's celebrate our new friendship and play together!" Sam agreed, and they played with the expensive car in the mist. They both had a lot of fun and became good friends.

Once upon a time there was a girl who was very sad. She wanted to do something to make her feel better so she decided to bake a cake. She got out her big mixer and started putting in the ingredients for a yummy cake. She mixed up the batter and put it in the oven. After a while, she took out a big delicious cake. She was so excited and wanted to show everyone. She asked her mum if all her friends could come have a piece. Everyone was so happy to enjoy the special cake she baked. The girl was no longer sad. She was so proud that everyone liked the cake. She had made something with her own hands and everyone enjoyed it. From then on, the girl put a lot of care into baking cakes and always shared them with her friends and family.

Once upon a time, there was a big, filthy dog named Max. Max liked to dig in the dirt and play with his friends. One day, Max saw a shiny black rock. It was a piece of coal. Max wanted to show his friends the coal, but he did not know it was wrong to take it. So, Max decided to steal the coal. He picked it up with his mouth and ran to his friends. "Look what I found!" Max said to his friends. They all looked at the coal and thought it was very pretty. But then, Max's friend, a wise old cat, told him, "Max, you should not steal. It is not nice to take things that are not yours." Max felt sad and knew he had to make it right. He took the coal back to where he found it and said sorry. From that day on, Max never stole anything again and played happily with his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim found a cheap whistle on the ground. He picked it up and blew it. It made a fun sound. Tim liked the whistle a lot. Tim took the whistle home. His mom asked, "Where did you get that whistle, Tim?" Tim said, "I found it on the ground. Can I keep it?" His mom said, "We should find who it belongs to. It's not nice to keep things that are not ours." They went to the park where Tim found the whistle. They saw a little girl crying. Tim asked, "Is this your whistle?" The girl stopped crying and said, "Yes, it's mine! Thank you for finding it!" Tim gave the whistle back to the girl. They played together and became good friends.
