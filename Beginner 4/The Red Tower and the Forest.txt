Category: Beginner
Title: The Red Tower and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a red tower. It stood in the middle of the forest and seemed to stretch all the way up to the sky. One day, two friends, Sam and Bob, were walking through the forest when they saw the red tower. Sam wanted to try and climb it but Bob said it was too dangerous and he had to rest. Sam wasn't happy. He wanted to reach the top. He said to Bob, "Come on, let's try and get to the top of the tower. It'll be fun!" Bob shook his head. "No way." So Sam decided that he would climb the tower himself. He started to carefully walk towards the base of the tower. But just as he was about to climb, Bob grabbed him and said, "No Sam! It's too risky. You must rest." Sam argued but finally conceded to Bob's wisdom and stayed at the bottom of the tower. After all, some adventures had to wait until it was safe to take them.

Once upon a time, there was a young sheep named Sam. Sam had lots of soft wool. He lived on a big farm with his friends. One day, Sam met a fox. The fox said, "Sam, I think you have the best wool. Can I have some?" Sam did not know what to do. He had never met a fox before. He thought the fox was nice, so he said, "Okay, you can have some of my wool." The fox took some of Sam's wool and went away. Later, Sam found out that the fox was not nice. The fox used Sam's wool to make a warm bed for himself. Sam felt sad and cold without his wool. The other animals on the farm told Sam, "You should not trust a fox. They are not our friends." Sam learned a lesson that day. He knew he should be careful and not trust everyone he meets.

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play on the beach. One day, they wake up early and go to the sand with Mom and Dad. They see many things: shells, rocks, birds, and water. "Look, Sara, a crab!" Ben says. He points to a small red thing that moves on the sand. Sara runs to see it. She thinks it is cute. It has two big eyes, eight legs, and two claws. "Can I touch it?" Sara asks. She bends down and reaches out her hand. "Be careful, Sara, it might pinch you!" Mom says. She comes closer and watches the crab. The crab sees Sara's hand and stops. It raises its claws and makes a clicking sound. Sara is not scared. She smiles and says, "Hello, crab. Do you want to be my friend?" The crab does not answer. It turns around and runs away. Sara and Ben laugh. They chase the crab for a while, but it is too fast. They give up and look for other things to play with. They have a fun day on the beach. They make a sandcastle, collect shells, and splash in the water. They are happy and tired. They say goodbye to the crab and go home with Mom and Dad. They sleep well and dream of the cute crab.

Once upon a time, in a small house near a big forest, there lived a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to draw pictures and mail them to her grandma who lived far away. One day, Amy felt impatient and wanted to see her grandma right away. Amy decided to walk through the forest to reach her grandma's house. As she walked, she saw a big, mean wolf blocking her path. The wolf growled at her and said, "You cannot pass!" Amy was scared, but she wanted to see her grandma, so she thought of a plan. Amy showed the wolf her drawings and told him, "If you let me pass, I will mail you a new drawing every week." The wolf liked her drawings and agreed to let her pass. So, Amy walked through the forest and finally reached her grandma's house. They were both very happy to see each other, and from that day on, Amy kept her promise to mail a drawing to the wolf every week. The wolf stopped being mean and became a friend to Amy and her grandma.
