Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Fat Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tim. Tim loved to play with his jet toy. One day, the jet went too far and got stuck in a tree. Tim was sad and did not know what to do. A little bird lived in the tree where the jet was stuck. The bird and Tim started to quarrel about the jet. The bird thought it was her toy now. Tim really wanted his jet back, but the bird did not want to give it back. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the jet out of the tree. The wind made the jet fly high in the sky. Tim and the bird stopped quarreling and watched the jet together. They became friends and played with the jet together from that day on.

One day, a smart bird named Tim was flying in the sky. He saw a tall pole and wanted to settle on it. Tim was very intelligent and liked to find new places to rest. As Tim settled on the pole, he saw a big cat named Max. Max saw Tim and wanted to catch him. Max said, "I will get you, little bird!" Tim was scared but didn't want to leave the pole. Max climbed up the pole to catch Tim. Tim tried to fly away, but Max was too fast. Max caught Tim and ate him. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big boat. The boat had a big anchor. The boat went to a pretty island. The people on the boat were happy. The people on the boat wanted to play on the island. They had to unpack their toys. They had harmless toys like balls and dolls. They played all day and had fun. When it was time to go home, they packed their toys. They lifted the anchor and sailed away. They waved goodbye to the pretty island. They had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play in the park. One day, Spot met a cat named Fluffy. Fluffy was dizzy from spinning around in circles. Spot wanted to help Fluffy feel better. Spot said, "Fluffy, why are you dizzy?" Fluffy said, "I was trying to catch my tail. I spun around too much and now I feel dizzy." Spot knew just what to do. He told Fluffy, "Sit down and close your eyes. It will help you feel better." As they sat, a big bird came and spoke to them. The bird said, "I saw you spinning, Fluffy. I have a surprise for you." The big bird took out a toy and gave it to Fluffy. It was a toy that looked just like Fluffy's tail! Fluffy was so happy and thanked the big bird. Spot and Fluffy played with the toy tail and became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a charming little house, lived a girl named Lily and her pet cat, Mimi. They loved to play together in their big yard. Lily's heart was full of love for Mimi, and Mimi loved Lily too. One sunny day, Lily and Mimi were playing outside. Mimi was hungry, so Lily said, "Mimi, let's go inside and I'll feed you." Mimi meowed happily and followed Lily into the house. Lily gave Mimi some yummy cat food, and Mimi ate it all up. After Mimi finished eating, they went back outside to play some more. They played hide-and-seek and tag until the sun went down. As they walked back to their charming little house, Lily said, "Mimi, I love you with all my heart." Mimi meowed softly and snuggled close to Lily, happy to have such a loving friend.

One day, a big cat named Tom went into a house. He saw a fun toy on the ceiling. Tom wanted to play with the toy, but it was too high for him to reach. He tried to jump, but he could not get the toy. Tom felt sad. Tom had a friend, a little bird named Tim. Tim saw Tom was sad and asked, "Why are you sad, Tom?" Tom said, "I want to play with the toy on the ceiling, but it is too high for me." Tim wanted to help his friend. Tim told Tom, "I can fly up and get the toy for you. But you have to stay here and not jump." Tom promised to stay. Tim flew up and got the toy for Tom. They played with the toy together and were very happy.
