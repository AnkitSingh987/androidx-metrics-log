Category: Beginner
Title: The Famous Sandwich and the Billy

Once upon a time, there was a famous sandwich. Everyone wanted to try it. So one day a boy named Billy decided to get the sandwich. Billy asked his mom, "Mom, can I get the famous sandwich?" "Yes, you can get the sandwich," his mom replied. Billy was so excited. He ran to the shop and got the famous sandwich. Then he took it home to eat. Billy bit into it and it was so delicious! "This is the best sandwich I've ever had," he said. From then on, Billy would always get the famous sandwich whenever he wanted one.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big red ball. He liked to play with it every day. One day, he saw a girl with a blue ball. She asked, "Do you want to play with me?" Tim was happy and nodded his head. Tim and the girl played with their balls. They laughed and had fun. Tim's mom saw them and was proud of her boy for making a new friend. The girl's mom was happy too. At the end of the day, Tim and the girl said goodbye. "Can we play again tomorrow?" asked the girl. Tim nodded and smiled. They were now good friends and couldn't wait to play again.

Lily and Ben were playing on the beach. They liked to dig in the sand and look for shells. They saw a big boat with a big anchor. The anchor was shiny and had a rope. "Let's go see the anchor!" Lily said. She was nosy and wanted to touch it. "No, Lily, we can't. It's not ours. It belongs to the boat. We have to stay here." Ben said. He was careful and followed the rules. But Lily did not listen. She ran to the anchor and pulled the rope. The rope was heavy and hard. She could not move it. She tried harder and harder. Suddenly, she heard a loud yell. It was the man from the boat. He was angry and scared. "Hey, stop that! You are making the anchor loose! You could hurt yourself or the boat! Go away!" He yelled. Lily was scared too. She let go of the rope and ran back to Ben. She was sorry and sad. "I'm sorry, Ben. I was wrong. I should have listened to you. The anchor was not a toy. It was dangerous." She said. "It's okay, Lily. I'm glad you are safe. The man was just worried. He did not mean to yell. But next time, please be careful and stay with me." Ben said. He hugged Lily and they went back to their sandcastle. They played and had fun. They learned to respect the boat and the anchor. They were good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big palace. In this palace, there lived a kind king and queen. They had a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play and have fun. One day, Lily went out to play in the mild sun. She saw a puzzle on the ground. It had many pieces. Lily wanted to complete the puzzle. So, she started to put the pieces together. She worked hard and soon, the puzzle was complete. It was a picture of the palace where she lived. Lily was happy and proud. She ran to show her mom and dad. They smiled and hugged her. They all had a great day together.

One day, a big cat named Tom found an enormous pan. Tom was very hungry, so he thought he could use the pan to cook some food. He tried to lift the pan, but it was too heavy. He needed help. Tom went to his friend, a small dog named Max. Tom said, "Max, I found a big pan, but I can't lift it. Can you help me?" Max was excited and wanted to help his friend. They went to the enormous pan together. Tom and Max tried to lift the pan together. They pushed and pulled, and the pan moved a little. They were happy and cooked some food in the pan. After they ate, they mentioned how much fun they had cooking and playing with the enormous pan.
