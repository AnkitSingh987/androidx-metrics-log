Category: Beginner
Title: The Graceful Bird Was Flying and the Sun

One day, a graceful bird was flying in the sky. The bird was very happy. The sun was hot, but the bird did not complain. The bird saw a little boy on the ground. The boy was sad. The bird wanted to help the boy. So, the bird flew down to the boy. The bird said, "Why are you sad?" The boy said, "It is hot, and I am tired." The bird said, "Do not complain. Let's play, and you will feel better." The boy and the bird played, and they both were happy.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the yard. The cat saw a weird bug and said, "Look, dog! A weird bug!" The dog looked at the bug and said, "Let's chase it!" They ran after the bug, but it was very fast. It went zoom all around the yard. The cat and dog kept chasing the bug. They were having a lot of fun. Suddenly, the bug flew up into a tree. The cat and dog looked at each other and said, "How can we get the bug now?" The cat had an idea. "I can climb the tree and freeze the bug!" The dog agreed, and the cat started to climb. As the cat was climbing, the dog saw something unexpected. The weird bug had a baby bug! The baby bug was scared and crying. The dog said, "Stop, cat! The bug has a baby!" The cat stopped climbing and looked at the baby bug. They both felt bad for scaring the bugs. The cat and dog decided to leave the bugs alone. They learned that it's important to be kind to all creatures, big and small. From that day on, the cat and dog promised to be more careful when playing in the yard.

Sally and her mom went for a walk. They put on their hats and Sally was so excited to march. Mom: "Hurry up Sally, let's march!" Sally quickly grabbed her hat and marched to the park. Her mom followed behind her, with a modest walk. When they arrived, they began looking around. Sally found a big tree that had the most beautiful leaves and began to march around it. Her mom smiled. Mom: "You sure love to march around, don't you Sally?" Sally nodded her head and smiled. She was so proud to march her way around the big tree.

Once upon a time, there was a man and a girl who wanted to marry each other. They were so excited! The man and girl held hands and ran to the swing. They swung together and it made them so happy. After a while, the man got down from the swing and got on one knee. He asked the girl to marry him! The girl was so excited that she said yes. Everyone was happy and excited for them. But then something embarrassing happened… the man put the ring on the wrong hand! Everyone laughed, but the man and the girl were still happy and didn't mind. And that was how the man and the girl got married. They were so happy and even more in love!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy named Mr. Bear. Mr. Bear had big arms that made Tim feel safe. Tim took Mr. Bear everywhere he went. One day, Tim and Mr. Bear went to the park to play. At the park, Tim saw a big slide. He wanted to go down the slide, but he was scared. Mr. Bear's big arms reminded Tim that he was safe. So, Tim held Mr. Bear tight and went down the slide. It was so much fun! Later, Tim and Mr. Bear played hide and seek. Tim hid behind a tree, and Mr. Bear was looking for him. Tim remembered how Mr. Bear's arms made him feel safe when he was scared. He found Mr. Bear and gave him a big hug. From that day on, Tim and Mr. Bear always had fun and felt safe together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom loved to play with his toy motorcycle. He would ride it all around the house, making loud noises. Tom's big sister, Sue, did not like the noise. She was tired after a long day at school. One day, Sue said to Tom, "I am so tired. Please stop playing with your motorcycle." Tom did not want to stop. He loved his motorcycle. So, he teased Sue by making louder noises with his motorcycle. Sue got very mad at Tom. She took his motorcycle away and hid it. Tom cried and cried. He wanted his motorcycle back. But Sue would not give it back to him. Tom was sad and Sue was still tired. They both went to bed with a bad ending to their day.
