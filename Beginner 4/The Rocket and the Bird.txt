Category: Beginner
Title: The Rocket and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little rocket. This rocket was very glad. It liked to fly high in the sky. The rocket had a friend. The friend was a bird. The bird and the rocket played together every day. One day, the bird wanted to race. The rocket said yes. They raced from one tree to another. The bird was fast, but the rocket was faster. The bird was not sad. It was happy to have a friend who could win. But one day, the bird and the rocket did not agree. The bird wanted to play near the ground. The rocket wanted to play high in the sky. They did not know what to do. Then, the bird had an idea. They could take turns. One day, they would play near the ground. The next day, they would play high in the sky. The rocket liked the idea. They were both glad and played happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a shiny diamond in her yard. She was so happy! Mia wanted to show her new diamond to her best friend, Tom. Mia went to Tom's house and said, "Tom, look at my shiny diamond! Let's play with it!" Tom laughed and said, "That's a silly idea, but I'll join you." They played with the diamond all day, pretending it was a magic stone. They made up a silly game where they had to make funny faces to get the diamond to work. They laughed a lot and had so much fun. Mia's mom called her for dinner, so she said goodbye to Tom. "Thanks for playing with my shiny diamond, Tom. You're a great friend!" Tom smiled and said, "You're welcome, Mia! I had a lot of fun." Mia went home, feeling very happy. She knew she had a special friend in Tom, and they had a great day playing with her shiny diamond.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a stubborn toy cat. The cat would not listen to Tim. Tim would ask it to sit, but the cat would not sit. Tim would ask it to jump, but the cat would not jump. One day, Tim and the cat were playing in the room. Tim saw a big frame on the wall. He said to the cat, "Let's play a game. Try to jump and touch the frame." The cat shook its head and said, "No, I don't want to." Tim got sad, but he still wanted to play the game. Tim tried to jump and touch the frame by himself. He jumped and jumped, but he could not reach it. He missed every time. Suddenly, the frame fell on the floor and broke. Tim's mom came into the room and saw the broken frame. She was not happy. Tim got in trouble, and he had to go to bed early. The stubborn cat just watched and did not help Tim.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim had a notebook. He loved to draw in it every day. One day, he wanted to hide his notebook so no one could see his drawings. It was not easy to find a good hiding spot. Tim put his notebook under his bed. But then, his dog found it and gave it to his mom. Tim's mom saw the drawings and smiled. She put the notebook back under the bed. Tim thought he had hidden it well, but he did not know his mom had seen it. The next day, Tim found a new drawing in his notebook. It was a drawing of him and his mom holding hands. Tim was surprised! He asked his mom if she drew it. She smiled and said, "Yes, I found your notebook and wanted to surprise you." Tim was happy that his mom saw his drawings and they drew together from that day on.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He felt lonely because he had no friends. As he walked, he looked at the pretty landscape. There were big trees, green grass, and colorful flowers. "Hi!" said a voice. Tim looked around and saw a talking tree. "What's your name?" asked the tree. "I'm Tim," the little boy replied. "Do you want to be friends?" the tree asked. Tim was happy and said, "Yes!" Tim and the tree were playing and having fun. Suddenly, the tree grew legs and arms! It was not a tree, but a friendly giant! The giant picked up Tim, and they went on many fun adventures together. Tim was not lonely anymore, and they were the best of friends.
