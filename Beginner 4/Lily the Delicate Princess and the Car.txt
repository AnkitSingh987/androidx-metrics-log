Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Delicate Princess and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a delicate princess named Lily. She lived in a big castle with her mom and dad. Princess Lily loved to play with her toy cars. Her favorite car was a red one that could speed very fast. One sunny day, Princess Lily was playing in the garden with her red car. She made the car speed on the grass. Her friend, Tom the cat, came to play with her. Tom said, "Wow, your car is so fast! Can I try?" Princess Lily smiled and said, "Yes, you can play with my car." Tom the cat and Princess Lily took turns making the red car speed on the grass. They laughed and played together all day long. When the sun went down, they went inside the big castle to have dinner with the king and queen. At the dinner table, Princess Lily told her mom and dad about her fun day with Tom the cat. The king and queen were happy that their delicate princess had a great day. They all ate yummy food and talked about their day. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Mia was very stubborn. She did not want to eat her lunch. Her mom said, "Mia, you must eat or your stomach will be sad." Mia said, "No, I don't want to eat!" She went to play with her toys. But soon, Mia felt her stomach hurt. She remembered her mom telling her to eat her lunch. Mia went back to her mom and said, "Mom, my stomach hurts." Her mom said, "That's because you didn't eat your lunch. Eat it now and your stomach will feel better." Mia ate her lunch and her stomach felt better. She learned that she should listen to her mom and not be stubborn. From that day on, Mia always ate her lunch and her stomach was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a very foolish dog. He loved to chase his tail in a circle. One day, Spot saw a big red ball. The ball was rolling very fast. Spot wanted to play with the ball. "Ball, wait for me!" Spot said. But the ball did not stop. It kept rolling and rolling. Spot chased the ball in a circle. He was having so much fun! But then, Spot felt sad. He could not catch the ball. Spot started to regret chasing the ball. He missed playing with his friends. Spot said, "I want to go back and play with my friends." So, Spot left the ball and went back to his friends. They were all happy to see him. Spot learned that it's more fun to play with friends than chasing a ball alone.

Once upon a time, there was a rich man named Bob. He had a big house and many toys. Bob loved to read magazines with big, colorful pictures. One day, he found a picture of a swing in a magazine. He thought, "I want a swing in my yard!" Bob went to the store and bought a swing. He put it in his yard under a big tree. Bob loved to swing high in the air. He felt like he could touch the sky. The wind blew his hair as he went up and down. One sunny day, a little girl named Sue walked by Bob's house. She saw the swing and asked, "Can I play on your swing?" Bob said, "Yes, you can!" Sue and Bob took turns swinging. They laughed and had fun. From that day on, they became best friends and always played together on the swing.

Once upon a time, there was a unique copper coin. This coin was shiny and round. It was different from all the other coins. The coin wanted to go on an adventure. One day, the coin rolled out of the house. It went down the street and saw many things. It saw a big dog, a red ball, and a tall tree. The coin was happy to go on this adventure. At the end of the day, the coin went back home. The unique copper coin had a fun time. It told all the other coins about the adventure. Now, all the coins wanted to go on an adventure too.
