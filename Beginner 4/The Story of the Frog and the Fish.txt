Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Frog and the Fish

One day, a little crane was blinking his eyes in the sun. He was an adorable crane with big, long legs. He liked to walk by the river and look for fish to eat. "Hi, adorable crane!" said a little frog. "Do you want to play with me?" The crane blinked his eyes and said, "Yes, I would like that!" So, the crane and the frog played by the river, jumping and splashing in the water. But then, something unexpected happened. The little crane blinked and saw a big fish in the water. He wanted to eat the fish, but he did not want to scare his new friend, the frog. He blinked again and had an idea. The crane pretended to play a game, but really, he was trying to catch the fish. The frog did not know and kept playing. At the end of the day, the crane caught the fish, and the frog was still his friend. The adorable crane and the frog played together by the river every day after that.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a toy skull that was orange. It was her favorite toy. She played with it every day. She called her toy Mr. Orange Skull. One sunny day, Lily and Mr. Orange Skull went to the park. They played on the swings and the slide. Lily was having so much fun! But then, she saw a boy who was sad. He was sitting alone and crying. Lily wanted to help him. Lily went to the boy with Mr. Orange Skull. She asked him why he was sad. The boy said he lost his toy. Lily thought for a moment. She knew she could make the boy happy if she shared her toy. So, Lily gave the boy Mr. Orange Skull to play with. The boy was so happy, he shouted, "Yay!" Lily and the boy played together with Mr. Orange Skull all day. They laughed and had fun. When it was time to go home, the boy gave Mr. Orange Skull back to Lily. He said, "Thank you for sharing your toy with me." Lily learned that sharing can make others happy. She went home with a big smile on her face, knowing that she made a new friend by sharing Mr. Orange Skull. And that is the story of how Lily and Mr. Orange Skull learned the value of sharing.

One day, a graceful cat named Kitty and a dog named Max were playing in the yard. They were best friends. They liked to play with a ball and run around. Kitty said to Max, "I am hungry. Let's go inside and ask for food." Max agreed, and they went to the kitchen. Max's mom was there. Max said, "Mom, can you give us a dish of food?" Max's mom gave them a dish of food. But they fought over who would eat first. They pushed and pulled the dish. The dish fell on the floor and broke. The food was all over the floor. Max's mom was very sad. She said, "You both made a big mess. Now, there is no food for you." Kitty and Max felt bad. They had to go to bed hungry that night.

Once upon a time, in a distant land, there was a bird named Bobo. Bobo loved to soar high in the sky. One day, while Bobo was flying, he saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad because her ball was stuck in a tree. Bobo flew down and said, "Lily, why are you sad?" Lily looked at Bobo and said, "My ball is stuck in the tree. I can't get it down." Bobo wanted to help Lily, so he flew up to the tree to get the ball. But when Bobo tried to grab the ball, he got a little scratch on his skin. It hurt, but Bobo was brave. He tried again and got the ball. He flew down and gave the ball to Lily. Lily was so happy and said, "Thank you, Bobo!" Bobo smiled and said, "You're welcome, Lily!" From that day on, Bobo and Lily were the best of friends. They played together every day, and Bobo always made sure to soar high in the sky to make Lily laugh.
