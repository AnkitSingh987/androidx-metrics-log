Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and the Tree

Anna did not like broccoli. She thought it was boring and green and yucky. She did not want to eat it for dinner. She pushed it away with her fork and made a face. "Anna, you have to eat your broccoli. It is good for you. It makes you strong and healthy," her mom said. Anna shook her head. She did not want to be strong and healthy. She wanted to be happy and have fun. She wanted to eat cake and ice cream and candy. "Please, mom, can I have something else? I don't like broccoli. It is boring," Anna said. "No, Anna, you have to eat what is on your plate. Broccoli is not boring. It is tasty and crunchy and colorful. You can dip it in cheese or ketchup or butter. You can make a game out of it. You can pretend it is a tree and you are a giant. You can talk to it and make it talk back. You can have fun with broccoli," her mom said. Anna looked at her broccoli. She picked up a piece and held it in front of her face. She made a funny voice and said, "Hello, I am broccoli. I am your friend. I want to go in your mouth and make you happy. Please eat me." Anna giggled. She thought it was silly to talk to broccoli. She put it in her mouth and chewed. It was not so bad. It was a little bit tasty and crunchy and colorful. She dipped it in cheese and ketchup and butter. She pretended it was a tree and she was a giant. She talked to it and made it talk back. She had fun with broccoli. She ate all her broccoli and smiled. She was proud of herself. She was strong and healthy and happy. She looked at her mom and said, "Thank you, mom, for broccoli. It is not boring. It is fun."

One sunny day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw an interesting mill. The mill was big and had a wheel. Tim saw the wheel move round and round. He wanted to see more. Tim went inside the mill. He saw a man working there. The man said, "Hello, I make flour here. Do you want to see how I do it?" Tim said, "Yes, please!" The man showed Tim how the wheel moved and made flour. Tim thought the mill was fun. He said, "Thank you for showing me the mill!" The man said, "You're welcome, come back anytime!" Tim went home and told his mom about the interesting mill. His mom said, "That sounds like a fun day!"

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. Lily had a friend named Tom. Tom was a reliable friend. They liked to play together. One day, they saw a big bag in the park. They did not know what was in the bag. Lily and Tom opened the bag. They found rice inside. They were happy and wanted to cook the rice. They went to Lily's house to cook it. While they cooked, they smiled and laughed. When they finished cooking, they saw the rice move. They were surprised! The rice turned into a funny toy. It could dance and sing. Lily and Tom frowned at first, but then they laughed. They played with the toy all day long. The unexpected toy made their day even more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved ice-cream very much. One sunny day, she went to the park with her mom. In the park, she saw a dirty dog. The dog was very sad. Mia asked her mom, "Can we help the dirty dog?" Her mom said, "Yes, let's give him a bath." They took the dog to the water and washed him. The dog was very happy and stopped trembling. After the bath, Mia and her mom got ice-cream. They shared it with the dog. The dog licked the ice-cream and wagged his tail. From that day on, Mia, her mom, and the dog became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish. The little fish loved to swim. He swam in a hidden stream near his home. The stream was small and pretty. The little fish was very happy. One day, the little fish saw a big fish. The big fish wanted to swim too. The little fish was scared. The big fish was not nice. The little fish tried to swim away. He swam very fast. But the big fish was faster. The big fish caught the little fish. The little fish was very sad. The big fish did not let the little fish go. The little fish could not swim in the hidden stream anymore.
