Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Marbles

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big bag of marbles. Tim loved to play with his marbles. They were all different colors: red, blue, green, and yellow. One day, it was very hot outside. Tim wanted to play with his marbles, but his mom said, "No, Tim, it's too hot to play outside. Let's play inside instead." Tim was sad, but he knew his mom was right. So, Tim and his mom played with the marbles inside. They rolled them on the floor and tried to hit each other's marbles. Tim had so much fun playing with his mom. They laughed and played until it was time for bed. Tim was happy that his mom let him play with his marbles, even if it was too hot outside.

---

One day, a little girl named Sue was playing with her marbles. She had a big, dark blue marble that she loved the most. She played with it all the time. But today, she lost her dark blue marble. Sue was very sad. Sue asked her friend Tom, "Can you help me search for my dark blue marble?" Tom said, "Yes, I will help you!" They searched and searched, but they did not find the marble. They looked under the bed, behind the sofa, and in the garden. But the dark blue marble was not there. After a long time, they stopped searching. Sue was very sad because they did not find her dark blue marble. Tom said, "I am sorry we did not find it." Sue went home and cried. She never found her dark blue marble again.

Once upon a time there was a friendly rabbit named Max. He had a very special tire. With it, he could run really fast, and that made him really happy. One day, a sneaky fox named Mike came to visit him. When he noticed the tire, he wanted it for himself. So he said to Max, “Can I steal your tire? Please!” Max was a very kind rabbit, so he said, “No, you can’t steal it. You can come run with me if you want, but I won’t let you take my tire.” Mike was disappointed, but he still said goodbye in a friendly way. He ran away, leaving Max with his tire. Max was happy that he got to keep his favorite tire!

One day, a little cat named Paws looked up at the sky. Paws saw a big, light cloud. The cloud was soft and fluffy. Paws wanted to touch the cloud, but it was too high. Paws had an idea. "I will stretch my legs and jump to touch the cloud," said Paws. So, Paws stretched her legs and jumped high. But the cloud was still too high. Paws saw a tall tree. "I will climb the tree and touch the cloud," said Paws. Paws climbed the tree and stretched her legs again. This time, Paws touched the light cloud. Paws was so happy!

Once upon a time, there was a big, powerful dog named Max. Max had a little friend named Tim. Tim was a small bird. They lived in a pretty house with a nice family. Max and Tim liked to play all day. One day, Max and Tim found a phone. Max wanted to play with the phone, but Tim did not. They disagree. Max said, "Let's play with the phone!" Tim said, "No, let's play with our toys." Max and Tim did not know what to do. Then, their friend, a cat named Lucy, came to help. Lucy said, "Let's take turns. Max, you can play with the phone first. Then, Tim can play with his toys." Max and Tim agreed. They played together and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a doll that she loved very much. One day, Lucy's doll disappeared. She looked everywhere for her doll, but it was hidden and she could not find it. Lucy asked her brother, Tom, "Have you seen my doll?" Tom shook his head and said, "No, I have not seen your doll. Let's look for it together." So, Lucy and Tom searched their house for the hidden doll. Finally, they found the doll under Lucy's bed. Lucy was so happy to see her doll again. She hugged Tom and said, "Thank you for helping me find my doll!" From that day on, Lucy made sure to keep her doll safe and never let it disappear again.
