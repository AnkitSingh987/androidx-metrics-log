Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Blouse

Lily liked to play dress-up with her mom's clothes. She found a pretty blouse in the closet that was pink and had flowers on it. She wanted to wear it and pretend she was a princess. She tried to put the blouse over her head, but it was too big and got stuck. She could not see anything and felt scared. She started to cry and call for her mom. "Mommy, help me! I can't get out!" she shouted. Her mom heard her and came to the closet. She saw Lily with the blouse on her head and smiled. "Don't worry, sweetie, I'm here. Let me help you," she said. She gently pulled the blouse off Lily's head and hugged her. "Are you okay, Lily? That blouse is too big for you. It's mine, not yours," she said. Lily sniffed and nodded. She felt better now that she could see and breathe. "I'm sorry, Mommy. I wanted to be a princess. The blouse is so pretty," she said. Her mom kissed her and smiled. "You are a princess, Lily. You don't need a blouse to be one. You are beautiful and smart and kind. That's what makes you a princess," she said. She gave Lily a comfortable dress that was her size and helped her put it on. "There, now you look like a princess. Do you want to play with me?" she asked. Lily smiled and nodded. She hugged her mom and said, "Yes, please. I love you, Mommy. You are the best." They went to the living room and played dress-up together. Lily felt happy and loved. She forgot about the blouse and had fun with her mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a soft toy bunny. She loved her bunny very much. They played together every day. One day, Sue saw a big piece of cake on the table. She wanted to eat it. Sue's mom said, "No, you cannot eat the cake now. You must eat dinner first." But Sue did not listen. When her mom was not looking, Sue took the big piece of cake and ate it all up. Her mom found out and was very sad. She said, "Sue, you did not listen to me. Now, I must punish you." Sue's mom took away her soft bunny for one day. Sue cried and said sorry. The next day, Sue got her bunny back. She promised to always listen to her mom.

One day, a boy named Tim found a sock. It was a scary sock with a big face on it. Tim wanted to play with the sock, so he put it on his hand. He made the sock talk and dance. Tim was having fun with the scary sock. Tim saw a big stick and had an idea. He wanted to make the sock balance on the stick. Tim put the sock on the stick and tried to make it balance. The sock wobbled and wobbled, but it did not fall. Tim was happy that the sock could balance on the stick. Then, something unexpected happened. The scary sock came to life! It jumped off the stick and started to dance all by itself. Tim was surprised and scared at first, but then he saw that the sock was friendly. They played and danced together all day long. And that's how Tim made a new friend with the scary sock.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big melon in his yard. Tim liked to play with the melon every day. He would roll it, sit on it, and even try to draw on it. Tim loved his melon very much. One day, Tim found his melon broken. He was very sad. He did not know who broke his melon. Tim looked around and saw his dog, Spot. Spot had melon on his face. Tim recognized that Spot had broken his melon. Tim was not mad at Spot. He knew that Spot did not mean to break the melon. Tim and Spot played with the broken melon together. They had fun even though it was broken. Tim learned that sometimes, even broken things can still be fun.
