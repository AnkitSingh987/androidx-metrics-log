Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and the Door

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tom went for a walk. He liked to see all the things around him. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Tom was very happy. On his walk, Tom saw something unusual. It was a door with a shiny handle. He had never seen a door like this before. Tom wanted to know what was behind the door. So, he reached out and turned the handle. When the door opened, Tom saw a big room full of toys. There were balls, dolls, and cars. Tom played with the toys and had lots of fun. He knew that he had found a very special place. After playing, Tom closed the door and went home. He couldn't wait to tell his friends about the unusual door with the shiny handle.

Once upon a time, there was a ball named Mark. Mark was a happy ball who loved to bounce all day long. He would bounce in the park, bounce in the house, and even bounce in the rain. Mark's favorite thing to do was to bounce as high as he could. One day, Mark was not feeling very strong. He felt weak and could not bounce as high as he used to. He tried and tried, but he just could not bounce high. Mark was sad and did not know what to do. Then, Mark's friend, a big ball, came to help him. The big ball told Mark to eat some food and drink some water to feel better. Mark did as his friend said, and soon, he started to feel strong again. Mark was so happy that he could bounce high once more, and he thanked his friend for helping him. From that day on, Mark and the big ball bounced together, having fun and playing in the sun.

Once there was a little boy, who went outside to play. He loved to look at the clouds and make shapes with them. On that day, there were a lot of white clouds, and the boy was so happy he could fly. He reached up and tried to grasp a cloud. Suddenly, he felt a chill and shivered. "Are you cold?" asked his mother. The little boy shook his head. "I think the cloud made me shiver," he said. "It felt like it was spinning around and making me dizzy!" His mother smiled and helped him back inside. "You must have felt the sun," she said. "It can make everything seem like it's spinning and make you feel a little dizzy. But it's nothing to worry about!" The little boy smiled and nodded. Inside, he was warm and felt better. And still, he wondered what it was like to fly up to those clouds and make shapes with them.

One day, Tim went to the garage. He wanted to restore his old toy car. It was a weird car. It had big eyes and a funny mouth. Tim thought it was the best car ever. In the garage, Tim found his dad. "Dad, can you help me fix my car?" Tim asked. "Of course," his dad said. They worked together to make the car look new again. The car's eyes were shiny, and its mouth was smiling. When they were done, Tim showed his mom. "Look, Mom! My car is like new!" he said. His mom smiled and said, "Great job, Tim! Your weird car is so happy now." Tim felt proud and played with his restored car all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim in the big pond. One sunny day, Fin saw a mosquito flying high above the water. "Hello, mosquito!" said Fin. "Do you want to swim with me?" The mosquito replied, "I cannot swim, but I can fly high in the sky." Fin wished he could fly high too, but he knew he could only swim. So, Fin and the mosquito played a fun game. The mosquito would fly high and drop little leaves for Fin to catch as they floated on the water. Fin would swim fast to catch the leaves. They laughed and played all day. In the end, Fin and the mosquito became the best of friends, even if one could only swim and the other could only fly high.
