Category: Beginner
Title: The Yellow Sun and the Hill

Once upon a time, there was a yellow sun. The sun loved to shine on a big hill. On the hill, there was a box of pastel colors. The colors liked to play with each other, but they were not friends yet. One day, a big wind came and blew the pastels all over the hill. The yellow sun saw this and wanted to help. The sun thought, "I will unite the pastels and make them friends." The sun shined very bright and warm on the hill. The pastels started to melt together. They mixed and made new colors. The pastels were happy and became friends. The yellow sun had done something unexpected, and now the pastels were united.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Amy. She lived in a house with her mom and dad. Amy liked to help her mom and dad with things. One day, Amy's mom asked her to attach a note to the dishwasher. The note said, "The dishwasher is fine now." Amy put the note on the dishwasher with a magnet. She was happy to help her mom. Later, her dad came home and saw the note. He thought the dishwasher was fine to use, so he put some dirty dishes in it and turned it on. But then, something unexpected happened! The dishwasher started to make a lot of noise and water came out of it. Amy's dad was confused because the note said it was fine. It turns out, Amy's mom meant that the dishwasher was fine, but they needed to call someone to fix it first. The note was not clear, and everyone laughed at the funny mistake. From that day on, they made sure to write clearer notes and always check before using the dishwasher.

Once upon a time, there was a blue bird. The bird lived in a big tree. One day, the blue bird saw a tube on the ground. The tube was long and thin. The bird was happy to find the tube. The blue bird picked up the tube with its beak. It flew back to the tree. The bird wanted to show the tube to its friends. The friends were excited to see the tube. They thought it was special. Then, a big truck arrived near the tree. The truck was very noisy. The blue bird and its friends were scared. But the truck left soon. The birds were happy again. They played with the tube all day long.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Lily and her mom. They had a big dish that they loved. One day, the dish broke into pieces. Lily was very sad. Her mom said, "Don't worry, we will unite the dish." Lily and her mom tried to put the dish back together. They asked their dog, Spot, to help. Spot was an obedient dog, so he tried to help them. But Spot didn't know how to unite the dish. He just barked and wagged his tail. Finally, Lily's mom said, "I have an idea! Let's ask our neighbor, Mr. Brown, to help us." They went to Mr. Brown's house and asked for his help. Mr. Brown said, "I will try my best to unite the dish for you." But even Mr. Brown couldn't unite the dish. In the end, the dish stayed broken and everyone was sad.

Once upon a time, there was a clever dog named Buddy. Buddy liked to play outside with his rake. One sunny day, Buddy was raking the leaves in his yard. He was very happy. As Buddy raked the leaves, he started to sneeze. "Achoo! Achoo!" Buddy sneezed and sneezed. The leaves flew up into the air and fell back down. Buddy did not know why he was sneezing so much. Then, something unexpected happened. A little cat jumped out from the pile of leaves! The cat had been hiding there, making Buddy sneeze. Buddy and the cat looked at each other and laughed. They became good friends and played together in the yard all day long.

Ben and Mia were playing in the park. They liked to look for rocks and sticks and leaves. They had a big box where they kept their treasures. "Look, Mia, I found a red rock!" Ben said, holding up a shiny stone. "It is so pretty and smooth. Can I put it in our box?" "Wow, Ben, that is a nice rock!" Mia said. "Yes, you can put it in our box. But we have to share it, okay?" "Okay, Mia, we can share it. It is our rock now." Ben said, smiling. He put the rock in the box and closed the lid. They were about to go and look for more rocks, when they heard a voice. "Hey, kids, what are you doing?" It was Mr. Lee, the park ranger. He was wearing a green hat and a brown coat. He was friendly and kind. "Hello, Mr. Lee!" Ben and Mia said. "We are playing with rocks. We have a box of rocks. Do you want to see?" "Sure, I would love to see your rocks." Mr. Lee said. He walked over to their box and opened the lid. He saw many rocks of different colors and shapes. He picked up the red rock that Ben had found. "This is a very special rock, kids." He said. "Do you know what it is?" Ben and Mia shook their heads. They did not know. "This is a ruby." Mr. Lee said. "It is a very rare and valuable rock. It is worth a lot of money. Where did you find it?" Ben and Mia looked at each other. They did not want to tell Mr. Lee where they found the rock. They liked the rock and wanted to keep it. "We found it in the park." Ben said. "Yes, in the park." Mia said. Mr. Lee looked at them with a curious smile. He did not believe them. He thought they were hiding something. "Well, kids, you are very lucky to find such a rock." He said. "But you should be careful. Some people might try to take it from you. You should not mention it to anyone, okay?" "Okay, Mr. Lee, we will not mention it." Ben and Mia said. "Good. Now, have fun with your rocks, but don't stay too long. It is getting dark soon." Mr. Lee said. He put the ruby back in the box and closed the lid. He waved goodbye and walked away. Ben and Mia looked at each other. They felt happy and scared at the same time. They had a secret rock. They did not know what to do with it. They decided to hide it under their bed and never tell anyone. They hoped no one would find it. They hoped it would not bring them trouble. They hoped it would make them happy.
