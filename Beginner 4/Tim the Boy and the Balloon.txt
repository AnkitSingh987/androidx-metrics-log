Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Balloon

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a big, red balloon tied to a bench. It was a hot day, so Tim was very happy to see the balloon. He asked his mom if he could have it. She said, "Yes, you can have it." Tim took the balloon and started to play with it. He pointed at the sky and said, "Look, Mom! The balloon can go up, up, up!" But suddenly, the balloon popped! Tim was very sad and didn't know what to do. Just then, a nice man came up to Tim and his mom. He had a big bag of balloons. He gave Tim a new red balloon and said, "Don't worry, little boy. You can have this one." Tim was very happy and said, "Thank you!" The man smiled and walked away. Tim and his mom went home with the new balloon, and Tim played with it all day long.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play with toys and books. One day, Anna has a new doll. She loves her doll very much. She values her doll more than anything. Ben comes to visit Anna. He sees her doll and wants to play with it. He asks Anna, "Can I play with your doll? She is very pretty." Anna says, "No, Ben. This is my doll. You can play with other toys, but not with my doll. She is mine." Ben feels sad and angry. He thinks Anna is not a good friend. He says, "You are not nice, Anna. You are selfish. You do not share. I do not want to play with you anymore." Anna feels hurt and confused. She thinks Ben is not a good friend. She says, "You are not nice, Ben. You are greedy. You do not respect. I do not want to play with you anymore." They both turn away and play alone. They do not talk to each other. They do not have fun. After a while, they both feel sorry. They miss each other. They want to be friends again. They look at each other and say, "I am sorry, Anna/Ben. I was not honest. I was jealous of your doll. She is very pretty. You can play with her if you want." They both smile and hug. They share the doll and other toys. They play together and have fun. They are honest and value their friendship.

Once upon a time, there was a little black cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to help her friends. One day, she went for a walk and saw her friend, Birdie, in a tree. Birdie said, "Kitty, can you help me? My nest is broken." Kitty looked up and saw the broken nest. She wanted to be successful in helping her friend. Kitty climbed the tree to help fix Birdie's nest. She used her soft paws to put the sticks back together. Birdie was very happy and said, "Thank you, Kitty! You are a great friend." Together, they fixed the nest and it was as good as new. Kitty felt proud and successful for helping her friend. They played together all day, knowing they could always count on each other for help.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a cat named Tom and a mouse named Jerry. Jerry was a smart mouse who liked to play tricks on Tom. Tom was a big cat who wanted to catch Jerry but could not resist chasing him all day. One day, Tom made a trap to catch Jerry. He put a big piece of cheese in a box and waited for Jerry to come. Jerry saw the cheese and knew it was a trap. He said to Tom, "I am not silly. I see your trap. I will not go in." Tom was angry and tried to make the trap better. He found a filthy old sock and put it over the box. He thought Jerry would not see the trap now. But Jerry was still very smart. He said, "I can smell the sock. I know the trap is there. I will not go in." In the end, Tom and Jerry became friends. They played together and had fun. Jerry taught Tom how to be smart, and Tom taught Jerry how to be strong. They lived happily ever after.
