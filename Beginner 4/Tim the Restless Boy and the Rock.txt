Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Restless Boy and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a restless little boy named Tim. He loved to rock in his chair all day long. One day, Tim saw a calendar on the wall. It showed a picture of a big, happy family. Tim asked his mom, "Why are they so happy?" His mom said, "They are happy because they help each other and care for each other. That is what makes a family strong and happy." Tim thought about it and decided to help his mom more. He stopped rocking in his chair all day and started to help with chores. He helped set the table, clean up toys, and even helped his little sister learn to walk. Soon, their family was just as happy as the one in the calendar. The moral of the story is that helping and caring for each other brings happiness and love to a family.

Once upon a time, a leopard was living in the forest. The leopard was feeling hot and dry. He wanted to find some shade and some water. As he was walking, he noticed something strange in the grass. It was a small yellow fruit. It was very tempting, so the leopard decided to try it. But it wasn't sweet and juicy like he expected. Instead, it was very sour and made him feel funny. The leopard knew this fruit had gone bad. The leopard said to himself, “This fruit is bad and starting to rot. Eating it will make me sick. I should stay away from bad food!” The leopard quickly left the fruit and continued his search for shade and water. He was glad he had chosen not to eat the bad fruit. The moral of the story is simple: if something looks bad, it's probably not good for you. It's important to always make wise decisions.

Lily and Ben were going to the park with Mom. They put on their coats and hats and gloves. Mom helped them zip their coats. "Are you ready?" Mom asked. "Yes!" Lily and Ben said. They liked the park. They liked the swings and the slide and the sandbox. They walked to the park. Lily held Mom's hand. Ben walked next to them. He saw a big dog on the sidewalk. The dog was black and furry and had a long tail. The dog barked at them. Ben was scared of the dog. He felt anxious. He hid behind Mom's shoulder. He did not want the dog to bite him. "It's okay, Ben," Mom said. "The dog is just saying hello. He won't hurt you. He has a leash and an owner. See?" Mom pointed to the man who held the dog's leash. Ben peeked from behind Mom's shoulder. He saw the man. The man smiled at them. "Don't worry, he's friendly," the man said. "Do you want to pet him?" Lily nodded. She liked animals. She let go of Mom's hand and walked to the dog. She reached out her hand and touched the dog's fur. The dog licked her hand. Lily giggled. Ben saw that Lily was not afraid. He saw that the dog was nice. He wanted to be brave. He took a deep breath and walked to the dog. He touched the dog's ear. The dog wagged his tail. Ben smiled. "Good job, Ben," Mom said. "You are very brave. Do you like the dog?" "Yes," Ben said. "He's soft and warm and funny." The man and the dog said goodbye and walked away. Lily and Ben waved at them. Then they ran to the park. They had fun on the swings and the slide and the sandbox. They forgot about being anxious. They were happy.

One day, a grumpy cat named Tom went to the park. He saw a big box with a film inside. The film was about fun cats playing. Tom wanted to watch the film, but he didn't know how. Tom asked his friend, a happy dog named Max, for help. Max said, "Let's include all our friends and watch the film together!" So, they went to find their friends, a bird and a squirrel. They all came back to the park to watch the film. But when they opened the box, something unexpected happened. There was no film inside! Instead, there were yummy treats for all of them. They were surprised but very happy. They all shared the treats and played together in the park. Tom was not grumpy anymore.
