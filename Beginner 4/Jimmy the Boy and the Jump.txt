Category: Beginner
Title: Jimmy the Boy and the Jump

Once, there was a boy named Jimmy. He was only three-years-old and he had a big dream. He wanted to jump over a hedge. He talked about it to his mum and she said, "That's a big dream!" So Jimmy kept dreaming about it and one day, he went outside the house. He ran over to the hedge and he tried to jump over it. But, he was only three and it was too high! He started to feel a bit ashamed. Suddenly, his mum was there. She said, "It's OK, Jimmy. You can still keep trying and one day, you will jump over this hedge. Just keep on dreaming." Jimmy smiled and hugged his mum. He was very happy. He knew that one day, he would be able to jump over the hedge and follow his big dream.

One day, a bee named Buzz was flying near his hive. He saw a hidden pond. Buzz thought, "I want to dive in the water like a fish!" So, he flew down and got ready to dive. Just before Buzz could dive, a fish named Fin popped out of the water. "Wait!" said Fin. "Bees can't dive like fish. You might get hurt." Buzz was sad, but he knew Fin was right. They sat and talked for a while. As they talked, a big wind came and blew Buzz's hive into the pond! Buzz was scared. But then, Fin had an idea. "I can dive and push your hive out of the water!" Fin dove deep and pushed the hive back to land. Buzz was so happy and thanked Fin. They became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boat. The boat was persistent. It wanted to go far away. One day, the boat went on a big trip. It went over the water. The boat was happy. It went on and on. But the boat did not know when to stop. It went too far. The boat got lost. In the end, the boat could not find its way back. The boat was sad. The end.

Once upon a time, in a great big forest, there was a tall pine tree. The pine tree was friends with a little bird. The bird loved to sing and play with the pine tree. One day, the bird found some food on the ground. The bird wanted to feed its friend, the pine tree. So, the bird picked up the food and flew up to the top of the pine tree. The pine tree was very happy to get the food from the bird. The pine tree and the bird became even better friends. They played and sang every day in the great big forest. And they always took care of each other, just like good friends do.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. He saw a wallet that he really wanted. It was red and had a picture of his favorite cartoon on it. He asked his mom, "Can I have this wallet, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but don't be stupid with your money." Tim was so happy to have his new wallet. He put all his money in it. He wanted to show his friend, Sam. So, he went to Sam's house. Tim said, "Look at my new wallet! It is so cool!" Sam liked it too and said, "Wow, that's a great wallet!" But Tim did not listen to his mom. He spent all his money on candy and toys. Soon, he had no money left. Tim felt sad and knew he had been stupid with his money. He wished he had listened to his mom. Now, he had a cool wallet, but no money to put in it.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to read books. One day, Lucy went to the library with her mom. The library was big and had many books. Lucy was very happy. In the library, Lucy saw a slow turtle. The turtle was lost. Lucy wanted to help the turtle. She dared to talk to the turtle. The turtle told Lucy he wanted to find his family. Lucy had an idea. She looked at the books in the library. Lucy found a book about turtles. The book showed where the turtle's family lived. Lucy and her mom took the turtle to his family. The turtle was very happy. Lucy was happy too. She learned that the library can help in many ways.
