Title: Lucy the Girl and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to cook with her mom in the kitchen. One day, Lucy was alone and she wanted to cook by herself. She felt scared, but she wanted to try. Lucy found a bottle of glue on the table. She thought it was something to cook with. She put the glue in the pot and started to cook. The glue became hot and sticky. Lucy said, "This is not right. What did I do?" Her mom came into the kitchen and saw the mess. She said, "Oh no, Lucy! We don't cook with glue. It's not safe." Lucy felt bad and started to cry. The glue made a big mess and it was hard to clean up. The moral of the story is to always ask for help when you are scared or unsure. It's better to be safe than sorry.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They sat on a seat near the playground. Tim saw a big, red ball. He wanted to play with it. Tim asked his mom, "Can I throw the ball, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful not to throw it too far." So Tim picked up the ball and threw it. It went very high and very far. Tim was so happy! But then, something unexpected happened. The ball hit an expensive toy car that another kid was playing with. The toy car broke! Tim felt sad and said sorry to the other kid. His mom told him it was okay and they would help fix the toy car. From that day, Tim learned to be more careful when throwing things.

Once, there was a little jet. The jet was troubled. It did not believe it could fly high. The jet was sad. One day, the jet met a bird. The bird said, "I believe in you! You can fly high, too!" The jet felt happy. It tried to fly high with the bird. But, guess what? The jet did not fly high. It went very fast on the ground! The jet was not troubled anymore. It was a fast car, not a jet! The car and the bird played together and had fun.

Once upon a time, there were two boys and one of them was always kindhearted. The other boy, however, was always unkind. One day, the kind boy said to his friend, "Let's play outside." But the unkind boy just said, "No". The kind boy asked him again and again, but the unkind boy refused each time. The kind boy thought and then he had an idea. He said, "You won't play with me, so I will just lie in bed and mind". So, the kind boy got into bed and he tried to be still in his mind. He closed his eyes and tried to be comfortable on his hard bed. Just then, the unkind boy realised how bad he had been. He was very sad and he apologised to the kind boy for not playing with him. From then on, the unkind boy became kind and the two boys became good friends. The moral of the story is that kindness and thoughtfulness are always the best way to treat others and that we should always think before we act.

Once upon a time, there was a little table. The table was cool and had many friends. It liked to be in the middle of the room where everyone could see it. One day, the table was sad because it had nothing on it. A nice girl saw the table and wanted to help. She put a pretty flower on the table. The table was happy to be useful. The table and the girl became best friends. They played games and had fun. The table was always there for the girl, and the girl was always there for the table. They were happy together, and the table was cool and loved.

Lily was a gifted girl. She could make things glow with her mind. She liked to play with her toys and make them shine in the dark. She also liked to glow her clothes and make them pretty. One night, she heard a noise outside her window. She got up and looked. She saw a big man with a red suit and a white beard. He had a big bag of gifts. He was putting them under a tree. Lily was curious. She opened her window and said hello. "Hello, little girl," the man said. "What are you doing up so late?" "I saw you and your gifts," Lily said. "Who are you?" "I'm Santa Claus," the man said. "I bring gifts to good children on Christmas Eve." "Wow, that's nice," Lily said. "Can I see your gifts?" "Sure, you can," Santa said. "But you have to promise me something. You have to go back to bed and sleep. And you have to keep your gift a secret until morning. Can you do that?" Lily nodded. She was excited. Santa took out a gift from his bag and gave it to Lily. It was a big box with a bow. Lily opened it and gasped. It was a doll that looked like her. But the doll had something special. It could glow too. "Thank you, Santa," Lily said. "This is the best gift ever. I love it." "You're welcome, Lily," Santa said. "You're a very gifted girl. And you have a good heart. I'm glad you like your gift. Now, go back to bed and sleep. And remember, don't tell anyone until morning." Lily hugged her doll and said goodbye to Santa. She went back to bed and snuggled with her doll. She made her doll glow and smiled. She felt happy and warm. She closed her eyes and fell asleep. She dreamed of glowing things and Santa. She couldn't wait to show her gift to her family in the morning.
