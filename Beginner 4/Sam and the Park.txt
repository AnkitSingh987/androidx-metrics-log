Category: Beginner
Title: Sam and the Park

Once upon a time, a purple cat named Sam loved to travel. One day, Sam went to the park to have fun. At the park, Sam saw a big tree with many birds. Sam thought, "I want to play with the birds." Sam said to the birds, "Hi, birds! Can I play with you?" The birds said, "Yes, come play with us!" So Sam climbed the tree and played with the birds. They had a lot of fun together. After playing, Sam was tired. Sam said, "Bye, birds! I need to go home now." The birds said, "Bye, Sam! Come back soon!" Sam went home and took a long nap. Sam had a great day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a nation. This nation was very cheap. But the people who lived there were not happy. One day, they decided to escape their nation and fly to a new land. So they put their things in a big basket and filled it with food and presents. They put the basket onto a big balloon and they all held on tight so they wouldn't fall out. The balloon rose up high into the sky. They wanted to go to a place where they could be happy and live in peace. The balloon sailed through the clouds until they arrived at a new, beautiful place. They were overjoyed! They jumped out of the balloon and celebrated! They had escaped their old nation and found a new, happy home. The people laughed and danced and told stories late into the night. Everyone was so happy to have escaped their old nation and have a fresh start in their new home. They were finally free!

Once upon a time there were two friends; Noah and Lou. They were very happy together, but one day Lou became worried. Noah asked, "What's wrong Lou?" Lou replied, "I'm worried because I have to stack these blocks by myself. I don't know how to do it." Noah said, "It's OK, I'll help you stack the blocks." Lou was happy and they started to stack the blocks. Lou was getting better and better at stacking the blocks. Finally, the stack was complete! Noah said, "See, you did it! You don't have to be worried anymore." Lou smiled and said, "Yes, thank you for your help!" Then both Noah and Lou laughed and talked about what they wanted to do next.

One day, a boy named Tim went to a big stadium with his dad. The stadium was very light and full of people. They went to watch a fun game. During the game, Tim saw a small ball roll under a seat. He said to his dad, "Look, a ball! Can I get it?" His dad said, "Yes, but be quick!" Tim went to get the ball, but it started to hide under more seats. He followed the ball and said, "Come back, ball! I want to play with you!" The ball stopped, and Tim picked it up. He was so happy. Tim showed the ball to his dad, and they played with it together. They had a great time at the stadium.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very excited because in one week, he would go to the zoo with his mom and dad. He couldn't wait to see all the animals. One day, Tim and his mom went to the store. They bought a big, sour lemon. Tim's mom said they would make lemonade. Tim liked lemonade a lot. They went home and squeezed the lemon. Then they mixed it with water and sugar. Tim tasted it and said, "Yum!" Finally, the week was over and it was time for the zoo. Tim was so happy. He saw lions, tigers, and bears. He even got to see a monkey up close! The monkey made a funny face at Tim. He laughed and laughed. It was the best day ever.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park with their mom. They liked to slide, swing and run. They saw a big tube that was lying on the grass. They wanted to go inside and see what was there. "Can we go in the tube, mom?" Lily asked. "Sure, but be careful and stay together," mom said. Lily and Ben crawled into the tube. It was dark and cold. They heard a small sound. It was a kitten. The kitten was scared and hungry. It was stuck in the tube and could not get out. "Look, Ben, a kitten!" Lily said. "Let's rescue it, Lily!" Ben said. They tried to pull the kitten out, but it was too tight. They called for mom, but she could not hear them. They were worried and sad. Then they had an idea. They used their scarves to make a rope. They tied one end to the kitten and the other end to Lily's shoe. They pushed the shoe out of the tube. Mom saw the shoe and the rope. She pulled the rope and the kitten came out. "Mom, mom, we rescued a kitten!" Lily and Ben shouted. Mom was proud and happy. She hugged them and the kitten. She was compassionate and kind. She took them and the kitten home. They gave the kitten some milk and a name. They called it Tube. They became friends and played together. They were glad they rescued Tube.
