Category: Beginner
Title: The Hero and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a hero. The hero had a thick cape. He liked to help people. He had a best friend named Dog. Dog had big teeth and liked to bite toys. One day, the hero saw a big tree. The tree had a cat stuck in it. The hero wanted to help the cat. He asked Dog to help him. Dog looked at the tree and wagged his tail. The hero and Dog had a plan. The hero climbed the tree to save the cat. Dog stayed on the ground, ready to help. The hero got close to the cat, but the cat was scared. The cat hissed and jumped. It landed on Dog. Dog got scared and bit the cat. The cat ran away, hurt and sad. The hero and Dog were sad too. They didn't want to hurt the cat. They learned to be more careful next time.

Once upon a time, in a small pond, there was a little frog named Lily. Lily loved to hop and play all day. She had many friends, like the fish, the ducks, and the turtles. They all liked to play in the pond together. Lily was a very happy frog. One day, a big terrible monster came to the pond. The monster was big and scary. All the animals were scared and ran away. The monster disturbed the water, making it hard for Lily to hop and play. Lily was very sad and missed her friends. But then, something unexpected happened. The big terrible monster started to cry. It was not a mean monster after all. It was just lonely and wanted to play too. Lily went to the monster and asked if it wanted to be friends. The monster said yes, and they all played together in the pond. The monster was not terrible anymore, and everyone was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy phone. He would ring it all day long. One day, Tim saw some kids playing a sport outside. They were having so much fun, so Tim wanted to join them. Tim brought his toy phone to the sport. The kids were playing with a big ball. They would kick it and run. Tim thought this was a great sport. They all played together and laughed. Tim's toy phone would ring, and it made the kids smile. They liked the funny sound it made. But then, a naughty dog came to the sport. The dog saw the ball and wanted to play too. The dog ran very fast and took the ball away. The kids were sad because they could not play the sport anymore. Tim had an idea. He rang his toy phone, and the naughty dog heard it. The dog brought the ball back, and they all played the sport again. Everyone was happy, and Tim's toy phone saved the day.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim had a great clock. This clock was not like other clocks. It could grow big and small. Tim loved his clock very much. He always played with it and showed it to his friends. One sunny day, Tim went to the park with his clock. He met a girl named Sue. Sue saw the great clock and asked, "What can your clock do?" Tim said, "Watch!" He made the clock grow big, then small. Sue was very happy and clapped her hands. "Wow! That's fun!" she said. Tim and Sue played with the great clock all day. They made it grow big and small many times. They laughed and had a lot of fun. When it was time to go home, they said goodbye. Tim and Sue promised to play with the great clock again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He had a box full of gold. Tim liked to play with his gold every day. But one day, he saw that his gold was all mixed up with rocks. Tim was anxious. He wanted to separate the gold from the rocks. Tim thought and thought about how to separate the gold. He tried using his hands, but it was too hard. Then, he had an idea. He would use a big bowl and water to help him. Tim put the gold and rocks in the bowl and filled it with water. As Tim moved the water around in the bowl, the gold and rocks started to separate. The gold was heavy and stayed at the bottom, while the rocks floated to the top. Tim was happy to see his gold all by itself again. Now, he could play with his gold without any rocks. And he was not anxious anymore.
