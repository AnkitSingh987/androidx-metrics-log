Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Lily and the Pond

Ben and Lily went to the pond with Mom. They liked to see the fish and ducks and bugs. They also liked to look for frogs. Mom said frogs were good for the pond. They ate the bad bugs and made funny sounds. Ben and Lily walked around the pond and tried to find frogs. They saw some small green ones and some big brown ones. They wanted to catch one and look at it. But the frogs were fast and jumped away. Ben and Lily ran after them. "Look, there is a huge frog!" Ben shouted. He pointed to a big green frog that was sitting on a rock. It had big eyes and a wide mouth. Ben ran towards the rock. He wanted to touch the huge frog. But the rock was wet and slippery. Ben did not see the water on the rock. He stepped on the rock and slipped. He fell into the pond with a splash. The huge frog jumped away and croaked. "Ben, are you okay?" Lily asked. She ran to the edge of the pond. She saw Ben in the water. He was wet and muddy. He looked sad and scared. "I'm okay, Lily," Ben said. He tried to smile. He got out of the pond. Mom came and hugged him. She said he was brave and helped him dry off. "Sorry, Mom," Ben said. "I wanted to see the huge frog." "It's okay, Ben," Mom said. "The huge frog is just a frog. He is not a toy. He likes to live in the pond. Maybe next time you can watch him from far away." Ben nodded. He learned his lesson. He did not want to slip again. He and Lily said goodbye to the pond and the frogs. They went home with Mom. They had a warm bath and a hot chocolate. They told Dad about their adventure. They still liked frogs, but they liked them better in the pond.

Once upon a time, there was a little frog named Tim. Tim lived in a safe and comfortable pond. He loved to jump all day long. One day, while he was jumping, he saw a new friend. It was a small fish named Fin. "Hi, Fin!" Tim said. "Do you like to jump like me?" Fin looked at Tim and smiled. "I can't jump, but I can swim fast!" Fin replied. Tim and Fin played together every day. They were happy to have each other as friends. Even though Fin couldn't jump, they still had fun together in their safe and comfortable pond.

Once upon a time there was a very lucky little boy. He loved to trade things with his friends. One day, his mom gave him a pencil and a sheet of paper. She said, "You can make something special with these." The boy was so excited! He wanted to make a special picture for mom. So he got to work. He used the pencil to cut the paper into different shapes. He worked hard until he was done. He had made a wonderful trade. He gave the paper shapes to his friends and they gave him something in exchange. He was very happy! He presented the special gift to his mom and she smiled. She said, "This was very lucky of you! You did a great job." The boy was proud of himself! He had traded something special and made his mom happy. He knew that he had done a good job.

Once there was a nosy girl named Lily. She liked to know what everyone was doing all the time. One day, Lily saw her mom and dad talking. She wanted to hear what they said, so she hid behind a chair. Lily heard her mom say, "We need to delay going to the store." Lily didn't know what "delay" meant, so she asked her mom. Her mom said, "Delay means we have to wait a little longer before we do something." Later, Lily saw her friend Tom playing with a toy. She wanted to play too, but Tom said, "No, it's mine!" Lily felt sad and went to tell her mom. Her mom said, "Sometimes, we need to share and not be nosy. That way, we can all be happy." From that day, Lily learned that being nosy could cause problems. She tried to be more patient and not to be nosy. She also learned that sharing was important. And when she shared her toys, she and her friends had more fun together.
