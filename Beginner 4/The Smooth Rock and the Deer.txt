Category: Beginner
Title: The Smooth Rock and the Deer

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a smooth rock. On this rock, a deer liked to sit and watch the birds. One day, a girl named Sally went to the forest to play. She saw the deer on the smooth rock and thought it was pretty. Sally wanted to be friends with the deer. She tried to point to show the deer a big tree where they could play together. But the deer did not understand. Sally tried again and again, but the deer just looked at her. Then, Sally had an idea. She would find a big stick and point with that. She found a big stick and ran back to the deer. But when she got there, the deer was gone. Sally looked and looked, but she could not find the deer. Sally was very sad and went home.

Once upon a time, there was a silly cat named Tom. Tom lived in a wild place with tall trees and green grass. Tom loved to play and run all day long. He liked to chase butterflies and jump on leaves. One day, Tom saw a big dog named Max. Max was not happy. He said, "Tom, you are too loud! I want to nap, but you make too much noise!" Tom felt sad. He did not want to make Max mad. He wanted to be a good friend. Tom thought of a plan. He found a soft, quiet place for Max to nap. It was under a big tree with lots of shade. Tom said, "Max, come here. You can nap in this nice spot." Max smiled and said, "Thank you, Tom." Then, Max took a long nap, and Tom played quietly. They were both happy, and they became good friends.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went on a tour with his mom and dad. They saw many fun things like big animals, pretty flowers, and tall trees. Tim was very happy. As they walked, Tim started to feel hungry. His mom gave him a yummy sandwich to eat. Tim loved the sandwich and his tummy felt better. They kept walking and saw a man who wanted them to pay to see a special surprise. Tim's dad gave the man some money and they went to see the surprise. They were so excited! But when they got there, it was just a big rock. Tim, his mom, and his dad were not happy about the rock. They thought it would be something fun, but it was not. Just then, the rock started to move! It was not a rock at all. It was a big, friendly turtle! The turtle said hello and played with Tim. Tim, his mom, and his dad laughed and had so much fun with the turtle. The tour was the best day ever, and they all went home with big smiles on their faces.

One day, a girl named Sue went to the park. It was a cold day, so Sue wore her warm sweater. She saw her friend, Tom, playing with a ball. Sue wanted to play too. She asked Tom, "Can I play with you?" Tom said, "Yes, but you have to be quiet." Sue and Tom played with the ball. Sue was very quiet. She did not want to make Tom mad. They played for a long time. Sue was happy. She said, "Thank you, Tom, for letting me play." Then, a big wind came. The wind took Sue's sweater away! Sue was sad and cold. She asked Tom to help her find her sweater. Tom did not want to help. He wanted to keep playing with the ball. Sue was very sad. She had to go home without her sweater.

Anna loves to act. She likes to pretend she is a princess, a doctor, a teacher, or anything she can imagine. She has a lot of costumes and props in her room. She also loves to sing and dance. She wants to be a musician when she grows up. One day, Anna's mom takes her to a big theater. There are many people and lights and sounds. Anna is very excited. She sees a sign that says "The Sound of Music". She knows this is a story about a family that sings and acts. She wants to watch it. Anna and her mom sit in their seats. The show starts. Anna sees a lady who plays the mother. She has a delicate voice and a beautiful dress. She sings to the children and makes them happy. Anna likes her. She also sees a man who plays the father. He is stern and strict, but he loves the family. He acts brave and strong. Anna likes him too. Anna watches the show with wonder. She claps and laughs and cries. She feels like she is part of the story. She wants to be on the stage too. She thinks she can act and sing like the lady and the man. She dreams of being a musician and an actor. When the show ends, Anna and her mom go to the backstage. They meet the lady and the man who played the mother and the father. They are very nice and friendly. They give Anna a hug and a kiss. They tell her she can be a musician and an actor if she works hard and follows her heart. They also give her a delicate flower and a souvenir program. Anna is very happy. She thanks them and says goodbye. Anna and her mom go home. Anna puts the flower and the program in her room. She looks at her costumes and props. She smiles. She decides to act out the story of The Sound of Music with her dolls and toys. She sings and dances and acts. She has a lot of fun. She feels like a musician and an actor. She is happy.
