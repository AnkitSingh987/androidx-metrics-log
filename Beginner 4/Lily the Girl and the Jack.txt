Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Jack

One day, a playful girl named Lily went to the store with her mom. They wanted to buy a toy for Lily's little brother, Jack. Lily was excited to pick a toy for him. At the store, Lily saw a toy with lots of numbers on it. She thought Jack would love it. "Mom, can we buy this toy with numbers for Jack?" she asked. Her mom looked at the toy and smiled. "Yes, Lily, that is a great choice. Jack will love it," she said. When they got home, Lily gave the toy to Jack. He was so happy and started playing with the numbers right away. Lily and Jack played together, and they both had a lot of fun.

One day, a little boy named Tom found a broken toy car. It was red and had a rough texture. Tom wanted to fix the broken car, so he asked his mom for help. His mom said, "Let's send it to the toy doctor!" So, they put the broken car in a box and sent it away. Tom was sad to see his car go, but he knew the toy doctor would make it better. After a few days, the box came back. Tom opened it and saw his red car was not broken anymore! The toy doctor had fixed it. Tom was so happy, he played with his car all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside. One day, Tim went to the park. At the park, Tim saw a big slide. Tim wanted to go down the slide. Tim climbed up and up to the top of the slide. Then, he slid down very fast. When he got to the bottom, he felt dizzy. Tim saw his neighbor, Sue, at the park too. Sue came to help Tim. Sue told Tim that she could slide down without getting dizzy. She showed Tim how to do it. But when she slid down, she got dizzy too! They laughed and laughed because they both felt dizzy. They had fun at the park together.

Once upon a time, there was a delicate clock. The clock was very pretty and lived in a small house with a little girl named Amy. Amy loved the clock very much and always took care of it. One day, she saw a big ice block outside her window. She thought it would be fun to put the clock on the ice block to see what would happen. The clock said, "Please don't put me on the ice block. I might freeze and stop working." But Amy didn't listen to the clock. She put it on the ice block, and the clock started to get very cold. The clock's hands stopped moving, and it was not able to tell time anymore. Amy felt very sad that her clock was not working. She realized that she should have listened to the clock and not put it on the ice block. The bad ending to the story is that the clock never worked again. The moral value of the story is to always listen to others and think about what might happen before doing something.

Once upon a time, there was a little rat. The rat had a heavy bag. He carried it every day. The bag was full of nuts and seeds. He wanted to share with his friends. One day, the rat met a bird. The bird was sad. The rat asked, "Why are you sad?" The bird said, "I have no food." The rat remembered his heavy bag. He shared his nuts and seeds with the bird. The bird was happy. Then, the rat met a cat. The cat was hungry too. The rat reminded himself to share. He gave the cat some nuts and seeds. The cat was happy. The rat felt good. He learned that sharing makes everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a pale boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his tools. He had a toy hammer, a toy saw, and a toy wrench. One day, Tim met a girl named Sue. "Hi, Sue," said Tim. "Do you want to play with my tools?" "Sure," said Sue. "Let's build something together!" Tim and Sue worked hard to build a little house out of blocks. They used the toy hammer and the toy saw to make the house strong. When they were done, they were very tired. "Wow, our hard work yielded a beautiful house!" said Tim. "Yes," said Sue. "I am glad we used your tools to build it." They were both very happy and played with their new house all day long.
