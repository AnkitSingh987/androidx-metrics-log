Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Glove

One day, a boy named Tom found a glove. The glove was wet. Tom wanted to make the glove dry. So, he put the glove in the sun. The sun was hot and bright. Tom's friend, Sue, came over. Sue saw the glove. She asked, "What are you doing, Tom?" Tom said, "I am making the glove dry. The sun will help." Sue wanted to help too. She said, "Let me help you, Tom." Sue and Tom played while they waited for the glove to dry. They played with a ball and ran around. After some time, they went back to the glove. The glove was now dry. Tom and Sue were happy. They put the glove on and played more games together.

Once upon a time, there was a kind bunny named Bob. Bob loved to eat carrots. One day, he went to find some carrots in the woods. Bob saw a big carrot near a tree. He was very happy. But, he was too small to get the carrot by himself. He needed help. So, Bob asked his friend, the bird, to help him. The bird tried to help Bob, but the carrot was stuck. They pulled and pulled, but the carrot did not come out. They were very sad. In the end, they could not get the carrot and went home hungry.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia liked to taste new things. One day, her mom was cooking in the kitchen. Mia saw her mom use a tool to cut food. She thought it looked fun. Mia wanted to help her mom. She reached for the tool. But her mom said, "No, Mia! That tool is dangerous!" Mia was sad. She wanted to help and taste the food too. Her mom saw Mia was sad. She gave Mia a safe tool to use. Mia helped her mom make a yummy meal. They tasted the food together. Mia was happy to help and learn about safe tools.

It was a sunny day and Ann was outside playing with her friends. They were having a great time, but soon it started to get dark and the rain began to pour down. Ann didn't recognize the signs of the storm coming, but her friends did. They knew what was happening but they didn't know what to do. The rain increased and Ann's friends urged her to go home because the flood from the nearby river was coming. Ann was a bit scared but she trusted her friends and went home. The rain continued to come down harder and a strong wind began to blow. Ann's parents arrived right away and took her indoors to safety. The flood was worse than anyone expected. The river filled quickly and spilled over, flooding the streets and into people's homes. Ann and her family were surprised by the force of the flood and its bossiness. They were very worried and they stayed inside until the flood was finally over. Ann learned a very important lesson that day. Now she can recognize the signs of a storm coming and is more prepared when bad weather hits. She still stays inside until it is over, though, because she's learned to respect the power of nature.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Amy. She had a big, red ball. Amy loved to play with the ball in the park. One day, she saw a lock on the park gate. The lock was big and strong. Amy was sad because she could not go in. Amy had a friend named Tom. Tom was a little bit crazy. He liked to do funny things. Tom wanted to help Amy. He had an idea to part the lock, so they could play with the ball in the park. Tom found a big stick and tried to hit the lock. He hit it again and again. But the lock did not part. It only got stronger. The park gate stayed closed. Amy and Tom could not play with the big, red ball in the park. They had to go back home. They were very sad. The end.
