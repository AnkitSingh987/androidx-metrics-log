Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Ben and the Cake

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys and books. One day, their mom says, "I have a surprise for you. Come to the kitchen and see." Sara and Ben run to the kitchen. They see a big cake on the table. It has chocolate and sprinkles on it. It looks yummy. "Wow!" Sara says. "Is that for us, mom?" "Yes, it is," mom says. "I made it for your birthday. You are both four years old today. Happy birthday, my sweeties!" Sara and Ben hug their mom. "Thank you, mom!" they say. "You are the best mom ever!" Mom smiles and cuts the cake. She gives a slice to Sara and a slice to Ben. They eat the cake with big spoons. It tastes yummy. They lick their lips and fingers. "Can we have more, mom?" Ben asks. "Of course, you can," mom says. "But first, you have to make a wish and blow the candles." Sara and Ben look at the candles on the cake. They are four candles, two for Sara and two for Ben. They think of a wish. They wish to have more fun and more love with their mom and their toys and books. They blow the candles together. The candles go out. "Yay!" Sara and Ben say. "We did it!" Mom claps and cheers. She gives them more cake and a big hug. "I love you, Sara and Ben," she says. "You make me happy every day." Sara and Ben smile and say, "We love you too, mom. You make us happy every day."

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a small frog named Fifi. Fifi had a friend named Tom, who was a little bird. They loved to play together. One day, Fifi found a big print on the ground. She did not know what it was, so she called Tom to take a look. "Tom, what is this big print?" Fifi asked. Tom looked at the print and said, "I think it is from a big animal. We should be careful and not go too far from home." But Fifi wanted to find the big animal, so she went deeper into the forest. As Fifi went further, she found a big fruit that was sour. She took a bite and made a funny face. Just then, the big animal who made the print appeared! It was a kind bear named Ben. Ben saw Fifi's funny face and laughed. "That fruit is too sour for you," he said. "But I can show you where to find sweet fruits." Fifi and Ben became friends and went back to Tom. They all played together and shared the sweet fruits. The moral of the story is to not judge someone by their size or prints, because they might be a kind friend. And always listen to your friends, as they may have good advice for you.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a hidden cave. In that cave lived a little rabbit named Remy. Remy liked to play with his friends and find new things in the forest. He had a lot of fun every day. One day, Remy and his friends were playing near a big tree. They saw a red and orange fire. The fire was hot and scary. Remy and his friends ran away from the fire to stay safe. They were very scared. Remy wanted to help stop the fire. He saw a big stick and got an idea. Remy used his teeth to bite the stick and carry it to a pond. He put the stick in the water to make it wet. Then, he ran back to the fire and hit it with the wet stick. The fire went away and the forest was safe again. Remy and his friends were happy and they continued to play.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue liked to pick flowers in the big field near her house. One day, she saw something shiny and gold far away. Sue wanted to find out what it was, so she walked to the distant place. As she got closer, Sue saw that the gold thing was a big key. She picked it up and looked around. Sue saw a little house that she had never seen before. She thought maybe the key would open the door to the little house. Sue walked to the house and used the gold key to open the door. Inside, she found a room full of toys! But, the toys were alive and they could talk! The toys were happy and they asked Sue to play with them. Sue loved the toys and played with them all day. The unexpected twist was that the toys were magic and Sue made new friends.
