Category: Beginner
Title: Bobo the Bird and the Leaf

Once upon a time, there was a bird named Bobo. Bobo loved to fly high in the sky. One day, Bobo saw a stable with many animals inside. In the stable, there was a horse named Hoho who was very upset. Hoho could not fly like Bobo, and that made him sad. Bobo wanted to help Hoho feel better. He thought and thought. Then, he had a big idea! Bobo found a big leaf and gave it to Hoho. "Hold this," said Bobo. Hoho held the leaf with his mouth. Bobo flew up and grabbed the leaf with his feet. He started to fly, and Hoho's feet left the ground! Hoho was flying too! They flew together, and Hoho was very happy. The other animals in the stable were very surprised to see Hoho fly. The moral of the story is that friends can help each other feel better, even when something seems impossible.

One day, a pretty bird was flying in the sky. It saw a little girl sitting by a tree. The girl looked sad. The pretty bird flew down to the girl and asked, "Why are you sad?" The little girl said, "I lost my toy. It is on the other side of the river, and I can't get it." The pretty bird wanted to help the girl. It said, "I can fly to the other side and bring your toy back. You just need to hold my feathers, and we can fly together." The little girl was happy. She held the pretty bird's feathers, and they flew to the other side of the river. They found the toy and flew back. The girl was very happy and thanked the pretty bird. They became good friends and played together every day.

Tim and Sam are friends. They like to play in the field near their homes. The field has grass, flowers and trees. Sometimes they see cows and sheep in the field. One day, they see a big black dog in the field. The dog is barking and running. Tim and Sam are scared. They hide behind a tree. "Who owns the black dog?" Tim asks Sam. "I don't know," Sam says. "Maybe he is lost. Maybe he is hungry. Maybe he is angry." The black dog comes closer to the tree. He smells Tim and Sam. He wags his tail. He licks their faces. Tim and Sam are surprised. The dog is not angry. He is friendly. "Hello, dog," Tim says. "What is your name?" The dog barks. He does not have a name tag. He does not have a collar. He does not have an owner. "Can we own the black dog?" Sam asks Tim. "Yes, we can," Tim says. "We can be his owners. We can give him a name. We can give him food. We can give him love." Tim and Sam hug the black dog. They name him Spot. They take him home. They ask their moms and dads if they can keep him. Their moms and dads say yes. They are happy. Spot is happy. They are a family.

One day, a boy named Tim and his friend, Sue, were playing in a big, empty space near their homes. They liked to run and jump in the open space. Tim had an idea and said, "Let's play hide and seek!" Sue agreed, so they started to play. Tim suggested, "I will count, and you go hide!" Sue ran to find a hiding spot. Tim closed his eyes and counted to ten. When he opened his eyes, he started to look for Sue. He looked behind trees and under bushes, but he could not find her. Just when Tim was about to give up, he heard a funny sound. He looked around and saw a big box in the empty space. He walked over to the box and heard the sound again. Tim opened the box and, to his surprise, he found Sue inside, laughing with a small, green alien. "I found a new friend," Sue said. Tim smiled and said, "Let's all play together!" So, Tim, Sue, and their new alien friend played together in the empty space all day long.
