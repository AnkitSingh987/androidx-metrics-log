Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Frog and the Hat

One day, a little frog named Tim was at the pond. He liked to jump very high. The wind would blow when he jumped. Tim had a friend named Sam. Sam was a deaf rabbit. They liked to play together. One day, Tim said to Sam, "I will jump so high, the wind will be very big!" Sam could not hear, but he saw Tim's big smile. They started to play, and Tim jumped higher and higher. Sam watched and clapped his hands. The wind got bigger and bigger as Tim jumped. Then, with one big jump, Tim made a very big wind. The wind blew a hat to Sam. Sam put the hat on his head and smiled. Tim and Sam laughed and played with the hat all day.

One day, a little fish named Fin went for a swim. Fin was a delicate fish with small fins and a tiny tail. He liked to dive deep into the water to find new friends. As Fin swam, he saw a big hand in the water. The hand was friendly and wanted to play. Fin swam around the hand and they had fun together. Fin and the hand became good friends. They played every day and had lots of fun. Fin loved to dive and the hand loved to help. They were happy and had many adventures in the water.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. Tim saw a big box with a label on it. The label had a picture of a toy car. Tim liked toy cars, so he wanted to touch the box. Tim's mom told him to be slow and careful. Tim moved slow and reached out to touch the box. But when he touched it, the box fell over! Tim was scared and thought he broke the toy car. But then, something unexpected happened. The box opened, and a lot of small toy cars came out! Tim's mom laughed and said it was okay. The box was full of toy cars, not just one. Tim was happy, and his mom let him pick one to take home.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was very excited because her mom got a new phone. Lucy loved to play games on the phone. She would laugh and clap her hands when she won the games. One day, Lucy was playing with the phone when it fell on the floor. The phone suffered a big crack on the screen. Lucy was sad and scared that her mom would be upset. She knew she needed to tell her mom what happened. Lucy went to her mom and showed her the broken phone. Her mom was not mad, but she told Lucy to be more careful next time. They went to the store and got the phone fixed. Lucy promised to be careful and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a red fish named Finn. Finn lived in a big lake with his friends. They loved to swim and play together all day long. One day, a naughty duck came to the lake. The duck liked to tease Finn and his friends. He would splash water on them and laugh. "You can't catch me!" he would say. Finn and his friends did not like the duck's teasing. Finn had an idea. He and his friends decided to work together to stop the duck from teasing them. They swam very fast around the duck, making a big whirlpool. The duck was surprised and could not tease them anymore. "Please stop," the duck said. "I am sorry for teasing you." Finn and his friends stopped swimming and the whirlpool went away. From that day on, the duck became friends with Finn and the other fish. They all played together in the big lake, and no one teased anyone ever again.

One day, Tom found a big trumpet in his toy box. He was very happy. He wanted to show it to his friend, Tim. Tom ran to Tim's house with the trumpet. He was excited to play it. When Tom got to Tim's house, he showed Tim the trumpet. Tim was scared of the loud noise it could make. Tom said, "Don't be scared, Tim. I will play it softly." Tom played the trumpet softly, and Tim liked the sound. They played with the trumpet all day. The next day, Tom and Tim wanted to write a song for the trumpet. They sat down and thought of fun words. But then, a big wind came and blew the trumpet away. Tom and Tim were very sad. They looked for the trumpet, but they could not find it. The wind had taken their fun toy away.
