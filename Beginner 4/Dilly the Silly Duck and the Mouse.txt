Category: Beginner
Title: Dilly the Silly Duck and the Mouse

Once upon a time, there was a silly duck named Dilly. Dilly loved to step and dance all day long. One day, Dilly was walking near a big mill. The mill was where they made flour from wheat. Dilly saw a little mouse by the mill. The mouse was sad. "Why are you sad?" asked Dilly. The mouse said, "I lost my cheese in the mill, and I'm too small to go in and get it." Dilly wanted to help the mouse. So, Dilly stepped into the mill, found the cheese, and gave it back to the mouse. The mouse was happy and said, "Thank you, Dilly!" They both danced and stepped together, laughing and having fun. And from that day on, Dilly the silly duck and the little mouse were the best of friends.

One day, a little duck named Dara went for a walk. She saw a big mill near a river. Dara felt lucky to find such a fun place to play. She went inside the mill to look around. She saw a big wheel turning in the water. Dara met a frog named Fred near the mill. Fred said, "I dare you to jump on the big wheel!" Dara was scared, but she wanted to be brave. She thought about it for a moment and then said, "Okay, I will try!" Dara jumped on the wheel and started to spin around. She was having so much fun! But suddenly, the wheel stopped. Dara was stuck! She was scared and didn't know what to do. Fred saw Dara was in trouble and jumped to help her. Fred pushed the wheel with all his might, and it started to spin again. Dara was able to jump off the wheel and land safely on the ground. She was so happy to be safe and thanked Fred for his help. From that day on, Dara and Fred became the best of friends, and they always played together near the lucky mill.

Tim and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump and slide. They saw a big hill with many rocks and sticks. They wanted to climb the hill and see what was on the other side. They ran to the hill and started to climb. Tim was faster than Lily. He got to the top first. He looked around and saw a big saw. It was shiny and sharp. He wanted to touch it. Lily was behind Tim. She was not as fast. She stumbled on a rock and fell down. She hurt her knee and cried. She called for Tim to help her. Tim heard Lily cry. He turned around and saw her on the ground. He felt sorry for her. He wanted to help her. But he also wanted to touch the saw. He did not know what to do. He decided to touch the saw first. He reached for it. But the saw was broken. It had a crack in the blade. When Tim touched it, the saw snapped and cut his hand. He screamed and dropped the saw. Lily heard Tim scream. She forgot her knee and got up. She ran to Tim and saw his hand. It was bleeding and hurt. She hugged him and said sorry. She told him to come with her. They needed to find a grown-up to help them. They left the hill and the saw. They ran to the park bench where their mom was sitting. They told her what happened. She was angry and scared. She took them to the car and drove them to the doctor. The doctor cleaned their wounds and gave them bandages. He told them to be careful and not to play with dangerous things. Tim and Lily learned their lesson. They said sorry to each other and to their mom. They promised to be more careful and to help each other. They hugged and kissed and went home. They still liked to play in the park, but they never climbed the hill again.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful princess. She loved to play outside but there was one problem - she was very slow! Whenever the princess tried to run and play, it would always delay her friends. The princess was worried that no one would want to play with her because she was too slow. But one day, the princess had an idea! She asked her friends to pretend to be the tortoise to her hare. This way, she could still join in the game but everyone would take turns going slower so she wouldn't be left behind. The princess and her friends' game of tag was one of the greatest funs anyone had ever had. The princess smiled and felt happy that she could still join in the fun, even though she was slow. From then on, the princess and her friends always played games that allowed her to be part of the fun.
