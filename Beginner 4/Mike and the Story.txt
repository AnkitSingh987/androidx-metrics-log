Category: Beginner
Title: Mike and the Story

Once upon a time, there were two friends. Their names were Mike and Sue. They were always together and they did everything together. One day, they went to the park. Mike wanted to play a game, so he suggested they made up a story. Sue thought it sounded like a lot of fun, so they both sat down to begin. At first, the story was easy. They made up new events and characters, and the story got more and more interesting. But then, Mike had a difficult idea. He said two of the characters should fight. Sue didn't like this idea. She said that it was too hard and it would ruin the story. But Mike kept suggesting it until Sue said she didn't want to play anymore. So, Mike just kept playing and talking to himself. But without Sue, it was no fun anymore. He tried hard to make up a good story, but without Sue, it was impossible. The story had a bad ending. Mike was sad about all this, but he never forgot what to do when it came to storytelling: always listen to your friend.

One day, a little white bird was in a big field. The bird liked to whistle. The bird saw a big white dog. The dog liked to run in the field. They wanted to play together. The bird said, "Let's play! I can whistle a song for you." The dog said, "I can't whistle, but I can run fast!" So, the bird whistled a song and the dog ran around the field. But then, a little gray cat came into the field. The cat did not like the bird's whistling. The cat said, "Stop! I don't like your whistle!" The bird felt sad. The dog said, "But we like to play together." The cat thought for a moment. The cat said, "Okay, we can all play. I will learn to like the whistle." So, they all played together in the big field. The bird whistled, the dog ran, and the cat learned to like the sound. They were all happy friends.

Once upon a time, there was a funny man named Bob. Bob loved to play with his ball. He would bounce it, throw it, and kick it all day long. One sunny day, Bob saw a big tree with a hole in it. He thought it would be fun to throw the ball into the hole. So, he did. But, the ball got stuck! Bob tried and tried to get the ball out, but he could not reach it. "Oh no!" said Bob. "I need help to get my ball back!" A little bird heard Bob and wanted to help. The bird flew to the hole and tried to prevent the ball from going deeper. "I will help you, Bob!" said the bird. The bird pushed the ball out of the hole with its beak. The ball fell down, and Bob caught it. Bob was so happy to have his ball back. He thanked the little bird for helping him. "Thank you, bird! You are my friend!" From that day on, Bob and the bird played together every day. They had lots of fun, and they always helped each other when they needed it.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. It was cold outside, but he wanted to play. He put on his big coat and ran to the park. In the park, he saw a big barrel. Tim was curious about what was inside. Tim's friend, Sam, came to the park too. Sam saw the barrel and said, "I think I saw someone shoot at the barrel." Tim wanted to look inside, but Sam was scared. "I don't think we should look," Sam said. But Tim didn't listen. He opened the barrel, and out came a big wind. The wind was so strong that it blew Tim and Sam away. They were cold and scared, and they wished they had listened to Sam's warning.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to visit the park with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. They would run and play on the trail every day. One day, Buddy saw a big dog on the trail. He barked at the big dog because he was scared. Lily told Buddy not to bark at the big dog, but he did not listen. He barked and barked, and the big dog ran away. Buddy felt ashamed for not listening to Lily. He knew he had to say sorry to the big dog. The next day, Buddy and Lily went to visit the trail again. They found the big dog and Buddy said sorry. The big dog forgave Buddy, and they all played together on the trail. Buddy learned to listen to his friend and not to be scared of new friends. And they all had a fun day at the park.
