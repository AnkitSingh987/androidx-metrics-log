Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Sun

One day, a little girl named Lucy went for a walk with her mom. The sun was hot, and they were both very happy. Lucy saw an old man sitting on a bench. The old man looked sad. Lucy wanted to make him smile. Lucy said to her mom, "Mom, can I give the old man my ice cream? It will make him smile and not feel so hot." Her mom said, "Yes, that's a very kind thing to do, Lucy." Lucy walked up to the old man and said, "Here, you can have my ice cream. It's very cold and will make you feel better." The old man smiled and said, "Thank you, little girl. You made me very happy." Lucy smiled too, because she knew she had done a good thing.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had a mild leg that hurt sometimes. One day, Spot saw his friend, the big bird, mix seeds to eat. Spot thought it looked fun, so he wanted to try too. The next day, Spot found some seeds and tried to mix them like the big bird. But his mild leg made it hard for him to mix the seeds well. Spot felt sad and wished he could mix the seeds just like his friend. Then, a kind cat saw Spot and wanted to help. The cat had an idea! The cat helped Spot mix the seeds with her strong leg. Spot was so happy and thanked the cat. They became best friends and mixed seeds together every day. Spot's mild leg didn't stop him from having fun anymore.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They wanted to buy some beef to cook for dinner. Tim was very alert, looking at all the things in the store. He liked to see the colors and shapes of the food. As they walked to the meat area, Tim saw a big box. He didn't know what was in it. He wanted to find out, so he went closer. Tim realized that the box was moving! He was scared and called his mom. His mom came and looked at the box. She smiled and said, "It's not scary, Tim. It's just a big, friendly dog!" The dog had been hiding in the box, and it was a surprise for Tim. He was so happy to have a new friend. They took the dog home, and they all had beef for dinner.

One day, a boy named Tim and a girl named Sue went to the park. They saw a swing and wanted to play on it. But the swing looked weak. They were scared it might break. So, they started to discuss what to do. Tim said, "The swing looks weak. I think it will break if we sit on it." Sue agreed and said, "Yes, let's not sit on it. Maybe we can find something else to play with." They looked around the park and saw a ball. They decided to play with the ball instead. Later, a big wind came and blew the weak swing away. Tim and Sue were happy they did not sit on it. They knew they made the right choice. They played with the ball all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man who owned a nice house and a lot of pets. Every day, he would unlock the door to his house to let all his pets in. Then, he would walk his pets to the veterinarian. At the veterinarian, the wealthy man's pets would get nice checkups. The veterinarian would make sure the pets were happy and healthy. After the checkups, the wealthy man would unlock the door to his house and let the pets back in. This happened every day for a very long time. The wealthy man's pets were always happy and healthy. All the people in the town were thankful for the veterinarian and the wealthy man, who took such good care of their pets.

Once upon a time, in a small valley, there lived a big bear and a small rabbit. They were very good friends. The valley was incredible, full of trees and flowers. The bear and the rabbit liked to play together. One day, it started to rain. The rabbit got wet and cold. The bear said, "Rabbit, let me cover you with my big body. You will be warm and dry." The rabbit was happy and said, "Thank you, Bear. You are a good friend." The rain stopped, and the sun came out. The rabbit was warm and dry, all because of the bear's help. They played together again, and the rabbit promised to help the bear when he needed it. The moral value of the story is that friends should always help each other.
