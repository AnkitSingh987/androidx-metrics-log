Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot loved to play in the dirt. One day, he found a big bone while digging. He was so happy to find the bone that he danced and wagged his tail. But the bone was filthy from being in the ground. Spot's friend, a bird named Chirpy, saw Spot with the filthy bone. Chirpy said, "Spot, that bone is dirty! You should clean it before you taste it." Spot didn't want to listen, but Chirpy was a good friend. So, Spot went to the river to wash the bone. As Spot washed the bone, something unexpected happened. The filthy bone turned into a shiny, golden bone! Spot was amazed. He thanked Chirpy for the advice. From that day on, Spot learned to listen to his friends and always keep things clean. The moral of the story is to listen to good advice from friends and to keep things clean.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was three years old and loved to read books. One day, Tom was walking through a dark forest looking for a special book. Suddenly, he saw something shining in the darkness. He went closer to take a look and found a book! He knelt down and picked it up. The book was old and dusty, but it looked interesting. Tom quickly opened it and saw it was covered with words. He couldn't understand what it said, but he knew it must be special! Tom wanted to take the book home and show it to his family. He carefully wrapped the book in a cloth. Then he started running home. He was excited to show everyone his new book! When he arrived home, he shouted: "Mama! Papa! Look what I found!" Tom's parents were amazed. He had been gone for a long time and they weren't sure what he had been doing. They asked him to unwrap the book and let them see it. When Tom opened the book, his parents were even more amazed. The book was full of beautiful stories and colorful pictures. They smiled warmly at Tom and told him how proud they were. Tom was so happy! He had found a special book in the dark forest and brought it home with him. He was proud of himself and never forgot that special day.

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat. The cat had a job. The job was to catch mice. The cat did not understand why he had to do this job. He did not like to catch mice. He wanted to be friends with them. One day, the cat met a mouse. The cat said, "Hello, mouse. I do not want to catch you. Let's be friends." The mouse said, "Okay, cat. I will be your friend." They played together and had fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The mouse told all his friends about the cat. The mice thought they could live in the house without fear. Soon, there were too many mice in the house. The people in the house were not happy. They said, "Cat, you did not do your job. We have too many mice now." The cat was sad. The cat and the mouse could not be friends anymore. The cat had to do his job and catch the mice. The cat and the mouse were not happy. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird. The bird liked to fly high in the sky. One day, while flying, the bird saw a dirty stone on the ground. The bird flew down to look at the stone. It was not shiny, but the bird thought it was special. The bird wanted to clean the dirty stone, so it could be shiny and pretty. The bird took the stone to a pond. It washed the stone in the water. The stone became clean and shiny. The bird was very happy and flew away with its new shiny stone.

Ben and Mia were playing in the park with their ball. They liked to kick the ball to each other and catch it. Sometimes the ball went too far and they had to run after it. One time, the ball went over the fence and into the street. Ben and Mia ran to the fence and looked for the ball. They saw it on the other side, near a big car. The car was shiny and smooth, but it had a scary face on the front. "Let's get the ball!" Ben said. "No, it's too dangerous!" Mia said. "The car might hurt us!" But Ben was brave and he wanted the ball. He climbed over the fence and ran to the car. He grabbed the ball and turned around. He saw the car's face move and make a loud noise. It was angry! "Help!" Ben shouted. "The car is a threat!" Mia heard Ben and she was scared. She saw the car move and try to catch Ben. She wanted to help him. She saw a big stick on the ground. She picked it up and threw it at the car. The stick hit the car's face and made a dent. The car made another noise and stopped. "Quick, Ben, escape!" Mia said. Ben ran back to the fence and climbed over it. He had the ball in his hand. He hugged Mia and thanked her. "You saved me!" Ben said. "You're welcome!" Mia said. "But don't do that again. It's not safe." They went back to the park and played with the ball. They stayed away from the fence and the car. They were happy and safe.
