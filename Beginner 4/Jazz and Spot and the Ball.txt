Category: Beginner
Title: Jazz and Spot and the Ball

One day, a cat named Jazz went to play with his friend, a dog named Spot. They liked to play ball in the park. The sun was out, and the sky was blue. Jazz and Spot were very happy. "Let's play catch," said Jazz. Spot nodded and wagged his tail. Jazz threw the ball, and Spot ran to catch it. They played and played until they were tired. But then, a rude boy came to the park. He saw Jazz and Spot playing and wanted to join. The boy tried to take the ball from Spot. Spot did not like this and let out a small growl. Jazz did not like the rude boy either. He wanted to protect his friend Spot, so he used his paw to slap the boy's hand. The boy was surprised and left the park. Jazz and Spot went back to playing their game. They were happy again, and the rude boy did not come back. They played until the sun went down and it was time to go home. That night, Jazz and Spot slept well, knowing they had stood up for each other. They were the best of friends, and they knew they would always be there for one another.

Once upon a time, there was a giant named Bob. Bob was very fast. He loved to run and play all day. One day, Bob met a little girl named Sally. Sally said, "Hi, Bob! Do you want to play with me?" Bob was happy and said, "Yes, Sally! Let's play!" But first, Sally wanted to dress up. She put on a pretty dress and a big hat. Bob wanted to dress up too. He found a big shirt and a funny hat. They both looked very nice. They played all day long. They ran fast, jumped high, and laughed a lot. Sally and Bob became best friends. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big ball game. Tom and his friends were playing. Tom was very good at kicking the ball. He wanted to win the game. The other team was strong too. They all wanted to reach the goal. Tom was feeling sleepy that day. He was not fast like the other days. His friends were sad. They needed Tom to kick the ball into the goal. The other team was close to winning. Tom's team needed one more goal to win. Tom closed his eyes for a bit. Then, he felt better. He ran fast to the ball. He kicked it very hard. The ball went into the goal! Tom's team won the game! All his friends were happy. They all went home to rest.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted little girl named Lucy. She was very good at making things with her hands. One day, Lucy wanted to make a big, pretty cake for her family. Lucy started to mix the cake, but she got her thumb stuck in the bowl. She tried to pull it out, but it was stuck very tight. She was sad and didn't know what to do. She had an idea to soak her hand in warm water to make her thumb come out. But when she put her hand in the water, the cake mix got all wet and ruined. Lucy was very sad because she couldn't make the cake for her family anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a big, round pumpkin. It lived on a farm with many other pumpkins. This pumpkin was very organized. It liked to keep things neat and tidy. One day, a little girl named Sara came to the farm. She wanted to find the best pumpkin to take home. She saw the organized pumpkin and said, "I want this one!" But the pumpkin said, "No, I insist you take my friend instead." Sara was surprised because pumpkins don't usually talk. Sara took the pumpkin's friend home and found out it was a magic pumpkin! It could change colors and even sing songs. Sara was so happy and thanked the organized pumpkin for insisting she take its friend. The organized pumpkin smiled, knowing it helped make Sara's day special.
