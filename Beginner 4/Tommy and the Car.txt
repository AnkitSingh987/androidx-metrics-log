Category: Beginner
Title: Tommy and the Car

Tommy liked to chew on things. He chewed on his toys, his books, his clothes, and sometimes even his mom's shoes. He liked the way they felt in his mouth and the sounds they made. But his mom did not like it. She said chewing on things was bad for his teeth and could make him sick. She told him to chew only on his food or his gum. One day, Tommy saw a new thing in his yard. It was a big, shiny, modern car. It belonged to his new neighbor, Mr. Lee. Mr. Lee had just moved in next door and he was very proud of his car. He said it was fast and smart and had many buttons and lights. Tommy was curious. He wanted to see the car up close. He waited until Mr. Lee went inside his house and then he ran to the car. He touched the car with his hands. It was smooth and cold. He looked at the car with his eyes. It was red and black and had a big logo on the front. He smelled the car with his nose. It had a strange and strong smell. He wondered how the car would taste. He opened his mouth and bit the car. "Ow!" Tommy cried. The car was hard and hurt his teeth. He spat out a piece of metal. He felt blood on his tongue. He heard a loud noise. It was Mr. Lee. He came out of his house and saw Tommy and his car. "What are you doing?" Mr. Lee shouted. "You bit my car! You ruined my car! You are a bad boy!" Tommy was scared and sorry. He ran to his mom and told her what happened. His mom was angry and sad. She took him to the dentist and then to Mr. Lee. She said sorry to Mr. Lee and paid for the damage. She said Tommy had to learn not to chew on things that were not food or gum. Tommy learned his lesson. He never chewed on anything but food or gum again. He also never went near Mr. Lee's car again. He found other things to do with his mouth, like talk and sing and laugh. He was a good boy.

One day there was a clever radio. It sang lovely songs and made everyone happy. But then something strange happened. The radio began to split in two. It split and split until the pieces were very small. Everyone was so sad to see their clever radio fall apart. But then a very special thing happened. A man came and fixed the radio, and soon it was singing again. Everyone was so happy and cheered that their clever radio was back!

A little girl was walking quickly towards a big case when she noticed something strange. She saw two people talking and arguing. She stopped to listen and heard one of them say, “It’s mine! I found it first.” The other one shrugged and said, “No way. It’s mine.” The little girl was curious, so she took a few steps closer. She saw that they were both trying to get the case open. She asked them each the same question: “What’s inside the case?” The first person shrugged and said, “It must be something valuable. I have to have it.” The second person said, “Same here. It must be mine.” The little girl was starting to worry, so she quickly ran away. She heard them arguing and fighting. Suddenly, she heard a loud bang! She looked back and saw that one of them had opened the case with a big kick. The person grabbed something from inside and ran away as fast as they could. The little girl sighed. She had wanted to find out what was in the case, but the bad ending was not what she had hoped for.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim was sad because he could not fly. All the other birds could soar high in the sky, but not Tim. He would watch them from his nest and wish he could join them. One day, a big fish named Sam swam up to Tim. Sam had a shiny scale that he thought was magical. He told Tim, "I think this scale can help you fly. Hold it tight and try to soar." Tim was excited and held the scale in his beak. Tim tried to fly with the scale. At first, he did not soar. But then, he flapped his wings harder and harder. Suddenly, Tim started to soar high in the sky! He was so happy and no longer sad. The scale had helped him fly, and now Tim could join his friends in the sky.
