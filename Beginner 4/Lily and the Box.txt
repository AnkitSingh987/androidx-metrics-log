Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a big, yellow box. A little girl named Lily found the box in her room. She was very happy. Lily thought the box would bring her fun things. One day, Lily decided to open the yellow box. She saw many toys inside. There was a ball, a doll, and a teddy bear. Lily played with the toys all day. She loved her yellow box. But one day, the yellow box was gone. Lily was very sad. She looked for the box everywhere. She asked her mom and dad, but they did not know where it was. In the end, Lily never found her yellow box again. She missed her toys and the fun the box brought her.

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn frog named Bob. Bob lived near a big pool. He loved to gather rocks and put them around the pool. Every day, he would gather more and more rocks. One day, Bob met a fish named Sue. Sue said, "Hi, Bob! Can I play with you?" Bob said, "Yes, but we have to gather rocks for the pool." Sue was happy to help. They gathered rocks together and put them around the pool. All of a sudden, a big rain came. The pool started to grow bigger and bigger. The rocks that Bob and Sue gathered moved away. They were surprised and said, "Oh no! Our rocks!" But then, they saw that the rain made a beautiful waterfall in the pool. They smiled and played in the waterfall, forgetting all about the rocks.

Lily and Tom were playing in the park with their dog, Spot. They liked to run and jump and slide on the grass. Spot liked to chase and bark and fetch the ball. They were having a lot of fun. But then the sky became dark and the wind became cold. Lily and Tom felt a shiver in their bodies. They looked at each other and said, "Let's go home. It's going to rain." They called Spot and put his tie around his neck. They started to walk back to their house. But on the way, they saw a big puddle. They wanted to splash in it, but they also wanted to stay dry. "What should we do?" Lily asked Tom. Tom thought for a moment and said, "I have an idea. Let's use our coats as umbrellas. We can hold them over our heads and still splash in the puddle." Lily liked Tom's idea. They took off their coats and held them over their heads. They ran to the puddle and jumped in it. They laughed and splashed and made waves. Spot joined them and shook his fur. They didn't care about the rain. They had a lot of fun in the puddle. But after a while, they felt cold and wet. They put on their coats and said, "Let's go home. We need a warm bath and a hot drink." They called Spot and walked back to their house. They saw their mom waiting for them at the door. She smiled and said, "You look like you had a lot of fun. But you also look like you need a warm bath and a hot drink. Come in, come in. You'll be safe and cozy inside." Lily and Tom hugged their mom and said, "Thank you, mom. We love you." They went inside with Spot and felt safe and cozy. They had a warm bath and a hot drink. They told their mom about their adventure in the park and the puddle. They felt happy and sleepy. They went to bed and dreamed of more fun.

One day, a big dinosaur named Dino was walking in the sun. It was very hot outside. Dino started to feel dizzy because of the heat. He sat down under a big tree to rest. The tree gave him shade and he felt better. Dino saw a big ice cream cone on the ground. The ice cream was melting because it was so hot. Dino wanted to eat the ice cream before it all melted away. He picked up the ice cream cone and took a big bite. It was very tasty and cold. After eating the ice cream, Dino felt dizzy again. He thought maybe he ate the ice cream too fast. Dino sat under the tree again and took a nap. When he woke up, he felt much better. Dino was happy that he found the ice cream and had a nice nap under the tree.
