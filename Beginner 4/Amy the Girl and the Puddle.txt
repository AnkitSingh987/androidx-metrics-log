Category: Beginner
Title: Amy the Girl and the Puddle

Once upon a time, in a small, tidy town, there lived a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to play outside with her friends. One sunny day, after the rain had stopped, Amy saw a big puddle near her house. She thought it would be fun to jump in it. Amy ran to gather her friends, Tim and Sue, to play in the puddle with her. They all jumped and splashed in the puddle, laughing and having a great time. But soon, they noticed that the water from the puddle had made the tidy town very messy. Amy, Tim, and Sue decided to clean up the mess they had made. They worked together to make the town tidy again. The people in the town were very happy and thanked the children for their hard work. The moral of the story is that it is important to clean up after ourselves and to work together to keep our world tidy and beautiful.

Once upon a time, there was a young girl named Lily. She had a big, red ball. She loved to play with her ball every day. It was her favorite toy. One day, while playing with her ball, it rolled far away from her. Lily ran after it, but it went into a big, green forest. She was scared, but she wanted her ball back. So, she went into the forest to find it. As Lily walked through the forest, she met a big, friendly bear. The bear said, "Hello, Lily. Can I help you?" Lily told the bear about her lost ball. The bear said, "I know where your ball is. Follow me." They walked together, and the bear led Lily to her ball. Lily was so happy to find her ball. She said, "Thank you, bear!" And she gave the bear a big kiss on his nose. The bear smiled and said, "You're welcome, Lily. Have fun playing with your ball." And they both went back to their homes. From that day on, Lily and the bear became best friends.

Tom and Lily went to the lake with Mom and Dad. It was a hot day and they wanted to swim and play. They put on their swim suits and ran to the water. "Look, Tom, I can throw this stick very far!" Lily said. She picked up a long stick and threw it into the lake. It made a big splash. "Wow, that was good, Lily! But I can throw it farther!" Tom said. He grabbed the stick and threw it again. It flew over the water and landed on the other side. "Hey, that's not fair, Tom! You are bigger than me!" Lily said. She pouted and crossed her arms. "Sorry, Lily. I didn't mean to make you sad. Here, you can have the stick back." Tom said. He swam to the other side and got the stick. He swam back and gave it to Lily. "Thank you, Tom. You are a nice brother." Lily said. She smiled and hugged Tom. "You're welcome, Lily. You are a nice sister." Tom said. He hugged Lily back. Mom and Dad watched them and smiled. They were happy to see Tom and Lily share and have fun. They joined them in the water and they all swam and played together. It was a good day at the lake.

Tim and Tom are brothers. They like to play hunter in the woods. They have sticks for guns and hats for helmets. They run and hide and shoot at each other. One day, they see a big deer in the grass. It has brown fur and long horns. Tim and Tom want to catch it. They run after it with their sticks. The deer is fast and jumps over a fence. Tim and Tom follow it. "Stop, deer, stop!" Tim shouts. "We are hunters and we want you!" But the deer does not stop. It runs into a farm. There are many animals in the farm. There are cows and pigs and chickens and ducks. The deer runs among them and makes them noisy. "Hey, what are you doing here?" a farmer says. He sees Tim and Tom with their sticks. He is angry. "You are not hunters, you are troublemakers! Go away from my farm!" Tim and Tom are scared. They drop their sticks and run away. They run back to the woods. They are tired and out of breath. "Wow, that was a busy adventure," Tom says. "We almost caught the deer." "Yes, but we also made the farmer mad," Tim says. "Maybe we should not play hunter anymore." They find a big tree and sit under it. They rest and watch the sky. They see birds and clouds and the sun. They are happy and quiet. The end.
