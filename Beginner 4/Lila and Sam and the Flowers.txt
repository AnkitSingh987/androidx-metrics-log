Category: Beginner
Title: Lila and Sam and the Flowers

Lila and Sam were twins who liked to play outside. They had a big yard with many trees and flowers. They also had a swing that their dad made for them. They loved to swing high and see the view of the sky and the hills. One day, they decided to have a contest. They wanted to see who could swing higher and yell louder. They took turns pushing each other on the swing and yelling as loud as they could. They had fun and laughed a lot. But then, something happened. Lila pushed Sam too hard and he flew off the swing. He landed on the grass and hurt his knee. He started to cry and yell for help. Lila was scared and sorry. She ran to him and hugged him. "I'm sorry, Sam. I didn't mean to push you so hard. Are you okay?" she asked. Sam sniffed and looked at his knee. It was red and bleeding. He felt pain and anger. He pushed Lila away and yelled at her. "Go away, Lila. You are mean. You hurt me. I don't want to play with you anymore." he said. Lila felt sad and hurt. She didn't want to lose her brother. She tried to talk to him and say sorry again. But he didn't listen. He turned his back on her and walked away. Lila didn't know what to do. She looked at the swing and the view. They were not beautiful anymore. They were lonely and sad. She wished she could make Sam happy again. She wished they could swing and yell and laugh together again. She wished they could be twins again.

Once upon a time, there was a pink bunny named Binky. Binky loved to eat broccoli every day. One day, he found a big box in the forest. Binky was very curious, so he decided to open the box. Inside the box, he found lots and lots of broccoli! Binky was so happy and started to eat all the broccoli. But then, something unexpected happened. The more broccoli Binky ate, the bigger he grew! Binky realized that sharing is important. So, he shared his broccoli with all his friends in the forest. They all became big and strong together. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a brave dog named Max saw a big grill. The grill was hot and had yummy food on it. Max wanted to eat the food, but he knew it was not safe. A mean cat named Tom saw Max looking at the grill. Tom told Max, "I will eat the food, and you can't stop me!" Max did not want Tom to get hurt, but Tom did not listen. Max tried to tell Tom it was not safe, but Tom ignored Max. Max knew he had to be brave and stop Tom. Max barked very loud and scared Tom away from the grill. Tom was mad, but Max was happy he saved Tom from getting hurt. Max was a brave dog, and he knew that it was better to be safe than to eat the yummy food on the grill.

Once upon a time, there was a sailor named Tom. He had an incredible big boat with a big sail. One sunny day, Tom went out on his boat to sail on the blue sea. As he was sailing, Tom saw a big fish jump out of the water. "Wow!" said Tom. "That fish is so big and incredible!" The fish jumped again and this time, it hit the sail. The sail got a big tear in it. Tom was sad and worried. He did not know how to fix the sail. Just then, a friendly bird flew by. "Don't be sad," said the bird. "I can help you fix the sail!" The bird used its beak to sew the sail back together. Tom was so happy and thankful. The unexpected help from the bird made it an incredible day for Tom. They sailed away together, and Tom and the bird became the best of friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. It was a hot day. Tim saw a strip of grass. He wanted to stand on it and play. A big boy named Sam was there too. Sam said, "You can't stand here. This is my spot!" Tim felt sad. He wanted to play on the strip of grass. Tim said, "Can we share?" Sam thought for a bit. Then he said, "Okay, we can share." They both stood on the strip of grass. They played and had fun together. And they became friends.
