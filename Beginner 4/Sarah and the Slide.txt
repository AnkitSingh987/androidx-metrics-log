Category: Beginner
Title: Sarah and the Slide

Once upon a time there was a pretty girl called Sarah. She was so excited because today was her very special day. Sarah's mummy took her to the park, which made her even more excited. Together, they ran to the big slide, which Sarah had never been on before. She ran all the way to the top and looked down, but she was scared. Mummy kneeled down so Sarah could see mummy's face. Mummy said, "Do not be scared, Sarah. Just close your eyes and go down the slide, it will be fun!" Sarah closed her eyes and she went down. It felt like a cut, and when she opened her eyes she reached the bottom safely. Sarah smiled and looked up at mummy, who was waving and clapping. Sarah had a big smile on her face, she was so proud and happy. Everyone watching said "That was pretty brave, Sarah!" That evening they went home with lots of happy memories.

Once upon a time, there was a fierce dog named Max. Max loved to play ball with his friend, a little boy named Tim. They played every day in the big park. One sunny day, Tim said, "Max, let's play ball! Our goal is to tear the ball!" Max barked and wagged his tail. They ran to the park and started to play. Max was very good at catching the ball. Tim threw the ball high into the sky. Max jumped up and caught the ball with a big chomp. The ball had a tear! Tim clapped and said, "Good job, Max! We reached our goal!" They both laughed and played until the sun went down.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly lady named Sue. Sue loved to dance. Every day, she would dance in her small house. One day, Sue saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad because she did not know how to dance. Sue wanted to help Lily. She took Lily's hand and said, "Let's dance together!" Sue and Lily danced and danced. They had so much fun. Lily was not sad anymore. Sue was happy to help her new friend. And from that day on, they danced together every day.

Tom and Anna liked to play with cardboard boxes. They had many boxes of different sizes and shapes. They used them to make forts, cars, rockets and more. One day, they decided to make a big truck with a cardboard box. They cut out a window and a door in the box. They drew wheels and lights with crayons. They found a long stick and tied it to the front of the box. It was the truck's horn. They were very proud of their truck. "Let's go for a ride!" Tom said. He climbed into the box and sat behind the window. He held the stick and pretended to steer. "Okay!" Anna said. She got some of their toys and put them in the back of the box. She loaded the box with dolls, cars, books and balls. She wanted to take them all with her. She climbed into the box and sat next to Tom. "Beep beep!" Tom said. He pushed the stick and made a loud noise. He moved the box with his feet and started to roll. Anna laughed and waved to their mom, who was watching from the window. They rolled down the sidewalk and saw many things. They saw a dog, a bird, a flower and a bike. They said hello to everyone they met. They had a lot of fun. But then, they came to a hill. It was very steep and high. Tom and Anna looked at each other. They were scared. "Can we go down?" Tom asked. "I don't know," Anna said. "It looks wild." They thought for a moment. Then, they decided to be brave. They held each other's hands and smiled. "Let's do it!" Tom said. "Let's do it!" Anna said. They pushed the stick and made a loud noise. They let go of their feet and started to roll faster and faster. They felt the wind in their hair and the sun on their faces. They screamed and laughed and cheered. They rolled down the hill and came to a stop at the bottom. They were safe and happy. They looked at their truck and saw that it was still in one piece. They looked at their toys and saw that they were still in the box. They looked at each other and hugged. "That was awesome!" Tom said. "That was awesome!" Anna said. They got out of the box and ran back to their mom. They told her all about their adventure. She smiled and hugged them. "I'm glad you had fun," she said. "But next time, be careful." They nodded and said sorry. They thanked her for the cardboard box. They asked her if they could play with it again tomorrow. She said yes. They said yay. They went inside and had a snack. They were very happy. They loved their cardboard truck.
