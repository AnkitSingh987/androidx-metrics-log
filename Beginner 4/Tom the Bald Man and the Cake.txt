Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Bald Man and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. Tom liked to mix things. One day, Tom was in his kitchen mixing things in a big bowl. He was making a cake for his friend's birthday. Tom's little dog, Spot, walked into the kitchen. Spot saw Tom and said, "What are you doing, Tom?" Tom smiled and said, "I am mixing a cake, Spot. Do you want to help me?" Spot wagged his tail and barked, "Yes, I want to help!" Tom and Spot mixed the cake together. They took a big breath with their lungs and blew out the candles on the cake. They laughed and ate the cake. It was a fun day for Tom and Spot.

One day, a sweet little boy named Tim found a toy gun in his room. He liked to play with it because it made fun sounds when he pressed the button. Tim's mom saw him playing and smiled. She knew he loved his toy gun very much. Tim wanted to take his toy gun outside to play with his friends. But when he ran out the door, he did not see that his toy gun was caught on a chair. The toy gun was pulled and it started to tear. Tim stopped and looked at his toy gun. He was sad because it was tearing and might break. Tim's mom saw what happened and came to help. She took the toy gun and fixed the tear with some tape. Tim was happy again and thanked his mom. He went outside to play with his friends and his toy gun, being more careful this time.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He loved to search for fun things in his yard. One day, while he was searching, he found a big nail. The nail was deep in the ground, so he pulled it out. Tim wanted to show his mom the big nail he found. But as he walked to the house, something unexpected happened. The ground started to shake, and a big hole appeared where the nail had been. Tim was surprised and a little scared. Then, a friendly rabbit jumped out of the hole. The rabbit said, "Thank you for taking out the nail. It was keeping me trapped in my home." Tim was happy he could help the rabbit. From that day on, Tim and the rabbit became best friends, and they searched for more fun things together.

Once there was a little girl called Tara. Tara had long brown hair and she was wearing a pretty skirt. Tara was very hungry, so she started to cry. Tears ran down her cheeks as she felt so sad. Just then, she heard a soft noise coming from outside. She went to the window to take a look. To her surprise, she saw a big red box full of treats. Tara was so happy that she ran outside. Once she reached the box, she opened it up and found lots of delicious things. She found some yummy apples, bread and sweets. Tara ate all of the yummy treats and felt much better. She had a big smile on her face as she ran around, twirling her pretty skirt. Tara was happy and full and she never had to cry again because of being hungry.

Once upon a time, there was a gray sheep. The gray sheep had lots of soft wool. The gray sheep liked to play with his friends. One day, the gray sheep saw a big slide. The gray sheep wanted to slide down with his friends. So, the gray sheep and his friends went to the slide. The gray sheep went up and then slid down. The soft wool made the slide very fast. The gray sheep and his friends had lots of fun. They played on the slide all day long.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a letter on the floor. The letter was big and red. It looked great! Tim picked up the letter and saw that it had his name on it. He was very happy. Tim opened the letter and found a cookie inside. The cookie was shaped like a star. It smelled so good! Tim decided to taste the cookie. He took a bite and it was the best cookie he ever had. Tim felt great! Tim shared the cookie with his friends. They all loved it too. They all agreed it was a great cookie. They laughed and played together, feeling happy about the special letter and the tasty cookie.
