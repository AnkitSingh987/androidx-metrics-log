Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Hat

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play outside. One day, they saw a lot of snow on the ground. They put on their warm clothes and boots and ran outside. They wanted to make a snowman and have a snowball fight. They found a big spot of snow and started to roll it into a ball. They made another ball and put it on top. Then they found a carrot, a hat and some buttons to make the snowman's face and clothes. They were very happy with their snowman. "Look, our snowman is so big and nice!" Tom said. "Yes, he is! Let's give him a name. How about Bob?" Lily said. "OK, Bob it is. Hi, Bob!" Tom said. They hugged their snowman and then decided to have some fun. They made some snowballs and threw them at each other. They laughed and dodged and ran around. They had a lot of fun. But then, they saw a big dog coming towards them. The dog was black and furry and had a long tail. He looked friendly, but he also looked curious. He saw the snowman and ran towards it. "Hey, stop! That's our snowman!" Tom shouted. But the dog did not stop. He jumped on the snowman and started to bite and pull and shake. He tore off the hat and the carrot and the buttons. He made the snowman fall apart. Tom and Lily were very sad and angry. They ran to the dog and tried to make him go away. But the dog was too strong and too fast. He ran away with the hat and the carrot and the buttons. "Bad dog! Bad dog!" Tom and Lily cried. They looked at their snowman. He was gone. There was only a pile of snow left. They felt like crying. They went back to their house. They told their mom what happened. Their mom hugged them and said: "I'm sorry, my loves. That was a naughty dog. He did not know that the snowman was your friend. He thought it was a toy. He did not mean to hurt you." "But he ruined our snowman! We worked so hard to make him!" Tom said. "I know, honey. But you can make another one. The snow is still there. And maybe you can find a hidden spot where the dog can't see it. Then your snowman will be safe." Mom said. "Really? Can we do that?" Lily asked. "Of course, you can. But first, let's have some hot chocolate and cookies. You must be cold and hungry." Mom said. "OK, Mom. Thank you. We love you." Tom and Lily said. They went inside and had some hot chocolate and cookies. They felt better. They learned that sometimes things can go wrong, but they can also try again. They also learned that they should be careful with their things and not leave them where others can take them. They decided to make another snowman the next day, in a hidden spot. They hoped that the dog would not find it. They hoped that Bob would be happy.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to play outside. She liked to wander around her big yard. There were many interesting things to see and do. Sue saw a big tree, a pretty flower, and a small bug. As she wandered, she found a small pit in the ground. Sue looked at the pit and wondered what was inside. She asked her friend, a little bird, "What is in the pit?" The bird said, "I don't know. Let's find out!" They looked into the pit and saw a shiny rock. Sue picked up the rock and showed it to the bird. They were both happy with their interesting find. Sue and the bird went back to play near the big tree. They had a fun day together, playing with the shiny rock and exploring the yard. They knew they would always remember the day they found the interesting pit.

Once upon a time, in a big green park, there was a small spring. The water in the spring was smooth and clear. Many animals came to drink water from the spring. One sunny day, a little rabbit named Remy wanted to learn how to jump like the big rabbits. He asked his mom to teach him. She said, "Remy, you need to be strong and fast to jump like the big rabbits." Remy was so excited to learn. Remy tried to jump, but he could not jump high. He felt sad. Then, Remy saw a big frog named Fred. Fred was good at jumping. Remy asked Fred to help him. Fred said, "Drink some smooth water from the spring, Remy. It will make you strong." Remy drank the smooth water from the spring. He felt strong. Fred and Remy practiced jumping together. Soon, Remy could jump high like the big rabbits. Remy was very happy, and he thanked Fred for helping him. From that day on, Remy and Fred were best friends, and they played in the big green park every day.
