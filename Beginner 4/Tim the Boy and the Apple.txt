Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Apple

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to play baseball. He took his ball and bat and went to the park. In the park, he saw a big tree with yummy apples on it. He wanted to get an apple, but the tree was too tall. A girl named Sue saw Tim and asked, "Why are you sad?" Tim said, "I want an apple from the tree, but I can't reach it." Sue had an idea. "Let's use your baseball and bat to get an apple!" Tim liked the idea. Tim tried to hit the baseball at the apples. He missed a few times, but he did not give up. Finally, he hit an apple and it fell down. Tim and Sue were so happy. They shared the yummy apple and then played baseball together. Tim was glad he succeeded in getting the apple and making a new friend.

One day, a little boy named Tim had a dizzy feeling. He did not like it. He told his mom, "Mom, I feel dizzy." His mom said, "Don't worry, it will go away soon." That night, Tim had a bad dream, a nightmare. In the dream, he was trying to gain a big toy. But the toy was on top of a high hill. He tried to climb the hill, but the dizzy feeling made it hard. Tim woke up and was scared. He told his mom about the nightmare. His mom hugged him and said, "It's okay, Tim. It was just a dream. Let's find a way to make the dizzy feeling go away." They went to the doctor the next day. The doctor gave Tim some medicine to help with the dizzy feeling. Tim was happy that he could play without feeling dizzy anymore. And he did not have any more nightmares about the high hill.

One day, Sammy was in the kitchen playing with his toys. He saw his Mom struggling to turn on the stove. Sammy wanted to help! He ran over and said: "Mommy, let me help you!" Mommy smiled and said: "Okay, Sammy. Can you turn the knob?" Sammy, excited to help, started to turn the knob. He tried and tried, but he couldn’t turn it all the way. Sammy was struggling, but his Mom reminded him not to give up. Suddenly, a colorful light shone from inside the stove and the stove was working! Sammy smiled and was so happy. Mommy said: "Good job, Sammy! You did it!" Sammy was proud of himself. He had helped his Mom turn on the stove. Sammy felt so happy and excited!

Once upon a time, in a big park, there was a kind girl named Amy. Amy loved to step and jump and play with her friends. They had lots of fun every day. One day, while Amy and her friends were playing, they saw a big box. "What is in the box?" they asked. They were very excited to see what was inside. They thought it would be more fun to play with. Amy and her friends opened the box, and out came a big, wet frog. The frog was not kind. It jumped on Amy and her friends. They were not happy. The frog made their fun day not fun anymore. The frog did not want to be friends. It jumped away and left Amy and her friends sad. They learned that not all surprises are good. Sometimes, things that look fun can be not fun at all.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big brother named Sam. Tim and Sam liked to play in the park. One day, Tim and Sam went to the park. Tim saw a big dog. He asked Sam, "What is that?" Sam just shrugged and said, "I don't know, Tim." They went closer to see the dog. The dog had a bowl with some sauce in it. Tim and Sam thought the dog looked hungry. They decided to give the dog some more sauce. The dog was happy and wagged its tail. Tim and Sam played with the dog for a while. Then, they went back home. They told their mom about the dog and the sauce. Their mom smiled and said, "You did a good thing, boys." Tim and Sam were happy to have a new friend in the park.
