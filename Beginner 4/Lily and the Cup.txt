Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Cup

Lily liked to play with her toys in the bath. She had a seal that could swim and squirt water. She also had a cup that she could fill and pour. Lily wanted to see if the seal could fit in the cup. She pushed and pushed, but the seal was too big. The seal got angry and bit Lily's finger. Lily cried and dropped the seal and the cup in the water. Mom heard Lily cry and came to the bathroom. She saw the seal and the cup in the bath. She asked Lily what happened. Lily said, "Seal angry, seal bite me, seal no fit in cup." Mom picked up Lily and hugged her. She said, "I'm sorry, Lily. The seal is not a toy for the cup. The seal needs space to swim and play. The cup is for water, not for animals. Let's put the seal back in the toy box and find another toy for the bath." Lily nodded and wiped her tears. She said, "Okay, Mom. I'm sorry, seal. I won't try to fit you in the cup again." Mom smiled and kissed Lily's cheek. She said, "That's good, Lily. I'm proud of you for saying sorry. Now, how about this duck? Do you think the duck can fit in the cup?" Lily giggled and said, "No, Mom. The duck is too big, too. The duck can swim with the seal. The cup can pour water on them." Mom said, "That sounds like fun. Let's try it." Lily and Mom played with the duck, the seal, and the cup in the bath. They had a lot of fun and forgot about the angry seal.

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a jog in the park. She loved to jog and play outside. Sue saw a big tree and under the tree, she found a muffin. It was a yummy muffin with blue spots. She was very happy to find it. Sue was a little fearful to eat the muffin. She thought, "What if this muffin is not good?" But she was very hungry, so she took a small bite. As soon as she ate it, something unexpected happened. Sue felt very funny and started to jump high, very high! Sue could now jump as high as the big tree! She was not fearful anymore. She knew that the muffin was a magic muffin. Sue had fun jumping high and playing in the park. She was happy that she ate the magic muffin.

There was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy box upstairs. One day, he took his sister's toy without asking. He felt guilty. Tim's sister, Sue, was sad. She could not find her toy. Tim saw Sue's sad face and felt bad. He wanted to forgive himself and make Sue happy. Tim went upstairs and got the toy. He gave it back to Sue and said sorry. Sue smiled and forgave Tim. They played together and were happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and her cat, Fluffy. Lucy loved to play outside, but today it was very cold. She put on her warm coat, hat, and gloves to go outside. Lucy found a box with numbers on it. She saw the numbers one, two, and three. She thought it was a fun game to change the numbers. She changed the number one to two, and then changed the number two to three. Fluffy the cat watched her play. As Lucy changed the numbers, she noticed that the cold air started to go away. She was very happy because she could play outside without feeling cold. Lucy and Fluffy played all day, and they knew that changing the numbers made their day so much better.

One day, a messy cat named Tom was playing in the kitchen. He saw a faucet with water coming out. Tom wanted to play with the water, so he put his paw under the faucet. The water made his paw wet and he liked it. Then, he jumped and turned the faucet more, so more water came out. The water made the floor wet, too. Tom's friend, a little bird named Tim, saw the wet floor and said, "Tom, you made a big mess! We need to clean it up." Tom nodded and they both started to wipe the floor with a towel. They wiped and wiped until the floor was dry. Just then, Tom's mom came into the kitchen. She saw the dry floor and said, "Good job, Tom and Tim! You cleaned up the mess you made. Remember, it's important to clean up after ourselves." Tom and Tim smiled, happy that they did the right thing. They learned that it's good to fix their mistakes and keep their home clean.
