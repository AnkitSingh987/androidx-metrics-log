Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Frog

One day, a girl named Sue put on her red skirt. She went to play in the open park. Sue saw her friend, Tim. Tim had a toy frog. They played together and laughed. Then, a big wind came. It blew Sue's skirt up. Sue felt sad and Tim laughed. Sue said, "Don't laugh at me, Tim!" Tim did not stop, so Sue slapped Tim's toy frog out of his hand. The toy frog flew far away. Tim and Sue looked for it, but they could not find it. Tim cried and said, "You made me lose my toy frog!" Sue felt bad, but she could not fix it. They both went home sad. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a very bright computer. It seemed like it had the power to do anything. Two children, Jake and Jane, watched the computer with amazement. "What can it do?" said Jane. "It can do a lot of things," said Jake. "It can make things move backwards." The children were still confused. "What happens when things move backwards?" asked Jane. "It's called reverse," said Jake. "Let's give it a try!" The children put their hands on the computer and watched as it reversed some of its actions. All of a sudden, the children started laughing and enjoyed the bright reverse effects. They had fun reversing all sorts of things, from moving cars to bouncing balls. The children were amazed by the power of the computer and had found a way to make things more difficult and fun!

Once upon a time, there was a normal boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys. One day, his mom told him they were going to the circus. Tim was very happy. At the circus, Tim saw a funny clown. The clown had a big red nose. The clown said, "Hi, Tim! Do you want to see a trick?" Tim said, "Yes, please!" The clown told Tim, "First, I will remove my hat. Then, I will show you something amazing!" The clown took off his hat, and a tiny car came out of it. The car went round and round. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. The clown smiled and said, "I hope you enjoyed my trick, Tim!" Tim said, "Thank you, Mr. Clown! That was the best trick ever!" From that day on, Tim loved the circus even more. He always remembered the funny clown and his amazing trick. And every time he saw a tiny car, he thought of the clown who removed his hat and made him laugh.

One day, a green lion was walking in the forest. He was looking for a friend to play with. He saw a big tree and heard a small voice. The voice was coming from a little bird. The bird asked the lion, "Do you want to play with me?" The lion was happy and said, "Yes, I want to play with you!" But the bird was scared of the lion because he was so big. The bird said, "I will play with you if you can answer my question. What is your favorite color?" The lion thought for a moment and said, "My favorite color is green, just like me!" The bird was happy with the lion's answer and they started to play together. They played hide and seek and had a lot of fun. But then, a big storm came and it started to rain. The lion and the bird were scared. They needed to find a safe place to hide. The lion said, "Let's go under that big tree to stay dry." They both ran to the tree and stayed there until the storm was over. After the storm, they continued playing and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a big bottle of lotion. She liked the lotion because it was useful. It made her skin feel soft and nice. One day, Sue wanted to play outside. She put on her shoes and went out. She saw a sad little bug on a leaf. The bug had dry skin. Sue felt bad for the bug. She let out a sigh. Sue had an idea. She went back inside and got her lotion. She put a little bit of lotion on her finger. Then, she gently rubbed it on the bug's skin. The bug felt better and smiled. Sue was happy she could help. The bug thanked Sue and they became friends. They played together all day long.
