Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Sam

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play at the beach with his friend, Sam. They liked to play in the sand and count how many shells they could find. One day, Tim and Sam were playing at the beach when Sam said, "Let's spin around and see who can do it the longest!" They started spinning and spinning until they both felt dizzy. Tim fell down in the sand and laughed. Sam stopped spinning and looked at Tim. "Are you okay?" he asked. Tim nodded and said, "Yes, but I don't want to spin anymore. It made me dizzy." Sam agreed and they went back to counting shells. They learned that it's better to play safely and not do things that can make them feel dizzy or hurt.

Once upon a time, there was a small planet. On this planet, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim had a difficult time going to sleep at night. He would toss and turn in his bed, but he just could not fall asleep. One night, Tim met a friendly alien named Zog. Zog was from another planet far, far away. Zog asked Tim, "Why can't you sleep?" Tim said, "I don't know. It's just difficult for me." Zog wanted to help Tim. So, Zog sang a soft and sweet song to Tim. The song made Tim feel very sleepy. Soon, Tim fell fast asleep. Every night, Zog would come and sing the song to help Tim sleep. Tim was very happy, and he slept well every night.

Lily and Ben liked to play with their toys. They had cars, dolls, blocks and books. One day, they found a new toy in the closet. It was a big black box with a red button and a long wire. It had a small round thing at the end of the wire. "What is this?" Lily asked. "I don't know. Maybe it is a record," Ben said. "A record? What is a record?" Lily wondered. "Maybe it is something that makes sounds. Let's try it," Ben said. He pressed the red button and spoke into the small round thing. "Hello, hello, this is Ben. Can you hear me?" Then he let go of the button and pressed it again. He heard his own voice coming out of the box. "Hello, hello, this is Ben. Can you hear me?" Lily and Ben laughed. They thought it was funny to hear their voices. They took turns to press the button and say different things. They said their names, their favorite colors, their favorite animals and their favorite songs. They played with the record for a long time. They did not notice that their mom came into the room. She smiled and watched them. "Hi, kids. What are you doing?" she asked. "We are playing with the record," Lily said. "The record? That is not a record. That is a tape recorder. It is a toy that can record and play back sounds. Do you like it?" mom explained. Lily and Ben looked at each other. They were confused. They did not know what a tape recorder was. They did not know what record and play back meant. They looked at the box and the wire and the small round thing. They did not understand how it worked. They looked at mom and shrugged. "We like it. It is fun," they said. Mom nodded and hugged them. "I'm glad you like it. It was my toy when I was a little girl. You can keep it and play with it as much as you want. But be careful not to break it or lose it. And don't forget to put it away when you are done," she said. "Okay, mom. Thank you, mom," Lily and Ben said. They gave mom a kiss and went back to playing with the tape recorder. They decided to call it the record anyway. They liked that name better. They thought it was a magic toy that could make their voices sound funny. They did not notice that mom took a picture of them with her phone. She wanted to record the moment and share it with dad. She thought they were adorable.
