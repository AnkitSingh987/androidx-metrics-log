Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Fish and the Bird

One day, a big fish and a small fish were talking. The big fish said, "I want to solve a problem." The small fish asked, "What problem?" The big fish said, "I want to find a fair way to share food." The two fish swam around the sea, looking for food. They found a big, yummy worm. The big fish said, "I am bigger, so I should eat more." The small fish was sad but agreed. They shared the worm, but the big fish ate most of it. Just then, a very big bird swooped down and tried to catch the big fish. The small fish was quick and helped the big fish hide. The bird flew away, and the big fish said, "Thank you, small fish. You saved me. I was wrong. We should share food in a fair way." From that day on, the two fish shared their food equally and were the best of friends.

Once upon a time there was a big boy. He was three years old. He was so excited to take a step out of his house one day. He was ready to take a big step. When he stepped outside, he saw something big and exciting. It was on the news. There were lots of people and they were so happy. The boy wanted to know what was happening, so he stepped closer to the news people. He joined the crowd and they all stepped together. They were all celebrating. The boy was so excited, he jumped up and down. He found out that it was a big birthday party for him! He was so happy and all the people celebrated with him.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim went to the park. The park had a big slide, a swing, and a sandbox. Tim loved to play at the park with his friends. One day, it was very wet at the park. The rain made the slide and the swing slippery. Tim's mom said, "Be careful, Tim!" Tim and his friends played in the wet sandbox. They made a big, wet sand castle. Then, Tim had an idea. He turned to his friends and said, "Let's play in the puddles!" So, they all took turns jumping in the wet puddles. They had so much fun at the wet park that day.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Buddy. Buddy had a diary. He liked to cut paper and put it in his diary. Every day, Buddy would play with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. One day, Buddy and Lily were playing in the park. Buddy saw a big tree and wanted to cut some paper from it for his diary. But when he tried to cut the paper, something unexpected happened. The tree started to talk! It said, "Please don't cut my paper, Buddy. I need it to grow big and strong." Buddy and Lily were very surprised. They promised not to cut the tree's paper anymore. Instead, they became friends with the tree and played together every day. And Buddy found other paper for his diary.

Sara and Ben are playing in the park. They see a big slide and run to it. Sara wants to go first, but Ben is faster. He grabs Sara's arm and pulls her back. "Let me go, Ben! I want to slide!" Sara says. "No, I want to slide! You are slow!" Ben says. They start to fight and push each other. A lady sees them and comes over. She has a kind face and a big bag. "Hello, children. Why are you fighting?" she asks. "He grabbed my arm and did not let me slide!" Sara says. "She wanted to slide before me and I was here first!" Ben says. The lady smiles and opens her bag. She takes out two books and shows them to Sara and Ben. "Look, I have some books for you. They are very nice and have many pictures. Do you like books?" she asks. Sara and Ben stop fighting and look at the books. They like books very much. They nod and say yes. "Good. Then I will give you each one book, if you promise to share the slide and be nice to each other. Can you do that?" she asks. Sara and Ben think for a moment. They want the books, but they also want to slide. They look at each other and decide to be friends. "Okay, we will share the slide and be nice. Thank you for the books, lady. You are very generous!" they say. The lady gives them each a book and a hug. She tells them to enjoy the slide and the books. She waves goodbye and walks away. Sara and Ben smile and hug each other. They take turns to slide and read the books. They have a lot of fun and learn new words. They are happy and grateful.
