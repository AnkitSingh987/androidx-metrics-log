Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben Find the Mom

Anna and Ben like to play at the beach. They make sand castles and look for shells. One day, they see a big store near the water. It has many pretty things inside. They want to go and see. "Can we go to the store, Mom?" Anna asks. "Not now, dear. Maybe later. The store is for grown-ups. You have to be careful near the water. There are big waves today," Mom says. But Anna and Ben do not listen. They sneak away from Mom and run to the store. They see shiny toys, colorful clothes, and sweet candies. They want to touch everything. "Look, Ben, a doll! She has a pretty dress and long hair. Can I have her?" Anna says. "I don't know, Anna. Maybe we should go back. Mom will be mad," Ben says. But Anna does not listen. She takes the doll and runs out of the store. She does not see the big wave coming. It crashes on the shore and knocks her down. She drops the doll and cries. "Anna, are you okay?" Ben says. He helps her get up and looks for the doll. But the doll is gone. The wave took her away. "Mom, Mom, help!" Anna and Ben shout. Mom hears them and runs to them. She hugs them and checks if they are hurt. "What were you doing at the store? You know you are not supposed to go there. You could have been in trouble. The store owner could have called the police. The wave could have hurt you. And you took something that was not yours. That is wrong," Mom says. "I'm sorry, Mom. I wanted the doll. She was so pretty. But I lost her. The wave took her away," Anna says. "I'm sorry, too, Mom. I should have stopped Anna. But I wanted to see the store. It had so many pretty things," Ben says. Mom sighs. She is glad they are safe, but she is also sad. She says, "I know you like pretty things, but you have to learn to be happy with what you have. And you have to respect other people's things. And you have to listen to me when I tell you something. I tell you things for your own good. I love you and I want you to be safe and good. Do you understand?" "Yes, Mom. We understand. We love you, too. We are sorry. Can you forgive us?" Anna and Ben say. Mom smiles. She says, "Of course I can forgive you. But you have to promise me you will not do that again. And you have to say sorry to the store owner, too. And you have to do something nice for someone else. That is how you can make up for your mistake. Can you do that?" "Yes, Mom. We can do that. Thank you, Mom," Anna and Ben say. They hug Mom and walk back to their spot on the beach. They see their sand castle still standing. They see some shells they can collect. They see the sun shining. They see the waves splashing. They think the beach is pretty. They think they are lucky. They think they have learned a lesson. They think they will be good.

Once upon a time, there was a rat who had a home in a cozy little hole. Every day, the rat would keep exploring and looking for food. One day, the rat went a bit too far and found itself in a place it had never seen before. The rat kept exploring until it came to the edge of a steep cliff. Below the cliff was a deep, dark, harsh cave. Afraid of what might be down there, the rat kept away from the cave and went back home. The next day, the rat was still curious about what was in the cave so it decided to keep its distance but take a peek. When it looked down into the cave, it spotted something that made the rat turn and run back home. Down in the cave, there was a huge, harsh rat. It had huge, sharp teeth and an ugly face. It seemed that the rat was trapped down there and it kept trying to get out. The little rat was too scared to even think of going back to the cave, but deep down it felt that it had to help the big rat. So, it kept thinking up a plan and finally came up with a solution. The little rat collected some rope and slowly but carefully lowered the rope down into the cave. After much effort, the little rat managed to help the big rat escape.
