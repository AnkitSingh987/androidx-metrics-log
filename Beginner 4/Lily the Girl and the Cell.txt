Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Cell

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily was very eager to learn new things. She loved to play outside, and she had many friends. One day, she found a tiny cell in her backyard. She looked at it and said, "Hello, little cell! What are you doing here?" The tiny cell said, "Hello, Lily! I am here to help you learn. I am a very special cell. I can show you how things grow and change. Would you like to see?" Lily was excited and said, "Yes, please! Show me, little cell!" The tiny cell showed Lily how plants and animals grow. They spent the whole day playing and learning together. When it was time for bed, Lily said, "Thank you, little cell, for showing me so many new things today. I am very tired now. I need to sleep." The tiny cell said, "You're welcome, Lily. Sleep well, and I will be here when you wake up." Lily smiled and went to sleep, knowing that she had made a new friend in the tiny cell. She was eager to learn more with her new friend the next day. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went for a walk. She saw a big tree with many apples. Lucy wanted to eat an apple, so she tried to pick one. She reached up high, but the apple was too far away. Then, Lucy had an idea. She found a long stick and used it to pick the apple. The apple fell down, and Lucy caught it. She took a big bite, and it was cold and sweet. Lucy was very happy. As Lucy walked more, she found a shiny trophy on the ground. She picked it up and smiled. She knew her mom and dad would be proud of her. Lucy went home and showed her trophy to her family. They clapped and cheered for her. Lucy felt very special that day.

Tom and Mia are playing in their room. They have many toys and clothes on the floor. Mom calls them from downstairs. "Tom, Mia, come and eat! The food is ready!" Tom and Mia want to eat, but they also want to keep playing. They look at each other and smile. They have an idea. "Let's rush to the kitchen and eat fast. Then we can come back and play more!" Tom says. "OK, let's go!" Mia agrees. They grab their hangers and put them on their heads. They pretend they are helmets. They run to the door and shout. "Zoom, zoom, we are space explorers! We are going to the moon!" They rush down the stairs, making loud noises. Mom sees them and laughs. "What are you wearing, you silly kids?" she asks. "These are our helmets, Mom. We are space explorers!" Tom says. "We are going to the moon, Mom. But first we need to eat!" Mia says. Mom hugs them and leads them to the table. She gives them each a plate of food. "OK, space explorers, enjoy your food. But don't eat too fast, or you will get a tummy ache!" she warns. Tom and Mia nod and start eating. They look at the window and see the clear sky. They see the moon shining bright. "Look, Mom, the moon is waiting for us!" Tom says. "Yes, it is, Mom. Can we go after we eat?" Mia asks. Mom smiles and nods. "Sure, you can go. But only in your imagination, OK? The moon is very far away, and you need a real rocket to go there. Not a hanger!" she explains. Tom and Mia understand. They finish their food and thank Mom. They run back to their room, holding their hangers. They lie on the floor and close their eyes. They imagine they are flying to the moon. They have fun in their space adventure. They are happy.

One day, a little mouse named Tim was very worried. He lost his favorite toy. He looked everywhere, but he could not find it. Tim felt sad and did not know what to do. Tim's friend, a big cat named Sam, saw that Tim was worried. Sam asked, "Why are you worried, Tim?" Tim said, "I lost my toy and I can't find it." Sam wanted to help his friend. So, they both looked for the toy together. They looked in the house, under the bed, and in the yard. Finally, they found the toy near a big tree. Tim was so happy! He said, "Thank you, Sam, for helping me!" Sam was happy too because he helped his friend. They played with the toy and had a fun day.
