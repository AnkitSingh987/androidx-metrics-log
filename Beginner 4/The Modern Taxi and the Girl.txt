Category: Beginner
Title: The Modern Taxi and the Girl

Once upon a time, there was a modern taxi. It was yellow and shiny. The taxi loved to drive around the town. It would go beep-beep so that people could hear it coming. One day, the taxi heard a little girl crying. She was lost and could not find her mom. The taxi wanted to help, so it stopped next to the girl. The girl looked at the taxi and stopped crying. The taxi said, "Don't cry. I will help you find your mom." The girl smiled and got in the taxi. They drove around the town until they found her mom. The girl and her mom were so happy. They hugged the taxi and said, "Thank you, modern taxi!"

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved her small pony, named Pippin. Pippin was an adventurous pony. They liked to play together in the big field near their house. One sunny day, Sue and Pippin went on a walk. They saw a big tree with a swing. Sue said, "Pippin, let's swing!" Pippin nodded, and Sue got on the swing. Pippin pushed Sue, and they both laughed. Sue's mommy came to the field and saw them play. She said, "Sue, you value your pony Pippin, and he values you too." Sue hugged Pippin and said, "I love you, Pippin." They went back home, happy to have each other.

Once upon a time, there was a smart cat named Tim. He lived in a small house with his friend, a little girl named Sue. They loved to play and have fun together. One day, Sue wanted to prepare a surprise for Tim. She thought of a fun game they could play with water. Sue went to the kitchen and turned on the faucet. Water came out and filled a big bowl. Sue was very happy and excited to show Tim the game. She called him to come and see. Tim came running, but he slipped on the wet floor and knocked the bowl over. The water spilled everywhere, and Sue and Tim were both surprised! The unexpected twist made them laugh and giggle. They decided to clean up the mess together and then play with the water outside. From that day on, they knew that even when things don't go as planned, they can still have fun together.

One day, a little car was driving down a long road. The car had four tires. One tire was hidden behind a big rock. The car did not know that the tire was there. The car saw a big truck and wanted to talk to it. The car said, "Hi, big truck! Can you help me find my hidden tire?" The big truck said, "Yes, little car, I can help you find your tire." They looked and looked, but they could not find the hidden tire. The little car was sad. The big truck said, "Do not worry, little car. We will find your tire." They kept looking and finally found the hidden tire behind the big rock. The little car was happy and said, "Thank you, big truck!" The big truck said, "You're welcome, little car!" And they both drove away, happy to have solved the problem together.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim and a little girl named Sue wanted to play a game. They went outside to play with a big, red ball. Tim said, "Let's play a game where we throw the ball and catch it. But we have to be alert so the ball doesn't hit us." Sue said, "That sounds fun! I'll throw the ball first." Sue threw the ball high in the air. Tim was alert and caught the ball with both hands. Then it was Tim's turn to throw the ball. He threw it far, but Sue was alert and caught it too. As they played the game, a little dog came running up to them. The dog had a collar with a tag that said "I belong to Sam." Tim and Sue knew that Sam was their friend who lived down the street. Tim said, "Let's take the dog back to Sam. He must be worried." So, Tim and Sue stopped their game and walked with the little dog to Sam's house. When they got there, Sam was so happy to see his dog. He said, "Thank you for bringing my dog back. He belongs with me." From that day on, Tim, Sue, and Sam played games together and always made sure they were alert when playing with the big, red ball.
