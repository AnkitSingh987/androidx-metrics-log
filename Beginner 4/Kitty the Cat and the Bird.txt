Category: Beginner
Title: Kitty the Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Kitty. Kitty lived near a big hedge. She liked to play and run around the hedge all day. One day, Kitty saw a poor little bird with a hurt wing. The bird could not fly. Kitty wanted to help the bird. She thought, "I can stretch my leg and try to reach the bird." Kitty stretched her leg and reached the bird. She was very happy to help. The bird said, "Thank you, Kitty!" Kitty and the bird became good friends. They played near the hedge every day.

One day, a bald man named Tom went for a walk. He saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was trying to write a word on the ground with a stick. Tom walked up to her and asked, "What are you doing?" Sue said, "I am trying to write a spell, but I don't know how. Can you help me?" Tom thought for a moment and then said, "I can remind you how to write the spell. First, draw a big 'S', then a 'P', next an 'E', after that an 'L', and lastly another 'L'. That is how you write 'spell'." Sue smiled and drew the letters on the ground. She looked at Tom and said, "Thank you for reminding me!" They both laughed and continued to have a fun day writing words on the ground.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a weak little dog named Spot. Spot was always hungry because he didn't have much meat to eat. He wanted to find more food to make him strong. One day, Spot saw a big man with a bag of meat. Spot thought, "If I can get that meat, I will be strong and not weak anymore." So, Spot followed the big man and tried to take the meat from him. The big man saw Spot and said, "No, this is my meat." Spot tried to jump and grab the meat, but he was too weak. The big man took the meat away, and Spot was still weak and hungry.

Once upon a time, there was an otter. His name was Tim. Tim lived in a deep pond. He liked to swim and play all day. One day, Tim met a big fish. The big fish did not have a name. Tim thought this was strange. He wanted to help the big fish find a name. They played together and tried to think of a name. As they played, the big fish said, "I have a secret." The big fish was not a fish at all! He was a whale! Tim was very surprised. They named the whale Sam. Tim and Sam became best friends and played in the deep pond every day.

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk in the park. The park was full of mist. Mia saw an old lady sitting on a bench. The old lady looked sad. Mia wanted to make her smile. Mia went up to the old lady and said, "Let's dance!" The old lady looked at Mia and smiled. They started to dance in the mist. They danced and danced, and the old lady felt happy. But then, a big dog came running towards them. The dog wanted to play, but Mia and the old lady were scared. The dog barked, and they stopped dancing. Mia had an idea. She said to the dog, "You can dance too!" The dog started to dance with Mia and the old lady. They all danced together in the mist. The big dog was not so scary after all. They all became friends and danced until the mist went away.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a small squirrel. This squirrel loved to find acorns. One day, he found a big acorn. It was the biggest acorn he had ever seen. He was very happy and wanted to show his friends. He went to his friend, the rabbit. The rabbit was not nice. He said harsh words to the squirrel. The rabbit wanted the big acorn for himself. The squirrel was sad but he did not give the acorn to the rabbit. He refused to let the rabbit have it. Then, something unexpected happened. The big acorn started to grow. It grew and grew until it became a big tree. The squirrel and his other friends played on the tree. They were all very happy. The rabbit was sad because he did not have any friends to play with. The moral of the story is to be kind to others and not be selfish.
