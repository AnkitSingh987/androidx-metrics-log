Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Gift

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom was very excited because it was his birthday. He knew he would get a gift from his mom and dad. Tom could not wait to see what it was! Tom's mom and dad gave him a big, shiny gift. It was wrapped in pretty paper. Tom opened it up and saw an expensive toy car! It was red and shiny, just like the ones he saw go fast on the street. Tom was so happy. Tom played with his toy car all day. He made it go fast and slow, and he raced it with his other cars. Tom loved his gift so much. He knew his mom and dad loved him, and he was very thankful for his special day.

Once there was a man who had a very rare iron. He kept it in a special chest. One day, he set out to find a new treasure. He took his iron with him. "I need to find something amazing," he said to himself. He journeyed far and wide but could not find the special thing he was looking for. He was almost ready to give up. Suddenly, he stumbled across an old house. He knocked on the door and a woman answered. "Do you have anything special?" he asked. The woman smiled and replied, "Yes, I have something very special and rare. I have a magical iron!" The man was so excited. He bought the iron and thanked the woman. He set off with his new found treasure and never looked back.

One day, Tom and Sue went to the park to play a nice game of catch. They took a big red ball with them. The sun was shining, and the sky was blue. They were very happy to play together. As they played, a small dog came running to them. The dog was fast and wanted to play too. The dog jumped and caught the ball in its mouth. Tom and Sue laughed and clapped their hands. They thought the dog was very good at the game. They played catch with the dog for a long time. Then, a big bird flew down from the sky. The bird picked up the dog and flew away. Tom and Sue were very surprised. They did not know that the dog was friends with the big bird. Now, they had a new story to tell their friends about their nice game of catch at the park.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. As he started to climb, he heard a loud noise. It was a big bird. The bird said, "Don't be scared, I am your friend." Tim said, "Hi, bird! I am not scared. Can we play?" The bird said, "Yes, let's play! I can show you something fun to taste." So, Tim and the bird played together and had lots of fun. The bird took Tim to a bush with red berries. The bird said, "Try these! They have a mild taste." Tim tried the berries, and he liked them. They played more and then said goodbye. Tim went home and told his mom about his new friend, the bird.

Once upon a time, there was a purple dog named Spot. Spot liked to serve food to his friends. He loved to feel the texture of different foods with his paws. One day, Spot made a big party for his friends. He served them soft cookies and hard crackers. All his friends were happy. They liked how the food felt and tasted. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all the food away! Spot was sad. But his friends were not sad. They had a secret. They had more food hidden! They served Spot new food and they all laughed and played. The party was fun again.

Once upon a time, there was a little yellow duck. The duck loved to play in the water and soak all day. One day, the duck found a magic rock. The rock was shiny and pretty. The duck wanted to see what the magic rock could do. The duck took the magic rock to the water. The duck wished for a friend to play with. Then, something unexpected happened. The water began to bubble and splash. A big yellow fish jumped out of the water. The duck was so happy to have a new friend. But, there was a twist. The big yellow fish was not a nice friend. The fish wanted to eat the little duck. The duck was scared and wished for the fish to go away. The magic rock heard the wish and made the fish go away. The duck was safe and learned to be careful with magic.
