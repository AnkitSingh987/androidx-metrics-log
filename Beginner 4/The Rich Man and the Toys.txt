Category: Beginner
Title: The Rich Man and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a rich man. He had a big house and a lot of toys. He liked to store all his toys in a big room. One day, he bought a new toy, a soft bathrobe. The rich man wore the bathrobe after his bath. It was so soft and warm. He loved it so much that he wore it all day. He played with his toys and danced around the house in his bathrobe. One day, his friends came to play. They saw the rich man in his bathrobe and they laughed. They asked him to take it off, but he did not want to. He loved his bathrobe too much. So, they all played with his toys and wore bathrobes too. They had a lot of fun and felt happy.

One day, a big, proud cat saw a sack. The cat wanted the sack, so he said to a small dog, "Can you request the sack for me, please?" The small dog wanted to help the proud cat, so he said, "Okay, I will try." The small dog went to the sack and said, "Can I have you for the proud cat?" The sack did not say anything. The small dog did not know what to do. He pushed and pulled the sack, but it did not move. The small dog felt sad. The proud cat saw the small dog trying very hard. The proud cat thought, "I will get the sack myself." The proud cat jumped on the sack. But the sack was full of water! The water came out and made the proud cat all wet. The proud cat was not proud anymore. The small dog laughed, and the proud cat went away, feeling sad.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a big bird in a tree. The bird was scary, but Tim liked to look at it. He would say, "I admire you, bird!" The bird looked down at Tim and said, "Why do you admire me? I am scary." Tim smiled and said, "You are big and strong. That is why I admire you." The scary bird felt happy. It was nice to be admired. The bird and Tim became friends. They would play and talk in the park every day. The bird was not so scary after all.

Once upon a time, there was a weak tiger. He was not like the other tigers. They were strong and fast, but he was slow and tired all the time. The weak tiger was sad because he wanted to play with his friends. He needed to improve so he could be strong like them. One day, the weak tiger met a wise old monkey. The monkey saw that the tiger was sad and wanted to help. "To improve, you must eat good food and practice running every day," said the wise old monkey. The weak tiger listened and started to do what the monkey said. After some days, the weak tiger started to feel stronger. He could run faster and play with his friends. The other tigers were happy to see him improve. They all played together and had fun. The weak tiger was now a strong tiger, and he was very happy.

One day, a girl named Mia found a mysterious bottle in her bathroom. It was a new shampoo. Mia wanted to test it. She felt excited. Mia put some shampoo on her hand and rubbed it in her hair. It made a lot of bubbles. She liked the bubbles. They were fun and smelled nice. After washing her hair, Mia's hair was soft and shiny. She was happy with the mysterious shampoo. Mia showed her mom and dad. They liked it too. They all had a good day.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Jack. He was hopping around in a giant maze. He started to feel a bit lost, so he shouted out to his mom, "Mom! I'm lost in this maze and I'm scared!" His mom replied, "Don't worry sweetheart, I'm almost there. Just turn around and keep hopping until you find me ok?" Jack listened to his mom and kept hopping. He turned a corner and noticed something pale. It was a big egg! He hopped closer and saw that it was split in two. Jack was excited and wanted to explore more. He kept hopping through the maze to find even more cool things. Eventually, he saw his mom waiting for him. She said, "Jack! I'm so glad you found me. Let's head home now ok?" Jack said goodbye to the maze and hopped away with his mom. What a fun adventure it was!
