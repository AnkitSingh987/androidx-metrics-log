Category: Beginner
Title: Winky the Flexible Cat and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a flexible cat named Winky. Winky loved to play and jump all day. One day, Winky saw a big box. The box had a note that said, "Please accept this gift." Winky was very happy and excited. Winky opened the box and found a lot of fun toys inside. There were balls, strings, and even a toy mouse! Winky played with the toys all day long. When Winky was tired, the cat gave a big wink to say "thank you" for the gift. Winky's friends saw how much fun Winky was having. They wanted to play too! Winky was very kind and shared the toys with all the friends. They all played together and had a great time. And every time they played, Winky gave a happy wink.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to school. She had a beautiful eraser. It was pink and had a picture of a cat on it. Mia loved her eraser very much. She used it to fix her mistakes when she drew pictures. At lunch time, Mia could not find her eraser. She looked everywhere, but it was gone. She was very sad. Then, her friend Sam came over and asked, "Mia, why are you sad?" Mia said, "I can't find my beautiful eraser." Sam wanted to help Mia. They looked all around the classroom together. They looked under the chairs and in the toy box. Finally, they found the beautiful eraser. It was under Mia's lunch box. Mia was so happy. She said, "Thank you, Sam!" Sam was happy too. He said, "You're welcome, Mia! Friends help each other." From that day on, Mia and Sam were the best of friends.

Tom and Sue were playing in the garden. They liked to run and jump and laugh. They saw a rabbit with long ears and a long tail. The rabbit was eating some grass. "Look, a rabbit!" Tom said. "Let's catch it and play with it!" Sue nodded. They ran after the rabbit. The rabbit saw them and ran away. It ran into a hole under a big tree. "Come out, rabbit!" Sue shouted. "We want to be your friends!" But the rabbit did not come out. It was scared of Tom and Sue. It shut its eyes and hoped they would go away. Tom and Sue tried to dig the hole with their hands. But the hole was too deep and too dark. They could not see the rabbit. "Maybe the rabbit is sleeping," Tom said. "Maybe we should let it sleep." Sue agreed. They stopped digging and sat on the grass. They felt a little sad. "Sorry, rabbit," Sue said. "We did not mean to scare you. We just wanted to play with you." They heard a soft noise from the hole. It was the rabbit. It opened its eyes and peeked out. It saw Tom and Sue sitting quietly. They looked nice and friendly. The rabbit came out of the hole. It hopped to Tom and Sue. It sniffed them and rubbed its nose on them. It let them touch its long ears and its long tail. Tom and Sue smiled. They were happy. They had a new friend. "Hello, rabbit," Tom said. "Do you want to play with us?" The rabbit nodded. It liked Tom and Sue. They played together in the garden. They ran and jumped and laughed. They had fun.

Once upon a time there was a little boy called Jack. He was very impatient and wanted to go home. Jack asked his Mom, "Can I go home now?" His Mom smiled and said, "Yes, you can go home Jack." Jack was so happy that he started jumping around. He was so excited and couldn't wait to get home. He started running very fast. He waved to his mom as he ran out of sight. He ran so fast and couldn't stop. He ran and ran until he got home. His Mom was waiting for him on the doorstep. Jack could not believe it, he was finally home. He hugged his Mom and they both went inside. Jack was very happy to be home and to be with his Mom. He knew he was safe and could now relax.
