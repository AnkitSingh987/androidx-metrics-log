Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Stubborn Girl and the Mom

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her color crayons every day. One day, her mom said, "Lily, you need to improve your coloring. Try to stay inside the lines." Lily did not like being told what to do, but she wanted her mom to be happy. So, she tried her best to improve her coloring. She practiced and practiced, coloring many pictures. One day, Lily showed her mom a new picture she colored. Her mom smiled and said, "Wow, Lily! You did it! Your coloring is much better now. I am so proud of you." Lily was happy that she improved and made her mom proud.

---

One day, a little dog named Spot went for a walk. He liked to walk in the park and play with his ball. The sun was bright and the grass was soft. Spot was very happy. As Spot was playing, he saw a big cat. The cat was sad and said, "I am sorry, I lost my toy. Can you help me?" Spot wanted to help the sorry cat. They looked for the toy together. They found the toy under a tree. The cat was so happy and said, "Thank you, Spot!" The cat rubbed Spot's head to say thank you. Spot and the cat became good friends and played together all day.

---

Once upon a time, there was a pilot. The pilot had a tidy plane. Every day, he flew the plane in the sky. One day, the pilot saw a big cloud. The cloud was sad. The pilot wanted to help the cloud. He flew his tidy plane up to the cloud. When the pilot reached the cloud, he gave the cloud a big hug. The cloud was happy now. The pilot flew his tidy plane back home. And they all lived happily ever after.

---

Lily and Tom were twins. They liked to play with their toys and watch cartoons. One day, their mom said they had to go to the doctor. Lily and Tom did not like the doctor. They were scared of the needles and the cold stethoscope. Mom took them to the bus stop. They waited for the bus. The bus was big and yellow. It made a loud noise. Lily and Tom held mom's hand. They hoped the bus would not come. But the bus came. It stopped in front of them. The door opened. Mom said, "Come on, kids. We have to get on the bus." Lily and Tom did not want to get on the bus. They cried and kicked. They said, "No, no, no!" Mom tried to pull them on the bus. The bus driver was angry. He said, "Hurry up, lady. I have a schedule to keep." Mom was sad and tired. She said, "Please, kids. It's not that bad. The doctor will be nice. He will give you a sticker and a lollipop." Lily and Tom did not care. They still cried and kicked. They did not listen to mom. Then, something bad happened. The bus driver closed the door. He did not see that Lily's arm was still outside. He started the bus. He drove away. Lily's arm was stuck in the door. She screamed. She felt a lot of pain. She suffered. Tom and mom screamed too. They were scared. They tried to open the door. They could not. The bus was too fast. They could not help Lily. Lily was hurt. She was alone. She was scared. The end.

One day, a little girl named Sue and her dog, Max, went for a walk. They saw a long road in front of them. Sue was a bit fearful because she had never been on this road before. Sue said to Max, "I am scared, but we can go together." Max wagged his tail and led Sue down the road. They walked and walked until they saw a big tree with lots of fruit. Sue and Max shared the fruit and played under the tree. They were very happy. When they went home, Sue told her mom about the new road and the big tree. Her mom smiled and said, "See, when you face your fears with a friend, you can find beautiful things." And from that day on, Sue was not fearful of new roads, because she knew her friend Max would always lead her safely.
