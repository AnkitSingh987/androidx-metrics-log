Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Tree

One day, a boy named Tim had a map. He wanted to find a big red ball. The map showed him where to go. His friend, Sue, saw the map and was envious. She said, "I want to find the big red ball too!" Tim and Sue walked and walked, following the map. They saw a big tree. The map said the big red ball was near the tree. They looked around, but couldn't see the ball. Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's kneel down and look under the tree." They knelt down and looked under the tree. There they found the big red ball! Tim and Sue were so happy. They took the ball and played with it all day. Sue was not envious anymore, because they both had the big red ball to play with.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a wise cat named Whiskers. Whiskers loved to sit by the bookcase and look at the books. One day, Whiskers saw a big cup on the floor. It was next to a small girl named Lily. "Whiskers, do you want to drink?" asked Lily. Whiskers looked at the cup and said, "No, thank you. I am not thirsty now." One day, a naughty dog named Spot came to the house. Spot saw the big cup next to the bookcase. He wanted to drink from it. But Whiskers knew that if Spot drank from the cup, the books would get wet. "Spot, do not drink!" said Whiskers. But Spot did not listen. He drank from the cup and spilled water all over the floor. The books got wet, and Lily was sad. Whiskers had an idea. He helped Lily clean the books and put them back on the bookcase. Then, Whiskers found a new place for the cup, far away from the books. From that day on, Spot never drank near the bookcase again.

Once upon a time, there was a small mouse named Tim. Tim lived in a tiny house near a big tree. Tim had many friends who liked to play with him. One day, Tim found a leak in his house. The water came in and made the floor wet. Tim was sad and told his friends about the leak. His friends said, "Don't worry, we will help you fix the leak." They all worked together to find things to stop the water. They found a big leaf and some mud to cover the hole. Tim and his friends fixed the leak in his house. They were all happy and played together again. When it was time to go home, Tim's friends said goodbye and returned to their own houses. Tim was very thankful for his friends' help. Now, his house was dry and cozy again.

Once upon a time, there was a shy little bunny named Bobo. Bobo loved to play in the grass and look for yummy food. One day, Bobo found a big, red radish. He had never seen a radish before, but it looked so tasty. Bobo was so happy with his radish. He loved it very much. He played with it all day, rolling it on the ground and nibbling on it. The radish was the best thing he had ever found. As the sun went down, Bobo knew it was time to go home. He took his radish with him and shared it with his family. They all loved the radish too. Bobo was very happy that he found the radish and could share it with the ones he loved. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a cool crab was walking on the beach. It was a sunny day and the crab liked the sand. The crab was happy and played near the water. A big bird saw the crab and wanted to tease it. The bird flew down and said, "You are small and slow!" The crab felt sad and didn't want to play anymore. The cool crab thought of a plan. It hid in the sand and waited. When the bird came back, the crab pinched its leg. The bird said, "Ouch!" and flew away. The crab was happy again and went back to playing on the beach.
