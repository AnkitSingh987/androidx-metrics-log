Category: Beginner
Title: Mittens the Cat and the Bird

One day, a little cat named Mittens went out to play. The wind blew strong and cold, but Mittens was persistent. She wanted to find a warm place to lie down. Mittens saw a big box in the yard. "This will be a good place to lie," she thought. But when she went in the box, she found a big, warm dog. The dog was not happy to see Mittens. He barked loud, and Mittens ran away. Mittens looked for another place to lie. She saw a small hole in a tree. She climbed up and went inside. To her surprise, she found a little bird in the hole. The bird was not scared of Mittens. Instead, the bird was happy to see her. They became good friends and played together all day. In the end, Mittens found a warm place to lie with her new friend, the little bird.

Once there was a little girl and she really wanted to find a present to give her mom. She walked into a store and saw a fine vase that her mom would love. She had to save up the money she got from chores, so she worked very hard. Finally, the little girl had enough money to buy the vase. She carefully brought it home and presented it to her mom. Her mom was so excited and lifted the vase up with a big smile. The little girl was so proud of herself and of her mom. She was happy to see her mom so happy. The vase was so fine and the little girl knew it was the perfect present. It was a memory that lasted forever.

Once upon a time, there was a happy fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim all day in the big blue sea. He also liked to paint pretty pictures with his tail. One day, Fin saw a sad fish named Sam. Sam could not swim very well. Fin wanted to help Sam, so he had an idea. Fin said, "Let's paint together, and you will learn to swim better too." Sam was not sure, but he tried it. They painted and swam together every day. Soon, Sam became very good at swimming. He was successful and happy. Fin was happy too, because he helped his friend. The moral of the story is: Helping others can make you feel happy and proud.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max had a big patch on his back. He loved to play outside with his friends. One day, Max saw a fierce cat in his yard. The cat was not nice and scared Max's friends away. Max wanted to help his friends, so he made a plan. He would send the fierce cat away. Max barked and barked at the fierce cat. The cat did not like the loud noise. It ran away from Max's yard. Max's friends came back to play. Max and his friends played all day long. They were happy the fierce cat was gone. Max was a hero. His friends thanked him for sending the cat away. They all had fun and played together every day after that.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue had her own small factory. She made toys for kids. Sue was very happy making toys. One day, a big man came to Sue's factory. He wanted to make fake toys. Sue did not like fake toys. She said no to the big man. The big man was mad. He wanted to take Sue's factory. Sue did not want to lose her factory. She asked her friends for help. They all came to help Sue. They made real toys, not fake ones. The big man saw how much fun they had. He learned that real toys were better than fake toys. In the end, the big man left Sue's factory. Sue and her friends were happy. They kept making real toys for kids. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He wanted to wear his favorite hat. He looked in his room, but he could not find it. Tim was sad because he thought he lost his hat. He was also tired from looking for it all morning. Tim walked outside to ask his friend, Sue, if she saw his hat. Sue was wearing a hat that looked just like Tim's hat. Tim was surprised and said, "That is mine!" Sue laughed and said, "No, this is my hat. I got it yesterday." Just then, Tim's mom came outside with a big smile. She was holding Tim's hat. She said, "I found your hat, Tim! It was under your bed." Tim was so happy to have his hat back. He wasn't sad or tired anymore. Tim and Sue wore their hats and played together all day long.
