Category: Beginner
Title: The Choo Choo and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a brave choo choo. He liked to march through the forest with his friends. One day, he came across a strange green liquid. He didn't know it was poison! His friends said, "Don't touch it". But Choo Choo didn't listen. He touched the poison and it made him feel very hurt. He had a big ouch. So he ran away as fast as he could. But he was too slow and the poison made him feel very sick. His friends decided they had to find a way to save him. They put on their marching boots and started to march. After a long time, they found a big pond. They jumped in the pond and swam as fast as they could. The cool water made Choo Choo feel better. And soon, he was all better! They celebrated by marching back through the forest, happy to be together again.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a restless dog who loved to run and play all day. One day, Spot saw a big red ball in the yard. He ran to the ball and started to play with it. Spot's friend, Tom the cat, came over and said, "Spot, let's cut this big red ball in half and share it." Spot liked the idea and wagged his tail. Tom found a small knife and carefully cut the ball in half. Now, Spot and Tom each had a piece of the big red ball to play with. Together, Spot and Tom played with their half of the ball. They laughed and had a lot of fun. After a long day of playing, they were both tired. Spot and Tom lay down in the soft grass and took a nap. They dreamed of more fun days playing together with their big red ball.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a friend named Sue. They liked to play together. One day, Sue had a birthday party. Tim went to the party and gave Sue a present. Tim saw that Sue got many presents. He started to feel jealous. He wanted presents too. Tim sat in a corner and began to think. He thought about how happy Sue was with her presents. Then, Tim remembered that he had a birthday too. He knew that when his birthday came, he would get presents. Tim stopped feeling jealous and started to feel happy. He played with Sue and all their friends at the party. They had a fun day together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play at the park. He saw a small bird who looked very sad. Tim asked the bird, "Why are you so miserable?" The bird said, "I am hungry, and I can't find any food." Tim wanted to help the bird, so he went home to get some food for the bird. When he came back to the park, he saw a big cat trying to catch the bird. Tim shouted, "Stop!" The cat stopped and looked at Tim. Tim gave the food to the bird, and the bird was very happy. The cat said, "I am hungry too." So, Tim shared the food with the cat as well. The bird, the cat, and Tim all played together in the park. They became good friends and learned that sharing makes everyone happy.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Lily. She had a toy named Bunny. Lily and Bunny were best friends. They played together every day. One day, Lily and Bunny went to the park. They played hide and seek. Lily was anxious because she could not find Bunny. She looked behind trees and under benches, but Bunny was not there. Lily started to feel sad. Then, Lily saw a hill. She climbed to the top and had a nice view of the park. She looked down and saw Bunny. Bunny was under a big tree. Lily was happy she found Bunny. They hugged and went home to play more games.

One day, a girl named Lucy wanted to read a book. She went to her room and picked her favorite book. But it was too dark to read. Lucy was sad. The weather outside was bad. It rained and the wind blew hard. Lucy's mom said, "You can't go out today. It's not safe." Lucy was sad and wanted the sun to come back. Lucy had an idea. She went to the window and opened it a little bit. She said, "Weather, please be nice and slow down the rain." The weather listened and the rain slowed down. Lucy was happy. Now she could read her book by the window with enough light.
