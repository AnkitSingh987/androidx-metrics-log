Category: Beginner
Title: Lila and Ben and the Park

Lila and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They run, jump, slide and swing. They have fun. One day, they see a big truck in the park. The truck is noisy. It makes loud sounds. Vroom, vroom, beep, beep. Lila and Ben are curious. They want to see the truck. They go near the truck. They see a man. The man is holding a palm. The palm is green and tall. The man is putting the palm in the truck. "Hello, man," Lila says. "What are you doing with the palm?" "Hello, kids," the man says. "I am taking the palm to a new place. The palm is too big for the park. It needs more space." "Can we go with you?" Ben asks. "We want to see the new place." "Sorry, kids," the man says. "You cannot go with me. The truck is full. And the new place is far. You have to stay in the park. But you can wave goodbye to the palm." Lila and Ben are sad. They like the palm. They hug the palm. They say goodbye to the palm. They wave to the man and the truck. The truck leaves the park. The truck is noisy. Vroom, vroom, beep, beep. Lila and Ben look at the empty spot. The spot is where the palm was. The spot is now small and brown. Lila and Ben are sad. "Let's play," Lila says. "Maybe we can find a new friend in the park." "OK," Ben says. "Let's play." They run, jump, slide and swing. They have fun. They end the day with a smile.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was very sleepy after playing all day. Lily's mom called her to come inside for dinner. Lily sat at the table and saw her plate. There was broccoli on it. She didn't like broccoli very much. She looked at her mom and said, "Can I leave the broccoli, please?" Her mom smiled and said, "You must eat some, then you can leave the rest." Lily tried the broccoli and found it was not so bad after all. She ate some more and then left the rest. After dinner, her mom tucked her into bed. Lily was so sleepy that she fell asleep right away, dreaming of playing and eating yummy food.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One sunny day, he went to the park with his mom. They saw a black dog playing near a puddle. The dog was very happy. Tim asked his mom, "Can I play with the dog, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful not to get wet from the puddle." Tim played with the dog and they had so much fun. They ran around and splashed in the puddle. After a while, the sun was very hot. Tim felt his skin burn a little. He told his mom, "I feel hot and my skin hurts." His mom said, "Let's go home and rest in the shade." So, Tim and his mom went home, leaving the black dog to play by the puddle.

One day, the bright sun smiled down on a family. Dad was in the car, ready to drive them all on an adventure. Mum and the two little kids were all excited, they could not wait to get going. Dad drove the car to the park. As they arrived, they saw a big pond with lots of ducks swimming on it. The kids loved watching the ducks and happily ran around, chasing each other. When they were done exploring, Dad found some car-parking pins and gave one to each of the kids. The pins were bright and colourful. The kids loved them and decided to keep them as mementoes of their adventure. All too soon, it was time to go home. The family got in the car and drove back. Everyone was happy and had had a wonderful day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big festival. All the people came to have fun. They played games, danced, and ate yummy food. In the town, there were two cats, Tom and Jerry. Tom saw that Jerry was having a lot of fun at the festival. Tom felt jealous. He wanted to have fun too, but he didn't want to surrender and join Jerry. One day, Tom decided to surrender and have fun with Jerry. They played games, danced, and ate yummy food together. They were no longer jealous of each other. They became best friends and had fun at the festival every year.
