Category: Beginner
Title: Lila and Ben and the Toys

Lila and Ben are playing with their toys in the living room. Lila has a doll with a delicate dress and a hat. Ben has a truck with big wheels and a horn. They are having fun making noises and moving their toys around. Suddenly, Ben sees something shiny on the shelf. He wants to see what it is, so he puts his truck down and climbs on the couch. He reaches for the shiny thing, but it is too far. He stretches his arm and tries to grab it. "Ben, no!" Lila shouts. "That is Mommy's snow globe. It is not a toy. It can break!" But Ben does not listen. He pulls the snow globe and it falls from the shelf. It lands on the floor with a loud crash. The glass shatters and the water spills. The snow and the little house inside are scattered everywhere. Lila and Ben are scared. They hear Mommy coming from the kitchen. They do not know what to do. They look at each other and then at the mess. "Ben, what did you do?" Mommy asks. She is angry and sad. She picks up the broken snow globe and shakes her head. "This was a gift from Grandma. It was very special. How could you be so careless?" Ben feels sorry. He lowers his head and says in a small voice, "I'm sorry, Mommy. I just wanted to see the shiny thing. I did not mean to break it." Mommy sighs. She puts the snow globe on the table and kneels down. She hugs Ben and Lila and says, "I know you did not mean to break it, Ben. But you have to be more careful with things that are not yours. And you have to listen to Lila when she tells you to stop. She was trying to help you." Lila nods. She says, "I'm sorry, too, Mommy. I tried to stop Ben, but he did not hear me." Mommy smiles. She says, "It's okay, Lila. You did the right thing. You are a good sister. Now, let's clean up this mess and then we can play with your toys again. But please, be gentle with them. They are not as delicate as the snow globe, but they are still important." Lila and Ben agree. They help Mommy clean up the glass and the water. They say sorry again and hug Mommy. Then they go back to their toys and play nicely. They do not touch the shelf or the shiny things anymore. They learn their lesson.

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn boy named Tom. He didn't like to taste new foods. One day, Tom's mom made a new food for dinner. She put it in a big chamber on the table. "Tom, come and taste this new food," said his mom. Tom looked at the food and said, "No! I don't want to taste it." His mom said, "Please, just try a little bit." Tom was still stubborn and said, "No, I won't taste it." Then, Tom's sister, Amy, came to the table. She tasted the new food and said, "Yummy! This is so good!" Tom saw Amy enjoying the food, and he decided to taste it too. Tom tasted the new food and found out it was delicious. He learned that trying new things can be good. From that day on, Tom was not stubborn anymore and always tasted new foods his mom made.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy had a lucky toe. When she touched her lucky toe, good things would happen. One day, Lucy was sad because she lost her ball. She touched her lucky toe and said, "Lucky toe, help me find my ball." Just then, her dog, Max, came running with the ball in his mouth. "Thank you, lucky toe!" she said. Lucy knew it was time to celebrate. Lucy and Max went to the park to celebrate. They played with the ball and had a lot of fun. Lucy said to Max, "We are so lucky to have each other and my lucky toe." They played until the sun went down, and they were very happy.
