Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Park

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a big tower and a swing. She ran to the swing and started to swing back and forth. She went up and down, higher and higher. She was very happy. Lily's friend, Max, came to the park too. He saw Lily on the swing and said, "Hi, Lily! Can I swing too?" Lily stopped swinging and said, "Sure, Max! Let's swing together!" So Max got on the swing next to Lily, and they both swung back and forth, laughing and having fun. After they were done swinging, Lily and Max were hungry. They went to a small shop near the park. There, they found tasty treats. Lily got a tasty apple, and Max got a tasty cookie. They sat on a bench, ate their treats, and talked about their fun day at the park.

Mara was a 3 year old girl who had a pet named Spot. Spot was a clumsy dog who loved to play fetch and run around the yard with Mara. One day, Spot was having trouble getting through the doggy door, so Mara decided to help him out. She called Spot over and said, “Come Spot, I can help you.” Spot wagged his tail and came running. Mara held the door open and said, “There you go. You can squeeze through.” Spot did and ran outside to the yard. He was very happy and thanked Mara with a few licks on her face. Mara laughed and said, “You’re welcome! You’re a clumsy pup, but I like you!” Spot wagged his tail in response, and they both ran around the yard and played the rest of the day.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to play in her yard. She saw a small box under a big tree. As Mia looked closer, she saw a key next to the box. She used the key to reveal what was inside the box. It was a safe! Mia was very excited. Just then, Mia's neighbor, Mr. Brown, came over. He was a compassionate man who always helped Mia when she needed it. Mr. Brown said, "Mia, what did you find?" Mia showed him the safe and the key. Mr. Brown smiled and said, "Let's see what's inside the safe." Mia and Mr. Brown opened the safe together. They were surprised to find a note and a small toy. The note said, "This toy is for the kindest child in the neighborhood." Mia was so happy that the toy was for her. She thanked Mr. Brown for his help and gave him a big hug. They both knew that Mia's kind heart was the reason she found the special toy.

One day, a brilliant sun was shining in the sky. A little girl named Mia was playing in her room upstairs. She had a yummy ice cream cone that she wanted to eat. But Mia's mommy told her, "Mia, you need to eat your lunch first, then you can have your ice cream." Mia didn't want to eat her lunch. She wanted to eat the ice cream right away. So, Mia hid the ice cream in her toy box and went to eat her lunch. When she finished her lunch, she ran upstairs to get her ice cream. But when she opened the toy box, she saw that her ice cream had melted! "Oh no!" cried Mia, "My ice cream is all gone!" Just then, Mia's mommy came into the room. "What happened, Mia?" she asked. Mia told her mommy about the melted ice cream. Her mommy said, "Mia, you should have listened to me. If you ate your lunch first, your ice cream wouldn't have melted." Mia learned that she should listen to her mommy and not try to hide things. From that day on, Mia always listened to her mommy and never hid her ice cream again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a toy box with many toys. One day, she found an unusual toy. It was a razor, but it was not sharp. It was a toy razor, just for pretend. Lucy wanted to make something with the toy razor. She thought and thought. Then, she had an idea! She took some play dough and started to make shapes. With the toy razor, she cut the play dough into little pieces. Lucy showed her mom the shapes she made with the toy razor. Her mom was happy and proud. They played together with the unusual toy and made lots of fun shapes. And they lived happily ever after.
