Category: Beginner
Title: Ben the Fast Bunny and the Bear

Once upon a time, there was a fast bunny named Ben. Ben loved to run and play in the forest. One day, he saw a big jar of jelly. Ben was hungry and wanted to eat the jelly. He tried to open the jar, but it was too tight. Ben saw his friend, the squirrel, and asked, "Can you help me open the jar?" The squirrel tried to open the jar, but it was still too tight. They both felt sad, because they could not eat the yummy jelly. Suddenly, a strong bear came and saw them trying to open the jar. The bear opened the jar easily! Ben and the squirrel were so happy. They all ate the jelly together and applauded the bear for his help. The bear smiled and said, "We can do anything when we help each other!"

Lila and Ben are playing in the park. They like to slide and swing and run. They see a big thing in the sky. It is round and shiny and has a basket. It is a balloon. "Wow, look at the balloon!" Lila says. "I want to go in it!" "Me too!" Ben says. "Let's go and ask." They run to the balloon. A man is there. He smiles and says, "Hello, kids. Do you want to see the balloon?" "Yes, please!" Lila and Ben say. The man lets them climb in the basket. He gives them hats and goggles. He says, "Are you ready for a ride?" "Yes, yes, yes!" Lila and Ben say. The man turns on a fire. The fire makes the balloon go up. The balloon goes higher and higher. Lila and Ben see the park and the trees and the houses. They see birds and clouds and cars. They feel happy and excited. But then, something bad happens. The fire goes out. The balloon stops going up. It starts to go down. It goes faster and faster. Lila and Ben feel scared and dizzy. "Help, help, help!" they cry. The man tries to fix the fire. He can't. He says, "Don't worry, kids. I will call for help." He takes out a phone. He talks to someone. He says, "We need a rescue. We are in a balloon. We are falling. Please hurry." Lila and Ben hold each other. They hope someone will come. They hear a noise. It is a loud and strong noise. It is a helicopter. The helicopter comes near the balloon. It has a rope. It throws the rope to the balloon. The man catches the rope. He ties it to the basket. He says, "Hold on, kids. We are safe." The helicopter pulls the balloon up. It takes them to the ground. Lila and Ben see people waiting for them. They see their mom and dad. They run to them. They hug them. They say, "We are sorry. We are glad. We love you." Their mom and dad say, "We are sorry too. We are glad too. We love you too." They thank the man and the helicopter. They say, "You are brave. You are kind. You rescued us." The man and the helicopter say, "You are welcome. You are lucky. You are brave too." Lila and Ben smile. They feel happy and safe. They learned a lesson. They say, "Balloon rides are fun. But they can be dangerous. We will be careful next time."

One day, a polite cat named Tom found a sack. He wanted to see what was inside. Tom opened the sack and found a soft blanket. He was very happy. Tom decided to take the blanket to his friend, a dog named Max. Max was cold and needed something warm. Tom carried the blanket in the sack to Max's house. When Tom gave the sack to Max, Max was very happy. He took out the blanket and wrapped it around himself. Now, Max was warm and cozy. Tom and Max played all day, and they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time there was a chubby cat named Flash. Flash liked to snack and he was very fast. One day, Flash decided to go to the market. He wanted to find something special to eat. He ran around the market and soon he found what he was looking for--a big, juicy fish! Flash grabbed the fish and ran away. But Flash didn't know that the market had a guard. The guard saw Flash running away with the fish and he shouted "STOP!" But Flash didn't hear the guard, he was running too fast. The guard chased Flash, but try as he might, he couldn't catch up to him. Flash was too chubby and fast for the guard. Later that day, Flash enjoyed his delicious fish dinner. And from then on, he remembered to always watch his speed when he goes to the market.
