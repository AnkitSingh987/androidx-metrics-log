Category: Beginner
Title: Ben the Bunny and the Leaf

Once upon a time, in a calm and pretty garden, there lived a happy bunny named Ben. Ben loved to hop and play all day long. The garden was his favorite place to be. One sunny day, Ben found a big, green leaf. He wanted to share it with his friend, Tim the bird. Ben tried to tear the leaf into two pieces, one for him and one for Tim. At first, it was hard, but then, with a big hop and a strong pull, he tore the leaf. Ben hopped to Tim and gave him half of the leaf. They sat in the calm garden, eating their leaf and enjoying the warm sun. It was a good day for Ben and Tim, and they were very happy friends.

Once upon a time, a nice doctor named Dr. Lily liked to wander in the park. She went there every day after work. She liked to see the birds, the trees, and the kids playing. One day, while Dr. Lily was wandering, she saw a little girl crying. She went to the girl and asked, "Why are you crying?" The girl said, "I fell and hurt my knee." Dr. Lily looked at her knee and saw a small cut. Dr. Lily said, "Don't worry, I am a doctor. I can help you." She took out a band-aid from her bag and put it on the girl's knee. The girl stopped crying and smiled. She said, "Thank you, Dr. Lily!" Now, they both wander in the park together, and they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a man named Jack. Jack was a nice man and he loved to teach. He wanted to meet other people who were interested in his teaching. One day, Jack decided to go to the store to buy some things he would need for his teaching. He had brought his purse with him, but it was very expensive. Jack was about to give up on his shopping, but then he met a kind lady in the store. She smiled at him and asked him why he was so sad. Jack told her all about his goal to teach. The kind lady was very nice and she offered to help Jack. She said she had some special things which could be used for teaching. She showed him all the things she had and said he could have them for free. Jack was so happy that he had met the kind lady! He said thank you and took the things home. With the help of the things, Jack was able to start teaching and he was very successful. He never forgot how he had met the kind lady in the store.

One day, a bird named Bob wanted to display his original dance. He went to the roof of a big house. He danced and danced on the roof. All the other birds watched him. On the ground, a little girl named Lily saw Bob. She said, "I want to dance like Bob!" Lily tried to dance, but she didn't know how. She asked her mom to teach her. Her mom said, "You can do it, Lily! Just keep trying!" Lily practiced her dance every day. She watched Bob and tried to copy him. Soon, she could dance like Bob. All her friends were happy for her. They all danced together, just like they saw Bob do on the roof.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, green mint plant in his yard. He loved the mint plant because it smelled so nice. One day, Tim saw that the mint plant was broken. The wind had made it lean and fall over. Tim was very sad. Tim's mom came outside and saw Tim looking at the broken mint plant. She told Tim that they could fix it together. Tim and his mom put a stick next to the plant and tied the plant to the stick. They gave the mint plant some water and waited. After a few days, the mint plant started to stand up straight again. Tim was so happy! He learned that when something is broken, we can try to fix it with help from others. And when we work together, we can make things better.
