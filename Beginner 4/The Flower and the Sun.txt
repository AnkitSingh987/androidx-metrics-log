Category: Beginner
Title: The Flower and the Sun

Once upon a time there was a very pretty flower, and it was called a Bloom. One day the bloom found a game and was very excited. She showed it to all her friends and asked if they wanted to play. The other plants agreed and everyone started playing. The bloom was winning, until one windy day when the game blew away. The bloom was so frustrated and couldn't understand where it had gone. Just then, the sun peeked through the clouds and said, "I've seen your game, Bloom! It's over there in the grass." The Bloom smiled and said, "Thank you, Sun! I'm so glad that you found it." The Bloom and her friends got back to playing their game, laughing and having fun. From then on, the Bloom was reminded to always stay alert and never give up, even when things get tough!

Once upon a time, there was a team of four friends. They were Tom, Tim, Sam, and Kim. They liked to play together. One day, they found a big, fragile egg. It was so big that they had never seen anything like it before. They were very curious about what was inside the egg. Tom said, "We should set the egg down gently so it won't break." They all agreed and set the egg on a soft bed of grass. They decided to watch the egg and wait for it to hatch. As they waited, they played games and had fun. One day, while they were playing, they heard a small crack. They looked at the egg and saw it was starting to hatch. They were very excited and watched as a baby bird came out of the egg. The baby bird looked at the team and said, "Thank you for taking care of my egg. I am so happy to have friends like you." The team was so happy that they had a new friend. They played together every day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play at the park with her friends. They would go on the swings and slide down the slide. They had lots of fun at the park. One day, Sue went home after playing at the park. Her mom was in the kitchen using the oven. Sue asked, "Mom, what are you making in the oven?" Her mom said, "I am making a regular cake for us to eat later." Sue was very excited to eat the cake. She helped her mom mix the cake batter and put it in the oven. When the cake was done, they took it out of the oven and let it cool. Then, they ate the cake together. It was a fun day for Sue and her mom.

One day, a rare cat went for a walk. The cat was big and had pretty colors. It walked and walked until it saw a little girl. The little girl was playing with her ball. The cat wanted to surprise the girl. The cat hid behind a tree and waited. When the girl came near, the cat jumped out and said, "Surprise!" The little girl smiled and said, "Hi, cat! You are so pretty!" The girl and the cat played with the ball. They had so much fun. The girl said, "I like you, cat. You are my friend." The cat was very happy. They played until the sun went down. The rare cat and the little girl were the best of friends.

Once there was a lovely little girl named Amy. She had a big pink ball. She loved her pink ball very much. One day, she went to the park to play with her ball. She saw her friend Tom there. Amy said, "Hi Tom! Do you like my pink ball?" Tom said, "No, I don't like pink. I like blue." Amy and Tom did not agree. They both wanted to play with their own balls. Then, a big wind came and took Amy's pink ball away. Amy was very sad. Tom saw Amy was sad and said, "Don't worry, Amy. We can play with my blue ball together!" Amy smiled and they played together. In the end, Amy and Tom learned that it is okay to disagree about colors. What is important is to play and have fun together. And they became the best of friends.
