Category: Beginner
Title: Tony and Jim and the Gun

Once upon a time there were two friends, Tony and Jim. Tony loved to play silly games and Jim loved to be outside. One day, Tony grabbed his gun and asked Jim to play. But Jim didn't want to play with a gun. He wanted to go explore the outdoors. Tony got angry and shook the gun in Jim's face. Jim was scared and ran away. Tony tried to follow him, but suddenly the gun changed and became a toy gun. Tony was confused and dropped the toy gun. He ran after Jim and tried to tell him it was just a silly toy. Jim wasn't sure, so he kept running from Tony. Tony eventually caught up to Jim and convinced him that the silly toy gun wasn't dangerous. They both laughed and went out to explore the outdoors. From then on, they enjoyed all the silly games they could play together.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play together. But today they disagree. Lily wants to go to the party. Ben does not want to go. "Why not?" Lily asks. "The party will be fun. There will be cake and games and balloons." "I don't like parties," Ben says. "They are too loud and too crowded. I want to stay home and read my book." Lily is sad. She wants Ben to come with her. She tries to make him change his mind. "Please, Ben," she says. "The party is for Mia. She is our friend too. She will be happy if you come. And maybe you will like the party. You can play with me and the other kids." Ben shakes his head. He does not want to go. He likes his book. He likes to be quiet and alone. "No, thank you," he says. "You can go to the party. I will stay here. Maybe we can play later." Lily is not happy. She thinks Ben is being silly and rude. She does not understand why he does not want to go. She thinks he will miss the fun. "Fine," she says. "I will go to the party. But I will not play with you later. You are not a good friend." She leaves the house. She slams the door. Ben hears her go. He feels sad too. He likes Lily. He does not want to make her angry. But he does not like parties. He looks out the window. He sees Lily walking to the party. She is wearing a pink dress and a bow. She looks pretty. He sees the sun shining and the birds singing. He sees the party house. It is decorated with colorful streamers and balloons. He hears the music and the laughter. He wonders if he is missing the fun. He looks at his book. It is gray and boring. He does not want to read it anymore. He wants to play with Lily and the other kids. He wants to be a good friend. He puts down his book. He runs out of the house. He calls Lily's name. He hopes she will hear him. He hopes she will forgive him. He hopes he can still go to the party.

One day, a nice man named Tom went to work. He worked in a big place where he found rocks with minerals. Tom liked his work because he found many pretty rocks. One day, while Tom was working, he found a very big rock. The rock could talk! The rock said, "Hi Tom, I am a magic rock. If you are nice to me, I will give you a surprise." Tom was very surprised but he was nice to the rock. Tom and the rock became good friends. One day, the rock said, "Tom, I will give you a gift. Close your eyes." Tom closed his eyes. When he opened them, he saw many small rocks that could talk too! Now Tom had many rock friends and they all worked together to find more minerals.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a boy named Tim. He had a little garden where he liked to grow things. One day, Tim found an interesting bean. He decided to plant it in his garden and see what would happen. After a few days, the bean grew into a big, tall beanstalk. Tim was very excited and called his friend, Sam. "Look at my beanstalk!" said Tim. "Wow! Can we use it to climb up and see what's there?" asked Sam. Tim thought it was a great idea, so they both started to climb the beanstalk. Up in the sky, they found a beautiful land with many fun things to do. They played with the clouds and slid down the rainbows. They had the best day ever. In the end, they climbed back down the beanstalk and went home. They never forgot the interesting bean that brought them so much joy.
