Category: Beginner
Title: Amy the Girl and the Dog

One day, a little girl named Amy got a new pet. It was a cute, small dog. She named her dog Buddy. Amy and Buddy played and had fun all day. They loved to run and jump in the park. One day, while playing, Buddy saw a big dog. The big dog barked at Buddy. Buddy was scared and ran away. Amy was sad and started to cry. She wondered where Buddy went. She looked everywhere for her lost pet. Amy asked her friends to help find Buddy. They all looked and called for Buddy. Finally, they found Buddy hiding under a tree. The big dog was gone. Amy hugged Buddy and promised to keep him safe. Now, Amy and Buddy play together, and they are more careful in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a big, pretty boat called a yacht. This yacht had a bell that could ring. The yacht liked to sail on the water with its friends, the ducks, and the fish. One sunny day, the yacht saw something in the water. It was an attractive hat. The yacht wanted to show its friends. So, it rang the bell. Ring, ring, ring! The ducks and the fish came to see the hat. The yacht, the ducks, and the fish all liked the pretty hat. They played with it all day, splashing in the water and having fun. At the end of the day, they said goodbye and went home. The yacht, the ducks, and the fish were happy to have a new friend, the attractive hat.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a graceful bird. The bird loved to fly high in the sky. The bird was friends with everyone in the town. One day, the bird was flying in the sky and saw mist. The mist was soft and wet. The bird liked the mist. The bird played in the mist and felt happy. The bird knew the value of being kind to others. The bird shared the mist with all the friends in the town. Everyone was happy and loved the graceful bird. The bird and the town lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to paint. One day, he wanted to paint his pants. He thought it would be fun to have colorful pants. Tim asked his mom, "Can I paint my pants?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful not to make a mess." Tim was very happy and started to paint his pants with many colors. While Tim was painting, a small cat came into the room. The cat was frightened by the colors on Tim's pants. The cat jumped on Tim's pants and they both fell into the paint. Now, the cat had colorful fur too! Tim and the cat looked at each other and laughed. They had made a big mess, but they had fun. Tim's mom came into the room and saw the mess. She was not mad, but she said, "Next time, let's paint outside!"

Once upon a time, there was a child named Tim. Tim was a happy child who liked to play with his toys. One day, Tim saw a new toy in the store. He wanted it very much, but his mom said no. Tim was angry and began to cry. The next day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw another child playing with the same toy he wanted. Tim was still angry and wanted to take the toy away. But, he remembered how sad it made his mom when he cried. So, Tim decided to be nice and asked the child if he could play with the toy too. The child said yes, and they played together. Tim was happy and didn't cry anymore. He learned that being nice and sharing with others made him and his mom happy. And that was the best feeling of all.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house near a church. Lily liked the church because it was calm and quiet. She would go there to play and sing songs. One day, Lily's mom asked her to help unpack a box. Inside the box were toys and books. Lily was excited to find a new toy car. She showed it to her friend, Tim. "Look, Tim! I have a new car!" said Lily. Tim smiled and said, "Wow! It is nice!" Together, Lily and Tim went to the church to play with the new car. They had fun racing it on the calm church floor. They laughed and played until it was time to go home. Lily was happy that she had helped her mom unpack and found a new toy to share with her friend.
