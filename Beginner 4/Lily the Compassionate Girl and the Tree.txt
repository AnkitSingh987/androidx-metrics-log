Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Compassionate Girl and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a compassionate girl named Lily. She had a pretty blouse that she loved to wear. One day, she went to play outside with her friends. While they were playing, a big wind came and blew Lily's blouse away. It got stuck in a tree. Lily and her friends tried to get it down, but they couldn't reach it. They were sad and didn't know what to do. Then, they had an idea. They found a long stick and used it to lock the tree branches together. They pulled and pulled until the blouse came down. Lily was so happy to have her blouse back, and they all continued to play together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a rare, beautiful sunset. Everyone loved to see it. A little boy named Tim wanted to see it too. He asked his mom, "Can I see the rare sunset?" His mom said, "Yes, but you must be home before dark." Tim went to the hill to see the sunset. He saw many kids playing there. They were having fun. Tim wanted to play too. He thought, "I want to gain new friends." So, Tim played with the kids. They all had a great time. But soon, it was getting dark. Tim had to go home. He said goodbye to his new friends. He ran home to his mom. Tim learned that he could gain friends and still keep his promise. The moral of the story is to always keep your promises and you can gain good things in life.

Mira and Ravi were friends who liked to play in the garden. One day, they found a big melon on a vine. It was green and round and smelled sweet. "Let's take it home and eat it!" Mira said. "But how will we carry it?" Ravi asked. "It is too big for our hands." Mira had an idea. She ran to the shed and brought a wheelbarrow. She put the melon in the wheelbarrow and pushed it. Ravi helped her. They were very happy and proud of their melon. They met a lady on the way. She smiled and said, "What a nice melon you have! Where did you get it?" "We found it in the garden," Mira said. "It is ours." The lady nodded. She said, "It is a very big melon. Do you think it will fit in your house?" Mira and Ravi looked at each other. They had not thought of that. They wondered if the melon would fit through the door or the window. They felt a little worried. The lady saw their faces. She said, "Don't worry, children. I have a knife and a bowl. I can help you cut the melon and share it with me. Then you can take some pieces home and eat them." Mira and Ravi liked the idea. They thanked the lady and followed her to her house. She cut the melon and gave them each a big slice. It was juicy and sweet. They ate it and felt happy. The lady said, "You are very humble and kind, children. You are willing to share your melon with me. I am glad to have met you." Mira and Ravi smiled. They said, "You are very nice and helpful, lady. You are our friend." They hugged the lady and took some melon pieces home. They learned that sometimes things don't fit, but that's okay. They can still enjoy them and share them with others.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was a daughter who was a bit spoiled. Her mom and dad gave her lots of toys and treats. Lily liked to play with her toys all day. One day, Lily's mom asked her to help load the car. They were going on a trip to see grandma. Lily said, "No, I want to play with my toys." Her mom was not happy, but she loaded the car by herself. On the way to grandma's house, Lily's mom told her, "You should help when asked. It's good to help others." Lily felt sad and said, "I'm sorry, mom. I will help next time." When they got to grandma's house, Lily helped unload the car. She learned that helping others made her feel happy.
