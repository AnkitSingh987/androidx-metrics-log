Category: Beginner
Title: The Bird and the Mouse

Once upon a time, there was a little bird who loved to sing. Every day, the bird would fly around and sing happy songs. The bird had a friend, a dull gray mouse, who liked to listen to the bird's songs. One day, while the bird was singing, the mouse found a shape on the ground. It was a big, round shape. The mouse was very excited and showed the shape to the bird. The bird stopped singing and looked at the shape, too. Together, the bird and the mouse played with the shape. They rolled it around and had lots of fun. Then, the bird started to sing a new song about the shape. The mouse danced to the song, and they both had a great day.

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys and books. But they also like to look at other things in the house. They are very nosy. One day, they find a jar on the shelf. It is full of shiny things. They want to see what they are. They climb on a chair and reach for the jar. They are not careful. The jar falls down and breaks. The shiny things spill out. They are pennies. "Wow, look at all the pennies!" Sara says. "Let's count them!" "No, let's shake them!" Ben says. He grabs a handful of pennies and shakes them in his hand. He likes the sound they make. But he shakes them too hard. Some of the pennies fly out of his hand. They hit Sara in the face. She cries out in pain. She has a cut on her cheek. It bleeds. "Ow, ow, ow!" Sara says. "You hurt me, Ben! You are mean!" "I'm sorry, Sara!" Ben says. He drops the pennies and runs to her. He hugs her and says he is sorry. But Sara is still angry. She pushes him away. Mama hears the noise. She comes to see what is wrong. She sees the broken jar, the pennies, and the twins. She is very mad. "What have you done?" Mama says. "You are very naughty! You could have hurt yourselves or broken something else! You are not allowed to touch things that are not yours! Go to your room, now!" Sara and Ben are very sad. They go to their room. They have no toys, no books, and no pennies. They have no fun. They wish they were not so nosy.

Once upon a time, there was a big red tomato. It was so enormous that it could not fit in the garden. Everyone in the town loved the tomato because it was very special. One day, a little boy named Tim and his friend, Sally, went to see the enormous tomato. They wanted to celebrate because it was the biggest tomato they had ever seen. Tim said, "Wow, Sally! Look at this big tomato!" Sally replied, "Yes, Tim! Let's celebrate and have a party with our friends!" So, Tim and Sally told all their friends about the enormous tomato. They all came together to celebrate and have fun. They played games, ate yummy food, and danced around the big red tomato. The enormous tomato made everyone very happy, and they all had a great time.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was excited because he was going on a trip with his family. He wanted to pack his favorite toy, a red marble, to play with on the trip. Tim put the red marble in a small bag and put the bag in his big bag. He was very happy that he could bring his favorite toy with him. He knew he would have lots of fun playing with the marble on the trip. When Tim and his family got in the car, Tim took out the small bag with the red marble. He played with it all the way to their new place. Tim had so much fun on the trip with his family and his favorite red marble.

Tom and Lily were playing with their toy cars. They liked to make them go fast and make zoom sounds. Tom had a red car and Lily had a blue car. They had a race on the floor. "Ready, set, go!" Tom said. He pushed his car hard and it went ahead of Lily's car. He laughed and said, "I am the fastest! You are too slow!" Lily felt sad. She pushed her car harder, but it did not catch up with Tom's car. She said, "That's not fair! Your car is bigger and newer than mine!" Tom did not listen. He kept pushing his car and making zoom sounds. He wanted to increase his lead and win the race. He did not see the big book on the floor. His car hit the book and flipped over. He gasped and said, "Oh no! My car!" Lily saw what happened. She stopped her car and ran to Tom. She helped him pick up his car and checked if it was broken. She said, "Are you okay? I'm sorry your car crashed." Tom felt ashamed. He realized he was not nice to Lily. He said, "I'm okay. Thank you for helping me. I'm sorry I was mean to you. Your car is not slow. It is just different. We can both have fun with our cars." Lily smiled and said, "That's okay. I forgive you. We can be friends and share our cars. We can take turns to make zoom sounds." Tom and Lily hugged and played with their cars together. They learned that being fast is not the most important thing. Being kind and fair is more important. They had more fun when they played nicely.
