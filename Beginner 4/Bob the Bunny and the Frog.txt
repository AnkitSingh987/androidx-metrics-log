Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Bunny and the Frog

One day, a little bunny named Bob went on a walk. He saw a big cow. The cow said, "Hi, Bob! Do you want some milk?" Bob said, "Yes, please!" The cow gave Bob some milk. Bob was very happy. Bob hopped back home with the milk. On his way, he met a frog. The frog said, "Bob, can I have some milk too?" Bob said, "Sure!" and gave the frog some milk. The frog was very happy. Then, something unexpected happened. The milk turned the frog into a big, fine bird! The bird said, "Thank you, Bob! This milk is magic!" Bob and the bird hopped and flew together, sharing the magic milk with their friends.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim went to the subway with his mom. Tim was very excited to ride the subway for the first time. The subway was big and noisy, but Tim was not scared. He held his mom's hand tightly as they walked. As they waited for the subway, Tim's mom told him, "Be careful, the subway can be dangerous if you don't pay attention. You must not go too close to the edge." Tim nodded and stayed close to his mom. Suddenly, a cat walked by, and Tim let go of his mom's hand to pet it. When the subway came, the cat got scared and ran. Tim ran after the cat, and he didn't see that he was getting too close to the edge. "Tim, stop!" his mom yelled, but it was too late. Tim tripped and started to fall. Everyone gasped as Tim fell onto the subway tracks. Just then, a kind man jumped down and quickly picked up Tim. The man tried to climb back up, but he couldn't do it fast enough. The subway hit the man, and he was gone. Tim was safe, but the kind man who saved his life was not. Tim and his mom were very sad, and they never forgot the man's bravery. From that day on, Tim always listened to his mom and never went too close to the edge.

Once upon a time, in a quiet little town, there was a boy named Tom. Tom loved to ride his bike. His bike had two pedals. Tom would push the pedals with his feet to make the bike go. One day, Tom saw his friend, Sue. Sue was pointing at a big tree. "Look, Tom!" she said. "I see a cat in the tree!" Tom looked up and saw the cat too. Tom and Sue wanted to help the cat. Tom had an idea. He rode his bike with the pedals to the tree. "I will climb the tree and save the cat," he said. Tom climbed up the tree and saved the cat. They were both very happy.

Ben likes to help his dad with the lawn. He has a small mower that makes noise but does not cut the grass. He follows his dad and pretends to mow the lawn too. He thinks he is very intelligent and hard-working. One day, his dad says he has to go inside for a minute. He tells Ben to stay on the lawn and not to touch anything. Ben nods and says he will finish the lawn by himself. He is very proud and happy. But then he sees a big red button on his dad's mower. He wonders what it does. He is very curious and wants to try it. He thinks it might make the mower go faster or louder. He forgets what his dad said and goes to the mower. He pushes the big red button. The mower starts to move by itself. It goes very fast and loud. It cuts the grass and the flowers and the bushes. It goes out of the lawn and into the street. It scares the cars and the people. Ben is very scared and sorry. He does not know how to stop the mower. He runs after the mower and tries to catch it. He shouts for his dad. His dad hears him and comes outside. He sees the mower and the mess. He is very angry and worried. He runs to the mower and turns it off. He hugs Ben and tells him he is glad he is safe. He also tells him he is very naughty and foolish. He says he has to fix the lawn and the plants. He says Ben has to help him and say sorry to the neighbors. Ben cries and says he is sorry. He says he will not touch the mower again. He says he is not very intelligent after all.
