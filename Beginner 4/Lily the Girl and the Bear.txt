Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Bear

One day, a little girl named Lily found a useful laser. She thought it would be fun to decorate her room with it. So, she took the laser and went to her room. In her room, Lily pointed the laser at the wall and it made pretty colors. She was very happy. She said, "Look, Mr. Teddy, the laser makes our room pretty!" Mr. Teddy, her toy bear, just sat and watched. Lily's mom heard her talking and came to see what was happening. She saw the pretty colors on the wall and said, "Wow, Lily, that's a nice way to decorate your room with the useful laser! You did a great job!" Lily smiled and kept playing with the laser, making her room pretty and colorful.

Once upon a time, in a quiet little town, there was a big gate. The gate was in a park where kids liked to play. Many kids would come to the park to have fun. One day, two friends, Tim and Sam, went to the park. Tim wanted to play by the gate, but Sam wanted to play far away from it. They could not agree. They disagreed about where to play. Tim played by the gate, and Sam played far away. The gate started to shake and it fell down. It fell on Tim and he got hurt. Sam was sad and ran to help his friend. From that day, they never disagreed again.

Lily and Tom were friends. They liked to play in the park. One day, they found a pepper on the ground. It was red and shiny. "Look, a pepper!" Lily said. "Can we keep it?" "I don't know," Tom said. "Maybe it belongs to someone." "Let's keep it," Lily said. "It can be our toy. We can pretend it is a ball or a car or a hat." "Okay," Tom said. "But we have to be careful. Peppers can be hot and make you cry." They took the pepper and played with it. They threw it in the air, rolled it on the grass, and put it on their heads. They had fun. But then, a big dog came. It saw the pepper and wanted it. It ran towards Lily and Tom and barked. "Go away, dog!" Lily said. "This is our pepper. You can't have it." But the dog did not listen. It jumped on Lily and Tom and bit the pepper. It was very hot. The dog cried and ran away. Lily and Tom were scared and sad. The pepper was gone. They had no toy. They had no friend. They went home. They did not play in the park again.

Once upon a time, there was a smart dog named Spot. Spot had a big yard on one side of his house. He liked to play in the yard with his ball. One sunny day, Spot's ball went under a big tree. Spot tried to get the ball, but it was too far away. He thought and thought about how to get the ball. Then, he had a great idea! Spot found a long stick and managed to push the ball out from under the tree. He said, "I did it! I am so smart!" Spot was happy and played with his ball all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a kind old man who liked to take walks in the park. One day, while he was strolling along the path, he saw a tree with juicy peaches hanging from its branches. The old man was feeling very hungry, so he decided to pick one of the peaches and take a bite. "Mmmmm, it's very fine," said the old man, taking another bite. "Oh, hello there!" he exclaimed as he saw a small, three-year-old girl standing beneath the tree. The old man offered the girl a peach. She smiled and took a bite. "It is delicious!" she said. The girl took another bite, and then a third. "Thank you for the peach, old man," she said. Then, she ran off to tell her friends about the fine peach she had found under the tree. The old man just smiled and took one last bite of the delicious peach before he continued on his walk.
