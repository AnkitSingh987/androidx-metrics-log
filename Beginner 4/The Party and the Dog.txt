Category: Beginner
Title: The Party and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a big party. All the kids were playing and having fun. There was cake, balloons, and lots of games. One little boy named Tim was playing with his toy car. He was having a great time, going fast and making car sounds. But then, a fierce dog came into the party. The dog saw Tim and wanted to play too. The dog took Tim's toy car in his mouth and ran away. Tim was very sad and wanted his toy car back. He thought of a plan. He took a big balloon and went near the dog. Then, Tim gave the fierce dog a big slap with the balloon. The dog dropped the toy car and ran away. Tim was happy and went back to the party. Everyone cheered and they all had a great time.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim had a wide smile. He loved to eat toast for breakfast. Every morning, Tim would sit at the table and wait for his mom to make toast. One day, Tim's mom was very busy. She forgot to make toast for him. Tim was sad and didn't know what to do. He wanted to make toast by himself. So, he went to the kitchen and tried to use the toaster. But Tim was too small to use the toaster. He couldn't reach it, and he didn't know how it worked. He tried to push the buttons and pull the levers, but nothing happened. Then, the toaster started to smoke. Tim was scared and didn't know what to do. His wide smile was gone. Soon, the smoke turned into a small fire. Tim's mom saw the fire and ran to the kitchen. She put out the fire, but the toaster was broken. Tim was very sad, and his mom was upset. They couldn't have toast anymore. From that day on, Tim didn't have toast for breakfast. He missed his toast and his wide smile. He learned that he should ask for help when he doesn't know how to do something.

Once upon a time, there was a little spider. The little spider liked to play in a big, green tree. One day, while the little spider was playing, he saw a big, red ball coming. He wanted to warn his friends. The little spider told his friends, "Watch out for the big, red ball!" But the friends did not listen. They kept playing and having fun. The little spider was sad because his friends did not listen to his warn. Then, something unexpected happened. The big, red ball did not hit the tree. Instead, it bounced away and went far, far away. The little spider and his friends were safe. They were happy that nothing bad happened, and they all played together again. The little spider learned that sometimes, things are not always as they seem.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved to dress up fancy. She loved wearing her fancy dress with her sparkly shoes and bow in her hair. One day, the little girl went out for a walk. She noticed a rail beside the path and she thought it looked like fun. She said to her mom, "Mom, can I wrap the rail?" Her mom said, "Yes, of course!" So, the little girl smiled and carefully wrapped her arms around the rail. She held on tightly and spun around and around. She laughed with joy and felt so free. Once she was done, she thanked her mom for letting her wrap the rail and said, "That was so much fun!" Her mom smiled and said, "Now go and show off your fancy dress!" And so the little girl skipped off, twirling her dress with a smile on her face.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy box with a tight lid. He loved to play with the toys inside the box. One day, he wanted to open the toy box but the handle was stuck. Tim tried to pull the handle but it was very tight. He did not know what to do. He asked his friend, the cat, for help. The cat looked at the toy box and just gave a shrug. Tim was sad, but he did not give up. He tried to open the toy box again and again. But the handle was still tight. Tim got very tired and sat down. He looked at the toy box and started to cry. He could not play with his toys anymore. And that was the bad ending for Tim.
