Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Salt

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He loved to eat delicious food. One day, he found a big bag in the kitchen. He opened it and it revealed salt. Tim thought, "I can use the salt to make my food even more delicious." Tim went to his friend, Sam, and said, "Look what I found! I can put it on our food to make it yummy." Sam was excited and they both decided to cook dinner together. But, they did not know how much salt to put in the food. They argued about it. Tim wanted to put a lot of salt, but Sam wanted only a little. They could not agree. Finally, they asked Tim's mom for help. She told them, "Too much salt is not good. Just put a little, and your food will be delicious." Tim and Sam listened to her and cooked dinner together. They put just a little salt, and it was very delicious. They were happy and learned that sometimes, it is better to listen to others.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. He lived in a big pond with his family. Fin was a frightened fish because he was scared of the big fish in the pond. He had a pretty scale on his body that shined in the sun. One day, Fin saw a big fish coming near him. He was so scared, he swam fast to hide behind a rock. The big fish swam by and did not see him. Fin was safe, but his pretty scale got slapped by a plant when he swam fast. Fin was sad that his pretty scale was hurt. But his mom told him not to worry. She said, "Fin, your scale will be okay. You are a brave fish for swimming fast when you were frightened." Fin felt happy and loved. From that day on, Fin knew he could be brave even when he was scared.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the creek. He liked to play there. He saw a big frog. The frog was green and fat. Tim wanted to talk to the frog. "Hi frog, do you want to eat with me?" Tim asked. The frog looked at Tim and said, "Yes, I like to eat bugs. But I feel guilty because my friend Bee does not like it when I eat her bug friends." Tim thought for a moment. He wanted to help the frog and Bee. He had an idea. "Why don't we find some food that you both like?" Tim said. The frog and Bee agreed to try. Tim, the frog, and Bee looked around the creek. They found some yummy berries. They all ate the berries together. The frog didn't feel guilty anymore. They were all happy and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a good player named Tom. Tom liked to play with his ball and his dog, Spot. They would play in the yard every day. One day, while they were playing, the ball went over the fence. Tom and Spot could not see it. So, Tom stood on his toes and tried to peek over the fence. He saw the ball in the neighbor's yard. Tom asked his mom if he could go get the ball. She said yes. Tom went to the neighbor's yard, got the ball, and came back. Tom and Spot were very happy. They played together all day long.

One day, a clown named Bobo went to the park. Bobo liked to make kids laugh. He wore a big red nose and had big shoes. Bobo was a foolish clown. At the park, Bobo saw a big box. He did not know what was inside. He wanted to open it. When he opened the box, a big cake appeared. Bobo was very happy. He wanted to share the cake with the kids. Bobo started to cut the cake. But when he cut it, something unexpected happened. The cake was not a real cake! It was a toy cake. The kids laughed and laughed at the foolish clown. Bobo felt silly but happy because he made the kids laugh.
