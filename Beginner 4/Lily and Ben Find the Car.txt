Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben Find the Car

Lily and Ben like to play pretend. They have a big box of dress-up clothes and toys. One day, they decide to play car drivers. They find hats and sunglasses and keys and a license. The license is a card with a picture and some letters. Lily says it is her license and she can drive any car she wants. Ben says no, that is not fair. He wants to drive too. He says he can change the license to make it his. He takes a crayon and draws on the card. He makes a new picture and some new letters. He says now it is his license and he can drive any car he wants. Lily is angry. She says Ben is crazy. He cannot change the license like that. He ruined it. She says he cannot drive any car he wants. He can only drive a toy car. She takes the keys and runs to the toy car. She gets in and drives away. Ben is sad. He says he is sorry. He says he just wanted to play. He says he will make a new license for Lily. He takes a paper and a crayon and draws a new card. He makes a nice picture and some nice letters. He says now it is Lily's license and she can drive any car she wants. Lily sees Ben. She sees the new license. She smiles. She says thank you. She says Ben is not crazy. He is nice. She says he can drive with her. She gives him the keys and invites him to the toy car. They get in and drive away. They have fun. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was playing outside her house. She saw many pretty flowers. Sue wanted to pick the flowers for her mom. Sue went to the garden and started to pick flowers. She picked red flowers, yellow flowers, and blue flowers. Sue held the flowers tight in her hand. She was happy to give the flowers to her mom. But then, a big wind came. The wind blew the flowers out of Sue's hand. Sue was sad. She could not give the flowers to her mom. The wind took the flowers far away. Sue went back to her house with no flowers.

One day, a little crab was walking on the sand near the sea. He saw a broken toy boat by the water. The crab was sad because he wanted to play with the boat, but it was broken. The crab said to his friend, the little fish, "Look at this broken boat. I wish I could play with it, but it is not good to play with now." The little fish thought for a moment and said, "Let's ask the big octopus for help. He can fix things." As they went to ask the octopus for help, they saw that the tide was coming in. The water was getting higher and higher. The crab and the fish had to hurry because the broken boat would soon rot if it stayed in the water for too long. The big octopus listened to the little crab and the little fish. He said, "I can fix your boat, but you must be fast. Bring it to me before the tide comes in more." The crab and the fish raced back to the broken boat and brought it to the octopus. The octopus fixed the broken boat just in time before the tide came in more. The little crab and the little fish were happy. They played with the boat all day long, and they thanked the big octopus for his help.

Once upon a time, there was a squirrel named Sam. Sam was restless. He liked to run and play all day. One day, Sam saw a big nut. He wanted to eat it. He picked up the nut and started to eat. A little bird named Tina saw Sam. Tina was hungry too. She flew down and said, "Can I have some nut too, please?" Sam was kind. He said, "Yes, you can have some." So, Sam and Tina shared the nut. They were happy and full. After they finish eating the nut, Sam and Tina became good friends. They played together every day. They were not restless anymore. They knew that sharing made them happy.
