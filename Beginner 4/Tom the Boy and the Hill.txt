Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Hill

One day, a boy named Tom saw a hill. He wanted to go up the hill to see the view. He asked his friend, Sam, to come with him. "Do you want to go up the hill with me?" Tom asked. Sam said, "Yes, let's go!" As they walked up the hill, Tom saw a big rock. He wanted to kneel on it to rest. It was not comfortable, but he was tired. Sam did not want to stop. He said, "I want to see the view now!" Tom was sad, but he got up and they kept going. When they got to the top of the hill, they saw the view. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew them down the hill. They fell and got hurt. They did not get to enjoy the view. The end.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a big yellow circle. She picked it up and saw that it was a yummy cookie. She wanted to taste it, but she knew she should ask her mom first. "Mom, can I taste this yellow cookie?" Lily asked. Her mom looked at the cookie and said, "No, Lily, that cookie belongs to your brother. He will be sad if you eat it." Lily felt bad and put the cookie back. Her brother came in and saw the cookie. "Thank you for not eating my cookie, Lily," he said. They both smiled and played together. In the end, Lily learned that it is important to ask before taking something that is not yours. She also learned that being kind to her brother made them both happy.

Sara loves to dance. She dances in the morning, she dances in the afternoon, she dances at night. She dances with her mom, she dances with her dad, she dances with her teddy bear. She dances when she is happy, she dances when she is sad, she dances when she is bored. One day, Sara wants to dance in the bathroom. She thinks the bathroom is a fun place to dance. She sees the faucet and the water. She turns on the faucet and the water comes out. She puts her hands under the water and feels the cold. She laughs and splashes the water. She says, "Water, water, dance with me!" But Sara does not see the soap on the floor. She steps on the soap and slips. She falls on the floor and hurts her knee. She cries and says, "Ow, ow, ow!" She turns off the faucet and the water stops. She feels the wet and the pain. She does not want to dance anymore. Her mom hears her cry and runs to the bathroom. She sees Sara on the floor and the water and the soap. She picks up Sara and hugs her. She says, "Oh, Sara, what happened? Are you okay?" She looks at Sara's knee and sees the red. She says, "You have a boo-boo. Let me get a band-aid." She takes Sara to the bedroom and puts a band-aid on her knee. She kisses Sara and says, "There, there, it will be better soon. You are a brave girl. Do you want to dance with me?" Sara nods and smiles. She says, "Yes, mom, I want to dance with you. But not in the bathroom. The bathroom is not a good place to dance." She hugs her mom and says, "I love you, mom. You make me happy." They dance together in the bedroom and laugh. Sara forgets the wet and the pain. She only feels the love and the joy. She dances with her mom, she dances with her dad, she dances with her teddy bear. She dances when she is happy, she dances when she is sad, she dances when she is bored. Sara loves to dance.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very excited because he had a wiggly tooth. He couldn't wait for the tooth to come out. He wanted to show it to his friends and his mom and dad. One day, Tim was playing in his room. He found a big, old box. Tim opened the box and saw something amazing. It was an ancient toy. The toy was very old and had a big smile with a missing tooth too! Tim took the ancient toy to his mom and dad. They were very surprised. They told Tim that the toy belonged to his grandpa when he was a little boy. Tim was so happy to have a toy just like his grandpa's. That night, as Tim was sleeping, his wiggly tooth finally came out! In the morning, Tim looked under his pillow and found a shiny coin. The tooth fairy had come to visit him. Tim ran to his mom and dad to reveal his lost tooth and the shiny coin. Tim put his tooth next to the ancient toy, and they both smiled with their missing teeth. Tim knew that he and the toy were now special friends. They would have many more adventures together, and Tim could always remember the day he found the ancient toy and lost his tooth.
