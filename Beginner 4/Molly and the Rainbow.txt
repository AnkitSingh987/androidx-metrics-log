Category: Beginner
Title: Molly and the Rainbow

One day, Molly was walking through the park. Everywhere she looked she saw people playing and laughing. Suddenly she yawned as she was feeling sleepy. She looked up and saw a wonderful sight. A beautiful rainbow was painted across the sky. "Wow, that is so pretty!" She exclaimed. Molly was so grateful for being able to see the rainbow that she yawned again. However, all the other children were too busy playing to notice the rainbow. Molly wanted to show everyone the beauty in the sky. She tried to call out to them, but they just kept playing. Molly was so determined to share the sight that she decided to try again. "Hey everyone! Look at the wonderful rainbow in the sky!" This time the other children looked up and were surprised at the sight. The children cheered with joy and then ran towards Molly to thank her. Finally, Molly was grateful that everyone was able to see the magical rainbow.

Once upon a time, there was a brave human named Tom. He lived in a small house with his mom, dad, and dog. Tom loved to play outside and run fast like the wind. One day, Tom saw a big red car zoom by his house. He thought it was so cool and wanted to zoom too. So, he put on his shoes and started running as fast as he could. His dog, Spot, ran with him and they had so much fun. Tom felt very brave as he zoomed around the yard with Spot. They played and laughed until the sun went down. Then, they went back to their small house, happy and tired. Tom knew he would always remember the day he learned to zoom like the big red car.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play together. One day, they want to travel to the sea. They ask their moms if they can go. "Yes, you can go, but be careful and stay close to us," their moms say. Anna and Ben are happy. They get in the car with their moms and drive to the sea. They see many things on the way. They see trees, flowers, cows, and birds. "Look, Ben, the sea is blue and shiny!" Anna says when they arrive. "Yes, and the sand is yellow and soft!" Ben says. They run to the beach and play with the sand. They make a big castle with a yellow flag. They also find some shells and stones. They put them on the castle to make it pretty. "Wow, your castle is so nice!" a girl says. She comes to Anna and Ben. She has a yellow hat and a yellow dress. "Thank you! What is your name?" Anna asks. "My name is Lily. I like your flag. Can I play with you?" the girl says. "Sure, you can play with us. We are Anna and Ben. We are friends," Ben says. They all play together and have fun. They travel to the sea and make a new friend. They are happy.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim loved to play. He had a big tap in his yard. Every day, he would run to the tap and play with the water. One sunny day, Tim met a new friend named Sam. "Let's play with the tap!" Tim said to Sam. They both ran to the tap and turned it on. But, something unexpected happened. The water that came out was not clear, it was spoiled and brown. Tim and Sam were sad. They wanted to play with the water, but they couldn't. Then, a kind neighbor saw them and said, "Don't worry, kids! I will fix the tap for you." The neighbor worked hard and fixed the tap. The water became clear again. Tim and Sam were so happy. They played with the water all day and became best friends. The tap brought them together, and they had lots of fun.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He walked by a creek. The creek had regular water, not too much and not too little. Tim liked to play near the creek. As Tim played, he saw a frog. The frog was green and small. Tim said, "Hi, frog! Do you want to play with me?" The frog said, "Yes, I want to play!" So, they played together near the creek. While they played, the frog showed Tim how to slap the water with his hand. Tim tried it and laughed. They both had fun slapping the water. At the end of the day, Tim said, "Bye, frog! I had fun playing with you!" The frog said, "Bye, Tim! Let's play again soon!"
