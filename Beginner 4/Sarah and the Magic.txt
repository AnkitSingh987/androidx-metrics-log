Category: Beginner
Title: Sarah and the Magic

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Sarah. She had a magic wand and wanted to use it to do great things. One morning, Sarah waved her wand and a stage appeared. It was big and had lots of steps on it. Sarah was excited and started to climb the stage. When she reached the top she waved her wand again. Suddenly, all the steps around her became alive and started to move. Sarah smiled. She had completed the first stage of the magical journey! The steps then began to turn into a long platform, so Sarah could travel far. She jumped on it and set off. Along the way, Sarah encountered a few challenges. With the help of her magic wand, she successfully conquered every stage. At the end of the journey, Sarah was thrilled as she had completed all the stages. She waved her wand one last time and the platform and steps disappeared. Sarah smiled: she was happy to have completed her magical journey!

Sara is a big girl. She likes to do things by herself. She is independent. She does not need help from Mom or Dad. One day, Sara wants to go to the park. She puts on her coat and shoes. She takes her ball and her doll. She says to Mom, "I go to park. Bye bye, Mom." Mom says, "Wait, Sara. You cannot go to park by yourself. It is too far and too dangerous. You need to go with me or Dad." Sara says, "No, Mom. I am big. I can go. I am independent." Mom says, "No, Sara. You are not big enough. You are still little. You need to stay with me or Dad. We love you and we want to keep you safe." Sara is sad and angry. She does not want to stay. She wants to go. She runs out of the door. She does not see the car. The car does not see her. The car hits her. Sara falls down. She is hurt. She cries. She drops her ball and her doll. The ball rolls away. The doll lands on a pine. The pine is prickly. The doll gets stuck. Mom hears Sara cry. She runs out of the door. She sees Sara on the ground. She sees the car. She sees the ball and the doll. She is scared and worried. She hugs Sara. She says, "Oh, Sara. I am so sorry. Are you okay?" Sara says, "Mom, I am sorry. I was bad. I did not listen. I wanted to go. I wanted to be independent. But I was wrong. I need you, Mom. I love you, Mom." Mom says, "I love you too, Sara. You are not bad. You are brave and smart. But you are also little. You need to be careful and follow the rules. You need to go with me or Dad. We are here to help you and protect you." Sara says, "Okay, Mom. I understand. I will go with you or Dad. I will not run away. I will be good." Mom says, "Good, Sara. Now let's go to the hospital. We need to check if you are hurt. And let's get your ball and your doll. They are your friends. They need you too." Sara says, "Okay, Mom. Let's go. Thank you, Mom." Mom and Sara go to the hospital. They get Sara's ball and her doll. The pine is not happy. It is lonely. It wants a friend too.

Once upon a time there was a man. He was feeling very heavy so he decided to get up. He was carrying something big and heavy. Then he went to the park to get some fresh air. He was walking and walking until he saw a pond. The man was so worn out that he thought he could get some rest by the pond. He got down to the ground and took a long nap. When he woke up, he felt much better, so he got up and kept walking. Soon he was feeling light and happy again. The end!

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the store with her mom. Lily's mom had a big bag with her. Inside the bag was a wallet. Lily's mom used the wallet to buy things. At the store, Lily saw a toy she really wanted. "Mom, can I have that toy, please?" she asked. Her mom looked at the toy and said, "Not today, Lily. We need to buy food first." Lily felt frustrated but she knew her mom was right. When they got home, Lily helped her mom unpack the big bag. As they took out the food, Lily found a surprise at the bottom of the bag. It was the toy she wanted! Her mom had bought it for her after all. Lily was so happy and gave her mom a big hug.
