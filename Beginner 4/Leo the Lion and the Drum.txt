Category: Beginner
Title: Leo the Lion and the Drum

Once upon a time, there was a big lion named Leo. Leo was very popular in the jungle. Everyone liked him. Every day, Leo would roar very loud and play his drum. All the animals would come to dance and have fun. One day, while Leo was playing his drum, a tiny mouse named Mia came to dance. All the animals were surprised to see Mia dance. They did not know that a little mouse could dance so well. Mia was very brave and danced right next to Leo. Then, something unexpected happened. As Leo roared and played his drum, he saw Mia dance and he stopped. Leo did not want to scare Mia away. Instead, he wanted to be friends with her. So, Leo and Mia became best friends and danced together every day. All the animals in the jungle were happy and loved to watch them dance.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy. He was 3 years old and his name was Jack. Jack loved springtime because the world was so green. He loved to run around his garden, feeling the soft grass under his feet and watching the flowers grow. One spring day, Jack was very hungry. He went to his kitchen to eat something. He opened the fridge and found a big, ripe apple. It was green and shiny and Jack loved it. He picked it up, took a big bite and he was so happy! Jack then went for a walk in the garden. Suddenly he heard a sound. It was a little bird. It said to Jack, "I want to eat some of your apple too!" Jack smiled and said, "Sure! It's green, juicy and delicious!" So Jack shared his apple with the little bird. Together they ate the apple until it was all gone! Jack was so happy that he had made a new friend. The End

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big slide and wanted to play on it. He ran to the slide and started to climb up the steps. At the top, he saw a knob. Tim thought the knob was boring and didn't touch it. He sat down and slid down the slide very fast. Tim's friend, Sam, came to the park too. Sam went to the slide and saw the knob. "Hey Tim, did you see this knob?" Sam asked. Tim said, "Yes, but it's boring. I didn't touch it." Sam thought maybe the knob was not boring, and he turned it. Suddenly, the slide became very slippery. Tim didn't know about the slippery slide and climbed up again. This time, when he slid down, he went too fast and fell off the slide. Tim got hurt and cried. Sam felt bad for turning the knob. The moral of the story is not to judge things too quickly. Sometimes, what seems boring can be important, and we should be careful when trying new things.

Lila and Ben went to the big water with Mom and Dad. They saw many fish and boats and birds. They also saw a dolphin show. The dolphins jumped and splashed and made funny noises. Lila and Ben clapped and laughed. One dolphin came close to the edge of the water. He looked at Lila and Ben with his big eyes. He opened his mouth and showed his teeth. Lila and Ben wanted to touch him. They asked Mom and Dad if they could. Mom and Dad said yes, but be careful. They gave Lila and Ben some fish to feed the dolphin. The fish were slimy and smelly. Lila and Ben held them in their hands and reached out to the dolphin. The dolphin took the fish from Lila's hand. He ate it and made a happy sound. He nudged Lila's hand with his nose. Lila smiled and stroked his skin. It was smooth and wet. The dolphin took the fish from Ben's hand. He bit it and made a bad sound. He spit it out and shook his head. He looked at Ben with his big eyes. The fish was bitter. Ben frowned and threw the fish back at the dolphin. The dolphin was angry. He splashed Ben with his tail. Ben was wet and cold. Mom and Dad said sorry to the dolphin. They said no to Ben. They said throwing is not nice. They said Ben should say sorry too. Ben was sorry. He said sorry to the dolphin. The dolphin nodded and made a funny noise. He gave Ben a kiss on the cheek. Ben smiled and hugged the dolphin. Lila and Ben said bye to the dolphin. They waved and thanked him for the show. The dolphin waved and thanked them for the fish. He jumped and splashed and made funny noises. Lila and Ben clapped and laughed.
