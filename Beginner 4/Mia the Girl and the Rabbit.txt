Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Rabbit

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk with her mom. They saw a pretty garden with many flowers. Mia saw a red rose and wanted to touch it. "No, Mia, don't touch the rose," said her mom. Mia was an obedient girl, so she did not touch it. They walked some more and saw a big tree. Under the tree, there was a small rabbit. "Look, mom!" Mia said, pointing at the rabbit. The rabbit came closer to Mia and started to talk. "Hello, Mia! I saw that you were a good girl and did not touch the rose." Mia and her mom were very surprised that the rabbit could talk. The rabbit said, "Because you were obedient, I will give you a gift." Then, the rabbit gave Mia a beautiful flower that smelled very nice. Mia and her mom thanked the rabbit and went home, happy with their new friend and the lovely gift.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went on a walk. They wanted to find a beautiful park. Tim's mom gave him a map to help him find the park. Tim put the map in his bag and they left the house. On their way, Tim and Spot met a girl named Sue. Sue asked, "Where are you going?" Tim said, "We are going to a beautiful park. Do you want to come with us?" Sue said, "Yes, please!" So, Tim, Spot, and Sue walked together. Tim looked at the map to find the park. But, something unexpected happened! The map was gone! Tim was sad. Then, Spot started to bark. He had found the map! Spot had brought the map back to Tim. They all laughed and went to the beautiful park.

Once upon a time, in a big farm, there was a pink pig named Pippa. Pippa loved to play in the hay with her friends. One day, Pippa and her friends found a big box in the hay. Pippa's friend, a little duck named Dolly, said, "Let's open the box!" They opened the box and saw a lot of balloons. Pippa had an idea, "Let's release the balloons and watch them fly!" Pippa, Dolly, and their friends released the balloons into the sky. They all laughed and clapped as they watched the balloons fly up, up, and away. Pippa and her friends had a fun day playing with the balloons and hay on the farm.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. They saw a big bench near the swings. Lily wanted to sit on the bench with her mom and eat their snacks. So they sat down, and Lily said, "Mom, I want more snacks, please." Her mom gave her more snacks, and they enjoyed eating them on the bench. After they finished, Lily saw a boy crying near the slide. She felt sad for him and wanted to help. She asked her mom if she could go talk to the boy. Her mom said, "Yes, go see if he needs a friend." Lily walked to the boy and said, "Hi, I am Lily. Why are you sad?" The boy looked at her and said, "I fell off the slide and now I feel ashamed." Lily smiled and said, "It's okay, we all fall sometimes. Do you want to sit on the bench with me and my mom?" The boy stopped crying and said, "Yes, thank you, Lily." They all sat on the bench and talked. The boy felt better and wasn't ashamed anymore. Lily was happy she could help her new friend.

One day, a little dog named Max was playing outside. He was having fun in the sun. Max saw a dirty ball on the ground. He picked it up and started to play with it. Soon, a stranger came near Max. The stranger wanted to play with Max and his ball. Max did not know the stranger. He was scared. The stranger said, "Hi, Max! Can I play with you and your ball?" Max thought about it and decided to be brave. He said, "Okay, but be nice." They played together and had a lot of fun. Max learned that the stranger was a good person. He did not bite the stranger. Instead, he made a new friend. The moral of the story is: don't be scared of new people, and always be nice.
