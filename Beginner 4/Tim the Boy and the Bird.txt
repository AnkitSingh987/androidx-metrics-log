Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Bird

One day, a little boy named Tim found a sack in his room. Inside the sack, there were many waffles. Tim was very happy because he loved waffles. He took the sack and went to the park to share the waffles with his friends. At the park, Tim met his friend, Sue. "Look, Sue! I have a sack full of waffles," Tim said. Sue was excited and took a waffle from the sack. They both ate the waffles and laughed. They thought the waffles were yummy. While they were eating, a big bird flew down and took the sack of waffles. Tim and Sue felt sad and stupid for not holding the sack tightly. They looked at the bird flying away with their waffles. Tim said, "Next time, we will hold the sack strong so no bird can take it away." Sue agreed, and they both went home to get more waffles.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim found a map. The map showed the way to a perfect park. Tim was very happy and wanted to go there to relax and play. He asked his mom if they could go to the park. His mom said yes, and they started walking. As they followed the map, they saw many fun things. They saw dogs, cats, and birds. They also saw a big tree with pretty flowers. Tim was so excited to get to the perfect park. He knew it would be the best place to relax and have fun. Finally, they got to the place on the map. But it was not a perfect park. It was a big, dirty pond with lots of bugs. Tim was sad that the map was wrong. They could not relax and play there. They had to go back home, and Tim did not have the fun day he wanted.

Once upon a time, there was a small house with a big door. The door was always locked. A little boy named Tim wanted to unlock the door. He knew that behind the door, there was a big yard with lots of green grass. Tim wanted to play on the grass. One sunny day, Tim found a light, shiny key under his bed. He knew it was the key to unlock the big door. He was very happy. Tim ran to the door and put the key in the lock. He turned the key and the door opened. Tim saw the light from the sun make the grass look very pretty. He ran outside and played on the soft grass all day long. He was very happy. Tim knew that he unlocked the door to a fun world of play.

Once there was a purple girl. She was very independent. One day she decided to put on her purple coat and go for a walk. She walked all the way by herself. She didn't need anyone to come with her! Soon she found herself in a park. She put her purple coat on the grass and started to play. She ran around, pretending to fly like a butterfly. After having so much fun, she put her coat back on and walked home. She was very proud to have been an independent purple girl.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a black pen that she loved very much. She would use her pen to draw pretty pictures every day. One day, Amy's friend Tim came to play. "Can I use your black pen?" Tim asked. Amy said, "Yes, but be careful. Don't squeeze it too hard." Tim took the pen and started to draw a big cat. As they were drawing, Tim squeezed the pen too hard. The black ink went all over the paper. "Oh no!" said Tim. "I'm sorry, Amy." Amy was sad but said, "It's okay. We can still have fun." Amy and Tim cleaned the mess and then played with other toys. They had a great day together, and Tim learned not to squeeze the pen too hard.

One day, a little girl named Lily found an unusual thread on the floor. It was shiny and pretty. She picked it up and started to gaze at it. Lily thought that the thread was very special. Lily showed the thread to her friend, Tom. Tom wanted the thread too. "Can I have it?" he asked. Lily said, "No, I found it first." Tom was sad and walked away. Lily felt bad that her friend was sad. Then, Lily had an idea. She found more threads and gave one to Tom. They both had shiny threads now. They played together and were happy. The moral of the story is to share with your friends, so everyone can be happy.
