Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Wheel

One day, a little girl named Lily found a broken toy in the open. It was a small car with a missing wheel. Lily was sad and wanted to fix it. She went to her friend, Tom, and said, "Look, the car is broken. Can you help me fix it?" Tom looked at the broken car and said, "I don't know. Let's try to find a wheel." They looked everywhere but could not find a wheel. They started to panic. They didn't know what to do. Then, they saw a big boy named Sam walking by. He was carrying a bag of toy wheels. Tom and Lily were scared to ask Sam for help because he was big and they thought he might be mean. But they knew they had to try. So, they went up to Sam and said, "Can we have a wheel to fix our broken car?" To their surprise, Sam smiled and said, "Of course! Here you go." He gave them a wheel and helped them fix the car. In the end, Tom and Lily learned that they should not panic and judge others by their looks. They made a new friend in Sam, and they all played with the fixed car together. The moral of the story is not to judge a book by its cover and always ask for help when you need it.

Once upon a time, there was a warm little house in the woods. A kind bunny lived there. The bunny liked to help his friends. One day, a squirrel came to the bunny's house. The squirrel felt sick. He said he ate a poison nut. The bunny wanted to help the squirrel feel better. The bunny thought of a way to help the squirrel. He found a big book that showed how to make the squirrel feel better. The book said to weigh some good nuts and give them to the squirrel. The bunny did this, and the squirrel felt better. They were happy and played together.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly lady named Grandma Sue. She lived in a small house with her cat, Fluffy. Grandma Sue loved to send letters to her friends and family. One day, Grandma Sue wrote a letter to her granddaughter, Lily. She wanted to tell Lily about her new cat, Fluffy. "Dear Lily," she wrote, "I have a new cat. Her name is Fluffy. She is very soft to touch. I hope you can come and play with her soon. Love, Grandma Sue." Grandma Sue put the letter in an envelope and walked to the mailbox. She put the letter inside and closed the mailbox. The next day, the mailman came and took the letter to send it to Lily. A few days later, Lily got the letter from Grandma Sue. She was so excited to read it. "Mommy, look!" Lily said, "Grandma Sue has a new cat named Fluffy. She says Fluffy is soft to touch. Can we go visit her soon?" Lily's mommy smiled and said, "Yes, we can go visit Grandma Sue and meet Fluffy next weekend." Lily was very happy, and she couldn't wait to see Grandma Sue and touch Fluffy, the soft cat.

One morning, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a huge tree. Tim wanted to climb it. His mom said, "Be careful, Tim!" Tim climbed the tree and found a big bird's nest. Inside the nest, there were three eggs. Two of the eggs were small, but one was very big. Tim thought, "This big egg does not belong here. I will replace it with a rock." Tim put the big egg in his bag and replaced it with a rock. He climbed down the tree and showed the egg to his mom. She was surprised and said, "Tim, we need to put the egg back. It's not ours." Just then, a big bird came to the tree. It looked at the nest and saw the rock. The bird was sad. Tim felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, bird. I will put your egg back." He climbed the tree again and replaced the rock with the egg. The bird was happy, and Tim learned to not take things that were not his.
