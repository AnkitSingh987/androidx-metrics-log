Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Dog and the Toys

One day, a little dog named Max found a hidden toy under his bed. He was so happy to play with it. Max loved to play with his toys all day long. Max's friend, Sam the cat, saw the toy and wanted to play too. Max and Sam played with the toy together. They ran and jumped and had lots of fun. Sam was very good at finding hidden things. When they were done playing, they went for a walk. They saw a big truck on the road. The truck had a license on it. Max and Sam decided to follow the truck to see where it was going. The truck stopped at a park. Max and Sam were so excited because they loved the park. They played on the swings, slid down the slide, and made new friends. At the end of the day, Max and Sam went back home. They were very happy that they followed the truck and had a fun day at the park. They couldn't wait to tell their friends about their adventure.

John and his dad were at the garage. John had never seen the garage before and was feeling excited. His dad showed him around and explained it was a place to take care of cars. John liked the garage. He saw all the tools and said, "Dad, I like the garage!" His dad replied, "Oh, it's fine. But we're here to look at something special. Come, let me show you." As they approached, John saw an old car. His dad said, "This is my old car. I used to love it." John was confused. He said, "Dad, it's so rusty and old. How could you love it?" His dad smiled and said, "I love it because it was my first car. I know it looks old and rusty but I still love it." John smiled, understanding how his dad felt. He was glad he loved his old car. The end.

One day, a lovely girl named Sue went to the park. Sue saw a vendor selling yummy treats. Sue thought, "I want a treat!" She walked up to the vendor and asked, "Can I have a treat, please?" The vendor smiled and said, "Of course! What would you like?" Just then, a big wind came and blew all the treats away! Sue and the vendor were very sad. They did not know what to do. Sue had an idea. "Let's make new treats!" she said. The vendor thought it was a great idea. They made new treats together and shared them with everyone at the park. Everyone was happy and the day turned out to be lots of fun.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim was anxious to go to school. He woke up early, as the sun began to rise. Tim got dressed and ate his breakfast. He was excited to meet new friends and learn new things at school. On his way to school, Tim met a talking cat. The cat said, "Hello, Tim! Are you going to school?" Tim was very surprised and said, "Yes, I am! Can you come with me?" The cat agreed and they walked to school together. When they got to school, Tim and the cat found out that the school was only for animals! Tim was sad, but the cat said, "Don't worry, Tim! I will teach you fun things too!" So, Tim and the cat became best friends and learned together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim lived in a nest with his family. They lived in a tree near a big wire fence. One day, Tim's mom mentioned not to go near the wire fence. She said it was not safe. But Tim was a curious bird and wanted to see the fence up close. He felt a little guilty for not listening to his mom, but he went anyway. Tim flew close to the wire fence. He saw a big, scary cat on the other side. The cat jumped and tried to catch Tim. Sadly, Tim got stuck in the wire and could not fly away. The cat caught Tim and he was never seen again.
