Category: Beginner
Title: The Beautiful School and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful school in a small town. In this school, many little kids came to learn new things. One day, a new girl named Lily joined the school. She was shy and scared to talk to the other kids. At playtime, a boy named Tom saw Lily sitting alone. He walked up to her and said, "Hi, I am Tom. Do you want to play with me?" Lily looked up and smiled. She said, "Yes, I would like that." They played together and had a lot of fun. As the days went by, Lily and Tom learned many things together in the beautiful school. They learned to share their toys and help each other. The other kids saw them and started to do the same. Everyone in the school became kind and helpful. The moral of the story is that when we are kind and help others, we can make the world a beautiful place.

---

Lila and Tom were playing in the park. They liked to look for things in the grass and the trees. They found some stones, some leaves, and some twigs. Lila picked up a twig and tried to whistle with it. She put it in her mouth and blew hard, but nothing happened. "Look, Tom, I have a whistle!" she said. Tom looked at her and laughed. "That is not a whistle, Lila. That is a twig. You cannot whistle with a twig. You need a hole in it, like this." He showed her his whistle, which was a small plastic tube with a hole in the middle. "Wow, can I try?" Lila asked. "Sure, here you go." Tom gave her his whistle and took her twig. He blew on the twig, but nothing happened. He smiled and said, "See? It does not work." Lila put the whistle in her mouth and blew hard. She made a loud sound that scared some birds. She was very happy and said, "Look, Tom, I can whistle!" Tom clapped and said, "Good job, Lila! You are very smart. Can I have my whistle back?" Lila nodded and gave him his whistle. They took turns whistling and laughing. They did not see a hidden squirrel that was watching them from a tree. The squirrel was curious and wanted to try the whistle too. It waited until Lila and Tom were busy with something else, and then it jumped down and grabbed the whistle. It ran away with it, making a funny noise. Lila and Tom heard the noise and looked around. They saw the squirrel with the whistle in its mouth. They were surprised and said, "Hey, that is our whistle! Give it back, squirrel!" They chased the squirrel, but it was too fast and too clever. It climbed up another tree and hid the whistle in a hole. Lila and Tom could not reach it. They were sad and said, "Oh, no, we lost our whistle. How will we whistle now?" They looked at each other and had an idea. They put their lips together and blew. They made a sound that was like a whistle, but softer and sweeter. They smiled and said, "Look, Tom, look, Lila, we can whistle with our mouths!" They whistled with their mouths and forgot about the whistle and the squirrel. They were happy and had fun. The squirrel watched them and felt sorry. It took the whistle out of the hole and dropped it on the ground. It hoped that Lila and Tom would find it and forgive it. It whistled with its mouth and ran away.

Lily and Ben were playing in the backyard. They found a big box with some tools inside. Lily picked up a hammer and wanted to hit the box. "Look, Ben, I have a hammer!" she said. Ben saw the hammer and felt scared. He did not like loud noises or hard things. He urged Lily to put the hammer down. "Please, Lily, no hammer. Hammer is bad. Hammer makes noise. Hammer hurts box. Put hammer back," he said. Lily did not listen to Ben. She liked the hammer. She thought it was fun to hit the box. She hit the box harder and harder. The box made a loud bang. Ben covered his ears and cried. "Stop, Lily, stop! You are mean. You make me sad. You make box sad. Hammer is not fun. Hammer is mean," he said. Lily heard Ben cry and felt sorry. She did not want to make Ben or the box sad. She stopped hitting the box and put the hammer back. She hugged Ben and said sorry. "I'm sorry, Ben. I did not know you did not like hammer. I did not know hammer was mean. I will be gentle. I will not hit box. I will play with you. Do you want to play with me?" she said. Ben nodded and smiled. He forgave Lily. He was glad she was gentle. He wanted to play with her. They looked for something else to play with in the box. They found a ball and a teddy bear. They played with the ball and the teddy bear. They had fun. They were happy.
