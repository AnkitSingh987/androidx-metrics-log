Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Drawing

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to decorate her room with pretty things. One day, Lucy's friend Tom came to play. Tom was a generous boy who always shared his toys. "Let's decorate my room!" Lucy said to Tom. They found some paper and colors, and started to make beautiful drawings. They were having a lot of fun. But then, Lucy saw Tom's drawing and wanted it for her room. She tried to take it from him. "No, Lucy, this is mine!" Tom said, holding his drawing tight. Lucy was sad, and they both stopped playing. They didn't know what to do. Then, Lucy's mom came in and saw the problem. She had an idea. "Why don't you both make a new drawing together? Then you can share it and hang it in your room, Lucy." So, Lucy and Tom made a new drawing together. They took turns, each doing a little part of the drawing. They were happy to pass the colors to each other. In the end, they made a beautiful drawing that they both liked. Lucy was proud to hang it in her room, and Tom was happy to have helped his friend. They learned that sharing and being generous made their friendship even stronger.

One day, a boy named Tim found a mysterious thing in the woods. He did not know what it was. Tim wanted to examine it, so he picked it up. It was a weapon, but he did not know that. Tim showed the weapon to his friend, Sue. Sue also did not know what it was. They both looked at it and tried to find out. They thought it was a toy. In the end, they gave the mysterious weapon to a grown-up. The grown-up knew what it was and took it away. Tim and Sue played with their toys instead. They were happy and safe.

Once upon a time, there was an old, honest dog named Spot. Spot lived in a small house with a boy named Tim. Tim and Spot were best friends. They loved to play together. One day, Tim and Spot went to the park. They saw a cat stuck in a tree. The cat said, "Help! I need to get down!" Tim wanted to help the cat, but he was too small to reach. So, he asked Spot for help. Spot was a very honest dog, so he said, "I will try my best, Tim." Spot stood on his back legs and tried to help the cat down. With a little push, the cat was free! The cat said, "Thank you, Spot and Tim!" And they all became good friends.

Jack was very excited because he was going to ride his new bike! He had been waiting for so long for this day to come. His mom put his helmet on, and he was ready to go. "Ready to ride?", Jack's mom asked. "Yup! I ready!" Jack said. He hopped on the bike and his mom held him steady. She pushed him off and said, "Don't forget to count down to zero!" So Jack started to count, "Ten, nine, eight, seven..." By the time he reached zero, he was really far away. He had ridden his bike for a long time. He felt so proud when he finally arrived back at home. His mom clapped and cheered as he hopped off the bike. Jack smiled and said, "That was a very long ride!"

One day, a baby went to the park with her mom. They saw a big pond with ducks. The baby liked the ducks and wanted to play with them. The mom said, "Okay, but be careful not to splash too much." The baby went close to the pond and started to splash. She was having so much fun. The ducks liked the splashing too. They were all very happy and healthy. Suddenly, a big fish jumped out of the water! It made a huge splash! The baby was surprised and the ducks swam away. The baby laughed and clapped her hands. The fish smiled and splashed back into the water. The baby and her mom had a great day at the park, and they made a new fish friend.
