Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Grass

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to race with his friends in the park. One day, Tim and his friends found a baseball in the grass. A deaf girl named Sue was watching them play. She wanted to join, but she was too shy. Tim saw Sue and asked her to play with them. At first, Sue was scared, but then she joined the race. Tim and Sue raced together, and they both won. Tim learned that it's good to be kind and include everyone. And Sue learned that she could play and have fun, even if she was deaf.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a brave little bunny. The bunny loved to eat food with his friends. They would play and laugh all day long. One day, the bunny and his friends were eating food when something unexpected happened. A big, scary monster came out of the forest! The monster was hungry and wanted to take the bunny's food. The bunny and his friends were scared and didn't know what to do. But the brave bunny had an idea. He told the monster, "If you don't take our food, we will help you find more food." The monster thought about it and agreed. The bunny and his friends helped the monster find lots of food, and they all became friends. The monster didn't make the bunny and his friends suffer anymore, and they all lived happily in the forest together.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who loved to go to the beach. Every day she would play in the sand and swim in the sea. One day, the little girl woke up early to go to the beach. But when she got there, she saw that the beach was covered in bad tuna. Everywhere she looked, she saw lots of bad tuna on the beach. The little girl was very sad. She felt like she would never be able to play in the sand or swim in the sea again. Luckily, the little girl's mom saw what was happening. She went to the beach and worked hard to clean up the bad tuna. After a few hours of hard work, the beach was back to its usual sunny, sandy glory. The little girl was so happy! She thanked her mom for the hard work, and the two of them went to the beach to play and swim all day. From then on, the little girl always appreciated the beach and made sure to keep it clean.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to visit his friend, Sam. They wanted to play and have fun. Sam was very fit and had big muscles. Tim thought Sam was strong and wanted to be like him. They played in the park, running and jumping. They were having a great time. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew Sam's hat away. Tim and Sam tried to catch the hat, but it went too far. Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's use our muscles and run fast to get the hat!" They ran and ran, but they could not catch the hat. Then, a small bird flew down and picked up the hat with its beak. The bird brought the hat back to Tim and Sam. They were so happy and thanked the bird. They learned that even if you are not big and strong, you can still help your friends.

Lily and Ben liked to play with rods. They had many rods of different colors and sizes. They used the rods to make shapes, towers, bridges, and animals. One day, they found a big rod that was shiny and had a button on it. "Wow, look at this rod!" Lily said. "It is so pretty and cool. What do you think it does?" "I don't know," Ben said. "Maybe it is a magic rod. Let's press the button and see." They pressed the button and the rod made a loud noise. Then it started to grow longer and longer. It increased so much that it reached the ceiling and the walls. "Wow, it is a magic rod!" Lily said. "It can increase and make things bigger. Let's make our toys bigger too." They put their toys near the rod and pressed the button again. The toys also increased and became as big as the rod. They had a giant teddy bear, a giant ball, a giant car, and a giant doll. "This is so fun!" Ben said. "We have the biggest toys in the world. Let's play with them." They played with their big toys for a while. They hugged the teddy bear, bounced the ball, drove the car, and dressed the doll. They had a lot of fun. But they did not notice that the rod was still increasing. It pushed the ceiling and the walls harder and harder. It made cracks and holes in the house. It was too strong and too big. The rod was not a magic rod. It was a lazy rod. It did not stop when it should. It did not listen to the button. It did not care about the house or the toys or the children. It only cared about itself. Soon, the house could not hold the rod anymore. It broke and fell apart. The rod and the toys fell outside and made a big mess. Lily and Ben were scared and ran away. They looked at the broken house and the big rod and the big toys. They were sad and sorry. They wished they had not played with the rod. They wished they had listened to their mom, who had told them not to touch things they did not know. They learned a lesson. They never played with rods again.
