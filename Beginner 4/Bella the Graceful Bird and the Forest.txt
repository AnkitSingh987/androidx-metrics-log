Category: Beginner
Title: Bella the Graceful Bird and the Forest

One day, a graceful bird named Bella lived in a big tree. She had many friends in the forest. They liked to play and sing together. But one day, a big rain came. The rain was so heavy that it soaked everything in the forest. The rain made a big fire go out. The fire left a lot of ash on the ground. Bella's friend, a squirrel named Sam, saw the ash and was sad. He said, "Oh no! The ash is everywhere. What can we do?" Bella thought for a moment and said, "Let's clean up the ash together! We can make the forest pretty again." Sam liked the idea. They called their friends to help. The animals worked together to clean up the ash. As they cleaned, the sun came out. It made the forest warm and dry again. The animals were happy. They danced and sang together. The conflict was over, and the forest was beautiful again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boat that loved to sail. The boat had a friend named Olive. Olive was a tiny fish who lived in the big sea. They liked to play together. One day, Olive and the boat had a fun race. They were both competitive and wanted to win. Olive swam fast, and the boat sailed fast too. They raced from one side of the sea to the other side. In the end, Olive and the boat both reached the finish line at the same time. They were both very happy. They learned that playing together was more fun than winning. And from that day on, they played and sailed together, always having a great time.

Lily and Ben were best friends. They liked to play in the park with their bikes. One day, they saw a big truck with a long hose. The truck said "Gas" on the side. "What is that?" Lily asked. "I don't know. Maybe it is a snake," Ben said. They went closer to the truck. They saw a man with a hat and a suit. He was holding the hose and putting it in a hole in the ground. "Hello, kids. Do you know what I am doing?" the man asked. "No. What are you doing?" Lily and Ben said. "I am filling up the gas tank for the park. Gas is a kind of air that makes things work. Like your bikes, or the swings, or the lights," the man said. "Wow, that is cool. Can we see the gas?" Lily asked. "Sorry, no. Gas is invisible. You can't see it, but you can feel it and smell it. It is very important, but also very dangerous. You should never touch it or play with it," the man said. "OK, we won't. Thank you for telling us," Ben said. The man smiled and waved. He finished his job and drove away. Lily and Ben went back to their bikes. "Did you believe him?" Lily asked. "Yes, I did. He seemed nice and smart. And I think I felt the gas. It was warm and funny," Ben said. "Me too. I think gas is amazing. But we have to be careful," Lily said. They nodded and rode their bikes. They had a lot of fun in the park. They learned something new and made a new friend. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a green frog named Tom. Tom loved to jump and play by the pond. One day, he saw a big jar of jam near the pond. Tom was very happy and wanted to eat the jam. Tom tried to open the jar, but it was too tight. He pushed and pulled, but the jar did not open. Then, Tom had an idea. He jumped high and landed on the jar's lid. But, he slipped and fell into the pond. Tom was wet and sad. Just then, a big fish came out of the pond. The fish saw Tom and the jar of jam. The fish opened the jar with a quick flip of its tail. Tom was surprised and happy. Together, Tom and the fish shared the jam and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly little flea. He lived in a big, green park. He liked to jump and play all day. One day, the flea found a colorful toy. It was round and fun to play with. He wanted to take it home. But, he had to be careful not to break it. The flea jumped and played with the toy. He was so happy! But, he did not break it. He took it home to his family. They all played with the toy and had a jolly time. They were careful not to break it, too. And they all lived happily ever after.
