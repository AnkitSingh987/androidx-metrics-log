Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy gun. One day, he went to the park to play. He saw a big tree and decided to hide behind it. Tim was ready to shoot with his toy gun. While Tim was hiding, he saw a girl named Sue. Sue was sitting on a bench and eating a sandwich. The sandwich looked different. It was not like the ones Tim had at home. Tim wanted to know more about it, so he walked over to Sue. "Hi, I'm Tim. What are you eating?" he asked. Sue looked at him and smiled. "Hi Tim, I'm Sue. I'm eating a sandwich. It has cheese and tomato in it. That's why it looks different." Tim thought it sounded yummy. They became friends and played together all day.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a heavy trumpet in his toy box. He did not know how to play it, but he liked the sound it made. He blew into the trumpet and it made a loud noise. Tim's mom said, "Tim, it's time for your nap. You can play with your trumpet later." Tim went to his room and laid down for his nap. While he was sleeping, a tiny mouse named Max came out from under his bed. Max saw the heavy trumpet and wanted to try it too. He pushed and pushed, but the trumpet was too heavy for him. Suddenly, the trumpet made a loud noise, and Tim woke up. Tim saw Max with his trumpet and said, "Hey, you can play with my trumpet too!" Tim and Max became friends and played the trumpet together, making loud and happy music. They had so much fun that they forgot all about naps and played until the sun went down.

One day, a little girl named Anna wanted to make a cake. She put all the things she needed on the table. Anna was very happy. She started to stir the cake mix in a big bowl. She stirred and stirred until it was all mixed up. As she was stirring, she heard a loud noise. She looked around and saw a big, scared cat. The cat had knocked over a cup. Anna was not mad, but she did not know what to do. The cat looked at her and said, "I am sorry. I was trying to find some food." Anna was very surprised. She did not know that cats could talk. The cat told her that he could help her make the cake if she gave him some food. Anna agreed and they worked together. The cat helped her stir the cake mix and showed her how to make it even better. When they were done, the cake was the best one Anna had ever made. She felt like she had won a big victory. The cat was no longer scared and they became good friends. From that day on, Anna and the cat made many more cakes together, and they always shared their food with each other.

One day, a little girl named Lily went outside to play. She saw a big purple cloud in the sky. The cloud looked like a big cat. Lily thought it was funny. She told her friend Sam about the cloud. "Sam, look at the big purple cloud! It looks like a cat!" said Lily. Sam looked up and laughed. "It does look like a cat!" he said. They both watched the cloud as it moved across the sky. Lily and Sam played outside all day. They paused to look at the cloud every now and then. As the day went on, the cloud got bigger and darker. The wind started to blow. Lily and Sam knew it was going to rain soon. "Let's go inside before we get wet!" said Lily. Sam agreed, and they went inside just in time. The rain started to fall, but Lily and Sam were safe and dry. They watched the rain from the window, happy they had seen the big purple cloud before it brought the rain.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play in the park. He saw a big tree and thought it would be fun to climb. As he was climbing, he heard a soft voice say, "Help me, please!" Tim looked around and saw a small ghost. The ghost was stuck in the tree and needed help to get down. Tim was scared but wanted to help, so he said, "Don't worry, I will save you." Tim was patient and thought about how to save the ghost. He found a long stick and used it to help the ghost get out of the tree. The ghost was so happy to be free. It said, "Thank you for saving me, Tim! I want to be your friend." Tim was surprised but happy. He had never had a ghost friend before. From that day on, Tim and the ghost played together in the park every day. They had lots of fun and went on many adventures. One day, while they were playing, the ghost said, "I have a secret. I can make you fly!" Tim was so excited. He had always wanted to fly. The ghost used its magic to make Tim fly, and they flew all around the park together. Tim was very glad he had saved the ghost and made a new friend.
