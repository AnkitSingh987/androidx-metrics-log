Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Tom and the Duck

Lily and Tom went to the park with Mom. They saw a big pond with many ducks. Lily liked the ducks. She wanted to feed them bread. "Can I give them bread, Mom?" Lily asked. "Sure, but be careful. Don't go too close to the water," Mom said. Lily and Tom broke some bread and threw it to the ducks. The ducks came closer and ate the bread. They quacked and splashed. Lily and Tom laughed. One duck was different from the others. It was yellow, not brown. It had a red beak, not orange. It was smaller, too. "Look, Tom, a yellow duck!" Lily said. Tom looked at the yellow duck. He recognized it. He had seen it before, in a book. "That's not a duck, Lily. That's a rubber duck. Someone left it in the pond," Tom said. "A rubber duck? How did it get here?" Lily asked. "I don't know. Maybe someone forgot it. Or maybe it wanted to swim with the real ducks," Tom said. Lily and Tom watched the rubber duck. It floated on the water. It did not eat the bread. It did not quack or splash. It looked lonely. "Can we take it home, Mom?" Lily asked. Mom looked at the rubber duck. She smiled. "Sure, if no one else wants it. Maybe it needs a friend," Mom said. Lily and Tom were happy. They picked up the rubber duck. They said goodbye to the real ducks. They went home with Mom. They gave the rubber duck a name. They called it Quacky. They played with it in the bath. They made it swim and splash. They gave it bread, too. Quacky was happy. Lily and Tom were happy, too. They liked their new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. They played in the yard, but they also liked to play in Tim's room. One day, Tim and Spot went into the room to play with their toys. Tim wanted to play with his toy cars, and Spot wanted to play with his ball. They had so much fun playing together in the room. After playing for a long time, they were both very tired. Tim and Spot went to sleep in the room. Spot was so loyal that he always stayed with Tim, even when they were sleeping. They were the best of friends and always had fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One day, he wanted to bring his toy car to the park. He ran so fast, he felt dizzy. But he was happy because he loved his toy car. At the park, Tim saw a beautiful sunset. The sky was pink and orange. He played with his toy car under the big, pretty sky. Tim was having so much fun, he didn't see a little girl named Sue come up to him. Sue saw Tim's toy car and wanted to play too. But instead of asking, she took the toy car when Tim wasn't looking. Tim got sad and looked everywhere for his toy car. Then, he saw Sue playing with it. Tim got mad and wanted his toy car back. But then, something unexpected happened. Sue said sorry and gave Tim his toy car back. She asked if they could play together. Tim thought for a moment and said yes. They played with the toy car until the sun went down. Tim and Sue became good friends and always played together at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a gray cat named Fluffy. Fluffy lived in a small house with a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with Fluffy and they were best friends. One day, Sue and Fluffy were playing with glue. They were making a pretty picture with colorful paper. Fluffy said, "Meow, this is fun!" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, Fluffy, I love playing with glue too!" Sue's mom came into the room and saw their pretty picture. She was happy and said, "Wow, that's so nice!" Then she mentioned, "Be careful with the glue, Sue and Fluffy. We don't want it to stick to your fur or hands." Sue and Fluffy nodded and kept playing, making sure to be careful with the glue. They had a fun day together, making pretty pictures and being best friends.
