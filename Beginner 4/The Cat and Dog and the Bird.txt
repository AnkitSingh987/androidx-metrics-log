Category: Beginner
Title: The Cat and Dog and the Bird

One day, a nice cat and a little dog went to play. They ran to a big hedge. They liked to play hide and seek there. The sun was shining, and the sky was blue. They were very happy. As they played, they heard a strange sound. They stopped and listened. It was coming from inside the hedge. They were scared but curious. They looked at each other and decided to find out what it was. They went closer to the hedge and saw a tiny door. They opened it and found a small bird. The bird was sad because it was lost. The nice cat and the little dog decided to help the bird. They played with the bird and showed it the way home. The bird was very happy and thanked them. From that day, they all became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big red truck. The truck loved to help people. One day, the truck went to sleep. It was very tired. In the morning, the truck did wake up. It was time to go to work. The truck was happy to help people again. It drove down the road and saw a little girl. The little girl had a delicious apple. She wanted to give it to the truck. The truck was happy and took the apple. The truck and the little girl became good friends. They played together every day. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, a happy dog named Max lived in a clean little house. Max loved to play with his toys and find things that match. One sunny day, Max went on a big adventure to find matching things. On his walk, Max saw a big red ball. Next to it, he found a red cup. "These match!" he barked, excitedly. Max kept walking and found a pretty blue bird. He looked around and found a blue flower. "These match too!" Max wagged his tail. As Max walked, he saw a man with a purple bottle. The man said it was wine. Max looked around and found a purple toy. He showed the man, "Look, the wine and toy match!" The man laughed and thanked Max. Max felt happy and went home, excited to find more things that match tomorrow.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play in the park. The park has swings, slides and a big tree. Tom and Lily run to the swings and take turns to push each other. They laugh and have fun. "Look, Lily, a dog!" Tom says, pointing to a brown dog that walks by with a man. The dog is tall and has a long tail. The man holds a leash that is attached to the dog's heel. "Can we pet the dog, please?" Lily asks the man. She likes dogs. She thinks they are cute and soft. "Sure, you can pet him. His name is Max. He is friendly and gentle. But you have to ask him first. Can you say 'Hello, Max'?" the man says, smiling. "Hello, Max!" Tom and Lily say together. They reach out their hands to touch the dog's fur. Max wags his tail and licks their faces. Tom and Lily giggle. "Thank you, Max. You are a good dog. And thank you, sir, for letting us pet him." Lily says politely. "You're welcome, kids. Enjoy the park. Come on, Max, let's go." the man says, waving goodbye. He and Max walk away. Tom and Lily go back to the swings. They are happy. They like the park. They like Max. They like each other. They are friends.

Harry was an enthusiastic little boy who loved to study. One day, he asked his mom, "Can I have some jelly?" His mom said, "Yes, Harry. That sounds like something you'll enjoy." Harry ran to the kitchen and poured himself a bowl of his favorite jelly. He began to study with enthusiasm while eating his jelly. When Harry finished his bowl of jelly, he said to his mom, "Thank you for the jelly. It helped me with my studying!" His mom said, "My pleasure, Harry. I'm glad you like it."
