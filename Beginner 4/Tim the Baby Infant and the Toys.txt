Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Baby Infant and the Toys

One day, a baby infant named Tim was playing with his toys. He was not happy. He was angry because he could not find his favorite toy. He looked everywhere, but it was gone. Tim started to cry. His big sister, Amy, heard him cry and came to help. She asked, "Why are you angry, Tim?" Tim said, "I can't find my toy. I want it now!" Amy knew she had to help her brother find the toy. They looked all around the room. They did not quit. At last, they found the toy under the bed. Tim was happy again. He said, "Thank you, Amy!" They played together and had lots of fun.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play and sing. One day, they find a big book with many pictures. The book is about opera. Opera is when people sing very loud and wear funny clothes. Lily and Ben want to try opera. They put on their mom's hats and scarves. They stand on the sofa and sing. They sing very loud and make up words. They have fun. But mom is not happy. She is working on her laptop. She needs quiet. She comes to the living room and sees Lily and Ben. She is angry. "Stop that noise!" she says. "You are not singing opera, you are making a mess. And look at my hats and scarves. They are dirty and torn. How could you do this?" Lily and Ben are scared. They stop singing. They look at mom. They look at the book. They look at the hats and scarves. They feel sorry. "We are sorry, mom," they say. "We just wanted to sing opera. We like the book. We did not mean to make a mess. We did not know you were working. Please do not be mad." Mom sighs. She is still angry, but she loves Lily and Ben. She sees that they are sorry. She hugs them. "I love you, Lily and Ben," she says. "But you have to be more careful. Opera is not for playing. It is for listening. And you have to respect my things. They are not for wearing. They are for special occasions. And you have to be quiet when I am working. It is important. Do you understand?" Lily and Ben nod. They understand. They say sorry again. They help mom clean up the mess. They put the book back on the shelf. They promise to be good. Mom smiles. She is not angry anymore. She is proud of Lily and Ben. She says they can watch opera on TV later. They can learn more about it. They can see the people sing and wear funny clothes. They can enjoy it. Lily and Ben are happy. They hug mom. They say thank you. They are excited to watch opera. They hope they can sing like that one day. But not too loud. And not with mom's hats and scarves.

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a small, amazing box in her room. She did not know where it came from. It was a mystery. The box was shiny and pretty. Lucy wanted to know what was inside. Lucy opened the box and saw a tiny, cute bug. The bug had big eyes and could blink very fast. Lucy liked the bug and wanted to play with it. She named the bug Blink. Lucy and Blink played all day. They played with toys and drew pictures. Blink was very good at drawing. At the end of the day, Lucy was tired. She put Blink back in the amazing box and went to bed. She was happy to have a new friend.

Lila was a creative girl who liked to make things with her toys. She had many toys, but her favorite was a pan that she found in the kitchen. She liked to pretend that the pan was a hat, a drum, a boat, or anything else she could imagine. One day, she was playing with her pan in the living room, when her brother Max came in. Max was older than Lila and he liked to tease her. He saw the pan on her head and said, "What are you doing, Lila? That is not a hat, that is a pan. You look silly." Lila did not like what Max said. She felt angry and sad. She said, "No, Max, this is my hat. I am a queen and this is my crown. And this is my pan, I own it. You cannot have it." Max laughed and said, "You do not own the pan, Lila. The pan belongs to mom. She uses it to cook food. You have to give it back to her. And you are not a queen, you are a baby." Lila felt more angry and sad. She said, "No, no, no. You are wrong, Max. I am not a baby, I am a big girl. And I am a creative girl. I can make anything with my pan. You are not creative, you are boring." Max felt annoyed and said, "I am not boring, Lila. I am smart and fun. And I can be creative too. Watch me." He grabbed the pan from Lila's head and ran away. Lila cried and chased him. She wanted her pan back. She shouted, "Give me my pan, Max. Give me my pan."
