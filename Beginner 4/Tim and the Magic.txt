Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Magic

One day, a boy named Tim found an interesting wallet on the ground. It was red and shiny. He picked it up and looked inside. There were no cards or money, just a note. The note said, "Snap to make magic." Tim went to his friend, Sue. He showed her the wallet and the note. "What do you think it means?" he asked. Sue thought for a moment and said, "Maybe if you snap your fingers, something will happen." Tim decided to try it. He held the wallet and snapped his fingers. Suddenly, a big pile of toys appeared! Tim and Sue were very surprised. They played with the toys and had a lot of fun. The next day, Tim tried snapping his fingers again. This time, a big ice cream cone appeared! Tim and Sue shared the ice cream. They knew the wallet was very special. One day, Tim lost the wallet. He and Sue looked everywhere but could not find it. They were sad, but they knew they would always remember the magic wallet and the fun times they had.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big hill of sand. They want to make a castle. "Look, Mia, we can use this salt to make windows!" Tom says. He has a small bag of salt from his lunch. He pours some salt on the sand. "No, Tom, that is not a good idea. Salt is for food, not for sand. It will make the sand dirty and hurt the plants and animals," Mia says. She has a clean cloth. She wipes the salt off the sand. "But I want to make a castle with windows. It will look nice," Tom says. He is angry. He pours more salt on the sand. "Stop, Tom, stop! You are being bad. You are wasting salt and making a mess. You should share and care, not be selfish and mean," Mia says. She is sad. She tries to take the bag of salt from Tom. Tom and Mia start to fight. They pull and push each other. The bag of salt breaks. The salt spills everywhere. The wind blows the salt in their eyes. They cry and scream. A lady sees them. She runs to help them. She washes their eyes with water. She scolds them. "What are you doing, children? Why are you fighting and throwing salt? Don't you know that salt is precious and useful? You should not waste it or use it to harm others. You should be grateful and respectful. You should apologize and make up," the lady says. Tom and Mia feel ashamed. They say sorry to each other and to the lady. They hug and smile. They learn their lesson. They decide to make a castle with sticks and stones instead. They have fun and play nicely. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his friends in the park. One day, while playing, Spot saw a shiny crystal on the ground. He thought it was very pretty. Spot wanted to show the crystal to his friends. But the crystal was stuck in the ground. Spot tried to pull it out with his mouth. He pulled and pulled, but it would not come out. Spot was sad. Then, Spot's friend, a strong horse named Max, came to help. Max used his big teeth to pull the crystal out. They did it together! Spot and Max were so happy. They showed the crystal to all their friends, and everyone thought it was very pretty.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a fragile toy cat that she loved very much. One day, Lucy's toy cat broke, and she was very sad. Lucy went to her mom and said, "Mom, I want to fix my toy cat. Can you help me?" Her mom looked at the broken toy and said, "I will try, but it may not be the same as before." Lucy's friend, Tim, came over to play. He saw the broken toy cat and said, "I have a memory of my toy breaking too. It made me sad, but then I got a new one and it was even better!" Lucy didn't want a new toy cat, she wanted her old one. Her mom tried to fix the toy cat, but it was still fragile. Lucy was careful when she played with it, but it broke again. She was very sad. In the end, Lucy's mom bought her a new toy cat. It wasn't the same as her old one, but Lucy loved it just as much. She learned that sometimes things break, but new memories can be made with new toys.
