Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Star and the Boy

One night, a little boy was looking up at the sky. He saw something sparkly - it was a star! He wanted to touch the star, so he wished very hard. Suddenly, the icy star began to fall. The boy ran outside and saw the star twinkling down from the sky. He jumped up, trying to catch the star. But it was icy cold and slippery, and he couldn't catch it. The star kept falling and falling until it made a big sparkly splash into the sea. The little boy looked at the twinkling star, far away in the sea. He smiled, knowing that he had been close to a real star.

Once upon a time, in an ordinary town, there was a little girl named Mia. One day, Mia went to the zoo with her mom. Mia loved the zoo and wanted to see all the animals, especially the big birds that could fly. At the zoo, Mia saw a big bird in a cage. The bird looked sad. Mia asked the bird, "Why are you sad?" The bird said, "I want to fly, but I am stuck in this cage." Mia wanted to help the bird, so she looked for a way to open the cage. Mia found a key and opened the cage. The big bird was so happy that it could fly again. The bird said, "Thank you, Mia!" Mia and her mom watched the bird fly away, and they knew they had done a good thing. They went home with big smiles on their faces.

Lila and Sam were friends who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big slide that looked like a temple. It had stairs, windows, and a roof. They wanted to go on the slide, but there was a problem. The slide had two paths that split at the top. One path was red and the other was blue. "Let's go on the red path," said Lila. "It looks more fun." "No, let's go on the blue path," said Sam. "It looks faster." They could not agree on which path to choose. They started to argue and push each other. They were not being nice. A lady who worked at the park saw them and came over. She had a kind smile and a whistle. "Hello, children," she said. "Why are you fighting?" "We want to go on the slide, but we don't want the same path," said Lila. "Well, why don't you take turns?" said the lady. "One of you can go on the red path first, and then the other can go on the blue path. Or you can go together and split at the top. That way, you can both have fun and be fair." Lila and Sam thought about it. They realized that the lady was right. They said sorry to each other and hugged. They decided to go together and split at the top. They thanked the lady for her help and ran to the slide. They climbed the stairs, reached the roof, and sat on the slide. They looked at each other and smiled. They were grateful for their friendship and for the slide. They shouted "whee!" and slid down the paths. They had a lot of fun. They learned to share and to be kind. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Ben. Ben loved to play hide and seek with his friends. One day, Ben woke up early to see the sunrise. The sky was pink and orange, and it was beautiful. Ben wanted to play hide and seek with his friends at sunrise. He hopped to his friend Sally the squirrel's house. Sally was eating a delicious acorn for breakfast. Ben asked Sally if she wanted to play hide and seek, and Sally said yes. They played hide and seek until the sun was high in the sky. Ben was very good at hiding, but Sally was good at finding him too. They laughed and played all morning, and they were very happy. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a kid named Tim. Tim was a tidy kid. He liked to clean his room and help his mom in the house. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, can you perform a show for me?" Tim was happy and said, "Yes, mom!" He put on a funny hat and danced around the room. His mom laughed and clapped her hands. After the show, Tim's friend, Sam, came to play. Sam saw how tidy Tim's room was and said, "Wow, your room is so clean!" Tim smiled and said, "I like to be tidy. It makes my mom happy." Sam learned from Tim and started to be tidy too. The moral of the story is that being tidy can make others happy and teach friends to be tidy too.
