Category: Beginner
Title: Spot and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a nervous dog because he always had an itch. One day, Spot went to the park to play with his friends. While at the park, Spot saw a big ball. He wanted to strike the ball with his paw. Spot ran fast and hit the ball. The ball went far, and all his friends clapped for him. Spot felt happy and his itch went away for a while. But soon, the itch came back. Spot felt nervous again. His friend, a kind cat named Fluffy, saw Spot's itch. Fluffy helped him by scratching the itch. Spot felt much better and thanked Fluffy. They played together all day, and Spot learned that friends can help when you are nervous or have an itch.

Ben and Lily were playing with their toys in the living room. They had a lot of fun, but they also made a big mess. There were blocks, cars, dolls, and books everywhere. "Mommy will be mad if she sees this," Ben said. "We have to clean up." "But I don't want to clean up," Lily said. "I want to play more." Just then, they heard a squeak from under the sofa. They looked and saw a little mouse. The mouse had a piece of cheese in its mouth. It was trying to run away. "Look, a mouse!" Ben shouted. "Let's catch it!" They ran after the mouse, but it was too fast. It ran behind the curtains, under the table, and into the kitchen. Ben and Lily followed it, making more noise and more mess. "Stop, mouse!" Lily yelled. "We want to play with you!" The mouse was scared. It did not want to play with the big kids. It wanted to eat its cheese and hide. It saw a hole in the wall and ran towards it. But before it could reach the hole, Ben grabbed it by the tail. He lifted it up and smiled. "Got you, mouse!" he said. "You are so cute and soft. I will give you a name. How about Cheese?" The mouse did not like being held. It dropped its cheese and bit Ben's finger. Ben screamed and let go of the mouse. The mouse ran into the hole and disappeared. "Ow, ow, ow!" Ben cried. "The mouse bit me! It was mean!" Lily picked up the cheese and looked at it. She shrugged and said, "Can I have it?" Ben glared at her and said, "No, you can't. It's mouse cheese. It's yucky." They heard footsteps coming from the stairs. It was their mommy. She looked at the living room and the kitchen. She saw the mess they had made. She was not happy. "Ben, Lily, what have you done?" she asked. "Why is everything so messy?" Ben and Lily looked at each other. They did not know what to say. They pointed at the hole in the wall and said, "The mouse did it."

Alice was playing with blocks when she heard a noise. She looked around and saw a rabbit. "Hello!" she said. The rabbit hopped away and Alice ran after him. It was not easy to stay close to the rabbit, though, because he was so quick. Alice followed the rabbit until they both came to a big hill. "How am I going to get to the top?" she wondered. The rabbit had already started to hop up the hill, but it looked very difficult. Alice tried to climb the hill, but she couldn’t make it over the edge. She was about to give up, when she heard the rabbit's voice. "If you keep trying, you can finish!” Alice tried harder and harder. She managed to climb the hill, and when she reached the top, the rabbit was waiting for her with a surprise. "I knew you could finish!" said the rabbit, as he handed Alice a big bunch of carrots. Alice smiled with joy as she held the carrots. She had done it! The hill was very difficult, but with help from her friend, she had finished it.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom loved candy more than anything. He had a friend, Sam, who was very dependable. Tom always believed that Sam would help him find the best candy. One day, Tom and Sam found a big candy store. They were very excited. But, there was a problem. The store had a big, mean dog. The dog would not let them get the candy. Tom and Sam tried to think of a way to get past the dog. Tom had an idea. He told Sam to give the dog a bone. Sam did not think it was a good idea, but he wanted to help Tom. So, Sam gave the dog a bone. The dog took the bone and went away. Tom and Sam went into the store and got a lot of candy. But, when they left the store, the dog came back. The dog was very mad. The dog chased Tom and Sam. They ran and ran. But, the dog caught them. The dog ate all their candy. Tom and Sam were very sad. They learned that sometimes, even dependable friends can make mistakes.
