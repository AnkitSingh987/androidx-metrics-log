Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Bunny

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the store with her mom. Mia saw a big shelf with many toys on it. She wanted to play with all of them, but her mom said, "Mia, you can only pick one toy." Mia felt sad but nodded her head. Mia looked at the toys and saw a scared little bunny toy hiding behind a big bear. The bunny was all alone and separate from the other toys. Mia thought that the bunny needed a friend. She picked up the bunny and said, "Don't be scared, little bunny. I will be your friend." Mia's mom saw that she picked the scared bunny and smiled. She knew that Mia had a big heart. They bought the bunny and took it home. Mia played with the bunny every day, and it was not scared anymore. The bunny was happy to have a friend like Mia, and Mia was happy that she could help the bunny not be scared.

Once upon a time, there was a modest bird named Tim. Tim had a small nest in a big tree. He liked to play with his friends in the sun. One day, Tim and his friends found a slide in the park. They were very happy. They took turns to slide down the slide. Tim went down the slide very fast. His friends clapped and laughed. After playing on the slide, Tim and his friends went back to their nests. They were tired but happy. They had a fun day at the park. They could not wait to play on the slide again.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly eraser. He lived in a big box with other erasers. They all lived in a nice, warm house. One day, the friendly eraser decided to go on an adventure. He jumped out of the box and lay on the table. He saw a big, colorful drawing on a piece of paper. The eraser thought it looked fun and wanted to play with it. The friendly eraser slid across the paper, making the colors change. He was having so much fun! After playing for a while, the eraser got tired. He lay back down in the box with his friends and told them all about his adventure. They all wanted to play with the drawing too. And so, they all had a fun day together.

Ben and Lily are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a big slide. Ben says, "Let's go on the slide. It is fun." Lily says, "OK, but be careful. The slide is high." They run to the slide. Ben goes first. He climbs up the ladder. He sits on the slide. He waves to Lily. He says, "Look at me. I am strong." He slides down fast. He laughs. Lily goes next. She climbs up the ladder. She sits on the slide. She waves to Ben. She says, "Look at me. I am strong too." She slides down fast. She screams. There is a big hole in the slide. Lily falls into the hole. She hurts her leg. She cries. Ben runs to her. He says, "Oh no. Lily, are you OK?" Lily says, "No, I am not OK. It hurts. Help me." But Ben cannot help her. The hole is too deep. He cannot reach her. He cries too. He says, "I am sorry, Lily. I am sorry." He looks for help. But there is no one in the park. They are alone. They are scared. They are sad. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big quarrel between two friends, Cat and Dog. They both had a dream to find a cheap toy to play with. They looked everywhere, but they could not find the toy they wanted. One day, they saw a cheap toy in a shop. Cat said, "I saw it first! It's mine!" Dog said, "No, I saw it first! It's mine!" They both wanted the toy so much that they started to quarrel. A wise old Owl heard their quarrel and said, "Stop fighting, friends! Why don't you share the toy and play together?" Cat and Dog looked at each other and agreed. They bought the cheap toy and played happily together. From that day on, they never had a quarrel again and always shared their toys.
