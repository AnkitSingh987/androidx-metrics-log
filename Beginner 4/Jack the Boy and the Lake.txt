Category: Beginner
Title: Jack the Boy and the Lake

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Jack. Jack was very stubborn and usually didn't want to listen to Mama. Mama warned Jack not to go near the lake, but one day, Jack was feeling brave and decided to dare himself to go see the lake. Jack grabbed a bottle and walked towards the lake. He was so excited to explore, and he didn't even think twice about Mama's warning. He was about to touch the lake's water when he heard a voice coming from behind him. "Jack, what do you think you're doing?" said Mama, standing behind him with her hands on her hips. Jack was so embarrassed and quickly hid the bottle behind his back. "Mama, I was just... looking," he said, so shyly. Mama shook her head, but then said warmly, "Well, Jack, next time you have to listen to me, okay?" Jack nodded and promised he would be more obedient next time. Mama was happy to hear that and took Jack's hand. Together, they walked away from the lake. All the while, Jack held onto the bottle, feeling thankful that Mama was so stubborn; otherwise, he might've gotten into trouble.

Mia and her dad went to the park. Mia was so excited to play. She ran to the swing and hugged it tight. "Look dad! I can swing really high!" Mia said. "That's fine Mia," said her dad. Mia kept swinging higher and higher. She felt so happy. But then a gust of wind blew and it was too strong. Mia let go of the swing and tried to hold on, but she couldn't. Mia fell off the swing and landed on the ground. "Ow!" she screamed. Her dad ran to Mia and hugged her tight. "I'm sorry Mia! Are you ok?" Mia shook her head and held back her tears. "It's ok Mia," said her dad. "But next time don't yield to the wind!" Mia nodded. Even though she felt better, she felt sad too. She wished she had listened to her dad so she wouldn't have gotten hurt.

Once upon a time, there was a big show. Many people came to see it. They were happy and excited. In the show, there were animals who could perform tricks. The animals were very good at what they did. One day, a little girl named Lucy wanted to make a print of the show. She had a small box that could take pictures. She took a picture of the animals performing. Lucy loved her print and wanted to show it to her friends. Lucy was very organized. She put the print in a special box with her other pictures. She showed her friends the print of the animals performing. Her friends loved the print too. They all laughed and clapped. Lucy was happy that she could share her print with her friends.

Tom is a nosy boy. He likes to look at everything and ask many questions. One day, he sees a big truck on the road. The truck is red and shiny. It has a loud horn and a long piece of wood on the back. Tom wants to see the truck better. He runs after it on the sidewalk. He does not look where he is going. He does not see the stop sign or the crosswalk. He only sees the truck. He tries to speed up to catch it. But the truck is too fast for Tom. It turns the corner and disappears. Tom is sad and angry. He stops running and stamps his feet. He does not hear the car that is coming. The car is also fast. It does not see Tom. Beep! Beep! The car honks its horn. Tom is scared. He jumps back to the sidewalk. He falls down and scrapes his knee. He cries and cries. His mom hears him and runs to him. She hugs him and kisses his knee. She tells him he is a silly boy. He should not chase trucks. He should be careful and watch the road. Tom says he is sorry. He says he was nosy. He says he wanted to see the truck. His mom says she understands. She says trucks are interesting. But they are also dangerous. She says he can see trucks in books or on TV. But he should not run after them on the road. Tom nods and wipes his tears. He says he will not do it again. He says he will be a good boy. His mom smiles and takes him home. She gives him a cookie and a glass of milk. She reads him a story about trucks. Tom listens and learns. He likes the story. He likes his mom. He is happy.
