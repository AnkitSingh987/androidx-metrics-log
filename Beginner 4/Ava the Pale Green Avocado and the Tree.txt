Category: Beginner
Title: Ava the Pale Green Avocado and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a pale green avocado named Ava. Ava lived in a big tree with her friends. One day, Ava saw a little boy named Timmy walking by the tree. Ava wanted to talk to Timmy, so she called out, "Hi Timmy, do you want to play?" Timmy looked up at the tree and saw Ava. He smiled and said, "Yes, I want to play with you, Ava." They played fun games together. But, while playing, Ava fell from the tree. She felt embarrass. Timmy saw her and said, "Don't worry, Ava. It's okay." They both laughed and continued to play. Ava and Timmy became best friends. They played together every day and were always happy.

Once upon a time, there was an old man who was always well behaved. He was very kind and generous, and his neighbors looked up to him as a role model. One day, the old man was working in his garden when a young boy came over to him. The boy asked the old man if he had a razor that he could use. The old man said, "I'm sorry, I don't have anything available right now". The young boy was disappointed and walked away. The old man thought to himself, 'That boy looks like he is up to no good. I better watch him.' The old man kept his eye on the boy as he continued to work in his garden. After a while, he noticed the boy go back to the same spot. This time, the boy had a large bag in his hands. The old man quickly ran over to the spot and grabbed the bag. Inside was a razor! He looked at the boy and said sternly, "I told you I don't have anything available. Now why don't you behave yourself and go home?" The young boy realized his mistake and ran away. The old man shook his head and thought to himself, 'It's important to be a good role model so that others will behave too'. He was glad he could prevent something bad from happening by setting a good example.

Once upon a time, there was a little kangaroo named Kippy. Kippy liked to jump and play all day. One day, Kippy got very dirty while playing in the mud. His mom said, "Kippy, you need to wipe yourself clean." Kippy found a soft cloth and started to wipe. He wiped his face, his arms, and his legs. He was happy to be clean again. "I'm all clean now, Mom!" said Kippy. Kippy's mom smiled and hugged him. "Good job, Kippy! Now you are safe and clean. Let's go home and have a snack." Kippy and his mom hopped back home, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was an adorable little bunny named Ben. Ben loved to zip around the forest and play with his friends. One day, Ben found a tool. It was a big, shiny key. Ben thought the key was very cool. Ben showed the key to his friend, a squirrel named Sam. Sam said, "That's a big key, Ben! What does it open?" Ben didn't know, but he wanted to find out. They decided to look for a door or a box that the key could open. As they zipped around the forest, they met a grumpy old bear named Mr. Brown. Mr. Brown saw the key and said, "That's my key! Give it back!" Ben didn't want to give the key back, but he didn't want to make Mr. Brown angry. So, Ben said, "Okay, Mr. Brown. Here is your key. I'm sorry." Mr. Brown took the key and said, "Thank you, Ben. You are a good bunny." Ben and Sam felt happy because they helped Mr. Brown. They zipped back home, knowing they did the right thing.

Tom is a hunter. He likes to go to the forest and look for animals. He has a big gun and a big hat. He is not afraid of anything. One day, Tom goes to the forest when it is very dark. He wants to find a deer. He walks and walks, but he does not see any deer. He only sees trees and rocks and birds. He is very tired and hungry. Then, it starts to rain. The rain is cold and wet. It makes Tom's hat and gun and clothes wet. It makes the forest muddy and slippery. Tom does not like the rain. He wants to go home. "Where is my car?" Tom says. He looks around, but he does not remember where he parked his car. He is lost. He is scared. He does not know what to do. He hears a noise. He thinks it is a deer. He lifts his gun and points it at the noise. He hopes to shoot the deer and take it home. But it is not a deer. It is a bear. A big, brown, angry bear. The bear sees Tom and his gun. The bear roars and runs at Tom. Tom is very afraid. He tries to run away, but he falls in the mud. The bear is faster than Tom. The bear catches Tom and bites him. Tom screams and cries. He wishes he never came to the forest. He wishes he stayed at home. He wishes he listened to his mom.
