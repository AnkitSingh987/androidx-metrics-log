Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Puppy and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a little puppy named Max. Max loved to decorate his house with pretty things. One day, Max saw a big cake on the table. Max thought the cake needed to be decorated too. Max asked his friend, a bird named Lucy, "Can you help me decorate the cake?" Lucy said, "No, Max, that cake is not for us. It is for a party later." But Max was a persistent puppy. He did not listen to Lucy and decided to decorate the cake all by himself. Max jumped on the table and started to put flowers, leaves, and sticks on the cake. He was very happy with his work. But when the people came home and saw the cake, they were very sad. The cake was ruined, and the party was not fun anymore. Max learned that he should have listened to his friend Lucy.

---

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a yummy cake on the table. He wanted to eat it, but his mom said, "No, Tim. You must wait for dinner." Tim was sad, but he knew he had to listen to his mom. Later, Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. They played with toys and had fun. Then, Sam saw the yummy cake too. He said, "Wow, Tim! That cake looks so good. Can we eat it now?" Tim remembered what his mom said and told Sam, "No, we have to wait for dinner." Finally, it was time for dinner. Tim and Sam ate their food very fast because they wanted the yummy cake. After dinner, Tim's mom said, "Good job, boys. Now you can have the cake." Tim and Sam were so happy. They both grabbed a big piece of the yummy cake and ate it all up.

Sara and Ben are playing in the park. They see a big tunnel made of wood and metal. They want to go inside and explore. "Come on, Sara, let's go in the tunnel!" Ben says. "OK, Ben, but be careful. It might be dark and scary," Sara says. They hold hands and walk into the tunnel. It is dark and cold. They hear strange noises and see shadows. "Ben, I'm scared. What if there are monsters in here?" Sara says. "Don't worry, Sara. There are no monsters. It's just the wind and the cars," Ben says. He tries to be brave, but he is scared too. He sees a small light at the end of the tunnel. He thinks it is the way out. "Look, Sara, there is the light! We are almost there!" Ben says. He pulls Sara towards the light. But as they get closer, they see that it is not the light of the sun. It is the light of a fire. Someone has set a fire in the tunnel. It is hot and smoky. "Ben, it's a fire! We have to go back!" Sara says. She turns around and tries to run back. But Ben is too scared to move. He is frozen in fear. "Ben, come on, we have to go!" Sara says. She pinches his arm hard. He feels the pain and wakes up from his shock. "Ow, Sara, that hurts!" Ben says. "Sorry, Ben, but we have to go! The fire is getting bigger!" Sara says. She drags him back to the other end of the tunnel. They run as fast as they can. They see the park and the people. They are safe. They hug each other and cry. They are happy to be out of the tunnel. A nice lady sees them and comes to help. She gives them water and cookies. The cookies are sweet and crunchy. "Are you OK, kids? What happened?" the lady says. "We went in the tunnel and there was a fire. It was scary and hot. Sara pinched me and we ran out," Ben says. "That was very brave of you, Sara. You saved your friend. You are both very lucky. The firemen are coming to put out the fire. Don't go in the tunnel again, OK?" the lady says. "OK, lady. Thank you for the water and the cookies. They are sweet and yummy," Sara says. They smile and eat their cookies. They are glad to be alive. They are glad to have each other. They are friends.
