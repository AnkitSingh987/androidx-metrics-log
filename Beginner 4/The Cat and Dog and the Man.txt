Category: Beginner
Title: The Cat and Dog and the Man

One day, a cat and a dog went for a walk. The cat had smooth, soft fur. The dog was big and strong. They were best friends. As they walked, they saw a big room. Inside the room, a man was giving a lecture. The cat and the dog did not know what a lecture was, but they wanted to find out. The cat said, "Let's go inside." The dog whispered, "Okay, but we must be very quiet." They went inside the room and sat down. The man talked and talked. The cat and the dog listened, but they did not understand anything. They just liked the sound of the man's voice. Suddenly, the man stopped talking. He looked at the cat and the dog. The cat and the dog were scared. They thought the man would be angry. But the man smiled and said, "You two are so cute! I have never had a cat and a dog at my lecture before." The cat and the dog were happy. They made a new friend, and they learned what a lecture was.

Once upon a time, there was a big black dog. The dog lived in a yard with a fence. The dog liked to jump and play. One day, a little girl named Sue walked by. Sue saw the dog and wanted to be friends. Sue wanted to teach the dog a new trick. She went to the fence and called the dog. The dog came running to her. Sue showed the dog a ball. She threw the ball, and the dog jumped over the fence to get it. But the dog did not bring the ball back to Sue. Instead, the dog gave the ball to a little boy named Tim. Tim was hiding behind a tree. Sue was sad, but then she saw that Tim needed a friend too. So, Sue, Tim, and the big black dog all played together and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy man who lived in a small house. His house had a ladder and he used it to get things from his roof. One morning, the man was too lazy to climb the ladder and he decided to split it in two. He thought this would make it easier to climb the ladder. But, the man was wrong. When he tried to get things from the roof, he found his ladder was too short to reach. He had made a mistake and was now stuck. He knew that if he had been less lazy, he wouldn't have gotten into this mess. He wished he had taken the time to climb the ladder like he usually did. The lazy man learnt a lesson that day. He realised that even though it was hard work, it was worth taking the time to do things properly. It was better than trying to take shortcuts and making a mess of things. From then on, he put more effort into everything that he did. Moral of the story: Hard work pays off!

One day, a girl named Lily found a box. Inside the box, there was a lot of jewelry. She saw a big, impressive necklace. She wanted to wear it, so she tried to put it on. Lily's mom came in and said, "Oh no, Lily! That necklace is not for you." Lily felt sad but she listened to her mom. She put the necklace back in the box. Later, Lily's mom had a surprise for her. She took out a little box and opened it. Inside, there was a small necklace just for Lily. "Here you go, my dear," her mom said. Lily was so happy! She put on the necklace and it was perfect.

One day, a bright sun was in the sky. A girl named Ann wanted to mail a letter. She walked to the big blue box to put her letter in. On her way, she saw a cat. The cat was very cute and had a big smile. Ann looked around and saw no one. She thought the cat was lost. So, she picked up the cat and took it with her. She put her letter in the big blue box and went back home. At home, Ann gave the cat some food. Then, a lady came to the door. The lady said, "I saw you take my cat. Thank you for taking care of him!" Ann was happy the cat had a home. The lady was happy to see her cat again. They both laughed and became good friends.
