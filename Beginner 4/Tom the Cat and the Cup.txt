Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Cat and the Cup

One day, a playful cat named Tom was in the kitchen. He saw a cup of tea on the table. He wanted to play with it. So, he jumped up and knocked the cup over. The tea spilled all over the table. Tom's friend, a dog named Max, saw the mess. He wanted to help. Max found a cloth and started to wipe the spilled tea. He wagged his tail and was happy to help. Just then, Tom's mom came in. She saw the mess and thought Max made it. But then, Tom jumped up and down, meowing loudly. He wanted to show that he made the mess, not Max. Tom's mom understood and was proud of Tom for telling the truth. She gave both Tom and Max a treat for being good friends.

Tim and Sam are friends. They like to play in the woods. They find a big log. They use the log to make a bridge over a stream. One day, a big dog comes to the woods. The dog is mean. He barks at Tim and Sam. He wants to cross the bridge. He does not want to share. Tim and Sam are scared. They do not want the dog to bite them. They run away from the log. The dog runs after them. But then, they see their loyal pet, Rex. Rex is a small dog, but he is brave. He runs to the big dog. He barks louder. He tells the big dog to go away. The big dog is surprised. He does not want to fight Rex. He runs away from the woods. He does not come back. Tim and Sam are happy. They hug Rex. They thank him for saving them. They pay him with a bone and a pat. They go back to the log. They play on the bridge. They are safe and happy. Rex is their hero.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play in the park. It was a sunny day, and he wanted to throw his ball. Tim saw a big tree that had a dark spot under it. He thought it would be fun to throw his ball into the dark spot. As Tim threw the ball, he heard a small voice say, "Hey, watch out!" To his surprise, a tiny squirrel came out from the dark spot, holding his ball. Tim was very sorry and said, "I'm sorry, Mr. Squirrel. I didn't see you there." The squirrel smiled and said, "That's okay. I have a surprise for you." The squirrel went back into the dark spot and came out with a big bag of nuts. Tim was so happy and thanked the squirrel. From that day on, Tim and the squirrel became the best of friends, and they always played together in the park.

The cat was running very fast. He was so excited to play with the other animals. But when he arrived, the animals refused to let him join. "You are going too fast," they said. The cat felt sad. But then he looked around and saw a nice lady. She saw the cat and said, "Come here, little one. You can play with me." So, the cat went to the nice lady. He was going slowly, so the other animals didn't refuse him this time. They said he could play, and they all had lots of fun. The cat remembered that it was nicer to be nice, and to go at the right speed. So, he never ran too fast or refused to play again.

Once upon a time, there was a happy little girl called Emily who lived in the city. Every morning, Emily would go out to the park and watch the many pigeons flying around. On this particular morning, Emily noticed one pigeon had hurt his wing. She was worried that he won't be able to fly anymore, so Emily got out of her chair and tried to help him. Just then, her mum came by and said, "Emily, you should use your energy to help those who really need it." Emily was confused and asked, "What do you mean?" Her mum smiled and replied, "Well, the pigeon is trying his best to fly and you can only help him so much. You should help those who don't have the same chances as you." Emily realized that her mum was right. Worrying all the time won't solve the problem. She promised her mum that she will devote her energy to helping others and they both smiled. The moral of the story is that it is important to help those who don't have the same chances that we do. We should look out for them rather than worry about things that we can't control.
