Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Bug and the Flower

One day, a little bug named Tim wanted to become perfect. He looked at his wings and thought they were too small. He asked his friend, a big bug named Sam, "How can I be perfect?" Sam said, "You are already perfect, Tim. You don't need to change anything." But Tim didn't believe him. He wanted to find a way to become perfect. Tim went on a walk to think. He saw a beautiful flower and decided to sit on it. As he sat there, he saw a little girl crying. Tim asked her, "Why are you sad?" The little girl said, "I lost my toy and I can't find it." Tim wanted to help her. He flew around and found the toy under a bush. He brought it back to the little girl. She was so happy and said, "Thank you, Tim. You are a perfect bug!" Tim smiled and realized that he was already perfect just the way he was.

Once upon a time, there was an obedient dog named Max. Max loved to turn around in circles when he played. One day, Max found a big book. The book had many stories inside. It was a history book. Max wanted to show the history book to his friends. He went to find his friends, the cat, the bird, and the fish. They all lived in a big house. Max was very happy to show them the history book. But when Max got to the house, the cat, the bird, and the fish were fighting. They did not want to stop and look at the history book. Max felt sad. He tried to make them stop fighting, but they did not listen. In the end, Max's friends did not see the history book, and they kept fighting. Max was very sad, and the history book did not help.

Once upon a time, there was a judge. His job was to understand things and make wise decisions. But he was very slow. One day, the judge was walking down the street when he heard a cry. He looked around and saw a little boy with a toy. The boy had dropped his toy and began to cry. The judge understood the situation and decided to help the little boy. He went up to the boy and asked him what was wrong. The boy explained that he had dropped his toy and was very sad. The judge felt sorry for the little boy and said that he should try to pick up the toy. The Judge was slow at picking up the toy but he was gentle and careful. When the judge had picked up the boy's toy, he handed it back to him with a big smile. The little boy smiled back and thanked the judge. The judge had been kind and understanding and the little boy was very happy. He hugged the judge and then ran off, joyful that he had his toy back. The judge was glad that he had been able to help and made a wise decision. He continued walking down the street, happy in the knowledge that he had done a good thing.

Once upon a time, there were two chubby twins named Tom and Tim. They loved to play together in the big park near their house. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. One sunny day, Tom and Tim saw a big, round ball in the park. They both wanted to touch it. They ran as fast as they could to reach the ball first. Tom touched the ball just before Tim could. Tim was a little sad that he didn't touch the ball first. But then, Tom had an idea. He said, "Let's play together with the ball!" Tim smiled, and they played with the ball all day long. From that day on, Tom and Tim learned that it was more fun to play together than to be first. They became the best of friends and always shared their toys. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a friend named Tom. One day, Tom came for a visit. They liked to play together. While they were playing, a bossy cat appeared. The cat said, "You must play my way!" Lily and Tom did not like this. They wanted to play their own way. Lily and Tom said, "No, thank you," to the bossy cat. They played their own way and had lots of fun. The bossy cat learned that it is better to let friends play their own way. And they all played happily together. The end.
