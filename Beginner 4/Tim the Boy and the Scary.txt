Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Scary

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a scary dream one night. In the dream, a big, scary thing appeared. Tim was very scared and he woke up. It was very dark in his room. Tim saw a little light. It was a candle. The candle was not scary. The candle made him feel safe. Tim walked to the candle and picked it up. He looked around his room. The scary thing was not there. It was just a dream. Then, something funny happened. The scary thing appeared again, but this time it was not scary. It was just a big, silly cat. The cat was playing with a toy. Tim laughed and played with the cat. The scary dream was gone, and Tim was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a very careless dog. He would run and jump and play all day long. One day, Spot found a big stick. He was so happy! He picked it up and started to shake it. "Shake, shake, shake," said Spot, as he shook the stick. "This is so much fun!" A little girl named Lily was watching Spot. She was very patient. She waited for Spot to stop shaking the stick. She wanted to play with him too. "Spot," said Lily, "can I play with you?" Spot looked at Lily and wagged his tail. "Yes, let's play together!" he said. So, Spot and Lily played together all day long. They were very happy. And even though Spot was a careless dog, he learned to be more careful when playing with his new friend Lily.

Once upon a time, there was a white bunny named Bobo. Bobo loved to build things with his toy blocks. One day, he wanted to build something new and fun. He thought about what style he could make. Bobo hopped over to his friend, a brown bear named Benny. "Benny, can you help me build something?" asked Bobo. Benny said, "Yes, let's build a big tower!" So, Bobo and Benny started to build a tower with the blocks. They built and built until they had a tall, white tower. Bobo and Benny were very happy with their tower. They played with it all day and had lots of fun. At the end of the day, they took the blocks apart and said, "Tomorrow, we will build something new!"

Once upon a time, there were twin dogs named Max and Sam. They were strong dogs who loved to play and chew on toys all day. They lived in a big yard with a lot of space to run and play. One day, Max and Sam were playing with a ball. Max said, "I can chew this ball faster than you!" Sam replied, "No, I am the strong twin. I can chew it faster!" They both started to chew the ball as fast as they could. After a while, they stopped chewing and looked at each other. They both laughed and said, "We are both strong and fast at chewing!" They kept playing with the ball, happy to be strong twin dogs who loved to chew and play together.

One day, a wise cat named Tom saw a jet up in the sky. The jet was flying very fast. Tom wanted to touch the jet, but it was too high. So, he decided to stretch his body to reach it. Tom stretched and stretched, but he could not touch the jet. His friend, a dog named Max, saw Tom and asked, "What are you doing, Tom?" Tom said, "I want to touch the jet, but it is too high." Max wanted to help Tom. They both stretched together, but they still could not reach the jet. They felt sad. Then, Max had an idea. He said, "Let's build a tall tower with blocks. Maybe we can touch the jet from there." Tom and Max worked together to build the tower. They stretched up high and placed the blocks one by one. The tower got taller and taller. But the jet was still too high. They knew they could not touch the jet, but they were happy they tried their best. Tom and Max learned that working together and trying new things is what makes them wise friends.
