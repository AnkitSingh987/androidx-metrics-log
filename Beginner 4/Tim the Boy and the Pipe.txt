Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Pipe

One day, a little boy named Tim found a pipe in his yard. It was a wet day, and the pipe was wet too. He picked up the pipe and looked at it. He thought, "What can I do with this pipe?" Tim saw his friend, Sue, playing outside. "Sue, look what I found!" he said. "It's a wet pipe! What can we do with it?" Sue thought for a moment and said, "Let's play a game with the pipe!" As they played, they heard a loud noise. It was a big dog! The big dog wanted the pipe. Tim and Sue didn't want to give the pipe to the dog. They ran away, but the dog chased them. They were scared. Then, something unexpected happened. The pipe became a magic wand! Tim waved the wand, and the big dog turned into a small, friendly dog. Tim and Sue were not scared anymore. They played with the small dog and the magic pipe all day long. And they all became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red bird. The bird had a friend, a small, green frog. They liked to play together in the forest. One day, the frog mentioned a pretty gem he found. The bird thought it was interesting and wanted to see it. The frog took the bird to the gem. It was blue and shiny, just like the sky. The bird and the frog were very happy to find it. They started to play with the gem, throwing it in the air and catching it. But then, a big, scary wolf saw them playing with the gem. The wolf wanted the gem for himself. He ran to the bird and the frog, taking the gem away from them. The bird and the frog were very sad. They lost their new, pretty gem and could not play with it anymore.

One day, a small cat named Kitty went out to study life. She wanted to learn about the world. Kitty was very alert and watched everything around her. Kitty saw a big dog named Max. Max was not nice. He said, "Kitty, I will catch you!" Kitty was scared and ran away fast. She hid behind a tree. Max looked for her, but could not find her. Kitty thought she was safe. Then, something unexpected happened. A big bird came down and picked Kitty up. The bird took her high up in the sky. Kitty was very scared and cried, "Help me!" But no one could hear her. The bird took her far away, and Kitty was never seen again.

Once upon a time, there was a big, bald bird named Bob. He lived in a tall tree near a small river. Bob loved to sing all day long. His songs made everyone happy. One day, while Bob was singing, he saw a big log floating down the river. On the log, there was a small, scared cat. The cat cried, "Help me! I can't swim!" Bob wanted to help the cat. He flew down from the tree and landed on the log. Then, he sang a happy song to make the cat feel better. The cat smiled and said, "Thank you, Bob!" Together, Bob and the cat floated on the log down the river. They sang songs and became good friends. From that day on, they always played and sang together near the tall tree and the small river.

One day, a little cat named Mimi was sitting on the porch. It was a modest house, with a small yard and a big tree. Mimi liked to watch birds and play with her ball on the porch. Mimi's friend, a dog named Max, came over to play. "Hi Mimi, do you want to play catch?" Max asked. Mimi nodded and they started to play. They were having a lot of fun, but then Mimi threw the ball too hard and it hit Max's nose. Mimi felt embarrass and said, "I'm sorry, Max!" Max laughed and said, "It's okay, Mimi. You didn't mean to do it." Just then, a big wind came and blew the ball high up into the tree. Mimi and Max looked at each other, surprised. They didn't know how to get the ball down. They tried to jump, but they couldn't reach it. Suddenly, a friendly bird flew down from the tree with the ball in its beak. The bird dropped the ball to Mimi and Max, and they were so happy. They thanked the bird and continued to play catch. From that day on, Mimi and Max always played together, and they never felt embarrass again.
