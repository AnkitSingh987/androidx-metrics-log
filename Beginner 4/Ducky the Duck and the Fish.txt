Category: Beginner
Title: Ducky the Duck and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a little duck named Ducky. Ducky loved to play in the water. Every day, he would go to the pool with his friends. They would swim and bathe in the sun. It was so much fun! One day, Ducky saw a weak and small fish in the pool. The fish was sad because it could not swim well. Ducky wanted to help the fish. He thought of a plan to make the fish strong and happy. Ducky and his friends helped the weak fish swim in the pool. They played fun games and the fish got stronger. Soon, the fish was swimming and playing with Ducky and his friends. They all had fun in the pool and the fish was happy. And they all played together in the pool every day.

One sunny day, a boy named Tim went outside to play. He saw his friend, Sue, sitting on the lawn. Sue was very patient as she waited for Tim to come over. "Hi Sue!" Tim said. "What are you doing?" "I am waiting for my mom to answer," Sue said. "She is inside the house." Tim and Sue played together on the lawn. They had fun running, jumping, and laughing. They were both very happy. Soon, Sue's mom came outside. "Sue, time to go home," she said. Sue and Tim said goodbye, and Sue went home. Tim smiled as he looked at the lawn where they had played. He knew they would play together again soon.

Sara and Ben were playing in the park. They saw a big basket full of apples on a bench. Sara wanted to eat an apple, but Ben said no. "Those are not our apples. They belong to someone else. We have to ask first," Ben said. Sara did not listen. She ran to the basket and took an apple. She bit into it and smiled. It was sweet and juicy. Ben was angry. He followed Sara and pinched her arm. Sara cried out and dropped the apple. "Ow! That hurts! Why did you pinch me?" Sara asked. "Because you are bad. You took an apple without asking. That is stealing. You should feel guilty," Ben said. Sara looked at the apple on the ground. She felt guilty. She knew Ben was right. She had done something wrong. "I'm sorry, Ben. You are right. I should not have taken the apple. I will put it back in the basket," Sara said. She picked up the apple and walked to the basket. She saw a lady sitting on the bench. She was the owner of the apples. "Excuse me, lady. I'm sorry. I took one of your apples. I did not ask. I was hungry. Here is your apple. Please forgive me," Sara said. The lady looked at Sara. She saw her guilty face and her bitten apple. She smiled and said, "That's very brave of you to admit your mistake and apologize. I forgive you. You can keep the apple. But next time, please ask before you take something that is not yours." Sara thanked the lady and hugged Ben. She learned her lesson. She did not want to pinch or be pinched again. She wanted to be good and share.

The little boy liked to sleep. He loved to dream. One day he saw a big football and he wanted it. He was so envious of the football. So he asked his mom if she could get him one. She said she'd think about it. Later, she went out and bought him a football. The little boy was so happy. He practiced with it every day. He dreamed of one day becoming the best football player in the world. He knew that one day his dreams would come true.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park with her dad. She had a pretty kite with her. Sue was very happy and excited to fly her kite. The sky was blue and the sun was bright. It was a perfect day to play outside. Sue ran with her kite, trying to make it fly high in the sky. Her dad said, "Be careful, Sue. There are rocks on the ground." Sue smiled and nodded, but she was too excited to pay attention. She just wanted her kite to fly. Suddenly, Sue stumbled on a rock and fell down. Her kite fell too. She felt upset and started to cry. Her dad came over and helped her up. He said, "It's okay, Sue. We can fix your kite and try again." Sue's dad fixed the kite and they tried again. This time, Sue was more careful and looked out for the rocks on the ground. Her kite flew high in the sky, and she felt so happy. Sue learned to listen to her dad and be careful while playing.
