Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was bored. She thought her toys were boring. Her mom saw Sue was sad. Mom had an idea. "Let's go to the park, Sue!" said Mom. Sue was happy. She loved the park. At the park, Sue saw a big surprise. It was a leather ball! Sue liked the ball. She played with it all day. When it was time to go home, Sue asked, "Can I keep the ball, Mom?" Mom said, "Yes, you can." Sue was so happy. The ball was not boring. Sue and her new ball lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tom went to the beach with his mom. He saw a big pile of sand and wanted to play with it. Tom's mom mentioned that he should share the sand with other kids. Tom didn't want to share and felt envious when other kids played with the sand. Tom thought of a plan. He decided to hide the sand so no one else could play with it. He put the sand in his toy truck and moved it behind a rock. Tom felt happy because now, the sand was all his. But then, something unexpected happened. A girl named Lily found the sand behind the rock. She started to play with the sand and made a big sand castle. Tom saw this and felt sad. He realized that sharing the sand was more fun than keeping it all to himself. Tom joined Lily and they both played with the sand and made an even bigger sand castle together.

Once, a little girl and her mom went to the park. They saw a big pumpkin on the ground. The girl was very happy and wanted to take it home. The mom said, "We must make sure the pumpkin is safe to take." They looked around and saw no one. So, they picked up the pumpkin together and carried it to their car. When they got home, the girl and her mom made a fun face on the pumpkin. They put it by their door. Now, the pumpkin was their friend and made their home feel safe and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She loved to dress up and play with her friends. One day, Mia found a mask. It was a funny mask with a big smile. Mia thought it was perfect for a game. Mia called her friend, Ben. She said, "Ben, let's have a race! We can wear masks and see who is the fastest." Ben liked the idea. He was a competitive boy and loved to win. They both put on their masks and got ready for the race. Mia and Ben stood at the start line. They counted, "One, two, three, go!" They ran as fast as they could. Mia was fast, but Ben was faster. He won the race. Mia and Ben laughed and played more games. They had a fun day with the mask and being competitive.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to collect pretty rocks. She took a big bag and walked to the park. Amy was very careful when she picked up the rocks. She did not want to hurt her hands. Amy found many pretty rocks of different colors. In the park, Amy saw a mask on the ground. It was a funny mask with a big smile. She thought it would be fun to wear the mask and surprise her friends. So, Amy put the mask on her face and went to find her friends. Amy found her friends playing near the swings. She walked up to them with the mask on. But instead of being surprised, her friends started to laugh. Amy was confused. She took off the mask and saw that it was not a funny mask, but a picture of her own face! Her friends had made the mask as a joke. They all laughed together and went to play with the pretty rocks Amy collected.

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to play with his red ball. One day, when he was playing, the ball rolled under a big tree. Buddy went to get it, but it was stuck. He saw a little ant, and said, "Ant, can you help me get my ball?" The ant said, "Yes, but you must pay me with a thread." Buddy agreed and went to find a thread. Buddy found a thread in the house. He gave it to the ant. The ant took the thread and helped Buddy get his ball. They both were happy. Buddy and the ant played with the ball together. From that day on, they became good friends.
