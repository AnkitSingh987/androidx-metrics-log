Category: Beginner
Title: Jill and Tom and the Dog

Jill and Tom are friends who like to play in the park. One day, they find a big box under a tree. They open the box and see many shiny things inside. They are gems of different colors and shapes. "Wow, look at these gems! They are so pretty!" Jill says. "Can we take some home?" Tom asks. "Sure, why not? Let's pick our favorites!" Jill says. They start to look at the gems and choose the ones they like. Jill likes the red ones and Tom likes the blue ones. They put some gems in their pockets and bags. But then, a big dog comes running to the box. It is a lively dog that likes to chase and bite things. It sees the gems and thinks they are toys. It jumps into the box and starts to kick and toss the gems around. "Hey, stop that! Those are our gems!" Jill shouts. "Go away, bad dog! Leave us alone!" Tom yells. But the dog does not listen. It keeps kicking and throwing the gems. Some gems fly out of the box and land on the grass. Some gems hit Jill and Tom and hurt them. "Ow, ow, ow! That hurts!" Jill cries. "Ouch, ouch, ouch! That's not nice!" Tom whines. They try to get the dog away from the box, but the dog is too big and strong. They are scared and sad. They want their gems back. Then, they hear a whistle. It is the dog's owner. He is looking for his dog. He sees the dog in the box and the gems on the ground. He sees Jill and Tom crying and holding their gems. "Rex, what have you done? You naughty dog! Come here, now!" He says. He runs to the box and grabs the dog by the collar. He pulls the dog out of the box and scolds him. He says he is sorry to Jill and Tom. He says he will pay for the gems and help them clean up. Jill and Tom are still upset, but they are glad the dog is gone. They say thank you to the man and start to pick up their gems. They hope the gems are not broken or lost. They still think the gems are pretty, but they also think they are trouble. They decide to play with something else next time.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Alice. She was three years old and loved to learn new things. One day, Alice was playing with her toy in the garden. She saw a bird flying around and wondered how it could move around so easily. Alice's mum saw her and said, "Alice, do you want to learn how birds fly?" Alice was so excited and said yes! Her mum said, "You can learn but it will take some work. You will have to practice a lot!" Alice didn't understand but she said she would try. She started to try and practise with her toy plane. After a few days, she was able to make her plane fly around like the birds. Alice was very happy and proud of herself. She had learnt something new! Moral: When you want to learn a new skill, you must be willing to put in the hard work to achieve it. It may not be easy, but with dedication and practice, you can do it!

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. He loved to swim all day long. He lived in a small pond near a big house. Fin was a very fragile fish, but he was also very happy. One day, a big machine called a dishwasher was brought into the house. The people in the house were very excited about their new dishwasher. They used it to clean their plates and cups. Fin didn't know what a dishwasher was, but he could hear it making noise from his pond. One sunny day, as Fin was swimming in his pond, something unexpected happened. The dishwasher started to make a lot of bubbles. The bubbles went out of the house and into Fin's pond. Fin was surprised, but he liked the bubbles. He swam and played with them all day long. The bubbles made Fin's pond a magical place, and he enjoyed his new bubbly friends.
