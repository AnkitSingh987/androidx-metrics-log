Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Rainbow

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play outside. One day, they see a big rainbow in the sky. It has many colors: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and purple. "Wow, look at the rainbow!" Lily says. "It is so pretty!" "Yes, it is!" Ben says. "Do you want to meet the rainbow?" "How can we meet the rainbow?" Lily asks. "It is so high and far!" "Maybe we can use a ladder," Ben says. "Or a big balloon. Or a magic carpet." Lily and Ben look around. They do not see a ladder, a big balloon, or a magic carpet. They see a slide, a swing, and a sandbox. "Let's use the slide!" Lily says. "Maybe we can slide up to the rainbow." "OK, let's try!" Ben says. They run to the slide. They take turns to slide up and down. They try to reach the rainbow, but they cannot. The rainbow is still high and far. They go to the swing. They push each other to swing high and low. They try to touch the rainbow, but they cannot. The rainbow is still high and far. They go to the sandbox. They make a big pile of sand. They climb on top of the pile. They try to see the rainbow, but they cannot. The rainbow is gone. "Oh, no, the rainbow is gone!" Lily says. "We did not meet the rainbow!" "It is OK, Lily," Ben says. "We can meet the rainbow another day. Maybe it will come back when it rains." "Yes, maybe," Lily says. "But we had fun today. We played on the slide, the swing, and the sandbox. We are still friends." "Yes, we are," Ben says. "And you know what? You are my rainbow. You make me happy." Lily smiles. She hugs Ben. "You are my rainbow, too. You are smooth and nice." They hold hands and walk home. They are happy. They are friends. They are rainbows.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to read her favorite magazine. It had many pictures of tasty food. She liked to look at them and pretend she was eating them. One day, Sue's mom made a tasty cake. Sue was so happy! But she forgot to put her magazine away. She left it on the table near the cake. Sue went outside to play with her friends. When Sue came back inside, she saw that her magazine was on the floor. Her little brother, Tim, had spilled juice on it. The magazine was wet and the pictures were spoiled. Sue was sad, but she knew it was her fault for not putting it away. Sue's mom saw her sad face and hugged her. She said, "It's okay, we can get a new magazine next time." Sue smiled and hugged her mom back. They enjoyed the tasty cake together, and Sue learned to always put her things away.

Lila loved to fly. She had a metal toy plane that she played with every day. She made it zoom and swoop in the air, pretending she was a pilot. One day, she saw a real plane in the sky. It was big and shiny and made a loud noise. Lila wanted to fly in it. She asked her mom, "Can we go in the plane, mom?" Mom smiled and said, "Maybe someday, Lila. But not today. Today we have to go to the store and buy some food." Lila was sad. She wanted to fly now. She hugged her metal toy plane and said, "You are my perfect plane. You can take me anywhere." Mom saw Lila's face and said, "I know you love to fly, Lila. But you can also have fun on the ground. Look, there is a park over there. Do you want to play on the swings and the slide?" Lila looked at the park. It had bright colors and happy children. She thought it looked fun. She said, "OK, mom. Let's go to the park. But can I bring my plane?" Mom nodded and said, "Of course, Lila. You can bring your plane. And maybe you can make some new friends who also like to fly." Lila smiled and said, "Yay! Thank you, mom. You are the best." She ran to the park with her mom and her metal toy plane. She still loved to fly, but she also learned to enjoy other things. She was a happy girl.
