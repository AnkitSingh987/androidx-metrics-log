Category: Beginner
Title: Amy the Girl and the Door

Once upon a time, in a green forest, there lived a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to walk and play in the forest every day. One day, while walking, she found a small house. The house was old and had a locked door. Amy was curious and wanted to find a way in. Amy asked her friend, Tom the squirrel, "Do you know a way to open the locked door?" Tom said, "Yes, I know. But it's not a good idea to go inside." Amy didn't listen to Tom and found a key under a rock. She unlocked the door and went inside. The house was dark and scary. Amy heard a loud noise and got very scared. She tried to run out, but the door closed and locked behind her. No matter how hard she tried, she could not open the door. She was trapped inside the house. Tom watched from a tree outside, feeling sad for Amy. He wished she had listened to him.

Lily and Ben are playing with blocks. They like to make towers and houses with the blocks. The blocks are different colors and shapes. Some are red, some are blue, some are yellow. Some are round, some are square, some are triangle. Lily picks up a square block. She says, "Look, Ben, I have a square. It has four sides and four corners. It is a good block for building." Ben nods. He says, "Yes, Lily, you are clever. You know your shapes. I have a round block. It has no sides and no corners. It is a good block for rolling." They take turns to show each other their blocks and say their names. They have fun learning and playing. But then, Mom calls them. She says, "Lily, Ben, it is time for lunch. Come and wash your hands. Put away your blocks first." Lily and Ben do not want to stop playing. They say, "No, Mom, we want to play more. We like our blocks." Mom says, "You can play more later. But now you need to eat. You are hungry and I made your favorite soup. Please, be good and come." Lily and Ben sigh. They know Mom is right. They are hungry and they love soup. They decide to listen to Mom. They say, "OK, Mom, we are coming. But can we keep our blocks?" Mom smiles. She says, "Yes, you can keep your blocks. But you need to close the box first. You do not want to lose your blocks or make a mess." Lily and Ben agree. They close the box with their blocks. They carry the box to their room. They put it on the shelf. They say, "Bye, blocks, see you later." Then they run to the kitchen. They wash their hands. They sit at the table. They eat their soup. They say, "Thank you, Mom, for the soup and the blocks. You are clever and kind." Mom hugs them. She says, "You are welcome, Lily and Ben. You are clever and good. I love you."

Once upon a time, there was a spoiled tiger. It was very happy and it used to smile all the time. One day, the tiger was playing in its favourite spot in the jungle. Suddenly, a monkey jumped in and ruined the tiger's game. The tiger was mad and it started to growl and roar. The monkey was so scared that it started to tremble. The tiger wanted to chase the monkey away but it felt scared too. Just then, a big crack of lightning flashed across the sky. Both the tiger and the monkey were very scared. In that moment, the tiger realised that it wasn't so brave after all. So, the tiger forced a smile and shared its toys with the monkey. Now, they play together every day and they both smile. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red car. The car had many gears. The car was very patient. It liked to wait for the little boy who owned it. One day, the little boy had a new friend. They wanted to play with the big, red car. The car was happy to welcome the new friend. The little boy and his new friend took turns playing with the gears. They were having lots of fun. The big, red car was very patient as the little boy and his new friend played. They laughed and played all day. The car knew it was a good friend, too. And they all lived happily ever after.
