Category: Beginner
Title: The House with Door and the Magic

Once upon a time, there was a house with a door. Every day, the door made a noise. It was called a record. One day, it was extra weak. The people that lived in the house needed to find help. They searched until they found a man with magic. He said he could help. The man put his hands on the door and whispered some words. Suddenly, the record was strong again! The people were happy. They thanked the magic man for his help. They said goodbye and opened the door. The record was fixed! The house was happy again.

One day, a little red train was going down the tracks. The train was very stubborn. It did not want to stop or slow down. The train wanted to go fast all the time. The train saw a tunnel up ahead. "I will go through the tunnel fast," the train said. But a little bird flew by and said, "No, train! You must delay! You must go slow in the tunnel." The stubborn train did not want to listen. It wanted to go fast. But the little bird was right. The train delayed and went slow in the tunnel. The train was safe, and the little bird was happy. They both learned that sometimes, it is good to slow down and listen to others.

Once upon a time, there was a popular rabbit named Bob. Bob loved to eat salad every day. He would hop and play with his friends in the sun. One day, Bob saw a big house. He wanted to enter the house to find more salad. Bob hopped into the house and looked around. He saw a big bowl of salad on the table. Bob jumped on the table and started to eat the salad. But, he did not know that the salad was not good for him. Bob ate too much and got a bad tummy ache. He could not play with his friends anymore. The end.

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play together. Sometimes they play with blocks, sometimes they play with dolls, sometimes they play with cars. But today they want to play with math. "What is math?" Sara asks Ben. "Math is when you count things and make shapes and patterns," Ben says. "It is fun and smart." "Can we play math with our toys?" Sara says. "Yes, we can," Ben says. "Let's lay our toys on the floor and see what we can do." They lay their toys on the floor and start to play. They count how many blocks they have. They make a circle with the cars. They make a triangle with the dolls. They make a square with the books. They are happy and proud. "Look, we made math!" Sara says. "You are good at math, Sara," Ben says. "You are good at math, Ben," Sara says. They hug each other and smile. They hear their mom calling them for lunch. "Come on, kids, it's time to eat," mom says. "OK, mom, we are coming," Sara and Ben say. They pick up their toys and put them in their baskets. They run to the kitchen and wash their hands. They sit at the table and eat their sandwiches and fruits. They tell mom about their math game. "That sounds like a fun and smart game," mom says. "I'm glad you are learning and playing together. You are my sweet and clever twins." Sara and Ben feel happy and safe. They love their mom and their math. They finish their lunch and ask mom if they can play math again. "Of course you can, my darlings," mom says. "But first, let's have a nap. You need some rest after all that math." Sara and Ben agree. They go to their room and lay on their beds. They close their eyes and dream of math. They are happy and safe.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim went on a safari with his mom and dad. They saw many animals like lions, elephants, and giraffes. Tim gazed at the animals with big eyes, happy to see them. One day, Tim saw a selfish monkey. The monkey took all the bananas from the other monkeys. Tim did not like that. He wanted to teach the monkey to share. Tim asked his mom for a banana. He gave it to the selfish monkey and showed it how to share with the other monkeys. The monkey learned to share and all the animals were happy. Tim smiled, happy that he could help his new friends.
