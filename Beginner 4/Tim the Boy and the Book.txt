Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Book

One day, a boy named Tim saw a big book. The book was about history. Tim wanted to give the book to his friend, Sue. Sue loved to learn about old times. But Tim's dog, Max, was naughty. Max took the book and ran away. Tim said, "Max, give the book back!" But Max did not listen. Tim and Sue ran after Max to get the book. They were sad because they thought the book was gone forever. Then, something unexpected happened. A nice cat named Luna saw Max with the book. Luna was smart and could talk. She said, "Max, that is not nice. Give the book back to Tim and Sue." Max listened to Luna and gave the book back. Tim and Sue were so happy. They thanked Luna and learned that sometimes, even naughty pets can do good things with a little help from their friends.

Lily and Max were going to the zoo with their mom. They were very happy and excited. They waited for a taxi to come and pick them up. They saw a yellow car with a sign on top. "Is that a taxi, mom?" Lily asked. "Yes, it is. Let's go," mom said. They got in the car and the driver said hello. He asked them where they wanted to go. Mom told him the name of the zoo. He nodded and started to drive. The taxi went very fast and turned a lot. Lily and Max felt dizzy. They did not like the taxi. They wanted to get out. They held their mom's hand and closed their eyes. "Mom, I don't feel good," Max said. "Me too," Lily said. "I'm sorry, kids. We'll be there soon. Just try to breathe and relax," mom said. She looked at the driver and said, "Can you please slow down a bit? My kids are feeling dizzy." The driver did not listen. He did not care. He wanted to get to the zoo quickly and get more money. He did not know that mom was angry. He did not know that Lily and Max were scared. He did not know that he was making a big mistake. Suddenly, a police car came behind the taxi. It had a loud siren and a flashing light. It told the driver to stop. The driver was surprised and scared. He stopped the taxi and rolled down the window. The police officer came and asked him for his license and registration. He did not have them. He was in trouble. Mom, Lily and Max got out of the taxi. They were happy and relieved. They thanked the police officer for helping them. He smiled and said, "You're welcome. I'm glad you're okay. That driver was breaking the rules. He did not know how to drive a taxi. He will have to pay a big fine and go to jail." Mom, Lily and Max found another taxi. It was a nice and safe taxi. The driver was friendly and careful. He drove them to the zoo. They had a fun and wonderful day. They saw many animals and learned many things. They did not feel dizzy anymore. They felt happy. They knew that they had a good mom and a good police officer. They knew that they had each other. They knew that they were lucky.

One day, three year old Joey was playing in the park when he saw someone who was wearing a uniform. He asked the person what they were doing, and they replied that they were going to perform. Joey asked what sort of performance they had to do, and they told him it was an acrobatic performance. Joey was enthralled and asked if he could join. The person said they would need him to wear a uniform. Joey was happy to get dressed in a flexible white uniform with his name stitched onto it. When the performance began, Joey did an amazing job and everyone around clapped and cheered. He could not believe he performed such a great show! He felt proud and boasted about his performance to all his friends at the park.

Tom and Sam were friends. They liked to play with cars and balls. One day, they saw a big box in the park. It had a sign that said "Test Group". They were curious. "What is that?" Tom asked. "I don't know. Let's go and see," Sam said. They ran to the box. It had a door and a window. They looked inside. They saw many light bulbs and wires. They also saw a button that said "On". "Wow, this looks fun. Maybe we can make the lights turn on and off," Tom said. He opened the door and went inside. Sam followed him. "Be careful, Tom. We don't know what this is for," Sam said. "Don't worry, Sam. It's just a game. Look, I'll press the button and see what happens," Tom said. He pressed the button. The lights turned on. They were very bright. Tom and Sam smiled. "See, Sam? It's fun. Let's press it again," Tom said. He pressed the button again. The lights turned off. They laughed. "Let's do it one more time," Tom said. He pressed the button again. The lights turned on. But this time, they did not turn off. They became brighter and brighter. Tom and Sam felt hot. They tried to open the door. It was locked. They tried to break the window. It was too strong. They screamed for help. But no one heard them. The box was not a game. It was a test. A test for a new weapon. A weapon that used light to burn things. Tom and Sam were the test group. They did not know that. They did not know they were in danger. They did not know they were going to die.
