Category: Beginner
Title: Fin the Fish and the Crab

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin lived in the big ocean. He loved to swim and play with his friends. One day, while Fin was swimming, he saw something new. He wanted to observe it. Fin swam closer to observe the new thing. It was a poor, sad crab. The crab was crying. Fin asked the crab, "Why are you sad?" The crab said, "I lost my home." Fin wanted to help the poor crab find his home. Fin and the crab swam all around the ocean. They looked high and low for the crab's home. After a long time, they found it! The crab was so happy. Fin and the crab became best friends. They played together in the ocean every day.

The sun peeked through the window as Lucy stepped into the room. It was filled with a row of toys. She clapped her hands excitedly and spotted a big, pink teddy bear. “Mummy, look!” she said. Mummy smiled and said, “Let’s go to the park. We can take your new teddy.” Lucy skipped out of the room and stepped outside with Mummy. The park was noisy with children playing. Lucy spotted a toy swing and ran over to it. Mummy put the teddy bear on the swing and sat down with Lucy. “Let’s swing together, Mummy!” Lucy said, as she stepped onto the swing. Their laughter echoed through the park as they went higher and higher in the air. When they eventually stepped off the swing, they felt so happy and content. The sun was beginning to set and it was time to go home. Lucy’s teddy bear was tightly held in her hands as they took their last steps towards the door.

Once upon a time, there was a big orange cat. The cat liked to talk a lot. He had a loud voice. Every day, he would complain about the hot sun. He wished it would go away. One day, the orange cat met a little bird. The bird had a soft voice. The bird did not complain. Instead, the bird sang a happy song. The cat liked the song and wanted to sing too. But he did not know how. The cat asked the bird to teach him how to sing. The bird agreed, but only if the cat promised not to complain anymore. The cat promised. They sang together, and the cat's voice became soft and happy. The hot sun did not bother him anymore. The orange cat and the little bird became best friends, and they sang together every day.

One day, a little girl named Sue and her friend Tim went for a walk. They wanted to find a new way to the park. On their way, they saw a big, disgusting bug. Sue was scared, but Tim said, "Don't worry, Sue. It's just a bug. It won't hurt us." Sue and Tim walked past the bug and found a new way to the park. They saw a big tree and decided to play under it. They played tag and hide-and-seek. After a while, they got tired and sat down to rest. Sue looked at Tim and said, "I'm glad we found this new way to the park. And I'm glad you were here to help me when I was scared of the bug." Tim smiled and gave Sue a big hug. They were happy to be friends and to have a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree. This tree was not normal. It had a secret tip inside it. In the day, the tip would hide in the tree. At night, the tip would come out and play with the animals. One day, a little girl named Sue saw the tip. Sue said, "Hi tip! Why do you hide?" The tip said, "I hide because I am not normal. I am a magic tip." Sue thought that was fun. She asked, "Can I play with you?" The tip was happy. It said, "Yes, let's play together!" So Sue and the tip played with the animals. They played hide and seek. Sue and the tip became good friends. They played every night and had lots of fun.
