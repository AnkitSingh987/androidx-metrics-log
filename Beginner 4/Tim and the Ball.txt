Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Ball

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play ball with his friends. They would always have fun and try to get a high score. One day, Tim's mom said, "You can play ball, but do not go far. I will not permit you to play near the big road." Tim and his friends found a fit spot to play, but they did not listen to Tim's mom. They went close to the big road. While they played, the ball went on the big road. Tim ran to get the ball. A big car came fast and hit Tim. Tim's friends cried, and Tim's mom was very sad. They should have listened and not played near the big road.

Once upon a time, there was a hunter named Tom. Tom was always tired. One day, he went into the woods to look for food. He saw a big tree and thought it was a good place to rest. He sat down and let out a big yawn. Tom felt uncomfortable sitting on the hard ground, but he was too tired to care. A little bird saw Tom and flew down to him. "Why are you so tired?" the bird asked. Tom looked at the bird and said, "I am always tired. I can't find food when I am so tired." The bird thought for a moment and said, "I know a secret place where there is lots of food, and you won't be tired anymore." Tom followed the bird, still yawning, but hopeful. They walked deeper into the woods, and soon they found a hidden spot filled with fruits and berries. Tom ate and ate until he was full. He felt much better and not tired at all. The bird was right, and Tom was happy to have a new friend to help him. From that day on, Tom the hunter and the little bird were the best of friends, and Tom never felt uncomfortable or tired again.

One day, a big red car went for a drive. In the car was a nice man who had some meat. The man wanted to give the meat to his friend. The friend lived far away, so the man had to drive a long time. On the way, the man saw a big dog. The dog looked very hungry. The dog wanted the man's meat. The man did not want to give the dog the meat because it was for his friend. The dog was sad and angry. The man thought for a moment. He had an idea. He gave the dog a small piece of bitter meat. The dog did not like the bitter meat. The dog left the man alone. The man was happy and he continued to drive to his friend's house.

Once upon a time, there was a big shark. The shark was very dependable. He always helped his friends in the sea. One day, a little fish lost his way. The dependable shark saw the little fish and wanted to help. He said, "Little fish, I will help you find your family." The shark and the little fish swam together. They saw many sea animals. They swam past rocks and plants. Soon, they found the little fish's family. The little fish was so happy to return to his family. The dependable shark waved goodbye and swam away. The little fish knew that the shark was a good friend.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a gloomy room. In the room, there was a big mattress. A little girl named Lucy lived in the house. She did not like the gloomy room. Every night, she would count the stars to feel better. One day, Lucy's mom gave her a small, bright lamp. She said, "Lucy, this will help you when you feel scared in the gloomy room." Lucy was happy and thanked her mom. That night, she put the lamp next to her mattress. When it was time for bed, Lucy turned on the lamp. The gloomy room became bright and happy. Lucy smiled and did not need to count the stars anymore. From that day on, Lucy learned that a little light can make a big change. And she was never afraid of the gloomy room again.
