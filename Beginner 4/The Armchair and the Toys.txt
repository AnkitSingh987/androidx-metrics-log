Category: Beginner
Title: The Armchair and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a big armchair in a small room. It was a very nice armchair. A little boy named Timmy liked to sit on it. He loved to read books and play with his toys in the armchair. One day, Timmy thought it would be fun to push the armchair. He pushed and pushed, but it was very heavy. Timmy felt a little stupid because he couldn't move the armchair. He wanted to make it go to the other side of the room. Timmy's big sister, Lucy, saw him trying to push the armchair. She wanted to help. Together, they both pushed the armchair. It moved slowly across the room. Timmy and Lucy were very happy. They had moved the armchair together. They sat on it and read a book, happy that they had worked together.

Once there was a bear named Pete. He was playing outside in the woods when he saw something sparkly. He decided to rise up from where he was and move closer towards the sparkly thing. When he got closer it turned out to be a fake gem. He was sad and started to walk away when something else caught his eye. It was the same sparkly thing - but this time it was real! He wanted to rise and grab it but the ground started to shake and he tumbled down. Pete was scared, but he bravely tried again to rise and grab the real gem. He finally got up and hugged the gem tight. He was glad that he didn't give up!

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play all day. He liked to run, jump, and bark. Spot was very alert. He always knew when it was time to play or time to eat. One day, Spot was playing in the mud. He got very dirty. His mom said, "Spot, it's time for a bath!" Spot did not like baths, but he knew he had to be clean. Mom got the shampoo and put it on Spot's fur. She rubbed it in and made bubbles. Spot looked funny with all the bubbles on him. Then, Mom rinsed the shampoo off and Spot was clean again. After the bath, Spot was very tired. He yawned and went to his bed. He curled up in a soft, warm blanket. Spot closed his eyes and went to sleep. He had happy dreams about playing and running. And when he woke up, he was ready to play again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She saw something special in her living room. It was an armchair, just for her! It was big and brown, and it was so cozy. She was so excited to sit in it! The girl's mommy and daddy came over to her. They said it was time to introduce the little girl to her new armchair. It was so charming, the girl clapped her hands together in joy! When the girl sat down in the armchair, it was so soft and comfortable. She stayed there for what seemed like hours. She felt so safe and happy, just like she was cuddled in a big hug. The girl's mommy and daddy were so proud of her. They said she was no longer a little baby, but a big girl. She was ready to enjoy her new armchair! From then on, the girl loved to sit in her armchair and it was her special place for many years.

Once upon a time, there was a child named Tim. Tim liked to go to the park with his elderly grandma. They loved to play and have fun together. One sunny day, Tim and his grandma went to the park. Tim saw a big, pretty bird in the sky. He stopped to gaze at the bird as it flew by. Grandma smiled as she watched Tim look at the bird. After playing at the park, Tim and his grandma went home. They talked about the bird and the fun they had. Tim loved spending time with his elderly grandma, and they lived happily ever after.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They have a dog. His name is Spot. Spot is loyal. He follows Anna and Ben everywhere. One day, Anna and Ben see a gate. The gate is old and rusty. It has a sign. The sign says "Do not open". Anna and Ben are curious. They want to see what is behind the gate. They look at Spot. Spot wags his tail. He wants to go with them. Anna and Ben push the gate. The gate makes a loud noise. It opens. Anna and Ben see a path. The path is narrow and dark. It leads to the woods. Anna and Ben are brave. They hold hands. They walk on the path. Spot walks behind them. He barks. He warns them of danger. Anna and Ben hear a growl. They see a big bear. The bear is angry. It blocks the path. Anna and Ben are scared. They turn around. They run back to the gate. Spot runs with them. He bites the bear. He makes the bear go away. Anna and Ben reach the gate. They close it. They hug Spot. They thank him for saving them. Anna and Ben are happy. They have a loyal friend. They love Spot. They decide to never open the gate again. They go back to the park. They play with Spot. They have fun.
