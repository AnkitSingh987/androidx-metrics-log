Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and the Banana

Tom is a very intelligent boy. He likes to play with his toys and make new things. He has a lot of junk in his room, like boxes, bottles, cans and wires. He uses the junk to build robots, cars, rockets and other machines. One day, he makes a very special robot. He calls it Bob. He puts a battery inside Bob and turns him on. Bob can talk, move and beep. Tom is very happy. He shows Bob to his mom and dad. They are very proud of him. The next morning, Tom wakes up early. He wants to play with Bob again. He runs to his room and looks for Bob. But he cannot find him. He is very sad. He thinks someone took Bob away. He hears a noise in the kitchen. He goes there and sees Bob. Bob is on the table. He is eating a banana. He sees Tom and says, "Hello, Tom. I was hungry. Do you want some banana?" Tom is very surprised. He says, "Bob, how did you get here? How did you know how to eat a banana?" Bob says, "I am very intelligent, like you. I learned from the TV. I saw people eating bananas. They looked yummy. So I took one from the fruit bowl. I peeled it and ate it. It was yummy." Tom smiles. He says, "Bob, you are amazing. You are my best friend. Come on, let's play together." He takes Bob to his room. They play with the toys and the junk. They have a lot of fun. They are very happy.

Once upon a time, a witch lived in a big, dark house. She had a cat and a big, disgusting pot. Every day, she made bad soup in the pot. She liked to scare people with her loud voice. One day, a little girl named Lily walked by the house. She could hear the witch inside. "Go away!" the witch yelled. But Lily was not scared. She said, "I want to help you make better soup." The witch was surprised. She let Lily come in. Lily showed the witch how to make yummy soup. They put in good things like carrots, potatoes, and chicken. The witch tasted the soup and smiled. "This is so good!" she said. From that day on, the witch and Lily were friends. They made good soup for everyone in the town. The witch was not scary anymore, and everyone was happy. The witch learned that being kind was better than being mean. And Lily learned that sometimes, people just need a little help to change.

One day, a group of animal friends went to the park. In the group, there was a big bear, a small cat, and a nosy bird. The nosy bird liked to know what everyone was doing. They all played and had a lot of fun. But the nosy bird saw something far away and wanted to see what it was. The big bear and the small cat told the nosy bird to stay with the group. The nosy bird did not listen and went to see the thing far away. The big bear and the small cat were sad and scared for their friend, but they waited. The nosy bird found a big box. He opened it and found a lot of toys inside. He was very happy and brought the box back to the group. The big bear and the small cat were happy to see the nosy bird safe. They all said "thank you" to the nosy bird for finding the toys. They played with the toys and had even more fun at the park.

Tim and Sam are brothers. They like to play outside with their dog, Max. Max is big and hairy. He can run fast and catch a ball. Sometimes, he barks at the birds and the squirrels. One day, Tim and Sam are playing on the porch with Max. They throw the ball and Max runs after it. He brings it back and drops it on the porch. Tim and Sam clap and say, "Good boy, Max! You are so smart and strong!" Max wags his tail and licks their faces. He is happy and tired. He lies down on the porch and closes his eyes. He starts to snore. Tim and Sam laugh and say, "Look, Max is sleeping! He must have had a lot of fun today. He is a good dog and a good friend." They decide to join Max and lie down next to him. They cuddle with him and feel his warm and hairy fur. They close their eyes and fall asleep too. Mom comes out and sees them sleeping on the porch. She smiles and says, "What a sweet sight! My boys and their dog are having a nice nap. They must have had a lot of fun today. They are good boys and good friends." She covers them with a blanket and kisses them gently. She leaves them to sleep and goes back inside. She is happy and proud.
