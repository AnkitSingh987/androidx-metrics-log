Category: Beginner
Title: Sam and Mia and the Dog

Sam and Mia were playing in the backyard. They liked to make things with sticks, stones, and wire. They found some wire in the shed and twisted it into shapes. "Look, I made a star!" Sam said, holding up his wire star. "I made a circle!" Mia said, showing her wire circle. They put their wire shapes on the grass and ran to get more wire. But they did not see the big dog that came into the yard. The dog saw the shiny wire and thought it was a toy. He ran to the wire shapes and grabbed them with his teeth. "Hey, that's ours!" Sam shouted, seeing the dog. He ran to the dog and tried to take the wire back. But the dog growled and pulled harder. "Sam, be careful!" Mia said, following Sam. She was scared of the dog. She saw that the dog had a collar with a tag. She read the tag. It said "Rex". "Rex, please give us our wire!" Mia said, in a soft voice. She hoped the dog would listen. She reached out her hand to pet the dog. The dog looked at Mia and saw that she was different from Sam. She was not angry or loud. She was gentle and kind. The dog felt sorry for taking their wire. He let go of the wire and licked Mia's hand. "Good boy, Rex!" Mia said, smiling. She gave the dog a hug. Sam picked up the wire and joined them. "Thank you, Rex!" Sam said, feeling sorry for being mean. He gave the dog a pat. The dog wagged his tail and barked. He liked Sam and Mia. He decided to share their wire and play with them. They made more wire shapes and gave some to the dog. They had fun in the backyard. They learned that sometimes, it is better to yield than to fight. And that different can be good.

Once upon a time, there was a little mixer. The mixer was very good at mixing things. One day, the mixer went to a big, unknown place. The mixer was scared and started to tremble. In the unknown place, the mixer met a big dog. The dog looked at the mixer and said, "What are you doing here? This is not a place for little mixers!" The mixer trembled more and said, "I am lost. Can you help me find my way home?" The big dog thought for a moment and then said, "I will help you, but first you must mix something for me." The mixer agreed and mixed a big bowl of food for the dog. The dog was happy, but he did not help the mixer find its way home. The mixer was sad and still lost in the unknown place. The mixer trembled and cried, but no one came to help. The mixer never found its way home and stayed in the unknown place forever.

Once upon a time, a little girl was walking in a big, dark forest. Everything was very quiet and she felt quite frightened. Suddenly, she heard a loud noise. A man popped out from behind a tree. He had a big, scary face. The little girl said, "Who are you? Why are you here?" The man replied, "I work here." The little girl was even more frightened. She asked, "What kind of work do you do?" The man replied, "I work in the forest. I protect it from danger." The little girl felt much better hearing this. She smiled and thanked him for his work. She said goodbye and the man waved goodbye to her as she continued walking through the forest.

One day, a big ball went to play with his friends. They all wanted to roll down the hill and see who was the fastest. The big ball said, "Let's have a race! The one who rolls down the hill the fastest will get a prize!" All the balls lined up at the top of the hill. They were so excited. The big ball counted, "One, two, three, go!" All the balls started to roll down the hill. They rolled and rolled, faster and faster. But then, something unexpected happened. The big ball got stuck in a hole! The other balls kept rolling, but they saw their friend needed help. They all stopped rolling and went to help the big ball. They pushed and pushed, and finally, the big ball was free. The big ball was so grateful for his friends' help. He said, "You all stopped your race to help me. You are all winners!" He gave each of them a prize for being such good friends. They all smiled and were happy to have each other.
