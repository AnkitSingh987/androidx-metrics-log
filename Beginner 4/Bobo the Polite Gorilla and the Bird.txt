Category: Beginner
Title: Bobo the Polite Gorilla and the Bird

One day, a polite gorilla named Bobo went for a walk in the forest. He liked to say "hello" and "please" and "thank you" to all his friends. He met a small bird who was sad. "Hello, bird. Why are you sad?" asked Bobo. The bird said, "I lost my toy. Can you help me find it, please?" Bobo wanted to help, so they went to look for the toy together. They looked up and down and all around. Then, something unexpected happened! They found the toy, but it was high up in a tree. Bobo used his big arms to reach up and get the toy. "Thank you, Bobo!" said the bird. "You are a good friend." Bobo was happy to help. He said, "You're welcome! Let's return to our homes now." So, Bobo and the bird went back to their homes. They were both happy to have a new friend. And from that day on, they played together in the forest every day.

Once upon a time, there was a poor boy named Tom. He lived in a small house with his mom and dad. Tom loved to play outside with his toys. One day, he found a big gear in the yard. He did not know what it was for, but he thought it was fun to roll it around. Tom saw his friend, Jane, walking by. "Hi Jane!" he said. "Look at the gear I found!" Jane came over and stared at the gear. "Wow!" she said. "It's so big! Let's play with it together." Tom and Jane took turns rolling the gear and laughing. As they played, Tom's dad came outside. He saw the gear and smiled. "That's a special gear," he said. "It can help fix our old car." Tom and Jane were happy to help. They gave the gear to Tom's dad, and soon the car was fixed. Tom, Jane, and Tom's family went for a fun ride in the car, all thanks to the big gear they found.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a big cobweb in his room. He thought it was dirty, so he decided to fix it. He told his mom, "Mom, I want to fix the cobweb in my room." His mom said, "Okay, but be careful. It can be dangerous." Tim found a broom to clean the cobweb. As he started to fix the cobweb, something unexpected happened. A small, friendly spider came out of the cobweb and said, "Please don't fix my home. I am not dangerous. I am a good spider." Tim was surprised but he listened to the spider. He did not fix the cobweb. Instead, they became friends. Tim learned that not all spiders are dangerous, and sometimes, the unexpected can lead to new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and pet dog, Pepper. Sue loved Pepper very much. Pepper was a useful dog because he always helped Sue find her toys. One day, Sue could not find her ball. She asked Pepper, "Pepper, can you please help me find my ball?" Pepper wagged his tail and started to look for the ball. He sniffed around the house and the yard. Sue knew that Pepper would find her ball because he was very good at finding things. After a while, Pepper found the ball behind a big tree in the yard. He barked and wagged his tail to show Sue where the ball was. Sue was very happy and said, "Thank you, Pepper! You are the best dog ever!" Sue and Pepper played with the ball all day long. Sue knew that she could always count on her useful friend, Pepper, to help her find things.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to paint. One day, he found a pale rock in his yard. He thought it would be fun to paint the rock. So, he went inside to get his paint and brush. As Tim started to paint the rock, he saw a little bug. The bug said, "Hi, Tim! I can help you paint. I have a laser that can make your rock look really cool!" Tim was surprised but said, "Okay, let's try it!" The bug used its laser to paint the rock. The rock now had bright colors and fun shapes. Tim was so happy and said, "Thank you, bug! You made my rock look amazing!" They both laughed and played, and they became the best of friends.
