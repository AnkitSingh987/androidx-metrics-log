Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Ben and the Toys

Sara and Ben were at the airport with Mom and Dad. They were going to fly to Grandma's house. Sara and Ben liked the airport. It was big and wide and had many people and things to see. Sara saw a shop with toys and books. She wanted to look at them. She told Ben to come with her. Ben nodded and followed Sara. They walked away from Mom and Dad, who were busy with the bags. Sara and Ben wandered around the shop. They saw dolls and cars and puzzles and crayons. They touched and picked up some of them. They did not see the sign that said "Do not touch". A man who worked at the shop saw Sara and Ben. He was not happy. He said in a loud voice, "Hey, you two, stop that! You are making a mess! Where are your parents?" Sara and Ben were scared. They dropped the toys and ran out of the shop. They looked for Mom and Dad, but they could not find them. They saw many people and signs and doors, but they did not know where to go. They felt lost and sad. Mom and Dad were looking for Sara and Ben. They were worried. They asked a lady who wore a blue uniform and a badge. She said she would help them. She used a big phone and said, "Attention, please. We have two lost children at the airport. Their names are Sara and Ben. They are wearing red shirts and blue jeans. If you see them, please bring them to the information desk. Thank you." Sara and Ben heard the lady's voice. They recognized their names. They followed the sound and saw the lady at the information desk. They ran to her and hugged her. She smiled and said, "Are you Sara and Ben? I have been looking for you. Your Mom and Dad are here, too. They are very happy to see you." Mom and Dad came and hugged Sara and Ben. They were relieved and glad. They said, "We are sorry we lost you. We love you very much. Please do not wander away from us again. It is not safe." Sara and Ben said, "We are sorry, too. We love you, too. We will not wander away again. We are ready to go to Grandma's house." Mom and Dad said, "OK, let's go. We have to catch our plane. Come on, Sara and Ben. Hold our hands." Sara and Ben held Mom and Dad's hands. They walked to the gate. They boarded the plane. They waved goodbye to the lady who helped them. They sat in their seats and buckled their belts. They looked out the window and saw the airport. It was big and wide and had many people and things to see. They smiled and said, "Bye, bye, airport. Hello, Grandma."

One day, a shrimp named Sam went for a swim near the beach. The sun was shining, and it was a perfect day. Sam saw a big fish and said, "Hi, fish! Want to go swim with me?" The big fish smiled and said, "Yes, Sam! Let's go!" They swam together, and Sam was very happy. They saw pretty shells and fun seaweed. Sam didn't know the big fish had a secret plan. As they swam, the big fish said, "Look, Sam! A big, pretty shell!" Sam swam closer to see the shell. But it was a trap! The big fish wanted to eat Sam! Just then, a friendly crab named Cara came by. She saw the big fish's plan and quickly pinched the big fish's tail. The big fish yelled, "Ouch!" and swam away fast. Sam was safe, and he said, "Thank you, Cara!" Cara smiled and said, "You're welcome, Sam! Let's be friends and swim together." From that day on, Sam and Cara were best friends, and they always swam together on perfect days.

John and his mum were walking in the park. John had his camera and he was looking for things to take pictures of. Suddenly they saw something very cool. It was a big bird! John was so excited, he wanted to take a picture of it. But before he could, the bird flew away. John was so sad and felt very angry. His mum wanted to make him feel better. She said, "Let's go and look for something else to take a photo of." John started to feel better and he started to look for something special. He soon saw a big wave in the sea. He was very excited and he wanted to take a photo. His mum said, "Wait, I'll carry you over so you can take a closer look." John was so happy. He was looking through his camera as his mum carried him over. Finally he took the perfect picture. He and his mum walked home, happy and excited. They both knew they had a very special memory to carry with them always.
