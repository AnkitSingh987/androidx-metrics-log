Category: Beginner
Title: The Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little bird. The little bird was very miserable. It was cold and dark outside. The little bird had no home to stay warm. One day, the little bird saw a big tree. The big tree had many leaves. The little bird thought the leaves could cover it and keep it warm. So, the little bird went to the big tree. The little bird was happy in the big tree. The leaves covered the little bird and it was not cold anymore. The little bird was not miserable anymore. The dark was not scary anymore. The little bird had a warm home now.

Once upon a time, there was a big, round pumpkin. It was in a garden with many other pumpkins. It did not know where it belonged. The pumpkin was very angry because it felt alone. One day, a little boy came to the garden. He saw the angry pumpkin and said, "Hello, pumpkin! Why are you so angry?" The pumpkin replied, "I don't know where I belong. All the other pumpkins have friends, but I am alone." The little boy thought for a moment and then said, "You can belong with me! We can be friends, and you won't be angry anymore." The pumpkin was happy to have a friend. They played together in the garden, and the pumpkin was not angry anymore. They belonged together, and they were both very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy that he loved a lot. It was a cheap toy, but it made him very happy. One day, Tim was playing with his toy when he heard a snap sound. The toy broke! Tim was very sad. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, my toy broke! Can you fix it?" His mom looked at the broken toy and saw a spark in Tim's eyes. She knew how much he loved his toy, so she said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will try to fix it." Tim's mom worked very hard to fix the toy. She used glue and tape to put it back together. When she was done, she gave the toy back to Tim. Tim was so happy to see his toy fixed. He hugged his mom and said, "Thank you, Mom! You are the best!" From that day on, Tim played with his toy every day and took good care of it.

Tom and Sue were hungry. They wanted to eat chicken for dinner. But they had no chicken at home. They saw a farm with many chickens. They decided to take one chicken from the farm. They went to the farm when it was dark. They were scared of the big dog that guarded the farm. They heard the dog bark and growl. They shivered with fear. They saw a chicken sleeping in a coop. They tried to grab the chicken. But the chicken woke up and made a loud noise. "Cluck, cluck, cluck!" The chicken cried. The dog heard the chicken and ran to the coop. The dog saw Tom and Sue and chased them. Tom and Sue dropped the chicken and ran away. They ran as fast as they could. They were sorry for trying to take the chicken. The farmer heard the noise and came out. He saw the dog and the chicken. He was calm and kind. He petted the dog and the chicken. He said, "Good dog, good chicken. You protected the farm. You are brave and smart. I am proud of you." He gave the dog and the chicken some food and water. He thanked them for their work. The moral of the story is: Do not take what is not yours. It is wrong and dangerous. Be honest and respectful. It is good and safe.

Once upon a time, there was a pale cup. This cup could talk. The cup lived on a table. The cup had many friends. The friends were cups too. They all loved to talk. One day, a girl came to the table. She picked up the pale cup. The pale cup did not talk. The girl was sad. She wanted the cup to talk. She put the cup back on the table. The cup talked to the other cups. They all talked and laughed. The girl came back to the table. She picked up a different cup. The pale cup talked. The girl was happy. She did not know that cups could talk. The pale cup and the girl became best friends. And they all talked and laughed together.
