Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben and the Mouse

Anna and Ben were playing in the garden. They had a big bucket of water and some cups. They liked to fill the cups and pour the water on the flowers, the grass and each other. "Look, Ben, I can make a big splash!" Anna said, as she threw a cup of water in the air. "Wow, Anna, that's fun!" Ben said, as he caught some of the water on his head. "But look, there is a crack in the wall. Maybe we can soak it with water and see what happens." Anna and Ben ran to the wall. They saw a long, thin crack that went from the top to the bottom. They poured some water on it, but nothing happened. "Maybe we need more water," Anna said. She ran back to the bucket and filled her cup. Ben did the same. They came back to the wall and poured more water on the crack. This time, something happened. The crack opened wider and a pale, furry creature came out. It had big ears, a long nose and a curly tail. "Hello, who are you?" the creature said, in a squeaky voice. Anna and Ben were surprised and scared. They dropped their cups and backed away. "Please, don't be afraid," the creature said. "I'm a mouse. I live in the wall, but it was too dry and hot. Thank you for soaking the crack and letting me out. You are very kind." Anna and Ben looked at each other. They felt sorry for the mouse. They decided to be brave and friendly. "Hello, mouse," Anna said. "We are Anna and Ben. We are playing in the garden. Do you want to play with us?" The mouse smiled and nodded. He followed Anna and Ben to the bucket. They gave him a cup and showed him how to splash water. They had a lot of fun together. They became friends.

One day, a pale boy named Tom went to the park. He liked to read books under a big tree. He sat on the soft soil and opened his book. As Tom read, he saw a small bug in the soil. The bug was trying to find food. Tom wanted to help the bug, so he put a leaf near it. The bug was happy and ate the leaf. Tom felt good helping the bug. He went back to read his book. The sun was warm, and the soil was soft. Tom had a great day at the park.

Once there was a cat named Tom. Tom had a friend named Sue. Sue was a dog. Tom and Sue liked to speak to each other. They spoke about many things. One day, they spoke about a big party. The party was for a marriage. They wanted to go to the marriage party. Tom and Sue put on their best clothes. They felt a little uncomfortable. The clothes were too tight. But they wanted to look nice for the marriage party. They walked to the party together. They spoke and laughed on the way. At the marriage party, they saw something unexpected. The marriage was not for people. It was for two birds! Tom and Sue were surprised. They had never seen a bird marriage before. They laughed and danced at the party. They had a lot of fun. And they did not feel uncomfortable anymore.

A little girl was standing in her garden looking at the sky. She said, "Mommy, I want to soar like a bird!" Her mom replied, " You can't fly, dear. That's only for the birds." The little girl was sad, but her mom gave her a special seat. It was a cute little seat with wings on the sides. The little girl was happy. She loved to play with it. One day, she decided to give it a try. She ran with it, jumped, and flew. That made her so happy she kept doing it for hours and hours. One day, the little girl flew too high. Her seat suddenly stopped working, and she fell from the sky. The little girl wasn't hurt, but her seat was damaged beyond repair. The little girl was very sad. She looked up in the sky and said, "I should have never tried to soar like the birds".
