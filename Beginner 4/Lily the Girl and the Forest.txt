Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Forest

Once, in a big green forest, there lived a little girl named Lily. She had a small, red axe that she used to cut wood for her family. One day, while she was out in the forest, her axe vanished! Lily was very sad and began to cry. As she cried, a kind, old bird heard her and flew down. "Why are you sad?" asked the bird. Lily said, "My axe is gone, and I need it to help my family." The bird said, "Do not worry, I will help you find it." They looked high and low, under rocks and in trees. Finally, they found the axe stuck in a big tree. The bird helped Lily pull the axe out of the tree. Lily was very happy and said, "Thank you, bird! You are a very good friend!" The bird was happy too because he could help. They both went back to their homes, feeling fit and ready for a new day.

Once upon a time, in a quiet little town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to search for things in his big yard. He would look for bugs, rocks, and flowers. One day, Tim went out to search for something new. As he was searching, he found a small, round thing. It was a lung! Tim did not know what it was, but he knew it was important. He took the lung to his mom and dad. They were very surprised and told him that it was something special. Tim and his family took the lung to a doctor. The doctor said it was a magic lung that could help people who were sick. Tim and his family decided to share the magic lung with others. They helped many people in the town feel better. The moral of the story is that when you find something special, it is good to share it with others.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to dance. She danced in the sun and in the rain. She danced all day long. One day, Lily found a tiny pebble. The pebble was very pretty. She thought it was yummy, like candy. She put the pebble in her pocket and danced some more. Lily wanted to show her friends the yummy pebble. She took it out of her pocket and showed them. They all wanted to taste it. But when they tried, they got sick. The yummy pebble was not good to eat. Everyone was sad and stopped dancing.

Lily was a little girl who liked to question everything. She always asked her mom and dad why the sky was blue, why the grass was green, why the dog had a tail. She wanted to know more and more. One day, she saw a big cat in the park. It had a long, fluffy tail that moved up and down. Lily was curious. She ran after the cat and tried to touch its tail. She thought it would be soft and nice. But the cat did not like Lily. It did not want her to touch its tail. It was angry and scared. It turned around and hissed at Lily. It showed its sharp teeth and claws. It scratched Lily's face and made her cry. Lily's mom and dad heard her cry and ran to help her. They saw the cat run away and Lily's face bleeding. They were sad and worried. They took Lily to the doctor and gave her a hug. Lily learned a hard lesson. She learned that some things are not safe to question. She learned that some animals do not like to be bothered. She wished she had listened to her mom and dad. She was not glad. She was sorry.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. It was a wet day, and there were many puddles on the ground. Tim's mom told him, "Do not touch the wet puddles, or you will get your shoes wet." But Tim did not listen. He saw a big puddle and jumped in it. His shoes got very wet. Tim's mom was not happy. "I told you not to jump in the puddle," she said. Later, they saw a big, wet dog. The dog wanted to play, but Tim's mom said, "Do not touch the dog, or you will get even more wet." This time, Tim listened to his mom and did not touch the dog. They went home, and Tim learned to not ignore his mom's words.
