Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Rabbit and the Squirrel

Once upon a time there were two friends - a rabbit and a squirrel. They both lived in a tall, green tower in the forest. One day, the rabbit and the squirrel decided to have a play fight. They jumped, rolled and ran around the tower. Suddenly, the rabbit started to get dizzy and wobbly. The squirrel saw this and said "Rabbit, take a break and sit for a second". The rabbit replied "I'm too dizzy to sit and I'm very hot". The squirrel suggested "I know! Let's dry up in the tall tower. We can sit on the stairs and enjoy the cool breeze". The two friends eagerly ran to the tall tower and sat down on the stairs. They felt very comfortable and the cool breeze helped to dry them up. The rabbit and the squirrel are still friends today and still enjoy visiting the tall tower when they get too hot or dizzy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very happy because it was his birthday. He wanted to complete his birthday cake with his mommy. Tim and his mommy went to the kitchen to make the cake. They mixed all the things to make the cake. Tim wanted to put something special in the cake. He saw a bottle with a bitter drink. Tim thought it would make the cake taste better. So, he put the bitter drink in the cake mix. They baked the cake and waited for it to be done. When the cake was done, Tim and his mommy took it out of the oven. They were excited to eat the cake. But when they took a bite, they made a funny face. The cake was not sweet, but it was bitter! Tim and his mommy laughed and laughed. They did not know that the bitter drink would make the cake taste funny. It was an unexpected twist, but they still had fun on Tim's birthday.

One day, a noisy dog named Sam went for a walk. He liked to bark and run in the park. Sam saw a big tree and wanted to play with the leaves. Sam jumped and tried to snap the leaves with his mouth. He took a turn running around the tree, barking and snapping at the leaves. He was having so much fun! Then, Sam saw a little bird in the tree. The bird was scared of the noisy dog. Sam stopped snapping and barking. He sat down and watched the bird fly away. Sam learned to be kind to his new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a humble house. Inside the house there was a hall with two doors. One of the doors led outside and the other door led to a little junior's room. One day, the junior was playing in the hall when he heard the door to the outside open. He looked up and saw his father's best friend standing in the doorway. "Son, come with me," the friend said. So, the junior went with his father's best friend. They drove for a long time and then stopped in front of a tall building. The friend opened a door and the junior entered a room with a lot of people. The junior heard the friend mention the junior's name to one of the people. The person nodded and said, "Yes, I know that name." The junior felt confused. He knew something bad was about to happen, but he could not think about it. Suddenly, the person grabbed the junior's arm and dragged him outside. He was put in a strange vehicle and driven away. The junior never returned home and nobody ever saw him again.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with dough and paint. They make shapes and colors with their hands. They are happy and playful. One day, they go to Ben's house. Ben's mom says they can play in the kitchen. She gives them dough and paint. She says, "Have fun, but be careful. Do not make a mess." Lily and Ben say, "Okay, mom." They start to play with dough and paint. They make animals and flowers and cars. They laugh and talk. But soon, they get bored. They want to do something else. They look around the kitchen. They see a big cake on the table. It is for Ben's dad's birthday. It has white frosting and candles. Lily says, "Let's paint the cake. It will be more fun." Ben says, "No, mom will be mad. It is dad's cake." Lily says, "Don't be silly. It will be a surprise. Mom will like it." She takes some paint and brushes. She paints the cake with red and blue and green. She makes dots and stripes and stars. She says, "Look, Ben. It is pretty. Come and help me." Ben is scared. He does not want to paint the cake. But he does not want to make Lily sad. He takes some paint and brushes. He paints the cake with yellow and pink and purple. He makes circles and hearts and letters. He says, "Okay, Lily. It is done. Let's stop now." They look at the cake. It is not white anymore. It is a rainbow of colors. It is messy and sticky and wet. They think it is funny. They giggle and clap. But then, they hear a sound. It is Ben's mom. She is coming back to the kitchen. She says, "What are you doing? What have you done?" She sees the cake. She is shocked and angry. She says, "How could you? You ruined the cake. It was for dad's birthday. He will be sad. You are very naughty. You have to go to your room. No more dough and paint for you." She takes the cake and throws it in the trash. She takes the dough and paint and puts them away. She takes Lily and Ben and sends them to their room. She says, "You have to stay there until I say so. You have to think about what you did." Lily and Ben are sad and sorry. They cry and hug. They wish they did not paint the cake. They wish they listened to mom. They wish they could say sorry to dad. They wish they could play with dough and paint again. But they can't. They have to stay in their room. They have no fun. They have a bad ending.
