Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a funny little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and have fun with his friends. One day, Spot saw his friends playing in the park. He wanted to join them and play too. Spot's mom was baking a cake in the oven. She told him, "Spot, you can go play with your friends, but first, help me with the cake." Spot was so excited to play that he forgot to help his mom. He ran outside and joined his friends in the park. After playing for a while, Spot remembered his mom and the cake. He ran back home to help her. When he got there, the cake was all burnt because his mom needed his help. Spot felt sad that he did not help his mom. From that day on, Spot learned to always help others before having fun. This made Spot a very good and helpful dog.

One day, a little bunny named Tim was very anxious. He wanted to find a big radish to eat. He looked and looked, but he could not find one. Then, Tim met his friend, a squirrel named Sam. Sam said, "I know where to find a big radish. Follow me!" They went on a long walk to find the radish. Tim was so excited that he might manage to find one. At last, they found the big radish. Tim and Sam were very happy. They shared the radish and ate it together. Tim was not anxious anymore. He had managed to find the radish with the help of his friend, Sam.

Little Pete climbed into the car and asked his dad, "Where are we going?" His dad smiled and said, "We're going to the park!" Little Pete was so excited, he clapped his hands, and said, "Yay!" The drive was long, but it was fun. Little Pete watched out the window as the trees went by; sometimes he would see a branch that was long, thin, and bright blue. He thought it was so pretty. Finally, they made it to the park. Little Pete jumped out of the car and ran over to the swings. He sat down in one of them and started to swing, feeling the wind on his face. Suddenly, his dad shouted, "Look! A blue wing!" Little Pete looked up and saw an enormous blue bird perched on a branch. He was so amazed that he shouted "Wow!", and the blue branch flew away.

Lily and Tom were friends. They liked to play with dolls and cars. One day, they saw a big box in the park. The box had many toys inside. They were colorful and shiny. Lily and Tom wanted to have them. "Look, Tom, a fur bear! It is so soft and brown. I want to take it home," Lily said. "No, Lily, I want the fur bear. It is mine. You can have the ball. It is colorful and round," Tom said. They started to fight over the fur bear. They pulled and pushed and shouted. They did not see the man who came to the park. He was the owner of the box. He was angry and mean. "Hey, you little thieves! What are you doing with my toys? They are not for you. They are for the children in the hospital. They are sick and sad. You are bad and selfish. Give me back my toys!" the man yelled. He grabbed the fur bear from Lily and Tom. He took all the toys from the box. He left them with nothing. Lily and Tom felt sorry and ashamed. They had no toys and no friends. They learned a lesson. They should not take what is not theirs. They should share and be kind.

One day, a little ant named Andy wanted to visit his best friend, a dependable bee named Benny. Andy knew the way to Benny's home, but he had never gone there alone. He was a bit scared, but he knew Benny was waiting for him. As Andy walked, he saw a big rock in his way. He didn't know how to go around it. He felt sad and thought he might not see Benny today. But then, he remembered how dependable Benny always helped him. Andy knew he had to be brave and find a way. Andy decided to climb the big rock. It was hard, but he didn't give up. He finally reached the top and saw Benny's home. When Andy got to Benny's house, Benny was so happy to see him. They played all day, and Andy knew he could always depend on his friend Benny.
