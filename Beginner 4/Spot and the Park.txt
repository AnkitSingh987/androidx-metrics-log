Category: Beginner
Title: Spot and the Park

Once there was a little puppy called Spot. Spot loved to run and play. One day Spot was out in the park, and he found a tasty tube! Spot was so excited, he had never seen anything like it before. He picked it up, gave it a bite, and it tasted so good. Spot ran around the park, biting the tube everywhere he went. He wanted to explore more of the park while biting his tube. Suddenly, Spot felt a big bump on his head. He looked up, and saw a big tree! Spot had run right into it. But Spot wasn’t too sad because he still had his tasty tube. He kept on running around and biting it until the sun went down. He was so happy that he had found such a yummy and fun snack. He knew he would be back in the park soon to play with his new friend, the tasty tube!

One day, a gray car went for a drive. The gray car liked to drive fast. It was a sunny day and the car was happy. But then, the sky got dark and it started to rain. The rain came down hard and fast. Soon, there was a big flood on the road. The gray car got scared and stopped. A big, blue boat came to help the gray car. The boat could drive on the flood. The gray car jumped on the boat and they both drove away. They became best friends and had fun driving together. And that's how the gray car learned to love the water.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to eat pasta with cheese. Mom makes pasta for dinner. She puts it on the table. She says, "Pass the pasta, please." Anna and Ben want to pass the pasta. But they see something outside. It is a big dog. The dog is black and white. It has a loud bark. The dog runs to the fence. It jumps on the fence. It tries to get in the yard. Anna and Ben are alert. They are scared of the dog. They forget the pasta. They run to Mom. They say, "Mom, Mom, there is a big dog outside. It is scary. It wants to come in." Mom goes to the window. She sees the dog. She is not scared. She knows the dog. She says, "That is Spot. He is our neighbor's dog. He is friendly. He likes to play. He does not want to hurt us. He just wants to say hello." Mom opens the door. She calls Spot. Spot stops barking. He wags his tail. He comes to Mom. He licks her hand. He is happy. Mom pets Spot. She says, "Good boy, Spot. You are a nice dog. But you are too loud. You scared Anna and Ben. You should be quiet." Mom takes Spot to the fence. She opens the gate. She says, "Go home, Spot. Go to your owner. He is looking for you. He misses you. He will give you a treat." Spot runs to his owner. He barks goodbye. He is happy. Mom goes back to the table. She says, "Sorry, kids. That was Spot. He is not a bad dog. He is just a noisy dog. He likes to visit us. But he should not jump on the fence. He should stay in his yard. Now, let's eat our pasta. It is getting cold. Pass the pasta, please." Anna and Ben pass the pasta. They are not scared anymore. They are hungry. They eat their pasta with cheese. They like it. They say, "Thank you, Mom. This is yummy pasta. We like pasta. And we like Spot. But we like him better when he is quiet."

Once upon a time, Max and Bella were very good friends. But one day, Bella lost her memory. She couldn't remember who Max was or their friendship. Max was very sad and tried to help Bella remember. He kindly reminded her of all their fun adventures. But every day, it was a quiet struggle as Bella slowly forgot. Then one day, Bella had a big surprise. Max had written her a letter full of all of their stories, so she could remember. It all came flooding back: the laughter, happiness and fun. Suddenly, it was like no time had passed and they were friends again. They laughed and hugged and hugged, neither one ever wanting to lose their special friendship again.
