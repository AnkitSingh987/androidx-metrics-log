Category: Beginner
Title: Kitty the Cat and the Box

One day, a small cat named Kitty went for a walk. She saw her friend, a big dog named Max. Max had a serious look on his face. Kitty asked, "Max, why are you so serious?" Max said, "I want to fit the whole universe in my mouth!" Kitty thought Max was being silly. She said, "You cannot fit the universe in your mouth, Max. It's too big!" Kitty and Max kept walking. They saw a small box on the ground. Max had an idea. He thought if he could fit in the box, he could fit the universe in his mouth. Max tried to get in the box, but he was too big. Kitty laughed and said, "See, Max? You cannot even fit in the box!" Just then, a bird flew down and dropped a tiny candy in Max's mouth. The candy was called "Universe Candy." Max ate the candy and suddenly, he could taste the whole universe in his mouth! Max was so happy and said, "Kitty, I did it! I fit the universe in my mouth!" Kitty laughed and said, "I guess you did, Max!" They both smiled and continued their walk, knowing that sometimes, unexpected things can happen.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. It was a cold day, and the ground was icy. Tim saw a small fly on a leaf. He wanted to say hello to the fly. "Hi, fly!" said Tim. "Do you like the cold?" The fly looked at Tim and said, "No, I don't like it. It's too icy for me. I can't fly well." As they talked, Tim started to walk away. But he did not see the ice on the ground. He started to stumble and slip. Tim was scared he would fall. But then, something unexpected happened. The little fly flew up and caught Tim's hand. With all its strength, the fly pulled Tim back up. Tim was safe and did not fall. "Thank you, fly!" said Tim. "You saved me!" The fly smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim! Even though I don't like the cold, I'm happy I could help you."

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Dozie and Bell. Every day they went out to the park to play together. They would run around, go down slides and swing on the swings. One day, when Dozie and Bell were at the park, Bell saw a piece of paper on the ground. It was white and crinkled and full of interesting drawings. "Ooh, what is this?" Bell asked. Dozie was a bit jealous that Bell had found something, and he said, "That's my paper! Give it back to me!" But Bell said, "No, I found it first!" And she held onto the paper. Dozie was very upset and he said, "I don't want to swing with you anymore!" Bell was sad, but she said, "OK, I won't swing with you, but can I keep the paper?" Dozie thought about it for a moment and then said, "OK, you can keep it. But let's still swing together. Friends don't stay mad for long." And so Dozie and Bell went back to swinging together, and they were happy again.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. It was very quiet there. He saw something unusual. It was a big, colorful bird. The bird was sitting on a tree branch. Tim walked up to the bird and asked a question. "Why are you so unusual?" he asked. The bird looked at Tim and said, "I am unusual because I am not like other birds. I can talk to people like you!" Tim was very happy to meet the unusual bird. They talked and played in the quiet park all day. They became very good friends. And from that day on, Tim always had someone to talk to when he went to the park.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. He loved to play and fight with his toy. One day, Max saw a fine apron hanging on a chair. He thought it was a toy and wanted to play with it. Max's friend, a little cat named Lily, saw Max looking at the apron. She knew that the apron was not a toy, but she did not say anything. She just watched Max and waited to see what would happen. Max took the apron and started to fight with it. Suddenly, the apron turned into a big, soft pillow! Max and Lily were very surprised. They laughed and played with the pillow all day long. From that day on, they knew that sometimes, things are not what they seem.
