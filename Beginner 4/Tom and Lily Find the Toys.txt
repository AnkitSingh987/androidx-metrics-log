Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily Find the Toys

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play with their toys. One day, they found a big box in the garage. It had a lot of clothes and hats and shoes inside. They decided to play dress-up and change their looks. "Look at me, I'm a pirate!" Tom said, putting on a thick coat and a hat with a feather. He held a wooden sword and pretended to fight. "I'm a princess!" Lily said, wearing a long dress and a crown. She held a wand and pretended to cast spells. They had a lot of fun, until their mother came home. She saw the mess they made and was very angry. "What are you doing with my old clothes?" she asked. "You ruined them! They are dirty and torn and have holes!" "Sorry, mother, we were just playing," Tom and Lily said. "That's no excuse! You should have asked me first! And you should have been careful with them! These clothes are special to me! They remind me of my mother, who is in heaven now. She gave them to me before she died. You don't know how much they mean to me!" Tom and Lily felt very bad. They didn't know that the clothes were so important to their mother. They wished they could change what they did, but it was too late. Mother took the clothes and threw them in the trash. She told Tom and Lily to go to their room and think about what they did. She said she was very disappointed in them. Tom and Lily cried in their room. They learned a hard lesson. They learned that they should always respect other people's things, and that they should never play with something that is not theirs without asking. They learned that some things are more than just things, they are memories and feelings. They learned that they should always be sorry and say sorry when they make a mistake. They learned that they should always love their mother, because she loves them.

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to swing, slide and run. They saw a big cable on the ground. It was long and thick and shiny. "Look, a snake!" Tom said, pointing at the cable. "No, silly, that's not a snake. That's a cable. It helps people make phone calls and watch TV," Lily said. "How do you know?" Tom asked. "My dad told me. He works with cables. He fixes them when they are broken," Lily said. "Wow, that's cool. Can we play with the cable?" Tom asked. "No, we can't. It might be dangerous. It might have electricity inside. It might hurt us. We should leave it alone," Lily said. But Tom was curious. He wanted to touch the cable. He wanted to see what it felt like. He sneaked closer to the cable when Lily was not looking. He reached out his hand and grabbed the cable. "Ouch!" Tom cried. He felt a shock in his fingers. He dropped the cable and ran back to Lily. He was crying and rubbing his hand. "What happened? What did you do?" Lily asked. "I touched the cable. It bit me. It was a snake after all," Tom said. Lily looked at the cable. She saw a sign next to it. It said "Warning: High Voltage. Do not touch." She understood what it meant. She felt sorry for Tom. She hugged him and said, "I'm sorry, Tom. You were right. It was a snake. A very bad snake. But you are brave. You are not afraid of snakes. You are my friend." Tom stopped crying. He felt better. He smiled and said, "Thank you, Lily. You are my friend too. You are smart. You know a lot of things. But you are not mean. You are nice." They hugged each other and laughed. They forgot about the cable. They went back to playing in the park. They were happy. They were not miserable.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a musician named Tom. He loved to play the guitar and sing songs. Tom had a hidden talent for making people feel happy with his music. One day, Tom's friend, Sam, came to visit. Sam said, "Can I borrow your guitar? I want to try playing it too!" Tom smiled and said, "Of course, Sam! I will teach you how to play." Tom and Sam spent the day playing the guitar and singing songs together. They had so much fun, and everyone in the town felt happy listening to their music. And that's how Tom and Sam became best friends and shared their love for music.
