Category: Beginner
Title: The Mom and Her Daughter and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a mom and her daughter. They lived in a small house. The mom wanted to surprise her daughter with a fun day. She had a plan. They went to the park to play. The daughter was so happy. But, oh no! The weather was terrible. It was raining a lot. They could not play outside. The mom felt sad. She wanted to make her daughter happy. Then, the mom had a new idea. They went back home and made a big fort with chairs and blankets. They played inside the fort all day. The daughter was so surprised and happy. The terrible weather did not stop them from having fun. They had the best day ever.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to collect rocks. He would look for rocks in his yard, at the park, and on his walks with his mom and dad. One day, Tim saw a sign near the woods. The sign had a picture of a scary monster on it. Tim was scared, but he thought there might be cool rocks in the woods. So, he took a deep breath and went in. In the woods, Tim found lots of rocks. He collected them in his bag. He did not see the scary monster from the sign. Tim was very happy with his new rocks and ran home to show his mom and dad.

Once upon a time, in a big green field, there lived a rabbit. The rabbit was very filthy. He had been playing in the mud all day long. His fur was brown and sticky. The rabbit liked to play in the mud, but he didn't like being dirty. One day, the rabbit saw a big puddle of water. He thought, "I can clean my fur in the water!" So, the rabbit hopped into the puddle. He splashed and played in the water, trying to wash off the mud. After a while, the rabbit got out of the puddle. He looked at his fur, but it was still filthy. The rabbit frowned. He didn't know what to do. Just then, a kind bird flew down from the sky. The bird saw the rabbit's frown and said, "Don't be sad, rabbit. I can help you get clean." The bird took the rabbit to a clear stream, and the rabbit washed off all the mud. Now, the rabbit was happy and clean.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a big red ball. He loved to play with it. One day, Tim saw a perfect hill to roll his ball down. He was so happy! Tim ran up the hill with his ball. But, he saw a big dog. The dog wanted the ball too. The dog jumped and tried to take the ball. Tim was in shock. He didn't want the dog to have his ball. Tim had an idea. He would zip away with his ball. He ran very fast. But, the dog was faster. The dog caught the ball and bit it. The ball popped. Tim was very sad. His perfect ball was gone.

Once upon a time, there was a delicate hose who lived in a garden. The hose loved to help water the flowers and plants. One day, the hose wanted to marry a pretty flower in the garden. But there was a problem. The other flowers in the garden did not like the idea. They thought the hose was too big and the pretty flower was too small. They were worried the hose would hurt the pretty flower. The delicate hose was sad but did not give up. It asked the sun and rain for help. The sun and rain agreed and made the pretty flower grow bigger and stronger. The other flowers saw this and finally agreed to let the hose marry the pretty flower. They all lived happily ever after in the beautiful garden.

One day, a friendly pirate named Tom was walking in the forest. He heard a loud roar. Tom was scared, but he wanted to find out what made the noise. He walked and walked until he saw a big lion. The lion roared again. Tom said, "Please don't hurt me, I am a friendly pirate!" The lion looked at Tom and said, "I am not mean, I am just hungry. Can you help me find some food?" Tom thought for a moment and said, "Yes, I can help you." Tom and the lion walked together and found some food for the lion. The lion was happy and thanked Tom. They became good friends and played together in the forest.
