Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily Find the Dog

Tom and Lily liked to walk in the park with their dog Spot. They saw many things on their walk: trees, flowers, birds, and squirrels. Spot liked to chase the squirrels, but he never caught them. One day, they saw a big wreck on the road. A car and a truck had crashed into each other. There were loud noises and smoke and people shouting. Tom and Lily were scared and curious. They wanted to see the wreck closer. Spot saw a squirrel run across the road. He forgot about the wreck and ran after the squirrel. Tom and Lily followed him. They did not look for cars or trucks. They were foolish. A man saw them and yelled, "Stop! It's dangerous!" He ran to them and grabbed them by their hands. He pulled them back to the sidewalk. He was angry and worried. He said, "You could have been hurt! You should never run into the road! That was very foolish!" Tom and Lily felt sorry and ashamed. They said, "We're sorry, mister. We wanted to see the wreck. We didn't think about the cars or trucks." The man said, "The wreck is not for you to see. It's a bad thing. Someone could have been hurt. You should be careful and listen to your parents. They know what's best for you." Tom and Lily nodded and said, "Yes, mister. We will be careful and listen to our parents. Thank you for saving us." They hugged their dog Spot and walked back to their parents. They learned a lesson that day. They would never be foolish again.

One day, a calm mule named Max was walking in the woods. He saw a big tree with a bow on it. Max thought the bow was pretty and wanted to take it home. Max tried to pull the bow off the tree, but it was stuck. A small bird saw Max and flew down to help. The bird said, "Hi Max! I can help you get the bow." Max was happy and said, "Thank you, bird!" The bird pulled the bow and it came off the tree. But as the bow came off, the tree opened a secret door. Max and the bird were surprised. They went inside and found a room full of bows! Max and the bird took some bows and went home, happy with their new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom had a plan to get the big red ball on top of the tall tree. He wanted to play with it all day long. Tom asked his friend, a little bird named Sam, to help him. "Sam, can you fly up and push the ball with your arm?" Tom said. Sam nodded and said, "Yes, Tom! I will help you with your plan." Sam used his strong arm to push the big red ball. It fell down, and Tom was so happy. They played with the ball all day long. In the end, Tom thanked Sam for helping him with his plan. They were the best of friends.

Molly and her mom were walking home from the park. The sky was open and blue, and the sun was shining. Molly stopped to look at all the trees. "Molly, let's go sit on that bench," said her mom. Molly skipped over to the bench and hopped on. She leaned her shoulder against her mom and they both smiled. Molly opened her eyes and looked in the sky. She saw a bird flying high above. "Look, Mommy!" she shouted. Mommy smiled and pointed up into the sky. Molly looked up and smiled. She felt so happy and relaxed, sitting on the bench and looking at the sky. They stayed for a little longer, until the sun started to go down. Then Molly and her mom stood up and said goodbye to the open sky.

Once upon a time there lived an old man and a little boy. The old man had a spirit living in his house. The spirit was very bossy - she liked to be in charge of everything. Every day the old man would take the little boy for a walk in his backyard. But the spirit didn't like it. She wanted them to stay inside, so she would step in front of them and make them go back in. The old man was tired of the spirit's bossy behaviour, so one day he decided to do something. He decided to step into the spirit's house, to tell her she had to leave. But the spirit was not happy. She got so angry that she started to wail. The wailing was so loud it shook the ground and the house. The old man and the little boy were very scared and ran outside. Suddenly the house started to crumble and crumble until nothing was left. The spirit was gone and the old man was sad. The spirit had been in his house for so long, now it was gone. The bad ending came true.
