Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Stubborn Boy and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn boy named Tim. He did not like to listen to his mom. One day, mom said, "Tim, we need to replace your old toy with a new one." But Tim did not want a new toy. He loved his old toy. Tim's mom took him to a toy store. There were many toys that spoke a different language. Tim found a toy that looked fun. But when he played with it, the toy said words he did not understand. Tim was sad. Mom said, "Tim, let's replace this toy with one you can understand." But Tim was stubborn and said, "No, I want this one!" So they took it home. Tim played with the toy, but he could not understand the language. He was very sad and missed his old toy. The end.

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. He saw a small insect on a leaf. Tim thought it looked funny, so he decided to look closer. The insect had many legs and was a pretty green color. Nearby, a bird named Bob saw the insect too. Bob thought the insect looked delicious. He wanted to eat it for his lunch. This made Tim sad because he wanted to keep the insect as a friend. Tim and Bob did not know what to do. They both wanted the insect for different reasons. Tim had an idea. He found another delicious insect for Bob to eat. Bob was happy with the new insect, and Tim got to keep his new friend. They both played together and had a great day.

One day, a little girl named Sally went outside to play. She loved to skip and feel the gentle wind on her face. As she skipped, she saw her friend Tom playing with a ball. Sally wanted to play too, so she asked Tom, "Can I play with you?" Tom said, "No, you can't play. You are too little." Sally felt sad and did not know what to do. She decided to find something else to play with. She saw a big, hot rock under the sun. Sally thought it would be fun to touch it. Sally reached out to touch the hot rock, but it was too hot! She pulled her hand away and cried, "Ouch!" Tom heard Sally cry and came over to see what was wrong. When he saw the hot rock, he felt bad for not letting Sally play with him. Tom said, "I'm sorry, Sally. Let's play together." Sally smiled and they played with the ball all day. They had lots of fun and were happy to be friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. She loved to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, her mom was cooking on the stove. Sally wanted to help, but the stove was very hot. She was told to stay away. Sally saw a big pot on the stove. She wanted to help stir the pot. "Please, let me help," she said. Her mom said, "No, it's too hot. Stay distant." But Sally insisted. "I can do it," she said. Her mom looked at her and said, "Okay, but be very careful." Sally went to the stove and tried to stir the pot. But the pot was too hot, and she burned her hand. Sally cried, and her mom came to help. "I told you it was too hot," her mom said. Sally learned that sometimes, it's better to listen to mom.

Once upon a time, there was a table. The table was very organized. On the table, there were toys, books, and a cup. One day, a boy named Tim came to play. He looked at the table and said, "Wow! This table is so organized. I want to play with the toys." Tim picked up a toy car and started to play. While Tim was playing, his mom came in the room. She saw the table and said, "Good job, Tim! You are keeping the table organized." Tim smiled and said, "Yes, Mom. I like to be organized too." They played together and had a fun day.
