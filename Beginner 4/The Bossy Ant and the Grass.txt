Category: Beginner
Title: The Bossy Ant and the Grass

Once upon a time, there was a bossy ant. He liked to march all day. One day, the bossy ant told his friends, "We will march to the west!" They all started to march. They marched up and down hills. They marched through grass and mud. The bossy ant was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew them away! The bossy ant and his friends landed in a new place. They were not in the west anymore. The bossy ant said, "Oh no, where are we?" His friends laughed and said, "We don't know, but it's fun!" The bossy ant smiled and decided to have fun too.

Once upon a time, there was a wise owl named Ollie. Ollie lived in a big tree in the forest. One day, Ollie met a little bird named Blue. "Hi, Blue!" said Ollie. "Do you want to play with me?" "Yes, Ollie!" said Blue. "What do you want to play?" Ollie thought for a moment. "Let's print our names on a piece of metal!" he said. Blue was excited. They found a small piece of metal and used a stick to print their names on it. Ollie showed Blue how to write his name. They had lots of fun. After they finished, they hung the metal on the tree. Ollie and Blue were very happy. They played together every day and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big cup. It was an enormous cup. The cup was full of water. A little boy named Tim saw the cup. Tim wanted to play with the water. He wanted to splash in it. Tim tried to splash the water. But the cup was too high! Tim could not reach it. He felt sad. He wanted to play with the water so much. He thought about how to get the water. Then, Tim had an idea. He got a small chair. He put the chair next to the cup. Tim stood on the chair. Now, he could reach the cup. He was so happy! Tim started to splash in the water. He splashed and splashed. The water went everywhere. It was so much fun! Tim was glad he found a way to play with the water.

Once upon a time there was a little boy called Tom. Tom was very sad because he did not have a castle like his friends had. He wanted to have a castle very badly but he could not get one. So one day Tom's mom took him to the park and they started to look around. Suddenly, Tom saw a beautiful castle! He was so happy that he wanted to take it home with him. His mom saw how happy he was and said, “Let’s guess who the castle belongs to.” Tom looked around and he saw a group of children playing in the park. He asked them if they wanted to play a game and they said yes. The game was that he had to guess who the castle belonged to. He asked them some questions and after a while he figured out that the castle belonged to them. The kids were so happy that Tom had figured out who the castle belonged to and they told him he could keep the castle. Tom was so grateful for the kindness of the kids and he promised to always be kind to others. Tom went home with the castle and he found out the power of kindness from the kids. He was so happy to have his castle and now he knew how to make other people happy too. The moral of the story is that it is always better to be kind than to want something for yourself. Being kind will make you happy and others around you too.

There was a thoughtful man named Tom. Tom liked to go to his office every day. He wore a big hat and had a big smile. He liked to help people at the office. One day, Tom saw a little cat outside the office. The little cat was sad and hungry. Tom thought, "I want to help the little cat." So, Tom gave the little cat some food and a soft bed. The little cat was happy and Tom felt good. Now, every day, when Tom goes to the office, the little cat comes too. They are good friends and help each other.
