Category: Beginner
Title: John and the Window

John was very excited when it was time to go to the beach. He looked out his window and saw the sun shining. He grinned and took his number and bucket with him as he ran outside. John looked around and saw a shark swimming in the water. He quickly ran to his dad and asked, "Can we go into the water?" His dad answered, "No, the shark is too bossy and wants it all for himself". John then asked, "Can I go play in the sand?" Dad smiled and said, "Yes, you can go play in the sand, but be sure not to go too deep". So, off to the sand John went. He found a big number and started to make a sandcastle with it. He was so excited and started to jump around. Then he ran up to a bossy crab and started to make friends. The crab didn't want any friends and started to snap at John. John quickly ran back to his dad and said, "The crab was too bossy for me". His dad smiled and said, "That's okay. Let's just go for a walk and look at the beach instead". So, off they went for a walk and enjoyed looking at the beach. It was a fun day for John and he was very glad to have his dad with him.

Once upon a time, there was a generous boy named Tom. He loved to share his toys with his friends. One day, Tom got a new toy. It was a boat with a big sail. He was very happy and wanted to play with it. Tom took his boat to the park. He found a big puddle and put the boat in the water. He held the string that was tied to the boat. The wind blew and the sail caught the wind. The boat moved fast in the water. Tom laughed and ran with the boat. Soon, Tom's friends came to the park. They saw his boat and wanted to play too. Tom was generous and let them hold the string. They all took turns making the boat sail in the water. They had a fun day playing with the boat together.

Once upon a time, there was a blue bird named Bob. Bob liked to fly high in the sky. He loved to feel the wind on his wings. One day, Bob saw his friend, a little girl named Sue. Sue was trying to measure how tall she was. Bob wanted to help her. He flew down and stood on her head. Sue laughed and said, "Thank you, Bob! Now I know how tall I am with a blue bird on my head!" They both felt happy and played together all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a cold day near the sea. Two friends, Tom and Tim, saw a big yacht. They both wanted to play on the yacht, but they could not agree on how to play. Tom said, "Let's play pirates on the yacht!" But Tim didn't want to play pirates. He said, "No, let's play sailors on the yacht!" They started to quarrel because they both wanted to play their own game. Then, a wise old man saw them quarreling. He said, "Stop fighting, boys. If you share the yacht and play together, you can have more fun." Tom and Tim listened to the old man. They decided to play both pirates and sailors on the yacht. They learned that sharing and playing together is better than fighting.

Mum was having a picnic with her three children in the park. She had two cups of juice, one for each child, and she was carefully balancing the cups on the edge of the blanket. Mum smiled as she saw the children excitedly pointing and running around. But then she noticed one of her children, Theo, had picked up a napkin. Mum said, "Theo, be careful with the napkin. Put it back on the blanket please." Theo blushed. He was embarrassed by the fact he had been caught out. He put the napkin back on the blanket and carefully balanced it on the corner. All the cups of juice were safe again. Mum smiled and said, "Much better! Now, let's enjoy our picnic!"
