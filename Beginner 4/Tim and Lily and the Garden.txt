Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Lily and the Garden

Tim and Lily were playing hide and seek in the garden. Tim was the seeker and Lily was the hider. Lily ran behind the big hedge and hid there. She was very quiet and hoped Tim would not find her. Tim looked around the garden and tried to find Lily. He could not see her anywhere. He checked behind the tree, under the slide, and in the sandbox, but Lily was not there. He started to feel sad and worried. Where was Lily? He decided to shout and ask Lily to come out. He said, "Lily, where are you? I can't find you. Please come out. I miss you." He waited for Lily to answer, but he heard nothing. He shouted again, "Lily, please come out. It's getting dark and I'm scared. Don't hide anymore." Lily heard Tim's voice and felt sorry for him. She did not want to make him sad and scared. She wanted to play with him and have fun. She came out from behind the hedge and said, "Tim, I'm here. I'm sorry I hid too well. I didn't mean to scare you. Let's play together." Tim saw Lily and smiled. He ran to her and gave her a hug. He said, "Lily, I'm so happy to see you. You're a very good hider, but I'm a very good seeker. Let's play something else. How about tag?" Lily nodded and said, "OK, let's play tag. You're it!" She tapped Tim on the shoulder and ran away. Tim chased her and they laughed and played until it was time to go inside. They had a lot of fun and forgot about the dark.

Once upon a time, there was a small sack. It had many shapes inside. One day, the sack met a warm, friendly sun. "Hi, sack!" said the sun. "Why do you look sad?" "I have many shapes inside me, but I don't know what to do with them," said the sack. The sun thought for a moment. "I have an idea! Let's play a game. I will shine my warm light, and you can show me the shapes inside you." The sack was happy and agreed to play. The sun shined its warm light, and the sack showed a circle, a square, and a triangle. The sun loved the shapes and said, "You did a great job, sack!" In the end, the sack was happy because it found a friend in the warm sun. They played together every day, and the sack was never sad again.

One day, a pale girl named Lily went to the sea. She saw a dolphin. The dolphin was big and blue. It jumped up and down in the water. Lily was happy to see the dolphin. Lily wanted to play with the dolphin. She took off her shoes and socks. She put her feet in the water. The water was cold and wet. Lily saw the dolphin swim close to her. She smiled and waved at the dolphin. Lily tried to walk closer to the dolphin. But she did not see a wet rock. Her foot slipped on the rock. Lily fell into the water. The dolphin saw Lily fall. It swam to her and helped her stand up. Lily was wet, but she was okay. She said thank you to the dolphin. They played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a curious little boy named Tim. Tim loved to climb trees and play outside. One day, while he was climbing a big tree, he saw a big puddle on the ground. Tim wanted to play in the puddle, so he climbed down the tree very fast. When he got to the puddle, he saw a little frog. Tim said, "Hi frog! Do you want to play with me?" The frog looked at Tim and said, "Yes, I want to play, but I need your help. I lost my hat in the tree you climbed. Can you help me get it back?" Tim was happy to help his new friend. He climbed the tree again and found the frog's hat. As he was giving the hat to the frog, something unexpected happened. The frog turned into a little girl! She said, "Thank you, Tim, for helping me. I was a frog because of a magic spell, but your kindness broke the spell. Now, we can play together!" Tim and the little girl played in the puddle and climbed trees all day. They became the best of friends, and they always remembered the magic of kindness and helping others.
