Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Mom

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big, heavy statue in the middle of the park. Tim thought it was very cool. His mom said, "Be careful, Tim. It looks like it could fall." Tim wanted to play catch with his mom. They had a red ball to throw. Tim threw the ball high up in the air. The ball went towards the statue. Tim ran to catch the ball, but it hit the statue first. The heavy statue started to shake. Tim's mom yelled, "Watch out, Tim!" But it was too late. The statue fell down and broke into pieces. Tim and his mom were sad because the park was not as pretty anymore.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who liked to go out and play on the foggy mornings. She would go into the cold fog and poke the ground with her toe. One day, while she was poking, she saw something shiny. She bent down and reached out with her hand - and found a golden penny! She picked it up and tucked it in her pocket. The little girl was so excited that she ran back to her house with the shiny penny. But when she got there, she found her mother in the kitchen, looking at the penny sadly. "Where did you find this, my child?" her mother asked. "I found it out in the fog," the little girl replied. "Oh no, my dear," her mother said, shaking her head. "This is a spoiled penny. It's bad luck to keep it. We must throw it away." The little girl didn't want to throw away the shiny penny, but her mother was firm. So they opened the door and threw it out into the fog together. Then they closed the door, and the little girl's mother said, "That was wise, my child. Now, let's play!" And they did!

One day, a boy named Tim found a toy car. It was a very old and dirty car. It was so disgusting! Tim wanted to play with the car, but the car did not go. Tim was sad. Tim's dad saw him and asked, "What is wrong, Tim?" Tim showed the toy car to his dad. "The car does not go," Tim said. His dad knew how to solve the problem. "We need a new battery," he said. Tim and his dad went to the store and bought a new battery. They put the battery in the toy car. The car started to go! Tim was happy. He played with the car all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin lived in a big sea. The sea was very dull. Fin did not like the dull sea. He wanted to make the sea more fun. One day, Fin saw a big, shiny rock. The rock was not dull like the sea. He wanted the rock to be in the sea. So, Fin asked the sea, "Can I put the rock in you?" The sea did not say anything. Fin thought the sea would accept the rock. Fin pushed the rock into the sea. The sea started to change. The sea was not dull anymore. The sea was now full of colors and fun. All the other fish loved the new sea. They played and swam in the colorful sea all day long. And so, Fin and his friends lived happily in the colorful sea. The sea accepted the shiny rock, and it was not dull anymore. Fin was very happy because he made the sea a better place for all the fish.

Once there was a lady who had a very special phone. Every time someone called her it would ring. Every day the lady would take her phone out of her pocket. She would leave it on the kitchen counter. One day, the cheap phone started to ring. The lady ran to answer it. But when she got there, she saw that it wasn't the phone ringing. It was her toe! Her toe was pressed up against her phone and it was making a ringing sound. The lady laughed and laughed. She thought it was very funny. From that day on, she always kept her toe away from the phone. She never wanted it to make that ringing sound again!
