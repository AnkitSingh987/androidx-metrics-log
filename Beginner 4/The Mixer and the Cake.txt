Category: Beginner
Title: The Mixer and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a mixer. The mixer liked to create things. It was very tidy. It lived in a small house with its friend, the spoon. They liked to play and make things together. One day, the mixer and spoon decided to create a big cake. They mixed flour, eggs, and sugar. They had a lot of fun. They put the cake in the oven to cook. The cake smelled so good. They could not wait to eat it. But, they forgot to turn off the oven. The cake got too hot. It started to burn. The mixer and spoon were sad. They could not eat the cake. The house was full of smoke. The mixer and spoon learned to be more careful next time.

Once upon a time, there was an old man. He had a big, old ashtray. He liked to keep it in his room. One day, a little bird flew into the old man's room. The bird saw the old ashtray and thought it was a nest. The bird sat in the ashtray and made it her home. The old man came into the room and saw the bird. He was not happy. He told the bird, "Go away! That is my ashtray!" The bird did not want to go. She said, "No, this is my nest!" The old man and the bird both wanted the ashtray. Then, something unexpected happened. The old ashtray began to shake and wiggle. It turned into a magic flying ashtray! The old man and the bird were very surprised. They decided to share the magic flying ashtray and go on adventures together. And they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a crab. The crab was not nice. He was rude. He lived in a big sand hole near the sea. He had no friends because he was not kind. All the other sea animals did not want to play with the rude crab. One day, a big storm came. The waves were very big. The rude crab was scared. He needed help. The other sea animals saw the crab was in trouble. They knew they had to unite to save the rude crab from the storm. They all worked together to get the crab out of the big sand hole. After the storm was over, the rude crab was safe. He was very happy that the other sea animals helped him. He learned that being rude was not good. The crab decided to be nice and kind. He made many new friends. They all played together and had fun. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom loved to eat and play all day. One day, Tom saw a big tree with a lot of birds. He thought, "I will catch a bird today." Tom tried to climb the tree, but he was too fat. He had an idea. He found a long stick and attached it to his paw. Now, he could reach the birds. Tom tried to catch a bird, but they all flew away. He was sad, but he did not give up. The next day, Tom tried again with his stick. This time, he caught a bird! Tom was so happy, he danced around. This was his victory. From that day on, Tom was known as the clever cat who could catch birds with a stick.

One day, a girl named Lily went to a big tree. The tree had many small, pale green things on it. They were olives! Lily wanted to pick some olives to take home. Lily reached up high to pick the olives. She picked one, two, three, four olives. She was very happy. She put the olives in her little bag. Lily ran home to show her mom the olives she picked. Her mom was happy too. They ate the olives together and had a fun day.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk in the park. He had a large flashlight with him. He wanted to find new friends to play with. As he walked, he saw a big tree with lots of leaves. Tim heard a voice from the tree. It was a squirrel. The squirrel said, "Hi, I am Sam. Do you want to play with me?" Tim was happy to meet Sam. They played together under the tree. They laughed and had fun. But then, it started to get dark. Tim turned on his flashlight to see the way home. Sam wanted to see the flashlight too. He jumped and tried to touch it. But the flashlight was too big and heavy for Sam. It fell on the ground and broke. Now, Tim and Sam were sad. They could not see in the dark and had to go home.
