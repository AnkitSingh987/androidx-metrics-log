Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim lived in a nest high up in a tree. He loved his home very much. One day, Tim went out to find food. He looked hard for yummy bugs to eat. After a while, he found a big, juicy worm. Tim was very happy. Tim wanted to return to his nest to eat the worm. He flew up, up, up into the sky. Finally, he reached his nest and ate his yummy worm. Tim was a happy little bird in his cozy nest.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a favorite toy, a small velvet bear. The velvet bear was very old and dirty, but Tim loved it very much. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came to play with him. Sam saw the dirty velvet bear and said, "Ew, that bear is so dirty! Let's ignore it and play with my new toys." Tim felt sad but agreed to play with Sam's toys. As they played, Tim noticed that Sam's toys were not as fun as his dirty velvet bear. Tim said, "Sam, I want to play with my velvet bear. It may be dirty, but it makes me happy." Sam realized that the bear's dirtiness didn't matter, and they played with the velvet bear together. The moral of the story is that we should not ignore something just because it looks different or dirty. We should always be kind and understanding.

Once upon a time there were two friends called John and Jane. They were playing in the park one day and suddenly John shouted, "I have an idea!" Jane asked him, "What is your idea, John?" John said, "Let's play a joke on someone!" Jane giggled and said, "Ok, let's do it!" They saw a girl sitting near the swings and they tiptoed to her. John whispered to Jane, "I'll make an ugly face and you say 'boo!'" Jane said, "Ok!" John made an ugly face and Jane shouted, "Boo!" The girl laughed and said, "That was a great joke!" Then she gave them each a bowl of warm soup to enjoy. John and Jane were very happy and after that, they were always playing jokes on each other and laughing. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little blue witch. She lived in a small house in the woods. She had a big hat and a funny broom. She liked to fly in the sky. One day, the blue witch lost her broom. She started to panic. "Oh no! Where is my broom?" she cried. She looked in her house, but it was not there. She looked outside, but it was not there either. She was very sad. Then, she saw a little bird. "Excuse me, little bird," she said. "Have you seen my broom?" The bird nodded and flew away. The blue witch followed the bird. They found the broom near a tree. The witch was so happy! She hugged the bird and said, "Thank you, little bird!" From that day on, the blue witch and the little bird were best friends.

One day, a famous cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. He met a little bird named Sue on the tree. Sue was sad because she lost her salt. "Hi Sue, why are you sad?" Tom asked. "I lost my salt and I need it for my food," Sue said. Tom wanted to help Sue find her salt. They looked in the grass, behind the tree, and under the rocks. Suddenly, Tom realized that he had salt in his pocket. He had picked it up earlier when he was walking. "Sue, I found your salt!" Tom said, giving the salt to her. "Thank you, Tom! You are a great friend," Sue said, happy again. They became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a gloomy day. The sky was gray and the air was cold. The people in the village were sad because they didn't like the dark weather. In this village, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a dog named Spot. They loved to play together. One day, they found a box that made a strange noise. Tim and Spot were very curious about the noise. They wanted to know what was inside the box. Tim and Spot decided to bury the box in the ground. They dug a hole and put the box in it. Then, they covered the box with dirt. The noise stopped, and the sun came out. The gloomy day turned into a bright and happy day. The people in the village were happy again. Tim and Spot had a great time playing together in the sunshine.
