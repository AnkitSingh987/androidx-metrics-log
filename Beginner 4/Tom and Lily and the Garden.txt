Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Garden

Tom and Lily were playing with clay in the garden. They made animals and people and houses with the clay. They had fun and laughed a lot. But then, a big dog came into the garden. The dog saw the clay and wanted to play too. He ran to the clay and jumped on it. He broke the animals and people and houses with his paws. He barked and wagged his tail. Tom and Lily were scared of the dog. They did not want him to break their clay. They wanted to escape from the dog. They ran to the door of the house and called for their mom. "Mom, mom, help us! There is a big dog in the garden. He is breaking our clay. We are scared!" Mom heard Tom and Lily and came to the door. She saw the dog and the clay. She was not happy. She shouted at the dog and told him to go away. The dog was scared of mom. He ran away from the garden and did not come back. Mom hugged Tom and Lily and took them inside the house. She gave them some juice and cookies. She said she was sorry for the dog and the clay. She said they could make new clay things another day. Tom and Lily were tired from the dog and the clay. They drank their juice and ate their cookies. They thanked mom and said they loved her. They went to their room and took a nap. They dreamed of making new clay things.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She lived in a small house with her mom and her cat, Fluffy. Lucy loved to play outside in her big yard. One sunny day, Lucy was playing with her ball when it rolled away. She followed the ball and saw an elderly man. He was a stranger with a kind face. He said, "Hi Lucy, I believe this ball is yours." Lucy smiled and said, "Thank you, sir." Lucy's mom saw her talking to the stranger. She came outside and said, "Lucy, remember, we don't talk to strangers." Lucy nodded and said, "Yes, mom." The elderly man waved goodbye and walked away. Lucy and her mom went back to playing with the ball, happy and safe.

Once upon a time, there was a naughty little boy named Tom. He loved to play tricks on his friends. One day, Tom thought of a new trick. He would pinch his friend's foot when they were not looking. Tom went to the park to find his friend, Sue. Sue was playing with her ball. Tom hid behind a tree and waited for Sue to come close. When Sue was close enough, Tom jumped out and pinched her foot. Sue cried out in pain and dropped her ball. She was very sad and angry at Tom. She told Tom that he was a naughty boy and that she did not want to play with him anymore. Tom felt bad, but he did not say sorry. The next day, Tom went to the park again to play with Sue. But Sue did not want to play with Tom. She was still mad at him for being naughty. Tom was very sad and lonely. He wished he had not pinched Sue's foot. From that day on, Tom had no friends to play with. He learned that being naughty was not fun. But it was too late. Tom was all alone, and no one wanted to play with him ever again.

One dark night a little girl named Sara was playing in the garden. She heard a strange noise and suddenly started to shiver. She looked around, but couldn't see what it was. Suddenly she saw something orange in the bushes. It was a squash! Sara was scared, but then remembered it was just a squash. She touched it and it was so soft and sweet. She carried the squash carefullly inside and it became her special friend. From that day forward, Sara and the squash became the best of friends. Every day they'd go outside to play together in the garden. Sara was never scared again.
