Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Friend

Tim went to his friend's house after school. In the house there was a large gym. Tim was excited to play and started running around. As he was running, he slipped and fell on the floor. He felt embarrassed and tried to hide his face with his hands. His friend saw him fall and asked, "Are you okay, Tim?" Tim got up and said, "Yes, I'm ok. I was being stupid." "No, you're not stupid," said his friend, "Come on, let's play some games in the gym." So Tim and his friend played a few games in the gym. They laughed, had lots of fun, and in the end Tim learnt that it is ok to make mistakes sometimes.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim wanted to climb a small hill. He put on his warm coat and went outside. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. As Tim started to climb the hill, he met a cute squirrel. The squirrel said, "Hi, Tim! Do you want to play with me?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, let's play together!" They played and climbed the hill together. When they reached the top of the hill, Tim felt dizzy. He sat down and took off his coat. The squirrel sat next to him. They looked at the view and saw their homes. Tim said, "Wow, it's so pretty up here!" They laughed and played some more before going back down the hill.

Once upon a time, there was a big, strong man named Jack. Jack was a judge who helped people in the town. Jack was never mean or stupid, and everyone liked him. One day, Jack saw a little boy named Timmy trying to lift a heavy box. Timmy was too small to lift it, and Jack knew he could help. Jack went over to Timmy and said, "I can help you lift the box." Together, Jack and Timmy lifted the heavy box. Timmy was so happy that Jack helped him. Timmy said, "Thank you, Jack, for helping me. You are a good judge and a good friend." From that day on, Timmy and Jack were best friends. They played together and helped each other. Everyone in the town knew that Jack was a good judge, and no one ever thought he was stupid.

Once upon a time, there was a boy. He was very naughty, and he often broke things. One day, his mum caught him breaking some of her things. She was very angry with him and she said "You're guilty!" She put him in the corner and said "Stay there until I come back!" The boy stayed in the corner. A few minutes later, a big man came in the room. He was carrying a film and he said "I'm here to display this film for you, young man". The boy didn't know what that meant so he said "What's display?" The big man said "It's like showing you a picture, but it's a whole film". So the big man put the film on the wall, and it was the same one that the boy had broken. Everyone watched it and the boy felt very guilty. It showed the broken things he had done and made him feel even worse. When the film was done, the big man said "Now you know why you should never break things again". The boy promised he would never be naughty again and he stayed in the corner until his mum came back. He learned his lesson and everyone was happy.

One day, a big cat and a little dog were playing in the yard. The big cat had a wide smile on her face. The little dog was very happy too. The big cat found a mug on the ground. She blinked her eyes and said, "Look, little dog! I found a mug!" The little dog came running and looked at the mug. He blinked his eyes too. The big cat and the little dog played with the mug. They took turns holding it and running around. They laughed and had fun together. The big cat said, "I like playing with you, little dog!" The little dog said, "I like playing with you too, big cat!" And they played with the mug until it was time to go home.
