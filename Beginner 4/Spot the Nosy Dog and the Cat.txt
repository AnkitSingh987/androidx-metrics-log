Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Nosy Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little dog named Spot. Spot liked to play with a rod his owner had. One day, Spot saw a big cat in his yard. The cat was playing with Spot's rod. Spot did not like that. Spot went to the cat and tried to explain that the rod was his. The cat did not understand Spot. The cat kept playing with the rod. Spot felt sad. Then, Spot's owner came outside. She saw the cat and Spot. She took the rod and gave it back to Spot. The cat left, and Spot was happy again.

Once upon a time, in an ordinary town, there was a big wall. The wall was so big that it made the people in the town sad. They could not see what was on the other side. One day, a little girl named Lucy said, "I want to solve this problem. I want to see what is on the other side of the wall." Her friend Tom said, "Let's try to climb the wall together." So, Lucy and Tom tried to climb the wall. They tried and tried, but they could not reach the top. Then, they had an idea. They asked their friends to help them. All the kids in the town came and made a big ladder. They climbed the ladder and saw a beautiful park on the other side. They were so happy that they solved the problem and found the park.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly mole. This mole loved to sing and dance. One day, the mole had an idea. He wanted to record his songs. The mole asked his friend, a bird, to help him. The bird said yes. They started to make a plan. But, another animal, a big cat, heard them talk. The cat wanted to stop the mole and the bird. The cat did not like their songs. The mole and the bird did not know about the cat. They were happy and kept working on their plan. They found a big box to record the songs. The mole sang and danced, and the bird helped. But then, the cat came. The cat broke the big box. The mole and the bird were sad. They could not record their songs anymore. The cat was happy, but the mole and the bird were not. The end.

One day, Tom and Sue were playing with their new toy helicopter. They both liked it a lot. They flew it high in the sky. Tom said, "I want to fly it now!" Sue said, "No, I want to fly it!" They did not want to share. A big bird saw the helicopter in the sky. The bird thought it was a friend. The bird flew fast and took the helicopter away. Tom and Sue were very sad. They looked at each other and said, "We should have shared." Then, the bird saw that the helicopter was not a friend. The bird let it go. The helicopter fell down, down, down. Tom and Sue ran to catch it. They both caught it together. They laughed and said, "Let's share and play together now!"

Once upon a time, there was a shy little bee named Buzzy. Buzzy loved to visit flowers to get nectar. But he was scared to talk to other bees. One day, Buzzy saw a big, pretty flower in the garden. He flew to the flower to get some nectar. There, he met another little bee named Daisy. Daisy said, "Hi, I'm Daisy! Do you like this flower too?" Buzzy was shy, but he said, "Yes, I like it. My name is Buzzy." Buzzy and Daisy became friends. They visited many flowers together. Buzzy was not shy anymore. They were happy bees, and they loved their flower-filled days in the garden.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy car. One day, Tim wanted to push his car all the way to the park. He was very excited to play with his car outside. On his way to the park, Tim met a big dog. The dog was in the way, and Tim felt frustrated. He did not know what to do. Tim thought for a moment and decided to push his car around the dog to keep going. Finally, Tim made it to the park with his toy car. He pushed it all around the park, having lots of fun. Tim was happy that he found a way to get past the big dog. In the end, Tim had a great day at the park with his toy car.
