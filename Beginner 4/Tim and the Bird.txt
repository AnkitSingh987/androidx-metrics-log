Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Bird

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He felt sleepy but wanted to play outside. As he walked, he saw a big bush. The bush was green and pretty. Tim thought it would be fun to hide behind it. As he hid behind the bush, he heard a soft voice. "Sigh, I am so sleepy too," said the voice. Tim looked around and saw a little bird sitting on a branch. The bird looked very tired. "Hi, bird," said Tim. "Why are you so sleepy?" The bird looked at Tim and said, "I have been flying all day. I am very tired." Tim smiled and said, "I am sleepy too. Let's take a nap together." The bird agreed, and they both lay down near the bush. Soon, they were fast asleep, enjoying a nice nap in the warm sun.

One day, a polite cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big egg on the ground. Tom was very surprised. Tom had an idea. He would suggest to his friend, the dog, what to do with the egg. So, he went to find the dog. The dog was playing with a ball. Tom said, "I found an egg. What should we do with it?" The dog thought for a moment. He suggested, "Let's take care of the egg until it hatches." So, Tom and the dog took care of the egg. They were very happy when a little bird came out of the egg. They had a new friend to play with.

Once upon a time, there was a little house with a chimney. In the house, there was a very organized room. The room had many toys and books. A little girl named Lucy lived in the house. She loved her room and played there every day. One day, Lucy saw her toys tremble and shake. She was very surprised. She didn't know what was happening. Lucy was scared but also curious. She wanted to find out why her toys were shaking. Lucy looked up at the chimney and saw a big bird. The bird was making the toys tremble with its big wings. Lucy was not scared anymore. She laughed and clapped her hands. The bird became her new friend, and they played together in the organized room every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little frog named Bob. Bob was an intelligent frog who loved to hop. One day, Bob saw a big step in front of him. He wanted to hop over the step to see what was on the other side. Bob met a big bird named Sam. Sam said, "You can't hop over the step, it's too high!" But Bob didn't listen. He tried to hop over the step. He hopped and hopped, but he couldn't make it. Sam laughed at Bob. Bob felt sad that he couldn't hop over the step. He tried one more time, but he fell down. Bob was hurt and couldn't hop anymore. The big step was too much for the little frog. And so, Bob never made it to the other side of the step, and he never knew what was there.

Once there was an old pot in Grandpa's house that everyone liked to play with. One day, the little boy wanted to show off and started to reverse the pot around and around. Everyone laughed and cheered, but Grandpa shook his head and said, "No, no, no - don't do that!" But it was too late. The pot crashed against the wall and shattered into many pieces. Tears ran down the little boy's cheeks. Grandpa sighed and said, "Now you must learn an important lesson. When something is old, you must treat it with respect and care. You must not take things too recklessly or you will end up losing them forever." The little boy nodded and promised to never forget this lesson. From that day forward, he made sure to be gentle and cautious when playing with old things!

One day, a big dog named Max went for a walk. The sun was out, and the sky was clear. Max saw a little boy named Tim with a treat in his hand. Max wanted the treat, so he walked over to Tim. "Can I have the treat, please?" Max asked. Tim looked at Max and said, "You can have the treat if you can break this stick." Max was strong, so he broke the stick with his paw. Tim gave Max the treat, but when Max tried to eat it, the treat jumped out of his mouth! The treat was a little frog! The frog said, "Thank you for breaking the stick. I was stuck inside." Max and Tim laughed, and they all played together in the sun.
