Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Jar

Tom and Lily were playing in the kitchen. They saw a big jar on the shelf. It was full of cookies. They wanted some cookies, but they could not reach the jar. "Let's get a chair," Tom said. "We can stand on it and get the jar." "OK," Lily said. They got a chair and put it near the shelf. Tom climbed on the chair and reached for the jar. Lily watched him. "Be careful," Lily said. "The jar is heavy." "I can do it," Tom said. He grabbed the jar and tried to pull it down. But the jar was too heavy for him. He lost his balance and slipped off the chair. The jar fell with him and broke on the floor. Cookies and glass flew everywhere. "Ow!" Tom cried. He hurt his arm and his leg. Lily ran to him and hugged him. "Are you OK?" Lily asked. "I'm sorry," Tom said. "I was ignorant. I should have listened to you." "It's OK," Lily said. "I'm sorry too. I should have helped you." They heard their mom coming. They looked at the mess on the floor. They felt scared. "Mom, we're sorry!" they said together. "We wanted some cookies, but we slipped and broke the jar." Mom saw them and the jar. She was angry, but she was also worried. She checked Tom's arm and leg. They were not bad, but they were bleeding. She got some bandages and cleaned them. "Tom, Lily, you were very ignorant," Mom said. "You should not play with the jar. It was dangerous. You could have hurt yourselves more. And you made a big mess. You have to clean it up." "Yes, Mom," Tom and Lily said. They were sorry. They did not want to be ignorant again. They helped Mom clean up the mess. They threw away the glass and the cookies. They said sorry to the jar too. Mom hugged them and kissed them. She was not angry anymore. She was glad they were OK. "Next time, if you want some cookies, just ask me," Mom said. "I will give you some. But you have to be careful and listen to me." "OK, Mom," Tom and Lily said. They learned their lesson. They thanked Mom for the bandages and the cookies. They said they loved her. They went to their room and played with their toys. They did not play with the jar anymore. They were not ignorant anymore. They were happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a jolly man named Tim. Tim loved to visit the big temple near his house. One day, Tim met a new friend named Sam. Tim wanted to introduce Sam to the temple. Tim said to Sam, "Come with me to the big temple. It's a fun place to be!" Sam was happy and said, "Yes, let's go!" So, Tim and Sam walked to the temple together, talking and laughing. When they got to the temple, they saw a big door. Tim tried to open it, but it was locked. Tim and Sam were sad. They could not go inside the temple to play. Instead, they had to go back home. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a gym where all the kids loved to play. Every day, they would jump, run, and have fun. In this gym, there was a big, bouncy ball that was very clumsy. It would always bounce and bump into things, making the kids laugh. One day, the gym teacher had a big surprise for the kids. They were all very excited and couldn't wait to see what it was. The clumsy ball was also curious and wanted to be a part of the surprise. The gym teacher told the kids to close their eyes and count to ten. While they were counting, the clumsy ball started to bounce around the gym. It bumped into the surprise, making a loud noise. The kids opened their eyes and saw the surprise: a big, beautiful cake! But the clumsy ball had bumped into the cake, and now it was all over the floor. The kids laughed and laughed, as they knew it was the clumsy ball's way of being part of the surprise. They all helped clean up the mess and enjoyed the cake together. The clumsy ball was happy to be a part of the fun surprise, and the kids loved the gym even more.
