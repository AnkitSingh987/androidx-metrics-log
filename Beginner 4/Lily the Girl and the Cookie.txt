Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Cookie

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to sort her mommy's makeup. She liked the pretty colors and the shiny boxes. Lily's mommy would let her play with the makeup, but only if she was careful. One day, Lily's mommy made some yummy cookies. Lily could smell the sweet smell from her room. She ran to the kitchen and saw the cookies on the table. "Mommy, can I have a cookie?" Lily asked. "Yes, but only one," said her mommy. Lily took a cookie and went back to her room. She had an idea. She took her mommy's makeup and started to sort it on the table. Then, she used the makeup to make the cookie look pretty. She put pink on the cookie like blush and blue like eyeshadow. Lily was so proud of her pretty, yummy cookie. She showed her mommy, and they both laughed.

---

Ben and Lily were twins who liked to play with their toys. One day, they found a big box in the garage. They decided to make it into a castle. They used crayons to draw windows and doors on the box. They cut out a flag from a piece of paper and taped it on top. "Look at our castle!" Ben said. "It is the best castle ever!" Lily nodded. "We can fill it with our stuffed animals and pretend they are the king and queen!" They ran inside to get their stuffed animals. They came back with their arms full of bears, rabbits, and cats. They put them inside the box and arranged them on the floor. "Hello, your majesty!" Ben said, bowing to a big brown bear. "Welcome to our castle!" "Hello, your highness!" Lily said, curtsying to a pink cat. "We are your loyal subjects!" They had fun playing with their stuffed animals and making up stories. They pretended they were knights and princesses who had to defend the castle from dragons and giants. But then, they heard a noise outside. They looked out of the window and saw their neighbor, Sam, riding his bike. Sam had a new bike that was shiny and red. It had a bell and a basket and a horn. "Wow, look at Sam's bike!" Ben said. "It is so cool!" Lily felt a pang in her chest. She wished she had a bike like that. She felt jealous of Sam. She did not like that feeling. "Who cares about his bike?" she said. "We have a castle!" But Ben did not listen. He ran out of the box and waved to Sam. "Hi, Sam!" he said. "Can I try your bike?" Sam smiled and nodded. He got off his bike and handed it to Ben. "Sure, Ben!" he said. "It is very fast and fun!" Ben got on the bike and pedaled away. He rang the bell and honked the horn. He felt happy and excited. Lily watched from the box. She felt sad and angry. She did not like that Ben left her alone. She did not like that he liked Sam's bike more than their castle. She grabbed the flag from the box and threw it on the ground. She kicked the box and made a hole in it. She took out the stuffed animals and tossed them aside. She did not want to play with the castle anymore. She wanted to play with the bike. She ran after Ben and Sam. She shouted at them. "Hey, Ben!" she said. "That is not fair! You are supposed to play with me!" Ben stopped the bike and looked at Lily. He saw her red face and wet eyes. He felt sorry for her. "I'm sorry, Lily," he said. "I just wanted to try the bike. It is not mine. It is Sam's." He got off the bike and gave it back to Sam. "Thank you, Sam," he said. "Your bike is awesome. But I have to go back to my sister. She is my best friend." Sam nodded. He understood. "That's okay, Ben," he said. "Your sister is lucky to have you. And your castle is cool, too. Can I play with you guys?" Ben smiled and nodded. He took Lily's hand and led her back to the box. "Come on, Lily," he said. "Let's fix our castle. And let's invite Sam to join us. He can be our friend, too." Lily nodded. She felt better. She was not jealous of Sam anymore. She was happy to have Ben and Sam as her friends. They went back to the box and mended the hole. They picked up the flag and the stuffed animals. They made room for Sam inside. They resumed their play. They pretended they were in a big parade. They marched around the box and waved the flag. They sang and laughed and had fun. They forgot about the bike. They were happy with their castle.
