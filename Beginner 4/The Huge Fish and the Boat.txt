Category: Beginner
Title: The Huge Fish and the Boat

Once upon a time, there was a huge fish. The huge fish lived in the ocean. The ocean was his home. He had many friends in the ocean. One day, the huge fish saw a boat. The boat had people on it. The people wanted to play in the ocean. The huge fish thought, "I will permit them to play in my ocean." The people played in the ocean all day. The huge fish watched them and smiled. The people were happy, and the huge fish was happy too. They all had a fun day in the ocean.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the gym with his mom. The gym was big and had many games to play. Tim loved to play with the big red ball. His mom said, "Tim, don't go too far. Stay close to me." At the gym, there was a cheap toy box. It was blue and had a lock on it. Tim saw the box and wanted to open it. He tried to open the lock, but it was too hard. He asked his mom for help. "Mom, can you help me open this lock?" His mom said, "No, Tim. That is not ours. We should not touch it." Tim was sad but listened to his mom. They played with the big red ball and had fun. Then, a man came to the gym. He saw the locked toy box and opened it with a key. Inside the box, there were many new toys for all the kids to play with. The man said, "I locked this box because I wanted it to be a surprise for everyone." The kids at the gym were happy and played with the new toys. Tim learned that it is good to listen to his mom and not to touch things that are not his.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to skip and play with his friends in the park. One sunny day, Tim met a talking frog. The frog said he had a big brain and could help Tim find something delicious to eat. Tim and the frog went on a fun adventure, skipping through the park. They saw pretty flowers and happy birds. As they skipped, they found a big red apple tree. Tim loved apples, and the frog said that they were delicious. Tim and the frog picked some apples from the tree. They sat under the tree and ate the delicious apples together. They laughed and talked, enjoying their new friendship. From that day on, Tim and the talking frog were best friends, always skipping and looking for delicious things to eat.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there was a little bunny with a big head. The bunny loved to hop and play with his friends. One day, the bunny saw a big, red ball far away. He wanted to play with the big, red ball, but he had to step over many things to get there. The bunny stepped over a small rock, a big log, and a little stream. He was very happy to be getting closer to the big, red ball. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. The bunny knew something good was going to happen. Finally, the bunny reached the big, red ball. He used his big head to push the ball, and it rolled and rolled. All his friends came to play with him and the big, red ball. They had so much fun, and they played all day long. The bunny was very glad that he had stepped over all those things to find the big, red ball.

John and his mom were out walking in the park. He saw a little bunny close by and he wanted to go see it. John started to run, but he bumped into a tree and scraped his knee. His mom saw him and ran over. She was very wise and knew just what to do. She pulled out a bandage from her bag and carefully held John's knee as she wrapped the bandage around it. John was so brave and didn't cry - he knew his mom was there to help him. When the bandage was on, he smiled and ran off to see the bunny. His mom watched him go and smiled, feeling thankful that she had been there to help him.
