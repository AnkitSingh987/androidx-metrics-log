Category: Beginner
Title: The Purple Cat and the Mouse

One day, a big purple cat went for a walk. The cat liked to give things to his friends. He was very generous. The cat saw a small house and went inside. In the house, the cat met a little mouse. The mouse was scared. "Please don't eat me," said the mouse. The cat smiled and said, "I won't eat you. I want to give you a gift." The mouse was happy and took the gift from the generous cat. As they were talking, the door shut by itself. They were trapped inside! The cat and the mouse worked together to open the door. They pushed and pushed, and the door opened. They were free! The cat and the mouse became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a princess who lived in a big castle. She had a terrible problem. Her toys were all broken, and she could not play with them. One day, a kind man came to the castle. "I can work on your toys," he said. The princess was very happy. "Please fix my toys," she told the man. The man worked hard all day. He fixed all the princess's toys. The princess was very happy. She played with her toys and said, "Thank you, kind man!" The man smiled and went away. The princess was not sad anymore.

Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to dig in the soil and look for worms and bugs. Lily found a big worm and showed it to Ben. "Look, Ben, a big worm!" Lily said. Ben wanted to see the worm, but he was busy digging his own hole. He did not look at Lily. "Wait, Lily, I am digging," Ben said. Lily felt impatient. She wanted Ben to look at her worm. She walked over to Ben and put the worm on his head. "Look, Ben, look!" Lily said. Ben felt something slimy on his head. He screamed and jumped up. He saw the worm on the ground and got angry. "Lily, why did you do that?" Ben said. "That was not nice!" Lily laughed. She thought it was funny. She did not understand why Ben was angry. "It was just a worm, Ben," Lily said. "Don't be scared." Ben did not think it was funny. He did not like worms on his head. He picked up a handful of soil and threw it at Lily. "Take that, Lily!" Ben said. Lily felt the soil hit her face. She stopped laughing and started crying. She did not like soil on her face. She ran to the house and called for Mom. "Mom, Mom, Ben threw soil at me!" Lily said. Mom heard Lily crying and came out of the house. She saw Lily's face covered in soil and Ben's head covered in worm. She paused and sighed. She knew they were both in trouble. "Come here, both of you," Mom said. "We need to talk."

One day, a chubby cat named Tom went to the park. He saw a big tree with a knot in it. Tom wanted to climb the tree and play with the knot. As he climbed, he felt something stir in his tummy. He thought it was just hunger, so he kept going. When Tom reached the knot, he saw a little bird sitting on it. The bird was scared of Tom, but Tom just wanted to be friends. He tried to say hello, but the bird flew away. Tom felt sad and sat on the knot. Then, the knot began to stir and move. Tom was surprised and jumped off the tree. The knot turned into a chubby squirrel who had been hiding inside! The squirrel said sorry for scaring Tom and asked if they could be friends. Tom was happy and they played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little plant. This plant was very adventurous. It grew in a big garden with many other plants. The little plant liked to play games with its friends. They would all laugh and have fun together. One day, the little plant wanted to play a new game. It said, "Let's play 'guess what I am thinking!' I will think of something, and you all have to guess what it is." The other plants thought it was a great idea. They were excited to play. The little plant thought of something and said, "Okay, I am thinking of something green and it grows in the garden." One of the plants guessed, "Is it grass?" The little plant said, "No, guess again!" Another plant guessed, "Is it a leaf?" The little plant said, "Yes, you got it right!" All the plants clapped and cheered. They all had a great time playing the guessing game, and the adventurous little plant was happy to have shared a new game with its friends.
