Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Door

Tom and Lily were best friends. They liked to play with their toys and draw circles on paper. They also liked to explore the big house where they lived. Sometimes they found new rooms and things to see. One day, they found a shiny key on a table. Tom picked it up and said, "Look, Lily, a key! I wonder what it can unlock." Lily said, "Maybe it can unlock a secret door or a treasure box. Let's go and find out." They ran around the house, trying the key on different doors and boxes. But none of them opened. They came to the last door in the hall. It was dark and scary. Tom said, "Maybe this is the one. Let's try it." Lily said, "I don't know, Tom. It looks dangerous. Maybe we should go back." Tom said, "Don't be afraid, Lily. I'm your friend. I'll protect you. Trust me." He put the key in the lock and turned it. The door opened with a loud creak. They stepped inside the dark room. They saw a big circle on the floor. It had strange symbols and candles around it. In the middle of the circle, there was a big book. Tom said, "Wow, this is cool. Maybe it's a magic book. Let's read it." Lily said, "No, Tom. This is bad. This is not a game. This is a trap. Let's get out of here." But Tom did not listen. He walked to the book and opened it. As soon as he did, a loud voice boomed from the book. It said, "You have unlocked the circle of doom. You have freed me from my prison. Now you will pay the price. You will be my slaves forever." Tom and Lily screamed. They tried to run, but it was too late. The circle glowed and trapped them inside. The door slammed shut and locked itself. No one heard them cry. No one came to help them. They were stuck in the dark room with the evil voice. They wished they had never found the key. They wished they had listened to Lily. They learned that Tom was not a reliable friend. He was a foolish one. And they paid for his mistake. The end.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing outside. He was having fun with his toy car. Suddenly, he tripped and fell. His knee hurt and he saw a small cut. He started to cry. His mom heard him and came running. "Tim, what happened?" she asked. Tim showed her his knee. "Oh no, you need a bandage," she said. She went inside the house and came back with a bandage. She put it on Tim's knee. "Now, you will become better," she told him. Just then, they heard a loud noise. They looked up and saw a big, colorful bird in the tree. The bird was singing a happy song. Tim stopped crying and started to laugh. The bird flew down and landed on Tim's shoulder. The bird had a bandage on its wing too! Tim and the bird became best friends and played together all day.

John was a happy boy. He liked to play. One day, he saw something amazing. It was a van outside his house! John ran to the van and his eyes lit up with joy. He asked his mom, "Can I hang out with the van?" His mom said, "Yes!" John was so excited. He ran outside and jumped into the van. He looked around and saw so many things - like fun buttons to press, colorful lights and soft seats. He clapped his hands in delight. John had a lot of fun riding in the van. He cheerfully waved goodbye to the van when his mom said it was time to go. He knew the van would come back tomorrow, and he could hang out with it again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a deaf dog named Max. Max could not hear anything, but he was very good at smelling and seeing things. One day, Max was walking in the park with his friend, a little girl named Lily. They saw a big tree with many pieces of paper on the ground. "What is this?" asked Lily. Max sniffed the pieces and managed to find a note that said, "Find all the pieces and put them together to see a surprise." Lily and Max worked together to find all the pieces. Max used his nose to smell the pieces and Lily used her hands to put them together. After a while, they managed to find all the pieces and put them together to make a big picture. The picture was of a beautiful rainbow with a message that said, "You did a great job! Now go and enjoy the park!" Lily and Max were very happy and proud of themselves. They spent the rest of the day playing and having fun in the park. And from that day on, Max and Lily knew that even if Max was deaf, they could still manage to do anything together.
