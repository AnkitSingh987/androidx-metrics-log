Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben and the Necklace

Anna and Ben went to the mall with Mom. They liked to see the big lights and the many people. They saw a store with shiny things. Mom said they were jewels. Anna and Ben wanted to look at them. They went inside the store and saw a lady with a gentle smile. She said hello and asked if they wanted to see the jewels. Anna and Ben nodded. The lady showed them a necklace with a blue stone. She said it was a sapphire. It shone like the sky. Anna and Ben touched the necklace. It was cold and smooth. They liked how it sparkled. The lady said they could try it on. She put it around Anna's neck. Anna looked in the mirror and smiled. She felt like a princess. Ben wanted to try it too. He asked the lady if he could. The lady said yes and took the necklace from Anna. She put it around Ben's neck. Ben looked in the mirror and laughed. He felt like a king. Mom came and saw them. She said they looked very pretty. She thanked the lady and said they had to go. The lady said goodbye and took the necklace back. She put it in a box. Anna and Ben waved to her and followed Mom. They had fun at the mall.

One day, a sweet cat named Mimi went for a walk. She saw a big boot on the ground. Mimi thought it was a fun toy, so she decided to steal it. She took the boot in her mouth and ran back to her house. When Mimi got home, she showed the boot to her friend, a dog named Ben. "Look what I found!" said Mimi. Ben looked at the boot and said, "That's not a toy, Mimi. That's a boot! Someone must have lost it." Just then, they heard a knock at the door. It was a sad little boy named Tim. He was looking for his lost boot. Mimi felt bad for stealing the boot, so she gave it back to Tim. "I'm sorry I took your boot," said Mimi. Tim smiled and said, "That's okay, Mimi. Thank you for giving it back." Mimi and Ben learned that it's not nice to steal. They all became friends and played together every day. And from that day on, Mimi never took things that didn't belong to her.

Once upon a time there was a very loud pilot. He loved to fly around in his plane and take trips to explore the world. One day, the pilot decided to invite his friends along for an exciting ride. He called up all of his closest buddies and asked them to join him. One of his friends, a 3 year old child, was really excited for the trip. He loved flying around in the plane with the pilot. On the day of the trip the pilot was getting ready to board the plane. He said to the 3 year old child, "Are you ready to go on this trip with me?" The child was so excited that he shouted back loudly, "Yes! Let's go!" The pilot smiled and said, "Okay, let's go!" He then boarded the plane and they flew off into the sky. The 3 year old had the best time on the trip and felt like he was flying with a superhero. He was thankful to the pilot for inviting him on the trip. The End.

Once upon a time, there was a farmer named Ben. Ben had a big problem and needed to solve it. Everyday, Ben's bull was getting out of the pen and running around the farm. It was making a big mess and Ben was getting frustrated. One day, Ben was walking around the farm when he saw the bull in the corner. The bull saw Ben and started running towards him. Ben ran inside to the barn where he screamed for help. Just before the bull reached the door, a voice came out from inside the barn. It was Ben's neighbour, Jack. He said, "I'll help you solve your problem, Ben." Jack took out a gun and shot the bull. Ben gasped in horror. The bull fell down dead! "See, Ben? Now your problem is solved," said Jack. Ben was very grateful to Jack and gave him a big hug. From that day on, Ben never had any more problems with the bull escaping again.
