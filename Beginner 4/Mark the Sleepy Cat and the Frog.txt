Category: Beginner
Title: Mark the Sleepy Cat and the Frog

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy cat named Mark. Mark loved to eat blueberries. One sunny day, he went to the big tree to find some. As Mark picked the blueberries, he saw something big and green behind the tree. It was a frog! The frog was sad because he lost his way home. Mark wanted to help his new friend. Together, Mark and the frog looked for the frog's home. They found it near a big pond. The frog was happy and gave Mark a big hug. Then, the frog jumped into the pond and turned into a beautiful fish! Mark was surprised but happy for his friend. From that day on, Mark and the fish played together every day.

Once upon a time there was a lazy little bear. He liked to lie under a big tree and take nap. One day as he was lying there, he started to think. He thought about how he wished he could go to the store and buy some honey to eat. But he was too lazy to get up and go. Just then a little rabbit hopped by. The bear said, "Hey Rabbit, do you mind if I come with you to the store?" The rabbit said, "Sure! I was just heading there now". The bear smiled and said, "Thank you. That would be so nice." The two friends hopped their way to the store. They got some honey and then hopped their way back to the tree again. The bear smiled with joy as he ate the delicious honey. He thanked the rabbit for taking him and promised to come up with more ideas next time. The rabbit laughed with joy. They both agreed that it had been an amazing adventure.

One morning, Mummy and Baby Bear woke up and it was time for breakfast. Mummy Bear switched on the light and saw that Baby Bear was still sleepy, so she gave him a mild cuddle to wake him up. Mummy Bear said, “I think it’s time for breakfast, Baby Bear. Let’s go have something yummy.” Baby Bear rubbed his eyes and said, “OK! Let’s go eat!” So Mummy Bear and Baby Bear went downstairs to have breakfast. Baby Bear saw a big switch on the wall and asked, “What’s that thing, Mummy?” Mummy Bear replied, “That’s a switch. You can press it and the lights will turn on.” Baby Bear got excited and pressed the switch. The lights turned on and Baby Bear was thrilled! Now they could all have breakfast in the bright and mild morning.

Once upon a time, in a little town, there lived a bunny named Bob. Bob loved to hop all day long. He hopped to his neighbor's house, he hopped to the park, and he hopped to the store. Bob was very popular because he was always happy and hopping. One day, Bob saw a big hill. He thought it would be fun to hop up the hill. So, he started hopping. But the hill was very steep, and it was hard for Bob to hop up. He hopped and hopped, but he couldn't make it to the top. Bob was sad. The next day, Bob's neighbor, a wise old owl, saw him trying to hop up the hill again. The owl told Bob, "If you keep trying, you will make it to the top." Bob listened to the wise owl and kept hopping. After many tries, Bob finally made it to the top of the hill. He was so happy! All his friends in the town cheered for him. Bob knew that if he never gave up, he could do anything.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree. The tree had many pears. One day, a little girl saw a very nice pear on the tree. She wanted to eat it. But the girl was too tired to reach the pear. A kind bird saw the tired girl. The bird wanted to help her. The bird flew up to the tree. It picked the nice pear with its beak. The bird brought the pear down to the girl. The girl was very happy. She said, "Thank you, bird!" The bird felt good. It wanted to protect the girl. So, the bird stayed with the girl. They became good friends. The girl and the bird ate the nice pear together.
