Category: Beginner
Title: Thomas and Lisa and the Park

One day, two famous dinosaurs, Thomas and Lisa, headed to the park to play. On their way, they spotted a broken wire on the ground. Thomas, who was very curious, decided to pick it up. “What’s this?” Lisa asked. “It’s a wire,” Thomas replied. “What’s it for?” Thomas shrugged. They decided to leave it on the ground since it was broken. When they finished playing at the park, they noticed the wire was still there. Thomas felt bad that he didn’t clean up. “Thomas, no one is looking,” said Lisa. “Why don’t you pick it up?” Thomas knew he was right. He picked up the wire and put it into the trash can. The moral of the story is that even if no one can see us, we should always act responsibly and take care of our environment.

One day, a little cat named Tim was in his home. He was very tired after playing all day. He wanted to relax, so he looked for a good place to rest. He saw the light coming from the top of the stairs. Tim thought that maybe there was a nice place to relax up there. Tim began to climb the stairs. He went up one stair, then another, and another. The light grew brighter as he went up. He thought about how nice it would be to relax in the warm light. Tim was excited to see what was up there. When Tim reached the top of the stairs, he found a big, soft bed. The light was shining on the bed, making it warm and cozy. Tim was so happy that he had climbed the stairs. He jumped onto the bed and lay down. It was the perfect place to relax. Tim smiled, closed his eyes, and took a long nap in the warm light.

Once upon a time, there was a baby. The baby had a toy. The toy was clean and fun to play with. The baby liked to chew on the toy. One day, the baby played with the toy outside. The baby saw a big dog. The dog came close and looked at the toy. The baby was scared. But then, the dog did something unexpected. The dog took the toy and started to chew on it too! The baby laughed and laughed. The dog and the baby played together and became friends.

One day, a fearful boy named Tim went to school. He was scared because he did not know his numbers. In class, his teacher, Miss Jane, asked everyone to say a number. Tim felt his heart beat fast as it was almost his turn. When it was Tim's turn, he said "I don't know any numbers." Miss Jane smiled and said, "That's okay, Tim. We will learn them together." Tim felt a little better, but he was still scared. As they learned numbers, something unexpected happened. Tim found out that he was very good at counting. He could count higher than anyone else in the class. Miss Jane said, "Tim, you have a special value for numbers." Tim felt happy and was not fearful anymore.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to go to the store with Mom. Mom buys them toys and snacks. Anna and Ben are happy. One day, they go to the store again. Anna sees a big doll. She wants it. She asks Mom. Mom says no. Anna is sad. She cries. She runs away from Mom. She does not look where she is going. Ben sees a ball. He wants it. He asks Mom. Mom says yes. Ben is happy. He says thank you. He stays with Mom. He looks where he is going. Anna runs into a shelf. She falls down. She hurts her knee. She cries louder. Mom hears her. Mom runs to her. Mom hugs her. Mom says sorry. Mom says be careful. Mom says listen to Mom. Ben sees Anna. He feels sorry for her. He runs to her. He gives her his ball. He says share. He says be happy. Anna stops crying. She hugs Ben. She says thank you. She says sorry. She says love Ben. Mom sees Anna and Ben. She smiles. She says good twins. She says love Anna and Ben. She buys them both the doll and the ball. Anna and Ben are happy. They hug Mom. They say love Mom. The moral of the story is: listen to your parents, be careful, and share with your siblings.
