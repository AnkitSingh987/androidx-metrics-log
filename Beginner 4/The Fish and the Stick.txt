Category: Beginner
Title: The Fish and the Stick

Once upon a time, there was a little fish. The fish lived in a big pond. The fish liked to swim and play with his friends all day. One day, the fish saw a big stick in the water. The fish wanted to break the stick. He swam fast and hit the stick with his tail. The stick broke into two pieces. The fish was happy. The fish was alert and saw a big bird in the sky. The bird wanted to catch the fish. The fish swam fast and hid under a rock. The bird went away. The fish was safe and continued to play with his friends.

One day, a great big cat named Tom found a toy laser. He loved to play with the laser. Tom would march around the house, chasing the little red dot. He was very happy. Tom's friend, a little dog named Max, saw the laser too. Max wanted to play with the laser. So, Tom and Max started to march together. They chased the red dot all around the house. They had so much fun. At the end of the day, Tom and Max were tired. They had a great time playing with the laser. They knew they would play with the laser again soon. And they would march and chase the red dot together, as great friends do.

Once upon a time, there were two foxes, Red and Giddy. Red was clever and Giddy was energetic. One day, they had a plan. They'd find the spiciest food possible and lay it on the ground as a symbol of friendship. So, Red went searching for the best spicy food he could find. After a little while, he came back with a sack of peppers. Giddy was so surprised, he could hardly contain himself! But before he could say anything, Red lay the sack of peppers down on the ground and both foxes backed away. They waited to see what would happen. After a few minutes, a small rabbit hopped along. He caught a whiff of the spicy food and came to investigate. Then, much to their surprise, the rabbit started to share the peppers with his friends. Red and Giddy smiled with joy. They had not only fed the rabbit, but also helped create a symbol of friendship between him and the other animals. They had learned that when you share with others, it can make the world a better place.

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to slide, swing and run. They saw a big hill with many rocks and trees. Tom said, "Let's go up the hill and see what is there." Lily said, "No, it is too high and scary. Mom said we should stay in the park." Tom said, "You are a baby. I dare you to go with me. It will be fun." Lily did not want to be a baby. She wanted to be brave. She said, "OK, I will go with you. But we have to be careful." They climbed the hill, holding hands. They saw many bugs, flowers and birds. They also saw a small house with a fence and a sign. The sign said, "Do not enter. Danger." Tom said, "Look, a house. Let's go inside and see who lives there." Lily said, "No, the sign says no. It is bad to go inside. Maybe there is a monster or a witch." Tom said, "You are a chicken. I dare you to go with me. It will be fun." Lily did not want to be a chicken. She wanted to be curious. She said, "OK, I will go with you. But we have to be quiet." They opened the gate and walked to the house. They saw a window with a curtain. They peeked inside and saw a man lying on a bed. He had many tubes and wires on his body. He looked very sick and sad. He also had a big machine next to him that made a beep sound. Tom said, "Look, a man. Let's go inside and say hello." Lily said, "No, the man is sick. He needs to rest. We should not bother him. Maybe he is a patient." Tom said, "You are a mouse. I dare you to go with me. It will be fun." Lily did not want to be a mouse. She wanted to be friendly. She said, "OK, I will go with you. But we have to be nice." They opened the door and walked inside. The man heard them and opened his eyes. He saw two children standing next to his bed. He smiled and said, "Hello, who are you?" Tom said, "We are Tom and Lily. We are playing in the park. We saw your house and wanted to see who lives here." The man said, "I am Sam. I live here. I am sick and I cannot go out. I have a machine that helps me breathe. Thank you for coming to see me. I am very lonely." Lily said, "We are sorry you are sick. We hope you get better soon. You are brave and strong." The man said, "You are very kind and sweet. You made me happy. You are welcome to come again." Tom said, "We are glad you are happy. We will come again. We are your friends." The man said, "You are my friends. You are the best thing that happened to me today." They hugged the man and said goodbye. They walked out of the house and closed the door. They climbed down the hill and went back to the park. They felt good and proud. They learned something new and met someone special. They realized that sometimes it is good to dare, but it is also good to be patient and normal. They played some more and waited for their mom to pick them up. They could not wait to tell her about their adventure.
