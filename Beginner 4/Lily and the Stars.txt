Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Stars

Lily was upset. She did not want to go to bed. She wanted to play with her toys and watch TV. She tried to resist when Mom came to take her to her room. She kicked and screamed and cried. Mom was patient. She hugged Lily and said, "I know you are upset, but it is time to sleep. You need to rest your body and your mind. Tomorrow you can play and watch TV again." She carried Lily to her room and put her on her bed. Lily still did not want to sleep. She got up and ran to the door. Mom caught her and said, "No, Lily, you have to stay in your bed. I will read you a story and sing you a song, but then you have to close your eyes and sleep." She took Lily back to her bed and tucked her in. Lily liked stories and songs. She listened to Mom read her a book about a princess and a dragon. She liked the princess and the dragon. They were friends and had fun. Mom sang her a song about the stars and the moon. She liked the stars and the moon. They were bright and beautiful. Lily felt sleepy. She yawned and rubbed her eyes. She cuddled with her teddy bear and said, "I love you, Mom." Mom smiled and said, "I love you, too, Lily. Good night, sweetie." She kissed Lily on the forehead and turned off the light. She left the door open a little bit and said, "I will be right here if you need me." Lily closed her eyes and fell asleep. She dreamed of the princess and the dragon, the stars and the moon, and Mom and teddy. She was not upset anymore. She was happy and peaceful.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Milly. Milly loved to relax. Every night when it was dark, she would lie in her modern bed and close her eyes. The night would be quiet and she would have time to relax. When she relaxed, she would drift away to amazing places. In these places she would have wonderful adventures! She would laugh, dance and play. She would explore the beautiful world around her. She would get up high to look down at the clouds and sometimes even visit the stars. It was always so much fun. When the sun would come up, Milly would feel very happy. She would jump out of bed and be ready for a new day. It was never boring with Milly around!

Once upon a time, in a normal little town, there was a lot of fog. The fog was so thick that it was hard to see. In this town lived a boy named Tom and his dog, Max. They liked to play outside, but today, they could not see where to go. Tom and Max tried to guess where the park was. They walked and walked, but they could not find it. They were lost in the fog. Tom was sad, and Max was too. Then, a big wind blew the fog away. Tom and Max could see again. They found the park and played all day. They were happy and tired when they went home. The fog did not come back, and they played outside every day.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a noisy dog named Spot. Spot loved to play in the grass all day. One day, while playing, he found a big ball. Spot wanted to play with the ball, but it was too big for him. He asked his friend, a small cat named Fluffy, to help him. Fluffy was scared of the big ball, but Spot told her not to be scared. He said, "We can have fun together." So, Fluffy and Spot played with the big ball together. They had a lot of fun in the grass. They learned to accept each other's help and became best friends. They knew that friends help each other when they need it. And that is the moral of the story: always be there to help your friends.

One day, a little bird named Tim was scared. He was on a wire high up in the sky. He did not want to fly because he was scared to fall. He saw his friend, Sam the squirrel, on a tree near the wire. "Sam, help me! I am scared to fly," Tim said. Sam thought for a moment and then said, "Don't worry, Tim. Just remain on the wire and I will come to help you." Sam climbed the tree and jumped from a branch to the wire. He held Tim's wing and said, "Now, we will fly together. You don't have to be scared." With Sam by his side, Tim was not scared anymore. They both flew down from the wire and landed safely on the ground. Tim was so happy that he had a friend like Sam to help him when he was scared.
