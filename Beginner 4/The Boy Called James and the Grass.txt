Category: Beginner
Title: The Boy Called James and the Grass

Once upon a time there was a little boy called James. He was only three years old. One day, he and his mum were taking a walk in the park. As they walked, James noticed the bright green grass. It waved in the wind, like a bright green sea. He asked his mum, "What is that?" Mum replied, "That's the grass, dear." James started to skip along and when he reached the grass, he shouted out, "Hi grass!" Mum laughed and said, "James, that's very nice, but you mustn't be too rude." James nodded and said, "I will remember." Mum smiled and said, "I'm glad that you remembered. Let's keep walking." So James and his mum kept walking, but every now and then, Mum would remind James not to be rude. And so, James remembered, and he always showed the grass respect.

Lily and Max are at the zoo with their mom. They like to see the animals and learn their names. They see lions, monkeys, elephants, and more. They have fun and eat ice cream. But when they go to the bird house, Lily and Max have a problem. Lily wants to see the parrots, but Max wants to see the owls. They both pull their mom in different directions. Mom says they have to take turns and share. Lily goes first and sees the parrots. They are colorful and noisy. They say hello and bye-bye. Lily laughs and tries to talk to them. Max is bored and stares at the exit. He wants to see the owls. They are light and quiet. They have big eyes and sharp claws. Max thinks they are cool and scary. Mom says it is time to switch and see the owls. Lily is sad and cries. She wants to stay with the parrots. She does not like the owls. They are too dark and silent. She thinks they are mean and spooky. Mom tries to calm Lily and make her go with Max. But Lily does not listen and runs away. She goes back to the parrots and hugs the cage. Mom and Max chase after her and try to catch her. But Lily is fast and sneaky. The zookeeper sees Lily and gets angry. He says she is not allowed to touch the birds. He says she has to leave the bird house. He points to the exit and tells her to go. Lily is scared and sorry. She lets go of the cage and runs to her mom. She says she is sorry and hugs her mom. Mom says it is okay and hugs her back. Max says it is okay and hugs her too. They all say sorry to the zookeeper and leave the bird house. They go to see the other animals and have fun. They learn to take turns and share. They do not stare at the exit anymore. They enjoy the zoo.

Once upon a time, there was a little bug named Billy. Billy liked to zoom around the garden. One day, he saw a big rock with a funny texture. He thought it was harmless, so he played on it. Billy had a friend named Sally. Sally was a tiny bug who liked to play with Billy. Sally saw Billy on the rock and wanted to join him. She zoomed to the rock as fast as she could. When Sally got to the rock, it started to move! The rock was not a rock, but a big turtle! The turtle was nice and did not hurt the bugs. Billy and Sally had a fun ride on the turtle's back. They learned that even things that look scary can be harmless and fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big hairy bear. The bear loved to play in the woods. One day, the bear found a long rail to walk on. As the bear walked on the rail, he saw a big stick. The bear wanted to play with the stick. He picked it up and tried to break it. The bear broke the stick into two pieces. He was very happy. He played with the two pieces all day long. At the end of the day, the bear went back to his home in the woods.
