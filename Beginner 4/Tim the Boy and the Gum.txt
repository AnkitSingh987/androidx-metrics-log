Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Gum

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to organize his toys. He had many toys, but his favorite one was a big, fake dinosaur. The dinosaur was green, and it was very big. One day, while Tim was organizing his toys, he found a colorful gum under his bed. He was very happy to find it, as it was his favorite gum. He took the gum and went to show it to his mom. Tim's mom said, "That's a very old gum, Tim. You should throw it away and get a new one." Tim agreed and put the gum in the trash. Then, he and his mom went to the store to buy a new gum. Tim was very happy that day, because he got a new gum and his toys were all organized.

---

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw an old man sitting on a bench. The old man looked sad. Tim walked up to the old man and asked, "Why do you cry?" The old man said, "I lost my hat. It was my favorite hat." Tim wanted to help, so he held the old man's hand and they looked for the hat together. They looked near the trees, by the swings, and even in the sandbox. But they could not find the hat. Just when they were about to give up, a big wind came. It blew a hat right to Tim's feet! It was the old man's hat! The old man smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim!" They were both very happy and the old man did not cry anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to paint. One day, Lily was very hungry. She thought, "I want to make a yummy snack!" So, she went to the kitchen. In the kitchen, Lily saw the oven. She knew that the oven could make food hot and tasty. She asked her mom, "Can I use the oven to make a snack?" Her mom said, "Yes, but I will help you. The oven can be hot." Lily and her mom made a fun snack together. They put cheese on bread and put it in the oven. When it was done, the cheese was all melty and gooey. Lily was so happy. She ate her snack and thought, "I will paint a picture of this yummy snack later!" And that is what she did.

Once upon a time, there was a weak cat named Tom. Tom wanted to gain strength, so he went on a tour around the town to find food. He walked and walked, and soon he found a big house with a big yard. In the yard, Tom saw a little girl named Sue. Sue said, "Hi, cat! Do you want to come in and eat?" Tom was happy and went inside. Sue gave Tom food and they became friends. Tom started to gain strength and didn't feel weak anymore. One day, while they were playing, a big wind came and blew Sue's hat away. Tom ran and jumped high to catch the hat. Sue was surprised and said, "Wow, Tom! You are not weak anymore!" Tom was proud of his new strength and knew that his tour for food had helped him become strong.

Once upon a time, there was a big, wide tree. The tree was very happy because it was a perfect day. The sun was shining, and the sky was blue. The tree had many friends, like the birds and the squirrels. One day, a little girl came to the tree. She wanted to put on a show for the birds and the squirrels. The little girl danced and sang under the tree. The birds and the squirrels watched her and were very happy. When the little girl finished her show, the birds and the squirrels started to applaud. They clapped their wings and paws. The little girl was so happy that she did a perfect dance again. The big, wide tree was very proud of the little girl and her new friends. And they all lived happily ever after.
