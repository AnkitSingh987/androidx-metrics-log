Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his uncle. His uncle was very big and strong. They liked to play with a heavy ball. One day, it was very hot outside. The sun was shining, and Tim felt like he was going to melt. Tim and his uncle decided to play in the shade under a big tree. The tree had many leaves, and they were happy to be out of the hot sun. After playing for a while, Tim and his uncle got tired. They sat down under the tree and drank cold water. The heavy ball was left in the sun, and it felt like it was melting too. Tim and his uncle laughed, and they were happy to be together.

Sam and Mia are twins. They like to play in their room. They have many toys and books and clothes. But their room is very messy. Mommy is not happy. "Sam and Mia, you need to organize your room now!" Mommy says. "It is time to clean up. Put your toys in the box, your books on the shelf, and your clothes in the drawer." Sam and Mia nod. They start to organize their room. Sam picks up his cars and trucks and puts them in the box. Mia picks up her dolls and animals and puts them in the box. They work fast and have fun. But then Mia sees something on the floor. It is a bottle of lotion. Mommy uses lotion to make her skin soft. Mia likes lotion too. She opens the bottle and squeezes some lotion on her hand. She rubs it on her face and arms and legs. "Look, Sam, I have lotion!" Mia says. "Do you want some?" Sam looks at Mia. She has lotion all over her. She looks funny. Sam laughs. "No, thank you, Mia. I don't want lotion. I want to organize the room." Sam says. But Mia does not listen. She runs to Sam and tries to put lotion on him. Sam does not like that. He pushes Mia away. "Stop, Mia, stop!" Sam says. "You are making a mess. Mommy will be mad." Mia does not stop. She chases Sam around the room. She drops the bottle of lotion. Lotion spills on the floor and on the bed and on the toys. The room is very messy again. Mommy hears the noise. She comes to the room. She sees the lotion everywhere. She sees Sam and Mia. She is very mad. "Sam and Mia, what are you doing?" Mommy says. "You did not organize your room. You made it worse. You wasted the lotion. The lotion is dead. It is no good now. You are in big trouble." Sam and Mia feel bad. They say sorry to Mommy. They help Mommy clean the lotion. They organize their room again. They promise to be good. They hug Mommy. Mommy hugs them back. She is not mad anymore. She loves them.

Once upon a time, there was a creative little girl named Lily. She loved to draw, paint, and make things with her hands. Lily had a big day coming up - her birthday! On her birthday, Lily woke up and saw a big box. She couldn't believe it! She opened the box and found lots of colors, paper, and fun things to make art with. Lily was so happy and said, "Thank you, Mom and Dad!" Lily spent her birthday making pretty pictures for her friends and family. She had a great day, and her creative art made everyone smile.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. She loved to create things with her toys. One day, she made a big tower with her blocks. Jane was very happy with her tower. Soon, a little boy named Tom came to play with Jane. He saw the tower and said, "Wow, Jane! You create a great tower. But watch out, there might be a threat!" Jane asked, "What is a threat, Tom?" Tom said, "A threat is something that can make your tower fall down." Just then, a little cat walked into the room. The cat saw the tower and wanted to play too. The cat jumped and knocked down the tower. Jane and Tom looked at the cat and said, "Oh no, the cat was the threat!" They laughed and started to create a new tower together.
