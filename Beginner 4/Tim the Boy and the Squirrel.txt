Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Squirrel

One day, a boy named Tim found a long spear in the woods. He picked it up and played with it. He thought it was fun to have a spear. Tim saw a big tree with an empty hole. He thought, "I can put my spear in the hole!" Tim went to the tree and tried to put the spear in the hole. But a squirrel came out and said, "Stop! You can't put your spear in my home!" Tim was sad, but he didn't want to make the squirrel mad. The squirrel said, "If you want to put your spear in the hole, you need to pay me with nuts." Tim looked for nuts to give to the squirrel. He found a big pile of nuts and gave them to the squirrel. The squirrel was happy and let Tim put the spear in the hole. But when Tim put the spear in the hole, he heard a loud noise. The tree started to shake and the ground opened up. Tim and the squirrel fell into a hidden room under the tree! In the hidden room, there were more empty holes and lots of spears. Tim and the squirrel became friends and played with the spears together. They were happy they found a secret place to play.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Sally. She was only three years old, and loved playing with her toys. One day, she was playing in her room with her dolls when she heard a noise. It was a yummy noise! Sally was intrigued and decided to take a look. Sally walked over to the kitchen, and there she saw her mum washing dishes in the sink. She ran up to her mum and asked, “What’s that noise, mummy? Is it yummy?” Mum smiled and said, “Yes, Sally. That’s the sound of the washing machine. Everything in the washing machine gets nice and clean and smells yummy too!” Sally smiled and said, “Can I help, mummy?” Mum laughed and said, “Of course. Come and help me wash these dishes.” So Sally and her mum washed the dishes together, and the yummy scent filled the air. When they were finished, Sally went back to her toys, pleased that she had helped her mummy.

Lily and Tom were best friends. They liked to play in the park and share their toys. One day, they found a big box under a tree. It had a lock on it, but no key. "Wow, what is in the box?" Lily asked. "I don't know, maybe a treasure or a toy," Tom said. They tried to open the box, but it was too hard. They looked around for a key, but they could not find one. "Maybe someone knows the secret to open the box," Lily said. "Let's ask the old man who sits on the bench. He knows many things," Tom said. They ran to the old man, who was reading a book. He smiled when he saw them. "Hello, Lily and Tom. What can I do for you?" he asked. "Hello, sir. We found a big box under the tree, but we can't open it. Do you know the secret?" Lily asked. The old man looked at the box and nodded. "Yes, I do. But it's not easy. You have to say the magic word," he said. "What is the magic word?" Tom asked. The old man whispered in their ears. "The magic word is... name. You have to say your name three times, and the box will open," he said. Lily and Tom looked at each other. They thought it was a strange magic word, but they wanted to see what was in the box. They ran back to the box and said their names three times. "Lily, Lily, Lily!" "Tom, Tom, Tom!" The box clicked and opened. Inside, they saw a pile of books, toys, and candy. "Wow, look at all these things!" Lily said. "They are for you, my dear children. They are my gifts to you, because you are so kind and curious," the old man said, walking behind them. Lily and Tom turned around and hugged the old man. "Thank you, sir. You are so nice and generous," they said. The old man smiled and winked. "You're welcome, my dear children. And remember, the magic word is name. It can open many doors and hearts," he said. Lily and Tom nodded and smiled. They learned a new secret and made a new friend. They played with their gifts and shared them with each other. They had a very happy day.
