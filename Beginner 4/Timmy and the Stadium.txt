Category: Beginner
Title: Timmy and the Stadium

One day, Timmy and his family went to a stadium. It was so big that Timmy felt very tiny. Everything around him was so tall and he felt like he was balancing when he walked. Inside, everything looked dull and grey. Timmy did not like looking at the stadium and he got bored. He asked his mom if he could go explore the stadium. She said he could, as long as he stayed close. Timmy got so excited. He went around the stadium and saw all kinds of people. He saw some playing football and some running very fast in a race. He saw a man balance on a log, and he waved to Timmy. Even though the stadium was dull, it was exciting and Timmy had a great time!

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk with her mom. They saw a smooth rock on the ground. Mia picked it up and said, "Look, Mom! A smooth rock!" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, Mia, it's very smooth." As they walked, Mia saw a green plant with sharp points. She asked her mom, "What's that?" Her mom said, "That's a cactus, Mia. Be careful, it's not smooth like the rock. It can hurt you." Mia wanted to see the cactus closer, so she went to peek at it. Her mom told her, "Don't touch it, Mia. It's not safe." But Mia didn't listen. She reached out to touch the cactus. Suddenly, Mia cried out in pain. The cactus had hurt her. Her mom ran to her and said, "I told you not to touch it, Mia. Let's go home and make it better." Mia was sad that she didn't listen to her mom, and she learned to be more careful around things that can hurt her.

One day, a humble cat and a happy dog went to the market. They wanted to unite and become friends. They walked side by side, looking at all the things in the market. "Look, cat! Yummy food!" said the dog. "Yes, dog! Pretty toys!" said the cat. They were having lots of fun together. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew everything away. The cat and dog were sad. They thought their fun day was over. But then, they saw a little bird. The bird said, "Don't be sad, friends! I can help you find your things!" So, the cat, dog, and bird united to look for the things. Together, they found the food and toys. They were happy again. The cat, dog, and bird became the best of friends. They learned that when they unite, they can do anything.

Tom and Anna are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, Tom has a good idea. He says to Anna, "Let's invite Sam and Lily to play with us. We can have a picnic in the park. I have a basket with some food and drinks. Do you want to come?" Anna nods and smiles. She likes Sam and Lily. They are nice and funny. She says to Tom, "Yes, I want to come. That sounds fun. What food and drinks do you have in your basket?" Tom opens his basket and shows Anna. He has some sandwiches, some apples, some cookies, and some juice. He says to Anna, "You can have some of everything. But we have to share with Sam and Lily. They will be here soon. Let's find a good spot to sit and eat." They walk around the park and look for a good spot. They see a big tree with some shade and some grass. They think it is perfect. They put the basket under the tree and spread a blanket on the grass. They sit on the blanket and wait for Sam and Lily. Soon, they see Sam and Lily running towards them. They wave and shout, "Hi, Tom and Anna. We are here. Thank you for inviting us. What a nice picnic you have." Tom and Anna welcome them and say, "Hi, Sam and Lily. We are happy you are here. You can have some of our food and drinks. But we have to be tidy. We don't want to make a mess in the park." Sam and Lily agree and say, "OK, we will be tidy. We don't want to make a mess either. The park is beautiful and clean. We want to keep it that way." They all sit on the blanket and enjoy the picnic. They eat, drink, talk, and laugh. They have a lot of fun. They are good friends. They are happy.
