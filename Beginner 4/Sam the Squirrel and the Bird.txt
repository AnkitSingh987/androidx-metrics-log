Category: Beginner
Title: Sam the Squirrel and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a squirrel named Sam. Sam loved to eat delicious nuts. One sunny day, Sam went to look for nuts in the forest. Sam saw a big nut on the ground. He was very happy. Sam said, "I want to eat this delicious nut!" But the nut was very hard. Sam tried to snap it open with his teeth, but he could not do it. A little bird saw Sam and said, "Sam, don't be sad. I can help you snap the nut." The bird flew down and helped Sam snap the nut open. Sam was so happy and said, "Thank you, little bird!" The moral of the story is: when you help others, you make them happy.

Once, a boy named Tim had a basketball. He loved to play with it every day. One day, Tim saw a dead tree in the park. He wanted to play, but the dead tree was in the way. Tim tried to move the tree, but it was too big. He felt sad. Tim had an idea. He would ask his friends to help. His friends came and they all pushed the tree together. The tree fell down and Tim was happy. Now, Tim and his friends could play basketball. They played all day long. Tim learned that when they work together, they can do anything. The basketball and the park belonged to all of them, and they had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy vehicle that he loved to play with. He would push it around and make funny noises. One day, Tim saw a big, yummy cake on the table. He wanted to eat it, but he knew he had to wait. Tim had an idea. He thought if he could use his vehicle to reach the cake, he could take a little bite. So, he put his vehicle on the floor and stood on it. He tried to get closer to the cake, but the vehicle started to wobble. Suddenly, the vehicle went "crack" and broke into pieces. Tim fell down and started to cry. The cake fell off the table too and got all dirty. Tim didn't get to eat the yummy cake, and his favorite toy vehicle was broken. He was very sad and learned that he should always ask for help instead of trying to do things on his own.

Lily and Sam went to the museum with their mom. They liked to see the big animals and the shiny rocks. But they did not like the dark room with the old bones. It was scary. "Mom, can we go to another room?" Lily asked. She held her mom's hand tight. "Sure, honey. Let's go see the paintings. They are very pretty and colorful," Mom said. She smiled and led them to the next room. But when they got there, they saw a big sign that said "Closed for cleaning". The door was locked and there was a cloth that covered the paintings. "Oh, no. We can't see the paintings today," Mom said. She looked sad. Lily and Sam felt sad too. They wanted to see the paintings. They wondered what they looked like under the cloth. "Maybe we can peek a little," Sam said. He was curious and brave. He walked to the door and tried to lift the cloth. But as soon as he touched the cloth, a loud alarm went off. It made a scary noise that hurt their ears. Lily and Sam were fearful. They thought they did something bad. "Sam, what did you do?" Mom asked. She ran to the door and pulled Sam away. She looked worried. "I'm sorry, Mom. I just wanted to see the paintings," Sam said. He started to cry. "It's okay, Sam. It was an accident. You didn't mean to cause trouble," Mom said. She hugged Sam and Lily. She tried to calm them down. Soon, a man came to the door. He wore a badge and a hat. He looked angry. "Who set off the alarm? Who touched the cloth?" He asked. He looked at Mom and the kids. Mom explained what happened. She said they were sorry and they didn't know the alarm was there. She asked the man to forgive them. The man listened to Mom. He saw that they were scared and sorry. He felt sorry for them too. He turned off the alarm and smiled. He said it was okay and he understood. He said he worked at the museum and he loved the paintings too. He said he would show them the paintings if they promised not to touch them again. Mom and the kids thanked the man. They promised not to touch the paintings again. They followed the man to the door. He lifted the cloth and showed them the paintings. Lily and Sam were happy. They saw the paintings and they were very pretty and colorful. They forgot about the scary alarm and the dark room. They learned something new and had fun at the museum.
