Category: Beginner
Title: The Flood and the Rainbow

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big flood. The flood made everything very wet. The people in the town were sad because they could not play outside. They had to stay inside their homes. One day, a kind girl named Lily had an idea. She wanted to separate the wet water from the town. Lily asked her friends to help her. They all worked together to dig a big hole. The wet water went into the hole, and the town started to become dry. But then, something unexpected happened. As the water went into the hole, a big rainbow appeared in the sky. The people in the town were surprised and happy. They all went outside to play and dance under the rainbow. The flood had brought them a beautiful gift, and they were thankful for the colorful surprise.

One day, Sam was playing with his toys when he heard a beep. It was his dad's computer. The computer was alert and had a message on the screen. Sam was curious, so he went to look at the computer. Sam saw that the message was from his friend, Max. Max wanted to play at the park. Sam called out, "Dad, can we go to the park? Max wants to play!" Dad smiled and said, "Sure, let's go!" At the park, Sam and Max played on the swings and slides. They laughed and had fun together. After a while, they heard a loud noise. It was an ice cream truck! Sam and Max ran to the truck and each got a yummy ice cream cone. They enjoyed the sweet treat and continued playing until it was time to go home.

One day, a kind cat named Lily was playing in the park. She saw a small bird sitting on a tree. The bird was sad because it could not fly. Lily wanted to help the bird. She saw a long thread on the ground and had an idea. Lily took the thread and tied one end to the bird's leg. She then tied the other end to a big balloon. The balloon started to lift the bird up into the sky. The bird was so happy that it could fly again. Later, the bird saw Lily sitting alone in the park. The bird wanted to thank Lily for her help. It flew down and sat next to her. They became good friends and played together every day. And the thread that helped the bird fly was always with them, a reminder of their special friendship.

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a heavy purple ball. She wanted to play with her friend, Tom. She took the ball to Tom's house. "Tom, look at my ball! Let's play!" said Lucy. Tom looked at the ball and said, "That ball is too heavy for us. We can't play with it." Lucy was sad. She wanted to find a way to play with the heavy purple ball. Lucy had an idea. She asked her dad to give her a big stick. Lucy and Tom used the stick to push and roll the heavy purple ball. They played all day and had lots of fun.

Once, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to mix colors. She had a big box of colors to play with. One day, she wanted to make a new color that she had never seen before. Sue took out her colors and started to mix them. She mixed blue and yellow, and it made green. She mixed red and blue, and it made purple. Then, Sue mixed all the colors together. It made a gray color. She was very happy with her new color. Sue showed her gray color to her mom. "Look, Mom! I made a new color. I mixed all my colors to make it," she said. Her mom smiled and said, "Good work, Sue! I like your gray color. Now you can use it to make more pictures." Sue felt proud of her work and used her new gray color to make lots of fun pictures.

Lily and Tom were playing with their toy trains on the floor. They liked to make noises and pretend they were driving fast. Lily had a red train and Tom had a blue one. "Look, my train can take more things than yours!" Lily said. She put some blocks and a teddy bear on her train. "It is very strong and big!" Tom did not like that. He wanted his train to be the best. He looked around and saw a kettle on the stove. He had an idea. He ran to the kitchen and grabbed the kettle. It was hot and had steam coming out of it. "Ha! My train can take steam!" Tom said. He put the kettle on his train and pushed it towards Lily. "It is very fast and brilliant!" Lily was scared. She saw the steam and felt the heat. She thought the train was going to explode. She screamed and ran away. "No, no, no! Your train is bad and dangerous!" Tom felt sorry. He did not mean to scare Lily. He took the kettle off his train and put it back on the stove. He went to Lily and hugged her. "I'm sorry, Lily. I was just playing. Your train is good and nice." Lily smiled and hugged him back. "It's okay, Tom. I was just playing too. Your train is cool and fun." They went back to their toy trains and played together. They shared their blocks and teddy bears. They made noises and pretended they were driving fast. They were happy and friendly.
