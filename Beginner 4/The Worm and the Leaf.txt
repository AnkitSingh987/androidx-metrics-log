Category: Beginner
Title: The Worm and the Leaf

Once upon a time, there was a shy little worm. He lived underground in a tiny hole. He was very happy there. He had many friends, like the ants and the bugs. One day, the shy worm went for a walk. He saw a big tree. The tree had many leaves. The wind blew, and a leaf began to fall. The worm watched the leaf as it came down. The leaf fell right next to the shy worm. He was scared at first, but then he saw how pretty the leaf was. The worm and the leaf became friends. They played together every day. The shy worm was happy to have a new friend. And they lived happily ever after.

Lily and Tom were best friends. They liked to play in the park near their homes. There was a big oak tree in the park. It had many green leaves and brown acorns. Lily and Tom liked to climb the oak tree and pretend they were birds. One day, a man came to the park with a big truck and a loud saw. He wanted to cut down the oak tree and take it away. Lily and Tom were very sad and scared. They ran to the oak tree and hugged it. "Please don't cut our tree!" Lily cried. "We love our tree! It is our friend!" The man looked at Lily and Tom. He saw their tears and heard their words. He felt sorry for them. He put down his saw and walked to them. "Why do you love this tree so much?" he asked. "Because it is beautiful and strong and healthy," Tom said. "It gives us shade and air and fun. It is part of our park and our home." The man nodded. He understood. He had a home and a family too. He valued them more than anything. "I'm sorry, kids," he said. "I didn't know this tree meant so much to you. I was hired to cut it down and sell it for wood. But I don't want to make you unhappy. I will leave your tree alone. You can keep playing with it." Lily and Tom smiled and thanked the man. They were very happy and relieved. They hugged the oak tree again and told it they loved it. The oak tree seemed to smile too. It was glad to have such good friends who valued it.

Once upon a time, there was a big, fat cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play in the water. One day, Kitty heard about a fun splash party. She was so happy and wanted to go. At the party, Kitty met a small dog named Buddy. Buddy said, "Hi Kitty, let's play and splash in the water!" They played together and had so much fun. They splashed each other and laughed. But then, something unexpected happened. A big fish jumped out of the water and said, "Hello, friends! I want to play too!" Kitty and Buddy were surprised, but they let the big fish play with them. Now, they were all friends and had the best splash party ever.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby bird named Bobo. Bobo loved to fly high in the sky. He had many friends, but his best friend was a little girl named Lucy. One day, Lucy had an idea. She wanted to make a club for all the birds. She called it "The Flying Friends Club." Bobo was very happy to be a part of the club. He told all of his bird friends to join too. Every day, the birds in the club would fly and play together. They would sing songs and have fun. Lucy would watch them and clap her hands. The Flying Friends Club made Lucy and Bobo very happy. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a gray town, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to walk and play with his friends. One day, while walking with his friends, they saw a big, gray cat. The cat looked sad and lonely. Tim asked his friends, "Why is the cat sad?" His friends said, "We don't know, maybe it needs a friend." Tim thought about it and said, "Let's be friends with the cat." So, they all walked up to the cat and said, "Hi cat, we want to be your friends." The cat looked at them and the silence was broken when it said, "Thank you, I am happy now." Tim and his friends played with the cat every day. They learned that even a small act of kindness could make someone happy. The moral of the story is to always be kind and make new friends, even if they are different from you.
