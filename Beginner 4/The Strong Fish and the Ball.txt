Category: Beginner
Title: The Strong Fish and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a strong fish. He lived in a tank. The tank was big and blue. The strong fish liked to swim all day. One day, the strong fish saw a little fish. The little fish was sad. The strong fish wanted to help. He swam to the end of the tank to find a toy for the little fish. The strong fish found a ball at the end of the tank. He pushed the ball to the little fish. The little fish was happy. They played with the ball together. The strong fish and the little fish were friends. And they lived happily in the big, blue tank.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys in their room. One day, they find a big box in the corner. It is brown and has a lot of tape on it. They wonder what is inside. They try to open the box, but it is too hard. They pull and tear at the tape, but it does not come off. They get angry and frustrated. They start to cry and shout. Mom hears them and comes to their room. She sees the box and smiles. She says, "This is a surprise for you. I was going to give it to you later, but you can have it now. Do you want to see what is inside?" Lily and Ben nod and wipe their tears. Mom takes a pair of scissors and cuts the tape. She opens the box and lifts something out. It is a big, fluffy, white bear. It has a red bow around its neck and a smile on its face. Lily and Ben gasp and clap their hands. They hug the bear and feel its texture. It is soft and warm and cozy. They thank Mom and say they love the bear. Mom says, "I'm glad you like it. It is a special bear. It can talk to you and tell you stories. Do you want to hear one?" Lily and Ben say yes and sit on the bed with the bear. The bear says, "Hello, I'm Snowy. I'm your new friend. I have many stories to tell you. Some are funny, some are scary, some are sad, and some are happy. But they are all true. Do you want to hear one?" Lily and Ben say yes again and listen to the bear. The bear tells them a story about a forest, a wolf, and a rabbit. It is an unknown story that they have never heard before. They are curious and excited. They ask the bear questions and laugh and gasp. They forget about the box and the tape. They are happy with their new friend.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to speak to his toys. He had many toys, but his favorite was a big red truck. Tim would play with his truck every day after school. One day, Tim found some equipment in the garage. It was regular equipment like a hammer, a screwdriver, and some nails. He thought, "I can use this equipment to fix my truck!" So, he started to work on his truck. As he was working, Tim heard a small voice. "Hello!" it said. Tim looked around and saw a little bird. "Hi, bird!" Tim said. The bird replied, "Do you need help with your equipment?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, please!" They worked together and fixed the truck. From that day on, Tim and the bird became best friends, and they played with the truck every day.

Tim and Lily were twins who liked to play on the farm. They had many animals to see and feed, but their favorite was the sheep. The sheep were soft and white and made funny noises. One day, Tim and Lily decided to have a contest. They wanted to see who could repeat what the sheep said better. They each picked a sheep and listened carefully. Then they tried to copy the sounds. "Baa, baa," said Tim's sheep. "Baa, baa," said Tim. "Meh, meh," said Lily's sheep. "Meh, meh," said Lily. They both thought they did a good job, but they also wanted to win. They started to argue about who was better. "Mine sounds more like a sheep," said Tim. "No, mine does," said Lily. "Yours is too high," said Tim. "Yours is too low," said Lily. They got louder and louder, until the sheep got scared and ran away. Tim and Lily felt bad. They had made the sheep unhappy. They looked at each other and said sorry. They realized that it was not nice to fight over something silly. They decided to be more thoughtful and share the sheep. They ran after the sheep and gave them some hay and hugs. The sheep came back and were happy again. Tim and Lily smiled and hugged each other. They learned that repeating what the sheep said was fun, but being kind to the sheep and each other was more important.
