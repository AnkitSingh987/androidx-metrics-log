Category: Beginner
Title: The Dog of Tim the Boy

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a big hill with a lot of dust. Tim ran up the hill and found a small, red ball. He was very happy and started to play with it. While Tim was playing, a rude dog came near him. The dog wanted to bite the red ball. Tim did not like this. He wanted the dog to go away. He said, "No, dog! This is my ball!" The dog did not listen to Tim. It jumped and bit the red ball. The ball popped and the dust flew everywhere. Tim was sad, but the dog ran away. Tim went home and told his mom about the rude dog. She gave him a hug and a new ball to play with. Tim was happy again.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim had a big toy box, and he loved to play with his toys. One day, Tim wanted to play outside, but it was gloomy. The sky was gray, and it looked like it would rain soon. Tim felt sad but still wanted to play outside. Tim asked his mom if he could go outside to play. His mom disagreed. She said, "No, Tim, it's too gloomy outside. You might get wet and cold." Tim didn't like that answer. He wanted to go outside and play with his friends. So, Tim thought of a plan. Tim put on his boots, one on each foot, and his raincoat. He showed his mom that he was ready for the gloomy weather. His mom saw that he was prepared and finally agreed. "Okay, Tim, you can go outside, but be careful and don't stay out too long." Tim was so happy, and he went outside to play with his friends, even though it was gloomy.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named True. True liked to sort her toys. She had a big box of toys. True felt very comfortable when her toys were sorted. One day, True went outside to play. She left her toys in the box. A big wind came and blew her toys away. True did not know where her toys went. True was sad. She looked for her toys but could not find them. The wind took them far away. True never found her toys again. She was not comfortable anymore.

One day, two friends, Tom and Martha, were walking in the park. Tom was carrying a modern purse. Martha said to Tom, "Where did you come this purse?" Tom replied, "I came it from the store." Martha was excited and said, "Let me come and see it!" When they got to the store, Martha was amazed. The modern purse was beautiful and stylish. Tom said, "Let me come you some money to buy it." Martha thanked Tom and said, "Yes!" She was so happy and could not believe her luck. Tom smiled and said, "Now, let's come it with you wherever you go." As they left the store, Martha held the modern purse tightly, knowing it was a special gift from her friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big pit. It was not a fancy pit, but it was a fun pit to play in. One day, a little boy named Tim went to play in the pit with his friends. They liked to dig and build things in the pit. They were very happy. Then, it started to rain. The rain came down hard, and the pit got wet. Tim and his friends had to go home. They were sad because they could not play in the pit anymore. They thought the rain had ruined their fun. The next day, the rain stopped. Tim and his friends went back to the pit. They found something unexpected. The rain had filled the pit with water, and now it was a big pool! Tim and his friends were so happy. They jumped in the water and splashed around. The rain had made their pit even more fun than before.

One day, an elderly man named Tom went for a jog in the park. While jogging, he saw a big crane near a pond. The crane was looking for fish to eat. Tom stopped jogging to watch the crane. Tom saw a little girl named Sally near the pond too. She was watching the crane too. Sally said, "Hi, I like the crane. Do you like it too?" Tom said, "Yes, I like the crane too. It is very big." Suddenly, the crane caught a fish and started to fly away. But the crane dropped the fish by accident! The fish fell into Sally's hands. Tom and Sally laughed and put the fish back in the pond. They were happy to see the fish swim away.
