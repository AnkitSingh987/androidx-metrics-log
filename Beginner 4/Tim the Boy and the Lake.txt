Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Lake

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to buy a kayak. He went to the store with his mom. They saw many kayaks, but Tim wanted a hard one. Tim and his mom looked at all the hard kayaks. They found a big, hard kayak that was red. Tim liked the red kayak a lot. His mom said, "Yes, we can buy this one." Tim and his mom bought the red, hard kayak. They took it to the lake. Tim had fun in his new kayak. He paddled it all day. Tim was very happy with his hard kayak.

Rex was a big dog who loved to play. He was very enthusiastic about everything. He liked to run, jump, bark and chase. He also liked to chew on bones. He had many bones in his backyard. He was very proud of his bones. One day, Rex saw a new bone in his neighbor's yard. It was a big, juicy bone. Rex wanted that bone very much. He thought it would be the best bone ever. He decided to go and get it. He dug a hole under the fence and crawled through. The neighbor's yard had a small cat. The cat was not happy to see Rex. The cat hissed and scratched at Rex. Rex did not care. He ran towards the bone. He managed to grab it with his teeth. He was very happy. He turned around and ran back to the hole. But the hole was too small for Rex and the bone. Rex could not fit through. He tried to push and pull, but he was stuck. He dropped the bone and tried again, but it was no use. He was trapped. The cat saw Rex and the bone. The cat was very clever. The cat decided to take the bone. The cat walked over to the bone and picked it up with its mouth. The cat ran away with the bone. Rex saw the cat and the bone. Rex was very angry and sad. He barked and cried, but no one heard him. He had lost his bone and his way home. He was very sorry. He wished he had never seen the new bone. He wished he had stayed in his own yard with his own bones. He learned a hard lesson. He was not enthusiastic anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Every morning, Lucy's dad would make a hot drink called coffee. Lucy liked the smell of coffee, but she knew it was for grown-ups only. One day, Lucy saw her dad put the coffee on the table. She thought, "Maybe I can try just a little bit." She reached for the cup, but it was very hot. "Ouch!" she said, as it felt like a burn on her hand. Her dad heard her and came running. "Oh no, Lucy!" he said. "You must be careful with hot things. They can hurt you." Lucy's dad gave her a hug and a kiss to make her feel better. He told her, "When you grow up, you can try coffee. But for now, let's find something else for you to drink." He gave Lucy a cold glass of milk and a hard cookie to dip in it. Lucy was happy with her milk and cookie, and she promised to be careful with hot things from now on.

Once upon a time, there was a little train. It liked to shake and wiggle as it went down the tracks. One day, the train had to go to the station to pick up some delicate things. At the station, there were many people with boxes. The train was very careful not to shake too much. It did not want to break the delicate things inside the boxes. The people put the boxes on the train, and it started to go down the tracks again. The train went slow and steady, trying not to shake. It wanted to keep the delicate things safe. When the train got to the next station, the people took the boxes off. They were so happy that the delicate things were not broken. The train felt very proud and went on to its next adventure.
