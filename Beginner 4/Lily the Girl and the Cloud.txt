Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Cloud

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing outside. She saw a big cloud in the sky. The cloud was dark and it made a loud sound. Lily knew that it was thunder. She felt a little scared, but she was brave. Soon, the sky got dark and the wind blew. The air became bitter and cold. Lily knew that it was time to go inside. She ran to her house and opened the door. Her mom was waiting for her with a warm hug. Lily said, "Thank you, Mom, for being here when the thunder came." Her mom smiled and said, "You're welcome, Lily. I will always be here for you." They sat together, feeling warm and safe, as they listened to the thunder outside.

One day, a friendly dog named Max found a football in the park. Max loved to play with balls. He picked up the football in his mouth and ran around the park. He was very happy. Max saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad because she could not find her football. Max wanted to help her. He ran to Lily with the football in his mouth. "I found your football!" Max barked. Lily was so happy to see her football. She said, "Thank you, Max!" They played together in the park. They laughed and had a lot of fun. Max and Lily became good friends. They could always hear each other's laughs when they played with the football.

Once upon a time, there was a regular boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys all day. One day, Tim found a big tube in his yard. He thought it was a new toy, so he took it inside his house. Tim played with the tube all day. He put his cars in it and watched them go fast. But he did not finish his lunch or help his mom clean up. His mom was sad, but Tim did not notice. At night, Tim wanted to sleep with the tube. His mom said no, but Tim did not listen. He hid the tube in his bed. When Tim went to sleep, the tube fell on his head. Tim cried and cried. He wished he had listened to his mom and finished his chores.

Once upon a time, there were two friends called Tom and Sarah. Tom was tall, but Sarah was shy. They both lived in the same neighbourhood. One sunny day, Tom and Sarah decided to have a race. Sarah was a bit worried because she was not as fast as Tom. But Tom said, "don't worry Sarah, it's okay!" Tom and Sarah put their feet at the starting line. Tom shouted "Ready, steady, go!" The two friends raced as fast as they could. Tom's long legs took big strides, while Sarah's short legs ran quickly. Tom was getting closer to the finish line and Sarah was struggling to keep up. But in the end, Sarah was the first to reach the finish line. Tom was amazed and said, "Good job, Sarah! You're so fast!" Sarah smiled, feeling proud. She had not expected to win. From then on, she felt less shy and more confident.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He had a big bag with him. In the bag, he had a ball, a toy car, and a loud horn. Tim was happy to play with his toys at the park. At the park, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue liked to play with her new toy dog. Tim and Sue played together with their toys. They had a lot of fun. The sun was hot, and they were getting tired. Tim and Sue sat under a tree to rest. They put their toys in the big bag. Suddenly, a strong wind came and took the bag away. Tim and Sue were sad. They looked for the bag everywhere. When they found it, they saw that the wind had blown the bag to a new part of the park. There, they found a new toy in the bag. It was a magic wand! Tim and Sue gained a new toy to play with, and they were very happy.
