Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Paint

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He liked to paint. He had many colors of paint. He painted every day. He painted trees, sun, and flowers. Tim was a regular boy who loved to paint. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came to play. Sam saw Tim's paint and asked, "Can you lend me some paint? I want to paint too." Tim said, "Yes, I can lend you my paint. Let's paint together." They started to paint. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew their paint away. Tim and Sam were sad. They looked for the paint but could not find it. Then, they saw a bird. The bird had the paint in its beak. The bird flew down and gave the paint back to Tim and Sam. They were so happy. They said, "Thank you, bird!" Tim and Sam continued to paint. They painted a big picture of the bird who helped them. They knew that even in a regular day, something unexpected can happen. And sometimes, it can be a good thing.

Once upon a time, there was a strong boy named Tom. He lived in a small house with his mommy, daddy, and a big, soft armchair. Tom loved to sit in the armchair and read books with his mommy. One day, Tom heard a loud noise outside. He ran to the window and saw a man playing drums. Tom liked the beat and started to clap his hands. He clapped and clapped, and the man smiled at him. Tom's mommy saw how much he liked the music, so she bought him a small drum. Tom played the drum every day, sitting in the big armchair. He became very good at it, and everyone loved to hear him play. They would clap and dance while Tom made music, and it made everyone very happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the kitchen. He wanted some yummy cereal for breakfast. He saw a big box on the top shelf. Tim tried to get the box but it was too high. He felt sad. Then, Tim's mom came into the kitchen. She saw Tim and asked, "What do you need, Tim?" Tim said, "I want the yummy cereal, but it's too high for me." His mom smiled and said, "I can help you!" Tim's mom reached up and got the box. She opened it with a snap. Tim was so happy! He said, "Thank you, mom!" They poured the cereal into a bowl and added milk. Tim ate the yummy cereal and felt happy again. The conflict was over, and Tim had a great day.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a brave little girl named Amy. Amy loved to pretend she was a strong hero, saving her animal friends. One day, she saw a cat stuck up in a tree. The cat was scared and crying. Amy wanted to help the cat, so she found a long vine hanging from another tree. She said, "Don't worry, cat! I will save you!" Amy held the vine and swung like a hero to the tree where the cat was. She climbed up and gently took the cat in her arms. As Amy and the cat came down, all the animals clapped and cheered. The cat was so happy, it gave Amy a big hug. From that day on, Amy learned that even when you pretend, you can still be brave and help others. And all her animal friends loved her even more.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy butter for their bread. Tim was very excited because he loved butter on his bread. At the store, Tim's mom let him hold the butter. But as they walked, Tim's hand began to slip. The butter fell on the floor! Tim felt very ashamed. He did not want his mom to be mad at him. Tim's mom saw what happened and hugged him. She said, "It's okay, Tim. We all make mistakes. Let's clean it up and get another butter." Tim felt better and helped his mom. They went home and enjoyed their bread with butter.
