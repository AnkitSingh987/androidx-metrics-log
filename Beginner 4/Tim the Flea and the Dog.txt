Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Flea and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little flea named Tim. Tim lived on a big dog named Max. Tim thought Max was amazing. They were best friends. One day, Tim saw a pretty cat named Lily. Tim wanted to be friends with her too. He jumped from Max to Lily. But, Lily did not like fleas. She tried to get Tim off her. Then, something unexpected happened. Max saw Tim on Lily and got sad. Max did not want to lose his friend. He thought of a plan. Max barked at Lily, and she got scared. Tim jumped back to Max. Now, Tim knew Max was not just amazing, but also very smart. They were happy to be together again. From that day on, Tim and Max stayed best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little duck. The duck lived near a big pond. He had many friends. They liked to play and swim all day. One day, the duck saw a baby bird in trouble. The baby bird's neck was stuck in a small hole. The duck wanted to save the baby bird. He called all his friends to help him. They all tried to save the baby bird. The duck was very sorry for the baby bird. At last, they saved the baby bird! The baby bird was happy and thanked the duck and his friends. They all played together and had a fun day.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to play basketball. He picked up his basketball and went to the park. When he got there, he saw a big dog. Tim was scared of the big dog. He did not want the dog to take his basketball. Tim said to the dog, "Please don't take my basketball. I want to play with it." The big dog looked at Tim and wagged its tail. The dog was not mean; it just wanted to play too. Tim and the big dog played basketball together. They had a lot of fun. After they played, Tim sat down and read a book. The big dog lay down next to him. Tim was not scared anymore. They became good friends and played basketball every day.

Once there was a little turtle called Tom. Tom was quite naughty, and he didn't like to behave. One day, Tom decided to go on an adventure. He went for a swim in the big pond. Tom was having great fun swimming but then he realized that he was far away from his shell. He was scared as he couldn't find his way home. Soon he heard a voice. It was his grandmother! She had come looking for him. She told him, "Tom, why don't you behave? It's not safe to be so far away." But Tom didn't listen. He kept swimming. Suddenly it started raining, and the pond became very wet. Tom looked around desperately for his shell, but it had gone. He was so scared and alone, and he couldn't find his way home. Tom never found his shell, and he never went on an adventure again. He learnt his lesson – if only he had behaved, his story might have had a happy ending.

One day, a lively human named Tom went for a walk. He liked to walk in the park. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. Tom saw a big tree and wanted to sit under it. As Tom sat under the tree, he saw a talking bird. The bird said, "Hello, Tom!" Tom was surprised. He didn't know that birds could talk. Tom said, "Hello, bird! How do you know my name?" The bird smiled and said, "I know many things, Tom. I can tell you something you don't know." Tom was curious. "What is it?" he asked. The bird told Tom that he could fly like a bird if he tried very hard. Tom didn't believe the bird at first, but he wanted to try. He closed his eyes and thought about flying. When he opened his eyes, he was flying in the sky! Tom was so happy. He flew with the bird and saw many things. At the end of the day, Tom thanked the bird for showing him how to fly. They became good friends. From that day on, Tom could walk and fly. And he always went to the park to play with his new friend, the talking bird.
