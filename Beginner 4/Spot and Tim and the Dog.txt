Category: Beginner
Title: Spot and Tim and the Dog

Once upon a time, a loyal dog named Spot lived with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. They played all day and were always happy. One day, Tim's mom had big news. A new baby was coming to live with them! Spot did not know what a baby was, but he tried to resist being sad. He wanted to be a good dog for Tim and the new baby. The day the baby came home, Spot was surprised. The baby was not what he thought. It was a little puppy! Tim's mom had tricked them. Spot was so happy and could not resist playing with the new puppy. They all played together and were one big, happy family.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very hungry. He went to his mom and said, "Mom, I am hungry. Can I have some food, please?" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. Look in the chamber. You will find some yummy food." Tim went to the chamber and opened the door. Inside, he saw a big, red apple. He picked it up and took a big bite. It was very sweet and juicy. Tim was very happy. As Tim ate the apple, his friend Sam came over to play. Sam saw Tim eating the apple and said, "I am hungry too. Can I have some food?" Tim said, "Sure, Sam. Let's look in the chamber for more food." They went to the chamber and found a big, green apple for Sam. They ate their apples and played all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Mr. Rich. He lived in a big house with many toys. Mr. Rich liked to tie his shoes with fancy laces. One sunny day, Mr. Rich went for a walk. He met a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad because her shoes were untied. She did not know how to tie them. "Can you help me tie my shoes?" asked Sue. Mr. Rich said, "Of course! I have an opinion on the best way to tie shoes." He showed Sue how to make two bunny ears and loop them together. Sue tried it and her shoes were tied. "Thank you, Mr. Rich!" said Sue. They both smiled and continued to walk. From that day on, Sue knew how to tie her shoes and felt happy.

Once upon a time, there was a smart cat named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy mixer. One day, he heard a knock on the door. A new friend had arrived! "Hi, I'm Sara," said the new friend. "Can I play with you?" Tim was happy to have a new friend and said, "Yes, let's play with my mixer!" Tim and Sara played with the mixer all day. They mixed and mixed, making fun sounds and having a great time. Their laughter filled the air. They were so happy that they had met and become friends. At the end of the day, Sara said, "Thank you, Tim, for letting me play with your mixer. I had a lot of fun!" Tim smiled and said, "You're welcome, Sara! I had fun too. Let's play together again soon!" From that day on, Tim and Sara played together every day. They shared their toys and had many fun adventures. And they always remembered the day they first met and played with the mixer.

One day, a cat named Tim and a dog named Max went for a walk. They walked and walked until they found an amazing tree. The tree was very big and had many leaves. Tim and Max wanted to climb the tree. Tim said, "I will stretch my legs and jump to reach the first branch." Max said, "I will use my brush to help you climb." Tim jumped and Max used his brush to push Tim up. They both climbed the tree together. When they got to the top, they saw a big bird. The bird said, "Hello, I am the amazing bird who can talk!" Tim and Max were very surprised. They never met a talking bird before. The bird said, "I will teach you how to talk to other animals." Tim and Max were very happy and they learned to talk to all their animal friends.
