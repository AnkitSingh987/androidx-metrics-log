Category: Beginner
Title: Mia and Sam and the Word

Mia and Sam were best friends. They played together every day. One day, Mia had a weird idea. She said to Sam, "Let's send a word!" Sam was confused. He asked, "What do you mean?" Mia laughed and said, "Let's pick a word and send it as far as we can!" Sam thought it was a fun idea. He smiled and said, "Alright, let's do it!" Mia and Sam tried to think of the best word to send. Mia suggested, "Let's pick 'friendship'!" Sam was happy. He said, "That's perfect!" Mia and Sam closed their eyes and yelled out, "Friendship!" They laughed as they sent the word away. The word flew high and far, and Mia and Sam were glad.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big red balloon. He loved the balloon very much. He played with it all day. He wanted to show it to his friend, Sue. So, Tim thought he would send the balloon to her. Tim went to Sue's house with the balloon. He said, "Sue, look at my big red balloon! I want to send it to you." Sue was happy and excited. But when she tried to hold the balloon, it flew away into the sky. They both felt bitter and sad. Just then, a bird saw the balloon in the sky. The bird thought it was a friend. The bird played with the balloon and then brought it back to Tim and Sue. They were so surprised and happy. They thanked the bird and played with the balloon together. The bitter feeling was gone, and they had a fun day with their new friend, the bird.

Sam and Lily were playing with their toys in the living room. Sam had a laser that made red lights and sounds. Lily had a tea set that had cups and a pot. Sam liked to pretend that his laser was a spaceship that could fly and shoot. Lily liked to pretend that she was a wealthy lady who had tea parties. "Can I see your laser?" Lily asked Sam. "OK, but be careful. It is very powerful," Sam said. He gave his laser to Lily and took a cup from her tea set. Lily held the laser and pressed the button. She saw the red light and heard the sound. She smiled and pointed the laser at the wall. "Wow, this is fun!" she said. Sam poured some water from the pot into the cup. He pretended that it was hot tea. He sipped it and made a face. "Yuck, this is not good!" he said. Lily turned to look at Sam. She saw the water in the cup and the pot. She had an idea. "Hey, Sam, do you want to see something cool?" she asked. "What is it?" Sam asked. Lily pointed the laser at the pot. She pressed the button. The red light hit the water and made it bubble and steam. "Look, I can make the water boil with my laser!" she said. Sam was amazed. He dropped the cup and ran to Lily. "Wow, that is cool! How did you do that?" he asked. Lily shrugged. She did not know how the laser worked. She just liked to play with it. "I don't know. It just does," she said. They laughed and hugged. They decided to share their toys and have more fun. They poured more water and made more bubbles. They flew the laser around the room and made more sounds. They were happy and had a good time.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird who wanted to fly. He was so excited but he was also a bit worried. One day, he found a microphone on the ground and he picked it up. Suddenly he had an idea - what if he could use the microphone to help him fly? He got ready by flapping his wings and he held the microphone tight. He counted to three and then he flew into the sky! He went higher and higher as he flew, until he was so far up he couldn't even see his home. The little bird was so happy and excited, he had done it! But then he started to feel a bit worried again. Would he ever be able to fly back home? He looked all around, searching for answers. Then, he found a friendly cloud who told him all he had to do was think of his home, close his eyes and flap his wings while holding the microphone. The little bird did as the cloud said and soon he felt a warm breeze beneath his wings, as if he was being lifted up. He opened his eyes and saw he was now back in his nest. He put the microphone beside him and smiled - he had learnt he could fly with the power of his imagination!
