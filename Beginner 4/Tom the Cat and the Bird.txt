Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Cat and the Bird

One day, a shy cat named Tom saw a figure in the yard. He did not know what it was. He started to wonder who or what it could be. Tom wanted to find out, but he was too shy to get close. Tom asked his friend, a bird named Sue, to help him. Sue was not shy. She flew near the figure and saw that it was a big dog. Sue told Tom, and they both got scared. They did not like big dogs. They thought the big dog would be mean. But then, something unexpected happened. The big dog came over to Tom and Sue. He was not mean at all. He was nice and just wanted to play. Tom and Sue were happy they made a new friend. The big dog was not scary after all.

Once upon a time, there was a little avocado. It was green and round. The avocado lived with a big tree. The tree was nice to the avocado. The avocado wanted to grow big like the tree. One day, the avocado met a stupid bird. The bird told the avocado that if it jumped off the tree, it would grow big. The avocado believed the bird. The avocado jumped off the tree. It fell on the ground. The avocado felt sad. A kind girl saw the avocado on the ground. She picked it up and took it home. She put the avocado in a pot with dirt. The avocado started to grow. It grew into a big avocado tree. The avocado learned not to listen to stupid things. It was happy to grow big with the help of the kind girl.

One day, a little dog named Spot went for a walk. Spot liked to play and run. He was a very glad dog. He saw a big tree, and he wanted to see what was behind it. Spot ran to the tree and found a little cat. The little cat looked at Spot and gave a small shrug. Spot did not know what to do. He wanted to be friends with the cat. So, he put out his paw to the cat. The cat looked at Spot's paw and then touched it with her paw. Now, Spot and the cat were friends. They played together all day. They ran and jumped, and they were very glad. At the end of the day, they said goodbye and went home. Spot was happy to have a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a great slide near a bridge. Many kids loved to play on the slide. One day, a little boy named Tim and a little girl named Sue went to the slide to play. Tim said to Sue, "Let's go on the slide together!" Sue was very happy and agreed. They both climbed up and slid down the great slide, laughing and having fun. After they played on the slide, Tim and Sue walked to the bridge. They looked at the water and saw some fish. They waved at the fish and the fish seemed to wave back. Tim and Sue had a fun day playing on the slide and visiting the bridge.

Lily and Tom love to help Mom bake in the kitchen. They put on their aprons and wash their hands. Mom gives them each a ball of dough to roll and cut. Lily makes stars and Tom makes circles. Mom puts the dough shapes on a big tray and puts some jam and cheese on them. She says they are pastries and they will be yummy. She puts the tray in the oven and tells Lily and Tom to wait for the timer to beep. Lily and Tom play with their toys while they wait. They hear the timer beep and run to the kitchen. Mom takes the tray out of the oven and puts it on the counter. She says the pastries are very hot and they need to cool down. Lily and Tom want to taste the pastries. They smell so good. They climb on a chair and reach for the tray. But the chair is wobbly and they slip and fall. The tray slides off the counter and the pastries fly everywhere. Mom hears the noise and comes to see what happened. She sees Lily and Tom on the floor and the pastries all over the kitchen. She is not angry. She hugs them and says they are okay. She says they can still eat the pastries if they are not too dirty. She says they can make more another day. Lily and Tom smile and say they are sorry. They pick up the pastries and share them with Mom. They are still hot and yummy.
