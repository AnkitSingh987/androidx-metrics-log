Category: Beginner
Title: Tim Finds the Dog

One day, Tim and his mom went to the store. They saw a cute dog outside. The dog was very adorable. Tim wanted to play with the dog, but his mom said they had to go inside the store first. Inside the store, Tim saw a big box. He heard a funny noise coming from it. He opened the box and found a little bird inside! The bird started to sing a pretty song. Tim and his mom were very happy. Then, the adorable dog from outside came into the store. The dog started to dance to the bird's song. Everyone in the store stopped and watched. They all began to clap and applaud for the dog and the bird. It was a fun and unexpected day at the store.

Sam liked to play with his toy cars. He had many cars of different colors and sizes. He liked to make them zoom and crash on the floor. He had one car that was very special. It was shiny and brown, like a penny. His dad told him it was made of copper. Sam loved his copper car the most. One day, Sam's cousin Lily came to visit. Lily was older than Sam and liked to boss him around. She saw his copper car and wanted to play with it. She asked Sam if she could have it. Sam said no, it was his favorite car. Lily got angry and tried to grab it from him. Sam held on tight and shouted for his dad. Dad came and saw what was happening. He told Lily to stop and let go of Sam's car. He said it was not nice to take things without asking. He said Sam did not permit her to have his car, and she had to respect that. He said she could play with other cars, but not the copper one. Lily pouted and said Sam was mean and selfish. She said his copper car was ugly and old. She said she did not want to play with him anymore. She ran to the other room and slammed the door. Sam felt sad and hurt. He did not understand why Lily was so terrible. Dad hugged Sam and told him he was not mean or selfish. He said he was brave and smart to stand up for his car. He said Lily was wrong and rude to say those things. He said he was proud of Sam and his copper car. He said they could play together and have fun. Sam smiled and thanked his dad. He felt happy and loved. He and his dad played with his copper car and other cars for a long time.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his ball. One day, he went to the park to play with his friends. At the park, Tim saw a big pitch. He smiled and said, "Let's play on the pitch!" His friends all agreed and they started to play. They kicked the ball and laughed. They were having so much fun. After a while, Tim's mom called out, "Time to go home, Tim!" Tim felt sad but knew he had to listen. He waved goodbye to his friends and walked home with a big smile. He knew he could come back and play again soon.

One day, a little fish named Sam was swimming in the sea. He wanted to be brave and swim far away from his home. As Sam swam, he saw a big stand with many fish. They were all talking and playing. Sam swam up to the stand and said, "Hi! Can I play with you?" The other fish looked at him and said, "Yes, you can, but you have to be very brave to play with us." Just then, a big wave came and pushed all the fish away from the stand. Sam was scared, but he remembered he wanted to be brave. He swam as fast as he could and reached the stand first. The other fish were surprised and said, "Wow, Sam! You are really brave!" From that day on, Sam was always brave and played with his new friends at the stand. They had lots of fun swimming and playing together, and Sam was never scared again.
