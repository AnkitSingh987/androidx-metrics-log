Category: Beginner
Title: The Rug and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a big rug in a room. The rug was very pretty with lots of colors. It was in a distant corner of the room, far away from the door. One day, a little boy and girl wanted to play on the rug. They ran to the rug and started to play with their toys. They were having so much fun. But then, they heard a strange noise coming from under the rug. The little boy and girl were scared, but they wanted to know what was making the noise. They decided to separate the rug from the floor. When they lifted the rug, they found a secret door! They opened the door and found a room full of candy and toys. The little boy and girl were so happy. They played in the secret room and ate candy all day. The rug was hiding a magical place just for them. From that day on, the distant corner of the room became their favorite place to play.

Mum was teaching her three year-old son how to use a crayon. She was very patient, explaining to him how to hold it and what colours to choose. But he was still having trouble. He kept scribbling all over the paper. Mum smiled and encouragingly said, “It’s okay, we can try again.” But the next time was terrible: he dropped the crayon and it broke. Mum said, “It’s okay, I can get you a new crayon.” “No!” he cried, and his little face crumpled up. Mum hugged him and said, “No problem. I can teach you how to use it. We’ll get it right this time.” He smiled and with Mum’s help, he held the crayon correctly, making lovely marks on the paper. Mum was very proud of her three year-old son for learning so quickly with her help.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing near a big tire. The cat was light and could jump on the tire with no problem. The dog tried to jump too, but he was too heavy. The dog said, "Why can I not jump on the tire like you?" The cat laughed and said, "I am light, and you are not." The dog was sad and walked away. As he walked, he felt a sneeze coming. "Achoo!" he sneezed very loud. The sneeze was so strong that it made the tire roll fast. The tire hit a big tree and bounced back. The cat was still on the tire and could not jump off. The tire rolled back to the dog and hit him. Both the cat and the dog were hurt. They did not play near the tire again. They learned that playing with a tire can be bad.

One day, a little mouse named Tim saw a big piece of cheese near an icy pond. He wanted to eat the cheese, but he was scared of the icy water. He thought, "What if I slip and fall in the water?" Tim's friend, a rabbit named Sam, saw him looking at the cheese. Sam said, "I can help you get the cheese. I can spring very high and far!" Tim was happy and asked Sam to try. Sam got ready to spring over the icy water. He knew he had to be careful. Sam jumped and got the cheese. But, he slipped on the ice! Tim was scared for his friend. But Sam was okay. He held the cheese high and did not let it fall in the water. Sam gave the cheese to Tim, and they both laughed. They knew they could do anything when they helped each other.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little dog. He liked to test everything he saw. One day, he found a staff. The staff was big and brown. The nosy dog wanted to test the staff. He picked it up with his mouth and ran. He ran to the park and showed it to his friends. They all thought the staff was fun to play with. In the end, the nosy dog and his friends played with the staff all day. They were very happy. The dog learned that testing new things can be fun, and he became even more nosy.
