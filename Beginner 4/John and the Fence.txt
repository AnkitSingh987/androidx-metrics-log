Once upon a time, there was a messy dog named Spot. Spot liked to play with a ball of yarn. One day, Spot found a big knot in the yarn. He tried to untangle the knot, but it was very hard. Spot's friend, a funny cat named Whiskers, saw Spot having trouble with the knot. Whiskers started to laugh. "Ha, ha, ha! You look so funny with that big knot, Spot!" Whiskers said. Spot and Whiskers worked together to untangle the messy knot. They laughed and played with the yarn all day long. In the end, they had lots of fun and became even better friends.

Lily and Tom are playing in the park. They see a big dog with black fur. They want to pet the dog, but the dog runs away. "Let's search for the dog!" Lily says. "Maybe he is scared or lost." "OK, let's go!" Tom says. They follow the dog's tracks on the grass. They find the dog behind a tree. He is shaking and whimpering. He has a big bald spot on his back. It looks red and sore. "Hello, doggy," Lily says softly. "We are not going to hurt you. We want to be your friends." The dog looks at them with sad eyes. He wags his tail a little. "Maybe he has a boo-boo," Tom says. "We should help him." Lily nods. She takes off her scarf and wraps it around the dog's bald spot. The dog licks her hand. "Good doggy," she says. "You are very brave." The dog barks happily. He follows Lily and Tom to the bench. They sit with him and give him some water and cookies. "Look, Mommy!" Lily says, when she sees her mother coming. "We found a dog! He has fur and a bald spot. Can we keep him?" Mommy smiles. She sees the scarf on the dog's back. She pets the dog and says, "He is very cute. But he belongs to someone else. See, he has a collar with a name and a phone number. His name is Spot. We should call his owner and tell them we found him. Maybe they are searching for him too." Lily and Tom are sad. They hug the dog and say goodbye. They hope he will be OK. Mommy calls the phone number on the collar. A man answers. He sounds very happy and relieved. He says he is Spot's owner. He says Spot ran away from the vet. He says Spot has a skin problem and needs medicine. He says he will come to the park right away. Lily and Tom wait with Mommy and Spot. They play with him and tell him stories. They make him laugh. Soon, the man arrives. He is very grateful to Lily and Tom. He says they are very kind and smart. He says they saved Spot's life. He gives them a big hug and a reward. Lily and Tom are happy. They say thank you and good luck. They wave to Spot and his owner as they leave. They go home with Mommy. They tell her about their adventure. They say they love dogs. They say they want to be vets when they grow up. Mommy says she is very proud of them. She says they are very good children. She says she loves them very much.
