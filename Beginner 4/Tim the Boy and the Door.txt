Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Door

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a fancy house with a big door. Tim wanted to see what was inside. He walked up to the door and it opened by itself. Tim went inside. In the house, Tim found a big pie on the table. It smelled so good. Tim said, "Wow! I want to eat this pie!" He took a big bite of the pie. It was very yummy. As Tim ate the pie, he heard a loud noise. He turned around and saw a big, scary monster. The monster was angry because Tim ate his pie. Tim tried to run away, but he stumbled and fell. The monster caught Tim and he was never seen again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a sweet girl named Lily. She had a toy saw that she loved to play with. She would pretend to cut wood and build things with her saw. Lily was a kind girl, and she liked to share her toys with her friends. One day, Lily's friend, Tim, asked to borrow her saw. Lily said yes, and Tim took the saw to play. But after a while, Lily missed her saw and went to find Tim. She saw Tim with a broken saw and felt sad. Lily was upset, but Tim said sorry and gave her a big hug. They decided to fix the saw together. With some help from their parents, they made the saw like new again. The unexpected thing that happened taught them a lesson. They learned that when you borrow something, you must take good care of it. And if something goes wrong, it's important to say sorry and make it right.

Once upon a time, there was a rich man. He lived near a big hill. The rich man had many things. He liked his things a lot. He thought they were very important. One day, the rich man went for a walk. He walked up the big hill. At the top of the hill, he saw a big box. The rich man was very surprised. He opened the box. Inside the box, there was a note. The note said, "Value your friends, not your things." The rich man thought about the note. He walked back down the hill. He went to see his friends. The rich man learned to value his friends more than his things. He was very happy. His friends were happy too. They all played together and had fun. The rich man now knew that friends are more important than things.

Tim and Sam were brothers who liked to play in the pool. They had many toys, but their favorite was a big shark that could squirt water. Tim and Sam would take turns to hold the shark and chase each other. They had a lot of fun with the shark. One day, their mom said they had to share the shark with their cousin Lily, who was coming to visit. Tim and Sam did not like Lily. She was spoiled and always wanted to have the best toys. They did not want to share the shark with her. They made a plan to hide the shark under the water and pretend they lost it. When Lily came, she saw the pool and wanted to play. She asked Tim and Sam where the shark was. They said they did not know. They said maybe it swam away. Lily did not believe them. She jumped into the pool and looked for the shark. She did not see it. Tim and Sam smiled. They thought their plan worked. They waited for Lily to give up and go away. But Lily did not give up. She kept looking for the shark. She swam to the deep end of the pool, where Tim and Sam had hidden the shark. She saw something under the water. She reached for it. Suddenly, the shark sprang out of the water and squirted Lily in the face. Lily screamed and ran out of the pool. She was wet and scared. She cried and told her mom what happened. Tim and Sam laughed. They thought it was funny. They did not know that their mom had seen everything. She was not happy. She took the shark away from them and gave it to Lily. She said they had to learn to share and be nice. Tim and Sam were sad. They wished they had not been mean to Lily. They learned their lesson. They never hid the shark again.
