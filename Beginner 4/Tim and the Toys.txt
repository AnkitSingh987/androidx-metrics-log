Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Toys

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy some cheap toys for his friends' birthday party. Tim saw a big box with lots of toys on the left side of the store. He was very excited. Tim picked up a toy car and said, "Look, Mom! This car is so cheap. Can we buy it for my friends?" His mom looked at the car and said, "Yes, Tim. That's a great choice." Then, Tim saw a toy doll. He guessed that his friend Lisa would like it. He asked his mom, "Can we buy this doll too?" His mom smiled and said, "Of course, Tim. I think Lisa will love it." They bought the toys and went home to wrap them for the party.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a good witch. She had a big hat and a long dress. She was very nice to everyone. The good witch liked to help people. One day, the good witch went to the park. She saw a little girl who was sad. The little girl had a big red ball stuck in a tree. The good witch wanted to help her. The good witch used her magic to remove the ball from the tree. The little girl was very happy. She said, "Thank you, good witch!" The good witch smiled and went back to her home. Everyone in the town loved the good witch.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim had a fragile toy. He loved his toy very much. One day, Tim saw his mom put the toy in a high cupboard. He wanted to get it back. Tim asked his big sister, Sue, for help. "Sue, can you get my toy from the cupboard?" Sue said, "I will help you, but you must follow me and be careful." Tim agreed and followed Sue to the cupboard. Sue stood on her toes to reach the cupboard. She opened it and got the fragile toy. She gave it to Tim, and he was very happy. Tim learned that sometimes it is better to ask for help and follow others when things are too hard or risky.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a restless boy named Tom. Tom did not like to sit still. He always wanted to play and run around. One day, Tom found an arrow on the ground. He picked it up and showed it to his friends. His friends were excited to see the arrow, but they did not know what to do with it. They tried to play with it, but soon they started to complain. "This is not fun," said one friend. "We can't play with this," said another. Tom felt sad and decided to ask his dad for help. His dad showed him how to make a bow and use the arrow. Tom and his friends learned to shoot the arrow and had lots of fun. They were not restless or complaining anymore. They played with the arrow all day and had a great time.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big closet in his room. One day, he was careless and left his toys all over the floor. His mom came into his room and saw the mess. "Tim, you need to put your toys back in the closet," she said. Tim started to pick up his toys and push them into the closet. He pushed and pushed until all the toys were inside. Then, he closed the closet door. "Mom, I put all my toys away!" Tim said proudly. His mom came back into the room and saw the clean floor. "Good job, Tim!" she said with a smile. "Now you can play with your toys again, but remember to put them back in the closet when you're done." Tim promised to be more careful next time and always put his toys away.

Once upon a time, two friends named John and Sarah were playing in the park. They were playing tag and having lots of fun. Suddenly, John got really thirsty. He asked Sarah if she had any water, but she said she didn't. But then Sarah remembered that she had a bottle of coffee in her bag. She offered it to John and he took it eagerly. John opened the bottle and took a big sip. His face changed as soon as he tasted it. The coffee was very bitter. John made a funny face and said, “Yuck, this is so bitter!” Sarah laughed and said, “I know! That's why I hardly ever drink it. But why don't you perform a trick to make it taste better?” John thought for a moment and then said, “That's a great idea!” So he took a big breath and started to blow into the bottle. As he blew, the coffee started to taste a little better. John smiled. “You were right! That worked! Now this coffee tastes much better!” Sarah smiled and said, “See, I told you that performing a trick would make it better.” Then they finished their playtime in the park with smiles on their faces.
