Category: Beginner
Title: The Tall Tree and the Toys

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a tall tree. On this tree, there were many avocado fruits. A little boy named Tim loved to eat avocados. One day, he saw the tall tree and wanted to get the avocados. Tim asked his mom to help him pick the avocado fruits. His mom said, "I will help you, but you must repeat after me: 'I will share the avocados with my friends.'" Tim repeated the words and they picked many avocados together. The next day, Tim took the avocados to his friends to share. But when he opened the bag, he found toys instead of avocados! Tim and his friends were very happy. They learned that sharing brings happiness and surprises.

---

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the kitchen. He saw a dirty cupboard. Tim's mom told him, "Tim, behave and do not open the dirty cupboard." But Tim did not listen. He opened the dirty cupboard. Inside, he found a big, shiny box. Tim was very excited. He opened the box and found a lot of toys. He played with the toys and made a big mess in the kitchen. Tim's mom came back and saw the mess. She was very sad. She said, "Tim, you did not behave. Now, you have to clean all this mess." Tim started to clean, but it was too much work for him. He wished he had listened to his mom and not opened the dirty cupboard.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play with blocks and dolls. One day, they are playing in the garden when Tom starts to sneeze. "Achoo! Achoo! Achoo!" Tom sneezes loud. "Are you sick, Tom?" Mia asks. She is worried. "No, I am not sick. I am allergic to flowers. They make me sneeze." Tom says. Mia looks at the flowers. They are pretty and colorful. She likes them. "Can I pick some flowers for you, Tom? Maybe they will make you feel better." Mia says. Tom shakes his head. He knows the truth. Flowers will make him sneeze more. "No, thank you, Mia. Flowers are not good for me. They make my nose itch and my eyes water." Tom says. Mia thinks for a moment. She wants to help Tom. She has an idea. "I know what will make you feel better, Tom. A hug!" Mia says. She runs to Tom and gives him a big hug. Tom smiles. He feels warm and happy. He stops sneezing. "Thank you, Mia. You are a good friend. A hug is better than flowers." Tom says. Mia smiles too. She is proud. She helped Tom. "You are welcome, Tom. You are a good friend too. A hug is better than anything." Mia says. They hug again. They are happy. They go back to playing with blocks and dolls.

Once upon a time, there was a charming little dog named Buddy. Buddy lived in a big palace with his best friend, a kind girl named Lucy. They played all day and had lots of fun. One day, while they were in the garden, a naughty cat came by. The cat liked to tease Buddy. It would jump on Buddy's tail and run away. Buddy would bark and chase the cat, but he could never catch it. Lucy saw the cat teasing Buddy and said, "Stop teasing my friend!" The cat stopped and said, "I'm sorry, I just wanted to play." Lucy smiled and said, "Let's all play together!" From that day on, Buddy, Lucy, and the cat played together and had lots of fun in their big palace.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, wanted to go upstairs to find a toy. Tim looked at the big stair and felt scared. He was not sure if he could climb it all by himself. Spot looked at Tim and said, "Trust me, I will help you." Tim and Spot started to climb the stair together. They went up one step, then another, and another. Tim was happy that Spot was with him. The stair was very big, but with Spot's help, it was clear that they could do it. When they reached the top of the stair, Tim found his toy. He was so happy and gave Spot a big hug. "Thank you, Spot!" Tim said. "I knew we could do it because I trusted you." From that day on, Tim and Spot were always there to help each other.
