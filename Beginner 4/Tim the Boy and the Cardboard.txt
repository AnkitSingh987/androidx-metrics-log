Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Cardboard

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with cardboard. One day, he decided to make a big cardboard house. Tim felt a little anxious, but he knew he could do it. Tim asked his friend, Sam, to help him. "Sam, can you help me make a cardboard house?" Sam said, "Yes, Tim, I would love to help you!" Together, they started to make the house. They used big pieces of cardboard and tape. As they worked, they talked and laughed. Soon, the house was finished. Tim and Sam were very happy. They went inside the cardboard house and played all day. Tim was not anxious anymore. He knew that with a little help, he could make anything.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play golf. He played with his dad in their big yard. It was a safe place for Tim to play. One day, while Tim was playing golf, he heard a ring. He looked around and saw a little bird. The bird was holding a tiny phone. Tim was surprised! He had never seen a bird with a phone before. The bird said, "Hi Tim, I can play golf too!" Tim laughed and said, "Okay, let's play together!" They played golf and had lots of fun. The bird was very good at golf. Tim and the bird became best friends and played golf every day.

Once upon a time, there was a cat. The cat was very compassionate. It loved to help others. One day, it met a small bird. The bird was sad because it could not fly high. "Can you help me?" the bird asked the cat. The cat thought for a moment and said, "Yes, I will help you." The cat found a way to shoot the bird up into the sky. The bird was so happy. It could now fly high like its friends. The bird thanked the cat for being so kind. From that day on, the cat and the bird were friends. They played and helped each other. The other animals saw this and learned to be compassionate too. And they all lived happily together.

Once upon a time, there was a special princess. She liked to play all day at the park. One day, she found the biggest and best swing in the park. It was so much fun, she could swing really high. Every day, the princess would go to the park and swing on the swing. She felt so happy, she would laugh and laugh. She even sang while she was swinging. All her friends started calling her the 'crazy princess'. The princess loved her swing so much she never wanted to leave. But one day a storm came and the swing broke. The princess was sad, no more swing for her. But all her friends came together and fixed the swing. And now the princess could swing as much as she wanted, and laugh even louder. The princess was very happy and the friends were glad too. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a new video. He was so happy to watch it. He wanted to show his best friend, Sam. Tim ran to Sam's house with the video in his hand. When Tim got to Sam's house, he said, "Sam, look! I have a new video. Let's watch it together!" Sam was excited too. They went to Sam's room to watch the video. But, there was a problem. The video was too big to fit in Sam's video player. Tim and Sam were sad. They wanted to watch the new video. They thought and thought. Then, Sam had an idea. "Let's go to my dad. Maybe he can help us." So, they went to Sam's dad and showed him the video. Sam's dad smiled and said, "Don't worry, boys. I have a bigger video player that will fit your new video." Sam's dad brought the bigger video player to Sam's room. The new video fit perfectly. Tim and Sam were so happy. They watched the new video together, laughing and having fun. From that day on, they always remembered to make sure their videos would fit before they tried to watch them.
