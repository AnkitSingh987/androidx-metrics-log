Category: Beginner
Title: True the Truck and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a big truck named True. True was a very useful truck. He loved to help his friends. One day, True saw his friend, a small car named Tiny. Tiny was sad. True asked, "Why are you sad, Tiny?" Tiny said, "I need to move all my toys to my new home, but I am too small." True wanted to help Tiny. So, he said, "Don't worry, Tiny! I will load all your toys in my big back and take them to your new home." Tiny was happy and said, "Thank you, True!" Together, they loaded all the toys into True's big back. Then, they went to Tiny's new home. True was very useful and made Tiny happy. From that day on, True and Tiny were best friends.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park on Sunday. They see many birds and flowers and trees. They have fun on the swings and the slide. One day, they see a big house near the park. It has a tall fence and a gate. They hear a dog barking inside. They wonder who lives there. "Maybe a rich person lives there," Lily says. "They have a lot of things and money." "Maybe they are not happy," Tom says. "They have no friends to play with." They walk to the gate and peek inside. They see a big garden and a pool. They also see a man sitting on a chair. He looks sad and lonely. He has a book in his hand, but he does not read it. "Hello," Lily says. "Can we play with you?" The man looks up and sees them. He smiles a little. He says, "Hello. Who are you?" "We are Lily and Tom. We play in the park. We like your house. It is very big and nice." "Thank you. My name is Mr. Brown. I live here alone. I have a lot of things and money, but I have no friends to play with. I am very bored and sad." "That is not good," Tom says. "You need friends to be happy. We can be your friends. We can play with you and your dog. We can share our toys and snacks. We can make you laugh and smile." "Really?" Mr. Brown says. "You would do that for me?" "Yes, of course," Lily says. "We like to make new friends. We can provide you with joy and fun. Can we come in?" Mr. Brown opens the gate and lets them in. He says, "Thank you. You are very kind and sweet. You are welcome to come in. You can play with me and my dog. You can use my garden and my pool. You can have anything you want. You are my new friends." Lily and Tom are very happy. They hug Mr. Brown and his dog. They say, "Thank you. You are our new friend. You are very generous and nice. You have a lot of things and money, but you also have a big heart. You are rich in many ways."

Once upon a time, there lived a wise old king. He had lots of luxury in his home but no friends to share it with. He was very lonely. One day, he heard a voice calling his name. It was a little girl! Her name was Amy, and she was just three years old. The king asked Amy why she was there. She said, "I saw your castle and knew that you must be wise. I came to be your friend." The king was so pleased that he invited Amy to stay in his palace. Together, they shared their luxury and enjoyed many happy days. The king was so wise that he chose to share his luxury with a friend. That made him the happiest king of all.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to her mom's sewing room. She saw many needles on the table. Amy wanted to choose a needle to help her mom sew a dress. She picked the smallest needle because it looked comfortable to hold. Amy's mom came into the room and saw her holding the needle. "Amy, be careful with the needle. It can poke you," her mom said. Amy nodded and put the needle back on the table. She felt happy that she could help her mom. Together, Amy and her mom sewed a beautiful dress using the comfortable needle. They laughed and talked as they worked. When they finished, Amy tried on the dress and it fit her perfectly. She twirled around, feeling so proud of the dress they made together.
