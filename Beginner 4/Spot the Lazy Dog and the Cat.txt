Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Lazy Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a lazy dog named Spot. Spot did not like to jog or play. He just liked to sleep all day. One day, Spot's friend, a cat named Fluffy, came to visit him. Fluffy wanted Spot to jog with her. "Spot, let's jog!" said Fluffy. But Spot just shook his head. He did not want to jog. Fluffy was sad, but she went to jog alone. While Fluffy was gone, Spot found a thermometer. He did not know what it was. He played with the thermometer and broke it. The pieces were sharp, and Spot got hurt. When Fluffy came back from her jog, she saw Spot hurt and crying. She tried to help him, but it was too late. Spot should have listened to Fluffy and gone for a jog. Then, he would not have found the thermometer and got hurt.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a toy cat. The cat was very old and had a dead leg. But Amy still loved her cat. One day, Amy found a small bell. She thought, "I will attach the bell to my cat's neck." So, she tied the bell to the cat's neck with a string. The bell made a nice sound when the cat moved. Amy and her cat played all day. The bell made Amy and her cat very happy. They were best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had an incredible notebook. He loved to draw in it every day. One day, he took his notebook to the park to draw. At the park, Tim saw a big tree. He thought it would be fun to climb it. So, he put his notebook down and started to climb. But something unexpected happened. A dog came and took Tim's notebook away! Tim felt regret for leaving his notebook on the ground. He climbed down the tree and ran after the dog. The dog stopped and dropped the notebook. Tim picked it up and saw that the dog had drawn a picture in it! The picture was of Tim and the dog playing together. Tim was happy and surprised. The dog had made a new friend and they played together all day. The unexpected twist was that the dog was an incredible artist too!

Once upon a time, there was a blue beetle. The blue beetle lived in a big garden. The garden had many flowers, trees, and bugs. One day, the blue beetle saw a little bird. The little bird was stuck in a net. The little bird was sad and scared. The blue beetle wanted to help and save the little bird. The blue beetle went to the net. He used his strong legs to break the net. The little bird was free and happy. The blue beetle and the little bird became good friends. They played together in the big garden every day.

Lily and Ben are playing with their toys in the living room. They have a lot of fun making noises and pretending to be animals. Lily is a lion and Ben is a monkey. They roar and jump and swing around the room. But then they hear their mom call them from the kitchen. "Lily, Ben, come here please. It's time for a snack." Mom sounds happy, but also a bit tired. Lily and Ben love snacks, but they don't want to stop playing. "Can we play a little more, mom?" Lily asks. "We are having so much fun." "Sorry, sweeties, but you need to eat something. And you also need to calm down a bit. You are making too much noise and mess." Mom says. "Come on, let's go to the kitchen. I have some cookies and milk for you." Lily and Ben sigh and put away their toys. They follow mom to the kitchen and sit on the chairs. Mom gives them each a plate with two cookies and a glass of milk. They say thank you and start eating. The cookies are yummy and the milk is cold and refreshing. "Did you have a good time playing?" Mom asks. "Yes, mom, we did. We were a lion and a monkey." Ben says. "That sounds fun. But you know, sometimes you need to be quiet and calm too. Like a cat or a bunny." Mom says. "Why don't you go to the couch and read a book or watch a show after you finish your snack? That will help you relax and rest." Lily and Ben nod. They like reading books and watching shows too. They finish their snack and hug mom. Then they go to the couch and pick a book and a show. They snuggle together and enjoy their calm time. Mom smiles and joins them on the couch. She is happy to see them happy.
