Category: Beginner
Title: Wooly the Sheep and the Bird

One day, a sheep named Wooly was in a hurry. Wooly was intelligent and liked to find new things. He ran to his friend, Bunny, and said, "Bunny, come see what I found!" Bunny hopped fast to follow Wooly. They went to a big tree. Wooly said, "Look up! I found a big nest with a baby bird!" Bunny looked up and saw the baby bird. But then, something unexpected happened! The baby bird jumped out of the nest and started to fly. Wooly and Bunny were surprised and happy. The baby bird flew down to them and said, "Thank you for finding me! I was lost, but now I can go home!" Wooly and Bunny smiled. They were happy to help the baby bird. They knew that being intelligent and kind was a good thing.

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play outside. One day, they saw a big package on their porch. It had their names on it. They ran to open it. Inside the package, there was a big slide. It was red and yellow and shiny. Tom and Lily were very happy. They thanked their mom and dad for the gift. They wanted to try the slide right away. They carried the slide to the backyard. They set it up on the grass. Tom went first. He climbed up the ladder and sat on the top. He looked down and smiled. He slid down the slide very fast. He laughed and said, "This is fun!" Lily waited for her turn. She climbed up the ladder and sat on the top. She looked down and frowned. She saw a big bug on the slide. It was black and hairy and ugly. It had many legs and eyes. Lily was scared. She screamed and said, "This is not fun!" Tom heard Lily scream. He ran to the slide and saw the bug. He was not scared. He picked up the bug and threw it away. He said, "Don't worry, Lily. It's just a bug. It can't hurt you. Come on, slide with me!" Lily calmed down. She saw that the bug was gone. She smiled and said, "Thank you, Tom. You are brave. I will slide with you." She slid down the slide very fast. She laughed and said, "This is fun!" Tom and Lily slid down the slide many times. They had a lot of fun. They loved their new slide. They forgot about the ugly bug. They were happy twins.

Once upon a time, there was a poor little cat. The cat was wet from the rain. It wanted to be dry and warm. The cat saw a big stage nearby. The stage was dry and warm. The cat went on the stage to get dry. A dog saw the cat on the stage. The dog wanted to be dry too. The dog said, "Can I come on the stage too, cat?" The cat said, "Yes, dog. We can both be dry here." Then, something unexpected happened. The stage began to move! The cat and the dog were scared. But, they were brave together. The stage took them to a warm, dry home. They were happy and became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a small kitchen, there was a little fork. The fork was always nervous because it was the smallest in the family. It wanted to be brave like the big forks and help people eat their food. One sunny day, a big spoon got stuck in a bowl of sticky jam. The big forks tried to rescue the spoon, but they were too big to fit in the bowl. The little fork saw this and knew it was time to be brave. It went to the bowl and tried to help the big spoon. With all its strength, the little fork pushed and pushed until the big spoon was free. The big spoon and the big forks were so happy and thanked the little fork for the rescue. From that day on, the little fork was never nervous again and felt proud to be small.

Once upon a time, there was a giant. He was very big and strong. He lived in a warm cave. The giant was very kind and loved to help others. One day, a little girl named Lucy came to the giant's warm cave. She was lost and scared. The giant saw her and said, "Don't be scared, I can help you." Lucy looked up at the giant and said, "Can you help me find my way home?" The giant smiled and said, "Yes, I can help you." The giant and Lucy started to walk. They walked and walked, and then they saw a big tree. The giant told Lucy, "Turn left here." But when they turned, they saw something unexpected. There was a big, colorful party! All the people from the town were there. They were all so happy to see the giant and Lucy. They had planned a surprise party for the giant to thank him for all his help. The giant and Lucy had so much fun at the party, and Lucy found her way home.
