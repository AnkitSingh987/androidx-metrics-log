Category: Beginner
Title: The Thick Tree and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a thick tree in a big, green forest. The tree was very tall and strong. One day, a little bird came and sat on a branch of the tree. The bird was happy and sang a pretty song. Suddenly, there was a loud snap! The branch broke and the bird fell down. It was scared and didn't know what to do. Just then, a nice squirrel came to help the bird. The squirrel picked up the bird and put it on a safe branch. That night, the squirrel and the bird had a party under the tree. They had a little candle to make light. They danced and played all night long. The bird and the squirrel became best friends and lived happily in the big, green forest.

Once upon a time, there was a dirty animal named Bob. Bob loved to play in the mud all day. He would jump and splash, getting dirt all over his body. Bob was a happy animal, but he was always dirty. One day, Bob's friend, Sue, came to visit. Sue was a clean animal who didn't like mud. She told Bob, "You need to stop playing in the mud. It will spoil your fun." Bob didn't want to spoil his fun, so he decided to try being clean for a day. Bob and Sue played together without the mud. They ran and jumped in the grass, and Bob stayed clean. At the end of the day, Bob realized that he could still have fun without being dirty. He decided to play in the mud less often, so he wouldn't spoil his fun with his friends.

One day, a little girl named Suzy went to the opera with her mom. Suzy saw a lady on the stage who looked sad. The lady was singing a song with a slow voice. Suzy asked her mom, "Why is the lady sad?" Her mom said, "She is sad because she lost her friend. She is singing to tell everyone how she feels." Suzy felt bad for the lady. She wanted to help her. Suzy had an idea. She stood up and started to wave at the lady. The lady saw Suzy and stopped singing for a moment. She smiled at Suzy and waved back. Then, she started to sing again, but this time, she was not so sad. Everyone at the opera clapped for the lady. Suzy felt happy because she helped the lady feel better. After the opera, Suzy and her mom went home. Suzy told her mom that she wanted to go to the opera again one day.

One day, a big dog named Max found a hat. The hat was red and very pretty. Max wanted to show the hat to his friend, a small cat named Lily. Max put the hat on his head and went to find Lily. Max saw Lily and said, "Look at my new hat! Do you like it?" Lily paused and looked at the hat. She was impatient and wanted to play. "Yes, it is nice. But can we play now?" she asked. Max and Lily played with a ball. They had a lot of fun. After playing, Max's hat fell off his head. They both laughed and went home, happy with their day.

Once upon a time, there was a big field. In the field, there was a lot of wheat. The wheat was tall and yellow. It was very pretty. In the field, there was a bossy bird. The bird liked to tell the other birds what to do. The other birds did not like the bossy bird. They wanted to play and have fun. One day, the bossy bird flew away. The other birds were happy. They could play and have fun now. The wheat in the field danced in the wind. The birds played and the wheat danced until the day had to end.

Once upon a time, there was a noisy dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and tease his friends in the park. One day, while playing, Spot saw a big red truck. It was a fireman's truck! Spot ran to the truck and saw a fireman named Tom. Tom was very nice and said, "Hi, Spot! Do you want to learn about being a fireman?" Spot barked and wagged his tail. Tom showed Spot how to use the hose to put out fires. Then, Spot heard a cat meow. It was stuck in a tree! Spot barked to tell Tom about the cat. Tom climbed the tree and saved the cat. The cat said, "Thank you, Tom and Spot!" Spot was happy to help and learned that being a fireman is fun.
