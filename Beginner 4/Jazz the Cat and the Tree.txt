Category: Beginner
Title: Jazz the Cat and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a busy town, there was a cat named Jazz. Jazz was a very happy cat. He loved to play with his ball and run around with his friends. One day, Jazz saw a tree and wanted to try to climb it. Jazz tried to climb the tree, but it was hard. He tried again and again. He was not going to give up. His friends, a dog and a bird, were watching him. They wanted to help Jazz. The dog tried to help Jazz by pushing him up the tree. The bird flew up and tried to lift Jazz by his tail. With the help of his friends, Jazz finally made it to the top of the tree. They were all very happy and had a fun day playing together.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They walked down the street and saw a big hotel. A wealthy man stood outside the hotel. He wore nice clothes and shiny shoes. Tim's mom said, "Look, Tim! That man is very wealthy." Tim wanted to see the hotel, so he asked his mom, "Can we go in the hotel, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but stay close to me." They went inside the hotel and saw many pretty things. As they walked, Tim saw a cat. The cat was very cute. Tim wanted to follow the cat. He said, "Mom, can I follow the cat?" His mom said, "Okay, but don't go too far." Tim followed the cat down a hall. Suddenly, the cat jumped into a big pot of gold! Tim couldn't believe his eyes. The wealthy man came running and said, "Wow! I didn't know my cat could find gold!" Everyone was very happy and surprised.

Ben likes to print pictures with his crayons. He prints cars, animals, and stars. He prints many pictures and puts them on his wall. He is proud of his pictures. One day, Ben's mom tells him that they have to go to a meeting. She says that the meeting is for grown-ups who work with books. Ben likes books, but he does not want to go to the meeting. He wants to stay home and print more pictures. "Can I bring my pictures to the meeting?" Ben asks his mom. "Sure, you can bring some of your pictures," his mom says. "Maybe you can show them to some of the people at the meeting. They might like them." Ben picks his favorite pictures and puts them in a folder. He goes with his mom to the meeting. The meeting is in a big room with many chairs and tables. There are many grown-ups who talk and write and look at books. Ben feels shy and bored. He sees a table with some paper and crayons. He asks his mom if he can print some more pictures. His mom says yes, but he has to be quiet and not make a mess. Ben goes to the table and starts to print. He prints a big rainbow, a happy sun, and a funny clown. Some of the grown-ups see Ben's pictures and smile. They come to the table and talk to Ben. They say that his pictures are very nice and colorful. They ask him his name and what he likes to print. Ben feels happy and tells them. They say that he is very talented and creative. They ask him if they can have some of his pictures. Ben says yes and gives them some of his pictures. They thank him and say that they will put them on their walls or desks. Ben's mom sees that Ben is having fun and making friends. She is proud of him. She tells him that he is very popular at the meeting. Ben smiles and prints some more pictures. He likes the meeting. He likes to print.

Sara and Tom were friends who liked to play together. One day, they saw a huge jewel in a shop window. It was shiny and red and looked like a big candy. "Wow, look at that!" Sara said. "I want it!" "Me too!" Tom said. "But it is too high and too expensive. How can we get it?" Sara had an idea. She saw a big rock near the shop. She said to Tom, "Let's use this rock to break the window. Then we can grab the jewel and run away." Tom was scared, but he wanted the jewel too. He said, "OK, let's do it. But we have to be fast and quiet." They picked up the rock and threw it at the window. The window shattered and the alarm went off. The shop owner came out and saw them. He was very angry and shouted, "Hey, you little thieves! Stop right there!" Sara and Tom tried to run away, but they were too slow. The shop owner caught them and called the police. The police came and took them to the station. They had to call their parents and tell them what they did. Their parents were very sad and mad. They had to pay for the window and the jewel. They also had to say sorry to the shop owner and the police. Sara and Tom felt very bad and ashamed. They wished they had never seen the huge jewel. They learned a hard lesson: stealing is wrong and has bad consequences.
