Category: Beginner
Title: The Alligator and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a small alligator. He lived in a pond with his mom and dad. The small alligator liked to play with his friends, the fish and the frogs. One night, the small alligator had a dream. In his dream, he saw a big cake with many colors. The cake was so big that all his friends could eat it too. The small alligator was very happy in his dream. When the small alligator woke up, he told his mom and dad about his dream. They all laughed and said it was a funny dream. The small alligator hoped that one day, his dream would come true, and he could share a big cake with all his friends.

Lily and Ben were playing in the sand with their spades. They made a big castle with towers and a moat. They were very happy and proud of their work. Then, a boy came and kicked their castle. He laughed and ran away. Lily and Ben were very sad and angry. They cried and shouted at the boy. "Hey, you are very bad and selfish!" Lily said. "Why did you break our castle? We worked hard on it!" The boy did not care. He said, "I don't like your castle. It is ugly and boring. I like to kick things and have fun. Go away!" Lily and Ben did not go away. They picked up their spades and chased the boy. They wanted to teach him a lesson. But their mom saw them and stopped them. She said, "Lily and Ben, what are you doing? You should not fight with other children. That is not nice. And you should not run with your spades. You could hurt someone or yourself." Lily and Ben said, "But mom, he broke our castle! He was very mean and selfish!" Mom said, "I know, and I am sorry. But fighting is not the answer. Maybe he does not know how to share and play nicely. Maybe he needs a friend. Maybe he is lonely or sad. You should try to be kind and forgiving, not angry and revengeful." Lily and Ben thought about what mom said. They felt a little sorry for the boy. Maybe he did need a friend. Maybe they could help him. They decided to go back to the sand and make a new castle. They invited the boy to join them. They said, "Do you want to play with us? We can make a big and beautiful castle together. We can use our spades and share our toys. We can have fun and be friends." The boy was surprised and curious. He said, "Really? You want to play with me? Even after I kicked your castle?" Lily and Ben nodded. They said, "Yes, we do. We are not selfish. We are generous and friendly. We want to give you a chance. Will you give us a chance?" The boy smiled. He said, "OK, I will. I am sorry for kicking your castle. That was not nice. I was bored and angry. I don't have many friends. Maybe we can be friends." Lily and Ben smiled back. They said, "That's OK. We forgive you. We don't have many friends either. Maybe we can be friends." They hugged and shook hands. Then, they started to play in the sand. They made a new castle, bigger and better than before. They used their spades and their imagination. They had fun and relaxed. They were happy and proud of their work. And they were happy and proud of their new friendship.

Once upon a time, there was a bright sun in the sky. It was a nice day. A little boy named Tim went outside to play with his ball. He saw a drain near his house. Tim was very curious about the drain. One day, Tim found a sharp stick near the drain. He thought it would be fun to poke the stick into the drain. He pushed the stick into the drain and heard a funny noise. The noise made Tim laugh. He played with the stick and the drain for a long time. Suddenly, Tim's mom came outside. She saw Tim playing with the sharp stick and the drain. She told Tim to be careful with the sharp stick. Tim listened to his mom and put the stick away. They went inside and had a snack. Tim had a fun day playing outside.
