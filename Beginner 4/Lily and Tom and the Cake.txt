Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Tom and the Cake

Lily and Tom were playing in the kitchen. They liked to pretend they were chefs and make yummy food. They used pots and pans and spoons and bowls. They also used some flour and sugar and eggs that they found in the cabinet. "Look, I made a cake!" Lily said, holding up a messy bowl. "Do you want some?" "Yes, please!" Tom said, smiling. He took a big spoonful and put it in his mouth. But it did not taste good. It was very bitter and sticky. He spat it out and made a face. "Blech! This is not a cake! This is yucky!" Lily was sad. She thought her cake was good. She started to cry. "Don't be mean, Tom! I worked hard on my cake! You are a bad brother!" Tom felt sorry. He did not want to hurt Lily's feelings. He hugged her and said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I was just joking. Your cake is very nice. You are a good sister." They heard a loud noise. It was their grandma, who was elderly and lived with them. She came into the kitchen and saw the mess. She was angry. She shouted, "What are you doing, children? You made a big mess! You wasted the food! You are in trouble!" Lily and Tom were scared. They ran to their room and hid under the bed. They hoped grandma would calm down soon. They decided to play something else next time.

Lily and Max are going to the zoo with Mom. They are very excited to see the animals. They have to take the subway to get there. The subway is a big train that goes under the ground. When they get to the subway station, they see a lot of people waiting. Mom says they have to be careful and hold her hand. She buys three tickets and they go through a gate. They walk down some stairs and wait on a platform. Soon, they hear a loud noise and see a bright light. The subway is coming! It is orange and shiny. It stops in front of them and the doors open. Mom says, "Come on, let's get in!" They follow her and find some seats. Lily and Max look out the window and see the dark tunnel. They hear the subway go "whoosh" and feel it move fast. They like the subway. It is fun. After a while, the subway stops again and the doors open. Mom says, "This is our stop. Let's get out!" They follow her and get off the subway. They walk up some stairs and see the sun and the sky. They also see a big sign that says "ZOO". Lily and Max are happy. They say, "Yay, we are at the zoo!" Mom smiles and says, "Yes, we are. Let's go see the animals!" They hold her hand and walk to the entrance. They can't wait to see the lions, the monkeys, the elephants, and more. They love the zoo. It is fun.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a mighty dog named Max. Max loved to watch cartoons on TV. His favorite cartoon was about a super dog who helped people. One day, Max saw a little girl named Lily. She was trying to carry a big box. Lily said, "Oh no, this box is too heavy for me." Max wanted to help Lily, just like the super dog in his favorite cartoon. Max went to Lily and helped her carry the box. Together, they took the box to Lily's house. Lily said, "Thank you, Max! You are a mighty and helpful dog." Max felt happy because he could help someone, just like the super dog in his favorite cartoon. The moral of the story is: Helping others can make you feel strong and happy, just like a mighty hero.

Mia was a shy girl who did not like to talk to new people. She liked to play with her dolls and draw pictures in her room. One day, her mom said they had to go to the store to buy some food. Mia did not want to go, but her mom said she had to. At the store, Mia saw a boy who was looking at some toys. He had a big smile and a red hat. He saw Mia and said hello. Mia felt scared and hid behind her mom. The boy came closer and said his name was Ben. He asked Mia what her name was and what she liked to play with. Mia did not say anything. She just looked at the floor. She saw a crack in the floor and wondered what was under it. Maybe there were bugs or worms or treasure. She wished she could go home and play with her dolls. Ben saw that Mia was shy and did not want to talk. He wanted to be nice and make her feel happy. He said he liked to play with cars and trains and planes. He said he had a lot of toys at home and he could share them with Mia if she wanted. He said he also liked to draw pictures and he had some crayons and paper in his backpack. Mia heard what Ben said and felt a little curious. She liked to draw pictures too. She looked at Ben's backpack and saw some colorful crayons sticking out. She wondered what kind of pictures he drew. She looked at his face and saw that he was still smiling. He did not look mean or scary. He looked friendly and fun. Mia decided to be brave and say something to Ben. She whispered her name and said she liked to play with dolls and draw pictures. She asked Ben what he drew with his crayons. Ben was happy that Mia talked to him. He said he drew animals and cars and stars. He asked Mia if she wanted to see his pictures and draw some with him. Mia nodded and smiled. She followed Ben to a corner of the store where there was a table and some chairs. Ben took out his backpack and showed Mia his pictures. Mia liked them and said they were good. Ben gave Mia some crayons and paper and said they could draw together. Mia took the crayons and paper and started to draw. She drew a doll and a flower and a heart. Ben drew a plane and a sun and a smiley face. They showed each other their pictures and said they liked them. They laughed and talked and became friends. Mia was not shy anymore. She was happy.
