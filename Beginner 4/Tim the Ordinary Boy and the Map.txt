Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Ordinary Boy and the Map

Once upon a time, there was an ordinary boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars and look at maps. One day, he found a big map in his room. Tim was very happy and wanted to show his friend, Sam. Tim took the map to Sam's house. They spread the map on the floor and saw that it was a map of their town. But, there was a problem. Tim's little sister, Lily, came into the room with a cup of water. She tripped and poured the water all over the map. The map was wet and the colors started to fade. Tim and Sam were sad and upset. They did not know what to do. Then, Tim had an idea. They took the wet map outside and let the sun dry it. After the map was dry, they used their crayons to color the map again. In the end, Tim and Sam were happy because they fixed the map together.

Once upon a time, in a big farm, there was a cow named Bessie. Bessie was a very wealthy cow. She had a lot of grass to eat and a big pond to drink from. Bessie was very happy on the farm. One day, a little boy named Timmy came to visit the farm. He saw Bessie and wanted to play with her. Timmy said, "Hi Bessie, can I play with you?" Bessie was happy to have a new friend. They played near the pond and had a lot of fun. As the sun started to set, it was time for Timmy to go home. Bessie said, "Bye Timmy, come back soon!" Timmy waved goodbye and promised to visit Bessie again. Bessie went back to her barn, feeling even more happy after making a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little sheep. The sheep had soft wool. The sheep lived on a big farm with many friends. They played all day in the sun. One day, the little sheep wanted to become big like its friends. It thought, "If I eat a lot of grass, I will grow big and strong." So, the little sheep ate and ate. Its wool became bigger and bigger. Soon, the little sheep became a big sheep. Its wool was so big that it could share with its friends. They were all very happy. The big sheep was proud of its big wool, and it played with its friends every day.

Once upon a time, there was a mechanic named Tom. He loved to fix cars and help people. One day, Tom was very tired and wanted to relax. So, he went to the park to sit under a tree. While Tom was at the park, he saw a big, dangerous snake. The snake was close to a little girl. Tom knew he had to help her. He slowly walked to the snake and caught it with a stick. The girl was safe now. But then, something unexpected happened. The snake started to talk! It said, "Thank you, Tom, for not hurting me. I am a magic snake and I will give you a wish." Tom wished for a big ice cream cone. The snake made his wish come true, and Tom enjoyed his ice cream while he relaxed at the park.

One day, a little dog named Spot was very filthy. He had been playing in the mud all day. Spot's mom saw him and said, "Spot, you need to take a shower. You are very dirty." Spot did not want to take a shower. He tried to run away, but his mom managed to catch him. She put him in the shower and turned on the water. Spot did not like the water, but he knew he had to get clean. While Spot was in the shower, a big frog jumped in with him. Spot was scared at first, but then he saw the frog was also filthy from the mud. They both laughed and played in the water together. Spot's mom heard them and said, "Well, I did not expect a frog to join you, but at least you are both clean now!"

One day, a little girl named Lily was very enthusiastic to travel with her mom to the store. On the way, they saw a big tree. Under the tree, there was a small tooth. Lily picked it up and showed it to her mom. "Mom, look! I found a tooth!" said Lily. Her mom looked at the tooth and smiled. "That's a special tooth, Lily. It must belong to the tooth fairy!" Lily was excited and wanted to give the tooth back to the tooth fairy. That night, she put the tooth under her pillow and went to sleep. When she woke up, she found a note next to the tooth. The note said, "Thank you for finding my tooth, Lily! Love, the tooth fairy." Lily was so happy that she helped the tooth fairy, and she couldn't wait to tell her mom about it.
