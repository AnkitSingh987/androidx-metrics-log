Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a girl named Mia. Mia loved to do things with her mom. One day, Mia and her mom wanted to make cocoa. They went to the kitchen and started to mix cocoa with milk. Mia's mom said, "Mia, you must share the cocoa with your friends. It is fair to share with others." Mia nodded her head and said, "Yes, mom. I will share with my friends." Mia and her mom made a big cup of cocoa. They went outside to find Mia's friends. But when they looked around, they saw a little bird shivering in the cold. Mia had an idea. She said, "Mom, let's give the cocoa to the bird. It will help it feel warm." Mia and her mom gave the cocoa to the bird. The bird drank it and felt warm. It was so happy that it started to sing a beautiful song. Mia's friends heard the song and came to see. They all shared the cocoa and had a great time. The moral of the story is: sharing is caring, and doing good things can make everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a young frog called Misty. Misty lived in a large pond. One day, she saw something strange floating on the surface of the water - it was a delicious, juicy worm! She tried to catch it with her tongue, but it kept slipping away. Just then, Misty spotted an old frog swimming over. "This worm will be hard to catch," said the old frog. "But I have a trick. You need to be patient. Try once again and the worm will be yours." Misty was excited and this time, she tried really hard. Sure enough, the worm was soon sitting in her mouth! The old frog was pleased and said, "You see, Misty, when you try your hardest, magic can happen!" The moral of the story is that when we try our best, we can achieve anything we set our minds to!

Lily and Tom are playing in the garden. They see a big box near the fence. They want to see what is inside. "Let's take the box and open it," Lily says. "OK, but it is very heavy. We need to be strong," Tom says. They try to lift the box, but it is too hard. They push and pull, but it does not move. "Maybe we can use something to help us," Lily says. She looks around and sees a cloth on the grass. It is red and soft. "I have an idea. We can tie the cloth to the box and pull it," she says. She runs to get the cloth and wraps it around the box. She gives one end to Tom and holds the other end. "Ready? One, two, three, pull!" she says. They pull the cloth with all their might. The box slides on the ground. They pull it to the middle of the garden. "Yay, we did it! We are so strong!" Tom says. They untie the cloth and open the box. Inside, they find a lot of toys and books. They are very happy. "Wow, look at all these things! They are so fun and pretty!" Lily says. "Maybe someone left them for us. They are very nice," Tom says. They play with the toys and read the books. They have a lot of fun. They share the cloth and use it as a cape, a blanket, and a flag. They thank the cloth for helping them. They are very good friends.

Louis was a brave boy who loved to explore. One day, he decided to go to the seaside. When he got there, he saw a big crane. He was curious about it and wanted to have a closer look. “Wow! That’s big!” he said in awe. He decided to go nearer to the crane, but it was very rough. He tried to reach the crane but he kept slipping and falling. Just then, a friendly seagull came over. “Don’t worry, Louis! Just fly like me!” it said. Louis was so excited. He flapped his arms and tried to fly, but he just couldn’t do it. The seagull was very understanding. “Why don’t you run as fast as you can?” it suggested. Louis listened and ran towards the crane. As he got closer, he could feel the roughness of the crane’s feathers. He smiled with satisfaction. He had conquered the crane!
