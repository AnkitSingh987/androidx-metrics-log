Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Messy Dog and the Park

One day, a messy dog named Spot arrived at a park. Spot loved to play in the mud. His collar was always dirty. At the park, he saw a big tree with a swing. Spot saw a little girl named Amy near the swing. Amy said, "Hi, Spot! You are very messy! Let's play on the swing." Spot wagged his tail and jumped on the swing. Amy pushed him high in the air. Spot felt happy. After playing on the swing, Spot and Amy ran around the park. They played with a ball and laughed. When it was time to go home, Amy said, "Bye, Spot! See you next time!" Spot barked and waved his tail. He couldn't wait to play with Amy again.

Sam was a patient at the hospital. He had a big cut on his leg and it hurt a lot. He did not like the hospital. It was cold and noisy and smelled bad. He wanted to go home and play with his toys. One day, he saw a door that said "exit". He thought that meant he could leave the hospital. He waited until the nurse was busy and then he ran to the door. He opened it and saw a big street with many cars and buses. He was scared, but he wanted to go home. He ran across the street, but he did not look both ways. He did not see the big truck that was coming fast. The truck driver saw him, but it was too late. He honked his horn and tried to stop, but he could not. He hit Sam with a loud crash. Sam felt a lot of pain and then he felt nothing. He did not go home. He did not play with his toys. He did not see his mom and dad again. He was gone. The nurse and the doctor and the truck driver were very sad. They wished Sam had not run away. They wished he had been more patient and more careful. They wished they could have helped him. But they could not. It was too late.

Once upon a time, in a big pond, there lived a happy otter named Ollie. Ollie loved to play all day with his friends. They would swim, jump, and laugh together. Ollie was always smiling and having fun. One day, Ollie found a pretty ribbon near the pond. It was red and shiny. He wanted to wear it, so he tried to tie it around his neck. But Ollie didn't know how to tie the ribbon. He asked his friends for help, but they didn't know how to tie it either. Then, a kind bird flew down from a tree and saw Ollie and his friends trying to tie the ribbon. The bird knew how to tie things and wanted to help Ollie. The bird used its beak to tie the ribbon around Ollie's neck. Ollie was so happy and thanked the bird. Now, Ollie and his friends played together in the pond, and Ollie wore his pretty red ribbon. Everyone was happy and had a great time.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big present with her name on it. Lily was very happy and excited. Lily opened the present and found a delicious cake inside. It was her favorite, a chocolate cake. She wanted to share her cake with her best friend, Tom. So, she went to Tom's house. When she got there, she said, "Tom, I have a present for us to enjoy. It is a delicious cake!" Tom was so happy and they both ate the cake together. They enjoyed the cake and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim wanted to improve his muscles. He wanted to be strong and big like his dad. Tim thought he had a good plan. One day, Tim saw a big rock. He thought it would be a good idea to lift it. But Tim was foolish. The rock was too big and heavy for him. Tim tried to lift the rock, but he could not. He felt sad. Tim's dad saw him and came to help. He told Tim that he needed to start with something small. Tim listened to his dad and started lifting little rocks. Slowly, Tim's muscles improved. He was happy and not foolish anymore.
