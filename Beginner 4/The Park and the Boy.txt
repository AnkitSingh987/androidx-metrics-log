Category: Beginner
Title: The Park and the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a pretty park. In the park, there were many kids who liked to play. One day, a big boy took a small girl's toy. The small girl was very sad. A nice boy saw what happened. He wanted to help the small girl. He went to the big boy and asked him to give the toy back. The big boy said no. The nice boy did not get mad. He had an idea. The nice boy offered the big boy his own toy. The big boy liked the new toy and gave the small girl's toy back. Everyone was happy. The kids learned that it is good to share and be kind to each other.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had a toy named Mr. Bear. Lily and Mr. Bear liked to play outside. They would display their toys on the grass and have a fun time. One day, Lily and Mr. Bear were playing in the park. They saw a boy with a peaceful face sitting alone. Lily said, "Hello, do you want to play with us?" The boy smiled and said, "Yes, please." They played together all day. But when it was time to go home, the boy took Mr. Bear and ran away. Lily was very sad and cried. She did not see Mr. Bear again. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a brave little girl named Lily. She loved to go on big adventures. One day, she decided to climb a mountain with her best friend, a fluffy bunny named Binky. Lily and Binky walked and walked until they saw the big mountain. They started to climb up, up, up. They were very brave. The mountain was very high, but they wanted to see what was at the top. When they got to the top, they found a big surprise. There was a big, shiny treasure chest. They opened it and found lots of yummy treats inside. They were so happy and shared the treats with each other. The brave little girl and her fluffy bunny friend had a fun day on the mountain.

Ben and Lily went to the hospital with their mom. They wanted to see their grandma, who was sick. Mom said grandma had a broken bone and needed an x-ray. Ben and Lily did not know what an x-ray was, but they wanted to help grandma feel better. They followed mom to a big room with a big machine. A nice lady in a white coat told them to wait outside. She said the machine would take a picture of grandma's bone. Ben and Lily were curious. They wanted to see the picture. They tried to peek through the door, but mom told them to shut the door. She said the machine made a bright light that could hurt their eyes. Ben and Lily shut the door and waited. They heard a beep and then the nice lady came out. She had a paper with a picture on it. She showed it to mom and said it was grandma's x-ray. Ben and Lily looked at the paper. They saw a white shape that looked like a stick. The nice lady said it was grandma's bone. She pointed to a dark spot and said it was where the bone was broken. She said grandma needed a cast to fix it. Ben and Lily felt sorry for grandma. They wanted to give her a hug and a kiss. They followed mom and the nice lady to another room, where grandma was lying on a bed. She smiled when she saw them. She said she was happy they came to visit. She said she was elderly and sometimes bones got weak. She said the cast would help her bone heal. She said she loved them very much. Ben and Lily hugged and kissed grandma. They said they loved her too. They said they hoped she would feel better soon. They said they liked her x-ray. They said they wanted to see her at home. Grandma said she would be home soon. She said she was proud of them for being brave and kind. She said they were the best grandchildren ever.
