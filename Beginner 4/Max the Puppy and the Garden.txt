Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Puppy and the Garden

One day, there was a puppy named Max. He was very lazy, so he laid down in the garden and took a nap. Suddenly, he heard a noise. It was a spinning noise, like wheels spinning! Max looked up and saw a girl, spinning around in circles, holding a leash. She was having lots of fun! Max wanted to join in, so he jumped up and started running around in circles with the girl. The leash spun around and around, faster and faster. Max felt so happy that his tail started wagging! After a while, the girl stopped spinning and got out of breath. Max was still feeling energetic, so he kept spinning around and around. He spun so much that he eventually got so dizzy he fell down! The girl smiled and laughed. She picked up Max's leash and said "time to go home, Max! You were very lazy, but now you sure had lots of energy to spin around like that!"

Anna and Ben were playing in the park. They saw some big equipment that looked like fun. It had slides, swings, ladders and tunnels. Anna wanted to try the slide, but Ben wanted to try the swing. "Come on, Anna, the swing is more fun!" Ben said. "No, the slide is more fun!" Anna said. They argued for a while, but then they saw a boy on the swing. He was swinging very high and fast. He looked happy and brave. "Wow, look at him!" Ben said. "He is so good at swinging!" Anna looked at the boy too. She thought he was foolish. He was swinging too high and fast. He could fall and hurt himself. "Ben, he is not good, he is foolish!" Anna said. "He is not safe on the swing!" But Ben did not listen to Anna. He wanted to swing like the boy. He ran to the swing and pushed the boy off. "Hey, that's not nice!" the boy said. "That's my swing!" But Ben did not care. He sat on the swing and started to swing. He tried to swing as high and fast as the boy. He felt happy and brave. Anna watched Ben from the slide. She felt worried and scared. She knew Ben was being foolish. He was not safe on the swing. "Ben, stop swinging so high and fast!" Anna said. "You could fall and hurt yourself!" But Ben did not listen to Anna. He wanted to swing higher and faster. He did not see the branch above his head. He swung into the branch and hit his head. He fell off the swing and landed on the ground. "Ow, ow, ow!" Ben cried. "My head hurts!" Anna ran to Ben. She saw blood on his head. She felt sorry and sad. She hugged Ben and said: "Ben, I'm sorry you hurt yourself. You should have listened to me. You were being foolish on the swing. You were not safe on the swing." Ben sobbed and said: "Anna, you were right. I was being foolish on the swing. I was not safe on the swing. I'm sorry I pushed the boy off. I'm sorry I did not listen to you." Anna and Ben learned a lesson that day. They learned to be careful and smart on the equipment. They learned to listen to each other and share. They learned to have fun and be safe.

One day, a cat and a dog were in the kitchen. The cat was stirring a big pot of soup. The dog was jealous because he wanted to stir the soup too. The cat said, "No, I am the one stirring the soup today." Suddenly, water started to come into the kitchen. It was a flood! The cat and the dog were scared. The water was getting higher and higher. They knew they had to do something to stop the flood. The dog had an idea. He said, "Let's work together to stop the flood!" They took the big pot of soup and put it on the floor. The water went into the pot, and the flood stopped. The cat and the dog were happy. From that day on, they were friends and shared everything, even stirring the soup.
