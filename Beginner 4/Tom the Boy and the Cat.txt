Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was a good boy who liked to play with his toys. One day, Tom found a pipe in his yard. He picked it up and looked at it. It was a very big pipe, and he thought it was fun to play with. Tom's friend, Sam, came over to play. Tom showed Sam the pipe and said, "Look! I found a big pipe! Let's play with it!" Sam looked at the pipe and said, "That looks fun! But, we must be careful not to spoil it." As they played with the pipe, something unexpected happened. A small, friendly cat jumped out of the pipe! Tom and Sam were very surprised. The cat looked at them and said, "Thank you for finding my home. I was lost, but now I am safe." Tom and Sam were happy to help the cat. They played together all day long and had lots of fun. And they learned that even when something unexpected happens, it can still be a good day.

Once upon a time, a little dog named Max went for a walk in the park. He saw a big box with a wrap on it. Max was very curious and wanted to see what was inside the box. Max barked, "Woof! Woof! What's in the box? I want to see!" His friend, a little bird named Mia, flew down from a tree and said, "Be careful, Max! It could be dangerous." Max didn't listen and opened the box. To his surprise, there was a big, shiny ball inside. Max and Mia played with the ball all day long. They were very happy and had so much fun. At the end of the day, Max and Mia were tired. They went back to their homes and said goodbye. Max learned that sometimes surprises can be fun, but he should always listen to his friends.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play in the garden. They have a big box of toys. They have dolls, balls, cars, and books. They have fun with their toys. One day, they see a rabbit in the garden. The rabbit is white and fluffy. It has long ears and a pink nose. It hops around the grass. Tom and Lily want to play with the rabbit. They think the rabbit is cute. They look for something to give the rabbit. They want the rabbit to like them. They find a carrot in the kitchen. They know rabbits like carrots. They take the carrot to the garden. They hold the carrot out to the rabbit. The rabbit sees the carrot. It smells the carrot. It comes closer to Tom and Lily. It nibbles the carrot. It makes a happy sound. Tom and Lily smile. They pet the rabbit. The rabbit lets them. They make a new friend. Tom and Lily are helpful. They share their carrot with the rabbit. They play with the rabbit. They are kind to the rabbit. The rabbit is happy. They have a good day.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat and a little dog. They were not friends. They would always fight. The cat was naughty and liked to take the dog's food. One day, the cat said, "I will take your food and fill my tummy!" The little dog was sad. He did not want to fight the big cat. He just wanted to eat his food. But then, something unexpected happened. A big bird came down from the sky. The bird said, "Stop being naughty, cat! No more fights!" The cat was scared of the big bird. The bird helped the dog fill his tummy with food. The cat and the dog stopped fighting and became friends. They all lived happily together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim wanted to go to the market with his mom. His mom said, "Yes, I permit you to come with me." Tim was happy and they went together. At the market, Tim saw a dirty dog. The dog was sad and alone. Tim asked his mom, "Can we take the dog home?" His mom thought about it and then said, "Yes, we can help the dirty dog." But when they got close to the dog, they saw it was not a dog at all. It was a big, dirty stuffed animal! Tim and his mom laughed. They took the stuffed animal home and cleaned it. Now, Tim has a new friend to play with.
