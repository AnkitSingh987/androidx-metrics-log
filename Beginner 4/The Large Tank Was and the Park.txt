Category: Beginner
Title: The Large Tank Was and the Park

One day, a large tank was in the park. It was big and green. The tank did not move. It was just sitting there. A little boy named Tim saw the tank. He wanted to play with it. Tim walked up to the tank and said, "Hi, tank! Do you want to play with me?" The tank did not say anything. Tim was not scared. He climbed on top of the tank and played. Then, a girl named Sue came to the park. She saw Tim on the tank. Sue was scared of the tank. She said, "Tim, that tank is big! It scares me!" Tim said, "Don't be scared, Sue. The tank is nice. It does not move. Come play with me!" Sue went to the tank and played with Tim. They had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a big red ball. The ball loved to bounce all day long. One day, the ball bounced into a garage. The garage was big and dark. The ball was not scared because it knew it was harmless. Inside the garage, the ball met a toy car. The toy car was blue and shiny. The ball said, "Hi, I am Red Ball. I like to bounce. Do you want to play with me?" The toy car said, "Yes, I am Blue Car. Let's play together." So, Red Ball and Blue Car played together. They had so much fun. Red Ball would bounce high, and Blue Car would race fast. They played in the garage all day long. They became best friends and had many more fun days together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Olive. Olive loved to play in the sun and watch things grow. She had a garden with many pretty flowers. Olive was always happy and generous, sharing her flowers with her friends. One day, Olive found a small seed. She thought, "I will plant this seed and help it grow!" So, she dug a little hole in the ground, put the seed in, and covered it with dirt. Olive watered the seed every day and waited for it to grow. Soon, a tiny green plant came out of the ground. Olive was so happy! She kept taking care of the plant, and it grew bigger and bigger. Olive shared the beautiful flowers from the plant with her friends. They all loved the flowers and thanked Olive for being so generous. And Olive felt very happy watching her garden grow.

John was a little boy. He went to school every day. Today his teacher asked him to spin around in the playground. John was happy and he spun and spun in circles till his head was spinning. John's Mom saw him from the window and screamed, "John! Stop spinning!" John stopped and looked up. He noticed his Mom was pointing at something. He looked down and saw his shoes were very dirty. John felt sad and said, "I'm sorry Mom, I didn't mean to get so dirty." His Mom smiled and said, "That's ok. It's time to go home and wash those shoes so they are clean again". John nodded and skipped back inside the school. He couldn't wait to have his shoes sparkling clean again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a girl named Amy. Amy loved to have fun with her friends. One day, she found a box of pastels in her room. The pastels were very pretty, and Amy was very happy. Amy had a rare idea. She wanted to make a big, beautiful drawing with her pastels. She asked her friends to come and help her. They all came and started to draw on a big piece of paper. They drew flowers, trees, and animals. When they were done, their drawing was very pretty. They showed it to their moms and dads, who were very proud. Amy and her friends had a fun day with the rare pastels, and they made a beautiful drawing together.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He wore his regular shoes with long strings. Before he played, he had to tie his shoes. He was very good at it. At the park, Tim saw a big jar of jam. He loved jam a lot. He wanted to eat it. He opened the jar and found a note inside. It said, "I am magic jam. Say a wish, and I will make it true." Tim was very surprised. He thought for a moment and then said, "I wish for a big, fun toy!" The jam started to glow, and in a flash, there was a big, fun toy in front of him. Tim played with his new toy and was very happy. The magic jam made his wish come true.
