Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Sunshine

Once upon a time, in a quiet little town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the sunshine. Every day, he would run and jump in the park with his friends. One day, Tim found a spray bottle. He thought it would be fun to spray water at his friends. They laughed and played, spraying each other with water in the warm sunshine. But then, Tim sprayed water at a big dog. The dog did not like it. The dog chased Tim and bit him. Tim was sad and hurt. The sunshine did not feel happy anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny who loved to hop. One sunny day, the bunny went to play in the park. As he hopped around, he found a tent. The tent was big and blue. The bunny was very happy. The bunny went inside the tent to play. He hopped and hopped all day long. The sun was hot, but the tent kept the bunny cool. The bunny was grateful for the tent. He knew it was a special place to play. At the end of the day, the bunny said goodbye to the tent. He hopped all the way back home. The bunny told his mom and dad about the tent. They were happy that the bunny had a fun day. The bunny was grateful for the tent and his day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a mysterious witch. She lived in a big house in the woods. She had a black cat and a big hat. The witch knew a lot of things, but she was nice to everyone. One day, a little girl named Sue went to the woods. She saw the witch's house and knocked on the door. The witch opened the door and smiled at Sue. "Do you know how to find my lost ball?" asked Sue. The witch nodded and said, "I can help you." The witch took Sue's hand, and they walked through the woods. They found the ball under a big tree. Sue was happy and thanked the witch. The witch smiled and said, "You're welcome, Sue." From that day on, Sue and the witch were good friends, and they played together in the woods.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play with her mom's makeup. She would put it on her face and pretend to be a princess. But one day, her mom said, "Lily, you can't have my makeup anymore. It's not for little girls." Lily was very sad. She wanted to have her mom's makeup so much. She thought of a plan to get it back. When her mom was not looking, Lily took the makeup and ran to her room. She put it on her face and smiled. She felt like a princess again. But then, Lily heard a loud noise. It was her mom! Her mom found out that Lily took the makeup. She was very upset. Lily knew it was wrong to take the makeup without asking. She said sorry to her mom and promised not to do it again. Her mom hugged her and said, "It's okay, Lily. When you are older, you can have your own makeup." And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very organized. He liked to put his toys in a big box when he did not play with them. One day, Tim's mom told him she would leave to buy treats. Tim was very happy because he loved treats. While Tim's mom was gone, Tim played with his toys. After a while, he got tired and put his toys back in the box. Just then, his mom came back with a big bag of treats. Tim was so excited! He opened the bag and found a big, yummy cookie. He took a bite and smiled. But then, something unexpected happened. Tim's toy box started to move! The toys jumped out and started to dance. Tim could not believe his eyes. The toys wanted a treat too! Tim laughed and shared his cookie with the toys. They all had a fun time eating treats and playing together.
