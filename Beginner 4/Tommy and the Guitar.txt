Category: Beginner
Title: Tommy and the Guitar

Tommy loved to play with his guitar. He liked to make loud noises and sing silly songs. He did not care if his guitar was old and broken. He thought it was fun and special. One day, he saw a new guitar in the shop window. It was shiny and red and had many strings. Tommy wondered how it would sound. He wanted to try it. He asked his mom if he could have it. "Sorry, Tommy, we don't have enough money for that guitar. Maybe you can save some of your allowance and buy it later," his mom said. Tommy was sad. He did not want to wait. He wanted the new guitar now. He thought his old guitar was boring and ugly. The next day, he saw a boy playing with his old guitar in the park. The boy was smiling and laughing. He made beautiful noises and sang happy songs. He looked like he was having fun. Tommy felt curious. He went to the boy and said, "Hi, I'm Tommy. That's my guitar. How did you get it?" The boy said, "Hi, I'm Danny. I found your guitar in the trash. Someone threw it away. I was looking for something to play with. I like your guitar. It's fun and special." Tommy felt ashamed. He realized he had thrown away his guitar in the trash. He wanted the new guitar so much that he did not care about his old guitar. He was not grateful for what he had. He said, "I'm sorry, Danny. I was wrong. I should not have thrown away my guitar. It was a gift from my grandpa. He taught me how to play it. It has a lot of memories. Can I have it back?" Danny said, "Sure, Tommy. You can have it back. But can we be friends and play together? I don't have many toys. And I don't have many friends." Tommy said, "Yes, Danny. We can be friends and play together. You can share my guitar. And I can share my other toys. And maybe we can save some money and buy the new guitar together. What do you think?" Danny said, "I think that's a great idea. Thank you, Tommy. You are very kind." Tommy said, "Thank you, Danny. You are very smart." They hugged and played with the guitar. They learned to appreciate what they had and to share with others. They were happy.

One day, a kind dog named Max went for a walk. He liked to discover new things. He walked near a big tree and heard a small sound. Max looked up and saw a little bird with a hurt ear. "Hi, little bird. What happened to your ear?" Max asked. "I fell from my nest and hurt my ear," the bird said with a sad face. Max wanted to help the bird. He had an idea. He stood tall on his back legs and let the bird jump on his head. Then, Max walked near the tree and the bird jumped back into the nest. "Thank you, Max," the bird said, now happy. "You are very kind." Max felt happy too. He had made a new friend and discovered that he could help others. From that day on, Max and the bird played together every day.

One day, a little girl named Lucy was playing in her room. She loved to play with her toys and her long hair. She would make her hair into different shapes and styles. But today, Lucy felt frustrated because her hair was not staying in place. Lucy's mom heard her and came to help. She tried to make Lucy's hair look pretty, but Lucy was not happy. She wanted her hair to look perfect. Lucy's mom told her to be careful and not to pull her hair too hard, but Lucy did not listen. As Lucy played with her hair, she stumbled and fell. She cried because she was hurt and her hair was a mess. Her mom tried to help her, but Lucy was still frustrated. She did not learn to be careful and listen to her mom. In the end, Lucy's hair was still messy, and she was sad.
