Category: Beginner
Title: Leo the Loud Leopard and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a loud leopard named Leo. He lived in the forest with his friends. Leo loved to run and play with his friends, but he was very loud. When he ran, his big feet made a lot of noise. His friends didn't like it when he was loud. One day, Leo found a little bird who was sad. The bird told Leo that she lost her family. Leo wanted to help the little bird. He said, "I hope we can find your family!" So, Leo and the little bird went on an adventure to find her family. As they looked for the bird's family, Leo tried to be quiet. He didn't want to scare away the bird's family with his loud noises. They looked high and low, and finally, they found the bird's family in a big tree. The little bird was so happy and thanked Leo for helping her. Leo was happy too, and he learned that sometimes, it's good to be quiet.

Sam and Ben are brothers. They like to play with their gear. They have cars, trucks, trains and planes. They make loud noises and zoom around the room. "Vroom, vroom, beep, beep!" Sam says as he drives his car. "Choo, choo, honk, honk!" Ben says as he pushes his train. Mom comes in and sees the mess. She is not happy. She says, "Boys, it is time to clean up. Put your gear in the box. Now!" Sam and Ben do not want to clean up. They think it is boring. They want to play more. "Mom, can we play for five more minutes?" Sam asks. "No, you have to clean up now. It is almost dinner time. And you have to wash your hands too." Mom says. Sam and Ben sigh. They do not like to wash their hands. They think it is boring too. They put their gear in the box. They drag their feet to the bathroom. They wash their hands with soap and water. "Good job, boys. Now you can come to the table. We have pizza for dinner." Mom says. Sam and Ben smile. They like pizza. They think it is yummy. They run to the table. They forget about their boring gear and their boring cleaning. They eat their pizza and talk to Mom. They are happy.

One day, a girl named Sue wanted to bake a cake. She put all the things she needed in a big bowl. She mixed and mixed until the cake mix was wet. Then, she put the mix in the oven to bake. While the cake was baking, Sue's friend Sam came over. They played with toys and had fun. When the cake was done, Sue took it out of the oven. She said, "Let's put the cake in the cooler so it can get cold and yummy!" Sam said, "Yes, I like cold cake!" They put the cake in the cooler and went to play more. But when they came back, the cake was gone! They looked and looked but could not find it. Then, they saw their dog, Max, with cake on his face. Max had eaten the cake! Sue and Sam laughed and said, "Silly Max, you ate our cake!"

Once upon a time there was a mommy and a little boy. The little boy wanted to go somewhere special. Mommy said they could go to the restaurant. The restaurant was a noisy place. People talked and laughed, dishes clattered and music played. The little boy watched all the people and smiled. Mommy and the little boy went to the tables. Mommy pointed out all the yummy things on the menu. The little boy couldn't decide what to eat. Eventually, he chose his favorite - macaroni and cheese. Mommy and the little boy enjoyed their meal. They ate until they felt full. Afterward, they hugged and said goodbye to the restaurant. On their way home, the little boy couldn't stop talking about how much fun he had.

Tom liked to play with his toy rocket. He pretended it could fly to space and see many stars and planets. One day, he put his teddy bear inside the rocket and said, "Bye bye, teddy. Have fun in space!" He pushed a button and the rocket made a loud noise. It zoomed up and up and up. Tom watched it go higher and higher until he could not see it anymore. He clapped his hands and smiled. He was proud of his rocket. But then he felt sad. He missed his teddy bear. He wanted to hug him and play with him. He wondered if teddy was lonely in space. He hoped he would come back soon. He waited and waited and waited. He looked at the sky and tried to find his rocket. He did not see it. He started to cry. He thought he had lost his teddy bear forever. Then he heard another loud noise. He saw a big shiny thing in the sky. It was coming down fast. It looked like a flying saucer. It had lights and windows and a door. It landed in his backyard. Tom was scared and curious. He did not know what it was. The door opened and a friendly voice said, "Hello, little boy. We have something for you." Tom saw two green creatures come out of the flying saucer. They had big eyes and antennas and smiles. They were holding his teddy bear. They gave him his teddy bear and said, "We found him in space. He was floating and lost. We rescued him and brought him back to you. He is a brave and cute teddy bear. He told us you are his best friend." Tom was happy and surprised. He hugged his teddy bear and thanked the green creatures. He said, "You are very kind and smart. Thank you for rescuing my teddy bear. Do you want to play with me and my rocket?" The green creatures nodded and said, "Yes, we would love to play with you and your rocket. We like to explore space and make new friends. Maybe we can show you some unknown planets and stars." Tom smiled and said, "That sounds fun. Let's go to space together!" He invited the green creatures to his rocket and they all got inside. He pushed the button and the rocket made a loud noise. It zoomed up and up and up. They went to space and had a great adventure.
