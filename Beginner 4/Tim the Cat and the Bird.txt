Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Tim. Tim was an independent cat who liked to go on walks all by himself. One day, Tim went for a walk and saw a big hedge. It was very tall and green. Tim wanted to see what was on the other side of the hedge. He started to climb up, but he began to struggle. Tim tried and tried, but he could not get to the top. He was very sad. Just then, a friendly bird named Sue flew by. Sue saw Tim and asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim told her about the hedge and how he could not get to the other side. Sue said, "Don't worry, Tim. I can help you!" Sue flew over the hedge and told Tim about the beautiful garden on the other side. Tim was happy and thanked Sue for her help. From that day on, Tim and Sue became best friends and explored the world together.

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn boy named Tim. He was a youth who liked to play with his friends. They played games like tag and hide-and-seek. One day, Tim and his friends found a big log. They decided to split it. They pushed and pulled, but they could not move the log. Tim was very stubborn and did not want to give up. Finally, they asked a big, strong man to help them. With his help, they split the log. Inside, they found a lot of bugs and a pretty stone. Tim and his friends were very happy. They learned that sometimes it is good to ask for help.

One day, a girl named Mia went with her mom to unpack a big box. Inside the box, Mia found a small bag with unknown stuff. She asked her mom, "What is this?" Her mom looked at the bag and said, "This is coffee. It is for grown-ups to drink in the morning." Mia wanted to be a grown-up too. So, when her mom was not looking, she took the coffee and ran to the kitchen. She put the coffee in a cup and filled it with water. But, when she tried to drink it, it tasted bad! Mia made a funny face and her mom came in. Her mom saw the cup and laughed. "Mia, you need to make the coffee first. But, you are too young for coffee. Let's make hot cocoa instead." They made hot cocoa together and enjoyed their drink. Mia learned that some things are just for grown-ups.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went to play outside. They saw a big, expensive tent in the yard. Tim and Spot wanted to have fun, so they went into the tent. They played with their toys and had a great time. As they played, they heard a strange noise outside the tent. Tim and Spot were scared. They peeked out and saw a big, fluffy bunny. The bunny wanted to escape from a mean cat that was chasing it. Tim and Spot wanted to help the bunny. Tim had an idea. He and Spot made a loud noise to scare the mean cat away. The cat ran away, and the bunny was safe. The bunny was happy and jumped into the tent to play with Tim and Spot. They all had fun together, and the bunny became their new friend.

Once upon a time, in a tiny town, there lived a happy boy named Tom. Tom loved to play with his toy arrow. He would run and jump with it all day long. He was always glad when he had his arrow. One day, Tom's mom asked him to carry a big bag of apples to their friend's house. Tom wanted to take his arrow too. So, he put the arrow in the bag with the apples and carried it on his back. As Tom walked, he tripped on a rock and the bag fell. The apples and arrow rolled out onto the ground. Tom was sad as he picked up the apples, but he could not find his arrow. He looked and looked, but it was gone. Tom was not glad anymore.
