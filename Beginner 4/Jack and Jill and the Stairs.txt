Category: Beginner
Title: Jack and Jill and the Stairs

Once upon a time, there were two friends called Jack and Jill. They always did fun things together. One day, Jack and Jill decided to play a game. Jack told Jill to go upstairs and he said he would send something. Jill was very excited for the surprise. She ran to the top of the stairs and waited. Soon, Jack appeared holding something sweet in his hands! He ran up the stairs and gave it to Jill. It was a delicious cake. Jill was so happy, she jumped with joy. Then, she and Jack shared the cake and laughed. They had a sweet time playing together!

Ben and Mia liked to watch movies on the big TV. They had many movies with animals and colors and songs. One day, they wanted to watch a movie with a white bear and a girl. They asked Mom if they could watch it. Mom said, "Yes, you can watch the movie, but only for a little while. Then you have to do your homework and help me with dinner. And don't make a mess, please." Ben and Mia said, "OK, Mom, we will be good." They put the movie in the TV and sat on the couch. They saw the white bear and the girl on the screen. They liked the movie. It was fun and happy. But then, something bad happened. A man with a gun came to the forest where the white bear and the girl lived. He wanted to hurt the white bear and take him away. The girl tried to protect the white bear. She shouted, "No, no, leave him alone! He is my friend!" The man did not listen. He shot the white bear with his gun. The white bear fell down and did not move. The girl cried and ran to the white bear. She hugged him and said, "Please, please, wake up! Don't leave me!" Ben and Mia saw this and they were very sad. They did not like the movie anymore. They wanted the white bear to be alive and happy with the girl. They turned off the TV and ran to Mom. They told her what they saw. They asked her, "Why did the man hurt the white bear? Why did the movie end like that?" Mom hugged them and said, "I'm sorry, my loves. I did not know the movie was like that. It was a very sad and bad movie. Sometimes, people do bad things to animals and other people. It is not right and it is not fair. But we can try to protect the animals and the people we love. We can be kind and good and help them. Do you understand?" Ben and Mia nodded, but they still felt sad. They wished the movie had a different ending. They wished the white bear and the girl were happy. They wished the man with the gun was not bad. They wished the world was not bad.

Once upon a time, there was a small group of animals. They all lived in a big forest. The group had a big bird, a small cat, and a funny dog. They were all friends and liked to play together. The big bird could fly high in the sky, the small cat could climb trees, and the funny dog could dig holes in the ground. One day, they found a broken house in the forest. The big bird said, "Let's settle here and make this our home." The small cat and the funny dog agreed. They were excited to have a new place to live. But the broken house looked old and not safe. The small cat said, "We need to fix it first." The funny dog said, "Yes, we can do it together." They all started to work on the broken house. The big bird tried to fix the roof, the small cat tried to fix the walls, and the funny dog tried to fix the floor. They worked hard all day long. But the broken house was too old and not safe. They didn't know that something bad was going to happen. As they were working, a big storm came. The wind was very strong, and it blew the broken house down. The big bird, the small cat, and the funny dog were all very sad. Their new home was gone, and they had nowhere to live. The group of friends learned that they should have listened to the small cat when she said they needed to fix the house first. Now, they had to find a new place to settle and start all over again. But this time, they would make sure their new home was safe and strong, so nothing bad would happen again.
