Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Fast Dog and the Apple

One day, a fast dog named Max wanted to have a race. He asked his friend, a slow turtle named Tim, to race with him. Tim said, "Okay, let's race!" Before the race, Max and Tim had to pack. Max packed a sweet apple, and Tim packed a sour lemon. They were ready for the race. They stood at the start line and began to run. Max was very fast, and Tim was very slow. Max thought he would win the race, so he stopped to eat his sweet apple. But when he bit into it, he found out it was a sour lemon! Max made a face, "Yuck! This is not my apple!" While Max was busy with the sour lemon, Tim passed by him slowly. Tim won the race! Max was sad, but he learned that sometimes, slow and steady wins the race.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play ball with his friends. One sunny day, they went to the park to play a fun game. They wanted to see who could score the most points. During the game, Tim saw a wide tree. He thought it would be a good place to hide from his friends. But as he ran to the tree, he stumbled on a rock. "Ouch!" cried Tim, as he fell on the ground. His friends came running to help him. "Are you okay, Tim?" asked his friend, Sam. Tim nodded and smiled. "Yes, I am okay. Let's keep playing!" So, Tim and his friends played ball until the sun went down, and they all had a great time.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to make a yummy treat. He asked his mommy to help him bake a cake. They put all the things they needed in a big bowl. They mixed it all up and it was fun. Tim liked the taste of the cake mix on his fingers. Then, they put the cake in the oven to cook. Tim was excited, but he had to wait. He felt frustrated because he wanted to eat the cake now. His mommy told him to be patient and that the cake would be ready soon. After a while, they took the cake out of the oven. But, oh no! The cake was burned! Tim was sad, but his mommy said they could try again. They made a new cake and this time it was perfect. Tim was happy and he shared the yummy cake with his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a famous engine. It was big and noisy, but it was also very reliable. The engine worked really hard, helping lots of people in the town. One day, the engine was tired and could barely turn its wheels. No matter how hard it tried, it could not start. People were sad, as they really needed the engine to keep going. Then, a small girl called Ami came up with an idea. She asked the engine, "How can I help?". The engine started to think, and then it said, "If you get some of your friends to help, then I should be able to start again!" Ami smiled and ran off to find some friends. Soon, lots of children had arrived to help the engine. With all the help, the engine was able to start again! The moral of the story is that working together can help us when we are stuck. We all need a helping hand to get things done. So why not ask your friends or family for help when you need it?

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a big, interesting hat. She loved her hat very much. One day, she went to the park to play with her friends. They all liked her hat too. At the park, a big wind came. It blew Sue's hat off her head! Sue was sad and said, "Oh no! My hat!" Her friends said, "Don't worry, Sue! We will help you find it!" They all started to look for the hat. They looked under the slide, behind the swings, and in the sandbox. Suddenly, a small puppy came running up to them. The puppy had Sue's hat in its mouth! Sue said, "Hurry, puppy! Give me my hat back!" The puppy dropped the hat, and Sue picked it up. She was so happy to have her hat back. She thanked the puppy and her friends. They all played together in the park, and Sue made sure to hold onto her hat tightly.
