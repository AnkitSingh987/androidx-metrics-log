Category: Beginner
Title: The Peanut and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a small peanut. The peanut was very grumpy. He lived in a big tree with his friends. He did not like the cold, and it made him shiver. One day, a squirrel came to the tree. The squirrel saw the grumpy peanut and said, "Why are you so grumpy, little peanut?" The peanut replied, "I am cold, and it makes me shiver. I don't like it." The squirrel felt sad for the peanut. He said, "I will help you. Let's find a warm place for you to live." The squirrel and the peanut looked for a warm place. They found a cozy hole in the tree. The peanut was happy and stopped shivering. He was not grumpy anymore. They became good friends and lived happily in the tree.

Ben and Mia like to play with blocks. They build a big tower with many colors. They are very proud of their tower. They want to show it to Mom and Dad. But Mom and Dad are busy. They are wrapping gifts for Grandma's birthday. They have paper, tape, and ribbons. They make the gifts look pretty. Ben and Mia have an idea. They want to wrap their tower too. They take some paper and tape from Mom and Dad. They do not ask. They think it is a surprise. They wrap their tower with the paper. They use a lot of tape. They make it very tight. They do not see their tower anymore. They only see paper. They run to Mom and Dad. They say, "Look, look! We have a gift for Grandma too! It is our tower!" Mom and Dad are surprised. They see the big paper ball. They do not see the tower. They are not happy. They say, "Oh, no! You took our paper and tape! You wrapped your tower! You ruined your tower and our paper!" Ben and Mia are sad. They do not understand. They say, "But we wanted to make Grandma happy! We wanted to give her our tower!" Mom and Dad sigh. They hug Ben and Mia. They say, "We know, we know. You are very sweet. But you should not take things without asking. And you should not wrap things that are not gifts. Your tower was beautiful. Grandma would have loved to see it. But now it is gone. Can you unwrap it?" Ben and Mia nod. They try to unwrap their tower. But it is hard. The tape is very sticky. The paper is very tight. They need help. Mom and Dad help them. They unwrap their tower. They see the blocks again. They are happy. They say, "Yay! Our tower is back! It is still beautiful!" Mom and Dad smile. They say, "Yes, it is. And we have an idea. Why don't we take a picture of your tower? Then we can send it to Grandma. She will see your tower and your smiles. She will be very happy." Ben and Mia like the idea. They pose with their tower. Mom and Dad take a picture. They send it to Grandma. Grandma is very happy. She calls them. She says, "Thank you, thank you! I love your tower and your gift! You are very clever and kind!" Ben and Mia are happy. They say, "You're welcome, you're welcome! We love you, Grandma!" They hug Mom and Dad. They hug each other. They play with their tower. They are careful. They do not wrap it again.

Once upon a time, there was a young cat named Dare. He was a very curious and playful cat. Dare loved to jump, run, and play with his friends in the yard. One day, Dare saw a big tree. He thought, "I want to climb that tree." But Dare was ignorant about how to climb trees. He did not know that it could be very hard and maybe even dangerous. Dare tried to climb the tree. He went up and up, but then he got scared. Dare was too high and did not know how to get down. His friends saw him and called for help. Soon, Dare's mom came and helped him get down from the tree. Dare learned that he should be careful and not do things he does not know about.
