Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Leaf and the Bird

One day, a big bossy bird sat on a tree. It saw a small leaf fall from the tree. The bird wanted to relax, so it told the leaf to stop falling. "Stop falling, leaf!" said the bossy bird. The leaf did not stop. A tiny ant heard the bird and the falling leaf. The ant wanted to help. It tried to push the leaf back up to the tree. The ant was very small, and the leaf was very big. The ant could not push the leaf up. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the leaf up into the air. The leaf flew back to the tree and stuck there. The bossy bird was happy and could relax. The ant was happy too. They both thanked the wind for helping them.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a clumsy dog named Mark. Mark loved to play outside in the dirt. One day, Mark went outside and played all day long. He got very dirty. Mark's owner, a nice lady, saw how dirty Mark was. She said, "Mark, you are so dirty! It's time to bathe!" Mark wagged his tail and went inside the house. The nice lady filled a big tub with warm water. Mark jumped in and she began to bathe him. Mark splashed the water and laughed, "Woof, woof!" Soon, Mark was clean and happy. The nice lady hugged Mark and they went outside to play some more.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a cheerful boy named Tim. He loved to play, laugh, and spend time with his friends. One day, Tim met a new friend named Gary. Gary was a friendly ghost. He was white and see-through, but he was not scary. Tim and Gary played all day long. They played with toys, ran in the park, and laughed a lot. Tim's friends were scared of Gary because he was a ghost. But Tim knew that Gary was a good friend. He told his friends not to be scared, and soon they all played together. In the end, Tim and his friends learned that it is not right to judge someone by how they look. They learned that even a ghost can be a good friend. They spent many happy days playing with Gary. The moral of the story is to be kind to everyone, no matter what they look like.

Once upon a time, there was a witch. She lived in a small house. The witch had a big chair. She liked to seat on it. The chair was her favorite. One day, the witch found a cat. The cat was dirty and disgusting. The witch felt bad for the cat. She took the cat home to clean it. The witch and the cat became good friends. They played and ate together. The cat was happy. The witch was happy too. And they lived happily ever after.

Lila liked to bake with her mom. They made cakes and pies and cookies. Lila loved to mix the flour and the eggs and the sugar. She also loved to lick the spoon and the bowl. One day, mom said they would bake something special. She said they would bake a port cake. Lila did not know what port was. Mom said it was a sweet drink that grown-ups liked. She said it made the cake very yummy. They baked the port cake and put it in a big tin. Mom said they would take it to grandma's house. Grandma was troubled. She was sad because grandpa was sick. Mom said the port cake would make her happy. They got in the car and drove to grandma's house. Lila held the tin on her lap. She smelled the port cake. It smelled good. She wanted to taste it. When they got to grandma's house, they saw grandpa in bed. He looked pale and tired. Grandma hugged mom and Lila. She thanked them for the port cake. She said it was very kind of them. They cut the port cake and gave a slice to grandpa. He smiled and took a bite. He said it was delicious. He said it made him feel better. Lila was glad. She also took a bite of her slice. She liked the port cake. She said it was sweet and moist. They all ate the port cake and talked and laughed. Lila saw that grandma was not troubled anymore. She was happy. Lila was happy too. She liked to bake with her mom. She liked to make people happy.
