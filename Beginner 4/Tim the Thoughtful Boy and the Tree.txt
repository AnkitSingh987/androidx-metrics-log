Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Thoughtful Boy and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful boy named Tim. Tim had a toy called a missile. He loved to play with it all the time. One day, Tim wanted to show his friends how he could release the missile. He went to the park and met his friend, Sue. Tim said, "Sue, look at my missile. I can release it, and it will fly far away." Sue was excited to see. Tim pushed the button, and the missile flew into the sky. But something unexpected happened. The missile hit a big tree and broke a branch. Tim and Sue were sad. They knew they should not have played with the missile near the tree. They learned that it is important to think about where to play and what to play with. From that day on, Tim and Sue were more careful when playing with their toys.

One day, there was a big parade in the town. All the kids were very happy. They wanted to be creative and make fun things for the parade. Tom, a little boy, wanted to make something special too. He told his friend, Sam, "I will change my bike for the parade." Tom worked hard to change his bike. He used colors, paper, and funny things. He was so excited to show his bike in the parade. Sam was excited too and said, "I can't wait to see your bike!" The day of the parade came. All the kids were ready with their creative things. But, when Tom went to get his bike, it was gone! He was very sad. Then, he saw Sam with a big smile. Sam had a surprise for Tom. Sam had changed his bike too, and he made it look like Tom's bike! They both laughed and rode their bikes in the parade together. The parade was so much fun and everyone loved their bikes.

Once upon a time, in a small house, a little boy named Tom lived with his dog, Max. Tom was a troubled child. He always had a sad face. Max, his dog, would always sit by his shoulder to make him feel better. One sunny day, Tom and Max went to the park. They saw a girl with a cat. The girl said, "Hi, my name is Sue. This is my cat, Kitty." Tom said, "Hi, my name is Tom. This is my dog, Max." Sue saw that Tom was troubled. She asked, "Why are you sad, Tom?" Tom said, "I don't know. I just am." Sue said, "Let's play together. Maybe you will feel better." So, Tom, Max, Sue, and Kitty played all day. They ran, they jumped, and they laughed. At the end of the day, Tom was not troubled anymore. He had a big smile on his face. He said, "Thank you, Sue. I feel happy now." Sue said, "You're welcome, Tom. Let's play together again soon." And from that day on, Tom, Max, Sue, and Kitty became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a great chair. A boy named Tim and a girl named Sue lived in that house. They both loved to sit on the great chair. One day, they both wanted to sit on the chair at the same time. They did not want to share. They began to fight over the chair. Tim and Sue's mom saw them fighting. She said, "You must pay for the chair if you want to sit on it." Tim and Sue did not have any money. They were sad. They wanted to sit on the great chair, but they could not pay for it. Then, Tim had an idea. He said to Sue, "Let's share the chair. We can both sit on it together." Sue agreed. They sat on the chair together and were happy. They learned that sharing is good and that fighting is not nice.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. He saw a lot of sand and thought it was boring. Tim didn't know what to do with the sand. He asked his mom, "What can I do with the sand?" His mom said, "I recommend making a sand castle. It is fun!" So Tim and his mom started to make a big sand castle. They used buckets and shovels to make it. They made tall towers and a big wall around the castle. After they finished the sand castle, Tim thought the beach was not boring anymore. He played with his mom and they had a lot of fun. They laughed and played until it was time to go home. Tim couldn't wait to come back to the beach and play in the sand again.
