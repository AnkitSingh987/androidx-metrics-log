Category: Beginner
Title: Fluffy the Cat and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was an elderly cat named Fluffy. Fluffy lived in a small house with a little girl named Lily. They were best friends and did everything together. Fluffy loved to think about all the fun things they could do each day. One day, Lily had an idea. She wanted Fluffy to have a bath with her. She filled the tub with water and put in her favorite shampoo. The shampoo smelled like flowers and made lots of bubbles. Fluffy was scared at first, but Lily was gentle and helped him into the tub. They played with the bubbles and splashed water. Fluffy started to like the bath and thought it was fun. When they were all clean, Lily wrapped Fluffy in a warm towel and they snuggled together. They were both happy and knew they would have many more fun days together.

One day, a little boy named Tim found an empty jar. He had an idea. He wanted to make a message and put it in the jar. He wrote a message on a little paper. He said to his mom, "Look, Mom! I made a message. Can we put it in the jar?" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. That's a great idea." They put the message in the jar and closed it. Tim's mom said, "Now, let's go to the park and see if someone finds it." At the park, they hid the jar behind a big tree. They waited and watched from far away. A little girl named Sue found the jar. She opened it and read the message. It said, "You are a good friend." Sue smiled and looked around. She saw Tim and his mom. They waved at her. Sue knew she had a new friend because of the message in the jar.

Anna likes to play with her toy cars. She has many cars of different colors and sizes. She likes to make them go fast and make noises. She has a big car that can carry other cars. It has a driver who can sit inside and steer. Anna calls him Bob. One day, Anna wants to play with Bob and his big car. She looks for them in her toy box, but she cannot find them. She feels sad and angry. She asks her mom, "Where is Bob and his big car? I want to play with them!" Her mom says, "I don't know, Anna. Maybe you left them somewhere else. Did you use them yesterday? Where did you play with them?" Anna thinks hard. She remembers that she used them yesterday in the garden. She played with them on the grass and in the dirt. She had fun, but she forgot to bring them back inside. She says, "I used them in the garden, mom. I left them there. I'm sorry." Her mom says, "That's okay, Anna. But you should always tidy up your toys after you play with them. Otherwise, they might get lost or dirty or broken. Let's go to the garden and see if we can find them." Anna and her mom go to the garden. They look for Bob and his big car. They find them under a bush. They are covered in dirt and leaves. Anna feels sorry for them. She says, "Oh, Bob and big car, I'm sorry I left you here. You must be cold and dirty. I will clean you and take you inside." Anna and her mom take Bob and his big car to the sink. They wash them with water and soap. They dry them with a towel. They make them shiny and clean again. Anna hugs them and says, "I'm sorry, Bob and big car. I love you. I will always tidy up my toys after I play with them. Thank you, mom, for helping me find them." Her mom smiles and says, "You're welcome, Anna. I'm proud of you for being honest and responsible. Now you can play with them again. But remember to tidy up when you're done." Anna nods and says, "I will, mom. I will." She takes Bob and his big car to her room. She plays with them happily. She makes them go fast and make noises. She has fun. And when she's done, she puts them back in her toy box. She is a tidy girl.
