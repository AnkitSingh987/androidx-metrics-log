Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Cat

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to play outside. The sun was shining, and she saw a big tree. Under the tree, there was a small, regular cat. The cat looked sad and wet. Lucy thought the cat needed a hug. So, she walked over to the cat and hugged it gently. "Meow," said the cat. It seemed to feel better after the hug. Lucy smiled and asked, "Why are you wet, little cat?" The cat told Lucy, "I was playing in the shower. It was fun, but now I am cold." Lucy wanted to help the cat feel warm and dry. So, she took the cat to her house. Inside, Lucy found a soft towel. She wrapped the towel around the cat and hugged it again. The cat purred happily. Soon, it was warm and dry. Lucy and the cat played together all day. They became the best of friends.

There was a boy named Tim. Tim had a great toy car. He loved to play with his car all day. One day, Tim played with his car outside. He put the car on the ground and pushed it fast. The car went into a big tree. The car had a wreck. Tim was sad. Tim took the car to his dad. His dad fixed the car. Tim was happy. He played with his great car again.

Once upon a time, there was a messy little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in the mud and dirt all day. His clothes and face were always dirty. One day, his mom told him, "Tim, you need to clean up. You can't be messy all the time." Tim didn't want to clean up, but his mom gave him a big smile and encouraged him. "Come on, Tim, you can do it! I'll help you." Tim and his mom went to the bathroom. His mom showed him a razor. "This is for grown-ups to shave their faces. You don't need it yet, but you can use soap and water to clean your face." Tim and his mom washed his face and hands together. They laughed and had fun. When they were done, Tim looked at his clean face in the mirror. He felt happy and proud. He learned that it's important to stay clean and that his mom will always encourage him to do his best.

The sun was shining in the sky and a small child was playing in the park. She skipped and ran around, feeling happy and free. Suddenly, the sky grew dark as clouds came from far away. The wind started to blow, and the little girl could feel it on her face. She stopped and shivered. "It's freezing!" she shouted. Then, a tall oak tree nearby started to shake. The leaves rustled and the little girl could feel the branches brush against the ground. She stepped closer and saw something shining in the trunk of the tree. "It's smooth!" she gasped. She reached out a hand and touched the thing. Instantly, a loud noise filled the air and the little girl felt a sharp pain. She screamed and started to cry as she saw the shapes of big men running towards her. The little girl was too scared to move as the men grabbed the shiny object. When the men had gone, the little girl looked up at the oak tree. The wind had stopped, the leaves had stopped shaking, and the little girl was left feeling very cold and alone.

Once there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play hockey. He played hockey every day with his friends. They had lots of fun playing together. One day, Tim found an old hockey stick in his yard. It was very old and starting to rot. Tim was worried. He did not want his new hockey stick to break. Tim showed his dad the old hockey stick. His dad said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can get a new hockey stick for you to play with." They went to the store and bought a new hockey stick. Tim was very happy. Now, he could play hockey with his friends again.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red crayon. The crayon lived in a box with other crayons. They all loved to play and draw together. One day, the big, red crayon met a little girl named Lily. Lily said, "Hi, big red crayon! I want to draw with you. Can you help me make a pretty picture?" The big, red crayon was happy to help. They started to draw a big, red sun on a piece of paper. As the big, red crayon spread its color on the paper, Lily smiled. "Wow, you make the sun look so big and bright!" she said. The big, red crayon felt proud and happy. Lily and the big, red crayon drew more pictures together, spreading colors all over the paper. In the end, Lily had a big stack of pretty pictures. She showed them to her mom and dad. They loved the pictures too. Lily said, "Thank you, big red crayon, for helping me make these pretty pictures. You are the best crayon ever!" The big, red crayon felt very special and happy to have a new friend in Lily.
