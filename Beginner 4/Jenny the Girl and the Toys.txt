Category: Beginner
Title: Jenny the Girl and the Toys

Once there was a little girl named Jenny. She was walking in the park when she saw a sack on the ground. She was curious and wanted to know what was inside. Jenny went to pick it up, but it was too heavy! She looked around for help, and that’s when she saw a little puppy looking at her. The puppy said, “Hi Jenny, my name is Jack. Can I help you?” Jenny smiled and said, “Yes please! Can you help me carry this sack?” Jack nodded his head and they both grabbed the sack together and carried it to a bench. They opened it up and found lots of toys inside. They had so much fun playing with the toys until they heard somebody call out “Jenny?” It was Jenny’s mom! Jenny and Jack quickly put the sack back together and Jenny said goodbye to Jack. Jenny met a new furry friend that day, and she had a whole bunch of fun!

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big red seat. Tim's mom said, "Tim, please sit on the seat and wait for me. I will be back soon." Tim was an obedient boy, so he sat on the seat. A funny dog came to the park. The dog could talk! The dog said, "Hello, Tim! Let's play!" Tim said, "Okay, dog! But I must sit on the seat." The dog said, "No, Tim. Let's go far away to play." Tim thought for a moment and then said, "Okay, dog. Let's go." They played and played, but Tim forgot to repeat to the dog that he needed to go back to the seat. Tim's mom came back and saw that Tim was gone. She was very sad and cried. Tim and the dog were lost and could not find their way back to the park. They were both very sad too.

One day, little Susie got a new puppy. She was so happy and grateful! She named him Brownie, and they did everything together. One day, Susie noticed that Brownie seemed very sick. He couldn't eat or play. His eyes were sad, and he wouldn't jump around like he used to. So she called a veterinarian to help him. The veterinarian came and listened to Brownie's heart. Then he shook his head sadly. He said that Brownie was very sick and there was nothing he could do. He said that Brownie was too sick and he had to go. Susie was so sad and tears filled her eyes. She hugged Brownie and kissed him goodbye. Then she heard a bell begin to ring - it was the bell of the Heavens, ringing for Brownie to go. Susie was very sad, but she was also very grateful for the time she had with her beloved puppy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boat that liked to sail. The boat was light and blue. It loved to go fast on the water. One day, the boat saw a trail in the water. The trail was made by a big fish. The boat wanted to follow the trail to see where it went. The boat sailed on the trail for a long time. At the end of the trail, the boat found a beautiful island. The boat was happy it followed the trail. It made new friends and played with the fish. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a piece of cardboard on the ground. Sue had a naughty idea. She thought it would be fun to make a joke with the cardboard. So, she drew a funny face on it and cut it out. Sue went to her friend Tom and said, "Look, Tom! I made a funny face!" Tom laughed and said, "That's a good joke, Sue!" They both laughed and played with the cardboard face. But then, something unexpected happened. The cardboard face started to talk! "Hello, Sue and Tom!" it said. Sue and Tom were very surprised. They didn't know that the cardboard face could talk. The face was nice and funny, and they all played together. In the end, Sue and Tom were happy that their naughty joke turned into a new friend.
