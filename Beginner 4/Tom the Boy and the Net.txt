Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Net

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was very intelligent. He loved to play outside with his friends. One day, Tom and his friends decided to play a game with a ball and a net. Tom's friend, Sam, stood by the net. Tom and his friends took turns to throw the ball into the net. Every time they got the ball in the net, they would clap and cheer. They were having so much fun. As they played, Tom showed how intelligent he was. He knew just when to throw the ball so it would go into the net. His friends were very happy. They all had a great time playing with the ball and the net. And they learned that being intelligent can be lots of fun too.

One day, a girl named Lily wanted to make a cake with her mom. They went to the kitchen and started to mix all the things they needed. They took a big bowl and put in eggs, milk, and sugar. Lily was very happy because she loved making cakes. As they were making the cake, Lily's mom said, "Be careful, Lily! We don't want to put anything bad in the cake. That would make it poison and we couldn't eat it." Lily nodded and said, "I will be careful, mom." They put the cake in the oven to bake. When it was done, they took it out and it was a dull brown color. Lily was sad because she wanted a bright and fun cake. But then, her mom had a great idea. They could put colorful icing on the cake to make it pretty! They put blue, pink, and yellow icing on the cake, and it looked beautiful. Lily was so happy, and they shared the cake with their family.

Once upon a time, there was a brave cat named Tom. Tom liked to lie on a big, soft rug in the sun. He loved to play with his ball and run around the yard. One day, Tom saw a little bug on the rug. He thought it was a toy and tried to play with it. But the bug was not a toy. It was a magic bug! The magic bug could talk and it said, "Tom, I will give you a wish if you let me go." Tom was very surprised but he was also kind. So, he let the magic bug go. The bug said, "Thank you, Tom! Now, what do you wish for?" Tom thought for a moment and then said, "I wish to be the biggest, bravest cat in the world!" Suddenly, Tom began to grow and grow. He was now the biggest, bravest cat in the world! But Tom was not happy. He was too big to lie on his rug and play with his ball. He missed being small and playing with his toys. Tom asked the magic bug if he could go back to being small. The bug agreed and made Tom small again. Tom was very happy to be back on his rug, playing with his ball. He learned that being brave was not about being big, but about being kind and helping others.

Once upon a time, there was a lively dog named Spot. Spot lived with his friend, Tim. One day, Tim needed to go to the station. He asked Spot to remind him when it was time to leave. Spot played in the yard while Tim worked. Spot saw the sun go down and knew it was time to go. He ran to Tim and barked. Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Spot! Let's go to the station." At the station, they saw a big, red train. Tim and Spot got on the train. But, surprise! It was not a normal train. It was a train that went to the moon! Tim and Spot had a fun trip to the moon and back. They had a great story to tell their friends when they got home.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful dog named Ben. Ben loved to follow his little friend, Tim, everywhere he went. They played together all day long. One day, Tim went into a room with lots of toys. Ben followed Tim into the room. "Wow, Ben! Look at all these toys!" said Tim. They played with the toys and had lots of fun. After playing, they were very tired. Tim said, "Let's rest, Ben." They sat down in the room and closed their eyes. Ben was happy to be with his friend Tim, and they had a great day together.
