Category: Beginner
Title: John the Charming Boy and the Park

Once there was a charming boy named John. He loved wearing his yellow and blue striped shirt. Every day, John would wear his yellow and blue striped shirt and go outside to explore. One day, John was exploring in the park and found a big, blue mushroom. He was very excited to see such a big mushroom and he decided to pick it. He looked at it closely, and it looked so colourful and shiny that John couldn't help but take it home with him. When John's mom saw the huge blue mushroom, she said "John, this is a very special find! We should cook it and eat it for dinner." John smiled, and he said "Mommy, this mushroom is so big and charming. I'm sure it will be delicious!" So they cooked the big, blue mushroom and ate it together. John enjoyed it so much that from then on, he went out and explored the park more, looking for more mushrooms.

One day, a little bird wanted to soar high in the sky. He flapped his wings and went up, up, up. The bird saw a big tire on the ground. It was round and black. The bird flew down to see the tire. He met a small, honest bug who lived in the tire. The bug said, "Hello, bird! I like my home. Do you want to play with me?" The bird and the bug played together all day. They were very happy. When the sun went down, the bird said, "Goodbye, bug! I will come back to play again." The bird flew away and the bug went back into his tire home.

Anna and Ben are sister and brother. They like to play in the park. They have a ball and a kite. They throw the ball and run with the kite. They are happy. But then the wind blows hard. The kite flies away. Ben tries to catch it, but he is too slow. The kite goes over the fence and into the street. Ben is sad. He loves his kite. "Ben, don't cry," Anna says. "We can get your kite back. I have an idea." Anna is strong. She climbs the fence and jumps to the other side. She looks for the kite. She sees it on a tree. She hopes she can reach it. She walks to the tree and pulls the kite. It is stuck. She pulls harder. The kite comes loose. She smiles. She has the kite. She runs back to the fence. She throws the kite over the fence to Ben. He catches it. He is happy. He hugs his kite. "Thank you, Anna," he says. "You are the best sister ever. You are brave and strong." Anna smiles. She climbs the fence and jumps back to the park. She hugs Ben. They are happy. They play with the ball and the kite. They hope the wind will not blow hard again.

Once upon a time, there was a gray cat named Tom. Tom had a best friend, a dog named Max. They loved to play together and support each other when they were sad. One day, Tom and Max found a little book on the ground. It was a passport. They did not know what a passport was, but they thought it was special. They decided to take the passport to their friend, a wise old owl named Sam. Sam told Tom and Max that a passport was for people who wanted to go far away. Tom and Max wanted to help, so they took the passport and went on a big adventure to find the person who lost it. After a long walk, they found a little girl named Lucy, who was looking for her lost passport. Tom and Max gave the passport to Lucy, and she was very happy. They all became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat named Tom. Tom loved to play in the park. His favorite thing to do was swing on the swings. One sunny day, Tom went to the park to swing. He saw a new yet by the swings. The yet was big and round. Tom was very curious and wanted to see what was inside. Tom slowly opened the yet. Suddenly, out popped a tiny bird! The bird said, "Hello, I am your new friend!" Tom was so surprised but happy to have a new friend. They played on the swings together and had lots of fun.
