Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Lily and the Tree

Ben and Lily were twins who liked to play outside. They saw a big tree with many flowers and bees. They wanted to climb the tree and see the bees up close. "Let's go, Lily!" Ben said. "We can climb the tree and find the hive." "OK, Ben!" Lily said. "But we have to be careful. Bees can sting us if we make them angry." They ran to the tree and started to climb. They used their hands and feet to hold on to the branches. They saw the bees flying around the flowers and making a buzzing sound. "Look, Lily! There is the hive!" Ben said. He pointed to a round thing hanging from a branch. It was brown and had a hole in it. "The bees live there and make honey." "Wow, Ben! It is so big and shiny!" Lily said. She wanted to touch it and see what it felt like. She reached out her hand and moved closer to the hive. "Wait, Lily! Don't do that!" Ben said. "The bees will not like it. They will think you are trying to take their honey. They will come out and sting you!" But Lily did not listen. She was too curious and playful. She touched the hive with her finger. It was sticky and warm. She smiled and said, "It feels nice, Ben!" But then, the bees noticed her. They came out of the hole and flew around her. They were angry and scared. They made a loud buzzing sound and tried to sting her. "Ow, ow, ow!" Lily cried. She felt a sharp pain on her hand and arm. She let go of the hive and fell from the branch. She landed on the ground and started to cry. "Help, help, help!" Ben shouted. He saw his sister fall and get hurt. He was scared and sad. He climbed down the tree and ran to her. He hugged her and said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I'm sorry. Are you OK?" Lily was not OK. She had many red bumps on her skin. They were swollen and itchy. She felt sick and dizzy. She said, "I'm sorry, Ben. I'm sorry. I was silly and naughty. I should have listened to you." Ben said, "It's OK, Lily. It's OK. We will go home and tell Mom and Dad. They will help you. They will make you feel better." They held hands and walked home. They learned a lesson. They learned that bees are not playful. They learned that they should not touch the hive. They learned that they should listen to each other.

Once there was a small baby bunny. She was a very persistent bunny, and loved finding new things in the garden to explore. One day she found some coloured tape, and she knew she wanted to find something fun to do with it. She thought for a while and then the bunny hopped across the garden, looking for the perfect place to use the tape. The baby bunny hopped around until she found a log. She was so excited, and so she immediately started to make her tape balancing masterpiece. The bunny carefully laid down the tape from one end of the log to the other. She would lay some down, but then it would roll away. The bunny became more and more disappointed and frustrated as she couldn't get the tape to stay in one place. She felt like giving up and leaving the tape, but something inside her kept her going. She was persistent! Finally, after lots and lots of work the tape was balanced on the log! The little bunny was so happy and proud of herself. She hopped around and did a victory dance. The baby bunny had figured out how to balance the tape, and she was so happy.

One day, a jolly man went to sell a statue. The statue was small and cute. It was a statue of a happy cat. The man walked to the town with the statue in his hand. In the town, he met a little girl. The little girl saw the statue and said, "I like the cat statue. Can I have it?" The jolly man smiled and said, "Yes, you can have it. It will make you happy." The little girl gave the man some coins. She took the statue and ran home. She put the statue in her room. Every day, she looked at the happy cat statue and felt happy too. The jolly man was glad he could make the little girl smile.
