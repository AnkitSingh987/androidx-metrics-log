Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Noisy Dog and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a noisy dog named Spot. Spot liked to run and play all day. One day, Spot saw a row of big boxes in the park. Spot wanted to play with the boxes. He tried to fit in the first box, but it was too small. Then, he tried the next box, but it was too big. Spot was sad because he could not find a box that was just right. Then, something unexpected happened. Spot heard a soft "meow" from the last box in the row. He looked inside and saw a little cat. The cat was scared and needed a friend. Spot knew that he did not need to fit in a box to have fun. He had a new friend, and they played together in the park all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had a big paw. One day, Spot was sad. He lost his toy bone. He looked everywhere for it. Spot saw his friend, Cat. Cat said, "Why are you sad, Spot?" Spot said, "I lost my toy bone." Cat said, "I will help you look for it." They looked and looked but could not find it. Then, Spot blinked his eyes and saw his toy bone under a bush. He was so happy! Spot and Cat played with the toy bone together. Spot was not sad anymore. They were both happy and played all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a soft, fluffy bunny named Mr. Hoppy. Lily and Mr. Hoppy loved to play together. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. One day, Lily found a big folder in her room. She opened it and saw many colorful papers inside. Lily thought it would be fun to make a picture for Mr. Hoppy. She took out her crayons and started to draw. Mr. Hoppy sat next to her and watched. As Lily was drawing, Mr. Hoppy said, "I want to help too!" So, Lily gave Mr. Hoppy a crayon. But Mr. Hoppy couldn't hold the crayon very well. He tried and tried, but he just couldn't do it. Mr. Hoppy felt sad and said, "I quit." Lily saw Mr. Hoppy feeling sad and hugged him. She said, "It's okay, Mr. Hoppy. We can find another way for you to help." Lily thought for a moment and then had an idea. She put some paint on Mr. Hoppy's soft paws and let him walk on the paper. Together, Lily and Mr. Hoppy made a beautiful picture with both their drawings and paw prints. They put the picture in the big folder to keep it safe. From that day on, Lily and Mr. Hoppy always found ways to have fun and play together, no matter what.

One day, a boy named Tim was ready to eat his lunch. He had a big salad with many colors. He liked to eat salad because it was good for him. Tim sat at the table and looked at his salad. It was very big and yummy. Just then, his dog, Spot, came into the room. Spot saw the salad and said, "What is that? Can I have some?" Tim looked at Spot and said, "No, Spot! This is my salad. You can't have any." Spot was sad but he listened to Tim. Suddenly, a big wind came through the window. It was so strong that it blew the salad off the table. The salad was destroyed! Tim and Spot looked at each other and laughed. Now, they had to make a new salad together. And this time, they shared it.

Lily and Ben are playing with their toys in the living room. Lily has a doll with a pink dress and long hair. Ben has a car with four wheels and a loud horn. They are having fun making noises and moving their toys around. "Look, Lily, my car can go fast!" Ben says, pushing his car on the floor. "Wow, Ben, your car is cool!" Lily says, watching his car zoom by. "But my doll is pretty. She has a nice dress and a bow." Lily picks up her doll and shows it to Ben. Ben looks at the doll and smiles. "Your doll is adorable, Lily. She has a cute face and soft hair." Ben says, touching the doll's hair gently. "Thank you, Ben. Do you want to play with her?" Lily asks, holding out her doll to Ben. "Sure, Lily. I can be her friend." Ben says, taking the doll from Lily. They sit on the couch and pretend to have a tea party with the doll. They use cups and plates from Lily's toy kitchen. They pour water from a bottle and pretend it is tea. They talk to the doll and each other. "Hello, doll. What is your name?" Ben asks, holding the doll in his lap. "Her name is Rose. She likes tea and cookies." Lily answers, giving the doll a cookie from a plate. "Hello, Rose. I like tea and cookies too. You are very sweet." Ben says, giving the doll a sip of water from a cup. "Thank you, Ben. You are very kind." Lily says, making the doll's voice. They giggle and continue to play. They are happy and friendly. They share their toys and their stories. They are close friends. They have fun until it is time for dinner. Then they put their toys away and go to the table. They put the doll on the top of the couch, where she can watch them eat. They say goodbye to her and thank her for playing. They promise to play with her again tomorrow. They are adorable.
