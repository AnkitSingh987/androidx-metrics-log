Category: Beginner
Title: Max and the Sun

One day, a cute little dog named Max went to play outside with his friends. Max was very adorable and loved to play in the sun. He saw a grill in the yard and became curious. Max went close to the grill and tried to touch it, but his friend, Sally the cat, stopped him. "No, Max! Don't touch the grill! It can be hot and hurt you," Sally said. Max did not want to get hurt, so he listened to Sally. Max felt a little worry, but Sally was there to help him. Later, Max and Sally played with their ball. They were having so much fun! Max forgot about the grill and his worry went away. Max and Sally played until it was time to go home. They were happy that they had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, a smart dog named Max had a job. His job was to help his owner, Tim, find things. One sunny day, Tim lost his red ball. He asked Max, "Can you find my ball?" Max looked all around the yard. He saw a big tree and thought, "Maybe the ball is there." He found the red ball under the tree and felt happy. He ran back to Tim and gave him the ball. Tim said, "Good job, Max! You are so smart!" Later, Tim's mom told him to clean his room. But Tim did not want to do it. He thought, "If I don't do it, Mom will punish me." So, he asked Max for help. Max, being a smart dog, helped Tim clean the room by picking up toys with his mouth. In the end, Tim's room was clean, and his mom was happy. She said, "Thank you, Max, for helping Tim!" Max wagged his tail and felt proud. He knew that being smart and doing his job made everyone happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a bossy cat named Tom. He liked to tell everyone what to do. One sunny day, Tom saw a little red bike with pedals. He thought it would be fun to ride the bike. Tom jumped on the bike and started to pedal. He pedaled fast and went all around the town. The wind blew through his fur as he rode the bike. Everyone in the town saw Tom on the bike and laughed. They thought it was funny to see a cat riding a bike. After a long day of pedaling, Tom felt very tired. He found a soft, warm spot under a big tree and decided to sleep. As he slept, he dreamed of riding the bike again the next day. The town people smiled, watching the bossy cat sleep under the tree, and they knew Tom would have more fun adventures.

Once upon a time, there was a bunny named Bitzi. She went on a journey to find a special carrot. She hopped to the top of a hill and saw a brown carrot sticking up from the ground. She was very excited and hopped closer. "Oh wow!" whispered Bitzi. Just then, a wolf appeared. He grinned at Bitzi and said, "That is my carrot. I found it first." Bitzi shurgged. She didn't know what to do. She wanted the carrot so badly, but she also didn't want to make the wolf mad. The wolf stepped closer and said, "I will let you have it if you answer a question for me." Bitzi nodded. She was happy and smiled. "What color is the carrot?" asked the wolf. "It is brown," said Bitzi. The wolf smiled and said, "You are right. You can have it." Bitzi was so excited! She thanked the wolf, hopped over to the carrot, and nibbled away. It was delicious.

Once upon a time, there was a little white dog named Spot. Spot lived in a big house with his best friend, a boy named Tim. Tim and Spot loved to play together all day long. But one day, Tim got a new toy, a red ball. Spot felt jealous because Tim played with the red ball more than with him. One day, while Tim was at school, Spot decided to hide the red ball. He took the ball and ran outside. Spot dug a hole and put the red ball in it. He covered the hole with dirt and went back inside the house. Spot thought that now Tim would play with him more. When Tim came home, he looked for his red ball but could not find it. He was sad because he thought he had lost his ball. Spot felt bad for making Tim sad. He went outside, dug up the red ball, and brought it back to Tim. Tim was happy to see his ball again and gave Spot a big hug. From that day on, Tim made sure to play with both Spot and the red ball, so no one would feel left out.
