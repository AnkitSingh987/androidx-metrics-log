Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Man and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a man named Tom. Tom was a bit troubled. He had a wallet that he cared a lot about. One day, he went to the store and took his wallet with him. At the store, Tom bought some toys. He paid with the money from his wallet. But when he left the store, he forgot his wallet on the counter. Tom walked away, not knowing it was gone. A little boy found Tom's wallet. He saw Tom walking away and ran to give it back. Tom was so happy! He thought his wallet was lost forever. The little boy and Tom became friends. From that day on, they cared for each other and had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a big brown dog. He liked to stand on his back legs and dance. One day, he saw a blue ball in the yard. He wanted to play with the blue ball. So, he ran to it and started to play. The brown dog was having so much fun with the blue ball. He would throw it up in the air and catch it. He would stand on his back legs and tap it with his front paws. The brown dog was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The blue ball went up in the air and did not come back down. The brown dog looked up and saw a big bird holding the blue ball in its beak. The bird flew away with the blue ball, and the brown dog was very sad. He could not play with his blue ball anymore.

One day, a large dog named Spot went for a walk. He saw something in the grass. Can you guess what it was? It was a skeleton! Spot had never seen a skeleton before. He was very curious. Spot took the skeleton to his friend, a little cat named Fluffy. Fluffy looked at the skeleton and said, "I guess this is a very old thing." They decided to show the skeleton to their friend, a big bird named Tweet. Tweet saw the skeleton and thought it was very interesting. "I guess this was a big animal a long time ago," said Tweet. They all agreed and decided to keep the skeleton in their secret playhouse. Every day, Spot, Fluffy, and Tweet would visit the skeleton and try to guess what kind of animal it was. They had so much fun guessing and playing with their new, old friend.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play together. Sometimes they play hide and seek. Lily is good at hiding. Ben is good at finding. One day, they play hide and seek in the living room. Mom is doing yoga on the mat. She breathes deep and moves slow. She does not see the twins. Lily hides behind the couch. Ben counts to ten. Then he looks for Lily. He looks under the table. He looks behind the curtain. He does not see Lily. He walks to the couch. He hears a giggle. He peeks behind the couch. He sees Lily. She smiles and says, "You found me!" Ben says, "Yes, I did! You are a good hider. But I am a good finder. Let's play again!" Lily says, "Okay! But this time, I will find you!" They run to the other side of the room. Mom hears them and smiles. She is happy that they are having fun. She finishes her yoga and joins them. She says, "Can I play too?"

One day, a girl named Sue wanted to paint. She had a warm, red paint and a big white paper. She was very happy. Sue started to paint a big sun, and it looked nice. She wanted to show her mom. "Mom, look at my paint!" Sue said. Mom looked at the paint and smiled. "That's a nice sun, Sue. But can you paint a spell too?" Sue thought hard. She did not know what a spell was. She asked her friend, Tom. Tom did not know what a spell was either. They both felt sad. Then, a little bird came and sang a song. The song was a spell! Sue and Tom were surprised. They painted the spell with warm, red paint. They learned that a spell can be a nice song too. They showed their moms the paint, and everyone was happy. The moral of the story is: Don't be afraid to ask for help. You can learn new things from unexpected places.
