Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Friends

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to play a game. He asked his friends if they were available to play. They all said yes, and they started to play a game of tag. Tim was very fast, and he wanted to win. He ran and ran, trying to catch his friends. Then, something unexpected happened. Tim tripped and fell on the ground. He felt sad and started to cry. His friends saw him cry and came to comfort him. They told him that it was okay to fall and that they would help him get up. Tim realized that winning wasn't the most important thing. Having friends who cared about him was better than any win.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little dog named Sunday. Sunday lived in a big house with a nice family. The family loved Sunday very much. They played with Sunday every day. One day, Sunday went outside to play in the yard. Sunday saw a pretty bird in a tree. The bird was singing a happy song. Sunday wanted to play with the bird. Sunday tried to call the bird, but the bird did not come down. Sunday felt sad, but then the family came outside. They called Sunday to play with them. Sunday was happy again. They played and laughed all day long. Sunday knew that the family loved him, and he loved them too.

Once upon a time, there was a little pupil named Tim. Tim was a worried boy. He did not trust people. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big dog. The dog looked nice, but Tim was scared. "Mom, I am worried about the dog," said Tim. His mom said, "Don't worry, Tim. Trust me, he is a good dog." Tim wanted to trust his mom, but he was still scared. Then, something unexpected happened. The big dog started to dance! Tim was not scared anymore. He laughed and clapped his hands. Tim trusted his mom and the big dog. They all had fun at the park together.

Once upon a time, there was a big, old tree. It had many green leaves. The tree was near the sea. Every day, the wind would turn the leaves and make a wave in the sea. One day, a little boy came to the tree. He played with the leaves and had fun. The boy saw the wave in the sea and wanted to play with it too. He ran to the sea, but the wave was too big for him. The boy was sad. Then, something unexpected happened. The old tree saw the sad boy and wanted to help. The tree turned its leaves again, but this time it made a small wave in the sea. The boy was happy and played with the small wave. The old tree and the boy became good friends.

One day, a little dog named Max saw a big billboard. The billboard had a picture of a cat on it. Max was very proud of his ability to climb. He thought the cat on the billboard needed help. He wanted to rescue the cat. Max started to climb the billboard. He climbed up, up, up. When he reached the top, he said to the cat, "I am here to rescue you!" But the cat did not answer. Max was confused. Just then, a little girl named Lily saw Max on the billboard. She said, "Max, that is not a real cat! It is just a picture!" Max looked closer and saw that Lily was right. He felt silly, but also happy that the cat did not need rescuing. Max climbed down and they both laughed.

One day, a boy named Tim found a toy pistol in his room. He was very excited. He wanted to play with it outside. So, he went to the park to find his friend, Sam. Sam was at the park too. He was playing with a toy boat. The boat could sail on the water. Tim showed Sam his toy pistol. Sam liked it a lot. They decided to play together. Tim said, "Let's make the boat sail and use the pistol to make it go faster!" Sam agreed. They put the boat in the water and Tim used the pistol to make a loud noise. The boat sailed very fast. They both laughed and played all day long. They had a lot of fun with their toys.
