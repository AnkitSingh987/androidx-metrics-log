Category: Beginner
Title: The Peaceful Town and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a peaceful town. In this town, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved Sundays because she could play all day. One Sunday, Lily was playing with her ball. She kicked it too hard and it went into the tall tree. She was sad and didn't know what to do. A kind boy named Tom saw Lily was sad. He came to help her. Tom climbed the tree and got the ball for her. Lily was very happy. She said, "Thank you, Tom!" They played together all day and had a great Sunday.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big, clear jar. She liked to catch bugs and keep them in the jar. One day, she found a big, green bug that could crawl very fast. She put the bug in the jar and closed the lid tight. Lily wanted to show her mom the bug. She went inside the house and put the jar on the table. She went to find her mom, but her mom was busy. Lily went back to the table, but the jar was open and the bug was gone. It had crawled away. Lily looked all over the house for the bug. She looked under the table and behind the couch. She could not find it. She felt sad and started to cry. Her mom came and hugged her, but they never found the bug. Lily was very careful with her jar after that, but she never caught another bug that could crawl as fast as the green one.

One day, a shy cat and a happy dog were playing in the park. The cat was sad because it could not make new friends. The dog wanted to help the cat. The dog said, "Let's play a game! We will try to hug as many friends as we can. We will get one score for each hug. The one with the most scores wins!" The cat liked the idea and they started the game. The dog hugged many friends and got a lot of scores. But the shy cat was too scared to hug anyone. The dog saw the cat was sad and said, "Come on, just try to hug one friend!" The cat tried to hug a big bear, but the bear did not like it. The bear got mad and chased the cat and the dog out of the park. The cat and the dog did not win the game, and they were both very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had an ordinary bathrobe that she loved very much. Every night, she wore her bathrobe before going to bed. One day, Lily's bathrobe started to talk. "Hello, Lily! Do you know I can talk?" the bathrobe said. Lily was very surprised. "Wow! I didn't know you could talk!" she said. Lily and her bathrobe became very good friends. They talked and played together every day. And from that day on, her ordinary bathrobe was not so ordinary anymore.

Sam and Mia liked to play at the beach. They made sand castles and looked for shells. One day, they saw a big boat with colorful flags. They wanted to go closer and see what was on the boat. "Can we go, Mom?" Sam asked. "No, it's too far and too deep. You might get lost or hurt," Mom said. "But we have our floaties and our goggles. Please, Mom, please," Mia begged. Mom shook her head. "No, it's not safe. There might be sharks in the water. They have big teeth and they bite." Sam and Mia did not listen to Mom. They waited until she was busy with the baby and then they ran to the water. They put on their floaties and their goggles and swam towards the boat. They thought they were brave and smart. They did not see the big shadow under the water. They did not hear the splash. They did not feel the pain. They only saw the colorful flags for a moment before everything went dark. Mom looked up and saw the boat. She saw the blood in the water. She screamed and ran to the shore. She was too late. Sam and Mia were gone. The shark had eaten them. Mom cried and cried. She wished she had stopped them. She wished they had listened to her. She wished they were still alive.
