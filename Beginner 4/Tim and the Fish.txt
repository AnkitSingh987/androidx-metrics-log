Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a tie. It was a very nice tie. Tim's friend, Sam, saw the tie and was envious. Sam wanted a tie like Tim's. One day, Sam said to Tim, "Can I have your tie?" Tim said, "No, it is my tie." Sam was sad. He wanted a tie so bad. Tim thought of a way to help Sam. Tim and Sam went to play in the sand. They found a big shell. Tim said, "Let's pour water in the shell." Sam agreed. They poured water in the shell. Then, a magic fish appeared. The fish said, "I will give you one wish." Sam wished for a tie like Tim's. The fish gave Sam a tie. Now, both friends had nice ties and they were happy.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to eat from her favorite dish. One day, she found her dish empty. Fluffy was sad and hungry. She went to her friend, the dog named Spot. Spot saw Fluffy and said, "You look sad, Fluffy. What's wrong?" Fluffy told Spot about her empty dish. Spot wanted to help Fluffy. He had an idea. "Let's go find food for your dish!" said Spot. Fluffy agreed, and they went on a hunt for food. They found a big, dead bug. Fluffy did not like the bug. She insisted, "No, Spot. I don't want to eat that." So they kept looking. Then, they found some yummy fish. Fluffy was happy! They put the fish in her dish, and Fluffy ate it all up. From that day on, Fluffy and Spot were the best of friends.

Once there was a little boy. He had a bathtub just for him. He liked to bathe every day. Today something unusual happened. A stranger came to the door. He was very quiet. The boy was a little worried. The stranger asked if he could bathe in the boy's tub. He said he hadn't had a bath in weeks! The boy wasn't sure what to do. But he didn't want the stranger to go away unhappy. So he said yes and let him take a bath. The stranger was very happy. And the boy was happy too. He felt proud that he had done something kind. It felt good to help the stranger. The boy knew he had done the right thing.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly squirrel. The squirrel lived in a big tree. The squirrel loved to eat nuts. One day, the squirrel saw a nut on the ground. He ran to the nut and picked it up. As he was about to eat the nut, a little bird came by. The little bird said, "Please pass the nut to me. I am very hungry." The elderly squirrel felt sorry for the little bird and passed the nut to him. But then, something unexpected happened. The little bird took the nut and started to grow. It grew bigger and bigger until it was as big as the tree! The elderly squirrel was very surprised. The big bird smiled and said, "Thank you for sharing your nut with me. Now, I will help you find more nuts." And they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big rhinoceros. He lived in a big forest. The rhinoceros was deaf, but he was very kind and liked to play with his friends. One day, the rhinoceros was playing with his friend, a small bird. The bird liked to kick a little ball. The rhinoceros wanted to try, too. He kicked the ball with his big foot. The ball went very, very far away. The bird and the rhinoceros looked for the ball. They found it near a big tree. They were very happy. They played together all day long. The bird and the rhinoceros were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to travel with her mom and dad. One day, they went to a big house with a big fireplace. The fireplace was weird because it could talk! The fireplace said, "Hello, Sue! I am a magic fireplace. I can take you anywhere you want to travel." Sue was very happy and said, "I want to go to the beach!" The fireplace made a big fire and Sue, her mom, and her dad traveled to the beach. At the beach, they played in the sand and swam in the water. Sue's mom said, "This is fun!" Sue's dad said, "Thank you, magic fireplace!" They had a great day at the beach. When it was time to go home, they went back to the fireplace and it took them home.
