Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Cake

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw many kids playing and having fun. Tim wanted to join them, but he felt a little shy. Then, a girl named Sue came over and said, "Hi! We are having a party. Would you like to come?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes!" So, Tim and Sue went to the party together. At the party, they played games and ate cake. But, oh no! The cake was terrible. Everyone laughed and said, "This cake is not good!" Tim and Sue became good friends and played at the park every day after that.

Once upon a time, there was a red bird. The red bird had a key. One day, the red bird saw a cat. The cat said, "Hello, red bird. What do you have?" The red bird said, "I have a key. It can open a big door." The cat nodded and said, "Can I see the door?" The red bird nodded too and said, "Yes, let's go." They went to the big door. The red bird used the key to open it. Inside, they found a big room full of toys. The cat and the red bird played with the toys and had fun all day.

One day, a small cat named Tiny found a towel in the yard. She played with the towel all day. She did not know that the towel was magic. At night, she was very tired and slept on the towel. The next day, Tiny woke up and felt different. She did not know what was going on. She started to run and found out she could speed very fast. Tiny was very happy and wanted to show her friends. Tiny ran to her friends and showed them how fast she could go. They were all very surprised! They played all day, and Tiny won every race. At the end of the day, Tiny knew the magic towel had made her fast. She was very thankful for the towel and took it home with her.

Once, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to dress up in her mom's big clothes. One day, Mia found a small desk in her room. She thought it was magic. She put her mom's clothes on the desk. Mia's big dog, Max, came into the room. Max was deaf, so he could not hear Mia talking to him. Mia said, "Max, look at this magic desk!" Max looked at the desk and wagged his tail. He was very happy. Then, something unexpected happened. The clothes on the desk started to move! Mia and Max were very surprised. The clothes changed into a big, friendly bear! The bear said, "Thank you for using the magic desk!" Mia and Max smiled and played with the bear all day.

Lily is a cat who likes to play outside. She has orange fur and green eyes. She likes to chase birds and bugs and climb trees. One day, she sees a big butterfly with orange orange and black wings. She wants to catch it and play with it. She runs after the butterfly, but it flies away. She jumps and leaps, but it is too fast. She follows it across the yard, over the fence, and into the park. She does not see where she is going. She only sees the butterfly. She loses the butterfly in a big bush. She is sad and tired. She does not know where she is. She does not see her home or her family. She is scared and alone. She wants to go back. She starts to search for a way home. She looks for familiar things, like the big tree, the red car, or the blue house. She does not find them. She sees many people and dogs and cars and noises. She does not like them. They are loud and scary. She hears a soft voice calling her name. She turns and sees her mom. She is holding a leash and a collar. She is smiling and happy. She runs to her mom and purrs. Her mom hugs her and puts the leash and collar on her. She says she was looking for her everywhere. She says she is sorry she lost the butterfly. She says she loves her very much. Lily is happy and relieved. She loves her mom too. She does not care about the butterfly anymore. She only wants to go home and be safe and warm. She follows her mom out of the park and back to the yard. She sees her home and her family. She is glad she found them. She is glad they found her. She is a lucky cat.
