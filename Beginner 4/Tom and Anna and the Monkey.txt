Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Anna and the Monkey

Tom and Anna went to the zoo with their mom. They saw many animals, but their favorite was the monkey. The monkey was fast and funny. He jumped from tree to tree and made loud noises. "Look, mom, the monkey is so fast!" Tom said. "Yes, he is. He is very good at climbing and swinging," mom said. "I want to be like the monkey. He is so cool," Anna said. She tried to copy the monkey and jumped on a bench. But she slipped and fell on the ground. She hurt her knee and started to cry. "Ow, ow, ow! It hurts!" Anna said. Mom ran to Anna and hugged her. She checked her knee and saw a small cut. "It's okay, Anna. It's just a scratch. You are brave and strong," mom said. She took a band-aid from her bag and put it on Anna's knee. "Thank you, mom. You are the best," Anna said. She smiled and hugged mom back. Tom came to Anna and gave her a hug too. "I'm sorry, Anna. I didn't mean to laugh at you. You are my sister and I love you," Tom said. "I love you too, Tom. You are my brother and I admire you," Anna said. They both looked at the monkey again. He was still fast and funny. But they knew they didn't have to be like him. They were happy to be themselves.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to collect things. He had a big box of toys, and he liked to find more toys to put in it. One day, Tim's mom said they needed new furniture for their home. Tim wanted to be brave and help his mom. So, they went to a big store to find the furniture they needed. While walking around, Tim saw a little girl who was lost and scared. He went up to her and asked, "Are you lost? I can help you find your mom." The little girl smiled and said, "Yes, please. I can't find my mom." Tim held her hand, and they walked together to find her mom. When they found her, the mom said, "Thank you, Tim, for being so brave and helping my little girl." After that, Tim and his mom picked out the new furniture they needed. They went home and put the new furniture in their house. Tim felt happy and proud because he was brave and helped someone that day.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and dog, Buddy. Lily had a favorite blanket that she took everywhere. The blanket was soft and warm, and it made her feel safe and happy. She would squeeze it tight when she felt lonely or scared. One day, Lily and Buddy went for a walk in the park. They played with a ball and had lots of fun. Suddenly, a big wind came and took Lily's blanket away. "Oh no! My blanket!" she cried. Buddy barked and ran after it, but he couldn't catch it. Lily felt very sad and lonely without her blanket. Lily and Buddy searched the park, asking everyone they met if they had seen her blanket. "Have you seen my blanket?" she asked a friendly squirrel. "No, I'm sorry," said the squirrel. They asked a kind bird, too. "I saw it fly away," said the bird, "but I don't know where it went." Finally, they found a big tree where the blanket was stuck high in the branches. Lily tried to reach it, but she was too small. Buddy tried to jump high, but he couldn't reach it either. They both felt sad and didn't know what to do. Just then, a tall man came by and saw them. "What's wrong?" he asked. Lily told him about her blanket. The tall man smiled and reached up into the tree. He grabbed the blanket and gave it back to Lily. "Thank you!" she said with a big smile. Lily squeezed her blanket tight, feeling happy and not lonely anymore. She and Buddy went home, and they all lived happily ever after.
