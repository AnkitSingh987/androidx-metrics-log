Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Unique Bird and the Squirrel

Once upon a time, there was a unique bird named Bob. Bob was different from other birds because he had big, colorful wings. One day, Bob saw dark clouds in the sky. He knew a storm was coming. Bob flew quickly to his friend, Sam the squirrel. "Sam, a storm is coming! We need to bring food to our homes," said Bob. Sam nodded and they both went to gather food. They picked up nuts and berries to eat during the storm. As the storm arrived, Bob and Sam were safe in their homes. They were happy they had food to eat. The storm was loud and wet, but they were not scared. They knew they were safe and had each other as friends. After the storm, they went outside to play and enjoy the sunny day.

Once upon a time there was a boy named Max, who was three years old. He lived in a nice house with his mom and dad. One day his mom put a delicious pie in the oven. Max was so excited to eat it soon! His mom told him the pie needed to bake for a little bit longer and it would be done soon. After a few minutes, his dad said it was time to take the pie out of the oven. But then Max's mom said, "Wait! We need to delay taking it out. It needs a few more minutes to bake." Max waited and waited, and then it was finally time to eat! He cut the delicious pie and gave some to his mom and dad. Max was so excited, it tasted delicious!

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim was very excited. He wanted to play with his favorite toy, a pipe. But he could not find it. Tim started to search for his pipe all over his room. Tim looked under his bed and said, "Pipe, where are you?" He searched in his toy box and asked, "Are you in here, pipe?" But the pipe was not there. Finally, Tim found his pipe in the garden. He was so happy! He picked it up and said, "I found you, pipe! Let's play together!" And they played all day long.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Maria. She was very organized. One day, her mommy told her to imagine a dishwasher in her room. Maria was so excited! She imagined a tall dishwasher that was pink, with sparkly stars on it. Maria ran to her mommy and said, “Mommy, look at my dishwasher!” Her mommy smiled and said, “That’s wonderful, darling. Now you can wash your dishes in a fun and organized way.” Maria was very pleased. She ran to her room and played with her dishwasher all day long. She loved having a dishwasher in her room. The end!

Tim likes to whistle. He can make many sounds with his mouth. He can whistle like a bird, like a train, or like a wind. He likes to whistle when he plays with his toys, when he walks to the park, or when he helps his mom. One day, Tim finds a plastic toy in his room. It is a plastic dog. It has black and white spots, a red collar, and a blue bone. Tim thinks it is a smart toy. He whistles to the plastic dog and says, "Come here, boy. Let's play." The plastic dog does not move. Tim whistles again and again, but the plastic dog does not move. Tim feels sad. He thinks the plastic dog does not like him. He says, "Why don't you play with me? Are you broken?" Tim's mom hears him and comes to his room. She sees Tim and the plastic dog. She smiles and says, "Tim, this is not a real dog. It is a plastic toy. It cannot hear you or move. It is not broken. It is just a toy." Tim looks at his mom and says, "Oh, I see. It is not a smart toy. It is a silly toy." He laughs and says, "But I still like it. Can I keep it?" Tim's mom says, "Of course, you can keep it. It is your toy. You can whistle to it, hug it, or pretend it is a real dog. You can use your imagination and have fun." Tim smiles and says, "Thank you, mom. You are smart and nice. I love you." He whistles to his mom and gives her a kiss. Then he whistles to the plastic dog and says, "Come on, boy. Let's go to the park." He picks up the plastic dog and runs to the door. He is happy. He likes to whistle.
