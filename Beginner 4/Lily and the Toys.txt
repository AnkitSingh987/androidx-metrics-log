Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Toys

Lily was a little girl who liked to play with her toys. She had a lot of dolls and cars and blocks. She liked to make stories with them and act them out. She was very proud of her toys and did not like to share them with anyone. One day, her cousin Ben came to visit. He was also a little boy who liked to play with toys. He saw Lily's toys and wanted to play with them too. He asked Lily, "Can I play with your dolls and cars and blocks?" Lily said, "No, they are mine. You can't touch them. Go away!" Ben felt sad and angry. He said, "That's not nice. You should share your toys. Sharing is caring." Lily said, "I don't care. I don't want to share. Go play with something else." Ben looked around and saw a big stair that led to the second floor. He thought, "Maybe I can find something fun to play with upstairs." He climbed the stair and went to the second floor. Lily did not notice him. She was busy playing with her toys. She made a big tower with her blocks and put a doll on top. She said, "Look at my tower. It's the best tower ever. I'm so proud of it." But then, she felt a tickle in her nose. She tried to hold it in, but she could not. She sneezed very loud and hard. Her sneeze made her tower fall down and her doll fly away. She was shocked and sad. She said, "Oh no! My tower! My doll! What happened?" She looked around and saw Ben coming down the stair. He had a big smile on his face. He said, "I found a lot of fun things to play with upstairs. There were books and puzzles and games. They were not yours, so I could play with them. And guess what? I also found a feather. I used it to tickle your nose and make you sneeze. That's what you get for not sharing your toys." Lily realized that Ben had tricked her. She felt ashamed and sorry. She said, "I'm sorry, Ben. You're right. I was not nice. I should have shared my toys with you. Sharing is caring. Can you forgive me?" Ben said, "Yes, I forgive you. But you have to promise me that you will share your toys from now on. And maybe we can play together and have more fun." Lily said, "I promise. And yes, let's play together. You can choose any toy you want. And maybe you can teach me how to play with the books and puzzles and games you found upstairs." Ben said, "Okay, that sounds good. Come on, let's go." They hugged and went upstairs. They played with the toys and learned from each other. They had a lot of fun and became good friends. The moral of the story is: Sharing is caring. If you share your toys, you can make friends and have more fun. If you don't share your toys, you can lose them and make enemies. And you never know when someone might sneeze on your tower.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Kitty. Kitty liked to wear a big hat with a bow. She loved to play dress up and have fun with fashion. One day, Kitty saw a big box in the living room. She wanted to enter the box and see what was inside. So, she jumped into the box and found many colorful clothes and hats. Kitty was so happy! She tried on all the clothes and hats. She danced and played in her new fashion. Her friends came to see her and they all had a fun time playing dress up together.

Once upon a time, there was a girl called Jane. Jane was very special - she had a top that was very delicate. She treated it with lots of love and care, making sure that it was always kept safe. One day, Jane was playing with her top. She was spinning it around the ground, making it dance in the air. She was so happy and excited. Suddenly, the top slipped out of her hand and dropped onto the ground. Jane was so sad, so she ran to her mother, who was sitting in the garden. "Mama, my top dropped," Jane said, with teary eyes. "Don't worry, sweetheart," her mother said. "We will find another way to make it spin again." So, Jane and her mother made a new plan. They set up a string from the roof, and the delicate top was attached to it. When the wind blew, it made the top spin around and around. Jane was so happy she began to jump up and down, singing and dancing. Her delicate top was spinning again and the whole world seemed like a magical place.
