Title: The Incredible Ant and the Mountain

Once upon a time, there was an incredible ant. The ant lived near a big mountain. The ant had many friends that lived with him. One day, a big storm came. The rain started to strike the ground. The wind blew hard. The ant and his friends were scared. They needed to find a safe place to stay. The incredible ant had an idea. He said, "Let's go to the mountain! We can hide there." The ant and his friends went to the mountain. They found a small cave to hide in. The storm was still outside, but they were safe in the cave. The incredible ant was very brave. He helped his friends feel better. When the storm was over, the ant and his friends went back to their home. They were happy and safe again. The moral of the story is: always help your friends and work together when things are hard.

Once there was a lucky frog. This frog lived in a big pond. The frog liked to play with his friends, the fish and the ducks. One day, the frog saw a new friend. The new friend was a little lost bird. The frog wanted to help the bird. He said, "I suggest you go to the big tree. Your family is there." The bird was happy and flew to the big tree. But when the bird got to the tree, it was not his family. It was the frog's family! The frog had made a mistake. The bird was sad. But then, the frog's family said, "You can be our friend too!" Now, the lucky frog, the bird, and the frog's family all played together in the pond.

Once upon a time, a blue bird named Bob lived in a big tree. Bob loved to skip and play with his friends, the squirrels. One day, while they were playing, they found a small acorn on the ground. The acorn was shiny and brown. Bob and the squirrels wanted to see who could skip the farthest with the acorn. They took turns, and Bob went first. He skipped very far and was very happy. Then, the squirrels tried to skip with the acorn too. They were fast and went far, but not as far as Bob. At last, it was time for the last squirrel to try. He ran very fast and skipped so far that he went too close to the road. A big car came and hit the squirrel. Bob and the other squirrels were very sad. They cried for their friend who was hurt. The little acorn was left on the road, and they never played with it again. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play in the sand at the park. One day, Lily saw a gentle cat near the sand. The cat was looking at something, but Lily did not know what it was. The next day, Lily went back to the park to play in the sand. She saw the gentle cat again. This time, the cat was digging in the sand. Lily was curious and wanted to know what the cat was doing. On the third day, Lily went to the park again. She saw the gentle cat one more time. The cat came to Lily and showed her a shiny rock in the sand. Lily was so happy to see the rock. The cat had found a treasure. Lily and the gentle cat became good friends and played in the sand every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big, graceful cat. The cat liked to wander in the woods. One day, the cat found a cave. The cat thought, "What is in the cave?" The cat went into the cave. Inside, the cat saw a small, friendly mouse. The mouse said, "Hi, cat! Do you want to play?" The cat said, "Yes, let's play!" They played together in the cave. They were happy and had fun. From that day on, the cat and the mouse were best friends. They wandered and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a slow turtle named Tim. Tim had a friend, a fast bunny named Ben. Ben liked to tease Tim about being slow. One day, Ben said, "I bet I can run to the big tree and back before you even move!" Tim felt sad, but he knew he was slow. On the way to the big tree, Ben saw a shiny ashtray. He stopped to look at it. He thought it was very pretty. He wanted to show it to Tim. Ben picked up the ashtray and started to run back to Tim. Tim saw Ben coming back with the shiny ashtray. He was happy that Ben wanted to share it with him. When Ben reached Tim, he said, "Look what I found! Isn't it pretty?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, it is! Thank you for showing me." From that day on, Ben didn't tease Tim about being slow. They both learned that it's not always about being fast or slow, but about sharing and being kind to each other.
