Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Duck

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big lake. Tim had a toy boat with a sail. He wanted to play with it in the lake. He asked his mom, "Can I play with my boat?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Tim put his boat on the water. It started to sail away. But then, a big duck came and took the boat in its beak. Tim was sad. He did not know what to do. His mom said, "Let's try to get your boat back." Tim and his mom walked around the lake. They saw the duck lay the boat on the ground. The duck looked at the boat and then at Tim. It seemed like the duck wanted to play too. Tim said, "Okay, duck, you can play with us." So Tim, his mom, and the duck played with the boat. They had a fun day at the park. And from that day on, the duck became their regular friend. They all played together, and Tim learned that sometimes, unexpected friends can be the best friends.

One day, a cute dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a big building called a hospital. Max was curious and wanted to see what was inside. Max went into the hospital and saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad. Max asked, "Why are you sad?" Lily said, "I am sick and I miss my home." Max felt bad and wanted to help her. Max had an idea. He went outside and found a pretty flower. He brought it back to Lily. When Lily saw the flower, she smiled. But then, Max started to sneeze. He did not know he was allergic to flowers! Max regretted picking the flower, but he was happy to see Lily smile. Now, they both laughed at the unexpected sneezes.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a mitten that he loved very much. One day, the wind blew crazy and Tim's mitten flew away. Tim was very sad and wanted his mitten back. Tim saw his mitten in a tall tree. He wanted to get it, but the tree was very high. Tim tried to climb the tree, but he started to struggle. He tried and tried, but he could not reach his mitten. A kind bird saw Tim and wanted to help. The bird flew up and got the mitten from the tree. The bird gave the mitten back to Tim. Tim was so happy and thanked the bird. From that day on, Tim and the bird were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, a brown bear and a rabbit. They were best friends and did everything together. One day, they were walking along and came across a stream. The rabbit wanted to get across, but the bear said it was too wide and it couldn't be done. The rabbit said he knew a way and the bear should give it a try. The rabbit showed the bear how to take the thread that was lying nearby and slide it across the water. The bear was surprised and grateful, so he tried it out. Sure enough, the thread held and the bear was able to slide across the stream. The bear thanked the rabbit, who said he was glad to help. The rabbit explained that friends should always help each other, no matter what. The bear and the rabbit learned an important lesson that day: when friends work together, they can do anything!

Once upon a time, there was a big factory. In this factory, they made toys. One day, a toy bunny named Bob was made. He was a fine bunny with soft fur and long ears. Bob had a friend named Tim. Tim was a toy bear. They liked to play together. One day, they heard a noise. "What is that?" asked Bob. "I don't know," said Tim, "Let's go see." They went to find the noise. They had to hurry because it was time for the toys to be packed in boxes. They found a toy train that was stuck. "Help me," said the train, "I can't move." Bob and Tim helped the train. They pushed and pulled until the train was free. "Thank you," said the train. "You are good friends." They all smiled and went back to their places in the factory. They were happy to be fine toys, ready to meet new friends.
