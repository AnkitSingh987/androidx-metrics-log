Category: Beginner
Title: The Girl Called Sally and the Window

Once there was a girl called Sally. Sally wanted a reward for cleaning her room. But her mum said no. Sally was very sad and started to cry. Then she heard a noise from outside. She looked outside the window and saw a boy looking for something. Sally wanted to help the boy but her mum said she couldn't go outside. Sally was so sad she started to cry again. The boy asked Sally if she could help him find the reward he had lost. She was excited to help him. She looked everywhere but couldn't find it. Soon it was dark and the boy had to go. He thanked her for her help but sadly he didn't have the reward to give Sally. Sally felt bad as she really wanted the reward. But she realised that helping people can be its own reward. Even if she didn't get a reward, she had done the right thing by trying to help the boy. No matter how sad she was, she was glad she had helped the boy and it had taught her an important lesson.

Once upon a time, in a small hut, there lived a strong man named Tom. Tom loved to polish his big red ball. He would polish it every day, and it would shine like the sun. One day, Tom's friend Sam came to visit. Sam saw the shiny red ball and said, "Wow, Tom! Your ball is so shiny! Can you show me how to polish my ball too?" Tom smiled and said, "Of course, Sam! I will teach you how to make your ball shine like mine." Tom and Sam spent the day polishing their balls. They laughed and had lots of fun. By the end of the day, Sam's ball was just as shiny as Tom's. They were both very happy and proud of their shiny balls. From that day on, Tom and Sam would polish their balls together and show them off to everyone in their little village.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat sweet things. His favorite sweet thing was a waffle. He wanted to make a waffle all by himself. One day, Tim asked his mom for help. She showed him how to mix the flour, eggs, and milk. Then, she told him to be careful with the hot waffle maker. Tim tried his best to make the waffle. He poured the mix into the waffle maker and waited. Finally, the waffle was ready. Tim was so happy that he could succeed in making his sweet waffle. He shared the waffle with his mom. They both enjoyed the sweet waffle together.

Tom and Jen were gifted twins. They could do many things that other kids could not. They could read, write, draw, sing, and play games very well. They liked to show off their skills to their friends and family. One day, they found a big box in their room. It had a tag that said "For Tom and Jen, from Mom and Dad". They were very excited and curious. They wanted to open the box and see what was inside. They thought it was a new toy or a book or a game. They lay on the floor and tried to lift the lid of the box. But it was too heavy and hard. They pushed and pulled and kicked and hit the box. But it did not open. They started to get angry and frustrated. They shouted and cried and complained. "Mom! Dad! Help us open the box!" they yelled. But Mom and Dad did not come. They were busy in the kitchen, making a cake for Tom and Jen's birthday. They did not hear their twins' cries. They thought they were playing nicely in their room. The box was not a gift. It was a trap. It had a lock and a timer. It was set to explode in ten minutes. Mom and Dad did not know. They had bought the box from a bad man who wanted to hurt them. He had lied to them and said it was a special surprise for their gifted twins. Tom and Jen did not know. They kept trying to open the box. They did not see the red numbers on the side of the box. They did not hear the ticking sound. They did not smell the smoke. They did not have a happy birthday. They did not get to eat the cake. They did not get to see their friends and family. They did not get to enjoy their skills. They lay on the floor and waited for the box to open. But it never did. It only exploded. And they were gone.
