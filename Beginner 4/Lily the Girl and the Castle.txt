Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Castle

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was a curious girl who loved to play and have fun. One day, she went to a big park with her mommy and daddy. In the park, she saw something very exciting. It was a big, colorful bounce castle! Lily was so happy and excited. She ran to the bounce castle and started to jump and bounce with her new friends. They laughed and had so much fun together. The bounce castle was like a magical place for Lily and her friends. After a while, it was time for Lily to go home. She said goodbye to her new friends and left the bounce castle with her mommy and daddy. She was tired but very happy. That night, Lily dreamed of the curious bounce castle and all the fun she had. She couldn't wait to go back to the park and play again.

One day, a beautiful bird was in a tree. The bird saw a small squirrel playing near the tree. The squirrel was trying to jump from one tree to another, but it was too far away. The bird said, "Do you want me to lend you my wings? You can use them to fly to the next tree." The squirrel was happy and said, "Yes, please!" The bird lent the squirrel its wings, and the squirrel started to fly. But the squirrel did not know how to fly. It went up and up, and then it had a big fall. The bird tried to catch the squirrel, but it was too late. The squirrel fell to the ground and did not get up. The bird was very sad and wished it did not lend its wings.

Once upon a time, there was a playful woman named Sue. Sue loved to splash in the water at the big blue sea. One day, she went to the sea to have fun and play. Sue met a small fish in the water. The fish said, "Hi! Can I play with you?" Sue replied, "Yes, let's splash and have fun!" So, they played and splashed all day long. But then, a big wave came and took Sue far away. The fish tried to help, but it was too small. Sue could not swim back. The fish was sad and cried. Sue was lost and could not go back home.

One day, a big dog and a small cat had a race. The dog was very fast, but the cat was intelligent. They wanted to see who was the best. Before the race, a bird saw them and wanted to play too. The bird was nice and offered to help the cat. The cat was happy and said yes. During the race, the dog ran fast. But the cat and the bird had a smart idea. The bird flew up high and saw a shortcut. The cat took the shortcut and won the race. The dog was sad, but they all became friends and played together.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing. The cat was ugly, but the dog did not care. They were good friends. They liked to speak to each other and listen to music. They were having a fun time. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise. The ugly cat and the dog went to see what it was. They found a big box. The box was making music. The cat and the dog looked at each other, surprised. The ugly cat said, "Let's open the box!" When they opened the box, they found a beautiful bird inside. The bird was making the music. The cat, dog, and bird became friends. They all played together and made pretty music. The ugly cat was not sad anymore, because she had many friends who loved her.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a doll that she loved very much. The doll had a thick, warm dress that Sue liked to touch. One day, Sue and her doll went to play outside. It started to rain, so Sue said, "Oh no! We need to cover you, doll!" Sue took off her hat and put it on her doll's head. They ran back to the house, but the rain got heavier. When they got home, Sue's mom saw them and said, "You two are all wet!" Sue smiled and said, "We had fun, Mom! But I covered my doll with my hat to keep her dry." Sue's mom hugged her and said, "That's very kind of you, Sue." Then they all had hot cocoa and dried off by the fire.
