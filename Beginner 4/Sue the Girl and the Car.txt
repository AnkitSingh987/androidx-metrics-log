Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was very enthusiastic. She loved to play with her toys and make her friends smile. One day, Sue saw her friend Tom frown. "Why are you sad, Tom?" Sue asked. Tom said, "I lost my favorite toy car." Sue wanted to help Tom find his toy car. Sue and Tom looked everywhere. At last, they found the toy car under a big wrap. Tom was so happy! He said, "Thank you, Sue!" They played together all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big red train. The train loved to go fast and make lots of steam. The train knew that it was the biggest and the strongest train in the town. One day, the big red train saw a small blue train. The small blue train was slow and didn't make much steam. The big red train laughed and said, "I am big and fast. I know I am better than you." The small blue train felt sad but didn't say anything. Later, the big red train had to go up a big hill. The big red train tried and tried, but it was too heavy. The small blue train saw the big red train and wanted to help. The small blue train pushed the big red train up the hill. The big red train was surprised and said, "Thank you, small blue train. I now know that being big and fast is not everything. We can all help each other." From that day on, the big red train and the small blue train were friends. They knew that working together was better than being the biggest or the fastest. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball. One day, Spot could not remember where he left his ball. He looked and looked, but he could not find it. Spot felt sad. Spot saw his friend, Cat. Cat said, "I will help you find your ball." They looked under the tree, and there it was! Spot was so happy. He gave Cat a big hug and said, "Thank you, Cat!" After finding the ball, Spot and Cat played together. They took turns giving the ball a cool rub. The sun was shining, and they had lots of fun. At the end of the day, Spot remembered to take his ball home. Spot and Cat were very happy friends.

Once upon a time, there was a hungry caterpillar named Cat. Cat lived in a big tree. One day, Cat met a bird named Birdy. Birdy was also hungry. "Hello, I am Cat. I am hungry," said the caterpillar. "Me too," said Birdy. "Let's pull some leaves from the tree and eat them together!" So, they started to pull leaves from the tree. As they pulled more and more leaves, they found a big, tasty fruit hidden behind the leaves. "Wow! Look at this!" said Cat. They shared the fruit and ate it all up. Now, they were not hungry anymore. They became best friends and played together every day. And they always remembered to look for hidden surprises when they were hungry.

Once upon a time, there was a big, fierce lion. He lived near a river. The lion was very thirsty. He walked to the river to drink water. When the lion got to the river, he saw a little bird. The bird was by the water too. The bird said, "Hi, Mr. Lion! The water here tastes very good." The lion smiled and said, "Thank you, little bird." Then, the lion tasted the water. It was cool and sweet. The lion and the bird drank the water together. They became good friends and played near the river every day.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He took a step outside his house and saw a big red bird. The bird had a napkin in its beak. Tim said, "Hi, bird! What do you have there?" The bird replied, "Hi, Tim! I have a spicy snack in this napkin. Do you want to try some?" Tim was scared to try the spicy snack because he never had one before. But he wanted to be brave. So, he took a small bite. Suddenly, Tim felt his mouth getting very hot. He didn't like the spicy snack at all! He ran to his mom and said, "Mom, my mouth is on fire!" His mom gave him some milk to drink. The milk made Tim feel better. Later, Tim told the bird, "Thank you for sharing your snack, but I don't like spicy food." The bird said, "That's okay, Tim. It's good to try new things, even if you don't like them. You were brave today." Tim learned that it's important to try new things, even if they might be scary. And he also learned that he doesn't like spicy snacks.
