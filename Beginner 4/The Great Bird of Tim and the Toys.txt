Category: Beginner
Title: The Great Bird of Tim and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toys in the bathtub. One day, Tim saw a wild bird outside his window. The bird looked so happy and free. Tim wanted to be like the wild bird. One day, Tim decided to take his toys to the bathtub and pretend he was a wild bird. He splashed and played, making a big mess. Tim's mom came into the bathroom and saw the water everywhere. She was not happy. Tim felt embarrass. Tim's mom told him that it was okay to play, but he needed to be careful and not make a mess. Tim learned that it was important to have fun, but also to be responsible. He promised to be more careful next time. And from that day on, Tim played in the bathtub without making a mess, just like a good little wild bird.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a naughty little boy named Tim. He loved to play tricks on his friends. One day, he found a tunnel near his house. He thought it would be fun to play a trick on his friend, Sam. Tim said to Sam, "Let's play in the tunnel! I will supply you with a flashlight." Sam was excited and agreed to play. They went into the dark tunnel with the flashlight. But, Tim had a naughty plan. He turned off the flashlight and ran away, leaving Sam alone in the dark tunnel. Sam was scared and cried, "Help me, Tim! I can't see anything!" Tim heard Sam crying and felt bad for what he did. He went back into the tunnel and turned on the flashlight. They both came out of the tunnel and Tim said, "I'm sorry, Sam. I was naughty and I shouldn't have left you in the dark." Sam forgave Tim and they became good friends again. From that day on, Tim learned not to play naughty tricks on his friends. The moral of the story is to always treat others the way you would like to be treated.

One day, a big truck was driving down the road. The truck was carrying a lot of things to mix. The truck driver was a little uncomfortable because it was very hot outside. He wanted to get to his place fast so he could rest. As the truck drove, it saw a little boy playing with his toy truck. The boy was trying to mix some mud to make it soft. The big truck stopped and asked the boy if he needed help. The boy said yes, and the big truck used its big mixer to mix the mud. The boy was happy, and the truck driver felt good for helping. The truck driver was not uncomfortable anymore. He smiled and waved goodbye to the little boy. The big truck drove away to finish its job, and the little boy played with his toy truck and the soft mud.

One day, a helpful dog named Max saw a big tree. The tree had a lot of ash on it. Max wanted to help clean the tree. Max said to his friend, Tom the cat, "I will pull the ash off the tree." Tom said, "Be careful, Max. The tree looks weak." Max did not listen to Tom. He started to pull the ash off the tree. The tree began to shake. Max was not scared. But then, the tree fell down. Max was sad. He did not know that pulling the ash would make the tree fall. Tom said, "I told you to be careful, Max." Max learned that he should listen to his friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a spoiled boy who always wanted more toys and candy. One day, Tim saw his mom put some money in a jar on a high shelf. He wanted to buy more toys with that money. The next day, Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. Tim told Sam about the money on the shelf. "Let's get the money and buy more toys!" Tim said. Sam didn't think it was a good idea but didn't want to upset Tim. Tim and Sam tried to reach the jar on the high shelf. They stumbled and the jar fell down. The money went everywhere. Tim's mom came into the room and saw what happened. She was not happy. "Tim, you should not take things that are not yours. The money was for helping people who need it," she said. Tim felt bad and learned that being spoiled and taking things is not good. From that day on, Tim decided to share and help others.
