Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Cat and the Slide

One day, a cat named Tom found a weird stack of blocks. He wanted to use them to make a tall tower. Tom started to stack the blocks one by one. He was very happy with his tower. As Tom was stacking the blocks, a dog named Max came by. Max saw the weird stack and wanted to help. Tom and Max worked together to make the tower even taller. They were very excited and proud of their work. But then, something unexpected happened. The weird stack of blocks started to move! It turned out that the blocks were magic. The blocks made a big, fun slide for Tom and Max to play on. They laughed and played on the slide all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly little bunny named Bob. Bob liked to hop and play all day. One day, while hopping, he saw a big hole. Bob hopped closer to the hole and peeked inside. He saw a new friend, a tiny mouse named Tim. Tim was shy but wanted to play too. "Hi, Tim! Want to play with me?" asked Bob. Tim smiled and said, "Yes, let's play!" They played all day and were very happy to meet each other.

Sara and Ben are playing in the park. They like to slide and swing and run. They see a big pond with ducks and fish. Sara wants to feed the ducks. She has some bread in her bag. "Come on, Ben, let's go to the pond!" Sara says. "OK, Sara, but be careful. The water is deep and cold," Ben says. They walk to the pond and Sara throws some bread to the ducks. The ducks come closer and quack. Sara laughs and throws more bread. Ben watches and smiles. Sara sees a big fish in the water. She wants to touch it. She leans over the edge of the pond and reaches out her hand. But she slips and falls into the water. The water is thick and dark. Sara can't see or breathe. She panics and screams. "Help! Help! I can't swim!" Sara cries. Ben hears Sara and runs to the pond. He sees Sara in the water. He is scared and doesn't know what to do. He shouts for help too. "Help! Help! My sister is in the water!" Ben yells. But no one hears them. No one comes to help. Sara's body sinks deeper and deeper. Ben cries and cries. He is alone and sad. He wishes he had stopped Sara from going to the pond. He wishes he had saved her. He wishes he had a sister.

One day, a happy little girl named Lily went outside to play. She loved to run and jump in the grass. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. It was a perfect day. As Lily played, she saw a small insect crawling on the ground. She got down on her hands and knees to take a closer look. The insect was a tiny ant. It looked like it was trying to find its way home. Lily said, "Hi, little ant! Are you lost?" Just then, the wind blew, and the tiny ant started to panic. It ran around in circles, not knowing what to do. Lily wanted to help, so she said, "Don't worry, little ant! I will help you find your way home." Lily carefully picked up the tiny ant and placed it on a leaf. She carried the leaf to the ant's home, a small hole in the ground. The ant was so happy to be home, it did a little dance. Lily smiled and said, "You're welcome, little ant! Have a great day!" And with that, Lily continued to play in the grass, feeling happy that she had helped her new insect friend.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a stubborn girl named Lily. She did not like to sleep at night. She would look at her mom and say, "No sleep, no sleep!" Her mom would sigh and say, "Okay, Lily." One night, Lily had a bad dream, a nightmare. She saw a big, scary monster. The monster had big teeth and a loud roar. Lily woke up and was very scared. She did not want to be alone in her room. The next night, Lily was ready to sleep. She did not want to have another nightmare. But before she went to bed, her mom gave her a small, soft toy. It was a cute, little bunny. Her mom said, "This bunny will keep the nightmares away and protect you." Lily hugged the bunny and went to sleep. That night, she did not have any nightmares. The little bunny had saved her from the scary monster. From that night on, Lily was not stubborn about bedtime anymore.
