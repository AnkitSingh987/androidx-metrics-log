Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Window

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play soccer. He had a big goal in his backyard. Tim wanted to do an incredible kick. One day, Tim had an idea. He wanted to record his incredible kick. He asked his friend, Sam, to help. Sam held the camera, and Tim got ready to kick the ball. Tim ran fast and kicked the ball very hard. But, oh no! The ball went too high and broke a window. Tim and Sam were scared. They knew they were in trouble. Tim's mom came outside and saw the broken window. She was not happy. Tim and Sam had to clean up the mess and say sorry. They learned to be more careful when playing soccer.

---

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big mattress in his room. It was very uncomfortable. He didn't like it at all. So, he decided to kick it. He kicked the mattress really hard. Suddenly, a small door opened up in the wall. Tim was very surprised. He looked inside and saw a tiny room with a small bed. Tim called his mom and said, "Mom, look! I found a new room!" His mom came and looked inside. She was surprised too. They both went into the tiny room and found a new, comfortable mattress. They moved the new mattress into Tim's room and he slept very well.

---

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had a book called a passport. This passport was not normal. It had many colors and fun pictures. Lily loved her passport very much. One day, Lily went to the park with her mom. She brought her passport with her. At the park, she put her passport on a bench and went to play. When she came back, she saw a big dog. The dog was trying to cover her passport with dirt. Lily was scared, but she wanted her passport back. She asked the dog, "Can I have my passport, please?" To her surprise, the dog talked! He said, "I'm sorry, I thought it was a toy. Here you go!" The dog gave Lily her passport back. Now, Lily had a new friend, and they played together at the park.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to walk and play outside. One day, they see a big mountain far away. They want to go there and see what is on top. "Can we walk to the mountain, Mom?" Anna asks. "It is too far, Anna. You will get tired and hungry," Mom says. "But we want to see the mountain, Mom. Please, please, please," Ben says. Mom thinks for a moment. She smiles and says, "OK, but we need to take a bus first. The bus will take us closer to the mountain. Then we can walk a little bit and have a picnic." Anna and Ben are happy. They say, "Thank you, Mom. You are the best." They get ready and go to the bus stop. They wait for the bus. They see many cars and people and dogs and birds. They talk and laugh and sing. Soon, the bus comes. They get on the bus and find a seat. They look out the window and see the mountain getting bigger and bigger. They are excited. The bus stops near the mountain. They get off the bus and follow Mom. They walk on a path that goes up and up. They see trees and flowers and rocks and animals. They say hello to a rabbit and a squirrel and a bird. They take pictures and collect stones and leaves. They reach a nice spot with a view of the mountain. They sit down and open their backpacks. They have sandwiches and fruits and cookies and juice. They eat and drink and enjoy the view. They are happy. They are lucky. They have a good day. They love the mountain. They love Mom. They love each other.

One day, a boy named Tom went to play with his friend, Sam. They liked to play with toy cars and have fun. The sun was shining, and it was a cool day. Tom was happy because he had some news to tell Sam. Tom ran to Sam and said, "Guess what, Sam? I know some good news!" Sam looked at Tom and asked, "What is the news?" Tom smiled and said, "I got a new, cool toy car! We can play with it together!" Sam was very excited to see the new toy car. But when they went to get the toy car, it was gone! Tom and Sam were sad. They looked everywhere for the car. Suddenly, Tom's little sister, Lily, came out with the cool toy car. She said, "I found it! Can I play with you?" Tom and Sam were surprised but happy. Now, they all played together with the cool toy car, and it was a fun day.
