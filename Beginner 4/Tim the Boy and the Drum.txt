Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Drum

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big drum in his room. He was so happy! Tim thought the drum was amazing. He picked up the drumsticks and started to play. Tim's friend, Sue, heard the drum and rushed to his house. "What is that amazing sound?" she asked. "It's my new drum!" said Tim. "Do you want to play too?" Sue nodded and picked up a drumstick. Together, Tim and Sue played the drum, making loud and fun sounds. They laughed and danced as they played. The drum brought them so much joy. And that is how Tim and Sue had an amazing day with the big drum.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. The park was very organized with many toys to play with. Tim saw his friend, Sam, playing with a toy car. Tim wanted to play too, so he went to Sam and said, "Can I play with the car too?" Sam said, "Yes, but we need more fuel for the car." They looked around and found a small can of fuel near the slide. They were very happy and started to play together. They did not know that someone was watching them from behind a tree. After a while, a big dog came out from behind the tree. The dog was hungry and wanted the fuel for himself. The dog took the fuel and ran away very fast. Tim and Sam were very sad and started to weep. They did not know what to do. Just then, Tim's mom came to them and said, "Don't worry, I have more fuel at home. We can go and get it." Tim and Sam were very happy and went home with Tim's mom. They got more fuel and played with the toy car all day long. The big dog never came back, and they had lots of fun.

The elderly man walked slowly to the bench. He sat down and sighed. He noticed a small boy playing nearby. The man smiled and said, "Hi there. What are you playing?" The little boy peered up at the elderly man. "I am playing with my toy plane," he said proudly. The elderly man nodded. "That looks like fun. Could I join you?" he asked. The boy nodded shyly, and the elderly man sat next to him. Together, they played with the plane. The elderly man laughed and the little boy smiled. Soon, it was time for the boy to go home. The elderly man said goodbye, got up and slowly walked away. The little boy waved goodbye and kept playing with his plane until it was time for him to go home. The elderly man had made his day special.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. One day, his mom said, "Tim, let's go outside and play." Tim put on his coat because it was cold. They went outside and saw a big box. Tim's mom said, "This is mail for us." Tim was happy and wanted to open the box. His mom said, "No, Tim. Let's go inside and open it." But Tim was sad and did not want to go inside. He wanted to open the box outside. So, Tim's mom suggested, "Let's take the box inside and then we can open it." Tim did not listen to his mom. He opened the box outside. Inside the box, there were many ice cream cones. But because it was cold and they were outside, the ice cream cones became very frozen. Tim and his mom could not eat the ice cream cones. They were too hard to eat. Tim was sad because he did not listen to his mom.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to a big maze with her mom. They were very excited to play in the maze. Before they went in, Amy's mom said, "Remember, if we get lost, just stay in one place and I will find you." As they walked in the maze, Amy started to feel tired. She told her mom, "Mom, I am tired. Can we go home now?" Her mom said, "Let's find the way out first, then we can go home." But they got lost in the maze. Amy remembered what her mom said and stayed in one place. She remained there and waited for her mom. Soon, her mom found her and they were able to leave the maze together. Amy was happy that she listened to her mom and they could go home.
