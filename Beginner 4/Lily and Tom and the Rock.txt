Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Tom and the Rock

Lily and Tom were playing in the park. They saw a big pole with a swing on it. Lily wanted to swing high and fast. Tom wanted to rock on the swing. He said, "Rocking is fun. You can feel the wind on your face." Lily said, "No, swinging is better. You can go up and down and see the sky and the trees." She pushed Tom off the swing and got on it. She swung hard and fast. Tom was angry. He picked up a rock and threw it at Lily. The rock hit Lily on the head. She fell off the swing and hit the ground. She cried and cried. She had a big bump on her head and blood on her hair. Tom was scared. He ran away and hid behind a bush. A lady saw what happened. She ran to Lily and helped her. She called Lily's mom and the doctor. Lily's mom came and took her to the hospital. She was very sad and mad. She said, "Who did this to you, Lily?" Lily said, "Tom did it. He was clumsy and mean. He threw a rock at me because I wanted to swing." Lily's mom said, "That was very bad of Tom. He hurt you and ran away. He is not a good friend. You should not play with him anymore." Lily nodded. She felt sorry for herself. She wished she had not pushed Tom off the swing. She wished she had rocked with him instead. She learned a hard lesson. Rocking is better than swinging. And being nice is better than being mean.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful youth named Tim. He lived in a small house with his mom and dad. Tim loved to play with his red ball in the yard. He would throw it high in the air and try to catch it. Tim was always happy when he played with his ball. One sunny day, Tim threw his red ball very high. But this time, it got stuck in a big tree. Tim looked up and saw the ball in the branches. He wanted to get it back, but he didn't know how. He tried to jump, but he was too small. Tim started to struggle. "Mom, my ball is stuck in the tree!" Tim called out. His mom came outside and saw Tim looking sad. "Don't worry, Tim," she said. "I will help you get your ball back." Tim's mom got a long stick and tried to reach the ball. She poked the ball with the stick, and it fell down. "Thank you, Mom!" Tim said with a big smile. He picked up his red ball and hugged it tight. Tim went back to playing in the yard, feeling cheerful again. He knew that even when things were tough, his mom would always be there to help him. And that made Tim very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted little girl named Lily. She was very good at making things neat and tidy. One day, Lily wanted to organize her toys. She wanted to make sure that everything was in the right place. As she started, Lily began to worry. There were so many toys, and she didn't know where to start. She thought and thought, and then she had an idea. She would start with her dolls, then her cars, and then her blocks. Lily worked hard and soon, her toys were all organized. The dolls were with the dolls, the cars were with the cars, and the blocks were with the blocks. She felt happy and proud of her work. From that day on, Lily knew that she could do anything if she tried her best.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Molly. She was 3 years old and had lots of energy. One day she was really excited because her mom had bought her a ticket to a fun show. Molly quickly ran to the show with her ticket and when she arrived she started looking around. Everywhere she looked she saw smooth surfaces, even the floor was so smooth. Molly started exploring the show, until her mouth grew very wide in surprise. She found a big table with lots of yummy snacks! She could smell the sweet smell of candy and marshmallows. Molly quickly looked at her ticket for the show which was on the table. She was so excited and asked her mom, "Can I eat?" Her mom laughed and said, "Yes, you can eat the snacks, but be careful not to get too full." Molly jumped with joy and started eating the smooth candy which she loved. It was a great day full of excitement and Molly couldn't wait to come back to the show.
