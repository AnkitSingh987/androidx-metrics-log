Category: Beginner
Title: The Cat and the Tube

Once upon a time, there was a friendly cat. The cat liked to play with a tube. The tube was big and fun. The cat would jump in and out of the tube all day. One day, the cat found a soft cloth. The cat wanted to wrap the tube with the cloth. The cat thought it would be fun to make the tube soft and cozy. The cat pulled the cloth over the tube, and it looked nice. The friendly cat loved the soft tube. The cat played in the tube all day long. The cat was so happy. The cat and the tube were best friends. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was an adventurous little bird named Bobo. Bobo loved to fly high in the sky and play with his friends. He liked to match the colors of his feathers with the colors of the flowers. One day, Bobo saw a big, red fruit hanging from a tree. He thought it would be fun to put it in his mouth. Bobo flew up to the fruit and took a big bite. The fruit was very sweet and tasty. He wanted to share it with his friends. Bobo called his friends to come and try the fruit. But when they came, they saw that the fruit was not good for them. Bobo did not know that the fruit was bad. His friends flew away, and Bobo was sad. He learned that not all things that look fun and tasty are good for you.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely child. She loved to play in the park near her house. One day, she was in the park playing with her friends. Suddenly, a bad man came and grabbed the child. He was very mean, and he wouldn't let the child go. The child was so scared. She started to cry and scream for help, but no one came. The bad man was getting angry, so he gave the child a shake. The lovely child's eyes grew wide in fear. The bad man put the child in his car and drove off. The poor child wept for help, but nobody was there to save her. The bad man drove away and never came back. The lovely child was never seen again. The end.

Lily liked to play with her dolls in her room. She had many dolls, but her favorite was Olive. Olive had a soft green dress and a big smile. Lily liked to brush Olive's hair and make her talk. One day, Lily heard a loud noise outside her door. She stopped playing and listened. It was her brother, Max. He was running and shouting in the hall. Lily did not like it when Max disturbed her. She wanted him to go away. She opened her door and said, "Max, stop it! You are too loud! You disturb me and Olive!" Max looked at her and laughed. He saw Olive in her hand and said, "Olive? That's a silly name for a doll. That's a name for a food. You should eat Olive, not play with her." He reached for Olive and tried to grab her. Lily was scared and angry. She pulled Olive back and said, "No! Olive is not food! Olive is my friend! You can't have her!" She ran back to her room and slammed the door. She hugged Olive and said, "Don't worry, Olive. I won't let Max hurt you. You are safe with me." She did not know that Max was not done. He had a plan. He went to the kitchen and found a jar of olives. He took one out and put it in his pocket. He smiled and said, "I'll show Lily who Olive really is." He walked back to her door and knocked. He said, "Lily, I'm sorry. I was just joking. Can I see Olive?" Lily was suspicious, but she was also curious. She opened the door a little and said, "What do you want?" Max said, "I want to say sorry to Olive. I have a present for her. Look." He showed her the olive in his hand. He said, "This is Olive's sister. She missed her a lot. Can I give her to Olive?" Lily looked at the olive and saw that it was green and round, like Olive's dress. She thought that maybe Max was telling the truth. She said, "OK, but be nice." She opened the door more and let Max in. She gave Olive to him and said, "This is Olive. Say hello to your sister." Max took Olive and said, "Hello, Olive. Hello, Olive's sister." He pretended to make them talk. Then he said, "Olive, your sister is hungry. She wants to eat you. Yum, yum, yum." He opened his mouth and put Olive inside. He bit down and said, "Mmm, delicious." Lily screamed and cried. She saw Olive's head and arms sticking out of Max's mouth. She said, "Max, you are mean! You ate Olive! You lied to me! Give her back!" Max spit out Olive and said, "Ha, ha, ha. I got you, Lily. Olive is not a doll. Olive is a food. I told you so." He ran out of the room and left Lily alone. Lily picked up Olive and saw that she was broken and wet. She sobbed and said, "Olive, I'm sorry. I should have known. I should have protected you. You were my friend. I love you." She buried her face in Olive's soft green dress and cried. She did not hear her mom coming. She did not see Max getting in trouble. She did not care. She only cared about Olive.
