Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and the Dog

Sara was a queen who liked to relax. She had a big bed with soft pillows and a warm blanket. She also had a loyal dog named Max who always stayed by her side. Sometimes, Sara would read a book or sing a song while Max would listen and wag his tail. One day, Sara heard a loud noise outside her window. She looked and saw a big dragon with red scales and yellow eyes. The dragon was breathing fire and roaring. Sara was scared and hugged Max. Max barked and growled at the dragon. "Who are you and what do you want?" Sara asked the dragon. "I am Rax, the king of dragons. I want your bed. It looks so cozy and nice. I need to relax too, you know," the dragon said. "But this is my bed. You can't have it. It's not fair," Sara said. "I don't care. I am bigger and stronger than you. Move or I will burn you and your dog," the dragon said. Sara was brave and did not move. She said, "No, you can't bully me. This is my home and Max is my friend. We are loyal to each other. You should be nice and share, not mean and greedy." The dragon was surprised and curious. He said, "What is loyal? What is share? I don't know these words." Sara explained, "Loyal means you love and protect someone. Share means you give and take something with someone. Like this, I can share my bed with you, but you have to be gentle and kind. Then we can all relax and be happy." The dragon thought for a moment. He said, "That sounds good. I want to be loyal and share. I want to relax and be happy. Can I be your friend too?" Sara smiled and said, "Yes, you can. Come on, let's make room for you on the bed. But don't forget to brush your teeth first. Your breath is very smelly." The dragon laughed and said, "OK, I will. Thank you, Sara. You are a good queen and a good friend." Sara, Max, and Rax snuggled on the bed and relaxed. They read a book and sang a song together. They were loyal and happy. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was very independent. She liked to do things all by herself. One day, Sue had a big wish. She wanted to design a pretty dress for her doll. Sue asked her mom for help, but her mom was busy. Sue thought, "I can do it by myself!" So, she took some cloth, scissors, and thread. She started to cut and sew the cloth to make the dress. But Sue didn't know how to use the scissors and thread very well. She cut the cloth too small and sewed it all wrong. The dress looked bad, and her doll could not wear it. Sue was sad. She wished she had asked for help instead of trying to do it all by herself.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside with his friends. One day, they found a big box. They opened the box and saw a skeleton. The skeleton was not scary, it was a toy. Tim said, "Let's play with the skeleton!" His friends said, "Yes!" They took the toy skeleton out of the box. They played with it in the sun. They made it dance and jump. Then, Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's play strike with the skeleton!" So, they used a stick and the toy skeleton. They took turns hitting the skeleton with the stick. They laughed and had lots of fun. And they played together all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy snail named Shell. She was always very slow and clumsy but she was very determined. One day she decided to surprise her friends by reaching the top of a nearby tree. Shell took a deep breath and slowly started to climb the tree. She used all of her strength and tried hard not to be clumsy. As she climbed, she made progress and her friends watched with excitement. Just as Shell was nearing the top of the tree, a nasty crow swooped down and started pecking at her shell. Shell was so scared and couldn't understand why the crow was doing this. "Why are you doing this to me?", She cried. The crow just laughed and continued to peck at Shell. Her friends ran towards them and started to yell at the crow. Suddenly, the crow flew away, leaving Shell safe and unharmed. Shell was so surprised that her friends had come to her rescue. With the help of her friends, Shell was able to reach the top of the tree and fulfill her wish. From that day onwards, she always remained thankful to her friends.
