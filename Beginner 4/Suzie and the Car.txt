Category: Beginner
Title: Suzie and the Car

Suzie was a small girl with a big dream. She wanted to go very fast. One day she saw a bright red car that was fast and shiny. The car was speeding through a beautiful landscape. The trees were tall and the grass was thick. The sun was shining and birds were chirping. Suzie wanted to go as fast as the car. She started running as fast as she could. She ran so fast that the wind pulled against her hair. She felt like she was flying through the landscape. When Suzie stopped running, she saw the car was no longer there. But she still felt fast and free. She smiled to the sky and the trees, feeling thankful for the thick, beautiful landscape.

Once upon a time, there was a wise cat named Kitty. Kitty liked to walk around the yard and sit on the porch. One day, while Kitty was on the porch, a big dog named Max came by. Max wanted to play with Kitty, but Kitty was scared of Max. Max was too big and loud. Kitty tried to walk away, but Max followed her. Kitty didn't want to play with Max, so she had to think of a way to make him go away. Kitty had a smart idea. She walked to the tree and climbed up. Max couldn't climb the tree because he was too big. Max barked and barked, but Kitty was safe in the tree. After a while, Max got tired and walked away. Kitty was happy and went back to the porch to rest.

Tom was a curious boy who liked to touch and see everything. One day, he saw a big knob on the wall of his room. He wondered what it was for. He climbed on his bed and reached for the knob. "Tom, what are you doing?" his mom asked, coming into the room. "I want to see what this knob does," Tom said. "That knob is not a toy, Tom. It is for the heater. It makes the room warm or cold. If you turn it too much, you might get too hot or too cold," his mom explained. Tom looked at the knob and his mom. He wanted to try it, but he also did not want to be too hot or too cold. He decided to listen to his mom. "Okay, mom. I will not touch the knob. Can you show me how it works?" he asked. "Sure, Tom. I will show you. But first, you have to get down from the bed. It is not safe to stand on the bed," his mom said. Tom nodded and got down from the bed. He held his mom's hand and watched her turn the knob a little bit. He felt the air change in the room. "See, Tom? If I turn the knob this way, the room gets warmer. If I turn it the other way, the room gets cooler. But we have to be careful not to turn it too much, or it will be uncomfortable," his mom said. Tom understood. He thanked his mom for explaining the knob to him. He was still curious, but he also knew how to be careful. He hugged his mom and went to play with his toys. He was happy to learn something new.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a colorful mushroom. It was very special. The mushroom could talk and walk. It had many friends like birds, bugs, and trees. They all liked to play together. One day, the colorful mushroom wanted to gain more friends. It walked and walked to find new friends. The mushroom found a big river. It saw a big fish in the water. The mushroom said, "Hello, fish! Can we be friends?" The fish said, "Yes!" The mushroom was very happy. It wanted to play with the fish. But the mushroom could not swim. The fish said, "Jump in the water, I will help you." The mushroom was scared but wanted to be brave. It jumped into the water to play with the fish. The colorful mushroom tried to swim, but it could not. The fish tried to help, but it was too late. The mushroom went under the water and did not come back up. The fish was very sad. The other friends in the forest missed the colorful mushroom too. They all learned to be more careful when playing with new friends.
