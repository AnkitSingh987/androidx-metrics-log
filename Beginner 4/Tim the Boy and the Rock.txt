Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Rock

One day, a boy named Tim found a hard board. He put the board on two big rocks. Tim said, "I will rock on this board like a fun toy!" Tim's friend, Sue, came over. She saw Tim rocking on the board. Sue said, "Can I try too?" Tim said, "Yes, but be careful. It is hard to rock on the board." Sue started to rock on the board. Suddenly, the board broke in half! Tim and Sue were surprised. They did not know that the board had a big crack in it. Now, they had two smaller boards. Tim said, "Let's use these boards to make something new and fun!"

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play with crayons and paper. They make marks of many colors on the paper. They make marks of flowers, stars, animals and houses. One day, Tom and Lily are playing with crayons and paper in the living room. Lily makes a mark of a big heart on the paper. She shows it to Tom and says, "This is for you, Tom. You are my friend. I give you a hug." Tom smiles and says, "Thank you, Lily. You are my friend too. I give you a hug." He makes a mark of a big heart on the paper and shows it to Lily. They hug each other and laugh. They are happy. But then, Mom comes into the living room. She sees the marks on the paper. She also sees the marks on the wall, the sofa, the table and the floor. She is not happy. She is serious. She says, "Tom and Lily, what have you done? You have made a mess. You have marked everything. You are not supposed to mark anything but the paper. You are in trouble." Tom and Lily are scared. They say, "We are sorry, Mom. We did not mean to mark anything but the paper. We were just playing. We were just making marks of love." Mom sighs and says, "I know you were just playing. I know you were just making marks of love. But you have to be careful. You have to respect the things in the house. You have to clean up the mess. Do you understand?" Tom and Lily nod and say, "We understand, Mom. We will clean up the mess. We will not mark anything but the paper. We love you, Mom." Mom says, "I love you too, Tom and Lily. But you have to learn from your mistake. Now, come on. Let's get some soap and water and a cloth. Let's clean up the marks." Tom and Lily say, "OK, Mom." They follow Mom and help her clean up the marks. They are sorry. They are also glad. They still have their marks of love on the paper. They still have their hugs. They still have their friend. They still have their Mom.

Once upon a time, Annabelle was walking in the woods. It was a deep and dark forest. Suddenly, Annabelle heard a loud noise. It sounded like something was spraying. Annabelle was scared and ran away. But then, a friendly voice said, "Hey there, don't be scared". Annabelle looked around and saw a mysterious figure. He was covered in black from head to toe and had a spray bottle in his hand. The figure said, "I'm spray painting grass and rocks. I need to make this forest look more beautiful." Annabelle smiled and said, "That sounds great. What color are you going to spray?" The figure said, "I'm going to spray a deep black." Then he winked at Annabelle and said, "See you around". Annabelle smiled and waved goodbye. She continued walking in the forest, happy to have made a new friend.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to build a big house with his toy blocks. He was very patient as he put each block on top of the other. Tim was making a strong frame for his house so it would not fall down. His little sister, Sue, came into the room and saw the big house Tim was building. Sue wanted to play too, but she did not want to wait. She said, "I want to build now!" Tim did not want to stop building his house. "No, Sue, you must wait. I am not done yet," he said. Sue was upset and started to cry. Their mom heard the crying and came into the room. She saw what was happening and said, "Tim, please let Sue help you build the house. You can both work together." Tim agreed, and they both built the big house together, happy and patient.
