Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Bathtub

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to take a bath. He went to the bathroom and saw the empty bathtub. He thought, "I need water in the bathtub to take a bath." So, he turned on the faucet and filled the bathtub with water. As Tim started to play in the water, he saw his toy boat. He wanted to make big waves for his boat. He jumped up and down in the bathtub. Suddenly, he heard a loud "CRACK!" Tim had broken the bathtub! Tim's mom heard the noise and came running in. "What happened, Tim?" she asked. Tim said, "I was playing and I broke the bathtub." His mom looked at the broken bathtub and said, "That's okay, we can fix it. But next time, be more careful when you play in the bathtub." Tim promised to be more careful and they both laughed at the unexpected adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a youth named Tim. Tim loved to wear his big red hat. One day, Tim went for a walk in the dry park. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Tim was so happy to see the tree. As Tim climbed the tree, he saw something shiny. He wanted to see what it was. Tim went higher and higher. When he got close, he saw that it was a key! Tim was very surprised. He took the key and put it in his pocket. Tim went back home and showed the key to his mom. She was shocked! His mom said, "This key is magic!" Tim was so excited. With the magic key, he could open any door. Now, Tim could go on many adventures. And he always wore his big red hat.

One day, a boy went to the store with his mom. They saw a big, expensive muffin. The boy said, "Mom, can I have the muffin?" The mom replied, "No, it is too big and expensive." The next day, the boy went back to the store alone. He saw the big muffin again. He wanted the muffin so much. So, he asked the store man, "Can I have the muffin?" The store man replied, "You can have it if you help me in the store." The boy was happy and helped the store man. But when he got the muffin, he found out it was not a real muffin. It was a toy muffin! The boy was sad, but then he thought it was funny. He took the toy muffin home and played with it all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He had a small toy dog that he loved very much. One day, Tom played a foolish game with his dog. He put the dog on a board and pushed it down a hill. The dog fell off the board and got hurt. Tom was very sad. He wanted to help his dog heal. He took the dog home and put a bandage on its leg. He hugged the dog and said sorry. Tom's mom saw what he did and told him that he should be more careful when playing with his toys. From that day on, Tom learned to be more careful with his toys and friends. He never played foolish games again. The moral of the story is to always be careful and think about the feelings of others.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly cat named Tom. He loved to play all day long. Tom liked to run, jump, and sign to his friends when it was time to eat. His friends were a dog, a bird, and a fish. One day, at noon, Tom and his friends were playing in the park. They were very happy. Tom saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. He signed to his friends to watch him. The dog barked, the bird chirped, and the fish swam in a circle. Tom climbed higher and higher. But then, he fell from the tree. Tom was hurt and sad. His friends tried to help him, but they could not. Tom could not play with his friends anymore. They all felt very sad.
