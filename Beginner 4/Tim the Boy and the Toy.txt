Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Toy

One day, a boy named Tim went to play in his backyard. He liked to be creative and make fun games. Today, he wanted to make a joke for his friend, Sam. Tim set up a funny joke in the backyard. He put a toy snake on a chair. When Sam sat on the chair, he would see the toy snake and laugh. Tim waited for Sam to come over. When Sam came to play, he saw the chair and sat down. But, the toy snake did not make him laugh. Instead, Sam picked up the toy snake and said, "Look, Tim! I found a new toy!" Tim was surprised. His joke did not work, but they both played with the toy snake and had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a funny little dog named Spot. Spot had a big, round handle on his back. He loved to play and run around with his friends in the park. One day, Spot saw a big, red ball near a tree. He wanted to play with it, but his friend, a small bird named Chirpy, told him to promise not to touch it. Spot promised Chirpy he wouldn't touch the ball. But when Chirpy flew away, Spot broke his promise and started to play with the ball. He pushed the ball with his handle, and it rolled down a hill. The ball hit a big rock and broke! Spot was very sad and scared. When Chirpy came back, he saw the broken ball and knew Spot had touched it. Chirpy was sad that Spot broke his promise. Spot said sorry to Chirpy and learned that keeping promises is important. From that day on, Spot always kept his promises to his friends. They all played happily together, and Spot became a very good dog. The moral of the story is to always keep your promises to your friends.

One day, a cold frog named Tim lived in a swamp. Tim was not happy. He wanted to be warm and play with his friends. But his friends did not want to play because it was too cold. Tim saw a big sun in the sky. He said, "Sun, please make me warm. I want to play with my friends." The sun did not answer. Tim was sad. He sat on a rock and cried. A nice bird saw Tim crying. The bird said, "Why are you sad?" Tim said, "I am cold and want to be warm. I asked the sun, but it did not help." The bird thought for a moment. Then she said, "I have an idea! I will fly up and talk to the sun for you." The bird flew up to the sun. She demanded, "Sun, please make Tim warm so he can play with his friends." The sun felt bad for Tim. The sun said, "Okay, I will make Tim warm." The sun shone brightly on Tim. Tim felt warm and happy. He played with his friends all day. From that day on, the swamp was never too cold for Tim and his friends to play.

Once upon a time, there was a young and helpless girl. She loved to play with her friends and family, but one day a man came to steal her away. The girl felt scared and tried to run away, but the man was too strong for her. He grabbed the girl and took her away. The girl wept and yelled for help, but no one came to save her from the man. He took her to a far away place and kept her there. The poor girl was so young and helpless and she had no choice but to stay with the man. The girl was very sad and scared and her family didn't even know where she was. She longed to go home to her family and friends, but the man wouldn't let her. She was stuck with the man and felt so lonely, helpless and scared. Sadly, the girl never got to go home again.

Once upon a time, there was a lively monster who loved to spend time with his friends. He loved playing with toys, running around, and having lots of fun! One day, the monster decided to spend the day outside, thinking of what to do. He spotted some birds in the sky and watched them soar through the clouds. Then, while spending time on the grass, he found some buttercups and picked one to give to his mom. Then the monster came across a playground full of children and happily joined in with all of the fun activities. He chased the children around, and laughed as all their laughter filled the air. Finally, when it was time to go home, the monster said goodbye to all his friends with a big hug and promised to see them the following day. He spent the rest of the day thinking of new ways to have fun. The end.
