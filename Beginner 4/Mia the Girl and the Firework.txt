Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Firework

One day, a kind girl named Mia saw a firework in the sky. It was big and bright. Mia liked the firework a lot. She said, "Wow! I want to see more fireworks!" Mia asked her friend Tom, "Do you know where the firework came from?" Tom did not know. They went to ask their friend Sue. "Sue, do you know where the firework came from?" Mia asked. Sue did not know, but she wanted to help. Sue said, "Let's look for more fireworks!" All three friends went to look for more fireworks. They were happy to find more and watch them together.

Lily and Sam were playing with clay in the garden. They liked to make shapes and animals with the clay. Lily made a snake and Sam made a frog. "Look at my frog!" Sam said. "It can hop and croak." "Look at my snake!" Lily said. "It can slither and hiss." They showed each other their clay animals and made noises. "Can I join your snake and frog?" a voice asked. They looked up and saw Ben, their neighbor. He had a ball of clay in his hand. "Sure, you can join us!" Lily said. "What are you going to make?" "I don't know yet," Ben said. "Maybe a bird or a fish." He sat down next to them and started to shape his clay. The sun was shining and the clay was wet. It felt soft and sticky in their hands. They had fun making and sharing their clay creations. They were happy to play together.

Once upon a time, there was a bird who loved to sing. This bird had a special song. The song was very pretty. The bird sang the song every day. The bird was happy. One day, a big, fierce cat heard the bird's song. The cat liked the song too. The cat wanted to be friends with the bird. The cat went to find the bird. The bird did not know about the cat. The cat found the bird and said, "I like your song. Can we be friends?" The bird was scared of the fierce cat, but said yes. They played together for a while. Then, the bird realized the cat was not a good friend. The cat wanted to eat the bird. The bird tried to fly away, but the cat was too fast. The cat caught the bird. The bird was very sad. The pretty song was gone.

Once upon a time, there was a rich crab who lived by the sea. He had many friends who played with him every day. They loved to play in the sand and swim in the water. The crab was very happy. One day, the tide went out and the crab's friends wanted to play in the water. But the crab was scared of the deep water. His friends said, "Don't worry, we will help you." They allowed the crab to hold onto their fins as they swam together. As they swam, the crab became braver and started to enjoy the water. He was no longer scared of the deep water. The tide came back in, and they all played together in the sand and water. From that day on, the crab was very happy and loved to swim with his friends.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lucy lived in a small village. Lucy loved to get post from her friends. But one day, the sky was gloomy, and Lucy felt sad. She did not want to go outside to play because of the dark clouds. Lucy's mom saw her looking sad and said, "Why don't you accept the gloomy day and find something fun to do inside?" Lucy thought about it and decided to make a pretty card to send to her friend. She used colors and drew a big sun to make it happy. When Lucy finished her card, she went to the post to send it. The next day, the sun came out, and Lucy got a happy card from her friend too. Lucy learned that even on gloomy days, we can accept them and find something fun to do. And sometimes, that can make others happy too.
