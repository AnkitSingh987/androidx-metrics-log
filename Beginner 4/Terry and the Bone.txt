Category: Beginner
Title: Terry and the Bone

Terry was an old man who suffered for a long time. He had a sore bone in his leg and it hurt very badly. Every day it ached and sometimes it was too hard to walk. Terry's family wanted to help him, so they went to the doctor. The doctor said that the bone needed fixing so he could be strong again. With a special machine, they made the bone strong and healthy. Every day Terry did special exercises to get his bone fixed. After a long time of exercising, the bone was strong and Terry could walk without pain. It took a long time, but Terry's bone was finally healthy and strong.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird who loved to whistle. He lived in a nest with his egg. The bird was very happy and excited to have a baby bird soon. One day, a big, ignorant cat came by the nest. The cat did not know that the bird was a good friend to all the other animals. The cat saw the egg and thought it would be a good meal. The little bird tried to whistle for help. But the cat did not care. The cat took the egg and ate it. The little bird was very sad and cried. The other animals heard the bird's cry but they were too late to help. The bird learned that not all animals are friends, and the cat never knew how special the egg was.

Milly was a happy three-year-old. She loved playing with her friends, drawing pictures for her family, and playing in the park. One day, Milly wanted to play with her favourite toy. She went to the park, but she couldn't find it. She asked her friend, "Where is my toy?" Her friend replied, "I hate to tell you this, but a mean trap caught it. It was very harsh and there was nothing we could do." Milly was sad. She hated the trap. Later that day, she put breadcrumbs in the trap so no other animals would get caught. Hopefully, someone would be able to free her toy.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy cat named Tom. Tom did not like to play with his toys. He only liked to play with a sock. The sock was red and very soft. One day, Tom's friend, a dog named Max, came to play. Max wanted to play with a ball, but Tom wanted to play with the sock. Max tried to get Tom to play with the ball, but Tom ignored him. He just wanted to play with the sock. Max was sad, but he had an idea. He took the sock and put it on the ball. Now, Tom and Max could play together. Tom was not grumpy anymore, and they both had fun playing with the sock and the ball.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. They saw a big, tight net in the water. Tim asked, "Mom, what is that?" His mom said, "That is a net to catch fish, Tim." As they got closer, they saw a jellyfish appear in the net. Tim was very excited. He had never seen a jellyfish before. "Wow, look at that!" Tim said. His mom was happy too. But then, something unexpected happened. The jellyfish started to talk! It said, "Please help me, I am stuck!" Tim and his mom were very surprised. They helped the jellyfish get out of the net. The jellyfish said, "Thank you!" and swam away. Tim and his mom smiled and went back to playing on the beach.

Sam liked to help his mom in the kitchen. He liked to stir and pour and taste. One day, his mom said, "We are going to make a cake. A lemon cake. Do you like lemon cake, Sam?" Sam nodded. He loved lemon cake. It was sweet and sour and soft. His mom gave him a lemon and a knife. She said, "Be careful, Sam. The knife is sharp. Cut the lemon in half and squeeze the juice in this bowl." Sam did as his mom said. He cut the lemon in half and squeezed the juice. But some of the juice squirted in his eye. It stung and burned. Sam dropped the lemon and the knife and cried. His mom hugged him and wiped his eye. She said, "I'm sorry, Sam. That must have hurt. Let me see your eye. It's okay. It will get better soon." Sam felt better, but he was still grumpy. He didn't want to help his mom anymore. He wanted to eat the cake. He said, "When will the cake be ready, mom?" His mom said, "Not yet, Sam. We have to mix the batter and put it in the oven. Then we have to wait for it to rise and bake. It will take some time, Sam. But I have a surprise for you. Do you want to see it?" Sam nodded. He liked surprises. His mom opened the fridge and took out a bowl. She said, "This is the frosting for the cake. It's also lemon. Do you want to taste it, Sam?" Sam smiled. He loved frosting. He dipped his finger in the bowl and licked it. It was sweet and sour and creamy. He said, "Mmm, this is good, mom. Can I have some more?" His mom laughed and said, "Sure, Sam. But not too much. We have to save some for the cake. Come on, let's finish the cake together. It will be delicious, Sam. You'll see." Sam forgot his grumpiness and helped his mom finish the cake. He stirred and poured and tasted. He watched the cake rise and bake in the oven. He spread the frosting on the cake. He decorated it with lemon slices. He was proud of his cake. He said, "Can we eat it now, mom?" His mom said, "Yes, Sam. We can eat it now. You did a great job, Sam. You are a good helper. I love you, Sam." Sam hugged his mom and said, "I love you too, mom. And I love lemon cake."
