Category: Beginner
Title: The Fair and the Toys

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a fair. It was a fun place where kids could play games and win toys. One day, a little boy named Tim saw a big scale at the fair. He wanted to try it out. Tim asked his mom, "Can I come and try the scale, please?" His mom said, "Sure, but be careful, Tim." Tim was very excited. He stood on the scale, but it was too big for him. He tried to reach the top, but he could not. Tim's friend, Sam, saw him trying. Sam said, "I can help you, but you have to give me your toy." Tim thought about it and said, "Okay, help me." Sam helped Tim to reach the top of the scale, but when they were done, Sam took Tim's toy and ran away. Tim was sad because he lost his toy, and the scale did not work.

One day, a big frog named Bob met a small bird named Tim. Tim was very impatient. He wanted to play a game with Bob. Bob said, "Let's play a game called repeat. I will say a word, and you will say it back to me." Tim was excited to play. Bob said, "Muscle!" Tim tried to say "Muscle," but it was hard for him. He said, "M-m-muscle?" Bob laughed and said, "Good job, Tim!" They played the game many times, and Tim got better at saying the word. Suddenly, a big wind came and blew Tim up into a tree. Bob was worried. He used his strong muscles to jump high and save Tim. Tim was so happy that he gave Bob a big hug. They became best friends and played the repeat game every day.

Once upon a time, in a wide green park, a little girl named Lucy found a magic stone. The stone was very pretty and shiny. Lucy loved the stone so much that she wanted to make it even shinier. So, she decided to polish it. Lucy took a soft cloth and started to polish the magic stone. As she polished it, the stone began to speak! "Thank you for making me shiny," said the magic stone. Lucy was very surprised but happy to have a talking stone. The magic stone told Lucy that it could teach her one special word. Lucy was excited and said, "Please teach me the special word." The stone taught her the word "friend." Lucy said the word, and the stone became her best friend. They played together in the wide green park every day, and they were both very happy.

One day, a big van came down the street. It was a thick and sunny day. The van stopped in front of a little red house. The van was here to deliver a big box. A man got out of the van. He said, "I have a big box for you!" The boy who lived in the little red house came out to see. He was very happy. He said, "Thank you for the big box!" The boy opened the big box. Inside was a big, soft teddy bear. The boy loved the teddy bear. He hugged it and played with it all day. The van drove away, and the boy waved goodbye.

Once upon a time, a kind duck named Dolly lived near a big pond. Every day, Dolly loved to splash in the water with her friends. They would play and swim all day long. One day, Dolly went on a trip to find new friends. She walked and walked, but she could not find anyone. Suddenly, she saw something big and round. It was not a duck, but it looked fun to play with. Dolly went closer and gave the big round thing a big splash. To her surprise, it splashed back! It was a big bouncy ball! Dolly and the ball became friends and played together all day. They splashed and bounced, and Dolly was happy she found a new friend on her trip.

Sara and Ben were bored. It was a gloomy day and they could not go outside. They looked at the window and saw ice on the ground. They wished they could play with the ice. "Mom, can we go outside?" Sara asked. "No, dear, it is too cold and slippery. You might get hurt," Mom said. "Can we do something fun inside?" Ben asked. "Sure, how about I teach you how to make ice cream?" Mom said. "Ice cream? How do you make ice cream?" Sara and Ben said. Mom smiled and took them to the kitchen. She showed them a big bowl, some milk, some sugar, some vanilla, and some salt. She also showed them a small bag, a big bag, and some ice cubes. "First, we put the milk, the sugar, and the vanilla in the small bag. Then we seal it tight. This is our ice cream mix," Mom said. "Then, we put the ice cubes and the salt in the big bag. This is our ice maker," Mom said. "Then, we put the small bag inside the big bag and shake it hard. The salt makes the ice colder and the ice makes the mix freeze. This is how we make ice cream," Mom said. Sara and Ben nodded and helped Mom with the bags. They shook them for a long time. They felt the small bag get colder and harder. "Is it ready?" Sara asked. "Let's see," Mom said. She opened the bags and took out the small bag. She cut a corner and squeezed out some ice cream. "Wow, it looks like ice cream!" Ben said. "It is ice cream! And it is yummy!" Sara said. They all tasted the ice cream and smiled. They forgot about the gloomy day and enjoyed their treat. "Mom, you are the best teacher!" Sara and Ben said. "Thank you, my sweeties. You are the best learners!" Mom said.
