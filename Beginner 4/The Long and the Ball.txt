Category: Beginner
Title: The Long and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a long, long road. On this road, a little girl named Lily walked every day. She liked to see the pretty sunrise in the morning. The sunrise made the sky look like a big, beautiful painting. One day, Lily wanted to add more fun to her walk. She took a big, red ball with her. She bounced the ball as she walked down the long road. The ball went up and down, up and down. Lily was happy to play with her ball. As the sun came up, the pretty sunrise made Lily smile. She knew it was time to go back home. She took her red ball and walked down the long road. When she got home, she told her mommy and daddy about her fun walk with the ball. They all hugged, and Lily felt so loved.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to take photos of everything. One day, he took a photo of a big, yummy cake in the shop. Tim wanted to eat the cake, but he had no money. Tim went home and showed the photo to his mom. He asked her if she could buy the cake. Mom said no. Tim was sad. He did not accept that he could not have the yummy cake. The next day, Tim had an idea. He took a photo of his toys and showed it to his friend, Sam. Tim asked Sam if he wanted to trade toys. Sam said yes. Tim was happy. He took the toys to the shop and traded them for the yummy cake. In the end, Tim learned that he could solve problems in different ways. He took a photo of the yummy cake to remember how he got it. His mom was proud of him, and they both enjoyed the cake together.

Sara and Ben are friends. They like to draw with crayons. Sara has a big box of crayons. She has many colors. Ben has only one crayon. It is blue. "Can I have a red crayon, please?" Ben asks Sara. He wants to draw a fire truck. Sara is polite. She says, "Sure, you can have a red crayon. Here you go." She gives Ben a red crayon from her box. "Thank you, Sara. You are very nice," Ben says. He smiles and starts to draw his fire truck. Sara is happy. She likes to share with Ben. She draws a flower with her yellow crayon. But then, Lily comes. Lily is not polite. She sees Sara's box of crayons. She wants them all. She grabs the box from Sara. "Hey, give that back!" Sara says. She is angry and sad. "No, they are mine now!" Lily says. She is greedy and rude. Ben sees what Lily does. He does not like it. He says, "That is not fair, Lily. You have to share. Sara was nice to me. She gave me a red crayon. You have to give her back her box." Lily does not listen. She runs away with the box of crayons. Sara and Ben are sad. They have no crayons to draw with. They look at each other. "Maybe we can find some more crayons," Ben says. He tries to cheer up Sara. "Maybe," Sara says. She hopes Lily will learn to be polite.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived an ugly duck. The ugly duck didn't have many friends. One day, the ugly duck went to the pond to drink water. He saw a beautiful swan there. The swan was wearing a pretty cap. The ugly duck wished he had a cap too. The ugly duck said to the swan, "Hello, I like your cap. Can I have one too?" The swan smiled and said, "Sure, let's go find a cap for you." So, they went to look for a cap together. They found a little shop that sold caps. The ugly duck tried on a cap that was just like the swan's cap. It fit perfectly on his head. The ugly duck and the swan went back to the pond. They both wore their pretty caps and drank water together. The other ducks at the pond saw the ugly duck with his new friend and his pretty cap. They wanted to be friends with the ugly duck too. From that day on, the ugly duck was not lonely anymore. He had many friends, and they all wore pretty caps and drank water together at the pond.
