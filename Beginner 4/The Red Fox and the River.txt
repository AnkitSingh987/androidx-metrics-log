Category: Beginner
Title: The Red Fox and the River

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a little red fox. This fox was incredible at playing games with his friends. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. One day, after playing in the mud, the fox was very dirty. His mom said, "You need to bathe before dinner." The fox did not like to bathe, but he knew he had to listen to his mom. So, he went to the river to wash off the mud. As the fox bathed in the river, he splashed water on his face and wiggled his tail. The water felt nice and cool. When he was all clean, he went back home. His mom was happy to see him clean and they had a yummy dinner together. The little fox learned that taking a bath wasn't so bad after all.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to draw pictures. One day, he drew a picture of a big, dangerous lion. "Mom, look at my picture!" Tim said, showing his mom the drawing. "This lion is dangerous, but he is also my friend." Mom smiled and said, "That's a great picture, Tim! But remember, real lions can be dangerous, so we must be careful around them." One night, Tim had a bad dream about the dangerous lion. He woke up scared and crying. Mom came into his room and gave him a big hug to comfort him. "It's okay, Tim," Mom said softly. "It was just a dream. The lion in your picture can't hurt you." Tim felt better and went back to sleep, knowing he was safe with his mom nearby.

One day, a little boy named Tim found an ugly whistle in his toy box. He did not know how it got there. Tim thought it was very strange. He wanted to play with it and see what it could do. So, he took a big breath and blew into the whistle. To his surprise, the whistle made a loud noise that scared his cat. The cat ran away, and Tim felt bad. He did not want to scare his cat. Tim's mom heard the noise and came into the room. "What was that sound?" she asked. Tim showed her the ugly whistle. "I found this whistle and I blew it. I did not know it would be so loud." Tim's mom looked at the whistle and said, "This whistle is not a toy. It is used to call for help when you need it. You should not play with it. You should surrender it to me, and I will keep it safe." Tim felt sad, but he knew his mom was right. He gave the ugly whistle to his mom, and she put it away. Tim learned that not all things in his toy box were toys, and he should be careful when playing with new things.

Once upon a time there was a cute bear. He was all alone in his home, deep in the woods. Every day he would pray for a friend. One day he noticed something shiny in the leaves. He ran over to see what it was, and it was a cardboard box! He opened it up and inside was a cute little rabbit. The bear was so happy that he hugged the rabbit. From then on, the two were the best of friends. They would play together in the woods and built cardboard castles. They even prayed together every night before bed. The bear was so thankful for his new friend, and so was the rabbit.

One day, a big cat went for a walk. The cat saw a fancy hat. It was very pretty. The cat wanted to wear the hat. But the hat was up high in a tree. The cat tried to jump, but it was too high. The sky got dark. The cat knew rain was coming. The cat did not like rain. The cat wanted the fancy hat to stay dry. So, the cat had an idea. The cat took a deep breath and roared very loud. The roar was so strong, it shook the tree. The fancy hat fell down. The cat put on the hat just as the rain started. The cat was happy and stayed dry in the rain.
