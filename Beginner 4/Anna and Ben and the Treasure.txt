Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben and the Treasure

Anna and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to slide, swing and run. But their favorite thing was to lift rocks and look for bugs. They found ants, worms and beetles. They were very happy. One day, they saw a big rock near the fence. It was bigger than their heads. They wondered what was under it. They decided to lift it together. They pushed and pulled and grunted. The rock was very heavy. "Come on, we can do it!" Anna said. "Yes, we can!" Ben said. They lifted the rock a little bit. They peeked under it. They saw something shiny and colorful. It was incredible. It was a treasure! They lifted the rock more and more. They saw coins, jewels and toys. They gasped and smiled. They could not believe their eyes. They heard a voice behind them. It was the park keeper. He was angry. "Hey, what are you doing? Put that rock back! That's my treasure!" he shouted. Anna and Ben were scared. They dropped the rock. It made a loud noise. They ran away. They left the treasure behind. They ran to their mom. She was sitting on a bench. She saw their faces. She asked them what happened. They told her everything. She hugged them and said they were brave. She said they should not touch other people's things. She said they should say sorry to the park keeper. They nodded. They felt bad. They walked back to the park keeper. He was still there. He was still angry. They said sorry to him. They said they did not know it was his treasure. They said they were curious. The park keeper looked at them. He sighed. He said he was sorry too. He said he was not really angry. He said he was lonely. He said he collected the treasure to make himself happy. He said he had no friends. Anna and Ben felt sorry for him. They said they would be his friends. They said they liked his treasure. They asked him if he would share it with them. The park keeper smiled. He said he would. He said he had too much treasure. He said he would rather have friends. He lifted the rock again. He showed them his treasure. He gave them some coins, jewels and toys. They thanked him and hugged him. They played with the treasure and the park keeper. They had fun. They forgot about the slide, the swing and the run. They only heard laughter and joy. They did not hear silence. They felt incredible.

Once upon a time, a messy boy named Tim went to a lecture with his mom. The lecture was about how to gain new friends. Tim was very excited, because he wanted to make new friends at school. During the lecture, Tim's mom said, "Tim, listen carefully. If you want to gain friends, you need to be clean and tidy." But Tim didn't like to be clean. He loved to play in the mud and make a mess. One day at school, Tim tried to make friends with another boy named Sam. Tim said, "Hi, Sam! Do you want to be friends?" Sam looked at Tim's messy clothes and said, "No, thank you. You are too messy." Tim felt sad, and he didn't gain any friends.

Lily was a snowman. She had a hat, a scarf, a carrot nose and two coal eyes. She liked to play with the children who made her. They threw snowballs, made snow angels and sang songs. Lily was happy. But one day, the sun came out. It was very bright and warm. Lily felt hot. She saw the snow around her start to melt. She saw her hat and scarf fall to the ground. She saw her carrot nose and coal eyes slide off her face. She felt smaller and smaller. She was scared. "Help!" she cried. "I don't want to melt!" She saw a bird on a branch. The bird looked at her and said, "Don't be sad, Lily. You are not alone. I am here with you. And soon, you will be part of the water. The water will go to the sky and become a cloud. Then, you will come back as snow. You will be a snowman again." Lily felt a little better. She smiled at the bird and said, "Thank you, bird. You are very kind. Will you be my friend?" The bird nodded and said, "Of course, Lily. I will be your friend. And I will sing to you until you fall asleep." Lily closed her eyes and listened to the bird's song. She felt peaceful. She was not lonely anymore. She was an inch away from becoming a cloud. She was ready to start a new adventure.
