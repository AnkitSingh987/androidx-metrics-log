Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Fish and the Ocean

Once upon a time, there was a big fish named Bob. Bob lived in a big ocean with his fish friends. Bob was a very obedient fish. He always listened to his mom and dad. Bob loved to swim and play with his friends in the ocean. One day, Bob and his friends wanted to have a race. They wanted to see who was the fastest swimmer in the ocean. Bob was very excited. He wanted to win the race and show his mom and dad how fast he could swim. The race began and all the fish swam as fast as they could. Bob swam very fast too. In the end, Bob won the race! All his friends were happy for him. Bob was very proud and his mom and dad were very proud too. From that day on, Bob was known as the fastest fish in the ocean.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a puppy. The puppy was from an unknown place. It was very cute. Tim wanted to take the puppy home. Tim asked his mom, "Can I keep the puppy, please?" His mom said, "No, we don't know where it came from." But Tim insisted, "Please, Mom! I will take good care of it!" Just then, a girl came running. She said, "I lost my puppy! Have you seen it?" Tim and his mom looked at the unknown puppy. They knew it was her puppy. Tim felt sad but knew the right thing to do. He said to the girl, "Here is your puppy! I found it!" The girl was so happy. She thanked Tim and gave him a big hug. Tim learned that it is good to help others, even if it means giving up something you want.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim wanted to paint. He loved to paint big pictures with lots of colors. Tim was very happy when he painted. One day, it was very cold outside. Tim put on his warm coat and went to find his friend, Sue. When Tim found Sue, he asked her a question. He said, "Do you want to paint with me?" Sue said, "Yes, I want to paint with you, but it is cold outside. Let's paint inside." So, Tim and Sue went inside Sue's house to paint. They painted many pictures with lots of colors. But, they made a big mess with the paint. The floor and walls were covered in paint. When Sue's mom came home, she was very angry. She said, "No more painting for Tim and Sue!" Tim and Sue were sad because they could not paint together anymore.

Nina was feeling fierce. She wanted to fix up her toys, so she asked her mom to help. "Mom, will you help me fix the toys?" Nina asked her mom. Her mom smiled. She knew just what Nina wanted. "Sure, honey," Mom said. "Let's get to work!" They gathered up all the toys and started fixing them. They replaced broken parts and tested them out. Nina was so excited to see all her toys looking brand new again. The work was tiring and tough, but it was worth it when they were done. "Way to go, sweetheart!" Mom said. "You did a great job!" "Thanks, Mom!" Nina smiled. She felt fierce—she had fixed all her toys!

Lily was a little girl who liked to play with her toys. She had a big teddy bear, a small doll, and a soft cushion. She liked to pretend that they were her friends and have tea parties with them. One day, Lily was very tired after playing all morning. She wanted to sleep, but she did not want to leave her toys alone. She had an idea. She took her cushion and put it on the floor. She said to her toys, "Come, let's sleep together on the cushion. It is cozy and warm." She lay down on the cushion and hugged her teddy bear. She put her doll next to her and covered them with a blanket. She closed her eyes and said, "Good night, my friends. I love you." Her toys did not say anything, but they smiled. They were happy to be with Lily. They felt safe and snug on the cushion. They closed their eyes and slept too. Lily's mom came to check on her. She saw Lily and her toys sleeping on the cushion. She smiled and kissed Lily on the forehead. She whispered, "Sweet dreams, my darling. I love you." She turned off the light and left the room. She was proud of Lily. She knew that Lily was a kind and creative girl. She hoped that Lily would always have fun and make friends. She hoped that Lily would always sleep well and wake up happy.
