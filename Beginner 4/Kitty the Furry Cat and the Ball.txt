Category: Beginner
Title: Kitty the Furry Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a furry cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with her ball and run around the house. One day, Kitty saw her family gather in the kitchen. They were all sitting around a big table. Kitty jumped on the table and said, "What are we doing?" Mommy smiled and said, "We are eating dinner, Kitty. Come join us!" Kitty was very happy and sat next to her best friend, a little girl named Lucy. Lucy gave Kitty a small plate of food. They ate and laughed together. After dinner, Kitty and Lucy played with the ball. They had a fun day, and Kitty was happy to be with her family at the table.

Once upon a time, there was a band of three friends. They loved to play together. They had a ball, a car, and a big box of toys. Every day, they would meet and have lots of fun. One day, they were playing with their ball. They were very happy. But then, they were careless. They did not see the big rock. The ball hit the rock and split in two. The friends were very sad. They did not know what to do. Just then, a kind girl came by. She saw the split ball and had an idea. She took out some tape and fixed the ball. The friends were so happy! They learned to be more careful. And the kind girl joined their band. Now, they were four friends playing together.

Lily and Ben were playing in the garage. They saw a big sack with many things inside. They wanted to see what was in the sack. They opened the sack and took out some things. They saw a screw, a hammer, a saw, and some nails. They thought they could make something with them. They started to hit the screw with the hammer. They made a loud noise. They did not hear Mom coming. Mom saw what they were doing. She was angry. She said, "What are you doing with my tools? You could hurt yourself! You are not allowed to touch them! Put them back in the sack now!" Lily and Ben felt guilty. They knew they did something bad. They said, "Sorry, Mom. We just wanted to play. We did not mean to make a mess." They put the tools back in the sack. They hugged Mom and said, "We love you, Mom. Please don't be mad at us." Mom was still angry, but she also loved them. She said, "I love you too, but you have to listen to me. These tools are not toys. They are dangerous. You have to ask me before you touch anything in the garage. Do you understand?" Lily and Ben nodded. They said, "We understand, Mom. We will not do it again." Mom said, "OK, then. Come on, let's go inside. It's time for a snack. But first, you have to wash your hands. They are dirty from the tools." Lily and Ben followed Mom. They washed their hands and ate some cookies. They learned their lesson. They never touched the tools in the sack again.

Once upon a time, there was a big black dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and go on adventures. He had many friends in his neighborhood who liked to play with him. One day, Spot found a big stage in the park. The stage was very high, and Spot wanted to go on it. He asked his friends to help him climb the stage. They all worked together and pushed Spot up onto the stage. When Spot got on the stage, he was very happy. He danced and played with his friends. They all had a great time on the stage. Then, they went home and told their families about their fun day on the stage.

One hot day, a boy named Tim was very hot. He wanted to cool down. He saw a cooler under a big tree. Tim walked to the cooler and opened it. Inside, there were cold drinks. Tim was very happy. Tim's friend, Sue, came to the tree. She was hot too. Tim cared for Sue and wanted to help her. He said, "Sue, I have cold drinks. Do you want one?" Sue said, "Yes, thank you, Tim!" Tim and Sue sat under the tree, drinking cold drinks from the cooler. They felt much better. They cared for each other and were happy to share. The hot day was now fun and cool for both Tim and Sue.
