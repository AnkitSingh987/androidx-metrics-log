Category: Beginner
Title: Lila and the Veil

Lila was a princess who liked to play dress-up. She had many pretty clothes and jewels in her big closet. She also had a long white veil that she got from her grandma. She liked to wear the veil and pretend she was a bride or a fairy. One day, she wanted to play with her friend Ben, who lived next door. She put on the veil and ran outside. Ben was waiting for her with his bike. He said, "Do you want to ride with me?" Lila nodded and climbed on the back of the bike. Ben pedaled fast and they went around the block. Lila felt the wind in her hair and the veil flying behind her. She was having fun, but she did not see the tree branch that was hanging low. The veil got caught on the branch and pulled Lila off the bike. She fell on the grass and hurt her knee. She started to cry. Ben stopped the bike and ran to her. He saw the veil was torn and dirty. He said, "I'm sorry, Lila. I did not see the branch. Are you okay?" Lila looked at her knee and saw blood. She said, "It hurts. And my veil is ruined. Grandma will be mad at me." Ben felt bad. He wanted to help Lila. He said, "Don't worry, Lila. I have an idea. Come with me." He took her hand and led her to his house. He went to his mom's sewing room and found some scissors, a needle and some thread. He said, "Look, Lila. We can fix your veil. We just need to cut off the bad part and sew it back together. It will be shorter, but still pretty. And your grandma will not know." Lila smiled. She liked Ben's idea. She said, "Thank you, Ben. You are a good friend. And you are very flexible. You can provide a solution for any problem." Ben blushed. He said, "You're welcome, Lila. You are a good friend too. And you are very brave. You can wear any veil and look beautiful." They hugged and then worked on the veil. They made it look nice and new. Then they put some band-aid on Lila's knee and went back to play. They had a happy day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She loved going to the park. Every day, she would take her tire and roll it around the park. Today, the little girl wanted to do something fun. She decided to go up a big hill. She pushed the tire hard, and it rose up the hill. She ran along with it, making sure it got all the way up. Soon, the tire reached the top of the hill. The little girl was very lively. She jumped on top of the tire and rode down the hill. The tire was rolling faster and faster. It was so much fun! At the bottom of the hill, the little girl stopped the tire. She smiled with excitement. Then, she took the tire and ran back up the hill. She was ready to run down the hill again!

Once upon a time, there was a nice garden. It had many pretty flowers and yummy fruits. A little girl named Lily liked to play in the garden every day. One day, Lily saw a big red fruit on a tree. She thought, "I want to taste it!" So, she picked the fruit and took a big bite. It was very sweet and juicy. Lily was very happy. Then, Lily shared the fruit with her friends. They all said, "Thank you, Lily! This fruit tastes nice!" They played together in the garden and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, a cow named Bessie lived on a farm. Bessie loved to travel. She was eager to see new places. One day, Bessie decided to leave the farm and travel far away. Bessie walked and walked. She saw many new things. She saw big trees, little bugs, and fast cars. Bessie was happy. But soon, she started to miss her home and her friends. Then, something unexpected happened. Bessie found a big sign that said "Beef." She did not know what it meant. Bessie was scared. She did not want to travel anymore. Bessie wanted to go home. So, she turned around and went back to the farm. Bessie was happy to be home with her friends.
