Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Toy

One day, a little girl named Mia found a plastic toy in her room. She liked to touch it because it was smooth and fun to play with. Mia had a friend named Tim who came over to play with her. When Tim saw the plastic toy, he felt jealous. He wanted to touch and play with the toy too. Tim said, "Mia, can I play with the toy too? I like it a lot." Mia thought about it and said, "Yes, Tim, we can both play with the toy. We can share and have fun together." So Mia and Tim played with the plastic toy and had a great time. They learned that sharing made them both happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. He saw a new game that he really wanted. The game had big, bright colors and fun pictures. Tim asked his mom, "Can we buy the new game, please?" His mom said, "Not today, Tim. We need to save money for your birthday party. But if you are good and help me with the chores, maybe we can buy the game later." Tim was sad, but he knew his mom was right. Days went by, and Tim was a good boy. He helped his mom with the chores and was very nice to his friends. On his birthday, Tim's mom said, "You have been so good, Tim. I have a surprise for you." She gave him the new game he wanted from the store! Tim was so happy and said, "Thank you, Mom!" They played the new game together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to climb trees in the big park near her house. One day, she found a very old tree. It was so big and tall. Lily thought it was an ancient tree. Lily wanted to climb the ancient tree. She started to climb up, up, up. As she climbed, she saw a pretty image on the tree trunk. It was a picture of a happy sun and a smiling moon. Lily climbed higher and higher. She reached the top and saw the whole park. She felt so happy to see the image on the ancient tree. Lily climbed down and ran home to tell her mom about her adventure. They went back to see the image and the ancient tree together.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a magic ball. The ball could glow in the dark. Sue was very happy and grateful. She wanted to play a game with her friends. She called her friends Tom and Lily to come over and play. When Tom and Lily came, they started to play the game. They took turns throwing the glowing ball in the dark room. It was so much fun! They laughed and played for a long time. Suddenly, the ball started to glow even brighter. "Wow! Look at the ball!" said Sue. "It's glowing so bright!" Tom and Lily looked at the ball in surprise. The glowing ball then started to talk! "Thank you for playing with me," the ball said. "I am so grateful to have friends like you!" Sue, Tom, and Lily were amazed that the ball could talk. They played with the ball all night, and they became the best of friends. The glowing ball was grateful to have found Sue, Tom, and Lily, and they were grateful for their new talking friend.

Once upon a time, in a big country, there was a king. The king liked to give orders. One day, he ordered everyone to be glad and happy. The people in the country did what the king said. They were all glad and happy. But, there was a small cat who did not want to be glad. The cat wanted to be sad. The king found out about the sad cat. He ordered the cat to be glad too. The cat did not like the king's order. Then, something unexpected happened. The sad cat found a big box. Inside the box, there were many toys. The cat played with the toys and became glad. The king saw the glad cat and was happy too. From that day, the king let everyone be glad in their own way.
