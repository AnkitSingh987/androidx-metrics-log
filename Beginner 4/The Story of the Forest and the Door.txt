Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Forest and the Door

One day, a big, thick tree in the forest had a little secret. It had a tiny door at the bottom. A small boy named Tim found the door and opened it. Inside, he saw a room full of broccoli! Tim was very surprised. He never saw so much broccoli in one place. Tim said, "Hello! What is happening here?" A tiny green man came out from behind the broccoli. He said, "I am Mr. Green. I grow broccoli for everyone in the forest. Would you like to help me?" Tim was happy to help Mr. Green. They picked the broccoli together and put it in a big basket. When they were done, Mr. Green gave Tim a big piece of broccoli to take home. Tim said, "Thank you, Mr. Green! I will come back and help you again." Tim went home and showed his mom the big piece of broccoli. She was very happy too. They cooked it for dinner and it was the best broccoli they ever had. Tim couldn't wait to go back and help Mr. Green again.

One day, a big cat named Tom was very hungry. He went to the kitchen to find some food. He looked and looked but only found a disgusting bug. Tom did not want to eat the bug, so he thought of another way to fill his tummy. Tom went to a small store to buy food. He gave the man his order for a big sandwich. But when Tom got his sandwich, it was very small. Tom was sad and still hungry. He did not know what to do. Just then, a nice dog named Max saw Tom. Max had a big sandwich and wanted to share with Tom. They both ate the big sandwich and were happy. Tom learned that sharing with friends can make things better, even when something unexpected happens.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He wanted to help his dad repair a shelf in their house. Tim was very eager to learn and be a good helper. As they started to fix the shelf, Tim saw a big red ball behind it. He was so surprised! Tim showed the ball to his dad, who smiled and said, "I forgot about that ball! It was mine when I was little like you." Tim and his dad played with the ball after they fixed the shelf. They had a lot of fun and laughed a lot. Tim was happy to help his dad and find the special ball.

Lily was a little girl who liked to play with her dolls. She had many dolls and gave them names and clothes. She was happy when she played with them. One day, her mom had a meeting at work. She told Lily to stay at home with her dad. She said she would be back soon. Lily said okay and gave her mom a hug. But Lily was bored. Her dad was busy on his phone and did not play with her. She wanted to see what her mom was doing. She thought maybe her mom was playing with dolls too. She found her mom's bag and opened it. She saw a shiny thing inside. It was her mom's phone. Lily liked shiny things. She took the phone and pressed some buttons. She saw pictures and words and colors. She smiled and played with the phone. But she did not know that she was calling someone. She was calling her mom's boss. Her mom's boss was angry. He heard Lily's voice and noises. He thought Lily was making fun of him. He said bad words and hung up. He was very mad at Lily's mom. He said she was poor at her job and fired her. When Lily's mom came back, she saw Lily with her phone. She was shocked and scared. She checked her phone and saw what Lily had done. She cried and hugged Lily. She said Lily was a bad girl and made her lose her job. She said they had no money and had to move. Lily was sad and sorry. She did not mean to make her mom sad. She wished she had not opened her mom's bag. She wished she had played with her dolls.
