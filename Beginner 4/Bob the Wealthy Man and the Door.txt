Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Wealthy Man and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Bob. He lived in a big house with many rooms. Bob liked to lock all of his doors to keep his things safe. One day, he found a message on his table. The message said, "I have hidden a fun surprise for you in one of your rooms!" Bob was excited to find the surprise. He went to unlock all the doors in his house. When he opened each door, he found nothing. He was sad and thought that maybe the message was a trick. But then, he remembered that he had one more door to open. It was the door to the small room where he kept his toys. When he opened the toy room door, he saw all his friends from the town. They had planned a big party for him! Bob was so happy that he forgot about the locked doors and the message. He had a great time at the party with his friends. From that day on, Bob learned that the best surprises come from the people you love.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy driver named Tom. Tom had a big red bus. Every day, he drove the bus around town. He liked his job, but he didn't like to add more stops. One day, a little girl named Lisa got on the bus. She asked Tom, "Can you add a stop near my house, please?" Tom didn't want to, but he said, "Okay, I will add it tomorrow." The next day, Tom was too lazy to add the new stop. But when he got to Lisa's house, he saw a big surprise. There was a party with cake and balloons! All the people in the town came to say thank you to Tom for his hard work. Tom was so happy that he added the new stop for Lisa. From that day on, he was not lazy anymore. He added more stops for all the kids in the town. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max liked to follow his friend, Sam, a big cat. They played with their stuff all day long. Max was deaf, but he was very happy. One day, Max and Sam found a big box full of new stuff. They were so excited! They played with the new stuff all day. They had so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. Max heard a loud sound! Max was not deaf anymore! He could hear Sam and all the fun sounds around him. Max and Sam were very happy and played together with their stuff all the time.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a messy room. She did not like to clean it. Sue had a bell she loved. She would keep it by her bed. One day, Sue could not find her bell. She asked her mom, "Mom, where is my bell?" Her mom said, "I don't know, Sue. Maybe it is in your messy room." Sue looked and looked, but she could not find her bell. Sue was sad. She decided to clean her room. As she cleaned, she found many toys she forgot she had. Then, she found her bell under her bed! Sue was so happy. She learned that if she keeps her room clean, she will not lose her things.

One day, a big, open box came to the town. All the kids were very happy. They went to the box and saw a funny toy. The toy had a laser. The toy was a robot. The robot said, "Hi, kids! Do you want to dance with me?" The kids said, "Yes, robot! We want to dance!" The robot's laser made pretty colors on the ground. The kids and the robot danced and laughed. The kids danced with the robot all day. When the sun went down, the robot said, "Bye, kids! I had fun dancing with you!" The kids said, "Bye, robot! We had fun too!" The robot went back into the big, open box. The kids went home and told their moms and dads about the fun day they had.
