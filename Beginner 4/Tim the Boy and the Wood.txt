Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Wood

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Tim. Tim liked to help his dad. His dad needed wood to make a fire. Tim wanted to bring the wood to his dad. Tim went to the yard to find wood. He saw a big pile of wood. Tim picked up the wood, but it was heavy. He tried to bring the wood to his dad. While he was walking, Tim tripped and fell. The wood fell on his leg and it hurt. Tim started to cry. His dad heard him and came running. Dad saw that Tim was hurt. He hugged Tim and said, "It's okay. You are a good helper. Next time, let's bring the wood together." Tim smiled and hugged his dad. They brought the wood together and made a big fire.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had a very pretty veil. The veil was her favorite because it was original. She wore it every day. One day, Lily went to the market with her mom. She saw a big box with a sign that said "Weigh". Lily put her veil on the box. The box moved and made a loud sound. Lily was scared and her veil fell inside the box. Then, a nice lady came and opened the box. Inside the box, Lily found her veil and a new friend, a little cat. The cat had been hiding in the box all along. Lily and the cat hugged each other. From that day on, Lily and the cat were best friends, and they both loved the original veil.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a lively festival. Many people came to play games, eat food, and have fun. The town was happy and full of laughter. One day, a little boy named Timmy wanted to play a game. He saw a big, scary game that he was too small for. His friends told him, "Don't play that game, Timmy! It's too big and scary for you!" But Timmy wanted to dare and show that he was brave. Timmy played the big, scary game. But the game was too hard for him. Timmy fell and hurt his leg. Everyone was sad because Timmy got hurt. The moral of the story is: don't do things that are too big and scary for you.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted cat named Tom. Tom was very good at sack races. He loved to play with his friends in the park. One day, Tom saw a new friend named Tim. Tim had a funny haircut. It was short and spiky. Tom asked Tim, "Do you want to play sack race with me?" Tim said, "Yes, I would love to!" So, they got into their sacks and started hopping. Tom was very fast, but Tim was faster. They both laughed and had a lot of fun. After the race, Tom and Tim became best friends. They played sack races every day. Tom's gifted skills made him better, and Tim's funny haircut made everyone smile. They were happy and loved playing together.

Once upon a time, there was a sad little girl named Lily. She had a toy box with many toys, but she did not know which toy was the best. One day, she sat near her toy box and looked at all her toys. She wanted to know the value of each toy. Lily's mom saw her looking sad and asked, "Why are you sad, Lily?" Lily said, "Mom, I don't know which toy is the best. I want to know their value." Her mom smiled and said, "I have an idea. Let's play a game tomorrow, and you will find out." The next day, Lily's mom set up a game for her. She hid all the toys around the house, and Lily had to find them. As Lily found each toy, she started to feel happy. She played with each toy and remembered why she loved them so much. At the end of the game, Lily's mom asked her, "So, what do you think now? Which toy has the most value?" Lily smiled and said, "Mom, I understand now. They all have value because they make me happy. I love all my toys." From that day on, Lily was never sad about her toys again. She knew that the value of her toys was in the joy they brought her. And she played with them every day, always happy and never sad.
