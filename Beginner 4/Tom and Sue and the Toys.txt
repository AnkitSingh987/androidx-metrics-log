Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Sue and the Toys

Tom and Sue are twins. They like to play and eat. Today, they go to the market with Mom. Mom gives them some money. She says, "You can buy something you want." Tom and Sue look around. They see many things. They see fruits, toys, books, and clothes. But they do not want any of them. They want something else. They see a big pile of potatoes. They love potatoes. They can make fries, chips, soup, and mash. They run to the potatoes. They pick two big ones. They give the money to the man. The man smiles and says, "You are lucky. These are the best potatoes in the market." Tom and Sue are happy. They hug the potatoes. They say, "Thank you, Mr. Potato Man." They go back to Mom. Mom is surprised. She says, "You bought potatoes? Why did you buy potatoes?" Tom and Sue say, "Because we love potatoes. And they are lucky. The man said so." Mom laughs. She says, "Okay, then. Let's go home and cook them. You can help me." Tom and Sue are very happy. They think they have the best potatoes in the world. They can't wait to eat them. They think they are the luckiest twins in the world.

Lily and Tom were playing in the garden. They liked to run and jump and look for bugs. They saw a big tree with many branches. They wanted to climb the tree and see what was up there. They held hands and walked to the tree. They saw a hole in the trunk. They looked inside and saw a dark and scary place. They saw many cobwebs and spiders. They heard a loud noise. It was a growl. "Who is there?" a voice said. It was a deep and angry voice. Lily and Tom were afraid. They wanted to run away. But they saw two enormous eyes in the hole. They saw a big mouth with sharp teeth. It was a bear! The bear was hungry. He saw Lily and Tom and wanted to bite them. He moved his head out of the hole and reached for them with his paws. "Help!" Lily and Tom screamed. They let go of each other and ran in different directions. The bear was confused. He did not know who to chase. He roared and tried to catch them. But Lily and Tom were fast. They ran to their house and slammed the door. They locked the door and hugged each other. They were safe. They were glad they escaped the bear. They told their mom and dad what happened. Their mom and dad were shocked. They called the animal control and told them about the bear. They said the bear was dangerous and needed to be moved away. Lily and Tom learned a lesson. They learned not to go near the big tree. They learned not to look inside the hole. They learned to be careful in the garden. They learned to stay away from bears.

Once upon a time, there was a little train. The train was upset because it could not glow like the other trains. The other trains had bright lights and colors. They were very pretty. One day, the little train went to find a way to glow. It chugged along the tracks, looking for something to help. It saw a big tree with shiny leaves. The train thought maybe the leaves would make it glow. The little train took some leaves and put them on its body. Suddenly, the train started to glow! It was so happy. Now, the little train could be like the other trains. It chugged along, glowing brightly, and everyone loved the little glowing train.

Once upon a time, there was a person. This person was very thin. They liked to play outside with their friends. One day, the sky was full of big, dark clouds. The person knew it was going to rain soon. The thin person wanted to keep playing, so they found a big tree. The tree had many leaves that could cover them. They stood under the tree and called their friends to join them. The friends all came and stood under the tree too. The rain started to fall, but the tree kept them dry. The thin person and their friends laughed and played under the tree. They were happy that the tree could cover them from the rain. When the rain stopped, they all went back to playing in the sun.
