Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Stamps

Once upon a time, there was a small girl named Sue. Sue loved to collect stamps. One day, she found a big, harmless bug near her stamps. Sue was not scared of the bug, but she began to worry. Sue asked her mom, "Mom, will the bug eat my stamps?" Her mom replied, "No, Sue, the bug is harmless. It won't eat your stamps. It's just looking for a place to rest." Sue felt better and let the bug stay near her stamps. She learned that sometimes, things that look scary are not scary at all. Sue shared her stamps with her friends and told them about the harmless bug. They all learned not to worry too much about things that seem scary at first. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a girl. She was very pretty. One day, the girl went to a big barn. Inside the barn, there was a cute grey bunny. The girl said "Hello, Bunny!" and the bunny said, "Hello!" The girl reached out and stroked the bunny's soft fur. The bunny closed its eyes as it enjoyed the girl's touch. The girl smiled and said, "Can I give you a kiss?" The bunny said, "Yes please!" The girl leaned forward and kissed the bunny on the forehead. The bunny hopped up into the air and said, "Thank you! That was so pretty." The girl smiled and said, "You're welcome, Bunny." The girl petted the bunny one last time before saying goodbye and leaving the barn. The bunny smiled, happy to have made a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a small pigeon. The pigeon had a friend, a little girl named Lily. They liked to play together in the park. One day, Lily had to go to the doctor. She told the pigeon to wait for her in the park. The pigeon did not want to delay, so it flew to the park and waited for Lily. But, the pigeon saw a big cat. The cat wanted to catch the pigeon. The pigeon tried to fly away, but the cat was too fast. The cat caught the pigeon and the pigeon could not play with Lily anymore. Lily was very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a team of animals who lived in a big forest. In this team, there was a big bear, a small rabbit, and a funny bird. They were very good friends. One sunny day, the team decided to walk together to find a new place to play. They walked and walked, but all they could see were trees and more trees. The walk started to feel boring for them. Then, the funny bird had an idea. He said, "Let's play a game while we walk!" So, they started to play games and sing songs as they walked. Soon, the boring walk became a fun adventure for the team. They had a great day and found a new place to play together.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He was a lucky boy. Tim had a toy top. He loved to play with the top. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He took his top with him. At the park, Tim played with his top on a bench. He was very happy. Then, a big wind came. It blew Tim's top away. Tim was very sad. He looked for his top, but he could not find it. The wind took his top far away. Tim and his mom went home from the park. Tim did not have his top anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a weak boy named Tim. He had a big jug. One day, the jug broke. Tim was sad. He went to his friend, Sam. "Sam, can you help me?" Tim asked. Sam said, "Yes, let's solve this problem together." They found some glue. They put the glue on the broken jug. The jug was fixed. Tim and Sam were happy. They solved the problem.

Once upon a time, there was a big, hairy bear. He had a big stomach. One day, he was very hungry. He went to look for food in the forest. In the forest, he found a big rock. He thought there might be food under the rock. He tried to move the rock to reveal what was under it. But the rock was very heavy. The bear pushed and pushed, but the rock would not move. Then, a little ant came by. The ant saw the bear and asked, "Why are you sad?" The bear said, "I am hungry, and I want to see what is under this rock. But I can't move it." The ant said, "I will help you." The ant called all his ant friends, and they all pushed the rock together. The rock moved, and they found lots of yummy food under it. The bear was happy, and he shared the food with the ants. They all ate and had a good time together.
