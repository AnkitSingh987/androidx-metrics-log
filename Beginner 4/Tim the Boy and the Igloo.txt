Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Igloo

Once upon a time, in a cold land, there was a kind boy named Tim. Tim lived in a small igloo with his family. He loved to play with his little dog, Spot, in the snow. One day, Tim and Spot saw a little bird shivering in the cold. Tim felt bad for the bird and wanted to help. He said, "Please, come to our igloo, little bird. You can be warm there." The little bird was happy and said "Thank you!" So, Tim and Spot took the bird to their igloo. Inside, the bird was warm and safe. Tim's mom gave the bird some food to eat. From that day on, Tim, Spot, and the little bird became best friends. They played together in the snow and stayed warm in the igloo. Everyone in the igloo was kind and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a fine house with a big window. Every day, the sun would shine through the window and make the room feel warm and happy. A small cat lived in the house. The cat loved to sit in the sun and take long naps. One day, while the cat was sleeping, a big bird flew by the window. The bird saw the cat and wanted to play. The bird flew into the window with a loud noise. The cat woke up and saw the bird on the floor. The cat and the bird looked at each other. The cat did not know what to do. Then, the bird started to sing a pretty song. The cat liked the song and started to purr. The bird and the cat became friends, and they played together every day in the fine house by the big window where the sun would shine.

Once upon a time, there was a playful lion named Leo. He lived in a big cave in the forest. Leo loved to play with his friends, the birds, the monkeys, and the rabbits. One day, Leo and his friends were playing near the cave. A big bear came and said, "This is my cave now. Go away!" Leo did not want to give up his cave, so he roared very loud at the bear. The bear got scared and ran away. Leo's friends were happy and said, "Thank you, Leo! You saved our home." But Leo was not nice anymore. He thought he was very strong and started to roar at his friends too. They all got scared and left him alone. In the end, Leo was all alone in his cave. He was very sad because he had no friends to play with. The moral of the story is that being strong is good, but being kind is better.

One day, a graceful cat named Luna found a rag on the floor. She picked it up with her soft paws and carried it to her seat. The rag was red and soft, just like her favorite toy. Luna's friend, a small dog named Max, saw her with the rag. "Luna, can I play with the rag too?" he asked. Luna nodded and said, "Yes, Max, let's play together!" They took turns tossing the rag in the air and catching it with their mouths. After playing for a while, they were both tired. Luna and Max went back to their seat and laid down. They snuggled up with the rag, happy that they had spent the day playing together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very hungry. It was time for lunch. His mom made him a big, heavy sandwich. Tim was happy. Tim wanted to use his new lunch box. He put the heavy sandwich in the lunch box. He closed the lunch box and carried it outside. He sat under a big tree to eat his lunch. Tim opened the lunch box and took out the heavy sandwich. He ate the whole thing. It was very yummy. Tim was not hungry anymore. He was so happy he used his new lunch box for his lunch.

Once upon a time, there was a youth named Tim. Tim was a troubled boy. He did not like to share his toys with others. One day, Tim found a sharp stick while playing outside. He thought it was very cool and wanted to keep it all for himself. While playing with the sharp stick, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue asked Tim, "Can I play with your sharp stick too?" Tim said, "No! It is mine!" Sue felt sad and walked away. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took the sharp stick away from Tim's hand. The wind blew the stick to Sue. Sue looked at Tim and said, "Now we both can play with the sharp stick!" Tim felt happy and they both played with the sharp stick together.
