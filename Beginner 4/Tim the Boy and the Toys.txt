Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys. One day, he wanted to find his favorite toy car. He looked in the deep drawer where he kept all his toys. Tim opened the drawer and saw many toys inside. He dug through the toys until he found his favorite car. It was red and shiny. Tim was very happy. Now that he had his favorite toy car, Tim decided to relax. He sat on the floor and played with his car, making it go fast and slow. Tim had a fun day playing with his toys and relaxing.

---

One day, a little boy named Tim and his enthusiastic dog, Max, went to the beach. They loved to play near the water. Max would run and jump in the waves, while Tim would laugh and clap. As the tide started to come in, Max saw a big ball in the water. He wanted to get it, but Tim said, "No, Max! The water is too deep now!" Max did not listen and ran into the water to get the ball. Tim was scared and called for Max to come back. Max tried to swim back, but the tide was too strong. Tim cried, "Help! My dog is in the water!" A man tried to help, but it was too late. The tide took Max away. Tim was very sad, and he learned to always watch the tide and keep Max close.

Once there was a clever fish. It loved to swim in the deep blue sea. One day it swam up to a cliff. It was so close that it could splash against the rocks. The fish splashed over and over again. It was so much fun! But then the clever fish noticed something curious. There was a big cave at the bottom of the cliff. It swam inside and it was very dark. It kept swimming until it saw a light. It turned out to be another fish! The clever fish said hello. The other fish said hello too. The two fish started to play together. They splashed and jumped through the cave. Then they swam back out into the open water. They were so happy! They keep splashing together forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was very anxious. He wanted to celebrate his birthday. He had a big dream. Max wanted a cake with lots of cream on it. Max went to his friend, the cat. "I want a cake with cream," said Max. "Can you help me?" The cat nodded and said, "Yes, let's make a cake together." They got flour, sugar, and cream. They mixed it all up and baked the cake. When the cake was ready, Max and the cat put cream on top. All their friends came to celebrate. They sang "Happy Birthday" to Max. Max was so happy. He shared the cake with all his friends. They all had fun, and Max's dream came true.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to eat meat. One day, Max saw a big piece of meat on the table. Max wanted to eat it, but it was too high for him to reach. Max had an idea. He found a big box to stand on. Max jumped on the box, then onto the table. Max was so happy! He was about to eat the meat when he saw something. The meat was green and had a bad smell. Max thought it was disgusting. Just then, Max's friend, a bird named Lily, flew into the room. Lily saw Max on the table and the disgusting meat. She knew Max should not eat it. Lily picked up the meat in her beak and flew out the window. She released the meat into the trash can outside. Max was sad at first, but then he realized that Lily saved him from eating the bad meat. Max and Lily played together and had a fun day.

One day, a cat named Tom was very tired. He wanted to take a nap. He found a soft, warm spot under a big tree. But when he lay down, he saw a rope hanging from the tree. Tom thought it was fun, so he played with the rope instead of napping. A dog named Max saw Tom playing with the rope. Max wanted to play too. He said, "Can I play with the rope too, Tom?" Tom did not want to share. He said, "No, this is my rope!" Max was sad and angry. Max saw a big, disgusting bug on the ground. He had an idea. He picked up the bug with his mouth and put it on the rope. Tom saw the disgusting bug and did not want to play with the rope anymore. Max laughed and played with the rope. Tom went to take his nap.
