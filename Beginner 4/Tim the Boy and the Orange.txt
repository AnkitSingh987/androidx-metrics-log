Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Orange

One day, a little boy named Tim found an orange toy in his room. It was a small toy car. He liked the toy very much. He played with it all day. Tim showed the toy to his mom. "Look, Mom! I found an orange toy car!" he said. "Wow, that's a nice toy!" Mom said. "Why don't you display it on your shelf?" Tim put the toy on the shelf, next to his other toys. He was very happy. He knew that the toy would be safe there. The next day, Tim's friend came over to play. They played with many toys, but Tim did not show his friend the orange toy car. He wanted to keep it a secret. But then, his friend saw the car on the shelf. "What's that?" his friend asked. Tim smiled and said, "That's my special orange toy car!" They played with the car together, and they had a lot of fun.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a ticket on the floor. He was very happy. He showed his mom the ticket. She said, "This is a ticket to the zoo! We can go tomorrow." Tim was grateful and excited. The next day, they went to the zoo. Tim saw big lions, tall giraffes, and funny monkeys. Then, they went to see the birds. There were many birds of different colors. But one bird flew out of its cage. The bird went to Tim and sat on his head. Tim was scared. Mom said, "Don't move, Tim." She slowly put her hand up and the bird went on her hand. Then, she shut the cage door. The bird was back with its friends. Tim was not scared anymore. He was happy and grateful for his mom. And that day, they had more fun at the zoo than they ever thought they would.

One day, Ben and Lily went to the park with their mom. They saw a big tree with many leaves and nuts. They also saw a squirrel that was running and jumping on the branches. "Look, mom, a squirrel!" Ben said, pointing at the furry animal. "Yes, it's a squirrel. Do you know what it likes to eat?" mom asked. "Nuts!" Lily said, picking up a brown nut from the ground. "Can I give it to the squirrel?" Ben asked, holding another nut in his hand. "Sure, but be careful. Squirrels are shy and fast. You have to be quiet and gentle," mom said. Ben and Lily walked slowly to the tree, holding their nuts. They saw the squirrel looking at them with its black eyes. They smiled and stretched their hands with the nuts. "Hello, squirrel. We have some nuts for you. Do you want them?" Lily said softly. The squirrel sniffed the nuts and then took one from Ben's hand. It bit the nut and made a crunchy sound. Ben and Lily giggled. The squirrel took another nut from Lily's hand and then ran up the tree. "Thank you, squirrel. Bye-bye!" Ben and Lily said, waving at the squirrel. The squirrel waved back with its tail and then hid the nuts in a hole. It was happy and full. Ben and Lily went back to their mom, who hugged them and praised them for being kind and brave. They had a fun day at the park, but they also made a messy pile of nut shells on the ground. They helped mom to clean it up before they left. They hoped to see the squirrel again.

Mommy said, "It's time to measure the grass!" So Daddy, Mommy, and Anna went outside to measure the grass. Daddy took out the ruler and showed it to Anna. She held on to it and it felt big and strong. Mommy said, "Let's organize this measurement." So they got to work. Daddy held the ruler straight and Mommy held the end of the ruler with one hand and placed her other hand at the other end of the ruler. Anna said, "Mommy, I want to help!" So Mommy and Daddy both held the ruler and Anna moved her hands under the ruler. She was so excited to measure the grass like a grown-up! Mommy said, "We did it! We measured the grass." Anna smiled and said, "Yay! I did it!" Daddy gave her a hug and they all went back inside.
