Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Ice

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to help his dad fix things. His dad had a big hammer that Tim liked to use. One day, Tim's dad asked him to help fix a broken chair. Tim was very happy and used the hammer to fix it. In the town, there was a shop that sold delicious ice cream. Tim wanted to have some, but he needed to pay for it. He decided to help his neighbors fix things with his hammer, and they would give him some money. Tim was very good at fixing things, and soon he had enough money to buy the ice cream. Tim went to the shop and paid for the delicious ice cream. As he was walking home, he saw a little girl crying. She had dropped her ice cream on the ground. Tim felt sad for her and decided to give her his ice cream. The girl was very happy, and Tim felt good about helping her. The moral of the story is that it's better to help others and share than to only think of ourselves.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. She loved to play with her toy bear, Bobo. One day, Sally and Bobo went to the park to have a picnic. They brought some jelly sandwiches to eat. Sally said, "Bobo, do you like jelly sandwiches?" Bobo didn't say anything, because he was just a toy bear. While they were eating, it started to rain. The rain was very serious. Sally and Bobo got very wet. The rain began to soak Sally's clothes and Bobo's fur. Sally said, "Oh no, Bobo! We must go home now!" They quickly packed up their things and ran back to their house. When they got home, Sally's mom saw how wet they were. She was not happy. She said, "Sally, you should have come home earlier. Now your clothes and Bobo are soaked!" Sally felt sad because she knew her mom was right. Bobo's fur was ruined from the rain, and he was never the same again. Sally learned to listen to her mom and not stay out in the rain.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a friend named Tom. They liked to play together. One day, Tom had a letter. It was from his grandma. He wanted to show Lily the letter. Tom walked to Lily's house. He saw a big dog on the way. The dog was not safe. Tom was scared. He did not want the dog to take his letter. So, he hid behind a tree. He waited for the dog to go away. When the dog was gone, Tom went to Lily's house. He knocked on the door. Lily opened the door. Tom offered the letter to Lily. "Look at my letter from grandma!" he said. Lily smiled. They sat down and read the letter together. They were happy and safe.

Ben and Mia were playing in the garden. They liked to dig and find things in the dirt. Sometimes they found worms, sometimes they found stones, sometimes they found nothing. One day, they found something round and fuzzy. It was a peach. It was hidden under a leaf. Ben and Mia were happy. They liked peaches. They wanted to eat it. But then, a big bird came. It saw the peach. It wanted to eat it too. It flew down and grabbed the peach with its beak. It flew away with the peach. Ben and Mia were sad. They wanted their peach back. They ran after the bird. They shouted, "Give us our peach! Give us our peach!" The bird flew to a tree. It landed on a branch. It tried to eat the peach. But the peach was too big for its beak. It dropped the peach. The peach fell to the ground. Ben and Mia saw the peach. They ran to the peach. They picked it up. They rescued the peach. They were happy. They shared the peach. They ate it. It was sweet and juicy. They said, "Thank you, peach. You are yummy. We are sorry, bird. You are hungry. But this is our peach. You can find another one." The end.
