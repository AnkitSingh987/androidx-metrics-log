Category: Beginner
Title: Sally the Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. She lived in a small house with a big porch. Sally liked to play on the porch with her toys. One sunny day, Sally and her friend Tom were playing with their dolls. Sally said, "Let's make our dolls marry each other!" Tom said, "Yes, that's a fun idea!" So, they put the dolls together and made them say, "I do." After the dolls were married, Sally and Tom had a silly dance party on the porch. They laughed and danced until they were tired. Then, they sat on the porch and watched the sun go down. They were happy to have such a fun day.

Lila and Ben are twins. They like to play dress up. They have many hats and coats and shoes. They pretend to be different people. One day, they find a big box in the garage. It is light and empty. They have an idea. They can make a ship with the box. They take the box to the backyard. They use crayons and stickers to decorate it. They draw windows and a flag and a name. They call their ship The Rainbow. They put on their dress up clothes. Lila wears a blue hat and a red coat. She is the captain. Ben wears a yellow hat and a green coat. He is the sailor. They get inside the box. They pretend to sail on the sea. "Ahoy, matey!" Lila says. "Do you see any land?" "No, captain," Ben says. "But I see a big fish. It is pink and purple and has a horn." "Wow, that is a unicorn fish!" Lila says. "Let's follow it. Maybe it will lead us to a magic island." They move the box and make waves with their arms. They laugh and have fun. They are happy on their ship. They love to play dress up.

One day, a nice cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a rod on the ground. Tom was curious and wanted to play with it. So, he lay down next to the rod and started to touch it with his paw. A little bird named Tim saw Tom playing with the rod. Tim flew down and said, "Hi, Tom! Can I play with the rod too?" Tom looked at Tim and said, "Yes, let's play together!" Tom and Tim had lots of fun playing with the rod. They took turns using it to draw in the dirt. They laughed and played all day long. In the end, they both lay down, tired but happy, and knew they had made a new friend.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there was an unusual fox. This fox could spring very high. One sunny day, the fox met a little bird. "Hi, bird," said the fox. "I can spring high. Do you want to see?" "Yes, please," said the bird. The fox sprang high into the air, and the bird clapped its wings. They laughed and played together. The next day, the fox and the bird told their friends about their new game. All the animals came to watch the unusual fox spring. The fox jumped, and everyone clapped and cheered. They all had a fun day in the big green forest.

One day, a little girl named Jill went outside to play in the garden. It was a hot summer day and the ground was very dry and dusty. So Jill decided to take her bucket and fill it up with water from the pond. She carried the water carefully and when she got to the garden, she began to soak up the ground with the water. Jill felt so happy, she did this every day! Soon, new flower buds started to appear and the garden was so beautiful. One day while walking in the garden, Jill noticed something. She saw a small cut on one of the plant stems. She was very worried so she called her mom and asked her to help. Her mom took her to the garden and examined the cut. She told Jill that it was an ordinary cut in the stem and that she should not be worried. She then took some special plants from the kitchen and put it on the cut. This special plants was made so it can help the cut get better. Within days the cut healed and Jill was happy that her special flowers were safe.
