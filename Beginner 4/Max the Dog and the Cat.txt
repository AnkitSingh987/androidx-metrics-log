Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Dog and the Cat

One day, a big dog named Max wanted to bring his favorite toy to a meeting with his friends. The toy was a wet, red ball. He loved to play with it all the time. Max was so excited to show his friends his wet ball. On the way to the meeting, Max saw a small cat named Lily. Lily was not nice and took Max's wet ball. Max was very sad and did not know what to do. He wanted to bring his wet ball to the meeting, but Lily would not give it back. Max asked his friend, a bird named Sam, to help him get the wet ball back. Sam was fast and smart. Sam flew up high and took the wet ball from Lily. Sam gave the wet ball back to Max. Max was so happy! He could now bring his wet ball to the meeting with his friends. They all played together and had a great time.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a pretty rose that she loved very much. One day, a fierce wind came and blew her rose away. Lily was very sad and began to weep. A big, friendly dog named Max saw Lily crying. He came up to her and said, "Why do you weep, little girl?" Lily told Max about her lost rose. Max wanted to help, so they went on a search to find the rose. As they looked, something unexpected happened. They found a big, beautiful garden with many roses! Lily was so happy, she stopped weeping. She picked a new rose and thanked Max. From that day on, Lily and Max were the best of friends.

Once upon a time there was a mom and a son. They went to the store to buy some things. The son wanted to buy a rubber. He saw one that was fine and he asked his mom if he could have it. His mom said yes and the son was very happy. He spent a few minutes playing with the rubber before they left the store. He felt very proud of his new rubber. When they got home, the son showed his dad the rubber. His dad got down on the floor and played with him. They both had a lot of fun playing together. The son and his dad enjoyed their rubber for a long time. They played so much that the rubber started to wear out. The son’s mom said it was time to buy a new rubber and the son was so excited. The son and his dad returned to the store and found a new rubber that was even better and finer than the one before. The son was so pleased with his new rubber and he was always ready to play with it. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a farm. On the farm, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a pet dog named Max. They were lucky to live on the farm. One day, Tim and Max were playing. They saw a big box. Tim said, "Max, let's raise the box." They raised the box, but it was very heavy. Inside the box, there was a big, mean cat. The cat jumped out and said, "This is my farm now!" Tim and Max were sad. They tried to make the cat go away, but the cat was too strong. In the end, the cat stayed on the farm. Tim and Max had to leave. They were not lucky anymore. They had to find a new home.

Mama and her three-year-old daughter were outside, sitting on the lawn. It was a sunny day, and the grass was soft and green. Mama said, "This is so yummy! Let's go play in the garden." The daughter jumped up and shouted, "Yes!" Mama said, "Now, be careful not to strike the lawn, okay?" The daughter nodded and ran off to the garden. In the garden, there was a big red apple tree. The daughter reached up to grab the apples. She shook the branches and apples dropped all around her. She found a yummy one and ran back to show Mama. Mama smiled and gave her a hug. She said, "Great job! I'm so proud of you!" Then they both happily ate the yummy apple.
