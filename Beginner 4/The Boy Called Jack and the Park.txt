Category: Beginner
Title: The Boy Called Jack and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a boy called Jack who loved to explore the park. One day, Jack's mum told him, "We are going to the park today". Jack was very excited and he ran out of the house faster than he ever had. At the park, he saw a frame on the ground and he bent over to pick it up. Jack's mum saw the frame and said to him, "I think the frame looks really nice. It's very special. Would you like to keep it?" Jack said "yes" and his mum said, "Well now you must look after it carefully." A few days later, Jack was playing with the frame in the park. Suddenly, he saw an old man walking his way. The old man stopped in front of Jack and said, "What a nice frame. Would you like to offer it to me? I would love to have it!" Jack was sad, but he said, "No, this was given to me by my mum and I promised to look after it." The old man looked at Jack and chuckled. "That wasn't a very stupid thing to do," the old man said. "And I think your mum would be proud that you kept your promise." Jack smiled and hugged the frame. From then on, he decided to always keep his promise, no matter how stupid it might seem.

Once upon a time, in a big green park, there was an enormous pear tree. The tree was very tall and had many big, sweet pears. The pears were so high that no one could reach them. All the kids wanted to eat the pears, but they were not allowed to climb the tree. One sunny day, a kind lady came to the park. She saw the big pear tree and the kids who wanted the pears. The lady had a very long stick. She asked the kids if they wanted her to help get the pears. The kids were so happy and said "yes please!" The kind lady used the long stick to reach the pears. She gave each kid a big, sweet pear. The kids were very happy and thanked the lady. They all sat under the tree and ate their pears. From that day on, the kind lady was always permitted to pick pears from the enormous tree for the kids to enjoy.

One day, a curious little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He brought his favorite bucket to play with in the sand. Tim saw a big girl playing with a toy car. He wanted to play with her. "Hi, can I play with you?" Tim said. The big girl said, "Yes, but can I use your bucket?" Tim was happy and said, "Sure!" They played with the toy car and the bucket. They made roads and hills in the sand. Suddenly, a dog came running and took the bucket in his mouth. Tim and the big girl were surprised. They ran after the dog. The dog dropped the bucket near a tree. Tim and the big girl laughed and played with the bucket and the toy car again. They had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had a red wagon. She liked to play with her wagon all day long. She would fill it with toys, flowers, and even her cat, Tom. One sunny day, Lily saw a boy named Jack. Jack had a long toy train. Lily and Jack played with the train and wagon. They had so much fun together. They laughed and smiled all day long. As they grew up, Lily and Jack stayed friends. They still loved to play with the wagon and train. One day, when they were older, Lily and Jack decided to marry. They had a big party with cake and balloons. And, of course, the red wagon and long toy train were there too.

One day, a big dog and a little cat were playing in the park. The dog was fast, and the cat was slow. They liked to run and jump, but the cat could not keep up with the dog. The dog would always win, and this made the cat sad. The next day, they went to the park again. It was a hot day, and they were both very thirsty. They found a small puddle to drink from, but the dog drank all the water very fast. The cat did not get any water, and this made the cat even more sad. Then, a wise old owl saw the cat and gave her a wink. The owl told the cat to wipe her face and be happy. The cat wiped her face, and the owl showed her a secret pond with lots of water. The cat drank from the pond and felt much better. She thanked the owl and went back to play with the dog. From that day on, the dog and the cat learned to share and play together, making sure both of them were happy.
