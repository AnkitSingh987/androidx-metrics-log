Category: Beginner
Title: Ada and the Moon

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. Her name was Ada. Ada loved the moon. Every night, she would look up at the moon and smile. One night, however, it wasn't the same. The moon was wet and Ada was sad. She wanted to touch it but knew it was too far away. Then, something happened that made Ada hate the moon. A big dark cloud was blocking the moon from the sky! Ada was so mad. She looked up at the sky and yelled: "Go away, cloud!" But the cloud wouldn't go away. The cloud kept growing and blocking the moon more and more each day. Ada was so frustrated. She wanted to touch the moon but it was too wet. Finally, Ada had an idea. She grabbed a bucket and filled it with water. She found a ladder and climbed up high. Then, she put the bucket of water right in front of the cloud. More and more water began to fill the sky and soon the moon was visible again. Ada smiled and said: "I love the moon!" She touched the shining moon and was glad that she had made it shine again. From then on, she never had to hate the moon again.

One day, a fancy car went zoom down the road. It was very fast and very shiny. The sun made the car look like a rainbow. People stopped to watch the car go by. As the car zoomed, it saw a big hill. The car wanted to go up the hill very fast. So, the car went zoom up the hill. At the top, the car saw a beautiful rainbow in the sky. The car was so happy to see the rainbow. But then, something unexpected happened. The fancy car turned into a rainbow car! It was not a car anymore. It was a big, beautiful rainbow in the sky. People were so surprised and happy to see the new rainbow. They clapped and cheered for the fancy rainbow.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She loved her pretty dress. One day, she saw a big cake on the table. It looked very yummy. She wanted to taste it. Lily's mommy saw her and said, "Be careful, Lily. Don't get your dress dirty." Lily was worried, but she still wanted to taste the cake. So, she took a small bite of the cake. It was sweet and yummy. But, oh no! Some cake fell on Lily's dress. She was sad and worried. Her mommy saw and said, "Don't worry, Lily. We can clean your dress." They washed the dress together, and it was clean again. Lily was happy and promised to be more careful next time.

Once upon a time, there was a pale bird named Tim. Tim lived in a big tree near a small house. Every day, Tim would sing a pretty song. The people in the house loved to hear Tim sing. One day, Tim saw a shiny pipe on the ground. He picked it up with his beak and brought it back to his tree. Tim had an urge to play with the pipe, so he tried to make music with it. He blew into the pipe, and it made a funny sound. The people in the house heard the sound and came outside. They saw Tim with the pipe and laughed. They clapped their hands and urged Tim to play more. Tim was happy to make the people smile, so he played his pipe every day. And they all lived happily ever after.

Lila and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a big tree with green fruits. They want to pick some fruits. "Look, Ben, there are limes on the tree. They are sour and yummy. Let's get some," Lila says. "OK, Lila, but how can we reach them? They are too high," Ben says. Lila has an idea. She sees a long stick on the ground. She picks it up. "I can use this stick to poke the limes. You catch them when they fall, Ben," Lila says. "OK, Lila, I will catch them. Be careful, Lila," Ben says. Lila holds the stick and walks to the tree. She stretches her arm and tries to poke a lime. But the stick is too short. She stretches more and more. She loses her balance. She falls down. She hits her head on a rock. She cries. "Ow, ow, ow, Lila, are you OK?" Ben says. He runs to Lila. He sees blood on her head. He is scared. "Help, help, someone help Lila. She is hurt. She needs a doctor," Ben says. He shouts and cries. But no one hears them. No one comes to help them. They are alone in the park. The limes are still on the tree. They are wide and green. They are sour and yummy. But Lila and Ben can't eat them. They are sad and in pain. The end.
