Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Cat

Lily and her mom went to the park. Lily saw a big cat on a tree. The cat was black and white and had a long tail. Lily liked the cat. She wanted to pet it. "Mom, can I pet the cat?" Lily asked. "Be careful, Lily. The cat might scratch you. He is not our cat. He is a wild cat." Mom said. Lily walked slowly to the tree. She said hello to the cat. The cat looked at her. He did not run away. He stretched his legs and yawned. He seemed sleepy. Lily reached out her hand. She touched the cat's fur. It was soft and warm. The cat did not mind. He closed his eyes and purred. He liked Lily. Lily smiled. She gave the cat a gentle massage. She rubbed his ears and his back. The cat moved his head and his tail. He was happy. Mom watched Lily and the cat. She was surprised. The cat was very friendly. He was not afraid of Lily. He was a graceful cat. He moved like a dancer. "Look, Mom. The cat likes me. He is a nice cat." Lily said. "Yes, he is. You are very lucky, Lily. You made a new friend." Mom said. Lily and the cat played for a while. Then they said goodbye. Lily hugged the cat. The cat licked her cheek. They were both happy.

Once upon a time, there was a colorful bathtub. It was in a room with a big window. The bathtub had many friends, like the sink and the toilet. Every day, they would supply water to help people stay clean. One day, a big bird flew in through the open window. The bird looked at the colorful bathtub and thought it was a big bowl of food. The bird started to eat the bathtub's colors. The bathtub was sad because it loved its colors. Then, a little girl saw the bird eating the bathtub's colors. She shooed the bird away and closed the window. The little girl found a box of paint and painted the bathtub with new colors. The bathtub was happy and colorful again. The bathtub, sink, and toilet thanked the little girl for saving the day.

Once upon a time there was a little girl. She loved town and she was very lucky. The little girl went for a walk with her dad. They stopped in front of a store with windows full of wool. The little girl admired all the bright colors, pinks and blues, greens and yellows. The storekeeper saw the little girl and she was so cute! He gave her some bright colored wool and said that it was a present from him. The little girl was so excited! She thanked him and then ran to show her dad. They both admired the wool she was so lucky to have. The little girl and her dad continued their walk and the little girl couldn’t wait to get home and make a wool blanket out of the wool she had. That’s the story of the little girl and the wool. She was very lucky and she was always happy to admire her wool.

One day, a dog named Spot went for a walk. He was a very glad dog. He liked to run and play in the sun. Spot saw a big tree and ran to it. He wanted to find something fun to do. Under the tree, Spot found a stick. He picked it up in his mouth. Then, he tried to snap the stick. He bit and bit, but the stick did not break. Spot was not happy. Just then, a little girl came by. She saw Spot with the stick. She took the stick and threw it far away. Spot ran and ran to catch the stick. He brought it back to the girl. Now, Spot was a very glad dog again.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a funny figure near the big tree. The figure looked like a man made of leaves. Lily felt a little dizzy because she was so surprised. Lily went to the figure and said hello. The figure said hello back. They played games and had fun. Then, the figure told Lily that he could do a magic trick. He said he could disappear. Lily wanted to see the magic trick. The figure closed his eyes and counted to three. When he opened his eyes, the figure disappeared! Lily was so amazed. She went home and told her mom about the fun day she had at the park with her new friend.
