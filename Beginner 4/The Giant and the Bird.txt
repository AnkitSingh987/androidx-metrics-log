Category: Beginner
Title: The Giant and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a giant. The giant was very tall and strong. He lived in a big forest. The giant had a friend, a little bird. The bird was small and could fly high in the sky. They liked to play together. One day, the bird heard a loud sound. "Giant, do you know what that loud sound is?" asked the bird. The giant listened and said, "I do not know, but let's go see." So, they went to find the loud sound. They found a big tree that had fallen down. The loud sound was the tree falling. The giant and the bird were happy they found the sound. They played near the tree all day. The giant and the bird were best friends.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big box. He did not know what was in it. It was from an unknown place. Tim was very excited to open the box. Inside the box, Tim found a big freezer. He did not know what to do with it. So, he asked his mom, "Mom, what can we do with this freezer?" His mom said, "We can put food in it to keep it cold." Tim and his mom put food in the freezer. Then, Tim had an idea. He took his toy and put it in the freezer too. He wanted to record how cold it would get. After some time, Tim took his toy out of the freezer. It was very cold! Tim was happy and showed his cold toy to his mom. They both laughed and had fun with the new freezer.

One day, a nosy cow named Bessie went to the farm. She saw a big pile of hay. Bessie wanted to eat the hay, but it was not hers. Bessie asked her friend, the horse, "Can I have some hay?" The horse said, "No, Bessie. This hay is mine. You cannot have it." Bessie was sad and hungry. She walked away and found a big apple tree. Bessie ate the apples and felt happy again. Later, Bessie saw the horse again. The horse said, "I am sorry, Bessie. You can have some hay now." Bessie and the horse shared the hay. All the animals on the farm were happy. They all clapped and said, "Yay, Bessie and horse are friends!" They all applauded and played together.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the jungle. She was troubled because she lost her toy bunny. Lucy searched high and low for her toy bunny. She looked under the big trees and behind the green bushes. In the jungle, Lucy met a kind monkey. The monkey said, "Lucy, why are you troubled?" Lucy told the monkey, "I lost my toy bunny. I am searching for it." The monkey wanted to help Lucy. They searched together for the toy bunny. After a while, the monkey found the toy bunny under a small rock. Lucy was very happy. She said, "Thank you, monkey, for helping me." They became good friends and played together in the jungle.

Lily and Tom were twins who liked to play together. They had many toys, but their favorite was a big box. They could change the box into anything they wanted. Sometimes it was a castle, sometimes a spaceship, sometimes a cave. One day, they decided to change the box into a boat. They put some pillows and blankets inside and pretended to sail on the sea. They made noises like waves and seagulls and fish. They had a lot of fun. But then, their mom came and said, "It's time for a nap, kids. You need to rest after playing so hard." Lily and Tom did not want to nap. They wanted to keep playing. They said, "No, mom, we are not tired. We are sailors. We have to explore the sea." Mom smiled and said, "I know, but even sailors need to sleep sometimes. You can play again after your nap. Come on, let's go to your room." Lily and Tom followed mom to their room. They were not happy. They left the box in the living room. They hoped no one would touch it. They lay down on their beds and closed their eyes. Mom kissed them and said, "Good night, my little sailors. I love you." She turned off the light and left the room. There was silence. Lily and Tom could not hear the waves or the seagulls or the fish anymore. They could only hear their own breathing. At first, they felt bored and sad. They missed their boat and their adventure. They wished they could play some more. But then, they started to feel sleepy. They felt warm and cozy in their beds. They felt safe and loved by mom. They felt peaceful. They drifted into a dream. They dreamed of sailing on the sea again. They dreamed of seeing new places and meeting new friends. They dreamed of having more fun. They smiled in their sleep. They were happy.
