Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Normal Boy and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a normal boy named Tim. Tim had a folder. He loved his folder very much. Tim took the folder with him everywhere he went. One day, Tim went to the park with his folder. At the park, Tim played with his friends. He put his folder on a bench. But a big wind came and took the folder away. Tim was sad. He did not want to suffer without his folder. He looked and looked for it, but he could not find it. Then, a nice dog came to Tim. The dog had something in its mouth. It was Tim's folder! Tim was so happy. The dog was happy too. The dog and Tim became friends. They played together every day. Tim never lost his folder again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toys, but he did not like to clean up. His room was very messy. One day, Tim's mom told him he needed to become a big boy and clean his room and the bathroom. Tim was not happy. He did not want to clean. He wanted to play with his toys. His mom said if he did not clean, he could not play outside with his friends. Tim was sad. He knew he had to clean to play with his friends. Tim started to clean his room. He put his toys away and made his bed. Then, he went to the bathroom. He cleaned the sink and the tub. The bathroom became very clean. Tim's mom was happy. She said he was a big boy now. Tim was happy too. He could play with his friends outside.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a toy trumpet that she loved very much. She thought her trumpet was very pretty. She liked to hold the trumpet and play it all day. One day, Mia went to the park with her mom. She brought her pretty trumpet with her. She played her trumpet while her mom watched her. The sound of her trumpet made her happy. At the park, Mia saw a little boy named Tim. Tim also had a toy trumpet. Mia and Tim played their trumpets together. They made music and had a fun time. Mia's mom and Tim's dad thought they looked very nice playing their trumpets. They all had a great day at the park.

One day, a small orange fish named Fin wanted to have a swim party. He told his best friend, a blue fish named Bubbles. "Let's have a swim party!" said Fin. Bubbles was very happy and said, "Yes! Let's invite all our friends!" Fin and Bubbles told all their friends about the swim party. They all came to the party and had lots of fun. They swam, played games, and ate yummy fish treats. Everyone was having a good time. Then, something unexpected happened. A big, friendly whale named Wally came to the party! All the little fish were scared at first, but Wally said, "Don't be scared, I just want to swim and have fun too!" The little fish were surprised, but they let Wally join their swim party. They all swam, played, and had even more fun together. The swim party was a big success, and all the fish and Wally became great friends.

Andy was playing in the garden when he saw his dad. He ran over and said, "Hi, Dad!" His dad smiled and gave him a big hug. Then he brought out a box filled with soft toys. Andy smiled and clapped his hands. His dad said, "These toys will supply you with lots of motion. You can move them around the garden and play." Andy took a few of the toys and started racing them around the garden. His favourite one was a soft bear. He hugged it and said, "Thank you, Dad." His dad smiled and said, "You're welcome, Andy."

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to observe things around him. One day, he saw a big bell in the park. The bell was shiny and made a loud sound when it was hit. Tim saw a small bird fly near the bell. The bird was angry because it could not find its nest. The bird tried to hit the bell with its beak to make a loud sound. Tim wanted to help the bird find its nest. So, Tim and the angry bird looked around the park together. They observed the trees and the flowers. Finally, they found the bird's nest in a tall tree. The bird was happy and thanked Tim. They became good friends and played together every day.
