Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Lady

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk with her mom. They saw a big house. A rich lady lived in the house. The lady was sad. Mia wanted to know why she was sad. Mia asked the lady, "Why are you sad?" The lady said, "I have no friends to play with." Mia felt bad for the lady. She wanted to help. Mia gave the lady a big hug. The lady smiled. She was happy now. Mia said, "We can be friends and play together." The lady and Mia played all day. They had lots of fun.

One day, a big red bus went down the road. It was a very icy day. The bus driver had to be very careful. The bus picked up kids to go to school. The kids were happy to see their friends on the bus. The kids liked to play a game. They would close their eyes and imagine things. One kid imagined he was a big dinosaur. Another kid imagined she was a pretty princess. They liked to share their stories with each other. But today, something unexpected happened. When they closed their eyes and imagined, they all saw the same thing. They all saw a big, warm, sunny beach with sand and water. They were all playing together on the beach. When they opened their eyes, they were really at the beach! The icy day was now warm and sunny. The kids and the bus driver all laughed and played together on the beach. They didn't know how it happened, but they were happy for the fun surprise.

Once upon a time, there was a nice yard. In this yard, there were many pretty flowers and green trees. A little boy named Timmy lived near the yard. He liked to play there every day. One day, while Timmy was playing in the yard, he saw a small bird. The bird had a hurt wing and could not fly. Timmy wanted to help the bird. He said, "Don't worry, little bird. I will help you." Timmy managed to find a soft cloth and wrapped it around the bird's wing. He took care of the bird until its wing was better. The bird was very happy and said, "Thank you, Timmy. You are very kind." Timmy smiled and they played together in the nice yard.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had an aunt who was very rude. One day, Lily went to the park with her mom and saw her aunt there. Lily went to her aunt and said, "Hello, aunt!" But her aunt did not recognize Lily. She said, "Who are you? Go away!" Lily felt sad and went back to her mom. Lily's mom saw what happened and said, "Don't worry, Lily. Some people are just rude." Then, they went home. Lily did not play with her aunt again, and they did not have a good time.

One day, a cat and a dog went to a party. The party had many different things to see and do. The cat and the dog were very happy. At the party, there was a game with numbers. The cat said to the dog, "Let's play the number game!" The dog said, "Yes! I love numbers!" They played the game and had lots of fun. In the corner of the party, there was a table with drinks. The cat saw a bottle of wine. The cat did not know what wine was. The dog said, "That is for big people, not for us." The cat and the dog played more games and ate yummy food. They had a great time at the party.

One day, a lovely deer was walking in the woods. The deer saw a little boy. The boy was sad. He was crying. The deer went to the boy to help him. "Hi, little boy," said the deer. "Why are you sad?" The boy looked up and saw the deer. "I fell down and hurt my hand," said the boy. The deer wanted to help the boy, so she wiped the dirt off his hand with her soft nose. Just then, a big bird came down from the sky. The bird wanted to take the boy away! But the lovely deer was brave. She stood in front of the boy and told the bird, "You cannot take him!" The bird saw how brave the deer was and flew away. The boy was happy and said, "Thank you, deer!" The deer and the boy became good friends. They learned that helping others is a good thing to do.
