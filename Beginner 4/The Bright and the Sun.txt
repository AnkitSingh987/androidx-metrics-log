Category: Beginner
Title: The Bright and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a bright, big sun in the sky. It was a fun day to play outside. A boy named Tim and his dog, Max, went to the park. They took their favorite toy, a football, with them. They loved to play catch and run around. After playing for a while, Tim and Max felt tired. They found a big tree with lots of shade. Tim said, "Let's sleep here for a bit, Max." So, they lay down under the tree and closed their eyes. The wind blew softly, and the birds sang sweet songs. Soon, Tim and Max woke up from their sleep. They felt happy and full of energy again. They picked up their football and played some more. It was a great day for Tim and Max, and they had lots of fun under the bright sun.

One day, a nice boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big tree with a strange thing stuck in it. The strange thing was a weapon. Tim did not know what it was, but he wanted to help get it out. He tried to pull the weapon out of the tree. Tim pulled and pulled, but the weapon did not move. Then, a small bird flew down and said, "I can help you, Tim." The bird went to the tree and started to pull the weapon with its beak. Together, they pulled and pulled, and the weapon came out of the tree. Tim and the bird were very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The weapon turned into a big, shiny key! The bird said, "This key opens a secret door. Let's go find it!" Tim and the bird went on a new adventure to find the secret door, and they became the best of friends.

---

One day, a little girl named Sue went to a shop with her mom. In the shop, there were many things to see. Sue saw a pretty cushion. It was dark blue and soft. Sue asked her mom, "Can I have this cushion, please?" Her mom looked at the cushion on display and said, "Not today, Sue. We need to buy food and other things first." Sue was sad but she knew her mom was right. As they walked away from the shop, it started to rain. The rain was heavy and soon the whole street was wet. Sue and her mom ran home as fast as they could. When they got home, they were wet and cold. Sue thought about the dark cushion and wished she had it to make her feel better. But the cushion was still in the shop, and Sue was sad.

---

Jimmy was so happy. He loved to skip. He skipped around the house and he skipped down the street. Today, Jimmy was skipping to buy some cereal. He stopped in front of the shop and saw that the window was broken. Jimmy was a little sad but he remembered why he was there and kept skipping in! Inside the shop, Jimmy beamed with excitement as he saw all the different cereals. He grabbed the one he liked the most and skipped to the checkout. The shopkeeper said "That will be one dollar!" Jimmy skipped to get the money and gave it to the shopkeeper. Jimmy skipped happily back home with his cereal. He couldn't wait to eat it!

One day, a little bird with a hurt wing sat on a tree. The bird was sad and frustrated because it could not fly with its friends. It wanted to play and have fun in the sky, but the hurt wing made it stay on the tree. The bird saw a big cat on the ground. The cat was hungry and wanted to eat the bird. The bird thought and thought. Then, it decided to try and fly away from the cat, even with its hurt wing. The bird tried very hard to fly, but it could not. The cat jumped up and caught the bird. The bird was very sad because it could not get away. The cat ate the bird and went away. The bird's friends missed it and wished it could still be with them.
