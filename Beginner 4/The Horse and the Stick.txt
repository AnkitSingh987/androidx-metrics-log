Category: Beginner
Title: The Horse and the Stick

Once upon a time, there was a little horse. The horse was eager to run and play. One day, the horse went to the big field to play with his friends. They ran and jumped all day long. But then, something unexpected happened. The horse stepped on a big stick and it started to break. The horse was scared and didn't know what to do. He thought he broke his leg. His friends came to help him. They looked at the stick and saw that it was not his leg that broke, but the stick! The horse was so happy that he didn't break his leg. He thanked his friends and they all went back to playing in the big field.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Jack. He was three years old. Jack was walking in the park one day with his dad, when he saw a group of birds in the sky. Jack stopped and looked up at the birds with wonder. His dad said, "Just look at those birds speaking to each other. How cool." Jack was delighted and started asking his dad questions about the birds. His dad said, "Birds fly up high and they speak using their wings. It's pretty amazing to watch them." Then, the sun started to shine and the birds glowed in the light sky. Jack was mesmerised as he watched the group of birds soar gracefully through the air. He asked his dad, "Will I be able to speak like the birds one day?" His dad smiled and said, "Yes, Jack. You will be able to speak like the birds soon enough."

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He lived by the sea with his mom and dad. Tim loved to play on the beach and watch the waves. One day, he found an enormous shell. Tim's mom told him that if he held the shell to his ear, he could hear the waves. So, Tim held the shell to his ear and listened. He heard the sound of a big wave coming. Tim was excited and wanted to see the big wave. The next day, Tim went to the beach with his mom and dad. They watched the waves together. Suddenly, the big wave that Tim heard in the shell came. It was so big and beautiful. Tim and his family laughed and clapped as they watched the enormous wave crash onto the beach. And Tim knew that holding the shell had shown him something very special.

One day, a white cat named Snow was playing outside. Snow saw a big rainbow in the sky. Snow wanted to find the end of the rainbow. She looked and looked, but could not find it. Snow felt sad and sat under a tree. A bird was sitting in the tree. The bird saw Snow and asked, "Why are you sad?" Snow said, "I want to find the end of the rainbow, but I can't." The bird said, "I can help you. Just follow me." Snow was happy and followed the bird. They looked for the rainbow together. Suddenly, the sky became dark. It started to rain. The rainbow was gone. The bird said, "Oh no! The rainbow is gone. I am sorry, Snow." Snow was sad again. The rain made Snow's white fur all wet and dirty. Snow went home, sad that she could not find the end of the rainbow.

Once upon a time, there was a big tent in the park. Many kids liked to play in it. One sunny day, a girl named Lily and a boy named Tim went inside the tent to have a fun time. They played games and shared some delicious snacks. They laughed and shouted as they played. They were having a great time in the tent. Suddenly, they heard a funny noise outside the tent. Lily and Tim peeked out and saw a big, fluffy dog. The dog was making the funny noise. They thought the dog was lost. But then, the dog's owner came running and thanked Lily and Tim for finding her dog. The owner gave them more delicious snacks as a thank you gift. The day became even more fun, and they all played together in the big tent.
