Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Grumpy Cat and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a grumpy cat named Tom. Tom had a red mitten that he loved to play with. One day, Tom could not find his mitten. He looked everywhere but could not find it. Tom went to his friend, a dog named Max. Tom asked Max, "Did you see my red mitten?" Max said, "I saw it near the tree." Tom went to the tree and saw his mitten high up on a branch. Tom was sad because he could not reach it. Tom had an idea. He asked Max to help him raise the mitten. Max stood on his back legs and Tom climbed on Max's head. Tom reached the mitten and they both were happy. Tom thanked Max and they played together with the red mitten.

One day, a clown was at a fun party. The clown had a big red nose, funny shoes, and a high hat. He made the kids laugh and smile. The sun was shining bright in the sky. At the party, there was a big, pretty balloon. The balloon was high up in the sky. The clown wanted to make the kids happy by getting the balloon for them. The clown had a plan. He jumped very high to get the balloon. But something unexpected happened. The balloon popped and made a loud sound. The kids thought it was a funny joke. They laughed and clapped. The clown was happy that the kids were happy, even if it was not what he planned.

Once upon a time there was a puppy and a kitty. The puppy wanted to have a meeting with the kitty, but the kitty did not want to meet with the puppy. The puppy was sad and tried hard to make the kitty happy. One day, the puppy had an idea. He wished upon a star, and the star made his wish come true. He put some soft treats in the kitty's doorway and rang the doorbell. The kitty came out and was so surprised! They both started to play and became friends. The moral of this story is that sometimes you need to be kind and caring to others, even if they don't want to be friends with you. Not everyone wants the same thing, but kindness can help a friendship grow.

Once upon a time, there was a sailor named Sam. He had a big boat. Sam loved to sail on the water. One day, the wind was slow, so Sam had to try very hard to make his boat go fast. As Sam tried, he saw another boat. The other boat was going fast. Sam wanted to go fast too. He tried and tried, but his boat was still slow. Sam felt sad. Then, Sam had an idea. He asked his friend, the big whale, for help. The whale pushed Sam's boat with its tail. Now, Sam's boat was going fast. Sam was happy. He waved to the other boat as he sailed by.

Tom and Anna were playing with their toy cars in the living room. They liked to pretend they were racing on a big track. They had a coin that they used as a prize for the winner. "Ready, set, go!" Tom shouted, and they pushed their cars along the floor. Anna's car was pink and Tom's car was blue. They zoomed past the sofa, the table, and the lamp. They were very fast and very excited. "Who will finish first?" Anna asked, as they reached the last corner. "I will!" Tom said, and he tried to make his car go faster. But he pushed too hard and his car flipped over. Anna's car passed him and crossed the finish line. "I win! I win!" Anna cheered, and she picked up the coin. "This is my prize!" Tom was sad. He wanted the coin too. He looked at his car and saw that one of its wheels was broken. He started to cry. "Hey, don't cry, Tom," Anna said. She saw that he was upset and felt sorry for him. "It's just a game. Here, you can have the coin. It's only a toy, like our cars." "Really?" Tom asked, wiping his tears. He looked at the coin and smiled. "Thank you, Anna. You are very nice." Anna smiled too. She gave him the coin and hugged him. "You're welcome, Tom. You are my friend." They put their cars away and decided to play something else. They were happy and excited again.
