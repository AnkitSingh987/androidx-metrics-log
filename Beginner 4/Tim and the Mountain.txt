Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Mountain

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very persistent boy. He always wanted to know about new things. One day, he asked his mom, "Mom, what is a volcano?" His mom said, "A volcano is a big mountain that can make hot red stuff come out. It's not safe to go near it." Tim was very interested in the volcano. He wanted to see it with his own eyes. So, he went on a long walk to find the volcano. But he missed his way and got lost. A kind lady found Tim and asked, "Little boy, why are you here all alone?" Tim said, "I want to see the volcano, but I am lost." The kind lady took Tim back to his mom. Tim's mom was very happy to see him. She said, "Tim, you should not go far away alone. It can be dangerous." Tim learned that he should always listen to his mom and not go to unsafe places alone.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree in a big park. The tall tree did not like sunshine. The tall tree would say, "I hate sunshine! It makes me too hot!" The tall tree liked the night time when it was cool and dark. One day, a small bird came to the tall tree. The small bird said, "Hello, tall tree! I love sunshine. It makes me happy and warm. Why do you hate sunshine?" The tall tree said, "Sunshine is too hot for me. I like the night time better." The small bird tried to help the tall tree like sunshine. They played and talked in the sunshine. But the tall tree still hated sunshine. One very hot day, the tall tree got so hot that it dried up. The small bird was sad because the tall tree was gone. The tall tree never learned to like sunshine, and that made the story end in a bad way.

Once upon a time, there was a furry dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his toys. His favorite toy was a big, red drum. Every day, Spot would hit the drum with his tail and make a loud sound. One day, Spot found a long string. He had an idea. Spot decided to tie the string to his drum. He put the string in his mouth and pulled it tight. Then, he tied a big knot. Now, Spot could pull the drum around with him wherever he went. Spot was so happy with his drum. He would pull it around the yard, and all the other animals would come to listen. They would dance and play as he hit the drum with his tail. And they all lived happily ever after.

Lila and Sam like to play in the garden. They look for bugs and flowers and rocks. One day, they see a cricket on a leaf. It is green and shiny and makes a loud sound. "Look, a cricket!" Lila says. "I want to take it home and show Mommy." "No, don't take it!" Sam says. "It is not nice to take a cricket. It wants to live in the garden with its friends." "But it is so pretty and original," Lila says. "I have never seen a cricket like this before. Please, can I take it?" Lila reaches for the cricket, but Sam stops her. He holds her hand and shakes his head. "No, Lila, you can't take it," he says. "It is not a toy. It is a living thing. It needs air and water and food. It will be sad and scared in a jar or a box. It will stop making its sound. It will die." Lila looks at the cricket and thinks. She sees its big eyes and long legs and feelers. She hears its sound again. She feels sorry for the cricket. "Okay, Sam, you are right," she says. "I don't want to take the cricket. I want it to be happy and free. Let's leave it alone and find something else to play with." They smile at each other and walk away. The cricket stays on the leaf and keeps making its sound. It is happy and free.
