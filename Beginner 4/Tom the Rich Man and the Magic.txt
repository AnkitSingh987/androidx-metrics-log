Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Rich Man and the Magic

Once upon a time, there was a rich man named Tom. He had a magic pen. This pen could shrink anything it touched. Tom loved to play with his pen and show it to his friends. One day, Tom's friend, Sam, came to visit. Tom said, "Look at my magic pen! It can shrink anything!" Sam did not believe Tom. He said, "I don't think it is true. Show me!" Tom wanted to prove Sam wrong. He touched the pen to a big ball. The ball became very small. Sam was amazed and said, "Wow! It is true!" But then, the pen started to shrink everything in the house. The chairs, the tables, and even the food became tiny. Tom and Sam were scared. They had to find a way to fix it. They worked together and found a way to make everything big again. In the end, they learned that magic can be tricky and they should be careful with it.

Once upon a time, there was a powerful dog named Max. Max loved to eat pie. One day, he found a big pie on the table. It smelled so good! Max jumped up and took a big bite. But, oh no! The pie was hot. Max felt bad in his mouth. He suffered because of the hot pie. He ran around the room and tried to make his mouth feel better. Max learned his lesson. Next time, he would wait for the pie to cool down before he ate it. He knew that even powerful dogs need to be careful. And from that day on, Max enjoyed his pie without any suffering.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big parade with many people. They were happy and clapped their hands. Tim saw a dog with a rough coat. The dog was part of the parade. "Look, Mom! A dog!" Tim said. His mom smiled and said, "Yes, it's a nice dog, isn't it?" As they watched the parade, Tim saw the dog dig a hole. The dog put a bone in the hole and started to bury it. But then, something unexpected happened. The dog dug up a big, shiny key! The dog showed the key to everyone at the parade. People were shocked and excited. They did not know where the key came from or what it could open. Tim and his mom smiled and knew they had seen something very special that day.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She loved to read books. One day, she found a big book with many different stories. Lily was very happy and started to read the book. In one story, there was a kind judge. The judge was different because he had a big, round nose. People in the town would come to him when they had a problem. One day, a boy and a girl came to the judge. They were fighting over a toy. The boy said, "It's mine!" The girl said, "No, it's mine!" The judge thought for a moment. He said, "I have an idea. Let's cut the toy in half. You can each have half." The boy and the girl looked at each other. They did not want to cut the toy. They decided to share the toy and be friends. The judge was happy that he could help them. Lily liked the story about the kind judge. She read more stories in the big book. She learned that being different is okay and that it is important to share and be kind to others.

One day, Tom and Sam went for a jog in the park. It was a cold day with icy air. They both wore big coats and hats to stay warm. They jogged near the lake and saw ducks swimming. Tom said, "Look, Sam! Ducks! Let's give them some bread." Sam nodded and they went to the tip of the lake. They threw small pieces of bread to the ducks. The ducks were happy and quacked. But then, something unexpected happened. A big fish jumped out of the water and ate the bread too! Tom and Sam were surprised. They laughed and said, "Silly fish, bread is for ducks!" They went home and told their mom about the big fish, and she laughed too.
