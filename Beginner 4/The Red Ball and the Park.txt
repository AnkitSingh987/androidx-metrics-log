Category: Beginner
Title: The Red Ball and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a red ball. The red ball had a friend named Sam. Sam and the red ball liked to play in the park. They would push the red ball to each other and laugh. One day, Sam said to the red ball, "You are one year old now! Let's have a big fun day!" The red ball was very happy. They played all day long. They pushed the red ball up and down the hills and had lots of fun. At the end of the day, Sam said, "Happy one year, red ball! I love playing with you." The red ball was very happy and loved playing with Sam too. They went home and rested, ready for another fun day together.

Millie was playing with her toys in her room when she heard a knock on the door. She opened it and saw her Aunt Sally outside. "Hello Millie! I have a surprise for you," said Aunt Sally. Millie was very excited and asked, "What is it?" Aunt Sally held up a quiz. "I made this special quiz for you," she said. "It's very important, so you have to do your best." Millie took the quiz and admired it. She sat down and began to answer the questions. She gave it her best effort and was very happy when she had done it. Aunt Sally gave Millie a hug. "That was great work, Millie," she said. "I am so proud of you." Millie smiled and gave Aunt Sally a big hug.

One day, a charming crocodile wanted to decorate his home. He asked his friend, a little bird, for help. "Bird, can you help me make my home pretty?" he asked. The bird said, "Yes, I can help you." They started to work together. The crocodile put up colorful pictures on his walls. The bird sang a happy song as they worked. But then, they found a big problem. The crocodile could not reach the high spots to decorate. The crocodile was sad. He said, "I cannot make my home pretty all by myself." The bird had an idea. "I can fly and help you with the high spots," the bird said. The crocodile was happy and said, "Thank you, bird!" Together, they finished decorating the crocodile's home. It was very pretty, and they were both proud. The charming crocodile and the little bird became the best of friends. They learned that working together can solve problems.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to march up and down the street. One day, Sue saw a sign that said "No Marching." This made Sue upset. Sue went home and told her mom about the sign. Her mom said, "Don't be upset. Let's make a new sign." So Sue and her mom made a sign that said, "Let's March!" They put the sign next to the other sign. The next day, Sue and her friends marched up and down the street. They were happy and not upset anymore. They marched and played all day. And they never stopped marching again.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom liked to play ball. One day, he saw a big pitch near his house. The pitch had a sign that said, "Do not play here." But Tom was a foolish boy and did not listen. Tom took his ball and went to the pitch. He played and played. He had fun. He did not see the big tree on the pitch. The tree had a big branch. Tom kicked the ball very high. The ball went up and up. The ball hit the big branch. The branch started to break. Tom was scared. He ran away. The branch fell down and broke the fence. Tom's mom saw what happened. She was very sad. Tom was in trouble. The end.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the mall with her mom. Lucy was restless. She wanted to see and touch everything. Her mom said, "Lucy, please hold my hand. We have a surprise for you." At the mall, they saw a big, shiny toy store. Lucy wanted to go in, but her mom said, "Not yet. We have to get your surprise first." Lucy was sad, but she listened to her mom. They walked until they found a big box with a bow. Lucy's mom said, "This is your surprise! Open it!" Lucy opened the box and inside was a new toy. She was so happy! Then, her mom said, "Now we can go to the toy store." Lucy and her mom went to the toy store and played with more toys. The surprise made Lucy's day at the mall even more fun.
