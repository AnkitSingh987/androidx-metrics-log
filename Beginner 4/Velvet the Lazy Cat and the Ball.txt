Category: Beginner
Title: Velvet the Lazy Cat and the Ball

One day, a lazy cat named Velvet was sleeping under a big tree. She loved to nap all day and did not like to play with the other cats. They would run and jump, but Velvet just wanted to sleep. Then, a bird came to the tree and said, "Velvet, why are you so lazy? Come play with us!" Velvet just yawned and said, "No, thank you. I like to sleep." The bird flew away, and Velvet went back to her nap. But then, something happened! A loud noise woke Velvet up. She saw all the other cats playing and having fun. They were playing a game and one of them hit a ball. The ball was flying right at her! Velvet jumped up and caught the ball with her paw. The other cats cheered and said, "Wow, Velvet! You can play with us!" Velvet smiled and joined the game. She learned that playing with friends can be fun too. The moral of the story is that we should try new things and not be lazy all the time.

Tom and Jen are twins. They like to play in their room. They have many toys and books. But they do not like to share. They fight a lot. One day, Tom wants to play with the red car. But Jen has it. She says it is hers. Tom gets angry. He tries to grab the car. Jen holds it tight. They pull and pull. Then, Tom kicks Jen. He kicks her hard. Jen cries. She drops the car. Tom takes it. He laughs. He says he wins. But Jen is hurt. She has a big bruise on her leg. It is thick and purple. It hurts a lot. She runs to Mom. She tells her what Tom did. Mom is mad. She scolds Tom. She takes the car away. She says he is naughty. She says he has to stay in his room. She says he can not play with Jen or any toys. Tom is sad. He is alone in his room. He has no car. He has no fun. He wishes he did not kick Jen. He says he is sorry. But it is too late. He has a bad ending.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a bird who loved to whistle. He had an original whistle that everyone in the town could hear. One day, the bird heard some news that a big storm was coming. The bird flew to his friend, the dog. "Big storm coming!" the bird said. The dog was scared. "Oh no! What can we do?" The dog knew the bird's whistle was special and asked, "Can you whistle loud to tell everyone?" The bird thought for a moment. He remembered how everyone loved his whistle. He knew he had to help his friends. So, the bird flew up high and whistled very loud. Everyone in the town heard the bird's whistle and knew it meant something important. All the animals in the town worked together to get ready for the storm. They helped each other find safe places to hide. When the storm came, everyone was safe and sound. After the storm, the animals thanked the bird for his special whistle that warned them. The bird was happy that he could help his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a family with two very cute children, brother Billy and sister Sarah. They loved exploring and learning new things. One day, Mommy said, "Let's go to the lecture!" Billy and Sarah were very excited. At the lecture, they saw an amazing design. It was very colourful and had lots of different shapes in it. Everyone said it was very beautiful. Billy said, "Wow! I want to design, too!" Sarah said, "Me too!" So, Mommy got out some paper and crayons, and the two children started designing. They made all sorts of colourful shapes, stars, and even a sun! "Look at us!" said Billy. "We look so cute!" said Sarah. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a powerful cat named Tom. Tom lived in a small house with a stove. He liked to settle down by the stove and sleep. One day, Tom was very tired. He wanted to settle down by the stove and sleep. But when he got there, he saw a mouse! The mouse was dancing on the stove. Tom was so surprised! Tom thought he would catch the mouse. But the mouse was too fast! The mouse jumped off the stove and ran away. Tom chased the mouse, but he could not catch it. In the end, Tom and the mouse became friends. They both settled by the stove and slept together.
