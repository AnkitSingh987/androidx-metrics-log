Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Cat and the Dog

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the park. They found a shiny toy. The toy was a laser. The cat said, "I want the laser!" The dog said, "No, I want the laser!" The cat and the dog started to fight. They ran around and around, trying to get the laser. They both felt very dizzy. They stopped fighting and sat down. A little girl came over and saw the cat and the dog. She picked up the laser and said, "Why are you fighting? You can both play with the laser together!" The cat and the dog looked at each other and smiled. They played with the laser and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy sailor who sailed the ocean blue. One day, he got so grumpy he decided to take a break. So he stopped his boat, and took a nap in the sun. Suddenly, he heard a noise. It was a little crab who said, "Please let me go! I want to go back in the water!" The grumpy sailor wanted to be nice, so he released the crab back in the water. The crab said, "Thank you for releasing me! Now I can go see my family and friends again!" The sailor was glad he had done something kind and he said goodbye to the crab. The sailor smiled as he continued his voyage, feeling much happier than before. He was glad he had taken the time to release the crab.

One day, a calm cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a pepper on the ground. He picked it up and thought it was a toy. Tom played with the pepper all day. Tom met his friend, a dog named Max. Max said, "What do you have, Tom?" Tom replied, "I found this pepper. I think it's a toy." Max wanted to play too, so they both played with the pepper. Tom had an idea. He said, "Let's record our fun day with the pepper." Max agreed. They found a camera and started recording. They played and played, but then the pepper broke. When the pepper broke, it made Tom and Max sneeze a lot. They couldn't stop sneezing. The camera fell and broke too. They were sad that their fun day ended badly.

One day a girl went to the beach. She saw something very impressive. A big crab was walking around on the sand. She wanted to touch it. The girl put out her hand real slow. The crab didn't move. She carefully put her hand closer. Just before she touched it, the crab rushed away. The girl was sad. But then, she saw another crab. This crab was even more impressive. It was a lot bigger. The girl wanted to touch this crab too. She put out her hand slow and steady, but this time the crab stayed still. She put her hand right on top of it. The girl felt very happy and excited. She was so proud that she could touch such an impressive crab.

Lily and Max were playing in the park with their dog, Spot. Spot was a big, hairy dog who liked to run and jump and bark. Lily and Max loved Spot very much. "Look, Spot, the sun is going down!" Lily said, pointing to the sky. The sky was orange and pink and purple. It was very pretty. "That is a sunset, Spot. Do you like it?" Spot looked at the sky and wagged his tail. He liked the sunset too. He barked and licked Lily's face. "Spot, you are silly!" Lily giggled. She hugged Spot and gave him a kiss. "Let's go home, Lily and Spot," Max said. "It is getting dark and cold. Mom and Dad are waiting for us." "Okay, Max," Lily said. She took Max's hand and they walked to their house. Spot followed them, happy and tired. When they got home, Mom and Dad greeted them with hugs and smiles. They gave them some warm milk and cookies. They asked them about their day. "We had fun, Mom and Dad," Lily said. "We played with Spot and we saw a sunset. It was beautiful." "We shook Spot's paw and he shook his fur," Max said. "He made us laugh." Mom and Dad smiled and nodded. They were glad that Lily and Max had a good time. They told them to brush their teeth and get ready for bed. Lily and Max did as they were told. They put on their pajamas and climbed into their beds. Spot jumped on Lily's bed and snuggled with her. He was a good dog. "Good night, Lily," Max said from his bed. "Good night, Max," Lily said. "Good night, Spot," they both said. Spot wagged his tail and closed his eyes. He dreamed of sunsets and shakes. He was happy.
