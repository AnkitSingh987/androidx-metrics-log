Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved cookies. One day, Sue's mom made black cookies. Sue was very happy. "Mom, can I have a cookie, please?" asked Sue. "Yes, you can have one," said her mom. Sue took a big bite of the black cookie. But it did not taste good. Sue was sad. Just then, a funny cat came into the room. The cat saw Sue was sad and wanted to comfort her. The cat jumped up and knocked the black cookies onto the floor. Sue laughed. The cat had made her feel better. Sue's mom came back into the room and saw the cookies on the floor. She laughed too. "I guess we should make new cookies," she said. Sue, her mom, and the funny cat made new cookies together. They were not black, and they tasted very good.

Anna and Ben were playing in the snow. They made a big snowman with a hat and a scarf. They gave him a carrot nose and two rocks for eyes. They were very happy with their snowman. "Let's go inside and have some hot cocoa," Anna said. "It's very cold out here." "OK, but first I want to show you something," Ben said. He took Anna's hand and led her to the back of the house. There was a big metal thing with a hole in it. It was a sink, but Anna and Ben did not know that. "What is it?" Anna asked. "I don't know, but look inside," Ben said. He lifted Anna up so she could see the hole. There was something shiny and hard inside. It was ice, but Anna and Ben did not know that. "Wow, it's so pretty," Anna said. "Can we touch it?" "Sure, why not?" Ben said. He reached his hand inside the hole and touched the ice. He felt a sharp pain and pulled his hand back. He started to cry. "Ow, ow, ow, it hurts, it hurts!" he sobbed. Anna was scared. She did not know what to do. She hugged Ben and tried to comfort him. "Shh, shh, it's OK, Ben, it's OK," she said. "Let's go inside and tell Mommy. She will make it better." They ran inside the house, leaving the sink and the ice behind. They did not know that they had discovered something very old and very cold. They only knew that they wanted to be warm and safe.

Once upon a time, there was a generous lady. She loved playing the piano every day. One day, she was missing something. She looked and looked but could not find it. Suddenly, she saw it - it had slipped behind the piano! The lady was very happy and decided to play the piano. She played for a very long time, with big smiles on her face. Everyone admired how generous she was with her music. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She was very excited because her mom told her a story about a far away planet. Mia wanted to go to the planet and see what it was like. One day, Mia put on her favorite shoes and went outside. She saw a big, round ball in the sky. "Is that the planet?" she asked herself. She started to jump up and down, trying to reach it. "I want to go there!" she said out loud. A bird heard Mia and flew down to her. "You can't go to that planet," said the bird. "It is too far away, and you need to rest. It is not safe for little girls like you." Mia didn't listen to the bird and kept jumping. She got very tired and fell down. She cried because she couldn't go to the planet and her shoes were dirty.

Once upon a time, there was a brown wolf. The brown wolf liked to play in the water. One day, he went to the river to soak his feet. He loved how the water felt on his toes. As the brown wolf played, a little girl came to the river too. She saw the wolf and said, "Hi, brown wolf! Can I play with you?" The wolf looked at her and said, "Yes, let's play together!" They splashed and soaked in the water all day. But then, something unexpected happened. The little girl took off her hat, and she was a wolf too! She said, "I am a wolf like you! Let's be friends forever!" The brown wolf was so happy. They played and soaked in the river every day, and they were the best of friends.
