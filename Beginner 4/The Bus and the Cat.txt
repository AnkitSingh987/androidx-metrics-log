Category: Beginner
Title: The Bus and the Cat

One day, a big bus went down the road. It was very far away, but it got closer and closer. A little boy named Tim saw the bus and wanted to get on it. He ran to the bus stop to wait. As Tim waited, he saw a small cat. The cat was playing with a ball. The cat hit the ball and it began to slip away. Tim ran to catch the ball for the cat. "Here you go, cat!" said Tim as he gave the ball back. Just then, the bus came. Tim got on the bus and waved goodbye to the cat. The cat meowed and waved back with its paw. Tim sat down on the bus and looked out the window. He saw the cat become distant as the bus drove away. Tim smiled, happy to have helped the cat.

Once upon a time there was a little girl. She was happy and liked baking. Today she wanted to bake something special with her mom. They went to the store and bought some pears. As they were walking home, something scared her. She hid behind her mom. Back at home, they washed the pears and peeled them. Then, they put the pears into a bowl with eggs and flour, and mixed them all together. Mom put the mix into the oven and they waited. When it was done baking, they took it out and put it on a plate. It smelled delicious and tasted even better! They both smiled with delight. The girl was so happy she had made something special with her mom. She even forgot about being scared!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was adorable with big eyes and a sweet smile. She had a cat named Tom. Tom was her best friend. They played together all day. One day, Mia and Tom were watching a movie on a screen. They saw a cat on the screen who was cold and shivering. Mia asked her mom, "Why is the cat shivering?" Her mom said, "The cat is cold and needs a warm place to stay." Mia wanted to help the cat. She found a soft blanket and wrapped it around Tom. Tom stopped shivering and was warm and cozy. Mia learned that it is good to help others when they are cold or in need. Moral: Always help others when they need it.

Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to look at the flowers and smell them. They saw a big yellow flower that was very pretty. Lily wanted to pick it and give it to her mom. "Can I have the flower, Ben?" Lily asked. "No, Lily, you can't. The flower is not ours. It belongs to the garden. If you pick it, it will die," Ben said. "But I want to make mom happy. She likes flowers," Lily said. "You can make mom happy in other ways. You can draw her a picture, or give her a hug, or tell her you love her. But you can't take the flower. That is bad," Ben said. Lily was sad. She didn't want to be bad. She wanted to be good. She looked at the flower and saw a little bee on it. The bee was making honey from the flower. The flower was helping the bee. "Look, Ben, the flower is helping the bee. The bee is making honey. Honey is good. It can heal us when we are sick," Lily said. "Yes, Lily, you are right. The flower is helping the bee. And the bee is helping us. And we can help the flower. We can water it, and protect it, and sing to it. Then the flower will be happy, and mom will be happy, and we will be happy," Ben said. Lily smiled. She liked Ben's idea. She didn't want to pick the flower anymore. She wanted to help the flower. She and Ben got a bucket of water and poured some on the flower. Then they sang a song to the flower. The flower seemed to smile back at them. Lily and Ben felt good. They had done a good thing. They ran to mom and told her what they did. Mom hugged them and praised them. She said they were very kind and smart. Lily and Ben were happy. They had learned a lesson. They had learned to love the flower.
