Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Bird and the Leaves

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim was light and small. He liked to fly around and sing all day. One day, Tim saw a big hedge. The hedge was green and pretty. Tim thought it would be a good place to settle and make a home. Tim met a big bird named Sam near the hedge. Sam was nice and friendly. Tim said, "Hello, Sam! I want to settle in this hedge. Can I be your friend?" Sam smiled and said, "Yes, Tim! Let's be friends and live in the hedge together." Tim and Sam settled in the hedge. They made a cozy nest with soft leaves and twigs. They played and sang songs every day. They were very happy to be friends and live in the beautiful hedge.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to twist her hair with her fingers. One day, she found a brush in her room. Sue thought it was a magic brush. She wanted to see if it could really do magic. Sue had a toy cat named Tom. Tom was very stupid. He would always fall and bump into things. Sue thought, "Maybe the magic brush can make Tom smart!" So she brushed Tom with the magic brush. Suddenly, Tom started to move and talk! Tom was not stupid anymore. He was very smart and could do many tricks. Sue was so happy. She played with Tom all day long. The magic brush had made her toy cat come to life. It was a fun and unexpected day for Sue and her new smart friend, Tom.

Lila and Ben like to sort things. They sort their toys by color, shape and size. They sort their books by big and small. They sort their clothes by warm and cool. Sorting is fun and helps them find things. One day, they find a whip in the garage. It is long and gray and has a handle. They do not know what it is for. They ask their mom, but she is busy. They ask their dad, but he is on the phone. They decide to sort it with their other things. They try to sort it by color, but it does not match anything. They try to sort it by shape, but it is too bendy. They try to sort it by size, but it is too long. They get frustrated and start to swing it around. The whip makes a loud noise when it hits the air. It sounds like a crack. Lila and Ben think it is funny and exciting. They take turns whipping the whip and making noise. They do not see their cat, Milo, hiding under the couch. Milo is scared of the whip and the noise. He thinks it is a snake or a monster. He tries to run away, but the whip catches his tail. He yowls and scratches. Lila and Ben drop the whip and run to him. They see that he is hurt and bleeding. They feel sorry and sad. They hug Milo and say sorry. They get a bandage and wrap his tail. They bring him some water and a treat. They promise to never play with the whip again. They put it back in the garage and close the door. They learn that some things are not for sorting or playing. They learn to be careful and kind.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends, Joe and Pat. Joe was a clever and curious little boy who loved thinking up new ideas. One day, Pat had an idea. He said to Joe, "Let's load some sauce in a spoon and go fly a kite!" Joe thought this was a great idea and they both hurried outside. As Joe and Pat were running to the kite, they heard a strange noise coming from the nearby garden. Joe and Pat stopped in their tracks to see what was making the noise. It was a little green rat! Joe was excited and he quickly reached for the rat. Pat shouted, "Wait! I have an even better idea. Maybe if we load the spoon with some sauce, the rat will come out of hiding!" Joe thought it was a clever idea, so they did that. Sure enough, the rat came out and started munching on the sauce. Joe and Pat were so happy they let out a cheer! Then they grabbed the kite and ran off to the park. Little did they know this was the start of a long and exciting friendship!
