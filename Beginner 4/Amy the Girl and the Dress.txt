Category: Beginner
Title: Amy the Girl and the Dress

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a big room with a big wardrobe. Amy loved to play dress-up with her pretty clothes. One day, she wanted to find the perfect dress to wear. Amy opened her wardrobe and started to look. She saw a red dress, a blue dress, and a yellow dress. She tried on the red dress, but it was too long. She tried on the blue dress, but it was too small. Then, she tried on the yellow dress, and it was perfect! Amy was so happy to find the perfect dress. She wore it all day and showed her mom and dad. They smiled and said she looked very pretty. Amy played in her perfect dress all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a poor little bear who lived in the forest. One day, the bear walked into the clearing and he saw lots of junk laying around on the ground. Scattered among the junk was an old ice cream cone. The bear thought it looked so delicious that he wanted to try it. "Wait a minute," the bear thought. He noticed that the ice cream was starting to melt. He quickly got an idea and ran over to the pond to get some water. He filled the cone with the water and stirred it around the cone with his paws. The bear had made an ice pop! The bear enjoyed the ice pop and ate it all up. The junk in the clearing had melted away and the bear had a nice treat. The poor bear was so happy that he had turned the junk into such a delightful snack. From then on, the bear would always run to the pond whenever he found junk in the forest.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a jog in the park. He saw a big dog. Tim was scared of the big dog. The dog looked at Tim and wagged its tail. Tim thought the dog might be nice after all. Tim said, "Hi, dog. Want to play?" The dog barked and ran to Tim. They played catch with a ball. After playing, they were both tired and hungry. Tim had a plate of cookies in his bag. He shared the cookies with the dog. A girl named Sue saw Tim and the dog. She said, "That dog was scared too, but you were kind to it. Now you have a new friend!" Tim smiled and learned that being kind can make friends, even when you are scared.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to build things. He had a belt with all his tools. Every day, he would use his belt to build something new. One day, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue loved to cook. She made delicious treats for her friends. Tim and Sue decided to build a big table and have a party with their friends. "Let's build a table and share your delicious treats," said Tim to Sue. "Yes, let's do it!" Sue replied. They worked together to build the table. Tim used his belt and Sue helped him. After they finished building the table, Sue placed her delicious treats on it. Their friends came and they all had a great party. Tim and Sue were very happy. From that day on, they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to travel and explore. One day, Max saw a big fence. He wanted to see what was behind it. Max was a noisy dog, so he barked and barked at the fence. A little girl named Lily heard the noisy barks. She came out of her house and said, "Why are you barking, Max?" Max wagged his tail and looked at the fence. Lily understood and said, "Let's go see what's behind the fence!" They walked around the fence and found a big surprise. Behind the fence, there was a party! Max and Lily were so happy. They danced and played with the other kids and dogs at the party. The noisy barks led them to a fun day they would never forget.
