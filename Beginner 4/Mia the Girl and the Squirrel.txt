Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Squirrel

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to collect things. She would collect rocks, leaves, and flowers. One day, she went to a wild park with her mom. The wild park had many trees, birds, and animals. In the park, Mia saw a squirrel. The squirrel looked anxious. It was running around and looking for something. Mia asked the squirrel, "What are you looking for?" The squirrel said, "I am looking for nuts to collect for the winter." Mia wanted to help the squirrel. She looked around and found some nuts. She gave the nuts to the squirrel. The squirrel was very happy. Mia and the squirrel became friends. They spent the day playing and collecting nuts together in the wild park.

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They saw a pond with many ducks and fish. They wanted to feed them some bread. "Look, Lily, a big fish!" Tom said, pointing to the water. "Wow, it is big!" Lily said. "Can we give it some bread?" Tom nodded and tore a piece of bread from his sandwich. He threw it into the pond. The big fish saw the bread and swam fast to get it. It opened its mouth and bit the bread. Then it splashed its tail and made a big wave. "Ha ha, that was fun!" Tom said, laughing. He threw another piece of bread. But Lily saw something shiny in the water. It was a gem. It was round and blue and sparkled in the sun. Lily wanted to have it. She was careless. She did not think about the fish or the water. She reached out her hand and tried to grab the gem. "Ouch!" Lily cried. The big fish saw her hand and thought it was food. It bit her finger and made her bleed. Lily pulled her hand back and started to cry. "Help, help!" Lily shouted. "The fish bit me!" Tom heard her and ran to her. He saw her finger and the gem. He was scared and sorry. "I'm sorry, Lily, I'm sorry!" Tom said. He wrapped his napkin around her finger and hugged her. "Don't cry, don't cry!" A lady who worked in the park heard them and came to help. She saw what happened and took them to the office. She cleaned Lily's finger and put a band-aid on it. She also gave her a lollipop and a sticker. "Are you okay, Lily?" the lady asked. Lily nodded and smiled. She felt better. She thanked the lady and said sorry for being careless. "It's okay, Lily, but you have to be careful next time," the lady said. "The pond is not a place to play. The fish can bite and the water can be deep. You could get hurt or drown. Do you understand?" Lily nodded and said yes. She learned her lesson. She did not want the gem anymore. She gave it to the lady and said she could keep it. "Thank you, Lily, that's very kind of you," the lady said. "But you know what? This is not a real gem. It's just a glass bead. Someone must have dropped it in the pond. It's not worth anything." Lily was surprised. She thought the gem was precious and beautiful. But it was not. It was fake and dangerous. She was glad she gave it away. "Come on, Lily, let's go home," Tom said. He held her hand and walked with her. He was glad she was okay. He did not want to play in the pond anymore. He wanted to play in the sandbox. "Okay, Tom, let's go home," Lily said. She held his hand and walked with him. She was glad he was nice. He was her best friend. She wanted to play with him in the sandbox.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a jog in the park. He liked to run and play outside. The sun was shining, and he was happy. Tim saw a girl with a scooter. The girl was riding it very fast. Tim wanted to play with her, so he asked, "Can I try your scooter?" The girl said, "Yes, but be careful, it is hard to ride." Tim tried to ride the scooter, but it was hard at first. He fell down, but he got up and tried again. Soon, he got better at riding the scooter. Tim and the girl had fun playing together. At the end of the day, Tim said, "Thank you for letting me play with your scooter." The girl smiled and said, "You're welcome! Let's play again soon." Tim went home, feeling happy about his new friend and the fun day at the park.
