Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Toys

One day, a boy named Tom went to the park. He saw a sign that said, "Do not touch the stuff." Tom was curious and wanted to know what the stuff was. It looked like a big pile of sand, but it was a different color. Tom's friend, Sam, came to the park too. Sam said, "Hey Tom, let's play with the stuff! The sign is stupid. I don't think it's important." Tom thought about it for a moment, but he wanted to follow the sign. He told Sam, "No, I don't want to play with the stuff. The sign says not to." Just then, a big wind came and blew the stuff away. Underneath the stuff, there were lots of fun toys! Tom and Sam were very surprised. They played with the toys and had a great time. Tom learned that sometimes it's good to follow signs, even if they seem stupid at first.

Once upon a time, there was a young girl named Molly. She was only three years old. One day, Molly was walking in her backyard when she saw a blue lollipop. She was so excited! She yelled, "Lollipop!". Molly rushed to pick it up. She licked the lollipop and it was so tasty. Just as Molly began to enjoy her lollipop, a big gust of wind blew her lollipop away. Molly was very sad. She yelled as loud as she could, hoping the lollipop would come back. Suddenly, a blue bird flew down and picked up the lollipop in its mouth. It flew back to Molly and gently placed the lollipop in her hand. Molly was so excited and happy that she yelled with joy! She thanked the blue bird and waved goodbye as it flew away. Molly returned home to enjoy her lollipop and knew that she was very lucky. She found a friend in the blue bird and a yummy treat in the blue lollipop.

Sara and Lily were best friends. They liked to play with dolls and dress up. One day, Sara found a new skirt in her closet. It was pink and shiny, with many flowers. Sara loved it and wanted to wear it. "Look at my new skirt!" Sara said to Lily. "It is so pretty and soft. Do you want to try it on?" Lily nodded. She also liked the skirt. She took it from Sara and put it on. She twirled around and smiled. "It is very nice. Can I keep it?" she asked. Sara frowned. She wanted her skirt back. She did not want to share it. "No, it is mine. Give it to me!" she said. She pulled the skirt from Lily. Lily was sad. She did not expect Sara to be so mean. She felt the skirt tear in her hands. She gasped. "Oh no! Look what you did! You broke your skirt!" she said. Sara looked at the skirt. It had a big hole in it. She was angry and sad. She blamed Lily. "You are a bad friend! You ruined my skirt! Go away!" she said. She threw the skirt on the floor and ran to her room. Lily was hurt. She did not want to fight with Sara. She wanted to say sorry and fix the skirt. She picked up the skirt and followed Sara. She knocked on her door. "Sara, please listen to me. I am sorry. I did not mean to tear your skirt. Can we be friends again?" she said. Sara heard Lily's voice. She felt a pang in her heart. She remembered how much she liked Lily. She realized she was wrong to be selfish and rude. She opened the door and saw Lily holding the skirt. She saw the tears in Lily's eyes. She felt sorry too. She hugged Lily. "I am sorry too. I should have shared with you. You are a good friend. Can you forgive me?" she said. Lily smiled. She forgave Sara. She hugged her back. "Of course I forgive you. You are a good friend too. Can we fix the skirt together?" she said. Sara nodded. They went to Sara's mom and asked for help. Sara's mom was happy to see them make up. She helped them sew the skirt. It was not as shiny as before, but it was still pretty. Sara and Lily decided to take turns wearing it. They learned to listen to each other and share. They were best friends again.
