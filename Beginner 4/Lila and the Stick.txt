Category: Beginner
Title: Lila and the Stick

Lila liked to write. She wrote on paper, on walls, on books. She wrote her name, and shapes, and animals. She wrote with pens, and crayons, and markers. She wrote every day. One day, it was very cold. Lila wanted to go outside and play. She put on her coat, and hat, and boots. She looked for her mittens, but she could not find them. She saw a pair of mittens on the table. They were not hers, they were her brother's. But they looked warm and cozy. She took them and put them on. She went outside and saw the snow. It was white and soft and sparkly. She wanted to write on it. She found a stick and started to write. She wrote her name, and shapes, and animals. She wrote with the stick, and her fingers, and her mittens. But she did not know that her brother's mittens were wise. They had a magic power. They could make what she wrote come true. As she wrote, the snow changed. Her name became ice, and the shapes became rocks, and the animals became alive. They were not friendly, they were angry. They saw her and started to chase her. Lila was scared. She ran back to the house. She tried to take off the mittens, but they were stuck. They did not want to let her go. They wanted to make more trouble. She cried for help, but no one heard her. The animals caught her and bit her and scratched her. She fell down and did not get up. The mittens laughed. They had won.

Once upon a time, there was a beach. On this beach, there were many kids playing. They liked to play in the sand and the water. The beach was a fun place for everyone. One day, a weird bird came to the beach. It was big and had funny feathers. The kids saw the bird and thought it was funny. They laughed and pointed at the bird. The bird did not like being laughed at. It started to yell very loud. The kids were scared and ran away. The bird stopped yelling and flew away. The kids learned not to laugh at others, even if they looked weird. From that day on, they played nicely at the beach.

Once upon a time, there was a mommy and a baby. The baby wanted to go outside to play, but the mommy wasn't sure that was a good idea. "But, Mommy, I want to go outside!" the baby said. The Mommy replied, "Oh, I don't know. It's so west out there." The baby began to pout. Suddenly, the Mommy smiled and said, "I know! Let's go outside and joke around!" The baby was very excited. So, they both put on their jackets and went outside. The baby laughed and had lots of fun joking around in the west. But then a terrible wind started blowing and it started to rain. The baby and mommy looked at each other and quickly ran back inside. The baby said, "That was a funny joke from the wind and the rain!" The Mommy smiled and said, "Sometimes the jokes nature tells can be pretty terrible!" The baby and the Mommy laughed, and then went inside to have some warm cocoa.

One day, a shy cat named Tom came to a new home. Tom was scared and hid under a big, soft rug. He felt safe there. A little girl named Sue found Tom under the rug. Sue said, "Don't be scared, Tom. I promise to be your friend and take care of you." Tom looked at Sue and felt a little better. Sue and Tom played all day. They ran and jumped and had fun. At night, they sat on the rug and Sue gave Tom a hug. Tom was happy and not shy anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to joke and play with his toys. One day, Tim saw a big bug on the floor. He was scared, but he had an idea. "Hey, bug!" Tim said, "Why did the chicken cross the road?" The bug did not move. Tim thought the bug was dead. So, he went to find his mom. "Mom, there's a dead bug on the floor!" Tim told her. "I tried to joke with it, but it didn't laugh." His mom smiled and went to see the bug. She saw the bug and told Tim, "It's not dead, Tim. It's just sleeping." Tim felt better knowing the bug was not dead. The next day, the bug was gone. Tim knew his joke had made the bug happy and it went on its way. From that day on, Tim always tried to make others happy with his jokes.
