Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Dog

Lily and Ben were playing in the park with their dog, Spot. They liked to run and jump and throw a ball for Spot to catch. Spot was a good dog and loved to play with them. One day, a big and mean dog came to the park. He saw Spot and wanted to fight him. He ran towards Spot and barked loudly. Spot was scared and tried to run away, but the big dog was faster and caught him. He bit Spot's neck and made him cry. Lily and Ben saw what was happening and ran to help Spot. They shouted at the big dog and threw sticks and stones at him. But the big dog was not afraid and kept biting Spot. Spot was helpless and in pain. Then, Lily had an idea. She saw a tie on the ground. It belonged to a man who was sitting on a bench and reading a book. Lily grabbed the tie and ran to the big dog. She wrapped the tie around his mouth and pulled hard. The big dog could not bite anymore and let go of Spot. He tried to shake off the tie, but Lily held on tight. "Go away, you bad dog!" Lily said. "You can't hurt Spot anymore. He is our friend and we will protect him." The big dog gave up and ran away. Lily and Ben hugged Spot and checked if he was okay. He had some scratches and blood, but he was alive and happy. He licked their faces and wagged his tail. The man on the bench saw what happened and came to them. He was not angry that Lily took his tie. He was proud of her and thanked her for saving Spot. He gave them some water and a bandage for Spot's wound. He said they were brave and smart kids. Lily and Ben smiled and thanked the man. They said they were sorry for his tie, but he said it was not important. He said he had another tie at home and he did not need this one. He said they could keep it as a souvenir of their adventure. Lily and Ben were happy and proud. They put the tie around Spot's neck like a collar. They said it was a special tie that showed how much they loved him and how they would always protect him. Spot barked and agreed. They went home together, holding hands and laughing. They had a new story to tell their parents and a new friend in the park.

One day, a funny cat named Tom had a plan. He wanted to find something pink. Tom liked pink a lot. It was his favorite color. He thought it would be fun to look for pink things. Tom went outside and started to look around. He saw a pink ball near a big tree. He was so happy! But when he got closer, he saw that it was not a pink ball. It was a big pink bird! The bird was sleeping and looked so funny. Tom wanted to be friends with the pink bird. He tried to wake the bird up. But when the bird woke up, it was not a bird at all! It was a funny pink balloon with a face on it. Tom laughed and played with the pink balloon all day. The plan to find something pink turned into a fun day with a new pink friend.

Once upon a time, a large train named Tim was on his way to the town. He had to go through a tunnel to get there. Tim was very excited, but he was also a little scared of the dark tunnel. As Tim got closer to the tunnel, he saw his friend, a small bird named Bella. Bella could tell that Tim was scared. She said, "Tim, don't be scared. I will go with you through the tunnel." Tim was happy to have a friend by his side. Together, they entered the tunnel. It was dark, but Bella sang a happy song to make Tim feel better. They delayed a little in the middle of the tunnel to listen to Bella's song. Soon, they came out of the tunnel and saw the bright sun. Tim was not scared anymore, and he was very thankful to Bella for helping him. They continued their journey to the town, happy and brave.
