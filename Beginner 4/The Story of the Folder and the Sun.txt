Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Folder and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a big, thick folder. It had lots of pretty pictures inside. One day, the folder was left outside in the rain. When the rain stopped, the folder was wet and sad. A little girl named Mia found the folder. She said, "Oh no, you are all wet! I will help you." She took the folder home and put it in the sun to dry. The folder felt happy and warm. As the folder dried, it started to shrink. It became smaller and smaller. Mia saw the folder and said, "Wow, you are so small now! Let's show my friends." Mia and the folder had a fun day with her friends. They all loved the little folder, and it was happy to have a new home.

Lily and Ben were playing with their blocks. They liked to build towers and houses and bridges. Lily had a lot of purple blocks. She liked purple. It was her favorite color. "Look, Ben, I made a purple castle!" Lily said. She showed Ben her blocks. They were stacked high and had windows and doors. "That's nice, Lily, but I want some purple blocks too," Ben said. He reached for some of Lily's blocks. He wanted to make his own castle. "No, Ben, these are mine!" Lily said. She pulled the blocks away from Ben. She was stubborn. She did not like to share. "Please, Lily, just give me a few," Ben said. He tried to grab the blocks again. He was stubborn too. They both pulled and tugged on the blocks. They did not see that their blocks were about to fall. They did not hear their mom coming. "Crash!" The blocks fell down. They made a loud noise. They scattered on the floor. "What's going on here?" Mom said. She saw Lily and Ben and the blocks. She was not happy. "Lily and Ben, you need to share your toys. And you need to clean up this mess," Mom said. "But, Mom, I made a purple castle and Ben wanted to take my blocks," Lily said. "But, Mom, Lily had all the purple blocks and she did not let me have any," Ben said. Mom sighed. She knew they were both stubborn. She had an idea. "Why don't you work together and make a bigger castle? You can use purple blocks and other colors too. It will be more fun and more beautiful," Mom said. Lily and Ben looked at each other. They thought about it. They nodded. "Okay, Mom, we will try," they said. They picked up the blocks and started to build. They set the blocks on top of each other. They made a big castle. It had purple blocks and other colors too. It was more fun and more beautiful. They smiled at each other. They were not stubborn anymore. They were happy.

One day, a little penguin was playing outside. The penguin saw a big, rare bird. The rare bird was not nice. It said, "Go away, penguin! This is my home." The penguin was sad. It said, "But I want to play here too." The rare bird did not want to share. The penguin was very sad and did not know what to do. Then, a big, nice bear came. The bear said to the rare bird, "You need to share with the penguin." The rare bird listened to the bear. The penguin and the rare bird played together. They were happy and had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a busy ant. The ant liked to touch things and change them. One day, the ant saw a big rock. He wanted to touch it and change it into something fun. The ant touched the rock and it changed into a big cake. The ant was very happy. He called his friends to come and see the cake. All the ants came and they ate the cake together. But then, something unexpected happened. The cake changed back into a rock! The ants were surprised and a little sad. But they learned that sometimes, things can change in a way we don't expect. They still had fun together and went back to their busy ant lives.
