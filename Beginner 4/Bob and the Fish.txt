Category: Beginner
Title: Bob and the Fish

Once upon a time, a happy boat named Bob lived in a port. Bob loved to collect shells from the sea. One day, while Bob was out collecting shells, he saw a big fish. The big fish was sad. "Hi, big fish! Why are you sad?" asked Bob. The big fish said, "I lost my friend, little fish. Can you help me find her?" Bob wanted to help, so he said, "Yes, let's go look for little fish together." Bob and the big fish looked all around the sea. Then, something unexpected happened. They found a big pile of shells. And there, hiding in the shells, was little fish! Big fish was so happy to see little fish, and they both thanked Bob for his help. From that day on, they all became the best of friends and collected shells together.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had a tiny flea friend named Flick. Flick lived on Spot's back and they played together all day. One day, Spot and Flick saw a big puzzle. They wanted to complete it, but they felt helpless because it was too big for them. Spot had an idea. He asked his friends, Bird and Cat, to help them. Bird, Cat, Spot, and Flick worked together to complete the puzzle. They were very happy when they finished it. From that day on, they knew they could do anything if they worked together.

Lily likes to play soccer with her big brother Sam. They have a ball and two goals in the backyard. Lily tries to kick the ball into Sam's goal, but Sam is faster and stronger. He kicks the ball into Lily's goal and says, "I win!" Lily feels sad and angry. She does not like to lose. She kicks the ball very hard and it flies over the fence. She hears a loud noise and a dog barking. She thinks the ball hit the dog. She is scared of the dog. She is anxious. Sam sees Lily's face and feels sorry. He says, "Don't worry, Lily. The dog is nice. He won't hurt you or the ball. Come on, let's go get it." He holds Lily's hand and they walk to the fence. They see the dog wagging his tail and playing with the ball. He is not mad. He is happy. Lily smiles and says, "Hi, dog. Can we have our ball back?" The dog drops the ball and licks Lily's face. Lily giggles and says, "Thank you, dog. You are a good dog." She picks up the ball and throws it to Sam. They say bye to the dog and go back to their backyard. They play soccer again, but this time they take turns kicking the ball. They have fun and laugh. When they are tired, they go inside and eat rice and chicken with their mom and dad. They tell them about the dog and the ball. Mom and dad are proud of them for being brave and kind. Lily and Sam are happy. They hug each other and say, "I love you."

Lila and Anna are best friends. They like to play with beads and make bracelets. They have many colors of beads: red, blue, yellow, green, pink, and purple. They sit on the floor and string the beads on a thin rope. "I like your bracelet, Anna. It is very light and shiny," Lila says. She looks at her own bracelet. It is almost done. She only needs one more bead. "Thank you, Lila. I like yours too. It is very pretty and colorful," Anna says. She sees that Lila is looking for the last bead. She has an idea. She picks up a special bead from her box. It is a heart-shaped bead. It is silver and sparkles in the sun. "Here, Lila. You can have this bead. It is for you," Anna says. She gives the bead to Lila. "Wow, Anna. Thank you so much. This bead is beautiful. It is perfect for my bracelet," Lila says. She smiles and hugs Anna. She puts the bead on her rope and ties a knot. She finishes her bracelet. "Look, Anna. We both have light and shiny bracelets. They are like stars on our wrists," Lila says. She holds up her arm and admires her bracelet. "Yes, Lila. They are like stars. And we are like sisters. We share everything," Anna says. She holds up her arm and touches her bracelet. They giggle and clap their hands. They are happy. They love their bracelets. And they love each other.
