Category: Beginner
Title: Sam and the Dad

Sam liked to play with his dad's mustache. He thought it was funny and soft. Sometimes, he would rub it with his fingers and make noises. His dad would laugh and tickle him. One day, Sam saw a pair of scissors on the table. He had an idea. He wanted to make his dad's mustache look different. He grabbed the scissors and ran to his dad, who was sleeping on the couch. "Dad, dad, wake up!" Sam shouted. He held the scissors near his dad's face. His dad opened his eyes and saw the scissors. He was scared and angry. He grabbed Sam's hand and took the scissors away. "Sam, what are you doing? You could have hurt me or yourself! Scissors are not toys! You can't cut my mustache!" his dad yelled. Sam felt bad. He did not mean to make his dad mad. He just wanted to have fun. "I'm sorry, dad. I wanted to make your mustache look like a star. Or a snake. Or a rainbow. Your mustache is dull. It needs some color," Sam said. His dad sighed. He hugged Sam and kissed his head. "I love you, Sam. But you can't cut my mustache. It's part of me. And it's not dull. It's gray. Gray is a nice color. It means I'm wise and old. And it matches your mom's hair," his dad said. Sam looked at his mom, who was smiling. She had gray hair too. He thought they looked nice together. "Okay, dad. I won't cut your mustache. But can I still rub it?" Sam asked. His dad smiled. He nodded and let Sam rub his mustache. "Sure, Sam. You can still rub it. But only gently. And only when I'm awake," his dad said.

One day, a cat and a dog were friends. They liked to play and walk together. They found a big door. The door was old and filthy. The cat said, "Let's open the door and see what is inside." The dog was scared. He said, "No, the door is filthy. We should not go in." But the cat wanted to see what was inside the door. So, the cat opened the door and went inside. The dog waited outside. He was worried for his friend. After a while, the cat came out. He was happy. He said, "I found a nice toy inside! Let's play together!" The dog was glad that his friend was safe, and they played together.

Once there was a little girl named Lily. She was three years old and had the most graceful way of walking. Every day Lily's parent would take her to the park. She loved going to the park and playing on the swings and slide. One day, Lily saw a mommy duck and her ducklings walking across the grass. She thought they were so graceful. She wanted to be just like them. So, Lily asked her parent if they could help her gain gracefulness, just like the ducks. Her parent said yes, and together they started practicing. Everyday, Lily's parent would watch her walk around with the gracefulness that only comes with time and practice. In no time at all, Lily was able to walk with the same gracefulness as the ducks. She had gained gracefulness. Lily was very proud of herself and she loved the feeling of being graceful. From that day on, Lily felt extra graceful when she and her parent went to the park.

Anna and Ben were playing in the garden. They saw a big tree with red apples. They wanted to pick some apples to eat. "Look, Ben, there is an enormous apple up there!" Anna said, pointing to the top of the tree. "I want to have it!" "How can we get it?" Ben asked. "It is too high for us." Anna had an idea. She saw a long stick on the ground. She picked it up and said, "I can raise the stick and hit the apple. Maybe it will fall down." She tried to raise the stick, but it was too heavy for her. She could not lift it high enough. "Let me help you, Anna," Ben said. He grabbed the other end of the stick and helped Anna raise it. Together, they swung the stick at the enormous apple. They hit the apple, and it fell down with a thud. It rolled to their feet. "Yay, we did it!" Anna and Ben cheered. They picked up the apple and shared it. It was juicy and sweet. They smiled at each other and ate the apple. They were happy.
