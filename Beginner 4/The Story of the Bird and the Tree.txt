Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Bird and the Tree

One day, a little bird with a gentle wing was looking for a place to seat. It flew around and saw a big tree. The tree had many branches and leaves. The bird thought, "This is a good place for me to seat." The bird flew to the tree and seated on a branch. It looked around and saw a little girl. The girl was sitting on the grass under the tree. The bird said, "Hi, little girl! I am a bird with a gentle wing. Can I seat here in your tree?" The little girl looked up and smiled. She said, "Yes, bird with a gentle wing, you can seat here. I like to watch birds in the tree." The bird was happy and sang a song for the girl. They became good friends and played together every day.

One day, a girl named Sue found a flute. She wanted to play it, so she put it to her lips. She blew into it and made a wide sound. It was very loud. Sue was happy to play the flute. Sue walked to her friend Tim's house. She showed him the flute. "Look, Tim! I can play this flute," Sue said. Tim was excited. He wanted to try too. Sue let Tim put the flute to his lips. He blew into it, but the sound was not wide. It was very small. Tim was sad. He wanted to play the flute like Sue. Sue had an idea. She told Tim to blow harder. Tim tried again. This time, when he blew into the flute, a big, wide sound came out. But something unexpected happened! A big, friendly bear came out of the woods, dancing to the music. Tim and Sue laughed and played the flute together, making the bear dance. They had a fun day with their new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was very brave. One day, she went for a walk in the woods with her mom. They saw a big, tall tree. Mia wanted to climb it. "Mom, can I climb the tree?" Mia asked. Her mom looked at the tree and said, "Yes, but be careful." Mia started to climb the tree. She put her hands on the branches and her feet on the trunk. She went higher and higher. Suddenly, Mia felt something on her shoulder. It was a little bird. The bird said, "Hello, Mia! You are brave to climb so high!" Mia smiled and said, "Thank you, little bird. I like to be brave." The bird flew away, and Mia climbed back down. Mia and her mom walked back home, talking about their adventure. Mia was proud to be brave and couldn't wait to tell her friends about the little bird on her shoulder.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was hungry and wanted to eat. His mom gave him a big apple from a tree. Tim was happy and took the apple to eat. While he was eating, a little bird came and said, "Can I have a small piece of your apple? I am very hungry too." Tim was kind and gave the bird a piece of the apple. The bird was happy and said, "Thank you, Tim. I will return the favor one day." Days went by, and Tim learned about history in school. He wanted to see a real dinosaur. One day, the bird came back and said, "I told you I would return the favor. Hop on my back, and I will take you to see a dinosaur." Tim and the bird went on a magical trip and saw a dinosaur from far away. Tim was so happy and thanked the bird again. They returned home safely. The moral of the story is to be kind and share with others, and good things will come back to you.

One day there was a tall ranch. On the ranch, a boy named Jack liked to stir a pot. Every day he stirred, stirred, and stirred. One morning, Jack heard a noise outside. He looked and saw a huge tall horse. The horse was white. Jack was so excited. He wanted to take the horse for a ride. So Jack grabbed a carrot, and waved it in the air. The tall horse walked closer. Jack jumped on its back. Then, they began to stir. Jack and the tall horse stirred around the ranch. They went higher and higher and it was so much fun. When they had finished stirring, Jack climbed down from the horse and said goodbye. The tall horse whinnied, and trotted away. Soon after, Jack went back to stirring his pot. But now, he was thinking about his tall horse and the fun he had stirring around the ranch.
