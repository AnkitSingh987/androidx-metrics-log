Category: Beginner
Title: The Fish and the Orange

One day, a little fish lived in the big sea. The fish was orange. He had many friends. They liked to play and swim together. But one day, dark clouds came. It started to rain. The little fish was scared. The rain made the sea go up and down. The little fish and his friends got lost. They could not find their way home. The orange fish was very sad. But then, he saw a big rock. He had an idea. He told his friends to follow him. The little fish and his friends swam to the big rock. They climbed up and waited for the rain to stop. When the rain stopped, they could see their home. They swam back, happy and safe. The little fish was a hero. The moral of the story is to be brave and help your friends.

Sara liked to do yoga with her mom. Yoga was when they moved their bodies in different ways and breathed slowly. Sara felt calm and happy when she did yoga. She had a pink mat that she put on the floor to do yoga on. The mat was hers and she loved it. One day, Sara's mom had a friend over. Her name was Mia and she also liked to do yoga. Sara's mom said they could all do yoga together. Sara was excited, but also a little fearful. She did not know Mia very well and she did not want to share her mat. They went to the living room and Sara's mom and Mia put their mats on the floor. Sara ran to get her mat, but Mia stopped her. "Can I use your mat, Sara?" Mia asked. "It's so pretty and soft. I forgot to bring mine." Sara felt a knot in her stomach. She did not want to give her mat to Mia. It was hers and it belonged to her. She shook her head and said, "No, it's mine. You can't have it." Mia looked sad and said, "Please, Sara. I'll be careful with it. We can share it. It's just for a little while." Sara felt torn. She wanted to be nice to Mia, but she also wanted to keep her mat. She looked at her mom, who smiled and nodded. Sara's mom said, "Sara, it's okay to share your mat with Mia. She's our guest and she likes yoga too. Sharing is caring. You can still use your mat after she's done." Sara thought about it. She did not want to make Mia sad or her mom mad. She decided to share her mat with Mia. She said, "Okay, you can use it. But be gentle with it. It's my favorite." Mia smiled and said, "Thank you, Sara. You're very kind. I'll be gentle with it. It's a beautiful mat." Sara gave her mat to Mia and watched her do yoga on it. She felt a little sad, but also a little proud. She had shared her mat with Mia and made her happy. She had listened to her mom and been caring. She had done something hard and been brave. She realized that sharing her mat did not mean losing it. It still belonged to her, but she could also let others enjoy it. She felt calm and happy again. She joined her mom and Mia on the floor and did yoga with them. She had fun and learned some new moves. She made a new friend. She was glad she had shared her mat.

One day, a little boy named Tim was watching his favorite cartoon on TV. He loved the funny animals and the silly things they did. But today, the cartoon had a bad character who made the other animals worry. Tim felt sad seeing his cartoon friends worry and wanted to help them. Later, Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a group of kids playing and laughing. Tim thought about the cartoon and said to his mom, "Mom, I don't want my friends to worry like the animals in the cartoon. What can I do to help?" His mom smiled and said, "Just be a good friend and always be kind to others. That will help them not worry." The next day, Tim played with his friends at the park. He remembered what his mom said and was very kind to everyone. He saw that his friends were happy and not worrying, just like the animals in the cartoon after the bad character went away. Tim was happy too, knowing that being kind made everyone feel good.
