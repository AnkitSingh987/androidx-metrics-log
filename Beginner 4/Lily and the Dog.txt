Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Dog

Lily loved her pig. His name was Oink and he was pink and soft. He liked to eat apples and play in the mud. Lily hugged Oink every day and told him stories. One day, a big dog came to the farm. He was terrible. He barked and growled and chased the chickens. He saw Oink and wanted to bite him. Oink was scared and ran away. Lily saw the dog and shouted, "No! Leave Oink alone!" Lily ran after the dog and threw a stick at him. The dog was surprised and dropped Oink. Oink ran back to Lily and she hugged him. The dog ran away and did not come back. Lily and Oink were safe and happy. Lily's mom and dad came and saw what happened. They were proud of Lily and Oink. They gave them both a big hug and a treat. They said, "You are brave and smart. We love you and your pig." Lily smiled and hugged them back. She said, "I love you and Oink too."

Once upon a time, there was a big farm. On the farm, there was a high hill. On top of the hill, there was a lot of hay. The animals on the farm loved to play on the hay. One day, a little cow and a little pig were playing on the hay. They were having so much fun. But then, the cow pushed the pig too hard. The pig fell down the hill and got hurt. The pig was very sad and angry. The cow felt bad for hurting the pig. The cow said, "I am sorry for pushing you. Please forgive me." The pig thought about it and said, "I forgive you. Let's be careful when we play next time." The cow and the pig became friends again and played on the hay all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was very intelligent. Lily had a best friend named Teddy. Teddy was a soft and cuddly bear. They played together every day. One day, while they were playing, Teddy got a cut. Lily was sad and wanted to help him. She hugged Teddy tight to comfort him. She knew she had to do something to make him feel better. Then, something unexpected happened. Teddy began to talk! He said, "Thank you, Lily, for being so kind and smart. Your hug made my cut feel better!" Lily was so surprised but happy that her friend Teddy could talk. From that day on, they could talk and play together even more.

The sun was shining as little Jake skipped outside. He looked around and saw his mom on the porch. He ran to her and asked for a lollipop. "Well, Jake," said his mom, "That's not very fair. You have had lollipops all week!" Little Jake thought hard and then put on a big smile. He said, "But mom, I can thank you lots and lots". His mom laughed and said "Alright then, one lollipop coming right up!". Jake thanked his mom as she handed him the lollipop. He ran off to play in the garden while enjoying his sweet treat. Jake was happy, and his mom was happy too. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his dog, Spot. They would run, jump, and have fun all day. One day, a big truck arrived at Tim's house. The truck had something special inside. It was a big box of beef. Tim's dad opened the box and took out the beef. The beef was rare and looked very yummy. Tim's mom cooked the rare beef for dinner. Tim, his mom, his dad, and Spot all sat down to eat the yummy beef. They all enjoyed the rare beef and had a great time together. After dinner, Tim and Spot went outside to play again. They were very happy.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to play outside. She saw a big door. It was old and looked dead. Sue wanted to know what was behind the door. She went to ask her mom. "Mom, can I open the door?" Sue asked. Her mom said, "No, Sue. It's not safe. You must stay away from it." Sue was sad but she listened to her mom. She went to play with her ball. She kicked the ball very hard. The ball went far away and hit the door. The door opened a little bit. Sue was scared but she wanted to see what was inside. She went close to the door and peeked. She saw a beautiful garden with many flowers. She was happy to see it. Sue ran to her mom and told her about the garden. Her mom smiled and said, "I knew it was there. I wanted it to be a surprise for you." They went to the garden and played together.
