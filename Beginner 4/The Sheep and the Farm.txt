Title: The Sheep and the Farm

Once upon a time, there was a playful sheep. The sheep loved to run, jump, and play all day. The sheep had many friends, like the cow, the pig, and the horse. They all lived on a big farm. One day, the sheep found a hole in the fence. The sheep went through the hole and found a new place to play. The sheep played all day, but soon it was time to go home. The sheep went back through the hole to the farm. The farmer saw the hole in the fence and knew the sheep had gone through it. The farmer wanted to keep the sheep safe, so he replaced the broken fence with a new one. The playful sheep was happy to be back with its friends on the farm. They all played together and lived happily ever after.

One day, a small boy named Tim saw a big tree. The tree was powerful and strong. Tim wanted to gain a friend, so he said, "Hello, tree! Can you be my friend?" The tree did not say anything. But a bird in the tree said, "Hello, Tim! I can be your friend!" Tim was happy to have a new friend. Together, Tim and the bird played near the powerful tree. They ran, they jumped, and they laughed. The tree watched them play and felt happy too. The bird and Tim gained a powerful friendship that lasted a long time.

Once upon a time there was a big tree in the mine. Little Ben was playing near the tree. He was having lots of fun. "Come on Ben," shouted Dad. "Let's go to the picnic area and have a delicious lunch." Little Ben jumped with joy and ran to the picnic area. When they arrived Dad set the table and they sat down to eat. "Hmm, this looks delicious," said Ben as he stared wide-eyed at the food. Dad nodded and said, "Let's see if you can balance the food on your fork." And so Ben tried his best to balance the food. He giggled with delight when he succeeded. What a happy day it had been.

Once upon a time, there was a rich man. He had a lot of money. One day, he saw a poor man with bad skin. The poor man was sad. The rich man wanted to help the poor man. He thought and thought. Then, he had an idea. He would pray for the poor man's skin to get better. The rich man prayed very hard. Then, something unexpected happened. The poor man's skin got better, but the rich man's skin got bad. The rich man was sad, but the poor man was happy. In the end, they both learned that helping others can sometimes help you too.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a famous cafe. People loved to go there to eat. The cafe had a big menu with many yummy things to eat. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the famous cafe with his mom. Tim was very happy to go there. He looked at the menu and picked a big ice cream to eat. His mom said yes, and they waited for the ice cream. But then, something bad happened. A big wind came and blew the menu away. The menu went far away, and nobody could find it. Tim and his mom could not eat the ice cream. They were sad and went back home.

One day, a big, hot sun was shining in the sky. A little girl named Mia wanted to play outside. She saw her friend, Tom, sitting on a mattress under a tree. The mattress was very soft and nice. "Hi Tom!" said Mia. "Can I sit on the mattress too?" Tom smiled and said, "Yes, come sit with me. The mattress will support both of us." Mia sat down and they both felt the mattress support them. They talked and laughed under the tree. The hot sun did not bother them because the tree gave them shade. They played on the mattress all day, having lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog. His name was Spot. Spot was a very happy dog. He liked to play with his ball and run in the park. One day, Spot saw a big red balloon. He wanted to play with it. Spot jumped up and grabbed the balloon with his mouth. But something unexpected happened! When Spot grabbed the balloon, it started to fly away. Spot held on tight and did not let go. He did not want to release the balloon. The balloon took Spot high up in the sky. Spot was scared. Then, a kind bird saw Spot and wanted to help. The bird flew up to Spot and said, "Let the balloon go. I will catch you." Spot was embarrassed, but he listened to the bird. He released the balloon and the bird caught him. They both landed safely on the ground. Spot was happy to be back on the ground and thanked the bird for helping him. From that day on, Spot and the bird were the best of friends.
