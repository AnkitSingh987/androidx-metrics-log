Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys and make new friends. One day, Tim went to the park to play. He saw a big statue in the park. The statue was of a man who used to work very hard for the town. Tim met a new friend named Sam. Sam was a bossy boy. He always told Tim what to do. Tim did not like it, but he wanted to be friends with Sam. They played together near the statue. Sam told Tim to climb the statue, but Tim was scared. Tim thought about the man who used to work hard and help others. He decided to be brave like the man in the statue. Tim told Sam that it was not nice to be bossy. Sam listened to Tim and said sorry. They both played happily and learned to be kind to each other. The moral of the story is to treat others with kindness and stand up for what is right.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to look for stones of different shapes and colors. Lily found a shiny stone that was round and smooth. She admired it and put it in her pocket. "Look, Ben, I found a pretty stone!" she said. Ben looked at her stone and wanted it. He thought it was better than his stones. He tried to grab it from her pocket. "Give me your stone, Lily!" he said. "No, Ben, it's mine! I found it first!" Lily said. She held her pocket tight. They started to pull and push each other. They were angry and sad. A big dog came by and saw them. The dog was friendly and graceful. It wagged its tail and licked their faces. It made them laugh and forget their fight. "Hi, doggy, you are so nice!" Lily said. "Can we pet you, doggy?" Ben said. The dog nodded and let them pet him. They felt happy and calm. "Sorry, Lily, I was mean to you," Ben said. "Sorry, Ben, I was mean to you too," Lily said. They hugged and shared their stones. They thanked the dog for helping them. They played with the dog and their stones until it was time to go home.

Every day he would take a different route, until one day he found a bridge that he had not seen before. “What a nice bridge,” he thought, and he decided to stop and have a look. As he crossed the bridge, he could hear a voice calling his name. He looked around but he could not find anyone. “Who calls me?” he thought. He decided to keep walking to the other side of the bridge and find out. When he reached the other side of the bridge, he saw a child. It was a small girl with big, brown eyes. She had a big smile on her face and she was holding up a red balloon. Bob happily asked her name, “What’s your name?” “Alice,” she said. Bob smiled and said, “Alice, why did you call my name?” She replied, “I knew you were coming! I wanted you to have a special present. Here, catch!” She threw him the red balloon, which he caught easily. Bob was so happy that he almost started to cry. He thanked Alice and kept walking. Lily and Ben were playing in the kitchen. They liked to pretend they were chefs and make yummy food. They found a big pot and some spoons and cups. They filled the pot with water and put it on the stove. "Let's make soup!" Lily said. "We need some salt to make it tasty." Ben nodded and looked for the salt. He saw a blue jar on the counter. He reached for it and opened it. He saw white crystals inside. He thought it was salt. He poured some into the pot. "Turn on the stove, Lily!" Ben said. "Then our soup will be hot and ready." Lily did what Ben said. She turned the knob on the stove. She heard a click and saw a flame. She smiled and clapped her hands. "Good job, Lily!" Ben said. "Now we wait for our soup." They waited and waited. They smelled something funny. They saw smoke coming from the pot. They heard a loud hiss. "What's that?" Lily asked. She was scared. "I don't know!" Ben said. He was scared too. They ran to the pot and looked inside. They saw bubbles and foam. They saw the water was gone. They saw the white crystals were not salt. They were soap. "Oh no!" Lily and Ben said. "We used the wrong jar! We made soap soup!" They were very sad. They had no soup to eat. They had an empty pot and a messy stove. They heard a voice. "What are you doing, kids?" It was Mom. She came into the kitchen. She saw the pot and the jar and the smoke. She was not happy. Lily and Ben looked at Mom. They felt sorry. They said together: "Sorry, Mom. We made a mistake."
