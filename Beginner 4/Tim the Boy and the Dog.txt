Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Dog

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to play. He had to choose a game. He looked at his toys and saw a hockey set. Tim thought playing hockey would be fun and healthy. So, he took the hockey set outside to play. As Tim played, a dog came to watch. The dog wagged its tail and barked. Tim thought the dog wanted to play too. So, he threw the hockey ball for the dog to catch. The dog ran fast and caught the ball in its mouth. But then, something unexpected happened! The dog did not bring the ball back to Tim. Instead, the dog ran away with the ball. Tim was sad, but then he saw the dog come back. The dog had a new ball! The dog dropped the ball and barked. Tim smiled and they played together. The dog was a good friend to play hockey with.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a small toy car that he loved to play with. One day, Tim's friend, Lily, came over to play. They wanted to see who could make their toy car go the farthest. "Let's have a race!" said Tim. "My car will go farther than yours." "No way! My car is faster," replied Lily. They both pushed their cars as hard as they could. Tim's car went far, but Lily's car went even farther. Tim was sad because he lost the race. He wanted to increase the speed of his car. Tim and Lily started to fight about whose car was better. They were both very upset. Tim's mom heard them fighting and came into the room. "Stop fighting, kids," she said. "It's just a game. You can play together and help each other make your cars go faster." Tim and Lily listened to Tim's mom. They stopped fighting and started to share ideas on how to make their cars go faster. They both learned new ways to increase the speed of their cars, and they had a lot of fun playing together. In the end, Tim and Lily realized that playing together was more fun than fighting. They promised to help each other and not fight anymore. And from that day on, their friendship grew stronger, and they had many more fun days playing with their toy cars.

Once upon a time, there was a famous cat named Kitty. Kitty was very good at singing. One day, Kitty wanted to sing in the park. She asked her friend, Doggy, to come with her. Doggy and Kitty went to the park. Kitty started to sing. Many people came to listen. Doggy had an idea. He suggested, "Kitty, let's sing together!" Kitty liked the idea. They started to sing together. People loved it even more. Kitty and Doggy learned that when they worked together, they made people happy. They became famous friends. The moral of the story is to work together and help each other.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They wanted to buy a new toy for Tim. Tim saw a big, cheap toy car. He loved it so much. He said, "Mom, can I have this car, please?" His mom looked at the car and said, "Yes, Tim. It is cheap, so we can buy it." Tim was very happy. He held the car tight and stepped out of the store with his mom. As they walked home, Tim tripped on a rock and fell down. His nose hurt a little. Tim's mom helped him up and said, "Be careful when you step, my dear. Watch where you are going." Tim said, "I will, Mom," and held her hand. They walked home together, Tim holding his new cheap toy car and his mom's hand. His nose felt better, and he couldn't wait to play with his new car.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her toy square. One day, Sue's mom made a spicy pie. She told Sue, "Be careful, the pie is spicy." Sue went outside to play with her toy square. She threw it high up in the air. The square would rise and then fall back down. Sue would catch it and laugh. She did this again and again. As Sue played, she smelled the spicy pie from inside the house. She remembered her mom's words. Sue went inside and looked at the pie. She thought about taking a bite, but she did not. Sue knew she should listen to her mom. Later, when her mom saw that Sue did not eat the spicy pie, she was very proud. Sue and her mom shared a big hug.
