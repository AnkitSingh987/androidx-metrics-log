Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Toy

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big heart and loved to give hugs. One day, Lily saw a sad, broken toy on the ground. She felt bad for the toy and wanted to help. Lily picked up the toy and gave it a big hug. She hoped her hug would fix it. Suddenly, something unexpected happened! The toy started to glow and magically came to life. It was a special toy that could talk and play. The toy was so happy that Lily had given it a hug. It thanked her for fixing its broken heart. From that day on, Lily and the toy became the best of friends. They played together every day and gave each other lots of hugs. And they both had very happy hearts.

One day, a funny cat named Tom found an envelope. He loved to joke and play. He thought the envelope was for him. He wanted to show it to his best friend, a dog named Max. Tom and Max met under a big tree. Tom showed Max the envelope. Max was very happy. They opened the envelope together. Inside, there was a note. It said, "Do not be rude." Tom and Max did not understand. They were never rude. They liked to joke, but they were always nice. They decided to ask their friend, a bird named Sue. Sue was very wise. She read the note and laughed. The note was not for Tom and Max. It was for a rude fox who lived in the woods. Tom and Max laughed too. They played a joke on the fox by putting the note on his door. The fox learned to be nice, and they all became friends.

---

Sara and Tom were playing hide and seek in the park. Sara was the seeker and Tom was the hider. Tom ran behind a big bush and waited for Sara to find him. He was very quiet and still. Sara looked around the park and called out, "Tom, where are you? I can't see you!" She walked near the bush where Tom was hiding, but she did not look behind it. She went to another place and looked some more. Tom thought Sara would never find him. He felt bored and lonely. He wanted to play with Sara again. He decided to come out of the bush and surprise her. He jumped out and shouted, "Boo!" But Sara was not there. She had gone to the other side of the park. Tom did not see her. He felt scared and sad. He wondered where Sara had gone. He thought she had vanished. He started to cry and called out, "Sara, Sara, where are you? Please come back!" He hoped Sara would hear him and come back. Sara heard Tom's voice and ran back to him. She saw him crying and hugged him. She said, "Tom, I'm sorry. I did not know you were behind the bush. I did not vanish. I was looking for you." Tom stopped crying and smiled. He said, "Sara, I'm sorry too. I wanted to surprise you, but I scared myself. I'm glad you're here." Sara and Tom hugged again and said, "We're friends." They decided to play another game, but this time they stayed close to each other. They did not want to vanish again. They wanted to have fun.

One day, a sweet cat went for a walk. She saw a big house. She wanted to see who lived in the house. She walked up to the door and gave a little bow. Then, she knocked on the door. A kind dog opened the door. "Hello, sweet cat!" he said. "Do you want to come in?" The sweet cat said, "Yes, please!" She went inside the house. They played and had fun. But the cat saw a big, dark room. She felt scared. The sweet cat said, "What is in that big, dark room?" The kind dog said, "Don't go in there!" The sweet cat did not listen. She went into the dark room. The door closed behind her. She could not get out. The sweet cat should have listened to the kind dog.
