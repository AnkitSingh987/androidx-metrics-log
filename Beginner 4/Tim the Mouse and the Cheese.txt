Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Mouse and the Cheese

One day, a little mouse named Tim was very hungry. He went to look for some cheese. He walked and walked until he saw a big piece of cheese. The cheese was in a dark room. Tim was scared, but he wanted the cheese so much. As Tim got closer to the cheese, he saw something glow. "Hello," said a small glowing bug. "I am a glow bug. I can help you get the cheese. Don't be scared." Tim and the glow bug went to the cheese together. The glow bug made the room bright with its glow. Tim was not scared anymore. They ate the cheese and became good friends. From that day on, Tim and the glow bug played together and were never scared again.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the sea with his mom. They had a big black ball to play with. They were very happy. "Mom, let's raise the black ball high and throw it into the sea!" said Tim. So, they raised the ball and threw it. The ball went far, far away. But then, something unexpected happened. A big fish came and took the black ball in its mouth! Tim and his mom were very surprised. "Mom, the fish took our ball!" said Tim. His mom smiled and said, "Don't worry, Tim. The fish will give it back." And the big fish did! It swam back to them and gave the black ball back. Tim and his mom laughed and played with the big fish. It was a fun day at the sea.

Once upon a time, in a land far away, there was a fat prince. The fat prince was not happy because he could not reach his toys. He tried and tried, but he was too fat to reach them. One day, the fat prince met a wise old man. The old man told the prince to eat healthy food and play outside. The prince listened to the old man and started to eat good food like fruits and vegetables. He also played outside with his friends. As time went by, the prince became strong and healthy. He was not fat anymore. Now, he could reach his toys and play with them. The prince was very happy. The moral of the story is that eating healthy food and playing outside can make us strong, healthy, and happy.

Lily and Max were friends who liked to paint. They had many colors and brushes to make pretty pictures. One day, they found a big rock in the park. They decided to paint the rock together. Lily wanted to paint the rock blue, like the sky. Max wanted to paint the rock green, like the grass. They argued and pulled the rock from each other. The rock was too heavy and it fell on the ground. It broke into two pieces. "Oh no!" Lily cried. "We broke the rock! Now we can't paint it!" "It's your fault!" Max shouted. "You wanted to paint it blue! Blue is a silly color for a rock!" "No, it's your fault!" Lily yelled back. "You wanted to paint it green! Green is a boring color for a rock!" They were so angry that they stopped being friends. They took their colors and brushes and went home. The next day, they saw the rock again in the park. It was still broken, but someone had painted it. One piece was blue and the other piece was green. They also had flowers and stars and hearts on them. The rock looked very pretty. Lily and Max felt sorry for breaking the rock and fighting with each other. They realized that they both had good ideas and that they could share and cooperate. They also saw a note next to the rock. It said: "Dear Lily and Max, I saw your rock and I liked it. I wanted to make it more beautiful for you. I hope you like it too. I think blue and green are both nice colors for a rock. They remind me of the earth. You are lucky to have each other as friends. Friends are more important than rocks. Love, Mom" Lily and Max smiled and hugged each other. They thanked their mom for painting the rock and for teaching them a lesson. They learned that they should respect each other's opinions and that they should not fight over small things. They also learned that their mom loved them very much and that she was very clever and kind. They decided to paint more rocks together and to give them to their mom as a gift.
