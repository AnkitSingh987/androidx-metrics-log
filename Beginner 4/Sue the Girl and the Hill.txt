Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Hill

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to climb. She was very adventurous. Every day, she would find new things to climb. She would climb trees, walls, and even her bed. Sue was very happy when she climbed. One day, it was Tuesday. Sue wanted to climb something new. She looked around and saw a big hill. The hill was very tall, and Sue was excited. She put on her shoes and went outside to climb the hill. Sue climbed and climbed. She was very tired, but she did not stop. When she got to the top of the hill, she saw a beautiful view. Sue was very happy. She sat on the hill and looked at everything. Then, she went back home and told her mom about her adventurous day.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a box of crayons. He loved to draw with them. One day, he found a fake crayon in the box. It looked like a real crayon, but it was not. Tim showed the fake crayon to his friend, Sam. Sam looked at the crayon and nodded. He said, "Let's draw with it and see what happens." So, Tim and Sam started to draw with the fake crayon. But it did not work. It did not make any colors on the paper. Tim and Sam were sad. They wanted to draw with the fake crayon, but they could not. They tried to fix the crayon, but it stayed fake. In the end, they had to throw the crayon away. They wished it was a real crayon, but it was not. And that made them very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a clever little dog named Max. Max lived in a small house with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. They loved to play together every day. One day, Max and Lily were playing in the backyard when they heard a noise coming from the door. "What's that?" asked Lily. Max barked and ran to the door. He used his teeth to pull the door open. Behind the door was a big, friendly cat named Whiskers. "Hello, Max and Lily!" said Whiskers. "Can I play with you?" Max and Lily were so happy to have a new friend. They played together all day long, and had lots of fun. From that day on, Max, Lily, and Whiskers became the best of friends, and they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a big coat in her closet. She was very glad because it was a cold day outside. She put on the coat and went outside to play. As she played, she saw her friend Tom. Tom was cold, so Lucy said, "Tom, do you want to wear my coat? I can change and wear another one." Tom was happy and said, "Yes, please!" So, Lucy went inside and changed her coat. When she came back outside, Tom was warm and glad. They played together all day, sharing the coat when they felt cold. At the end of the day, they both said, "We are so glad we have coats to keep us warm!"

Once upon a time there was a red hook. It was on the beach and the waves were loud. One day the hook heard a noise, a murmuring noise. The hook listened carefully and could just make out a whisper from a starfish. The starfish said he was stuck underneath a rock. The hook felt sorry for the starfish. Quickly, it wriggled free from the sand and stretched out as far as it could. The hook rattled against the rock. He shook and shook until finally the rock moved and the starfish was free. He thanked the hook and swam away. The hook smiled and settled back in the sand. From then on, it was happy to always listen for calls for help.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his dad. Tim was very glad because he had a new kite. The kite was big and had many colors. Tim could not wait to fly the kite in the sky. Tim and his dad found a good spot to fly the kite. Tim's dad helped him lift the kite into the air. The wind blew the kite high in the sky. Tim held the string tight and watched the kite dance in the wind. Tim's dad said, "Wow, Tim! Your kite is flying so high!" Tim smiled and said, "I love my kite, Dad!" They both laughed and watched the kite fly until it was time to go home.
