Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to play with her toys and enjoy the sunny days. One day, Sue was playing with her toy ambulance. She liked to pretend she was helping people in an emergency. One day, Sue's toy ambulance got stuck under the big chair. She tried to get it out, but it was not comfortable. She was sad and didn't know what to do. Then, her big brother Tom came into the room. He saw Sue was sad and asked her what was wrong. Sue told Tom about the toy ambulance stuck under the chair. Tom was big and strong, so he moved the chair and got the toy ambulance out. Sue was so happy! She thanked Tom and they both played with the toy ambulance together. They helped people in emergencies and enjoyed the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, there was a dirty fox. The fox liked to play in the dirt all day. He had lots of fun and was very happy. One day, the dirty fox found a magic pond. He went in to take a bath. When he got out, he began to shrink! The fox was very scared. But then, something unexpected happened. The fox became a tiny, clean fox. He made new friends with the bugs in the dirt. They all played together, and the tiny fox was happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia liked to wear pretty dresses. She had a dress for each day of the week. One day, the sky was gloomy. It was dark and cloudy. Mia did not like gloomy days. She wanted to wear her pretty dress, but she was scared her dress would get wet. Mia's mom saw her sad face. She had an idea. She gave Mia a big, bright umbrella. Mia smiled and put on her pretty dress. She went outside with her umbrella. The gloomy day did not make her sad anymore.

One day, a little girl named Sara was playing with her toy drum. She loved to make her own rhythm as she played. Her mom was in the kitchen making a yummy cake for her. Sara went to her mom and said, "Mom, I want to show you my modern rhythm!" Her mom smiled and said, "Okay, let me lock the door first so we can dance together." Sara was very happy. As they started to dance, they heard a knock at the door. It was a little boy who was lost. Sara and her mom helped him find his way home. The boy said, "Thank you for helping me!" Sara learned that it is good to help others and share her rhythm with new friends.

Lily and Ben were playing with a board and some blocks. They liked to make towers and houses and cars with the blocks. But sometimes they fought over the blocks and the board. "Stop! That is my block!" Lily shouted, as she grabbed a blue block from Ben's hand. "No, it is mine! I saw it first!" Ben shouted back, as he pulled the block from Lily's hand. They both pulled and pulled, until the block broke in half. They both looked at the broken block and felt sad. "Look what you did! You broke the block!" Lily cried. "You did it too! You were too rough!" Ben cried. They both started to cry louder and louder. Their mom heard them and came to see what was wrong. "What is going on here? Why are you crying?" Mom asked. "Lily and Ben broke the block! They were fighting over it!" Mom saw the broken block and the board with the blocks. "Oh, I see. That is not nice. You should share the blocks and the board. Fighting over them will only make you unhappy and break things. Do you want to play nicely or not?" Mom asked. Lily and Ben looked at each other and felt sorry. They did not want to fight or break things. They wanted to play and have fun. "We are sorry, Mom. We want to play nicely." Lily and Ben said. "That is good. Here, I have some glue. We can fix the block together. And then you can play with the board and the blocks again. But remember to share and take turns. And if you have a problem, you can talk to me or each other. OK?" Mom said. "OK, Mom. Thank you." Lily and Ben said. They helped Mom glue the block back together. Then they played with the board and the blocks again. They shared and took turns and had fun. They did not fight or cry anymore. They learned that sharing is caring and that fighting is not fun.
