Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Anna Find the Park

Tom and Anna were friends. They liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big sign. The sign said: "Display of blueberries. Come and see." "What is a display?" Tom asked. "A display is when you show something nice or interesting," Anna said. "Like our drawings?" Tom said. "Yes, like our drawings. But this display is of blueberries. Blueberries are small and sweet and blue. They grow on bushes," Anna said. "Can we eat them?" Tom asked. "I don't know. Maybe we can ask," Anna said. They walked to the display. There were many blueberries on a table. Some were in baskets, some were in jars, some were in pies. A lady was standing next to the table. She smiled at Tom and Anna. "Hello, children. Do you like blueberries?" she asked. "Yes, we do. They look yummy," Anna said. "Can we have some?" Tom asked. "Of course, you can. You can have one blueberry each. But be careful, they are very juicy. You might stain your clothes," the lady said. She gave Tom and Anna each a blueberry. They put them in their mouths. They were sweet and sour and soft. They made their tongues blue. "Mmm, these are good," Tom said. "Thank you, lady," Anna said. "You're welcome, children. I'm glad you like them. This is a display of blueberries from our farm. We grow them with love and care. We want to share them with everyone," the lady said. "That's very nice of you," Anna said. "Can we see your farm?" Tom asked. "Maybe another day. It's very far from here. You have to go by car. And it's very slow. There are many hills and curves and animals on the road," the lady said. "Wow, that sounds fun," Tom said. "Maybe we can draw it," Anna said. "That's a good idea. You can draw your own display of blueberries. And maybe you can show it to me next time I come to the park," the lady said. "Okay, we will," Tom and Anna said. They waved goodbye to the lady. They ran to the playground. They found some paper and crayons. They started to draw their display of blueberries. They were happy. They had learned something new. And they had made a new friend.

One day, a big, thick cat named Tom sat on a porch. He saw a small, thin mouse named Jerry. Tom wanted to get Jerry, but he did not dare to jump off the porch. Tom was too scared. Tom said, "Jerry, come here!" But Jerry did not want to go near Tom. Jerry knew that Tom wanted to get him. Jerry said, "No, Tom! I will not come to you!" Tom thought and thought. He wanted to be brave and dare to jump off the porch. So, he closed his eyes and jumped. He landed on the ground and felt happy. Now, Tom could try to get Jerry. But Jerry was fast and ran away. Tom and Jerry played all day.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lucy. She was very curious and loved to talk to everyone. One day, she was going to the store to buy some fruit. She saw a big green apple and decided to get it. When Lucy got home she asked her mum, "Can I eat the apple?" Mum said, "Yes, you can eat the green apple, but you have to eat other fruit too, like bananas, oranges and grapes. Then you will be very healthy and strong.” Lucy was very excited and she started talking about all the different kinds of fruit she would eat. She couldn't wait to go to the store and pick out her favorite fruit. That night, Lucy showed her mum all the fruit she had chosen. They both ate the fruit together and it was delicious! Lucy was so happy that she decided to talk about all the fruit she had eaten for the rest of the night. The End.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. As he climbed, he found a little door on the tree. "What is this?" he asked. Tim opened the door and found a small room. In the room, there was a little man. The man said, "Hi, I am from a far away planet. I am bored here. Can you spend time with me?" Tim said, "Okay, let's play!" They played and played. The little man showed Tim a magic ball. He said, "This ball can do something fun, but I need your help." Tim and the little man used the magic ball and the tree turned into a big slide! They slid down and had lots of fun. The little man was not bored anymore.
