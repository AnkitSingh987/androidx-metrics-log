Category: Beginner
Title: The Enormous Sun and the Cloud

Once upon a time, there was an enormous sun who loved to shine. Every day, the sun would shine bright and light up the whole sky. The sun had a big goal: to make everyone happy with its light. One day, while the sun was shining, a small cloud came by. The cloud was sad because it couldn't shine like the sun. The sun wanted to help the cloud, so it had an idea. It told the cloud to go near the sun, and the sun would make the cloud shine too. The cloud went near the sun, and something unexpected happened. The cloud started to shine! The sun's light made the cloud glow like a big, shiny ball in the sky. The sun and the cloud were both very happy. From that day on, the sun and the cloud became best friends, and they both shined together, making everyone happy.

One day, a poor cat was walking in the sun. The cat was very hot and its skin was dry. It needed lotion to make its skin feel good. The cat saw a dog and said, "Dog, can you help me? I am poor and need lotion for my skin." The dog was kind and wanted to help the cat. The dog had lotion in its home. The dog and the cat went to the dog's home. The dog gave the cat some lotion. The cat put the lotion on its skin. Now, the cat's skin was not dry anymore. The cat was happy and said, "Thank you, dog! You are a good friend."

Once upon a time, there was a brave girl. She wanted to explore her attic, but she was scared. She looked around for something brave to do. She noticed her paintbrush lying on the floor. That gave her an idea. She would paint the attic! She grabbed the paintbrush and climbed up the stairs to the attic. As the brave girl opened the attic door and stepped inside a shiver ran down her spine, but she kept on going. She started to paint the walls. Soon the old, dusty attic was filled with bright colours. The brave girl smiled as she looked at her work. Suddenly she heard a voice calling from downstairs. "What are you doing up there, brave girl?" The brave girl looked down and said, "I'm painting the attic, come and see!"

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was very excited because she got a new toy. She wanted to show her friend Tim the new toy. So, she went to Tim's house. Sue said, "Tim, look at my new toy! I want to play with you!" Tim was happy and wanted to play too. They played with the new toy all day long. One day, they saw a sign near their play area. The sign said, "No playing here." Sue and Tim were sad. They wanted to find a new place to play. They looked around and found a nice park. They played with their new toy at the park and were happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sara. She loved to have tea with her mom. Every day, they would sit and talk while they drank tea. Sara liked to cover her tea with a small plate, so it stayed warm. One day, the sky was dark and full of clouds. Sara's mom said, "We should have tea inside today. The dark sky tells me it will rain soon." Sara agreed, and they went inside to have their tea. As they were drinking their tea, they heard a loud noise outside. They looked out the window and saw that the big tree in their yard had fallen. It covered their car! Sara and her mom were sad because their car was broken. They wished they had moved the car before the dark sky brought the rain and the tree fell.

Once upon a time, there was a little town near a big river. In this town, there was a nice boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy boat. One day, Tim wanted to set his boat in the river. So, he went to the river with his toy boat, ready to play. As he played, he saw his friend Sally. Sally said, "Be careful, Tim! The river is very big and your boat is very small." But Tim didn't listen. He wanted to see his boat float in the river. Tim set his boat in the river, but the water started to rise. It was a flood! The water went up and up, and Tim's boat floated away. Tim and Sally were sad. The flood took Tim's boat, and they never found it again. The end.
