Category: Beginner
Title: The Lion and the Window

Once upon a time, there was a lion who wanted to be king of the jungle. He was very proud and strutted around the savannah telling all his friends, who were other animals, how great he was. One day, he was walking by a window and he saw his reflection. He was so impressed with how strong and proud he looked, that he decided he wanted to grab the reflection out of the window so he could show everyone. He reached out with his paw but the reflection wouldn't budge. He tried again, but he just couldn't grab it. All the other animals gathered to watch, and they laughed at the lion's failed attempt. The lion was so embarrassed that he slunk away in shame. The other animals taught the lion a lesson; he should take pride in who he is, and not be so concerned about how he looks. Because the reflection he saw in the window was always there, and couldn't be grabbed or taken away.

One day, a big red bus went down the road. The bus went fast. The bus was full of people. They were going to the park. In the park, there was a spicy food party. Everyone on the bus loved spicy food. At the park, the sun began to rise. It was a beautiful day. The people on the bus got off and went to the spicy food party. They ate spicy food and talked to each other. One man said, "I love spicy food! It is so hot!" Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all the spicy food away! The people were sad. They did not know what to do. But then, a little girl had an idea. She said, "Let's go back on the bus and find more spicy food!" Everyone agreed and got back on the bus. They went to find more spicy food. The people were happy again. The big red bus saved the day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a favorite mug that he used for his milk every day. One day, his mug went missing. Tim looked all over his house, but he could not find it. He felt very sad. Tim went outside to ask his friend, Sue, if she had seen his mug. Sue said, "I saw a big bird take your mug. It flew that way!" Tim and Sue decided to follow the bird to get the mug back. They walked and walked until they found a big, open tree. In the tree, they saw the big bird and Tim's mug. Tim called out, "Please, give me my mug back!" The big bird looked at them and said, "I took your mug because I thought it was a pretty nest for my eggs. I'm sorry." The bird gave the mug back to Tim, and they all became friends. From that day on, Tim shared his milk with the bird and her babies.

Tim and Mia are friends. They like to play and share. One day, they have yogurt for snack. Tim has strawberry yogurt. Mia has blueberry yogurt. Tim thinks it is funny to joke with Mia. He says, "Your yogurt is yucky. It is blue like a monster. You will turn into a monster if you eat it." Mia feels sad. She does not like Tim's joke. She likes her yogurt. She says, "No, it is not yucky. It is yummy. It is blue like the sky. You are mean, Tim." Tim does not stop. He says, "Yes, it is yucky. Watch me. I will show you." He takes a spoon of Mia's yogurt and pretends to eat it. But he does not eat it. He spits it out on the floor. He says, "Blech! It is so yucky. I told you so." Mia is angry. She does not want to play with Tim anymore. She says, "You are not my friend. You are rude. You wasted my yogurt. You made a mess. Go away, Tim." Tim feels bad. He sees that Mia is right. He was not nice. He was not funny. He was silly. He says, "I'm sorry, Mia. I was wrong. I was trying to joke, but it was not a joke. It was a bad thing to do. Can you forgive me?" Mia looks at Tim. She sees that he is sorry. She says, "OK, Tim. I forgive you. But you have to promise me something. You have to promise me that you will not joke like that again. You have to be kind and respectful. You have to say sorry to the teacher for the mess. And you have to give me some of your yogurt." Tim nods. He says, "I promise, Mia. I will do all that. And I will share my yogurt with you. I like strawberry and blueberry. They are both good. And you are a good friend." Mia smiles. She says, "Thank you, Tim. You are a good friend, too. Come on, let's eat our yogurt and clean up the floor. And then we can play together." Tim and Mia eat their yogurt and clean up the floor. They say sorry to the teacher. They play together. They are happy. They learn a lesson. They learn that jokes are only fun when they are not hurtful. They learn that sharing is caring. They learn that saying sorry is important. They learn that being friends is better than being embarrassed.
