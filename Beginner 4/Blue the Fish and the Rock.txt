Category: Beginner
Title: Blue the Fish and the Rock

One day, a fish named Blue was swimming in the sea. Blue saw a big rock and wanted to play. Blue asked his friend, Red, "Do you want to dive with me?" Red said, "Yes! Let's dive together!" They both swam fast and dove deep down. They saw many pretty shells and colorful plants. Blue and Red swam in circles and made a fun motion with their tails. Suddenly, Blue bumped into a crab. The crab was angry and said, "Watch where you swim!" Blue felt sorry and said, "I am sorry, Mr. Crab. I did not see you." Then, Blue and Red swam away to find more fun things to do in the sea.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a chubby witch. She lived in a little house all by herself. She had a big black hat and a long broom. The witch liked to play tricks on the people in the village. One day, the witch decided to repeat a trick she had played before. She turned all the apples in the village into big, round stones. The people were very sad because they loved to eat apples. They did not know what to do. A brave little boy named Tom decided to talk to the witch. He walked to her house and knocked on the door. The witch opened the door, and Tom asked her to please change the apples back. The witch thought about it and said, "Okay, but only if you promise to be my friend." Tom agreed, and the witch turned the apples back into real apples. From that day on, the witch and Tom were friends, and the witch never played tricks on the village again.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a strong bear named Ben. Ben loved to eat and play all day long. One day, while walking in the forest, he met a little bird named Billy. "Hi, I am Billy," said the little bird. "Do you want to be friends?" "Yes, I would like that," replied Ben. Every day, Ben would feed Billy some yummy food. They would share berries, nuts, and other treats they found in the forest. Together, they grew strong and happy. One day, a big storm came to the forest. Trees fell down and the wind was very loud. Ben and Billy were scared, but they stayed close to each other. After the storm, they helped each other clean up the mess. "Thank you for being my friend," said Ben to Billy. "We are strong together." "You're welcome," said Billy. "I am happy to have a friend like you." And from that day on, Ben and Billy stayed best friends, playing and sharing food in the big forest. They knew that as long as they were together, they would always be strong and happy.

Anna liked to travel with her dad. He drove a big truck and took her to many places. Sometimes they saw mountains, sometimes they saw lakes, sometimes they saw cities. Anna always brought her flute with her. She liked to play music for her dad and the people they met. One day, they stopped at a farm. There were many animals there: cows, sheep, chickens, and a horse. Anna wanted to see the horse. She asked her dad, "Can I go and say hello to the horse?" Dad said, "Yes, but be careful. Don't go too close. And don't forget your flute." Anna nodded and ran to the fence. She saw the horse eating grass. He was brown and big and had a long mane. Anna thought he was beautiful. She took out her flute and played a song for him. She hoped he would like it. The horse lifted his head and looked at Anna. He heard her music. He liked it. He walked towards her and nuzzled her hand. Anna smiled and petted his nose. She said, "Hello, horse. I'm Anna. Do you like my flute?" The horse neighed and nodded. He liked Anna. He liked her flute. He liked her clear voice. He wanted to be her friend. He said, "Hello, Anna. I'm Max. I like your flute. I like your voice. Can you play more music for me?" Anna was happy. She had made a new friend. She said, "Yes, Max. I can play more music for you. Do you want to travel with me and my dad?" Max said, "Yes, Anna. I want to travel with you and your dad. Can you take me with you?" Anna said, "I don't know, Max. You are very big. How can you fit in the truck?" Max said, "I don't know, Anna. Maybe we can find a way. Or maybe you can come back and visit me again." Anna said, "I will try, Max. I will try to come back and visit you again. Or maybe we can find a way to travel together. You are my friend. I like you." Max said, "You are my friend too, Anna. I like you. Thank you for playing music for me." Anna hugged Max and played more music for him. They were happy. They were friends. They liked to travel and play the flute.
