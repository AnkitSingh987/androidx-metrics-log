Category: Beginner
Title: The Monster and the Cave

Once upon a time, there was a friendly monster. The monster lived in a comfortable cave. He had a big, soft bed to sleep on. He liked to play and have fun with his friends. One day, it rained a lot. The monster's cave got wet. His big, soft bed was wet too. The monster was sad. He did not like the wet cave. The sun came out and shined brightly. The monster opened the cave door. The sun helped to dry the wet cave. The big, soft bed became dry too. The monster was happy again. Now, he could play and have fun with his friends in his comfortable cave.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Tom and Jane. They were walking around the park, looking for something new and fun. Suddenly, Tom saw something shiny in the grass. He shouted with excitement, “Look what I found!” Jane ran over and saw the shiny black object. Tom said, “It looks like a win game! We can try it together and maybe win something!” Jane was so excited. “Yes! Let’s play!” They took turns flipping the small black object in the air. Each time the black object landed in the grass, they screamed with joy. In the end, Jane won the game! Tom smiled and said, “Congrats, Jane! You did a great job!” Jane was so happy and excited. She thanked Tom and they hugged.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a big jug for water. One day, she found an unknown seed in her yard. Sue did not know what the seed would grow into, but she wanted to help it grow. So, she put the seed in the ground and gave it water from her jug. Sue's friend, Tim, saw her with the jug and asked, "What are you doing, Sue?" She said, "I found a seed and want to help it grow. I'm giving it water from my jug." Tim thought it was a good idea and wanted to help too. They both took care of the seed every day. After some time, the seed grew into a big, beautiful plant. Sue and Tim were so happy to see it grow. They did not know what the plant was, but they accepted it and loved it just the same. The plant brought joy to their lives, and they learned that even unknown things can be wonderful.

Once upon a time, there was a big red ball. The ball was very comfortable to hold. A little girl named Amy loved to play with the ball. She would throw it up in the sky and watch it fall back down. Every day, Amy would play with her ball in the park. One day, Amy saw a big orange fire in the park. She was scared. The fire was near her favorite tree. Amy wanted to help put out the fire. She had an idea! She could use her big red ball to stop the fire. Amy threw her ball up in the sky, higher and higher. She wanted to release the ball right on top of the fire. The ball went up and up, and then it fell down, right on the fire! The fire went out, and the tree was safe. Amy was happy that she could save her favorite tree.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a toy cat that she loved very much. Lily owned the toy cat, and she took it everywhere she went. One day, Lily and her toy cat went for a walk. They saw a weak, little bird on the ground. The bird could not fly. Lily felt sad for the bird. She wanted to help it. Lily picked up a small cross made of sticks. She put it next to the bird so it could rest. The bird got better and flew away. Lily and her toy cat were happy they could help the bird. They went home, feeling good about their day.

Ben and Lily were playing in the garden. They liked to dig in the dirt and look for bugs. They had a small shovel and a bucket. They were having fun. "Look, Lily, I found a shiny thing!" Ben said. He held up a metal thing with teeth. It was a key. "Wow, Ben, what is it for?" Lily asked. She touched the key with her finger. "I don't know. Maybe it opens a door or a box. Maybe it has a treasure inside!" Ben said. He put the key in his pocket. "Let's go and see if we can find it!" Lily said. She grabbed her shovel and bucket. "Okay, but we have to be careful. The key is fragile. It can break or bend. We have to stay away from the rocks and the water," Ben said. He followed Lily. They searched around the garden, looking for a door or a box that matched the key. They did not find anything. They were getting tired and hungry. "Ben, Lily, time for lunch!" Mom called from the house. "Okay, Mom, we're coming!" Ben and Lily said. They ran to the house. "Did you have fun in the garden?" Mom asked. "Yes, Mom, we did. Look what we found!" Ben said. He showed Mom the key. "Wow, that's a nice key. Where did you find it?" Mom asked. "We found it in the dirt. We don't know what it is for. Do you know, Mom?" Lily asked. Mom looked at the key. She smiled. "I do know, my loves. This is a very special key. It belongs to your grandma. She gave it to me when I was a little girl. It opens a music box that plays a beautiful song. Do you want to hear it?" Mom asked. "Yes, please, Mom!" Ben and Lily said. Mom took them to her bedroom. She opened a drawer and took out a wooden box. She used the key to open it. The box played a sweet melody. Ben and Lily listened with wonder. They hugged Mom. "Thank you, Mom, for sharing this with us. We love you," they said. "I love you too, my loves. And I'm glad you found the key. It is a precious gift from grandma. She would be happy to know that you appreciate it. But remember, it is fragile. You have to be gentle with it and keep it safe. Okay?" Mom said. "Okay, Mom. We will. We will stay careful with the key and the box," Ben and Lily said. They enjoyed the music and the lunch. They were happy.
