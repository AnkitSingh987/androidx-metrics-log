Category: Beginner
Title: Anton the Elephant and the Giraffe

Once upon a time, there was an elephant named Anton. He was huge and grey. One day while Anton was walking through the jungle, he stumbled upon a cell phone. He curiously picked it up and wanted to chew on it. He tried to chew it and found it was much too hard so he decided to ask his friends for help. He could hear them making noise by the nearby pond. When Anton asked them for help, they all laughed and said, “Why don’t you just ask someone else?” Then Anton asked his friend the giraffe, “Will you please help me chew this phone?” The giraffe smiled and said, “Yes, of course, I can help you.” The giraffe held the huge phone in front of her and with her long neck she was able to reach her teeth to the phone and chew it until it became small enough for Anton to eat. Anton was so delighted that he thanked the giraffe and happily ate the cell phone. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to design things. He made many fun things to play with. One day, it was very hot outside. Tim wanted to make something to keep his drinks cold. So, he thought of a cooler. Tim used a big box to make the cooler. He put ice inside the box. Then, he put his drinks in the box too. The ice made the drinks cold. Tim was happy because he could drink cold juice on a hot day. Tim's friends liked his cooler too. They all wanted one. So, Tim helped them make coolers for their drinks. Now, everyone could have cold drinks when they played outside. They were safe from the hot sun and had fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a happy dog named Spot. Spot liked to play with his ball. One day, Spot saw a big stack of blocks. The blocks were very colorful. Spot wanted to lead his friends to the stack of blocks. He barked and wagged his tail. His friends, a cat and a bird, followed him. They all ran to the stack of blocks and wanted to play. But then, a big wind came. The wind blew the stack of blocks down. Spot, the cat, and the bird were sad. They could not play with the blocks anymore. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot liked to play with his ball. He would run and jump and catch the ball. Spot was very happy when he played with his ball. One day, Spot was playing with his ball in the park. He saw a big tree and wanted to see what was behind it. He ran around the tree and the ball bounced away. Spot felt frustrated because he lost his ball. Suddenly, Spot heard a giggle. He looked around and saw a little girl holding his ball. Spot felt embarrassed because he thought the girl was laughing at him. But, the girl wasn't laughing at Spot. She was happy because she found a new friend to play with. They played with the ball together and had lots of fun. In the end, Spot wasn't frustrated or embarrassed anymore. He was very happy to have a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful cat. This cat loved to count. One day, the cat met a little dog who wanted to learn how to count too. The cat said, "I will teach you how to count." The little dog was very happy. They started with one, two, and three. The little dog learned quickly and soon, they could count together. After some time, the thoughtful cat and the little dog became good friends. They counted many things together, like flowers and stars. They were very happy and had lots of fun. And that is how the thoughtful cat taught the little dog how to count.

One day, a girl named Sue wanted to make soup. She had a huge pot to cook it in. She asked her friend, Tim, to help her. Tim said, "Yes, I will help you make soup." Sue and Tim put many things in the pot. They put in water, carrots, and peas. Tim wanted to put in a toy car. Sue said, "No, we can't put a toy car in the soup." Tim did not agree. He said, "But the car is fun!" Sue said, "No, cars are not for soup." Just then, a big bird flew into the kitchen. The bird saw the toy car and picked it up. It flew away with the car. Sue and Tim were surprised. They looked at each other and laughed. They finished making the soup without the car.
