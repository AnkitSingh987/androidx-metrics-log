Category: Beginner
Title: The Lizard and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a pretty lizard. The pretty lizard lived in a big tree. The big tree was near a small pond. The pretty lizard liked to play near the pond. One day, the pretty lizard found a big stick. The pretty lizard wanted to play with the big stick. The pretty lizard used the big stick to stir the water in the small pond. The water made little waves when the pretty lizard stirred it. The pretty lizard had fun stirring the water. The pretty lizard played all day. When the sun went down, the pretty lizard went back to the big tree. The pretty lizard was happy and sleepy. The pretty lizard slept well in the big tree.

One day, a little worm named Wiggly was in the garden. He saw a big bird come. The bird looked at Wiggly and said, "You are not a regular worm. You are very small." Wiggly was sad. He wanted to be a regular worm like his friends. He asked his friend, a ladybug, for help. The ladybug said, "Don't worry, Wiggly. You are special just the way you are." The next day, the bird came back. It tried to eat Wiggly's friends. But Wiggly was too small for the bird to see. Wiggly helped his friends hide from the bird. They were all safe. In the end, Wiggly learned that it is good to be different. His friends loved him for who he was, and he helped to keep them safe. The moral of the story is that being different can be a good thing.

Once upon a time, there was a light frog named Bob. Bob loved to splash in the water all day. One day, while he was playing, he saw a new symbol on a big rock. The symbol looked like a smile. Bob called his friend, a fish named Sam. "Sam, come look at this!" said Bob. Sam swam over and looked at the symbol. "Wow!" said Sam, "It looks like a happy face!" They both laughed and decided to make more symbols around the pond. Bob and Sam spent the day splashing and drawing symbols on rocks. They made a sun, a tree, and a big heart. They were very happy with their art. At the end of the day, they sat by the pond, looking at their work. "We did a great job, Bob!" said Sam. "Yes, we did!" agreed Bob. And they continued to splash and play until the sun went down.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big blue balloon in the sky. They want to catch it. "Look, look, a blue balloon!" Lily says. "Let's run and get it!" Tom says. They run after the balloon. They run very fast. But the balloon is faster. It flies away from them. "Oh no, we can't catch it!" Lily says. "Maybe we can ask someone to help us," Tom says. They see a man with a yellow hat. He has a bunch of bananas in his hand. He is eating one. "Excuse me, sir, can you help us?" Lily says. The man with the yellow hat looks at them. He smiles. "Help you with what, little ones?" he says. "We want to catch the blue balloon. It is in the sky. Can you give us a boost?" Tom says. The man with the yellow hat laughs. "I'm sorry, but I can't help you with that. The blue balloon is too high. It belongs to the wind. You can't catch it," he says. Lily and Tom feel sad. They really want the blue balloon. "Can we have a banana, then?" Lily says. The man with the yellow hat nods. "Sure, you can have a banana. Here you go. Bananas are good for you. They make you strong and happy," he says. He gives them each a banana. They peel them and eat them. They are sweet and yummy. "Thank you, sir. You are very nice," Tom says. "You're welcome, little ones. Enjoy your bananas. And don't be sad about the blue balloon. Maybe you will see it again someday. Or maybe you will find another balloon. There are many balloons in the world. And many colors, too. Not just blue," he says. Lily and Tom listen to him. They feel better. They smile. "Thank you, sir. You are very wise," Lily says. They finish their bananas. They wave goodbye to the man with the yellow hat. They go back to the park. They play some more. They have fun. They forget about the blue balloon. For now.
