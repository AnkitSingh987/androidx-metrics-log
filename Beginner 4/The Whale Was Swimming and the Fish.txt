Category: Beginner
Title: The Whale Was Swimming and the Fish

One day, a big whale was swimming in the deep blue sea. The whale was very happy. It liked to play with the fish and splash water with its big tail. The sun was shining, and the whale felt warm and loved. But then, the whale saw something that made it feel frightened. It saw a big net in the water. The net was going to separate the whale from its fish friends. The whale did not want to be alone. It wanted to stay with its friends and play. So, the whale swam very fast and broke the net with its strong body. The fish friends were free, and they were not separated. The whale was not frightened anymore. They all played together, and the sea was a happy place again.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived an old man. He had a big sack. The sack was full of toys. The old man loved to give toys to children. One sunny day, the old man went to the park. He saw many children playing. They were happy. The old man wanted to give them toys from his sack. But the sack was too full. The old man tried to open it. The sack split! Toys fell out. The children saw the toys and ran to them. They played with the toys and said thank you to the old man. The old man was happy. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish. He swam in the blue sea and he was very fine. One day, a bird flew up to him in the water and said “let’s have some fun”. The fish clapped his fins and said ok. So, the bird and the fish started to play in the water. They swam around and around and had a great time. The fish clapped his fins again and again. He was so happy. Suddenly, the sun went down. So the bird said goodbye to the fish and flew away. The fish smiled and said goodbye too. He was still very fine. He swam around and around and made other fish glad. Everyone clapped to show how fine he was. The fish was very happy and he started to swim around again. He had lots of fun playing with the bird and swimming with all the other fish. He was very fine, and always clapped his fins. The end.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play outside. He liked to play in the mud. He made mud pies and mud castles. The sun was shining, and Tim was happy. A girl named Sue saw Tim playing. She wanted to play too. But when she came over, she slipped in the mud. Sue was upset. She started to cry. Tim saw Sue crying and wanted to help. He shouted, "Don't cry, Sue! We can play together!" Tim showed Sue how to make mud pies and mud castles. Soon, Sue was not upset anymore. They played in the mud all day and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy. He liked to explore and one day he decided to go exploring in a cave. The cave was dark and scary but the boy was brave and he kept going. Soon, he arrived in a big room. The room was funny and the boy laughed. Suddenly, the boy heard a noise and he froze. He looked around and he saw a big monster. It was smiling and the boy was scared. He started to run but it was too late. The monster caught him and he received a big shock. The monster said that it would let the boy go if he gave it something. The boy had nothing, so the monster kept him in the cave and the boy never saw his family again. It was a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and run in the park. One day, Spot saw a big dog named Max. Max was not nice. Max was a threat to Spot. Spot was scared of Max. Spot thought it would be stupid to run away from Max. So, Spot decided to yield and be friends with Max. They started to play together. But Max was still not nice. Max played too rough and hurt Spot. In the end, Spot was very sad. He learned that it was not a good idea to be friends with Max. Spot should have run away from the threat. Spot wished he had not been so stupid.
