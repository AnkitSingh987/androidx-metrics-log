Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Man and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was an elderly man named Tom. He liked to carry wood from the forest to his cozy house. One day, the fog came to the village. It was hard for Tom to see. Tom met a big, friendly dog named Max. Max said, "Woof! I can help you carry wood." Tom smiled and said, "Thank you, Max!" So, they went to the forest together. Even with the fog, Tom and Max found wood. They carried it back to Tom's house. They were very happy. Tom said, "Max, you are a good helper!" And they became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very obedient. He always listened to his mom and dad. They were so proud of him. One day, Tim's mom asked him to polish his shoes. She told him that if he did a good job, she would give him a prize. Tim was excited. He wanted to make his shoes very clean and shiny. Tim worked hard to polish his shoes. He made them so shiny that they looked like new. When his mom saw them, she was very happy. She gave Tim his prize - a big, yummy cookie. Tim was so proud of himself, and he enjoyed his tasty prize.

One day, a chubby cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a little bird on the ground. The bird was sad because it was lost. Tom wanted to help the bird find its way home. Tom asked the bird, "Why are you sad?" The bird said, "I forget how to get home. Can you help me?" Tom nodded and said, "Yes, I will help you." They walked and walked. They saw a lot of dust on the ground. Tom used his tail to sweep the dust away. The bird was happy. They found the bird's home. The bird said, "Thank you, Tom!" Tom smiled and went back to his own home.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy cat named Mindy. Mindy was a navy-blue cat who loved to nap all day. She had a friend, a little bird named Bree. One day, Mindy was taking a nap under a big tree. Bree flew down to Mindy and said, "Mindy, do you want to play with me?" Mindy yawned and said, "I am sleepy, Bree. I want to nap right now. Can we play later?" Bree agreed, "Okay, Mindy. Have a good nap. We can play later." Bree flew away to find other friends to play with. After her nap, Mindy woke up and saw Bree playing with other friends. Mindy felt happy and joined them. They all played together until the sun went down. Then, Mindy went back to her big tree, ready for another sleepy nap.

One day, a girl named Sue went to the store with her mom. They wanted to buy a new blouse for Sue. Sue saw a pretty blouse and said, "Mom, I want that one!" Her mom looked at the price and said, "It is too much. Let us order a fake one." Sue and her mom went home and ordered the fake blouse on the computer. They waited for the mail to come. One day, the mail came with a big box. Sue and her mom opened the box and found not just one, but ten fake blouses! Sue's mom said, "Oh no, we made a mistake when we ordered!" Sue laughed and said, "Now I have a lot of pretty blouses to wear!" They both laughed and decided to give some of the extra blouses to Sue's friends. And everyone was happy with their new blouses.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a new ball that he loved to play with. One day, Tim saw a big trophy in the park. The trophy was shiny and pretty. Tim wanted to touch the trophy, but he was not sure if he could. He asked his mom, "Can I touch the trophy?" His mom said, "Yes, I will allow you to touch the trophy, but be careful." Tim was happy and he touched the trophy gently. It was very smooth and cold. He felt proud and excited. After touching the trophy, Tim played with his new ball in the park. He had a lot of fun. When it was time to go home, Tim said goodbye to the trophy and went home with his mom. Tim had a great day at the park.
