Category: Beginner
Title: The Lizard and the Toys

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a friendly lizard. The lizard lived in a little boy's room. The boy's name was Tim. Every morning, the lizard would wake Tim up. One day, the lizard said, "Tim, wake up! It's time to play!" Tim opened his eyes and smiled. "Good morning, friendly lizard," he said. They played with toys and had lots of fun. After a long day of playing, Tim and the friendly lizard were tired. They said goodnight to each other. "Goodnight, friendly lizard," said Tim. "Goodnight, Tim," said the lizard. They both went to sleep, ready to wake up and play again the next day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, interesting bag. He liked to put many things in his bag. One day, he found a big stick. He put the stick in his bag too. Tim went to the park to play. He saw a big puddle. He took the stick from his bag and started to stir the water. The water became muddy. Tim was having fun. A little bird came by and saw Tim. The bird said, "Please don't stir the water. My friends and I drink from this puddle." Tim felt bad. He stopped stirring the water and said sorry to the bird. Tim learned that his fun should not hurt others.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a kind dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his toy sausage. He would spin it around and around in the air. Spot was very happy when he played with his toy. He knew that one day, he would spin it so high that it would touch the sky. One sunny day, Spot went to the park with his toy sausage. He saw his friend, a little bird named Tweet. Tweet was very good at flying high in the sky. Spot had an idea. He asked Tweet if he could help him spin his toy sausage very high, up to the sky. Tweet said yes, and they started to play together. Tweet held onto the toy sausage while Spot spun it around and around. They spun it faster and faster. Then, with a big push, Tweet let go of the toy sausage. It flew high up into the sky, higher than the trees. Spot was so happy! He barked and jumped with joy. Tweet flew up and brought the toy sausage back down to Spot. They both smiled and played together for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet little girl named Lily. She had a big red ball that she liked to play with. One day, Lily went to the park with her mom to play with her ball. At the park, Lily saw a boy with a blue ball. The boy said, "Hi, my name is Tom. Do you want to play with me?" Lily said, "Yes, let's play!" So, Lily and Tom played with their balls, throwing them high in the air. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took both balls away! Lily and Tom were sad. They looked and looked, but could not find their balls. Just when they were about to give up, a kind dog came running to them with both balls in his mouth. The dog wagged his tail and dropped the balls at their feet. Lily and Tom were so happy! They thanked the dog and played with him until the day had to end.

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing in her room. She was very happy. But she was also anxious because her mom told her to clean her room. Lily did not want to clean, she wanted to play. Lily saw a soap on the floor. She had an idea! She said to her toy bear, "I will make the floor soapy and slide around. It will be fun!" So she put the soap on the floor and began to slide. Suddenly, Lily's mom came into the room. She did not see the soap and stumbled. "Oh no!" said Lily. Her mom was okay, but she was surprised. Lily said, "I'm sorry, Mom. I wanted to have fun. I will clean my room now." And they both cleaned the room together.
