Category: Beginner
Title: Bo and Mimi and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Bo and a little cat named Mimi. They lived in a small house with a nice man. Bo and Mimi liked to play together, but they did not like to share their toys. One day, Bo wanted to play with Mimi's ball, but Mimi said, "No, it is mine! You can't play with it!" Bo was not happy. He said, "I will take it from you!" Mimi got scared and ran away. Bo and Mimi started a small war over the ball. They ran around the house, trying to take the ball from each other. The nice man saw them and said, "Stop! You need to share your toys and be friends." Mimi thought about it and said, "Okay, we can share the ball." But Bo was not in a fit mood. He refused to share at first. The nice man talked to Bo and helped him understand that sharing is good. In the end, Bo and Mimi learned to share their toys and play together. They became the best of friends and had lots of fun in their small house with the nice man.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big waste bin that was very tight. Tim's mom told him to go and throw away his trash. Tim was a good boy, so he went to the waste bin. As Tim went to throw his trash, he saw a cat stuck in the tight waste bin. The cat looked sad and scared. Tim wanted to help the cat, so he tried to open the waste bin. But it was too tight for him to open. Just then, a big dog came running to the waste bin. Tim thought the dog wanted to eat the cat! But the dog was very smart. It used its strong nose to open the tight waste bin. The cat was free and happy. Tim, the cat, and the dog all played together in the park. Tim learned that sometimes, things are not what they seem.

Once upon a time, there was a small farm with a big red barn. In the barn, there lived a harmless old horse named Daisy. Daisy loved to eat hay every day. One day, Daisy saw a little boy named Timmy playing in the farm. Timmy saw Daisy and said, "Hi, Daisy! Do you want to play with me?" Daisy nodded her head and played with Timmy. They had a lot of fun. After a while, Daisy was very hungry. She looked at Timmy and pointed to the hay in the barn. Timmy smiled and said, "Oh, you want some hay? I will bring it to you!" Timmy went to the barn and brought some hay for Daisy. Daisy was very happy and ate the hay. After eating, Daisy played with Timmy again. The sun began to set, and Timmy knew it was time to go home. He said goodbye to Daisy and promised to come back and play with her again. Daisy was happy to have a new friend and looked forward to the next time they would play together.

Ben and Lily liked to play with their paper planes. They made them from old newspapers and colored them with crayons. They threw them in the air and watched them soar in the wind. Sometimes they made funny noises like "vroom" and "zoom". One day, they found a big black balloon in the park. It was shiny and round and had a string attached to it. Ben and Lily wondered who left it there. They decided to take it home and play with it. They tied the balloon to a stick and ran around the yard with it. They pretended it was a rocket or a bird or a dragon. They had a lot of fun with the balloon. But then, they heard a hissing sound. The balloon had a leak. It was getting smaller and smaller. Ben and Lily were sad. They didn't want to lose their balloon. "Can we fix it?" Lily asked. "I don't know," Ben said. "Maybe we can use some tape or glue." They ran inside the house and looked for something to patch the balloon. They found some tape and some glue and some stickers. They tried to cover the hole with them. But it didn't work. The balloon kept leaking air. They went back outside and held the balloon in their hands. It was almost flat. They looked at each other and sighed. "Bye bye, balloon," they said. They let go of the string and watched the balloon fly away. It was very slow and low. It didn't soar anymore. It landed on a tree and stayed there. Ben and Lily felt sad for a while. But then they remembered their paper planes. They still had them. They picked them up and threw them in the air. They watched them soar in the wind. They smiled and laughed. They still had fun.
