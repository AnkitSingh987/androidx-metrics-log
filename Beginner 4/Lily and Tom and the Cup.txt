Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Tom and the Cup

Lily and Tom were playing with their blocks. They liked to build towers and houses and cars. Lily had a big idea. She wanted to attach a coffee cup to the top of her tower. She thought it would look funny and cool. "Look, Tom, I have a coffee cup!" she said, holding the cup in her hand. "Where did you get that?" Tom asked. He knew that coffee was for grown-ups, not for kids. "From the kitchen. Mommy left it on the table. She was busy on the phone," Lily said. She did not ask Mommy if she could take the cup. She thought Mommy would not mind. "That's not good, Lily. You should not take things that are not yours. And you should not play with coffee. It is hot and bitter and can make you sick," Tom said. He was a wise boy. He listened to Mommy and Daddy and learned from them. "But I want to attach it to my tower. It will be fun," Lily said. She did not listen to Tom. She put the cup on the top of her tower and smiled. But then, something bad happened. The tower was too tall and wobbly. It fell down and made a loud noise. The cup broke and the coffee spilled on the floor and on Lily's dress. Lily cried and Tom was scared. Mommy came running to see what happened. She saw the mess and the broken cup and the crying Lily. She was angry and sad. "Lily, what did you do? Why did you take my coffee cup? Why did you play with it? You could have hurt yourself or Tom. You made a big mess and wasted my coffee. You were very naughty and silly," Mommy said. "I'm sorry, Mommy. I just wanted to attach it to my tower. I thought it would be fun," Lily said. "That's not a good reason, Lily. You should not take things that are not yours. You should not play with things that are dangerous or messy. You should listen to your brother. He is wise and cares for you. You should learn from him," Mommy said. Lily felt ashamed and sorry. She hugged Mommy and said, "I'm sorry, Mommy. I love you. I will not do it again. I will listen to Tom. He is wise and kind." Mommy hugged Lily back and said, "I love you too, Lily. I'm glad you are okay. But you have to clean up the mess and say sorry to Tom. And you have to promise me to never play with coffee again." Lily nodded and said, "I promise, Mommy. I will clean up and say sorry to Tom. And I will never play with coffee again." Tom came over and said, "It's okay, Lily. I forgive you. But please be careful next time. Coffee is not for kids. It is for grown-ups who need to stay awake and work hard." Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Tom. You are a wise and good brother. I will be careful next time. And I will play with blocks and toys that are for kids." They hugged and helped Mommy clean up the mess. They learned a lesson that day. They learned to respect other people's things, to be safe and neat, and to listen to wise advice. They also learned that coffee is not for kids. It is for grown-ups who need to stay awake and work hard.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play at the park. One day, while playing at the park, he saw something shiny on the ground. It was a piece of gold. Tim was very happy. Tim showed the gold to his friend, Sue. Sue said, "Wow! That's interesting. We should share it." But Tim didn't want to share. He wanted to keep the gold all for himself. This made Sue sad. Later, Tim saw Sue playing with other kids. They were having fun and laughing. Tim felt lonely and sad. He realized that sharing the gold with Sue would make both of them happy. So, he gave half of the gold to Sue. Sue smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim!" They played together and had a great time. Tim learned that sharing is important and it makes everyone happy. And they all lived happily ever after.
