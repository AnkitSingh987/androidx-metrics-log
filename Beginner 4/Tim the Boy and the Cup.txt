Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Cup

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big red ribbon. He wanted to tie it on his pet cat, Sam. Sam was a fast cat who liked to run and play. Tim thought the ribbon would make Sam look pretty. As Tim tried to tie the ribbon on Sam, he saw a small flame in the kitchen. The flame was on the stove. Tim was scared, but he knew he had to help. He ran to the sink and filled a cup with water. Tim threw the water on the flame, and it went away. He was happy that he saved the day. But then, Sam jumped up and the ribbon got caught on the stove. The ribbon was the flame all along! Tim learned that he should be more careful and not play with things he shouldn't.

Once upon a time, there was a big, hungry bird. The bird loved to fly high in the sky. One day, while flying, the bird saw a small, yummy bug on the ground. The hungry bird flew down and picked up the bug with its beak. It wanted to carry the bug to its nest. The bird's nest was up in a tall tree, close to the sky. As the bird carried the bug, it felt happy. When it got to the nest, the bird ate the bug. The hungry bird was not hungry anymore. It looked up at the sky, feeling full and glad.

Once upon a time there was a mighty king. He wore a golden crown and ruled his kingdom with love. One day a tiny mouse came to the king and said, "Please allow me to help rule your kingdom." The king looked at the brave little mouse and smiled. He said, "I can see you are very brave and I do need help. You may have the job, but first you must find a mighty crown." The mouse was excited and set off to find the perfect crown. He looked far and wide, but nothing seemed special enough. Just as he was ready to give up, the mouse stumbled upon a crown made of rubies and diamonds! It was the most beautiful crown he had ever seen. The tiny mouse ran back to the king and presented the crown to him. The king was amazed and said, "Why this is the perfect crown for my kingdom. You have proved to be very brave and I allow you to help me rule my kingdom." The mouse was so excited and the two of them formed a team. With their mighty crown, they ruled the kingdom with love and justice. And they lived happily ever after!

Once upon a time, there was a blue horse. The blue horse lived in a big field. The field had green grass and pretty flowers. The blue horse liked to eat the grass and smell the flowers. One day, the blue horse saw a big tree. The tree had a long branch. The blue horse wanted to touch the branch. So, the blue horse leaned on the tree. The branch moved a little, but the blue horse could not touch it. The blue horse tried again. This time, the blue horse leaned harder on the tree. The branch moved more, and the blue horse touched it! The blue horse was very happy. The blue horse played with the branch and then went back to eating grass and smelling flowers.

One day, a cute bird named Lily lived by a big cliff. She had a friend named Tim, who was a little bug. They liked to play and fly near the cliff. One day, while they were playing, a big wind came and struck Tim. Tim was blown away and got lost. Lily was very sad and wanted to find her friend. Lily asked other animals, "Have you seen Tim, my cute bug friend?" The animals said they did not know where he was. Lily did not give up. She kept looking for Tim. Finally, Lily found Tim stuck in a bush. She was so happy! She helped him get out of the bush and they went back to the cliff to play. They were careful when the wind blew, so they would not get lost again.
