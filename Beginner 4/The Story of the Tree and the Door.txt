Title: The Story of the Tree and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a tall mummy and a small baby. The mummy was in a rush, trying to get ready to go out. She picked up her pen and said to the baby: "Come on, hurry! We need to go now". The baby smiled and jumped off the sofa. He ran to the mummy and held out his hands. She smiled and took his hands in hers. Then they ran out the door together. Outside, they saw a tall tree. The mummy said: "Let's go explore the tree". So they went up to the tree and looked around. Suddenly, the baby noticed a bright blue pen lying underneath the tree. He bent down, picked it up and said: "Mummy, look! Here's a pen!" The mummy smiled and said: "That's a very pretty pen, isn't it? Now, hurry! We need to get going". So they held hands again and ran along the path to their car. When they got there, the mummy opened the door and said: "Come on in, hurry". And they both jumped in. As they drove away, the baby waved one last time at the tall tree with the bright blue pen underneath.

Once upon a time, there was a pilot named Tom. Tom was a nervous man. He flew a big plane in the sky. One day, Tom had a quarrel with his friend, Sam. Sam said, "You are not a good pilot." Tom was sad and said, "I am a good pilot, and I can fly the plane well." Tom decided to show Sam that he was a good pilot. He took Sam on a plane ride. Tom flew the plane very high and very fast. Sam was scared, but he saw that Tom was a good pilot. After the plane ride, Sam said, "You are a good pilot, Tom. I am sorry for the quarrel." Tom said, "It's okay, Sam. We all make mistakes." They became friends again. The moral of the story is to not judge others and to say sorry when you are wrong.

Once upon a time, there was a big pink bear. The bear lived in a pretty forest with many trees and flowers. The pink bear had a special secret. It could fly! One sunny day, the pink bear wanted to fly high in the sky. It flapped its arms and started to fly. The bear flew over the trees and saw many friends below. It saw birds, rabbits, and squirrels playing. The pink bear wanted to play with its friends too. So, it flew down and joined them. They played all day long. When the sun went down, the pink bear flew back to its home in the forest. The pink bear was happy it could fly and play with its friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat. He liked to eat with sauce. He liked the original sauce the most. His mom made the best sauce. Tim always wanted more sauce. One day, Tim saw a big bottle of original sauce on the table. He wanted it. He tried to get it, but it was too high. Tim got a chair to stand on. He reached up, but the bottle was still too high. Tim had an idea. He got his dog, Spot, to help him. Tim and Spot made a plan. Tim stood on Spot's back to get the sauce. They were so close! But then, something unexpected happened. The bottle of original sauce fell on the floor. Tim and Spot looked at the sauce on the floor. They were sad. They did not want to waste the sauce. But then, Tim's mom came into the room. She saw the sauce on the floor. She was not mad. She smiled and said, "It's okay, Tim and Spot. We can make more sauce together." Tim and Spot were happy. They learned that it was better to ask for help than to try to do things alone. And they all enjoyed the new, original sauce that they made together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a rich man named Tom. He had a big house, and he loved candy. He had a big room full of all the candy you could think of. One day, Tom met a little boy named Tim. Tom said, "Hi, Tim! Do you like candy?" Tim said, "Yes, I love candy! But I don't have any." Tom wanted to help Tim. So, he took Tim to his big house and showed him the candy room. Tim's eyes got big when he saw all the candy. He had never seen so much candy before. Tom said, "You can have some candy, but first, we must cover the candy with a big cloth so it stays clean." They covered the candy, and then Tom gave Tim a big bag to fill with candy. Tim was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tom!" They became good friends, and Tim always had candy to share with his friends.
