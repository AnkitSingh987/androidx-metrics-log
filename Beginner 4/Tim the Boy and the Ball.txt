Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Ball

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big, famous dog. The dog was very nice. Tim said, "Hello, dog! Do you want to play?" The dog wagged its tail and barked, "Yes, let's play!" As they played, they saw a cross on the ground. They did not want to disturb it, so they played around it. They had so much fun playing together. Tim threw a ball, and the dog ran to catch it. Then, something unexpected happened. The cross started to move! It was not a real cross. It was a big bug! The bug looked at Tim and the dog and said, "Thank you for not disturbing me. Can I play with you, too?" Tim and the famous dog looked at the bug and said, "Yes, let's all play together!" And they all had a great time playing together.

Once upon a time, there was a creative girl named Lily. She loved to wake up early and play with her toys. One day, she found a big stick and had an idea. Lily went to her friend Tom and said, "Let's use this stick as a measure! We can see how tall we are and how far we can jump!" Tom was excited and agreed to play with her. They took turns measuring their height and how far they could jump. They had so much fun playing together. As they played, they learned that it is important to share and be kind to one another. And that was the moral of their day: sharing and being kind makes everyone happy.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a restless wolf. He liked to run and jump all day. One day, the wolf met a little bird. The bird said, "Hi, wolf! Can you repeat what I say?" The wolf said, "Yes, I can repeat. What do you want me to say?" The bird sang a pretty song. The wolf tried to repeat the song, but he could not sing. He felt sad. The next day, the wolf met a big bear. The bear was in trouble. He had a thorn in his paw. The bear asked the wolf, "Can you help me?" The wolf remembered the bird's song and started to hum it. The bear felt calm and let the wolf take the thorn out. The bear said, "Thank you, wolf! Your song helped me." The wolf felt happy and knew he could sing in his own way.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man who had a big house. One day, he wanted to introduce his new friend to his family. His new friend was a funny clown with a big mouth. The clown came to the house and said, "Hello, I am a funny clown. I have a big mouth. I can make you all laugh." The family was very excited to meet the clown and they all sat down to watch him perform. The clown started to do tricks and make funny faces. Suddenly, a little bird flew out of his big mouth! The family was very surprised and they all laughed even harder. The bird became their new friend and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Will. Will liked to make loud noises all the time. He would bang on pots, yell, and stomp his feet. His mom and dad told him, "Will, if you keep making loud noises, you might become deaf. Then, you won't be able to hear the nice sounds around you." One day, Will went to the park. He saw a big tree and thought it would be fun to make loud noises there. He started to bang on the tree and yell. A little bird was sitting on a branch, trying to sing a pretty song. But Will's loud noises scared the bird away. Will felt sad that he scared the bird. He remembered what his mom and dad said about being deaf. Will decided to be quiet and listen to the sounds around him. He heard the wind, the leaves, and the pretty songs of other birds. From that day on, Will learned that it is good to be quiet sometimes and listen to the world around him. And that was the day Will learned the moral value of being a good listener.
