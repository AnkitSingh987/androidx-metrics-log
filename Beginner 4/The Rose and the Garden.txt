Category: Beginner
Title: The Rose and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a pretty rose. The rose was red and it lived in a big garden. Many people liked the rose because it was so pretty. One day, a dangerous bug came to the garden. The bug wanted to eat the pretty rose. The rose was scared and did not want to be eaten. The other flowers in the garden were scared too. A nice bird saw the dangerous bug and knew it was not good. The bird flew down and picked up the bug in its beak. The bird took the bug far away from the garden. The rose and the other flowers were happy and safe. The dangerous bug was gone, and the garden was a happy place again. And that is how the story ends.

John and his mom were out for a walk. John wanted to run ahead, but mom said no. John was getting impatient. He frowned, and started hopping from one foot to the other. Mom pointed to a field, and said that if he could count to five, then he could go and play. John was excited. He started counting, going really fast. When he reached three, mom stopped him. She said he had to count slowly so that she could see. John frowned and started counting again, but still too fast. Mom said they needed to practice counting. They walked around the field and counted numbers together. John focused and followed mom's lead. When they were finished counting, mom smiled and said John could now go and play in the field. John happily ran off, happy that he had learned how to count numbers.

Once upon a time, there was a big red coat. The coat was in a room with a small cat. The cat liked to play with the coat. One day, the cat saw the coat lie on the floor. The cat thought it would be fun to jump on the coat. The cat jumped on the coat and it made a loud noise. The noise was so loud that it woke up a big dog. The dog was in the next room. The dog was dangerous and did not like the cat. The dog ran into the room to see what made the noise. The cat was scared of the dog. The cat knew the dog was dangerous. The cat had to find a way to be safe. The cat jumped on the coat again and the coat moved. The coat moved and covered the cat. The dog could not see the cat. The cat was safe under the coat. The dog went away and the cat was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to eat cereal every day. One day, Mia saw a big box of cereal on the top shelf. She wanted to start eating it right away. Mia had a friend named Ben. Ben was very flexible. He could stretch and reach things that were high up. Mia asked Ben for help to get the cereal box. Ben said, "I will help you, but first, we need to find something to stand on." Mia and Ben found a small chair. Ben stood on the chair and stretched his long, flexible arms. He grabbed the cereal box and gave it to Mia. Mia was so happy! She knew that her friend Ben would help her. Mia and Ben sat down to eat the cereal. They knew they made a great team. From that day on, Mia and Ben always helped each other. They knew that with a little help, they could reach anything they wanted.

One day, a boy named Tim was very restless. He wanted to play with his mom's phone. Tim's mom said, "No, you cannot play with my phone." But Tim did not listen. When mom was not looking, Tim took the phone. He played with it and had fun. Then, something unexpected happened. Tim dropped the phone into a big puddle of water. The phone started to soak! Tim was scared. He did not want mom to be mad. He picked up the phone and tried to dry it. But it was too late. The phone did not work anymore. Tim felt bad and told his mom what happened. His mom was not mad. She said, "It's okay, Tim. We can get a new phone. But next time, please listen when I say no."
