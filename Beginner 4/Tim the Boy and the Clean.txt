Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Clean

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a smelly sack. His mom told him, "Tim, prepare to clean your smelly sack today." Tim did not want to clean his smelly sack. He wanted to play with his toys. But his mom said, "You must clean it now, or you cannot play with your toys." Tim was sad, but he knew he had to clean his smelly sack. He took soap and water and started to clean the sack. As he cleaned, he found a toy he thought he lost. He was happy to find it. In the end, Tim's smelly sack was clean, and he could play with his toys. He learned that it was good to clean and take care of his things. And he played happily ever after with his clean sack and toys.

One day, a busy fox was walking in the woods. He had a lot of things to do. He saw a big tree with a little hole in it. The fox thought, "I want to own this tree for my home." A little bird lived in the tree. The bird said, "This is my home. You can't own it." The fox said, "I am big, and you are small. I will own this tree, and you will find a new home." The bird was sad, but she flew away to find a new home. The fox was happy to own the tree. He went inside the hole and made it his home. But, one day, the tree fell down. The fox lost his home, and the bird did not have a home either. They were both sad.

One day, a little girl named Lucy was very enthusiastic to make a salad. She went to her mom and said, "Mom, can we make a salad?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, Lucy, let's make a salad together." They took out lettuce, tomatoes, and cucumbers. Lucy and her mom washed and cut the vegetables. They put them in a big bowl and mixed them up. Then, they put the salad on a plate. Lucy said, "Look, mom! We made a salad!" They were both happy and proud to display their salad on the table. They sat down and ate the salad together. It was yummy and they enjoyed it a lot.

Once there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was 3 years old and he loved to explore. One day, Tom decided to go exploring in his back yard. Tom found a big vase in the grass. He was so excited! Tom picked up the vase and then his mom saw him. She came running over to Tom and said in a very serious voice, "Tom, don't touch that vase. It's very breakable and you can hurt yourself". Tom was sorry and he put the vase back down. His mom looked at him with a smile and said, "It was nice of you to listen. That's very important". Then she gave Tom a hug and he felt happy. Tom learned an important lesson that day. He should always listen to his mom's warnings.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man. He had a big house and lots of toys. One day, he saw a cactus outside his house. He thought it was very funny. He went to his friend and said, "Look at this funny cactus! I want to make it my friend." His friend laughed and said, "You can't make a cactus your friend! It has sharp points and can't talk or play." The wealthy man was sad, but he still wanted the cactus to be his friend. So, he put the cactus in his house and gave it water and sun. One day, the cactus started to shake and wiggle. Then, it turned into a big green man with no sharp points! The green man said, "Thank you for being my friend and taking care of me. I am a magic cactus. Because you were so nice, I will give you a wish." The wealthy man was very surprised and happy. He wished for more friends to play with. The magic cactus made his wish come true. The wealthy man and his new friends played and had fun every day.
