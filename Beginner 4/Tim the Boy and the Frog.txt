Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Frog

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to go to the park and play with his ball. One day, he saw a big net on the ground. Tim thought it was a very silly net because it was not in the right place. Tim decided to pick up the net and take it to the right place. As he walked with the net, he saw a big hole in the ground. Tim thought, "I will put the net over the hole so no one falls in." Just as Tim put the net over the hole, he heard a strange noise. He looked around and discovered a small, green frog. The frog was very happy because the net saved it from falling in the hole. The frog thanked Tim for being so helpful. Tim felt very proud and happy. He realized that the net was not silly after all. It was there to help him save the frog. From that day on, Tim and the frog became best friends and had many fun adventures together. And they always remembered to look for unexpected ways to help others.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Mia loved to play with her toys and eat spicy food. One day, Mia's mom told her, "Mia, please don't drop your toys on the floor. It's not nice." Mia listened to her mom and didn't drop her toys. Later, Mia saw her dad drop his key. She remembered what her mom said and told her dad, "Dad, don't drop the key. It's not nice." Her dad smiled and said, "Thank you, Mia. You're right." Mia felt happy that she could help her dad. From that day on, Mia always tried to be careful and not drop things. She knew that being careful made her mom and dad happy. The moral of the story is to always listen to your parents and be careful with your things.

The sun was getting ready for bed and the moon was in the sky. Bob and his Mom were out on a walk. Mom said, "Let's take a train home." Bob was so excited. The train was big and shiny. But then, Bob's stomach started to grumble. He was feeling very hungry. As they sat down on the train, Bob noticed something strange. The train was shrinking! It felt just like they were sitting in a toy car. Mom laughed and said, "Don't worry, Bob. We'll still be able to get home on this little train." Little did Bob know that the small train that he was riding would take them to the best ice cream shop in town. The most delicious ice cream he had ever tasted! It was the perfect way to end the night.

One day, a little boy named Tom went out to play. The weather was cool, so he wore a big coat. Tom saw his friend, Sue, and wanted to tell her a secret. He walked up to Sue and whispered in her ear. As Tom told Sue the secret, they both saw a big, round thing in the sky. It was not the sun, but it was bright and shiny. Tom and Sue were very surprised. They did not know what it was, but they wanted to find out. They walked closer to the big, round thing. When they got near, they saw it was a big balloon. The balloon had a note tied to it. The note said, "I am lost. Please help me find my way home." Tom and Sue looked at each other, and they knew they needed to help the balloon. They held hands, and together, they walked with the balloon to find its home.

Once upon a time, there was a big red truck. The truck liked to help people. It was a sunny day with a mild breeze, just perfect for the truck to go out and play. One day, the truck saw a small boy with a toy. The toy was broken and the boy was sad. The truck wanted to help the boy. So, the truck went to find something to fix the toy. The truck found a soft wrap. The truck used the wrap to fix the broken toy. The boy was happy and the truck was happy too. They played all day and became the best of friends. And they lived happily ever after.
