Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Secret

One day, a little girl named Sue saw a red ball in the park. She ran to get it. Sue picked up the ball and saw a small bug on it. The bug had red skin. Sue was not scared of the bug. She said, "Hi, little bug." The bug looked at Sue and said, "Hi, Sue. I can tell you a secret." Sue was very surprised that the bug could talk. She asked, "What is the secret?" The bug said, "If you let me go, I will give you a big gift." Sue let the bug go, and it flew away. She waited for her gift. Suddenly, the red ball started to grow bigger and bigger. It was now as big as a house! Sue was so happy. She played with the big red ball all day long.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He wanted to play with the other kids. He saw a girl named Sue. Tim wanted to introduce himself to Sue. He walked up to her and said, "Hi, I'm Tim. Want to play?" Sue looked at Tim and saw that he was wearing a big, funny boot. She started to laugh at him. Tim felt embarrassed. He didn't know what to do. He tried to play with Sue, but she kept laughing at his boot. Tim felt very sad. He went home and told his mom about the bad day he had. His mom hugged him and said, "Don't worry, Tim. Tomorrow will be better." But Tim knew that tomorrow he would still have to wear the big, funny boot.

Once upon a time, in a bright sunny land, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his friends. They would run, jump, and have lots of fun together. One day, Tim wanted to improve his game of catch. He asked his friends to help him. They all played catch together, and Tim got better and better at it. They all laughed and had so much fun. As the sun went down, they knew it was time to go home. Tim thanked his friends for helping him improve. They all smiled and said, "That's what friends are for!" From that day on, they had even more fun playing together in the bright sunny land.

One day, a boy named Tim had a brilliant idea. He wanted to pour water on his dad's head and take a picture with his camera. Tim thought it would be very funny. He took a big cup and filled it with water. Then, he went to find his dad. Tim saw his dad sitting on a chair. He tiptoed behind him, ready to pour the water. But, just as he was about to pour it, his dad turned around and said, "What are you doing, Tim?" Tim got scared and the water splashed all over the floor. Tim felt sad, but his dad just laughed. He said, "Let's try again, but this time, I will pour the water on you!" Tim thought it was a great idea. They went outside, and Tim's dad poured water on Tim's head. Tim's mom took a picture with the camera. They all laughed and had a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lucy. She had a pretty uniform that she wore to school every day. All her friends were envious of her uniform because it was so nice. One day, Lucy's friend, Tom, spilled juice on her uniform. Lucy was sad and upset. Tom said, "I am sorry, Lucy. Please forgive me." Lucy was still sad, but she knew that Tom did not mean to spill the juice. Lucy forgave Tom and they stayed good friends. Lucy's mom helped clean her uniform, and it looked nice again. Lucy and Tom played together and had fun. They learned that it is important to forgive and be kind to each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim lived in a nice nest made of sticks and wire. The wire helped keep the nest strong and safe. Tim loved his home and always had a big smile on his face. One hot day, Tim saw a new friend. It was a cute bunny named Amy. Amy was very hot and needed some shade. Tim smiled and invited Amy to sit under his tree. They both felt much cooler under the tree. Tim and Amy played all day. They laughed, smiled, and had fun. Even on a hot day, they found ways to be happy together. Tim was glad he had a new friend, and Amy was happy she had found a new place to stay cool.
