Category: Beginner
Title: Mr the Wise Owl and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a great big forest, there lived a wise old owl named Mr. Hoot. Mr. Hoot was known for his wisdom and loved to help others. One day, a little rabbit named Hoppy came to Mr. Hoot with a problem. "Mr. Hoot, I can't find my way home. Can you help me?" asked Hoppy. Mr. Hoot agreed to help and told Hoppy to follow him. As they walked through the forest, they saw a big bear named Grumpy. Grumpy wanted to eat Hoppy, but Mr. Hoot had an idea. "Grumpy, if you let Hoppy go, I will share my wisdom with you," said Mr. Hoot. Grumpy thought about it and decided to surrender. He let Hoppy go and listened to Mr. Hoot's wise words. Grumpy learned that being kind was better than being mean. In the end, Hoppy found his way home, and Grumpy became a nicer bear. The forest was a happier place because of Mr. Hoot's wisdom. The moral of the story is that kindness can change even the grumpiest of hearts.

Lily and Max were helping Mom and Dad decorate the big green tree in the living room. They had a box of shiny ornaments that they could hang on the branches. Lily liked the round ones that looked like balls. Max liked the ones that had shapes like stars and snowflakes. "Look, Lily, I found a modern ornament!" Max said, holding up a silver one that had wires and lights. "It can twist and blink and make sounds. Do you want to try it?" Lily nodded and took the ornament from Max. She pressed a button and the ornament started to twist and blink and make sounds. It sounded like a robot. Lily giggled and hung it on a branch near the top of the tree. "Wow, that's cool!" Mom said, coming over to see. "Where did you get that ornament, Max?" "Dad gave it to me. He said it was a special gift from his work. He said they make things like this in the future." Max said, proudly. "That's very nice of him. And very clever of you to find a place for it on the tree. It looks very modern and festive." Mom said, smiling. Lily and Max smiled too. They liked their modern ornament. They liked all the ornaments. They liked decorating the tree with Mom and Dad. They liked Christmas.

Lily loves her dog, Max. Max is big and brown and soft. Lily likes to rub Max's fur and play with him. Max likes to lick Lily's face and wag his tail. One day, Lily and Max go to the park with Mom. The park has grass and trees and flowers. Lily and Max run and jump and have fun. Mom sits on a bench and watches them. "Look, Max, look!" Lily says. She sees a pink balloon in the air. It is shiny and pretty. Lily wants the balloon. She runs after it. Max follows her. "Wait, Lily, wait!" Mom says. She sees a road near the balloon. The road has cars and trucks and buses. The road is dangerous. Mom gets up and runs after Lily and Max. Lily and Max do not hear Mom. They only see the balloon. They get closer and closer to the road. The balloon is almost in Lily's hand. She reaches for it. But then, Max sees a big truck coming. The truck is loud and fast and scary. Max barks and jumps. He pushes Lily away from the road. He saves Lily. But the truck hits the balloon. The balloon pops. The pink pieces fly everywhere. Lily and Max are sad. They wanted the balloon. Mom catches up with Lily and Max. She hugs them and kisses them. She is happy they are safe. She is proud of Max. "Good boy, Max, good boy!" Mom says. She rubs Max's fur and gives him a treat. Max wags his tail and licks Mom's face. Lily smiles and hugs Max. She says, "Thank you, Max, thank you! You are my best friend. I love you, Max, I love you!" Max barks and licks Lily's face. He loves Lily, too. He does not care about the balloon. He is happy with Lily and Mom. They are his family.
