Category: Beginner
Title: Lisa the Girl and the Blocks

One day, a little girl named Lisa wanted to play with her toy truck. She thought it would be fun to load the truck with her toy blocks. She was very organized and put the blocks in the truck one by one. Lisa was happy and said, "Look, my truck is full of blocks!" Lisa's little brother, Tim, saw her playing and wanted to join. He asked, "Can I play too?" Lisa said, "Yes, but we need to be quiet. Baby sister is having her wake." They played together, loading and unloading the toy truck with blocks. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise. It was their baby sister! She had woken up and knocked over all the blocks. Lisa and Tim looked at each other and laughed. The unexpected had happened, but they didn't mind. They decided to load the blocks again, this time with their baby sister joining in the fun.

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to play with cars and balls. One day, they go to the park with Mom and Dad. They see a big slide and a swing. They want to try them. Tom goes first. He climbs up the slide and slides down fast. He laughs and says, "That was fun!" Sam goes next. He slides down too, but he falls off at the end. He hurts his knee and cries. Mom and Dad run to him and hug him. They say, "It's okay, Sam. You are brave. Let's put a band-aid on your knee." Tom feels sorry for Sam. He says, "I'm sorry, Sam. Do you want to play with me?" Sam nods and smiles. He says, "Yes, Tom. Thank you. You are a good friend." They go to the swing and take turns. They push each other and fly high. They feel happy and healthy. They play until the sun goes down. Mom and Dad say, "It's time to go home, boys. You had a lot of fun today." Tom and Sam say, "Yes, we did. We love the park." They pack their cars and balls and say goodbye to the slide and the swing. They have some left for tomorrow. They walk home with Mom and Dad. They hold hands and talk about their day. They say, "We had a good day. We learned how to slide and swing. We helped each other and shared. We are good friends." Mom and Dad say, "We are proud of you, boys. You are kind and smart. You are good sons." They hug and kiss them and say, "We love you, boys. Good night." Tom and Sam say, "We love you, Mom and Dad. Good night." They go to bed and dream of the park. The end.

One day, an elephant went to play. The elephant saw a fragile toy. The toy was pretty. The elephant wanted to play with the toy. But the elephant knew it was big and strong. The toy was small and weak. The elephant did not want to break the toy. It wanted to play with it. The elephant thought for a moment. It had an idea. The elephant went to find a friend. The friend was a tiny ant. The elephant asked the ant to play with the toy. The ant was small and could not break the toy. The ant and the elephant played together. They did not break the toy. They had a lot of fun. In the end, the elephant and the ant were happy. They learned that they could play together. They took care of the fragile toy. The toy stayed pretty and safe. The elephant and the ant were good friends.

Once there was a boy named Joe. He was a very curious 3 year old. One day, Joe was looking around his apartment building and he noticed an elevator. He was so amazed by it! But when he walked up to it, he realized it was broken. He felt so sad that something so cool was not working. Joe's dad noticed his sadness, so he put his arm around Joe's shoulders and told him that even though the elevator was broken, it still had value. Joe looked up at his dad and asked what he meant. His dad explained that the elevator was still valuable because it played an important role in the apartment building. It helps people go from one place to another quickly and easily. Joe liked this explanation and he started to smile. He realized that there are many things that are valuable even if they are broken. Joe's dad said that he should always remember to value the things that he had, no matter if they were broken or not. Joe smiled and hugged his dad. From that day, Joe always remembered to value things, even when they were broken.
