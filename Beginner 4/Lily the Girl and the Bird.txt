Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with a big chimney. One day, Lily saw a bird trying to go into the chimney. The bird was very small and was having a hard time. Lily felt bad for the bird and wanted to help. Lily called out to the bird, "Hey bird, don't go in the chimney! It's dirty and you'll get stuck!" The bird looked at her and felt ashamed. It didn't want to be dirty or stuck. So, the bird flew down and landed on Lily's shoulder. Together, they went to the park and played all day. The bird was so happy that it didn't go into the chimney. And Lily was happy because she made a new friend. From that day on, Lily and the bird were the best of friends and they always looked out for each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One day, he saw a deep hole in the ground. He wanted to know what was inside. Tim went to his mom and asked, "What is in the deep hole?" His mom did not know. So, Tim went to his dad and asked the same thing. His dad did not know either. They all went to look at the hole together. When they got to the hole, they saw a little bird stuck inside. Tim's dad found a long stick and used it to help the bird. The bird was very happy. Tim learned that it is good to ask for help when you don't know something. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. It was very cold, and he started to shiver. His mom saw him and said, "Tim, if you promise to wear your big coat, I have an incredible surprise for you." Tim put on his big coat and said, "I promise, Mom!" He was excited for the surprise. They walked to the park, and Tim saw something amazing. There was a big, beautiful ice castle! Tim's eyes got big and he smiled. His mom said, "This is your surprise! You can play in the ice castle, but remember to keep your big coat on." Tim had so much fun playing in the ice castle. He was warm in his big coat and didn't shiver anymore. He was happy he made the promise to his mom. The incredible ice castle was a day he would never forget.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a cat stuck in a tree. The cat looked scared. Tim wanted to help the cat, so he called his friend, Sam. "Sam, there is a cat in the tree. Let's help it get down," Tim said. Sam came running to help. They tried to reach the cat, but they were too short. Sam had an idea. He picked up a stick and used it to release the cat's tail from the branch. The cat was free and jumped down. The cat was very happy. It gave Tim and Sam a big rub with its head. "Thank you for helping me," the cat said. Tim and Sam felt proud. They knew they did a good thing. But then, a stupid bird came and took the cat's toy. The cat was sad again. Tim and Sam promised to help the cat get its toy back. They all became good friends and played together in the park every day.

One day, a girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a big slide and wanted to play. Lily ran to the slide and started to climb up. When she got to the top, she saw a big zip on the slide. Lily was scared, but she knew she could do it. She sat down and zipped down the slide very fast. It was so much fun! But then, something unexpected happened. A little boy came to the slide and started to cry. He was ashamed because he was too scared to slide. Lily wanted to help him. She held his hand and they went down the slide together. The boy stopped crying and smiled. Lily was happy she could help her new friend.
