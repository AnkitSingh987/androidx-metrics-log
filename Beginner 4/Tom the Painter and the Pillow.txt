Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Painter and the Pillow

Once upon a time, there was a painter named Tom. He liked to paint pretty pictures. One day, he felt uncomfortable. His chair was too hard. He wanted to make it better. Tom thought, "I know! I will improve my chair with paint!" So, he painted his chair with many colors. But, it was still uncomfortable. Tom was sad. Then, his friend, Sue, came to visit. She saw Tom's sad face and asked, "Why are you sad?" Tom told her about his uncomfortable chair. Sue said, "I can help!" She found a soft pillow and put it on Tom's chair. Tom sat on the pillow and smiled. His chair was no longer uncomfortable. Tom and Sue painted pretty pictures together, happy and comfy.

One day, a small bird was in its nest. It was going to rain. The bird felt helpless because it could not fly yet. It saw a big tree and wanted to go there to stay dry. The bird saw a cat and asked, "Can you help me get to the big tree?" The cat said, "Yes, I can help you." The bird was happy and let the cat help it. But when they got to the big tree, the cat ate the bird. The rain came and the cat stayed dry. The bird was gone. The moral of the story is to not trust everyone who offers help.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to run and play outside. One day, he saw a big, fierce dog near his house. The dog had a big frame and looked scary. Tim wanted to be friends with the dog, but he was afraid. Tim's mom said, "Don't be scared. Just be nice and the dog will be nice too." Tim listened to his mom and went to the dog. He said, "Hi dog, I want to be friends." The dog wagged its tail and smiled. Tim and the dog played together. They ran and had fun. But Tim didn't see a big rock in the grass. He stumbled on the rock and fell down. The dog came to help Tim. It licked Tim's face and made him feel better. Tim learned that even if something looks scary, it can be nice if you are nice to it. And that was the day Tim made a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a new rock. This rock was special because it could provide a magic mineral. The magic mineral made people happy. The rock lived in a big forest with many friends. One day, a little bird found the rock. The bird was sad because it could not fly high. The rock wanted to help the bird. It gave the magic mineral to the bird. The bird ate the mineral and felt happy. It tried to fly high, but it still could not. The rock felt bad and wanted to find a way to help the bird. It talked to its friend, the wise tree. The wise tree said that the magic mineral could not help the bird fly high. The magic mineral only made people happy. The rock had an idea. It asked the bird to bring its friends to the forest. The bird brought its friends to the forest. The rock provided the magic mineral to all the birds. They all felt happy. The birds sang and played together. They did not worry about flying high anymore. The rock was happy too, because it could help its new friends feel happy. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was always alert and loved to play with his gold toy car. One day, he took his car to the park to play. At the park, Tim met a girl named Sue. She saw Tim's gold car and said, "Wow, your car is pretty. Can I play with it?" Tim liked Sue and wanted to share. So, they played together with the gold car. After a while, a big dog came running to them. It wanted to play too. The dog took the gold car in its mouth and ran away. Tim and Sue yelled, "Stop, dog! That's our car!" But the dog didn't listen. It ran far away and they never saw the gold car again. Tim and Sue were very sad.
