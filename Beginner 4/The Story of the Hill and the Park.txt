Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Hill and the Park

Once upon a time, there were two brothers living in a small house. One day, the brothers decided to go outside. So, they hopped in their van and drove to the nearby park. When they arrived, the brothers were amazed by all of the icy snow that was around them. The older brother said: "Wow, it's so icy and cold outside!" The younger brother said: "Yes! Let's have some fun!" So, they got out of the van and started to build the biggest snowman they had ever seen. They used icy snow to make a big and sturdy body. Then, they used stones and twigs to make the face. The older brother said: "It looks awesome!" The younger brother said: "Let's go sledding!" So, they grabbed their sleds and went to the icy hill nearby. They had lots of fun sliding down the hill together. They laughed and made a lot of noise! In the end, everyone was very happy. The brothers went home in their van and the family lived happily ever after.

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to run, jump and slide on the grass and the swings. They saw a big orange on the ground. It looked juicy and sweet. "Can we eat it?" Lily asked Tom. "I don't know. Maybe it is someone's snack. We should ask first." Tom said. They looked around, but they did not see anyone. The orange was alone on the grass. "Maybe they forgot it. Let's try it." Lily said. She picked up the orange and peeled it. She gave half to Tom and kept half for herself. They took a big bite of the orange. But it was not juicy and sweet. It was sour and bitter. It made their faces twist and their tongues hurt. "Yuck! This is disgusting!" Tom spat out the orange. "Me too! This is not a good orange!" Lily spat out the orange too. They threw the orange away and ran to the water fountain. They drank some water to wash away the bad taste. They learned a lesson. They should not eat things they find on the ground. They should ask first. Or better, they should bring their own snacks.

---

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump and slide on the swings. But Lily saw a big hill with green grass and wanted to roll down it. She said to Ben, "Come on, let's roll down the hill. It will be fun!" Ben was not sure. He looked at the hill and saw some rocks and sticks. He said to Lily, "No, I don't want to roll down the hill. It might hurt. Look, there are rocks and sticks. They can scratch and poke us." Lily did not listen to Ben. She said, "You are scared. I am not scared. I will roll down the hill and you will see how fun it is." She ran to the top of the hill and lay down on the grass. She started to roll down, faster and faster. But Lily did not see a big rock in the grass. She rolled over it and bumped her head. She cried out, "Ow, ow, ow! That hurt!" She stopped rolling and held her head. She felt a bump and some blood. Ben heard Lily cry and ran to her. He saw her head and said, "Oh no, Lily, are you okay? You have a boo-boo. You should have listened to me. The hill was not safe. Come on, let's go to the nurse. She will help you." Lily felt sorry and said, "I'm sorry, Ben. You were right. The hill was not fun. It was hurt. I should have listened to you. Thank you for being my friend. Can you help me get up?" Ben nodded and said, "Of course, Lily. I'm your friend. I will help you. Come on, let's go." He helped Lily get up and they walked to the nurse. They did not complain anymore. They learned to be careful and to listen to each other.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable train named Tom. Tom was a steam train who loved to deliver things. Every day, he would chug along the tracks, carrying big boxes from one town to another. One sunny day, Tom met a little bird named Bella. Bella was looking for her lost nest. Tom wanted to help Bella, so he said, "Choo choo! I can deliver you to your nest, Bella. Hop on!" Bella hopped onto Tom's back, and they went on a fun adventure. They chugged through the forest, over the hills, and across the river. Finally, they found Bella's nest high up in a tree. "Thank you, Tom, for helping me find my nest!" chirped Bella. "You are a very dependable friend." Tom smiled and chugged away, happy to have helped his new friend. And from that day on, Tom and Bella were the best of friends, always there to help each other when they needed it.
