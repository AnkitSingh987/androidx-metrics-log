Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Bossy Cat and the Mouse

Once upon a time, there was a big bossy cat named Tom. He lived in a pretty house with his kind friend, Sarah. Sarah liked to sew and had many pins. Tom loved to play with the pins, but Sarah told him, "No, Tom! Pins are not toys." One day, Sarah went out to buy food. Tom saw a shiny pin on the table. He thought, "I will play with it, just for a little bit." But when he tried to pick up the pin, it poked his paw. "Ouch!" cried Tom. He felt bad and thought, "I should have listened to Sarah." Then, a small mouse came into the house. The mouse saw the pin and wanted to play with it too. Tom knew it was not safe, so he said, "No, mouse! Pins are not toys." The mouse refused to listen and tried to play with the pin. But Tom quickly hid the pin away. The mouse was sad, but Tom said, "Let's play with my ball instead." They played together and had lots of fun. When Sarah came home, she saw Tom and the mouse playing. She was very happy and proud of Tom for being a good friend. Tom learned that it is important to listen and help others. And from that day on, Tom never played with pins again.

One day, a young boy named Tim went for a walk. He liked to walk when the sun was out. It was a nice day, and the sun was big and bright in the sky. Tim walked and walked until he saw a big tree. He decided to sit under the tree and rest. While Tim was sitting, he saw a small bug. The bug was walking on the tree. Tim looked at the bug and said, "Hi bug! Do you like the sun too?" The bug looked at Tim and said, "Yes, Tim. I like the sun. It makes me happy." Tim and the bug talked and talked. They had fun under the tree. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the bug away. Tim was sad. He looked for the bug, but he could not find it. The sun went down, and it was time for Tim to go home. He walked home, thinking about his new friend, the bug. But he knew he would never see the bug again.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was an enormous tree. Many animals lived near the tree. They played together and had fun. They were a happy group of friends. One day, a new little bird came to the forest. The bird was shy and did not talk much. The group of friends did not play with the little bird. They ignored the little bird because he was different. After some time, there was a big storm. The enormous tree fell down. All the animals were sad and scared. But the little bird knew how to build a new home for everyone. The group of friends learned that they should not ignore anyone because they are different. Everyone can help and be a friend.

Mother and son were walking through the park on a cool, sunny day. They had seen many things ashore the walk, including some modern and colourful buildings. The son was in awe of it all and wanted to run in and explore more. But, mother held his hand tightly, not wanting him to get lost. Suddenly, the son saw something shiny sticking out of the ground. He let go of his mother's hand and ran as fast as his small legs could carry him to it. It was a small skull! The son tried to pick it up and take it home, but mother stopped him. Mother: "No, dear. It's too dangerous. We must leave it here." The son let out a big sigh, disappointed that he couldn't keep the skull. He kept the skull in sight until they walked out of the park.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot liked to play with his ball. He would run and jump to catch the ball. Spot had many friends. They were all different, but they liked to play together. One day, Spot and his friends went to the park. They played a fun game of catch. They ran and laughed together. After a while, they got tired and sat down to rest. Spot's mom came to the park with a treat for them. The treat was a big bowl of yummy ice cream. All the friends were so happy. They ate the ice cream and shared it with each other. The ice cream was cold and sweet. Spot and his friends loved the treat. After they ate the ice cream, they played more games. They played until the sun went down. Then, they said goodbye to each other and went home. Spot was very happy that he had such good friends to play with and share treats.
