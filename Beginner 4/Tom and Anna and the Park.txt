Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Anna and the Park

Tom and Anna are playing in the park. They like to hide behind the trees and bushes and say "boo" to each other. They laugh and have fun. Suddenly, the ground starts to shake. It is an earthquake. Tom and Anna are scared. They hear loud noises and see things falling and breaking. They hold hands and run to find their mom and dad. "Mom! Dad! Where are you?" they shout. They see their mom and dad near the swings. They are safe. They hug them and feel better. "Are you okay, my little ones?" their mom asks. "Yes, we are okay. But we were very afraid," Tom says. "Me too," Anna says. Their dad says, "Don't worry, the earthquake is over. We are lucky. Nothing bad happened to us or our home. But some people need help. We should be kind and share what we have with them." Tom and Anna nod. They are glad they have their mom and dad. They are glad they have each other. They are brave and generous. They love their family and their park.

Once upon a time, there was a little child named Tim. Tim was very flexible. He could bend and stretch in many ways. One day, Tim was playing in his room when he saw his toys were all over the floor. Tim's mom came into the room and said, "Tim, you need to clean your room. It is very messy." Tim looked at the toys and said, "Okay, mom. I will clean my room now." Tim started to pick up his toys. Because he was so flexible, he could reach the toys under the bed without a problem. He also could lift his leg high to step over the toys without stepping on them. Tim's mom was very happy to see the clean room. She said, "Thank you, Tim. You did a great job!" Tim smiled and said, "You're welcome, mom!" And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy yet. One sunny day, Tim went outside to play. He saw a big ice block and thought it would be fun to play with it. Tim pushed the ice block and said, "Wow, it's so cold!" He played with the ice block all day. The sun was hot, and the ice block started to melt. Tim saw the water and said, "Uh-oh, it's not dry anymore." Tim went inside to ask his mom for help. She said, "Don't worry, Tim. The sun will dry it up soon." Tim went back outside and saw that the ice block was gone. He looked around and saw his yet was dry. He smiled and said, "Mom was right!" Then, he played with his yet and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys and have fun. One day, she was playing with her toy car when she saw the number zero on it. Lily was curious about the number zero and wanted to learn more. Lily asked her mom, "Mom, what is zero?" Her mom smiled and said, "Zero means nothing, Lily. It's when you have no toys, no cookies, or no friends." Lily was scared to have zero of anything. She wanted to have at least one toy, one cookie, and one friend. Lily's mom told her, "If you behave and listen to me, you will never have zero of anything." So, Lily decided to behave and be a good girl. She listened to her mom and shared her toys with her friends. And from that day on, Lily was never scared of having zero of anything because she knew how to behave.

Tom and Anna are friends. They like to play outside in the sun. But sometimes the sun is too hot and they feel thirsty. They look for a place to rest and drink some water. They see a big tree with many green leaves. The tree makes a nice shade under it. Tom and Anna run to the tree and sit down. They drink some water from their bottles and feel better. "Thank you, tree, for your shade," Tom says. He talks to the tree like it is a person. He likes to talk to plants and animals. "You're welcome, Tom," the tree says. The tree talks back to Tom. It likes to talk to children who are kind and respectful. Anna is surprised. She did not know that trees can talk. She wants to talk to the tree too. She asks the tree a question. "Hello, tree. How are you today?" Anna says. "Hello, Anna. I'm fine, thank you. How are you?" the tree says. "I'm fine too. You are a very helpful tree. You give us shade and fresh air. Do you need anything from us?" Anna says. The tree smiles. It is happy that Anna is polite and caring. It tells Anna what it needs. "I need water and sunlight to grow and stay healthy. You can help me by not wasting water and not cutting my branches. You can also help me by planting more trees and flowers. They are my friends and they make the world more beautiful," the tree says. Tom and Anna nod. They agree with the tree. They want to help the tree and the other plants and animals. They promise to do their best. "Thank you, Tom and Anna. You are very good friends. You can come and visit me anytime. I will always give you shade and talk to you," the tree says. Tom and Anna hug the tree. They thank the tree again. They say goodbye and go back to their play. They are happy and grateful. They have learned something new and made a new friend.
