Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Ball and the Dog

One day, a big bossy cat and a little happy dog went to play near a stream. The cat liked to act like she was the boss of the dog. The dog just wanted to play and have fun. While they were playing, they saw a ball in the stream. They both wanted the ball. The bossy cat said, "I am the boss, so I get the ball!" The little dog was sad but still wanted to play with the ball. The dog had an idea. He said, "Let's act like we are in a race. Whoever gets the ball first can play with it." The bossy cat agreed. They both ran fast to get the ball. The little dog was faster and got the ball first. The bossy cat was not happy, but she learned that it is more fun to play together than to be bossy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her blocks. She had big blocks, small blocks, and smooth blocks. She would build tall towers with her blocks every day. One day, Sue built a very tall tower. It was so tall that it reached the sky. Sue was very happy with her tower. But she saw a little bird on top of the tower. The bird looked like it might fall. Sue was worried about the bird. Sue called her friend, Tom, to help her. Tom was very good at climbing. He climbed the tall tower to help the little bird. He was very careful not to let the bird fall. Tom helped the bird fly away safely. Sue was happy that her friend could help. They played with the blocks and built more towers together, but not too tall this time.

Once upon a time, there was a big farm. On the farm, there was a lot of wheat. The wheat was very impressive. It was tall and strong. The farmer was very happy with his wheat. One day, a little boy came to the farm. He saw the impressive wheat and wanted to play in it. He ran into the wheat and started to play. The farmer saw the little boy and said, "Stop! You will hurt the wheat!" The little boy did not listen. He kept playing in the wheat. The farmer was sad. The wheat was not strong anymore. The little boy felt regret. He said, "I am sorry, farmer." But it was too late. The wheat was gone, and the farmer was sad. The end.

Lily and Max were playing in the park. They liked to run, slide and swing. They also liked to look for things in the grass and the sand. "Look, Lily, I found a shiny thing!" Max said, holding up a metal ring. "Wow, Max, that is pretty!" Lily said. "Can I see?" Max gave Lily the ring. She put it on her finger and smiled. "Look, Max, I found something too!" Lily said, pointing to a pair of sunglasses on a bench. "Let's go see!" Max said, running after Lily. They reached the bench and picked up the sunglasses. They were big and black and had yellow lenses. "Wow, Lily, these are cool!" Max said, trying them on. "They make everything look yellow!" Lily said, taking them from Max and putting them on. They laughed and took turns wearing the sunglasses. They pretended to be spies and detectives. "Who left these here?" Max wondered. "Maybe someone forgot them," Lily said. "Or maybe they are a clue!" Max said. "A clue for what?" Lily asked. "I don't know, let's find out!" Max said. They looked around the bench for more clues. They found a bottle of water, a hat and a book. "Maybe these belong to the same person," Lily said. "Maybe they are thirsty, hot and like to read," Max said. "Or maybe they are hiding something!" Lily said. "Like what?" Max asked. "I don't know, let's discover!" Lily said. They opened the book and looked inside. They saw words and pictures. They could not read the words, but they liked the pictures. They saw animals, plants and places. "Wow, Lily, this is a book about the world!" Max said. "Wow, Max, this is amazing!" Lily said. They forgot about the sunglasses and the clues. They sat on the bench and looked at the book. They learned about lions, roses and mountains. They had fun and felt happy. The end.
