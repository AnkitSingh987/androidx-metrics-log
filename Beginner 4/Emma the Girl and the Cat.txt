Category: Beginner
Title: Emma the Girl and the Cat

One day, a little girl named Emma found a dark tube in her room. She did not know what it was for. Emma showed it to her mom. Her mom said, "Be careful with that, it can move things." Emma went outside to play. She saw her friend, Tom, and showed him the dark tube. "Watch this," she said, and pointed the tube at a ball. The ball started to move! Tom was very surprised. They took turns using the tube to move things around. Later, they saw a cat stuck in a tree. The cat was scared and could not get down. Emma remembered how the dark tube could move things. She pointed it at the cat and the cat gently moved down to the ground. The cat was happy and so were Emma and Tom. They knew that the dark tube was a special tool that could help them and others.

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. He wore his favorite gray pants. He saw his friend Sam playing with a water spray. Tim wanted to play with the water spray too. Tim asked Sam, "Can I play with the water spray?" Sam said, "No, it's mine!" Tim was sad. He wanted to have fun with the water spray. He thought about what to do. Then, Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's take turns with the water spray!" Sam thought about it and said, "Okay!" They both played with the water spray and had lots of fun. Tim was happy, and so was Sam.

Once upon a time, there was a lively umbrella. It was very happy. The umbrella loved to be open when it rained. It helped people stay dry. One day, the umbrella went for a walk with a little girl. The girl was happy too. They jumped in puddles and laughed. The umbrella was very good at keeping the girl dry. At the end of the day, the girl and the umbrella went home. They were tired but happy. The umbrella knew it was a good friend. It could not wait to go out and play again.

Once upon a time there was a little boy called Michael. Every morning, his Mom would give him a big hug and ask him how he was feeling. "I'm feeling lovely, Mom!" he said one day. His Mom smiled and said "That's wonderful! Could you please get the thermometer? I want to check how you feeling". Michael ran to the bathroom, where he found a shiny thermometer. He brought it back to his Mom and said excitedly "Here it is!" His Mom took the thermometer, put it under his tongue and smiled. After doing this, she hugged Michael and said "Thank you. You are a lovely boy". Michael smiled and hugged his Mom back. He was so happy that his Mom loved him so much. Together they hugged and laughed and shared their lovely feeling.

Once upon a time, there was a big, dependable dog named Max. Max loved to play near the water. One day, Max saw a surf board. He wanted to try it. Max's friend, a little bird named Sam, said, "Max, you should resist. It might be too hard." But Max wanted to try. He got on the surf board and started to surf. Max had so much fun! As Max surfed, Sam watched him. He saw that Max was doing a great job. Sam said, "Wow, Max! You are good at this!" Max was happy that he did not resist. He had a fun day with his friend, Sam.

Once upon a time, there was a man named Tom. Tom had a friend named Sam. Sam was jealous of Tom's big, red ball. One day, Sam thought of a plan to surprise Tom. Sam went to Tom's house and said, "Let's play hide and seek!" Tom was happy and said, "Okay! You hide, and I will find you." Sam ran to hide behind a tree, and Tom began to count. While Tom was counting, Sam quickly took the big, red ball and hid it behind the tree. When Tom found Sam, he was surprised to see his ball was gone. Sam laughed and said, "I hid your ball to surprise you!" Tom was sad, but then Sam said, "I'm sorry, let's play together with your ball." Tom smiled, and they played happily together.
