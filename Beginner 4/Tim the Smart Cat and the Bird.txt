Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Smart Cat and the Bird

One day, a smart cat named Tim went to school. At school, Tim had a quiz. He wanted to do well on the quiz so he could get a treat. Tim's friend, a dog named Sam, also wanted to do well on the quiz. During the quiz, Tim said to Sam, "Sam, I need help. Can you tell me the answer?" Sam said, "No Tim, I cannot. We must do the quiz on our own." Suddenly, a loud noise came from outside. A big bird had come to the school and opened the door. The bird wanted to release all the animals from the school. The bird said, "Come with me, animals! Let's go play outside!" All the animals left the school to play with the bird. The quiz was forgotten. The teacher, a wise owl, smiled and said, "Sometimes, it is more important to have fun and be free than to worry about a quiz."

Once upon a time, there was an old house. In the house, there was a ghost. The ghost was friendly and liked to play with the kids who lived nearby. The kids were not scared of the ghost because they knew it was nice. One day, a new kid came to play. The ghost wanted to be friends with the new kid too. The other kids urge the new kid to play with the ghost. They said, "Don't be scared, the ghost is nice!" The new kid was brave and played with the ghost. They all had fun together. The old ghost was happy to have a new friend. From that day on, the kids and the ghost played together every day. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a race. Two kids wanted to compete in the race. They were both excited. "Ready, set, go!" shouted the man. The race had begun. The kids ran down the track. One of the kids was a bit faster, and he started to pull ahead. Suddenly, the other kid got mad. He reached out and slapped his friend on the back. "No fair! Let's have a modern race," he said. Both kids smiled and started running together. They reached the finish line at the same time and cheered. "We won!" they said together.

One day, a clown came to play. He could act and dance in a graceful way. All the kids liked to watch him. They laughed and clapped their hands. The clown was happy to see the kids smile. But then, a big wind came. It blew the clown's hat away. The clown was sad. The kids wanted to help him. They all looked for the hat. The wind made it hard to find. At last, a small girl found the hat. It was on a tree. The clown did not know how to get it. The small girl had an idea. She asked a tall boy to help. The tall boy got the hat from the tree. The clown was happy again. He acted and danced for the kids. They all laughed and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree in a big park. Many kids loved to play near the tree. One sunny day, a little girl named Lily came to the park with her mom. They brought a big basket of laundry to hang on a line near the tall tree. Lily's mom hung the laundry on the line. The wind made the clothes dance in the air. Lily liked to watch the clothes move. She saw a pretty, red dress on the line. She wanted to touch it. Lily tried to reach the red dress, but it was too high. She found a big rock and stood on it. She reached up to touch the dress but she started to slip. Just then, her mom saw her and caught her. Lily was safe and her mom hugged her tight. They laughed and went back to their laundry, hand in hand.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Timmy. Timmy lived in a small nest with his family. One day, Timmy saw a shiny wire near his nest. He wanted to play with it, but his mommy said, "Please, Timmy, stay away from the wire." Timmy was a good bird, so he listened to his mommy. But he still looked at the wire and wondered what it would feel like. Days went by, and Timmy grew more and more curious about the wire. One day, when his mommy was away, Timmy decided to touch the wire. As he got closer, he saw a big cat near the wire. The cat was looking at Timmy with big, hungry eyes. Timmy was frightened and remembered what his mommy had said. Timmy quickly flew back to his nest and hid. The cat tried to catch him but couldn't reach the nest. When Timmy's mommy came back, she saw the cat and chased it away. Timmy realized that his mommy was right, and he should have listened to her from the start. From that day on, Timmy never went near the wire again, and he always listened to his mommy.
