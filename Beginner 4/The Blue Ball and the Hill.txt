Category: Beginner
Title: The Blue Ball and the Hill

Once upon a time, there was a big blue ball. It was so wide that it could roll down the hill very fast. One day, a little girl named Mia saw the blue ball in the park. She wanted to touch it and play with it. Mia ran to the blue ball and touched it with her small hands. She pushed the wide ball and it started to roll down the hill. Mia laughed and clapped her hands as she watched the ball roll faster and faster. At the bottom of the hill, there was a small pond. The wide blue ball splashed into the water. Mia ran down the hill to get her ball back. She was so happy to play with her new blue ball all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a toy dog named Stitch. Mia and Stitch did everything together. They played, they ate, and they slept side by side. Mia thought Stitch was very interesting. One day, Mia's mom gave her a red lead for Stitch. She told Mia, "This will help you take Stitch on walks." Mia was so excited to walk with Stitch. She knew something fun would happen. The next day, Mia took Stitch for a walk with the red lead. They went to the park and saw a big tree. Mia and Stitch played hide and seek around the tree. Suddenly, a real dog came and joined them. The dog was friendly and played with Mia and Stitch. Mia had a great day with her new friend. She knew the red lead was special and would lead her to more interesting adventures.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree. In the tree, there was a whip. The whip did not belong there. One day, a little boy found the whip. The little boy took the whip and played with it. He made a big sound with the whip. He was happy to play with the whip. He knew the whip did not belong in the tree. The little boy took the whip back to his house. He showed his mom and dad. They were happy that the whip was not in the tree. They knew the whip belonged with the little boy. Now, the whip had a home and the little boy had a new toy.

Mama and Tommy were getting ready to go out. Mama was wearing her jacket, which was very comfortable. She zipped up the jacket and asked Tommy, "Are you ready to go, darling?" Tommy put on his own jacket and zipped it up. He said, "Yes, Mama. I'm all zipped up. Let's go!" Mama smiled and took Tommy's hand. She said, "Let's go for a walk in the park. It's a beautiful day." Tommy and Mama stepped outside and enjoyed their walk. They looked at the birds and felt the sunshine on their faces. Everyone was wearing comfortable jackets to keep warm on this chilly day. When they got back home, Mama and Tommy were both feeling happy and refreshed. Mama gave Tommy a big hug and thanked him for making her day so special. Tommy smiled and said, "No problem, Mama. We'll go on a walk together again soon!"

Once upon a time, in a large house, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys and run around the big yard. One day, while playing, Tim saw a small bug on a leaf. He wanted to touch the bug with his finger. Tim's mom saw him and said, "Tim, do not touch the bug. It might not be safe. I will allow you to look at it, but do not touch it with your finger." Tim listened to his mom and just looked at the bug. He saw that the bug had large, pretty wings. The next day, Tim went back to the yard and saw the same bug. This time, the bug had a friend with large wings too! Tim was happy to see the bugs, but he remembered what his mom said. So, he did not touch them with his finger. Tim knew that it was better to just look at the bugs and let them be.
