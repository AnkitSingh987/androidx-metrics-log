Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Song

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to sing. He had a toy microphone that he always played with. He would sing to his friends and family all the time. One day, Tim wanted to sing a new song. He tried very hard, but he had a struggle to learn it. The words were not easy for him. But Tim was a persistent boy. He did not give up. He kept on trying and trying. Finally, after many tries, Tim learned the new song. He was so happy! He picked up his microphone and sang the song to his friends and family. They all clapped and cheered for him. Tim felt proud of himself for not giving up.

One day, a little oyster was in the sea. The oyster was scared because it was lost. It could not find its family. The oyster was very sad and wanted to go home. A big fish saw the scared oyster and wanted to help. The fish had a special power. It could spray water very far. The fish thought it could use its power to help the oyster find its family. The fish and the oyster went on a trip. The fish sprayed water to show the way. They met other sea friends who wanted to help too. They all looked for the oyster's family together. At last, they found the oyster's family. The oyster was very happy and not scared anymore. They all played together and had a great time.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a little boy named Tom. Tom loved to play with his friends in the forest. One day, while playing, they found a big stone. It was so big that they could not lift it. Tom had an idea. He said to his friends, "Let's unite and push the stone together!" They all agreed and started to push the stone. With all their strength, they moved the stone. Behind the stone, they found a dead tree. The tree was old and had no leaves. Tom and his friends decided to plant a new tree next to the dead one. They worked together and soon, the new tree started to grow. The forest became a happy place again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had long hair that she loved to play with. She lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and her deaf dog, Max. Max could not hear, but he could see very well. Sue and Max were best friends. One day, Sue and Max went to the park to play. They played games like catch and hide and seek. While they were playing, Sue's hair got caught in a tree. She tried to get it out, but it was stuck. Sue was scared and didn't know what to do. Max saw Sue's hair in the tree and knew he had to help her. He ran to find a grown-up who could help. Max found Sue's mom and dad, and they recognized that Max wanted them to follow him. They ran to Sue and helped her get her hair out of the tree. Sue was so happy that Max had saved the day. They all went home and had a big hug.

Once upon a time, there was a princess. She was a tough princess. She liked to play and run all day. One day, she found a big ball. The ball was old and not fun to play with. The princess wanted to replace the ball with a new one. The princess went to the store to find a new ball. She saw many balls there. Some were big, some were small. Some were blue, some were red. The princess picked a new ball to replace the old one. She was very happy. But on her way home, the princess tripped and fell. The new ball rolled away into a big hole. The princess was sad. She could not get the ball out. She went home without the ball. The princess was very sad and did not play that day.

Once upon a time there was a small boy called Jack. He liked to stand by the river and watch the boats pass by, wondering what sort of adventures they were going on. One day Jack found a long rod in the grass. He picked it up and looked at it, wondering what it could be used for. He decided to play a game. He put the rod in the river and waited. Suddenly, a fish came and bit the rod. Jack felt so excited! But then he heard a loud voice from down the path. It was Jack's dad. He felt a bit guilty and put the rod back in the grass. He ran off to meet his dad, feeling ashamed for taking something without asking. When Jack's dad arrived, he was surprised to see the rod lying in the grass. He told Jack that it was ok. He had been fishing far away and the rod must have been blown here by the wind. So Jack and his dad went fishing together, and from then on, Jack knew it was ok to take things to play sometimes.
