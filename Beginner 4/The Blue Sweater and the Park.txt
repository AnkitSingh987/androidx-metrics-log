Category: Beginner
Title: The Blue Sweater and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a blue sweater. The blue sweater was very warm and soft. It was friends with a little girl named Lily. Lily loved her blue sweater very much. One day, Lily and her blue sweater went to the park. They played on the swings and the slide. The sun was shining and it was a very happy day. Lily and her blue sweater were having so much fun. As the day went on, it started to get cold. Lily was so glad she had her blue sweater to keep her warm. They went home and had a nice warm cup of milk. Lily and her blue sweater were happy to be together.

Once upon a time, there was a big jug. The jug was very lucky. It lived in a small house with a nice family. The jug was always full of tasty juice. The family loved the jug very much. One day, the jug heard a whisper. It tried to listen. The whisper said, "I am a little bird. I am thirsty. Can you help me?" The lucky jug wanted to help the little bird. So, the jug tipped over just a little bit. Some juice came out for the bird to drink. The little bird was very happy. It said, "Thank you, lucky jug." The bird sang a pretty song for the jug. The jug felt very good. It was happy to help the little bird. The family came and listened to the bird's song. They all felt very lucky to have the jug in their home.

One day, a powerful cat named Zip was playing with his friend, a little dog named Toe. They were running and having fun in the park. Zip loved to run fast, and Toe loved to chase Zip. "Zip, you are very fast!" said Toe. Zip smiled and said, "Thank you, Toe! You are fast too!" Just then, a big wind came and took Zip up into the sky! Toe was scared and didn't know what to do. He thought of a plan to save his friend. Toe ran to find a big kite. He held the kite and the wind took him up too. Toe found Zip in the sky and grabbed his hand. Together, they used the kite to fly back down to the ground. They were happy to be safe and together again. From that day on, Zip and Toe knew they could do anything with their powerful friendship.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a tough face, but she was very nice. Lily liked to play in the park with her friends. One day, Lily and her friend Tim were playing hide and seek. Lily hid behind a big tree. Tim looked and looked but could not find her. He started to panic. "Lily, where are you?" Tim called out. Lily heard Tim's voice and came out from behind the tree. "Here I am, Tim!" she said with a smile. Tim was happy to see her face. They laughed and played some more. And they were the best of friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy phone that he loved to play with. One day, the phone began to ring. Tim was very excited and picked it up. "Hello?" Tim said into the phone. "Who is there?" A voice on the other end asked, "Is your mom available?" Tim did not know what "available" meant, so he asked the voice, "What is 'available'?" The voice explained, "It means your mom is free to talk." Tim thought about it and shared his opinion. "Mom is in the kitchen. I think she can talk." The voice thanked him and asked to speak to his mom. Tim ran to the kitchen and gave the phone to his mom. She talked for a little while and then hung up. She smiled at Tim and said, "Thank you for being so helpful, Tim. You did a great job." Tim felt happy and proud that he could help his mom.

Once upon a time, in an ancient house, there lived a cat and a dog. The cat loved to play with her ball, and the dog loved to run in the yard. They did not play together, but they both loved their home. One day, the cat said to the dog, "I love the way you run in the yard. Can I play with you?" The dog was happy and said, "Yes, come play with me!" But when they tried to play, they could not find a way to play together. The cat wanted to play with her ball, and the dog wanted to run. This made them both sad. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the cat's ball into the yard. The dog saw the ball and started to run after it. The cat saw the dog run and joined him. They both ran and played with the ball together. They found a new way to play and became best friends. And they all lived happily ever after.
