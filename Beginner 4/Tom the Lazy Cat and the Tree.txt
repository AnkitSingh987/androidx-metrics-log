Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Lazy Cat and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. He lived in a small cabin in the woods. Tom liked to sleep all day and did not like to work at all. One day, Tom saw a big tree with a lot of fruit. He thought, "I want to eat that fruit, but I don't want to work for it." So, he asked his friend, a smart bird, to help him. The bird said, "Let us examine the tree first to see if the fruit is good to eat." They looked at the tree and saw that the fruit was very yummy. The bird told Tom, "If you want to eat the fruit, you must work hard and climb the tree." Tom did not want to work, but he was very hungry. So, he climbed the tree and ate the fruit. The lazy cat learned that working hard can bring good things. From that day on, Tom was not lazy anymore. He worked hard and shared the yummy fruit with his friend, the smart bird. The moral of the story is: if you work hard, you will get good things.

One day, a clever cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big wall and wanted to see what was on the other side. So, Tom started to climb the wall. He used his sharp claws to help him go up fast. When Tom got to the top of the wall, he saw a big dog named Max. Max looked at Tom and said, "You are a clever cat to climb this wall!" Tom said, "Thank you, Max. I wanted to see what was on the other side." Just then, a big wind came and started to send Tom back down the wall. But Max was quick and caught Tom with his mouth. Tom was scared at first, but then he saw that Max was helping him. The two became good friends and played together every day. The big wall had brought them together in a very unexpected way.

"Run!" shouted Mom. Mom and Dad quickly grabbed their three year old daughter, Minnie, and ran away from their house. Minnie felt scared and confused. She asked, "What's happening?" Mom and Dad looked at each other and then back at Minnie. Dad said, "There's a tornado coming. We have to find a safe place." Minnie saw the wind outside getting stronger. She tried to resist the storm, but it was too powerful. Then she heard an enormous roar. When the noise stopped, Minnie was relieved to see that Mom and Dad were still there. She then noticed that their house was gone. Minnie felt confused and sad. But she was also proud to have resisted the tornado with her family.

Once upon a time, there was a little mixer. The mixer liked to mix things. One day, the mixer went to a party. At the party, there were many kids. The kids were playing and having fun. The mixer wanted to play too. The mixer saw a scary mask. The mask was big and had a mean face. The mixer was scared, but it wanted to be brave. So, the mixer put on the mask and went to play with the kids. The kids got scared when they saw the mask. They did not know it was the mixer. The mixer felt sad. It did not want to scare the kids. Then, the mixer had an idea. It started to clap and dance. The kids saw the mixer clapping and dancing. They thought it was funny. They clapped and danced too. The mixer took off the scary mask. The kids were not scared anymore. They all played and had fun together. The mixer was happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a king. The king had a big, shiny crown. He was an honest king and everyone liked him. He had a cat that was his best friend. One day, the king went for a walk with his cat. They saw a big bug on the ground. The cat wanted to slap the bug. But the king said, "No, be nice to the bug." So, the cat did not slap the bug. The bug was very happy. It jumped up and said, "Thank you for being nice to me." The bug was really a fairy! She gave the king a wish. The king wished for his town to be happy forever. The fairy made the wish come true. The king and his cat went back to the town, and everyone was happy. The moral of the story is to always be kind to others.
