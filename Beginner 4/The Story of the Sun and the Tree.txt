Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Sun and the Tree

One day, a big fog came to a little town. The people in the town could not see well. They were patient and waited for the fog to go away. But the fog did not go away, and the people started to worry. A little girl named Sue saw her friend Tom. She said, "Tom, I have an idea. Let's give the fog some sun so it will go away." Tom agreed, and they went to look for the sun. They found the sun hiding behind a big tree. Sue and Tom asked the sun to help them. The sun was scared of the fog, but Sue and Tom were very patient. They said, "Please, sun, help us make the fog go away." The sun agreed and came out from behind the tree. The sun shone bright, and the fog went away. The people in the town were happy and thanked Sue and Tom for their help. The town was bright and clear again, and everyone could see well.

Once upon a time, there was a hidden island. On this island, there were many animals. There was a big bear, a small rabbit, and a fast bird. They all liked to play together. The island was their home. One day, the rabbit said, "Let's clean our island!" The big bear and the fast bird agreed. They wanted their island to be nice and clean. So they all started to clean. The big bear picked up big sticks. The small rabbit picked up small sticks. The fast bird picked up leaves. They all worked together to clean their hidden island. When they were done, the island was very clean. They were all happy and played together on their clean island.

Sara liked to paint. She had many colors and brushes. She wore an apron to keep her clothes clean. She painted flowers and trees and animals on paper. One day, Sara saw a big white wall in the hall. She was curious. She wondered how it would look with paint. She took her colors and brushes and went to the wall. She started to paint a big rainbow. "Stop!" Mom shouted. She saw Sara painting the wall. She was angry. "That is not paper, Sara. That is the wall. You are making a mess. You are not allowed to paint the wall." Sara felt sad. She did not know she was doing something bad. She wanted to make the wall pretty. She said sorry to Mom. But Mom was still angry. She took away Sara's colors and brushes. She said Sara could not paint for a week. Sara cried. She loved to paint. She did not like Mom's punishment. She wished she had not painted the wall. She wished she had asked Mom first. She learned a hard lesson.

One day, a dog named Spot was walking with his friend, a cat named Fluffy. They were in the park, and they saw a big, ugly bug. The bug was on a heel of a shoe. They wanted to know why the bug was there. Spot said, "Listen, Fluffy. Let's ask the bug why he is on the heel." So they went to the bug and asked, "Why are you on the heel, ugly bug?" The bug said, "I am not ugly! I am a magic bug. I can make wishes come true. If you say sorry for calling me ugly, I will give you one wish." Spot and Fluffy were very surprised. They said sorry to the bug. Then, they wished to be the best of friends forever. The magic bug smiled and made their wish come true. From that day on, Spot and Fluffy were always happy and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was an ordinary boy named Tim. Tim was like all the other kids, but he had a big heart. He loved to help others and make them happy. One day, Tim was walking to the park. On his way, he saw a little girl who was sad. She had lost her toy. Tim wanted to help her. He looked around and saw the toy behind a tree. Tim passed the toy to the girl, and she was very happy. From that day on, Tim made it his mission to help others. He would pass things to people who needed them, like a ball to a friend or a snack to someone hungry. Everyone in the town loved Tim, the ordinary boy with a big heart.
