Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Acorn

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They saw a big tree with many green leaves. They ran to the tree and looked for acorns. Acorns were brown and round and had little hats. "Look, I found an acorn!" Lily said. She picked up an acorn and showed it to Ben. "Me too!" Ben said. He found another acorn and held it in his hand. They heard a noise in the tree. They looked up and saw a squirrel. The squirrel had a bushy tail and black eyes. It was holding an acorn in its mouth. "Hello, squirrel!" Lily said. She waved to the squirrel. The squirrel looked at Lily and Ben. It was not happy. It wanted to keep all the acorns for itself. It made a loud sound and shook its tail. "Go away, go away!" the squirrel said. It threw an acorn at Lily and Ben. "Ouch!" Lily said. The acorn hit her on the head. "Hey, that's not nice!" Ben said. He threw an acorn back at the squirrel. The squirrel dodged the acorn and ran to another branch. It threw another acorn at Lily and Ben. Lily and Ben ran away from the tree. They found a bench and sat down. They still had their acorns. "Are you okay, Lily?" Ben asked. "Yes, I'm okay. That squirrel was mean." Lily said. "Maybe the squirrel was hungry. Maybe the acorns are yummy." Ben said. He bit his acorn. It was hard and bitter. He made a face and spit it out. "Yuck! This is not yummy!" Ben said. Lily laughed. She bit her acorn. It was also hard and bitter. She made a face and spit it out. "Yuck! You're right, this is not yummy!" Lily said. They threw their acorns away. They did not want them anymore. "Let's go and play on the swings. The swings are fun." Lily said. "Okay, let's go. The swings are fun." Ben said. They ran to the swings and pushed each other. They forgot about the squirrel and the acorns. They had a good time.

Once upon a time, there were two friends. One was a bunny and the other was a fox. Bunny and Fox were walking across a bridge, when suddenly Bunny noticed that it was very dirty. Bunny said to his friend, "Fox, this bridge is so dirty. What shall we do?" Fox thought for a minute and then came up with a great idea. He said, "Let's give the bridge a clean up and make it look more beautiful". So Bunny and Fox got to work. They gave the bridge a scrub, collected all the trash, and swept away all the dirt. Soon the bridge was shining bright. It was so clean that the sun was reflected off its surface! The two friends smiled. They were proud to see that their hard work had made a difference. The End.

Tom and Lily were playing hide and seek in the park. Tom was the seeker and Lily was the hider. Lily ran to a big tree and hid behind it. She hoped Tom would not find her. Tom looked around the park. He saw the swings, the slide, the sandbox and the fountain. He did not see Lily. He was anxious. He wanted to find Lily and win the game. He called out, "Lily, where are you? Come out, come out, or I will count to ten!" Lily heard Tom's voice. She was scared. She did not want to lose the game. She did not want to come out. She stayed behind the tree and kept quiet. Tom counted to ten. He said, "Lily, I counted to ten. You have to surrender or you lose the game. Do you surrender?" He waited for Lily to answer. Lily did not answer. She did not want to surrender. She wanted to win the game. But she also did not want to make Tom sad. She thought for a moment. Then she had an idea. She said, "Tom, I have a pass. I can stay hidden for one more minute. Then I will come out. OK?" Tom heard Lily's voice. He was surprised. He said, "A pass? What is a pass? How do you have a pass?" Lily said, "A pass is a special thing. It lets you do something different. I have a pass because I am your sister. And I love you. Do you love me?" Tom smiled. He said, "Yes, I love you, Lily. And I love your pass. OK, you can stay hidden for one more minute. But then you have to come out. Deal?" Lily smiled too. She said, "Deal. Thank you, Tom. You are a good seeker. And a good brother." They both laughed. They were happy. They enjoyed the game. And they loved each other.
