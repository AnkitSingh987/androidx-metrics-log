Category: Beginner
Title: Mama and Papa and the Calendar

Mama and Papa were in the kitchen. Mama was crying. "What happened?" asked Papa. Mama held up a calendar and said, "I made a mistake. I was supposed to be at work today, but I wrote it down wrong on the calendar. My boss was so angry and now I'm embarrassed." Papa hugged Mama and said, "It's ok, don't be sad. Let's get to work so you won't be late again." They both got ready to go to work. As they were leaving, Mama went to slap the calendar with her hand in frustration. But then Papa said, "No! Slapping the calendar won't make you feel better. Let's go!" Mama and Papa left for work, but Mama felt embarrassed. She knew she had to be more careful from now on so she wouldn't make the same mistake again.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big desk in his room. He liked to pretend that he was a king. He sat at the desk and put on a paper crown. He felt very happy. Tim's sister, Amy, came into the room. She saw Tim and said, "You look foolish with that crown on your head!" Tim felt sad and a little angry. He did not like his sister's words. Tim and Amy started to argue. They both wanted to use the desk. Tim wanted to be a king and Amy wanted to draw pictures. They could not agree on what to do. Their mom came into the room and saw them arguing. She said, "Why don't you take turns using the desk? Tim can pretend to be a king for a while, and then Amy can draw her pictures." Tim and Amy agreed to take turns. They stopped arguing and played together nicely. The big desk became a special place where they could both have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree. It was very loud when the wind blew. Two birds lived in the tree. They were best friends. One day, a storm came. It was very strong. The wind split the tree in half. The birds were sad. They each had to live in a different part of the tree. They missed each other a lot. One bird said, "I have a memory of us playing together. I miss that." The other bird said, "Me too. Let's help each other fix the tree." So, they worked together to fix the tree. It took a long time, but they did it. In the end, the birds were happy again. They learned that working together is better than being apart. And they lived happily ever after.

Lily wanted to bake a cake for her mom's birthday. She asked her dad to help her. They went to the kitchen and looked for the things they needed. They found flour, sugar, eggs, butter, and milk. They also found a big bowl, a spoon, a pan, and an oven. "Let's mix everything in the bowl," dad said. "Can you crack the eggs, Lily?" Lily nodded and took an egg. She tried to crack it on the edge of the bowl, but she hit it too hard. The egg broke and spilled all over the table. Lily gasped and started to cry. "It's okay, Lily," dad said. "It's just an egg. We have more. Don't cry. Here, I'll help you." Dad wiped the table with a cloth and gave Lily another egg. He showed her how to crack it gently and put it in the bowl. Lily smiled and did the same with the other eggs. Then they added the flour, the sugar, the butter, and the milk. They stirred everything with the spoon until it was smooth. "Good job, Lily," dad said. "Now we have to pour the batter into the pan and put it in the oven. Do you want to do it?" Lily nodded and took the pan. She poured the batter carefully, but some of it dripped on the floor. She didn't notice. She put the pan in the oven and closed the door. "Let's set the timer for 30 minutes," dad said. "Then we can decorate the cake with frosting and sprinkles." Lily clapped her hands and said, "Yay! Mom will love it!" They went to the living room and waited for the cake to bake. Lily saw her brother's trumpet on the couch. She picked it up and blew into it. It made a loud noise. "Wow, Lily, you can play the trumpet!" dad said. Lily giggled and blew again. She liked the sound. She walked around the room, playing the trumpet. But she didn't see the dog. The dog was sleeping on the rug. The dog didn't like the noise. The dog woke up and barked. The dog ran after Lily and tried to bite the trumpet. Lily screamed and dropped the trumpet. The dog grabbed it and ran away. Lily chased the dog and shouted, "Give it back! Give it back!" Dad ran after them and said, "Stop! Stop! Don't hurt the trumpet!" They followed the dog to the kitchen. The dog jumped on the table and dropped the trumpet. The trumpet landed on the cake. The cake was still hot. The cake melted the trumpet. The trumpet made a disgusting smell. Lily, dad, and the dog stared at the cake. The cake was ruined. The cake looked like a mess. The cake smelled like a trash. Lily burst into tears and said, "Mom's cake! Mom's cake!" Dad hugged Lily and said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I'm sorry. We'll make another cake. We'll make a better cake. Don't cry. Don't cry." The dog wagged its tail and licked Lily's face. The dog said, "Woof! Woof!"
