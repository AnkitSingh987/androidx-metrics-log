Category: Beginner
Title: Pinky and Blue and the Garden

Once upon a time, there were two cats, Pinky and Blue. They were the best of friends and played every day in the garden. One day, the two cats decided to go for a walk. As they were walking, they came across a player. The player was having a really great time and looked so happy. Pinky and Blue stopped to watch the player play. "We must stay and watch the player, said Pinky. "Yes, stay and watch, agreed Blue. And so they did, they watched and enjoyed the player, who appeared even more lovely in their eyes as they watched. But as the day drew to a close, it was time for Pinky and Blue to go home. As they said goodbye and walked away, they looked back one last time at the player still happily enjoying the game. As they turned away, Pinky said to Blue, "We can have such lovely times too, if we only stay and play”. The two cats learned a valuable lesson that day that if we want to experience a lovely day, all we have to do is stay and play.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a long thread on the floor. She felt very happy because she liked to play with threads. She picked it up and started to dance around the room, holding the thread in her hand. Lily saw her friend, Tom, and said, "Look, I found a thread! Let's play with it!" Tom felt angry because he wanted to play with something else. "No, I don't want to play with the thread," Tom said with a frown. But suddenly, the thread started to move by itself! It was a magic thread! Lily and Tom felt very surprised. The thread made a big, fun slide for them to play on. Tom was not angry anymore. They both played with the magic thread all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a man named Tom. He had a big farm with many fat pumpkins. Tom loved his pumpkins very much. One day, he decided to sell his fat pumpkins at the market. At the market, a little girl named Sue saw the fat pumpkins. She wanted to buy one. Sue walked up to Tom and said, "Hello! Can I buy a fat pumpkin, please?" Tom smiled and said, "Yes, you can pick any fat pumpkin you like!" Sue looked at all the fat pumpkins and picked the biggest one. She was very happy. Tom was happy too, because he could sell his fat pumpkins to make people happy. Sue took her fat pumpkin home and showed it to her mom. They made a yummy pumpkin pie together. Everyone in the town loved Tom's fat pumpkins, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a sailor. He had a big boat. He liked to sail on the water. One day, a little boy saw the sailor. The boy wanted to go on the boat. He asked the sailor, "Can I go on your boat?" The sailor said, "Not today, little boy." The boy was persistent. He asked the sailor every day. The sailor always said, "Not today, little boy." The boy did not give up. Finally, the sailor said, "Yes, you can go on my boat." The boy was very happy. They sailed on the water together. The boy and the sailor became good friends.

One day, a big fierce lion and a little bird sat under a tree. They saw a long vine hanging from the tree. The lion and the bird began to discuss. The lion said, "I can swing on the vine very fast!" The bird said, "I can fly and sit on the vine!" They both wanted to play with the vine. The lion swung on the vine first. He went very high and laughed. Then, the bird flew and sat on the vine. The bird sang a happy song. The fierce lion and the little bird became good friends. They played with the vine every day and talked under the tree. They were very happy.

Lila and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they find a big box. It is brown and has a hole. They look inside. "Wow!" Lila says. "There is a start in the box!" Tom sees the start. It is yellow and shiny. It has five points. It looks like the starts in the sky at night. "Can we take it?" Tom asks. Lila nods. "Yes, let's take it. Maybe it is magic." They take the start out of the box. It is warm and soft. They hold it in their hands. They feel happy. But then, they hear a loud roar. They turn around. They see a wild lion. It is big and furry. It has sharp teeth and claws. It is angry. It wants the start. "Run!" Lila shouts. They run away from the lion. They run to the gate. They see a man. He is wearing a hat and a coat. He has a net. "Help!" Tom cries. The man sees them. He sees the lion. He throws the net over the lion. He catches it. "Don't worry, kids," the man says. "I am a zookeeper. This lion escaped from the zoo. I have been looking for it. Thank you for finding it for me." He takes the lion and the net. He puts them in a truck. He drives away. Lila and Tom are safe. They still have the start. They wonder where it came from. They decide to keep it as a secret. They go home. They are brave. They are friends.
