Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a normal dog with four legs and a wagging tail. He loved to play with his ball and run around the yard. One day, Spot's owner, a little girl named Lily, made a promise to Spot. She promised that she would take him for a walk every day after school. One day, as they walked, Spot saw a big, red ball. He got very excited and forgot to stay at Lily's heel. He ran away from Lily to get the ball. Lily called for Spot to come back, but he did not listen. He just wanted to play with the big, red ball. Lily was sad and scared. She looked everywhere for Spot, but she could not find him. A big truck came by and hit Spot as he played with the ball. Spot was hurt very badly. The moral of the story is that we should always listen to our promises and stay close to the ones we love. If Spot had remembered to stay at Lily's heel, he would not have been hurt.

One day, a clumsy cat named Tim was playing with his favorite marble. Tim loved this marble because it was big and shiny. He liked to roll it around and chase it all day. But sometimes, Tim would sneeze and scare himself while playing. One sunny day, Tim was playing with his marble near a big tree. He saw a tiny bug and felt a sneeze coming. He sneezed so hard that the marble rolled far away. Tim looked and looked, but he could not find his marble. He was very sad and missed his favorite toy. The next day, Tim was playing near the big tree again. He saw a bright light coming from a hole in the tree. He looked inside and found his shiny marble! Tim was so happy that he found his favorite toy. He knew that he should be more careful when he sneezes and plays with his marble.

Lily and Tom are playing in the garden. They have a big ball and a small car. They like to kick the ball and push the car. They are happy and laugh. But then the sky becomes dark and grey. Lily and Tom hear a loud sound. It is thunder. They are scared and run to the house. They see drops of water falling from the sky. It is rain. They go inside and find their mom. She hugs them and says, "Don't worry, it's just a storm. It will be over soon. Come, let's have some cookies and milk. They are warm and yummy." Lily and Tom follow their mom to the kitchen. They sit on the table and eat the cookies and drink the milk. They feel warm and cozy. They hear the rain and the thunder, but they are not scared anymore. They talk to their mom and tell her about their game. They say, "We like to play in the garden, but we also like to be with you. You make us feel safe and happy. We love you, mom." Mom smiles and says, "I love you too, Lily and Tom. You are my sweet and brave children. Now, let's read a story until the storm is gone."

Once upon a time, there was a peaceful little duck. The duck lived near a big pond. On one side of the pond, there were tall trees. On the other side, there were pretty flowers. One day, the duck saw a frog. The frog liked to clap his hands. The duck thought this was fun. So, the duck and the frog clapped together. They made a happy sound. The duck and the frog became good friends. They played by the pond every day. They clapped and laughed. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was frustrated because he was too small to reach the rail on the big bridge. He wanted to rest on top of it, but it was too high. One day, his mom saw how frustrated Tom was. She decided to help him. She said, "Tom, come over here." She then took him over to a smaller bridge. "See? You can reach the rail on this bridge," she said. Tom smiled and jumped up on top of the rail. He rested there and looked at all of the boats on the river below. The wind was blowing in his hair and it felt so good. He was so happy and content. "Thank you, Mom!" he said. "That was so nice of you!" His mom smiled and replied, "You're welcome, Tom. I'm glad you are happy now."
