Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Dog and the Fly

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max was an independent dog who did not need help from anyone. One day, Max saw a little bird who was sad. The bird was sad because he did not know how to fly. Max wanted to help the little bird. He thought, "I can teach the bird to fly." So, Max and the little bird became friends. They played together and had lots of fun. Max showed the bird how to jump, run, and flap his wings. After many days, the little bird learned to fly. He was so happy! Max and the little bird played a game called "war." They pretended to fight and protect their friends. In the end, they were both very tired but happy. The little bird was now independent, just like Max. They knew they would be best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a popular dog named Spot. Spot had many friends in the park. One day, it was very cold and all the dogs were shivering. Spot saw a cross on the ground. He was curious and wanted to find out what it was. He asked his friends, but they did not know either. They all decided to look for clues together. They played and looked around the park. Soon, they found a warm blanket next to the cross. They all sat on the blanket and stopped shivering. Spot and his friends were happy and warm. The popular dog and his friends had a great day at the park.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play on the farm. The farm has many animals. Anna likes the cows. Ben likes the pigs. One day, Anna and Ben see a big red tractor. The tractor is shiny and loud. They want to ride the tractor. They ask the farmer if they can borrow the tractor. The farmer says no. The tractor is not for children. It is for work. Anna and Ben are sad. They go to the barn. They see a small green wagon. The wagon is old and quiet. They think the wagon is for children. They do not ask the farmer if they can borrow the wagon. They take the wagon and run to the field. They have fun with the wagon. They pretend it is a tractor. They make noises and drive around. They do not see the farmer. They do not see the cow. The cow is hungry. The cow sees the wagon. The wagon has hay. The cow likes hay. The cow walks to the wagon. The cow eats the hay. Anna and Ben hear a moo. They turn around. They see the cow. The cow has eaten all the hay. The wagon is empty. Anna and Ben are scared. They think the farmer will be angry. They think the cow will be angry. They run to the barn. They leave the wagon. They hide behind the pigs. They hope the farmer will not find them. They hope the cow will not find them. They are not lucky. The farmer finds them. The farmer is not happy. He asks them why they took the wagon. He tells them the wagon is not for children. It is for the cow. He tells them the cow needs the hay. He tells them they have to say sorry to the cow. Anna and Ben are sorry. They say sorry to the farmer. They say sorry to the cow. They promise to not take the wagon again. They promise to ask before they borrow anything. The farmer is kind. He forgives them. He lets them pet the cow. He lets them feed the pigs. He tells them they can play on the farm. But they have to be careful. And they have to be polite. Anna and Ben are happy. They thank the farmer. They hug the cow. They smile at the pigs. They learn a lesson. They are still friends. They still like the farm.

Lila was an adventurous girl who liked to explore new things. She had a toy laser that made bright lights and funny sounds. She wanted to see what would happen if she put the laser on different things. One day, she went to the park with her mom and her laser. She saw a big slide that looked fun. She climbed up the ladder and put the laser on the top of the slide. She pressed the button and the laser made a red light that went down the slide. "Wow, look at that!" she said. She slid down after the light and laughed. She felt happy and excited. She wanted to try more things with her laser. She saw a pond with some ducks. She ran to the edge and put the laser on the water. She pressed the button and the laser made a green light that bounced on the water. "Quack, quack!" the ducks said. They were curious and followed the light. Lila giggled and waved at the ducks. She liked to make new friends. She saw a big tree with some birds. She wanted to see if they liked the laser too. She put the laser on the trunk of the tree. She pressed the button and the laser made a blue light that went up the tree. "Tweet, tweet!" the birds said. They were surprised and flew away. Lila frowned and felt sorry. She did not mean to scare them. She heard her mom calling her. It was time to go home. She picked up her laser and ran to her mom. "Did you have fun, Lila?" her mom asked. "Yes, mom. I put the laser on many things. It was very fun. But I scared the birds. I am sorry." Lila said. "It's okay, Lila. You were just being adventurous. But you have to be careful with your laser. It can hurt some things or some people. You have to ask before you put it on something." her mom said. "I understand, mom. I will be careful next time. Can I play with the laser again tomorrow?" Lila asked. "Sure, Lila. But only if you promise to be safe and respectful." her mom said. "I promise, mom. Thank you. I love you." Lila said. "I love you too, Lila. You are my adventurous girl." her mom said. They hugged and went home. Lila was happy. She had a great day with her laser. She wondered what else she could put it on tomorrow.
