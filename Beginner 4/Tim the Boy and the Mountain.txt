Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Mountain

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim wanted to go on a trip to the big mountain. He was very excited. Before he left, he had to unpack his bag. He took out his good shoes, his hat, and his coat. On his way to the mountain, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue wanted to go to the mountain too. "Can I come with you?" she asked. "Yes!" said Tim, "We can go together!" So, Tim and Sue walked to the mountain, talking and laughing. When they got to the mountain, it started to rain. Tim and Sue did not have an umbrella. They got very wet and cold. They tried to go back home, but they could not find the way. They were lost. Tim and Sue were very sad, and they wished they had stayed home.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She wanted to make a great present for her mom's birthday. Lily thought and thought about what to make. She decided to draw a big, beautiful picture. Lily found her crayons and paper. She drew a big heart and colored it with her favorite colors. She drew her mom, herself, and their house. Lily was very happy with her picture. She said to her cat, "Do you think Mom will like it?" The cat meowed, as if to say, "Yes, it's great!" On her mom's birthday, Lily gave her the present. Her mom opened it and saw the great picture. She smiled and said, "Lily, this is the best present ever! Thank you so much!" Lily felt very proud and happy. They hung the picture on the wall, and it made their home even more special.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing together. They found a big sheet. The sheet was different. It had many colors on it. The cat and the dog wanted to play with the sheet, but they did not know how. The cat had an idea. He said, "Let's play a game. We can hide under the sheet and see who can find the other first." The dog liked the idea. They played the game and had lots of fun. But then, the cat and the dog both wanted to keep the sheet. The cat and the dog began to argue about who should keep the sheet. They could not agree. They were both very sad. Then, a bird flew by and saw them. The bird said, "Why don't you share the sheet? You can both have fun with it." The cat and the dog thought about it. They decided that sharing the sheet was a good idea. They played with the sheet together and had even more fun. The cat and the dog were very happy. They both began to applaud and thank the bird for helping them.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved the color purple. She had a purple dress, purple shoes, and even a purple room. Sue's mom and dad knew how much she loved purple, so they bought her a big purple ball. One day, Sue was playing with her purple ball in the yard. She threw it up high and tried to catch it. But this time, the ball went too high and got stuck in a tree. Sue was sad and didn't know what to do. Sue's mom saw her looking sad and asked what was wrong. Sue told her mom about the purple ball stuck in the tree. Her mom had an idea. She took a photo of the tree with the ball in it. Then, she raised the photo up high and showed it to Sue. Sue smiled and knew that her mom would help her get the ball back. Together, they found a long stick and got the purple ball out of the tree. Sue was happy again and continued to play with her purple ball.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was playing in his room near the curtain. He knew that if he did something terrible, his mom would punish him. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. They were having fun, but Tim remembered his mom's warning. He told Sam, "We can play, but we can't do bad things, or my mom will punish us." Sam listened to Tim, and they played nicely. But then, Sam accidentally pulled the curtain down. They both looked at the curtain and felt bad. Tim said, "Oh no! We have to fix it before my mom sees it!" They worked together to put the curtain back up. Just as they finished, Tim's mom walked in. She saw that the curtain was fixed and said, "Good job, boys! I'm glad you fixed your mistake." Tim and Sam were happy they didn't get punished. They learned that it's important to fix their mistakes and not do terrible things. And from that day on, they were always careful when playing near the curtain.
