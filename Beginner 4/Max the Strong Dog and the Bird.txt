Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Strong Dog and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a strong dog named Max. Max loved to swim in the big pond near his home. One sunny day, Max went to the pond to swim and play. As Max was swimming, he saw a little bird that could not fly. The bird was cold and wet. Max wanted to help the bird, so he used his strong legs to swim over and cover the bird with his warm fur. Max and the bird became good friends. They learned that by helping each other, they could both be strong and happy. The little bird taught Max how to find food, and Max helped the bird learn to fly. And they lived happily ever after.

Tom and Jen are husband and wife. They live in a big house with a big bed. Every night, they sleep in the big bed and dream. Tom dreams of cars and planes and boats. Jen dreams of flowers and dresses and shoes. One night, Tom wakes up and sees Jen is not in the bed. He hears a noise from the living room. He gets up and walks to the living room. He sees Jen on the sofa with another man. They are kissing and hugging. Tom is very angry and sad. He shouts at Jen and the man. "Jen, you are a naughty wife! You are not loyal to me! Who is this man? Go away from my house!" Jen and the man are scared and ashamed. They get up and run to the door. Jen says to Tom. "Tom, I am sorry. I don't love you anymore. I love this man. He makes me happy. Goodbye, Tom." Tom is left alone in the house. He cries and cries. He goes back to the bed, but he cannot sleep. He cannot dream. He only feels pain and hate. He wishes he never met Jen. He wishes he never married her. He wishes he never loved her. He wishes he could forget her. But he can't. He is stuck with his broken heart. He has a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her favorite toy, a shiny marble. One sunny day, Lily took her marble outside to play in the park. As she played, she met a friendly dog named Spot. Spot saw the marble and wanted to play too. Lily said, "Spot, do you want to play with my marble?" Spot wagged his tail and barked happily. They played together, laughing and having fun. After playing for a while, Lily and Spot were tired. They found a big tree and sat down under it to relax. Lily held her marble and smiled at Spot. They had a great day and became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She liked to play in the woods near her home. One day, while playing, she saw an old lady. The old lady looked a little uncomfortable. Lily asked her, "Why are you uncomfortable?" The old lady said, "I am a witch, and people do not believe in me. They think I am not real. That makes me feel bad." Lily thought for a moment and said, "I believe in you. You are real to me." The witch smiled and felt better. She thanked Lily and said, "Because you believe in me, I will give you a gift." The witch gave Lily a pretty flower. Lily was happy and ran home to show her mom. From that day on, Lily and the witch were good friends.

Jack was walking to the shop. He was feeling very angry because his mom wouldn't let him buy the toy he wanted. He was so mad that he started stomping his feet and yelling. When he reached the shop, he went right in. Jack saw the toy and he wanted it so much. He asked the shopkeeper if he could buy it. But the shopkeeper told him it was too expensive. Jack started to cry. He said, "Please, I really want it." The shopkeeper shook his head. He said, "I'm sorry, but your mom wouldn't like it." Jack was so angry that he ran out of the shop. He was trying to reach the toy, but he knew it wasn't possible now. So, he started walking home with his head down. Jack was still mad, but he knew that he had to follow his mom's rules.
