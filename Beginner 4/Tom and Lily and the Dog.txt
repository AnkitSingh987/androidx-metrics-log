Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Dog

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play with blocks. They want to make a big tower with many colors. They work hard to stack the blocks on top of each other. They are careful not to make the tower fall. "Look, Lily, our tower is so high!" Tom says. He is happy with their work. "Yes, Tom, it is very high. And it has many colors. It is a modern tower!" Lily says. She is proud of their result. They smile at each other. They want to show their tower to their mom and dad. They run to get them. They want them to see their work and result. But when they come back, they see a big mess. Their tower is not there anymore. The blocks are all over the floor. A big dog is sitting on the blocks. He looks at them with his tongue out. "Whoa, what happened?" Tom says. He is sad and angry. "Oh no, our tower! It is gone!" Lily says. She is sad and angry too. The dog barks. He thinks the blocks are toys. He wants to play with them. He does not know that Tom and Lily worked hard to make a tower. Tom and Lily cry. They do not want to play with the dog. They want their tower back. They do not like the dog's work and result. They think he is a bad dog.

Once there was a cow. She was walking in a field very happily. Suddenly a farmer passed by with a big cart full of vegetables. The farmer waved at the cow and the cow was so scared. She ran away, feeling ashamed. The cow stopped after a while and looked back. She was very ashamed that she had run away. Then she heard the farmer calling her. He was asking her to come back, because he wanted to give her a big surprise. So the cow passed hesitantly back to the farmer. The farmer was smiling and gave the cow a big bunch of grass. The cow was so happy that she started to moo and jump around. She was no longer feeling ashamed. The farmer and the cow were friends now, and they passed many happy days together in that field.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim had a pair of pants that he loved very much. They were his favorite pants because they were a weird color. The pants were a mix of blue and green, and they made him very happy. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. Sam saw Tim's weird pants and said, "Wow, Tim! Your pants are so weird! I want pants like that too!" Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Sam! I like my weird pants a lot." Tim and Sam played all day, and they had so much fun. They ran in the yard, jumped in the leaves, and laughed together. When it was time for Sam to go home, he said, "I had so much fun today, Tim. I still want weird pants like yours!" Tim hugged his friend and said, "Maybe we can find some weird pants for you too, Sam!" And they both laughed as they said goodbye.

Once upon a time, there was a smelly dog named Max. Max liked to play in the mud and run around the park. One day, Max saw a big, red ball. He wanted to play with the ball, so he ran to it and gave it a big push. The ball started to roll very fast, and Max could not stop it. The ball rolled into a big mess of paint. Max felt regret because he knew he should not have pushed the ball. The paint made a big mess all over the park. Then, something unexpected happened. The people at the park took the paint and made beautiful pictures on the walls. They thanked Max for helping them start their art project. Max learned that sometimes, even when we make mistakes, good things can happen.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a glove on the ground. She picked it up and saw a small hole in it. Sue wanted to find the owner of the glove, so she went to ask her friends. She walked up to Tom, who was playing with his ball. "Did you lose a glove?" Sue asked. Tom shook his head and said, "No, that's not mine." Sue then went to ask her friend, Lily. Lily was playing with her doll. "Is this your glove?" Sue asked Lily. Lily looked at the glove and said, "No, it's not mine. But I think I saw someone with a glove like that." Sue thanked Lily and went to find the person with the glove. As Sue walked, she saw a boy named Max peeking from behind a tree. Max was known for being rude to others. Sue thought that maybe the glove was his. She went up to Max and asked, "Is this your glove?" Max snatched the glove from Sue's hand and said, "Yes, it's mine! And you shouldn't touch other people's things!" Sue felt sad that Max was rude to her, but she was happy she found the owner of the glove. Sue walked away and decided to play with her kind friends, Tom and Lily, instead.
