Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily Find the Door

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They saw a big truck with a horn. They wanted to see the horn up close. They ran to the truck and tried to open the door. "Let me try, let me try!" Lily said. She pulled the handle, but the door did not open. "It is locked," she said. "Maybe we can unlock it," Tom said. He looked around and saw a key on the ground. He picked it up and put it in the hole. He turned the key and heard a click. "I did it, I did it!" he said. He opened the door and climbed inside. Lily followed him. They saw a big wheel, some buttons, and a lever. They wondered what they did. Tom pushed the lever and heard a loud sound. It was the horn. "Wow, that is impressive!" he said. Lily agreed. She pushed the lever again and again. The horn made a lot of noise. They laughed and cheered. But they did not see the man who owned the truck. He was angry. He heard the horn and ran to the truck. He saw Tom and Lily inside. He shouted at them. "Hey, what are you doing? Get out of my truck! You have no right to unlock it and play with the horn!" Tom and Lily were scared. They jumped out of the truck and ran away. They did not want to get in trouble. They learned a lesson. They should not touch things that do not belong to them. They should ask before they try to unlock something. And they should be careful with the horn. It is impressive, but it can also be annoying.

Anna and Ben liked to play with chalk. They drew pictures on the sidewalk. They made a sun, a flower, a house and a cat. They were happy. "Look, Anna, I can draw a cake!" Ben said. He made a big circle with pink chalk. He added some dots for sprinkles. "Do you like it?" "Yes, Ben, it is a nice cake. But I am hungry. Can we go inside and cook something?" Anna asked. She rubbed her tummy. "OK, Anna, let's go. Maybe Mom has some cookies for us." Ben said. He picked up his chalk and followed Anna. They ran to the door. But they did not see the big dog that was sleeping on the porch. The dog woke up and saw the chalk. He thought it was food. He jumped up and ran after Anna and Ben. He barked loudly. "Help, help, a dog!" Anna and Ben screamed. They dropped their chalk and tried to open the door. But it was locked. They were scared. Mom heard the noise and came to the window. She saw the dog chasing her children. She was angry. She opened the door and shouted at the dog. "Go away, you bad dog! Leave my kids alone!" The dog was afraid of Mom. He saw that she was strong. He dropped the chalk and ran away. He did not like the taste of chalk. Mom hugged Anna and Ben. She was glad they were safe. She said, "I'm sorry, kids, I forgot to unlock the door. Are you OK?" "Yes, Mom, we are OK. But we are hungry. Can we cook something?" Anna and Ben said. "Of course, my loves. Let's go to the kitchen and make some cookies. And maybe we can draw some more pictures later. But not with chalk. Chalk is not for eating." Mom said. Anna and Ben smiled. They liked to cook with Mom. They liked to draw with chalk. But they did not like the big dog. They hoped he would not come back.

Tommy was excited for the day he was about to have. He was going to his first ever football game in the stadium! He looked at his outfit with glee. It was all purple - a vibrant purple t-shirt, some purple shorts and purple shoes. Tommy's mom sighed. "Are you sure you want to wear all purple, Tommy? It's quite a lot!" Tommy smiled, nodding eagerly. "Yes, Mommy! Please!" Tommy's mom sighed again and smiled. "Okay, Tommy. You look adorable." Tommy was so excited. He was ready to go to the stadium and cheer for his favorite team. When he arrived, the sound of cheering and the sight of the green football field filled him with energy and happiness. He smiled and felt the magic of the stadium. It was an adventure he would never forget.
