Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Fish and the Tim

One day, a big fish named Bob lived in a deep blue sea. Bob liked to dive down to find new things. He had a friend named Tim, who was a small fish. Tim was not comfortable with diving deep, but he liked to watch Bob dive. One day, Bob saw a shiny thing in the water. He told Tim, "I will dive down to see what it is." Tim said, "Be careful, Bob!" Bob dived down and found a big gun. He thought it was a toy and played with it. Bob swam back to Tim and showed him the gun. Tim was scared and said, "Put it back, Bob!" But Bob did not listen. He played with the gun, and it went "bang!" Bob was hurt and floated in the water. Tim was sad, and he wished Bob had listened to him.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big, crazy dog named Max. Tim and Max loved to play outside in the sun. One day, while they were playing, Max got hurt. Tim was very sad. Tim's mom saw Max was hurt and said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can help Max heal." Tim asked, "How, Mom?" She said, "We can make a magic cake for Max. It will help him feel better." So, Tim and his mom made a big, yummy cake for Max. They put a candle on the cake and sang a song. Tim made a wish for Max to heal. Max ate the cake, and soon, he felt better. Tim was so happy, and they played together again.

One day, a strong boy named Tim saw a helicopter in the sky. He liked the helicopter and wanted to enjoy a ride. He asked his mom, "Can I go on the helicopter, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Tim went on the helicopter with a big smile. He enjoyed the ride high in the sky. He saw many things like trees, cars, and his house. Tim was very happy and said, "I love the helicopter ride!" But then, the helicopter went too high. Tim got scared and started to cry. The helicopter came down and Tim got off. He ran to his mom and hugged her tight. The strong boy was not happy anymore. The helicopter ride had a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Spot. He loved to run, jump, and play with his ball. Spot had a little house in the yard where he liked to sleep. One day, Spot found a box with a label on it. He could not read, but he liked the pretty colors on the label. He tried to open the box, but it was closed very tight. Spot was curious and wanted to see what was inside. Spot carried the box to his friend, a little girl named Lily. Lily could read the label and opened the box for Spot. Inside, there was a new ball for Spot to play with. They both laughed and played with the new ball all day long.

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play with toys. One day, they found a box of matches in the kitchen. They wanted to see what would happen if they lit them. "Let's go to the store and buy some candles," Tom said. "Then we can make a fire and watch it." "Okay," Lily agreed. "But we have to be careful. Matches are dangerous." They put on their coats and shoes and went outside. They walked to the store and bought some candles with their pocket money. Then they went to the park and found a quiet spot under a tree. They set up the candles on the ground and took out the matches. Tom struck one and lit a candle. He smiled and gave it to Lily. "Look, it's so pretty and warm," he said. Lily nodded and lit another candle. She held it close to her face and felt the heat. "It's also peaceful," she said. "Like the stars at night." They lit more candles and made a circle of fire. They sat inside and watched the flames dance. They felt happy and cozy. But they did not see the smoke that rose from their fire. They did not hear the sirens that came closer and closer. They did not notice the people who ran towards them and shouted. "Hey, kids, what are you doing? Stop that right now! You're starting a fire!" Tom and Lily looked up and saw the angry faces. They felt scared and confused. They dropped the matches and the candles and ran away. They did not understand why they were in trouble. They only wanted to play with matches.
