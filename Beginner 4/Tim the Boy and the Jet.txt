Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Jet

One day, a little boy named Tim found a toy jet in his room. It was red and blue. He loved to play with it. He would fly it around and make it go fast. Tim's friend, Sara, came over to play. She saw the toy jet and thought it was very useful. She said, "I think your jet can help us clean your room." Tim did not agree. He said, "No, my jet is for playing, not for cleaning." But Sara had an idea. She took the toy jet and started to fly it around the room. It made a big wind and blew all the toys into a big pile. Tim saw how useful the jet was and they both laughed. They cleaned the room together with the help of the toy jet.

Once upon a time, there was a reliable dog named Spot. Spot loved to help his family. Every day, he would fetch the ball and help put away toys. Spot was very good at his job. One day, Spot's family asked him to find a key. The key was in a drawer in the big room. Spot went to the big room and looked in the drawer. He found the key and brought it back to his family. They were very happy. After a long day of helping, Spot was tired. He went to his favorite spot by the window to rest. Spot lay down and closed his eyes. He knew he had done a good job today. As he slept, Spot dreamed of more fun days with his family.

Once upon a time, there was a brave dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside with his friends. They would run and jump all day long. One day, Spot met a new friend named Bow. Bow was a happy cat. Bow said, "Hi, Spot! Do you want to play with me?" Spot was excited to make a new friend and said, "Yes, Bow! Let's play!" Spot and Bow played together all day. They chased each other and had so much fun. At the end of the day, Spot and Bow were very tired. They said goodbye and went home to rest. Spot couldn't wait to play with Bow again.

One day, a boy named Tim found a long cord in his room. He thought it was a useful cord. He wanted to show his friend, Sam. Tim ran to Sam's house and knocked on the door. "Look, Sam!" Tim said. "I found this useful cord! Let's play with it!" Sam was very excited. They decided to play a game. They would say a word and then the other would repeat it. "Cat," said Tim. "Cat," Sam repeated. "Dog," said Sam. "Dog," Tim repeated. As they played, something unexpected happened. The cord started to wiggle and move on its own! Tim and Sam were very surprised. The cord turned into a friendly snake. The snake could talk! "Hello, Tim and Sam!" said the snake. "Thank you for playing with me! I was under a spell, and your game helped me change back. Now, I can be your friend!" Tim and Sam were very happy. They had a new friend, and they played together all day.

Once there was a duck who loved to wear hats. She tried lots of different ones but never found one she liked. She felt so frustrated. One day, it started to rain. She decided to to try and find shelter, but all the places she looked were too small for her. She was getting more and more frustrated. That's when she saw the hat. It was lying on the ground and was a perfect fit for her. She just knew it was the hat for her. She put it on and wore it proudly. The duck smiled and she wasn't frustrated anymore. She was very happy in her new hat. She went off to look for a big puddle and have some fun splashing around.

Billy the little boy was playing in the backyard. He saw something very interesting on the ground. It was a piece of cardboard. He was so excited that he marked it with a big black marker! The cardboard looked like it had something moving inside it. He was curious and opened it up. What a surprise he got! Inside were little disgusting bugs. They were crawling all over the cardboard. He quickly closed it and ran away. He was so scared he didn't want to go near the cardboard ever again.
