Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Cat and the Dog

One day, a cat named Tom was walking down the street. He saw a dog named Max. Max looked guilty. Tom asked, "Max, why do you look guilty?" Max said, "I took a toy from my friend. It was wrong." Tom wanted to help Max. Tom said, "I suggest you give the toy back to your friend. Say sorry too." Max listened to Tom. He gave the toy back to his friend and said sorry. His friend forgave him. Max felt happy. Tom and Max played together and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toy boat in the big tub. One day, Tim found a shiny coin in his room. He thought it would be fun to play with it in the tub too. Tim put the coin in the tub and said, "Look, boat! You are rich now!" The boat floated near the coin, and they played together. Tim didn't know that the water would make the coin change. The next day, Tim saw the coin was not shiny anymore. It was now dark and not pretty. The water had soaked the coin and changed it. Tim was sad but learned that some things should not go in the water. From that day on, he played with his boat and kept the coin safe in his room.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a restless boy who loved to joke and play. One sunny day, Tim went to the park with his friend, Sam. At the park, they saw a big pit. "Let's play a joke," said Tim. "We can hide in the pit and scare people when they walk by!" Sam laughed and agreed. They jumped into the pit and waited for someone to come. After a while, an old lady passed by the pit. Tim and Sam jumped out and yelled, "Boo!" The old lady laughed and said, "You got me, you funny kids!" Tim and Sam giggled and went back into the pit, waiting for their next joke. They had a great day full of laughter and fun.

Once, there was a smelly dog named Spot. Spot liked to stay in the park and play all day. He had many friends in the park, like birds and cats. They all had fun together. One day, a police car came to the park. Spot was scared because he thought the police would take him away. He hid behind a big tree and watched the police talk to a man. Spot did not know what they were saying, but he stayed very quiet. Then, something unexpected happened. The police man gave the man a big box. The man opened the box and out came many toys! The police man was not there to take Spot away, he was there to give toys to everyone in the park. Spot was very happy and played with his new toys with all his friends.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went for a walk in the park. She saw a big tree and wanted to discover what was behind it. As she walked around the tree, she saw a small, harmless bug on the ground. Lucy watched the bug as it crawled on the grass. Then, the bug started to climb the tree. Lucy wanted to see where the bug was going, so she followed it with her eyes. But suddenly, the bug took a fall from the tree and landed on a soft leaf. Lucy felt happy that the bug was okay. She learned that even harmless bugs can have a fall. From that day on, Lucy loved to discover new things in the park while being careful not to hurt any small creatures.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a boy named Tom. Tom had a normal pen that he liked very much. He took it everywhere with him. He used it to draw and write. Tom loved his pen. One day, Tom went to the park to play with his friends. He took his pen with him. While playing, he put his pen down on a bench. After a while, Tom forgot about his pen and went home. When he got home, he noticed that his pen was not with him. Tom had lost his pen. Tom was very sad. His mom saw him and asked, "Why are you sad?" Tom told her about the lost pen. His mom said, "Don't worry, we can go back to the park and look for it." So, they went back to the park. They looked everywhere and finally found the pen on the bench. Tom was very happy. He learned that he should always take care of his things and not forget them.
