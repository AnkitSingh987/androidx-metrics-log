Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Roof

One day, a little girl named Lucy was playing outside. She saw a big structure. It was a new playhouse. Lucy was very happy and ready to play in it. She ran to the playhouse and looked inside. Lucy saw her friend, Tom, in the playhouse. Tom was looking at the walls and the roof. Lucy asked Tom a question. She said, "Do you like the playhouse?" Tom smiled and said, "Yes, I love it! It is a fun place to play." Lucy and Tom played in the playhouse all day. They made up games and had a lot of fun. When it was time to go home, Lucy said, "I can't wait to come back and play again!" Tom agreed, and they both went home, ready for more fun the next day.

One day, a little toy car wanted to go fast. It tried to go fast, but it could not. The toy car felt sad. It knew it needed a new battery to go fast. The toy car saw its friend, the toy robot, and asked, "Please, can I have a battery?" The toy robot felt guilty because it did not have a battery for the toy car. The toy car did not give up. It asked a toy doll, "Please, can I have a battery?" The toy doll felt guilty too, because it did not have a battery for the toy car. The toy car was very sad and thought it would never go fast again. Then, something unexpected happened. A big toy truck came by and said, "I have a battery for you, toy car!" The toy car was so happy! It said, "Please, can I have the battery?" The big toy truck gave the battery to the toy car. Now, the toy car could go fast! The toy car thanked the big toy truck, and they all played together happily.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree in a big park. The tree had many green plants on it. A happy bird lived in the tree. The bird liked to sing songs. One sunny day, a little boy named Tim came to the park. He saw the tall tree and the happy bird. He said, "Hello, bird! I like your tree. It is very tall and has many plants." The bird said, "Thank you, Tim! I enjoy living in this tall tree. The plants are my friends. They give me food and a place to rest." Tim and the bird became good friends. They played in the park every day. They both enjoyed the tall tree and the green plants. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a big ugly bird was in a tree. He saw a little boy walking by. The bird wanted to be friends with the boy. So, the bird tried to whistle a fun song. But the whistle did not sound nice. The little boy looked up and saw the ugly bird. "Hello," said the boy. "Why do you whistle like that?" "I don't know," said the bird. "I want to make a nice song, but it sounds bad." The little boy thought for a moment. Then, he had an idea. He took out a menu from his bag. The menu had pictures of food on it. The boy pointed at a picture of a pie. "Maybe you need to eat this pie," the boy said. The ugly bird ate the pie from the menu. Suddenly, something unexpected happened. The bird became very pretty! Now, the bird could whistle a beautiful song. The boy and the bird became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who loved to make dough. She had the best recipe and each time she made it the dough was soft and yummy. One day, while she was playing with the dough, an alert bird flew in. The little girl was scared, so she pushed the bird away. The bird tried to stay but she kept pushing it away. Finally, the bird flew away and she pushed the dough back into a bowl and happily continued playing. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog. The dog had soft skin. He liked to run and play. One day, he saw a big cat. The cat was not nice. The dog was not glad to see the cat. The dog wanted to be friends with the cat. He tried to play with the cat. But the cat did not want to play. The cat was mean to the dog. The dog was sad. The dog tried one more time to play with the cat. The cat got mad. The cat hurt the dog's skin. The dog was very sad. The dog learned not to play with the mean cat. The dog was not glad anymore.
