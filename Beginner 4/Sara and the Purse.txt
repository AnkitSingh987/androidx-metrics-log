Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and the Purse

Sara liked to play with her mom's purse. It was red and shiny and had many things inside. Sara liked to open the purse and look at the things. She liked the keys, the phone, the wallet and the lipstick. She liked to pretend she was a grown-up lady. One day, Sara saw her mom's purse on the table. She wanted to play with it, but her mom was not home. She was at work. Sara thought it was okay to play with the purse, because her mom would not know. She took the purse and went to her room. Sara opened the purse and took out the things. She put the keys in her pocket, the phone in her ear, the wallet in her hand and the lipstick on her lips. She felt very happy and proud. She looked in the mirror and smiled. She looked like a grown-up lady. But then, she heard the door open. It was her mom. She came home early from work. She wanted to surprise Sara. She went to Sara's room and saw Sara with her purse and her things. She was not happy. She was angry. "Sara, what are you doing with my purse?" she asked. "You know you are not allowed to touch my purse. You could lose or break my things. You could hurt yourself with the lipstick. You are in big trouble, young lady." Sara felt very sad and scared. She did not want to make her mom angry. She did not want to be in trouble. She wanted to say sorry, but she could not find the words. She had gained a purse, but she had lost her mom's trust. She wished she had not played with the purse. She wished she had waited for her mom. She wished she had been a good girl.

One day, a little boy named Tim went upstairs to find his toy. When he got there, a big, intelligent cat appeared. The cat said, "Hi, I am Cat. I can talk!" Tim was very surprised. He asked, "How can you talk?" The cat just smiled and said, "I am a special cat. I can help you find your toy." So, the cat and Tim looked everywhere upstairs. They looked under the bed, in the closet, and behind the door. Then, something unexpected happened. Tim's toy was not a toy anymore. It was a real dog! The dog said, "Hi, I am Dog. I was a toy, but Cat made me real." Tim, Cat, and Dog were so happy. They played together all day and became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big, useful box with lots of pictures inside. Tim loved to look at the pictures and tell stories about them to his friends. One day, Tim found a new picture in the box. It was a picture of a big tree. He showed the picture to his friend, Sue. "Look, Sue!" he said. "A big tree! Let's play hide and seek near the tree!" Sue agreed, and they went to find the big tree. When they found it, Tim hid first. He found a great spot behind the tree. Sue started to count. "One, two, three!" she yelled. Tim peeked out from behind the tree to see if Sue was coming. "Ready or not, here I come!" Sue shouted. She looked around and saw Tim's feet. "I found you, Tim!" she said, laughing. Tim and Sue played hide and seek all day by the big tree. They had lots of fun, and the useful box with the pictures helped them find a new place to play. The end.

A little girl named Lisa smiled when she saw her new toy. It was a big, soft toy, made of fluffy velvet. She couldn't wait to have fun with it. She hugged the toy tight and hugged it again and again. Then, Lisa jumped up and down, holding the toy close to her. She made a loud noise, like a lion roaring, and then laughed and laughed. Then, Lisa pushed the toy around the room, chugging along like a little train. She giggled as the toy clattered and swayed back and forth. Lisa was having so much fun. Finally, she collapsed onto the floor, tired from all of the playing. But she still had a big smile on her face. She had loved every minute of her day with her new velvet toy.
