Category: Beginner
Title: Mum and the Park

One day a little boy wanted to go and play cricket with his friends. His mum saw him getting ready and asked him, "Where are you going?" The little boy said, "Mum, I'm going to play cricket with my friends!" His mum smiled and said, "That's fine. But remember, cricket can also serve a purpose. It can help you learn important lessons like how to work with others, how to be kind and how to share." The little boy nodded and said, "Yes, Mum. I will remember." He then went off to the park where he and his friends played cricket all afternoon. The little boy enjoyed the game and he learnt a lot from it. He learnt how to serve others by being patient and understanding. He learnt how to be a good team player and how to be respectful. By the end of the day, the little boy felt so proud of himself for learning these valuable lessons. The moral of the story is that when we serve others, we learn important lessons that stay with us forever.

Lily and Anna are best friends. They like to play dress-up with their dolls and clothes. Today, they are going to a tea party at Anna's house. Lily is very excited. She wants to wear her pink skirt. It is smooth and shiny. She likes how it spins when she twirls. She puts on her skirt and runs to the door. She calls to her mom. "Mom, I am ready! Can we go now?" Mom smiles and picks up her purse. "Yes, we can go. But first, you need to put on your shoes and coat. It is cold outside." Lily hurries to find her shoes and coat. She does not want to be late. She wants to see Anna and have fun. She puts on her shoes and coat and grabs her doll. She says to her mom, "Come on, mom! Let's go!" Mom nods and opens the door. They walk to the car and get in. Mom drives to Anna's house. Lily looks out the window and sees the trees and houses. She sings a song to her doll. She is happy. She can't wait to have tea with Anna.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to eat apples. One sunny day, Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw many trees with apples. Tim was very happy. At the park, Tim met a stranger. The stranger was a big, friendly dog. The dog said, "Woof, woof!" Tim was a little upset because he did not know the dog. But the dog wagged its tail and smiled. Tim thought the dog was nice. Tim asked the dog, "Do you like to eat apples too?" The dog barked, "Woof, woof!" Tim gave the dog an apple. They both ate apples together and played in the park. Tim and the dog became good friends, and Tim was not upset anymore. They had a fun day at the park.

One day, Amy decided to visit her grandma. She jumped in the car with mom and dad and she was excited to go. When they arrived, Amy opened the door and saw a big old cat sleeping in a chair. She said "Woof! Woof! I want to pet the cat!" Mom and Dad smiled and told Amy "It's okay, but be gentle. Grandma, this is Amy, our daughter." Grandma smiled and said, "Amy, this is our old cat. Can you pet him?" Amy carefully petted the old cat and he purred contentedly. She was very happy and she and the cat spent the whole visit together!

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Max, went on a trip to the park. They liked to play ball and run around. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. At the park, they saw a big girl named Sue. Sue was loud. She said, "Hi Tim and Max! Do you want to play with me?" Tim said, "Yes! Let's play tag!" They all played tag and had fun. Then, something unexpected happened. A big, loud bird came to the park. It took the ball and flew away. Tim, Max, and Sue were sad. They wanted to complete their game. So, they all worked together and made a new ball from leaves. They played and had more fun than before. The trip to the park was a happy day for all.
