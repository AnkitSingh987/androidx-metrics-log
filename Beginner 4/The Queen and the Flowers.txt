Category: Beginner
Title: The Queen and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a queen who lived in a big castle. The queen had a beautiful garden with many flowers. Every day, she would go out and spray water on the flowers to help them grow. One dark night, the queen heard a noise from the garden. She went outside to see what it was. "Who is there?" she asked. "It's me, the cat!" said a voice. The queen was surprised to see a cat in her garden. The cat was spraying water on the flowers too. The queen and the cat became friends, and they sprayed water on the flowers together every day. And the flowers grew big and beautiful.

Once there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy saw. He loved to play with his saw all day. One day, he saw his dad using a real saw. It was sharp and cut wood fast. Tim wanted to help his dad. Tim went to his dad and said, "Dad, can I help you with my saw?" His dad looked at Tim's toy saw and smiled. "Thank you, Tim," he said, "but your saw is not sharp like mine. It is a toy. You can watch me work, but be careful." Tim watched his dad work. His dad was very good at using the sharp saw. Tim's toy saw got dirty from the wood. It was filthy. Tim did not like his saw being filthy. He went inside and cleaned it. When he came back, his dad was done working. "Look, dad, my saw is clean now," Tim said. His dad smiled and said, "Good job, Tim! Now we can play together with your clean saw." Tim was happy. He knew his toy saw was not sharp like his dad's saw, but he still loved it. He and his dad played together and had a great day.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play together. Today is their birthday. They are very happy. Mom and Dad have a surprise for them. They give them a big box with a bow. Lily and Ben open the box. They see two thick books with pictures and words. They are story books. "Wow, thank you, Mom and Dad!" Lily and Ben say. They hug Mom and Dad. They love books. They want to read them. Mom and Dad smile. They are proud of Lily and Ben. They say, "You are welcome, Lily and Ben. We hope you enjoy the books. They have many stories for you to learn and imagine." Lily and Ben sit on the couch. They open the books. They look at the pictures. They try to read the words. They ask Mom and Dad to help them. They listen to the stories. They are very happy. They have a wonderful birthday.

Once upon a time, there was a lively cat named Whiskers. Whiskers loved to run and play all day. One day, while playing, Whiskers saw a big ball of yarn. It was so big and colorful! Whiskers wanted to play with it. Whiskers jumped on the yarn and started to roll it. The yarn went faster and faster. Suddenly, Whiskers saw a stitch on the yarn. It looked like a little door! Whiskers was curious and wanted to see what was inside the stitch. Whiskers used his paw to open the stitch. He heard a tiny whisper. Out came a little mouse! The mouse was scared at first, but Whiskers didn't want to hurt him. They became friends and played together all day. The unexpected thing was that a cat and a mouse became best friends. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a normal dog named Spot. Spot liked to play outside with his friends. One day, Spot found a piece of gum on the ground. He thought it was a toy and started to play with it. He threw it up in the air and caught it in his mouth. Spot did not know that gum was not good for him. He started to feel sick and shiver. His friends saw him shivering and knew something was wrong. They went to find help from a big person. The big person saw the gum and knew it was bad for Spot. The big person tried to help Spot, but it was too late. Spot was very sick from the gum. The moral of the story is to always be careful with what you find on the ground and never eat things that are not good for you.
