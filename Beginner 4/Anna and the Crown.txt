Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and the Crown

Anna liked to play with her dolls. She had a big doll that was a queen. The queen had a shiny crown and a long dress. Anna liked to make the queen talk and tell stories. One day, Anna's mom said they had to go to the store. Anna wanted to bring her queen doll, but mom said no. "The store is too rough for your doll," mom said. "She might get dirty or lost. You can play with her when we come back." Anna was sad, but she put her doll on her bed and kissed her goodbye. She went with mom to the store. The store was very big and had many things. Anna saw some toys and books and candy. She wanted to look at them, but mom said they had to hurry. Anna followed mom, but she felt bored. She wished she had her queen doll with her. She missed her doll's voice and stories. She wondered what her doll was doing on her bed. Then, Anna saw something that made her eyes wide. It was a crown, just like her doll's crown. It was shiny and had many colors. It was on a shelf with other things that looked like jewels. Anna ran to the shelf and reached for the crown. She wanted to touch it and wear it. She thought it would remind her of her doll. But before she could grab the crown, a loud voice stopped her. It was a man who worked at the store. He looked angry and scared. He said, "Hey, kid, don't touch that! That's not a toy, that's a real crown! It belongs to the real queen, who is coming to visit the store today!" Anna was shocked and scared. She dropped the crown and ran back to mom. She did not want to see the real queen. She only wanted her doll queen. She hugged mom and said, "Mom, can we go home now? I want to play with my doll. She is the best queen ever."

Once upon a time, there was a big brown house. In the house, there was a family. The family loved to sit by the fireplace. The fireplace was very warm. One day, the fireplace broke. The family was sad. They wanted to repair the fireplace. So, they called a man to fix it. The man came and fixed the fireplace. He worked hard to repair it. The family was happy again. They sat by the warm fireplace and had fun together. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park. The sky was gray, but Mia wanted to play. She saw a boy named Tim. Tim was eating a jam sandwich. "Can I have some jam?" Mia asked. Tim said, "No, this is my jam!" Mia felt sad, but she didn't give up. She insisted, "Please, can I have some jam?" Tim didn't want to share, so he ran away with his jam sandwich. Mia chased him, but she tripped and fell. The jam sandwich fell too, and it was all dirty. Mia and Tim both cried. They didn't get to eat the jam sandwich, and they didn't have fun at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a modest boy named Tim. He had a big book called a dictionary. Tim liked to talk to the dictionary every day. The dictionary was his best friend. One day, Tim said, "Hello, dictionary! Let's talk." The dictionary replied, "Hello, Tim! I am happy to talk with you." They talked about many things like colors, animals, and toys. Tim learned new words from the dictionary. As they talked, Tim's mom came into the room. She saw Tim and the dictionary talking. She smiled and said, "I am glad you have a friend, Tim." Tim smiled back and said, "Yes, mom. The dictionary is my best friend!"

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim found a big boot in his yard. He did not know where it came from. He asked his mom to explain. She said a rich man lost it. Tim wanted to find the rich man and give the boot back. The next day, Tim went to the park with the boot. He saw a man with only one boot on. Tim thought this must be the rich man. The man looked sad. Tim went to the man and showed him the big boot. The man was very happy to see his boot. He said, "Thank you, Tim!" He told Tim he would give him a big gift for being so nice. Tim felt happy that he could help the rich man find his lost boot. And they both lived happily ever after.
