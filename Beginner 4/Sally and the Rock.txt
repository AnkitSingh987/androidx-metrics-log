Category: Beginner
Title: Sally and the Rock

Once upon a time, a little girl named Sally went to find a victory. She walked and walked until she saw a big tree. Under the tree, she found a shiny rock. Sally was happy and said, "I found a victory!" A green frog, named Fred, saw Sally and the shiny rock. Fred was envious. He wanted a victory too. He hopped to Sally and said, "Can I have the shiny rock?" Sally smiled and said, "We can share the victory!" They both played with the shiny rock and were very happy. From that day on, Sally and Fred were best friends, and they always shared their victories.

Once upon a time, there was a little car named Zoomy. Zoomy loved to drive fast on the road. One day, Zoomy saw a big strip of mud. He thought it would be fun to drive through it. Zoomy drove through the mud and got very filthy. He was so dirty that he could not see where he was going. Zoomy tried to reverse, but he was stuck in the mud. He felt sad and didn't know what to do. A kind truck named Helpy saw Zoomy and pulled him out of the mud. Helpy told Zoomy that it is important to stay clean and be careful where we go. Zoomy learned that being clean and safe is better than being dirty and stuck.

One day, a little girl named Lucy was playing in her room. She was confused because she could not find her favorite toy. Lucy looked everywhere, under the bed, behind the chair, and even in the cupboard. She asked her mom, "Mom, have you seen my toy?" Her mom said, "No, but maybe it is in the kitchen." Lucy went to the kitchen and looked in the cupboard. She saw a big pot and a small pot, but no toy. Then, she heard a noise. It sounded like something striking the cupboard door. Lucy was scared, but she opened the cupboard anyway. Inside the cupboard, Lucy found a little bird! The bird had been striking the door to get out. Lucy was so happy to help the bird. She opened the window, and the bird flew away. Then, Lucy saw her toy on the windowsill. It had been there all along! She laughed and took her toy back to her room to play.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy he did not like. It was cheap and not fun. He wanted to own a new toy. So, Tim went to the park to find a friend who had a better toy. At the park, Tim saw a girl named Sue. Sue had a nice toy car. Tim liked the car a lot. He asked Sue, "Can I play with your toy car?" Sue said, "Yes, but can I play with your toy too?" Tim said, "Sure, but it is not a fun toy." Tim and Sue played with the toys at the park. They had a lot of fun. They became good friends. Tim learned that even a cheap toy can be fun when you share it with a friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a big wardrobe in her room. Inside the wardrobe, there were many pretty clothes and sweet treats. One night, Mia had a dream. In the dream, she went inside her wardrobe and found a magical world. There were big trees, happy animals, and a rainbow in the sky. Mia played with her new animal friends and shared her sweet treats with them. They all laughed and had a fun time together. When Mia woke up, she smiled and remembered her wonderful dream.

Tom and Jen are friends. They like to play soccer. They have a big game today. They want to win a trophy. The trophy is shiny and gold. It has a soccer ball on top. Before the game, Tom and Jen organize their team. They tell their teammates what to do. Tom is the captain. He says, "We have to work together. We have to pass the ball. We have to score goals." Jen is the goalie. She says, "I will stop the ball. I will catch it. I will throw it to you." The game starts. Tom and Jen's team plays well. They pass the ball. They score goals. The other team is not happy. They wear gray shirts. They are mean. They push and kick. They cheat. The game is almost over. Tom and Jen's team is winning. They have one more goal to score. Tom has the ball. He runs to the goal. He sees Jen. She is open. He passes the ball to her. Jen kicks the ball. The ball flies in the air. It goes over the other goalie's head. It lands in the net. Goal! Tom and Jen's team cheers. They win the game. They win the trophy. They are happy. They hug and high-five. They lift the trophy in the air. They say, "We did it! We are the best!" The other team is sad. They walk away. They have no trophy. They are gray.
