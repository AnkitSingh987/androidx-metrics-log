Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Walk

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They went down the clean road, and Tim held his mom's hand. They saw many pretty things like flowers and birds. As they walked, they met a girl named Sue. Sue said, "Hi, Tim! I like your clean road. Can I walk with you?" Tim said, "Yes, you can walk with us." Tim, Sue, and Tim's mom walked down the clean road together. They held hands and talked about the fun things they saw. They all had a great time on their walk.

Once upon a time there was a unique frog. It had two giant feet, two giant eyes and two giant hands. One day, the frog went to explore a pond. When it got there, it saw something special. The water was so clear that the frog could see a big open treasure chest on the bottom. The frog was so excited that it dived right in to get the treasure. It was a long way down, but finally the frog made it to the bottom of the pond. With its two giant hands, it opened the treasure chest up. Inside, the frog found many beautiful things. The frog was so happy and filled with joy! It shared the unique treasure with all of its friends. They all had so much fun playing with all of the special items from the chest. The frog had a great day and a very special memory!

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy dove named Dolly. Dolly lived in a big tree. Every day, she would fly and play with her friends. One day, Dolly saw a big cat near the tree. The cat wanted to catch Dolly's friends. Dolly was scared. She wanted to prevent the cat from catching her friends. Dolly thought of a plan. She went to her friend, the wise owl. "Help me, Owl," said Dolly. "A big cat wants to catch my friends. What can I do?" Owl thought for a moment. "Make a loud noise," said Owl. "The big cat will be scared and run away." Dolly flew back to the tree. She flapped her wings and made a loud noise. The big cat was scared and ran away. Dolly's friends were safe. They all thanked Dolly for being brave and saving them.

Anna and Ben like to walk in the park with their mom. They see many things: trees, flowers, birds, and bugs. They have fun and learn new words. One day, they see something long and thin on the grass. It is brown and black and has a tongue that goes in and out. Anna and Ben are curious and want to touch it. "Mom, what is that?" Anna asks. "That is a snake, Anna. It is an animal that has no legs and moves on the ground. Some snakes are nice, but some snakes are mean. They can bite and hurt you. You should not touch a snake without asking first," mom says. "Wow, a snake! It looks cool. Can we say hello to it?" Ben asks. Mom shakes her head. "No, Ben. This snake is not friendly. It is a smart snake. It knows we are here and it does not like us. It will try to scare us away or attack us if we get too close. We should leave it alone and walk away," mom says. Anna and Ben nod. They understand that the snake is not a toy or a pet. They respect the snake and its space. They walk away and look for other things to see in the park. They are smart kids.

Once upon a time, a little bird named Bobo lived in a tree. Every morning, Bobo would wake up early and sing a happy song. One day, Bobo felt anxious because he could not guess what was wrong with his friend, the squirrel. Bobo asked the squirrel, "Why are you sad?" The squirrel said, "I lost my nuts and now I have nothing to eat." Bobo wanted to help his friend, so they looked for the nuts together. They looked high and low, and finally, they found the nuts under a big leaf. The squirrel was very happy and said, "Thank you, Bobo! You are a good friend." From that day on, Bobo learned that helping friends is important. The moral of the story is to always help your friends when they need it.
