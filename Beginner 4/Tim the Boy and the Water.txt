Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Water

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with water. He would fill up the sink and splash around. One day, Tim's mom told him not to play with the sink water. She said it was not safe. But Tim could not resist. The next day, Tim's mom went to the store. Tim saw his chance to play with the sink water. He filled the sink up high and started to splash. He had so much fun! But then, the water started to taste bitter. Tim did not like it. He stopped splashing and looked around. Tim saw that the sink was leaking. Water was everywhere! He remembered his mom's warning. Tim felt bad for not listening. He quickly cleaned up the water before his mom came back. From that day on, Tim never played with the sink water again. He learned to listen to his mom and stay safe.

Once upon a time, there was a lemon. The lemon was thick and round. It lived in a big tree with many other lemons. They were all very happy. One day, a boy came to the tree. He saw the thick lemon and wanted it. The boy tried to get the lemon, but it was too high. He was sad. A tall man saw the boy and wanted to help. The man reached up and picked the thick lemon from the tree. The boy was very happy. He said, "Thank you!" The man and the boy became friends, and they shared the lemon. They were both happy.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big, dry tree. In the tree, he saw a parrot. The parrot was very pretty. It had many colors. "Hello, parrot!" said Tim. The parrot looked at him and said, "Hello, Tim!" Tim was very surprised. He did not know that parrots could talk. Then, a big wind came. The wind blew the parrot out of the tree. Tim tried to catch the parrot, but he missed. The parrot fell into a big puddle. The parrot was wet and sad. Tim felt bad for the parrot. He picked it up and gave it a soft slap on its back to get the water off. The parrot smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim!" And then, something unexpected happened. The parrot turned into a beautiful fairy! The fairy said, "I was stuck as a parrot, but you helped me. Now I am free!" Tim and the fairy became good friends and played in the park all day.

Mia and Ben are twins. They like to play in the garden with their mom. Mom grows many things in the garden, like flowers, carrots, and tomatoes. Mia and Ben love to eat tomatoes. They are red and juicy and sweet. One day, Mia and Ben see a big tomato on a vine. It is bigger than their hands. They want to pick it and share it. They ask mom if they can. "Sure, you can," mom says. "But be careful. The vine is very tall and you might fall." Mia and Ben nod. They hope they can reach the tomato. They take a stool and a basket. They take turns to stand on the stool and try to grab the tomato. But it is too high. They stretch and jump and wiggle, but they cannot touch it. "Let me try," Ben says. He climbs on the stool again. He leans forward and reaches with both hands. He feels the tomato. He pulls hard. The tomato comes off the vine. But Ben loses his balance. He falls off the stool. He lands on the grass. The tomato rolls away. "Ben, are you okay?" Mia asks. She runs to him. She sees him lying on the grass. He looks dizzy. He rubs his head. "I'm okay," Ben says. He sits up. He smiles. He sees the tomato. He picks it up. He gives it to Mia. "Here, you can have it. You are my sister." Mia hugs Ben. She is happy. She thanks him. She breaks the tomato in half. She gives one half to Ben. They eat the tomato together. It is delicious. They share a big bite with mom. Mom is proud of them. She says they are very kind and brave. Mia and Ben are happy. They hope they can find more big tomatoes in the garden.
