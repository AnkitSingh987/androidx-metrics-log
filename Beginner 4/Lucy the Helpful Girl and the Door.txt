Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Helpful Girl and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a helpful little girl named Lucy. She loved to welcome her mother home every day. Lucy would wait by the door and give her mother a big hug when she came in. One day, Lucy's mother was very tired. She had a long day at work. Lucy wanted to be helpful, so she made her mother a cup of tea. Lucy's mother was so happy to see her little girl being so kind. Lucy and her mother sat down and drank the tea together. They talked and laughed, and Lucy's mother felt much better. From that day on, Lucy always welcomed her mother home with a smile and a hug. They both knew that being helpful and kind made them very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to pretend he was a prince. He wore a shiny hat and a big, red cape. One day, Tim met a weak cat in his yard. The cat was very thin and tired. Tim, being a kind prince, wanted to help the cat. He gave the cat some food and water, and the cat felt better. The cat was so happy that it played with Tim all day. They had lots of fun together. Tim was a good prince, and the cat was his new friend. They lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a gloomy tree. Under the tree, he found a small, green lizard. Tim wanted to be friends with the lizard, so he said, "Hi, lizard! Can we play?" The lizard looked at Tim and said, "Yes, let's play! But first, can you help me find my hat? I lost it near the big rock." Tim and the lizard went to the big rock. They looked and looked, but they could not find the hat. Suddenly, a big bird came down from the sky. The bird had the hat in its beak! The bird said, "I saw your hat and thought it was a snack. I'm sorry." Tim was quick and he grabbed the hat from the bird. He gave the hat back to the lizard, and they all became friends. From that day on, Tim, the lizard, and the bird played together under the gloomy tree.

Bob wanted to go to the opera. He got dressed in his suit and put on his best shoes. He was so excited! He went to the theater and put his heavy bag on the seat. Bob looked around and saw a lot of people dressed up in nice clothes. The music was loud and exciting. Bob got to see the opera. He watched the singers and the dancers. He was so amazed! When the opera finished, Bob put his heavy bag back on his shoulder and went home. He was so happy he got to see the opera, and he couldn't wait to tell his friends all about it.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to take a nap. He was very tired from playing all day. He went to his room and lay down on his bed. Before he closed his eyes, he saw his toy knife on the floor. He picked it up and put it on his pillow. "Good night, toy knife," Tim said. "I hope we both have a successful nap." While Tim was napping, a small bug crawled into his room. It saw the toy knife on the pillow and thought it was real. The bug was scared and ran away to tell its friends. The bug's friends didn't believe the story. They wanted to see it for themselves. So, they all went to Tim's room and saw the toy knife. They realized it was just a toy and started to laugh. When Tim woke up from his nap, he saw the bugs laughing and playing with his toy knife. He laughed too and joined them. They all had a fun time playing together.

Ben and Lily were twins who liked to play together. They had many toys, but their favorite was a big red ball. They would throw it, catch it, kick it, and roll it. Sometimes they would repeat the same game over and over, until their mom called them for dinner. One day, they decided to play a new game. They would take turns throwing the ball as high as they could, and see who could catch it. Ben went first. He threw the ball very high, and waited for it to come down. But it did not come down. It got stuck on a branch of a tall tree. "Oh no!" Ben said. "Our ball is gone!" Lily tried to cheer him up. "Don't worry, Ben. Maybe we can get it later. Let's go have dinner now. Mom made spaghetti and meatballs, your favorite!" Ben nodded, but he was still sad. He loved his ball. He hoped it would not stay in the tree forever. They went inside and washed their hands. Mom served them plates of spaghetti and meatballs. They looked yummy and smelled good. Ben and Lily started to eat, but Ben did not have much appetite. He kept thinking about his ball. "Ben, why are you not eating?" Mom asked. "Is something wrong?" Ben looked at Mom and sighed. He told her what happened to his ball. He hoped she would not be mad. Mom smiled and hugged him. "I'm sorry, Ben. I know how much you love your ball. But don't worry, we can get it back. After dinner, we can go outside and use a ladder to reach the branch. How does that sound?" Ben's eyes lit up. He felt happy again. He hugged Mom back and said, "Thank you, Mom. You are the best!" He finished his dinner quickly. He ate all his spaghetti and meatballs, even though they were heavy in his tummy. He wanted to have energy to get his ball back. He and Lily helped Mom clear the table and wash the dishes. Then they put on their jackets and shoes and went outside. Mom brought a ladder and leaned it against the tree. She climbed up and reached for the ball. She got it and threw it down to Ben. "Here you go, Ben. Your ball is back!" Mom said. Ben caught the ball and hugged it. He was so happy. He thanked Mom and said, "I love you, Mom. And I love my ball!" He and Lily played with the ball again. They repeated their game of throwing and catching, but this time they were careful not to throw it too high. They had fun until it was dark. Then they went inside and got ready for bed. They brushed their teeth, put on their pajamas, and snuggled in their beds. Mom read them a story and kissed them goodnight. "Goodnight, Ben. Goodnight, Lily. I love you both," Mom said. "Goodnight, Mom. We love you too," Ben and Lily said. They closed their eyes and dreamed of their ball. They were happy.
