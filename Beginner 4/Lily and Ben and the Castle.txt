Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Castle

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play and dance together. One day, they go to the park with their moms. There is a big slide and a swing and a sandbox. Lily and Ben are happy. They run to the slide and take turns. They go fast and laugh. Then they go to the swing and push each other. They go high and feel the wind. Then they go to the sandbox and make a castle. They use a bucket and a shovel and some sticks. They are proud of their castle. But then a big boy comes. He is mean. He kicks their castle and breaks it. He says, "This is my sandbox. Go away." Lily and Ben are sad. They frown and say, "No, this is our castle. You are bad. Go away." But the big boy does not listen. He kicks their bucket and shovel and sticks. He laughs and says, "You are babies. You can't stop me." Lily and Ben are scared. They run to their moms and tell them what happened. Their moms are angry. They go to the big boy and say, "You are rude. You can't do that. You have to say sorry and share." But the big boy does not listen. He says, "No, I don't. This is my park. You go away." He sticks out his tongue and runs away. Lily and Ben are still sad. They don't want to play anymore. They hug their moms and say, "We want to go home." Their moms say, "OK, let's go home. But first, let's do something fun. Let's dance." They turn on some music and start to dance. They move their arms and legs and hips and heads. They smile and giggle. They forget about the big boy and the broken castle. They are happy again. They dance and dance until it is time to go home. They say bye to each other and hug. They say, "We are friends. We like to dance. We will play again tomorrow." They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a modern house near the sea. In this house, lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play on the beach and look for fun things in the sand. One day, Tim went to the beach to play. He saw a jellyfish in the water. The jellyfish was big and colorful. Tim wanted to touch it, but he knew that jellyfish could hurt him. As Tim was walking away from the jellyfish, he started to stumble on the sand. He fell down and laughed. Tim's mom came to help him up, and they went home together. They had a fun day at the beach.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a humble cat. The cat loved to move around and play with its friends. The cat was very quick and could run very fast. One day, the cat saw a big ball. The cat wanted to play with the ball, so it moved towards the ball quickly. The cat was very excited and happy to play with the ball. But the cat did not see the big car coming. The car was moving very fast too. The cat tried to move away, but it was too late. The car hit the cat and the cat was hurt. The cat could not play with the ball anymore. The cat's friends were very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a brave bird named Billy. Billy lived in a big tree with his friends. They all played together every day. One day, Billy saw a long wire near the tree. He told his friends, "Look at that wire! I want to try and sit on it." His friends were scared, but Billy was brave. He said, "Trust me, it will be fun!" Billy flew up to the wire and sat on it. Suddenly, the wire started to move! It was not just a wire, but a big snake! Billy's friends shouted, "Billy, be careful! It's a snake!" Billy was scared, but he stayed brave. He quickly flew back to his friends in the tree. Billy and his friends learned to be more careful and to trust each other. They still played together every day, but they always watched out for snakes. And they never forgot the day when brave Billy sat on the wire that was really a snake.
