Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and the Hat

Sara liked to play dress-up with her friends. She had many hats in her closet. Some were big, some were small, some were colorful, some were plain. She liked to try them on and pretend to be different people. One day, she invited Anna and Ben to her house. They were her popular friends. They always had fun together. They went to her room and opened her closet. Sara said, "Let's play dress-up! You can choose any hat you want." Anna picked a pink hat with flowers. She put it on and said, "I am a princess. I live in a castle. You are my guests." Ben picked a black hat with a star. He put it on and said, "I am a magician. I can do tricks. Watch this." He took a wand from his pocket and waved it. Sara looked for a hat. She saw a brown hat with a hole. She wondered what was inside. She picked it up and put it on. She felt something move on her head. She said, "I am a... a..." She did not know what to say. Anna and Ben looked at her. They saw something peek out of the hole. It was a furry ear. Then they saw another ear. Then they saw a nose. Then they saw two eyes. It was a rabbit! The rabbit jumped out of the hat and ran around the room. Sara, Anna, and Ben screamed. They were scared and surprised. They did not know how the rabbit got in the hat. They did not know what to do. The rabbit hopped on the bed, on the desk, on the chair. It knocked over books, toys, and pictures. Sara's mom heard the noise. She came to the room and saw the mess. She saw the rabbit. She said, "Oh, Sara, you found my old hat. I used to have a pet rabbit when I was a girl. He liked to hide in the hat. I forgot I still had it. I'm sorry he scared you. He is very friendly. Do you want to pet him?" Sara, Anna, and Ben calmed down. They looked at the rabbit. He looked cute and soft. They nodded. They went to the bed and petted the rabbit. He licked their hands and wiggled his nose. They smiled. They liked the rabbit. They said, "He is a nice rabbit. Can we play with him?" Sara's mom said, "Sure, you can play with him. But be careful. He likes to chew on things. And don't forget to put him back in the hat when you are done. He likes to sleep there." Sara, Anna, and Ben said, "Okay, thank you." They played with the rabbit and the hat. They had fun. They forgot about the other hats. They decided the brown hat with the hole was the best hat. It was a magic hat. It could reveal a surprise.

Once upon a time, there was a strong boy named Tim. Tim had a red wagon that he loved to play with. One day, he went to the park with his wagon. At the park, Tim saw a big pond. He saw kids dive into the pond and swim. Tim wanted to dive too, but he was scared. So, he played with his wagon near the pond. Tim watched the kids dive and have fun. He knew he was strong like them. So, he took a deep breath and dived into the pond. Tim felt happy and brave. The other kids cheered for him. From that day, Tim loved to dive and play with his wagon at the park.

Once upon a time there was a girl called Rose. Rose loved chocolate. One day Rose went to the sweet shop and saw that they had lots of chocolate. Rose wanted to buy all of the chocolate, but she didn't have enough money. Rose was very selfish and asked her mum for some money. Her mum said no, so Rose had to pass the chocolate and go home. On the way home, Rose saw a boy called John. John was also looking at the chocolate and wanted to buy some. Rose wanted to keep all of the chocolate for herself, so she passed it and walked away. John was sad because he didn't have enough money to buy the chocolate. Rose felt bad and gave John some of her own chocolate. They both share it and ate it happily. Rose learnt that it was better to be nice and share than to be selfish. From then on, Rose was always kind and shared with all of her friends.
