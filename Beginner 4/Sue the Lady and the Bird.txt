Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Lady and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was an elderly lady named Sue. Sue liked to take part in a fun class. The class was for dancing. Sue loved to dance with her friends. They would dance and laugh all day long. One sunny day, Sue went to her dance class. She danced and danced with her friends. Suddenly, a big wind came and blew the roof off the class! Sue and her friends were very surprised. They did not know what to do. Then, a big bird flew down from the sky. The bird said, "I can help you!" The bird picked up the roof with its strong wings. It put the roof back on the class. Sue and her friends clapped and cheered. The big bird had saved the day! From then on, the bird took part in the dance class too. They all danced happily together.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Tim. He loved to smile and play with his toys. One day, his mom made some yummy soup for lunch. Tim sat at the table and looked at the soup. It was hot and smelled good. He took a small spoon and tasted it. The soup made him smile because it was so tasty. After lunch, Tim went outside to play. He saw a small cat and smiled at it. The cat came close and Tim shared some of his soup with the cat. They both smiled and enjoyed the soup together. And from that day, Tim and the cat became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big, hairy dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to play with his stuff, like his ball and his bone. One day, Buddy saw a little girl named Sally. Sally was sad because she lost her toy. Buddy wanted to help Sally. He looked and looked for her toy. When he found the toy, he brought it back to her. Sally was so happy! She hugged Buddy and said, "Thank you!" Buddy learned that helping others is a good thing to do. From that day on, Buddy always tried to help his friends when they needed it. And when Buddy was tired, he would sit and rest, knowing he did a good thing.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a piece of cloth. She wanted to make a pretty dress for her doll. Sue asked her mom to help her. Her mom said, "Let's add some stitches to make the dress." Sue and her mom took a needle and thread. They made little stitches on the cloth. Sue was very proud of her work. She said, "Look, mom! I made a stitch!" When they finished, Sue put the dress on her doll. The doll looked very pretty. Sue was proud of the dress she made with her mom. They had fun adding stitches together.

Once upon a time, in the middle of the forest, there was a small village. In this village, there lived a young boy named Jack. One day, he decided to take a walk in the woods. Suddenly, he heard a voice calling for help! It was a voice of an old man who had sunk in the soil. Jack tried to help the old man by pulling him out, but he was too weak. Jack then called his friends to help him. With their combined strength, they managed to get the old man out. The old man thanked the boys, but he had nothing to pay them. Jack, however, remained humble and insisted that they didn't need to be paid. The old man then said "Not being paid will make you ignorant, kind boy". To which Jack responded "Ignorance is not bliss; but compassion is worth more than gold". The old man was moved by these words and thanked Jack and his friends. From then on, Jack was known in the village as "The Compassionate Boy".

Once upon a time, there was a small volcano. It was not like other big volcanoes. This volcano was very nice and safe. It did not hurt anyone. One day, a magic bird came to the volcano. The bird had a special power. It could make things shrink. The bird saw the volcano and thought it would be fun to use its power. The bird waved its wings and said a magic word. The volcano started to shrink. It became smaller and smaller. Now, the volcano was tiny like a toy. All the animals came to see the tiny volcano. They were happy because it was safe and fun to play with.
