Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Blue Bird and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a blue bird named Bob. Bob lived in a big tree with his friends. One day, he saw a shiny bracelet on the ground. It was very pretty and sparkly. Bob wanted to grab the bracelet and show his friends. Bob flew down and grabbed the bracelet in his beak. He took it to his friends in the tree. They all loved the bracelet too. But then, a little girl came looking for her lost bracelet. She was very sad and cried. Bob's friends told him to give the bracelet back to the little girl. Bob knew it was the right thing to do. He grabbed the bracelet and flew down to the little girl. He dropped the bracelet into her hand. The little girl was so happy and thanked Bob. Bob felt good because he did the right thing. The moral of the story is to always do the right thing, even if it means giving up something you like.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Rose. Rose loved playing with her dolls and having fun. One day, Rose went on a long walk in the dry desert. She wanted to see what she could see. When she arrived at the end of the desert, she found a tall tower. Just then, Rose saw a small raccoon on the tower and he was smiling. Rose smiled too and asked, "What is your name, little raccoon?" The raccoon replied, "My name is Victor. I am looking for someone to play a game with me." "Let's play!" said Rose. They played a game called "Victory" and Rose and Victor both tried their hardest to win. In the end, Rose won! She was so happy and Victor looked very proud of her. Rose and Victor hugged each other. Rose loved the way Victor cheered for her. Then, they both walked back to Rose's house, where they said goodbye and promised to play again.

Ben and Sam were brothers who liked to play outside. One day, they decided to make a camp in the woods behind their house. They took a big sheet, some sticks, and a rope. They found a nice spot under a big tree and built their camp. They were very proud of their camp. They played in their camp for a long time. They pretended to be explorers, hunters, and pirates. They had a lot of fun. But they also got very dirty. Their clothes, hands, and faces were covered in mud, leaves, and bugs. They did not care. They liked to be dirty. But their mom did not like them to be dirty. She called them from the house. "Ben! Sam! Time to come in and have a bath!" she shouted. Ben and Sam did not want to have a bath. They liked their camp. They did not reply. They hid in their camp and hoped their mom would not find them. But their mom was not fooled. She knew where they were. She walked to the woods and saw their camp. She smiled. She remembered when she was a kid and liked to play outside too. But she also knew they needed to be clean. She lifted the sheet and peeked inside. She saw Ben and Sam crouching in the corner. They looked scared. "Hello, boys," she said. "I see you made a nice camp. It looks very cozy. But you also look very dirty. You need to have a bath. Come on, let's go inside." Ben and Sam knew they could not hide anymore. They crawled out of their camp and followed their mom. They were sad to leave their camp. But they also knew their mom was right. They needed to have a bath. Maybe they could play in their camp again tomorrow.

One day, a fat cat went for a walk. The cat saw a well. The cat was very thirsty, so it went to the well to drink some water. As the cat was drinking, it heard a snap sound. The cat looked around and saw a big frog. The frog said, "Hi, fat cat! I live in the well. What are you doing here?" The cat said, "I was thirsty and came to drink water. What was that snap sound?" The frog smiled and said, "That snap sound was me. I can snap my fingers, and something fun will happen. Watch!" The frog snapped its fingers, and the well turned into a big pool of ice cream. The cat and the frog laughed and ate ice cream together. They became best friends and had lots of fun at the ice cream well.
