Category: Beginner
Title: The Filthy Sheep and the Butterfly

Once upon a time, there was a filthy sheep. The sheep lived on a big farm with many friends. The sheep liked to play in the mud with the pigs. They had a lot of fun together. One day, the farmer wanted to cut the sheep's wool. The sheep did not want to be cut. It ran and hid behind a big tree. The farmer looked and looked, but he could not find the sheep. Then, something unexpected happened. The filthy sheep saw a big butterfly. The butterfly was stuck in a bush. The sheep used its wool to help the butterfly. The wool got cut on the bush, and the butterfly was free. The farmer saw this and smiled. He did not need to cut the sheep's wool anymore. The sheep and the butterfly became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a helpful girl named Sue. Sue liked to prepare food for her family. One day, she wanted to make a big, yummy cake. But she did not know how to make it. Sue asked her friend, Tim, to help her. They started to make the cake together. They mixed the flour, sugar, and eggs. But when they were mixing, a big bubble came out of the bowl! The bubble grew bigger and bigger. Sue and Tim were scared. They tried to pop the bubble, but it was too big. They thought of a plan. They found a big stick and poked the bubble. The bubble popped and the cake mix went all over the room. Sue and Tim laughed and cleaned up the mess. They made a new cake and it was very yummy. Sue and Tim were happy because they solved the problem together.

One hot day, Tom and Sue were playing outside. The sun was very big and bright. They saw the sky get dark and heard a loud sound. Tom looked up and saw a big tornado coming. He said, "Sue, we need to go inside now!" Sue was scared and asked, "What is that, Tom?" Tom said, "It's a tornado, Sue. It is big and strong. We need to hide." They ran inside their house and went to the safe room. The tornado was outside, but they were safe. They blinked their eyes because it was dark in the room. They held hands and waited for the tornado to go away. After some time, the sound of the tornado stopped. Tom and Sue peeked outside the room. The tornado was gone, and the sun was shining again. They went outside and saw that everything was okay. They were happy that they were safe and could play outside again.

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Max. Max loved to run and jump in the park. One day, Max saw a big cross in the park. He was very curious about it. Max went to the cross and saw a little boy named Tim. Tim was holding a ball. Max wanted to play with Tim and his ball. Max barked at Tim and wagged his tail. Tim looked at Max and said, "Do you want to play with me, Max?" Max barked happily, and Tim threw the ball. Max ran and caught the ball in his mouth. They played together all day. When it was time for Tim to go home, Max received a big hug from Tim. Max was very happy and couldn't wait to play with Tim again.

One day, a crazy cat and a funny dog saw a big ball. They both wanted to play with it. They started to fight. "Stop!" said a little boy. He held up his finger. "We can all play together." The cat and the dog stopped fighting. They looked at the little boy. They wanted to be friends. The three friends played with the big ball. They had a lot of fun. They laughed and played all day. The cat and the dog did not fight anymore. They were happy to share.

Once upon a time, there was a big whale named Bob. Bob was famous because he could jump very high. All the little fish loved to watch him jump. One day, a new fish named Tim came to the sea. Tim was sad because he could not jump like Bob. Bob saw Tim and realized he wanted to help. Bob swam to Tim and said, "Do not be sad. We can be friends and I will teach you how to jump." Tim was happy to have a new friend. They practiced jumping every day, but Tim still could not jump like Bob. Then, one day, Tim tried very hard and jumped high like Bob. All the little fish cheered for Tim. Tim was so happy and thanked Bob for helping him. Bob was happy too because he could help his friend. From that day on, Bob and Tim were best friends and they jumped together in the sea.
