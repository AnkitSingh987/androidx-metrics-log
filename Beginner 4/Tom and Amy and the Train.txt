Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Amy and the Train

Tom and Amy were twins who liked to go on adventures. One day, they went with their mom to the big city. They saw many tall buildings, noisy cars and colorful shops. They also saw a sign that said "subway". "What is a subway, mom?" Tom asked. "A subway is a train that goes under the ground. It can take us to different places in the city faster than a bus or a car," mom explained. "Can we ride the subway, mom? Please, please, please!" Amy begged. "Okay, but you have to stay close to me and hold my hand. The subway can be crowded and loud," mom agreed. They followed mom to the subway station. They bought tickets and waited for the train. They saw a map on the wall that showed different lines and colors. "Look, mom, there are so many lines! How do you know which one to take?" Tom wondered. "Well, you have to choose the right line for where you want to go. See, we want to go to the park, so we have to take the green line. The green line stops at the park station," mom pointed. "I see, I see! That's clever, mom! You are so smart!" Amy praised. "Thank you, Amy. You are very clever too. And so is Tom. Now, let's get on the train and enjoy the ride," mom said. They got on the train and found seats. They watched the windows as the train zoomed under the ground. They saw lights and tunnels and signs. They heard the train go beep and whoosh. They felt the train go fast and slow and stop. They had fun on the subway. They learned something new. They were happy. They loved their mom. And they loved each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a fragile toy bird that he loved very much. One day, Tim went to the park to play with his toy bird. While playing, Tim saw a small animal in a trap. The animal looked sad and scared. Tim wanted to help the animal, so he tried to open the trap. But the trap was too hard to open for Tim. Then, Tim had an idea. He went to his mom and asked her to give him a tool to open the trap. His mom gave him a tool, and Tim went back to the park. He used the tool to open the trap, and the happy animal ran away. Tim felt good that he could help the animal, and he went back to playing with his fragile toy bird.

Sara liked to play with her toys in the big room. There was a piano in the big room. Sara liked the piano. It had many keys that made sounds. Sometimes, Sara would press the keys and make music. One day, Sara was playing with her toys when she heard a knock on the door. She ran to the door and opened it. She saw a man with a big box. The man smiled and said, "Hello, little girl. I'm here to fix the piano. Can I enter?" Sara nodded and let the man enter. She followed him to the piano. The man put the box on the floor and opened it. He took out some tools and some wires. He opened the piano and started to work. Sara was confused. She did not know what the man was doing. She asked, "What are you doing to the piano?" The man looked at Sara and said, "I'm fixing the piano. It has some broken parts. I need to replace them. Then the piano will sound better." Sara did not understand. She said, "But I like the piano. It sounds good to me." The man smiled and said, "Don't worry, little girl. The piano will still sound good to you. It will just sound better to everyone else. It will be more in tune." Sara did not know what "in tune" meant. She said, "OK. Can I help you?" The man said, "Sure. You can hand me the tools and the wires. And you can watch me work. Maybe you will learn something." Sara smiled and said, "OK. I like to learn. And I like to help. And I like the piano."
