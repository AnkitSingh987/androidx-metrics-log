Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Ball

One day, a girl named Lily went to the park. She saw her friend, Tim, playing with a ball. Lily walked up to Tim and said, "Can I play too?" Tim looked at her with a big smile on his face and said, "Yes, let's play!" As they played, a little bird flew down and sat on the ball. Lily and Tim stopped and looked at the bird. The bird looked at them and said, "I insist on playing too." Lily and Tim were very surprised because they did not know that birds could talk. The bird told them, "I am a magic bird, and I can make your day clear and sunny, or I can make it rain." Lily and Tim were happy to have a new friend who could do such amazing things. They played all day long with their new magic bird friend, and they had the best day ever.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They wanted to buy some food. Tim saw a big box of pears. He wanted to eat them. So, he asked his mom, "Can we buy the pears, please?" His mom said yes, and they put the pears in the cart. As they walked, a man with a big, stupid hat came up to them. He was not happy. He said, "You took the last pears! I wanted them!" Tim's mom said, "Sorry, but we need them too." The man was angry and wanted to take the pears from them. Just then, a big truck came into the store. It was full of pears! The driver said, "I have a big load of pears for the store!" The man with the stupid hat was happy now. He could get his own pears. Tim's mom said, "See, now we can all have pears." They all laughed and went to buy their food.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a pair of pants that she loved very much. These pants were very special because they were powerful pants. Mia wore them every day. One day, Mia's powerful pants got dirty from playing outside. Her mom said it was time to change her pants. Mia did not want to change her pants, but she knew she had to listen to her mom. Mia changed her pants and put on a new pair. She was sad to take off her powerful pants. But, when she put on her new pants, she felt happy. Mia learned that it's okay to change and try new things.

Once upon a time, there was a rare round key. It was so special that it could unlock any door! One day, a little girl called Sarah saw the key. She wanted to unlock a big, wooden door that she'd never seen in her house. Sarah grabbed the key, held it tight and put it in the keyhole. She turned the key and knew that her wish was coming true! She heard a clicking sound and the door unlocked. Sarah was so excited! She opened the door and found a world of surprises. The room was filled with toys and games that Sarah had never seen before! She was so happy and had lots of fun. When Sarah was ready to go, she put the key back in the same place and the door locked itself again. No one else could find the key and Sarah knew she was lucky to have discovered it. She had a secret!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very thirsty. He went to the kitchen to get a drink. He saw a faucet. He turned the faucet on and water came out. Tim was happy. Tim took a big sip of the water. But, guess what? The water was bitter! Tim made a funny face. He didn't like the bitter water. He wanted something sweet to drink instead. Tim's mommy saw him and knew what to do. She poured some sweet juice for him. Tim drank the juice and was very happy. He thanked his mommy and gave her a big hug. The bitter water was all gone, and Tim was not thirsty anymore.
