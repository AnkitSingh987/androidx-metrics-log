Category: Beginner
Title: Bessie the Cow and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a happy cow named Bessie. Bessie lived on a ranch with her friends. The ranch was big and had lots of grass to eat. Bessie liked to play with her friends in the sun. One day, Bessie saw a little bird who was sad. The bird said, "I am hungry and can't find any food." Bessie wanted to help. She thought about what she could do to provide food for the bird. Bessie had an idea. She found some bugs in the grass and showed them to the bird. The bird was happy and said, "Thank you, Bessie! Now I can eat and not be hungry anymore." Bessie felt good because she helped her new friend. The bird and Bessie played together every day after that. They were both very happy on the ranch. The moral of the story is: always help your friends when they need it.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big red box in his room. He did not know where it came from. Tim was very excited to open the box. He called his friend, Sam, to come over and see the box too. When Sam came, he said, "Don't open the box, Tim. It might be bad." But Tim did not listen. He ignored Sam and opened the box anyway. Inside the box, there was a big mess of toys and clothes. They were all mixed up and dirty. Tim and Sam started to fight. They were mad at each other. While they were fighting, a big wind came and blew all the toys and clothes away. Now, Tim and Sam had nothing to play with. They were very sad and still mad at each other.

Once upon a time, there was a cool dog named Spot. Spot liked to play and run around all day. One day, Spot found some lumber in the yard. Spot did not know what it was, but he liked to jump over it and play with it. One day, Spot's friend, a little bird, came to play. Spot wanted to introduce his friend to the lumber. So, Spot said, "Bird, this is lumber. It is fun to play with!" Bird looked at the lumber and thought it was cool too. Bird and Spot played with the lumber all day long. After a while, they got tired and sat on the lumber to rest. They looked at each other and smiled. Spot was happy that he could introduce his friend to something new and cool. They both knew they would have many more fun days playing together with the lumber.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a fake toy cat that she loved very much. She took her toy cat with her everywhere she went. One day, Sue went to her friend's house to play. She brought her fake toy cat with her. While they were playing, Sue's friend accidentally made a cut on the toy cat with scissors. Sue felt sad and her friend felt embarrass. Sue's friend said sorry and they both tried to fix the toy cat. They put a band-aid on the cut and Sue felt happy again. Sue and her friend learned to be more careful when playing with toys. They played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to take walks in the park with his mom. One sunny day, they went for a walk and found a rare tree. The tree had special fruits on it. They were small and green. Tim's mom told him they were called olives. Tim wanted to taste the olives, so he asked his mom if he could take one. His mom said, "Yes, but only one, Tim." So, Tim took an olive from the tree and tasted it. It was a little bit salty, but he liked it a lot. Tim and his mom went home after their walk. Tim told his dad about the rare olive tree they found in the park. His dad said, "That's great, Tim! Maybe we can all go to the park and see the tree together next time." Tim smiled and agreed. And they all lived happily ever after.
