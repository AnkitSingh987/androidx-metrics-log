Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and the Dog

Ben was a little boy who liked to play with his toys. He had many toys, but his favorite was a big red car. He liked to zoom it around the floor and make loud noises. He did not like to share his car with anyone. One day, his mom took him to the park. There were many other children there, playing and having fun. Ben saw a girl with a doll. He wanted to play with the doll, so he ran to her and grabbed it. The girl cried and tried to get her doll back, but Ben was stronger. He did not resist his bad feeling. Then, he saw a boy with a figure. It was a figure of a superhero, with a cape and a mask. Ben wanted to play with the figure, so he ran to the boy and grabbed it. The boy shouted and tried to get his figure back, but Ben was faster. He did not resist his bad feeling. He ran away with the doll and the figure, feeling happy. But then, he saw a big dog. The dog was friendly, but Ben did not know that. He thought the dog wanted to take his toys, so he got scared. He dropped the doll and the figure and ran to his mom. He did not resist his fear. The girl and the boy followed him and picked up their toys. They were happy to have them back. They saw the dog and smiled. They knew the dog was friendly, so they petted it and gave it a treat. The dog wagged its tail and licked their faces. They did not resist their good feeling. Ben saw them and felt sad. He realized he had been mean and selfish. He wanted to say sorry and play with them, but he did not know how. He resisted his good feeling. His mom saw him and hugged him. She said, "Ben, you should not take other people's toys. You should ask nicely and share. That is how you make friends. Do you want to try?" Ben nodded and wiped his tears. He did not resist his mom's words. He walked to the girl and the boy and said, "I'm sorry. Can I play with you?" The girl and the boy looked at him and smiled. They said, "Sure. You can play with us. We have enough toys for everyone." They gave him the doll and the figure and invited him to join them. Ben felt happy and thanked them. He did not resist their kindness. He played with them and the dog for a long time. He learned to share and take turns. He made new friends and had fun. He did not resist his happy feeling. The end.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a graceful deer. The deer had long legs and was very fast. She loved to run and jump in the forest. One day, the deer met a little bird. The bird was sad because she forgot where her nest was. The deer wanted to help the bird. The bird's nest reminded her of her own home. The deer and the bird looked for the nest together. They asked other animals in the forest if they had seen the nest. Finally, they found the nest high up in a tree. The bird was so happy and thanked the deer for helping her. From that day on, the deer and the bird became best friends. They played together in the forest every day. The bird always reminded the deer to be careful when running and jumping, and the deer always reminded the bird to be happy and sing her beautiful songs.

One day, a blue bug went for a walk. The sun was bright and the sky was clear. The blue bug was happy to start his day. He walked and walked, looking for his friends to play with. As the blue bug walked, he saw a big red ball. He thought it was his friend, so he went to say hello. But when he got close, the ball started to roll away. The blue bug was sad, but he did not give up. He ran after the ball, trying to catch it. The blue bug ran and ran, until he was very tired. Then, something unexpected happened. The big red ball stopped rolling and turned into a big red bug! The red bug smiled at the blue bug and said, "I was playing a trick on you! I wanted to see if you would chase me." The blue bug laughed and said, "You got me! Let's be friends and play together." And so, the blue bug and the red bug played all day long, happy to have found a new friend.
