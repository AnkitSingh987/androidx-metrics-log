Category: Beginner
Title: Randy and the Bench

Randy was rolling across the park when he saw a fat bench. He wanted to try it out, so he ran up to it. He jumped onto the bench and sat down with a thud! "Woah! This bench is really fat!", Randy exclaimed. His friend, Lilly, laughed and came to sit beside him. "It sure is! Let's roll across the park on it together!", she said. Randy smiled and they both jumped onto the bench. Holding their hands tight they rolled across the park together. Randy and Lilly laughed as they rolled and rolled. When they got to the other side Randy jumped off and looked back at the bench. "Bye fat bench!", he called out. Lilly smiled and waved. Then they ran off together to explore the park some more.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play hockey. He had a hockey stick and a ball. Tim played hockey with his friends every day. One day, while playing hockey, Tim fell down. He got a deep boo-boo on his knee. Tim cried and went to his mom for help. His mom looked at the boo-boo and said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will make it better." Tim's mom put a bandage on the deep boo-boo. She gave it a gentle rub to make it feel better. Tim felt better and smiled. He said, "Thank you, mom!" Tim went back to play hockey with his friends. They played and had lots of fun.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw an enormous red ball in his yard. "Wow, what a big surprise!" said Tim. He was so happy to play with the big ball. Tim played with the ball all day. He kicked it, threw it, and rolled it. The sun was shining bright and hot. Tim started to feel his skin burn from the hot sun. "Ouch!" he said. Tim knew he had to go inside to cool down. He said goodbye to the enormous red ball and went inside his house. He put on some cool clothes and felt better. Tim couldn't wait to play with the big surprise ball again tomorrow.

Once upon a time, there was a hunter. He had a big dog. One day, they went into the woods to look for food. They saw a big tree with lots of fruits. The hunter said, "Look, dog! Let's get some fruits!" The dog barked and wagged its tail. They picked many fruits and put them in a big bag. After that, they walked deeper into the woods. They saw a little bird with a hurt wing. The bird could not fly. The hunter felt sad and wanted to help. "Hello, little bird. Let me help you," said the hunter. He picked up the bird and put it in his big bag with the fruits. The dog licked the bird's wing to make it feel better. The bird was grateful and gave the dog a little kiss. "Thank you, kind hunter and big dog," said the bird. They all went back to the hunter's house. The hunter took care of the bird until it could fly again. The bird, the dog, and the hunter became the best of friends. They lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a small lime tree in her backyard. Every day, she would play near the tree and watch the limes grow bigger and bigger. Mia loved the tree because it gave her tasty limes to eat. One day, a big wind came and made the tree tremble. Mia was scared that the tree would fall. She ran to her mom and told her about the tree. Her mom said, "Don't worry, Mia. The tree is strong and will be okay." The wind stopped, and the tree was still standing. Mia was happy that her lime tree was safe. She picked a tasty lime from the tree and shared it with her mom. They both smiled and enjoyed the tasty lime together.

One day, a little boy named Tim was very hungry. He wanted to eat a big pizza. But he was fearful to go to the kitchen alone. It was dark and scary at night. Tim thought about what to do. Tim remembered his toy robot, Robby. He thought Robby could help him. He went to his room and found Robby. Tim kneeled down and whispered to Robby, "Let's go to the kitchen and get pizza." Robby nodded, and they walked together. In the dark kitchen, Tim and Robby found the pizza. They were not scared anymore. They ate the pizza and were happy. Tim knew that with Robby, he could do anything.
