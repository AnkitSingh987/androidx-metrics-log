Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Tom and the Slide

Lily and Tom were playing in the park with their mom. They saw a big slide and ran to it. Lily went first and slid down with a big smile. Tom followed her and laughed. They liked the slide a lot. "Can we go again, mom?" Lily asked. "Sure, sweetie, but be careful," mom said. Lily and Tom climbed up the stairs again. But when they reached the top, they saw a man sitting on the slide. He had a big bag and a wallet in his hand. He looked angry and sad. "Hey, you, get off the slide!" Tom shouted. The man looked at them and frowned. "No, this is my slide now. Go away, you little brats." Lily and Tom were scared and confused. They did not understand why the man was so mean. They wanted to slide, but they did not want to make the man more angry. "Mom, there is a man on the slide. He won't let us slide. He is mean," Lily said. Mom heard them and came to see. She saw the man and his bag and wallet. She guessed that he had no home and no money. She felt sorry for him, but she also wanted her children to have fun. "Excuse me, sir, can you please move from the slide? My children want to play here," mom said in a kind voice. The man looked at mom and saw that she was not angry or scared. He saw that she was cheerful and nice. He felt a little ashamed and sorry. He realized that he was being rude and selfish. "I'm sorry, ma'am, I didn't mean to bother you. I'm just having a hard time. I have no home, no job, no family. I don't know what to do," the man said in a low voice. Mom nodded and smiled. "I understand, sir, life can be hard sometimes. But you don't have to be alone. There are people who can help you. There is a shelter nearby where you can stay and get some food and clothes. They can also help you find a job and a home. Would you like to go there?" The man thought for a moment. He felt a little hope and gratitude. He nodded and smiled back. "Yes, ma'am, I would like that. Thank you for being so kind to me." Mom took out her phone and called the shelter. They said they would send a car to pick up the man. Mom gave the man a hug and wished him good luck. The man thanked her again and gave her his wallet. He said he did not need it anymore. He said he wanted to give it to her children as a gift. Lily and Tom were surprised and happy. They thanked the man and hugged him too. They looked at the wallet and saw that it had a picture of a dog on it. They liked dogs a lot. "Wow, this is a cool wallet. Thank you, mister," Tom said. "You're welcome, kid. I hope you enjoy it. And have fun on the slide," the man said. The man got in the car and waved goodbye. Lily and Tom waved back. They ran to the slide and slid down together. They were cheerful and proud. They had made a new friend and learned a new lesson. They had learned to be kind and helpful to others. They had learned to question and understand. They had learned to share and care. They had learned to be happy.

One day, a dolphin named Fin was swimming in the sea. Fin loved to sing. He would sing all day and all night. His songs made all the fish and sea animals happy. Fin was a huge dolphin, but he was also very kind. One day, while Fin was singing, he saw a little fish crying. "Why are you sad?" asked Fin. The little fish said, "I want to sing like you, but I'm too small and no one can hear me." Fin had an idea. He told the little fish, "I will help you sing louder." Fin and the little fish swam to a big rock. Fin told the little fish to sit on the rock and sing. The little fish started to sing, and something unexpected happened. The rock was magic! When the little fish sang, his voice became very loud and beautiful. All the animals in the sea came to listen to the little fish sing. They were so happy and surprised. Fin and the little fish became best friends, and they sang together every day. The sea was always filled with their beautiful songs.
