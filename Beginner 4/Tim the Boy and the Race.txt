Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Race

One day, a little boy named Tim and his friend, Sue, wanted to have a race. They wanted to see who was the fastest. They went to a distant place with lots of space to run. They found two seats and put them at the end of the race path. "Are you ready?" Tim asked Sue. Sue nodded her head and said, "Yes, I am ready!" They started to race. Tim and Sue ran as fast as they could. They both wanted to win. But as they got closer to the seats, they saw a big hole in the ground. They did not see it before. They tried to stop, but they could not. They both fell into the hole. Tim and Sue were sad. They did not finish the race. They learned to always look where they were going before starting a race.

Once upon a time, there was a fast rabbit. The rabbit liked to run and jump. One day, the rabbit found a pole. He wanted to measure the pole. The rabbit hopped around the pole. He counted his hops. But, something unexpected happened. A big bird came down from the sky. The bird wanted to help the rabbit measure the pole. The rabbit and the bird worked together. They found out the pole was very long. Then, they heard a noise. It was a big surprise! A squirrel came out of the pole. The squirrel was hiding inside the pole. The rabbit, the bird, and the squirrel became good friends. They played together every day.

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a walk. She saw a huge tree and wanted to climb it. As she started to climb, she heard a sound. She stopped to listen. It was a voice saying, "Don't go into the fog!" Sue looked around and saw fog coming. She was scared, but she did not listen to the voice. She walked into the fog. It was cold and wet. She could not see anything. Sue got lost in the fog. She called for help, but no one heard her. The fog was too thick. Sue stayed in the fog, and she could not find her way back home.

One day, on Tuesday, a boy named Tim and a girl named Sue were playing with their toys. They had many toys, but they both liked the big red ball the most. Tim thought it was important to play with the ball, and Sue thought so too. Tim said, "I want to play with the ball now." Sue said, "I want to play with the ball too." They both wanted to play with the ball, but they did not know how to share. They both held the ball and did not let go. Then, something unexpected happened. A little dog came running into the yard. The dog saw the ball and wanted to play too. Tim and Sue looked at the dog and then at each other. They decided to share the ball with the dog. They threw the ball and the dog ran to catch it. They all laughed and played together. From that day on, Tim and Sue learned to share their toys and had more fun playing together.

Once upon a time, there was a barber named Tom. Tom was a normal man who liked to cut hair. Every day, he went to his shop and cut hair for many people. One day, a little boy named Tim came into the shop. Tim wanted a haircut too. Tom started to cut Tim's hair, but something unexpected happened. Tim began to act like a cat. He meowed and jumped around the shop. Tom was surprised, but he did not stop cutting Tim's hair. When Tom finished, Tim stopped acting like a cat. It turned out that Tim was just having fun and playing a game. Tom laughed and gave Tim a high five. They both had a fun day at the barber shop.

One day, a little girl saw a problem in her garden. Some plastic was stuck on a fence, and it was very thick. The little girl was determined to solve the problem. So she walked up to the fence and tried to pull the plastic off, but it was too strong. She tried and tried, but she just couldn't get it off. Finally, the girl went to get help from her mum. "Mum, I can't solve the problem," the little girl said. "The plastic is too thick and I can't get it off the fence." But, to the little girl's dismay, all her mum did was laugh. "It's OK sweetheart, we'll just have to leave it there," said her mum, still laughing. The little girl was very disappointed, but there was nothing that she could do. The plastic stayed stuck to the fence, and she never solved the problem.
