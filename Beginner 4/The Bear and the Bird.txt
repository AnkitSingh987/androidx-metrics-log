Category: Beginner
Title: The Bear and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a little bear. This bear was very kind and harmless. He liked to help his friends. One day, the bear saw a tired bird. The bird had a heavy bag to carry. The bear wanted to help the bird. So, he said, "Let me carry your bag for you." The bird was very happy. The bear carried the bag for the bird. They walked and talked together. The bird thanked the bear for his help. In the end, the bear and the bird became good friends. They played and laughed in the forest. And they helped each other whenever they could.

One day, a big, useful machine named Printy lived in a room. Printy loved to print tasks for people. Printy had many friends who liked to watch it print. One day, Printy's friend Tom came to the room. Tom said, "Printy, can you print a task for me?" Printy was happy and said, "Yes, Tom! I will print a task for you." But when Printy tried to print, nothing came out. Printy was sad and did not know what to do. Then, a small, quiet mouse came out from behind Printy. The mouse had a paper in its mouth. The mouse had taken the paper away from Printy! The mouse gave the paper back, and Printy could print again. Tom was happy, Printy was happy, and the mouse was happy too. They all became good friends and worked together on tasks.

---

One day, a big bird and a small bug were talking. The bird said, "I can fly high in the sky!" The bug said, "I can't fly, but I can walk on the ground." The bird and the bug went for a walk. The bird flew and the bug walked. They saw a tree with a big root. The bug tried to climb over the root, but it was too high. The bird said, "I can help you!" The bird picked up the bug and flew over the root. As they went on, the bird started to feel hurt. "My wing hurts," the bird said. The bug looked and saw a small stick stuck in the bird's wing. The bug carefully pulled the stick out of the bird's wing. The bird said, "Thank you, bug! You helped me even though you can't fly." The bird and the bug were now friends. They learned that they could help each other even though they were different. And they lived happily together, flying and walking side by side.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet cat named True. True lived in a small house with a girl named Lily. They loved to play together all day. One day, True was playing with a ball when it rolled under the door. True tried to get the ball, but the door was too close. True called out to Lily, "Lily, help me! I can't get my ball!" Lily heard True and came to help. She opened the door and got the ball for True. They were both very happy and played together for the rest of the day.

Josh was a little boy who was three years old. Josh had a big smile and he liked to laugh. One day, he saw something special. It was a mustache. He thought it was really funny and he wanted to join it. Josh went over to the mustache. He put his hand on it, and it moved! He was so surprised. He thought it was magical. He wanted to join the mustache and he started to laugh. Josh went back to his house and told his mom what had happened. His mother said that it was very important for children to join things and learn. She said that it was why Josh needed to go to school. Josh was excited to join the school and learn new things. He knew that it was important for his future. Josh was ready for an adventure! He hoped one day he would see the magical mustache again.

Once there was a dog named Spot. Spot was a perfect dog. He was nice and loved to play with kids. Spot had a big, happy family. One day, Spot's family went on a trip. They took a long ride in their car. Spot was very excited. He knew they would arrive at a fun place. When they arrived, Spot saw a big park. There were lots of kids and other dogs to play with. Spot had so much fun running and playing all day. It was a perfect day for Spot and his family.
