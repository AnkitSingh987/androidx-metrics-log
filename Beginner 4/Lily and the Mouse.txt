Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Mouse

Lily was a chubby cat who liked to sleep a lot. She had a soft bed, a warm blanket and a toy mouse to cuddle with. Every night, she had a dream that she was a fast cat who could run and jump and catch birds and mice. In her dream, she had long legs, a sleek fur and a sharp nose. She ran with speed across the grass, the wind in her ears and the sun on her back. She chased a blue bird that flew over the fence, and a brown mouse that hid under the bush. She felt happy and free and strong. But then, she heard a loud noise that woke her up. It was her owner, who came to feed her and pet her. She opened her eyes and saw her chubby body, her short legs and her round face. She felt sleepy and lazy and hungry. She yawned and stretched and walked to her bowl. She ate her food and drank her water and licked her paws. She did not mind being a chubby cat who liked to sleep a lot. She knew that her owner loved her and cared for her. She knew that she had a safe and cozy home. And she knew that she could always have a dream that she was a fast cat who could run and jump and catch birds and mice.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to drive his toy car around the house. One day, he saw a big, shiny, new car outside. It looked so real, but it was fake. Tim wanted to drive it, but he knew it was not real. His friend, Sam, came over to play. Sam had an opinion about the fake car. He said, "I think we can still have fun with it." Tim was not sure, but he wanted to try. They played with the fake car, making it go fast and slow, and they had lots of fun. Then, Tim's mom came outside. She saw them playing with the fake car and smiled. She told them, "It's good to see you both having fun, even with a fake car. It shows that you can make the best of any situation." Tim and Sam learned that day that even if something is not real, you can still have fun and be happy. And that was a very important lesson for them to learn.

Once upon a time, a furry cat went for a walk. The cat saw a big swan near a pond. The swan was white and pretty. The cat wanted to play with the swan. So, the cat went near the swan and said, "Hi swan, let's play!" The swan looked at the cat and said, "Okay, let's play!" They played a game where they scatter leaves. The cat and the swan had lots of fun. They laughed and played for a long time. The cat was very happy to have a new friend. But then, something unexpected happened. The swan started to change. It was not a swan anymore. It turned into a big, furry dog! The cat was very surprised. The dog said, "Surprise! I was a dog all along. I just looked like a swan." The cat laughed and they kept playing. Now, the cat and the dog were best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Youth. He liked to wear fancy clothes. One day, Youth went for a walk in the park. The sun was shining bright in the sky. Youth felt happy. As he walked, he saw a big tree. Youth wanted to climb it. He started to climb, but something unexpected happened. A little bird in the tree said, "Hello!" Youth was surprised. He didn't know that birds could talk. The little bird asked Youth, "Do you want to play with me?" Youth said, "Yes!" They played together all day. The sun began to shine even brighter. At the end of the day, Youth had a new friend. And that was the best day ever.

Once upon a time, there was a naughty little dog named Max. Max loved to play in the mud and chase birds. One day, Max's mom told him not to go outside because it was going to rain. Max wanted to play outside, but his mom did not allow him. Max was sad and looked out the window. He saw the dark clouds and knew his mom was right. As he watched, the rain started to fall. Max wished he could go outside and play, but he knew he had to listen to his mom. After the rain stopped, Max saw something beautiful outside. It was a big, bright rainbow! Max's mom saw it too and said, "Now, you can go outside and play!" Max ran outside and played under the rainbow. He was so happy he listened to his mom and stayed inside during the rain.
