Category: Beginner
Title: Bobo the Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Bobo. Bobo was very obedient. He loved to serve his owner, Tim. Tim was a little boy with a big boot. Bobo and Tim were best friends. They played together every day. One day, Tim and Bobo went to the park. Tim wore his big boot. They played fetch with a ball. Bobo would run and bring the ball back to Tim. Tim was very happy. Bobo was happy too because he could serve Tim. But then, something bad happened. Tim threw the ball too far. Bobo ran very fast to get the ball. He didn't see a big hole in the ground. Bobo fell into the hole. Tim tried to help Bobo, but the hole was too deep. Tim cried and cried. Bobo could not serve Tim anymore.

Once upon a time, a soft cat named Kitty and a dog named Doggy lived in a small house. They were best friends. One day, they found a big box of sugar with a teaspoon in it. They thought it would be fun to play with the sugar. Kitty said, "Doggy, let's play with the sugar! But we must not make a big mess." Doggy agreed and they started to play. They used the teaspoon to scoop the sugar and make small hills. But soon, they started to have more fun and made a big mess. Kitty's mom came into the room and saw the mess. She was very mad. She said, "Kitty, Doggy, you must quit playing with the sugar! Look at the mess you made!" Kitty and Doggy felt bad for not listening to Kitty's mom. They tried to clean up the mess but it was too much. The sugar was all over the floor and even on the walls. Kitty's mom had to clean it all by herself. She was very tired and sad. The moral of the story is to always listen to your parents and not make a big mess. If you don't, you might make them very tired and sad.

John was playing in the garden. Suddenly, a small, flexible lip appeared in front of him. He screamed with surprise. He hadn't seen the lip before. John's mom came running to see what was happening. She saw the lip too, and felt scared. "Don't worry, it's just John's father," said John's mom. John's dad had come home early. He couldn't help but smile at his son's surprise. John hugged his dad and they both laughed. The flexible lip was not scary at all, it was just John's dad wearing a funny mask. John asked his dad to put on the mask again, which made his dad chuckle. He happily obliged and put it back on. John and his dad played make believe for the rest of the day. John smiled with joy, happy that his dad was home and even happier because he had a new friend to play with.

Once upon a time, there was a skull who lived in a cave. He was a very spoiled skull, as he was surrounded with special treats and treasures. One day, a young bunny came to the skull’s cave. He had a mop in his hand and a big smile on his face. “Hi there!” said the bunny, “I am here to clean your cave!” The skull gasped in surprise. “You want to clean my cave?” he asked. “Yes!” said the bunny. “Let me make it nice and shiny.” The bunny worked all day, scrubbing and polishing until the cave sparkled. At the end of the day, the skull was so excited he did a special dance. “Thank you so much for cleaning,” said the skull. “My cave looks so amazing!” The bunny smiled and waved goodbye. “You’re welcome,” he said. “I’m glad I could help!” And from that day on, the cave stayed nice and clean, just the way the spoiled skull liked it.

Once upon a time, in a big swamp, there lived a frog and a fish. They were good friends. They liked to play with a fake ball. The ball looked like a real ball, but it was not. One day, while they were playing, the fake ball went into the deep part of the swamp. The frog and the fish were scared, but they wanted their ball back. So, they went to find it. As they looked for the ball, a big bird came out of nowhere. It tried to slap the frog and the fish with its wings. The frog and the fish were so scared! But then, the bird saw the fake ball and thought it was a real ball. The bird took the ball and flew away. The frog and the fish were happy that the bird took the fake ball and not them. They laughed and went back to playing in the swamp.
