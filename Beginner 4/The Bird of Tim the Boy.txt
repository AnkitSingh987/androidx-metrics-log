Category: Beginner
Title: The Bird of Tim the Boy

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big tree with a tiny bird on it. The bird looked sad and far away from its nest. Tim wanted to help the bird. He said, "Little bird, why are you sad?" The bird said, "I am far from my nest and I am tired. I can't fly back home." Tim had an idea. He put out his finger and said, "Jump on my finger, bird. I will help you go back to your nest." The bird jumped on Tim's finger. Tim walked closer to the nest. As they walked, the bird let out a big yawn. Tim felt something on his finger. The bird was not a bird anymore! It was a tiny fairy! The fairy said, "Thank you, Tim. You helped me when I was sad. Now, I will give you a wish." Tim wished for a new friend. The fairy smiled and said, "I will be your friend forever." The story shows that when we help others, good things can happen. Tim helped the bird and made a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to run and play with her friends. One day, they decided to have a race to see who could run the fastest. "Ready, set, go!" they all shouted. Lily and her friends started running as fast as they could. Lily felt confused because she didn't know where the line was to win the race. She asked her friend Tom, "Where is the line to win?" Tom said, "Just keep running, you will see it soon!" So, Lily kept running and running until she saw a big line on the ground. She knew that was the line to win the race. Lily ran as fast as she could and crossed the line first. She was so happy and excited to win the race. Her friends cheered, "Yay, Lily! You won!" They all gave her a big hug, and they continued to play and have fun for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, there was a big soldier. He lived in a small house near the woods. The big soldier was very strong and liked to help people. One day, he met a little girl named Lily. Lily was lost and needed help to find her way home. "Hi, I am a big soldier. I can help you," said the big soldier. "Thank you," said Lily. They walked and talked, and soon they found Lily's home. But when they got there, they saw a big surprise. Lily's house was not a house, but a big castle! Lily looked at the big soldier and said, "I have a secret. I am not just a little girl. I am a princess!" The big soldier was very surprised. He had never met a princess before. From that day on, the big soldier and Princess Lily became best friends, and they had many more adventures together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She liked to play outside in the warm sun. One day, she saw a big box in her yard. The box was open, but she did not know what was inside. Mia went to her friend Tom and said, "Tom, let's go see what is in the open box!" They both ran to the box and looked inside. There were many toys in the box! Mia and Tom were very happy. As they played with the toys, Mia's mom came outside and said, "Stop, kids! We need to share the toys with others." Mia and Tom listened to her and decided to take some toys to their friends. They all played together and had a great day.

One day, a good duck went to the park. The duck saw a big tree and wanted to play. It walked around the tree and found a small pond. The duck was very happy because it loved to swim. The duck jumped into the pond and swam around. It saw some fish and played with them. The duck had a lot of fun in the water. It splashed and made big waves. After a while, the duck got tired. It was time to leave the pond and go home. The duck said goodbye to the fish and climbed out of the pond. It walked back to its home, feeling happy about the fun day it had.
