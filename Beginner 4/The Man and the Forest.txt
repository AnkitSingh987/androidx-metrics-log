Category: Beginner
Title: The Man and the Forest

One day, a man went to the forest. He wanted to find a raven to send a message. He searched for ages, but couldn't find one. Suddenly, he saw a filthy raven sitting on a tree branch. He was excited and ran towards it. "Hello, can you send my message?" asked the man. The raven looked at him and said, "Yes, I can." The man wrote down the message and said, "Thank you, raven. Please deliver it to the other side of the forest." The raven took the note in its beak and flew away. The man watched as the raven flew far away to deliver the message. Soon, the raven came back with a reply. The man opened the note, read it and was very happy. He thanked the raven and said goodbye. The raven smiled and flew away. The man said, "What a great help, you filthy raven!"

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Sue. Sue liked to play outside where it was hot. One day, her mom told her to put on lotion before she went out to play. Sue did not want to, but her mom said she must. Sue went outside to play with her ball. She kicked it high and far. The ball went to a big tree. Sue ran to get the ball, but she felt very hot. She wished she had put on the lotion her mom told her to. Sue started to feel very bad. She ran back to her house and told her mom she felt hot. Her mom was sad and sent her to bed. Sue did not get to play outside anymore that day. She learned to always listen to her mom and put on lotion when it is hot.

One morning, the sun was bright in the sky and a lily bloomed in a meadow. A 3-year old girl, Emma, was fascinated by the beauty of it. She was so excited that she ran up to pick it. "No, no Emma, you need to be careful," her mom said. She explained that the lily was quite sharp. Emma listened and touched the lily carefully. She marveled at how light it felt in her tiny hands. She picked it up and hugged it like it was a special treasure. Mom smiled at her daughter's innocence and pointed to the textured leaves. She said, "Look at that, Emma! Doesn't it look amazing?" The girl smiled and nodded in agreement. She hugged the lily tighter, happy that she had something so beautiful and special to remember this moment. As Emma kept her lily, a butterfly slowly and gracefully fluttered by. She gasped in surprise and marveled at its beauty and light. This moment of wonder foreshadowed the many more adventures in the meadow that Emma and her mom would have in the near future - with the lily always in hand.

One day, a gifted cat named Tom found a toy. The toy was a laser. Tom liked the laser a lot. He played with it all day. The laser made a red dot on the floor. Tom tried to catch the red dot, but it was too fast. Tom had a friend named Max. Max was a dog. Tom showed Max the laser. Max saw the red dot on the floor. Max tried to catch the red dot too. They played with the laser together. They had so much fun. When it was time to go to bed, Tom and Max were very tired. They nodded their heads to say goodnight. They slept well after a fun day of playing with the laser. They knew they would play with it again the next day.

Ben and Mia are friends. They like to play outside in the sun. They have a ball and a bike and a doll. They share their toys and have fun. One day, a big dog comes to their lawn. The dog is brown and furry and loud. He barks and runs and jumps. Ben and Mia are scared. They run to the porch and hide behind the door. The dog sees their toys. He grabs the ball with his teeth and shakes it. He grabs the bike with his paws and drags it. He grabs the doll with his mouth and rips it. Ben and Mia are sad. They cry and hug each other. "Go away, dog! Leave our toys alone!" Ben shouts. "Bad dog! Bad dog!" Mia shouts. The dog hears them. He stops and looks at them. He wags his tail and runs to them. He wants to play. He drops the doll and licks Mia's face. He drops the bike and nudges Ben's hand. He drops the ball and barks. Ben and Mia are surprised. They see the dog is friendly. He is not mean. He is just lonely. He wants to be their friend. "Maybe he is sorry for breaking our toys," Ben says. "Maybe he just wants to have fun," Mia says. They wipe their tears and smile. They pet the dog and talk to him. They give him a name. They call him Spot. They play with Spot and their toys. They throw the ball and he fetches it. They ride the bike and he runs beside them. They fix the doll and he sniffs it. They have fun. They are not scared anymore. They are not sad anymore. They are happy. They have a new friend.
