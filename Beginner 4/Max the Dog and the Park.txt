Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Dog and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max lived in a small house near a big city. Max was scared of the city because it was very noisy and had many big cars. One day, Max's friend, a cat named Bella, came to visit. Bella said, "Max, I want to go to the city to see the big park. Will you come with me?" Max was scared, but he did not want to let his friend down. So, he said, "Yes, Bella, I will go with you." Max and Bella went to the city together. At first, Max was very scared. But soon, he saw that the city was not so bad. There were many fun things to do, like playing in the park and eating yummy food. Max was happy that he went to the city with Bella, and he was not scared anymore.

One day, a girl named Lily went to the park. She saw something unusual on a bench. It was a big, red purse. Lily looked around, but no one was there. Lily's mom said, "We should wait and see if someone comes for the purse." So they sat on the bench and played with Lily's toys. Soon, a nice old lady arrived at the park. She looked sad and worried. The old lady said, "Oh dear, I lost my purse." Lily smiled and showed her the red purse. The old lady was very happy and thanked Lily for finding it. They all went home with big smiles on their faces, knowing they did a good thing.

Anna was a curious girl. She liked to ask many questions and explore new things. One day, she saw a big red nose on a clown at the park. She wondered what it felt like and how it smelled. She ran to the clown and said, "Hello, clown! Can I touch your nose?" The clown smiled and nodded. He bent down and let Anna touch his nose. It was soft and squishy. Anna giggled and squeezed it. It made a funny sound. "Beep beep!" the clown said. Anna liked the sound. She wanted to hear it again. She squeezed the nose harder. The clown laughed and said, "Careful, little girl! Don't eat my nose!" Anna looked at the clown with big eyes. She said, "Eat your nose? Why would I do that?" The clown said, "Some people like to eat noses. They think they are tasty and crunchy. But they are not. They are yucky and ouchy. You don't want to eat my nose, do you?" Anna shook her head. She said, "No, I don't. I like to eat cookies and apples and cheese. Not noses. Noses are for smelling and breathing and making beep beep sounds." The clown said, "That's right. You are a smart and curious girl. Here, have a cookie. It's much better than a nose." He gave Anna a cookie. It was sweet and chewy. Anna said, "Thank you, clown. You are very nice and funny. I like your nose and your cookie." The clown said, "You're welcome, Anna. I like you too. You are very polite and friendly. I hope you have a fun day at the park." Anna said, "I will. Bye bye, clown!" She waved and ran back to her mom. She told her mom about the clown and his nose and his cookie. Her mom smiled and hugged her. She said, "I'm glad you had a good time, Anna. You are a curious and wonderful girl."

Once, there was a man who liked to sell hats. He had a big navy hat that he loved very much. One day, he went to the park to sell his hats. He met many kids and grown-ups who wanted to buy his hats. The sun was warm and bright that day. The man felt happy selling his hats in the warm sun. The kids and grown-ups liked the warm sun too. They played and laughed in the park. At the end of the day, the man had sold all his hats, except for his big navy hat. He put on his navy hat and went home. He felt happy and warm inside, knowing he had sold so many hats to make people happy.
