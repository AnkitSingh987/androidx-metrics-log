Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Pastry

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the store with her mom. They wanted to buy a rich pastry. Lucy was so happy because she loved sweet treats. At the store, Lucy saw a pastry that she liked. She asked her mom, "Can we get this one, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, let's take it home and share it." As they walked home, Lucy saw a cat. The cat started to dance! Lucy was so surprised. She laughed and danced with the cat. Then, the cat jumped up and took the pastry from Lucy's hands. The cat ran away with the pastry. Lucy was sad, but she still had fun dancing with the cat.

One day, a lively dog named Spot went for a walk. He wanted to find a lake to play in. Spot walked and walked until he saw a big lake. He was so happy! Spot jumped into the lake to swim. He splashed and played in the water. Then, something unexpected happened. Spot saw a big fish in the lake. The fish could talk! "Hello, Spot!" said the fish. Spot was very surprised. He did not know fish could talk. The fish and Spot became good friends. They played in the lake all day. Spot had so much fun and could not wait to come back to the lake again.

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat named Ziggy. Ziggy liked to play in the park. One day, Ziggy went to the park and saw a big dog. The dog was very big and Ziggy was very small. Ziggy said, "Oh no! Big dog, please don't hurt me!" The big dog just wagged his tail and said, "Don't panic, little furry cat. I just want to play with you." Ziggy and the big dog played together. They ran in zigzag lines, and they both had lots of fun. Ziggy learned that big dogs can be nice, and they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a small TV in a little house. The TV lived with a girl named Lily and her mom. They loved to watch fun shows together. One day, Lily's mom said, "Lily, we must lock the door when we go out." Lily nodded and helped her mom lock the door. They went to the park to play. When they came back, they saw a small bird sitting on the TV. The bird said, "Hello, I am Tim. Can I watch TV with you?" Lily and her mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. Let's watch together." They all watched TV and had a great time.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing with his toys. He had a question for his mom. "Mom, where is my big red ball?" he asked. His mom looked around and said, "I think it is on the shelf, Tim." Tim was anxious to find his ball, so he went to the shelf. He saw a box there. He thought his ball was inside the box. He opened the box and found a small, cute puppy! The puppy jumped out and licked Tim's face. Tim was very happy and surprised. "Mom! Look, there is a puppy in the box!" Tim shouted. His mom came to see and smiled. "Oh, Tim! The puppy is your birthday gift. I forgot to tell you. Happy birthday, my dear!" Tim hugged the puppy and his mom. He was so happy that he forgot about his big red ball.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He brought his favorite blanket with him. Tim loved to run and play with his blanket. He would run fast, holding the blanket high in the air. It made him feel like a superhero. While Tim was playing, a big wind came and took his blanket away. Tim felt sad and guilty because he thought he did not hold the blanket tight enough. He looked everywhere, but he could not find it. His mom said they would look for the blanket after they ate lunch. While they were eating, a little girl named Sue came up to them. She had Tim's blanket! Sue said she found it when the wind blew it to her. Tim was so happy and thanked Sue. Tim and Sue became friends and played together at the park. They took turns running with the blanket, and they both felt like superheroes.
