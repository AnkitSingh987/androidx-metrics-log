Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Popular Fish and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a popular fish named Bob. Bob was very big and had a shiny scale on his body. He lived in a big pond with many other small fish. One day, a small fish named Tim came to Bob and said, "Bob, can you teach me how to be popular like you?" Bob said, "Okay, Tim. Just do what I do and repeat after me." So Tim followed Bob and repeated everything he did. They swam fast, jumped high, and played with the other fish. But Tim was not very good at being like Bob, and the other fish did not like him. They said, "Tim, you are not like Bob. Stop trying to be like him." Tim was very sad and did not know what to do. He tried to be like Bob, but it did not work. One day, while they were playing, Tim got stuck in a big net. Bob tried to help him, but the net was too strong. Tim could not get out, and the other fish could not help him. In the end, Tim was taken away by the big net, and everyone was very sad. The other fish learned that they should not try to be like someone else, but be themselves. And even though Tim was not like Bob, they all missed him very much.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom had a brother named Sam. They liked to play together. One day, Tom and Sam went to the park. They played on the swings and slides. They were having a lot of fun. Tom thought his brother Sam was tough because he could climb high and run fast. When it was time to go home, their mom called them. Tom and Sam were happy to go home and tell their mom about their fun day at the park. They knew they would come back to play again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max was very selfish. He did not like to share his toys with his little sister, Daisy. One day, Max's mom made some yummy soup for lunch. Max wanted all the soup for himself. Max thought of a plan. He told Daisy that she had to wash her paws before she could eat the soup. Daisy went to wash her paws. While she was gone, Max ate all the soup very fast. He did not leave any for Daisy. When Daisy came back, she was sad to see that there was no soup left. Max's mom saw what happened and told Max that it was not nice to be selfish. She made more soup for Daisy and told Max to share his toys. Max learned to be kind and share with his sister. They played and ate soup together, and they were both happy.

Tim and Kim were twins who liked to play outside. They had many toys, like balls, bikes, and dolls. But they did not like to clean their toys. They left them on the grass, on the road, and on the porch. Their mom was not happy. "Tim and Kim, you have to clean your toys!" she said. "They will get dirty and broken. And they are in the way of other people. Please put them in the box." But Tim and Kim did not listen. They said, "No, mom, we want to play more. Cleaning is boring. We will do it later." Mom sighed and went inside. She hoped they would change their mind. But then, something bad happened. A big, loud, scary tornado came. It was spinning and roaring and throwing things around. Tim and Kim saw it and ran inside. They were very afraid. "Mom, mom, help us!" they cried. "There is a tornado!" Mom hugged them and took them to the basement. They waited there until the tornado was gone. When they came out, they saw a big mess. The tornado had taken their toys and thrown them everywhere. Some were broken, some were dirty, some were gone. The grass, the road, and the porch were covered with their toys and other things. It was disgusting. Tim and Kim felt very sad and sorry. They wished they had cleaned their toys. They looked at mom and said, "We are sorry, mom. We were wrong. We should have cleaned our toys. Can we help you clean the mess?" Mom nodded and said, "Yes, you can. But it will take a long time and a lot of work. And you may not find all your toys. You have learned a hard lesson. Next time, listen to me and clean your toys."
