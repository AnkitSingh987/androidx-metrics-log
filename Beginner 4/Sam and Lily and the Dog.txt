Category: Beginner
Title: Sam and Lily and the Dog

Sam and Lily were playing in the snow. They made a big snowman with a hat, a scarf and a carrot nose. They also gave him a mustache with some sticks. "Look at our snowman, he is amazing!" Sam said. "Yes, he is. But I am cold and hungry. Can we go home now?" Lily asked. Sam nodded. He was also cold and hungry. They said goodbye to their snowman and started to walk home. On the way, they saw a big dog. The dog was black and fluffy. He had a collar with a bell. He ran towards them, wagging his tail. "Hello, doggy. What is your name?" Sam said. The dog licked Sam's face and made him laugh. He also licked Lily's face and made her shiver. "Brrr, your tongue is wet and cold!" Lily said. The dog barked and jumped around them. He wanted to play. "Sorry, doggy. We have to go home. Maybe we can play with you another day." Sam said. The dog looked sad, but he understood. He followed them until they reached their house. He waved his paw and ran away. Sam and Lily went inside their house. They smelled something good. "Mommy, what are you cooking?" Lily asked. "I am making soup and bread for you. You must be very cold and hungry after playing in the snow." Mommy said. She hugged them and kissed their cheeks. She gave them warm soup and bread. They ate and drank and felt better. "Thank you, Mommy. The soup and bread are amazing!" Sam said. "Yes, thank you, Mommy. And thank you, doggy, for being our friend." Lily said. They smiled and finished their soup and bread. They were happy and warm. They loved their snowman, their doggy and their mommy.

Once upon a time, in a small town near the sea, there was a tidy little house. In the house lived a boy named Tim and his mom. They loved to go to the shore and play in the water. One sunny day, Tim and his mom went to the shore. They saw a big fish in the water. The fish liked to dive in and out of the water. Tim and his mom laughed as they watched the fish play. After a fun day at the shore, Tim and his mom went back to their tidy little house. They were happy and tired. Tim went to bed with a big smile on his face, dreaming of the fish that liked to dive.

Once upon a time, in a distant land, there was a queen. She was kind and loved by all. One day, a little girl named Lily came to the queen's big house. "Hello, queen," said Lily. "I believe you can make wishes come true. Can you help me find my lost cat?" The queen smiled and said, "Yes, I can help you. Let's look for your cat together." They looked everywhere for the cat. They looked behind trees, under rocks, and in the tall grass. Finally, they found the cat hiding in a bush. Lily was so happy to see her cat. "Thank you, queen," said Lily, hugging her cat. "You made my wish come true!" The queen smiled and said, "I'm glad I could help you, Lily. Remember to believe in yourself and good things will happen." From that day on, Lily and the queen became best friends, and they always believed in each other.

Once there was a big, red house. In the house, there was a boy, a girl, and a loyal dog. They all played together and had fun. The sun was bright and everyone was happy. One day, a small fire started in the house. The boy and the girl saw the fire and they were scared. The loyal dog barked loud to warn them. The boy, the girl, and the dog tried to put out the fire, but it was too big. The fire grew bigger and bigger. It started to burn the house. The boy, the girl, and the loyal dog had to run away. They were very sad. The big, red house was gone, and they had no place to play. The fire had won.
