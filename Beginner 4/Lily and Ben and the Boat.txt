Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Boat

Lily and Ben were bored. They had nothing to do. They looked out the window and saw the big lake. They saw some boats with white sails. They wanted to go on a boat. "Can we go on a boat, Mom?" Lily asked. "Maybe later, sweetie. It's too hot right now. Why don't you play with your toys?" Mom said. Lily and Ben did not want to play with their toys. They had played with them many times. They wanted to do something new. They looked at the clock. It was almost dark. "Can we go on a boat now, Mom?" Ben asked. "OK, fine. Let's go on a boat. But only for a little while. And you have to wear your life jackets." Mom said. Lily and Ben were happy. They put on their life jackets and followed Mom to the lake. They got on a small boat with a red sail. Mom pushed the boat away from the shore. The boat moved slowly on the water. Lily and Ben looked at the sky. They saw the stars. They saw the moon. They saw something else. They saw a big ball of light. It was glowing. It was changing colors. It was beautiful. "Look, Mom! What is that?" Lily said. "I don't know, honey. It looks like a firework. But it's not making any noise. It's very strange." Mom said. The glowing ball came closer to the boat. It was very bright. It made Lily and Ben feel warm. It made them feel happy. It made them feel curious. "Hello, little ones. Do you like my glow?" The ball said. Lily and Ben were surprised. The ball could talk. They did not know what to say. "Who are you?" Mom asked. "I am a fairy. I live in the lake. I like to glow and make people smile. Do you want to play with me?" The ball said. Lily and Ben nodded. They wanted to play with the fairy. They wanted to see her glow. They wanted to have fun. The fairy smiled. She flew around the boat. She made the sail glow. She made the water glow. She made the stars glow. She made Lily and Ben glow. They laughed and clapped. They played with the fairy until it was very late. Lily and Ben were not bored anymore. They had a wonderful adventure. They made a new friend. They thanked the fairy and said goodbye. They went back to the shore with Mom. They went to bed and dreamed of glowing.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play in the snow. One day, it snowed a lot, and Spot went outside to have fun. He jumped and rolled in the snow. Spot was very happy. While playing, Spot saw a little bird. The bird was cold and scared in the snow. Spot wanted to help the bird. He thought about how to rescue the bird. Spot had an idea. He ran back to his house to get a warm blanket. Spot brought the blanket to the bird and wrapped it around the bird to keep it warm. The bird was not cold anymore. Spot rescued the bird, and they became good friends. They played together in the snow every day. Spot was a hero, and the bird was not spoiled by the cold snow anymore. They were very happy together.

Once upon a time, there was a unique dog named Spot. Spot had a big yard where he loved to play. He would run and jump all day long. One day, a mean cat came into Spot's yard. The cat wanted to take Spot's toys. Spot did not want the cat to take his toys. He had to think of a way to prevent the cat from taking them. Spot barked very loud at the cat. The cat got scared and ran away. Spot had prevented the cat from taking his toys. He was very happy that he could keep playing in his yard.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They had many toys, but their favorite was a pink vehicle. It had four wheels, a horn, and a seat for two. They liked to drive it around the grass and pretend they were going on adventures. "Let's go to the zoo!" Lily said. "We can see the lions and the monkeys and the elephants!" "Okay!" Ben agreed. "But we have to be careful. The zoo is very big and there are many people." They got on their pink vehicle and started to drive. They made noises like animals and had fun. But when they got to the zoo, they saw a problem. There was a big gate and a man who said they had to pay money to enter. "We don't have money," Lily said. "We are just kids." "Sorry," the man said. "You can't come in without money. You have to go back." Lily and Ben were sad. They wanted to see the animals. They turned around and drove away. But then they saw something else. There was another gate, and it was open. It had a sign that said "Staff Only". "Maybe we can go in there," Ben said. "Maybe no one will see us." "Okay," Lily said. "But we have to be quick and quiet." They drove their pink vehicle through the gate. They hoped to find the animals. But they did not. Instead, they found a big garage with many vehicles. There were trucks and vans and cars and buses. They were all different colors and sizes. "Wow," Ben said. "Look at all these vehicles. They are so cool." "Maybe we can try them," Lily said. "Maybe they are more fun than our pink vehicle." They got off their pink vehicle and started to search for a new one. They looked at the trucks and the vans and the cars and the buses. They tried to open the doors and push the buttons and turn the wheels. But they did not know how to drive them. They only made noises and lights and alarms. "Stop!" a voice shouted. "What are you doing here? This is not a place for kids. This is a place for workers." Lily and Ben turned around and saw a woman. She was wearing a uniform and a badge. She looked angry. "We are sorry," Lily said. "We just wanted to see the animals. But we had no money. And we saw this gate. And we saw these vehicles. And we wanted to try them." "Well, you can't," the woman said. "You are not allowed here. You are making a mess and a noise. You have to leave. Now." She took them by the hand and led them out of the garage. She saw their pink vehicle and frowned. "Is this yours?" she asked. "Yes," Ben said. "It is our favorite toy. We like to drive it around." "Well, you should take better care of it," the woman said. "You should not leave it here. Someone could take it or break it. You should be happy with what you have." She gave them their pink vehicle and told them to go back to the park. Lily and Ben felt ashamed. They got on their pink vehicle and drove away. They did not say anything. They realized they had made a mistake. They wished they had never left the park. They wished they had never searched for a new vehicle. They wished they had been happy with their pink vehicle.
