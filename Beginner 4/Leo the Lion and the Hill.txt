Category: Beginner
Title: Leo the Lion and the Hill

Once upon a time, there was a big lion named Leo. Leo was very popular in the jungle. All the animals liked him. One day, he saw a pretty view from a hill. He wanted to show the view to his friends. He told his friend, the little bird, "Let's go to the hill. I want to show you a nice view." The little bird was excited and said, "Yes, let's go!" They went to the hill together. As they walked, Leo said, "I will roar when we get there." When they reached the top of the hill, they saw the beautiful view. The little bird was very happy. Leo roared loudly. All the animals in the jungle heard the roar and knew that Leo found something special. Soon, all the animals came to the hill to see the view. They liked it too. Leo was happy that he could share the pretty view with his friends. From that day on, the hill became a popular place for all the animals to visit and enjoy the view.

Once upon a time, there was a modest farm. On the farm, there was a small boy named Tim. Tim liked to watch the animals play. He had a big smile on his face when he did. One day, Tim saw a little mouse. The mouse was hungry. Tim wanted to help the mouse. He went to the barn to find some food. Tim saw some oats in a big bag. He knew that the mouse would like the oats. Tim took the oats to the mouse. The mouse was very happy. It ate the oats and then played with Tim. They became best friends. From that day on, Tim and the mouse always played together on the modest farm.

One day, a fast dog named Max saw a big cat. The cat was doing exercise. Max wanted to play with the cat. He ran to the cat and asked a question. "Do you want to play with me?" Max said. The cat looked at Max and said, "I don't want to play. I am busy doing exercise." Max was sad. He wanted a friend to play with. He looked around and saw a slow turtle. Max ran to the turtle and asked the same question. "Do you want to play with me?" Max said. The turtle looked at Max and said, "I can't play. I am too slow." Max was very sad. He had no one to play with. He went home and cried. The fast dog did not find a friend to play with.

One day, a modest rhinoceros named Remy was walking in the forest. He saw a big tree with a small kite stuck in it. Remy wanted to help, so he thought of a plan. "Hi, little bird," Remy said to a bird on the ground. "Can you help me get the kite out of the tree? I will attach it to my back, and you can fly up and pull it free." "Okay, Remy," said the bird. "I will help you." So, Remy attached the kite string to his back, and the bird flew up to the tree. The bird pulled and pulled, and the kite came out of the tree. The bird and Remy were happy they could help. They played with the kite together, and they had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a little house. It was the only available house in the town. A big family wanted to live in the house. They tried to fit inside, but they were too many. They were sad because they could not fit in the house. One day, a small family came to the town. They saw the little house and wanted to live there. They tried to fit inside the house, and they did! They were very happy. The big family saw this and thought they could not fit because the house was too small. But then, something unexpected happened! The little house began to grow bigger and bigger. Now, it was a big house! The big family tried to fit inside again, and this time they did! Both families were happy and lived in the big house together. And they all became good friends.
