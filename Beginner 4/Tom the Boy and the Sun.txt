Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He lived in a small house with his mom and dad. Tom had a big wish. He wanted to understand why the sun was so hot and bright. One day, Tom went to the park with his friend, Sam. They saw a big tree with harsh branches. Tom asked Sam, "Do you understand why the sun is so hot?" Sam said, "I don't know, but I wish I did." Tom and Sam decided to ask their teacher, Miss Lily, about the sun. Miss Lily told them, "The sun is hot because it has fire inside. It gives us light and warmth." Tom was happy that he finally understood. Now, he could share his new knowledge with everyone.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his dog, Spot. They saw a big wreck in the park. Tim felt upset because the wreck was very messy. He said, "Oh no, Spot! The park is not pretty anymore." Spot looked at the wreck and barked. Tim wondered who made the mess. He thought, "Maybe we can help clean it up." Tim and Spot started to pick up the trash and put it in a big bag. As they were cleaning, a girl named Sue came to the park. She saw Tim and Spot and said, "Thank you for helping clean the wreck. I was upset too." Tim, Spot, and Sue all worked together to make the park pretty again. They were happy to help and became friends.

Once, in a little town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a red cap that he loved very much. He wore it every day. One day, Tim went to the park to play with his friends. At the park, Tim saw a big tree. He heard a voice coming from the tree. It was a bird! The bird said, "Hi, Tim! I like your red cap. It's incredible!" Tim was very surprised. He didn't know that birds could talk. The bird asked, "Can I try your cap?" Tim said, "Yes, you can." The bird put the cap on its head. Then, something unexpected happened. The bird changed into a big, colorful bird with the cap on! The bird said, "Thank you, Tim! Your cap has magic!" Tim smiled and they played together at the park.

One day, a young fish named Bob wanted to swim with his friends. They went to the big pond to play. They were having a lot of fun swimming and jumping in the water. Then, a police frog named Fred came to the pond. He said, "Hey, kids! You need to be careful when you swim! There are big fish in the pond that can hurt you." The young fish and the police frog became friends. They played a game of hide and seek. While they were playing, a big fish came near them. The big fish wanted to eat the young fish. But the police frog jumped high and scared the big fish away. All the young fish were safe and happy. They thanked the police frog and kept swimming, but now they knew to be more careful.

One day, a boy named Tim found a rod. He wanted to use the rod to catch a fish. Tim went to the river with the rod. He saw some fish in the water. Tim tried to catch the fish, but the fish swam away. Tim was sad. A big bear named Ben saw Tim. Ben was hungry and wanted to eat something yummy. Ben thought the fish looked yummy too. Ben wanted to help Tim catch the fish. Tim and Ben worked together to catch the fish with the rod. At last, they caught a big fish. Tim and Ben were very happy. They cooked the fish and shared it. The fish was very yummy. Tim and Ben became good friends. They gave each other a big hug. They played together every day, and they always helped each other.

One day, a little girl named Amy wanted to mail a letter to her friend Tom. She was very careful when she wrote the letter. She put it in an envelope and went outside to the mailbox. On her way, she saw a small pony. The pony was lost and looked sad. "Hello, pony," said Amy. "Why are you sad?" The pony replied, "I am lost and can't find my way home." Amy wanted to help the pony, but she also needed to mail her letter. She thought about what to do. Then, she had an idea. "I will help you find your home, and we can mail my letter on the way," she said. The pony agreed, and they went on their journey together.
