Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Fearful Boy and the Money

Once upon a time, there was a fearful little boy named Tim. He found some money on the floor. Tim did not know what to do with the money. He was scared to keep it, because it did not belong to him. Tim thought about it and decided to return the money. He asked his mom to help him find the person who lost it. They walked around the neighborhood, asking people if they had lost any money. Finally, they found a kind old lady who had lost her money. Tim gave the money back to her. The old lady was very happy and thanked Tim. Tim was no longer fearful, and he felt good for doing the right thing.

One day, a little car named Cari was driving with his friends. They were going to a big park to play. Cari was a normal car and he loved to show his friends new places. On the way, Cari saw that his fuel was very low. He needed to find more fuel to keep going. His friends were worried too. They did not want to stop playing. Cari and his friends looked for a fuel station. They saw one far away, but it was hard to get there. They all worked together and made a plan. They pushed Cari to the fuel station and got more fuel. Then, they went to the park and had a great day playing.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a lamp that was very important to her. She took the lamp everywhere she went. One day, she put the lamp on a table near her bed. That night, something unexpected happened. Sue heard a small voice coming from the lamp. It was a tiny fairy! The fairy said, "I am here to help you, Sue!" Sue was very surprised and happy. The next day, Sue took the lamp outside to play. The fairy came out and did something amazing. She turned the lamp into a big, fun slide! Sue and the fairy played on the slide all day. From that day on, Sue knew that her important lamp was even more special than she thought.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his friends in the park. One day, Tim and his friends found a club. They were excited to play with it. As they played, Tim's friend stumbled and dropped the club. The club hit a big rock and broke. When it broke, they saw something shiny inside. Tim picked it up and said, "Wow, look at this!" Tim showed the shiny thing to his mom. She said, "This is a rich, old coin!" Tim and his friends were very surprised. They never thought that they would find something so special inside a club. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in an ordinary little town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a pet bird named Blue. Blue lived in a cage in Tim's room. Tim loved Blue very much and took good care of him. One day, Tim went to his friend's house to play. He wanted to show Blue to his friend, so he asked his mom if he could borrow the cage. His mom said yes, and Tim took Blue in the cage to his friend's house. At his friend's house, they played and had fun with Blue. Blue liked to sing and make the boys laugh. When it was time to go home, Tim put Blue back in the cage and took him home. They had a great day together, and Tim was happy he could borrow the cage to share Blue with his friend.

One day, a little boy named Ben was playing with his toy soldiers in the garden. He liked to make them march and fight and hide behind the flowers. He had a big soldier with a red hat and a shiny sword, and a small soldier with a green hat and a smooth stone. "Come on, big soldier, let's go and find the enemy!" Ben said, holding the red hat soldier in his hand. He ran across the grass, looking for a good spot to attack. But he did not see a big rock in his way, and he stumbled and fell. He scraped his knee and dropped his soldier. "Ow, ow, ow!" Ben cried, holding his knee. He looked for his soldier, but he could not see him. He was afraid he had lost him forever. "Ben, what happened?" his mom asked, hearing his cry. She came out of the house and saw him on the ground. She ran to him and hugged him. "Are you okay, sweetie?" "I hurt my knee, and I lost my soldier!" Ben sobbed, pointing to the rock. "He was my favorite!" Mom looked around and saw the red hat soldier lying under a bush. She picked him up and gave him to Ben. "Here he is, he's not lost. He was just hiding from the enemy. He's a brave soldier, just like you." Ben smiled and hugged his soldier. He felt better, and he thanked his mom. "I love you, mom. And I love my soldier. He's my best friend." Mom kissed his cheek and smoothed his hair. "I love you too, Ben. And I'm glad you have a best friend. Now, let's go and clean your knee, and then you can play some more." Ben nodded and followed his mom. He held his soldier tight and whispered to him. "Don't worry, big soldier, we'll win the war. You and me, we're a team."
