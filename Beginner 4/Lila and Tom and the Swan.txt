Category: Beginner
Title: Lila and Tom and the Swan

Lila and Tom were playing in the park. They liked to swing and slide and run. They saw a big bird in the sky. It was black and white and had a long neck. It was a swan. "Look, Tom, a swan!" Lila said. "It can soar high and low. It is so pretty!" Tom nodded. He liked the swan too. He wanted to touch it. He ran to the pond where the swan was landing. He reached out his hand. "Tom, no!" Lila shouted. "The swan can bite you. It is not nice. Come back!" But Tom did not listen. He got closer to the swan. The swan saw him and hissed. It opened its beak and snapped at Tom. Tom screamed and ran away. He was scared and hurt. Lila ran to him and hugged him. She was sorry for him. She saw a piece of wood on the ground. It was weird. It had a shape like a swan. It had a head and a neck and a body and wings. It was a toy. "Look, Tom, a toy!" Lila said. "It is a swan too. But it can not bite you. It can only soar in your hand. Do you want it?" Tom nodded. He liked the toy. He took it and smiled. He forgot the bad swan. He waved the toy in the air. He made it soar high and low. He was happy. Lila and Tom played with the toy. They had fun. They liked the weird wood swan. It was so pretty.

Once upon a time, there was a small beetle named Pat. Pat was always busy examining the world. He had never seen anyone like him before so he felt quite alone. One day, Pat was examining a tiny, brown leaf. Suddenly he saw something moving. It was a small worm named Liz. Pat smiled and shouted "hi". Liz looked up and said "Hello Pat". Pat was so happy that he had found someone like him that he ran over to Liz, and rubbed her back with his tiny antennae. Liz was delighted. She said to Pat, "You know, sometimes it's helpful to have a friend to examine the world with. We can learn so much together!" Pat agreed and they vowed to explore the world together. Pat gave Liz a big hug and they flew off to explore their next big adventure. The moral of this story is to always be helpful and kind to your friends. You never know what kind of adventure you might have together if you lend a helping hand.

Once upon a time, there was a wise old owl. The owl loved to travel far and wide. One day, the owl found a mystery box in the woods. The wise owl was very curious about the mystery box. He tried to open it, but it was locked. The owl decided to travel with the mystery box to find the key. As the wise owl traveled, he met a big bear. The bear said, "I have the key to the mystery box!" The wise owl was happy, but the bear did not want to share the key. The wise owl tried to be nice, but the bear was mean. In the end, the wise owl could not open the mystery box. He was very sad and went back home. The wise owl learned that sometimes, you cannot solve every mystery.

Once upon a time, there was a clever sailor. He had a big boat. The sailor loved to dress in blue. One day, the sailor met a little boy. The boy said, "I want to be a sailor like you!" The sailor smiled and said, "You can come on my boat." They went on the boat. The wind was strong. The boat went very fast. The sailor and the boy were scared. The boat hit a big rock. The boat broke and they fell into the water. The clever sailor and the boy could not fix the boat. They were sad.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, round, red ball that he loved to play with. One day, Tim was playing with his ball in the house. His mom told him not to, but he did not listen. Tim threw the ball, and it hit a pretty glass cup. The cup fell and broke. Tim's mom was sad and said, "Tim, that was a stupid thing to do. Now the cup is broken." She told Tim he needed to be punished for breaking the cup. Tim's mom made him sit in a chair for a short time. She wanted him to learn to be more careful. After his punishment, Tim said he was sorry and promised to play with his ball outside. His mom hugged him and said, "Now you'll be safe, and our cups will be safe too." Tim learned his lesson and played outside with his ball from that day on.
