Category: Beginner
Title: The Mystery of the Dog

One day, a loud dog and a quiet cat were playing with a ball. The dog liked to bark and the cat liked to meow. They both had an opinion on how to play with the ball. The loud dog said, "I want to drop the ball and you catch it." The quiet cat said, "No, I want to roll the ball and you chase it." They could not agree on how to play, so they started to argue. The loud dog barked and the quiet cat meowed. They were both very upset. Then, a little bird saw them and said, "Why don't you both take turns? First, the dog can drop the ball and the cat can catch it. Then, the cat can roll the ball and the dog can chase it." The loud dog and the quiet cat thought this was a good idea. They took turns and played with the ball all day. They were happy and became good friends.

One day, a little girl named Mia was playing in her room. She saw her toy telephone on the floor. Mia picked it up and started to talk to her friend, Tom. They talked about the icy day outside. They wanted to play, but it was too cold. Mia and Tom were sad, but they did not complain. They had an idea to play inside. They played with the toy telephone and pretended to call each other. They talked and laughed for a long time. After a while, the icy day turned warmer. Mia and Tom went outside to play. They were happy and did not complain anymore. They played all day and had a lot of fun.

One day, a boy and a girl went to the store. They saw a big, yellow banana. The boy said, "I want the banana!" The girl said, "No, I want the banana!" They began to quarrel. A nice store man came over. He said, "Stop! You can both have a banana. But, they are expensive." The boy and the girl looked at each other. They knew they had to share. The boy and the girl decided to buy the expensive banana together. They stopped quarreling and said sorry to each other. They went home, shared the banana, and played together. They were happy and learned to share.

One day, a girl named Lily went for a walk. It was a sunny and peaceful day. She saw a big puddle on the ground. Lily loved to play in puddles. She jumped in the puddle and splashed water everywhere. A boy named Tom saw Lily play in the puddle. He wanted to play too. Tom asked Lily, "Can I play in the puddle with you?" Lily said, "Yes, come play with me!" Tom and Lily jumped and splashed in the puddle together. After playing in the puddle, Lily and Tom were very wet. They sat down on the grass to dry off. Lily had an idea. She said, "Let's write our names in the mud with sticks!" Tom and Lily found sticks and wrote their names in the mud. They had a fun and peaceful day playing together.

One day, a thoughtful little bird named Tim was flying near a big cliff. He saw a small bunny named Ben sitting near the edge. Ben looked sad, so Tim flew down to talk to him. "Hi, I am Tim. Why are you sad?" asked the thoughtful bird. Ben looked up and said, "I lost my way home. I can't find it." Tim wanted to help Ben. He asked, "Can you tell me where your home is?" Ben tried to answer, but he didn't know. "I live near a big tree and a small pond," he said. Tim thought for a moment. He knew where that big tree and small pond were. "I can take you there!" he said. So, Tim and Ben went on a fun trip to find Ben's home. They were so happy when they found it. From that day, Tim and Ben became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird who lived in a cage. The bird was anxious because it wanted to fly and play with its friends outside. It did not know how to open the cage to be free. One day, a kind girl saw the bird in the cage. She knew the bird was anxious to fly. The girl opened the cage door and let the bird out. The bird was so happy and said "thank you" to the girl. Now, the bird could fly with its friends in the sky. It was not anxious anymore. The bird and the girl became best friends, and they played together every day. The bird knew it was free and happy because of the kind girl.
