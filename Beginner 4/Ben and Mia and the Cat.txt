Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Mia and the Cat

Ben and Mia like to play knights and dragons. They use old boxes and paper to make swords and shields. They are very creative. They cut and color and stick things on their shields. Ben makes his shield blue and yellow. Mia makes her shield pink and purple. They go to the backyard and pretend it is a castle. They see a big tree and think it is a dragon. They shout and run to the tree. They lift their swords and shields. They want to fight the dragon. But the tree is not a dragon. It is a cat. The cat is sleeping on a branch. The cat hears the noise and wakes up. The cat is angry. It hisses and jumps down. It runs to Ben and Mia. Ben and Mia are scared. They drop their swords and hide behind their shields. They hope the cat will go away. "Go away, cat!" Ben says. "We are knights. You are not a dragon. You are a mean cat." The cat does not listen. It scratches Ben's shield. It tries to bite Mia's shield. It makes a loud noise. Mia has an idea. She sees a ball on the grass. She picks up the ball and throws it. The ball bounces and rolls away. "Look, cat!" Mia says. "A ball. Go play with the ball. Leave us alone." The cat sees the ball. It likes to play with balls. It forgets about Ben and Mia. It chases the ball. It runs away from the tree. Ben and Mia are happy. They hug each other. They lift their shields and cheer. "We did it!" Ben says. "We scared the cat. We are brave knights." "We are!" Mia says. "And we have the best shields. They saved us." They smile and laugh. They pick up their swords and go to find another adventure.

One day, a boy named Tim went to school. He wore his uniform. It was a blue shirt and blue pants. He was excited to see his friends. At school, Tim saw his friend Sam. Sam looked frustrated. Tim asked, "Why are you frustrated, Sam?" Sam said, "I lost my toy car." Tim wanted to help Sam find the car. They looked everywhere. They looked under the desks, in the bags, and even in the playground. They could not find the car. Then, something unexpected happened. A bird flew into the classroom. The bird had Sam's toy car in its beak! The bird dropped the car on Sam's desk. Sam was so happy! He said, "Thank you, Tim, for helping me!" Tim smiled and said, "You're welcome!" They played with the toy car and had a fun day at school.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly little girl named Lucy. She loved to run and jump and play with her friends. And one of her very favorite things to do was hug her mommy and daddy! Every season, Lucy and her mommy and daddy would take a special walk together in the park. They'd look at the flowers and the trees, and Lucy would often find a special rock or a leaf to take home and keep forever. But the best part of the walk was when Mommy or Daddy would pick her up and give her a big, tight hug! It was always so warm and cozy and Lucy would smile and giggle and hug even tighter to show them how much she loved them. And when they got home, Lucy would give her mommy and daddy one last hug goodnight, before she fell fast asleep, dreaming of all the fun they'd have together in the next season!

Once upon a time, there was a happy shrimp named Sam. Sam was very small but loved to play in the sea with his friends. One day, Sam met a pretty shrimp named Sally. They liked to play together and soon became best friends. Sam and Sally heard a story about how two shrimps can marry and become a family. They thought that was a great idea. They played together more and more, and they knew they wanted to marry one day. As they grew up, Sam and Sally still played together all the time. They were so happy. One day, Sam asked Sally if she wanted to marry him. Sally said yes, and they had a big party with all their friends. Sam and Sally were now a family, and they lived happily ever after.
