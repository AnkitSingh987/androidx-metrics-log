Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was very excited because his birthday was coming soon. He knew there would be a big cake and lots of fun games to play with his friends. One day, Tim saw his mom lift a big box and put it on the table. Tim felt guilty because he wanted to know what was inside the box. He thought it might be a present for his birthday. Tim waited until his mom left the room and then went to the table to look at the box. As he got closer, he heard a soft noise coming from the box. Tim was scared but also curious. He slowly lifted the top of the box and saw a cute little puppy inside. The puppy jumped out and licked Tim's face. Tim laughed and knew that this was the best birthday present ever.

One day, a big dog named Max went to the doctor with his mom. The doctor wanted to look at Max's leg because it was hurting. The doctor said, "I need to take an x-ray of your leg, Max." Max was scared, but his mom said, "Don't worry, Max. It will be fast and won't hurt." They went into a room with a big machine. The doctor took the x-ray and looked at it. He saw something strange. The doctor called another doctor to come and look at the x-ray. The other doctor said, "Wow, I see it too!" They showed Max and his mom the x-ray. There was a toy car inside Max's leg! Max had swallowed the toy car, and it got stuck in his leg. Max's mom said, "Oh no, Max! We need to be careful with our toys." The doctors helped Max feel better, and he promised to be more careful. From that day on, Max always played with big toys that he could not swallow.

One day, a little dog named Spot was playing in the park. He saw a big tree and thought it would be a fine place to rest. Spot ran to the tree and sat down under it. As Spot was sitting, he saw a big bird on a branch. The bird was very pretty. Spot stared at the bird and the bird stared back at him. They looked at each other for a long time. Then, the bird flew down and landed on a point near Spot. They became friends and played together in the park all day. Spot and the bird had a fun and happy day under the fine big tree.

Once upon a time, there was a little rubber ball named Bouncy. Bouncy loved to play with his friends, but sometimes he got frustrated when they didn't trust him. One sunny day, Bouncy and his friends went to the park. They wanted to play catch, but Bouncy's friends didn't trust him to be the ball. They thought he was too small and would get lost. This made Bouncy very frustrated. Bouncy decided to show his friends that they could trust him. He bounced as high as he could, higher than the trees, and landed right in his friend's hands. His friends were so happy that they could trust Bouncy to play catch. From that day on, Bouncy and his friends played together all the time, and they never felt frustrated again.

Once upon a time, there was an intelligent dog named Max. Max loved to play in the sun and soak in the warm water. One day, Max saw a shiny glass on the table. Max said to his friend, the cat, "I want to see the shiny glass up close." The cat said, "Be careful, Max. The glass can break." Max did not listen and jumped on the table. Max was too excited and hit the glass. The glass broke and fell on Max. Max was hurt and sad. The cat said, "I told you to be careful, Max." Max learned to listen to his friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big station. At the station, there were many people and trains. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the station with his mom. They wanted to take a train to visit grandma. Tim was very excited. At the station, Tim saw a small bird with a hurt wing. The bird could not fly. Tim felt sad for the bird. He told his mom, "Mom, we need to rescue the bird! It needs help." Tim's mom was very generous and kind. She said, "Okay, Tim. Let's help the bird." Tim and his mom took the bird to a nice man who could fix its wing. The man helped the bird and soon, it could fly again. The bird was very happy and thanked Tim and his mom. They all went back to the station and took the train to grandma's house. And they all lived happily ever after.
