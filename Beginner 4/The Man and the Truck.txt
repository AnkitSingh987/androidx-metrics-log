Category: Beginner
Title: The Man and the Truck

Once there was a man who had a big red truck. He was loading up his truck with all the things he needed to do some work. He put a big dirty helmet on his head. Then he hopped into his big truck and drove off to start his job for the day. The man drove for a long time. When he finally came to his job, he was so tired and hot. He took off the dirty helmet and wiped the sweat off his forehead. He was so relieved to finally be there. He got to work right away. He unloaded all the tools and supplies he had loaded onto his truck. He got to work and worked hard all day. When he was done, he loaded everything back onto his truck and put his dirty helmet back on. He drove his truck home and was happy to be back safe and sound. He gave a big sigh of relief and took off his dirty helmet. He was glad he had his helmet to keep him safe. He was very proud of himself for getting his job done for the day.

One day, a pale blue bird found a small cube in the grass. The bird was very happy and wanted to play with the cube. It picked up the cube in its beak and flew up high into the sky, zooming around. As the bird zoomed up and down, the cube began to shake. The bird was surprised and dropped the cube. The cube fell to the ground and broke open. Inside the cube, there was a tiny, shiny star. The bird and the star became friends. They played together all day, zooming through the sky. The pale blue bird and the shiny star were very happy, and they had lots of fun.

One day, a small bird named Tim was searching for a new nest. He looked high and low but could not find the perfect spot. He saw a big tree with a mighty branch and thought it would be a great place for his nest. As Tim started to build his nest, a big squirrel named Sam came by. Sam said, "This is my tree! You can't build your nest here!" Tim was sad but he did not give up. He told Sam, "I will find another mighty branch for my nest." Tim searched and searched, and finally found another big tree with a mighty branch. He built his nest and was very happy. The next day, Sam the squirrel came to visit Tim. Sam saw how happy Tim was in his new nest and said, "I'm sorry I was mean. Can we be friends?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, we can be friends!" From that day on, Tim and Sam were the best of friends. They played and shared the trees in the forest. Tim's search for a nest led him not only to a new home but also to a great friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max had a little friend named Tom. Tom was a small cat. They liked to play in the open yard near their house. One day, Max saw a ball far away. He said to Tom, "Let's go get the ball!" Tom was scared because the ball was at a high point on a hill. Max said, "Don't worry, I will lead you there." As they went up the hill, they met a mean bird. The bird did not want them to get the ball. The bird said, "This is my ball!" But Max and Tom wanted to play with the ball too. They did not know what to do. Tom had an idea. He said to the bird, "We can all play with the ball together!" The bird thought about it and agreed. So, Max, Tom, and the bird played with the ball and had lots of fun. They all became good friends.

One day, Jack and his mom went shopping. Jack picked out a new box of crayons and his mom paid for them. He was so excited to get home and start using them. When they arrived at the house, Jack opened the box and gave one crayon to his mom. He said, "Thank you for the crayons!" His mom smiled and said, "You're welcome, Jack!". They both got to work. Jack was drawing a very colourful picture of his house and mom was writing a cheerful story. Jack was so excited and said, "Thank you for sharing the crayons with me!" His mom smiled and said, "You're very welcome, my boy!". Jack was having so much fun. He thanked his mom again and again for the cheerful box of crayons. He was so grateful that he was able to draw and colour such beautiful things.
