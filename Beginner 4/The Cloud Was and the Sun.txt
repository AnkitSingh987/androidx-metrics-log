Category: Beginner
Title: The Cloud Was and the Sun

One day, a big cloud was in the sky. It was a mild day, and the sun was warm. A little girl named Lily saw the cloud and said, "Hi, Cloud! Can you let me play with you?" The cloud said, "Yes, Lily! I will let you play with me. Jump on my back and we can go for a ride." So, Lily jumped on the cloud and they went for a ride in the sky. As they were flying, something unexpected happened. The cloud started to change colors! It turned pink, then blue, then green. Lily was so surprised and said, "Wow, Cloud! I didn't know you could change colors!" The cloud said, "I didn't know either, but playing with you made me happy, and that's why I changed colors." They both laughed and continued to play in the sky.

One day, a little dog named Max went for a walk. It was a wet day, and Max got wet too. He did not like being wet, but he loved to play outside. While Max was playing, a small flea jumped on him. The flea wanted to be Max's friend. Max did not like fleas, but he allowed the flea to stay with him for a while. They played together in the wet grass. Soon, it was time for Max to go home. He said goodbye to the flea and went inside. His owner, a little girl named Lily, gave Max a warm bath to help him get clean and dry. Max was happy to be home and no longer wet. He thought about the flea and hoped it found a new friend to play with.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars and watch the sun shine through his window. He would sit on the floor and make the cars go fast, up and down the room. One day, Tim found a big screen in the living room. It was shiny and new. He saw pretty colors and fun shapes moving on the screen. Tim clapped his hands and laughed as he watched the screen. Tim's mom came into the room and smiled. She sat down with Tim and they watched the screen together. The sun shone through the window, making the room bright and warm. Tim felt happy and safe with his mom, watching the shiny screen and playing with his toy cars.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Amy. Amy had a nosy cat named Whiskers. Whiskers liked to watch Amy dress up in pretty clothes. Whiskers liked to play with Amy's shoes and ribbons. One day, Amy's mom asked her to go to the store to buy some milk. "Amy, can you go to the store and get some milk, please?" Amy said, "Yes, Mom!" She put on her coat and shoes, and Whiskers watched her dress. Whiskers wanted to go with Amy, so he followed her to the store. At the store, Amy picked up a big jug of milk. Whiskers was being nosy and sniffed around the store. He jumped onto a shelf and knocked down a box. The storekeeper saw Whiskers and said, "Amy, please keep your nosy cat outside next time." Amy said, "I'm sorry, sir." She paid for the milk and left the store with Whiskers. They walked back home together. Amy's mom was happy to see them and thanked Amy for the milk. Whiskers went back to being nosy and played with Amy's ribbons while she dressed.

One day, a boy named Tim found a hose in his yard. He thought it would be fun to play with it. So, he turned on the water and started to shoot water at the plants. The plants were happy to get a drink. Then, Tim had an idea. He wanted to shoot water at his friend, Sam. He called Sam over and said, "Sam, come here! I want to show you something." Sam came running, and Tim shot water at him with the hose. Sam got wet and was upset. He said, "Tim, that's not nice! Now I am all wet." Tim felt bad for making Sam upset. He said, "I am sorry, Sam. I didn't mean to make you upset. I just wanted to have fun." Sam forgave him, and they both learned that it's important to think about how our actions can make others feel. From that day on, they always tried to be kind to each other and think about how their actions could make others feel.
