Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Tom and the Garden

Lily and Tom were playing in the garden. They liked to dig and look for bugs. They saw a big red radish in the dirt. Lily wanted to eat it. She pulled it out and took a bite. "Yuck!" she said. "This is not good. It is spicy and bitter." Tom laughed. He said, "That is not for eating. That is for mommy. She likes radishes. She puts them in salads." Lily felt bad. She said, "I'm sorry. I did not know. Will mommy be mad at me?" Tom said, "Maybe. She might punish you. She might make you eat more radishes. Or she might make you go to bed early." Lily did not want that. She said, "What can we do? How can we fix it?" Tom thought for a moment. He said, "I have an idea. We can find another radish. We can wash it and make it look nice. We can give it to mommy and say we found it for her. Maybe she will be happy and not punish you." Lily liked the idea. She said, "OK. Let's do that. Let's find another radish." They looked around the garden. They found another radish. It was bigger and redder than the first one. They washed it and dried it. They put it in a basket. They went to the kitchen. They saw mommy. She was making dinner. She looked warm and tired. They said, "Mommy, mommy, look what we found for you. A big red radish. We know you like radishes. We dug it out for you. We washed it and made it look nice. Do you like it?" Mommy smiled. She said, "Thank you, Lily and Tom. That is very sweet of you. I do like radishes. This is a very nice radish. You are very good children. I'm not mad at you, Lily. I'm proud of you. You shared and you helped. You did not need to be punished. You can have some dessert after dinner. How about some ice cream?" Lily and Tom were happy. They said, "Yay! Ice cream! We love ice cream. Thank you, mommy. We love you." They hugged mommy. They put the basket on the table. They went back to the garden. They played some more. They did not eat any more radishes. They waited for dinner and ice cream. They had a good day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom loved to play outside. One day, he saw a gray bunny. The bunny was very hungry. Tom said, "Hello, bunny! Are you hungry? I have a potato." The bunny nodded its head. Tom gave the potato to the bunny. The bunny ate the potato and was happy. The next day, Tom went to play again. He saw the gray bunny. The bunny had found more potatoes. The bunny said, "Tom, look! I yield potatoes for us!" Tom smiled and they ate the potatoes together. They became best friends forever.

One day, a small cat named Sam was playing near a deep drain. Sam liked to play with his ball and chase it around. But today, his ball went too close to the drain and fell in. Sam was sad and didn't know what to do. Sam's friend, a big dog named Max, saw Sam was sad. Max asked, "Why are you sad, Sam?" Sam told Max about his ball in the deep drain. Max wanted to help Sam get his ball back. Max looked in the deep drain and saw the ball. He used his long paw to reach in and grab the ball. Max gave the ball back to Sam. Sam was so happy and thanked Max for his help. Now they could play together again.

One day, a little girl named Lucy was eager to help her mom exercise. Lucy and her mom went to the park to play and have fun. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. At the park, Lucy saw her friend Tim. Tim was also eager to help his mom exercise. Lucy said, "Hi, Tim! Do you want to help our moms exercise together?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, Lucy! Let's do it!" Lucy and Tim helped their moms by playing catch with a big, red ball. They all laughed and had a great time. The moms got good exercise, and Lucy and Tim felt happy that they could help. After they finished playing, they all went home to rest.
