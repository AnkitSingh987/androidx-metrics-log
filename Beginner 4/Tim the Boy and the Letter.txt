Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Letter

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very tired. He had a letter in his hand. He wanted to pay for a toy. Tim walked to the toy store with the letter. He saw a big, red ball. He wanted the ball so much. Tim gave the letter to the man in the store. The man looked at the letter and smiled. The man said, "This is not money, but it is a special letter." The man gave Tim the big, red ball for free! Tim was so happy. He went home and played with his new ball all day long.

Sara and Ben were twins who liked to play in the garden. They had a big tree with red cherries that they could pick and eat. But they had to be careful, because there was a naughty bird who liked to steal cherries too. The bird was black and white and had a long beak. One day, Sara and Ben decided to make a cherry pie for their mom. They took a basket and went to the tree. They picked many cherries and put them in the basket. They were very happy and proud. They said to each other, "Mom will love our pie. It will be so yummy and sweet." But the naughty bird was watching them from a branch. He saw the basket full of cherries and he wanted them for himself. He waited until Sara and Ben went inside the house to get some flour and sugar. Then he flew down and landed on the basket. He started to peck at the cherries and eat them. He made a big mess and threw the pits on the ground. Sara and Ben came back and saw the bird and the basket. They were very angry and sad. They shouted at the bird, "Go away, you bad bird! You stole our cherries! You ruined our pie!" The bird was scared and flew away. He left behind a few cherries, but they were not enough for a pie. Sara and Ben cried and hugged each other. They did not know what to do. Then they heard their mom calling them. She said, "Come here, my darlings. I have a surprise for you." They wiped their tears and went to the kitchen. There they saw a big cherry pie on the table. It smelled so good and looked so tidy. Their mom said, "I saw you picking cherries and I wanted to help you. I made this pie for you. You worked so hard and you deserve a treat." Sara and Ben smiled and hugged their mom. They said, "Thank you, mom. You are the best. We love you and we love your pie." They cut a slice for each of them and ate it. It was so yummy and sweet. They forgot about the naughty bird and the stolen cherries. They were very happy and proud.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to her aunt's house. Her aunt had a big, harmless dog named Fido. Mia was scared of Fido, but she wanted to try to play with him. Mia said to her aunt, "I want to try to play with Fido, but I am scared." Her aunt smiled and said, "Fido is harmless. He will not hurt you. Just try to play with him." Mia tried to play with Fido. She threw a ball, and Fido ran to get it. But then, something unexpected happened. Fido brought back a big, colorful bird instead of the ball! Mia and her aunt laughed, and Mia was not scared of Fido anymore.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to swing on the big tree in his backyard. One day, while he was swinging, he saw a weird bird. The bird was big and had funny colors. Tim was very curious about the bird. Tim went inside his house and found his dad reading a newspaper. He asked his dad, "Dad, did you see the weird bird outside?" His dad looked up from the newspaper and said, "No, Tim. I didn't see it. Let's go look together!" So, Tim and his dad went outside to look for the weird bird. They found it sitting on the swing, singing a happy song. Tim and his dad laughed and watched the bird. They had a fun day together, and they would always remember the day they met the weird bird.
