Category: Beginner
Title: The Tower and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big tower. The tower was very tall and heavy. Many animals lived near the tower. They liked to play and have fun together. One day, a little bird wanted to see the top of the tower. The bird flew up, up, up. It was so high! The bird could hear the wind blowing. The bird felt happy to be so high. But the bird was tired. It was hard to fly so high. The bird went back down to its friends. They were happy to see the bird again. They played and had fun together. And the bird told them all about the top of the tower.

Mom picked up a tall strip from the kitchen table. She asked Sally, “Do you want to tie it?” Sally smiled and nodded. She grabbed the strip, but it was too long for her little hands to handle. Mom said, “I can help you.” She took the strip and knelt down to the floor. She showed Sally how to make a knot with the end. Then she looped it around her hands and made another knot. Sally clapped. “That looks like a butterfly!” Mom smiled and said, “Yes, it does! You’re so clever! Now we can hang it in the tree.” They went outside and found the tallest tree in the garden. Mom and Sally placed the strip around the tallest branch. As they stood back to admire it, the bright strip blew gently in the breeze. Mom said, “We made a beautiful butterfly!” Sally hugged Mom and said, “Thanks for helping me Tie!”

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim loved to play on the swings, slide, and make new friends. Today, he saw a small bird with a hurt wing. The bird needed support to fly again. Tim's mom told a joke to make the bird feel better. The bird laughed and felt happy. Tim and his mom were alert and watched the bird closely. With their help and support, the bird tried to fly again. It flew a little bit, then a little more, and soon it was flying high in the sky. Tim and his mom were very happy they could help the bird. They went home with a big smile on their faces.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play with blocks. They can build many things with blocks. They can build houses, towers, bridges, and cars. One day, Mom says, "We need to go to the grocery. We need to buy milk, eggs, bread, and fruit. Do you want to come with me?" Lily and Ben say, "Yes, Mom. We want to come with you. Can we bring our blocks?" Mom says, "Sure, you can bring your blocks. But you have to put them in a bag. And you have to be careful with them. Don't lose them or throw them." Lily and Ben put their blocks in a bag. They go to the grocery with Mom. They see many things at the grocery. They see apples, bananas, oranges, and grapes. They see cheese, yogurt, butter, and ham. They see cookies, cakes, pies, and candy. Lily and Ben say, "Wow, Mom. The grocery has many things. Can we have some cookies?" Mom says, "No, cookies are not good for you. You can have one cookie after dinner. But first, you have to eat your vegetables." Lily and Ben say, "OK, Mom. We will eat our vegetables. But can we play with our blocks?" Mom says, "Yes, you can play with your blocks. But only in the corner. And don't make a mess. And don't bother other people." Lily and Ben go to the corner. They take out their blocks. They start to build something with their blocks. They build a funny animal. It has a big head, a long neck, a round body, and four legs. It has two eyes, a nose, a mouth, and two ears. It has red, blue, green, and yellow blocks. Lily and Ben say, "Look, Mom. We made a funny animal. It is a giraffe. Do you like it?" Mom says, "Yes, I like it. It is very funny. And very creative. You are very good at building things with blocks. I am proud of you." Lily and Ben say, "Thank you, Mom. We love you. And we love our blocks. And we love our funny animal."
