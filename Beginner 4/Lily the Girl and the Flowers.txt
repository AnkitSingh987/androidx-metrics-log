Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Flowers

Once there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mommy and daddy. One day, Lily went outside to play in the garden. She saw a vine with big, pretty flowers. Lily wanted to touch the flowers, but she was scared. Lily ran back to her mommy and said, "Mommy, can I touch the flowers on the vine?" Her mommy smiled and said, "Yes, Lily, the vine is harmless. You can touch the flowers." Lily went back to the vine and touched the flowers. They were very soft. She saw that the flowers were dirty. So, Lily decided to wash them. She got a bucket of water and washed the flowers. They became clean and shiny. Lily was happy that she helped the flowers on the vine. She ran to her mommy and said, "Mommy, I washed the flowers! Now they are clean and pretty." Her mommy hugged her and said, "Good job, Lily! You are a big helper." From that day on, Lily loved to wash and take care of the flowers on the vine. She learned that it was good to help and that harmless things can be fun to play with.

One day, Tim and his dog, Spot, were ready to go play outside. They put on their shoes and ran out the door. The sun was shining, and they were very happy. Suddenly, dark clouds came, and a storm started. The wind blew, and the rain fell. Tim and Spot were scared. They wanted to go back home, but they did not know the way. Tim saw a big tree and pointed at it. "Look, Spot! Let's hide under the tree until the storm stops." Spot barked and wagged his tail. They ran to the tree and waited for the storm to end.

Once upon a time, there was an enormous snake named Sam. Sam lived in a big sack near a tall tree. Sam was a good snake who liked to play and have fun. One day, Sam met a little girl named Mia. Mia was not scared of Sam. She said, "Hi, snake! Want to play with me?" Sam was happy and nodded his head. They played together all day long. At the end of the day, Sam went back to his big sack. Mia said, "Bye, Sam! Let's play again tomorrow!" Sam smiled and waved goodbye. From that day on, Sam and Mia became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a kind dog named Max. Max lived in a lab. In the lab, Max had a job to stir a big pot every day. Max liked his job because he could help others. One day, Max saw a little bird with a hurt wing. The bird was sad and could not fly. Max wanted to help the bird. He thought about the big pot in the lab. Max had an idea. Max took the bird to the lab. He stirred the big pot and gave some to the bird. The bird felt better and could fly again. Max was happy to help the bird. The bird said, "Thank you, Max!" They became good friends. The moral of the story is to always help others when they are in need.

One day, a girl named Lucy went to school. She was very excited. She liked school because she could play with her friends and learn new things. Lucy was a very happy girl. In school, there was a boy named Max. Max was rude. He did not care about others. He would push and yell at the other kids. Lucy did not like how Max acted. She wanted to be nice and help her friends. One day, Lucy saw Max being rude to a little girl. The girl was sad and cried. Lucy went to the girl and said, "Do not worry. I will help you." Lucy took care of the girl and they became friends. Max saw this and felt bad. He learned that being rude was not good. Max decided to be nice and care for others too. From that day on, everyone in school was happy and kind.
