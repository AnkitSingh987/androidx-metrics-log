Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Dog

One day, a little girl named Sue found a delicate card on the floor. She picked it up and saw a pretty picture on it. Sue wanted to show her mom the card, but she was playing with her dog, Max. Max was a big dog and loved to play with Sue. Sue ran to her mom and said, "Mom, look at this card I found! It's so pretty!" Her mom looked at the card and said, "Yes, Sue, it is very pretty. Be careful with it because it is delicate." Sue held the card carefully in her hands. Suddenly, Max jumped up and tried to grab the card from Sue. He wanted to play with it too. But Max's jump made Sue tear the card by accident. Sue was sad, but then she saw that the tear made the card into two cards with two pretty pictures. Now, Sue and Max could each have a pretty card to play with.

One day, a bright sun was in the sky. A big van was on the road. A little dog saw the van. The little dog wanted to play. The dog liked to jump. The dog jumped near the van. The van was very big. The dog was very small. The dog tried to jump high. The dog wanted the van to see him. But the van did not see the little dog. The dog jumped and jumped. The van went fast. The dog was sad. The van went away. The little dog could not play.

Once upon a time, there was a group of animals. In this group, there was a brave lion, a big elephant, a fast deer, and a small mouse. They all lived in the forest and played together every day. One day, the brave lion said, "I want to go on an adventure. Let's go find the big lake!" The other animals agreed, and they all set off on their journey. The small mouse was scared but wanted to be brave like the lion. As they walked, the sky grew dark and scary. The brave lion led the group, but the small mouse was very scared. The other animals noticed and tried to help, but the small mouse was too scared to listen. Finally, the group reached the big lake. But when they got there, they found that the lake had dried up. The animals were sad and tired. The small mouse felt bad because he was too scared to enjoy the adventure. In the end, the group of animals went back to the forest, sad about their journey. The brave lion learned that sometimes, being brave isn't enough. And the small mouse learned that it's okay to be scared, but it's important to listen to friends who want to help.

Lily wanted to play with her new doll. It had a pink dress with a zip on the back. She liked to zip and unzip the dress. It was her original doll, not like the others. But her brother Max wanted to play with it too. He said it was his turn. He tried to grab the doll from Lily's hands. Lily said no. She did not want to share. "Mom, Lily won't allow me to play with the doll!" Max shouted. He was angry and sad. Mom came to see what was wrong. She saw Lily and Max fighting over the doll. She said, "Stop, both of you. Why are you fighting?" "Because Lily won't allow me to play with the doll!" Max repeated. "But it's Lily's original doll. She got it for her birthday. She can decide who plays with it." Mom said. "But I want to play with it too! It has a zip!" Max said. Mom thought for a moment. She said, "How about this? Lily, you can play with the doll for ten minutes. Then you have to allow Max to play with it for ten minutes. And then you can take turns. Is that fair?" Lily nodded. She said, "Okay. But only if Max is careful with the zip. It might break." Max nodded too. He said, "Okay. I will be careful. Thank you, Lily." Mom smiled. She said, "Good. Now you can play nicely together. And remember to say please and thank you." Lily and Max agreed. They stopped fighting and started playing. They both liked the doll with the zip. It was original and fun.
