Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Jellyfish

One day, a girl named Mia went to the beach. She saw many jellyfish in the water. Mia wanted to count them all. She put her toes in the sand and looked at the water. She saw one, two, three jellyfish. Mia was happy. But then, a boy named Tim came. Tim said the jellyfish were dull. Mia was sad. She liked the jellyfish. Mia and Tim did not agree. They did not know what to do. Mia had an idea. She asked Tim to count the jellyfish with her. They counted one, two, three, four, five jellyfish. Tim smiled. He thought the jellyfish were not dull anymore. Mia and Tim were happy. They played on the beach and counted jellyfish all day.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red fish. The big, red fish liked to show off his style. He swam fast and jumped high. All the other fish loved to watch him. One day, a little, blue fish wanted to learn the big, red fish's style. The big, red fish said, "I will show you, but you must do what I say." The little, blue fish agreed. The big, red fish told the little, blue fish to swim very close to a boat. The little, blue fish did not want to, but he wanted to learn the style. So, he swam close to the boat. The boat hit the little, blue fish, and he became dead. The big, red fish was sad. He learned that showing off can be bad, and it is better to be safe than sorry.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a piece of cake on the table. He was very hungry and wanted to eat it. But he knew that the cake was for his sister, Sue. Tim thought for a moment and then took a small bite. Sue came into the room and saw the cake with a bite taken out of it. She was sad and said, "Who ate my cake?" Tim felt sorry for eating the cake. He looked at Sue and said, "I am sorry, Sue. I ate your cake." Sue smiled and said, "It's okay, Tim. We can share the cake." They both ate the cake together and were happy. Tim learned to always say sorry when he did something wrong, and Sue learned to share with her brother.

Once upon a time, there was a red dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and run in the park. One sunny day, Spot saw a pink ice cream truck. The ice cream truck had many yummy treats. Spot wanted to taste the pink ice cream. He wagged his tail and jumped up and down. The nice man in the truck saw Spot and gave him a pink ice cream cone. Spot licked the ice cream and it was so cold and sweet. Spot's friend, a little girl named Sue, came to the park too. Sue had a red ball to play with. Spot shared his pink ice cream with Sue, and they both tasted it together. Then, they played with the red ball all day long. They were very happy and tired when the sun went down.

Once upon a time, there was a pigeon. The pigeon lived in a park. It was very icy in the park. The pigeon was hungry. It looked for food. A nice man came to the park. He provided food for the pigeon. The pigeon was happy. It ate the food. The pigeon was not hungry anymore. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a small girl named Mia. She was going on a tour with her family to see animals. Mia was so grateful and happy to go. She wanted to catch a ball at the tour. Her mom told her to be ready for a surprise. At the tour, Mia saw many animals like cats, dogs, and birds. She had a lot of fun. But she still wanted to catch a ball. She knew her mom had a surprise for her. Mia was excited and could not wait. At the end of the tour, Mia's mom gave her a big box. Mia thought it was a ball to catch. But when she opened the box, there was a small, cute kitten inside! Mia was so surprised and happy. She loved the kitten more than a ball. Mia was very grateful for the best surprise ever.
