Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Brown Turkey and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a brown turkey named Tom. Tom had a big job to do. He had to deliver a box to his friend, the cow. Tom was scared because he had never done this before. As Tom walked, he met a dog. The dog said, "What are you doing, Tom?" Tom said, "I have to deliver this box to my friend, the cow. But I am scared." The dog said, "I will help you, Tom. Let's go together." Tom and the dog walked and walked. They saw a big river. The river was too big to cross. Tom was sad. The dog said, "Don't worry, Tom. I will swim across the river and bring a boat back for you." The dog swam fast and brought a boat back. Tom and the dog got in the boat and crossed the river. At last, they found the cow. Tom gave the box to the cow. The cow said, "Thank you, Tom. You are a good friend." Tom was happy. He had delivered the box and made a new friend, too.

Once upon a time, there was a famous dog named Spot. Spot liked to play with his friends in the park. They would run, jump, and have fun together. One day, Spot and his friends saw some smoke in the sky. They didn't know where it came from. They wanted to find out, so they followed the smoke. They walked and walked until they found a big fire. The fire was very hot and smoky. Spot knew he had to help put out the fire. He thought very hard and then had an idea. He took a big breath and began to sneeze. He sneezed and sneezed until the fire was all gone. Spot and his friends were very happy. They went back to the park to play and have fun. Spot was a hero, and everyone loved him even more.

Once upon a time, there was a small bug who loved to crawl. He was very enthusiastic about crawling all day long. One day, he met a big bug who was very patient. The small bug asked, "Can you crawl very fast like me?" The big bug smiled and said, "No, I can't crawl fast, but I can do something else." The small bug was curious and wanted to see what the big bug could do. The next day, they met again. The small bug crawled fast, and the big bug showed him how to jump high. The small bug was amazed and tried to jump too. At first, he couldn't jump, but he remembered how patient the big bug was. So, he kept trying and soon, he could jump high too! The small bug was very happy, and they both played together, crawling and jumping all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Lucy. She was three years old and loved the beach. One sunny day, Lucy decided to go to the beach. She put on her swimsuit and a hat, and headed outside. Lucy was so excited to be going to the beach. As she arrived, she saw the cool, blue sea and the sand was so soft under her feet. She started running towards the sea. Then, she stopped and saw a big rock. She climbed the rock and said, 'This is the best spot!' Just then, she heard a voice say, "Hello Lucy!" She looked around, but there was nobody there. Then Lucy heard the voice again. This time it said, "Look down!" Lucy looked and saw a little crab. The crab said, "Hey Lucy, why don't you start playing in the sand?" Lucy smiled and said, "That sounds like fun!" So, Lucy and the crab started making a sandcastle on the beach. They worked and played until the sun went down, and then Lucy said goodbye to her new friend and went home.

Once upon a time, there was a wise cat named Tom. Tom loved to play catch with his best friend, a little girl named Sue. They played catch every day, and they always had lots of fun. One sunny day, Tom and Sue were playing catch with a soft towel. Sue threw the towel high into the air, and Tom jumped up to catch it. But this time, the wind blew the towel far, far away. Tom and Sue looked at each other and said, "Oh no! What do we do now?" They decided to look for the towel. They walked and walked until they saw a big tree. The towel was high up in the tree! Tom said, "I can climb and catch it!" But as Tom climbed the tree, a friendly bird came by and picked up the towel in its beak. The bird flew down and gave the towel back to Sue. Tom and Sue were very happy and surprised. They thanked the bird and went back home to play catch again. And from that day on, they always played catch with their new friend, the bird.
