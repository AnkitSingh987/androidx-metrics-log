Category: Beginner
Title: Jimmy and the Toys

Jimmy was so excited. He had a new green bin for his bedroom. It was so big, he could fit all his toys in it. He wanted to show it to his mom. Jimmy rolled the bin to the living room and showed his mom. She said it was a great bin and Jimmy was very happy. He rolled the bin back to his bedroom and started filling it with his toys. He was so excited to put all of his toys inside and he kept rolling the bin around his bedroom. He was so excited that he decided to roll the bin down the hallway. He went back and forth up and down the hallway, rolling the bin and smiling. It felt like so much fun!

Lily and Ben liked to play with their toys. They had many toys, but their favorite was a big train. The train had many parts that they could put together and take apart. They liked to make different shapes and colors with the train parts. One day, they found a new part in their toy box. It was shiny and round, and it had a button on it. They did not know what it was or where it came from. It was a mystery. They wondered if it was part of their train or something else. They decided to try it on their train. They put it on the front of the engine and pressed the button. Suddenly, the train made a loud noise and started to move by itself. It went very fast around the room, and it did not stop when they said "stop". It was a modern part that made their train go. Lily and Ben were scared and excited. They chased the train and tried to catch it. They laughed and shouted as the train zoomed past them. They had fun with their new part, but they also wanted to know where it came from and how it worked. Maybe they could find out more about it later. For now, they enjoyed their mystery part and their modern train.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty cat named Lucy. Lucy loved to celebrate her birthday with her friends. Every year, they would go to the market to buy fun things for her party. One day, Lucy and her friend, a dog named Max, went to the market. They saw many things like balloons, cake, and toys. Max said, "Let's get the cake and balloons for your party!" Lucy agreed and they bought the items. At the party, Lucy and her friends were having lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind blew all the balloons away! Lucy and her friends were sad. But Max had an idea. He said, "Let's make our own balloons with paper!" They all worked together and made pretty paper balloons. The party was even more fun than they thought it would be.

Once upon a time, there was an attractive machine that could fold things. It lived in a big room with other machines. One day, a little girl named Lucy came to the room. She saw the attractive machine and wanted to use it to fold her clothes. "Can you help me fold my clothes?" Lucy asked the machine. The machine nodded and started to fold her clothes. But then, another machine got angry. It didn't like that the attractive machine was getting all the attention. "No, I want to help Lucy!" said the angry machine. The two machines started to fight. Lucy didn't like this. She wanted them to be nice to each other. So, she said, "Please stop fighting! You both can help me fold my clothes." The machines stopped and listened to Lucy. They worked together to fold her clothes. In the end, they became friends and learned that it is better to work together than to fight. And Lucy learned that asking for help and being kind can make everyone happy.

Sara loves her pony. She calls him Spot. He has brown fur and white spots. He is very sweet and likes to eat apples and carrots. Sara likes to brush his fur and give him hugs. One day, Sara and Spot go to the park. They see other kids and animals. Sara wants to play with them. She sees a girl with a bike. She thinks the bike is fun. She asks the girl if she can try it. The girl says yes. Sara leaves Spot by a tree. She tells him to wait for her. She gets on the bike and rides around. She likes the bike. She forgets about Spot. Spot is sad. He misses Sara. He sees a boy with a ball. He thinks the ball is fun. He wants to play with it. He runs to the boy and tries to take the ball. The boy gets angry. He yells at Spot. He tells him to go away. Sara hears the boy. She looks for Spot. She sees him with the ball. She sees the boy is mad. She feels sorry. She runs to Spot and the boy. She removes the ball from Spot's mouth. She gives it back to the boy. She says sorry. She tells Spot he is bad. She takes him to the tree. She ties him with a rope. She tells him to stay. Spot is sad. He does not understand. He loves Sara. He loves the ball. He does not know why he is bad. He wants to play with Sara. He wants to be sweet. He whines and cries. Sara hears Spot. She looks at him. She sees he is sad. She feels sorry. She remembers she loves him. She forgets the bike. She runs to Spot and the rope. She removes the rope from his neck. She gives him a hug. She says sorry. She tells him he is good. She tells him she loves him. She takes him to the bike. She asks the girl if Spot can play too. The girl says yes. Spot is happy. He likes Sara. He likes the bike. He likes the girl. He likes the ball. He is sweet. He plays with them. Sara is happy. She likes Spot. She likes the bike. She likes the girl. She likes the ball. She is sweet. She plays with them. They have fun.
