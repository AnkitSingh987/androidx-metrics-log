Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Necklace

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had a box of incredible jewelry. One day, she went to play outside with her friend, Tim. Lily showed Tim her jewelry. Tim asked a question, "Can I wear the shiny necklace?" Lily said, "No, it's mine." So they played together, but Tim was sad. Later, a big wind came and blew the jewelry away. Lily and Tim looked for it, but they could not find it. They were both very sad, and the jewelry was gone forever.

Once upon a time, there was a strong little ant. The ant lived in a tunnel under the ground with all its friends. The ant and its friends liked to play all day. One day, the strong little ant met a big bug. The big bug said, "I want to play too!" The ant thought for a moment and said, "Okay, you can play, but you must be nice." The big bug was very happy and started to play with the ant and its friends. But then, something unexpected happened! The big bug was too strong and broke the tunnel. All the ants were sad. The big bug said, "I am sorry, I did not mean to break the tunnel." The strong little ant said, "It is okay, we accept your sorry. Let's all work together to fix the tunnel." So, the big bug and all the ants worked together and fixed the tunnel. They all played happily ever after.

One day, a helpful boy named Mark went to play at the park. He saw a little girl crying by the swing. Mark went to her and asked, "Why are you sad?" The girl said, "I lost my toy. Can you help me find it?" Mark wanted to help, so he said, "Yes, I will help you." They looked everywhere in the park, but they could not find the toy. Then, a big dog came up to Mark and the girl. The dog had something in its mouth. It was the girl's toy! The dog offered the toy to the girl. She was very happy and said, "Thank you, Mark and dog!" They all played together and had a fun day at the park.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the store with her mom. Sue saw a big, red ball. She wanted the ball very much. Sue asked her mom, "Can I have the ball, please?" Her mom looked at the price and nodded her head. Sue was very happy. At home, Sue played with the ball all day. She was very proud of her new toy. Sue's friend, Tim, came over to play. They had fun with the ball together. They threw the ball up high and caught it. Then, something unexpected happened. The ball went too high and got stuck in a tree. Sue and Tim were sad. They didn't know how to get the ball down. Sue's mom came outside and saw the problem. She got a long stick and used it to get the ball down. Sue and Tim were happy again. They thanked Sue's mom and continued to play with the ball.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Tom. He had a big house and many things of value. One day, Tom met a new friend named Joe. "Hi Joe, want to see my valuable things?" Tom asked. Joe said, "Yes, I'd love to see them!" So, Tom showed Joe all the valuable things in his house. One night, Tom forgot to lock his door. When he woke up, he found that all his valuable things were gone! Tom was very sad and told Joe about it. Joe said, "Don't worry, Tom. I will help you find your things." As they looked, they found a big box. Inside the box, they found all of Tom's valuable things! But there was also a note. The note said, "I took your things to teach you a lesson. Always lock your door." Tom thanked Joe, and from that day on, he always locked his door.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird who loved to sing. The bird lived in a big tree in a beautiful park. The bird liked to sing to all the people who visited the park. One sunny day, the bird found something shiny and gold on the ground. It was a small, unknown gold toy. The bird picked it up and showed it to a friendly squirrel. "Look, I found gold!" the bird said. The squirrel was very excited. "Let's show it to everyone!" the squirrel said. So, the bird and the squirrel went around the park to show their new gold toy to all their friends. As they went around the park, the little bird sang a happy song about their gold toy. All the animals and people in the park stopped to listen to the bird's beautiful song. They all clapped and cheered when the bird finished singing. The bird and the squirrel felt very happy and proud of their new gold toy.
