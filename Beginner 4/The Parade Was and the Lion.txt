Category: Beginner
Title: The Parade Was and the Lion

One day, a big parade was in town. It had many colors and fun things to see. There were big balloons and people in costumes. Everyone was happy and clapping. A boy named Tim saw a scary mask in the parade. He was scared and said, "Mom, that mask is scary!" His mom said, "Don't worry, it's just pretend. It's not real." Tim's friend, Sue, wanted to bring a toy to the parade. She said, "Tim, I will bring my toy lion. He will help us not be scared!" Tim liked that idea. So Sue brought her toy lion to the parade. Tim and Sue held the lion and watched the parade. They were not scared anymore. They had fun and laughed at the big balloons and the funny costumes.

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play all day long. He would observe the birds in the sky and the bugs on the ground. He liked to watch them fly and crawl. One day, Spot saw a big ball. He wanted to test how high it could bounce. So, Spot pushed the ball with his nose. The ball went up, up, up into the air. Spot was so happy to see it bounce so high! Spot played with the ball all day long. He had so much fun testing how high the ball could bounce. Spot was a very playful dog and loved to observe the world around him. And that is the end of the story about Spot, the playful dog.

Tom and Lily were playing in the living room. They liked to pretend they were spies and use the cable from the TV as a rope. They tied one end to the sofa and the other end to the table. They crawled under the cable and whispered to each other. "Shh, we have to be quiet. The bad guys are looking for us," Tom said. "I have a plan. We can use this blanket to disappear," Lily said. She grabbed a big blanket from the couch and threw it over them. They giggled and hid under the blanket. They felt safe and cozy. But then they heard a loud noise. It was their mom. She came into the living room and saw the mess. She saw the cable stretched across the floor and the blanket on top of it. "What are you doing?" she asked. She sounded angry. Tom and Lily peeked out from under the blanket. They saw their mom's face. They felt scared and confused. "We were playing spies, mom. We used the cable as a rope and the blanket to disappear," Lily said. "That's not a good idea. You could trip over the cable and hurt yourself. And you could break the TV. You have to be careful with the cable. It's not a toy," mom said. She untied the cable and put it back in the TV. She folded the blanket and put it back on the couch. She looked at Tom and Lily. "I'm sorry, mom. We didn't mean to make a mess," Tom said. "We just wanted to have fun," Lily said. "I know, sweeties. But you have to play with your own toys. And you have to ask me before you use anything else. OK?" mom said. "OK, mom. We're sorry," Tom and Lily said. Mom smiled and hugged them. She was not angry anymore. "Come on, let's go to the kitchen. I have some cookies for you," she said. Tom and Lily followed her. They were happy again. They forgot about the cable and the blanket. They only wanted the cookies.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a dark room. In the room, there were many colors. A little girl named Mia lived in the town. She loved colors very much. One day, she found the dark room and wanted to see the colors inside. Mia went to the dark room and saw a big box. The box had a string. She said, "I want to see the colors!" So, Mia used her little hands to pull the string. When she pulled the string, the box opened, and all the colors came out. The colors made the dark room bright and pretty. Mia was very happy. She said, "Thank you, colors, for making the room beautiful!" The colors said, "Thank you, Mia, for pulling the string and letting us out!" Mia and the colors became good friends and played together every day.
