Category: Beginner
Title: A Tale of the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big, red bird. The bird had a key. The key was special. The bird liked to sit on a comfortable branch. The branch was in a big tree. One day, the bird saw a small, sad cat. The cat was under the tree. The cat wanted the key. The bird did not want to give the key. They both wanted the key. They did not know what to do. The bird and the cat decided to talk. They talked about the key. They became friends. They learned to share the key. The bird and the cat were happy. They learned that sharing is good.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a girl named Lily. Lily liked to draw pictures. She used many colors to make her pictures pretty. One day, she found an unknown color in her box. It was a color she had never seen before. Lily wanted to prepare a new picture with this color. She thought about what to draw. She decided to draw a big tree with lots of leaves. She used the unknown color for the leaves. It made the tree look very special. When Lily showed her picture to her mom, her mom was very happy. She said, "This is the most beautiful tree I have ever seen!" Lily felt proud and happy. From that day on, Lily used the unknown color in all her pictures to make them special.

Once upon a time, there was a big, normal house. In this house, there lived a little girl named Amy and her mom. They loved to play and laugh together. One day, Amy and her mom were playing hide and seek. Amy ran to a dark room to hide. She saw a cobweb in the corner. She didn't like cobwebs, but she thought it was a good place to hide. So, she knelt down and hid behind a box. Her mom looked for her and called out, "Amy, where are you?" Amy giggled and stayed quiet. Her mom came into the dark room and saw Amy's shoes. She knelt down and said, "I see you, Amy!" Amy laughed and came out from behind the box. "You found me, Mom!" she said. They both smiled and continued to play hide and seek all day long. The cobweb in the corner stayed there, but Amy wasn't scared of it anymore.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a harmless bug on the ground. Tim wanted to play with the bug, but it crawled away. Tim was sad. Tim's friend, Sue, came to the park too. She had a cable in her hand. Sue said, "Look, Tim! I found this cable. Let's play with it." Tim and Sue played with the cable and had lots of fun. Suddenly, the harmless bug came back. It crawled onto the cable. Tim and Sue were surprised. The bug began to talk! It said, "Thank you for playing with the cable. It made me happy. Now, I want to play with you!" Tim and Sue laughed and played with the talking bug all day.

Once there was a boy. His name was Tom. Tom was very weird. One day he spotted a knot on the ground. He bent down and slapped it. The knot started to move! Tom was so surprised. He couldn't believe it. He had never seen anything so weird. The knot kept bouncing around the room, slapping things, and making a lot of noise. Tom didn't know what to do. Just then, his mom came in the room. She saw Tom and the weird knot and quickly made it stop moving! Tom's mom was so relieved. She was glad Tom knew better than to slap strange knots.

Once upon a time, there was a big mill. The mill was very fast. It could make things very quick. People from the town loved the mill. One day, a little girl named Lily went to the mill. She loved to imagine things. She imagined the mill as a big, friendly giant. The giant would help the people in the town. Lily was happy. The mill and Lily became good friends. They played fun games together. The mill would spin fast, and Lily would laugh. The town was happy too. The mill and Lily made everyone smile.
