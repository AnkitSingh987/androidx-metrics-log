Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Car

One day, a little boy named Tim got a new scooter. He was very happy. Tim loved to ride his scooter up and down the street. He liked to go fast and feel the wind in his hair. One day, Tim saw his friend, Sue. Sue was playing with her toy car. Tim said, "Sue, do you want to mix our toys? I can ride my scooter and you can drive your car." Sue said, "Yes, that sounds fun!" So, they played together, mixing their toys and having a good time. But then, a big dog came and barked at them. The dog was bad and scared Tim and Sue. They stopped playing and ran to their moms. Their moms told them not to mix with bad dogs. Tim and Sue learned that it is important to be careful and not mix with bad things. They promised to be more careful and continued to play with their scooter and car, having fun and staying safe.

Once upon a time, there was a popular tree. This tree had many prunes. The prunes were very good to eat. Many kids and animals liked to come to the tree to eat the prunes. One day, a little boy saw the tree. He wanted to eat the prunes too. But the tree did not belong to him. The tree belonged to a mean man. The mean man did not like to share his prunes. The little boy tried to take a prune from the tree. The mean man saw him. He got very mad. He did not let the little boy have any prunes. The little boy went home sad. The mean man kept all the prunes for himself. And so, the story ends with the little boy sad and the mean man still not sharing his prunes.

Once upon a time, there was a little boat named Bob. Bob lived in a big sea. One day, Bob wanted to reach the shore. But, there was a fierce fish named Fifi in the sea. Fifi did not want Bob to go to the shore. Bob was sad. He said, "I want to reach the shore. Please let me go, Fifi!" Fifi thought for a moment and said, "Okay, Bob. You can go to the shore. But, you must be quick!" Bob was happy. He went very fast to reach the shore. When he got there, he saw his friend, a little crab named Carl. Carl was happy to see Bob. They played all day on the shore. And, Fifi was happy too because she could still swim in the big sea.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby painter. He loved to sit and paint all day. He painted big trees, little flowers, and pretty birds. The chubby painter was very happy. One day, a big wind came. The wind blew and blew. The chubby painter could not sit and paint. His paints and brushes flew away. The chubby painter was very sad. The chubby painter tried to find his paints and brushes. He looked up, down, left, and right. But the big wind took them far away. The chubby painter could not paint anymore. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved playing with clay. One day, she found some purple clay in her playroom. She was very happy and started to make a big clay ball. Mia's friend, Tom, came over to play. He saw the purple clay ball and said, "Wow! That's a big purple ball! Can I play too?" Mia smiled and said, "Yes, let's play together!" They both played with the purple clay and made it even bigger. Then, something strange happened. The purple clay ball started to shrink! Mia and Tom watched as it got smaller and smaller. They looked at each other and laughed. They played with the small purple clay ball and had lots of fun. And they learned that sometimes, even when things shrink, they can still be fun to play with.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom was a healthy boy who liked to run and play. One day, Tom found a passport. The passport was blue and small. Tom wanted to keep the passport safe. So, he went to find something to wrap it in. He found a big, soft blanket. Tom wrapped the passport in the blanket very carefully. Tom showed the wrapped passport to his mom. She was happy he found it. They went on a fun trip with the passport. Tom was a good helper. They all lived happily ever after.
