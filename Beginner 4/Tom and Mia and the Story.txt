Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Mia and the Story

Tom and Mia were playing in the living room. They liked to pretend they were grown-ups and do things like cook, clean and iron. Tom found an iron in the closet and plugged it in. He wanted to make his shirt smooth. "Look, Mia, I can iron like Mommy!" he said, holding the iron over his shirt. "No, Tom, that's not a toy! It's hot and dangerous!" Mia said, running to him. "You can get hurt!" But Tom did not listen. He thought he knew better. He pressed the iron on his shirt and felt a sharp pain. He dropped the iron and yelled. "Ow, ow, ow! It burns!" he cried, holding his hand. Mia ran to get Mommy. Mommy came and saw what happened. She unplugged the iron and took Tom to the sink. She put his hand under cold water and wrapped it in a cloth. "Tom, what were you thinking?" she asked, looking at him with a sad face. "You could have burned yourself badly. You should never play with the iron. It's not a toy. It's a tool for grown-ups. You have to listen to Mia. She was trying to help you." Tom felt sorry and ashamed. He looked at Mia and said, "I'm sorry, Mia. You were right. I was wrong. I should have listened to you. Thank you for getting Mommy." Mia hugged him and said, "It's okay, Tom. I'm glad you're not hurt too much. I love you, brother." Mommy smiled and said, "I love you both, too. But please, be careful and obey the rules. They are there to keep you safe and happy." Tom and Mia nodded and said, "We will, Mommy. We learned our lesson." The moral of the story is: Listen to your elders and don't play with dangerous things.

One day, a small boy named Tim found a rough fork on the ground. He picked it up and looked at it. It was not like the forks he used at home. This fork was not shiny and smooth. Tim sighed, "I wish I could find a shiny fork." Tim walked to the park with the rough fork. He saw his friend, Sally, playing in the sand. Tim showed Sally the rough fork. "Look what I found," he said. Sally looked at the fork and said, "It's not shiny like our forks at home." Tim and Sally played in the sand with the rough fork. They dug holes and made sandcastles. They worked hard and sighed when they were tired. At the end of the day, they left the rough fork in the sand. As they walked home, Tim said, "Even if it was not shiny, the rough fork was still fun to play with." Sally smiled and said, "Yes, it was. Maybe we can find more things to play with tomorrow."

Once upon a time, there was a funny dog named Spot. Spot loved to watch television with his family. They would sit on the floor and laugh at the funny things they saw on the screen. One day, Spot was playing in the yard when he heard a noise. It was a little bird singing a happy song. Spot wanted to see if the bird wanted to watch television with him. He asked the bird, "Do you want to watch television with me? It is very funny!" The little bird thought it would be fun to watch television with Spot. So, they went inside and sat on the floor together. They laughed and laughed at the funny things they saw on the television. Spot and the bird had a great time and became best friends. From that day on, they always watched television together and shared many happy moments.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little bell. The bell was very proud of its shiny look. It hung near the door and made a happy sound when people came in. One day, a small boy came to the house. He saw the bell and wanted to hear its happy sound. He tried to jump and poke the bell, but he was too short. He felt sad and sat down. A kind cat saw the boy and had an idea. The cat went to the boy and let him sit on her back. The boy was happy and thanked the cat. Together, they went to the bell, and the boy could now poke it. The bell made a happy sound, and everyone in the house heard it. The boy and the cat were very happy and proud of their teamwork. They learned that helping each other can make things better. And from that day on, the boy and the cat were the best of friends.
