Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He had a whistle that he loved to play. One day, Tom went to the park with his whistle. He was very happy. While at the park, Tom saw a big dog. The big dog looked at Tom and his whistle. Tom wanted to change the dog's mood, so he blew his whistle. The dog started to dance! Tom was very surprised. Tom felt embarrassed because everyone at the park was watching him and the dog dance. But then, the dog changed into a little bird! It was a magic whistle. Tom and the little bird became best friends and played together every day.

One day, a pale lizard was walking in the sun. He felt very hot and tired. He wanted to find a cool place to rest. He saw a big tree and thought, "That tree will give me shade." Under the tree, the lizard met a kind girl named Mia. She saw the lizard and said, "You look hot and tired. Do you want some water?" The lizard nodded and Mia gave him water to drink. The lizard felt better and thought, "Mia is very nice. She cares for me." The next day, Mia came back to the tree with a surprise. She had a small house for the lizard! The lizard was very happy and thanked Mia. They became best friends, and Mia always cared for the pale lizard. And the lizard knew that he would always have a friend who cared for him.

One day, a blue bird named Bob lived in a big tree. Bob loved to fly and play with his friends. He also liked to chew on seeds he found on the ground. One day, Bob saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she lost her toy. Bob wanted to help Sue, so he flew down and said, "Don't be sad, I can help you find your toy." They looked and looked, but they could not find the toy. Suddenly, they saw a big hand in the sky. The hand had picked up Sue's toy! Bob and Sue were surprised. They never saw a big hand in the sky before. The big hand saw that Sue was sad, so it gave the toy back to her. Sue was happy again, and she said, "Thank you, big hand!" Then, Bob and Sue played together with the toy. They had a fun day, and Bob learned that even a blue bird can help make someone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sarah. She was three years old. One day, Sarah was walking in the park when she fell. She was very sad because she hurt her knee. Sarah's mommy knew they had to go to the hospital right away. On the way, Sarah saw a dirty alley. Suddenly, a guy ran out with a big bag. Sarah asked her mommy, "What is he doing?" "He's stealing money," Mommy said. Sarah knew she could save someone. She ran after the man. "Stop! Stop!" she yelled. The thief ran away, and Sarah hugged her bag safely. Mommy and Sarah took the bag to the hospital. The nurses were very happy and thanked Sarah for saving them. Sarah was so proud of herself. The end!

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a swing and wanted to play. He ran to the swing and sat on it. Tim asked his mom to push him. "Mom, can you push me high?" he said. His mom smiled and pushed him on the swing. Tim was very happy. As Tim was swinging high, he saw a big red apple on a tree. He wanted to eat the apple. So, he jumped off the swing and picked the apple. But when Tim tried to bite the apple, it was very hard. "Ouch!" he said, rubbing his teeth. Suddenly, the apple started to talk! "I'm sorry, I'm not a real apple," it said. "I'm a toy apple." Tim was very surprised. He didn't know that apples could talk. The toy apple became Tim's new friend, and they played on the swing together.
