Title: Tom and Lily and the Crown

Tom and Lily were playing in the garden. They found a shiny crown under a big tree. They did not know who it belonged to. It was a mysterious crown. "Wow, look at this crown!" Tom said. "It is so pretty and sparkly. I want to wear it." "No, I want to wear it!" Lily said. "It is mine. I saw it first." They both grabbed the crown and pulled hard. The crown broke into two pieces. They were very sad and angry. "Look what you did!" Tom said. "You broke the crown. It is your fault." "No, it is your fault!" Lily said. "You pulled too hard. You are mean." They threw the pieces of the crown at each other. They did not want to play anymore. They ran away to their homes. The next day, their mommies came to the garden. They saw the broken crown on the ground. They were very surprised and worried. "Where did this crown come from?" Tom's mommy said. "It looks very old and dirty. It might be dangerous." "Maybe it belongs to someone important." Lily's mommy said. "We should clean it and return it. It might be worth a lot of money." They picked up the pieces of the crown and took them to their homes. They tried to clean them with soap and water. But as soon as they touched the crown, they felt a sharp pain in their hands. They screamed and dropped the crown. Their hands turned red and swollen. They had been poisoned by the crown. The crown was not a toy. It was a trap. Someone had hidden it under the tree to hurt anyone who found it. Tom and Lily were lucky they did not wear it. Their mommies were not so lucky. They had to go to the hospital. They were very sick and unhappy. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom loved to play with his toy cars. One day, he found a light, shiny rock in his yard. He picked it up and ran to show his mom. "Mom, look what I found!" Tom said, holding up the rock. His mom looked at the rock and smiled. "That's a pretty rock, Tom. It has value," she said. Tom didn't know what "value" meant, but he liked how the rock looked. Tom decided to keep the rock and show it to his friends. He put it in his pocket and went to the park. He showed the rock to his friends, and they all thought it was cool. They played with the rock and their toys all day, having lots of fun. In the end, Tom learned that his light, shiny rock had value because it made him and his friends happy. And that was the best kind of value to have.

Once upon a time there was an orange monster. The monster created a big case to stop people from going outside. Everyone in the village was scared and upset. One day, a brave 3 year old called Timmy decided to solve the case. He searched high and low for a way to solve the trouble the monster had caused. Eventually, Timmy found a way to get rid of the orange monster. He put together a plan, and with the help of his friends, he caught the monster. The village was safe again. Timmy had a lesson to learn from his adventure. He learned that it was important to not be scared and to use his creativity to figure out a way to solve big problems.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird who loved to speak. He lived in a high tree in a big forest. Every day, the bird would fly around and speak to all the animals. He liked to give lectures about how to be a good friend. One day, a big, mean cat came to the forest. The cat did not like the bird's lectures. He wanted to catch the bird and stop him from speaking. The cat would hide and wait for the bird to come near. The little bird was scared, but he knew he had to keep speaking to help his friends. So, he thought of a plan. He told all his friends to work together and scare the mean cat away. They made loud noises and chased the cat out of the forest. The bird was happy that he could keep giving lectures and speaking to his friends. The mean cat never came back, and they all lived happily ever after.
