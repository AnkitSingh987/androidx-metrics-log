Category: Beginner
Title: Zoom the Red Car and the Hill

Once upon a time, there was a little red car named Zoom. Zoom was very fast and always impatient. He did not like to wait for anything. One day, Zoom was playing with his friends when he saw a big hill. He knew he needed to go up the hill to see what was on the other side. Zoom went very fast up the hill, but he was so impatient that he did not look where he was going. At the top of the hill, Zoom found a big, deep hole. Because he did not look, he fell into the hole. Zoom needed help to get out. His friends heard him and came to help. They worked together and got Zoom out of the hole. Zoom learned that sometimes it is good to slow down and not be so impatient.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was only three years old and liked to explore the world. One day, her parents took her to an ancient gym. She was so excited to see what it was like! Inside, Lily saw that the gym was filled with lots of different things to do. There was a trampoline, a slide and even a game of hopscotch. Lily ran around exploring everything. As she was examining one of the walls, Lily saw some old pictures. She asked her dad, "What are those?" Her dad smiled and said, "Those are pictures of ancient people folding their hands together. It looks like this gym used to be something very special!" Lily thought about how special it must have been. She was so happy to explore this ancient gym and learn about how people folded their hands together. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a king. He lived in a big, pretty house. The king had a perfect crown on his head. He liked to talk to all the people in his land. One day, the king went for a walk. He saw a little girl. She was sad. The king talked to her. "Why are you sad?" he asked. The little girl said, "I lost my toy." The king wanted to help her. The king and the little girl looked for the toy. They looked under a tree. They looked by a big rock. They found the toy near a pond. The little girl was happy. She thanked the king for his help. They talked and laughed all the way back to the big, pretty house.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a big, clean room with lots of toys. Amy loved to play with her toys all day. One day, she found a new toy in her room. It was a funny-looking toy with a big button on it. Amy pushed the big button on the toy. Suddenly, the toy started to dance and sing. Amy was very happy and clapped her hands. She called her toy Mr. Button. Mr. Button loved to make Amy happy. He danced and sang for her every day. One day, Amy's friends came to play with her. They saw Mr. Button and wanted to play with him too. Amy pushed the big button, and Mr. Button started to dance and sing again. All the kids clapped and laughed. They played with Mr. Button all day, and everyone had so much fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big purple dog. He liked to play in the park. One day, he found a suit. The suit was very pretty. The purple dog wanted to keep the suit. The purple dog thought, "I will bury the suit in the ground. Then, no one else can take it." So, the purple dog dug a hole and put the suit in it. He covered the hole with dirt. But, he did not know that the suit belonged to a small cat. The small cat was sad. She could not find her suit. She asked her friends for help. The purple dog heard the small cat. He felt bad. The purple dog went to the hole and dug up the suit. He gave it back to the small cat. The small cat was very happy. She thanked the purple dog. They became good friends. The purple dog learned that it is not nice to take things that do not belong to you. Sharing and being kind is better.
