Category: Beginner
Title: The Girl Called Amy and the Hello

Once upon a time, there was a girl called Amy. She was very sad. Each day she would walk around, looking at the ground and not talking much. One day, when she was walking, a friendly man said hello. He was ready with a big smile. Amy felt a little bit happy. She made a tiny wave and looked up for a second. Suddenly, she realized that the man was very nice. She smiled back and said hello. The man said it was very nice to see her smile and waved before walking away. Amy felt a little bit better. From that day onwards, she would smile and wave at anyone who said hello. She even started to say hello to others first with a big smile on her face. Amy was no longer sad - happiness had found its way into her heart.

---

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big spoon. He liked to play with it. Tim would stand and pretend the spoon was his friend. He named the spoon Mr. Spoon. Tim and Mr. Spoon would play all day. They would play in the sand and eat with the spoon. One day, Tim asked Mr. Spoon, "Are you an honest spoon?" Mr. Spoon did not say anything because spoons can't talk. Then, something unexpected happened. Mr. Spoon began to talk! He said, "Yes, Tim, I am an honest spoon. I will always be your friend." Tim was very happy and they played together every day.

---

Tom and Lily are twins. They like to play together. But today they have to go to the barber. They do not like the barber. The barber has a big scissors and a loud machine. The barber makes their hair short and neat. "Tom, you go first," the barber says. He puts a cape around Tom's neck. He picks up the scissors. Tom feels scared. He does not want a haircut. "No, Lily goes first," Tom says. He points at his sister. Lily shakes her head. She does not want a haircut either. "No, Tom goes first," Lily says. She hides behind their mom. Mom smiles. She knows they are nervous. "Come on, kids, it won't hurt," Mom says. She holds Tom's hand. She leads him to the chair. Tom sits down. He closes his eyes. He hopes it will be over soon. The barber cuts Tom's hair. He makes it short and neat. He uses the loud machine to trim the edges. He sprays some water on Tom's head. He combs Tom's hair. He polishes Tom's shoes. He gives Tom a lollipop. "See, that wasn't so bad, was it?" the barber says. He shows Tom a mirror. Tom looks at himself. He sees his haircut. He likes it. He smiles. "Thank you, barber," Tom says. He hops off the chair. He gives the lollipop to Lily. He wants to share with her. "Your turn, Lily," the barber says. He puts a cape around Lily's neck. He picks up the scissors. Lily feels brave. She wants a haircut now. "Okay, barber, make me pretty," Lily says. She opens her eyes. She watches the barber cut her hair. He makes it short and neat. He uses the loud machine to trim the edges. He sprays some water on Lily's head. He combs Lily's hair. He polishes Lily's shoes. He gives Lily a lollipop. "You look lovely, Lily," the barber says. He shows Lily a mirror. Lily looks at herself. She sees her haircut. She likes it. She smiles. "Thank you, barber," Lily says. She hops off the chair. She gives the lollipop to Tom. She wants to share with him. Mom pays the barber. She hugs Tom and Lily. She is proud of them. They have been good and brave. "Let's go home, kids," Mom says. She takes their hands. They walk out of the barber shop. They pass by a trash can. It is full of old food and dirty diapers. It is smelly. "Yuck, what is that?" Tom says. He covers his nose. "That is a trash can," Lily says. She covers her nose too. "It is smelly," Tom says. "It is very smelly," Lily says. They laugh. They run away from the trash can. They are happy. They have new haircuts and lollipops. They are twins. They like to play together.
