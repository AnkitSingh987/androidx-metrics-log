Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Mountain

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk. She saw a big mountain. Mia wanted to climb the mountain. She put on her shoes and started her walk up the mountain. As Mia walked, she met a sweet bunny. The bunny said, "Hi! My name is Bobo. What's your name?" Mia said, "Hi, Bobo! My name is Mia. I am going to the top of the mountain." Bobo said, "I will go with you!" Mia and Bobo walked and walked. They saw many fun things on the mountain. They saw pretty flowers and big trees. They played and laughed. At last, Mia and Bobo reached the top of the mountain. They looked around and saw a beautiful view. They were so happy. Mia and Bobo walked back down the mountain. They were very tired, but they had a great day. They said goodbye and went home. Mia and Bobo would always remember their fun day on the mountain.

Once upon a time, there was a mule named Max. Max was very foolish. He liked to play and jump all day. Max lived in a big farm with many friends. One day, Max saw a big tree. He wanted to clap his hooves on the tree. Max ran to the tree and tried to clap. But his hooves were too big and he could not clap well. Max's friends saw him and laughed. They said, "Max, mules don't clap!" Max felt sad. But his friends helped him. They showed Max how to kick the tree instead. Max was happy and played with his friends all day.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk in the woods. He noticed a big cave. Tim was very brave and wanted to see what was inside. He walked closer to the cave, but he did not know that something was hiding in there. As Tim walked inside the cave, he saw a big table with a lot of delicious food. He was very hungry, so he started to eat the food. But he did not know that the food was not for him. The food was for someone else, and that someone was watching him. The big bear who lived in the cave came out from behind a rock. He saw Tim eating his food and he was very angry. The bear roared and scared Tim. Tim tried to run away, but he was too slow. The bear caught him and Tim could not go home. The boy should have known that the cave was not a safe place to play.

Once upon a time, there was a little yellow bird named Bob. Bob loved to give things to his friends. One day, he found a big, round pizza. It smelled really good! Bob wanted to share the pizza with his friends. He went to find his friend, a big, green frog named Sam. When Sam saw the pizza, his eyes got big and he smiled. Bob gave Sam a piece of the pizza, and Sam said, "Yum! Thank you, Bob!" After Sam ate the pizza, they went to find more friends to share with. They found a small, red cat named Lily. Bob gave Lily a piece of the pizza too. Lily was very happy and said, "This is the best pizza ever!" All the friends laughed and played together, feeling happy that they could share the yummy pizza.

Once upon a time, there was a big yacht. It was very pretty. A happy girl named Sally lived near the water. She loved to look at the yacht every day. Sally always wanted to spend time on the yacht. One sunny day, Sally saw a nice man near the yacht. He asked, "Do you want to spend time on my yacht?" Sally was very glad. She said, "Yes, please!" She knew that today was a special day. Sally and the nice man went on the yacht. They had a fun day. They played with toys and ate yummy food. The sun was going down. Sally said, "Thank you for the fun day!" The nice man smiled and said, "You're welcome!" Sally went home very glad and knew she would always remember her special day on the yacht.
