Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Flower

Once upon a time, in a lovely little home, there lived a girl named Mia. Mia loved to gather pretty flowers from her garden. She had a dream to make the most beautiful flower crown ever. One day, Mia saw a big, pretty flower in her neighbor's garden. She thought, "I want that flower for my crown!" So, she went to her neighbor and asked, "Can I have that flower, please?" Her neighbor said, "No, Mia. That flower is mine." Mia was sad but she decided to take the flower anyway. She picked the flower and put it in her crown. But, oh no! The flower was magic, and it made her crown very heavy. Mia's head hurt and she could not wear her lovely crown anymore. The moral of the story is, we should not take things that do not belong to us.

Once upon a time, there was a little bulb who lived in a big garden. The little bulb was very important because it would grow into a beautiful flower one day. All the other plants in the garden loved the little bulb. One cold day, a tiny bird came into the garden. The bird was shivering because it was so cold. The little bulb wanted to help the bird feel warm. It told the bird to sit on the ground near it. Slowly, the little bulb started to grow. It grew and grew until it became a big, pretty flower. The flower was so big that it could cover the tiny bird. The bird stopped shivering and felt warm under the flower. The bird was happy and thanked the important little bulb for helping it. They became good friends and lived happily in the garden.

Sara was very happy. She had a loose tooth. She wiggled it with her tongue. She wanted it to fall out. She knew that the tooth fairy would come and give her a coin. She went to the park with her mom and dad. She saw her friend Ben. He was playing with a ball. He smiled at her and said, "Hi, Sara. Do you want to play?" Sara nodded and ran to him. They passed the ball to each other. They had fun. But then, Sara felt something in her mouth. She spit it out. It was her tooth! She picked it up and showed it to Ben. "Look, Ben. My tooth came out!" she said. Ben looked at her tooth. It was small and white. He said, "Wow, Sara. That's great. You can put it under your pillow tonight. The tooth fairy will come and give you a coin." Sara was very excited. She thanked Ben and said, "Bye, Ben. I have to go home and show my mom and dad." She ran to her mom and dad. They were sitting on a bench. She showed them her tooth. They hugged her and said, "We're so proud of you, Sara. You're a big girl now." They went home. Sara put her tooth in a pink envelope. She wrote her name on it. She put it under her pillow. She brushed her teeth and went to bed. She dreamed of the tooth fairy. The next morning, she woke up and looked under her pillow. She found a shiny coin. She was very happy. She ran to her mom and dad and showed them her coin. They smiled and said, "The tooth fairy came, Sara. She liked your tooth. She left you a coin." Sara said, "Thank you, tooth fairy. Thank you, mom and dad. I love you." She gave them a big hug and a kiss. She put her coin in her piggy bank. She wondered when her next tooth would fall out.

One day, a boy named Mark went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy yogurt for a snack. Mark liked yogurt a lot. It was yummy and cold. His favorite was the one with fruit at the bottom. As they walked through the store, Mark saw a big dog. The dog was very loud. It barked and barked. Mark was a little scared. He held his mom's hand tight. "Mom, why is the dog so loud?" Mark asked. His mom smiled and said, "The dog is just saying hello. He is happy to see us." Mark felt better. They bought the yogurt and went home. Mark was happy to eat his yogurt and tell his dad about the loud dog.
