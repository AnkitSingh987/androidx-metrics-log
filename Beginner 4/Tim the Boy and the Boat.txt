Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Boat

One day, a boy named Tim and his mom went to a museum. In the museum, they saw a big boat. Tim said, "Mom, can we sail on the boat?" His mom said, "No, Tim, the boat is just for looking." But Tim really wanted to sail. So when his mom was not looking, he got on the boat. He started to pretend he was sailing fast. He said, "Look at me, I am sailing fast like a big bird!" Just then, the boat started to move! Tim was scared, but he kept sailing. The boat went out of the museum and into a big lake. Tim's mom saw him and said, "Tim, come back!" But the boat was going too fast. A nice duck saw Tim and said, "I can help you!" The duck swam fast and pushed the boat back to the museum. Tim's mom was happy to see him safe. Tim said, "Thank you, duck!" From that day, Tim and the duck were best friends.

One day, a nice girl named Lucy went to play in the park. She saw a shiny thing on the ground. It was pretty jewelry. Lucy picked it up and looked at it. It was a necklace with a big heart. Lucy saw an old lady sitting on a bench. The old lady looked sad. Lucy went to her and said, "Is this your jewelry?" The old lady looked at the necklace and smiled. "Yes, my dear, it is. I lost it while walking here." The old lady took the necklace and put it around her neck. She said, "Thank you, Lucy. You are a very nice girl." Lucy smiled and said, "You're welcome. I'm happy to help." They both felt happy and played together in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a graceful bird named Ella. Ella lived in a tree near the sea. She loved to fly high in the sky and play with her friends. One day, a big hurricane came. It made the wind blow very hard. Ella tried to fly, but the wind was too strong. She was scared and hid in her tree. But then, something unexpected happened. Ella saw a little boy crying near the sea. He was all wet and needed help. Ella wiped her own tears and flew down to the boy. She helped him wipe his tears too. The boy smiled and hugged Ella. They became best friends. The hurricane went away, and they played together in the sun.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park. She wore her favorite sweater. It was a sunny day, but she loved her sweater so much. In the park, she saw a big boy named Tom. Tom looked troubled. "Tom, why are you sad?" Mia asked. Tom said, "I lost my toy ball. I can't find it." Mia wanted to help Tom. They started to look for the toy ball together. As they looked, Mia saw something under a bush. She reached in and felt a squeeze. "Ouch!" she cried. She pulled out her hand and saw a tiny kitten. The kitten was scared and had squeezed Mia's hand. Mia and Tom forgot about the toy ball. They decided to help the kitten. They took the kitten home and gave it some food. Tom was not troubled anymore. He was happy to have a new friend. Mia was happy too. They both loved the little kitten.

Sara was a little girl who liked to explore. One day, she found a big, old house in the woods. She wanted to see what was inside. She opened the door and walked in. The house was dark and dusty. Sara saw many cobwebs on the walls and the ceiling. She thought they were pretty and shiny. She wanted to touch them. She reached out her hand and touched a cobweb. But the cobweb was sticky and icky. Sara felt something crawl on her hand. She looked and saw a big, black spider. She was scared and screamed. She tried to shake off the spider and the cobweb, but they stuck to her hand. Sara ran out of the house, crying. She saw her mom waiting for her outside. Her mom saw her hand and hugged her. She helped her get rid of the spider and the cobweb. She cleaned her hand and put a band-aid on it. Sara felt better, but still uncomfortable. She learned a lesson that day. She learned that some things are not as pretty as they seem. She learned that she should not touch things that she does not know. She learned that she should listen to her mom and not go to places that are not safe. She learned to be careful and smart.
