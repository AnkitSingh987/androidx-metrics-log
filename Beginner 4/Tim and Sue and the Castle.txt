Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Sue and the Castle

One sunny day, Tim and Sue went to play at the beach. They liked to play in the sand. They made a big, messy sand castle. It was so much fun! A little crab came to see the castle. The crab said, "Wow, that is a big castle! Can I play with you?" Tim and Sue said, "Yes, you can play with us, but please do not disturb our castle." The crab was happy to play and not disturb the castle. They all played together and had a great day at the beach. The sun was shining, and they laughed a lot. In the end, they said goodbye to the crab and went home. The messy sand castle was still there, not disturbed at all.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim had a big, dull green bottle with him. It was his favorite bottle. He drank water from it all the time. While Tim played, he put his bottle on a bench. He ran and played with other kids. He was having a lot of fun. But then, Tim started to feel thirsty. He went back to the bench, but his bottle was gone. Tim felt sad and said, "Mom, I miss my bottle. Where did it go?" His mom looked around and saw a girl holding a bottle that looked like Tim's. She walked over to the girl and said, "Excuse me, is that your bottle?" The girl looked at the bottle and said, "No, I found it on the bench." Tim's mom thanked the girl and took the bottle back to Tim. Tim was so happy to have his dull green bottle back. He drank water from it and felt much better. He said, "Thank you, Mom, for finding my bottle." They both smiled and continued to have a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who wanted to go on an adventure. She had never been down the road before and wondered what it was like. One day, she decided to go and see for herself. She put on a fancy dress and grabbed a big basket full of food. As she walked down the road, she saw lots of exciting things. She saw a big river, a big tree, and lots of friendly animals. Then, she saw a cottage at the end of the road. She was intrigued and knocked on the door. An old woman opened the door and asked the little girl what she wanted. The girl said she wanted to serve the old woman some of the food in her basket. The old woman was so happy! She thanked the little girl and invited her in for a cup of tea. They chatted, and the old woman told the little girl all about her adventures. The little girl had the most wonderful time. She thanked the old woman, said goodbye, and then started walking down the road again. She was excited to have gone on such a fancy adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a little baby who lived in a crib. The baby had a toy tiger that was fierce. Baby and tiger were best friends. They played together all day long. One day, baby said "please" to the tiger and asked him to play hide and seek. The tiger agreed and started to count. Baby hid behind a big chair. The tiger found baby very fast. They laughed and played more games. Then, baby said "please" again and asked the fierce tiger to play outside. They went to the yard and played near the big tree. Suddenly, it started to rain very hard. Baby and the fierce tiger ran back inside, but they slipped and fell. The baby cried and the tiger was sad. They both got hurt and could not play anymore.

Tom and Anna are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they find a big box. They want to make something with it. "Let's make a house!" Tom says. "OK, how do we make a house?" Anna asks. "We need a door, a window, and a roof," Tom says. They cut a hole in the box for the door. They cut another hole for the window. They find a stick and put it on top of the box for the roof. "Look, we made a house!" Tom says. They go inside the box. They pretend to cook, read, and sleep. They have fun. But then, it starts to rain. The rain makes the box wet and soft. The roof falls down. The door and the window break. "Oh no, our house is broken!" Anna says. They run out of the box. They are wet and sad. "Let's go under the big tree," Tom says. They go under the big tree. The tree keeps them dry. They hug each other. "It's OK, we can make another house another day," Tom says. "Yes, we can. We are good at making things," Anna says. They smile. They are still friends.
