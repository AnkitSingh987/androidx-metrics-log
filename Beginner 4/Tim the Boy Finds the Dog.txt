Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Dog

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw his friend, Sam, and they started to play. They ran and jumped, and Tim's pants had a zip. Tim zipped it up and down as they played. "Let's go eat pizza!" said Sam. They went to the pizza place and saw a crazy dog. The dog was dancing and jumping. Tim and Sam laughed at the dog. "That dog is so funny!" said Tim. They ate their pizza and went back to the park. The crazy dog followed them. Suddenly, the dog took Tim's pants and ran away. Tim was sad and Sam was surprised. They chased the dog and got Tim's pants back. "Thank you, Sam!" said Tim. "We should always help our friends." And they learned that day that helping friends is important and fun.

Once upon a time, there was a powerful prince. He lived in a big castle with his mommy and daddy, the king and queen. The prince liked to play with his toys and his friends all day. One day, the prince found a magic wand. He learned that he could add things to his playtime with the wand. He waved the wand and added more toys, more friends, and even more yummy snacks. The powerful prince was very happy. He played with his new toys and friends all day long. He shared his snacks with everyone too. The prince knew that adding more fun made everyone happy. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty dog named Max. Max lived in a small house with a chimney. He loved to play outside with his ball. But there was one thing Max did not like. He hated when it rained because he could not play outside. One day, it was raining very hard. Max was sad and said to his friend, Cat, "I hate rain. I want to play outside with my ball." Cat thought for a moment and said, "Let's play inside. We can have fun even if it's raining." So Max and Cat played inside near the chimney. They played catch with the ball and had a lot of fun. The rain stopped, and they went outside to play again. Max learned that even when he hated something, like rain, he could still have fun with his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toy car. It was red and had a cord. Lily would pull the cord and the car would go fast. One day, the car broke. Lily was sad. She wanted to restore her car and make it work again. She asked her mom for help. Her mom tried to fix the car, but it was still broken. Lily tried to stay calm, but she was very sad. She knew her car would never work again. She put the broken car in her toy box and went to bed. The next day, Lily still felt sad about her car. The end.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big cardboard box. He thought it was an expensive treasure box. He wanted to see what was inside. He tried to open it, but it was very hard. He felt sad and thirsty. Tim saw a big cup with a drink in it. He took a sip and felt strong. The drink was magic! He went back to the cardboard box and tried to open it again. This time, he opened it with ease. Inside the box, Tim found a beautiful toy car. He was so happy! But his friend, Sam, wanted the car too. They both tried to grab it. Tim thought for a moment and decided to share the toy car with Sam. They played together all day and became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy cat named Tom. Tom liked to be clean and neat. Every day, he would drink a big bowl of milk. Tom loved milk very much. His bowl was always shiny and clean. One sunny day, Tom went to drink his milk. But the milk was gone! Tom was sad and confused. He looked around to see where the milk could be. He saw a small trail of milk on the floor. Tom decided to follow the trail to find his milk. As Tom followed the trail, he saw a tiny mouse. The mouse was also drinking milk. Tom was surprised! He did not know that mice liked milk too. The mouse looked at Tom and smiled. Tom decided to share his milk with the mouse. From that day on, Tom and the mouse became friends, and they always drank milk together.
