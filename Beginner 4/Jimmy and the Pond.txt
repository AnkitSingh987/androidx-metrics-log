Category: Beginner
Title: Jimmy and the Pond

Jimmy had an amazing day at the park. He loved seeing all the animals and he had lots of fun running around in the sunshine. Next he saw the pond. He was so excited to see the ducks swimming in the water. He wanted to get closer, so he rolled up his pants and soaked his feet in the pond. The water was cold and Jimmy smiled as he felt the chill. He splashed around and noticed some fish swimming in the water. They were so colourful! He even saw some little tadpoles! It was amazing. Before leaving he rolled down his pants and gave the pond one last splash. Goodbye pond! It was an amazing day!

Once there was an old man and a young boy. The old man had a cherry tree in his garden and he was fond of cherries. Every time the old man passed the tree, he saw that a cherry had fallen from it. He thought it was very strange. One day, the old man asked the boy, “Have you been naughty? Have you been taking the cherries?” The boy said, “No, I haven’t taken any cherries.” The old man couldn’t understand why the cherries kept disappearing, so he decided to set a trap. The next day, when the old man came to the cherry tree, he saw the boy pinching a cherry. The boy had been naughty and took the cherries! The old man laughed, but the boy felt so bad. He apologized to the old man, who forgave him. The old man showed the boy how to pick cherries gently, and the boy promised to be more careful. He never pinched a cherry again.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play dress up. One day, he found a big box of costumes. He tried on a funny costume with a big hat. He wanted to show his friends his new look. Tim went to the park where his friends were. They were all working on a big sand castle. Tim felt embarrassed, but he wanted to surprise them. He jumped out from behind a tree and yelled, "Boo!" His friends looked up and laughed. They thought Tim's costume was very funny. But then, something unexpected happened. A dog saw Tim's big hat and thought it was a toy. The dog ran up to Tim and grabbed the hat. It pulled the hat off Tim's head and ran away. Tim and his friends chased after the dog, laughing all the way. They had so much fun, they forgot all about working on the sand castle.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to run fast. He was very competitive. One day, Tim went to the park to race with his friends. They wanted to see who was the fastest. Tim and his friends saw a path. They decided to race on the path. Tim had a plan. He would cut through the grass to win the race. As they started to run, Tim ran through the grass to get ahead. But, Tim did not see a big hole in the grass. He fell into the hole! His friends kept running on the path. In the end, Tim did not win the race. He learned that taking shortcuts is not always the best way to win.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to reach for a big, red ball on a high shelf. He tried and tried, but he could not get it. He saw a tall, white guard standing near the shelf. Tim thought the guard could help him get the ball. "Mr. Guard, can you help me reach the red ball?" asked Tim. The guard looked down at Tim and said, "No, I cannot help you. It is not safe for you to play with that ball." Tim was sad and angry. He wanted the red ball so much. So, he thought of a plan. When the guard was not looking, Tim found a big box. He stood on the box and reached for the ball. But, the box was not strong. It broke and Tim fell down. The red ball fell too and hit Tim on the head. Tim cried and the guard came running. The guard said, "I told you not to try to reach the ball. Now you are hurt." Tim was sad and hurt, and he learned that he should listen to the guard next time.
