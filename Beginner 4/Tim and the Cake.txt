Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Cake

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim had a big board. He liked to draw on the board. One day, his mom asked him to help her. She needed to deliver a box to their friend, Sue. Tim said, "Mom, can I bring my board? I want to show Sue my drawings." His mom said, "Yes, that's an easy thing to carry with you." When they got to Sue's house, they gave her the box. Sue opened it and found a big cake inside! But then, something unexpected happened. Tim tripped and his board fell on the cake! "Oh no!" said Tim. "I'm sorry, Sue." Sue laughed and said, "That's okay, Tim. Now we have a cake with your drawing on it! Let's eat and enjoy your art together." And they all laughed and had a fun time.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to go outside and play in the sun. He loved the warm heat on his skin. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom and dad. At the park, Tim saw a big, red ball. He wanted to play with it. Tim ran to the ball and kicked it very hard. But, it hurt his foot. "Ouch!" Tim cried. His mom and dad came to help him. They took Tim to a nice, cool place to sit down. They put some ice on his foot to make it feel better. The hurt went away soon. Tim learned to be more careful when he played. He still loved the heat, but he knew not to go too fast or kick too hard.

Tim was a gifted boy. He could do many things with his computer. He could play games, draw pictures, and learn new words. He loved his computer very much. He wanted to show his computer to his friends. One day, he brought his computer to school. He put it on his desk and turned it on. He was very proud of his computer. He wanted his friends to see how smart he was. He said, "Look at my computer. It is very good. I can do many things with it. Do you want to try?" Some of his friends were curious. They came to his desk and looked at his computer. They said, "Wow, your computer is very nice. Can we play with it?" Tim said, "Yes, you can. But be careful. Do not break it. It is very special to me." But some of his friends were not nice. They were jealous of Tim and his computer. They did not like that he was gifted. They wanted to make him sad. They said, "Your computer is not good. It is boring. We do not want to play with it. We want to play with something else." They pushed his computer off his desk and kicked it. They laughed and ran away. Tim was very sad. He saw his computer on the floor. It was broken. It did not work anymore. He cried and cried. He did not understand why his friends did that. He did not understand why they were so mean. He did not understand why they did not like him. He lost his computer and his friends. He felt very lonely. He wished he had never brought his computer to school. He wished he had never shown it to anyone. He wished he was not gifted. He wished he was just like everyone else.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a shiny coin that she loved very much. One day, Lucy saw a big, scary dog on her way to the store. She was afraid, but she knew she had to be brave. Lucy walked up to the dog and said, "Hello, scary dog. I want to be your friend." The dog looked at her and wagged its tail. Lucy felt happy and knew they would be friends. The next day, Lucy and the dog played in the park. Lucy showed the dog her shiny coin. But the dog jumped on Lucy and the coin fell into a puddle. The water spoiled the coin and made it dirty. Lucy was sad, but the dog licked her face and made her smile. They were still friends, even though the coin was spoiled.
