Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Jug

One day, a boy named Tom went to the store with his mom. They wanted to buy a new jug. Tom saw a big, modern jug with a funny shape. He liked it a lot. He asked his mom, "Can we buy this jug, please?" His mom looked at the jug and said, "Yes, we can buy this one." Tom was very happy. They took the jug home and put it on the table. Later, Tom's dad came home. He saw the modern jug and said, "Wow! What a cool jug!" But when he tried to pour water from it, the jug started to talk! The jug said, "Hello! I can answer any question you have!" Tom, his mom, and his dad were very surprised. They did not know the jug could talk. They asked the jug many questions and had fun with their new talking jug friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big, soft mattress in his room. Tim thought his mattress was perfect for jumping on. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. They went into Tim's room and saw the perfect mattress. "Let's jump!" said Tim. Sam and Tim started to jump on the mattress. They were having so much fun. Suddenly, Tim's mom came into the room. She saw the boys jumping on the mattress. Tim's mom started to panic. "Stop jumping!" she said. "The mattress is for sleeping, not for jumping!" Tim and Sam stopped jumping and said sorry to Tim's mom. They learned to play safely and had fun with their toys instead.

Max was three and very eager to go to the airport. He asked his mom why they were going there and she told him they were going to see a jet. She said it was really big and fast, and would fly really high in the sky. Max was so excited to see the jet. When they got to the airport, Max was amazed by how many different types of airplanes were there. He asked his mom, “What kind of jet is it?” She said it was a long jet, which would include lots of passengers on their journey. Max was a bit scared at first but he was also excited. He asked his mom if he could ride the long jet. She said, “Maybe some day, but not today.” He was disappointed but curious to see what it looked like. They went to the big glass window so Max could get a good view of the long jet. He was in awe at how big it was. He pointed his finger excitedly at the jet and said, “I want to fly in that!” His mom smiled at him and said, “Some day, you will!”

Once upon a time, in a small cheerful town, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to imagine things. He would pretend to be a big lion or a fast car. One day, Tim found a ticket on the ground. Tim showed the ticket to his mom. "Look, Mom! I found a ticket!" he said. "Wow, Tim! That's a special ticket for a fun park. Let's go there tomorrow," his mom replied. The next day, Tim and his mom went to the fun park. They saw a big red slide and a blue swing. Tim imagined he was a bird flying high in the sky as he swung back and forth. They had a great day, and Tim was so happy he found the ticket.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Amy. She had a pretty bow in her hair. One day, she went for a walk and saw a sad dog. The dog looked troubled. Amy wanted to help the dog. Amy asked the dog, "Why are you troubled, dog?" The dog said, "I can't find my way home. Can you help me, please?" Amy wanted to be kind, so she said, "Yes, I will help you find your home." They walked and walked, but they could not find the dog's home. Then, something unexpected happened. The dog took Amy's bow and ran away. Amy was very sad. She had lost her pretty bow and had not helped the dog. The moral of the story is: Be careful who you trust, even if they seem sad or troubled.
