Category: Beginner
Title: The Mystery of the Cat

One day, a humble cat and a big dog were playing near a big rocket. The cat said, "I want to go up and see the sky." The dog said, "No, it is too high. We can get hurt." The cat did not listen to the dog. He went to the rocket and tried to open the door. The dog was scared and tried to stop the cat. The cat did not want to listen, so the dog gave the cat a little slap. The cat was sad and cried. The dog said, "I am sorry, but it is not safe." The cat understood and they played near the rocket, but did not go inside. They had fun and stayed safe. The cat was happy that the dog cared for him.

Once upon a time, there was a proud white cat. The cat could speak like people. She lived in a big house with a little girl. The little girl loved the cat very much. They played together every day. One day, a big dog came to the house. The dog was loud and wanted to play with the cat. The cat was scared of the dog. The cat did not want to speak to the dog. The dog felt sad because the cat would not speak to him. The little girl saw the cat and the dog. She wanted them to be friends. She said, "Please, speak to the dog, cat." The cat looked at the little girl and decided to try. The cat spoke to the dog. They talked and played together. The cat, the dog, and the little girl were all happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had long hair that she liked to comb every day. She had a special comb that her mom gave her. Mia was always obedient and listened to her mom. One day, Mia was playing with her toys when she felt sleepy. Her mom said, "Mia, it's time for your nap." Mia put her toys away and went to her room. She took her comb with her and combed her hair before her nap. Mia had a nice nap and woke up with a big smile. She took her comb and combed her hair again. Her mom was happy to see that Mia was obedient and took her nap. They spent the rest of the day playing and having fun together.

One day, a boy named Tim found a screw on the floor. He picked it up and looked around. He saw an empty box and had an idea. Tim wanted to act like a builder and use the screw to fix the box. Tim took the screw and tried to put it in the box. But the screw was too big! He pushed and pushed, but it would not go in. Tim felt sad and didn't know what to do. Just then, Tim's big sister came into the room. She saw Tim was upset and asked what was wrong. Tim showed her the screw and the empty box. His sister smiled and said, "Don't worry, I can help you!" She found a smaller screw and they fixed the box together. Tim felt happy and thanked his sister. They played with the box all day, acting like builders.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a toy mask that she loved to play with. One day, she wanted to show her friends how she could balance the mask on her head. Lily was very impatient to show her friends. She ran to the park where they were playing. When she got there, she put the mask on her head and started to balance it. She was so excited that she started to dance. Her friends clapped and laughed as they watched Lily balance the mask on her head. They all took turns trying to balance the mask too. They had a lot of fun together, and Lily was very happy that she could share her special trick with her friends.

One day, a little boy named Mark went outside to play. The sky was gray and wet, but Mark did not care. He put on his boots and ran out to jump in the puddles. He was so happy to play in the wet. But then, something unexpected happened. Mark saw a tiny, miserable kitten sitting by a tree. The kitten was wet and cold. Mark wanted to help the kitten. He took off his coat and put it around the kitten to keep it warm. Mark and the kitten went back inside his house. They became best friends and played together every day. The kitten was not miserable anymore. Mark was happy that he found the kitten, and they both loved to play in the wet, even on the gray days.
