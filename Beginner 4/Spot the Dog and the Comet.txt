Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog and the Comet

One day, a little dog named Spot felt restless. He wanted to go outside and play. Spot went to his friend, a big tree, and said, "Tree, I feel shaky and want to play. Will you play with me?" The tree said, "Spot, I cannot play. I am a tree. But look up at the sky. Do you see the bright thing? That is a comet. It is a special thing to see." Spot looked up and saw the comet. It was very pretty. But then, something unexpected happened. The comet came closer and closer to the ground. Spot began to tremble because he was scared. The comet landed near Spot and the tree. A small, friendly alien came out of the comet. The alien said, "Hello, Spot and Tree. I am here to play with you." Spot, the tree, and the alien played all day. Spot was not restless or trembling anymore. He was very happy to have a new friend from the comet.

There was a boy who liked gold very much. He wanted to have all the gold in the world. He did not like to share or play with other children. He only liked to look at his gold and count it. One day, he found a big gold coin under his bed. He was very happy and put it in his pocket. He went outside to find more gold. He saw a girl who had a gold necklace. He wanted to take it from her. He ran to her and tried to grab it. But the girl was not normal. She was a fairy. She saw the boy and knew he was greedy. She did not like greedy people. She waved her hand and said a magic word. The boy felt very sleepy. He fell to the ground and closed his eyes. The fairy took the gold coin from his pocket and put it back under his bed. She also took the gold necklace and flew away. She said to herself, "Gold is not for greedy people. Gold is for good people who share and care." The boy slept for a long time. When he woke up, he did not remember what happened. He went back to his room and saw the gold coin under his bed. He thought he had dreamed it. He picked it up and looked at it. He felt bored and lonely. He wished he had friends to play with. He learned a lesson. He decided to be nice and share his gold with other children. He went outside and looked for someone to play with. He saw the girl who was a fairy. She smiled at him and gave him back the gold necklace. She said, "This is for you. You can keep it if you are good. But you have to share it with others. Do you want to be my friend?" The boy nodded and smiled. He felt happy and grateful. He said, "Yes, I want to be your friend. Thank you for the gold necklace. I will share it with others. I am sorry for being greedy. I learned my lesson. Gold is not everything. Friends are more important." The end.

Once upon a time, there were two sweet twins named Tim and Tom. They liked to play, laugh, and share. They lived in a small house with their mom and dad. The twins had many friends and they all played together. One day, Tim and Tom went to the park. They saw their friend, Sam, by the swings. Sam said, "Come play with me!" The twins said, "Yes!" and they all played on the swings. They went high up in the sky and laughed. After playing on the swings, the twins and Sam saw an ice cream truck. They ran to the truck and each got a sweet ice cream cone. Tim and Tom said, "Thank you!" to the ice cream man. They ate their ice cream and smiled. It was a fun day for the sweet twins and their friend Sam.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Jo. She loved to play outside and rub her hands on things. One day, Jo was playing in the garden when it started to get very cloudy. Jo looked up at the sky and thought it was interesting. "Mommy, what can I do if the weather is heavy?" she asked. "You can come inside, or rub your hands together," Mommy replied. So Jo decided to rub her hands together to keep warm. She rubbed them together for several minutes, and soon the weather started to clear up. Jo was very happy that she could go outside and play again. The end.
