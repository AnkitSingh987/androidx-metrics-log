Category: Beginner
Title: The Bull and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a big bull. The bull was very strong and had a loud ring. The bull lived on a farm with many animals. One day, an adorable little cat came to the farm. The cat was very small and had a soft fur. The bull and the cat became friends. They played together in the sun. The bull liked to ring his bell, and the cat liked to jump and run. The other animals on the farm liked to watch them play. They were all happy. But one day, the bull rang his bell too loud. The loud ring scared the adorable cat. The cat ran away very fast. The bull tried to find the cat, but he could not. The bull was very sad, and the other animals missed the cat too. The farm was not happy anymore.

Once upon a time, in a big orange tree, there was a little squirrel. The squirrel loved to find nuts. Every day, he would look for nuts on the ground and in the tree. He was very good at finding nuts. One day, the squirrel could not find any nuts. He looked under the leaves and in the grass, but there were no nuts. The squirrel was sad and hungry. He did not know what to do. So, he decided to pray. He closed his eyes and prayed for nuts. After he prayed, the squirrel opened his eyes and saw a big nut on the ground. He was very happy and thanked the sky for the nut. He took the nut to his home in the orange tree and ate it. From that day on, the squirrel always prayed when he needed help. And he always found the nuts he needed.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park. She wore her favorite yellow shirt. At the park, she saw a boy named Sam. Sam was sad and began to cry. "Why are you crying?" Lucy asked Sam. Sam said, "I lost my toy car. It is my favorite." Lucy wanted to help Sam. They looked for the toy car together. As they searched, a dog came running up to them. The dog had a yellow shirt in its mouth. It was Lucy's shirt! The dog had taken it off the bench where she left it. The dog dropped the shirt and out rolled Sam's toy car. Sam and Lucy laughed and played together, happy to have found both the shirt and the toy car.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim was eager to play. He put on his shoes and went to the big field near his house. The field was full of green grass and pretty flowers. Tim liked to play there. In the field, Tim saw a small dog. The dog had a wagging tail and a happy face. Tim asked the dog, "What is your name?" The dog barked and jumped around. Tim thought the dog's name was Barky. Tim and Barky played in the field all day long. They ran and laughed together. They were very happy. At the end of the day, Tim said, "Bye, Barky! See you tomorrow!" Barky barked and wagged his tail. They couldn't wait to play again.

One day, a big ball was on top of a stair. The ball was troubled because it was scared to roll down. It did not want to fall and get hurt. It was a very big stair and the ball was very high up. A small ball came and saw the big ball. The small ball said, "Why are you troubled, big ball? Let's play and roll down the stair together!" The big ball was still scared, but it wanted to play with the small ball. As they started to roll down the stair, something unexpected happened. The big ball was not hurt at all! It was having fun with the small ball. They rolled and laughed all the way down the stair. At the end, the big ball was not troubled anymore and they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big bubble. The bubble was very fine and pretty. It lived in a world with many other bubbles. All the bubbles were friends. One day, a wise bubble said, "Let's unite and be one big bubble!" All the bubbles agreed and said, "Yes, let's do it!" So, they all came together and became one big, fine bubble. But then, something unexpected happened. A little girl saw the big bubble and thought it was pretty. She reached out to touch it, and POP! The big bubble burst into many little bubbles again. The little bubbles laughed and said, "That was fun! Let's do it again!" And they all lived happily ever after.
