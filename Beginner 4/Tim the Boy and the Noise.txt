Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Noise

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy named Mr. Bear. They loved to play together. One day, Tim felt very tired. He let out a big yawn. "I want to sleep," Tim said. Mr. Bear had an idea. "Let's find the switch to turn off the light," he said. Tim and Mr. Bear looked around the room. They saw the switch on the wall. Tim reached up and turned off the light. The room became dark. In the dark, Tim heard a noise. It was a terrible noise. Tim felt scared. "What is that noise?" he asked. Mr. Bear hugged Tim and said, "Don't worry, it's just the wind outside." Tim felt better and went to sleep with Mr. Bear by his side.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a small pocket on her dress. Inside the pocket, she kept her favorite toy, a tiny cat. One day, Sue went to the park with her mom. While they were playing, Sue whispered to her toy cat, "You are my best friend." The tiny cat liked Sue too because she was obedient and always listened to her mom. When it was time to go home, Sue put her toy cat back in her pocket. They walked home together, holding hands with mom. Sue was happy to have her toy cat with her, and the tiny cat was happy to have Sue as a friend. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a tire, rolling down a hill. It was a very hard tire, and it was going very fast. Along the way it bumped into a tree, a big rock, and a log. But it kept going. Down the hill it went until it ran into a little boy who was taking a walk. When he saw the tire, he said, "Whoa! What is that thing? Can it hurt me?" The tire stopped and replied, "I will not hurt you. I'm just a tire, rolling down the hill! Please let me continue!" The little boy thought for a moment. Then he said, "I understand. But I am scared. Can you please surrender where you are now? That way you will stay safe and I won't be scared." So the tire surrendered, and it stayed right there. The little boy felt happy, and the tire was safe too. And they both went on their way.

Once upon a time, there was an incredible octopus named Ollie. Ollie lived in the big blue sea with his friends. They liked to play and swim all day long. Ollie had many long arms that could wrap around things. One day, a big mean shark came to their home. The shark wanted to eat Ollie and his friends. They were scared and did not know what to do. Ollie had an idea to use his long arms to help. Ollie swam fast to the shark and used his long arms to wrap around the shark's mouth. The shark could not eat anyone now. Ollie's friends cheered and clapped for him. The shark swam away, and they were safe again. Ollie the incredible octopus was a hero to his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little kitten. This kitten was very stubborn. It never wanted to do what its mom said. One day, the mom told the kitten not to go near the big tree. The kitten didn't listen. The kitten went to the big tree and started to climb. It went up and up, but then it got stuck. The kitten was scared and didn't know what to do. It started to scream for help. The mom heard the scream and came running. The mom helped the kitten get down from the tree. The kitten was so happy to be safe. It learned that it should listen to its mom. The kitten knew that mom knows best and will always help. From that day on, the kitten was not stubborn anymore.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a lazy cat named Tom. Tom liked to sleep all day under a big tree. One day, a little bird named Billy came to the tree. Billy saw Tom and said, "Why are you so lazy? You should play with me!" Tom did not want to play, but he did not want to disturb Billy. So, Tom said, "Okay, I will play with you." They played together, running and jumping in the forest. But Tom was not happy. He missed his lazy sleep under the tree. One day, while they were playing, they saw a big motion in the bushes. It was a big, scary wolf! The wolf saw them and said, "I am going to eat you!" Tom and Billy were very scared. They tried to run away, but the wolf was faster. The wolf caught Tom and ate him. Billy was sad and scared. He flew back to the tree and said, "I wish I did not disturb Tom." From that day on, Billy never disturbed anyone again.
