Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a pale house, there lived a little girl named Lucy. Lucy had a secret toy. It was a spray bottle. She loved to play with it all day. One day, Lucy went outside with her spray bottle. She saw a cat. Lucy thought the cat was hot, so she sprayed it with water. The cat jumped and ran away. Lucy laughed and kept playing. Later, Lucy found a big tree. She sprayed the tree and the leaves. Suddenly, a bird flew out of the tree. The bird was happy because the water felt nice. Lucy's secret spray bottle made her and her new friends very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little bug. This bug was not like other bugs. He was ugly. Other bugs did not want to play with him. They would separate from him when he came near. This made the ugly bug very sad. One day, the ugly bug saw a big rock. All the bugs were playing on it. The ugly bug wanted to play too. But when he got close, the other bugs stopped playing. They separated from him again. The ugly bug felt more sad. Then, a nice bug came to the ugly bug. She said, "I will play with you." The ugly bug was so happy! They played together on the big rock. The other bugs saw them playing and having fun. They decided to join them too. From that day on, the bugs did not separate from the ugly bug anymore. They all played together and became good friends.

One day, little Anne's mom and dad decided to go camping in the forest. When they arrived, Anne was surprised to see a big tent already set up! "Let's unpack our things," said Mom, and they all started to take out their camping supplies. Anne found a rope with a big knot on the end. She tried to untie it, but it was too hard. "It's too scary, Daddy! Anne said. "Don't worry, sweetheart," her Dad said. "I'll help you with the knot." Dad untied the knot and then he and Anne put the tent up together. "There!" He said, "We're all done!" Anne smiled. And then she and her parents went out to explore the forest.

Once upon a time, there was a poor dog named Max. Max was very tired and wanted to take a nap. He had a big day ahead of him. Max had to serve food to his friends at his big party. He knew he needed some rest before the fun began. Max found a soft spot under a tree to take his nap. He closed his eyes and began to dream. In his dream, he saw all his friends at the party. They were laughing and eating the food Max served. Max knew his nap was helping him get ready for the big day. When Max woke up from his nap, he felt happy and ready to serve his friends. They all came to the party and had a great time. Max's friends loved the food he served, and they all played games together. Max was glad he took a nap, and his friends were happy too.

Lily and Tom were best friends. They liked to play with dolls and cars and blocks. They always shared their toys and snacks. They made each other happy. One day, Lily had a surprise for Tom. She made him a card with a big heart on it. She wrote "I love you, Tom" with her crayons. She put the card in her backpack and went to school. At school, she saw Tom playing with Anna. Anna had a new doll that could talk and sing. Tom liked the doll very much. He said to Anna, "You are so nice. Can I be your friend?" Lily felt sad. She felt a pain in her chest. She thought Tom did not love her anymore. She thought he loved Anna more. She ran to the bathroom and cried. She took out the card from her backpack and tore it into pieces. She threw the pieces in the trash. She said to herself, "I don't love you, Tom. You broke my heart."
