Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Cake

One day, a little boy named Tim woke up early. He was excited to have fun today. He went to the kitchen and saw his mom. She was making a delicate cake. "Can I help you, Mom?" Tim asked. His mom said, "Yes, Tim. You can help me put the icing on the cake." As they were putting the icing on the cake, they heard a loud noise from the living room. They ran to see what it was. They saw a big, colorful box in the middle of the room. "What's this?" Tim asked, surprised. "I don't know, let's open it!" his mom said. When they opened the box, they found a big, bouncy ball inside. Tim and his mom started to play with the ball. They had so much fun bouncing it all around the house. The cake was forgotten, but it didn't matter. They had a great day filled with laughs and fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. Sally liked to collect pretty rocks. She had a big goal to find the most beautiful rock in the world. One day, Sally went to the park with her friend, Timmy. Timmy was jealous of Sally's rocks. He wanted to find a beautiful rock too. So, they both started to look for rocks together. Sally found a very pretty rock, but Timmy got mad. He wanted that rock too. They both started to fight over the rock. In the end, the rock fell into a deep hole, and they could not get it out. Sally and Timmy were very sad and did not find the most beautiful rock in the world.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was worried because he could not find his toy car. He looked everywhere in his room, but it was not there. Tim decided to ask his mom for help. Tim's mom was in the kitchen with a tray of cookies. She told Tim that she did not know where his toy car was, but maybe it was in the living room. Tim decided to look there. In the living room, Tim found his toy car under the couch. He was so happy! But then, he tripped on the tray of cookies his mom left on the floor. The cookies fell and broke. Tim was sad and worried that his mom would be mad. And she was. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play outside. One day, she saw a glow in the woods. She wanted to find out what it was. So, she walked towards the glow. As Sue got closer, she saw a small box. The box was the treasure! Sue opened the box and found many shiny things inside. She was very happy. Just then, a polite squirrel came by and said, "Hello, Sue! Can I see what's in the box?" Sue smiled and showed the squirrel the treasure. They both looked at the shiny things together. Then, Sue and the squirrel played with the treasure all day long. They had so much fun and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a nice bedroom with a big bed. Lily loved playing in her bedroom all day long. One day, Lily felt very tired after playing all day. She let out a wide yawn. Her mommy saw her yawn and said, "Lily, it's time for a nap." But Lily wanted to keep playing and didn't want to nap. Mommy said, "Lily, taking a nap will give you more energy to play later." So, Lily listened to her mommy and took a nap in her bedroom. When she woke up, she felt very good and had lots of energy to play again. The moral of the story is to listen to your parents, as they know what's best for you.

One day, Tom the Taxi was driving down the road. He saw a little girl standing by the road. She looked helpless. Tom stopped and said, "Hi! I am Tom the Taxi. Can I help you?" The little girl said, "I lost my mom. Can you help me find her?" Tom said, "Yes! Get in and we will find her together." The girl got in and Tom zipped down the road. They looked and looked for the girl's mom. Then, they saw her. She was looking for the girl too. Tom stopped and the girl jumped out. She hugged her mom and said, "Thank you, Tom the Taxi! You helped me." Tom felt happy. He zipped away to help more people who needed a taxi.
