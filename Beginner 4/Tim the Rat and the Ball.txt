Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Rat and the Ball

One day, a little rat named Tim found a big red ball. He wanted to use it to play with his friends. So, Tim took the ball and went to find his friends. When Tim found his friends, they were playing with a small blue ball. Tim showed them the big red ball and said, "Look what I found! Let's use this ball to play." But one of his friends, a rude cat named Max, didn't want to use the big red ball. Max said, "No, I don't want to use that ball. I like our small blue ball." Tim felt sad because Max was being rude. But then, another friend, a kind dog named Lucy, had an idea. Lucy said, "Why don't we use both balls to play? That way, everyone is happy." Tim and Max agreed to use both balls, and they all played together. In the end, they had lots of fun and learned to share with each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to dance. One day, Lucy saw a big dance show. She wanted to join and win a medal. Lucy was a bit scared, but her mom said, "You can do it, Lucy! Just dance and have fun." Lucy felt better and went to the dance show. There were many kids, but Lucy was not scared anymore. At the dance show, Lucy danced with a big smile. Some kids were miserable because they were scared. But Lucy helped them feel happy. In the end, Lucy won a shiny medal. She was so proud and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big park. In the park, there was a dog with big ears. The dog was very hungry. He liked to run and play with kids in the park. One day, a girl came to the park. She had a big bag. The hungry dog with big ears went to the girl. He thought she had food in her bag. The dog wanted to eat, so he tried to open the bag. But, the bag did not have food. It had a cat! The cat jumped out of the bag. The dog was very surprised. The cat and the dog with big ears looked at each other. Then, they became friends. They played together in the park. From that day, they were always happy and not hungry anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to climb trees and play with her friends. One sunny day, she went outside to find something new to play with. Kitty saw a potato on the ground. "Hello, potato!" she said. "Do you want to play with me?" The potato did not say anything, but Kitty took it with her anyway. They climbed a big tree together and had lots of fun. As they were playing, something unexpected happened. The potato began to speak! "Thank you for playing with me, Kitty," said the potato. "I was feeling lonely on the ground." Kitty and the potato became best friends and played together every day.

True is a dog who likes to play outside. He has a ball that he chases and catches. He also likes to dig holes in the dirt and find bones. Sometimes he gets very dirty from playing, but he does not mind. One day, True sees a big truck in the street. The truck is shiny and red and makes a loud noise. True is curious and wants to see the truck up close. He runs across the street, but he does not look for cars. He does not know that the street is dangerous. "True, stop!" a voice shouts. It is True's owner, Sam. Sam is a boy who loves True very much. He sees True running to the truck and he is scared. He runs after True and tries to catch him. But True is too fast and he reaches the truck. He jumps on the truck and starts to lick the shiny red paint. He thinks it tastes good. He also scratches the truck with his paws and makes marks on it. He does not know that the truck belongs to someone else. "Hey, get off my truck!" another voice yells. It is the truck driver, who is angry. He sees True on his truck and he is mad. He gets out of the truck and tries to shoo True away. But True does not want to leave. He thinks the truck is fun. He barks at the truck driver and shows his teeth. He does not know that the truck driver is bigger and stronger than him. "True, come here!" Sam calls. He reaches the truck and grabs True by the collar. He pulls True off the truck and hugs him. He is glad that True is safe. He also says sorry to the truck driver for the trouble. The truck driver looks at his truck and sees the dirt and scratches. He is still angry, but he sees that Sam is sorry and that True is just a dog. He decides to be nice and forgive them. "Next time, keep your dog away from my truck," he says. "And give him a bath. He is very dirty." Sam nods and thanks the truck driver. He takes True home and gives him a bath. True does not like the bath, but he likes Sam. He wags his tail and licks Sam's face. He is happy. He also learns a lesson. He does not run to the street or the truck again. He plays with his ball and his bones instead. He is a good dog.
