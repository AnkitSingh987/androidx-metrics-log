Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Clever Cat and the Stick

One day, a clever cat named Tom found a big red mug. He liked the mug a lot. He wanted to show it to all his friends. But first, he needed to find a way to carry the mug. Tom thought and thought. Tom saw a long, strong stick. He had an idea. He could use the stick to support the mug. So, he put the stick under the mug and held it with his mouth. Now, he could carry the mug to show his friends. Tom walked and walked, showing his friends the big red mug. They all liked it too. The stick helped Tom carry the mug all day long. In the end, Tom was happy. His clever idea worked, and the stick supported the mug just like he thought it would.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She lived near a big, wet lake with her mom and dad. Mia loved to play near the lake and wave at the ducks that swam on the water. One mild day, Mia saw a little duck that needed help. The duck said, "Quack! I can't find my mom!" Mia wanted to help, so she said, "Let's look for your mom together!" They looked and looked, but they could not find the duck's mom. Then, something unexpected happened. A big fish jumped out of the lake and said, "I saw your mom! She is on the other side of the lake!" Mia and the little duck waved thank you to the fish. They walked around the lake and found the duck's mom. The duck's mom said, "Thank you, Mia, for helping my baby!" The moral of the story is to always help others when they need it.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He was a brave boy who loved to play all day. One day, Tom's mom said, "Tom, tonight we will test how brave you are." When night came, Tom's mom told him a story about a big, friendly bear who was also brave. The bear lived in a dark forest but was never scared. Tom listened to the story and said, "I want to be brave like the bear!" Tom's mom took him outside to look at the stars. "See, Tom, the night is not scary," she said. "If the bear can be brave in the dark, so can you." Tom looked up at the stars and smiled. "I am brave like the bear," he said. From that night on, Tom was never scared of the dark again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very sorry because he did not have any money. He wanted to buy a toy car but he could not. One day, while playing outside, it started to rain. Tim was sad and ran home. When he got home, he found a big box in his room. It was a surprise! Inside the box was the toy car he wanted. He was so happy. He played with the toy car all day long. The next day, Tim saw a girl who was sad. She did not have any toys to play with. Tim thought about the toy car and decided to share it with the girl. They played together and became friends. Tim learned that sharing makes everyone happy.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play in the kitchen. Sometimes they help Mom and Dad with the dishes. They put the dirty dishes in the dishwasher. Then they push a button and the dishwasher makes noise and water. One day, Anna and Ben are bored. They see the dishwasher is open. They think it is funny to put things in the dishwasher that are not dishes. They put their toys, their books, their shoes, and even their cat in the dishwasher. They laugh and tease each other. "Look, Ben, the cat is in the dishwasher! He is wet and scared!" Anna says. "Ha ha, Anna, the cat is silly! He does not like the dishwasher!" Ben says. They do not hear Mom coming. Mom sees the mess in the kitchen. She sees the dishwasher is open. She sees the cat in the dishwasher. She is angry and sad. "Anna and Ben, what have you done? This is not a game! You are naughty and mean! You could have hurt the cat and broken the dishwasher! You have to say sorry and clean up!" Mom says. Anna and Ben are sorry. They see the cat is wet and scared. They see the dishwasher is broken. They see Mom is angry and sad. They say sorry to Mom and to the cat. They take out the things from the dishwasher. They dry the cat and hug him. They put the toys, the books, and the shoes back where they belong. They help Mom fix the dishwasher. Mom is happy and proud. She hugs Anna and Ben. She says, "Thank you for saying sorry and cleaning up. I love you. But you have to be careful and kind. You should not tease each other or the cat. You should not put things in the dishwasher that are not dishes. The dishwasher is for dishes only. Do you understand?" Anna and Ben understand. They say, "Yes, Mom, we understand. We are sorry. We will not do it again. We love you too." The end.
