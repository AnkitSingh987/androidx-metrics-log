Category: Beginner
Title: Bobby the Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Bobby. Bobby lived in a tree with his family. One day, Bobby saw a beautiful hanger in a big tree. It was shiny and pretty. He wanted the hanger for his home. Bobby asked his friend, Sally the squirrel, to help him get the hanger. Sally was very good at climbing trees. She was envious of the hanger too. So, they made a plan to get the hanger for both of them to share. Sally climbed the big tree and got the hanger. She gave it to Bobby, who was very happy. Bobby and Sally used the hanger to supply food for their friends. All their friends loved the hanger too, and they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim was eager to play. He put on his shoes and went to the park. In the park, he saw a big hill. He wanted to rush down the hill as fast as he could. As Tim ran down the hill, he found a skull. He picked it up and said, "Wow, what is this?" A voice said, "I am a magic skull. I can talk and help you play!" Tim was very surprised. Tim and the magic skull played all day. They had so much fun. But then, the magic skull said, "I have to go now." Tim was sad but the skull said, "Don't worry, I will be back." And just like that, the skull was gone. Tim knew he would see his new friend again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby little sheep named BaaBaa. BaaBaa loved to play in the green grass all day. He had lots of wool that was soft and fluffy. One sunny day, BaaBaa saw a big hill. He wanted to climb it and see what was on the other side. So, he started to climb the hill, step by step. It was hard work, but BaaBaa kept going. When BaaBaa got to the top of the hill, he saw a beautiful meadow full of flowers. He was so happy that he danced and jumped in his soft wool. BaaBaa played in the meadow all day and then climbed back down the hill to go home.

Once upon a time, in a big field, there was a mighty horse named Max. Max was very strong and fast. He loved to run and play with his friends all day long. One day, Max heard his mom say, "Max, you must stay near the big tree today." Max did not know why he had to stay near the tree, but he listened to his mom. He played by the tree all morning. Soon, he saw dark clouds in the sky. The wind started to blow, and Max felt scared. Then, the rain came down very hard. Max was happy he stayed by the big tree. The tree kept him dry and safe from the rain. When the rain stopped, Max's mom came to him and said, "Good job, Max! You stayed by the tree and stayed safe." Max was proud of himself for listening to his mom.

One day, a queen wanted to ride her big horse. She was a bald queen, but she was very nice. She loved to ride her horse in the sun. All the kids liked to watch her ride. But one day, a mean man came to the town. He did not like the queen. He wanted to stop her from riding her horse. The kids were sad. They did not want the queen to stop riding. The queen tried to ride her horse, but the mean man put a big rock in her way. The queen did not see the rock. Her horse tripped on the rock and fell. The queen was hurt and could not ride her horse anymore. The kids were very sad, and the mean man was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to complete puzzles. One day, she found a bean in her puzzle box. Sue was confused, but she decided to plant the bean in her yard. Sue waited for the bean to grow. She was frustrated because it took a long time. One day, the bean grew into a big beanstalk. Sue was very surprised! She decided to climb the beanstalk to see what was at the top. As Sue reached the top, she found a big, friendly giant. The giant was sad because he lost his bean. Sue gave the bean back to the giant, and he was so happy! The giant helped her complete her puzzle, and they became best friends.
