Category: Beginner
Title: Bow the Thin Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Bow. Bow loved to play with his ball all day. He would run and jump and have lots of fun. One day, Bow found a small nail on the ground. He didn't know what it was, but he thought it looked interesting. Bow tried to play with the nail like he played with his ball, but it wasn't as fun. Bow decided to take the nail to his friend, a big dog named Spot. Spot knew all about nails and told Bow to be careful. Bow thanked Spot and went back to playing with his ball. He was happy to have a friend who could help him learn new things.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Finny. Finny loved to swim all day long. One day, while he was swimming, he heard a loud noise. Finny was scared and wanted to find out what made the loud noise. Finny swam to the place where the loud noise came from. He saw a big stable with many animals. Inside the stable, there was a big horse named Harry. Harry was making the loud noise. Finny asked Harry, "Why are you so loud?" Harry said, "I am sorry, Finny. I was just having fun. I didn't mean to scare you." Finny and Harry became good friends. They played together every day. Finny would swim in the water, and Harry would run in the stable. They were happy and had lots of fun together. And from that day on, Harry tried to not be too loud when he was having fun.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big frame in his room. He was very happy because he could put his favorite picture in it. Tim took the frame to his mom and said, "Mom, can you help me put my picture in the frame?" His mom smiled and said, "Of course, Tim. Let's do it together." They put the picture in the frame and Tim's mom used a zip to keep it safe. Tim was excited to hang it on the wall. But when they put it up, the frame fell down and broke. Tim felt miserable and started to cry. His mom hugged him and said, "Don't worry, we can fix it." Tim's mom found another frame, and they put the picture in it. This time, they made sure it was safe on the wall. Tim was happy again. They looked at the picture and his mom said, "See, Tim? Sometimes things go wrong, but we can always fix them if we try." And so, Tim learned that even when bad things happen, you can always make it right if you don't give up.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very sad and miserable on a day called Wednesday. He didn't want to play or eat. His mom asked him, "Why are you so sad, Tim?" Tim said, "I don't want to touch my toys. They make me sad today." His mom tried to make him happy, but Tim was still sad. Later, Tim went outside to play. He saw a big ball and tried to kick it. But he missed the ball and fell down. He started to cry. His mom came out and said, "Oh no, Tim! I'm sorry you are having a bad day." Tim went back inside, still feeling sad and miserable.

There was a boy named Tim. He had a normal scooter. He liked to ride it every day. One day, he went to the park with his scooter. He saw his friend, Sam, there. "Hi, Sam!" said Tim. "Do you want to ride my scooter?" Sam said, "Yes, please!" So, Tim let Sam ride his scooter. They took turns riding it. They had a lot of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The scooter started to fly! Tim and Sam were very surprised. They did not know what to do. They decided to remain calm. "Wow!" said Tim. "My scooter can fly! Let's ride it together in the sky!" So, Tim and Sam flew on the scooter. It was not a normal scooter anymore. But they had even more fun.
