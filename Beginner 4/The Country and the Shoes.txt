Category: Beginner
Title: The Country and the Shoes

Once upon a time, there was a country. Everyone in the country was bored. They didn't know what to do, so they decided to go get a drink. So the people put on their shoes and got in their cars. They drove to the store to get a drink. When they got there, they saw lots of drinks to choose from. One person chose a juice drink. Another person chose a milk drink. Someone else chose a soda. They were all happy with their drinks. They drove back to the country with their drinks. They drank their drinks and talked about how much fun it was to get a drink. Everyone was much less bored afterward. The end.

Lily and Tom were going to the park with Mom. Mom let them sit in the front seat of the car. They liked to drive with Mom. They saw many things on the road: big trucks, small bikes, red lights, green trees. "Look, Mom, a fruit stand!" Lily said. She pointed to a place where a man was selling apples, oranges, bananas and more. "Can we buy some fruit, please?" "Sure, we can buy some fruit," Mom said. She stopped the car and got out. She gave Lily and Tom some money. "You can choose one fruit each. Be careful and stay close to me." Lily and Tom ran to the fruit stand. They looked at all the different fruits. They smelled them and touched them. Some were smooth, some were rough, some were soft, some were hard. "I want an apple," Lily said. She picked a big red one. It was smooth and shiny. "I want a banana," Tom said. He picked a long yellow one. It was rough and bendy. They gave the man the money and thanked him. They ran back to Mom. They got in the car and buckled up. "Did you enjoy your fruit?" Mom asked. "Yes, we did," Lily and Tom said. They ate their fruit and smiled. They were happy and ready to play at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Tom. Tom had a friend, a small bird named Tim. They liked to play in the big yard. One day, while they were playing, Tom saw a big, red ball. Tom and Tim wanted to play with the ball, but it was too high for them to reach. Tom said, "Tim, let's get the ball!" Tim agreed and they tried to jump and get it. But they couldn't jump high enough. They tried and tried, but they were still an inch too short. They were sad and didn't know what to do. Then, something unexpected happened. The big, red ball started to move on its own! It came down and floated right in front of Tom and Tim. They were so happy and started to play with the ball. But suddenly, the ball vanished! Tom and Tim were surprised, but they didn't give up. They kept looking for the ball and found it hiding behind a tree. They laughed and played with the ball all day long.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park. She saw a pigeon on the ground. The pigeon was very generous. It shared its food with other birds. Lucy had a ball. She wanted to play with the pigeon. So, she threw the ball and it started to bounce. The pigeon saw the ball and started to bounce too. It was so happy. Lucy said to the pigeon, "Do you want to be my friend?" The pigeon nodded its head. From that day, Lucy and the pigeon played together every day at the park. They loved to bounce the ball and share their food with other friends.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside in the sun with her friends. One day, Lily and her friends decided to go to the park. The park had many fun things to do. It had swings, slides, and a big sand area. Lily and her friends played in the sand. They used their hands and toys to build a big sand castle. Their castle was very big and pretty. They were very happy with their work. They also included a moat around the castle to make it look even more special. After they finished their sand castle, Lily and her friends played on the swings and slides. They laughed and had lots of fun. When it was time to go home, they said goodbye to the park and their lovely sand castle. They knew they would come back to play again another day.
