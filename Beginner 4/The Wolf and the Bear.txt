Category: Beginner
Title: The Wolf and the Bear

Once upon a time, in a distant land, there was a little wolf. The little wolf had many friends in the forest. They would play and laugh all day long. But sometimes, the little wolf would worry. He worried that his friends would not like him anymore. One day, the little wolf met a big bear. The big bear was sad. "Why are you sad?" asked the little wolf. "I am sad because I have no friends," said the big bear. The little wolf felt bad for the big bear. The little wolf had an idea. He said, "Come play with me and my friends. We can all be friends together!" The big bear was happy. They played all day and had fun. The little wolf did not worry anymore, because he knew that his friends liked him and the big bear too. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big dog with a ball. The big dog was very dirty. Tim thought the dog was filthy. But he still wanted to play with the dog. Tim said, "Dog, can I play with you?" The dog wagged its tail. Tim requested, "Please give me the ball." The dog dropped the ball at Tim's feet. Tim took a step back and threw the ball far away. The dog ran to get the ball. Suddenly, the filthy dog jumped into a big puddle. The dog came out clean! Tim was surprised. The dog brought the ball back to Tim. They played together all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a jolly man named Tom. He had a big job to do. He had to deliver a box to a faraway house. Tom did not know what was in the box. It was a mystery. Tom walked and walked, and then he saw a little lost cat. It was sad and hungry. Tom wanted to help, so he opened the mystery box. Inside, he found a warm blanket and some food. He gave the food to the cat and wrapped it in the blanket. The cat was happy and followed Tom to the faraway house. When they got there, the house owner was so happy to see the cat. It was her lost cat! She thanked Tom for bringing her cat back home. The mystery box was for her cat all along. The moral of the story is that being kind and helpful can bring happiness to others.

Once upon a time, there was a big hill. On top of the hill, was a place with a beautiful view. But to get to the top of the hill, one had to climb it. The climb wasn’t easy but when one got to the top, it was worth it. A little girl wanted to see the view from the top but she was very anxious. She was afraid that when she started climbing the hill, she wouldn't be able to make it to the top. But then she decided to try and grabbed a rock with both hands. She took a breath and started to climb. Step by step, the little girl made it to the top and saw the beautiful view she had been dreaming of. From up there, everything looked small and far away. The little girl was so happy and excited that she made it to the top with her own strength!

One day, a little girl named True went to the park. She saw her friend, a dog named Spot. True was very happy and ran to Spot. Spot was an obedient dog and always listened to True. True asked, "Spot, do you want to play with me?" Spot wagged his tail and barked, "Yes, True! Let's play!" They played catch and had a lot of fun. True threw the ball and Spot ran fast to get it. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took the ball away. True and Spot looked up and saw the ball flying in the sky. They were sad, but then a bird flew by and caught the ball in its beak. The bird brought the ball back to True and Spot. True asked, "Bird, will you play with us too?" The bird nodded and they all played together. True, Spot, and the bird became good friends and had a great day at the park.
