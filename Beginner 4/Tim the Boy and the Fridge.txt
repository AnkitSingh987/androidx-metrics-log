Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Fridge

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big fridge in his house. One day, Tim wanted to get a cold drink from the fridge. But the fridge was too high for him to reach. Tim felt sad and ignorant because he did not know how to get the drink. Tim's mom saw him and wanted to help. She knew that she needed to teach Tim how to get the drink. So, she found a small chair for Tim to stand on. She said, "Tim, use this chair to reach the fridge." Tim stood on the chair, and he could reach the fridge. He opened the fridge and got his cold drink. Tim was so happy! He thanked his mom for teaching him. Now, Tim was not ignorant anymore. He knew how to get a drink from the fridge all by himself. And Tim and his mom lived happily ever after.

Anna and Ben were friends. They liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big fence. It had a sign that said "Keep Out". Ben wanted to see what was behind the fence. He said to Anna, "Let's skip over the fence and look. It will be fun." Anna was scared. She said to Ben, "No, we can't. The sign says no. It might be dangerous. There might be bad things or mean people behind the fence. We should stay here and play with our toys." Ben did not listen to Anna. He said, "You are a baby. I am brave. I want to see what is behind the fence. I want to have a wonder. You can stay here if you want. I will go alone." He ran to the fence and tried to climb over it. Anna was sad and worried. She did not want Ben to get hurt or lost. She shouted, "Ben, stop! Come back! Don't go over the fence!" But Ben did not hear her. He jumped over the fence and disappeared. Anna did not know what to do. She wanted to help Ben, but she was afraid to go over the fence too. She started to cry. She hoped that Ben would come back soon. She hoped that he would be safe and sorry. She wished that he had listened to her. She wished that they had played together in the park.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a sharp pencil and an eraser. He wanted to draw a big tree. Tim started to draw, but he made a mistake. He felt confused. "Mom, help me!" Tim said. "I made a mistake with my sharp pencil." Mom came and showed Tim how to use the eraser. "Rub the eraser on the mistake like this," she said. Tim tried to use the eraser, and the mistake went away. He was happy. Tim finished his big tree drawing. He showed it to Mom, and she was proud. Tim learned that it was okay to make mistakes because he could use the eraser to fix them. Now, Tim was not confused anymore.

Mummy and Daddy were in the living room talking. Mummy was explaining to Daddy how she had failed her exam. She looked very sad. Daddy smiled and said it wasn't a big deal and she wasn't foolish! He explained that she just needed to try a little bit harder next time. Mummy was so happy that Daddy understood. She began to smile and gave Daddy a big hug! They both walked off together, with Mummy feeling much better about her exam now.

Once upon a time, there was a big peach tree in a little boy's yard. The boy loved to eat peaches. One day, the boy saw a peach on the tree. It looked ready to eat. He was very happy. The boy asked his mom if he could eat the peach. His mom said, "Wait, let's make sure it is ready." The boy insisted, "I want to eat the peach now!" His mom smiled and said, "Okay, let's check the peach." They went to the tree and saw the peach was big and ready. The boy picked the peach from the tree. He and his mom washed the peach. Then, the boy took a big bite. It was sweet and yummy. The boy was very happy he got to eat the ready peach from his tree.
