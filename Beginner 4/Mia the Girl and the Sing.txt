Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Sing

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to sing. She would sing all day long. One day, Mia found a switch on the wall. She didn't know what it did. Mia asked her mom, "What does this switch do?" Her mom said, "It turns on the music, so you can sing and dance." Mia felt independent and happy. She turned on the switch and started to sing. But then, something unexpected happened. Instead of music, the switch made funny animal sounds! Mia laughed and said, "This is not music, but it's fun!" So she kept singing with the funny animal sounds and had the best day ever.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a big toy box. In the box, he found a fake wand. He liked to play with it. One day, Tim saw his mom stir a big pot in the kitchen. He thought, "I can use my wand to help!" Tim waved his wand and said, "Stir, stir, stir!" The pot stirred all by itself! Mom was happy and gave Tim a big hug. From that day on, Tim used his wand to help others. He learned that even a fake wand can bring wisdom and love. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a regular envelope on the floor. She did not know what was inside. Sue picked it up and went to find her friend Tom. "Tom, look what I found!" said Sue. Tom came and sat next to Sue. They both looked at the envelope. They were very curious. "Let's open it," said Tom. Sue and Tom opened the envelope. Inside, there were colorful stickers! They were so happy and surprised. They never thought they would find something so fun in a regular envelope.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom had a long toy sack. He loved to put all his toys in it. One day, Tom's mom gave him a basket. She told him to put his toys in the basket. Tom liked the basket, but he loved his sack more. He wanted to play with both his sack and the basket. So, he put his toys in the basket and put the basket in the sack. Tom was very happy. Tom played with his sack and basket all day long. He would take the toys out of the basket and put them back in the sack. Tom had lots of fun with his sack and basket. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big board. He was very happy. Tim wanted to show his friend, Sam. So, he took the board to Sam's house. Sam saw the board and said, "Wow! What a big board! What can we do with it?" They thought and thought. Then, they had an idea. They put the board on two big rocks and made a seesaw! Tim and Sam played on the seesaw all day. They went up and down, up and down. They were very grateful for the big board. But then, something unexpected happened. The board broke in half! Tim and Sam were sad. They thought the fun had to end. But then, Tim's dad came out and saw the broken board. He smiled and said, "Don't worry, kids. I can fix it!" Tim's dad fixed the board, and they were able to play again. Tim and Sam were grateful and happy that the fun did not end.

Once upon a time, in a big sea, there was a little shrimp. The shrimp had many friends. They liked to play and swim together. One day, the shrimp met a wise old fish. The wise fish knew many things. The wise fish said, "I recommend you go to the pretty coral. You will find a surprise." The shrimp told his friends about the wise fish's advice. They all swam to the pretty coral. They found a beautiful shell. Inside the shell, there was a shiny pearl. The shrimp and his friends were so happy. They thanked the wise fish for his help.
