Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Candy

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved candy. One day, he saw a modern candy store. It was big and shiny. Tim was very excited. Tim went inside the store and saw a girl. The girl said, "Hi, my name is Sue. Do you want some candy?" Tim said, "Yes, please!" Sue gave Tim a big bag of candy. Tim was so happy. But then, Tim did something that made him embarrass. He dropped the candy on the floor. All the candy was dirty. Tim was sad. Sue was sad too. They could not eat the candy now. This was a bad ending for Tim and Sue.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a theater. In this theater, there were many fun shows. People in the town loved to go there and watch the shows. One day, a foolish man named Tim came to the theater. Tim did not like the theater. He said, "I hate this place." A little girl named Sue heard Tim say this. Sue was sad. She loved the theater very much. Sue went to Tim and asked, "Why do you hate the theater?" Tim looked at her and said, "I don't know. I just do." Sue thought for a moment. Then, she said, "Come with me. Let's watch a show together." Tim agreed. They watched a funny show with lots of laughs. After the show, Tim did not hate the theater anymore. He said, "Thank you, Sue. I was foolish. The theater is fun!" Sue smiled and they became good friends.

One day, a famous dog named Spot won a big trophy. He was very happy. His friend, a cat named Fluffy, saw the trophy and thought it was pretty. Fluffy wanted to play with it. "Can I play with your trophy?" asked Fluffy. Spot said, "No, it is mine. I do not want it to break." Fluffy did not like this and they disagreed. They both wanted the trophy. Then, something unexpected happened. A little girl named Lucy came and picked up the trophy. She said, "Wow, this is a great toy!" Spot and Fluffy looked at each other and decided to share the trophy with Lucy. In the end, Spot, Fluffy, and Lucy all played together with the trophy. They learned that sharing and playing together is more fun than disagreeing.

Once upon a time there was a seal who loved to dance. He would dance in the sea, in the sand and all around the shore. He was so happy when he was dancing! One day, the sea was very, very calm and calm. Then, a whale came to watch the seal. The seal was so grateful to have an audience. He danced even more joyfully than ever before. He spun and twirled and jumped, and the whale happily watched. When the seal was done, he bowed to the whale and thanked him for watching. The whale smiled and gave the seal a friendly goodbye. The seal was so happy and grateful that he had a friend to watch him dance. He thanked the whale again and went on his way. From then on, the seal and whale danced together whenever the seas were calm.

Once upon a time, there was a good boy named Tim. Tim loved to look at the sky. He liked to see the big sun and the white clouds. One day, Tim saw a big box in his room. He wanted to know what was inside. Tim started to unpack the box. He found a big red ball, a blue hat, and a soft teddy bear. He was very happy. Tim put on the blue hat and played with the red ball. The teddy bear sat next to him, watching. After playing, Tim went outside with his new toys. He looked at the sky and saw a big, pretty rainbow. Tim knew it was a good day. He played with the red ball, wore the blue hat, and hugged his new teddy bear under the beautiful sky.

Tom and Sam were best friends. They liked to play with cars and balls and blocks. They also liked to record their voices with a toy phone. They would say funny things and laugh when they heard them. One day, they found a big exit sign in the park. It was red and shiny and had a button. Tom wanted to press the button and see what would happen. Sam was scared. He said, "No, Tom, don't do that. It might be dangerous. Let's go back and play with our phone." But Tom did not listen. He said, "Don't be a baby, Sam. It will be fun. Maybe it will make a loud noise or a bright light. Come on, let's try it." He grabbed Sam's hand and pulled him to the exit sign. Sam tried to get away, but Tom was stronger. He pushed the button and waited. Nothing happened. He pushed it again and again. He said, "This is boring. It doesn't work. Let's go." But then, they heard a loud siren and saw flashing lights. They saw people running and screaming. They saw smoke and fire. They saw a big truck with a hose and a ladder. They saw a man with a helmet and a coat. He was angry. He said, "What did you do? You set off the alarm. You started the fire. You are in big trouble." Tom and Sam were scared. They wanted to run away, but they could not. The man grabbed them and took them to his truck. He said, "You are going to the police. They will teach you a lesson. You are bad boys." Tom and Sam cried. They wished they had not pressed the button. They wished they had listened to Sam. They wished they had played with their phone. They wished they had been loyal friends.
