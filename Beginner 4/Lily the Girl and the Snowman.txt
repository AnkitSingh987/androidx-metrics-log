Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Snowman

One day, a girl named Lily went outside to play. It was an icy day, and Lily was excited to see the snow. She put on her warm coat, hat, and gloves. She ran outside to build a snowman. Lily began to roll a big snowball for the snowman's body. She rolled it and rolled it until it was very big. Next, she rolled a smaller snowball for the snowman's head. She put the small snowball on top of the big snowball. Now, the snowman could stand! Lily wanted to make the snowman look pretty. She ran inside and found her mom's makeup. She took the makeup outside and put it on the snowman. The snowman had red cheeks and blue eyes. Lily was very happy with how the snowman looked. Lily's mom came outside and saw the snowman. She said, "Lily, you did a great job making the snowman! But, let's not use my makeup on him next time, okay?" Lily nodded and smiled. She was happy her mom liked the snowman too. From that day on, Lily and her mom would build snowmen together on icy days. They would use sticks, stones, and other things they found outside to make the snowman look pretty. And they never used makeup on a snowman again.

Danny was watching golf on TV. He loved to watch golf. It was his favorite sport. His little brother Jack was playing on the floor. Jack wanted to play golf too. "Danny, let me play golf too," Jack said. Danny said, "No Jack, golf is too hard for you. You can't play it yet." Jack didn't understand why he couldn't play. He wanted to try. He asked his mom if he could play. Mom said, "No Jack. You can't play. The clubs are too big and the golf ball is too small. Wait until you're a little bit older, then you can play golf." Jack was sad and mad. He went to his room and grabbed his blue boat. "I'm gonna play golf with my boat!" Jack said. Danny laughed and said, "Ok, go ahead and play. Maybe you can surprise us!" Jack was excited. He raced outside and started swinging his blue boat like it was a golf club. He laughed as he watched the boat go through the air. Danny and Mom watched Jack have fun and got a good laugh out of it. They knew that one day he would be able to play real golf.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in a room. They were running very fast. They were having a lot of fun. In the room, there was a big, nice rug. The cat and the dog liked the rug very much. But they did not know that they had to keep the rug clean. Soon, the cat and the dog got very tired. They wanted to rest on the rug. But when they looked at the rug, they saw that it was dirty. They had made the rug dirty with their fast running. The cat and the dog were sad. They knew they had to clean the rug. The cat had an idea. She said, "Let's use our tails to clean the rug." The dog thought it was a good idea. So, they started to clean the rug with their tails. They cleaned and cleaned, but the rug was still dirty. The cat and the dog were very sad. Just then, a little mouse came into the room. The mouse saw the cat and the dog and the dirty rug. The mouse said, "I can help you clean the rug." The cat and the dog were surprised. They did not think a little mouse could help them. But the mouse was very smart. It showed the cat and the dog how to use a little water and soap to clean the rug. They all worked together and soon, the rug was clean again. The cat and the dog were very happy. They thanked the little mouse and they all became good friends. From that day on, they always kept the rug clean.

Once upon a time, there was a new butterfly. The butterfly liked to hop and play. It had big wings that were pretty. The butterfly had many friends in the garden. One day, the butterfly saw a big frog. The frog could hop too. They played hop and had fun. The butterfly and the frog became best friends. They played in the sun all day long. Then, something unexpected happened. The butterfly tried to hop, but it could not. The frog looked at the butterfly and said, "You can fly!" The butterfly tried, and it could fly! The butterfly and the frog were very happy. They played together all day, with the butterfly flying and the frog hopping.
