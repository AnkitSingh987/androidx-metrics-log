Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben and the Dog

Anna and Ben were friends. They liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big dog in the park. The dog was barking and running. Anna and Ben were fearful. They ran away from the dog. "Help! Help!" Anna cried. "The dog is scary!" "Where can we hide?" Ben asked. "The dog is fast!" They saw a big tree. The tree had many leaves and flowers. Anna and Ben ran to the tree. They hid behind the tree. They hoped the dog would not find them. They saw a daisy on the ground. The daisy was yellow and white. It was pretty. Anna picked up the daisy. She gave it to Ben. "Here, Ben. This is for you. You are brave," Anna said. "Thank you, Anna. You are kind," Ben said. "The daisy is nice." They smiled at each other. They felt better. They heard the dog bark again. They looked around the tree. They saw the dog. The dog was not scary. The dog was happy. The dog had a ball in its mouth. The dog wanted to play. "Look, Anna. The dog is not mean. The dog is friendly," Ben said. "Maybe you are right, Ben. Maybe the dog just wants to have fun," Anna said. They came out from behind the tree. They waved at the dog. The dog wagged its tail. The dog dropped the ball. The dog looked at Anna and Ben. "Do you want to play with us, dog?" Anna asked. The dog barked. The dog nodded. Anna and Ben laughed. They picked up the ball. They threw the ball. The dog chased the ball. The dog brought the ball back. They played with the dog. They had fun. They were not fearful. They were happy.

One day, a girl named Sue wanted to do yoga. She liked to be gentle when she did yoga. Sue had a yoga mat and a yoga book. She was very happy. Sue's little brother, Tim, wanted to play with her. He did not know about yoga. Tim tried to add his toys to Sue's yoga mat. This made Sue sad. Sue told Tim that yoga is not for toys. Tim was sad too. Then, Sue had an idea. She said, "Let's do yoga together!" Tim smiled and they did yoga. Now, they were both happy.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a kind bunny named Bobo. Bobo loved to wake up early and watch the sunrise. Every morning, he would sit on a hill and look at the sky. The colors in the sky made him feel happy and alert. One day, as Bobo was watching the sunrise, he met a little bird named Chirpy. Chirpy was sad because she lost her favorite toy. Bobo wanted to help his new friend. "Can I borrow your toy?" Chirpy asked. "Yes, of course," said Bobo. He gave Chirpy his toy to play with. Chirpy was so happy to have a new toy to play with. She thanked Bobo and they played together until the sun went down. From that day on, Bobo and Chirpy became best friends. They always watched the sunrise together and shared their toys. They knew that sharing and helping each other made life more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a naughty bird called Tom. Tom liked playing tricks on everyone but he rarely did anything useful. One day, Tom’s friends asked him to help fix a broken birdhouse. Tom shrugged his shoulders and said, “Sorry, I don’t know how to fix it.” Tom’s friends said, “You should learn to do things that help others. It’s more fun than playing naughty tricks.” Tom thought a minute, then nodded his head. He decided to try and fix the birdhouse. He put it back together carefully, and soon it was as good as new. Tom’s friends were really happy and thanked him. Tom was happy too and felt proud of what he had done. From then on, Tom tried to find ways to help his friends. He had learned from his experience that it was better to be kind and helpful than to just be naughty.

Once upon a time, there was a big quarrel between the Sun and the Moon. The Sun said it was the best because it was very bright and made the day warm. The Moon said it was the best because it was pretty and made the night cool. One day, they decided to play a game to see who was the best. They went to the west side of the sky. The Sun put on a tight yellow dress, and the Moon put on a tight silver dress. They both looked very nice. They played a game of hide and seek. The Sun hid first, and the Moon looked for it. The Moon found the Sun very fast. Then, it was the Moon's turn to hide. The Sun looked and looked, but it could not find the Moon. The Moon won the game. They both laughed and became friends. The Sun and the Moon learned that they were both special in their own way.
