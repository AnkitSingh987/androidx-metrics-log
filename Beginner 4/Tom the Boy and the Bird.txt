Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a small cabin in the woods, there lived a boy named Tom. Tom was scared of the dark. At night, he would hide under his blanket and shake. One day, Tom found a small, friendly bird trapped in a net. Tom wanted to help the bird. He said to the bird, "Don't be scared. I will help you." Tom worked hard to release the bird from the net. The bird was free and happy. The bird thanked Tom and said, "You were brave to help me. I will help you too." That night, the bird stayed with Tom in the cabin. The bird sang a soft song. Tom was not scared anymore. He slept well and had sweet dreams. They became best friends, and Tom was never scared of the dark again.

Once upon a time, there was a little ball named Bounce. Bounce loved to jump and play all day. He was a very enthusiastic ball, always happy and full of energy. One day, Bounce went to the grocery store with his friend, a little girl named Lily. They wanted to buy some fruit for a snack. Bounce liked to help Lily pick the best fruit. He would bounce up high to see the ones at the top of the shelf. At the grocery store, they found a big pile of apples. Bounce jumped up and down to help Lily find the best one. They had so much fun together. When they were done, they went home to eat their snack and play some more. Bounce and Lily were the best of friends.

Jimmy was a three-year-old boy who had been playing outside with his friends. He loved to run and play in the park. As he was playing, he spotted a shiny car in the distance. He wanted to take a closer look, so he ran to the car. As he got closer, he saw a man near the car. Jimmy asked the man, "Mister, is that a new car?" The man looked at Jimmy with a kind smile and said, "Yes, it is a new car. Do you like it?" Jimmy nodded his head and said, "Yes! Can I please sign it?" The man laughed and said, "No, that's not a good idea. You could make it look bad." Jimmy was a bit disappointed, but he still had a huge smile on his face. He thanked the man and ran back to his friends in the park.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play near the river. The river is big and blue and makes a nice sound. Sometimes they see fish and ducks in the river. One day, Anna and Ben find a big red apple on a tree. The apple looks tasty and shiny. They want to eat the apple, but it is too high for them to reach. "I have an idea," says Ben. "We can use this stick to knock the apple down. Then we can share it." Anna thinks it is a good idea. She holds the stick and Ben climbs on her shoulders. He tries to hit the apple with the stick, but he misses. He tries again, but he misses again. "Be careful, Ben," says Anna. "You are making me wobble." Ben tries one more time, but he hits the branch instead of the apple. The branch breaks and the apple falls into the river. The river carries the apple away. "Oh no!" says Ben. "We lost the apple!" Anna and Ben are sad. They watch the apple float away. They wish they had the apple. They regret trying to get the apple with the stick. "Maybe we can find another apple," says Anna. "Maybe," says Ben. "But I don't think it will be as tasty as that one."

Once upon a time, there was a charming little dog named Spot. Spot had white fur and loved to play outside. One day, Spot saw a big book and wanted to study it. Spot opened the book and saw many pictures. He saw a picture of a cat and said, "Meow!" Then he saw a picture of a cow and said, "Moo!" Spot's friend, a little girl named Mary, heard him and came to see what he was doing. She asked, "Spot, what are you doing?" Spot replied, "I am studying this big book!" Mary smiled and said, "That's great! Let's study together!" So Spot and Mary studied the big book together. They learned about many animals and had lots of fun. And from that day on, Spot and Mary loved to study and learn new things together.
