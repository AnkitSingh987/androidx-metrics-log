Category: Beginner
Title: Remy the Reindeer and the Forest

One day, a reindeer named Remy was in the forest. Remy was a naughty reindeer. He liked to play tricks on his friends. He would hide their things and make them look for them. One day, Remy saw his friend, a squirrel named Sam. Sam was sad because he could not find any nuts to eat. Remy had an idea. He said, "I will provide nuts for you, Sam." Sam was happy and thanked Remy. But Remy played a trick on Sam. He gave Sam rocks instead of nuts. Sam tried to eat the rocks, but they were too hard. Sam was sad again. Just then, a wise old owl saw what happened. The owl told Remy that being naughty was not nice. Remy felt bad and decided to change. He found real nuts for Sam and they became good friends. From that day on, Remy was a helpful reindeer.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toys and draw on his blackboard. One sunny day, Tim drew the perfect picture of a cat on his blackboard. He was so happy with his drawing. Later that day, Tim went to bathe. While he was in the tub, he heard a strange sound. He got out of the tub and went to see what it was. To his surprise, the cat he drew on the blackboard was now a real cat! It was sitting on the floor, looking at him. Tim couldn't believe it. He played with the cat all day long. They became the best of friends. From that day on, Tim knew that his blackboard was magical. He could draw anything and it would come to life. Tim and his new cat friend had many fun adventures together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to feed her pet fish, Goldie. Every day, she would take fish food from the fridge and give it to Goldie. Goldie would swim around and eat the food happily. One day, Sue went to the fridge to get some fish food. But when she looked at Goldie, she saw that he was not moving. Sue was very sad and thought Goldie was dead. She started to cry. Then, something unexpected happened. Sue's mom came into the room and saw her crying. She looked at Goldie and said, "Sue, Goldie is not dead! He is just sleeping." Sue's mom gently tapped the fishbowl, and Goldie started to swim around again. Sue was so happy, and she fed Goldie his food from the fridge. From that day on, Sue knew that sometimes fish like Goldie just need a little tap to wake up.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big toy truck. Tim loved to play with his truck. He would load it with his favorite toys and drive it around the house. He made sure his toys were safe in the truck. One day, Tim's mom said if he helped her with a job, he would get a reward. Tim was very excited. He wanted to help his mom and get the reward. So, he took his big toy truck and went to help his mom. Tim's mom asked him to load his truck with apples from the tree. Tim worked hard and loaded all the apples into his truck. He drove his truck to his mom, making sure the apples were safe. When he was done, his mom gave him a big hug and a sweet treat as his reward. Tim was very happy, and he knew that helping others was a good thing to do.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a modern house with his mom and dad. One day, Tim saw a big bottle on the table. He wanted to play with it, but his mom said, "No, Tim. That bottle is not a toy." Tim was sad, but he listened to his mom. Later, when his mom was not looking, he saw the bottle again. Tim thought, "Maybe I can play with it for a little bit." He took the bottle and started to play. But then, the bottle fell and broke on the floor. When his mom saw the broken bottle, she was not happy. She said, "Tim, I told you not to play with the bottle. Now you must be punished." Tim felt bad for not listening to his mom. He said, "I'm sorry, Mom. I will listen next time." His mom forgave him, and Tim learned to always listen to his mom and dad.
