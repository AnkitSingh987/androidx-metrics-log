Category: Beginner
Title: Molly the Proud Cow and the Box

One day, a proud cow named Molly lived on a farm. Molly had lots of friends, like the pigs, the sheep, and the chickens. They all played together every day. Molly liked to eat beef, and she was very good at finding it. One day, Molly found a big box. She asked her friend, Pig, "What is in this box?" Pig said, "I do not know. It is locked." Molly and Pig tried to open the box, but they could not. They asked their friend, Sheep, to help them. Sheep said, "I will try." Sheep used her soft wool to unlock the box. Inside the box, they found a big piece of beef. Molly was so happy! She said, "Thank you, Sheep! Now we can all eat the beef together." They all shared the beef and had a big party. The next day, the farmer saw the open box and said, "Wow, you animals worked together and shared the beef. I am proud of you!" The animals learned that if they worked together, they could do anything. And they all lived happily on the farm.

One day, in the month of March, a helpful cat named Sam heard a ring. The ring came from a big red phone. Sam ran to the phone and said, "Hello?" A voice said, "Help! My ball is stuck in a tree!" Sam wanted to help, so he went to the park. He saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad because her ball was high up in the tree. Sam tried to climb the tree, but he couldn't. He looked around and saw a big, tall giraffe. Sam asked the giraffe, "Can you help get the ball?" The giraffe reached up with its long neck and got the ball. Sue was so happy! She hugged Sam and the giraffe. But then, something unexpected happened. The giraffe turned into a big, friendly bear! The bear said, "Thank you for helping me. I was stuck as a giraffe, but your kindness turned me back into a bear!" Sam, Sue, and the bear all played with the ball and had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a big barrel. It was in a small house. The barrel was full of toys. Every night, it would dream about the fun it had with the kids. One day, a crazy bird saw the barrel. The bird thought the barrel was a big tree. It tried to make a nest on top of the barrel. The barrel did not like it. It wanted the bird to go away. The kids saw the crazy bird on the barrel. They laughed and played with the bird. The bird liked the kids. It forgot about the barrel. The barrel was happy again. It could dream about the fun with the kids.

Once upon a time, there was a large car. It was red and shiny. The car had a special power. It could speak! One day, the car met a little girl named Sue. Sue was surprised when the car said, "Hello!" Sue and the car became friends. They would talk and play every day. Sue and the car went on fun trips. They saw big hills, green trees, and pretty flowers. Sue was very happy with her new friend. They lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in an ordinary little house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim was sad because there was a leak in his room. The leak made his toys wet and his floor messy. Tim asked his mom for help. "Mom, can you fix the leak?" Tim said. "My toys are all wet!" Mom tried to fix the leak, but it was too big. She had to leave to get help from Dad. While they were gone, Tim tried to fix the leak by himself. He climbed on a chair and reached for the leak. But he slipped and fell on the wet floor. Tim started to cry. Mom and Dad came back and saw Tim on the floor. They were sad because they couldn't fix the leak in time. They knew they needed to be more careful next time. The ordinary little house stayed wet and messy, and Tim learned a lesson about asking for help.
