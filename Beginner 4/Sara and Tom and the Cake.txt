Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Tom and the Cake

Sara and Tom are playing with their dolls in the living room. Sara has a pink doll and Tom has a blue doll. They pretend that the dolls are talking to each other. "Hello, I am Sara's doll. What is your name?" Sara makes her doll say. "I am Tom's doll. I like to play with you." Tom makes his doll reply. They make the dolls hug and kiss. Then they hear a loud noise from the kitchen. It is their daddy. He is making a cake for their mommy's birthday. "Daddy, daddy, can we help you?" Sara and Tom run to the kitchen. "Sure, my sweeties. You can help me decorate the cake. But be careful, it is hot." Daddy says. He gives them some icing and sprinkles. Sara and Tom have fun putting them on the cake. They make flowers and stars and hearts. "Daddy, you are the best. We love you." Sara and Tom say. "I love you too, my darlings. You are very good helpers." Daddy says. He hugs them and kisses them. Then he looks at the cake. It is very big and round and colorful. "Daddy, is the cake fat?" Tom asks. Daddy laughs. He says, "No, the cake is not fat. It is just yummy. But we should not eat too much of it, or we will be fat." Sara and Tom giggle. They say, "We will share the cake with mommy and our friends. They will like it too." They are very happy. They can't wait to surprise mommy with the cake. They love their daddy and their mommy and their dolls. They are a happy family.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Grace. She was three years old and very curious. One day, Grace's mommy took her outside to play. After running and climbing for awhile, Grace started to get very tired. Her mommy said it was time for a nap. Grace laid down in the grass and drifted off to sleep. When Grace was fast asleep, something incredible happened. Floating in the air was a shiny medal! Grace woke up from her nap and saw it. She was so excited that she jumped up and scrambled to try and catch it. But it was too high and she couldn't reach it. Grace's mommy knew what to do. She grabbed a big stick, reached up high, and caught the medal! She handed it to Grace and said, "This is for you, my brave and incredible girl". Grace smiled and thanked her mommy. She held the medal tightly, feeling so proud. Grace had the most amazing nap ever!

One day, a noisy tutor came to a little girl's house. The tutor was there to help the girl learn. The girl did not like the noisy tutor. She wanted to play, but the tutor said, "No, we need to learn first." The girl's mom was in the kitchen. She was cooking a big yummy cake. The girl wanted to eat the cake, but her mom said, "You can eat the cake after you learn with the tutor." The girl did not want to learn. She wanted the cake now. So, she tried to cook her own cake. But the girl did not know how to cook. She made a big mess in the kitchen. The cake was bad. The girl's mom was sad. The tutor was sad too. The girl did not get to eat the yummy cake. She learned that she should listen to her mom and the tutor. The moral of the story is to listen and learn before trying to do things on your own.

Once upon a time, there was a nice cat named Daisy. Daisy lived in a small house with a big floor. She liked to play with her ball on the floor all day long. One day, Daisy's ball rolled out the door and into the street. Daisy was sad and didn't know what to do. Then, she saw her friend, Max the dog. Max saw Daisy's sad face and asked, "Daisy, why are you sad?" Daisy told Max, "My ball is in the street and I can't get it." Max wanted to help Daisy, so he ran into the street to save the ball. He picked it up with his mouth and brought it back to Daisy. Daisy was so happy to have her ball back, and they played together on the big floor. They were the best of friends, and they lived happily ever after.
