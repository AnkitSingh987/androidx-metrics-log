Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Dog and the Turtle

One day, a dog named Max was playing in the park. Max liked to run and jump. He was a happy dog. Max saw a big ball and wanted to play with it. He ran to the ball and put his paw on it. But the ball did not move. Max was very confused. Max tried to push the ball again. This time, he used his nose. The ball still did not move. Max started to get serious. He used his heel to push the ball. The ball still did not move. Max was getting mad. Max decided to yell at the ball. He barked and barked. Suddenly, the ball started to move! But it was not a ball. It was a big turtle! The turtle was hiding in its shell. Max and the turtle became friends and played in the park together.

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat named Kitty. Kitty liked to play with her friend, a little dog named Doggy. They lived in a house with a big yard. They liked to run and jump and play all day. One day, Kitty found a skull in the yard. It was small and white. Kitty thought it would be fun to tease Doggy with it. So, she picked it up with her mouth and ran to show her friend. Doggy saw Kitty with the skull and wanted to play too. Kitty would run and Doggy would chase her, trying to get the skull. They played and teased each other all day long. At the end of the day, they were tired but happy. They knew they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big boot. It was red and shiny. The boot was proud of its color. It liked to walk in the park and show off to all the other boots. One day, the boot found a paint can. The paint was blue and pretty. The boot thought it would be fun to paint the park. So, the boot dipped itself in the paint and walked all around. The park looked so nice with blue paint everywhere. The boot was very proud of its work. All the other boots came to see the park and they thought it was great too. And they all played happily in the colorful park.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful alligator named Al. Al liked to help his friends in the forest. One day, he saw a little bird with a hurt wing. The bird was sad because it could not fly. Al said, "Don't worry, bird. I will help you." He hurried to find a soft leaf to wrap around the bird's wing. The bird smiled and said, "Thank you, Al." After a few days, the bird's wing was better, and it could fly again. The bird and Al became good friends. They played together every day, and all the animals in the forest knew that Al was a kind and thoughtful alligator.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his uncle. They played with a red ball and had so much fun. Tim looked at his uncle and said, "I like playing with you, uncle." As they played, Tim saw a big tree. He looked at the tree and saw a small cat stuck high up in the branches. The cat looked scared. Tim told his uncle about the cat, and his uncle said, "Let's help the cat get down." Tim's uncle found a long stick and carefully pushed the cat out of the tree. The cat fell into Tim's arms, and the cat was grateful. Tim looked at his uncle and said, "Thank you, uncle, for helping the cat." His uncle smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim. I'm glad we could help."

One day, a little boy named Tim went to his class. He had a brilliant teacher named Miss Jane. Miss Jane was nice and always helped the kids learn. In the class, Tim had a friend named Lily. Lily liked to complain a lot. One day, Lily said, "I don't want to color anymore." Tim tried to help her feel better. He said, "Lily, let's try to color a big, fun picture together." So, Tim and Lily started to color a big picture. They were having fun, but then something unexpected happened. A little dog ran into the class! The dog took the picture in its mouth and ran away. Tim and Lily looked at each other and started to laugh. They didn't complain anymore and had a fun day in class.
