Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Sam and the Lion

Tim and Sam were twins who loved animals. They wanted to go on a safari and see lions, elephants, and zebras. Their dad said they could go to the zoo and pretend it was a safari. He gave them each a hat and a binoculars. They got in the car and drove to the zoo. They were very excited and talked a lot. "I want to see the lions first," said Tim. "No, I want to see the elephants first," said Sam. "They are big and gray and have long noses." They argued until their dad said, "Stop! We will see all the animals, don't worry." They got to the zoo and got out of the car. They put on their hats and held their binoculars. They followed their dad to the gate. He bought the tickets and gave them to the man. The man smiled and said, "Welcome to the zoo. Have a nice safari." They walked into the zoo and saw many animals. They saw monkeys, giraffes, crocodiles, and flamingos. They used their binoculars to look closer. They made noises like the animals. They had a lot of fun. They came to the lion enclosure. They saw a big lion lying on a rock. He had a mane and sharp teeth. He looked sleepy. Tim and Sam wanted to see him roar. They got closer to the fence and shouted, "Roar, lion, roar!" The lion opened his eyes and looked at them. He did not roar. He yawned. Tim and Sam were disappointed. They wanted to hear a loud roar. They shouted again, "Roar, lion, roar!" The lion did not roar. He got up and walked away. He went to a shady spot and lay down again. Tim and Sam were angry. They wanted to see a real lion, not a lazy lion. They stomped their feet and threw their hats on the ground. They did not see the sign that said, "Do not feed the animals." They did not see the peacock that was walking behind them. The peacock saw the hats. He liked shiny things. He saw the buttons on the hats. He thought they were jewels. He wanted to have them. He walked to the hats and picked one up with his beak. He turned around and walked away. Tim and Sam heard a noise. They turned around and saw the peacock. They saw their hat in his mouth. They gasped. They ran after the peacock. They shouted, "Hey, give us back our hat!" The peacock was scared. He ran faster. He ran into the crowd. He bumped into people and animals. He made a lot of noise. He dropped the hat. He ran away. Tim and Sam chased the hat. They pushed and shoved. They stumbled and fell. They got up and ran. They caught the hat. They were happy. They put it back on their head. They looked around. They saw their dad. He was not happy. He was angry. He saw them make a mess. He saw them break the rules. He saw them scare the peacock. He said, "Tim and Sam, what have you done? You have been very naughty. You have made a big mess. You have to say sorry to the people and the animals. You have to leave the zoo now. No more safari for you." Tim and Sam felt bad. They did not mean to make a mess. They did not mean to be naughty. They said sorry to the people and the animals. They followed their dad to the car. They were quiet and sad. They learned their lesson. They did not shout at the lion anymore. They did not throw their hats anymore. They did not chase the peacock anymore. They said, "We are sorry, dad. We love you. We love the zoo. We love the animals. Can we come back another day?" Their dad sighed. He was still angry, but he loved them. He said, "Maybe, if you behave better. Now, let's go home and have some lunch. You must be hungry after your messy safari."

Once upon a time, there was a red car. The car had a big job to do. It had to take a gift to a friend's house. The car had to be very careful on the road. The car went slow and looked both ways. It saw a cat and stopped. The cat said "thank you" and crossed the road. The car was happy to be careful. At last, the car got to the friend's house. The friend was so happy to see the car. The car gave the gift to the friend. The friend said "thank you" and accepted the gift. The car went back home, feeling good about its careful trip.
