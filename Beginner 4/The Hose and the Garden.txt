Category: Beginner
Title: The Hose and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a hose in the garden. The hose was very tough and it was used to help and support the flowers and trees. Every day, a little boy would come to the garden and help water the plants using the garden hose. He would really play with the hose, spraying good water onto the plants. The flower felt so happy and strong each time the little boy watered them, and they really grew and bloomed. To show their appreciation, the flowers would give off their biggest and brightest petals. At night, the little boy would go to sleep, while the hose stayed outside. During rainy days, it would provide the flowers and plants with extra water, so they could stay healthy. The hose worked so hard to support the plants and keep them growing! The little boy and the hose became the best of friends, as the tough garden hose continued to provide support for the garden every day.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to play in his yard. One day, he found a mysterious ball. The ball was big and blue. Tim was very happy. Tim saw his friend, Sue, and said, "Sue, look at my ball! Let's play!" Sue came to the yard and they started to throw the ball to each other. They laughed and had a lot of fun. As they played, a little dog came to the yard. The dog wanted to play too. Tim and Sue threw the ball and the dog ran to catch it. They played together all day long. The mysterious ball made them all very happy.

Lila and Ben were playing in the farm. They saw a big horse in the field. The horse was brown and had a long tail. Lila and Ben liked the horse. They wanted to pet it. They ran to the fence and called the horse. The horse came to them and let them touch its nose. Lila and Ben were happy. They gave the horse some grass and some apples. The horse ate them and made a loud sound. "Hello, horse," Lila said. "You are very nice. Do you want to play with us?" The horse nodded its head. Lila and Ben smiled. They climbed over the fence and got on the horse's back. The horse walked slowly around the field. Lila and Ben laughed and hugged the horse. But then they saw a big cloud in the sky. The cloud was dark and scary. It made a loud noise and flashed a bright light. Lila and Ben were afraid. They wanted to go back to the house. "Come on, horse," Ben said. "Let's go. It's going to rain." But the horse did not listen. The horse was foolish. The horse liked the cloud. The horse thought the cloud was fun. The horse ran to the cloud and tried to touch it. "Stop, horse, stop!" Lila and Ben shouted. "The cloud is bad. The cloud will hurt you." But the horse did not stop. The horse reached the cloud and the cloud hit the horse. The cloud was not fun. The cloud was angry. The cloud made a big noise and a big light and a big rain. The horse was scared and hurt. The horse fell to the ground. Lila and Ben fell too. They were wet and cold and sad. They ran to the house and told their mom what happened. Their mom was worried and angry. She went to the field and saw the horse. The horse was still on the ground. The horse was not moving. The horse was not breathing. The horse was dead. Lila and Ben cried. They were sorry. They loved the horse. They wanted to say goodbye. They went to the field and covered the horse with a blanket. They said sorry to the horse and hugged the horse. They learned a lesson. They learned to be careful and listen and not be foolish. They learned to respect the cloud and the rain and the horse. They learned to be good.

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a dependable owl. The owl had many friends in the forest. One day, the owl met a little mouse. The mouse was sad because he lost his way home. The owl said, "Don't worry, little mouse. I will help you find your home." The mouse was happy and said, "Thank you, owl. You are a good friend." The dependable owl carried the little mouse on his back. They flew high in the sky and looked for the mouse's home. They saw many trees and animals. Finally, they found the mouse's home. The little mouse was very happy and thanked the owl. The owl said, "You are welcome, little mouse. I am always here to help my friends." From that day on, the owl and the mouse were best friends. They played together and helped each other when they needed it. And the owl was always dependable.
