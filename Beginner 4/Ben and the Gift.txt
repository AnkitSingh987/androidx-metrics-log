Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and the Gift

Ben was very happy. He was going to a party. His friend Sam invited him. Sam was gifted. He could count very well. He could count to ten and more. Ben wanted to give Sam a gift. He looked for a gift in his room. He saw his toy cars. He liked his toy cars. He counted them. He had six toy cars. He thought Sam would like them too. He put his toy cars in a bag. He went to the party with his mom. He saw Sam and his mom. He said hello. He gave Sam the bag. He said, "This is for you. I counted them. There are six toy cars." Sam opened the bag. He smiled. He said, "Thank you, Ben. I like toy cars. You are very kind. I counted them too. There are six toy cars. You are gifted too. You can count very well." Ben and Sam hugged. They played with the toy cars. They had fun. They counted the toy cars together. They counted to six and more. They were happy. They were friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a thoughtful boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat. His favorite thing to eat was cake. One day, Tim's mom made a big, yummy cake for him. Tim was so happy! He said, "Mom, can I eat the cake now?" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim, you can eat the cake. But be careful, it is still hot." As Tim was eating the cake, he felt something strange. He looked down and saw that he had cake on his toe! Tim laughed and said, "Oh no! I have cake on my toe!" He carefully wiped the cake off his toe and continued to eat. Tim's mom watched him and said, "You're such a thoughtful boy, Tim. You always make sure to clean up after yourself." Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Mom. I love you and your yummy cake!" And so, Tim finished eating the cake, making sure not to get any more on his toes. He was a happy and thoughtful boy, who loved to eat cake with his mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to relax in the sun. One day, she saw a hot dish on the table. Kitty jumped on the table to look at the hot dish. She smelled something yummy. But, just as she was about to taste it, the dish started to shake! Kitty was scared, but she stayed to see what would happen next. The hot dish jumped up and turned into a friendly dish monster! The dish monster said, "Don't be scared, Kitty. I just want to play!" Kitty and the dish monster became best friends, and they played all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy. His name was Mat. Mat was three years old and he liked to explore and play. One day Mat decided to go on an adventure. Soon he arrived at an old cabin in the woods. He noticed a strip of wood on the ground. Mat decided to pick it up. He wanted to see what it was. When he looked closer, he noticed that the strip was hiding something underneath. Mat bent down to sort the strip away from whatever was underneath. He carefully moved it aside and couldn't believe what he was seeing. There were two big rocks! He was so excited and he quickly ran to get his friends. Mat's friends came over to see the discovery. After they had a look, they all cheered. Mat said, "Let's play a game, who can hide a rock the best." Everyone agreed and they had lots of fun hiding the rocks in the cabin's hidden parts. They spent the whole day playing their game. They found lots of different hiding spots and had lots of fun!

Once upon a time, there was a little ant. The little ant liked to number the things he found. One day, he found a big apple. He was very happy and grateful for the apple. The little ant wanted to share the apple with his ant friends. He said, "Look, I found a big apple! Let's eat it together!" His friends were very happy and grateful too. They all started to eat the apple. Suddenly, a big bird came down from the sky. The bird wanted to take the apple away. The little ant and his friends were scared. But then, the bird saw how grateful the ants were for the apple. The bird said, "You can keep the apple, little ants. I am happy to see you happy." The little ant and his friends were surprised and grateful. They all became friends and shared the apple together.
