Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Star and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a big sky, a foolish star lay all alone. It was sad and wanted a friend to play with. The other stars were far away and did not talk to the foolish star. One day, a kind bird flew by the foolish star. The bird saw the star was sad and asked, "Why are you sad, star?" The star said, "I am all alone, and I want a friend to play with." The kind bird felt bad for the star and decided to help. The bird flew far and wide, telling all the other stars about the foolish star. Soon, many stars came to play with the foolish star. They all became good friends and the foolish star was not sad anymore. They all played together in the big sky, shining brightly and happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy that could spray water. He loved to play with it in his wide backyard. One day, Tim saw a big, bad bug in the grass. He wanted to make it go away. Tim said to the bug, "Go away, bad bug! I don't want you here!" But the bug did not listen. It stayed in the grass. Tim knew what to do. He ran to get his water toy. Tim came back with his toy and said, "Now, I will spray you, bad bug!" He sprayed the water at the bug. The bug got wet and ran away. Tim was happy that the bug was gone. Now, he could play in his wide backyard without any bad bugs.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree in a big square. The tree did not know how it got there. It just knew it was very tall. It could see all the other trees and flowers around it. The tall tree liked to be in the big square. One day, a little boy came to the big square. He saw the tall tree and said, "Hello, tall tree! Do you know why you are so tall?" The tall tree did not know why, but it liked to be tall. The little boy liked the tall tree too. Then, something unexpected happened. The tall tree started to get smaller and smaller. Soon, it was not a tall tree anymore. It was just a small tree like all the others in the big square. The little boy was surprised! But he still liked the small tree, and the tree was happy to have a new friend.

Once upon a time, a little clock lived in a big house. The clock was not happy. It wanted to be fierce like a lion, but it was just a small clock. Every day, the clock would watch the lion on TV and wish to be like it. One day, the clock decided to be fierce. It tried to roar like a lion, but it could only make a small "tick-tock" sound. The clock began to panic. It wanted to be fierce so badly, but it didn't know how. Then, the clock had an idea. It would make its "tick-tock" sound very loud and strong. The clock practiced and practiced until its "tick-tock" was as fierce as a lion's roar. From that day on, the little clock felt fierce and happy, and it loved being a clock.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a new toy. The toy was a little car. The car had a friend, a small doll. They liked to play together. One day, there was a loud sound. It was thunder! The car got scared and hid under the bed. The doll wanted to find her friend, but she did not know where the car was hiding. The doll looked everywhere. Then, she found the car under the bed. The car was sorry for hiding. The doll said, "I forgive you." They hugged and played together again. And they were not scared of the thunder anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big thumb. One day, he went to play with his friends. While playing, Tim's friend, Sam, saw Tim's big thumb. Sam said, "Wow, your thumb is so big!" Tim felt proud and decided to show off. He started to slap his thumb on the table, making a loud noise. All the other kids laughed and clapped. But then, Tim's mom came into the room. She heard the loud noise and saw Tim slapping his thumb. She said, "Tim, it's not polite to slap your thumb like that. You should be kind and gentle with your friends." Tim felt sad, but he knew his mom was right. From that day on, Tim learned to be polite and gentle. He never slapped his thumb again. And all his friends liked him even more. The moral of the story is: always be kind and polite to others.
