Category: Beginner
Title: The Bush and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a big bush in the middle of a field. The bush had many broken branches. Near the bush, there was a small house with a girl named Lucy and her dog, Max. One day, Lucy and Max went outside to play. Max saw a ball near the bush and ran to get it. Lucy followed him. When they got to the bush, they found a secret path. Max wanted to see what was on the other side, so he lead Lucy down the path. As they walked, the path became darker and scarier. Lucy wanted to go back home, but Max kept leading her further. Finally, they reached a big hole in the ground. Max was too excited and fell into the hole. Lucy tried to save him, but she couldn't. She was very sad and cried all the way back home.

Once upon a time, there was a brave dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his favorite toy, a missile. One day, Spot was playing with his missile when it suddenly disappeared. Spot was very sad and looked for his missile everywhere. While Spot was looking for his missile, he met a big cat. The cat said, "I saw your missile go into the dark woods." Spot was scared, but he was brave and wanted his missile back. So, Spot went into the dark woods to find his missile. Spot walked and walked in the woods. Then, he found his missile! But, it was not just his missile. There were many missiles, all the same! The big cat came out and said, "Surprise! I made more missiles for you to play with!" Spot was so happy and thanked the big cat. They became friends and played with the missiles together.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to school. She was very happy to see her friends. In her class, there was a new pupil named Tim. Tim had a cool bag with a big zip. Lily felt a little jealous because her bag did not have a zip. At lunch time, Lily said to Tim, "I like your bag with the zip. Can you show me how to use it?" Tim smiled and said, "Sure, Lily! You just pull the zip like this, and it opens the bag. Then, you can put your things inside." Lily and Tim became good friends. They played together and shared their toys. One day, Lily's mom bought her a new bag with a zip. Lily was very happy and showed it to Tim. "Now, we both have bags with zips!" she said. Tim smiled and said, "Yes, we do! We can open and close our bags together!" From that day on, Lily and Tim always played together, and they were never jealous of each other again. They learned that sharing and being friends was more important than having things that others did not have.

One day, Sam and his mom went to the park. Sam liked to play on the swings and the slide. He saw a big dog with a cop. The dog was brown and had a shiny badge. Sam wanted to pet the dog. "Mom, can I pet the dog?" Sam asked. "Sure, but you have to ask the cop first. He is the dog's friend and he can tell you if the dog is nice or not," his mom said. Sam ran to the cop and the dog. He said hello to the cop and asked if he could pet the dog. "Hello, little boy. This is Rex. He is a police dog. He helps me catch bad guys. You can pet him, but be gentle. He is very strong and smart," the cop said. Sam smiled and reached out his hand to touch Rex's fur. But Rex did not like strangers. He barked loudly and snapped at Sam's hand. Sam was scared and pulled back his hand. He started to cry. "Rex, no! Bad dog! I'm sorry, he usually doesn't do that. He must be nervous today. Are you okay, little boy?" the cop said. Sam's mom ran to him and hugged him. She checked his hand and saw that it was not hurt. She was upset and angry. "How could you let your dog do that? He could have bitten my son! You should train him better or keep him away from children. You are a bad cop and a bad dog owner!" she yelled at the cop. The cop was embarrassed and sorry. He tried to apologize and explain that Rex was not mean, just protective. But Sam's mom did not listen. She took Sam and left the park. She said they would never come back. The cop felt bad. He looked at Rex and sighed. He wished he could make things right. He hoped Sam and his mom would forgive him and Rex. He hoped they would come back to the park someday. He hoped they would be friends.
