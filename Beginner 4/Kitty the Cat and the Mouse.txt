Category: Beginner
Title: Kitty the Cat and the Mouse

Once upon a time, there was a cute little cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with her toy mouse. One day, she found a bottle of ink on the floor. Kitty did not know what it was, so she sniffed it. Kitty's friend, Doggy, saw her and said, "Kitty, be careful! That is ink. It can make a big mess." Kitty did not listen and started to play with the ink. She dipped her paw in the ink and made paw prints all over the floor. "Oh no!" said Kitty, "I made a big mess." She tried to clean the ink, but it was hard. Kitty started to struggle. Doggy saw her struggle and decided to help. Together, they cleaned the ink and made the floor clean again. Kitty said, "Thank you, Doggy, for helping me. I will be more careful next time." Doggy smiled and said, "You're welcome, Kitty. That's what friends are for." From that day on, Kitty and Doggy played together and always helped each other.

Lily and Ben were friends who liked to play together. One day, they found a bottle of wine in the kitchen. They did not know what it was, but it looked pretty and shiny. "Let's drink it!" said Ben. "It must be yummy!" "No, we can't!" said Lily. "It is not for us. It is for grown-ups. It can make us sick!" But Ben did not listen to Lily. He disagreed with her. He opened the bottle and took a sip. He made a face. It was not yummy. It was sour and bitter. "Yuck!" he said. "This is bad!" He threw the bottle on the floor. It broke into pieces. The wine spilled everywhere. It made a big mess. Lily was scared. She knew they did something wrong. She ran to get help. She found their mom and dad. They were angry and worried. "What did you do?" they asked. "Why did you touch the wine? It is not for you. It is dangerous!" They took Ben to the doctor. He had a tummy ache. He felt sorry. He wished he listened to Lily. Lily was sad. She missed her friend. She felt lonely. She wished Ben was okay. The next day, Ben came back. He was better. He hugged Lily. He said he was sorry. "I'm sorry, Lily," he said. "You were right. I was wrong. I should have listened to you. You are a good friend." Lily smiled. She forgave Ben. She was happy. She said he was a good friend too. They played together again. They did not touch the wine anymore. They learned their lesson. They learned to respect each other and to be careful.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the garden. He saw many plants and things to look at. He found a big, round onion in the dirt. The onion was harsh and smelled funny. Tim picked it up and took it to his mom. "Mom, look!" said Tim. "I found an onion in the garden!" Mom smiled and said, "Good job, Tim! That is a big onion. Let's wash it and cook with it." They went inside and washed the onion. They cut it and put it in a pan. The onion made their eyes water, but it smelled good when it cooked. Tim and his mom had a yummy dinner with the onion. They were happy they found the onion in the garden.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet angel. The angel had a big heart and loved to help. One day, the angel saw a little girl crying. The girl had a big cookie in her hand. The angel asked, "Why are you crying, little girl?" The girl said, "I want to share my cookie with my friend, but I don't know how to split it in two." The sweet angel smiled and said, "I can help you with that!" The angel touched the cookie, and it split in two. The girl looked surprised and happy. She gave one half to her friend, and they both ate their cookies with big smiles. But then, something unexpected happened. The angel's wings changed colors! They turned from white to rainbow. The girl and her friend were amazed. The angel said, "Your kind heart made my wings change. Thank you for sharing!" From that day on, the sweet angel with rainbow wings flew around, helping and sharing with others. And everyone knew that a kind heart can make magic happen.
