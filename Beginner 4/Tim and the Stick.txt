Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Stick

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a good boy and had a best friend named Sam. Sam was a reliable friend who always played with Tim. They loved to play in the park and find sticks to play with. One day, Tim and Sam found a big stick in the park. They wanted to play with it, but Tim's mom said, "Do not play with that stick, or you will be punished." Tim and Sam did not listen and took the stick home. Later, Tim and Sam played with the stick in the yard. They swung it around and hit things. Suddenly, the stick broke and hit Tim's mom's favorite plant. Tim's mom saw what happened and punished Tim and Sam. They were very sad and knew they should have listened to Tim's mom. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a white dog named Fluffy. Fluffy had a best friend, a rough brown cat named Whiskers. They liked to play together every day. One sunny day, Whiskers decided to tease Fluffy. He jumped out from behind a tree and said, "Boo!" Fluffy laughed and chased Whiskers around the yard. They played and teased each other all day long. When the sun went down, Fluffy and Whiskers were tired. They laid down on the soft grass and fell asleep, happy to be best friends.

One day, Mommy and Baby were walking in the park. Mommy saw a nice cactus in the garden. Mommy said, "Look at that nice cactus, Baby! Would you like me to bring it home for you?" Baby smiled and nodded eagerly. So Mommy bent down and carefully picked up the cactus. She put it in a bag and brought it home for Baby. When they arrived home, Baby was so happy to have the cactus. She hugged it tightly and gave it lots of kisses. Mommy smiled and said, "Now that's a nice cactus you have there, Baby!"

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his original toy cars. One day, he wanted to have a race with his best friend, Sam. Tim said to Sam, "Let's have a car match. My car is very fast." Sam nodded and said, "Yes, let's race. My car is fast too." They lined up their cars at the start line and got ready to race. As they raced, Tim's mom called out, "Mind the cat!" Tim and Sam looked and saw a cat sitting in the middle of their race track. They stopped their cars just in time. The cat looked at them and walked away. Tim and Sam laughed and said, "That was close! Let's race again." They went back to the start line and raced again. This time, there were no cats in the way. In the end, Tim's car won the match. They both had fun and played together all day.

One day, a little girl named Lucy got a surprise in the mail. She was very happy to see a big, blue box. The box had her name on it. Lucy was excited to open it and find out what was inside. Inside the box was a unique toy. It was a soft, fluffy bunny with one green ear and one pink ear. Lucy loved her new toy very much. She named her bunny "Rainbow" because it was so special. Lucy played with Rainbow all day. They had tea parties and played hide-and-seek. At night, Lucy and Rainbow went to bed together. They had sweet dreams about their fun day.

One day, Tom and Sue went to the park. They saw a big dog with a broken leg. The dog was sad and hungry. Tom and Sue wanted to help the dog. Tom said, "I have some meat at home. We can give it to the dog." Sue agreed, and they went to get the meat. They brought the meat back to the dog. The dog was very happy and started to eat the meat. As the dog ate, Sue tried to rub the dog's broken leg to make it feel better. But the dog did not like it. The dog got mad and bit Sue's hand. Sue cried and Tom got scared. They ran away from the dog and never went back to the park again.
