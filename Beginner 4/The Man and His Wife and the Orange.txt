Title: The Man and His Wife and the Orange

Once upon a time, there was a man and his wife. They lived in a small house near the big tree. The man and his wife loved to eat yummy food. One day, they found a big orange fruit. The fruit was round and orange, just like the sun. The man and his wife were very happy to find the orange fruit. They wanted to eat it, but they did not know how. They tried to bite it, but it was too hard. They tried to break it, but it was too strong. Finally, the man had an idea. He went to get a big rock. The man used the rock to break the orange fruit. It opened and they saw the sweet inside. The man and his wife were very excited. They sat down and began to eat the orange fruit. It was so yummy and sweet. They were very happy. From that day on, the man and his wife always looked for more orange fruits. They loved to eat them and share them with their friends. The orange fruit made everyone feel happy and full. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim got a new toy. It was a twist toy. He loved it very much. Tim wanted to show his friend, Tom. So, he went to Tom's house. At Tom's house, there was a pool. Tom and Tim liked to play in the pool. Tim said, "Look, Tom! I have a new twist toy!" Tom said, "Wow, that is cool! Can I play with it?" Tim said yes, but he told Tom to be careful. Tom took the twist toy and played with it near the pool. But then, Tom dropped the twist toy in the pool. The twist toy sank to the bottom. Tim was very sad. His new twist toy was gone. Tim and Tom could not get it back. The twist toy stayed in the pool.

Once upon a time, there was a little frog named Freddy. Freddy loved to jump all day long. One day, while jumping, he found a dry leaf. He was very curious about it. Freddy saw his friend, Sally the squirrel. He said, "Sally, look at this dry leaf I found!" Sally looked at the leaf and said, "Be careful, Freddy! That leaf has poison on it. Do not touch it." Freddy was scared. He did not want to get sick from the poison. He and Sally decided to go far away from the leaf. They jumped and played together all day long. They made sure to stay away from the poison leaf. Freddy and Sally were happy and safe.

Once upon a time, there was a big, comfortable ball. It was so soft and nice to touch. One day, the ball went on a trip to the airport. It wanted to see the big planes fly in the sky. At the airport, the ball met a little boy. The boy liked the ball very much. He wanted to play with it. So, the boy gave the ball a little kick. The ball went up, up, up into the air. It was so fun! The ball and the boy played all day long. They kicked the ball near the big planes. They laughed and had so much fun. When it was time to go home, the boy took the comfortable ball with him. They were the best of friends.

Once upon a time there was a little girl. She wanted to try to be brave and do something different. Her mom encouraged her to try something new. The girl found a razor and wanted to try shaving. She was a bit scared but her mom encouraged her to try. Soooo, she picked up the razor and held it in her hand. She took a deep breath and took the razor to her face. Little by little, she shaved her face carefully and soon enough she was done. The little girl was so proud of herself and grateful for her mom’s encouragement. She hugged her mom and thanked her for helping her to be brave. They hugged, smiled and laughed together. And they both felt happy. The End.
