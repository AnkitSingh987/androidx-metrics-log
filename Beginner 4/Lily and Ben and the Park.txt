Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Park

Lily and Ben were best friends. They liked to play with circles. They had big circles, small circles, red circles, blue circles. They made pictures with circles, rolled circles, and wore circles on their heads. One day, they found a new circle in the park. It was shiny and silver, and it had a button on it. Lily and Ben wondered what it was. "Let's talk to it," Lily said. She pressed the button and said, "Hello, circle. What are you?" The circle did not answer. It just made a beep sound. "Maybe it is shy," Ben said. He pressed the button and said, "Don't be scared, circle. We are gentle. We want to be your friends." The circle did not answer. It just made a beep sound. Lily and Ben were sad. They thought the circle did not like them. They decided to leave it alone and play with their other circles. But as they walked away, the circle started to glow. It opened up and a little green creature came out. It had big eyes, a long nose, and four arms. It looked at Lily and Ben and said, "Hello, friends. I am Zee. I come from far away. I was sleeping in my circle. Thank you for waking me up." Lily and Ben were surprised. They turned around and saw Zee. They smiled and said, "Hello, Zee. We are Lily and Ben. We come from here. We were playing with our circles. You are welcome to join us." Zee smiled and said, "Thank you, Lily and Ben. You are very kind and gentle. I like your circles. Can I play with you?" Lily and Ben nodded and said, "Yes, Zee. You can play with us. We are happy to meet you." And so, Lily and Ben and Zee became friends. They played with circles, talked, and laughed. They had a lot of fun. They learned new things from each other. And they never forgot the day they found the shiny silver circle in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved to explore. One day, she went on a walk and saw a spider. She was so excited and wanted to play with it. But her mother warned her. She said, "No! That spider can be rude. Don't touch it!" But the little girl did not listen. She stuck out her finger and the spider climbed on it. But the spider was being very rude. It started to bite her finger. The little girl cried out for her mother. Her mother came running to her. She was very angry and warned her daughter again. She said, "I told you not to touch that spider! Now, next time you see a spider, don't go near it. Be sure to listen to me!" The little girl said sorry and promised never to be so rude again. And she never went near the spiders ever again.

One day, a frog named Bob lived near a well. He loved to spring up and down, up and down. He was very good at it. All his friends liked to watch him spring. One day, a big bird named Sam saw Bob spring. Sam wanted to spring too. He tried and tried, but he could not spring. He felt frustrated. "Why can't I spring like Bob?" Sam asked. Bob said, "Don't be sad. We can try together." They tried and tried. Sam still could not spring. He felt more frustrated. Then, one day, Sam had an idea. "What if I fly up and you hold on to me?" he asked Bob. Bob thought it was a great idea. They tried it and it worked! Sam could fly and Bob could spring. Now, Bob and Sam were very happy. They played together every day. They learned that even if they were different, they could still have fun and help each other.

Once upon a time, there was a happy sun who loved to shine and melt ice. One day, the sun saw a big ice cube and wanted to help it melt. The sun shined and shined, and the ice cube started to melt. The sun felt happy and safe. Inside the ice cube, there was a nice lady who gave massages. As the ice melted, the lady came out and saw the sun. She was so happy to be free and wanted to thank the sun. She asked the sun if it wanted a massage. But the sun did not want a massage. The sun was not a person, so it could not get a massage. The sun felt sad, but the nice lady had an idea. She said, "I will give a massage to the ground, and the sun can feel happy knowing it helped me." The sun agreed, and the lady gave a massage to the ground. The sun shined and felt safe and happy again. And they all lived happily ever after.
