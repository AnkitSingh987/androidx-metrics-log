Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Dog and the Team

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Tim. Tim loved to stretch his legs and run outside. One day, Tim met a group of animals. They were a team who played together. "Hi, I'm Tim! Can I play with you?" asked the little dog. The team said, "Yes, come play with us!" They played catch and ran around. Tim was so happy to have new friends. As they played, Tim showed the team how he could stretch his legs really far. The team thought it was cool. They all had a great time together. And from that day on, Tim and his new team played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a wish. He wanted to gain a big, red ball. He saw the ball in a store, but it was too tight in the box to get out. One day, Tim met a kind man. The man asked, "What is your wish, little boy?" Tim said, "I wish to gain a big, red ball from the store. But it is too tight in the box." The kind man went to the store with Tim. They worked together to get the big, red ball out of the tight box. Tim was so happy to gain his wish. He played with his new ball all day and thanked the kind man for his help.

One day, a smart fox went to the woods. The fox was hungry and wanted to find some food. He saw a big berry bush with many red berries. The fox was very happy and said, "Yum! I can eat these berries and not be hungry anymore!" As the fox ate the berries, he saw a big fire in the woods. The fire was burning the trees and the grass. The fox was scared and said, "Oh no! The fire will burn my berry bush too! I need to save it!" The fox thought of a plan to stop the fire. The fox ran very fast to the river. He filled his mouth with water and ran back to the berry bush. He spit the water on the fire to put it out. The fox did this many times and the fire stopped. The fox was very happy and said, "I saved my berry bush! Now I can eat berries anytime I want!" The other animals in the woods were very thankful to the smart fox for stopping the fire.

One day, a bald man went to the airport. He wanted to observe big planes. He saw many planes fly up and down. The bald man was very happy. The bald man saw a big blue plane. He watched it go up in the sky. He waved to the people on the plane. They waved back to him. The bald man stayed at the airport all day. He saw many planes and met new friends. When the sun went down, he went home. He told his family about his fun day at the airport.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to play in her yard. Lily saw an adorable cat come to her. The cat was soft and had big eyes. Lily was happy to see the cat. Lily and the cat played all day. They played with a ball and ran in the grass. The sun was bright and they were having fun. But then, trouble came. A big dog saw the cat and started to chase it. Lily was scared, but she knew she had to help her new friend. She called her mom and dad. They came and chased the big dog away. The cat was safe and happy. Lily and the cat hugged, and they knew they would be best friends forever.

Once there was a statue. It was big and tall. It was made of stone and it was very old. Every day, a boy named Tom came to the statue. He came with a bag of yummy treats. Each day, he tested the statue. He put a treat near it and waited to see what would happen. One day, Tom put down a very yummy cookie. He waited and waited, but nothing happened. He tried again with a yummy piece of cake, but still nothing happened. Finally, Tom put a yummy apple right in front of the statue. Then, something amazing happened! The statue moved its arm and grabbed the apple! Tom and the statue were both very happy. From then on, the statue waited for Tom and his yummy treats every day!
