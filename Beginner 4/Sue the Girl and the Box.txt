Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Box

Once there was a girl named Sue. Sue had a doll. She loved her doll very much. One day, Sue saw her doll was hurt. She was sad. Sue called her friend Tom to come and help her. Tom came very fast. He looked at the hurt doll. Tom had an idea. He ran home to get something to help the doll. Tom came back with a big box. Sue was surprised. Tom opened the box. Inside was a new doll that looked just like the hurt one. Sue was so happy. They played with the new doll and had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was just three years old and liked to play outside. One day, Tom was running around in the backyard when he tripped and fell. He started to cry and called for help. His mom heard him and ran to his side. “It’s okay, Tom. Show me what happened,” his mom said. Tom pointed to his leg and said, “I speared it on this bad stick.” His mom saw that Tom had stepped on a very sharp stick. “Oh no! Tom, you shouldn’t speed around like that,” his mom said. Tom nodded and understood that he had to slow down. He said sorry and his mom kissed his cheek. They hugged and Tom ran off. From then on, Tom was careful and played slower, so he wasn’t hurt again. The End.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little frog named Hoppy. Hoppy loved to hop all day long. He hopped on rocks, he hopped on logs, and he hopped on leaves. One day, Hoppy met a big tree with a face. The tree said, "Little frog, you should not hop on me. I have a big palm that can hurt you." But Hoppy was ignorant and did not listen to the big tree. He hopped on the tree's palm anyway. The tree's palm closed and Hoppy was stuck. He cried, "Help me, please!" But no one came to help him. In the end, Hoppy could not hop anymore. The moral of the story is to listen to others and not be ignorant.

One day, a curious little girl named Lily went for a walk in the park. She saw a big tree and wanted to see what was behind it. As she walked around the tree, she found a small, soft figure laying on the ground. It was a cute, lost toy bunny. Lily picked up the toy bunny and looked at it. She felt sad for the bunny because it was all alone. She thought, "I will take the bunny with me and find its home." So, Lily held the bunny tight and walked around the park, looking for the bunny's home. As she walked, she saw a little boy crying. He was looking for his lost toy bunny. Lily went up to the boy and showed him the bunny she found. The boy's face lit up with a big smile. He hugged Lily and thanked her for finding his bunny. The two of them became friends and played together in the park with the toy bunny.

Once there was a little boy who liked ice. He always asked his mom for some ice but his mom said no. One day, the little boy saw a broken ice machine. He couldn't resist it. He urged his mom to let him get some ice. But his mom said no. The little boy started to cry. He really wanted the ice. His mom hugged him and said she would get some for him. She took him to the store and they got a big bag of ice. The little boy was so happy. He thanked his mom. Every time he saw a broken ice machine, he would remember this special moment and urge his mom to get him some.

Once upon a time in a small town, there was a chubby cat named Tom. Tom lived near a mill. He liked to play with his friends all day long. The mill was their favorite place to play. One day, Tom and his friends had a race. They ran very fast. But Tom got tired and wanted to quit. His friends told him not to quit. They said, "If you quit, you will not win the race." Tom was sad, but he did not quit. He kept running. In the end, Tom did not win the race, but he was very happy. He learned that it is important not to quit, even when things are hard. Tom and his friends played at the mill every day, and Tom never quit again. The moral of the story is never give up, even when things are tough.
