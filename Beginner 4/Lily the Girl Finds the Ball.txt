Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl Finds the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a favorite toy. It was a smooth, plastic ball. She loved to play with it every day. One day, Lily went to the park with her mommy. She took her smooth, plastic ball with her. Lily played with the ball, and it made her very happy. She threw it up and down, and rolled it on the grass. But then, Lily lost her ball. She looked and looked, but she could not find it. Lily was very sad. Her mommy helped her look for the ball. They looked under the trees and behind the bushes. Finally, they found the smooth, plastic ball near the swings. Lily was so happy to have her ball back, and she hugged her mommy. They went home with big smiles on their faces.

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play with their toys. They had many toys, but their favorite ones were the cars and the dolls. They had a big box where they kept their toys, and they had labels on the box to show whose toys were whose. Tom's label was blue and Lily's label was pink. One day, Tom wanted to play with Lily's dolls, but Lily did not want to share. She said they were her dolls and Tom had to play with his cars. Tom was angry and said he did not like cars, he liked dolls. He tried to grab a doll from Lily, but Lily pulled it back. They started to fight over the doll, and they made a lot of noise. Their mom heard them and came to see what was wrong. She saw them fighting and scolded them. She said they had to share their toys and be nice to each other. She said they had labels on the box to remind them whose toys were whose, and they had to respect that. She said they had brilliant toys and they should be happy and grateful. Tom and Lily felt ashamed and said they were sorry. They hugged each other and said they loved each other. They decided to play together and share their toys. They took out some cars and some dolls and made a big story with them. They had fun and laughed a lot. But then, something bad happened. Their dog, Spot, came into the room and saw the toys. He thought they were his toys and he wanted to play with them. He jumped on the box and knocked it over. He grabbed some cars and some dolls and ran away with them. He chewed them and tore them and made them dirty. Tom and Lily saw what he did and cried. They ran after him and tried to get their toys back, but it was too late. Their toys were ruined and they could not play with them anymore. They were very sad and angry. They wished they had not fought over the doll and had put their toys away. They learned a hard lesson that day.

Lisa and Tom are playing in the garden. They are running, jumping and laughing. They are having fun. But then the sky turns dark and it starts to rain. Lisa and Tom run inside the house. They are wet and cold. "Mommy, mommy, we are wet and cold!" they say. Mommy smiles and hugs them. She says, "Don't worry, my loves. I have something for you. Come with me." Mommy takes them to the living room. She has a big fire in the fireplace. She also has two blankets. One is pink and one is blue. She gives Lisa the pink blanket and Tom the blue blanket. She says, "Here you go. These are your blankets. They are warm and dry. You can wrap them around you and sit by the fire. You will feel better soon." Lisa and Tom say, "Thank you, mommy. We love you." They wrap the blankets around them and sit by the fire. They feel warm and dry. They watch the fire and listen to the rain. They start to relax. They are happy and cozy. They close their eyes and fall asleep. Mommy smiles and kisses them. She says, "Good night, my loves. Sweet dreams."
