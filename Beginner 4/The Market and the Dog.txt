Category: Beginner
Title: The Market and the Dog

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a market. In this market, people sold many things. One day, a little girl named Mia went to the market with her mom. They wanted to buy some rare red apples. At the market, Mia saw a big dog. She wanted to pet it. Her mom warned her, "Be careful, Mia. The dog might not be friendly." Mia listened to her mom and did not pet the dog. Then, they found a man selling the rare red apples. Mia's mom asked, "How much for the apples?" The man said, "Two coins for one apple." So, Mia's mom bought two apples for Mia and her. They went home happy, and Mia learned to be careful with dogs.

Once upon a time, there was a yellow bird. The bird had a gift for his friend, a little girl. The gift was in a pretty box with a big bow. The bird wanted to give the gift to the girl on her birthday. The bird went to the girl's house. He saw that her toy car was broken. The bird wanted to help, so he said, "I will fix your toy car!" The girl was happy and said, "Thank you, bird!" The bird tried to fix the toy car, but he did not know how to fix it. The bird tried and tried, but the toy car did not work. The girl was sad because her toy car was still broken. The bird gave the girl the gift, but she was still sad. The bird felt bad because he could not fix the toy car. In the end, the girl's birthday was not a happy day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a magic book with many spells. One day, she found a naughty spell in the book. The spell could make her toys come to life. Lily wanted to try the naughty spell, so she said the magic words. Suddenly, her toys came to life! But they were not nice toys. They were mean and made a big mess in her room. Lily started to cry. Lily knew she had to fix the mess. She found a good spell in the magic book. She said the magic words, and the toys went back to being normal. Lily was happy again, and she promised never to use the naughty spell again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was a regular girl who liked to play with her toys and run in the park. One day, she saw a little puppy in her yard. The puppy had soft fur and a wagging tail. Lily liked the puppy and wanted to be friends. Lily went outside to play with the puppy. They played with a ball and ran around the yard. The puppy was very happy and so was Lily. They played until they were tired. Then, Lily gave the puppy a big hug. The puppy licked her face and wagged its tail. From that day on, Lily and the puppy were best friends. They played together every day and had lots of fun. Lily always gave the puppy a hug when they were done playing. And the puppy always licked her face to say thank you. They were very happy and loved each other very much.

One day, a zebra named Zoe was walking in the mild sun. She saw a big tree with yummy leaves. Zoe wanted to eat the leaves, but they were too high up. She felt sad. A tall giraffe named Gary saw Zoe and asked, "Why are you sad, Zoe?" Zoe said, "I want to eat the leaves, but I can't reach them." Gary smiled and said, "I can help you, Zoe!" Gary used his long neck to reach the leaves and gave them to Zoe. They became good friends and played all day. They did not ignore each other, but shared and had fun together.

Once upon a time, in a big city, there was a wide subway. Many people used the subway to go to work, school, and play. In the subway, a little boy named Tom met an old man. The old man looked sad and tired. Tom asked the old man, "Why are you sad?" The old man said, "I lost my way to my son's house. I don't know how to use the subway." Tom wanted to help the old man. He said, "I know the subway very well. I can help you find your son's house." Tom and the old man went on the subway together. They talked and laughed. The old man was happy. When they got to the son's house, the old man said, "Thank you, Tom. You are a good boy." Tom felt good because he helped someone. The moral of the story is to help others when they need it. When you help others, you gain happiness and make new friends.
