Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Quiet Girl and the Blanket

Once upon a time, there was a quiet little girl named Lily. She had a soft, warm blanket that she loved very much. Lily took her blanket everywhere she went. One day, Lily went to the park with her blanket. She saw her friend Tom playing with some rocks. "Hi Tom!" said Lily. "What are you doing?" Tom replied, "I am counting the rocks. Want to help?" Lily nodded, and they started counting together. One, two, three, four, five. They had so much fun! After they counted all the rocks, Lily and Tom sat on the blanket to rest. They talked and laughed. Soon, it was time to go home. Lily and Tom said goodbye and went to their homes. That night, Lily's mom asked her, "Did you have fun at the park?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, Mommy. I learned that counting is fun and sharing with friends is even better!" From that day on, Lily always shared her toys and blanket with her friends, and they all had fun together.

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk with her mom. They saw many cars in the street. There was a lot of traffic. Mia held her mom's hand tight as they waited to cross the road. Mia saw a big, green frog on the sidewalk. She wanted to pick it up and squeeze it. But her mom said, "No, Mia. We don't squeeze frogs. They might be scared." Just then, a boy named Tim walked by with a big, red balloon. Mia was envious of Tim's balloon. She wanted one too. Tim saw Mia looking at his balloon and said, "Do you want to trade? I'll give you my balloon if you let me hold the frog." Mia thought about it for a moment and then said, "Okay!" She gave Tim the frog and he gave her the balloon. They were both happy with their new things. But then, the frog jumped out of Tim's hands and onto the balloon. The balloon popped with a loud bang! Mia, Tim, and the frog were all very surprised. They laughed and decided to play together, without squeezing frogs or holding balloons.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved waffles. Every morning, he would eat a yummy waffle for breakfast. One day, when Tim was eating his waffle, a small piece fell on the floor. Tim was a very careful boy. He did not want to step on the waffle piece. So, he picked it up and put it on a plate. He wanted to return the waffle piece to the kitchen. Tim walked slowly and carefully, so he would not drop it. When Tim got to the kitchen, he saw his mom. She was making more waffles. Tim gave the waffle piece to his mom. She was happy that he was so careful. She gave him a big hug and a new waffle to eat. Tim was very happy, and they ate waffles together.

Anna likes to ride her lizard. Her lizard is big and green and has a long tail. She calls her lizard Lizzy. Lizzy is very fast and can run on rocks and sand. Anna likes to hold on to Lizzy's neck and feel the wind in her hair. One day, Anna and Lizzy go to the park. They see many other children and animals. They see dogs and cats and birds and fish. They see swings and slides and balls and bikes. Anna wants to play with them, but Lizzy is shy. Lizzy does not like loud noises and strange smells. Lizzy hides behind a tree and hisses. "Come on, Lizzy, don't be scared," Anna says. "They are nice. They want to be our friends. Look, that boy has a bike. He can ride it like I ride you. Maybe we can race him." But Lizzy does not listen. Lizzy is afraid of the bike. The bike has wheels and pedals and a bell. The bike makes a lot of noise and moves very fast. Lizzy thinks the bike is a monster. Lizzy growls and shows his teeth. The boy sees Lizzy and stops his bike. He is curious and brave. He wants to see the lizard. He walks towards Lizzy and says, "Hi, I'm Ben. What's your name? What's that thing you have? Is it a lizard? Can I touch it?" Anna smiles and says, "Hi, Ben. I'm Anna. This is Lizzy. He is my lizard. He is very nice, but he is shy. He does not like bikes. He likes to ride with me. Do you want to ride with us?" Ben nods and says, "Yes, I do. That sounds fun. Can I ride on Lizzy's tail?" Anna says, "Sure, you can. But be gentle. Lizzy is not used to having two riders. And don't pull his tail. He does not like that." Ben says, "OK, I won't. I'll be nice to Lizzy. Maybe he will like me too." Anna and Ben climb on Lizzy's back. Anna holds on to Lizzy's neck and Ben holds on to Lizzy's tail. Lizzy feels their weight and their warmth. He feels their kindness and their excitement. He decides to give them a chance. He opens his eyes and his mouth and makes a happy sound. He runs out of the tree and into the open. He runs faster and faster, making Anna and Ben laugh and scream. He runs around the park, past the dogs and cats and birds and fish, past the swings and slides and balls and bikes. He runs with joy and freedom. He runs with his new friends.
