Category: Beginner
Title: Bobby and the Trust

Bobby and his mom were walking outside on a cold, icy day. Bobby saw a note on the ground, and his mom said, "Let's pick it up and read it!" Bobby picked up the note and read it out loud. It said, "I want you to trust me even when I am far away." Bobby didn't know what it meant and he asked his mom. She replied, "It means that even when we are not together, you can trust me. I will always be there for you." Bobby nodded and said, "I trust you, Mommy." Mom smiled and told Bobby, "Sometimes in life, you will meet people who you might want to trust, but you don't know if you can. When you are unsure, just remember my message, and trust your heart." As they walked back home, Bobby thought about the note and what it meant for him. He knew that he could trust his mom and that he should trust his own heart.

One day, a little fish named Fin was swimming in the ocean. He saw a big, silly octopus named Otto. Otto was trying to wrap his long arms around a big rock. Fin swam over to Otto and asked, "What are you doing?" Otto said, "I am trying to wrap my arms around this big rock. I want to move it to my home, but it is too heavy." Fin wanted to help Otto, so they tried to move the rock together. They pushed and pulled, but the rock did not move. Fin had an idea. He swam around the ocean and found many friends to help. They all pushed and pulled together. Finally, the rock moved! Otto was so happy and thanked Fin and all his friends. From that day on, Fin and Otto had many fun adventures in the ocean.

One day, a red bird was in a tree. The bird loved to sing songs. It was a sunny day, and the bird was happy. A little girl named Sue walked by the tree. She heard the bird's song and looked up. "Hello, red bird!" Sue said. "I like your song!" The red bird was happy to hear this. It tried to sing another song for Sue, but it could not. The bird was sad. Sue saw the bird was sad and said, "Don't worry, red bird. We can find a way to help you sing again." Just then, a big wind blew by and shook the tree. A small bug fell out of the tree and landed near Sue. The red bird saw the bug and ate it. Suddenly, the bird could sing again! It managed to sing a new song, even better than before. Sue and the red bird were both very happy. They laughed and sang together. From that day on, they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a map. He wanted to find a big treasure. He took the map and went on a long walk. On his walk, Tim met a dog. The dog was not nice. It was a stupid dog. The dog tried to bite Tim's map. Tim was scared, but he did not let the dog bite the map. He ran away from the dog. Tim kept walking and found the treasure. It was a big box. He opened the box, and inside was a toy dog. The toy dog was nice, not like the stupid dog. Tim was happy. He knew the map was special and it helped him find the treasure.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went on a journey. They wanted to go to the park to play. They walked and walked, holding hands and wagging tails. When they got to the park, they saw a big, scary cat. Tim and Spot were scared, but they wanted to be brave. They walked up to the cat and said, "Hello, cat. We want to play. Can we be friends?" The cat looked at Tim and Spot and said, "Yes, we can be friends." So, they all played together in the park. They had so much fun on their journey. They were not scared anymore. They were very happy.
