Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Box

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tom. Tom loved to fold paper into fun shapes. He made birds, boats, and hats. Tom also liked to play hide and seek with his friends. One day, Tom found a hidden box under his bed. He opened the box and found some soap. Tom had an idea. He took the soap and went outside to play with his friends. They blew bubbles with the soap and had lots of fun. After playing, Tom's hands were dirty. He remembered the soap he found and used it to wash his hands. His friends saw him and did the same. They all had clean hands and felt good about it. Tom learned that it is important to keep clean and share with friends. He shared the soap with his friends and they all stayed clean and happy. The moral of the story is to always be clean and share with others.

Once upon a time, there was a smooth cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play with a soft ball. She would run and jump all around the house with the ball. Her motion was very fast and fun to watch. One day, Fluffy saw a big dog named Spot. Spot saw Fluffy's ball and wanted to play too. But, Spot did not ask Fluffy if he could play. Instead, he did a bad thing. Spot tried to steal the ball from Fluffy. Fluffy did not like that. She told Spot, "That is not nice! You should ask before you take my ball." Spot felt sorry and said, "I am sorry, Fluffy. Can we play together?" Fluffy agreed, and they both played with the ball, having lots of fun. From that day on, Spot never tried to steal again, and they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a rabbit called Bob. Bob was very scared and he wanted to find somewhere safe to hide. He noticed a dark cave and decided to go inside. Just as Bob was about to enter the cave, a group of staff appeared. They were wearing uniforms and carrying sticks. Bob was very scared but he stayed hidden. One of the staff members said: "We must release the animal back into the wild!" But the others disagreed. Bob was so scared that he started to tremble. He knew that they wanted to catch him and make him go away. They started to come closer, so Bob ran away as fast as he could. But he was too late. The staff caught up with him and released him into the wild. Bob was very scared and never found a safe place to stay. Instead, he lived in fear until the end of his days.

Sara and Ben like to play in the park. They have a big box of toys that they share. Sara likes to play with the dolls and Ben likes to play with the cars. One day, they find a shiny stone in the grass. It is smooth and round and sparkles in the sun. "Wow, look at this stone!" Sara says. "It is so pretty! Can I have it?" "No, I saw it first!" Ben says. "It is mine! Give it to me!" They both want the stone and they start to pull and tug on it. They are not happy and they are not nice to each other. "Stop it, you two!" Mom says. She sees them fighting and she is not happy either. "Why are you fighting over a stone? You have so many toys to play with. Why don't you share the stone or find another one?" "But this is the best stone ever!" Sara and Ben say. "We don't want to share it or find another one!" Mom thinks for a moment. She has an idea. She picks up a dirty stone from the ground. It is rough and brown and has mud on it. "Look, I found another stone!" Mom says. "It is just as good as the shiny one. Why don't you replace the shiny stone with this one? Then you can both have a stone and be happy." Sara and Ben look at the dirty stone. They do not like it. It is not pretty or smooth or sparkly. They do not want to replace the shiny stone with it. "Yuck, that stone is dirty!" Sara says. "I don't want it!" "Me neither!" Ben says. "It is ugly!" They look at each other. They look at the shiny stone. They look at Mom. They feel sorry for fighting. They have an idea. "Mom, we are sorry for fighting," Sara says. "We don't want to replace the shiny stone with the dirty one. We want to share it." "Yes, Mom, we are sorry too," Ben says. "We want to play nicely with each other and with our toys. Can we share the shiny stone and put it in our box?" Mom smiles. She is happy that Sara and Ben are sorry and that they want to share. She gives them a hug and a kiss. "Of course you can share the shiny stone and put it in your box," Mom says. "That is a very good idea. I am proud of you for being kind and generous. Now, let's go and have some fun in the park." Sara and Ben smile. They are happy too. They put the shiny stone in their box and they play with their toys. They take turns holding the shiny stone and admiring it. They are nice to each other and to Mom. They have a lot of fun in the park.
