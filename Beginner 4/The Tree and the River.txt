Category: Beginner
Title: The Tree and the River

Once upon a time, there was a big tree. The tree had thick branches. Birds lived in the tree. They sang songs every day. One hot day, the birds were thirsty. They needed water. They flew around to find water. They saw a river far away. The river had a lot of water. The birds told the tree about the river. The tree wanted to help. It used its roots to support the birds. The roots grew long and reached the river. They brought water back to the birds. The birds were happy and sang more songs. The tree and the birds lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw many pretty flowers and trees. She was very happy. As she walked, she noticed a small hole in the ground. She wondered what could be inside. Lily got closer to the hole and looked inside. She saw a tiny upset mouse. The mouse was sad because it was lost and could not find its way home. Lily wanted to help the mouse. Lily picked up the little mouse and walked around. They saw a tree with a small hole in it. The mouse was so happy! It was its home! Lily put the mouse back in its home, and the mouse was not upset anymore. Lily felt good about helping her new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a fish named Fin. He lived in a big reef with many other fish. Fin had a best friend named Bubbles. Bubbles was a very loyal friend. They liked to play together and swim in the reef. One day, a big fish named Sharky came to the reef. Sharky was not nice. He told Fin and Bubbles, "I know a fun place! You should come with me!" Fin and Bubbles were not sure, but they wanted to see the fun place. So they decided to follow Sharky. As they swam away from the reef, the water got darker and colder. Fin and Bubbles were scared. Sharky said, "Don't worry! We are almost there!" But when they got to the fun place, it was not fun at all. It was a trap! Sharky wanted to eat Fin and Bubbles. They tried to swim away, but it was too late. Sharky ate them up, and they were never seen again.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play in the park with their mom. One day, they see a big truck with many things on it. The truck says "Garage Sale". "Mom, can we look at the things?" Lily asks. "Sure, but we have to be careful. Some things might be broken or dirty," Mom says. Lily and Ben run to the truck. They see toys, books, clothes, and more. They touch and pick up some things. Lily finds a doll with a pink dress. Ben finds a horn that makes a loud noise. "Look, Mom, a doll!" Lily says. "Look, Mom, a horn!" Ben says. They show their mom what they found. Mom smiles. "Those are nice things. Do you want to buy them?" Mom asks. Lily and Ben nod. They are happy. They want to play with their doll and horn. Mom takes out some money from her purse. She gives some to Lily and some to Ben. "Here, you can pay for your things. They are very cheap. Only one dollar each," Mom says. Lily and Ben take the money. They go to the man who owns the truck. He is sitting on a chair. "Hello, little ones. What do you have there?" the man asks. Lily and Ben show him their doll and horn. "We want to buy these, please," Lily says. "Sure, sure. That's one dollar for the doll and one dollar for the horn. Here you go," the man says. He takes the money and gives them a bag. "Thank you, sir," Ben says. "Thank you, sir," Lily says. They put their things in the bag. They wave goodbye to the man. They run back to their mom. "Mom, we bought our things. They are very cheap. Only one dollar each," Lily says. "Mom, we bought our things. They are very cheap. Only one dollar each," Ben says. They show their mom their doll and horn. Mom hugs them. "That's great, my loves. You are very smart. You discovered some nice things at the garage sale," Mom says. Lily and Ben smile. They are proud. They discovered some nice things at the garage sale. They can't wait to play with their doll and horn.
