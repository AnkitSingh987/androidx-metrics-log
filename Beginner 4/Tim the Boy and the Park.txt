Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Park

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a pretty sunset in the sky. The colors were pink, orange, and yellow. Tim felt happy looking at the sunset. Tim saw a big tree with tasty red apples. He picked one and took a bite. "Yum!" said Tim, "I want to share this with my friend, Sam." So, Tim walked to Sam's house with the apple. At Sam's house, they played a game. They took turns trying to slap each other's hands. "Ha, I got you!" said Sam. Tim laughed and said, "You are fast, but I will get you next time!" They played until the sunset was gone and it was time for Tim to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a little lizard. The lizard liked to stay in the sun. The sun made him feel warm and happy. He had a serious face, but he was a nice lizard. One day, the little lizard saw a big ball. He wanted to play with the ball. He pushed the ball with his nose. The ball rolled and rolled. The lizard laughed and chased the ball. The little lizard and the ball became good friends. They played together every day. When the sun went down, the lizard and the ball would stay close to each other. They were very happy and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, a little dog and a big cat were friends. They liked to play together. One day, they found a ball in the park. The cat said, "Let's pass the ball to each other!" The dog was very patient. He waited for the cat to pass the ball. The cat passed the ball with a gentle push. The dog caught the ball with his neck. He was so happy. He said, "Good job, cat!" They played together all day. They passed the ball back and forth, laughing and having fun. When the sun went down, they went home. They knew they would play together again soon.

Lily and Ben were playing with their dolls in the garden. They liked to pretend that their dolls were their babies and they had to take care of them. Lily had a pink doll and Ben had a blue doll. "Shh, my baby is sleepy," Lily said. "I have to rock her to sleep." She held her doll in her arms and moved it gently back and forth. She sang a soft song to her doll. "My baby is sleepy too," Ben said. "But he likes to nap in the sun." He laid his doll on a blanket and covered him with a hat. He smiled at his doll. Lily and Ben looked at the sky. They saw a big white bird flying high. It had long wings and a long neck. It looked graceful and beautiful. "Wow, look at that bird!" Lily said. "What is it?" "I think it is a swan," Ben said. "It lives in the water and eats fish." "I wish I could fly like a swan," Lily said. "It must be fun." "Me too," Ben said. "But we can't fly. We are not birds. We are kids." Lily and Ben nodded. They were happy to be kids. They loved their dolls and their garden. They watched the swan until it disappeared behind the trees. Then they closed their eyes and took a nap too.

Once upon a time, there was a farmer. The farmer was mighty and strong. He lived on a big farm with many animals. He had cows, pigs, and chickens. The farmer loved all his animals and took good care of them. One sunny day, the farmer went to the park with his animals. The park had a big slide. The farmer wanted to play on the slide with his animals. So, they all went to the slide and took turns sliding down. The farmer was so mighty that he could slide very fast. After playing on the slide, the farmer and his animals were very happy. They had so much fun together. The farmer knew that he was very lucky to have his animals as friends. They all went back to the farm and had a big party to celebrate their fun day at the park.
