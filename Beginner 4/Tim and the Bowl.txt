Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Bowl

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. It was a dark day with big clouds in the sky. Tim saw a bowl on the ground. He picked it up and looked at it. It was a pretty bowl with a picture of a cat on it. Just then, it started to rain. Tim felt the rain on his face and laughed. He held the bowl out to catch the rain. The rain filled the bowl with water. Tim's friend, Sally, came outside too. She saw Tim with the bowl and asked, "What are you doing with that bowl, Tim?" Tim smiled and said, "I am catching rain with my bowl. Look, it has a cat on it!" Sally laughed and said, "That's a fun game! Can I play too?" Tim nodded, and they both played in the rain with their bowls. They had a great time, even though it was a dark and rainy day.

Fiona was going to have a special playdate with her friends. She was very excited and asked her mum if she could invite them. Mum said yes and Fiona wrote the invitations right away. On the day of the playdate, Fiona and her friends were very happy. They ran around and laughed together until it started to get dark. But outside, they could see the mist coming. Mum said they had to go home now because of the mist. Fiona was sad and said sorry to her friends. She wanted them to stay and play. Mum promised that they would have another playdate soon. Fiona was very excited and smiled. She promised to invite her friends again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He wanted to go for a ride in his car. But when he looked across the parking lot, he noticed his car had a flat tire. He was so sad, he wanted to drive his car everywhere. Then Tim remembered his friend, the mechanic. He was very lucky to have a friend who knew how to fix things. Tim asked his friend the mechanic to help him out and his friend said yes! The mechanic worked hard and fixed the flat tire. He was so good. Tim thanked him with a big hug. Then the mechanic gave him a tip. He told him to always wipe the car with a cloth and clean it every week. Then Tim would never need a mechanic again! He listened to his friend and gave the car a big wipe with a cloth and it looked so sparkly clean! Tim was so lucky to have such a good friend, and his car always looked so shiny and new!

Tom and Anna are twins. They like to play with their toys and watch cartoons. But on Sunday, they have to go to church with Mom and Dad. Church is a big house where people sing and pray and listen to a man talk. One Sunday, Tom and Anna are very excited. They have new toys from Grandma. They want to play with them all day. They forget about church. They do not hear Mom and Dad call them. They do not see them leave the house. Mom and Dad are in the car. They are almost at church. They look at the back seat. They do not see Tom and Anna. They are shocked. They forgot their twins at home! They turn the car around. They drive very fast. They hope Tom and Anna are safe. They are very sorry. They are not patient. Tom and Anna hear a loud noise. They look out the window. They see Mom and Dad's car. They run to the door. They open it. They see Mom and Dad. They are angry and scared. "Where were you?" Mom asks. "We forgot you at home!" "We are sorry, Mom," Tom and Anna say. "We forgot about church. We were playing with our toys." Mom and Dad hug them. They are glad they are okay. They are not angry anymore. They are patient. "Let's go to church now," Dad says. "We can play with your toys later." Tom and Anna nod. They hold Mom and Dad's hands. They go to the car. They go to church. They do not forget again.
