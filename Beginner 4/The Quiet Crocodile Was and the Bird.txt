Category: Beginner
Title: The Quiet Crocodile Was and the Bird

One day, a quiet crocodile was near the water. He wanted to soak in the sun. He laid on the warm sand and closed his eyes. He felt happy and warm. A little bird came by and saw the crocodile. The bird said, "Hello, Mr. Crocodile! Why are you so quiet?" The crocodile opened his eyes and said, "I am soaking in the sun. I like to be quiet when I do that." The bird wanted to play, so she thought of a fun game. She found a big hat and put it on the crocodile's head. The crocodile did not see it coming, and when he felt the hat, he was surprised! He laughed and said, "You got me, little bird! Let's play together." They played and had fun all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. He had a small, pretty ornament. The ornament was round and shiny. Tom loved it very much. One day, Tom was playing with his ornament outside. Suddenly, a big wind came and blew it away. Tom was very sad. He looked and looked, but he could not find it. He went home and told his mom about it. His mom gave him a magic band-aid. She said, "This band-aid can heal things. Maybe it can help you find your ornament." Tom put the band-aid on and went outside. He found a broken toy. He put the band-aid on the toy, and it was fixed! The toy was useful again. Tom kept looking for his ornament. Then, he saw a shiny thing in a tree. He climbed the tree and found his ornament! It was stuck in a bird's nest. The bird had taken it to make her nest pretty. Tom was happy to find his ornament, but he saw that the bird loved it too. Tom decided to let the bird keep the ornament. He knew that the bird needed it more than he did. The bird was very happy, and Tom felt good about his choice. From that day on, Tom and the bird were good friends, and the magic band-aid helped them fix many more things.

One day, a little duck named Bob went for a swim in the dark pond. Bob saw the dark water and started to panic. He was scared of the dark. Bob's friend, a fish named Tim, swam up to him and said, "Don't panic, Bob! I'll help you!" As they swam, they saw some oil in the water. The oil was making the water dirty. Bob and Tim wanted to clean the pond. They thought of a plan to get the oil out. They asked their friends, the frogs, to help them. The frogs jumped in the water and pushed the oil out of the pond. The water became clean again. Bob and Tim were happy. They learned that when they work together with their friends, they can do anything. The dark pond didn't scare Bob anymore, because he had his friends to help him.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a family. The family had a mom, a dad, and a little girl named Sue. Sue had a soft, cuddly teddy bear named Mr. Bear. Sue and Mr. Bear loved to play together every day. One day, Sue asked her mom, "Can I take Mr. Bear outside to play?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful. Don't let Mr. Bear get dirty." Sue was very happy and promised to keep Mr. Bear clean. Sue and Mr. Bear went outside to play. They played on the swings and slide. Then, they saw a big mud puddle. Sue remembered her mom's words and did not permit Mr. Bear to jump in the mud. They found a soft, dry spot to sit and have a picnic instead. Sue and Mr. Bear had a fun day, and Mr. Bear stayed clean, just like Sue promised her mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named George. One day, she wanted to go for a swim in the sink. It looked so inviting and gentle. But just when she reached out to touch the water, something scared her! She quickly pulled back her hand. The sink kept shaking and soon it became louder and louder. George was so scared that she started to cry. Then she heard a voice say: "Don't be scared George, I am here!" When she looked up, there was a giant monster standing in the sink. George was so scared that she couldn't move. The monster said in a gentle voice: "I won't hurt you George. Do you want to come to my house?" George was too scared to say anything. So before she could answer, the monster picked her up and flew away. George was never seen again. All that was left was her tears in the sink.
