Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Ball and the Cat

One day, a big cat and a little dog were playing near a pitch. They were having a fun time. The big cat saw a ball on the pitch and wanted to play with it. But the little dog said, "No, we can't play with that ball. It belongs to the kids." The big cat didn't listen and decided to steal the ball. He ran to the pitch and took the ball. The little dog was angry and said, "You shouldn't have taken the ball! The kids will be sad." Later, the kids came to the pitch to play. They saw that their ball was gone and they were very sad. The little dog told the big cat, "See, I told you they would be sad. Now we have to give the ball back." The big cat felt bad and gave the ball back to the kids. The kids were happy again and continued to play. The big cat and little dog learned not to take things that don't belong to them.

One day, a little dog named Max was very excited. He was going to the park with his friends. They were going to play, run, and have fun. Max could not wait for it to happen. At the park, Max played with his friends. They ran, jumped, and chased each other. They were all very happy. After a while, they started to feel tired. Max's friends said it was time for a nap. Max and his friends lay down under a big tree. They closed their eyes and took a nice nap. When they woke up, they felt good and ready to play again. They had a great day at the park, and Max could not wait for it to happen again.

Once upon a time, there was a pink bunny. The bunny lived in a small house in the woods. The bunny loved to hop and play all day. One sunny day, the pink bunny met a new friend. The new friend was a green frog. The frog liked to jump and swim in the pond. The pink bunny and the green frog played together all day. At the end of the day, the pink bunny wanted to thank the green frog. The bunny offered the frog a mint. The green frog loved the mint. They both smiled and were very happy. From that day on, the pink bunny and the green frog were best friends.

Lily was a thin girl who liked to draw and play with her toys. She was going to school for the first time and she was scared. She did not know the other kids or the teacher. She did not want to leave her mom. "Mom, I don't want to go to school. Can I stay with you?" Lily said, holding her mom's hand. "Sweetie, I know you are scared, but school is fun. You will make new friends and learn new things. And I will be here to pick you up later. You can trust me, okay?" Mom said, giving Lily a hug. Lily nodded, but she still felt nervous. She followed her mom to the school gate, where a nice lady with a big smile greeted them. "Hello, I'm Miss Rose, your teacher. You must be Lily. I'm so happy to meet you. Come, let me show you your classroom." Miss Rose said, taking Lily's hand. Lily looked at her mom, who smiled and waved. She decided to trust Miss Rose and go with her. Maybe school was not so bad after all.

Once upon a time there was a girl who liked to wander. She was always looking for something new and exciting. One day, she decided to take a walk in the woods and see what she could find. She wandered for what seemed like forever, until she came across a little hut with smoke coming out of the chimney. She knocked on the door and was welcomed inside by an old woman. The woman offered the girl some hot spicy soup, so she sat down and ate. After she was done the old woman said she had to wait for something special to happen. She waited for a long time, until finally a fairy appeared with a special magical gift. The little girl was so excited and she thanked the old woman for her kindness. She thanked the fairy too, and she left the hut with a big smile on her face. From then on, she always remembered to be patient and to never forget to wander!
