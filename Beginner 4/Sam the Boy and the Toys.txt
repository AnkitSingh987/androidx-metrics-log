Category: Beginner
Title: Sam the Boy and the Toys

Once there was a boy named Sam. He was a very busy boy. One day, Sam was playing with his toys when his mommy said it was time for a treat. He was so excited! Sam went to the bathroom to brush his teeth with his mommy. He looked in the mirror and saw his two teeth, one on the top and one on the bottom. He was worried about losing his tooth! When Sam was finished brushing, he was so relieved. He was so happy that his teeth were still there! Sam's mommy told him to go outside and play. He ran around, had fun in the sun, and laughed all day long. Sam was no longer worried about his tooth. He knew his mommy was taking care of him and he was happy. He was glad his teeth were safe and sound.

Lila and Tom were twins who liked to play outside in the snow. They had furry mittens that kept their hands warm and cozy. One day, they decided to act like snow bears and roar at each other. They had a lot of fun pretending to be big and scary. But then, Lila saw a real snow bear in the woods. She was very scared and ran to Tom. She pointed at the bear and said, "Look, Tom, a snow bear! Let's go inside!" But Tom did not listen. He wanted to act like a snow bear too. He said, "No, Lila, I want to play with the snow bear. Maybe he is lonely and wants a friend." Tom took off his furry mitten and waved it at the snow bear. He thought the snow bear would like his mitten and come closer. But the snow bear did not like Tom's mitten. He liked Tom's hand. He growled and ran to Tom. He bit Tom's hand and made him cry. Lila screamed and ran to the house. She told Mom and Dad what happened. They called for help and took Tom to the hospital. Tom was very hurt and sad. He lost his hand and his furry mitten. He wished he had listened to Lila and not acted like a snow bear. He learned a hard lesson that day. Some things are not good to act like. Some things are better to stay away from.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a shy little girl named Amy. She had a friend named Tom. They loved to play together. One day, Tom came to Amy's house. He saw a candle on the table. "Look, Amy! A candle!" he said. Amy looked at the candle and smiled. "Yes, let's play with it!" she said. Tom and Amy started to play near the candle. But, suddenly, Amy's mom came into the room. "Stop!" she said loudly. Tom and Amy looked at her. "We must be careful with the candle. It can be hot," mom told them. They nodded and said, "Okay, mom." From that day on, Tom and Amy learned to be careful around candles. They still had fun playing together, but they always listened to mom's words. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big, heavy box that she wanted to open. The box had a knob on it. Lily tried to turn the knob, but it was too hard for her. Lily went to her friend Tom and said, "Can you help me open this heavy box?" Tom tried to turn the knob too, but it was still too hard for him. They both wanted to gain what was inside the box. Then, they saw a big, strong bear. They asked the bear, "Can you help us open this heavy box?" The bear tried to turn the knob, and it worked! The box opened, and they all gained a big pile of yummy treats. They were so happy they worked together to solve the problem.

Lily and Ben were best friends. They liked to play with balls. They had many balls of different colors and sizes. They liked to bounce them high and catch them. Sometimes they bounced them to each other. One day, they saw a big building near their park. It had a sign that said "Ball Factory". They wondered what was inside. They decided to go and see. They walked to the door and knocked. A man opened the door. He had a smile and a hat. He said, "Hello, children. Do you like balls?" "Yes, we do!" Lily and Ben said. "Would you like to see how we make them?" the man asked. "Yes, please!" Lily and Ben said. The man took them inside. He showed them machines that made balls. They saw balls of all kinds. Some were shiny, some were fluffy, some were spiky. Some made noises, some had patterns, some had faces. Lily and Ben thought they were all attractive. "Can we bounce them?" Lily asked. "Of course, you can. But be careful, some of them are very bouncy. And don't touch the hot ones, they can burn you." the man said. Lily and Ben picked some balls and bounced them. They had so much fun. They thanked the man and said goodbye. They took some balls with them as a gift. They went back to the park and bounced them some more. They were very happy.
