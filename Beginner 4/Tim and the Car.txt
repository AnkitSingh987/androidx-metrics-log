Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to play with his friends at the club. They had a lot of fun together. Tim had a toy car that he loved to turn around and around. One day, a new kid named Sam came to the club. Sam saw Tim's toy car and said, "That's a stupid car!" Tim felt sad and didn't know what to say. He just wanted to play with his friends. Tim's friend, Lily, saw that Tim was sad. She went to Sam and said, "We don't say 'stupid' here. We are nice to each other." Sam said, "I'm sorry, I didn't know." They all played together, turning their toy cars around and around, and they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a black doll that she loved very much. One day, Sue and her black doll went to the park to play. While they were playing, Sue met a boy named Tom. Tom could whistle very well. Sue wanted to learn how to whistle too. Tom said, "I can teach you how to whistle. Just pucker your lips and blow air out." Sue tried to whistle but no sound came out. She tried again and again, but still no sound. Tom said, "Don't worry, keep trying. You will learn soon." Sue kept practicing with her black doll by her side. Finally, after many tries, Sue could whistle! She was so happy. She thanked Tom for teaching her how to whistle. Sue, her black doll, and Tom played and whistled together in the park all day. They became good friends and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a very smart boy named Tom. Tom liked to draw and make things. One day, he decided to design a big, pretty house for his mom and dad. Tom took a piece of paper and a pen. He drew a big square for the house. He added a triangle on top for the roof. Then, he drew a door and some windows. Tom thought his design was very good. He wrote a note to his mom and dad that said, "I made this house for you. Love, Tom." When Tom's mom and dad saw the note and the design, they smiled. They knew their son was very intelligent. They said, "Thank you, Tom! We love your house design!" Tom was very happy that his mom and dad liked his house. They all hugged and laughed together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a sleepy cat named Tom. Tom was always tired because he had nightmares every night. He did not like the dark and scary dreams. One day, Tom met a wise old dog named Max. Tom said, "Max, I have nightmares every night. Can you help me?" Max thought for a moment and said, "I will serve you some warm milk before bed. This might help you sleep better." That night, Tom drank the warm milk that Max served him. But sadly, Tom still had a nightmare. He dreamt of a big, scary monster chasing him. Tom woke up scared and sad. The warm milk did not help him stop having nightmares, and he was still a sleepy cat.

One day, a boy named Tim and a girl named Sue went to the park. They played on the swings, the slide, and the see-saw. They were having a lot of fun. Tim and Sue wanted to play a new game, but they did not know what to play. They sat down on the grass to think and discuss. Tim said, "Let's make up a story!" Sue thought that was a great idea. They started to make up a story about a big, hard rock that could talk. The rock was friends with all the animals in the forest. The animals would sit around the rock and listen to its stories. Tim and Sue laughed as they made up funny things for the rock to say. They had a great time making up their story.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim was sad. He was hungry and wanted a sandwich. He went to his mom and said, "Mom, I am hungry. Can I have a sandwich, please?" His mom said, "Of course, Tim! I will make you a sandwich. But you must remember to say thank you." Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, mom!" Tim sat at the table, waiting for his sandwich. Suddenly, a funny thing happened. A little mouse ran into the room and took Tim's sandwich! Tim was very surprised. He looked at the mouse and said, "Hey, that's my sandwich!" The mouse looked at Tim and said, "I'm sorry, I was hungry too. Can we share the sandwich?" Tim thought for a moment and then said, "Okay, we can share. But you must remember to say thank you." The mouse smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim!" They both ate the sandwich together and became good friends. And Tim was not sad anymore.
