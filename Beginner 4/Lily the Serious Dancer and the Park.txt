Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Serious Dancer and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a serious dancer named Lily. Lily loved to dance all day. She danced at home, at school, and at the park. She was always happy when she danced. One day, Lily had to prepare for a big dance show. She practiced her dance steps every day. She wanted to be the best dancer on the stage. Her friends helped her get ready. They were very excited for the show. On the day of the dance show, Lily danced so well that everyone clapped and cheered. Her friends were proud of her. Lily learned that when you work hard and prepare, you can do great things. And that is the moral of the story: always work hard and be ready for what you love to do.

One day, Tom and Sam went to the park to play. They wanted to play with their basketball. Tom was very excited, but Sam was a little bit ignorant. He did not know how to play basketball. Tom said, "Don't worry, Sam! I will teach you how to play." Sam was happy to learn. Tom started to unpack the bag with the basketball in it. They were ready to play. But then, a big boy came and took their basketball. Tom and Sam were sad. They asked the big boy, "Please give our basketball back." The big boy laughed and said, "No! It's mine now." Tom and Sam did not know what to do. They thought and thought. Then, they decided to ask a grown-up for help. The grown-up talked to the big boy and got the basketball back for Tom and Sam. In the end, Tom and Sam were very happy. They played basketball together and Sam learned how to play. They had lots of fun at the park.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big tree with a lot of birds. Tim loved to clap his hands when he saw the birds fly. "Look, Mom! Birds!" Tim said, clapping his hands. In the park, Tim met a new friend named Sue. Sue was deaf, but she liked to play just like Tim. They played a game where they touched their shoulders and then clapped their hands. "Touch, clap!" Tim said, and Sue copied him. Tim and Sue played all day in the park. They laughed and clapped their hands together. When it was time to go home, Tim hugged Sue and said, "Bye, friend!" Sue touched her shoulder and clapped, just like in their game. Tim smiled and waved goodbye.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a medal that he loved very much. It was a shiny medal that his mom gave him for being a good boy. He wanted to store it in a safe place so no one could take it. One day, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue saw the medal and said, "Wow! That's a pretty medal! Can I see it?" Tim showed Sue his medal, and she liked it too. They talked about the medal and played together. Later, Tim wanted to store his medal, but he could not find it. He asked Sue, "Did you see my medal?" Sue said, "No, I don't know where it is." Tim looked everywhere, but he could not find his medal. He felt very sad because his medal was lost, and he could not store it in a safe place.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim and his mom went to the zoo. They saw many animals like lions, monkeys, and birds. Tim was very happy. He thought the zoo was a nice place to visit. At the zoo, Tim saw a big elephant. He asked his mom, "Can I ride the elephant, please?" His mom said, "No, Tim. It is not safe to ride the elephant. But we can watch it and take a picture." Tim decided to look at more animals. He saw a funny monkey eating a banana. Tim laughed and said, "Look, Mom! The monkey is eating a banana just like me!" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. Monkeys like bananas too." They had a great day at the zoo. When it was time to go home, Tim said, "Thank you, Mom. I had a nice time at the zoo." His mom hugged him and said, "You're welcome, Tim. I'm glad you had fun."
