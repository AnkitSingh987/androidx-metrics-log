Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Lonely Boy and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a lonely boy named Tim. One day, he went to the park to play. He took a big bag of seeds with him. Tim wanted to scatter the seeds for the birds to eat. At the park, Tim met a girl named Sue. She had a big red ball. "Hi, I'm Sue. Do you want to play with me?" she asked. Tim was happy and said, "Yes, let's play!" They played together and had lots of fun. While they were playing, a big wind came and blew the seeds away. Tim was sad. But then, something unexpected happened. The seeds grew into big, strong trees with many arms! Tim and Sue were amazed. They played in the trees and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a pretty pin with a red heart. She loved her pin and wore it every day. One day, it started to rain. Sue put on her open raincoat and went outside to play. She jumped in the puddles and laughed as the water splashed. After playing, Sue went back inside. She took off her raincoat and noticed her pin was gone. She felt sad and looked everywhere for it. She put on her raincoat again and went back outside. Sue searched in the rain and found her pin in a puddle. She was very happy! She picked up the pin, put it back on her raincoat, and went back inside. Sue learned to be careful with her things, even when having fun in the rain.

Anna loved to play with her trumpet. She liked to make loud noises and pretend she was in a band. She did not care if her trumpet bothered anyone else. She thought it was fun. One day, she took her trumpet to the park. She saw some children playing on the swings and slides. She wanted to join them, but she did not ask nicely. She just blew her trumpet in their ears and said, "Move! I want to play here!" The children did not like Anna. They felt uncomfortable and scared. They ran away from her and did not want to be her friends. Anna was sad and lonely. She did not understand why they did not like her. A kind lady saw Anna and came to talk to her. She said, "Hello, little girl. Why are you playing alone? Don't you want to make friends?" Anna said, "I do, but they don't like me. They run away from me when I play my trumpet." The lady said, "Maybe they don't like your trumpet because it is too loud and rude. It hurts their ears and makes them feel bad. If you want to make friends, you have to be nice and respectful. You have to share and take turns. You have to use your words and not your trumpet." Anna thought about what the lady said. She realized she was wrong. She said, "I'm sorry. I did not know. I will try to be nice and respectful. I will share and take turns. I will use my words and not my trumpet." The lady smiled and said, "That's good. I'm sure you will make many friends if you do that. Why don't you go and say sorry to the children and ask them to play with you?" Anna nodded and did what the lady said. She went to the children and said sorry. She asked them to play with her. She put her trumpet away and played with them on the swings and slides. She had fun and made many friends. She learned that being nice and respectful is better than being loud and rude. She learned that using her words is better than using her trumpet. She learned that playing with others is better than playing alone.

Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They had a game of tag. Lily was it and she ran after Ben. Ben was fast and he dodged her. He laughed and said, "You can't catch me, Lily!" Lily did not give up. She chased Ben around the trees and the flowers. She was getting closer and closer. Ben saw a big bush and he hid behind it. He thought he was safe. He said, "Ha ha, Lily, you lost me!" But Lily was smart. She saw Ben's foot sticking out of the bush. She sneaked up behind him and tapped his foot. She said, "Got you, Ben! You are it now!" Ben was surprised and he jumped out of the bush. He saw Lily smiling at him. He felt foolish. He said, "Oh no, Lily, you found me! How did you do that?" Lily said, "I saw your foot, Ben. You should have hidden it better. Now you have to surrender and be it. Come on, let's play some more!" Ben nodded and he said, "OK, Lily, you win. You are a good tagger. I surrender and I will be it. But watch out, I will catch you soon!" They hugged and they ran off to play some more. They had fun in the garden. They were good friends.
