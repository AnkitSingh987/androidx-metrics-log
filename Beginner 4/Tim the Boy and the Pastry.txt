Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Pastry

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat pastry. One day, he saw a big, scary house. He heard that there was a special pastry inside. Tim wanted that pastry very much. Tim went into the scary house. It was dark and he was scared, but he managed to find the pastry. The pastry was on a big table. Tim was very happy and he took the pastry. But, when Tim ate the pastry, he started to feel sick. His tummy hurt and he wanted to go home. The scary house was not fun anymore. Tim learned that some things are not worth the trouble.

Lily liked to go to the park with her mom. She liked to see the flowers and the birds and the swings. But today, the park was boring. There were no other kids to play with, and the swings were broken. Lily felt sad and angry. "Mom, can we go home?" she asked. "The park is boring." "Not yet, sweetie," her mom said. "Look, there is a butterfly. Do you see it? It is yellow and black. It is very pretty." Lily looked at the butterfly. It was pretty, but she did not care. She wanted to go home and play with her toys. She tried to catch the butterfly, but it flew away. Lily ran after it, but it was too fast. She tripped and fell on the grass. She scraped her knee and started to cry. "Ow, ow, ow!" she shouted. "Mom, help me!" Her mom ran to her and picked her up. She hugged her and kissed her knee. She said, "I'm sorry, Lily. Are you okay? You are very brave." Lily sniffed and nodded. She felt better, but she still wanted to go home. She said, "Mom, can we go home now? Please?" Her mom smiled and said, "Okay, Lily. We can go home now. But first, let's say goodbye to the butterfly. Maybe we can see it again another day." Lily looked at the butterfly. It was still flying around the flowers. She said, "Bye, butterfly. You are pretty, but you are boring. I like my toys better." The butterfly did not answer. It did not care. It liked the flowers better. Lily and her mom went home. They were happy. The end.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the gym with his mom. The gym was big and clean. Tim saw many people there. They were running, jumping, and playing with balls. Tim felt excited and wanted to play too. Tim's mom said, "Tim, do you have a question about the gym?" Tim thought for a moment and asked, "Can I play with the balls, mom?" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, you can. But first, let's find a ball for you to play with." They found a big, red ball for Tim. He started to bounce it and throw it in the air. Tim was having so much fun at the gym. He played with the ball until it was time to go home. That night, Tim slept very well, dreaming of the next time he could go to the gym and play.

One day, a boy named Tom went to the beach with his mom. He saw the big blue sea and wanted to play in the water. Tom asked his mom, "Can I play in the water, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Tom went into the water and saw a surf. He had never seen a surf before. He watched as the surf went up and down. Tom wanted to play with the surf. He jumped on the surf and rode it like a big wave. Tom was having so much fun. An honest girl named Sue saw Tom playing with the surf. She wanted to play with Tom and the surf too. So, Sue asked Tom, "Can I play with you and the surf?" Tom said, "Yes, let's play together!" They both played with the surf and had a great day at the beach.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very curious boy. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a shiny chain on the ground. Tim wanted to examine the chain, so he picked it up. Tim was very careless with the chain. He swung it around and around, making it go very fast. The chain flew out of his hand and hit a big tree. Tim felt sad that he was careless with the chain. Tim's mom saw what happened and came to help. She told Tim that it was okay, but he needed to be more careful. Tim said sorry to the tree and promised to be more careful next time. He gave the chain to his mom, and they went home together, happy and safe.
