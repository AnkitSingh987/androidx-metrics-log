Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Tall Boy and the Leaf

One day, a tall boy named Tim went to play in the park. He saw a small ant walking on the ground. Tim stopped to watch the ant for a minute. He recognized that the ant was trying to carry a big leaf, but it was too heavy. Tim wanted to help the ant, but he did not know how. He tried to pick up the leaf, but it broke. The ant was sad, and Tim felt bad. He thought and thought, and then he had an idea. Tim found a stick and put the leaf on it. The ant was able to carry the stick with the leaf. In the end, the ant was happy, and Tim felt good for helping. The other ants saw this and clapped for Tim. The unexpected thing was that the ants became friends with Tim. The moral of the story is to always try to help others, even if they are very small.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a brilliant tape in his toy box. He did not know where it came from. He started to wonder who put it there. He took the tape and went to find his friend, Sue. Tim said, "Sue, look at my brilliant tape! Do you know who put it in my toy box?" Sue looked at the tape and said, "No, I don't know. Let's ask our friends." They went to ask their friends, but no one knew where the tape came from. Tim and Sue started to wonder if a kind fairy put the tape there. Tim and Sue decided to use the tape to make pretty things. They made a big, shiny star and hung it on the wall. They were so happy with their brilliant tape. They knew that even if they did not find out who put it there, they would always remember the fun they had with the tape.

Once upon a time, there was a modest little car named Cari. Cari loved to help people. One day, a nice man needed to go to the airport. The man asked Cari, "Can you take me to the airport?" Cari said, "Yes, I can help you reach the airport!" So, the man got in Cari and they drove down the road. They saw big trees, pretty flowers, and happy birds singing. At last, they got to the airport. The man said, "Thank you, Cari, for helping me reach the airport!" Cari felt happy to help the man. Cari drove back home, ready to help more people. And they all lived happily ever after.

Lila and Ben like to play in the park. They have a big furry dog named Spot. Spot likes to run and jump and chase balls. One day, Lila and Ben find a club in the park. It is a long stick with a big end. They think it is fun to swing it and hit things. They see a big tree with many nuts. They want to get some nuts for Spot. They swing the club and hit the tree. The nuts fall down. They pick them up and put them in a bag. They are happy. But then they hear a loud noise. It is a big squirrel. The squirrel is angry. It wants the nuts. It runs to Lila and Ben and shows its teeth. It makes a scary sound. Lila and Ben are afraid. Spot sees the squirrel. He barks and runs to Lila and Ben. He wants to help them. He jumps on the squirrel and bites it. The squirrel is scared. It runs away. It yields the nuts to Lila and Ben. Lila and Ben hug Spot. They thank him for saving them. They give him some nuts. He eats them and wags his tail. He is happy. They are happy. They go home with the club and the bag of nuts. They have a good day.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Mia. Mia wanted to make a brilliant cake. She took a big bowl and started to mix all the things she needed. She mixed and mixed until it was all ready. Mia was so happy with her cake mix. Mia's mom told her to be careful when she put the cake in the oven. Mia saw a small flame in the oven. She knew the oven was hot, but she didn't know that the flame could grow big. She put the cake in the oven and waited for it to bake. After a while, Mia smelled something funny. She went to the oven and saw the flame had grown really big! But then, something unexpected happened. The flame turned into a friendly fire dragon! The dragon said, "I can help you make your brilliant cake, Mia." Mia and the dragon made the most amazing cake ever and ate it together.
