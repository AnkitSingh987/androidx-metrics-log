Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Park

One day, a little boy named Tom saw an interesting aeroplane in the sky. He wanted to fly high like the aeroplane. He asked his mom, "Can I fly like the aeroplane?" His mom said, "No, you cannot fly like the aeroplane. It is not safe." Tom felt sad and began to cry. His mom saw him cry and wanted to make him feel better. She said, "I have an idea! Let's make a paper aeroplane and play with it. That way, you can still have fun with an aeroplane." They made a colorful paper aeroplane and went to the park to play. Tom threw the paper aeroplane and it flew high in the sky. He was very happy and stopped crying. Tom and his mom played with the paper aeroplane all day long, and they had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a unique bird named Bobo. Bobo lived in a chamber in a big tree. He had many friends who were birds too. One day, Bobo wanted to learn how to sing a new song. He asked his friends to teach him. They all sang the new song together. Bobo learned the new song and was very happy. He sang the new song every day in his chamber. All his friends loved the new song too. They all sang together and had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a boat. The boat was fit, but it was too small for a family of four. So, they decided to increase it by adding a bigger bottom. One day, the family of four was in the boat together. The Dad said, "We will be fit now! The boat is big enough for all of us." The children cheered and clapped happily in excitement. The boat now had enough room for everyone to fit. They even put chairs in the boat so the children could sit and enjoy the ride. The children were so happy and excited! Their Dad said, "This is the perfect boat for us. Let's go on a fun adventure together." And so, the family sailed off together, happy and fit in their perfect boat.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play chess. Chess is a game with black and white pieces. Anna has the white pieces. Ben has the black pieces. They take turns to move their pieces on the board. One day, they play chess in the park. They find a big rock to put the board on. They are having fun. But then, a big dog comes. The dog is curious. He wants to see the chess game. He jumps on the rock. The board and the pieces fall down. They split into many parts. "Oh no!" Anna says. "The dog broke our chess game!" "Bad dog!" Ben says. "Go away!" The dog runs away. He is scared. Anna and Ben are sad. They look at the broken pieces. They cannot play chess anymore. "Maybe we can fix it," Anna says. She picks up some pieces. She tries to put them together. "Or maybe we can play something else," Ben says. He looks around. He sees a ball. He picks up the ball. He throws it to Anna. "OK," Anna says. She catches the ball. She smiles. She throws it back to Ben. They play with the ball. They forget about the chess game. They are happy again. They are still friends.

Ben and Sam were friends. They liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big hill. They wanted to go up the hill and see what was on the other side. They asked their moms if they could go. Their moms said no. The hill was too far and too steep. They said it was not safe. Ben and Sam were sad. They wanted to have fun. They waited until their moms were busy. Then they ran to the hill. They did not hear their moms call them. They did not hear the cars on the road. They did not hear the dog that barked at them. They climbed the hill. It was hard. They slipped and fell. They got dirty and hurt. But they did not stop. They wanted to see what was on the other side. They reached the top of the hill. They looked down. They saw a big lake. It was blue and shiny. They wanted to swim in the lake. They did not see the sign that said "Danger. Deep water. No swimming." They ran to the lake. They jumped in. They did not know how to swim. They did not feel the cold. They did not see the fish that bit them. They did not hear their moms scream. They sank to the bottom of the lake. They did not come up. They were sorry. They were gone.
