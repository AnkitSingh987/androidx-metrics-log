Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Ball

One day, a girl named Sue was playing in her yard. She heard a ring at the door. She ran to the door to see who it was. There was a stranger with a big bag. The stranger said he had something useful for her. Sue wanted to see what was in the bag. The stranger opened the bag and pulled out a big, red ball. Sue was so happy! She loved to play with balls. The stranger said she could have it if she did something for him. The stranger asked Sue to bring him some water. Sue went to the kitchen and filled a cup with water. When she came back, the stranger was gone! But the ball was still there. Sue was happy that the stranger left the ball for her to play with.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to stretch her arms high in the sky. One day, while playing outside, she saw something hidden under a big rock. "What's that?" she asked her friend, Tom. Tom and Lily went to the rock and moved it. They found a big bowl of salad. "Yummy!" said Tom, "I love salad!" Lily liked salad too. They decided to share it and eat it together. As they ate the salad, they laughed and talked. They were very happy. After they finished, they stretched their arms and legs. "That was a good salad," said Lily. Tom agreed, "Yes, it was!" They played together all day, feeling strong and full from the hidden salad they found.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the shop with her mom. Sue loved going to the shop because she could see all the toys and yummy treats. She held her mom's hand as they walked inside. In the shop, Sue saw a filthy teddy bear. It was dirty and had a big rip. Sue felt sad for the teddy bear. She wanted to help it. Sue asked her mom if they could take the bear home and clean it. Her mom said yes. At home, Sue and her mom washed the filthy teddy bear. They fixed the rip and made it look new again. Sue was happy she didn't miss the bear at the shop. Now, she had a new friend to play with and the teddy bear was happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a big hippo named Bob. Bob liked to play with his friends in the water. One day, Bob saw his friends wrap a big gift. It was a surprise for someone. Bob wanted to help wrap the gift too. Bob tried to wrap the gift, but he was too big. His big feet made the paper rip. Bob felt embarrassed. His friends said, "It's okay, Bob. We can fix it." They all helped fix the ripped paper and wrap the gift again. The next day, it was time for the surprise. Bob's friends gave the gift to him! Bob was so happy and shocked. He didn't know the gift was for him. Inside the gift was a big, soft towel. Now, Bob could wrap himself in the towel after playing in the water.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear named Ben. Ben had a big stomach. One day, Ben went for a walk in the woods. He took big steps as he walked. The sun was bright and the wind was harsh. As Ben walked, he met a little bunny named Bella. Bella was sad. "Why are you sad?" Ben asked. Bella said, "My tummy hurts." Ben felt sad for Bella. He wanted to help her. Ben thought for a moment. He had an idea. He told Bella, "Let's step and jump together. Maybe it will help your stomach." So, they stepped and jumped together. Bella's stomach felt better. They both smiled and played in the woods all day.

Once upon a time, there was a big orange cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play with his toy ball. One day, Fluffy saw a box with a bow on it. It was a present! Fluffy wanted to see what was inside the present. He tried to open it with his paw, but it was hard. So, Fluffy had an idea. He would drop the present from high up to make it open. Fluffy climbed up a tree with the present in his mouth. He went to the top and dropped the present. It fell down and opened! Inside was a new toy for Fluffy to play with. Fluffy was so happy and played with his new toy all day long.
