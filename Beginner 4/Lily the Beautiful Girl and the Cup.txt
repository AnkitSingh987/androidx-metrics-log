Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Beautiful Girl and the Cup

One day, a beautiful girl named Lily went outside to play. She saw a big tree and decided to climb it. When she reached the top, she found a box. "What a surprise!" she said. She was very happy and excited. She took the box down from the tree and opened it. Inside, she found a cup and a note. The note said, "Pour water in the cup and see what happens." Lily ran to her house and got some water. Lily poured the water into the cup. Suddenly, the water turned into many colorful balls! She was so surprised and happy. She played with the balls all day and shared them with her friends. The beautiful surprise made everyone smile and have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a purple cat named Dot. Dot was a very dependable cat. She always did what she said she would do. One day, Dot met a little dog named Sam. Sam was sad because he lost his toy. Dot said, "I will help you find your toy, Sam." So, Dot and Sam looked for the toy together. They looked under the big tree, behind the red house, and in the tall grass. But they could not find the toy. Then, something unexpected happened. A big bird flew down and said, "I have your toy, Sam. I took it by mistake." The big bird gave the toy back to Sam. Sam was very happy and thanked Dot for her help. Dot and Sam became good friends. The moral of the story is to always be dependable, and good things will happen.

Sara hates Tuesday. Tuesday is the day she goes to school. She does not like school. School is hard and boring. She wants to stay home and play with her toys. On Tuesday morning, Sara cries and hides under her bed. She does not want to go to school. Her mom comes to her room and says, "Sara, it is time to get up. You have to go to school. School is good for you. You can learn and make friends." Sara says, "No, mom. I hate school. School is bad for me. I do not want to learn and make friends. I want to play with my toys." Her mom says, "Sara, you cannot play with your toys all day. You have to go to school. School is fun. You can sing and draw and read. And you have a sweet teacher. She is very nice and kind. She likes you and helps you." Sara says, "No, mom. School is not fun. I do not like to sing and draw and read. And I do not like my teacher. She is not sweet. She is mean and strict. She scolds me and gives me homework." Her mom says, "Sara, that is not true. Your teacher is sweet. She is not mean and strict. She scolds you and gives you homework because she cares about you and wants you to do well. She is proud of you and your work. She gives you stickers and hugs." Sara says, "Really, mom? She is sweet? She is proud of me? She gives me stickers and hugs?" Her mom says, "Yes, Sara. She is sweet. She is proud of you. She gives you stickers and hugs. Why don't you give her a chance and see for yourself? Maybe you will like school and your teacher after all." Sara thinks for a moment. She likes stickers and hugs. She wants to make her mom and her teacher happy. She says, "Okay, mom. I will give her a chance. I will go to school and try to have fun. But I still hate Tuesday."

Once upon a time, there was a little bird who lived in the forest. He had two strong wings and was able to fly very high in the sky. One day, the bird was feeling very angry because he couldn't understand why he had to fly so far away. He saw a rabbit and asked, "Why do I have to go so far away?" The rabbit shook her head and said, "I'm sorry, I don't understand. I only hop around the forest. Why don't you ask the wise owl?" The bird flew away and found the wise owl in the tree. He asked the owl why he had to fly so far away. The owl looked at the bird thoughtfully and said, "It is because you have wings. That is why you can fly so far away. You are special and you can do things that other animals cannot do. Would you like to fly away with me?" The little bird smiled and said, "Yes, I understand now!" So the little bird and the wise owl flew away together. They had a great time and the little bird was very happy.
