Category: Beginner
Title: The Birdcage and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was an old birdcage in a big tree. Inside the birdcage, there was a pretty bird. The bird was sad because it wanted to fly and play with its friends. A nice girl named Sue found the birdcage. She saw the sad bird and wanted to help it. Sue used her hands to rub the old birdcage to make it look nice and shiny. The bird felt happy when it saw Sue. Sue opened the door of the birdcage and let the bird out. The bird was so happy, it sang a beautiful song. The bird and Sue played together and became best friends.

Tom and Lily were playing in the park with their mom. They liked to run, swing, slide and climb. They also liked to look for rocks of different shapes and colors. They had a small bucket to collect their rocks. "Look, mom, I found a big round rock!" Tom said, holding up his rock proudly. "That's a nice rock, Tom. What color is it?" mom asked. "It's gray, like an elephant," Tom said. "I found a shiny rock, mom. It sparkles in the sun," Lily said, showing her rock. "That's a beautiful rock, Lily. What color is it?" mom asked. "It's pink, like a flower," Lily said. Mom smiled and hugged them. "You both have wonderful rocks. Do you want to keep them?" "Yes, please!" Tom and Lily said. "Okay, then. Let's put them in your bucket and take them home. But first, how about a nap? You must be tired after playing so much," mom said. Tom and Lily nodded. They were sleepy. They followed mom to a bench under a tree. Mom laid a blanket on the bench and tucked them in. She kissed them on their foreheads and sang a soft song. "Mom, you are so compassionate," Lily said, snuggling close to her. "What does compassionate mean?" Tom asked, yawning. "It means mom loves us and cares for us," Lily said. "I like that word. Mom, you are compassionate, too," Tom said. "Thank you, my sweeties. I love you both very much. Now, close your eyes and nap," mom said. Tom and Lily closed their eyes and napped. They dreamed of their rocks and their mom. They felt happy and safe.

Once upon a time, there was a cat. This cat had a very long tail. The cat lived in a small house with a little girl named Sue. One day, the sun started to rise, and the cat woke up. The cat saw a big ball in the room. The cat wanted to play with the ball. So, the cat hit the ball with its long tail. The ball went up and down, and the cat was very happy. Sue saw the cat playing and said, "Cat, do you like the ball?" The cat meowed and nodded its head. Sue laughed and played with the cat and the ball until the sun went down. They had a fun day together.

One day, a boy named Tim went to mail a letter. He put the letter in a big blue box. Tim heard a weird sound from the box. He was scared, but he wanted to know what made the sound. Tim opened the box and saw a little bird. The bird was making the weird sound. Tim thought the bird was hurt. He wanted to help the bird. He took the bird out of the box and held it in his hands. Then, something unexpected happened. The bird started to talk! The bird said, "Thank you, Tim, for saving me!" Tim was very surprised. He did not know birds could talk. The bird and Tim became best friends, and they had many fun adventures together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a pretty dress that she loved to wear. One day, she saw a small, furry cat sitting on a rock. The cat was staring at a big tree. Lucy walked up to the cat and said, "Hi, furry cat! What are you looking at?" The cat looked at Lucy and said, "I am staring at the big tree. My friend, the bird, is stuck in the tree. I want to help, but I can't climb that high." Lucy thought for a moment and said, "I can help! I will ask my dad to get a ladder." Lucy ran to her dad, and he came with a ladder to help the bird. Lucy's dad climbed the ladder and saved the bird. The furry cat and the bird were very happy. They thanked Lucy and her dad for their help. Lucy learned that it is good to help others when they are in trouble. She felt proud of herself and knew that her pretty dress wasn't the most important thing. Helping her friends was what really mattered.
