Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Book

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to read books. One day, she found a big book about space. Lily was very excited to learn about stars and the moon. Lily read the book to her little brother, Max. Max listened and said, "Wow, space is so big! I want to go there one day." Lily smiled and said, "Me too, Max. We can be proud astronauts together." One day, they saw a boy who was sad. He lost his toy. Lily and Max helped him find it. The boy was happy and said, "Thank you for helping me." Lily and Max learned that helping others is a good thing to do. They were proud of themselves and kept on reading and learning about space. Moral: Helping others is a good thing to do, and we can be proud of ourselves when we do it.

The sun was shining in the sky. Two best friends, Jack and Mia, had a plan. "Let's go west," said Jack. Mia smiled, then asked, "Where's west?" "It's that way," said Jack, pointing. "But why should we go there?" Jack said, "Because it's dry in the west. We won't get too wet if it rains!" So Jack and Mia set off to the west in search of dry land. But soon after, it started to pour. But fear not, for the two friends had their plan. They ran to a nearby cave and stayed dry. Jack smiled at Mia and said, "See, I told you our plan would work!"

Once upon a time, there was a strong bear named Bobo. Bobo lived in a big forest with his friends. He was a very happy bear and liked to play all day long. One day, it was very cold outside. Bobo saw his friend, a little bunny, shivering. Bobo wanted to help his friend, so he found a warm coat. He put the coat on the bunny and the bunny felt warm and happy. Then, Bobo gave the bunny a big hug. The bunny felt safe and loved. They played together all day long, even though it was cold. And from that day on, they became the best of friends.

Lila was a very intelligent girl. She liked to learn new things and make things with her hands. One day, she found a big box of thread in her mom's closet. She wanted to make a necklace with the thread and some beads. She took some thread and a needle and started to put the beads on the thread. She chose different colors and shapes of beads. She was very careful not to poke herself with the needle. She was having fun making her necklace. But then, she ran out of thread. She looked in the box and saw that there was more thread, but it was too high for her to reach. She tried to jump and stretch, but she could not touch the thread. She felt sad and frustrated. She thought of a way to solve her problem. She saw a chair in the corner of the closet. She pushed the chair to the box and climbed on it. Now she was tall enough to reach the thread. She smiled and took some more thread. She finished her necklace and was very proud of it. She showed her mom her necklace and told her how she made it. Her mom hugged her and told her that she was a very intelligent and creative girl. Lila felt happy and loved. She wore her necklace and felt very pretty.

Once upon a time, there was a little cube. The cube was red and shiny. It loved to roll around and play with its friends. One day, the cube saw a big hill. The cube wanted to go up the hill very fast. The cube asked its friend, the ball, "Do you mind if I go up the hill fast?" The ball said, "No, I don't mind. But be careful!" The cube was very happy and started to go up the hill. It went up and up, and it was having so much fun. When the cube got to the top of the hill, it looked down. The hill was very steep! The cube was a little scared, but it remembered what the ball said. The cube went down the hill very fast. It rolled and rolled, and it was so much fun! At the bottom of the hill, the cube was so happy that it did it. It thanked the ball for minding and they played together all day.
