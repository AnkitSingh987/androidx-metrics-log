Category: Beginner
Title: Sam and the Barber

Sam liked to come with his dad to the barber. The barber was an elderly man with white hair and a big smile. He had a chair that could go up and down and a machine that made a buzzing sound. Sam liked to watch the barber cut his dad's hair and make jokes. One day, the barber asked Sam if he wanted a haircut too. Sam was scared. He did not want the machine to touch his head. He did not want his hair to fall off. He hid behind his dad and shook his head. The barber said it was okay. He said he had a special tool for Sam. He showed him a pair of scissors that looked like a bird. He said they would not hurt him. He said they would make his hair look nice. Sam was curious. He liked birds. He wanted to see the scissors. He came out from behind his dad and climbed on the chair. The barber put a cape around him and lifted the chair. He said he would only cut a little bit of his hair. He said he would make him look like a superhero. He said Sam was very brave. Sam felt the scissors snip his hair. He did not feel any pain. He heard the barber sing a song. He saw his dad smile. He felt proud. He looked in the mirror and saw his new haircut. He liked it. He said thank you to the barber. The barber gave him a sticker and a lollipop. He said he was a good boy. He said he hoped to see him again. Sam and his dad left the barber shop. Sam held his dad's hand and his lollipop. He felt happy. He said he liked the barber. He said he liked his haircut. He said he wanted to come back. His dad said he was proud of him. He said he loved him. He said they would come back soon.

Once upon a time, there was a little kitten. The kitten was very gentle and liked to play. One day, the kitten saw a big box. The box was closed. The kitten wanted to know what was in the box. The kitten tried to open the box. It pushed and pushed with its little paws. The box was very hard to open. The kitten did not give up. It tried and tried until the box was open. Inside the box, there was a big dog. The dog was not gentle like the kitten. The dog was angry because it was in the box. The dog came out of the box and chased the kitten. The kitten ran away, but the dog was too fast. The dog caught the kitten, and the kitten was very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin lived in a small tank in a big room. The tank was his home, and he was happy there. He had a toy and some rocks to play with. One day, the sun was very hot. The water in the tank started to dry. Fin began to feel scared. He knew it was dangerous to be in a tank with no water. He tried to call for help, but nobody heard him. As the day went on, the water in the tank dried up more and more. Fin could not swim anymore. He was very sad and scared. In the end, Fin could not live without water. The dangerous, dry tank was not a happy home for Fin anymore.

One day, a little girl named Sue wanted to have a parade. She asked her mom, "Can we have a parade today?" Her mom said, "Yes, but first we need to measure the space in our yard for the parade." Sue was very excited and persistent. She helped her mom measure the yard. They found a perfect spot for the parade. Sue invited her friends to the parade. They all dressed up and marched around the yard. They had so much fun! In the end, Sue learned that if you keep trying and work together, you can make fun things happen.
