Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Magic

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a magic wand that could wave and make things happen. The magic wand had a special power that only Lily knew about. One day, Lily was playing with her wand in the park. She waved it at a tree, and the tree started to dance. She waved it at a dog, and the dog started to sing. Lily was very happy with her wand's power. But then, Lily waved her wand at a boy, and the boy fell down. Lily felt sorry for the boy. She did not want to hurt him. So, she waved her wand again, and the boy stood up. He was okay. Lily said sorry to the boy and they became friends. They played together in the park and had a fun day.

Anna likes to play with yarn. She has many colors of yarn in a big basket. She can make things with yarn, like hats and scarves and dolls. She likes to pour the yarn out of the basket and see all the colors. One day, Anna is playing with yarn in her room. She pours the yarn out of the basket and makes a big pile on the floor. She picks up a red yarn and a blue yarn and starts to braid them together. She wants to make a scarf for her teddy bear. But she does not see that her cat, Milo, is watching her. Milo likes to play with yarn too. He thinks the yarn is a toy for him. He is very alert and waits for the right moment. When Anna is busy braiding, he jumps on the pile of yarn and grabs a green yarn in his mouth. He runs away with the yarn, making a long trail behind him. Anna hears a noise and looks up. She sees Milo running away with her yarn. She is not happy. She drops the red and blue yarn and runs after Milo. She shouts, "Milo, stop! That is my yarn! Give it back!" Milo does not stop. He thinks it is a game. He runs around the house, dragging the green yarn. He goes under the sofa, over the chair, around the table, and into the kitchen. He makes a big mess with the yarn. Anna follows him, trying to catch him. She goes under the sofa, over the chair, around the table, and into the kitchen. She sees the yarn everywhere. She is very mad. She says, "Milo, you are a bad cat! You ruined my yarn!" Milo hears Anna and stops. He looks at her with his big eyes. He drops the green yarn and meows. He does not understand why Anna is mad. He thinks he was just having fun. He walks to Anna and rubs his head on her leg. He purrs. Anna looks at Milo and sees his cute face. She feels a little sorry for him. She knows he did not mean to be bad. She picks him up and hugs him. She says, "Milo, I love you, but you can't play with my yarn. That is for me to make things. You can play with your own toys." She puts Milo down and gives him a ball of yarn that she does not need. She says, "Here, this is for you. You can play with this yarn. But don't make a mess, okay?" Milo takes the ball of yarn and plays with it. He is happy. He does not make a mess. He rolls the ball around and chases it. He meows. Anna smiles and goes back to her room. She picks up the red and blue yarn and continues to braid them. She finishes the scarf and puts it on her teddy bear. She is proud. She says, "Look, teddy, I made you a scarf. Do you like it?" Teddy does not answer, but he looks warm and cozy. Anna hugs him and says, "I love you, teddy. You are my best friend." The end.

Nathan was playing in the park when he met Tom. Nathan yelled, "Hi Tom! Let's play tag." Tom laughed. "No way! I don't believe in tag. What about hide and seek?" Nathan smiled. "That's a great idea!" The two boys started to play and soon Nathan was trying hard to find Tom. But he couldn't find him anywhere. Suddenly, he heard a voice. It was Tom, teasing him from up high in the sky! "I'm up a powerful tree, Nathan! You'll never find me here!" Nathan felt frustrated. He was scared of the history of height, and he didn't want Tom to tease him like that. He looked up and tried to think of a plan. Suddenly, Nathan saw a powerful rope that went all the way up the tree. He grabbed the rope, and he started to pull. Soon enough, Tom started to move down. Nathan shouted, "I'm powerful enough to bring you down!" Tom laughed and they started to play tag again. Nathan wasn't scared anymore, and the two boys had an amazing time together.
