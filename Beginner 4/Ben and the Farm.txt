Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and the Farm

Ben likes to travel with his mom and dad. They go to many places in their big car. Ben has his own gear for travel. He has a backpack with his toys, books and snacks. He also has a pillow and a blanket for naps. One day, they travel to a farm. Ben is very excited. He sees cows, pigs, chickens and horses. He hears them make noises. He wants to touch them and feed them. His mom and dad say he can, but he has to be careful and gentle. Ben has a lot of fun at the farm. He learns the names of the animals and what they eat. He also helps the farmer collect eggs and milk. He feels like a regular farmer. He tells his mom and dad he wants to live on a farm. They laugh and hug him. They say they love him and they love to travel with him.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a big jar that she wanted to fill with pennies. Every day, she would find a penny and put it in the jar. One day, Amy's friend, Tim, came over to play. He saw the jar and asked, "What is that for?" Amy said, "I am filling it with pennies. When it is full, I will buy a toy." Tim thought it was a great idea, and he wanted to help. So, they both looked for pennies together. After many days, the jar was full of pennies. Amy and Tim were very proud of their hard work. They took the jar to the store and bought a big, fun toy to play with. And they were happy, playing together with their new toy.

Lily and Tom were best friends. They liked to play in the park every day. They liked to swing, slide and run. They also liked to make up stories about the things they saw. One day, they saw a big shadow on the grass. It looked like a monster with horns and claws. Lily and Tom were scared. They ran to hide behind a tree. "Who is that?" Lily whispered. "I don't know," Tom whispered back. "Maybe it is a bad guy who wants to hurt us." They peeked from behind the tree. The shadow was still there. It moved a little bit. Lily and Tom gasped. "Let's go home," Lily said. "I don't like it here." But Tom was curious. He wanted to see what the shadow was. He said to Lily, "Don't be afraid. I have an idea. Let's pretend we are brave heroes. We can fight the monster and save the park." Lily shook her head. She did not want to fight. She wanted to go home. "Please, Lily," Tom said. "Trust me. It will be fun. And maybe the shadow is not a monster. Maybe it is something amazing." Lily looked at Tom. She saw that he was excited. She liked Tom. He was her best friend. She decided to trust him. She nodded. "Okay, Tom," she said. "Let's be brave heroes. But you go first." Tom smiled. He took Lily's hand. They walked slowly towards the shadow. They were ready to fight. But when they got closer, they saw that the shadow was not a monster. It was not a bad guy. It was a big balloon. It had horns and claws because it was shaped like a dinosaur. It was tied to a pole. The wind made it move. Lily and Tom laughed. They were not scared anymore. They thought the balloon was amazing. They ran to touch it. They played with it. They had fun. They forgot about the monster. They forgot about the shadow. They only remembered their friendship. They were happy.

One day, a little girl named Lily was eating her cereal. She liked her cereal very much. It was tasty and made her strong. Lily's mom said, "Cereal is useful for you. It helps you grow big and tall." Lily finished her cereal and went outside to play. She saw her friend, Tim, who was trying to reach a ball stuck in a tree. Tim was sad because he could not get his ball. Lily had an idea. She told Tim, "I can help you get the ball. Let me try." Lily took a big step and jumped high. But she still could not reach the ball. Then, something unexpected happened. A big bird flew down from the sky and picked up the ball with its beak. The bird dropped the ball right into Lily's hands. "Wow, thank you, bird!" said Tim. Lily and Tim were happy. They played with the ball and had lots of fun. And from that day on, Lily knew that eating her useful cereal helped her jump high and be a good friend.
