Category: Beginner
Title: Blink the Bird and the Flap

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Blink. Blink was different from the other birds because he had a wire in his nest. One day, Blink met a big bird named Flap. "Hi Flap!" said Blink. "Do you want to see my nest? I have a wire in it!" Flap looked at Blink and said, "Yes, I want to see your nest. Show me the wire!" Blink and Flap went to the nest. Blink showed Flap the wire. They played with the wire all day. They were very happy and became best friends. And from that day on, Blink and Flap always played together.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had an owner named Tom. Tom was very bossy. He always told Spot what to do. Spot did not like it when Tom was bossy. One day, Tom told Spot to close the door. Spot did not want to close the door. He wanted to play outside. So, Spot ran away from Tom. Tom was mad. He looked for Spot everywhere. Spot hid behind a big tree. He saw Tom looking for him. Spot felt sad. He did not want Tom to be mad. So, Spot went back to Tom. He closed the door like Tom said. Tom was happy. He gave Spot a big hug. Now, they both played inside the house.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there was a little bird. This little bird lived in a tree with thin branches. The bird liked to sit on the thin branches and sing happy songs. One day, the little bird saw a big red ball. The ball was on the ground. The bird wanted to play with the ball. So, the bird tried to pick up the ball with its beak. But the ball was too big! The bird had an idea. It flew up to the thin branch and tried to drop it on the ball. The branch fell on the ball, and the ball started to roll. The bird was so happy! It played with the ball all day long. And the bird sang a happy song from its tree with the thin branches.

Once upon a time, in a warm and cozy house, lived a little girl named Lily and her cat, Tom. They loved to play together and share their toys. Lily and Tom were best friends, and they always took care of each other. One day, Lily saw a big, scary dog outside her window. She was scared that the dog might hurt Tom. So, Lily said to Tom, "We need to prevent the dog from coming in." Tom meowed and nodded his head. They closed the door and all the windows to keep the dog out. After they closed everything, Lily and Tom felt safe. They hugged each other and felt the warmth of their love. They knew they could always keep each other safe. And from that day on, Lily and Tom continued to have many fun and happy days together.

Once upon a time, there was a gentle bunny who lived in the woods. The bunny liked to play with his friends, the birds and the squirrels. One day, the bunny saw some steam coming from a little house. The bunny thought it was a new friend who lived in the house. The bunny went to the house and knocked on the door. A nice old lady opened the door and said, "Hello, little bunny. I am making soup. Would you like to come in?" The bunny was happy and went inside. The old lady gave the bunny a big bowl of soup. The bunny ate the soup and felt very full. Later, the bunny felt sick. His tummy hurt a lot. The gentle bunny started to weep because he was not feeling well. The old lady was not a nice friend. The bunny got very sick and never played with his friends in the woods again. The steam from the house was a sign that the bunny should have stayed away.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He had very long hair. It would twist and turn all around his head. Tim's mom said it was time for a haircut. They went to the haircut place. A nice lady named Sue said hello. She put a cape around Tim and sat him in a big chair. Sue took out her scissors and started to cut Tim's hair. Tim watched as his hair fell to the floor. It didn't twist anymore. When Sue was done, Tim had a nice, short haircut. He smiled and said thank you. Tim and his mom went home, happy with his new haircut.
