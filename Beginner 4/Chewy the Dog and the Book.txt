Category: Beginner
Title: Chewy the Dog and the Book

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Chewy. Chewy loved to chew on things all day long. One day, Chewy found an old book with many pages. The book was a passport. It was very ancient and had many pictures. Chewy started to chew on the ancient passport. As he chewed, he saw the pictures come to life! Chewy was so surprised. He saw people and animals from long, long ago. They were all so happy and played with Chewy. But then, something unexpected happened. Chewy chewed the last page of the ancient passport, and all the people and animals went back into the book. Chewy was sad, but he learned that he should not chew on things that are not his. From that day on, Chewy only chewed on his toys and treats.

One day, a frog named Hoppy went to the pond. He wanted to have a fun day. Hoppy loved to hop and swim. The pond was his favorite place to play. Hoppy hopped into the water with a big splash. He saw a fish and thought it was interesting. The fish was very colorful. Hoppy wanted to play with the fish, so he swam closer. Hoppy and the fish swam and played all day. They had so much fun together. When the sun went down, Hoppy hopped out of the pond and said goodbye to his new friend. He couldn't wait to come back and play again.

Tom and Mia are twins. They like to play with their toys. They have a big box of toys. They have cars, dolls, balls, and a jet. The jet is their favorite toy. It is red and shiny. It can fly very fast. One day, they are playing with the jet in the living room. They make zooming noises and pretend to fly around the world. They see mountains, oceans, and animals. They have a lot of fun. "Let's go to the kitchen and get a snack," Mia says. "I'm hungry." "OK," Tom says. "But don't forget the jet. We need it for our next adventure." They run to the kitchen and find some cookies and milk. They sit on the table and eat their snack. They talk about where they want to fly next. "I want to go to the moon," Mia says. "I want to see the stars and the aliens." "I want to go to the jungle," Tom says. "I want to see the monkeys and the snakes." They finish their snack and get ready to play again. But when they go back to the living room, they can't find the jet. They look under the sofa, behind the curtains, and in the closet. They can't find it anywhere. "Where is the jet?" Mia asks. "We need it to fly." "Maybe someone took it," Tom says. "Maybe Mom or Dad or the dog." They run to the other rooms and ask their parents and the dog. But no one has seen the jet. They are sad and confused. Then they hear a crunching noise. They follow the noise and find their baby brother, Sam. Sam is sitting on the floor with the jet in his mouth. He is biting and chewing it. He has broken the wings and the tail. He looks happy. "Sam!" Tom and Mia shout. "That's our jet! Give it back!" But Sam doesn't listen. He keeps biting the jet. He likes the taste. He thinks it is a candy. Tom and Mia are very angry and sad. They try to take the jet from Sam, but he cries and holds it tight. They can't get it back. They can't fly anymore. They wish they had not left the jet in the living room. They wish they had taken it with them to the kitchen. They wish they had not let Sam taste their jet. They learn a lesson. They have to be careful with their toys. They have to share and protect them. They have to find a new jet.

Once upon a time, there was a quiet little girl named Lily. She had a special cup that she loved very much. This cup was not like any other cup. It could speak! One day, Lily took her cup outside to play in the sun. The cup spoke to her and said, "I am thirsty." So, Lily filled the cup with water from the tap. The cup drank the water and said, "Thank you, Lily. I am happy now." Lily and her cup played all day. They spoke to each other and had lots of fun. When it was time to go inside, Lily put her cup away. She knew that tomorrow, they would play and speak again.
