Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Ben and the Hat

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play outside in the snow. They make a big snowman with a hat and a scarf. They give him a carrot nose and two coal eyes. "Hello, Mr. Snowman!" Sara says. "You are our friend." "Yes, you are very nice," Ben says. "But you look cold. Do you want a hug?" Sara and Ben hug the snowman gently. They do not want to break him. The snowman does not say anything. He is happy to have two friends. But then a big wind comes. It blows hard and makes the snowman shake. His hat falls off and his nose wobbles. "Oh no!" Sara and Ben cry. "The wind is too strong. It will hurt Mr. Snowman." They run to the snowman and hold him. They try to support him and keep him together. "Please, wind, stop blowing!" Sara says. "Please, leave Mr. Snowman alone!" "Please, wind, be gentle!" Ben says. "Please, let us play with Mr. Snowman!" The wind hears Sara and Ben. It feels sorry for them and the snowman. It stops blowing and becomes calm. The snowman stops shaking and smiles. "Thank you, wind," Sara and Ben say. "Thank you for being kind." They put the hat and the nose back on the snowman. They hug him again and say goodbye. "See you tomorrow, Mr. Snowman!" Sara says. "We will come back and play with you," Ben says. They go inside their house. They are cold and wet, but happy. They have a new friend and they have learned to support him.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She liked to play with her fancy doll. One day, she heard a voice from her doll. Sue was very surprise. The voice said, "I want to play too!" Sue was scared at first, but then she thought it was fun. She played with the doll and the doll talked to her. They had a good time. But one day, the doll wanted to play when Sue did not want to play. The doll was not happy. Sue and the doll did not know what to do. They were both sad. Sue's mom saw them and said, "Why not take turns to play?" Sue thought it was a good idea. So, Sue and the doll took turns to play. They were both happy and had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom loved to eat strawberries. Every day, he would drive his little red car to the strawberry farm. One day, Tom saw a mouse at the farm. The mouse was very hungry. Tom thought, "I have many strawberries. I can share with the mouse." So, Tom gave some strawberries to the mouse. The mouse was very happy. Tom and the mouse became friends. They learned that sharing is good. They played and ate strawberries together. And they lived happily ever after. The end.

Once upon a time, in a little town, there was a crazy cat. The cat liked to steal things from people. One day, the cat saw a big red ball in a yard. The cat wanted the ball, so it went to steal it. The cat jumped over the fence and grabbed the ball with its mouth. It ran away fast, but a little boy saw the cat. The boy said, "Hey, that's my ball! Give it back!" The cat did not want to give the ball back, so it kept running. The cat ran and ran until it was out of breath. It needed to use its lungs to breathe. The little boy caught up to the cat and said, "Please, give me my ball back." The cat felt bad for stealing the ball, so it gave the ball back to the boy. They both said sorry and became friends. From that day on, the crazy cat never stole again.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a pretty butterfly with a dizzy pattern on its wings. Lily wanted to keep the butterfly as her friend. She tried to catch it, but the butterfly flew away. "Please, come back!" Lily said. The butterfly stopped and looked at her. "I want to be your friend," Lily told the butterfly. The butterfly liked Lily and decided to stay. Together, they played all day. They made a game of making dizzy patterns in the air. They laughed and had fun. Lily was happy to keep the butterfly as her friend. And the butterfly was happy too.
