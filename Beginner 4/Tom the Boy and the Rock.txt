Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Rock

Once upon a time, in an ordinary town, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom liked to play near the rail tracks. He loved to watch the big trains go by. One day, Tom found a magic rock near the rails. Tom took the magic rock home. He found out that when he held the rock and said "vanish", things would disappear. Tom had fun making things vanish. He made his toys vanish and then come back. He made his food vanish and then come back. Tom was very happy with his magic rock. But one day, Tom was playing near the rail tracks again. He saw a big train coming. He thought it would be fun to make the train vanish. So, he held the rock and said "vanish". The big train disappeared. But Tom didn't know how to bring the train back. The people on the train were lost forever. Tom was very sad and he learned that magic can be dangerous.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to plan things. He would plan his day, his games, and even his snacks. One day, Tim wanted to learn math. He thought math would be easy and fun. Tim asked his mom to teach him math. His mom said, "Okay, let's plan to learn math tomorrow." Tim was very happy and went to bed early. He dreamed about how easy math would be and how smart he would become. The next day, Tim and his mom started to learn math. But, it was not easy like Tim thought. He tried and tried, but he could not understand. Tim felt sad and cried. He did not want to learn math anymore. The end.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy some things for their home. They saw many things at the store. Some things were big, and some things were small. Some things were cheap, and some things were not cheap. Tim saw a box with many toys. He wanted to sort the toys by color. He put the red toys in one pile, the blue toys in another pile, and the yellow toys in another pile. "Look, mom!" said Tim. "I sorted the toys by color!" "Good job, Tim!" said his mom. It was a hot day, so Tim and his mom felt the heat. They decided to buy a cheap fan to help them stay cool. They took the fan home and turned it on. The fan made a nice breeze, and they felt better. Tim and his mom were happy with their new, cheap fan.

One day, a little girl named Amy was very excited. She wanted to go play on the big hill near her house. It was a sunny day and the ground was dry. So, she put on her shoes and ran outside. When Amy got to the hill, she saw a little boy there. His name was Tim. He was excited too. "Hi!" said Amy. "Do you want to play with me?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes! Let's roll down the hill together!" So, they both started to roll down the hill, laughing and having fun. But as they were rolling, something unexpected happened. A big, friendly dog came running towards them. The dog wanted to play too! Amy and Tim were surprised, but they were not scared. They all played together on the hill, and it was the best day ever.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a toy box full of fun things to play with. One day, she found a fake chain in her toy box. Lucy thought it was very pretty and shiny. Lucy wanted to send the fake chain to her friend, Tim. She put the chain in a small box and went to her mom. She asked her mom to send the box to Tim's house. Her mom said yes and they went to the post office together. After a few days, Tim got the box from Lucy. He opened it and saw the shiny fake chain. Tim was so happy and he called Lucy to say thank you. They both played with their fake chains and had a lot of fun together.
