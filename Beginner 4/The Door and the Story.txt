Category: Beginner
Title: The Door and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a big door in a small house. A little girl named Sue lived in the house. She always wanted to know what was behind the big door. But, she could not find the key. One day, Sue saw a key on the floor. She was very happy. "This must be the key to the big door!" she thought. So, she took the key and went to the big door. She put the key in the door and turned it. The door opened. Behind the big door, there was a big, dark room. Sue was scared, but she went in. Suddenly, the door closed, and Sue was stuck in the dark room. She could not find the key to open the door again. She was very sad and scared. The moral of the story is: Do not open doors you are not supposed to, or you may end up in a bad place.

One day Peter and his mommy were walking through the park. Peter saw some orange flowers growing from the ground and he asked what they were. Mommy said, "Those are called tulips. They grow from the roots beneath the soil." Peter asked, "What are roots?" Mommy rubbed his head and smiled, "Roots are like fingers that grow from the Earth. Shall we go peek and see what we can find?" Peter was excited and they began to walk. Soon they could see the orange roots poking out of the ground. Peter reached down and touched the root with his delicate hands. He was amazed at how strong it felt. He smiled at Mommy, who was already peeking at deeper roots that were hidden. Mommy said, "We can't stay long, and must leave soon, but let’s enjoy this special moment in the park. Shall we?" Peter nodded and they stayed to peek and explore the roots together.

Anna liked to fix things. She had a box of tools and some old toys that she could take apart and put back together. She liked to see how they worked and make them better. She was very good at fixing things. One day, Anna had a visit from her cousin Tom. Tom was older than Anna and he liked to play rough. He did not like to fix things. He liked to break things. He saw Anna's box of tools and her old toys and he wanted to play with them. He did not ask Anna. He just took them and started to smash them. Anna was very sad and angry. She told Tom to stop breaking her things. She tried to get them back from him. But Tom was bigger and stronger than Anna. He pushed her away and laughed. He broke more of her things. He broke her favorite doll and her clock and her car. He broke everything. Anna cried and cried. She ran to her mom and told her what Tom did. Her mom was very sorry and tried to comfort Anna. She said she would talk to Tom's mom and try to fix some of the things. But Anna knew that some of the things were too broken to fix. She felt very bad. Tom did not feel bad. He did not say sorry to Anna. He did not care about her things. He just wanted to have fun. He thought breaking things was fun. He did not like to fix things. He was very mean.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had an adorable cat named Tippy. Tippy loved to play with Sue all day long. They were best friends. One day, Sue and Tippy went to the kitchen. Sue wanted to make a cake. She put some things in a big bowl. Sue said, "Tippy, let's stir the cake mix!" Tippy used her paw to help Sue stir. They stirred and stirred until it was all mixed up. After that, Sue turned on the tap to wash their hands and paws. The water was cold and fun to play with. Tippy said, "Meow!" as the water splashed on her. They laughed and played together. Sue and Tippy had a great day making a cake and playing with the tap.
