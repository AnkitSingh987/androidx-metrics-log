Category: Beginner
Title: Remy the Rabbit and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a lively forest, there lived a small rabbit named Remy. Remy was a very happy rabbit who loved to play with his friends. They would run and jump and have lots of fun together. One hot day, Remy and his friends were very thirsty. They wanted to find some water to drink. They looked and looked, but they could not find any water. Then, Remy saw a cooler under a big tree. He asked his friends, "Do you think there is water in the cooler?" His friends did not answer. They were too tired and thirsty to talk. Remy decided to check the cooler. When he opened it, he found cold water inside. He was very happy and shared the water with his friends. They all drank and felt much better. After that day, Remy and his friends always helped each other. They learned that it is good to share and help each other when someone is in need. This made them even happier and their forest a better place to live.

Jenny and Tom were friends. They liked to play with toys. One day, Jenny brought a new toy to Tom's house. It was a glass horse. It was very pretty and shiny. Jenny loved her glass horse. "Can I play with your horse?" Tom asked. "Okay, but be careful. It is fragile. It can break very easily. You have to let me hold it first." Jenny said. "I promise. I will be careful. Please let me play with it." Tom said. Jenny let Tom play with her horse. She held it in her hands and gave it to him. Tom smiled and took the horse. He looked at it and touched it. He liked how it felt. But then, Tom saw a ball on the floor. He wanted to play with the ball too. He forgot about the horse. He threw the horse on the couch and ran to the ball. He kicked the ball and laughed. Jenny saw what Tom did. She was very angry and sad. She ran to the couch and picked up her horse. But it was too late. The horse was broken. It had a big crack on its back. Jenny cried and cried. "Tom, you broke my horse! You did not keep your promise! You were not careful! You are a bad friend!" Jenny yelled. Tom heard Jenny cry. He felt sorry and scared. He ran to Jenny and tried to hug her. "I'm sorry, Jenny. I did not mean to break your horse. I forgot. Please don't be mad at me. Please be my friend." Tom said. But Jenny did not want to hug Tom. She pushed him away. She held her broken horse and ran to her mom. She told her mom what Tom did. Her mom was angry too. She told Tom to go home and never come back. Tom cried and cried. He lost his friend and his toy. He wished he had been careful. He wished he had kept his promise. He wished he had not broken the horse. But it was too late. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big sea. In the sea, there was a boat. The boat was very successful at catching fish. The boat had a man who liked to tie knots. One day, the man went to the sea. He wanted to catch more fish. He took his boat and tied it to the dock. Then, he went into the sea. The man caught many fish. He was very happy. He went back to the dock and untied his boat. He took the fish home to share with his friends. They all had a big and yummy dinner.

Anna was very happy. Today was her birthday. She had a big cake with four candles. She had many presents from her family and friends. She wanted to play with her new toys all day. But her mom said, "Anna, it is time to bathe. You are very dirty from playing outside. You need to wash your hair and your body. Then you can wear your new dress and have your cake." Anna did not want to bathe. She said, "No, mom, I do not want to bathe. I want to play more. Bathing is boring. I do not like water and soap. They make my eyes hurt." Her mom said, "Anna, you have to bathe. It is good for you. It makes you clean and fresh. You will feel better after bathing. And you will look very pretty in your new dress. Please, come with me to the bathroom. I will help you bathe." Anna still did not want to bathe. She ran away from her mom. She hid under the table. She said, "No, no, no, I do not want to bathe." Her mom looked for her. She found her under the table. She said, "Anna, I see you. You cannot hide from me. Come out, please. Do not be silly. Bathing is not so bad. It can be fun. You can play with your rubber duck and your bubbles. You can sing and splash. You can make your hair look funny. Come on, Anna, let's go bathe." Anna looked at her mom. She saw her smile. She saw her rubber duck and her bubbles. She thought, maybe bathing is not so bad. Maybe it can be fun. She said, "OK, mom, I will bathe. But only for a little bit. Then I want to have my cake and play with my toys." Her mom said, "OK, Anna, that is a good deal. Let's go bathe. I am proud of you. You are a big girl now. Happy birthday, Anna." They went to the bathroom. They filled the tub with warm water. They put soap and bubbles in the water. They put the rubber duck in the water. Anna got in the tub. She played with her rubber duck and her bubbles. She sang and splashed. She made her hair look funny. She laughed and smiled. She bathed. Her mom helped her wash her hair and her body. She rinsed her with clean water. She wrapped her in a soft towel. She dried her and combed her hair. She put her new dress on her. She said, "Anna, you look very pretty. You are very clean and fresh. You did a good job bathing. I love you, Anna." Anna said, "I love you, mom. Thank you for helping me bathe. It was not so bad. It was fun. Can we have my cake now?" Her mom said, "Yes, Anna, we can have your cake now. Let's go to the kitchen. Your dad and your friends are waiting for you. They have a surprise for you." They went to the kitchen. They saw a big cake with four candles. They saw many presents from her family and friends. They saw balloons and streamers. They heard a loud "Surprise!" They saw everyone smile and clap. Anna was very happy. She said, "Wow, this is the best birthday ever. Thank you, everyone. You are the best." She blew out the candles. She made a wish. She cut the cake. She shared it with everyone. She opened her presents. She thanked everyone. She played with her new toys. She had a lot of fun. She had a great birthday. She did not see the empty tub in the bathroom. She did not care. She was too busy enjoying her birthday. She was glad she bathed. She was glad she listened to her mom. She was glad she was a big girl now. She was glad she was happy.
