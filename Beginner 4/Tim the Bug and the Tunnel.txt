Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Bug and the Tunnel

One day, a little bug named Tim went into a tunnel. The tunnel was dark and harsh. Tim was not happy in the tunnel, but he wanted to find a friend. In the tunnel, Tim met another bug named Sam. Sam said, "Hi, Tim! Do you want to play?" Tim was happy to find a friend. They played in the tunnel for a long time. But then, they found a big rot. The rot was bad and smelly. Tim and Sam did not like the rot. They tried to go away from the rot, but they could not find the way out of the tunnel. They were lost and sad. The rot made them feel sick. In the end, they could not leave the tunnel and the rot.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy forest. In this forest, there were many animals that liked to play and have fun. One day, a big bear and a small rabbit were playing together. The big bear said, "I am so sleepy. I want to take a nap." The small rabbit said, "Okay, let's find a good place to rest." As they walked, they saw a sign. The sign had a picture of a tree and a bed. The big bear and the small rabbit knew this was the perfect place to sleep. They went to the spot and got ready to nap. The big bear and the small rabbit slept under the tree. They had sweet dreams and woke up happy. They thanked the sign for helping them find a good place to rest. From that day on, they always knew where to go when they felt sleepy.

Once upon a time, in a green forest, there lived a bunny and a squirrel. They were good friends. They liked to play hide and seek together. One day, the bunny said, "Let's play hide and seek. I will hide first, and you will find me!" The squirrel agreed, and the bunny ran off to hide. The squirrel counted to ten and then started looking for the bunny. The squirrel looked east, but the bunny was not there. Then, the squirrel looked west and saw a little tail behind a big tree. The squirrel said, "I found you, bunny!" The bunny laughed and said, "Good job, squirrel! Now it's your turn to hide!" And they played hide and seek all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little blue bird named Billy. Billy lived in a big tree with his parents. Every day, Billy would play with his friends and fly around the tree. One day, Billy's friend, Timmy, dared him to fly up to the highest branch on the tree. Billy looked up and felt scared. He had never flown that high before. But he wanted to show Timmy that he was brave. Billy took a deep breath and said, "I dare to fly high!" He flapped his wings and flew higher and higher. His parent watched him from below, cheering him on. When Billy reached the highest branch, he felt so proud. "Good job, Billy!" his parent called out. "You dared to fly high and you did it!" From that day on, Billy knew that with a little courage, he could do anything he dared to do. And he always remembered to be brave, just like his parent taught him.

Lily and Ben are playing with their toys in the living room. Lily has a purple doll that can move its arms and legs. Ben has a noisy truck that can make sounds and lights. They are having fun, but they are also making a lot of noise. "Shh, shh, shh," says Mom from the kitchen. "You are too noisy. Dad is working in his room. He needs quiet." "Sorry, Mom," says Lily. "We will play quieter." "OK, Mom," says Ben. "We will be good." But after a while, Lily and Ben forget what Mom said. They start to make more noise again. Lily makes her doll dance and sing. Ben makes his truck zoom and beep. They laugh and shout. "Hey, hey, hey," says Dad from his room. "You are still too noisy. I can't work. Please move to another room." "Oops, sorry, Dad," says Lily. "We didn't mean to bother you." "Yeah, sorry, Dad," says Ben. "We will move now." Lily and Ben pick up their toys and go to their bedroom. They close the door and play on the floor. They are still noisy, but they don't care. They are happy.
