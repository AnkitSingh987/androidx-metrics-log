Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Max and the Dog

Lily and Max were playing in the park. They saw a big red truck with a sign that said "Popcorn". They ran to the truck and asked for some popcorn. "Here you go, kids. Enjoy your popcorn," the man in the truck said. He gave them each a paper bag full of popcorn. Lily and Max thanked the man and sat on a bench. They opened their bags and started to eat the popcorn. It was warm and crunchy and salty. They liked it a lot. Suddenly, they heard a loud bark. They turned around and saw a big black dog. The dog was running towards them. He looked hungry and angry. "Help! Help!" Lily and Max shouted. They were scared of the dog. They held their bags of popcorn tightly. The dog jumped on the bench and tried to grab the popcorn. Lily and Max sprang off the bench and ran away. They dropped their bags of popcorn on the ground. The dog sniffed the popcorn and ate it. He liked it a lot. He wagged his tail and barked happily. Lily and Max ran to their mom. She was sitting on a blanket with a book. She saw them and looked alert. "What's wrong, kids? Why are you running?" she asked. "There was a big black dog. He wanted our popcorn. He was scary," Lily and Max said. "Oh, I'm sorry, kids. That was not nice of the dog. Are you OK?" mom said. "Yes, we are OK. But we lost our popcorn," Lily and Max said. "That's OK. We can get some more popcorn later. Or maybe we can have some ice cream instead. How does that sound?" mom said. Lily and Max smiled. They liked ice cream a lot. They hugged their mom and said, "We love you, mom." "I love you too, kids. Come on, let's go and have some fun," mom said. They walked away from the bench. The dog was still eating the popcorn. He did not see them. He was too busy.

Once upon a time, there was a nation filled with smart people. Every day, they would sit together and talk about how to make their nation even smarter. One day, a little three-year-old sat at the table with them. Everyone seemed surprised that a small child wanted to join their conversation. The little one said, "Let's be even smarter by thinking of new ideas!" Everyone was amazed. They all started to think very hard and come up with smart ideas. After a while, the little one said something very smart: "Let's all work together to make our nation special!" Everyone clapped! They all agreed that working together was the best way to make their nation a great place to live. From then on, they all worked together to make their nation even better. The end!

Once upon a time, there was a pretty tent in a big park. The tent was red and blue. It had a happy smile on it. Many kids loved to play near the pretty tent. One day, a little girl named Lily came to play. She saw the pretty tent and had a question. She asked her mom, "Can I go play in the tent?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can." Lily went inside the pretty tent and found many toys. She played with the toys and had lots of fun. Soon, her friends came to play too. They all had a great time in the pretty tent.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a small, broken table in his room. It had only three legs, and it would wobble all the time. Tim wanted to fix the table, so he asked his dad for help. Tim's dad said, "We can restore the table and make it like new!" They went to the store and bought a new leg for the table. Tim was very happy. When they got home, Tim and his dad put the new leg on the broken table. Now, the table was not broken anymore. Tim was so proud of his fixed table. He put his toys on it and played all day long.
