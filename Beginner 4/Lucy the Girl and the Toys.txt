Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She liked to play with her toy motor car. One day, Lucy met a boy named Tom. Tom had many toys, and he was very organized. He always put his toys away when he was done playing with them. Lucy asked Tom, "Do you want to play with my motor car?" Tom said, "Yes, let's play together!" They played together and had lots of fun. When they were done, Tom showed Lucy how to put the toys away in an organized way. As they grew up, Lucy and Tom became best friends. They always helped each other and played together. One day, Tom asked Lucy, "Will you marry me?" Lucy said, "Yes, I will marry you!" Lucy and Tom got married and lived happily ever after. They always kept their home organized and clean. Their friends and family loved to visit them. The moral of the story is that being organized and helpful can lead to a happy life.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very hungry. He went to his mom and said, "Mom, I am hungry. Can I have some food?" Mom said, "Yes, Tim. Go grab an apple from the tree outside." Tim went outside to get an apple from the tree. When Tim was outside, he saw a big, scary wolf. The wolf was hungry too. Tim was scared, and there was silence. The wolf said, "I am hungry, and I will eat you." Tim tried to run away, but the wolf was too fast. The wolf grabbed Tim, and there was no more Tim. The end.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to make a creative picture for his mom. He took some paper, paint, and a brush. He started to paint a big, red heart. Tim was very happy with his work. But then, his little sister, Sue, came into the room. She saw Tim's picture and wanted to help. Sue took a brush and painted a big, green circle around the heart. Tim was mad at Sue. "That's not what I wanted!" he cried. Sue felt sad and said, "I'm sorry, I just wanted to surprise you." Tim thought about it and realized that the picture was still creative and special. He said, "It's okay, Sue. Let's finish the picture together." So they both worked on the picture, and in the end, it was a big, beautiful surprise for their mom.

Once upon a time, in a wide green park, there lived a little bird named Tim. Tim loved to whistle and play with his friends. One day, while he was whistling, he saw a shiny plate on the ground. Tim was curious and wanted to show it to his friends. Tim picked up the plate in his beak and flew to his friends. "Look what I found!" he said. His friends were excited and wanted to play with the plate too. They took turns tossing the plate in the air and catching it. They were having so much fun. But then, a big wind came and blew the plate far away. Tim and his friends tried to catch it, but they couldn't. The plate fell and broke into many pieces. Tim and his friends were sad because they lost their new toy. The moral of the story is to be careful with things that are not yours.

Once upon a time, there was a queen. She was not a nice queen. She was harsh. She did not like to play or laugh. The people in her land were sad. One day, a funny man came to the queen's land. He made the people laugh. The queen did not like this. She did not like people laughing. She told the funny man to go away. The funny man did not go away. He had a plan. The funny man went to the queen. He did a funny dance. The queen tried not to laugh, but she could not help it. She laughed and laughed. She did not think she could laugh. But now, the queen was not harsh anymore. She was nice and liked to laugh. The people in her land were happy. The funny man showed the queen that laughing was good.
