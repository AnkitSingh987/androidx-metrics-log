Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Door

Lily and Ben were playing in the kitchen. They liked to pretend they were chefs and make yummy food. They used pots and pans, spoons and forks, and plastic fruits and vegetables. They also liked to open and close the fridge and see what was inside. "Look, Lily, I found some cheese!" Ben said, holding a yellow slice. "Do you want some?" "Yes, please!" Lily said, smiling. She took the cheese and put it on a plate. "We can make a cheese sandwich. Do you have bread?" Ben nodded and opened the fridge again. He saw a loaf of bread on the shelf. He reached for it, but he was too short. He stretched his arm, but he still could not reach it. He tried to stand on his toes, but he slipped and fell. He knocked over a bottle of juice that was on the door. The juice spilled all over the floor and made a big mess. "Uh-oh!" Ben said, looking at the juice. "Mommy will be mad!" Lily heard the noise and ran to see what happened. She saw Ben on the floor and the juice everywhere. She felt sorry for him. She helped him get up and gave him a hug. "It's okay, Ben," she said. "We can clean it up. Mommy won't be mad if we say sorry and wipe the floor." She found a cloth and gave it to Ben. They both started to wipe the juice with the cloth. They worked hard and soon the floor was dry and clean. "Good job, Lily and Ben!" Mommy said, coming into the kitchen. She saw the cloth and the empty bottle. She guessed what had happened. She was not mad. She was proud of them for being responsible and helpful. She gave them both a kiss and a hug. "You are such cute and good kids," she said. "I love you very much. How about we make a real cheese sandwich together?" Lily and Ben smiled and nodded. They were happy. They loved Mommy very much. They followed her to the fridge and helped her make a delicious cheese sandwich. They shared it and ate it. It was the best cheese sandwich ever.

One day, a little sheep was eager to play. He had a ball of wool that he loved. He wanted to find his friend, the dog, to play with him. So, the little sheep started to search for his friend. He looked near the big tree, but the dog was not there. He looked near the pond, but the dog was not there either. The little sheep felt sad. He wanted to find his friend so they could play together. Finally, the little sheep found the dog near the hill. "I found you!" said the little sheep. "Let's play with my wool!" But the dog did not want to play. The dog was tired. The little sheep felt sad again. Then, the dog had an idea. "Let's play tomorrow," said the dog. The little sheep agreed. The next day, the little sheep and the dog played with the wool all day. They were both happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin was not like the other fish. He was weak and could not swim fast. One day, Fin saw a big number on the sea floor. He wanted to dive down and see what it was. As Fin tried to dive, a big, mean fish named Bubbles came up to him. Bubbles laughed at Fin and said, "You are too weak to dive down there!" Fin felt sad and swam away. But then, something unexpected happened. A friendly turtle named Tilly saw Fin and asked, "Why are you sad?" Fin told Tilly about the number and Bubbles. Tilly smiled and said, "I can help you!" Tilly took Fin on her back and they dived down together. They reached the number and saw that it was a treasure chest! Fin and Tilly opened the chest and found beautiful shells inside. They shared the shells and became best friends. And from that day on, Bubbles never laughed at Fin again.

Once upon a time, there was a pale squirrel named Sam. He liked to write on the ground with a stick. One day, he found a big acorn under a tree. Sam was very happy and wanted to show his friends. Sam took the acorn and ran to his friends. He showed them the acorn and said, "Look! I found a big acorn!" His friends were happy too. They all wanted to write about the acorn on the ground with sticks. So, Sam and his friends sat under the tree and wrote about the big acorn. They drew pictures of the acorn and wrote about how happy they were. The sun was shining, and they played and wrote until it was time to go home. At the end of the day, Sam and his friends said goodbye. They were very happy they had found the big acorn and had fun writing about it. Sam took the acorn home and put it in a special place, so he could remember the fun day with his friends.
