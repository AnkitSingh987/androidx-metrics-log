Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Sue and the Pond

One day, a little boy named Tim and a little girl named Sue went to play. They found a row of fake ducks near the pond. The ducks were colorful and pretty. Tim and Sue wanted to play with the ducks. Tim said, "Let's shout to see if the ducks will move." Sue agreed. They both shouted, "Ducks, move!" But the ducks did not move. They were fake ducks, so they could not hear Tim and Sue. Tim and Sue did not give up. They tried to push the ducks into the pond. The ducks floated on the water. Tim and Sue laughed and clapped. They played with the ducks until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to kick a ball. One day, Tim and his mom went to a big building. They had to use an elevator to go up. Tim was so excited! In the elevator, Tim saw a button with a picture of a yummy cake. He asked his mom if they could go to the floor with the yummy cake. His mom said, "Maybe later, we have to visit grandma first." After they visited grandma, Tim remembered the yummy cake. He asked his mom if they could go now. His mom said yes, and they went back to the elevator. Tim pushed the button with the cake picture, and the elevator took them to a big room full of yummy treats. Tim was so happy to see all the yummy treats. He saw a big cake with a ball on top. Tim's mom said, "You can kick the ball and win a prize!" Tim kicked the ball and won a big, yummy cake to take home. It was the best day ever for Tim.

One day, a big dog named Max was playing in the park. He was having a lot of fun. He was running and jumping and barking. He saw a little girl named Lily with her ice cream. Max wanted some, so he took it from her hand. Lily was sad and cried. Max's mom saw what happened and said, "Max, that was not nice! You must give it back and say sorry." Max felt embarrassed. He gave the ice cream back to Lily and said, "I am sorry, Lily." Max's mom told him, "Now you will be punished. You can't play in the park for two days." The next day, Max stayed at home. He was sad and missed playing in the park. Then, something unexpected happened. Lily came to his house with her mom. They had a big box with them. Lily said, "Max, I want to give you this. I know you were sorry, and I want to be friends." Max opened the box, and inside was a big toy. It was a new ball to play with. Max was so happy! He thanked Lily and they played together. From that day on, Max and Lily were best friends, and Max never took anything without asking again.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He liked to bounce his red ball as he walked. The sun was out, and Tim was happy. The ball was harmless and soft, so it was safe to play with. As Tim walked and bounced his ball, he saw a big tree. He threw the ball at the tree, and it bounced back to him. Tim laughed and did it again. But this time, the ball bounced very high and went into the tree. Tim was sad. He looked up and saw his ball stuck in the tree. Just then, a kind squirrel saw Tim's sad face. The squirrel climbed the tree and pushed the ball out. The ball fell and landed on a big leaf. The leaf acted like a trampoline and made the ball bounce very high. To Tim's surprise, the ball bounced all the way to the moon! The moon smiled and bounced the ball back to Tim. Tim caught the ball and was very happy. He thanked the squirrel and the moon, and continued his walk with his red ball, knowing that even the moon liked to play with it.
