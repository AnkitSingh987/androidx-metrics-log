Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Ball

Lily liked to wander in the park. She saw many things: trees, flowers, birds, dogs. She liked to play with her ball and her doll. Sometimes she saw other children and said hello. One day, she saw a band in the park. They had big drums, shiny horns, and funny hats. They made loud and happy sounds. Lily wanted to see them closer. She left her ball and her doll on the grass and ran to the band. "Hello, band!" she said. "Can I play with you?" The band did not hear her. They were too busy playing. Lily tried to touch a drum, but a man stopped her. "Go away, little girl!" he said. "You are not useful here. You are in the way." Lily felt sad and scared. She wanted to go back to her ball and her doll, but she did not know where they were. She looked around, but she did not see them. She did not see her mom or dad either. She was lost. She started to cry. She wanted her mom and dad to find her. She wanted her ball and her doll to hug. She wanted the band to be nice to her. But nobody came. Nobody helped. Nobody cared. Lily was alone.

Once upon a time, there was a black cat. The cat was very hungry. It wanted to find some meat to eat. The cat looked and looked, but it could not find any meat. One day, the black cat saw a big number on a door. The number was three. The cat went in the door to see if there was any meat inside. But there was a big dog in the house. The dog did not want the cat to eat the meat. The black cat and the big dog had a big fight. They both wanted the meat. The cat was fast and the dog was strong. But the cat was smarter. It jumped up high and got the meat. The dog could not jump as high as the cat. The black cat got the meat and ran away. The big dog was sad, but the cat was very happy to have some meat to eat.

Once upon a time, there was a dancer named Lily. She loved to dance and shine like the sun. Lily was a happy dancer and had many friends. One day, Lily met a new friend named Tim. Tim said, "I have a yummy treat for you, Lily." Lily was excited and wanted to try the yummy treat. Tim gave her a big, red apple. Lily took a big bite of the apple. But the apple was not yummy. It was bad and made Lily very sick. Her friends tried to help her, but they did not know what to do. In the end, Lily could not dance and shine anymore. She was too sick from the bad apple. All her friends were sad, and they missed their happy dancer friend.

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a nest. In the nest, there were three playful baby birds. They liked to jump and sing all day. The mommy bird told them to stay in the nest when she went to find food. One day, a big, loud dog came to the tree. The dog saw the baby birds in the nest. The dog wanted to play with the baby birds. The mommy bird saw the dog and was scared. She told the baby birds to ignore the dog and stay in the nest. But one of the playful baby birds did not listen. He jumped out of the nest to play with the dog. The dog was too rough and the baby bird got hurt. The mommy bird was sad and flew down to help her baby. The dog felt bad and went away. From that day on, the baby birds listened to their mommy and stayed safe in their nest.

Once upon a time, there was a yellow monkey. He lived in a big tree in the jungle. The yellow monkey loved to play all day with his friends. They would swing from tree to tree, laughing and having fun. One day, the yellow monkey saw a bright light far away. He went closer to see what it was. He found a small fire burning on the ground. The yellow monkey had never seen fire before, so he was very curious. The yellow monkey wanted to touch the fire, but his friends stopped him. They told him that fire could burn and hurt him. The yellow monkey listened to his friends and stayed away from the fire. They all went back to playing in the trees, happy and safe.
