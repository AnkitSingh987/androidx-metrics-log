Category: Beginner
Title: Spring the Bunny and the Woods

One day, a little bunny named Spring was hopping in the woods. She had soft, fair fur that made her look very pretty. Spring loved to play with her friends, the birds and squirrels. Spring's mom called her home for a bath. She used shampoo to make Spring's fair fur clean and shiny. Spring liked the bubbles from the shampoo, and she splashed in the water. After her bath, Spring went back to the woods to play. She felt so happy and clean. Her friends said her fair fur looked very nice. Spring smiled and played with them until the sun went down.

Ben and Mia like to play with water. They have a big spray bottle and a small stamp. They fill the bottle with water and go outside. They find a smooth wall and spray water on it. Then they use the stamp to make shapes on the wet wall. "Look, I made a star!" Mia says, showing her stamp. "I made a circle!" Ben says, showing his stamp. They spray and stamp more water on the wall. They make different shapes and colors. They have fun. But then they hear a voice. It is Mom. "Ben, Mia, what are you doing?" Mom asks, coming outside. She sees the wet wall and the spray bottle and the stamp. "Uh-oh," Ben and Mia say. They look at Mom. They think she is angry. But Mom smiles. She is not angry. She likes their art. "That's very creative," Mom says. "But next time, please ask me before you spray water on the wall." "OK, Mom," Ben and Mia say. They hug Mom and say sorry. Mom hugs them back and says it's OK. They go inside and dry off. They are happy. They like to play with water. They like to make art. They like Mom.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted a glass of water. He asked his mom for it. His mom said, "Sure, Tim. Let me get it for you." Tim wanted to get the glass himself. He said, "No, Mom. I want to do it." His mom insisted that she should do it, but Tim wanted to try hard. Tim got a chair and stood on it. He reached for the glass. He was very careful. He got the glass and filled it with water. He was so happy and proud. He said, "Look, Mom! I did it!" His mom smiled and said, "You did a great job, Tim. I'm proud of you." From that day on, Tim always tried hard to do things by himself. His mom was always there to help, but she let him try first.

One day, a big bear named Bob wanted to test a new game. He saw his friend, Cat, doing yoga in the icy cold park. Bob thought yoga looked fun, so he wanted to try it too. Bob tried to do yoga like Cat, but he was too big and clumsy. When he tried to bend and stretch, he slipped on the icy ground. He fell down and made a big hole in the ice. Under the ice, Bob found a secret door. He opened the door and found a warm, cozy room. Inside, there were many other animals doing yoga together. Bob joined them and learned how to do yoga the right way. The animals were happy to have a new friend, and Bob was happy that he found a warm place to do yoga.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a loyal dog named Buddy. He loved his owner, a kind girl named Lily. Buddy and Lily played all day and had lots of fun. They were very happy together. One day, Lily's mom told her, "Lily, I am going to marry a nice man named Tom. He will be your new dad." Lily was not sure about this, but she saw her mom was happy. So, she said, "Okay, Mom. If he makes you happy, then I am happy too." Tom moved in with Lily and her mom. Buddy was not sure about Tom at first, but he saw that Tom was kind to Lily and her mom. Soon, Buddy and Tom became best friends. One day, Lily ate too much candy and her stomach hurt. Tom helped her feel better by rubbing her belly and telling her a funny story. Buddy saw this and knew that Tom was a good man. From that day on, Buddy, Lily, and Tom were a happy family. They learned that when you are kind, loyal, and love each other, everything will be okay. And they all lived happily ever after. The moral of the story is: when you are kind, loyal, and love each other, you can face any change together.
