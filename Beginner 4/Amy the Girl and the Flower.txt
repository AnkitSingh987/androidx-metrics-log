Category: Beginner
Title: Amy the Girl and the Flower

One day, a girl named Amy went to her yard. She saw many plants. She wanted to sort them. She put big plants on one side and small plants on the other side. Amy felt comfortable doing this. She loved her plants very much. Amy's mom gave her a new plant. It was very small. Amy put the small plant with the other small plants. She knew it would grow big one day. She took care of it every day. After some days, the small plant grew bigger and bigger. One morning, Amy saw a beautiful flower on the plant. She was very happy. Her hard work had helped the small plant grow into a big plant with a pretty flower.

Once upon a time there was a lively boy named Max. He went with his mommy to an exciting place called the lab. Max looked around and saw lots of interesting things. "Mommy, what is this?" asked Max. "This is a lab," mommy said. "What do you do here?" Max asked. "We come here to learn lots of things and discover new things," said mommy. Max was so excited to learn about new things. He looked around and saw a big mystery box. He wondered what was inside. "Mommy, what is in this box?" asked Max. "This box contains a big surprise," she said. Max was very curious. He wanted to find out what the surprise was. Suddenly, mommy opened the box, and it revealed a big toy train. Max's eyes lit up with excitement. He couldn't believe his eyes. He was so happy to see the toy train. Mommy said, "Let's play with it!" Max was delighted. He knew his visit to the lab was going to be very special.

One day, a boy named Tim found a box. The box had a dry label with a number on it. The number was "3". Tim did not know what was in the box. He wanted to open it and see. He asked his friend, Sue, "Do you know what is in the box with the number 3 on it?" Sue said, "No, I do not know. Let's open it and see what is inside." They opened the box and found three big, green balls. They were very surprised! Tim and Sue thought the balls were just toys. But when they touched the balls, they started to bounce all by themselves! The balls bounced and bounced all around the room, making Tim and Sue laugh and clap their hands. They had so much fun playing with the unexpected bouncy balls.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw the sun was going down. It was a pretty sunset. Tim knew it would get dark soon. He wanted to play a little more before going home. Tim saw his friend, Sue. They started to play a game of hide and seek. Tim hid behind a big tree. Sue shouted, "Ready or not, here I come!" She looked all around but could not find Tim. The sky got darker and darker. Sue was scared. She shouted, "Tim, where are you?" Tim heard Sue and came out from behind the tree. He said, "I'm here, Sue. Let's go home now." They held hands and walked home together, happy to be safe before it was too dark.

Once upon a time, there was a little hen. She had a small house. In her house, she had a nest with a pretty egg. One day, a tiny mouse came to visit. The mouse said, "Hi, little hen! Can I stay with you?" The hen said, "Yes, you can stay, but please be careful with my egg." The mouse and the hen played all day. They were happy. The mouse was careful with the egg. The egg stayed safe in the nest. At night, they went to sleep. The mouse said, "Thank you, little hen, for letting me stay." The hen said, "You're welcome, tiny mouse. We are friends now." And so, the little hen and the tiny mouse stayed friends forever. They took care of the egg, and they were always happy together.

One sunny day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw an amazing big red ball. Tim wanted to play with it. He ran to the ball and gave it a big kick. The ball went very far. Tim saw a little girl named Amy. She was sad because the ball was hers. Tim said, "I'm sorry. Let's play together." Amy smiled and they started to play with the ball. They played for a long time. Then, it was time for Tim to go home. He told Amy, "I have to go, but let's play again soon." Amy said, "Okay, but please stay for one more kick." Tim stayed and they had one more fun kick. Then, Tim went home, excited to play with Amy again.
