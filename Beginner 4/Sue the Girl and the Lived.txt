Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Lived

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue lived on a big ranch with her mom and dad. They had many animals like cows, horses, and chickens. Sue loved to play with the animals every day. One day, Sue's mom mentioned they were getting a new animal. Sue was very excited but also a little nervous. She did not know what kind of animal it would be. She hoped it would be a friend for her to play with. The next day, Sue's dad brought home a cute little puppy. Sue was so happy and not nervous anymore. She named the puppy Max. Sue and Max played together every day on the ranch. They became best friends and lived happily ever after.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the yard. The cat was very mild and nice, but the dog was a bit wild. They saw a hose on the ground. The cat wanted to drink water from the hose, but the dog wanted to play with it. The cat said, "Stop, dog! I am thirsty. Let me drink water first." But the dog did not listen. He picked up the hose and started to run around the yard. The cat chased the dog, trying to get the hose back. Finally, the cat caught the dog and took the hose away. The dog said, "Sorry, cat. I did not mean to be wild. I just wanted to have fun." The cat and the dog learned to share the hose and became good friends. They played together in the yard every day, and the dog learned to be more mild like the cat.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Bob. Bob loved to swim in the big blue sea. He liked to play with his friends and have fun all day. One day, while Bob was swimming, he saw a big pedal. The pedal was from a bike that was on the bottom of the sea. Bob wanted to take the pedal to show his friends, but he was too small to move it. He tried and tried, but he just couldn't do it. Bob felt frustrated. He didn't want to give up, so he asked his friends for help. They all swam together and pushed the pedal. With their teamwork, they moved the pedal. Bob was happy and thanked his friends. They all swam away, excited to find more fun things in the sea.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to read books. Her room was full of books. They were clean and neat because she took good care of them. One day, Lily found a new book in her room. It was a special book about values. She read it with her mom. The book told her about being kind, sharing, and helping others. Lily learned the value of being a good friend. She shared her toys and helped her friends when they were sad. Everyone loved Lily because she was kind and caring. And she always kept her books clean and neat.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Max, went for a walk. They saw a big temple. Tim said, "Max, I want to go inside the temple." Max barked, "Woof, woof!" which means "Okay, let's go!" Inside the temple, they met a man. The man looked at Max and said, "Your dog is stupid." Tim got sad and said, "No, Max is not stupid. He is smart." The man laughed and walked away. Suddenly, the floor started to shake. A big hole opened in the ground. The man almost fell in, but Max grabbed his shirt and saved him. The man said, "I was wrong. Max is not stupid. He is a hero!" Tim and Max smiled, and they all became friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a humble bunny. This bunny loved to play and jump in the spring. The sun was warm, and the grass was green. One day, the bunny met a little girl. The girl did not understand why the bunny was so happy. She asked the bunny, "Why do you jump so much in the spring?" The humble bunny told the girl, "I jump because I love the warm sun and the green grass. I like to play, and I am happy." The girl smiled and started to jump with the bunny. From that day on, the girl and the humble bunny became good friends. They played and jumped together in the warm spring sun. And they both understood the joy of being happy in the simple things.
