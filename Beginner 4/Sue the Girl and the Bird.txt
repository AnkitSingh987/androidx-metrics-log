Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She liked to play outside with her friends. One day, she saw a shadow on the ground. The shadow was long and thin. Sue wanted to know where the shadow came from. She decided to follow the shadow. She walked and walked until she found a tall tree. Sue looked up and saw a big bird on a branch. The bird said, "Hello, I am the one who made the shadow. Do you want to play with me?" Sue was happy to find a new friend. She said, "Yes, let's play together!" The bird flew down and they played all day. They played tag and hide-and-seek. Sue was glad she followed the shadow and found an available friend to play with. They had lots of fun and became best friends.

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing in her room. She had a beautiful smile on her face. She loved to play with her toy bear, named Mr. Bear. They would talk and laugh together. Lily asked Mr. Bear, "Do you like to play with me?" To her surprise, Mr. Bear replied, "Yes, Lily, I love to play with you!" Lily was so happy that her toy could talk. They played and talked all day. Later, Lily's mom came into her room. She saw Lily talking to Mr. Bear and smiled. She said, "Lily, I have a surprise for you." Lily looked at her mom and saw her holding a new toy bear. "This is Mr. Bear's sister, Miss Bear!" said her mom. Lily was so excited to have a new friend to play with. From that day on, Lily, Mr. Bear, and Miss Bear had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a special eraser. This eraser was rare because it could talk! The eraser lived in a big box with many other erasers, but they could not talk. The eraser wanted a friend to play with. One day, a bell rang in the box. The eraser heard the ring and saw a new eraser beside it. This new eraser was also rare because it could talk too! The two erasers became best friends and played together all the time. The two rare erasers laughed and talked together. They liked to listen to the bell ring in the box. They were happy to have each other as friends. And from that day on, the two rare erasers were never lonely again.

Once upon a time, there was a big, impressive dog named Max. Max liked to lie down next to the fence in his yard. One day, a small cat named Lucy walked by the fence. Max saw Lucy and wanted to play with her. Max said, "Hi Lucy, come play with me!" But Lucy was scared of Max, because he was so big. She said, "No, Max. You are too big and I am too small. I don't want to play." Max was sad, but he didn't give up. The next day, Max found a big, soft ball. He thought that maybe Lucy would like to play with the ball. Max said, "Look, Lucy! I have a ball. We can play together!" Lucy looked at the ball and thought it was fun. She said, "Okay, Max. Let's play!" And so Max and Lucy played with the ball and became good friends.

One day, a boy named Tim went out to play. He saw a distant tree that looked fun to climb. The sky was getting dark, but Tim didn't care. He wanted to have an adventure. As he walked to the tree, he found a spray bottle on the ground. He picked it up and said, "I will use this to spray water on my friends!" He kept walking to the tree, excited about his new toy. When Tim got to the tree, he saw his friend, Sam. "Look what I found!" Tim said, showing Sam the spray bottle. "Let's spray each other!" Sam agreed, and they started to play. But the sky was very dark now, and they didn't see the big hole in the ground. Tim and Sam were laughing and spraying each other when they both fell into the hole. They were scared and couldn't get out. The dark night made it hard for anyone to find them. They stayed in the hole all night, wishing they had gone home earlier.
