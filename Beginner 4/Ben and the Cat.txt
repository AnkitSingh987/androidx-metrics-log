Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and the Cat

Once, a little dog named Ben went on a trip with his family. They had a big car with lots of luggage. Ben was anxious because he had never been on a trip before. As they drove, Ben saw a big tree. Ben wanted to play, so he asked his family to stop the car. They stopped, and Ben jumped out. He saw a cat and wanted to say hello. Ben bowed to the cat, like a good dog. But the cat did not like Ben. The cat was mean and scratched Ben's nose. Ben was sad and hurt. His family took him back to the car, and they went home. Ben's trip was not fun at all.

One sunny day, Tom and Sue went to the beach. They took a big bucket with them. They wanted to make an impressive sand castle. They dug and dug until their bucket was full of sand. They put the sand on the ground and made a big tower. "Look at our tower!" said Tom. "It's so impressive!" Sue agreed. They were very happy. They wanted to show their friends, so they ran to find them. While they were gone, the sun was very hot. The sun began to melt the sand tower. When Tom and Sue came back with their friends, the tower was not so impressive anymore. It was smaller and not as pretty. "It's okay," said Sue. "We can make another one." So, they all worked together to make a new, even more impressive sand castle. They had a fun day at the beach.

One sunny day, Tom and his dog, Buddy, were eager to go to a new place. They left their home and walked down the street. They saw a big park with many trees, flowers, and a pond. Tom said, "This place looks fun, Buddy! Let's play here." Tom threw a ball, and Buddy ran to catch it. They played for a long time, and then they sat under a tree. Tom saw a big rock near the pond and thought, "That would be a fun place to play later." After resting, Tom and Buddy went to the big rock by the pond. Tom said, "Buddy, be careful by the water." They played on the rock, and Buddy slipped. Tom caught him just in time. They laughed and went back to the tree to rest. At the end of the day, Tom and Buddy left the park. They were happy they found a new place to play. Tom said, "We had so much fun, Buddy. We will come back to this place again soon."

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a mild dragon. The dragon was green and big. The dragon liked to spread its wings and fly high in the sky. The people in the village were not scared of the dragon because it was very nice and friendly. One day, the dragon met a little girl named Lily. Lily looked at the dragon and said, "Hi, dragon! Can you spread your wings and fly with me?" The dragon smiled and said, "Yes, Lily! I would love to fly with you." So, the dragon spread its wings wide, and Lily climbed on its back. Together, they flew high in the sky, laughing and playing. The dragon and Lily became the best of friends, and they had lots of fun adventures together. And the people in the village loved their mild dragon friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little red bird named Tim. He loved to dive and fly fast in the sky. Tim had a friend, a big blue cat named Sam. They played together every day. One day, Tim saw a shiny pan on the ground. He picked it up with his beak and flew to Sam. "Look, Sam! I found a pan!" said Tim. Sam was very excited. "Let's play with it!" said Sam. Tim and Sam took turns diving into the pan. They laughed and had so much fun. Tim would dive fast into the pan, and then Sam would jump in. They played with the pan all day long, and they were very happy.

One day, a little cat named Tom saw a ball near a drain. The ball was stuck and could not come out. Tom wanted to help, so he thought of a plan. He was going to try to get the ball out of the drain. Tom tried to use a stick to get the ball out. But he was not strong enough. He felt foolish because he could not do it. He did not want to give up, so he thought of another plan. This time, Tom asked his friend, a big dog named Max, to help him. Max was very strong. Together, they tried to get the ball out of the drain. With Max's help, they got the ball out! Tom was happy and no longer felt foolish. He learned that sometimes, it is good to ask for help.
