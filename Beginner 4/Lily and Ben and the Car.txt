Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Car

Lily and Ben were playing with their toy cars on the floor. They made a road with some books and blocks. They zoomed their cars along the road and made loud noises. "Vroom, vroom, I'm the fastest car!" Ben said. "No, I'm the fastest car!" Lily said. They raced their cars on the road, but it was difficult. The road had many turns and bumps. Sometimes their cars crashed or fell off the road. "Oops, sorry!" Lily said when her car bumped into Ben's car. "It's okay, let's try again!" Ben said. They fixed their cars and put them back on the road. They smiled and had fun. Then, Lily had an idea. "Hey, Ben, do you want to imagine something?" she asked. "What do you want to imagine?" Ben asked. "Let's imagine that our road is not on the floor, but in the sky!" Lily said. "In the sky? How can we do that?" Ben asked. "Close your eyes and pretend!" Lily said. They closed their eyes and pretended that their road was in the sky. They felt the wind and the sun on their faces. They saw the clouds and the birds around them. They heard their cars flying and honking. "Wow, this is amazing!" Ben said. "I know, right?" Lily said. They opened their eyes and laughed. They hugged their cars and each other. They were happy. They loved to imagine.

Once upon a time, there was a funny frog. The funny frog liked to jump and play. One day, the funny frog found a big rock. He thought it was a fun place to play. The funny frog jumped on the big rock and began to clap. He clapped and clapped, and all his friends came to see him. They laughed and clapped with the funny frog. But then, the big rock started to move. It was not a rock, it was a big, mean animal! The animal did not like the noise. It scared all the friends away. The funny frog was sad and alone.

Once upon a time, there was a small dog. The small dog had a blanket. He liked to spread the blanket on the grass and lay on it. He would play with his ball and eat treats on the blanket. The small dog was very happy. One day, the small dog went to the park with his blanket. He spread the blanket under a big tree. The small dog played with his ball and ate treats on the blanket. Then, he got tired and took a nap on the blanket. While the small dog was sleeping, a big wind came. The wind blew the blanket away. The small dog woke up and saw his blanket was gone. He looked for the blanket, but could not find it. The small dog was very sad. He went home without his blanket.

Once there was a small girl who wanted to try a puzzle but she was scared to touch it. She felt embarrassed to try something new. While walking around the room, she stared at the puzzle with wide eyes, dreaming of solving it. But the more she looked, the more afraid she got. Then, her mother saw the little girl looking at the puzzle. She encouraged her to touch it and said: "It's OK! I'll help you." So, the little girl slowly reached out her small hand and touched the puzzle. To her surprise, it felt very soft. She knew then that she could do it. She took the pieces and started to work on the puzzle, her smile growing wider and wider as she placed the last piece into the right spot. The little girl was so happy. She had done something that she had thought was impossible before. She was proud of herself!

Lily wanted a new doll for her birthday. She saw a pretty doll in a book and asked her mom to order it for her. Her mom said yes and ordered the doll online. Lily was very happy and waited for the doll to come. One day, a big box arrived at the door. Lily ran to open it and saw her new doll inside. She was so excited that she hugged the doll and took it to her room. She wanted to play with the doll and make her hair and clothes look nice. But when she opened the box, she saw a note that said: "Warning: Do not get this doll wet. It will break." Lily did not know what the note meant. She did not know how to read. She threw the note away and took the doll out of the box. She played with the doll for a while and then decided to give her a bath. She filled the tub with water and put the doll in. She thought the doll would like the water and be clean. She did not know that the doll had a battery inside and that water would make it stop working. As soon as the doll touched the water, it started to make a loud noise and sparks. Lily was scared and tried to take the doll out, but it was too late. The doll was broken and wet. Lily started to cry and called for her mom. Her mom came and saw what happened. She was angry and sad. She told Lily that she should have read the note and not get the doll wet. She said that the doll was expensive and that they could not get another one. Lily felt very bad and wished she had listened to her mom. She lost her new doll and her mom was mad at her. She had a very bad birthday.
