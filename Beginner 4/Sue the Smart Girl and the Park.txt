Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Smart Girl and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a smart girl named Sue. Sue loved to plan fun trips with her friends. One day, she found a big map in her room. The map showed a secret park that no one knew about. Sue was very excited and called her friend Tim. "Tim, I found a map to a secret park! Let's plan a trip there!" Sue said. Tim was excited too, so they made a plan to go the next day. The next day, Sue and Tim met at Sue's house. They looked at the map and started to walk. They walked and walked, but they couldn't find the secret park. They were very sad. But then, something unexpected happened. A big, friendly dog came up to them. The dog had a note on its collar that said, "Follow me to the secret park!" Sue and Tim were surprised and happy. They followed the dog, and it led them to the secret park. The park was full of fun things to do, and they played all day. Sue and Tim had the best day ever, and it was all thanks to the smart girl, the map, and the friendly dog.

Once upon a time, there was a puppy called Rusty. He was an adventurous pup, always up for a challenge. One day, he saw a grassy hill and decided to climb it. After a few jumps, he was at the top! He was so excited and wanted to jump off the hill so he did. But when he came down, he felt something sharp in his paw. It felt like pain. Rusty was so sad and started to cry. Suddenly, he heard a soft voice. It was a friendly old cat. She said, “There, there, let me take a look.” The cat was very gentle and slowly examined Rusty's paw. She then said, “It looks like a small thorn was digging into your paw. Let's try to take it out.” The cat took out something soft and put it on Rusty's paw. The soft thing was a bandage and it soothed the pain. After a few minutes, Rusty felt much better. He thanked the cat and ran off, happy to continue his adventures. The cat waved goodbye and watched as Rusty's tail wagged with joy.

Tommy was so excited to go to the park. His mom and dad said it would be fun and warm today. They arrived in the park and Tommy was amazed at how big and green it was. He started running, looking for something to do. “Look, Mommy, look!” he cried, pointing to the swings. “Yes, Tommy, we’ll go there soon,” his mom said. Tommy ran off to explore some more. He came across a slide and some jungle gyms, and then suddenly he heard a funny noise. “It sounds like someone’s singing,” he said. Mom and Dad walked over and smiled when they saw what it was: a man was playing a long and singing a song. “Let’s sit down and enjoy the music,” Dad said. Tommy felt the warm sun on his face as the man filled the park with his beautiful singing. Tommy clapped his hands and smiled. This was the best part of the day so far.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She was a thoughtful girl who loved to play outside. One sunny day, she saw a kite in the sky. Mia wondered who was flying the kite. Mia walked around and found a boy named Tom. Tom was flying the kite. Mia asked if she could fly the kite too. Tom said yes, and they took turns flying the kite in the sky. After a while, the wind stopped and the kite fell down. Mia and Tom were sad. They wondered if they could fly the kite again. But then, they saw the wind start to blow again. They smiled, picked up the kite, and started to play once more.

Jill and Ben liked to play in the shower. They would turn on the water and make it warm. Then they would spin and splash and laugh. They had a lot of fun. One day, Mom said, "You can play in the shower, but only for a little bit. Then you have to get out and dry off. Do you understand?" Jill and Ben nodded. They said, "Yes, Mom. We understand." But they did not listen to Mom. They played in the shower for a long time. They did not hear Mom call them. They did not see the water go down the drain. They did not notice the shower get cold. Soon, the water was gone. The shower was cold and dark. Jill and Ben were wet and shivery. They cried and called for Mom. But Mom did not hear them. She was busy in the kitchen. She thought they were dry and warm. Jill and Ben wished they had listened to Mom. She was wise. She knew what was good for them. They learned a hard lesson. They were sorry. They were sad. They were cold.
