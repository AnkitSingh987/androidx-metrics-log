Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Cat and the Hat

One day, a little cat named Lucy was walking in the woods. She saw a fancy hat on the ground. Lucy thought it was very pretty, so she put the hat on her head. She felt very happy and walked around showing off her new hat. Lucy met her friend, a big dog named Max. Max said, "Lucy, that hat looks nice on you!" But then, Max started to sneeze. "Achoo! Achoo!" he sneezed. Lucy asked, "Are you okay, Max?" Max said, "I don't know, but every time I see your hat, I sneeze." As they walked, they found a trap in the woods. It was a big net, and it looked scary. Lucy and Max decided to stay away from the trap. But then, Lucy's fancy hat flew off her head and landed in the trap. Max sneezed again, "Achoo!" and the sneeze was so big that it pushed the hat out of the trap and back onto Lucy's head. Lucy and Max laughed and walked away from the trap. They learned that sometimes, things that look fancy and pretty can make you sneeze and even lead you into a trap. But with a good friend by your side, you can always find a way out.

One day, a little boy named Tim was very excited. He was going to a big party. Tim wanted to wear a funny hat. He looked in his room and found a monster hat. It had big eyes and a wide smile. Tim put the hat on and went to the party. At the party, Tim saw his friend Sue. Sue looked at Tim's hat and said, "Wow! I like your monster hat!" Tim smiled and said, "Thank you! I like it too!" They played games, ate cake, and had fun. But then, Tim started to feel tired. He forgot where he put his monster hat. Tim was sad. He looked everywhere for his hat. Then, he saw his friend Sue. She was wearing the monster hat! Tim was surprised. Sue said, "I found your hat! I wanted to keep it safe for you." Tim was happy and thanked Sue. They played more games and had more fun at the party.

Sam was a boy who liked to make lemonade. He had a big jar of lemons, a jug of water, and a spoon of sugar. He squeezed the lemons with his hands and poured the juice into the water. He added the sugar and stirred it well. He tasted his lemonade and smiled. It was sweet and sour, just how he liked it. He wanted to share his lemonade with his friend Tom, who lived next door. He filled two cups with his lemonade and went outside. He saw Tom playing with his ball in the yard. He called him, "Hey, Tom, do you want some lemonade? I made it myself!" Tom looked at Sam and his cups. He was curious. He liked lemonade, too. He said, "Sure, Sam, thank you. That's very nice of you." He put down his ball and walked over to Sam. He took one of the cups and drank a sip. But Tom did not smile. He made a face. He spat out the lemonade and said, "Yuck! This is awful! This is the most sour lemonade I ever had! How can you drink this? It hurts my tongue!" Sam was hurt and confused. He did not understand why Tom did not like his lemonade. He thought it was good. He said, "But, Tom, I made it with lemons and sugar and water. It's supposed to be sour. That's what lemonade is. Don't you like sour things?" Tom shook his head. He did not like sour things. He liked sweet things. He said, "No, Sam, I don't like sour things. I like sweet things. You should have put more sugar in your lemonade. Or better yet, you should have used oranges instead of lemons. Oranges are sweet and yummy. Lemons are sour and yucky. You don't know how to make lemonade. You're a bad lemonade maker." Sam felt sad and angry. He did not like what Tom said. He thought Tom was mean and rude. He said, "No, Tom, you're wrong. You don't know how to taste lemonade. You're a bad lemonade taster. You're a bad friend. You don't encourage me. You make fun of me. You're not nice. I don't want to play with you anymore." He took back his cup from Tom and poured the lemonade on the ground. He turned around and ran back to his house. He slammed the door behind him. He did not want to see Tom ever again. He cried in his room. He wished he had a better friend who liked his lemonade. He wished he had a friend who encouraged him.
