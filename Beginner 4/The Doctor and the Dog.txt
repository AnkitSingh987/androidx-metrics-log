Category: Beginner
Title: The Doctor and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a big doctor who liked to organize things. He had a special tool called an x-ray. The x-ray could see inside people and help them feel better. One day, a little independent dog came to the doctor. The dog could do many things all by himself. He could sit, stay, and even fetch a ball. But the dog was not feeling well. The big doctor used the x-ray to look inside the dog. He found a small toy stuck in the dog's tummy. The doctor helped the dog feel better. The independent dog was very happy and went back to playing with his ball. The big doctor was happy too because he could organize and help his friends.

One day, a girl named Sue was walking to the store. She saw a yellow cat sitting by a box. The cat looked sad. Sue wanted to help the cat feel better. She went to the store and bought a toy for the cat. Sue gave the toy to the cat and said, "Here you go, cat. I hope you like it." The cat looked at the toy and then at Sue. The cat started to play with the toy. Sue was happy to see the cat play. The next day, Sue went back to see the cat. But the cat was not there. Instead, there was a big box with a note. The note said, "Thank you for the toy. I have a surprise for you." Sue opened the box and inside was a lot of toys! The cat had received many toys from other kids too. Sue was surprised and happy. She knew that the cat was now happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a cat named Tom. Tom was a harmless cat who loved to play with yarn. One day, Tom saw his friend, the dog named Max. "Max," Tom said, "Can I borrow some yarn to play with?" "Sure, Tom," Max said, "I have some yarn in my house." Tom was very happy and went to Max's house to get the yarn. But when Tom got there, the yarn was gone! Tom and Max were very sad. Just then, a little girl named Lily came to the house. Lily was holding the yarn! She said, "I found this yarn and I made a toy for you both to play with!" Tom and Max were so happy. They played with the toy all day long. And they learned that sharing is more fun than just borrowing.

Once upon a time, there was a furry dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his best friend, a little girl named Sue. They would run and jump in the park every day. Sue and Spot were very happy. One day, Sue saw a new kid in the park. His name was Tim. Sue wanted to be friends with Tim, but she felt shy. Spot saw this and had an idea. He whispered to Sue, "Go say hi to Tim." Sue listened to Spot and walked to Tim. She said, "Hi, I am Sue. Do you want to play?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, I would love to play!" They all played together and had lots of fun. Sue, Spot, and Tim became good friends and learned that the language of friendship is easy to speak.

One day, a little boy named Tim was very excited. He was going to the park with his mom. When they got there, Tim saw a big dog. The dog stared at Tim, and Tim stared back. They both liked to stare. "Can I pet the dog, Mom?" Tim asked. His mom said, "Yes, but be gentle." Tim walked up to the dog and pet its head. The dog wagged its tail and seemed happy. Suddenly, the dog grabbed Tim's waist with its mouth! But it was not mean. The dog was playing a game! It lifted Tim up and put him on its back. Tim laughed and held on tight. The dog ran around the park, giving Tim a fun ride. In the end, Tim and the dog became best friends.

John had a busy day. He needed to organize his toys, clean up his room, and go outside to play. He was scared when he looked out the window and saw the scary weather. It was windy and the trees were shaking. John's mom hugged him and said, "It's ok, you don't need to be scared. We can organize something fun together inside." So John and his mom organized some games to play while they stayed inside. They enjoyed a game of hide and seek and read some books together. John didn't feel so scared anymore. Finally, after the storm passed, John was able to go outside and continue organizing his toys. He was happy to see that the sun was out and the weather was nice. John was grateful to have had someone there to help him feel less scared on a scary day.
