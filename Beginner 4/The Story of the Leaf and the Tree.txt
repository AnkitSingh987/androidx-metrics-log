Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Leaf and the Tree

One day, a happy leaf fell from a tree. The wind blew it far away. The leaf met a small bug. The bug said, "Hi, I am Bug. You look nice." The happy leaf said, "Thank you, Bug. I like the wind. It makes me dance." Bug and the leaf played all day. They had lots of fun. But then, the leaf started to rot. It turned brown and soft. Bug was sad. The leaf said, "Don't be sad, Bug. It is okay. I will turn into food for the ground." Bug smiled and said, "You are a good friend, Leaf. I will miss you." The wind blew the leaf away and Bug waved goodbye.

Once upon a time, there was a high tree. A robot lived in the tree. The robot had a friend, a little bird. They liked to play together. One day, the robot tried to fly like the bird. He jumped from the tree. But robots cannot fly, so he began to fall. The bird saw and yelled, "Robot, be careful!" The bird flew fast to help the robot. The bird caught the robot before he hit the ground. The robot said, "Thank you, bird!" They were happy and played more games on the ground.

One day, a little girl named Sue wanted to go on a trip. She had a big, square bag to pack her things in. Sue was very happy. Sue's friend, Tim, came over to help. "What are you packing?" he asked. "I am packing my mighty toy," said Sue. "It can do many things!" Sue showed Tim the toy, and they played for a while. As they played, something unexpected happened. The mighty toy turned into a real, live dog! Sue and Tim were very surprised. They did not know what to do. But then, they realized they had a new friend to play with. So, they all played together and had a great time.

Once upon a time, there was a clever dog named Spot. Spot loved to play in the mud. One day, he played in the mud all day long. When he got home, he was very dirty. Spot's mom said, "Spot, you need to bathe. You are too dirty!" Spot did not like to bathe, but he knew his mom was right. So, he went to the bathroom and started the water. The warm water felt nice on his fur. As Spot washed, he made a fun motion with his paws to make the water splash. He laughed and said, "Look, Mom! I am making a big splash!" Spot's mom smiled and said, "Good job, Spot! Now you are clean and clever again." And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a magazine on the floor. He picked it up and saw a picture of a man whistling. Tim wanted to whistle too, so he tried to make the same sound. He blew air out of his mouth, but no sound came out. Tim felt sad that he couldn't whistle. Tim's big sister, Lily, saw him looking sad and asked what was wrong. Tim showed her the magazine and told her he wanted to whistle like the man in the picture. Lily smiled and said she could teach him how to whistle. Tim felt happy and excited to learn. Lily showed Tim how to put his lips together and blow air gently. Tim tried again and this time, he made a small whistling sound. He felt so happy and proud of himself. He looked at Lily and said, "Thank you for helping me." Lily felt a little guilty because she knew she should have helped him sooner, but she was happy that Tim could now whistle. They both laughed and whistled together, making music with their new skill.

Once upon a time, there was a big red bus. The bus was very uncomfortable. People did not like to sit on the bus. One day, the bus wanted to make people laugh. The bus thought, "If I make people laugh, they will not feel uncomfortable." The bus tried to be funny. It made funny noises and danced. The bus was very good at dancing. The people laughed and laughed. They forgot about the uncomfortable seats. They were happy on the bus. But then, the bus danced too much. It danced so hard that it broke. The people stopped laughing. They were sad because the bus could not take them home. The bus was also sad. It just wanted to make people happy, but now it could not help the people at all.
