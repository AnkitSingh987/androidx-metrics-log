Category: Beginner
Title: The Castle and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big castle. In the castle, there lived a king and a queen. They had a little boy named Tom. Tom liked to play with his toy castle every day. One day, Tom was playing with his toy castle when he heard a loud sound. He saw a big bird fly into his room. The bird was very big and made a loud noise. The bird flew around the room and then it hit the toy castle. The toy castle broke into many pieces. Tom was very sad. Tom's mom and dad came into the room. They saw the broken castle and the big bird. The bird was not moving. It was dead. Tom's dad said, "Don't worry, Tom. We can fix the castle." They all worked together to fix the castle. When they were done, the castle was even better than before. Tom was happy again, and they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a restless little dog named Spot wanted to travel. He left his house and went to a big valley. In the valley, he saw many pretty flowers and tall trees. "Hello, Spot!" said a small bird named Beaky. "Where are you going?" Spot told Beaky that he was going to travel and see new things. Beaky wanted to go with Spot, so they went together. As they walked, they found a big, red ball. They had never seen a ball so big before! Spot and Beaky played with the ball and had lots of fun. After playing, they went back home, happy that they had a new friend and a big, red ball.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing outside. He saw a big, red ambulance drive by his house. Tim was frightened because he did not know what an ambulance was. He ran to his mom and asked, "Mom, what is that big, red car?" His mom said, "That is an ambulance, Tim. It helps people who are hurt or sick." Later, Tim was playing with his friend Sue. They saw a cat stuck in a tree. Sue was scared and said, "Tim, the cat is stuck! What do we do?" Tim thought for a moment and said, "Let's ask my mom for help." Tim's mom came outside and saw the cat. She tried to reach it, but she could not. She shrugged and said, "I can't reach the cat. Let's call the big, red ambulance for help." The ambulance came and the helpers got the cat out of the tree. Tim, Sue, and Tim's mom were happy. The cat was safe and they all thanked the ambulance helpers. The conflict was solved and everyone was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a green frog named Tim. Tim loved to jump and play with his friends in the big pond. One day, Tim and his friends decided to have a race. They wanted to see who could jump the farthest. Tim was very excited and wanted to win. "Ready, set, go!" said Tim's friend, Sam the snail. Tim and his friends started jumping as far as they could. Tim jumped and jumped, but he did not win. His friend, Lily the ladybug, jumped the farthest. Tim was sad, but he said, "Good job, Lily! You won!" Later that day, Tim found a big, shiny rock. He picked it up and showed it to his friends. Lily said, "That's a memory rock! It can show you a happy memory if you hold it tight." Tim held the rock, and he saw a memory of him and his friends playing together. Tim smiled and realized that even if he did not win the race, he had won something better – a happy memory with his friends.

Jim was a little boy. He was three years old. He was playing in the park one day. He saw a hairy creature. It was a bear. He wanted to get closer. Jim: "Hi bear! What are you doing?" Bear: "I am looking for something to eat. I am so hungry!" Jim: "I have some candy!" Jim gave the bear some candy. The bear was very happy. Bear: "Thank you! Now I can complete my lunch!" Jim: "You're welcome!" The bear ate all the candy. He was very full. They said goodbye and Jim went on his way.
