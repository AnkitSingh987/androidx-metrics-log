Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and the Cake

One day, Tom and his mom were in the kitchen. They wanted to make a cake. Tom saw a mixer on the table. His mom said, "We will use this mixer to mix the cake batter." Tom's mom put all the things in a big bowl. She put the mixer in the bowl too. It was tight, but it fit. They turned on the mixer and it went round and round. Tom was very happy. Suddenly, Tom saw something sharp in the bowl. "Mom, there is something sharp in the cake!" he said. His mom stopped the mixer and looked. She saw a big key in the bowl. "Oh no, that is the key to the toy box!" she said. They laughed and took the key out. Then they finished making the cake.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with water. One day, he found a spray bottle. He was very happy. Tim went to his mom and said, "Look, Mom! I have a spray bottle. I have the power to make things wet!" His mom smiled and said, "That's great, Tim! But remember, only use your power where it's okay. Not everything can be wet." Tim nodded and went outside. He saw a big tree. He thought, "This tree needs water to grow. I will use my power here." So, he sprayed the tree with water. The tree seemed to smile at him. Tim felt very happy. He knew that he used his power in the right place. He learned that it's important to use your power only where it's available and good.

Once upon a time, there was a swamp. In the swamp, there lived a big, green frog named Fred. Fred was an unusual frog because he could talk. One day, a little girl named Sara came to the swamp to play. Sara saw Fred and said, "Hi, frog! You are unusual. Can we play?" Fred smiled and said, "Yes, we can play. But don't spoil the swamp. We must keep it clean." They played all day in the swamp. They jumped and laughed. But then, Sara saw a big, brown bag. She wanted to open it. Fred said, "No, don't open it. It will spoil our swamp." Sara listened to Fred and did not open the bag. They played more and had lots of fun. The swamp stayed clean and pretty. And Fred and Sara became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a blue bird who lived in a tree. The bird was very busy, singing and talking to all of her bird friends. Then one day something special happened. The bird stopped singing, flapped her wings, and became something else. She grew bigger and bigger until she could fly. The bird flew all over the sky, finding new friends and seeing new places. Everywhere she looked, everything was blue! The bird felt so happy and free. But then it was time to come back home. The bird had to be very busy getting ready for the night, eating food, and finding a safe place to sleep. The bird closed her eyes and soon she became a blue bird again. In the morning, she was happy to be back in the tree with all of her bird friends.

One day, a boy named Tim and his friend, Sue, went to play. They found a big tree with many leaves. They wanted to climb the tree. "Look, Tim! I can shoot the leaves with this stick," said Sue. Tim laughed and tried to shoot the leaves too. They had fun shooting the leaves, but then it started to get dark. As it got dark, they heard a loud noise. Tim and Sue were scared. They saw a big fall of leaves from the tree. They didn't know what made the noise. Then, they saw a small cat come out of the leaves. The cat was not hurt. Tim and Sue were happy it was just a cat. They laughed and played with the cat until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet little girl named Lily. She loved to look at the stars in the sky. Every night, she would sit by her window and watch the stars twinkle. One day, Lily found a tiny star on the ground. It was sad because it wanted to go back to the sky. Lily had an idea. She would help the star go back home. Lily took a big balloon and tied the star to it. She held the balloon and said, "I will release you now, little star." The balloon floated up into the sky, taking the star with it. The star was happy and thanked Lily. Now, the sweet little star was back with its friends in the sky, and Lily felt happy too.
