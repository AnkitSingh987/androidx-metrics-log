Category: Beginner
Title: Sam and the Window

A little boy named Sam was feeling very sad. He was looking out of the window in his house and feeling blue. Suddenly, he heard a loud snap. He turned around to see what had happened and saw that his mom had dropped her favorite vase. "Oh no!" cried Sam's mom. "My vase is broken!" Tears started falling down her face. Sam wanted to help. He ran up to his mom and hugged her tight. "It's okay Mommy," he said. "It's just a vase. We can buy another one!" Sam's mom smiled. She hugged Sam back and wiped away the tears from her eyes. "Thank you for being so kind, sweetheart," she said. From then on, any time either of them felt sad, they would remember that moment and give each other a hug.

One day, a big dog and a small cat were playing in the park. The dog had a pretty hat on his head. The cat was very impatient. She wanted to wear the hat too. The dog did not want to share his hat. The cat jumped up to grab the hat from the dog's head. The dog warned the cat, "No, you cannot have my hat." The cat did not listen. She tried again and again to take the hat. The dog was not happy. In the end, the cat was too tired to keep trying. The dog felt bad and decided to share his hat. They took turns wearing the hat and played happily in the park. The cat learned to listen when her friend warned her, and the dog learned to share.

Once upon a time, in a dark forest, there lived a small bird named Tim. Tim was very scared of the dark. Every night, he had a bad dream. In his dream, a big scary monster would come and take him away. Tim did not like this dream at all. One day, Tim met a wise old owl named Mr. Owl. Tim wanted to explain his bad dream to Mr. Owl. Tim hoped Mr. Owl could help him. So, Tim told Mr. Owl about the big scary monster in his dream. Mr. Owl listened very carefully. Mr. Owl thought for a moment and said, "Tim, you must be brave. You must face the dark and the scary monster in your dream." But Tim was too scared. He did not want to face the dark or the scary monster. That night, Tim had the same bad dream again. And because he did not face his fears, the big scary monster took him away.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Max, went to the park. The park was gray and dull. Tim felt sad to see the park like this. He wanted to make the park pretty and fun. So, Tim and Max thought of a plan to decorate the park. They found some colorful stones and put them all around the park. They also painted the old, gray benches with bright colors. Tim said to Max, "Let's make the park the best place to play!" Max barked happily, agreeing with Tim. The next day, when Tim and Max went to the park, they saw that the park was full of people. The people were happy and playing together. The park was not gray and dull anymore. It was colorful and fun. Tim and Max were surprised and happy. They didn't know that their little plan would bring so many people to the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a fine dog with a big, red ball. He loved to play with his ball every day. One day, Spot was playing with his ball near a tree. He saw the ball go high up in the tree. Spot tried to lean on the tree to reach the ball, but he fell down. Spot got a small cut on his leg. Spot's mom saw him and came to help. She put a bandage on his leg to make it all better. Spot felt fine after that. He played with his ball again, but this time, he stayed away from the tree.

One day, a pretty bird was flying high in the sky. She saw a firework on the ground. The bird was curious and wanted to see it up close. So, she flew down and landed next to the firework. "Hello, firework!" said the pretty bird. "Would you like to play with me?" The firework did not say anything, but the bird did not mind. She started to play with the firework, hopping around and chirping happily. Suddenly, the firework started to zoom in the sky. The pretty bird was surprised and scared. She flew away as fast as she could. The firework went up, up, up, and then it made a big, beautiful boom in the sky. The pretty bird was safe, but she learned to be more careful with new friends.
