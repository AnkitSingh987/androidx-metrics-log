Category: Beginner
Title: Wiggly the Lonely Worm and the Garden

Once upon a time, in a small garden, there lived a lonely worm named Wiggly. Wiggly was sad because he had no friends to play with. One sunny day, Wiggly decided to go out and look for a friend. As Wiggly crawled through the garden, he met a happy bird named Chirpy. "Hi, I am Wiggly. Will you be my friend?" asked Wiggly. Chirpy smiled and said, "Yes, I will be your friend, Wiggly!" Together, Wiggly and Chirpy played fun games and had a great time. They laughed and sang songs. When Wiggly did a funny dance, Chirpy clapped her wings and said, "I applaud you, Wiggly! That was a fun dance!" Wiggly felt so happy to have a friend who liked his dance. From that day on, Wiggly and Chirpy were best friends. They played together every day and were never lonely again. And whenever Wiggly did a funny dance, Chirpy would always applaud and cheer for him.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim had a cooler with snacks and drinks for their fun day. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. At the park, Tim met a girl named Sue. They played on the swings and slide together. But then, Sue started to tease Tim. She said, "You can't catch me!" Tim felt sad because he didn't like being teased. Tim's mom saw him looking sad and asked, "What's wrong, Tim?" Tim told her about Sue teasing him. His mom said, "Don't worry, just tell her you don't like it." So Tim told Sue, "Please don't tease me. It makes me sad." Sue said sorry and they played happily together for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy shelf in his room. Tim loved to admire all his toys on the shelf. He would look at them every day and play with them. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. Sam saw the toys on the shelf and said, "Wow, Tim! You have so many toys! I wish I had a shelf like this." Tim felt happy and said, "Thank you, Sam. We can play with them together." But then, Tim's mom came in and saw the toys on the floor. She said, "Tim, you have so many toys, but you don't take care of them. They are all spoiled." Tim felt sad and promised to take better care of his toys. From that day on, he made sure to put his toys back on the shelf and keep them clean.

Once upon a time, there was a happy dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play all day. One day, Spot found a map in the park. The map showed how to find a big red ball. Spot showed the map to his friend, Cat. "Look, Cat! A map to find a big red ball!" said Spot. Cat looked at the map and said, "Let's go find the ball!" So, Spot and Cat went on a long walk to find the big red ball. They walked and walked, getting very tired. Spot said, "Let's rest under this tree." While they rested, a big bird saw the map and took it away. Spot and Cat woke up and saw the map was gone. They were very sad and did not find the big red ball.

Once upon a time, there was a brilliant model. She was so beautiful and she could call out to birds. Every day the birds would come to her and she would talk to them. One day a little girl came to see her. She was only three years old and her name was Sarah. Sarah asked the model, "Can you teach me how to call out to the birds?" The model replied, "Yes, but first you must be patient. Just say 'Hello birds' and clap your hands three times. Do this every day and they will come to you." So Sarah followed the model's instructions. She said "Hello birds" and clapped her hands three times. Sure enough, the birds came flying over to Sarah. She was so excited and clapped her hands in delight. The model smiled and said, "See, I told you. You are a brilliant student!"
