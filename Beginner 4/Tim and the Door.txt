Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He lived in a small house with his mom and dad. Tim loved to play with his toy cars and his big red ball. One day, his mom told him that they would have a surprise for him soon. Tim was so excited, he could not wait! In Tim's house, there was a door with a big knob. Tim always wanted to open the door, but he was too small to turn the knob. He thought that behind the door there must be something powerful, like a big toy or a magic wand. Every day, he tried to open the door, but he just could not do it. Finally, the day of the surprise came. Tim's mom and dad gave him a little step to help him reach the big knob. Tim was so happy! He climbed up the step and turned the knob. Behind the door, there was a big, powerful bicycle just for him! Tim knew that one day he would be able to open the door, and now he had the most amazing surprise. Tim's mom and dad were very proud of him.

Anna was waiting for her birthday. She wanted a big package with a doll inside. She liked to imagine how she would play with the doll and dress her up. She hoped her mom and dad would get her the doll she saw in the store. On her birthday, Anna woke up early and ran to the living room. She saw a big package on the table with a bow on it. She was so happy. She ripped the paper and opened the box. But there was no doll inside. There was a book. Anna was upset. She did not want a book. She wanted a doll. She threw the book on the floor and started to cry. "Anna, what's wrong?" her mom asked. She came to hug her. "I don't like this book. I want a doll. You did not get me a doll. You are bad," Anna said. "Anna, don't say that. We love you. We got you this book because it is a special book. It has stories about dolls. You can read them and imagine how they live and what they do. It is a fun book. You can learn new words and pictures. And you can still get a doll later. We just wanted to give you something different and good for you," her mom said. Anna looked at the book. She saw the cover had a picture of a doll with a dress and a hat. She opened the book and saw more pictures of dolls and words. She felt a little curious. "Can you read me a story, mom?" she asked. "Of course, Anna. I would love to. Come, let's sit on the couch and read together. Happy birthday, my sweet girl," her mom said. Anna smiled and hugged her mom. She still wanted a doll, but she also wanted to hear the stories. She decided to give the book a chance. Maybe it was not so bad after all. Maybe she could imagine having a doll while she read.

One day, a patient cat named Spark went for a walk. He liked to see the sun and feel the wind. Spark saw a bird on the ground. The bird had a hurt wing and could not fly. "Can you help me?" asked the bird. Spark said, "Yes, I will heal your wing." The bird was happy. Spark gently touched the bird's wing, and it started to heal. As the wing healed, the bird started to change. The bird became a big, beautiful butterfly! Spark and the butterfly played all day. They were very happy and became good friends.

Once upon a time, in a big jungle, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a loyal dog named Buddy. They loved to play and explore the jungle together. One day, while they were walking, they saw a big tiger. The tiger looked at them and said, "Hello, I am Tia the tiger. Will you be my friend?" Tim and Buddy were not scared because they could recognize that Tia was a nice tiger. Tim said, "Yes, Tia, we can be friends!" They all played together and had lots of fun. Buddy, the loyal dog, was happy to have a new friend too. And from that day on, Tim, Buddy, and Tia the tiger became the best of friends in the jungle.
