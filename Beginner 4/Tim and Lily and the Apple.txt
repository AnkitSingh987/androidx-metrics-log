Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Lily and the Apple

Tim and Lily are playing in the park. They see a large tree with many red apples. They want to pick some apples, but they are too high. "Look, Tim, there is a ladder!" Lily says. She points to a wooden ladder leaning on the tree. "We can use it to get the apples!" "Good idea, Lily!" Tim says. He runs to the ladder and climbs up. He reaches for an apple, but it is still too far. He leans over the ladder, but he loses his balance. He falls down and lands on the grass. "Ouch!" Tim cries. He rubs his head. "That hurt!" Lily runs to him and hugs him. "Are you okay, Tim?" she asks. She is worried. "I'm okay, Lily," Tim says. He smiles. "But I didn't get any apples." Lily looks at the tree. She sees a low branch with a big apple. She has an idea. She lets go of Tim and runs to the branch. She jumps and grabs the apple. She runs back to Tim and gives him the apple. "Here, Tim, this is for you," she says. "You are brave. This is your reward." Tim takes the apple and bites it. It is sweet and juicy. He shares it with Lily. They both smile. "Thank you, Lily," he says. "You are kind. This is the best apple ever." They hug each other and eat the apple. They are happy. They are friends.

Once upon a time, there was a kind old man. He had a big job to do. The old man needed to build a big barrel out of wood. He found some wood and put it together. But the wood was too harsh, and it just fell apart. The old man was getting tired and frustrated. He said to himself, "What am I going to do?" Suddenly, he heard a tiny voice from the forest. It was a 3 year old child. The child said, "Why don't you use softer wood?" The old man said, "That's a good idea! I'll get softer wood and try again." And so the old man got softer wood and he built the barrel. He was very pleased with the result. The old man thanked the little child and said, "Thank you for your help. You are very wise for such a small one." The child smiled, happy to have helped. The old man put the barrel over his shoulder and went off, to use his barrel for his big job.

Jim was very eager. He had just been given his first license. It was a very special license, and Jim was very proud of it. Walking home, Jim was looking at his license and smiling. Then, he stopped. He saw a bird staring at him from a nearby tree. It was a big, beautiful bird with shiny green feathers. It seemed to be enjoying the sunshine too. Jim couldn't help but stare back. When Jim looked closer, he noticed a small piece of paper tied to the bird's leg. Curious, he slowly got closer. With a gentle touch, he untied the piece of paper and read it carefully. It said: "Thank you! This is my freedom license. I am so excited to be able to fly around and explore this beautiful world. Please help me if you can. Ann"(the year old bird). Jim smiled and nodded. He was eager to help so he took his license and waved it towards the bird. Ann flew away, singing a happy song. Jim watched Ann fly away and knew that he had done the right thing. He put his license back in his pocket and smiled.

One day, a tall girl named Sue wanted to enter a fun place. She saw a big pen with many ducks inside. Sue loved ducks and wanted to play with them. But the pen was locked. Sue was sad. Sue saw a nice man who worked there. She asked, "Can I go in the pen and play with the ducks?" The man said, "No, the ducks might get scared. You can watch them from here." Sue was not happy, but she understood. Sue stood outside the pen and watched the ducks. She clapped her hands and laughed as they waddled around. Even though she could not enter the pen, Sue still had a good time watching the ducks play.
