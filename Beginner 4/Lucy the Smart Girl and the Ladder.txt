Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Smart Girl and the Ladder

Once there was a smart girl named Lucy. She was only three years old, but she was already a very clever girl. One day, Lucy wanted to get a sheet from her room. She asked her mom for help, but mom said she had to try and do it on her own. So Lucy set off to reach the sheet. But it was too high and Lucy could not reach it. She tried many things, like standing on her tippy toes, but nothing seemed to work. Then, Lucy had a very smart idea. She decided to get a chair and use it as a ladder. She climbed up carefully and was able to reach the sheet. She grabbed it and proudly picked it up. Mom was so proud of Lucy for being so smart and finding a way to get the sheet. Lucy smiled, happy that she had made her mom proud.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She loved to go outside and explore. One day, she went for a walk in her garden and saw a big leaf. She wanted to take it home and she remembered that her mom said it was ok. But when she tried to take it, the leaf wouldn't move. She tugged and pulled, but it wouldn't come off the ground. It seemed stuck, no matter what she did. She started to get sad. Then suddenly, the wind began to blow. It blew really hard and suddenly the leaf lifted off the ground and began to float! She was so happy! She ran and chase the leaf as it flew around in the cool and breeze. When it finally landed in the grass, she knew that it was hers to keep forever. She stepped on it and put it in her pocket so she could remember the cool windy day she flew a leaf.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big box. The box was not a regular box. It had a lot of holes in it. Tim was very curious about the box. Tim asked his dad, "Dad, what can I do with this box?" His dad said, "You can add screws into the holes, Tim." Tim got very excited. He wanted to add screws to the box. Tim and his dad went to the garage. They found many screws. They added screws into the holes of the box. Tim was very happy. He had a great time with his dad and the special box.

One day, a big cat named Tom went to play outside. He saw a big red wheel near the tree. Tom was very happy and wanted to play with the wheel. He pushed the wheel and it started to roll very fast. Tom followed the wheel as it rolled into a small house. He entered the house to find the wheel. Inside, he saw many toys in a very organized way. There were dolls, cars, and balls all in neat rows. Tom was very surprised to see so many toys. As Tom looked around, a little girl named Lily came into the room. She saw Tom and the wheel and was very happy. The plot twist was that the wheel belonged to Lily, and she had lost it. Tom and Lily became good friends and played with the wheel and other toys together. They had lots of fun in the organized toy room.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He had a toy gun. Tim liked to play with his gun. But one day, he lost his gun. He felt terrible. Tim asked his mom to replace his gun. His mom said, "No, Tim. Guns are not good toys." Tim was sad, but his mom gave him a ball to play with instead. Tim played with the ball. He threw the ball very high. The ball hit a window and broke it. Tim's mom was angry. Tim learned that playing with guns or balls near windows was not a good idea. The moral value is to be careful when playing with toys.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy duck. The duck liked to rush and play with his friends in the pond. One day, the duck saw a big frog sitting near the pond. The duck went to the frog and said, "Hi, frog! Do you want to play with me?" The frog looked at the duck and said, "No, I am too big to play with you." The nosy duck felt sad, but he did not give up. He rushed to find a new friend. Soon, he met a little fish in the pond. The duck said, "Hi, fish! Do you want to play with me?" The fish smiled and said, "Yes, I would love to play with you!" The nosy duck and the little fish played together in the pond all day. They had so much fun, and the duck learned that it is okay if some friends do not want to play. There are always other friends who will be happy to play and have fun together.
