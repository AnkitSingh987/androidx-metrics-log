Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Bell and the Pin

Once upon a time, there was a small, round pin. The pin was red and shiny. It lived on a big board with other pins. One day, the pin wanted to move to a new spot on the board. It asked its friend, the blue pin, for help. "Can you help me move, please?" asked the red pin. The blue pin was kind and said, "Yes, I will help you." They moved together to a new spot on the board. They were very happy in their new place. But then, a loud bell rang. It scared the red pin and the blue pin. They jumped and moved back to their old spot on the board. They were safe and not scared anymore. The pins stayed there and played together.

Tim and Sam were brothers. They liked to play in the kitchen when Mom was not there. They saw a big jar of jam on the shelf. They wanted to eat some jam. "Let's get the jam," Tim said. "We can use the chair to reach it." Sam nodded. He pushed the chair to the shelf. Tim climbed on the chair and grabbed the jar. But the jar was heavy and slippery. Tim lost his balance and fell down. The jar broke on the floor. Jam and glass were everywhere. "Ow, ow, ow!" Tim cried. He had cuts on his hands and legs. Jam was on his face and clothes. He was bleeding and sticky. Sam was scared. He ran to the door and shouted. "Help, help, help! Tim is hurt!" Mom heard Sam and came to the kitchen. She saw the mess and Tim. She was angry and worried. She called Dad and the doctor. She took Tim to the bathroom and tried to dry his wounds. But it was too late. Tim had lost too much blood. He closed his eyes and did not wake up. Mom and Sam were very sad. They wished they had not played with the jam. They learned that jam was dangerous.

Once upon a time there was a little girl. She wanted to explore outside. Everywhere she looked seemed mysterious and fun. She wanted to know what was around the corner. The little girl did not know what to do. She asked her mom if she could go explore. But her mom said no, it was too dangerous. The little girl was sad and did not know what to do. But then she had an idea! She went to the top of the hill and saw a safe. It was big and mysterious. She wanted to open it, but her mom said to stop. The little girl listened and stopped opening the safe. She wanted to know what was inside, but it was more important to be safe. Her mom was happy that she listened and gave her a big hug. The little girl was happy. She found a way to be safe and still explore. She was now ready to explore the mysterious outside world!

Once upon a time, there was a mum who loved baking in the kitchen. She had lots of tasty recipes that she liked to make every day. But one day she realised that all her ingredients were not available. She was very sad. She sat in the kitchen and thought about what to do. Then she had an idea. She went around the neighbourhood and asked her friends if they had any ingredients that she could use. Some of them did and she was very happy. She baked delicious treats in the kitchen with the ingredients she found. She shared them with her friends and thanked them for being so helpful. Moral of the story: We should always love and help each other.

Once upon a time, there was a big lawn. On the lawn, there were two friends, Tim the turtle and Dan the dog. Tim and Dan loved to play on the lawn every day. They were very happy. One day, Tim said to Dan, "I trust you to be a dependable friend." Dan smiled and said, "I trust you too, Tim. We are good friends." As they played, they saw a little boy. The boy was sad because he lost his toy. Tim and Dan wanted to help. They looked all over the lawn. At last, they found the toy. The boy was so happy. He thanked Tim and Dan for being dependable friends. Tim and Dan were happy too. They knew they could trust each other.
