Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Moon

Lily liked to pick flowers in the garden. She picked red ones, yellow ones, and purple ones. She put them in a big vase on the table. She was very tidy and did not leave any mess. One night, Lily looked out of the window and saw the moon. It was big and round and bright. She wanted to pick the moon and put it in her vase with the flowers. She thought it would look very pretty. She asked her mom, "Can I pick the moon, please?" Her mom smiled and said, "No, Lily, you can't pick the moon. The moon is very far away and very big. You can only look at it and admire it." Lily was sad. She wanted to pick the moon so much. She said, "But I like the moon. It is shiny and nice. Can I pick it tomorrow?" Her mom hugged her and said, "No, Lily, you can't pick the moon tomorrow either. The moon is not a flower. It is a star. It belongs in the sky, not in a vase. But you can always pick more flowers in the garden. They are beautiful and smell good. And you can always watch the moon at night and make a wish." Lily nodded. She understood. She said, "Okay, mom. I will pick more flowers tomorrow. And I will watch the moon and make a wish. I wish I could pick the moon, but I know I can't. I love you, mom." Her mom kissed her and said, "I love you too, Lily. Good night, sweetie." She tucked her in and turned off the light. Lily closed her eyes and dreamed of the moon and the flowers. She was happy.

Sara was a little girl who liked to play with her toys. She had many toys in her cabinet, but she wanted more. She saw a big box on the shelf that said "New Toys". She wanted to open it and see what was inside. She climbed on a chair and reached for the box. But the box was too heavy and too far. She struggled to pull it down, but she could not. She tried harder and harder, but she only made the box shake and wobble. She did not listen to her mom, who told her to stop and be careful. Suddenly, the box fell down and hit Sara on the head. It hurt a lot and made a loud noise. Sara cried and cried. Her mom came and hugged her. She checked her head and said, "You are lucky, Sara. You only have a small bump. But you could have been hurt badly. Why did you do that?" Sara sniffed and said, "I wanted to see the new toys. I was curious." Her mom said, "I understand, Sara. But you should not take things that are not yours. And you should not climb on the shelf. It is dangerous. The new toys are for your cousin, who is coming to visit tomorrow. He has no toys at home, because he is poor. We wanted to surprise him and make him happy. How do you think he would feel if he saw the box broken and the toys scattered?" Sara felt sorry and ashamed. She said, "I am sorry, mom. I did not know. I was selfish and greedy. I have many toys, but I wanted more. I did not think about my cousin. I was grumpy and naughty." Her mom said, "It is okay, Sara. I forgive you. But you have to learn to be happy with what you have, and to share with others. And you have to listen to me, and obey the rules. They are for your own good. Can you do that?" Sara nodded and said, "Yes, mom. I can do that. I love you, mom. And I love my cousin. Can I help you fix the box and the toys?" Her mom smiled and said, "Of course, Sara. You are a good girl. I love you too. Come on, let's go and do it together."

Once upon a time, there was a little shrimp. The shrimp lived in a big, blue sea. It loved to swim and play with its friends. The shrimp had a lot of fun every day. One day, the shrimp met a big fish. The big fish was amazing. It could change colors and make funny faces. The shrimp and the big fish became friends. They swam and played together every day. One day, they found a treasure chest on the sea floor. The big fish helped the shrimp open it. Inside, they found a shiny, pretty crown. The shrimp put the crown on its head and kneeled like a king. The big fish laughed and clapped. They played with the crown and had a great day.
