Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Helpful Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a helpful dog named Max. Max liked to enjoy playing with his toy ball. One day, Max saw a tap near his house. He thought it would be fun to play with the tap. Max played with the tap, and water came out. He was very happy. But soon, the water began to make a big puddle. Max did not know what to do. He was scared that his ball would get wet. Just then, a friendly bird flew by and saw Max's problem. The bird knew how to turn off the tap, so it helped Max. The water stopped, and Max's ball was safe. Max was very thankful for the bird's help. They became best friends and enjoyed playing together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly sun. The sun loved to shine all day. The sun had a friend named Hanger. Hanger was a happy cloud. They played together in the sky. One day, Hanger wanted to shine like the sun. The sun had an idea. The sun told Hanger to go near the water. Hanger did what the sun said. The sun shined on Hanger. The water made Hanger shine too! But then, something unexpected happened. Hanger shined so much that it turned into a big, shiny rainbow! The sun and Hanger were so happy. They played together with the pretty rainbow. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had his own bed in his room. The bed was big and had colorful blankets. Every night, Tim would sleep in his bed and have sweet dreams. One day, Tim's bed became uncomfortable. It was hard to sleep on it. Tim tried to fix it, but he didn't know how. He felt sad and couldn't sleep well. His mom and dad noticed that he was tired and unhappy. Tim's mom and dad decided to help him. They went to the store and bought a new, soft bed for Tim. They put the new bed in his room and took the uncomfortable bed away. Tim was so happy! That night, he slept in his new bed and had the best dreams ever.

One day, a big snake was in the grass. A small boy named Tim saw the snake. He was scared. The snake was not easy to see because it was hiding. Tim wanted to play, but the snake was in his way. Tim said, "Snake, please move. I want to play here." The snake did not move. Tim thought, "How can I make the snake go away?" He had an idea. He would lift a big rock and put it on the snake. Tim tried to lift the rock. It was heavy. He tried again and again. At last, he lifted the rock. He put it near the snake. The snake saw the rock and got scared. It moved away from the grass. Now, Tim could play. He was happy. The snake was gone, and the grass was safe. Tim learned that he could solve problems if he tried hard.

Once upon a time, there was a fish who loved to swim. He would swim all day and blink his eyes. One day, he saw a big, impressive rock in the water. He thought it would be fun to swim around it. As the fish swam closer to the rock, he blinked his eyes and saw that the rock was moving! The fish was scared, but he kept swimming. The rock was not a rock at all, but a big turtle! The fish and the turtle became best friends. They would swim and play together all day. The fish was happy that he found such an impressive friend in the water. And they lived happily ever after.

One summer day, a little dog stood in the park. He liked to be useful, so he helped kids play with their toys. He also liked to stand next to the swings and slide. He was a very good dog. One day, a big dog came to the park. He wanted to play with the little dog, but the little dog was scared. The big dog was too big and too loud. The little dog did not want to play with him. The little dog had an idea. He found a big ball and gave it to the big dog. The big dog liked the ball and played with it. The little dog stood near the swings and slide again. The big dog was happy, and the little dog was happy too. They both had fun at the park in the summer.
