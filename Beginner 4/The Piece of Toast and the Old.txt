Category: Beginner
Title: The Piece of Toast and the Old

Once upon a time, in a small kitchen, there lived an old piece of toast. The old toast had been there for many days. It had been forgotten by the people who lived in the house. The old toast felt lonely and sad. One day, a young and happy piece of bread came into the kitchen. The young bread saw the old toast and said, "Hi, friend! Why are you so sad?" The old toast replied, "I am sad because I am old and nobody wants me." The young bread thought for a moment and said, "Let's unite and be friends. Together, we can make people happy." The old toast liked the idea and they both became friends. They played and laughed together in the kitchen. Soon, the people in the house noticed the happy old toast and the young bread. They decided to make a sandwich with them. The old toast and the young bread were so happy to be useful and make people happy. From that day on, the old toast and the young bread stayed united as friends, making people happy in the kitchen. They were never lonely or sad again.

One day, an adorable little plane wanted to perform a show for all its friends. The plane was very excited. It wanted to make everyone happy with its tricks in the sky. The day of the show came. The sun was shining and the sky was blue. The adorable plane started to perform. It went up and down, round and round. All the friends watched and clapped. When the show was over, the adorable plane was very happy. It had made its friends smile and laugh. The plane knew it had done a good job. And from that day on, the adorable plane loved to perform for all its friends.

One day, a rhinoceros named Ron was walking in the forest. He was upset because he lost his toy. Ron met his friend, a bird named Ben. "Why are you upset, Ron?" asked Ben. "I lost my toy and I can't find it," said Ron. Ben wanted to help Ron. They looked for the toy together. While they were looking, they saw a big tree with a hole in it. They went to look inside the hole. Inside the hole, they found a big pile of toys! They were so happy. But then, they heard a loud snore. A bear was taking a nap next to the toys. The bear woke up and said, "These are my toys now!" Ben had an idea. "Let's all play with the toys together!" he said. The bear, Ron, and Ben played with the toys and had so much fun. They all became friends and shared the toys. The bear was not upset anymore and Ron found his toy.

Lily was a princess who liked to play with her dolls. She had a big pink crown that sparkled in the sun. She put it on her head and pretended to be the queen of the castle. One day, a hairy dog came to the castle. He was brown and fluffy and had a long tail. He saw Lily's crown and wanted to play with it. He ran up to Lily and jumped on her. He knocked the crown off her head and ran away with it. Lily was sad and angry. She chased the dog and shouted, "Give me back my crown! It is mine!" But the dog was fast and ran all over the garden. He thought it was a fun game. Lily asked her friends to help her. They were a knight, a fairy, and a dragon. They all had costumes and toys to play with. They followed the dog and tried to catch him. But the dog was clever and dodged them. Finally, the dog got tired and stopped by a pond. He saw his reflection in the water and thought it was another dog. He barked and wagged his tail. He dropped the crown and tried to play with the other dog. Lily saw her chance and ran to the pond. She grabbed her crown and put it back on her head. She was happy and relieved. She said, "Thank you, friends, for helping me. You are the best!" The dog realized his mistake and felt sorry. He bowed his head and wagged his tail. He wanted to say sorry to Lily and be her friend. He licked her hand and looked at her with big eyes. Lily smiled and petted the dog. She said, "It's okay, dog. You didn't mean to be bad. You can be my friend, too. But you have to be gentle and share. Do you understand?" The dog nodded and barked. He understood. He was happy and grateful. He joined Lily and her friends and played with them. They all had fun and were happy. The end.
