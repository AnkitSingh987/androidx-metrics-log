Category: Beginner
Title: Billy and the Ceiling

Billy was very sleepy. He had been playing all day and his eyes were far too droopy for him to focus on anything for too long. Suddenly, he heard a loud noise coming from up high. He looked up to the ceiling and saw something very sharp poking through the paint. He was scared and asked his mother what it was. "That is a nail," she said. "It has come out from the top of the walls." Billy was still scared. He wanted to go away from the nail, "Can we go?", he asked. "No, we can't go," she said. "We need to stay here until it's fixed." Billy was starting to get scared. He thought it was never going to be fixed. But then he heard another loud noise. He looked up and now there were two nails poking through the ceiling. His mother told him to go away, but Billy was just too sleepy to move. Suddenly, the whole ceiling came crashing down and both of them were buried under the rubble. Billy fell asleep forever, never to wake up again.

Once upon a time, there was a little car. The car was red and shiny. The car loved to speed on the road. It was very fast and it liked to race with other cars. The car did not like to be slow because it was boring. One day, the car saw a figure far away. The figure was a blue car. The red car wanted to race the blue car. So, the red car went fast to the blue car. The blue car saw the red car and they started to race. The red car and the blue car raced together. They went very fast. The race was not boring. The red car was happy to have a friend to race with. In the end, they both won the race and became best friends. They raced together every day and had lots of fun.

Lily liked to feed the ducks at the pond. She had a bag of bread and a cute hat. She saw a big duck with a red beak. She threw some bread to him. He came closer and closer. "Hello, big duck," Lily said. "Do you want more bread?" The big duck nodded. He opened his mouth wide. Lily smiled. She reached out her hand with more bread. But the big duck did not want the bread. He wanted Lily's hand. He bit it hard. "Ow!" Lily cried. "Let go, bad duck!" She tried to pull her hand away, but the big duck was stronger. He dragged her into the pond. Lily screamed. She was scared and wet. "Help! Help!" she shouted. She hoped someone would hear her. She hoped someone would come and save her. She hoped for a hero. But no one came. The big duck pulled her under the water. Lily could not breathe. She closed her eyes. She did not see the cute hat floating on the pond.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play with her hoop. One sunny day, she went outside to play with her hoop in the park. Lucy saw her friend, Tom, who was trying to burn some leaves. "Tom, be careful with fire!" said Lucy. Tom was dependable, so he stopped burning the leaves and joined Lucy to play with the hoop. They had lots of fun playing with the hoop. They laughed and played until the sun went down. Lucy and Tom were happy to have each other as dependable friends. And they promised to always be careful with fire.

Once upon a time, there was a wide tree. A little boy named Tim lived near the tree. One day, Tim saw a big red ball stuck in the tree. He wanted to get the ball down. Tim tried to shake the tree, but the ball did not move. Then, he saw a stick on the ground. He picked it up and tried to poke the ball. The stick was too short. Tim found a longer stick and tried again. This time, the stick broke. "Oh no," said Tim, "I need help." A tall man heard Tim and came to help. The man reached up and grabbed the ball. As he did, he felt an itch on his arm. "Ouch, that tickles!" he said. Tim thanked the man and they both laughed. Tim was happy to have his ball back and they played together under the wide tree.
