Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Max. Max liked to play with a copper ball. The ball was shiny and fun to chase. Max would run and jump to catch the ball. One day, Max's friend, a little girl named Lily, came to play with him. They played in the yard. They played games that include Max's copper ball. Max was very happy to play with Lily. After a while, Max and Lily got tired. They sat under a big tree to rest. Max put his copper ball next to him. Then, Max and Lily talked and laughed. They had a great day playing with the copper ball.

Once upon a time, there was a kind aunt named Sue. She had a little niece named Lily. Lily loved to admire her aunt's pretty dresses and shiny shoes. One day, aunt Sue told Lily, "I will give you a surprise if you are good and help me today." Lily was excited and helped her aunt all day. They picked up toys, washed dishes, and folded clothes. Aunt Sue smiled and said, "You are a big help, Lily. Now, it is time for your surprise!" Aunt Sue gave Lily a pretty dress and shiny shoes just like hers. Lily was so happy! She put them on and admired herself in the mirror. Aunt Sue said, "You look lovely, Lily. Remember, it is good to help others and be kind." From that day on, Lily always helped her aunt and others. She learned that being kind and helpful made her feel happy, too. And whenever she wore her pretty dress and shiny shoes, she remembered the day she learned to be kind like her aunt Sue.

Once upon a time, there was a king who lived in a very big and beautiful palace. In the palace, there was a special room that the king particularly liked. Whenever the king was feeling stressed, he would come to this room and relax. The room was very tidy. Everything was in its place, and that made the king feel good. One day, he asked his servant to come to the tidy room. "My king, why did you summon me to this room?", his servant asked. The king replied, "I come here to relax. The room is so tidy, it fills me with calm. Now, I want you to relax too. Come, take a seat, it will make you feel better." The servant did as commanded and was soon feeling more relaxed. He thanked the king and said, "Your palace is so lovely. There is no better place to relax!"

Once upon a time, there was a wide tree. In the tree, there were two birds, Tom and Sue. They liked to sing songs. They liked to sing songs with a fun rhythm. They would sing all day long. One day, Tom and Sue had a problem. They could not agree on which song to sing. Tom wanted to sing a fast song. Sue wanted to sing a slow song. They were not happy. Tom and Sue needed to discuss their problem. They talked and talked. They found a way to fix the problem. They would take turns singing the songs they liked. First, they would sing Tom's fast song. Then, they would sing Sue's slow song. They were happy again.

Tom liked to play golf with his dad. They had a bag with sticks and balls. They hit the balls with the sticks and tried to make them go in a hole. Tom had a small stick and a small ball. His dad had a big stick and a big ball. One day, they went to the golf place. But there were many cars and people there. They had to wait and wait. Tom was bored. He wanted to play golf. He asked his dad, "Why do we have to wait?" His dad said, "There is a delay. That means something is making us wait. Maybe there is a problem with the golf place. Maybe there are too many people who want to play golf. We have to be patient and wait our turn." Tom was not happy. He wanted to play golf now. He looked at the sky. It was gray and cloudy. He saw some raindrops falling. He was worried. He asked his dad, "What if it rains? Can we still play golf?" His dad said, "If it rains, we might have to stop playing golf. Rain can make the grass wet and slippery. Rain can make the balls hard to see and hit. Rain can make us cold and wet. We don't want that. We want to have fun and play golf." Tom was sad. He hoped it would not rain. He hoped the delay would end soon. He hoped they could play golf. He loved to play golf with his dad.
