Category: Beginner
Title: The Frog and the Pond

Once, in a small village by a pond, there lived a frog. The frog was very popular, as everyone knew her. One morning, the frog decided she wanted to raise the pond so she could explore more of the world. But she hadn't thought it through, as she realised she didn't know how to do it. The frog was sad and thought she may never achieve her goal. Then one night, a mysterious dragonfly buzzed by and told her the secret she needed. The frog was so happy, she immediatly started to raise the pond. With a lot of hard work, the frog was able to make the pond nice and tall. From her much higher vantage point, she was able to explore the world and happily swim around. The frog was so proud of herself and thanked the dragonfly for helping her. From then on, the frog enjoyed much more of the world than before and even more people became interested in her. She was now even more popular than before!

Once upon a time, there was a fox named Spot. Spot had a secret spot in the woods that he found which was always open. Spot had a fun time playing in this secret spot every day and it was his favorite place to play. One day, Spot saw that somebody else had signed the tree in his secret spot. He was very upset and he wanted to find out who had done it. Spot thought long and hard about what he was going to do. Then he had an idea! He decided to set a trap in his secret spot and he made sure it was open so that the intruder could get in. He hoped that the person who had signed the tree would come back. The next day, Spot scurried to the secret spot. When he arrived, he saw that the intruder had set off his trap! He jumped out of his hiding spot and saw that the intruder was an owl. The owl explained to Spot that he had signed the tree because he thought that this was his special spot too. Spot forgave the owl and the two friends began to play together in the secret spot. From then on, Spot and the owl played in the same spot and it was a wonderful open space for them to play.

Anna and Ben were playing on the beach. They liked to dig in the sand and look for shells. They saw a big rock near the water. They ran to the rock and climbed on it. "Look, Ben, a crab!" Anna said. She pointed to a small red crab that was hiding under the rock. The crab had two big claws and many legs. "Wow, a crab!" Ben said. He wanted to touch the crab. He got down from the rock and kneeled on the sand. He reached his hand to the crab. "Be careful, Ben, the crab can pinch you!" Anna said. She was serious. She knew that crabs could hurt with their claws. Ben did not listen to Anna. He touched the crab. The crab did not like that. The crab pinched Ben's finger with its claw. Ben cried out loud. He pulled his hand back and held his finger. "Ow, ow, ow, the crab bit me!" Ben said. He was sad and angry. He wanted to throw the crab away. "Ben, don't do that!" Anna said. She was sorry for Ben, but also for the crab. She knew that the crab was scared and only wanted to be left alone. "Let's leave the crab here, Ben. It's his home. He did not mean to hurt you. He was just protecting himself." Anna said. She tried to calm Ben down. Ben looked at Anna. He looked at the crab. He looked at his finger. He was still in pain, but he nodded. He agreed with Anna. "OK, Anna, let's leave the crab here. I'm sorry, crab. I won't touch you again." Ben said. He and Anna got up from the sand. They walked away from the rock. They went back to their bucket and shovel. They continued to play on the beach. They were friends again.
