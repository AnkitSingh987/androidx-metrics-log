Category: Beginner
Title: The Colorful Bird and the Fish

One day, a colorful bird went to the beach. The bird saw a big surf. The bird wanted to play in the surf. The bird was very happy. The bird met a fish. The fish said, "Be careful in the surf, you might lose something." The bird did not listen. The bird wanted to play in the surf. The bird played in the surf. The bird had fun. But then, the bird lost its colors. The bird was sad. The bird was not colorful anymore. The bird wished it had listened to the fish.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a girl named Amy. Amy was a little bit crazy. She liked to play and have fun all day. One day, she found a small pin on the floor. She picked it up and looked at it. Amy had an idea. She dared her friend, Tim, to play a game with her. They would see who could jump over the pin without touching it. Tim was scared at first, but he wanted to be brave, so he said yes. They took turns jumping over the pin. Amy jumped very high and did not touch the pin. Tim tried his best too. They laughed and played together. In the end, they both won the game because they had so much fun. They put the pin away safely and went to eat some yummy snacks.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to play outside. She saw a cool, big rock and sat on it. Sue felt happy and hoped her friend, Tim, would come and play with her. Soon, Tim came running to the rock. "Hi, Sue!" he said. "I hope we can play a fun game today." Sue smiled and said, "Yes, let's play hide and seek!" They played hide and seek for a long time. Tim hid behind a tree and Sue tried to find him. When Sue found Tim, she gave him a friendly rub on his back. "Good hiding, Tim!" she said. They played and laughed all day. When it was time to go home, Sue and Tim said goodbye. "I hope we can play again soon," Sue said. Tim smiled and said, "Me too, Sue. That was a lot of fun!" Sue went home feeling happy and tired. She had a great day playing with Tim and couldn't wait to play again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Lily loved to perform for her family. She would sing and dance in the living room every day. She felt happy and ready to perform all the time. One day, Lily was dancing in the living room. She was so excited to perform a new dance for her mom and dad. But she didn't see the pretty vase on the table. She bumped into the table and the vase fell down. It broke into many pieces. Lily felt very sad and her mom was upset too. Lily wanted to make her mom happy again. She thought and thought about what to do. Then, she had an idea! She picked up all the broken pieces of the vase and put them back together with her dad's help. When they were done, the vase looked almost like new. Lily's mom was so happy and gave her a big hug. From that day on, Lily made sure to be careful when she performed her dances.

Rosie and her dad were walking on the beach. Dad said, “Rosie, stay close. Look around and you can see the ocean. It’s beautiful.” Rosie saw a big wreck standing in the water and said, “Dad, what’s that?” Dad said, “It’s an old ship that got cut off a long time ago.” They kept walking, but when they passed the wreck again, something had changed. “Look at the wreck,” Dad said. “It’s opened!” Rosie and dad stepped closer and Dad said, “That was very brave. Now let’s go.” But Rosie wanted to look inside the wreck. She stepped forward, and when she did, she saw all sorts of spoiled things on the floor of the ship. As Rosie and dad walked away from the wreck, Dad said, “You know, some of those things could have been very valuable. We have to be careful what we do around old wrecks.” Rosie knew she should have listened to her dad, but now the wreck was open and all the goodies inside were ruined.
