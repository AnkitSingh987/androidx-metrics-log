Category: Beginner
Title: Mark the Dog and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Mark. Mark lived in a small house with a big cupboard. The cupboard was full of toys and yummy treats. One day, Mark wanted to play with his toys. He tried to open the cupboard with his paws, but it was too hard. Mark was sad, but he did not give up. He had an idea! Mark found a big stick and used it to open the cupboard. The toys and treats fell out, and Mark was very happy. He played all day with his toys and shared the treats with his friends. From that day on, Mark and his friends had lots of fun playing together.

Once upon a time, there was a little sock. This sock was very delicate. It was soft and pretty. The sock had a mark on it. The mark was a tiny heart. The heart was pink. One day, the sock went on an adventure. It walked and walked. The sock met a big dog. The dog was nice. The sock and the dog played together. They played catch and had fun. At the end of the day, the sock was tired. It said goodbye to the dog. The sock walked back home. The sock was happy. It had a great adventure with its new friend, the dog. The sock went to sleep, dreaming of more fun days.

Once upon a time, there was a skeleton. The skeleton liked to stand all day. One day, he met a dog. The dog was important to the skeleton because the dog was his friend. The skeleton said, "Dog, let's play!" The dog said, "Yes, let's play!" They played all day. They were happy. But then, it started to rain. The rain was not good for the skeleton. The skeleton said, "Oh no, I don't like rain!" The dog said, "I will help you!" The dog tried to help the skeleton, but it was too late. The rain made the skeleton fall apart. The dog was sad. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little pupil named Tim. Tim liked to help his friends at school. One day, a new kid named Sam came to school. Sam was very good at drawing and all the kids liked his drawings. Tim felt a little jealous because he wanted to be the best at drawing too. Tim asked his teacher how he could be better at drawing. The teacher told him to practice and help others with their drawings. So, Tim started to help Sam and the other kids with their drawings. He learned new ways to draw and got better every day. Soon, Tim was not jealous anymore. He was happy to help his friends and be a good pupil. All the kids liked Tim and Sam, and they all drew pictures together. They had lots of fun and became the best of friends.

One day, a girl named Lily went for a walk with her mom. They saw a big tree with red fruit on it. Lily asked her mom, "What is that fruit, Mom?" Her mom said, "That is a bitter fruit. It does not taste good." Lily wanted to see if her mom was right. She picked one fruit and took a small bite. "Yuck!" she said. "It tastes bad, just like you said, Mom." Later, they walked to a park. The sun was shining with a bright light. Lily felt happy and warm. She forgot about the bitter fruit and played with her friends. At the end of the day, Lily and her mom went home. They had a fun day, even though the fruit tasted bad. Lily learned to listen to her mom and enjoy the light of the sun.

One day, a smart cat named Tom found a big pencil. He wanted to draw a picture. He started to draw a house, a tree, and a sun. Tom was very happy with his picture. Tom saw his friend, a dog named Max. Tom said, "Max, look at my picture! I used a big pencil to draw it." Max looked at the picture and said, "Wow, Tom, you are a smart cat! Can you teach me how to draw too?" Tom and Max started to draw together. They were having fun. Then, a big wind came and blew their pictures away. Tom and Max were sad. But they did not give up. They learned to load rocks on the pictures to keep them safe from the wind. The moral of the story is to never give up and to always find a way to fix a problem. Tom and Max were smart friends who learned how to keep their pictures safe.
