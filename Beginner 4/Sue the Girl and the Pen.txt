Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Pen

One day, a girl named Sue was ready to write a letter. She wanted to write a letter to her friend, Tom. She had a big shock when she saw that her pen was gone. "Oh no, where is my pen?" Sue said. Sue looked for her pen everywhere. She looked in her room, in the kitchen, and even in the yard. She asked her mom, "Mom, have you seen my pen?" Her mom said, "No, but maybe your brother has it." Sue went to her brother's room and found her pen on his table. "Hey, that's my pen!" Sue said. Her brother said, "Sorry, I didn't know it was yours." Sue was happy to find her pen. She went back to her room and started to write the letter to Tom. When she was done, she put the letter in an envelope and gave it to her mom to send. The next day, Tom got the letter and had a big shock. He did not know that Sue could write so well. Tom learned that he should not judge people too quickly. Sue was happy that she could write a nice letter, and she learned to always look for her things before they go missing.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy volcano. One day, his friend Sam came over to play. Sam saw the toy volcano and asked, "Can I borrow your volcano, Tim?" Tim said yes, and they played together. But then, Tim and Sam started to argue. They both wanted to play with the volcano and did not want to share. The volcano was not safe anymore. Tim's mom heard them and came into the room. "What is going on?" she asked. Tim and Sam told her about the volcano. Tim's mom said, "You need to share and play safe." So, Tim and Sam learned to take turns with the volcano. They played together and had fun. The volcano was safe again, and they were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a silly clown named Bobo. He had a big red nose and wore funny clothes. Bobo liked to make people laugh and smile. One day, Bobo was walking in the park when he saw a little girl named Lucy. She was crying. Bobo wanted to help her. So, he walked up to her and said, "Hi, I am Bobo the clown. Why are you sad?" Lucy looked up and said, "I lost my toy." Bobo wanted to make her happy, so he did a funny dance and made a funny face. Lucy started to giggle. Bobo said, "I will help you find your toy if you accept my help." Lucy nodded her head and said, "Yes, please." They looked for the toy together. They found it under a big tree. Lucy was so happy, she hugged Bobo. From that day on, Lucy and Bobo became the best of friends.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the doctor with her mom. Sue was healthy and happy. The doctor smiled and said, "Hi Sue! How are you today?" Sue said, "I am good. I can read now!" She showed the doctor her book. The doctor was happy for Sue. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took Sue's book away. Sue was sad. The doctor said, "Don't worry, Sue. I will help you find your book." They looked and looked, and then they found the book in a tree. The doctor got the book for Sue. Sue was happy again. She said, "Thank you, doctor!" The doctor smiled and said, "You're welcome, Sue. Keep reading and stay healthy!"

One day, a little girl named Sue found a pretty dress in her closet. The dress had a zipper on the back. Sue wanted to wear the pretty dress to the park. She asked her mom for help. "Mom, can you help me with the zipper?" she said. Her mom came to help. She told Sue to kneel down, so she could reach the zipper. Sue kneeled on the floor. Her mom pulled the zipper up. "There you go, Sue. Your dress looks so pretty!" her mom said. Sue went to the park with her mom. They played on the swings and slides. Sue's friends saw her pretty dress and liked it too. They all had a fun day at the park.
