Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Flowers

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing in his room. He was enthusiastic about his new toy car. As he played, he saw a pretty vase on a table. The vase was full of beautiful flowers. Tim thought the vase was very nice. Tim stopped playing and began to gaze at the vase. He wanted to touch the flowers, but he was too small. So, he got a chair and stood on it. Now, he could reach the flowers. As he touched the flowers, the vase started to wobble. Tim tried to hold it, but it fell and broke. He felt sad. His mom came and saw the broken vase. She hugged Tim and told him it was okay. They cleaned up the mess together and put new flowers in a new vase. Tim learned to be careful around things that can break.

Sara and Tom were friends who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big tree with red fruits. They wanted to try them, but they did not know what they were. "Let's hang from the branch and get some fruits," Tom said. He was brave and strong. "No, we should not do that," Sara said. She was careful and smart. "We don't know if they are good or bad. They might make us sick." But Tom did not listen to Sara. He climbed the tree and hung from the branch. He reached for a fruit and bit it. It was sour and bitter. He made a face and spit it out. "Yuck! This fruit is not good. It has a bad taste," he said. He tried to climb down, but he slipped and fell. He hurt his arm and cried. Sara ran to him and helped him. She was kind and gentle. "I'm sorry, Tom. Are you okay?" she asked. "I'm sorry, Sara. I should have listened to you. You were right. I was wrong. I was too fancy and greedy. I wanted to try something new, but I did not think of the danger," he said. Sara hugged him and said, "It's okay, Tom. We all make mistakes. But we can learn from them. Next time, we should be more careful and ask someone who knows before we try something new." Tom nodded and said, "Thank you, Sara. You are a good friend. I learned a lesson today. I will be more careful and ask someone who knows before I try something new." They went to the nurse and got a bandage for Tom's arm. Then they went home and told their moms what happened. Their moms were glad that they were safe and that they learned a lesson. The moral of the story is: Be careful and ask someone who knows before you try something new.

Once upon a time, there was a green frog named Bob. Bob lived in a big attic. He loved to hop and play all day. One sunny day, Bob met a little girl named Sue. Sue asked, "Do you enjoy living in the attic?" Bob said, "Yes, I do! I have lots of fun up there." Sue and Bob played together every day. They had so much fun in the attic. They became best friends and enjoyed their time together. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to visit her aunt. Her aunt lived in a small house near the park. Mia thought her aunt's house was very dull. It was not fun or pretty like her own house. Mia did not want to stay there for long. While Mia was sitting in her aunt's dull house, she heard her aunt say, "Mia, I have a gift for you." Mia was excited to receive a gift. She thought it might make her visit more fun. Her aunt gave her a small box. Mia opened it and found a pretty, shiny stone inside. Mia looked at the stone and said, "Thank you, Aunt! This is so pretty!" Her aunt smiled and said, "That stone is magic. If you are kind to others, it will bring you good luck." Mia was surprised and happy. She decided to be kind to everyone she met. The dull house did not seem so dull anymore. And from that day on, Mia learned that even in dull places, kindness can bring happiness and magic.
