Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Magic

One day, a boy named Tom went to a party. He wore a big hat and was very happy. At the party, Tom saw his friend, Sue. Sue had a present for Tom. Tom was so excited to see what was inside. Tom opened the present very fast. Inside was a magic wand. He waved the wand and something unexpected happened. All the food at the party began to dance! The cake, the cookies, and the juice were all dancing around the room. Tom and Sue laughed and laughed. They waved the magic wand again, but the food did not stop dancing. They tried to eat the cake, but it was too fast for them. In the end, Tom and Sue had to ask the wand nicely to make the food stop dancing. The wand listened and the food stopped dancing. Tom and Sue had a great time at the party with their new magic wand.

One day, a clever goose went for a walk. The sun was shining, and the sky was blue. The goose was very happy. As the goose walked, he saw a big tree. Under the tree, there was a sad cat. The goose asked, "Why are you sad, cat?" The cat sighed and said, "I lost my toy. I don't know where it is." The clever goose wanted to help the cat. They looked for the toy together. They looked near the tree, by the pond, and in the grass. At last, they found the toy under a bush. The cat was happy and said, "Thank you, clever goose!" The goose smiled, and they played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a noisy ghost named Boo. He lived in a big, old house. Boo loved to play and make loud sounds. He would stomp his feet and shout, "Boo!" to scare everyone. One day, a little girl named Lily moved into the house with her family. Lily heard the loud noises and saw the ghost. Instead of being scared, she smiled and said, "Hi, Boo! Let's be friends!" Boo was happy to have a friend. Boo and Lily played together every day. They laughed and had fun. One day, Lily was playing outside when a big dog came near her. Boo wanted to protect his friend. He stomped his feet and shouted, "Boo!" The big dog ran away, scared. Lily hugged Boo and said, "Thank you for protecting me, Boo. You are my best friend." And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a big dog and a small cat were playing in the yard. They were very happy and liked to smile. The sun was shining and the grass was soft. The dog said, "I am tired. Let's take a nap." The cat agreed, "Yes, a nap sounds good." They found a big tree with lots of shade. They lay down and closed their eyes. They felt safe and warm. As they slept, a little bird came and sat on a branch above them. The bird saw the dog and cat and wanted to play too. The bird had an idea. It flew down and tickled the dog's nose with its tail. The dog sneezed and woke up. The cat jumped up, surprised. They looked around and saw the bird. The bird said, "I wanted to play too!" The dog and cat smiled and let the bird join their fun. Now they were all friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time there was a smooth freezer. The freezer was in a special room in a big house. The room was cold and still. The smooth freezer was full of happy treats. It had ice cream, popsicles and happy fruits. Every day, the little boy liked to go to the room with the smooth freezer. He opened the door, his eyes wide with wonder. He would get an ice cream or a popsicle. His smile was so big and the treats tasted so sweet. He always felt so free and happy when he was in the room with the smooth freezer. One day, his mom came in the room. She smiled and gave the little boy an extra special treat. He ate it and smiled again. He hugged his mom as she said, "It's good to be free and happy, isn't it?" The little boy nodded and hugged his mom tightly. From that day on, whenever the little boy went in the room with the smooth freezer, he felt free and happy.
