Category: Beginner
Title: The Pink Dog and the Lily

Once upon a time, there was a little pink dog. The dog lived in a big house with a girl named Lily. Lily and the pink dog loved to play together. One day, Lily wanted to lift the pink dog up high. She said, "Dog, I will lift you up!" The pink dog wagged its tail, happy to play with Lily. But when Lily tried to lift the dog, she found it was too heavy for her. Lily called her mom for help. "Mom, can you help me lift the dog?" Her mom came and helped her lift the pink dog. Together, they lifted the dog up high. The pink dog was so happy, it barked and wagged its tail. From that day on, Lily and her mom would lift the pink dog together. They all played and had fun every day. And the little pink dog was the happiest dog in the whole world.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat waffles. One day, he saw a big waffle on the table. He was very happy. Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. Sam saw the waffle and said, "Can I have some?" Tim frowned and said, "No, this is my waffle!" Sam felt sorry and walked away. Later, Tim saw Sam sitting alone. Tim felt bad for not sharing. He went to Sam and said, "I am sorry. Let's share the waffle." Sam smiled and they ate the waffle together. They were both happy and played all day.

Bob was at the park with his mom. He watched as the kids got on the stage. Bob wanted to reach the stage so he could play, too. Mom said, "You have to walk the path to the stage, Bob." Bob looked around. He saw the green path to the stage and began to run. Bob reached the stage and smiled. He hugged his mom and thanked her. Then he ran to find his friends. Bob found his friends playing tag. He called out to them, "Come, let's go to the stage!" The kids ran to the stage and began to play. They laughed and had fun until it was time to go home. Bob waved goodbye to his friends. Then he and his mom began walking down the green path.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a charming little girl named Lily. She loved to help her mom and dad. One day, her mom asked her to bring her cell to her. Lily did not know what a cell was, but she wanted to help. Lily looked all around the house for the cell. She asked her dog, "Do you know what a cell is?" The dog just wagged its tail. Then she asked her cat, "Do you know what a cell is?" The cat just blinked at her. Lily felt sad that she could not find the cell. Then, Lily saw her dad using a small thing to talk to someone. She remembered her mom using it too. She thought, "Maybe that is the cell!" She waited for her dad to finish, and then she took the small thing to her mom. Her mom smiled and said, "Thank you, Lily, for bringing me my cell!" Lily felt happy that she could help her mom. From that day on, Lily knew what a cell was and she was always ready to help.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Kayla. She was 3 years old and lived near the beach. One summer day, Kayla woke up early in the morning and went to the beach. It was so bright and sunny that she had to squint her eyes. "Look," said Kayla to her mummy, "the sun is shining so brightly". Kayla spent the whole day playing at the beach. She built sandcastles, dug holes and ran around in the sand. Suddenly, she saw something big in the water. "Mummy, what is that big thing in the water?" asked Kayla. Her mummy replied, "that's a shark, sweetie. Remember not to go too close to the water". Kayla looked at the shark and noticed how shiny it was. Suddenly, she felt something on her toe. She bent down and saw a fish stirring at her feet. It tasted really bitter. Kayla screamed and ran away. The next day, Kayla was back at the beach. The sun was still shining and the shark was still swimming. But this time, they remained far away from each other.
