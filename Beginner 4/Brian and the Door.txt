Category: Beginner
Title: Brian and the Door

Once there was a little boy. He was three years old, and his name was Brian. Every day, his mom would give him a job to do. Brian had to manage the lock on the door. One day, Brian's mom said, "Brian, can you manage the lock on the door?" Brian nodded, and tried to do the job. But it was too hard for him. He tried and tried, but he could not manage the lock. Suddenly, his mom came back and helped him. With her help, he managed to lock the door. The next day, Brian put on his best clothes. He was ready to do the job. This time, he managed the lock all by himself. He was so proud of himself. He had managed the lock all by himself! He smiled and ran to show his mom.

Once upon a time, there was a brave little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and her dog, Max. Lily loved to play with Max in the big yard outside her house. One day, Lily and Max were playing in the yard when they saw a big red ball rise high in the sky. They both wanted to catch the ball, so they started to run very fast. Lily and Max ran and ran, but the ball kept rising higher and higher. Finally, the ball stopped rising and started to come back down. Lily and Max were very tired, but they still wanted to catch the ball. They jumped and jumped, and at last, Lily caught the ball. They were so happy! Lily's mom saw how brave they were and gave them a yummy snack to share. Lily and Max sat under the tree, eating their snack and feeling very proud of their big adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a good butterfly. The butterfly had many friends. They liked to play in the sun. One day, the butterfly saw a big hill. The butterfly wanted to run up the hill. So, the butterfly and friends went to the hill. They ran up the hill. They had fun. They saw more butterflies at the top. They all played together. The end.

One day, a boy named Tim found some clay. He was very happy. He wanted to make something with the clay. He thought and thought about what to make. Tim decided to make a little clay fish. He worked hard and made a fish. The fish was not real, so it was dead. Tim showed the fish to his mom. She said it was very nice. Tim's friend, Sam, came to play. Tim showed Sam his dead clay fish. Sam liked it too. They both played with the clay fish and had a fun day. They said it was the best day ever.

Once upon a time, there was a strong girl named Lily. She loved to play with her friends. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. One day, Lily saw a new case in her room. It was a pretty case with colors and shapes on it. She opened the case and found a puzzle inside. The puzzle had many pieces, but Lily knew she could put it together. Lily asked her friends to join her and help with the puzzle. They all sat down and started to work. With each piece they found, the picture became clearer. After some time, they finished the puzzle. It was a beautiful picture of a rainbow. They were so happy that they joined together to make something so nice.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man. He lived near a big lake. The lake had a lot of water. The bald man liked the water. He liked to swim in it every day. One day, the bald man saw a fish. The fish was big and pretty. The fish could talk. The fish said, "Hello, bald man!" The bald man was happy. He said, "Hello, fish!" They talked and played in the water. But the fish was not nice. The fish wanted the water all for itself. The fish said, "Bald man, go away. This is my water." The bald man was sad. He did not want to leave the water. But the fish was big and strong. The bald man had to go away. He never swam in the water again.
