Category: Beginner
Title: Amy the Girl and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She liked to play with her soft ball. One day, Amy wanted to play a new game. She asked her big sister, Lisa, what to play. Lisa said, "I suggest we play a sport." Amy did not know what a sport was. Lisa told her, "A sport is a fun game we play outside." Amy and Lisa went outside to play. They played a sport called catch. Amy threw the soft ball and Lisa caught it. They laughed and had fun playing the new sport. And from that day on, Amy loved playing sports with her big sister.

Once upon a time, a black cat named Tom lived in a small house. Tom liked to play outside and chase butterflies. One day, while playing, Tom saw a big cross in the park. He was very curious and wanted to see it up close. As Tom walked closer to the cross, he started to feel funny. Suddenly, something unexpected happened! Tom turned into a black dog! He was so embarrassed because he didn't know how to be a dog. He tried to bark, but it sounded like a cat meow. All the other dogs in the park laughed at him. Tom didn't like being a dog, so he decided to go back to the cross. When he touched the cross, he turned back into a cat! Tom was very happy to be a cat again. He learned that it's okay to be different and that being himself is the best thing to be. From that day on, Tom didn't feel embarrassed anymore, and he continued to play and have fun with his friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a bright green cricket. The cricket jumped high and Tim liked it. He wanted to play with the cricket. Tim tried to catch the cricket, but it was too fast. He noticed that the cricket liked to jump on the grass. Tim thought of a fun game they could play together. Tim picked up a ball and rolled it to the cricket. The cricket jumped on the ball and they played together. Tim and the cricket had a lot of fun. They became good friends and played every day.

One day, a little bee was flying in the sky. The bee loved to speed through the air. He liked to see how fast he could go. The bee saw a big tree and wanted to see what was on the other side. As the bee sped around the tree, he saw a little boy. The boy was crying. The bee felt sad and wanted to help. He flew close to the boy and asked, "Why are you crying?" The boy looked up and saw the bee. He said, "I fell down and now I hurt." The bee wanted to help the boy feel better. He had an idea. The bee flew away and came back with a pretty flower. He gave it to the boy. The boy smiled and said, "Thank you, bee!" The flower made the boy feel happy, and his hurt didn't feel as bad. The bee and the boy became friends. They played together, and the bee showed the boy how fast he could speed through the sky. The boy laughed, and they had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to slide down the big hill near his house. He would go there every day to play with his friends. One day, Tim's heel started to hurt. He told his mom, and she said, "Maybe you are just tired, Tim. Let's see how you feel tomorrow." The next day, Tim's heel still hurt, but he wanted to play with his friends. So, he put on his shoes and went to the big hill. Tim was very tired, but he still wanted to slide. He took a deep breath and started to slide down the hill. As Tim slid down the hill, he saw something shiny at the bottom. When he got closer, he saw that it was a magic stone! Tim picked up the stone and held it tight. Suddenly, his heel stopped hurting, and he wasn't tired anymore. Tim knew that the magic stone had the power to make him feel better. From that day on, Tim and his friends would slide down the hill and play all day, never getting tired or hurt.
