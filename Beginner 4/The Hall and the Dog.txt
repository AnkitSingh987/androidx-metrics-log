Category: Beginner
Title: The Hall and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a big hall. In the hall, there were many lively kids. They liked to play, run, and have fun. They always remembered to be kind and share with each other. One day, while they were playing, they heard a loud noise. They did not know what it was. It was a big surprise! They looked around and saw a big, friendly dog. The dog wanted to play too! The kids and the dog played together all day. They had a lot of fun. They laughed and ran around the hall. The kids remembered this day as the best day ever. And they all became good friends with the big, friendly dog.

---

Sara and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they find a big potato in the dirt. Sara says, "Look, Ben, a potato! We can make a face on it and pretend it is our baby." Ben says, "Yes, Sara, that is a good idea. We can use sticks and stones and leaves for the eyes and nose and mouth." They make a face on the potato and hug it. They say, "We love you, potato baby. You are our baby." They play with the potato baby for a long time. They give it a name. They call it Pat. Sara says, "Ben, do you want to marry me? We can be a family with Pat." Ben says, "Yes, Sara, I want to marry you. You are my best friend. How do we marry?" Sara says, "We just have to say, 'I marry you', and kiss each other on the cheek." Ben says, "OK, Sara, I marry you." Sara says, "I marry you, too." They kiss each other on the cheek and smile. But then, a big dog comes to the park. It sees the potato baby and thinks it is a toy. It runs to Sara and Ben and grabs the potato baby in its mouth. It runs away with it. Sara and Ben are very sad. They cry and shout, "No, dog, no! Give us back our potato baby! We love Pat!" The dog does not listen. It bites the potato baby and breaks it. The potato baby is hurt. It has no face anymore. It is just a potato. Sara and Ben are very hurt, too. They feel like their hearts are broken. They hug each other and cry. They say, "We miss Pat. We miss our potato baby."

One day, a red ball and a blue ball were friends. They liked to play a game. The game was to roll fast and see who was first to stop. The sun was very hot, and the ground was dry. The red ball said, "Let's play our game now!" The blue ball agreed, "Yes, let's play!" They started to roll fast. The red ball rolled faster and faster. The blue ball tried to catch up. At the end, the red ball stopped first. The blue ball was sad but said, "Good job, red ball. You are very fast." They played the game many times. The red ball won every time, but they both had fun.

---

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a big tree with a yummy red apple on it. Tim wanted to get the apple, but it was too high. He tried to jump, but he could not reach it. A kind cat saw Tim and wanted to help. The cat said, "I can climb the tree and get the apple for you." The cat climbed the tree and got the yummy apple for Tim. Tim was so happy and said, "Thank you, kind cat!" But then, they saw a leak in the tree. Water was coming out. Tim and the cat wanted to help the tree. They bowed to the tree and said, "We will help you, tree!" They found some mud and put it on the leak. The tree was happy, and Tim and the cat played under the tree all day.

---

One morning, a big cat named Tom was very hungry. He wanted to serve himself a great big meal. He looked around and saw a small mouse named Jerry. Tom thought Jerry would be a great snack. He ran after Jerry, but Jerry was very fast. They ran and ran, but Tom could not catch Jerry. Tom was sad and still hungry. Then, something unexpected happened. Jerry stopped running and turned to Tom. Jerry said, "I can help you find a great big meal if you don't eat me." Tom agreed, and they became friends. Together, they found a great big meal and shared it. Tom and Jerry were happy and full.
