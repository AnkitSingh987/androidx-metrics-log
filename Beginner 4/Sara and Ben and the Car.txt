Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Ben and the Car

Sara and Ben are playing with their toys in the living room. Sara has a doll and Ben has a car. They are having fun, but then Sara sees Ben's car and wants it. "Give me your car, Ben. It is shiny and fast. I like it more than my doll," Sara says. "No, Sara. It is my car. You have your doll. Go away," Ben says. Sara does not listen. She tries to grab the car from Ben's hands. Ben pulls it back. They start to fight. They yell and cry. Mom hears the noise and comes to the living room. She sees Sara and Ben fighting over the car. She is angry. "Stop it, Sara and Ben. You are being bad. You are hurting each other. Why are you fighting?" Mom says. Sara and Ben stop fighting. They look at Mom. They are scared. "Sara wanted my car. She tried to take it from me. It is not fair," Ben says. "But I like his car. It is shiny and fast. My doll is boring," Sara says. Mom sighs. She picks up the car and the doll. She holds them in front of Sara and Ben. "Look, Sara and Ben. Do you remember where you got these toys? Do you remember who gave them to you?" Mom says. Sara and Ben think. They try to remember. They realize something. "I remember. Grandma gave me the car. She said it was special. She said she loved me," Ben says. "I remember too. Grandpa gave me the doll. He said it was pretty. He said he loved me," Sara says. Mom smiles. She gives the car to Ben and the doll to Sara. "That's right. Grandma and Grandpa gave you these toys. They are your memories of them. They are not just toys. They are gifts of love. You should not fight over them. You should share them and play nicely. Do you understand?" Mom says. Sara and Ben nod. They feel sorry. They hug Mom. "We understand, Mom. We are sorry. We love you. We love Grandma and Grandpa. We love each other," Sara and Ben say. Mom hugs them back. "I love you too, Sara and Ben. I am glad you remember. Now, why don't you play together and have fun?" Mom says. Sara and Ben agree. They take their toys and go to the floor. They play together and have fun. They are happy.

Once upon a time, in a sleepy town, there lived a kind girl named Sue. Sue loved to help everyone. She wanted to improve her town and make it a happy place for all. One day, Sue saw a big tree with no leaves. She thought, "I can help this tree." Sue watered the tree every day. Soon, the tree grew big and strong with many green leaves. The tree was very happy, and so was Sue. Sue did not stop there. She cleaned the park, painted the houses, and fixed the broken toys. The sleepy town became a happy town. Everyone loved Sue and her big heart. Sue improved the town and made many new friends.

Once there was a big ostrich named Ollie. Ollie lived in a place with lots of rocks. He liked to play with his friends. One day, Ollie found a big rock. It was a very big and terrible rock. Ollie was scared of the rock, but he wanted to show it to his friends. Ollie called his friends and said, "Look at this big rock I found! It is a terrible rock!" His friends came and looked at the rock. They were scared too. They all talked about the rock and what to do with it. They decided to push the rock away so they would not be scared anymore. The friends worked together to push the big, terrible rock. They pushed and pushed, and the rock started to move. They pushed the rock far away from their home. When they were done, they were very happy. They had worked together to make their home safe again. They played and had fun, and they were not scared of the terrible rock anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a fine boy who loved to jog. One day, he went for a jog in his yard. While jogging, Tim saw a big refrigerator. He was hungry, so he opened it. Inside the refrigerator, he found an apple, a banana, and a cake. Tim ate the apple and the banana. He felt full and happy. Then, he closed the refrigerator and continued to jog. He had a fun day in the yard.
