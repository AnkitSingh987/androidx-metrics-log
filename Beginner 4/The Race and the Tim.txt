Category: Beginner
Title: The Race and the Tim

Once upon a time, there was a big race. Many kids came to play. They all wanted to win the race and get the victory. The prize was a big, expensive toy. The kids were very excited. One little boy, Tim, was scared. He did not think he could win. His friend, Sue, said, "Don't be scared, Tim. Just try your best and have fun!" Tim smiled and said, "Okay, I will try." The race began, and all the kids ran fast. Tim tried his best to keep up. He saw the finish line and ran faster. Tim shut his eyes and hoped for the best. When he opened his eyes, he saw that he had won the race! Tim got the victory and the big, expensive toy. All the kids cheered for Tim. Sue said, "I knew you could do it, Tim!" Tim was so happy. He learned that it was important to try, even when he was scared.

Jack and his mom were walking through the park. Jack noticed the popular swings and asked to go over to them. His mom said, "you can go and play after I sit on this bench for a minute." Jack was excited and quickly ran to the swings. He hopped onto the swing and started to go high. Suddenly, he felt his knee starting to hurt. Jack said, "Mommy, my knee hurts!" His mom said, "No, you must keep swinging!" So, Jack kept swinging higher and higher, despite the pain. Jack was having a lot of fun, but suddenly something felt wrong. With one more swing, Jack's knee gave way and he fell with a loud thump. He started to cry and his mom quickly ran over. She said, "Jack, why didn't you stop when your knee hurt? Now you're hurt." Jack said, "You told me to keep swinging!" His mom just shook her head and hugged him tightly. The end of Jack's fun day was not good. Jack had a big bruise on his knee and was really sad. Even though his mom warned him, Jack couldn't predict what happened and didn't know how to end the pain in his knee. Jack quickly learned that sometimes it's best to listen to your mom.

One day, a clumsy cat named Tim found a big bag of marbles. He wanted to organize them by color. So, he started to put the blue marbles in one pile, the red marbles in another pile, and the yellow marbles in a third pile. As Tim was organizing the marbles, his friend, a dog named Sam, came over. Sam saw the marbles and said, "Wow, what are you doing, Tim?" Tim replied, "I am organizing these marbles by color. It's fun!" While they were talking, a big wind came and blew all the marbles away. Tim and Sam were very surprised. They had to start organizing the marbles all over again. But this time, they worked together and had even more fun.

One day, Tim and Sue went to the park. They saw a big, mysterious box. It was full of junk. Tim said, "What's in the box, Sue?" Sue said, "I don't know, let's look!" They started to load the junk out of the box. They found old toys, broken bikes, and ripped clothes. Tim and Sue argued about who got to keep the toys. Tim said, "I want them!" Sue said, "No, I want them!" A kind man walked by and saw Tim and Sue fighting. He said, "Why don't you share the toys?" Tim and Sue thought about it. They decided to share the toys and play together. They were happy and had fun at the park with their new toys.

One day, a humble cat and a dog were in the kitchen. They wanted to make a yummy sauce. The cat said, "Let's encourage each other to make the best sauce ever!" They worked together, but the dog was not good at making sauce. The cat got mad at the dog. The dog felt sad and said, "I'm sorry, I'm not good at making sauce." Just then, a little bird flew in through the window. The bird said, "I can help you make the sauce!" The cat and the dog were surprised. They didn't know that birds could make sauce. The bird showed them how to make the sauce better. The cat, the dog, and the bird all worked together. In the end, they made the yummiest sauce ever. They were all happy and became good friends.
