Category: Beginner
Title: The Lonely Plate and the Sue

Once upon a time, there was a lonely plate. This plate had no friends. It was sad. One day, a girl named Sue found the plate. She saw how lonely it was and felt sad too. Sue wanted to help the plate. She thought of a plan to make the plate feel better. Sue showed the plate how much she valued it. She put yummy food on it and shared it with her friends. The plate was not lonely anymore. It was happy because it had friends who cared. And Sue was happy too, because she had a new friend in the plate. They lived happily ever after.

One day, a little dog named Spot was playing in the park. He was an adorable dog with a big round spot on his back. Spot loved to run and jump on the green grass. He saw a big tree and wanted to come closer to it. As Spot came closer to the tree, he saw something shiny up in the branches. He barked and jumped, trying to get the shiny thing. Spot tried and tried, but he could not reach it. He was sad and sat down under the tree, looking at the shiny thing. Just then, a tall man came to the park. He saw Spot looking sad under the tree. The man looked up and saw the shiny thing too. He reached up and got the shiny thing for Spot. It was a beautiful red ball. Spot was so happy, his tail wagged fast. The man and Spot played with the ball all day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a famous dog named Buddy. Buddy was famous because he could laugh. Everyone loved Buddy and wanted to hear his funny laugh. One day, the town decided to have a big holiday. They wanted to celebrate Buddy and his laugh. They had a big party with lots of yummy food, fun games, and pretty decorations. All the people and their pets came to the holiday party. At the party, Buddy laughed and laughed. All the people and pets laughed too. They were so happy to be with Buddy on this special holiday. And from that day on, they had a big holiday every year to remember the day they all laughed with the famous dog named Buddy.

Once upon a time, there was a clever dog. His name was Max. Max liked to play with his ball. One day, Max saw his ball near a big tree. But, the ball was stuck. Max tried to pull the ball with his mouth. He pulled and pulled, but the ball did not move. Max sat down and thought. He had an idea! He would use a stick to help him. Max found a stick and put it near the ball. He pulled the stick and the ball came out! Max was so happy. He ran to his owner with the ball. They played and had lots of fun. Max was a very clever dog.

One day, a big chair was in a room. A little girl, named Sue, saw the chair and wanted to sit on it. Sue asked her mom, "Can I sit on the chair?" Her mom said, "Yes, let's sit on the chair together." Sue and her mom sat on the chair. They were happy. They laughed and talked. But then, the chair made a loud sound. Sue and her mom were scared. They did not know what the sound was. The chair was not a real chair. It was a big, dead bug! The bug woke up and started to move. Sue and her mom fell off the bug. They were not happy anymore. The big, dead bug walked away, and Sue and her mom never sat on the chair again.

One day, a man and his wife went for a walk. The wife was a bit crazy and liked to do funny things. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. They were very happy. The man said, "I think I need to sneeze." The wife laughed and said, "You always sneeze when we go for a walk!" The man smiled and they kept walking. Soon, they saw a big tree. The wife had an idea. She said, "When you sneeze, I will jump from the tree and say 'boo'!" The man laughed and thought it was a great plan. They walked to the tree and the wife climbed up. The man felt a sneeze coming. He sneezed very loud. Just like they planned, the wife jumped from the tree and said, "Boo!" They both laughed and laughed. It was a fun and crazy day.
