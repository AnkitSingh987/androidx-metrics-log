Category: Beginner
Title: The Raven and the Necklace

Once upon a time, there was a big raven. The raven loved to nap. Every day, he would nap under a tree. One day, he saw something shiny and pretty. It was an expensive necklace. The raven wanted it very much. The raven took the necklace and flew away. He was so happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The necklace fell from his beak and into a pond. The raven was sad. A little fish saw the necklace and swam up to it. The fish tried to give the necklace back to the raven. But the raven had an idea. He said, "Thank you, fish. You can keep the necklace. I don't need it. I just want to nap." The raven and fish became best friends, and the raven learned that naps were better than expensive things.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to shake his tail and play with his friends. One day, Max saw a big, incredible tree. He wanted to find a treat from the tree. Max called his friend, a little bird named Sam. "Sam, can you help me get a treat from the tree?" asked Max. Sam said, "Yes, I can help you!" So, Sam flew up to the tree and shook the branches. A treat fell down from the tree. Max was very happy with the treat. But, suddenly, a big, mean cat came and took the treat away from Max. The cat ate the treat and didn't share with Max. Max and Sam were sad. The bad cat left, and Max and Sam did not get a treat that day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy who went on a walk in the woods. He looked around and noticed a cobweb in a tree. He saw something black and shiny in the web and wanted to see what it was. The little boy was very brave and reached out to touch it. Suddenly, he felt a sharp pain. It was a spider, who had bit him! He started to cry and ran back home. When he arrived, he told his mom that he had been bit by a spider. She saw the hard red bump on his hand and knew he must have touched a cobweb. She comforted him and kissed the bump. The little boy felt better and began to smile. "Mommy, can I go back to the woods and see if the spider is still there?" he asked her. She smiled and said, "I think that would be a good idea. But this time, if you see a cobweb, be careful!"

Lily and Ben are twins. They share a room with two beds and many toys. They like to play together, but sometimes they make a mess. Their mom tells them to tidy their room before they go to sleep. One night, Lily and Ben are very excited. They hear that it will snow tomorrow. They want to make a snowman and have a snowball fight. They promise to tidy their room in the morning. They put on their pajamas and go to bed. The next day, Lily wakes up first. She looks out the window and sees a lot of snow. It is white and fluffy and sparkly. She jumps out of bed and runs to Ben. She shakes him and says, "Ben, Ben, wake up! It snowed! Let's go outside and play!" Ben opens his eyes and sees Lily. He smiles and says, "Wow, it snowed! That's awesome! Let's go outside and play!" He jumps out of bed and follows Lily. They grab their coats and boots and hats and gloves. They run downstairs and open the door. But their mom is waiting for them. She looks at their room and sees a mess. There are toys and clothes and books everywhere. She says, "Lily and Ben, you can't go outside and play until you tidy your room. You made a promise last night. You have to keep your promises." Lily and Ben look at their mom and then at their room. They feel sad and sorry. They say, "We're sorry, mom. We forgot to tidy our room. We were too excited about the snow. Can we please go outside and play for a little bit?" Their mom shakes her head and says, "No, you have to tidy your room first. Then you can go outside and play. The snow will not melt so fast. You still have time to enjoy it. But you have to do your chores first. That's the rule." Lily and Ben nod and say, "Okay, mom. We understand. We will tidy our room now. We will be quick and careful. We will put everything in its place. Then we will go outside and play. We love you, mom." Their mom smiles and says, "I love you too, Lily and Ben. I'm proud of you for being responsible. Now go and tidy your room. I'll make you some hot chocolate when you're done." Lily and Ben go back to their room. They work together to tidy their room. They put their toys in the toy box, their clothes in the closet, their books on the shelf. They make their beds and fluff their pillows. They look around and see a tidy room. They feel happy and proud. They run downstairs and show their mom their tidy room. Their mom hugs them and says, "Well done, Lily and Ben. You did a great job. Now you can go outside and play. Have fun and be careful. Don't forget your hot chocolate when you come back." Lily and Ben thank their mom and put on their coats and boots and hats and gloves. They run outside and see the snow. It is white and fluffy and sparkly. They make a snowman and have a snowball fight. They laugh and play and have fun. They love the snow. They love their mom. They love each other.
