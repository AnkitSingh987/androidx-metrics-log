Category: Beginner
Title: The Weird Toy and the Tom

One day, Tom saw a weird toy. It was a chain that could balance on things. Tom wanted to play with it, but his friend Sue wanted to play with it too. They both grabbed the chain and pulled. "I want it!" said Tom. "No, I want it!" said Sue. Tom's mom saw them fight. She said, "Kids, let's make a game. You both can play with the chain." Tom and Sue looked at each other and nodded. They were ready to play together. Tom and Sue took turns balancing the chain on their heads, hands, and feet. They had fun and laughed a lot. They learned that playing together was better than fighting. And the weird chain toy helped them be friends again.

Once, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom liked to play outside. One day, he found a small pebble. The pebble was shiny and pretty. Tom thought it was a rich pebble. He was very happy. Tom took the pebble to his mom. She said, "Wow, that's a nice pebble!" Tom wanted to keep it safe. He put the pebble in a little box. Tom played with the pebble every day. One day, Tom lost the pebble. He was very sad. He looked and looked for it. Then, his friend Sam came to play. They searched together. Soon, they found the pebble under a big tree. Tom was so happy to return the pebble to its box. He thanked Sam and they played together all day.

One day, a little girl named Mia set a cup on her desk. She was very happy because it was her first desk. She liked to draw and write on it. Mia's best friend, Tom, came over to play with her. They were having a lot of fun. "Look at my desk, Tom!" Mia said, showing off her new desk. Tom thought it was very cool. They decided to draw pictures together. As they were drawing, Tom's hand slipped, and he knocked over the cup. The water in the cup spilled all over the desk. "Oh no!" Tom said, feeling hurt that he made a mess on Mia's new desk. Mia was sad too, but she knew it was an accident. They quickly cleaned up the water and dried the desk. Just when they thought everything was back to normal, they saw something unexpected. The water had made a beautiful rainbow on the desk! They were both amazed and happy. "Wow, it's like magic!" Mia said, smiling at Tom. They continued drawing, now with an even more special desk to work on.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat and a small dog. They were good friends. The big cat was very fair and always helped the small dog. They played together every day and had lots of fun. One day, the small dog was very tired. He wanted to take a nap. The big cat saw this and wanted to support his friend. So, the big cat found a soft and warm place for the small dog to sleep. The small dog was very happy and said, "Thank you, big cat!" After the small dog's nap, he felt much better. He was ready to play again. The big cat and the small dog played all day long. They knew that being fair and supporting each other made them the best of friends. And they were happy together forever.

Once upon a time there was a bad monster. Its name was Destroy and it wanted to destroy the world. One day, Destroy saw a top spinning on the ground. With a loud roar, Destroy ran towards the top with his big teeth. He wanted to destroy it! But the top was too quick for Destroy. No matter how hard he tried, he couldn't get the top. Suddenly, a little girl saw what was happening. She was brave and yelled, "Stop, Destroy!" But it was too late. Destroy was already too deaf to hear the little girl. He smashed the top into pieces. The little girl was very sad. She had watched the top spin and hoped it would keep spinning forever. But Destroy made sure the top could never spin again. From then on, the world was not the same.
