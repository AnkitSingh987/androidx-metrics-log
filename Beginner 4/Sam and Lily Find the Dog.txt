Category: Beginner
Title: Sam and Lily Find the Dog

Sam and Lily liked to play in the park. They liked to slide, swing, and run. One day, they saw a big dog in the park. The dog had a leather collar and a leather leash. The dog was brown and black and very furry. Sam and Lily wanted to pet the dog. They walked to the dog and said hello. The dog wagged his tail and licked their faces. Sam and Lily laughed. They liked the dog. They asked the dog's name. The dog's owner said his name was Rex. Rex liked to play too. He ran with Sam and Lily. He chased a ball and brought it back. He jumped and barked. He was loud, but Sam and Lily did not mind. They had fun with Rex. Then, Rex saw a squirrel. He wanted to catch the squirrel. He pulled on his leash and ran after the squirrel. The leash slipped from the owner's hand. Rex ran fast. Sam and Lily ran after Rex. They shouted his name. The squirrel ran up a tree. Rex stopped and looked up. He could not reach the squirrel. He barked loud. Sam and Lily caught up with Rex. They picked up his leash and gave it to the owner. They said sorry. The owner said it was okay. He thanked them for helping. He said Rex was a good dog, but sometimes he was naughty. Sam and Lily said goodbye to Rex and the owner. They went back to the slide, the swing, and the run. They had a good day in the park. They hoped to see Rex again. They liked Rex. He was a loud, leather, and furry friend.

Tim and Jen are playing with their blocks. They like to build towers and houses and cars. Tim has a red block with a star on it. He says it is his symbol. Jen has a yellow block with a heart on it. She says it is her symbol. They hear a loud noise outside. They look out the window and see a big truck. The truck is spraying water on the street. Tim and Jen think it is fun to watch. They see the water make rainbows in the sun. But then, the truck comes closer to their house. The water hits their window and makes it wet. Tim and Jen get scared. They think the water will break their window and come inside. They run to their mom and dad in the kitchen. "Mom, Dad, help!" Tim says. "The truck is spraying water on our window!" "Don't worry, kids," Mom says. "The truck is just cleaning the street. It won't hurt our window or our house. We are safe here." Mom hugs Tim and Jen and gives them cookies. They feel better. They go back to their blocks and play some more. But then, Jen sneezes. She sneezes again and again. She has a cold. She feels bad. She drops her yellow block with the heart on it. It breaks in half. "Oh no!" Jen says. "My symbol is broken!" Tim sees Jen's broken block. He feels sorry for her. He has an idea. He takes his red block with the star on it and breaks it in half too. He gives one half to Jen and keeps the other half for himself. "Here, Jen," Tim says. "Now we have two symbols. One for you and one for me. They are still the same, but different. Like us. We are brother and sister, but we are not the same. We can share our symbols and our blocks and our cookies. And we can help each other when we are sick or scared." Jen smiles. She takes Tim's half of the red block and gives him her half of the yellow block. They hug each other. They are happy. They have new symbols and new friends. They are safe and loved.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Percy. She loved to explore the world around her, especially the garden. One day, she decided to go outside to look for some herbs. As she searched, she began to feel desperate; after looking for a while, she couldn't find any herbs. Suddenly, she spotted a beautiful purple flower and she gingerly picked it. Percy was grateful she found the herb and proudly ran back in the house, holding her new treasure. When she went inside, she asked her mom what type of herb she had found. Her mom smiled and said, "That's a basil plant. It's great in cooking dishes, and can be used to make delicious aromas in home remedies as well." Percy was so excited, she started jumping around. She couldn't believe how lucky she was to reach the herb! Mom continued, "Here, why don't you add it to your tea." Percy thanked her, beaming with happiness. As the aroma from her tea filled the room, Percy felt grateful that she had been able to find this herb and would always remember the moment.
