Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Bird

One day, a wide-eyed girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a big tree and a little bird sitting on a branch. Lily asked the bird, "Why is the earth so big?" The bird replied, "The earth is big so there is room for all the animals and people to live and play." Lily thought about this and smiled. She looked around and saw a wide river. Fish were swimming in the water. Lily asked the fish, "Why is the earth so pretty?" The fish replied, "The earth is pretty so we can all enjoy the beautiful things like trees, flowers, and rivers." Lily felt happy as she walked back home. She knew that the earth was big and pretty for everyone to share. And now, every time she saw something wide or beautiful, she remembered her friends, the bird and the fish, and their replies.

Anna and Ben went to the park with Mom. They saw a pond with many frogs. Anna and Ben liked frogs. They wanted to catch one. "Look, there is a big green frog!" Anna said. She ran to the pond and tried to grab the frog. But the frog was too fast. It jumped into the water and made a splash. "Ha ha, you missed it!" Ben said. He laughed at Anna. He thought he was smarter. He walked to the pond and looked for another frog. He saw a small brown frog on a rock. He reached out his hand and touched the frog. But the frog was not happy. It opened its mouth and croaked loudly. "Ribbit, ribbit, leave me alone!" the frog said. Ben was surprised. He did not know frogs could talk. He let go of the frog and backed away. The frog jumped off the rock and hid under a leaf. "Ha ha, you scared it!" Anna said. She laughed at Ben. She thought she was braver. She and Ben looked at each other and smiled. They both liked frogs. They wanted to be friends with them. "Sorry, frog, we did not mean to bother you," Anna said. She and Ben said sorry to the frog. They hoped the frog would forgive them. Mom came to the pond and saw Anna and Ben. She was happy to see them having fun. She brought them some cookies. They were sweet and yummy. "Do you want to share some cookies with the frogs?" Mom asked. "Yes, please!" Anna and Ben said. They broke some cookies and threw them into the pond. They hoped the frogs would like them. The frogs saw the cookies and came out of the water. They smelled the cookies and tasted them. They liked them. They thanked Anna and Ben and Mom. "Thank you, thank you, you are very kind!" the frogs said. Anna and Ben and Mom were amazed. They did not know frogs could talk. They laughed with the frogs. They made new friends. They had a good day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to dance. She danced all day and all night. Mia was very careful when she danced. She did not want to fall or hurt herself. One day, Mia's mom said she could not dance in the house. Mia was sad. She wanted to dance, but her mom would not allow it. Mia did not know what to do. She wanted to dance, but she did not want to make her mom upset. Mia thought of a great idea. She went outside to dance. She was very careful as she danced on the grass. Mia's mom saw her dancing outside and smiled. Mia was happy because she could dance and her mom was not upset. They both learned that sometimes, you just need to find a new place to do the things you love.

Once, there was a boy named Joel. He was three years old and very healthy. He loved to go outside with his mom and explore the world. One day, Joel and his mom went to the park. Joel ran around and played in the grass. He was having so much fun! Suddenly, Joel noticed a tall stand in the middle of the park. He wanted to hold it and see how tall it was. He ran over and hugged the stand as tight as he could. Joel was so proud of himself. He asked his mom to take a picture of him holding the stand, so he could remember this moment forever. His mom took the picture and, of course, he smiled. Joel and his mom stayed in the park all day, holding hands and exploring all the different things they could find. It was a healthy and fun day for them both.
