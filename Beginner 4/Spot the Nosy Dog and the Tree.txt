Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Nosy Dog and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little dog named Spot. Spot loved to sniff and dig in the yard. One day, he found a big pear on the ground. He picked it up with his mouth and ran to his friend, a little girl named Susie. "Look, Susie! I found a pear!" said Spot. Susie was happy and clapped her hands. "Good job, Spot! Let's eat it!" But when they tried to eat the pear, they found out it was not very good. It was hard and not sweet at all. Susie had an idea. "Let's plant the pear in the ground to improve it!" So they dug a hole and put the pear inside. They waited and waited. After a long time, a small tree began to grow. The tree grew bigger and bigger, and soon it had many sweet pears. Susie and Spot were so happy. They picked the sweet pears and shared them with all their friends. The nosy little dog and the little girl had improved the pear, and now everyone could enjoy them. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once there was a little boy called Tom. He was three years old and loved to explore outside. One day, Tom decided to go for a walk in the park nearby. As he walked along, Tom noticed a large dark cauliflower sticking up from the ground. Tom bent down and touched the cauliflower with his finger. "Stand up!" Tom said in surprise. To his amazement, the cauliflower started to stand tall and strong. Tom stood up and laughed with delight. He ran back home to tell his family about the dark cauliflower that he found. But no one believed him. "Tom, that's impossible!", said his mum. But Tom knew he was right. That night, Tom ran back to the park to show his family the dark cauliflower that could stand. Everyone was amazed and Tom smiled from ear to ear.

Lila was a girl who liked to drink water. She drank water every day, from a cup or a bottle. She drank water when she was thirsty, when she was hot, and when she was bored. She drank water so much that her mom called her "Water Girl". One day, Lila went to the park with her mom and her brother Sam. Sam was older than Lila and liked to play rough. He ran, jumped, and climbed on the swings, the slide, and the monkey bars. Lila wanted to play with Sam, but he said she was too little and slow. He said she only knew how to drink water. Lila felt sad and angry. She wanted to show Sam that she could play rough too. She saw a big puddle near the sandbox. She ran to the puddle and jumped in it. She splashed water all over herself and the sand. She laughed and shouted, "Look, Sam, I can play rough!" Sam heard Lila and turned around. He saw her in the puddle, wet and dirty. He was surprised and annoyed. He said, "Lila, what are you doing? You're making a mess! You're not playing rough, you're being silly!" Lila didn't care what Sam said. She was having fun. She said, "I'm not silly, I'm Water Girl! And I can drink this water too!" She bent down and took a sip of the puddle water. It tasted bad, like dirt and grass. She spat it out and made a face. She said, "Yuck! This water is gross!" Sam laughed and said, "Of course it is, Lila. You can't drink puddle water. It's dirty and yucky. You should drink clean water, like from the fountain or the bottle. Come on, let's go get some. And maybe you can play with me on the slide, if you dry off first." Lila nodded and smiled. She followed Sam to the fountain and drank some water. It tasted good, like fresh and cool. She said, "This water is better. Thank you, Sam. Can we play on the slide now?" Sam said, "Sure, Lila. You're welcome. And you know what? You're not too little or slow. You're brave and fun. You're my Water Girl sister." He hugged Lila and they ran to the slide. They played rough and happy, until it was time to go home.
