Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Tim. Tim was a naughty dog. He liked to dig holes in the yard and hide his toys under the dirt. One day, Tim found a big stick and wanted to hide it too. Tim dug a big hole under the tree. As he dug, he started to shiver because it was cold outside. His friend, the cat, came over and said, "Tim, why are you shivering? You should go inside where it's warm." Tim looked at the cat and said, "But I need to hide my stick under the dirt." The cat shook her head and said, "You're being naughty, Tim. Let's go inside and play with our toys there." So, Tim and the cat went inside, and they played together, warm and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat named Tom. Tom had a friend named Sam, who was a small bird. They lived in a big tree and played all day long. They were very happy. One day, Tom had a bad dream, a nightmare. In the dream, Sam quit being his friend and flew away. Tom woke up scared and sad. He looked around for Sam, but Sam was not there. Tom thought the dream was real and felt very sad. Tom searched for Sam and found him playing with another furry cat. Tom felt even sadder. But then, Sam saw Tom and flew to him. Sam said, "Tom, meet my sister, Sue! She is your new friend too!" Tom was so happy, and they all played together. The nightmare was not real, and Tom had two furry friends now.

One day, a little sheep was in the field. The sheep saw a big, fierce dog. The dog had sharp teeth and looked very mean. The sheep was scared and did not know what to do. The sheep thought, "I need help!" The sheep ran to a big cow. The cow asked, "Why are you so scared?" The sheep said, "There is a fierce dog coming!" The cow looked at the dog and said, "I will try to help you." The cow and the sheep tried to run away from the fierce dog. But the dog was too fast for them. The dog caught up to the cow and the sheep. The cow tried to fight the dog, but the dog was too strong. The fierce dog hurt the cow and the sheep. The cow and the sheep could not run anymore. They were very sad and scared. The dog went away, but the cow and the sheep were hurt and could not play in the field anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty big tree. The tree had many leaves. One day, a little leaf was ready to fall. It was scared. A kind bird saw the leaf and said, "Do not be scared, little leaf. When you fall, I will offer you help. I will catch you." The leaf said, "Thank you, kind bird." Then, the leaf let go and began to fall. The bird flew fast and caught the leaf in its beak. The bird put the leaf on the ground, and the leaf was happy. The leaf said, "You are a good friend, kind bird." The bird and the leaf became best friends.

Once, there was a little bird who loved to whistle. He found an incredible gem in the forest. It was so shiny and pretty. The little bird wanted to show his friends the gem. The bird took the gem to his friends. They all saw the gem and were amazed. They all wanted to whistle like the little bird. So, they all practiced whistling together. It was so much fun. But one day, a big, mean bird saw the gem. He wanted it for himself. The mean bird took the gem away from the little bird and his friends. They were very sad. They could not whistle anymore because the gem was gone.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Millie and Amelia. Millie wanted to go to the station and asked Amelia to come with her. But Amelia was not sure- she was a bit scared. Millie tried to reassure her by saying "It will be fun!" But Amelia was still worried. Amelia had to choose what to do. Should she go to the station with Millie or stay at home? She wanted to be brave, so she decided to go with Millie. When they got to the station, they saw a bad man. He was shouting and swearing. Millie wanted to stay and Amelia wanted to go home. This was a difficult decision to make. Amelia started to cry. Millie put her arm around her. They decided to go to the other station, at the other side of town. As they arrived, they saw lots of nice friendly people. It was much safer there. Amelia smiled and said "Let's choose this station". So, they stayed there and had a happy day!
