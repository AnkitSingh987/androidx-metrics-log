Category: Beginner
Title: The Family and the Park

Once there was a family who wanted to go on a tour. They were so excited and proud to go. They put on their special clothes and called for a taxi. When it arrived, they jumped in and said, "Please take us on a tour!" The taxi-driver drove them to the park. It was beautiful and green and so much fun. The family went on the swings, rode the slides and saw the animals. They were so excited and proud. When it was time to go home the family thanked the driver and said, "Please take us home now." They climbed into the taxi and drove back. They had a wonderful tour and were so proud of it!

John was scared. He looked at the deep blue lake with uncertainty. He couldn't tell if the water was going to be cold. He sighed, as he thought of getting in the water. His mom smiled. “It’s okay,” she said. “I’ll be with you.” She held his hand, as they slowly walked towards the lake. John felt a little better, but he was still scared. He took a deep breath and stepped into the water. He gasped as the cold water hit his skin. He tried to move forward, but he was afraid. Suddenly, he felt his mom’s arms around him. “It’s okay,” she said. “I’m here.” John felt a little less scared. He gradually moved his feet and soon he was splashing around in the blue lake. He glanced up at his mom with a smile and she smiled back. He had taken a big step forward, and he knew that he could do anything!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a dark blue sweater that she loved to wear. One day, Mia and her mom went to the park to spend the whole day playing. While they were at the park, Mia saw her friend, Tim. Tim said, "Hi Mia! I like your sweater. It is very dark blue!" Mia smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim! My mom gave it to me." Mia, Tim, and Mia's mom spent the day playing on the swings, slide, and in the sandbox. They had so much fun! When it was time to go home, Mia hugged her mom and said, "I had a great day and I love my dark blue sweater!"

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She liked to wear her mom's clothes and play with her makeup. One day, she found a big box of powder in her mom's room. Sue was very happy. Sue put the powder on her face and wore a fake necklace. She looked in the mirror and smiled. She felt like a big girl. She went to show her mom and dad. Her mom and dad laughed and said, "You look so pretty, Sue!" They gave her a big hug. Sue was very happy. From that day on, she loved to play dress up and wear her mom's clothes.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom liked to play with his friends, a dog named Max and a bird named Polly. They loved to unite and have fun together. One day, Tom, Max, and Polly heard a loud alarm. They were scared and didn't know what to do. Max said, "We must find where the alarm is coming from!" So, they went on a long walk to find the alarm. As they walked, they found a big red truck with many people around it. The alarm was coming from the truck. Tom, Max, and Polly tried to help, but they were too small. The people did not see them, and they could not stop the alarm. In the end, the alarm kept going, and Tom, Max, and Polly were sad. They went back home, wishing they could have helped. The alarm was too big for them, and they felt small and useless.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to make a jelly snack. He saw a big, dull spoon on the table. He thought it was the perfect tool to measure the jelly. Tim was excited to make his snack all by himself. Tim took the big, dull spoon and dipped it into the jelly jar. He measured one, two, three spoonfuls of jelly. He put the jelly on a piece of bread. It looked so yummy. Tim's mom saw him making his jelly snack. She was proud of him for measuring the jelly all by himself. Tim shared his snack with his mom. They both loved the jelly and had a fun time together.
