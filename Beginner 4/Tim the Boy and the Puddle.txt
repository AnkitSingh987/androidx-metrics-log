Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Puddle

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. He saw a big mud puddle and thought it would be fun to jump in it. As he was jumping, he saw a little bird trying to balance on a branch nearby. "Hello, little bird!" Tim said. The bird looked at him and said, "Hello, Tim! I am an intelligent bird, and I can talk!" Tim was very surprised, but he was happy to have a new friend. They played together all day, until it was time for Tim to go home. The bird said, "I have a secret to tell you. I am not just a bird, I am a magic bird!" Then, with a flap of its wings, the bird turned Tim's muddy clothes clean again. Tim smiled, said goodbye to his new friend, and went home to tell his mom about his amazing day.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing on the lawn. The cat found a hidden toy under a big leaf. The dog saw the cat and said, "What did you find?" The cat said, "I found a toy. Let's play with it!" They played with the toy for a while. Then, they saw a big bird fly down and bow to them. The cat and the dog were very surprised. The big bird said, "Hello, I am a magic bird. I saw you playing and I want to give you a gift." The cat and the dog looked at each other, not sure what to do. The magic bird waved its wings, and the hidden toy turned into a big, colorful ball. The cat and the dog were very happy and played with the big ball all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a nice train. It had a big engine. The train loved to go fast. One day, the train saw a big hill. The train wanted to go up the hill. The train tried to go up the hill, but it was too hard. The engine got very hot. The train did not want to stop. It wanted to go up the hill. The engine started to burn. The train felt sad. A kind bird saw the train. The bird flew to the train and said, "Don't worry, I will help you." The bird blew on the engine. The burn went away. The train felt better. The train and the bird went up the hill together. They were happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a belt. He thought it was a great belt. It was blue and had a big, shiny buckle. Tim wanted to wear the belt. But it was too big for him. So, he asked his mom, "Please, can you make this belt fit me?" His mom said, "Sure, Tim." She took the belt and made it smaller. Tim was very happy. He wore the belt every day. It made him feel great. One day, Tim was playing outside with his friends. They were playing hide and seek. Tim hid behind a tree. Suddenly, the belt started to glow. It was a magic belt! The belt made Tim invisible. His friends could not find him. They were so confused. Tim laughed and said, "Surprise! My belt made me invisible!" They all laughed and played with the magic belt. It was a great day for Tim and his friends.

Lucy was a lovely little girl. She was three years old and full of life. One day she went out for a walk with her mum and dad. As they walked, Lucy heard a beautiful music coming from the park. She was amazed and she wanted to find out where it was coming from. They followed the music and soon came across a band of little children playing instruments. Lucy was so excited that she started to jump up and down, clapping her hands. Her mum and dad smiled at her. They admired the music that the children were playing. They realised how talented the little players were and clapped along with Lucy. The music was so beautiful that Lucy felt like she was in a different world. She was so impressed that she started to dance as the music carried her along. It was a beautiful sight and Lucy’s mum and dad were so proud of her. She was a little girl filled with joy, that had admired a beautiful music.
