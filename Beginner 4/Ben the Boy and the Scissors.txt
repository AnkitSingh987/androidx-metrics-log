Category: Beginner
Title: Ben the Boy and the Scissors

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Ben. Ben was at school, and he was playing with his friend, Tom. Tom had a pair of scissors, and Ben wanted to play with them. "Can I play with your scissors, Tom?" asked Ben. Tom said no. "No," he said rudely. "These scissors are mine." Ben was a bit upset, but he remembered something. He remembered that his mom had given him his own pair of scissors. "That's ok," he said to Tom. "I have my own scissors at home!" Ben ran home to get his scissors, and when he came back, Tom was very happy that Ben remembered where he got them. They had a great time playing with the scissors together. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Zip. Zip was a very fast dog. He loved to run and play all day long. One day, it was Zip's birthday. He was so happy! His friends came to his birthday party. They played games, ate cake, and had a lot of fun together. At the end of the party, Zip's friends gave him a big, red ball. Zip loved his new ball. He ran fast with it in his mouth, zipping around the yard. Zip had the best birthday ever.

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. The wind was strong and naughty. It blew Tim's hat right off his head! Tim tried to resist the wind, but it was too strong for him. He started to chase after his hat, running as fast as his little legs could carry him. Tim saw a big tree and his hat was stuck in it. He tried to reach it, but he was too small. A nice girl named Lily saw Tim and asked, "What's wrong, Tim?" "My hat is stuck in the tree," Tim said with a sad face. Lily wanted to help, so she tried to get the hat down. With a big jump, she grabbed the hat and gave it back to Tim. "Thank you, Lily!" Tim said with a big smile. They both agreed to play together and resist the naughty wind. They held hands and ran around, laughing and having fun. The wind tried to be naughty again, but they were too strong together.

Lily and Ben were adventurous kids. They liked to play outside and pretend they were pilots. They had a big toy jet that they could sit in and make noises. They also had helmets and goggles to look like real pilots. One day, they decided to take their jet to the park. They pushed it along the sidewalk, making zoom and beep sounds. They saw other kids playing with balls and bikes, but they did not care. They wanted to fly their jet. They found a big hill in the park. They thought it would be fun to go down the hill in their jet. They put on their helmets and goggles and got ready. "Are you ready, Ben?" Lily asked. "Yes, I am, Lily," Ben said. "Let's go!" They pushed their jet down the hill. It went very fast and bumpy. They felt the wind in their faces and the thrill in their bellies. They shouted and laughed. They felt like they were flying. But they did not see the big tree at the end of the hill. Their jet crashed into the tree and broke. They fell out of their jet and got hurt. They cried and moaned. They wished they had not gone down the hill. A nice lady came to help them. She saw their jet and their helmets and goggles. She said, "Oh, you poor kids. You were trying to be pilots, weren't you?" They nodded and sniffed. She said, "Being a pilot is fun, but it is also dangerous. You have to be careful and follow the rules. You can't just fly anywhere you want. You have to park your jet in a safe place and watch out for obstacles. Do you understand?" They nodded and said, "Yes, we do." She said, "Good. Now let me take you to your parents and get you some band-aids." Lily and Ben learned their lesson. They still liked to play with their jet, but they did not go down hills anymore. They parked their jet in a safe place and played with other kids. They realized that being adventurous was good, but being reckless was bad. They thanked the nice lady for helping them and for teaching them. They became smarter and safer pilots.
