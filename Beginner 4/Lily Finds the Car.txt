Category: Beginner
Title: Lily Finds the Car

Lily was very anxious. She wanted to see her uncle. He was coming to visit her today. He lived far away and he had a big car. He always brought her fun toys and books. Lily loved her uncle. She waited by the window and looked for his car. She saw many cars, but not his. She asked her mom, "When will uncle come?" Her mom said, "Soon, honey. He is on his way. Be patient." Lily nodded, but she was still anxious. She heard a loud honk. She turned and saw her uncle's car. It was red and shiny. She saw her uncle waving at her. She was so happy. She ran to the door and opened it. She sprang out of the house and hugged her uncle. He lifted her up and spun her around. He said, "Hello, Lily. I missed you so much. How are you?" Lily said, "I'm good, uncle. I'm so glad you're here. I love you." He said, "I love you too, Lily. Let's go inside and see what I brought you." He put her down and took her hand. They walked to the house together. Lily was not anxious anymore. She was excited.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful dog named Max. Max loved to play outside during the sunset. Every day, Max would play with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. They loved to play on the swing and run in the grass. One day, Max saw a big box. Max was very curious, so he went to the box to weigh it. The box was very heavy. Max wanted to show the heavy box to Tim. Max thought that Tim would like the box too. But when Max showed the box to Tim, something unexpected happened. The box opened and a mean cat jumped out. The cat scared Max and Tim, and they both ran away. Max and Tim were not happy. They learned to be careful with heavy boxes.

One day, a cat named Tom went to a restaurant. He wanted to eat a big meal. The restaurant was light and pretty. Tom sat at a table and looked at the food. A waiter came and asked, "What do you want to eat?" Tom said, "I want a big fish!" The waiter went to get the big fish for Tom. Tom was very happy. But when the waiter came back, he had a big plate with a big fish on it. The big fish was hard to balance. The waiter tried to carry it, but the big fish fell off the plate! Tom was sad and said, "My big fish is on the floor!" The waiter said, "I am sorry, Tom. I will get you a new big fish." Tom waited for the new big fish. This time, the waiter was careful and balanced the big fish. Tom ate the big fish and was happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was tired from playing all day, but he did not want to sleep. He wanted to play with his ball. Max's ball was his favorite toy. He loved to run and catch it. One day, Max was playing with his ball outside. He threw the ball very far. But when he went to get it, he could not find it. Max was sad. He missed his ball very much. He looked and looked, but it was not there. Max's friend, a little bird, saw him looking for the ball. The bird wanted to help. The bird flew high in the sky and looked for the ball. After a minute, the bird found the ball. It was under a big tree. Max was very happy to see his ball again. He thanked the little bird for helping him. From that day on, Max and the bird were best friends. They played together every day, and Max never missed his ball again.

Once upon a time, there was a bald baby. The baby had a big, round head and no hair. The baby lived in a small house with his mom and dad. They loved the baby very much. One day, the baby saw a part in the wall. The part was a little hole. The baby was curious and wanted to see what was inside the hole. He crawled closer and closer to the part in the wall. When the baby reached the part, he saw a tiny, friendly bug. The bug had many legs and was very cute. The baby and the bug became friends. They played together every day. The baby was happy, and so was the bug.
