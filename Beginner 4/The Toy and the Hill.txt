Category: Beginner
Title: The Toy and the Hill

One day, a funny toy went on a big trip. The toy wanted to find a new friend to play with. It walked and walked, looking for a friend. Then, the toy saw a big hill. It thought, "If I climb this hill, I will succeed in finding a friend." So, the toy climbed and climbed, and when it got to the top, it saw a little house. The toy went to the little house and knocked on the door. A little girl opened the door, and she was so happy to see the funny toy. But then, something unexpected happened. The little girl said, "You are my toy! I lost you a long time ago!" The toy was so surprised, but it was happy to be back with its friend. They played and had fun together, and the toy knew it had succeeded in its big trip.

John and Jane were playing on the playground. Jane was swinging on the swing and John was pushing her. Suddenly, John said to Jane "Do you like salad?" Jane thought for a moment and then said "Yes, I do!" John nodded and then said, "Let's have a salad for lunch today". Jane clapped her hands and smiled. John and Jane went back home and made a salad. John put some tomatoes, cucumbers, lettuce, and cheese in the salad. Jane was thoughtful as she watched John make the salad and said, "This looks so good". When the salad was ready, it looked very delicious. John and Jane sat down to eat. Jane put a spoonful of the salad in her mouth and started to laugh. John saw her laughing and started to laugh too. John said, "This salad is so yummy!" Jane nodded in agreement. John and Jane ate the salad and laughed. They enjoyed their lunch together and were both thankful for the thoughtful salad that John had made.

One day, a little bird named Tim was flying around. He saw a nice tree to relax in. The tree had many twigs. Tim found a big twig to sit on. He was very happy. Tim saw a dead bug on the ground. He wanted to eat it. But, a big cat named Sam was near the bug. Tim was scared of cats. He thought, "What should I do?" Tim called to Sam, "Hey, Sam! I see a big treat for you over there!" Sam looked and saw a big bowl of milk. Sam ran to the milk and started to drink it. While Sam was busy, Tim flew down and got the dead bug. He went back to his twig and ate it. He was very happy. From that day on, Tim and Sam were friends. They both learned to share and help each other. They played and relaxed in the tree together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tom. Tom had an unusual costume. It was a big, green frog suit. He loved to wear it and play all day. One sunny day, Tom saw a little girl crying near a tree. He went to her and asked, "Why are you sad?" The little girl said, "My kitty is stuck in the tree. I can't get her down." Tom wanted to help her. Tom put on his frog costume and started to climb the tree. He went up and up until he reached the kitty. He held the kitty tight and climbed back down. The little girl was so happy. Tom saved the kitty, and they all played together for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a toy bunny with a pink nose. She loved her bunny very much and took it everywhere she went. One day, Sue and her bunny went to the park. Sue played and played, and then she got tired. She sat on the grass to rest her legs. While she was sitting, she looked at her bunny and saw that its nose was not pink anymore. It was dirty from playing in the park. Sue felt embarrassed because her bunny's nose was not pretty and pink. Sue took her bunny home and asked her mom for help. Her mom washed the bunny's nose with soap and water. Soon, the nose was pink again. Sue was happy and thanked her mom. From that day on, Sue made sure to keep her bunny's nose clean and pink.
