Category: Beginner
Title: The Proud Fly and the Flower

Once upon a time there was a proud fly who lived in a meadow. Every day, the fly would fly around the meadow, from one flower to the next. The fly loved the meadow and was very proud of his home. One day something strange appeared in the meadow. It was big, green and didn't move. The fly was a bit scared of it, so he decided to approach it slowly. "Hello! Who are you?" asked the fly. The big green thing didn't respond. The fly tried again. "What is this big, green thing?" Suddenly, the big, green thing opened its mouth and a mild voice said, "I am the Wind". The wind started blowing the fly around the meadow. Although the fly was scared at first, he soon realized that the wind was actually helping him to get around faster. From that day forward, the fly felt proud of the wind's help. The moral of the story is that things that seem strange or scary at first can turn out to be helpful in the end.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big drum. The drum had a special rhythm that made everyone dance and feel happy. Everyone was grateful for the big drum. One day, a bad man came to the town. He wanted to steal the big drum's rhythm. He thought if he had the rhythm, he could make people dance for him. So, he took a small box and caught the rhythm inside it. After that, the town was very sad. No one could dance or feel happy anymore. The bad man tried to use the rhythm, but it did not work for him. The rhythm was gone forever, and the town was never the same again.

Jill and Tom were twins who liked to play outside. One day, they saw a big bush with red berries in their neighbor's yard. They wanted to try the berries, but their mom had told them not to go into other people's yards. "Let's go get some berries," Tom said. "They look yummy." "No, we can't," Jill said. "Mom will punish us if she finds out. And the neighbor might be angry too." Tom did not listen to Jill. He was nosy and hungry. He ran to the bush and picked some berries. He put one in his mouth and smiled. "Mmm, they are sweet. Come on, Jill, don't be scared." Jill was scared, but she did not want to be left alone. She followed Tom and picked some berries too. She ate one and liked it. She forgot about mom and the neighbor. But the neighbor did not forget. He saw the twins eating his berries from his window. He was not happy. He came out of his house and shouted at them. "Hey, you little thieves! Get away from my bush! Those are my berries!" The twins were startled and dropped the berries. They ran back to their yard, but it was too late. The neighbor had seen their faces and knew where they lived. He knocked on their door and told their mom what they had done. Mom was very angry. She scolded the twins and made them apologize to the neighbor. She also punished them by sending them to their room without dinner. They cried and wished they had listened to mom. They learned their lesson: never be nosy and steal other people's berries.

One day, a dog named Max went for a jog. He liked to run fast and be the best. Max was competitive. As he ran, he saw his friend, a cat named Luna. "Hi Luna! Want to jog with me?" Max asked. Luna said, "Yes, I like to run too!" They jogged together and saw a garden. In the garden, there was a herb. The herb smelled nice. Max and Luna stopped to smell it. Max said, "I like this herb. It is nice to share with you." Luna said, "Yes, it is fun to jog and smell herbs together." They jogged back home and told their friends about the nice herb they found.

Once upon a time, there was a big, bright sun in the sky. A little girl named Sue went outside to play. She saw a box on the ground. The box had a big button on it. Sue was very curious about the button. A little boy named Tim arrived. He wanted to play with Sue. But Sue and Tim started to fight. They both wanted to push the button on the box. They pushed and pulled each other, trying to get to the button first. In the end, Sue pushed the button. But it was not a good thing. A loud noise came out of the box. It scared Sue and Tim. They cried and ran away. The bright sun went away, and the day became dark and sad.
