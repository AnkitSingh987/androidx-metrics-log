Category: Beginner
Title: Polly the Parrot and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a parrot named Polly. Polly was a great parrot who lived in a big tree. She had many friends like Tom the dog, and Sam the cat. They all played together every day. One day, Polly saw Tom and Sam fighting. She flew down and said, "Stop! We must behave and be nice to each other." Tom and Sam listened to Polly and stopped fighting. They said sorry to each other and started to play again. From that day on, all the friends learned to behave well and be kind to each other. They played happily and had lots of fun. And they all knew that being nice was the best way to have great friends.

---

One day, a dog named Max found a big bone in the park. Max was so happy! He picked up the bone with his mouth and started to walk home. On his way, he saw his friend, a bird named Sam. Sam could whistle very well. Max wanted to show Sam his new bone. "Look, Sam! I found a big bone!" Max said to Sam. Sam looked at the bone and said, "Wow, Max! That is a big bone! Can I whistle for you?" Max nodded, and Sam whistled a happy song. Max danced with the bone in his mouth while Sam whistled. When Max got home, he saw that his little sister had a smaller bone. She was sad because her bone was not as big as Max's. Max did not want his sister to be sad or feel spoiled. So, Max gave his big bone to his sister and said, "Here, you can have my big bone. We can share." His sister smiled, and they played with the bones together.

---

Sam was a 3 year old boy. He liked to play in his backyard. One day, he saw a lumber truck drive in and thought it was the most exciting thing. The driver was so kind and warm, and he waved to Sam. Sam waved back and said "Thank you!" The driver said he had a present for Sam and asked if Sam wanted to help him unload the truck. Sam was overjoyed! He jumped up and ran to help. Together, they unloaded lots of lumber, and when they were done, the driver gave Sam a big hug and said "Thank you for your help!" Sam was so happy, he thanked the driver again. The driver smiled and said he was happy to be so kind. Then the driver wished Sam a good day and drove away. Sam stood in the backyard looking at the lumber. He realized the driver was very kind and thanked him in his mind.

---

Tom and Lily were excited to play with their new toy cars. They had red and blue cars that could zoom very fast on the floor. They made noises like vroom and beep as they raced their cars around the room. "Look, Lily, my car can zoom under the chair!" Tom said, as he pushed his red car under the big chair in the corner. "Wow, that's cool, Tom! But my car can zoom over the chair!" Lily said, as she lifted her blue car and threw it over the chair. The car landed on the other side with a thud. "Hey, that's not fair, Lily! You can't throw your car, you have to zoom it!" Tom said, feeling angry. "But it's fun, Tom! And it's faster!" Lily said, smiling. Tom and Lily started to argue about who had the better car and how to zoom it. They did not notice that their mom came into the room. She saw their cars on the floor and their faces red. "Tom, Lily, what are you doing?" she asked, in a stern voice. "We are playing with our cars, mom!" Tom and Lily said, at the same time. "Playing or fighting?" mom asked, raising her eyebrows. Tom and Lily looked at each other and felt sorry. They knew they had been mean to each other. "Sorry, mom. Sorry, Tom. Sorry, Lily." they said, in a low voice. Mom smiled and hugged them. She said, "It's okay, my loves. I'm glad you like your cars, but you have to share and be nice to each other. How about we zoom your cars together and see who can make the best noise?" Tom and Lily nodded and smiled. They picked up their cars and followed mom to the living room. They zoomed their cars on the carpet, the sofa, and the table. They made noises like vroom, beep, and honk. They had fun and laughed a lot. They were excited to play with their cars and with each other.
