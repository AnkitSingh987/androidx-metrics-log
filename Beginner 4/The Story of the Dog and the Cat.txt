Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Dog and the Cat

One day, a big dog and a small cat were at a stage. They wanted to sing and dance for all their friends. The big dog was very good at singing, but the small cat was not. The small cat was sad and said, "I hate singing. I wish I could sing like you." The big dog said, "I can help you. Let's try together." So, they started to sing and dance. But then, a thick cloud came and covered the stage. The friends could not see the big dog and the small cat anymore. They were scared and started to cry. The big dog and the small cat tried to make the cloud go away, but they couldn't. The thick cloud stayed, and their friends went home. The big dog and the small cat were sad. They could not sing and dance for their friends. The day had a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to play outside in the mud. One day, she found a dirty rope in her yard. She picked it up and started to swing it around. Mia saw her friend Tom walking by. "Hey Tom, look at my dirty rope!" she said. Tom came over and looked at the rope. "Wow, that's a really dirty rope, Mia!" he said. They both laughed and played with the rope together. Later, Mia and Tom saw a small fire. They knew they had to be careful. Mia had an idea. "We can use my dirty rope to put out the fire!" she said. They took the rope and hit the fire until it was all gone. The fire didn't burn them, and they were very happy. Mia and Tom knew they had done a good thing. They went home feeling proud and had a fun day playing with the dirty rope.

Once upon a time, there was a mop. The mop was very uncomfortable. It did not like to clean the floor. It wanted to do something else. One day, the mop saw a boy write on a paper. The mop wanted to write too. It asked the boy, "Can I write with you?" The boy said, "Yes, you can!" The mop was very happy. It wrote and wrote with the boy. They had so much fun. The mop was not uncomfortable anymore. It found something it loved to do. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a razor. He did not know what it was. Tim showed it to his mom. She said, "That is not a toy. Give it to me, please." Tim felt sorry for taking it. He gave it to his mom. The next day, Tim saw a man with a beard. The man looked sad. Tim thought, "Maybe he needs the razor to change his beard." Tim ran home and asked his mom for the razor. She said, "No, Tim. Razors are not for kids." Later, Tim saw the man again. This time, the man was smiling. He had a new friend with a beard too. They were happy together. Tim learned that sometimes things change in a good way, even without a razor. He was not sorry anymore.

Maggie was very excited. She had taken her little toy basket and was off to collect smiles. Everywhere she went, people stopped and smiled at her. She could collect more than she could ever imagine! But then Maggie saw something upsetting. A little girl was crying. She had lost her teddy bear and was very sad. Maggie looked around and promised to help the little girl find her bear. Maggie looked and looked, but she couldn't find the bear. Finally she asked a nearby lady for help. The lady closed her eyes and thought of a place the bear could be. She opened her eyes and said, "I'm sure if you look in the park you'll find the teddy bear". Maggie smiled and thanked the lady. She ran to the park and soon enough, she found the little girl's bear. She handed it to the little girl, who was so happy. She gave Maggie a big hug. Maggie was filled with joy. She had collected the greatest smile of all – the hug of a grateful child.
