Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Fish

Tom and Lily were at the park with their mom. They saw a huge fountain with water that went up and down. It was very pretty and shiny. Tom and Lily wanted to go closer and touch the water. "Can we go, mom?" they asked. "Okay, but be careful. Don't go too close or you will get wet. And don't run or you will fall. And stay where I can see you. And trust me, I will watch you and keep you safe," mom said. Tom and Lily nodded and ran to the fountain. They held hands and walked around it. They saw fish and ducks and flowers in the water. They laughed and pointed and said what they saw. "Look, a red fish!" Tom said. "Look, a yellow duck!" Lily said. "Look, a pink flower!" Tom said. They were having so much fun. They forgot to listen to mom. They went too close to the fountain. They got wet. They ran. They fell. They cried. Mom heard them and ran to them. She picked them up and hugged them. She dried them and kissed them. She said, "It's okay, my loves. You are not hurt. You are just wet. But you have to listen to me. I told you to be careful. I told you to trust me. I know what is good for you. I love you." Tom and Lily said, "We are sorry, mom. We love you too. We will listen to you. We will trust you." Mom smiled and said, "Good. Now let's go home and change. And maybe we can come back another day and see the fountain again. But from a safe distance." Tom and Lily nodded and said, "Yes, mom. Thank you, mom." They held mom's hand and walked to the car. They were happy and they learned a lesson. They trusted mom.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely tree. It had no friends to play with. One day, a girl named Lily came to the tree. She saw the tree and said, "Hello, tree! You look lonely. Let's be friends!" The tree was very happy to have a friend. Lily touched the tree's bark and said, "Wow, your texture is so nice!" Together, they would dance and play under the sun. The tree would sway its branches, and Lily would twirl and jump around. As they danced and played, other kids saw them and wanted to join in. Soon, the lonely tree had many friends. The tree was very happy, and so was Lily. They all danced and played together, and the tree was never lonely again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to smile and play with his friends. One day, Tim's mom made a yummy pie for everyone to eat. Tim was very happy and could not wait to share the pie with his friends. Tim and his friends went outside to play. They saw a broken toy on the ground. They felt sad for the toy. Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's fix the toy and make it like new." So, they all worked together to fix the broken toy. When the toy was fixed, they all smiled because they were happy they could help. After they fixed the toy, Tim's mom called them inside to eat the pie. They all sat down and shared the pie. Tim and his friends learned that helping others and sharing can make everyone smile. They promised to always help and share with each other. And they lived happily ever after, with big smiles on their faces.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there was a tall oak tree. The oak tree was troubled because it felt lonely. It wanted to have friends and play with them. One sunny day, a little bird came to the oak tree. The bird said, "Hi oak tree, I see you are troubled. I want to help you." The oak tree was happy to see the bird and said, "Thank you, little bird. I want to have friends and not be lonely." The little bird had an idea. It said, "Let's unite all the animals in the forest and have a big party. We can all be friends and play together." The oak tree liked the idea and agreed. The little bird went to all the animals in the forest and told them about the party. The animals were excited and came to the oak tree. They all played and had fun together. The oak tree was not troubled anymore and was happy to have so many friends. From that day on, the oak tree and the animals were always united and played together in the big green forest.
