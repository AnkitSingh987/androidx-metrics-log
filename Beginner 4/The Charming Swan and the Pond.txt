Category: Beginner
Title: The Charming Swan and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a charming swan. The swan lived in a big pond with many friends. They liked to swim and play all day. The swan was very happy and loved its home. One day, a little girl came to the pond. She saw the charming swan and wanted to be its friend. The little girl sat by the pond and talked to the swan. They became very good friends and played together every day. One night, the little girl had to leave her friend, the charming swan. She was sad and didn't want to say goodbye. So, the little girl closed her eyes and started to pray. She prayed for the swan to always be happy and safe in the big pond. The charming swan knew the little girl cared and felt happy. They both knew they would be friends forever.

One day, a kind boy named Tim found a big lollipop. He was very happy. Tim wanted to eat the lollipop, but he saw his friend Ben. Ben looked sad. Tim was thoughtful. Tim thought of a plan. He broke the lollipop into two parts. He gave one part to Ben. Ben was very happy now. The two friends ate their parts of the lollipop together. The moral of the story is to share. When we share, we make others happy. And when they are happy, we are happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy vehicles. One day, he saw a big, empty box in his room. He started to wonder what he could do with it. Tim had a great idea! He decided to make a big vehicle out of the empty box. He used some crayons to draw wheels and windows. He also made a door so he could get inside his new vehicle. Tim had so much fun playing in his new vehicle. He pretended to drive it all around his room. He even invited his friends to come and play with him. They all had a wonderful time in Tim's new, empty box vehicle.

One day, a boy named Tom wanted to play football. He asked his friend, Sam, to play with him. "Sam, do you want to play football with me?" Tom asked. Sam said, "Yes, let's play!" They went to the park to play. Tom had a tight grip on the football. They played for a long time. They were having so much fun. But then, a big dog came. The dog wanted the football too. Tom and Sam were scared. The dog growled at them. Tom said, "We have to surrender the football to the dog." They gave the football to the dog. The dog took the football and ran away. Tom and Sam were sad because they lost their football.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly cat named Tom. Tom liked to eat a lot. His stomach was always full. One day, Tom met a pretty cat named Sue. Tom and Sue played together and had fun. Tom and Sue liked each other very much. They wanted to marry. They were so happy. All the other cats were happy too. They danced and sang at the wedding. Tom and Sue loved each other. But one day, Tom ate too much food. His stomach hurt. He could not play with Sue. He could not dance or sing. Tom's stomach hurt so bad that he could not be with Sue anymore. Sue was sad, and they could not be happy together again.

Once upon a time, there was a little infant named Tim. Tim had a black ball that he loved to play with. He would throw it, kick it, and roll it all day long. One day, Tim saw a big boy named Jack. Jack wanted to play with the black ball, too. Tim did not want to share his ball, but he knew he had to resist being mean. So, he said, "Okay, Jack, we can play together." They played with the black ball all day, and Tim learned that sharing can be fun. They laughed and had a great time. At the end of the day, Tim said, "Thank you, Jack, for playing with me. I am happy we shared my black ball." From that day on, Tim and Jack were good friends. They played together and shared their toys. Tim was glad he resisted being mean and learned to share with others.
