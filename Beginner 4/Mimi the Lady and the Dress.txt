Category: Beginner
Title: Mimi the Lady and the Dress

One day, an elderly lady named Mimi found a pretty dress in her closet. She wanted to wear it to a party. But, oh no! The dress was too big. Mimi felt sad. Mimi's friend, Tim, came to visit. He saw her sad face and asked, "Why are you sad, Mimi?" Mimi said, "My dress is too big. I can't wear it to the party." Tim had an idea. "Let's try to shrink the dress!" They washed the dress in warm water. The dress got smaller. Mimi put on the dress and it fit just right. Mimi and Tim went to the party and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a rich man named Tom. Tom had a big house and many toys. One day, he saw a boy named Sam playing outside. Sam had a ball. Tom went outside and asked Sam, "Do you want to play with me?" Sam smiled and said, "Yes, let's play!" They took turns to throw the ball to each other. The ball moved in a motion through the air. They laughed and had fun. After playing, Tom asked Sam, "Do you want to come to my big house and see my toys?" Sam said, "Yes, thank you!" They went inside Tom's big house and played with the toys. They became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big war between two groups of ants. One group was red and the other group was black. They were not friends and always fought. One day, a small red ant named Tim wanted to surprise the black ants. He had a plan to make them friends. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, I am ready to make the black ants our friends." His mom was happy and said, "Okay, Tim. You can try." Tim went to the black ants' home and said, "Hi, I am Tim. I want to be friends." The black ants were surprised. They talked to their leader, and the leader said, "We are ready to be friends too." So, the red ants and the black ants became friends. They stopped the war and lived happily together. The surprise from Tim made everyone happy.

One day, a shy cat named Bob had a plan. He wanted to play golf with his friends. He was very excited, but also a little scared. Bob went to the park with his golf toys. He saw his friends, a dog and a bird, waiting for him. They were happy to see him. They all played golf together. In the end, Bob was not shy anymore. He had fun playing golf with his friends. They all went home, tired but happy. They planned to play golf again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside in the sun. One day, she saw a big tree with pretty red apples on it. Lily thought they would make a perfect snack. Lily asked her mom for help to get an apple. Her mom got a long stick and cut an apple from the tree. The apple fell into Lily's hands. She was so happy to have a yummy snack. Lily and her mom sat under the tree and shared the apple. It was the perfect snack for a sunny day. They laughed and played together. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He had a pillow that he loved very much. It was soft and cozy. Tom took his pillow everywhere he went. One day, Tom met a big dog. The dog could whistle. "Can you teach me to whistle?" Tom asked the dog. The dog said, "Yes, but you must promise not to be rude." Tom promised and the dog taught him how to whistle. Tom was so happy he could whistle. But he forgot his promise to the dog. He went around whistling loudly and being rude to everyone. People and animals did not like it. They all got mad at Tom. In the end, Tom lost all his friends because of his rude whistling. He felt very sad and lonely. He wished he had never learned to whistle.
