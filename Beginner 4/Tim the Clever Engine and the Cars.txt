Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Clever Engine and the Cars

Once upon a time, there was a clever little engine named Tim. Tim lived in a big town with many friends. Tim loved to play and help others. One day, Tim saw a line of cars that needed to go to a far away place. Tim wanted to help them. So, he said, "Follow me, cars! I will take you there." The cars were happy and said, "Thank you, Tim!" Tim and the cars went on a long trip. They went up hills and down hills. They saw many pretty things. At last, they reached the far away place. The cars were so happy, they cheered for Tim. Tim felt very proud to be such a clever engine.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird with delicate wings. The bird lived in a big tree with other birds. Every day, they would fly and play together. One day, the little bird tried to fly high, but it could not. The bird felt sad and sat on a branch. A big bird saw the little bird and came to help. The big bird said, "Why are you sad, little bird?" The little bird told the big bird, "I can't fly high like the other birds. My wings hurt." The big bird looked at the little bird's wings and saw they were not well. The big bird said, "I will help you." The big bird gently rubbed the little bird's wings. The little bird felt better. They both smiled and flew high together. Now, the little bird could play with the other birds again.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside in the sun. He had a fine, big ball that he loved to kick and throw. One day, Tim and his friends were playing with the ball. They were having so much fun. But then, Tim kicked the ball very hard, and it went up, up, up into a big tree. The ball got stuck in the tree, and Tim was sad. His friends tried to help him get the ball down. They shook the tree, but the ball did not move. Then, they threw sticks at the ball, but it still did not come down. Finally, Tim had an idea. He found a long stick and used it to poke the ball. The ball split in half and fell down from the tree. Tim was happy to have his ball back, even if it was broken. His friends were happy too, and they all went to find a new ball to play with.

Once upon a time, there lived a mom and her little girl. The little girl was often very anxious. Every day the mom would take her daughter to the park to play. One day, when they got to the park, the little girl was feeling a bit more anxious than usual. So the mom said, “Don’t worry, sweetheart. Here, have a cookie and remain here by the swing. I’ll be right back, ok?” The little girl nodded, but she remained anxious. She held her cookie in her hands and took a peek at it from time to time. She worried she would not be able to eat the whole thing. Suddenly, she saw an old woman walking slowly toward her. The old woman stopped in front of the little girl and said in a warm voice, “Would you like to share your cookie with me, dear?” The little girl was so happy to share her cookie. So she split it in two and gave one half to the old woman. When they were done, the little girl immediately felt less anxious. The moral of this story is that sharing something we have with someone else can make us feel less anxious. Despite the fact that the little girl had to share her cookie, at the end of the day she was happy and felt much better.

Once upon a time, in a white house, there lived a cat and a dog. They did not like each other very much. They always had fights. One day, the cat said, "I can balance better than you!" The dog did not believe her. "Show me!" he said. So, the cat jumped on a ball and balanced on it. The dog was amazed. The dog wanted to try too. He jumped on the ball, but he could not balance. He fell down. The cat laughed at him. The dog felt sad. Then, the cat said, "I will help you balance." The dog was happy. They practiced together, and soon, the dog could balance too. They stopped fighting and became friends. They lived happily ever after.
