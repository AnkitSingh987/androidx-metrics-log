Category: Beginner
Title: Bobo the Boat and the Fish

One day, a little boat named Bobo was in the water. He loved to speed around the open sea. Bobo saw a big island far away. He was very excited and wanted to go there. As Bobo sped towards the island, he met a big fish named Fifi. Fifi said, "Slow down, Bobo! If you speed too much, you might get hurt." But Bobo did not listen and kept speeding to the island. When Bobo reached the island, he hit a rock and got a big hole in his side. He felt sad and scared. Fifi came to help him and said, "See, Bobo? I told you to slow down. Now you are hurt." They worked together to fix Bobo's hole. From that day on, Bobo learned to listen to his friends and not to speed too much. He knew that going too fast can be bad. Bobo and Fifi played together in the open sea, and they were very happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a nosy boy named Tim. He liked to yell and ask about everything. One day, Tim saw a man with a big bag made of leather. He wanted to know what was inside the bag. Tim yelled, "Hey, what is in your bag?" The man did not want to tell Tim. But Tim was nosy and kept asking. The man got tired of Tim's yelling and said, "Okay, I will show you what is in my bag." The man opened his bag and out came a big, friendly dog! Tim was so happy to see the dog. He learned that being nosy can sometimes lead to good things. But, he also learned not to yell at people. Instead, he should ask nicely. And that was the day Tim became a better boy.

Once upon a time, there was a wild dog. This dog liked to play near a bridge. One day, the dog saw a big cat on the bridge. The cat was not nice. The dog wanted the cat to go away. The dog had an idea. He would tell the cat to surrender. The dog went to the cat and said, "Surrender, cat!" The cat looked at the dog and laughed. The dog was sad. He thought his idea did not work. But then, something unexpected happened. The cat said, "Okay, I surrender. Let's be friends." The dog was very happy. The wild dog and the big cat became best friends and played near the bridge every day.

Once upon a time, in a small school, there was a classroom full of wild kids. They liked to play and run all day. They did not like to listen to their teacher. The teacher was sad because the kids did not learn. One day, a big rain came down. The kids could not go outside and play. They had to stay in the classroom. The teacher had an idea. She said, "Let's play a new game. It is called 'bathe the toy'. We will learn to clean our toys." The kids liked the idea and started to play. As they played, the wild kids learned to listen and share. They found out that learning can be fun. The teacher was happy, and the kids were happy too. The rain brought something good. The moral of the story is that even when things seem bad, good things can happen.

Ben and Lily went to a big restaurant with their mom and dad. They sat at a table with a red cloth and a shiny fork and knife. A nice lady came and gave them a menu. It had many pictures of food and drinks. "What do you want to eat?" mom asked. "I want pizza!" Ben said. "I want pasta!" Lily said. Mom and dad looked at the menu and chose their food too. The nice lady wrote down their order and went away. Soon, the food came. Ben and Lily were happy to see their pizza and pasta. They smelled good and looked yummy. Mom and dad had a big salad and a brown drink. "Can I try your drink, dad?" Ben asked. "Sure, son. But you might not like it. It's bitter." dad said. "What does bitter mean?" Ben asked. "It means it tastes sour and not sweet." dad said. Ben took a sip of the drink. He made a face and spit it out. "Yuck! That's bitter!" he said. Lily laughed. "I don't want to try it. I like sweet things." she said. Mom smiled. "It's okay, Ben. You don't have to like everything. But I'm proud of you for trying something new. That's how you learn and grow." she said. She encouraged Ben to eat his pizza and enjoy his meal. Ben felt better and nodded. He liked his mom and dad. They were nice and smart. He liked his sister too, even when she laughed at him. He liked the restaurant and the food. He had a good time. He decided he would try more new things, but not bitter things. He liked sweet things too.
