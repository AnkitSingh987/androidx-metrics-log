Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Race

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was very excited because today was the day of the big race. All his friends would be there, and they would race to see who was the fastest. Before the race, Tom's mom said, "Tom, you need to wash your hands with soap." Tom went to the sink and washed his hands with the soap. The soap was blue and smelled nice. "Now I am ready to race," said Tom. At the race, Tom and his friends lined up. "Ready, set, go!" they shouted. They all started to run as fast as they could. Tom was running so fast that his feet felt like they were flying. In the end, Tom won the race and his friends all cheered for him.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily was an honest girl who always told the truth. She had a toy telephone that she loved to play with all day. She would pretend to talk to her friends and family on the telephone. One night, Lily had a dream about her telephone. In her dream, the telephone could talk to her! It said, "Hello, Lily! I can talk to you now. Let's be friends." Lily was so happy and they talked and laughed together. When Lily woke up, she ran to her toy telephone and tried to talk to it again. But it did not talk back. Lily was sad, but she knew it was just a dream. She still loved her toy telephone and continued to play with it every day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Lucy. She had a pretty dress with many colors. It was an unusual dress because it could change colors when she danced. Lucy loved her dress so much. One day, Lucy was playing with her friend Jack. Jack was very curious about the dress. He asked, "Why does your dress change colors?" Lucy said, "I don't know, but it's fun!" They both laughed and played together. While they were playing, Jack accidentally stepped on Lucy's dress. The dress started to break. Lucy was sad, but Jack said, "Don't worry, I can fix it!" Jack tried his best to fix it, and the dress was good as new. They both smiled and continued to play.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He had a tie. One day, he wanted to complete a task. The task was to put on his tie all by himself. Tim felt alert and ready to try. He asked his dad, "Dad, can you help me put on my tie?" His dad said, "You can do it, Tim! Just try and you will complete it." Tim felt brave and tried to put on his tie. Tim pulled and twisted the tie. After trying hard, he finally did it! He completed the task and put on his tie all by himself. Tim felt proud and smiled. His dad hugged him and said, "Good job, Tim! You did it!"

One day, a powerful wind came to the town. It blew leaves all over the place. Tom, a little boy, wanted to help his mom clean their yard. He took the rake and started to gather the leaves. "Mom, look! I can wrap the leaves with the rake!" Tom said. But the powerful wind blew the leaves away again. Tom was sad and didn't know what to do. Just then, a big bird came and spoke to Tom. "Don't be sad, I can help you!" The bird used its big wings to blow the leaves into a big pile. Tom was happy and thanked the bird. The wind stopped blowing, and Tom and the bird became good friends.

One day, a little cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Tom was very glad because he loved to climb. He blinked his eyes and started to climb the tree. Up in the tree, Tom saw a small bird. The bird was taking a nap. Tom blinked again and said "hello" to the bird. The bird woke up and said "hello" back to Tom. They became friends and played together in the tree. After playing for a long time, Tom and the bird were tired. They decided to take a nap together. Tom was very glad to have a new friend. They both blinked their eyes, said "good night" and went to sleep.
