Category: Beginner
Title: Jill and Tom and the Dish

Jill and Tom were hungry. They wanted to eat something sweet. They saw a big bowl of peaches on the table. They liked peaches. They took two peaches and washed them. "Let's prepare the peaches," Jill said. "We can cut them and put them in a dish." "OK," Tom said. "But how do we cut them? They have a hard thing in the middle." Jill showed Tom how to cut the peaches. She used a small knife and cut around the hard thing. Then she twisted the peaches and pulled them apart. She took out the hard thing and threw it in the trash. "Look, Tom, this is the pit. It has a weird shape. It is not round like the peach. It is pointy and bumpy." Tom looked at the pit. He touched it with his finger. It felt rough and hard. He did not like it. "Yuck, I don't want to eat the pit. I only want to eat the peach. It is soft and juicy and sweet." Jill agreed. She put the peach halves in a dish and gave one to Tom. They ate the peaches and smiled. They were happy. They liked to prepare and eat peaches. They did not like the weird pits.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a selfish cat named Tom. Tom never shared his toys with his friends. One day, Tom was playing with his ball near a tree. He saw his friend, a little bird named Sally, sitting on a branch. "Hi, Sally! Can I play with you?" asked Tom. Sally was a kind bird and said, "Yes, Tom! Let's play together." They started to play, but Tom wouldn't share his ball. "This is my ball! You can't play with it!" Tom said in a selfish way. Suddenly, Sally heard an alarm. She looked up and saw that the tree was about to fall! "Tom, we need to move! The tree is going to fall!" she said. Tom was scared and ran away, but he forgot his ball. Sally was brave and picked up the ball. She flew away just in time before the tree fell. After the tree fell, Tom realized that he had been selfish. He thanked Sally for saving his ball and said, "I'm sorry for being selfish, Sally. Let's share the ball and play together." Sally smiled and they played happily. From that day on, Tom learned to share and be kind to his friends. The moral of the story is to always be kind and share with your friends, because you never know when you might need their help.

One day, a lovely group of animals were playing in the forest. There was a big bear, a small rabbit, and a fast deer. They liked to run, jump, and laugh together. The sun was shining, and the sky was blue. They were all very happy. Suddenly, the big bear saw a baby bird fall from its nest. The baby bird could not fly, and it was scared. The big bear wanted to save the baby bird but he was too big and might hurt it. The small rabbit and the fast deer also wanted to help, but they did not know how. Then, a tiny ant walked by and saw the baby bird. The tiny ant said, "I can save the baby bird!" The lovely group of animals laughed because the tiny ant was so small. But the tiny ant was strong and smart. It found a leaf and carried the baby bird back to its nest. The lovely group of animals clapped and cheered. They learned that even the smallest friend can do big things.

Once upon a time, there was a little spark. The spark was so small that no one could see it unless looked very closely. It wanted to shine, but it was too small to do it on its own. One day, the spark saw an open window. It knew this was it's chance to shine! But it was too scared to jump out the window. Still, the spark mustered up it's courage and, with one big leap, jumped out the window. As soon as it was outside, something amazing happened. The spark started to glow and shine brighter and brighter! The spark was so happy and couldn't believe it was finally shining. In that moment, it realized that sometimes it pays to take risks!
