Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Lovely Dog and the Cup

One day, a lovely dog named Max went for a walk. He liked to sniff and play. Max saw a big cup on the ground. It was a cup of coffee. Max was curious about the cup. Max went closer to the cup of coffee. He sniffed it and did not like the smell. Max wanted to know how it tasted. So, Max decided to bite the cup. When Max bit the cup, the hot coffee spilled on him. Max cried and ran away. He was hurt and sad. Max learned not to bite things he did not know. It was a bad day for Max.

One day, Tim saw a dull, red tube. He wanted to know what was inside it. He tried to lift the tube, but it was too heavy. He called his friend, Sam, to help him. "Sam, can you help me lift this tube?" Tim asked. Sam came over and together, they lifted the tube. Inside, they found a big, soft ball. Tim and Sam played with the ball all day. They threw it, kicked it, and even rolled on it. They were very happy they found the ball in the dull, red tube. And they were even happier they could lift it together.

One day, a little boy named Tim was ready to play outside. He put on his shoes and went to the park. He saw his friend, a dog named Spark. Spark was a happy dog with a big smile. Tim and Spark liked to run and play together. They ran up and down the hills, laughing and having fun. The sun was shining and the sky was blue. They played catch with a ball and ran as fast as they could. After a while, Tim and Spark were tired but happy. They sat under a tree and shared a snack. Tim gave Spark a treat and they both smiled. They were the best of friends and loved to run and play together.

Tom and Sue are friends. They like to play with toys and books. One day, they find a big map in the library. The map shows many countries. Some countries are big, some are small. Some have mountains, some have water. "Look, Sue, this is where we live," Tom says, pointing to a country on the map. "It is not very big, but it is nice." "Yes, Tom, I like our country," Sue says. "But what about these other countries? Do you know anything about them?" Tom shakes his head. He does not know much about other countries. He only knows what he sees on TV or hears from his mom and dad. "Maybe we can plan a trip to see them," Sue says. "We can save our money and buy a plane ticket. Then we can fly to any country we want." Tom likes Sue's idea. He thinks it would be fun to see new places and people. He wonders what they would find in other countries. "Maybe we can go to this country," he says, pointing to a big country on the map. "It looks very rich. It has many buildings and cars and lights. Maybe they have many toys and books too." "Or maybe we can go to this country," Sue says, pointing to a small country on the map. "It looks very green. It has many trees and flowers and animals. Maybe they have many fruits and nuts too." Tom and Sue keep looking at the map. They plan their trip in their minds. They imagine what they would see and do in different countries. They are very happy and excited. They do not notice that it is time to go home. They do not hear the librarian calling them. They only hear each other and their dreams.

One day, a girl named Sue and a boy named Tom were playing in the yard. They saw a big, thick pot under the tree. Sue said, "Hey, Tom! Look at this big pot! What do you think is in it?" Tom joked, "Maybe it's full of candy!" They both laughed and ran to the pot. They opened it and found many colorful balls inside. Sue and Tom were very happy and played with the balls. Suddenly, a funny-looking bird came down from the tree. The bird said, "Hello, Sue and Tom! I put the pot there as a joke. I wanted to see you smile and have fun!" Sue and Tom were surprised but laughed. They thanked the bird and played together with the balls and the bird all day long.
