Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom had a big red ball. He liked to play with the ball every day. Tom was deaf, so he could not hear, but he could see and feel everything around him. One day, Tom went to the park to play with his ball. He saw a girl named Lucy. Lucy had a kite. The kite had a long string. Lucy tried to tie the string to her kite, but it was hard. Tom wanted to help her. He knew how to tie knots very well. Tom went to Lucy and showed her how to tie the string. They played together and had a lot of fun. Tom did not feel any pain when he played with Lucy. They became good friends and played together every day.

---

One day, a big blue rock sat in the park. The rock was not nice. It was rude. The rock would say mean things to the kids who played near it. The kids did not like the rude blue rock. A little girl named Sue came to the park. She saw the blue rock and said, "Hi, rock! Want to be friends?" The rude blue rock said, "No, go away!" Sue was sad, but she had an idea. Sue went home and came back with a big red ball. She played with the ball near the blue rock. The rock saw how much fun Sue was having. The rock started to feel lonely. The rude blue rock said, "Sue, can I play too?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, but you must be nice." The blue rock promised to be nice. They played together and had lots of fun. The blue rock was not rude anymore. Now, the kids liked the blue rock and everyone was happy.

Once upon a time there was a little boy. He was only three years old. His mum was very happy with him because he was always healthy. One day, the little boy was sitting in his bedroom listening to the radio when his mum came in. "Do you want to come outside and have something to drink?," asked his mum. The little boy was so excited! He put on his shoes and grabbed his cup. He followed his mum outside and there was a glass of cold, healthy juice waiting for him. He drank it all and enjoyed it very much. The little boy was so happy that he wanted to stay outside and play for a while longer. So he and his mum enjoyed the rest of the day together.

One day, a fish and a shark were swimming in the sea. They were friends and liked to discuss things. Today, they were talking about how the sea water tasted bitter. The fish said, "I don't like the bitter taste." The shark said, "Me neither, but we have to live here." As they swam, they saw a big boat. The fish was curious and wanted to see it up close. The shark said, "Be careful, fish. Boats can be dangerous." The fish didn't listen and swam closer to the boat. Suddenly, a net came down and caught the fish. The shark saw this and quickly swam to help. He used his sharp teeth to cut the net and free the fish. They swam away from the boat, safe and sound. The fish said, "Thank you, shark. I should have listened to you." The shark said, "That's okay, fish. We all make mistakes. Just remember to be more careful next time."

Once upon a time, there was an ancient box. In the box, there was a film. The film was very old. A little boy found the box in his room. He did not know what was inside. The little boy asked his mom, "Can we see the film?" His mom said, "Yes, let's watch it." They put the film in a big machine. The film started to play on the wall. At first, the film showed a happy dog running in a park. But then, something unexpected happened. The dog jumped out of the film and into the room! The little boy and his mom were very surprised. They played with the dog and had lots of fun. The dog became their new friend.
