Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Lion and the Grass

Once upon a time, a playful lion was walking in the jungle. He was looking for something fun to do. Suddenly he saw a little bunny hopping in the grass. The bunny was counting all the carrots he could find. The lion thought this was a fun game and he asked the bunny, "Will you let me count with you?" The bunny looked up and said, "Yes, I will." So the two of them went on counting carrots. They even danced and sang and made funny noises as they counted. When they were done counting, the bunny thanked the lion for joining him in his game. The lion said, "It was lots of fun, I hope we can count together again someday." The little bunny smiled and waved goodbye as the lion walked away.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his friend, a little girl named Lily. They played in the park every day. One day, Spot found a ball. He said, "Lily, I found a ball! Let's play!" Lily looked at the ball and said, "Spot, that's not a ball. It's a rock. It's not good for playing." Spot didn't want to accept the truth. He wanted to play with the ball. Lily said, "Spot, I will help you find a real ball." They looked and looked until they found a nice, round ball. Spot was happy and said, "Thank you, Lily. You are very helpful." They played with the ball all day long and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big, happy lion. The lion liked to roar very loud. One day, he saw a little pigeon. The pigeon was not scared of the lion's roar. The lion and the pigeon became friends. They played together every day. The pigeon would fly high, and the lion would roar loud. They were very happy. One day, the pigeon made a loud noise too. The lion was surprised! The pigeon had learned to roar like the lion. Now they both roared together and played all day. They were the best of friends.

One day, a little dog named Spot found a soft toy. Spot loved the toy and played with it all day. He would tease the toy by biting it, then let it go. He was very happy. One day, a big dog named Max came to play with Spot. Max saw the soft toy and said, "Hey Spot, can I play with your toy?" Spot didn't want to share and said, "No, it's mine!" Max felt sad and walked away. Later, Spot found a paper on the ground. It was a license that said, "This soft toy belongs to Max." Spot felt bad for not sharing the toy with Max. He went to find Max and said, "I'm sorry, Max. This toy is yours. Let's play together!" Max was happy, and they played together. They learned that sharing is important and makes everyone happy.

Once upon a time, in a cheerful little town, there lived a round ball named Rolly. Rolly loved to roll around and play with his friends. Every day, Rolly would roll down the street, saying hi to everyone he saw. One day, Rolly was rolling in the park when he saw some food on the ground. It was a big, red apple. Rolly wanted to eat the apple, but he didn't know how. He tried to roll on it, but the apple didn't break. Rolly's friend, Birdy, saw him trying to eat the apple. Birdy had a good idea. He picked up the apple in his beak and flew up high. Then, Birdy dropped the apple, and it broke into pieces on the ground. Rolly was happy and said thank you to Birdy. They both shared the apple and enjoyed their tasty food.

Once upon a time, there was a cute dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and have fun. He wanted to find a friend to spend time with. One day, Spot went to the park. He saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she had no friends to play with. Spot walked up to Sue and wagged his tail. Sue saw Spot and smiled. They played all day and had lots of fun. Spot and Sue became best friends. They spent every day together, and they were both very happy.
