Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a favorite sweater. It was her original sweater. It was red and very soft. Mia loved it very much. One day, Mia went to the park with her mom. They took a seat on a bench. Mia saw her friend, Ben. Ben was wearing a blue sweater. Mia said, "Hi, Ben! I like your sweater!" Ben smiled and said, "Thank you, Mia! I like your red sweater too!" Mia and Ben played on the swings and slides. They had lots of fun. It was time to go home. Mia and her mom said goodbye to Ben. Mia was very happy. She loved her original red sweater and her day at the park.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a weak little boy named Tim. Tim was not strong like the other kids, but he had a big heart. He wanted to become a brave hero one day. One day, Tim met a wise old man who told him the truth about being a hero. The old man said, "To become a hero, you don't need to be strong. You just need to be kind and help others." Tim listened carefully and knew what he had to do. From that day on, Tim helped everyone in the village. He shared his toys, helped carry things, and always had a smile on his face. Soon, everyone in the village loved Tim. And even though he was still weak, Tim had become a true hero in their eyes.

Once upon a time, in a big field, there was a popular cat named Dare. Dare had many friends because he was very nice. He liked to play with his friends all day long in the field. One day, while playing, Dare found a big box. He did not know what was inside. Dare and his friends were very excited. They all wanted to see what was in the box. Dare opened the box and, surprise! There were many hats inside. Dare and his friends put on the hats and started to dance. They had a fun hat party in the field. Dare was happy he found the box, and his friends loved their new hats.

Once upon a time, there was a small white cricket. He lived in a tiny house under a big tree. The cricket loved to play with his friends. They all liked to throw things and have fun. One sunny day, the cricket went to the park. He saw his friend, a little brown dog. The dog had a red ball. The cricket said, "Let's play throw and catch!" The dog wagged his tail and said, "Yes, let's play!" The white cricket and the brown dog played all day. They threw the red ball back and forth. They laughed and had lots of fun. When the sun went down, they said goodbye and went home. They were happy and tired from their fun day of playing.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a colorful bird. The bird loved to tell jokes. Every day, the bird would fly around and tell jokes to all the animals. The animals would laugh and have fun. One day, the bird met a big cat. The bird wanted to tell the cat a joke. But the cat did not want to listen. The cat chose to ignore the bird. The bird tried again and again, but the cat still ignored him. The bird was very sad. The bird did not know that the cat was not nice. The cat did not like jokes. The cat did not like to have fun. In the end, the cat caught the bird and ate him. The town was not happy anymore. The animals missed the bird and his jokes.

One day, a small boy named Tim found a light tube. He played with it all day. He liked how it made pretty colors when he waved it around. Tim's friend, Sue, saw him playing with the light tube. She wanted to play with it too. "Can I play with your light tube?" she asked. Tim said, "No, it's mine!" Sue felt sad and went away. Later, Tim broke the light tube by accident. He was very sad. He wished he had let Sue play with it too. Tim went to Sue and said, "I'm sorry I didn't share. Can you forgive me?" Sue said, "Yes, I forgive you." But the light tube was still broken and they couldn't play with it anymore.
