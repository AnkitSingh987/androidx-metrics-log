Category: Beginner
Title: The Crab and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a little crab who lived near the sea. The crab liked to play with his friends, the fish and the starfish. They would swim and dance in the water all day long. One day, a girl came to the beach to play. She saw the crab and wanted to be friends. The girl knelt down to say hello to the crab. The crab felt uncomfortable because he was not used to people. But the girl was nice and gentle, so the crab slowly came out to meet her. The girl and the crab became good friends. They played together every day. The crab learned that people could be kind, and the girl learned how to be a good friend to the crab. They had many happy days together, and the crab was never uncomfortable again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a nice man named Tom. Tom loved to drink coffee every day. He thought that coffee was incredible. He would always share his coffee with his friends. One day, it started to rain. The rain was very heavy. Tom and his friends were sad because they couldn't play outside in the park. But Tom had an idea. He invited all his friends to his house to drink coffee and have fun. When they all got to Tom's house, they started to drink coffee and talk. But then, the rain got even worse. The water came into the house. Tom and his friends tried to stop the water, but they couldn't. The house was full of water, and their coffee was all gone. They were all very sad. The rain had ruined their fun day.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He liked to hear the birds sing. Tim was very gentle with all the animals he met. He walked and walked until he found a big pit. Tim was curious and wanted to know what was inside. Tim looked into the pit and saw a small, scared kitten. He wanted to help the kitten. Tim reached down with his gentle hands, but he couldn't reach the kitten. He thought and thought about how to save the little kitten from the pit. Then, Tim had an idea. He ran home and got a long rope. He came back to the pit and threw the rope down. The kitten was smart and climbed up the rope! When the kitten was safe, it did something unexpected. The kitten turned into a magic fairy! The fairy thanked Tim for being so kind and gentle. She gave him a wish for being a good friend.

One day, a little boy named Tom went for a walk in the woods. He found a long, pointy spear lying on the ground. It was very clear that someone had left it there. Tom picked up the spear and decided to find its owner. As he walked, he met a big, friendly bear. The bear looked at Tom and said, "Hi, little boy! What are you doing with that spear?" Tom replied, "I found it lying in the woods. I want to give it back to its owner. Can you help me?" The bear nodded and said, "Of course, I will help you!" Together, they walked deeper into the woods. Soon, they found a small house with an old man sitting outside. The old man saw the spear and said, "That's my spear! I lost it while hunting. Thank you for finding it!" Tom gave the spear to the old man, and he and the bear went back home, happy to have helped a friend.

Once upon a time there was a little girl. She had beautiful eyes and wore a pretty dress. She was excited to go to school for the first time. The little girl looked out of the window and saw a big bus. It seemed very colourful and she thought it looked quite pretty. She said to her mum, 'Mummy, what’s that big bus? I want to go on it!' Her mum smiled and explained, ‘That’s a school bus. All the children go to school on it.’ The little girl asked, ‘Can I go on it too?’ Her mum replied, ‘Yes, you can, as long as you’re always kind and respectful to the people around you. That way you’ll make lots of friends. Then you’ll have lots of fun on the bus!’ The little girl was very excited and smiled at her mum. She was ready to go on the bus and make new friends. The moral of the story is that being kind to others will help you make friends and have fun!
