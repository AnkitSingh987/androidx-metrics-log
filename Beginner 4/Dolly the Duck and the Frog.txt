Category: Beginner
Title: Dolly the Duck and the Frog

Once upon a time, there was a duck named Dolly. Dolly was different from the other ducks. She had a big red spot on her head. All the other ducks had no spots. One day, Dolly was sad because she felt left out. Dolly met a kind frog named Freddy. Freddy said, "Dolly, don't be sad. I will lead you to a place where you will feel happy." Dolly and Freddy went on a long walk. They saw many animals, but no one had a red spot like Dolly. Finally, they reached a big pond. Dolly saw many ducks with red spots like her. She was so happy! One duck said, "Hello, Dolly! We are the Red Spot Ducks. You are not different here. You are just like us!" Dolly played with her new friends all day long. The moral of the story is that being different is not bad. Sometimes, it just takes time to find the place where you belong. And when you do, you will be very happy.

Sam was a bald boy who liked to play with his toys. He had many cars, trains, and planes that he could zoom around his room. He also had a big key that he found in his dad's drawer. He liked to pretend that the key could open any door. One day, he saw a new girl in his class. Her name was Mia and she had long curly hair. She looked sad and lonely, so Sam decided to introduce himself. He walked up to her and said, "Hi, I'm Sam. Do you want to play with me?" Mia smiled and nodded. She said, "Hi, I'm Mia. I'm new here. I don't have any friends yet." Sam said, "Don't worry, I'll be your friend. Come, I'll show you my toys. I have a lot of them. Look, this is my key. It can open any door." Mia was curious and followed Sam to his room. She saw his cars, trains, and planes, and said, "Wow, you have so many toys. They look fun. Can I play with them too?" Sam said, "Sure, you can play with them. You can choose any one you like. We can share them." Mia and Sam played together for a long time. They zoomed the cars, raced the trains, and flew the planes. They also tried to open different doors with the key, but none of them worked. They laughed and had fun. Sam and Mia became good friends. They learned to share, to be kind, and to have fun. They also learned that the key to friendship is not a thing, but a feeling. The feeling of being happy and caring for each other.

Mama and daddy were very busy. They wanted to paint the room for their little one on Sunday. Little one was so anxious to help that week. On Sunday, mama and daddy had a surprise for her. They went to the store and bought paint. Mama smiled and said, "Today we will paint the room together". Little one was so happy that she squealed with delight. They got to work right away. Little one was excited to help, but she made a mess with the paint that mama and daddy had to clean up. Little one got so anxious that she started to cry. Tears rolled down her cheeks, and mama comforted her. "It's ok, we can do it again tomorrow," she said. The next day, little one was more careful when she painted. Mama and daddy stood by and watched her proudly. They finally got to finish the room and it looked wonderful! Little one couldn't wait to show her friends.

Sara likes to drive her toy car. She has a red car with four wheels and a horn. She drives it around the house and makes vroom-vroom noises. She likes to pretend she is going to the park or the store or the zoo. One day, she sees a pretty flower on the side of the road. It is yellow and has many petals. She stops her car and gets out. She picks the flower and smells it. It smells sweet and nice. She puts it in her hair and smiles. But then, she hears a loud bark. It is a big dog that lives next door. The dog does not like Sara or her car. He runs towards her and growls. He wants to bite her and her car. Sara is scared and cries. She runs back to her car and gets in. She drives away as fast as she can. She honks her horn and yells, "Go away, bad dog! Leave me alone!" She drives to her house and parks her car. She runs inside and hugs her mom. Her mom sees the flower in her hair and the tears in her eyes. She asks, "What happened, Sara?" Sara tells her about the dog and the flower. Her mom says, "I'm sorry, Sara. That dog is mean and rude. But you are brave and smart. And you have a pretty flower. I love you, Sara." She kisses her and gives her a cookie. Sara feels better and smiles. She loves her mom and her car.
