Category: Beginner
Title: The Mom and Daughter and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a mom and a daughter. They were going on a trip. The mom told the daughter to pack her bag. The daughter was happy and packed her toys and clothes. They went to a high hill to have a picnic. The daughter played with her toys and the mom watched her. The sky was blue and the sun was warm. They ate their food and had a lot of fun. But then, the sky got dark and it started to rain. The mom and the daughter tried to pack their things fast. The wind blew their things away. They ran to their car, but the car was stuck in the mud. They were sad and wet. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a slow turtle named Tim. Tim loved to joke with his friends in the forest. One day, Tim got some mail. He was very happy. The mail was from his best friend, a rabbit named Ray. "Hi Tim!" said the mail. "Do you want to play today? I will wait for you near the big tree." Tim wanted to play with Ray, so he went to the big tree. It took a long time because he was slow. When he got there, Ray was waiting for him. They played hide and seek, and Tim joked with Ray. "You are so slow, Tim!" Ray laughed. "But I love playing with you!" Tim and Ray played all day. They had lots of fun. When the sun went down, they said goodbye. Tim went back to his home, feeling happy. He loved his friend Ray and their fun days together.

Danny was having a great day. He was running and jumping in the bright sunshine. It was so much fun! Suddenly, it got dark and Danny started to feel scared. He started to cry. The dark was so big and Danny was all alone. He decided to keep jumping until he found his mum. He jumped over rocks and he jumped higher and higher. But then he got clumsy and tripped over a big rock. Danny landed in a big, dark hole. He tried to jump out, but he just kept sliding back down. The dark hole was very deep and Danny couldn't escape. Danny was so scared and he just kept on crying and crying. Danny didn't come out of the deep, dark hole. Mum never found him. Danny spent the rest of his life in the dark.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a boy named Tim. Tim had a blue notebook that he loved. One day, the notebook was gone. Tim was very sad and started to search for it. Tim asked his friend, Sam. "Did you see my notebook?" Sam said, "No, but let's go look for it together." They went to the park, the store, and the school, but the notebook was still unknown. As they walked home, a big wind came and blew a paper to Tim's foot. It was from his notebook! Tim and Sam followed the papers, hoping to find the notebook. But when they found it, the notebook was all wet and torn. Tim cried, and Sam tried to make him feel better. But the notebook was gone forever.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big, red ball on the ground. Tim felt very proud as he kicked the ball. He saw a little girl named Sue sitting on a bench with a sad face. She started to cry. Tim wanted to help Sue, so he gave her the red ball. Sue stopped crying and her face had a big smile. Tim felt happy too. They played with the red ball and had lots of fun. But then, a dog came running fast and took the red ball away. Tim and Sue were sad, but the dog came back with a blue ball. They were surprised and happy. They played with the blue ball and had even more fun. Now, Tim, Sue, and the dog were all friends with big smiles.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Ella. She had a lovely garden, where she liked to play. One day, Ella's mother offered her a spade. It was a lovely, soft spade with a pink handle. Ella was so happy. Ella took her spade outside into the garden. She planted some soft flowers and dug up some of the soil. She was so delighted with her new spade. "I love my spade," she said. "Thank you, Mummy, for offering this to me." Mummy smiled and said, "You're welcome, Ella. I'm glad you like it." And Ella played happily in the garden with her new soft spade.
