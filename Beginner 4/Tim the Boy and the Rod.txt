Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Rod

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy rod. He loved to play with it. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He took his rod with him. At the park, Tim met a big, fierce dog. The dog was very nice to Tim, and they played together. The dog liked to chase the rod when Tim threw it. They had a lot of fun. After playing, Tim and his mom went home. But Tim forget his rod at the park. He was very sad. The next day, they went back to the park. The fierce dog was there with the rod. Tim was so happy to see his rod again. From that day on, Tim and the dog became best friends.

One day, a jolly boy named Tim found a little boat. He wanted to sail it in the pond. Tim asked his mom if he could go to the pond. His mom said, "Yes, but promise to be back before dark." Tim went to the pond with his boat. He put the boat in the water and watched it sail. A girl named Sue saw Tim and his boat. She said, "Wow, your boat is so nice! Can I sail it too?" Tim said, "Yes, you can!" Tim and Sue had fun sailing the boat. They laughed and played all day. They kept their promise and went home before dark. Tim's mom was happy to see them. They all had a jolly time.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Lily. She was three years old and loved to explore the world. One day, Lily was in the city centre when she wanted to get back home. She got to the corner, but she didn't know how to continue. All of a sudden, she spotted a big yellow taxi with a helpful driver inside. The driver rolled down his window and asked, "Where are you going, little one?" Lily told him where she needed to go and the driver said, "No problem. I can support you." The driver was organized and soon, the taxi was on its way to Lily's house. Lily was so excited and enjoyed the ride. She finally arrived home safe and sound. She waved goodbye to the taxi driver and thanked him for his help. The driver smiled and drove away. Lily knew he was a helpful person and was always happy to support others.

Once upon a time, there was a new little girl named Amy. Amy had a big hand. With her big hand, she liked to help her mommy and daddy. One day, Amy's mommy was making food. Amy wanted to help. She used her big hand to supply the food to her mommy. Her mommy was happy and said, "Thank you, Amy!" Amy felt good helping her mommy. She knew her big hand was special. From that day on, Amy always used her big hand to supply help to others. And everyone loved her for it.

One day, Tom was very nervous. He had new shoes with a zip. Tom wanted to show his friends his new shoes at the park. At the park, Tom saw his friend, Sue. "Look at my new shoes!" Tom said. Sue looked at the shoes and liked them too. They played and had fun. Then, a dog came running to them. The dog looked at Tom's shoe and bit the zip. The zip broke! Tom was sad, but then the dog's owner came. "I am sorry," the owner said. "I will fix your shoe zip." The owner fixed the zip and Tom was happy again. They all played together and had a great day.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the bookshelf to find a book. He saw a big book that he wanted to read. Tim took the book and sat down to look at it. As he read, he began to yawn because he was tired. Tim's mom saw him yawn and said, "Are you sleepy, Tim?" Tim nodded his head. "Yes, Mom, I am sleepy." His mom was compassionate and said, "Let's read the book together before bedtime." As they read the book, something unexpected happened. The book started to talk! The book said, "Hello, Tim and Mom! I am a magic book! I can tell you stories that no one else knows!" Tim and his mom were very surprised. They listened to the magic book and had the best bedtime stories ever.
