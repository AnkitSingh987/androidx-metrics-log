Category: Beginner
Title: The Gentle Bird and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a gentle bird who loved to sing. Every day, the bird would sing outside a big gray jail. The people in the jail would listen and feel happy. One day, the bird met a big cat. The cat was nice and wanted to hear the bird sing too. So, the bird sang a pretty song for the cat. The cat liked the song and they became friends. But one day, the cat got very hungry. The cat did not want to be friends anymore. The cat ate the gentle bird. The people in the jail were sad because they could not hear the bird sing anymore. And that was the end of the gentle bird who loved to sing.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends called Kay and Tim. They were both very excited to go to the park. Tim couldn’t wait to get there and sat down in the grass. He lay down and spread his arms wide. Kay smiled and pulled out a beautiful red comb from her pocket. Kay asked her friend, “Do you want me to comb your hair? It will make it look so fine!” “Yes please!” Tim said. Kay gently ran the comb through Tim’s hair and it felt so nice. When Kay was finished, Tim smiled proudly. His hair was so shiny and it looked really fine. They both laughed and jumped up. It was time to explore the park. They both ran off, full of energy and joy.

Lily liked to play with her mom's computer. She liked to see the pictures and hear the sounds. But mom said she could only play when mom was with her. Lily wanted to play by herself. One day, mom was busy in the kitchen. Lily saw the computer on the table. She climbed on a chair and reached for it. She was very careful. She did not want to drop it. She managed to get the computer and put it on her lap. She was very happy. She turned on the computer and saw a game. It was a game with animals. Lily loved animals. She clicked on the mouse and saw a cat. She clicked again and saw a dog. She clicked more and more and saw many animals. She laughed and clapped her hands. But then, something went wrong. The computer made a loud noise and the screen went black. Lily was scared. She did not know what to do. She heard mom coming. She tried to hide the computer under a pillow. Mom came into the room and saw Lily on the chair. She saw the pillow on her lap. She knew something was wrong. She asked Lily what she was doing. Lily did not answer. She looked down. Mom lifted the pillow and saw the computer. She was angry. She asked Lily why she took the computer. Lily said she wanted to play. She said she was sorry. She said she did not mean to break it. Mom sighed. She took the computer and checked it. She was glad it was not broken. She told Lily it was not a toy. She told Lily she could only play with it when mom said yes. She told Lily she had to be careful with it. She told Lily she loved her. Lily felt bad. She said she was sorry again. She said she loved mom too. She hugged mom and said she would not do it again. Mom hugged her back and said it was okay. She said they could play together later. Lily smiled and said yes. She was glad mom was not mad. She was glad mom was nice. She was glad mom was her mom.

Once upon a time, in a small, quiet town, there lived a thief. The thief liked to pick things that did not belong to him. He was very quiet, so no one could hear him when he took things. One day, the thief saw a big, red ball in a yard. He wanted the ball for himself. So, he waited until it was dark and very quiet. Then, he went to the yard and picked up the ball. As the thief was leaving, a little girl saw him. She said, "Hey, that's my ball!" The thief was scared and said, "I'm sorry, I did not know it was yours." The little girl was kind and said, "It's okay, but you should not take things that are not yours." The thief learned his lesson and never picked things that did not belong to him again. From that day on, the town was happier, and the thief became a good friend to everyone.
