Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Cat and the Tree

One day, a small cat named Tom went outside. It was a hot day. Tom saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. He thought, "This will be a fun test!" As Tom climbed the tree, he met a bird. The bird said, "Hi! My name is Bob. Be careful, the top of the tree is very hot." Tom did not listen. His ear heard the bird, but he still wanted to go up. At the top of the tree, Tom felt the hot sun. He said, "Oh, Bob was right. It is too hot up here!" Tom climbed back down to find Bob. "Thank you, Bob," he said. "You were right. I will listen to you next time." They became good friends and played together in the shade.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play and pretend. She had a big box of dress-up clothes. In the box, there was a belt that she liked very much. One day, Lily put on the belt and pretended to be a superhero. She ran around the house, saving her toys from danger. She felt very brave and strong with her belt on. After a fun day of playing, Lily took off her belt and put it back in the box. She was tired, but happy. She knew that tomorrow she could pretend to be anything she wanted with her special belt. And with that, Lily went to bed, dreaming of her next adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy dog named Spot. Spot loved to go outside and play. One sunny day, Spot went to the park with his best friend, a little girl named Sue. Sue and Spot liked to run and jump and have fun. In the park, they found a big wet puddle. Spot jumped in the wet puddle and splashed water everywhere. Sue laughed and clapped her hands. She thought it was so funny! Spot was happy to make Sue laugh. But then, something unexpected happened. As Spot jumped in the wet puddle again, he found a shiny ball under the water. Spot picked up the ball with his mouth and showed it to Sue. Sue was so surprised! They had found a new toy to play with. From that day on, Spot and Sue played with the shiny ball every time they went to the park, and they had even more fun together.

Tim and Sue like to play in the park. They have a lot of fun on the swings, the slide and the seesaw. But one day, a big boy comes to the park. He is very bossy and mean. He tells Tim and Sue to get off the swings, the slide and the seesaw. He says they are his and he will hit them if they do not listen. Tim and Sue are scared of the big boy. They do not want to fight. They try to find another place to play. But the big boy follows them everywhere. He laughs at them and calls them names. He says they are babies and they have to do what he says. He is a big threat to them. Tim and Sue do not know what to do. They look for help, but they do not see anyone. They feel very sad and alone. The big boy comes closer and closer. He tells them to kneel down and say sorry. He says he will kick them if they do not. Tim and Sue kneel down and say sorry. They hope he will leave them alone. But he does not. He kicks them hard and runs away. Tim and Sue cry and cry. They have no fun in the park. They have a bad ending.

One day, a little girl named Sue had an adorable red bow. She loved her bow so much. She wore it every day. It made her very happy. One day, Sue went to play with her friends. They played hide and seek in the park. While playing, Sue lost her adorable red bow. She was very sad. She looked everywhere but could not find it. Just when Sue thought her bow was gone forever, her dog, Max, came running to her. Max had something in his mouth. It was the adorable red bow! Sue was so happy. She did not know that Max was the one who took it. Max wanted to play too. Now, Sue and Max played together with the bow, and they were both very happy.
