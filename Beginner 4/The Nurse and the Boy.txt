Category: Beginner
Title: The Nurse and the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a nice nurse. She had a gray dress and a kind smile. She always wanted to lend a helping hand. One day, a little boy needed help. He was only three years old and he was hungry. The nurse kindly lent him a snack. She watched him enjoy his snack and smiled. Next, the nurse lent the boy a toy. He was so excited to play with it and thanked the nurse. He was so happy as he went off to play with his new toy. The nurse was happy too. She was glad that she could lend a helping hand to the little boy. The nurse knew that she made him smile and it made her feel good inside.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had an ancient vest that his grandpa gave him. Tim loved his vest very much. It was blue and had big buttons. He wore it every day. One day, Tim's mom told him to be careful with his vest. She said, "Promise me you won't lose it." Tim promised his mom that he would be careful. He did not want to lose his special vest. Tim went to play outside with his friends. They played in the park and had lots of fun. But when it was time to go home, Tim noticed his vest was gone. He looked everywhere, but he could not find it. Tim was very sad because he broke his promise to his mom. He went home and told her the bad news. His mom was sad too, and they never found the ancient vest again.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play in the park. There is a big slide in the park. Lily and Ben want to go on the slide. "Me first!" Lily says. She runs to the slide and climbs up the ladder. She sits on the top and smiles. "Whee!" she says as she slides down. "Me too!" Ben says. He follows Lily and climbs up the ladder. He sits on the top and waves. "Whee!" he says as he slides down. They do this again and again. They have fun on the slide. They laugh and shout. After a while, they feel tired. They go to a big oak tree. The oak tree has green leaves and brown bark. It gives shade and cool air. Lily and Ben sit under the oak tree. They drink some water and eat some cookies. They rest and talk. "That was fun!" Lily says. "I like the slide." "Me too!" Ben says. "I like the slide." They hug each other. They are happy. They love the park. They love the slide. They love the oak tree. They love each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to play with her toy train. The train had a long, long rail. Amy was a bit crazy about her train. Every day, she would play with it for hours and hours. One day, Amy's toy train was missing. She looked and looked, but she could not find it. She was very sad. She missed her train so much. She asked her mom and dad for help, but they did not know where the train was. Then, something unexpected happened. Amy's little brother, Tim, came into her room. He had the toy train! Tim had taken it to play with, but he did not know it was Amy's. They both laughed and played with the train together. From that day on, they shared the toy train and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was little boy who was jealous of other children. He wanted to have a wheel of his own that he could ride. One day, he stumbled across a broken wheel at the park. He bent down and picked it up, then was very excited to play with it! He rolled the wheel around the park, pretending it was a car or a space-ship. He used his imagination to fly it through the sky and make it go fast. Everywhere he went, the other kids were jealous of his wheel. The little boy kept rolling and playing until he was too tired to go any farther. He was sad to leave the wheel behind, so he proudly dragged it back home. He kept the wheel with him all the time after that, taking it everywhere he went. His friends were still jealous, but he was happy he found something so special.
