Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Sleepy Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy cat named Tom. He loved to sleep all day and play all night. One day, Tom found a big orange ball in the yard. He liked the orange ball very much and wanted to play with it. Tom's friend, a little girl named Sue, saw him with the orange ball. She said, "Tom, can you print my name on the ball?" Tom didn't know how to print, but he wanted to help his friend Sue. Tom tried to print Sue's name on the orange ball, but he made a mess. Then, a magic bird came and said, "Don't worry, Tom. I can help you print Sue's name on the ball." The magic bird printed Sue's name on the orange ball, and it looked very nice. Tom and Sue were very happy and played with the orange ball all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy cat named Tom. He lived in a small house with his friend, a happy dog named Max. One day, Max said he would go to the store to get some food. He made a promise to Tom that he would be back soon. While Max was at the store, Tom felt lonely and sad. He missed his friend and wished he would come back soon. He looked out the window and saw a big, dark cloud in the sky. Tom knew that rain was coming and Max did not like to get wet. Just as the rain started to fall, Max came running back from the store. He was wet and cold, but he had a big smile on his face. He had kept his promise to Tom and brought back lots of yummy food. Tom felt happy again and they both enjoyed a big meal together. From that day on, Tom was not so grumpy anymore, because he knew that Max would always keep his promises.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Paul. He was three years old and he loved flowers. One day, he went to the park and saw lots of beautiful flowers. They were pink, yellow and purple! He wanted to pick one of them but his mom said, "No, Paul. You have to stop". Paul was angry and he started to cry. His mom looked at him and told him, "Paul, why do you want to pick the flower?" Paul said, "I just want to take it with me". His mom smiled and said, "Flowers need to stay in the park so other people can enjoy them. Flowers are meant to be shared with everyone, not just you. That's why we need to stop picking them." Paul understood his mom and stopped crying. He smiled as he watched the flowers. From that day on, Paul always remembered the lesson he learned. He also understood the importance of sharing and respecting nature.

One day, a little brown dog found a lamp. The lamp was shiny and pretty. The dog picked up the lamp in its mouth and ran to show it to its friend, the cat. The cat looked at the lamp and said, "Wow, what a nice lamp! Let's pray and see what happens." So they closed their eyes and prayed together. As they prayed, the lamp started to shake. Then, a big frog came out of the lamp! The frog said, "Thank you for praying. I am a magic frog, and I will give you one wish." The dog and the cat were very surprised and happy. They wished to be best friends forever, and the magic frog made their wish come true.

One day, a little dog named Spot was walking with his friend Tim. Spot had a hurt heel, so he could not walk fast. Tim tried to help Spot, but Spot felt frustrated. He wanted to walk on his own. "Tim, I want to walk alone," said Spot. Tim felt sad, but he let Spot walk alone. As they walked, they saw a big box. Spot and Tim did not know what was inside. Spot tried to open the box, but it was too high for him. Tim helped Spot and they opened the box together. Inside the box, they found a big, soft shoe. Spot put his hurt heel in the shoe and he felt better. "Thank you, Tim!" said Spot. They both smiled and walked home together, happy that they had found the shoe.
