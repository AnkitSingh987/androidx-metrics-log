Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Cat

One day, a boy named Tim was eager to go to school. He had a plan to play with his friends. He put on his shoes, picked up his bag, and went outside. On his way, he saw a cat. The cat said, "Hello, Tim. Can I come to school with you?" Tim was very surprised. He never heard a cat talk before. "Okay, cat. Let's go to school together," he said. They walked to school and met Tim's friends. They all played together and had lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The cat turned into a big, friendly dog! Tim and his friends were amazed. They all laughed and played with the dog. The dog said, "Thank you for letting me come to school with you, Tim." And from that day on, Tim and the dog were the best of friends.

One day, a soft rain fell from the sky. A cat and a dog were outside, and they did not like the rain. The cat said, "I don't like rain. It makes me wet." The dog said, "I don't like rain too. Let's find a dry place." They saw a big tree and ran to it. Under the tree, they found a bird. The bird was singing a song. The cat and the dog did not understand the bird's language. The cat said, "What is the bird saying?" The dog said, "I don't know. Let's ask the bird." The cat and the dog asked the bird, "What are you saying?" The bird said, "I am singing a song to make the rain stop." The cat and the dog were happy. They all sang the song together. Soon, the rain stopped, and they all played in the sun.

One day, a fat cat named Tom was playing outside. The sky was getting dark, and soon, rain started to fall. Tom did not like getting wet, so he ran to find a dry place. Tom saw a big tree and sat under it. Another cat named Sue was already there. She was also hiding from the rain. Tom said, "Hi, Sue! The rain is wet. Can we replace it with sun?" Sue said, "I wish we could, Tom! But we have to wait for the rain to stop." They waited under the tree, talking and playing. The rain stopped, and the sun came out. Tom and Sue were happy. They ran and played in the sun, and the rain was replaced with a beautiful day.

One day, a thoughtful dog named Max went to a big field to play. He saw a small cat named Lily sitting on the grass. Max wanted to play with Lily, so he asked, "Do you want to play with me?" Lily looked at Max and said, "No, I refuse to play with you. I don't like dogs." Max was sad, but he didn't give up. He thought of a fun game that Lily might like. "What if we play hide and seek?" Max asked. Lily thought about it and said, "Okay, I will play hide and seek with you." They played the game in the big field and had lots of fun. Max and Lily became good friends, even though Lily didn't like dogs at first.

Once upon a time there was an ugly duck. It was different than the other ducks and felt bad about it. It never went in the water to swim or surf like the other ducks. Instead, it stayed away and was sad. One day, a kind creature saw the ugly duck. It saw how sad the duck was, so it decided to help. With a wave of its wand, it restored the duck's feathers so that they shone brightly. Now the duck was beautiful! The duck was so happy, it hopped right into the pond. For the first time, the duck learned to surf. It was so much fun and the ugly duck was filled with joy. From that day on, the duck always surfed. It was filled with happiness, no longer feeling ugly or different. The kind creature had restored the duck's confidence and it forever stayed happy.

Once there was a small boy named Joe. He was 3 years old and loved to play. One day, Joe saw a trumpet in the corner of his room and he was really curious about it. He asked his mom what it was and she explained that it was a trumpet. She then suggested he play it. Joe was really excited and he immediately picked up the trumpet and started blowing on it. But it was too loud! His mom asked him to stop because it was too loud for her ears. Joe felt sad, he didn't understand why he couldn't play the trumpet. Just then, Joe's grandpa came into the room. Joe's mother explained the situation and his grandpa had an idea. He suggested Joe play the trumpet with special soft ears that he had made. Grandpa had made these special soft ears because he was a bit deaf. This way Joe could play the trumpet as loud as he wanted and grandpa wouldn't be able to hear it. Joe was so happy and he thanked grandpa. He picked up the trumpet and tried the special soft ears. Joe could now play the trumpet as loud as he wanted and he was very happy.
