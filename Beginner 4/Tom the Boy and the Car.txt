Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Car

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom had a toy car that he loved very much. It was a harmless toy car that he played with every day. Tom's mom told him to be careful with his toy car so he would not lose it. One day, Tom went to the park with his mom and took his toy car with him. They played and had fun all day. But when it was time to go home, Tom could not find his toy car. He was very sad because he lost his toy car. Tom and his mom looked everywhere, but they could not find it. A week went by, and Tom was still sad about his lost toy car. One day, Tom's mom took him to the park again. When they got there, Tom saw a little girl playing with a toy car. It was his toy car! The little girl found it in the park and was taking good care of it. Tom was very happy to get his toy car back, and he learned to be more careful with his toys.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to look at her big book with many pages. One day, Lucy could not find her book. She felt anxious and started to think. "Where could my book be?" she asked herself. Lucy looked under her bed, behind the chair, and in the toy box. But her book was not there. She felt sad and asked her mom for help. "Mom, I can't find my book with the pages," she said. Her mom thought for a moment and said, "Let's look in the living room." Together, they looked around the living room. Lucy saw her book on the shelf! She felt happy and said, "Mom, I found it!" She took the book down and opened it to her favorite page. Lucy and her mom sat down and read the story together.

Once upon a time, there was a little ant. The ant had a friend, a fake bug. The fake bug was made of wax. The ant and the fake bug liked to play together. They had a lot of fun in the sun. One very hot day, the ant and the fake bug went to play outside. They played near a big tree. The sun was very strong that day. The ant felt hot, but he was happy. The fake bug started to feel soft. Suddenly, the fake bug began to melt. The ant was sad and worried. He did not want to lose his friend. So, the ant quickly carried the fake bug to a cool, shady place under the tree. In the shade, the fake bug stopped melting. The ant was very happy. He knew that he saved his friend. From that day on, the ant and the fake bug always played in the shade when it was hot outside. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty bird named Bella. She lived in a big tree. One day, Bella wanted to go on a journey. She wanted to see new things and meet new friends. Bella met a little bug named Ben. Ben said, "I want to go on a journey too, but I am too small. I don't believe I can do it." Bella told him, "You can do it! I believe in you!" They went on the journey together. They saw a big hill. Bella and Ben went up the hill. At the top, they saw a big surprise. There was a big party with all their friends. They did not know their friends were there. Bella and Ben were so happy. They danced and played with their friends. The journey was fun and they learned to believe in themselves.

One day, a dependable dog named Max went to the park. Max saw a small patient cat sitting under a tree. The cat was waiting for her friend, a tiny bird. Max wanted to play with them too. Max had a magic toy that could shrink things. He thought if he could shrink, he could play with the cat and bird better. So, Max used the toy on himself. In a second, Max became very small. Now, Max, the cat, and the bird could play together. They had a lot of fun. They were happy that Max was a dependable friend. After playing, Max used the toy to grow big again. They all went home, excited to play again the next day.
