Category: Beginner
Title: The Dog and the Cat

One day, a big cat and a little dog were best friends. They were very loyal to each other. They played together, ate together, and slept together. The big cat was sometimes a bit mean to the little dog, but the little dog did not mind. One day, the big cat found a magic toy. The toy could make things shrink! The big cat thought it would be fun to play a trick on the little dog. So, the big cat used the magic toy to make the little dog shrink. The little dog was now very, very small. The little dog was sad and scared. But, he knew his big cat friend would help him. The big cat felt bad for what he did. He wanted to fix it. They looked and looked for a way to make the little dog big again. They worked together and never gave up. At last, they found a magic flower that made the little dog big again. They were so happy! This was a big success. The big cat and the little dog learned to be kind to each other and stayed loyal friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a cold day. A boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, were playing outside. They saw a big box on the ground. Tim wanted to lift the box, but it was too heavy. He asked Spot to help him, but Spot was too small. Tim went to his friend, Sue, and said, "Sue, can you help me lift the box?" Sue said, "Yes, I can help you, Tim!" They both went to the box and tried to lift it. With Sue's help, they were able to lift the box. Inside the box, they found a naughty cat. The cat jumped out and ran away. Tim, Sue, and Spot laughed and chased the cat. They played together all day, even though it was cold. They had a lot of fun and were happy they lifted the box.

Once upon a time there was a happy puppy named Max. One day, Max wanted to go outside and play in the sunshine. He poked his head out of the door and saw something winking at him in the yard. Max was fascinated! What could it be? Max barked and poked at the thing that was winking. Suddenly, the object jumped high in the air and flew away. Max was so amazed that he didn't even notice the butterfly. "There it goes!", said Max. The butterfly flew up to the sky and winked one last time at Max. Max felt so happy as he waved goodbye to his new flying friend. Max ran back inside and told his family about his magical experience. This made them all very happy. Max was so glad that he had taken the time to explore the garden and make a new friend.

Lily liked to read books. She had many books on her bookshelf. She liked to look at the pictures and learn new things. Sometimes she read with her mom or dad, and sometimes she read by herself. One day, Lily wanted to read a book about animals. She went to her bookshelf and looked for the book. But she could not find it. She looked on the floor, under the bed, in the closet, but the book was not there. She felt upset. Where was her book? She asked her mom if she knew where the book was. Her mom said she did not know, but maybe her dad did. She said her dad was in the garage, loading some boxes into the car. Lily ran to the garage to find her dad. She saw her dad putting boxes into the car. She asked him if he had seen her book. He said he had not, but maybe it was in one of the boxes. He said he was loading the boxes because they were going to visit grandma and grandpa. He said they could take some books with them. Lily helped her dad look for her book in the boxes. She opened one box and saw some clothes. She opened another box and saw some toys. She opened another box and saw some books. She smiled. She found her book! She took her book out of the box and hugged it. She thanked her dad for helping her. He said he was happy she found her book. He said he was sorry he had packed it by mistake. He said he was proud of her for reading so well. Lily felt happy. She had her book and her dad. She said she was excited to go to grandma and grandpa's house. She said she wanted to read her book to them. She said she loved her dad. He said he loved her too. They hugged and loaded the box back into the car.
