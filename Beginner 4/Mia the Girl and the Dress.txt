Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Dress

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a white dress and a pretty jewel. She loved her dress and jewel very much. One day, while playing outside, her dress got wet and dirty. She was very sad. Mia's mom saw her crying and asked, "Why are you sad, Mia?" "My dress is wet and dirty," Mia said with tears in her eyes. "Don't worry, we can clean and dry it," her mom said. Mia's mom washed the dress and hung it up to dry. Mia watched the dress and waited. She was worried that it would not be white anymore. After some time, the dress was dry and still white. Mia was so happy! She put on her white dress and her pretty jewel. She went outside to play, making sure to stay clean and dry. Mia learned that sometimes things can get dirty, but with help, they can be clean again.

Once upon a time, there was a big race. Many insects came to race. There was a fast bug and a slow bug. The fast bug was jealous of the slow bug. The fast bug thought he would win the race. The race began. All insects ran very fast. The fast bug was in front. The slow bug was behind. They all wanted to win the race. The slow bug did not give up. Then, something unexpected happened. The fast bug fell down. The slow bug kept going. The slow bug won the race. The fast bug was sad. The slow bug showed that slow and steady wins the race.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had an orange shirt that he loved to wear. He would wear it every day, even when it was dirty. One day, his mom told him, "Tim, you need to wear a clean shirt. Let's go upstairs and find one." Tim and his mom went upstairs to his room. They looked in his closet and found many shirts, but no orange ones. Tim felt sad. He wanted to wear his favorite orange shirt, but it was dirty. His mom said, "Don't worry, Tim. We will wash your orange shirt, and you can wear it tomorrow." The next day, Tim's mom gave him his clean orange shirt. He was so happy! He wore it all day long, and everyone told him how nice he looked. Tim learned that it's important to wear clean clothes, even if it means waiting for his favorite shirt to be washed.

One day, a little squirrel was playing in the park. The squirrel was very lucky because it was a sunny day. The squirrel liked to jump and run in the park. The squirrel saw a big nut on the ground. It wanted to eat the nut. But a bird came and said, "That's my nut. You can't have it." The squirrel did not want to fight with the bird, so it said, "Okay, you can have the nut." The bird was not nice. It did not want to share the nut. The squirrel asked, "Can I have some of the nut, please?" The bird said, "No, I refuse to share with you." The squirrel was sad but did not give up. It looked for more nuts and found a bigger nut. The squirrel was happy and lucky again. It ate the big nut and played in the park all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy car that he loved a lot. It was a very reliable car and would always go fast when Tim pushed it. One day, Tim's mom told him they needed to go to the store. At the store, Tim saw a new toy car. It was shiny and had a sharp edge. Tim wanted the new car, but his mom said the price was too high. When they got home, Tim played with his old car. Suddenly, the new toy car appeared on the floor! Tim was so happy and didn't know how it got there. His old car had turned into the new car! The reliable toy car had become the sharp, shiny car he wanted, and they didn't have to pay the high price.
