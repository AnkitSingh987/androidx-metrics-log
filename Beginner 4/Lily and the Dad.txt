Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Dad

Lily liked to write poems. She wrote them on paper with her crayons. She wrote about her toys, her cat, and her mom. She was very proud of her poems. One day, she wanted to show her poems to her dad. He was in the kitchen, making dinner. She ran to him with her paper and said, "Dad, dad, look at my poems!" Her dad smiled and said, "Wow, Lily, these are very nice poems. You are very smart and creative. Can I read them?" Lily nodded and gave him the paper. Her dad took it and started to read. He said, "This one is about your teddy bear. It says, 'I love my teddy bear. He is soft and brown. He hugs me when I sleep. He is my best friend.' That is very sweet, Lily." Lily beamed and said, "Thank you, dad. Do you like the next one?" Her dad said, "Yes, let me see. This one is about your cat. It says, 'My cat is funny. He likes to play with yarn. He chases mice and birds. He purrs when I pet him.' That is very cute, Lily." Lily clapped and said, "Thank you, dad. Do you like the last one?" Her dad said, "Yes, of course. This one is about your mom. It says, 'My mom is beautiful. She has long hair and blue eyes. She sings to me and reads to me. She loves me very much.' That is very lovely, Lily." Lily hugged her dad and said, "Thank you, dad. I love you very much, too." Her dad hugged her back and said, "I love you, too, Lily. You are a very good poet. Can I keep your poems?" Lily said, "Yes, dad. You can keep them. But be careful. Don't burn them." Her dad said, "Don't worry, Lily. I won't burn them. I will put them on the fridge, so everyone can see them." Lily said, "Okay, dad. That is a good idea. But don't put them near the stove. The stove is hot. It can burn things." Her dad said, "Okay, Lily. I won't put them near the stove. I will put them on the other side of the fridge. That is safe." Lily said, "Okay, dad. Thank you for reading my poems. You are the best dad ever." Her dad said, "You are welcome, Lily. And you are the best poet ever." They smiled and kissed. Then they went to the table to eat dinner. Lily was happy. Her dad was happy. They both loved poems. And they both loved each other.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She liked to design dresses for her dolls. One day, she made a dress with a zip. The zip went up and down, and Lily thought it was fun. Lily's friend, Tom, came over to play. Tom saw the dress with the zip and said, "Wow, that's a high zip! Can I try to zip it?" Lily said, "Yes, you can try." Tom zipped the dress up and down, and they both laughed. Then, something unexpected happened. When Tom zipped the dress all the way up, the doll turned into a real, tiny person! The person said, "Thank you for using the magic zip. Now, I can be your friend too!" Lily and Tom were very surprised, but happy to have a new friend. They played together all day long.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park with their dog, Spot. They liked to run and jump and throw a ball for Spot to catch. Spot was very fast and smart and loved to play with them. One day, they saw some ducks by the pond. The ducks were swimming and quacking and eating bread that someone had thrown for them. Lily and Ben wanted to see the ducks up close, so they walked slowly and quietly towards them. "Look, Ben, the ducks have a trail of babies behind them!" Lily whispered, pointing at the fluffy ducklings. "They are so cute and tiny!" "Can we touch them?" Ben asked, reaching out his hand. "Shh, no, we have to be careful. They might be scared of us," Lily said. But it was too late. Spot saw the ducks and ran towards them, barking loudly. The ducks were very alert and saw Spot coming. They quickly scattered in different directions, leaving the pond and flying away. The ducklings followed their mothers, but some of them got lost and confused. "Spot, no, bad dog!" Lily and Ben shouted, running after Spot. They tried to catch him and stop him from chasing the ducks, but he was too fast and excited. The ducks were gone, and so was the bread. Lily and Ben felt sad and sorry. They wished they had not scared the ducks away. They hoped the ducks and their babies would come back soon. They hugged Spot and told him to be more gentle and quiet next time. They decided to go home and have a snack. Maybe they could come back another day and see the ducks again.
