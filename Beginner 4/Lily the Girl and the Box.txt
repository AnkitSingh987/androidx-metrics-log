Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big box with a pretty wrap. Lily wanted to give the box to her friend, Sam. Lily walked to Sam's house. She knocked on the door. Sam opened the door and said, "Hi, Lily! What do you have?" Lily said, "I have a gift for you, Sam! I hope it will fit you." Sam was very happy. He took the box and opened the pretty wrap. Inside the box was a nice hat. Sam put the hat on his head. It was a perfect fit! Sam said, "Thank you, Lily! I love my new hat." Lily and Sam played outside all day. They were very happy. Sam wore his new hat, and it kept him warm. They had a lot of fun together.

---

One day, a girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a hidden door under a big tree. She opened the door and saw a quick bunny. The bunny said, "Hi! My name is Binky." Lily sighed. She was happy to find a new friend. They played together all day. They ran, jumped, and laughed. Binky was very fast, but Lily tried to keep up with him. As the sun went down, Lily said, "I have to go home now." Binky nodded and they hugged. Lily went back to her house, excited to tell her mom about her new friend, the quick bunny named Binky.

---

One day, a lively little boy named Tim went to the bus stop with his mom. They were going to the park to play. At the bus stop, Tim saw a big red bus coming. He was very excited. "Mom, can you explain how the bus works?" Tim asked. His mom smiled and said, "The bus takes us to different places. We get on, and it goes. When we reach the park, we get off." As they got on the bus, Tim saw a big box at the back. He wondered what was inside. When they reached the park, the bus driver opened the box. Out came a big, bouncy ball! Tim and his mom were very surprised. The bus driver said, "I found this ball, and I wanted to give it to a happy kid. Tim, you can have it!" Tim was so happy and played with the ball all day at the park. The big red bus made his day even more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He found an enormous box in his room. The box was very big and very pretty. Tim wanted to open the box, but it was locked. Tim's mom gave him a coin. She said, "Put the coin in the box and turn it. The box will open." Tim did what his mom said. He put the coin in the box and turned it. The box opened! Inside the enormous box, Tim found many toys. There were cars, dolls, and balls. Tim was very happy. He played with his new toys all day long. And from that day on, the enormous box was Tim's favorite place to keep his toys.

---

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy some food. Tim saw a big, thick piece of beef. He asked his mom, "Can we get this beef, please?" His mom said, "Yes, we can." On their way home, they saw a little girl crying by the road. Tim asked her, "Why do you cry?" The little girl said, "I lost my toy. I can't find it." Tim wanted to help, so he looked around with the girl. Then, something unexpected happened. Tim saw a dog with the girl's toy in its mouth! The dog was happy and wagged its tail. Tim gave the toy back to the girl. She stopped crying and said, "Thank you, Tim!" They all went home with big smiles on their faces.

Robbie was very tired. He had been playing all day and he was ready for a nap. He yawned as he watched his favorite movie. His eyes started to close just a few minutes later, but then he heard something outside. He opened his eyes and went to his window. It was raining! The street was wet and it was all because of the rain. Robbie was so excited. He loved playing in the rain! He quickly got ready and rushed outside. He jumped in the puddles, feeling the wetness on his feet. The rain was so cool and he felt very awake now. He looked up in the sky at the clouds, and he smiled. Then he yawned again. He was so happy and so tired! His favorite movie was still on, so he quickly rushed back inside for a cozy nap.
