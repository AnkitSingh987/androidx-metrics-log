Category: Beginner
Title: The Castle and the Cloud

Once there was a big castle. In the castle, there was a compassionate king. The king liked to move and play with his friends. One day, the king saw a big, dark cloud. The cloud was coming near the castle. The king did not like the big, dark cloud. He thought it would bring rain and stop him from playing outside. He told his friends to help him move the castle. They pushed and pushed, but the castle did not move. The big, dark cloud came closer. The king and his friends tried to move the castle again. They pushed harder, but the castle still did not move. The big, dark cloud was now very close. It started to rain. The king and his friends could not play outside anymore. They were sad because they could not move the castle and stop the rain.

Lily was sad. She missed her mom and dad. They went to a far place for work. They said they would come back soon, but Lily did not know when. She stayed with her grandma, who was nice, but not the same. One night, Lily could not sleep. She heard a loud noise outside. She was scared. She ran to her grandma's room and knocked on the door. "Grandma, grandma, can I sleep with you?" she asked. "Of course, my dear, come in," grandma said. She opened the door and saw Lily shivering. She wrapped her in a soft bathrobe and hugged her. "What's wrong, Lily?" "I heard a noise. It was like a big boom. I thought it was a monster," Lily said. "Oh, no, Lily, that was just thunder. It's a storm. There's no monster, don't worry," grandma said. She comforted Lily and stroked her hair. "Do you want to hear a story?" "Yes, please," Lily said. "Okay, let me tell you a story about a princess who was very rich. She had a big castle, a lot of dresses, and many toys. But she was not happy. She wanted to have a friend. One day, she met a boy who was very poor. He had a small hut, a few clothes, and no toys. But he was happy. He had a dog, a bird, and a flower. He shared them with the princess. They became friends. They played, laughed, and learned together. The princess realized that being rich was not important. She gave some of her things to the poor people. She was happy too. The end." "That was a nice story, grandma. Thank you," Lily said. "You're welcome, Lily. I love you," grandma said. "I love you too, grandma. Can I stay with you until mom and dad come back?" Lily asked. "Of course, Lily. You can stay with me as long as you want. Now, let's sleep. Good night," grandma said. "Good night, grandma," Lily said. She snuggled with grandma and the bathrobe. She felt warm and safe. She closed her eyes and fell asleep.

Lily and Sam are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a big slide, a swing, and a sandbox. They run to the slide and take turns to go down. Whee! Then they see a new boy. He has orange hair and a blue shirt. He looks sad. He sits on a bench and does not play. Lily and Sam want to be nice. They go to the new boy and say hello. "Hi, I am Lily. This is Sam. What is your name?" Lily asks. "I am Max. I am new here. I don't have any friends," Max says. "That's okay. We can be your friends. Do you want to play with us?" Sam says. Max smiles. He nods. He says yes. Lily and Sam are happy. They welcome Max to the park. They show him the slide, the swing, and the sandbox. They have fun together. They are friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to wash her stuff. She had many toys and dolls, and she was always very careful with them. One sunny day, Sue decided to wash her toys outside. She filled a big tub with water and soap. One by one, she carefully washed her toys and dolls. She was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened! A big wind came and blew her toys away! Sue was very sad. She looked everywhere for her toys, but they were gone. Just when she was about to give up, a big bird came and brought her toys back. The bird had seen them fly away and wanted to help. Sue was so happy and thanked the big bird. From that day on, Sue and the big bird became best friends, and they always played together.
