Category: Beginner
Title: The Toys of Tim the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big toy box full of fun toys. One day, Tim saw a new toy on the top shelf. It was a big, red ball. He wanted to play with it, but it was not easy to reach. Tim asked his mom, "Mom, can you help me get the ball?" His mom said, "Yes, but you must promise to put your toys away when you are done playing." Tim agreed and pulled a chair to stand on. With his mom's help, he pulled the ball down. Tim played with the ball all day. He kicked it, threw it, and bounced it. But soon, his stomach started to hurt. He had been playing too long without eating. Tim's mom said, "It's time to eat, and don't forget to put your toys away." After eating, Tim felt better. He remembered his promise and put all his toys back in the toy box. From that day on, Tim always put his toys away and took breaks to eat. He learned that taking care of himself made playtime more fun. The moral of the story is to always take care of yourself and keep your promises.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful toy car. It was red and shiny. The toy car had a battery inside. The battery made the car go fast. One day, a little boy named Tim found the toy car. He said, "Wow! I believe this car will go very fast!" Tim played with the car all day. He made it go up and down the hills in his yard. But then, something unexpected happened. The toy car stopped moving. Tim said, "Oh no! What's wrong with my car?" He looked and saw that the battery was gone. Tim was sad. Just then, a friendly squirrel came by with the battery in its mouth. The squirrel said, "Here is your battery, Tim! I thought it was a nut." Tim laughed and said, "Thank you, squirrel!" He put the battery back in the car and they played together happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy jazz band. The band had a drum, a trumpet, and a piano. Tim would play with them all day. One day, Tim wanted to add a new toy to his jazz band. He asked his mom for a saxophone. His mom said, "You can have it if you are a good boy." Tim was nervous because he wanted the saxophone so much. Tim tried to be a good boy, but he was too excited. He ran around the house and knocked over a big vase. It broke into many pieces. His mom was very sad and said, "No saxophone for you, Tim." Tim cried and his jazz band stayed the same.

Lily and Ben were playing with their toys in the living room. They had a lot of fun making noises and pretending to be animals. Lily had a doll that she liked to dress up and comb her hair. Ben had a truck that he liked to drive around and honk the horn. After a while, they got tired and decided to take a nap. They put their toys away and went to their bedroom. Lily put her doll on the shelf and Ben put his truck on the floor. They crawled into their beds and closed their eyes. But then, they heard a loud noise from the living room. It sounded like something was crashing and breaking. They opened their eyes and got scared. What was that noise? They got out of their beds and tiptoed to the door. They peeked outside and saw a big mess. The shelf had fallen down and all the toys were on the floor. The doll was broken and the truck was smashed. There was also a big hole in the wall. And in the hole, they saw a furry face with big teeth and claws. It was a bear! The bear had smelled their toys and wanted to play with them. But he was too big and strong and he made a big mess. He saw Lily and Ben and growled. He wanted to play with them too. But Lily and Ben did not want to play with the bear. They were very scared. They ran back to their bedroom and locked the door. They hid under their blankets and hoped the bear would go away. They wished their mom and dad would come home soon. They did not like this display of the bear. They wanted their nap back.
