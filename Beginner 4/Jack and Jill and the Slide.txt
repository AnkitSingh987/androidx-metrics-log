Category: Beginner
Title: Jack and Jill and the Slide

Once upon a time there were two children called Jack and Jill. Jack was 3 years old, and Jill was his big sister. They were in the park one day when their mum called them for a lecture. They were both scared of getting a lecture but Jack was too proud to say so. He decided to hide under the slide, so his mum couldn't find him. Jill saw him and knew what he was doing. She wanted to help, so she said she would take the lecture for both of them. Jack was so proud of his big sister for being so brave. He came out from under the slide and mum saw him. He quickly ran to his sister and gave her a big hug. Mum then gave the lecture to just Jill and pushed Jack away, but he was still proud of his big sister. They made a deal that they would always help each other, even if they needed to hide.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Sally and Jack. They were playing with Jack's new fancy toy. It seemed like lots of fun. Suddenly, Sally felt a sharp pain in her arm. She started to cry. Jack asked, "What's wrong, Sally?" "My arm hurts," she said. Jack said, "Maybe you should rest. Here, sit on this log." Sally sat down and Jack looked into her eyes. He knew he could help her. He said, "I have an idea. Tomorrow, let's take a fancy walk in the woods. You'll feel better!" Sally was happy and the pain seemed to go away. The next day, Jack and Sally went for a long walk. It seemed like the perfect cure for Sally's aching arm. As they walked deeper into the woods, Sally felt a lot better. She was sure the pain was gone. At the end of their fancy walk, Jack and Sally looked back to where they started. The pain in Sally's arm was completely gone. Jack knew his plan worked. The two friends shared a happy hug, and they knew their friendship would last forever.

Once upon a time, there was a smooth red mailbox. It lived in front of a little blue house. The mailbox liked to help people send letters to their friends. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the mailbox. He said, "Hello, mailbox! I want to send a letter to my friend, Sam." The mailbox smiled and said, "Put your letter inside me, and I will send it for you." But Tim was not careful. He threw his letter on the ground and ran away. The mailbox was sad. Tim's mom saw what happened and said, "Tim, that was not nice. You must pick up your letter and put it in the mailbox gently. If you don't, I will punish you." Tim listened to his mom and picked up the letter. He said sorry to the mailbox and put the letter inside. The mailbox was happy, and Tim learned to be careful and kind. From that day on, Tim always treated the mailbox nicely, and they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside with his friends. One day, Spot and his friends were playing a game where they had to change seats. Spot was very good at the game. He could change seats very fast. But one of his friends, a small cat named Mittens, was not so good at it. Mittens was always the last one to change seats. This made Mittens very sad. Spot saw that Mittens was sad and wanted to help. So, he told his friends to play a new game, a game where everyone could be good at. They all played the new game and had lots of fun. Mittens was happy, and Spot was glad that he could help his friend.

Sara was sad. She had a big ouch on her arm. She fell down when she was playing with her bike. Her arm was red and pale and it hurt a lot. Her mom came and saw her arm. She said, "Oh, Sara, I'm sorry. You have a bad scrape. Let me help you." She took Sara inside the house and got a wet cloth and a band-aid. She rubbed the cloth gently on Sara's arm. She said, "This will make it clean and cool. It will feel better soon." Sara winced a little, but she was brave. She said, "Thank you, mom. You are nice." Her mom smiled and kissed her arm. She said, "You are welcome, Sara. You are strong." She put the band-aid on Sara's arm. She said, "This will protect your scrape. It has a pretty flower on it. Do you like it?" Sara looked at the band-aid and nodded. She said, "I like it. It is pretty. And it makes my arm feel better." She hugged her mom and said, "I love you, mom." Her mom hugged her back and said, "I love you too, Sara. Now, do you want some juice and a cookie?" Sara smiled and said, "Yes, please." They went to the kitchen and had a snack. Sara felt happy again.
