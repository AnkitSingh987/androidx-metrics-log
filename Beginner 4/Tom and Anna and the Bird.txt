Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Anna and the Bird

Tom and Anna like to go to the library with Mom. They can find many books with pictures and stories. They can also play with toys and puzzles. Mom says they have to be quiet in the library, because other people are reading. One day, they go to the library and see a big bird outside. It is lying on the ground and not moving. Tom and Anna are curious. They run to the bird and touch it. It is cold and stiff. "Mom, look at the bird!" Tom says. "Is it sleeping?" Mom comes over and sees the bird. She pauses and looks sad. She kneels down and hugs Tom and Anna. "No, sweeties, the bird is not sleeping. It is dead. That means it stopped living and breathing. It can't fly or sing anymore." "Why is it dead, Mom?" Anna asks. "Did it get hurt?" Mom shakes her head. "I don't know, honey. Sometimes birds die of old age, or sickness, or accidents. It is part of nature. But we can be gentle and respectful to the bird. We can say goodbye and thank it for being a beautiful creature." Tom and Anna nod. They say goodbye to the bird and stroke its feathers. Mom finds a shovel and digs a hole in the grass. She puts the bird in the hole and covers it with dirt. She makes a cross with two sticks and puts it on top. "Rest in peace, bird," Mom says. "We will remember you." Tom and Anna smile and hold Mom's hand. They go back to the library and find a book about birds. They learn about different kinds of birds and how they live. They also learn to appreciate life and nature. They are happy and smart.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red car. The car was very reliable. It always took people where they wanted to go. One day, the car had to take a family to the airport. They had a lot of luggage. As they drove, they saw a banana on the road. The car didn't want to slip, so it split the banana. The family inside the car laughed. "Silly banana," said the little girl. "You can't stop our reliable car!" When they got to the airport, they took out their luggage. They thanked the big, red car for being so reliable. The car was happy it could help. The family went on their trip, and the car went home to rest.

One day, a big, tough cat named Tom went to the park. Tom saw a little bird in a tree. He wanted to catch the bird, but the bird was too fast. Tom said, "Please, bird, come down and play with me." But the bird was smart. It knew that Tom wanted to catch it. So, the bird said, "No, Tom, I don't want to play with you. You are a tough cat, and you will put me in jail." Tom did not like what the bird said. He tried to catch the bird again, but it flew away. Tom was sad. He did not want to be a tough cat anymore. He just wanted a friend to play with. Then, Tom saw a little dog named Tim. Tim was also looking for a friend. Tom asked, "Tim, will you please play with me?" Tim said, "Yes, Tom, I will play with you. We can be friends." So, Tom and Tim played together in the park. They had lots of fun. They forgot about the bird and the jail. They were happy to have each other as friends.

One day, a small ball went for a bounce. It liked to bounce high and low. The ball was red and happy. It bounced all day and all night. The ball was not fearful. One day, the ball found a big open. The open was very big and very new. The ball wanted to bounce in the open. So, it bounced in and out, up and down. The ball was very happy in the open. But then, something unexpected happened! A big wind came and took the ball far away. The ball was fearful now. It did not want to be far away. But the wind was nice and said, "Don't be fearful, little ball. I will take you back to the open." The wind took the ball back, and the ball was happy again. It bounced in the open, no longer fearful.
