Category: Beginner
Title: The on Farm and the Bull

Once upon a time, a powerful bull lived on a farm. The bull loved to run and play in the big field. One day, a man came to the farm. The man wanted to drive a big truck. The man said to the bull, "Can you help me? I need to drive this truck." The bull said, "Yes, I can help you." So, the man and the powerful bull went to the big truck. The man tried to drive the truck, but it was too hard. The powerful bull pushed the truck to help the man. They went too fast and the truck went crash! The man and the bull were sad. They could not fix the truck.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a toy volcano. This volcano was incredible. It could make small rocks jump and dance. Mia loved her volcano very much. One day, Mia played with her volcano. She said, "Volcano, I want to see you jump and dance!" The volcano heard her and started to jump and dance. Mia was so happy. She knew something big would happen soon. Later that day, Mia's friend, Ben, came over to play. He saw the incredible volcano and said, "Wow, Mia! Your volcano is so cool!" Mia smiled and said, "Yes, Ben! It can jump and dance!" They both watched the volcano jump and dance together. When it was time for Ben to go home, Mia hugged her volcano. She said, "Thank you, volcano, for making us so happy today." The volcano stopped jumping and dancing, and Mia knew it was happy too.

One day, a good dog saw a big tree. The dog wanted to help his friend, the bird, make a home. The dog found an axe and started to hit the tree. As the dog hit the tree, a kind man entered the forest. The man saw the dog and said, "Hello, dog! Why are you hitting the tree?" The dog replied, "I want to help my friend, the bird, make a home." The kind man smiled and said, "That is a good idea, dog. Let me help you." Together, they made a nice hole in the tree for the bird. The bird was happy and said, "Thank you, dog and man!" They all became good friends and lived happily in the forest.

Once upon a time, there was an old lion named Leo. Leo was very hungry, but he was too slow to catch other animals. One day, he saw a big piece of meat near a tree. Leo was very happy and said, "I can eat this meat and not be hungry anymore!" As Leo walked closer to the meat, a small mouse named Max saw him. Max was scared and wanted to escape. Max said, "Please, Mr. Lion, don't eat me! I can help you get the meat!" Leo thought for a moment and said, "Okay, little mouse, you can help me. But if you can't, I will eat you instead!" Max was still scared, but he wanted to try. Max ran to the tree and tried to get the meat. But the meat was too high for Max to reach. Max tried and tried, but he could not get the meat. Leo was very angry and said, "You did not help me, little mouse. Now I will eat you!" And with that, Leo caught Max and ate him. Leo was still hungry, but now he had no one to help him get the meat. Leo was sad and hungry, and he stayed that way for a long time.

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a pen. It was a different pen. It had many colors. Lucy loved the pen very much. She knew she could make pretty pictures with it. Lucy showed her friend, Tom, the pen. Tom said, "Wow, that is a different pen! Can I try it?" Lucy said, "Yes, you can, but please be careful. I love this pen." Tom and Lucy drew many pictures with the pen. They had a fun day. The next day, Lucy could not find her pen. She was sad. She asked Tom, "Have you seen my pen?" Tom said, "No, I have not." They looked for the pen together. They found it under a big rock. Lucy was happy. She said, "Thank you, Tom. I love my pen and I am glad we found it."
