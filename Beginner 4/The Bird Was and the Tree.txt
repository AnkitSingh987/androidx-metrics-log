Category: Beginner
Title: The Bird Was and the Tree

One day, a little bird was in a tree. The bird was very lazy. It did not want to fly or sing. It just wanted to sit and watch the world. A big bug came and sat next to the bird. The bug said, "Why are you so lazy, bird? You should fly and sing like other birds." The bird looked at the bug and said, "I don't want to. I like to sit and watch." The big bug did not like the lazy bird. It wanted the bird to go away. So, the bug pinched the bird's wing. The bird jumped and said, "Ouch! Why did you pinch me?" The bug said, "I want you to fly and sing, not be lazy." The bird thought about what the bug said. It decided to fly and sing. The bird flew high in the sky and sang a beautiful song. The big bug was happy, and the lazy bird learned that it was fun to fly and sing.

Once upon a time, in a big zoo, there was a smart rhinoceros. His name was Remy. Remy loved to play with his friends. They played hide and seek, and tag all day long. One day, Remy's friends wanted to have a big party. They asked Remy to help them prepare. Remy was very happy to help. He got balloons, yummy food, and fun toys for the party. The day of the party came, and all the animals had a great time. They ate, played, and laughed together. Remy's friends thanked him for his help. They all agreed that Remy was the smartest rhinoceros they knew.

Once upon a time, there was a little fork who lived in a big kitchen. The fork had a very important job. It had to protect all the food on the plate. It was a hard job, but the fork was very strong. One day, a big, scary monster came into the kitchen. The monster wanted to eat all the food on the plate. The fork knew it had to protect the food. So, it stood up tall and strong in front of the plate. The monster tried to get past the fork, but the fork was too hard and strong. The monster could not eat the food. The fork had protected the food and saved the day. The food on the plate was safe, and everyone in the kitchen was happy. And from that day on, the fork was known as the best protector in the whole kitchen.

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn dog named Max. Max had a hurt leg, but he still wanted to play. One day, Max's friend, a bird named Bella, came to visit him. Bella said, "Max, let's play!" Max was happy to see Bella, but he knew his leg was still hurt. He said, "Bella, I want to play, but my leg hurts." Bella felt sad for Max, but she had an idea. Bella said, "Max, we can play a game where you don't need to use your leg. Let's play hide and seek!" Max was excited and agreed. They played hide and seek all day, and Max's leg got better. They were happy and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was an ancient tree. This tree was very old and big. Near the tree, there was a little girl named Olive. Olive loved to play near the tree every day. One sunny day, Olive found a little bird near the tree. The bird could not fly. Olive wanted to help the bird. She held the bird gently in her hands. She was very careful not to hurt the bird. Olive took the bird to her home. She gave the bird water and food. Soon, the bird felt better. The bird was happy and could fly again. Olive and the bird became best friends. They played near the ancient tree every day, and they were happy together.

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn little dog named Max. Max liked to play in the park with his friends. One day, a thick fog came to the park. Max could not see his friends. Max's mom said, "Stay close to me, Max. I will provide help if you need it." But Max wanted to find his friends. So, Max walked into the fog to look for them. He called out, "Friends, where are you?" Max's friends heard him and called back, "We are here, Max!" Max followed their voices and found them. They were all happy to be together again. Max learned that it is good to listen to his mom and stay close when it is foggy.
