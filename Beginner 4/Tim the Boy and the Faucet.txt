Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Faucet

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside with his ball. One day, he saw a faucet in his yard. Tim was very happy because he was thirsty. Tim said to himself, "I will get a drink from the faucet." When he turned the faucet on, water did not come out. Instead, a big spring jumped out! Tim was very surprised. The spring said, "Hello, Tim! I am here to play with you." Tim was happy to have a new friend. They played together all day and had lots of fun. And that is how Tim met his new friend, the spring.

Once upon a time, there was a big, dark house. In the house lived a little girl named Amy and her mom. Amy was scared of the dark. One day, her mom said, "Amy, let's remove the dark from the house!" Amy and her mom went to the chimney. It was very dirty. They took out all the dirt from the chimney. The house was not dark anymore. The sun came in through the chimney and made the house bright and happy. Amy and her mom were very happy. They played and laughed in the bright house. Amy was not scared of the dark anymore. She said, "Thank you, mom, for removing the dark from our house!" And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a messy cat named Tom went to play outside. He saw his friend, a dog named Max. Max was very sad. Tom asked, "Why are you sad, Max?" Max said, "I want to eat my apple, but I can't. I need a knife to cut it." Tom wanted to help his friend, so he went to find a knife. He looked in the kitchen, but he could not find one. Then, Tom had an idea. He asked his mom to provide a knife. His mom said, "No, knives are not for little ones like you." Tom was sad, but he still wanted to help Max. Tom went back to Max and said, "I have an idea!" He took Max to a big, strong tree. They put the apple on a branch and let it drop. The apple fell and broke into two pieces! Max was so happy that he could eat his apple now. Tom and Max ate the apple together and had a great time. They learned that they could solve problems without using things that are not safe for them.

Tom and Lily were twins. They liked to play outside with their ball. One day, they saw a big park across the street. They wanted to go there and have fun. "Let's go to the park, Lily!" Tom said. "OK, Tom! But we have to be careful. The street is busy and noisy. We have to wait for the green light and hold hands," Lily said. They waited for the green light and held hands. They looked both ways and started to cross the street. But they did not see the big truck coming fast. The truck driver did not see them either. He was in a hurry and did not stop at the red light. "Look out, Tom and Lily!" a man shouted. But it was too late. The truck hit them and they flew in the air. They landed on the ground with a thud. Their ball rolled away. Their clothes were dirty and bloody. They did not move or make a sound. The man ran to them and called for help. He was very sad and angry. He said bad words to the truck driver. The truck driver was scared and sorry. He said he did not mean to hurt them. He said he was in a hurry to deliver some clean clothes to a store. But it did not matter. Tom and Lily were gone. They never got to play in the park. They never got to grow up and have more fun. They were gone forever.

Once upon a time, there was a creative little girl named Lily. She loved to count and play with her toys. One day, Lily found a candle in her room. She was very excited and wanted to show her mom. Lily ran to her mom and said, "Mommy, look! I found a candle!" Her mom smiled and said, "That's great, Lily! Do you want to count how many candles we have in the house?" Lily nodded her head and said, "Yes, Mommy! I want to count!" Together, they went around the house and counted all the candles. Lily counted one, two, three, four, and five candles. She was so happy to learn how to count. Her mom was very proud of her. That night, Lily's mom lit the candles they had counted. The warm, soft light filled the room. Lily felt cozy and safe. She knew that her creative counting game had made her mom happy too. From that day on, Lily loved to count everything she saw. She counted her toys, the flowers in the garden, and even the stars in the sky. Lily's creative counting games brought joy to her and her family.
