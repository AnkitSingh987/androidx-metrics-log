Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Book

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a purple stamp in her toy box. She was very happy because purple was her favorite color. She wanted to show her mom, so she ran to the kitchen. "Mom, look what I found! A purple stamp!" she said. Mom smiled and said, "That's great, Lucy! But we need to measure it to see if it fits in your stamp book." They went to the table and used a small ruler to measure the stamp. It was the perfect size. Lucy was very excited to put the stamp in her book. Later, Lucy went to play outside with her friends. She showed them the purple stamp and told them how she and her mom measured it. They all wanted to see the stamp in her book. When they went inside and opened the book, they saw the purple stamp fit just right. They all clapped and cheered for Lucy.

Once upon a time, there was a hot sun up in the sky. It was so hot that everyone started to sweat. Tommy was walking by, and he said to himself, “This sun is too hot. I need something to cool me down!” He saw a beautiful tree with many colourful leaves. He went underneath the tree and said, ”Wow! All these leaves have different colors. That’s cool!” Tommy wanted to own one of the leaves so he reached up and plucked one off the tree. He put it in his pocket and said, “This is my own color! Now I can feel cool!” The next day, Tommy went back to the tree and saw that the leaves had fallen off. He was sad, but then he realized that the sun wasn’t hot anymore. He smiled, happily, knowing that he had his own color to keep him cool.

Lily and Ben were friends. They liked to play in the forest near their house. One day, they saw a big pink flower. It was very pretty and smelled good. "Can I pick it?" Lily asked. "No, it is not ours. It belongs to the forest," Ben said. But Lily did not listen. She picked the flower and put it in her hair. She felt happy. They walked some more and saw a big tree. It had a hole in it. Ben wanted to look inside. "Maybe there is something fun in there," he said. "Okay, let's see," Lily said. They went to the hole and peeked inside. But they did not see anything fun. They saw a big angry bee. It was the bee's home. And it was the bee's flower. The bee buzzed and flew out of the hole. It saw Lily and her flower. It was very mad. "Give me back my flower!" the bee said. Lily was scared. She tried to run away. But the bee followed her. It stung her on the nose. It hurt a lot. Lily cried and dropped the flower. The bee took it back and flew away. Ben ran to Lily and hugged her. He got a leaf and put it on her nose. "I'm sorry, Lily. Are you okay?" he said. Lily nodded. She felt sorry too. "I'm sorry, Ben. I'm sorry, bee. I should not have taken the flower. It was wrong," she said. They went back to their house. They told their mom what happened. She gave them some ice and a hug. They learned their lesson. They never picked flowers from the forest again. They regretted what they did. They wanted to be good friends to the forest and the bee.

One day, a dog named Max saw a wet ball. The ball was very attractive. Max was excited to play with the ball. He picked it up with his mouth and ran around the yard. Max saw his friend, a cat named Luna. Max said, "Luna, come play with me and my ball!" Luna looked at the wet ball and said, "Okay, Max! I will play with you." They played with the ball all day. Max and Luna were very happy. They were excited to find a new toy. The wet, attractive ball made their day fun.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big brain and he loved to play with his friends. One day, Tim saw a big, angry bear in the forest. He was very scared and ran away to hide behind a big tree. Tim knew that the angry bear could hurt him and his friends. He used his big brain to think of a plan. He found a big stick and put honey on it. Tim then put the stick near the bear's cave and ran back to his hiding spot. The angry bear saw the honey on the stick and started to eat it. While the bear was busy eating, Tim told his friends to run back to their homes. The bear finished eating the honey and went back to his cave. Tim and his friends were safe, but they knew the bear was still angry. One day, Tim's friend got too close to the bear's cave and the bear started to chase him. Tim saw this and got very scared. He wept because he didn't want his friend to get hurt. He used his big brain to think of another plan. This time, he found a big net and put more honey in it. The angry bear saw the honey in the net and went to eat it. As the bear ate the honey, he got stuck in the net! Tim and his friends ran to get help from the grown-ups in the village. The grown-ups came and took the angry bear far away from the forest. Tim and his friends were safe and happy, and they never saw the angry bear again.
