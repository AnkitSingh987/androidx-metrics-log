Category: Beginner
Title: The Tall Baby and the Bear

Once there was a tall baby. He was walking through the park, enjoying the sunny day. Suddenly, he felt something squeezing his hand. It was a big, friendly-looking bear. "Hello baby," said the bear. "Come play with me!" The baby smiled. He liked to play. So he said, "Sure!" The bear and the baby ran around, laughing and squeezing each other's hands. They played for hours until it was time for the baby to go home. Before leaving, the baby hugged the bear and said, "Thank you for squeezing my hand!" "You're welcome," said the bear, "Come back soon and we'll squeeze hands again!" The baby smiled and waved goodbye. He was so happy that he had made a new friend in the park.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the yard. The cat was very thirsty, so she asked the dog to help her find some water. The dog said, "I know where to find water! Follow me!" They walked and walked until they found a big bowl of water. The dog started to pour water into a small cup for the cat. The cat gave the dog a grateful look and said, "Thank you, dog!" But then, something unexpected happened. As the dog poured the water, the cup started to change color! It turned from red to blue, then to green, and finally to yellow. The cat and the dog were very surprised. They looked at each other and laughed. "What a funny cup!" said the cat. "Let's show our friends!" And so, the cat and the dog took the color-changing cup to show all their friends in the yard. They all laughed and played with the cup, and they were all very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a nice boy named Tom. He liked to play with his toys all day. One day, Tom got a new toy. It was a big, red ball. He was so happy and played with it all year. One day, Tom was playing with his ball near a big tree. He kicked the ball very hard. The ball went up, up, up and hit the tree. The ball started to break. Tom was sad because his ball was not nice anymore. Tom's mom saw him crying. She came to him and gave him a hug. She told him not to worry. They will get a new ball that will not break. Tom smiled and felt happy again. He knew his mom would find a nice ball for him to play with.

Once upon a time there was a team. This team was honest, and they wanted to have a lot of fun. So, the team decided to spin like a tornado! Every member of the team spun around and around until they were out of breath. At first, their spinning made them laugh. But then, something strange happened. The more and more they spun, the faster and faster they turned. They felt like the whole world was spinning! The team started to panic. "Will we ever stop spinning?" they wondered. But then, the spinning slowed down and finally stopped. The team was so relieved! They had learned an important lesson. Spinning can be a lot of fun, but it's important to be careful. If they hadn't been honest and careful, they could have spun forever!

Once upon a time, there was a big animal named Tom. Tom was a very graceful animal. He loved to jump and play with his friends in the forest. One day, Tom saw a little bird stuck in a tree. The little bird was scared and yelled, "Help, help!" Tom wanted to help the bird, but he didn't know how. So, he asked his friend, the wise old owl, for help. The wise old owl said, "Tom, you can help the bird by shaking the tree gently." Tom shook the tree, and the little bird was free. The bird said, "Thank you, Tom!" They became good friends and played together in the forest every day.

Lila and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to climb on the big slide and swing on the ropes. They saw a new thing in the park. It was a big ball with many spikes. It was dark and shiny. "Wow, look at that!" Ben said. "It looks fun. Let's go and touch it." Lila was not sure. She thought the spikes looked sharp and scary. She said, "No, Ben. I don't want to touch it. It might hurt us. And maybe it belongs to someone else. We should not take things that are not ours." Ben did not listen. He ran to the ball and reached out his hand. He wanted to feel the spikes. But as soon as he touched the ball, he screamed. The ball was very hot. It burned his hand. He dropped the ball and ran back to Lila. He was crying and holding his hand. Lila hugged him and said, "I'm sorry, Ben. Are you okay? Let me see your hand. We should go and find a grown-up. They can help you." Ben nodded and said, "Okay, Lila. You are right. I was silly. I should have listened to you. I don't like the ball anymore. It is not fun. It is mean. I don't value it at all." Lila and Ben walked to the bench where their mom was sitting. They told her what happened. She looked at Ben's hand and said, "Oh, Ben. You are very brave. But you should be careful. That ball is not a toy. It is a sculpture. It is made of metal. And metal gets very hot in the sun. That's why it has spikes. They are to keep people away. It is not for touching. It is for looking." Lila and Ben learned a new word. They learned what a sculpture was. They also learned to value their safety and their friendship. They said sorry to the ball and to each other. They decided to play with something else. They found a nice sandbox and made a castle. They had fun and they were happy.
