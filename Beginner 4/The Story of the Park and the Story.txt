Title: The Story of the Park and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a little boy who loved to play. Every day he would run around with his friends in the park, laughing and having fun. One day, the little boy saw a truck filled with coal. He asked the driver for some coal, and the driver said he would deliver it the next day. The next day, the little boy went to the park again. He saw some other kids playing with coal. But when he asked them where they got it from, they said that a truck had delivered it from the driver. The little boy realised that the driver had lied. He was fake! He was pretending to deliver coal so he could trick people into paying for something he never would have given. The moral of the story is that you should always be careful when dealing with people you don't know. Don't trust everyone you meet and don't believe things that sound too good to be true.

Once upon a time, a smart cat named Tom lived in a small house. Tom had a toy vehicle that he loved to play with every day. It was his favorite toy. One sunny day, Tom's toy vehicle got very dirty. So, Tom decided it was time to bathe the toy. He filled a little tub with water and put the toy vehicle in it. Tom splashed water on the toy and made sure it was clean. After the bath, Tom's toy vehicle looked shiny and new. Tom was very happy to have a clean toy to play with again. He played with his toy vehicle all day long, and they had lots of fun adventures together.

Once upon a time, there was a fat tuna living in the ocean. He liked to play with his friends and explore the deep waters. One day, he decided to visit the beach. He asked his friend, "Do you want to come too?" His friend said, "No, I don't think it's a good idea." But the fat tuna was too curious, so he swam to the beach alone. When he got there, it was very quiet. Suddenly, he heard a loud sound behind him. It was a fisherman and he was carrying a large sack! The fat tuna was too scared to move, so the fisherman easily grabbed him and put him in the sack. The fat tuna was so scared and sad. He knew his adventure was coming to an end. Unfortunately, the fisherman had already foreshadowed a bad ending for the fat tuna, so the adventure ended with a sad sack.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big dog. The dog is brown and has a long tail. The dog is hungry. He smells beef in Lily's bag. "Can I have some beef?" the dog asks Lily. He tries to take the bag from her. "No, this is my beef!" Lily says. She holds the bag tight. "You are rude, dog. You can't take my things." Ben is scared of the dog. He runs to the slide. He hopes the dog will go away. But the dog does not go away. He barks at Lily. He wants the beef. He jumps on Lily and knocks her down. The bag falls on the ground. The dog grabs the beef and runs away. Lily is sad and hurt. She cries. Ben comes back to help her. He hugs her and says, "I'm sorry, Lily. That was a bad dog. He should not have done that. You need a band-aid for your knee." Lily smiles at Ben. She says, "Thank you, Ben. You are a good friend. I don't need the beef. I have you."

Tom and Anna are friends. They like to play in the park. Today is very hot. Tom and Anna are thirsty and tired. "Let's go to the shop and buy some juice," Tom says. "OK, but I want to bring my fan," Anna says. She has a fan that she made by herself. It is original. It has colorful feathers and glitter. "Your fan is very pretty, but why do you need it?" Tom asks. "Because it makes me feel cool and happy," Anna says. She waves her fan in front of her face. Tom and Anna walk to the shop. They see a big dog on the way. The dog is friendly. It wags its tail and licks their hands. "Hello, dog. Do you want to play with us?" Tom says. The dog barks and jumps. It sees Anna's fan. It likes the feathers and glitter. It tries to grab the fan with its mouth. "Hey, no! That's my fan!" Anna says. She pulls the fan away from the dog. The dog is sad. It whines and looks at Anna. "I'm sorry, dog. You can't have my fan. It is mine. I made it by myself. It is original," Anna says. The dog understands. It nods and licks Anna's face. Tom and Anna smile. They pet the dog and give it a biscuit. Then they go to the shop and buy some juice. They share their juice with the dog. They are all happy and cool.
