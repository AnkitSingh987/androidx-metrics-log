Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Lion

Lily likes to paint. She has many colors and brushes. She paints on paper, on walls, on rocks, on anything. She likes to make pictures of flowers, animals, stars, and her family. One day, Lily finds a big pit in the yard. It is deep and dark. She wonders what is inside. She thinks it might be fun to paint the pit. She gets her paints and brushes and runs to the pit. She starts to paint the pit with blue and green and yellow. She paints the walls and the floor and the edge. She makes swirls and dots and stripes. She thinks the pit looks pretty. She is happy. But then, she hears a loud roar. She looks up and sees a big lion. The lion is angry. It was sleeping in the pit. It does not like Lily's paint. It does not like Lily. It shows its teeth and claws. It jumps out of the pit and chases Lily. Lily is scared. She drops her paints and brushes and runs away. She screams for help. She runs to her house and slams the door. She hugs her mom and dad. They are serious. They tell Lily to never go near the pit again. They tell Lily to be careful with her paint. They tell Lily they love her. Lily is sorry. She says she will not paint the pit again. She says she loves them too. She is glad she is safe.

One day, a little bird named Tim wanted to fly. Tim had a big idea. He asked his mom, "Can I fly to the moon?" His mom said, "No, Tim. You are too small. Be obedient and stay near the tree." Tim was sad but had another idea. He asked his friend, a big bird named Sam, "Can you take me to the moon?" Sam said, "I don't know, but we can try." So Tim jumped on Sam's back and they started to fly up, up, up! As they flew higher, they saw a big balloon in the sky. It looked like the moon! Tim said, "Wow, we made it to the moon!" But Sam said, "No, Tim. This is a balloon, not the moon." They landed on the balloon and played. It was fun and unexpected. Tim was happy with his new adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his big, red ball. One day, Tim went to the park to play with his ball. He saw a tight net in the park. Tim thought it would be fun to throw his ball into the net. Tim threw his ball into the net, but the ball got stuck. He tried to get it back, but the net was too tight. Tim was sad. Just then, a girl named Sue came to the park. She saw Tim and asked, "Why are you sad?" Tim said, "My ball is stuck in the tight net. I can't get it back." Sue wanted to help Tim. She had an idea. Sue found a long stick and used it to push the ball out of the net. The ball returned to Tim. He was very happy. Tim and Sue played with the ball together and became good friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, a boy named Tim saw a distant storm. He blinked his eyes and looked again. He saw a big, gray tornado. Tim was scared but he wanted to know more about it. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, I see a tornado far away!" His mom looked out the window and blinked her eyes too. "Don't worry, Tim. It's just a cloud. It won't hurt us," she said. But then, something unexpected happened. The gray tornado turned into a big, colorful rainbow! Tim and his mom smiled and hugged. They were happy to see the beautiful rainbow instead of the scary tornado.

Once upon a time, in a hidden forest, there lived a deer named Dotty. Dotty was a kind and happy deer who loved to play with her friends. One day, Dotty and her friends decided to play a game of hide-and-seek. Dotty was very good at hiding, and her friends always had a hard time finding her. Dotty's mom told her that she should not go too far away while playing. But Dotty, being very good at hiding, went far away from her friends. Dotty's mom had warned her that if she went too far, she would be punished. As Dotty was hiding, she heard a loud noise. It was her mom calling her name. Dotty knew she had gone too far and that she would be punished. But she also knew that her mom loved her and wanted her to be safe. Dotty came out of her hiding spot and went back to her mom. Her mom was happy to see her safe and sound. Dotty was punished, but she learned a valuable lesson - to always listen to her mom and stay close to her friends.
