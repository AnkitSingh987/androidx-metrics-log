Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog and the Grass

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to rub his back on the soft grass. One sunny day, Spot was playing in the park when he heard a voice. It was a little girl named Sue. Sue said, "Hi, Spot! Can you play with me?" Spot wagged his tail and barked, "Yes!" They played catch with a red ball. They were very happy, and Sue's voice was full of joy. After playing, Sue and Spot were tired. They sat under a big tree. Sue gave Spot some water from her cup. They looked at the sky and saw that the sun was going down. Sue said, "We should go home now." Spot barked, "OK!" They walked home together, and Sue's mom was happy to see them. She said, "I'm glad you had fun, but now it's time for bed." Sue and Spot hugged, and they knew they would be available to play again tomorrow.

Lila was a little girl who liked to play outside. She had a big ball that she could kick and throw. She liked to play with her ball in the park, where there were many trees and flowers and other children. One day, Lila's mom said they had to move to a new house. Lila was sad. She did not want to leave her park and her friends. She did not know where they were going. She asked her mom, but her mom said it was a surprise. They got in the car and drove for a long time. Lila looked out the window and saw many roads. Some roads were big and busy, with many cars and trucks and noises. Some roads were small and quiet, with only a few houses and animals. Lila did not like the roads. She missed her park. They arrived at their new house. It was on a lonely road, far from the city. There were no other houses nearby, only fields and hills. Lila did not see any children or toys or flowers. She only saw a dusty yard and a broken fence. She did not like the house. She wanted to go back to her old home. She got out of the car and followed her mom inside. Her mom showed her her new room. It was small and dark, with no pictures or colors. Lila did not like the room. She felt scared and angry. She threw her ball on the floor and said, "I hate it here! I want to go back!" Her mom hugged her and said, "I know, honey. I know. It's hard to leave what you know and love. But we have to stay here for a while. It's for our own good. Trust me, okay?" Lila did not understand. She did not think it was for their own good. She did not trust her mom. She pushed her away and ran outside. She grabbed her ball and ran down the lonely road. She did not care where she was going. She just wanted to get away from the new house. She did not see the car coming. She did not hear the horn. She did not feel the pain. She only saw a flash of light. Then everything went black.

Once upon a time there was a bald aunt who wanted to help her nephew lots. She had heard that he was very sad and wanted to supply him with everything he needed to make him happy again. So the bald aunt set off to her nephew's house with a bag full of games and toys. When she arrived, she said to her nephew, "I heard you were sad and I wanted to supply you with things that can make you happy." Her nephew replied, "Thank you aunt! That was so kind of you. I didn't think anyone cared." His aunt replied, "Of course I care! That's what aunts are for - to help in any way we can." The two spent the rest of the day playing with the games and toys the aunt had brought. The nephew was so excited and his aunt was so happy that she had supplied him with the things he needed to make him happy. The moral value of this story is that it's important to show people you care about them and support them. Aunts are especially good at doing this. Even the smallest thing can mean so much when it comes to making someone feel special.
