Category: Beginner
Title: The Van and the Window

There was a van. It was a big, blue van. Inside was a boy and his dad. The boy said, "Where are we going daddy?" "Guess," said his dad. The boy looked out the window and saw a playground. He said," I think we are going to the playground!" His dad smiled and said, "Yes, you guessed it!". They parked the van and hopped out. But when they got to the playground, it was all broken and bad. The boy said, "Oh no! What happened?" His dad said, "I don't know, but I guess it was too much fun!"

Once upon a time, there was a big lion. He liked to roar very loud. One day, he found a wallet on the ground. The lion was very happy. He wanted to show it to his friends. The lion went to his friends and showed them the wallet. They all looked at it and laughed. The lion felt embarrassed. He didn't know why they were laughing. The lion got sad and walked away. As the lion was walking, a big storm came. The rain made the wallet wet and the wind blew it away. The lion tried to catch it, but he couldn't. The wallet was gone forever. The lion was very sad and he never found another wallet again.

Once upon a time, in an ordinary little town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim had a friend named Cactus. Cactus was not like other friends. He was green and pointy, but Tim loved him. One day, Tim wanted to play with his friends. He asked his mom, "Can I include Cactus in our game?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful. Cactus has sharp points." Tim and his friends played outside. They were having fun, but then Tim forgot about Cactus's sharp points. He threw Cactus to his friend, and ouch! Cactus hurt his friend's hand. Everyone was sad. Tim's friend had to go home. The game was over. Tim learned that he should be more careful with his special friend Cactus.

One day, a little girl named Sally went to a fancy store with her mom. Inside the store, there were many pretty things. Sally saw a big jar of yummy cookies on a high shelf. She wanted a cookie very much. Sally's mom saw her looking at the cookies. She said, "Sally, I warn you, do not try to get a cookie. It is too high for you." But Sally did not listen to her mom. She wanted a cookie so badly. Sally tried to get the cookie jar. She climbed on a chair, but the chair wobbled and she fell down. She started to cry. Her mom came and hugged her. She said, "Sally, you should have listened to me. It is not safe to climb on chairs." Sally learned her lesson and said sorry to her mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird who wanted to grow big and strong. He lived in a small nest with his mom and dad. They all loved to eat healthy food like worms and bugs. One day, the little bird said to his mom, "Mom, I want to grow big and strong like you and dad. What can I do?" His mom smiled and said, "Eat your healthy food, and soon you will grow." Every day, the little bird ate his healthy food. He played with his friends and had fun. As the days went by, the little bird started to grow. His wings got bigger, and he could fly higher. One day, his dad said, "Look at you! You are growing so big and strong. Keep eating your healthy food, and you will be just like us." The little bird was happy and proud. He knew that eating healthy food and playing with his friends helped him grow.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to swing and play in the park. One day, it rained and made a big puddle in the park. Lily felt sad and miserable because she could not play outside. The next day, the sun came out, and Lily saw the big puddle in the park. She had an idea. She put on her boots and went to the park. Lily stood near the puddle and thought about how much fun she would have. Lily started to swing high in the air. She swung back and forth, higher and higher. Then, she jumped off the swing and landed in the big puddle with a big splash! The water went everywhere, and Lily laughed and laughed. The puddle made her happy, not miserable anymore.
