Title: Tim the Boy and the Cloud

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a big, dark cloud in the sky. It made him feel uncomfortable. He saw lightning in the cloud and it scared him. He ran to his mom and said, "Mom, the sky is angry!" His mom looked at the sky and said, "Yes, Tim, there is a storm coming. Let's go inside the house to be safe." They went inside and Tim felt better. But then, the wind started to blow very hard. It pushed a big tree near their house. The tree fell on their house and made a loud noise. Tim and his mom were very sad. The storm had broken their home.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a test at school the next day. He was playing with his toys and forgot about the test. His mom came into the room and asked, "Tim, did you study for your test tomorrow?" Tim looked at his mom and said, "Oh no, I forgot! I am sorry, Mom. I will study now." He put away his toys and started to study for his test. Tim's mom was happy that he was going to study. The next day, Tim took the test at school. When he got home, he told his mom, "I did good on the test, Mom! Thank you for reminding me to study." His mom hugged him and said, "I am proud of you, Tim. Don't forget to study next time!"

One day, a friendly bird named Bobo was flying in the sky. He had a job to deliver a big oyster to his friend, Sally the squirrel. Bobo held the oyster in his beak and flew towards Sally's tree. As Bobo got close to the tree, a mean crow named CawCaw saw the oyster. "Give me that oyster, Bobo!" CawCaw yelled. Bobo was scared, but he wanted to help his friend Sally. "No, CawCaw, this oyster is for my friend Sally!" Bobo said. CawCaw tried to take the oyster from Bobo, but Bobo flew fast and high. He was brave and did not let CawCaw take the oyster. Finally, Bobo reached Sally's tree and gave her the oyster. Sally was very happy and thanked Bobo for being such a good friend.

Once there was a little boy named Jack who was just three years old. One day he was with his mum at a shop and saw a green mug with a picture of a frog on it. He asked his mum if he could get it and she said yes. Jack was elated and thanked his mum for the mug. He heard two other boys around his age looking at the mug and saying, "That's a nice mug, I want one too!" He knew they were jealous of his new mug. Still, Jack was feeling generous, so he called out to the boys, "Do you want my mug? You can have it!" The boys were surprised but then one said, "No, we demand it!" Jack was confused and looked over to his mum. She smiled and said to them, "It's Jack's mug and if he wants to give it away, then that's okay." The other boy said, "Really? Can we have it then?" Jack smiled and said, "Sure you can, it's my treat!" So the boys got their very own green mug with a frog on it and they were so happy and excited. Jack smiled to himself, glad that he could make someone else happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a green frog. The green frog lived in a pond. The green frog liked to play with his friends. One day, the green frog found a cheap toy. The cheap toy was a small car. The green frog was very happy. The green frog showed the cheap toy to his friends. They all wanted to play with the cheap toy. They took turns playing with the cheap toy. The green frog and his friends had a lot of fun. They played all day long. But then, something bad happened. The green frog and his friends played too hard. They broke the cheap toy. The cheap toy was destroyed. The green frog and his friends were very sad. They could not play with the cheap toy anymore. The green frog learned to be more careful with his toys.
