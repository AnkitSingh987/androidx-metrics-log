Category: Beginner
Title: Sam the Boy and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Sam. He loved to draw and make prints. One day, he made a big print of his favorite animal, a cat. He was very happy and wanted to show his mom. Sam went to his mom with the big print, but it was too big to fit through the door. He felt sad and thought about what to do. Then, he had an idea! He would try to shrink the print to make it fit. Sam went back to his room and started to shrink the print. But something unexpected happened. As the print got smaller, the cat came to life! Sam was so surprised and happy. Now, he had a new friend to play with.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat named Tom. Tom was very hungry and wanted to find dinner. He saw an enormous tree with many birds in it. Tom thought, "If I catch a bird, I will have a yummy dinner." Tom tried to climb the tree, but the birds saw him coming. They flew away and said, "You can't catch us, Tom!" Tom was sad and thought, "I must find another way to catch a bird for dinner." The next day, Tom had an idea. He found a big box and put a small piece of food inside. Tom waited and waited. Soon, a bird came and went into the box. Tom quickly closed the box and said, "I got you, bird! Now you must surrender and be my dinner!" The bird was scared but had an idea. It said, "Tom, if you let me go, I will bring you more food than you can eat." Tom thought about it and decided to let the bird go. The bird flew away and came back with many friends. They all brought food for Tom. He had an enormous dinner and was very happy. From that day on, Tom and the birds were friends and shared their food.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to school. In his classroom, there was a big blackboard. The blackboard was very light. All the kids liked to write on it. Tim wanted to help his teacher, Miss Sue, write on the blackboard. "Miss Sue, can I help you write on the blackboard?" Tim asked. Miss Sue smiled and said, "Yes, Tim, you can help me. Thank you for asking!" Tim picked up a piece of chalk and started to write on the blackboard. He wrote his name and drew a sun. The other kids clapped and cheered. They all wanted to help too. Miss Sue let each kid come up and write on the blackboard. They all had fun and learned how to write their names. Tim was very happy he could help his teacher and friends.

Once upon a time, there lived a family of three. Mom, Dad and the little boy. The little boy was very happy, because today was the day they were going to organize a fun day. The little boy was very excited and he couldn’t wait! He smiled and asked his Dad, “Can we make it very high fun?” Dad laughed and said, “We can try!” So, Mom, Dad and the little boy set out to organize the high fun. They made some games and prepared some treats. Then, just as they were about to start, a strong wind came and blew away all their fun! The little boy was so sad and Dad said, “Don’t worry! We can organize it again. Let’s try to find everything and make it even higher fun!” So they all looked high and low and found all their fun! The little boy smiled, and said, “Yay! Our fun day was so high and so much fun!”

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys and eat yummy food. One day, Lily found a big bottle of lotion. She thought it was fun to play with, so she took it to her room. Lily started to weigh the lotion in her hands. It was heavy! She opened the bottle and put the lotion on her toys. But, oh no! The lotion made her toys all spoiled and sticky. Lily was sad and didn't know what to do. Lily's mom came into the room and saw the spoiled toys. She told Lily that they should clean them together. Lily learned that it's important to take care of her things and not to play with things that can spoil them. From that day on, Lily was more careful with her toys and only played with them the right way.
