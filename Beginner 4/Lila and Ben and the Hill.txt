Title: Lila and Ben and the Hill

Lila and Ben are friends. They like to play outside. They see a big hill. They want to go up the hill. "Let's go up the hill!" Lila says. "We can see a lot of things from there." "OK!" Ben says. "But we have to be fast. The sky is dark. It may rain soon." They run up the hill. They are happy. They see the trees, the houses, the cars, and the birds. They are very high. "Look, Lila! I can see our school!" Ben says. "Look, Ben! I can see our park!" Lila says. They hear a loud sound. It is thunder. They see a bright light. It is lightning. They are scared. "Let's go down the hill!" Lila says. "It is going to rain!" But it is too late. The rain starts to fall. It is heavy. It is cold. It is wet. They have no umbrella. They have no coat. They have no hat. They are soaked. They cry. They want to go home. They want their mom and dad. They are sad. They are sick. They are stuck. The rain does not stop. The end.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a golf ball in his yard. He had never seen a golf ball before. It was an original, shiny ball. Tim was very excited and wanted to play with it. Tim dared to hit the golf ball with a stick. He swung the stick and hit the ball very hard. The golf ball flew high into the air and landed far away. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. He liked playing with the golf ball. Tim showed his friends the golf ball. They all wanted to play with it too. They took turns hitting the golf ball with the stick. They had lots of fun playing together. The golf ball was a new and original toy for them.

Once upon a time, there was a charming cop named Jack. Jack was very nice and everyone liked him. One day, Jack met a pretty girl named Jill. They liked each other a lot and wanted to marry. Before they could marry, Jack had to catch a bad guy. The bad guy was very sneaky. Jack and Jill made a plan to catch the bad guy together. Jill said, "Let's hide and wait for the bad guy to come." They hid and waited. The bad guy came, but he was not a bad guy at all! It was their friend Tom, who was playing a trick on them. Tom said, "I am sorry, I just wanted to have fun." They all laughed and were happy. Jack and Jill got married and had a big party with all their friends, including Tom. They lived happily ever after, catching bad guys and having fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a giant who lived in an icy land. Each day, he would wander around looking for something to do. One day, while the giant was walking, he stumbled upon a mountain that had a cave at the top. The giant thought this would be a great place to explore so he made his way up the icy path. As the giant got closer to the cave, he noticed a terrible stench coming from it. He went inside and saw that the walls were covered with bones and broken weapons. The giant was surprised and scared by what he saw but he knew he had to investigate. He began to wander around the cave when suddenly, he heard a loud roar. The giant turned and saw a huge monster! He knew that it would destroy everything in its path and he was terrified. He quickly ran out of the cave and back down the icy path. He had to find a way to stop the monster from destroying his home. The next day, the giant rallied an army of brave warriors to help him stop the monster and protect the icy land. With their help, the giant was able to defeat the monster and save his home. The icy land was safe once again and the giant was very proud of himself for finding a way to destroy the monster.
