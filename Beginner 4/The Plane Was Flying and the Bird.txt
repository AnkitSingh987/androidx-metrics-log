Category: Beginner
Title: The Plane Was Flying and the Bird

One day, a little plane was flying in the sky. The plane was very interesting because it could talk to the birds. The birds liked the plane because it was nice and funny. They all played together in the sky. The plane saw a little bird who was sad. The bird was lost and could not find its family. The plane wanted to help the little bird. The interesting plane told the bird not to be sad. It said, "I will help you find your family." The plane and the little bird flew together. They looked for the bird's family. The plane was very good at comforting the little bird. It made the bird feel better. Soon, they found the bird's family. The bird was very happy. The interesting plane and the birds all played together in the sky.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to eat cereal every day. But one day, he found an unusual cereal box in the kitchen. It was big and had many colors. Tim was very excited to try it. Tim opened the box and saw that the cereal was in two parts. One part was very small and round, and the other part was long and thin. He did not know which part to eat first. Tim felt a little sad because he could not decide. Then, Tim had a great idea. He mixed both parts of the cereal together in his bowl. It looked funny, but he liked it. Tim was happy again. He learned that sometimes, it is good to try new and unusual things. And he enjoyed his new cereal very much.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a friend named Sam. One day, Tim went to visit Sam. Sam had a big, tough dog. The dog was not nice. The tough dog did not like Tim. The dog would growl and bark at him. Tim was scared. He did not like the dog. He started to hate the dog. Tim did not want to visit Sam anymore because of the dog. One day, Tim went to visit Sam again. The tough dog was there. The dog barked and growled at Tim. Tim was very scared. He ran away from the dog, but the dog chased him. Tim tripped and fell. He started to cry. The dog stopped chasing Tim, but he was still not nice. Tim never went to visit Sam again because of the tough dog.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man. He had a big house and many candles. One day, he met a little girl who was lost. She was sad and scared. The wealthy man asked the girl, "Why are you sad?" The girl shrugged and said, "I can't find my way home." The man thought for a moment and said, "I will help you." They walked and walked until they found the girl's house. Her family was so happy to see her. They thanked the wealthy man and gave him a candle as a gift. The wealthy man smiled and went back to his big house, feeling happy that he could help the little girl.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom had a boat. He loved his boat very much. One day, Tom took his boat to the big water. He wanted to see what was far away. In the water, Tom saw a big fish. The fish was from an unknown place. The fish said, "Hello, Tom! Do you own this boat?" Tom said, "Yes, I do. This is my boat." The big fish smiled and said, "Your boat is nice. Can I go with you?" Tom and the big fish went far away together. They saw many new things. They played and had fun. At the end of the day, Tom and the big fish went back home. They were happy to be friends.

One day, a wide bird named Bob and a small bug named Tim were in the park. They were friends and loved to play together. They wanted to settle a race to see who was faster. They both wanted success. "Ready, set, go!" said Bob. They started to run. Bob flapped his wings and Tim moved his little legs. They raced and raced. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew Bob far away from Tim. "Oh no!" said Tim. He did not know what to do. He kept running and running. Then, he saw Bob in a tree. "Are you okay?" asked Tim. Bob said, "Yes, I am fine. The wind just blew me here." Tim helped Bob get down from the tree. They were both happy and laughed. They did not care who won the race. They were just happy to be together and safe. They learned that success was not about who was faster, but about being good friends.
