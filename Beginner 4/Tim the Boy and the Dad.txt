Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Dad

One day, a boy named Tim found a pistol under his dad's bed. He did not know what it was, but he thought it looked like a toy. Tim wanted to play with it, but he knew he should ask his dad first. Tim went to find his dad, who was in the kitchen. "Dad, can I play with this?" he asked, holding the pistol. His dad looked at it and said, "No, Tim. That is not a toy. It is not safe for you to play with. Now go to sleep, it's your nap time." Tim was sad but he listened to his dad. He went to his room and tried to sleep. But he could not stop thinking about the pistol. He thought it would be fun to play with, even if it was not safe. While his dad was busy, Tim went back to his dad's room and took the pistol. He started to play with it in his room, pretending to be a hero. But then, the pistol went off and made a loud noise. Tim was scared and started to cry. His dad ran into the room and saw what had happened. Tim had hurt himself with the pistol. His dad was very upset and sad. He wished he had kept the pistol in a safer place, so Tim would not have found it.

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat named Tom. Tom liked to play with a water jug. One day, Tom found a big jug. He was very happy. Tom said to his friend, the dog, "Look at my big jug!" The dog said, "Wow, it is big!" They played together with the jug. They had a lot of fun. Then, Tom had an idea. He filled the jug with water. He sprayed the water at the dog. The dog laughed and said, "That is fun!" They played with the water and the jug all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was very spoiled. She had many toys to play with. She liked to play all day long. She played with her toys every day of the week. One day, Lily met a new friend named Tim. Tim did not have many toys to play with. Lily wanted to share her toys with Tim. They played together and had lots of fun. They played every day for a week. At the end of the week, Lily was not spoiled anymore. She learned to share her toys with her new friend, Tim. They played together and were very happy. Lily and Tim became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy raft that he loved to play with. He would take it to the park and drive it in the pond. One day, Tim found an old, big book. The book told a story about an ancient raft. Tim wanted to find this raft and drive it on the pond too. Tim looked and looked for the ancient raft. He found it hidden behind the trees. With a big smile, he took the ancient raft to the pond. Tim drove both his toy raft and the ancient raft on the water. He had so much fun playing with his new friend, the ancient raft.

One day, a dog named Ben went for a jog. He liked to jog in the park. He saw his friend, a cat named Sam. Sam looked confused. Ben wanted to know why. "Sam, why are you confused?" asked Ben. Sam said, "I heard some news, but I don't understand it." Ben wanted to help Sam with the news. Ben and Sam sat under a big tree. They talked about the news. The news was about a new toy store in town. They both got excited. They decided to jog to the toy store together. Now, Sam was not confused anymore.

Lily loved her new toy. It was a soft bear with a red bow. She hugged it and kissed it and took it everywhere. She named it Chewy, because it liked to chew on things. One day, Lily and Chewy went to the kitchen. Lily saw a big jar of butter on the table. She wanted to make toast with butter, like Mommy did. She climbed on a chair and reached for the jar. But she was not careful, and the jar fell on the floor. The butter spilled everywhere. Lily was scared. She knew Mommy would be angry. She grabbed Chewy and ran to her room. She hid under the bed with Chewy. She hoped Mommy would not find them. But Mommy heard the noise. She came to the kitchen and saw the mess. She was very angry. She looked for Lily and Chewy. She followed their footprints of butter. She found them under the bed. Lily saw Mommy and started to cry. She said she was sorry. She said she just wanted toast with butter. She said Chewy was hungry too. Mommy looked at Chewy. She saw that he had butter on his fur. She saw that he had chewed on the red bow. She saw that he had chewed on the bed sheet. She saw that he had chewed on her favorite book. Mommy was very angry. She said Chewy was a bad toy. She said he had to go away. She took Chewy from Lily and threw him in the trash. Lily was very sad. She loved Chewy. She did not want him to go away. She cried and cried and cried.
