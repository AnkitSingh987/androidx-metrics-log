Category: Beginner
Title: The Humble Boy and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a humble boy who lived on the ground with his family. He loved to go outside and play with his friends. One day, the boy wanted to have more fun, so he decided to run! As he ran, he saw a big bird. He wanted to touch it, so he reached out. The bird did not like being touched. It flapped its wings and slapped the boy's hand away. The boy was sad and looked at the ground. Then he remembered something his parents had told him. They said to always stay humble and listen to adults. The boy was happy again and he thanked the bird for teaching him an important lesson. He said an apology and ran back to his parents to show them how humble he had become.

One day, a big dog and a small cat went out to play. They were friends, but sometimes they would fight. The sun was harsh and made them feel hot. They ran around and played near the big tree. The dog said, "I will catch you!" The cat laughed and ran fast. The dog tried to catch the cat but could not. They were having fun, but then the dog tripped on a rock. His leg hurt a lot, and he cried. The cat came back to help, but the dog was still sad. "My leg hurts too much," said the dog. The cat could not make the leg better. They could not play anymore. The dog and cat went home, feeling sad and hurt.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy named Bob. Bob was a round knob with a big smile. Tim loved to play with Bob every day. One rainy day, Tim and Bob went outside to play. They saw a wet puddle. Tim said, "Bob, do you want to jump in the puddle?" Bob nodded with his big smile. Tim carried Bob and they jumped in the puddle together. They laughed and had so much fun. After playing in the wet puddle, Tim and Bob went back inside. Tim's mom saw them and said, "Oh no, you both are wet! Let's get you dry and warm." Tim and Bob had a fun day, and they couldn't wait to play again.

One day, a young boy named Tim found a big toy engine. He loved the engine and wanted to play with it all day. But his mom said, "Tim, you need to let your little sister play with the engine too." Tim did not want to share the engine. He said, "No, I found it first. It is mine!" His little sister, Sue, started to cry. She wanted to play with the engine too. Tim's mom thought of a way to make both kids happy. She said, "Let's take turns. Tim, you can play with the engine for a little bit, and then Sue can play with it too." Tim and Sue agreed. They took turns playing with the engine, and both were happy.

One day, a boy named Tom found an ordinary glove on the ground. He wanted to organize his toys, so he thought the glove could help. He put the glove on and started to pick up his toys. Tom's friend Sam came over and saw the glove. Sam said, "Hey, that's my glove! I lost it yesterday." Tom didn't want to give the glove back, because he liked it. They both wanted the glove, so they started to argue. Then, Tom had an idea. He said, "Let's share the glove. You can have it today, and I can have it tomorrow." Sam agreed, and they both felt happy. They took turns using the glove to organize their toys, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Tuesday. Tuesday was a very safe dog. He always looked both ways before crossing the street and never went too far from home. Tuesday loved to play with his ball in the park. One day, when Tuesday was playing in the park, he saw a little girl crying. She had lost her toy in the big tree. Tuesday wanted to help her. He knew he could save the toy if he tried really hard. So, he ran and jumped high into the air to grab the toy. Tuesday saved the toy and gave it back to the little girl. She was so happy and thanked Tuesday for keeping her toy safe. From that day on, Tuesday and the little girl became best friends. They played together every day and always stayed safe. And they lived happily ever after.
