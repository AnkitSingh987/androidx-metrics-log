Category: Beginner
Title: Sam the Caterpillar and the Park

One day, a little caterpillar named Sam went for a skip in the park. The sun was hot, and the sky was blue. Sam liked to skip on the green grass and play with his friends. As Sam was skipping, he found a smelly sock. "Ew!" he said, and all his friends laughed. They did not want to play with the smelly sock. But Sam thought it was funny, so he put the sock on his head and skipped around. Then, something unexpected happened. The smelly sock turned Sam into a big, beautiful butterfly! His friends were so surprised. They all wanted to be like Sam. So, they found smelly socks too, and they all turned into pretty butterflies. They all skipped and played together, happy and free.

One day, in a small town, there lived a little girl named Amy. Amy had a toy that could glow. She loved her toy very much. Every night, she would make her toy glow before she went to sleep. One morning, Amy woke up and her toy was gone! She looked all around her room, but she could not find it. Amy felt very angry. She asked her mom, "Mom, have you seen my toy?" Her mom said, "No, Amy. I have not seen it." Amy decided to look for her toy all around the town. She asked her friends, the birds, and even the trees if they had seen her toy. Nobody knew where it was. Then, something unexpected happened. As the sun went down, Amy saw a glow in the sky. It was her toy! Her toy was flying high in the sky, making the whole town glow. Amy was so happy to see her toy again. She said, "Come back, toy! I miss you!" The toy heard Amy's voice and flew back to her. From that day on, Amy and her toy were never apart again.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and run. One day, Spot saw a big red ball. The ball was in motion, bouncing up and down. Spot wanted to play with the ball. Spot ran to the ball and tried to bite it. His first bite was not successful. The ball was too big for his little mouth. But Spot did not give up. He tried again and again. Each time, he tried to bite the ball, but it was not easy. Finally, after many tries, Spot was successful. He bit the ball and held it in his mouth. He was so happy! Spot played with the big red ball all day long. He learned that if he tried hard, he could do anything. And from that day on, Spot and the big red ball were the best of friends.

Mia and Ben are best friends. They like to play in the park. They see a big aeroplane in the sky. It makes a loud noise. "Wow, look at the aeroplane!" Mia says. "It is so big and fast!" "I want to fly in an aeroplane one day," Ben says. "It must be fun to see the clouds and the birds." They pretend to be aeroplanes. They spread their arms and run around the park. They make zooming sounds. They have a lot of fun. Then they see a chubby dog. It is brown and fluffy. It has a red collar. It barks at them. "Hello, doggy!" Mia says. "Do you want to play with us?" The dog wags its tail. It likes Mia and Ben. They pet the dog and give it a hug. "Can we skip with the dog?" Ben asks. "Skipping is fun!" "Sure, let's skip with the dog!" Mia agrees. "Maybe the dog can skip too!" They hold hands and skip with the dog. The dog jumps and runs with them. They laugh and smile. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big yellow box with a gift inside. A little girl named Lily found the box in her room. She was so happy to see the gift, and she wanted to open it right away. Before she could open the gift, her mom said, "Lily, first you must cover your eyes." Lily did as her mom said, and covered her eyes with her hands. She was so excited to see what was inside the yellow box. When Lily opened her eyes, the yellow box was gone! She was very surprised. Then, she saw her mom holding the box with a big smile on her face. Mom had moved the box to play a fun game with Lily. They both laughed, and then Lily opened the gift. Inside was a cute toy just for her.
