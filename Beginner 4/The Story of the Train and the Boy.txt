Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Train and the Boy

Once there was a little boy who loved trains. Every night, he dreamed of going on an adventure on one. One day, he saw something amazing: a real train! He couldn't believe it, so he rushed over. When he got there, he saw a little train all squeezed in the corner, so he decided to squeeze in too. He squeezed in and explored around the train. He marveled at the things he found and it felt like a magical place. He could not believe his luck.` But then, something terrible happened. He heard a voice and realized that somebody was coming. He was scared and tried to find a way out, but he couldn't. He was trapped! He was so scared he started to cry, and then the person arrived. It was a big bully and he grabbed the boy and squeezed him. The bully was angry and he said, "You think you can take my train?" Then he squeezed the boy tighter and tighter until the little boy couldn't move. The bully left and the little boy was still there, all alone and scared. He lay there, unable to move or call for help, until someone finally found him and took him away. The little boy never squeezed into any more trains ever again.

There was once a big, loud bird. It had lots of pretty colours, but the most beautiful colour of all was its bright yellow ear. Every morning the bird created something new. It made circles in the air and sang its loudest song. Its song was so noisy that everyone around it stopped to listen. One day it created a very special ear. It was like no other! It was shiny, rainbow-coloured and bigger than the bird's other ear. The bird was so happy with its new creation. It wanted everyone to see, so it began to fly around the area, singing out its loudest, noisiest song. And ever since then, that ear has been the pride and joy of the bird - and its loud singing is enjoyed by everyone who hears it!

Lily and Tom were playing in the garden. They liked to dig holes and find bugs. But they had to be quiet, because Mom was working inside. She said they could not make noise or she would be angry. "Look, I found a big worm!" Tom said. He showed Lily his worm. It was brown and slimy. "Wow, that's a big one!" Lily said. She wanted to touch it, but Tom did not let her. He said it was his worm. "Let me see, let me see!" Lily said. She tried to grab the worm, but Tom pulled it away. They started to fight over the worm. "Stop it, you two!" Mom said. She came out of the house. She looked very angry. She had a measure in her hand. She was measuring something for her work. "Sorry, Mom," Lily and Tom said. They stopped fighting. They did not want Mom to be more angry. "What are you doing with that worm?" Mom asked. She saw the worm in Tom's hand. She did not like worms. She thought they were gross. "It's my worm, Mom. I found it in the hole," Tom said. He held the worm tight. "Well, you can't keep it, Tom. It's not a pet. It belongs in the ground. Put it back where you found it," Mom said. "But Mom, I like it. It's my friend. Can I keep it, please?" Tom said. He made a sad face. "No, Tom. You can't keep it. It's not good for the worm. It needs to be free. Now, put it back and go play something else. And be quiet, please. I have to finish my work," Mom said. Tom did not want to put the worm back. He wanted to keep it. He had an idea. He waited until Mom went back inside. Then he whispered to Lily. "Let's escape with the worm. Let's go to the park. There we can play with the worm and no one will bother us," Tom said. "OK, let's go," Lily said. She liked the idea. She wanted to play with the worm too. They took the worm and ran to the gate. They opened the gate and ran to the street. They did not see the car coming. They did not hear the horn. They did not feel the bump. They did not escape.
