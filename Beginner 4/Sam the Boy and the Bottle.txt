Category: Beginner
Title: Sam the Boy and the Bottle

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Sam. He had a big bottle. Sam loved his bottle very much. One day, Sam met a girl named Lily. Lily was a successful helper. She helped everyone. Sam said to Lily, "Please do not disturb my bottle." Lily said, "Ok, I will not touch your bottle." They played together and had fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the bottle away. Sam was sad. But Lily had an idea. She found a new bottle for Sam. Sam was happy again. They played and laughed together. And they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very glad boy. He loved to play with his toys and have fun with his friends. One day, Tim went to the store with his mom. He saw a toy he wanted to have. But the toy had a price. Tim asked his mom, "Can I have this toy?" His mom said, "We need to save our money for other things." Tim was sad, but he knew his mom was right. He put the toy back on the shelf. Then, he saw his friend, Sam. Sam had the same toy that Tim wanted. Tim asked, "Can I play with your toy?" Sam said, "Yes, of course!" Tim and Sam played with the toy together. They had so much fun. Tim was glad that he could play with the toy, even if he didn't have it. He learned that having fun with friends is more important than the price of a toy.

One day, a white cat named Snow and a yellow dog named Sunny were playing in the park. They were best friends. They saw a big tree with a kite stuck in it. The kite had a long, pretty tail. They wanted to get the kite down. Snow said, "I can climb the tree and cut the kite free." Sunny said, "No, I can jump high and cut it down." They both tried, but they could not reach the kite. They started to argue about who should try again. A little bird saw them and asked, "Why are you fighting?" Snow and Sunny told the bird about the kite. The bird said, "I can help you get the kite down." The bird flew up and cut the kite free. Snow and Sunny were happy. They thanked the bird and played with the kite together. They learned that working together is better than fighting.

Tom and Anna were very excited. Today, their grandma would arrive. They liked to play with grandma. She always had fun stories and games. She also gave them hugs and cookies. They looked at the clock on the wall. It was almost time. They put on their coats and shoes. They helped mom and dad carry some bags to the car. They wanted to go to the airport and meet grandma. They got in the car and drove for a while. They saw many things on the way. They saw cars and trucks and buses. They saw trees and flowers and birds. They saw signs and lights and buildings. They pointed and named everything they saw. They reached the airport and parked the car. They walked to the big hall where people waited. They saw many people with suitcases and bags. They saw people hug and kiss and say hello and goodbye. They looked for grandma. They heard a loud voice say something. Mom said it was grandma's flight. They went to a gate where a plane was. They saw people come out of the plane. They looked for grandma. They saw her! She had a big smile and a red hat. She saw them too. She waved and walked to them. They ran to her and hugged her. She hugged them back. She said she was happy to see them. They said they were happy to see her too. They were all cheerful. They had grandma.

One day, a little fish named Fin was swimming in the sea. Fin was a careless fish and always forgot to listen to his mom. His mom told him, "Fin, be careful and do not swim too far away from home." One day, while Fin was swimming, he saw a big, soft bed on the sea floor. He was very tired and wanted to take a nap. Fin swam down to the bed and slept on it. He did not know that the bed belonged to a big, scary shark. When the shark came back to his bed, he saw Fin sleeping on it. The shark was very angry and said, "Who is this little fish sleeping on my bed?" But the shark saw that Fin was just a small, careless fish and decided to teach him a lesson instead of eating him. The shark woke Fin up and said, "Little fish, you should be more careful and listen to your mom." Fin was so scared and surprised. He swam back to his mom as fast as he could. From that day on, Fin was never careless again and always listened to his mom.
