Category: Beginner
Title: The Wealthy Man and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man who lived in a big house. He had a dog named Spot. One day, Spot went out to play but he did not come back. The wealthy man was sad and went to look for him. The wealthy man met a little girl named Sue. Sue said, "I saw Spot! He went that way!" and pointed to the left. The wealthy man thanked Sue and went to the left. He listened carefully for any sounds of Spot. As he walked, the wealthy man heard a bark. He followed the sound and found Spot stuck in a hole. The wealthy man helped Spot get out of the hole. Spot was happy to see him and barked, "Thank you!" The wealthy man and Spot went back home, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in an ancient forest, there lived a big bear named Bobo. Bobo loved to jog through the trees and play with his friends. He was very strong and always ready to protect them from danger. One sunny day, Bobo and his friends were playing near the river. They saw a small bunny stuck on a rock in the water. The bunny was scared and needed help. Bobo knew he had to protect the bunny, so he came up with a plan. Bobo asked his friends to find a big, strong branch. Then, he held the branch with his strong paws and reached out to the bunny. The bunny grabbed the branch and Bobo pulled him to safety. They all cheered and thanked Bobo for protecting the little bunny. From that day on, Bobo and the bunny became best friends, and they all continued to jog and play happily in the ancient forest.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a gentle rabbit. This rabbit had soft gray fur. He loved to hop around and eat the green grass that grew in the forest. One day, while he was eating, he met a little bird. "Hi, rabbit!" said the bird. "I am trying to grow big and strong like you. What do you eat?" The rabbit smiled and said, "I eat the green grass that grows here. It helps me grow big and strong." The bird became excited and started to eat the grass too. They both ate and ate, hoping to grow big and strong. But something unexpected happened. Instead of growing big and strong like the rabbit, the little bird turned gray just like the rabbit! They both looked at each other and laughed. The bird said, "I guess I should eat worms to grow big and strong like other birds." From that day on, the gentle gray rabbit and the gray bird became the best of friends. They played and laughed together in the big green forest.

Mum was sitting in her chair, looking at her radio. She said, "This radio isn't working right. I don't understand why it's so weak." She turned the knob on the radio, but it was still quiet. Mum's daughter came up to her and asked, "What's wrong, Mum?" Mum showed her the radio and said, "This isn't working properly. It's too weak." The little girl looked at it and said, "Let me try!" She grabbed the knob and turned it to the right. Suddenly, the radio came on and music filled the room. "Wow!" said the little girl. Mum smiled. "You understood what was wrong! I think this radio is going to work now!" The little girl was proud she could help. She knew that she had understood what the problem was.

Lily and Ben were hungry. They wanted to eat pizza. They saw a big pizza box on the table. It smelled good. "Let's catch the pizza!" Lily said. "OK!" Ben said. They ran to the table. They tried to grab the pizza box. But the pizza box was not real. It was a trap. A big net fell on them. They were stuck. "Ha ha ha!" a voice said. It was Sam, their big brother. He was hiding behind the sofa. He had made the fake pizza box. He wanted to play a prank on them. "You are foolish!" Sam said. "You fell for my trick. Now you are my prisoners. And I will eat the real pizza by myself." Lily and Ben felt sad and angry. They did not like Sam's prank. They wanted to eat pizza. They wanted to get out of the net. But they could not. They had to wait for Mom and Dad to come home. They hoped Mom and Dad would scold Sam and give them some pizza. But they did not know when that would happen. They had a bad day.
