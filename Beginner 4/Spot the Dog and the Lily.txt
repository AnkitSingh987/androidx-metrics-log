Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog and the Lily

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was sad because he lost his favorite toy. It was a soft strip of cloth that he loved to play with. He looked all around his house, but he could not find it. Spot felt very sad. One day, Spot went for a walk in the park. He was still sad about his lost toy. As he walked, he saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was playing with a strip of cloth that looked just like Spot's toy! Spot ran to Lily and wagged his tail. Lily saw how happy the strip of cloth made Spot, so she gave it to him. Spot was so happy and thankful. That night, Spot went to sleep with his favorite toy. He was not sad anymore. Spot and Lily became best friends, and they played together every day. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very hungry and wanted a sandwich. He asked his mom to make him a sandwich with lots of cheese and ham. As Tim waited for his sandwich, he became impatient. He wanted his sandwich now, not later! So, he decided to make the sandwich himself. He put lots of cheese and ham on the bread. Tim was so excited that he put more and more cheese and ham. The sandwich started to increase in size! Finally, Tim's big sandwich was ready. He took a big bite and smiled. It was the best sandwich ever. His mom saw the big sandwich and laughed. She was happy that Tim could make his own sandwich. From that day on, Tim enjoyed making sandwiches for himself and his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a fancy red ball. The ball lived in a big yard with a fence. It loved to play with the kids who lived in the house. One day, the ball saw a cat. The cat was on the other side of the fence. The ball wanted to play with the cat. So, it asked the kids, "Can I play with the cat, please?" The kids said, "Yes, but be careful not to split." The ball was so happy. It jumped over the fence to play with the cat. They had a lot of fun. But then, the ball hit a rock and split. The kids were sad, but they got a new ball to play with. And they all played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a swan named Sally. Sally was a spoiled swan who lived in a big pond. She loved to swim all day and play with her friends. One day, Sally met a new friend named Timmy the Turtle. Timmy asked Sally, "Do you want to play with me?" Sally replied, "Yes, I want to play with you!" They played together all day and had lots of fun. As they played, Sally learned to share and not be so spoiled. She became a better friend to Timmy and all her other friends too. They all lived happily in the big pond.

Lily likes to play dress-up with her toys. She has a crown that is shiny and pretty. She puts it on her head and pretends to be a queen. She tells her toys what to do and they listen to her. One day, she sees a big mud puddle in the yard. She thinks it looks fun. She runs to the puddle and jumps in. She splashes and laughs. She does not see that her crown falls off her head and into the mud. When she is done playing, she looks for her crown. She can't find it. She feels sad. She thinks she lost her crown. She cries and goes inside. Her mom sees her and hugs her. She asks her what is wrong. Lily tells her she lost her crown. Her mom says she will help her look for it. They go outside and look around. They find the crown in the mud. It is dirty and wet. Lily is happy to see it, but she is also sorry. She says she is sorry for making her crown dirty. Her mom says it is okay. She says they can wash it and make it shiny again. She says she loves Lily and her crown. Lily smiles and hugs her mom. She says she loves her mom and her crown too.
