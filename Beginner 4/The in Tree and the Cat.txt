Title: The in Tree and the Cat

One day, a yellow bird and a yellow cat lived in a big tree. They did not like each other. The bird liked to sing and the cat liked to sleep. The bird's song woke the cat up every day. The cat was mad. One hot day, the sun was very bright. The yellow bird and the yellow cat were both hot. They both needed shade to cool down. But there was only one big leaf in the tree for shade. The bird and the cat both wanted the shade. Then, something happened. A big wind came and blew the big leaf away. The yellow bird and the yellow cat were both sad. They decided to unite and find the big leaf together. They looked and looked until they found the big leaf on the ground. The yellow bird and the yellow cat carried the big leaf back to the tree. They put the big leaf in the tree for shade. Now, they both had shade and were not hot anymore. The bird and the cat became friends and lived happily in the big tree.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved ice. He liked to take ice from the freezer and play with it. One day, Tim saw a big ice truck outside his house. He was very glad. The ice truck driver saw Tim and said, "Hi, little boy! Do you like ice?" Tim said, "Yes, I love ice! Can I take some ice from your truck?" The driver smiled and said, "Of course! Here you go!" He gave Tim a big piece of ice. Tim took the ice and played with it all day. He showed it to his friends, and they all had fun. They made ice slides and watched the ice melt in the sun. At the end of the day, Tim said, "Thank you, ice truck driver!" The driver said, "You're welcome! Have a good day!" Tim was very glad and went home to tell his mom about his fun day with the ice.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to play ball. One day, he went to the park to play with his friends. They saw a big sign that said, "Do not play ball here." But they did not care. They wanted to play ball. Tim had a cheap ball that he brought from home. He threw the ball high in the sky. His friend, Sam, was ready to strike the ball with a bat. When Sam hit the ball, something unexpected happened. The ball did not go far. Instead, it went up and then came back down very fast. The ball hit the big sign and broke it into pieces. The kids were scared. They did not know what to do. Then, a man came and said, "Do not worry, kids. That sign was old and wrong. You can play ball here now." The kids were happy and played ball all day long.
