Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Duck

Lily and Ben went to the park with Mom. They saw many ducks in the pond. Lily and Ben liked the ducks. They wanted to feed them bread. "Can we feed the ducks, Mom?" Lily asked. "Sure, but be careful. Don't go too close to the water. And don't try to hold the ducks. They might bite you," Mom said. Lily and Ben nodded. They took some bread from Mom and threw it to the ducks. The ducks came closer and ate the bread. They quacked and splashed. Lily saw a small duck that was yellow and fluffy. She thought it was cute. She wanted to hold it. She forgot what Mom said. She walked to the edge of the pond and reached out her hand. "Come here, little duck. I want to hold you," Lily said. But the little duck was naughty. It did not want to be held. It bit Lily's finger. Lily cried out. She let go of the duck and ran back to Mom. "Ow, ow, ow! The duck bit me!" Lily said. Mom hugged Lily and looked at her finger. It was red and sore. "I'm sorry, Lily. That was a naughty duck. But you should have listened to me. You should not try to hold the ducks. They are wild animals. They don't like to be touched," Mom said. Lily nodded. She was sorry too. She learned her lesson. She did not try to hold the ducks again. She and Ben fed them bread from a distance. They had fun at the park. They said goodbye to the ducks and went home.

Once upon a time, there was a store. In this store, there were many tasty things to eat. There were fruits, cookies, and other yummy treats. People loved to go to this store. One day, two groups of kids came to the store. They wanted to play a game. They called it "The Tasty War." They would take the tasty treats and throw them at each other. The kids laughed and had fun. One little girl said, "I love this war! It's so much fun!" Another little boy said, "Me too! But we should clean up after." So, when the game was over, all the kids helped clean the store. They put the tasty treats back where they belonged. The store was nice and clean again. Everyone was happy.

Once upon a time, in a quiet little town, there was a big bush. The bush was very pretty with many colors. All the kids in the town liked to play near the bush. One day, a small bunny hopped to the bush. The bunny was very happy. The bunny liked to enjoy the bush too. It played and jumped around the bush. The kids saw the bunny and smiled. The kids and the bunny became friends. They all enjoyed the quiet bush together. They played and laughed every day. The bush, the bunny, and the kids were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful dog named Max. Max liked to run and play with his friends. One day, Max and his friends were talking about their favorite game. Max had an opinion about the best game to play. He said, "I think running is the best game." His friends listened and thought about his opinion. They all liked to run too. So, Max and his friends decided to play a running game. They ran and ran, having lots of fun. They were happy they listened to Max's thoughtful opinion. And from that day on, they played the running game every day.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Mia went to the ocean with her mom and dad. The sun was yellow and bright in the sky. Mia liked to pick up shells and rocks to take home. One day, Mia saw a big yellow shell far away on the beach. She wanted to pick it up and show her mom and dad. Mia was very excited and ran towards the big yellow shell. When Mia picked up the big yellow shell, she found out it was not a shell at all! It was a yellow hat that looked like a shell. Mia put the hat on her head and laughed. Her mom and dad laughed too. They all had a fun day at the ocean, and Mia had a new yellow hat to take home.
