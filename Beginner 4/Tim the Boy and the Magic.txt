Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Magic

Once upon a time, in a cheerful little house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys all day long. He had a big red ball, a soft teddy bear, and a funny toy car. One day, Tim saw a new toy on a high shelf. He could not reach it, so he called his mommy. "Mommy, can you help me get the toy?" he asked. His mommy came and saw the toy. She pointed at the toy and said, "That is a magic toy, Tim! It has a special button on it." Tim's mommy took the toy down from the shelf and gave it to him. Tim looked at the toy and found the button. He pushed the button and the toy started to sing and dance! Tim was so happy and he danced with the toy all day long. They had so much fun together, and Tim loved his new magic toy.

Once upon a time, there was a good dog named Max. Max liked to play with his toy skull. He would pick it up in his mouth and drop it. Then, he would run and pick it up again. One day, Max was playing with his toy skull in the park. He dropped it near a big tree. But when he went to pick it up, the toy skull was gone! Max looked all around, but he could not find it. Suddenly, a little bird came flying down from the tree. The bird had Max's toy skull in its beak! The bird dropped the skull right in front of Max. Max was so happy! He and the bird became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big piano. It was in a small room. A girl named Amy loved to play the piano. She played it every day. The piano made her very happy. One day, Amy wanted to write a song. She thought of a sour lemon. The sour lemon made her face funny. She laughed and started to write her song. The song was about the sour lemon. Amy played her sour lemon song on the piano. She played it very loud. The noise was too much for the piano. The piano broke into pieces. Amy was very sad. She could not play the piano anymore. The end.

Anna and Ben were twins who liked to play with paper and tape. They liked to wrap things with paper and tape and make them look different. One day, they found a big box in the garage. They decided to wrap it with paper and tape and make it look like a building. They used blue paper for the sky, green paper for the grass, and brown paper for the walls and roof. They cut out windows and doors with scissors and taped them on. They drew flowers and trees and people with crayons. They were very happy with their building. But their dad was not happy. He was grumpy. He wanted to use the box for something else. He came to the garage and saw the building. He said, "What have you done? You ruined my box! You made a mess! You have to clean it up!" Anna and Ben were sad. They did not want to clean up their building. They liked their building. They said, "But dad, we made a building! It is pretty! We had fun!" Their dad looked at their building again. He saw the blue sky, the green grass, the brown walls and roof, the windows and doors, the flowers and trees, and the people. He saw how much work they put into it. He saw how proud they were of it. He felt sorry for being grumpy. He said, "I'm sorry, Anna and Ben. I was grumpy. I did not see your building. It is pretty. You had fun. You can keep your building. But can you help me find another box?" Anna and Ben smiled. They said, "Yes, dad. We can help you find another box. Thank you for letting us keep our building." They hugged their dad and went to look for another box. They were happy. Their dad was happy. Their building was happy.
