Category: Beginner
Title: The Ranch and the Sheep

Once upon a time, there was a big ranch. On the ranch, there were cows, sheep, and horses. The ranch was very fun and busy. One day, a tired horse named Max needed a break. Max was always running and playing with his friends. But today, he was very tired. Max wanted to lay down and rest. Max found a nice spot under a big tree. He laid down and closed his eyes. Soon, Max felt better. He got up and went back to play with his friends. Now, Max knew that when he was tired, he needed to rest. And everyone at the ranch lived happily ever after.

Lily and Ben were playing in the kitchen. They wanted to make a cake for Mom. They had flour, eggs, milk and sugar. But they needed something else. "Ben, we need salt for the cake. Mom always puts salt in the cake. It makes it yummy," Lily said. "OK, Lily. I will supply the salt. You mix the flour and the eggs," Ben said. Ben looked for the salt. He saw a big jar on the shelf. He climbed on a chair and reached for the jar. But the jar was too heavy for him. He dropped the jar and it broke on the floor. Salt spilled everywhere. "Oh no, Ben! You made a big mess. Look at all the salt. It is fine and white. It looks like snow," Lily said. "Sorry, Lily. I did not mean to drop the jar. I wanted to supply the salt for the cake. But now we have no salt. And Mom will be mad at us," Ben said. Lily and Ben were sad. They did not know what to do. They heard Mom coming home. They hid behind the table. "Hello, kids. I'm home. What are you doing in the kitchen?" Mom said. She saw the broken jar and the salt on the floor. She was surprised. "Wow, what happened here? Did you try to make a cake?" Mom said. Lily and Ben came out from behind the table. They looked at Mom with big eyes. "Mom, we are sorry. We wanted to make a cake for you. But we dropped the salt. We made a big mess. We are sorry," Lily and Ben said. Mom smiled. She hugged Lily and Ben. "It's OK, kids. I'm not mad at you. I'm happy that you wanted to make a cake for me. That's very sweet of you. But next time, please ask me for help. Salt is not good for cakes. It makes them salty. And you have to be careful with jars. They can break and hurt you," Mom said. "OK, Mom. We will ask you for help next time. We love you, Mom," Lily and Ben said. Mom cleaned up the mess. She found some chocolate and sprinkles. She helped Lily and Ben make a new cake. It was yummy and pretty. They ate the cake together. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to count. One day, he went to a big wall. The wall was in an unknown place. Tim had never been there before. He saw many things on the wall. He saw a cat, a dog, and a bird. Tim said, "I want to count them all!" Tim started to count. He counted one cat, one dog, and one bird. But as he counted, something unexpected happened. The cat, the dog, and the bird started to talk! They said, "Thank you for counting us, Tim. We were so lonely on this wall!" Tim was surprised. He didn't know that animals could talk! He asked, "How can you talk?" The cat, the dog, and the bird told Tim that they were magic. They could only talk when someone counted them. Now, Tim had new friends. They all played together and had fun. And every day, Tim counted the cat, the dog, and the bird, so they could talk and play. And they all lived happily ever after.

Hannah was a very happy little girl. She loved exploring her world and playing in her garden. One day she was walking in her garden when she saw a little white bunny hop across the land. "Oh!", she said. She wanted to follow it. She started to laugh and ran after it. The bunny hopped faster and faster. "Hurry, Hannah, hurry!" it said. Hannah could barely keep up with it. Finally, the rabbit stopped and went in a bush. She was so excited! When she very cautiously peered in, she saw the bunny eating something sweet. She was so happy. The bunny saw Hannah peeking in and said, "Come on in, Hannah - I've saved some for you too!" Hannah happily joined the bunny in the bush, munching away on the sweet treat. She never forgot this moment in her garden.
