Category: Beginner
Title: Leo the Gifted Cat and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a gifted cat named Leo. Leo was very smart and could do many things. One day, Leo found a phone in the park. The phone was big and shiny. Leo wanted to find the owner of the phone, so he took it with him. As Leo walked, he met a dog named Max. Max was friendly and liked to play. Leo showed Max the phone and asked, "Is this your phone?" Max shook his head and said, "No, that's not mine. But let's find who it belongs to." Together, Leo and Max looked for the phone's owner. They asked a bird, a squirrel, and a rabbit, but no one knew whose phone it was. Finally, they found a little girl named Emma, who was crying. She had lost her phone while playing. Leo and Max went to Emma and asked, "Is this your phone?" Emma looked at the phone and her face lit up. "Yes, that's my phone! Thank you for finding it!" she said happily. Leo and Max were glad to help. They lay the phone in Emma's hands and watched her smile. From that day on, they all became good friends and played together in the park.

Mommy was pouring something yummy into a bowl. She gave the bowl to her little girl. The little girl was so happy. It was a bowl of cookie mix! Next, Mommy poured some milk into the bowl. The little girl watched carefully, then giggled when the milk made a splash. Mommy got a big spoon and then used it to stir the cookie mix. The little girl couldn't wait - it was going to be so yummy! Mommy poured the cookie mix into a tray and put it in the oven. The little girl danced around and sang, waiting for the cookies to be done. Finally, the cookies were ready. They smelled so yummy! The little girl and Mommy ate the cookies together, and the little girl was so happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was very patient and liked to play a game called "match". One day, Tim's mom said to him, "Tim, we will play 'match' next week, okay?". Tim was so happy and he waited for the next week. The week went by so slow, but Tim was still patient. He played with his toys and thought about how fun it would be to play "match" with his mom. Finally, the day came when it was time to play the game. Tim ran to his mom and said, "Mom, let's play 'match' now!" But his mom looked sad. She said, "I'm sorry, Tim. I have to go to work today, so we cannot play 'match'." Tim was very sad and cried. He didn't understand why his mom had to go to work. Tim went to his room and played with his toys, but he was not happy. The next day, Tim's mom came home from work. She said, "Tim, I am so sorry. I promise we will play 'match' tomorrow." But Tim was still sad. He didn't want to wait anymore. The next day, Tim and his mom tried to play "match". But then it started to rain, and they couldn't play outside. Tim was so sad that he cried again. He didn't get to play "match" with his mom, and it was a bad ending for Tim.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a mysterious box in her room. She opened the box and found a toy bike with shiny pedals. She was very happy and wanted to ride the bike. Lily put the toy bike on the floor and tried to ride it. But she was too big for the bike. The bike started to suffer and the pedals were not moving. Lily was sad and didn't know what to do. Then, Lily had an idea. She called her little brother, Tim. Tim was just the right size for the toy bike. He sat on the bike and pushed the pedals. The bike moved and Tim was very happy. Lily was happy too because her brother could enjoy the mysterious toy bike.
