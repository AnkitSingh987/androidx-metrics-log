Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Lily

One day, a little girl named Mia was playing in her room. She was very happy. She liked to play with her doll, Lily. Mia wanted to find new clothes for Lily, so she started to think. Mia looked in her toy box and found a pretty dress for Lily. She put the dress on Lily and said, "Lily, you look so nice in your new clothes!" Lily did not talk, but Mia knew that Lily was happy too. Mia and Lily played all day. They had a tea party and danced together. Mia was very happy with her best friend, Lily. At the end of the day, Mia gave Lily a hug and said, "I love you, Lily. We had a fun day!"

One day, a boy named Tom went to play outside. He took his light blue spade with him. Tom loved to dig in the dirt with his spade. He found a big hill and started to dig. As he dug, a little worm came out of the dirt. The worm said, "Please, Tom, don't dig my home!" Tom stopped and looked at the worm. He felt bad for the worm. Tom urged the worm to find a new home. He said, "Let's find you a new home, little worm." They looked and looked until they found a nice spot under a big tree. Tom used his spade to make a new home for the worm. The worm was happy and thanked Tom. They became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was an attractive plane. The plane loved to fly high in the sky. It was very happy when it could go fast and see all the people down below. One day, the plane met a big bird. The bird was not nice. It wanted to chew the plane's wings. The plane was scared and did not want the bird to chew its wings. So, the plane tried to fly away very fast. But then, something unexpected happened. The bird saw a big worm and wanted to eat it. The bird forgot about the plane and went to chew the worm instead. The plane was safe and could fly high in the sky again.

Once upon a time, there was a great big room with a soft carpet. In the middle of the carpet, there was a small seat. Every day, a little girl named Lucy would come to the room to play with her toys. One day, Lucy brought her favorite toy, a teddy bear named Mr. Snuggles. She put Mr. Snuggles on the seat and said, "You sit here, Mr. Snuggles. You can watch me play." Then, Lucy played with her blocks on the carpet. After a while, Lucy got tired and sat down on the seat next to Mr. Snuggles. She gave him a big hug and said, "I had so much fun playing on the great carpet with you today! Let's come back tomorrow and play again." And so they did, and they had many more happy days playing together in the great big room.

Once upon a time, there was a shy boat. The boat lived in a big port with other boats. The shy boat was sad because it did not have any friends. It wanted to play with the other boats, but it was too scared to say hello. One day, a big storm came to the port. The wind blew and the rain fell. The other boats were scared. The shy boat saw them and wanted to help. It made a big promise to itself. "I will be brave and help my friends," said the shy boat. The shy boat went to the other boats and helped them. It pulled them to a safe place in the port. The storm went away, and the other boats were happy. They thanked the shy boat and said, "You are a good friend." The shy boat was not shy anymore, and it had many friends to play with.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Max, went to a big place called a stadium. Max was a loyal dog and always stayed by Tim's side. They liked to watch games and have fun together. While they were at the stadium, they saw a man point at something far away. Tim looked and saw a big ball coming their way. "Max, catch the ball!" Tim said. Max ran very fast to catch the ball. But something unexpected happened. Max did not see a big hole in the ground and fell in. Tim ran to the hole and called for Max, but Max didn't come out. Tim was very sad. He asked people to help, but they could not get Max out. The sun went down, and it was time to go home. Tim had to leave Max behind. The moral of the story is to always be careful and watch where you are going. If Max had looked where he was going, he would not have fallen into the hole, and Tim would not be sad.
