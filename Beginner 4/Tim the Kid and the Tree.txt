Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Kid and the Tree

One day, a kid named Tim went to the park. He wanted to play with his friends. But when he got there, he saw that no one was at the park. Tim felt sad because he thought it was boring to play alone. Then, Tim saw a big tree. He realized he could have fun by climbing the tree. He started to climb the tree and felt happy. But when he got high up, he got scared. He didn't know how to get down. Tim called for help. A nice lady heard him and came to the tree. She helped Tim get down from the tree. Tim was happy to be safe. He learned that even if he was alone, he could still have fun, but he needed to be careful.

One day, a girl named Lucy went to play outside. She saw her mom's pretty blouse hanging on a line. The blouse was red and blue. Lucy wanted to wear it to feel like a big girl. Lucy put on the blouse, but it was too big. She went to the yard and saw many leaves. Lucy had an idea. She would scatter the leaves and make a big pile. "I will jump in the leaves and have fun!" Lucy said. Lucy's mom came outside and saw Lucy in her blouse. "Lucy, that is my blouse," her mom said. "You should be honest and ask before you take things." Lucy felt bad and said, "I am sorry, Mom. I will ask next time." Mom hugged Lucy and they played in the leaves together.

Once upon a time, in a peaceful bamboo forest, there lived a panda named Pandy. Pandy loved to eat bamboo and play with his friends. One day, it started to rain. The rain made the forest wet and fun to play in. Pandy saw his friend, Bunny, hopping in the rain. Pandy called out, "Hi Bunny! Do you want to play in the rain with me?" Bunny was happy and said, "Yes, Pandy! Let's play and have fun!" So Pandy and Bunny played in the rain. They splashed in puddles and danced under the raindrops. They laughed and had a great time. When the rain stopped, they went home to rest. Pandy and Bunny were happy they played in the rain together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They walked by a big park. In the park, there was a smooth statue. The statue was of a man with a big yawn. Tim asked his mom, "Why is the man yawning?" His mom said, "The man is tired from helping others all day. He shows that it is good to help and care for others, even when we are tired." Tim and his mom walked more and saw a little girl who dropped her toy. Tim picked it up and gave it to her. The girl said, "Thank you!" Tim felt happy to help, just like the yawning statue.

Once there was a foolish King. He wanted to test all of his subjects, so he gave his subjects a test. The test was very tough and the King wanted to see how well his people could do. Most of the people didn't pass the test. They were so sad and scared. But one brave boy named Bob did pass the test. He was so happy! The King was so proud of Bob that he made him the new King! Everyone cheered for Bob and he was very excited to be the new King. Bob did his best to rule the kingdom. He was a kind and wise King who always listened to the people. He never gave any foolish tests, and everyone lived happily ever after!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a thin dress that she loved to wear. One day, she got a present from her mom. She was very happy and excited to see what was inside. Sue opened the present and found a new dress. She liked the new dress, but she loved her thin dress more. She put on her thin dress and went outside to play. While she was playing, the dress got dirty and had a big tear. Sue was very sad. She went back home and showed her mom the thin dress. Her mom could not fix the dress. Sue cried and wished she had worn her new dress. Now, she had no thin dress to wear and felt very sad.
