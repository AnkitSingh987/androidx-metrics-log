Category: Beginner
Title: The Secret of the Cat and the Toys

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the yard. The cat saw a can that could spray water. The dog found a toy that looked like beef. The cat said, "Look at my spray can!" The dog said, "Look at my fake beef!" They played with their new toys. The cat sprayed water on the dog. The dog laughed and threw the fake beef for the cat to catch. They had lots of fun. Then, something unexpected happened. The fake beef fell into a big puddle. The cat and the dog were sad. But the cat had an idea. The cat used the spray can to clean the fake beef. They were happy again and kept playing.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Tom. Tom was three years old and liked to explore. One day, he went on an adventure to the park. While he was at the park, Tom looked around. He saw a big tree with lots of leaves. It looked very interesting, so he decided to hide underneath the tree. He was very quiet but he could still see the other kids playing. Tom stayed under the tree for a whole year. He had a lot of fun. He saw lots of strange and beautiful things while he was underneath the tree. He kept it secret so nobody else would know he was there. One day, he finally decided to come out from under the tree. Everyone was so surprised to see him! He was very clean, too. That was because he had been hiding under the clean leaves for a whole year. Tom was very happy to be back with all his friends. He had had a wonderful time exploring and hiding at the park!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a slow walker, but he loved to play outside with his friends. One day, he saw a pretty flower with a big bulb in his garden. Tim wanted to show the flower to his friend, Sue. He went to Sue's house and asked, "Sue, can you come and see my flower? It has a big bulb!" Sue was excited and said, "Yes, I want to see it!" They walked back to Tim's garden, but Tim was slow. Sue said, "Tim, can I lend you my fast shoes? You can walk faster with them." Tim was happy and said, "Thank you, Sue!" They put the fast shoes on Tim, and he walked faster. When they reached the garden, Tim and Sue looked at the flower with the big bulb. They smiled and played together all day. Tim was happy that he could share his flower with Sue and that Sue lent him her fast shoes.

Once upon a time, there was a smart cat named Tim. Tim was very good at making friends. One day, Tim met a big dog named Max. Max liked to trade toys with his friends. Tim wanted to trade toys with Max too. Tim had a small ball and Max had a big bone. They decided to trade their toys. Tim was very happy with his new toy, but Max was not. The big bone was too big for Tim, and the small ball was too small for Max. Max felt embarrass because he gave Tim a toy that was too big for him. Tim saw that Max was sad, so he had an idea. Tim found another friend who had a toy that was just right for Max. They traded toys again, and everyone was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. He was a happy youth, always glad to play with his friends. One day, Tim and his friends went to the park to play. They had to choose which game to play. Tim wanted to play ball, but his friend, Sam, wanted to play tag. "Let's play tag," Sam said. Tim agreed, and they started to play. They ran around the park, laughing and having fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A little dog joined their game of tag. The dog was very fast and could catch everyone. The kids were surprised and laughed even more. They played with the dog all day. When it was time to go home, the dog's owner came to the park. The owner was glad to see her dog having fun with the kids. She said, "Thank you for playing with my dog. He loves to play tag too!" From that day on, Tim, Sam, and their friends always invited the little dog to play with them. They were all very happy and became the best of friends.
