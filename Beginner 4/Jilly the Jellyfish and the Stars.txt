Category: Beginner
Title: Jilly the Jellyfish and the Stars

One day, a pretty jellyfish named Jilly was swimming in the sea. She liked to wave her long, pretty legs to say hello to her friends. Jilly had many friends in the sea, like fish, crabs, and sea stars. One day, Jilly met a new friend named Sam. Sam was a small fish with bright colors. Jilly waved her legs to say hello. "Hi, I'm Jilly the jellyfish!" she said. Sam smiled and said, "Hi Jilly! I'm Sam the fish. Do you want to play with me?" Jilly and Sam played together all day. They swam through the water and chased each other. They were very happy. When it was time to go home, Jilly waved her legs to say goodbye. "Bye, Sam! See you tomorrow!" she said. Sam waved his tail and said, "Bye, Jilly! I had fun today!"

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing outside. He saw a big orange ball in the sky. It was very pretty. Tim wanted to talk to the orange ball. He looked up and said, "Hi, orange ball! How are you?" The orange ball looked troubled and replied, "Hi, Tim. I am not feeling well. I am very hot and tired." Tim was surprised! He did not know the orange ball could talk. He wanted to help the orange ball. He thought and thought. Then, he got an idea. Tim ran inside and got a big fan. He turned the fan on and pointed it at the orange ball. The orange ball started to feel better. It smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim! I feel much better now." Tim was very happy that he could help the orange ball. They became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a crab named Timmy. Timmy lived on a beach with many other crabs. All the crabs liked to find the best shells to wear. They were always competitive about it. One day, Timmy found a very pretty shell. He was so happy and showed it to his friend, Sally the crab. Sally said, "Wow, Timmy! That is a nice shell. I want to find one, too!" Timmy and Sally looked for more pretty shells together. Soon, Timmy and Sally fell in love. They decided to marry each other. All the crabs on the beach came to their wedding. They learned that it was more fun to share and help each other than to be competitive. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a big red bus went on a tour. The bus had many kids on it. They were all happy and excited. The bus drove deep into the woods. The kids saw many trees and animals. They all sang songs and played games. As they went deeper into the woods, they saw a big tree. The tree was very old and had a big hole in it. The hole was so deep that they could not see the bottom. The kids wanted to see what was inside the hole. They all got off the bus and went to the tree. They looked into the hole and saw something strange. It was a big pile of fruit, but the fruit was all rot. The kids were sad because they wanted to eat the fruit. Just then, a friendly squirrel came out of the hole. The squirrel said, "I can help you!" The squirrel ran fast and brought back fresh fruit for the kids. The kids were so happy and thanked the squirrel. The tour ended, and the big red bus took the kids back home. They all had a great day and would never forget the friendly squirrel who saved their day.

Sara and Ben liked to play in the park. They liked to slide, swing, and run. One day, they saw a big pile of ash near a bench. It was dry and black. "What is this?" Sara asked. "I don't know. Maybe it is dirt," Ben said. "Let's play with it. It looks fun," Sara said. She took a stick and poked the ash. It made a cloud of dust. She coughed and rubbed her eyes. "Ow, it hurts!" she cried. Ben tried to help her. He gave her his water bottle. She drank some and washed her face. "Are you okay?" he asked. "I think so. But the ash is not fun. It is bad," she said. They heard a voice behind them. It was a man in a uniform. He had a hose and a helmet. "Hey, kids, what are you doing here? This is not a place to play. This is the remains of a fire. It is dangerous and dirty. You could get hurt or sick," he said. He sprayed some water on the ash. It made a hissing sound. "Sorry, sir. We didn't know. We were curious," Ben said. "Curiosity is good, but you have to be careful. Always ask an adult before you touch something you don't know. And stay away from fire and ash. They can burn and harm you," he said. He led them to their parents, who were waiting for them. They hugged them and checked them. "We're sorry, mom and dad. We learned our lesson. We won't play with ash again," Sara and Ben said. "That's good, kids. We're glad you're safe. And we're proud of you for telling the truth and apologizing. Now let's go home and have some ice cream," their parents said. They smiled and followed them. They were happy and grateful. They learned to be careful and respectful. And they still liked to play in the park. But not with ash.
