Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Castle

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a girl named Sue playing with some dough. Tim was curious and asked his mom, "What is Sue doing?" Mom said, "Sue is playing with dough. She is making something original." Tim wanted to play too. Mom mentioned, "You can play with Sue, but remember to be nice and share." Tim went to Sue and asked, "Can I play with you?" Sue said, "Yes, let's make something fun!" They played together and made a big, original dough castle. Later, it started to rain. Tim and Sue were sad because their dough castle would get wet. But then, Tim remembered what his mom mentioned about being nice. He said, "Let's take our castle home and share it with our families!" So, Tim and Sue took their original dough castle home. They were happy because they had fun and shared their creation. And they knew they could make more castles on sunny days.

Once upon a time, there was a smart cat named Tom. Tom loved to eat big meals. He ate so much that he started to weigh a lot. One day, Tom saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. But he could not because he was too heavy. Tom's friend, a little bird named Sue, saw him trying to climb the tree. Sue told Tom that if he ate smaller meals, he would not weigh so much. Tom thought about it and decided to try. He wanted to be able to climb the tree and play with Sue. Days went by, and Tom started to eat smaller meals. He began to weigh less and less. One day, Tom tried to climb the tree again. This time, he made it all the way to the top! Tom was so happy that he listened to Sue's smart advice. From that day on, Tom and Sue played together in the tree every day.

The baby was very excited, he wanted dessert. He asked his mum if he could have some, she said yes and smiled. The baby put on his apron and went to the kitchen. He found some berries in the pantry and he put them in a bowl. He filled up the bowl with milk and stirred it slowly. He watched as the milk started to soak into the berries. When it was ready, the baby took the bowl to the table and sat down. He spooned the dessert into a plate and smiled with delight. He ate it up as quickly as he could and asked for more. Mum said maybe next time, then gave him a big hug. He hugged her back, feeling warm and content. Then he scooped up the last few berries, taking his time to enjoy them. He sure loved his dessert!

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They saw a healthy tree with big green leaves. Tim saw a small bird sitting on a branch. The bird looked sad. Tim asked his mom, "Why is the bird sad?" His mom said, "The bird is hungry. We can help by giving it some food." Tim's mom took some seeds from her palm and gave them to Tim. Tim held out his palm and the bird flew down to eat the seeds. The bird was happy and started to sing. Tim and his mom walked back home. Tim said, "I like helping the bird. It made me feel good." His mom nodded and said, "Yes, helping others is a good thing to do. It makes everyone happy." Tim learned that being kind and helping others is important.

Once upon a time, there was a little red car. The car loved to speed down the road. It would go fast, faster, and fastest. The car was very enthusiastic about going fast. It would say "Vroom! Vroom!" as it sped down the road. One day, the little red car saw a row of trees. The trees were tall and green. The car wanted to speed past the row of trees. So, it went fast, faster, and fastest. The car was so enthusiastic that it said "Vroom! Vroom!" louder than ever before. As the little red car sped past the row of trees, it saw a big blue sky. The car felt happy and free. It knew that it could speed down the road and be enthusiastic forever. And that's what the little red car did, always saying "Vroom! Vroom!" as it went fast, faster, and fastest.
