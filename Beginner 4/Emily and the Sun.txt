Category: Beginner
Title: Emily and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a regular girl called Emily. Emily liked to explore the world around her and every day she would look for something new. One day, Emily found a copper bucket and she decided to take it home. Once home, Emily filled the copper bucket with water and set it in the sun. She watched as the water in the copper bucket slowly filled up with sunshine and soon it was full. Emily was so excited, she knew the sunshine would fill her bucket with warmth. From then on, Emily made sure to fill the copper bucket with sunshine every day. It always made her feel so happy, like she had a secret place filled with warmth and love. The End

Once upon a time, there was a big cat named Tom. Tom was an easy-going cat who liked to stare at the sky. One day, while Tom was staring at the sky, he saw a big bird. The bird was very pretty and Tom wanted to be friends with it. As Tom tried to get closer to the bird, the bird flew away. Tom felt sad and sat down. He stared at the sky for a long time, hoping the bird would come back. But the bird did not come back. Then, something unexpected happened. A small bug appeared right in front of Tom. The bug said, "Hi, I am Bob. I can be your friend." Tom was surprised but happy. He learned that friends can come in all shapes and sizes. And from that day on, Tom and Bob were best friends.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play with stamps. They have many stamps with animals and colors. They stick the stamps on paper and make pictures. One day, Tom has a new stamp. It is a stamp with a dead bug. It is brown and ugly. Tom thinks it is funny. He wants to joke with Lily. He waits for Lily to come to his house. He puts the stamp on his hand. He hides his hand behind his back. He says, "Lily, I have something for you. It is a surprise. Do you want to see it?" Lily is curious. She likes surprises. She says, "Yes, please. What is it?" Tom shows his hand. He says, "Look, it is a dead bug. Do you like it?" Lily screams. She is scared. She does not like dead bugs. She says, "No, no, no. I do not like it. It is gross. Take it away." Tom laughs. He says, "It is not a real bug. It is a stamp. It is a joke. Do you get it?" Lily is angry. She does not think it is funny. She says, "It is not a joke. It is a mean trick. You are not nice. I do not want to play with you." She runs away. She cries. Tom feels bad. He did not mean to make Lily sad. He says, "I am sorry, Lily. I was just joking. Please come back. I will give you a nice stamp. A stamp with a flower. Do you forgive me?" Lily stops crying. She looks at Tom. She sees the stamp with the flower. She likes flowers. She says, "OK, I forgive you. But do not joke with dead bugs again. They are not funny. They are scary." Tom nods. He says, "I promise. I will not joke with dead bugs again. I will only joke with nice things. Like clowns and balloons. Do you want to play with me?" Lily smiles. She says, "Yes, I want to play with you. You are my friend. But no more dead bugs. OK?" Tom smiles. He says, "OK. No more dead bugs. Come on, let's play with stamps. We can make a picture of a clown and a balloon. Do you like that?" Lily says, "Yes, I like that. That sounds fun. Let's do that." They hug. They play with stamps. They are happy. They are friends. The end.

Robin was a 3 year old girl. She loved to explore outdoors. Today she was playing in the garden. Suddenly, her heel started hurting a lot. She sat down and tried to settle the pain. When her dad came outside, he saw her struggling and said, "Let me take a look". He bent down closer to have a look. He said, "You have stepped on something, the heel is a little tough". Robin said, "Will it heal soon, Daddy?" Her dad reassured her, "Yes, with some time, all will be okay". He patted her on the back, and Robin went to rest. With some time her heel started feeling better. It had been a tough experience, but Robin knew she could always rely on her dad for help. She was feeling much better, and was now ready to run around and explore the garden once again!
