Category: Beginner
Title: Max and Tim and the Cat

Once upon a time, a dog named Max had an owner named Tim. Max and Tim loved to play together. They would run, jump, and have fun all day long. One day, Max and Tim went to the park. Max saw a big cat and wanted to chase it. But the cat was too fast and Max got lost. Max started to feel sad and scared. He missed his owner, Tim, very much. Max knew he had to be brave to find Tim again. He walked and walked, asking other animals if they saw Tim. Finally, a nice bird told Max where Tim was. Max ran as fast as he could and soon found Tim. They were so happy to be together again. Max felt brave and loved by his owner, Tim. And they lived happily ever after.

Tom and Sue are wife and husband. They like to go to the park and play with their dog. One day, they see a big and impressive balloon in the sky. It is red and yellow and has a basket. Tom and Sue want to go in the balloon and see the world. They ask the man with the balloon if they can go with him. The man says yes, but he wants money. Tom and Sue have no money, but they have a dog. The man says he will take the dog instead of money. Tom and Sue love their dog, but they want to go in the balloon more. They give their dog to the man and go in the basket. The balloon goes up and up and up. Tom and Sue are happy and excited. They see the park, the trees, the houses, and the cars. They wave to the people below. They feel like birds. They forget about their dog. But the man with the balloon is not nice. He is a thief. He does not care about Tom and Sue. He only wants their dog. He has a knife and he cuts the rope that holds the balloon. The balloon goes away with the wind. Tom and Sue scream and cry. They have no way to come down. They have no dog. They have no money. They have no help. They are alone and scared in the sky. The balloon goes higher and higher and higher. They cannot breathe. They cannot see. They cannot live. The balloon goes to the sun and burns. Tom and Sue are gone. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a painter named Tom. Tom loved to paint pretty pictures. But one day, Tom felt frustrated. His pictures did not look good. Tom's friend, Sue, saw him feeling sad. Sue wanted to help. She said, "I will encourage you, Tom. You can make nice pictures again!" Tom smiled and felt better. Tom and Sue painted together. They made a big, happy sun and a blue sky. They painted green trees and red flowers. Tom was not frustrated anymore. He was a happy painter again.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Tim. Tim loved to play all day long. One day, he saw a big red ball near the wall. Tim was very happy and wanted to play with the ball. Tim pushed the ball and it rolled very fast. The ball hit the wall and made a loud noise. Tim started to panic because he thought he broke the wall. He ran back to his house and hid under his bed. After a long time, Tim's friend, a dog named Max, came to visit. Max told Tim that the wall was not broken at all. It was just a funny painting on the wall that looked like a crack. Tim was very happy and they both laughed. They played with the big red ball together and had a great day.

Once upon a time there was a silver bird. It lived in a tree in the woods. One day, the bird saw the light and said, "I want to fly!" But it didn't know how. Then an old owl said, "If you want to fly you must practice. You must flap your wings and keep trying." So the bird practiced and practiced. Soon it was able to fly up high in the light. Whenever the bird flew in the sky it always remembered the owl's words; never give up and keep trying. The moral of this story is that if you want to achieve something you must never give up, no matter how hard it may seem.
