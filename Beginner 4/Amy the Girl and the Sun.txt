Category: Beginner
Title: Amy the Girl and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She lived on a big round planet with her mom and dad. Amy loved to run and play outside in the sun. She was a very happy girl. One day, while Amy was running, she saw a big, fearful monster. The monster was green and had long arms. Amy was scared, but she was also brave. She said to the monster, "Why are you so fearful? Don't you have friends to play with?" The monster looked sad and said, "No, I don't have any friends. Everyone is scared of me." Amy felt bad for the monster. She thought maybe the monster was not bad after all. So, she asked the monster if he wanted to be her friend. The monster smiled and said, "Yes, I would love to be your friend!" From that day on, Amy and the monster played together every day. They ran and had fun in the sun. And the monster was not fearful anymore. He was happy because he had a friend.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. The sun was warm on her face. She saw a big rainbow in the sky. The colors were so pretty. Lily wanted to find the end of the rainbow. She walked and walked until she saw a small bug. The bug could talk! "Hello, I can glow," said the bug. Lily was so surprised. She had never met a bug that could talk and glow before. The bug told Lily that he could help her find the end of the rainbow. They walked together and found a big tree. The tree was the end of the rainbow! The tree had warm, glowing fruit. Lily and the bug ate the fruit and became best friends.

Lily and Ben were twins who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big hill with many flowers. They wanted to climb the hill and see what was on the other side. "Come on, Ben, let's go up the hill!" Lily said. "OK, Lily, but be careful. The hill is very high and steep," Ben said. They started to climb the hill, holding hands and helping each other. They saw many bugs and birds and smelled the sweet flowers. They were having fun. But when they reached the top of the hill, they saw a big lake. The lake was very deep and blue. It looked like a mirror. "Wow, look at the lake, Lily! It is so pretty!" Ben said. "Yes, Ben, it is. But we should not go near it. It is too deep and we cannot swim," Lily said. But Ben was curious. He wanted to see his face in the lake. He let go of Lily's hand and walked closer to the edge of the hill. "Ben, no, come back! It is not safe!" Lily shouted. But Ben did not listen. He lay down on the grass and leaned over the lake. He saw his face and smiled. But then he lost his balance and fell into the lake. "Ben, Ben!" Lily screamed. She ran to the edge of the hill and looked for Ben. But she could not see him. He was under the water. Lily was very scared and sad. She cried and cried. She did not know what to do. Then she heard a voice. "Lily, Lily, can you hear me?" It was Ben. He had managed to swim to the shore of the lake. He was wet and cold and scared. "Ben, Ben, is that you?" Lily said. "Yes, Lily, it is me. I am sorry. I was silly. I should have listened to you. Can you help me?" Ben said. Lily was very happy to hear Ben's voice. She ran down the hill and found Ben. She hugged him and helped him get up. "Ben, I am so glad you are OK. I was so worried. Don't ever do that again. Promise me," Lily said. "I promise, Lily. I am sorry. I love you. Thank you for helping me," Ben said. They walked back to the park, holding hands and talking. They learned a lesson. They learned to be careful and to listen to each other. They learned that the lake was deep and dangerous. They learned that they were lucky and loved.
