Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Book

Tim likes to read big books with many words. He has a big book with all the words in it. It is called a dictionary. He likes to lean on his bed and look at the words. He learns new words every day. One day, his sister Lily comes into his room. She wants to play with him. She sees the dictionary on his bed. She thinks it is a boring book. She grabs the dictionary and throws it on the floor. She says, "Let's play with my dolls instead. They are more fun." Tim is very angry. He loves his dictionary. He does not like dolls. He says, "No, give me back my dictionary. It is my book. You are mean." He tries to get the dictionary from the floor, but Lily is faster. She runs away with the dictionary. Tim chases Lily around the house. He shouts, "Stop, stop, give me back my book." Lily laughs and says, "No, no, catch me if you can." She runs into the living room, where their mom is. She sees them running and yelling. She says, "What is going on? Why are you fighting?" Tim says, "Lily took my dictionary. She threw it on the floor. She does not want me to read it. She wants me to play with her dolls. I don't like dolls. I like words. She is not friendly." Mom says, "Lily, is that true? Did you take Tim's dictionary?" Lily says, "Yes, but it is a boring book. He does not want to play with me. He only wants to read words. He is not friendly." Mom says, "Both of you, listen to me. You are brother and sister. You should be friendly to each other. You should share your toys and books. You should respect what each other likes. You should not take things without asking. You should not throw things on the floor. You should say sorry when you do something wrong. Do you understand?" Tim and Lily nod. They say, "Yes, mom, we understand." They look at each other. They feel sorry for making each other angry. They say, "Sorry, Tim. Sorry, Lily." They hug each other. They give back the dictionary and the dolls. They decide to play together. They find a game that they both like. They are friendly again. Mom smiles and says, "That's better. I'm proud of you." She gives them a kiss and a cookie. They say, "Thank you, mom. We love you." They are happy. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Tim. Tim had a big tie. He liked to wear his tie every day. But Tim was deaf. He could not hear anything. One day, Tim met a cat named Kim. Kim saw Tim's tie and said, "Wow, what a big tie you have!" Tim could not hear Kim, but he could see her mouth move. Tim wondered what Kim was saying. Tim and Kim played together all day. They ran and jumped, and Tim's tie would flap in the wind. Kim would meow and Tim would wag his tail. Even though Tim could not hear, they had a lot of fun together.

It was a sunny day. Two little children were walking in the park. One of them was looking for something. Child 1: I've been searching everywhere, but I can't find it. Child 2: What are you looking for? Child 1: A secret. But it's not here. Child 2: Maybe it's in the trees? Child 1: That's a good idea, let's go look! They walked closer to the trees. All of a sudden, they spotted a furry animal in the tree. Child 2: Look! It's a secret! Child 1: We should give it some space, it looks scared. The children moved away and watched as the animal skittered down the tree, and ran away. Child 1: See? We can keep secrets safe by being kind and giving nature some respect. Child 2: Let's remember this lesson the next time we go for a walk!

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a tough boy named Tim. Tim had big muscles and loved to show them off. He would run, jump, and play with his friends every day. Tim was very proud of his muscles. One day, Tim was playing with his friends when he saw a little girl named Sally. Sally was trying to lift a heavy box. Tim said, "I can help you, Sally. I have big muscles!" Sally looked at Tim and said, "Thank you, Tim!" So Tim tried to lift the box, but it was too heavy for him. Tim felt embarrass. His face turned red and he said, "I am sorry, Sally. My muscles are not strong enough." Sally smiled and said, "That's okay, Tim. We can ask for help." So they asked their friends to help and together, they were able to lift the heavy box. Tim learned that it's okay to ask for help, and that being tough doesn't always mean having the biggest muscles.
