Category: Beginner
Title: Mimi the Cat and the Story

One day, a little cat named Mimi was very tired. She sat under a big tree and let out a big yawn. The sun was hot and the air was dry. Mimi wanted to find a cool place to rest. Mimi saw a little girl named Lily sitting in the shade. Lily was reading a story from a big book. Mimi walked over to Lily and sat down next to her. Lily looked at Mimi and smiled. She started to read the story out loud to Mimi. As Lily read the story, Mimi listened and yawned again. The story was about a funny dog who liked to play in the water. Mimi liked the story a lot. Soon, Mimi fell asleep next to Lily. They both had a nice rest under the big tree.

One day, an adorable little cat named Kitty wanted to play. Kitty saw a big hill with a track on it. Kitty thought it would be fun to climb the hill and see what was on the other side. Kitty started to climb the hill. It was hard work, but Kitty was having fun. Kitty climbed and climbed until she was at the top of the hill. She looked down and saw the track going down the other side. Kitty was very happy she climbed the hill. She ran down the track and found a nice, big tree to play in. Kitty had a great day playing on the hill and the track.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim found a weird gum under his bed. He did not know what it was, so he went to his mom to ask her. "Mom, what is this?" Tim said, showing her the weird gum. His mom looked at it and said, "Oh, that is gum. It is something you chew, but you should not eat it. It can make your tummy hurt." She tried to explain to Tim that the gum was old and dirty, and he should not put it in his mouth. The next day, Tim saw his friend, Sam. Tim showed Sam the weird gum and told him what his mom had explained. Sam was very curious about the gum, but he knew not to eat it. They decided to throw the weird gum away and play with their toys instead. And they had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a nice lawyer named Tom. He was very excited to help people. He had many friends who trust him. One day, Tom went to the park. He saw a big, red ball. He wanted to play with it. But, the ball was not a ball. It was a big, red balloon. Tom was very surprised. He did not know what to do. Then, his friends came to the park. They trust Tom to find a way to play with the big, red balloon. They all played together and had fun. The big, red balloon made them more excited than ever.

Once upon a time, there was a big comet in the sky. It was very pretty and bright. It could fly very fast and far away. One day, the big comet fell down to the Earth. It landed in a park where many kids were playing. A little boy named Tim saw the big comet. He went to it and said, "Wow! You are so big and pretty!" The big comet looked at Tim and said, "Thank you! I am a comet from far away. I am lost and need help to go back home." Tim wanted to help the big comet. He thought and thought. Then, he had an idea. He said, "I can kick you back up into the sky!" The big comet was happy and said, "Yes, please help me!" Tim took a big step back. He ran and kicked the big comet very hard. The big comet flew up, up, up into the sky. It went back to its home far away. Tim waved goodbye to the big comet. He was happy he could help his new friend.

One day, a jolly dog named Sam went for a walk. He saw a cane on the ground. He picked it up with his mouth and carried it with him. Sam was very happy with his new cane. Sam walked to a big tree. He saw a big red apple on the ground. Sam wanted to taste the apple. He put the cane down and took a bite of the apple. It was very sweet and yummy. Sam wagged his tail. Sam carried the cane and the apple home. He showed his mom and dad. They were very proud of him. Sam played with his cane and ate his apple. It was a good day for Sam.
