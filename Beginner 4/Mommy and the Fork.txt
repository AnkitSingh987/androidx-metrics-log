Category: Beginner
Title: Mommy and the Fork

Once upon a time, Mommy was having dinner with her little one. She put a hot, steaming plate of food in front of him and placed a fork on the plate. “OK, go ahead and eat, my love,” she said with a smile. Little one was excited to get stuck in and started to reach for the fork. But Mommy quickly pointed to the plate. “No, not the fork,” she said. Little one furrowed his brow and surveyed the plate carefully. After a few moments of searching, his face lit up with a smile. He pointed to the hidden piece of broccoli buried beneath the mashed potato. “Broccoli!” he exclaimed. Mommy smiled and handed him the fork. “Yes, have some broccoli,” she said. Little one dug into his dinner with the fork, happy to have found the hidden veggie.

Once upon a time there was a girl called Bella. Bella liked to wander around the town. One day she went to the mechanic's shop. It was a very modest shop. Bella saw all the mechanic's tools, which were shiny and new. She wanted to play with them but the mechanic said it was not time for playing. So Bella wandered around the street again, imagining what it would be like to work with the mechanic. Then she found a toy car that was just like one of the mechanic's cars. Bella picked up the car and took it back to the mechanic's shop. The mechanic smiled when he saw the car and showed Bella how it worked. Bella was so happy! She learned a lot that day and she had fun too. From then on she dreamed of becoming a mechanic, helping people to make their cars shine like new.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a razor in the bathroom. He did not know what it was for, so he asked his mom. "Mom, what is this?" Tim said, holding the razor. His mom looked at it and said, "Tim, that is a razor. It is not a toy. It is sharp and can hurt you. Please give it to me and I will set it in a safe place." Tim gave the razor to his mom and she set it high up on a shelf where he could not reach it. Later that day, Tim saw his dad sitting in a comfortable chair. His dad was using the razor to shave his face. Tim watched as his dad carefully moved the razor on his face. Tim understood now that the razor was not for playing. The moral of the story is to always ask an adult when you find something you do not know about. They can help keep you safe and teach you what things are for.

One day, a man and his wife went to the park to play. The man had a ball that he liked to kick. The wife was good at kicking the ball too. They were happy and laughed a lot. The man said, "Let's see who can kick the ball the farthest!" The wife said, "Okay, I will try my best!" They took turns kicking the ball. The man kicked the ball very far, but the wife kicked it even farther. As the wife went to get the ball, she saw a big rock in the way. She did not see it before. She tried to kick the ball over the rock. But when she kicked the ball, it hit the rock and went very high in the air. The ball fell down and hit the wife on her head. She fell down and did not get up. The man was sad and cried. They could not play with the ball anymore.

Ben and Tom are youths. They like to play with cars and balls. But they do not like to share. One day, they find a big black car in the park. It is shiny and fast. They both want to play with it. "Give me the car!" Ben says. He tries to grab it from Tom. "No, it is mine!" Tom says. He holds it tight and runs away. Ben runs after him. They fight for the car. They push and pull and shout. They do not see the big dog that is watching them. The dog is black too. It is hungry and angry. It barks and runs to Ben and Tom. It wants to bite them. "Help!" Ben and Tom scream. They drop the car and run away. They are scared of the dog. They hide behind a tree. They hug each other. They are sorry for fighting. "Let's be friends," Ben says. "OK, let's be friends," Tom says. They smile and shake hands. They do not want the car anymore. They want to play with something else. They see a ball near the tree. They pick it up and throw it to each other. They have fun. The dog finds the car. It sniffs it and bites it. It does not like it. It spits it out and walks away. It looks for something else to eat.
