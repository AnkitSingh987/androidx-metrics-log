Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Movie

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to watch a movie. He asked his mom, "Can I watch a movie, please?" His mom said, "Yes, you can watch a movie, but first you need to clean your room." Tim was not glad, but he knew he needed to listen to his mom. Tim cleaned his room very fast. He put away his toys and made his bed. When he was done, he went to his mom and said, "I am done cleaning my room. Can I watch the movie now?" His mom was glad and said, "Yes, you can watch the movie now." Tim and his mom watched the movie together. They laughed and had fun. Tim was very glad that he cleaned his room. He knew that sometimes you need to do things you don't want to do, but it can make you happy in the end.

Once upon a time, there was a generous flea. His name was Jack. He wore very nice clothes, with lots of colours. One day, he decided to take a walk in the park. As he walked, he saw a little boy. He looked sad. Jack went over and said, “What’s wrong?” The little boy replied, “My new shirt was torn. I don’t have any other clothes to wear.” Jack was very generous, so he said, “I have extra clothes I can give you.” He gave the little boy a brand new shirt. The little boy was very happy. He said, “Thank you, Jack!” Jack smiled and said, “You’re welcome.” Then he went on his walk feeling very pleased that he was able to help.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a careless boy who liked to play in the wind. One day, he went outside to fly his red kite. As he played with his kite, the wind blew very hard. Tim was not careful and he let go of the string. The kite went up, up, up in the sky. Tim was sad because he did not want to lose his kite. Tim's big sister saw him cry and came to help. They both looked up at the sky and saw the kite stuck in a tree. Tim's sister helped him get the kite down. Tim was happy and said he would be more careful next time.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the ocean with his mom. The ocean was big and had high waves. Tim liked to play in the water and watch the waves break on the sand. He saw a lot of fish and birds near the water too. Tim's mom said, "Tim, be careful when you play near the ocean. The high waves can break and splash you." Tim listened to his mom and stayed close to her while they played. As they were playing, Tim found a pretty shell. He showed it to his mom and said, "Look, Mom! I found a nice shell!" His mom smiled and said, "That's a beautiful shell, Tim. Let's keep it to remember our fun day at the ocean." They both had a great day and went home happy.

Once upon a time there was a little girl. She was three years old. She liked to go exploring and that's what she wanted to do today. She decided to go for a walk in the woods. The little girl followed a little path until she found a bed in the forest. She thought it looked comfy and cozy and decided to take a nap. She lay down on the bed, snuggled up and soon fell fast asleep. While she was sleeping, she had a wonderful dream. In her dream, she was so proud of herself that she had done such an adventure. When she woke up, she was so proud of herself that she had gone out and explored the forest. She smiled and hugged herself, feeling so happy and excited. The little girl decided that exploring was the best thing she could ever do. She knew that she could do anything if she put her mind to it.

One day, a big hippo named Bobo lived by a pond. He loved to jump and play with his friends. But today, Bobo felt frustrated because he could not jump as high as his friends. "Bobo, why are you sad?" asked his friend, Birdy. "I can't jump high like you and the others," said Bobo. Birdy thought for a moment and said, "Let's ask Wise Owl for help." Wise Owl listened to Bobo's problem and said, "Bobo, you don't have to jump high like your friends. You are good at other things. Be happy with what you can do." Bobo felt better and thanked Wise Owl. From that day on, Bobo was happy being himself and did not try to be like his friends. The moral of the story is to be happy with who you are and not to compare yourself to others.
