Title: Jasper and Bobble and the Flower

Once upon a time there lived two hamsters - Jasper and Bobble. Jasper was very popular and always surrounded by lots of friends. Whenever he saw Bobble, he would never stand still and always say "Buzz off!" One spring day, Jasper and Bobble went for a walk together. They saw a bright yellow flower growing in a field. Jasper liked it so much that he wanted to pick it, but Bobble said it was wrong. He said that if they took it without asking, other animals would not be able to see it and enjoy it. Jasper thought about it and agreed with Bobble. He smiled and said "Ok, let's leave it here and we can come back again next spring to see it." The moral of the story is that we should always be kind to others, take care of our natural environment and share the things we love with everyone.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a white ruler. She wanted to measure things. Sue looked around her room and saw her closet. She thought, "I will measure my closet!" Sue started to measure her closet. But when she opened the door, she saw her brother Tom inside. Tom was hiding in the closet. He was playing a trick on Sue. Sue was mad at Tom. She said, "You should not play tricks on me!" Tom felt bad. He said, "I am sorry, Sue. I will not do it again." Sue and Tom learned that it is not nice to play tricks on each other. They decided to measure the closet together. And they had fun doing it.

Jimmy was observing the park. He loved to visit the park because he could always find something interesting. One day, he noticed a dove. It was very pretty. Jimmy was so amazed by the dove and wanted to play with it. The dove was very friendly and soon it became very popular in the park. Everyone wanted to observe the dove. It was so peaceful. Jimmy was so happy that he had found the dove and that everyone else could enjoy it too. He watched the dove fly around the park and land in the trees. Jimmy continued to observe the dove. He was so pleased that the dove was popular among everyone in the park. He was excited to keep visiting the park so that he could keep an eye on the dove and observe it's beauty.

Tom and Ann are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a fat man on a bench. He has a big pie in his hands. He looks happy. "Look, Ann, a pie!" Tom says. "I want some pie. Do you want some pie?" "Yes, Tom, I want some pie too. But we can't take his pie. That is not nice," Ann says. Tom thinks for a moment. He has an idea. He whispers to Ann. "Let's ask him for some pie. Maybe he will share." "OK, Tom, let's try," Ann says. They walk to the bench. They smile at the fat man. "Hello, sir," Tom says. "You have a nice pie. Can we have some, please?" The fat man looks at them. He frowns. He does not want to share his pie. He says, "No, you can't have any pie. This is my pie. Go away." Tom and Ann are sad. They turn to leave. But then, Tom sees a dog. The dog is hungry. The dog likes pie. The dog runs to the bench. He jumps on the fat man. He leans on his chest. He grabs the pie with his mouth. He runs away with the pie. The fat man is angry. He shouts at the dog. He tries to chase him. But he is too fat. He falls off the bench. He lands on the ground. Tom and Ann are surprised. They laugh. They say, "Thank you, dog. You are smart. You are funny. You are our friend." They run after the dog. They hope he will share his pie. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big truck. The truck liked to drive on a rough road. The road was very bumpy, but the truck was strong. The truck was happy driving on the rough road. One day, the truck saw a big rock on the road. The truck wanted to destroy the rock. The truck went fast and hit the rock very hard. The truck thought the rock would break. But, the rock did not break. The rock was not a rock at all! It was a big turtle. The turtle was not hurt, and the truck was surprised. The truck said sorry to the turtle. They became friends and helped each other on the rough road.
