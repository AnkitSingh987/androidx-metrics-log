Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Max and the Valley

Once upon a time, there was a big valley. In the valley, there lived a small boy named Tim and a big dog named Max. They were best friends and loved to play together. One day, Tim and Max went for a walk in the valley. The sky was gray and Tim felt a little miserable. As they walked, they saw a big tree. Tim had an idea. "Max, let's climb the tree!" he said. Max barked happily and they started to climb. Tim was very good at climbing trees, but Max was not. Max tried to climb, but he was too big and heavy. Suddenly, Max slipped and started to fall. Tim saw Max falling and he yelled, "Max, watch out!" But it was too late. Max hit the ground with a thud. Tim climbed down the tree as fast as he could and ran to Max. Max was hurt and couldn't get up. Tim was very sad and cried. He tried to help Max, but he was too small. The gray sky began to rain, making Tim feel even more miserable. They were stuck in the valley, and no one came to help them. Tim and Max had a very bad day, and they never climbed trees again.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his basketball. He would bounce it and throw it all day. Tim was deaf, but he did not let that stop him from having fun. One day, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue saw Tim playing with his basketball and wanted to play too. Sue asked Tim, "Can I play with you?" Tim could not hear her, but he saw her smile and knew she wanted to play. Tim and Sue played basketball together. They had so much fun. Tim taught Sue how to wear a special headband to keep her hair out of her face. Sue was happy to learn new things from Tim. They became best friends and played basketball every day.

Lily and Max were playing on the beach. They liked to dig in the sand and look for shells. Lily found a big shell that was shiny and smooth. She showed it to Max and said, "Look, Max, I found a pretty shell!" Max wanted to see the shell too. He reached out his hand to take it from Lily. But Lily did not want to share. She pulled the shell away and said, "No, Max, it's mine! I found it first!" Max was sad and angry. He tried to grab the shell again, but Lily ran away. She ran towards the water, where the waves were crashing. She did not see the wet rock that was in her way. She stepped on the rock and slipped. She fell down and dropped the shell. The shell rolled into the water and was gone. Lily cried and cried. She hurt her knee and lost her shell. She wished she had shared with Max. Max ran to Lily and helped her up. He said, "I'm sorry, Lily. Are you okay?" Lily nodded and said, "I'm sorry, too, Max. I was mean to you. I should have let you see the shell." Max hugged Lily and said, "It's okay, Lily. We can find another shell. Come on, let's go back to the sand. Maybe we can find a tough shell that won't break."

One day, Billy was walking outside. He saw something moving in the grass. It was a little lizard. Billy was excited and he got closer to it. “Hi lizard!” said Billy, and the little lizard looked up. The little lizard was not scared. It was staring at Billy and twitching its powerful tail. Suddenly, the lizard started to move quickly away from Billy. Billy tried to catch the powerful lizard, but it was too fast. Then, Billy heard something. He heard a rip. He had accidentally ripped his trousers! Billy started to cry. He didn't want to tear his trousers. He sat down and put his head in his hands. Just then, the powerful lizard slowly came back and sat beside Billy. Billy stopped crying and looked up. He was so glad to see his little friend again.
