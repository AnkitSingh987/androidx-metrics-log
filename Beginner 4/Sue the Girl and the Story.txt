Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Story

Once upon a time, in a cold little town, there lived a girl named Sue. Sue liked to write and draw. One day, she wanted to write a story about her town. Sue put on her coat and went outside. It was very cold, so she took her thermometer with her. She looked at the thermometer and saw it was very cold outside. Sue saw things that were cold like snow, ice, and her breath in the air. Sue went back inside her warm house. She sat down and started to write her story. She wrote about the cold snow, the ice, and her breath in the air. Sue was happy with her story. She showed it to her mom and dad, and they loved it too. And they all stayed warm and cozy inside their house.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to jump. She jumped all day long. One day, Sue saw an unusual bug. The bug was big and green. It was near a big squash in the garden. "Hi, bug!" said Sue. "Do you like to jump too?" The bug looked at Sue and jumped high in the air. Sue was so happy. She jumped with the bug. They jumped together all day long. As the sun went down, Sue knew it was time to go home. "Bye, bug!" she said. "I will come back tomorrow and we can jump some more." The bug waved goodbye to Sue. Sue went home, excited to jump with her new friend again.

Once upon a time, there were two little kids. The first kid was called Chris and the second one was called Paul. Chris had an orange toy box which he wanted to unpack. He opened the box and pulled out a match. Paul was very excited and asked Chris if he could play with the match. But Chris said no and shook his head. Chris said to Paul "No, I won't let you play with the match. That's naughty, Paul". Paul was sad and started to cry. So Chris gave Paul something else to play with. Paul found some toy blocks and started to build a tower. Chris was busy unpacking and he soon found lots of fun toys. He found a truck, a plane, and a teddy bear. Then, he found a box of crayons and a coloring book. Paul stopped crying and asked Chris to show him the toys. So, Chris put all the toys on the floor and Paul started playing with them. They had lots of fun together with their new toys.

One day, a girl named Sue wanted to taste something new. She went to her mom and said, "Mom, I want to try a new food today!" Her mom thought it was a creative idea and said, "Okay Sue, let's make something new together." Sue and her mom went to the kitchen. They mixed many things in a big bowl. They put in milk, eggs, and sugar. They added some red, blue, and green food colors too. They put the mix in the oven and waited. After a while, the oven beeped. Sue's mom took out the mix. The result was a big, colorful cake! Sue tasted it and said, "Yum! This is the best cake ever!" Her mom smiled and said, "I'm glad you like it, Sue. We made a creative cake together!"

One sunny day, a little bird named Dizzy felt very dizzy. He could not fly straight. Dizzy tried to wake up his friend, the big bear. But the big bear was very sleepy and did not want to wake up. Dizzy felt sad and his heart was not happy. Dizzy asked a kind bunny to help him wake up the big bear. The bunny hopped and hopped around the big bear. The big bear finally woke up! The big bear saw Dizzy and his dizzy flying. The big bear's heart felt happy to see his friend. The big bear helped Dizzy feel better. They played and laughed all day. Dizzy was not dizzy anymore. The big bear and Dizzy were very happy. Their hearts were full of joy and they had a fun day together.
