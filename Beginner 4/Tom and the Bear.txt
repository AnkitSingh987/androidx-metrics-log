Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and the Bear

Tom is a mailman. He likes to deliver letters and packages to people. He has a big blue truck and a red bag. He puts the letters and packages in the bag and drives to different houses. One day, he has a very big package. It is for Anna, who lives at the end of the street. He tries to put the package in his bag, but it is too big. He has to tie it to the back of his truck with a rope. He makes a big knot to keep it safe. He drives to Anna's house. He sees Anna playing in the yard with her dog. She is very happy to see Tom. She likes to get mail. She runs to the truck and sees the big package. She says, "Wow, what is that?" Tom says, "It is a special delivery for you, Anna. It is from your grandma. She sent you something incredible." He unties the knot and gives the package to Anna. She opens it and finds a big teddy bear. It is soft and brown and has a red bow. She hugs the teddy bear and says, "Thank you, Tom. Thank you, grandma. This is the best mail ever!"

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing in his house. He saw a fragile toy on the top stair. Tim wanted the toy, but he was scared to climb the stairs. He called his mom for help. "Mom, can you help me get the toy?" Tim asked. "I hate climbing the stairs. They are too big and scary." Mom came and saw the toy on the stair. She said, "Tim, I will help you this time, but you need to learn to climb the stairs. They are not so scary." Tim watched as Mom climbed the stairs and got the fragile toy for him. He said, "Thank you, Mom. I will try to learn to climb the stairs." The next day, Tim practiced climbing the stairs with Mom's help. Soon, he was no longer scared and could climb the stairs all by himself. Tim was happy and proud that he could do it.

One day, a group of kids went to the park to play a game. The sun was shining and it was a mild day. They wanted to play a game of scatter. In this game, one kid would throw a ball, and all the other kids would scatter and run away. As they played, something unexpected happened. A big dog came running into the park. The dog saw the ball and wanted to play too. The kids were surprised, but they let the dog join their game. The dog was very good at the game. Every time the ball was thrown, the dog would run fast and catch it. The kids were amazed and loved playing with the dog. In the end, the kids and the dog had the best game of scatter ever.

One day, a little bird named Tim was sitting on a branch. He looked at the sky and saw how big it was. He wanted to fly high and see the world. But he was scared. The wind was harsh and strong. Tim's friend, Sam the squirrel, saw Tim looking sad. Sam asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim said, "I want to fly high, but the wind is harsh and I am scared." Sam thought for a moment and decided to help Tim. Sam said, "Let's go to the big tree. The wind is not so harsh there." Tim and Sam went to the big tree. Tim took a deep breath and decided to try. He flapped his wings and started to fly. He flew higher and higher. Tim was so happy he could fly high in the sky. And he was not scared anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a boy. His name was Tom. Tom had a bookshelf in his bedroom. One day, Tom was in his bedroom and he was playing. He went over to the bookshelf and he tried to do something, but he couldn't. Then he got upset. Tom didn't know what to do. He started to think. Suddenly, he noticed a lever on the bookshelf. He pulled the lever and the bookshelf started to move! Tom was so excited! He opened the bookshelf and he found a big surprise inside. There were lots of books, toys and games! Tom was so happy, he couldn't believe it. From then on, Tom and his bookshelf were very good friends. Every day, he would look inside and do something fun. He was never upset again!
