Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Cake and the Toys

One day, a big, tasty cake arrives at a little girl's house. The girl is very happy. She loves cake. She wants to eat the cake in her bedroom, so she takes it there. In her bedroom, the girl finds her toys. She wants to share the cake with them. She gives a piece to her teddy bear, her doll, and her toy car. They all like the cake too. After they eat the cake, they play games and have fun. The girl is very happy to share her tasty cake with her toys in her bedroom. They all have a great day together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a wise old man. He had a red car that he loved very much. Every day, he would drive his car around the town and wave to all the kids playing outside. One sunny day, the wise old man saw a little girl crying near the park. He stopped his car and asked her what was wrong. The little girl said that her cat was stuck in a tree and she could not get it down. The wise old man knew just what to do. He got a long stick and gently pushed the cat out of the tree. The cat jumped down and the little girl was so happy. She hugged her cat and gave it a big kiss. The wise old man smiled and got back into his red car. The little girl waved goodbye as he drove away, knowing that the wise old man had saved the day.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He was very glad because it was a sunny day. Tim wanted to build a tower with his toy bricks. He took his toy bricks and started to build. Tim had many bricks, but he needed one more big brick to finish his tower. He started to search for the big brick. He looked under the tree, behind the bushes, and in the sandbox, but he could not find it. Finally, Tim found the big brick near the swing. He was so glad that he found it! He took the brick and finished his tower. It was the tallest tower he had ever built. Tim played with his tower all day long and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a light and happy forest, there lived a little bunny named Ben. Ben loved to run with speed, hopping and jumping all day long. He had lots of friends like birds, squirrels, and other bunnies. One day, Ben saw a big, round, shiny thing up in the sky. He stopped to gaze at it. It was so pretty! His friend, a small bird, came to see what Ben was looking at. The bird told Ben it was called the moon. Ben liked the moon very much. Every night, Ben would hop out of his little home and gaze at the moon. He loved how it made the night light and pretty. He would run with speed under the moonlight, playing with his friends. They all had so much fun in the light of the moon.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tom. Tom had a toy axe. He loved to play with it. One day, Tom went to the park with his axe. At the park, Tom saw a tree. He thought it would be fun to shake the tree with his toy axe. He hit the tree. It made a loud noise. Tom was scared, but he did it again. Suddenly, a big bird came out of the tree. The bird was not happy. It told Tom, "Please stop shaking my home." Tom felt bad. He said sorry to the bird. From that day on, Tom learned to be kind to all living things.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very sad and miserable. He did not have any friends to play with. Every day, he would just sit in his room and look up at the ceiling. One day, Tim saw a big, scary monster under his bed. The monster wanted to play with Tim, but Tim was scared. The monster tried to make Tim happy, but Tim just cried and cried. The monster felt bad and went away. The next day, Tim was still miserable and alone. He wished he had played with the monster. But now, the monster was gone. Tim learned that he should not be scared of new friends. Sometimes, new friends can make us happy.
