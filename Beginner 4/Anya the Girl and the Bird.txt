Category: Beginner
Title: Anya the Girl and the Bird

Once there was a little girl named Anya. She loved to explore new places and ask questions about the world around her. One day, she asked her parents about a big round building she saw in the distance. They said it was a university. Anya was curious and asked if she could go - but her parents said she was too young. Anya was so upset that she began to weep. Nearby, a bird saw her and came closer to try and help. It was a colorful bird with a blue chest and green wings. The bird sang a sweet song and soon Anya felt better. She thanked the bird and promised that one day she would go to university. The bird flew away, and Anya felt better. She walked away with a smile on her face and a new understanding of the big round building in the distance.

Tom and Lily were friends. They liked to play with toys and books. One day, they had a task. They had to help Mom clean the house. Mom gave them a big bag. She said, "Put all the toys and books in the bag. Then, take the bag to the garage. Be careful, the bag is heavy." Tom and Lily did what Mom said. They put all the toys and books in the bag. The bag was very heavy. They had to drag it to the garage. They were tired and sweaty. They wanted to finish the task and play again. But when they got to the garage, something bad happened. Tom had a sneeze. He sneezed so hard that he let go of the bag. The bag fell on the floor. It made a loud noise. All the toys and books came out of the bag. They were everywhere. Some of them broke. Some of them got dirty. Mom heard the noise. She came to the garage. She saw the mess. She was angry. She said, "Tom and Lily, what did you do? You did not finish the task. You made a bigger mess. You have to clean it up. And you have to say sorry for breaking the toys and books. No more playing for you today." Tom and Lily were sad. They had to clean up the mess. They had to say sorry for breaking the toys and books. They could not play anymore. They wished they did not have a sneeze. They wished they finished the task. They wished they had a good day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big party. Everyone was happy and excited. They were going to see fireworks at night. Tim, a little boy, wanted to help make the fireworks. His dad said, "Okay, Tim. Let's mix the colors together." Tim saw a brown bag on the floor. He opened the bag and found many colors inside. Tim and his dad mixed the colors together. They made a big, beautiful firework. Tim was very proud of his work. At night, everyone gathered to watch the fireworks. Tim's firework was the last one. When it lit up the sky, everyone clapped and cheered. Tim's mom hugged him and said, "You did a great job, Tim!" Tim smiled and knew he had made the best firework ever.

Anna was sad. She had a big cut on her knee from falling down. It hurt a lot and it was red and wet. She cried and ran to her mom. "Mom, mom, help me! My knee is bad!" she said. Mom hugged her and looked at her knee. She said, "Oh, Anna, I'm sorry. You have a boo-boo. But don't worry, it will heal soon. We just need to clean it and put a band-aid on it." Mom took Anna to the bathroom and washed her knee with water and soap. She dried it with a towel and put a band-aid on it. The band-aid had a picture of a unicorn on it. Anna liked unicorns. She smiled a little. "Thank you, mom. It feels better now. But how will it heal?" she asked. Mom said, "Your body is very smart. It knows how to make new skin and close the cut. It will take some time, but every day it will heal a little more. You just need to be careful and not touch it too much." Anna nodded. She was curious. She said, "Can I help it heal faster? Do you have an idea?" Mom thought for a moment. She said, "Well, I have an idea. Maybe you can sing a song to your knee. A happy song. That might make it heal faster and make you feel happier too." Anna liked that idea. She said, "Okay, mom. I will sing a song to my knee. What song should I sing?" Mom said, "You can sing any song you like. But I have a suggestion. How about this one?" Mom started to sing a song that Anna knew. It was a song about the sun and the sky and the stars. It was a light and cheerful song. Anna joined her and sang along. She felt her knee and her heart warm up. She felt less sad and more hopeful. She said, "Mom, I like this song. And I like you. And I like my band-aid. And I like my knee. It will heal soon. I know it." Mom smiled and kissed her. She said, "I like you too, Anna. And I'm proud of you. You are brave and strong. And you have a beautiful voice. Now, let's go and have some cookies and milk. How does that sound?" Anna said, "That sounds great, mom. I love cookies and milk. And I love you." They hugged and went to the kitchen. Anna felt happy. She knew her knee would heal soon. And she knew her mom would always be there for her. She sang the song in her head and smiled.
