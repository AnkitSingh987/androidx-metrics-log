Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Lily and the Grass

Tim and Lily were playing in the garden. They liked to dig in the dirt and look for bugs. They had a bucket and a shovel each. Tim noticed a big red bottle under a bush. He was curious and picked it up. "Look, Lily, what is this?" he asked, showing her the bottle. Lily looked at the bottle and saw a skull and crossbones on the label. She had seen it in a book and knew it meant poison. Poison was very bad and could make you sick or hurt you. "Tim, put it down! That is poison!" she shouted, feeling scared. Tim was surprised and dropped the bottle. It landed on a rock and broke. The poison spilled on the grass and made a bad smell. "Uh-oh, we have to tell Mommy!" Lily said, grabbing Tim's hand and running to the house. Mommy was in the kitchen, making cookies. She was thoughtful and wanted to surprise Tim and Lily with a treat. She heard them coming and smiled. "Hi, kids, what are you doing?" she asked. "Mommy, Mommy, we found poison in the garden!" Lily said, out of breath. "Poison? What do you mean?" Mommy asked, feeling worried. Tim and Lily told her about the bottle and the skull and crossbones and the bad smell. Mommy listened and hugged them. "Thank you for telling me, Tim and Lily. You were very brave and smart. That bottle was very dangerous and you did the right thing. Come on, let's go and see what we can do to clean it up," she said. She took them to the garden and saw the mess. She put on gloves and a mask and picked up the pieces of the bottle. She put them in a bag and threw them in the trash. She poured water on the grass and sprinkled some salt to neutralize the poison. She told Tim and Lily to stay away from that spot and to never touch anything that looked like poison again. "Are you okay, Mommy?" Tim asked, feeling sorry. "I'm okay, Tim. Don't worry. I'm proud of you and Lily for being careful and honest. You are good kids," Mommy said, smiling. She took them back to the kitchen and gave them each a cookie. They were warm and sweet and had chocolate chips. Tim and Lily ate them and felt happy. They hugged Mommy and said they loved her. "I love you too, Tim and Lily. You are my thoughtful and wonderful kids," Mommy said.

One day, a black cat named Tom saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad. Tom wanted to help Sue feel better. Tom had a shiny pin that he found in the grass. He thought it might make Sue happy. Tom walked up to Sue and said, "Do not be sad, Sue. Look at this shiny pin I found. It is for you." Sue looked at the pin and smiled. She said, "Thank you, Tom. The pin is very pretty." Sue put the pin in her hair. She felt happy now. Tom was happy too. He said, "You look nice with the pin, Sue." Tom encouraged Sue to play and have fun. They played all day and had a great time. The shiny pin made Sue feel special and happy.

Once upon a time, there was an honest little girl named Lily. She loved to play at the park. One day, she found a big onion near the slide. Lily thought it was funny to find an onion at the park. Lily took the onion and went up the slide. She wanted to see if the onion could slide too. She put the onion at the top of the slide and let go. The onion went down the slide very fast. Lily laughed and clapped her hands. After that, Lily and the onion became friends. They played together at the park every day. Lily would always make the onion slide with her. They had lots of fun and were very happy.

---

One day, Tom and Lily went to camp with their mom and dad. They slept in a big tent and ate hot dogs and marshmallows. They liked camp very much. In the morning, Tom and Lily woke up early. They wanted to play outside. They put on their shoes and jackets and told their mom and dad. "Can we go outside, mom?" Tom asked. "Yes, but stay close to the tent, okay?" mom said. "Okay, mom," Lily said. Tom and Lily went outside. They saw many trees and flowers and birds. They heard a stream nearby. They wanted to see the water. "Let's go to the stream, Lily," Tom said. "Okay, Tom," Lily said. They walked to the stream. They saw fish and frogs and rocks. They threw pebbles and splashed water. They had fun. But they did not see that they wandered too far from the tent. They did not hear their mom and dad calling them. They did not know that they were lost. "Tom, I'm hungry," Lily said. "Me too, Lily," Tom said. "Let's go back to the tent," Lily said. "Okay, Lily," Tom said. But they could not find the tent. They looked around and saw only trees and bushes. They felt scared and sad. "Where is the tent, Tom?" Lily asked. "I don't know, Lily," Tom said. "What do we do, Tom?" Lily asked. "I don't know, Lily," Tom said. They hugged each other and cried. They wished they had listened to their mom and dad. But then they heard a whistle. It was their dad. He had followed their footprints and found them. He ran to them and hugged them. "Tom, Lily, are you okay?" dad asked. "Yes, dad," Tom and Lily said. "I'm sorry, dad," Tom said. "Me too, dad," Lily said. "It's okay, I'm glad you're safe," dad said. He took them back to the tent. Mom was waiting for them. She hugged them and kissed them. "I'm so happy to see you," mom said. "We're sorry, mom," Tom and Lily said. "It's okay, I love you," mom said. They ate some soup and bread. They felt warm and cozy. They learned their lesson. They never wandered away from the camp again. They had a lovely time at camp.
