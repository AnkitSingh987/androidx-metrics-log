Title: A Tale of the Dog

One day, a cat and a dog were walking down the street. The cat was crazy and liked to jump on the dog's shoulder. The dog did not like it when the cat did this. "Please stop jumping on my shoulder," said the dog. "I don't like it." The cat did not listen and kept jumping on the dog's shoulder. This made the dog very sad. One day, the dog had enough. When the cat jumped on his shoulder, he shook the cat off. The cat fell down and got hurt. The dog felt regret for what he did, but it was too late. The cat and the dog were not friends anymore.

Once upon a time there was a beautiful lake. One day, a boy went to the lake to pour some staff in. He asked his mom for permission. "Mom, can I pour some staff in the lake?" "Yes, but don't pour too much," said his mom. So the boy grabbed a bucket and went to the lake. He started to pour the staff into the lake, little by little. When he was finished, the lake was very clean and the water sparkled. It was so beautiful. The boy was very proud and went back to his mom. "Look, Mom! I made the lake beautiful by pouring the staff in," said the boy. His mom was very happy and gave him a big hug. "You did a wonderful job, my boy. I'm so proud of you!" said his mom. The boy was very happy and smiled from ear to ear. They both went back home, with the boy feeling very proud of his accomplishment.

Sara and Tom are playing in the living room. They have many toys, but they like to make things with paper. Sara shows Tom how to fold a paper into a hat. Tom tries to copy her, but his hat is too big and falls off his head. "Look, Sara, I made a hat!" Tom says. "That is not a hat, Tom. That is a boat. A hat has to fit your head," Sara says. Tom is sad. He wants to make a hat like Sara. He looks around and sees an ashtray on the table. It is round and shiny and has a picture of a flower on it. Tom thinks it is pretty. He puts the ashtray on his head and smiles. "Look, Sara, I made a hat!" Tom says again. Sara looks at Tom and laughs. She thinks he is funny. She takes the ashtray off his head and puts it back on the table. "Tom, that is not a hat. That is an ashtray. An ashtray is for grown-ups who smoke. It is not for your head," Sara says. Tom is confused. He does not know what smoke is. He thinks the ashtray is pretty and he likes it. "But I like it, Sara. It is pretty and it has a flower. Can I keep it?" Tom asks. Sara shakes her head. She knows that Mom and Dad do not like them to touch the ashtray. She tells Tom to find another paper and try to fold a hat again. "Tom, you have to listen to me. The ashtray is not yours. It is Mom and Dad's. They will be mad if they see you with it. Come on, let's make a hat with paper. It is more fun," Sara says. Tom nods. He does not want to make Mom and Dad mad. He follows Sara and picks up another paper. He tries to fold a hat like Sara. He hopes it will fit his head this time. He wants to make Sara happy. He likes to play with her. She is his best friend.
