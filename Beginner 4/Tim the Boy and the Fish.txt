Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Fish

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Max, went to the river. They saw a big raft. Tim said, "Max, let's sit on the raft and play!" Max wagged his tail and jumped on the raft with Tim. As they sat on the raft, it started to move in the water. Tim felt a little fearful, but Max was happy. They went faster and faster. Tim said, "Oh no, Max! What do we do?" Suddenly, a big fish jumped out of the water and landed on the raft. Tim and Max were surprised! The fish said, "Don't be fearful, I will help you!" The fish pushed the raft back to the shore. Tim and Max were safe and happy. They thanked the fish and went home to tell their friends about their big adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a big theater in a little town. The theater had a tall stage where people would dance and sing. Many people would come to watch and clap their hands. One day, a little cat named Stretch came to the theater. Stretch loved to dance and sing too. He wanted to be on the stage. So, he went inside the theater and jumped on the stage. The stage was very clear, so everyone could see him. Stretch began to dance and sing. He was so happy! The people in the theater clapped their hands and cheered for him. Stretch danced and sang all day long. At the end of the day, everyone agreed that Stretch was the best dancer and singer they had ever seen.

Once upon a time, there was a clever dog named Spot. Spot loved to dig with his spade in the soft dirt. One sunny day, he dug a big hole in his yard. As Spot dug, he saw a little mouse. The mouse looked very tired and let out a big yawn. Spot asked the mouse, "Why are you so tired?" The mouse said, "I lost my way home and I walked all night." Spot wanted to help the little mouse. He used his clever nose to sniff and find the mouse's home. They walked together, and soon they found the mouse's home. The mouse was so happy and thanked Spot. From that day on, Spot and the little mouse became good friends. They played together, and Spot always used his spade to dig fun tunnels for them to explore. And every time the little mouse yawned, Spot knew it was time for them to rest.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom had a bicycle. It was a very nice bicycle. One day, Tom saw a big part on the ground. He thought it would be fun to put the part on his bicycle. But Tom was a little spoiled, and he did not know how to do it right. Tom tried to put the part on his bicycle. But he did it wrong. His bicycle broke. Tom was sad. The end.

Lily and Ben were friends who liked to play together. One day, they found a big box of blocks and decided to build a temple. They worked hard and used many blocks to make a tall and beautiful temple. They were very proud of their temple. But then, Sam came along. Sam was a mean boy who liked to break things. He saw the temple and wanted to destroy it. He kicked the blocks and laughed as the temple fell down. Lily and Ben were very sad and angry. They cried and shouted at Sam. Sam felt bad when he saw how much he hurt Lily and Ben. He realized he was wrong to break their temple. He said he was sorry and asked if he could help them. Lily and Ben were still upset, but they decided to forgive Sam and let him join them. They taught him how to build a temple and shared their blocks with him. They worked together and made a new temple. This time, they used some pink blocks to make it more colorful. They also made a sign that said "Lily, Ben and Sam's Temple". They were very happy and proud of their new temple. They learned that it is better to be kind and share than to be mean and break.
