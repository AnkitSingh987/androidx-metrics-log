Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Sue and the Castle

Tom and Sue are friends. They like to play in the sand. They make a big castle with a flag. They are happy. But then a big boy comes. He is mean. He kicks the castle and laughs. He says, "Your castle is stupid. You are stupid. Go away!" Tom and Sue are sad and angry. They want to make him stop. They see a towel on the sand. It is wet and dirty. They have an idea. They run to the big boy. They throw the towel on his head. They say, "You are stupid. You are wet. Go away!" But the big boy is stronger. He takes the towel off his head. He is mad. He grabs Tom and Sue. He pushes them into the water. He says, "You are stupid. You are wet. You are cold. Go away!" Tom and Sue are scared and cold. They cry for help. But no one hears them. They sink into the water. They cannot breathe. They die. The end.

Once upon a time there was a chubby little bear called Honey. Honey lived in a big forest and every day she would wonder what fun things she could do. One night, she came across a beehive full of honey. She was so excited that she started to giggle and smile. Honey licked the honey until it was all gone. The next day she went back to the beehive and she wondered what else she would find. She found a huge strawberry and it made her so happy. She grabbed the strawberry and ran back to her den with it. The strawberry was so juicy and sweet that Honey ate it all up in one go. She was so full and happy that she went outside and rolled around in the grass. Honey was so chubby and happy that she went back to the beehive every day to see what other sweet treats she could find.

Once there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a restless boy who liked to play all day. He had a red ball that he loved to throw. One day, Tim threw the ball very high. It went up, up, up, and then down, down, down. The ball landed in a big tree. Tim knew he was in trouble. Tim asked his friend, Sue, to help him get the ball. They tried to reach it, but the tree was too tall. Then, a kind bird flew up and got the ball for them. Tim and Sue said "thank you" to the bird and played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to step on things. She would step on toys, leaves, and any other stuff she could find. One day, Amy found a sweet, red apple on the floor. She picked it up and held it tight. Amy went to her friend Tom and showed him the sweet apple. "Look, Tom! I found a sweet apple!" she said. Tom looked at the apple and said, "Be careful, Amy. Don't step on the apple like you step on other stuff." Amy didn't listen to Tom. She went outside to play and put the sweet apple on the ground. She saw a big pile of stuff and started stepping on it. She was having so much fun, she forgot about the apple. Later, Amy went back to pick up her sweet apple. But she didn't see it, and she stepped on it by mistake. The apple was squished and not good to eat anymore. Amy was sad and wished she had listened to Tom.

Once upon a time, there was an old tree in a forest. The old tree had many fruits. The tree was very big and it was difficult for the small animals to get the fruits. The small animals were sad because they were hungry. One day, a kind bird saw the small animals. The bird wanted to help. The bird flew up to the tree and picked the fruits. The bird then provided the fruits to the small animals. The small animals were very happy. The small animals said thank you to the kind bird. The bird felt happy too. The old tree saw this and smiled. The moral of the story is to help others when they have a difficult time.
