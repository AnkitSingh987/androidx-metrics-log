Category: Beginner
Title: Peter and the Mum

Peter was a young boy that everyone liked. He was polite and always said ‘please’ and ‘thank you’. One day, his mum wanted him to do something, but Peter didn’t want to. Mum: “Peter, I urge you to do your homework.” Peter: “No, I don’t want to.” Mum: “It’s important for you to use your brain. It helps you learn things.” Peter thought about it for a bit and realized that his mum was right. He knew he had to try his best. So, he decided to be polite and said “Okay, Mum”. Peter worked hard on his homework and soon finished. His mum was proud of him and gave him a big hug. From then on, Peter made sure to always be polite and use his brain.

Once upon a time in a normal town, there was a big red alarm. The alarm was friends with a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to clap his hands and play with the alarm. They had lots of fun together. One day, Tim and the alarm went to the park. They played on the swings and the slide. Tim clapped his hands and the alarm made a loud noise. The other kids at the park laughed and clapped too. It was a fun day. As the sun went down, Tim and the alarm went home. They were tired but happy. Before bed, Tim gave the alarm a hug and clapped his hands. The alarm made a soft noise and they both went to sleep.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a shy cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to lie in the warm sun all day. She also liked to play with her toy, a small key on a string. One day, as Kitty was lying in the sun, she saw a big dog. She got scared and ran away. She ran so fast that the key fell off her toy. Kitty was very sad. Kitty looked and looked for the key, but she could not find it. She felt very sad and missed her toy. Then, a little girl found the key and gave it back to Kitty. Kitty was so happy! She played with her key all day and was not shy anymore.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Mia. Everyday she would go out with her mum to play in the park. There she would explore, slide down slides and swing on swings. One day, when Mia was playing on the swings, she saw a young boy. He was swinging high and reached the sky. Mia wanted to swing just like him, but she was scared to let go. Mia's mum said to her "It's ok darling, if you want to keep swinging higher you have to let go! Go on, you can do it!" Memory of Mia's mum's words made her brave, and she let go of the swing. She flew higher and higher, feeling like she could almost reach the sky too. After a few minutes, Mia stopped and looked around her. But to her disappointment, the boy was gone. Mia felt sad. She knew she should have said hello when he was still there, but she was too scared. She regretted not talking to him. Mia's mum hugged her and said "Don't worry, darling. It's ok to make new friends next time. You will make one soon". These words comforted Mia and helped her to feel a bit better. Mia learnt an important lesson that day: never regret a memory, but look forward to making new ones!

One day, a curious little boy named Tom went outside to play. He saw a tiny bug on the ground. He got very close to look at it. The bug was green and had six legs. Tom said, "Hello, little bug! Do you want to be my friend?" The bug looked at Tom and said, "Yes, I would like that. But we might disagree sometimes, and that's okay." Tom and the bug played together all day. They played hide and seek, and tag. They had so much fun. But then, they found a big red ball. Tom wanted to play with the ball, but the bug didn't. Tom said, "Let's play with the ball!" The bug said, "I disagree. I want to play in the grass." Tom thought about it and said, "That's okay. We can play in the grass, and then play with the ball later." So, Tom and the bug played in the grass and then played with the ball. They had a great day together, even though they disagreed sometimes. They learned that friends can still have fun, even if they don't always want to do the same things.
