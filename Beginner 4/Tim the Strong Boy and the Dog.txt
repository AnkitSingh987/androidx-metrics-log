Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Strong Boy and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a strong boy named Tim. He loved to help his mom and dad. One day, his mom asked him to give a post to Mr. Brown who lived far away. Tim said, "Yes, Mom!" He was happy to help. Tim walked and walked to Mr. Brown's house. On the way, he met a big dog. The big dog looked at the post and took it from Tim. Tim was sad. He did not know what to do. But then, something unexpected happened. The big dog did not run away with the post. Instead, the big dog gave the post back to Tim and walked with him to Mr. Brown's house. Tim was very happy. Mr. Brown got the post and said, "Thank you, Tim and big dog!" Tim and the big dog became best friends, and they helped people together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat named Tom. Tom was a happy cat who liked to play with his friends. One day, Tom found a broken toy. He wanted to restore it and make it new again. Tom went to his friend, a wise old owl. He asked the owl, "Can you help me fix this toy?" The owl looked at the toy and said, "I think I can help you, but we need to find a special touch." The two friends went on a journey to find the special touch. They met a kind rabbit who showed them where to find it. The special touch was a magic hug. Tom gave the broken toy a big hug, and it was restored! The toy was new again, and Tom was so happy. He thanked his friends and they all played together with the fixed toy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Suzie. Suzie was three years old and she loved to play. One day, Suzie went to the playground to play. When she got there, Suzie saw a big ball made of rubber. She smiled and went over to the ball. She grabbed the ball and hugged it tight. “I love this ball,” she said. But then she dropped the ball because it was dirty. “Ew!” Suzie said. She started to complain. “This ball is dirty!” Just then, a friendly adult came over. “I know, it looks a bit dirty,” they said. They grabbed a wet rag and started to clean the ball. After they finished cleaning, the ball looked like new. Suzie grinned. She grabbed the ball and hugged it again. “Thank you for cleaning it!” she said. The adult smiled. “No problem,” they said. “Now, let’s go play with the rubber ball!” Suzie smiled and they both went off to play. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big, hairy dog. The dog liked to play with a red ball. Every day, the dog would bounce the ball in the yard. One day, while the dog was playing, he found a nail. The nail was long, and it was on the ground. The dog was curious and sniffed the nail. The dog's owner saw the nail and picked it up. He put the nail away, so the dog could not get hurt. The dog was happy and went back to bouncing the red ball. They played all day, and the dog was safe and sound.

It was a nice sunny morning and Sally was standing in her garden. She was pouring some water onto the plants when suddenly, she saw a stranger. Sally was a bit scared, but she was also ready to find out who this stranger was. She took a deep breath, stepped forward and said, “Hello, who are you?” The stranger smiled and replied, “My name is Momo, I'm here to say hello.” Sally felt a bit more ready and said back, “Well hello Momo, nice to meet you.” Momo then took out a basket and said, “I brought some delicious treats for you. Would you like one?” Sally smiled, “Yes please!” Momo then opened the basket and started to pour the treats into Sally's hands. Sally was so excited as she tasted the delicious treats! The two of them then spent the rest of the day playing and having fun together.
