Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Stone

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a round, happy face. Lily loved the color orange. She had an orange ball, an orange dress, and even an orange cat. One day, Lily found a magic orange stone. When she picked it up, it began to glow! Lily was so excited. She showed the glowing stone to her orange cat. The cat's eyes grew big and round. Every night, Lily put the magic stone by her bed. The stone's glow made her face look orange too. She loved to watch it as she fell asleep. The orange glow made her feel warm and safe. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a nice man with a string. He wanted to soak the string in the lake, so he walked to the lake. When he got there, he saw a rude man sitting at the edge of the lake. The nice man felt scared and worried, so he decided to go back home. The next day, the nice man still wanted to soak his string, so he went back to the lake. This time, the same rude man was still there. But this time, there were also a lot of colorful bubbles in the water. The nice man was curious so he asked the rude man what was in the water. The rude man smirked and said that he put soap in the lake so he could take a bath! The nice man was so surprised that he forgot all about his string. He said goodbye to the rude man and ran back home. When the nice man arrived back home, he realized he had left his string in the lake. So he went back to the lake and found the string floating in the middle. Sure enough, it was soaked with soap and smelled like flowers. The nice man smiled and thanked the rude man for soaking his string!

One day, a big wide van came to the park. The van was full of fun toys for kids to play with. A little girl named Lily and her friend Sam saw the van and ran to it. They wanted to play with the toys. Lily picked up a toy wand and said, "I will make you shrink, Sam!" She waved the wand at Sam, and he pretended to be very small. "Oh no, I'm tiny now!" Sam said, laughing. They played with the toys from the wide van all day. They had so much fun pretending to shrink and grow. When it was time to go home, they said goodbye to the van and their new toys. They couldn't wait to play at the park again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a girl named Mia. Mia had a shiny copper coin. She loved her copper coin very much. One day, Mia felt anxious. She had to leave her house to go to the store with her mom. Mia took her copper coin with her. At the store, she saw a boy who was sad. He had no money to buy a toy. Mia thought about her copper coin. She wanted to help the boy. Mia gave her copper coin to the boy. The boy was happy. He could buy his toy now. Mia felt good for helping the boy. When they went back home, Mia's mom gave her a hug. She was proud of Mia for being kind. The next day, Mia found two copper coins in her room. She was very happy. Now, she had more than before. Mia learned that when you help others, good things happen to you too.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play outside with his big red ball. One sunny day, Tim's uncle came to visit. Tim was very happy to see his uncle. His uncle brought a heavy bag with him. Tim was curious about what was inside the bag. He asked his uncle, "What's in the bag?" His uncle smiled and said, "Let's go outside and I'll show you." They went outside and Tim's uncle opened the bag. Inside, there were many colorful balloons. Tim's eyes got big and he was very excited. He and his uncle started to blow up the balloons. They made some big and some small. Tim's uncle tied a string to each balloon. Tim held the strings and started to run. He felt the balloons rush with him as he ran. The wind made the balloons dance in the sky. Tim laughed and played with the balloons all day. When it was time for his uncle to go, Tim hugged him tight. He thanked his uncle for the fun day and the colorful balloons. Tim's uncle promised to come back soon for more fun and games. Tim waved goodbye, still holding his balloons, as his uncle drove away.
