Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Tall Man and the Flower

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a tall man named Tom. Tom had a pet bird named Blue. Tom and Blue were good friends. They played together every day. One day, Tom saw an ashtray on the table. He thought it was not safe for Blue. So, he put the ashtray up high on a shelf. But Blue was curious and wanted to see the ashtray. Blue flew up to the shelf and tried to touch it. Suddenly, the ashtray fell down and broke. Tom was scared and sad. He thought it was time to teach Blue a lesson. Tom decided to release Blue outside, so Blue could learn to be careful. Blue flew away, and Tom was very sad. But then, something unexpected happened. Blue came back with a pretty flower in his beak. He gave the flower to Tom. Tom was so happy and proud of Blue. They both learned to be more careful and to always help each other. The moral of the story is to always be careful and to help your friends when they need it.

Once upon a time, there was a clean little garden. In the garden, there was a start of a pretty flower. The flower wanted to bloom and be big. One sunny day, the flower started to bloom. It grew and grew. But something unexpected happened. When the flower bloomed, it was not a flower at all. It was a big, green tree! The tree was happy to be in the clean little garden. It gave shade to the flowers and made the garden even more pretty. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a small dog. He had a flea. The flea was fierce. The dog did not like the flea. The dog felt itchy all the time. One day, the dog met a big cat. The cat said, "You look tired, dog. You should rest." The dog said, "I can't rest. I have a fierce flea." The cat said, "I can help you. I will eat the flea. Then you can rest." The dog was happy. He let the cat try to eat the flea. But the cat was not nice. The cat ate the dog too. The flea was still fierce. The flea found a new home on the cat. The cat did not like the flea. The cat felt itchy all the time.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a filthy cookie on the floor. He picked it up and looked at it. He had a question in his mind. He wondered if he should eat it or not. Tim thought it might still taste good, even if it was dirty. He decided to ask his mom. He showed her the filthy cookie and asked his question. "Mom, can I eat this cookie?" His mom looked surprised and said, "No, Tim! That cookie is filthy. It is not good to eat." Tim felt sad, but he listened to his mom. Just then, his mom had an idea. She took Tim to the kitchen and they made new cookies together. They were clean and yummy. Tim was very happy. He learned that it is better to eat clean cookies and not filthy ones.

One day, a cat named Tom was on a roof. He was dizzy and scared. He did not know how to get down. He saw his friend, a dog named Max, on the ground. Tom shouted, "Max, help me! I am dizzy and stuck on the roof!" Max looked up and saw his friend Tom on the roof. He wanted to help, so he thought of a plan. Max found a big, soft bed and pushed it under the roof. "Jump, Tom! The bed will catch you!" Max shouted. Tom was scared, but he trusted his friend. He closed his eyes and jumped. Tom landed on the soft bed and was safe! Tom and Max were very happy. They learned that friends should always help each other. The unexpected thing was that a cat and a dog could be such good friends. The moral of the story is that we can find friends in unexpected places, and we should always help our friends when they need us.
