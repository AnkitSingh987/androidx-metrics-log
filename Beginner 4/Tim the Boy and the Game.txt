Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Game

One day, a boy named Tim and his friend, Sue, wanted to make a game. They wanted to have fun and play together. Tim said, "Let's make a reliable game that we can play every day." Sue thought it was a good idea. They got some paper and crayons. Tim drew a big square with many little squares inside. Sue drew a smiling sun and some trees around the big square. They made a dice with a box and some stickers. When they finished, they played their new game. They took turns rolling the dice and moving on the squares. Tim said, "I like our game. It is fun and reliable. We can play it every day." Sue agreed and they both smiled, happy with their new game.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful dog named Spot. Spot loved to deliver things to people. One day, Spot had to go to a hospital to deliver a big box. The box was for a sick boy named Tim. In the hospital, Spot saw many sick people. He wanted to help them too. So, Spot opened the box and found a lot of toys inside. Spot had an idea. He gave a toy to each sick person in the hospital. They all felt happy and smiled. But, Spot forgot to give a toy to Tim, the sick boy. Tim felt sad. Then, something unexpected happened. Spot found one more toy in the box. It was the best toy of all. Spot gave the toy to Tim, and Tim felt very happy. The moral of the story is that sharing can make everyone happy, even when things don't go as planned.

Once upon a time, there was a brown bird. The bird loved to fly high in the sky. Every day, the bird would lift its wings and fly with its friends. One day, the brown bird saw a little girl. The girl was sad. She wanted to fly like the bird. The bird wanted to help the girl. The bird had an idea. It found a big leaf. The bird lifted the leaf with its beak. It gave the leaf to the girl. The girl held the leaf and jumped. She felt like she could fly. The girl was happy, and the brown bird was happy too. They played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. They played in the sun all day long. One day, Tim had to do work at his desk. Spot wanted to play, but he knew Tim had to finish his work. So, Spot lay down by the desk and waited for Tim. As Tim worked, he saw Spot by the desk. Spot reminded Tim to take a break and have fun. Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Spot!" They went outside and played together. They were very happy.

Lily and Sam were twins who loved candy. They always wanted to have more candy than each other. One day, their mom took them to the store and said they could each pick one candy. Lily saw a big bag of gummy bears and ran to grab it. She did not see Sam, who was looking at a chocolate bar. She bumped into him and made him drop his candy. Sam started to cry. "Hey, that's not fair!" he said. "You took my candy!" Lily felt bad. She did not mean to hurt Sam. She was just in a rush. She looked at her gummy bears and then at Sam's chocolate bar. She had an idea. "Sorry, Sam," she said. "I did not see you. Do you want to share my gummy bears?" Sam stopped crying. He looked at Lily and smiled. He liked gummy bears too. He nodded and said, "Okay. And you can have some of my chocolate bar." Lily and Sam hugged and said thank you to each other. They were happy to share their candy. Their mom saw them and smiled. She was proud of her compassionate twins. She gave them a kiss and said, "You are very kind and sweet, just like your candy."
