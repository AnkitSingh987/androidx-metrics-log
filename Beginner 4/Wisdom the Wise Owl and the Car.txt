Category: Beginner
Title: Wisdom the Wise Owl and the Car

Once upon a time, in a land full of gentle animals, there was a wise old owl named Wisdom. Wisdom loved to watch the animals play and have fun. He would sit in his big tree and smile as he saw them laugh and run. One day, a little red car came to the land. It could zoom very fast! All the animals wanted to ride in the car. They took turns and went zoom, zoom, zoom all around the land. They were so happy and excited. Wisdom the owl watched the fun from his tree. He knew that the gentle animals were having a great time. He felt happy to see them enjoy the zooming car. And from that day on, the animals learned to have fun and be kind to one another, just like Wisdom the wise owl.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the big hall with her mom. Mia saw a cute cat sitting near the door. The cat had soft fur and big eyes that would blink. Mia wanted to pet the cat, so she walked closer. "Meow," said the cat, as Mia reached out her hand. The cat's fur was not harsh, but very soft. Mia smiled and the cat blinked its eyes again. They were becoming friends. Mia's mom called her from the other side of the hall. "Mia, come here!" Mia waved goodbye to the cat and went to her mom. She told her mom about the cat with the soft fur and the blinking eyes. Mia's mom smiled and said, "That sounds like a very nice cat."

Once upon a time, there was a big, thick forest. In this forest, there was a small house. A girl named Amy lived in the house. She had a pet cat named Coal. One day, Coal went out to play in the forest. As Coal was playing, he saw a big, thick tree. He wanted to climb it. Coal went up the tree and said, "I can see the whole forest from here!" But when he looked down, he could not see Amy's house. Coal was scared. He did not know how to get back home. Suddenly, a bird came and sat on the tree. The bird said, "Coal, don't be scared. I can help you find your house. But first, you must make a wish." Coal closed his eyes and made a wish. When he opened his eyes, the big, thick tree started to disappear. Coal was now standing in front of Amy's house. He was so happy to be home. From that day on, Coal and Amy always played together in the forest, and they never got lost again.

One day, a boy named Tim found a toy gun in his room. He thought it was very creative. He ran to his friend, Sam, to show him the toy. "Look, Sam!" Tim said, "I have a creative toy gun!" Sam looked at the toy and smiled. "Wow, that's fun!" Sam said. They played with the toy gun, making pretend sounds and laughing. While they played, Tim's toy gun started to make real sounds. It was unexpected! Tim and Sam were surprised. They learned that the toy gun could yield fun music. They danced and played with the toy gun all day long.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big egg in his yard. He did not know what was inside. He took the egg to his room and put it in a warm place. He hoped it would hatch soon. The next day, the egg started to crack. Tim was very happy. A little dinosaur came out of the egg. "Hi, I am Dino," said the dinosaur. "Can you bring me some yummy food?" Tim thought and then went to the kitchen. He brought Dino some fruit and vegetables. Dino ate the yummy food and grew bigger and bigger. Tim knew that Dino could not stay in his room forever. One day, Dino said, "Tim, I need to go home to my family." Tim was sad, but he knew Dino was right. They went to the forest and found Dino's family. Tim said goodbye to his friend and knew he would always remember the dinosaur that came from the egg.
