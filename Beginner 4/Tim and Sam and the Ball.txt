Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Sam and the Ball

One day, a boy named Tim had a friend named Sam. They liked to play together. They played with cars, balls, and blocks. Tim thought Sam was a bit weird, but he liked him a lot. One day, while they were playing, Sam said, "I can change into something big and fun!" Tim didn't believe him. "No way," Tim said. But then, Sam changed into a big, bouncy ball! Tim was very surprised. Tim and Sam played with the big bouncy ball. They had so much fun. They bounced and laughed all day. Tim thought it was weird, but he loved his friend even more. And they played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Amy. Amy lived with her mom, dad, and little brother. One day, Amy's aunt came to visit. Amy's aunt was very successful. She had a big car and a nice house. When Amy's aunt got to the house, Amy did not recognize her. "Who is that?" Amy asked her mom. "That is your aunt," her mom said. "She is here to see us and play with you and your brother." Amy's aunt smiled and said, "Hi Amy! I am your aunt. I have a surprise for you and your brother." Amy's aunt gave them a big box. Inside the box were toys and games. Amy and her brother were very happy. Amy, her brother, and her aunt played together all day. They laughed and had fun. Amy's aunt told them stories about her job and the places she had been. Amy and her brother liked their aunt very much. At the end of the day, Amy's aunt had to go home. She hugged Amy and her brother and said, "I will come back to visit soon. I love you both very much." Amy and her brother said goodbye and waved as their aunt drove away. They knew they would see her again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a big duck who loved to splash in the water. One sunny day, the duck went to the pond to play with his friends. They all splashed and had fun together. Near the pond, there was a big tree with a little bird on the tip of a branch. The bird watched the ducks splash and wanted to join them. But the bird felt guilty because he was not a duck and could not swim. The duck saw the bird and invited him to join the fun. The bird hopped onto the duck's back, and they splashed together. The bird was happy, and the duck was happy too. They played all day and became the best of friends.

One day, a fine snake went for a walk. The snake met a little boy. The boy was afraid of the snake. The snake said, "Do not be scared. I am a nice snake." The boy and the snake became friends. They played in the park. The boy urged the snake to go on the slide. The snake said, "I cannot go on the slide. I am too long." The boy and the snake played all day. They had fun. They were very tired. The boy said, "Goodbye, snake. I will see you soon." The snake said, "Goodbye, friend."

Tom and Lily were friends. They liked to watch movies together. One day, they went to see a movie about a furry dog. The dog was very funny and smart. He could do tricks and talk to people. Tom and Lily laughed a lot at the movie. They liked the dog very much. They wanted to applaud him when the movie was over. But some other kids in the movie theater did not like the dog. They thought he was silly and annoying. They booed and threw popcorn at the screen. Tom and Lily were sad and angry. They did not like the other kids. They wanted to tell them to stop. But the other kids were bigger and louder. They did not listen to Tom and Lily. They kept booing and throwing popcorn. The movie ended. The dog said goodbye and thanked everyone for watching. Tom and Lily tried to applaud him. But they could not hear him over the noise. The other kids laughed and ran out of the theater. Tom and Lily felt bad. They did not enjoy the movie. They did not get to say thank you to the dog. They wished the other kids were nicer. They walked home quietly, holding hands. They hoped the dog was happy somewhere. They hoped they could see him again.
