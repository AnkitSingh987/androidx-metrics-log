Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Basket

One day, a little girl named Mia found a mysterious basket. It was a pretty basket with many colors. Mia was very curious about the basket. She wanted to know what was inside. Mia tried to open the basket, but it was hard. She had to pause and think. She tried again and this time, she opened it. Inside the basket, Mia found a big, soft ball. Mia was very happy. She played with the ball all day. She threw it up and down, and rolled it around. The mysterious basket and the ball became her favorite toys.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy hammer that he loved to play with. One day, his mom told him not to hit things with his hammer inside the house. Tim promised his mom that he would not do it. But, Tim forgot his promise. He played with his hammer inside the house and broke a cup. Tim felt very ashamed because he did not keep his promise. He knew he had done something wrong. Tim told his mom what happened. She was not happy, but she hugged him and said it was okay. Tim promised again not to hit things with his hammer inside the house. This time, he kept his promise and played with his hammer outside. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a fast dog named Max. Max liked to race with his friends. His friends were a pretty cat and a big bird. They all lived in a nice park. One day, Max wanted to race with his friends. But the pretty cat wanted to take a nap. And the big bird flew away. Max was sad. He wanted to play and race. Then, Max had an idea. He would find a new friend to race with. He looked around and saw a small bug. Max asked the bug to race, and the bug said yes. They raced and had fun. The pretty cat woke up from her nap and saw them racing. She wanted to join, so they all raced together. Max was happy to race with his friends again.

One day, a nice girl named Lily had a little plant. She loved her plant very much. She gave it water and sun to help it grow big and strong. But one day, the plant did not look happy. Lily started to worry. She did not know what to do. She asked her mom for help. Her mom said, "Maybe the plant needs more sun and water." So Lily moved the plant to a sunny spot and gave it more water. After a few days, the plant started to look happy again. It grew big and strong. Lily was so happy that her plant was better. She knew that with love and care, her plant would always be healthy.

Once upon a time, there was a train that loved to dance. The train would dance all day and all night. The train was very happy. One day, a little boy named Tim came to see the train. "Hello, train! Can you dance for me?" Tim asked. The train started to dance, and Tim clapped his hands. They both had a lot of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The train stopped dancing and started to cry. "Why are you sad, train?" Tim asked. The train told Tim that it felt spoiled because it only danced for itself and never helped anyone. Tim had an idea. "Why don't you help people by taking them places while you dance?" he suggested. The train loved the idea! From that day on, the train danced and took people where they needed to go. Everyone was happy, and the train didn't feel spoiled anymore. The train and Tim became the best of friends, and they danced together every day.

Tom and Lily were having lunch with Mom. Mom gave them some soup and bread. Tom liked soup, but Lily did not. She made a face and pushed her bowl away. "No soup!" she said. "I want pasta!" Mom shook her head. "You can have pasta for dinner, Lily. Now eat your soup, please. It is good for you." Lily refused. She crossed her arms and pouted. She did not want to eat soup. She wanted pasta. Tom saw his sister being silly. He picked up his fork and dipped it in his soup. He made a loud slurping noise and smiled. "Soup is yummy!" he said. "Look, Mom, I can eat with a fork!" Mom smiled back. She was proud of Tom. He was a good boy. He ate his soup and bread with his fork. Lily felt angry. She did not like soup. She did not like Tom. She grabbed her fork and threw it at him. "Stop it!" she yelled. "You are mean!" The fork hit Tom on the head. He cried out. He dropped his fork and bowl. The soup spilled on the table and the floor. Mom was shocked. She got up and took Lily by the hand. She was not proud of Lily. She was mad at her. "Lily, that was very bad!" she said. "You hurt Tom and made a mess. You have to say sorry and help me clean up. And no pasta for you tonight!" Lily felt sorry. She did not mean to hurt Tom. She did not like soup, but she did not like being bad either. She looked at Tom and said sorry. She helped Mom clean up. She ate her soup and bread with her fork. She hoped Mom and Tom would forgive her.
