Category: Beginner
Title: Alice and the Ball

Once upon a time there was a regular girl called Alice. One day, she went out to explore the wild woods and found a big, red ball. Excited, she picked it up and kicked it away. Suddenly, a strange voice said, "Hey! Don't kick my ball!" Startled, Alice looked around, but there was nobody there. Strangely, Alice heard the same voice again, "Hey Alice, can you give me my ball back? Please?" Alice couldn't believe it. But, she was brave and ran to get the ball. This time, she saw a little creature standing there. It was a furry, little fox! But, it had wings! Alice asked, "Are you the one who asked for the ball?". The fox nodded and smiled. Alice gave the fox the ball and the fox thanked her. Together, they kicked the ball, up and down and around, until the sun went away. Alice said goodbye to the fox and promised to come back soon. She left the wild woods happy, with a big smile on her face.

Once upon a time, there was a polite girl named Lucy. She had a new friend named Tom. Lucy wanted to introduce Tom to her family. So, she asked Tom to come to her house. At Lucy's house, they sat down to eat. Lucy's mom put a fork on the table. Tom did not know what a fork was. He looked at the fork and was confused. Lucy saw Tom's face and smiled. She showed him how to use the fork. Tom was happy to learn. They all ate their food and had a good time. Tom was happy that Lucy was his friend.

One day, a tall man went for a walk. He saw a mule by the road. The mule was big and strong. The man said, "Hi, mule!" The mule looked at the tall man. The tall man saw the mule was sad. The man asked, "Why are you sad, mule?" The mule said, "I want a friend." The tall man said, "I can be your friend, mule!" The mule was happy. They played all day. They saw many fun things. They were best friends.

One day, a cat named Tom went to play with his friends. Tom was very popular. All his friends liked him a lot. They liked to spin around and play fun games together. Today, they played a new game with milk. In this game, they had to spin around three times and then drink some milk. The one who drank the most milk would win. Tom and his friends were excited to play. They all took turns spinning and drinking milk. But then, something went wrong. Two of Tom's friends, Sam and Max, both wanted to drink the same milk. They both thought it was their turn. They started to argue and could not agree. Tom did not like to see his friends upset. So, he thought of a way to fix the problem. He found more milk for everyone to share. Sam and Max stopped arguing and they all played happily together again.

Tom and Lily were playing with their toy trucks in the garden. They liked to load the trucks with dirt, leaves and rocks and drive them around. Sometimes they pretended to be builders, farmers or explorers. "Look, Lily, I have a big load of dirt. I can make a hill with it," Tom said, dumping his truck on the ground. "That's nice, Tom, but I have a better load. I have juice in my truck. I can drink it when I am thirsty," Lily said, showing him her truck with a bottle of juice inside. "Juice? That's not a good load. That's a drink. You can't build anything with juice," Tom said, shaking his head. "Yes, I can. I can build a juice fountain. Watch this," Lily said, tipping her truck over and letting the juice spill out. The juice splashed on the grass, the dirt and the rocks. It also splashed on Tom, who was not happy. "Hey, stop that! You are making a mess. And you are wasting juice. Juice is for drinking, not for playing," Tom said, wiping his face. "Sorry, Tom. I was just having fun. Do you want some juice? I have some left," Lily said, offering him the bottle. "OK, thank you. But next time, don't load your truck with juice. Load it with something else," Tom said, taking a sip. "Like what?" Lily asked. "Like... like... cookies. Cookies are a good load. You can eat them and share them and they are yummy," Tom said, smiling. "OK, cookies sound good. Let's go and ask Mom if we can have some cookies. And then we can load our trucks with them and drive them fast," Lily said, nodding. "OK, let's go. But be careful. Don't spill the juice or the cookies. We don't want to make more mess," Tom said, holding his truck. "OK, I will be careful. Come on, Tom, let's go fast," Lily said, holding her truck. They ran to the house, holding their trucks and their juice. They were happy and hungry. They liked to play with their trucks and they liked to load them with different things. But they liked to drink juice and eat cookies more.
