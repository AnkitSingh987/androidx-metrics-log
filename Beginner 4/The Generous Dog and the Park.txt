Category: Beginner
Title: The Generous Dog and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a generous dog. His name was Sam. Every day he would go to the park and play with the other dogs. One day he was playing when a 3 year old girl walking with her mom saw him. She ran up to him and said, "Hi Dog! Can I pet you?" Sam wagged his tail happily and the girl started petting him. The girl's mom smiled and said, "You know, maybe a generous dog like Sam deserves a treat!" The girl was so happy she quickly pulled a treat out of her pocket and said, "Here you go, Sam!" She pointed to the treat and said again, "Pay!" Sam was so happy he ate the treat right away. The girl giggled and gave Sam a big hug. From that day on, whenever Sam visited the park, the 3 year old girl always had a treat waiting for him. Sam was so happy to have such a generous friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. Sally had a pet cat named Tuna. Tuna was a weak cat, but Sally loved her very much. They played together every day. One day, Sally and Tuna went to the park. They saw a big dog. The dog's mom warned Sally, "Be careful! My dog likes to chase cats!" Sally held Tuna tight to keep her safe. They walked far away from the dog. But then, something unexpected happened. The big dog came running towards Sally and Tuna. Sally was scared, but Tuna did not look weak anymore. Tuna hissed and scared the big dog away. Sally was so proud of her brave cat. From that day on, Tuna was not a weak cat anymore.

One day, a girl named Mia found a patch in her yard. The patch was full of pretty flowers. She thought it was a good place to play. Mia saw a small cat near the patch. The cat said, "I can't manage to find my way home. Can you help me?" Mia nodded and said, "Yes, I will help you." As they walked, they found a big twisty tree. The cat jumped up and said, "This is my home!" Mia was surprised that the cat lived in a tree. They became good friends and played in the flower patch every day.

One sunny day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big tree with interesting shapes on it. Tim's mom said, "Look, Tim! This tree is so interesting. What do you see?" Tim gazed at the tree for a moment and said, "I see a face! It has a big smile!" His mom smiled too, and they walked under the tree to sit in the shade. Tim felt happy and safe under the big tree. As they sat there, a little squirrel came by. Tim's mom whispered, "Do you remember the face on the tree? Maybe the tree is smiling because it's friends with the squirrel." Tim watched the squirrel run up the tree and thought about how the tree and squirrel were friends. He felt even happier knowing that the interesting tree had a friend to keep it company.

Tom and Ben are brothers. They like to play with toys and books. But sometimes they fight and say mean things. One day, they find a big box in the garage. It has a label that says "lab". They open the box and see many things inside. There are tubes and bottles and wires and lights. They look like fun. "Wow, look at this!" Tom says. He picks up a tube with a red liquid. "I wonder what it does." "Let me see!" Ben says. He tries to grab the tube from Tom. "It's mine!" "No, it's mine!" Tom says. He pulls the tube back. "You always want what I have!" They tug and pull and yell. The tube slips from their hands and falls on the floor. It breaks and the red liquid spills everywhere. It makes a loud snap and a big smoke. "Uh-oh," Tom and Ben say. They cough and cover their eyes. Dad hears the noise and runs to the garage. He sees the mess and the smoke. He is angry and scared. "What have you done?" he shouts. "This is not a toy! This is my lab! It has chemicals and experiments! It is dangerous!" He picks up Tom and Ben and takes them outside. He puts them on the grass and checks if they are hurt. "Are you okay?" he asks. Tom and Ben nod. They are sorry and scared. "We're sorry, Dad," they say. "We didn't know. We just wanted to play." Dad hugs them and sighs. He is glad they are okay. "I know, boys," he says. "But you have to be careful. You can't touch things you don't know. You could get hurt or break something. You have to ask me first." Tom and Ben nod again. They understand. "We will, Dad," they say. "We won't touch your lab again. We promise." Dad smiles and kisses them. "Good," he says. "Now let's go inside and clean up. And then we can read a book or play a game. Something safe and fun." Tom and Ben smile and hug Dad. They are happy and love him. They think his lab is cool, but also stupid. They don't want to play with it anymore. They have better things to do.
