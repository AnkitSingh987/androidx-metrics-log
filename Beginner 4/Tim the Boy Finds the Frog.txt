Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Frog

One day, a little boy named Tim found a green frog. Tim took the frog to his room to show his mom. The frog liked the room. "Mom, look at my green frog!" Tim said. But mom did not like the frog. "Frogs do not belong in rooms," mom said. Tim was sad. Tim wanted to display his frog in the room. So, he made a plan. He took the frog outside and made a nice home for it. Now, Tim can see the frog and mom is happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man. He had a big house and many toys. One day, he wanted to play a game. So, he took out a ball and started to play. As the man played with the ball, he saw a small boy. The boy was sad. The man asked, "Why are you sad?" The boy said, "I want to play, but I have no ball." The man felt bad and wanted to help. The man thought of a plan. He would use his ball to make the boy happy. He gave the ball to the boy, and they played the game together. The boy was very happy, and the man was happy too. They became best friends and played games every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy boats in the sink. One day, he had a race with his two favorite boats, Red and Blue. Tim wanted to see which boat would win. As the race began, Red and Blue floated in the sink. They bumped into each other and got very close to the fragile cups on the side of the sink. Tim was worried that the cups might break. Tim quickly moved the cups away and cheered for his boats. Red and Blue raced to the other side of the sink. In the end, Red won the race. Tim was so happy that Red won and nothing broke. From that day on, Tim always made sure to keep the fragile cups away from his boat races.

Once upon a time, there was a red bird named Bob. Bob loved to sing. He sang every day. One day, Bob saw a firework. He thought it was very pretty. The firework made a loud noise, and Bob wanted to sing with it. Bob told his friend, Cat, "I want to sing with the firework!" Cat said, "That sounds fun, but be careful. Fireworks can be hot and dangerous." Bob listened to Cat, but he still wanted to try. Bob flew close to the firework and started to sing. He was so happy! But then, the firework got too hot. Bob got scared and flew away. He realized that Cat was right. Fireworks were not safe to sing with. Bob learned that it is important to listen to friends when they give advice. He decided to keep singing, but only with other birds and not with fireworks. Bob and Cat played together and had fun, staying safe and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a nice man named Tom. Tom worked in a big office. He had many friends in the office. They all liked to talk and laugh together. Tom was very happy at his office. One day, Tom wanted to buy a gift for his friends. He went to the store and bought many colorful balloons. He also bought a big cake for them to eat. Tom was excited to share the gifts with his friends. Tom brought the balloons and cake to the office. His friends were so happy and surprised. They all said "thank you" and gave Tom a big hug. They ate the cake and played with the balloons. It was a very fun day at the office.

Once upon a time, there was a big blue dog named Max. Max loved to play with his ball. One day, Max lost his ball under a tree. He looked and looked, but he could not find it. Max saw a little girl named Lily. She had a big head and a funny hat. Max asked, "Can you help me find my ball?" Lily said, "Yes, I can help you." They looked under the tree and found Max's ball. Lily had an idea. She took a long string and attached it to Max's ball. Now, Max could play with his ball and not lose it again. Max was very happy. He wagged his tail and said, "Thank you, Lily!" They played together all day long.
