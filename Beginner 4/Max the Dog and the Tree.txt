Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Dog and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to play and run outside. One day, Max saw a big bird in the sky. The bird was very pretty. Max blinked his eyes and looked at the bird. Max ran to the bird and said, "Hello, bird! Do you want to play with me?" The bird looked at Max and said, "Yes, Max! Let's play together. But first, I need to find some meat for my babies. They are hungry and ready to eat." Max and the bird looked for meat. They found some meat under a tree. Max picked up the meat and gave it to the bird. The bird said, "Thank you, Max! Now my babies can eat." Max and the bird played together all day. They had so much fun. When the sun went down, Max went home. He was happy to help his new friend. Max blinked his eyes and went to sleep, dreaming of more adventures with the bird.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty parrot named Polly. Polly loved to enjoy flying high in the sky. Every day, Polly would fly around and play with her friends. One day, while Polly was flying, she saw a big tree. She thought it would be fun to play in the tree. But when she got there, she saw a big cat. The cat looked at Polly and smiled. Polly was scared, but she didn't want to show it. Instead of being mean, the cat said, "Let's be friends!" Polly was surprised, but happy. So, Polly and the cat played together in the big tree. They had lots of fun and enjoyed their new friendship. From that day on, Polly and the cat were best friends and always played together.

Once upon a time, in a normal little town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to have fun with his friends. They would play games, laugh, and run around together. Tim was always happy when he could have fun with his friends. One day, Tim's mom said they were going to have a big party for Tim's birthday. She asked Tim to help her prepare for the party. Tim was very excited to help. He helped his mom make a cake, blow up balloons, and put up decorations. Tim knew the party would be a lot of fun for everyone. Finally, the day of the party came. All of Tim's friends came to the party, and they all had a great time. They played games, ate cake, and had lots of fun together. Tim was so happy that he got to prepare for his party with his mom and have a fun day with his friends. And they all lived happily ever after.

Sara and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big road near the park. They want to cross the road and see what is on the other side. "Let's go, Sara!" Ben says. "It looks fun over there!" But Sara is scared. She remembers what her mom told her. "You must never cross the road alone. It is dangerous. You must wait for a grown-up to help you." "Ben, we can't go. We have to wait for a grown-up. The road is dangerous. There are cars and trucks and buses. They can hurt us." Sara says. Ben is not scared. He thinks he is big and smart. He does not listen to Sara. He says, "I don't need a grown-up. I can cross the road by myself. I know how to look both ways. You are a baby, Sara. You are afraid of everything." Ben runs to the road. He does not look both ways. He does not see the car coming fast. The car honks and brakes. Ben is scared. He freezes on the road. He does not know what to do. Sara sees a police officer near the park. She runs to him and says, "Please help! My friend is on the road! He is in danger!" The police officer runs to the road. He stops the car. He grabs Ben and brings him to the sidewalk. He is angry. He says, "What were you doing on the road? Don't you know how dangerous it is? You could have been hurt or killed. You must never cross the road alone. You must always wait for a grown-up to help you. You must always look both ways before you cross. You must always be careful and follow the rules." Ben is ashamed. He is sorry. He says, "I'm sorry, officer. I was wrong. I did not listen to my friend. She tried to teach me. She tried to stop me. I was foolish and stubborn. I will never do it again. I will always wait for a grown-up. I will always look both ways. I will always be careful and follow the rules." Sara is happy. She is grateful. She says, "Thank you, officer. Thank you for saving my friend. Thank you for teaching him. He is my friend. I love him. I don't want him to get hurt." The officer smiles. He says, "You're welcome, Sara. You are a good friend. You are smart and brave. You did the right thing. You should be proud of yourself. You should always help your friends. You should always teach them what is right and wrong. You should always be grateful for your friends." Sara and Ben hug. They say, "We are sorry. We are grateful. We are friends." The end.
