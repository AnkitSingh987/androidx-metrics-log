Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Stick

One day, a boy named Tim went to play near the creek. He saw a big stick and thought, "I can use this stick to poke the water." Tim was very happy to find the stick. He started to poke the water and make big splashes. A girl named Sue saw Tim and said, "Don't be careless, Tim! You might fall in the creek!" But Tim did not listen to Sue. He kept poking the water and having fun. Sue was worried and watched Tim from the side. Soon, Tim lost his balance and fell into the creek. He was scared and cried for help. Sue ran to get Tim's mom. But when they came back, it was too late. Tim was gone, and everyone was very sad. If only Tim had listened to Sue and not been careless.

Once upon a time, there was a bright bubble floating in a beautiful blue sky. A little boy named Ben was playing in his garden. He looked up at the bubble and said to himself, "That's so pretty, I wish I could meet it!" Suddenly, the bubble started to move towards him. When it reached Ben, the bubble asked him, "Where would you like to go?" Ben looked around and smiled. "Let's go explore the ocean!" The bubble then started to make its way to the sea. As they zipped past the birds and swayed with the wind, Ben noticed how the bubble was sparkling in the sunlight. When they finally reached the ocean, Ben was like a kid in a candy store. He looked at the waves and thought of all the adventures the bubble and him could have together. But when the sun started to set and the sky began to darken, Ben was sad because he knew he had to say goodbye to the bubble. Before they parted, the bubble whispered something to Ben. He smiled and said, "I'll never forget. I'll always remember our adventure and to always be kind. Thank you, Bubble." Ben waved goodbye to the bubble, knowing his day had been filled with a beautiful memory. From then on, Ben was kinder to everyone he met and never forgot the lessons he learned from his bubble friend.

Once, there was a cool cat named Tim. Tim loved to play with his friends in the park. They would run, jump, and yell as they played together. Tim was very happy when he was with his friends. One day, Tim saw a big pile of waste near the park. He thought it was not good for the park. Tim wanted to find a way to clean it up so that the park would be clean and safe for everyone. Tim called all of his friends to help him. They all came to the park and started to clean up the waste. They worked together, and soon the park was clean again. Tim and his friends were very proud of what they had done. They played in the park and yelled with joy because they had made the park a better place for everyone.

One day, a messy little boy named Tim was playing with his toys. He had a big box of arrows. He liked to add more and more arrows to his collection. Tim's room was very messy with arrows everywhere. Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. Sam saw the messy room and said, "Wow, Tim! Your room is so messy! Let's clean up the arrows." Tim and Sam started to pick up the arrows and put them back in the box. As they were cleaning, Tim found a new arrow under his bed. He was excited and said, "Look, Sam! I found a new arrow!" Sam smiled and replied, "That's great, Tim! Now, let's add it to your collection." They put the new arrow in the box and finished cleaning the room. Now, Tim's room was nice and clean, and he had a new arrow to play with.

Tom and Lily were playing with their toys in the living room. They liked to make different things with their blocks, cars, dolls and animals. Tom had a big idea. He wanted to make a rocket that could fly to the moon. He found a big cardboard box and some tape. He started to attach the box to his toy car. He said to Lily, "Look, this is my rocket. Do you want to help me?" Lily nodded. She liked rockets. She helped Tom to find some more things to attach to the box. They found some paper cups, some spoons, some stickers and some string. They made the rocket look very nice. They put some stickers on the box to make windows and stars. They used the spoons to make wings and the string to make a tail. They used the paper cups to make helmets for their dolls. They put their dolls inside the box. They said, "Now our dolls are ready to go to the moon." They were very proud of their rocket. They wanted to show it to their mom. They pushed the box with the car to the kitchen. Their mom was making juice for them. She saw their rocket and smiled. She said, "Wow, what a great rocket. You two are very clever. Do you want some juice?" Tom and Lily said, "Yes, please." They were thirsty. They left their rocket in the kitchen and went to the table. Their mom poured them some juice in their cups. They drank their juice and talked about their rocket. They said, "We are going to fly to the moon with our dolls. We are going to see the stars and the aliens. It will be fun." But they did not see that their cat, Fluffy, was curious about their rocket. Fluffy jumped on the box and started to scratch and bite it. He made holes in the box and tore the tape. He knocked down the spoons and the string. He pulled out the dolls and played with them. He made a big mess. Tom and Lily finished their juice and went back to their rocket. They saw what Fluffy had done. They were very sad and angry. They said, "Fluffy, you are a bad cat. You ruined our rocket. How can we go to the moon now?" Fluffy looked at them and meowed. He did not understand why they were mad. He thought he was having fun. He ran away to hide under the sofa. Tom and Lily cried. They hugged their dolls. They were scared that they could not make another rocket. They said, "We want to go to the moon. We want to see the stars and the aliens. It is not fair." Their mom heard them crying. She came to the kitchen and saw the mess. She felt sorry for them. She said, "Don't cry, my loves. I know you worked hard on your rocket. But it is not the end of the world. You can make another one. Or you can play something else. There are many ways to have fun. And you can still see the stars and the aliens in your dreams. Come on, let me help you clean up. And then we can have some cookies and read a story. How does that sound?" Tom and Lily looked at their mom. They wiped their tears. They said, "OK, mom. Thank you. We love you." They helped their mom to clean up the mess. They threw away the broken box and the tape. They put their toys back in their place. They had some cookies and read a story. They felt better. They forgot about their rocket. They said, "Maybe we can make another one tomorrow. Or maybe we can play something else. There are many ways to have fun." They hugged their mom and their dolls. They went to bed. They dreamed of the moon and the stars and the aliens. They were happy.
