Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Fruit

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to create a big fruit party for his friends. He was very glad because he loved fruit. He told his mom, "Mom, I want to have a fruit party with my friends!" His mom said, "That sounds like fun, Tim!" Tim went to the store with his mom to buy fruit. He got apples, bananas, and grapes. He was very excited. But when he got home, he saw that his dog, Spot, had eaten some of the fruit! Tim was sad and didn't know what to do. Tim's mom saw him crying and asked, "What's wrong, Tim?" He told her about Spot eating the fruit. His mom said, "Don't worry, we can go back to the store and get more fruit for your party." Tim was glad again. They went back to the store and got more fruit for the party. At the party, Tim and his friends had a great time eating fruit and playing games. Tim was very glad that he could share his love for fruit with his friends. And from that day on, Tim always made sure to keep the fruit away from Spot.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to a big field with her mom. The field had many pretty flowers. Mia saw a big red ball in the field. She wanted to play with the ball, but her mom said, "No, Mia. That ball is not available. It belongs to someone else." Mia was sad and she frowned. She wanted to play with the ball. Suddenly, a little boy came running to the field. He saw Mia and her mom. The boy said, "Hi! I am Tom. That is my ball. Do you want to play together?" Mia's face lit up and she stopped frowning. She said, "Yes! I want to play with you, Tom!" So Mia, Tom, and her mom played with the ball in the field. They had lots of fun and laughed a lot. The field was a happy place for them.

One day, a small ant named Andy was walking to his friend's house. He saw a big rod on the ground. Andy wanted to welcome his friend, so he thought he could use the rod as a gift. He picked up the rod and started walking again. When Andy reached his friend's house, he said, "Hi, friend! I welcome you with this big rod!" His friend was happy and said, "Wow, thank you, Andy! Let's play with the rod!" They started to play, and they were very successful at making fun games with the rod. But then, something unexpected happened. The rod started to move on its own! Andy and his friend were surprised. The rod was not a normal rod, it was a magic rod! They played with the magic rod all day and had the best time ever.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty train. This train had big wheels and went very fast. It ran on a long rail that went through the town. The train loved to talk to everyone it met. One day, the train saw a small car stuck near the rail. The car was sad and scared. The mighty train stopped and talked to the car. "Don't worry, I will help you," said the train. The train pushed the car away from the rail and made sure it was safe. The car thanked the train and they became good friends. They learned that when you help others and talk to them kindly, you make new friends. The train and the car lived happily in the town, always helping and talking to others.

Once upon a time, there was an adorable little cat. She had soft fur and big eyes. She liked to play with her toy mouse and run around the house. One day, she found a big book. It was a dictionary. The cat did not know what it was, but she liked to sit on it. One day, a big dog came to visit the house. The cat was scared. She started to tremble because the dog was so big. She did not know what to do. So, she ran and hid under the big book, the dictionary. The dog looked around for the cat. He could not find her. The cat stayed very still under the dictionary. After a while, the dog left. The cat came out from under the dictionary. She was happy that she was safe. The cat learned that the big book, the dictionary, was not just for sitting on, but it could also keep her safe.
