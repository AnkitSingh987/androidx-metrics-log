Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Sue

One day, a boy named Tim set out to go to the hospital. He had a big toy car that he wanted to give to the kids there. Tim thought it would make them happy. He walked with his mom to the hospital. At the hospital, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue was very sick, but she was not selfish. She wanted to share her toys with the other kids too. Tim and Sue played with the toys together and had lots of fun. They both felt happy. Then, something unexpected happened. A big, funny clown came into the room. The clown was not selfish either. He made everyone laugh and smile. The clown even gave Tim and Sue more toys to share with their new friends. In the end, Tim, Sue, and all the kids at the hospital learned that sharing is better than being selfish.

Once upon a time, in a cheerful garden, there lived a little flower. The flower was very happy because it was going to become a big flower soon. It loved to dance in the sun and play with the other flowers. One day, a happy bee came to visit the flowers. The bee said, "Hello, little flower! How are you today?" The flower replied, "I am good, thank you! I am excited to become a big flower soon!" The bee smiled and said, "That's great! I can't wait to see you grow big and strong!" As the days went by, the little flower grew bigger and bigger. It became the most beautiful flower in the garden. The bee came back to visit and said, "Wow, you have become such a big flower! I am so happy for you!" The flower smiled and said, "Thank you, bee! I am happy too!" And they all lived happily ever after in the cheerful garden.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a hidden path in the woods. Tim walked down the path and found a big temple. The temple was old and had many trees around it. Inside the temple, Tim met a talking bird. The bird said, "Hello, I am Bob. This temple is very old. Some of the food here is bad. It can rot." Tim looked around and saw some old food on the ground. It was brown and smelled bad. Tim and Bob became friends. They played games and talked in the temple. They were careful not to touch the bad food. Before it was time to go home, Tim said, "I will come back and see you, Bob." Bob was happy and said, "I will wait for you, Tim."

One day, a boy named Tim found a key. It was a big, shiny key. Tim was an honest boy, so he wanted to find the owner of the key. He asked his friends if they knew who lost the key, but no one knew. Tim was sad because he wanted to help. Then, Tim saw a man with a spray bottle. The man was spraying water on the flowers. Tim thought the man might know about the key. He walked up to the man and showed him the key. The man smiled and said, "That's my key! Thank you for finding it." Tim was happy that he found the owner of the key. The man was very thankful and let Tim help spray the flowers. Tim had fun spraying the water and helping the man. He learned that being honest and helping others can be fun and make you feel good.

One day, a cat and a dog were in a big room. The cat saw a dish with good food. The cat was glad and wanted to eat the food. The dog saw the cat and said, "Wait, I want some too!" So, they both decided to share the food. The cat and the dog started to rush to the dish. They both wanted to get there first. They ran fast and bumped into each other. The cat said, "Ouch! That hurt!" The dog said, "I'm sorry, let's try again." They tried to rush to the dish again, but they bumped into each other once more. This time, the dish fell on the floor. All the food was gone. The cat and the dog were sad. They didn't get to eat the good food.
