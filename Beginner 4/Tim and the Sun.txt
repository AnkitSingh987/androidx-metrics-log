Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Sun

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. The sun was bright, and the water was calm. Tim saw a jellyfish in the water. He was scared of the jellyfish, but his mom said, "Don't worry, it won't hurt you." Tim saw a big towel on the sand. He wanted to make a tent. His mom showed him how to fold the towel to make it stand up. They sat under the tent and talked. "Mom, why was I scared of the jellyfish?" Tim asked. "Sometimes, we are scared of things we don't know," said his mom. Tim thought about it and said, "I want to learn more, so I won't be scared." His mom smiled and hugged him. They had a fun day at the beach, and Tim learned that knowing more helps to not be scared.

Once upon a time, there was a big ranch. On this ranch, there were many animals. There were cows, pigs, and chickens. The animals were happy on the ranch. One day, it rained a lot. The rain was very bitter and cold. All the animals wanted to stay inside. The little pig said, "Brrr, it is too cold to play outside!" The cow agreed, "Yes, let's stay in the barn." The rain made a big puddle. The puddle was so big, it started to soak the ground. The animals watched the water from the barn. They were glad to be warm and dry. When the rain stopped, the sun came out. The animals went outside to play. They splashed in the puddle and had fun. The ranch was a happy place again.

There once was a young boy. He only had a bit of food. He knew he mustn't rest, but he was so tired. He had been walking for what felt like days. Finally, he found a place he thought he could rest. He settled down under a big tree and waited for the sun to wake him up. But the sun never woke him up. The little boy was too tired and he closed his eyes and he fell asleep. When he woke up again, it was dark and he was still in the same spot. He was so scared his heart was pounding. He couldn't rest anymore. He needed to find food and somewhere to sleep. He never made it. He was alone and lost in the dark and he never found his way home.

Once upon a time, in a big green park, there was a little bird named Bobo. Bobo was unique because he had a blue tail. He loved to fly and play with his friends. One sunny day, Bobo saw something on the ground. Bobo decided to dive down and take a closer look. When he got close, he saw a wiggly worm. The worm was trying to find a home. Bobo wanted to help the worm find a home, so he called his friends. Bobo and his friends looked around the park. They found a nice, soft spot for the worm to live. They all said goodbye to the worm and flew back up to the sky. They were happy they could help their new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big box. A cat named Tom lived in the box. Tom was a happy cat. He liked to play in the box. The box was just the right size for him. It fit Tom very well. One day, Tom heard a noise outside the box. Tom was sad. He did not like noises. He wanted to see what it was. Tom jumped out of the box and saw a small dog named Max. Max was sad too. He did not like the noise either. Tom and Max looked around. They found a big drum making the noise. It was not a scary thing after all! Tom and Max became friends. They played together and forgot about the noise. In the end, they both fit in the big box and were happy.

Once upon a time there was a girl called Sarah. She was three years old and had a dog called Brownie. Every summer, Sarah would take Brownie for a walk in the park and Brownie loved it! But one day, Sarah noticed a fierce cat. The cat was teasing Brownie. Sarah was really mad and shouted at the cat. "Go away!" she said. The cat ran away but Sarah wasn't happy. She knew the cat would be back. So the next day, Sarah took Brownie to the park again... but they were careful this time. They were very careful not to let the fierce cat get close to them. And they were lucky: they had a lovely day in the park, free of teasing cats!
