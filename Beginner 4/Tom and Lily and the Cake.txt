Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Cake

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play chess. Chess is a game with black and white pieces. They move the pieces on a board with squares. One day, Tom and Lily are playing chess at Tom's house. Tom's mom is in the kitchen. She is making a cake. She tells Tom and Lily to be quiet and not to touch anything. Tom and Lily are having fun. They take turns to move their pieces. Tom is winning. He has more pieces than Lily. Lily is sad. She wants to win too. Then, the phone rings. It is a loud ring. Tom's mom goes to answer it. She talks to someone on the phone. Tom and Lily hear the ring. They look at the phone. They are curious. They want to see who is calling. They leave the chess board and go to the phone. They see a button on the phone. It is red. It says "hang up". They wonder what it does. They press the button. The phone stops ringing. Tom's mom stops talking. She says "hello? hello?" She is confused. She does not hear anyone on the phone. She goes to the phone. She sees Tom and Lily. They are holding the phone. They are smiling. They think they did something funny. Tom's mom is angry. She says "Tom! Lily! What did you do? You hung up the phone! That was rude! That was my friend! She was telling me something important!" Tom and Lily are scared. They did not know they did something bad. They did not know they made Tom's mom angry. They did not know what "hang up" means. They say "sorry, mom. Sorry, mom. We did not know. We were curious. We wanted to see the phone. We did not mean to be rude." Tom's mom is still angry. But she sees that Tom and Lily are sorry. She sees that they are little. She sees that they did not understand. She sighs. She says "it's okay, Tom. It's okay, Lily. I know you did not know. But you should not touch the phone. You should not press the button. You should ask me first. You should be careful. You should be polite." Tom and Lily nod. They say "yes, mom. Yes, mom. We will be careful. We will be polite. We will ask you first. We will not touch the phone. We will not press the button." Tom's mom hugs them. She says "good, Tom. Good, Lily. I love you. Now, go back to your chess game. And be quiet. I have to call my friend again. And finish the cake." Tom and Lily hug her back. They say "we love you too, mom. Thank you, mom. We will go back to our chess game. And we will be quiet." They go back to the chess board. They see that their pieces are still there. They resume their game. They are happy. They are friends. They are learning.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy and her baby sister named Lily. They loved to play together. One day, Lucy and Lily were playing in their room. Lucy was happy, but Lily was sad. Lily wanted to go outside and play, but the door was locked. "Lucy, can you unlock the door?" asked Lily. Lucy tried to unlock the door, but she was too small. She couldn't reach the lock. They both felt sad because they couldn't go outside to play. Just then, their dog, Max, came running into the room. Max saw that Lucy and Lily were sad. He wanted to help them. Max jumped up and unlocked the door with his paw. Lucy, Lily, and Max were all very surprised! Now they could go outside and play. Lucy, Lily, and Max went outside and played all day. They were very happy, and Lily was not sad anymore.

Once upon a time, in a tidy little house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys and eat yummy food. One day, Tim found a big spoon in the kitchen. He decided to take the spoon on an adventure. Tim and the spoon went outside to play. They saw a little bug on the ground. The bug started to move, and Tim and the spoon decided to follow it. They walked and followed the bug all around the yard. They saw flowers, trees, and even a big rock. After a fun day of following the bug, Tim and the spoon went back to the tidy little house. Tim put the spoon back in the kitchen where it belonged. He felt happy that he had a fun adventure with his new friend, the spoon.
