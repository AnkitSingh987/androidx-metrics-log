Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and the Star

Sara loves to sing. She has a microphone that her dad gave her. She likes to pretend she is a star on stage. She sings loud and happy songs. One day, Sara cannot find her microphone. She looks in her room, under her bed, in her closet. She is sad and angry. She wants to sing, but she has no microphone. She goes to the living room, where her mom is reading a book. She says, "Mom, have you seen my microphone? I need it to sing." Mom looks at Sara and smiles. She says, "I have not seen your microphone, but maybe you can search for it in the basement. There is a big box of toys there. Maybe your microphone is hiding there." Sara runs to the basement. She opens the big box of toys. She sees many things: dolls, cars, puzzles, books. She digs and digs. She hopes to find her microphone. At last, she sees something shiny and black. She pulls it out. It is her microphone! She is so happy. She hugs it and says, "I found you! You are my favorite toy. I missed you so much." She runs back to the living room. She shows her mom her microphone. She says, "Mom, look! I found my microphone. I searched and searched, and I was successful. Can I sing for you now?" Mom nods and claps. She says, "Of course, you can sing for me. You are a great singer. I am proud of you for searching and finding your microphone. You are a smart and brave girl." Sara smiles and sings. She sings loud and happy songs. She feels like a star on stage. She loves her microphone and her mom. She is happy.

Ben and Lily were restless. They did not want to nap. They wanted to play. They saw a big jar of jelly on the table. They had an idea. "Let's play shoot the jelly!" Ben said. "How do we play?" Lily asked. "We use our fingers as guns and make noises. Pew pew pew!" Ben said. They climbed on the chairs and reached for the jar. They opened it and dipped their fingers in the jelly. They pointed at each other and pretended to shoot. "Pew pew pew! I got you!" Ben said. "No, you missed! Pew pew pew! I got you!" Lily said. They laughed and made a mess. Jelly was everywhere. On their faces, on their clothes, on the table, on the floor. Mom came in and saw the mess. She was not happy. "Ben! Lily! What are you doing?" Mom said. "We are playing shoot the jelly!" Ben said. "That's not a game! That's a waste of food! And look at the mess you made! You need to clean up and go to nap right now!" Mom said. Ben and Lily felt sorry. They did not mean to make Mom angry. They helped Mom clean up the mess. They said sorry to Mom. They went to nap. They dreamed of jelly.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a toy box with many toys. One day, she saw a big cube in her toy box. The cube had many colors on it. Lily liked the cube a lot. Lily went to her friend, Tom. She said, "Look at my cube, Tom! It is very important to me. I need it to play and have fun." Tom looked at the cube and said, "Wow! That is a nice cube, Lily. Can I play with it too?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, we can play with it together." Lily and Tom played with the cube all day. They made up many games with the cube. They laughed and had fun. At the end of the day, Lily said, "Thank you, Tom, for playing with me and my important cube. We had a great time." Tom smiled and said, "Thank you, Lily. I had fun too. We can play with your cube again soon." And they did.

Once upon a time, there was a popular street where all the kids liked to play. One day, a boy named Tim and a girl named Sue were playing near the street. They saw a big tree and decided to have a race to see who could lean on the tree first. "Ready, set, go!" said Tim. They both ran as fast as they could. But when they got close to the tree, something unexpected happened. A big, funny bird jumped out of the tree and started to talk. "Hello, kids!" said the bird. Tim and Sue were very surprised. They didn't know that birds could talk. The bird told them that he was a magic bird who lived in the tree. He said he could grant them one wish. Tim and Sue looked at each other and thought for a moment. They decided to wish for a big, fun playground on the popular street for all the kids to play on. The magic bird nodded, and with a wave of his wings, a beautiful playground appeared. All the kids came running to play, and Tim, Sue, and the magic bird had a great time together.
