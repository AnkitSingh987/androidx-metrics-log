Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Red Cat and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a big red cat named Tom. Tom loved to take a long nap in the warm sun. One day, while he was napping, he smelled something delicious. He woke up and followed the smell to find out what it was. Tom found a big cake on a table. He wanted to eat the cake, but it was too high for him to reach. Tom saw a small chair nearby. He pushed the chair to the table and climbed up. Now he could reach the cake! But, as he took a bite, he felt a hot burn on his paw. Tom had not seen the hot candle on the cake. His paw hurt very much from the burn. He jumped down from the chair and cried. The cake fell on the floor and broke into pieces. Tom's paw hurt, and he could not eat the delicious cake. It was a bad day for Tom.

Once upon a time, a little rabbit named Tim lived near a thick forest. Tim loved to play in the forest with his friends. One day, Tim and his friends were playing hide and seek in the forest. Tim wanted to find the best hiding spot. He saw a big tree and decided to hide behind it. While he was hiding, he heard a loud noise. Tim was scared and started to rush back to his friends. But Tim got lost in the thick forest. He called out for his friends, but they could not hear him. Tim was very sad and scared. He could not find his way back home.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She liked to collect pretty things. One day, she saw a little bird in her yard. The bird was worried. It could not fly. Mia wanted to help the bird. She gave the bird some food and water. The bird felt better. Mia was happy to see the bird eat and drink. After a few days, the bird could fly again. It flew up into the sky. Mia waved goodbye to the bird. She was happy she could help the bird. And the bird was happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a humble boy named Tim. He liked to play a game called golf. Tim would hit a small ball with a stick and try to get the ball into a hole. One day, Tim went to play golf with his dad. They had a lot of fun. They hit the ball far and close. Sometimes, the ball went into the hole. Tim was very happy when that happened. As they played, the sun began to go down. Tim and his dad knew it was time to go home. They said goodbye to the golf game and walked back to their house. Tim felt happy and tired after a fun day of playing golf with his dad.

Once upon a time, there was a big palace. In the palace, there lived a king, a queen, and their little boy. The little boy was very adventurous. He liked to play outside and explore new things. One day, the little boy found a small box. He wanted to bury the box in the ground. So, he took a shovel and dug a hole in the palace garden. He put the box in the hole and covered it with dirt. He was very happy with his work. Later, the king saw the little boy playing in the garden. The king asked, "What did you do, my son?" The little boy said, "I found a box and buried it in the ground!" The king smiled and said, "You are very adventurous, just like me when I was young. Let's dig it up together and see what's inside the box." They dug up the box and found a beautiful treasure inside. They were very happy and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a big cow. The cow loved to chew grass all day. The grass was delicious and green. The cow had many friends who also liked to chew the grass. One day, the cow and her friends found a new field. This field had the most delicious grass they had ever seen. They were very happy and chewed the grass all day long. But the next day, the cow and her friends felt sick. Their tummies hurt because they ate too much delicious grass. They were sad and couldn't play anymore. The cow learned that eating too much can make you feel bad.
