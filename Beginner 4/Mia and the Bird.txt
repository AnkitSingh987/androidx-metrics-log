Category: Beginner
Title: Mia and the Bird

Once upon a time, a little girl named Mia had a doll. Mia loved her doll very much. Every day, Mia played with her doll in the park. One day, Mia saw a big tree that was easy to climb. She thought it would be fun to play with her doll up in the tree. Mia climbed the tree with her doll. She was very happy. But then, something happened. A big bird came and took her doll. Mia was very sad. She wanted her doll back. The bird flew far away with the doll. Mia thought of a plan to get her doll back. She found a stick and waited for the bird to come back. When the bird came back, Mia tried to strike the bird with the stick. But, the bird dropped the doll before Mia could hit it. The doll fell into a bush, and Mia found a new toy hidden in the bush. Mia was happy to have her doll back and now she had a new toy to play with too. The big bird flew away, and Mia learned to be careful when playing in the tree.

One day, a girl named Lily went to visit her aunt. Her aunt lived in a small house near the woods. Lily loved to play with her aunt's dog, Spot. They would run and jump in the big yard. As they were playing, Lily saw a big, rough rock. "Aunt, can we wash this rock?" she asked. Her aunt said, "Yes, let's wash it and see what happens." They took the rock to the water and started washing it. As they washed the rock, something unexpected happened. The rock started to change color! It turned from gray to shiny gold. "Wow!" said Lily, "We found a gold rock!" Her aunt was very surprised too. They were so happy that they decided to keep the rock and show it to their friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little blue bird. The bird loved to fly high up in the sky. One day, the bird saw a sad boy. The boy was upset because he lost his toy. The blue bird wanted to help the boy. The bird flew around and looked for the toy. It saw the toy near a big tree. The bird wanted to prevent the boy from being upset any longer. So, the blue bird picked up the toy with its beak and brought it back to the boy. The boy was very happy to see his toy again. He thanked the little blue bird. From that day on, the boy and the blue bird became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy man with a big mustache. He lived in a small house near the park. One day, he went for a walk with his dog, Spot. While they were walking, the clumsy man tripped on a rock and fell down. "Ouch!" he said. Spot looked at him and started to cry. The man said, "Don't cry, Spot. I am okay." A little girl saw the man and his dog. She walked up to them and said, "I like your mustache!" The clumsy man smiled and said, "Thank you!" They all laughed and played together in the park. From that day on, they became good friends and had many more fun days at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Elise. She was playing in the garden, when suddenly she saw a big, round bubble floating in the wind. She was excited! She wanted to get the bubble, so she rushed towards it. "Wait!" her mum shouted. She was walking quickly towards Elise. "You're not allowed to rush," her mum said. Elise was impatient. She wanted to go get the bubble, while it was still there! She bounced on her toes, excited to reach it. "No," said her mum. "Wait until I catch up with you, then you can get the bubble." Elise listened to her mum and waited for her to reach her. Finally, her mum caught up and Elise raced towards the bubble. But by the time she got there, it had already floated away! Elise was sad. "Come on," said her mum, gently putting her arm around Elise. "Let's go and play something else. That bubble is gone now." Elise smiled, and they went back to the garden to play a different game. She knew that with her mum, she could always find something fun to do!
