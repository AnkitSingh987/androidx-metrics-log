Category: Beginner
Title: The Loud Bull and the Farm

Once upon a time, in a big farm, there was a loud bull. His name was Tom. Tom liked to sing. He sang all day and all night. The other animals on the farm did not like it. They could not sleep. One day, a small bird came to the farm. The bird asked Tom to stop singing. Tom did not want to stop. He liked to sing. The bird was sad. The other animals were sad too. The bird and the animals had a plan. They all sang very loud together. Tom did not like it. He could not sleep. Tom got very tired. In the end, Tom left the farm. The other animals were happy, but Tom was sad.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Bo. He loved to go outside and play. Bo's mom would always tell him to be careful and not run too fast, but he never listened. One day, Bo was playing in the garden and he ran so fast that he tripped over a rock and hurt his knee. He started to cry, but his mom was there to help him. She hugged and kissed his knee and said, “It’s okay Bo, I forgive you for not listening.” Then, Bo had a great idea. He wanted to zoom around the garden, so he asked his mom for help. She picked him up, and together they zoomed around the garden. Bo laughed and giggled the whole time. Finally, they stopped to rest in the sunshine, and Bo’s mom said, “Look at the dry grass, Bo. You were running so fast that it dried up.” Bo felt so bad, but his mom said, “Don’t worry, I forgive you. Now let’s go get a drink before we go inside.” Bo and his mom had a great time the rest of the day, and Bo knew that it was important to listen to his mom. He was so glad that his mom had forgiven him.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Will. He was a regular boy who loved to play and have fun. One sunny day, Will went to the park with his mom. At the park, Will saw a little girl who was sad. He went to her and asked, "Why are you sad?" The little girl answered, "I lost my toy." Will wanted to help her, so he said, "Let's look for your toy together." They looked under the slide, behind the swings, and in the sandbox. Finally, they found the toy near the big tree. The little girl was so happy and said, "Thank you, Will!" From that day on, Will and the little girl became best friends, and they always played together at the park.

Once upon a time, in a dark forest, there lived a little spirit. The spirit loved to taste sweet fruit from the trees. Every day, the spirit would fly around and find the best fruit to eat. One day, the spirit found a new tree with big red fruit. The spirit was very happy and wanted to taste the fruit. So, the spirit picked one and took a big bite. But, the fruit did not taste good. It was very bad and made the spirit feel sick. The spirit tried to fly away, but it could not. The bad taste from the fruit made the spirit too weak. The little spirit stayed in the dark forest, feeling sad and sick. And it never tasted sweet fruit again.

Lila and Ben were bored. They had no toys to play with. They looked around the room and saw a big bowl of popcorn on the table. "Can we eat some popcorn?" Lila asked. "Mom said no. She said it is for the movie night later." Ben said. "But I am hungry. And popcorn is yummy." Lila said. She reached for the bowl and grabbed a handful of popcorn. She put some in her mouth and crunched. "Hey, that's not fair. Give me some too." Ben said. He also took some popcorn and ate it. He made a funny face. "Yuck. This popcorn is not good. It is not salty or sweet. It is original." He said. "What is original?" Lila asked. "It means it has no flavor. It is boring." Ben said. He looked at the bowl and saw a red button. He pushed it. Suddenly, the bowl started to rock. It made a loud noise. Popcorn flew out of the bowl and landed everywhere. "Wow. This is fun. Look at the popcorn. It is like snow." Lila said. She laughed and threw some popcorn at Ben. "Hey, stop that. You are making a mess." Ben said. He threw some popcorn back at Lila. They had a popcorn fight. They forgot they were bored. Mom came into the room and saw the popcorn everywhere. She was not happy. "What are you doing? You ruined the popcorn. And the bowl. And the carpet." She said. She picked up the bowl and saw the button. "This is not a popcorn bowl. This is a popcorn maker. You turned it on. That's why it rocked and popped." She said. She shook her head. "You are in big trouble. You have to clean up this mess. And no movie night for you." She said. Lila and Ben felt sorry. They said they were sorry. They helped Mom clean up the popcorn. They learned their lesson. They never touched the original popcorn maker again.
