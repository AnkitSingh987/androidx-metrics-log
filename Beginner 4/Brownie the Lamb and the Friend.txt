Category: Beginner
Title: Brownie the Lamb and the Friend

Once upon a time, there was a lamb named Brownie. Brownie was very happy but one day that changed. His best friend had gone away and he knew he would never come back. Brownie was so sad he wept and wept. No matter how hard Brownie tried, his friend would never come back. In his sadness, he saw the telephone and knew he could call his friend. But without a number, Brownie had no way to call. Brownie sighed then picked up the telephone. He began to cry yet again almost as if he already knew what would happen next. He dialed the number, but to his disappointment, it was wrong. When he hung up the telephone, Brownie started to weep even harder and he knew his sadness would stay for a very long time.

Once upon a time, there was a big storm. The rain made everyone feel miserable. The wind blew the trees, and the sky was very dark. In a small house, two friends, a cat and a dog, lived together. During the storm, they started to quarrel. The cat wanted to play with a toy, but the dog wanted it too. They both felt sad and didn't want to share. Then, the cat and the dog saw how the storm made everyone feel bad. They stopped their quarrel and decided to share the toy. They played together and felt happy again. The storm went away, and the sun came out. The cat and the dog learned that sharing is better than quarreling.

Lily liked to look at the birds in the birdcage. She liked their colors and their songs. She wanted to touch them and play with them. But her mom said no. She said the birds were not toys. They were alive and needed space and care. One day, Lily saw the birdcage door was open. She thought it was a chance to get a bird. She reached her hand inside the cage. But the birds did not like that. They flew away from her. They pecked at her fingers. They made loud noises. "Ow! Stop it!" Lily cried. She was angry and scared. She pulled her hand out of the cage. She saw a bird on the floor. It had fallen out of the cage. It looked hurt. Lily felt sorry for it. She tried to pick it up. But the bird was stubborn. It did not want her help. It bit her hand. It flapped its wings. "Ow! Stop it!" Lily cried again. She slapped the bird. She wanted it to leave her alone. But the bird did not like that. It made a louder noise. It flew up in the air. It landed on Lily's head. It pulled her hair. "Ow! Stop it!" Lily screamed. She ran to her mom. She wanted her mom to make the bird go away. But her mom was not happy. She saw the birdcage door was open. She saw the bird on Lily's head. She saw the other birds in the cage were scared. "Lily, what did you do?" her mom asked. She was angry and worried. She took the bird off Lily's head. She put it back in the cage. She closed the door. She checked the birds for injuries. She hugged Lily. "Lily, you should not touch the birds. They are not toys. They are alive and need space and care. You hurt them and yourself. You scared them and me. You were naughty and selfish. You need to say sorry to the birds and to me." Lily felt bad. She knew her mom was right. She looked at the birds in the cage. They looked at her. They were quiet. Lily said sorry to the birds and to her mom. She learned her lesson. She did not touch the birds again. She only looked at them and listened to them. She liked their colors and their songs.

On Tuesday, Lily and Ben went to the park with Mom. They liked to play on the swings, the slide and the sandbox. Mom told them to be obedient and stay close to her. She said there were some big dogs in the park that could be scary. Lily and Ben listened to Mom and had fun. But then they saw a big red ball near the fence. They wanted to play with it. They forgot what Mom said and ran to the ball. They did not see the big dog that was behind the fence. The big dog saw Lily and Ben and got angry. It barked and jumped over the fence. It ran to Lily and Ben and bit them. Lily and Ben screamed and cried. Mom heard them and ran to them. She was very scared and sad. She tried to help them and called for help. The big dog ran away. Mom hugged Lily and Ben and took them to the hospital. They had many hurts and needed stitches. They were very sorry and said they would never disobey Mom again. Mom said she loved them but they had to be more careful. They learned a hard lesson.
