Category: Beginner
Title: Mum and Davey and the Park

Mum and Davey were at the park. Davey liked to swing. Mum said: "Davey, do you want a push on the swing?" Davey was so excited. He shouted: "Yeah!" Mum gave Davey a big push. Davey went so high that he felt like he was flying. He laughed and shouted: "More, mum!" Mum laughed and gave him a few more pushes. Later, Davey wanted to buy something from the ice cream truck. He asked mum: "Mummy, can I buy something?" Mum smiled and said: "What would you like?" Davey said: "I want a mint ice cream! It's cheap." Mum said: "Okay, here you go!" Davey was so happy and quickly bit into the ice cream. It was cool and tasted so good. He could taste the mint and he smiled. Mum was happy too. She said: "Let's go back to the swing!" Davey said: "Yay! Swing!" So, mum and Davey went back to the swing, and Davey had lots of swings with mum. He was so happy and tired after all the fun they had.

Tom and Sue were playing with their dolls in the garden. They liked to pretend that they were a family and that their dolls were their children. They had a big house made of cardboard and some old clothes for their dolls. One day, a lady came to visit their mom. She had a big bag with her. She said hello to Tom and Sue and gave them a smile. She asked if she could see their dolls. Tom and Sue were happy to show her their dolls and their house. But the lady did not like their dolls. She said they were dirty and ugly. She said they needed new dolls that were clean and pretty. She took out some dolls from her bag and gave them to Tom and Sue. She said they were a gift from her. Tom and Sue did not want the new dolls. They loved their old dolls. They said thank you, but they wanted to keep their old dolls. The lady got angry. She said they were rude and ungrateful. She grabbed their old dolls and threw them in the trash. She said they had to play with the new dolls or she would tell their mom. Tom and Sue were very sad and angry. They cried and shouted at the lady. They said she was mean and bad. They said they hated her and her new dolls. They ran to their mom and told her what the lady did. Their mom was shocked and angry too. She went to the trash and got their old dolls back. She cleaned them and hugged them. She said they were sorry and that they loved their old dolls. She went to the lady and told her to leave. She said she was not welcome in their house. She said she was wrong and cruel. The lady was ashamed. She realized she had made a big mistake. She had hurt Tom and Sue and their mom. She had been rude and selfish. She said she was sorry and that she wanted to make up. She asked if they could forgive her and be friends. Tom and Sue and their mom looked at each other. They were still sad and angry, but they also felt sorry for the lady. They knew she had made a bad choice, but they also knew she was sorry. They decided to forgive her and give her a chance. They said they accepted her apology and that they would try to be friends. They gave her a hug and a smile. The lady was happy and relieved. She thanked them and said she was sorry again. She said she had learned a lesson. She said she would never judge or hurt anyone again. She said she would respect and love everyone. She said she would be a good friend. They all went inside and had some cookies and milk. They talked and laughed and played with their dolls. They had a good time and became friends. They learned to forgive and to be kind. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big white dog named Fluffy. Fluffy was very compassionate and loved to help others. One day, Fluffy saw a small bird lying on the ground. The bird looked sad and tired. Fluffy knew he had to do something to help his new friend. The next day, Fluffy went on a long walk to find something to make the bird feel better. He found a soft, warm blanket and brought it back to the bird. Fluffy put the blanket around the bird, and the bird started to feel better. The bird knew Fluffy was trying to help, and it made the bird happy. After a few days, the bird felt strong enough to fly again. The bird was so thankful for Fluffy's help. Fluffy and the bird became best friends, and they played together every day. The bird would fly high in the sky, and Fluffy would run and jump on the ground. They had so much fun, and they were happy to have each other.
