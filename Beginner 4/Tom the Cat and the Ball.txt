Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a happy cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with yarn. Every day, he would find a ball of yarn and roll it around the house. One day, Tom's yarn got stuck under a big chair. Tom tried to get it out, but he couldn't. He started to frown because he was sad. He wanted to play with his yarn. Then, Tom's friend, a little girl named Sue, came to help. Sue moved the chair and got the yarn out for Tom. Tom was happy again. He played with the yarn and Sue all day. They had a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved candy. His favorite candy was very expensive. So, he only got it on special days. One day, Tim got a big bag of his favorite candy. He was so happy! He wanted to share his candy with his friends. So, Tim spread the candy on a table for all his friends to see. His friends came and saw the candy. They were happy too. They all took a piece of candy and said "thank you" to Tim. They all ate the candy and smiled. Tim felt good about sharing his special candy.

One day, a little girl named Daisy went to the park. The park was very quiet. Daisy liked to play on the slide. She would climb up and slide down, again and again. She was very happy. One sunny day, Daisy saw a big, quiet cat near the slide. The cat was very nice. Daisy wanted to play with the cat. The cat liked Daisy too. They became good friends. Then, something unexpected happened. The cat jumped on the slide! The cat slid down the slide very fast. Daisy and the cat laughed and played. They had a lot of fun together. From that day on, Daisy and the quiet cat played on the slide every day.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal bird who could fly very high. The bird lived on a big ranch with many animal friends. One day, the bird saw a big cloud in the sky, and it looked like rain was coming. The bird wanted to help its friends on the ranch, so it tried to fly and tell them about the rain. But the bird was very tired from flying high all day. The bird knew it had to help its friends, so it tried to fly even though it was tired. The bird tried to fly, but it was too tired and fell down. The rain came and the ranch got very wet. The bird's friends got wet too, and they were sad. The bird felt bad that it could not help its friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to look up at the sky and see the stars. One day, he saw a small, shiny light in the sky. It was a new planet! Tim was very happy and excited. Tim wanted to know more about the new planet. He asked his mom to whisper a story about it. Mom said, "This planet is a special place where everything is sour. The trees, the grass, and even the water are all sour." Tim listened carefully and smiled. One night, Tim had a dream. He went to the sour planet and played with the sour trees and grass. He even drank some sour water. It was a fun adventure for Tim. When he woke up, he told his mom about his dream. They both laughed and whispered about the sour planet all day.

One day, a young bird named Tim wanted to fly. He saw a tall tree with a vine. Tim thought, "I can fly if I jump from the vine." Tim asked his friend, Cat, "Can you help me fly?" Cat said, "Yes, I will help you." They went to the tall tree together. Tim went up the tree and held the vine. He looked down and was scared. Tim jumped and tried to fly. But, oh no! The vine broke. Tim fell down. Cat tried to catch Tim, but he missed. Tim was sad. He did not fly that day.
