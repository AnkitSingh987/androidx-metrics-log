Category: Beginner
Title: Eva the Girl and the Give

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Eva. She was very curious and one day she saw a tall raven in the sky. She asked her mom, "What is that?" Her mom smiled and said, "That's a raven. They are beautiful, big birds but they don't give hugs." Eva put her hands on her hips and asked, "Why? Why don't they give hugs?" Her mom sighed and said, "Because, Eva, it's not nice to take something without giving something in return. That's a kindness rule we should all follow." Eva thought about this for a moment and then said, "I understand. So if I want to give a hug to the raven, I should give him something too." Her mom nodded with a smile. "That's right, Eva. Giving something to show your kindness can make the world a better place. Like your hug!" Eva smiled wide and said, "I'm going to get something nice to give to the raven!" And she ran off to give the tall raven something in return for its beauty.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big urge to play outside in the mud. He put on his boots and went to the dirty puddle in his yard. As he played, he saw a cute frog. The frog looked at Tim and nodded. Tim nodded back and they became friends. They played in the dirty puddle all day long. When it was time to go home, Tim's mom saw how dirty he was. She shook her head and smiled. Tim and the frog nodded and knew it was time for a bath. They cleaned up and promised to play together again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big book with many words. He liked to try to read the words, but some were hard for him. Tim had a friend, a funny dog named Max. Max had a big, ugly spot on his back, but Tim loved him very much. One day, Tim saw a new word in his book. He did not know this word, so he tried to read it. Max, the dog, was with him. Tim tried and tried, but the word was too hard. Max saw that Tim was sad, so he gave him a big, wet kiss. It made Tim feel better. Tim asked his mom for help with the ugly word. She smiled and told him the word was "friend". Tim was happy, and he hugged Max. Max wagged his tail because he knew that he was Tim's best friend. From that day on, Tim and Max tried to learn more words together.

Once upon a time, there was a big bookcase in a little room. The bookcase was full of colorful books. A small, enthusiastic dog named Spot lived in the house with the bookcase. One day, Spot saw a little girl named Anna. She wanted to read a book from the bookcase. Spot wanted to help Anna. He barked and wagged his tail. Spot led Anna to the bookcase. Anna was happy that Spot helped her. She picked a book from the bookcase and read it with Spot. They both enjoyed the story and had a fun day together.

Anna likes to tie ribbons in her hair. She has many ribbons of different colors. She calls them pastels. She thinks they are amazing and pretty. One day, she wants to tie a new ribbon in her hair. She asks her mom to help her. Her mom is busy making breakfast. She tells Anna to wait a little bit. Anna does not want to wait. She sees a ribbon on the table. It is pastel pink. She thinks it is amazing and pretty. She takes it and tries to tie it in her hair. But she does not know how to tie it well. She makes a big knot. Her mom sees her and smiles. She says, "Anna, what did you do? You made a big knot in your hair. Let me help you." She unties the knot and ties the ribbon in a nice bow. She says, "There, now you look amazing and pretty." Anna smiles and hugs her mom. She says, "Thank you, mom. I love you. And I love pastels."
