Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Fat Cat and the Bird

One day, a fat cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. When he got to the top, he saw a little bird. The bird had a big smile on its face. "Hi, cat!" said the bird. "Why do you climb my tree?" Tom smiled back and said, "I just want to see what's up here." As they were talking, a big wind came and shook the tree. Tom was scared and held on tight. But the bird just laughed and said, "Don't worry, I'll help you get down." The bird flew around Tom and lifted him up with its strong wings. They flew down to the ground together. Tom was surprised and happy. He had made a new friend, and he couldn't wait to tell everyone about his adventure with the bird.

Once upon a time, there was an adorable little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball. He would run and jump all day long. One day, Spot's ball rolled under a big tree. Spot looked and looked, but he could not find it. Then, Spot saw a little bird sitting on a branch. "Hello, little bird," said Spot. "Can you help me find my ball? It is under the tree." The little bird flew down and looked under the tree. She found the ball and gave it back to Spot. "Thank you, little bird," said Spot. "You are very kind." The little bird smiled and said, "You're welcome, Spot. Let's play together and change the game. Instead of playing alone, we can be friends and have more fun!" Spot agreed, and they played together all day long. And from that day on, Spot and the little bird were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a nice little girl named Lily. Lily had a big circle of friends. They liked to play together and have fun. Lily was always nice to her friends and they were nice to her too. One day, Lily's friend Timmy was sad. He had a big problem. He didn't know how to fix it. Lily wanted to help Timmy, so she asked her circle of friends to support him. They all said yes, because they were nice friends. Together, the circle of friends helped Timmy solve his problem. They all worked together and supported each other. In the end, Timmy was happy again. The nice circle of friends learned that when they support each other, they can do anything. And they all lived happily ever after.

Sara and Ben liked to play on the beach. They made sand castles and looked for shells. One day, they saw a big boat on the water. Sara wanted to go closer and see the boat. Ben said no, it was too far and the tide was coming in. But Sara did not listen. She said she was a big girl and she could swim. She insisted on going to the boat. She ran into the water and swam away from Ben. Ben was scared and called for Sara to come back. The boat was busy with people and noise. Sara could not see Ben or the shore. She felt the water pull her deeper and deeper. She was cold and tired and scared. She cried for help, but no one heard her. Ben ran to find a grown-up. He saw a lifeguard and told him about Sara. The lifeguard got his whistle and his float and ran to the water. He saw Sara's head bobbing in the waves. He swam to her and grabbed her. He brought her back to the beach. Sara was shivering and crying. The lifeguard wrapped her in a towel and gave her some water. He told her she was very lucky he saw her. He said the tide was very strong and dangerous. He said she should never go into the water without a grown-up. He said she should listen to Ben, who was a good friend. Sara said she was sorry. She hugged Ben and thanked him. She said she was silly and stubborn. She said she learned her lesson. She said she would never insist on doing something bad again. Ben said he was glad she was safe. He said he forgave her. He said they could still play on the beach, but only near the sand. Sara agreed. They smiled and held hands. They went to their sand castle and added some shells. They were happy and careful.
