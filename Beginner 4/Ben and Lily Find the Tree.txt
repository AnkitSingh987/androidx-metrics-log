Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Lily Find the Tree

Ben and Lily were playing in the garden. They liked to dig holes and look for worms. They had a big bucket to put the worms in. "Look, I found a big one!" Lily said, holding up a long, wriggly worm. "Wow, that's a good one!" Ben said. "Let me see!" He reached out to take the worm, but Lily pulled it back. "Joke!" she said, laughing. "It's not a worm, it's a carrot!" She showed him the orange tip of the carrot. She had found it in the vegetable patch and peeled off some of the skin. Ben laughed too. He thought it was a funny joke. "Let's see if we can find more carrots!" he said. They ran to the vegetable patch and started to dig. They found some small carrots and some big carrots. They put them in their bucket. They were having so much fun, they didn't notice the deep hole next to them. It was where their dad had dug up a tree stump. Lily stepped back and fell into the hole. She screamed. "Help! Help!" she cried. Ben heard her and ran to the hole. He saw her lying at the bottom, holding her bucket. "Lily, are you okay?" he asked. "I'm okay, but I'm scared!" she said. "It's deep and dark!" Ben looked around. He saw a rope hanging from a tree. He had an idea. "Hold on, Lily, I'll get you out!" he said. He tied the rope to the bucket handle and threw it down to Lily. "Grab the rope, Lily, and I'll pull you up!" he said. Lily grabbed the rope and held on tight. Ben pulled and pulled. He was strong. He lifted Lily out of the hole. She was dirty and shaky, but she was happy. "Thank you, Ben, you saved me!" she said. She hugged him and gave him a carrot. They ate the carrots and went back to their house. They told their mom and dad what happened. They were proud of Ben and Lily. They said they were brave and smart. They gave them more carrots and a big hug. They said they loved them. Ben and Lily smiled. They loved them too.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a penny on the ground. He was very happy. He wanted to buy a toy car with it. But his big sister, Sue, wanted the penny too. She was very impatient. Tim did not want to give the penny to Sue. So, they both tried to hold the penny at the same time. They pulled and pulled. Then, they heard a snap sound. The penny was now in two parts! Tim and Sue were both sad. They could not buy anything with the broken penny. But then, their mom came and gave them a new penny each. Tim and Sue learned to share and not fight over things. They both got toy cars and played together happily.

Once, a small bear wanted to go to the market to get delicious treats. He packed his bag with food and set off. On the way, he encountered a stream. Unfortunately, he couldn't cross it. Suddenly, a little bunny popped out of the bushes. The bear was sure he saw a hint of mischief in the bunny's eyes. The bunny said, "I'll help you cross the stream if you give me half of your food!" The bear refused, knowing the bunny wouldn't keep his word. The bunny grew angry and tried to push him into the water. But the bear was determined to not give up his treats. Luckily, a friendly frog saw what was happening and jumped in to save the bear. Together, they were able to push the bunny away. The bear thanked the frog and crossed the stream safely. The bear learned a valuable lesson that day - never give up and be kind to others in need. He went to the market and shared his treats with the adorable frog. They both enjoyed the market and left with full bellies and smiles on their faces.

One day, a girl named Lucy went to play outside. She saw her friend Tom. Tom had a stamp in his hand. Lucy asked, "What do you make with the stamp?" Tom said, "I make pretty pictures with it." Lucy wanted to try too. So, Tom gave her the stamp. Lucy pressed the stamp on a paper. It made a nice flower shape. But the stamp was dry. No color came out. Lucy and Tom went to find some color. They found a small box of colors. They put color on the dry stamp. Lucy pressed the stamp on the paper again. This time, a pretty, colorful flower appeared. They both smiled and made more pretty pictures with the stamp.
