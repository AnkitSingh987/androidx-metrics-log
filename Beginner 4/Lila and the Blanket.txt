Category: Beginner
Title: Lila and the Blanket

Lila liked to do yoga with her mom. Yoga was when they made shapes with their bodies and breathed slowly. It made them feel peaceful and happy. Sometimes, Lila's mom played soft music or lit a candle to make the yoga more special. One day, Lila wanted to do yoga with her mom, but her mom was busy. She had to work on her laptop and make some calls. Lila felt sad and bored. She looked around the living room and saw her mom's yoga mat, her music player and her candle. She had an idea. She took her mom's yoga mat and replaced it with her own blanket. She took her mom's music player and replaced it with her own toy piano. She took her mom's candle and replaced it with her own flashlight. She thought she was making her own yoga. She lay down on her blanket and turned on her flashlight. She pressed some keys on her toy piano and made some noises. She tried to make some shapes with her body and breathe slowly, but it did not feel peaceful or happy. It felt silly and wrong. Her mom heard the noises and came to the living room. She saw what Lila had done and felt angry and sad. She said, "Lila, what are you doing? You took my things and replaced them with yours. That is not nice. That is not yoga. That is stealing and lying. You hurt my feelings and broke my trust. You need to say sorry and give me back my things." Lila saw her mom's face and felt sorry and ashamed. She said, "Mom, I'm sorry. I wanted to do yoga with you, but you were busy. I thought I could make my own yoga, but I was wrong. I did not mean to hurt you or break your trust. Please forgive me and take back your things." Her mom hugged her and said, "Lila, I forgive you. I know you love yoga and you love me. But you cannot take things that are not yours and replace them with yours. That is not fair or respectful. You need to ask before you borrow or use something. And you need to be honest and tell the truth. That is how you show love and kindness. That is how you do yoga." Lila nodded and said, "Mom, I understand. I will ask before I use something. I will be honest and tell the truth. I will show love and kindness. I will do yoga the right way." Her mom smiled and said, "Lila, I'm proud of you. You learned a valuable lesson today. And I'm sorry I was busy. How about we do some yoga together now? I have some time and I want to spend it with you." Lila smiled and said, "Yes, mom, I would love that. Thank you for teaching me and forgiving me. I love you, mom." They rolled out their yoga mats and turned on some soft music. They lit the candle and breathed slowly. They made some shapes with their bodies and felt peaceful and happy. They did yoga together and they did it with love.

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse. The mouse lived in a small house near a big tree. The mouse liked to play and eat cheese. One day, the mouse saw a fair. The fair had many colors and fun games. The mouse wanted to go to the fair. So, the mouse ran to the fair and saw many kids. The kids played games and ate sweet treats. The mouse wanted to play too. The mouse saw a game where kids kneel and pick up toys with their mouths. The mouse thought it was fun. The mouse joined the game and started to kneel. The mouse picked up a toy with its mouth. The kids laughed and clapped. The mouse felt happy. After the game, the mouse went back to its small house. The mouse had a fun day at the fair.

One day, a big bear went to the woods. The bear was honest and kind. He liked to help his friends. In the woods, the bear met a little rabbit. The rabbit was sad. "What's wrong?" asked the bear. "I lost my toy," said the rabbit. "Can you help me find it?" The bear nodded and they began to look for the toy. As they looked, they tried not to disturb the other animals. They walked quietly and looked around. Finally, they found the toy under a bush. The rabbit was so happy! "Thank you, bear!" said the rabbit. "You are a good friend." And they played together for the rest of the day.
