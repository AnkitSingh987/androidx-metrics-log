Category: Beginner
Title: A Wonderful Bird with Tim and Park

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. The park was big and peaceful. Tim loved to play there. His mom had a cooler with yummy food for them to eat. While they played, a big bird came and took the cooler. Tim was sad. He wanted his food back. So, Tim and his mom went to find the bird. They looked in the trees and by the pond. They could not find the bird. Then, they saw the bird by a big tree. The bird wanted to show them something. Tim and his mom watched as the bird opened the cooler. Inside, there were new toys for Tim to play with! The bird had put the toys in the cooler to make Tim happy. Tim and his mom were surprised and happy. They played with the bird and the new toys all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Ann. She had a pretty dress with a thin patch on it. One day, Ann went outside to play in the mud. The thin patch on her dress got very dirty. Ann's mom saw the dirty patch and said, "Ann, you need to wash your dress." Ann nodded and took her dress off. She put it in a big tub of water and washed it with soap. The thin patch became clean again. After the dress was dry, Ann put it back on. She went outside and saw a friend. "Look!" she said, "I washed my dress, and the thin patch is clean now." Her friend smiled and said, "Good job, Ann! Now we can play together without getting dirty."

Mum was really busy sorting out the bookshelf. She had lots of books that needed to go into the bookshelf. She was sorting them out very quickly. A little three year old girl come into the room. She looked a bit confused. "What are you doing, mum?" the little girl asked. "I'm sorting out my bookshelf," mum replied. The little girl could see mum was going very fast. "Can I help?" Mum smiled. "Yes," she said. "You can hand me books and I'll put them on the bookshelf." The little girl was very excited. She grabbed the books from the pile and handed them to mum. She was so proud to help her mum in sorting out the bookshelf. Mum and the little girl worked together for a while and soon the bookshelf was organised. "We did it!" said the little girl. Mum smiled and said, "Yes, we did! Great job! It was fun sorting books together!"

One day, a little red bird was flying in the sky. The bird saw a big cloud. The cloud was not happy. The cloud said, "I am sad because I want to be red like you." The bird wanted to help the cloud. The bird said, "I will ask the sun to make you red." The sun heard the bird and made the cloud red. The cloud was very happy. But then, the other clouds did not like the red cloud. They said, "You are not like us. You are red and we are white." The red cloud was sad again. The bird told the red cloud, "It is okay to be different. You are special." The red cloud decided to accept being different. Soon, the other clouds saw how special the red cloud was and they all became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a kind girl named Lily. She loved to plant flowers in her garden. One day, she found a big, yellow sunflower seed. She was very happy and wanted to plant it. Lily dug a small hole in the ground and put the sunflower seed inside. She covered it with dirt and gave it some water. Every day, she would water the seed and watch it grow. Soon, a tiny green plant came out of the ground. The plant grew and grew until it was tall and strong. One day, Lily saw that the sunflower had many seeds. She decided to separate the seeds from the flower. She gave the seeds to her friends so they could plant their own sunflowers. Now, everyone had beautiful, yellow sunflowers in their gardens, all thanks to the kind girl named Lily.
