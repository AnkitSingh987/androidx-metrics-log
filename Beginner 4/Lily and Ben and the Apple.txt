Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Apple

Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They saw a big red apple on the tree. Lily wanted to eat the apple. She climbed on a chair and reached for the apple. But the chair was wobbly and Lily fell down. She hurt her knee and it was bleeding. "Ow, ow, ow!" Lily cried. Ben ran to her and saw the blood. He was scared and sorry. He wanted to help Lily. He ran to the house and got a bandage. He came back and wrapped the bandage around Lily's knee. "There, there," Ben said. "It will be better soon. I'm sorry you fell. Do you want the apple?" Lily looked at the apple. She was still hungry, but she felt guilty. She knew she should have asked Mommy before taking the apple. She shook her head. "No, thank you," Lily said. "I don't want the apple. I want Mommy." Ben nodded. He felt guilty too. He took Lily's hand and they walked to the house. They found Mommy in the kitchen. They told her what happened. Mommy hugged them and kissed their heads. "It's okay, my loves," Mommy said. "I'm glad you're okay. And I'm proud of you for being honest and caring. You can have the apple later, after lunch. But first, let me check your bandage."

Lily and Max were adventurous twins who liked to explore the woods behind their house. They always followed the trail that their dad had made for them, with signs and arrows to show them the way. One day, they saw a new sign that said "Restore the old bridge". They were curious and decided to check it out. They walked along the trail until they reached a creek. There was a wooden bridge that crossed the creek, but it was broken and old. Some of the planks were missing and some were rotten. Lily and Max wondered who wanted to restore the bridge and why. They saw a man on the other side of the creek, holding a hammer and some nails. He waved at them and said, "Hello, kids. Do you like the bridge?" Lily and Max nodded and said, "Yes, but it looks sad and unsafe. What are you doing?" The man smiled and said, "I'm trying to restore the bridge. That means I'm fixing it and making it strong and nice again. I used to play here when I was a kid, and I want to share this place with my grandchildren. Do you want to help me?" Lily and Max agreed and crossed the creek carefully. The man gave them some gloves and showed them how to hammer the nails and replace the planks. They worked hard and had fun. After a while, the bridge looked much better and safer. The man thanked them and said, "You did a great job, kids. You are very adventurous and helpful. Now you can enjoy the bridge and the creek anytime you want. Just be careful and follow the trail." Lily and Max thanked the man and said goodbye. They crossed the bridge and followed the trail back to their house. They felt happy and proud. They had restored the bridge and made a new friend.

One day, a fat cat named Tom was playing in the park. He saw a little bird with a hurt wing. The bird was sad and needed help. Tom wanted to help the bird, so he said, "Bird, I can help you. Let's find someone to fix your wing." They walked and walked until they found a big red car with a loud noise. The car had a sign that said, "Emergency." Tom and the bird asked the people in the car for help. The people said, "We can help the bird, but we need something to wrap the wing." Tom and the bird looked around and found a soft, long thing to wrap the wing. They gave it to the people in the big red car. The people tried to fix the bird's wing, but it was too late. The bird's wing could not be fixed. Tom and the bird were very sad. They said goodbye to the people in the big red car and went back to the park. They knew they tried their best to help, but sometimes things don't have a happy ending. The bird could not fly again, and Tom learned that not all problems can be fixed.
