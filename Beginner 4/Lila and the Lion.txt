Category: Beginner
Title: Lila and the Lion

Lila was a curious girl who liked to explore. One day, she found a big shield in the attic. It was shiny and had a picture of a lion on it. Lila wondered what it was for. She took the shield and went outside. She saw some boys playing with sticks and stones. They pretended to be knights and dragons. Lila wanted to join them, but they said she was too small and had no sword. Lila felt sad, but then she had an idea. She held the shield in front of her and said, "I have a shield! It is stronger than a sword. I can be a knight too!" The boys laughed and threw sticks and stones at her. But Lila did not run away. She stayed behind the shield and blocked them. The shield made a loud noise when it was hit. Lila felt brave and proud. The boys stopped throwing and looked at her. They saw that she was not afraid. They saw that the shield was cool. They said, "Wow, you have a nice shield. Can we see it?" Lila smiled and said, "Yes, you can see it. But you have to be nice and share. And you have to let me play with you." The boys agreed and said, "OK, you can play with us. You can be a knight too. But what is your name?" Lila said, "My name is Lila. And this is my shield. It is my friend."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to play with her toys and make things with paper. One day, she found a pair of scissors in her room. Amy was very excited and wanted to cut paper with the scissors. Amy's mom saw her with the scissors and said, "Amy, be careful with the scissors. They can hurt you if you are not careful." Amy felt a little weak in her hands, but she wanted to try anyway. She started to cut the paper, but then she felt the scissors slip. She tried to hold on, but they were too heavy for her weak hands. Amy knew she had to drop the scissors before they hurt her. The scissors fell on the floor, and Amy was safe. Her mom came over and gave her a big hug. She was proud of Amy for knowing when to let go of the scissors. From that day on, Amy learned to ask for help when she needed it. The moral of the story is to always be careful with things that can hurt you and to ask for help when you need it.

One day, a girl named Sue got a new pet. It was a cute and attractive cat. Sue was very happy. She loved her cat and took good care of it. They played together and had fun. One day, while they were playing, the cat said, "Sue, you are nice to me. You make me happy." Sue was surprised. She did not know her pet could talk! She said, "Wow, you can talk! This is fun. We can be best friends and talk all day." But then, the cat said, "Sue, I need to tell you something important. It is good to be nice to others, not just pets. It makes everyone happy." Sue thought about it and said, "You are right. I will be nice to everyone now." From that day on, Sue was nice to everyone she met. She learned that being kind to others made her happy too. And her talking cat was always there to remind her of the important lesson she learned.

Once upon a time, there was an attractive bird who lived in a big tree. One day, the bird saw a small bug trying to push a big leaf. The bug was having a hard time. The bird wanted to help, but first, he thought of a fun quiz. The bird asked the bug, "What is big and green, and we can find it on trees?" The bug thought hard and said, "A leaf!" The bird was happy with the answer and decided to help the bug push the big leaf. Together, they pushed and pushed, and the leaf moved. The bird and the bug became good friends. They learned that helping each other makes things easier and more fun. The moral of the story is to always help your friends when they need you.
