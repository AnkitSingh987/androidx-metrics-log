Category: Beginner
Title: The Rat and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little rat. The rat was very tired. He wanted to find a place to sleep. He saw a big tree with a hole in it. The rat thought the hole would be a good place to sleep. The rat went up to the tree and asked, "Can I sleep in your hole? I am very tired." The tree said, "Yes, I will allow you to sleep in my hole." The rat was very happy. The rat went inside the hole and fell asleep. The tree was happy to help the tired rat. They both had a good night. The next day, the rat said, "Thank you, tree, for allowing me to sleep in your hole." The tree and the rat became good friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a girl named Lily. Lily loved to store her toys in a big box. One day, she found a shiny jewel in her toy box. She did not know how it got there, but she thought it was very pretty. Lily took the jewel to her friend, Ben. "Look," she said, "I found a jewel in my toy box." Ben looked at the jewel and felt sad. "I lost my jewel yesterday," he said. "I think this is mine." Lily wanted to keep the jewel, but she knew it was right to give it back to Ben. She handed the jewel to him and said, "Here, this is yours." Ben smiled and said, "Thank you, Lily. You are a good friend." Even though Lily was sad to give the jewel away, she felt happy to help her friend. Ben and Lily played together with their toys, and they had a lot of fun. They knew that being kind to each other was more important than any shiny jewel.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a wealthy man. He had a big mirror. Every day, he looked in the mirror and smiled. One day, a little bird flew into the house. The bird saw the mirror and thought it was another bird. It tried to play with the bird in the mirror. The wealthy man saw this and laughed. The man wanted the bird to stop. He said, "Stop, little bird! That is not a real bird. It is just a mirror." The little bird stopped and looked at the man. The man opened the window and the bird flew away. The wealthy man was happy to help the little bird.

One day, a messy dog named Spot set out to play in the park. He saw a big red ball and ran to get it. Spot had lots of fun playing with the ball. Then, he found a shiny knob on a door. He wanted to see what was behind the door. Spot pushed the door with his nose, and it opened. He saw a room full of toys and treats. He was so happy and went inside. Spot played with the toys and ate some treats. The room became very messy because of Spot. Soon, it was time for Spot to go home. He left the room and set off to find his house. On his way, he saw a big puddle and jumped in. Spot got even more messy, but he did not mind. He was a happy, messy dog who had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a cool jet. The jet could fly high in the sky. It had big wings and went very fast. The jet loved to play with the birds and the clouds. One day, the jet met a pretty bird. The bird was very nice and they became friends. The jet and the bird played together all day long. They flew high and low, and the jet would make funny sounds to make the bird laugh. At the end of the day, the jet wanted to say goodbye to the bird. The jet thought it would be nice to give the bird a kiss. But the jet was too big and too fast. When it tried to kiss the bird, it bumped into the bird very hard. The bird got hurt and fell to the ground. The jet felt very sad that it had hurt its friend. From that day on, the jet never tried to kiss anyone again.
