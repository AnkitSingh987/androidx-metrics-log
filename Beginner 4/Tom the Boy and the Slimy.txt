Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Slimy

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom liked to value things by touching them. He liked to feel the texture of everything he saw. One day, he went for a walk with his mom. On the walk, Tom saw a new thing. It was slimy and green. He wanted to touch it and feel its texture. His mom said, "No, Tom! It is disgusting!" But Tom did not listen. Tom touched the slimy green thing. It was so disgusting! The slimy thing stuck to his hand and would not come off. Tom cried and cried, but the slimy thing stayed on his hand. And that was the bad ending of Tom's walk.

Once upon a time there was a shy little girl named Emily. She loved to watch the aeroplanes go by and dream of flying one day. One day Emily went to the library to get some books. She found the perfect book and she was very excited. She sat down and started to read. When she was finished with her book, Emily looked outside and saw an aeroplane in the sky. She started to feel brave and wondered if she could ever fly one. Emily got back to the library and asked the librarian for more aeroplane books. She read more and more about aeroplanes and her dream of getting in an aeroplane came to life. The end.

Tom and Anna were playing in the living room. They had many toys and books to have fun with. But Tom wanted to sit on the big armchair that was by the window. He liked to look at the birds and cars outside. "Move, Anna!" he said. "I want to sit on the armchair!" "No, Tom!" Anna said. "I was here first! You can sit on the sofa!" Tom was angry. He pushed Anna off the armchair and climbed on it. Anna fell on the floor and started to cry. "Tom, that's not nice!" their mom said. She came from the kitchen and saw what happened. "You have to share with your sister. Say sorry to Anna and let her sit on the armchair too." "But mom, I want to sit on the armchair!" Tom said. "It's cozy and soft and warm!" Mom shook her head. She had an idea. She went to the closet and took out a big blanket. She threw it over the armchair and said, "Look, Tom, now the armchair is disgusting! It has bugs and dirt and worms on it! You don't want to sit on it, do you?" Tom looked at the blanket and made a face. He jumped off the armchair and ran to the sofa. He did not like bugs and dirt and worms. He said, "Ew, mom, that's gross! I don't want to sit on the disgusting armchair!" Anna stopped crying. She smiled and said, "I do! I like bugs and dirt and worms! They are fun and cool and friendly!" She ran to the armchair and pulled the blanket off. She saw that it was still cozy and soft and warm. She sat on it and hugged a pillow. Mom winked at Anna and said, "Good girl, Anna. You are brave and smart and kind. You can sit on the armchair as long as you want. And Tom, you can sit on the sofa and finish your puzzle. Maybe later you can switch places and share nicely." Tom nodded. He felt sorry for pushing Anna. He said, "Sorry, Anna. You can have the armchair. I will finish my puzzle and then we can play together." Anna said, "It's okay, Tom. I forgive you. We can play together and share the armchair and the sofa. I love you, Tom." Tom said, "I love you too, Anna." Mom smiled and said, "I love you both. You are my sweet children. Now, let's have some cookies and milk and read a story."

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys. One day, she saw a big toy she wanted. It was on a high shelf. Lily wanted to get it, but her hands could not reach it. Lily had an idea. She found a chair and put it next to the shelf. She stood on the chair and raised her arms high. She tried to reach the toy, but it was still too high. Her hands were only at her waist. Lily did not give up. She looked around and saw a big stick. She used the stick to get the toy. She was so happy! Now, the toy was available for her to play with. Lily played with her new toy all day long.
