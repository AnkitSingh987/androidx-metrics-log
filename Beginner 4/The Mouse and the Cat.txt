Category: Beginner
Title: The Mouse and the Cat

Once upon a time there was a small mouse. She was a little mouse with a big dream. She wanted to find a pot of gold. "I want to find a pot of gold!" said the mouse. A friendly cat heard her, and he said, "I know how you can get a pot of gold!" "Really?" asked the mouse, her eyes wide with hope. "Yes!" said the cat. "It's very simple. All you need to do is be harmless and do your best to succeed." So the mouse started to work hard. All the while, she was careful to be harmless. Finally, after much effort, she found the pot of gold! She was so happy that she hopped up and down and squeaked for joy. The mouse had succeeded, and now she had a pot of gold!

One day, a little boy named Tim found an old cloth. He decided to wipe his toy car with it. As he wiped, his toy car became shiny and clean. Tim was very happy. Tim took his shiny toy car outside to play. He saw his friend, Sue, with her toy police car. Sue's police car was dirty. Tim said, "I can help you make your police car shiny, too!" Sue was excited. Tim used the old cloth to wipe Sue's police car. They both watched as the dirt went away. Sue said, "Thank you, Tim! Our cars are so shiny now." They laughed and played with their clean, shiny toy cars all day.

Once upon a time, in a big box, there was a little eraser. The eraser had no hair, so he was bald. He lived with many friends like pens, pencils, and crayons. They all liked to draw and write. One day, a little girl named Lily found the box. She opened it and said, "Wow! So many friends to play with!" She picked up the bald eraser and a pencil. She started to draw a big sun on a paper. While Lily was drawing, the pencil said, "Look! The sun is so bright!" The bald eraser was happy and wanted to help too. He pointed at a small mistake on the paper and said, "I can fix that for you!" Lily smiled and used the eraser to fix the mistake. They all had so much fun drawing and writing together. From that day on, the bald eraser and his friends lived happily with Lily, always ready to help her with her drawings.

Once there was a small boy named Tim. He loved to play in his yard with his toys. One sunny day, he found a toy car he had never seen before. Tim was very happy to find the new toy. Tim showed the toy car to his friend Sam who lived next door. Sam said, "That toy car does not belong to you. It is mine." Tim was ignorant and did not know what "belong" meant. Sam explained, "Belong means that the toy is mine, not yours." Just then, a little girl named Lily came running to them. She said, "I lost my toy car! Have you seen it?" Tim and Sam looked at each other. They now knew that the toy car belonged to Lily, not Sam. They gave the toy car back to Lily. She was very happy and thanked them. Tim and Sam learned a new word and made a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a unique bird. This bird had many gears. The bird liked to wake up early. One day, the bird woke up late. The bird was sad. It could not find its gears. The bird looked everywhere. The bird did not find its gears. The bird was very sad. The bird did not wake up early again. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to run, jump, and stretch his legs. One day, Spot saw a distant tree with a big red ball stuck in it. He wanted to get the ball down, so he thought of a plan. Spot asked his friend, Bird, to help him. "Bird, can you help me get the ball?" Spot said. Bird agreed to help, and they started to work together. Bird would fly up and try to pick the ball out of the tree, while Spot would stretch his legs and jump as high as he could. They tried and tried, but the ball wouldn't come down. Then, something unexpected happened. A big gust of wind blew the ball out of the tree, and it started to roll away! Spot and Bird chased after the ball, but it was moving too fast. The ball rolled down a hill and into a dark, scary forest. Spot and Bird were too scared to go in. They looked at each other, sad that they couldn't get the ball back. In the end, they had to go home without the big red ball, and they learned that sometimes, things don't work out the way you want them to.
