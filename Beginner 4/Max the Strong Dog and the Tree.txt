Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Strong Dog and the Tree

One day, a big strong dog named Max was playing in the park. Max had big muscles and loved to run and jump. His friend, a small cat named Luna, liked to watch Max play. Max said to Luna, "I bet I can rush to the tree and back before you can say my name!" Luna smiled and said, "Okay, let's see!" Max got ready to run. He looked at the tree and felt excited. Max started to rush to the tree. He was very fast, but the ground was dry and dusty. As Max ran, a lot of dust went up into the air. Max did not see the big rock in his way. He tripped and fell down. Luna ran to Max and asked, "Are you okay?" Max said, "Yes, but my muscles hurt a little." Luna helped Max get up, and they walked back to their spot in the park. Max learned to be more careful when running on dry ground.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a different ticket on the floor. It was a ticket to the big fair. Tim was very happy. He wanted to go to the fair and gain a new toy. At the fair, Tim saw many things. There were big rides, yummy food, and fun games. Tim wanted to play a game to win a toy. He saw a game with balls and cups. He said to the man, "I want to play this game, please." The man said, "Okay, Tim. You have three tries. If you get the ball in the cup, you gain a toy." Tim tried and tried. On his last try, the ball went into the cup! Tim gained a big, soft bear. He was so happy. He took his new bear home and they became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived with her mom, dad, and her dog, Max. Lily liked to play outside, but she did not like when her mom and dad drank coffee. She would always complain, "Yuck! I don't like coffee!" One day, Lily and Max found a big, interesting box in the backyard. They opened the box and saw a lot of toys inside. Lily thought, "These toys must be magic!" She picked up a toy and wished for a coffee-free day with her mom and dad. The next morning, Lily's mom and dad did not drink their coffee. Instead, they played with Lily and Max all day. Lily was so happy that her wish came true. She knew the interesting box and the toys inside were very special. From that day on, Lily never complained about coffee again, because she knew she had a magic toy to make her days fun and full of play.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big rain. The rain made a big flood. Everyone was scared. They did not know what to do. They wanted to stop the flood. One day, a slow turtle came to the town. The turtle said, "I can help you stop the flood." The people did not believe the turtle because he was slow. But the turtle was smart. He said, "We need to measure the water." The people worked with the turtle to measure the water. The turtle had a big surprise. He took out a big sponge. The sponge soaked up all the water from the flood. The people were so happy. They thanked the turtle for his help. The slow turtle saved the town from the big flood.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a messy boy named Tim. He loved to play in the mud and make a big mess. His mom always told him to clean up, but Tim did not like to listen. One day, the sun was shining and the sky was blue. Tim wanted to play outside, but his mom would not permit him to go out until he cleaned his room. Tim was sad, but he knew he had to listen to his mom. So, he cleaned his room and put away all his toys. After he finished, his mom let him go outside to play. When he went out, he saw a beautiful rainbow in the sky. Tim was so happy and knew that listening to his mom was the right thing to do. The moral of the story is to always listen to your parents and do what they say, because they know what is best for you.
