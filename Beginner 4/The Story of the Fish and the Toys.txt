Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Fish and the Toys

One day, a little shrimp was swimming in the sea. He was a selfish shrimp and did not like to share his toys with other sea friends. He loved to play with his ball all by himself. A big fish saw the shrimp playing alone and swam over to him. "Can I play with you?" the big fish asked. The selfish shrimp thought for a moment, and then he said, "No, I want to play alone." The big fish felt sad and swam away. Then, a little crab came up to the shrimp and said, "Let's clap and dance together!" The shrimp thought about it and decided to give it a try. They clapped their little claws and danced around in the water. As they danced, more sea friends joined them. The shrimp began to feel happy playing with his friends. He learned that sharing and playing together was more fun than being selfish and playing alone. From that day on, the shrimp shared his toys and played with all his sea friends.

Ben and Lily are twins. They like to play in the garden with their dog, Spot. One day, they see a big green plant with red balls on it. They run to the plant and pick some red balls. "Look, Spot, we have peppers!" Ben says. He holds a pepper in his hand and shows it to the dog. Spot sniffs the pepper and barks. "Can we eat them, Ben?" Lily asks. She likes to eat fruits and vegetables. She thinks they are healthy and yummy. "I don't know, Lily. Maybe we should ask Mommy first," Ben says. He is not sure if the peppers are good or bad. He remembers that some plants can make you sick. They take the peppers to the kitchen, where Mommy is making lunch. They show her their peppers and ask her if they can eat them. "Wow, you found some peppers in the garden! Good job, Ben and Lily!" Mommy says. She smiles and hugs them. "Yes, you can eat them. They are healthy and tasty. But be careful, some of them are very hot and spicy. They can make your mouth burn." Mommy washes the peppers and cuts them into pieces. She gives each twin a piece of pepper to try. Ben bites his pepper and makes a funny face. He spits it out and drinks some water. "Ow, ow, ow! This pepper is too hot! It hurts my mouth!" Ben says. He does not like the pepper. Lily bites her pepper and smiles. She likes the pepper. It is sweet and crunchy. "Yum, yum, yum! This pepper is good! It makes my mouth happy!" Lily says. She eats more pepper. Mommy laughs and gives them some bread and cheese to eat with the peppers. She tells them that peppers are good for them. They have vitamins and colors. They can make them strong and smart. Ben and Lily eat their lunch and share some peppers with Spot. They like to try new things. They start to like peppers more and more. They say thank you to Mommy and the plant. They are happy and healthy.

One day, a sweet cat named Kitty was playing with her ball. She loved to have fun with her friends. They played all day in the sun. But then, something unexpected happened. Kitty's ball rolled into the street. She ran after it and got hurt. Her friends were so sad and scared. A big, red ambulance came to help Kitty. The nice people took care of her. And guess what? The ambulance driver was Kitty's long-lost brother! They were so happy to see each other. Kitty got better and they all played together again.

One day, a little dog named Max went for a walk. He saw many cars in the traffic. Max wanted to cross the road, but there were too many cars. He had to wait for the cars to stop. Max started to shiver because it was cold and windy. Max saw a girl named Lily on the other side of the road. She had a big, warm coat. Lily called out to Max, "Don't shiver, little dog! I will help you cross the road." When the cars stopped, Lily took Max's paw and they crossed the road together. Max was happy and stopped shivering. Lily said, "Let's go to the store and buy you a coat." At the store, they found an expensive coat for Max. It was too much money. They looked more and found a coat that was not expensive. Max put on the coat and felt warm. Max and Lily were happy and walked home together.
