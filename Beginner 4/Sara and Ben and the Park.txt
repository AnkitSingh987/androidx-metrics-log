Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Ben and the Park

Sara and Ben are playing with their dolls. Sara has a pink doll and Ben has a blue doll. They pretend that the dolls are friends and go to the park. "Look, my doll has a big nose. She can smell the flowers very well," Ben says. Sara looks at Ben's doll and laughs. "Your doll has a funny nose. It is too big for her face. My doll has a pretty nose. It is small and cute," she says. Ben feels sad. He likes his doll's nose. He thinks it makes her special. He does not like Sara's laugh. He thinks it is rude. "No, your doll has a bad nose. It is too small for her face. She cannot smell anything. My doll has a good nose. It is big and strong," he says. Sara and Ben start to argue. They say mean things about each other's dolls. They do not want to play together anymore. "Fine, I do not want to be your friend. You are rude and silly. Go away," Sara says. "Fine, I do not want to be your friend either. You are mean and bossy. Leave me alone," Ben says. They turn away from each other and hug their dolls. They are angry and hurt. Their mom hears them and comes to see what is wrong. She sees them sitting on the floor with their dolls, not talking. "What is going on here? Why are you not playing nicely?" she asks. Sara and Ben tell her what happened. They both insist that their doll has the best nose and that the other one is rude. Mom shakes her head. She does not like their behavior. She thinks they are being silly and unkind. "Listen, Sara and Ben. There is no best nose. All noses are different and good. Your dolls are both beautiful and they can be friends. You should not laugh at or insult each other's dolls. That is rude and hurtful. You should apologize and share your dolls. That is polite and friendly," she says. Sara and Ben look at each other. They feel sorry for what they said. They realize that they were wrong. They do not want to lose their friend. "I am sorry, Ben. I should not have laughed at your doll's nose. It is big and special. Can I see it?" Sara says. "I am sorry, Sara. I should not have said your doll's nose is bad. It is small and cute. Do you want to hold it?" Ben says. They smile and hug. They swap their dolls and look at them. They see that they are both pretty and they can smell the flowers very well. They thank their mom and go back to playing. They pretend that their dolls are friends and go to the park. They are happy and kind.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy box in his room. One day, Tim's mom told him to shut the toy box because it was very messy. Tim felt a little miserable because he wanted to play with his toys. Tim went to the park with his mom. He played on the swings and slides. But he left his favorite toy, a small red car, at home. Tim was sad because he wanted to play with the car at the park. When Tim and his mom came back home, they saw that the dog had chewed up the small red car. Tim was very miserable because his favorite toy was broken. He wished that he had shut the toy box and not left the car out.

Jim was walking down the street one day when he noticed something strange. He saw a silver animal leaning against a tree. He didn't know what it was, but it looked harmless. Jim cautiously walked closer, wondering if it would move. But instead, the animal suddenly spoke! "Hello, I'm Carl. What's your name?" Jim was very surprised and couldn't help but smile. "My name is Jim," he said. Carl nodded. "Nice to meet you, Jim. Do you want to be friends?" Jim thought for a moment and then nodded his head. This harmless animal was the best friend Jim had ever had. He and Carl have been the best of friends ever since.
